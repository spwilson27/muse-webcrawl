NIC — Nepal Network Information Centre (.np ccTLD)

The .np country-code top-level domain is Nepal's namespace on the internet. Registration of .np domains has historically been administered locally in Nepal (by Mercantile Communications, which has run the .np registry for decades, as recalled), with domains offered free to Nepali citizens and organisations who document eligibility.

Registering a .np domain

As long recalled, .np registrations were famously free of charge for personal and organisational names matched to citizenship certificates or company registration documents, under second-level labels such as com.np, org.np, gov.np, edu.np and net.np, processed through the registry's online system (register.com.np in recent years).

Nepal's internet

Nepal's country code is NP and its calling code +977; the .np namespace dates to the mid-1990s (1995, as recalled), among the earliest in South Asia, and government technology coordination has involved the National Information Technology Center.
