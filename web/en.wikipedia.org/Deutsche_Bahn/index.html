Deutsche Bahn

Deutsche Bahn AG (DB) is the national railway company of Germany. Headquartered in Berlin, it is a state-owned joint-stock company and one of the world's largest transport and logistics groups, operating passenger and freight rail services across Germany and internationally.

History

Deutsche Bahn was formed on 1 January 1994 through the merger of West Germany's Deutsche Bundesbahn and East Germany's Deutsche Reichsbahn following German reunification. The reform created DB as a commercial company wholly owned by the federal government, organized into subsidiaries for long-distance passenger services (DB Fernverkehr), regional services (DB Regio), freight (DB Cargo), infrastructure (DB Netz, later part of DB InfraGO), and stations.

Services

DB operates the high-speed Intercity Express (ICE) trains, Intercity and regional services, and many urban S-Bahn networks. Its logistics arm, DB Schenker, operates worldwide. The company employs several hundred thousand people and carries billions of passengers per year.

Criticism

Deutsche Bahn has faced persistent criticism in recent years for declining punctuality on long-distance services, aging infrastructure, and underinvestment, prompting large federal investment programs for network renewal.
