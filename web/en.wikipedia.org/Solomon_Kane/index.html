Solomon Kane

Solomon Kane is a fictional character created by the American pulp writer Robert E. Howard. A late-16th- to early-17th-century Puritan, Kane is a sombre, gloomy man of pale skin and cold eyes, who wanders the world righting wrongs and battling evil in all its forms, driven by an unshakeable belief in his divine mission. He first appeared in Weird Tales in 1928, in the story "Red Shadows".

Character

Kane is typically described as tall and gaunt, dressed in the black slouch hat and plain garb of a Puritan, armed with a rapier, a dirk and flintlock pistols, and later with a mysterious juju staff given to him by the African shaman N'Longa. Unlike Howard's other famous creation, Conan the Barbarian, Kane is a fanatical champion of justice and protector of the weak, though his righteous wrath is itself terrifying.

Stories

The Kane tales, set in Europe and Africa, blend adventure with horror: "Skulls in the Stars", "The Hills of the Dead", "Rattle of Bones", "Wings in the Night" and the poem "Solomon Kane's Homecoming" among them. Several stories were left unfinished at Howard's death and were completed by other hands, notably Ramsey Campbell.

Adaptations

The character has appeared in Marvel Comics adaptations, role-playing games, and the 2009 film Solomon Kane starring James Purefoy.
