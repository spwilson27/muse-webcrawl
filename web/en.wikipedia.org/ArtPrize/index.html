ArtPrize

ArtPrize is an annual international art competition and festival held in Grand Rapids, Michigan. Founded in 2009 by entrepreneur Rick DeVos, the event displays works by hundreds of artists in venues across the city — museums, galleries, bars, parks, bridges and public spaces — and awards substantial cash prizes determined largely by public vote, alongside juried awards.

Format

ArtPrize is radically open: any artist over 18 may enter, and any space in the designated district may register as a venue and host art. Visitors vote for their favourite works by mobile app, and early editions awarded a top prize of $200,000 — among the largest art prizes in the world — by popular vote alone. From 2012 a parallel system of juried awards, chosen by art professionals, was given equal weight, and the prize structure has since been reorganised into categories with both public and juried winners.

Impact and criticism

The event draws hundreds of thousands of visitors and has been credited with significant economic and cultural benefits for Grand Rapids, becoming a flagship of the city's identity. Critics have debated whether popular voting rewards spectacle over artistic merit; defenders argue the model democratises art and creates an unusually broad public conversation about it. The competition took a hiatus after 2022 while its format was reimagined.
