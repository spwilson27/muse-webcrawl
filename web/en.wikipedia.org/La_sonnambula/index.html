La sonnambula

La sonnambula (The Sleepwalker) is an opera semiseria in two acts by Vincenzo Bellini, with a libretto by Felice Romani based on a scenario by Eugène Scribe. It premiered at the Teatro Carcano in Milan on 6 March 1831 and remains one of the cornerstones of the bel canto repertoire.

ComposerVincenzo Bellini
LibrettistFelice Romani
Premiere6 March 1831, Milan
Acts2

Synopsis

In a Swiss village, the orphan Amina is about to marry the wealthy farmer Elvino. Found asleep in the room of Count Rodolfo, she is denounced as unfaithful, but when she is seen sleepwalking across a perilously high mill bridge, her innocence and condition are revealed and the couple are reunited.

Music

The role of Amina, written for Giuditta Pasta, contains some of Bellini's most celebrated long-breathed melodies, culminating in the cabaletta "Ah! non giunge uman pensiero".
