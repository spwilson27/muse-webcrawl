EuroAirport Basel Mulhouse Freiburg

IATA: BSL / MLH / EAP
Location: Saint-Louis, France, near Basel (Switzerland) and Mulhouse (France)
Opened: 1946

EuroAirport Basel Mulhouse Freiburg (IATA: BSL, MLH, EAP) is an international airport in the administrative commune of Saint-Louis, in the French Alsace region, near the Swiss and German borders. It serves the trinational region around Basel (Switzerland), Mulhouse (France), and Freiburg (Germany).

History and status

The airport opened in 1946 and is governed by a Franco-Swiss treaty of 1949 that gives it a unique binational status: it is operated jointly by France and Switzerland, lies entirely on French soil, but includes a Swiss customs sector connected to Basel by a duty-free customs road. Passengers can choose between French (MLH) and Swiss (BSL) airport codes, and the airport also markets itself with the code EAP.

Facilities and traffic

The airport has two runways and a single terminal divided into French and Swiss sectors. It is a base for easyJet Switzerland and handles around nine million passengers a year in peak years, making it one of the busiest airports serving Switzerland. Cargo operations take advantage of the region's pharmaceutical industry.
