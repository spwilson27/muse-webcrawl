The 2014 FIFA World Cup Final was the final match of the 2014 World Cup, the 20th edition of FIFA's competition for national football teams. The match was played at the Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on 13 July 2014, and was contested by Germany and Argentina.

Match

The match was goalless after 90 minutes despite chances for both sides, including a missed one-on-one by Argentina's Gonzalo Higuaín and a header against the post by Germany's Benedikt Höwedes. In the second half of extra time, substitute Mario Götze volleyed in André Schürrle's cross in the 113th minute for the only goal of the game.

Significance

Germany's victory was its fourth World Cup title and the first by a European team in the Americas. Germany had reached the final after a famous 7–1 semi-final win over hosts Brazil. Argentina's Lionel Messi was awarded the tournament's Golden Ball.
