Joe Perry (musician)

Joe Perry
Born10 September 1950, Lawrence, Massachusetts, U.S.
OccupationGuitarist, songwriter
Known forAerosmith

Joseph Anthony Pereira, known professionally as Joe Perry, is an American guitarist best known as a founding member and lead guitarist of the rock band Aerosmith, which he formed in Boston in 1970 with Steven Tyler and Tom Hamilton.

Career

With Tyler, Perry formed the "Toxic Twins" songwriting partnership behind Aerosmith hits including "Dream On", "Walk This Way", "Sweet Emotion" and "Back in the Saddle". He left the band in 1979, formed the Joe Perry Project, and returned in 1984 for the band's major comeback.

Style and honours

Perry's blues-based riffing helped make Aerosmith the best-selling American rock band of all time. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with Aerosmith in 2001 and into the Songwriters Hall of Fame with Tyler in 2013. He is also a member of the supergroup Hollywood Vampires.
