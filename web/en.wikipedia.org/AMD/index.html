Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is an American multinational semiconductor company that develops computer processors and related technologies for business and consumer markets. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and colleagues from Fairchild Semiconductor, it is headquartered in Santa Clara, California.

History

AMD became Intel's principal rival in x86 microprocessors, producing the Athlon, Opteron, and later Ryzen processor families, and extended the x86 architecture to 64 bits with AMD64. It acquired graphics company ATI in 2006, spun off its manufacturing arm as GlobalFoundries in 2009, and acquired Xilinx in 2022.

Products

Its main product lines are Ryzen CPUs for desktops and laptops, EPYC processors for servers, Radeon graphics cards, and Instinct accelerators used in supercomputing and AI data centres.
