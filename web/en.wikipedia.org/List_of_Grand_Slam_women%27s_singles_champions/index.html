List of Grand Slam women's singles champions

Margaret Court holds the record with 24 Grand Slam singles titles.

This article lists the winners of the Grand Slam women's singles championships in tennis: the Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon and US Open. Grand Slam tournaments began with Wimbledon in 1884 for the women's singles event, followed by the US Championships in 1887, the French Championships in 1897, and the Australasian Championships in 1922.

Calendar-year Grand Slam

Only three women have won all four majors in a single calendar year: Maureen Connolly (1953), Margaret Court (1970) and Steffi Graf (1988), the latter also winning Olympic gold in the same year for a "Golden Slam".
