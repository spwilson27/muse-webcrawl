Reference ellipsoid

A reference ellipsoid is a mathematically defined surface that approximates the geoid — the true, irregular shape of Earth — for use in geodesy, cartography and satellite navigation. Earth is flattened at the poles by rotation, so an oblate spheroid defined by its semi-major (equatorial) axis and flattening fits far better than a sphere.

Different ellipsoids suit different regions; the global standard is WGS 84, used by GPS, with a semi-major axis of 6,378,137 m and flattening of about 1/298.257. Latitude and longitude coordinates are defined relative to a chosen ellipsoid and datum; using the wrong datum can shift positions by hundreds of metres.
