Charles Taylor (philosopher)

Charles Margrave Taylor (born 5 November 1931) is a Canadian philosopher, professor emeritus at McGill University, known for his contributions to political philosophy, the philosophy of social science, and the history of philosophy.

Life and career

Born in Montreal, Taylor studied at McGill and at Balliol College, Oxford, where he earned his doctorate in 1961. He stood unsuccessfully for the New Democratic Party in federal elections in the 1960s and taught at McGill for most of his career.

Work

His books include Hegel (1975), Sources of the Self: The Making of the Modern Identity (1989), The Ethics of Authenticity (1992), Modern Social Imaginaries (2004), and A Secular Age (2007), which examined secularization in the West. Taylor defends communitarian and multicultural positions, notably in the essay "The Politics of Recognition."

Awards

He received the Templeton Prize in 2007, the Kyoto Prize in 2008, and the Berggruen Prize in 2016.
