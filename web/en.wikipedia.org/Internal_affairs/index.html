Internal affairs

Internal affairs is a division of a law enforcement agency that investigates incidents and suspicions of lawbreaking and professional misconduct attributed to officers on the force. It is sometimes known as an internal affairs bureau, professional standards unit or inspectorate.

Function

Internal affairs investigators handle complaints from the public and from within the department, investigate shootings and other uses of force, and may conduct integrity tests. In many jurisdictions their work is complemented by civilian review boards and independent ombudsmen.

Criticism

Critics argue that police investigating police risks bias and a "code of silence", while defenders stress the need for specialised knowledge of police procedure. High-profile misconduct cases have periodically led to reforms strengthening outside oversight.
