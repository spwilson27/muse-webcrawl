Breakdance

Breakdance, also known as breaking or b-boying/b-girling, is a style of street dance that originated among African American and Puerto Rican youth in the Bronx, New York City, during the 1970s. It is one of the foundational elements of hip hop culture.

Elements

Breaking consists of four main categories: toprock (standing footwork), downrock (footwork performed on the floor), power moves (acrobatic spins such as headspins and windmills), and freezes (stylish poses balanced on the hands). Dancers perform in circles called cyphers and compete in judged battles.

History

DJ Kool Herc's breakbeat parties provided the music; dancers who performed during the breaks became known as b-boys and b-girls. Crews such as the Rock Steady Crew and the Zulu Kings brought the form to a global audience in the early 1980s.

Olympic inclusion

Breaking made its Olympic debut at the Paris 2024 Summer Games, with medals awarded in B-Boys and B-Girls competitions. It had earlier appeared at the 2018 Youth Olympic Games in Buenos Aires.
