Religious tourism

Religious tourism encompasses travel motivated by faith – pilgrimages, visits to sacred sites, religious conferences and heritage touring. India's Kumbh Mela, the largest religious gathering in the world, exemplifies its scale: the 2025 Maha Kumbh at Prayagraj was reported to have drawn hundreds of millions of visits over 45 days.

Largest eventsKumbh Mela; Hajj; Arbaeen pilgrimage
Key regionsSouth Asia; Middle East; Europe; Latin America

Dimensions

Analysts estimate that hundreds of millions of journeys each year are undertaken for religious purposes, generating significant income for host cities from Varanasi and Karbala to Lourdes and Santiago de Compostela. Governments increasingly plan infrastructure – tent cities, transport corridors, riverfront works – around mega-events like the Kumbh.

Issues

Faith tourism raises questions of crowd management, environmental pressure on sacred rivers and sites, and the balance between devotion and commerce at shrines.
