Camino de Santiago

The Camino de Santiago is the pilgrimage route – in fact a network of routes – to the Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela in Galicia, Spain, where tradition holds the remains of St James the Great are buried. As one of the flagship journeys of religious tourism, it draws several hundred thousand recorded pilgrims a year.

Modern pilgrimage

Walkers and cyclists follow yellow arrows and scallop-shell markers, sleeping in municipal and private albergues. Motivations today mix devotion, culture, sport and well-being; Holy Years (when 25 July falls on a Sunday) bring exceptional crowds.

Heritage

The Routes of Santiago de Compostela were inscribed as UNESCO World Heritage Sites, recognising the road's medieval role in spreading Romanesque art, ideas and peoples across Europe.
