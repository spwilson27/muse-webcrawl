Activision Blizzard, Inc.

Type: Subsidiary of Microsoft
Industry: Video games
Founded: July 10, 2008
Headquarters: Santa Monica, California, U.S.
Parent: Microsoft Gaming

Activision Blizzard, Inc. is an American video game holding company based in Santa Monica, California. It was founded in July 2008 through the merger of Activision, Inc. and Vivendi Games. The company comprises five business units: Activision Publishing, Blizzard Entertainment, King, Major League Gaming, and Activision Blizzard Studios.

Franchises

The company owns and operates major franchises including Call of Duty, Warcraft, Diablo, Overwatch, StarCraft, Hearthstone, Candy Crush Saga, Tony Hawk's, Crash Bandicoot, and Spyro.

Acquisition by Microsoft

Microsoft announced its intent to acquire Activision Blizzard in January 2022 for $68.7 billion. After extensive regulatory review, the deal closed on October 13, 2023, making Activision Blizzard a division of Microsoft Gaming. Long-time CEO Bobby Kotick departed at the end of 2023.

Controversies

In 2021, California's Department of Fair Employment and Housing filed a lawsuit alleging widespread sexual harassment and workplace discrimination, precipitating employee walkouts, leadership departures, and settlements; a $54 million settlement with the state agency was approved in 2023.
