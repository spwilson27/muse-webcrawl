Belfast

Belfast is the capital and largest city of Northern Ireland, standing on the banks of the River Lagan on the east coast. By population it is among the largest cities in the United Kingdom and on the island of Ireland.

History

Belfast grew into a major industrial centre in the 19th century, noted for linen, rope-making, tobacco and shipbuilding; the Harland and Wolff shipyard built RMS Titanic there. The city was heavily affected by the Troubles in the late 20th century and has undergone regeneration since the Good Friday Agreement of 1998.

Culture and economy

Landmarks include Belfast City Hall, the Titanic Belfast visitor attraction, Queen's University and the Cathedral Quarter. The city is served by two airports.
