Enoch

Enoch
Parents: Jared
Children: Methuselah
Known for: Walking with God, apocalyptic literature

Enoch is a biblical figure, the son of Jared and father of Methuselah, described in the Book of Genesis as a man who "walked with God" and was taken by God rather than dying.

Biblical account

Genesis 5 gives Enoch's genealogy and states that he lived 365 years before God took him. The New Testament Epistle to the Hebrews praises his faith, and Jude quotes a prophecy attributed to him.

Enochic literature

Several ancient works are attributed to Enoch, most famously 1 Enoch (the Book of Enoch), which describes fallen angels, heavenly journeys and judgement. It is canonical in the Ethiopian Orthodox Church but not in most other traditions. 2 Enoch and 3 Enoch are later related texts.

Legacy

Enoch appears in Jewish, Christian, Islamic (as Idris) and other traditions as a righteous scribe, prophet and mediator.
