Cotai Strip

The Cotai Strip is a strip of reclaimed land in Cotai, Macau, between the islands of Taipa and Coloane, developed into a district of large casino resorts modelled on the Las Vegas Strip. The name is a trademark of Las Vegas Sands.

Development

Cotai was created by land reclamation and developed after Macau liberalised gaming in 2002. The Venetian Macao opened in 2007 as one of the world's largest buildings, followed by City of Dreams, Galaxy Macau, Studio City, Wynn Palace, MGM Cotai, the Parisian and the Londoner.

Economy

The resorts helped Macau surpass Las Vegas in gaming revenue. Cotai is connected by the Lotus Bridge to Hengqin in mainland China and by Macau's light rail.
