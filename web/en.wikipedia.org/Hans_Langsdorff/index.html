Hans Langsdorff

Hans Wilhelm Langsdorff (20 March 1894 – 20 December 1939) was a German naval officer, captain of the pocket battleship Admiral Graf Spee during the Battle of the River Plate in the Second World War.

Career

Langsdorff joined the Imperial German Navy in 1912 and served in the First World War. In 1936 he took command of the newly commissioned Graf Spee. At the outbreak of war in 1939 he raided Allied merchant shipping in the South Atlantic, sinking nine ships while treating captured crews humanely.

Battle of the River Plate

On 13 December 1939, Graf Spee fought the British cruisers Exeter, Achilles, and Ajax off Uruguay. Believing a superior force awaited him and under diplomatic pressure, Langsdorff scuttled his ship in the River Plate estuary on 17 December. He shot himself in Buenos Aires three days later.
