Kolam

A kolam, also known as muggu, tarai alankaranalu or rangoli in some regions, is a form of traditional decorative art drawn using rice flour, chalk powder or coloured powders on floors and doorsteps in Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka and other parts of southern India and Sri Lanka.

Practice

Kolams are traditionally drawn each morning, usually by women, in geometric patterns built around a grid of dots (pulli), with lines looped or joined in symmetrical, often mathematically intricate designs. They are believed to bring prosperity and welcome guests and deities, while the rice flour also feeds ants and birds.

Variations

Festival kolams for occasions such as Pongal are larger and coloured. Related traditions include rangoli in northern India and alpana in Bengal.
