Ideogram

An ideogram is a symbol that represents an idea or concept rather than a specific word or sound. Ideograms form the basis of ideographic writing systems and are also used in modern signage, mathematics, and computing.

Writing systems

Chinese characters are often described as ideograms, though most are in fact logograms with phonetic components; true ideographs are a small subset. Ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs and cuneiform likewise mixed ideographic and phonetic signs.

Modern use

Universal pictograms — such as traffic signs, airport symbols, and the figures on restroom doors — function ideographically across languages. Blissymbols and Isotype are constructed ideographic systems.

Versus pictograms and logograms

A pictogram depicts its referent pictorially, a logogram stands for a word or morpheme, and an ideogram stands for a concept; in practice the categories overlap.
