Produsage

Produsage is a term coined by media scholar Axel Bruns to describe the type of user-led content creation that takes place in a variety of online environments, in which participants both produce and use content. It blends the concepts of production and usage, and is distinguished from traditional models in which producers and consumers are separate groups.

Characteristics

Bruns identifies four defining characteristics of produsage: open participation and communal evaluation; fluid roles, with participants moving between producer and user; unfinished artefacts and continuing development (products are never complete but constantly evolving); and common property with individual rewards. Examples include Wikipedia, open-source software projects, citizen journalism such as OhmyNews, and collaborative tagging on platforms like Flickr.

Related concepts

Produsage builds on Alvin Toffler's notion of the "prosumer" but emphasises a collaborative, communal process rather than individual consumption-production. It is central to Bruns's book Blogs, Wikipedia, Second Life and Beyond: From Production to Produsage (2008).
