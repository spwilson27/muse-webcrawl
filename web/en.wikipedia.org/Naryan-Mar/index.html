Naryan-Mar (Russian: Нарьян-Мар; Nenets for "red town") is a town and seaport in northern Russia and the administrative centre of the Nenets Autonomous Okrug. It lies on the right bank of the Pechora River near its delta on the Barents Sea, north of the Arctic Circle.

History

The town was founded in 1931, incorporating the earlier sawmill settlement of Beloshchelye and the village of Nosovaya, and received town status in 1935. It grew as the administrative and supply centre for the Nenets region, whose economy traditionally rested on reindeer herding, fishing and hunting, and from the later twentieth century on oil and gas development in the Timan-Pechora basin.

Geography and transport

Naryan-Mar has a subarctic climate with long, cold winters and short, cool summers. The town has no road or rail connection to the rest of Russia; transport depends on its airport, the Pechora River, sea shipping during the navigation season, and a winter road (zimnik). The Epiphany Cathedral and the wooden former post office building are local landmarks, and the town hosts the Nenets Regional Museum.
