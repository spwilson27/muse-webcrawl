ASICS Corporation (株式会社アシックス, Kabushiki gaisha Ashikkusu) is a Japanese multinational corporation that produces sportswear and sporting equipment. The name is an acronym of the Latin phrase anima sana in corpore sano ("a healthy mind in a healthy body"). Founded in 1949 by Kihachiro Onitsuka in Kobe as Onitsuka Co., Ltd., the company is especially known for running shoes and is one of the world's largest sportswear manufacturers.

History

Onitsuka began by making basketball shoes in his living room, then achieved fame with designs such as the Marathon Tabi (1953) and the Magic Runner. As Onitsuka Tiger, the company exported shoes to the United States, where Phil Knight and Bill Bowerman distributed them through Blue Ribbon Sports before founding Nike; the later rupture between the firms was dramatized in accounts of Nike's early years. In 1977 Onitsuka Tiger merged with GTO and Jelenk to form ASICS. Signature technologies followed, notably the GEL cushioning system introduced in 1986, and the long-running Gel-Kayano stability trainer (from 1993). ASICS acquired brands including Haglöfs (2010) and the race-registration platform Race Roster, and in 2015 launched the "ASICS World" services business.

Products and sponsorships

ASICS is strongest in running, volleyball, wrestling, tennis, and cricket footwear, and it is a major supplier to marathon events and national teams. It was an official partner of the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games, supplying the Japanese team, and sponsors runners including elite marathoners and tennis players such as Novak Djokovic (footwear). In the mid-2020s the brand grew rapidly in the performance-running boom, with racing shoes such as the Metaspeed series and everyday trainers like the Novablast and Gel-Nimbus, alongside a fashion revival of the Onitsuka Tiger line.
