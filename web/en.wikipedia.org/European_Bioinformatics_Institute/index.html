The European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) is an international research institute and service centre that provides freely available data from life science experiments. It is part of the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) and is located on the Wellcome Genome Campus at Hinxton, near Cambridge, England, alongside the Wellcome Sanger Institute.

History and mission

The EBI grew out of EMBL's nucleotide sequence database activities in Heidelberg and was established at Hinxton in 1994, when EMBL's outstation moved to share the campus with the Sanger Centre. Its mission is to maintain the world's public biological data archives and to enable research across the life sciences. The institute employs several hundred staff in service, research, and training roles.

Data resources

The EBI maintains many of the core databases of modern biology, including:

  • European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) — raw and assembled DNA/RNA sequence data, the European mirror of GenBank within INSDC
  • Ensembl — genome browser and annotation for vertebrate (and, via Ensembl Genomes, other) species
  • UniProt — protein sequence and functional information (in consortium with SIB and PIR)
  • Protein Data Bank in Europe (PDBe) — 3D macromolecular structures
  • ChEMBL — bioactive drug-like molecules; ChEBI — chemical entities of biological interest
  • ArrayExpress / BioStudies and PRIDE — functional genomics and proteomics data
  • AlphaFold Protein Structure Database, developed with DeepMind

ELIXIR and training

The EBI hosts the hub of ELIXIR, the European infrastructure for life science data. It runs an extensive programme of courses and webinars in bioinformatics for working scientists worldwide.
