Integrated Forecast System

The Integrated Forecasting System (IFS) is a global numerical weather prediction system developed and maintained by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) in Reading, England. It is widely regarded as one of the most accurate global weather models in the world and is used by national meteorological services across Europe and beyond.

Components

The IFS couples an atmospheric model, including the HRES high-resolution deterministic forecast and a 51-member ensemble prediction system, with an ocean wave model and, in extended-range products, the NEMO ocean model. It uses four-dimensional variational data assimilation (4D-Var) to initialise forecasts from observations worldwide.

Development

The model is updated in regular "cycles" that improve resolution, physics and data assimilation; a major upgrade in 2023 raised the ensemble resolution substantially. Since 2024, ECMWF has also run the AIFS, a machine-learning forecasting system, alongside the physics-based IFS, and IFS model data have been released under an open data policy.
