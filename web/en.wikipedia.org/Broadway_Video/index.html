Broadway Video

Broadway Video
Founded: 1979
Founder: Lorne Michaels
Headquarters: New York City (30 Rockefeller Plaza)
Industry: Television and film production

Broadway Video is an American entertainment production company founded in 1979 by Lorne Michaels, the creator of Saturday Night Live (SNL). Named with a nod to Broadway theatre, the company is based at 30 Rockefeller Plaza in New York City and has produced SNL for NBC since Michaels' return to the show in 1985.

Productions

Through and alongside SNL, Broadway Video has produced the late-night talk shows hosted by SNL alumni (including Late Night in its Conan O'Brien, Jimmy Fallon and Seth Meyers incarnations and The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon), comedy series such as 30 Rock (through its association with Tina Fey), Portlandia, The Kids in the Hall, Documentary Now!, the variety series Maya & Marty, and That Damn Michael Che. In film, the company produced SNL-related features through Paramount including Wayne's World, Tommy Boy, Mean Girls (2004, with Fey), Hot Rod and MacGruber. Its post-production arm, Broadway Video Exchange, long handled editing and distribution services for SNL and other productions.

Above Average

In 2012 Broadway Video launched Above Average, a digital comedy network and YouTube channel producing short-form series with SNL writers and performers. The company has also been involved in the distribution and marketing of SNL's digital shorts and in producing SNL specials and anniversary programming, including the show's 40th anniversary special in 2015.
