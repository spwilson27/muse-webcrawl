The Greater Toronto Area, commonly known as the GTA, is the most populous metropolitan area in Canada. It consists of the central City of Toronto and the four surrounding regional municipalities of Durham, Halton, Peel and York. The region had a population of about 6.3 million in the 2021 census; together with the nearby city of Hamilton and other communities it forms part of the wider Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area and the Golden Horseshoe conurbation.

Geography and municipalities

The GTA stretches along the northwestern shore of Lake Ontario from Oshawa and Clarington in the east to Burlington in the west, and north to the southern shore of Lake Simcoe. Its major suburban municipalities include Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Ajax, Pickering and Whitby. The area code overlay region (416, 647, 437, 905, 905's overlays 289 and 365) roughly matches local usage of the term GTA.

Economy and transport

The GTA is Canada's principal financial, commercial and media centre and home to Toronto Pearson International Airport, the country's busiest. Regional transit is provided by GO Transit's trains and buses, coordinated by the provincial agency Metrolinx, alongside municipal systems such as the Toronto Transit Commission.
