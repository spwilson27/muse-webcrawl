101955 Bennu

101955 Bennu is a carbonaceous near-Earth asteroid in the Apollo group, discovered by LINEAR in 1999. Named after the Egyptian mythological bird Bennu, it is about 490 metres in diameter and is a rubble-pile body with a spinning-top shape.

Exploration

NASA's OSIRIS-REx spacecraft arrived at Bennu in December 2018, mapped its surface, and in October 2020 collected a sample of regolith. The sample return capsule landed in Utah on 24 September 2023, delivering at least 121 grams of material — the largest asteroid sample ever returned.

Characteristics

Bennu's surface is covered in boulders, and OSIRIS-REx observed particles being ejected from its surface. Its carbon-rich material preserves compounds from the early Solar System.

Impact hazard

Bennu is classified as a potentially hazardous asteroid, with a small cumulative probability of impacting Earth in the late 22nd century, most notably in 2182.
