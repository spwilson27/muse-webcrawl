Twelfth Doctor

Peter Capaldi as the Twelfth Doctor

The Twelfth Doctor is an incarnation of the Doctor, the protagonist of the BBC science fiction television series Doctor Who. He is portrayed by Scottish actor Peter Capaldi. The character first appeared in the 2013 Christmas special "The Time of the Doctor" and starred in series 8 to 10 (2014–2017), departing in the 2017 Christmas special "Twice Upon a Time".

Characterisation

The Twelfth Doctor is fiercer, more abrasive and more alien than his recent predecessors, often blunt to the point of apparent callousness while wrestling with the question "Am I a good man?" Beneath the severity he remains deeply compassionate, committed to kindness and to standing up for the vulnerable. His era explored his centuries-long friendship and rivalry with Missy (the Master), his guilt over the Time War, and a long, meditative arc about identity, loss and redemption. His costume — a dark Crombie coat, white shirt and (later) a velvet coat — gave him a distinctive magician-like silhouette.

Companions and stories

His principal companions were Clara Oswald (Jenna Coleman) and, later, Bill Potts (Pearl Mackie) with Nardole (Matt Lucas). Notable episodes include "Listen", "Mummy on the Orient Express", "Flatline", "Dark Water", "Last Christmas", the acclaimed solo episode "Heaven Sent", "The Husbands of River Song", "The Pilot", "World Enough and Time" and "The Doctor Falls".

Reception

Capaldi's performance was widely praised for its intensity, gravitas and emotional depth, and "Heaven Sent" is frequently cited among the finest episodes of the revived series.
