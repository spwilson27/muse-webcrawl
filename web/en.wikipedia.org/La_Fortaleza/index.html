La Fortaleza

La Fortaleza, also known as the Palacio de Santa Catalina, is the official residence of the governor of Puerto Rico, in Old San Juan. Built between 1533 and 1540, it is the oldest executive mansion in continuous use in the Americas.

History

Originally a fortress defending San Juan harbour, it was repeatedly attacked and rebuilt, and was transformed into a palatial residence in the 19th century under Governor Rafael Primo de Rivera. Nearly fifty governors have resided there.

Heritage

La Fortaleza is part of the San Juan National Historic Site and was inscribed, with Old San Juan, as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.
