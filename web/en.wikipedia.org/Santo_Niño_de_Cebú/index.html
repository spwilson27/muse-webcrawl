Santo Niño de Cebú

DepictsChrist Child
LocationBasilica del Santo Niño, Cebu City, Philippines
DateGifted 1521; found 1565
Feast dayThird Sunday of January

The Santo Niño de Cebú is a Roman Catholic title of the Christ Child associated with a religious image of the infant Jesus venerated in Cebu City, Philippines. It is the oldest Christian artefact in the country.

History

The image was presented by Ferdinand Magellan to Queen Juana of Cebu on her baptism in 1521. Forty-four years later, in 1565, a soldier of Miguel López de Legazpi's expedition found the statue preserved in a pine box amid the ashes of a burned house, an event regarded as miraculous. It is enshrined in the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño in a bulletproof glass case.

Devotion

The annual Sinulog festival, held each January in Cebu, draws millions of devotees honouring the Santo Niño with processions and street dancing.
