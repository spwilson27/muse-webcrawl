Einstein

Einstein is a German surname. This article lists notable people bearing the name; for the physicist, see Albert Einstein.

Einstein is a German-Jewish surname meaning roughly "one stone" or "enclosed by stone." The name is borne by a number of notable people, above all the theoretical physicist Albert Einstein (1879–1955), developer of the theory of relativity and winner of the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics. The name is also used for various places, institutions, scientific units and concepts named in his honour.

People with the surname

  • Albert Einstein (1879–1955), German-born theoretical physicist
  • Alfred Einstein (1880–1952), German-American musicologist, editor of the Köchel catalogue
  • Arik Einstein (1939–2013), Israeli singer, songwriter and actor
  • Bob Einstein (1942–2019), American comedian and actor, creator of Super Dave Osborne
  • Carl Einstein (1885–1940), German art historian and writer
  • Hans Albert Einstein (1904–1973), Swiss-American engineer, son of Albert Einstein
  • Eduard Einstein (1910–1965), son of Albert Einstein
  • Lina Einstein, family name borne by several members of the Einstein family

Named after Albert Einstein

The chemical element einsteinium (Es, atomic number 99), the unit of photochemistry the einstein, the Albert Einstein College of Medicine, numerous schools, and the Einstein Telescope gravitational-wave project are all named for the physicist. In popular culture "Einstein" has become a byword for genius.
