MusiCares

MusiCares Foundation is a non-profit organisation established in 1989 by the Recording Academy (the organisation behind the Grammy Awards) to provide a safety net of critical assistance for music people in times of need. Its services cover financial, medical and personal emergencies, as well as addiction recovery and preventive health programmes.

Programmes

MusiCares provides grants for needs such as rent, utilities, medical and dental expenses, and funeral costs; it also runs addiction recovery services, including "safe harbor" rooms at major events, and free health clinics and screenings. During the COVID-19 pandemic it distributed substantial relief funding to music workers whose livelihoods were disrupted.

Person of the Year

The foundation's best-known fundraising event is the annual MusiCares Person of the Year gala, held during Grammy Week, which honours an artist both for musical achievement and philanthropy. Honorees have included Paul McCartney, Bruce Springsteen, Dolly Parton, Lionel Richie, Aerosmith, Joni Mitchell and Bon Jovi.
