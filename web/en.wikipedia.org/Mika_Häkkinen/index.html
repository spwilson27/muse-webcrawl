Mika Pauli Häkkinen (born 28 September 1968) is a Finnish former racing driver, twice Formula One World Champion, winning the drivers' championship in 1998 and 1999 with McLaren-Mercedes. Nicknamed "The Flying Finn", he is widely regarded as one of the fastest drivers of his era and was Michael Schumacher's most respected rival; Schumacher himself named Häkkinen as the opponent he most admired.

Early career

Häkkinen began karting at the age of five and won multiple Finnish, Scandinavian, and Nordic karting titles. Managed by former world champion Keke Rosberg, he progressed through Formula Ford and Formula Opel Lotus — winning the Opel Lotus Euroseries in 1988 — and won the British Formula 3 championship in 1990, beating Mika Salo. He famously won the 1990 Macau Grand Prix's first heat before colliding with Schumacher in the second.

Formula One

Lotus (1991–1992)

Häkkinen debuted with Team Lotus in 1991, scoring his first points at Imola, and stayed through the team's decline in 1992 before joining McLaren as test driver for 1993.

McLaren (1993–2001)

When Michael Andretti left McLaren late in 1993, Häkkinen took the seat beside Ayrton Senna and immediately outqualified him at Estoril, scoring a podium in Japan. He became team leader after Senna's departure in 1994. In 1995 he survived a near-fatal crash in practice for the Australian Grand Prix at Adelaide — a tyre failure sent him into a wall, fracturing his skull; an emergency tracheotomy at the trackside saved his life, and he returned to racing the following season.

After years of rebuilding with Mercedes power, Häkkinen took his first win at the 1997 European Grand Prix at Jerez. In 1998, with the Adrian Newey-designed McLaren MP4/13, he won eight races and clinched the championship at the final round in Suzuka. He retained the title in 1999 in a tighter battle with Ferrari's Eddie Irvine, again sealing it at Suzuka. In 2000 he lost a close championship fight to Schumacher despite memorable victories, including an audacious pass on Schumacher at Spa-Francorchamps via the lapped Ricardo Zonta. His final win came at Indianapolis in 2001; he took a sabbatical that became retirement, with 20 wins from 161 starts.

Later career

Häkkinen raced in the DTM touring car series for Mercedes from 2005 to 2007, winning three races, and later made occasional GT appearances, winning a GT1 race at Zhuhai in 2013. He has worked as a driver manager, commentator, and road-safety ambassador in Finland.
