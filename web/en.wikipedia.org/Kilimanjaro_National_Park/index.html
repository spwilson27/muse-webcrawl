Kilimanjaro National Park

Kilimanjaro National Park is a Tanzanian national park located near Moshi in the Kilimanjaro Region. It covers an area of about 1,688 square kilometres (652 sq mi) surrounding Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest mountain in Africa (5,895 m / 19,341 ft) and the world's highest free-standing mountain.

History

The mountain and its forests were first protected as a game reserve and forest reserve in the early 20th century. Kilimanjaro National Park was established in 1973, initially covering the area above the treeline, and was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987. In 2005 the park was expanded to include the surrounding montane forest.

Wildlife and climbing

The park spans habitats from cultivated foothills and montane rainforest to heath, moorland, alpine desert and the summit glaciers, sheltering elephants, buffalo, leopards, colobus monkeys and many endemic plants. Tens of thousands of climbers attempt the summit (Uhuru Peak) each year via routes such as Marangu, Machame, Lemosho and Rongai; altitude sickness is the main hazard, and the glaciers have retreated dramatically since the early 20th century.
