The Prince's Trust

The Prince's Trust is a United Kingdom youth charity founded in 1976 by Charles, Prince of Wales (now Charles III), using his £7,400 severance pay from the Royal Navy. It helps disadvantaged young people aged 11 to 30 to build confidence, gain skills, and move into education, employment, or business. In 2024 the charity was renamed The King's Trust following its founder's accession to the throne.

Programmes

The Trust runs courses including the twelve-week Team programme of personal development, the Enterprise programme that provides loans, grants, and mentors for young people starting businesses, and shorter Get Into courses with employers in fields such as retail, construction, and healthcare. It reports having supported more than one million young people since its foundation.

Organisation and support

The charity is supported by celebrity ambassadors, corporate partners, and fundraising events, and works with schools, job centres, and community organisations across the UK. Its international arm, Prince's Trust International, extends its model to countries in the Commonwealth and beyond.
