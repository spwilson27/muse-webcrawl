Rama II is a science fiction novel by Arthur C. Clarke and Gentry Lee, published in 1989. It is the sequel to Clarke's 1973 novel Rendezvous with Rama and begins the later Rama series continued in The Garden of Rama and Rama Revealed.

Plot

Seventy years after the first Rama vessel passed through the Solar System, a second cylindrical alien craft arrives. A new international expedition is sent to explore it, but political intrigue, media interference, and the flaws of the human crew contrast sharply with the indifferent precision of the alien machine. The novel focuses heavily on the character Nicole des Jardins and the expedition's internal conflicts.

Reception

The book was a commercial success but received mixed reviews, with critics noting a shift from the hard science fiction of the original toward character-driven drama, reflecting Lee's larger role in the writing.
