Grand Central Madison is an underground Long Island Rail Road (LIRR) terminal station in Midtown Manhattan, New York City, located beneath Grand Central Terminal and Madison Avenue between 43rd and 48th Streets. It opened on 25 January 2023 as the terminal of the East Side Access project, the first expansion of the LIRR in over a century.

History

East Side Access was conceived in the 1960s to bring Long Island commuters to Manhattan's East Side, using the lower level of the 63rd Street Tunnel under the East River, whose construction began in 1969. The project was revived in the 1990s, received a federal full-funding grant agreement in 2006, and was initially projected to open in 2011 at a cost of about $4.3 billion; decades of delay and cost growth brought the final price to roughly $11–12 billion, making it among the most expensive rail projects per mile ever built. Full service to the new terminal began on 27 February 2023.

Design

The station occupies vast caverns mined beneath Park Avenue, with eight tracks (numbered 201–210) on two levels around four island platforms, and a 350,000-square-foot concourse about 150 feet below street level—said to be the largest railway concourse in the United States. Long banks of escalators, among the longest in New York, connect the platforms to the concourse and to Grand Central Terminal above. The name "Grand Central Madison" was announced by Governor Kathy Hochul in 2022.

Service

The terminal substantially increased LIRR capacity into Manhattan, adding trains to Penn Station as well by freeing capacity, and enabled new reverse-commute and intra-Queens services. Ridership built gradually after opening as schedules were adjusted in response to commuter feedback.
