Amazon Echo

Amazon Echo is a line of smart speakers developed by Amazon, first announced in November 2014. The devices connect to the Alexa voice assistant, allowing users to play audio, ask questions, and control smart-home equipment by voice.

ManufacturerAmazon
TypeSmart speaker
Released2014
AssistantAlexa

History

The original Echo, a tall cylinder with a seven-microphone array, was initially offered to Prime members by invitation. Its success helped create the smart-speaker market; the line expanded to include the smaller Echo Dot, the Echo Show with a display, and many other variants, and cumulative sales of Alexa-equipped devices reached into the hundreds of millions.
