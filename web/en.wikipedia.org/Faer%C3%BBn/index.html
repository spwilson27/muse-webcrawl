Faerûn

Faerûn (/feɪˈruːn/ fay-ROON) is a fictional continent, the primary setting of the Forgotten Realms campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons fantasy role-playing game. It first appeared in print in 1987 and was created largely by designer Ed Greenwood.

Geography

Faerûn lies on the planet Abeir-Toril. Its western coast borders the Sea of Swords and the Trackless Sea. Notable regions include the Sword Coast, home to the cities of Baldur's Gate, Neverwinter, and Waterdeep; the Dalelands in the east; the frozen north of Icewind Dale; and the desert of Anauroch near the empire of Netheril's ruins.

Nations and peoples

The continent is home to humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, and many other peoples. Major powers have included Cormyr, Amn, Thay, Rashemen, and the city-states of the Sword Coast. The Harpers, the Zhentarim, and other factions vie for influence across the continent.

In popular culture

Faerûn is the setting for numerous novels, including R. A. Salvatore's tales of Drizzt Do'Urden, and video games such as Baldur's Gate, Neverwinter Nights, and Baldur's Gate 3.
