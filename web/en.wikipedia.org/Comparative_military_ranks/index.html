Comparative military ranks

Comparative military ranks are tables and references that align the rank structures of different countries' armed forces so that equivalent grades can be identified across nations. Such comparisons are essential for multinational operations, protocol, and combined command.

NATO rank codes

The most widely used framework is the NATO rank code system defined in STANAG 2116. Officer ranks are coded OF-1 to OF-10 (from second lieutenant up to the highest field marshal/general of the army grades) and other ranks OR-1 to OR-9 (from private to sergeant major/warrant officer equivalents). NATO member and partner forces publish national rank titles against these codes, even though titles, insignia, and seniority customs differ widely.

Complications

Equivalence is approximate: some countries have grades with no counterpart elsewhere (such as the Soviet-era marshal ranks or the US five-star grades), naval and air force titles differ from army ones, and warrant officer systems vary greatly between states.
