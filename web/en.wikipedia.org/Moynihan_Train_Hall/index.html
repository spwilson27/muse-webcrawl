Moynihan Train Hall is an expansion of New York Penn Station within the James A. Farley Building, the former main post office, across Eighth Avenue from the existing station in Manhattan. It opened on January 1, 2021.

Background

The project restored a grand rail hall reminiscent of the original Pennsylvania Station, demolished in 1963–1966. Senator Daniel Patrick Moynihan championed converting the Farley Post Office into a station from the 1990s; the hall is named in his honour. The 255,000-square-foot concourse was designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill with a 92-foot-high skylight over the former mail sorting hall.

Services

The hall serves all Amtrak trains using Penn Station and Long Island Rail Road services (following the later opening of LIRR access), providing ticketing, waiting areas, retail and food halls, and relieving congestion in the underground Penn Station concourse.
