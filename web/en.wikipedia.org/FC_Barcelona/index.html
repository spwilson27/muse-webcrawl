FC Barcelona

Futbol Club Barcelona, commonly known as Barcelona and colloquially as Barça, is a professional football club based in Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain, that competes in La Liga, the top flight of Spanish football. Founded in 1899 by a group of Swiss, Catalan, German, and English footballers led by Joan Gamper, the club has become a symbol of Catalan culture and Catalanism, hence the motto "Més que un club" ("More than a club"). Unlike many other football clubs, the supporters own and operate Barcelona through a system of membership (socis).

History

Barcelona was founded after Joan Gamper placed an advertisement in Los Deportes in October 1899 seeking players. The club won its first trophy, the Copa Macaya, in 1902. Under coach Johan Cruyff, who had been a star player at the club in the 1970s, Barcelona's "Dream Team" won four consecutive league titles (1991–1994) and the club's first European Cup in 1992. The era of Pep Guardiola (2008–2012), with Lionel Messi, Xavi, and Andrés Iniesta developed at the La Masia academy, is widely regarded as one of the greatest in football history, producing two Champions League titles and a sextuple of trophies in 2009. Messi left in 2021 for Paris Saint-Germain amid a financial crisis; the club remained one of the world's most valuable and widely supported teams.

Honours

Barcelona has won a record number of Copa del Rey titles (over 30), numerous La Liga championships (more than 25), and five European Cup/UEFA Champions League titles (1992, 2006, 2009, 2011, 2015). Domestically and internationally it ranks among the most successful clubs in world football.

Rivalries

The club's fiercest rivalry is El Clásico against Real Madrid, a fixture laden with political and cultural symbolism contrasting Catalan and Spanish identities. The local derby against Espanyol is known as the Derbi Barceloní.

Identity

The club's colours are the blaugrana (blue and garnet) stripes, and its crest incorporates the red cross of Saint George and the Catalan senyera. Barcelona is one of three founding La Liga members never relegated, alongside Real Madrid and Athletic Bilbao, and historically declined shirt sponsorship, wearing UNICEF's logo (initially as a donation) before accepting commercial sponsors.
