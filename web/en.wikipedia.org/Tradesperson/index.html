Tradesperson

A tradesperson or tradesman is a skilled worker in a particular craft or trade, such as an electrician, plumber, carpenter, stonemason, welder, or mechanic. Tradespeople typically train through apprenticeships that combine on-the-job experience with classroom instruction, and in many jurisdictions they must be licensed or certified to practise.

Training

The traditional path runs from apprentice to journeyman to master craftsman. Modern systems include vocational schools, technical colleges, and union-run training programmes. Certification examinations often test both practical skill and knowledge of building codes and safety standards.

Role in the economy

Tradespeople build and maintain the built environment, infrastructure, and machinery. Many countries report persistent shortages of skilled trades workers, driven by retirements and lower enrolment in vocational training compared with university education.
