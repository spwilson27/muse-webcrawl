Public Citizen

Public Citizen is an American non-profit, progressive consumer rights advocacy group and think tank based in Washington, D.C. It was founded in 1971 by consumer advocate Ralph Nader.

History and work

Public Citizen grew out of Nader's network of advocacy organizations, sometimes called "Nader's Raiders". Its divisions work on issues including drug and auto safety, government ethics, campaign finance reform, trade policy, and corporate accountability. Its Health Research Group, long led by Sidney Wolfe, petitions for the removal of dangerous drugs from the market, while its Litigation Group argues public-interest cases before the U.S. Supreme Court and federal appeals courts. The organization also founded the U.S. Public Interest Research Group network's predecessors and helped spur the creation of the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.

Structure and funding

Public Citizen does not accept funding from corporations or governments, relying on individual members and foundations. Its longtime president Robert Weissman has led the organization since 2009; Nader left the board in 1980 to pursue broader activism.
