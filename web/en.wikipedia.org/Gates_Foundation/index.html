Gates Foundation
Founded: 2000
Headquarters: Seattle, Washington, U.S.
Focus: Global health, poverty reduction, education
Endowment: among the largest of any charitable foundation

The Gates Foundation, founded as the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, is an American private foundation established in 2000. It is one of the largest charitable foundations in the world, focusing on improving healthcare and reducing extreme poverty globally, and on expanding educational opportunities in the United States.

Programmes

The foundation's global health work supports vaccine delivery through Gavi, the Vaccine Alliance, and campaigns to eradicate polio and control malaria, tuberculosis, and HIV. Its development programmes fund agricultural research and financial services for the poor. In the United States, it has been a major funder of education reform and scholarships.

Structure and funding

The foundation was endowed by its founders and received a major pledge of Berkshire Hathaway stock from Warren Buffett in 2006. Following the founders' divorce in 2021, the foundation was renamed the Gates Foundation in 2024, and its governance was broadened with an expanded board of trustees.
