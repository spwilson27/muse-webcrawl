Alternative R&B

Alternative R&B (also called alt-R&B or PBR&B) is a genre of contemporary rhythm and blues that emerged in the late 2000s and 2010s, characterized by atmospheric production, electronic textures, and lyrical themes that depart from mainstream R&B conventions.

Characteristics

Alternative R&B artists often blend R&B vocals with elements of electronic music, hip hop, indie rock, and downtempo production. Lyrics frequently address introspection, relationships, and identity with a frankness less common in commercial R&B of earlier eras.

Artists

Artists associated with the genre include Frank Ocean, The Weeknd in his early work, FKA Twigs, Miguel, Janelle Monáe, Solange, and SZA. Frank Ocean's Channel Orange (2012) and Blonde (2016) are frequently cited as landmark releases.
