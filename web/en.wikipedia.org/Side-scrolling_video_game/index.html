Side-scrolling video game

Genre: Video game genre
Perspective: Side view, scrolling

A side-scrolling video game (or side-scroller) is a game in which the action is viewed from a side camera angle and the screen scrolls, usually horizontally, as the player moves. The genre was foundational to early arcade and home video games.

History

Early examples include Defender (1981) in arcades and platform games on home systems. Super Mario Bros. (1985) defined the side-scrolling platformer, while games such as Metroid and Castlevania added exploration, and beat 'em ups and run-and-gun shooters used the format extensively in the 1980s–90s.

Modern era

Three-dimensional graphics displaced side-scrollers commercially in the late 1990s, but independent developers revived the form in the 2000s–2010s with titles such as Braid, Limbo, Hollow Knight and Celeste.
