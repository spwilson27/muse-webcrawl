Computer forensics

Computer forensics, or digital forensics, is a branch of forensic science relating to the investigation and recovery of material found in digital devices, often in relation to computer crime.

Process

Investigators identify and seize devices, acquire bit-for-bit images using write blockers, verify them with cryptographic hashes, and analyze files, logs, and deleted data while maintaining a chain of custody so findings are admissible in court.

Branches

Subfields include disk and data forensics, network forensics, mobile device forensics, and malware forensics. Findings support criminal prosecutions, civil litigation, and incident response.
