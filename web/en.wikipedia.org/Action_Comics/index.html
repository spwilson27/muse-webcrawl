Action Comics

Action Comics is an American comic book series published by DC Comics. It is best known for introducing Superman in its first issue, cover-dated June 1938, an event widely regarded as the beginning of the superhero genre and the Golden Age of Comic Books. The series is one of the longest-running in American comics and reached its 1,000th issue in 2018.

History

Action Comics #1 featured Superman, created by writer Jerry Siegel and artist Joe Shuster, lifting a car over his head on its famous cover. The character's popularity quickly made him the anchor of the anthology title, which in its early years also carried features such as Zatara the Master Magician and Congo Bill. Over the decades the book introduced or developed characters including Lex Luthor, Lois Lane, Supergirl, and the Fortress of Solitude.

Significance and value

The series was relaunched with new numbering during DC's 2011 "New 52" initiative before returning to its original numbering in 2016. Original copies of Action Comics #1 are among the most valuable comic books in the world; a high-grade copy sold for millions of dollars at auction, reflecting its landmark status in popular culture.
