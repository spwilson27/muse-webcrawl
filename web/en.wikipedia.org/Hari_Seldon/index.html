Hari Seldon is a fictional character in Isaac Asimov's Foundation series of science fiction novels. A professor of mathematics on Trantor, capital of the Galactic Empire, he develops psychohistory, a science that predicts the statistical future of large populations.

Role in the series

Foreseeing that the Empire will fall and be followed by 30,000 years of barbarism, Seldon establishes the Foundation at the edge of the galaxy to shorten the dark age to a single millennium. After his death he appears in recorded holographic messages during successive "Seldon crises", guiding the Foundation as events unfold according to his Plan.

Background

The prequel novels Prelude to Foundation and Forward the Foundation recount Seldon's flight across Trantor, his service as First Minister, and the development of psychohistory with collaborators including Yugo Amaryl and his adopted son Raych. His wife Dors Venabili is revealed to be a robot.

Legacy

Seldon is often read as a partial self-portrait of Asimov. The character is portrayed by Jared Harris in the Apple TV+ series Foundation (2021–).
