AD 5 was a common year starting on Thursday of the Julian calendar, the fifth year of the Common Era and Anno Domini designations. In the Roman world it was known as the Year of the Consulship of Messalla and Cinna.

Events

Roman Empire

Rome acknowledges Cunobelinus, chief of the British Catuvellauni tribe, as king of Britain. The Roman general Tiberius continues his campaigns in Germania, advancing to the river Elbe. The future emperor's stepsons Germanicus and Claudius are prominent young members of the imperial household of Augustus, who was in the forty-ninth year of his rule.

Asia

In China, the Han dynasty under Emperor Pingdi continued, with Wang Mang serving as the powerful regent and acting emperor in all but name, consolidating power at court in the years before he founded the short-lived Xin dynasty.

Births

Paul the Apostle is traditionally estimated to have been born around this era (c. AD 5), in Tarsus in Cilicia. Other approximate births include Julia, daughter of Drusus Julius Caesar.
