TOPAZ nuclear reactor

TOPAZ is a Soviet thermionic nuclear reactor system designed to supply electrical power to spacecraft. The name is a Russian acronym for "Thermionic Experiment with Conversion in the Active Zone".

Design

Unlike the earlier BES-5 (Buk) reactors, which used thermoelectric conversion, TOPAZ converts heat directly to electricity using thermionic emitter cells built into the fuel elements, producing around 5–6 kilowatts of electrical power.

Flights

Two TOPAZ reactors flew aboard the Cosmos 1818 and Cosmos 1867 satellites in 1987, operating for periods of several months in high orbits, where the spent reactors remain as orbital debris.

Later history

In the early 1990s several TOPAZ units and related hardware were exhibited and studied in the United States under a technology demonstration programme.
