Nijisanji (Japanese: にじさんじ) is a Japanese virtual YouTuber (VTuber) agency operated by AnyColor Inc. (formerly Ichikara Inc.). Launched in early 2018, it was one of the first agencies to popularize VTubers who interact with audiences through live streaming using 2D animated avatars, originally through its own iPhone face-tracking application.

Unlike some competing agencies that emphasize idol-style performances, Nijisanji is known for a large and diverse roster and for streamers focused on casual conversation, gaming and improvisation. Its first wave included Tsukino Mito, who became one of the pioneering figures of VTuber culture.

Expansion

Nijisanji expanded internationally with branches in China (in partnership with bilibili), Indonesia and Korea, and launched the English-language branch NIJISANJI EN in 2021, whose groups such as LazuLight and Luxiem achieved large international audiences. AnyColor listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange in 2022, reflecting the scale of the VTuber industry. The agency has also organized live concerts, including performances at the Nippon Budokan.
