List of compositions by Johann Sebastian Bach

Johann Sebastian Bach (1685–1750) composed over 1,000 works, catalogued in the Bach-Werke-Verzeichnis (BWV) compiled by Wolfgang Schmieder, first published in 1950 and organised by genre rather than chronology.

Principal categories

  • BWV 1–224: Cantatas — over 200 surviving church cantatas plus secular cantatas (e.g. Coffee Cantata BWV 211, Peasant Cantata BWV 212)
  • BWV 225–231: Motets — including Singet dem Herrn, Jesu, meine Freude
  • BWV 232–242: Masses and Magnificat — Mass in B minor BWV 232, Magnificat BWV 243
  • BWV 243–249: Oratorios and Passions — St Matthew Passion BWV 244, St John Passion BWV 245, Christmas Oratorio BWV 248
  • BWV 525–771: Organ works — preludes, fugues, trio sonatas, Toccata and Fugue in D minor BWV 565
  • BWV 772–994: Keyboard works — Inventions and Sinfonias, The Well-Tempered Clavier BWV 846–893, English and French Suites, Partitas, Goldberg Variations BWV 988
  • BWV 995–1013: Lute and solo string works — Cello Suites BWV 1007–1012, Sonatas and Partitas for solo violin BWV 1001–1006
  • BWV 1014–1040: Chamber music
  • BWV 1041–1071: Concertos and orchestral suites — Violin Concertos, Brandenburg Concertos BWV 1046–1051, Orchestral Suites BWV 1066–1069
  • BWV 1072–1080: Canons and late contrapuntal works — Musical Offering BWV 1079, The Art of Fugue BWV 1080

Anhang (BWV Anh.) lists lost, doubtful and spurious works. Bach Digital provides modern scholarship and sources.
