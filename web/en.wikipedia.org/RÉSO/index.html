RÉSO, commonly called the Underground City (French: ville souterraine), is a network of interconnected office towers, shopping centres, hotels, museums, metro stations and other complexes beneath downtown Montreal, Quebec. The name is a pun on réseau, French for "network".

Overview

The network began with the opening of Place Ville Marie in 1962 and expanded with the construction of the Montreal Metro for Expo 67. Today it comprises roughly 32 kilometres of tunnels connecting dozens of buildings, thousands of shops and restaurants, and several metro, commuter rail and bus stations. Hundreds of thousands of people pass through it daily, especially during Montreal's cold winters, and it is regarded as one of the largest underground complexes in the world.
