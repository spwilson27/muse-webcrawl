Weekly Shōnen Jump

[Image: Weekly Shōnen Jump]

Weekly Shōnen Jump (Japanese: 週刊少年ジャンプ, Shūkan Shōnen Janpu) is a weekly shōnen manga anthology published in Japan by Shueisha under the Jump line of magazines. It is the best-selling manga magazine, as well as one of the longest-running, with the first issue released in July 1968. The manga series within the magazine target young teen males (shōnen) and generally consist of a large number of action scenes and a fair amount of comedy.

History

Weekly Shōnen Jump was launched by Shueisha in 1968 to compete with the popular Weekly Shōnen Magazine and Weekly Shōnen Sunday. At its peak in the mid-1990s, its circulation exceeded 6 million copies per issue, a record for a Japanese manga magazine. Many of the best-selling manga series of all time were serialized in its pages, including Dragon Ball, One Piece, Naruto, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Slam Dunk, Kochikame and Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba.

Features

The magazine famously uses reader survey cards to judge the popularity of its series; poorly ranked series risk cancellation, a system that has shaped the competitive character of the magazine. Its mascot is the Jump Pirate. Since 2013, an English-language digital edition has been published by Viz Media in North America, and in 2019 it switched to simultaneously publishing chapters free of charge through the Shonen Jump app and Manga Plus service.

Circulation

Although print circulation has declined from its 1990s peak alongside the wider magazine industry, the brand remains the flagship of shōnen manga publishing, with its titles anchoring global franchises in anime, film and merchandise.
