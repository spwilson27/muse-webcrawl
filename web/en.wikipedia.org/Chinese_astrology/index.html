Chinese astrology

Chinese astrology is a traditional system that assigns each year in a twelve-year cycle to an animal sign — Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig — combined with five elements and yin-yang in a 60-year cycle.

A person's sign is traditionally based on the birth year (and, in fuller Ba Zi charts, month, day and hour). The cycle is tied to the Chinese lunar calendar and remains central to Lunar New Year celebrations.
