Unified Command Plan

Unified Command Plan
Issued by: President of the United States
Prepared by: Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff
Establishes: Unified combatant commands

The Unified Command Plan (UCP) is a United States Department of Defense document, approved by the President, that establishes the missions, responsibilities, and geographic and functional areas of responsibility of the unified combatant commands. It is reviewed roughly every two years by the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff in consultation with the commanders and service chiefs.

Commands

The plan currently defines eleven combatant commands: six geographic — Northern, Southern, European, Indo-Pacific, Central, and African Command — and five functional — Space, Cyber, Transportation, Strategic, and Special Operations Command. Changes to the plan have marked major strategic shifts, such as the creation of Africa Command in 2007 and the re-establishment of Space Command in 2019.
