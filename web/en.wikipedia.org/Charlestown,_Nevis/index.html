Charlestown, Nevis

Charlestown is the capital of the island of Nevis in the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis in the Leeward Islands, West Indies. It is situated on the western, leeward coast of the island, south of Pinney's Beach.

History

The town grew around Fort Charles, built to protect the island's sugar trade in the 17th century. Charlestown is noted as the birthplace of Alexander Hamilton, the American statesman, and as a childhood home of Horatio Nelson, who married Frances Nisbet there in 1787.

Landmarks

Sights include the Museum of Nevis History, housed in Hamilton's birthplace, the Alexander Hamilton Museum, the Nevis Philatelic Bureau, and nearby Bath Hotel and Spring House, built in 1778 and among the first tourist hotels in the Caribbean.

Geography

The town lies beneath Nevis Peak and serves as the island's main port, with ferry connections to Basseterre on Saint Kitts.
