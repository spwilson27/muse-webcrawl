List of prime ministers of Canada

The prime minister of Canada is the head of government of Canada. This is a list of the prime ministers since Confederation in 1867. The first prime minister was Sir John A. Macdonald, and the longest-serving was William Lyon Mackenzie King, who held office for a total of over 21 years across three separate terms. The shortest term was that of Sir Charles Tupper, who served for 68 days in 1896.

Records

  • Longest total service: William Lyon Mackenzie King — 21 years, 154 days.
  • Longest single continuous term: Sir Wilfrid Laurier — 15 years, 86 days.
  • Shortest term: Sir Charles Tupper — 68 days.
  • First prime minister born in Canada: Sir John Abbott (the earlier prime ministers were born in Scotland or England).
  • Youngest prime minister: Joe Clark, who took office the day before his 40th birthday.
  • Only woman prime minister: Kim Campbell (1993).
