Second Ypres

The Ypres salient, 1915.

Second Ypres is a common short name for the Second Battle of Ypres, fought from 22 April to 25 May 1915 around the Belgian town of Ypres during the First World War. The battle saw the first large-scale use of poison gas on the Western Front, when German forces released chlorine gas against French and Canadian positions on 22 April 1915. Over the following month, fighting at Gravenstafel, St. Julien, Frezenberg, and Bellewaarde ridges reduced the Ypres salient but failed to break through to the Channel ports. The battle is closely associated with John McCrae's poem "In Flanders Fields" and with the baptism of fire of the 1st Canadian Division.
