DRDO

Defence Research and Development Organisation
Formed: 1958
Headquarters: New Delhi, India
Parent agency: Ministry of Defence, Government of India
Motto: "Strength's Origin is in Science"

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) is the premier agency under the Department of Defence Research and Development in the Ministry of Defence of the Government of India. It is charged with the military's research and development and is headquartered in Delhi, India. It was formed in 1958 by the merger of the Technical Development Establishment and the Directorate of Technical Development and Production of the Indian Ordnance Factories with the Defence Science Organisation.

History

The DRDO was established in 1958 by amalgamating the Defence Science Organisation with several technical development establishments. It started with just 10 laboratories, and over the decades has grown into a network of around 50 laboratories across the country working in areas such as aeronautics, armaments, electronics, land combat engineering, life sciences, materials, missiles, and naval systems.

Programmes

DRDO has developed a wide range of systems for the Indian Armed Forces, including the Agni and Prithvi series of ballistic missiles under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, the Arjun main battle tank, the Tejas light combat aircraft programme in partnership with other agencies, the Pinaka multi-barrel rocket launcher, and various radar, electronic warfare, and sonar systems. It has also worked on unmanned aerial vehicles such as the Rustom series.

Organisation

The organisation is headed by the Director General of DRDO, who also serves as the Secretary of the Department of Defence Research and Development and as Scientific Adviser to the Defence Minister. Its laboratories are grouped into technology clusters covering different domains.
