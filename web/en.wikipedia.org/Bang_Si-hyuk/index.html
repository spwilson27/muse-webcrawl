Bang Si-hyuk

Bang Si-hyuk (born August 9, 1972), known professionally as "Hitman" Bang, is a South Korean record producer, songwriter, and record executive. He is the founder of Big Hit Music (formerly Big Hit Entertainment) and the chairman of Hybe Corporation, and is best known as the creator of the boy band BTS.

Career

Bang began his career as a composer and producer at JYP Entertainment, working with artists such as Rain and 2AM. In 2005 he founded Big Hit Entertainment. The company achieved global success with BTS, which debuted in 2013 and became one of the best-selling music acts in the world. In 2021 the company rebranded as Hybe Corporation and expanded through acquisitions, including Ithaca Holdings.

Recognition

Bang has appeared on international rich lists as one of South Korea's wealthiest entertainment figures following Hybe's 2020 stock market listing, and he has been named to Billboard's Power 100 and Time's list of influential figures in music.
