Michel Gondry (born 8 May 1963) is a French film director, screenwriter, and producer celebrated for his inventive visual style, much of it developed in music videos and advertising. He won an Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay for Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), written with Charlie Kaufman and Pierre Bismuth.

Early life and music videos

Gondry was born in Versailles and studied graphic design in Paris. As drummer of the band Oui Oui he began directing the group's videos, which led to acclaimed work for Björk, beginning with "Human Behaviour" (1993). He went on to direct landmark videos for Daft Punk ("Around the World"), The White Stripes ("Fell in Love with a Girl", shot in LEGO), The Chemical Brothers, Radiohead, Beck, Kylie Minogue and Paul McCartney, often achieving surreal effects through in-camera tricks rather than digital post-production.

Film career

Gondry's feature debut was Human Nature (2001), written by Kaufman. Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), starring Jim Carrey and Kate Winslet, became a critical landmark. He followed with The Science of Sleep (2006), the ensemble musical documentary Dave Chappelle's Block Party (2005), Be Kind Rewind (2008) — which popularised the term "sweding" for homemade remakes — The Green Hornet (2011), The We and the I (2012), Mood Indigo (2013), adapted from Boris Vian, and Microbe & Gasoline (2015). He also directed episodes of television, including Kidding with Jim Carrey.

Style

Gondry is known for handcrafted, analog effects, forced perspective, stop-motion textures and dream logic, frequently exploring memory, childhood and the boundary between imagination and reality.
