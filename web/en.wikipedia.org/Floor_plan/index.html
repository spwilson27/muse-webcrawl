Floor plan

In architecture and building engineering, a floor plan is a technical drawing to scale, showing a view from above, of the relationships between rooms, spaces, traffic patterns, and other physical features at one level of a structure. Dimensions are usually drawn between the walls to specify room sizes and wall lengths. Floor plans may also include details of fixtures like sinks, water heaters, furnaces, and so on, and notes about construction.

Overview

A floor plan is an orthographic projection of a horizontal section through a building, conventionally cut at a height of about 1.2 metres above the floor. It shows walls as thick lines, door swings, window openings, stairs, and often furniture. Similar drawings include reflected ceiling plans and structural plans.

Plan arrangement concepts

Designers describe plans in terms of the relationships between spaces: the open plan, in which few partitions divide a large area; the enfilade, a suite of rooms aligned with each other; and arrangements around a central hall or courtyard. In residential design the floor plan is a key selling document, widely used in real-estate listings.
