Balance of performance

Balance of performance (BoP) is a system used in motorsport to equalize the performance of competing cars of different designs, principally in GT and touring car racing. Sanctioning bodies adjust parameters such as minimum weight, engine power, fuel capacity, and aerodynamics so that dissimilar cars can race competitively.

Application

BoP is a defining feature of classes such as LM GTE, GT3, and the Hypercar class of the FIA World Endurance Championship, where adjustments are made per event or per circuit based on measured performance data.

Criticism

Teams and manufacturers sometimes accuse regulators of favoring rivals, and competitors may "sandbag" in qualifying or practice to earn favorable adjustments — a persistent controversy in BoP-managed series.
