Acorn squash
Species: Cucurbita pepo
Type: Winter squash
Also called: Pepper squash, Des Moines squash

Acorn squash (Cucurbita pepo var. turbinata), also called pepper squash or Des Moines squash, is a winter squash with distinctive longitudinal ridges and sweet, yellow-orange flesh.

Description

The fruit is typically dark green (sometimes golden or white in newer varieties), shaped like an acorn, and about 10–20 cm long. Unlike summer squash, it is harvested when the rind has hardened, giving it a long storage life.

Cultivation and history

Acorn squash was cultivated by Native peoples of North America and was introduced to European settlers. It is grown as an annual vine and harvested in autumn.

Culinary use

It is most commonly baked, roasted, steamed, or sautéed, often halved and filled, or sweetened with butter and brown sugar. The seeds can be roasted like pumpkin seeds.
