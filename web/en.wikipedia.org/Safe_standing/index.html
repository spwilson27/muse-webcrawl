Safe standing

Safe standing is a term for areas of sports stadiums designed to allow spectators to stand safely to watch events, principally association football. Modern safe standing designs — most commonly "rail seating", in which each row has a waist-high barrier and a flip-up seat — are intended to eliminate the dangers of traditional terraces and of persistent standing in seated areas.

Background

Standing terraces were the norm in British football grounds until the Hillsborough disaster of 1989, in which 97 Liverpool supporters were fatally crushed. The Taylor Report that followed recommended all-seater stadiums in the top divisions, and standing accommodation was banned in England's top two tiers. However, many supporters continued to stand in front of their seats, creating risks of falls and surges with no barriers to break them up, and supporter groups campaigned for purpose-built standing areas, pointing to Germany, where standing never disappeared and grounds such as Borussia Dortmund's Westfalenstadion hold tens of thousands of standing fans behind rails.

Designs

In rail seating, each spectator has an allocated space with a seat that can be locked upright for standing matches or folded down where seated viewing is required (for example, under UEFA rules for European competitions). Every row has its own barrier, preventing progressive crowd collapse. Alternative designs include seats with independent barriers and convertible "dual-use" sections.

Adoption

Celtic became the first British club to install rail seating, opening a 2,600-capacity safe standing section at Celtic Park in 2016 under Scottish rules. In England, after years of trials and research by the Sports Grounds Safety Authority, the government lifted the all-seater requirement in 2022: several Premier League and Championship clubs, including Chelsea, Manchester United, Manchester City, Tottenham Hotspur and Cardiff City, introduced licensed standing areas. Safe standing is also common in Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, and in North America's Major League Soccer, where purpose-built standing sections house supporters' groups.
