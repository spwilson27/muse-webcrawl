San Juan County is a county in southwestern Colorado. As of the 2020 census its population was 705, making it the least populous county in the state. Its county seat and only incorporated municipality is Silverton.

Geography

The county lies entirely within the San Juan Mountains and has the highest mean elevation of any county in the United States, at about 3,426 metres (11,240 feet). The Animas River flows through it, and the Durango and Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad serves Silverton.

History

San Juan County was created in 1876 during Colorado's mining boom. Gold and silver mining around Silverton and settlements such as Eureka and Animas Forks drove its early economy; today tourism and recreation predominate.
