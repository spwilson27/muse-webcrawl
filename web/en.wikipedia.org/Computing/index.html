Computing

Computing is any goal-oriented activity requiring, benefiting from, or creating computing machinery. It includes the study and experimentation of algorithmic processes and the development of hardware and software.

History

Computing predates electronic computers, encompassing devices such as the abacus and mechanical calculators, the analytical engine designs of Charles Babbage, and the human computers who performed calculations by hand.

Fields

Subfields include computer science, information technology, software engineering, computer engineering, and information systems. Computing now underpins communication, commerce, science, and entertainment worldwide.
