Charlie Kaufman

Charles Stuart Kaufman (born 19 November 1958) is an American screenwriter, film director and novelist, widely regarded as one of the most inventive screenwriters of his generation.

Career

Kaufman began writing for television comedy, including Get a Life and The Dana Carvey Show. His breakthrough screenplay was Being John Malkovich (1999), followed by Adaptation (2002) and Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), for which he won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.

Directorial work

He made his directorial debut with Synecdoche, New York (2008), and later directed the stop-motion Anomalisa (2015) and I'm Thinking of Ending Things (2020). His debut novel, Antkind, appeared in 2020.

Style

His work features self-reflexive narratives, unreliable protagonists, and explorations of identity, memory and mortality.
