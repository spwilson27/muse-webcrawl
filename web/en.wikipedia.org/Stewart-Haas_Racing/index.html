Stewart-Haas Racing was an American professional stock car racing team that competed in NASCAR from 2002 until the end of the 2024 season. Co-owned by three-time Cup Series champion Tony Stewart and machine-tool magnate Gene Haas, the team was based in Kannapolis, North Carolina, and fielded Ford Mustangs in the NASCAR Cup Series.

History

Gene Haas entered NASCAR in 2002 as Haas CNC Racing, with middling results. In 2009 Stewart joined as co-owner and driver, transforming the organisation: Stewart won the 2011 Cup Series championship in a famous tie-breaker with Carl Edwards — decided on race wins after both finished level on points — giving the team its first title. Kevin Harvick joined in 2014 and won the championship in his first season with the team. Other drivers included Ryan Newman, Danica Patrick, Kurt Busch (who won the 2017 Daytona 500 with the team), Clint Bowyer and Aric Almirola. The team also won Xfinity Series races and the 2023 Xfinity championship with Cole Custer.

Closure

In May 2024 Stewart and Haas announced the team would shut down at the end of the season; its four charters were sold to other teams, with Haas retaining one charter to compete in 2025 as the Haas Factory Team.
