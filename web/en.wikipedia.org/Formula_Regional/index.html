Formula Regional

Formula Regional
Category: Open-wheel single-seater (regional)
Introduced: 2018
Position: Between Formula 4 and Formula 3
Key championships: European, Americas, Asian, Oceania
Chassis: Tatuus (most series)

Formula Regional (officially Formula Regional Championships) is a category of single-seater racing created by the FIA in 2018 as a step between Formula 4 and Formula 3 in the Global Pathway from Karting to Formula One.

Concept

The category consolidates the level previously occupied by diverse national Formula Renault 2.0 and regional F3 series, offering standardized, cost-controlled cars and FIA Super Licence points.

Championships

The flagship Formula Regional European Championship (FRECA, merged with the Formula Renault Eurocup in 2021) is joined by Formula Regional championships in the Americas, Asia (later Middle East), Oceania (Toyota Racing Series lineage), Japan, and India.

Specifications

Most series use the Tatuus F3 T-318 chassis (or its successors) with turbocharged engines of around 270 hp from suppliers such as Alpine/Renault, Honda, Toyota, or Alfa Romeo.
