Corse-du-Sud

Corse-du-Sud (South Corsica) is a former department of France, numbered 2A, covering the southern part of the island of Corsica. Its prefecture was Ajaccio, the birthplace of Napoleon Bonaparte.

Geography

The territory includes the Gulf of Ajaccio, the Gulf of Valinco, the Alta Rocca mountains and the port town of Bonifacio, whose white limestone cliffs face Sardinia across the strait.

History

Created in 1975 when the single Corsica department was split, Corse-du-Sud ceased to be a departmental council in 2018 when Corsica became a single territorial collectivity, though 2A remains in postal and statistical use.
