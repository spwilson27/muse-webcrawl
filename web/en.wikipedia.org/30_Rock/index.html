30 Rock is an American satirical sitcom television series created by Tina Fey, which aired on NBC from October 11, 2006, to January 31, 2013. Loosely based on Fey's experiences as head writer for Saturday Night Live, the series takes place behind the scenes of a fictional live sketch comedy show, TGS with Tracy Jordan, produced at 30 Rockefeller Plaza in New York City.

Plot and characters

Fey stars as Liz Lemon, the show's harried head writer and showrunner, who must manage her eccentric stars Tracy Jordan (Tracy Morgan) and Jenna Maroney (Jane Krakowski), her naive page Kenneth Parcell (Jack McBrayer), and her demanding but oddly supportive boss, network executive Jack Donaghy (Alec Baldwin). The series is known for its rapid-fire jokes, surreal cutaway gags, and satire of the entertainment industry and corporate culture.

Reception and legacy

Despite modest ratings, 30 Rock was a major critical success, winning the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series three consecutive times (2007–2009) and 16 Emmys overall. It is frequently ranked among the greatest television series of all time and launched catchphrases such as "Blerg" and "I want to go to there." The cast reunited for a one-off special episode in 2020.
