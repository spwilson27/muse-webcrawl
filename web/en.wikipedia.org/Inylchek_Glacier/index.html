Inylchek Glacier

The Inylchek Glacier (Engilchek) is a large glacier in the Tian Shan mountains of Kyrgyzstan, near the borders with Kazakhstan and China. It is one of the largest non-polar glaciers in the world, about 60 kilometres long.

Geography

The glacier has northern and southern branches flowing from the massifs of Khan Tengri (7,010 m) and Jengish Chokusu (Victory Peak, 7,439 m). Meltwater feeds Lake Merzbacher, an ice-dammed lake that periodically drains.

Mountaineering

Base camps on the glacier serve expeditions to Khan Tengri and surrounding peaks.
