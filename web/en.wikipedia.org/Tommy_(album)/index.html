Tommy (album)

Tommy may refer to several albums in music. The best known is Tommy, the 1969 rock opera by the Who. The name has also been used as an album title by other artists, and this page serves as a disambiguation entry for albums titled Tommy.

Albums titled Tommy

Chief among them is the Who's double album of 1969, written mainly by Pete Townshend, which tells the story of a deaf, dumb and blind boy who becomes a pinball champion. Albums sharing the title by other performers are generally listed in discographies under the artist's name with the year of release to distinguish them.
