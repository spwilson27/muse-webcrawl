Crabeater seal

The crabeater seal (Lobodon carcinophaga) is a true seal with a circumpolar distribution around Antarctica. Despite its name, it feeds mainly on Antarctic krill, not crabs.

Description

Adults reach about 2.6 m and 200–300 kg. Their specially adapted lobed teeth strain krill from the water. They are thought to be the most abundant large mammal species by biomass after humans, with population estimates in the millions, though difficult to census.

Ecology

They live on pack ice, are preyed on by leopard seals and orcas, and are the principal prey species for leopard seals.
