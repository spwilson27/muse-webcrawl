Bernie Sanders 2020 presidential campaign

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The 2020 presidential campaign of Bernie Sanders was the second bid by the United States senator from Vermont for the Democratic Party's nomination for President. Sanders announced his candidacy on February 19, 2019, raising a record $5.9 million from some 225,000 donors in the first 24 hours.

Platform and campaign

Building on his 2016 run, Sanders campaigned on Medicare for All, a Green New Deal, cancellation of student debt, tuition-free public college, a federal jobs guarantee and expanded labour rights. The campaign assembled a large multiracial, working-class coalition and a formidable small-donor fundraising operation, while the slogan "Not me. Us." emphasized collective movement politics.

Primary results and suspension

Sanders effectively tied the Iowa caucuses, won the New Hampshire primary and won Nevada by a wide margin, briefly becoming the clear frontrunner. After Joe Biden's decisive win in South Carolina and consolidation of moderate support on Super Tuesday, Biden built an insurmountable delegate lead. Sanders won several later contests, including California, but suspended his campaign on April 8, 2020, and endorsed Biden five days later, pledging to work with him to defeat President Donald Trump.
