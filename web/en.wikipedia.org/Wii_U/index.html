Wii U

The Wii U is a home video game console developed by Nintendo and released in 2012 as the successor to the Wii. Its defining feature is the Wii U GamePad, a controller with a built-in touchscreen that can display the game itself or supplement the television image.

Hardware and software

The console supported high-definition graphics, backward compatibility with Wii games and controllers, online services through Nintendo Network, and the Miiverse social platform. Notable games included Mario Kart 8, Super Mario 3D World, Splatoon and The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, the last also launching on Switch.

Reception and legacy

Confusing marketing and limited third-party support led to modest sales of about 13.5 million units. Production ended in 2017, but many Wii U games found larger audiences as Nintendo Switch ports.
