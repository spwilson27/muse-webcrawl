Gautam Gambhir (born 14 October 1981) is an Indian former international cricketer, politician and cricket coach. A left-handed opening batter, he played all formats of the game for India between 2003 and 2016 and was one of the most successful Indian openers of his era, particularly in match-winning performances in world tournament finals.

International career

Gambhir made his One Day International debut against Bangladesh in 2003 and his Test debut against Australia in 2004. He became India's first-choice Test opener alongside Virender Sehwag, enjoying a prolific run in 2008–2010 that included five centuries in consecutive Test matches and the ICC Test Player of the Year award in 2009, when he also reached No. 1 in the ICC Test batting rankings.

His most celebrated innings came in World Cup finals: he top-scored with 75 in the final of the 2007 ICC World Twenty20 against Pakistan, and scored 97 in the 2011 Cricket World Cup final against Sri Lanka, anchoring India's successful chase in Mumbai.

Domestic and league career

Gambhir represented Delhi in domestic cricket. In the Indian Premier League he captained the Kolkata Knight Riders to titles in 2012 and 2014, after an earlier stint with the Delhi Daredevils, whom he also captained on his return in 2018, his final season.

Politics and coaching

After retiring from all forms of cricket in 2018, Gambhir joined the Bharatiya Janata Party and was elected Member of Parliament for East Delhi in 2019; he did not contest the 2024 election. He received the Padma Shri in 2019. As a coach and mentor, he guided Kolkata Knight Riders to the 2024 IPL title as team mentor, and in July 2024 he was appointed head coach of the Indian men's national team, succeeding Rahul Dravid.
