Trinity College of Music

Trinity College of Music was one of the oldest music conservatoires in London, founded in 1872. In 2005 it merged with the Laban Dance Centre to form Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance. The college was based at King Charles Court of the Old Royal Naval College in Greenwich.

History

The institution began as the Church Choral Society and grew into Trinity College London, which developed both a teaching college and an international examinations board. The music college trained performers and composers and was known for its diplomas and for introducing graded music examinations, a model widely imitated. Trinity College London continues as an independent exam board, while the conservatoire's degree programmes are delivered by Trinity Laban.
