Almatti Dam

The Almatti Dam, officially the Lal Bahadur Shastri Dam, is a hydroelectric and irrigation project on the Krishna River in Vijayapura district, Karnataka, India. Completed in 2005, it is the main reservoir of the Upper Krishna Irrigation Project and supplies irrigation water to drought-prone districts of northern Karnataka as well as generating electricity.

Details

The dam is about 1,565 metres long and up to about 49 metres high, impounding a reservoir with a gross storage of roughly 123 thousand million cubic feet (TMC) at its full reservoir level of 519.6 metres. Its powerhouse has an installed capacity of about 290 MW. The full height of the dam was a long-running point of dispute with the neighbouring states of Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh, and the permissible reservoir level was a central issue before the Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal.

Tourism

The gardens developed around the dam site — including rock gardens and musical fountains — have made Almatti a popular tourist attraction in the region, alongside the nearby historic city of Vijayapura (Bijapur).
