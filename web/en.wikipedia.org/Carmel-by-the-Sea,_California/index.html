Carmel-by-the-Sea, California

Carmel-by-the-Sea, commonly known simply as Carmel, is a city in Monterey County, California, situated on the Monterey Peninsula. Founded in 1902 and incorporated in 1916, it is known for its natural scenery, white-sand beach, historic mission, and rich artistic history.

Character

The one-square-mile village has distinctive local ordinances — no street addresses or street lights in the residential area, no chain restaurants, and permits famously once required to wear high heels. The actor Clint Eastwood served as mayor from 1986 to 1988. Mission San Carlos Borromeo de Carmelo, founded in 1770, is a major landmark.

Arts colony

After the 1906 San Francisco earthquake, artists, writers and musicians flocked to Carmel, including photographer Edward Weston and poet Robinson Jeffers, whose Tor House still stands. The town retains numerous galleries and hosts the Carmel Bach Festival.
