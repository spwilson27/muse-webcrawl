Consumers International

Consumers International is the international federation of consumer organisations. Founded in 1960 as the International Organisation of Consumers Unions, it brings together over 200 member organisations in more than 100 countries and works to promote consumer rights worldwide. Its head office is in London.

History

The organisation was founded in 1960 by national consumer groups from the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Belgium and the Netherlands. It played a key role in securing the United Nations Guidelines for Consumer Protection (1985) and in establishing the eight basic consumer rights, building on the four rights articulated by U.S. President John F. Kennedy in 1962.

Activities

Consumers International campaigns on issues including food safety, financial services, digital rights and privacy, product safety, sustainable consumption, and access to essential services. It coordinates World Consumer Rights Day, observed annually on 15 March, and supports consumer groups in developing countries through research, training and advocacy.
