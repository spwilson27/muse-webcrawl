Aditya Chopra

Aditya Chopra (born 21 May 1971) is an Indian film director, producer, screenwriter and studio executive. He is the chairman and managing director of Yash Raj Films, one of India's most successful film production companies, founded by his father Yash Chopra.

Career

Chopra began assisting his father on films such as Chandni and Lamhe. He made his directorial debut at age 23 with Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995), one of the highest-grossing and longest-running Indian films of all time. He followed it with Mohabbatein (2000) and Rab Ne Bana Di Jodi (2008).

Producer

As producer he expanded Yash Raj Films into a modern studio, backing films in the Dhoom, Ek Tha Tiger and Pathaan franchises, and launching YRF Spy Universe. See also Yash Raj Films and Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge.

Personal life

He was previously married to Payal Khanna; in 2014 he married actress Rani Mukerji. The couple have a daughter, Adira.
