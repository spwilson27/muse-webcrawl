Flight instructor

A flight instructor is a pilot qualified to teach others to fly aircraft. Instructors give dual instruction in the air and on the ground, supervise student solo flights, and endorse students for examinations and practical tests.

Certification

In the United States, the Federal Aviation Administration issues the Certified Flight Instructor (CFI) certificate, with additional ratings for instrument instruction (CFII) and multi-engine instruction (MEI). Applicants must hold at least a commercial pilot certificate, pass knowledge and practical tests, and demonstrate the ability to teach aeronautical subjects. In Europe, flight instructor certificates are issued under EASA Part-FCL rules.

Role in pilot training

Many professional pilots begin their careers as instructors, building flight hours while teaching. Instructors are responsible for judging when a student is ready to solo, and they remain a common source of recurrent training, flight reviews, and endorsements throughout a pilot's career.
