List of monarchs of Cambodia

The monarchy of Cambodia traces its origins through the kingdoms of Funan and Chenla to the Khmer Empire founded at Angkor in 802. The following is a summary list of the principal rulers of Cambodia across its major periods; dates for early rulers are approximate and partly legendary.

Post-Angkor and middle period (1431–1863)

After the fall of Angkor the capital moved to Phnom Penh, Longvek and Oudong. Kings such as Ponhea Yat (r. 1431–1463), Ang Chan I (r. 1516–1566) and Chey Chettha II (r. 1618–1628) ruled a shrinking kingdom pressed between Siam and Vietnam. Longvek fell to Siam in 1594; Oudong became the royal seat in the 17th century. Ang Duong (r. 1848–1860) worked to reform and preserve the kingdom.

The monarchy was abolished in 1970 with the Khmer Republic, and Cambodia endured the Khmer Rouge period and civil war before the monarchy was restored in 1993 under the constitution, with the king reigning as a constitutional monarch chosen by the Royal Council of the Throne.
