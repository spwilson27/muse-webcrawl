ASEAN

Association of Southeast Asian Nations
Founded: 8 August 1967 (Bangkok Declaration)
Headquarters: Jakarta, Indonesia
Members: 10 (11 with Timor-Leste admitted in principle)
Combined population: approx. 680 million

The Association of Southeast Asian Nations, commonly abbreviated as ASEAN, is a regional intergovernmental organisation comprising ten Southeast Asian countries: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam. It was established on 8 August 1967 in Bangkok, Thailand, with the signing of the ASEAN Declaration (Bangkok Declaration) by the five founding members — Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand.

Aims and structure

ASEAN's stated aims include accelerating economic growth, social progress and cultural development in the region, promoting regional peace and stability, and providing a forum for resolving intra-regional disputes peacefully. Decisions are traditionally taken by consensus ("the ASEAN Way"), with an emphasis on non-interference in members' internal affairs. The organisation is headed by a Secretary-General based at the ASEAN Secretariat in Jakarta, and member states rotate the chairmanship annually, hosting the ASEAN Summit.

Expansion and Cambodia

Brunei joined in 1984, Vietnam in 1995, Laos and Myanmar in 1997. Cambodia was admitted on 30 April 1999, becoming the tenth member; its accession had been delayed by the political turmoil of 1997. Timor-Leste was admitted in principle in 2022 and granted observer status on a roadmap to full membership. The ASEAN Economic Community, launched in 2015, aims at regional economic integration, and ASEAN sits at the centre of wider frameworks such as ASEAN+3, the East Asia Summit and the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP).

Population and economy

Together the member states cover about 4.5 million km² with a combined population of roughly 680 million people and a combined nominal GDP of several trillion US dollars, making ASEAN collectively one of the world's largest economies.
