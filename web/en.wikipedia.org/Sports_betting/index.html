Sports betting

Sports betting is the activity of predicting sporting results and placing a wager on the outcome. It is a form of gambling that takes place in many jurisdictions, both through licensed bookmakers and online betting exchanges. Sports on which bets are commonly placed include association football, American football, basketball, baseball, horse racing, greyhound racing, boxing and mixed martial arts.

Types of bets

The most straightforward wager is a bet on which team or competitor will win a contest outright. Other common bet types include point spreads, in which a bookmaker handicaps the favourite; totals (over/under) bets on the combined score; parlays combining multiple outcomes; prop bets on occurrences within a game unrelated to the final result; and futures bets on results decided later in a season, such as a championship winner.

Odds and bookmaking

Bookmakers set odds intended to balance action on both sides of a contest so that the bookmaker profits regardless of the outcome, a margin known as the overround or vigorish. Odds may be expressed in fractional, decimal, or moneyline formats. Betting exchanges allow customers to bet against each other rather than against a bookmaker.

Legality

The legal status of sports betting varies widely by country and region. In the United States, a federal law known as PASPA restricted sports betting from 1992 until the Supreme Court struck it down in Murphy v. NCAA in 2018, after which many states legalised and regulated the practice.
