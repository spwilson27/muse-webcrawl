The Navy Midshipmen football team represents the United States Naval Academy in college football. Navy is known for its triple-option offence and for its storied annual rivalry with Army, first played in 1890.

History

Navy played its first football game in 1879. The Midshipmen won the national championship in 1926, and running back Joe Bellino won the Heisman Trophy in 1960; Roger Staubach, later a Pro Football Hall of Fame quarterback, won the Heisman in 1963. The team competes for the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy against Army and Air Force.

Home stadium

Home games are played at Navy–Marine Corps Memorial Stadium in Annapolis, Maryland, which opened in 1959.
