National Museum of Scotland

The National Museum of Scotland in Edinburgh is part of National Museums Scotland. It was formed in 2006 from the merger of the Museum of Scotland, which opened in 1998 and covers Scottish history and culture, and the adjacent Royal Museum, a Victorian building dating from 1861 with collections of science, technology, natural history, art and world cultures.

Buildings

The Royal Museum building on Chambers Street is noted for its soaring glass-roofed Grand Gallery, modelled on London's Crystal Palace. The modern Museum of Scotland building, opened in 1998, is clad in golden Moray sandstone. A major redevelopment completed in 2011, with further galleries in 2016 and 2019, created new displays on science, fashion, art and design.

Collection highlights

Famous exhibits include Dolly the sheep — the first mammal cloned from an adult cell — the Lewis chessmen (shared with the British Museum), the medieval Monymusk reliquary, an articulated sperm whale skeleton, and the Millennium Clock. The museum is the most visited attraction in Scotland outside London's national museums.
