Zamalek SC

Zamalek Sporting Club (Arabic: نادي الزمالك الرياضي), commonly referred to as Zamalek, is an Egyptian sports club based in Giza, Greater Cairo. Founded on 5 January 1911, it is one of the most successful football clubs in Egypt and Africa, and contests one of world football's fiercest rivalries, the Cairo derby, with Al Ahly SC.

History

The club was founded in 1911 as a social and sports club open to Egyptians as well as foreigners, initially under the name Kasr El Nile Club, and was led in its early years by the Belgian lawyer George Merzbach. It moved through several names — the Mixed Club, King Farouk Club — before adopting the name Zamalek, after the Cairo district where it was long based, following the 1952 Egyptian revolution.

Zamalek has won the Egyptian Premier League more than a dozen times and the Egypt Cup over two dozen times. In African competition it has won the CAF Champions League five times (1984, 1986, 1993, 1996, and 2002) as well as the CAF Confederation Cup in 2019 and 2024. Zamalek was named the top African club of the 20th century by some measures of continental titles in February 2003, reflecting its nine African trophies at that point.

Rivalry and identity

The Cairo derby against Al Ahly is one of the most intense fixtures in African football; Zamalek supporters style themselves the "White Knights" after the club's white kit with its two red stripes. The club also fields leading teams in handball, basketball, and volleyball, and its handball team is among the most successful in Africa.

Honours

  • Egyptian Premier League: 14 titles (most recently 2021–22)
  • Egypt Cup: 28 titles
  • CAF Champions League: 5 titles
  • CAF Confederation Cup: 2 titles (2019, 2024)
  • CAF Super Cup: multiple titles
