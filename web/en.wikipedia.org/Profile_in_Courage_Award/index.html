Profile in Courage Award

The Profile in Courage Award is a private award created by the Kennedy family to recognize displays of political courage similar to those John F. Kennedy originally described in his book of the same name. It is given to individuals (often elected officials) who, by acting in accord with their conscience, risked their careers or lives.

History

The award was established in 1989 and is administered by the John F. Kennedy Library Foundation, which also maintains the award's presentation each May at the Kennedy Library in Boston.

Recipients

Notable recipients include John McCain, Gerald Ford, Hilda Solis, Gabrielle Giffords, Mitt Romney and, in 2022, five Ukrainian and international honorees including Volodymyr Zelenskyy.
