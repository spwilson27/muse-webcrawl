Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6

The Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6 is a turboprop aircraft engine produced by Pratt & Whitney Canada. The PT6A is the best known version, a very popular turboprop design used in a wide range of aircraft including the Beechcraft King Air, Cessna Caravan, de Havilland Canada DHC-6 Twin Otter and Pilatus PC-12.

Design

The PT6 is a free-turbine turboshaft design with a reverse-flow configuration: air enters at the rear and exhausts near the front, a layout that simplifies maintenance. Output ranges from roughly 500 to 2,000 shaft horsepower across variants. First run in 1960 and certified in 1963, the engine family has become renowned for its reliability.

Applications

More than 50,000 PT6 engines have been delivered, powering over 100 different aircraft types.
