McLaren Automotive

McLaren Automotive is a British luxury automotive manufacturer based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, Surrey, England. Founded in 1985 as McLaren Cars and relaunched in 2010, it is part of the McLaren Group alongside the McLaren Formula One team, founded by Bruce McLaren.

History

The company's first car was the legendary McLaren F1 (1992), a three-seat supercar with a gold-lined engine bay and a BMW V12, long the world's fastest production car. After collaborating with Mercedes-Benz on the SLR McLaren, McLaren Automotive launched the MP4-12C in 2011, built around a carbon-fibre MonoCell tub.

Models

Its range has included the 570S and 720S in the Sports and Super Series, the hybrid hypercars P1 and Speedtail, the Senna and W1, and the Artura hybrid. McLaren is known for lightweight carbon construction, twin-turbo V8 and V6 engines, and dihedral doors.
