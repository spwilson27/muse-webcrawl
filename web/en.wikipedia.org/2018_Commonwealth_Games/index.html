The 2018 Commonwealth Games, officially known as the XXI Commonwealth Games, were an international multi-sport event for members of the Commonwealth held on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia, between 4 and 15 April 2018. It was the fifth time Australia hosted the Commonwealth Games.

Participation

A total of 71 Commonwealth Games Associations took part, with around 4,400 athletes competing in 275 events across 18 sports. The Games featured an integrated para-sport programme, the largest in Commonwealth Games history, and for the first time at a major multi-sport event there were an equal number of medal events for men and women.

Medal table

Hosts Australia topped the medal table with 80 gold medals and 198 medals overall, followed by England and India. Vanuatu won its first ever Commonwealth Games medal at these Games.
