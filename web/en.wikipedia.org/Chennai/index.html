Chennai

Chennai, formerly known as Madras, is the capital city of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. Located on the Coromandel Coast of the Bay of Bengal, it is one of the largest cultural, economic and educational centres in South India. According to the 2011 Indian census, Chennai is the sixth-most populous city in India and forms the fourth-most populous urban agglomeration.

History

The region around Chennai has been part of successive South Indian kingdoms, including the Pallava, the Chola, the Pandya and the Vijayanagara empires. The modern city grew around the English settlement of Fort St. George, founded in 1639 by the British East India Company. The city was renamed Chennai in 1996, after Chennapatnam, the town that grew up around the fort.

Geography and climate

Chennai lies on a flat coastal plain on the southeastern coast of India. It has a tropical wet and dry climate, with hot summers and a monsoon season concentrated in October to December, when the northeast monsoon brings most of the city's rainfall. The city is crossed by the Cooum and Adyar rivers, and the man-made Buckingham Canal runs parallel to the coast.

Economy

Chennai has a broad industrial base in the automobile, computer, technology, hardware manufacturing and healthcare industries. It is sometimes called the "Detroit of India" for its large automobile industry, and it is a major centre for information technology and business process outsourcing. The city is also known for its film industry, based in Kodambakkam and nicknamed "Kollywood".

Culture

Chennai is a centre of Carnatic music and Bharatanatyam dance; the December Music Season, centred on the Margazhi festival, is one of the world's largest cultural events. Marina Beach, one of the longest urban beaches in the world, is a landmark of the city, as are the Kapaleeshwarar Temple in Mylapore and Fort St. George.
