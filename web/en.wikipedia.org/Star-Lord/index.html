Star-Lord

Star-Lord (Peter Quill), leader of the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Star-Lord (Peter Jason Quill) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Steve Englehart and artist Steve Gan, the character first appeared in Marvel Preview #4 (January 1976). Quill is the son of a human mother and an alien father, and as an adult becomes an interstellar adventurer and the leader of the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Fictional biography

Peter Quill was born in Colorado to Meredith Quill and the alien J'son of Spartax. After his mother's death, he was abducted from Earth by the Yondu and his Ravagers, growing up among space pirates. As Star-Lord he formed the modern Guardians of the Galaxy during the Annihilation storyline, alongside Gamora, Drax the Destroyer, Rocket Raccoon, and Groot.

In other media

Star-Lord reached a mass audience through the Marvel Cinematic Universe, where he is played by Chris Pratt, beginning with Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) and continuing in its sequels and the Avengers films. The film version is known for his Walkman and love of 1970s pop music, his elemental blasters, and his role as the team's irreverent leader.
