Mr. Money Mustache

Mr. Money Mustache is a website and blog run by Canadian-born blogger Peter Adeney. The site, and its self-proclaimed persona of a "financial guru", focuses on frugality and early retirement through investing. Adeney retired from his job as a software engineer at about age 30 and started the blog in 2011 to share his financial philosophy.

Philosophy

The blog's philosophy centers on extreme saving — often saving 50–75% of income — investing in low-cost index funds, and achieving financial independence far earlier than the conventional retirement age. Adeney champions bicycling as primary transport, small houses, DIY skills and a stoic-inspired attitude of "badassity", arguing that frugality increases, rather than decreases, life satisfaction. The site popularized the "4% rule" as a guide to calculating how much must be saved for retirement (25 times annual spending).

Influence

Mr. Money Mustache became one of the best-known voices in the FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) movement, and its forum community developed an active culture around debt payoff challenges ("face-punching" overspenders) and frugal living.
