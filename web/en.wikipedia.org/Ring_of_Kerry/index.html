The Ring of Kerry (Irish: Mórchuaird Chiarraí) is a 179-kilometre (111 mi) circular tourist route in County Kerry, south-western Ireland. Following the N70 road around the Iveragh Peninsula, the route passes through or near Killarney, Kenmare, Sneem, Waterville, Cahersiveen and Killorglin, taking in views of MacGillycuddy's Reeks, the Lakes of Killarney and the Atlantic coast.

Sights

Attractions along the route include Muckross House and Killarney National Park, the Staigue stone fort, Derrynane House (home of Daniel O'Connell), views of the Skellig Islands, and the Gap of Dunloe. The smaller Skellig Ring branches off toward Portmagee and Valentia Island.

Travel

Tour buses traditionally travel the ring counter-clockwise from Killarney to avoid meeting on the narrow roads. The route is also popular with cyclists, and the Kerry Way walking trail follows a related course through the peninsula's interior.
