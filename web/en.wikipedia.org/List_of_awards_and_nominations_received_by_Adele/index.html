List of awards and nominations received by Adele

English singer-songwriter Adele has received numerous awards for albums 19 (2008), 21 (2011), 25 (2015) and 30 (2021), and for singles including "Rolling in the Deep", "Someone Like You", "Skyfall", "Hello" and "Easy on Me".

Major awards

  • Grammy Awards: 16 wins from numerous nominations, including Album of the Year for 21 and 25, Record and Song of the Year for "Rolling in the Deep" and "Hello", and Best New Artist (2009). She is one of few artists to win the top three general-field categories twice.
  • Academy Award: Best Original Song for "Skyfall" (2013).
  • Golden Globe Award: Best Original Song for "Skyfall".
  • Brit Awards: multiple wins including British Album of the Year for 21, 25 and 30, British Single of the Year, and the BRITs Global Success Award.
  • American Music Awards, Billboard Music Awards, Ivor Novello Awards: multiple wins.
  • Primetime Emmy Award: for the television special Adele One Night Only, making her close to EGOT status.

Her album 21 is among the best-selling albums of all time and won Album of the Year at the 54th Grammys in 2012, where she won all six categories in which she was nominated.
