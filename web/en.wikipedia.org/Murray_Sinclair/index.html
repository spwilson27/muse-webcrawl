Murray Sinclair

Calvin Murray Sinclair (January 6, 1951 – November 4, 2024) was a Canadian lawyer, judge and politician. Of Ojibway heritage, he was the first Indigenous judge appointed in Manitoba and the second in Canada. He is best known as the chair of the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada (2009–2015).

Career

Sinclair studied law at the University of Manitoba and was called to the bar in 1980. Appointed to the Provincial Court of Manitoba in 1988 and to the Court of Queen's Bench in 2001, he co-chaired the Aboriginal Justice Inquiry of Manitoba and led the Truth and Reconciliation Commission, whose 2015 report documented the history and legacy of the Canadian residential school system and issued 94 Calls to Action.

Senate

In 2016 Sinclair was appointed to the Senate of Canada, where he served until retiring in 2021. He received numerous honours for his work in Indigenous rights and reconciliation.
