Movement for Democratic Change
Founded: 1999, Zimbabwe
Founder leader: Morgan Tsvangirai
Ideology: social democracy
Successor currents: MDC-T, CCC and others

The Movement for Democratic Change (MDC) was a Zimbabwean political party founded in 1999 from the labour movement and civil society to challenge Robert Mugabe's ZANU-PF government.

History

Led by former union leader Morgan Tsvangirai, the MDC won strong urban support in 2000 elections. After disputed 2008 polls and violence, Tsvangirai became prime minister in a 2009–2013 unity government. The party split several times (notably 2005 and 2014) and later regrouped under the MDC Alliance; Nelson Chamisa later formed the Citizens Coalition for Change (2022).

Legacy

The MDC reshaped Zimbabwean opposition politics within the wider African context of the African Union and appeals to the United Nations over elections and human rights.
