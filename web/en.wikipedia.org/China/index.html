China

China, officially the People's Republic of China (PRC), is a country in East Asia. With a population exceeding 1.4 billion, it is the second-most populous country after India. China spans five geographical time zones (though a single standard time is used) and borders fourteen countries by land, the second most of any country.

Geography

China's landscape ranges from the Gobi and Taklamakan deserts in the north and west, through the fertile North China Plain and Yangtze and Yellow River deltas, to subtropical forests in the south and the high Tibetan Plateau. Oasis cities such as Kashgar, Turpan and Hotan ring the Tarim Basin.

History

One of the world's oldest continuous civilisations, China was unified under Qin Shi Huang in 221 BC and ruled by successive dynasties — Han, Tang, Song, Ming and Qing — until the Republic of China was founded in 1912. After civil war, the People's Republic was proclaimed by Mao Zedong on 1 October 1949. Reform and opening under Deng Xiaoping from 1978 produced decades of rapid growth, making China the world's second-largest economy by nominal GDP and largest by purchasing power parity.

Politics and economy

The Chinese Communist Party governs under a unitary system; the head of state is the President (currently Xi Jinping, also CCP General Secretary). China is a manufacturing and export superpower, a permanent member of the UN Security Council, and operator of initiatives such as the Belt and Road.
