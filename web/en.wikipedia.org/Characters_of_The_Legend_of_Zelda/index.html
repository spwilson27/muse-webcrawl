Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda video game series features an extensive cast of characters, many of whom recur across games in different incarnations. The central trio is bound to the three pieces of the Triforce, a sacred artefact embodying Power, Wisdom, and Courage.

Main characters

Link is the silent protagonist of nearly every game, a young hero (usually a Hylian swordsman) destined to save the kingdom of Hyrule. Princess Zelda is the wise ruler or heir of Hyrule, often possessing magical and prophetic abilities. Ganon (in humanoid form, Ganondorf) is the principal antagonist, a Gerudo king who seeks the Triforce's power.

Recurring characters

Recurring figures include Impa, Zelda's Sheikah attendant; the eccentric map-seller Tingle; the Goron, Zora, Rito, and Gerudo peoples; the Deku and Great Deku Tree; Epona, Link's horse; and companions such as Navi the fairy, Midna, and Fi. The sage-like figures and the goddess Hylia feature prominently in later entries.
