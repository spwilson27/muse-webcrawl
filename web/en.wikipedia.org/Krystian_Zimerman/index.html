Krystian Zimerman (born 5 December 1956) is a Polish classical pianist, composer and conductor who has been hailed as one of the greatest pianists of his generation. In 1975, at age 18, he won the IX International Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw, becoming its youngest winner at the time.

Career

Zimerman studied with Andrzej Jasiński in Katowice. After his Chopin Competition victory he performed with the Berlin Philharmonic under Herbert von Karajan and built a career marked by a deliberately limited number of concerts and recordings, each meticulously prepared. He has recorded extensively for Deutsche Grammophon, including celebrated accounts of the Chopin concertos (conducting from the keyboard with the Polish Festival Orchestra, which he founded in 1999), the Brahms concertos, Liszt, Debussy, Szymanowski and Lutosławski, whose Piano Concerto was written for him and premiered by him in 1988.

Style and views

Famous for his perfectionism, Zimerman travels with his own Steinway piano and personally prepares its action. He has also been outspoken on political questions, notably criticising United States foreign policy in a 2009 Los Angeles concert and protesting a confiscated piano episode at US customs in 2001.
