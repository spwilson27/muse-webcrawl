List of counties in Colorado

The U.S. state of Colorado is divided into 64 counties. Two of them, the City and County of Denver and the City and County of Broomfield, are consolidated city-counties. The most populous is El Paso County (home to Colorado Springs); the largest by area is Las Animas County.
