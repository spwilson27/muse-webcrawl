New South Wales rugby league team

The New South Wales rugby league team, nicknamed the Blues, represents the Australian state of New South Wales in rugby league football. Since 1980 the team has contested the annual State of Origin series against Queensland, the Maroons.

History

Interstate matches between New South Wales and Queensland date to 1908, but for decades New South Wales dominated because its wealthier Sydney clubs attracted the best Queensland players. The introduction of state-of-origin selection rules in 1980, under which players represent the state where they first played senior football, made the contest evenly balanced and hugely popular.

Home grounds and colours

The Blues wear sky blue jerseys and traditionally play home matches at Stadium Australia in Sydney. Notable captains have included Wally Lewis's great rival-era leaders and more recent figures such as Paul Gallen and James Tedesco.
