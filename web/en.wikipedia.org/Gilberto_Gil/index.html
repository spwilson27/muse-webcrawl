Gilberto Gil

Gilberto Passos Gil Moreira (born 26 June 1942) is a Brazilian singer-songwriter and politician, known for both his musical innovation and political commitment. A founder, with Caetano Veloso, of the Tropicália movement of the late 1960s, he helped transform Brazilian popular music by fusing bossa nova, rock, and Afro-Brazilian styles.

Career

Imprisoned and then exiled by Brazil's military dictatorship in 1969, Gil lived in London before returning home in 1972. Albums such as Expresso 2222, Refavela, and Realce made him one of Brazil's biggest stars; he won multiple Grammy Awards. Gil entered politics as a Green Party city councillor in Salvador and served as Brazil's Minister of Culture from 2003 to 2008 under President Lula da Silva.
