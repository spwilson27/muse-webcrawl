Amazon Music

Amazon Music (formerly Amazon MP3) is a music streaming platform and online music store operated by Amazon. Launched in public beta in September 2007, it was the first music store to sell downloads free of digital rights management (DRM) from all four major record labels.

Services

The platform evolved from downloads into streaming. Prime Music, included with Amazon Prime membership from 2014, was expanded in 2022 to offer the full catalogue of around 100 million songs in shuffle mode. Amazon Music Unlimited, launched in 2016, is the premium subscription tier offering on-demand streaming, high-definition and spatial audio. A free, advertising-supported tier is also available in some countries. The service integrates with Amazon's Alexa voice assistant and Echo devices, and supports podcasts as well as live-streamed content via Twitch integration.

Market position

Amazon Music consistently ranks among the largest music streaming services globally, competing with Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music, with tens of millions of subscribers. It was among the first major services to promote lossless and high-resolution audio tiers.
