Lucin Cutoff

LocationGreat Salt Lake, Utah, United States
Opened1904
OperatorUnion Pacific Railroad

The Lucin Cutoff is a 102-mile (164 km) railroad line in Utah, United States, operated by the Union Pacific Railroad. Completed in 1904 for the Southern Pacific, it crossed the Great Salt Lake on a long wooden trestle, shortening the original Central Pacific route around the north end of the lake via Promontory Summit.

History

The centerpiece of the cutoff was a 12-mile wooden trestle across the lake — at the time the longest trestle in the world. In the 1950s the trestle was largely replaced by a parallel rock causeway, which altered the lake's circulation and contributed to the visible difference in water color between the lake's north and south arms.
