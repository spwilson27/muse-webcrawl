Jack Michael Antonoff (born 31 March 1984) is an American singer, songwriter and record producer. He is the lead singer of the rock band Bleachers, and was previously the guitarist and drummer of the pop rock band Fun and the frontman of Steel Train. As a producer and songwriter he has become one of the most influential figures in modern pop, collaborating extensively with Taylor Swift, Lorde, Lana Del Rey, The 1975, Florence + the Machine and many others, and winning numerous Grammy Awards including Producer of the Year, Non-Classical three years in a row (2022–2024).

Early life

Antonoff grew up in Bergenfield and Woodcliff Lake, New Jersey, in a Jewish family. He began writing music as a teenager and toured with punk and indie bands while still in school, experiences he has described as formative.

Career

With Fun, Antonoff achieved international success with the album Some Nights (2012) and its singles "We Are Young" and "Some Nights". After Fun went on hiatus, he launched Bleachers in 2014 with the single "I Wanna Get Better"; the project has released several albums including Strange Desire (2014), Gone Now (2017), Take the Sadness Out of Saturday Night (2021) and Bleachers (2024).

Production work

Antonoff's production partnership with Taylor Swift began on her album 1989 (2014) and continued through Reputation, Lover, Folklore, Evermore, Midnights and the re-recorded "Taylor's Version" albums. He co-produced Lorde's Melodrama (2017) and worked on Lana Del Rey's Norman Fucking Rockwell! (2019). His sound — synthesisers, layered drums and confessional songwriting — helped define 2010s and 2020s pop. He also co-wrote and produced music for films and founded the Ally Coalition, which supports LGBTQ youth.

Personal life

Antonoff married actress Margaret Qualley in August 2023. He has spoken publicly about anxiety, depression and the death of his younger sister, themes that recur in his songwriting.
