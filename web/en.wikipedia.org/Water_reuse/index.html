Water reuse

Water reuse (also water recycling or water reclamation) is the process of converting wastewater or other non-potable sources into water that can be reused for purposes including irrigation, groundwater recharge, industrial processes, and in some systems drinking water supply.

Types

Reuse ranges from unplanned reuse (downstream abstraction of river water containing treated effluent) to planned non-potable reuse — often delivered through "purple pipe" networks — and indirect or direct potable reuse, where highly treated water is returned to reservoirs, aquifers, or directly to treatment plants.

Examples

Notable schemes include Orange County's Groundwater Replenishment System in California, Windhoek's direct potable reuse in Namibia, and large irrigation reuse programmes in Israel and Australia. Treatment typically combines membrane filtration, reverse osmosis and advanced oxidation to meet health standards.
