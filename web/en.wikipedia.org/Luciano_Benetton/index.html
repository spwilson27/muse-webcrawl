Luciano Benetton (born 13 May 1935) is an Italian businessman and co-founder of the Benetton Group, one of the world's best-known fashion brands. He led the company for decades and later served as a senator in the Italian parliament.

Founding of Benetton

Born in Treviso, Luciano left school at fourteen after his father's death to work in a clothing shop. In 1965 he founded the Benetton company with his siblings Giuliana, Gilberto and Carlo, beginning with Giuliana's brightly coloured knitted sweaters sold door to door. The firm pioneered garment dyeing, franchising and provocative advertising — the "United Colors of Benetton" campaigns photographed by Oliviero Toscani.

Later career

The family holding company, Edizione, diversified into motorway services (Autogrill, Atlantia), agriculture and finance. Benetton served as a senator from 1992 to 1994 for the Italian Republican Party. After years away from the operating company, he returned as executive chairman in 2018 following losses, before stepping back again.
