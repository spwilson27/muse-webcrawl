Elmbridge

The Borough of Elmbridge is a local government district with borough status in Surrey, England. Its council is based in Esher. The borough covers an area of mainly affluent suburbs of south-western Greater London, lying mostly within the M25 motorway and bordering the River Thames.

Settlements

The borough's principal towns and villages include Weybridge, Esher, Walton-on-Thames, Cobham, Molesey (East and West Molesey), Hersham, Thames Ditton, Long Ditton, Hinchley Wood, Claygate, Oxshott, Stoke d'Abernon, and Byfleet-adjacent communities. It is named after the Elmbridge — originally a bridge over the River Mole.

History

The borough was formed on 1 April 1974 under the Local Government Act 1972, merging the former urban districts of Esher and Walton and Weybridge. The area has a long history of settlement along the Thames and the Wey; Hampton Court Palace lies just across the river in Richmond upon Thames, and Henry VIII built a hunting lodge at what became Esher Place.

Character

Elmbridge is frequently described as one of the most affluent districts in England, with high house prices in areas such as St George's Hill and the Wentworth-adjacent estates. The borough contains Sandown Park Racecourse, Brooklands (the birthplace of British motor racing and aviation, now partly a museum), and extensive commons and heathland.
