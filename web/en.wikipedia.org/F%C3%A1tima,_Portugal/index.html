Fátima, Portugal

Fátima is a city in the municipality of Ourém, in the Central Region of Portugal. It is one of the most important Catholic pilgrimage sites in the world, associated with the Marian apparitions reported by three shepherd children in 1917.

Fátima
Country: Portugal
Municipality: Ourém
Known for: Sanctuary of Fátima

The apparitions

Between May and October 1917, Lúcia Santos and her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto reported apparitions of the Virgin Mary at the Cova da Iria. The events culminated in the "Miracle of the Sun" witnessed by a large crowd on 13 October 1917.

Sanctuary

The Sanctuary of Fátima includes the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary, the Chapel of the Apparitions, and the modern Basilica of the Holy Trinity, drawing millions of pilgrims each year, especially on 13 May and 13 October.
