Neptune's Staircase

Location: Banavie, near Fort William, Scotland
Canal: Caledonian Canal
Number of locks: 8
Total rise: 19 m (64 ft)
Engineer: Thomas Telford

Neptune's Staircase is a staircase lock comprising eight locks on the Caledonian Canal at Banavie, near Fort William in Scotland. The longest staircase lock in Britain, it raises boats a total of 19 metres (64 feet) over a distance of about 457 metres (500 yards).

History and operation

The locks were built between 1803 and 1822 as part of Thomas Telford's Caledonian Canal, which crosses the Great Glen from Inverness to Fort William. Originally operated manually, the gates are now mechanised and worked by lock keepers; a boat takes roughly an hour and a half to pass through the flight. The staircase is a popular visitor attraction, with Ben Nevis towering in the background.
