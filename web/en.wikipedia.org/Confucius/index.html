Confucius (Chinese: 孔夫子, Kǒng Fūzǐ, "Master Kong"; 551–479 BCE), born Kong Qiu, was a Chinese philosopher and politician of the Spring and Autumn period who is traditionally considered the paragon of Chinese sages. His philosophy, known as Confucianism, emphasised personal and governmental morality, correctness of social relationships, justice, kindness and sincerity, and has influenced China and East Asia profoundly ever since.

Life

Confucius was born into a family of modest means in the state of Lu, in modern Shandong. His father died when he was young, and he worked in various minor posts before becoming a teacher — traditionally credited as one of the first in China to teach students regardless of their birth. He held office in Lu, reportedly rising to a ministerial post, but left around 497 BCE and spent some thirteen years travelling among the states of China, seeking a ruler who would implement his ideas of government by virtue and ritual rather than by force and punishment. Unsuccessful, he returned to Lu and spent his last years teaching and editing classical texts.

Teachings

Confucius's sayings were compiled by his disciples in the Analects (Lunyu). He taught that society would flourish if rulers cultivated virtue, families practised filial piety, and individuals followed li — ritual propriety and proper conduct — guided by ren, humaneness toward others. His version of the Golden Rule ran negatively: "Do not impose on others what you yourself do not desire." Education, self-cultivation and respect for tradition and ancestors were central; he described himself as "a transmitter, not a creator".

Legacy

His disciples Mencius and Xunzi developed the doctrine in divergent directions. From the Han dynasty onward, Confucianism became China's orthodox ideology and the core of the imperial examination system, making Confucius the most influential figure in Chinese intellectual history. Temples to Confucius were built across China and East Asia, and his descendants — the Kong family of Qufu — hold the longest recorded genealogy in the world. Though attacked in the twentieth century as a symbol of feudal backwardness, his standing has been restored in contemporary China.
