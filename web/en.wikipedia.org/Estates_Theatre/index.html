The Estates Theatre (Czech: Stavovské divadlo) is a historic theatre in Prague, opened in 1783 as the Nostitz Theatre, built by Count Franz Anton von Nostitz-Rieneck. It is one of the finest surviving examples of Neoclassical theatre architecture in Europe and the oldest theatre in Prague still in its original form.

Mozart premieres

The theatre is world-famous as the venue of the world premieres of two operas by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart: Don Giovanni on 29 October 1787, conducted by the composer, and La clemenza di Tito in 1791. Prague audiences had already acclaimed The Marriage of Figaro there, prompting Mozart's remark that "my Praguers understand me".

Later history

In 1826 the song Kde domov můj ("Where is my home"), later the Czech national anthem, was first performed here. The theatre was the first in the Czech lands to stage plays in the Czech language regularly. It now operates as one of the stages of the National Theatre, specialising in Mozart.
