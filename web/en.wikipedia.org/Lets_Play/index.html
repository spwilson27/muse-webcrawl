Let's Play

A Let's Play (LP) is a video — or a series of screenshots with commentary — in which a player records themselves playing through a video game, typically adding narration, reactions and humour. Let's Play videos became a major genre of online entertainment on platforms such as YouTube and Twitch.

History

The format originated on the Something Awful forums around 2006–2007, where users posted screenshot threads documenting playthroughs; the term is often credited to poster Michael Sawyer ("slowbeef"). As video capture became easy, the genre migrated to YouTube, where creators built large audiences for personality-driven commentary.

Styles and impact

Let's Plays range from informative walkthroughs to comedy, horror-game reactions and completionist runs. Popular LP creators became influential in game marketing, and publishers' attitudes to the format helped shape modern streaming culture.
