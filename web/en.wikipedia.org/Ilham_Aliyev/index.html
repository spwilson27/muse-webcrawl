Ilham Aliyev

Born: 24 December 1961, Baku, Azerbaijan SSR, Soviet Union
Office: President of Azerbaijan (since 31 October 2003)
Party: New Azerbaijan Party

Ilham Heydar oghlu Aliyev (born 24 December 1961) is an Azerbaijani politician who has served as the fourth president of Azerbaijan since 2003. He is the son of Heydar Aliyev, who was president from 1993 to 2003, and succeeded him after winning the 2003 presidential election. He has since been re-elected several times, with term limits removed by a 2009 referendum.

Early life and career

Aliyev was born in Baku and educated at the Moscow State Institute of International Relations (MGIMO), earning a degree in history. He worked at the State Oil Company of Azerbaijan (SOCAR) as a vice-president in the 1990s and was elected to the National Assembly in 1995. He also served as head of Azerbaijan's National Olympic Committee. In August 2003 he was appointed prime minister, shortly before succeeding his ailing father.

Presidency

Aliyev's presidency has been marked by an oil and gas boom that funded large infrastructure projects, and by the long-running conflict with Armenia over Nagorno-Karabakh. In 2020, Azerbaijan regained significant territory in the Second Nagorno-Karabakh War, and in September 2023 a military offensive led to the dissolution of the de facto Republic of Artsakh and Azerbaijan's full control over the region, prompting the exodus of its Armenian population. His government has been criticised by international observers and human rights organisations for authoritarianism, restrictions on the press, and the conduct of elections.
