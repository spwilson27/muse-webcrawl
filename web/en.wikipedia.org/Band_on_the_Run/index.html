Band on the Run

Band on the Run is the third studio album by the British–American rock band Paul McCartney and Wings, released on 30 November 1973. It was McCartney's most successful album after the Beatles and remains one of the best-selling albums of the 1970s, topping the charts in the United States and the United Kingdom.

Recording

Most of the album was recorded at EMI Studios in Lagos, Nigeria, after guitarist Henry McCullough and drummer Denny Seiwell quit the band on the eve of departure, leaving McCartney, Linda McCartney, and Denny Laine to record largely on their own. Sessions were eventful: the McCartneys were robbed at knifepoint of demo tapes, and Paul collapsed in the studio from a bronchial spasm.

Reception

The album produced the hit singles "Jet", "Helen Wheels", and the title track "Band on the Run". It is widely regarded as McCartney's finest post-Beatles work and appears on numerous critics' lists of the greatest albums of all time, including Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Albums.
