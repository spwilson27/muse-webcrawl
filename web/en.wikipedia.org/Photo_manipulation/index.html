Photo manipulation

Photo manipulation involves the transformation or alteration of a photograph using various methods and techniques to achieve desired results. Some are considered skillful artwork, while others are frowned upon as unethical practice, especially when used to deceive the public.

History

Manipulation dates to the earliest days of photography: 19th-century practitioners combined negatives, retouched prints and staged scenes. Political regimes of the 20th century famously airbrushed purged officials from official photographs.

Digital era

Software such as Adobe Photoshop, released in 1990, made sophisticated editing widely accessible; the verb "to photoshop" entered common usage. Modern debates concern manipulated imagery in advertising, journalism and social media, and increasingly realistic synthetic media.
