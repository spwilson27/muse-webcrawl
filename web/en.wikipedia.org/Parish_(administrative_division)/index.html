Parish (administrative division)

Parish
Type: Administrative division
Examples: Civil parishes (England), parishes of Louisiana, Andorra, Caribbean states

A parish is a territorial entity forming an administrative division in many countries, derived originally from the area served by a parish church. Civil parishes may be governed by a parish council or parish meeting and are the smallest unit of local government in England.

Usage by country

In England, civil parishes were separated from ecclesiastical parishes in the 19th century and today number over 10,000. The US state of Louisiana is divided into parishes instead of counties, a legacy of French and Spanish colonial administration. Parishes are also first-level divisions in Andorra, Jersey and Guernsey, several Caribbean nations, and parts of Canada and Australia.
