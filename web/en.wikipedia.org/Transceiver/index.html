Transceiver

A transceiver is an electronic device that is a combination of a radio transmitter and a receiver, hence the name. It can both transmit and receive radio waves using a common antenna, and it is used for telecommunications. Transceivers range from handheld amateur radio units and mobile phones to optical modules in data-centre networking.

Types

Half-duplex transceivers transmit or receive at any one time through a shared channel, switching between the two functions; full-duplex designs allow simultaneous transmission and reception, usually on different frequencies. In computer networking, pluggable optical transceivers such as SFP and QSFP modules link switches and routers over fibre.

History

Combined transmitter-receiver units became common in military radio in the 1930s and 1940s and later in citizen's band and amateur radio equipment, replacing separate transmitter and receiver sets.
