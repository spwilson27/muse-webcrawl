Arsenal Stadium

Location: Highbury, London

Opened: 1913

Closed: 2006

Tenants: Arsenal F.C.

Arsenal Stadium, commonly known as Highbury, was the home stadium of Arsenal Football Club from 1913 until 2006. Located in Highbury, north London, it was celebrated for its distinctive Art Deco East and West Stands, designed in the 1930s.

History

Arsenal moved from Woolwich to Highbury in 1913 under chairman Henry Norris. Under manager Herbert Chapman the stadium was extensively remodelled, and after the Second World War crowds regularly exceeded 60,000. Following the Taylor Report the ground became all-seater, reducing capacity to about 38,000.

Closure and redevelopment

Arsenal played its final match at Highbury on 7 May 2006, moving to the Emirates Stadium. The site was redeveloped into housing as Highbury Square, with the listed East and West Stand façades retained.
