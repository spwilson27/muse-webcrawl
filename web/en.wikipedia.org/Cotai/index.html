Cotai is a piece of reclaimed land between the islands of Taipa and Coloane in the Special Administrative Region of Macau, China. The name is a portmanteau of the first syllables of Coloane and Taipa. The area was created to provide Macau with a dedicated zone for large-scale casino resort development, modelled on the Las Vegas Strip.

Development

Land reclamation linking Taipa and Coloane began in the 1990s. After Macau liberalised its gaming industry in 2002, international operators built mega-resorts along the Cotai Strip, including The Venetian Macao (opened 2007), City of Dreams, Galaxy Macau, Studio City, Wynn Palace, and the Parisian Macao. The area also hosts the Cotai Arena and the Cotai Water Jet ferry terminal at Taipa.

Transport

Cotai is served by the Macau Light Rapid Transit Taipa line, which opened in 2019, and by road links to the Macau Peninsula and to Hengqin Island in Zhuhai via the Lotus Bridge.
