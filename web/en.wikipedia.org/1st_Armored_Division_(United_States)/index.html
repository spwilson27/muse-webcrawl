1st Armored Division

The 1st Armored Division, nicknamed "Old Ironsides", is a combined arms division of the United States Army. The division is part of III Corps and is based at Fort Bliss, Texas. It is the first armored division of the United States Army to see battle in World War II.

Activated on 15 July 1940 at Fort Knox, Kentucky, the division fought in the North African and Italian campaigns of the Second World War. After the war it served in Germany during the Cold War, deployed to the Gulf War in 1991, and later served tours in Iraq and Afghanistan.

Organization

The division comprises armored brigade combat teams, a combat aviation brigade, a sustainment brigade, and division artillery, with a total strength of roughly 17,000 soldiers.
