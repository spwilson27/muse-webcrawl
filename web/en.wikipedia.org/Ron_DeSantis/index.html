Ron DeSantis

Ronald Dion DeSantis (born September 14, 1978) is an American politician and former naval officer serving since 2019 as the 46th governor of Florida. A member of the Republican Party, he previously represented Florida's 6th congressional district in the United States House of Representatives from 2013 to 2018.

Early life and military service

DeSantis was born in Jacksonville, Florida, and raised in Dunedin. He graduated from Yale University, where he captained the baseball team, and from Harvard Law School. He joined the United States Navy in 2004, serving as a legal adviser (JAG officer), including a deployment to Iraq with SEAL Team One, and received the Bronze Star Medal.

Governor of Florida

DeSantis narrowly won the 2018 gubernatorial election and was reelected by a wide margin in 2022. His tenure drew national attention for his handling of the COVID-19 pandemic — opposing lockdowns and mandates — and for education and cultural legislation, including the Parental Rights in Education Act, as well as a public dispute with The Walt Disney Company over the governance of its Florida resort district.

2024 presidential campaign

DeSantis ran for the Republican nomination in the 2024 United States presidential election, launching his campaign in May 2023. He suspended his campaign in January 2024 after the Iowa caucuses and endorsed Donald Trump.
