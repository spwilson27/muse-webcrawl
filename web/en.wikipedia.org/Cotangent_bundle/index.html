Cotangent bundle

Cotangent bundle
FieldDifferential geometry
NotationT*M for a manifold M
FibreCotangent space T*pM
StructureCanonical symplectic form

In mathematics, especially differential geometry, the cotangent bundle of a smooth manifold is the vector bundle of all the cotangent spaces at every point in the manifold. It may be described as the dual bundle to the tangent bundle. If M is a smooth manifold of dimension n, its cotangent bundle T*M is a manifold of dimension 2n whose fibre over a point p is the cotangent space T*pM, the dual of the tangent space at p.

Canonical structure

The cotangent bundle carries a canonical one-form, the tautological one-form, whose exterior derivative gives a canonical symplectic form. This makes every cotangent bundle a symplectic manifold, and cotangent bundles serve as the standard examples of phase spaces in classical mechanics: a mechanical system with configuration space M has phase space T*M, and its dynamics are described by Hamiltonian mechanics on that space.

Sections

Smooth sections of the cotangent bundle are differential one-forms. The cotangent bundle is central to the modern formulation of Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, geometric quantization, and microlocal analysis.
