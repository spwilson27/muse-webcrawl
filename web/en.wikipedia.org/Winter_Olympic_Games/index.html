The Winter Olympic Games are a major international multi-sport event held once every four years for sports practised on snow and ice. The inaugural Games were held in Chamonix, France, in 1924, growing out of the Nordic Games and the success of winter sports included in earlier Summer Olympics. They have been hosted on three continents, and since 1994 have alternated with the Summer Olympic Games in even-numbered years, two years apart.

History

Figure skating appeared at the 1908 London Summer Olympics and ice hockey at Antwerp 1920. In 1924 an "International Winter Sports Week" was held in Chamonix under IOC patronage; its success led the IOC to create the Winter Games retroactively, with Chamonix recognised as the first edition. The Games were held every four years alongside the Summer Games until 1992, after which the IOC staggered the cycle: the 1994 Lillehammer Games followed Albertville 1992 by only two years. Norway has hosted twice and tops the all-time medal table; the United States has hosted a record four times.

Sports

The Winter programme is smaller and more stable than the summer one. Core sports are ice skating (figure skating, speed skating, short track), skiing (alpine, cross-country, freestyle, ski jumping, Nordic combined, snowboarding), bobsleigh (including skeleton), luge, ice hockey, curling and biathlon. New events such as big air, mixed team formats and, for 2026, ski mountaineering are periodically added.

All-time medal table

Norway leads the all-time Winter Olympic medal standings, followed by the United States, Germany (including East and West German results in some tallies), Austria, Canada and the former Soviet Union. Norway's cross-country skier Marit Bjørgen is the most decorated Winter Olympian, with 15 medals.

Challenges

Rising costs, limited numbers of plausible host cities, low post-Games use of sliding and ski-jumping venues, and the effects of climate change on reliable snow have led the IOC to favour regional, multi-city bids and existing facilities, as in the Milano Cortina 2026 and French Alps 2030 plans.
