List of islands of Greece

[Image: The caldera of Santorini in the Aegean Sea]
Santorini, one of the Cyclades.

Greece has thousands of islands, islets and rocky outcrops in the Aegean and Ionian seas; estimates range from about 1,200 to 6,000 depending on the minimum size counted. Only about 227 are inhabited. The islands are traditionally grouped into clusters including the Cyclades, Dodecanese, Sporades, Ionian Islands, Saronic Islands and the North Aegean islands.
