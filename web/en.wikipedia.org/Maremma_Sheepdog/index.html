The Maremma Sheepdog, known in Italian as the Cane da pastore Maremmano-Abruzzese, is a breed of livestock guardian dog indigenous to central Italy, particularly the coastal Maremma region of Tuscany and Lazio and the mountainous Abruzzo. It has been used for centuries by Italian shepherds to guard sheep and goats against wolves and other predators.

Characteristics

The Maremma is a large, strongly built dog with a thick white double coat that helps it blend in with the flocks it protects and withstand harsh mountain weather. It is independent, loyal and protective, bonding closely with its flock rather than herding it.

Use

The breed traditionally accompanies transhumant shepherds moving flocks between summer mountain pastures and winter lowlands. Maremma-type guardian dogs are now also used in other countries, including Australia and the United States, for livestock and even penguin protection.
