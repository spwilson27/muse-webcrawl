PlayStation Plus is a video game subscription service offered by Sony Interactive Entertainment for its PlayStation consoles. First launched in 2010, the service originally offered subscribers a rotating selection of free downloadable games each month, exclusive discounts on the PlayStation Store, early access to demos and betas, and cloud storage for saved games.

With the launch of the PlayStation 4 in 2013, a PlayStation Plus subscription became required for most online multiplayer games on the platform. In June 2022, Sony relaunched the service in a three-tier structure — Essential, Extra and Premium — merging it with the former PlayStation Now cloud streaming service and adding a large catalogue of downloadable and streamed games, including titles from earlier PlayStation generations.

History

PlayStation Plus was announced at E3 2010 by Kaz Hirai and launched on June 29, 2010. Unlike its competitor Xbox Live Gold, multiplayer gaming on the PlayStation 3 remained free at the time; Plus was positioned purely as a premium content subscription. The Instant Game Collection, which offered subscribers multiple games per month at no additional charge, proved to be the service's most popular feature.

The service grew steadily alongside the PS4, reaching more than 47 million subscribers by 2021. The 2022 relaunch aligned the service more closely with Microsoft's Xbox Game Pass, though Sony stated it would not place its first-party titles on the service at launch.

Tiers

Essential is the base tier, providing online multiplayer access, two monthly downloadable games, cloud storage and exclusive discounts. Extra adds a catalogue of several hundred downloadable PS4 and PS5 games. Premium (called Deluxe in some regions) adds streaming of PS3 games, a catalogue of classic titles from the original PlayStation, PS2 and PSP, game trials and, in supported regions, cloud streaming of PS5 games.

Reception

The original service was generally praised for the value of its monthly games. The 2022 tiered relaunch received a mixed reception, with reviewers welcoming the size of the Extra catalogue while criticising the limited selection of classic games and the requirement of streaming for PS3 titles.
