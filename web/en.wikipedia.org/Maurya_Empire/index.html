Maurya Empire

The Maurya Empire was a geographically extensive Iron Age historical power in ancient India, based in Magadha and founded by Chandragupta Maurya in 322 BCE. At its height under Ashoka it ruled most of the Indian subcontinent, covering over 5 million square kilometres.

History

Chandragupta, advised by Chanakya (Kautilya), overthrew the Nanda Empire and repelled the successor states of Alexander's empire. His grandson Ashoka embraced Buddhism after the bloody conquest of Kalinga and spread edicts inscribed on pillars and rocks across the empire. The empire declined after Ashoka's death in 232 BCE and ended when the last ruler, Brihadratha, was assassinated in 185 BCE by Pushyamitra Shunga.

Administration

The capital was Pataliputra. The empire maintained a large standing army, a bureaucratic administration described in the Arthashastra, and extensive trade networks.
