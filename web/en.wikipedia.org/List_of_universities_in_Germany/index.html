List of universities in Germany

Heidelberg University, founded in 1386, is Germany's oldest university.

Germany has more than one hundred universities (Universitäten) and an even larger number of universities of applied sciences (Fachhochschulen). This list of universities in Germany covers institutions entitled to award doctorates. German universities are mostly public and charge little or no tuition; the oldest, Heidelberg University, dates to 1386, and the largest by enrolment include the universities of Cologne, Munich (LMU) and the distance-learning FernUniversität in Hagen.

Organisation

Universities are the responsibility of the federal states (Länder), which fund them and set higher-education law. Under the Excellence Strategy, a group of "Universities of Excellence" receives additional federal and state funding. The nine leading technical universities cooperate in the TU9 alliance. Admissions generally require the Abitur school-leaving qualification, and most bachelor's and master's programmes are taught in German, though English-taught programmes are increasingly common, especially at master's level.
