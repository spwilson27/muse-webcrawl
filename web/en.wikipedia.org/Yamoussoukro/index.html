Yamoussoukro is the political capital of Ivory Coast, located about 240 kilometres north-west of Abidjan, the country's economic capital. It was the hometown of Félix Houphouët-Boigny, the first president of Ivory Coast, who transferred the capital there in 1983.

History

Originally a village named N'Gokro, Yamoussoukro was renamed after Queen Yamousso. Under Houphouët-Boigny it was transformed with wide boulevards, a presidential palace, and monumental public buildings, though most government functions remained in Abidjan for many years.

Basilica of Our Lady of Peace

The Basilica of Our Lady of Peace, completed in 1990, is one of the largest churches in the world, modelled on St Peter's Basilica in Rome. Its construction, funded by Houphouët-Boigny, drew international attention and controversy over its cost.

Geography

The city lies in the central part of the country among rolling hills and lakes, including an artificial lake by the presidential palace known for its crocodiles, which are fed daily and regarded as sacred.
