Russo-Turkish War (1787–1792)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Date19 August 1787 – 9 January 1792
LocationEastern Europe, Black Sea, Caucasus
BelligerentsRussian Empire and Habsburg Monarchy vs. Ottoman Empire
ResultRussian victory; Treaty of Jassy

The Russo-Turkish War of 1787–1792 involved the Russian Empire and the Habsburg Monarchy fighting the Ottoman Empire. The war arose from Ottoman determination to recover territories lost under the Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca (1774), above all Crimea, annexed by Catherine the Great in 1783.

Course of the war

The Ottomans declared war in August 1787. Their early attack on Kinburn was repulsed by Alexander Suvorov, while Grigory Potemkin's army besieged and stormed Ochakov in December 1788. Austria entered the war in 1788, capturing Belgrade in 1789, but withdrew after the Treaty of Sistova (1791). Russian victories at Focșani and Rymnik (1789), where Suvorov and Prince Josias of Coburg defeated much larger Ottoman armies, were followed by Suvorov's bloody storming of the fortress of Izmail in December 1790. At sea, Admiral Fyodor Ushakov defeated the Ottoman fleet at Tendra and Cape Kaliakra. In the Caucasus and Anapa campaigns Russia extended its position further.

Treaty of Jassy

The war ended with the Treaty of Jassy, signed on 9 January 1792. The treaty confirmed Russian possession of Crimea and the Ochakov region and advanced the Russian frontier to the Dniester River, consolidating Russia as the dominant power on the northern Black Sea coast.
