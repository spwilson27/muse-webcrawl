Panathenaic amphora

Panathenaic amphorae were large ceramic vessels produced in ancient Athens and awarded as prizes at the Panathenaic Games. Filled with olive oil from the sacred groves of Attica, they were decorated in the black-figure technique long after red-figure had become fashionable elsewhere.

Design

The canonical design shows the goddess Athena on one side and the athletic event for which the prize was given on the other, with an inscription meaning "one of the prizes from Athens".

Survival

Many examples survive, including the famous Burgon vase of about 560 BC, and they provide a dated chronological framework for Greek vase painting.
