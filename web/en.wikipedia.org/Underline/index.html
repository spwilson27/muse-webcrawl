Underline

An underline, also called an underscore in some contexts (though that term properly denotes the character "_"), is a horizontal line drawn immediately below a portion of text. Underlining was historically used on typewriters to emphasize words, because typewriters could not produce bold or italic type: the typist would backspace and overstrike the text with the underscore character.

Uses

In handwritten and typewritten manuscripts, underlining conventionally signals text to be set in italics, such as book titles. In proofreading, different numbers of underlines indicate small capitals, bold type, and other styles. In computing, underlined text on web pages conventionally denotes a hyperlink, and underlining is generally discouraged for emphasis in digital typography in favour of italics or bold.

In other scripts and contexts

In Chinese and Japanese typography, proper names may be marked with a proper-name mark consisting of an underline. In mathematics, underlining can denote vectors. The underscore character itself is widely used in file names and identifiers where spaces are not permitted.
