Day spa

A day spa is a business that provides a variety of services for the purpose of improving health, beauty, and relaxation through personal care treatments such as massages and facials. It is distinguished from a destination spa by the absence of overnight accommodation; guests visit for a day or part of a day and return home.

Services

Typical day spa services include body massage, facials, manicures and pedicures, body wraps and scrubs, aromatherapy, hair removal, and hydrotherapy. Facilities often include saunas, steam rooms, whirlpools, relaxation lounges, and sometimes swimming pools or fitness areas.

Types

Day spas range from small salon-based operations to large resort-style facilities. Medical spas ("medspas") offer treatments such as laser therapy and injectables under physician supervision. Hotel and resort spas provide day services to guests and local patrons.

Industry

The day spa model grew rapidly in North America and Europe from the 1980s onward as wellness services became mainstream consumer offerings. Industry associations set standards for hygiene, licensing of therapists, and professional practice.
