Moro National Liberation Front

The Moro National Liberation Front (MNLF) is a political organisation in the Philippines founded in 1972 by Nur Misuari. It led the Moro separatist movement seeking independence or autonomy for the Muslim-majority Bangsamoro region of Mindanao and the Sulu Archipelago, and signed a Final Peace Agreement with the Philippine government in 1996.

History

The MNLF emerged after the 1968 Jabidah massacre and growing Moro discontent with the government of Ferdinand Marcos. Under Misuari, it waged an armed campaign through the 1970s, leading to the 1976 Tripoli Agreement brokered by Libya. A splinter group, the Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF), broke away in the late 1970s over ideology and strategy.

Peace process

The 1996 agreement created the Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM), with Misuari as governor. Dissident MNLF factions later clashed with the government, notably in the 2013 Zamboanga City crisis. The ARMM was superseded by the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in 2019 following the MILF's own peace deal, in which the MNLF also participates.
