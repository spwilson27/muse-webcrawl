MBDA

MBDA
Industry: defence, aerospace, missiles
Founded: December 2001
Headquarters: Le Plessis-Robinson, France
Owners: Airbus (37.5%), BAE Systems (37.5%), Leonardo (25%)
Employees: more than 13,000

MBDA is a European multinational developer and manufacturer of missiles. It is the only European group capable of designing and producing missile systems across the full range of current and future operational requirements. The company was formed in December 2001 by the merger of the missile businesses of EADS (via Matra BAe Dynamics), BAE Systems, and Finmeccanica (now Leonardo).

Products

Well-known MBDA products include the Meteor beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile, the Storm Shadow / SCALP air-launched cruise missile, the Exocet anti-ship missile, the Aster family of naval and ground-based air-defence missiles used in the SAMP/T and PAAMS systems, the CAMM family, the Brimstone precision strike missile, the ASRAAM short-range air-to-air missile, and the Mistral and MICA families.

Structure

MBDA operates in France, the United Kingdom, Italy, Germany, and Spain, with a subsidiary in the United States. Its shareholding reflects its pan-European origins: Airbus and BAE Systems each hold 37.5 percent and Leonardo holds 25 percent.
