TG4

TG4 (Irish: Teilifís na Gaeilge) is an Irish free-to-air public television channel aimed at speakers of the Irish language. It launched on 31 October 1996 as Teilifís na Gaeilge (TnaG) and rebranded as TG4 in 1999. The channel is operated by a statutory corporation based in Baile na hAbhann, County Galway, in the Gaeltacht, and commissions most of its programming from independent producers.

Programming

TG4 broadcasts a mix of Irish-language drama, documentary, sport, music, children's programmes and news. Its soap opera Ros na Rún has aired since 1996. The channel has won acclaim for its coverage of Gaelic games, rugby and cycling, including the Tour de France, and for documentary strand programming. It broadcasts bilingual content with on-screen subtitles on some programmes.

Funding and governance

The channel is funded by a combination of public funding from the Irish state and commercial advertising revenue. Although it was initially supplied with programming hours by RTÉ, it became a fully independent statutory body in 2007 under the Broadcasting Acts.

Reach

TG4 is available across Ireland on Saorview and cable and satellite platforms, and in Northern Ireland. Its content is also streamed through the TG4 Player. Despite the small population of daily Irish speakers, the channel draws a significant national audience, particularly for sport.
