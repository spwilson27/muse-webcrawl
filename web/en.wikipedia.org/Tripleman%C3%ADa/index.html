Triplemanía is an annual lucha libre event held each year in Mexico by the Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide (AAA) promotion. It is the promotion's flagship event, comparable to WWE's WrestleMania, and traditionally features the climax of long-running feuds, often in lucha de apuestas (betting) matches in which wrestlers wager their masks or hair.

History

The first Triplemanía was held on April 30, 1993, at the Plaza de Toros México in Mexico City, drawing one of the largest crowds in Mexican wrestling history for the retirement match of Cien Caras against Konnan. For several years AAA staged multiple Triplemanía events per year (labelled A, B and C) before settling into a single annual show, later expanded again into multi-city editions.

Notable matches

The events are renowned for high-profile mask-versus-mask and hair-versus-hair matches, cage matches, and the culmination of storylines involving stars such as Rey Mysterio Jr., Dr. Wagner Jr., L.A. Park and Psycho Clown.
