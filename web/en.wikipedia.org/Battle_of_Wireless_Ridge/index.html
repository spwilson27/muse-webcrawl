Battle of Wireless Ridge

Date13–14 June 1982
LocationWireless Ridge, near Stanley, Falkland Islands
ResultBritish victory
BelligerentsUnited Kingdom vs. Argentina
Commanders2 PARA (Lt. Col. David Chaundler) vs. Argentine 7th Infantry Regiment

The Battle of Wireless Ridge was an engagement of the Falklands War fought on the night of 13–14 June 1982 between British and Argentine forces. The 2nd Battalion, Parachute Regiment (2 PARA), supported by armour, artillery and naval gunfire, captured the ridge — one of the ring of hills defending Stanley — in the last major battle before the Argentine surrender.

Wireless Ridge, the last high ground before Stanley.

Background

After the victories at Goose Green, Mount Longdon and Two Sisters, British forces closed on Stanley. Wireless Ridge, a long low ridge north-west of the capital held by the Argentine 7th Infantry Regiment, formed part of the final defensive line. 2 PARA, whose original commanding officer Colonel "H" Jones had been killed at Goose Green, was given the task of taking it, with support from Scimitar and Scorpion light tanks of the Blues and Royals, massed artillery and the guns of HMS Ambuscade.

The battle

The attack went in after dark on 13 June following a heavy bombardment. Unlike the close-quarters fighting at Mount Longdon, the assault combined artillery, armour and infantry in phases along the ridge. Argentine resistance stiffened in places and the defenders counter-attacked briefly, but by dawn 2 PARA held the whole feature. From the ridge the paratroopers could see Argentine troops streaming back into Stanley.

Aftermath

The fall of Wireless Ridge, together with the capture of Mount Tumbledown by the Scots Guards the same night, broke the Stanley defences. White flags appeared over the town, and on 14 June the Argentine commander, General Mario Menéndez, surrendered all Argentine forces in the Falklands. British losses at Wireless Ridge were light compared with earlier battles; Argentine casualties were heavier, and many prisoners were taken.
