Dublin Airport (Irish: Aerfort Bhaile Átha Cliath) is an international airport serving Dublin, the capital of Ireland. It is located about 7 km north of the city in Collinstown, civil parish of Cloghran, County Dublin. It is the largest airport in Ireland and the main international gateway to the island, handling the great majority of Ireland's international air traffic.

History

The airport opened in 1940 with a grass runway at Collinstown, a former Royal Flying Corps station. Its original terminal, designed by Desmond FitzGerald, is now a listed building. Traffic grew steadily with Aer Lingus transatlantic services from 1958, and the airport expanded with new piers and, in 2010, Terminal 2. A second parallel runway, the North Runway, opened in August 2022.

Facilities

The airport has two terminals connected landside. Terminal 1 serves Ryanair and most short-haul carriers, while Terminal 2 is the base of Aer Lingus and hosts United States border preclearance facilities, allowing passengers to clear US immigration before departure.

Traffic

Passenger numbers exceeded 30 million in several pre-2020 years and recovered strongly after the COVID-19 pandemic, setting successive records. A planning-imposed passenger cap of 32 million per year has been the subject of legal and political dispute.
