Gianluigi Buffon (born 1978) is an Italian former professional footballer who played as a goalkeeper and is widely regarded as one of the greatest goalkeepers of all time. Nicknamed "Gigi" and "Superman", he spent most of his career with Juventus, winning ten Serie A titles, and captained Italy to victory at the 2006 FIFA World Cup.

Career

Buffon made his Serie A debut for Parma at age 17 in 1995 and won the UEFA Cup and Coppa Italia with the club in 1999. In 2001 he moved to Juventus for a then-world-record fee for a goalkeeper of about €52 million. With Juventus he won ten league titles (two later revoked in the Calciopoli scandal), five Coppa Italia trophies, and reached three Champions League finals without winning the competition. After a single season with Paris Saint-Germain in 2018–19, winning Ligue 1, he returned to Juventus before finishing his career back at Parma in Serie B, retiring in August 2023 at the age of 45.

International career

Buffon earned 176 caps for Italy, a national record, and played at five World Cups (1998–2014). At the 2006 World Cup in Germany he kept five clean sheets and conceded only two goals — an own goal and a penalty — as Italy won the tournament, earning him the Yashin Award as the tournament's best goalkeeper. He captained Italy to the final of Euro 2012 and retired from international football in 2018 after Italy failed to qualify for the World Cup. He was named the IFFHS World's Best Goalkeeper a record five times and goalkeeper of the decade for 2001–2010.
