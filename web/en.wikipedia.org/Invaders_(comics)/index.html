Invaders (comics)

The Invaders is a fictional superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Roy Thomas and artist Sal Buscema, the team was introduced in Marvel Premiere #29 (1976) and starred in the series The Invaders (1975–1979), set during World War II.

Concept and history

Thomas retroactively united Marvel's Golden Age heroes — Captain America and his sidekick Bucky, the android Human Torch and his partner Toro, and Namor the Sub-Mariner — into a wartime super-team that had supposedly operated during the 1940s fighting Axis villains, though the characters had never actually appeared together in the Golden Age comics. The name was said to have been bestowed by Winston Churchill. Later stories added British heroes Union Jack and Spitfire, among others.

The original series ran for 41 issues. The team has been revived repeatedly: in the New Invaders series of the 2000s, in storylines reuniting the surviving members (a resurrected Jim Hammond, Namor and an ageing Steve Rogers), and in later relaunches dealing with the team's wartime legacy and Namor's conflicts with the surface world.

Legacy

The Invaders are notable as one of the earliest and most successful examples of retroactive continuity in superhero comics, and their adventures established much of the canonical history of the Marvel Universe's Second World War, including Captain America's wartime partnership with Namor and the Human Torch.
