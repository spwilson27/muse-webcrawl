The Marikina River is a river in eastern Metro Manila, Philippines. Rising in the Sierra Madre mountains of Rodriguez, Rizal, it flows southward through the Marikina Valley, passing San Mateo, Marikina and Pasig before joining the Pasig River near the boundary of Pasig and Makati.

Flooding

The river is notorious for severe flooding of the low-lying Marikina Valley during typhoons. During Tropical Storm Ondoy (Ketsana) in September 2009 the river rose to record levels, inundating much of Marikina City and neighbouring areas; flooding recurred during the 2020 Typhoon Ulysses (Vamco). Dredging, river walls and the riverside Marikina River Park form part of continuing flood-mitigation and rehabilitation efforts.
