Glacis

A glacis in military engineering is an artificial slope in front of a fortification, such as a castle or star fort, designed to expose attackers to defensive fire while giving them no cover. The slope is typically kept clear and gently graded so that defenders on the parapet can sweep it with shot along its whole length.

The term derives from the French glacis, related to glace (ice), evoking a smooth exposed surface. In the bastion-fort era the glacis sloped down from the covered way to the surrounding countryside. In geology and geomorphology, "glacis" is also used for a gently sloping erosional or depositional surface at the foot of a slope, similar to a pediment.
