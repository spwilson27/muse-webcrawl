The AK-12 is a Russian assault rifle chambered in 5.45×39mm, designed and manufactured by the Kalashnikov Concern. It is the fifth generation of Kalashnikov rifles and was adopted as the standard service rifle of the Russian Armed Forces as part of the Ratnik infantry modernization program.

Design

The AK-12 retains the long-stroke gas piston system of earlier Kalashnikovs but adds a Picatinny rail dust cover, an adjustable folding stock, improved ergonomics, a two-round burst mode on early versions, and compatibility with modern optics and accessories.

Variants

Related models include the AK-15 in 7.62×39mm, the AK-19 in 5.56×45mm NATO, and compact AK-12K versions. The rifle has seen widespread use in the Russo-Ukrainian War.
