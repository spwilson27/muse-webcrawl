List of longest arch bridge spans

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of the longest arch bridge spans ranks bridges by the length of their main arch span. Arch bridges carry loads primarily in compression through a curved arch to abutments at each end. Modern long-span arches are built of steel or concrete and are common in China, which dominates the upper ranks of this list.

Types

Arch bridges may be deck arches (roadway on top of the arch), through arches (roadway passing between the arch ribs, as at Sydney), or tied arches in which the deck itself resists the arch's outward thrust. Concrete-filled steel tube arches, developed extensively in China, allow very long concrete arches to be erected with lighter falsework.
