Vincent Kennedy McMahon (born 24 August 1945) is an American professional wrestling promoter and businessman. As the longtime chairman and chief executive of WWE, he transformed the regional wrestling business run by his father, Vincent J. McMahon, into a global sports entertainment enterprise, and also performed as an on-screen character, "Mr. McMahon".

Early life

McMahon was born in Pinehurst, North Carolina, and raised in trailer parks in North Carolina before meeting his father, a wrestling promoter, at age 12. He graduated from East Carolina University in 1968 with a business degree and joined his father's Capitol Wrestling Corporation, working as a ring announcer and promoter in Maine.

Building the WWF/WWE

In 1982 McMahon purchased the company from his father and embarked on a national expansion, signing stars such as Hulk Hogan, staging the first WrestleMania in 1985, and taking the company public in 1999. The WWF's "Attitude Era" of the late 1990s won the Monday Night Wars against rival WCW, which McMahon bought in 2001. The company was renamed WWE in 2002 and merged with UFC under TKO Group Holdings in 2023.

On-screen character and controversies

As "Mr. McMahon", he became one of the company's principal villains, feuding with Stone Cold Steve Austin and occasionally wrestling in matches. McMahon stepped down from WWE in 2022 amid board investigations into misconduct allegations, returned as executive chairman in 2023, and resigned from TKO Group in January 2024 following further allegations, which he has denied.
