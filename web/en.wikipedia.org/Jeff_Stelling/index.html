Robert Jeffrey Stelling

Born: 18 March 1955, Hartlepool, County Durham, England

Occupation: Television presenter, journalist

Known for: Soccer Saturday; Countdown

Robert Jeffrey Stelling (born 18 March 1955) is an English television presenter and journalist, best known for presenting Soccer Saturday on Sky Sports from 1994 until 2023, anchoring the live football scores programme with a mixture of statistics, humour and enthusiasm.

Career

Stelling began as a reporter on the Hartlepool Mail and worked in local radio before joining TV-am and later Sky. His stewardship of Soccer Saturday, alongside pundits such as Chris Kamara, made him one of British sports broadcasting's most recognizable figures; the show's cult following inspired the book and stage show built around his catchphrases and score updates. From 2009 to 2011 he also hosted the Channel 4 gameshow Countdown.

Charity work and honours

A lifelong supporter of Hartlepool United, whose honorary president he became, Stelling completed a series of marathon walks — notably "Jeff's March for Men" — raising over £1 million for Prostate Cancer UK. After leaving Sky in 2023 he joined talkSPORT as a breakfast show host.
