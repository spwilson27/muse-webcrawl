Oxford University Boat Club

Founded: 1829 (traditionally)

Location: River Thames (Isis), Oxford

Colours: Dark blue

Rivals: Cambridge University Boat Club

Oxford University Boat Club (OUBC) is the rowing club of the University of Oxford, responsible for selecting and training the crew that represents Oxford in the annual Boat Race against the University of Cambridge. Its crews race in dark blue. The club's home water is the stretch of the River Thames known in Oxford as the Isis, and it trains from a boathouse and gym at Wallingford.

History

Rowing at Oxford traces its organised beginnings to the challenge that produced the first Boat Race in 1829, when a Cambridge crew of former Westminster schoolboys challenged Oxford to race at Henley-on-Thames; Oxford won easily in the boat Eclipse. The race became an annual London fixture from 1856, rowed over the Championship Course of 4 miles 374 yards (6.8 km) from Putney to Mortlake. OUBC was formally constituted as the university club coordinating the colleges' rowing and the Dark Blue crew.

The club has produced numerous Olympic rowers, including Matthew Pinsent, who won the Boat Race with Oxford before his four Olympic gold medals. In 1987 an "American mutiny" split the squad when several US internationals rebelled against the coaching regime; Oxford nonetheless won that year's race with a depleted crew.

Crews and races

OUBC fields the first eight, known simply as the Blue Boat, and a reserve crew, Isis, which races Cambridge's reserves (Goldie) on Boat Race day. The women's counterpart is Oxford University Women's Boat Club; since 2015 the Women's Boat Race has been held on the same day and course as the men's, and the openweight and lightweight clubs have progressively merged their administration. Lightweight men's crews race as OULRC against Cambridge at Henley. College rowing, separate from OUBC but feeding it talent, centres on the Torpids and Summer Eights bumps races.

Facilities

After fire destroyed facilities, Oxford's university crews train at the Fleming Boathouse at Wallingford, opened in 2007, and use the university gym in Oxford. Trial eights in December — traditionally named crews racing each other over the full course — serve as the final selection contest before the March fixture.
