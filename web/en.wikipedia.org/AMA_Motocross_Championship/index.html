The AMA Motocross Championship, commonly called the Pro Motocross Championship, is an American motorcycle racing series sanctioned by the American Motorcyclist Association (AMA). Founded in 1972, it is the premier outdoor motocross series in the United States.

Format

The championship is contested over a summer schedule of rounds at outdoor natural-terrain tracks such as Hangtown, High Point, RedBud, and Unadilla. Each event consists of two motos per class, with combined results determining overall standings. Classes are the premier 450cc class and the 250cc class.

History

Early champions included Gary Jones and Tony DiStefano; Ricky Carmichael later set records with ten consecutive titles across classes. Since 2023 the series has formed part of the SuperMotocross World Championship playoff structure with AMA Supercross.
