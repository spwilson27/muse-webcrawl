Lujiazui


Illustration: Lujiazui

Lujiazui is a locality in Pudong, Shanghai, on a peninsula formed by a bend in the Huangpu River. Since the 1990s it has been developed as China's leading financial district, facing the Bund across the river.

Skyline

Lujiazui contains many of China's most recognisable skyscrapers: the Oriental Pearl Tower, the Jin Mao Tower, the Shanghai World Financial Center and the Shanghai Tower, the tallest building in China.

History

The name, meaning "Lu's mouth", refers to the family of the Ming-dynasty scholar Lu Shen and the river-mouth shape of the peninsula. Until its designation as a finance and trade zone in 1990, the area consisted largely of warehouses, factories and housing.
