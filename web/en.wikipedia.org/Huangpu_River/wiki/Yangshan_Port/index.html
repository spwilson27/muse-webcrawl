Yangshan Port

Yangshan Port (Yangshan Deep-Water Port) is a deep-water seaport for container ships in Hangzhou Bay south of Shanghai, China. Built on the islands of Greater and Lesser Yangshan — which belong administratively to Zhejiang province but are administered by Shanghai — the port forms the deep-water component of the Port of Shanghai, the world's busiest container port.

Construction

The port was built because Shanghai's existing terminals on the shallow Huangpu and Yangtze estuaries could not accommodate the largest container vessels. Its phases opened between 2005 and 2017 on reclaimed land, and it is connected to the mainland by the 32.5-kilometre Donghai Bridge, one of the longest sea bridges in the world.

Automation

Phase IV, opened in December 2017, is the world's largest automated container terminal, using remotely controlled ship-to-shore cranes, automated guided vehicles, and automated stacking cranes, with capacity for several million TEU annually.
