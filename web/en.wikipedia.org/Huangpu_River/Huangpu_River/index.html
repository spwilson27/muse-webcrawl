Huangpu River


Illustration: Huangpu River

The Huangpu River is a 113 km-long river flowing through Shanghai, China, and a major tributary of the Yangtze River near its estuary. It divides the city between Pudong to the east and Puxi to the west.

Geography

The river originates at Dianshan Lake and flows past the Bund, the historic waterfront of colonial-era banks and trading houses, before joining the Yangtze at Wusongkou. Its banks include Lujiazui, the financial district facing the Bund.

Importance

The Huangpu is the principal source of drinking water for Shanghai and a busy shipping channel serving one of the world's largest container ports. Crossings include the Nanpu and Yangpu cable-stayed bridges and numerous tunnels.
