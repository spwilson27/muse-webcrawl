Shanghai Tower


Illustration: Shanghai Tower

The Shanghai Tower is a 632-metre (2,073 ft) megatall skyscraper in Lujiazui, Pudong, Shanghai. Completed in 2015, it is the tallest building in China and was, on completion, the second-tallest building in the world after Dubai's Burj Khalifa.

Design

Designed by the American firm Gensler, the tower has a cylindrical inner form wrapped in a triangular glass façade that twists roughly 120 degrees as it rises, reducing wind loads. Its double-skin façade creates nine vertical "neighbourhoods" of sky lobbies.

Features

The tower houses offices, a hotel and observation decks near its summit, served by some of the world's fastest elevators. It stands beside the Jin Mao Tower and Shanghai World Financial Center, completing a trio of supertall towers overlooking the Huangpu River.
