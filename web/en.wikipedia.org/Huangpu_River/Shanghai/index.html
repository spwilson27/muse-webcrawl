Shanghai


Illustration: Shanghai

Shanghai is a direct-administered municipality and the most populous urban area in China, with a population of around 29 million. Located on the southern estuary of the Yangtze River, it is divided by the Huangpu River into Puxi and Pudong.

History

Shanghai grew from a fishing village into a major port after becoming a treaty port following the First Opium War in 1842. In the early 20th century it was one of Asia's great commercial and cultural cities. Since the economic reforms of the 1990s it has re-emerged as a global financial centre, symbolised by the skyline of Lujiazui.

Economy and transport

The Port of Shanghai is the world's busiest container port. The city hosts the Shanghai Stock Exchange, a maglev line to Pudong International Airport, and one of the largest metro systems in the world.
