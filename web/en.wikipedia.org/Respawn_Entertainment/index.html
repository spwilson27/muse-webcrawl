Respawn Entertainment, LLC is an American video game development studio based in Sherman Oaks, Los Angeles, California. It was founded in 2010 by Jason West and Vince Zampella, formerly of Infinity Ward, the studio responsible for much of the Call of Duty series. The studio was acquired by Electronic Arts in 2017.

History

After a dispute with Activision led to the departure of West and Zampella from Infinity Ward in 2010, the pair founded Respawn and signed a publishing deal with Electronic Arts. The studio's first game, the mech-based first-person shooter Titanfall, was released in 2014, followed by Titanfall 2 in 2016, whose single-player campaign was widely praised.

Games

The studio developed the free-to-play battle royale game Apex Legends, released without prior announcement in February 2019, which became one of the most played games in the genre. Respawn also developed the action-adventure games Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order (2019) and Star Wars Jedi: Survivor (2023). A second studio in Vancouver and an additional location in Wisconsin support its projects.
