Roger W. Smith (born 1970) is a British independent watchmaker based on the Isle of Man. A protégé of the celebrated horologist George Daniels, Smith is one of the few watchmakers who produce watches almost entirely by hand using traditional methods, making only around ten watches per year.

Career

Smith trained at the Manchester School of Horology and, after being rejected on his first approach to Daniels, spent years building a pocket watch by hand to prove his ability; Daniels accepted him as an apprentice in the late 1990s. The two collaborated on the Daniels Millennium series. Smith later founded his own workshop, producing the Series 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 watches, and co-developed the co-axial escapement's industrial application legacy through his mentor's work. He is a Fellow of the British Horological Institute and has been recognised for keeping traditional English watchmaking alive.
