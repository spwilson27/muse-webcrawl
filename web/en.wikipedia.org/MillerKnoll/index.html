MillerKnoll

MillerKnoll, Inc. is an American company that produces office systems, textiles, and home furnishings, headquartered in Zeeland, Michigan. It was formed in 2021 through the merger of Herman Miller and Knoll, two of the most influential names in modernist furniture design.

History

Herman Miller, founded in 1905 as the Star Furniture Company and renamed in 1923, became famous for mid-century modern designs by George Nelson, Charles and Ray Eames, and Isamu Noguchi, and for the Aeron chair. Knoll, founded in 1938 by Hans Knoll, produced designs by Florence Knoll, Mies van der Rohe, and Eero Saarinen.

Brands

MillerKnoll's collective of brands includes Herman Miller, Knoll, Design Within Reach, Muuto, HAY, Geiger, and Maharam, serving both contract (workplace) and retail markets.
