The Cessna Citation is a family of business jets produced by Cessna (now Textron Aviation) since 1969. The Citation line spans light jets such as the Mustang and CJ series to midsize and super-midsize models including the Latitude, Longitude and the high-speed Citation X.

History

The original Citation I pioneered the light business jet category with straight wings and modest speed, prioritising simple operation from short runways. The family grew through the Citation II/Bravo, the Excel/XLS series — among the best-selling business jets ever — and the Sovereign. The Citation X, certified in 1996, was the fastest civilian aircraft in service at the time, cruising at Mach 0.92.

Models

Current production models include the Citation M2, CJ3+ and CJ4 light jets, the Latitude and the flagship Longitude. Military variants include the UC-35 and OT-47 used by the United States armed forces.
