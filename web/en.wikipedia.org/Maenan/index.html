Maenan

Maenan is a village and area in the Conwy Valley in Conwy County Borough, north Wales, situated near the River Conwy between Llanrwst and Dolgarrog. It is best known as the second site of Aberconwy Abbey and for Maenan Abbey Hotel, which stands on the abbey lands.

History

The Cistercian monks of Aberconwy Abbey, originally founded at Rhedynog Felen near Conwy, moved to Maenan in 1283 after the Edwardian conquest of Wales, when Edward I required their original site for Conwy Castle and its walled town. The abbey at Maenan flourished until its dissolution under Henry VIII in the 1530s. The site later became a private estate; the present Maenan Abbey Hotel occupies a house built over and near the abbey remains.

Geography

Maenan lies on the eastern side of the Conwy valley on the A470 road, in a rural landscape of farmland and woodland beneath the Carneddau mountains.
