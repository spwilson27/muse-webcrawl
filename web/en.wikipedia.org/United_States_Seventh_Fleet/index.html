United States Seventh Fleet

The United States Seventh Fleet is a numbered fleet of the United States Navy, forward deployed to Yokosuka, Japan. It is the Navy's largest forward-deployed fleet, responsible for operations in the western Pacific and eastern Indian Oceans under the U.S. Pacific Fleet.

Area of operations

The Seventh Fleet's area covers some 48 million square miles — from the International Date Line to the India–Pakistan border and from the Kuril Islands to Antarctica — encompassing 36 maritime nations and more than half the world's population.

History

Formed in Australia in 1943, the fleet supported operations in New Guinea and the Philippines, landed occupation forces in Japan, fought in the Korean War (including the Inchon landing), and conducted combat operations throughout the Vietnam War. Since 1950 it has been based in Japan.

Today

Its forces include a forward-deployed carrier strike group centered on Yokosuka, an amphibious ready group at Sasebo, submarines at Guam, and patrol and logistics forces across the region, conducting exercises with allies such as Japan, South Korea and Australia.
