Haas Automation

Haas Automation, Inc. is an American machine tool builder headquartered in Oxnard, California. The company designs and manufactures a line of lower-cost computer numerical control (CNC) machine tools and rotary products, including vertical and horizontal machining centers, CNC lathes, and rotary tables.

History

Haas Automation was founded in 1983 by Gene Haas in Sun Valley, California, to produce economical and reliable machine tools. Its first product was a fully programmable 5C collet indexer, introduced at the Westec trade show in 1983. In 1988 the company introduced the VF-1, its first vertical machining center, priced to compete on affordability; the machine became the foundation of the VF series that remains central to the product line.

In 1997 Haas moved into a purpose-built facility in Oxnard, which has since grown into one of the largest machine tool production sites in the United States. Haas machines are notable for using a proprietary control designed and manufactured in-house alongside the machines themselves. The company sells through a global network of factory outlet stores.

Motorsport

Gene Haas has used the company to sponsor motorsport extensively, including NASCAR teams (Stewart-Haas Racing) and the Haas F1 Team, which entered Formula One in 2016 as the first American-led team in the championship in three decades.
