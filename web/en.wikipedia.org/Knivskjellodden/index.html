Knivskjellodden

Knivskjellodden is a cape on the island of Magerøya in Nordkapp Municipality, Finnmark county, Norway. At latitude 71°11′08″ N it is the northernmost point of mainland Europe (the island being connected to the mainland by tunnel), lying slightly farther north than the more famous North Cape.

LocationMagerøya, Finnmark, Norway
Coordinates71°11′08″ N
ClaimNorthernmost point of mainland Europe

Description

Unlike the nearby North Cape, which has a visitor centre and is reachable by road, Knivskjellodden is reached only by a marked hiking trail of about 9 kilometres from a roadside car park. A logbook is kept at the cape for hikers who reach it.
