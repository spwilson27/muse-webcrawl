Elections in the Republic of Ireland

Elections in the Republic of Ireland are conducted under the Constitution of Ireland and electoral law. The principal elections are general elections to Dáil Éireann, elections to Seanad Éireann, presidential elections, local elections, and elections to the European Parliament.

Proportional representation by single transferable vote is used for Dáil, local, and European elections. The Dáil has a maximum term of five years and is elected from multi-member constituencies. Fine Gael, Fianna Fáil, and Sinn Féin have been the largest parties in recent elections.

Referendums are required for constitutional amendments and are decided by simple majority. Citizens aged 18 and over resident in the state may vote in Dáil elections; presidential voting is restricted to Irish citizens.
