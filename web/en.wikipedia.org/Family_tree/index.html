Family tree

A family tree, also called a genealogy or pedigree chart, is a chart representing family relationships in a conventional tree structure. More detailed family trees, as used in medicine and social work, are known as genograms.

Representations

Genealogical data can be represented in several formats; the most common charts show ancestors (an ahnentafel, listing ancestors by generation) or descendants of a common progenitor. Conventions typically place the subject at the top or left, with parents, children, and marriages arranged by generation. Given names, dates, and places of birth, marriage, and death are commonly included.

Uses

Family trees are used in genealogy and family history research, in medicine to assess hereditary disease risk, in law for inheritance and succession, and in many cultures to record lineage and clan membership. Digital databases and DNA testing have transformed genealogical research in recent decades.
