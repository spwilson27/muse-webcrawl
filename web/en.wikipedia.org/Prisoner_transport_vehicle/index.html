Prisoner transport vehicle

A prisoner transport vehicle is a specially designed or converted vehicle used to move prisoners between jails, courts, prisons, and other secure locations. Such vehicles range from modified police vans to purpose-built buses with individual cells.

Design

Transport vehicles feature secure compartments separated from the driver's cab, barred or mesh-covered windows, interior surfaces resistant to tampering, and restraints such as bench handcuffs or full cell blocks. Larger versions used for inter-facility transfers can hold dozens of prisoners in separate compartments segregated by classification. Ventilation and safety standards for these compartments have been subjects of legal scrutiny and reform.

Terminology

In the United States, older slang called police transport vans "paddy wagons," a term of disputed origin now considered dated. In the United Kingdom, "prison van" is the usual term; in Australia, divisional vans are nicknamed "divvy vans."
