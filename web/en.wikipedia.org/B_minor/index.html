B minor

B minor is a minor scale based on B, consisting of the pitches B, C♯, D, E, F♯, G and A. Its key signature has two sharps, F♯ and C♯, and its relative major is D major. The natural minor is often altered in practice: the harmonic minor raises A to A♯, and the melodic minor raises G and A when ascending.

Character

Commentators have long associated B minor with quiet resignation and melancholy; Christian Friedrich Daniel Schubart described it as a key of patience and calm awaiting one's fate. It is a comfortable key for the violin family and for the guitar, whose open strings fit B minor chords readily.

Notable works

Famous compositions in B minor include Bach's Mass in B minor and the Badinerie from his Orchestral Suite No. 2, Chopin's Piano Sonata-like Scherzo No. 1 and his Prelude Op. 28 No. 6, Liszt's Piano Sonata, and Tchaikovsky's Sixth Symphony, the Pathétique.
