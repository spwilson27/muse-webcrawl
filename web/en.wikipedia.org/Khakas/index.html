Khakas

The Khakas are a Turkic Indigenous people of Siberia, living mainly in the Republic of Khakassia in southern Siberia, Russia. They number about 75,000 and speak the Khakas language, a Turkic language written in Cyrillic.

History

The Khakas formed from Yenisei Kyrgyz and other groups of the Minusinsk Basin. Traditionally cattle and horse herders with shamanist beliefs, they were incorporated into the Russian Empire in the 18th century.
