Charles Marc Hervé Perceval Leclerc (born 16 October 1997) is a Monégasque racing driver who competes in Formula One for Scuderia Ferrari. He won the GP3 Series championship in 2016 and the FIA Formula 2 Championship in 2017.

Career

Leclerc began karting in 2005 and rose through the junior formulae with the backing of Jules Bianchi's family and later the Ferrari Driver Academy. After winning Formula 2 in his rookie season, he made his Formula One debut with Sauber in 2018, impressing enough to be promoted to Ferrari for 2019. At Spa-Francorchamps that year he took his maiden Grand Prix victory, emotionally dedicated to his friend Anthoine Hubert, killed in a Formula 2 crash the previous day; he won again at Monza a week later.

Driving and reputation

Renowned for qualifying pace and a haul of pole positions, Leclerc has been a central figure in Ferrari's campaigns through the 2020s, winning multiple Grands Prix including his home race at Monaco in 2024, when he became the first Monégasque to win the Monaco Grand Prix in the Formula One era.

Personal life

Leclerc's father Hervé, a former racing driver, died in 2017, days before Leclerc won the Formula 2 feature race in Baku. His younger brother Arthur is also a racing driver.
