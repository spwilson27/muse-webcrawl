Titanic (1997 film)

Titanic is a 1997 American epic romantic disaster film directed, written, co-produced and co-edited by James Cameron. Incorporating both historical and fictionalised aspects, it is based on accounts of the sinking of the RMS Titanic in 1912. The film stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Jack Dawson and Kate Winslet as Rose DeWitt Bukater, members of different social classes who fall in love aboard the ship during its ill-fated maiden voyage.

Production

Cameron's interest in the shipwreck inspired the film, for which he undertook deep-sea expeditions to the wreck site. Principal photography took place largely at Baja Studios in Mexico, where a nearly full-scale replica of the ship was built. With a budget of about $200 million it was the most expensive film ever made at the time.

Reception and legacy

Titanic was released on 19 December 1997 and became a phenomenal success. It was the first film to gross over $1 billion worldwide and remained the highest-grossing film of all time until Cameron's Avatar surpassed it in 2010; its totals exceed $2.2 billion with re-releases. It won 11 Academy Awards, tying Ben-Hur for the most Oscars won by a single film, including Best Picture and Best Director. Its theme song, Celine Dion's "My Heart Will Go On", became one of the best-selling singles of all time.
