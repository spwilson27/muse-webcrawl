Martin-Baker

Martin-Baker Aircraft Co. Ltd. is a British manufacturer of ejection seats and safety-related equipment for aviation. The company was originally founded as Martin's Aircraft Works by James Martin; it became known as Martin-Baker after the death of co-founder Captain Valentine Baker in a 1942 aircraft crash.

History

The company began investigating ejection seats during the Second World War. Its first live ejection test took place on 24 July 1946, when Bernard Lynch ejected from a Gloster Meteor. Martin-Baker seats have since been fitted to many military aircraft worldwide.

Ejection seats

Martin-Baker has supplied ejection seats to air forces around the world, and the company maintains a counter of lives saved by its seats, which numbers in the thousands. Owners of Martin-Baker seats who eject successfully become members of the Ejection Tie Club. The company is based at Higher Denham, Buckinghamshire, England.
