Blair Witch is a 2016 American found-footage supernatural horror film directed by Adam Wingard and written by Simon Barrett. It is the third film in the Blair Witch franchise and a direct sequel to The Blair Witch Project (1999), ignoring the events of the 2000 sequel Book of Shadows.

Plot

James Donahue, brother of Heather Donahue from the first film, finds a video online that he believes shows his sister still alive in the woods near Burkittsville, Maryland. He and a group of friends, accompanied by two local guides, venture into the Black Hills Forest with cameras and a drone to find the house from the original footage — and encounter the same supernatural force.

Production and release

The film was produced in secrecy under the title The Woods by Lionsgate and Vertigo Entertainment, and its identity was revealed at San Diego Comic-Con in July 2016. It premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and was released theatrically in September 2016.

Reception

The film grossed about $45 million worldwide against a budget of roughly $5 million, but received mixed reviews and was broadly considered inferior to the original, with praise for some set pieces and criticism for its reliance on jump scares.
