Adrenal gland

The adrenal glands (suprarenal glands) are endocrine glands situated above the kidneys. They produce hormones that regulate metabolism, blood pressure, immune function and the response to stress.

Structure

Each gland has an outer cortex and inner medulla. The cortex has three layers — zona glomerulosa, zona fasciculata and zona reticularis — while the medulla contains chromaffin cells.

Hormones

The cortex secretes mineralocorticoids such as aldosterone, glucocorticoids such as cortisol, and adrenal androgens. The medulla secretes catecholamines, mainly adrenaline and noradrenaline.

Disorders

Important disorders include Addison's disease, Cushing syndrome, primary aldosteronism and phaeochromocytoma. See also Adrenaline, Cortisol and Endocrine system.
