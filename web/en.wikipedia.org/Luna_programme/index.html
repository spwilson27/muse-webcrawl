Luna programme

The Luna programme was a series of robotic spacecraft missions sent to the Moon by the Soviet Union between 1959 and 1976. Fifteen were successful, each designated with a sequential Luna number and many achieving historic firsts.

Achievements

Luna 1 (1959) was the first spacecraft to reach the vicinity of the Moon; Luna 2 first impacted the surface; Luna 3 photographed the far side; Luna 9 made the first soft landing (1966); Luna 10 became the first lunar orbiter. Later missions landed Lunokhod rovers (Luna 17 and 21) and returned lunar soil samples to Earth (Luna 16, 20 and 24). Russia revived the name with Luna 25 in 2023, which crashed into the Moon.
