Crimson

Crimson is a rich, deep red colour inclining to purple. The name derives from the kermes insect (Kermes vermilio), from which a red dye was produced in ancient and medieval times; the word entered English through Old Spanish and Arabic (qirmiz).

History

Kermes dye, harvested from scale insects living on the kermes oak around the Mediterranean, was prized from antiquity and was one of the most valuable red dyes before cochineal from the Americas largely replaced it in the 16th century. Crimson cloth signified wealth and status and was used for royal and ecclesiastical garments.

Usage

Crimson is the institutional colour of several universities, including Harvard University, whose teams are the Harvard Crimson, and the University of Alabama (the Crimson Tide). Alizarin crimson is a well-known artists' pigment.
