IceCube

IceCube may refer to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, a particle detector at the Amundsen–Scott South Pole Station in Antarctica. The name also evokes a cube of ice in everyday usage. This article concerns the observatory.

Overview

The IceCube Neutrino Observatory instruments a cubic kilometre of clear Antarctic ice with 5,160 optical sensors suspended on 86 strings at depths of 1,450 to 2,450 metres. Completed in December 2010, it detects Cherenkov light from charged particles produced when high-energy neutrinos interact with the ice.
