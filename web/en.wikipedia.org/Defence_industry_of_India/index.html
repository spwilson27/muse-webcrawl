Defence industry of India

The defence industry of India comprises the public and private sector organisations that design and manufacture military equipment for the Indian Armed Forces. India has historically been one of the world's largest importers of arms, and recent policy has emphasised indigenous production under the "Make in India" and Atmanirbhar Bharat (self-reliant India) programmes.

Structure

The sector is led by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) for research, and by defence public sector undertakings including Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (aircraft), Bharat Electronics, Bharat Dynamics, Mazagon Dock and Garden Reach (shipbuilding). The former Ordnance Factory Board was corporatised in 2021 into seven new companies. Private firms such as Tata, Larsen & Toubro, Mahindra and Bharat Forge have taken growing roles, and foreign direct investment limits were raised to encourage joint ventures.

Programmes and exports

Major indigenous programmes include the Tejas light combat aircraft, Arjun tank, Agni and Prithvi missile families, BrahMos supersonic cruise missile (a joint venture with Russia), INS Vikrant aircraft carrier and Arihant-class nuclear submarines. India has set export targets and supplied equipment including patrol vessels, helicopters and artillery to partners in Asia and Africa, while remaining a major buyer from Russia, France, Israel and the United States.
