Château

Type: Manor house or palace

Origin: France

A château is a French manor house, castle, or palace, often the residence of a lord or noble family and the centre of an estate. The word derives from Latin castellum and covers buildings from fortified medieval strongholds to elegant Renaissance and classical country houses.

History

Early châteaux were defensive, but as royal power grew and warfare changed, many were rebuilt for comfort, display, gardens, and hunting. The Loire Valley is famous for Renaissance châteaux such as Chambord and Chenonceau.

Wine usage

In Bordeaux and other wine regions, château often denotes a wine estate, whether or not a grand house stands on it. The name forms part of many celebrated wine brands.
