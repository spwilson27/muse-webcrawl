Rough Collie

The Rough Collie (also known as the Long-Haired Collie) is a long-coated herding dog breed that originated in Scotland. It is distinguished by its long, flowing double coat, wedge-shaped head, and semi-pricked ears. The Rough Collie and the short-coated Smooth Collie are considered the same breed in some countries and separate breeds in others. The breed became world-famous as the fictional dog Lassie.

History

Collies descend from herding dogs used for centuries by shepherds in Scotland and northern England. The name may derive from the Scots word for coal ("coll") or from the black-faced Colley sheep they tended. Queen Victoria became an enthusiast of the breed after encountering them at Balmoral in the 1860s, which helped transform the collie from a working farm dog into a fashionable show and companion dog. Collies were imported to the United States in large numbers in the late 19th century.

Appearance

Rough Collies stand about 56–66 cm (22–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh roughly 18–29 kg (40–65 lb). The abundant double coat is harsh on the outside and soft and dense underneath, forming a mane around the neck. Recognised colours include sable and white, tricolour (black, white, and tan), blue merle, and white with coloured markings. The expression is considered a hallmark of the breed — sweet and intelligent, with almond-shaped eyes.

Temperament

Rough Collies are intelligent, loyal, and gentle, generally good with children and responsive to training. They retain herding instincts and can be vocal. They are sensitive dogs that thrive on companionship and moderate exercise, and their heavy coat requires regular brushing to prevent matting.

Health

The breed is predisposed to certain inherited conditions, including Collie eye anomaly (CEA), progressive retinal atrophy, and a mutation of the MDR1 gene that causes sensitivity to certain drugs such as ivermectin. Responsible breeders screen for these. Average life expectancy is about 12–14 years.

In popular culture

Eric Knight's 1940 novel Lassie Come-Home and the ensuing films and television series made the Rough Collie one of the most recognisable dogs in the world; all the dogs that played Lassie on screen were male Rough Collies.
