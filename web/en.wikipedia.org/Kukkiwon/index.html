Kukkiwon

Kukkiwon
Also known as: World Taekwondo Headquarters
Founded: 1972
Location: Gangnam, Seoul, South Korea

Kukkiwon (Korean: 국기원; Hanja: 國技院), also known as the World Taekwondo Headquarters, is the official governing organisation for taekwondo dan certification, established by the South Korean government.

History and role

Founded in 1972 in the Gangnam District of Seoul, Kukkiwon oversees the standardisation of taekwondo technique, forms (poomsae) and terminology, conducts instructor and examiner courses, and issues the dan and poom certificates recognised by World Taekwondo for Olympic-style taekwondo.

Its demonstration team promotes taekwondo worldwide, and its complex includes a museum and competition hall. Kukkiwon was designated by Korea's national sports legislation as the central body for the "national martial art".
