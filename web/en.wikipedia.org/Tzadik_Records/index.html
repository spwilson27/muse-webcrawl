Tzadik Records is a record label in New York City founded in 1995 by composer and saxophonist John Zorn. Tzadik is a non-profit label dedicated to releasing avant-garde and experimental music, much of it connected with the downtown New York scene and with what Zorn calls "Radical Jewish Culture".

The label's catalogue is organized into series, including the Composer Series (contemporary classical works), the Radical Jewish Culture series, New Japan, Film Music, Key Series, and the Archival Series devoted to Zorn's own work, including Masada and Naked City related releases. Tzadik releases are known for consistent, high-quality packaging, often designed with artwork by independent artists, and the label pays no advances but returns all profits on sales directly to the artists after costs.

Artists released on Tzadik include Marc Ribot, Fred Frith, Ikue Mori, Bill Laswell, Wadada Leo Smith, and many others.
