Evil twin
TypeStock character / narrative trope
Related conceptsDoppelgänger, alter ego

The evil twin is a stock character and plot device in fiction: an identical twin or exact duplicate of a character who embodies that character's dark, immoral or destructive qualities. The concept is closely related to the doppelgänger of folklore and to stories of lookalikes and impostors.

In fiction

Evil twins are especially common in soap operas, comic books and science fiction, where a duplicate may arise through birth, cloning, parallel universes or supernatural means. Classic examples include the mirror-universe counterparts in Star Trek, and numerous mistaken-identity plots in serialized melodrama, where the evil twin schemes to take over the protagonist's life.

Themes

The trope dramatises the conflict between the good and bad sides of a single personality, allowing a character's inner struggle to be played out between two people. It also plays on fears of identity theft and the uncanny effect of meeting one's double.

Other uses

In computer security, an "evil twin" is a fraudulent wireless access point that mimics a legitimate one in order to intercept users' communications.
