The Third United States Army is a field army of the United States Army, best known for its campaigns in northwestern Europe in 1944–45 under General George S. Patton. Since 1982 it has served as the Army component of U.S. Central Command (ARCENT), headquartered at Shaw Air Force Base, South Carolina, with a forward headquarters in Kuwait.

Second World War

Activated in England in 1944, Third Army became operational in France on 1 August 1944 and raced across France after the Normandy breakout. It fought through the Lorraine campaign, relieved Bastogne during the Battle of the Bulge by wheeling north in winter conditions, crossed the Rhine in March 1945, and ended the war in Czechoslovakia and Austria.

Modern role

As ARCENT, Third Army commanded ground forces in Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm (1990–91) and served as the Coalition Forces Land Component Command during the 2003 invasion of Iraq and subsequent operations across the Middle East and Central Asia.
