Clarence Leonard "Kelly" Johnson (27 February 1910 – 21 December 1990) was an American aeronautical and systems engineer. He founded Lockheed's Skunk Works advanced development division and is credited with designing more than 40 aircraft, including the P-38 Lightning, the U-2 spy plane, the SR-71 Blackbird and the F-104 Starfighter.

Skunk Works

In 1943 Johnson's small team built the XP-80 jet fighter in 143 days, establishing the Skunk Works model of small, autonomous, highly trusted engineering teams. His "14 Rules of Management" became famous in the aerospace industry, along with the maxim "be quick, be quiet, and be on time".

Honours

Johnson received two Collier Trophies and was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 1964 and the National Medal of Science in 1983.
