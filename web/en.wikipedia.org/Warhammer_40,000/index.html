Warhammer 40,000 is a miniature wargame produced by Games Workshop. It is the most popular miniature wargame in the world and is particularly popular in the United Kingdom. First published in 1987 as Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader, the game has been revised through successive editions — its tenth edition was released in 2023. Players assemble and paint miniature models representing futuristic soldiers, creatures, and vehicles, and wage tabletop battles using dice, tape measures, and terrain.

Setting

The game is set in a dystopian science-fantasy universe in the 41st and early 42nd millennia. Humanity's vast Imperium of Man, ruled in name by the god-like Emperor entombed on Terra, is beset by alien species (such as Orks, Aeldari, T'au, Necrons, and Tyranids) and by the daemonic forces of Chaos spilling from the Warp, a parallel dimension of psychic energy. The setting is renowned for its deliberately bleak "grimdark" tone — a term taken from the game's own tagline.

Media and spin-offs

The franchise has expanded into an enormous body of fiction published by Black Library (including the long-running Horus Heresy series), dozens of video games such as Dawn of War, Space Marine, and Darktide, board games, role-playing games, and animated productions. A live-action project with Amazon Studios was announced in 2022.
