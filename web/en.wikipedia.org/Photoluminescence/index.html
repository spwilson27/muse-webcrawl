Photoluminescence

Photoluminescence (abbreviated as PL) is light emission from any form of matter after the absorption of photons. It is one form of luminescence and is initiated by photoexcitation, hence the prefix photo-.

Forms

Following excitation, various relaxation processes occur in which other photons are re-radiated. If the emission occurs quickly (about 10−8 seconds), it is called fluorescence; slower emission involving a change of electron spin state is called phosphorescence.

Applications

Photoluminescence spectroscopy is a widely used, non-destructive technique for characterising the electronic properties of semiconductors, and is applied in materials science, solar-cell research and the study of quantum dots and organic light-emitting diodes.
