Olaf Scholz

Olaf Scholz (born 14 June 1958) is a German politician of the Social Democratic Party (SPD) who served as Chancellor of Germany from December 2021 to May 2025, succeeding Angela Merkel. A lawyer by training, he previously served as First Mayor of Hamburg (2011–2018), Federal Minister of Labour and Social Affairs (2007–2009), and, under Merkel, Vice-Chancellor and Federal Minister of Finance (2018–2021).

Chancellorship

Scholz led the SPD to first place in the 2021 federal election and formed a "traffic light" coalition with the Greens and the Free Democrats. His government confronted the energy and security crisis following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022, announcing a Zeitenwende ("turning point") with a €100 billion defence fund and major increases in military spending, while accelerating Germany's shift away from Russian gas. The coalition collapsed in November 2024 amid disputes over fiscal policy; Scholz lost a confidence vote, and after the February 2025 election he was succeeded by Friedrich Merz.
