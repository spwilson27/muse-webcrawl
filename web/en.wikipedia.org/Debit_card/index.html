A debit card (also known as a bank card or check card) is a payment card that can be used in place of cash to make purchases. The card usually consists of the bank's name, a card number, the cardholder's name, and an expiration date on the front with a magnetic stripe and signature on the back. Unlike a credit card, the money for the purchase is transferred from the cardholder's bank account in real time (or close to it) instead of the cardholder repaying the money at a later date.

History

Debit cards developed out of ATM cards in the late twentieth century as banks and card networks extended point-of-sale acceptance. In the United States, regional ATM networks such as NYCE and Interlink enabled PIN-based purchases in the 1980s and 1990s, while Visa and Mastercard introduced signature-based "check cards" that could be used anywhere their credit cards were accepted. Chip (EMV) and contactless technology have since become standard in most markets.

Types of debit

Online (PIN) debit transactions are authorised and settle almost immediately against available funds. Offline (signature) debit transactions are processed through the credit card networks and may take days to post, creating a risk of overdrafts. Prepaid and stored-value cards function similarly but are loaded in advance rather than tied to a conventional deposit account.

Fees and consumer protection

Merchants pay interchange and processing fees for debit acceptance, which regulators have capped in some jurisdictions — for example, the Durbin Amendment to the Dodd–Frank Act in the United States and the EU Interchange Fee Regulation. Consumer liability for unauthorised transactions differs by country and network; debit cards generally offer weaker statutory protection than credit cards, and a compromised debit card exposes the customer's own funds rather than the issuer's.

Use worldwide

Debit is the dominant card payment method in many countries, including Canada (Interac), Germany (Girocard), and Australia (EFTPOS), while in others credit cards account for a larger share of spending. Debit cards are also used for ATM withdrawals, cash back at the till, and increasingly for online and mobile-wallet payments.
