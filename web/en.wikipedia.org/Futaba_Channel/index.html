Futaba Channel (ふたば☆ちゃんねる, Futaba Channeru), also known as 2chan, is a Japanese imageboard website launched in 2001 as an offshoot of the textboard 2channel. Users post images and comments anonymously, and threads are automatically deleted after a period of time.

Influence

Futaba Channel pioneered the modern imageboard format and became a hub of Japanese internet culture, spreading memes and in-jokes widely. Its software and layout directly inspired the English-language imageboard 4chan, founded in 2003, which modelled itself on Futaba's anime and random boards.
