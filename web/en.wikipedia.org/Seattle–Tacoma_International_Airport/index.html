Seattle–Tacoma International Airport

Seattle–Tacoma International Airport (IATA: SEA), commonly called Sea-Tac Airport, is the primary international airport serving Seattle and its surrounding metropolitan area in the U.S. state of Washington. Located in the city of SeaTac, about 14 miles (23 km) south of Downtown Seattle, it is operated by the Port of Seattle and is a major hub for Alaska Airlines and a hub for Delta Air Lines.

History

The airport was built by the Port of Seattle in 1944 to serve military operations during the Second World War and began commercial service in 1947. It has expanded repeatedly, adding satellite terminals connected by an automated subway system in the 1970s and a third runway in 2008. A new international arrivals facility opened in 2022.

Facilities and transport

Sea-Tac has a main terminal with four concourses and two satellite terminals. It is connected to downtown Seattle by the Link light rail 1 Line, and to the region by Interstate 5 and State Route 518. In recent years it has handled roughly 50 million passengers annually, ranking among the busiest airports in the United States.
