Train set

A train set is a boxed set of model railway equipment — typically a locomotive, rolling stock, a loop of track and a controller — sold as a single package, often as a starter or toy product. Train sets are a common entry point into the hobby of railway modelling.

Contents and scales

Electric train sets in HO scale (1:87) and N scale (1:160) dominate the market, with OO gauge common in Britain and O scale in larger sets. Power is usually supplied from a wall adapter through the rails to the locomotive's motor, increasingly using Digital Command Control (DCC). Battery-powered and wooden train sets are aimed at young children.

History

Clockwork and live-steam toy trains were sold in the nineteenth century; firms such as Märklin, Lionel and Hornby popularised complete sets in the twentieth century. The classic "oval of track with a siding" layout remains the standard starter arrangement.
