Saint Demetrius

Diedc. 306, Thessalonica
Venerated inEastern Orthodox Church; Roman Catholic Church
Feast26 October (Orthodox); 9 April / 8 October (Catholic traditions)
PatronageThessaloniki; soldiers

Saint Demetrius of Thessalonica was a Christian martyr of the early fourth century, martyred under the Diocletianic persecution around 306. He is one of the most important saints of the Eastern Orthodox Church, honoured as a great-martyr and myrrh-streamer.

Cult

According to tradition Demetrius was a young Roman officer or proconsul who was imprisoned for his faith and run through with spears. The great basilica of Hagios Demetrios in Thessaloniki, built over the site associated with his martyrdom and relics, became a major pilgrimage centre, and Demetrius was revered as the protector of the city through centuries of sieges.
