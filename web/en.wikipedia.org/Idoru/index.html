Idoru

Author William Gibson
Published 1996
Series Bridge trilogy

Idoru is a 1996 science fiction novel by William Gibson, the second book in his Bridge trilogy, following Virtual Light and preceding All Tomorrow's Parties.

Plot

The novel follows Colin Laney, a data analyst with a talent for spotting "nodal points" in information flows, who is hired to investigate the intentions of rock star Rez, of the band Lo/Rez, who has announced his wish to marry Rei Toei — an idoru, a wholly synthetic Japanese media idol. Parallel threads follow Chia Pet McKenzie, a teenage fan travelling to Tokyo, whose story converges with Laney's amid corporate intrigue over nanotechnology.

Themes

Idoru explores celebrity, virtual identity, fame, and the manufacture of synthetic personalities, anticipating later real-world virtual idols.
