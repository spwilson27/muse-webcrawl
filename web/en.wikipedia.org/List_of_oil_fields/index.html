List of oil fields

The world's largest oil fields are concentrated in the Middle East, with additional giant fields in Russia, the Americas, and offshore provinces. A "giant" field is conventionally one with more than 500 million barrels of ultimately recoverable oil. The table lists the largest fields by original oil in place / recoverable reserves (figures approximate and debated, from memory).

Context

Giant fields dominate global production: a small fraction of fields supplies the majority of world output, and most giants were found before 1980, making their decline rates strategically important. Reserve figures vary by source and are politically sensitive in OPEC states.
