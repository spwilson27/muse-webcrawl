Evo Morales

Juan Evo Morales Ayma (born 26 October 1959) is a Bolivian politician and trade unionist who served as the 65th president of Bolivia from 2006 to 2019. Widely regarded as the country's first president to come from its indigenous population, Morales was a leader of the Movement for Socialism (MAS) party and a long-time leader of the coca growers' (cocalero) union federation.

Born in Isallavi, in the department of Oruro, to an Aymara family of subsistence farmers, Morales moved to the Chapare region as a young man, where he became involved in organizing coca farmers resisting US-backed eradication campaigns. He rose to national prominence during the Bolivian gas conflict of 2003 and won the presidency in the December 2005 general election with 53.7 percent of the vote, an unusually large margin in Bolivian politics.

Presidency

In office, Morales nationalized the hydrocarbons industry in 2006, convened a constituent assembly that produced a new constitution in 2009 recognizing Bolivia as a plurinational state, and directed natural-gas revenues toward social programs that reduced poverty and illiteracy. His governments maintained macroeconomic stability while expanding state control of strategic sectors, and Bolivia's economy grew steadily during the commodity boom.

Morales won re-election in 2009 and again in 2014. A 2016 referendum rejected a constitutional amendment that would have allowed him to seek a fourth term, but in 2017 the Plurinational Constitutional Tribunal ruled that term limits infringed his political rights, clearing the way for his candidacy in 2019. The disputed October 2019 election, in which the vote count was interrupted and an audit by the Organization of American States reported irregularities, triggered mass protests; Morales resigned on 10 November 2019 after the commander of the armed forces suggested he step down, and he left the country, first for Mexico and later Argentina.

Return and later career

Morales returned to Bolivia in November 2020 after the MAS candidate Luis Arce won the presidency. Relations between Morales and Arce subsequently soured, splitting the MAS into rival factions. In 2025 Bolivian prosecutors pursued cases against him and courts ruled him ineligible to run for president again, while his supporters staged protests on his behalf.
