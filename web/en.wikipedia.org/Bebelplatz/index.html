Bebelplatz

The Bebelplatz is a public square in the central Mitte district of Berlin, Germany, on the south side of Unter den Linden. It is named after August Bebel, a founder of the Social Democratic Party of Germany.

History

Laid out in the 18th century as part of Frederick the Great's Forum Fridericianum, the square is flanked by the State Opera, St. Hedwig's Cathedral and the Old Library of Humboldt University.

Book burning memorial

On 10 May 1933 the square was the site of a Nazi book burning in which works by Jewish, pacifist and other authors were destroyed. Micha Ullman's underground memorial, a sunken library with empty shelves, commemorates the event.
