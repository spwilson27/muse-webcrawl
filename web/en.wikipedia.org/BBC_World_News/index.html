BBC World News

BBC World News is an international English-language pay television channel owned and operated by the BBC. Launched in 1991 as BBC World Service Television, it broadcasts news bulletins, documentaries, lifestyle programmes, and interviews around the clock to an audience in more than 200 countries and territories.

History

The channel began broadcasting on 11 March 1991 as a commercial offshoot of the BBC World Service. Unlike the BBC's domestic services, it is funded by subscription and advertising revenues rather than the UK licence fee. It was relaunched as BBC World in 1995 and renamed BBC World News in 2008. In 2023 it merged with the domestic BBC News channel into a single service retaining the BBC News name outside the UK opt-outs.

Programming

The schedule is built around rolling news bulletins from the BBC's newsroom at Broadcasting House in London, including flagship programmes such as BBC World News, World News Today, Focus on Africa, Impact, and Global, plus the interview show HARDtalk and business and sport bulletins.

Distribution

The channel is carried on cable and satellite platforms worldwide and reaches several hundred million households. It is not officially available as a channel in the United Kingdom, where the domestic BBC News channel serves viewers.
