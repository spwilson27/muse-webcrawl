Cochin Shipyard

Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL) is one of the largest shipbuilding and ship repair facilities in India. Located in the port city of Kochi (Cochin), in the state of Kerala, it is a public sector undertaking administered by the Government of India through the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.

History

The shipyard was incorporated in 1972 and began operations with technical collaboration from Japan's Mitsubishi Heavy Industries. Its first ship, the bulk carrier MV Rani Padmini, was launched in 1981. The yard has since built some of the largest vessels constructed in India, including tankers, bulk carriers, offshore vessels and passenger ships.

Facilities

CSL operates two large dry docks: a shipbuilding dock of about 255 by 43 metres and a ship repair dock of about 270 by 45 metres. The yard can handle vessels of up to roughly 110,000 deadweight tonnage for construction and 125,000 DWT for repair. Additional facilities include an International Ship Repair Facility on Willingdon Island and smaller yards at other Indian ports.

Notable vessels

The shipyard's most prominent project is the INS Vikrant, India's first indigenously designed and built aircraft carrier, which was commissioned into the Indian Navy in 2022. CSL has also constructed dredgers, offshore support vessels, and research vessels for domestic and export customers.
