Texas Children's Hospital

[Placeholder image: Texas_Childrens_Hospital.png]

Texas Children's Hospital is an American not-for-profit pediatric hospital located in the Texas Medical Center in Houston, Texas. Founded in 1954, it is one of the largest children's hospitals in the United States and is affiliated with Baylor College of Medicine.

Services

The hospital provides specialised paediatric care across a wide range of subspecialties, including cardiology, oncology, neurology, and surgery, and operates additional campuses in the Houston area, including in The Woodlands and West Campus in Katy.
