Milica Bogdanovna Jovovich (born 17 December 1975), known professionally as Milla Jovovich, is an American actress, model and singer. Born in Kyiv to a Serbian father and a Russian mother, she emigrated to the United States as a child and began modelling at age 11. She rose to prominence as an action star, headlining the Resident Evil film series (2002–2016) as Alice — the highest-grossing film series based on a video game — and starring as Leeloo in Luc Besson's The Fifth Element (1997).

Jovovich is one of the most successful action-film actresses of her generation.

Early life

Jovovich was born in Kyiv, then in the Soviet Union, to Bogdan Jovovich, a Serbian doctor, and Galina Loginova, a Russian actress. The family left the Soviet Union in 1980, living in London before settling in Los Angeles. She began modelling at 11, appearing on magazine covers photographed by Richard Avedon for Revlon's "Most Unforgettable Women in the World" campaign.

Career

Early roles and breakthrough

Her first film roles included Two Moon Junction (1988) and the television movie The Night Train to Kathmandu. She starred in Return to the Blue Lagoon (1991) and appeared in Dazed and Confused (1993). A brief music career followed with the album The Divine Comedy (1994). Her breakthrough came as the alien Leeloo in The Fifth Element (1997), speaking a constructed "divine language". She then played Joan of Arc in Besson's The Messenger: The Story of Joan of Arc (1999).

Resident Evil and action stardom

In 2002 Jovovich starred as Alice in Resident Evil, directed by Paul W. S. Anderson, whom she later married. The series ran to six films, concluding with Resident Evil: The Final Chapter (2016), and grossed over $1.2 billion worldwide. She consolidated her action reputation with Ultraviolet (2006), The Fourth Kind (2009), The Three Musketeers (2011), Hellboy (2019) and Monster Hunter (2020).

Other work

Jovovich has modelled for L'Oréal, Versace and many other brands, co-founded the short-lived clothing line Jovovich-Hawk, and has continued to release music independently. She is frequently cited among the highest-earning models and action actresses of her era.
