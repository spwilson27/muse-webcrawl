The Revolution is an American rock band formed in Minneapolis, Minnesota, best known as the backing band for the musician Prince. They rose to international fame in the mid-1980s with the album and film Purple Rain (1984).

History

The band backed Prince on the albums Purple Rain, Around the World in a Day (1985), and Parade (1986), and appeared in the films Purple Rain and Under the Cherry Moon. Prince disbanded the group in 1986.

Reunions

The Revolution reunited for performances in 2011 and, following Prince's death in 2016, regrouped for tribute concerts and tours under their own name.
