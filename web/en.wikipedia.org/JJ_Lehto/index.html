Jyrki Juhani Järvilehto (born 31 January 1966), better known as JJ Lehto, is a Finnish former racing driver. He competed in Formula One between 1989 and 1994 and won the 24 Hours of Le Mans twice, in 1995 and 2005.

Career

A protégé of Keke Rosberg, Lehto won the 1988 British Formula 3 Championship before entering Formula One with Onyx. He later drove for Scuderia Italia, Sauber, and Benetton — where he was teammate to Michael Schumacher in 1994 — scoring a best result of third place at the 1991 San Marino Grand Prix and suffering a serious neck injury in testing in 1994.

Sports cars and later life

Lehto found greater success in sports car racing, winning Le Mans overall with the McLaren F1 GTR in 1995 and with the Audi R8 in 2005, as well as the American Le Mans Series championship in 2004. He later worked as a television commentator. In 2011 he was convicted of negligent homicide and drunk driving in connection with a fatal boating accident in Finland in 2010.
