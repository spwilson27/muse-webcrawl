Erykah Badu

Born: 26 February 1971, Dallas, Texas, US
Genres: Neo soul, R&B, funk, hip hop
Occupation: Singer-songwriter

Erica Abi Wright (born February 26, 1971), known professionally as Erykah Badu, is an American singer and songwriter. Influenced by R&B, soul, and hip hop, Badu rose to prominence in the late 1990s and is often dubbed the "Queen of Neo Soul".

Career

Her debut album Baduizm (1997), featuring the hit "On & On", won two Grammy Awards and established her blend of jazz-inflected vocals and socially conscious lyrics. She followed it with the live album Live (1997), including the single "Tyrone", and the acclaimed Mama's Gun (2000), which contained "Bag Lady".

Later work

Subsequent albums include Worldwide Underground (2003) and the two-part New Amerykah series (2008, 2010). In 2010 her guerrilla-style video for "Window Seat", filmed in Dallas, attracted significant media attention. Her 2015 mixtape But You Caint Use My Phone continued her experimental approach.

Personal life

Badu has three children and has been involved in philanthropic work in her native Dallas, including support for arts education.
