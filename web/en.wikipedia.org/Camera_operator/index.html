Camera operator


Placeholder image: A camera operator at work on a film set

A camera operator, or depending on the context cameraman or camerawoman, is a professional operator of a film camera or video camera. In filmmaking, the leading camera operator is usually called a cinematographer, while the camera operator in a film crew is a person who operates the camera at the direction of the cinematographer.

Duties

The camera operator physically controls the camera, framing and moving it during a take in accordance with the director's and cinematographer's instructions — panning, tilting, tracking, and adjusting focus in coordination with the focus puller. In television studio production, operators work under a vision control team and take shots called by the director over talkback.

Skills include an instinctive sense of composition and timing, the ability to operate equipment such as dollies, cranes, Steadicams and gimbals, and maintaining consistent framing while following moving subjects.
