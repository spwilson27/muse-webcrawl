PSV Eindhoven
Full name: Philips Sport Vereniging
Founded: August 31, 1913
Ground: Philips Stadion (capacity 35,000)
League: Eredivisie
Nickname: Boeren (Farmers), Rood-witten (Red-whites)

Philips Sport Vereniging (English: Philips Sports Association), abbreviated as PSV and internationally known as PSV Eindhoven, is a Dutch sports club from Eindhoven, Netherlands. It is best known for its professional football department, which has played in the Eredivisie, the top tier in Dutch football, since its inception in 1956. Along with Ajax and Feyenoord, PSV is one of the country's "big three" clubs that have dominated the Eredivisie.

The club was founded in 1913 as a team for employees of the Philips electronics company. PSV's history contains two golden eras centring on the UEFA Cup victory in 1978 and the European Cup victory in 1988, as part of the seasonal treble that year. The team has won the Eredivisie 25 times, the KNVB Cup 11 times and the Johan Cruyff Shield a record 14 times. Various players including Ruud Gullit, Romário, Ronaldo, Ruud van Nistelrooy, Arjen Robben and Cody Gakpo began their careers at PSV or rose to prominence there.

Stadium and identity

PSV has played at the Philips Stadion since its foundation in 1913, making it one of the oldest continuously used stadiums in professional football. The club's home kit is a red-and-white vertically striped shirt with white shorts, and its traditional rivals are Ajax (against whom they contest De Topper) and Feyenoord.
