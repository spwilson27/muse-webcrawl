Poltergeist

In ghostlore and parapsychology, a poltergeist (from German poltern 'to make noise' and Geist 'ghost') is a type of spirit or force responsible for physical disturbances such as loud noises and objects being moved or destroyed.

Claims date back centuries; famous cases include the Rosenheim poltergeist in Germany and the Enfield poltergeist in London in the 1970s. Skeptics attribute reports to fraud, misperception, and natural causes, while some parapsychologists proposed unconscious psychokinesis by living agents. The concept is a staple of horror fiction and film.
