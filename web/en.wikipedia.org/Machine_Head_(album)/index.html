Machine Head (album)

Machine Head
Artist: Deep Purple
Released: 25 March 1972
Genre: Hard rock, heavy metal
Producer: Deep Purple

Machine Head is the sixth studio album by the English rock band Deep Purple, released in March 1972 by Purple Records. It is widely regarded as one of the most influential heavy metal albums of all time.

Recording

The album was recorded in December 1971 at the Grand Hotel in Montreux, Switzerland, using the Rolling Stones Mobile Studio, after the band's intended venue, the Montreux Casino, burned down during a Frank Zappa concert — an event immortalised in the song "Smoke on the Water".

Track listing and legacy

Other classic tracks include "Highway Star", "Lazy" and "Space Truckin'". Machine Head reached number one in the UK and has sold millions of copies worldwide.
