The Lockheed AC-130 is a heavily armed, long-endurance, ground-attack variant of the C-130 Hercules transport, used by the United States Air Force. Its primary missions are close air support, air interdiction, and force protection, and it is one of the most powerful ground-attack aircraft in service.

Armament

Gunship variants have carried side-firing weapons including 20 mm cannon, 40 mm Bofors cannon, and a 105 mm howitzer, aimed out of the left side of the aircraft as it flies a pylon turn around the target. The current AC-130J Ghostrider adds precision-guided munitions and a 30 mm cannon.

History

The AC-130 first saw combat in the Vietnam War, where it gained a reputation as a devastating truck-killer along the Ho Chi Minh Trail. Successive variants — the AC-130H Spectre, AC-130U Spooky, AC-130W Stinger II, and AC-130J Ghostrider — have served in conflicts from Grenada and Panama to Iraq and Afghanistan.
