Amar Jawan Jyoti

The Amar Jawan Jyoti (Hindi: अमर जवान ज्योति, lit. 'Flame of the Immortal Soldier') is an Indian memorial conceptualised after the Indo-Pakistani War of 1971 and inaugurated on 26 January 1972 by Prime Minister Indira Gandhi. It was constructed under the India Gate in New Delhi to commemorate the fallen soldiers of the Indian Armed Forces.

Structure

The memorial consists of a marble pedestal bearing an inverted L1A1 Self-Loading Rifle on top of which rests a soldier's war helmet. Bound by four marble urns on the four corners, one of which kept the flame burning continuously since 1972, fuelled first by LPG cylinders and later by piped natural gas. Guards from the three services stood watch at the memorial.

National War Memorial

A new Amar Jawan Jyoti was inaugurated at the National War Memorial (Rashtriya Samar Smaarak) at the C-Hexagon of Rajpath in February 2019. On 21 January 2022, the flame at India Gate was merged with the flame at the National War Memorial, ending its fifty-year continuous burn at India Gate, a decision that attracted public debate.
