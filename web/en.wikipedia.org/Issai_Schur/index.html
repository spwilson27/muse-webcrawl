Issai Schur

Issai Schur (January 10, 1875 – January 10, 1941) was a Russian-German mathematician who worked in group theory, representation theory, number theory, and analysis.

Life and work

Born in Mogilev, then in the Russian Empire, Schur studied in Berlin under Ferdinand Georg Frobenius and received his doctorate in 1901. He developed the representation theory of finite groups, introducing Schur's lemma, the Schur multiplier, and Schur polynomials. His students included many leading algebraists, and with Frobenius he made Berlin a centre of representation theory.

Later years

As a Jew, Schur was progressively removed from his academic positions after the Nazi rise to power in 1933; he was forced out of the Prussian Academy and dismissed from the university in 1935. He emigrated to Palestine in 1939 and died in Tel Aviv on his 66th birthday, in poverty.
