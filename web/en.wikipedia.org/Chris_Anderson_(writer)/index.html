Chris Anderson (writer)

Chris Anderson (born July 9, 1961) is an English-American author, journalist, and entrepreneur. He was editor-in-chief of Wired magazine from 2001 to 2012 and is known for popularizing the concept of "the long tail" in a 2004 article and his 2006 book The Long Tail: Why the Future of Business Is Selling Less of More.

Chris Anderson, former editor-in-chief of Wired and author of The Long Tail

Career

Anderson studied physics at George Washington University and worked as a researcher at the Los Alamos National Laboratory before moving into journalism. He wrote for Nature and Science, and served as an editor at The Economist before joining Wired. His later books include Free: The Future of a Radical Price (2009) and Makers: The New Industrial Revolution (2012).

3D Robotics

In 2009 Anderson co-founded 3D Robotics, a manufacturer of drones and aerial vehicles founded after he experimented with unmanned aerial vehicles and built the DIY Drones online community. He left Wired in 2012 to become the company's CEO.
