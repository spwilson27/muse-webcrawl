SBV Vitesse

SBV Vitesse (Stichting Betaald Voetbal Vitesse), widely known as Vitesse Arnhem or simply Vitesse, is a Dutch professional football club based in Arnhem, Gelderland. Founded on 14 May 1892, it is one of the oldest professional football clubs in the Netherlands.

History

Vitesse spent much of its history in the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football, with notable periods of success in the late 1990s under the chairmanship of Karel Aalbers, playing European football regularly. The club plays its home matches at the GelreDome, a stadium opened in 1998 with a retractable roof and a sliding pitch.

In 2010 the club was taken over by Georgian businessman Merab Jordania, becoming the first Dutch club under foreign ownership, and in 2013 it passed to Russian businessman Alexander Chigirinsky. Vitesse developed a close relationship with Chelsea of the English Premier League, taking many young Chelsea players on loan.

Honors and recent years

The club's greatest triumph came in 2017, when Vitesse won the KNVB Cup — the first major trophy in its 125-year history — defeating AZ Alkmaar 2–0 in the final. Financial difficulties in the 2020s led to licensing problems and relegation from the Eredivisie in 2024, amid disputes over ownership and the club's professional license.

The club's traditional colors are yellow and black, and its supporters are known as the Arnhem Boys. Local rivals include NEC Nijmegen, against whom Vitesse contests the Gelderse derby.
