Legal guardian

A legal guardian is a person appointed to care for another person (the ward) and make legal, personal and financial decisions on their behalf, typically because the ward is a minor or an adult lacking capacity.

Appointment

Guardians are usually appointed by a court, or named by parents in a will. Guardianship may cover the person, the estate, or both, and the guardian owes fiduciary duties and is subject to court supervision. Related arrangements include conservatorship and, in England and Wales, deputyship under the Mental Capacity Act 2005.
