Royal Foundation

The Royal Foundation (formally The Royal Foundation of The Prince and Princess of Wales) is a British charitable organisation that supports the official and charitable work of William, Prince of Wales, and Catherine, Princess of Wales. Established in 2009 as the Foundation of Prince William and Prince Harry, it acts as the main philanthropic vehicle for the couple's charitable initiatives, convening partners and funding programmes in areas such as early childhood, mental health, homelessness, and conservation.

History

The foundation was launched in 2009 by Princes William and Harry to pursue their charitable ambitions. Catherine Middleton joined as a patron after her marriage in 2011, and Meghan Markle after her marriage to Harry in 2018, when the charity was renamed the Royal Foundation of The Duke and Duchess of Cambridge and The Duke and Duchess of Sussex. In 2019 the Sussexes left to establish their own charitable vehicle, and the foundation became the Cambridge family's charity; it was renamed again in September 2022 when William and Catherine became Prince and Princess of Wales.

Programmes

The Foundation works by researching issues, building coalitions, and launching delivery programmes. Notable initiatives include:

Heads Together (2016), a campaign with mental-health charities that sought to change the national conversation on mental health, linked to the London Marathon. The Earthshot Prize, launched in 2020, is a global environmental prize awarding £1 million in each of five categories annually to 2030 for solutions to repair the planet. The Royal Foundation Centre for Early Childhood (2021) promotes research and action on the importance of the first five years of life, a signature cause of the Princess of Wales, including her "Shaping Us" campaign. Homewards (2023) is a five-year programme aiming to demonstrate that homelessness can be ended, working with six locations across the UK. The foundation also supported United for Wildlife, founded by Prince William in 2014 to combat the illegal wildlife trade, and the Invictus Games in their early years.

Governance and funding

The foundation is a registered charity in England and Wales, governed by a board of trustees and funded by donations from individuals, trusts, and corporate partners. Its offices are based at Kensington Palace.
