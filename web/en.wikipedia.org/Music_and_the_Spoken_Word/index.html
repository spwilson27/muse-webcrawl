Music and the Spoken Word

Music and the Spoken Word is a weekly radio and television broadcast featuring the Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square (formerly the Mormon Tabernacle Choir) and the Orchestra at Temple Square. Originating from Salt Lake City, Utah, it is the longest-running continuous network broadcast in the world.

History

The programme was first broadcast on 15 July 1929, with a single microphone suspended above the choir. It has aired weekly ever since, from the Salt Lake Tabernacle and later also the Conference Center. Long-serving announcers include Richard L. Evans and Lloyd D. Newell, who delivered the inspirational "spoken word" segments.

Format

Each 30-minute episode combines choral and orchestral music, organ solos — traditionally played on the Tabernacle organ — and a short uplifting spoken message. The broadcast is syndicated to thousands of stations and streamed online, and received awards including induction into the National Radio Hall of Fame.
