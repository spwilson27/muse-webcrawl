NorthLink Ferries

NorthLink Ferries (formerly NorthLink Orkney and Shetland Ferries) is a Scottish ferry company providing lifeline passenger and freight services between mainland Scotland and the Northern Isles of Orkney and Shetland. The service is operated under contract to the Scottish Government (Transport Scotland); since 2012 the operator has been Serco NorthLink Ferries.

Routes

NorthLink operates three routes: Aberdeen to Kirkwall (Orkney) and Lerwick (Shetland), an overnight sailing of up to about 12 hours to Lerwick; Scrabster (near Thurso) to Stromness (Orkney), a roughly 90-minute crossing of the Pentland Firth; and freight services on the Aberdeen–Lerwick corridor.

Fleet

The main passenger vessels are the sister ships MV Hamnavoe (Scrabster–Stromness), MV Hjaltland and MV Hrossey (Aberdeen routes), supplemented by freight vessels such as MV Hildasay and MV Helliar. Ship names draw on Norse and Old Scots names for the islands: Hrossey is an old name for Orkney Mainland, and Hjaltland an old name for Shetland.

History

The company took over the Northern Isles services from P&O Scottish Ferries in 2002. The contract has been re-tendered several times; Serco won it in 2012 and retained it in subsequent rounds, with the ferries being brought into public ownership arrangements under the Scottish Government.
