The International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance (International IDEA) is an intergovernmental organisation that works to support and strengthen democratic institutions and processes around the world, to develop sustainable, effective and legitimate democracies. It has regional offices in Europe, Latin America and the Caribbean, Asia and the Pacific, and Africa and West Asia. The organisation's headquarters is in Stockholm, Sweden.

Mission and work

International IDEA works in the fields of electoral processes, constitution building, democracy assessment, and political parties, participation and representation, including gender and diversity, conflict and security, and development. It produces comparative knowledge resources—databases, handbooks and reports such as the Global State of Democracy—and provides technical assistance and capacity development to national institutions including election management bodies and parliaments.

Membership

Founded in 1995 by 14 member states, the Institute's membership has grown to more than 30 countries from all continents. It holds observer status at the United Nations. Its work is financed by its member states and by donors for specific projects.
