The Clube de Regatas do Flamengo, more commonly referred to as simply Flamengo, is a Brazilian multi-sports club based in Rio de Janeiro, in the neighbourhood of Gávea. Founded in 1895 as a rowing club, it is best known for its professional football team, which plays in the Campeonato Brasileiro Série A, the top tier of Brazilian football.

Flamengo is Brazil's most widely supported club, with an estimated following of more than 40 million fans. Its home colours are red-and-black horizontal stripes, white shorts and red-and-black striped socks. The club's fiercest rivalry is with Fluminense, contested in the Fla–Flu derby, and it also rivals Vasco da Gama and Botafogo.

History

The club was founded by rowers from the Flamengo beach district who wished to compete with Botafogo's rowing crews. Football was adopted in 1911 by dissidents from Fluminense. Flamengo's golden era came in the early 1980s under captain Zico, winning the Copa Libertadores and the Intercontinental Cup in 1981, beating Liverpool in Tokyo. A second great era arrived in 2019 under Jorge Jesus, winning the Brasileirão and the Copa Libertadores on the same weekend in November 2019, and again the Libertadores in 2022.

Honours

Flamengo has won multiple Brazilian league titles, Copa do Brasil trophies, Campeonato Carioca titles (the most of any club), three Copa Libertadores titles and the 1981 Intercontinental Cup.
