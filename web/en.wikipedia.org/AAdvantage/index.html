AAdvantage

Launched1 May 1981
OperatorAmerican Airlines
TypeFrequent-flyer program

AAdvantage is the frequent-flyer program of American Airlines. Launched on May 1, 1981, it was the second such loyalty program in the airline industry and the first to allow members to earn miles through partner activity on a large scale.

History

The program grew out of American's early experiments with special fares for repeat customers, using its Sabre reservation system to identify its most frequent flyers. Membership grew rapidly, and similar programs spread across the industry.

Structure

Members earn miles or, since 2022, Loyalty Points toward elite status tiers (Gold, Platinum, Platinum Pro, and Executive Platinum). Miles can be redeemed for flights on American and its Oneworld partners, upgrades, and other products.
