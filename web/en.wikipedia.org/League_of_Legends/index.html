League of Legends

League of Legends (LoL) is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Riot Games, released in 2009 for Windows and macOS. Inspired by the Warcraft III custom map Defense of the Ancients, it is free-to-play and monetised through cosmetic customisation.

Two teams of five players each control a unique champion with distinct abilities, battling across the map Summoner's Rift to destroy the opposing team's Nexus while defending their own, gaining experience and gold to buy items during each match. The game has more than 160 champions and is continuously updated.

League of Legends is one of the world's most-played PC games and a leading esport, with regional leagues culminating in an annual World Championship watched by tens of millions. Its fictional universe has expanded into the animated series Arcane, music projects, and other games.
