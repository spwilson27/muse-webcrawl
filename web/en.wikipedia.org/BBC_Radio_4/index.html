BBC Radio 4

BBC Radio 4 is a British national radio station owned and operated by the BBC. It broadcasts a wide variety of spoken-word programmes, including news, drama, comedy, science and history. Launched on 30 September 1967 as the successor to the BBC Home Service, Radio 4 is available on FM, LW, DAB and online throughout the United Kingdom.

Programming

Radio 4's schedule is built around flagship news programmes such as Today, The World at One and PM, alongside long-running institutions including the soap opera The Archers (transferred from the Home Service), Desert Island Discs, Woman's Hour, The Shipping Forecast and the comedy panel show I'm Sorry I Haven't a Clue. Its drama output ranges from the Afternoon Play to serialisations of classic literature.

Character

The station is noted for the breadth and intellectual ambition of its output and for a loyal, predominantly older audience. Radio 4 Extra, its sister station, rebroadcasts archive drama, comedy and readings from the BBC's back catalogue.
