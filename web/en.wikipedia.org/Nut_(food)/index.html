Nut (food)

A nut in culinary terms is the edible kernel of a seed or fruit, typically enclosed in a hard shell. Botanically, a true nut is a hard-shelled fruit that does not split open to release its seed — as in acorns, hazelnuts and chestnuts — but in the kitchen the term covers many other seeds and drupes, including almonds, walnuts, cashews, pecans and pistachios; the peanut is a legume.

Nutrition and use

Nuts are energy-dense and rich in unsaturated fats, protein, fibre, vitamin E, magnesium and other micronutrients, and regular consumption is associated with cardiovascular benefits in epidemiological studies. They are eaten raw or roasted, ground into butters and flours, and used in confectionery and cooking worldwide. Tree-nut allergy is among the most common food allergies and can be severe.
