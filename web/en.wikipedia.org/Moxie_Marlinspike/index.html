Moxie Marlinspike

Moxie Marlinspike (born Matthew Rosenfeld) is an American cryptographer and computer security researcher. He is the co-founder of Signal and co-author of the Signal Protocol, the end-to-end encryption system used by Signal, WhatsApp, and other messaging services, serving billions of users.

Career

Marlinspike earlier founded Whisper Systems, whose apps TextSecure and RedPhone were acquired by Twitter in 2011; he then led Twitter's security team before leaving to develop TextSecure as an open-source project at Open Whisper Systems. The Signal app launched in 2015, and the non-profit Signal Foundation was created in 2018 with Brian Acton. Marlinspike stepped down as Signal's CEO in 2022. His earlier research included notable SSL vulnerabilities and the sslstrip attack; he is also an accomplished sailor who has crossed oceans in small boats, an experience recounted in his writing.
