League of Legends World Championship

The League of Legends World Championship, commonly referred to as Worlds, is the annual professional League of Legends world championship tournament hosted by Riot Games. It is the culmination of each competitive season, in which teams from professional leagues around the world compete for the Summoner's Cup and the title of world champion.


The Summoner's Cup, awarded to the winning team

History

The first championship was held in 2011 at DreamHack in Jönköping, Sweden, with a modest prize pool; Fnatic won the inaugural title. The tournament has since grown enormously: the 2013 final was held at the Staples Center in Los Angeles, and later finals filled stadiums such as the Seoul World Cup Stadium (2014) and the Beijing National Stadium (2017). Viewership has reached tens of millions of concurrent viewers for finals.

Format

The tournament has traditionally featured a play-in stage, a group stage, and a knockout bracket. From 2023, the main event adopted a Swiss-format stage in place of groups. Teams qualify through their regional leagues, such as Korea's LCK, China's LPL, Europe's LEC, and North America's LCS.
