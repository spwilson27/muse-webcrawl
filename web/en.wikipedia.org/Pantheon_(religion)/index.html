Pantheon (religion)

A pantheon is the particular set of all gods of any individual polytheistic religion, mythology or tradition. The word derives from the Greek pantheion, "temple of all gods".

Examples

Well-known pantheons include the Greek Olympians headed by Zeus, the Norse Æsir and Vanir, the Egyptian gods of Heliopolis, and the Hindu devas. Pantheons typically organise gods into families and hierarchies with distinct domains such as war, love, the sea and the underworld.

Study

Comparative mythology examines structural similarities between pantheons, while scholars caution that the "pantheon" model can impose a tidier system on fluid local cults than existed in practice.
