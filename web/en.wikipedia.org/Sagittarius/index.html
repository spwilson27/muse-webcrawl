Sagittarius is one of the constellations of the zodiac and one of the 48 constellations listed by Ptolemy. Depicted as a centaur drawing a bow, it lies in the southern sky in the direction of the centre of the Milky Way. A familiar asterism within it, the Teapot, is formed by its brightest stars.

Features

Because it points toward the galactic centre, Sagittarius is exceptionally rich in deep-sky objects, including the Lagoon Nebula, the Omega Nebula, the Trifid Nebula and numerous globular clusters. The radio source Sagittarius A* at its heart is a supermassive black hole of about four million solar masses.

Mythology

In Greek myth the archer is usually identified as a centaur — sometimes Chiron, though Chiron is more often linked with Centaurus — while Babylonian astronomy associated the figure with the god Pabilsag.
