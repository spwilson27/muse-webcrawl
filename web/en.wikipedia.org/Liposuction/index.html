Liposuction

Liposuction, or simply lipo, is a type of fat-removal cosmetic surgery that uses suction to remove fat from specific areas of the body, such as the abdomen, hips, thighs, buttocks, arms or neck, and to shape those areas.

Techniques include tumescent, ultrasound-assisted and laser-assisted liposuction. It is not a treatment for obesity or a substitute for diet and exercise, and carries risks including infection, contour irregularities, fluid accumulation and adverse reactions to anaesthesia.
