BigBang (Korean: 빅뱅) is a South Korean boy band formed by YG Entertainment. The group consists of three members: G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung. Originally a five-member group, T.O.P departed in 2023, and Seungri retired from the entertainment industry in 2019 following the Burning Sun scandal. Frequently dubbed the "Kings of K-pop", BigBang helped spread the Korean Wave internationally.

Career

The group debuted in August 2006 at the YG Family 10th Anniversary concert. Their breakthrough came with "Lies" (2007), which topped Korean charts for a record-breaking number of weeks. Subsequent hits including "Haru Haru", "Fantastic Baby", "Bang Bang Bang" and the Made album series cemented their status, and their world tours drew millions of attendees.

Artistry and influence

BigBang are noted for their involvement in writing and producing their own music, unusual for idol groups at the time of their debut, and for their fashion influence, particularly G-Dragon's. Their success helped establish the model of the self-producing K-pop group.

Legacy

With over 150 million records sold worldwide, BigBang rank among the best-selling boy bands of all time. They were the first Korean group to enter the Forbes Celebrity 100 and appeared on the Forbes Korea Power Celebrity list multiple times.
