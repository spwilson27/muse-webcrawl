List of multiple Olympic medalists

This is a list of multiple Olympic medalists — athletes who have won multiple medals at the Summer and Winter Olympic Games. The most decorated Olympian of all time is the American swimmer Michael Phelps, with 28 medals, including 23 golds, won between 2004 and 2016. The most decorated female Olympian is the Soviet gymnast Larisa Latynina, with 18 medals.

Most gold medals

Michael Phelps (23 golds) leads by a wide margin. The next most successful are Larisa Latynina, Paavo Nurmi, Mark Spitz, Carl Lewis, Katie Ledecky and Caeleb Dressel, each with nine gold medals. In team events, the American basketball player Diana Taurasi won six consecutive gold medals (2004–2024), and the Hungarian fencer Aladár Gerevich won seven consecutive team sabre golds (1932–1960) plus three individual medals.
