Chronostratigraphy

Field: Stratigraphy, geology

Units: Eonothem, erathem, system, series, stage

Chronostratigraphy is the branch of stratigraphy concerned with rock bodies defined by their age and time of formation. Its units correspond to intervals of geologic time: systems were deposited during periods, series during epochs, and stages during ages.

Principles

Chronostratigraphic boundaries are ideally the same age everywhere and are defined at global reference sections called GSSPs. Fossils, magnetic reversals, isotope patterns, volcanic ash layers, and radiometric dates help correlate rocks between regions.

Relation to geochronology

Geochronology names time itself, whereas chronostratigraphy names the material record formed during that time. In practice the two scales are developed together to produce the international geologic time scale.
