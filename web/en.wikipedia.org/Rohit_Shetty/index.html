Rohit Shetty

Born: 14 March 1974, Bombay (now Mumbai), Maharashtra, India
Occupation: Film director, producer, television host
Known for: Golmaal franchise, Cop Universe films

Rohit Shetty (born 14 March 1974) is an Indian film director, producer, and television personality known for his work in Hindi cinema. He is among the most commercially successful Bollywood filmmakers, noted for large-scale action sequences, elaborate car chases and stunts, and ensemble comedy. The son of stuntman and fight choreographer M. B. Shetty, he began his career as an assistant director.

Career

Shetty made his directorial debut with Zameen (2003). He achieved major success with the comedy Golmaal: Fun Unlimited (2006), which grew into a long-running franchise. His "Cop Universe" began with Singham (2011), starring Ajay Devgn, and expanded with Simmba (2018) and Sooryavanshi (2021), creating an interconnected series of police action films. Other hits include Chennai Express (2013), one of the highest-grossing Indian films of its time, and Dilwale (2015).

Television

Shetty has hosted the stunt-based reality show Fear Factor: Khatron Ke Khiladi for multiple seasons and has been associated with Comedy Circus and India's Next Superstars. He founded Rohit Shetty Picturez in 2016, which also produces the animated series Little Singham.
