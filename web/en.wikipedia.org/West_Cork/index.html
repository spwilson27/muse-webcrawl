West Cork

West Cork (Irish: Iarthar Chorcaí) is a region of County Cork in south-western Ireland, generally taken to mean the part of the county west of a line roughly through Macroom and Bandon. It is noted for its rugged peninsulas, long indented coastline, mild climate and popularity as a tourist destination and a centre for food production and the arts.

Geography

The region is dominated by five peninsulas which extend into the Atlantic Ocean: the Beara, Sheep's Head, Mizen Head, and the long fingers of land around Dunmanus Bay, Roaringwater Bay and Bantry Bay. Mizen Head, at the tip of the Mizen peninsula, is one of the most southerly points of mainland Ireland. The coastline is deeply indented with bays and harbours, and the offshore islands include Bere Island, Whiddy Island, Sherkin Island and Cape Clear Island, the last of which is part of the Gaeltacht.

The principal towns include Bantry, Skibbereen, Clonakilty, Kinsale (often regarded as the gateway to West Cork), Bandon, Dunmanway, Castletownbere, Baltimore and Schull.

History

West Cork has a rich archaeological heritage, including stone circles such as Drombeg, ring forts, ogham stones and wedge tombs. During the early medieval period the region was divided among Gaelic lordships, notably the O'Driscolls, O'Mahonys and O'Sullivans. After the Tudor conquest and the Plantation of Munster, English settlers arrived, and the region saw fighting during the Irish Confederate Wars and the Williamite War.

The Great Famine of the 1840s struck West Cork with particular severity; Skibbereen became a byword for famine suffering. In the twentieth century the region played a notable part in the Irish War of Independence, associated with figures such as Michael Collins, who was born near Clonakilty, and Tom Barry, leader of the West Cork flying column responsible for the Kilmichael and Crossbarry ambushes.

Economy and culture

Agriculture, fishing and tourism underpin the local economy. West Cork has developed a strong reputation for artisan food — cheeses such as Durrus and Gubbeen, smoked fish, and farmhouse produce — marketed under the "A Taste of West Cork" brand. The area attracts many artists, writers and craftworkers, and hosts festivals including the West Cork Chamber Music Festival in Bantry and the Baltimore Seafood Festival. The mild Gulf Stream climate allows subtropical plants to flourish, as in the gardens of Garnish Island (Ilnacullin) in Bantry Bay.
