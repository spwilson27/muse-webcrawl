Taxation

Taxation is the imposition of compulsory levies on individuals or entities by governments in almost every country of the world. Taxation is used primarily to raise revenue for government expenditures, though it serves other purposes as well, such as redistributing income and discouraging harmful activities.

Types of taxes

Major categories include income taxes on individuals and corporations, consumption taxes such as sales tax and value-added tax (VAT), payroll taxes, property taxes, excise duties on specific goods, customs duties, and wealth or inheritance taxes. Taxes may be progressive, proportional or regressive depending on how the effective rate varies with the tax base.

History

Taxes in kind and in labour existed in ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia and Rome. Modern broad-based income taxes date from the 19th century, and the VAT, invented in France in 1954, now operates in more than 160 countries. The OECD average tax-to-GDP ratio is roughly one third of GDP.
