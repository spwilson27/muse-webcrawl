Chesapeake, Virginia

Chesapeake is an independent city in the Commonwealth of Virginia, United States. As of the 2020 census, its population was 249,422. It is the third-most populous city in Virginia and part of the Hampton Roads metropolitan area, bordering Norfolk and Virginia Beach.

History

The city was formed in 1963 by the merger of the independent city of South Norfolk with Norfolk County. The area played a role in the American Revolutionary War: the Battle of Great Bridge was fought nearby in 1775. The Intracoastal Waterway and the Dismal Swamp Canal pass through the city, and the Great Dismal Swamp National Wildlife Refuge covers part of its western area.

Geography

Chesapeake is one of the largest cities in Virginia by land area, encompassing extensive protected wetlands, forests and farmland as well as suburban development in its northern districts. The Elizabeth River's southern branch flows through the city.
