Madhya Pradesh

Capital: Bhopal
Largest city: Indore
Area: 308,252 km² (2nd in India)
Population: ~72.6 million (2011)
Language: Hindi

Madhya Pradesh is a state in central India – its name means "central province". It is the second-largest Indian state by area and the fifth-largest by population.

Geography and history

The state covers the Malwa plateau, Bundelkhand, Baghelkhand and the Narmada valley, bordered by Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra and Chhattisgarh (carved out in 2000). Historic sites include the Buddhist stupas at Sanchi, the temples of Khajuraho, and Bhimbetka rock shelters – all UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Economy and wildlife

Agriculture, especially soybeans and wheat, mining and tourism dominate. Madhya Pradesh has India's largest tiger population, with reserves including Kanha, Bandhavgarh and Pench, which inspired Kipling's Jungle Book setting.
