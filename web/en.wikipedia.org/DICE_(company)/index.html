EA Digital Illusions CE AB, known as DICE, is a Swedish video game developer based in Stockholm and a subsidiary of Electronic Arts. The company was founded in 1992 by members of the demoscene group The Silents, and began by developing pinball games for the Amiga — Pinball Dreams (1992), Pinball Fantasies, and Pinball Illusions — before moving into racing and then first-person shooter games.

Battlefield and Frostbite

DICE achieved international success with Battlefield 1942 (2002), whose large-scale combined-arms multiplayer warfare became the studio's signature. The Battlefield series grew into one of EA's flagship franchises, with entries including Battlefield 2, Bad Company (2008), Battlefield 3 (2011), Battlefield 1 (2016), Battlefield V (2018), and Battlefield 2042 (2021). The studio's proprietary Frostbite engine, debuting in Bad Company with its destructible environments, became EA's standard engine across many studios and genres. Electronic Arts acquired DICE fully in 2006 after a public takeover process.

Other works

Beyond Battlefield, DICE developed the parkour action game Mirror's Edge (2008) and its sequel Mirror's Edge Catalyst (2016), and revived the Star Wars Battlefront series (2015, 2017) under EA's Star Wars licence. A Los Angeles studio (DICE LA, later Ripple Effect Studios) was opened in 2013.
