The Nintendo Switch is a video game console developed by Nintendo and released worldwide on March 3, 2017. A hybrid console, it can be used as a home console connected to a television via a dock, as a tabletop device with its built-in kickstand, or as a handheld. Its wireless Joy-Con controllers detach from the console's sides and feature motion sensing and HD Rumble haptic feedback. The Switch became one of the best-selling consoles of all time and revitalized Nintendo's business after the commercial failure of the Wii U.

History

Announced in October 2016 under the codename "NX," the Switch was unveiled with a trailer emphasizing seamless transitions between home and portable play. It launched alongside The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, which was met with universal acclaim. A handheld-only revision, the Nintendo Switch Lite, followed in 2019, and a model with an OLED screen in 2021.

Hardware

The console unit has a 6.2-inch capacitive touchscreen (7 inches on the OLED model) and is built around a custom Nvidia Tegra X1 system-on-chip. Games are distributed on proprietary flash-based cartridges or digitally through the Nintendo eShop. The Switch supports local wireless multiplayer between consoles and online play via Nintendo Switch Online.

Games

The Switch library includes Super Mario Odyssey, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe — the platform's best-seller at over 60 million copies — Animal Crossing: New Horizons, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Pokémon Sword and Shield, and The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. By 2024, Switch software sales had surpassed 1.3 billion units, and hardware sales exceeded 146 million, making it Nintendo's best-selling home console and one of the top-selling consoles overall. A successor, the Nintendo Switch 2, was released in 2025.
