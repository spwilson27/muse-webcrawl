Farmer

A farmer is a person engaged in agriculture, raising living organisms for food or raw materials. The term usually applies to people who grow field crops, orchards, vineyards, or raise poultry and livestock.

Farming is among humanity's oldest occupations, dating to the Neolithic Revolution about 12,000 years ago. Today farmers range from subsistence smallholders — still the majority of the world's agricultural workforce — to operators of large mechanised commercial farms using tractors, irrigation, fertilisers, and satellite guidance.

Types

Farmers may specialise as arable farmers, dairy farmers, ranchers, market gardeners, or fish farmers (aquaculture). Many work land they own; others are tenant farmers or sharecroppers.
