Natural Resources Wales

Natural Resources Wales (NRW; Welsh: Cyfoeth Naturiol Cymru) is a Welsh Government sponsored body responsible for the sustainable management of the natural resources of Wales. It was established on 1 April 2013, taking over the functions of the Countryside Council for Wales, the Environment Agency Wales and the Forestry Commission Wales, along with some Welsh Government functions.

Role

NRW is Wales's environmental regulator and conservation adviser: it manages flood risk, water quality, waste and industrial regulation, designates protected sites, manages the Welsh Government Woodland Estate (about 15% of Wales's land area), and operates visitor centres and national nature reserves. Its statutory purpose, set by the Environment (Wales) Act 2016, is the sustainable management of natural resources.
