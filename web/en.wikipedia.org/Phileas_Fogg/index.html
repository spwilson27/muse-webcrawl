Phileas Fogg

Phileas Fogg is the protagonist of Jules Verne's 1872 novel Around the World in Eighty Days. A wealthy, precise and unflappable English gentleman living in London, Fogg wagers £20,000 with members of the Reform Club that he can circumnavigate the world in eighty days, and sets out accompanied by his newly employed French valet, Jean Passepartout.

Character

Fogg is depicted as a man of exact habits, living his life like clockwork. During the journey he rescues the Indian princess Aouda from being sacrificed, is pursued by Detective Fix, who mistakes him for a bank robber, and overcomes missed connections, storms and delays. He ultimately wins the wager thanks to having gained a day by crossing the International Date Line eastward, a factor he had not counted on.

Legacy

Fogg has become an archetype of the phlegmatic English traveller and has been portrayed in many adaptations, including by David Niven in the 1956 film and David Tennant in the 2021 television series.
