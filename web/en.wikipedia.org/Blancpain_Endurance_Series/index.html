Blancpain Endurance Series

GT cars contest the Total 24 Hours of Spa, the series' centerpiece.

The Blancpain Endurance Series, later renamed the Blancpain GT Series Endurance Cup and since 2020 known as the GT World Challenge Europe Endurance Cup, is a sports car racing series developed by the SRO Motorsports Group. Founded in 2011, it is contested by grand touring cars built to FIA GT3 regulations, with drivers classified into Pro, Pro-Am, and Am categories sharing each car.

Format

Seasons typically consist of five endurance rounds, most of three hours' duration, held at major European circuits such as Monza, Silverstone, Paul Ricard, and the Nürburgring. The centerpiece is the Spa 24 Hours at Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps in Belgium, one of the world's premier GT races, which the series revived as a GT3 event and which regularly attracts factory-backed entries from manufacturers including Audi, BMW, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Mercedes-AMG, and Porsche.

History

The series grew out of the success of the Blancpain-sponsored Lamborghini Super Trofeo and the FIA GT Championship's endurance tradition. Along with its sprint counterpart, it formed the Blancpain GT Series from 2014. When Blancpain's sponsorship ended after 2019, the championship was rebranded under the GT World Challenge banner, aligning it with SRO's sister series in America, Asia, and Australia. Balance of Performance regulations are used to equalize the diverse GT3 machinery.
