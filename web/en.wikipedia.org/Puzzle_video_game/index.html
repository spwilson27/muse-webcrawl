Puzzle video game

Puzzle video games are a genre of video games that emphasise puzzle solving as the primary gameplay activity. The puzzles can test pattern recognition, logic, spatial reasoning, sequence solving, and word completion, among other skills.

Types

Major subgenres include falling-block games such as Tetris (1984), the genre's defining commercial breakthrough; tile-matching games such as Bejeweled and Candy Crush Saga; hidden-object games; physics-based puzzles; and puzzle-platformers and first-person puzzle adventures such as Portal (2007), which combine puzzle mechanics with other genres. Puzzle design is also central to adventure games and escape-room games.

History

Puzzle elements appeared in early arcade and computer games, but Tetris established the puzzle game as a mass-market genre in the 1980s and helped launch the Game Boy in 1989. Lemmings (1991), Myst (1993), and Professor Layton games broadened the genre in later decades, and mobile app stores made match-three and casual puzzle games among the most downloaded titles in the world.
