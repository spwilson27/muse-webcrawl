Channel 4

Channel 4 is a British free-to-air public broadcast television channel owned and operated by the Channel Four Television Corporation, a public corporation of the Department for Culture, Media and Sport. It began broadcasting on 2 November 1982, the day after its Welsh counterpart S4C.

Remit and model

Channel 4 was created to provide an alternative to BBC and ITV programming, with a statutory remit to innovate, appeal to diverse audiences and commission programmes from independent producers rather than making its own. Uniquely among British public broadcasters, it is funded entirely by its own commercial activities, principally advertising, while remaining publicly owned. Its headquarters are in London, with a national headquarters in Leeds since 2021.

Programming

Notable programmes include Channel 4 News, Countdown (its first programme), Brookside, Father Ted, Peep Show, Black Mirror (in its early series) and The Great British Bake Off (from 2017). Its film division, Film4, has backed acclaimed British cinema. Periodic government proposals to privatise the channel have been politically contentious and were abandoned in 2023.
