Chief diversity officer

The chief diversity officer (CDO) is an organisation's executive-level diversity and inclusion strategist, responsible for programmes and policies intended to promote diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI). The role exists in corporations, universities, hospitals, government agencies and non-profits, and grew rapidly in the United States from the 1990s onward.

Role

A chief diversity officer typically oversees workforce diversity initiatives, supplier diversity, training, compliance with equal-opportunity requirements, employee resource groups and reporting on demographic representation. In higher education, CDOs coordinate diversity efforts across admissions, hiring, curriculum and campus climate. Depending on the organisation, the CDO may report directly to the chief executive or provost and may hold a vice-presidential title.

Debate

The effectiveness and purpose of DEI leadership have been politically contested. Supporters credit CDOs with improving representation and workplace culture; critics question measurable outcomes, and in the 2020s several U.S. states restricted DEI offices in public institutions, prompting restructuring or elimination of some roles.
