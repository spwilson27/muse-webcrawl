Dark pool

Illustration: Dark pool

A dark pool is a privately organised financial forum for trading securities in which orders and executed prices are not displayed to the public before execution, unlike "lit" exchanges. Dark pools are a type of alternative trading system (ATS) in the United States.

Purpose

Institutional investors use dark pools to trade large blocks without moving the market price before the order completes, reducing market impact and information leakage. Trades are reported to the public tape after execution.

Criticism and regulation

Critics argue dark pools reduce price discovery and may disadvantage retail investors; supporters note lower trading costs. Dark trading accounts for a substantial share—roughly 15–20%—of US equity volume, and is regulated by the SEC and, in Europe, capped under MiFID II.
