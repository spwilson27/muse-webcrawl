Statue of Unity

Statue of Unity
Location: Kevadia, Gujarat, India
Designer: Ram V. Sutar
Height: 182 metres (597 ft)
Construction: 2013–2018
Inaugurated: 31 October 2018
Depicts: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

The Statue of Unity is the world's tallest statue, with a height of 182 metres (597 feet). Located in the state of Gujarat, India, it depicts Indian statesman and independence activist Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (1875–1950), who was the first deputy prime minister and home minister of independent India and is remembered for integrating the princely states into the union.

Construction

The statue stands on Sadhu Bet island facing the Sardar Sarovar Dam on the Narmada River, near Kevadia (now Ekta Nagar). It was designed by sculptor Ram V. Sutar and built by Larsen & Toubro with a steel frame, reinforced concrete, and bronze cladding. Iron for the project was famously solicited from farmers across India as a symbolic contribution.

Features

A viewing gallery inside the statue at about 153 metres offers views over the dam and valley, and the surrounding complex includes a museum, memorial garden, and valley of flowers. The statue can withstand winds of up to about 180 km/h and earthquakes of magnitude 6.5.

Reception

Inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Patel's birth anniversary in 2018, the statue became a major tourist attraction drawing millions of visitors. It has also drawn criticism over its cost—around ₹2,989 crore (roughly US$420 million)—and over displacement of local tribal communities.
