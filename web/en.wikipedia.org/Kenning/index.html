Kenning

A kenning is a circumlocution, an ambiguous or roundabout figure of speech, used instead of an ordinary noun in Old Norse, Old English and later Icelandic poetry. Kennings are typically compound metaphors: the sea is "whale-road", a ship is a "wave-steed", battle is "spear-din", and gold is "fire of the sea".

A kenning has a base word and a determinant that together form a riddle the listener must solve. Skaldic verse stacks kennings within kennings, sometimes five elements deep. Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda catalogued kennings and the myths behind them, preserving the system for poets. The device survives in modern coinages such as "bookworm" and "skyscraper".
