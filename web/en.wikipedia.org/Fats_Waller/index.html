Fats Waller

Thomas Wright "Fats" Waller (May 21, 1904 – December 15, 1943) was an American jazz pianist, organist, composer, and comic entertainer. A master of stride piano, he composed standards including "Ain't Misbehavin'" and "Honeysuckle Rose", both inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame.

Born in New York City, Waller became one of the most popular performers of his era, recording hundreds of sides with Fats Waller and His Rhythm. His keyboard technique influenced Art Tatum and many others. He died of pneumonia aged 39.
