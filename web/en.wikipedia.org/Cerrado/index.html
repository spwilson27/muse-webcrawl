Cerrado

Area~2 million km²
LocationCentral Brazil; parts of Bolivia and Paraguay
BiomeTropical savanna

The Cerrado is a vast tropical savanna ecoregion of Brazil, covering about a fifth of the country's area, mainly on the central plateau. It is the second-largest biome in South America after the Amazon rainforest and the most biodiverse savanna in the world.

Ecology

The Cerrado is characterised by twisted, fire-adapted trees, deep root systems and a rich herbaceous layer. It harbours thousands of endemic plant species and animals such as the maned wolf, giant anteater and giant armadillo. Its deep soils and headwaters feed three of South America's major river systems: the Amazon, Paraná-Paraguay and São Francisco.

Threats and agriculture

Since the 1970s large areas have been converted to soybean farming and cattle pasture, made possible by liming of the naturally acidic soils. Less than a tenth of the original biome is under strict protection, and conservationists rank it among the world's most threatened biodiversity hotspots.
