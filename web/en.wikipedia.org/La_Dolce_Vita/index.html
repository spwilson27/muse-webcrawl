La Dolce Vita

La Dolce Vita ('the sweet life') is a 1960 Italian drama film directed by Federico Fellini, starring Marcello Mastroianni as gossip journalist Marcello Rubini over seven days in Rome.

Plot and themes

Episodic in structure, the film follows Marcello among celebrities, aristocrats and intellectuals, including movie star Sylvia Rank (Anita Ekberg), whose wade in the Trevi Fountain became iconic. It critiques media celebrity culture and moral emptiness amid postwar prosperity.

Reception

The film won the Palme d'Or at Cannes and an Academy Award for costume design, popularised the term paparazzi (from the character Paparazzo), and is regarded as one of the greatest films ever made.
