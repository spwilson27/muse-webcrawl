The Ritz-Carlton Hotel Company, L.L.C. is an American multinational company that operates the luxury hotel chain known as The Ritz-Carlton. It is a subsidiary of Marriott International. The brand name descends from the hotels of César Ritz — the Hôtel Ritz in Paris (1898) and the Ritz Hotel in London — combined with the Carlton hotels; Horst Schulze and partners formed the modern company in 1983 after acquiring the rights to the name in North America.

Service philosophy

The company is famous for its service culture, summarised in its credo and motto, "We are Ladies and Gentlemen serving Ladies and Gentlemen," and for daily staff "line-ups" reviewing guest preferences. It is the only hotel company to have twice won the United States' Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award (1992 and 1999), and its training arm, the Ritz-Carlton Leadership Center, teaches its methods to other industries.

Portfolio

As of the 2020s the brand operates roughly 100 hotels and resorts in about 30 countries, plus Ritz-Carlton Reserve properties, branded residences, and the Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection, which launched its first vessel, Evrima, in 2022. Marriott acquired full ownership of the company in 1998, having bought a stake in 1995.
