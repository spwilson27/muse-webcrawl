Audio-Animatronics

Audio-Animatronics is a registered trademark of Walt Disney Imagineering for a form of robotics animation used in Disney theme park attractions. Audio-Animatronic figures combine movement, sound and speech synchronised by a control system.

History

The technology debuted in 1963 with Walt Disney's Enchanted Tiki Room at Disneyland, featuring singing mechanical birds. The 1964 New York World's Fair brought lifelike human figures, including Abraham Lincoln in Great Moments with Mr. Lincoln.

Technology

Early figures used pneumatic and hydraulic actuators triggered by recorded tones on tape; later systems became computer-controlled, culminating in highly articulated figures such as the Na'vi Shaman in Pandora – The World of Avatar.
