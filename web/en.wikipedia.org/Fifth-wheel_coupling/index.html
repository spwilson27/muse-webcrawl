Fifth-wheel coupling

The fifth-wheel coupling is the device that provides the link between a semi-trailer and the towing truck, tractor unit or dolly. It consists of a horseshoe-shaped coupling plate on the tractor and a vertical kingpin on the trailer that locks into it.

Design

The plate, mounted on a pivot above the tractor's rear axles, allows the trailer to articulate when turning while carrying the vertical load (the "fifth-wheel load"). The name derives from the pivoting wheel fitted to horse-drawn carriages. Fifth-wheel hitches are also used in pickup trucks towing large recreational trailers, in a scaled-down form. Regular greasing of the plate and correct locking of the jaws around the kingpin are essential to safety.
