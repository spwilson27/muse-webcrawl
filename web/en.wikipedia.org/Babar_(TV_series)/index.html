Babar (TV series)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Babar is an animated television series based on the children's books by Jean de Brunhoff and Laurent de Brunhoff. Produced by the Canadian studio Nelvana with Ellipse Programme, it premiered in 1989 and ran for six seasons and 78 episodes, with Babar the elephant king recounting stories of his youth and reign to his children.

Premise

Each episode is framed as King Babar telling his children stories from his past — from his early days in the jungle and his education in the city, to his founding of Celesteville and his dealings with the scheming rhinoceros Lord Rataxes. The series adapted original book plots and created new ones, keeping the gentle, civilizing tone of the source material.

Broadcast and reception

The show aired on CBC and Global in Canada, on HBO in the United States, and in many other countries with local dubbing, becoming one of Nelvana's signature productions of the era. A theatrical feature, Babar: The Movie, appeared in 1989, and a later film, Babar: King of the Elephants (1999). The franchise was revived in the CGI series Babar and the Adventures of Badou (2010), centred on Babar's grandson.

Legacy

The 1989 series introduced the de Brunhoff characters to a generation of television viewers and remains the best-known screen adaptation of the books.
