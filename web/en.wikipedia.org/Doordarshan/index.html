Doordarshan


Doordarshan is India's public service broadcaster.

Doordarshan (abbreviated as DD) is the public service television broadcaster of India. Founded on 15 September 1959 as an experimental service in Delhi, it grew into one of the largest broadcasting organisations in the world by number of studios and transmitters. Since 1997 it has operated under the autonomous corporation Prasar Bharati.

History

Television in India began with UNESCO-assisted experimental broadcasts from a makeshift studio in All India Radio's building in New Delhi. Regular daily transmission began in 1965. Colour broadcasts arrived for the 1982 Asian Games in Delhi. For decades Doordarshan held a monopoly on Indian television, and its serials such as Hum Log, Ramayan, and Mahabharat drew enormous national audiences in the 1980s.

Channels

The network operates dozens of channels, including the national flagship DD National, DD News, DD Sports, DD Bharati, regional language services, and the international DD India. Doordarshan's programming covers news, agriculture (notably the long-running Krishi Darshan), education, entertainment, and parliamentary coverage.
