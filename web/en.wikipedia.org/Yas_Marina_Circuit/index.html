The Yas Marina Circuit (Arabic: حلبة مرسى ياس) is a motor racing circuit on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Designed by Hermann Tilke and opened in 2009, it hosts the Formula One Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, traditionally the final race of the season and famously run at twilight, starting in daylight and finishing under floodlights.

Layout

The circuit originally measured 5.554 km with 21 corners; a 2021 reconfiguration shortened it to 5.281 km and 16 turns to improve overtaking. Distinctive features include the pit-lane exit that passes under the track through a tunnel, the long back straight, and the W Abu Dhabi hotel spanning the circuit near its end. The track winds around a yacht marina modelled on Monaco.

Notable races

The 2010 finale decided a four-way title fight in favour of Sebastian Vettel; the 2021 finale decided the championship controversially on the final lap in favour of Max Verstappen. The circuit also hosts F2, endurance racing, drag racing and cycling events, and offers public driving experiences.
