Mexican Riviera

The Mexican Riviera refers collectively to twenty cities and lagoons lying on the western coast of Mexico. Although there are long distances between cities, they are often collectively referred to as the Mexican Riviera because of their popularity among tourists. Cruise ships commonly visit the region on a series of port stops along the Pacific coastline.

Ports and resorts

Ports of call typically include Ensenada, Cabo San Lucas, Mazatlán, Puerto Vallarta, Manzanillo, Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo, Acapulco and Huatulco. Puerto Vallarta and Cabo San Lucas are among the most visited, while Acapulco, the region's pioneering resort of the 1950s, remains an important destination.

Tourism

The term was popularised by the cruise industry in the 1960s, notably by Princess Cruises, whose ship appearances in the television series The Love Boat made Puerto Vallarta and other ports famous with North American audiences.
