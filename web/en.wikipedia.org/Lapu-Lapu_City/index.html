Lapu-Lapu City

Lapu-Lapu City is a highly urbanized city in Cebu, Philippines, occupying most of Mactan Island. It is named after the chieftain Lapulapu, who defeated Ferdinand Magellan at the Battle of Mactan in 1521. The city hosts Mactan–Cebu International Airport, the country's second-busiest airport, and major resorts and export processing zones.

History

The municipality of Opon was renamed Lapu-Lapu in 1961 and became a city in 1962. The Lapu-Lapu shrine in Punta Engaño commemorates the Battle of Mactan.

Economy

Tourism, airport services and the Mactan Export Processing Zone drive the economy. Bridges connect Mactan to mainland Cebu, including the Marcelo Fernan Bridge and the Cebu–Cordova Link Expressway.
