Daisy, Princess of Pless
Born: Mary Theresa Olivia Cornwallis-West, 28 June 1873
Died: 29 June 1943
Spouse: Hans Heinrich XV, Prince of Pless

Daisy, Princess of Pless (1873–1943) was an English-born socialite who became a German princess by marriage to Hans Heinrich XV, Prince of Pless, one of the wealthiest men in the German Empire, in 1891.

Life

Raised at Ruthin Castle in Wales, Daisy became renowned as a society hostess at the family's Silesian seats, Schloss Fürstenstein (Książ) and Pless (Pszczyna), receiving guests including Edward VII and Wilhelm II. During the First World War she worked as a nurse, and she campaigned for social welfare and peace between Britain and Germany.

Diaries

Her published diaries are a valuable record of European high society before 1914. After the war the family's fortunes declined; Daisy died in reduced circumstances at Wałbrzych in 1943.
