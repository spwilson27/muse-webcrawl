Akihito

Akihito
Born: 23 December 1933, Tokyo, Japan
Reign: 7 January 1989 – 30 April 2019
Predecessor: Shōwa (Hirohito)
Successor: Naruhito
Spouse: Michiko Shōda

Akihito (born 23 December 1933) is a member of the Imperial House of Japan who reigned as the 125th emperor of Japan from 1989 until his abdication in 2019. He succeeded his father, Emperor Shōwa (Hirohito), upon his death. His reign era was named Heisei.

Reign

Akihito broke with several traditions: in 1959 he married Michiko Shōda, the first commoner to marry into the imperial family, and as emperor he travelled widely, expressed remorse for Japan's wartime conduct in Asia, and made widely noted visits to console victims of disasters, including the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami.

Abdication

In August 2016 Akihito addressed the nation to hint at his wish to retire on grounds of age and health. Parliament passed special legislation, and on 30 April 2019 he became the first Japanese emperor to abdicate in about two centuries, taking the title Jōkō (Emperor Emeritus). He was succeeded by his elder son, Naruhito.

Personal interests

Akihito is an accomplished ichthyologist who has published scientific papers on the taxonomy of goby fish, and he plays the cello.
