Discovery, Inc.

Discovery, Inc. was an American multinational mass media and factual-television company founded in 1985 and based in New York City. The company operated a group of factual and lifestyle television brands, including the flagship Discovery Channel, Animal Planet, TLC, Investigation Discovery, HGTV, Food Network, and the Oprah Winfrey Network, as well as the streaming service discovery+.

History

The business began with the launch of The Discovery Channel in 1985 by John Hendricks. As Discovery Communications, it expanded internationally and acquired Scripps Networks Interactive in 2018, adding HGTV, Food Network, and Travel Channel, after which it adopted the name Discovery, Inc.

Merger with WarnerMedia

In May 2021, AT&T announced that WarnerMedia would be spun off and merged with Discovery to form a new company. The transaction closed on 8 April 2022, creating Warner Bros. Discovery, led by Discovery's chief executive David Zaslav; Discovery, Inc. ceased to exist as a separate company.

Assets

At its dissolution, the company operated networks and digital services in more than 200 countries and territories, with a portfolio concentrated in nonfiction, factual entertainment, lifestyle programming, and sports through Eurosport.
