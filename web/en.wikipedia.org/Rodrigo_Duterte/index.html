Rodrigo Duterte

Rodrigo Roa Duterte
Born: 28 March 1945, Maasin, Southern Leyte, Philippines
Office: 16th President of the Philippines (2016–2022)
Previous office: Mayor of Davao City
Party: PDP–Laban

Rodrigo Roa Duterte (born 28 March 1945), also known as Digong and Rody, is a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as the 16th president of the Philippines from 2016 to 2022. He was the first president from Mindanao and the oldest to assume office, beginning his term at age 71.

Mayor of Davao City

Before his presidency, Duterte served seven terms as mayor of Davao City, in office for over 22 years in total. He gained a reputation for transforming Davao from a crime-ridden city into one promoted as among the safest in the Philippines, although human rights groups linked him to extrajudicial killings by the so-called Davao Death Squad, allegations he alternately denied and boasted about.

Presidency

Duterte won the 2016 presidential election with 39% of the vote on a populist platform promising to suppress crime, illegal drugs, and corruption. His administration launched a nationwide anti-drug campaign in which thousands of suspects were killed by police and vigilantes, drawing condemnation from human rights organizations and an investigation by the International Criminal Court. His other policies included the "Build! Build! Build!" infrastructure program, a shift toward closer relations with China and Russia, the 2017 Battle of Marawi against Islamist militants, and the TRAIN tax reform law.

After the presidency

Duterte was succeeded by Ferdinand "Bongbong" Marcos Jr. on 30 June 2022, whose running mate was Duterte's daughter, Sara Duterte. In March 2025, he was arrested in Manila under an International Criminal Court warrant related to the drug war and transferred to The Hague.
