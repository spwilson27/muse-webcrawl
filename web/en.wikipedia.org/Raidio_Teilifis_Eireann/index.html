Raidió Teilifís Éireann (RTÉ; pronounced roughly "RAW-dee-oh TEL-uh-feesh AIR-un") is Ireland's national public service media organisation. It broadcasts television, radio and online services in English and Irish, funded by a television licence fee supplemented by commercial revenue.

History

Irish public radio began in 1926 with station 2RN under the Department of Posts and Telegraphs. The Radio Éireann service expanded over the following decades; the broadcaster adopted the name Radio Telefís Éireann in 1966 and later the Irish-language form Raidió Teilifís Éireann. Television broadcasting began on 31 December 1961 with the launch of Telefís Éireann (later RTÉ One), followed by a second channel in 1978.

Services

RTÉ operates the television channels RTÉ One and RTÉ2, the children's and news channels, national radio stations including RTÉ Radio 1, 2fm, lyric fm and the Irish-language Raidió na Gaeltachta, the RTÉ Concert Orchestra and other performing groups, and the RTÉ Player streaming service. Long-running programmes include The Late Late Show, broadcast since 1962.
