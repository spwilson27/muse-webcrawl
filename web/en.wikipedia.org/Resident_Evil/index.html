Resident Evil, known in Japan as Biohazard, is a Japanese horror game and entertainment franchise created by Capcom. The franchise focuses on a series of survival horror video games in which the player controls characters fighting against zombies and other creatures created by the pharmaceutical company Umbrella Corporation through viral outbreaks and biological weapons.

History

The first game, Resident Evil, was released for the PlayStation in 1996 and is credited with defining the survival horror genre, with its fixed camera angles, limited resources, and emphasis on puzzle-solving and exploration. Sequels such as Resident Evil 2 (1998) and Resident Evil 4 (2005) were major critical and commercial successes; the latter shifted the series toward an over-the-shoulder action style. Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (2017) returned to a first-person horror focus, and was followed by Resident Evil Village (2021) and a series of remakes of earlier titles.

Other media

The franchise has expanded into a series of live-action films beginning with Resident Evil (2002), animated films, novels, comic books, and merchandise. The live-action film series grossed over a billion dollars worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing film series based on video games.

Sales

Resident Evil is Capcom's best-selling video game franchise, with well over 150 million units sold worldwide.
