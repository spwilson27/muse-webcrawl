The Daily Planet
Type: Fictional newspaper
Location: Metropolis
Known staff: Clark Kent, Lois Lane, Jimmy Olsen, Perry White

The Daily Planet is a fictional newspaper appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, most famously as the workplace of Clark Kent (Superman), Lois Lane, and Jimmy Olsen in the city of Metropolis.

Depiction

The Planet building is usually crowned by a giant globe. Editor-in-chief Perry White runs the paper, whose motto and fierce commitment to truth make it a symbol of journalistic integrity in the Superman mythos.

History

The newspaper first appeared in Action Comics #23 (1940); Clark Kent originally worked for the Daily Star, modelled on the Toronto Star, before the name changed to the Daily Planet.
