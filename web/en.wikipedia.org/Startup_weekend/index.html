Startup Weekend

Founded2007
FounderAndrew Hyde
TypeNonprofit / event series
FocusEntrepreneurship education
ParentTechstars

Startup Weekend is a series of 54-hour weekend events during which groups of developers, business managers, startup enthusiasts, marketing gurus, and graphic artists pitch ideas for new startup companies and work together to develop working prototypes, demos, or presentations by Sunday evening. The concept was created in Boulder, Colorado, in 2007 and grew into a global movement, with events held in hundreds of cities around the world.

Format

Events typically begin on Friday evening with open-microphone pitches, in which attendees present their best ideas and try to inspire others to join their teams. Over Saturday and Sunday the teams focus on customer development, validating their ideas, practicing lean startup methodologies, and building a minimal viable product. On Sunday evening, teams demo their prototypes and receive feedback from a panel of judges.

History

The first Startup Weekend was founded by Andrew Hyde in Boulder, Colorado, in July 2007. The organization later merged with Startup America initiatives and became part of UP Global before being acquired by the startup accelerator Techstars in 2015. By that time, Startup Weekend events had been held in more than 1,000 cities, helping launch thousands of startup ideas and building a volunteer community of local organizers and mentors.

Impact

Startup Weekend is widely credited with popularizing the weekend hackathon-style format for entrepreneurship education, emphasizing experiential learning over classroom instruction: participants are encouraged to "take action, test ideas, and learn by doing" over the course of a single intensive weekend.
