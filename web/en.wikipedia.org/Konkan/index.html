Konkan

The Konkan is a coastal plain in western India, lying between the Western Ghats mountain range and the Arabian Sea. It stretches roughly 530 kilometres from the region of Mumbai in Maharashtra through Goa to coastal Karnataka.

LocationWestern coast of India
StatesMaharashtra, Goa, Karnataka
LanguageKonkani, Marathi

Geography

The region is a narrow, monsoon-drenched strip of laterite plateaus, creeks and paddy fields, crossed by short rivers descending from the Ghats. It is served by the Konkan Railway, completed in 1998, which links Mumbai with Mangaluru through difficult terrain of tunnels and viaducts.

Culture

The Konkan is home to Konkani-speaking communities, a cuisine rich in coconut, rice and seafood, and historic ports such as Sindhudurg and Vijaydurg built under the Maratha empire.
