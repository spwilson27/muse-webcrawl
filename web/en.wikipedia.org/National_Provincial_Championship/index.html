The National Provincial Championship (NPC), known for sponsorship reasons as the Bunnings NPC, is an annual professional rugby union competition in New Zealand. First held in 1976, it is contested by 14 provincial unions and sits below Super Rugby in the New Zealand rugby structure, serving as a key development pathway for players.

Format

Teams play a round-robin regular season followed by quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final. Between 2006 and 2021 the competition was split into Premiership and Championship divisions with promotion and relegation, and for much of that period was branded successively as the Air New Zealand Cup, ITM Cup and Mitre 10 Cup. The unified format under the NPC name returned in 2022. The separate Heartland Championship, contested by 12 amateur unions, includes the Meads Cup and Lochore Cup.

History

The inaugural 1976 championship was won by Bay of Plenty. Auckland dominated the competition in the 1980s and 1990s, while Canterbury has been the most successful side of the professional era. The Ranfurly Shield challenge system, dating to 1904, is woven into NPC home fixtures, adding a parallel trophy competition.
