Freeview (UK)

Freeview
Type: Digital terrestrial television platform
Country: United Kingdom
Launch date: October 30, 2002
Owner: Everyone TV (BBC, ITV, Channel 4, Channel 5)
Services: 70+ TV channels, radio stations, Freeview Play

Freeview is the United Kingdom's sole digital terrestrial television platform, operated by Everyone TV, a joint venture of the BBC, ITV, Channel 4, and Channel 5. It provides free-to-air television and radio channels through an aerial, without subscription. Launched on October 30, 2002, Freeview filled the void left by the collapse of the pay-TV service ITV Digital earlier that year and became the main vehicle for the UK's digital switchover, completed in 2012.

Services

Freeview offers more than 70 standard-definition and around 15 high-definition television channels, plus radio stations, interactive text services, and an electronic programme guide. Receivers include set-top boxes and televisions with integrated tuners, indicated by the Freeview and Freeview HD logos. Freeview Play, launched in 2015, combines live terrestrial channels with catch-up and on-demand apps such as BBC iPlayer and ITVX on connected TVs, with a backward-scrolling programme guide. Its successor streaming service, Freely, launched in 2024, delivers public service channels over broadband.

Technology

Freeview broadcasts use the DVB-T standard for standard-definition multiplexes and DVB-T2 for high-definition services, transmitted from a network of main and relay transmitters covering about 98.5 percent of UK households with the main public service channels. Spectrum clearance programs, including the release of the 700 MHz band for mobile services in 2020, have periodically reshuffled channel lineups.
