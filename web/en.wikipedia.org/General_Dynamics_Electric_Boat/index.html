General Dynamics Electric Boat
Founded: 7 February 1899
Headquarters: Groton, Connecticut, U.S.
Parent: General Dynamics
Industry: Shipbuilding, defence

General Dynamics Electric Boat (GD/EB) is an American shipbuilding company and the principal builder of submarines for the United States Navy. Founded in 1899 as the Electric Boat Company, it is a subsidiary of General Dynamics with its main shipyard at Groton, Connecticut, and a hull-fabrication plant at Quonset Point, Rhode Island.

History

The company was founded by Isaac Rice to complete John Philip Holland's submarine designs, and delivered the USS Holland, the US Navy's first commissioned submarine, in 1900. Electric Boat built much of the American submarine fleet in both world wars. In 1952 it became the core of the newly formed General Dynamics, and in 1954 it launched USS Nautilus, the world's first nuclear-powered submarine.

Products

Electric Boat has built the Los Angeles, Ohio, Seawolf, and Virginia classes of nuclear submarines, and is prime contractor for the Columbia-class ballistic missile submarines intended to replace the Ohio class.
