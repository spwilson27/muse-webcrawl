Marvel Cinematic Universe

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is an American media franchise and shared universe centred on a series of superhero films produced by Marvel Studios, based on characters appearing in Marvel Comics. Beginning with Iron Man (2008), the franchise has grown to encompass dozens of films and television series and is the highest-grossing film franchise of all time, with worldwide box office takings exceeding $30 billion.

The MCU is organised into phases grouped into sagas, beginning with the Infinity Saga.

Development

Marvel Studios, led by Kevin Feige, conceived a shared universe of interconnected films modelled on the comics. The plan was hinted at in Iron Man's post-credits scene, in which Nick Fury (Samuel L. Jackson) mentions the "Avenger Initiative". Walt Disney Pictures bought Marvel Entertainment in 2009, and distribution of later films moved to Disney.

Phases and sagas

The films are released in phases. Phases One to Three (2008–2019) form the Infinity Saga, building toward the crossover films Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019); the latter became the highest-grossing film of all time on its release. Phases Four to Six form the Multiverse Saga, which expanded the franchise onto the Disney+ streaming service with series such as WandaVision (2021) and Loki (2021–2023), and introduced multiversal storylines.

Reception and impact

The MCU has been commercially dominant and mostly well reviewed, though critics have debated its formulaic elements and its effect on the broader film industry. Its model of long-form interconnected storytelling across films and streaming series has been widely imitated. Notable performers include Robert Downey Jr. (Iron Man), Chris Evans (Captain America), Scarlett Johansson (Black Widow), and Chris Hemsworth (Thor).
