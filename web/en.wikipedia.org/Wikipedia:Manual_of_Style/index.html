Wikipedia: Manual of Style

The Wikipedia: Manual of Style is the main style guideline for articles on the English Wikipedia. It aims to make articles clear, consistent and easy to read by giving recommendations on spelling, punctuation, capitalisation, numbers, quotations, layout and formatting.

Scope

The manual covers article structure, lead sections, headings, lists, tables, images, citations and accessibility. Topic-specific style pages supplement it for subjects such as medicine, film, music and military history. National varieties of English are generally respected according to an article's ties to a region.

Status

The Manual of Style is a guideline rather than a rigid rulebook. Editors are expected to use judgement, and local consensus can justify departures when they improve an article. Disputes are usually resolved by discussion and reference to examples.
