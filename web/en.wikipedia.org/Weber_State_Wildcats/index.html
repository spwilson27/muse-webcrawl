The Weber State Wildcats are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Weber State University in Ogden, Utah. The Wildcats compete in NCAA Division I as members of the Big Sky Conference, which Weber State helped found in 1963; the football team plays in the Division I Football Championship Subdivision (FCS).

Programs

Weber State fields teams in sports including football, men's and women's basketball, cross country, track and field, golf, soccer, softball, tennis, and volleyball. Men's basketball is historically the school's flagship program, with numerous Big Sky titles and NCAA tournament appearances; the team famously upset North Carolina in the 1999 NCAA tournament. NBA star Damian Lillard played at Weber State from 2008 to 2012.

Facilities

Home venues include Stewart Stadium for football and the Dee Events Center for basketball. The school colors are purple and white, and the mascot is the wildcat, nicknamed Waldo.
