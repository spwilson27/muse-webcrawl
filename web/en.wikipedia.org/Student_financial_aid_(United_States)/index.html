Student financial aid (United States)

Student financial aid (US)
Key form: FAFSA
Major programs: Pell Grants, federal loans

Student financial aid in the United States is funding available to students attending post-secondary institutions, provided by federal and state governments, colleges, and private sources. It includes grants, scholarships, work-study employment and loans.

Federal aid

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) determines eligibility for programs under Title IV of the Higher Education Act of 1965, including Pell Grants for low-income undergraduates, subsidised and unsubsidised Direct Loans, and Federal Work-Study. Aid is generally need-based, merit-based, or a combination.

Scale and debate

Federal student loan debt exceeds one trillion dollars, fuelling debate over college costs, loan forgiveness and the value of aid in expanding access to higher education.
