The Prime Minister of Kazakhstan (Kazakh: Қазақстан Республикасының Премьер-Министрі) is the head of government of the Republic of Kazakhstan. The Prime Minister directs the executive branch on a day-to-day basis, heads the Government, and is responsible for implementing the policies of the President, in whose hands the greater part of executive authority in the presidential republic is concentrated.

Appointment

The President appoints the Prime Minister with the consent of the Mäjilis, the lower house of Parliament, after consultations with parliamentary factions. The Prime Minister proposes the structure and composition of the government and reports to the President and Parliament on its work.

History

The office was established at independence from the Soviet Union in 1991; its first holder was Sergey Tereshchenko. Later premiers included Akezhan Kazhegeldin, Nurlan Balgimbayev, Kassym-Jomart Tokayev (later President), Imangali Tasmagambetov, Daniyal Akhmetov, Karim Massimov, Serik Akhmetov, Bakhytzhan Sagintayev, Askar Mamin, and Älihan Smaiylov. Oljas Bektenov became Prime Minister on 6 February 2024.
