Business jet

A business jet, private jet or bizjet is a jet aircraft designed for transporting small groups of people, typically company executives, officials or wealthy individuals. They range from small four-seat very light jets to intercontinental airliner-derived models.

Classes and models

Classes include very light jets such as the Embraer Phenom 100, light and midsize jets such as the Cessna Citation family and Learjet, and large-cabin ultra-long-range jets such as the Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global series, which can fly non-stop between continents. Airliner conversions, the Boeing Business Jet and Airbus Corporate Jet, sit at the top of the market.

Operations

Business jets are owned outright, shared through fractional ownership schemes such as NetJets, or chartered. They use smaller airports and fixed-base operators, offering schedule flexibility at far higher cost per seat than airlines, and have drawn criticism for their per-passenger carbon emissions.
