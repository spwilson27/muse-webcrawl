List of green political parties

Green political parties are political parties whose ideologies centre on environmentalism, social justice, grassroots democracy, and nonviolence — the "four pillars" of the Global Greens charter. Green parties emerged in the 1970s and 1980s and now exist in most democracies.

Government participation

Green parties first entered national government in Finland in 1995. Germany's Greens have served in federal coalition governments, and green parties have provided ministers in many European and Australasian governments.
