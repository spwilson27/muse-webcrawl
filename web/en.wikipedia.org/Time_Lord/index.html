Time Lord

The Time Lords are a fictional ancient race from the British science fiction television series Doctor Who. They come from the planet Gallifrey and are named for their mastery of time travel technology, above all their time machines, the TARDISes. The show's protagonist, the Doctor, is a renegade Time Lord, as is their recurring enemy the Master.

History within the series

The term "Time Lord" was first used in the 1969 serial The War Games, which also first named the Doctor's people. Gallifreyan society is traditionally portrayed as stagnant, bureaucratic and committed to a policy of non-interference in the universe, enforced by a High Council presided over by a Lord President. Figures from Gallifreyan legend include Rassilon and Omega, founders of Time Lord society.

Biology and abilities

Time Lords have two hearts and a number of abilities beyond humans, including a respiratory bypass system and extreme resistance to cold and radiation. Most famously, they can regenerate: when fatally injured, a Time Lord's body transforms into a new form with a changed appearance and personality, traditionally up to a maximum of twelve regenerations (thirteen incarnations).

The Time War

In the revived series (2005 onward), the Time Lords were initially said to have been destroyed, along with the Daleks, in the Last Great Time War, with the Doctor the sole survivor; later stories revealed Gallifrey preserved and restored, before further upheavals in the show's mythology.
