World Trade Center (2001–present)

The World Trade Center is a complex of buildings in Lower Manhattan, New York City, built largely on the site of the original World Trade Center, which was destroyed in the September 11 attacks of 2001. The centrepiece is One World Trade Center, completed in 2014, the tallest building in the United States at 1,776 feet (541 metres) including its spire.

Components

The rebuilt complex includes One, Three, Four and Seven World Trade Center, the National September 11 Memorial & Museum, the Oculus transportation hub designed by Santiago Calatrava, the Ronald O. Perelman Performing Arts Center, and Liberty Park with St. Nicholas Greek Orthodox Church. Two World Trade Center remains planned.

Development

Rebuilding was overseen by the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey and developer Larry Silverstein, following Daniel Libeskind's master plan. The memorial's two reflecting pools occupy the footprints of the Twin Towers.
