Lost is an American science fiction drama television series created by Jeffrey Lieber, J. J. Abrams and Damon Lindelof that aired on ABC from 2004 to 2010. It follows the survivors of Oceanic Airlines Flight 815, which crashes on a mysterious tropical island in the South Pacific. Episodes interweave the survivors' struggle on the island with flashbacks (and later flashforwards and "flash-sideways") exploring their lives.

Overview

The ensemble cast included Matthew Fox as Dr. Jack Shephard, Evangeline Lilly as Kate Austen, Josh Holloway as Sawyer, Terry O'Quinn as John Locke and Michael Emerson as Ben Linus. The island's mysteries — polar bears, a smoke monster, the DHARMA Initiative, the "Others" and the numbers 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42 — drove an elaborate mythology that made the show a cultural phenomenon.

Reception

Lost won numerous awards, including the Emmy for Outstanding Drama Series in 2005 and a Golden Globe for Best Television Series – Drama. Its finale, "The End", drew 13.5 million viewers in the United States and remains one of the most debated series endings in television history.
