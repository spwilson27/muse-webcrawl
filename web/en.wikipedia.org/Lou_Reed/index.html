Lou Reed

Lewis Allan Reed (March 2, 1942 – October 27, 2013) was an American musician and songwriter. He was the guitarist, vocalist and principal songwriter of the Velvet Underground, whose provocative lyrics and experimental sound — though commercially unsuccessful at the time — made them one of the most influential bands in rock history.

His solo career included the album Transformer (1972), produced by David Bowie, featuring "Walk on the Wild Side" and "Perfect Day"; the bleak concept album Berlin (1973); and the noise experiment Metal Machine Music (1975). Later works ranged from New York (1989) to collaborations with Metallica on Lulu (2011). Reed was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame both with the Velvet Underground and as a solo artist.
