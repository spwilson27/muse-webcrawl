Pep band

A pep band is a musical ensemble associated with a school, college or university that performs at sporting events, pep rallies and other school functions to build enthusiasm and support athletic teams. Pep bands are especially common in the United States, where they play at basketball, volleyball, hockey and indoor events for which a full marching band would be impractical.

Instrumentation and repertoire

Instrumentation typically includes brass (trumpets, trombones, mellophones, baritones, sousaphones), woodwinds (saxophones, clarinets, flutes), and a rhythm section of drum set, electric bass and sometimes guitar and keyboards. At the college level the ensemble may be drawn from the marching band and can number from a dozen to over a hundred players. Repertoire consists of the school fight song and alma mater, short "stand tunes", and arrangements of popular rock, funk and pop songs chosen to energise the crowd during timeouts and breaks in play.

Role

Pep bands contribute to the atmosphere of games, lead cheers and chants, and play during team introductions and victories. Many university pep bands travel with their teams to conference and NCAA tournament games, where they receive national television exposure; some, such as those of historically black colleges and universities, perform elaborate routines. In high schools, pep band is often an extracurricular extension of the concert band programme.
