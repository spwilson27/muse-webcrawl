JYP Entertainment

JYP Entertainment Corporation is a South Korean multinational entertainment and record label conglomerate founded in 1997 by singer-songwriter J. Y. Park. It is one of the largest entertainment companies in South Korea and is regarded as one of the "Big Three" K-pop agencies alongside SM Entertainment and YG Entertainment.

History

Park founded the company in 1997. It rose to prominence with acts such as g.o.d, Rain and the Wonder Girls, whose song "Nobody" charted in the United States in 2009. The agency later debuted 2PM, Miss A and GOT7.

Artists

JYP's best-known acts include Twice, Stray Kids, Itzy, Day6, Nmixx and NiziU, the last formed through a Japanese survival programme with Sony Music. The company also operates subsidiaries and joint ventures in Japan, China and the United States.
