Abitur

Abitur is a qualification granted at the end of secondary education in Germany. It is conferred on students who pass their final exams at the end of ISCED 3, usually after twelve or thirteen years of schooling. The Abitur examinations are set and administered within each of Germany's sixteen states (Länder), and the certificate entitles the holder to study at any German university.

Final grades are calculated from coursework in the final two years (the Qualifikationsphase) together with written and oral examinations in four or five subjects, and are expressed on a scale from 1.0 (best) to 4.0 (lowest pass). The Abitur is broadly comparable to the British A-levels or the American high-school diploma with Advanced Placement examinations.
