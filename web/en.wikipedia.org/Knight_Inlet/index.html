Knight Inlet

Knight Inlet (Kwak'wala: Tsawataineuk) is one of the principal inlets of the British Columbia Coast in Canada. It is one of the longest inlets on the coast, extending about 125 kilometres (78 mi) into the Coast Mountains from its entrance at Queen Charlotte Strait.

LocationBritish Columbia, Canada
Length~125 km
Fed byKlinaklini River, Franklin River

Geography

The inlet cuts through steep, glacier-carved terrain. Its head is fed principally by the Klinaklini River, one of the great glacial rivers of the Coast Mountains, and the Franklin River drains Franklin Glacier into the inlet's upper reaches.

Wildlife

Knight Inlet is noted for its wildlife, in particular one of the largest concentrations of grizzly bears in British Columbia, which gather along the estuary at Glendale Cove to feed on sedges in spring and salmon in autumn.
