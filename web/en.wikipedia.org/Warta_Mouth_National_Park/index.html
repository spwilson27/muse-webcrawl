Warta Mouth National Park

LocationLubusz Voivodeship, Poland
Area79.56 km²
Established2001

Warta Mouth National Park (Polish: Park Narodowy „Ujście Warty") is a national park in western Poland, at the confluence of the Warta River with the Oder (Odra) near Kostrzyn nad Odrą. Established in 2001, it covers about 80 square kilometres of wetlands, meadows and oxbow lakes.

Wildlife

The park is one of Poland's most important refuges for water and marsh birds. Around 270 bird species have been recorded, including large migratory gatherings of geese and ducks, cranes, white-tailed eagles, and breeding species such as the aquatic warbler. It is designated a Ramsar site and part of the Natura 2000 network.

Landscape

Seasonal flooding of the Warta creates a shifting mosaic of wet meadows, reed beds and open water, which is managed partly through grazing by cattle and horses to keep habitats open for birds.
