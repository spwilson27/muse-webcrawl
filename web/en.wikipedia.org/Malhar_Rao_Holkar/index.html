Malhar Rao Holkar

Died20 May 1766, Alampur

Malhar Rao Holkar (1693–1766) was a prominent Maratha general and statesman who founded the Holkar dynasty of Indore. Born into a Dhangar (shepherd) family, he rose through military service under the Peshwa Baji Rao I to become one of the most powerful commanders of the Maratha Empire and subedar of Malwa.

Career

Malhar Rao distinguished himself in campaigns across Malwa, Gujarat and the Deccan, receiving the Indore region as a grant in the 1730s. He led Maratha forces deep into northern India, campaigning against the Mughals, the Rohillas and the forces of Ahmad Shah Durrani, and played a key role in the Maratha expansion that preceded the Third Battle of Panipat (1761), in which he advised a guerrilla strategy that was not adopted; he survived the defeat and continued to rebuild Maratha power in the north, notably aiding the Jat ruler Suraj Mal and intervening in Delhi politics.

Legacy

He died at Alampur in 1766. His daughter-in-law, Ahilyabai Holkar, became the dynasty's most celebrated ruler. Malhar Rao is remembered as one of the architects of Maratha supremacy in central and northern India.
