United Left (Spain)

United Left (Izquierda Unida, IU)
Founded: 1986
Headquarters: Madrid, Spain
Ideology: Democratic socialism, republicanism
Largest component: Communist Party of Spain (PCE)

United Left (Spanish: Izquierda Unida, IU) is a Spanish political coalition and movement of left-wing parties, founded in 1986 in opposition to Spain's membership of NATO. Its principal component is the Communist Party of Spain, alongside smaller socialist, republican, and green groups, and regional federations.

Electoral history

Under Julio Anguita, IU reached its high point of about 10.5 percent of the vote in the 1996 general election. It later declined as the Socialist Party (PSOE) dominated the left, then participated from 2016 in the Unidos Podemos alliance with Podemos, entering the Spanish government as a junior coalition partner in 2020. From 2023 it has been part of the broader Sumar platform.
