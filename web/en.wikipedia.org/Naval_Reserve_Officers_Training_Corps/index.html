Naval Reserve Officers Training Corps

The Naval Reserve Officers Training Corps (NROTC) is a programme of the United States Navy that educates and trains qualified young men and women for service as commissioned officers in the Navy and the Marine Corps. Students attend civilian colleges and universities while receiving naval science instruction and participating in drills and summer training.

History

NROTC was established in 1926, with the first units at six universities. It expanded greatly during and after the Second World War and remains one of the Navy's principal officer accession sources alongside the Naval Academy and Officer Candidate School.

Programme

Midshipmen follow naval science courses, physical training and leadership development in addition to their degree studies. Scholarship students receive tuition support in exchange for a service obligation, and on graduation are commissioned as ensigns or second lieutenants. A Marine Option allows students to pursue a Marine Corps commission.
