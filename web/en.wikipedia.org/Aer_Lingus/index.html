Aer Lingus

Aer Lingus is the flag carrier airline of Ireland. Founded in 1936, it is headquartered at Dublin Airport and operates flights to destinations across Ireland, the United Kingdom, Europe, and North America. The name is an anglicisation of the Irish Aer Loingeas, meaning "air fleet".

History

Aer Lingus was established by the Irish government in 1936 and began transatlantic services to New York in 1958. The airline was progressively privatized from 2006 and became part of International Airlines Group in 2015 after a takeover bid by the parent of British Airways and Iberia.

Fleet and services

The fleet consists mainly of Airbus A320-family aircraft for short-haul routes and A330 and A321XLR aircraft for transatlantic services. Aer Lingus is not a member of a global alliance but maintains codeshare partnerships, including with United Airlines and JetBlue.
