Bruno Manser

Bruno Manser (1954–2005, declared dead 2005) was a Swiss environmentalist and human rights activist. From 1984 to 1990 he lived with the Penan people, a nomadic hunter-gatherer group in the rainforest of Sarawak, Malaysian Borneo.

Manser became an outspoken critic of logging that destroyed Penan lands, helping organise blockades and campaigns that drew international attention. Wanted by Malaysian authorities, he returned to Switzerland and founded the Bruno Manser Fonds in 1992.

In May 2000 he disappeared during a solo trip into the Sarawak interior near the Indonesian border. Despite searches, he was never found and was legally declared dead in 2005. His life was dramatised in the 2019 film Paradise War.
