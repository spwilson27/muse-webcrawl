Michael Fred Phelps II (born 30 June 1985) is an American former competitive swimmer and the most successful and decorated Olympian of all time, with 28 Olympic medals — 23 of them gold — won across five Olympic Games from 2000 to 2016. He holds the records for Olympic gold medals (23), gold medals in individual events (13), and medals in individual events (16).

Early life and rise

Phelps grew up in the Baltimore suburb of Towson, Maryland, and began swimming at seven, partly as an outlet after being diagnosed with ADHD. Coached from age eleven by Bob Bowman at the North Baltimore Aquatic Club, he broke age-group records relentlessly and at fifteen became the youngest American male swimmer at the Olympics in 68 years, finishing fifth in the 200 m butterfly at Sydney 2000. Months later he broke the world record in that event — the youngest male ever to set a swimming world record.

Olympic career

At Athens 2004 Phelps won six gold and two bronze medals. At Beijing 2008 he achieved the feat for which he is most famous: eight gold medals in a single Games, breaking Mark Spitz's record of seven, including a hundredth-of-a-second victory over Milorad Čavić in the 100 m butterfly and an epic relay win anchored by Jason Lezak. He added four golds and two silvers at London 2012, retired, then returned for Rio 2016, where as US flag bearer he won five more golds and a silver before retiring for good. Across his career he set 39 world records.

Physiology and technique

Phelps's success was attributed to an unusual combination of a long torso, a 2.01 m wingspan, size-14 feet acting like flippers, high flexibility and low lactic-acid production — together with Bowman's famously gruelling training programme, at its peak some 80 km of swimming a week.

Life after swimming

Phelps has spoken openly about depression and suicidal thoughts, becoming a prominent mental-health advocate; he founded the Michael Phelps Foundation to promote water safety and healthy living. His dominance made him, for many, the greatest swimmer and one of the greatest athletes in history.
