Liathach

Liathach
Elevation: 1,055 m (3,461 ft)
Location: Torridon, Wester Ross, Scotland
Listing: Munro

Liathach (Scottish Gaelic for "the grey one") is a mountain in the Torridon Hills of Wester Ross in the Scottish Highlands. Its highest summit, Spidean a' Choire Lèith, reaches 1,055 metres, and the mountain has a second Munro summit, Mullach an Rathain, at 1,023 metres.

Geology and character

Liathach is composed of Torridonian sandstone resting on Lewisian gneiss, forming steep terraced slopes. Its main ridge is narrow and exposed, and the traverse crosses the Am Fasarinen pinnacles, a committing scramble that gives the hill a formidable reputation among walkers.

Ascent

The usual route starts from Glen Torridon on the A896 road. The complete traverse of both Munros is regarded as one of the classic mountain days in Scotland, with extensive views over the Torridon hills and the sea lochs of the west coast.
