Jonathan Rea

Jonathan Andrew Rea (born 2 February 1987) is a Northern Irish professional motorcycle racer. He competes in the Superbike World Championship and is a six-time world champion in the series, winning consecutive titles from 2015 to 2020 with Kawasaki, the most championships in the championship's history. He also holds the record for the most race wins in World Superbike history.

Career

Rea was born in Larne, Northern Ireland, and began in motocross before switching to road racing. He raced in British Superbikes and then joined the Ten Kate Honda World Superbike team in 2009, winning races but not a title. Moving to the Kawasaki Racing Team in 2015, he dominated the championship, securing six straight titles. In 2024 he switched to Yamaha. Rea has also raced in MotoGP as a substitute rider and won the prestigious Suzuka 8 Hours endurance race.

Honours

Rea was appointed MBE and later OBE in the New Year Honours for services to motorcycle racing. He finished runner-up in the BBC Sports Personality of the Year award in 2017.
