Liu Zhang (died 219/220), courtesy name Jiyu, was a Chinese warlord of the late Eastern Han dynasty. He succeeded his father Liu Yan as Governor of Yi Province (covering present-day Sichuan and Chongqing), ruling the region from about 194 until 214.

Rule of Yi Province

Liu Zhang inherited a wealthy but faction-ridden province. His rule was marked by internal rebellions, including the revolt of Zhao Wei, and conflict with Zhang Lu, the theocratic ruler of Hanzhong. He is generally portrayed in historical records as a weak and indecisive ruler.

Fall

Fearing Zhang Lu and Cao Cao, Liu Zhang invited the warlord Liu Bei into Yi Province in 211 as an ally. Liu Bei instead turned against him, campaigning through the province until Liu Zhang surrendered Chengdu in 214. He was moved to Jing Province and later died there after coming under Sun Quan's control. His loss of Yi Province gave Liu Bei the territorial base for the later Shu Han kingdom.
