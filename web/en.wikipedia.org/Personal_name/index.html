Personal name

A personal name, or full name, is a set of names by which an individual person is known. Most cultures combine a given name with a family name (surname), though ordering, number and structure vary widely — family name first in much of East Asia and Hungary, patronymics in Iceland and historically many other societies, and multiple surnames in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking cultures.

Structure

Components may include given names, middle names, surnames, patronymics or matronymics, clan and tribal names, generational names, titles and honorifics. Naming conventions reflect kinship systems, religion and law; many jurisdictions regulate names and permit legal name changes.

History

Single names predominated in many ancient societies, with bynames added for disambiguation that eventually became hereditary surnames in medieval Europe. The study of personal names is anthroponymy, a branch of onomastics.
