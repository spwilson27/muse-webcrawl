List of Formula One constructors

In Formula One racing, a constructor is the person or corporate entity that designs the chassis and engine (where applicable) of a car; the Constructors' Championship has been awarded since 1958.

Most successful constructors

Ferrari, the only team to have competed in every season since 1950, has won the most Constructors' Championships. McLaren, Williams, Mercedes and Red Bull Racing follow among the most successful. Historic names include Lotus, Brabham, Cooper, BRM, Renault/Alpine and Benetton.

Current constructors

The modern grid comprises ten teams, including Ferrari, McLaren, Mercedes, Red Bull Racing, Williams, Aston Martin, Alpine, Haas, Racing Bulls and Sauber (becoming Audi).
