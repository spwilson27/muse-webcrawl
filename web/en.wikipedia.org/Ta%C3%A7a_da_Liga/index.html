Taça da Liga

Taça da Liga — association football league cup competition in Portugal, organized by the Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional (LPFP). Founded 2007. All clubs in the top two tiers participate.

The Taça da Liga (Portuguese pronunciation: [ˈtasɐ dɐ ˈliɣɐ]), known outside Portugal as Portuguese League Cup, is an annual football knockout competition organized by the Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional (LPFP) for teams competing in the top two tiers of Portuguese football – the Primeira Liga and Liga Portugal 2.

Not to confuse with the Taça de Portugal (Portuguese Cup) organized by the FPF, played by lower division teams. The Taça da Liga was established in the 2007–08 season. Unlike the Portuguese Cup, whose winners qualify for the UEFA Europa League (unless they have qualified for the UEFA Champions League through the Primeira Liga), the League Cup winners do not receive European qualification.

History

The competition was proposed by Sporting CP and Boavista in 2006, unanimously approved by clubs at a LPFP general assembly, and launched in 2007. The first final was won by Vitória de Setúbal against Sporting CP on penalties. Benfica is the most successful club in the competition, having won the trophy a record number of times. The final rounds are played as a "final four" mini-tournament at a neutral venue, traditionally Leiria or Braga.

Format

The cup uses a group stage followed by knock-out rounds, with the semi-finals and final played in a single week at a neutral stadium.
