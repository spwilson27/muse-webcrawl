List of busiest airports by passenger traffic

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The busiest airports by passenger traffic are ranked annually by Airports Council International (ACI) using total passengers—enplaned plus deplaned, with transit passengers counted once. Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport in the United States has held the top position in nearly every year since 1998, interrupted in 2020 when Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport ranked first during the COVID-19 pandemic.

History

Chicago Midway, then Chicago O'Hare, dominated early passenger rankings before Atlanta took the lead in 1998. Global traffic passed 4 billion passengers annually in the late 2010s, fell by more than half in 2020 during the pandemic, and recovered to record levels of roughly 9.5 billion passengers in 2024 according to ACI.

Other rankings

Separate lists rank airports by international passenger traffic (led by Dubai), aircraft movements (led by Atlanta), and cargo tonnage (led by Hong Kong and Memphis).
