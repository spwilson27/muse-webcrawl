São Miguel Island

ArchipelagoAzores
Area744.7 km²
Populationc. 133,000
Largest settlementPonta Delgada

São Miguel Island is the largest and most populous island in the Portuguese archipelago of the Azores, in the North Atlantic Ocean. It is nicknamed "the Green Island" (Ilha Verde) for its lush, fertile landscape.

Geography

The island is dominated by volcanic features, including the Sete Cidades twin lakes, the Furnas valley with its hot springs and fumaroles, and Lagoa do Fogo. Its capital, Ponta Delgada, is the largest city of the Azores.

Economy

Agriculture (dairy cattle, tea, and pineapple grown in greenhouses), fishing, and increasingly tourism form the basis of the island's economy. The local dish cozido das Furnas is slow-cooked in volcanic soil.
