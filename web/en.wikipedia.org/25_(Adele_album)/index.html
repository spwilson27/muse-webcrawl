25 is the third studio album by English singer-songwriter Adele, released on 20 November 2015 by XL Recordings and Columbia Records. Following the enormous success of 21 (2011), Adele wrote the album as a "make-up record" reflecting on turning 25, motherhood and coming to terms with the past. Like her earlier albums, it is named after her age during its production.

Release and commercial performance

The lead single, "Hello", released in October 2015, became a global phenomenon, its video reaching a billion views faster than any before it. 25 sold over 3.3 million copies in its first week in the United States, breaking the record for the largest single-week album sales ever recorded there, and over 17 million copies worldwide in 2015 alone, becoming the best-selling album of the year globally. Withholding the album from streaming services initially only heightened demand for physical and download copies.

Reception and awards

The album received generally positive reviews and won five Grammy Awards in 2017, including Album of the Year, while "Hello" won Record and Song of the Year. Adele supported the album with a world tour in 2016–17. 25 went on to sell more than 22 million copies worldwide, ranking among the best-selling albums of all time.
