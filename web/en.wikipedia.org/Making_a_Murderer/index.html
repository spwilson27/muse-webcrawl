Making a Murderer is an American true crime documentary television series written and directed by Laura Ricciardi and Moira Demos. It tells the story of Steven Avery, a man from Manitowoc County, Wisconsin, who was exonerated after 18 years in prison for a wrongful conviction, and who was later convicted of the 2005 murder of Teresa Halbach.

Content

The first part, filmed over ten years, follows Avery's exoneration through DNA evidence, his civil lawsuit against the county, his arrest and trial for Halbach's murder, and the related conviction of his nephew Brendan Dassey, whose interrogation as a teenager with intellectual difficulties became a focus of public debate.

Reception

The series won several Emmy Awards and prompted widespread public discussion, petitions, and further legal proceedings concerning both convictions. A second part, released in 2018, covers post-conviction efforts by Avery's attorney Kathleen Zellner.
