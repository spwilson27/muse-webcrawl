Central Military Commission

Central Military Commission
Country: China
Function: Supreme military command
Armed forces: People's Liberation Army

The Central Military Commission (CMC) is the highest military authority in the People's Republic of China. It commands the People's Liberation Army, the People's Armed Police and the militia. In practice there are two bodies with identical membership: the CMC of the Chinese Communist Party and the CMC of the state, an arrangement described as one institution with two names.

Structure

The Commission is chaired by the CCP general secretary, who is commander-in-chief, with uniformed vice-chairs and members overseeing services and departments. Reforms in 2015–2016 reorganised the former general departments into CMC agencies and created joint theatre commands.

Role

The CMC embodies the principle that the Party commands the gun. See People's Liberation Army and Chinese Communist Party.
