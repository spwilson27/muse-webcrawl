Cannabis Act

Cannabis Act
Parliament of Canada
Royal assent: 21 June 2018
In force: 17 October 2018

The Cannabis Act (Bill C-45) is a law in Canada that permits and regulates the recreational use of cannabis. It made Canada the second country in the world, after Uruguay, to formally legalize the cultivation, possession, acquisition, and consumption of cannabis and its by-products.

Provisions

Adults may possess up to 30 grams of dried cannabis in public, buy from licensed retailers, and grow up to four plants per household. The minimum age is set by the provinces (18 or 19). The act created offences for selling to youth and for impaired driving.

History

The bill was introduced in April 2017 as part of Prime Minister Justin Trudeau's 2015 election pledge, passed in June 2018, and came into force on 17 October 2018.
