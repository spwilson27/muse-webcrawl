Pesto (penguin)

Pesto is a king penguin chick at Sea Life Melbourne Aquarium in Australia who became an internet celebrity in 2024 for his exceptionally large size. Born in early 2024, Pesto reached about 23 kilograms (51 lb) by nine months old — heavier than both of his foster parents combined.

Background

Pesto was raised by foster parents Hudson and Tango after his biological father, Blake, was considered too old to incubate the egg. His size was attributed to good genetics — Blake is one of the aquarium's largest and oldest penguins — combined with a healthy appetite of up to 25 fish a day.

Popularity

Videos of the notably round, fluffy brown chick drew millions of views on social media, boosted visitor numbers to the aquarium, and attracted international media and celebrity attention, including a visit connected to singer Katy Perry's Australian tour.
