Dalkey

Dalkey
Location: County Dublin, Ireland
Irish: Deilginis ("thorn island")
Features: Dalkey Island, Coliemore Harbour

Dalkey is an affluent suburb of Dublin and a seaside resort in Dún Laoghaire–Rathdown, Ireland, historically a significant medieval port.

History

The name derives from Dalkey Island, visible offshore. In the Middle Ages Dalkey served as an important port for Dublin, with cargo transferred at Coliemore Harbour. The town retains two Norman castles on Castle Street.

Today

Dalkey is known for its literary associations — residents and visitors have included Maeve Binchy, Bono, and Van Morrison — and for the Dalkey Book Festival. The Dalkey Archive and Heritage Centre recounts links to James Joyce, whose Ulysses features the Martello tower nearby at Sandycove.
