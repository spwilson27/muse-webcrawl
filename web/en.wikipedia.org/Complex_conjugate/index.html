Complex conjugate

In mathematics, the complex conjugate of a complex number is the number with an equal real part and an imaginary part equal in magnitude but opposite in sign. If z = a + bi (with a and b real numbers), then the conjugate of z, denoted z̄ or z*, is a − bi.

Properties

  • The product of a number and its conjugate is a non-negative real number: z·z̄ = a² + b² = |z|².
  • Conjugation distributes over addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
  • A complex number is real if and only if it equals its conjugate.
  • Geometrically, conjugation is reflection across the real axis in the complex plane.

Complex conjugation is fundamental in quantum mechanics, signal processing and electrical engineering, and complex roots of real polynomials occur in conjugate pairs.
