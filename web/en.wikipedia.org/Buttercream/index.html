Buttercream

Buttercream, also called butter icing, is a type of icing or filling used inside cakes, as a coating, and for decoration. It is made by creaming butter with icing sugar, sometimes with milk or cream, and flavoured with vanilla, chocolate, fruit, or other flavourings.

Types

Common varieties include American buttercream (butter beaten with powdered sugar), Swiss and Italian meringue buttercreams (based on whipped egg whites and sugar syrup, giving a silkier, less sweet result), French buttercream (based on egg yolks and syrup), and German or ermine buttercream (based on a cooked flour custard). Buttercream is prized for piping decorations such as rosettes and borders and for producing a smooth finish on celebration cakes. Because it softens in heat, it is less stable than fondant in warm conditions.
