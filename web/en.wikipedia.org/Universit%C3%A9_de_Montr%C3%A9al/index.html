Université de Montréal

Type: Public research university
Established: 1878
Location: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Students: ~67,000

The Université de Montréal (UdeM) is a French-language public research university in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Founded in 1878 as a satellite campus of Université Laval, it became an independent institution in 1919. It is one of Canada's largest universities and a leading francophone research institution worldwide.

Campus

The main campus sits on the northern slope of Mount Royal in the Outremont and Côte-des-Neiges districts; its Art Deco main pavilion, designed with input from Ernest Cormier and crowned by a distinctive tower, is a Montreal landmark. Two affiliated schools, HEC Montréal and Polytechnique Montréal, share the mountain campus.

Academics and research

UdeM has 13 faculties and a major teaching-hospital network. It is internationally known for artificial intelligence research through Mila, the Quebec AI institute founded by Yoshua Bengio, as well as for medicine, law and the social sciences. Its sports teams are the Carabins.
