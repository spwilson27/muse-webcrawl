Costa Smeralda

The Costa Smeralda ("Emerald Coast") is a coastal area and tourist destination in northern Sardinia, Italy, about 55 km long, centred on the resort of Porto Cervo in the municipality of Arzachena.

History

The coast was developed from 1961 by a consortium led by Prince Karim Aga Khan, who enforced strict architectural controls in a neo-Sardinian style. It became famous for luxury hotels, marinas and summer visitors arriving by superyacht.

Places

Localities include Porto Cervo, Liscia di Vacca, Capriccioli and Romazzino. Olbia Costa Smeralda Airport serves the region, and the area hosts sailing events such as the Maxi Yacht Rolex Cup.
