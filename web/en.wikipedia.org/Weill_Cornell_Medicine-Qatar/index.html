Weill Cornell Medicine-Qatar (WCM-Q) is a branch campus of Weill Cornell Medicine of Cornell University, located in Education City in Doha, Qatar. Established in 2001 through a partnership between Cornell University and the Qatar Foundation, it was the first American medical school to open a campus offering a US medical degree abroad.

Programs

WCM-Q offers a six-year integrated program combining a two-year pre-medical curriculum with a four-year medical curriculum, as well as a two-year pre-medical program and a foundation program. Graduates receive Cornell University's Doctor of Medicine (MD) degree. Instruction is in English and the curriculum mirrors that of the New York campus.

Research and clinical training

The school conducts biomedical research in areas such as diabetes, obesity and genetic disorders relevant to the region. Clinical training takes place at affiliated institutions including Hamad Medical Corporation facilities and Sidra Medicine.

History

The agreement creating the school was signed in January 2001; the first pre-medical students enrolled in 2002 and the first MD class graduated in 2008. The school was initially named Weill Cornell Medical College in Qatar and adopted its current name alongside the parent institution's rebranding.
