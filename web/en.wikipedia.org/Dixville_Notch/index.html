Dixville Notch

Dixville Notch is an unincorporated community in Dixville township, Coös County, New Hampshire, United States. It lies in a mountain pass (a "notch" in New Hampshire usage) about 1,800 feet (550 m) above sea level, along New Hampshire Route 26. The community is best known as one of the first places in the United States to report its results in presidential elections, under a tradition of midnight voting.

Geography

The notch separates Dixville Peak to the north from Sanguinary Ridge to the south. The community sits amid the mountains of New Hampshire's Great North Woods region, near the headwaters of the Androscoggin River. Lakes Gloriette and the grounds of the former Balsams resort lie just west of the pass.

Midnight voting

Since the 1960 presidential election, the eligible voters of Dixville Notch have gathered at midnight on election day (and at midnight on the day of the New Hampshire primary) to cast their ballots and report the results immediately, a practice enabled by New Hampshire law allowing polls to close once all registered voters have voted. The ritual, staged in the Ballot Room of The Balsams Grand Resort Hotel, brought national media attention to the tiny community — which has had as few as a handful of voters — and made it a symbol of New Hampshire's first-in-the-nation primary. Nearby Hart's Location and Millsfield have held similar midnight votes.

The closure of The Balsams in 2011 and disputes over residency requirements later threatened the tradition; the township briefly lost its ability to hold its own elections, but voting resumed, and the midnight vote in Dixville Notch continued into the 2020s with a small electorate casting ballots at a neighbouring property.

The Balsams

The Balsams Grand Resort Hotel, opened in 1866 under an earlier name and expanded in the early twentieth century, was one of New Hampshire's grand hotels and the community's economic anchor. It closed in 2011, and redevelopment proposals for the resort and an expanded ski area have been pursued since.
