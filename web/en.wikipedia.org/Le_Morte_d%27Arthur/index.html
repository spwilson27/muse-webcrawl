Le Morte d'Arthur

Author: Thomas Malory
Published: 1485 (William Caxton)
Language: Middle English
Genre: Arthurian romance, chivalric prose

Le Morte d'Arthur ("The Death of Arthur") is a 15th-century Middle English prose reworking by Sir Thomas Malory of tales about King Arthur, Guinevere, Lancelot, Merlin and the Knights of the Round Table, drawn chiefly from French sources. William Caxton printed it in 1485.

Contents

The work follows Arthur from his conception and birth through the founding of the Round Table, the quest for the Holy Grail, the love of Lancelot and Guinevere, and the final battle in which Arthur is mortally wounded.

Influence

Malory's book became the standard English account of the Arthurian legend and a major influence on later writers, from Spenser and Tennyson to T. H. White's The Once and Future King.
