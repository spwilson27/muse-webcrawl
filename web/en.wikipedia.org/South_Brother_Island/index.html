South Brother Island

LocationEast River, New York City
AreaAbout 6 acres (2.4 ha)
BoroughThe Bronx
StatusUninhabited; bird sanctuary

South Brother Island is a small island in the East River of New York City, situated between the Bronx and Rikers Island. Together with its larger neighbour North Brother Island, it was once used for private recreation; it is now uninhabited and protected as a wildlife sanctuary.

History

The island passed through various private owners in the 19th century and was used as a summer retreat, including by brewing and ice-making interests. Unlike North Brother Island, which hosted the Riverside Hospital quarantine facility (and the famous patient "Typhoid Mary"), South Brother Island never had major public institutions. New York City acquired it in the 1970s for a nominal sum; it was long administered with the city's parks and natural resources.

Wildlife

In 2007 the island was transferred to management as a coastal bird sanctuary, supporting nesting colonies of herons, egrets, and other wading birds alongside the larger heronry historically associated with the Brother Islands. Public access is restricted to protect the habitat.
