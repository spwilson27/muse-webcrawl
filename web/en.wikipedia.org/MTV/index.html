MTV (originally an initialism for Music Television) is an American cable television channel owned by Paramount Global. Launched on August 1, 1981, with the words "Ladies and gentlemen, rock and roll" and the Buggles' "Video Killed the Radio Star" as its first music video, MTV revolutionized the music industry by popularizing the music video format.

History

Hosted by video jockeys ("VJs"), MTV became a central force in 1980s and 1990s pop culture, launching the careers of artists such as Madonna and Michael Jackson and hosting the annual Video Music Awards. Programs like Total Request Live, Beavis and Butt-Head, and The Real World — a pioneer of reality television — defined youth culture for a generation.

Shift away from music

From the 2000s onward MTV progressively reduced music programming in favor of reality and scripted series such as Jersey Shore and Teen Mom; in 2010 it removed "Music Television" from its logo. The brand now spans dozens of international channels and digital properties.
