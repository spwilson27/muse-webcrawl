Central securities depository

A central securities depository (CSD) is a financial market infrastructure that holds securities in dematerialised (book-entry) form and enables their transfer between accounts. CSDs sit at the top of each national securities settlement system.

Functions

CSDs maintain the definitive record of securities ownership, operate settlement systems that deliver securities against payment, and process corporate actions such as dividends and interest payments. International CSDs such as Euroclear Bank and Clearstream Banking additionally settle cross-border transactions and Eurobonds.

Examples

Examples include the Depository Trust Company in the United States, Euroclear affiliates in Europe, and national depositories in most major markets. In the European Union, CSDs are regulated under the Central Securities Depositories Regulation (CSDR).
