TransLink, formally the South Coast British Columbia Transportation Authority, is the statutory authority responsible for the regional transportation network of Metro Vancouver in British Columbia, Canada, including public transport and major roads and bridges.

Services

TransLink plans and funds services operated largely by subsidiaries and contractors: the automated SkyTrain rapid transit system, an extensive bus and trolleybus network, the SeaBus passenger ferry across Burrard Inlet, the West Coast Express commuter railway, and HandyDART paratransit. The Compass Card smart fare system was introduced in 2015.

Governance and funding

Created under provincial legislation in 1998, TransLink is governed by a board overseen by the Mayors' Council of regional mayors. Its funding comes from fares, fuel and property taxes, parking taxes, and senior-government contributions. A 2015 plebiscite rejected a proposed sales tax for a major expansion plan, prompting later phased funding agreements.

Major projects

Recent expansions include the Evergreen Extension of the Millennium Line (2016), the Broadway Subway project, and the Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension.
