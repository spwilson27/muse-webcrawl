Makati is a highly urbanized city in the National Capital Region of the Philippines. It is the country's principal financial centre, home to the Makati Central Business District, the Philippine Stock Exchange, and the headquarters of many banks and multinational companies.

History

Before Spanish colonization the area lay along the Pasig River. Under Spanish rule it was part of Santa Ana de Sapa and later became a municipality of Rizal province. After the Second World War, the Ayala Corporation developed the former airfield lands into a planned business and residential district, transforming Makati from the 1950s onward.

Economy

Ayala Avenue and the surrounding streets host corporate towers, hotels and shopping centres such as Greenbelt and Glorietta. The city also includes the residential village enclaves of Forbes Park, Dasmariñas Village and Bel-Air, as well as densely populated older barangays in the north and west.

Government

Makati is governed by a mayor and city council. The city has figured in a long-running boundary dispute with Taguig over the Fort Bonifacio area and the Enlisted Men's Barrios (EMBOs).
