Pierre Lassonde

Pierre Lassonde (born 1947) is a Canadian businessman and philanthropist, best known as a co-founder of Franco-Nevada Corporation, a leading gold royalty and streaming company.

Career

Lassonde, a mining engineer and MBA graduate, co-founded Franco-Nevada in 1982 with Seymour Schulich, pioneering the royalty model in the gold mining industry. He later served as president of Newmont Mining Corporation (2002–2007) before returning to Franco-Nevada as chairman when the company was relaunched in 2007.

Philanthropy and honours

Lassonde has been a major benefactor of Canadian universities and cultural institutions, including the Lassonde School of Engineering at York University and support for the Musée national des beaux-arts du Québec in his home city of Quebec City. He was appointed to the Order of Canada and has served as chair of the board of the Canadian Museum of History.
