Ryde is a seaside town and civil parish on the north-east coast of the Isle of Wight, England. It is the island's largest town and the main gateway for passengers travelling between the island and Portsmouth on the mainland.

Transport and landmarks

Ryde Pier, opened in 1814, is among the oldest pleasure piers in the United Kingdom and carries a railway line and road out to the pier head, where catamaran ferries dock at Ryde Pier Head station. Hovertravel operates the world's longest-running passenger hovercraft service from Ryde to Southsea. The town developed as a fashionable Victorian resort, with an esplanade, sandy beaches extending at low tide, and the Island Line railway running south to Shanklin and Sandown.
