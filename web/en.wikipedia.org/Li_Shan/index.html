Li Shan

Li Shan is the name of several figures in Chinese history and scholarship. Among the most notable is Li Shan (李善, d. 689), a Tang dynasty scholar best known for his extensive commentary on the Wen Xuan (Selections of Refined Literature), the great anthology compiled by Xiao Tong. Li Shan's annotations, drawing on a vast range of earlier texts, remain a primary source for the study of early Chinese literature.

Other uses

The name may also refer to Li Shan (李山), a Ming dynasty painter of the Yangzhou circle, or to Mount Li (Lishan) near Xi'an, famous for the Huaqing Pool and the tomb of the First Emperor. In Chinese literature, commentary traditions like Li Shan's were essential to transmitting and explaining classical texts across the centuries.
