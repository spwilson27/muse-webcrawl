Villain protagonist

A villain protagonist is a type of protagonist who is a clearly evil or immoral character around whom the story is centred. Unlike an antihero — a flawed protagonist who may still pursue broadly heroic ends — the villain protagonist's goals and actions are those of a villain, yet the narrative adopts their point of view and invites the audience to follow, and sometimes sympathize with, their story.

Characteristics

Works with villain protagonists are often driven by the character's ambition, revenge, or desire for power, and frequently end in their downfall or in the corruption of others around them. The device lets a story explore morality from within the antagonist's perspective and generates dramatic irony and tension about whether the character will succeed or be stopped.

Examples

Classical examples include Macbeth in Shakespeare's tragedy and Richard III. In modern fiction, examples include Humbert Humbert in Lolita, Hannibal Lecter in some of the Hannibal novels and television series, Light Yagami in Death Note, Walter White in Breaking Bad, and the Joker in the 2019 film Joker. Video games with villain protagonists include Overlord and Dungeon Keeper.
