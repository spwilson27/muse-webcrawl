Also sprach Zarathustra (Strauss)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Also sprach Zarathustra, Op. 30, is a tone poem by the German composer Richard Strauss, composed in 1896 and inspired by Friedrich Nietzsche's philosophical novel Thus Spoke Zarathustra. Strauss conducted the premiere in Frankfurt in November 1896.

Music

The work is divided into nine sections named after chapters of Nietzsche's book, including "Of the Backworldsmen", "Of the Great Longing", and "The Dance Song". Its famous opening, "Sunrise", depicts a rising sun with a brass fanfare built on the intervals of a fifth and an octave — the so-called "nature motif" — over a deep organ pedal.

In popular culture

The opening became world-famous through its use in Stanley Kubrick's film 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968), and it has since been widely quoted in film, television, and advertising.
