Witt algebra

In mathematics, the complex Witt algebra, named after Ernst Witt, is the Lie algebra of meromorphic vector fields on the Riemann sphere that are holomorphic except at two fixed points. It is also the complexification of the Lie algebra of polynomial vector fields on a circle, and the Lie algebra of derivations of the ring C[z, z−1].

Definition

The Witt algebra has a basis of elements Ln for n ∈ ℤ with bracket [Lm, Ln] = (m − n) Lm+n. Realised as vector fields, Ln = −zn+1 ∂/∂z.

Related algebras

Over finite fields, certain related algebras were studied by Witt in the 1930s. The Witt algebra has a unique one-dimensional central extension, the Virasoro algebra, which plays a central role in two-dimensional conformal field theory and string theory.
