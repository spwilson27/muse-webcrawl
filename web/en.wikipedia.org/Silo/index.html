Silo

A silo is a structure for storing bulk materials. Silos are used in agriculture to store fermented feed known as silage, and in industry to store grain, cement, coal, carbon black, woodchips, and other materials. The word derives from the Greek siros, a pit for holding grain.

Types

Tower silos are tall cylinders, often built of concrete staves or steel, unloaded from the top. Bunker silos are trenches or walled bays filled and packed with tractor weight and covered with plastic. Bag silos are long plastic tubes filled on site. Missile silos are hardened underground structures for launching ballistic missiles, a separate usage of the term.

Safety

Silos are hazardous: silage generates nitrogen dioxide ("silo-filler's disease"), grain can engulf workers, and dust explosions are a risk in grain elevators. Filling and unloading typically use augers, conveyors, or pneumatic systems.
