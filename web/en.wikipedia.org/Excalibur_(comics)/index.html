Excalibur is a superhero team and comic book series published by Marvel Comics. The team is a British-based offshoot of the X-Men, created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Alan Davis, debuting in Excalibur Special Edition #1 in 1987, with an ongoing series running from 1988 to 1998.

Founding lineup

The original team brought together former X-Men Kitty Pryde (Shadowcat), Nightcrawler and Rachel Summers (Phoenix) with the British heroes Captain Britain and Meggan, operating from a lighthouse headquarters in England. Lockheed, Kitty Pryde's pet dragon, was also a regular presence.

Publication history

The Claremont and Davis run blended superhero action with comedy, whimsy and cross-dimensional adventure, including the "Cross-Time Caper" storyline. Later volumes of the series have periodically revived the concept, including a 2019 relaunch as part of the X-Men line's Dawn of X era, focusing on mutant magic and the realm of Otherworld.
