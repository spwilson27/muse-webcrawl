List of largest reservoirs

This is a list of the largest reservoirs in the world, ranked by water storage capacity (volume) and by surface area. The largest reservoir by volume is Lake Kariba on the Zambezi River between Zambia and Zimbabwe, with a capacity of about 180 km³. The largest reservoir by surface area is Lake Volta in Ghana, at 8,482 km². The reservoir behind the Three Gorges Dam is notable for the size of its dam and power station, though its storage volume (39.3 km³) is smaller than Kariba's.
