The Pew Charitable Trusts

The Pew Charitable Trusts is an independent non-profit, non-governmental organisation based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Founded from trusts established by the children of Sun Oil Company founder Joseph N. Pew, it works to improve public policy, inform the public, and invigorate civic life in the United States and internationally.

History

Between 1948 and 1979, seven individual trusts were created by the Pew family, initially with a conservative outlook under J. Howard Pew. In 2004 the trusts were combined and the organisation converted to a public charity, allowing it to engage more directly in policy work and accept outside funding. Rebecca W. Rimel served as president and CEO for over three decades, succeeded in 2020 by Susan K. Urahn.

Work

Pew is known for evidence-based policy programmes in areas including ocean and land conservation, state fiscal policy, public health, criminal justice reform, and biomedical research (including the Pew Scholars Program). Its research arm, the Pew Research Center, is a widely cited nonpartisan source of polling and demographic research on politics, religion, media, technology, and social trends.
