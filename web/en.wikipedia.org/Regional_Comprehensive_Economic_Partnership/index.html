Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership

The Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP) is a free trade agreement among fifteen Asia-Pacific nations: the ten ASEAN member states plus China, Japan, South Korea, Australia and New Zealand. Signed on 15 November 2020 and in force from 1 January 2022, it is the world's largest trading bloc, covering about 30% of global GDP and population.

RCEP eliminates tariffs on about 90% of goods traded among members over 20 years and harmonises rules of origin, but has weaker provisions on labour, environment and state-owned enterprises than the rival CPTPP. India withdrew from negotiations in 2019 over concerns about trade deficits with China.
