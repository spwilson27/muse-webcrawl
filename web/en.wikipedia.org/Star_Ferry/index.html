Star Ferry

Founded1888 (as Kowloon Ferry Co.)
LocaleVictoria Harbour, Hong Kong

The Star Ferry is a passenger ferry service operator and tourist attraction in Hong Kong. Its principal routes carry passengers across Victoria Harbour between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon.

History

The service traces its origins to 1880, when Parsi businessman Dorabjee Naorojee Mithaiwala began ferrying passengers across the harbour; the Star Ferry Company was formally established in 1898. The distinctive green-and-white double-ended ferries became icons of the city.

Routes

The main route links Central on Hong Kong Island with Tsim Sha Tsui in Kowloon, a short crossing famed for its skyline views. A second route runs between Wan Chai and Tsim Sha Tsui. National Geographic rated the crossing among the world's must-do ferry journeys.
