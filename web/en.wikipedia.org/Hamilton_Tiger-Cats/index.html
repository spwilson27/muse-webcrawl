Hamilton Tiger-Cats

The Hamilton Tiger-Cats are a professional Canadian football team based in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. They are members of the East Division of the Canadian Football League (CFL). The team was formed in 1950 by the merger of the Hamilton Tigers and the Hamilton Wildcats.

History

Football in Hamilton dates to 1869. The Tigers, founded that year, were one of Canada's oldest sporting clubs. After the merger, the Tiger-Cats — nicknamed the Ti-Cats — became a dominant CFL franchise in the 1950s and 1960s under coaches such as Jim Trimble and Ralph Sazio. The team has won the Grey Cup eight times, most recently in 1999, though it endured a long championship drought thereafter despite appearances in 2013, 2014, 2019 and 2021.

Stadium

The Tiger-Cats played at Ivor Wynne Stadium until 2012. Their current home, Tim Hortons Field, opened in 2014 on the same site and also hosted events for the 2015 Pan American Games.

Identity

Team colours are black, gold and white. The traditional cheer is "Oskee Wee Wee, Oskee Waa Waa", and the mascot tradition includes T.C. and the pigskin Pete figure associated with the chant. The Tiger-Cats have a fierce rivalry with the Toronto Argonauts, contested in the annual Labour Day Classic.
