Breakthrough Listen

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


The Green Bank Telescope

Breakthrough Listen is a US$100 million, ten-year initiative launched in 2015 to search for evidence of intelligent life beyond Earth. Funded by Yuri Milner as part of the Breakthrough Initiatives, it is the largest scientific search for extraterrestrial intelligence (SETI) ever undertaken, using some of the world's most powerful radio and optical telescopes to survey the nearest million stars and the nearest hundred galaxies.

Facilities

The project buys observing time on the 100-metre Green Bank Telescope in West Virginia and the 64-metre Parkes Telescope in Australia for radio observations, and uses the Automated Planet Finder at Lick Observatory to search for laser signals. It later added data from China's Five-hundred-metre Aperture Spherical Telescope (FAST) and the MeerKAT array in South Africa. A key feature of the project is that its vast datasets are made publicly available, and its software is open source.

Results

Breakthrough Listen has catalogued millions of narrowband radio signals, all so far attributable to human technology or natural sources. In 2020 the project reported a candidate signal, BLC1, detected toward Proxima Centauri; subsequent analysis concluded it was interference from human-made electronics. The project has also published extensive surveys of nearby stars and galaxies and one of the largest SETI datasets ever released, amounting to petabytes of data.
