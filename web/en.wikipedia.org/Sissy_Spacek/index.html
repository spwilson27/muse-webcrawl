Mary Elizabeth "Sissy" Spacek (born December 25, 1949) is an American actress and singer. She is the recipient of numerous accolades, including an Academy Award, three Golden Globe Awards, and a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.

Career

Spacek gained attention with Badlands (1973) and achieved stardom as the title character of Brian De Palma's horror film Carrie (1976), earning her first Academy Award nomination. She won the Oscar for Best Actress for portraying country singer Loretta Lynn in Coal Miner's Daughter (1980), performing her own vocals. Further nominations followed for Missing (1982), The River (1984), Crimes of the Heart (1986), and In the Bedroom (2001).

Later work

Spacek has continued to work in film and television, including the series Bloodline, Castle Rock, and Homecoming, and published a memoir, My Extraordinary Ordinary Life, in 2012. She is married to production designer Jack Fisk, a frequent collaborator since Badlands.
