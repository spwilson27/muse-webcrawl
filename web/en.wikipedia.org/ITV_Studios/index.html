ITV Studios

ITV Studios is a British television production and distribution company owned by ITV plc. It is the largest television production company in the United Kingdom and one of the largest independent producers in the world, making programmes for ITV's own channels, other British broadcasters, and international networks and streaming services.

History

ITV Studios descends from the production arms of the ITV franchise companies, principally Granada Productions and Carlton Productions. Following the creation of ITV plc in 2004, these were combined as Granada Productions, then rebranded ITV Productions in 2006 and ITV Studios in 2009. In 2013 ITV plc began expanding the business substantially through acquisitions, buying into or purchasing production companies in the United States, the Netherlands, Germany, France, Australia, the Nordic countries and elsewhere.

Major acquisitions and labels have included Talpa Media (the Dutch format business founded by John de Mol, creator of The Voice), Mammoth Screen (Poldark, Endeavour), World Productions (Line of Duty, Bodyguard), Twofour Group, and Blumhouse's television interests. ITV Studios also manages global sales of formats and finished programmes.

Productions

The company's catalogue includes some of British television's longest-running programmes, such as the soaps Coronation Street and Emmerdale, and formats exported worldwide, including Love Island, I'm a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here!, The Voice, Hell's Kitchen, Come Dine with Me and Countdown. Dramas include Mr Bates vs The Post Office, Vera, Victoria and, through its labels, Bodyguard and Line of Duty.

Operations

ITV Studios is divided between its UK production business and ITV Studios International, with production bases in the United States, Australia, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland. It is a key part of ITV plc's Media & Entertainment division and one of the company's principal growth engines as viewing shifts to streaming.
