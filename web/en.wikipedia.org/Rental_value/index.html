Rental value

Rental value is the fair market value of property while rented out under a lease. It is a concept used in real estate appraisal, taxation, and insurance, representing the amount of rent a property could reasonably be expected to command in the open market.

Determination

Rental value is typically estimated by comparing rents of similar properties in the same area, adjusted for size, location, condition, and amenities. Appraisers may use the market (sales comparison) approach or the income capitalization approach.

Uses

The concept figures in property tax assessment (in some jurisdictions taxes are based on annual rental value), in insurance for loss-of-rent coverage, and in economics, where the imputed rent of owner-occupied housing is included in some national income measures.
