Wii Sports

DeveloperNintendo EAD
PublisherNintendo
PlatformWii
ReleaseNovember 19, 2006 (North America)
GenreSports simulation

Wii Sports is a 2006 sports simulation video game developed and published by Nintendo for the Wii video game console. The game was released in North America along with the Wii on November 19, 2006, and was bundled with the console in most regions outside Japan and South Korea, making it the first game included with a Nintendo system at launch since Mario's Tennis for the Virtual Boy.

Gameplay

The game consists of five sports simulations — tennis, baseball, bowling, golf, and boxing — each using the motion-sensing Wii Remote to mimic the actions of the real-life sport: players swing the controller to hit a tennis ball, roll a bowling ball, or throw punches. Players use their Mii avatars, and the game includes training modes and a fitness test that estimates a player's "Wii Fitness Age."

Reception and legacy

Wii Sports was a critical and commercial success and is considered one of the greatest and most influential video games of all time. It introduced video gaming to a wide non-traditional audience, including elderly players, and became a cultural phenomenon, with reports of use in nursing homes and physical rehabilitation. With over 82 million copies sold, largely through the console bundle, it is among the best-selling video games ever. A sequel, Wii Sports Resort, followed in 2009, and a successor, Nintendo Switch Sports, in 2022.
