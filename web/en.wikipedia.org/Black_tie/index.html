Black tie
Type: Dress code
Formality: Semi-formal evening wear

Black tie is a semi-formal Western dress code for evening events, originating in British and American conventions of dress in the 19th century. For men it consists of a black dinner jacket (tuxedo) with satin facings, matching trousers, a white dress shirt, a black bow tie, and black patent leather shoes.

History

The dinner jacket emerged in the 1860s as a less formal alternative to the tailcoat. The name "tuxedo" derives from Tuxedo Park, New York, where the style was popularized in the United States in 1886.

Women's attire

For women, black tie generally calls for an evening gown or cocktail dress. The code sits below white tie in formality and above business formal.
