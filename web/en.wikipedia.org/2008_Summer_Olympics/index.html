The 2008 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XXIX Olympiad and commonly known as Beijing 2008, were an international multi-sport event held from 8 to 24 August 2008 in Beijing, China. A total of 10,942 athletes from 204 National Olympic Committees competed in 28 sports and 302 events.

Beijing was awarded the Games over Toronto, Paris, Istanbul and Osaka following a vote of the International Olympic Committee on 13 July 2001. It was China's first time hosting the Olympic Games, and the third time the Summer Games were held in East Asia.

Opening ceremony

The opening ceremony, directed by film director Zhang Yimou, began at 8:08 pm China Standard Time on 8 August 2008 — the number eight being associated with prosperity in Chinese culture. It featured a cast of over 15,000 performers and a fireworks display. The Olympic cauldron was lit by former gymnast Li Ning, who was hoisted into the air and appeared to run around the rim of the stadium.

Sporting highlights

American swimmer Michael Phelps won eight gold medals, breaking Mark Spitz's record of seven golds at a single Games set in 1972. Jamaican sprinter Usain Bolt set world records in both the 100 metres (9.69 s) and 200 metres (19.30 s). China topped the gold medal table with 48 golds, while the United States won the most medals overall with 112.

Venues

The centrepiece venue was the Beijing National Stadium, nicknamed the "Bird's Nest" for its distinctive steel lattice structure, designed by Herzog & de Meuron with artist Ai Weiwei as artistic consultant. Aquatics events were held at the National Aquatics Center, the "Water Cube".
