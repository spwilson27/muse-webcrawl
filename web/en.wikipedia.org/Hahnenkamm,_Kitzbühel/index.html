Hahnenkamm, Kitzbühel

The Hahnenkamm is a mountain in the Kitzbühel Alps of Tyrol, Austria, rising to 1,712 metres above the town of Kitzbühel. It is world-famous as the venue of the Hahnenkamm Races (Hahnenkammrennen), held since 1931 and among the most prestigious events of the FIS Alpine Ski World Cup circuit.

The Streif

The mountain's downhill course, the Streif, is considered the most demanding and dangerous downhill on the World Cup circuit. It features gradients up to about 85 percent on the Mausefalle (mousetrap) jump, the steep Steilhang, and the high-speed Hausbergkante and traverse into the finish. Racers reach speeds around 140 km/h over a course of roughly 3.3 kilometres. The weekend programme also includes a super-G and the traditional slalom on the neighbouring Ganslernhang course, drawing tens of thousands of spectators to Kitzbühel each January.
