Kemal Reis

Kemal Reis (c. 1451 – 1511) was an Ottoman privateer and admiral, and the uncle of the famous cartographer Piri Reis. He served under sultans Bayezid II and (briefly before his death) was one of the most renowned Ottoman seamen of his era, active throughout the Mediterranean.

Career

Kemal Reis first gained fame as a corsair in the western Mediterranean, raiding the coasts of Spain and Italy and helping to evacuate Muslims and Jews from Spain after 1492. Entered into Ottoman imperial service, he commanded fleets against Venice during the Ottoman–Venetian War of 1499–1503, winning engagements near Zonchio and Modon.

In 1511 he drowned when his ship was wrecked in a storm in the Mediterranean while returning from an expedition. His nephew Piri Reis, who had sailed with him from youth, went on to produce the famous Piri Reis map of 1513.
