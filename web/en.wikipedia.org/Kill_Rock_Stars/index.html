Kill Rock Stars

Kill Rock Stars is an independent record label founded in 1991 by Slim Moon in Olympia, Washington, and now based in Portland, Oregon. It became one of the most influential labels of the American indie rock and punk underground, closely associated with the riot grrrl movement and the Pacific Northwest music scene.

History

Moon launched the label with a spoken-word 7-inch and a compilation album whose title — Kill Rock Stars — became the label's name. Early releases documented the Olympia scene, home to K Records and the Evergreen State College. The label's word-of-mouth releases by bands such as Bikini Kill helped define the feminist punk of riot grrrl.

Artists

Over three decades Kill Rock Stars has released music by Bikini Kill, Sleater-Kinney, Elliott Smith (Roman Candle and Elliott Smith), The Decemberists, Gossip, Deerhoof, Bratmobile, Quasi, and comedy recordings by acts including Cameron Esposito. The label also ran a sister imprint, 5 Rue Christine, for experimental music.

Later years

Slim Moon left the label in 2006 to work at Nonesuch Records; ownership passed to his wife, Portia Sabin, who later became president of the Music Business Association. The label continues to release new music and reissues, and marked its 30th anniversary in 2021 with a series of cover-version singles celebrating its catalogue.
