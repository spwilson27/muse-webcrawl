Lucas number

Sequence2, 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, 18, 29, 47, …
Named afterÉdouard Lucas

The Lucas numbers are an integer sequence closely related to the Fibonacci numbers. They satisfy the same recurrence, Ln = Ln−1 + Ln−2, but begin with L0 = 2 and L1 = 1, giving 2, 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, 18, 29, 47, …

Properties

Lucas numbers have the closed form Ln = φn + (1 − φ)n, where φ is the golden ratio. They relate to Fibonacci numbers through identities such as Ln = Fn−1 + Fn+1. Lucas primes are Lucas numbers that are prime; like Mersenne primes, they are connected with the search for large primes.
