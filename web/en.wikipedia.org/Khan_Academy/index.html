Khan Academy is an American non-profit educational organization founded in 2008 by Salman Khan. It produces short lessons in the form of videos, supplemented by practice exercises and tools for educators, with the stated mission of providing "a free, world-class education for anyone, anywhere."

History

The platform began when Salman Khan, then a hedge fund analyst, remotely tutored his cousin in mathematics using a Yahoo drawing tool and then YouTube videos, which attracted a wide audience. Khan quit his job in 2009 to work on the project full-time. Major philanthropic support followed from the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and Google, among others.

Content

Khan Academy offers material in mathematics, science, computing, history, economics, and test preparation, including official SAT preparation developed with the College Board and LSAT and MCAT courses. Content is localized into dozens of languages. The site tracks student progress with points and badges, and dashboards let teachers monitor classes. In 2023 Khan Academy launched Khanmigo, an AI-powered tutoring assistant built on large language models.

Reception

The organization has been praised for expanding access to instruction and popularizing mastery learning and the flipped classroom; criticism has included questions about video pedagogy and some errors in early materials.
