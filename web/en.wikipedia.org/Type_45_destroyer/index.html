The Type 45 destroyer, also known as the Daring class, is a class of six guided-missile destroyers built for the United Kingdom's Royal Navy. The class is primarily designed for anti-aircraft and anti-missile warfare and is built around the PAAMS (Sea Viper) air-defence system, which uses the SAMPSON multifunction radar and the Aster 15 and Aster 30 missiles.

Characteristics

The ships — Daring, Dauntless, Diamond, Dragon, Defender, and Duncan — were the first Royal Navy vessels to use integrated electric propulsion, powered by two WR-21 gas turbines and diesel generators driving electric motors. The SAMPSON radar sits atop a distinctive main mast. The class has been described as among the most capable air-defence destroyers in the world.

Service

The class has deployed worldwide, including air-defence operations in the Middle East and, in 2024, HMS Diamond's engagements against drones and missiles in the Red Sea. Under the Power Improvement Programme, the ships are receiving additional diesel generators to improve propulsion reliability in warm climates.
