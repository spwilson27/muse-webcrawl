Timeline of space exploration


A crewed launch

This is a timeline of space exploration, recording major milestones in humanity's exploration of outer space, from the first artificial satellite to present-day missions.

DateMilestone
4 October 1957Sputnik 1 (USSR) becomes the first artificial satellite.
3 November 1957Laika the dog becomes the first animal to orbit Earth aboard Sputnik 2.
12 April 1961Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space aboard Vostok 1.
16 June 1963Valentina Tereshkova becomes the first woman in space.
18 March 1965Alexei Leonov performs the first spacewalk.
20 July 1969Apollo 11: Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first humans to land on the Moon.
19 April 1971Salyut 1, the first space station, is launched.
1977Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 are launched to explore the outer planets.
12 April 1981First flight of the Space Shuttle (Columbia, STS-1).
25 April 1990The Hubble Space Telescope is deployed.
20 November 1998The first module of the International Space Station is launched.
2004SpaceShipOne makes the first privately developed crewed spaceflights.
14 July 2015New Horizons flies by Pluto.
25 December 2021The James Webb Space Telescope is launched.
2022Artemis I completes an uncrewed flight around the Moon.

Space exploration continues with crewed stations in low Earth orbit, robotic missions throughout the Solar System, and planned returns to the Moon under the Artemis programme.
