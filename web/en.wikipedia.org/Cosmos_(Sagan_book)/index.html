Cosmos (Sagan book)

Cosmos is a 1980 popular science book by astronomer Carl Sagan, companion to the television series Cosmos: A Personal Voyage, which Sagan co-wrote with Ann Druyan and Steven Soter.

Contents

Its thirteen chapters trace the history of astronomy and science from ancient Greece to the space age, covering the Library of Alexandria, Kepler, the Voyager missions, the search for extraterrestrial intelligence and the threat of nuclear war.

Reception

The book spent 70 weeks on bestseller lists and is among the best-selling science books ever published in English. A sequel volume updating it, by Ann Druyan, accompanied the 2020 series Cosmos: Possible Worlds.
