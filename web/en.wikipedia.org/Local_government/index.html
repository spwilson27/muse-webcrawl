Local government

Local government is a form of public administration which, in a majority of contexts, exists as the lowest tier of administration within a given state, below national and regional levels.

Functions

Local authorities typically provide services close to residents: roads, waste collection, planning, libraries, parks, local policing or fire services, and sometimes schools, housing and social care, depending on the country.

Forms

Forms include municipalities, counties, districts, parishes and special-purpose districts, governed by elected councils and sometimes directly elected mayors. Arrangements vary widely: unitary states tend to standardise local tiers, while federal states leave them to constituent states or provinces.

Finance

Local governments are funded by local taxes (such as property taxes or council tax), user charges and transfers from higher governments, and their autonomy is limited by law — in common-law countries by the principle that they may only do what statute permits.
