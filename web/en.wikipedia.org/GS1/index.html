GS1

GS1
Founded: 1974 (as Uniform Code Council)
Headquarters: Brussels, Belgium
Type: Non-profit standards organisation
Focus: Supply-chain standards, barcodes

GS1 is an international non-profit organisation that develops and maintains standards for business communication, most famously the barcode system used on retail products worldwide. Its best-known standard is the Global Trade Item Number (GTIN), encoded in barcodes scanned billions of times each day.

History

The organisation grew out of the Uniform Code Council in the United States, which administered the Universal Product Code (UPC) introduced in 1974, and the European Article Numbering association, founded in 1977. The two merged their standards work under the GS1 name in the 2000s.

Standards

GS1 manages the system of company prefixes that keep identifiers globally unique, and maintains standards including GTIN, the Global Location Number (GLN), the electronic product code (EPC) for RFID, and the Global Data Synchronisation Network (GDSN). National member organisations in more than 100 countries allocate prefixes and support local businesses.
