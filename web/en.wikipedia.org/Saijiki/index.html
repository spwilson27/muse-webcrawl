Saijiki

A saijiki is a Japanese reference book that lists and explains kigo — the seasonal words used in haiku and related forms of Japanese poetry. Because a haiku conventionally contains a word evoking a particular season, the saijiki serves as an essential guide for poets.

Contents

Saijiki are organised by the five Japanese poetic seasons (spring, summer, autumn, winter, and New Year). Each entry gives the kigo, its seasonal classification, an explanation of its associations and usage, and example verses by notable poets. Entries may be subdivided into categories such as the season itself, the heavens, the earth, human affairs, animals and plants.

History

Collections of seasonal words appeared from the Edo period onward as haikai practice developed. Modern saijiki, greatly expanded in the twentieth century, remain standard references; large editions may list thousands of kigo, and many are now available in digital form.
