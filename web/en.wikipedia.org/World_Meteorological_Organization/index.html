The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for promoting international cooperation on atmospheric science, climatology, hydrology, and related geophysical sciences. It succeeded the International Meteorological Organization, founded in 1873.

The WMO headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland.

Activities

The WMO coordinates the World Weather Watch, a global system of satellites, stations, and data-processing centers for observing and exchanging weather data. It also coordinates the naming of tropical cyclones through regional specialized meteorological centers and issues the annual State of the Global Climate report.

Structure

The World Meteorological Congress, meeting every four years, is the supreme body. An Executive Council and a Secretariat in Geneva carry out its decisions. The Secretary-General heads the Secretariat; Celeste Saulo of Argentina took office in January 2024 as the organization's first female Secretary-General.
