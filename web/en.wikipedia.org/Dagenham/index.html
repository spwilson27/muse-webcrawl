Dagenham
Location: East London, England
Borough: Barking and Dagenham
Historic county: Essex

Dagenham is a town in East London, England, within the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham, about 11.5 miles east of Charing Cross.

History

First mentioned in a charter of 666 AD as Dæccanhaam, Dagenham remained a rural village until the 1920s, when the London County Council built the vast Becontree housing estate. Ford opened its Dagenham plant in 1931, long a major employer in vehicle and engine production.

Transport

The area is served by Dagenham Heathway, Dagenham East, and Becontree stations on the District line of the London Underground.

Sport

It is home to Dagenham & Redbridge Football Club, nicknamed the Daggers.
