Tessa Wheeler

Tessa Wheeler is a fictional character in the Librarians universe of television films and series. She appears in the fantasy-adventure franchise that began with the 2004 television film The Librarian: Quest for the Spear, which follows the adventures of the custodians of a vast hidden collection of magical and historically significant artifacts stored beneath a great library.

Character

Within the franchise's mythology, the Library is an ancient institution that collects and safeguards powerful artifacts — from Excalibur to the Ark of the Covenant — keeping them out of the hands of those who would misuse them. Characters in the series serve as Librarians and Guardians, travelling the world to recover lost or stolen items, solve historical mysteries, and battle rival organisations such as the Serpent Brotherhood.

The franchise

The original trilogy of films starred Noah Wyle as Flynn Carsen, a perennial student recruited to protect the Library's collection. The films were followed by the television series The Librarians (2014–2018), which expanded the premise to a team of new Librarians working alongside Carsen and their Guardian, Eve Baird.
