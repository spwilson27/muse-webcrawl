Kauto Star (19 March 2000 – 29 June 2015) was a French-bred British-trained Thoroughbred racehorse regarded as one of the greatest steeplechasers of all time. Trained by Paul Nicholls and usually ridden by Ruby Walsh, he won the Cheltenham Gold Cup twice and the King George VI Chase a record five times.

Career

Kauto Star won the Tingle Creek Chase twice over two miles and proved equally effective at the Gold Cup trip of three and a quarter miles, an unusual versatility. In the 2006–07 season he won the inaugural Betfair Million bonus by winning the Betfair Chase, King George VI Chase and Cheltenham Gold Cup. He regained the Gold Cup in 2009 after losing it to stablemate Denman in 2008, becoming the first horse to regain the trophy. His fifth King George in 2011, at age eleven, confirmed his longevity at the top level.

Retirement and death

Retired in 2012, Kauto Star began a new career in dressage. He was put down on 29 June 2015 after injuring himself in a fall in his paddock.
