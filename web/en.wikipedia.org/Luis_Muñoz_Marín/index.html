Luis Muñoz Marín

Luis Muñoz Marín
Born: 18 February 1898, San Juan, Puerto Rico
Died: 30 April 1980, San Juan
Office: 1st Governor of Puerto Rico (1949–1965)
Party: Popular Democratic Party

José Luis Alberto Muñoz Marín (18 February 1898 – 30 April 1980) was a Puerto Rican journalist, politician, statesman and poet regarded as the "Father of Modern Puerto Rico" and the "Architect of the Puerto Rican Commonwealth".

Early life

Muñoz Marín was born in San Juan, the son of Luis Muñoz Rivera, a prominent politician and Resident Commissioner of Puerto Rico in Washington, D.C. He studied in the United States and worked as a journalist and poet before entering politics.

Political career

He founded the Popular Democratic Party (Partido Popular Democrático) in 1938, which won the legislative elections of 1940. As Senate president he championed land reform, economic development and the "Operation Bootstrap" industrialisation programme. In 1948 he became the first democratically elected governor of Puerto Rico, taking office on 2 January 1949, and served four terms until 1965.

Commonwealth

Under his leadership Puerto Rico adopted its own constitution in 1952, establishing the Commonwealth (Estado Libre Asociado) in free association with the United States. He remains one of the most influential figures in twentieth-century Puerto Rican history.
