Arthashastra

Author: Attributed to Kautilya (Chanakya)

Language: Sanskrit

Subject: Statecraft, economics, military strategy

The Arthashastra is an ancient Indian Sanskrit treatise on statecraft, political science, economic policy and military strategy. Tradition attributes it to Kautilya, also identified with Chanakya, the adviser of Chandragupta Maurya, though the surviving text was likely composed and redacted over several centuries.

Contents

The treatise covers the duties of the king, administration, law and justice, taxation, foreign policy (including the mandala theory of inter-state relations), espionage, and the management of markets, mines and forests. It advocates a strong, centralised state guided by the ruler's welfare calculus.

Rediscovery

The text was lost for centuries and rediscovered in 1905 when a manuscript was identified by R. Shamasastry, who published and translated it, transforming the study of ancient Indian political thought.
