List of German football champions

The German football champions are the annual winners of the highest domestic competition in German football. A national championship was first contested in 1903 under the German Football Association (DFB), decided by a knockout final between regional champions. From 1963 the champion has been the winner of the Bundesliga, a national round-robin league. Between 1949 and 1991 East Germany held its own championship (the DDR-Oberliga), whose titles are not counted in the unified list. The record champions are Bayern Munich, who won eleven consecutive Bundesliga titles from 2013 to 2023.

Champions by era

Early finals were dominated by clubs such as VfB Leipzig (the first champions, 1903), 1. FC Nürnberg and SpVgg Fürth in the 1920s, and Schalke 04, who won six titles between 1934 and 1942. The post-war Oberliga era saw championships for 1. FC Nürnberg, Hamburger SV, Borussia Dortmund and others; the last pre-Bundesliga champions were Dortmund in 1963.

Titles overall

Counting all national championships since 1903, Bayern Munich lead by a wide margin (more than 30 titles), followed by 1. FC Nürnberg (9), Borussia Dortmund (8), Schalke 04 (7), Hamburger SV (6), Borussia Mönchengladbach and VfB Stuttgart (5 each). Kaiserslautern's 1998 title, won the season after promotion, remains unique in Bundesliga history; Bayer Leverkusen's unbeaten 2023–24 season ended Bayern's eleven-year streak.
