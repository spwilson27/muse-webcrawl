The Argentina national football team (Spanish: Selección de fútbol de Argentina) represents Argentina in men's international football and is administered by the Argentine Football Association, the governing body for football in Argentina. Nicknamed La Albiceleste ("The White and Sky Blue"), Argentina is one of the most successful national teams in the world, having won three FIFA World Cups (1978, 1986, and 2022), a record 16 Copa América titles, two Olympic gold medals (2004, 2008), and the 2022 Finalissima.

History

Argentina played its first international match against Uruguay in 1902 and was a founding power of South American football, reaching the first World Cup final in 1930, losing to Uruguay. After decades of near-misses, the team won the World Cup as hosts in 1978 under César Luis Menotti, with Mario Kempes the tournament's star. Diego Maradona inspired the second title in Mexico in 1986 — including the "Hand of God" goal and the "Goal of the Century" in the same quarter-final against England — and Argentina finished runners-up in 1990 and again in 2014, when Lionel Messi's side lost to Germany in extra time.

The Messi era and 2022 triumph

Under coach Lionel Scaloni, appointed in 2018, Argentina ended a 28-year trophy drought by winning the Copa América in 2021 in Brazil, Messi's first senior international title. In Qatar in 2022 Argentina recovered from a shock opening defeat to Saudi Arabia to win the World Cup, defeating France in a final widely described as the greatest ever: Messi scored twice, Kylian Mbappé scored a hat-trick, and Argentina prevailed 4–2 on penalties after a 3–3 draw. Messi won the Golden Ball, and the triumph sparked mass celebrations in Buenos Aires. Argentina then defended the Copa América in 2024, defeating Colombia in the final in the United States, and spent an extended period ranked first in the FIFA World Rankings.

Style and rivalry

Argentina's traditional style emphasises technique, improvisation, and the playmaking enganche, though Scaloni's teams added tactical flexibility and defensive solidity. The team's principal rivalries are with Brazil (the Superclásico de las Américas), Uruguay, and England. Home matches are usually played at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires.

Honours

  • Copa América: 16 titles, most recently 2024
  • Olympic gold: 2004, 2008
