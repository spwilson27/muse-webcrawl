9/80

A 9/80 work schedule is a compressed workweek arrangement in which employees work 80 hours over nine working days in a two-week period, instead of the conventional ten working days. In the most common implementation, an employee works eight 9-hour days and one 8-hour day in each pay period, receiving one day off every two weeks, typically a Friday.

Structure

The schedule usually splits a Friday into two 4-hour halves belonging to consecutive workweeks, so that over two weeks the employee works 40 hours each week while taking every other Friday off. The arrangement originated in the United States defence and aerospace industries and is common among government agencies and contractors.

Advantages and criticism

Supporters cite improved morale, reduced commuting, and easier recruitment. Critics note payroll and overtime complications under the Fair Labor Standards Act, since the workweek boundary must be set so that no week exceeds 40 hours before overtime applies.
