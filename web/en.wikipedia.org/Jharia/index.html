Jharia

Jharia is a town in Dhanbad district in the Indian state of Jharkhand, famous as the centre of India's richest coalfield. The Jharia coalfield contains the country's largest reserves of prime coking coal.

Mining began in the late 19th century. The field is notorious for underground coal fires burning since 1916, which have damaged land and buildings and forced plans to relocate residents. Jharia was once among the most prosperous mining towns in India, ruled before nationalization by the Raja of Jharia.
