Presidential $1 Coin Program

The Presidential dollar coin program was a United States Mint series of one-dollar coins featuring portraits of U.S. presidents in chronological order. Authorized by the Presidential $1 Coin Act of 2005, the program issued four coins per year beginning with George Washington in 2007.

Design

Each coin bears the president's portrait and name on the obverse and the Statue of Liberty on the reverse, with edge lettering including the year, mint mark, and the mottos "E Pluribus Unum" and "In God We Trust" (the latter moved to the obverse in 2009). A president had to have been deceased for at least two years to be honored.

History

Coins were released from 2007 through 2016, ending with Ronald Reagan, and were issued for general circulation until 2011; thereafter they were produced for collectors only. George H. W. Bush was added by later legislation in 2020. The companion First Spouse gold coin program honored the presidents' spouses.
