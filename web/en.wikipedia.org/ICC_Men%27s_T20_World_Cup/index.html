The ICC Men's T20 World Cup is the Twenty20 International championship of cricket, organized by the International Cricket Council (ICC). The tournament has generally been held every two years since the inaugural edition in 2007, which was won by India.

History

The first tournament in South Africa in 2007 introduced the short Twenty20 format to a global championship stage and finished with India defeating Pakistan in a celebrated final. Subsequent editions have been hosted around the cricketing world, with the West Indies and England among the multiple winners. From 2024 the field expanded to twenty teams.

Format

Teams progress from a group stage through knockouts or a Super 8 stage to semi-finals and a final. Matches are played over twenty overs per side, typically lasting about three and a half hours.
