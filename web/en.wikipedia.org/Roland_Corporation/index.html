Roland Corporation

Founded: 18 April 1972, Osaka, Japan
Founder: Ikutaro Kakehashi
Products: Electronic musical instruments, synthesizers, drum machines, digital pianos
Subsidiaries: Boss Corporation

Roland Corporation (ローランド株式会社, Rōrando Kabushiki-gaisha) is a Japanese multinational manufacturer of electronic musical instruments, electronic equipment, and software. Founded by Ikutaro Kakehashi in Osaka in 1972, Roland has profoundly influenced popular music through products such as the TR-808 and TR-909 drum machines, the Jupiter and Juno synthesizers, and the Boss line of guitar effects pedals.

History

Kakehashi founded Roland after leaving Ace Tone, which he had also founded. Early successes included rhythm machines and space-echo tape delays. The SH-1000 (1973) was Japan's first domestic synthesizer with a keyboard, and the System-700 modular and Jupiter-8 (1981) established Roland among professional musicians. The TR-808 (1980), initially a commercial disappointment, became a cornerstone of hip hop, house, and electro; the TB-303 bass synthesizer defined acid house.

Later developments

Roland helped develop the MIDI standard in 1983. Its D-50 (1987) was a landmark digital synthesizer, and the V-Drums and digital pianos expanded its range. Kakehashi received a Technical Grammy Award in 2013 for his contributions. The company went private in 2014 through a management buyout supported by Taiyo Pacific Partners. Its subsidiary Boss is a leading maker of compact effects pedals.
