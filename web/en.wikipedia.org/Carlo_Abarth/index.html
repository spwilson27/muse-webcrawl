Carlo Abarth

Carlo Abarth
Born: 15 November 1908, Vienna, Austria-Hungary
Died: 24 October 1979, Vienna, Austria
Occupation: Automobile designer, racer
Known for: Founding Abarth

Carlo Abarth (15 November 1908 – 24 October 1979), born Karl Albert Abarth, was an Austrian-born Italian automobile designer and racing driver, founder of the Abarth marque famous for high-performance versions of Fiat cars.

Early career

Abarth raced motorcycles and sidecars in his youth, winning several European events, and later worked with the Cisitalia racing team under Piero Dusio in Italy. When Cisitalia collapsed, he founded his own company in Turin in 1949, adopting his zodiac sign, the scorpion, as its badge.

Abarth & C.

Abarth & C. built racing cars and, especially, performance exhaust systems and tuning kits that transformed small Fiat models — above all the Fiat 500 and 600 — into successful competition cars. Abarth cars won numerous class victories and set speed records in the 1950s and 1960s. In 1971 Abarth sold the company to Fiat, where the name became the group's racing and performance division; the brand was relaunched as a standalone marque in 2007 with models such as the Abarth 500.
