Kurdistan Province

Kurdistan Province (Persian: استان کردستان; Kurdish: پارێزگای کوردستان) is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, located in the west of the country bordering Iraq. Its capital is the city of Sanandaj. The province covers an area of about 29,000 square kilometres and has a population of roughly 1.6 million, the majority of them ethnic Kurds.

Geography

The province is mountainous, forming part of the Zagros range, with a continental climate of cold, snowy winters and mild summers. Its counties include Sanandaj, Marivan, Saqqez, Baneh, Qorveh, Bijar, Kamyaran, Divandarreh, Dehgolan and Sarvabad.

Culture and economy

The population speaks Kurdish (mainly Sorani and Southern Kurdish dialects) and Persian. The province is known for its music, handicrafts such as carpets and woodwork, and the terraced village of Palangan and the lake Zarivar near Marivan. Agriculture, animal husbandry and border trade form the main economic activities.
