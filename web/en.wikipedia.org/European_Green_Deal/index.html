European Green Deal

The European Green Deal is a set of policy initiatives by the European Commission presented in December 2019, with the overarching aim of making the European Union climate neutral by 2050.

Goals

Proposals include cutting greenhouse gas emissions by at least 55% by 2030 compared to 1990 levels, investing in renewable energy, energy efficiency, circular economy and biodiversity protection, including through the Fit for 55 package and the European Climate Law.
