Disney+ is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming media service owned and operated by Disney Streaming, the streaming division of Disney Entertainment, a major business segment of the Walt Disney Company. Launched on November 12, 2019, the service primarily distributes films and television series produced by Walt Disney Studios and Disney Television Studios, built around content hubs for Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, National Geographic, and, in some regions, the more general-oriented Star brand.

History

Disney announced the service in 2017 as part of its shift toward direct-to-consumer distribution, and ended its film distribution deal with Netflix in preparation. Disney acquired a majority stake in BAMTech to power the platform. Disney+ launched in the United States, Canada and the Netherlands on November 12, 2019, and gained more than 10 million subscribers on its first day and over 100 million within its first sixteen months. It subsequently rolled out across Europe, Latin America and Asia-Pacific, in some markets as Disney+ Hotstar.

Content

The service hosts the Disney animated and live-action catalogue, Pixar films, the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Star Wars films and series, and National Geographic programming. It became the exclusive streaming home of original series such as The Mandalorian, WandaVision, Loki, The Falcon and the Winter Soldier, Ms. Marvel and Andor, as well as the complete library of The Simpsons. An advertising-supported tier was introduced in late 2022, and Hulu content was progressively integrated into the service in the United States.

Subscribers

Disney+ grew rapidly after launch, reaching roughly 130 million subscribers by early 2022 and around 150 million combined subscribers (including Disney+ Hotstar) at its peak, before subscriber counts declined in some regions amid price rises and content-strategy changes. It remains one of the largest streaming services in the world alongside Netflix and Amazon Prime Video.
