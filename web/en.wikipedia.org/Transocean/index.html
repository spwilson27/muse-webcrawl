Transocean Ltd. is an American-Swiss offshore drilling contractor, one of the world's largest. The company rents floating mobile platforms (semisubmersibles and drillships) and provides the crews and equipment to drill wells for its customers, principally oil and gas exploration and production companies.

History

Transocean traces its origins to the Southern Natural Gas Company and its Sonat Offshore division, spun off in the 1990s. A series of mergers — with Sedco Forex in 1999, GlobalSantaFe in 2007, Aker Drilling, Songa Offshore, and Ocean Rig — built the modern fleet. The company is incorporated in Switzerland and operationally based in Houston, Texas.

Deepwater Horizon

Transocean owned and operated the Deepwater Horizon semisubmersible, leased to BP, whose blowout on 20 April 2010 caused the largest marine oil spill in history. Transocean reached settlements with the U.S. government and plaintiffs totalling billions of dollars, while official investigations assigned shared responsibility among BP, Transocean, and Halliburton.

Fleet

The fleet specialises in ultra-deepwater and harsh-environment floaters, including drillships capable of drilling in water depths exceeding 3,000 metres (10,000 ft).
