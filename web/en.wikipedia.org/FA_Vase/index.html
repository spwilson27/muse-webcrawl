FA Vase

The Football Association Challenge Vase, generally known as the FA Vase, is an annual football competition for teams playing in Steps 5 and 6 of the English National League System (or equivalent). The competition is played on a knockout basis with a final traditionally held at Wembley Stadium.

Founded1974
RegionEngland
Number of teams~600

History

The FA Vase was introduced in the 1974–75 season, following the abolition of the FA Amateur Cup, to cater for clubs that had turned semi-professional. The first final was won by Matlock Town, who beat Scarborough at Wembley.

Records

Romford, Chertsey Town, and Whitley Bay are among the notable winners; Whitley Bay won the trophy three years in succession between 2009 and 2011.
