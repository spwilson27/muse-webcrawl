Thrilla in Manila

Date1 October 1975
VenueAraneta Coliseum, Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines
FightersMuhammad Ali vs Joe Frazier
ResultAli won by technical knockout after round 14
TitlesWBA and WBC heavyweight championships

The Thrilla in Manila was the third and final boxing match between Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier, held on 1 October 1975 at the Araneta Coliseum in Quezon City, Philippines, for the world heavyweight championship. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest and most brutal fights in boxing history.

The fight

The bout was the rubber match of the rivalry: Frazier had won their first meeting in 1971 (the "Fight of the Century"), and Ali the rematch in 1974. Fighting at 10:45 in the morning local time — scheduled for American prime-time television — in punishing heat, the two men fought at a ferocious pace. Ali dominated the early rounds with jabs; Frazier rallied in the middle rounds with relentless body attack and hooks; Ali then seized control in the late rounds, closing Frazier's eyes with swelling. After the fourteenth round, Frazier's trainer Eddie Futch stopped the fight despite his fighter's protests, giving Ali a technical-knockout victory.

Aftermath and legacy

Ali later said the fight was "next to death" and that he had told his trainer to cut his gloves off after the fourteenth; both fighters' careers declined sharply afterwards, and the fight is remembered as the summit of their rivalry and of the golden age of heavyweight boxing. The event's rhyming name was coined by Ali himself, who taunted: "It will be a killa and a thrilla and a chilla, when I get that gorilla in Manila."
