Academic library

Academic library
Type: Library
Parent: University or college
Users: Students, faculty, researchers

An academic library is a library that is attached to a higher education institution and serves two complementary purposes: to support the curriculum, and to support the research of the university faculty and students.

Services

Academic libraries provide access to books, journals, databases, and special collections; reference and research assistance; instruction in information literacy; interlibrary loan; and study spaces. Many also maintain institutional repositories for theses, dissertations, and faculty publications.

History

University libraries date to the medieval universities of Europe. In the United States, college libraries grew substantially after the Second World War with the expansion of higher education and research funding.

Digital era

Modern academic libraries increasingly provide electronic resources, learning commons, makerspaces, and research data management services, shifting emphasis from print collections to digital access and student learning spaces.
