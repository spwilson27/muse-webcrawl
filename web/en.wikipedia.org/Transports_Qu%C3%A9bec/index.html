The Ministère des Transports et de la Mobilité durable du Québec (Ministry of Transport and Sustainable Mobility), commonly known as Transports Québec, is the ministry of the Government of Quebec responsible for transport infrastructure and policy in the province, including the provincial highway network, and for supporting public transit, rail, marine, and air transport.

Responsibilities

The ministry plans, builds, and maintains Quebec's autoroutes and national highways, oversees road safety policy (with the Société de l'assurance automobile du Québec handling licensing and insurance), and funds municipal and regional public transit authorities. It is also responsible for winter road maintenance standards across the provincial network.

History

The ministry descends from the provincial roads department created in the early 20th century as Quebec built its highway system. Major projects under its authority include the Turcot Interchange reconstruction in Montreal and the third link projects debated for Quebec City.
