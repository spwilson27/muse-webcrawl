Kurup (film)

Kurup (2021), the Malayalam biographical crime thriller about fugitive Sukumara Kurup.

Kurup is a 2021 Indian Malayalam-language biographical crime thriller film directed by Srinath Rajendran and written by K. S. Aravind, Jithin K. Jose, and Daniell Sayooj Nair. The film stars Dulquer Salmaan in the title role as Sukumara Kurup, one of India's most wanted fugitives, alongside Sobhita Dhulipala, Indrajith Sukumaran, and Shine Tom Chacko. It was produced by Dulquer's Wayfarer Films in association with M-Star Entertainments.

Background

The film fictionalizes the case of Sukumara Kurup, who in 1984 allegedly faked his own death by murdering a hitchhiker, Chacko, whose body was burned in Kurup's car in order to claim a large life-insurance payout. Kurup fled and was never apprehended, becoming a legendary absconder in Kerala; the film frames the story through the investigation of a police officer (played by Indrajith Sukumaran) across decades. The makers took care to keep the identity of the real killer partly ambiguous, presenting the story as inspired by, rather than a literal account of, the case.

Release and reception

Released theatrically on 12 November 2021, Kurup became one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films of its year, earning over ₹100 crore worldwide, and was dubbed into Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and Kannada. Critics praised Dulquer Salmaan's performance, the period production design, and Sushin Shyam's score, while noting the film's romanticized treatment of its subject. The family of the victim Chacko publicly objected to the film, and their concerns contributed to the filmmakers' disclaimers that the work is fictionalized.
