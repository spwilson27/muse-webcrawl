Tamiya Corporation (株式会社タミヤ) is a Japanese manufacturer of plastic model kits, radio-controlled cars, battery- and solar-powered educational models, sailboat models, modeling tools, and paints. Founded in 1946 in Shizuoka by Yoshio Tamiya, the company began as a sawmill and lumber business before moving into wooden and then plastic models.

Products

Tamiya is renowned for the precision and detail of its scale model kits of aircraft, military vehicles, ships, and automobiles. It pioneered high-performance radio-controlled off-road buggies in the 1980s and launched the hugely popular Mini 4WD miniature racing car series in 1982.

Logo

The company's two-star logo was designed in 1960; the stars represent creativity and passion, in red and blue.
