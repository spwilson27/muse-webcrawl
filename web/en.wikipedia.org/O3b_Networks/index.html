O3b Networks is a satellite communications company operating a constellation of satellites in medium Earth orbit (MEO) to provide broadband connectivity to remote and underserved regions. The name "O3b" stands for "other three billion", referring to the share of the world's population once lacking internet access.

Constellation

Founded by Greg Wyler, the company launched its first four satellites in June 2013 aboard a Soyuz rocket from French Guiana, with services beginning in 2014. The fleet grew to around 20 satellites orbiting at roughly 8,000 km, giving lower latency than geostationary systems. SES, an early investor, took full ownership in 2016. A second-generation system, O3b mPOWER, with higher throughput and flexible beam shaping, began launching in December 2022.

Customers

Services target telecom operators, governments, maritime and cruise ships, and energy platforms in equatorial and mid-latitude regions.
