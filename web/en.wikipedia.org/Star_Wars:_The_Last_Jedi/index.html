Star Wars: The Last Jedi

Theatrical release poster.

Star Wars: The Last Jedi (also known as Star Wars: Episode VIII – The Last Jedi) is a 2017 American epic space opera film written and directed by Rian Johnson. Produced by Lucasfilm and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures, it is the second film in the Star Wars sequel trilogy, following The Force Awakens (2015), and the eighth episode of the main Skywalker saga.

Plot

Rey seeks out the reclusive Luke Skywalker on the island planet Ahch-To to be trained in the ways of the Force, while the Resistance, led by General Leia Organa, flees the pursuing First Order under Supreme Leader Snoke. Kylo Ren, torn between the light and the dark, confronts Rey and his master, and Luke ultimately projects himself across the galaxy to confront his former student on the salt planet Crait, buying the Resistance time to escape.

Cast

The ensemble cast includes Mark Hamill, Carrie Fisher, Adam Driver, Daisy Ridley, John Boyega, Oscar Isaac, Andy Serkis, Lupita Nyong'o, Domhnall Gleeson, Anthony Daniels and Gwendoline Christie, with Kelly Marie Tran, Laura Dern and Benicio del Toro joining. The film was Fisher's final completed role before her death in December 2016 and is dedicated to her.

Reception

The Last Jedi grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2017. Critics praised its direction, performances and visual style, while audience reaction was sharply divided over its treatment of Luke Skywalker and its plot choices, making it one of the most debated entries in the franchise. It was followed by The Rise of Skywalker (2019).
