UCI Asia Tour

The UCI Asia Tour is a series of road bicycle races held in Asia, organised by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI). It is one of the UCI Continental Circuits, created in 2005 to develop professional cycling below the level of the UCI World Tour.

Structure

The calendar includes stage races and one-day events in countries such as Malaysia (Tour de Langkawi), Japan, China, Iran and Taiwan, classified in categories from 2.2 up to 2.HC/ProSeries level. Riders, teams and nations earn ranking points based on their results.

Development

The Asia Tour has helped raise the profile of Asian cycling, providing a pathway for Asian continental teams and riders to compete against visiting European squads and to qualify for events such as the UCI Road World Championships.
