Cao Xueqin
Born: c. 1715, Nanjing
Died: c. 1763/1764, Beijing
Author of Dream of the Red Chamber

Cao Xueqin (Chinese: 曹雪芹; c. 1715 – c. 1763) was a Chinese novelist of the Qing dynasty, best known as the author of Dream of the Red Chamber, one of the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature.

Life

Cao was born into a wealthy family of bondservants who served as textile commissioners in Nanjing and enjoyed the favour of the Kangxi Emperor. After the family's property was confiscated in 1728 under the Yongzheng Emperor, Cao lived in poverty near Beijing, writing his great novel in straitened circumstances.

Dream of the Red Chamber

The novel, circulated in manuscript during his lifetime, chronicles the rise and fall of the Jia clan. Only the first 80 chapters are generally attributed to Cao; the 120-chapter printed edition of 1791 was completed by other hands.
