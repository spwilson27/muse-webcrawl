François Hollande

François Gérard Georges Nicolas Hollande (born 12 August 1954) is a French politician who served as President of France from 2012 to 2017.

Career

A graduate of HEC Paris, Sciences Po, and the École nationale d'administration, Hollande served as First Secretary of the Socialist Party (1997–2008), mayor of Tulle, and president of the general council of Corrèze. He defeated incumbent Nicolas Sarkozy in the 2012 presidential election.

Presidency

His presidency oversaw the legalization of same-sex marriage, military interventions in Mali and the Central African Republic, the Paris climate agreement (COP21) of 2015, and responses to the 2015–2016 Paris terrorist attacks. Facing record unpopularity, he announced in December 2016 that he would not seek re-election—the first president of the Fifth Republic to do so. He later returned to the National Assembly as a deputy in 2024.
