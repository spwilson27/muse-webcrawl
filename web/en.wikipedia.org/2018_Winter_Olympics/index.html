The 2018 Winter Olympics, officially the XXIII Olympic Winter Games, were held from 9 to 25 February 2018 in Pyeongchang County, South Korea. They were the first Winter Olympics in South Korea and the country's second Olympic Games after Seoul 1988. Pyeongchang won the bid at its third attempt, defeating Munich and Annecy.

Korean diplomacy

The Games were marked by a diplomatic thaw between North and South Korea. The two countries marched together at the opening ceremony under the Korean Unification Flag, and fielded a unified women's ice hockey team, following last-minute talks held against the background of North Korean nuclear tensions. North Korean leader Kim Jong Un's sister, Kim Yo Jong, attended — the first member of the Kim family to visit the South since the Korean War.

Russian doping fallout

Because of the state-sponsored doping scandal, Russia's Olympic Committee was suspended, and cleared Russian athletes competed as "Olympic Athletes from Russia" under the Olympic flag. Two of them nevertheless failed doping tests during the Games.

Sporting highlights

Norway topped the medal table with a record-equalling 14 gold medals and 39 medals in total. Norwegian cross-country skier Marit Bjørgen became the most decorated Winter Olympian ever with 15 career medals. Czech athlete Ester Ledecká stunned the Games by winning gold in both alpine skiing (super-G, on borrowed skis) and snowboarding (parallel giant slalom) — the first woman to win gold in two different sports at a single Winter Olympics. New events included big air snowboarding, mass start speed skating, mixed doubles curling and the alpine team event.
