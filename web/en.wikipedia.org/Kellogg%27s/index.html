Kellogg's is the brand name under which the Kellogg Company (now Kellanova) has produced breakfast cereals and convenience foods since the early twentieth century. Its best-known products include Corn Flakes, Rice Krispies, Frosted Flakes (Frosties), Special K, and Pop-Tarts.

History

The company grew out of John Harvey Kellogg's Battle Creek Sanitarium, where he and his brother Will Keith Kellogg developed flaked cereal as a health food; corn flakes were patented in the 1890s. After a bitter dispute between the brothers, W. K. Kellogg founded his own company in 1906 and built the brand through mass advertising, mascots such as Tony the Tiger, and prizes in cereal boxes.

Recent developments

Kellogg acquired the Pringles brand in 2012, becoming a major global snack company. In 2023 the Kellogg Company split into two businesses: WK Kellogg Co, holding the North American cereal portfolio, and Kellanova, holding snacks and international cereals. In 2024 Mars, Incorporated agreed to acquire Kellanova.
