Citi Field
Location: Flushing Meadows–Corona Park, Queens, New York
Opened: 2009
Capacity: about 41,900
Tenants: New York Mets (MLB)

Citi Field is a baseball stadium in Flushing Meadows–Corona Park in Queens, New York City. It is the home ballpark of the New York Mets of Major League Baseball. The stadium opened in 2009, replacing the adjacent Shea Stadium, which had been the Mets' home since 1964.

Design

Designed by the architectural firm Populous (then HOK Sport), Citi Field's brick, limestone, and arched exterior recalls Brooklyn's Ebbets Field, while its entrance features the Jackie Robinson Rotunda, honouring the player who broke baseball's colour barrier. Citigroup purchased the naming rights in a 20-year deal announced in 2006.

Events

Citi Field hosted the 2013 MLB All-Star Game and the 2015 World Series, in which the Mets lost to the Kansas City Royals. It has also hosted soccer matches, concerts, and the NHL Winter Classic in 2018.
