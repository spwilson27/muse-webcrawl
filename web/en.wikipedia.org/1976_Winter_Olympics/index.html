The 1976 Winter Olympics, officially the XII Olympic Winter Games, were held from 4 to 15 February 1976 in Innsbruck, Austria. Innsbruck stepped in after Denver, Colorado — the original host — withdrew in 1972 when voters rejected public funding in a referendum.

Highlights

Austrian Franz Klammer's daring downhill run before a home crowd became one of the most famous in Olympic history. American Dorothy Hamill won the ladies' figure skating title and popularised the "wedge" haircut. The Soviet Union led the medal table, and ice dance made its Olympic debut.

Venue note

Innsbruck had previously hosted the 1964 Winter Olympics, making it one of the few cities to stage the Winter Games twice.
