Lieutenant-Colonel John By (1779–1836) was a British military engineer best known for supervising the construction of the Rideau Canal in Canada, around whose Ottawa terminus grew Bytown — the settlement that became Ottawa, Canada's capital.

Rideau Canal

Sent to Canada in 1826, By built the 202-kilometre canal between Kingston and the Ottawa River in just over five years, an engineering feat accomplished with hand labour through wilderness, swamp and rock. The canal was intended as a secure supply route in case of war with the United States.

Recall and legacy

By was recalled to London in 1832 amid questions over expenses and was never officially commended, dying in 1836. Today the canal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site; Ottawa's ByWard Market and Colonel By Drive bear his name, and a statue stands by the canal's Ottawa locks.
