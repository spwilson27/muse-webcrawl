Night Ranger

Night Ranger is an American rock band formed in San Francisco in 1979, best known for the power ballad "Sister Christian" (1984) and other 1980s arena-rock hits such as "(You Can Still) Rock in America" and "Don't Tell Me You Love Me".

Career

The band's core lineup featured Jack Blades (bass, vocals), Kelly Keagy (drums, vocals), Brad Gillis and Jeff Watson (guitars) and Alan Fitzgerald (keyboards). Their debut album Dawn Patrol (1982) and follow-up Midnight Madness (1983) brought commercial success, with "Sister Christian" reaching the US top five. After declining sales and lineup changes the band split in 1989, reuniting in the mid-1990s and continuing to record and tour, with later albums including Somewhere in California (2011) and ATBPO (2021).
