List of states and territories of the United States

The United States is a federal union of 50 states, a federal district (the District of Columbia), and several territories. The states share sovereignty with the federal government; the most populous is California (about 39 million) and the largest by area is Alaska. This article lists all fifty states with their capitals and admission dates, followed by the territories.

Territories

The inhabited territories are Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands; the United States also administers several minor outlying islands. Residents of territories are generally U.S. citizens (American Samoans are U.S. nationals) but do not vote in presidential elections and have non-voting delegates in Congress.
