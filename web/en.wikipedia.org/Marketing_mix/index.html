Marketing mix

The marketing mix is a foundation model in marketing, commonly summarised as the "four Ps": product, price, place, and promotion. The term was popularised by E. Jerome McCarthy in 1960 and developed by Philip Kotler.

Product refers to what is offered; price to what customers pay; place to distribution and availability; and promotion to advertising, selling, and publicity.

Extended versions add people, process, and physical evidence, particularly for services marketing.
