Emperor Shenzong of Song

Emperor Shenzong of Song (25 May 1048 – 1 April 1085), personal name Zhao Xu, was the sixth emperor of the Song dynasty of China. His reign from 1067 to 1085 is best known for the New Policies, the sweeping reform programme of his chancellor Wang Anshi.

Reign:25 January 1067 – 1 April 1085
Predecessor:Emperor Yingzong
Successor:Emperor Zhezong
Born:25 May 1048
Died:1 April 1085 (aged 36), Kaifeng, Henan

Reign

Shenzong ascended the throne at nineteen, determined to strengthen the dynasty against the Liao and Western Xia and to remedy the fiscal strain caused by a large army and bureaucracy. In 1069 he appointed Wang Anshi as chancellor and backed his New Policies: state agricultural loans (the Green Sprouts programme), a redesigned land survey and tax system, the baojia militia system, reform of the examination curriculum, and state trading agencies.

The reforms provoked fierce opposition from conservative officials led by figures such as Sima Guang and Su Shi, splitting the court into factions whose struggle outlasted Shenzong's reign. His military ventures against Western Xia ended in costly defeats, notably at Yongle in 1082, which are said to have broken his health and spirit. He died in 1085, aged 36, and was succeeded by his young son Zhezong, under whose regency the New Policies were largely repealed.
