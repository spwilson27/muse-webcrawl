St Helens R.F.C.

St Helens R.F.C., commonly known as Saints, is a professional rugby league club in St Helens, Merseyside, England. Founded in 1873, the club was one of the 22 original members of the Northern Rugby Football Union in 1895, the breakaway that created rugby league. Saints play in the Super League and are one of the most successful clubs in British rugby league history.

History

The club played at Knowsley Road for over a century before moving to the Totally Wicked Stadium (Langtree Park) in 2012. Saints have won numerous league championships and Challenge Cups; their honours include a celebrated treble in 2006 and four consecutive Super League Grand Final victories from 2019 to 2022, followed by a World Club Challenge triumph in Australia in 2023.

Rivalries

St Helens' fiercest rivalry is with Wigan Warriors, contested in the Good Friday derby, one of the most famous fixtures in rugby league. The club also has local rivalries with Warrington Wolves and Widnes Vikings.
