Cola wars

The cola wars are the long-running rivalry between soft drink producers The Coca-Cola Company and PepsiCo, marked by mutually targeted marketing campaigns, especially in the 1970s and 1980s.

History

Pepsi's "Pepsi Challenge" (1975) used blind taste tests to claim consumers preferred Pepsi. Coca-Cola responded in 1985 with New Coke, a reformulation that provoked a consumer backlash and the swift return of the original as Coca-Cola Classic. Celebrity endorsements, Super Bowl advertising and exclusive restaurant contracts were major battlegrounds.

Legacy

The rivalry became a classic business-school case study in branding, and the phrase "cola wars" entered popular usage for intense two-brand competition.
