Invention

An invention is a unique or novel device, method, composition, idea, or process. It may be an improvement upon an existing machine or product, or a wholly new process for creating an object or result. Inventions that achieve a completely unique function or result may be radical breakthroughs, such as the printing press, steam engine, light bulb, and transistor.

Invention and law

The concept is central to patent law, which grants inventors time-limited exclusive rights in exchange for public disclosure of how the invention works. Invention is distinguished from discovery (finding what already exists) and from innovation (bringing an invention into practical use). Invention has been studied as a creative process involving insight, experimentation, and frequently simultaneous independent discovery.
