International Astronomical Union

The International Astronomical Union (IAU) is an international organisation of professional astronomers, founded in 1919, that promotes astronomy and standardises astronomical nomenclature. Its headquarters are in Paris.

Role

The IAU names celestial bodies and their features, defines astronomical constants and terminology, and organises scientific meetings. Its Minor Planet Center collects observations of asteroids and comets.

Planet definition

In 2006 the IAU adopted a definition of planet that reclassified Pluto as a dwarf planet, sparking public and scientific debate.

Organisation

Members include individual astronomers and national adhering organisations. General assemblies are held every three years.
