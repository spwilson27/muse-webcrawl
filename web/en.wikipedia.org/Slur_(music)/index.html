Slur (music)

A slur is a symbol in Western musical notation indicating that the notes it embraces are to be played without separation — that is, with a singing tone and connected articulation, called legato. A slur is denoted with a curved line generally placed over the notes if the stems point downward, and under them if the stems point upward.

Interpretation

For bowed string instruments, a slur means the enclosed notes are played in one bow stroke. For wind instruments, it means the notes are tongued only at the start of the phrase. For singers, a slur over several notes sung to one syllable indicates a melisma. Slurs also serve as phrase marks showing musical structure.

Distinction from ties

A slur resembles a tie, but a tie joins two notes of the same pitch into a single sustained sound, whereas a slur connects notes of different pitches or groups notes into a phrase.
