Transformer (machine learning)

A transformer is a deep learning architecture introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Ashish Vaswani and colleagues at Google. It relies entirely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with the recurrence and convolutions used by earlier sequence models, and it underpins modern large language models.

Architecture

The original transformer has an encoder and a decoder, each a stack of layers containing multi-head self-attention and position-wise feed-forward networks, with residual connections and layer normalisation. Positional encodings supply word-order information. Self-attention lets every position attend to all others, so the model can capture long-range dependencies and be trained in parallel.

Impact

Decoder-only descendants such as the GPT series, encoder models such as BERT, and vision transformers transformed natural language processing and beyond. Scaling transformers with more data and compute produced emergent abilities and led to widespread deployment of generative AI systems in the 2020s.
