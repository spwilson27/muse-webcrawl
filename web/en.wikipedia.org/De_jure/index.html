De jure

Scales of justice, symbol of legal recognition de jure

De jure (Latin: "by law"; classical Latin de iure) describes practices that are legally recognized, regardless of whether the practice exists in reality. It contrasts with de facto ("in fact"), which describes situations that exist in practice but are not formally recognized.

Usage

In law and government, a de jure government is the legally recognized ruler of a state, even if a rival de facto regime holds actual power. De jure segregation, such as that once enforced in the southern United States and apartheid South Africa, was segregation mandated by law, whereas de facto segregation arises from social and economic conditions.
