Orlando is a city in central Florida and the county seat of Orange County. It is the centre of the Orlando metropolitan area, which has a population of over 2.6 million. Orlando is one of the most visited cities in the world, primarily due to tourism, major events, and convention traffic.

History

The area was settled during the Seminole Wars around Fort Gatlin (established 1838). The settlement, first called Jernigan, was renamed Orlando in 1857—tradition credits the name to Orlando Reeves, a soldier killed by Seminoles, though other theories exist. Orlando became a centre of Florida's citrus industry before the Great Freeze of 1894–95. The city grew with the arrival of Walt Disney World in 1971 in nearby Bay Lake and Lake Buena Vista.

Tourism and economy

Orlando's attractions include Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando Resort, and SeaWorld, drawing more than 70 million visitors in peak years. The city is also a hub for the convention industry (the Orange County Convention Center is among the largest in the United States), digital media and simulation, aerospace and defence contracting, and health care at Lake Nona Medical City. The University of Central Florida, one of the largest universities in the U.S. by enrollment, is located in the metro area.

Sports

Orlando is home to the Orlando Magic (NBA) and Orlando City SC (MLS), and hosts college football bowl games at Camping World Stadium.
