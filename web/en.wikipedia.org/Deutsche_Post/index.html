Deutsche Post

Deutsche Post AG, trading under the DHL brand for its logistics operations, is a German multinational package delivery and supply chain management company headquartered in Bonn, Germany. It is one of the world's largest logistics companies, operating in over 220 countries and territories.

History

The company was created in 1995 when the former German federal postal authority, Deutsche Bundespost, was split into three companies: Deutsche Post, Deutsche Telekom and Postbank. Deutsche Post was privatised in stages beginning in 2000, when its shares were listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange.

In 1998 Deutsche Post began acquiring shares in the express delivery company DHL, taking full control by 2002. The group subsequently adopted DHL as its global brand; in 2023 the corporate name was changed to DHL Group, though Deutsche Post remains the name of the German postal division.

Operations

Deutsche Post delivers mail and parcels within Germany, while DHL divisions handle express parcels, freight forwarding, supply chain services and e-commerce logistics internationally. The company employs roughly 590,000 people worldwide.
