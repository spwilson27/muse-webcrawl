Kronecker limit formula

The Kronecker limit formula is a result in mathematics, due to Leopold Kronecker, describing the constant term in the Laurent expansion of a real analytic Eisenstein series (or Dedekind zeta function) at the point s = 1.

Statement

For the Eisenstein series E(τ, s) attached to the modular group, the limit formula expresses the constant term at s = 1 in terms of the Dedekind eta function and the logarithm of the imaginary part of τ: roughly, the residue and constant term involve −2π log(|η(τ)|² Im τ). Generalisations apply to zeta functions of number fields.

Significance

The formula connects analysis, elliptic functions and number theory, and plays a role in the theory of complex multiplication, Stark's conjectures and the explicit class field theory of imaginary quadratic fields (Kronecker's Jugendtraum).
