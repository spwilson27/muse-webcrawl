Spessartine

Spessartine
Category: Garnet group
Formula: Mn2+3Al2(SiO4)3
Colour: yellow to orange to reddish brown
Hardness: 6.5–7.5 (Mohs)

Spessartine is a nesosilicate, manganese aluminium garnet species, Mn3Al2(SiO4)3. It is named after Spessart in Bavaria, Germany, the type locality. Spessartine occurs most often in granite pegmatites and in certain low-grade metamorphic rocks.

Properties

Colours range from yellow and orange to red and reddish brown, with the vivid orange "mandarin garnet" variety from Namibia and Nigeria prized as a gemstone. Spessartine forms a solid solution series with almandine, and intermediate compositions are common.

Occurrence

Notable localities include Bavaria, Madagascar, Brazil, China, and San Diego County, California. Gem-quality crystals are cut as collector stones and jewellery gems.
