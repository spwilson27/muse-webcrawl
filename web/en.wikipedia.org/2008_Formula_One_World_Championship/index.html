2008 Formula One World Championship

2008 FIA Formula One World Championship
Champion driver: Lewis Hamilton (McLaren)
Constructors' champion: Ferrari
Races: 18
Season: 16 March – 2 November 2008

The 2008 FIA Formula One World Championship was the 62nd season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It featured the 59th Formula One World Championship for Drivers and the 51st Formula One World Championship for Constructors, contested over an eighteen-race series that began on 16 March and ended on 2 November. Lewis Hamilton won the Drivers' Championship at the age of 23 years and 300 days, becoming the youngest driver to win the title at that time, while Ferrari won the Constructors' Championship.

The championship was decided at the final race, the Brazilian Grand Prix at Interlagos. Hamilton needed only fifth place to beat Felipe Massa, and after a late rain shower shuffled the order, Hamilton passed Timo Glock on the final corner of the last lap to take fifth and the title by a single point, even as Massa won the race in front of his home crowd.

Teams and drivers

Eleven teams contested the championship. Ferrari fielded defending champion Kimi Räikkönen alongside Felipe Massa. McLaren-Mercedes ran Hamilton and Heikki Kovalainen. Other entrants included BMW Sauber, Renault, Williams, Red Bull Racing, Toyota, Toro Rosso, Honda, Force India and Super Aguri, the latter withdrawing after four races for financial reasons. Sebastian Vettel, driving for Toro Rosso, took a surprise victory in the rain at Monza, becoming the youngest Grand Prix winner at the time.

The season saw the first Formula One night race, held in Singapore, and the debut of the Valencia Street Circuit for the European Grand Prix. The Singapore race was later overshadowed by the "Crashgate" scandal, in which Renault driver Nelson Piquet Jr. was revealed to have crashed deliberately to help team-mate Fernando Alonso win.
