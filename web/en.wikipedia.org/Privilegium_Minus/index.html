Privilegium Minus

The Privilegium Minus is a deed issued by Emperor Frederick Barbarossa on 17 September 1156, at the Hoftag in Regensburg. It converted the March of Austria from a margraviate into a duchy, granting it to Henry II "Jasomirgott" of the House of Babenberg, who had surrendered the Duchy of Bavaria to Henry the Lion.

Provisions

The deed granted Austria unusual privileges: hereditary succession in both the male and female lines, exemption from attendance at imperial diets except those in Bavaria, no obligation to provide military service beyond neighbouring campaigns, and the duke's right to name his successor if childless. The name "Minus" distinguishes it from the (forged) Privilegium Maius of the 14th century.

Significance

The Privilegium Minus laid the legal foundation for Austria's development into a distinct territorial power within the Holy Roman Empire.
