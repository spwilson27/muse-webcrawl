Dream Team (basketball)

The 1992 United States men's Olympic basketball team, nicknamed the "Dream Team", was the first American Olympic team to feature active professional players from the National Basketball Association (NBA). The team competed at the 1992 Summer Olympics in Barcelona and won the gold medal, defeating its opponents by an average of 43.8 points per game.

Roster and results

Coached by Chuck Daly, the roster included Michael Jordan, Magic Johnson, Larry Bird, Charles Barkley, Karl Malone, John Stockton, Patrick Ewing, David Robinson, Scottie Pippen, Clyde Drexler, Chris Mullin, and college player Christian Laettner. Eleven of the twelve players were later inducted into the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame individually, and the team itself was inducted as a unit in 2010.

The Dream Team beat Croatia 117–85 in the gold medal game. Its dominance is credited with greatly accelerating the global popularity and internationalisation of basketball; many future international NBA stars cited watching the Dream Team as their inspiration.

Legacy

The Dream Team is widely regarded as the greatest team ever assembled in any sport. The term "Dream Team" has since been applied to other star-laden squads, and subsequent U.S. Olympic teams have continued to use NBA players.
