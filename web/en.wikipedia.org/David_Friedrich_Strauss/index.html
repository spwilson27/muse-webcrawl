David Friedrich Strauss

David Friedrich Strauss (German: Strauß; 27 January 1808 – 8 February 1874) was a German liberal Protestant theologian and writer associated with the Young Hegelians. His scandal-causing book The Life of Jesus, Critically Examined (1835) argued that the miraculous elements of the Gospels were myths rather than historical events.

Influence

Strauss's work was a landmark of the historical-critical study of the Bible and helped launch the "quest for the historical Jesus". The controversy destroyed his academic career in Germany; a later appointment at Zurich was withdrawn after public protests. His The Old Faith and the New (1872) set out a naturalistic worldview that influenced later secular thought. George Eliot translated The Life of Jesus into English.
