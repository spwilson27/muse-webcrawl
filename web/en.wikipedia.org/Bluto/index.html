Bluto

Bluto is a fictional character created by E. C. Segar for the Popeye comic strip and cartoons. A hulking, bearded sailor and brute, he is Popeye's principal rival, frequently competing with him for the affections of Olive Oyl.

History

Bluto first appeared in the Thimble Theatre strip in 1932 and became the main antagonist of the Fleischer and Famous Studios animated shorts. In some cartoons the similar character was named Brutus, owing to confusion over the rights to the name, though later productions restored "Bluto".

Portrayals

He was voiced in classic cartoons by several actors including Jackson Beck, and played by Paul L. Smith in the 1980 live-action film Popeye.
