Camilla (born Camilla Rosemary Shand, 17 July 1947) is Queen of the United Kingdom and the 14 other Commonwealth realms as the wife of King Charles III. She became queen consort upon Charles's accession on 8 September 2022, following the death of Elizabeth II, and was crowned alongside her husband at Westminster Abbey on 6 May 2023.

Queen Camilla, consort of Charles III

Early life and first marriage

Camilla was born into a prosperous family; her father, Major Bruce Shand, was a British Army officer and wine merchant, and her mother Rosalind was the daughter of Roland Cubitt, 3rd Baron Ashcombe. She was educated in England, Switzerland, and France, and worked as a secretary and receptionist in London. In 1973 she married British Army officer Andrew Parker Bowles, with whom she had two children, Tom (born 1974) and Laura (born 1978). The marriage ended in divorce in 1995.

Relationship and marriage to Charles

Camilla first met Charles, then Prince of Wales, in the early 1970s. Their relationship, which resumed during their respective first marriages, became the subject of intense media scrutiny in the 1990s, particularly during the breakdown of Charles's marriage to Diana, Princess of Wales. After Diana's death in 1997, Camilla's public role was gradually rebuilt. Charles and Camilla were married in a civil ceremony at Windsor Guildhall on 9 April 2005, followed by a service of prayer and dedication at St George's Chapel, Windsor Castle. On marriage she took the title Duchess of Cornwall, rather than Princess of Wales, out of sensitivity to public opinion.

Duchess of Cornwall

As Duchess of Cornwall, Camilla undertook royal engagements in support of the Prince of Wales and became patron of numerous charities. Her principal causes included osteoporosis (inspired by her mother's death from the disease, she became president of the Royal Osteoporosis Society), literacy, animal welfare, and support for victims of rape and sexual abuse. Over time, public acceptance grew: in February 2022, Elizabeth II expressed her "sincere wish" that Camilla be known as Queen Consort when Charles became king.

Queen consort

On Charles's accession in September 2022, Camilla became queen consort, styled simply "Queen Camilla" after the coronation period. She supports the King in his duties, undertakes solo engagements, and promotes reading through the Queen's Reading Room charity. She is patron or president of more than 90 charities and organizations.
