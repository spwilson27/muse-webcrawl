Muhammad (c. 570 – 8 June 632 CE) was an Arab religious, social, and political leader and the founder of Islam. According to Islamic doctrine, he was a prophet divinely inspired to preach and confirm the monotheistic teachings of Adam, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and other prophets. He is believed by Muslims to be the Seal of the Prophets within Islam, with the Quran as his central religious text and his teachings and normative example (the sunnah, composed of accounts called hadith) forming the basis for Islamic religious belief.

Early life

Muhammad was born around 570 CE in Mecca into the Banu Hashim clan of the Quraysh tribe. His father Abdullah died before his birth and his mother Amina died when he was six, leaving him an orphan raised first by his grandfather Abd al-Muttalib and then his uncle Abu Talib. He worked as a merchant and shepherd, and at about 25 married the wealthy merchant Khadija, some fifteen years his senior.

Revelation and preaching

Around 610 CE, while retreating to the cave of Hira near Mecca, Muhammad is said to have received his first revelation from the angel Gabriel (Jibril). Over the following 23 years he preached monotheism and social reform, gathering followers in Mecca despite persecution by the Quraysh. In 622 CE he emigrated to Medina — the Hijra, which marks the start of the Islamic calendar — where he established a political and religious community (ummah) and drafted the Charter of Medina.

Later life

Conflict with Mecca led to battles including Badr (624), Uhud (625) and the Trench (627), followed by the Treaty of al-Hudaybiyyah (628) and the largely peaceful conquest of Mecca in 630. Muhammad delivered his Farewell Sermon during his final pilgrimage in 632 and died in Medina later that year. He is buried in the Prophet's Mosque in Medina.

Legacy

Muhammad's life, actions and sayings (sunnah) are the second source of Islamic law and theology after the Quran. His unification of Arabia under Islam set the stage for the Rashidun and Umayyad caliphates and the rapid expansion of Islam across the Middle East, North Africa and beyond.
