Grab (company)

Grab Holdings Limited is a Singaporean multinational technology company and Southeast Asia's leading "super-app", providing ride-hailing, food and grocery delivery, digital payments, and financial services through a single mobile application. It operates in countries including Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines, Cambodia, and Myanmar.

Formerly: MyTeksi / GrabTaxi

Founded: 2012, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Headquarters: Singapore

Founders: Anthony Tan, Tan Hooi Ling

Industry: Ride-hailing, delivery, fintech

Listed: Nasdaq (GRAB), via SPAC merger in 2021

History

Grab was founded in 2012 by Anthony Tan and Tan Hooi Ling, classmates at Harvard Business School, as MyTeksi, a taxi-booking app in Malaysia. Rebranded as GrabTaxi and later Grab, it moved its headquarters to Singapore, expanded across Southeast Asia, and broadened into private-car and motorcycle hailing (GrabBike), food delivery (GrabFood), parcel delivery, and the GrabPay wallet. In 2018 Grab acquired Uber's Southeast Asian operations in exchange for Uber taking a stake in Grab, ending a costly rivalry.

Super-app and financial services

Following the super-app model, Grab added hotel booking, insurance, lending, and digital banking. It won a digital full bank licence in Singapore (in a consortium with Singtel, operating as GXS Bank) and a digital bank licence in Malaysia (GXBank). Grab went public on the Nasdaq in December 2021 through a merger with the SPAC Altimeter Growth Corp., at the time the largest SPAC deal ever, though its share price fell sharply afterwards. Its main regional competitor is Indonesia's GoTo (Gojek).
