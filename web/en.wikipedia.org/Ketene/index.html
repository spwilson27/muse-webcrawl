Ketene

Ketene is an organic compound with the formula CH2=C=O, the simplest member of the ketene family characterised by cumulated carbon–carbon and carbon–oxygen double bonds. It is a colourless, pungent, toxic gas.

Production and uses

Ketene is produced by pyrolysis of acetone or acetic acid and is used industrially to make acetic anhydride and diketene. It acetylates nucleophiles readily and participates in [2+2] cycloadditions.
