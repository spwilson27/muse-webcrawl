Unfinished building
Causes: Funding failure, war, economic crisis, legal disputes
Notable examples: Sagrada Família, Ryugyong Hotel

An unfinished building is a structure whose construction was abandoned, indefinitely suspended, or left incomplete for a prolonged period. Causes include exhaustion of funds, bankruptcy of the developer, war, political change, planning disputes, and economic downturns that leave projects — especially speculative high-rises — economically unviable.

Notable examples

The Sagrada Família in Barcelona has been under construction since 1882. The Ryugyong Hotel in Pyongyang stood as an empty shell for decades after work halted in 1992. Spain's 2008 property crash and China's property-sector slowdown each produced large numbers of unfinished residential towers; in China such projects are colloquially called "rotten-tail buildings". Some unfinished structures are later completed, converted, or demolished, while others become landmarks or targets for urban exploration.
