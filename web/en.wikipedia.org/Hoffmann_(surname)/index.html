Hoffmann (surname)

Hoffmann is a German surname meaning steward or farm manager, from Middle High German hofman, a person managing a court or estate. It is a common surname in Germany and among German diaspora communities, with variants including Hofmann, Hoffman, and Hoffmann.

Origin

The name originated as an occupational surname for a steward, tenant farmer, or official in charge of a manor or farmstead (Hof). Over centuries it spread widely in German-speaking Central Europe.

Notable people

Notable bearers include writer and composer E. T. A. Hoffmann (1776-1822), chemist August Wilhelm von Hofmann, Nobel laureate Roald Hoffmann, physician and chemist Friedrich Hoffmann, and many academics, artists, politicians, and athletes across Europe and the Americas.

Variants

Related forms include Hofmann (single f), Hoffman in English-speaking countries, and Hofman in Dutch and other languages.
