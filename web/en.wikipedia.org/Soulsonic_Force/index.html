Soulsonic Force

Soulsonic Force (also rendered Soul Sonic Force) is an American hip hop group from the Bronx, New York, associated with DJ Afrika Bambaataa and his Zulu Nation. The group is best known for the landmark electro-funk singles "Planet Rock" (1982) and "Looking for the Perfect Beat" (1983), released on Tommy Boy Records.

History

The group grew out of Afrika Bambaataa's party crews, with members including Mr. Biggs, Pow Wow, and MC Globe. "Planet Rock," produced by Arthur Baker with synthesizer work by John Robie, famously drew on Kraftwerk's "Trans-Europe Express" and "Numbers," fusing European electronic music with hip hop and creating a template for the electro genre and much of dance music that followed. Its success carried the group to international touring and helped establish hip hop as a recorded art form.

Later releases included "Renegades of Funk" (1983), later covered by Rage Against the Machine. The group's work has been extensively sampled, and "Planet Rock" is regularly ranked among the most influential singles in popular music history.
