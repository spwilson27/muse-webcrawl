Lenin Peak

Lenin Peak (7,134 metres) is a mountain in the Trans-Alay Range of the Pamirs, on the border of Tajikistan and Kyrgyzstan. Known in Tajikistan since 2006 as Ibn Sina Peak, it is the second-highest point in both countries and one of the five "Snow Leopard" peaks of the former Soviet Union.

Climbing

First climbed in 1928 by a Soviet-German expedition, Lenin Peak is considered one of the most accessible 7,000-metre peaks, approached from base camps on the Kyrgyz side via the Achik-Tash valley. Despite its reputation, severe weather and altitude have caused many fatalities, including a major avalanche disaster in 1990.
