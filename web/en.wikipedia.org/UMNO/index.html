United Malays National Organisation

The United Malays National Organisation (Malay: Pertubuhan Kebangsaan Melayu Bersatu), abbreviated UMNO or PEKEMBAR, is a nationalist political party in Malaysia and the country's largest party. Founded on 11 May 1946 by Onn Jaafar in opposition to the British Malayan Union plan, UMNO championed Malay interests and led the Alliance coalition (later Barisan Nasional) that governed Malaysia from independence in 1957 until its historic defeat in the 2018 general election.

History

UMNO arose from the Malay backlash against the Malayan Union of 1946, which would have reduced the powers of the Malay rulers and extended citizenship to non-Malays; the Union was replaced by the Federation of Malaya in 1948. Under Tunku Abdul Rahman, UMNO formed the Alliance with the Malaysian Chinese Association and Malaysian Indian Congress, won the 1955 elections, and led the Federation to independence (Merdeka) on 31 August 1957. Tunku's successors included Abdul Razak Hussein, who launched the New Economic Policy favoring the bumiputera after the 1969 racial riots, Hussein Onn, Mahathir Mohamad — prime minister for 22 years (1981–2003) and the party's dominant modern figure — Abdullah Ahmad Badawi, and Najib Razak.

The party's dominance ended in 2018 amid the 1MDB corruption scandal surrounding Najib, who was later convicted and imprisoned. UMNO returned to government as a coalition partner in 2020 and joined Anwar Ibrahim's unity government after the 2022 election, though with much-reduced electoral strength.

Ideology

UMNO describes itself as defending Malay rights, Islam, the Malay rulers, and the Malay language, within Malaysia's constitutional framework of a multiethnic society. Membership is restricted to Malays and other bumiputera. The party has historically practiced consociational, coalition-based politics through Barisan Nasional.
