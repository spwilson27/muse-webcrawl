Tripe
Edible lining of the stomach of ruminant farm animals

Tripe is a type of edible lining from the stomachs of various farm animals. Most tripe is from cattle and sheep. Ruminants have four stomach compartments — the rumen, reticulum, omasum and abomasum — which yield different varieties, including blanket tripe, honeycomb tripe and book tripe.

Preparation and dishes

Tripe must be thoroughly cleaned and is usually parboiled before cooking; it requires long, slow cooking to become tender. It features in dishes worldwide: tripes à la mode de Caen in France, trippa alla fiorentina in Italy, menudo in Mexico, flaki in Poland, callos in Spain, and pepper soup in West Africa. In northern England tripe was traditionally eaten dressed with vinegar and onions.

Nutrition

Tripe is a lean source of protein and contains vitamin B12, selenium and zinc, with relatively few calories.
