CBC Radio
Country: Canada
Owner: Canadian Broadcasting Corporation
Networks: CBC Radio One, CBC Music

CBC Radio is the English-language radio operations of the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation. The CBC operates a number of radio networks serving different audiences and programming niches in Canada. CBC Radio operates two main English-language networks: CBC Radio One, a primarily news and talk service, and CBC Music, a music service; it also operates an international service, Radio Canada International, and services in northern Canada.

History

The CBC's radio service dates to the corporation's founding in 1936, taking over from the Canadian Radio Broadcasting Commission. CBC built a national network of stations and transmitters and became known for news, drama, and cultural programming, including the national news and long-running music and interview programmes. French-language services were operated alongside, later as Radio-Canada.

Services

CBC Radio One broadcasts news, current affairs, talk and documentaries with extensive local and regional programming; it is available on FM and AM across Canada and is largely commercial-free. CBC Music (formerly CBC Radio Two) focuses on classical, jazz and Canadian music. Radio Canada International produces programming for international audiences, largely online since 2012.
