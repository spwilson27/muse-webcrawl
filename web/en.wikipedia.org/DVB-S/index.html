DVB-S

DVB-S
Standard: ETSI EN 300 421
Introduced: 1995
Modulation: QPSK
Successor: DVB-S2

DVB-S (Digital Video Broadcasting – Satellite) is the original DVB standard for satellite television transmission, published by ETSI in 1995 and used worldwide for direct-to-home broadcasts.

Technical description

DVB-S uses QPSK modulation, MPEG-2 transport streams, concatenated Reed–Solomon and convolutional forward error correction, and typically carries several television channels in a single transponder multiplex.

Adoption

The standard enabled the rapid growth of digital satellite services in the late 1990s. It has largely been superseded by the more efficient DVB-S2, though many legacy services remain.
