St Matthew Passion

St Matthew Passion, BWV 244
Composer: Johann Sebastian Bach
Text: Gospel of Matthew; libretto by Picander
First performed: 1727, Leipzig

The St Matthew Passion (German: Matthäus-Passion), BWV 244, is a Passion oratorio by Johann Sebastian Bach, setting chapters 26 and 27 of the Gospel of Matthew with libretti by Picander. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest works of sacred music.

Structure

Written for two choirs, two orchestras, soloists and continuo, the work interweaves Gospel narration (the Evangelist, a tenor), arias, chorales and crowd choruses over roughly two and a half hours.

Revival

First performed in Leipzig in 1727, the Passion was largely forgotten after Bach's death until Felix Mendelssohn's celebrated 1829 Berlin performance, which launched the modern Bach revival.
