Derbyshire

Derbyshire is a ceremonial county in the East Midlands of England. Its county town is Matlock and its largest settlement is the city of Derby. Much of the Peak District National Park lies within the county.

Geography

The north features the gritstone hills and limestone dales of the Peak District, rising to Kinder Scout (636 m). The south is lower, centred on the Trent valley.

History and economy

Derbyshire was a centre of lead mining and of the early cotton industry — the Derwent Valley Mills are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Today tourism, manufacturing (including Rolls-Royce in Derby) and quarrying are important.
