Wolverine (character)

Wolverine (born James Howlett), commonly known by his alias Logan, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most often in association with the X-Men. Created by writer Len Wein and artist John Romita Sr., he first appeared in The Incredible Hulk #180 (1974) before joining the X-Men in Giant-Size X-Men #1 (1975).

Fictional biography

Logan is a mutant born in Canada in the late 19th century. His mutant healing factor greatly slows his ageing and allows him to recover from almost any wound, and he possesses retractable bone claws in each hand and enhanced animal-like senses. Captured by the Weapon X programme, his skeleton was bonded with the nearly indestructible metal adamantium and his memories were repeatedly tampered with, leaving his past fragmented. He later found a home with Professor Charles Xavier's X-Men, where his gruff, solitary temperament often clashes with his role as a mentor to younger mutants.

Powers and character

Wolverine's defining traits are his healing factor, adamantium-laced skeleton and claws, and a berserker ferocity in combat tempered by a strict personal code of honour. His catchphrase — that he is "the best there is at what he does" — reflects a character who became one of Marvel's most popular antiheroes.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Hugh Jackman in the X-Men film series from 2000 onwards, including the solo films X-Men Origins: Wolverine (2009), The Wolverine (2013) and Logan (2017), which won wide critical acclaim. Wolverine has also appeared in numerous animated series and video games.
