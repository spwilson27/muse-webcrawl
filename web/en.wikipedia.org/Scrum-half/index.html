Scrum-half

In rugby union, the scrum-half is the player wearing number 9 who links the forwards and the backs. The scrum-half feeds the ball into the scrum, retrieves it from the base of scrums, rucks and mauls, and distributes it — usually with a quick pass to the fly-half — or runs or kicks tactically.

Role

The scrum-half is typically one of the smaller players on the field but handles the ball more than almost any other player. Key skills are fast, accurate passing, box-kicking, sniping runs around the fringe of rucks, and decision-making that sets the tempo of the team's attack. At the scrum, the scrum-half introduces the ball and then follows it to collect it once the scrum completes.

In rugby league, the scrum-half (often just "halfback") wears number 7 and plays a more playmaking role comparable to a stand-off partner.

Notable players

Celebrated scrum-halves include Gareth Edwards of Wales, Joost van der Westhuizen of South Africa, and Antoine Dupont of France.
