St Stephen's Day

St Stephen's Day
Date: 26 December (Western); 27 December (Eastern)
Honours: Saint Stephen, first martyr
Also known as: Boxing Day (in many countries)

Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast day commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr, celebrated on 26 December in the Western Church and 27 December in the Eastern Church, immediately following Christmas Day.

Observance

It is a public holiday in many European countries, including Ireland, Germany, Austria, Italy, Spain (notably Catalonia), Poland and the Nordic countries. In Ireland it is also known for the tradition of Wren Day, when costumed groups parade with a model wren.

Connection to Boxing Day

In the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and other Commonwealth nations, 26 December is widely celebrated as Boxing Day, a holiday with roots in charitable giving.
