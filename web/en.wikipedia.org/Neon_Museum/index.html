Neon Museum

The Neon Museum in Las Vegas, Nevada, is a non-profit organization founded in 1996 dedicated to collecting, preserving, and exhibiting iconic Las Vegas neon signs. Its outdoor exhibition space, the Neon Boneyard, holds more than 250 unrestored signs, with a smaller number of restored signs displayed in the museum's galleries and as public art around the city.

Site and collection

The museum's visitors' centre occupies the restored lobby of the historic La Concha Motel, a concrete shell-shaped building moved to the site. The collection includes signs from the Moulin Rouge, the Stardust (whose vast lettering is among the largest pieces), the Desert Inn, Caesars Palace, and the guitar of the former Hard Rock Cafe. Some restored signs stand along Las Vegas Boulevard and Fremont Street as part of the museum's public displays.

Exhibitions

Guided day and night tours interpret the history of Las Vegas through its signage design and typography. In 2018 the museum hosted Lost Vegas, a light-projection exhibition by Tim Burton, one of several special exhibitions staged in the Boneyard.
