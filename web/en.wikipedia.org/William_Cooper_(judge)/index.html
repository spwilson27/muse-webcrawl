William Cooper (December 2, 1754 – December 22, 1809) was an American merchant, land speculator, and politician who founded the village of Cooperstown, New York, at the foot of Otsego Lake. He was the father of novelist James Fenimore Cooper.

Career

Born in Philadelphia to a Quaker family, Cooper acquired a large tract of land around Otsego Lake in central New York in the 1780s and began selling lots to settlers, establishing Cooperstown in 1786. He became the first judge of Otsego County and served two terms in the United States House of Representatives (1795–1797 and 1799–1801) as a Federalist.

Legacy

His son James Fenimore Cooper fictionalized the settlement experience in The Pioneers, portraying the judge in part through the character of Judge Marmaduke Temple.
