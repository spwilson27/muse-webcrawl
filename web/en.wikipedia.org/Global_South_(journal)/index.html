Global South is a peer-reviewed academic journal published by Indiana University Press, founded in 2007. It provides an interdisciplinary forum for scholarship on the literatures, cultures and histories of the regions encompassed by the term "Global South" — Africa, Asia, Latin America, the Caribbean and the Pacific — particularly in relation to colonialism and its legacies.

Scope

The journal publishes essays in comparative and postcolonial studies that cross national and disciplinary boundaries, with attention to transnational exchanges, translation, migration and the cultural dimensions of globalisation. Special issues have addressed topics such as the cultures of the Indian Ocean world and South–South literary relations.
