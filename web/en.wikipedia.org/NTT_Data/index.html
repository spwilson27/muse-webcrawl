NTT Data

NTT Data Corporation (株式会社エヌ・ティ・ティ・データ) is a Japanese multinational information technology services company headquartered in Koto, Tokyo, and part of the NTT Group. Founded in 1967 as the Data Communication Bureau of Nippon Telegraph and Telephone Public Corporation, it became a separate company in 1988 and is now one of the largest IT services providers in the world, employing well over 100,000 people.

NTT Data provides consulting, systems integration, application development and IT outsourcing to clients in government, finance, healthcare, manufacturing and other sectors. It built and operates some of Japan's most critical information systems, including banking and public-sector networks.

Expansion

From the 2000s onward NTT Data grew internationally through acquisitions, including the German firm itelligence (SAP services), the American firms Keane and Dell Services, and Spain's Everis. In 2022, NTT consolidated its overseas IT services businesses, combining NTT Data's international operations with NTT Ltd.'s services arm under the NTT Data brand to form one of the world's largest IT services companies.
