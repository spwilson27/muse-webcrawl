The Boeing 737 MAX is the fourth generation of the Boeing 737, a narrow-body airliner series manufactured by Boeing Commercial Airplanes. It succeeds the Boeing 737 Next Generation (NG) and competes with the Airbus A320neo family. The new series was announced in August 2011 in response to the A320neo's success and Southwest Airlines' fleet needs. It is powered by more efficient CFM International LEAP-1B engines, features aerodynamic improvements, and has a redesigned flight deck.

Development and design

To accommodate the larger LEAP-1B engines under the low-slung 737 wing, the engines were mounted higher and further forward on the wing, altering the aircraft's aerodynamic pitch behaviour at high angles of attack. Boeing compensated with the Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS), flight-control software that automatically trims the nose down when it senses an excessively high angle of attack — a system not initially described in flight manuals.

Grounding and recertification

Following the crashes of Lion Air Flight 610 in October 2018 and Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302 in March 2019 — which together killed 346 people and were traced in part to MCAS activations based on a single faulty angle-of-attack sensor — the worldwide 737 MAX fleet was grounded from March 2019. Investigations criticised Boeing's design and certification processes and the FAA's delegation practices. Boeing redesigned MCAS to use both sensors, added training requirements, and paid billions in settlements, including a $2.5 billion deferred prosecution agreement with the U.S. Department of Justice in 2021. The FAA recertified the type in November 2020; most other regulators followed in 2021.

Later incidents and production

In January 2024 an Alaska Airlines MAX 9 lost a mid-cabin door plug in flight, prompting a temporary grounding of MAX 9s with that configuration, an FAA production cap, and renewed scrutiny of Boeing's quality control. The MAX 7 and MAX 10 variants long awaited certification. Despite the crises, the MAX remains Boeing's best-selling programme, with thousands of orders.
