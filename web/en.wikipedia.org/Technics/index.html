Technics is a Japanese audio brand of the Panasonic Corporation, established in 1965 for the company's high-end audio equipment. Technics products are especially celebrated among audiophiles and DJs, most famously the SL-1200 series of direct-drive turntables.

History

The Technics name was introduced in 1965 for premium loudspeakers and components sold by Matsushita (later Panasonic). The brand pioneered technologies such as direct-drive turntables (SP-10, 1969; SL-1200, 1972) and Class AA amplification. The SL-1200 became the standard turntable of hip-hop and club DJs because of its torque, durability and precise pitch control.

Discontinuation and revival

Panasonic discontinued the SL-1200 in 2010 and wound down the Technics brand, but announced its return at the IFA fair in 2014, relaunching it as a high-end audio line. A new SL-1200GAE anniversary model followed in 2016, and updated SL-1200 models have remained in production since.
