David's Sling

David's Sling (Hebrew: קלע דוד, Kela David) is an Israel Defense Forces air and missile defence system jointly developed by Israel's Rafael Advanced Defense Systems and the American Raytheon company. It is designed to intercept tactical ballistic missiles, medium- to long-range rockets and cruise missiles at ranges of roughly 40 to 300 km, forming the middle layer of Israel's multi-tier missile defence between Iron Dome and the Arrow systems.

Development

The system's Stunner interceptor uses a two-stage design with radar and electro-optical seekers and hit-to-kill technology. David's Sling was declared operational in April 2017 and was first used operationally in 2018. Finland selected the system in 2023 in a major export deal.
