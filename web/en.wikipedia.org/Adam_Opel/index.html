Adam Opel

Born: 9 May 1837, Rüsselsheim, Grand Duchy of Hesse
Died: 8 September 1895, Rüsselsheim, German Empire
Known for: Founding Adam Opel AG

Adam Opel (9 May 1837 – 8 September 1895) was a German entrepreneur and the founder of the automobile manufacturer Adam Opel AG. Born in Rüsselsheim, he trained as a locksmith and spent journeyman years in Belgium, England, and France, where he encountered sewing machine manufacture in Paris.

Company

In 1862 Opel founded a sewing machine workshop in a former cowshed in Rüsselsheim. The business expanded steadily; in 1886 the company added bicycles, which soon became its most successful product, with Opel becoming one of the largest bicycle manufacturers in Germany.

Legacy

Opel died of typhoid fever in 1895, before the company entered the automobile business. His widow Sophie and their five sons—Carl, Wilhelm, Heinrich, Fritz, and Ludwig—partnered with Friedrich Lutzmann and launched the first Opel automobile, the Patent Motor Car "System Lutzmann", in 1899.
