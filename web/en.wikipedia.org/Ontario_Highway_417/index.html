King's Highway 417, commonly called Highway 417, is a 400-series freeway in Ontario, Canada. It is the main east–west highway through Ottawa, where its central section is known as the Queensway, and it forms the Ontario portion of the fastest road route between Ottawa and Montreal, continuing into Quebec as Autoroute 40. It is also part of the Trans-Canada Highway.

History

The Queensway through Ottawa was built in stages during the 1950s and 1960s, following the corridor of a former railway line and the Greber Plan for the capital. The rural sections east toward the Quebec border and west to Arnprior were completed during the 1970s and later. Successive widenings have added lanes through Ottawa, including HOV and transit-priority facilities.

Route

From its western terminus near Arnprior, Highway 417 passes Kanata and the technology parks of Ottawa's west end, crosses the city along the Queensway corridor, splits around the Highway 416 interchange, and continues past Orleans and the eastern suburbs into Prescott and Russell, reaching the Quebec border near Pointe-Fortune/Chute-à-Blondeau.
