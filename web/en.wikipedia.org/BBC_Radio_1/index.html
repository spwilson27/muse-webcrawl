BBC Radio 1 is a British national radio station owned and operated by the BBC. It specialises in modern popular music, dance music and alternative rock, broadcast to a target audience of 15–29-year-olds. It was launched in 1967 to counter the popularity of pirate radio stations such as Radio Caroline, which had been broadcasting from ships in international waters.

History

Radio 1 launched at 7 a.m. on 30 September 1967 with Tony Blackburn playing "Flowers in the Rain" by the Move as the first record on the first Breakfast Show. The station inherited much of the Light Programme's pop output and quickly became the dominant popular-music station in Britain. Famous presenters have included John Peel, Annie Nightingale, Dave Lee Travis, Chris Moyles, Zane Lowe, and Greg James. The station's annual outdoor festival, Radio 1's Big Weekend, began in 2003.

Programming

The schedule is built around The Radio 1 Breakfast Show, daytime contemporary hits, the Official Chart on Friday afternoons, and specialist evening programming covering dance, hip hop, rock and new music. Station sound emphasises new British music and emerging artists, and the playlist is central to the UK singles market.
