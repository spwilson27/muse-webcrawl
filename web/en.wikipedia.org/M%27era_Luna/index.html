M'era Luna

M'era Luna Festival
Location: Hildesheim, Germany
Held: annually in August (since 2000)
Genres: gothic rock, darkwave, industrial, metal
Attendance: about 20,000–25,000

The M'era Luna Festival is an annual alternative music festival held at Hildesheim airfield in Germany. It is one of the largest events of the gothic and dark-music scene in Europe.

History

The festival was first held in 2000, evolving out of the Zillo festival. It takes place over a weekend in August and features dozens of acts on open-air and hangar stages, alongside readings, a fashion show and a medieval market.

Music and scene

Performers have included The Sisters of Mercy, Fields of the Nephilim, ASP, Eisbrecher, Nightwish and Within Temptation. The festival is noted for the elaborate dark fashions of its audience and is a fixture of the German "schwarze Szene" (dark scene).
