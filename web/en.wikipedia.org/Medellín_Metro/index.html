Medellín Metro

The Medellín Metro (Spanish: Metro de Medellín) is a rapid transit system serving Medellín and the Aburrá Valley in Antioquia, Colombia. Opened on 30 November 1995, it is the only metro system in Colombia.

Network

The system began with Lines A and B, elevated and at-grade rail lines crossing the valley north–south and to the west. It is internationally noted for its Metrocable gondola lines, opened from 2004, which connect hillside informal settlements to the rail network, together with a tram line, Metroplús bus rapid transit, and the San Javier escalators as part of an integrated social urbanism strategy.

Impact

The metro is credited with improving mobility and civic pride in districts once affected by violence, and it is kept notably clean under a promoted "Metro culture" of civic behaviour.
