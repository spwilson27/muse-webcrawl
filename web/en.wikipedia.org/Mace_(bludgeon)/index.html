Mace (bludgeon)

Mace
Type: Blunt melee weapon
Era: Antiquity to late Middle Ages

A mace is a blunt weapon, a type of club that uses a heavy head on the end of a handle to deliver powerful strikes. It developed from the club and was used throughout the ancient and medieval world, especially against armoured opponents against whom edged weapons were less effective.

History

Stone-headed maces appear in prehistory; metal flanged maces became popular in the 12th to 15th centuries, when they could dent or penetrate plate armour. Ceremonial maces survive today as symbols of authority in parliaments and universities.

Design

Heads were made of stone, copper, bronze, iron or steel, sometimes with flanges or spikes; handles were usually wood or metal.
