Cousin problems

In complex analysis, the Cousin problems are two questions about meromorphic functions on domains in Cn, posed by Pierre Cousin in 1895 for the case of a polydisc. The first concerns prescribed zeros and poles (additive data), the second prescribed divisors (multiplicative data).

Formulation

The first Cousin problem asks whether local meromorphic data differing by holomorphic functions can be glued to a global meromorphic function; the second asks the multiplicative analogue. On Stein manifolds both are solvable due to vanishing of sheaf cohomology groups H1.
