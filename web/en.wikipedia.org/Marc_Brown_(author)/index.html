Marc Brown (author)

Marc Tolon Brown (born November 25, 1946) is an American author and illustrator of children's books. He is best known as the creator of Arthur, the best-selling book series about Arthur Read, an anthropomorphic aardvark, which was adapted into the long-running PBS Kids animated television series Arthur (1996–2022).

Life and career

Brown was born in Erie, Pennsylvania, and studied painting at the Cleveland Institute of Art. He worked as a truck driver, short-order cook, and college instructor before becoming a full-time illustrator. The first Arthur book, Arthur's Nose, was published in 1976; more than sixty Arthur titles followed, along with other series such as the D.W. books.

The Arthur series

The television adaptation, produced with WGBH Boston, ran for 25 seasons and became one of the longest-running children's animated series in the United States. Brown served as executive producer and has continued to write and illustrate new works, including books encouraging children's creativity.
