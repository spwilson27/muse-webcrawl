Taça de Portugal

The Taça de Portugal (Portuguese Cup) is the premier knockout cup competition in Portuguese football, organised by the Portuguese Football Federation. It has been held since 1938–39, succeeding the earlier Campeonato de Portugal.

Taça de Portugal
Founded: 1938
Region: Portugal
Most successful: Benfica (26 titles)

Format and history

Hundreds of clubs from the top divisions down to district level enter the single-elimination tournament. The final is traditionally held at the Estádio Nacional in Oeiras, near Lisbon. Benfica is the most successful club, followed by Porto and Sporting CP. The winners qualify for the UEFA Europa League and contest the Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira against the league champions.
