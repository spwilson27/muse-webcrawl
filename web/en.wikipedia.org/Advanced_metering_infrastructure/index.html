Advanced metering infrastructure

Advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) is an integrated system of smart meters, communications networks, and data management systems that enables two-way communication between utilities and customers. AMI differs from automatic meter reading (AMR) in that it enables two-way communication with the meter.

AMI
Components: Smart meters, networks, MDMS
Communication: Two-way
Related: Smart grid

Components

An AMI system comprises smart meters that record consumption in intervals of an hour or less, a communications network carrying data to the utility, and a meter data management system that processes the information for billing and operations.

Benefits and concerns

Utilities use AMI for remote meter reading, outage detection, time-based pricing, and load management. Critics have raised concerns about privacy, data security, health effects of radio transmitters, and the cost of deployments borne by ratepayers.

Deployment

Large-scale AMI rollouts took place in Italy, Sweden, the United States, and China from the 2000s onward, and AMI is considered a core element of the smart grid.
