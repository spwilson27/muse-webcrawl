Focke-Wulf Fw 190

The Fw 190A, one of the finest fighters of the Second World War.

The Focke-Wulf Fw 190, nicknamed Würger ("Shrike") is a German single-seat, single-engine fighter aircraft designed by Kurt Tank at Focke-Wulf in the late 1930s and widely used during World War II. Along with its well-known counterpart, the Messerschmitt Bf 109, the Fw 190 became the backbone of the Jagdwaffe (Fighter Force) of the German Luftwaffe. The twin-row BMW 801 radial engine that powered most operational versions enabled the Fw 190 to lift larger loads than the Bf 109, allowing its use as a day fighter, fighter-bomber and ground-attack aircraft.

Design

Unlike the Bf 109, the Fw 190 used a robust air-cooled radial engine, a wide-track undercarriage that made ground handling far safer, electrically operated systems in place of hydraulics, and a roomy cockpit with excellent visibility. Its controls were light and well harmonised, its roll rate was exceptional, and it could absorb considerable battle damage. A sophisticated Kommandogerät unit automatically managed engine settings, reducing pilot workload.

Operational history

The Fw 190 entered service over France in August 1941 and quickly proved superior to the Spitfire Mk V, dominating the Channel Front until the arrival of the Spitfire Mk IX. On the Eastern Front it served as both fighter and heavily armed ground-attack aircraft (Schlachtflugzeug). Heavily armed variants with cannon and rockets were thrown against American daylight bomber formations. Late-war "long-nose" Fw 190D models, powered by the liquid-cooled Junkers Jumo 213, restored high-altitude performance and were among the best piston-engined fighters of the war; the further-developed Ta 152 was its ultimate expression. Captured examples were evaluated with respect by Allied test pilots, who regarded the Fw 190 as one of the finest fighters of its era.

Variants

  • Fw 190A — the main production series with the BMW 801 radial engine.
  • Fw 190F — dedicated ground-attack version with additional armour.
  • Fw 190G — long-range fighter-bomber version.
  • Fw 190D "Dora" — long-nose high-altitude interceptor with the Jumo 213 engine.
