¡No pasarán!

"¡No pasarán!" ("They shall not pass!", literally "They will not pass") is a slogan expressing determination to defend a position against an enemy. It became famous during the Spanish Civil War as the rallying cry of the Republican defenders of Madrid.

Origins

The phrase echoes the French "Ils ne passeront pas" of the Battle of Verdun in 1916, associated with General Robert Nivelle and Philippe Pétain. In Spain, communist leader Dolores Ibárruri, "La Pasionaria", popularized "¡No pasarán!" in speeches during the siege of Madrid in November 1936, and it was adopted by the International Brigades and Republican forces.

Later use

The slogan has since been used internationally by anti-fascist and left-wing movements, appearing on posters, banners, and in political rhetoric across Europe and Latin America. Despite the slogan, Madrid ultimately fell to Franco's Nationalists in March 1939, near the end of the civil war.
