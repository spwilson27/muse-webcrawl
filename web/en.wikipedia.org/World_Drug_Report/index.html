The World Drug Report is an annual publication of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) presenting comprehensive statistics and analysis on global illicit drug markets, drug use, and drug-related health consequences.

Contents

The report covers cultivation and production of opium, coca, and cannabis; trafficking flows and seizures; synthetic drug manufacture; prevalence of use; and treatment and harm data. Recent editions have estimated that roughly 290 million people used a drug in the preceding year, and have highlighted the rapid growth of synthetic drugs such as methamphetamine and fentanyl.

Methodology

Data derive from member states' responses to the Annual Report Questionnaire, supplemented by UNODC field surveys and other sources. Commentators note uneven national reporting and discuss the report's role in international drug-control policy debates.
