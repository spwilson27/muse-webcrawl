Agricultural revolution (disambiguation)

An agricultural revolution is a period of major change in farming. The term may refer to:

Historical transitions

  • Neolithic Revolution (c. 10,000 BCE) — the first transition from hunting and gathering to farming and settled life.
  • Arab Agricultural Revolution (8th–13th centuries) — the diffusion of new crops and irrigation techniques across the Islamic world.
  • British Agricultural Revolution (17th–19th centuries) — enclosure, crop rotation, and selective breeding in Britain.
  • Scottish Agricultural Revolution (17th–19th centuries) — parallel changes in Lowland Scotland.
  • Green Revolution (1940s–1970s) — high-yielding varieties, fertilizer, and pesticides in the developing world.
  • Second Green Revolution — proposed later transformations in sustainable agriculture.
