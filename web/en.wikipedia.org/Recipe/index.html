Recipe

A recipe is a set of instructions that describes how to prepare or make something, especially a dish of prepared food. A recipe typically includes a list of ingredients with quantities, and a series of steps describing the method of preparation, cooking times, and temperatures.

Components

Modern recipes usually consist of a title, a yield (number of servings), a list of ingredients in the order used, step-by-step instructions, and notes on equipment, substitutions, or variations. Recipes may also include preparation and cooking times and nutritional information.

History

The earliest known written recipes date to around 1730 BC and were recorded on cuneiform tablets from Mesopotamia. Ancient Roman cuisine is documented in Apicius, a collection of recipes from the late 4th or early 5th century AD. Recipe collections expanded greatly in the medieval and early modern periods, and the modern standardized recipe format emerged in the 19th and 20th centuries with authors such as Fannie Farmer, who popularized precise level measurements.

Cookbooks

Recipes are collected in cookbooks, magazines, and online databases. Copyright law generally does not protect a bare list of ingredients, though the expressive text of instructions may be protected.
