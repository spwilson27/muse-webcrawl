Urban Light

Urban Light is a large-scale public art installation by Chris Burden at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art (LACMA). Completed in 2008, it consists of 202 restored cast-iron street lamps from the 1920s and 1930s arranged in a grid.

Description

The lamps, painted in uniform grey and lit at night, stand at the museum's Wilshire Boulevard entrance. Burden collected them from Los Angeles and surrounding cities.

Reception

Urban Light became one of Los Angeles's most photographed landmarks, appearing in films, tourism campaigns and countless social media posts.

Artist

Chris Burden (1946-2015) was known for performance and large-scale sculpture; Urban Light is among his most accessible public works.
