Lane departure warning system

A lane departure warning system (LDWS) is a vehicle safety system designed to warn the driver when the vehicle begins to move out of its lane on freeways and arterial roads, unless a turn signal is on. Lane keeping assist systems go further and automatically take corrective action to keep the vehicle in its lane.

Operation

Systems typically use a forward-facing camera near the rear-view mirror to detect lane markings, and alert the driver with visual, audible or haptic warnings such as steering-wheel vibration.

History and regulation

Commercial systems appeared in Japan and Europe in the early 2000s. Euro NCAP and other safety rating programmes award points for lane support systems, and they are increasingly standard equipment.

Limitations

Performance can degrade with worn lane markings, snow, glare or poor weather. The systems are driver assistance features and do not replace attentive driving.
