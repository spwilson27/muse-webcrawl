Spanish football rivalries
Most famous: El Clásico (Barcelona–Real Madrid)
Other majors: Madrid derby, Seville derby, Basque derby
Sport: Association football

Spanish football rivalries encompass the historic derbies and antagonisms between football clubs in Spain, many of which reflect deeper regional, political and social identities. Matches such as El Clásico and the great city derbies are among the most watched club fixtures in world football.

El Clásico

The most famous rivalry is El Clásico, between FC Barcelona and Real Madrid CF, first played in 1902. Beyond sport, it came to symbolise the tension between Catalan identity and Spanish centralism, particularly during the Franco era. Contested in La Liga, the Copa del Rey and European competition, it has featured many of the greatest players in history and draws a global television audience in the hundreds of millions.

City derbies

The Madrid derby pits Real Madrid against Atlético Madrid, traditionally the club of the city's working class. The Seville derby (El Gran Derbi) between Sevilla FC and Real Betis is famed for its passion. Barcelona also has its local derby against RCD Espanyol.

Regional rivalries

The Basque derby between Athletic Bilbao and Real Sociedad is notable for the friendly mixing of supporters alongside fierce on-pitch rivalry. Other historic fixtures include the Galician derby (Celta Vigo–Deportivo La Coruña), the Asturian derby (Sporting Gijón–Real Oviedo), the Valencian derby (Valencia–Levante) and the Canary Islands derby (Tenerife–Las Palmas).
