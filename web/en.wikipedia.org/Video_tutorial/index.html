Video tutorial

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A video tutorial is an instructional video that teaches viewers how to perform a task, use a product, or understand a subject, usually through step-by-step demonstration with narration. Video tutorials are a common form of online learning content, distributed on platforms such as YouTube and Vimeo as well as on dedicated learning sites, product-support pages and learning-management systems.

Formats

Common formats include screencasts, in which the instructor records their computer screen while explaining software or a digital process; hands-on demonstrations of physical tasks such as cooking, crafts, repair and makeup; whiteboard and animated explainer videos for academic subjects; and lecture recordings. Tutorials may be single short clips addressing one problem, or structured series forming a complete course.

Uses and growth

The spread of broadband video in the 2000s made video tutorials a primary way people learn practical skills: surveys regularly find that large numbers of users turn to "how-to" videos before manuals or help desks. Organizations including Khan Academy built educational libraries around short instructional videos, and software companies adopted tutorials for onboarding and customer support. Research on multimedia learning suggests that demonstration videos are effective when segmented into short steps, narrated conversationally, and paired with opportunities for practice.

Video tutorials also underpin the "flipped classroom", in which students watch instruction at home and practise in class, and they are a staple of massive open online courses (MOOCs) and corporate training.
