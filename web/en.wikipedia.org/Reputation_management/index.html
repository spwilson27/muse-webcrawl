Reputation management

Reputation management refers to the influencing, controlling, and monitoring of an individual's or group's reputation. Originally a public relations term, it has expanded with the internet and social media into online reputation management (ORM), including search engine optimisation tactics to shape what appears about a person or brand online.

Techniques

Practitioners monitor mentions, respond to reviews, generate positive content, and seek removal or suppression of negative material. Methods range from legitimate customer-service responses to controversial practices such as astroturfing, fake reviews, and litigation threats, which have drawn criticism and regulatory scrutiny.

Ethics

Professional associations have issued guidelines distinguishing acceptable reputation work from deceptive practices; several high-profile incidents of sockpuppetry and covert editing have shaped debate over the field.
