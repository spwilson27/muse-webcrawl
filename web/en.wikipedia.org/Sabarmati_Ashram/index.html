Established1917
LocationAhmedabad, Gujarat, India
FounderMahatma Gandhi

Sabarmati Ashram, also known as Gandhi Ashram, is a former residential community on the banks of the Sabarmati River in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Mahatma Gandhi lived here from 1917 to 1930, making it a centre of the Indian independence movement.

History

Gandhi founded the ashram in 1917 (relocating an earlier community from Kochrab) as a place to practise truth, non-violence, self-reliance and simple living, including spinning khadi cloth. On 12 March 1930 he set out from the ashram on the Salt March to Dandi, vowing not to return until India was free; the ashram was never again his home. His cottage, Hriday Kunj, and a museum designed by Charles Correa (opened 1963) preserve his writings, photographs and personal effects.
