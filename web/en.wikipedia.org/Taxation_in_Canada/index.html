Taxation in Canada

Taxation in Canada is levied by the federal government, the provinces and territories, and municipalities. The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) administers federal taxes and, for most provinces, provincial income taxes as well.

Main taxes

Federal and provincial income taxes apply to individuals and corporations at graduated rates. A federal Goods and Services Tax (GST) of 5% applies nationwide; several provinces harmonise it with their provincial sales tax as the HST, while Quebec administers its own QST and Alberta has no provincial sales tax. Municipalities rely chiefly on property taxes. Excise taxes apply to fuel, alcohol, tobacco and cannabis, and Canada introduced a federal carbon pricing system in 2019.
