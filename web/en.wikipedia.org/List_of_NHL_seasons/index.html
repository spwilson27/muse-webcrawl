List of NHL seasons

The National Hockey League (NHL) was founded in 1917 in Montreal, succeeding the National Hockey Association. This list covers each season from 1917–18 onward, with champions and major events.

Overview

  • 1917–18: first season; Toronto won the Stanley Cup.
  • 1942–1967: the "Original Six" era — Montreal Canadiens, Toronto Maple Leafs, Boston Bruins, New York Rangers, Chicago Black Hawks, Detroit Red Wings.
  • 1967: great expansion doubled the league to 12 teams.
  • 1979: four WHA teams joined after the merger.
  • 2004–05: season cancelled entirely due to a lockout — the first time a North American major league lost a full season to a labour dispute.
  • 2020–21: shortened season due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

The Montreal Canadiens have won the most Stanley Cups overall (24). Recent champions include the Tampa Bay Lightning (2020, 2021), Colorado Avalanche (2022), Vegas Golden Knights (2023) and Florida Panthers (2024).
