Moya Brennan

Moya Brennan (born Máire Ní Bhraonáin, 4 August 1952) is an Irish folk singer, songwriter, harpist and philanthropist, known as the lead singer of the family band Clannad and the elder sister of Enya. She is sometimes called the "First Lady of Celtic Music".

Career

Brennan began performing with her siblings and uncles in Clannad in 1970 in Gweedore, County Donegal. The band achieved international success in the 1980s with "Theme from Harry's Game" and the soundtrack to Robin of Sherwood. Her solo career began with the album Máire (1992); she has since released numerous solo records blending Celtic tradition with contemporary production. Her duet "I Will Find You" featured in the film The Last of the Mohicans. Brennan won a Grammy Award with Clannad in 1999.
