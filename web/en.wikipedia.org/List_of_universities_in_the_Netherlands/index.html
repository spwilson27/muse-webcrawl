List of universities in the Netherlands

This is a list of universities in the Netherlands. Dutch higher education distinguishes research universities (universiteiten) from universities of applied sciences (hogescholen). Leiden University, founded in 1575, is the country's oldest university.

Research universities

  • Leiden University (1575)
  • University of Groningen (1614)
  • University of Amsterdam (1632)
  • Utrecht University (1636)
  • Delft University of Technology (1842)
  • Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (1880)
  • Erasmus University Rotterdam (1913)
  • Radboud University Nijmegen (1923)
  • Tilburg University (1927)
  • Maastricht University (1976)
  • Eindhoven University of Technology (1956)
  • University of Twente (1961)
  • Wageningen University & Research (1918)
  • Open University of the Netherlands (1984) — distance learning

Universities of applied sciences

There are more than 30 hogescholen, including the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences, and The Hague University of Applied Sciences, offering professionally oriented bachelor's and master's programmes.
