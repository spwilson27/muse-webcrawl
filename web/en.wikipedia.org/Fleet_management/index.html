Fleet management

Fleet management is the management of an organization's motor vehicles, including cars, vans, trucks and sometimes ships, aircraft and rail cars. It encompasses vehicle acquisition and financing, maintenance, fuel management, driver management, safety and regulatory compliance, and the use of telematics to track vehicle location and performance.

Functions

Fleet managers aim to minimize cost and risk while maximizing efficiency and asset utilization. Core tasks include selecting and leasing or purchasing vehicles, scheduling preventive maintenance, tracking fuel consumption and emissions, managing insurance and accident claims, ensuring drivers are licensed and trained, and disposing of vehicles at the end of their service lives.

Technology

Modern fleet management relies heavily on telematics and fleet management software: GPS trackers and vehicle sensors transmit data on position, speed, idling, harsh braking and engine diagnostics, enabling route optimization, predictive maintenance and driver-behaviour scoring. Increasingly, fleet operators plan transitions to electric vehicles, incorporating charging infrastructure and total-cost-of-ownership analysis.

Sectors

Large fleets are operated by logistics and delivery companies, utilities, rental car firms, public transit agencies, police and emergency services, and governments. Specialized fleet management companies provide outsourced services to businesses that do not manage vehicles in-house.
