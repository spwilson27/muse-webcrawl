Chimera

In Greek mythology, the Chimera was a monstrous fire-breathing hybrid — the body and head of a lion, a goat's head rising from its back, and a serpent for a tail. Offspring of Typhon and Echidna, it ravaged Lycia until slain by Bellerophon riding Pegasus.

The word chimera came to mean any fanciful, impossible composite, and in biology a chimera is an organism containing cells from different zygotes.
