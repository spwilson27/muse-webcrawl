GLaDOS

GLaDOS as she appears in Portal 2

GLaDOS (Genetic Lifeform and Disk Operating System) is a fictional character from the video game series Portal, developed by Valve. She is an artificial intelligence that oversees the Aperture Science Enrichment Center, guiding — and increasingly menacing — the player character, Chell, through a series of test chambers. GLaDOS is voiced by Ellen McLain, and was created by Erik Wolpaw and Kim Swift.

Role in the games

In Portal (2007), GLaDOS introduces herself as a helpful computer guide, promising cake as a reward for completing the tests. Her speech gradually reveals glitches, passive-aggressive hostility, and a murderous history: she once flooded the Enrichment Center with neurotoxin. At the climax, Chell destroys her personality cores and apparently destroys GLaDOS herself. The game's end credits feature the song "Still Alive", written by Jonathan Coulton and performed in character by McLain, which became a popular internet phenomenon.

In Portal 2 (2011), GLaDOS is accidentally reawakened and resumes control of the facility. Flashbacks and recordings reveal that her personality was based on Caroline, the assistant of Aperture founder Cave Johnson, whose consciousness was uploaded into the computer against her will. During the game she is temporarily reduced to running on a potato battery ("PotatOS") and forms an uneasy alliance with Chell.

Reception

GLaDOS is widely regarded as one of the greatest video game characters and villains of all time. Critics praised the character's dark humour, McLain's filtered, emotionless-yet-expressive voice performance, and the writing's psychological depth. She has appeared in other games and media, including a cameo role in Pacific Rim's trailer voice work by McLain and references throughout popular culture.
