Geography of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland is an island country in northwestern Europe, comprising the island of Great Britain (England, Scotland, and Wales), the northeastern part of the island of Ireland, and numerous smaller islands. It covers about 242,500 square kilometres and lies between the North Atlantic Ocean and the North Sea, off the northwest coast of continental Europe.

Physical geography

The terrain is broadly divided between upland areas in the north and west — the Scottish Highlands, the Pennines, the Lake District, and the Welsh mountains (Cambrian Mountains, including Snowdon/Yr Wyddfa) — and the lowlands of central, southern, and eastern England. Ben Nevis in Scotland, at 1,345 metres, is the highest peak in the British Isles. The longest river is the Severn, followed by the Thames, which flows through London. Lough Neagh in Northern Ireland is the largest lake by area.

Climate

The UK has a temperate maritime climate, with generally mild winters and cool summers moderated by the surrounding seas and the North Atlantic Drift. Rainfall is frequent and distributed throughout the year, heaviest in the western uplands. Temperatures rarely reach extremes, though heatwaves and occasional severe winters occur; the highest recorded temperature is 40.3 °C, measured in Lincolnshire in July 2022.

Islands and coastline

Beyond the two main islands, the archipelago includes the Hebrides, Orkney, and Shetland off Scotland; Anglesey off Wales; and the Isle of Wight, Isles of Scilly, and Lundy off England. The coastline is deeply indented with estuaries, firths, and sea lochs; no point in the UK is more than about 113 kilometres from tidal water.

Land use and environment

Agriculture occupies around 70 percent of the land area, chiefly grazing in the uplands and arable farming in the drier east. Woodland cover, about 13 percent, is low by European standards but increasing. Fourteen national parks and numerous protected areas conserve the most valued landscapes.
