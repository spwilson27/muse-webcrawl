La Seu d'Urgell

La Seu d'Urgell is a town and municipality in the Catalan comarca of Alt Urgell, in the province of Lleida, Spain, close to the border with Andorra. It lies at the confluence of the Segre and Valira rivers in the Pyrenees and is the seat of the Bishop of Urgell — who is, jointly with the President of France, co-prince of Andorra.

Landmarks

The town's cathedral of Santa Maria, built in the 12th century, is a notable example of Catalan Romanesque architecture, with an attached diocesan museum holding illuminated manuscripts including a copy of the Beatus of Urgell.

Sport

The Parc Olímpic del Segre, built for the 1992 Barcelona Olympics, hosted the canoe slalom events in the town and remains an international whitewater venue.
