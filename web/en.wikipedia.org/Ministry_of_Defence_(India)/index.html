Ministry of Defence (India)

The Ministry of Defence (MoD) is the Government of India ministry responsible for national defence and the Indian Armed Forces. Headquartered in the South Block of the Secretariat Building in New Delhi, it is headed by the Minister of Defence. The President of India is the ceremonial commander-in-chief of the armed forces.

Ministry of Defence
Formed: 1947
Headquarters: South Block, New Delhi
Minister responsible: Minister of Defence
Departments: Defence, Military Affairs, Defence Production, Defence R&D, Ex-Servicemen Welfare

Organisation

The ministry consists of five departments, including the Department of Military Affairs, headed by the Chief of Defence Staff (a post created in 2019), the Department of Defence Production, and the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO). It oversees the Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force, and the Indian Coast Guard.

History

The ministry traces its origins to the Military Department of the British East India Company; after independence in 1947 it became the Ministry of Defence, with Baldev Singh as the first Defence Minister.
