Mexico City metropolitan area

Population~22 million (2020)
Area~7,866 km²
Constituent unitsMexico City, State of Mexico, Hidalgo

The Mexico City metropolitan area (Spanish: Zona Metropolitana del Valle de México, ZMVM) is the metropolitan area of Mexico City, comprising Mexico City itself, 60 municipalities of the State of Mexico and one municipality of Hidalgo state. With a population of about 22 million at the 2020 census, it is one of the largest metropolitan areas in the world and the largest Spanish-speaking city region.

Geography

The metropolis occupies the Valley of Mexico, a high basin at roughly 2,240 metres elevation surrounded by mountains, including the volcanoes Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl. Its growth has spilled into surrounding valleys, forming a wider megalopolis (the corona regional) with Toluca, Cuernavaca, Puebla and other cities.

Challenges

The area faces severe air pollution, water scarcity and aquifer depletion, land subsidence, and heavy traffic congestion, addressed through measures such as the Hoy No Circula driving restrictions and expansion of the Metro and Metrobús networks.
