Bonifacio Global City (abbreviated BGC, also known as The Fort) is a central business district and township in Taguig, Metro Manila, Philippines. Developed on land formerly part of the Fort Bonifacio military reservation, it is one of the country's premier financial, commercial, and lifestyle centres, housing corporate headquarters, embassies, international schools, and the Philippine Stock Exchange's trading floor.

History

The site was part of Fort William McKinley, established during the American colonial period and renamed Fort Bonifacio. In 1995 the Bases Conversion and Development Authority sold a controlling stake in the development to a private consortium (Bonifacio Land Corporation, later joined by Ayala Land and the Campos group), initiating the district's master-planned development.

Development

BGC is known for its wide avenues, grid layout, underground utilities, Bonifacio High Street shopping strip, Mind Museum, and numerous office and residential towers. Its disciplined traffic rules distinguish it from much of Metro Manila.

Jurisdiction

The district falls under the city of Taguig, though its location within the former Fort Bonifacio area was long the subject of a territorial dispute between Taguig and Makati, resolved by the Supreme Court in Taguig's favour.
