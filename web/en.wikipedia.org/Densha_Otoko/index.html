Origin2channel message board, 2004
MediaNovel, manga, film (2005), television drama (2005)

Densha Otoko (電車男, "Train Man") is a Japanese story, purportedly based on real events, about a young otaku who defends a woman from a drunk man harassing her on a train, and then seeks advice on the internet forum 2channel about how to pursue a relationship with her. The thread's posts were compiled into a bestselling book in 2004.

Story

Posting under the name Densha Otoko ("Train Man"), the shy 22-year-old reports receiving an expensive Hermès tea set as a thank-you gift from the woman, whom forum users nicknamed "Hermès". With coaching from the thread's participants on grooming, clothing and dating, he gradually gains confidence and eventually confesses his feelings successfully. The authenticity of the story has been debated, but it became a cultural phenomenon regardless.

Adaptations

The story was adapted into several manga series, a 2005 feature film, and a popular Fuji TV drama series the same year. It is frequently cited as a landmark of internet-based storytelling and of early-2000s Japanese popular culture.
