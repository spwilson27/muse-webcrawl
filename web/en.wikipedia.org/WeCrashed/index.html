WeCrashed

WeCrashed is an American drama miniseries that premiered on Apple TV+ on March 18, 2022. Based on the Wondery podcast WeCrashed: The Rise and Fall of WeWork, it dramatizes the growth and collapse of the coworking company WeWork and the story of its co-founder Adam Neumann and his wife Rebekah Neumann.

Premise

Genre: Drama
Starring: Jared Leto, Anne Hathaway
No. of episodes: 8
Network: Apple TV+
Release: March 18 – April 22, 2022

The series follows Adam Neumann (Jared Leto), an Israeli-born entrepreneur who, with Miguel McKelvey, builds WeWork from a single shared-office space in New York into a global company valued at $47 billion. Driven by Neumann's grandiose vision and heavy spending financed by SoftBank's Masayoshi Son, the company's planned initial public offering in 2019 exposes losses and governance problems, forcing Neumann's resignation and a dramatic fall in valuation. Anne Hathaway plays Rebekah Neumann, whose spiritual enthusiasms influenced the company's culture and branding.

Production

The series was created by Lee Eisenberg and Drew Crevello. It was filmed in 2021, with Leto adopting Neumann's Israeli accent and mannerisms for the role.

Reception

WeCrashed received generally positive reviews, with praise directed at the lead performances, particularly Hathaway's, while some critics found the series sympathetic to its subjects and similar to other rise-and-fall business dramas of the period.
