Ceremony

A ceremony is a unified ritualistic event with a purpose, usually consisting of a number of artistic components performed on a special occasion. Ceremonies mark important transitions and communal events, such as weddings, funerals, graduations, inaugurations and religious festivals.

Functions

Anthropologists note that ceremonies reinforce social bonds, legitimise authority, and mark changes of status — the "rites of passage" described by Arnold van Gennep, with phases of separation, transition and incorporation. Ceremonies typically involve prescribed words, gestures, music, dress and symbolic objects.

Types

Ceremonies may be religious (liturgies, ordinations, coming-of-age rites) or secular (state openings of parliament, citizenship ceremonies, award presentations). Their scale ranges from private family observances to national televised events such as coronations and Olympic opening ceremonies.
