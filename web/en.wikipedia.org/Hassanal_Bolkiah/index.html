Hassanal Bolkiah Mu'izzaddin Waddaulah (born 15 July 1946) is the Sultan of Brunei since 1967 and the country's prime minister since its independence from the United Kingdom in 1984. He is one of the last absolute monarchs in the world and among the longest-reigning current monarchs; in 2022 he became the longest-reigning living monarch following the death of Queen Elizabeth II.

Reign

Hassanal Bolkiah ascended the throne on 5 October 1967 after the abdication of his father, Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien III, and was crowned on 1 August 1968. Brunei's oil and gas wealth funded extensive development and a comprehensive welfare state with free education and health care and no personal income tax. The Sultan rules under the philosophy of Melayu Islam Beraja (Malay Islamic Monarchy); in 2014 Brunei began phasing in a Syariah penal code, whose most severe provisions drew international criticism and boycotts in 2019, after which the Sultan announced a moratorium on the death penalty.

Wealth and lifestyle

The Sultan was once the world's richest man; his fortune, derived from Brunei's petroleum revenues, has been estimated in the tens of billions of dollars at its peak. He resides in the Istana Nurul Iman, among the world's largest residential palaces, and is famous for a vast collection of rare cars. Control of state finances has been controversial, notably in litigation against his brother Prince Jefri over the Brunei Investment Agency.
