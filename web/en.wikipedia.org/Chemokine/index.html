Chemokine

Chemokines are a family of small secreted proteins that direct the movement of immune cells — chemotaxis — toward sites of infection or injury. They bind to G protein-coupled chemokine receptors on cell surfaces.

About 50 chemokines and 20 receptors are known in humans, grouped into CC, CXC, C and CX3C families by the arrangement of conserved cysteine residues. They are central to inflammation and are targets in HIV and cancer research.
