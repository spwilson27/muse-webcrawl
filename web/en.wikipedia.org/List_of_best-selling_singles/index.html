List of best-selling singles

The best-selling singles of all time are ranked by physical sales, digital downloads and, in modern lists, streaming-equivalent units. The best-selling physical single is generally considered to be Bing Crosby's "White Christmas" (1942), with estimated sales of around 50 million copies.

Top physical singles

  • "White Christmas" — Bing Crosby (~50 million)
  • "Candle in the Wind 1997" / "Something About the Way You Look Tonight" — Elton John (~33 million)
  • "Silent Night" — Bing Crosby (~30 million)
  • "Rock Around the Clock" — Bill Haley & His Comets (~25 million)
  • "We Are the World" — USA for Africa, "I Will Always Love You" — Whitney Houston, "My Heart Will Go On" — Celine Dion

Digital era

Best-selling digital singles include "Despacito" by Luis Fonsi featuring Daddy Yankee, "Shape of You" by Ed Sheeran, "See You Again" by Wiz Khalifa featuring Charlie Puth, "Uptown Funk" by Mark Ronson featuring Bruno Mars, and in China, Xiao Zhan's "Made to Love" and other high-selling digital releases. Streaming lists are headed by songs such as "Blinding Lights" by The Weeknd and "Shape of You".
