Vacuum permittivity

Vacuum permittivity (ε₀)
Value≈ 8.854 × 10⁻¹² F/m
UnitFarads per metre

Vacuum permittivity, denoted ε0 (epsilon zero) and commonly called the electric constant, is the value of the absolute dielectric permittivity of classical vacuum. It relates the electric displacement field to the electric field in free space and appears in Coulomb's law.

Value

Its value is approximately 8.8541878 × 10⁻¹² farads per metre. Since the 2019 revision of the SI, ε0 is determined experimentally from the measured fine-structure constant rather than being an exactly defined quantity.

Role in physics

Together with the vacuum permeability μ0, ε0 determines the speed of light in vacuum, c = 1/√(ε0μ0). It underlies the capacitance of empty capacitors and the strength of electric forces between charges.
