United States Golf Association

USGA
Founded: December 22, 1894
Headquarters: Liberty Corner, New Jersey
Scope: United States and Mexico

The United States Golf Association (USGA) is the United States' national association of golf courses, clubs, and facilities and the governing body of golf for the U.S. and Mexico. Together with The R&A, the USGA produces and interprets the Rules of Golf.

Championships

The USGA conducts 14 national championships, most notably the U.S. Open, the U.S. Women's Open, the U.S. Amateur, and the U.S. Women's Amateur.

Handicapping and research

The association administers the World Handicap System in its territory, maintains the USGA Museum, and funds turfgrass and environmental research.
