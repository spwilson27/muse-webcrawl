Lantern Festival

The Lantern Festival (traditional Chinese: 元宵節) is a Chinese festival celebrated on the fifteenth day of the first month in the lunisolar Chinese calendar, marking the final day of the traditional Chinese New Year celebrations. It dates to the Han dynasty more than 2,000 years ago.

Customs

People light and display elaborate lanterns, solve riddles written on lanterns, perform lion and dragon dances, and eat tangyuan, glutinous rice balls symbolising family unity.

History

The festival is associated with worship of Taiyi in Han times and later with Buddhist customs of lighting lanterns. It is celebrated across China and in Chinese communities worldwide, and in Taiwan the Sky Lantern Festival at Pingxi is famous.
