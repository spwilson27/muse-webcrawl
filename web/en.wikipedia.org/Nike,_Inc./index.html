Nike, Inc.

Nike, Inc. is an American multinational corporation that designs, manufactures and markets athletic footwear, apparel and equipment. Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, it is the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and apparel, known for the "Swoosh" logo, the slogan "Just Do It", and brands including Air Jordan and Converse.

History

The company was founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by University of Oregon runner Phil Knight and his coach Bill Bowerman, initially distributing Japanese Onitsuka Tiger shoes. It launched the Nike brand — named for the Greek goddess of victory — in 1971, with the swoosh designed by Carolyn Davidson. Innovations such as the waffle sole and Air cushioning, combined with endorsements from athletes like Michael Jordan, drove global growth. Nike went public in 1980.

Criticism and business

Nike has faced sustained criticism over labour conditions in overseas factories, prompting monitoring and codes of conduct, and is a major sponsor of athletes, teams and events worldwide.
