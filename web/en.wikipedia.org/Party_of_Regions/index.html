Party of Regions
Founded: 1997
Headquarters: Kyiv, Ukraine

The Party of Regions was a political party in Ukraine, founded in 1997 and for much of its existence the country's largest party. It drew its strongest support from the Russian-speaking industrial east and south of Ukraine and was closely associated with Viktor Yanukovych.

History

The party first gained national prominence in the 2002 parliamentary election. Yanukovych served as prime minister under its banner and won the 2010 presidential election. The party's parliamentary faction dominated the Verkhovna Rada during his presidency.

Collapse

Following the Euromaidan protests and Yanukovych's flight from Kyiv in February 2014, the Party of Regions fractured and lost most of its influence; many former members joined successor groups such as the Opposition Bloc. The party effectively ceased operating as a national force.

Ideology

It generally advocated regionalism, closer ties with Russia alongside European integration rhetoric, and support for the status of the Russian language in Ukraine, though critics described it primarily as a vehicle for its leaders and allied business interests.
