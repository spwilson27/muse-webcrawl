Digital painting

Digital painting is an emerging art form in which traditional painting techniques such as watercolor, oils, impasto, and so on are applied using digital tools by means of a computer, a graphics tablet and stylus, and software. Traditional painting is painting with a pigment medium on a surface, while digital painting uses software to simulate the behavior of traditional media on a virtual canvas.

An artist creating a digital painting using a pen display.

Techniques

Digital painting differs from other forms of digital art, particularly computer-generated art, in that it does not involve the computer rendering from a model. Artists use painting techniques to create the image directly on the computer. Software offers brushes that mimic oils, acrylics, pastels, charcoal, and airbrushes, as well as layers, undo functions, and blending modes unavailable in traditional media.

Software and hardware

Popular programs include Adobe Photoshop, Corel Painter, Krita, and Procreate. Pressure-sensitive tablets from manufacturers such as Wacom allow control of stroke opacity and width. Digital painting is widely used in concept art for film, video games, and illustration.
