List of secretaries of state of the United States

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The United States secretary of state is the head of the Department of State and the country's chief diplomat, ranking fourth in the presidential line of succession. The office was created in 1789, with Thomas Jefferson as its first holder. Many early secretaries of state later became president—Jefferson, Madison, Monroe, John Quincy Adams, Van Buren, and Buchanan all held the office first.

The office

The secretary of state conducts U.S. foreign policy, negotiates treaties and agreements, oversees embassies and consulates, and administers the Foreign Service. The position is widely considered the most senior cabinet post; the secretary is often described as the nation's second-most important executive official after the president.
