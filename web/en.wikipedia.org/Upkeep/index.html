Upkeep

Upkeep is the maintenance and care required to keep property, equipment or a system in good condition. In games, upkeep also denotes a recurring cost or a phase in which ongoing effects are paid or resolved.

Property

Building upkeep includes cleaning, repairs, servicing systems such as heating and plumbing, and grounds maintenance. Deferred upkeep can lead to costly deterioration.

Games

In strategy and card games, upkeep costs balance powerful assets; in Magic: The Gathering the upkeep step begins each turn after untapping.

Costs

Upkeep is part of total cost of ownership alongside purchase price, operation and eventual replacement.
