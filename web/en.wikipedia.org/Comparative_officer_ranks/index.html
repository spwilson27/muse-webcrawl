Comparative officer ranks

Comparative officer ranks set out, service by service, the commissioned officer ranks of the world's armed forces and their equivalents. Separate comparisons are maintained for armies, navies, and air forces, and for groupings such as NATO members, Commonwealth countries, and the Americas.

Army officer ranks

Most armies share a common ladder inherited from European practice: junior officers (lieutenant and captain grades), field officers (major through colonel), and general officers (brigadier through field marshal or five-star general). NATO codes OF-1 to OF-10 standardise these grades for coalition use.

NATO codeGeneric title
OF-10Field marshal / General of the Army
OF-8Lieutenant general
OF-5Colonel

OF-1Lieutenant

Naval and air force ranks

Naval officer ranks run from midshipman/ensign through lieutenant, commander, and captain to flag officers (commodore, admirals). Air forces in the Commonwealth tradition use a distinct RAF-derived system (pilot officer, squadron leader, group captain, air marshal), while others mirror army titles.
