Chronic fatigue syndrome

Chronic fatigue syndrome (CFS), also called myalgic encephalomyelitis (ME) or ME/CFS, is a serious long-term illness characterised by profound fatigue not relieved by rest and post-exertional malaise.

Symptoms and diagnosis

Other features include unrefreshing sleep, cognitive difficulties ("brain fog"), orthostatic intolerance and pain. Diagnosis is clinical, based on criteria such as those of the US Institute of Medicine (2015), after excluding other causes. Onset often follows infection.

Cause and management

The cause is unknown; immune, metabolic and neurological abnormalities are under study. There is no cure or approved drug treatment; management centres on pacing activity, symptom relief and supportive care.
