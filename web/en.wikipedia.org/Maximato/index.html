Maximato

The Maximato is the period in Mexican history from 1928 to 1934 in which former president Plutarco Elías Calles, titled Jefe Máximo ("Maximum Leader"), exercised dominant behind-the-scenes power while three other men formally held the presidency.

Background

After president-elect Álvaro Obregón was assassinated in 1928, Calles — barred from immediate re-election — governed through Emilio Portes Gil, Pascual Ortiz Rubio, and Abelardo L. Rodríguez. In 1929 he founded the National Revolutionary Party (PNR), the ancestor of the PRI, to institutionalize the revolutionary regime.

End

The Maximato ended when president Lázaro Cárdenas, elected in 1934, asserted his independence and exiled Calles in 1936.
