List of Ottoman grand viziers

The Grand Vizier (Ottoman Turkish: Sadr-ı Azam) was the chief minister of the Ottoman Empire, equivalent to a modern prime minister, holding the imperial seal and presiding over the Imperial Council (Divan). The office lasted from the 14th century until the abolition of the sultanate in 1922. The following table lists notable holders of the office.
