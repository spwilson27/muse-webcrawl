Kerala cuisine

Kerala cuisine is the culinary tradition of Kerala, a state in southwestern India. It is known for its use of coconut, rice, seafood, and spices including black pepper, cardamom, curry leaves and mustard seeds tempered in coconut oil.

Overview

The vegetarian sadya, a banquet of many dishes served on a banana leaf during festivals such as Onam, contrasts with the seafood and meat dishes of Malabar and Syrian Christian communities. Staples include appam, puttu, idiyappam, dosa, fish curry, and biryani in the north.

Influences

Arab, Portuguese, Dutch and British trade shaped the cuisine, introducing chillies, potatoes and baking techniques alongside indigenous traditions.
