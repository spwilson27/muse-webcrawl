Blackberry
Genus: Rubus
Family: Rosaceae
Fruit type: Aggregate fruit

The blackberry is an edible aggregate fruit produced by many species in the genus Rubus in the rose family Rosaceae. The fruits are dark purple to black when ripe and are composed of many small drupelets clustered around a central core.

Cultivation

Blackberries grow wild throughout temperate regions and have been cultivated since the 19th century. Modern cultivars include thornless varieties. The plants are perennial, with biennial canes called primocanes and floricanes.

Uses

Blackberries are eaten fresh and used in pies, jams, jellies, wines and desserts. They are rich in vitamin C, vitamin K, manganese and dietary fibre, and their deep colour comes from anthocyanin pigments.
