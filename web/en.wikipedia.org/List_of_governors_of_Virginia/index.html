List of governors of Virginia

The governor of Virginia is the head of government of the Commonwealth of Virginia and the commander-in-chief of the state's military forces. Virginia is the only U.S. state that prohibits its governor from serving consecutive terms: a governor may serve multiple terms, but not back-to-back. The first governor of the independent Commonwealth was Patrick Henry, who served from 1776 to 1779. Virginia was governed before independence by colonial governors appointed by the British Crown and by the Virginia Company of London.

Notes

Douglas Wilder, inaugurated in 1990, was the first African American elected governor of a U.S. state. Eight men born in Virginia became President of the United States, earning Virginia the nickname "Mother of Presidents"; three of them — Jefferson, Monroe and John Tyler — also served as governor.
