List of defence contractors

Defence contractors supply weapons, systems and services to armed forces. Rankings by arms revenue are published annually by SIPRI and Defense News. US companies dominate the top of the list.

Largest contractors (by defence revenue)

  • Lockheed Martin (USA) — F-35, missiles, space systems
  • RTX (Raytheon Technologies, USA) — missiles, engines, avionics
  • Northrop Grumman (USA) — B-21, space, sensors
  • Boeing (USA) — aircraft, defence and space
  • General Dynamics (USA) — submarines, tanks, IT
  • BAE Systems (UK/USA) — largest European contractor
  • L3Harris Technologies (USA)
  • AVIC, CETC, Norinco, CASIC, CSGC (China — state-owned groups)
  • Airbus (Europe), Leonardo (Italy), Thales (France), MBDA, Rheinmetall (Germany), Saab (Sweden), Rafael and Elbit Systems (Israel), Hanwha and LIG Nex1 (South Korea), Rostec (Russia)

Contractors range from platform manufacturers (ships, aircraft, vehicles) to electronics, munitions, cyber and services firms. The industry consolidated heavily after the Cold War.
