Labour Party (Ireland)

Labour Party
Irish: Páirtí an Lucht Oibre
Founded: 1912
Ideology: Social democracy, democratic socialism
Position: Centre-left to left-wing

The Labour Party (Irish: Páirtí an Lucht Oibre) is a centre-left and social democratic political party in the Republic of Ireland. Founded in 1912 in Clonmel, County Tipperary, by James Connolly, James Larkin and William O'Brien as the political wing of the Irish Trades Union Congress, it is the oldest political party in Ireland and the only one pre-dating independence still in existence.

Labour has served in coalition governments on many occasions, most often with Fine Gael, including 2011–2016 following its best-ever election result in 2011, when it won 37 seats. That government implemented austerity after the financial crisis, and the party suffered heavy losses in 2016. Labour is a member of the Party of European Socialists and Socialist International. Ivana Bacik became party leader in 2022.
