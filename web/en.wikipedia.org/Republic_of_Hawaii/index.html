Republic of Hawaii
Status: Unrecognised republic / U.S. protectorate in practice
Capital: Honolulu
Existed: 1894–1898
President: Sanford B. Dole

The Republic of Hawaii was a short-lived one-party state in Hawaii between July 4, 1894, when the Provisional Government of Hawaii ended, and August 12, 1898, when it was annexed by the United States as the Territory of Hawaii.

History

The republic was proclaimed by the oligarchy that had overthrown Queen Liliʻuokalani in 1893, after the Cleveland administration in Washington declined to approve immediate annexation. Sanford B. Dole served as its only president. A royalist counter-rebellion in 1895 failed, and Liliʻuokalani was placed under house arrest.

Annexation

During the Spanish–American War, Hawaii's strategic value led to annexation by the Newlands Resolution in 1898; sovereignty was formally transferred on August 12, 1898.
