Mount Meron (Hebrew: Har Meron; Arabic: Jabal al-Jarmaq) is a mountain in the Upper Galilee region of Israel. At 1,208 metres it is the highest peak within Israel's pre-1967 boundaries (the Golan Heights' Mount Hermon is higher). Much of the mountain is a large nature reserve.

Religious significance

At the foot of the mountain lies the village of Meron, site of the tomb of Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, traditionally credited as author of the Zohar. Each year on Lag BaOmer hundreds of thousands of pilgrims gather there for the hillula (celebration) at the tomb. In 2021 a crowd crush during the festival killed 45 people, Israel's deadliest civilian disaster.

Nature

The Meron nature reserve is the largest in northern Israel, with Mediterranean woodland of oak, pistachio and juniper, and a summit trail offering views across the Galilee to the Mediterranean and Mount Hermon.
