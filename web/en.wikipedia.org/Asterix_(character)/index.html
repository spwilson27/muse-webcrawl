Asterix is the fictional hero of the French comic book series of the same name, created by writer René Goscinny and illustrator Albert Uderzo. A small but cunning warrior, he lives in a village in Armorica (Brittany) in 50 BC — the last corner of Gaul still holding out against the Roman occupation.

Character

Asterix derives his superhuman strength from a magic potion brewed by the village druid, Getafix, which gives him the power to outwit and outfight Roman legionaries several times his size. He is recognisable by his winged helmet, blond moustache, and black tunic. His best friend is the menhir delivery man Obelix, who fell into the potion cauldron as a baby and is permanently super-strong, and his adventures — missions for the village that take the pair across the ancient world — are narrated with wordplay, anachronisms, and satire of contemporary national stereotypes.

Legacy

The series has sold hundreds of millions of copies in over a hundred languages, making Asterix France's most famous comic character; he has appeared in numerous animated and live-action films, and gives his name to the Parc Astérix theme park near Paris and to France's first satellite, Astérix (1965).
