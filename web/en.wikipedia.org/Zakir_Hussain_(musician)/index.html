Zakir Hussain (9 March 1951 – 15 December 2024) was an Indian tabla player, composer, percussionist, music producer and film actor. Widely regarded as one of the greatest tabla players of all time, he was a child prodigy and the eldest son of the legendary tabla maestro Ustad Alla Rakha.

Early life

Hussain was born in Bombay (now Mumbai) and began playing the tabla at a very early age under his father's tutelage. He gave his first professional performance at the age of twelve and began touring internationally by his late teens, accompanying leading Hindustani classical musicians.

Career

Hussain became famous both as an accompanist to vocalists and instrumentalists of Hindustani classical music and as a soloist. He moved to the United States in 1970. He collaborated widely across genres, co-founding the fusion group Shakti with guitarist John McLaughlin and violinist L. Shankar, and later performing with the groups Remember Shakti, Tabla Beat Science and Planet Drum, the last with Grateful Dead drummer Mickey Hart. He composed music for films and appeared as an actor in several productions.

Awards and honours

He received the Padma Shri in 1988, the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award in 1990, the Padma Bhushan in 2002 and the Padma Vibhushan in 2023. He won multiple Grammy Awards, including three at the 2024 ceremony for his work with Shakti and with Béla Fleck and Edgar Meyer.

Death

Hussain died in San Francisco on 15 December 2024 from complications of idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, aged 73.
