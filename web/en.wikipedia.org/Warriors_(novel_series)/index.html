Warriors (novel series)

Warriors is a series of children's fantasy novels about the adventures of feral cats living in Clans, published by HarperCollins. The books are written by a team of authors under the pseudonym Erin Hunter, including Kate Cary, Cherith Baldry and Tui T. Sutherland, with editor Victoria Holmes originally developing the concept.

Overview

The series began in 2003 with Into the Wild, following Rusty, a house cat who joins ThunderClan, one of four Clans of wild cats living by a warrior code and guided by the spirits of their ancestors, StarClan. It is organised into multi-book arcs — including The Prophecies Begin, The New Prophecy, Power of Three, Omen of the Stars, Dawn of the Clans, A Vision of Shadows and later arcs — supplemented by Super Edition stand-alone novels, novellas, manga and field guides.

Reception

The books have appeared on the New York Times bestseller list and sold tens of millions of copies worldwide, spawning a large fan community. A film adaptation has been in development.
