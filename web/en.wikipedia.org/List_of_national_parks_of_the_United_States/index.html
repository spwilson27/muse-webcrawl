List of national parks of the United States

The United States has 63 national parks, areas designated by Congress for their natural beauty, unique geological features, diverse ecosystems, and recreational opportunities. They are operated by the National Park Service, an agency of the Department of the Interior created in 1916. Yellowstone, established on March 1, 1872, was the world's first national park.

Overview

Thirty states and two US territories (American Samoa and the US Virgin Islands) have national parks. Alaska has the most (eight) and the largest. The park system as a whole covers more than 85 million acres and receives over 300 million recreation visits in busy years; the busiest park, Great Smoky Mountains, alone draws more than ten million visitors annually.
