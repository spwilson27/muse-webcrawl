Perk&

Perk& offered workstation coffee services to office clients.

Perk& was a Canadian workplace coffee and refreshment services company. It operated in the office coffee service (OCS) industry, supplying coffee brewing equipment, coffee and tea products, and related break-room supplies and services to business customers across Canada.

History

Perk& grew out of the Canadian office coffee service sector, a business-to-business market in which providers install and maintain brewers in client workplaces and deliver consumables on a recurring schedule. The company positioned itself around workplace "perks" — better coffee and break-room amenities as an employee benefit — and its branding drew on the ampersand in its name.

Services

The company's offerings included coffee and espresso machines, single-cup brewers, water filtration, teas, hot beverages, and break-room snacks and supplies. Like other operators in the sector, it provided equipment installation, servicing, and restocking as part of subscription-style arrangements with employers.
