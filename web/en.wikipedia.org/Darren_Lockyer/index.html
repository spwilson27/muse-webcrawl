Darren Lockyer

Darren James Lockyer (born 22 March 1977) is an Australian former professional rugby league footballer who captained Australia in international matches and his home state Queensland in State of Origin as well as the Brisbane Broncos in the National Rugby League. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the history of the game, Lockyer began his career as a fullback before switching to five-eighth in 2004.

Playing career

Lockyer made his first-grade debut for the Broncos in 1995 and won premierships with the club in 1997, 1998, 2000 and 2006. He won the Clive Churchill Medal as player of the match in the 2000 Grand Final and the Golden Boot Award as the world's best player in 2003 and 2006. He holds records for the most games played for the Broncos, the most State of Origin appearances for Queensland, and at his retirement was the most-capped Australian international of all time.

Later career

Lockyer retired at the end of the 2011 season after 355 games for Brisbane. He was inducted into the NRL Hall of Fame and has worked as a rugby league commentator for the Nine Network.
