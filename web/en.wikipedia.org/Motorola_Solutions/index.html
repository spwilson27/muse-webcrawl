Motorola Solutions, Inc. is an American data communications and telecommunications equipment provider headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. It is the legal successor of the original Motorola, Inc., following the spin-off of the mobile phone division into Motorola Mobility in 2011.

History

On January 4, 2011, Motorola, Inc. split into two independent public companies: Motorola Mobility and Motorola Solutions. Motorola Solutions became the direct legal successor to Motorola, Inc., retaining the company's pre-split stock price history and the ticker symbol MSI on the New York Stock Exchange. Motorola Mobility was later sold to Google in 2012 and subsequently to Lenovo in 2014.

The company sold its enterprise business to Zebra Technologies in 2014 for approximately US$3.45 billion, refocusing on public safety communications and mission-critical services.

Products and services

Motorola Solutions supplies land mobile radio (LMR) systems, including APX and ASTRO 25 digital radios used by police, fire and emergency services. It also sells video security and access control systems (including through acquisitions such as Avigilon and Pelco), body-worn cameras, license plate recognition technology, and command center software such as 9-1-1 call handling systems.

Acquisitions

Notable acquisitions include Avigilon (2018), WatchGuard and Pelco (2019–2020), Openpath (2021), and Silent Sentinel and other video and analytics firms, expanding the company into integrated public-safety and enterprise security ecosystems.
