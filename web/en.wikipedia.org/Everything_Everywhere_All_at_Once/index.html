Everything Everywhere All at Once is a 2022 American absurdist comedy-drama film written and directed by Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert, collectively known as "Daniels". It stars Michelle Yeoh as Evelyn Wang, a Chinese-American laundromat owner who discovers she must save the multiverse by channelling the lives of her alternate selves.

Plot

Audited by the IRS and struggling with her family relationships, Evelyn learns from an alternate version of her husband Waymond that a multiversal threat, Jobu Tupaki — a version of her daughter Joy — seeks to destroy everything. Evelyn must "verse-jump" into parallel lives, including a movie star, a chef, and a world in which people have hot dogs for fingers, to confront despair and find meaning through kindness.

Reception and awards

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing over $140 million against a modest budget. At the 95th Academy Awards it won seven awards, including Best Picture, Best Director, Best Actress for Yeoh, and Best Supporting Actor for Ke Huy Quan, becoming one of the most awarded films of all time.
