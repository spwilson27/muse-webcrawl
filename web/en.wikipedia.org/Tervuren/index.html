Tervuren

[Placeholder image: Tervuren.png]

Tervuren is a municipality in the province of Flemish Brabant, in the Flemish Region of Belgium. Situated just east of Brussels, it is known for the Royal Museum for Central Africa, founded after the Brussels International Exposition of 1897, and for the surrounding Tervuren Park and the Sonian Forest.

History

Tervuren was a hunting residence of the Dukes of Brabant. The Africa Museum's collections were assembled during the period of the Congo Free State and Belgian Congo; the museum reopened in 2018 after a major renovation addressing its colonial history.
