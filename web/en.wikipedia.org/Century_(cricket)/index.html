Century (cricket)

In cricket, a century is a score of 100 or more runs in a single innings by a batter. It is regarded as a landmark achievement for a batter, roughly comparable to a five-wicket haul for a bowler.

Significance

Reaching a century is marked by applause from spectators and teammates, and the batter typically acknowledges the crowd by raising their bat. Scores between 50 and 99 are called half-centuries. A century in each innings of a match, or a double century (200) or triple century (300), are rarer feats celebrated in record lists.

Records

Sachin Tendulkar holds the record for most centuries in international cricket, with 100 across Tests and One Day Internationals. In first-class cricket, Jack Hobbs scored 199 centuries in his career. The first double century in ODI cricket was scored by Tendulkar in 2010.
