Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (India)

Formed: 15 August 1947

Headquarters: Shastri Bhawan, New Delhi

Jurisdiction: Government of India

Subordinate bodies: Prasar Bharati, Press Information Bureau, Central Board of Film Certification

The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) is a ministerial agency of the Government of India responsible for the formulation and administration of rules, regulations, and laws relating to information, broadcasting, the press, and films in India.

Responsibilities

The ministry oversees public broadcasting through Prasar Bharati, which operates Doordarshan television and All India Radio. Its attached and subordinate offices include the Press Information Bureau (the government's communications arm), the Central Board of Film Certification, the Registrar of Newspapers for India, the Publications Division, and the Indian Institute of Mass Communication.

Policy areas

The MIB frames broadcasting content rules, cable television regulation, film certification policy, and official advertising policy, and it organises events such as the International Film Festival of India. Its regulatory reach over news media and digital content has made it a subject of recurring debate about press freedom in India.
