The American Silver Eagle is the official silver bullion coin of the United States. It was first released by the United States Mint on November 24, 1986, under the authority of the Liberty Coin Act of 1985. Each coin contains one troy ounce of 99.9% pure silver and has a nominal face value of one dollar, though its market price tracks the value of silver plus a premium.

Design

The obverse adapts Adolph A. Weinman's Walking Liberty design, originally used on the half dollar from 1916 to 1947. The reverse originally featured a heraldic eagle designed by John Mercanti; in 2021 a new reverse by Emily Damstra, showing an eagle coming in for a landing, was introduced alongside enhanced security features.

Versions

The coin is produced in bullion, proof, and uncirculated (burnished) finishes, and has been struck at the Philadelphia, San Francisco, and West Point mints. Special issues have included the 1995-W proof, a key rarity, and various anniversary sets.
