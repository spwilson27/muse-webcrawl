Jenner Institute

The Jenner Institute is a research institute on the Old Road Campus in Headington, east Oxford, England. It is a partnership between the University of Oxford and the Institute for Animal Health (now part of the Pirbright Institute). The institute is named after Edward Jenner, the inventor of vaccination.

History

The institute was formed in November 2005, bringing together vaccine research groups previously based at the University of Oxford and the Institute for Animal Health's Compton Laboratory. Its founding director was Adrian Hill.

Research

The Jenner Institute develops vaccines for diseases including malaria, tuberculosis, HIV, Ebola, influenza and hepatitis. It is known for its viral-vectored vaccine platform based on chimpanzee adenovirus (ChAdOx). During the COVID-19 pandemic, researchers at the institute, led by Sarah Gilbert and Andrew Pollard, developed the Oxford–AstraZeneca COVID-19 vaccine (ChAdOx1 nCoV-19 / AZD1222), which was approved for use in the United Kingdom in December 2020 and distributed worldwide through AstraZeneca and the Serum Institute of India.
