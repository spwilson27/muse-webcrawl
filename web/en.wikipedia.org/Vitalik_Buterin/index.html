Vitaly Dmitrievich "Vitalik" Buterin (born 31 January 1994) is a Canadian computer programmer and writer who is best known as one of the co-founders of Ethereum. Buterin became involved with cryptocurrency early in its inception, co-founding Bitcoin Magazine in 2011. In 2013, he visited developers in other countries who shared his enthusiasm for code, and the following year he published the white paper proposing Ethereum, a decentralized platform for smart contracts and decentralized applications.

Early life and education

Buterin was born in Kolomna, Russia, and emigrated to Canada with his parents at the age of six, settling in Toronto. He was placed in a class for gifted children in elementary school and was drawn to mathematics, programming, and economics. He attended the Abelard School, a private high school in Toronto, and briefly enrolled at the University of Waterloo before leaving to work on cryptocurrency projects full-time after receiving a Thiel Fellowship in 2014.

Ethereum

Ethereum's development was funded by an online crowdsale in mid-2014, and the network went live on 30 July 2015. Buterin has remained a central figure in its research community, advocating the transition to proof of stake (completed in "the Merge" in September 2022) and writing extensively on protocol design, governance, and the social philosophy of decentralization.

Philanthropy and recognition

Buterin has donated large sums of cryptocurrency to charities, including a widely reported 2021 donation of Shiba Inu coin tokens to India's Crypto Relief fund during the COVID-19 pandemic. He has appeared on Forbes' 30 Under 30 list and was named to Time magazine's 100 most influential people in 2021.
