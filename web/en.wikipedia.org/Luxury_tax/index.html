Luxury tax

A luxury tax is a tax on luxury goods — products deemed non-essential or primarily consumed by the wealthy. It may be levied as a sales tax or value-added tax applying only above a threshold price.

Rationale

Supporters argue luxury taxes raise revenue progressively and discourage conspicuous consumption. Critics note they can harm domestic industries producing the taxed goods and may raise little revenue if demand falls or buyers shop abroad.

Examples

Historically taxes have applied to items such as yachts, private aircraft, jewellery, expensive cars and furs. In professional sports, "luxury tax" also denotes the payroll penalty systems used by leagues such as Major League Baseball and the NBA — a separate usage covered at luxury tax (sports).
