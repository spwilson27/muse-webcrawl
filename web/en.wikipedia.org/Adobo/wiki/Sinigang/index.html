Sinigang

Sinigang is a sour soup or stew from the Philippines, considered one of the country's most popular and characteristic dishes. It is traditionally soured with tamarind (sampalok), though calamansi, guava, green mango, kamias, santol, or watermelon may also be used. In 2021, the food website TasteAtlas named sinigang the world's best vegetable soup.

Preparation

A protein — most commonly pork, but also milkfish (bangus), salmon head, shrimp, or beef — is simmered with tomatoes, onions, and the souring fruit, then finished with vegetables such as labanos (daikon), kangkong (water spinach), okra, eggplant, string beans, and taro (gabi), which thickens the broth. The dish is seasoned with fish sauce and eaten with steamed rice.

Variants

Regional and household variants include sinigang na baboy (pork), sinigang na hipon (shrimp), sinampalukang manok (chicken with tamarind leaves), and the Batangas dish bulalo-adjacent sinigang na baka. Modern households often use powdered tamarind soup mixes.
