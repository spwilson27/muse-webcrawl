Esher is a town in Surrey, England, to the east of the River Mole. It lies in the borough of Elmbridge, close to the border with Greater London, and forms part of the London commuter belt. The town is known for Sandown Park Racecourse and for its historic associations with Claremont, a former royal residence.

History

Esher appears in the Domesday Book of 1086 as Aissela. In the 16th century Henry VIII commandeered the area as a hunting ground around Hampton Court, and the manor passed through several noble hands. Cardinal Wolsey was held at Esher Place after his fall from favour. Claremont House was later home to Princess Charlotte and Prince Leopold, and Queen Victoria spent time there as a child.

Transport and amenities

Esher railway station, on the South West Main Line, provides services to London Waterloo. The A307 Portsmouth Road runs through the town, which retains a high street with a cinema, restaurants, and independent shops. Sandown Park, opened in 1875, hosts flat and jump racing.
