List of mountains

This is a list of notable mountains arranged by elevation above sea level. The highest peaks on Earth are concentrated in the ranges of High Asia — the Himalaya, Karakoram, Hindu Kush, Pamir and Kunlun — formed by the ongoing collision of the Indian Plate with the Eurasian Plate. Only fourteen mountains, the eight-thousanders, exceed 8,000 metres.

Most prominent mountains

Topographic prominence measures a peak's height relative to the surrounding terrain. Everest is the most prominent mountain on Earth by definition (8,849 m), followed by Aconcagua (6,961 m), Denali (6,144 m), Kilimanjaro (5,885 m) and Mount Elbrus (4,741 m).
