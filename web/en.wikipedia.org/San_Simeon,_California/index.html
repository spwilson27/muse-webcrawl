San Simeon is a small unincorporated community and census-designated place on the Pacific coast of San Luis Obispo County, California, along State Route 1 between Cambria and the Big Sur coast.

History

The settlement developed as a whaling and shipping point on land owned by the Hearst family. It is best known for Hearst Castle, the opulent hilltop estate built by newspaper publisher William Randolph Hearst with architect Julia Morgan between 1919 and 1947, which overlooks the village from the Santa Lucia Range and is now a California State Park attracting about a million visitors annually.

Nature

Just north of the village, the Piedras Blancas beach hosts one of the largest mainland rookeries of northern elephant seals, where thousands of seals haul out to breed, moult and rest. The historic Piedras Blancas Light Station stands nearby.
