WBEZ (91.5 FM) is a non-commercial public radio station in Chicago, Illinois, operated by Chicago Public Media. It is the Chicago area's flagship National Public Radio (NPR) member station, broadcasting a news and talk format.

History

The station traces its origins to broadcasts by the Chicago Board of Education (hence the call sign) beginning in the 1940s. It joined NPR as a charter member in 1970 and was transferred to the independent Chicago Public Media in the 1990s.

Programming

WBEZ carries NPR news programmes alongside local journalism and is best known as the home of This American Life, hosted by Ira Glass, and the popular motoring show Car Talk in its earlier syndication era. Its podcasts and productions, distributed through Chicago Public Media, include the long-running storytelling programme The Moth broadcasts and various news features.
