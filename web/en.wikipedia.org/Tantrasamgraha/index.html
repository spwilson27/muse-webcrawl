The Tantrasamgraha ("compendium of astronomical systems") is a major treatise in Sanskrit on astronomy and mathematics, composed around 1500–1501 CE by Nilakantha Somayaji (1444–1544) of the Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics in Tirur, Kerala.

Contents

Written in 432 verses across eight chapters, the work covers planetary motions, eclipses, the rising and setting of planets, and lunar phases. Nilakantha presented a partially heliocentric model in which Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn orbit the Sun, which in turn orbits the Earth — comparable to the later Tychonic system. The text also gives infinite series results for π and trigonometric functions, building on Madhava's work.

Commentary

The author's son Sankara Variar wrote the commentary Tantrasamgraha-vyakhya, and the work remained authoritative in Kerala for centuries, notably through Jyeshtadeva's Malayalam commentary tradition (Yuktibhasha).
