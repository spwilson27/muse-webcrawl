Holmenkollbakken
Location: Holmenkollen, Oslo, Norway
Opened: 1892; rebuilt many times
Event: Holmenkollen Ski Festival

Holmenkollbakken is a large ski jumping hill located at Holmenkollen in Oslo, Norway. It has a hill size of HS134, a construction point of K-120, and spectator capacity around 70,000, and is one of the most famous ski jumping venues in the world.

History

Ski jumping at Holmenkollen began in 1892, and the hill has been rebuilt repeatedly, including for the 1952 Winter Olympics. The distinctive current structure, with its steel profile and wind screens, opened in 2010 ahead of the 2011 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships.

The venue

The site includes the Holmenkollen Ski Museum, founded in 1923 and among the oldest ski museums in the world, and a ski simulator. The hill is a national symbol of Norway's skiing heritage.
