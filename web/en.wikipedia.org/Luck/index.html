Luck

Luck is the phenomenon and belief that defines the experience of improbable events, especially improbably positive or negative ones. Naturalistic interpretations view luck as the product of chance and probability; supernatural interpretations treat it as an attribute of a person or object, or the result of a favourable or unfavourable view of events by a deity.

Beliefs and symbols

Cultures attach luck to symbols such as the four-leaf clover, horseshoes, lucky numbers and charms, and to goddesses such as Fortuna and Tyche. Psychologists note that beliefs about luck relate to locus of control, superstition and risk-taking behaviour, and that "lucky" outcomes are often explained after the fact by cognitive biases.
