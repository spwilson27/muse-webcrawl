Marine Corps Base Quantico

Marine Corps Base Quantico is a United States Marine Corps installation located near Triangle, Virginia, covering nearly 86 square miles across three counties on the Potomac River. Established in 1917, it is known as the "Crossroads of the Marine Corps" because so many Marines pass through its schools during their careers. The base's town, Quantico, is entirely surrounded by the installation.

Major commands and activities

Quantico hosts Marine Corps University, the Officer Candidates School (OCS) and The Basic School (TBS), through which every Marine officer passes, as well as the Marine Corps Combat Development Command, which develops Marine doctrine and equipment. Its ranges and training areas support live-fire exercises, and its airfield (Turner Field) hosts the presidential helicopter squadron HMX-1, which operates Marine One.

Civilian agencies

The base also hosts major federal law-enforcement training facilities. The FBI Academy, opened in 1972, trains new FBI special agents and intelligence analysts at Quantico, as does the DEA Training Academy on the same grounds. The Naval Criminal Investigative Service (NCIS) headquarters is located on base, and the National Museum of the Marine Corps stands just outside the main gate, its silhouette modelled on the flag-raising on Iwo Jima.

History

The Marine Corps first leased land at Quantico in 1917 for wartime training, purchasing it in 1918. Between the world wars, officers there — under leaders such as Smedley Butler and later the schools' staff — developed the amphibious warfare doctrine that the Corps applied in the Pacific in the Second World War. Today more than 12,000 military, civilian and contractor personnel work or train at Quantico.
