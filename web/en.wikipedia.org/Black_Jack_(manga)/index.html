Black Jack (manga)

AuthorOsamu Tezuka
MagazineWeekly Shōnen Champion
Original run1973–1983
GenreMedical drama

Black Jack (Japanese: Burakku Jakku) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Osamu Tezuka, himself trained as a physician. It was serialized in Weekly Shōnen Champion from 1973 to 1983 and consists largely of self-contained stories.

Premise

Black Jack is an unlicensed but brilliant surgeon who charges exorbitant fees for seemingly impossible operations, aided by his childlike assistant Pinoko — a girl he constructed from a parasitic twin. Beneath his mercenary exterior he frequently shows compassion, waiving fees or defying corrupt patients and officials.

Legacy

The series is one of Tezuka's most popular works and a landmark of medical manga. It has been adapted into numerous anime series, films, OVAs, and live-action productions, and it influenced later medical dramas. A spin-off, Young Black Jack, follows the character's student years.
