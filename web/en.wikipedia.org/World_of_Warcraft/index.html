World of Warcraft (WoW) is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game released in 2004 by Blizzard Entertainment. Set in the fantasy world of Azeroth, it is the fourth released game in the Warcraft franchise and takes place four years after the events of Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne.

Gameplay

Players control a character avatar within a persistent game world, exploring landscapes, fighting monsters, completing quests, and interacting with other players and non-player characters. Characters belong to one of two factions, the Alliance or the Horde, and advance by gaining experience levels, acquiring equipment, and joining guilds to undertake group content such as dungeons and raids.

Expansions and success

The game has received numerous expansions, including The Burning Crusade (2007), Wrath of the Lich King (2008), Cataclysm (2010), Mists of Pandaria (2012), Warlords of Draenor (2014), Legion (2016), Battle for Azeroth (2018), Shadowlands (2020), Dragonflight (2022), and The War Within (2024). At its peak in 2010 it had 12 million subscribers, making it the most popular MMORPG of its era. A recreation of the original game, World of Warcraft Classic, was released in 2019.

Cultural impact

World of Warcraft became a major cultural phenomenon, referenced in television and popular culture, and has been studied by researchers in fields ranging from epidemiology (the 2005 Corrupted Blood incident) to economics.
