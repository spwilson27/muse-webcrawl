Chen Jingrun

Chen Jingrun (22 May 1933 – 19 March 1996) was a Chinese mathematician who made important contributions to analytic number theory, particularly on Goldbach's conjecture.

In 1966 (published in detail in 1973 due to the Cultural Revolution), Chen proved what is now called Chen's theorem: every sufficiently large even number is the sum of a prime and a number with at most two prime factors (a semiprime) — the closest result to Goldbach's conjecture for many years. He also improved results on twin primes and Waring's problem.

Born in Fuzhou, Chen overcame poverty and persecution during the Cultural Revolution, working in difficult conditions. Xu Chi's 1978 biographical essay "Goldbach's Conjecture" made him a national hero in China, symbolizing scientific dedication. Asteroid 7681 Chenjingrun is named for him.
