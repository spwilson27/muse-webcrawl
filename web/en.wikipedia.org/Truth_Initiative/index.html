The Truth Initiative is a nonprofit tobacco control organization based in Washington, D.C., founded in 1998 as the American Legacy Foundation under the Tobacco Master Settlement Agreement between US states and major cigarette companies. It was renamed Truth Initiative in 2015.

truth campaign

Its best-known programme is the truth youth smoking prevention media campaign, launched in 2000, which uses advertising exposing tobacco industry marketing practices; studies have credited it with a significant share of the decline in US youth smoking rates.

Other work

The organization funds research and grants, runs cessation programmes such as BecomeAnEX and the text-based This is Quitting service, and has expanded its focus to e-cigarettes and youth vaping.
