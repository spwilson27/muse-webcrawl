Swedish Air Force

The Swedish Air Force (Swedish: Svenska flygvapnet) is the air force branch of the Swedish Armed Forces. Founded in 1926, it operates primarily domestically designed combat aircraft, reflecting Sweden's long policy of armed neutrality and self-reliance.

Aircraft

Its front-line fighter is the Saab JAS 39 Gripen, which followed the Saab 35 Draken and Saab 37 Viggen. The force also flies Saab 340 airborne early warning aircraft, Lockheed C-130 Hercules transports, and helicopters shared with the joint Helicopter Wing.

Organisation

The Air Force is organised into wings (flottiljer) such as F 7 at Såtenäs, F 17 at Ronneby and F 21 at Luleå, and historically used a dispersed basing system of road runways (Bas 90) designed for wartime survival. Following Sweden's accession to NATO in 2024, its forces are integrated into alliance air policing and planning.
