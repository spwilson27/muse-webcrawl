The Gilded Age (TV series)

The Gilded Age
Genre: Historical drama
Created by: Julian Fellowes
Starring: Carrie Coon, Morgan Spector, Louisa Jacobson, Denée Benton, Cynthia Nixon, Christine Baranski
Country: United States
Original network: HBO
Original release: January 24, 2022 – present

The Gilded Age is an American historical drama television series created and written by Julian Fellowes for HBO. Set in the United States during the Gilded Age, the boom years of the 1880s in New York City, the series follows the conflict between the old-money van Rhijn family and their new-money neighbors, the Russell family, who have made a fortune in railroads.

Premise

In 1882, Marian Brook, the orphaned daughter of a Union general, moves from rural Pennsylvania to New York City to live with her estranged aunts, Agnes van Rhijn and Ada Brook. Marian becomes entangled in the social war between her aunts' old-money world and the fabulously wealthy Russell family across the street, railroad tycoon George Russell and his ambitious wife Bertha. The series also follows Peggy Scott, a young African-American writer returning home to Brooklyn after attending school in Philadelphia.

Cast and characters

The ensemble cast includes Carrie Coon as Bertha Russell, Morgan Spector as George Russell, Louisa Jacobson as Marian Brook, Denée Benton as Peggy Scott, Cynthia Nixon as Ada Brook (later Forte), and Christine Baranski as Agnes van Rhijn. Taissa Farmiga, Blake Ritson, Kelli O'Hara, Donna Murphy, Debra Monk, and Nathan Lane also appear in prominent roles.

Production

Fellowes, best known for creating Downton Abbey, first developed the series for NBC in 2018 before it moved to HBO in 2019. Filming took place in New York City and in the historic mansions of Newport, Rhode Island. The series premiered on January 24, 2022. A second season premiered in October 2023, and a third season followed in 2025.

Reception

The series received generally positive reviews, with praise for its costumes, production design, and performances, particularly those of Coon and Baranski. It has been nominated for several Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Drama Series for its second season.
