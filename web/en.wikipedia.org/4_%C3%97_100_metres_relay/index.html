The 4 × 100 metres relay or sprint relay is an athletics track event run in lanes over one lap of the track with four runners each completing 100 metres. The first runners must begin in the same staggering as for the individual 400 metres race. Each runner hands a baton to the next within a 30-metre changeover box.

Rules and technique

The baton must be passed within the transfer zone; dropping the baton does not by itself cause disqualification if the runner who dropped it retrieves it, but a pass completed outside the zone does. Teams typically arrange their fastest sprinter on the anchor (fourth) leg, with strong starters and curve runners on the first and third legs. The "upsweep" and "downsweep" are common pass techniques.

History

The men's event entered the Olympics at Stockholm 1912 and the women's at Amsterdam 1928. Jamaica's men's team set the world record of 36.84 seconds at the London 2012 Games, anchored by Usain Bolt, while the United States women's team set their record of 40.82 at the same Games.
