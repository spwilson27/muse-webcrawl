Multi-generational household

DefinitionHousehold including two or more adult generations

A multi-generational household is a household in which two or more adult generations live under one roof, or in which grandparents and grandchildren share a home. Such arrangements are common in many Asian, African and Latin American societies and have become more common in North America and Europe.

Trends

In the United States, the share of people living in multigenerational households rose markedly after the Great Recession, driven by housing costs, student debt, delayed marriage, immigration patterns and the care needs of older relatives. Benefits cited include shared expenses and childcare; challenges include crowding and reduced privacy.
