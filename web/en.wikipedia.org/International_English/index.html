International English

International English is the concept of the English language as a global means of communication in numerous dialects, and the movement towards an international standard for the language. It is used in international business, diplomacy, science and aviation, often by speakers for whom English is not a first language.

Features

As a working lingua franca, International English tends to favour clarity over idiom, avoiding regional slang and complex phrasal verbs. Proposals for controlled forms, such as Globish, attempt to codify a simplified vocabulary for non-native speakers.

Debate

Linguists debate whether a single international standard exists or is desirable; the "World Englishes" model instead recognises multiple legitimate national and regional standards, from British and American English to Indian and Nigerian English.
