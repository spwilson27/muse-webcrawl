Charlotte, North Carolina

Charlotte is the most populous city in the U.S. state of North Carolina and the county seat of Mecklenburg County. With a population around 900,000, it is the 15th-most populous city in the United States.

Nicknamed the "Queen City", Charlotte was named for Charlotte of Mecklenburg-Strelitz, queen consort of George III. It is a major financial center — home to Bank of America and Truist Financial — and the second-largest banking center in the U.S. after New York City.

The city is home to Charlotte Douglas International Airport, one of the busiest in the world, the NASCAR Hall of Fame, the Carolina Panthers (NFL), Charlotte Hornets (NBA), and Charlotte FC (MLS).

Charlotte grew after the discovery of gold in 1799 and later as a textile and railroad hub. Its modern growth was driven by finance beginning in the 1970s and 1980s under leaders like Hugh McColl.
