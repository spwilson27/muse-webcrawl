Content management

Content management (CM) is a set of processes and technologies that supports the collection, managing, and publishing of information in any form or medium. It covers the digital content lifecycle — creation, editing, review, publishing, archiving, and retirement — and is implemented through content management systems (CMS).

Systems

A content management system supports multiple users in a collaborative environment, separating content from presentation through templates. Web content management systems such as WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla power a large share of websites; enterprise content management extends the concept to documents, records, and digital assets across an organization. "Headless" CMS products deliver content via APIs to arbitrary front ends.

Features

Key capabilities include content creation and editing tools, workflow and approval processes, version control, access control, metadata and taxonomy management, search, and multichannel publishing.
