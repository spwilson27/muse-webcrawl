Longchenpa

Longchen Rabjam
Born: 1308, Samyé, Tibet
Died: 1364 (or 1369)
School: Nyingma
Known for: Systematising Dzogchen

Longchen Rabjam Drimé Özer (1308–1364), commonly known as Longchenpa, was a Tibetan Buddhist scholar-saint of the Nyingma school and one of the most important writers in the tradition of Dzogchen, the “Great Perfection”. He systematised the Dzogchen teachings, particularly the Nyingtig (Heart Essence) tradition.

Works

His vast output of over 250 works includes the Seven Treasuries (Dzödün), among them The Treasury of the Supreme Vehicle and The Treasury of Precious Qualities, the Trilogy of Natural Freedom, the Trilogy of Natural Ease, and commentaries on the Guhyagarbha Tantra. He also linked the Vima Nyingtig and Khandro Nyingtig lineages in his own Yangti teachings.

Life

Ordained young and educated at Sangpu, he studied with the Third Karmapa Rangjung Dorje and spent long periods in retreat at places including Chimphu near Samyé, and a period of exile in present-day Bhutan, where he founded monasteries including Tharpa Ling.
