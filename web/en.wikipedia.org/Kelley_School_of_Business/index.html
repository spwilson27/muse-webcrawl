Kelley School of Business

The Kelley School of Business is the business school of Indiana University, with its flagship programmes at Indiana University Bloomington and a presence in Indianapolis. Founded in 1920 as the School of Commerce and Finance, it is consistently ranked among the leading public business schools in the United States, known particularly for marketing, entrepreneurship and its pioneering online MBA.

History

Indiana University began business instruction in 1920 with a School of Commerce and Finance, launching its MBA in the following decades. In 1997 the school was renamed for alumnus E. W. Kelley, chairman of Steak 'n Shake, following his major gift. The school's undergraduate home is Hodge Hall in Bloomington; graduate and executive programmes are also offered in Indianapolis, where Kelley partners with the IU medical and other professional schools.

Programmes

Kelley offers undergraduate majors across accounting, finance, marketing, management, information systems, business analytics, supply chain and entrepreneurship; full-time and part-time MBA programmes; specialised master's degrees; and a large doctoral programme. Its Kelley Direct online MBA, launched in 1999 as one of the first online MBAs from a major university, is routinely ranked at or near the top of online MBA rankings in the United States.

Rankings and student life

The undergraduate programme is regularly placed among the top ten to fifteen nationally, and its marketing and entrepreneurship departments are especially highly regarded. Signature features include the Integrated Core curriculum, first-year Kelley Academies in areas such as consulting and investment banking, and a large career-services operation connecting students to employers across the Midwest and nationally.
