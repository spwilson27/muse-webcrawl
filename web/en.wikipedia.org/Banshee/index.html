Banshee

A banshee (from Irish bean sí, "woman of the fairy mound") is a female spirit in Irish folklore who heralds the death of a family member, usually by wailing, shrieking or keening. Her cry, heard at night, is an omen that someone in the household will soon die.

Folklore

Traditions describe the banshee as attached to particular old Irish families, often appearing as a woman in white with long flowing hair, combing it with a silver comb. She may be young and beautiful or an old hag. The keening of the banshee is linked to the practice of professional mourning women at Irish funerals, and related figures appear in Scottish and Welsh tradition, such as the bean nighe and the cyhyraeth.

In modern culture

The banshee has become a stock figure of horror fiction, comics and games, typically depicted as a screaming ghost; her name is also used for military aircraft, vehicles and roller coasters.
