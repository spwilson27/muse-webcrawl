The Art of Computer Programming (TAOCP) is a comprehensive multi-volume monograph by computer scientist Donald Knuth analysing algorithms and their implementation. Widely regarded as the definitive work in the field, it began publication in 1968; volumes 1–4A have appeared, covering fundamental algorithms, seminumerical algorithms, sorting and searching, and combinatorial algorithms.

Details

Knuth presents examples in MIX and MMIX assembly language, famously offers a reward cheque for errors found, and developed the TeX typesetting system to produce the books.
