Neutron generator

Neutron generators are neutron source devices that contain compact particle accelerators and produce neutrons by fusing hydrogen isotopes. The fusion reactions take place when deuterium, tritium, or a mixture of the two is accelerated into a metal hydride target that also contains deuterium or tritium.

Operation

The deuterium–tritium reaction yields neutrons of about 14.1 MeV, while deuterium–deuterium fusion yields neutrons of about 2.5 MeV. Sealed-tube generators, in which the ion source, accelerator and target are enclosed in a gas-tight housing, are widely used in oil-well logging, where pulsed neutron sources help characterise underground formations.

Applications

Besides well logging, neutron generators are used in neutron activation analysis, explosives and fissile-material detection, materials research and as start-up sources and laboratory neutron sources.
