Theresa May

Theresa Mary May, Baroness May of Maidenhead (born 1 October 1956), is a British politician who served as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom from 2016 to 2019 and leader of the Conservative Party. She was Member of Parliament for Maidenhead from 1997 to 2024.

Premiership

May succeeded David Cameron after the 2016 referendum on EU membership and was tasked with delivering Brexit. She triggered Article 50 in March 2017 but lost her parliamentary majority in a snap election that year; her withdrawal agreement was rejected by the Commons three times, and she resigned in 2019, succeeded by Boris Johnson.

Earlier career

Before Downing Street she was Home Secretary from 2010 to 2016, one of the longest-serving holders of that office, noted for tough immigration policies including the "hostile environment". She entered the House of Lords in 2024.
