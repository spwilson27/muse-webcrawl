House Targaryen is a fictional noble family in George R. R. Martin's fantasy series A Song of Ice and Fire and its television adaptations Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon. The Targaryens are dragonlords descended from Old Valyria who escaped the Doom of Valyria and settled on the island of Dragonstone off the coast of Westeros.

Background

A century after the Doom, Aegon I Targaryen ("Aegon the Conqueror") invaded Westeros with his sister-wives Visenya and Rhaenys and their dragons, uniting six of the Seven Kingdoms under his rule and founding King's Landing and the Iron Throne. The dynasty ruled for nearly 300 years, surviving crises such as the civil war known as the Dance of the Dragons, which destroyed most of the family's dragons.

Downfall

The dynasty ended with the reign of the "Mad King" Aerys II, whose tyranny provoked Robert's Rebellion; Aerys was killed and the surviving children, Viserys and Daenerys Targaryen, fled into exile across the Narrow Sea. Daenerys's story — hatching three dragons and seeking to reclaim the throne — forms one of the central arcs of the novels and the television series.
