Krasnaya Polyana, Sochi


Krasnaya Polyana is a mountain resort settlement in the Western Caucasus, near Sochi, Russia.

Krasnaya Polyana (Russian: Кра́сная Поля́на, meaning "Red Glade") is an urban-type settlement in the mountains of the Western Caucasus, administratively part of the city of Sochi in Krasnodar Krai, Russia. It lies in the valley of the Mzymta River at about 500 metres above sea level, surrounded by peaks exceeding 2,000 metres, and has a population of a few thousand permanent residents.

History and tourism

The valley was inhabited by Circassian tribes before their expulsion in 1864 at the end of the Caucasian War; the settlement was later colonised by Greek and Estonian settlers (the neighbouring village of Esto-Sadok preserves the Estonian heritage). Krasnaya Polyana developed into Russia's best-known mountain and ski resort.

2014 Winter Olympics

Krasnaya Polyana served as the mountain cluster for the 2014 Winter Olympics hosted by Sochi, with venues including the Rosa Khutor Alpine Resort, the RusSki Gorki Jumping Center and the Laura Biathlon and Ski Complex hosting alpine skiing, snowboarding, ski jumping, Nordic combined, biathlon, cross-country skiing, bobsleigh, luge and skeleton events. Massive investment before the Games brought new hotels, lifts, a railway and a highway linking the resort to the coast.
