San Diego is a city in the U.S. state of California, on the Pacific coast adjacent to the Mexico–United States border. It is the county seat of San Diego County and the eighth-most populous city in the United States. Its mild year-round climate — famously around 70 °F (21 °C) — earns it the slogan "America's Finest City".

History

The Kumeyaay people inhabited the region for thousands of years before the explorer Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo claimed the bay for Spain in 1542. In 1769, Gaspar de Portolá and Junípero Serra founded the Presidio of San Diego and Mission San Diego de Alcalá, the first European settlement in what is now California. After Mexican independence the town became part of Mexico, and it passed to the United States following the Mexican–American War in 1848.

Economy

San Diego's economy is anchored by the military and defence — Naval Base San Diego and Marine Corps Air Station Miramar are major employers — as well as biotechnology, telecommunications (Qualcomm is headquartered here), tourism and cross-border trade with Tijuana. San Diego International Airport serves the region.

Attractions

Well-known attractions include the San Diego Zoo in Balboa Park, the USS Midway Museum, Old Town San Diego State Historic Park, the beaches of La Jolla and Coronado, and the Gaslamp Quarter. The city hosts the annual Comic-Con International convention.
