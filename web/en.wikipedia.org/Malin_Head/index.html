Malin Head

LocationInishowen Peninsula, County Donegal, Ireland

SignificanceMost northerly point of mainland Ireland

Malin Head (Irish: Cionn Mhálanna) is the most northerly point of mainland Ireland, located on the Inishowen Peninsula in County Donegal at latitude 55.38° N. It lies on the Atlantic coast and is the starting (or finishing) point of the Wild Atlantic Way touring route.

Features

The headland is marked by a signal tower built by the Admiralty in 1805 during the Napoleonic Wars, and by a ground-level "ÉIRE" sign formed from stones during the Second World War (the Emergency) to warn aircraft that they were over neutral Ireland; the restored sign at Malin Head is one of many along the coast. The area is known for its dramatic cliffs, sea stacks such as Hell's Hole, birdwatching and views toward Inishtrahull Island and, on clear days, Scotland. The weather station at Malin Head is one of the stations named in the BBC Shipping Forecast (sea area Malin).

In culture

Scenes for the film Star Wars: The Last Jedi (2017) were filmed at Malin Head, where a replica of the Millennium Falcon was constructed on the headland. The waters off Malin Head contain numerous shipwrecks, including vessels lost in both world wars.
