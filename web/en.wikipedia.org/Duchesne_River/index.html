Duchesne River

The Duchesne River is a tributary of the Green River in northeastern Utah in the United States. It drains an area of the Uinta Basin south of the Uinta Mountains and joins the Green River near Ouray, Utah.

Geography

The river is formed by the confluence of several forks in the Uinta Mountains and flows generally southeast through Duchesne County and Uintah County. Its principal tributaries include the Strawberry River, Currant Creek, and the Lake Fork River. The river passes near the towns of Duchesne, Myton, and Randlett.

History and use

The river is named for the Duchesne Fork and ultimately for French trappers and the Fort Duchesne area. Water from the Duchesne system is important for irrigation in the Uinta Basin and is partly managed through projects such as the Central Utah Project and Starvation Reservoir on the Strawberry River.
