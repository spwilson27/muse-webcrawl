The International (Dota 2)

[Placeholder image: The_International_(Dota_2).png]

The International is an annual esports world championship tournament for the video game Dota 2, organised by Valve. First held in 2011 at Gamescom in Cologne, it became famous for record-breaking prize pools funded partly by crowdfunding through in-game compendium sales; The International 2021 (TI10) had a prize pool of over US$40 million, then the largest in esports.

Format

Teams qualify through the Dota Pro Circuit and regional qualifiers, playing a group stage followed by a double-elimination main event. Winners receive the Aegis of Champions trophy.
