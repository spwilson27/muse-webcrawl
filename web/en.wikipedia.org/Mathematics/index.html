Mathematics is a field of study that discovers and organises methods, theories and theorems about abstract objects and structures, including numbers, formulas, shapes and the ways they change. There is no general consensus among mathematicians about a single definition of their subject, but mathematics is generally divided into pure mathematics, studied for its own sake, and applied mathematics, used to solve problems in science, engineering, business and everyday life.

History

Mathematical activity began with counting, calculation and measurement in ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, India, China and Greece. Greek mathematicians such as Euclid developed rigorous deductive proof; algebra and decimal numerals were systematised in the Islamic world and India; calculus was created in the 17th century by Newton and Leibniz. The 19th and 20th centuries saw a vast expansion — non-Euclidean geometry, set theory, abstract algebra, mathematical logic and computer science.

Main areas

Number theory studies integers and their properties. Algebra generalises arithmetic through symbols and structures such as groups, rings and fields. Geometry concerns shapes, sizes and spaces, including topology. Calculus and analysis study continuous change, limits, derivatives and integrals. Probability and statistics quantify uncertainty and analyse data. Discrete mathematics and combinatorics underpin computing.

Proof and practice

Mathematics proceeds by proof: starting from axioms, results are deduced according to the rules of logic, and a statement that is proved is called a theorem. Famous results include Pythagoras' theorem, Euler's identity and the classification of finite simple groups; celebrated open or formerly open problems include Fermat's Last Theorem (proved 1994) and the Millennium Prize Problems.

Applications and recognition

Mathematics is indispensable to physics, engineering, economics, medicine, cryptography and artificial intelligence. Major honours include the Fields Medal and the Abel Prize. UNESCO proclaimed 14 March the International Day of Mathematics.
