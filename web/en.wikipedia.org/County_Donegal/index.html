County Donegal

County Donegal (Irish: Contae Dhún na nGall) is a county of Ireland in Ulster, in the north-west. It is named after the town of Donegal. The county town is Lifford and the largest town is Letterkenny. Population is about 167,000 (2022).

Geography

Donegal has a rugged Atlantic coastline, the Blue Stack and Derryveagh Mountains, Mount Errigal, Slieve League cliffs, and Glenveagh National Park. Much of the west is Gaeltacht (Irish-speaking). Despite belonging historically to Ulster, it is in the Republic of Ireland and largely isolated from the rest of the Republic by Northern Ireland.
