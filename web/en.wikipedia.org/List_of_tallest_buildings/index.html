List of tallest buildings

The Burj Khalifa in Dubai has been the world's tallest building since 2010.

This is a list of the tallest buildings in the world, ranked by architectural height as measured by the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH). A building differs from a tower in that it must be a habitable structure.

History

The title of world's tallest building has passed from the Empire State Building (381 m, 1931) through the World Trade Center and the Sears (Willis) Tower to Taipei 101, and since 2010 to the Burj Khalifa. Historically, structures such as the Great Pyramid of Giza and Lincoln Cathedral held the record for centuries before the skyscraper era.
