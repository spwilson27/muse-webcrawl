President of the Chamber of Deputies (Italy)

The President of the Chamber of Deputies (Italian: Presidente della Camera dei deputati) is the presiding officer of the lower house of the Italian Parliament. The holder ranks third in the Italian order of precedence, after the President of the Republic and the President of the Senate.

Role

The President chairs debates in the Chamber of Deputies, ensures observance of its rules, and represents the Chamber externally. The officeholder is expected to act impartially, moderating proceedings and guaranteeing the rights of the opposition.

Election

The President is elected by the deputies at the start of each legislature, requiring a two-thirds majority in the first ballots and an absolute majority thereafter. Recent holders have included Laura Boldrini, Roberto Fico, and Lorenzo Fontana, elected in 2022.
