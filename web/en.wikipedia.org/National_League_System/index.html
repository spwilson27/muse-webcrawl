The National League System (NLS) is the structure of semi-professional and amateur association football leagues in England administered by the Football Association, comprising the tiers of the English football league system immediately below the fully professional English Football League.

Structure

The system is organized into "steps." Step 1 is the National League; Step 2 comprises the National League North and National League South. Steps 3 and 4 are occupied by the Premier and lower divisions of the Northern Premier League, Southern League and Isthmian League, while Steps 5 and 6 consist of numerous regional leagues such as the Combined Counties, Eastern Counties and Hellenic leagues. Promotion and relegation link the steps, subject to clubs meeting ground grading and facilities criteria.

History

The FA formally structured the pyramid in the late 1970s and 1980s, replacing the patchwork of regional leagues with a coherent system beneath the Alliance Premier League (later the Football Conference and now the National League). Periodic restructurings, including the creation of Conference North and South in 2004 and a fourth Step 3 league in 2018, refined regional coverage.
