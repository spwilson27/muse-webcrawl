List of airports by IATA code

IATA codes appear on baggage tags worldwide.

This is a list of airports by IATA code. An IATA airport code, also known as an IATA location identifier, IATA station code, or simply a location identifier, is a three-letter geocode designating many airports and metropolitan areas around the world, defined by the International Air Transport Association (IATA). The characters prominently displayed on baggage tags attached at airport check-in desks are an example of a way in which these codes are used.

Organization

Because there are thousands of airports with IATA codes, the list is divided into subpages by the first letter of the code, from A to Z. Examples include:

  • ATL — Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport, United States
  • LHR — London Heathrow Airport, United Kingdom
  • HND — Haneda Airport, Tokyo, Japan
  • CDG — Charles de Gaulle Airport, Paris, France
  • DXB — Dubai International Airport, United Arab Emirates
  • SYD — Sydney Airport, Australia
  • JFK — John F. Kennedy International Airport, New York, United States
  • PEK — Beijing Capital International Airport, China
