Ladder

A ladder is a vertical or inclined set of rungs or steps used for climbing up or down. Rigid ladders are usually portable and lean against a surface or stand free, while flexible ladders, such as rope or Jacob's ladders, hang from a single point.

Common types include step ladders (self-supporting A-frame ladders), extension ladders (two or more sliding sections), step stools, orchard and library ladders, fixed vertical ladders, and ships' ladders. Ladders are typically made of wood, aluminium, steel or fibreglass — the latter preferred around electricity for its insulating properties. Falls from ladders are a significant cause of occupational injury, and safety standards prescribe inspection, correct angle (about 75 degrees for leaning ladders) and three points of contact while climbing.
