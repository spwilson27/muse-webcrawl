James Bond music

The music of the James Bond films — the long-running British spy film series based on the novels of Ian Fleming — is among the most recognisable in cinema. It is defined above all by the "James Bond Theme," the title songs performed by leading popular artists, and the scores of composers such as John Barry, David Arnold and Hans Zimmer.

The James Bond Theme

The "James Bond Theme" was written by Monty Norman for the first Bond film, Dr. No (1962), and arranged and performed by John Barry and his orchestra. Its twangy electric guitar riff — played by Vic Flick — driving brass and swinging rhythm became the musical signature of the character, heard in the gun barrel sequence that opens nearly every film. The authorship was long disputed; a 2001 court case upheld Norman as the composer, while Barry's arrangement is credited with defining its sound.

John Barry

John Barry scored eleven Bond films between 1963 and 1987, beginning with From Russia with Love. Barry established the series' lush orchestral style, blending jazz, brass and strings, and co-wrote many of the famous title songs, including "Goldfinger" (performed by Shirley Bassey), "Thunderball" (Tom Jones), "You Only Live Twice" (Nancy Sinatra), "Diamonds Are Forever" (Bassey) and "Moonraker." His score for On Her Majesty's Secret Service (1969) featured the instrumental theme "We Have All the Time in the World," sung by Louis Armstrong.

Title songs

Nearly every Bond film since Goldfinger has featured a title song performed over the opening credits, usually by a major recording artist. Notable examples include "Live and Let Die" by Paul McCartney and Wings (1973), "Nobody Does It Better" by Carly Simon (1977), "A View to a Kill" by Duran Duran (1985) — the only Bond theme to reach number one on the US Billboard Hot 100 — "GoldenEye" by Tina Turner (1995), "Skyfall" by Adele (2012) and "Writing's on the Wall" by Sam Smith (2015), both of which won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, and "No Time to Die" by Billie Eilish (2021), which also won the Oscar.

Later composers

After Barry, composers included George Martin, Marvin Hamlisch, Bill Conti, Michael Kamen and Éric Serra. David Arnold scored five films from Tomorrow Never Dies (1997) to Quantum of Solace (2008), modernising Barry's style with electronic elements. Thomas Newman scored Skyfall and Spectre, and Hans Zimmer composed the score for No Time to Die (2021).
