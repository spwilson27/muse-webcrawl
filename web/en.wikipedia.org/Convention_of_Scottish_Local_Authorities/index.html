Convention of Scottish Local Authorities

The Convention of Scottish Local Authorities (COSLA) is the national association of councils in Scotland and acts as the employers' association for the 32 local authorities in Scotland. It was formed in 1975 following the reorganisation of Scottish local government under the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973.

Formation1975
HeadquartersEdinburgh, Scotland
Membership32 Scottish local authorities

Role

COSLA represents the interests of Scottish local government to the Scottish Government, the UK Government, and European institutions. It negotiates on behalf of councils on funding, pay and conditions for local government staff, and policy areas such as education, social care, housing, and the environment.

Structure

The organisation is governed by a convention of elected councillors drawn from its member councils, and its political leadership includes a president and vice-president elected from among Scotland's councillors. Policy boards cover major service areas, supported by a staff team based at Verity House in Edinburgh. See also local government in Scotland.
