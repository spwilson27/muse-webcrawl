Starmania
Music: Michel Berger
Lyrics: Luc Plamondon
Premiere: April 10, 1979, Palais des congrès de Paris
Productions: 1979 Paris; 1988 Paris revival; 1993 Paris (Tycoon); multiple tours

Starmania is a Canadian-French cyberpunk rock opera with music by Michel Berger and lyrics by Luc Plamondon. First conceived in 1976, the show premiered in Paris in 1979 at the Palais des congrès. It became one of the most famous French-language musicals of all time and has been performed in several languages and to millions of spectators.

Synopsis

Set in a futuristic city called Monopolis, ruled by the tyrannical businessman Zéro Janvier, the story follows several characters whose lives intersect amid a contest for control of the city: the rebel group led by Johnny Rockfort, the cafe waitress Cristal, the fading erotic star Stella Spotlight, and the unemployed worker Ziggy. Themes include media manipulation, terrorism, and the hunger for fame.

Music

The original concept album, released in 1978, featured performers including Daniel Balavoine, France Gall, Diane Dufresne, and Nanette Workman. Well-known songs include "Le monde est stone", "Quand on arrive en ville", "Besoin d'amour", "SOS d'un terrien en détresse", and "La chanson de Ziggy".

Later versions

An English-language version titled Tycoon, with lyrics by Tim Rice, was produced in 1993. A new large-scale staging directed by Thomas Jolly premiered in Paris in 2022 and toured internationally.

Legacy

Starmania is considered a landmark of French musical theatre, credited with bringing the rock opera format to the francophone stage and influencing later productions such as Notre-Dame de Paris.
