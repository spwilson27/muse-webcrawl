Nippon Telegraph and Telephone Corporation (日本電信電話株式会社, Nippon Denshin Denwa Kabushiki-gaisha), commonly known as NTT, is a Japanese telecommunications holding company headquartered in Tokyo. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world by revenue.

History

NTT was established in 1952 as a government-owned public corporation responsible for Japan's domestic telephone and telegraph services. It was privatized in 1985, though the Japanese government retained a significant shareholding, and NTT shares were listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange in 1987 in what was then one of the largest share offerings in history. In 1999 the group was reorganized under a holding-company structure, with regional operators NTT East and NTT West, long-distance and international carrier NTT Communications, and mobile operator NTT Docomo among its principal subsidiaries.

Operations

The NTT Group provides fixed-line and mobile telecommunications, data communications, system integration, and information technology services. Its research arm, NTT Laboratories, is known for work in photonics, network science, and basic research. Subsidiaries include NTT Data, a major global IT services provider.
