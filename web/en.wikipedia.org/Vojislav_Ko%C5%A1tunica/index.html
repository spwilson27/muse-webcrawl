Vojislav Koštunica

Vojislav Koštunica (born 24 March 1944) is a Serbian former politician who served as the last President of the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (2000–2003) and twice as Prime Minister of Serbia (2004–2007 and 2007–2008). A constitutional lawyer by training, he founded the Democratic Party of Serbia (DSS) in 1992.

Presidency

Koštunica defeated Slobodan Milošević in the Yugoslav presidential election of September 2000; after Milošević initially refused to concede, mass protests known as the Bulldozer Revolution forced his resignation on 5 October 2000. Koštunica was known for a legalistic, moderate nationalist stance, criticising both Milošević's authoritarianism and the NATO bombing of Yugoslavia in 1999.

Premiership

As prime minister he headed minority and coalition governments. He resigned in March 2008 over disagreements within the coalition regarding Kosovo's declaration of independence and Serbia's EU integration path. He led the DSS until 2014.
