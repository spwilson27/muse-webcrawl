The 2016 World Series was the championship series of Major League Baseball's 2016 season, contested between the National League champion Chicago Cubs and the American League champion Cleveland Indians. Both franchises carried famously long championship droughts: the Cubs had not won a World Series since 1908, and Cleveland since 1948.

Series

Cleveland took a 3–1 series lead, but Chicago rallied to force a deciding Game 7 at Progressive Field on 2 November. The Cubs led 6–3 late, but Cleveland tied the game in the eighth inning, capped by Rajai Davis's two-run home run. After a brief rain delay before the tenth inning, the Cubs scored twice in the top of the tenth; Cleveland managed one run in reply, and the game ended with Kris Bryant throwing to Anthony Rizzo for the final out.

Significance

The Cubs' 8–7 victory ended their 108-year title drought, then the longest in North American professional sports. Game 7 is widely regarded as one of the greatest baseball games ever played, and the championship parade in Chicago drew an estimated five million people.
