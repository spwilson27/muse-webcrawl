Faker (gamer)

Real name: Lee Sang-hyeok
Born: 7 May 1996, Seoul, South Korea
Game: League of Legends
Role: Mid laner
Team: T1

Lee Sang-hyeok (born 7 May 1996), better known as Faker, is a South Korean professional League of Legends player and mid laner for T1. Widely regarded as the greatest League of Legends player of all time, he has spent his entire career with the same organization (formerly SK Telecom T1) since debuting in 2013.

Career

Faker won the World Championship in his debut year, 2013, and again in 2015 and 2016 — the first back-to-back titles in the game's history. After further finals appearances, he led T1 to consecutive world titles again in 2023 and 2024, making him the only player to win five World Championships. He has also won two Mid-Season Invitationals and ten LCK domestic titles, and was part of South Korea's gold-medal team at the 2022 Asian Games. Nicknamed the "Unkillable Demon King", he is famed for his mechanical skill, longevity and loyalty to one team; in 2024 he became the first inductee into the League of Legends Hall of Legends.
