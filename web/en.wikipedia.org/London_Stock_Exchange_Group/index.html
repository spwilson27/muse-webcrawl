Type: Public limited company
Traded as: LSE: LSEG
Industry: Financial services
Founded: 1801 (exchange); 2007 (group)
Headquarters: 10 Paternoster Square, London

London Stock Exchange Group plc, also known as LSEG, is a United Kingdom-based stock exchange and financial information company headquartered in the City of London. It owns the London Stock Exchange, Refinitiv, LSEG Technology, FTSE Russell, and a majority stake in the clearing house LCH.

History

The London Stock Exchange traces its origins to 1801 and to coffee houses of the 17th century. The modern group formed as the exchange demutualised and listed. It acquired Borsa Italiana in 2007 (later sold to Euronext in 2021), the index business that became FTSE Russell, and in 2021 completed the $27 billion acquisition of Refinitiv, transforming LSEG into a major financial data provider rivalling Bloomberg.

Operations

LSEG operates across data and analytics, capital markets, and post-trade services. Its partnership with Microsoft, announced in 2022, includes cloud migration to Azure and Microsoft's purchase of a stake in the group.
