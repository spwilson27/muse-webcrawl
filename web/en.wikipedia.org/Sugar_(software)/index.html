Sugar (software)

Sugar is a free and open-source desktop environment designed for interactive learning by children. It was originally developed as part of the One Laptop per Child (OLPC) project, and has been developed by Sugar Labs since 2008. Unlike most desktop environments, Sugar does not use the desktop metaphor with files and folders; instead it uses the concept of "activities" and a journal that automatically saves the learner's work.

Design principles

Sugar was designed around three principles: everyone is a teacher and a learner; humans should be engaged in authentic and joyful learning; and learning should be collaborative. The interface presents a simple home view with a ring or grid of activity icons around a central figure representing the user, known as the "XO" icon.

Activities

In Sugar, applications are called activities. Core activities include a web browser, a word processor (Write), a drawing program (Paint), a music sequencer (TamTam), a programming environment based on Logo called Turtle Art, and Etoys, based on Squeak. Activities can be shared with other users over a mesh network, allowing children to collaborate on a document or game in real time.

The Journal

The Journal is Sugar's equivalent of a file manager. Rather than requiring children to organize files into folders, it automatically records everything the user does, presenting entries in chronological order. Learners can search, resume, and share journal entries.

Sugar Labs

In May 2008, Walter Bender left OLPC and founded Sugar Labs, an independent non-profit organization, to continue development of Sugar as a platform available for any computer, not only the OLPC XO laptop. Sugar is written largely in Python and runs on Linux.
