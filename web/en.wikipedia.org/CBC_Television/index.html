CBC Television (also known simply as CBC) is a Canadian English-language broadcast television network owned by the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation, the national public broadcaster. It is the oldest television network in Canada, having begun broadcasting in 1952 from Montreal and Toronto.

Programming

The network is known for news and current affairs programming such as The National, comedies and dramas including Schitt's Creek, Kim's Convenience and Anne with an E, and extensive sports coverage, historically including Hockey Night in Canada. Its French-language counterpart is Ici Radio-Canada Télé.

Distribution

CBC Television operates a network of owned-and-operated stations in major cities and reaches nearly all Canadian households over the air, by cable and satellite, and via streaming on CBC Gem.
