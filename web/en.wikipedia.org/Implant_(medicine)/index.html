Implant (medicine)

Implant (medicine)
TypeMedical device
PurposeReplace, support, or enhance a biological structure
ExamplesPacemakers, dental implants, joint prostheses, cochlear implants

A medical implant is a medical device manufactured to replace a missing biological structure, support a damaged biological structure, or enhance an existing biological structure. Medical implants are human-made devices, in contrast to a transplant, which is a transplanted biomedical tissue.

Types

Implants range from passive devices such as artificial joints, dental implants, and intraocular lenses to active electronics such as cardiac pacemakers, implantable defibrillators, and cochlear implants. Some implants deliver medication, such as contraceptive implants and drug-eluting stents.

Materials and risks

Implants are made from biocompatible metals, ceramics, and polymers. Risks include infection, immune rejection of the device, mechanical failure, and—for devices placed near the surface—the body's fibrous encapsulation of the implant.
