Super Bowl LV halftime show

The Super Bowl LV halftime show, officially known as the Pepsi Super Bowl LV Halftime Show, took place on February 7, 2021, at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida, as part of Super Bowl LV. It was headlined by Canadian singer the Weeknd and was the first Super Bowl halftime show held under COVID-19 pandemic restrictions.

Performance

The Weeknd performed a medley of his hit songs, including "Starboy", "The Hills", "Can't Feel My Face", "I Feel It Coming", "Save Your Tears", "Earned It", and concluded with "Blinding Lights" on the field surrounded by dancers wearing bandaged-face costumes echoing his After Hours persona. He reportedly contributed $7 million of his own money to the production, which made extensive use of a cityscape set and a mirrored funhouse-style chamber.

Reception

The show drew about 96.4 million television viewers as part of the broadcast. Critics praised the performance's spectacle given the constraints of a partly empty stadium, and images of the Weeknd in the gold-lit corridor during "Can't Feel My Face" became a widely circulated internet meme. The show was produced by Roc Nation and Jesse Collins.
