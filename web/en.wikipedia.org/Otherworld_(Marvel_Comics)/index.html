Otherworld (Marvel Comics)

Otherworld is a fictional dimension in Marvel Comics, a realm of magic connected to the British Isles and the legendary Avalon. It is the home of the Captain Britain Corps and a central setting in the Excalibur series and stories featuring the mutant nation of Krakoa.

Description

Otherworld is governed from the Starlight Citadel and is divided into provinces ruled by figures including the Omniversal Majestrix Opal Luna Saturnyne. It is associated with King Arthur, Merlyn, and Roma, and serves as a nexus to many alternate Earths. During the X of Swords storyline (2020), Otherworld's Sevalith and other kingdoms became a battleground for Krakoa's champions.

Role in the multiverse

Because every alternate reality's Britain is linked to Otherworld, its guardians—the Captain Britain Corps—monitor the Marvel multiverse, and threats to the realm have omniversal consequences.
