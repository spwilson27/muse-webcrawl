Draugr

A draugr or draug (Old Norse: draugr, plural draugar) is an undead creature from Norse mythology and Icelandic folklore. Draugar are said to inhabit graves, often those of men buried with treasure, and to guard the wealth interred with them. The word is related to the Old Norse draugr meaning a ghost or revenant.

Characteristics

In the Icelandic sagas, draugar are corporeal beings — corpses that have retained or regained a physical presence, often described as swollen, enormously heavy, and blue-black in colour like a drowned body. They possess superhuman strength, can increase their size at will, and are credited with abilities such as shape-shifting, entering dreams, and killing livestock and men by crushing them or driving them mad. Unlike ethereal ghosts, a draugr could be fought and destroyed physically; saga heroes such as Grettir Ásmundarson famously wrestle and behead draugar (notably the mound-dweller Kárr the Old and the shepherd Glámr, whose curse haunts Grettir afterwards).

In modern culture

Draugar appear in modern fantasy and games, including the video game The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, where they are undead warriors in ancient Nordic tombs, and in tabletop role-playing games. The concept also influenced Tolkien's barrow-wights.
