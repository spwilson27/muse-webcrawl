2009 Formula One World Championship

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The 2009 FIA Formula One World Championship was the 60th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 17 Grands Prix, beginning with the Australian Grand Prix on 29 March and ending with the inaugural Abu Dhabi Grand Prix on 1 November. Jenson Button won the Drivers' Championship driving for the new Brawn GP team, which also won the Constructors' Championship in its only season of existence.

Background

The season was shaped by major regulation changes: aerodynamic rules were overhauled, slick tyres returned for the first time since 1997, and the Kinetic Energy Recovery System (KERS) was introduced. In December 2008 Honda announced its withdrawal from the sport; its team was bought by team principal Ross Brawn and rebranded Brawn GP, retaining drivers Jenson Button and Rubens Barrichello.

Season summary

Brawn GP exploited a loophole in the new aerodynamic rules with a "double diffuser" design, and Button won six of the first seven races. Red Bull Racing, with Sebastian Vettel and Mark Webber, emerged as the main challenger in the second half of the season. Button secured the Drivers' title with fifth place at the Brazilian Grand Prix, while Brawn clinched the Constructors' Championship at the same race. Vettel finished runner-up in the championship, 11 points behind Button.

Aftermath

After the season, Mercedes-Benz bought a controlling stake in Brawn GP and rebranded it as Mercedes GP for 2010, with Button moving to McLaren. The 2009 season is remembered as one of the great underdog stories in Formula One history.
