Star of Bethlehem

The Star of Bethlehem, also called the Christmas Star, is the star that in the Gospel of Matthew revealed the birth of Jesus to the Magi ("wise men") and led them to Bethlehem.

Biblical account

In Matthew 2, the Magi from the East see the star, travel to Jerusalem to ask King Herod about the newborn "king of the Jews", and follow the star to the house where the child Jesus is, offering gifts of gold, frankincense and myrrh.

Explanations

Scholars and astronomers have proposed explanations including a conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn, a comet, a nova, or purely theological symbolism. The star is a central motif of Christmas imagery and Nativity plays.
