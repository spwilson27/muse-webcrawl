Film score

A film score is original music written specifically to accompany a film, typically underscoring dialogue and action to shape emotion and narrative. It is usually composed after editing, spotted with the director, and recorded by orchestra, ensemble or electronic means.

Notable composers include Max Steiner, Bernard Herrmann, John Williams, Ennio Morricone and Hans Zimmer. A score is distinct from a soundtrack album, which may also include songs and dialogue.
