Coat

A coat is a long outer garment worn outdoors, extending below the hips and generally to the knees or lower, open down the front and fastened with buttons, toggles or a zip, and usually fitted with long sleeves. Coats are worn for warmth or protection from the weather and as fashion garments.

Types

  • Overcoat — a heavy coat worn over other clothing, including the topcoat, greatcoat, trench coat and pea coat.
  • Frock coat and morning coat — formal 19th- and early-20th-century styles.
  • Raincoat — a waterproof or water-resistant coat, such as the mackintosh.
  • Tailcoat — a formal coat with a cut-away front and long tails.

The word derives from Old French cote. Historically, coats evolved from medieval tunics and doublets; the modern tailored coat developed in the 17th–19th centuries alongside modern tailoring.
