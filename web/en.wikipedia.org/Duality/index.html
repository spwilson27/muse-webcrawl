Duality

Duality may refer to a number of concepts in mathematics, physics, philosophy and other fields in which two things are related as complementary or opposing counterparts of a single whole.

Mathematics

In mathematics, duality is a pervasive phenomenon: dual vector spaces pair vectors with linear functionals; in projective geometry, points and lines can be interchanged; in category theory every concept has a dual obtained by reversing arrows; and in optimisation, primal and dual linear programs bound one another's values.

Physics

In physics, dualities relate apparently different theories or descriptions, such as wave–particle duality in quantum mechanics, electric–magnetic duality in electromagnetism, and the gauge/gravity (AdS/CFT) duality in string theory.

Philosophy and religion

Dualism in philosophy of mind holds that mental and physical phenomena are distinct kinds of substance or property. Religious and metaphysical traditions worldwide employ dualities such as yin and yang, light and darkness, and good and evil as organising principles.
