The British Grand Prix is a Formula One motor race held annually in the United Kingdom. Together with the Italian Grand Prix, it is the oldest continuously staged Formula One Grand Prix, having been held as the first ever World Championship race at Silverstone in 1950.

History

The race has also been held at Aintree and Brands Hatch. Silverstone, a former RAF airfield in Northamptonshire, has hosted it continuously since 1987. Lewis Hamilton holds the record for most victories, winning his home race nine times.
