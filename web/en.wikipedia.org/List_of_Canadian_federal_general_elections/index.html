List of Canadian federal general elections

This is a list of federal general elections in Canada since Confederation in 1867. Elections are held to choose members of the House of Commons; the party winning the most seats normally forms government.

The 1867 election was the first, held under the British North America Act. Notable elections include the 1917 conscription election, the 1988 "free trade" election, and the 1993 election that reduced the Progressive Conservatives to two seats.
