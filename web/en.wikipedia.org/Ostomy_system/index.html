Ostomy system

An ostomy system (or pouching system) is a medical device worn by a person with a stoma — a surgically created opening that diverts urine or faeces out of the body after colostomy, ileostomy, or urostomy surgery. The system collects waste in a discreet, odour-resistant pouch attached to the abdomen.

Components

A system consists of a skin barrier (wafer or flange) that adheres to the skin around the stoma and protects it from effluent, and a detachable or integrated collection pouch. In a two-piece system the pouch can be changed without removing the barrier; one-piece systems combine both. Pouches may be drainable or closed-ended.

Use

Ostomy systems are used following surgery for colorectal cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, diverticulitis, or trauma. Proper fit and skin care are essential to prevent leakage and peristomal skin complications.
