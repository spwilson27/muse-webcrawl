Wally the Green Monster

Wally the Green Monster is the official mascot for the Boston Red Sox, a Major League Baseball team based in Boston, Massachusetts. He is a big furry green monster who wears a Red Sox jersey. Wally's name is derived from Fenway Park's left field wall, the Green Monster, off of which his character lives.

History

According to the Red Sox promotions department, Wally was a huge Red Sox fan who had decided in 1947 to move inside the left field wall of Fenway Park, since it "matched his color," and lived there quietly for 50 years. In 1997 he outed himself to the public, appearing for the first time during that year's home opener. He was an instant hit with children, though initially disliked by some older fans.

Character

Wally is known for his elaborate pranks, including driving around Fenway on a four-wheeler and engaging in races and antics with visiting mascots. He is frequently accompanied by other characters, and in 2016 the Red Sox introduced his sister, Tessie the Green Monster, named after the Dropkick Murphys song "Tessie," and his parents. Wally wears the number 97, commemorating the year he debuted.
