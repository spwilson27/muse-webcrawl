The Alaska Permanent Fund is a constitutionally established sovereign wealth fund managed by the state-owned Alaska Permanent Fund Corporation. Alaskan voters approved a constitutional amendment in 1976 dedicating at least 25 percent of state mineral royalties to the fund.

Permanent Fund Dividend

Since 1982, the fund has paid an annual Permanent Fund Dividend (PFD) to eligible Alaska residents, a policy championed by Governor Jay Hammond. Dividend amounts vary with fund earnings and legislative decisions; the size of the dividend has become a central issue in Alaska politics.

Structure and role

The fund is invested in a diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds, real estate, and private equity. Its earnings reserve account has become increasingly important to the state budget, funding a large share of Alaska's government services in the absence of a state income or sales tax.
