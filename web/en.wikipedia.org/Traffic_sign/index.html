Traffic sign

A traffic sign or road sign is a sign erected at the side of or above roads to give instructions or provide information to road users. The earliest signs were simple milestones; modern signs use standardised pictograms so that they can be understood across language barriers.

Categories

Signs are generally grouped as regulatory signs (such as speed limits and prohibitions), warning signs (alerting drivers to hazards ahead) and guide or information signs (directions, distances and services). The 1968 Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals harmonised systems across much of the world, though the United States follows its own MUTCD standard, which relies more on text.

Design

Sign design follows rules on shape, colour and retroreflectivity: octagons for stop, inverted triangles for yield or give way, and red borders for prohibitions in most European-style systems. Legibility research informs lettering, spacing and sign placement.
