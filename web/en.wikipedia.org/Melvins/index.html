The Melvins (sometimes written as "the Melvins") are an American rock band formed in 1983 in Montesano, Washington. Their early work, blending the slow tempos of Black Sabbath with the aggression of hardcore punk, was a crucial influence on the development of grunge, sludge metal, and drone music.

History

Singer and guitarist Buzz Osborne and drummer Dale Crover have been the band's constant core through dozens of lineups and more than thirty studio albums. After early releases on C/Z Records and Boner Records, the Melvins signed to Atlantic Records, releasing Houdini (1993), Stoner Witch (1994), and Stag (1996).

Influence

The Melvins were close associates of Kurt Cobain, who auditioned for the band and championed them; their down-tuned, dirge-like riffs shaped the sound of Nirvana and the wider Pacific Northwest scene, as well as later sludge and stoner rock acts. Osborne and Crover have maintained a famously prolific output, including experiments with two drummers and numerous collaborations.
