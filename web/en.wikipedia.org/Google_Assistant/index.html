Google Assistant

Developer: Google

Initial release: May 18, 2016

Written in: C++

Operating system: Android, ChromeOS, iOS, KaiOS, Linux

Type: Virtual assistant

Google Assistant is a virtual assistant software application developed by Google that is primarily available on mobile and home automation devices. Based on artificial intelligence, Google Assistant can engage in two-way conversations, unlike the company's previous virtual assistant, Google Now.

Google Assistant debuted in May 2016 as part of Google's messaging app Allo, and its voice-activated speaker Google Home. After a period of exclusivity on the Google Pixel smartphones, it was deployed on other Android devices in February 2017, including third-party smartphones and Android Wear; it was released as an iOS app in May 2017.

Features

Users primarily interact with Google Assistant through natural voice, though keyboard input is also supported. Assistant can search the Internet, schedule events and alarms, adjust hardware settings on the user's device, and show information from the user's Google account. Google has also announced that the Assistant will be able to identify objects and gather visual information through the device's camera, support purchasing products and sending money, and identify songs.

History

At the Consumer Electronics Show in January 2018, the first Assistant-powered smart displays were announced, the first of which was released in July 2018. In 2020, Google Assistant was already available on more than 1 billion devices. In 2023–2024, Google began transitioning Assistant features toward its Gemini chatbot, announcing that Gemini would replace Assistant on Android devices.
