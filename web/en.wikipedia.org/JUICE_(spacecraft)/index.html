JUICE (spacecraft)

JUICE, the Jupiter Icy Moons Explorer, is a European Space Agency spacecraft launched on 14 April 2023 on an Ariane 5 from Kourou, bound for Jupiter. It is scheduled to arrive in July 2031.

Mission

JUICE will study Jupiter and its icy moons Ganymede, Europa and Callisto, which are thought to harbour subsurface oceans. After flybys, it will enter orbit around Ganymede in the mid-2030s, the first spacecraft to orbit a moon other than Earth's.

Trajectory

The journey uses gravity assists from Earth, Venus and the Moon, including a lunar–Earth flyby in August 2024.

Instruments

Instruments include cameras (JANUS), spectrometers, a radar sounder (RIME), magnetometer and laser altimeter (GALA).
