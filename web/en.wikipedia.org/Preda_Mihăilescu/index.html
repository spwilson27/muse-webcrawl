Preda Mihăilescu

Preda V. Mihăilescu (born 23 May 1955) is a Romanian mathematician, best known for his proof of Catalan's conjecture, now called Mihăilescu's theorem. Born in Bucharest into a family of intellectuals, he left Romania as a teenager and settled in Switzerland, where he studied mathematics and computer science at ETH Zurich, receiving his doctorate in 1997.

Mihăilescu's theorem

Catalan's conjecture, posed by Eugène Charles Catalan in 1844, states that the only solution in the natural numbers of xa − yb = 1, for x, a, y, b > 1, is 32 − 23 = 1. Mihăilescu proved the conjecture in 2002 using deep results on cyclotomic fields, building on his earlier work bounding the problem during his doctoral research. The proof was published in the Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik in 2004, and the result is now known as Mihăilescu's theorem.

Career

After positions at the University of Paderborn in Germany, Mihăilescu became a professor at the University of Göttingen in 2005. His research interests include number theory, algebra and cryptography. He was elected a member of the Göttingen Academy of Sciences and Humanities in 2013.
