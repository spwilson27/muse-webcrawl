List of treaties

This list of treaties collects some of the most significant international agreements in history, from ancient peace settlements to modern multilateral conventions. A treaty is a formal, legally binding agreement between sovereign states or international organisations, governed by international law.

Ancient and medieval treaties

  • Treaty of Kadesh (c. 1259 BC) — peace treaty between Egypt under Ramesses II and the Hittite Empire; one of the earliest surviving peace treaties.
  • Treaty of Verdun (843) — divided the Carolingian Empire among the sons of Louis the Pious.
  • Treaty of Tordesillas (1494) — divided the newly encountered lands outside Europe between Spain and Portugal.

Early modern treaties

  • Peace of Westphalia (1648) — ended the Thirty Years' War and is associated with the modern concept of state sovereignty.
  • Treaty of Utrecht (1713) — ended the War of the Spanish Succession.
  • Treaty of Paris (1783) — recognised the independence of the United States.
  • Congress of Vienna (1815) — redrew the map of Europe after the Napoleonic Wars.

20th century

  • Treaty of Versailles (1919) — formally ended the First World War with Germany.
  • United Nations Charter (1945) — founding treaty of the United Nations.
  • Treaty of Rome (1957) — established the European Economic Community.
  • Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (1968) — limits the spread of nuclear weapons.
  • Camp David Accords (1978) and the Egypt–Israel peace treaty (1979).

21st century

  • Paris Agreement (2015) — international treaty on climate change mitigation.
  • Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (2017) — adopted at the United Nations.
