Oscar Jack Piastri (born 6 April 2001) is an Australian racing driver who competes in Formula One for McLaren. He is one of only a handful of drivers to win the Formula Renault Eurocup, FIA Formula 3 Championship and FIA Formula 2 Championship in consecutive seasons, a feat matched only by Charles Leclerc and George Russell in winning the F3 and F2 titles back-to-back as a rookie.

Junior career

Born in Melbourne to parents of Italian heritage, Piastri began in remote-control car racing before moving into karting and then single-seaters in Britain. He won the 2019 Formula Renault Eurocup, joined the Alpine Academy, and won the 2020 FIA Formula 3 title with Prema. In 2021 he dominated Formula 2, taking the championship with five wins.

Formula One

With no Formula One seat available for 2022, Piastri served as Alpine's reserve driver. In August 2022 he became the subject of a famous contract dispute: Alpine announced him as their 2023 driver, which he publicly denied, and the FIA Contract Recognition Board ruled in favour of McLaren, where he replaced Daniel Ricciardo for 2023 alongside Lando Norris. He scored his first podium in Japan in 2023 and won the Qatar sprint race. His first Grand Prix victory came at the 2024 Hungarian Grand Prix, followed by a win in Azerbaijan, as McLaren won the Constructors' Championship. Further wins followed in 2025, including in China, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Miami, Spain and Belgium, making him a championship frontrunner. His calm, analytical driving style is frequently noted by commentators.
