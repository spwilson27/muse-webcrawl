Taylor Swift

Taylor Alison Swift (born December 13, 1989) is an American singer-songwriter. Known for her autobiographical songwriting, artistic reinventions, and cultural impact, she is widely regarded as one of the most influential musicians of her generation and one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with estimated sales of over 200 million records worldwide.

Career

Raised in Pennsylvania, Swift moved to Nashville, Tennessee, as a teenager to pursue country music, signing a songwriting deal with Sony/ATV at age 14 — the youngest signing in the company's history. Her self-titled debut album (2006) and Fearless (2008) established her in country music; Fearless single "Love Story" and "You Belong with Me" crossed over to pop radio. After the country-pop Red (2012), she moved decisively to pop with 1989 (2014), which won the Grammy Award for Album of the Year, as had Fearless. She later explored hip-hop-influenced sounds on Reputation (2017), synth-pop on Lover (2019), indie folk on Folklore and Evermore (2020), and pop on Midnights (2022) and The Tortured Poets Department (2024).

Masters dispute and re-recordings

In 2019, the sale of her former label Big Machine Records placed the master recordings of her first six albums under the control of executive Scooter Braun, which Swift publicly condemned. She responded by re-recording those albums, releasing them as "Taylor's Version" editions beginning in 2021 to widespread commercial success, a campaign that drew broad attention to artists' ownership of their masters.

The Eras Tour and influence

Her Eras Tour (2023–2024) became the highest-grossing concert tour of all time, surpassing US$2 billion in ticket sales, and its concert film was a box-office phenomenon. Swift's songwriting, characterized by narrative detail and "Easter egg" clues for fans, her devoted fanbase known as Swifties, and her measurable effects on local economies during the tour have made her a subject of extensive academic and journalistic study; in 2023 she was named Time Person of the Year.
