Sled dog racing

Sled dog racing (sometimes termed dog sled racing) is a winter dog sport involving the timed competition of teams of sled dogs that pull a sled with the dog driver or musher standing on the runners. The team finishing with the shortest elapsed time wins.

Races are held over various distances: sprint races of a few miles, mid-distance races such as the Copper Basin 300, and long-distance races such as the Iditarod Trail Sled Dog Race in Alaska (about 1,000 miles) and the Yukon Quest between Alaska and Canada's Yukon.

Popular racing breeds include the Siberian Husky, the Alaskan husky, and various pointer crosses bred for speed and endurance. The sport is governed internationally by bodies such as the International Federation of Sleddog Sports.
