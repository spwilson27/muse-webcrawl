List of phobias

A phobia is an anxiety disorder characterised by an intense, irrational fear of a specific object, situation, or activity. Specific phobias are among the most common anxiety disorders. Phobia names are typically formed from Greek roots with the suffix -phobia. The DSM-5 groups specific phobias into types: animal, natural environment, blood-injection-injury, situational, and other.

Treatment

Specific phobias are most commonly treated with exposure therapy, a form of cognitive behavioural therapy in which the person is gradually and repeatedly exposed to the feared stimulus. Medication is not generally first-line for specific phobias, though it is used for social anxiety disorder and agoraphobia in some cases.
