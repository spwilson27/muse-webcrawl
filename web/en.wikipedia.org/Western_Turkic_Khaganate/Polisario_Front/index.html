Polisario Front

Polisario Front
Full name: Popular Front for the Liberation of Saguia el-Hamra and Río de Oro
Founded: 10 May 1973
Founders include: El-Ouali Mustapha Sayed
Secretary-General: Brahim Ghali (since 2016)
Armed wing: Sahrawi People's Liberation Army

The Polisario Front is a Sahrawi nationalist movement seeking independence for Western Sahara from Morocco. Its name is an acronym of the Spanish Frente Popular de Liberación de Saguia el-Hamra y Río de Oro, referring to the two districts of the former Spanish Sahara.

History

Founded in 1973, the movement began armed resistance against Spanish colonial rule and, after Spain's withdrawal, against the Moroccan and Mauritanian partition of 1975. In February 1976 it proclaimed the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic (SADR). Backed by Algeria, its army fought the Western Sahara War until the 1991 UN-brokered ceasefire, and it resumed armed struggle after the ceasefire collapsed in 2020.

Organisation

The Polisario is recognised by the United Nations as the representative of the Sahrawi people. It governs the refugee camps near Tindouf, Algeria, and the "Free Zone" east of the Moroccan Berm, and the SADR is a member of the African Union — a status that prompted Morocco to leave the Organisation of African Unity in 1984 (it rejoined the AU in 2017).
