Twenty20 International

Twenty20 International
Governing body: ICC
First match: 17 February 2005 (Australia v New Zealand)
Format: 20 overs per side

A Twenty20 International (T20I) is a form of cricket played between two international members of the International Cricket Council (ICC), in which each team faces a maximum of twenty overs. The matches are the highest standard of Twenty20 cricket and carry international status.

History

The first T20I was played on 17 February 2005 between Australia and New Zealand at Eden Park, Auckland. The first women's T20I had been played the previous year, in August 2004, between England and New Zealand. The inaugural ICC World Twenty20 (now the T20 World Cup) was held in South Africa in 2007 and won by India.

Status

Since 2019, all ICC member nations have held T20I status for men's matches, greatly expanding the number of internationals played worldwide. T20Is are known for aggressive batting, athletic fielding and close finishes, and the format has driven the growth of domestic franchise leagues.
