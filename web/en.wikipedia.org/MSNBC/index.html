MSNBC is an American cable news channel owned by the NBCUniversal News Group division of NBCUniversal, a subsidiary of Comcast. It provides news coverage and political commentary from NBC News, along with its own reporting and programming.

History

MSNBC was launched on July 15, 1996, as a joint venture between NBC and Microsoft — the name combines "Microsoft" and "NBC" — and launched simultaneously with the msnbc.com website. Microsoft divested its stake in the channel in 2005 and in the website in 2012. Over the 2000s and 2010s the channel developed a liberal and progressive editorial identity in its prime-time opinion programming, with hosts such as Keith Olbermann, Rachel Maddow, and Lawrence O'Donnell.

Programming

Daytime is largely straight news under the MSNBC Reports banner, while evenings feature opinion and analysis shows including The Rachel Maddow Show and The Last Word with Lawrence O'Donnell. The morning program Morning Joe is one of its flagship shows.
