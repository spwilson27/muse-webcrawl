Tata Nano

The Tata Nano is a city car manufactured by the Indian company Tata Motors. Launched in 2008 and sold from 2009, it was famously marketed as the world's cheapest production car, with a launch price of about ₹100,000 (roughly US$2,000), earning it the nickname the "one-lakh car".

Tata Nano
Manufacturer: Tata Motors
Production: 2008–2018
Engine: 624 cc two-cylinder, rear-mounted
Layout: Rear engine, rear-wheel drive

Design

Conceived by Ratan Tata to give Indian scooter-owning families a safer all-weather alternative, the Nano cut costs with a single windscreen wiper, no power steering, three wheel nuts per wheel and a rear engine accessed from inside the cabin. Production began at Singur in West Bengal but protests over land acquisition forced a move to Sanand, Gujarat. Sales never met projections, hurt by safety concerns, fires reported in early cars, and the stigma of being "the cheapest car"; production ended in 2018.
