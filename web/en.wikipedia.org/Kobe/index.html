Kobe

Kobe (神戸市, Kōbe-shi) is the capital city of Hyōgo Prefecture in Japan and one of the country's principal port cities. With a population of about 1.5 million, it forms part of the Keihanshin metropolitan area together with Osaka and Kyoto.

History

Kobe grew around its natural harbour and was opened to foreign trade in 1868, developing a cosmopolitan foreign settlement. The city was devastated by the Great Hanshin earthquake of 17 January 1995, which killed more than 6,400 people in the region, and was subsequently rebuilt.

Economy and culture

The Port of Kobe is among Japan's busiest. The city is famous for Kobe beef, the sake breweries of the Nada district, and landmarks including the Kitano-chō foreign residences and Mount Rokkō.
