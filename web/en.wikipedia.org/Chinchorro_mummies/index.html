Chinchorro mummies

The Chinchorro mummies of northern Chile and southern Peru are the oldest known artificially mummified human remains, dating from around 5000 BCE — some two thousand years before Egyptian mummies.

The Chinchorro, a fishing culture of the Atacama coast, removed organs, reinforced bodies with sticks and reeds, and modelled features in clay, painting many mummies black or red. Their sites were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021.
