Eyelash

An eyelash (also called lash; Latin: cilium, plural cilia) is one of the hairs that grows at the edges of the eyelids in humans and other mammals. Eyelashes protect the eye from debris and small particles, and perform some of the same functions as whiskers do on a cat or mouse, in the sense that they are sensitive to touch: when an object comes near the eye, the eyelid closes reflexively.

Anatomy and growth

The upper eyelid typically carries 90 to 150 lashes arranged in several rows, while the lower lid has 60 to 80 shorter lashes. Each lash grows for about 45 days before entering resting phases; the full life cycle of an eyelash is roughly three to six months, much shorter than that of scalp hair, which keeps lashes from growing very long. Follicles are associated with the glands of Zeis and the glands of Moll at the lid margin.

The embryological development of the eyelashes begins between the 22nd and 26th weeks of gestation. Eyelash length and curvature vary between individuals and populations, and unusually long lashes can be a sign of certain genetic conditions.

Function

Experiments show that eyelashes of a length about one third the width of the eye optimally deflect airflow, reducing evaporation of the tear film and deposition of airborne particles on the cornea by roughly half. They also trigger the blink reflex when touched, shielding the eye from irritants.

Conditions

Madarosis is the loss of eyelashes, which can result from infection, inflammation such as blepharitis, autoimmune disease, or chemotherapy. Trichiasis is a condition in which lashes grow inward toward the eye, irritating or damaging the cornea. A stye (hordeolum) is an infection of the glands at the lash base.

Cosmetics

Long, dark eyelashes have long been considered a feature of beauty in many cultures. Mascara, eyelash curlers, false eyelashes and eyelash extensions are widely used to enhance their appearance, and the prostaglandin analogue bimatoprost is approved in some countries as a prescription treatment to promote lash growth.
