Gran Sabana

The Gran Sabana ("Great Savanna") is a vast upland region of grasslands in Bolívar State, southeastern Venezuela, within the Guiana Shield near the borders with Brazil and Guyana. Much of it lies within Canaima National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The landscape is famous for its tepuis — flat-topped sandstone mountains rising abruptly from the savanna — including Roraima and Kukenan, and for waterfalls such as Angel Falls, the world's tallest.

The savanna lies at an elevation of roughly 1,000 metres, giving it a cooler climate than the surrounding lowland tropics. It is home to the Pemón indigenous people and is crossed by the Troncal 10 highway, the only road link between Venezuela and Brazil in the region.
