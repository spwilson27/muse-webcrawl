Association football

Association football, more commonly known as football or soccer, is a team sport played between two teams of 11 players each, who almost exclusively use their feet to propel a ball around a rectangular field called a pitch. The objective of the game is to score more goals than the opposing team by moving the ball beyond the goal line into a rectangular-framed goal defended by the opponent. A goalkeeper on each team may use their hands within the penalty area.

Gameplay

A standard match lasts 90 minutes, played in two halves of 45 minutes with stoppage time added for interruptions. If a knockout match is drawn, extra time and a penalty shoot-out may decide the winner. Players may not touch the ball with their hands or arms, except the goalkeeper within their penalty area. Fouls are punished with free kicks or penalty kicks; the referee may issue yellow (caution) and red (dismissal) cards. The offside law restricts the positions of attacking players relative to defenders.

History

Ball games resembling football were played in many ancient cultures, including Chinese cuju, recognised by FIFA as the earliest form of the game. The modern game originated in England: the Football Association (FA) was founded in 1863 and drew up the first Laws of the Game under Ebenezer Morley. The laws are now maintained by IFAB. FIFA, the world governing body, was founded in Paris in 1904. The first FIFA World Cup was held in Uruguay in 1930.

Popularity

Football is the world's most popular sport, with an estimated global following of several billion people and over 250 million players in more than 200 countries. Major competitions include the FIFA World Cup, the UEFA European Championship, the Copa América, and club tournaments such as the UEFA Champions League. Famous players of the modern era include Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Pelé and Diego Maradona.

Governance

FIFA has 211 member associations organised into six continental confederations, including UEFA (Europe), CONMEBOL (South America), CONCACAF (North and Central America), CAF (Africa), AFC (Asia) and OFC (Oceania).
