Linkin Park

Linkin Park is an American rock band formed in Agoura Hills, California, in 1996. The band's current lineup consists of vocalist/rhythm guitarist/keyboardist Mike Shinoda, lead guitarist Brad Delson, DJ/turntablist Joe Hahn, bassist Dave Farrell, vocalist Emily Armstrong, and drummer Colin Brittain. During the band's early years, Chester Bennington served as lead vocalist until his death in 2017.

Linkin Park rose to international fame with their debut studio album, Hybrid Theory (2000), which became one of the best-selling albums of all time and launched hit singles such as "In the End" and "Crawling". Their second album, Meteora (2003), continued the success, and subsequent albums such as Minutes to Midnight (2007) broadened their sound beyond nu metal. The band has sold over 100 million records worldwide and won two Grammy Awards.

Later years

Following Bennington's death in July 2017, the band went on hiatus. In September 2024 they announced a reunion with Armstrong and Brittain, releasing the comeback album From Zero in November 2024. Linkin Park's fusion of rap, metal, and electronic elements made them central figures in the nu metal movement, and they are regarded as one of the most successful bands of the 21st century.
