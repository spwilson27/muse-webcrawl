Yahtzee

Image placeholder: Yahtzee dice and scorecard

Yahtzee is a dice game made by Milton Bradley (a company of Hasbro). First marketed in 1956 by the E.S. Lowe Company, the game's objective is to score points by rolling five dice to make certain combinations. The game is a development of earlier dice games such as Yacht and Generala.

Gameplay

Players take turns rolling five dice up to three times per turn, setting aside dice between rolls, and then must record a score in one of thirteen categories, including straights, full house, four of a kind, and chance. A roll of five of a kind is a "Yahtzee", worth 50 points. The player with the highest total after all categories are filled wins.

History

According to Hasbro, the game was invented by an anonymous Canadian couple who played it on their yacht — hence the name, adapted from "Yacht".
