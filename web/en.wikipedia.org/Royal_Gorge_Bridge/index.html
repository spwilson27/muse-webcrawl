The Royal Gorge Bridge is a tourist attraction near Cañon City, Colorado within Royal Gorge Bridge and Park, a 360-acre (150 ha) amusement park owned by the city of Cañon City and managed under lease by Royal Gorge Company of Colorado. The bridge crosses the Royal Gorge 955 feet (291 m) above the Arkansas River and held the record of being the highest bridge in the world from 1929 until 2001.

Construction

The bridge was built in six months between June and November 1929 at a cost of $350,000. It was constructed by the Royal Gorge Bridge and Amusement Company, headed by Lon Piper of San Antonio, Texas. Remarkably, the construction was completed without any fatalities.

Structure

The bridge deck is suspended from two steel cables anchored in the granite walls of the gorge, supported by four towers. The main span is 880 feet (268 m) long, and the wooden-planked deck is 18 feet (5.5 m) wide. It remains the highest suspension bridge in the United States.

2013 wildfire

In June 2013 the Royal Gorge Fire destroyed most of the park's buildings and damaged 32 of the bridge's 1,292 wooden deck planks. The bridge itself survived, and the park was rebuilt and reopened in stages from 2014.
