Affinity Publisher

DeveloperSerif
Initial release19 June 2019
PlatformmacOS, Windows, iPadOS
TypeDesktop publishing

Affinity Publisher is a desktop publishing application developed by Serif for macOS, Windows, and iPadOS. Launched in June 2019, it completes the Affinity suite with Affinity Photo and Affinity Designer, and competes with Adobe InDesign and QuarkXPress.

Features

Publisher supports master pages, grids, tables, text styles, OpenType typography, and pre-flight checking for print production. Its StudioLink technology lets users access the tools of Affinity Photo and Designer inside Publisher without switching applications, provided those apps are installed. It can import IDML and PDF files and export print-ready PDFs.

Reception

The application won an Apple Design Award in 2019. Version 2, released in 2022, added features including footnotes, a books feature for long documents, and an iPad version.
