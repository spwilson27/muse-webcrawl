Space Marine (Warhammer 40,000)

Space Marines, also known as the Adeptus Astartes, are fictional superhuman soldiers in the Warhammer 40,000 universe created by Games Workshop. They are genetically engineered warriors created from human youths through extensive biological modification, psycho-indoctrination, and rigorous training, and they serve as the elite fighting forces of the Imperium of Man. Space Marines are among the most iconic elements of the franchise and appear across its tabletop games, novels, and video games.

Creation and physiology

Prospective Marines are recruited as adolescent boys, usually from the most promising warriors of a Chapter's home world. They receive a series of gene-seed implants — up to nineteen extra organs in the original process, with later Primaris Marines receiving additional implants — that grant them superhuman strength, endurance, accelerated healing, the ability to survive in hostile environments, and extended lifespans. The gene-seed of each Chapter descends from one of the twenty Primarchs, demigod sons of the Emperor of Mankind, and it shapes the character and abilities of the Chapter.

Organisation

Space Marines are organised into autonomous Chapters of roughly 1,000 battle-brothers each, following the guidelines of the Codex Astartes attributed to Roboute Guilliman, Primarch of the Ultramarines. A Chapter is typically divided into ten companies of one hundred Marines. Famous Chapters include the Ultramarines, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Space Wolves, Imperial Fists, Salamanders, Raven Guard, White Scars, and Iron Hands. During the Horus Heresy, nine of the original eighteen surviving Legions fell to Chaos and became the Traitor Legions.

Equipment

Marines fight in powered armour, most famously the Mark VII "Aquila" pattern and its successors, and carry weapons such as the boltgun, chainsword, and plasma weapons. They deploy in Rhino and Land Raider transports, Dreadnought walkers piloted by mortally wounded veterans, and drop pods. In 2017 Games Workshop introduced the larger, stronger Primaris Space Marines, created by Archmagos Belisarius Cawl at Guilliman's order, along with new marks of armour and vehicles.

In other media

Space Marines star in video games such as Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War (2004) and Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine (2011) and its sequel Space Marine 2 (2024), as well as in an extensive range of novels published by Black Library.
