Ukraine

Ukraine is a country in Eastern Europe, the second-largest in Europe after Russia. It is bordered by Belarus, Poland, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Moldova and Russia, with coasts on the Black Sea and Sea of Azov.

History

Kyivan Rus' was a major medieval state. Ukraine later came under Polish-Lithuanian, Russian and Austrian rule, suffered the Holodomor famine of 1932-33 under Soviet rule, and declared independence from the USSR in 1991.

Russo-Ukrainian War

Russia annexed Crimea and backed separatists in Donbas in 2014 after the Euromaidan protests. On 24 February 2022 Russia launched a full-scale invasion; Ukraine resisted with Western support, liberating territory around Kyiv, Kharkiv and Kherson, while millions fled as refugees. The war continued through the mid-2020s, with heavy impact on nuclear power safety at Zaporizhzhia.
