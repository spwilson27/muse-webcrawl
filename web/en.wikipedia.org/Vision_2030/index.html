Vision 2030

Saudi Vision 2030 (Arabic: رؤية السعودية 2030) is a strategic framework launched by Saudi Arabia on 25 April 2016 to reduce the country's dependence on oil, diversify its economy, and develop public service sectors such as health, education, infrastructure, recreation, and tourism.

[Image: A rendering of the futuristic linear city THE LINE, a flagship Vision 2030 project.]

Goals and programs

Announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, the plan rests on three themes — a vibrant society, a thriving economy, and an ambitious nation — implemented through Vision Realization Programs including the Public Investment Fund Program, the Housing Program, and the National Industrial Development and Logistics Program. Flagship "giga-projects" include NEOM, the Red Sea Project, and Qiddiya.

Assessment

Progress is tracked through dozens of key performance indicators covering non-oil GDP, foreign direct investment, unemployment, and social reforms such as the lifting of the ban on women driving (2018). Critics note ambitious targets have been scaled back, notably for NEOM.
