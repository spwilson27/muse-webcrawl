Long Telegram

The Long Telegram was an 8,000-word telegram sent on 22 February 1946 by George F. Kennan, the United States chargé d'affaires in Moscow, to the U.S. Department of State. It analysed the sources of Soviet conduct and became a foundational document of the Cold War.

Content

Responding to a Treasury Department query about Soviet behaviour, Kennan argued that Soviet expansionism stemmed from a combination of traditional Russian insecurity and Marxist-Leninist ideology, that the USSR was hostile to the West but cautious and opportunistic rather than bent on war, and that it could be contained by firm, patient resistance — the intellectual basis of the containment policy.

Impact

The telegram circulated widely in Washington and shaped the Truman administration's approach. Kennan elaborated its arguments publicly in the 1947 Foreign Affairs article “The Sources of Soviet Conduct”, signed “X”, known as the X Article.
