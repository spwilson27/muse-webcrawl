RIM-174 Standard ERAM


Placeholder image: RIM-174 Standard ERAM

The RIM-174 Standard Extended Range Active Missile (ERAM), or Standard Missile 6 (SM-6), is a United States Navy anti-air and anti-surface missile in the Standard Missile family, produced by Raytheon. It combines the airframe of the SM-2ER Block IV with the active seeker of the AIM-120 AMRAAM.

Capabilities

The SM-6 provides extended-range anti-air warfare capability against aircraft, helicopters, unmanned aerial vehicles and cruise missiles, and can engage targets over the horizon using Cooperative Engagement Capability data. It also has terminal ballistic-missile defence and anti-ship strike modes. Its range exceeds 240 km.

Service

The missile reached initial operational capability in 2013 and is deployed on Aegis-equipped cruisers and destroyers. In 2024 an air-launched variant, the AIM-174B, was revealed in US Navy service.
