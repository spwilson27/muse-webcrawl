Aerobatic maneuver

An aerobatic maneuver (American spelling: aerobatic maneuver) is a deliberate flight manoeuvre involving an abrupt change in an aircraft's attitude, abnormal attitude, or abnormal acceleration not necessary for normal flight. Aerobatics are performed for recreation, training and competition.

Common maneuvers

Basic figures include the loop, the barrel roll, the aileron roll, the hammerhead (stall turn), the Immelmann turn and the Cuban eight. Competitive aerobatics are judged using Aresti symbols, a standardised notation cataloguing figures and their difficulty coefficients.

Competition

Aerobatic competitions are held from primary to unlimited categories, scored by judges on precision and positioning within a marked aerobatic box. Pilots must manage high g-forces and energy precisely; aircraft such as the Extra 300 are designed specifically for the sport.
