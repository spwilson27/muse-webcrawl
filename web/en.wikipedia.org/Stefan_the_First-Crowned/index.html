Stefan the First-Crowned

Reign: Grand Prince 1196–1217; King 1217–1228

Father: Stefan Nemanja

Brother: Saint Sava

Stefan Nemanjić, known as Stefan the First-Crowned (c. 1165–1228), was ruler of the Serbian Grand Principality and, from 1217, the first crowned king of Serbia.

Reign

Stefan succeeded his father Stefan Nemanja in 1196 after Nemanja abdicated and became a monk. He survived a civil war with his brother Vukan, supported by Hungary, and navigated between the Byzantine Empire, Venice and the papacy.

Coronation and church

In 1217 a papal legate crowned Stefan king, giving his epithet. In 1219 his brother Sava secured autocephaly for the Serbian Orthodox Church and crowned Stefan again with an Orthodox crown in 1220/1221. Stefan is venerated as a saint and wrote a biography of his father.
