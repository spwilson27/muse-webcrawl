Caesar (Planet of the Apes)

Caesar is a fictional character in the Planet of the Apes franchise. In the original film series he is the son of the talking chimpanzees Cornelius and Zira, played by Roddy McDowall in Conquest of the Planet of the Apes (1972) and Battle for the Planet of the Apes (1973), where he leads an ape revolution against oppressive humans. In the reboot series he is portrayed by Andy Serkis through motion capture, becoming one of the most acclaimed CGI characters in cinema.

Reboot series

In Rise of the Planet of the Apes (2011), Caesar is a chimpanzee born to a test subject given the experimental Alzheimer's drug ALZ-112, which he inherits as greatly enhanced intelligence. Raised by scientist Will Rodman, he is eventually confined to a brutal primate shelter, where he leads an uprising and releases a smarter-making virus among apes, escaping to the Muir Woods. In Dawn of the Planet of the Apes (2014) he leads an ape society while trying to keep peace with human survivors, and in War for the Planet of the Apes (2017) he leads his people against a ruthless human colonel, dying after bringing them to a safe home. His legacy is central to Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes (2024), set generations later, in which his teachings have become scripture.

Reception

Serkis's performance, combining physical acting with Weta Digital's effects, prompted debate about acting awards for motion-capture roles, and Caesar is frequently ranked among the greatest CGI characters ever created.
