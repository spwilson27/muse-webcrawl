Kinesiology

Kinesiology is the scientific study of human body movement, addressing physiological, anatomical, biomechanical, pathological and neuropsychological principles of movement. The term derives from the Greek kinesis (movement) and -logia (study).

Scope and applications

Kinesiology underpins exercise science, sports performance, rehabilitation, ergonomics and occupational health. Practitioners (in some jurisdictions, registered kinesiologists) apply assessment of movement and exercise prescription to prevent and manage injury and chronic disease. The field is distinct from "applied kinesiology", a controversial alternative-medicine muscle-testing technique not supported by scientific evidence.
