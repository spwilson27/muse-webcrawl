List of Roman bridges

The Romans built an extensive network of bridges across their empire, many of which survive today. Roman bridges were typically stone arch structures, often incorporating concrete (opus caementicium), and included aqueduct bridges as well as road bridges. This list includes notable examples, both surviving and historically recorded.
