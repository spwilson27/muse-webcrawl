Credit card fraud

Credit card fraud is a form of identity theft involving the unauthorized use of a credit card account to make purchases or withdraw funds. It includes the use of stolen physical cards, skimmed or cloned card data, and card-not-present fraud using stolen numbers online or by phone.

Methods

Common methods include skimming devices placed on payment terminals and ATMs, phishing, large-scale data breaches at merchants and payment processors, and mail theft. Fraudsters may test stolen numbers with small purchases before making larger ones.

Prevention and liability

Preventive measures include EMV chip technology, tokenization, two-factor authentication, transaction monitoring, and alerts. In many jurisdictions consumer liability for fraudulent charges is limited by law or card network policy, with issuers and merchants bearing most losses.
