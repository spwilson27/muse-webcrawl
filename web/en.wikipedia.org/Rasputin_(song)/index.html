"Rasputin" is a song by the German-Caribbean disco group Boney M., released as a single in 1978 and included on their third studio album, Nightflight to Venus. Written by group creator Frank Farian, George Reyam and Fred Jay, the song is a semi-biographical account of Grigori Rasputin, the Russian mystic and confidant of Tsar Nicholas II and his family.

Composition and lyrics

The song recounts Rasputin's rise to influence at the Russian court, describing him as "Russia's greatest love machine" and a legendary, larger-than-life figure. Its chorus quotes the manner of a Russian folk dance, and the arrangement incorporates a balalaika-like synthesiser line imitating traditional Russian instrumentation. The closing passages refer to the plot to assassinate Rasputin in 1916, including the poisoned wine and the shots fired at him, before concluding that he could not be easily killed.

Reception and legacy

"Rasputin" topped the charts in several countries, including Germany and Austria, and reached number two on the UK Singles Chart. It remains one of Boney M.'s best-known recordings. In 2021 the song experienced a major resurgence in popularity on the social media platform TikTok, returning to charts worldwide more than four decades after its release. The Finnish folk metal band Turisas recorded a well-known cover version in 2007.
