HMS Macedonian

HMS Macedonian
Type: Lively-class frigate, 38 guns
Launched: 2 June 1810
Captured: 25 October 1812 by USS United States
Later: USS Macedonian

HMS Macedonian was a 38-gun Lively-class frigate of the Royal Navy, launched at Woolwich Dockyard in 1810. She is best known for her capture by the American frigate USS United States during the War of 1812.

Capture

On 25 October 1812, south of the Azores, Macedonian, commanded by Captain John Surman Carden, engaged USS United States under Commodore Stephen Decatur. The American frigate was larger, more heavily armed with 24-pounder guns, and fought the action at long range; after a one-sided battle in which Macedonian suffered over 100 casualties and was dismasted, Carden surrendered. Macedonian was taken into the United States Navy as USS Macedonian, one of the most celebrated American prizes of the war.

Later service

In American service she operated during the closing phase of the war and later in the Mediterranean and Pacific. A later USS Macedonian sloop-of-war of 1836 was built incorporating material and the name of the prize.
