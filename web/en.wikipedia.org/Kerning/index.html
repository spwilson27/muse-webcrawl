Kerning

In typography, kerning is the process of adjusting the spacing between individual letter pairs to achieve a visually pleasing result. Certain pairs such as "AV", "Wa" and "Yo" look better with reduced space.

History

In metal type, kerned letters had parts of the sort overhanging the body. Digital fonts store kerning pairs or class-based adjustments in tables such as OpenType GPOS, and layout engines apply them automatically.

Kerning vs tracking

Kerning adjusts specific pairs, whereas tracking (letter-spacing) adjusts spacing uniformly across a range of text.
