DB Regio AG is a subsidiary of Deutsche Bahn (DB) responsible for regional and local passenger transport in Germany. It operates Regional-Express and Regionalbahn trains, most of the country's S-Bahn suburban networks (including Berlin and Hamburg, the latter through a subsidiary), and extensive regional bus services through DB Regio Bus. DB Regio carries several million passengers every day and is by far the largest provider of publicly funded local transport in Germany.

Organisation and contracting

Since Germany's 1994 rail reform and the 1996 "regionalisation" of local transport, the federal states (Länder) and their transport authorities order regional rail services through competitive tenders or direct awards. DB Regio competes for these contracts against operators such as Transdev, National Express, and regional railways; its market share, while still dominant, has declined as more contracts have gone to rivals. The company is organised regionally, broadly aligned with the states, and its vehicles traditionally wear a red livery (verkehrsrot).

Role in the fare system

DB Regio services are integrated into local transport associations (Verkehrsverbünde) and accept nationwide products. The launch of the Deutschlandticket in May 2023 — a flat-rate monthly subscription for local and regional transport across Germany — significantly changed the company's revenue model, with compensation paid by federal and state governments.
