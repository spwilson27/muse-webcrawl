Laylat al-Qadr

Laylat al-Qadr (Arabic: لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ), the Night of Decree or Night of Power, is the night in Islam when the first verses of the Quran were revealed to the Prophet Muhammad. It is described in Surah Al-Qadr (97) as "better than a thousand months".

Observance

The exact date is not specified; by tradition it falls on one of the odd-numbered nights in the last ten days of Ramadan — most commonly observed on the 27th night. Muslims mark it with extra prayer, Quran recitation, dhikr and charity, believing that deeds and prayers on this night carry multiplied reward and that God's decrees for the coming year are set.
