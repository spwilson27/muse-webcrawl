Under the Dome is an American science-fiction mystery drama television series developed by Brian K. Vaughan and based on Stephen King's 2009 novel of the same name. It premiered on CBS on 24 June 2013 and concluded on 10 September 2015 after three seasons. Stephen King served as an executive producer.

Premise

The residents of the small town of Chester's Mill, Maine, are suddenly cut off from the outside world when an enormous, transparent, indestructible dome descends over the town. Trapped inside with dwindling resources, the townspeople — including drifter Dale "Barbie" Barbara, journalist Julia Shumway, and councilman "Big Jim" Rennie, who schemes to consolidate power — struggle to survive and to discover the dome's origins.

Reception

The premiere drew 13.5 million viewers, one of the biggest summer drama launches in years. The first season received generally positive reviews, but later seasons were criticised for increasingly convoluted plotting; ratings declined, and CBS cancelled the series after season three, with the finale resolving the dome storyline.
