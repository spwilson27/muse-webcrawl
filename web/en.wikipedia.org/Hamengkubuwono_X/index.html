Hamengkubuwono X

Hamengkubuwono X (Javanese: ꦲꦩꦼꦁꦏꦸꦨꦸꦮꦤ꧇꧑꧐꧇; born 2 April 1946) is the Sultan of Yogyakarta, Indonesia, and has served as Governor of the Special Region of Yogyakarta since 1998. He succeeded his father, Hamengkubuwono IX, as sultan on 7 March 1989. Under Indonesia's 2012 law on Yogyakarta's special status (Undang-Undang Keistimewaan), the reigning sultan automatically serves as governor of the region, an arrangement unique among Indonesian provinces.

Early life and reign

Born Bendara Raden Mas Herjuno Darpito in Yogyakarta, he was educated at Gadjah Mada University, where he studied public administration. Before becoming sultan he was active in business and in the Golkar party's Yogyakarta chapter. As crown prince he received the title Kanjeng Gusti Pangeran Haryo Mangkubumi.

During the political crisis of 1998, Hamengkubuwono X played a notable role: with his father remembered as a hero of the revolution and a former vice-president, he supported the reform movement and addressed a mass gathering of hundreds of thousands in Yogyakarta on 20 May 1998, the day before President Suharto resigned. He was elected governor by the provincial legislature later that year and has been reappointed under the special-status law since 2012, serving alongside Vice-Governor Paku Alam X.

Succession controversy

In 2015 the Sultan issued royal decrees changing the title of his eldest daughter, Princess Mangkubumi, and giving her the male-style crown-prince title, signalling her as heir to the throne. The move — which would make her the first female sultan of Yogyakarta — was opposed by some of the Sultan's brothers and remains debated within the royal family and Javanese society.
