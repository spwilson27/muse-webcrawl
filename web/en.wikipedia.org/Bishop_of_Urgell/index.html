Bishop of Urgell

The Bishop of Urgell is the ordinary of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Urgell in Catalonia, Spain, seated at La Seu d'Urgell. Uniquely, the bishop is also ex officio Co-Prince of Andorra, jointly head of state of the Pyrenean principality together with the President of France.

History

The diocese dates to the early medieval period. Through medieval land donations and the paréage treaties of 1278 and 1288 between the bishop and the Count of Foix, shared sovereignty over Andorra was established between the Bishop of Urgell and the French co-prince (whose rights passed to the French crown and then to the President of the Republic).

Role in Andorra

Under Andorra's 1993 constitution, the co-princes are ceremonial heads of state who promulgate laws and accredit ambassadors; day-to-day government rests with the General Council and the Government of Andorra. Each co-prince maintains a personal representative in Andorra.

Recent bishops

Joan Martí i Alanis (1970–2003) played a part in Andorra's constitutional modernization. Joan-Enric Vives i Sicília served from 2003 and was granted the personal title of Archbishop. In 2024 Pope Francis appointed Josep-Lluís Serrano Pentinat coadjutor; he succeeded as Bishop of Urgell — and thus Co-Prince — in 2025.
