Linus Torvalds

Linus Benedict Torvalds (born 28 December 1969) is a Finnish-American software engineer who is the creator and lead developer of the Linux kernel. He also created the Git version control system and the Subsurface dive-log software.

Torvalds announced Linux in 1991 while a student at the University of Helsinki; it became the kernel for operating systems used in servers, Android devices and much of the world's computing infrastructure. He remains the kernel's maintainer, coordinating development through mailing lists from Oregon, where he lives.

Recognition

Awards include the Millennium Technology Prize (2012), shared with Shinya Yamanaka, and the IEEE Computer Pioneer Award (2014).
