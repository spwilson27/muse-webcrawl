Global Television Network

The Global Television Network, commonly known as Global, is a Canadian English-language terrestrial television network owned by Corus Entertainment. Launched in 1974 in southern Ontario, it is one of Canada's three main national broadcast networks, alongside CBC Television and CTV.

History

Global began broadcasting on 6 January 1974 from studios in Don Mills, Toronto, under founder Al Bruner and later Moses Znaimer's involvement, though financial difficulties soon led to its rescue by a consortium. Through a series of acquisitions it expanded from a regional Ontario service into a national network; CanWest Global Communications controlled it from the 1990s until 2010, when Shaw Media acquired it. Corus Entertainment took ownership in 2016.

Programming and stations

Global broadcasts a schedule heavy in American acquired dramas and comedies, alongside Global News national and local newscasts and Canadian productions such as news magazine programmes and entertainment shows. It operates owned-and-operated stations in most major Canadian markets from Vancouver to Halifax.
