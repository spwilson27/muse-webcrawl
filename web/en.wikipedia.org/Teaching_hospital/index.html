Teaching hospital

A teaching hospital is a hospital or medical centre that provides medical education and training to doctors, nurses and other health professionals, usually in affiliation with a medical school or university. Teaching hospitals are often also centres of clinical research and tertiary referral care.

Characteristics

Patient care is delivered by teams that include attending physicians, residents (junior doctors in specialist training), interns and medical students, supervised in a graded structure of responsibility. In the United States, major teaching hospitals are represented by the Association of American Medical Colleges, and graduate medical education is substantially funded through Medicare. Teaching hospitals tend to offer specialised services and handle more complex cases than community hospitals.
