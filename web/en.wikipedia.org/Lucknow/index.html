Lucknow

Lucknow is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh, situated on the banks of the Gomti River. Historically the capital of the Awadh region and seat of the Nawabs of Awadh, it is famed for its refined Urdu culture, cuisine (including kebabs and biryani), chikankari embroidery and monumental architecture such as the Bara Imambara and Rumi Darwaza.

History

Lucknow flourished in the 18th and early 19th centuries under the Nawabs, who patronised poetry, music and cuisine. In 1856 the British annexed Awadh; the city was a central theatre of the Indian Rebellion of 1857, including the Siege of the Residency. Today it is a major administrative, commercial and educational centre with a metropolitan population of several million.
