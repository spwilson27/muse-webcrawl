SkyTeam

SkyTeam is one of the world's three major airline alliances. Founded in June 2000 by Aeroméxico, Air France, Delta Air Lines, and Korean Air, it has grown to include carriers across Europe, Asia, the Americas, Africa, and the Middle East, headquartered in Amstelveen, Netherlands, near Amsterdam Airport Schiphol.

Members and network

Members have included, at various times, Air France–KLM, Delta, Korean Air, Aeroméxico, Aerolíneas Argentinas, Air Europa, China Airlines, China Eastern, Czech Airlines, Garuda Indonesia, ITA Airways, Kenya Airways, KLM, Middle East Airlines, Saudia, TAROM, Vietnam Airlines, Virgin Atlantic, and XiamenAir. Aeroflot's membership was suspended in 2022. Together the alliance's members have served well over 1,000 destinations in more than 160 countries.

Services

SkyTeam offers reciprocal frequent-flyer earning and redemption through its SkyTeam Elite and Elite Plus tiers, shared airport lounges (including branded SkyTeam lounges at major hubs), codesharing, and coordinated schedules. It competes with the Star Alliance and Oneworld. The alliance launched initiatives such as the Sustainable Flight Challenge to promote lower-emission aviation.
