Tanegashima

Tanegashima (種子島) is one of the Ōsumi Islands belonging to Kagoshima Prefecture, Japan. The island, 444.99 km² in area, is the second largest of the Ōsumi Islands and has a population of about 33,000. It is best known as the site of Japan's main spaceport, the Tanegashima Space Center, and as the place where firearms were first introduced to Japan in 1543.

History

In 1543 a Chinese junk carrying Portuguese traders was wrecked on Tanegashima, and the local lord, Tanegashima Tokitaka, purchased two matchlock arquebuses from the Portuguese — the first firearms seen in Japan. Within years, Japanese smiths were mass-producing copies, and guns named "Tanegashima" after the island transformed Sengoku-period warfare, most famously at the Battle of Nagashino in 1575.

Space center

The Tanegashima Space Center, established in 1969 by the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA), is Japan's largest rocket-launch complex. Its Yoshinobu Launch Complex has launched H-IIA and H3 rockets carrying satellites and cargo vehicles to the International Space Station. The island's equatorial proximity and open ocean downrange make it Japan's premier launch site.

Geography

Tanegashima is a long, narrow, low-lying island about 57 km from north to south. Its economy relies on agriculture — sugarcane, sweet potatoes, and the distinctive Anno potato — fishing, tourism drawn by surfing beaches, and the space center. The administrative divisions are the city of Nishinoomote and the towns of Nakatane and Minamitane.
