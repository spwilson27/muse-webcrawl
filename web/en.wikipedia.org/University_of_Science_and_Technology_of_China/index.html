University of Science and Technology of China

The University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) is a national research university in Hefei, Anhui, China, under the Chinese Academy of Sciences. Founded in Beijing in 1958, it moved to Hefei in 1970.

History

USTC was founded to train scientists for China's strategic needs, especially nuclear science. Its Special Class for the Gifted Young, started in 1978, became famous.

Academics

The university focuses on science and engineering, including physics, chemistry, computer science and quantum information.

Research

USTC led the Micius quantum satellite experiments and maintains national laboratories in synchrotron radiation and high magnetic fields.
