Siri

Siri Developer: Apple Inc.
Initial release: 4 October 2011
Platforms: iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, visionOS
Type: Virtual assistant

Siri is a virtual assistant developed by Apple Inc. that is part of Apple's iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, macOS, tvOS and visionOS operating systems. It uses voice queries, gesture-based controls, focus tracking and a natural-language user interface to answer questions, make recommendations and perform actions by delegating requests to a set of Internet services.

History

Siri originated as a project of SRI International's Artificial Intelligence Center and was spun off as a standalone app for the iPhone in February 2010. Apple acquired the company in April 2010, and Siri was integrated into the iPhone 4S, launched on 4 October 2011 as a headline feature of the device. The day after the launch, Apple co-founder Steve Jobs died, and the 4S has often been interpreted retrospectively as standing "for Siri".

Over subsequent releases Siri gained support for dozens of languages, additional voices, on-device processing for many requests, and integration with third-party apps through SiriKit. In 2024 Apple announced a major upgrade under the Apple Intelligence initiative, promising a more context-aware assistant with richer personal context understanding, though the rollout of the most advanced features was delayed.

Features

Siri can perform phone actions, check basic information such as weather and facts, schedule events and reminders, handle device settings, search the Internet, navigate areas, and provide information on entertainment. Activation is typically by the wake phrase "Siri" or "Hey Siri", or by pressing and holding a hardware button.

Criticism and privacy

Siri has been criticised for falling behind rival assistants in understanding and capability. In 2019 Apple faced controversy when it emerged that contractors had listened to anonymised Siri recordings for quality grading; Apple apologised, suspended the programme and made participation opt-in.
