Caribbean Netherlands

The Caribbean Netherlands (Dutch: Caribisch Nederland) is the group of three special municipalities of the Netherlands located in the Caribbean Sea: Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba, collectively known as the BES islands. They became part of the country of the Netherlands on 10 October 2010, following the dissolution of the Netherlands Antilles.

Administration

Unlike ordinary Dutch municipalities, the BES islands are public bodies (openbare lichamen) in which some national tasks are carried out by the National Office for the Caribbean Netherlands. The official language is Dutch, with Papiamento on Bonaire and English on Saba and Sint Eustatius widely used and recognised. The US dollar replaced the Netherlands Antillean guilder as currency in 2011.

Geography

Bonaire lies off the coast of Venezuela in the Leeward Antilles, while Saba and Sint Eustatius are part of the northern Leeward Islands near Sint Maarten. Together their population is roughly 30,000.
