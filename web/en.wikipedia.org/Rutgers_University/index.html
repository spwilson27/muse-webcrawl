Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey, is a public land-grant research university in New Jersey. Chartered in 1766 as Queen's College by the Dutch Reformed Church, it is the eighth-oldest college in the United States and one of the colonial colleges chartered before the American Revolution. It was renamed for benefactor Henry Rutgers in 1825.

Structure

The university comprises the flagship Rutgers–New Brunswick campus, Rutgers–Newark, Rutgers–Camden, and Rutgers Biomedical and Health Sciences. It became the state university of New Jersey through legislation in 1945 and 1956, and joined the Big Ten Conference and the Association of American Universities.

History and sport

Rutgers played Princeton in 1869 in what is regarded as the first intercollegiate American football game, which Rutgers won 6–4. Its athletic teams are the Scarlet Knights.
