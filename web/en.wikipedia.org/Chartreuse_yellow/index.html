Chartreuse yellow

Chartreuse yellow is a yellow-green color halfway between yellow and green on the color wheel. It is named after Chartreuse liqueur, produced by Carthusian monks in France since the 18th century.

The color is often confused with chartreuse green, which is closer to green. Chartreuse yellow corresponds approximately to hex code #DFFF00, a bright, highly visible yellow-green traditionally used for safety vests, tennis balls (optic yellow is similar), and fishing lures.

The first recorded use of "chartreuse" as a color name in English dates to the late 19th century. The liqueur that inspired it gets its hue from more than 130 herbs, plants and flowers.
