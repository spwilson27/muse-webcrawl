Scratch (programming language)

Scratch is a high-level, block-based visual programming language aimed primarily at children and beginners. It was developed by the Lifelong Kindergarten Group at the MIT Media Lab, under the leadership of Mitchel Resnick, and first released in 2007. Users create programs by dragging and snapping together colourful blocks, controlling sprites on a stage to make games, animations and interactive stories.

Community

The Scratch website hosts a large online community where users share projects and "remix" one another's work. It has been used by tens of millions of registered users in schools and coding clubs around the world, and is translated into dozens of languages. Scratch is free of charge and its projects are shared under a Creative Commons licence.

Versions

Scratch 2.0 (2013) moved the editor into the web browser. Scratch 3.0 (2019), built on HTML5, added support for tablets and extensions for hardware such as micro:bit and LEGO robotics kits. The Scratch mascot is an orange cat.
