Miracle on Ice

DateFebruary 22, 1980
VenueLake Placid, New York
ResultUnited States 4, Soviet Union 3
Competition1980 Winter Olympics, medal round

The "Miracle on Ice" was an ice hockey game during the 1980 Winter Olympics in Lake Placid, New York, in which the United States men's national team, composed largely of amateur and collegiate players and coached by Herb Brooks, defeated the Soviet Union 4–3 in the medal round. The Soviet team was a four-time defending gold medallist and overwhelming favourite, staffed by veteran professionals in all but name.

The Americans trailed for most of the game, tying it three times before captain Mike Eruzione scored the go-ahead goal with ten minutes remaining. As the clock ran down, broadcaster Al Michaels delivered his famous call: "Do you believe in miracles? Yes!" The victory became one of the most celebrated upsets in sporting history, freighted with Cold War symbolism.

Aftermath

Two days later the United States defeated Finland 4–2 to win the gold medal. The game has repeatedly been voted the greatest moment in American sports history; it inspired the 2004 film Miracle, and the arena in Lake Placid was later named for Brooks.
