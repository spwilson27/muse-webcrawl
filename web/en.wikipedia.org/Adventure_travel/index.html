Adventure travel

Adventure travel is a type of tourism involving exploration or travel to remote, exotic or physically challenging environments, often including activities such as trekking, mountaineering, white-water rafting, scuba diving and safaris.

Characteristics

Adventure travellers typically seek some combination of physical activity, cultural exchange and engagement with nature, and accept a degree of risk or novelty. The industry distinguishes "soft" adventure (such as hiking or snorkelling) from "hard" adventure (such as high-altitude climbing or caving).

Industry

Adventure tourism is one of the fastest-growing segments of the travel industry. Trade bodies such as the Adventure Travel Trade Association promote standards and sustainability, as concerns grow about environmental impact and the safety of travellers and guides.
