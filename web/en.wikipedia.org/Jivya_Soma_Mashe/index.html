Jivya Soma Mashe (1934–2018) was an Indian artist of the Warli tribe of Maharashtra, credited more than any other individual with bringing Warli painting to national and international attention.

Life and work

Born in Talasari in Thane district, Mashe began drawing as a child. Discovered in the 1970s through government programmes promoting tribal art, he transformed the ritual wall-painting tradition of Warli women into a free-flowing narrative art on paper and canvas, depicting tarpa dances, harvests, myths, animals and daily village life in white pigment lines and dots.

Recognition

His work was exhibited internationally, including in Paris, and featured in exhibitions of Indian contemporary and tribal art. He received the Padma Shri in 2011. His sons Sadashiv and Balu Mashe also became Warli artists.
