Pale of Settlement

The Pale of Settlement was a western region of the Russian Empire with varying borders between 1791 and 1915, within which permanent settlement by Jews was allowed and beyond which it was generally prohibited.

History

Created under Catherine the Great following the partitions of Poland, the Pale comprised some 25 provinces including parts of modern Poland, Ukraine, Belarus, Lithuania, and Moldova. At its height it held the largest Jewish population in the world — over five million people by the late 19th century, who constituted a large share of many towns within it.

Abolition

The May Laws of 1882 further restricted Jewish settlement within the Pale. The Pale was formally abolished by the Russian Provisional Government in 1917, after the February Revolution.
