Regina King

Regina Rene King (born 15 January 1971) is an American actress and director. She first gained attention as a teenager on the sitcom 227 (1985–1990) and in films including Boyz n the Hood (1991), Jerry Maguire (1996) and Ray (2004).

King won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for If Beale Street Could Talk (2018) and four Primetime Emmy Awards, for American Crime, Seven Seconds and Watchmen (2019), in which she starred as Angela Abar. Her feature directorial debut, One Night in Miami... (2020), earned wide acclaim and made her the first Black woman director to premiere a film at the Venice Film Festival.
