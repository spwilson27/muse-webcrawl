Trường Sơn

The Trường Sơn Range, also known as the Annamite Range / Annamese Cordillera, is a mountain range of eastern Indochina, stretching roughly 1,100 km through Laos, Vietnam and a small part of Cambodia. It forms the border between Vietnam and Laos for much of its length.

Trường Sơn
Length: ~1,100 km
Highest point: Phu Bia (2,819 m)
Countries: Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia
Also called: Annamite Range

Geography and ecology

The range includes peaks over 2,000 m, limestone karst, and wet evergreen forests of exceptional biodiversity, home to rare species such as the saola, large-antlered muntjac and Annamite striped rabbit, many described only in the 1990s.

History

During the Vietnam War the Ho Chi Minh Trail — a network of roads and paths through the Trường Sơn in Laos and Vietnam — carried North Vietnamese troops and supplies south, and was heavily bombed. See Tet Offensive.
