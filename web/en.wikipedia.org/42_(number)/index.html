42 (number)

42 (forty-two) is the natural number following 41 and preceding 43. It is an abundant number, a pronic number (the product of 6 and 7), and appears in numerous mathematical, scientific, religious, and cultural contexts.

In mathematics

Forty-two is the third primary pseudoperfect number, a Catalan number adjacent value in several sequences, and the sum of the first six even numbers after zero in one of its many decompositions. It gained mathematical fame in 2019 when the last remaining case of the "sum of three cubes" problem below 100 was solved: 42 was expressed as the sum of three enormous integer cubes, completing a puzzle posed in 1954. It is also the number of possible outcomes-related values in various combinatorial settings and the atomic number of molybdenum is 42 in science.

In science and technology

Molybdenum has atomic number 42. In astronomy, 42 is associated with the rainbow, whose angle of refraction is about 42 degrees. In computing, the asterisk character has ASCII code 42, and in the influential Unix tradition the asterisk is a wildcard meaning "whatever you want" — a coincidence Douglas Adams remarked upon.

In popular culture

In Douglas Adams's The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, the supercomputer Deep Thought announces that the Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything is "42", after computing for 7.5 million years — prompting the observation that the question itself was never properly understood. The number consequently became an in-joke across geek culture, appearing in search engine Easter eggs, programming examples, and the naming of schools and companies. In baseball, 42 was Jackie Robinson's jersey number and is retired across Major League Baseball in his honour.
