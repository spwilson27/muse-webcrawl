LeFevre Peninsula

The LeFevre Peninsula is a peninsula located in the north-western suburbs of Adelaide, South Australia, between Gulf St Vincent to the west and the Barker Inlet and Port River to the east. It extends roughly from the suburb of Largs Bay in the south to Outer Harbor in the north.

The peninsula is a largely residential and industrial area, home to suburbs including Semaphore, Largs Bay, Taperoo, Osborne and Outer Harbor. Port Adelaide lies at its southern base. Historically the area was associated with maritime industries, shipping, and the working port, and its western coast features beaches along the gulf.

The peninsula is named after Sir John George Shaw Lefevre, a British lawyer, civil servant and Whig politician. Major facilities include the Outer Harbor passenger terminal and container facilities, the Torrens Island area opposite across the river, and the former Pelican Point and Osborne shipbuilding precincts nearby.
