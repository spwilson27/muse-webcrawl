Jeethu Joseph

Jeethu Joseph
Born: 10 November 1972, Mutholapuram, Ernakulam, Kerala, India
Occupation: Film director, screenwriter
Years active: 2007–present
Known for: Drishyam (2013), Drishyam 2 (2021), Memories (2013)

Jeethu Joseph (born 10 November 1972) is an Indian film director and screenwriter who works primarily in Malayalam cinema. He is best known for the crime thriller Drishyam (2013), starring Mohanlal, which became one of the most acclaimed and commercially successful Malayalam films ever made and was remade in numerous Indian and foreign languages.

Career

Jeethu Joseph began his career assisting director Jayaraj before making his directorial debut with the investigative thriller Detective (2007), starring Suresh Gopi. After mixed results with Mummy & Me (2010) and My Boss (2012), he had a breakthrough year in 2013 with the police thriller Memories, starring Prithviraj Sukumaran, followed by Drishyam.

Drishyam and sequels

Drishyam tells the story of Georgekutty, an ordinary cable-TV operator who constructs an elaborate alibi to protect his family after an accidental killing. The film was a critical and box-office phenomenon, praised for its screenplay, and inspired remakes in Tamil (Papanasam), Telugu, Kannada, Hindi, Sinhala, and Chinese. Jeethu Joseph directed the sequel Drishyam 2 (2021), released directly on streaming, which was likewise widely praised, and the Hindi remake Drishyam 2 (2022) as a writer.

Other work

His later films include the Tamil thriller Thambi (2019), 12th Man (2022), Kooman (2022), and Neru (2023), a courtroom drama with Mohanlal. Jeethu Joseph is recognized for tightly constructed suspense narratives and twist-driven plotting.
