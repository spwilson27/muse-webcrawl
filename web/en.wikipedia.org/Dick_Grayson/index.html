Dick Grayson

Richard John "Dick" Grayson is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Batman. Created by Bob Kane, Bill Finger, and Jerry Robinson, he first appeared in Detective Comics #38 in April 1940 as the original and most popular incarnation of Robin, Batman's crime-fighting partner and sidekick.

Fictional biography

Grayson was a young circus acrobat, the youngest of the "Flying Graysons" trapeze act. After his parents were murdered by a mobster, Bruce Wayne took him in as his legal ward and trained him to fight crime as Robin. As an adult, Grayson struck out on his own as Nightwing, leader of teams including the Teen Titans, and he has occasionally taken up the mantle of Batman himself when Bruce Wayne was absent or presumed dead.

In other media

The character has appeared in numerous animated series, films, and video games, portrayed in live action by Burt Ward in the 1960s Batman television series and by Brenton Thwaites in Titans.
