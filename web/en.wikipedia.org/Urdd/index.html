Urdd

Urdd Gobaith Cymru, commonly called the Urdd, is a national youth organisation in Wales founded in 1922 by Ifan ab Owen Edwards. It promotes the Welsh language among children and young people through sport, arts, residential centres and the Urdd Eisteddfod.

Eisteddfod

The Urdd National Eisteddfod, held annually during May half-term, is one of Europe's largest youth festivals, with competitions in music, dance, drama and literature in Welsh.

Activities

The Urdd organises sports leagues, magazines, outdoor centres at Llangrannog and Glan-llyn, and humanitarian and international projects.

Symbol

Its red, white and green triangular logo and mascot Mistar Urdd are widely recognised in Wales.
