Monster Hunter is a Japanese media franchise centred on a series of fantasy action role-playing video games developed and published by Capcom. Players take the role of a Hunter who tracks, battles, and traps large monsters in varied environments, carving materials from them to craft stronger weapons and armour.

Gameplay

Quests are undertaken solo or in cooperative groups of up to four players. Combat emphasises deliberate, animation-committed attacks with fourteen weapon classes in recent entries, and success depends on learning each monster's behaviour. The series became a phenomenon in Japan on handheld consoles before breaking through globally with Monster Hunter: World (2018), Capcom's best-selling title.

Other media

The franchise includes anime, manga, a trading card game, and a 2020 live-action film directed by Paul W. S. Anderson.
