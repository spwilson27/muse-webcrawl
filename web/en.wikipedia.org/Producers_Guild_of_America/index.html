Producers Guild of America

The Producers Guild of America (PGA) is a 501(c)(6) trade association representing television producers, film producers and producers of emerging media in the United States. Founded in 1950 as the Screen Producers Guild, it has over 8,000 members and advocates for producers' working conditions, credits, and professional standards.

Producers Guild Awards

The Guild presents the annual Producers Guild of America Awards, inaugurated in 1990. Its Darryl F. Zanuck Award for Outstanding Producer of Theatrical Motion Pictures is a closely watched precursor of the Academy Award for Best Picture; the two have coincided frequently, and the PGA is notable for using the same preferential ballot system as the Academy.

The Producers Mark

To combat the proliferation of producer credits, the PGA introduced the Producers Mark ("p.g.a.") in 2012. The mark certifies that the credited producer actually performed a major portion of the producing functions on the production, as determined by the Guild's arbitration process.

Functions

Unlike a labour union, the PGA is a trade association: producers, as management, are generally not union-represented. The Guild nevertheless negotiates for health and pension benefits, offers mentorship and diversity programmes, and campaigns against film piracy and unsafe working conditions.
