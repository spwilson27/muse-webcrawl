Film theory

Film theory is the academic discipline that explores the essence, aesthetics, ideology and social effects of cinema. Early formalist theorists such as Rudolf Arnheim and Sergei Eisenstein emphasised montage and form; realists such as André Bazin valued photography's indexical relation to reality.

Later approaches include auteur theory, psychoanalytic and feminist film theory (Laura Mulvey's "male gaze"), structuralism, apparatus theory, and cognitive film theory.
