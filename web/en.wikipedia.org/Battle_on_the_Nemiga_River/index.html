Battle on the Nemiga River

The Battle on the Nemiga River was fought on 3 March 1067 on the Niamiha River near Minsk between the armies of Kievan Rus' princes and Vseslav of Polotsk. It is the earliest known reference to Minsk in historical sources.

Date: 3 March 1067
Location: Niamiha River, near Minsk
Result: Defeat of Vseslav of Polotsk

Background

Vseslav Bryachislavich, Prince of Polotsk, had sacked Novgorod and expanded at the expense of neighbouring principalities. The Yaroslavichi — Iziaslav of Kiev, Sviatoslav of Chernigov and Vsevolod of Pereyaslavl — marched against him in winter.

Battle and aftermath

In deep snow, the allied princes defeated Vseslav, who fled. Minsk was captured and plundered. The princes later invited Vseslav to negotiations under a sworn safe-conduct, then seized him — an episode remembered in the Primary Chronicle and echoed in The Tale of Igor's Campaign, which evokes the bloody harvest on the Nemiga.
