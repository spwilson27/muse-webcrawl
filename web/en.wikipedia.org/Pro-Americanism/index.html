Pro-Americanism

Pro-Americanism (or pro-American sentiment) is support, admiration, or a favourable attitude toward the United States of America, its government, culture, policies, or people. It is the counterpart of anti-Americanism, and the two attitudes often coexist within the same societies.

Expressions

Pro-American attitudes have historically been associated with admiration for American democracy, economic opportunity, popular culture, and technological achievement, as well as strategic alignment during the Cold War and afterwards. Immigration to the United States and the appeal of the "American Dream" are frequently cited manifestations.

Measurement

International favourability toward the United States is tracked in surveys such as the Pew Research Center's Global Attitudes project, which shows sharp variation over time and between countries, often responding to U.S. foreign policy and presidential administrations.
