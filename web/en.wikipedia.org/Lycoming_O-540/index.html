The Lycoming O-540 is a family of air-cooled six-cylinder, horizontally opposed, fixed-wing aircraft engines of 541.5 cubic inches (8.9 L) displacement, made by Lycoming Engines. The engine is a six-cylinder version of the four-cylinder Lycoming O-360, sharing its cylinder bore of 5.125 in (130.2 mm) and stroke of 4.375 in (111.1 mm).

First certified in 1957, the O-540 family powers a wide range of high-performance single-engine and twin-engine aircraft, with normally aspirated variants producing 230 to 350 horsepower and turbocharged variants reaching 350 hp and above.

Design and development

The O-540 follows the standard Lycoming layout: a horizontally opposed, air-cooled engine with pushrod-operated overhead valves, dual magnetos and a wet sump. The family includes carburetted (O-540), fuel-injected (IO-540), aerobatic (AEIO-540), helicopter (HIO-540), geared (GO-540, GSO-540 and IGSO-540) and turbocharged (TIO-540 and LTIO-540) versions. Counter-rotating LIO-540 and LTIO-540 models were produced for twins such as the Piper PA-31 Navajo and the Aerostar.

The IO-540 is among the most popular high-power piston aircraft engines ever produced. Notable applications include the Piper PA-24 Comanche, PA-32 Cherokee Six/Saratoga family, the Cessna 182 Skylane (O-540-J3C5D in the restarted production 182S/T182T), the Socata TB-20 Trinidad, the Maule M-7, and the Extra 300 and other aerobatic aircraft in AEIO-540 form. The turbocharged TIO-540 series powers the Piper Malibu (early models) and the twin-engine Piper PA-31 and PA-34 variants.

Variants

  • O-540 — carburetted, 230–290 hp.
  • IO-540 — fuel-injected, 230–350 hp; the -K1A5 (260 hp) powered the Piper PA-32-300 and the -D4A5 (250 hp) the PA-24-250 Comanche.
  • AEIO-540 — aerobatic, 260–300 hp; powers the Extra 300 series and the XtremeAir Sbach.
  • TIO-540 — turbocharged, 310–350 hp; powers the Piper PA-31-350 Chieftain and PA-46-310P Malibu.
  • LTIO-540 — counter-rotating turbocharged version for twins.
  • GSO-540 / IGSO-540 — geared, supercharged versions used in the Aero Commander 680F and Dornier Do 28.
  • HIO-540 — helicopter versions.
