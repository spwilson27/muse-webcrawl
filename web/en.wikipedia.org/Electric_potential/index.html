Electric potential

Electric potential is the amount of electric potential energy per unit charge at a point in an electric field. Its SI unit is the volt (V), equal to one joule per coulomb.

The potential at a point is the work done per unit charge in bringing a test charge from a reference point (often infinity) to that point without acceleration. The difference in potential between two points is the voltage. For a point charge Q, the potential at distance r is V = Q / (4πε₀r).

Relation to the field

The electric field is the negative gradient of the potential, E = −∇V. Surfaces of constant potential are equipotential surfaces and are perpendicular to field lines. In electrostatics, conductors in equilibrium are equipotential volumes.
