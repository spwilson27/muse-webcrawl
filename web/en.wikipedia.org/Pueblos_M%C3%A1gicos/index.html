Pueblos Mágicos

The Pueblos Mágicos (Spanish for "Magical Towns") is a programme launched in 2001 by Mexico's Secretariat of Tourism (SECTUR) to recognize and promote towns that offer visitors a "magical" experience by reason of their natural beauty, cultural riches, traditions, folklore, historical relevance, cuisine, art and crafts, and hospitality.

Designation

Towns apply for designation by meeting criteria including a tourism development plan, a committee representing the community, and distinctive symbolic attributes. The first town designated was Huasca de Ocampo in Hidalgo. The list grew to more than 170 towns across all 31 states by the mid-2020s; well-known examples include Tequila, San Cristóbal de las Casas, Pátzcuaro, Tulum and Taxco.

Purpose

The programme aims to diversify Mexico's tourism offering beyond beach resorts, generate employment in rural communities, and encourage the preservation of local heritage.
