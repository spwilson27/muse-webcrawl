L0 Series

The L0 Series (Lゼロ系) is a high-speed maglev train developed by the Central Japan Railway Company (JR Central) for use on the Chūō Shinkansen, the maglev line under construction between Tokyo and Osaka via Nagoya.

Development and records

The L0 Series has been tested on the Yamanashi Maglev Test Line since 2013. On 21 April 2015 a seven-car L0 train set a world land speed record for rail vehicles of 603 km/h (375 mph); a week earlier it had reached 590 km/h and carried passengers at 502 km/h. The trains use superconducting magnets and run levitated within a U-shaped guideway, with a planned commercial operating speed of 500 km/h.

Design

The trains are recognisable by their very long nose, about 15 metres, designed to reduce the pressure wave ("tunnel boom") when exiting tunnels at high speed. Passenger services on the first section, between Shinagawa (Tokyo) and Nagoya, were originally planned for 2027, with the opening subsequently delayed.
