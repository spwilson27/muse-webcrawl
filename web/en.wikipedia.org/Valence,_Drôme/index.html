Valence is a commune in southeastern France, the prefecture of the Drôme department in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region. It lies on the east bank of the Rhône river, between Lyon and Avignon.

History

Known in Roman times as Valentia, the city became an important trading post on the Rhône. It later passed through the hands of local bishops and counts before joining the French crown in the 15th century. Napoleon Bonaparte trained at its artillery school as a young officer.

Landmarks

Sights include the Romanesque Saint-Apollinaire Cathedral, the Maison des Têtes with its carved façade, and the Champ de Mars esplanade with the Peynet bandstand. Valence is a centre for the transport and food-processing industries and a gateway to the Drôme provençale.
