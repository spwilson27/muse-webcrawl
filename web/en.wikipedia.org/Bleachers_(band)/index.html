Bleachers (band)

Bleachers performing live.

Bleachers is an American rock band from Bergen County, New Jersey, formed in 2013 as the solo project of singer-songwriter and record producer Jack Antonoff, previously of the bands Steel Train and Fun. The project became known for its anthemic, 1980s-influenced pop-rock sound and energetic live performances featuring a large touring band.

History

Bleachers' debut single "I Wanna Get Better" was released in February 2014 and topped the Billboard Alternative Songs chart. The first album, Strange Desire (2014), included collaborations with Grimes and Yoko Ono. Antonoff followed with Gone Now (2017), which reflected on his New Jersey upbringing and the death of his sister, and featured the single "Don't Take the Money," co-written with Lorde. Take the Sadness Out of Saturday Night (2021) included a collaboration with Bruce Springsteen, "Chinatown." A self-titled fourth album, Bleachers, appeared in 2024.

Alongside the band, Antonoff became one of the most in-demand pop producers of the era, working with Taylor Swift, Lorde, Lana Del Rey, and others, while Bleachers remained his primary vehicle for his own songwriting.
