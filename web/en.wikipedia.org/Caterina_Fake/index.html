Caterina Fake

Caterina Fake (born 1969) is an American entrepreneur and businesswoman best known as a co-founder of the photo-sharing website Flickr. With Stewart Butterfield and Jason Classon she co-founded the Vancouver company Ludicorp, whose online game project spawned Flickr in 2004; the site pioneered tagging, folksonomies and other features of the social web, and was acquired by Yahoo! in 2005.

Career

Fake later co-founded Hunch, a recommendation technology company acquired by eBay in 2011, and the startup Findery (later renamed Pinwheel), a location-based storytelling service. She has served on the board of Etsy, was chairman of its board, and has been a prominent angel investor and commentator on internet culture, online communities and the early social web.
