Mineral del Monte

Mineral del Monte, commonly called Real del Monte, is a small mining town and municipality in the state of Hidalgo, Mexico, located at high altitude about 12 km from the state capital, Pachuca.

Mining history

Silver and gold were mined here from the colonial era. In the 19th century, Cornish miners from England brought mining technology — and football and the paste (pasty) — to the region, working under companies such as the Real del Monte and Pachuca Mining Company. The mines later passed to American and then Mexican state ownership.

Today

The town, designated a Pueblo Mágico, features steep cobbled streets, the English Cemetery (Panteón Inglés) where Cornish miners are buried, mining museums, and shops selling pastes, a local culinary icon. Its football heritage is celebrated as the cradle of the sport in Mexico.
