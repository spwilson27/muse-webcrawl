John M. Thomas

Sir John Meurig Thomas (15 November 1932 – 13 November 2020) was a Welsh scientist, educator and university administrator, known for his work on heterogeneous catalysis, solid-state and materials chemistry.

John M. Thomas

Born: 15 November 1932, Tumble, Carmarthenshire
Died: 13 November 2020
Known for: solid catalysts, electron microscopy of solids

Career

Thomas taught at Bangor, Aberystwyth and University College London, directed the Royal Institution (1986–91) and was Master of Peterhouse, Cambridge. He pioneered using electron microscopy and spectroscopy to study active sites in zeolites and designed single-site heterogeneous catalysts, bridging surface science and industrial catalysis.
