The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania is the business school of the University of Pennsylvania, a private Ivy League university in Philadelphia. Founded in 1881 by a donation from industrialist Joseph Wharton, it is the world's first collegiate school of business and one of the most prestigious, consistently ranked at or near the top of global MBA rankings.

Programs

Wharton offers undergraduate (BS in Economics), MBA, executive MBA, and doctoral programs, with particular strength in finance, as well as marketing, management, real estate, and business analytics. Its campus centers on Jon M. Huntsman Hall (opened 2002), and it operates a San Francisco campus and facilities through the Penn Wharton China Center in Beijing. Notable alumni include Elon Musk, Donald Trump, Warren Buffett (who transferred to Nebraska), Sundar Pichai, and Anil Ambani. The school has about 5,000 students and more than 100,000 alumni worldwide.
