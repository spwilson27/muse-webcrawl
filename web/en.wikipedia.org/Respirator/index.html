Respirator

A respirator is a device designed to protect the wearer from inhaling hazardous atmospheres, including fumes, vapours, gases, and particulate matter such as dusts and airborne microorganisms. Respirators range from simple disposable filtering facepieces (such as N95 masks) to powered air-purifying respirators and self-contained breathing apparatus.

Types

Air-purifying respirators filter ambient air: particulate filters remove aerosols, while chemical cartridges adsorb specific gases and vapours. Atmosphere-supplying respirators deliver clean air from an external source or a carried supply and are used in immediately dangerous environments. Effectiveness depends on a proper seal; occupational programmes therefore require fit testing and training.

Regulation

In the United States, respirators are certified by NIOSH under 42 CFR Part 84, and workplace use is regulated by OSHA. During the COVID-19 pandemic, respirators such as N95s became widely used by healthcare workers and the public.
