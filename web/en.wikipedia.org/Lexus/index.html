Lexus

Lexus (Japanese: レクサス, Rekusasu) is the luxury vehicle division of the Japanese automaker Toyota Motor Corporation. The Lexus brand is marketed in more than 90 countries and territories worldwide and is Japan's largest-selling make of premium cars. It has ranked among the 10 largest Japanese global brands in market value. Lexus is headquartered in Nagoya, Japan, with operational centres in Brussels, Belgium, and Plano, Texas, United States. The marque was created in 1989 and launched in the United States with the flagship LS 400 sedan, which won wide acclaim for its refinement and build quality.

History

In 1983, Toyota chairman Eiji Toyoda issued a challenge to build the world's best luxury car — the secret "Circle F" project, which consumed some $1 billion and produced the LS 400. Launched in September 1989 in the US alongside the smaller ES 250, the LS 400 undercut German rivals on price while matching or exceeding them in quietness and quality, and Lexus quickly became the best-selling luxury import brand in America. The brand expanded into SUVs (the RX of 1998 essentially created the luxury crossover segment), performance cars (the IS F and the limited-production LFA supercar of 2010–12), and hybrids — Lexus pioneered luxury hybrid vehicles starting with the RX 400h in 2005 and has since electrified most of its range. Sales in Japan itself began only in 2005.

Models and design

The range includes the IS, ES, GS (discontinued) and LS sedans; the RC and LC coupés; UX, NX, RX, GX and LX crossovers and SUVs; and the LM luxury minivan. Design language since 2012 has centred on the "spindle grille" and L-finesse philosophy. Lexus vehicles are produced mainly in Japan (the Tahara plant is famed for its quality control and takumi master craftsmen), with additional production in Canada and the United States. The F marque denotes high-performance models, and Lexus also fields cars in motorsport, including Super GT and endurance racing.

Reputation

Lexus has repeatedly topped dependability and customer-satisfaction surveys, such as those of J.D. Power, and is known for quiet cabins, hybrid technology and dealer service. In 2023 Lexus sold a record of roughly 824,000 vehicles worldwide.
