Wizards of the Coast LLC (WotC) is an American publisher of games, primarily role-playing and collectible card games. Founded in 1990 by Peter Adkison, it achieved enormous success in 1993 with Magic: The Gathering, designed by Richard Garfield, which created the collectible card game genre.

History

In 1997 Wizards acquired TSR, Inc., gaining Dungeons & Dragons, which it relaunched with a third edition in 2000 under the Open Game License. It also published the Pokémon Trading Card Game outside Japan until 2003. Hasbro acquired the company in 1999 for about $325 million.

Modern era

The fifth edition of D&D (2014) and digital offerings such as Magic: The Gathering Arena and D&D Beyond drove major growth in the 2010s and 2020s; the film Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves followed in 2023. A proposed revision of the Open Game License in 2023 provoked strong backlash and was withdrawn.
