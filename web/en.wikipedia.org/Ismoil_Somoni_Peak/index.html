Ismoil Somoni Peak

Ismoil Somoni Peak, the highest mountain in Tajikistan
Elevation: 7,495 m (24,590 ft)
Range: Pamir Mountains
Location: Tajikistan
First ascent: 1933 (Yevgeniy Abalakov)

Ismoil Somoni Peak is the highest mountain in Tajikistan, rising to 7,495 metres (24,590 ft) in the Academy of Sciences Range of the Pamir Mountains, in the northwest of the Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region. It was the highest point of the former Soviet Union.

Names and history

The peak was originally identified in the late 19th century and long thought to have been surveyed as Mount Garmo. In 1932–1933 Soviet surveyors established that it was a distinct, higher summit, naming it Stalin Peak. It was first climbed in 1933 by the Soviet mountaineer Yevgeniy Abalakov. In 1962 the mountain was renamed Communism Peak (Pik Kommunizma), and in 1998, after Tajik independence, it was renamed in honour of Ismoil Somoni (Isma'il ibn Ahmad), the 9th–10th-century founder of the Samanid dynasty, regarded as a national hero of Tajikistan.

Climbing

The standard route ascends from the Fortambek Glacier via the "Big Barrier," and the mountain is typically climbed by experienced high-altitude mountaineers as one of the five former Soviet "Snow Leopard" peaks (7,000-metre summits of the USSR).
