LCROSS

The Lunar Crater Observation and Sensing Satellite (LCROSS) was a NASA robotic spacecraft operated to search for water ice in a permanently shadowed crater at the Moon's south pole. It was launched together with the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) on 18 June 2009.

Mission

On 9 October 2009, the spent Centaur upper stage that accompanied LCROSS was deliberately crashed into the crater Cabeus near the lunar south pole. The LCROSS shepherding spacecraft flew through the resulting debris plume, analysing it for water, and impacted the surface itself minutes later. Ground and space telescopes observed the impacts.

Results

Analysis of the plume confirmed the presence of water — roughly 155 kilograms estimated in the plume material — along with other volatile compounds, in the permanently shadowed regolith, a finding significant for future lunar exploration.
