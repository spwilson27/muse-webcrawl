List of election calendars by country

This is a list of election calendars by country, organised by the national election cycles of sovereign states. An election calendar records the scheduled dates of general, presidential, parliamentary, regional and local elections, by-elections and referendums in each country. The frequency of elections depends on each country's constitution and electoral system: fixed-term systems (such as the United States) hold elections on predictable cycles, while parliamentary systems (such as the United Kingdom or Canada) may call elections at variable intervals within a maximum term.
