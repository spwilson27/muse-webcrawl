The West Australian Football League (WAFL) is an Australian rules football league based in Perth, Western Australia. Founded in 1885 as the West Australian Football Association, it was the premier state competition for a century and remains a major second-tier league feeding players to the Australian Football League (AFL), in which Western Australia is represented by West Coast and Fremantle.

Clubs and history

Historic clubs include East Fremantle, West Perth, East Perth, South Fremantle, Perth, Swan Districts, Claremont, and Subiaco; Peel Thunder joined in 1997 and a West Coast reserves side in 2019. The WAFL produced greats such as Graham "Polly" Farmer, Barry Cable, and Stephen Michael, and its finals are played each September, with the grand final traditionally at major Perth stadiums. The league's champion player receives the Sandover Medal, and its leading goalkicker the Bernie Naylor Medal. A women's competition, the WAFLW, began in 2019.
