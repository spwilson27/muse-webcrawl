The Fund for Peace (FFP) is an American non-profit research and educational organisation based in Washington, D.C. Founded in 1957, it works to prevent violent conflict and promote sustainable security by providing data and analysis on state fragility and resilience.

Work

The Fund for Peace is best known for the Fragile States Index (formerly the Failed States Index), published annually since 2005 (initially in partnership with Foreign Policy magazine). The index ranks countries using the organisation's CAST (Conflict Assessment System Tool) framework across twelve social, economic, and political indicators. FFP also conducts conflict early-warning analysis, supports local peacebuilding partners, and advises governments and companies on operating in fragile environments.
