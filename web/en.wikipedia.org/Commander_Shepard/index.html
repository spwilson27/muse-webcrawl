Commander Shepard

Commander Shepard is the player character and main protagonist of the Mass Effect video game trilogy developed by BioWare. Shepard is a veteran soldier of the Systems Alliance Navy and the first human Spectre, an elite agent of the Citadel Council. The character's first name, gender, appearance, and morality are customisable by the player, and choices made across the games shape Shepard's story and the fate of the galaxy.

Character creation

Players may choose Shepard's gender, first name, military background, and psychological profile, which affect dialogue options and starting reputation. The default male Shepard is voiced by Mark Meer and modelled on Dutch model Mark Vanderloo; the default female Shepard is voiced by Jennifer Hale. Shepard can follow Paragon (heroic, diplomatic) or Renegade (ruthless, pragmatic) paths.

Role in the games

In Mass Effect (2007), Shepard uncovers the threat of the Reapers, a race of sentient machines that periodically harvest advanced organic life. In Mass Effect 2 (2010), Shepard is killed in an attack and resurrected by the human-supremacist organisation Cerberus to fight the Collectors. In Mass Effect 3 (2012), Shepard unites the galaxy's species for a final stand against the Reaper invasion of Earth and the wider galaxy.

Reception

Shepard has been widely praised as one of the greatest video game characters, with the female version in particular celebrated for Hale's performance. The character's blank-slate customisation and branching morality have been cited as milestones in role-playing game design.
