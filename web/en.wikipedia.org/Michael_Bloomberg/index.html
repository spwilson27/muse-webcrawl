Michael Rubens Bloomberg (born February 14, 1942) is an American businessman, politician and philanthropist. He co-founded the financial information and media company Bloomberg L.P. in 1981 and is its majority owner. Bloomberg served three terms as the 108th mayor of New York City, from 2002 to 2013.

Business career

After working at the investment bank Salomon Brothers, where he became a partner, Bloomberg used his partnership payout to found Innovative Market Systems, which sold computerised financial data terminals to Wall Street firms; the company grew into Bloomberg L.P., a global financial-data, software and media empire.

Mayor and politics

Elected mayor as a Republican in 2001 in the aftermath of the September 11 attacks, Bloomberg oversaw the city's economic recovery, a sharp fall in crime, rezoning and development, and public-health measures including a smoking ban. He later became an independent, then a Democrat, and briefly campaigned for the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination. Through Bloomberg Philanthropies he is a major donor on climate, public health and gun control, co-founding Everytown for Gun Safety.
