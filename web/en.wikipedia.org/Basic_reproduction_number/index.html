Basic reproduction number

In epidemiology, the basic reproduction number, denoted R₀, of an infection is the expected number of cases directly generated by one case in a population in which all individuals are susceptible to infection.

Interpretation

If R₀ is greater than 1, an outbreak can grow; if less than 1, it will die out. Values vary widely: measles is among the highest, influenza much lower.

Estimation

R₀ depends on contact rates, infectious period and transmission probability, and is estimated with mathematical models fitted to outbreak data.

Limitations

R₀ assumes a fully susceptible, well-mixed population; the effective reproduction number accounts for immunity and interventions.
