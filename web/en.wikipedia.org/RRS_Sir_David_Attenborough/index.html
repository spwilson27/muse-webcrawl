RRS Sir David Attenborough

The RRS Sir David Attenborough is one of the most advanced polar research vessels in the world.

RRS Sir David Attenborough is a research vessel owned by the Natural Environment Research Council and operated by the British Antarctic Survey for the purposes of both research and resupply to British Antarctic stations. The ship was built by Cammell Laird in Birkenhead and launched in 2018.

The vessel is named after the broadcaster and naturalist Sir David Attenborough. In 2016 an online poll to name the ship saw the joke name "Boaty McBoatface" receive the most votes; the name was instead given to one of the ship's remotely operated submersibles, while the ship itself received Attenborough's name — a decision that became a widely reported story in its own right.

Design and capabilities

The ship is a polar research vessel with icebreaking capability, able to break through ice up to about one metre thick at three knots. It carries a crew of around 28 and accommodation for up to 60 scientists and support staff, with a range of laboratories, a hangar and helipad, and a moon pool for deploying instruments. Its robotic submersible, Boaty McBoatface, can operate under ice and at great depths.

Service

After sea trials, the ship made its first voyage to Antarctica in 2021, resupplying British Antarctic Survey stations including Rothera Research Station. It supports science across the Southern Ocean and in the Arctic, studying ocean circulation, ice sheets, marine ecosystems and climate change.
