Sergey Brin

Sergey Mikhailovich Brin (Russian: Сергей Михайлович Брин; born August 21, 1973) is an American computer scientist and businessman who co-founded Google with Larry Page. He was the president of Google's parent company, Alphabet Inc., until stepping down in December 2019.

Early life and education

Brin was born in Moscow in the Soviet Union to a Jewish family and immigrated to the United States with his parents at the age of six, settling in Maryland. He studied computer science and mathematics at the University of Maryland and then pursued a PhD at Stanford University, where he met Page. Together they developed the PageRank algorithm and built the search engine that became Google, incorporated in 1998.

Career

As Google's president of technology and later head of the X research division ("moonshot factory"), Brin oversaw projects including Google Glass and self-driving cars. With Page he restructured Google under Alphabet in 2015. After resigning as Alphabet president in 2019 he remained a board member and controlling shareholder, and in the 2020s returned to active work on Google's artificial intelligence efforts.
