ISO 286

ISO 286 is an international standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) that defines a system of limits and fits for smooth cylindrical workpieces — the ISO system of limits and fits, often called the ISO fit system. It provides the basis for specifying tolerances on mating parts, principally shafts and holes, in mechanical engineering.

System

The standard defines standard tolerance grades designated IT01, IT0 and IT1 through IT18 (International Tolerance grades), which determine the magnitude of the tolerance for a given nominal size, and fundamental deviations identified by letters — capital letters A to ZC for holes and lowercase a to zc for shafts — which position the tolerance zone relative to the nominal (zero) line.

Designation

A fit is designated by the nominal size followed by the hole tolerance class and the shaft tolerance class, for example 50 H7/g6. Two bases are recognised: the hole-basis system, in which the hole deviation H is used (lower deviation zero), and the shaft-basis system, based on the shaft deviation h. Common fits include clearance fits (such as H7/g6), transition fits (such as H7/k6) and interference fits (such as H7/p6).

Parts of the standard

ISO 286 is issued in two parts: Part 1 gives the bases of tolerances, deviations and fits, and Part 2 gives tables of the standard tolerance classes and limit deviations. It descends from the earlier ISA system developed in the 1930s and remains the universal language for interchangeable manufacture.
