Kidron Valley

The Kidron Valley (Hebrew: נחל קדרון, Naḥal Qidron; Arabic: وادي الجوز, Wadi al-Joz in its upper section) is the valley on the eastern side of the Old City of Jerusalem, separating the Temple Mount and the City of David from the Mount of Olives. It continues eastward through the Judaean Desert toward the Dead Sea.

History and sites

The valley is mentioned repeatedly in the Bible: David crossed it fleeing Absalom, and Jesus crossed it on the way to Gethsemane. Its slopes contain ancient burial grounds, including the rock-cut tombs traditionally called Absalom's Pillar, the Tomb of Benei Hezir, and the Tomb of Zechariah. The lower valley, known as Wadi en-Nar, runs some 30 kilometres southeast to the Dead Sea, passing the Mar Saba monastery.

Because of its biblical associations, the Kidron is identified in Jewish, Christian, and Muslim tradition with the Valley of Josaphat, where the Last Judgment is expected to take place.
