Smart meter

A smart meter is an electronic device that records information — such as consumption of electric energy, voltage levels, current and power factor — and communicates it to the utility supplier for monitoring and billing. Unlike traditional electromechanical meters, which record only total consumption and are read manually, smart meters log usage at short intervals (typically every 15 to 60 minutes) and transmit the data automatically, often daily or in near real time, over radio-frequency mesh, cellular or power-line networks.

Benefits

Smart meters eliminate estimated bills and manual meter reading, let utilities detect outages and restore service faster, and support time-of-use tariffs that charge more at peak times, encouraging consumers to shift consumption. Interval data shown through in-home displays or apps can help households reduce usage, and smart metering is considered foundational to the smart grid, enabling integration of rooftop solar, electric vehicles and demand-response programs. Similar devices exist for natural gas and water, sometimes battery-powered and reporting less frequently.

Deployment

Large-scale rollouts began in Italy in the early 2000s with Enel's Telegestore system, and smart meters are now standard across much of Europe, North America, China and Oceania, often under government mandates — the European Union set targets for member states to deploy them where cost-effective, and Great Britain ran a supplier-led national rollout. Global installations number in the hundreds of millions.

Criticism and concerns

Smart meter programs have attracted criticism over cost overruns and whether consumer savings justify the expense; over privacy, because fine-grained consumption data can reveal household activities; over accuracy complaints in some deployments; and over health concerns about radio-frequency emissions, which regulators generally consider far below safety limits. Data governance rules increasingly restrict how utilities may share consumption data with third parties.
