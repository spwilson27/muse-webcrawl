edX

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

edX is an online learning platform created by Harvard University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Officially launched in 2012, it hosts online course-level classes in a wide range of disciplines to a worldwide student body, including some courses at no charge. edX is a provider of massive open online courses (MOOCs) and also conducts research in learning and education technology.

History

edX was founded in May 2012 by scientists from Harvard and MIT, building on the earlier MITx initiative and Harvard's experiments in online education. The venture was announced as a nonprofit platform backed by $60 million in funding from the two universities. It grew rapidly, adding partner institutions around the world and offering courses in computer science, engineering, humanities, business, data science, and many other fields. In 2021, edX was acquired by education technology company 2U for a reported $800 million, with the proceeds used to seed a new nonprofit foundation.

Course offerings

Courses on edX are structured much like university classes, with video lectures, quizzes, discussion forums, and graded assignments. Many courses can be taken free in an audit track, with paid verified certificates available. edX also offers MicroMasters programs, professional certificates, executive education, and full online degree programs in partnership with universities.

Research

Because of its origins as a university research project, edX has been a major site for academic study of how people learn online, contributing findings on engagement, retention, and instructional design in large-scale digital courses.
