Republic of West Florida
Capital: St. Francisville
Existed: 1810 (about 90 days)
Governor: Fulwar Skipwith
Flag: Bonnie Blue Flag

The Republic of West Florida was a short-lived republic in the region of present-day southeastern Louisiana and the Gulf Coast in 1810. Anglo-American settlers, dissatisfied with Spanish rule, revolted, seized the Spanish fort at Baton Rouge, and declared independence on September 23, 1810.

History

The republic adopted a constitution modelled on that of the United States and elected Fulwar Skipwith as governor. Its flag, a single white star on a blue field, later became famous as the "Bonnie Blue Flag".

Annexation

President James Madison claimed the territory as part of the Louisiana Purchase and ordered its occupation; the republic ended in December 1810 when U.S. forces took possession, and the area was incorporated into the Territory of Orleans.
