Rock Steady Crew

The Rock Steady Crew is an American breaking and hip hop group which has become a franchise name for multiple groups in other locations. The group was initially formed in the Bronx, New York City in 1977 by b-boys Jimmy Dee and Jimmy Lee. The Puerto Rican members were essential in the early development of breaking.

The Manhattan branch was created by Crazy Legs, B-Boy Fresh, and several other members. The Rock Steady Crew quickly gained national attention in 1981 when they performed in the Lincoln Center battle against the Dynamic Rockers, footage of which was broadcast nationally. They appeared in the films Wild Style (1982), Style Wars (1983), and Flashdance (1983), which brought breaking to a worldwide audience.

History

By 1983, the crew had signed a recording contract and released the single "(Hey You) The Rock Steady Crew", which became an international hit, reaching the top ten in several countries. Despite this success, the crew struggled with management problems and the waning commercial fad for breakdancing in the late 1980s. In the 1990s, under president Crazy Legs, the crew was revived with chapters around the world, and it began holding annual anniversary events in New York.

Legacy

The Rock Steady Crew is credited with popularizing b-boying/breaking globally and is regarded as one of the most influential crews in hip hop history. Its anniversary celebrations have become a major gathering for the international breaking community.

Members

Notable members have included Crazy Legs, Ken Swift, Mr. Wiggles, Frosty Freeze, Tony Touch, Doze Green, and Q-Unique.
