List of Bohemian monarchs

This is a list of Bohemian monarchs now also referred to as list of Czech monarchs who ruled as dukes and kings of Bohemia from the 9th century until 1918, first as part of the Přemyslid dynasty, then under the Luxembourg, Jagiellonian and Habsburg dynasties.

Dukes of Bohemia (Přemyslids)

Tradition traces the line to the semi-legendary Přemysl the Ploughman. Documented dukes begin with Bořivoj I (c. 867–889), the first historically verified duke. Saint Wenceslaus (Václav I) ruled around 921–935 and became Bohemia's patron saint. Boleslaus I and Boleslaus II consolidated the duchy in the 10th century.

Kings of Bohemia

Vratislaus II received a royal title in 1085, and Ottokar I secured hereditary kingship in 1198. Ottokar II (1253–1278) built a vast Central European realm before falling at the Battle on the Marchfeld. Charles IV, King of Bohemia and Holy Roman Emperor, founded Charles University in Prague in 1348. The last native dynasty was followed by the Luxembourgs, the Hussite-era George of Poděbrady, the Jagiellonians, and from 1526 the Habsburgs, who held the crown until Charles I in 1918.
