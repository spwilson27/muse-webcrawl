Beer in Belgium

Notable stylesLambic, Trappist ales, Flemish red, saison, witbier, dubbel, tripel
Trappist breweriesSix in Belgium (incl. Westvleteren, Chimay, Orval, Rochefort, Westmalle, Achel)
RecognitionBelgian beer culture inscribed by UNESCO (2016)

Beer in Belgium encompasses one of the world's most diverse brewing traditions, with hundreds of breweries producing well over a thousand distinct beers, many in styles found nowhere else. In 2016 UNESCO added Belgian beer culture to its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Styles

Signature styles include spontaneously fermented lambic and its fruit variant kriek; strong abbey and Trappist ales such as dubbel, tripel, and quadrupel; sour Flemish red and oud bruin ales; farmhouse saisons; and spiced wheat witbier. Each beer is traditionally served in its own distinctive glass.

Trappist beer

Only monasteries meeting strict criteria may use the "Authentic Trappist Product" label; six of the world's Trappist breweries are Belgian, and Westvleteren 12 is frequently rated among the finest beers in the world.
