Spanakopita

CourseAppetiser, main
Place of originGreece
Main ingredientsFilo pastry, spinach, feta cheese
Serving temperatureHot or room temperature

Spanakopita is a Greek savoury pastry made of layers of filo dough filled with spinach and cheese, typically feta, sometimes combined with ricotta or other cheeses, along with eggs, onions, and herbs such as dill and parsley.

Preparation

The spinach is wilted or used raw, mixed with crumbled cheese and beaten egg, and layered between sheets of filo brushed with olive oil or melted butter. The pie is baked until golden and crisp, either in a large pan and cut into portions or folded into individual triangles.

Variations

Regional versions may include leeks, chard, or sorrel, and a similar dish, hortopita, uses mixed wild greens. Spanakopita is a staple of bakeries throughout Greece and the Greek diaspora and is eaten for breakfast, as a snack, or as part of a mezze spread.
