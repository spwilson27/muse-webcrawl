Las Vegas Sands Corp. is an American casino and resort operating company based in Paradise, Nevada. Founded by Sheldon Adelson in 1988, it pioneered the integrated resort model combining casinos with convention and exhibition space, and became the world's largest gaming company by revenue, driven by its properties in Macau and Singapore.

Properties

The company's flagship resorts include Marina Bay Sands in Singapore and, through Sands China, The Venetian Macao, The Parisian Macao and The Londoner Macao. In 2022 it sold its Las Vegas properties, including The Venetian Resort, to focus on Asia.

History

Adelson bought the Sands Hotel and Casino in 1988, replaced it with The Venetian in 1999, and expanded into Macau after gaming liberalisation in 2002. After Adelson's death in 2021, Robert Goldstein became chairman and CEO.
