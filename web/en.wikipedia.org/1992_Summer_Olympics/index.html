The 1992 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XXV Olympiad, were held in Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain, from 25 July to 9 August 1992. They were the first Games held after the end of the Cold War, and the first since 1972 with no significant boycott.

Highlights

The opening ceremony climaxed with Paralympic archer Antonio Rebollo lighting the Olympic cauldron by firing a flaming arrow over it. The United States men's basketball team — the "Dream Team", the first US Olympic team to feature active NBA players — dominated the tournament and is widely regarded as the greatest basketball team ever assembled.

Following the dissolution of the Soviet Union, the Baltic states competed independently for the first time since 1936, while the other former Soviet republics competed together as the Unified Team, which topped the medal table. South Africa returned to the Games after the end of apartheid, and a reunified Germany competed for the first time since 1964.

Legacy

The Games drove a sweeping regeneration of Barcelona's waterfront and infrastructure and are frequently cited as a model of Olympic urban transformation. The main stadium, the Estadi Olímpic on Montjuïc, had originally been built for the 1929 International Exposition.
