Ludwig Alois Friedrich Ritter von Köchel (1800–1877) was an Austrian musicologist, writer, composer, botanist and mineralogist. He is best remembered for compiling the Köchel catalogue, the standard chronological catalogue of the works of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, first published in 1862.

The Köchel catalogue

Köchel's Chronologisch-thematisches Verzeichniss sämmtlicher Tonwerke W. A. Mozart's assigned each Mozart work a number — the "K." numbers still used today, such as K. 550 for the Symphony No. 40. He also funded and helped prepare the first complete edition of Mozart's works. Beyond music, Köchel published studies in botany and mineralogy and served as tutor to the sons of Archduke Charles of Austria.
