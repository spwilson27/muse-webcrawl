Cholangiocarcinoma

Cholangiocarcinoma, or bile duct cancer, is a malignant tumour arising from the epithelial cells of the bile ducts inside and outside the liver.

Classification and risk factors

It is classified as intrahepatic, perihilar (Klatskin tumour) or distal. Risk factors include primary sclerosing cholangitis, bile duct cysts, chronic liver disease and, in Southeast Asia, infection with the liver flukes Opisthorchis viverrini and Clonorchis sinensis.

Signs and treatment

Symptoms include painless jaundice, weight loss, itching and abdominal pain. Diagnosis uses imaging, CA 19-9 and biopsy. Surgical resection offers the only chance of cure; chemotherapy regimens such as gemcitabine with cisplatin are used for advanced disease, whose prognosis remains poor.
