Origin (service)

Origin
Developer: Electronic Arts
Launched: 3 June 2011
Discontinued: 2022–2025 (replaced by the EA app)
Platform: Windows, macOS (formerly)
Type: Digital distribution, DRM, social networking

Origin was a digital distribution platform developed by Electronic Arts for purchasing and playing video games. Launched in June 2011, evolving from the earlier EA Download Manager, it provided downloadable games, social features such as profile management, in-game overlay chat, and the Origin Access subscription service (later renamed EA Play).

Features and history

Origin required players of EA's PC titles to use its client and account system, a policy that initially drew criticism, including the removal of games such as Crysis 2 from rival storefront Steam in a dispute over downloadable content. The platform offered cloud saves, automatic patching, achievements and a game library shared with EA's console and mobile offerings.

Replacement by the EA app

In 2020 EA announced Origin would be retired in favour of a new desktop client, the EA app. The EA app launched for Windows in October 2022, and the macOS version followed, with Origin for Mac finally shut down in April 2025 as Apple ended support for 32-bit applications.
