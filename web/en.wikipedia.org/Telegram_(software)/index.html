Telegram Messenger is a cloud-based instant messaging service founded by brothers Nikolai and Pavel Durov and launched in 2013. It offers encrypted chats, voice and video calls, large group chats and public channels, file sharing of up to several gigabytes, and a bot platform, across mobile, desktop and web clients.

Features

Ordinary chats are stored in Telegram's cloud, allowing seamless use across devices; optional "Secret Chats" use end-to-end encryption with the MTProto protocol. Channels can broadcast to unlimited subscribers and groups can hold up to 200,000 members, making Telegram influential as a news and community platform. Telegram reported more than 900 million monthly active users in 2024.

History and controversies

The Durov brothers previously founded the Russian social network VK. Headquartered nominally in Dubai, Telegram has been blocked at times in Russia, Iran and other countries, and has faced scrutiny over extremist content, misinformation and its moderation practices, as well as Pavel Durov's arrest in France in 2024 in connection with alleged failures to curb criminal activity on the platform.
