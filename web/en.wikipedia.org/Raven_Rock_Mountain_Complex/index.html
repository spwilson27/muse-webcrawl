The Raven Rock Mountain Complex (RRMC), also known as Site R, is a United States military installation buried within Raven Rock Mountain near Blue Ridge Summit, Pennsylvania, close to the Maryland state line and Camp David. Built during the early Cold War, it serves as an alternate command centre — often described as an "underground Pentagon" — designed to keep the U.S. military command functioning after a nuclear attack.

History

Construction began in 1951 and the facility became operational in 1953–1954. Excavated into greenstone rock, the complex contains multi-storey buildings inside the mountain, with its own power plant, water reservoirs, communications links, and food storage sufficient for prolonged operations. During the Cold War it housed the Alternate National Military Command Center.

Role

Site R supports continuity of operations for the Department of Defense and the military services. Following the September 11 attacks in 2001, senior officials including Vice President Dick Cheney were moved to secure locations, and Raven Rock was publicly reported as one of the undisclosed sites used. The facility remains active and has undergone modernisation of its communications and infrastructure.
