Kingstown, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

Kingstown is the capital, chief port and main commercial centre of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, located on the south-west coast of the island of Saint Vincent in the Caribbean.

Overview

Founded by French colonists around 1722, Kingstown came under British rule after 1763. The town has a population of around 12,000–13,000 and is sometimes called the "City of Arches" for its stone archways. It exports bananas, arrowroot and coconuts.

Sights

Landmarks include St George's Anglican Cathedral, St Mary's Catholic Cathedral, the Kingstown Market and the nearby Botanical Gardens, among the oldest in the Western Hemisphere, founded in 1765. Argyle International Airport serves the country from east of the capital.
