Half-Life: Alyx

Half-Life: Alyx is a 2020 virtual reality (VR) first-person shooter game developed and published by Valve. It is the third main game in the Half-Life series. Players control Alyx Vance on a mission to seize a superweapon belonging to the alien Combine. Like previous Half-Life games, Alyx incorporates combat, puzzles, exploration, and narrative elements, played from VR with motion controllers supporting actions such as manipulating objects with "gravity gloves".

Plot and development

Set five years before Half-Life 2 (2004), the story follows Alyx and her father Eli Vance in occupied City 17 as they learn of a floating Combine vault believed to hold a superweapon. Valve developed the game using the Source 2 engine, building it exclusively for VR after experiments convinced the team that VR was the right vehicle for reviving the series. It was released for Windows on March 23, 2020, and supports the Valve Index, HTC Vive, Oculus Rift, and Windows Mixed Reality headsets.

Reception

Half-Life: Alyx received universal acclaim, with reviewers praising its graphics, voice acting, writing, and atmosphere, and many calling it VR's first "killer app". It won Best VR/AR Game at The Game Awards 2020 and numerous other awards. Sales of Valve Index headsets surged following its announcement, and the game's ending, which revises the conclusion of Half-Life 2: Episode Two, reignited interest in a continuation of the series.
