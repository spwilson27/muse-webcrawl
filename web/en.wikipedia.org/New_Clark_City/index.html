New Clark City is a planned smart city under development in Capas and Bamban, Tarlac, Philippines, within the Clark Special Economic Zone. Developed by the Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA), the project is intended to decongest Metro Manila and serve as a new administrative and economic centre.

Development

The 9,450-hectare development is planned to eventually house up to 1.2 million residents. Its first phase included the National Government Administrative Center, with government offices designed as a backup hub, and a sports complex built for the 2019 Southeast Asian Games, which featured the 20,000-seat New Clark City Athletics Stadium and an aquatics centre.

Transport

The city is planned to be connected to Clark International Airport and Metro Manila by road expressways and the North–South Commuter Railway.
