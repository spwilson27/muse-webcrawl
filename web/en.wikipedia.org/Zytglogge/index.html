Zytglogge

Image placeholder: The Zytglogge tower in Bern

The Zytglogge is a landmark medieval tower in Bern, Switzerland. Built around 1218–1220, it served as the gate tower of Bern's first western fortifications; it has also been a guard tower, a prison, and since the 15th century a clock tower.

Clock

Its name derives from the Bernese German for "time bell". The tower's astronomical clock, with its moving figures — including a jester, bears, and Chronos — was installed in its present form in 1530, with the mechanism built by Kaspar Brunner. A manual winding raising of weights powers the clock.

Present day

The Zytglogge stands at the head of the Kramgasse in Bern's Old City, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and remains a symbol of the city; trams pass beneath its arch.
