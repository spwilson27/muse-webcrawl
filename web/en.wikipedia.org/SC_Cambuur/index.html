SC Cambuur

SC Cambuur (Dutch pronunciation: [ɛsˈseː ˈkɑmbyːr]), commonly known as Cambuur Leeuwarden or simply Cambuur, is a Dutch professional football club based in Leeuwarden, Friesland. The club was founded on 19 June 1964 and is named after the Cambuur district of the city. Its colours are yellow shirts and blue shorts.

The Cambuur Stadion in Leeuwarden, the club's longtime home.

History

Cambuur was formed in 1964 from the professional section of VV Leeuwarden. The club has spent most of its history alternating between the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football, and the second-tier Eerste Divisie, which it has won three times (1991–92 as champions via play-offs, 2012–13 and 2020–21 outright). In the 2020–21 season Cambuur stormed to the Eerste Divisie title under coach Henk de Jong, and in 2021–22 achieved its highest-ever Eredivisie finish, ninth place. The club was relegated in 2023 and again struggled after a brief return.

Stadium

For most of its history the club played at the Cambuur Stadion, capacity about 10,500, known for its close, steep stands and passionate atmosphere. In 2024 the club moved to the new Kooi Stadion, a modern arena of roughly 15,000 seats built on the site of the former WTC Expo grounds.

Rivalries

Cambuur's fiercest rival is SC Heerenveen, the other major Frisian club; meetings between the two are known as the Derby van het Noorden (Derby of the North). The club also has rivalries with FC Groningen and PEC Zwolle.
