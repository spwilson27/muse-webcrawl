Adjunct professor

An adjunct professor is a type of academic appointment in higher education, most common in North America. Adjuncts are typically hired on a part-time, contract basis to teach specific courses, without tenure and often without the research and service duties of full-time faculty.

Role and conditions

Adjuncts may be practising professionals who teach in their field, or academics holding multiple part-time posts. Their employment is usually semester-to-semester, with pay per course and limited benefits — conditions that have drawn criticism and labour-organising efforts within universities.

Variations

In some countries and institutions, "adjunct professor" is instead an honorary or part-time title for a distinguished scholar or clinician affiliated with a department.
