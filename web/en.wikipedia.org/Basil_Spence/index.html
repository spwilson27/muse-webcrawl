Basil Spence

Sir Basil Urwin Spence (1907–1976) was a Scottish architect, most associated with Coventry Cathedral, the new cathedral built after the wartime destruction of the old one.

Career

Born in Bombay and educated in Scotland, Spence designed country houses, exhibition pavilions, universities and churches in a modernist idiom tempered by tradition.

Major works

His works include Coventry Cathedral (consecrated 1962), the University of Sussex campus, and the British Embassy in Rome.

Legacy

Spence was knighted and served as president of the Royal Institute of British Architects.
