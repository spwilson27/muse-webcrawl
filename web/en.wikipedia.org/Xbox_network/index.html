Xbox network

Logo of the Xbox network

The Xbox network, formerly known as Xbox Live, is an online multiplayer gaming and digital media delivery service created and operated by Microsoft for use with Xbox consoles, Windows PCs, and other platforms. It was first made available to the original Xbox console on November 15, 2002. An updated version of the service, adding the Xbox Live Marketplace, became available with the Xbox 360 console in November 2005, and a further enhanced version was released in 2013 with the Xbox One. The service was rebranded from Xbox Live to Xbox network in 2021.

The service allows users to play games online, download games and game demos through the Microsoft Store, stream media, and communicate with friends through voice and text chat. Access to online multiplayer on consoles is provided through the paid Xbox Game Pass Core subscription (formerly Xbox Live Gold), while features such as the friends list, messaging, and media applications are available to free account holders.

History

Microsoft launched Xbox Live in 2002, distinguishing it from competing services by requiring a broadband connection and providing a unified identity — the Gamertag — along with a standardized friends list and voice chat in every title. The service grew substantially with the Xbox 360, introducing the Gamerscore achievement system, the Xbox Live Arcade for downloadable games, and video streaming applications. In March 2021, Microsoft rebranded the service as the Xbox network to distinguish the underlying online infrastructure from the Xbox Live Gold subscription membership.

Features

Core features include the Gamertag user profile, Gamerscore earned through in-game achievements, downloadable content through the Microsoft Store, online multiplayer matchmaking, party chat, and cloud saves. Subscriptions under the Xbox Game Pass family add a rotating library of games and, on the higher tiers, access to EA Play and cloud gaming.

Availability

The Xbox network is available in dozens of countries and has tens of millions of monthly active users, spanning the Xbox Series X/S and Xbox One consoles, Windows 10 and Windows 11 PCs, and mobile devices via cloud streaming.
