List of Billboard Hot 100 number ones

The Billboard Hot 100 is the music industry standard record chart in the United States for songs, published weekly by Billboard magazine. Chart rankings are based on sales (physical and digital), radio airplay, and online streaming in the United States. The chart was launched on August 4, 1958, when "Poor Little Fool" by Ricky Nelson became its first number-one song.

Overview

More than 1,100 different songs have topped the Hot 100 since its inception. Each annual list compiles the number-one singles of that year, along with the number of weeks each spent at the summit. The longest-running number one of all time is "Old Town Road" by Lil Nas X featuring Billy Ray Cyrus, which held the top spot for a record 19 weeks in 2019.
