List of multiple-system operators

[Image: Cable television headend equipment]
A cable headend, the technical hub of an MSO's network.

A multiple-system operator (MSO) is an operator of multiple cable or direct-broadcast satellite television systems. The term is used mainly in North America, where a small number of large companies serve most subscribers. This list ranks major MSOs by approximate subscriber numbers.

Consolidation since the 1990s has steadily reduced the number of independent MSOs, and the industry increasingly competes with streaming video services delivered over broadband.
