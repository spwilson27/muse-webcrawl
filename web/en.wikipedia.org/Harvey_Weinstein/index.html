Harvey Weinstein

Harvey Weinstein (born March 19, 1952) is an American former film producer. He and his brother Bob Weinstein co-founded the entertainment company Miramax, which produced several successful independent films, including Sex, Lies, and Videotape (1989), The Crying Game (1992), Pulp Fiction (1994), Heavenly Creatures (1994), Il Postino (1994), and Shakespeare in Love (1998). Weinstein won an Academy Award for producing Shakespeare in Love and also won seven Tony Awards for plays and musicals. He was dismissed from his company, The Weinstein Company, in October 2017 following sexual abuse allegations.

Early life and career

Born: March 19, 1952, New York City, U.S.

Occupation: Film producer (former)

Years active: 1979–2017

Known for: Co-founding Miramax and The Weinstein Company

Weinstein grew up in Queens, New York, and studied at the State University of New York at Buffalo. In the 1970s he promoted rock concerts in Buffalo with his brother Bob and Corky Burger under the name Harvey & Corky Productions. In 1979 the brothers founded Miramax Films, named after their parents Miriam and Max. Acquired by Disney in 1993, Miramax became the dominant force in American independent cinema, pioneering aggressive Oscar campaigning and wide releases of arthouse films.

The brothers left Miramax in 2005 to form The Weinstein Company, which produced films including The King's Speech (2010) and The Artist (2011), both Academy Award winners for Best Picture.

Allegations, trials and convictions

In October 2017, investigative reports by The New York Times and The New Yorker detailed allegations of sexual harassment and assault against Weinstein spanning decades, made by numerous women. The revelations were a catalyst for the worldwide #MeToo movement. Weinstein was arrested in New York in 2018 and charged with rape and other offences. In February 2020, a New York jury convicted him of rape in the third degree and a criminal sexual act in the first degree; he was sentenced to 23 years in prison. That New York conviction was overturned on appeal in April 2024 on procedural grounds concerning the admission of testimony, and a retrial followed; in June 2025 the retrial jury convicted him on one count of a criminal sexual act while acquitting him on another and failing to reach a verdict on a rape charge.

Separately, in December 2022, a Los Angeles jury convicted Weinstein of rape and other sexual offences, and he was sentenced to 16 years in prison, to run consecutively. He has denied all allegations of non-consensual sex. Weinstein has been incarcerated at Rikers Island in New York while proceedings continue.
