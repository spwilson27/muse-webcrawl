The French Wikipedia (Wikipédia en français) is the French-language edition of Wikipedia, launched in March 2001, shortly after the English and German editions. With well over two and a half million articles it is among the largest Wikipedia editions and one of the most visited websites in France and the Francophone world.

History

The edition became notable in 2013 when France's domestic intelligence agency, the DCRI, pressured a volunteer administrator to delete an article about the Pierre-sur-Haute military radio station, claiming it contained classified information; the article was restored by the community and became famous through the Streisand effect, turning into one of the most viewed French articles.

Characteristics

French Wikipedia has an active community in France, Belgium, Switzerland, Quebec and Francophone Africa, with strong coverage of French history, communes and culture. Wikimedia France supports outreach and partnerships with cultural institutions.
