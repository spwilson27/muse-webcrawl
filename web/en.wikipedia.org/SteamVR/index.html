SteamVR provides room-scale virtual reality on PC.

SteamVR is a virtual reality platform and software runtime developed by Valve Corporation, distributed through the Steam digital store. It provides a common interface between VR applications and a wide range of headsets, supporting room-scale tracking in which users physically walk around a play area.

History

Valve publicly demonstrated its VR research at Steam Dev Days in 2014 and announced SteamVR together with the HTC Vive, developed in partnership with HTC, at the Game Developers Conference in 2015. The Vive launched commercially in April 2016. Valve later released its own headset, the Valve Index, in 2019, along with updated "Lighthouse" 2.0 base stations.

Technology

SteamVR's Lighthouse tracking uses base stations that sweep infrared laser planes across the room; photosensors on the headset and controllers compute their positions with millimetre precision. The runtime provides the Chaperone safety system, which shows play-area boundaries, a compositor that reprojects frames to maintain smooth motion, and the OpenVR API allowing games to run on headsets from many manufacturers. SteamVR Home offers a social virtual environment, and SteamVR Input lets users remap controller bindings.
