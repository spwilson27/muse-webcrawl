Optus

Singtel Optus Pty Limited, trading as Optus, is an Australian telecommunications company headquartered in Macquarie Park, Sydney. It is a wholly owned subsidiary of Singapore Telecommunications (Singtel) and the second-largest telecommunications company in Australia, providing mobile, fixed-line, broadband and satellite services.

History

Optus traces its origins to Aussat, the government satellite company founded in 1981. It was selected in 1991 as Australia's second general telecommunications carrier, breaking the monopoly of Telecom Australia (later Telstra), and launched services in 1992. Singtel acquired the company in 2001. Optus also operates a fleet of communications satellites used across Australia and New Zealand.

Services

The company sells mobile services on its own 4G and 5G networks, National Broadband Network (NBN) plans, home wireless broadband, and entertainment products such as Optus Sport, which held Australian broadcast rights to the English Premier League. It serves consumer, business, enterprise and wholesale customers.

Incidents

In September 2022 Optus suffered a major cyberattack exposing the personal data of up to 10 million customers, prompting national debate about data retention. In November 2023 a nationwide network outage lasting most of a day disrupted millions of customers and emergency calls, leading to the resignation of its chief executive.
