Grenfell Campus is a campus of Memorial University of Newfoundland located in Corner Brook, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It is a small, primarily undergraduate liberal arts and sciences campus with about 1,300 students, known for programmes in fine arts, environmental studies and science.

History

The campus opened in 1975 as a regional college of Memorial University and was named Sir Wilfred Grenfell College in honour of the medical missionary Sir Wilfred Grenfell, who worked in northern Newfoundland and Labrador. In 2010 it was renamed Grenfell Campus, Memorial University of Newfoundland, as part of a rebranding to align the institution more closely with the university while retaining its distinct identity in western Newfoundland.

Academics

Grenfell offers undergraduate degrees in arts (including English, history, psychology, social/cultural studies and theatre), science (including environmental science, general science and computational mathematics), resource management, nursing (in partnership with the Western Regional School of Nursing), and fine arts degrees in theatre and visual arts that are unique in the province. It also offers master's programmes in environmental policy and boreal ecosystems and agricultural management. Research institutes on campus include the Environmental Policy Institute and the Bonne Bay Marine Station in Norris Point, in Gros Morne National Park.

Campus

The campus overlooks the Bay of Islands from a wooded hillside in Corner Brook. Facilities include the Arts and Science Building, the Fine Arts Building, residences, the Ferriss Hodgett Library, and the Grenfell Art Gallery. The campus hosts the Gros Morne Summer Music festival and is home to the Newfoundland Insectarium nearby at Reidville.
