Georg Wittig

Georg Wittig (16 June 1897 – 26 August 1987) was a German chemist who reported a method for synthesizing alkenes from aldehydes and ketones using compounds called phosphonium ylides in the reaction now known as the Wittig reaction. He shared the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1979 with Herbert C. Brown for their work on organic synthesis.

Life and work

Born in Berlin, Wittig studied at the University of Tübingen and Marburg, served in World War I, and held posts at Braunschweig, Freiburg, Tübingen, and from 1956 Heidelberg. Beyond the Wittig reaction (published in 1954), he contributed to the chemistry of organolithium compounds, the 1,2-Wittig rearrangement, and aryne (dehydrobenzene) chemistry.
