Richard Bean

Richard Anthony Bean (born 11 June 1956) is an English playwright. Known for his comedy and political satire, he is best known for One Man, Two Guvnors (2011), an adaptation of Carlo Goldoni's The Servant of Two Masters, which transferred to the West End and Broadway and starred James Corden.

Early life and career

Bean was born in Hull, East Yorkshire. He studied social psychology at Loughborough University and worked as an occupational psychologist before writing stand-up comedy and plays. His early work was staged at the Hull Truck Theatre.

Plays

His works include Under the Whaleback (2003), Harvest (2005), England People Very Nice (2009) at the National Theatre, The Heretic (2011) at the Royal Court, Great Britain (2014), and Jack Absolute Flies Again (2022), co-written with Oliver Chris. Many of his plays address British identity, immigration, and the press.

Awards

Bean has won Evening Standard and Critics' Circle awards, and One Man, Two Guvnors won the Evening Standard Award for Best Play.
