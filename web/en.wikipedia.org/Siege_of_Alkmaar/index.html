Siege of Alkmaar

Date: 21 August – 8 October 1573
Location: Alkmaar, Netherlands
Result: Dutch victory

The siege of Alkmaar (1573) was a turning point in the Eighty Years' War, in which the Dutch city of Alkmaar withstood a siege by Spanish forces under Don Fadrique, son of the Duke of Alba.

Siege

After Spanish victories at Haarlem, the army advanced on Alkmaar in August 1573. The defenders, townspeople and a small garrison, repelled assaults. On the advice associated with William of Orange, the dikes were opened, flooding the polders around Spanish positions.

Outcome

The Spanish lifted the siege on 8 October 1573 — Alkmaar's first Dutch victory of the war, still celebrated locally as Alkmaar Ontzet. The saying 'victory begins at Alkmaar' (bij Alkmaar begint de victorie) dates from this event.
