Telecommunications

Telecommunications is the industry and technology sector concerned with communicating over distance by electrical and electromagnetic means, encompassing telephone, mobile, broadcasting, data and internet services. It is one of the largest industries in the world economy and a key enabling infrastructure for nearly all other sectors.

Sector structure

The industry comprises network operators (incumbent carriers and mobile network operators), equipment manufacturers such as Ericsson, Nokia, Huawei and Cisco, internet service providers, and satellite and cable companies. Many national markets were historically state monopolies — such as the Bell System in the United States — and were liberalised and privatised from the 1980s onward, creating competitive markets overseen by national regulators.

Technology generations

Mobile telecommunications have advanced through successive generations: analogue 1G in the 1980s, digital GSM-based 2G, 3G with mobile data, 4G LTE broadband, and 5G networks rolled out from 2019, offering higher speeds and lower latency. Fixed networks have shifted from copper telephone lines to fibre-optic broadband.
