AirFuel Alliance

The AirFuel Alliance is an industry consortium promoting wireless power transfer standards for charging electronic devices. It was formed in 2015 by the merger of the Alliance for Wireless Power (A4WP) and the Power Matters Alliance (PMA).

Technology

The alliance championed two approaches: inductive charging based on the PMA standard, deployed in some coffee shops and airports, and resonant inductive coupling under the Rezence brand, which allows charging at greater distances and through surfaces.

Competition

AirFuel competed with the Wireless Power Consortium's Qi standard, which was adopted by most major smartphone makers. As Qi became dominant, AirFuel shifted its focus toward resonant and RF-based charging for applications such as laptops, wearables and infrastructure.
