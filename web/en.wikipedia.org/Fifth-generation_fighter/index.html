Fifth-generation fighter

A fifth-generation fighter is a jet fighter classification used since the 1990s for aircraft incorporating stealth technology, advanced avionics, sensor fusion, supercruise and networked data integration.

Examples include the American F-22 Raptor and F-35 Lightning II, China's Chengdu J-20, and Russia's Sukhoi Su-57. Definitions vary, but low observability and situational awareness are defining traits distinguishing them from fourth-generation designs.
