Claudia Sheinbaum

Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo (born 24 June 1962) is a Mexican politician, scientist, and academic serving as the 66th president of Mexico since 1 October 2024, the first woman and the first person of Jewish descent to hold the office. A member of the National Regeneration Movement (Morena), she previously served as Head of Government of Mexico City from 2018 to 2023.

Early life and scientific career

Sheinbaum was born in Mexico City to a family of scientists; her mother is a biologist and her father a chemical engineer. She studied physics at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), earning a doctorate in energy engineering. Her research focused on energy consumption and climate change mitigation, and she contributed to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC), which shared the Nobel Peace Prize in 2007.

Political career

Sheinbaum served as Secretary of the Environment of Mexico City under Andrés Manuel López Obrador from 2000 to 2006, and as mayor of the Tlalpan borough from 2015 to 2017. In 2018 she was elected Head of Government of Mexico City, where her administration emphasised public transport, security cameras, and environmental programmes. Chosen as Morena's candidate for the 2024 general election, she won with nearly 60 percent of the vote, campaigning on continuity with López Obrador's "Fourth Transformation" while promising advances in renewable energy and a science-based government.

Presidency

Sheinbaum took office on 1 October 2024. Her government has pursued judicial and social reforms begun under her predecessor, expanded social programmes, and faced challenges including public security and relations with the United States.
