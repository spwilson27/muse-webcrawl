Greater Manchester is a metropolitan and ceremonial county in North West England. It was created on 1 April 1974 under the Local Government Act 1972 and comprises ten metropolitan boroughs: Bolton, Bury, Manchester, Oldham, Rochdale, Salford, Stockport, Tameside, Trafford, and Wigan.

Geography and economy

The county covers 493 square miles and is centered on the city of Manchester, historically the heart of the world's cotton textile industry during the Industrial Revolution. Today its economy is dominated by services, media, finance, higher education, and sport; MediaCityUK in Salford is a major broadcasting hub, and Manchester Airport is the busiest UK airport outside London.

Governance

Since 2011, strategic functions have been coordinated by the Greater Manchester Combined Authority. In 2017, Andy Burnham was elected as the first Mayor of Greater Manchester under a devolution deal giving the region greater control over transport, housing, and health spending. Transport is branded under the Bee Network, an integrated public transport system.
