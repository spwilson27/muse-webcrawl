List of British monarchs

There have been 13 British monarchs since the political union of the Kingdom of England and the Kingdom of Scotland on 1 May 1707. England and Scotland had been in personal union under the same monarchs since 24 March 1603, when James VI of Scotland inherited the English throne on the death of Elizabeth I. The present monarch, Charles III, ascended the throne on 8 September 2022 on the death of his mother, Elizabeth II, the longest-reigning British monarch.

Before 1707, the monarchs of England and of Scotland are listed in separate articles. The Acts of Union merged the two kingdoms into the Kingdom of Great Britain; in 1801 the Kingdom of Ireland was merged to form the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland, renamed the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland in 1927.
