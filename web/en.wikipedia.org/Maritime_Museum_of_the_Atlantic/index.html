The Maritime Museum of the Atlantic is a maritime museum located in downtown Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. Founded in 1948, it is the oldest and largest maritime museum in Canada, with a collection of over 30,000 artefacts covering the province's seafaring history from the age of sail to the present.

Exhibits

Permanent displays include the Age of Steam, shipwrecks of Nova Scotia, the Halifax Explosion of 1917, and Canada's role in the Battle of the Atlantic. The museum holds the world's largest collection of wooden artefacts recovered from RMS Titanic, whose victims were brought to Halifax in 1912; many are buried in the city's Fairview Lawn Cemetery.

Vessels

Moored behind the museum is CSS Acadia (1913), a retired hydrographic survey ship and the only vessel known to have survived both world wars and the Halifax Explosion. Merlin, the museum's rainbow lorikeet mascot, was a popular attraction until his death in 2020.
