Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile

The Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) is an international organisation headquartered in Paris that promotes motor sport and represents motoring organisations worldwide. Founded in 1904, it is best known as the governing body for many auto racing series, including Formula One and the World Rally Championship.

FIA
Founded: 20 June 1904
Headquarters: Place de la Concorde, Paris
Region served: Worldwide

Activities

The FIA sanctions world championships, sets technical and sporting regulations, and advocates for road safety through initiatives such as the Action for Road Safety campaign. Its member clubs also provide mobility and touring services.

History

Founded as the Association Internationale des Automobile Clubs Reconnus, it adopted its present name in 1946 and has supervised Formula One since the championship's inception in 1950.
