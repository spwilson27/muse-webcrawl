Yamal LNG

LocationSabetta, Yamal Peninsula, Russia
OperatorNovatek
Capacity~17.4 million tonnes per annum
First cargoDecember 2017

Yamal LNG is a liquefied natural gas project in Sabetta, on the Yamal Peninsula in the Russian Arctic. Operated by Novatek, it draws gas from the South Tambey gas field and liquefies it in three trains of about 5.5 million tonnes per year each, plus a smaller fourth train.

Development

Construction began in 2013 under extreme Arctic conditions. The plant, port, and airport were built largely from pre-fabricated modules shipped to the site. The first cargo was loaded in December 2017 aboard the ice-class carrier Christophe de Margerie.

Shipping

Yamal LNG is served by a fleet of Arc7 ice-class LNG carriers able to navigate the Northern Sea Route. In summer, ships can sail east to Asian markets through the Arctic, substantially shortening voyages compared with the Suez route.

Shareholders

The project is owned by Novatek (50.1%), TotalEnergies (20%), and Chinese partners CNPC (20%) and the Silk Road Fund (9.9%).
