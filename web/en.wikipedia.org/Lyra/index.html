Lyra

Constellation
Brightest star: Vega (α Lyrae)
Symbolism: The lyre

Lyra is a small constellation in the Northern Celestial Hemisphere, one of the 48 listed by Ptolemy and one of the modern 88 constellations. Its brightest star, Vega, is one of the brightest stars in the night sky.

Features

Vega forms one corner of the Summer Triangle asterism with Altair and Deneb. Lyra contains the Ring Nebula (M57), the globular cluster M56, and the double star Epsilon Lyrae. Kepler mission observations in Lyra and Cygnus discovered many exoplanets.

Mythology

Lyra represents the lyre of Orpheus, whose music charmed all living things in Greek myth.
