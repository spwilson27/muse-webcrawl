Outer Harbor, South Australia

LocationLefevre Peninsula, Adelaide, South Australia
Established1908
FunctionDeep-water port and passenger terminal

Outer Harbor is a suburb and deep-water port at the northern end of the Lefevre Peninsula, about 22 kilometres northwest of the centre of Adelaide, South Australia. It is the principal seaport for Adelaide, handling container ships, bulk cargo, and cruise and passenger vessels.

History

Ships originally had to anchor offshore and lighter cargo into Port Adelaide because the Port River was too shallow. After decades of debate, the Outer Harbor was dredged and opened in 1908, allowing large steamers to berth. The passenger terminal has long been the arrival point for overseas ships visiting Adelaide, and the suburb is the terminus of the Outer Harbor railway line from the city.

Geography

The suburb sits near the mouth of the Port River, opposite the Barker Inlet and Torrens Island, with views across Gulf St Vincent. Nearby are the North Haven marina and the Adelaide International Bird Sanctuary.
