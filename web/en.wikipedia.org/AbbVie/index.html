AbbVie Inc. is an American biopharmaceutical company headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois. It was created in 2013 when Abbott Laboratories split into two publicly traded companies, with AbbVie taking the research-based pharmaceuticals business and Abbott retaining medical devices, diagnostics, and nutritionals.

Products

AbbVie's best-known product is Humira (adalimumab), a biologic used to treat rheumatoid arthritis and other autoimmune diseases, which for years was the world's best-selling prescription drug before losing United States patent exclusivity in 2023. Other major products include the immunology drugs Skyrizi and Rinvoq, the cancer drug Imbruvica (marketed with Janssen), and Botox, acquired with Allergan.

Acquisitions

In 2020 AbbVie completed the acquisition of Allergan for about $63 billion, adding Botox and a portfolio of aesthetics and eye-care products. The company has also made major acquisitions in neuroscience and oncology, including Cerevel Therapeutics and ImmunoGen.
