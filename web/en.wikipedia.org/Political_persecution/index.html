Political persecution

Political persecution is the systematic mistreatment of individuals or groups by governments or other powerful actors because of their political beliefs or affiliations. It can include imprisonment, torture, extrajudicial killing, forced exile, loss of citizenship, employment discrimination and suppression of expression.

International law, including the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, prohibits persecution, and political persecution is one ground for asylum under the Refugee Convention. Historical examples include purges, show trials and campaigns against dissidents in authoritarian states.
