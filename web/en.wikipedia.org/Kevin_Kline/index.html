Kevin Kline

Kevin Delaney Kline (born 24 October 1947) is an American actor. Trained at Juilliard, he won two Tony Awards for stage work and the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for A Fish Called Wanda (1988).

Career

His films include The Big Chill (1983), Silverado, Dave (1993), In & Out (1997) and voice work in animation. He is married to actress Phoebe Cates.
