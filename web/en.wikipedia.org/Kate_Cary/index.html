Kate Cary

Kate Cary (born 4 November 1967) is a British author, best known as one of the writers — along with Victoria Holmes, Cherith Baldry and Tui Sutherland — who publish under the pseudonym Erin Hunter. She has written many volumes of the Warriors series of children's fantasy novels about feral cat clans.

Life and career

Cary was born in Birmingham, England, and studied history at Royal Holloway, University of London. After working as an editor, she joined the Warriors project at Working Partners, writing books including Into the Wild (the first volume, 2003), Fire and Ice and several Super Editions. She has also written the Bloodline series under her own name.

Cary lives in Scotland. She has spoken publicly about the collaborative process of writing as Erin Hunter, in which editor Victoria Holmes devised the plots and the authors wrote alternating volumes.
