Falmer is a small village and civil parish in the Lewes District of East Sussex, England, situated between Brighton and Lewes, a few miles north of the English Channel coast.

History and geography

The village is recorded in the Domesday Book. Its parish church is dedicated to St Laurence. A large pond in the centre of the village is a notable feature. Falmer lies within the South Downs National Park and is split by the A27 trunk road.

Education and sport

Falmer is home to campuses of the University of Sussex and the University of Brighton, and to Falmer Stadium (the American Express Community Stadium), home of Brighton & Hove Albion F.C., which opened in 2011. Falmer railway station serves the village and the stadium.
