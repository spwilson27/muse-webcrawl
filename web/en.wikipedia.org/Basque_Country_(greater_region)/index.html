Basque Country (greater region)

The Basque Country (Basque: Euskal Herria) is the name given to the home of the Basque people, a region in the western Pyrenees straddling the border between France and Spain on the Atlantic coast.

Territory

It comprises the autonomous communities of the Basque Country and Navarre in Spain and the Northern Basque Country in France — seven historical provinces in total.

Language and culture

Basque (Euskara) is a language isolate unrelated to neighbouring Romance languages, with a distinct culture, sports, cuisine and traditions.

Politics

Basque nationalism has sought greater autonomy or independence; the Spanish Basque Country has extensive self-government.
