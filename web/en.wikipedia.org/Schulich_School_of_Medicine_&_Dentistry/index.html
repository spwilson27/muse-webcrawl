Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry

The Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry is the combined medical and dental school of the University of Western Ontario (Western University) in London, Ontario, Canada. The school was renamed in 2005 following a $26-million donation from philanthropist Seymour Schulich, then one of the largest gifts to a Canadian medical school.

History

Medical teaching at Western began in 1881 with the founding of the Faculty of Medicine, and dentistry was added in 1964. The two faculties were administratively merged in 1997. In 2011 the school opened a distributed campus in Windsor, Ontario, in partnership with the University of Windsor, allowing students to complete their medical education there.

Programs

The school offers the Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) program, a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) program, undergraduate and graduate degrees in medical sciences, and a range of postgraduate residency programs. Admission to the M.D. program is among the most competitive in Canada, with roughly 4,500 applicants for about 170 seats across the London and Windsor campuses.

Research

Research is conducted in partnership with institutes such as Robarts Research Institute and Lawson Health Research Institute, with strengths in neuroscience, medical imaging, and musculoskeletal health. Affiliated teaching hospitals in London include Victoria Hospital, University Hospital, and St. Joseph's Health Care.
