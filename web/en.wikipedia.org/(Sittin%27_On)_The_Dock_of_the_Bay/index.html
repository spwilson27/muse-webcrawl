(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay

Single by Otis Redding
Released8 January 1968
RecordedNovember 1967, Stax Studios, Memphis
GenreSoul
SongwritersOtis Redding, Steve Cropper

"(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay" is a song co-written by soul singer Otis Redding and guitarist Steve Cropper. Redding recorded it in November 1967, shortly before his death in a plane crash on 10 December 1967.

Success

Released posthumously in January 1968, it became the first posthumous single to reach number one on the Billboard Hot 100, and topped the charts for four weeks. Its whistled outro — improvised because Redding intended to add a final verse — became one of the most recognisable endings in popular music.

Legacy

The song won two Grammy Awards and is consistently ranked among the greatest songs of all time; it is also notable as a stylistic departure from Redding's earlier work, reflecting the influence of the Beatles' Sgt. Pepper era songwriting.
