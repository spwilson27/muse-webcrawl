Outremer

Outremer (French for "overseas") is a general name used for the Crusader states established in the Levant after the First Crusade: the County of Edessa, the Principality of Antioch, the County of Tripoli and the Kingdom of Jerusalem.

History

Founded between 1098 and 1099, the states were ruled by Western European ("Frankish") elites over local Muslim, Christian and Jewish populations. Edessa fell in 1144; Jerusalem fell to Saladin in 1187; the last mainland stronghold, Acre, fell to the Mamluks in 1291.

Society

Outremer developed a distinctive feudal society with its own law codes (the Assizes of Jerusalem), military orders such as the Templars and Hospitallers, and extensive castle building, including Krak des Chevaliers.
