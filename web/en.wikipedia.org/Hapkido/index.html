Hapkido

Hapkido is a Korean martial art characterised by joint locks, throws, kicks, strikes, and the use of traditional weapons. Its name can be translated as "the way of coordinating energy".

History

Hapkido was developed in the mid-20th century, principally by Choi Yong-sool, who returned to Korea after studying in Japan, and his students including Ji Han-jae, who helped popularise the art in Korea and abroad.

Techniques

Training emphasises circular motion, non-resistance, and redirecting an opponent's force. Practitioners learn kicks, hand strikes, joint manipulation, throws, pressure points, and weapons such as the cane, rope, and short stick. See also Taekwondo and Aikido.
