Carl Frederick Kendall Palmer (born 20 March 1950) is an English drummer, best known as a founding member of the progressive rock supergroup Emerson, Lake & Palmer (ELP) and the supergroup Asia. He is one of the most respected rock drummers to emerge from the 1960s, known for his virtuosic technique, speed, and elaborate stage setups including a rotating drum kit.

Career

Palmer played with The Crazy World of Arthur Brown and founded Atomic Rooster with Vincent Crane before joining Keith Emerson and Greg Lake in 1970 to form ELP, one of the most commercially successful progressive rock bands of the 1970s, with albums such as Tarkus and Brain Salad Surgery.

Asia and later work

In 1981 Palmer co-founded Asia with Steve Howe, John Wetton, and Geoff Downes; the band's self-titled debut topped charts in 1982 with hits including "Heat of the Moment". Palmer later toured with his own Carl Palmer Band, performing ELP material, and after the deaths of Emerson and Lake in 2016, he became the sole surviving member of ELP, continuing to tour their music with multimedia shows using archive footage of his bandmates.
