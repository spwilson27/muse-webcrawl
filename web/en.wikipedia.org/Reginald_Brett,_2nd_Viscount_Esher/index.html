Reginald Brett, 2nd Viscount Esher

Reginald Baliol Brett, 2nd Viscount Esher (30 June 1852 – 22 January 1930) was a British historian, courtier and Liberal politician who exercised great influence behind the scenes, especially on military and royal matters, while avoiding front-rank office.

Educated at Eton and Trinity College, Cambridge, Brett entered Parliament in 1880. He became a close adviser to Queen Victoria, Edward VII and George V, served as Secretary of the Office of Works, helped organise the Diamond Jubilee, and chaired the War Office Reconstruction Committee (the Esher Committee) in 1904, whose reforms created the Army Council and the Committee of Imperial Defence. He was Governor of Windsor Castle and a prolific diarist whose journals are a major source for the period.
