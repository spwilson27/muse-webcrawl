Ballon d'Or

Ballon d'Or
Presented by: France Football
First awarded: 1956

Most wins: Lionel Messi (8)

The Ballon d'Or (French for "Golden Ball") is an annual football award presented by the magazine France Football since 1956. It is widely regarded as the most prestigious individual honour in the sport, awarded to the player judged to have performed best over the previous season.

History

The award was conceived by France Football journalists Gabriel Hanot and Jacques Ferran. Originally limited to European players at European clubs, eligibility was widened in 1995 to all players at European clubs — George Weah became the first non-European winner — and in 2007 to players worldwide. From 2010 to 2015 it was merged with FIFA's World Player award as the FIFA Ballon d'Or before the partnership ended.

Winners

Lionel Messi holds the record with eight awards, followed by Cristiano Ronaldo with five. Johan Cruyff, Michel Platini and Marco van Basten each won three times. The first winner was England's Stanley Matthews in 1956.

Ballon d'Or Féminin

A women's award, the Ballon d'Or Féminin, was introduced in 2018; Ada Hegerberg was its first recipient, and Aitana Bonmatí has won multiple times. Additional trophies include the Kopa Trophy for the best player under 21 and the Yashin Trophy for goalkeepers.
