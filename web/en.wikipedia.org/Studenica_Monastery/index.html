Studenica Monastery

Studenica Monastery
Location: Kraljevo, Serbia
Founded: 1190 by Stefan Nemanja
UNESCO World Heritage Site: 1986

Studenica Monastery is a Serbian Orthodox monastery in central Serbia, founded around 1190 by Stefan Nemanja, founder of the medieval Serbian state. It is the largest and richest of Serbia's Orthodox monasteries and the mother church of the Nemanjić dynasty.

Churches

The complex includes the Church of the Virgin, a domed basilica in white marble blending Romanesque and Byzantine styles, and the smaller King's Church (1314), famous for its frescoes. Studenica's fresco paintings, notably the Crucifixion of 1208–09, are masterpieces of Byzantine art.

Heritage

The monastery holds the relics of Stefan Nemanja (St Simeon) and remains an active monastery. It was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1986.
