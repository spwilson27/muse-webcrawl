Nga Wai Hono i te Po

Nga Wai Hono i te Po (born 13 January 1997) is the eighth Maori monarch and the second woman to hold the role, reigning as Kuini (Queen) of the Kingitanga since 5 September 2024. She is the youngest child and only daughter of her predecessor, King Tuheitia.

Early life and education

She was raised within the Kingitanga at Turangawaewae Marae in Ngaruawahia and educated at Te Wharekura o Rakaumangamanga and the University of Waikato, where she completed a Bachelor of Arts and a Master of Arts in Tikanga Maori. She has worked in roles supporting Maori economic development and performed kapa haka at the highest level with Te Pou o Mangatawhiri.

Accession

Following the death of King Tuheitia on 30 August 2024, the Tekau-ma-rua, the Kingitanga's council of chiefs, selected her as monarch. She was crowned during her father's tangihanga on 5 September 2024, taking the title Te Arikinui Kuini Nga Wai Hono i te Po. Her name, given by her grandfather's generation of leaders, means "the waters joining in the night", symbolising the joining of rivers and peoples.
