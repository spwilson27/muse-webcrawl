White Ship

The White Ship (French: Blanche-Nef) was a vessel that sank in the English Channel near Barfleur on 25 November 1120. Among about 300 people aboard, only one survivor was reliably reported. The dead included William Adelin, the only legitimate son and heir of Henry I of England.

Disaster

The ship struck a rock after leaving Barfleur for England. Contemporary chroniclers blamed speed, darkness and drinking among the crew and passengers. William initially escaped by boat but returned to rescue his half-sister and was lost when the boat capsized.

Consequences

The loss destabilised the succession to the English throne. Henry I later designated his daughter Matilda as heir, but after his death her cousin Stephen seized the throne, leading to the civil war known as the Anarchy.
