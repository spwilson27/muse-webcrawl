Las Fallas

Las Fallas (Valencian: Falles) is a traditional celebration held annually in commemoration of Saint Joseph in Valencia, Spain, culminating on 19 March. Neighbourhood committees build enormous satirical monuments of wood and papier-mâché, called fallas or ninots, which are paraded, judged and finally burned in the cremà amid fireworks displays such as the daily mascletà.

Events

The festival runs from late February's crida through the plantà (installation), flower offering to the Virgin, and the Nit del Foc fireworks, ending with the burning on Saint Joseph's Day.

Recognition

In 2016 Las Fallas was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
