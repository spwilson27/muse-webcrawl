The ABRSM (Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music) is an examinations board and registered charity based in London, UK, which provides music examinations and assessments around the world. It was formed in 1889 by the Royal Academy of Music and the Royal College of Music to counter unregulated local examining, and today partners those institutions with the Royal Northern College of Music and the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland.

Examinations

ABRSM offers graded examinations in instrumental and vocal performance and music theory, from an initial Prep Test and Grades 1–8, followed by diploma-level qualifications (DipABRSM, LRSM and FRSM). More than 600,000 candidates take ABRSM assessments each year in over 90 countries, making it one of the world's largest music examination bodies. In recent years it has expanded digital, remotely recorded performance-grade exams alongside traditional face-to-face assessment.

Publishing

The board also publishes sheet music, syllabuses, recordings and learning apps, and its surplus supports scholarships and charitable grants for music education.
