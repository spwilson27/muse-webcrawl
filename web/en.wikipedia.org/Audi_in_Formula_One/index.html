Audi in Formula One

Audi in Formula One concerns the German manufacturer's entry into the FIA Formula One World Championship. In August 2022 Audi announced it would enter Formula One in 2026 as a power unit manufacturer under new hybrid regulations, in partnership with the Swiss-based Sauber team.

Takeover of Sauber

Audi progressively acquired ownership of Sauber, with the team set to become the Audi factory team for 2026. Audi develops its power unit at Neuburg an der Donau in Germany, while chassis operations remain at Sauber's Hinwil site in Switzerland.

Background

Audi's predecessor Auto Union competed in Grand Prix racing in the 1930s, but the modern Audi brand had previously concentrated on endurance racing, including 13 wins at the 24 Hours of Le Mans, and later Formula E and rally programmes.
