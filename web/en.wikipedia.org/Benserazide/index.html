Benserazide

Benserazide is a peripherally acting aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase inhibitor used in the treatment of Parkinson's disease.

Use

It is combined with levodopa in the preparation co-beneldopa (brand name Madopar). By blocking the breakdown of levodopa outside the brain, benserazide increases the amount reaching the brain and reduces peripheral side effects such as nausea and arrhythmia. Benserazide itself does not cross the blood–brain barrier in significant amounts.

Pharmacology

The usual fixed ratio is 4:1 levodopa to benserazide. The related drug carbidopa serves the same purpose in co-careldopa (Sinemet) and is more common in the United States, where benserazide is not marketed.
