Iron Man

Alter ego: Anthony Edward "Tony" Stark

First appearance: Tales of Suspense #39 (cover dated March 1963)

Created by: Stan Lee, Larry Lieber, Don Heck, Jack Kirby

Publisher: Marvel Comics

Iron Man is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Co-created by writer and editor Stan Lee, developed by scripter Larry Lieber, and designed by artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Tales of Suspense #39 in 1963, and received his own title in Iron Man #1 in 1968.

Fictional biography

Anthony Edward "Tony" Stark is an American billionaire business magnate, playboy, philanthropist, and ingenious engineer. While demonstrating weapons overseas he is kidnapped and gravely wounded by shrapnel near his heart; instead of building a weapon for his captors, he and fellow prisoner Ho Yinsen build a powered suit of armour that keeps him alive and lets him escape. Back home, Stark refines the armour and adopts the identity of Iron Man, initially claiming the hero is his bodyguard. He is a founding member of the Avengers. Stark's storylines have addressed alcoholism (the acclaimed "Demon in a Bottle" arc, 1979), arms dealing, and civil liberties in the "Civil War" crossover.

The armour

The Iron Man armour is a powered exoskeleton granting superhuman strength and durability, flight via boot jets, and weaponry including repulsor rays from the palms, a chest-mounted uni-beam, and smart micro-guns. The suit is powered by an arc reactor in Stark's chest.

In other media

Robert Downey Jr. portrayed Iron Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with Iron Man (2008) and ending with the character's death in Avengers: Endgame (2019). The films transformed Iron Man from a second-tier comics character into one of the most popular superheroes in the world.
