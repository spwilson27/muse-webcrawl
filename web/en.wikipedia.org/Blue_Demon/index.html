Blue Demon

Born24 April 1922, García, Nuevo León, Mexico
Died16 December 2000 (aged 78)
Ring nameBlue Demon (Demonio Azul)

Blue Demon (Spanish: Demonio Azul), born Alejandro Muñoz Moreno (1922–2000), was a Mexican masked professional wrestler (luchador) and film actor. He is considered one of the greatest figures in lucha libre, alongside El Santo and Mil Máscaras.

Career

Muñoz debuted in 1948 wearing a blue mask and costume from which his ring name derived. He formed a famous rivalry and later partnership with El Santo, and starred in a series of popular lucha libre films in the 1960s and 1970s.

Legacy

Blue Demon never lost his mask in the ring and was buried wearing it. His adopted son wrestles as Blue Demon Jr., continuing the character.
