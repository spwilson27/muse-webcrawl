S. S. Rajamouli (born 10 October 1973) is an Indian film director and screenwriter who works predominantly in Telugu cinema. He is widely regarded as one of the most successful and influential filmmakers in Indian cinema, known for big-budget spectacle films characterised by larger-than-life heroes, strong emotional narratives and technically ambitious action sequences.

Early life

Rajamouli was born in Raichur, Karnataka, and raised in Kovvur, Andhra Pradesh. His father, K. V. Vijayendra Prasad, is a prominent screenwriter who has written the stories for many of Rajamouli's films. Rajamouli began his career directing the television serial Santhi Nivasam under the mentorship of veteran director K. Raghavendra Rao.

Career

His feature directorial debut was Student No: 1 (2001). He built a reputation through a string of commercial successes including Simhadri (2003), Vikramarkudu (2006), the fantasy action film Magadheera (2009) — a major commercial and critical breakthrough — and Eega (2012), an inventive revenge drama whose protagonist is reincarnated as a housefly, which won international festival acclaim.

Rajamouli achieved pan-Indian fame with the two-part epic Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017). The second film became one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time, setting box-office records across India and demonstrating the nationwide market for dubbed regional cinema. His next film, RRR (2022), a fictionalised period action drama about revolutionaries Alluri Sitarama Raju and Komaram Bheem, became a global phenomenon, winning the Academy Award for Best Original Song for "Naatu Naatu" and the Golden Globe Award in the same category, and earning him the New York Film Critics Circle Award for Best Director.

Style and recognition

Rajamouli's films are noted for mythic storytelling rooted in Indian epics, operatic emotion, and set-pieces staged on a grand scale. Among the most decorated Indian filmmakers, he has received numerous honours including the Padma Shri (2016), India's fourth-highest civilian award.
