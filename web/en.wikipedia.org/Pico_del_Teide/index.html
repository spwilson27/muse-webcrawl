Pico del Teide

Elevation
3,715 m (12,188 ft)
Location
Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain
Type
Stratovolcano

Pico del Teide is a volcano on Tenerife in the Canary Islands, Spain. Its summit, at 3,715 metres (12,188 ft) above sea level, is the highest point in Spain and the highest peak of any Atlantic island. Measured from its base on the ocean floor, it is among the tallest volcanoes in the world.

Geology

Teide is a stratovolcano within the Las Cañadas caldera, part of Teide National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its most recent eruption was in 1909 from the Santiago del Teide vent. The mountain casts, at certain times of year, the largest sea shadow in the world.

Access

A cable car, the Teleférico del Teide, carries visitors from 2,356 m to near the summit; access to the final cone requires a permit. The clear skies above Teide also make it a prime site for astronomical observatories.
