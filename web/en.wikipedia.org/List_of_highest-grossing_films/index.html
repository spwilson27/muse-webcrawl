Adjusted for inflation

Adjusted for ticket-price inflation, Gone with the Wind (1939) remains the highest-grossing film of all time, followed by Avatar, Titanic, Star Wars (1977) and Avengers: Endgame. James Cameron has directed three of the top nominal earners.
