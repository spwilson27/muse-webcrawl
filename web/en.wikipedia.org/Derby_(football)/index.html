Derby (football)

Crowds at a local football derby

In association football, a derby is a match between two rival teams, usually from the same city or region (a "local derby"), marked by heightened passion among supporters. The word is also used for fierce rivalries between teams from different regions, sometimes called derbies by extension.

Origin of the term

The term is often traced to the horse race the Derby at Epsom, founded in 1780 by the Earl of Derby, or to the Shrovetide football match between parishes in Ashbourne, Derbyshire.

Famous derbies

  • El Clásico (Barcelona vs Real Madrid)
  • Manchester derby, North London derby, Merseyside derby (England)
  • Derby della Madonnina (Milan)
  • Old Firm (Celtic vs Rangers, Glasgow)
  • Superclásico (Boca Juniors vs River Plate, Buenos Aires)
