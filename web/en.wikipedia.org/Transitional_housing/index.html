Transitional housing

Transitional housing is temporary housing provided to people experiencing homelessness or housing instability, typically for a period of up to 24 months, combined with supportive services intended to help residents move into and maintain permanent housing. It sits between emergency shelter and permanent supportive housing in the continuum of care.

Models

Programmes may serve specific populations — veterans, families, young people ageing out of foster care, people in recovery, or survivors of domestic violence — and usually require participation in case management, employment, or treatment services. Since the 2010s, US federal policy has shifted funding toward Housing First and rapid rehousing models, though transitional housing remains common for youth and some specialised groups.

Outcomes

Evaluations generally find better outcomes when transitional housing is paired with strong aftercare, while critics question time limits that push residents out before they achieve stable income.
