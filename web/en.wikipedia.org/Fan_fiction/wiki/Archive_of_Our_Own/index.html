Archive of Our Own (often abbreviated as AO3) is a nonprofit open source repository for fanfiction and other fanworks contributed by users. The site was created in 2008 by the Organization for Transformative Works and went into open beta in 2009. It hosts millions of works in tens of thousands of fandoms, and is one of the largest fanfiction archives on the internet.

Features

AO3 is known for its folksonomy-based tagging system: users tag works freely, and volunteer "tag wranglers" link synonymous tags so that searches and filters work across the archive. Works can be filtered by fandom, rating, warnings, characters, relationships and freeform tags. The site permits adult content with appropriate ratings and warnings, allows authors to post anonymously or orphan their works, and provides downloads in formats such as EPUB, MOBI and PDF.

History and recognition

The archive grew rapidly as fans migrated from other platforms, particularly after content restrictions on sites such as FanFiction.Net and LiveJournal. In 2019, Archive of Our Own won the Hugo Award for Best Related Work, a recognition of the site's significance to science fiction and fantasy fandom. The site is maintained by volunteers and funded by donations to the Organization for Transformative Works.
