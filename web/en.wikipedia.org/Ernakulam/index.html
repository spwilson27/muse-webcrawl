Ernakulam refers to the mainland eastern portion of the city of Kochi in Kerala, India, and also to the surrounding district. Located on the Arabian Sea coast, Ernakulam is the major commercial and industrial centre of Kerala, while neighbouring Fort Kochi and Mattancherry across the backwaters form the historic core of the old Cochin port.

Geography

Ernakulam district stretches from the Western Ghats to the Arabian Sea, including Kochi's island system, the backwaters of Vembanad Lake, and hill country in the east. The district is one of the most densely populated and urbanised in Kerala, and the Kochi metropolitan area is the state's largest urban agglomeration.

History

The area was part of the Kingdom of Cochin, a princely state allied first with the Portuguese (from 1503), then the Dutch, then the British. Ernakulam became the capital of the princely state, and after Indian independence the region was reorganised into Ernakulam district in 1958. The Cochin Shipyard, established in 1972, is one of India's largest shipbuilding facilities.

Economy and landmarks

The district hosts the Port of Kochi, the Cochin Special Economic Zone, InfoPark Kochi, the Kochi Metro, and Lulu Mall — among India's largest shopping malls. Notable attractions include Marine Drive, the Hill Palace Museum, the Ernakulam Shiva Temple, and the Mattancherry Palace. The district is also the gateway to the Kerala backwaters and Fort Kochi's colonial heritage.
