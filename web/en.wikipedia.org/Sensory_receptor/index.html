Sensory receptor

A sensory receptor is a structure that reacts to a physical stimulus in the internal or external environment. It is a sensory nerve ending that receives information and conducts a process of generating nerve impulses to be transmitted to the brain for interpretation and perception.

Classification

Receptors are classified by the type of stimulus they detect: mechanoreceptors respond to mechanical pressure or distortion, chemoreceptors to chemical substances, photoreceptors to light, thermoreceptors to temperature, and nociceptors to potentially damaging stimuli perceived as pain. They may also be grouped by location, such as exteroceptors for external stimuli and interoceptors for internal ones.

Transduction

Each receptor performs sensory transduction, converting stimulus energy into graded potentials and, when threshold is reached, action potentials. Adaptation varies: tonic receptors fire throughout a stimulus, while phasic receptors respond mainly to changes.
