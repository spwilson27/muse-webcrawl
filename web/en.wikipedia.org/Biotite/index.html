Biotite is a common group of phyllosilicate minerals within the mica group, with the approximate chemical formula K(Mg,Fe)3AlSi3O10(F,OH)2. It is named after the French physicist Jean-Baptiste Biot.

Properties

Biotite occurs as dark, flexible, elastic flakes with perfect basal cleavage. It is a major constituent of many igneous rocks such as granite and of metamorphic rocks such as schist and gneiss.

Uses

Because biotite commonly contains trace amounts of radioactive elements, it is useful in argon-argon dating of rocks.
