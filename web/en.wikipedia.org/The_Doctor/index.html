The Doctor is the protagonist of the long-running British science-fiction television programme Doctor Who. A renegade alien Time Lord from the planet Gallifrey, the Doctor travels through time and space in the TARDIS, a time machine that normally appears as a blue British police box, accompanied by companions and frequently intervening to protect civilisations and individuals from threats.

Regeneration

When mortally wounded or worn out, the Doctor can regenerate into a new body, gaining a new appearance and personality. The concept, introduced in 1966 when William Hartnell left the role, has allowed numerous actors to play the character, including Patrick Troughton, Jon Pertwee, Tom Baker, Peter Davison, Colin Baker, Sylvester McCoy, Paul McGann, Christopher Eccleston, David Tennant, Matt Smith, Peter Capaldi, Jodie Whittaker, and Ncuti Gatwa.

Characterisation

The Doctor is depicted as eccentric, brilliant, and compassionate, armed with a sonic screwdriver and a strict aversion to violence. The character's name is a mystery within the story; "Doctor Who" is rarely used in the programme itself. The Doctor has become an enduring icon of British popular culture.
