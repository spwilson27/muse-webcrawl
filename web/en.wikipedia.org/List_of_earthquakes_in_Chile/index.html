List of earthquakes in Chile

Chile lies on the Pacific Ring of Fire, where the Nazca Plate subducts beneath the South American Plate. It has experienced some of the largest earthquakes ever recorded, including the 1960 Valdivia earthquake, the most powerful earthquake in recorded history.

Seismic culture

Chile has strict building codes shaped by its seismic history, and its civil protection system conducts regular evacuation drills in coastal cities at risk of tsunamis.
