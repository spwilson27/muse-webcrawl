Audi RS 6

Manufacturer: Audi Sport GmbH

Production: 2002–present

Class: Executive car (E)

Body style: 5-door estate (Avant); 4-door saloon (earlier generations)

The Audi RS 6 is a high-performance version of the Audi A6 range of executive cars, produced by Audi Sport GmbH for Audi AG, a member of the Volkswagen Group. The first two generations were produced as both a saloon and an Avant (estate); from the third generation onwards the RS 6 has been offered only as an Avant. The name refers to RennSport (racing sport) and its position above the Audi S6 in the A6 hierarchy.

Generations

The first-generation RS 6 (C5, 2002–2004) used a 4.2-litre twin-turbocharged V8 producing 450 PS. The second generation (C6, 2008–2010) featured a 5.0-litre twin-turbo V10 with 580 PS, making it one of the most powerful production cars Audi had built. The third generation (C7, 2013–2018) returned to a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 with up to 605 PS in performance trim.

C8 generation

The fourth-generation RS 6 Avant (C8) was launched in 2019 with a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 with a 48-volt mild-hybrid system, producing 600 PS (441 kW) and 800 N·m of torque. It accelerates from 0–100 km/h in 3.6 seconds. In 2024 the RS 6 Avant GT special edition, inspired by the RS 6 GTO concept, was announced with 630 PS.
