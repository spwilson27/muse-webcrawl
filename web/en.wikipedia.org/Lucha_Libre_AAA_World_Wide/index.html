Lucha Libre AAA World Wide

Lucha Libre AAA World Wide, commonly referred to as simply AAA (originally Asistencia Asesoría y Administración), is a Mexican professional wrestling promotion based in Mexico City, founded in 1992 by Antonio Peña after he left Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre (CMLL).

History

AAA became known for its colourful masked luchadores, six-sided ring used for some periods, and major annual shows such as Triplemanía, Guerra de Titanes and Rey de Reyes. It has maintained working relationships with promotions in the United States and Japan and has been home to stars including Rey Mysterio Jr., Konnan, La Parka and Psycho Clown.
