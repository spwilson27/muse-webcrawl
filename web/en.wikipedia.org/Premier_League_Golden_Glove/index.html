Premier League Golden Glove

The Premier League Golden Glove is an annual association football award presented to the goalkeeper who has kept the most clean sheets in the Premier League. In football, the team's defence is considered to have kept a "clean sheet" when they have not conceded a goal in a match.

History

The award was first presented in the 2004–05 season, when Petr Čech of Chelsea won it with a record 24 clean sheets in a single season — a mark that still stands. Originally a single winner was named even in cases of a tie; since the 2013–14 season the award has been shared among goalkeepers finishing level on clean sheets, the first shared award going to Čech and Wojciech Szczęsny. In the event of a tie in earlier seasons, the keeper with the better ratio of clean sheets to games played was declared the winner.

Records

Petr Čech and Joe Hart jointly hold the record for most Golden Glove wins, with four each; Čech won with both Chelsea and Arsenal, the only goalkeeper to win with two clubs. Other multiple winners include Pepe Reina (three consecutive awards with Liverpool from 2005–06), Ederson, and Alisson Becker. The award is the goalkeeping counterpart to the Golden Boot for top scorer and the Playmaker award for most assists.
