Aesthetic (social media)


A mood board in a popular online aesthetic style

On social media, an aesthetic is a set of visual and thematic qualities — colours, imagery, fashion, music and attitudes — that together form a recognizable style or subculture, commonly shared on platforms such as Tumblr, Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok. Users adopt aesthetics as a form of self-expression and identity, curating feeds, clothing and spaces to match the style.

Examples

Named aesthetics that have trended online include cottagecore (romanticized rural life), dark academia (classical education, autumnal tones and vintage clothing), vaporwave (1980s–90s consumer culture with pastel and neon palettes), Y2K aesthetics, "clean girl", coastal grandmother, and various "-core" suffix trends. Many aesthetics are documented by community wikis that catalogue their key visuals, motifs, music and activities.

History

The use of "aesthetic" as a noun for an internet style grew out of Tumblr culture in the 2010s, where themed blogs collected imagery around a shared visual mood. The trend accelerated on TikTok in the early 2020s, with new micro-aesthetics appearing and fading rapidly, and has influenced mainstream fashion and marketing.
