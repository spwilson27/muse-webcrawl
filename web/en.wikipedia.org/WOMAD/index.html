WOMAD

WOMAD (The World of Music, Arts and Dance) is an international arts festival. The central aim of WOMAD is to celebrate the world's many forms of music, arts and dance. The festival was founded in 1980 by English musician Peter Gabriel, Thomas Brooman, Bob Hooton, Mark Kidel, Stephen Pritchard, Martin Elbourne and Jonathan Arthur.

History

The first WOMAD festival was held in 1982 in Shepton Mallet, Somerset, England. Although the inaugural festival featured a celebrated line-up including Peter Gabriel, Echo & the Bunnymen, Simple Minds and artists from around the world, it ran at a financial loss, and Gabriel famously reunited with his former band Genesis for a one-off concert to help cover the debts.

Since then, WOMAD festivals have been held in more than 27 countries around the world, including long-running events at Charlton Park in Wiltshire, in Adelaide (WOMADelaide), in New Plymouth, New Zealand, and in Cáceres and Fuerteventura in Spain. The festivals typically feature performances, workshops, food stalls and markets presenting music and culture from many countries.

WOMADelaide

WOMADelaide has been held in Adelaide, South Australia, since 1992, and is one of the largest and most successful WOMAD events, attracting tens of thousands of attendees to Botanic Park over several days.
