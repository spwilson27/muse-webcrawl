Cannabinoids
Chemical class acting on cannabinoid receptors
Major examples: THC, CBD

Cannabinoids are a class of diverse chemical compounds that act on cannabinoid receptors in cells of the endocannabinoid system. They include the endocannabinoids produced naturally by animals, the phytocannabinoids found in cannabis plants, and synthetic cannabinoids.

Phytocannabinoids

More than 100 cannabinoids have been isolated from Cannabis sativa, most notably tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), responsible for the plant's psychoactive effects, and cannabidiol (CBD), which is non-intoxicating.

Receptors

Cannabinoids act mainly on the CB1 receptors of the central nervous system and CB2 receptors of the immune system.
