Traffic violation

A traffic violation is an infringement of traffic law by the operator of a vehicle. Violations range from minor infractions, such as exceeding the speed limit or illegal parking, to serious offences such as driving under the influence, reckless driving and hit-and-run.

Types

Moving violations occur while the vehicle is in motion — speeding, running a red light, improper lane changes — while non-moving violations include parking offences and defective equipment. Many jurisdictions distinguish civil infractions, handled with fines, from criminal misdemeanours and felonies for dangerous conduct.

Penalties

Penalties may include fines, points on the driving licence, licence suspension, mandatory courses, vehicle impoundment and imprisonment. Automated enforcement with speed and red-light cameras is widespread, and penalty-point systems can lead to disqualification after repeated offences.
