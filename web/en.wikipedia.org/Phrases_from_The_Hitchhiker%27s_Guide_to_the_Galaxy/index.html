Phrases from The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy series by Douglas Adams has given English a number of widely quoted phrases, several of which have passed into general usage far beyond the books' readership.

Notable phrases

"Don't Panic" is printed in large friendly letters on the cover of the fictional Guide. "The Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" is 42, computed by the supercomputer Deep Thought over 7.5 million years. "Mostly harmless" is the Guide's entire entry for Earth in its revised edition. Other favourites include "So long, and thanks for all the fish", the dolphins' farewell message, and the description of the Babel fish, a creature that translates languages when placed in the ear.

Legacy

Towel Day is celebrated by fans every 25 May, honouring the Guide's advice that a towel is "the most massively useful thing an interstellar hitchhiker can have". The number 42 and the phrase "Don't Panic" appear throughout popular culture and computing.
