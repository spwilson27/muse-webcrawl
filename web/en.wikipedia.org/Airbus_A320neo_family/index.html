Airbus A320neo family

Airbus A320neo family
Manufacturer: Airbus
First flight: 25 September 2014
Introduction: 25 January 2016 with Lufthansa
Status: In production, in service
Variants: A319neo, A320neo, A321neo (incl. A321LR and A321XLR)

The Airbus A320neo family is a series of narrow-body airliners developed by Airbus from the A320 family. The word "neo" means "new engine option"; customers may choose the CFM International LEAP-1A or the Pratt & Whitney PW1100G geared turbofan, which together with sharklet winglets deliver roughly 15–20% better fuel efficiency than the previous generation.

Development and service

Airbus launched the programme in December 2010. The A320neo first flew on 25 September 2014 and entered service with Lufthansa in January 2016. The stretched A321neo — including the long-range LR and extra-long-range A321XLR variants — has become the family's best seller, while the shortened A319neo has sold in small numbers, mainly to Chinese carriers and as a corporate jet.

Market

The family competes with the Boeing 737 MAX and has accumulated one of the largest order backlogs in commercial aviation history, with thousands of aircraft ordered by airlines worldwide.
