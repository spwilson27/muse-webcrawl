Île-de-France Mobilités, formerly the Syndicat des transports d'Île-de-France (STIF), is the public transport organising authority for the Île-de-France region around Paris. It plans, coordinates and funds public transport across the region.

Role

The authority contracts with operators including RATP, SNCF and the many private bus companies of the Optile association to run the Métro, RER, Transilien trains, trams and buses. It sets fares and oversees the Navigo pass smart-card system.

History

The body descends from the Syndicat des transports parisiens created in 1959. Renamed STIF in 2000, it adopted the Île-de-France Mobilités name in 2017 as its powers expanded over cycling schemes, car-sharing and new Grand Paris Express lines.
