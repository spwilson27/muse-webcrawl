Fazal Qureshi (born 13 February 1961) is an Indian tabla player. Born in Mumbai, he is the son of tabla maestro Alla Rakha and the younger brother of Zakir Hussain and Taufiq Qureshi.

Career

Trained by his father in the Punjab gharana tradition, Fazal Qureshi initially studied engineering before dedicating himself to music. He has accompanied leading Hindustani classical musicians and performed internationally both as a soloist and in fusion settings. His playing is noted for combining traditional repertoire with cross-cultural collaborations, including work with jazz and world music artists in Europe, particularly in Scandinavia.

Collaborations

He has been associated with projects such as the group Mynta, which blends Indian music with Swedish folk and jazz, and has toured and recorded widely. Like his brothers, he has contributed to popularising the tabla with global audiences while continuing to perform in classical contexts in India.
