OK Computer

OK Computer is the third studio album by the English rock band Radiohead, released on 21 May 1997. Produced by Nigel Godrich and recorded at Canned Applause studio in Oxfordshire and St Catherine's Court in Bath, the album marked a departure from the guitar-driven sound of its predecessor The Bends toward more abstract, layered arrangements.

Themes and music

The lyrics depict a world fraught with rampant consumerism, social alienation, political malaise, and technology — themes frontman Thom Yorke drew from touring life and modern anxiety. Singles included "Paranoid Android", "Karma Police", and "No Surprises". Other notable tracks include "Airbag", "Let Down", "Exit Music (For a Film)", and "The Tourist".

Reception and legacy

OK Computer received widespread critical acclaim and is frequently cited among the greatest albums of all time. It won the Grammy Award for Best Alternative Music Album and was preserved in the US National Recording Registry in 2014. A 20th-anniversary reissue, OKNOTOK 1997 2017, appeared in 2017.
