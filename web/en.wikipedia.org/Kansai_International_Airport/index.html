Kansai International Airport

Kansai International Airport (KIX)
IATA: KIX · ICAO: RJBB
Location: Osaka Bay, Japan
Opened: 4 September 1994
Operator: Kansai Airports
Hub for: All Nippon Airways, Japan Airlines, Peach

Kansai International Airport is an international airport located on an artificial island in the middle of Osaka Bay, off the shore of the cities of Izumisano, Sennan and Tajiri in Osaka Prefecture, Japan. It serves as the main international gateway for the Kansai region, including Osaka, Kyoto and Kobe.

History

The airport opened in 1994 to relieve the overcrowded Osaka International Airport (Itami). Because the island was built on soft seabed, it has sunk more than expected since construction, prompting extensive engineering measures. A second artificial island and runway opened in 2007.

Terminals

Terminal 1, designed by Renzo Piano, is among the longest airport terminal buildings in the world at about 1.7 km. Terminal 2, opened in 2012, serves low-cost carriers such as Peach Aviation.

Access

The airport is connected to the mainland by the Sky Gate Bridge and by JR and Nankai railway services to Osaka and Kyoto. See also Osaka.
