Lycoming IO-360

Type: Four-cylinder piston aircraft engine
Manufacturer: Lycoming Engines
Displacement: 361 cu in (5.92 L)
Power: typically 160–210 hp

The Lycoming IO-360 is a family of fuel-injected, air-cooled, horizontally opposed four-cylinder aircraft engines produced by Lycoming Engines. The "360" denotes the piston displacement of approximately 360 cubic inches.

Design and variants

Variants include the carburetted O-360, the fuel-injected IO-360, and the aerobatic AEIO-360 with a modified oil system. Power outputs range from about 160 to 210 horsepower depending on model and compression ratio.

Applications

The IO-360 powers many popular general aviation aircraft, including versions of the Cessna 172, Piper PA-28, Mooney M20 and numerous homebuilt designs.
