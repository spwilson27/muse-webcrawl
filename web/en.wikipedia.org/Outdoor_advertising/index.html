Outdoor advertising

Outdoor advertising, also called out-of-home (OOH) advertising, is advertising that reaches consumers while they are outside their homes. It includes billboards, transit advertising on buses and trains, street furniture such as bus shelters, posters, and digital signage.

Forms

Traditional billboards are large printed or painted boards along highways; digital billboards rotate multiple messages. Transit media place ads on vehicles and in stations. Place-based media appear in venues such as malls, cinemas and airports.

Regulation and effectiveness

Many jurisdictions regulate billboard size and placement for safety and aesthetic reasons; São Paulo's 2007 "Clean City Law" famously removed most outdoor ads. OOH is valued for broad reach and inability to be skipped, and digital OOH has grown rapidly, allowing dayparting and programmatic buying.
