Chronology

Subject: Arrangement of events in time

Methods: Calendars, dating, timelines

Chronology is the science and practice of arranging events in their order of occurrence in time. A chronology may be relative, placing events before or after one another, or absolute, assigning dates through calendars and dating methods.

Historical development

Ancient societies recorded king lists, annals, astronomical observations, and era systems tied to rulers or religious events. Modern historians reconcile calendars, inscriptions, genealogies, archaeology, dendrochronology, radiocarbon dating, and other evidence to build consistent timelines.

Applications

Chronology underpins history, archaeology, geology, biography, law, and project planning. Disputed chronologies can alter interpretations of cultural contact, environmental change, and causation.
