Staff college
Type: Military educational institution
Students: Mid-career officers
Purpose: Staff duties, command, strategy

A staff college is a military educational institution that trains commissioned officers in the administrative, staff and policy aspects of their profession, preparing selected officers for senior staff and command appointments.

History

The model developed in Prussia with institutions for training General Staff officers in the early 19th century, and was imitated widely, including Britain's Staff College, Camberley (founded 1858), France's École supérieure de guerre, and the US Army Command and General Staff College at Fort Leavenworth.

Curriculum

Courses typically last about a year and cover strategy, operational art, joint planning, logistics and leadership, often with officers from allied nations attending.
