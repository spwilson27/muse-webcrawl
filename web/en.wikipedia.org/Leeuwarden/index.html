Leeuwarden

Leeuwarden (West Frisian: Ljouwert) is a city in the northern Netherlands and the capital of the province of Friesland.

History

Settled on a former bay of the Zuiderzee, Leeuwarden received city rights in 1435 and became a residence of the Frisian stadtholders of the House of Nassau-Dietz. Its leaning Oldehove tower is a landmark.

Culture

Leeuwarden was European Capital of Culture in 2018 and is the traditional starting and finishing point of the Elfstedentocht skating tour. The dancer and convicted spy Mata Hari was born there in 1876.
