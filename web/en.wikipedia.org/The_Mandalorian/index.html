The Mandalorian is an American space Western television series created by Jon Favreau for the streaming service Disney+. It is the first live-action series in the Star Wars franchise, beginning five years after the events of Return of the Jedi (1983). It stars Pedro Pascal as the title character, a lone bounty hunter who protects the foundling Grogu from Imperial remnants.

Premise

Din Djarin, a Mandalorian bounty hunter operating in the outer reaches of the galaxy under the New Republic, is hired to recover a mysterious child, Grogu, of the same species as Yoda. Choosing to protect the child instead, he becomes a fugitive while confronting Moff Gideon and Imperial forces, reuniting with other Mandalorians, and ultimately joining the effort to reclaim the planet Mandalore.

Production

The series pioneered the use of "StageCraft" virtual production — vast LED video walls displaying real-time rendered environments — which reshaped television production techniques. Favreau serves as showrunner, with Dave Filoni as executive producer. Ludwig Göransson composed the distinctive score. The first season premiered with Disney+ on November 12, 2019; further seasons followed in 2020 and 2023, with a feature film, The Mandalorian and Grogu, announced.

Reception

The series was praised for its visual style, performances, and return to the Western and samurai-film influences of the original Star Wars. It won multiple Primetime Emmy Awards and helped launch Disney+ as a major streaming platform. Grogu, popularly called "Baby Yoda," became a cultural phenomenon.
