Emotet

Emotet is a family of malware and a cybercrime operation first identified in 2014. It originally functioned as a banking trojan stealing financial information, and later evolved into a loader that delivers other malware, including ransomware, to infected computers.

Operation

Emotet spreads mainly through spam emails containing malicious attachments or links, often replying within existing email threads to appear legitimate. Once installed it can steal email contacts, spread laterally, and download additional payloads such as TrickBot and Ryuk ransomware. Operators offered access to infected machines to other criminals as a service.

Takedown and return

In January 2021 an international law-enforcement operation coordinated by Europol and Eurojust disrupted Emotet's command-and-control infrastructure and pushed an uninstall update. The botnet reappeared in late 2021 and continued in altered forms, remaining one of the most widely distributed malware threats.
