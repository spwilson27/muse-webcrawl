Watford is a town and borough in Hertfordshire, England, about 15 miles (24 km) northwest of central London, on the banks of the River Colne. It developed as a market town on the route between London and the Midlands and grew rapidly with the arrival of the railway in the 19th century. Today it is a major regional retail, business and leisure centre, home to Watford Football Club and the Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden, where the Harry Potter films were made.

History

The name Watford is thought to derive from the Old English waþ ford ("hunters' ford") or from a ford over the River Colne used by travellers. The town is recorded in a charter of 1007, and in 1100 Henry I granted a charter for a market. St Albans Abbey held the manor for centuries. The Grand Union Canal (1798) and the London and Birmingham Railway (1837–38) brought industry, notably printing and brewing; Benskin's Brewery was a major employer. In the 20th century Watford became one of Britain's principal printing centres.

Geography

Watford lies just inside the M25 motorway, which bounds the town to the south, and is served by the M1 motorway to the east. The River Colne and the Grand Union Canal run through the town. Cassiobury Park, formed from the former grounds of Cassiobury House, is the largest public park. Neighbouring settlements include Bushey, Rickmansworth, Croxley Green and Garston.

Transport

Watford station is the terminus of the Metropolitan line of the London Underground, while Watford Junction is a major interchange on the West Coast Main Line with fast services to London Euston, and also serves London Overground trains. Watford High Street station lies on the Overground line to Euston.

Sport and culture

Watford F.C., nicknamed the Hornets, play at Vicarage Road and have spent several seasons in the Premier League; the club is famously associated with its former chairman, the singer Elton John. The Watford Colosseum and Watford Palace Theatre are notable venues. Leavesden Studios, just north of the town, houses the popular Warner Bros. Studio Tour London – The Making of Harry Potter.
