Munich

Munich (German: München) is the capital and most populous city of Bavaria, Germany. With a population of around 1.5 million, it is the third-largest city in Germany after Berlin and Hamburg. The city lies on the banks of the River Isar, north of the Alps.

History

Munich was founded in 1158 by Henry the Lion, Duke of Saxony and Bavaria, near a settlement of Benedictine monks — a reference preserved in the city's name and its coat of arms, the Münchner Kindl. It became the capital of Bavaria in 1506. In the 20th century Munich was the birthplace of the Nazi Party, the site of the 1938 Munich Agreement, and host of the 1972 Summer Olympics, marred by the massacre of Israeli athletes.

Culture and economy

Munich is a major centre of art, science, technology and publishing, home to companies such as BMW and Siemens, the Ludwig Maximilian University, and museums including the Alte Pinakothek and Deutsches Museum. It is internationally famous for the Oktoberfest beer festival, held annually since 1810, and for the FC Bayern Munich football club.
