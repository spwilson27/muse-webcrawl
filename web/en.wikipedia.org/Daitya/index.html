Daitya

In Hindu mythology, the Daityas are a clan of asuras, the children of Diti and the sage Kashyapa, who fought against their half-brothers the Devas (children of Aditi) for dominion over the universe.

Mythology

Famous Daityas include Hiranyakashipu, slain by Vishnu as Narasimha; Hiranyaksha, slain by Varaha; and Bali, subdued by Vamana. The churning of the ocean of milk (Samudra Manthana) is a central episode in their rivalry with the gods.

Character

Though often depicted as powerful antagonists, Daityas are not uniformly evil; figures such as Prahlada and Bali are celebrated for devotion and righteousness.
