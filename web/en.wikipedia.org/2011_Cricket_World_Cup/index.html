The 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup was the tenth edition of the Cricket World Cup, hosted jointly by India, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh from 19 February to 2 April 2011. Fourteen teams competed. Pakistan had originally been a co-host but was stripped of its matches following the 2009 attack on the Sri Lankan team in Lahore.

Tournament

The format featured two groups of seven, followed by quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final. India defeated arch-rivals Pakistan in a celebrated semi-final at Mohali, while Sri Lanka beat New Zealand in Colombo. Ireland produced the tournament's great upset, chasing down 327 against England thanks to Kevin O'Brien's 50-ball century, the fastest in World Cup history.

Final

In the final at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai on 2 April, Sri Lanka made 274 for 6, built around an unbeaten century by Mahela Jayawardene. India recovered from early wickets through Gautam Gambhir's 97 and an unbeaten 91 from captain MS Dhoni, who finished the match with a six. India won by six wickets, becoming the first team to win the World Cup on home soil and claiming their second title, 28 years after their first.
