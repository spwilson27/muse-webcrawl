Rysy is a mountain in the High Tatras on the border between Poland and Slovakia. It has three summits: the north-western peak, lying in Poland at 2,499 metres, is the highest point in Poland; the middle summit (2,503 m) and the south-eastern summit (2,473 m) lie in Slovakia, where the middle peak is the mountain's highest.

Climbing

Rysy is one of the most popular climbs in the Tatras, accessible in summer from the Polish side via the Morskie Oko lake and the Chata pod Rysami hut on the Slovak side. The first recorded ascent was made in 1840 by Eduard Blásy and guide Ján Ruman Driečny. The border summit is marked by a plaque, and the mountain sits within the Tatra national parks of both countries.
