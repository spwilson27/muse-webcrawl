The Italian motorcycle Grand Prix is a motorcycling event that is part of the FIM Grand Prix motorcycle racing season. Before 1993 it was known as the Nations Grand Prix (Gran Premio delle Nazioni). It has been held since the first world championship season in 1949.

History

The race was originally held at Monza and later at Imola, Misano, and other circuits. Since 1994 it has been staged almost continuously at the Mugello Circuit in Tuscany, a fast, flowing track that is a favourite of riders and fans. The event is famous for the passionate support of the Italian crowd and for home riders, notably Valentino Rossi, who won the premier-class race at Mugello seven consecutive times from 2002 to 2008.

Most successful riders

Giacomo Agostini is the most successful rider in the history of the event across all classes, while Rossi holds the record for consecutive premier-class victories at Mugello. The race was cancelled in 2020 because of the COVID-19 pandemic and returned in 2021.
