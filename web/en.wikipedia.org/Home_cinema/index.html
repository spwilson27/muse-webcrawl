Home cinema

Home cinema, also called a home theater in North America, is a home entertainment system that seeks to reproduce a movie theater experience using video and audio equipment in a private room, typically combining a large display or projector, surround sound, and comfortable seating.

Components

Systems range from a television paired with a soundbar to dedicated rooms with projectors, projection screens, AV receivers, multiple loudspeakers and subwoofers arranged in 5.1, 7.1, or Dolby Atmos configurations, acoustic treatment, and controlled lighting.

History

Home cinema grew with videocassettes and LaserDisc in the 1980s, expanded with DVD and surround formats in the 1990s, and matured with high-definition Blu-ray, flat-panel televisions, and streaming services in the 2000s and 2010s.
