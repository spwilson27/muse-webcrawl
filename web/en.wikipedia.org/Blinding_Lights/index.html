Blinding Lights

The Weeknd's "Blinding Lights" is among the most-streamed songs of all time.

"Blinding Lights" is a song by Canadian singer the Weeknd, released on November 29, 2019, as the second single from his fourth studio album, After Hours (2020). Written and produced by the Weeknd with Max Martin and Oscar Holter, the synthwave and electropop track is driven by a pulsing synthesizer riff reminiscent of 1980s pop, with lyrics describing driving through the night longing for a lover.

Commercial success

The song became one of the most successful singles of all time. It reached number one in more than 30 countries, including the United States, where it spent four weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100 and a record-setting run in the top ten — becoming the first song to spend a full year in the Hot 100's top 10. In November 2021, Billboard named it the greatest Hot 100 hit of all time, surpassing "The Twist." In January 2023 it became the most-streamed song in Spotify history, passing Ed Sheeran's "Shape of You," and it was the first song to surpass four billion streams on the platform.

Reception and legacy

Critics praised the song's production and retro styling. Its music video continues the bruised, red-suited persona the Weeknd maintained throughout the After Hours era. Despite being snubbed at the 63rd Grammy Awards — a controversy that led the Weeknd to boycott the ceremony — the song won numerous other awards and is widely cited as a defining hit of 2020 and a landmark of the streaming era.
