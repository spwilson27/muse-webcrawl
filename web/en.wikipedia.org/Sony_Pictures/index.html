Sony Pictures Entertainment Inc. is an American diversified multinational mass media and entertainment studio conglomerate, a subsidiary of the Japanese Sony Group Corporation. Based at the Sony Pictures Studios lot in Culver City, California, it encompasses motion picture production and distribution (Columbia Pictures, TriStar Pictures, Screen Gems, and Sony Pictures Animation), television production (Sony Pictures Television), and networks and streaming interests including Crunchyroll.

History

Sony entered Hollywood by acquiring Columbia Pictures from Coca-Cola in 1989 for $3.4 billion, following its purchase of CBS Records. The entertainment assets were consolidated as Sony Pictures Entertainment in 1991. Notable franchise successes include Spider-Man, Jumanji, Men in Black, Ghostbusters, and the James Bond films (distributed in partnership with MGM for a period). In 2014 the studio was the target of a major cyberattack, attributed by the U.S. government to North Korea, in retaliation for the comedy The Interview.

Divisions

Sony Pictures Television is one of the largest television production companies, producing shows such as Breaking Bad, The Crown, Jeopardy!, and Wheel of Fortune. Sony Pictures Animation produced the Spider-Verse films. The company also owns the anime streaming service Crunchyroll through a joint venture with Aniplex.
