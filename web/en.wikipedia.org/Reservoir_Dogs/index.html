Running time: 99 minutes

Reservoir Dogs is a 1992 American crime film written and directed by Quentin Tarantino in his feature-length debut. It stars Harvey Keitel, Tim Roth, Chris Penn, Steve Buscemi, Lawrence Tierney, Michael Madsen, Tarantino, and Edward Bunker as a group of jewel thieves whose planned heist of a jewellery store goes terribly wrong.

Plot

The film is told largely in flashback and never shows the robbery itself. After the botched heist, the surviving criminals regroup at a warehouse and begin to suspect that one of them is an undercover police officer — Mr. Orange, who has been shot and is bleeding out. Suspicion and violence escalate until nearly all of them are dead.

Reception and legacy

Premiering at the Sundance Film Festival, the film was a critical success noted for its dialogue, nonlinear structure, and stylised violence. It is frequently ranked among the greatest independent films and established Tarantino's career; its themes of loyalty and betrayal and its soundtrack of 1970s pop songs became highly influential.
