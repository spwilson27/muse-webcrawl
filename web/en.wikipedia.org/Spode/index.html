Spode

Spode is an English brand of pottery and homewares produced by Portmeirion Group in Stoke-on-Trent, England. The company was founded by Josiah Spode in the late 18th century and is renowned for the development of bone china and for patterns such as Blue Italian.

History

Josiah Spode established his works in Stoke in 1770. His son, Josiah Spode II, perfected the bone china body around the turn of the 19th century and pioneered underglaze blue transfer printing, introducing the famous Blue Italian pattern in 1816. The firm later traded as Copeland & Garrett and W. T. Copeland before the Spode name was revived.

Products

Spode is known for tableware and giftware patterns including Blue Italian, Woodland, and Christmas Tree (designed in 1938), which became one of the most popular holiday china patterns. The Spode Museum Trust preserves an extensive archive and collection at the Stoke site.

Ownership

After periods within the Royal Worcester group and administration in 2008, the Spode brand was acquired by Portmeirion Group in 2009, which continues production in Stoke-on-Trent.
