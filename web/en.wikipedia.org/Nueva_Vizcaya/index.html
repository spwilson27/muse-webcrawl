Nueva Vizcaya is a landlocked province in the Cagayan Valley region of Luzon, Philippines. Its capital is Bayombong. The province is largely mountainous, situated between the Sierra Madre and Caraballo ranges, and is known for citrus production and as a watershed area for northern Luzon.

Geography and history

Nueva Vizcaya was created in 1839, named after Vizcaya (Biscay) in Spain. It is bordered by Ifugao, Isabela, Quirino, Aurora, Nueva Ecija, Pangasinan and Benguet. Indigenous peoples of the province include the Isinai, Gaddang, Ilongot (Bugkalot) and Ifugao groups.

Economy

Agriculture dominates, with rice, corn, citrus and vegetables; the province markets itself as a citrus capital of the north. Mining, including the Didipio gold and copper mine, is also significant and has been locally controversial.
