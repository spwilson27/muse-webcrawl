The United Federation of Planets (UFP) is a fictional interstellar federal republic depicted in the Star Trek media franchise. Composed of numerous member worlds spread across the Alpha and Beta Quadrants of the Milky Way, it is depicted as a post-scarcity, democratic alliance dedicated to exploration, science, diplomacy, and the peaceful coexistence of species.

Background

The Federation was founded in 2161 by four species: humans, Vulcans, Andorians, and Tellarites, following the Earth–Romulan War. Its institutions include the Federation Council, the office of President (headquartered in Paris), and Starfleet (headquartered in San Francisco), which serves as its exploratory and defensive arm. By the 24th century the Federation comprised more than 150 member worlds.

Principles

The Federation Charter enshrines democratic government, the rights of sentient beings, and non-interference in the internal development of less advanced civilizations, codified as the Prime Directive. Its economy is portrayed as post-scarcity, with replicator technology eliminating material want and money largely obsolete on Earth.

In popular culture

Gene Roddenberry modeled aspects of the Federation on the United Nations and an idealized liberal democracy. Political scientists have debated it variously as an image of American liberal hegemony, a utopian social democracy, or a benign empire.
