South Beach

South Beach (SoBe)
City: Miami Beach, Florida
Location: southern end of the Miami Beach barrier island, below 23rd Street
Known for: Art Deco district, beaches, nightlife

South Beach, sometimes nicknamed SoBe, is a neighbourhood of Miami Beach, Florida, occupying the southern portion of the barrier island between Biscayne Bay and the Atlantic Ocean. It is famous for its wide sandy beach, its nightlife, and the Miami Beach Architectural District — the largest concentration of Art Deco architecture in the world, with hundreds of pastel-coloured hotels and apartment buildings from the 1920s–1940s along Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue.

History

The area was developed in the 1910s and 1920s by pioneers such as Carl Fisher, who promoted Miami Beach as a winter resort. After decades of decline in the 1970s, when it was largely a retirement community, preservationists led by Barbara Baer Capitman secured historic-district listing in 1979. A revival followed in the 1980s and 1990s, driven by fashion photography, film and television (notably Miami Vice), and an influx of restaurants, clubs and hotels.

Attractions

  • Ocean Drive and the Art Deco Historic District
  • Lincoln Road, a pedestrian shopping and dining street
  • The former Versace mansion (Casa Casuarina)
  • Lummus Park and the beach walk
  • South Pointe Park at the island's southern tip

Culture

South Beach is a major centre of nightlife and events such as Art Basel Miami Beach and the South Beach Wine and Food Festival, and it has a large LGBTQ community. It is also notably vulnerable to sea-level rise and "sunny-day" tidal flooding.
