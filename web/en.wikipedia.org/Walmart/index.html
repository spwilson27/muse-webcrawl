Walmart Inc. is an American multinational retail corporation that operates a chain of hypermarkets (also called supercenters), discount department stores, and grocery stores in the United States, headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas. The company was founded by brothers Sam and James "Bud" Walton in nearby Rogers, Arkansas in 1962 as a single discount store. It also owns and operates Sam's Club retail warehouses.

Walmart is the world's largest company by revenue, according to the Fortune Global 500 list, with annual revenue exceeding US$600 billion. It is also the largest private employer in the world, with 2.1 million employees. It is a publicly traded family-owned business, as the company is controlled by the Walton family. Sam Walton's heirs own over 50 percent of Walmart through both their holding company Walton Enterprises and their individual holdings.

History

Sam Walton, who had operated Ben Franklin variety stores since 1945, opened the first Walmart Discount City store at 719 West Walnut Avenue in Rogers, Arkansas, on July 2, 1962. Within its first five years, the company expanded to 24 stores across Arkansas. In 1968, it opened its first stores outside Arkansas in Sikeston, Missouri, and Claremore, Oklahoma. The company was incorporated under the name Wal-Mart, Inc. on October 31, 1969, and changed its name to Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. in 1970. That same year, the company began distributing stock publicly.

The 1980s saw continued growth: Walmart reached $1 billion in annual sales in 1980, opened the first Sam's Club in 1983, and opened the first Walmart Supercenter, combining a supermarket with a department store, in Washington, Missouri, in 1988. Walmart surpassed Toys "R" Us in toy sales in the late 1990s and overtook Sears as the largest retailer in the United States. In February 2018, the company changed its legal name from Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. to Walmart Inc.

Operations

Walmart operates under 46 different names in 19 countries. In the United States, operations are divided into Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club U.S. Stores in the U.S. include Supercenters, Discount Stores, Neighborhood Markets, and small-format concepts. Internationally, Walmart operates in countries including Mexico (Walmart de México y Centroamérica), Canada, Chile, China, and India (through its majority stake in Flipkart and PhonePe).

Corporate affairs

Walmart is headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas. Its business model is based on selling a wide variety of general merchandise at low prices, summarized in its slogan "Save Money. Live Better." The company has faced criticism concerning its labor practices, effects on small businesses, working conditions, and sourcing of products abroad.
