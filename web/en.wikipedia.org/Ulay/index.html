Ulay

Ulay (born Frank Uwe Laysiepen; 30 November 1943 – 2 March 2020) was a German artist based in Amsterdam and Ljubljana, best known for his pioneering work in Polaroid photography and performance art.

In the early 1970s Ulay experimented with large-format Polaroid self-portraits exploring identity and gender. From 1976 to 1988 he collaborated intensively with Marina Abramović, with whom he also lived. Their "Relation Works" tested endurance, trust, and the boundaries between two bodies and identities, in pieces such as Rest Energy (1980), in which they balanced a drawn bow and arrow pointed at Abramović's heart. Their collaboration ended with The Great Wall Walk (1988), in which each walked from opposite ends of the Great Wall of China to meet—and part—in the middle.

The pair were famously reunited in 2010 during Abramović's The Artist Is Present at the Museum of Modern Art in New York, when Ulay sat silently opposite her. He later won a lawsuit against Abramović over royalties and joint authorship of their works. Ulay died in Ljubljana, Slovenia, in 2020.
