Ulster University (Irish: Ollscoil Uladh; Ulster Scots: Universitie o Ulstèr) is a multi-campus public university located in Northern Ireland, United Kingdom.

History

It originated as the New University of Ulster at Coleraine in 1968 and merged with Ulster Polytechnic in 1984 to form the University of Ulster, rebranded as Ulster University in 2014.

Campuses and study

Major campuses are in Belfast (where a large city-centre campus opened in the 2020s), Coleraine, and Derry/Londonderry (Magee). The university is known for programs in health sciences, computing, business, and art and design.
