Thomas Howard, 2nd Duke of Norfolk (1443 – 21 May 1524) was an English nobleman and soldier, grandfather of two queens of England — Anne Boleyn and Catherine Howard — and great-grandfather of Queen Elizabeth I through the Boleyn line. He restored the Howard family's fortunes after his father's death fighting for Richard III at Bosworth.

Career

Howard was attainted after Bosworth in 1485 but gradually won the trust of Henry VII, recovering the earldom of Surrey and serving as the king's lieutenant in the north. Under Henry VIII he achieved his greatest triumph on 9 September 1513, commanding the English army that destroyed the invading Scots at the Battle of Flodden, where King James IV was killed.

Later life

Restored to the dukedom of Norfolk in 1514 as a reward for Flodden, he remained a dominant figure at court into his late seventies, serving as Lord High Treasurer and Earl Marshal. He died at Framlingham Castle in 1524, aged about 81, the premier peer of England.
