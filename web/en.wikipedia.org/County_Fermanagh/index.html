County Fermanagh

County Fermanagh (Irish: Contae Fhear Manach) is one of the six counties of Northern Ireland and one of the traditional thirty-two counties of Ireland. It is in Ulster, in the south-west of Northern Ireland, with a population of about 63,000.

Geography

The county town is Enniskillen. Fermanagh is dominated by Lough Erne, Upper and Lower, linked to the Shannon-Erne Waterway, and is known as Ireland's lakeland. Attractions include Devenish Island, Marble Arch Caves, Castle Coole and Enniskillen Castle.
