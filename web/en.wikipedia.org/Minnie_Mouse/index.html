Minnie Mouse is an American cartoon character created by Walt Disney and Ub Iwerks. An anthropomorphic mouse, she debuted alongside Mickey Mouse in the short film Steamboat Willie, released on November 18, 1928, and has remained Mickey's sweetheart and one of the Walt Disney Company's most recognizable characters.

Character

Minnie is typically depicted as a cheerful, fashionable mouse wearing a dress — most famously a red dress with white polka dots — a large bow, and high-heeled shoes. In the early cartoons she was often a damsel in distress rescued by Mickey, but over time her characterization developed into a more independent figure with a wide circle of stories in comics, television, and theme parks. Her pet cat is Figaro, and her best friend is Daisy Duck. Her full name is given as Minerva Mouse, though it is rarely used.

Voices and honours

Minnie was first voiced by Walt Disney himself, and for over thirty years (1986–2019) by Russi Taylor, who was married to Wayne Allwine, the voice of Mickey. In January 2018 Minnie received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, forty years after Mickey's, on the occasion of her 90th anniversary. She remains a central figure of Disney merchandising and parks worldwide.
