The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel is an American period comedy-drama television series created by Amy Sherman-Palladino and premiered on Amazon Prime Video in 2017. It is set mainly in the late 1950s and early 1960s in New York City.

Premise

Miriam "Midge" Maisel, an Upper West Side housewife, discovers a talent for stand-up comedy after her husband leaves her. With her manager Susie Myerson, she pursues a career in the male-dominated world of comedy clubs, touring, and television.

Reception

The series won numerous awards, including the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series in 2018, and Golden Globe Awards for Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy. Rachel Brosnahan won an Emmy and two Golden Globes for her performance.
