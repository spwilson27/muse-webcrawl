Association of American Geographers

The American Association of Geographers (AAG) is a non-profit scientific and educational society aimed at advancing the understanding, study, and importance of geography and related fields. Founded in 1904 in Philadelphia as the Association of American Geographers, the organisation adopted its current name in 2016 to reflect its increasingly international membership.

History

The association was founded by William Morris Davis, the influential physical geographer, and held its first meeting in December 1904. In 1948 it merged with the rival American Society for Professional Geographers. Its headquarters are in Washington, D.C.

Activities

The AAG publishes the Annals of the American Association of Geographers, The Professional Geographer, and the AAG Review of Books, and holds a large annual meeting attended by thousands of geographers. It is organised into regional divisions and numerous specialty groups covering topics from GIS to political geography.

Membership

Membership includes academic geographers, students, and professionals in government, business, and non-profit organisations from around the world. The association also presents awards, including the AAG Honors, recognising lifetime contributions to the discipline.
