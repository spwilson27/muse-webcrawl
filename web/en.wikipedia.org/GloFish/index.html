GloFish

GloFish are genetically modified zebrafish that fluoresce in bright colors.

GloFish is a trademarked brand of genetically engineered fluorescent fish. The name is also a registered trademark in several countries. The original GloFish were zebrafish (Danio rerio) modified with fluorescent protein genes originally discovered in jellyfish and sea anemones, giving them bright red, green, and orange fluorescence. The fish were originally developed in Singapore as a means of detecting environmental pollution, and were introduced to the United States aquarium market in 2003, becoming the first genetically modified animals sold as pets.

Development

Scientists at the National University of Singapore, led by Zhiyuan Gong, inserted genes for green fluorescent protein (GFP) and later red and other fluorescent proteins into zebrafish embryos. The original research goal was a fish that would fluoresce selectively in the presence of pollutants. A Texas-based company, Yorktown Technologies, licensed the fish for the ornamental market and launched them in the United States in late 2003 after concluding, in consultation with regulators, that the fish posed no greater environmental risk than ordinary zebrafish.

Regulation and controversy

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration stated in 2003 that, absent evidence of a threat to public health, it saw no basis to regulate the fish. California initially banned them under a prohibition on transgenic fish before granting an exception in 2015; they remain restricted in some jurisdictions, including the European Union. Animal-welfare and environmental groups objected to selling genetically modified pets without formal environmental assessment, while supporters argued the fish are sterilized-risk minimal, cannot survive in temperate wild waters, and are no more likely to escape or establish than the zebrafish already traded worldwide.

Species and colors

The brand has expanded beyond zebrafish to include tetras, barbs, rainbow sharks, bettas, and angelfish in colors marketed as Starfire Red, Electric Green, Sunburst Orange, Cosmic Blue, Galactic Purple, and Moonrise Pink.
