Court of Justice of the European Union

The Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU), seated in Luxembourg, interprets EU law to ensure its uniform application across member states. It comprises the Court of Justice (one judge per member state, assisted by advocates general) and the General Court.

Jurisdiction

The Court rules on preliminary references from national courts, infringement actions brought by the European Commission, and annulment actions. Landmark doctrines include direct effect (Van Gend en Loos, 1963) and supremacy of EU law (Costa v ENEL, 1964), central to European integration.
