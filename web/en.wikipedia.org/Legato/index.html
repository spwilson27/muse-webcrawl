Legato

In music performance and notation, legato (Italian for "tied together") indicates that notes are played smoothly and connected, with no perceptible silence between them.

Technique

Legato is the opposite of staccato. It is often marked with a slur over the phrase. On string instruments it is achieved within one bow stroke; on wind instruments by tonguing only the first note; on guitar by hammer-ons and pull-offs; and on piano by overlapping finger release and pedal use.
