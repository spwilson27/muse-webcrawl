Christmas

Christmas is an annual festival commemorating the birth of Jesus Christ, observed on 25 December by billions of people worldwide as a religious and cultural celebration.

History

The date was established by the Church in the fourth century. Traditions absorbed older midwinter festivals and developed over centuries: Christmas trees, cards, carols, feasting and gift-giving, popularised in the Victorian era partly through Charles Dickens's A Christmas Carol.

Customs

Celebrations include Nativity plays, church services, decorated trees and lights, the exchange of gifts associated with Saint Nicholas and Santa Claus, and festive meals. Some Eastern Orthodox churches observe Christmas on 7 January following the Julian calendar.
