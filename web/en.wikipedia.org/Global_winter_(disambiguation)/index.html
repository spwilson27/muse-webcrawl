Global winter (disambiguation)

Several distinct phenomena are described as a "global winter".

"Global winter" may refer to several hypothetical or historical episodes of worldwide cooling:

  • Nuclear winter — the severe global cooling hypothesized to follow a nuclear war, caused by soot from burning cities injected into the stratosphere.
  • Volcanic winter — the reduction in global temperatures caused by a major volcanic eruption, such as the "Year Without a Summer" of 1816 following the eruption of Mount Tambora.
  • Impact winter — cooling caused by the dust and aerosols thrown into the atmosphere by a large asteroid or comet impact, implicated in the extinction of the non-avian dinosaurs.
  • Snowball Earth — the hypothesis that the Earth's surface became entirely or nearly entirely frozen during parts of the Cryogenian period.
  • Little Ice Age — a period of regional cooling, most pronounced in the North Atlantic, from roughly the 16th to the 19th century.
