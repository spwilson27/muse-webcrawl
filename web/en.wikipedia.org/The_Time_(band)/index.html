The Time, also known as Morris Day and the Time and The Original 7ven, is an American funk rock band formed in Minneapolis in 1981. They are best known as a protégé act of the musician Prince, and for their work in the 1984 film Purple Rain, in which they appear as rivals to Prince's character. The band is fronted by the flamboyant Morris Day, whose cocky, comic persona anchors the group's sound and stage act.

History

Prince assembled the Time as part of his deal with Warner Bros. Records, drawing members largely from the Minneapolis funk band Flyte Tyme. Although Prince wrote and performed much of the material on the group's early records himself, the band became a formidable live act in its own right. Their debut album The Time (1981) yielded the singles "Get It Up" and "Cool," followed by What Time Is It? (1982) and Ice Cream Castle (1984), which featured the hit singles "Jungle Love" and "The Bird," both showcased in Purple Rain.

After the group disbanded in the mid-1980s, producers Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis went on to enormous success in their own right. The original lineup reunited for the album Pandemonium (1990), led by the single "Jerk Out," and again in later years under the name The Original 7ven for the album Condensate (2011), touring as Morris Day and the Time.
