Happiness

Happiness is a positive emotional state characterised by feelings of joy, contentment, satisfaction, and well-being. It is studied in philosophy, psychology, and economics, and pursued as a central human goal in many cultures.

Philosophy

Aristotle identified happiness (eudaimonia) with living well and flourishing through virtue over a lifetime. Utilitarians such as Jeremy Bentham and John Stuart Mill treated happiness as the ultimate criterion of right action. Many religious traditions link happiness to spiritual practice, virtue, or liberation.

Psychology and measurement

Positive psychology studies the conditions of flourishing, including relationships, meaning, engagement, and accomplishment. Research suggests genetics, circumstances, and intentional activities all contribute. Instruments such as the World Happiness Report rank countries by self-reported life satisfaction.
