Bloemenmarkt

LocationSingel canal, Amsterdam, Netherlands
Established1862
TypeFloating flower market

The Bloemenmarkt is the world's only floating flower market, located in Amsterdam, Netherlands. Founded in 1862, it is situated on the Singel canal between the Koningsplein and the Muntplein, in the city's southern canal belt.

The market's stalls stand on houseboats, a legacy of the era when flowers and plants were delivered daily to the city by barge from growing regions outside Amsterdam. At the time of its founding, the Singel was one of the city's main waterways and the market was held where the Amstel river flowed into it.

Today

The modern market sells tulip bulbs, flowers, seeds, bonsai trees, and souvenirs to a largely tourist clientele. Bulbs sold at the market are packaged for export, and many stalls cater to visitors seeking a portable reminder of the Netherlands' famous bulb trade. The market is open year-round and is one of Amsterdam's most popular attractions.
