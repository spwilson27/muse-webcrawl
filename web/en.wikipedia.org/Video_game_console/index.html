Video game console

A video game console is an electronic device that outputs a video signal or image to display a video game that can typically be played with a game controller. Consoles are usually connected to a television (home consoles), have a built-in screen (handheld consoles), or combine both (hybrid consoles such as the Nintendo Switch). The first commercial home console was the Magnavox Odyssey in 1972; the industry later centred on manufacturers including Atari, Nintendo, Sega, Sony (PlayStation) and Microsoft (Xbox).

Generations

Consoles are commonly grouped into generations of roughly contemporaneous hardware, from the first generation of dedicated Pong-style machines through the cartridge-based systems of the 1980s, the 3D and optical-disc systems of the 1990s, and the networked high-definition systems of the 2000s and beyond. The ninth generation, beginning in 2020, includes the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
