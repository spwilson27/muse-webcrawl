David Allen (author)

David Allen (born 28 December 1945) is an American author and productivity consultant best known as the creator of Getting Things Done (GTD), a personal-productivity methodology described in his 2001 book Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity.

Getting Things Done

GTD advocates capturing all tasks and commitments in a trusted external system, clarifying them into next actions, and organising them by context, so that the mind is freed from remembering them — a state Allen calls "mind like water". Allen founded the David Allen Company to provide coaching and training in the method, and later books include Ready for Anything (2003) and Making It All Work (2008).
