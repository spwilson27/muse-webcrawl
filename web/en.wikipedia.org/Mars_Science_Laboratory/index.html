Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) is a NASA robotic space probe mission that launched on 26 November 2011 and landed the Curiosity rover in Gale crater on Mars on 6 August 2012. Curiosity is a car-sized, nuclear-powered rover carrying the most advanced scientific payload ever sent to the Martian surface.

Landing

Because of the rover's size, the mission pioneered a new entry, descent and landing system: a supersonic parachute, powered descent stage and "sky crane" manoeuvre that lowered the rover to the surface on cables while the descent stage hovered above.

Findings

Curiosity found evidence that Gale crater once contained a freshwater lake with conditions favourable to microbial life, detected organic molecules preserved in ancient mudstones, and measured variable methane in the atmosphere. The rover has spent years climbing the sedimentary layers of Mount Sharp (Aeolis Mons) at the crater's centre.
