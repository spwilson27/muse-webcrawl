Saab AB is a Swedish aerospace and defence company founded in 1937 as Svenska Aeroplan Aktiebolaget. Headquartered in Stockholm, it develops fighter aircraft, radar and sensor systems, missiles, submarines and combat systems for customers worldwide.

Products

Saab's best-known products include the JAS 39 Gripen multirole fighter, the GlobalEye airborne early warning aircraft, Giraffe radars, the RBS 15 anti-ship missile, Carl-Gustaf recoilless weapons, and A26 submarines built by Saab Kockums.

History

Saab was created to provide Sweden with domestically produced military aircraft. It later diversified into automobiles: the car division Saab Automobile was spun off and eventually sold to General Motors; car production ended in 2011, and the defence company has no connection to the former carmaker beyond the shared name and heritage.
