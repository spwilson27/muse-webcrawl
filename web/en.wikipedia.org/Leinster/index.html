Leinster

Leinster (Irish: Laighin or Cúige Laighean) is one of the four provinces of Ireland, situated in the east and southeast of the island. It comprises twelve counties including Dublin, and is the most populous province, with Dublin — Ireland's capital — at its centre.

History

Historically the Kingdom of Leinster was one of the kingdoms of Gaelic Ireland. Its territory shifted over the centuries, and following the Norman invasion and later administrative changes it took roughly its modern form. The province's flag shows a golden harp on a green field.

Geography and cities

Leinster includes the cities of Dublin, Kilkenny and Wexford, the Wicklow Mountains, and fertile lowlands drained by rivers such as the Liffey, Boyne and Barrow. See also Leinster Rugby.
