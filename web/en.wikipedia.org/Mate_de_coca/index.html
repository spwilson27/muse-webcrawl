Mate de coca

TypeHerbal tea
OriginAndes, South America
Main ingredientCoca leaves

Mate de coca is an herbal tea made from the leaves of the coca plant (Erythroxylum coca), widely consumed in the Andean regions of Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador, and northern Argentina. It is traditionally used to alleviate altitude sickness (soroche) and as a mild stimulant.

Use and legality

The tea is served in tea bags (matecitos) or loose leaves steeped in hot water and is legal and socially normal in Andean countries. Because coca leaves contain trace alkaloids, coca tea is prohibited in many other countries.
