Kingfisher is an Indian lager beer brewed by the United Breweries Group and one of the best-known Indian consumer brands. Long controlled by Vijay Mallya’s UB Group and now majority-owned by Heineken, Kingfisher has historically held the largest share of the Indian beer market and is exported widely, often associated abroad with Indian restaurants.

History and products

The Kingfisher name derives from a brand first brewed in 1857; United Breweries relaunched Kingfisher Premium lager in 1978. Variants have included Kingfisher Strong, a higher-alcohol strong lager that became one of India’s biggest-selling beers, as well as Kingfisher Ultra and flavoured extensions such as Radler.

Marketing

Kingfisher built its image around the slogan "The King of Good Times", sponsorship of the Kingfisher Calendar, Indian cricket and Formula One through the Force India team, and the short-lived Kingfisher Airlines (2003–2012), whose collapse became part of the wider UB Group financial scandal.
