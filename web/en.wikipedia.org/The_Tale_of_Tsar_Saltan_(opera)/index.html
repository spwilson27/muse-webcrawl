The Tale of Tsar Saltan (opera)

[Placeholder image: The_Tale_of_Tsar_Saltan_(opera).png]

The Tale of Tsar Saltan (Russian: Сказка о царе Салтане) is an opera in four acts by Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov, with a libretto by Vladimir Belsky, based on Alexander Pushkin's 1831 poem. Composed in 1899–1900, it premiered in Moscow in 1900.

Music

Its best-known excerpt is the orchestral interlude 'Flight of the Bumblebee', depicting Prince Guidon, transformed into a bumblebee, flying to visit his father. The opera tells of Tsar Saltan, his banished wife and son, the magical Swan-Bird, and the wonders of the island of Buyan.
