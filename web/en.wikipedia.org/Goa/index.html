Goa is a state on the southwestern coast of India within the Konkan region, geographically separated from the Deccan highlands by the Western Ghats. It is India's smallest state by area and its fourth-smallest by population. Located in West India, it is bordered by the states of Maharashtra to the north and Karnataka to the east and south, with the Arabian Sea forming its western coast.

Panaji is the state's capital, while Vasco da Gama is its largest city. The historic city of Margão in Goa still exhibits the cultural influence of the Portuguese, who first voyaged to the subcontinent in the early 16th century and conquered it soon after. Goa was a former state of the Portuguese Empire.

History

Rock art engravings found in Goa are some of the earliest traces of human life in India. The region was ruled by a succession of Indian kingdoms, including the Mauryas, Satavahanas, Chalukyas, and the Kadambas, before coming under the Delhi Sultanate and later the Vijayanagara Empire. The Portuguese arrived in 1510, when Afonso de Albuquerque conquered the city of Goa, beginning over 450 years of Portuguese rule — the longest colonial tenure in India. Goa was annexed by India in December 1961 in a military operation codenamed Operation Vijay, and it became a full state of India in 1987.

Geography and economy

Goa covers about 3,702 square kilometres. Its rivers include the Mandovi and the Zuari, and its coastline stretches for about 160 kilometres. Tourism is Goa's primary industry, focused on its beaches, churches, and temples; it is also known for mining, pharmaceuticals, and fishing. Goa has the highest GDP per capita among Indian states, roughly two and a half times the national average.

Culture

Goa's culture blends Indian and Portuguese influences, visible in its architecture — including the Basilica of Bom Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site — its cuisine, which features dishes such as vindaloo and bebinca, and its festivals, including the Goa Carnival. East Indian and Goan Catholic communities form a significant part of the population alongside Hindus and Muslims. The state's official language is Konkani, written in the Devanagari script.
