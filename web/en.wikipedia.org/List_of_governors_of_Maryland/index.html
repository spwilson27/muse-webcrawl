List of governors of Maryland

The Governor of Maryland is the head of the executive branch of the Maryland state government. Maryland was one of the original Thirteen Colonies; its first state governor was Thomas Johnson (1777–1779). Governors serve four-year terms, limited to two consecutive terms.

Notable and recent governors

  • Thomas Johnson (1777–1779), first governor
  • John Eager Howard, Levin Winder, and other early governors
  • Spiro T. Agnew (1967–1969) — resigned to become U.S. Vice President, later resigned that office
  • Marvin Mandel (1969–1979)
  • Harry R. Hughes (1979–1987)
  • William Donald Schaefer (1987–1995)
  • Parris N. Glendening (1995–2003)
  • Robert L. Ehrlich Jr. (2003–2007) — Republican
  • Martin O'Malley (2007–2015)
  • Larry Hogan (2015–2023) — Republican, popular two-term governor in a Democratic state
  • Wes Moore (2023–present) — Maryland's first African American governor

The complete list runs from the colonial proprietary and royal governors through the 60-plus state governors, noting party, terms and significant events.
