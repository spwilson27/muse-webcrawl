Unitary operator

Unitary operator
Field: Functional analysis
Definition: Surjective linear isometry on a Hilbert space
Condition: U*U = UU* = I

In functional analysis, a unitary operator is a surjective bounded linear operator on a Hilbert space that preserves the inner product: U : H → H satisfying U*U = UU* = I, where U* is the adjoint and I the identity. Unitary operators are the Hilbert-space analogues of rotations and reflections: they preserve lengths, angles, and distances.

Properties and applications

Every unitary operator has spectrum lying on the unit circle in the complex plane, and the spectral theorem describes unitary operators as multiplication operators after a suitable change of representation. In quantum mechanics, the time evolution of a closed system is given by a unitary operator (by Stone's theorem, generated by the Hamiltonian), and quantum logic gates are unitary matrices. The Fourier transform on L² is a standard example of a unitary operator.
