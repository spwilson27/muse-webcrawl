List of CAD programmes

[Image: A 3D model displayed in a CAD application]
Computer-aided design software is used across engineering and architecture.

Computer-aided design (CAD) programmes are used to create, modify and analyse designs in engineering, architecture, electronics and product development. The following is a list of notable CAD software, grouped by licensing model.
