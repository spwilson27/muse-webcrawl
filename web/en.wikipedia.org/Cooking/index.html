Cooking

Cooking, also known as cookery or professionally as the culinary arts, is the art, science and craft of using heat to make food more palatable, digestible, nutritious, or safe. Cooking techniques and ingredients vary widely, from grilling food over an open fire, to using electric stoves, to baking in various types of ovens.

Methods

Major methods include baking, boiling, frying, grilling, steaming, roasting, and simmering. Heat changes food chemically: proteins denature, starches gelatinise, and the Maillard reaction between amino acids and sugars creates hundreds of flavour compounds in browned foods.

History

The controlled use of fire for cooking is a defining human behaviour, with evidence extending back hundreds of thousands of years. Cooked food supported the evolution of larger brains by increasing energy availability. Over time, cooking developed into elaborate regional cuisines shaped by local ingredients, trade, religion, and technology.
