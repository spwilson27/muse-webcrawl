Kelowna

Kelowna is a city in the Okanagan Valley of British Columbia, Canada, on the eastern shore of Okanagan Lake. It is the largest city in the Central Okanagan, with a population of about 145,000 in the city and over 220,000 in its metropolitan area (2021).

The name derives from an Okanagan language word for grizzly bear. Settled by missionaries in 1859 and incorporated in 1905, Kelowna grew on fruit orchards — apples, peaches, cherries — and is now the center of British Columbia's wine industry, technology sector and tourism, with beaches, ski resorts at Big White, and the William R. Bennett Bridge across the lake to West Kelowna.
