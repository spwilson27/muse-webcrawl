Cetshwayo

Cetshwayo kaMpande (c. 1826 – 8 February 1884) was the king of the Zulu Kingdom from 1872 to 1879 and led the Zulu nation during the Anglo-Zulu War of 1879.

Reign and the Anglo-Zulu War

Cetshwayo secured the succession after the Battle of Ndondakusuka in 1856 and was crowned in 1873. He rebuilt Zulu military strength while seeking to avoid war with the British. In December 1878 the British high commissioner Sir Bartle Frere issued an ultimatum designed to be rejected; British forces invaded in January 1879. Zulu forces won a striking victory at Isandlwana but were defeated at Ulundi in July 1879.

Captivity and later life

Cetshwayo was captured, exiled to Cape Town and later London, where he met Queen Victoria, and partially restored to a reduced kingdom in 1883 amid civil war. He died the following year, possibly by poisoning.
