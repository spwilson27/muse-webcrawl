Alliance française

The Alliance française is an international organisation that aims to promote the French language and francophone culture around the world. Created in Paris on 21 July 1883 by a group including the scientists Louis Pasteur and Ferdinand de Lesseps, the diplomat Paul Cambon, and the publisher Armand Colin, it is a network of local, independent associations operating French-language schools and cultural centres.

The original Alliance Française de Paris was founded under the name "Association nationale pour la propagation de la langue française dans les colonies et à l'étranger" (National association for the propagation of the French language in the colonies and abroad). Its founders reflected the prestige of the French Third Republic and the belief in the civilising mission of the French language.

Network and activities

Today the network comprises more than 800 Alliances in over 130 countries, teaching French to hundreds of thousands of students and organising cultural events such as film screenings, exhibitions, concerts, and the annual Fête de la Musique and Francophonie celebrations. Most Alliances are locally incorporated non-profit associations, run by local boards and supported by the Fondation des Alliances Françaises, created in 2007 to coordinate the network.

Alliances offer French courses for all levels, prepare candidates for official French diplomas such as the DELF and DALF, and maintain French-language libraries and media collections.
