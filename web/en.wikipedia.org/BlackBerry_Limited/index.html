BlackBerry Limited is a Canadian software company specialising in cybersecurity and the Internet of things. Founded in 1984 as Research In Motion (RIM), it was formerly known for the BlackBerry line of smartphones, which dominated the business market in the 2000s.

History

RIM launched the BlackBerry 850 pager in 1999 and its smartphones later became known for physical QWERTY keyboards and secure email. Market share collapsed after the rise of the iPhone and Android devices, and the company exited handset manufacturing in 2016.

Current business

BlackBerry now focuses on endpoint security software and its QNX embedded operating system, widely used in automotive systems.
