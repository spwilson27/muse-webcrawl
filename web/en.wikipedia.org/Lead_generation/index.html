Lead generation

Lead generation is a marketing process of identifying and cultivating potential customers — leads — for a business's products or services. A lead is a person or organisation that has shown interest, for example by filling in a form, downloading content, subscribing to a newsletter, or requesting a demonstration, and has provided contact information for follow-up.

Common lead generation methods include content marketing, search engine optimisation and advertising, social media, email campaigns, webinars, trade shows and referrals. Leads are often scored by their likely readiness to buy and classified as marketing-qualified or sales-qualified before being passed to sales teams, typically managed through customer relationship management (CRM) software.

Businesses may generate leads themselves (in-house or inbound marketing) or buy them from lead generation companies, a practice regulated in many jurisdictions by privacy and anti-spam laws.
