Kassym-Jomart Kemelevich Tokayev (born 17 May 1953) is a Kazakh politician and diplomat who has served as President of Kazakhstan since 2019, succeeding Nursultan Nazarbayev.

Early career

The son of the writer Kemel Tokayev, he studied at MGIMO in Moscow and served as a Soviet and then Kazakh diplomat, including postings to Singapore and Beijing, where he became fluent in Mandarin. After independence he was Kazakhstan's foreign minister for much of the 1990s and 2000s, prime minister from 1999 to 2002, and from 2011 to 2013 Director-General of the United Nations Office at Geneva — the first Kazakh to hold a UN Under-Secretary-General post. He then chaired the Senate of Kazakhstan.

Presidency

When Nazarbayev resigned in March 2019, Tokayev succeeded him as acting president and won the subsequent election in June 2019. During the violent unrest of January 2022 he controversially requested assistance from the Russia-led CSTO and ordered security forces to "shoot to kill" against armed rioters. Afterwards he curtailed the privileges of the Nazarbayev family, pursued constitutional reforms including a single seven-year presidential term, and won re-election in November 2022.
