Corporate spin-off

A corporate spin-off is a type of corporate action where a company "spins off" a section of its business as a separate publicly traded company, distributing shares in the new company to its existing shareholders on a pro-rata basis.

Reasons

Companies spin off divisions to unlock shareholder value, let each business pursue a focused strategy, separate fast-growing units from mature ones, or meet regulatory requirements. The parent and the spun-off company thereafter operate independently.

Tax treatment

In the United States, spin-offs meeting the requirements of Section 355 of the Internal Revenue Code can be carried out tax-free to both the parent and its shareholders.
