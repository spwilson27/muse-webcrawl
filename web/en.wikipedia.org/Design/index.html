A design is the concept of or proposal for an object, process, or system. The word design refers to something that is or has been intentionally created by a thinking agent, and is sometimes used to refer to the inherent nature of something. The verb to design expresses the process of developing a design.

In some cases, the direct construction of an object without an explicit prior plan may also be considered a design, such as in arts and crafts. Design is typically guided by goals and constraints, and it involves balancing functional, economic, and aesthetic considerations.

Design disciplines

Applied design fields include architecture, urban design, landscape architecture, interior design, industrial (product) design, graphic design, communication design, interaction design, user experience design, fashion design, service design, and systems design. Each applies design methods to a particular class of problems.

The design process

Design processes commonly involve research into user needs and context, ideation and sketching, prototyping, testing, and refinement. Influential frameworks include the "double diamond" model of divergence and convergence, and design thinking, which emphasises empathy with users and rapid iteration.

Philosophy of design

Scholars such as Herbert A. Simon characterised design as the transformation of existing conditions into preferred ones. Debates in design theory concern the relative priority of form and function — "form follows function" — and the social and environmental responsibilities of designers.
