Portrait

A portrait depicts a specific person, usually emphasising the face and expression.

A portrait is a painting, photograph, sculpture, or other artistic representation of a person, in which the face and its expressions are predominant. The intent is to display the likeness, personality, and even the mood of the person. For this reason, in photography a portrait is generally not a snapshot, but a composed image of a person in a still position. Portraiture is one of the oldest forms of representational art, extending back at least to ancient Egypt, where it flourished from about 5,000 years ago.

Historically, portraits have primarily memorialised the rich and powerful. Over time, however, it became more common for middle-class patrons to commission portraits of their families and colleagues. Today, portraits are still commissioned by governments, corporations, groups, clubs, and individuals.

History

Portraiture in the ancient world was largely reserved for rulers and deities. Egyptian funerary portraits, such as the Fayum mummy portraits of Roman Egypt, combined realistic painting with funerary practice. Roman portraiture emphasised verism, recording the wrinkles and features of ageing statesmen as signs of virtue and experience.

During the Renaissance, portraiture flourished in Italy and Northern Europe. Artists such as Leonardo da Vinci, whose Mona Lisa is perhaps the most famous portrait in the world, and Jan van Eyck elevated the portrait to a psychological study. The invention of photography in the 19th century made portraiture accessible to the general public and transformed portrait painting, pushing it toward expressive and abstract approaches.

Techniques and formats

Portraits may be executed in oil, watercolour, pastel, charcoal, or other media, and in sculpture in marble or bronze. Common formats include the head-and-shoulders bust view, the half-length, the three-quarter-length, and the full-length portrait. A self-portrait is a depiction of the artist by the artist; a group portrait depicts several sitters together.
