Odesa Oblast

Odesa Oblast (Ukrainian: Одеська область) is an oblast (province) of southwestern Ukraine, located along the northern coast of the Black Sea. Its administrative centre is the city of Odesa, the third most populous city in Ukraine and a major seaport. The oblast is the largest in Ukraine by area, covering about 33,300 square kilometres.

Geography

The oblast borders Moldova and Romania to the west, including a stretch of the Danube, and the Ukrainian oblasts of Vinnytsia, Kirovohrad, and Mykolaiv to the north and east. The southwestern part of the oblast, known as Budjak, is separated from the rest by the Dniester estuary.

Economy

The region's economy centres on its Black Sea ports — Odesa, Chornomorsk, and Pivdennyi — together with agriculture in the steppe interior, wine production, and Danube shipping through Izmail and Reni. The ports' role in grain exports made the oblast strategically prominent during the Russian invasion of Ukraine beginning in 2022.
