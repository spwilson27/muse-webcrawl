The Pokémon Company (Japanese: 株式会社ポケモン) is a Japanese company responsible for brand management, production, publishing, and licensing of the Pokémon franchise. It was founded in 1998 as a joint venture between Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures Inc., the three companies that hold the copyright in Pokémon.

Operations

The company oversees the Pokémon franchise's licensing, marketing, retail stores (Pokémon Centers), and much of its merchandising, anime, and trading card game business outside the direct development of the main video games. Subsidiaries include The Pokémon Company International, which manages the brand in the Americas and Europe, based in Bellevue, Washington, and London.
