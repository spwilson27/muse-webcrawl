Dawn of the Dead (1978 film)

Directed byGeorge A. Romero
Written byGeorge A. Romero
StarringDavid Emge, Ken Foree, Scott Reiniger, Gaylen Ross
Release dateSeptember 2, 1978 (Italy); April 1979 (US)
Budget$640,000
Box office$66 million

Dawn of the Dead is a 1978 independent zombie horror film directed by George A. Romero. The second installment in his Dead series after Night of the Living Dead (1968), it depicts a small group of survivors sheltering in a shopping mall as civilization falls to the undead.

Cast

David Emge plays Stephen "Flyboy" Andrews, Ken Foree plays SWAT officer Peter Washington, Scott Reiniger plays Roger DeMarco, and Gaylen Ross plays Francine Parker. Makeup effects artist Tom Savini appears as the biker "Blades."

Reception

Released unrated in the United States to avoid an X rating, the film was a major commercial success for an independent production and received strong reviews for its social satire and special effects. It holds a high approval rating among critics and frequently appears on lists of the greatest horror films.

Versions

Several cuts exist: Romero's theatrical cut (about 127 minutes), Dario Argento's shorter European cut known as Zombi, and longer festival and director's cuts that have circulated in home media editions.
