Chrism

Chrism, also called myrrh or holy anointing oil, is a consecrated mixture of olive oil and balsam used in the sacraments and rites of many Christian churches.

Use

Chrism is used in baptism, confirmation or chrismation, ordination, and the consecration of churches and altars. In the Roman Catholic Church it is blessed by the bishop at the Chrism Mass, traditionally on Holy Thursday; in Eastern Orthodoxy, myrrh is prepared by the primates of autocephalous churches.

Symbolism

Anointing with chrism signifies the gift of the Holy Spirit and consecration to God, echoing the anointing of kings and priests in the Old Testament.
