Luch Design Bureau

The State Kyiv Design Bureau "Luch" (Ukrainian: Луч, "ray/beam") is a Ukrainian defence design bureau based in Kyiv. Founded in 1965 as a developer of automated control and diagnostic systems for torpedo armament, it became a major designer of precision-guided weapons.

Products

Luch developed the Skif and Stugna-P anti-tank guided missile systems, the RK-360MC Neptune anti-ship cruise missile system (which gained prominence in 2022), the Vilkha multiple launch rocket system, and guided artillery projectiles such as the Kvitnyk.
