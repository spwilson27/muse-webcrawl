Popocatepetl (Spanish: Popocatépetl) is an active stratovolcano in the states of México, Puebla, and Morelos in central Mexico. It is the second-highest peak in the country and one of its most active volcanoes.

Geology and activity

The volcano forms part of the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt. After a long quiet period, its current eruptive phase began in December 1994, producing persistent degassing, ash columns, and dome growth within the summit crater. Popocatépetl is monitored continuously because of the large populations of Puebla and the Mexico City metropolitan area within reach of its ash plumes.
