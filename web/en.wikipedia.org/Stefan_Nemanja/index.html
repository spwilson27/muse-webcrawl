Stefan Nemanja

Reign: Grand Prince of Serbia, 1166–1196

Born: c. 1113, Ribnica

Died: 13 February 1199, Hilandar, Mount Athos

Canonised as: Saint Simeon the Myrrh-streaming

Stefan Nemanja (c. 1113–1199) was Grand Prince of Serbia and founder of the Nemanjić dynasty, which ruled Serbia for two centuries.

Reign

Nemanja united the Serbian lands of Raška, Zeta and neighbouring regions, alternating between submission to and war with the Byzantine Empire under Manuel I Komnenos. He met Frederick Barbarossa at Niš in 1189 during the Third Crusade and campaigned against Bogomils in his realm.

Abdication and legacy

In 1196 Nemanja abdicated in favour of his son Stefan the First-Crowned, became the monk Simeon, and with his son Sava refounded the Hilandar Monastery on Mount Athos. His endowment Studenica became the dynasty's spiritual centre; he is venerated as a saint of the Serbian Orthodox Church.
