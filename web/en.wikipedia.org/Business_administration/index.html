Business administration

Business administration
Field of study and practice
Key areas: Finance, marketing, operations, human resources, strategy
Degrees: BBA, MBA

Business administration is the administration of a commercial enterprise. It includes all aspects of overseeing and supervising the business operations of an organisation, as well as the academic discipline that studies how businesses are managed.

Functions

Henri Fayol identified core managerial functions — forecasting and planning, organising, commanding, coordinating and controlling — and the field is conventionally divided into functional areas including accounting, finance, marketing, operations management, human resource management, information systems and strategic management.

Education

Business administration is taught at undergraduate level (BBA) and postgraduate level, most famously in the Master of Business Administration (MBA), first offered by Harvard Business School in 1908. Accrediting bodies such as AACSB and EQUIS assess business schools internationally.

History

The discipline emerged in the late 19th and early 20th centuries with the professionalisation of management, Frederick Winslow Taylor's scientific management, and the rise of large managerial corporations described by Alfred Chandler.
