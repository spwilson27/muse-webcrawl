The 4 x 400 metres relay (written in standard form as the 4 × 400 metres relay) is an athletics track event in which teams of four runners each run 400 metres, passing a baton between legs. The designation "4 x 400" uses the letter x in place of the multiplication sign.

Format

The race covers 1,600 metres in total. The first runner starts in lanes from a staggered start; after the break line early in the second leg, runners may move to the inside of the track. Exchanges occur within a marked 20-metre zone. The event concludes most major championships and is contested by men's, women's, and mixed teams.

Records

The men's world record of 2:54.29 was set by the United States at the 1993 World Championships in Stuttgart. The women's world record of 3:15.17 was set by the Soviet Union at the 1988 Seoul Olympics.
