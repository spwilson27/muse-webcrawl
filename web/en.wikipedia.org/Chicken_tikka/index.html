Chicken tikka

Chicken tikka is a dish originating in the Punjab region of the Indian subcontinent, consisting of small pieces of boneless chicken marinated in yogurt and spices and baked in a clay tandoor oven, traditionally on skewers. The word tikka derives from the Turkic and Persian for "pieces" or "bits."

Preparation

The chicken is marinated — typically for several hours — in a mixture of yogurt, Kashmiri chilli or paprika (giving the characteristic red-orange colour), ginger, garlic, garam masala, cumin, and coriander, then grilled until lightly charred at the edges. It is commonly served with sliced onion, lemon wedges, and mint chutney, garnished with coriander leaves.

Related dishes

Chicken tikka is an ingredient in chicken tikka masala, the curried dish popularised in Britain, and resembles other tandoor-roasted meats such as tandoori chicken (which uses whole pieces on the bone) and the Persian joojeh kabab. A vegetarian analogue, paneer tikka, uses cubes of paneer cheese. Sizzling platters of chicken tikka are also served as sizzlers in many Indian restaurants.
