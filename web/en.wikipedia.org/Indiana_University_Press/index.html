Indiana University Press, also known as IU Press, is an academic publisher founded in 1950 at Indiana University in Bloomington, Indiana. It publishes scholarly books and journals, with particular strengths in African studies, Jewish studies, philosophy, film and media studies, music, Middle East studies, and the social sciences, and is recognised for its translations of scholarly works.

IU Press publishes around 140 books and dozens of journals annually.

Publishing programme

The Press publishes roughly 140 new books each year and a large portfolio of journals, including the Journal of Folklore Research, Film History, Africa Today, and Transactions of the Charles S. Peirce Society. Its list includes widely cited works in film theory, ethnomusicology, and Holocaust studies. In 2020 the Press marked its 70th anniversary as one of the largest American university presses.
