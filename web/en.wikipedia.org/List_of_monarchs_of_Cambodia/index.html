List of monarchs of Cambodia

The monarchy of Cambodia is one of the oldest in Southeast Asia, tracing its legendary origins to the union of the Indian Brahman Kaundinya I with the Naga princess Soma in the 1st century AD, founders of the kingdom of Funan. The king of Cambodia (Khmer: ព្រះមហាក្សត្រនៃព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា) is the head of state of the Kingdom of Cambodia, reigning — but not ruling — under the 1993 constitution, which declares that "the King shall reign but shall not govern."

Post-Angkor and middle periods

After the sack of Angkor by Ayutthaya in 1431, the court moved successively to Srei Santhor, Phnom Penh, Longvek and Oudong. King Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founded Longvek; the city was destroyed by Siamese invasion in 1594. The following centuries saw the monarchy weakened by Vietnamese and Siamese rivalry, with periods of dual vassalage.

The monarchy was abolished between 1970 and 1993 — encompassing the Khmer Republic, Democratic Kampuchea under the Khmer Rouge, and the People's Republic of Kampuchea — before being restored by the 1993 constitution following the Paris Peace Agreements and UNTAC-supervised elections.
