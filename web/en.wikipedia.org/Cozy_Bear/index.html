Cozy Bear is a Russian advanced persistent threat (APT) group, classified by the cybersecurity firm CrowdStrike and known in the industry by designations including APT29, Nobelium, and (by Microsoft) Midnight Blizzard. Western governments attribute the group to Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR). Microsoft also uses the name "Nobelium" for post-2020 activity, and an earlier label was "the Dukes".

Activity

The group conducts long-term cyber espionage against governments, diplomatic missions, research institutes, and defence and technology organisations. It was identified, alongside the separate group Fancy Bear (APT28), in the 2015–16 breach of the US Democratic National Committee. Cozy Bear is generally characterised as quieter and more patient than Fancy Bear.

SolarWinds and later operations

In 2020 the group was attributed responsibility for the SolarWinds supply-chain compromise (SUNBURST), in which a trojanised software update gave access to numerous US government agencies and companies; the United States formally attributed the operation to the SVR in April 2021 and expelled Russian diplomats in response. Subsequent reporting linked the group to attacks on cloud services, diplomatic targets in Europe, and a 2024 breach of Microsoft corporate email accounts.
