Kitwe

Kitwe is the third largest city in Zambia, a major centre of the Copperbelt Province with a population of several hundred thousand. It grew up around copper mining in the 20th century.

History

Kitwe was founded in 1936 as an adjunct to the nearby Nkana copper mine. It developed into a planned mining town and commercial hub for the Copperbelt, connected by rail and road to Lusaka and the Democratic Republic of Congo border.

Economy and education

Copper mining and processing remain central, alongside engineering and services. The city is home to the Copperbelt University, Zambia's second-largest university, founded in 1987, and Mindolo Ecumenical Foundation.
