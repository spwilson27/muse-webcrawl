Fiat 500

The Fiat 500, 1957 original and modern revival

The Fiat 500 (Italian: Cinquecento) is a small car produced by Fiat. The name was first used for the 1936–1955 500 "Topolino", and became famous for the rear-engined city car designed by Dante Giacosa and launched in 1957, which became an icon of post-war Italian motoring. Nearly four million of the 1957–1975 model were built.

The 1957 Nuova 500

Launched in July 1957, the Nuova 500 was one of the smallest cars in production, with a 479 cc two-cylinder air-cooled engine mounted at the rear. Cheap, economical and easy to park, it put Italy on wheels. Derivatives included the Giardiniera estate and the tuned Abarth versions.

The modern 500

In 2007, on the 50th anniversary of the original, Fiat launched a retro-styled front-engined 500 that became a best-selling city car in Europe and was also sold in North America. An all-electric version, the 500e, followed in 2020.
