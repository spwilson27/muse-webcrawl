Names of large numbers

This article presents names of large numbers and their definitions. Two naming scales are in use: the short scale, used in the United States and most English-speaking countries, in which each new term above a million is 1,000 times the previous one; and the long scale, historically used in much of continental Europe, in which each new term is 1,000,000 times the previous one.

Other notable large numbers

A googol is 10100, and a googolplex is 10googol, a number so large it cannot be written out in full. Systems for naming still larger numbers include the Conway–Guy system and notations such as Knuth's up-arrow notation used for numbers like Graham's number. Traditional Indian numbering uses lakh (105) and crore (107).
