Canada Line

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Canada Line is a rapid transit line in Metro Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, forming part of the SkyTrain system. Opened on August 17, 2009, ahead of the 2010 Winter Olympics, the 19.2-kilometre line connects downtown Vancouver with Richmond and Vancouver International Airport (YVR), serving 16 stations on a main line and two branches south of Bridgeport Station.

Route and construction

From Waterfront Station in downtown Vancouver, the line runs in a cut-and-cover and bored tunnel beneath Cambie Street, then elevated across the Fraser River to Richmond–Brighouse, with a second branch serving the airport on Sea Island. The project, delivered as a public–private partnership at a cost of about CA$2.05 billion, was controversial for using cut-and-cover tunnelling along Cambie Street that disrupted local businesses and for its single-track sections and short station platforms.

Operations

Trains are fully automated and driverless but use conventional steel-wheel technology incompatible with the linear-induction trains of the Expo and Millennium lines. The airport branch carries an additional fare (the YVR AddFare) for passengers leaving the airport. Ridership quickly exceeded forecasts, passing 100,000 boardings per weekday within a few years, and the line is credited with enabling dense transit-oriented development along Cambie Street in Vancouver and in Richmond's city centre.
