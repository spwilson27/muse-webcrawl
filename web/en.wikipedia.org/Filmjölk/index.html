Filmjölk

Filmjölk is a traditional Swedish fermented milk product, a mesophilic yogurt-like drink made from cow's milk fermented with Lactococcus and Leuconostoc bacteria at room temperature.

Mild and slightly tart with a viscous texture, it is commonly eaten at breakfast with cereal, muesli or berries. It has been consumed in Scandinavia for centuries and is related to Finnish piimä and Icelandic skyr traditions of cultured milk.
