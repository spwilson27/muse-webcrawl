350.org is an international environmental organization addressing the climate crisis. Its stated goal is to end the use of fossil fuels and transition to renewable energy by building a global, grassroots climate movement. The name derives from 350 parts per million, the concentration of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere that climate scientist James Hansen identified as a safe upper limit for avoiding dangerous climate change.

History

The organization was founded in 2008 by American author and activist Bill McKibben together with a group of university students and the Tibetan writer and organizer Tenszig Gyatso. It grew out of the 2007 Step It Up campaign and organized the International Day of Climate Action on 24 October 2009, which featured more than 5,000 events in over 180 countries.

Campaigns

350.org is best known for the fossil fuel divestment movement, launched in 2012, which urged universities, pension funds and other institutions to withdraw investments from fossil fuel companies, and for its campaigns against the Keystone XL pipeline. It has helped coordinate global climate mobilizations, including the People's Climate March of 2014 and subsequent global climate strikes in coordination with youth-led movements.
