Shōjo manga

Shōjo manga is a category of Japanese comics (manga) marketed toward an audience of adolescent girls and young adult women. It is, along with shōnen manga, seinen manga, and josei manga, one of the main editorial categories of manga.

Shōjo manga traditionally emphasizes character relationships, romance, and emotional development, though modern works span every genre including action, horror, science fiction, and sports. Notable creators include the Year 24 Group, CLAMP, and Naoko Takeuchi, author of Sailor Moon.

Magazines such as Ribon, Nakayoshi, and Margaret have long published shōjo series in Japan, and many titles have been adapted into anime with international success.
