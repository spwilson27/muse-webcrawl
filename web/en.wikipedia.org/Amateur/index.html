Amateur

An amateur (from French amateur, "lover of") is a person who engages in an activity for pleasure rather than for financial benefit or professional reasons. The term contrasts with "professional" and carries a double connotation: devotion and love of a pursuit on the one hand, and lack of formal training or skill on the other.

Amateurism in sport

The ideal of amateurism dominated 19th- and early 20th-century elite sport, especially in Britain, where it also functioned as a class barrier excluding working-class competitors. The Olympic Games enforced amateur status for decades before professional athletes were progressively admitted from the 1980s onward. Amateur standing remains central in fields such as golf and boxing, and amateur participation drives pursuits from amateur radio to amateur astronomy, where non-professionals have made genuine discoveries.
