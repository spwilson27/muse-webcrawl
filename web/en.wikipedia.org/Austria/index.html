Austria

Capital: Vienna

Official language: Austrian German

Population (2023): approx. 9.1 million

Area: 83,879 km²

Currency: Euro (€)

Government: Federal parliamentary republic

Austria, formally the Republic of Austria, is a landlocked country in Central Europe lying in the Eastern Alps. It is bordered by Germany, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Slovenia, Italy, Switzerland and Liechtenstein. Its capital and largest city is Vienna. The country occupies an area of about 83,879 square kilometres and has a population of just over nine million.

History

The margraviate of Austria emerged in 976 under the Babenbergs and became a duchy in 1156. From 1438 the Habsburg dynasty, based in Vienna, supplied nearly all Holy Roman Emperors, and Austria became the core of a vast multinational empire. After the Austro-Hungarian Compromise of 1867 it formed the Dual Monarchy of Austria-Hungary, dissolved in 1918 after the First World War. The First Republic was annexed by Nazi Germany in the Anschluss of 1938. The Second Republic, proclaimed in 1945, regained full sovereignty with the State Treaty of 1955 and adopted permanent neutrality.

Geography, economy and culture

Much of Austria is mountainous, with the Danube plain and Vienna basin in the east. It is a highly developed country with a strong industrial and tourism sector — Alpine skiing and historic cities draw millions of visitors — and has been a member of the European Union since 1995. Austria has an outsized musical heritage as the home of Haydn, Mozart, Beethoven, Schubert, Brahms and the Strauss family.
