Adobe Inc.

Adobe Inc. is an American multinational computer software company headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1982 by John Warnock and Charles Geschke, it first became known for PostScript and desktop publishing.

Products

Adobe's products include Photoshop, Acrobat and the PDF format, Illustrator, Premiere Pro and the Creative Cloud subscription suite, alongside Experience Cloud marketing software.

History

Key acquisitions include Aldus (1994), Macromedia (2005) and Magento and Marketo (2018). A proposed acquisition of Figma announced in 2022 was abandoned in 2023 after regulatory opposition.
