List of British flags

This is a list of flags used in the United Kingdom, its constituent countries, its overseas territories and Crown dependencies, and other entities associated with the country.

National flags

The Union Flag (Union Jack), adopted in 1801, combines the crosses of Saint George (England), Saint Andrew (Scotland) and Saint Patrick (Ireland). England flies the red cross of St George; Scotland the white saltire of St Andrew on blue; Wales the red dragon on green and white; Northern Ireland has no official flag of its own.

Ensigns

The Red Ensign is the civil ensign, the Blue Ensign is used by government vessels, and the White Ensign is flown by the Royal Navy. Defaced ensigns serve as the basis for many flags of British colonies, former colonies and overseas territories, including those of Australia, New Zealand and Fiji.

Royal and military flags

The Royal Standard represents the monarch, and each service and regiment maintains its own colours and standards.
