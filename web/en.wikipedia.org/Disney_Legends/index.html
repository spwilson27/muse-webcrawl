Disney Legends

Disney Legends is a Hall of Fame award that is awarded by the Walt Disney Company to individuals who "[have] made a significant contribution to the Disney legacy". The program was established in 1987, and recipients are honoured at a ceremony traditionally held during the D23 Expo; each honouree receives a Disney Legends Award sculpture depicting the arm of Mickey Mouse holding a wand with a star and sparkles.

History

The first recipient was actor Fred MacMurray, inducted in 1987 for his film work for the studio. Honourees are chosen by a selection committee and span categories including animation, Imagineering, music, film, television, and parks and resorts. Inductees have included the "Nine Old Men" of animation, Ub Iwerks, Mary Blair, Julie Andrews, Roy E. Disney, Stan Lee, and Angela Lansbury. In 1998 the ceremony moved to the Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, where a Legends Plaza displays bronze plaques with each recipient's handprints and signature.

Selection

Anyone may submit nominations, which are reviewed by a committee of executives, historians, and past recipients. Posthumous awards are common, recognizing figures from the company's early decades.
