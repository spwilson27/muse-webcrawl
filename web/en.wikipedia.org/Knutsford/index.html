Knutsford

Town in Cheshire East, Cheshire, England
Population: about 13,000
Postcode: WA16

Knutsford is a market town in the borough of Cheshire East in Cheshire, England. It lies about 14 miles south-west of Manchester and 9 miles north-west of Macclesfield, on the Cheshire Plain near Tatton Park.

History

Knutsford is recorded in the Domesday Book. Legend associates its name with King Canute (Cnut) fording a stream here in 1016. The town was granted a market charter in 1292. It is famous for its Royal May Day festival, held since 1864, and for its association with the novelist Elizabeth Gaskell, who based her novel Cranford on the town and is buried in the Unitarian chapel graveyard.

Landmarks

Nearby Tatton Park, a historic estate with a neo-classical mansion and deer park, hosts the RHS Flower Show Tatton Park. Other features include the Knutsford Heritage Centre and distinctive Italianate buildings commissioned in the 19th century.
