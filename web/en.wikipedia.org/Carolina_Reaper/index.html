Carolina Reaper

Carolina Reaper
Species: Capsicum chinense
Breeder: Ed Currie
Origin: South Carolina, U.S.
Heat: ~1.64 million SHU average; peaks above 2.2 million SHU

The Carolina Reaper is a cultivar of the Capsicum chinense pepper developed by American breeder Ed Currie of the PuckerButt Pepper Company in South Carolina. Guinness World Records recognised it in 2013 as the world's hottest chilli pepper, a title it held until it was surpassed by Pepper X in 2023.

Development

Currie bred the pepper, originally designated "HP22B", by crossing a Pakistani Naga pepper with a red habanero-type pepper from Saint Vincent. The fruit is red and gnarled, with a small pointed tail resembling the stinger of a scorpion.

Heat

Testing by Winthrop University gave an average heat of about 1,569,300 Scoville Heat Units, with individual peppers exceeding 2.2 million SHU — comparable to standard pepper spray. (The figure is often quoted as roughly 1.64 million SHU on average.) Eating challenges involving the pepper have led to documented medical case reports, including thunderclap headaches associated with reversible cerebral vasoconstriction.

Uses

The pepper is used in extremely hot sauces and in promotional eating contests; Currie has said proceeds and publicity support cancer research charities.
