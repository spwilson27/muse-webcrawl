Air Canada is the flag carrier and largest airline of Canada by fleet size and passengers carried. Founded in 1937 as Trans-Canada Air Lines, it provides scheduled service to destinations across Canada and worldwide and is a founding member of the Star Alliance.

History

Created as a Crown corporation, the airline was renamed Air Canada in 1965 and privatized in 1988–89. In 2000 it took over its main domestic rival, Canadian Airlines. The company restructured under bankruptcy protection in 2003–04 and later expanded its low-cost subsidiary Air Canada Rouge and its regional brand Air Canada Express.

Operations

The airline's main hubs are Toronto Pearson International Airport, Vancouver International Airport, and Montréal–Trudeau International Airport. Its fleet centers on Airbus and Boeing wide-body aircraft for long-haul routes, and its Aeroplan loyalty program is among the largest in Canada.
