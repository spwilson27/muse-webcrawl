Lenticular galaxy

A lenticular galaxy (denoted S0) is a type of galaxy intermediate between an elliptical and a spiral galaxy in the Hubble sequence classification. Lenticulars have a central bulge and a disc, like spirals, but have used up or lost most of their interstellar matter and show little ongoing star formation or spiral structure.

Properties

Examples include the Spindle Galaxy (NGC 5866) and the Cartwheel Galaxy's companions in classification discussions. Lenticulars are thought in many cases to be spirals that have lost their gas through stripping or consumption, especially in dense galaxy clusters.
