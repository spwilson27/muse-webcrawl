Canandaigua is a city in Ontario County, New York, United States. It lies at the northern end of Canandaigua Lake, one of the Finger Lakes, and serves as the county seat. The name derives from a Seneca word meaning "the chosen spot."

History

Canandaigua was a Seneca village (Ganandarque) destroyed in the Sullivan Expedition of 1779. The Phelps and Gorham Purchase opened the area to settlement, and the Treaty of Canandaigua (1794) between the United States and the Six Nations was signed here.

Geography

The city is a gateway to the Finger Lakes wine country. Sonnenberg Gardens, a Victorian-era estate, is a notable attraction.
