Asha Bhosle (born September 8, 1933) is an Indian playback singer, one of the most successful and versatile singers in the history of Indian cinema. She has recorded songs in over twenty languages and is credited with more than 12,000 songs; in 2011 she was acknowledged by Guinness World Records as the most recorded artist in music history.

Career

Daughter of the classical singer and actor Deenanath Mangeshkar, and younger sister of Lata Mangeshkar, Bhosle began singing in films in the 1940s. She rose to prominence in the 1950s with composers such as O. P. Nayyar, recording cabaret and romantic numbers, and later formed a celebrated partnership with R. D. Burman. Her range extended from ghazals in Umrao Jaan (1981) to classical, folk, pop and Western styles, and she collaborated with international artists including Boy George and Michael Stipe.

Honours

Bhosle received seven Filmfare Best Female Playback Awards (from 18 nominations), the Dadasaheb Phalke Award in 2000, and the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian honour, in 2008.
