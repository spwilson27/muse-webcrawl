Icosahedrite

Composition Al63Cu24Fe13
Type Natural quasicrystal
Locality Khatyrka, Russia

Icosahedrite is a naturally occurring quasicrystalline mineral with the composition Al63Cu24Fe13. It was the first natural quasicrystal to be identified and described, recognised by the International Mineralogical Association in 2010.

Discovery

The mineral was found in grains from the Khatyrka River region of the Koryak Mountains in Chukotka, Russia. Subsequent investigation linked the host rock to the Khatyrka meteorite, indicating that the quasicrystal formed under the extreme conditions of an impact in space billions of years ago.

Properties

Like synthetic quasicrystals, icosahedrite displays icosahedral symmetry with fivefold axes — an arrangement forbidden in ordinary periodic crystals — while remaining a solid with a well-defined chemical composition.
