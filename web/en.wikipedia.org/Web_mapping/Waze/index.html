Waze

Original author: Waze Mobile
Developer: Google
Initial release: 2008 (as FreeMap Israel, 2006)
Type: GPS navigation software

Waze provides turn-by-turn navigation informed by crowdsourced reports from its users.

Waze (pronounced "ways") is a navigation app developed by the Israeli company Waze Mobile, founded in 2008. The app provides turn-by-turn navigation, travel times, and route details while incorporating user-submitted traffic updates such as accidents, road closures, and police presence.

The app was originally developed in Israel under the name FreeMap Israel. Google acquired Waze Mobile in June 2013 for a reported sum of around US$1 billion. Unlike traditional navigation systems, Waze builds and updates its maps from the GPS traces of its own users and a community of volunteer map editors.

Features

Waze calculates routes in real time from the speeds of drivers currently on the road, rerouting users around congestion. Users ("Wazers") report hazards, cheap fuel prices, and road hazards, earning points and ranks. The app integrates with music streaming services and supports voice commands. Through the Waze for Cities program, municipalities receive anonymized traffic data and can push closure information to drivers.
