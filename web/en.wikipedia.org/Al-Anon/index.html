Al-Anon/Alateen

Al-Anon Family Groups is a worldwide fellowship that offers a programme of recovery for the families and friends of alcoholics, whether or not the alcoholic recognises the existence of a drinking problem or seeks help. Alateen, part of Al-Anon, serves younger members, usually teenagers.

History

Al-Anon was founded in 1951 by Lois Wilson, wife of Alcoholics Anonymous co-founder Bill Wilson, together with Anne B. It adapts AA's Twelve Steps to the needs of those affected by someone else's drinking.

Meetings

Al-Anon is non-professional, self-supporting and not allied with any sect or organisation. Meetings follow the principle of anonymity, and members share experience, strength and hope rather than giving advice.
