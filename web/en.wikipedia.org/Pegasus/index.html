Pegasus


Pegasus, the winged horse of Greek mythology.

Pegasus is a winged divine horse in Greek mythology, usually depicted as pure white. He was born from the blood of the Gorgon Medusa when she was beheaded by the hero Perseus, springing forth together with the warrior Chrysaor. Pegasus was tamed by the hero Bellerophon with the aid of a golden bridle given by Athena, and together they defeated the Chimera.

Myths

When Bellerophon later tried to ride Pegasus to Mount Olympus, Zeus sent a gadfly that stung the horse, throwing the rider back to earth. Pegasus continued to Olympus, where he carried Zeus's thunderbolts and was eventually placed among the stars as the constellation Pegasus. His hoof was said to have struck Mount Helicon, creating the spring Hippocrene, sacred to the Muses; hence Pegasus's long association with poetry and inspiration.

In later culture

The winged horse has been a favourite motif in art, heraldry, literature, and corporate logos, and gave its name to numerous ships, aircraft projects, and the notorious spyware developed by NSO Group.
