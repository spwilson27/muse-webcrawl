San Juan County is a county in the northwestern corner of New Mexico, part of the Four Corners region where New Mexico, Arizona, Utah and Colorado meet. Its population was 121,661 at the 2020 census. The county seat is Aztec and the largest city is Farmington.

Geography

The county is drained by the San Juan, Animas and La Plata rivers. A large portion lies within the Navajo Nation, and the Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute reservations also extend nearby. Shiprock, a towering volcanic formation sacred to the Navajo, rises in the county's northwest.

Economy and sites

Oil and natural gas production from the San Juan Basin has long anchored the economy, alongside agriculture along the rivers. Tourist attractions include Aztec Ruins National Monument, Salmon Ruins, and access to Chaco Culture National Historical Park to the south.
