The Star-Spangled Banner

[Placeholder image: The_Star-Spangled_Banner.png]

'The Star-Spangled Banner' is the national anthem of the United States. The lyrics come from 'Defence of Fort M'Henry', a poem written by Francis Scott Key in September 1814 after witnessing the British bombardment of Fort McHenry in Baltimore Harbor during the War of 1812; Key was inspired by the large American flag flying over the fort after the U.S. victory.

Adoption

The poem was set to the tune of the English song 'To Anacreon in Heaven' and was made the national anthem by an act of Congress in 1931. Only the first of Key's four stanzas is commonly sung.
