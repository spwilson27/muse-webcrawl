12 (twelve) is the natural number following 11 and preceding 13. It is the product of the first three factorials and a composite number, divisible by 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6. Twelve is a highly composite number and the smallest abundant number.

In mathematics

Twelve is the third composite number and a superfactorial. It is the base of the duodecimal (dozenal) numeral system, which some societies have used for counting. A dodecahedron has 12 faces, and the icosahedron has 12 vertices. Twelve is a Harshad number in base ten.

Cultural significance

The number twelve is prominent in many cultures: there are twelve months in a year, twelve signs of the zodiac, twelve hours on a clock face, twelve days of Christmas, twelve apostles of Jesus, and twelve Imams in Twelver Shi'ism. Goods are traditionally sold in dozens (12) and grosses (144 = 12²).
