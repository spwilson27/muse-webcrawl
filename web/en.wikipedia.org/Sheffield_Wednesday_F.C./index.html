Sheffield Wednesday F.C.

Sheffield Wednesday Football Club is a professional football club based in Sheffield, South Yorkshire, England. Nicknamed the Owls, the club was formed in 1867 as an offshoot of the Wednesday Cricket Club — named for the day of the week its members played — making it one of the oldest football clubs in the world.

History

Wednesday turned professional in 1887, joined the Football League in 1892, and has won four League titles (1902–03, 1903–04, 1928–29, 1929–30), three FA Cups (1896, 1907, 1935), and the League Cup in 1991, when they defeated Manchester United while a Second Division side. The club has played at Hillsborough Stadium since 1899; the ground was the site of the 1989 Hillsborough disaster, in which 97 Liverpool supporters were unlawfully killed at an FA Cup semi-final.

Rivalries

The club's fiercest rivalry is with Sheffield United in the Steel City derby, one of the oldest derbies in football.
