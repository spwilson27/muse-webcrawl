System of systems

A system of systems (SoS) is a collection of independently useful systems that are integrated into a larger system delivering capabilities beyond those of the constituent systems alone. The concept is widely used in defence, transportation, energy, and information technology.

Characteristics

Maier's criteria describe constituent systems as operationally and managerially independent, geographically distributed, and exhibiting emergent behaviour, with the whole evolving over time rather than being built in a single step.

Examples

Examples include national air traffic management, smart electrical grids, the Internet, and integrated military command-and-control networks.

Engineering challenges

Systems-of-systems engineering must cope with no single authority over the parts, evolving requirements, interoperability standards, and emergent behaviours that cannot be predicted from the components alone.
