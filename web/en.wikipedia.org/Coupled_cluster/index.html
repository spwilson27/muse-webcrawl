Coupled cluster

Coupled cluster (CC) is a numerical technique used to describe many-body systems, principally in quantum chemistry for calculating electron correlation in molecules. It uses an exponential ansatz for the wave function, ensuring size extensivity.

Theory

The coupled cluster wave function is written as |Ψ⟩ = eT|Φ0⟩, where T is the cluster operator generating single, double and higher excitations. Truncations give methods such as CCSD (singles and doubles) and CCSD(T), often called the gold standard of quantum chemistry for single-reference systems.

History

The method originated in nuclear physics with work by Fritz Coester and Hermann Kümmel in the 1950s, and was adapted to chemistry by Jiří Čížek in 1966.
