Jonathan Richard Guy Greenwood (born 5 November 1971) is an English musician and composer, the lead guitarist and keyboardist of the rock band Radiohead and a member of the Smile. He is also an acclaimed composer of orchestral works and film scores.

Radiohead

Greenwood formed On a Friday with schoolmates at Abingdon School, the youngest member of the band that became Radiohead. His aggressive guitar work — showcased on "Creep" and the albums OK Computer and Kid A — and his command of instruments such as the ondes Martenot and modular synthesisers helped define the band's sound. He is frequently ranked among the greatest guitarists of all time.

Composition and film scores

Greenwood was composer-in-residence with the BBC Concert Orchestra and has written works including smear and Popcorn Superhet Receiver. His film scores, beginning with Bodysong (2003), include a celebrated collaboration with director Paul Thomas Anderson: There Will Be Blood (2007), The Master (2012), Inherent Vice (2014) and Phantom Thread (2017), the last earning him an Academy Award nomination. He also scored Spencer (2021) and The Power of the Dog (2021), receiving a second Oscar nomination.

Other work

Greenwood has collaborated extensively with the Israeli musician Dudu Tassa, releasing the album Jarak Qaribak (2023), and performs with Thom Yorke and Tom Skinner as the Smile.
