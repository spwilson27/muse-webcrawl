Gary James Kemp (born 16 October 1959) is an English popular musician, songwriter, and actor, best known as the lead guitarist, backing vocalist, and principal songwriter of the New Romantic band Spandau Ballet. He wrote all of the group's major hits, including the international successes "True", "Gold", and "Through the Barricades". His younger brother Martin Kemp is the band's bassist.

Spandau Ballet

Kemp formed the band that became Spandau Ballet in Islington, London, in the late 1970s, emerging from the Blitz club scene that launched the New Romantic movement. The band achieved a string of top-ten albums and singles in the 1980s, including the album True (1983), whose title track topped charts worldwide. Spandau Ballet split in 1990 amid disputes over royalties — Kemp was sued unsuccessfully by three bandmates in 1999 over songwriting income — before reuniting in 2009 for tours and the album Once More.

Acting and other work

Alongside his brother, Kemp starred as Ronnie Kray in the 1990 film The Krays, and appeared in films including The Bodyguard (1992). His memoir I Know This Much: From Soho to Spandau was published in 2009. Since 2018 he has played guitar in Nick Mason's Saucerful of Secrets, performing early Pink Floyd material, and he co-hosts the music podcast Rockonteurs with Guy Pratt.
