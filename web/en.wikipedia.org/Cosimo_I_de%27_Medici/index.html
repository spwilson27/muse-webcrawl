Cosimo I de' Medici

Cosimo I de' Medici (12 June 1519 – 21 April 1574) was the second Duke of Florence from 1537 and the first Grand Duke of Tuscany from 1569, founding the grand-ducal line that ruled Tuscany until 1737.

Reign

Becoming duke at 17 after the assassination of Alessandro de' Medici, Cosimo consolidated Medici rule, defeated the republican exiles at Montemurlo, conquered Siena (1555–1559), founded the Order of Saint Stephen and strengthened Tuscan administration and fortifications.

Patronage

Cosimo was a major patron of the arts, employing Giorgio Vasari (who built the Uffizi), Bronzino, Cellini and Bartolomeo Ammannati, and revived the University of Pisa. He married Eleanor of Toledo; their son Francesco I succeeded him.
