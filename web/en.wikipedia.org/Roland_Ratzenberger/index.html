Roland Ratzenberger (4 July 1960 – 30 April 1994) was an Austrian racing driver who competed in Formula One for the Simtek team in 1994. He was killed in a crash during qualifying for the San Marino Grand Prix at Imola on 30 April 1994, when a front wing failure caused his car to strike a concrete wall at the Villeneuve curve at high speed.

Career and aftermath

Ratzenberger raced in Formula Ford, Formula 3, touring cars, and sports cars — including five starts at the Le Mans 24 Hours — and in Japanese Formula 3000 before finally reaching Formula One at age 33. His death came one day before three-time champion Ayrton Senna was killed at the same event, the first fatal Formula One weekend accidents in twelve years. The tragedies prompted sweeping safety reforms, and drivers re-formed the Grand Prix Drivers' Association in response.
