University of the Fraser Valley

The University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) is a Canadian public university in the Fraser Valley of British Columbia, with campuses in Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Mission and Hope. Founded in 1974, it gained university status in 2008.

History

UFV began as Fraser Valley College, created after community campaigning for post-secondary access in the region.

Academics

It offers bachelor's degrees, applied programs, trades training and continuing education, with strengths in agriculture, criminology and aviation.

Community

UFV emphasises small classes and regional engagement, including partnerships with Indigenous communities.
