PlatinumGames

PlatinumGames Inc. is a Japanese video game development studio based in Osaka, founded in 2007 by former Capcom and Clover Studio staff including Shinji Mikami, Hideki Kamiya and Atsushi Inaba.

History

The studio emerged from the merger of Seeds Inc. and ODD Incorporated in 2007. Its early titles, developed under a publishing deal with Sega, included MadWorld, Bayonetta, Infinite Space and Vanquish, earning a reputation for stylish, technically demanding action games.

Selected works

Later releases include Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance (2013) for Konami, The Wonderful 101 (2013), Bayonetta 2 (2014), Nier: Automata (2017) for Square Enix — the studio's biggest commercial success — Astral Chain (2019), and Bayonetta 3 (2022). The studio has also pursued self-publishing and received investment from Tencent in 2020.
