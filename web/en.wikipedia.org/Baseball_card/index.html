Baseball card

A baseball card is a type of trading card relating to baseball, usually printed on card stock, and typically featuring one or more baseball players or other baseball-related sports figures, with statistics on the reverse.

History

Cards began in the 19th century as trade cards and inserts in tobacco products. The T206 Honus Wagner card became the most famous and valuable of the tobacco era.

Manufacturers

Companies such as Topps, Bowman, Upper Deck and Panini have dominated production at various times.

Collecting

Baseball cards became a major collectible and investment market, with booms in the late 20th century and again in the 2020s.
