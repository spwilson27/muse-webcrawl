The Novgorod Kremlin, traditionally called the Detinets, is the historic fortified centre of Veliky Novgorod in north-west Russia, standing on the left bank of the Volkhov River. The first chronicle mention of fortifications dates to 1044; the present stone walls and towers were largely built in the late 15th century after Novgorod's annexation by Moscow.

Saint Sophia Cathedral

Within the walls stands the Cathedral of St Sophia (1045–1050), one of the oldest churches in Russia, built by Prince Vladimir Yaroslavich. Its five domes, frescoes and the famous Magdeburg bronze gates made it the spiritual heart of the Novgorod Republic.

Other monuments

The Kremlin contains the Chamber of Facets (1433), the bell tower of St Sophia, and the Millennium of Russia monument (1862), an enormous bronze celebrating a thousand years since the traditional founding of the Russian state. The complex forms part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing for the Historic Monuments of Novgorod and Surroundings.
