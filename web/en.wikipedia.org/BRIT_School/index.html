BRIT School

The BRIT School for Performing Arts and Technology is a British free school in Selhurst, Croydon, in south London. Opened in 1991, it provides education in the performing arts, media, art and design alongside a full academic curriculum for students aged 14 to 19.

History

The school was inspired by Fame and established with support from the British Phonographic Industry (BPI) and the BRIT Trust, with funding linked to the Brit Awards. It was the first school in the UK dedicated to the performing arts under the City Technology College initiative.

Curriculum

Students specialise in strands such as music, dance, theatre, musical theatre, visual arts and design, film and media production, and interactive digital design, while also studying for GCSEs and A levels or vocational qualifications.

Notable alumni

Alumni include singers Adele and Amy Winehouse, actor Tom Holland, singer-songwriter Jessie J, and members of bands including Black Midi. See also Croydon.
