Victor Gruen

Victor David Gruen, born Viktor David Grünbaum (July 18, 1903 – February 14, 1980), was an Austrian-born American architect best known as the pioneer in designing shopping malls in the United States. He is often described as the inventor of the modern shopping mall.

Life and career

Born in Vienna, Gruen studied architecture at the Vienna Academy of Fine Arts. He fled Austria after the Anschluss in 1938 and emigrated to the United States. In 1956 he designed Southdale Center in Edina, Minnesota, the first fully enclosed, climate-controlled shopping mall in the US.

Gruen intended malls to be community centres modelled on European town squares, with housing, schools, medical facilities and parks. He became disillusioned as developers built purely commercial, car-dependent centres, and in a 1978 speech in London he disavowed paternity of the shopping mall.
