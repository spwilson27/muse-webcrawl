The World Athletics Indoor Championships are a biennial indoor track and field competition organised by World Athletics. They were first held in Paris in 1985 as the World Indoor Games and assumed championship status in Indianapolis in 1987.

Events

The programme includes sprints over 60 metres, middle- and long-distance races, 60 metres hurdles, relays, and field events (high jump, pole vault, long jump, triple jump, shot put), plus the men's heptathlon and women's pentathlon. Indoor races are run on a banked 200-metre track, with the 400 metres and above involving break lines and tighter turns than outdoors.

History

The championships have been staged in cities including Toronto, Budapest, Doha, Istanbul, Portland, Birmingham, Belgrade, and Glasgow. The event is usually held in March, serving as the climax of the indoor season.
