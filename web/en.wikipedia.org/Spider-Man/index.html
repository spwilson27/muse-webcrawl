Spider-Man

Spider-Man is a superhero in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko, he first appeared in the anthology comic book Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962) in the Silver Age of Comic Books. He has since been featured in films, television series, video games and plays.

Fictional biography

Peter Parker is a high-school student from Queens, New York, raised by his Aunt May and Uncle Ben. Bitten by a radioactive spider, he gains spider-like abilities. When he fails to stop a thief who later murders Uncle Ben, he learns the lesson summed up in the famous phrase, "with great power comes great responsibility." Parker struggles to balance heroics with school, work and family, a relatability that made the character a landmark of the genre.

Supporting cast and villains

The series features love interests Gwen Stacy and Mary Jane Watson, editor J. Jonah Jameson, and a famous rogues' gallery including the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, the Lizard and Mysterio.

In other media

Spider-Man has starred in numerous animated series and in blockbuster film franchises, played in live action by Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield and Tom Holland, and in the acclaimed animated Spider-Verse films centred on Miles Morales. He is consistently ranked among the greatest and most popular comic-book characters of all time.
