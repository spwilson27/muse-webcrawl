The Falcon (Samuel Thomas "Sam" Wilson) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Gene Colan, the character first appeared in Captain America #117 (September 1969). The Falcon was the first African-American superhero in mainstream comics, and became Captain America's recurring partner. He uses mechanical wings to fly, and has a telepathic and empathic connection with his pet falcon Redwing.

Publication history

Sam Wilson was introduced as a social worker from Harlem who befriended Captain America (Steve Rogers) and adopted the Falcon identity. For much of his early history a controversial retcon suggested his memories of an earlier life as a criminal named "Snap" Wilson had been implanted or altered by the Red Skull using the Cosmic Cube. Wilson became a longstanding member of the Avengers.

Captain America

In 2014, Marvel announced that Wilson would take up the mantle of Captain America after Steve Rogers was aged and lost his powers. Wilson starred as the lead of Captain America: Sam Wilson (2015) and the Falcon solo series (2017). He later returned to the Falcon identity while also, in more recent storylines, sharing the Captain America role.

Powers and abilities

Wilson is an excellent hand-to-hand combatant trained by Captain America. His flight harness, originally a jet-powered wing suit and later a Wakandan-designed suit of vibranium, grants him flight. He shares a mental link with birds, especially Redwing, allowing him to see through their eyes and command flocks.

In other media

Sam Wilson is portrayed by Anthony Mackie in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014). In the Disney+ series The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021), Wilson takes up the shield and becomes Captain America, a role he continues in Captain America: Brave New World (2025).
