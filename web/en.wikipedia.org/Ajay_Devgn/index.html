Ajay Devgn (born Vishal Veeru Devgan; 2 April 1969) is an Indian actor, film director and producer known for his work in Hindi cinema. One of Bollywood's most successful leading men, he has appeared in over a hundred films and won numerous awards, including two National Film Awards and four Filmfare Awards.

Career

The son of stunt choreographer Veeru Devgan, Devgn made his debut in Phool Aur Kaante (1991), famous for an entry balanced on two motorcycles, which won him the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut. He built his career on action films in the 1990s, then broadened his range with acclaimed dramatic roles: Zakhm (1998) and The Legend of Bhagat Singh (2002) earned him National Film Awards for Best Actor. He won praise for ensemble and character work in Company (2002), Omkara (2006) and the police franchise Singham (2011), and for comedy in the Golmaal series.

Production and later work

Devgn founded Ajay Devgn FFilms in 2000 and has directed features including Shivaay (2016) and Runway 34 (2022). His performance as Vijay Salgaonkar in the Drishyam films and as Tanaji Malusare in Tanhaji (2020) — which won him a third National Film Award, shared — were major commercial successes. He is married to actress Kajol, with whom he has two children. In 2016 he received the Padma Shri, India's fourth-highest civilian honour.
