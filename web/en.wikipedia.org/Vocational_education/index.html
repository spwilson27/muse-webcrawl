Vocational education

Vocational education is education that prepares people to work as technicians or in skilled trades such as carpentry, plumbing, electrical work, culinary arts or healthcare support. It is sometimes referred to as career and technical education (CTE) or, in the United Kingdom and Commonwealth, as technical and vocational education and training (TVET). It stands in contrast to more academic or theory-based education, though the two often overlap.

Provision

Vocational education is offered at secondary schools, technical colleges, community colleges and polytechnics, and through apprenticeships in which trainees combine paid work with study. Germany's "dual system", combining company-based apprenticeships with part-time vocational school, is a widely studied model. Qualifications include certificates, diplomas and, increasingly, applied degrees.

Issues

Debates around vocational education concern its status relative to academic routes, funding, alignment with labour-market needs, and the risk that early tracking limits students' later choices. Supporters argue it raises employment rates and fills skills gaps in trades and technical occupations.
