Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide, commonly known as AAA, is a Mexican professional wrestling promotion founded in 1992 by Antonio Peña, who broke away from the long-established CMLL. Its name originally stood for Asistencia, Asesoría y Administración.

History

AAA became known for its flamboyant characters, six-sided ring, and the introduction of many young high-flying luchadores. Its flagship annual event is Triplemanía, held since 1993. Wrestlers such as Rey Mysterio Jr., Psicosis and Eddie Guerrero passed through AAA before achieving fame in the United States.

Style

The promotion blends traditional lucha libre — masked wrestlers, rapid aerial manoeuvres, and técnicos versus rudos storylines — with elements of American sports entertainment.
