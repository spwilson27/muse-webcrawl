Sayeret Matkal

Sayeret Matkal (Hebrew: סיירת מטכ״ל, "General Staff Reconnaissance Unit") is the premier special forces unit of the Israel Defense Forces, under the Military Intelligence Directorate. Founded in 1957 by Avraham Arnan, it was modelled partly on the British SAS and specialises in deep reconnaissance, counter-terrorism, and hostage rescue.

Notable operations

The unit is best known for Operation Thunderbolt (1976), the rescue of hostages from a hijacked Air France plane at Entebbe Airport, Uganda, in which its commander Yonatan Netanyahu was killed. Other operations include the 1972 rescue of Sabena Flight 571, the 1973 Beirut raid (Operation Spring of Youth), and the 1994 failed rescue of Nachshon Wachsman. Selection and training are famously gruelling, lasting around 20 months.

Alumni

Veterans of the unit have played an outsized role in Israeli public life: prime ministers Ehud Barak and Benjamin Netanyahu both served in it, as did numerous generals and politicians.
