Male infertility

Male infertility refers to a sexually mature male's inability to cause pregnancy in a fertile female. Male factors contribute to roughly a third to a half of infertility cases among couples, alone or in combination with female factors.

Causes

Causes include low sperm production (oligospermia) or absence of sperm (azoospermia), abnormal sperm function or motility, blockages of the ducts that carry sperm, hormonal disorders, genetic conditions such as Klinefelter syndrome, varicocele, infections, and lifestyle or environmental factors such as heat exposure, smoking, and certain medications.

Diagnosis and treatment

Diagnosis usually begins with a medical history, physical examination, and semen analysis. Treatment depends on the cause and may include surgery, hormone therapy, treatment of infection, or assisted reproductive technologies such as intrauterine insemination and intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI).
