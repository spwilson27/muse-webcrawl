The Falcon and the Winter Soldier is an American television miniseries created by Malcolm Spellman for the streaming service Disney+, based on Marvel Comics and featuring the characters Sam Wilson / Falcon and Bucky Barnes / Winter Soldier. It is the second television series in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) produced by Marvel Studios, set after the events of Avengers: Endgame (2019).

Premise

Six months after being handed the mantle of Captain America at the end of Endgame, Sam Wilson (Anthony Mackie) — who initially donates Steve Rogers's shield to a museum — teams up with Bucky Barnes (Sebastian Stan) on a globe-trotting mission against the Flag Smashers, an anti-nationalist group of super-soldiers who believe the world was better during the Blip. The pair contend with a government-appointed new Captain America, John Walker; the machinations of Sharon Carter and the Power Broker; and Baron Helmut Zemo. Wilson ultimately takes up the shield and the Captain America identity, addressing questions of race and representation in the process.

Production

The series was announced in April 2019 and premiered on March 19, 2021. Filming in Atlanta and Prague was interrupted by the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020. It consists of six episodes and forms part of Phase Four of the MCU. A fourth Captain America film continuing Wilson's story, Captain America: Brave New World, followed in 2025.

Reception

The series received positive reviews for the chemistry of its leads, action, and engagement with racial themes, though some critics found its plotting uneven. It was nominated for five Primetime Emmy Awards and was among the most-watched premieres on Disney+ at the time.
