Brown hyena

The brown hyena (Parahyaena brunnea) is a species of hyena found in Namibia, Botswana, western and southern Zimbabwe, southern Mozambique and South Africa. It is the rarest species of hyena, with a population estimated below 10,000 mature individuals.

Brown hyenas have long, shaggy dark brown coats with distinctive cream-coloured neck ruffs and striped legs. They are primarily scavengers, feeding on carcasses left by larger predators, supplemented by wild melons, eggs, and small animals.

They live in small clans in arid regions such as the Kalahari and Namib deserts, often foraging alone over long distances at night. The species is listed as Near Threatened due to persecution and habitat pressures.
