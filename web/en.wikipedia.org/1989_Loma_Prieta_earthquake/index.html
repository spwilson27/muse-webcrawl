The 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake occurred in Northern California on 17 October 1989 at 5:04 p.m. local time. The shock, centred in The Forest of Nisene Marks State Park in Santa Cruz County, had a moment magnitude of 6.9 and caused severe damage across the San Francisco Bay Area and Monterey Bay regions, killing 63 people and injuring 3,757.

Damage

The deadliest failure was the collapse of the Cypress Street Viaduct on Interstate 880 in Oakland, which killed 42 people. A section of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge deck collapsed, San Francisco's Marina District suffered extensive liquefaction and fires, and downtown Santa Cruz was devastated. Property damage was estimated at about $6 billion.

"World Series earthquake"

The earthquake struck shortly before Game 3 of the 1989 World Series between the Oakland Athletics and the San Francisco Giants at Candlestick Park, and was captured live on national television, earning it the nickname the "World Series earthquake". The Series resumed ten days later.
