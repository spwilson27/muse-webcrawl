Heautoscopy

Heautoscopy is a term used in psychiatry and neurology for the reduplicative hallucination of "seeing one's own body at a distance". It can be a symptom in psychiatry and in neurology, and is closely related to the phenomena of autoscopy and out-of-body experiences.

Features

In a heautoscopic experience, the subject sees a double of themselves — a doppelgänger — in extrapersonal space while remaining aware of being in their own physical body, unlike the out-of-body experience in which self-location shifts to the disembodied viewpoint. The double may mirror the subject's movements and appearance, and the experience is often emotionally charged.

Heautoscopy has been associated with epilepsy, migraine, brain lesions (particularly in the temporoparietal and insular regions), schizophrenia and other psychiatric conditions. Neurological research links it to disturbed multisensory integration of bodily signals.
