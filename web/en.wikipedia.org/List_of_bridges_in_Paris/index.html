List of bridges in Paris

Paris has 37 bridges over the River Seine within the city limits, plus bridges over its canals. Many are noted for their architecture and history.

  • Pont Neuf — oldest standing bridge in Paris, completed 1607, crossing the western end of Île de la Cité
  • Pont Alexandre III — ornate Beaux-Arts bridge (1900) linking Invalides to the Champs-Élysées
  • Pont des Arts — pedestrian bridge known for former love locks
  • Pont de l'Alma — known for the Zouave statue and the nearby Diana memorial
  • Pont Marie, Pont de Sully, Pont de la Tournelle, Pont Saint-Louis, Pont au Change, Pont Saint-Michel
  • Pont d'Iéna — by the Eiffel Tower; Pont de Bir-Hakeim — two-level bridge carrying Métro line 6
  • Pont Charles de Gaulle, Pont de Bercy, Pont Simone-de-Beauvoir — modern eastern bridges
  • Passerelle Léopold-Sédar-Senghor, Passerelle Simone-de-Beauvoir — pedestrian bridges
  • Pont du Carrousel, Pont Royal, Pont de la Concorde, Pont Mirabeau, Pont de Grenelle, Pont Aval, Pont Amont

Bridges over the Canal Saint-Martin and Canal Saint-Denis include swing and lift bridges in the north-east of the city.
