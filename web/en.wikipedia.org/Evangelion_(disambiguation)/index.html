Evangelion is the Greek-derived word for "gospel" or "good news" (Greek: εὐαγγέλιον, euangelion). It may also refer to:

Religion

  • The Gospel in Christianity
  • The Gospel Book, a liturgical book
  • Evangelion, the Syriac title of the Diatessaron gospel harmony

Anime and manga

  • Neon Genesis Evangelion, a Japanese mecha anime franchise created by Gainax and Hideaki Anno, comprising:
    • Neon Genesis Evangelion (TV series), 1995–1996
    • Neon Genesis Evangelion (manga)
    • The End of Evangelion, a 1997 film
    • Rebuild of Evangelion, a film tetralogy (2007–2021)
    • Evangelion (mecha), the giant biomechanical units in the franchise

Music

  • Evangelion (album), a 2009 album by Polish extreme metal band Behemoth
