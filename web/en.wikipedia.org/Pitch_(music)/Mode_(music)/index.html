Mode (music)

In music theory, a mode is a type of musical scale defined by its pattern of intervals and characteristic melodic behaviours. In Western music the term most often refers to the seven diatonic modes — Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian and Locrian — each a rotation of the major scale starting on a different degree.

Uses

Medieval church music was organised into eight modes guiding Gregorian chant. Modes also underpin much folk music, jazz improvisation (where modes are mapped to chord qualities), and rock and modal jazz compositions such as Miles Davis's "So What" in Dorian mode.
