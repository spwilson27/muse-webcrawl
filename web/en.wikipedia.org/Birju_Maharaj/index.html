Birju Maharaj

Pandit Birju Maharaj (4 February 1938 – 17 January 2022) was an Indian dancer, composer, singer and exponent of the Lucknow "Kalka-Bindadin" gharana of Kathak dance. He was a descendant of the Maharaj family of Kathak dancers, which includes his two uncles, Shambhu Maharaj and Lachhu Maharaj, and his father and guru, Acchan Maharaj.

Career

Birju Maharaj began teaching dance as a teenager and went on to head the Kathak Kendra in Delhi. He was celebrated for his command of rhythm, expressive facial storytelling (abhinaya) and innovative choreography, including group compositions and dance-dramas. He choreographed for films including Satyajit Ray's Shatranj Ke Khilari (1977) and, later, Devdas (2002), Vishwaroopam and Bajirao Mastani (2015).

Recognition

He received the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award in 1964 and the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian award, in 1986. After retiring from Kathak Kendra he founded his own institute, Kalashram, in Delhi, and trained generations of dancers who carried the Lucknow gharana worldwide.
