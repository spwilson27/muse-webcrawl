Amblin Partners

Amblin Partners, LLC is an American entertainment production company. It develops and produces film, television and digital content under its labels Amblin Entertainment and DreamWorks Pictures.

Amblin Partners
Founded: December 16, 2015
Headquarters: Universal City, California, U.S.
Key people: Steven Spielberg (chairman)
Products: Film and television
Labels: Amblin Entertainment, DreamWorks Pictures

History

Amblin Partners was announced in 2015 as a new content-creation company formed by Steven Spielberg, Reliance Entertainment, Entertainment One (eOne), and Participant Media. The venture brought together Spielberg's Amblin Entertainment and DreamWorks Pictures brand under one structure, with film distribution arrangements through Universal Pictures announced in 2015.

Productions

The company's films, released under the Amblin Entertainment and DreamWorks Pictures banners, include titles such as The BFG (2016), The Post (2017), Ready Player One (2018), Green Book (2018), 1917 (2019), West Side Story (2021), and The Fabelmans (2022), along with television productions under Amblin Television.
