Norodom Sihamoni (Khmer: នរោត្តម សីហមុនី; born 14 May 1953) is King of Cambodia. He became King on 14 October 2004, a week after the abdication of his father, Norodom Sihanouk. He is the eldest son of King Sihanouk and former Queen consort Norodom Monineath and was Cambodia's ambassador to UNESCO before his selection by a nine-member throne council to become the next king. Before ascending to the throne, Sihamoni was educated in Czechoslovakia and became known for his work as a cultural ambassador in Europe and as an instructor of classical dance. As a constitutional monarch he reigns but does not rule, and is regarded as a unifying national figure above politics.

Early life and education

Sihamoni was born in Phnom Penh in 1953. He was sent to Prague, Czechoslovakia, in 1962, where he completed his primary and secondary schooling and studied classical dance and music, graduating from the Academy of Musical Arts in Prague. He also studied filmmaking in North Korea in 1975–76. His name combines the first syllables of his parents' names, Sihanouk and Monineath.

Life before accession

During the Khmer Rouge period Sihamoni was kept under house arrest in the Royal Palace in Phnom Penh with the rest of the royal family. After the fall of the regime he lived in exile in Paris, where he founded a classical dance school and the ballet troupe Deva, and taught as a professor of classical dance at the Paris conservatories. In 1993 he became Cambodia's ambassador to UNESCO, a post he held until his election as king.

Reign

Following Sihanouk's abdication on 7 October 2004 (announced by his father, citing old age and health), the Royal Council of the Throne unanimously elected Sihamoni king on 14 October 2004. He was crowned in Phnom Penh on 29 October 2004. Sihamoni is a bachelor and has no children, a fact notable because the Cambodian throne is elective rather than hereditary. Fluent in Khmer, French and Czech (with English and some Russian), he is known for a modest, apolitical style, focuses on charitable and cultural patronage, and has been praised for his role in national reconciliation and unity after the civil-war era.
