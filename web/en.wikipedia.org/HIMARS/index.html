HIMARS, the M142 High Mobility Artillery Rocket System, is a light multiple rocket launcher developed by Lockheed Martin for the United States Army. Mounted on a standard medium tactical truck chassis, it carries a single pod of six GMLRS guided rockets or one ATACMS tactical ballistic missile, combining the firepower of the tracked MLRS with far greater road mobility and air-transportability in a C-130.

Design and use

HIMARS uses "shoot-and-scoot" tactics: its crew of three can emplace, fire GPS-guided precision rockets with ranges beyond 90 km, and displace within minutes to avoid counter-battery fire. The system saw extensive service in Iraq and Afghanistan.

Ukraine

From mid-2022 the United States and allies supplied HIMARS to Ukraine during Russia's invasion. Its precision strikes on Russian ammunition depots, command posts and bridges — notably around Kherson and the Antonivka Bridge — were widely credited with reshaping the war's logistics and became a celebrated symbol of Ukrainian resistance, inspiring songs and memes. Exports to Poland, Romania, Australia, the Baltic states and others followed.
