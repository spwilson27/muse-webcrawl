Beat Happening

Beat Happening is an American indie rock band formed in Olympia, Washington, in 1982. Composed of Calvin Johnson, Heather Lewis, and Bret Lunsford, the band is known for its radically simple, lo-fi sound — sparse guitar, minimal drumming, and Johnson's deep baritone monotone — and for its foundational role in American indie rock and the international pop underground. Beat Happening released its records on Johnson's own K Records, helping to build an independent distribution network that influenced the riot grrrl movement and bands such as Nirvana and Bikini Kill.

History

The three members met at The Evergreen State College. Taking their name from a student's film project, they adopted a deliberately amateur aesthetic — members swapped instruments and Lewis drummed standing with a minimal kit. Their self-titled debut album appeared in 1985, followed by Jamboree (1988), Black Candy (1989), Dreamy (1991), and You Turn Me On (1992), the last regarded by many as their most accomplished record. The band toured Japan and Europe and, in 1988, toured with Fugazi. Though never commercially large, Beat Happening's do-it-yourself ethos was hugely influential: Kurt Cobain famously had the K Records shield logo tattooed on his arm.
