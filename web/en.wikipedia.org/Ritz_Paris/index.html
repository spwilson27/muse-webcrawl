The Ritz Paris is a luxury hotel in Paris, France, located at 15 Place Vendôme in the 1st arrondissement. Founded by Swiss hotelier César Ritz in 1898 in a converted 18th-century townhouse, it became synonymous with luxury hospitality and gave rise to the words "ritzy" and "putting on the Ritz".

History and guests

The hotel was a home or haunt of figures including Marcel Proust, F. Scott Fitzgerald, Coco Chanel, who lived there for decades, and Ernest Hemingway, after whom a bar is named. Diana, Princess of Wales dined at the Ritz on the night of her fatal car crash in 1997. Egyptian businessman Mohamed Al-Fayed bought the hotel in 1979. After a major renovation closure beginning in 2012, the Ritz Paris reopened in June 2016, shortly after a fire during the works.
