Strong's Concordance
Full title: The Exhaustive Concordance of the Bible
Editor: James Strong
Published: 1890

Strong's Exhaustive Concordance of the Bible is a concordance of the King James Version compiled under the direction of James Strong, a Methodist biblical scholar, and published in 1890. It lists every word in the KJV with every verse in which it appears.

Strong's numbers

Strong assigned a unique number to each underlying Hebrew, Aramaic or Greek word — about 8,674 Hebrew and 5,624 Greek entries — allowing readers without knowledge of the original languages to look up word meanings in the accompanying dictionaries. "Strong's numbers" remain a standard referencing system in Bible software and study tools.

Production

Strong, assisted by more than a hundred collaborators, spent 35 years on the work while a professor at Drew Theological Seminary.
