The Council of Ministers of Education, Canada (CMEC) is an intergovernmental body founded in 1967 by Canada's provincial and territorial ministers of education. Because education in Canada is a provincial and territorial responsibility, with no federal ministry of education, CMEC provides a forum for coordination and a collective voice.

Role

Through CMEC, ministers discuss matters of mutual interest, undertake cooperative activities in elementary, secondary, and postsecondary education, and represent Canadian education interests internationally. The organisation coordinates Canada's participation in international assessments such as PISA.

Activities

CMEC runs pan-Canadian programs and assessments, including the Pan-Canadian Assessment Program (PCAP), works on copyright and official-languages education, supports student mobility and credential recognition, and maintains the Canadian Information Centre for International Credentials.

Structure

Membership comprises the ministers responsible for education in all ten provinces and three territories. A permanent secretariat in Toronto supports the council's work, and the chair rotates among members.
