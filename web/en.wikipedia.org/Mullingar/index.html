Mullingar (Irish: An Muileann gCearr) is the county town of County Westmeath in the midlands of Ireland. It is the third most populous town in the midlands region and a centre for the surrounding agricultural area.

History and economy

Mullingar grew as a market and military town, historically known for cattle trading and its large army barracks, Columb Barracks (closed in 2012). The town is situated near Lough Ennell and Lough Owel, noted for trout fishing, and is linked to Dublin by the Royal Canal and the Dublin–Sligo railway.

Culture

The town is associated with singer Joe Dolan and with author James Joyce, who stayed there briefly; the band The Academic and singer-songwriter Niall Horan also have Mullingar roots.
