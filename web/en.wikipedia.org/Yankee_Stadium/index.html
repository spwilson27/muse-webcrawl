Yankee Stadium is a baseball stadium in the Bronx, New York City, home of the New York Yankees of Major League Baseball. It opened in 2009 across the street from the original Yankee Stadium of 1923, which it replaced.

Design

The new stadium deliberately echoes the original: a limestone and granite facade, the famous white frieze along the roofline, and a Great Hall entrance lined with banners of Yankee greats. Monument Park, honouring retired players, sits beyond the centre-field fence.

Events

The Yankees won the World Series in the stadium's inaugural season, 2009. The venue also hosts New York City FC soccer matches, college football's Pinstripe Bowl, concerts, and outdoor NHL hockey games.
