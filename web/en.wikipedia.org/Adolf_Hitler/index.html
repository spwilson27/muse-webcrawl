Adolf Hitler

Adolf Hitler (20 April 1889 – 30 April 1945) was an Austrian-born German politician who became the dictator of Nazi Germany from 1933 until his suicide in 1945. Rising to power as leader of the Nazi Party, he became Chancellor in 1933 and then Führer in 1934. His invasion of Poland in September 1939 began the Second World War in Europe, and his regime carried out the Holocaust, the genocide of six million Jews and millions of other victims.

Early life

Hitler was born in Braunau am Inn, the son of customs official Alois Hitler. Rejected twice by the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna, he lived in Vienna and later Munich, where he volunteered for the Bavarian Army at the outbreak of the First World War. Decorated with the Iron Cross, he was wounded in 1916 and temporarily blinded by gas in 1918.

Rise to power

After the war Hitler joined the German Workers' Party, forerunner of the NSDAP, and became its leader in 1921. His failed Beer Hall Putsch of November 1923 led to imprisonment at Landsberg, where he dictated Mein Kampf. Amid the Great Depression, the Nazis became the largest party in the Reichstag, and President Paul von Hindenburg appointed Hitler Chancellor on 30 January 1933. Following the Reichstag fire and Hindenburg's death, Hitler merged the offices of Chancellor and President and ruled as dictator.

Nazi Germany

The regime suppressed all opposition, rearmed Germany in defiance of the Treaty of Versailles, annexed Austria in the Anschluss of 1938, and occupied Czechoslovakia. Racial policy escalated from the Nuremberg Laws of 1935 to the Kristallnacht pogrom of 1938 and ultimately to systematic extermination in the Holocaust.

Second World War and death

Hitler's invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939 triggered declarations of war by Britain and France. After early victories, the tide turned with defeats at Stalingrad and El Alamein and the Allied landings in Normandy. With Soviet forces closing on Berlin, Hitler married Eva Braun and took his own life in the Führerbunker on 30 April 1945. Germany surrendered unconditionally days later.
