DNA Data Bank of Japan

The DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ) is a biological database that collects DNA sequences. It is located at the National Institute of Genetics (NIG) in Mishima, Shizuoka, Japan. DDBJ began operations in 1987 and is one of the three major international nucleotide sequence databases.

International collaboration

DDBJ is a founding member of the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC), together with the European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) at EMBL-EBI in Europe and GenBank at the NCBI in the United States. The three databases exchange data daily so that a sequence submitted to any one of them becomes available from all three.

Activities

DDBJ accepts sequence submissions from researchers, principally in Japan and other Asian countries, assigns accession numbers, and provides search and analysis services. It also operates the DDBJ Sequence Read Archive for high-throughput sequencing data and develops bioinformatics tools and pipelines, including services running on the NIG supercomputer.
