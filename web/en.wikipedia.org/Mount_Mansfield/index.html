Mount Mansfield

Mount Mansfield is the highest mountain in Vermont, with a summit at 4,393 feet (1,339 m) above sea level. Located in the Green Mountains in Chittenden and Lamoille counties, its ridgeline is said to resemble a human face in profile, with features named the Forehead, Nose, Lips, Chin (the summit) and Adam's Apple.

Recreation and ecology

The mountain hosts the Stowe Mountain Resort ski area and the Smugglers' Notch pass. Its summit ridge carries one of Vermont's largest areas of alpine tundra, a fragile relic of the last ice age, and is crossed by the Long Trail. The name "Mansfield" derives from the former town of Mansfield, Vermont, annexed by neighbouring towns in the 19th century.
