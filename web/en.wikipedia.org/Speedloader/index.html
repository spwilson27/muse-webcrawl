Speedloader

A speedloader is a device used to reduce the time and effort required to reload a firearm, most commonly a revolver. It holds a full set of cartridges in the same circular arrangement as the chambers of the revolver's cylinder, so that all rounds can be inserted and released simultaneously.

Design

Revolver speedloaders are typically made of plastic or aluminium and hold five, six, or more cartridges. The loader is aligned with the open cylinder, pushed in, and a knob or the loader body is twisted or pressed to release the cartridges. Popular designs include the HKS twist-release and the Safariland push-release models.

Related devices

Stripper clips and moon clips serve similar purposes; moon clips hold rimless cartridges in a revolver and are inserted with the ammunition. Speed strips are flat, flexible strips that load two chambers at a time but carry flatter than a full speedloader.
