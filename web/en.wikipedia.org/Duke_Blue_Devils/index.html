Duke Blue Devils

Cameron Indoor Stadium, home of Duke basketball (placeholder image)

The Duke Blue Devils are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Duke University in Durham, North Carolina. Duke competes in the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) in NCAA Division I. The university fields 27 varsity teams, and its athletic director's programme is best known for the men's basketball team.

Name and colours

The name "Blue Devils" was adopted in 1922, drawn from Les Diables Bleus, the nickname of a French Alpine light infantry unit famed in the First World War. Duke's colours are Duke blue — officially a dark shade of blue — and white.

Men's basketball

The Duke men's basketball programme is one of the most successful in college history, with five NCAA championships (1991, 1992, 2001, 2010, 2015). Long-serving coach Mike Krzyzewski ("Coach K"), who led the team from 1980 to 2022, became the winningest coach in NCAA Division I men's history. The team plays at Cameron Indoor Stadium, famous for the student fans known as the "Cameron Crazies", and maintains a celebrated rivalry with the North Carolina Tar Heels.

Other sports

Duke teams have won national championships in men's lacrosse, women's golf, women's tennis and men's soccer, among others, and numerous ACC titles across sports. The football team plays at Wallace Wade Stadium and competes in the ACC's top division.
