The Port of Antwerp-Bruges is a Belgian seaport formed in 2022 by the merger of the ports of Antwerp and Zeebrugge. It is Europe's second-largest port after Rotterdam and one of the world's leading container and chemical ports, handling around 290 million tonnes of cargo annually.

Antwerp

The Antwerp platform, some 80 kilometres inland on the River Scheldt, is Europe's largest integrated chemical cluster and a major container, breakbulk, and petrochemical hub. Its dock system includes the Deurganckdok and the Berendrecht Lock, among the largest in the world.

Zeebrugge

The coastal Zeebrugge platform specializes in roll-on/roll-off cargo, new vehicles, liquefied natural gas through the Fluxys terminal, and short-sea shipping. The merger unites the complementary strengths of the two platforms under a single port authority.
