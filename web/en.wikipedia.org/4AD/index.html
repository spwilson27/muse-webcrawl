4AD is a British record label owned by the Beggars Group. Founded in London in 1980 by Ivo Watts-Russell and Peter Kent, initially as an offshoot of Beggars Banquet Records, it became one of the most influential independent labels of the 1980s, known for an atmospheric, ethereal roster and for distinctive sleeve artwork by the design studio 23 Envelope and photographer Nigel Grierson.

Artists

The label's defining 1980s acts included Cocteau Twins, Bauhaus, Dead Can Dance, This Mortal Coil, Clan of Xymox and the Birthday Party. In the late 1980s and 1990s 4AD achieved major success with American alternative acts licensed or signed to the label, including Pixies, Throwing Muses, Belly, the Breeders and Red House Painters.

Later years

After Watts-Russell's departure in the late 1990s, the label was restructured within the Beggars Group. A revived 4AD has since released acclaimed records by artists including Bon Iver (outside North America), The National, St. Vincent, Grimes, Future Islands, Aldous Harding and Big Thief.
