Colorado fourteeners

In mountaineering in the United States, a fourteener is a mountain peak exceeding 14,000 feet (4,267 m) in elevation. Colorado has the most fourteeners of any state — 53 or more, depending on the prominence criteria used.

Peaks

The highest is Mount Elbert at 14,440 feet (4,401 m), also the highest peak in the Rocky Mountains. Others include Mount Massive, Mount Harvard, Blanca Peak, Longs Peak and Pikes Peak. Fourteeners are conventionally counted when a summit rises at least 300 feet above the saddle to a higher peak.

Climbing

Climbing all the Colorado fourteeners is a popular goal; the Colorado Mountain Club keeps records. Most standard routes are non-technical, though weather, altitude sickness and lightning are serious hazards.
