Geoff Keighley is a Canadian video game journalist and television presenter. He is best known as the creator, producer, and host of The Game Awards, the annual ceremony honouring achievements in the video game industry, and of the Summer Game Fest showcase event.

Career

Keighley began writing about games as a teenager and worked for GameSpot, where he created the Behind the Games / The Final Hours series of long-form articles documenting the development of major titles. On television he hosted GameTrailers TV on Spike TV, and from 2009 he produced and hosted the Spike Video Game Awards (VGX). After Spike ended the show, Keighley funded and launched The Game Awards as an independent production in 2014; it grew into one of the most-watched entertainment livestreams in the world and became a major venue for game announcements.

Other work

In 2020, following the cancellation of the E3 expo amid the COVID-19 pandemic, Keighley launched Summer Game Fest, a season-long series of digital events and reveals. He also became closely associated with developer Hideo Kojima, appearing as a hologram character in Death Stranding (2019). Keighley has served as a prominent public advocate for the games industry and its recognition as an art form.
