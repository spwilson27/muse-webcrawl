Pentatonix

Pentatonix (abbreviated PTX) is an American a cappella group from Arlington, Texas, known for pop-style arrangements with vocal harmonies, basslines, riffing, percussion and beatboxing. The group won the third season of NBC's The Sing-Off in 2011 and went on to become one of the most successful a cappella acts in history.

Career

The original quintet — Scott Hoying, Mitch Grassi, Kirstin Maldonado, Avi Kaplan and Kevin Olusola — formed to compete on The Sing-Off; Olusola was recruited after the others saw his "celloboxing" (simultaneous cello and beatboxing) video online. After winning, the group built a huge following on YouTube, where their medleys and covers, including a Daft Punk medley that won a Grammy Award for Best Arrangement, attracted billions of views.

Pentatonix has won three Grammy Awards, including for the country collaboration "Jolene" with Dolly Parton. Their 2015 self-titled album of original material debuted at number one on the Billboard 200, a first for an a cappella group, and their Christmas albums have been perennial bestsellers. Kaplan departed amicably in 2017 and was replaced by bass singer Matt Sallee. The group tours worldwide and appeared in the film Pitch Perfect 2.
