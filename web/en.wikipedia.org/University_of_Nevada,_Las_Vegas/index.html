University of Nevada, Las Vegas

The University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) is a public land-grant research university in Paradise, Nevada, adjacent to Las Vegas. Founded in 1957, it is known for its hospitality, law, dental medicine and fine arts programs, and for the Runnin' Rebels basketball team.

History

UNLV began as a branch of the University of Nevada, Reno, holding its first classes in 1957. It became autonomous in 1968 and grew rapidly with Las Vegas.

Academics

The university offers bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees across colleges including the William F. Harrah College of Hospitality, William S. Boyd School of Law, and School of Dental Medicine.

Athletics

The Rebels compete in the Mountain West Conference. The men's basketball team won the NCAA championship in 1990 under Jerry Tarkanian.
