Bushmaster

Bushmaster may refer to several things. Most prominently, the bushmasters are pit vipers of the genus Lachesis, among the largest venomous snakes in the Americas, found in Central and South America. The name is also used for military equipment, including the Bushmaster Protected Mobility Vehicle used by the Australian Army, the M242 Bushmaster chain gun, and Bushmaster Firearms, an American firearms manufacturer.

Bushmaster snakes

The genus Lachesis includes the Central American bushmaster (Lachesis stenophrys), the South American bushmaster (Lachesis muta), and the black-headed bushmaster (Lachesis melanocephala). They inhabit forests, are nocturnal and terrestrial, and feed on rodents. Unusually among New World pit vipers, they lay eggs rather than giving birth to live young. Their venom is potent but they are generally not aggressive, and bites are rare though dangerous.

Military uses

The Bushmaster Protected Mobility Vehicle is an Australian-built infantry mobility vehicle widely exported and used in Afghanistan and Ukraine. The M242 Bushmaster is a 25 mm chain-driven autocannon fitted to armoured fighting vehicles such as the M2 Bradley. Bushmaster Firearms International is known for producing AR-15-pattern rifles.
