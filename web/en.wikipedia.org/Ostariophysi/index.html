Ostariophysi

Ostariophysi is a superorder of ray-finned fishes and the second-largest group of fishes by number of species, comprising roughly 8,000 species — about 28% of all known fishes and over 60% of freshwater fishes. It includes the carps, catfishes, characins (including piranhas and tetras), knifefishes, and milkfish.

Characteristics

Most ostariophysans possess the Weberian apparatus, a chain of small bones (ossicles) connecting the swim bladder to the inner ear, which gives them exceptionally keen hearing. Many species also produce alarm substances (Schreckstoff) released when the skin is damaged, warning nearby fish of predators.

Orders

The group is divided into the Anotophysi (Gonorynchiformes) and the Otophysi, which contains Cypriniformes, Characiformes, Siluriformes, and Gymnotiformes.
