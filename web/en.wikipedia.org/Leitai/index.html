Leitai

A leitai (擂台) is a raised fighting platform used for Chinese martial arts challenge matches. Traditionally an open square stage, about waist height or higher, on which fighters met under agreed rules before a crowd.

History

Leitai matches are described from the Song dynasty onward, where a host fighter would defend the platform against challengers. Victory could bring money, fame or students; defeat could mean injury with little recourse, and stories of lethal leitai bouts fill martial arts folklore.

Modern use

The format survives in sanda (Chinese kickboxing), whose competition platform — a raised square with no ropes — is a direct descendant, and in popular culture depictions of kung fu duels.
