Traffic engineering (transportation)

Traffic engineering is a branch of civil engineering that uses engineering techniques to achieve the safe and efficient movement of people and goods on roadways. It focuses on research and construction of the infrastructure needed for safe and efficient traffic flow, such as road geometry, sidewalks and crosswalks, cycling infrastructure, traffic signs, road surface markings and traffic lights.

Practice

Traffic engineers analyse traffic volumes, speeds, crash data and capacity; design signal timings; and plan for pedestrians, cyclists and public transport. Key references include the Highway Capacity Manual and, in the United States, the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD), which standardises signs, signals and markings.

Traffic flow theory

The discipline draws on traffic flow theory, which models relationships between flow, density and speed, and on simulation software that predicts the effect of design changes before they are built. Modern practice increasingly includes intelligent transportation systems and real-time traffic management.
