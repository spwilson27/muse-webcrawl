Davao Region

Country: Philippines
Island group: Mindanao
Regional centre: Davao City
Designation: Region XI

The Davao Region, designated as Region XI, is an administrative region in the Philippines, located in the southeastern portion of Mindanao. It comprises five provinces: Davao de Oro, Davao del Norte, Davao del Sur, Davao Occidental and Davao Oriental, plus the highly urbanised Davao City, which serves as the region's political, commercial and educational centre.

Geography

The region borders the Philippine Sea to the east and the Davao Gulf, into which the Davao River flows. Mount Apo, the highest peak in the Philippines at 2,954 metres, stands on the boundary of Davao City and Davao del Sur. The region is largely outside the typhoon belt and has a relatively uniform climate suited to agriculture.

Economy

Davao is one of the Philippines' leading agricultural regions, a major producer and exporter of bananas, pineapples, coconuts and cacao, and is often associated with the durian fruit. Davao City is Mindanao's principal port and commercial hub, with growing business-process outsourcing, tourism and real-estate sectors.
