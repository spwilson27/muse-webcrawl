Hilary term

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Hilary term is the second academic term of the University of Oxford and the University of Dublin. It runs from January to March, corresponding roughly to the spring term at other institutions. The term is named after Saint Hilary of Poitiers, whose feast day falls on 13 January (14 January in some calendars), near the start of the term. The name derives from the legal year of England and Wales, in which the Hilary term was traditionally the first of the four terms of court sittings, a usage that carried over into the universities.

Oxford

At Oxford, the academic year is divided into three terms of eight weeks each: Michaelmas term (autumn), Hilary term (spring), and Trinity term (summer). Hilary term begins in mid-January and ends in mid-March; the dates are determined so that the term includes the Sundays between 10 January and 18 January at its start. Undergraduate examinations known as Honour Moderations are often held during Hilary term, and many final examinations ("Finals") begin in Trinity. The period is also associated with the Torpids rowing regatta, held in the fifth week of term, and with the darkest and coldest weeks of the Oxford year.

Dublin

At the University of Dublin (Trinity College Dublin), the teaching year is organised into Michaelmas, Hilary, and Trinity terms. Hilary term spans the second part of the academic year, roughly from January to spring, and is followed by the Trinity term in which annual examinations are held around May and June; term dates were restructured under semesterisation in the 2010s, but the historic term names were retained for ceremonial and statutory purposes.

Legal usage

The term's name originates in the legal calendar. Historically, the courts of England and Wales sat in four terms—Hilary, Easter, Trinity, and Michaelmas—named for saints' days and seasons. Although the sittings of the courts are now organised differently, the names survive in the legal year and in the naming of terms at the Inns of Court, where Hilary term is the first session of the year, running from January into April.
