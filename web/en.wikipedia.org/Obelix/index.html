Obelix

Obelix (French: Obélix) is a fictional character in the French comic book series Asterix. He is the best friend of the hero Asterix and works as a menhir sculptor and deliveryman in their small Gaulish village in Armorica, which resists Roman occupation thanks to a magic potion brewed by the druid Getafix.

First appearanceAsterix the Gaul (1959)
Created byRené Goscinny, Albert Uderzo
OccupationMenhir deliveryman

Character

As a child, Obelix fell into a cauldron of magic potion, giving him permanent superhuman strength; he is therefore denied further potion (except in rare circumstances) and is comically sensitive about his weight, insisting he is merely "well covered". He is fond of wild boar, fighting Romans, and his tiny dog Dogmatix. His catchphrase is "These Romans are crazy!"

Name

His name plays on obélisque (obelisk, like a small menhir) and the typographic obelus (÷), complementing the asterisk symbol behind Asterix's name.
