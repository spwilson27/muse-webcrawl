TitlePresident and CEO of WWE

Nicholas Khan is an American business executive who serves as president of WWE (World Wrestling Entertainment). Before joining WWE, Khan was a prominent talent agent at Creative Artists Agency (CAA), where he co-headed the television department and negotiated major media rights deals.

Khan joined WWE in August 2020 as president and chief revenue officer. Following the departure of Vince McMahon in 2022, Khan became co-CEO alongside Stephanie McMahon, and after her resignation in January 2023 he became sole CEO. When WWE merged with UFC to form TKO Group Holdings in September 2023, Khan remained president of WWE.

Career

Khan grew up in Las Vegas and earned his law degree from Whittier Law School. At CAA he represented sports media properties and negotiated WWE's television deals with Fox and NBCUniversal, as well as Southeastern Conference media agreements. As a WWE executive he has overseen major media rights agreements, including a deal bringing Raw to Netflix, and the company's expansion of premium live events internationally.
