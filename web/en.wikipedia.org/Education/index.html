Education

Education is the transmission of knowledge, skills, and character traits, and is manifested in many forms. Formal education occurs in institutional frameworks such as public schools, following a curriculum; non-formal education also follows a structured approach but usually outside the formal system; and informal education is the unstructured learning that accompanies everyday experience.

Levels

Formal education is commonly divided into stages: preschool and kindergarten for young children; primary (elementary) school; secondary school; and tertiary or higher education at universities and colleges, including vocational training and postgraduate study. Most countries make schooling compulsory for a period of years, and the right to education is recognised in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and as the fourth United Nations Sustainable Development Goal.

History

Education in prehistory consisted of oral transmission and imitation. Organised schooling emerged in ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, China, and India, while Greece and Rome developed influential models of liberal education. Medieval Europe centred learning in cathedral schools and new universities. Mass public schooling expanded in the 19th century — Prussia's system of compulsory education was widely emulated — and in the 20th century access extended to girls and marginalised groups on an unprecedented scale.

Purposes and effects

Thinkers from Plato and Confucius to John Dewey have debated education's aims: personal development, socialisation, economic productivity, and the cultivation of critical thought. Empirically, education is associated with higher earnings, better health, and greater civic participation, and is a central concern of public policy and the economics of education economics.

Approaches

Pedagogies range from direct instruction and rote memorisation to inquiry-based, Montessori, and Waldorf methods, and delivery increasingly includes distance and online learning. Debates continue over curriculum content, assessment, funding, and equity of access.
