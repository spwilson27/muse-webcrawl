The Ministry of Transportation (MTO) is the provincial ministry of the Government of Ontario responsible for transport infrastructure and related law in Canada's most populous province.

Responsibilities

The ministry plans, builds and maintains the 400-series highways and other provincial highways, administers driver licensing and vehicle registration (delivered in part through ServiceOntario), and sets policy for public transit, including oversight of GO Transit's parent agency Metrolinx. It also oversees road safety programs, the Ontario Provincial Police's highway enforcement partners, and ferry services such as those to Pelee Island.

History

The ministry originated as the Department of Public Highways of Ontario in 1916, during the expansion of motor vehicle travel. It later absorbed rail and transit functions, becoming the Ministry of Transportation and Communications before taking its present name in 1987.
