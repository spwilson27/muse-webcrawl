Bloc Québécois
Founded: 1991
Ideology: Quebec nationalism, social democracy
Region: Quebec, Canada

The Bloc Québécois is a federal political party in Canada devoted to Quebec nationalism and the promotion of Quebec sovereignty. It was founded in 1991 by Lucien Bouchard, who led it to become the Official Opposition in the 1993 federal election.

History

The Bloc formed after the failure of the Meech Lake Accord, drawing MPs from the Progressive Conservative and Liberal parties. It runs candidates only in Quebec's federal ridings.

Platform

The party advocates the interests of Quebec in the House of Commons, including language rights, environmental policy and a distinct role for Quebec in Canadian federalism.
