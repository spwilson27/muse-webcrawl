Michael Kovats

Born1724, Karcag, Hungary
Died11 May 1779, Charleston, South Carolina
Known forHussar officer; Continental Army colonel under Casimir Pulaski

Michael Kovats de Fabriczy (1724 – 11 May 1779) was a Hungarian nobleman and cavalry officer who served in the Continental Army during the American Revolutionary War. He is regarded, alongside Casimir Pulaski, as a founder of the United States cavalry.

Career

Kovats gained experience as a hussar officer in the Prussian army of Frederick the Great during the Seven Years' War. Arriving in America in 1777 with a letter to Benjamin Franklin, he joined Pulaski's Legion as colonel commandant, training the legion's cavalry in European hussar methods.

Death and legacy

Kovats was killed leading a charge against British forces at Charleston, South Carolina, on 11 May 1779. The Citadel military college in Charleston honours him as a hero of the Revolution, and he is commemorated by Hungarian-American communities as one of their earliest heroes in America.
