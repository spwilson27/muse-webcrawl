Demotivational poster

A demotivational poster is a poster, usually in a distinctive format of a photograph on a black background with a bold title and a smaller caption beneath it, intended as a parody of the motivational posters displayed in offices and schools. Where a motivational poster pairs an inspiring image — a soaring eagle, a climbing team — with an uplifting word such as "Teamwork" or "Success" and an encouraging slogan, the demotivational version subverts each element: the image shows failure or absurdity, and the caption delivers an ironic, cynical, or mocking twist.

History

The genre is most closely associated with Despair, Inc., an American company founded in 1998 that built its business on "Demotivators®" products parodying the corporate motivational industry. The format then spread widely on the internet in the 2000s, where generator websites allowed users to create their own versions with arbitrary images and captions. Demotivational posters became one of the early widely shared forms of internet meme, and although the genre's popularity peaked around the late 2000s, the format remains recognisable and is still used for humorous commentary.

Style and themes

Typical demotivational posters mock ambition, effort, competence, and optimism: a sinking ship may illustrate "Mistakes", a lone penguin in the desert "Individuality", and similar juxtapositions are applied to topics from procrastination to meetings. The humour depends on the deadpan retention of the motivational poster's dignified layout while the content undercuts it — a visual example of bathos, the descent from the sublime to the ridiculous.
