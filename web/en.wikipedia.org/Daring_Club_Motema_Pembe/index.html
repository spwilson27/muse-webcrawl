Daring Club Motema Pembe, commonly known as DCMP, is a Congolese football club based in Kinshasa. Founded in 1936, it is one of the most popular and successful clubs in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, playing at the Stade des Martyrs and known by its supporters as Les Immaculés.

History

The club was founded as Daring Faucon and took the name Motema Pembe ("white ebony") in the era of Mobutu Sese Seko's authenticité policy. Its greatest continental achievement came in 1994, when DCMP won the African Cup Winners' Cup. Domestically it has won numerous Linafoot league championships and Congo Cups, and contests a fierce Kinshasa derby with AS Vita Club.
