New York City

New York, often called New York City (NYC), is the most populous city in the United States, with about 8.3 million residents and over 19 million in its metropolitan area. Located at the mouth of the Hudson River, it comprises five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.

History

Settled by the Dutch as New Amsterdam in 1624, taken by England in 1664 and renamed New York, the city was the first U.S. capital (1785–1790) and grew through immigration via Ellis Island into a global centre of finance, culture and media.

Economy and culture

Home to Wall Street, the United Nations headquarters, Broadway, and major museums, New York is a leading global city. Landmarks include the Statue of Liberty, Empire State Building, Times Square and Central Park.

Transport

The New York City Subway is one of the world's largest rapid-transit systems; three major airports serve the region.
