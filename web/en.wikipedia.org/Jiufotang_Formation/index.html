Jiufotang Formation
PeriodEarly Cretaceous (Aptian)
LocationLiaoning, China
GroupJehol Group

The Jiufotang Formation is an Early Cretaceous geological formation in Liaoning Province, northeastern China, forming the upper part of the Jehol Group. It is famous for exceptionally preserved fossils of feathered dinosaurs, early birds, pterosaurs, mammals and fish, deposited in lake sediments around 120 million years ago.

Palaeontology

Notable finds include the large compsognathid Sinocalliopteryx, the oviraptorosaur Similicaudipteryx, numerous enantiornithine birds, the pterosaur faunas of the Chaoyang area, and the fish Lycoptera. Together with the older Yixian Formation, Jiufotang documents the Jehol Biota ecosystem.

Geology

The formation consists mainly of mudstones, shales and sandstones laid down in lakes, interbedded with volcanic deposits that permit radiometric dating.
