Florida

A Florida beach on the Gulf coast.

Florida is a state in the Southeastern region of the United States. It borders the Gulf of Mexico to the west, Alabama to the northwest, Georgia to the north, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, and the Straits of Florida and Cuba to the south. Florida is the third-most populous state in the United States, with over 22 million residents. Its capital is Tallahassee and its most populous city is Jacksonville; the Miami metropolitan area is its largest urban region. Florida is known for its large tourism industry, warm climate, and extensive coastline — the longest of any state in the contiguous United States.

History

Florida was first inhabited by Indigenous peoples more than 12,000 years ago. Spanish explorer Juan Ponce de León claimed the land for Spain in 1513, naming it La Florida ("the land of flowers"). St. Augustine, founded in 1565, is the oldest continuously inhabited European-established settlement in the continental United States. Spain ceded Florida to the United States in 1819 under the Adams–Onís Treaty, and Florida became a U.S. territory in 1821 and the 27th state in 1845. It seceded during the Civil War and rejoined the Union in 1868.

Geography and climate

Florida occupies a large peninsula between the Gulf of Mexico and the Atlantic Ocean, with the low-lying Florida Keys extending to the southwest. The terrain is flat, and much of the state sits close to sea level; the highest point, Britton Hill, rises only 105 metres (345 ft). Lakes are numerous, including Lake Okeechobee, one of the largest freshwater lakes in the country. The Everglades, a vast wetland in the south, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The climate ranges from humid subtropical in the north to tropical in the far south; the state is highly exposed to hurricanes during the June–November Atlantic season.

Economy

Florida has one of the largest state economies in the United States. Tourism is central — the state draws over 100 million visitors a year to destinations such as Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando, Miami Beach, and the Kennedy Space Center. Agriculture, especially citrus, is significant, as are aerospace and aviation, international trade through ports such as Miami and Port Everglades, real estate, and financial services. Florida's large retiree population also drives healthcare and services.

Demographics

Florida's population is among the most diverse in the nation, with large Hispanic and Latino communities — particularly Cuban Americans in Miami-Dade County — and substantial African American, Caribbean, and retiree populations drawn from across the United States. Spanish is widely spoken in South Florida.
