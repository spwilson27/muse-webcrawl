Chrysler Town & Country (minivan)

The Chrysler Town & Country is a minivan that was manufactured and marketed by Chrysler from model years 1990 to 2016. As the luxury variant of the Chrysler minivan platform, it was positioned above the Dodge Caravan and Plymouth Voyager, and was sold in short- and long-wheelbase versions. The Town & Country nameplate had previously been used on station wagons from the 1940s onward.

Overview

Introduced for 1990 as a premium model with simulated woodgrain trim recalling the original "woodie" wagons, the Town & Country went through five generations alongside its Dodge and Plymouth siblings. Notable features across its life included all-wheel drive, integrated child seats, dual sliding doors, and the Stow 'n Go seating system (from 2005), in which second- and third-row seats fold flat into the floor.

Discontinuation

Production ended in 2016, when Chrysler replaced the Town & Country with the Pacifica for the 2017 model year. Over its run, the Town & Country was among the best-selling minivans in North America and won numerous industry awards.
