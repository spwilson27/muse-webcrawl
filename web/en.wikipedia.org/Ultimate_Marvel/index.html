Ultimate Marvel, later known as Ultimate Comics, was an imprint of comic books published by Marvel Comics, featuring reimagined and modernized versions of the company's superhero characters in a parallel universe designated Earth-1610. Launched in 2000 with Ultimate Spider-Man, the imprint freed writers from decades of continuity and proved a major commercial success that influenced later film adaptations.

History

The line began under Marvel president Bill Jemas and editor Joe Quesada, with Brian Michael Bendis and Mark Bagley on Ultimate Spider-Man, followed by Ultimate X-Men, Ultimate Fantastic Four and Mark Millar and Bryan Hitch's The Ultimates, a reimagining of the Avengers whose cinematic style strongly influenced the Marvel Cinematic Universe. The 2009 Ultimatum event killed many characters and relaunched the line. After the 2015 Secret Wars storyline destroyed the Ultimate Universe, some characters — notably Miles Morales, the second Spider-Man — were incorporated into the main Marvel Universe. A new Ultimate Universe line was launched in 2023 under writer Jonathan Hickman.

Characteristics

Ultimate titles featured slower, decompressed storytelling, contemporary settings, and often more cynical or realistic takes on classic characters: the Ultimates operated as a government strike team, and Ultimate Nick Fury was modelled on actor Samuel L. Jackson, who later played the role on screen.
