Woodworking

Woodworking is the craft of making items from wood, including furniture, cabinetry, joinery, carving, and construction carpentry. Along with stone and clay, wood was one of the first materials worked by early humans, and the craft remains both a trade and a widely practised hobby.

History

Ancient Egyptian, Chinese, Roman, and Japanese traditions each developed sophisticated joinery and tools. The craft professionalised through medieval guilds, expanded with the powered machinery of the industrial revolution, and was celebrated for its hand skills by the Arts and Crafts movement.

Techniques and tools

Core processes include measuring and marking, sawing, planing, chiselling, boring, turning on a lathe, sanding, gluing, and finishing with oils, varnishes, or paint. Hand tools such as saws, planes, and chisels are complemented by power tools including table saws, routers, and lathes. Joinery methods range from simple butt joints to dovetails and mortise-and-tenon joints.

Materials

Woodworkers choose among hardwoods (such as oak, maple, and walnut) and softwoods (such as pine and cedar), as well as engineered products like plywood and medium-density fibreboard.
