Infinity Gauntlet

The Infinity Gauntlet is a fictional artifact in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. It is a golden glove designed to hold the six Infinity Gems (also known as Infinity Stones) — Mind, Power, Reality, Soul, Space and Time — which together grant the wearer omnipotence over the universe. The gauntlet is most famously wielded by the villain Thanos.

The Infinity Gauntlet storyline

In the 1991 limited series The Infinity Gauntlet, written by Jim Starlin with art by George Pérez and Ron Lim, Thanos assembles the gems to impress Mistress Death and, with a snap of his fingers, wipes out half of all life in the universe. Earth's heroes and cosmic beings, led by Adam Warlock and Doctor Strange, confront him; Thanos's own hubris — and his granddaughter Nebula seizing the gauntlet — lead to his defeat, and the victims are restored. The series was a major commercial success and was followed by The Infinity War (1992) and The Infinity Crusade (1993).

In other media

The Infinity Gauntlet and Stones became central to the Marvel Cinematic Universe's "Infinity Saga": Thanos's quest to collect the stones drives the plots of Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019), the latter becoming one of the highest-grossing films of all time. Versions of the gauntlet also appear in animated series and video games.
