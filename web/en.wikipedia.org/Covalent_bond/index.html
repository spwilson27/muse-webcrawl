Covalent bond

A covalent bond is a chemical bond that involves the sharing of electrons to form electron pairs between atoms. These electron pairs are known as shared pairs or bonding pairs.

Types

Single, double and triple bonds correspond to sharing one, two or three pairs. Polar covalent bonds occur when atoms have different electronegativities, as in water. Nonpolar bonds occur between similar atoms, as in H2 and O2.

History

G. N. Lewis proposed the shared-pair bond in 1916. Linus Pauling developed theories of resonance and orbital hybridisation to explain molecular geometry.
