Formula 2 (F2) is a type of open-wheel racing car and the name of the class of motorsport defined by the FIA. Successive Formula Two championships have run since 1948 under several names; the current FIA Formula 2 Championship, launched in 2017 as the successor to the GP2 Series, is the principal feeder series to Formula One.

History

Formula Two originated in the late 1940s as a junior class below Formula One, initially for 2-litre unsupercharged engines. It evolved through the European Formula Two Championship (1967–1984), Formula 3000 (1985–2004), and the GP2 Series (2005–2016).

Current championship

The FIA Formula 2 Championship supports Formula One grands prix, with identical Dallara chassis and Mecachrome engines for all teams. Race weekends feature a sprint race and a longer feature race with a mandatory pit stop.

Graduates

Champions including Charles Leclerc, George Russell, and Oscar Piastri have progressed directly to Formula One.
