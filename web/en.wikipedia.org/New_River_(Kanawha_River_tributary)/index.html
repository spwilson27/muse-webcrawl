New River (Kanawha River tributary)

The New River is a river in the southeastern United States, flowing through North Carolina, Virginia and West Virginia before joining the Gauley River to form the Kanawha River at Gauley Bridge, West Virginia.

Geography

Despite its name, the New River is among the oldest rivers in North America, predating the Appalachian Mountains it cuts through. Its course of about 320 miles includes the dramatic New River Gorge in West Virginia, spanned by the New River Gorge Bridge and protected since 2020 as the New River Gorge National Park and Preserve.

Recreation

The river is renowned for whitewater rafting and kayaking, particularly the Class III–V rapids of the Lower New, and for bass fishing and rock climbing around the gorge.
