Singham (film series)

LanguageHindi

Singham is an Indian Hindi-language action film series created by Rohit Shetty and starring Ajay Devgn as Bajirao Singham, an honest and fearless police officer. The series forms the foundation of Shetty's Cop Universe, later joined by Simmba and Sooryavanshi.

Films

Singham (2011), a remake of the 2010 Tamil film Singam, introduced Inspector Bajirao Singham of Shivgad, Maharashtra, who confronts the corrupt politician-criminal Jaikant Shikre. Singham Returns (2014) brought the character to Mumbai against a fraudulent godman and corrupt politicians. Singham Again (2024) assembled the wider Cop Universe cast in a large-scale ensemble entry drawing on the Ramayana.

Reception

The films are known for stylised action sequences and dialogue centred on Singham's integrity. The first two entries were commercial successes, and the franchise expanded into an animated television series, Singham: Kaal Ka Mahakaal, aimed at younger audiences.
