PlayStation 5

The PlayStation 5 (PS5) is a home video game console developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment. It was announced as the successor to the PlayStation 4 in April 2019, launched on 12 November 2020 in Australia, Japan, New Zealand, North America, and South Korea, and worldwide a week later. The PS5 is part of the ninth generation of video game consoles, along with Microsoft's Xbox Series X/S consoles.

Hardware

The base model includes an optical disc drive compatible with Ultra HD Blu-ray discs. The system uses a custom AMD Zen 2 CPU, a RDNA 2-based GPU with hardware ray tracing, and a custom high-speed solid-state drive designed to virtually eliminate loading times. The DualSense controller features haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. A slimmer model was released in 2023, and the enhanced PlayStation 5 Pro in November 2024.

Software and services

The PS5 is backward compatible with most PlayStation 4 games. Its software includes the PlayStation Store, the PlayStation Plus subscription service, and media streaming apps.

Sales

Despite early supply shortages, the PS5 became one of the best-selling consoles of its generation, passing 50 million units sold by December 2023 and continuing to sell strongly thereafter.
