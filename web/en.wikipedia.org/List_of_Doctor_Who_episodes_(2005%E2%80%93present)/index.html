List of Doctor Who episodes (2005–present)

Doctor Who is a British science fiction television programme produced by the BBC. After the original 1963–1989 run, the programme was revived in 2005 by Russell T Davies, beginning with the episode "Rose" (26 March 2005) starring Christopher Eccleston as the Ninth Doctor.

Overview

The revived series has featured successive Doctors played by David Tennant, Matt Smith, Peter Capaldi, Jodie Whittaker and Ncuti Gatwa, among others, organised into series (seasons) of typically 8–13 episodes plus Christmas and other specials.

Series

Series 1 (2005) introduced Rose Tyler as companion. Later highlights include Series 4 (2008) with Catherine Tate, the 50th anniversary special "The Day of the Doctor" (2013), and the 2023 60th anniversary specials that launched a new era co-produced with Bad Wolf and distributed internationally on Disney+.
