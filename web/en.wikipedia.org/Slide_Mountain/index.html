Slide Mountain

Elevation4,180 ft (1,274 m)
Prominence3,305 ft (1,007 m)
LocationUlster County, New York, U.S.
RangeCatskill Mountains

Slide Mountain is the highest peak in the Catskill Mountains of New York, in the United States. It is located in the town of Shandaken in Ulster County. While the 4,180-foot (1,270 m) contour line on modern topographic maps puts it at that height, the summit elevation is often listed as 4,180 to 4,204 feet.

Geography

The mountain lies within the Slide Mountain Wilderness Area of the Catskill Park. Its broad, gently rounded summit made it a popular destination for hikers, and the Burroughs Range Trail crosses it, linking it with nearby peaks such as Cornell Mountain and Wittenberg Mountain. The peak is named for a large landslide, or "slide", on its north face.

History

Naturalist John Burroughs wrote fondly of the mountain, and a plaque on the summit commemorates his visits. The Wittenberg-Cornell-Slide traverse is one of the most demanding day hikes in the Catskills, and Slide is one of the 35 peaks required for membership in the Catskill Mountain 3500 Club.
