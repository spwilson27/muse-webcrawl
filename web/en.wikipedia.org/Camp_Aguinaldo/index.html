Camp General Emilio Aguinaldo is the general headquarters (GHQ) of the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP), located along Epifanio de los Santos Avenue (EDSA) in Quezon City, Metro Manila. It faces Camp Crame, the headquarters of the Philippine National Police, across EDSA.

History

The camp originated as Camp Murphy during the American colonial period and was renamed after Emilio Aguinaldo, the first President of the Philippines, in 1965. Camp Aguinaldo played a central role in the People Power Revolution of February 1986, when Defence Minister Juan Ponce Enrile and General Fidel V. Ramos withdrew support from President Ferdinand Marcos and were besieged in Camps Aguinaldo and Crame, prompting mass demonstrations along EDSA.

Functions

The camp houses the AFP General Headquarters, the Department of National Defense offices, service branch headquarters elements, the AFP Medical Center (V. Luna General Hospital) nearby, and various support units.
