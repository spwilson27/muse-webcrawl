New Parliament House, New Delhi

The New Parliament House in New Delhi is the seat of the Parliament of India, inaugurated on 28 May 2023 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Triangular in plan, it stands adjacent to the old Parliament House (Sansad Bhavan) in the Central Vista area and was built as the centrepiece of the Central Vista Redevelopment Project.

Design and construction

Designed by architect Bimal Patel's firm HCP Design, Planning and Management and built by Tata Projects, construction began in 2020. The building has seating for up to 888 members in the Lok Sabha chamber and 384 in the Rajya Sabha chamber, with a joint-session capacity of about 1,272. The Lok Sabha chamber is themed on the peacock, the Rajya Sabha chamber on the lotus. A Constitutional Hall and galleries display artefacts and exhibits on India's democratic heritage.

Sengol and inauguration

At the inauguration, a ceremonial sceptre known as the Sengol, presented to Jawaharlal Nehru in 1947, was installed near the Speaker's chair in the Lok Sabha chamber. The building began hosting sessions in September 2023, when Parliament moved from the old building during a special session.

Context

The new building replaced the 1927 colonial-era Parliament House as the working home of the legislature, citing the need for more seating ahead of a future delimitation, modern facilities, and seismic safety. The project was politically contested over its cost, timing during the COVID-19 pandemic, and the manner of its inauguration.
