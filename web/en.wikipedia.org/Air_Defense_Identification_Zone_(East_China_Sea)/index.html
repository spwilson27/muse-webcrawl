East China Sea Air Defense Identification Zone

The East China Sea Air Defense Identification Zone (ADIZ) is an air defense identification zone declared by the People's Republic of China on 23 November 2013. It covers most of the East China Sea and overlaps zones previously declared by Japan, South Korea and Taiwan.

Declaration and rules

China required aircraft flying through the zone to file flight plans, maintain radio contact and display identification, warning that defensive emergency measures could be taken against non-compliant aircraft. The zone includes the airspace over the Senkaku/Diaoyu Islands, disputed between China and Japan.

Reactions

Japan, the United States and South Korea protested the declaration; US B-52 bombers flew through the zone without notifying Beijing days later. Airlines' compliance varied. The zone remains a recurring source of friction in regional airspace.
