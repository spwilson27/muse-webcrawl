Daniel Wroughton Craig (born 2 March 1968) is an English actor. He gained international fame playing James Bond in five films — Casino Royale (2006), Quantum of Solace (2008), Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015), and No Time to Die (2021) — a tenure praised for bringing a grittier, more physical realism to the role.

Career

Craig trained at the National Youth Theatre and the Guildhall School of Music and Drama. He came to attention in the BBC drama Our Friends in the North (1996) and films such as Elizabeth (1998), Lara Croft: Tomb Raider (2001), Road to Perdition (2002), Layer Cake (2004), and Munich (2005). Announced as the sixth Bond in 2005 amid initial scepticism, he won acclaim with Casino Royale, one of the highest-grossing films in the franchise's history.

Other roles

Beyond Bond, Craig has starred as detective Benoit Blanc in Rian Johnson's Knives Out (2019) and its sequels, Mikael Blomkvist in The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011), and on stage in productions including Macbeth on Broadway (2022). He was appointed a Companion of the Order of St Michael and St George (CMG) in 2022 — the same honour held by his fictional counterpart.
