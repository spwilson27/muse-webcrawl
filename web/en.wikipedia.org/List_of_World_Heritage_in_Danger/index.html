List of World Heritage in Danger

The List of World Heritage in Danger is compiled by UNESCO under the World Heritage Convention for sites threatened by conflict, natural disaster, pollution, development or other ascertained or potential dangers.

Purpose

Listing calls international attention and can unlock assistance from the World Heritage Fund. Sites may be removed when threats are addressed, or lose World Heritage status in rare cases. See UNESCO and World Heritage Site.
