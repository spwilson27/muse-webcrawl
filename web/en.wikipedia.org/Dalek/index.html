The Daleks are a fictional extraterrestrial race of mutants from the British science fiction television programme Doctor Who, created by writer Terry Nation and first appearing in 1963.

Description

Daleks are Kaleds from the planet Skaro, mutated and encased by the scientist Davros in tank-like armoured shells equipped with a gunstick and a manipulator arm resembling a plunger. They are driven by hatred of all non-Dalek life and famously shriek "Exterminate!"

Impact

The Daleks became the programme's most iconic villains and a major British cultural symbol; "Dalekmania" swept Britain in the 1960s, and the word Dalek entered the Oxford English Dictionary.

History in the series

Arch-enemies of the Doctor, the Daleks fought the Time Lords in the Last Great Time War and have returned repeatedly in both the classic and revived series.
