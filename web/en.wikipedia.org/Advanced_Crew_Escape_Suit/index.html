Advanced Crew Escape Suit

The Advanced Crew Escape Suit (ACES) is the orange full-pressure suit worn by NASA Space Shuttle crews during launch and re-entry from 1994 onward, replacing the earlier Launch Entry Suit. It was modelled on US Air Force high-altitude pressure suits.

Features

The suit provides pressurisation, breathing oxygen, cooling, communications and survival equipment including a parachute, life raft and flotation devices for ejection or bailout and water landing scenarios.

Legacy

A modified version, the Orion Crew Survival System, was developed for the Artemis programme. See also Space Shuttle, Launch Entry Suit and Pressure suit.
