Adelaide Football Club

The Adelaide Football Club, nicknamed the Crows, is a professional Australian rules football club based in Adelaide, South Australia. The club has fielded a men's team in the Australian Football League (AFL) since 1991, and a women's team in the AFL Women's (AFLW) competition since 2017. The club plays its home matches at Adelaide Oval.

History

The club was formed in 1990 after the South Australian National Football League (SANFL) won an AFL licence, and played its first AFL season in 1991 under coach Graham Cornes. Adelaide famously won back-to-back AFL premierships in 1997 and 1998 under coach Malcolm Blight, defeating St Kilda and North Melbourne respectively. Club greats include Andrew McLeod, Mark Ricciuto, and Simon Goodwin.

AFL Women's

Adelaide was a foundation AFLW club and dominated the early years of the competition, winning premierships in 2017, 2019, and 2022 (season 6), the most of any club in the league's early history. Erin Phillips was a two-time league best and fairest with the Crows before moving to Port Adelaide.

Rivalries

The club's chief rivalry is with Port Adelaide, the other South Australian AFL club; their meetings are known as the Showdown, first played in 1997.
