International Airlines Group

International Consolidated Airlines Group S.A., trading as International Airlines Group (IAG), is a British-Spanish multinational airline holding company formed in January 2011 from the merger of British Airways and Iberia, the flag carriers of the United Kingdom and Spain.

Operations

IAG's airline brands include British Airways, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Vueling, and LEVEL, together operating a fleet of several hundred aircraft and carrying over 100 million passengers in peak years. The group is headquartered in London, with its registered office in Madrid, and is listed on both the London Stock Exchange and the Bolsa de Madrid.

Structure

The group also includes IAG Cargo and the Avios loyalty programme. Qatar Airways has been a major shareholder. IAG pursued further consolidation, including an ultimately abandoned bid for Air Europa.
