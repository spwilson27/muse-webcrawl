Warner Bros. Discovery

Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc. (WBD) is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate headquartered in New York City. The company was formed through the merger of AT&T's WarnerMedia and Discovery, Inc., which was completed on April 8, 2022. Its name is a combination of WarnerMedia's flagship property, the film studio Warner Bros. Pictures, and the Discovery cable television network.

Operations

The company's businesses are divided into three divisions: Studios (Warner Bros. Pictures, New Line Cinema, DC Studios, Warner Bros. Television, and the Warner Bros. studio lots), Network (cable channels including CNN, HBO, TNT, TBS, Cartoon Network, Discovery Channel, HGTV, and Food Network), and DTC (the streaming services Max and discovery+). WBD also owns stakes in The CW and operates Turner Sports properties, including rights partnerships covering U.S. sports broadcasts.

History

AT&T had acquired Time Warner in 2018 and renamed it WarnerMedia; in May 2021 it announced it would spin off the unit and merge it with Discovery, creating one of the world's largest media companies, led by Discovery CEO David Zaslav. The merger left the combined company with substantial debt, prompting cost-cutting, content write-downs, and restructuring. In June 2025 the company announced plans to split into two publicly traded companies — "Warner Bros." (studios and streaming) and "Discovery Global" (networks).
