Dodge

Dodge is an American brand of automobiles and a division of Stellantis North America, based in Auburn Hills, Michigan. Dodge vehicles have historically included performance cars, and for much of its existence Dodge was Chrysler's mid-priced brand above Plymouth.

History

Brothers John and Horace Dodge began as bicycle and machine-part manufacturers supplying the early automobile industry, including Ford Motor Company, before founding their own automobile company in 1914. Both brothers died in 1920, and the company was sold to Chrysler Corporation in 1928.

Products

Notable Dodge models include the Charger and Challenger muscle cars, the Viper sports car, the Caravan minivan, and the Durango SUV. Ram trucks, once sold as Dodge Ram, became a separate brand in 2010. In the 2020s the brand emphasized performance models and began a transition toward electrified muscle cars.
