Cosworth

Cosworth is a British automotive engineering company founded in London in 1958 by Mike Costin and Keith Duckworth. It specialises in high-performance internal combustion engines, powertrains and electronics for automobile racing and mainstream automotive industries.

History

Cosworth is best known for the DFV (Double Four Valve) V8 Formula One engine, introduced in 1967 with Lotus and funded by Ford. The DFV won 155 Grands Prix and became the most successful Grand Prix engine of all time. Later engines powered McLaren, Williams and others, and Cosworth supplied IndyCar, Champ Car and rally programmes, including Ford Escort and Sierra Cosworth road cars.

Ownership

The company passed through United Engineering Industries, Vickers, Volkswagen/Audi and Ford ownership, before being bought by Gerald Forsythe and Kevin Kalkhoven in 2004. It is headquartered in Northampton, England.
