The Kurdish Democratic Party of Iran (KDPI; Kurdish: حیزبی دێموکراتی کوردستانی ئێران), also known as the Democratic Party of Iranian Kurdistan, is a Kurdish political party that has sought self-determination for the Kurdish people of Iran. Founded in 1945 in Mahabad by Qazi Muhammad, it is one of the oldest Kurdish political organisations in Iran.

History

The party was founded on 16 August 1945, shortly before the establishment of the short-lived Republic of Mahabad in 1946, in which Qazi Muhammad served as president. After the republic was crushed by the Iranian army in December 1946 and Qazi Muhammad was executed in 1947, the party continued underground and in exile.

Under the leadership of Abdul Rahman Ghassemlou, the party advocated "democracy for Iran and autonomy for Kurdistan". Ghassemlou was assassinated in Vienna in 1989, allegedly by Iranian agents. His successor, Sadegh Sharafkandi, was assassinated in the Mykonos restaurant assassinations in Berlin in 1992.

Activities

The KDPI maintained armed forces known as Peshmerga and was based for decades in the mountains along the Iran–Iraq border and in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. In 2016 the party announced a resumption of armed struggle, citing the deteriorating situation of Kurds in Iran. Cross-border Iranian missile and drone strikes on KDPI bases in Iraqi Kurdistan in 2018 and 2022 killed a number of members and civilians.
