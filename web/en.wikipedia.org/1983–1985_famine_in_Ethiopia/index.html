1983–1985 famine in Ethiopia

The 1983–1985 famine in Ethiopia was a widespread famine that devastated the northern regions of the country, particularly Tigray and Wollo, during the rule of the Derg military regime. Drought combined with civil war and counter-insurgency campaigns to produce mass starvation.

International response

Television reports by the BBC in October 1984 brought the famine to global attention, prompting an unprecedented relief effort. Bob Geldof organised the Band Aid single "Do They Know It's Christmas?" in 1984 and the Live Aid concerts of July 1985, which raised tens of millions of pounds for relief.

Death toll

Estimates of mortality range from about 300,000 to more than one million people. The famine remains one of the defining humanitarian crises of the late 20th century.
