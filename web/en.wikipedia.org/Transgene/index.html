Transgene

A transgene is a gene that has been transferred naturally, or by genetic engineering techniques, from one organism to another. An organism that carries a transgene is described as transgenic; the introduction of a transgene can change the organism's phenotype.

Methods

Transgenes are delivered by microinjection into embryos, viral vectors, Agrobacterium-mediated transformation in plants, electroporation and gene guns, among other methods. They are typically inserted with promoter and regulatory sequences so they are expressed in the desired tissues.

Applications

Transgenic organisms are used in research (for example fluorescent reporter mice and knockout models), agriculture (insect-resistant Bt crops and herbicide-tolerant varieties), and medicine (production of insulin and other proteins in engineered microbes and animals). Transgenes also underlie gene therapy approaches that add functional genes to patients' cells.
