Max Emilian Verstappen (born 30 September 1997) is a Dutch racing driver who competes in Formula One for Red Bull Racing. He is a four-time Formula One World Drivers' Champion, having won consecutive titles from 2021 to 2024, and is widely regarded as one of the most talented drivers of his generation.

Early career

The son of former Formula One driver Jos Verstappen and karting champion Sophie Kumpen, Verstappen excelled in karting, winning multiple world and European championships, and finished third in the 2014 European Formula Three Championship in his single season of car racing. Signed to the Red Bull Junior Team, he was promoted directly to Formula One with Toro Rosso.

Formula One career

Verstappen made his debut at the 2015 Australian Grand Prix aged 17 years and 166 days, becoming the youngest driver in Formula One history — a record that prompted the FIA to impose a minimum age of 18. Promoted to Red Bull Racing mid-season in 2016, he won on his debut for the team at the Spanish Grand Prix, becoming the youngest Grand Prix winner ever at 18 years and 228 days.

After several seasons as a regular race winner, Verstappen fought a season-long duel with Lewis Hamilton in 2021, winning the championship on the last lap of the final race in Abu Dhabi in highly controversial circumstances. He dominated the 2022 and especially 2023 seasons, the latter setting records including ten consecutive Grand Prix victories and 19 wins in a season, and secured a fourth consecutive title in 2024 despite a more competitive field.

Driving style and records

Known for aggressive defending, exceptional car control, and consistency, Verstappen holds numerous Formula One records, including youngest points scorer and most wins in a season. He also competes in sim racing with Team Redline and has expressed interest in endurance racing.
