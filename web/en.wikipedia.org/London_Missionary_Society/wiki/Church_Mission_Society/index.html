Church Mission Society

The Church Mission Society (CMS), formerly the Church Missionary Society, is a collection of independent mission societies working with the Anglican Communion and Protestant Christians around the world. Founded in London in 1799, it should not be confused with the interdenominational London Missionary Society (founded 1795).

Founded12 April 1799, London
TypeAnglican mission society
Former nameChurch Missionary Society

History

Founded by evangelical Anglicans including William Wilberforce and John Venn, CMS sent missionaries first to Sierra Leone, then India, China, East Africa, New Zealand and elsewhere. Missionaries contributed to Bible translation, education and the abolition of slavery.

Work

Today CMS partners work in mission, development and evangelism across Africa, Asia, Europe and the Middle East. See London Missionary Society context, David Livingstone and London.
