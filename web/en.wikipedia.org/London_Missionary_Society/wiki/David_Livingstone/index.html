David Livingstone

David Livingstone (19 March 1813 – 1 May 1873) was a Scottish physician, Congregationalist and pioneer medical missionary with the London Missionary Society, and an explorer in Africa.

Born19 March 1813, Blantyre, Scotland
Died1 May 1873, Chief Chitambo's Village, present-day Zambia
Known forExploration of Africa, Victoria Falls

Missionary and explorer

Livingstone arrived in southern Africa in 1841, crossed the Kalahari, reached the Zambezi and in 1855 saw the falls he named Victoria Falls. He sought routes for commerce and Christianity and campaigned against the slave trade.

Later years

His search for the source of the Nile drew Henry Morton Stanley, whose 1871 meeting produced the famous greeting 'Dr Livingstone, I presume?' Livingstone died in 1873; his heart was buried in Africa and his body in Westminster Abbey, London. See Church Mission Society for a related society.
