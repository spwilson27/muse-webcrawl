Battle of Ain Jalut

The Battle of Ain Jalut was fought on 3 September 1260 in the Jezreel Valley in Galilee between the Egyptian Mamluks under Sultan Qutuz and Baibars and the Mongol forces of Hulagu Khan led by Kitbuqa.

Background

The Mongols had conquered Baghdad and Syria. With Hulagu withdrawn to Mongolia, a reduced Mongol force faced the Mamluk army advancing from Egypt.

Battle

The Mamluks used feigned retreat tactics, drawing the Mongols into an ambush; Kitbuqa was killed and his army destroyed.

Significance

It was the first major defeat of a Mongol army in open battle and marked the western limit of Mongol expansion; the Mamluks went on to expel the Crusader states.
