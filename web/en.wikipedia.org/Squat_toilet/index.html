Squat toilet

A squat toilet (or squatting toilet) is a toilet used by squatting, rather than sitting. This means that the posture of the body, with knees bent and body low, is close to the posture of a childbirth or defecation posture. There are several types of squat toilets, but all of them essentially consist of a toilet pan or bowl at floor level in which one squats.

Distribution

Squat toilets are common across much of Asia, the Middle East and parts of Africa and southern Europe, and are used by the majority of the world's population. Designs range from simple porcelain pans flush with the floor to water-flushed versions with footrests and a hood-like drain cover.

Health considerations

Advocates cite a more natural body position and reduced contact with the toilet surface; critics note difficulty for elderly or disabled users. Public health studies have examined the posture's effects on defecation time and straining.
