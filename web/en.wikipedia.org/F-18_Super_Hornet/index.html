Boeing F/A-18E/F Super Hornet

The Boeing F/A-18E and F/A-18F Super Hornet are a series of American supersonic twin-engine, carrier-capable, multirole fighter aircraft derived from the earlier McDonnell Douglas F/A-18 Hornet. The single-seat E and two-seat F variants are larger, with greater fuel capacity, more powerful General Electric F414 engines, and increased payload.

F/A-18E/F Super Hornet
Manufacturer: Boeing
First flight: 29 November 1995
Introduction: 1999
Primary users: United States Navy; Royal Australian Air Force

Development

The Super Hornet entered service with the US Navy in 1999, replacing the Grumman F-14 Tomcat in fleet air defence while also performing strike missions. The EA-18G Growler electronic warfare variant shares its airframe.

Design

The aircraft features enlarged leading-edge extensions, a 25% larger wing, two extra hardpoints, and modest radar cross-section reduction measures while retaining carrier suitability.
