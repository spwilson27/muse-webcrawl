Frost/Nixon (film)

Frost/Nixon is a 2008 American historical drama film directed by Ron Howard and written by Peter Morgan, adapted from Morgan's stage play of the same name. The film dramatises the series of televised interviews that British journalist David Frost conducted with former U.S. President Richard Nixon in 1977, three years after Nixon resigned over the Watergate scandal.

Plot and cast

Frank Langella stars as Nixon and Michael Sheen as Frost, reprising their roles from the stage production, which premiered in London in 2006 and transferred to Broadway. Frost, whose career was in decline, paid Nixon a large fee for the interviews in hopes of a career-making confrontation, while Nixon sought rehabilitation and a share of the profits. The film focuses on the preparation by Frost's researchers and the final interview session, in which Frost pressed Nixon into an admission that he had participated in a cover-up and let down the American people. The cast also includes Sam Rockwell, Kevin Bacon, Rebecca Hall, and Oliver Platt.

Reception

The film received critical acclaim and five Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, Best Director, and Best Actor for Langella. It was praised for turning the interviews into a psychological duel, though journalists and Nixon associates noted dramatic liberties with the historical record.
