Planty Park

Planty Park (Polish: Planty) is a park in Kraków, Poland, encircling the Old Town in a ring about 4 kilometres long and covering roughly 21 hectares. It was created in the early 19th century on the site of the demolished medieval city walls.

History

When Kraków's fortifications were dismantled in the 1820s, the moats were filled and gardens laid out in their place, following the fashion for ring promenades in European cities. The park is divided into named sections and contains monuments, fountains and mature trees, with the Barbican and St Florian's Gate surviving from the old defences at its northern edge.

Today

The Planty is one of the city's best-loved green spaces, used by walkers, students and tourists, and forms part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing of Kraków's historic centre.
