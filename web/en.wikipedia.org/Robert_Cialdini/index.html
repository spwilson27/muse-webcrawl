Robert Beno Cialdini (born 27 April 1945) is an American psychologist and author. He is the Regents' Professor Emeritus of Psychology and Marketing at Arizona State University and was a visiting professor of marketing at the Stanford Graduate School of Business. Cialdini is best known for his 1984 book Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion, one of the best-selling business books of all time.

Principles of persuasion

In Influence, based on three years of undercover observation of salespeople, fundraisers, recruiters, and advertisers, Cialdini identified six key principles of persuasion: reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, liking, authority, and scarcity. In his 2016 book Pre-Suasion he introduced the concept of priming attention before a request, and in the 2021 expanded edition of Influence he added a seventh principle, unity, based on shared identity.

Other work

Cialdini's academic research has covered the door-in-the-face technique, the low-ball technique, altruism, and using social-norms messaging to encourage environmentally responsible behaviour, such as the famous studies of littering and of hotel towel reuse. He co-authored Yes! 50 Scientifically Proven Ways to Be Persuasive (2007) with Noah Goldstein and Steve Martin.
