Ignazio Porro

Born 25 November 1801, Pinerolo
Died 8 October 1875, Milan
Field Optics, surveying

Ignazio Porro (25 November 1801 – 8 October 1875) was an Italian inventor, engineer, and optician, known for his work in optics and surveying instruments, including the Porro prism used in binoculars.

Work

Porro served as an officer of engineers in Piedmont and worked in Paris, where he founded an institute for applied optics. He designed tachymetric surveying methods and instruments, an early system of stadia measurement, and optical devices including anamorphic systems.

Porro prism

His name is best remembered in the Porro prism erecting system patented in 1854, which uses two right-angle prisms to invert and revert an image; the design remains standard in many binoculars.
