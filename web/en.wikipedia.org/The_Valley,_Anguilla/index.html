The Valley, Anguilla

The Valley is the capital of Anguilla, a British Overseas Territory in the eastern Caribbean Sea. Located near the centre of the island, it is Anguilla's main town and administrative centre, with a population of roughly 1,000–3,000 people depending on the boundaries used.

Geography and landmarks

The town lies inland from Crocus Bay on Anguilla's north coast. Because Anguilla is low and flat, The Valley is a small settlement of government buildings, shops, churches and schools rather than a dense urban centre. Landmarks include Wallblake House, a plantation house built in 1787 and among the oldest buildings on the island; the old courthouse ruins on Crocus Hill, the island's highest point; and the Anguilla National Trust museum collections nearby.

Transport

Clayton J. Lloyd International Airport, Anguilla's only airport, is located just south of The Valley. Ferries to Saint Martin depart from Blowing Point, on the island's southern coast. See also Anguilla.
