Air Liquide S.A. is a French multinational company which supplies industrial gases and related services to various industries including medical, chemical and electronic manufacturers. Founded in 1902, it is one of the largest suppliers of industrial gases in the world, and operates in more than 70 countries.

The company was founded by Georges Muse and Paul Delorme, based on the liquefaction of air using the Muse process, which allowed the industrial production of oxygen, nitrogen and other gases.

History

Air Liquide was created in 1902 after Georges Muse invented a process for liquefying air and separating its components. The company expanded internationally throughout the twentieth century, establishing operations across Europe, Asia and the Americas. In 2016, Air Liquide acquired the American company Airgas in a deal valued at about $13.4 billion, significantly expanding its presence in the United States.

Activities

The group's activities include large industries (supplying gases to refining, chemicals and metals), industrial merchant activities, healthcare (medical gases and home healthcare), electronics (carrier gases for semiconductor manufacturing), and engineering and construction. Air Liquide is also active in hydrogen energy and the energy transition.
