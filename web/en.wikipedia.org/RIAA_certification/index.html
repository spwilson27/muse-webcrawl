RIAA certification

RIAA certification
Awarded by: Recording Industry Association of America
First awarded: 1958
Levels: Gold, Platinum, Multi-Platinum, Diamond

RIAA certification is a system by which the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA) certifies the shipment and sales figures of singles and albums in the United States. The program was launched in 1958 with the Gold single award; Perry Como's "Catch a Falling Star" was the first Gold single, and the original cast recording of Oklahoma! the first Gold album.

Levels

For albums, Gold denotes 500,000 units, Platinum 1,000,000 units, Multi-Platinum each further million, and Diamond 10,000,000 units. Singles historically used higher thresholds (Gold at 1,000,000) until 1989, when single thresholds were aligned with albums at 500,000 for Gold. Since 2013, on-demand audio and video streams count toward certifications under a formula in which 1,500 streams equal one album unit for album awards.

Programs

Related programs include Los Premios de Oro y Platino for Latin recordings (launched 2000) and separate awards for music videos and ringtones ("Mastertone"). Michael Jackson's Thriller and the Eagles' Their Greatest Hits (1971–1975) are among the highest-certified albums in the program's history.
