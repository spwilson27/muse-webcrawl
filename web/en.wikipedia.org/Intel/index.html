Intel

Intel Corporation is an American multinational semiconductor company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Founded in 1968 by Gordon Moore and Robert Noyce, Intel invented the x86 microprocessor architecture and for decades was the dominant supplier of CPUs for personal computers.

History

Intel introduced the 4004 microprocessor in 1971, the 8086 in 1978, and the Pentium in 1993. Its partnership with Microsoft, known as Wintel, defined the PC era. The 'Intel Inside' campaign made it a consumer brand.

Products

Products include Core processors, Xeon server chips, chipsets, GPUs under the Arc brand, and foundry services through Intel Foundry. Competitors include AMD, TSMC, Samsung and Arm-based designers.

Challenges

In the 2010s and 2020s Intel faced manufacturing delays, loss of process leadership to TSMC, and competition in data centres and AI accelerators.
