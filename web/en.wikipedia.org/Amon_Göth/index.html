Amon Leopold Göth (December 11, 1908 – September 13, 1946) was an Austrian SS functionary and war criminal. He served as commandant of the Kraków-Płaszów concentration camp in German-occupied Poland during World War II and was responsible for the murder of thousands of prisoners. Göth also directed the liquidation of the Kraków and Tarnów ghettos in 1943.

Crimes

Göth was notorious for personally shooting prisoners, including from the balcony of his villa at Płaszów, and for other arbitrary killings. He was arrested by the SS in 1944 on charges including theft of Jewish property, though he was never tried by the Nazi regime.

Trial and execution

After the war, Göth was extradited to Poland and tried by the Supreme National Tribunal in Kraków. He was convicted of war crimes and personally murdering tens of thousands of people and was hanged on September 13, 1946. He was portrayed by Ralph Fiennes in the 1993 film Schindler's List.
