Punchestown Racecourse

Punchestown Racecourse is located near Naas in County Kildare, Ireland. It is Ireland's premier National Hunt (jumps) racing venue and the home of the annual Punchestown Festival, one of the highlights of the Irish horse racing calendar.

History

Racing at Punchestown dates to the 19th century, when the Kildare Hunt organised meetings on the site. The course developed into a major venue over the following decades, attracting large crowds from Dublin and across Ireland. Major redevelopment in the late 1990s and early 2000s modernised the facilities, including a new grandstand and parade ring.

The Festival

The Punchestown Festival is held over five days in late April or early May and effectively closes the Irish National Hunt season. Its feature races include the Punchestown Gold Cup, the Punchestown Champion Hurdle, the Champion Stayers Hurdle, and the Punchestown Champion Chase. The meeting regularly attracts leading British-trained horses to compete against the best of Ireland.

Course

The track is a right-handed, undulating oval of about two miles with a testing uphill finish. In addition to the festival, the racecourse hosts fixtures throughout the jumps season and a variety of non-racing events.
