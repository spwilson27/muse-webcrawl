List of pharaohs

Pharaoh is the vernacular term for the kings of ancient Egypt, from the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt under Narmer around 3100 BC until the annexation of Egypt by Rome in 30 BC. Egyptian kingship endured for over three millennia and some thirty dynasties, as conventionally divided by the priest Manetho in the 3rd century BC. The king was regarded as a living god, the incarnation of Horus and son of Ra, responsible for maintaining ma'at — cosmic order.

Late Period and Ptolemaic dynasty

The Third Intermediate Period saw Libyan (22nd–23rd) and Kushite (25th) dynasties; the 26th (Saite) dynasty restored native rule before Persian conquests in 525 and 343 BC. Alexander the Great was crowned pharaoh in 332 BC; after his death his general Ptolemy I Soter founded the Ptolemaic dynasty, whose last ruler, Cleopatra VII, died in 30 BC as Egypt became a Roman province.
