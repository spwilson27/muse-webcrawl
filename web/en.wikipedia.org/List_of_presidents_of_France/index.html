List of presidents of France

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Élysée Palace in Paris, seat of the French presidency.

The President of France is the head of state of the French Republic. The first officeholder was Louis-Napoléon Bonaparte, elected in 1848 during the Second Republic; he later proclaimed himself Emperor Napoleon III. The office was re-established under the Third Republic (1870), continued under the Fourth Republic (1946), and took its modern, powerful form under the Fifth Republic founded by Charles de Gaulle in 1958. Since 1962 the president has been elected by direct universal suffrage; since 2002 the term is five years, renewable once.
