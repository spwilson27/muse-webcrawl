List of World War II flying aces

A flying ace is a military aviator credited with shooting down five or more enemy aircraft in aerial combat. The Second World War produced more aces than any other conflict, with the highest scores achieved by German Luftwaffe pilots on the Eastern Front, where individual tallies exceeded 300 victories — figures that historians note must be read alongside differing national confirmation practices and overclaiming common to all air forces.

Context

The extraordinary German scores reflect the target-rich environment of the Eastern Front, long careers without rotation home, and late-war conditions; Allied pilots typically flew limited tours. Soviet ace Ivan Kozhedub was the highest-scoring Allied pilot. Finland's Ilmari Juutilainen was the highest-scoring non-German ace. Verification standards varied: Germany required confirmation, while other systems credited shared and probable victories differently, making cross-country comparisons inexact.
