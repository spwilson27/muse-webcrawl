Media ethics

Media ethics is the branch of applied ethics dealing with the standards and principles of media, including journalism, broadcasting, film, and online media. Central values include truth and accuracy, independence, fairness and impartiality, humanity, and accountability.

Issues

Key issues include conflicts of interest, privacy versus the public interest, deception in newsgathering, plagiarism and fabrication, sensationalism, the naming of victims and suspects, and the ethics of images of violence. Professional codes, such as those of the Society of Professional Journalists, guide practitioners, while press councils and ombudsmen handle complaints.

Digital age

Social media, citizen journalism, algorithmic amplification, misinformation and artificial intelligence have raised new questions about verification, moderation, and the responsibilities of platforms.
