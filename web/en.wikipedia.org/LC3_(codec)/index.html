LC3 (codec)

LC3 is the mandatory codec of Bluetooth LE Audio

LC3 (Low Complexity Communication Codec) is an audio codec specified by the Bluetooth Special Interest Group for the LE Audio standard introduced with Bluetooth 5.2. Developed primarily by Fraunhofer IIS and Ericsson, LC3 is the mandatory codec for Bluetooth LE Audio, replacing the older SBC codec of classic Bluetooth audio profiles.

Technical features

LC3 operates at bitrates from 16 to 320 kbit/s with frame durations of 7.5 or 10 ms and supports sampling rates from 8 to 48 kHz, mono and stereo, and 16, 24 or 32 bits per sample. Despite the lower data rates, listening tests showed LC3 delivers better perceived audio quality than SBC at comparable settings — roughly comparable or better quality at about half the bitrate — thanks to more efficient spectral coding and noise shaping. An enhanced version, LC3plus, developed by Fraunhofer, offers still lower latency and higher sample rates and is used in some DECT and gaming products.

Applications

Because LE Audio is built on the low-energy radio, LC3 enables longer battery life in earbuds, hearing aids and headsets. LE Audio features built on the codec include Auracast broadcast audio, multi-stream connections to two earbuds, and improved hearing-aid support. LC3 support spread through smartphones, earbuds and hearing devices from the early 2020s onward.
