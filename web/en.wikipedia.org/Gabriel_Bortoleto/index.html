Gabriel Lourenzo Bortoleto Oliveira (born 14 October 2004) is a Brazilian racing driver who competes in Formula One. He won the FIA Formula 3 Championship in 2023 as a rookie and the FIA Formula 2 Championship in 2024, also as a rookie — a back-to-back feat matched by only a handful of drivers — before stepping up to Formula One with Sauber in 2025, becoming the first Brazilian full-time F1 driver since Felipe Massa's retirement in 2017.

Junior career

Bortoleto began karting in Brazil at age six and moved to Europe to pursue single-seaters, finishing third in the 2020 Italian F4 Championship. After two seasons in the Formula Regional European Championship, he joined Trident for the 2023 FIA Formula 3 season, winning two races and sealing the title with a round to spare. Managed by Fernando Alonso's A14 Management company, he was also a member of the McLaren Driver Development Programme.

Formula 2 and Formula One

With Invicta Racing in 2024, Bortoleto recovered from a pointless opening rounds to win the Formula 2 title at the final round in Abu Dhabi, highlighted by a victory at the Red Bull Ring and a charge from last to first at Monza's feature race. Released by McLaren, he signed for Sauber for 2025, ahead of the team's transition into the Audi works team in 2026.

Personal life

Bortoleto's father, Lincoln Oliveira, is a businessman involved in Brazilian motorsport and telecommunications. Gabriel is widely regarded as the leading Brazilian prospect of his generation.
