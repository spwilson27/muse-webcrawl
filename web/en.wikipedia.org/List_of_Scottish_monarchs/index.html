List of Scottish monarchs

This list covers the monarchs of the Kingdom of Scotland from its traditional foundation by Kenneth MacAlpin in 843 until the Acts of Union 1707 united Scotland and England as Great Britain.

Early monarchs

MonarchReign
Kenneth MacAlpin (Cináed mac Ailpín)843–858
Constantine I862–877
Malcolm I943–954
Macbeth1040–1057
Malcolm III Canmore1058–1093
David I1124–1153
Alexander II1214–1249
Alexander III1249–1286

Later monarchs

After the interregnum and Wars of Independence came John Balliol, Robert the Bruce (1306–1329), David II, then the Stewart/Stuart line: Robert II, Robert III, James I through James VI (who became James I of England in 1603), Charles I, Charles II, James VII, Mary II and William II, and Anne.
