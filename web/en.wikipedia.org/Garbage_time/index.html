Garbage time

Garbage time is a slang term in sports for the closing portion of a game after the outcome has effectively been decided. Coaches often substitute reserve players, rest starters, reduce injury risk and evaluate younger athletes, while the trailing team may play with little realistic chance of winning.

Usage

The phrase is common in basketball and American football, and by extension in other timed sports. Statistics compiled in garbage time are sometimes discounted by analysts because defences relax, rotations change and the game state no longer resembles competitive minutes.

Effects

Garbage time can give bench players experience and allow fans to see prospects, but it can also distort betting lines, player averages and late scoring totals. In blowouts, teams may slow the pace or run out the clock as a matter of sportsmanship.

Criticism

Some commentators dislike the term because athletes are still competing professionally; others use it only for minutes after both teams have clearly shifted to reserves.
