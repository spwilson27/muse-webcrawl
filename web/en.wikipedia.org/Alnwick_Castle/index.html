Alnwick Castle

Alnwick Castle is a castle and country house in Alnwick, Northumberland, England, and the seat of the Duke of Northumberland, head of the Percy family. Built following the Norman Conquest and extensively refurbished over the centuries, it is the second-largest inhabited castle in England after Windsor Castle.

History

The first parts of the castle were built around 1096 by Yves de Vescy, Baron of Alnwick. The Percy family acquired it in 1309, and under Henry Percy, 1st Earl of Northumberland, and his successors it was strengthened against Scottish incursions; the castle's towers and gatehouse largely date from the 14th century. After periods of decline, the 1st and 4th Dukes of Northumberland commissioned major restorations in the 18th and 19th centuries, the latter involving architect Anthony Salvin and sumptuous Italianate interiors by Luigi Canina.

Present day

The castle remains the family home of Ralph Percy, 12th Duke of Northumberland, and is open to the public in season. It is a popular filming location — it served as Hogwarts School in the first two Harry Potter films — and its grounds, landscaped by Capability Brown, include the modern Alnwick Garden, developed by Jane Percy, Duchess of Northumberland, from 1997. During the Second World War the castle housed evacuee schoolgirls, and since 1945 part of it has hosted a campus of St. Cloud State University (Minnesota) and teacher-training programmes.
