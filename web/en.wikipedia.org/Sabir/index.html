Sabir

Sabir may refer to several things:

Language

Sabir, also known as the Mediterranean Lingua Franca, was a pidgin language used for trade and diplomacy around the Mediterranean Sea from the medieval period into the 19th century. Drawing vocabulary mainly from Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Occitan, Catalan, Turkish, Greek and Arabic, it allowed merchants and sailors of different tongues to communicate. The word sabir comes from the Romance verb "to know".

Name

Sabir is also a male given name of Arabic origin (صبير, from ṣabr, "patience"), borne by various people, and a surname in South Asia and the Middle East.
