Language planning

Language planning is the deliberate effort to influence the function, structure, or acquisition of languages within a speech community, often undertaken by governments, education systems or language academies. It is commonly divided into status planning, corpus planning and acquisition planning.

Types

Status planning concerns the social functions of a language; corpus planning concerns its form, spelling and vocabulary; acquisition planning concerns who learns it, for example through schooling.

Examples

Examples include the promotion of Hebrew in Israel, the work of the Académie française, Norway's language reforms, and the standardisation of Bahasa Indonesia.
