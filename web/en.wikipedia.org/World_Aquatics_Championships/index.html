The World Aquatics Championships are the world championships for six aquatic disciplines — swimming, diving, high diving, open water swimming, artistic swimming, and water polo — organised by World Aquatics. The first edition was held in Belgrade in 1973.

History

The championships were held at irregular intervals until 2001, when they settled into a biennial cycle in odd-numbered years. Fukuoka (2001, 2023), Rome (2009), Shanghai (2011), Barcelona (2013), Budapest (2017, 2022), Gwangju (2019), and Doha (2024) have hosted recent editions.

Medal history

The United States, China, Australia, and Russia have historically led the all-time medal table, with Michael Phelps the most decorated swimmer in the event's history.
