Finneas O'Connell

Finneas Baird O'Connell (born July 30, 1997), known mononymously as Finneas, is an American singer-songwriter, record producer and actor. He is best known for co-writing and producing the music of his younger sister, Billie Eilish.

He has won multiple Grammy Awards, including Producer of the Year, and Academy Awards for the songs "No Time to Die" and "What Was I Made For?". His solo work includes the EP Blood Harmony (2019) and the album Optimist (2021).
