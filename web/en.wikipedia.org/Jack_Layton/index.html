Jack Layton

John Gilbert Layton (1950 – 2011) was a Canadian politician who served as leader of the New Democratic Party (NDP) from 2003 to 2011 and as Leader of the Official Opposition in 2011.

Born18 July 1950, Montreal, Quebec
Died22 August 2011, Toronto, Ontario
PartyNew Democratic Party
SpouseOlivia Chow

Career

The son of Progressive Conservative cabinet minister Robert Layton, Jack Layton taught political science at Ryerson University and served on Toronto City Council from 1982, becoming a prominent municipal voice on housing, the environment and public health. He won the NDP leadership in 2003 and entered Parliament for Toronto—Danforth in 2004.

2011 election and death

In the May 2011 federal election, the "Orange Wave" carried the NDP to 103 seats — its best result ever — sweeping Quebec and making Layton Leader of the Opposition. Weeks later he announced a leave of absence to fight a new cancer; he died in August 2011. His final letter to Canadians, urging "hope and optimism", was widely shared.
