Yu-Gi-Oh! is a Japanese media franchise created by Kazuki Takahashi. It began as a manga series serialised in Weekly Shōnen Jump from 1996 to 2004, following Yugi Mutou, a boy who solves an ancient Egyptian puzzle and becomes host to a mysterious gambler spirit.

Franchise

The manga's fictional card game, Duel Monsters, was developed into a real trading card game by Konami, which became one of the best-selling card games in the world. Multiple anime series, films, video games, and spin-off manga followed, including Yu-Gi-Oh! GX, 5D's, Zexal, and later series, each introducing new summoning mechanics and protagonists.

Legacy

The franchise is among the highest-grossing media franchises of all time, with the trading card game recognised by Guinness World Records for its sales, and competitive tournaments held worldwide.
