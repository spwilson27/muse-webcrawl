Crossref

Crossref (formerly styled CrossRef) is a non-profit membership organisation for scholarly publishing and the largest registration agency for Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs). Founded in 2000, it enables persistent linking between scholarly works: publishers deposit metadata for articles, books, conference papers and other outputs, and each receives a DOI that resolves to its current location on the web.

Services

Beyond DOI registration, Crossref provides services built on the deposited metadata, including reference linking between citing and cited works, Similarity Check for plagiarism screening, Crossmark for updates and retractions, and open metadata feeds used by libraries, funders and discovery services. It also co-founded the Initiative for Open Citations (I4OC), which has made citation data openly available.

Membership

Thousands of publishers, learned societies, universities and other organisations worldwide are Crossref members, and its registry covers well over one hundred million registered content items.
