Rosalynn Carter

Rosalynn Carter
Born: August 18, 1927, Plains, Georgia
Died: November 19, 2023, Plains, Georgia
Role: First Lady of the United States (1977–1981)
Spouse: Jimmy Carter

Eleanor Rosalynn Carter (née Smith; 1927–2023) was an American writer, activist, and humanitarian who served as First Lady of the United States from 1977 to 1981 as the wife of President Jimmy Carter. For decades she was a leading advocate for mental health care.

First Lady

Rosalynn Carter took an unusually active role in her husband's administration, sitting in on Cabinet meetings, serving as an envoy to Latin America, and campaigning for the Mental Health Systems Act of 1980. She was the first First Lady to maintain her own office in the East Wing.

Post-White House work

With Jimmy Carter she co-founded the Carter Center in 1982, working on disease eradication, election monitoring, and mental health policy. She also volunteered for many years with Habitat for Humanity. Her memoirs include First Lady from Plains (1984).
