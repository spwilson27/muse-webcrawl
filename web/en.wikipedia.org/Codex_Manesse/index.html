Codex Manesse

The Codex Manesse (also called the Große Heidelberger Liederhandschrift, "Great Heidelberg Song Manuscript") is a codex compiled between about 1300 and 1340 in Zürich, Switzerland. It is the single most comprehensive source of Middle High German Minnesang (courtly love poetry).

Contents

The manuscript contains the works of 140 poets, arranged roughly by social rank from emperors and kings down to commoners. It is famous for its 137 full-page miniatures, each depicting a poet — often in an idealised scene drawn from his poetry or biography — together with his coat of arms and helmet crest. Among the poets included are Walther von der Vogelweide, Hartmann von Aue, Wolfram von Eschenbach and the Emperor Henry VI.

History

The codex was probably commissioned by the Manesse family of Zürich, after whom it is named. After a long and wandering history through various collections, it was purchased in 1888 from Paris and returned to Heidelberg University Library, where it is held today as Codex Palatinus Germanicus 848.
