Joseph Trumpeldor

Joseph Trumpeldor
Born: 21 November 1880, Pyatigorsk, Russian Empire
Died: 1 March 1920, Tel Hai, Mandatory Palestine
Known for: Zion Mule Corps, defence of Tel Hai

Joseph Vladimirovich (Volfovich) Trumpeldor (21 November 1880 – 1 March 1920) was an early Zionist activist who helped found the British Army's Zion Mule Corps and the Jewish Legion, and became a Jewish national hero.

Life

Trumpeldor lost his left arm fighting in the Russo-Japanese War and was the most decorated Jewish soldier in Russia. He emigrated to Ottoman Palestine in 1912, worked in communal settlements, and during World War I helped organise Jewish units serving with the British in Gallipoli and Palestine.

Tel Hai

In 1920 Trumpeldor was sent to defend the isolated settlement of Tel Hai in the Upper Galilee. He was mortally wounded in an attack on 1 March 1920. His reputed last words — "Never mind, it is good to die for our country" — made him an enduring symbol in Israel; the town of Kiryat Shmona and the Betar movement's name commemorate him and Tel Hai.
