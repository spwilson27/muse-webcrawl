The Queensland Rugby League (QRL) is the governing body for rugby league football in the Australian state of Queensland. It is a member of the Australian Rugby League Commission and is responsible for the administration of the game from grassroots to elite level across the state.

History

Rugby league broke away from rugby union in Queensland in 1908, and the Queensland body was formed that year as the Queensland Amateur Rugby League. The Brisbane Rugby League premiership ran from 1909 until the late 1990s. In 1980 the QRL-approved State of Origin concept transformed interstate football: on the back of stars such as Wally Lewis and Mal Meninga, Origin became one of Australian sport's biggest events.

Competitions

The QRL administers the Queensland Cup (sponsored as the Hostplus Cup), the state's premier club competition and a key development pathway to the NRL, as well as the Hastings Deering Colts, women's competitions (BMD Premiership), and junior and regional leagues. It fields the Queensland Maroons men's and women's State of Origin teams and Queensland Residents.

Structure

The QRL operates through regional divisions — Northern, Central, South East and South West — and major affiliated clubs and leagues across the state.
