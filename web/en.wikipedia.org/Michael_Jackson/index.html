Michael Joseph Jackson (August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009) was an American singer, songwriter, dancer, and philanthropist. Dubbed the "King of Pop", he is regarded as one of the most significant cultural figures of the 20th century. His contributions to music, dance, and fashion, along with his publicized personal life, made him a global figure in popular culture for more than four decades.

Career

Jackson began his career in 1964 as the lead singer of the Jackson 5, the family group formed with his brothers that scored Motown hits such as "I Want You Back" and "ABC". His solo career took off with Off the Wall (1979), produced by Quincy Jones. His sixth studio album, Thriller (1982), became the best-selling album of all time, with estimated sales of around 70 million copies, and produced hits including "Billie Jean", "Beat It" and the title track. Its groundbreaking music videos helped break racial barriers on MTV and transformed the video into an art form and promotional tool.

Subsequent albums — Bad (1987), the first album to produce five consecutive Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles; Dangerous (1991); and the double album HIStory (1995) — kept him at the centre of global pop. He popularized dance moves including the moonwalk (debuted during his 1983 performance of "Billie Jean" on Motown 25) and the anti-gravity lean.

Personal life and death

Jackson's changing appearance, relationships, and eccentric lifestyle generated considerable controversy, as did allegations of child sexual abuse in 1993 (settled out of court) and in 2003, when he was tried and acquitted on all counts in 2005. He died on June 25, 2009, from acute propofol intoxication while preparing for the This Is It concert residency in London; his personal physician Conrad Murray was convicted of involuntary manslaughter.

Legacy

Jackson is among the best-selling music artists of all time, with estimated sales exceeding 400 million records worldwide. He won 13 Grammy Awards, was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame twice, and holds numerous Guinness World Records, including Most Successful Entertainer of All Time.
