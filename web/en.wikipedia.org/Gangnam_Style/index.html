"Gangnam Style" (Korean: 강남스타일) is a song by South Korean singer Psy, released on July 15, 2012, as the lead single of his sixth studio album Psy 6 (Six Rules), Part 1. The song's title refers to Seoul's Gangnam District, and its lyrics and video satirise the ostentatious lifestyle associated with the area. Its comic music video — featuring Psy's "horse dance" — became a global viral phenomenon.

Reception and records

The music video was the first in YouTube history to reach one billion views (in December 2012) and subsequently two billion views, and for a time it was the most-viewed video on the platform, famously exceeding the view counter's original 32-bit limit. The song topped the charts in more than 30 countries, reached number two on the US Billboard Hot 100, and introduced K-pop to a mass global audience. It inspired countless parodies, flash mobs, and covers, and the phrase entered dictionaries as a term for a fashionable and lavish lifestyle.

Composition and video

"Gangnam Style" is a dance-pop and electro house track built around a pounding beat and the catchphrase "Oppan Gangnam style" ("older brother is Gangnam style"). The video shows Psy performing deadpan antics in settings that mock conspicuous consumption — a stable, a sauna, a yacht, a carousel — interspersed with cameos by Korean entertainers including Hyuna, Yoo Jae-suk, and Noh Hong-chul.

Legacy

The song's success is credited with accelerating the Korean Wave (Hallyu) worldwide and demonstrating the promotional power of online video. Psy later released follow-ups including "Gentleman" and "Hangover."
