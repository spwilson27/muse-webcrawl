Bed sheet

A bed sheet is a rectangular piece of cloth used as bedding, placed between a sleeper and a mattress or blanket. Bedding sets typically include a flat top sheet and a fitted bottom sheet.

Types

The fitted sheet has elasticated corners — and sometimes edges — that grip the mattress and stay in place. The flat sheet (top sheet) lies over the sleeper, under blankets or a duvet; in many northern European traditions a duvet cover takes its place. Sheets are commonly made of cotton, linen, silk, or synthetic fibres such as polyester and microfiber, often woven in percale or sateen weaves.

Thread count

Quality is often marketed by thread count — the number of threads per square inch — though fibre type and weave also matter, and inflated thread counts are a common marketing practice.
