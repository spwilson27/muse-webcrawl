Sébastien Loeb (born 26 February 1974) is a French rally driver widely regarded as the most successful driver in the history of the World Rally Championship (WRC). Competing principally for Citroën, he won the world drivers' championship a record nine consecutive times between 2004 and 2012, amassing 80 rally victories — also a record.

Career

A former gymnast and electrician from Alsace, Loeb turned to rallying in the late 1990s, winning the French championship and the Junior WRC in 2001. He scored his first WRC win in Germany in 2002 and narrowly lost the 2003 title before beginning his nine-year championship streak with Citroën's Xsara, C4 and DS3 WRC cars. In 2010 he won every event of the season's asphalt rounds and set numerous records, including most wins at a single rally — nine at the Rallye Deutschland and Rally Catalunya.

Later ventures

After reducing his WRC programme in 2013, Loeb raced in the World Touring Car Championship with Citroën, set a record time at the Pikes Peak International Hill Climb in 2013, and contested the Dakar Rally for Peugeot and later BRX, finishing on the podium several times. He made occasional WRC returns with Hyundai and M-Sport Ford, winning the 2022 Monte Carlo Rally at age 47 to extend his victory record. He has also competed in the World Rallycross Championship and Extreme E, winning the latter's 2022 title with Cristina Gutiérrez.

Legacy

Loeb's consistency, precision and adaptability across surfaces made him the benchmark of his era; his partnership with Monegasque co-driver Daniel Elena is the most successful in rally history.
