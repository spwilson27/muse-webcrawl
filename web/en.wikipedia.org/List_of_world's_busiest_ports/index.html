List of world's busiest ports

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Port of Shanghai has been the world's busiest container port since 2010.

This is a list of the world's busiest ports, ranked by container traffic measured in twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU), and by cargo tonnage. Asian ports — particularly those of China — dominate modern rankings, reflecting the concentration of global manufacturing and trade in East and Southeast Asia.

Busiest ports by cargo tonnage

By total cargo tonnage, the Port of Ningbo-Zhoushan in China is the world's largest, handling over 1.2 billion tonnes annually, followed by Shanghai, Singapore, and Tangshan. Rotterdam remains Europe's largest port by tonnage, and Port Hedland in Australia is the world's largest bulk export port, shipping iron ore from the Pilbara.

History

Shanghai overtook Singapore as the busiest container port in 2010. Earlier leaders included Hong Kong (busiest for much of the 1990s and early 2000s), Rotterdam, and New York. The rise of Chinese ports mirrors China's emergence as the world's largest trading nation after joining the WTO in 2001.
