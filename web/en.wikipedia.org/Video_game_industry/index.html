Video game industry

The video game industry is the industry involved in the development, marketing and sale of video games. It encompasses dozens of job disciplines and thousands of jobs worldwide. Global revenues exceeded US$180 billion in the early 2020s, surpassing the film and North American sports industries combined.

Structure

The industry includes hardware manufacturers (console makers such as Nintendo, Sony and Microsoft), software developers, publishers, distributors, retailers and digital storefronts. Development is typically organised into first-party, second-party and independent studios.

History

Commercial video games emerged in the 1970s with arcade titles such as Pong. The industry suffered a crash in 1983 in North America before recovering with the Nintendo Entertainment System. The 1990s and 2000s saw 3D graphics, online gaming, mobile games and digital distribution transform the market.
