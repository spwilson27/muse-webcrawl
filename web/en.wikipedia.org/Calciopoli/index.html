Calciopoli

Calciopoli was a sports scandal in Italy's top professional football league, Serie A, and to a lesser extent Serie B, involving attempts by various clubs to influence the appointment of referees. It was uncovered in May 2006 by Italian police investigating the GEA World players' agency.

The intercepts

Wiretaps published in the press recorded Juventus general manager Luciano Moggi and others discussing referee appointments with officials of the Italian Football Federation, suggesting a network of influence over match officials.

Verdicts

In July 2006 a sporting tribunal relegated Juventus to Serie B and stripped its 2005 and 2006 league titles (the 2006 title was later assigned to Inter Milan). Fiorentina, Lazio, Milan, and Reggina received points deductions; Milan's was reduced on appeal, allowing Champions League participation.

Aftermath

Moggi received a lifetime ban from football and later criminal convictions in related proceedings, some overturned or time-barred. Juventus returned to Serie A after one season and rebuilt, while the scandal reshaped Italian football governance and referee designation.
