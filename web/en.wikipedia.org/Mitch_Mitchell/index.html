John Graham "Mitch" Mitchell (9 July 1947 – 12 November 2008) was an English drummer and child actor, best known as the drummer of the Jimi Hendrix Experience from 1966 until Hendrix's death in 1970.

Career

Mitchell began in show business as a child actor, appearing in the BBC children's television series Jennings, before concentrating on music. He drummed in Peter Nelson and the Travellers and Georgie Fame and the Blue Flames, and in the Coronets and the Riot Squad. In October 1966 he auditioned for Jimi Hendrix's new band — by his own account winning the seat over Aynsley Dunbar on the toss of a coin — joining bassist Noel Redding in the Experience.

Style and legacy

Influenced by jazz drummers such as Elvin Jones and Tony Williams, Mitchell developed a busy fusion style built on fast fills, cymbal work, and interplay with Hendrix's guitar, heard to notable effect on "Manic Depression", "Fire", and "Third Stone from the Sun". He continued with Hendrix through the Band of Gypsys and Cry of Love periods, later playing with the Gypsy Sun and Rainbows, Jack Bruce, and others. He was found dead of natural causes in a Portland hotel room while on the Experience Hendrix tribute tour in 2008, the last surviving member of the original Experience.
