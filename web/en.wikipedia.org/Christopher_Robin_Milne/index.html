Christopher Robin Milne

Born: 21 August 1920, London

Died: 20 April 1996

Known for: Inspiration for Christopher Robin

Christopher Robin Milne (1920–1996) was an English author and bookseller, the son of A. A. Milne. His childhood toys and experiences inspired the character Christopher Robin and the animal companions in Winnie-the-Pooh and The House at Pooh Corner.

Early life

Milne grew up in London and at Cotchford Farm in Sussex. The family's Ashdown Forest surroundings became the Hundred Acre Wood. Fame arrived early and followed him to school, where it was sometimes a burden.

Later life

After service in the Second World War and study at Cambridge, Milne opened a bookshop in Dartmouth with his wife Lesley. He wrote autobiographical books including The Enchanted Places, reflecting on his father, childhood celebrity, and efforts to live on his own terms.
