Personal health record

A personal health record (PHR) is a health record where health data and other information related to the care of a patient is maintained by the patient. This stands in contrast to the electronic medical record, which is operated by an institution such as a hospital or health system and contains data entered by clinicians.

Contents

A PHR may contain a wide range of data, including diagnoses, medications, immunisations, allergies, laboratory results, and notes recorded by the patient such as weight, blood pressure, and symptoms. Records may be paper-based or stored in computer systems and online services.

Benefits and challenges

Advocates argue that PHRs give patients greater control over their own health information, improve continuity of care across providers, and support patient engagement. Challenges include data accuracy, privacy and security, interoperability with provider systems, and variable health literacy among users.
