Matthew Sweet

Sidney Matthew Sweet (born October 6, 1964) is an American alternative rock musician and singer-songwriter. He was part of the alternative rock scene that emerged in Athens, Georgia, and achieved commercial success in the early 1990s.

Career

Sweet moved to Athens, Georgia, in the early 1980s and played in the band Oh-OK and later The Buzz of Delight, as well as collaborating with Michael Stipe. After two commercially unsuccessful solo albums in the late 1980s, he released Girlfriend (1991), featuring guitarists Robert Quine and Richard Lloyd, which became a critical and commercial breakthrough. Follow-ups included Altered Beast (1993) and 100% Fun (1995), the latter containing the hit single "Sick of Myself".

Later work

Sweet recorded three Under the Covers albums of cover songs with Susanna Hoffs of the Bangles. In 2017 he released the double album Tomorrow Forever, funded through crowdfunding, followed by Catspaw (2021).
