Zinedine Yazid Zidane (born 23 June 1972), popularly known as Zizou, is a French professional football manager and former player who played as an attacking midfielder. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, Zidane was a playmaker renowned for his elegance, vision, passing, ball control and technique.

Zidane's career highlights include winning the 1998 FIFA World Cup with France, scoring twice in the final, and receiving the Ballon d'Or that year. With Real Madrid he won the 2001–02 UEFA Champions League, scoring a famous left-foot volley in the final against Bayer Leverkusen. He retired after the 2006 World Cup final, in which he was sent off for headbutting Marco Materazzi.

Managerial career

As manager of Real Madrid from 2016 to 2018 and again from 2019 to 2021, Zidane won three consecutive UEFA Champions League titles (2016, 2017, 2018), two La Liga titles, and two FIFA Club World Cups. He is one of the most successful coaches in the club's history.

Legacy

Zidane was named FIFA World Player of the Year three times (1998, 2000, 2003) and was included by Pelé in the FIFA 100 list of the world's greatest living players in 2004. In France he is regarded as a national hero, his image projected onto the Arc de Triomphe after the 1998 World Cup triumph.
