The Naval Postgraduate School (NPS) is a public graduate school in Monterey, California, operated by the United States Navy. It provides master's and doctoral degrees and conducts research for the Navy, the Department of Defense, and other government agencies. Most students are active-duty officers of the U.S. and allied armed forces.

History

The school traces its origins to 1909, when a small marine engineering programme was established at the U.S. Naval Academy in Annapolis. It moved to Monterey in 1951, occupying the former Hotel Del Monte.

Academics

NPS is organised into graduate schools and institutes covering engineering, operational sciences, business, and international and security studies, and is known for research in areas such as oceanography, space systems, cyber operations and defence analysis.
