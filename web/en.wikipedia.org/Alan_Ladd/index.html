Alan Walbridge Ladd (September 3, 1913 – January 29, 1964) was an American actor best known for his roles in film noir and westerns, most famously as the title character in Shane (1953).

Early life and career

Born in Arkansas and raised in California, Ladd worked various jobs before breaking into film in the late 1930s. His breakthrough came with This Gun for Hire (1942), in which he played the cold-blooded killer Raven opposite Veronica Lake.

Stardom

Ladd became one of Paramount's biggest stars of the 1940s, appearing in film noirs such as The Glass Key and The Blue Dahlia. His performance as the gunfighter Shane in George Stevens's 1953 western is considered his defining role.

Later years and legacy

Ladd founded his own production company, Jaguar Productions. He died in 1964 at the age of 50. His son, Alan Ladd Jr., became a prominent film executive.
