A kung fu film (功夫片) is a subgenre of martial arts cinema centred on realistic hand-to-hand combat and Chinese kung fu, as distinct from the more fantastical, swordplay-driven wuxia tradition. Kung fu films are most closely associated with Hong Kong cinema, from which they conquered world markets in the 1970s.

History

Early martial arts films dramatised folk heroes such as the Cantonese master Wong Fei-hung, played by Kwan Tak-hing in a long-running series beginning in 1949. In the late 1960s the Shaw Brothers studio modernised the genre with bloodier, Mandarin-language productions like Chang Cheh's One-Armed Swordsman (1967) and King Hu's wuxia classics.

The genre exploded internationally with Bruce Lee. After The Big Boss (1971) and Fist of Fury (1972), Lee's Enter the Dragon (1973), a Hong Kong–Hollywood co-production, made kung fu a global phenomenon and triggered a wave of imitators ("Bruceploitation"). Following Lee's death in 1973, the genre reinvented itself through comedy kung fu — Jackie Chan's Drunken Master (1978) — and through the meticulously choreographed work of action directors such as Lau Kar-leung and Yuen Woo-ping.

Later developments

The 1980s and 1990s added gun-fu crossovers, wire-assisted spectacle and stars such as Jet Li (whose Once Upon a Time in China series revived Wong Fei-hung) and Michelle Yeoh. Hong Kong choreography reshaped Hollywood action, notably through Yuen Woo-ping's work on The Matrix (1999) and Ang Lee's wuxia crossover hit Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000). In the 21st century Donnie Yen's Ip Man films (2008–2019) revived the genre for a new generation, while Indonesian and Thai productions carried its influence forward.

Characteristics

Kung fu films emphasise extended, clearly photographed fight scenes performed by genuinely trained martial artists, minimal reliance on editing tricks, and recurring themes: loyalty between master and student, revenge, honour among rival schools, and the moral code of the jianghu underworld. Training sequences and stylistic showcases of particular animal or weapon forms are hallmarks.
