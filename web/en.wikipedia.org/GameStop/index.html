GameStop Corp. is an American video game, consumer electronics, and gaming merchandise retailer headquartered in Grapevine, Texas. The company operates thousands of stores across the United States, Canada, Australia, and Europe under brands including GameStop, EB Games, and Micromania. It is the world's largest video game retailer, known especially for its trade-in program for used games.

History

The company traces its origins to Babbage's, founded in 1984, which passed through mergers involving Software Etc. and FuncoLand before adopting the GameStop name. It acquired EB Games in 2005. As game distribution shifted to digital downloads, GameStop's business declined through the 2010s, with falling sales and store closures.

Short squeeze

In January 2021, GameStop became the center of a historic short squeeze: retail investors, coordinating heavily through the subreddit r/wallstreetbets and encouraged by figures such as Keith Gill ("Roaring Kitty"), drove the heavily shorted stock from under $20 to an intraday high near $483, inflicting heavy losses on hedge funds such as Melvin Capital and triggering controversial trading restrictions by brokers. The episode made GameStop the archetypal "meme stock" and prompted congressional hearings.
