Gigafactory Texas

Gigafactory Texas (also known as Giga Texas or Giga Austin) is an automotive manufacturing facility in Travis County, Texas, near Austin, built and operated by Tesla, Inc. The factory produces the Tesla Model Y and the Tesla Cybertruck, and serves as the site of Tesla's corporate headquarters, which relocated from Palo Alto, California, in December 2021.

Tesla announced the site selection on July 22, 2020, choosing Austin over a competing bid from Tulsa, Oklahoma. Construction began shortly afterward on a site of roughly 2,500 acres (1,000 ha) along the Colorado River, east of Austin–Bergstrom International Airport. The main building is approximately 10 million square feet (930,000 m²) of floor space, making it one of the largest buildings in the world by volume.

History

The factory was formally opened at the "Cyber Rodeo" event on April 7, 2022, when Tesla delivered the first Model Y vehicles built at the plant. These early vehicles were notable for using Tesla's 4680-format battery cells and structural battery pack design. Production of the Cybertruck began in mid-2023, with the first deliveries taking place at an event at the factory on November 30, 2023.

In 2024 and 2025 Tesla expanded construction on the site, including a large extension at the south end of the main building associated with future products such as the Cybercab robotaxi and continued development work related to the company's Optimus humanoid robot program.

Products

  • Tesla Model Y (2022–present)
  • Tesla Cybertruck (2023–present)

The plant also houses cathode production and lithium refining support facilities in the wider region, and employs well over 10,000 people.
