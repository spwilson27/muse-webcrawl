History Museum of Armenia

Established1919
LocationRepublic Square, Yerevan, Armenia
TypeArchaeological, historical, ethnographic museum
Collection sizeApprox. 400,000 objects

The History Museum of Armenia is a national museum in Yerevan, Armenia, located in the Republic Square complex alongside the National Gallery of Armenia. Founded in 1919, it holds a collection of around 400,000 objects spanning from the Palaeolithic era to the present day.

Collections

The museum's holdings include Bronze Age artifacts from the Ararat plain, Urartian inscriptions and bronzes, a rich numismatic collection, medieval manuscripts and khachkars, and ethnographic material. A highlight is the collection of Bronze Age wheeled vehicles and carts recovered from burial mounds at Lchashen near Lake Sevan.

History

The museum was established by the parliament of the First Republic of Armenia. Its building, designed as part of Alexander Tamanian's plan for Republic Square, was completed in the Soviet period. After materials were evacuated during the Second World War and later returned, the museum resumed its role as Armenia's principal repository of national antiquities.
