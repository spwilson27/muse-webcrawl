Chihuahua Pacific Railway

LocaleChihuahua and Sinaloa, Mexico
Length673 km (418 mi)
TerminiChihuahua City – Los Mochis
Completed1961

The Chihuahua Pacific Railway (Spanish: Ferrocarril Chihuahua al Pacífico), commonly known by its reporting mark CH-P or the nickname Chepe, is a major rail line in northwest Mexico, linking Chihuahua City in the state of Chihuahua with the port city of Los Mochis and its port Topolobampo in Sinaloa.

Route

The line crosses the Sierra Madre Occidental, climbing through the Copper Canyon region (Barrancas del Cobre) with 37 bridges and 86 tunnels, reaching an elevation of about 2,400 metres. It is considered one of the most scenic railway journeys in the world.

History and services

Conceived in the 19th century as a link between the American Midwest and the Pacific, the railway was only completed in 1961. Passenger services, including the Chepe Express tourist train, operate alongside freight traffic now run by Ferromex.
