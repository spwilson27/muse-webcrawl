E3

E3 (short for Electronic Entertainment Expo) was an annual trade event for the video game industry, organised by the Entertainment Software Association (ESA). Held primarily at the Los Angeles Convention Center from 1995 to 2019, it was for decades the industry's most prominent showcase, where console makers and publishers announced new hardware and games.

History

The first E3 took place in May 1995 after game companies sought an event separate from the Consumer Electronics Show. Landmark editions included the 1995 debut of the Sega Saturn and PlayStation in North America, the 2004 unveiling of the Nintendo DS and PlayStation Portable, and the 2013 launches of the PlayStation 4 and Xbox One.

Format

E3 combined press conferences by the major platform holders with a show floor of publisher booths. Originally trade-only, it admitted the public in limited numbers from 2017. Attendance peaked at about 70,000 in the mid-2000s.

Decline and cancellation

Major exhibitors such as Sony and Electronic Arts withdrew in the late 2010s in favour of their own events. The 2020 edition was cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic; virtual editions in 2021 drew mixed reactions. In December 2023 the ESA announced E3 was permanently cancelled, with companies increasingly preferring digital showcases such as Summer Game Fest and Nintendo Direct.
