Geoffrey Bawa

Geoffrey Manning Bawa (23 July 1919 – 27 May 2003) was a Sri Lankan architect, widely regarded as one of the most influential Asian architects of his generation and a leading figure in tropical modernism.

Born23 July 1919, Colombo, Ceylon
Died27 May 2003, Colombo, Sri Lanka
Alma materCambridge University; Architectural Association
Notable worksLunuganga, Kandalama Hotel, Sri Lankan Parliament

Life

Born to a wealthy family of mixed descent, Bawa studied law at Cambridge and was called to the Bar, travelled widely, then retrained as an architect in London in his thirties.

Work

His practice blended modernist forms with Sri Lankan vernacular traditions, courtyards, verandas and landscape. Major works include his Colombo house (No. 11, 33rd Lane), the Lunuganga garden estate at Bentota developed over decades, the Heritance Kandalama hotel built into a rocky outcrop, hotels at Bentota and Ahungalla, and the new Parliament complex at Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte.

Legacy

Bawa received the Aga Khan Award Chairman's Award in 2001. The Geoffrey Bawa Foundation preserves his archive and Lunuganga.
