Iberia (airline)

Iberia Líneas Aéreas de España, S.A., operating as Iberia, is the flag carrier of Spain. Founded in 1927, it is based in Madrid and operates an international network of services from its main hub at Madrid–Barajas Airport. Iberia is a member of the Oneworld alliance.

History

The airline was incorporated on 28 June 1927 and began operations that December with flights between Madrid and Barcelona. After the Spanish Civil War it became the state airline of Spain, rebuilding international services through the 1940s and 1950s, including pioneering routes to Latin America. It was privatised between 1999 and 2001. In January 2011 Iberia merged with British Airways to form the International Airlines Group (IAG), while continuing to operate under its own brand. Its regional franchise Iberia Regional is operated by Air Nostrum, and its low-cost subsidiary is Iberia Express.

Destinations and fleet

Iberia serves destinations across Europe, the Americas, Africa and Asia, with a particular emphasis on routes between Europe and Latin America. Its fleet consists largely of Airbus aircraft, including the A320 family for short-haul services and A330 and A350 widebodies for long-haul routes.
