BC Transit

Founded 1979 (as Urban Transit Authority)
Headquarters Victoria, British Columbia
Area served British Columbia outside Metro Vancouver
Service type Public transit

BC Transit is a provincial Crown corporation in British Columbia, Canada, responsible for planning, funding, and managing public transportation systems in most of the province outside the Greater Vancouver region, which is served separately by TransLink.

History

The agency was created in 1979 as the Urban Transit Authority and renamed BC Transit in the early 1980s. It took over bus operations in Victoria and communities across the province, developing a model in which transit systems are delivered in partnership with local governments and private operating companies.

Operations

BC Transit coordinates more than 130 transit systems in over 50 communities, including the Victoria Regional Transit System, its largest, as well as services in Kelowna, Kamloops, Prince George, Nanaimo, and Whistler. Services range from conventional fixed-route buses and double-decker buses in Victoria to community buses, handyDART paratransit for people with disabilities, and the BC Bus North service in northern British Columbia.

Fleet and technology

The fleet numbers over a thousand vehicles, including diesel, compressed natural gas, hybrid, and battery-electric buses, as BC Transit pursues a transition to a low-carbon fleet.
