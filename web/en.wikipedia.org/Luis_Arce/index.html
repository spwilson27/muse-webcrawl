Luis Arce

Luis Alberto Arce Catacora (born 28 September 1963), often referred to as Lucho, is a Bolivian banker and politician who has served as president of Bolivia since 8 November 2020.

Career

Arce worked at the Central Bank of Bolivia and served as Minister of Economy and Public Finance under Evo Morales from 2006 to 2019 (with a brief break), overseeing a period of strong growth, nationalisations and poverty reduction often called the Bolivian "economic miracle". Elected in 2020 as the candidate of the Movement for Socialism (MAS) after the 2019 political crisis, his presidency has faced economic difficulties and a split within his party; an attempted coup took place in June 2024.
