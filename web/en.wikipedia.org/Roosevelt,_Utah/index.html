Roosevelt, Utah

Roosevelt is a city in Duchesne County, Utah, United States, and the largest settlement in the Uinta Basin. The population was about 6,700 at the 2020 census. It serves as a commercial and service centre for the basin's agriculture and oil and gas industry.

History

The area opened to homesteading in 1905 when the Uintah Indian Reservation was allotted. The townsite was laid out in 1906 and named for President Theodore Roosevelt. Irrigation from the Uinta River and later the Strawberry Reservoir project supported farming, while twentieth-century oil development in the Uinta Basin expanded the economy.

Geography

Roosevelt lies at the southern edge of the Uinta Basin, south of the Uinta Mountains, on U.S. Route 40 between Duchesne and Vernal. The climate is semi-arid, with cold winters and hot summers. Nearby attractions include Dinosaur National Monument and the Uinta-Wasatch-Cache National Forest. See also Uintah Basin.
