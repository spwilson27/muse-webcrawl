New Jersey is a state in the Mid-Atlantic and Northeastern regions of the United States, bordered by New York to the north, the Atlantic Ocean and Delaware Bay to the east and south, and Pennsylvania and Delaware to the west. It is the most densely populated state in the country.

History

Indigenous peoples, principally the Lenape, inhabited the region for thousands of years before Dutch and Swedish colonisation in the 17th century, followed by English rule from 1664. New Jersey was a key battleground of the American Revolutionary War—George Washington's victory at Trenton in 1776 is among the war's turning points—and it became the third state to ratify the United States Constitution on December 18, 1787.

Economy and geography

The state's economy is driven by pharmaceuticals, finance, telecommunications, chemicals, food processing and tourism, including the Jersey Shore and Atlantic City. New Jersey is known for its high household incomes, extensive highway and rail networks serving the New York and Philadelphia metropolitan areas, and educational institutions such as Princeton University.
