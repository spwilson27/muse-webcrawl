Theme park

A theme park is an amusement park that bases its structures and attractions around a central theme, divided into distinctly themed areas or "lands". Unlike ordinary amusement parks, theme parks aim at immersive storytelling through architecture, landscaping, costumed staff and rides.

History

Precursors include European pleasure gardens such as Copenhagen's Tivoli Gardens (1843). The modern theme park is generally dated to Disneyland in Anaheim, California, which Walt Disney opened in 1955 with themed lands including Main Street, U.S.A., Adventureland and Frontierland.

Industry

Major operators include Disney Experiences, Universal Destinations & Experiences and Merlin Entertainments. Themed areas range from historical recreations to worlds based on film franchises.
