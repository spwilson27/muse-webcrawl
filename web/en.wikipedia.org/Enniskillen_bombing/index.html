Enniskillen bombing
Date: 8 November 1987
Location: Enniskillen, Northern Ireland
Perpetrator: Provisional IRA
Deaths: 12

The Enniskillen bombing, also known as the Remembrance Day bombing, occurred on 8 November 1987 in Enniskillen, County Fermanagh, during a Remembrance Sunday ceremony honouring British and Commonwealth war dead.

Attack

A Provisional Irish Republican Army bomb exploded beside the town's war memorial as civilians and service personnel gathered. The blast killed 11 people outright, with a twelfth victim dying later after years in a coma, and injured more than 60.

Aftermath

The bombing drew widespread condemnation. Gordon Wilson, whose daughter Marie was killed, became a prominent peace campaigner after publicly forgiving the bombers. The attack is often cited as a turning point that increased support for a political settlement in Northern Ireland.
