The Haas F1 Team, competing as the MoneyGram Haas F1 Team, is an American-licensed Formula One racing team established by NASCAR Cup Series team co-owner Gene Haas in April 2014. The team is headquartered in Kannapolis, North Carolina, alongside sister team Stewart-Haas Racing, with a secondary European base in Banbury, England.

History

Haas was the first American constructor to enter Formula One since 1986 when it debuted at the start of the 2016 season. Its model relied on a close technical partnership with Ferrari, which supplies power units and many permitted components, and with Italian manufacturer Dallara, which built its early chassis. The team scored points on its debut, with Romain Grosjean finishing sixth in Australia, and achieved its best constructors' championship result of fifth place in 2018.

Long-serving team principal Guenther Steiner, a popular figure from the Netflix series Drive to Survive, departed after the 2023 season and was succeeded by Ayao Komatsu in January 2024. Drivers for the team have included Grosjean, Kevin Magnussen, Esteban Gutiérrez, Pietro Fittipaldi, Mick Schumacher, Nikita Mazepin (whose contract and the Uralkali title sponsorship were terminated in March 2022 following the Russian invasion of Ukraine), Nico Hülkenberg, and from 2025 Esteban Ocon and rookie Oliver Bearman.

Racing record

Haas has never won a Grand Prix but has recorded notable results including a fourth-place finish by Grosjean at the 2018 Austrian Grand Prix and a shock pole position for Magnussen in the sprint qualifying at the 2022 São Paulo Grand Prix. The team has generally raced in the midfield and back of the grid, often fighting for the final points positions.
