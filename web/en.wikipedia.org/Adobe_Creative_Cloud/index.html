Adobe Creative Cloud

Adobe Creative Cloud is a subscription service from Adobe Inc. giving access to a collection of software for graphic design, video editing, web development and photography, along with cloud storage and services. It replaced the perpetual-licence Creative Suite in 2013.

Applications

Core applications include Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, InDesign, Lightroom, Audition, Dreamweaver and Acrobat Pro. Plans are offered for individuals, students, teams and enterprises, including single-app and all-apps subscriptions.

Services

Creative Cloud includes Adobe Fonts, stock assets, cloud documents, libraries for sharing assets, and generative AI features under Adobe Firefly.

Reception

The shift to subscription-only licensing drew criticism from some users over long-term cost and dependence on continued payment. See also Adobe Inc. and Adobe Photoshop.
