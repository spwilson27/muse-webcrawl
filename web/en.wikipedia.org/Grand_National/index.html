Grand National

The Grand National is a National Hunt horse race held annually at Aintree Racecourse near Liverpool, England. First run in 1839, it is the most valuable jump race in Europe and one of the world's most famous steeplechases, attracting a huge television audience.

The race is a handicap steeplechase run over about 4 miles 3½ furlongs (6.9 km), with horses jumping 30 large spruce fences, including celebrated obstacles such as Becher's Brook, the Chair and the Canal Turn. Famous winners include Red Rum, who won three times in the 1970s. The race has long prompted debate over horse welfare, leading to reductions in field size and modifications to fences.
