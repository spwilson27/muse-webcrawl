Peter Adkison

Peter D. Adkison is an American game designer and businessman, best known as the founder and first chief executive officer of Wizards of the Coast, the company that published the collectible card game Magic: The Gathering and acquired and revitalised Dungeons & Dragons. He later owned Gen Con, the largest tabletop-game convention in North America.

Wizards of the Coast

Adkison founded Wizards of the Coast in 1990 in Seattle, initially to publish role-playing game supplements. In 1993 the company released Richard Garfield's Magic: The Gathering, whose success transformed the hobby-games industry. Under Adkison, Wizards acquired TSR in 1997 and published the third edition of Dungeons & Dragons in 2000, launching it under the Open Game License. Hasbro bought Wizards in 1999, and Adkison left the company at the end of 2000.

Later career

In 2002 Adkison purchased the Gen Con convention and ran it for over a decade. He has also worked as a game designer, including on the fantasy role-playing game The Primal Order and later projects through his company Hostile Work Environment, and has been involved in film production.
