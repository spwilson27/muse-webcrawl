Master Chief Petty Officer John-117, or simply Master Chief, is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Halo video game series, originally created by Bungie and now managed by Halo Studios (formerly 343 Industries) for Xbox Game Studios. One of the few survivors of the SPARTAN-II supersoldier program, he fights humanity's wars against the alien Covenant and other threats, usually accompanied by the artificial intelligence Cortana.

Character design

The Chief is almost always depicted in his green MJOLNIR powered armor with a gold-visored helmet, and his face is never shown in the games — a deliberate choice to let players project themselves onto the character. Voiced by Steve Downes, he is famously laconic. Abducted as a six-year-old child and conscripted into the SPARTAN-II program, John was physically augmented and trained for war, becoming the unit's unofficial leader and, by reputation, humanity's greatest hero; in the games' lore he is credited with destroying the Halo ring in Halo: Combat Evolved and repeatedly saving Earth and the galaxy.

Appearances and reception

Master Chief stars in Halo: Combat Evolved (2001), Halo 2 (2004), Halo 3 (2007), Halo 4 (2012), Halo 5: Guardians (2015), and Halo Infinite (2021), as well as numerous novels and the Paramount+ television series (2022–2024), where he was played by Pablo Schreiber. As the mascot of the Xbox brand, he is regarded as one of the most iconic characters in video game history, and his story arcs in later games explore themes of humanity, duty, and the ethics of the program that created him.
