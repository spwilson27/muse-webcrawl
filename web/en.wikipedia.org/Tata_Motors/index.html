Tata Motors Limited is an Indian multinational automotive company headquartered in Mumbai and part of the Tata Group. It is India's largest automobile manufacturer, producing passenger cars, trucks, vans, buses, and defence vehicles, and it owns the British luxury marque Jaguar Land Rover.

History

Founded in 1945 as Tata Engineering and Locomotive Company (TELCO) to make locomotives, the company began building commercial vehicles in 1954 in collaboration with Daimler-Benz. It entered the passenger car market with the Tata Sierra and then the Indica (1998), the first fully indigenously developed Indian car. In 2008 Tata Motors acquired Jaguar and Land Rover from Ford for US$2.3 billion, and launched the Tata Nano, promoted as the world's cheapest car.

Products and operations

Popular models include the Nexon, Punch, Harrier, and Safari SUVs, and the company has become a leader in India's electric vehicle market with models such as the Nexon EV and Tiago EV. Tata Motors operates plants across India and in Argentina, South Africa, Thailand, and the United Kingdom, and produces commercial vehicles through subsidiaries including Tata Daewoo in South Korea.
