ChemChina

China National Chemical Corporation, commonly known by the abbreviation ChemChina, is a Chinese state-owned chemical company in product segments including agrochemicals, rubber products, chemical materials and specialty chemicals, petrochemical processing, and industrial equipment. Founded in 2004 and headquartered in Beijing, it grew out of companies affiliated with the former Ministry of Chemical Industry.

Operations

ChemChina expanded internationally through acquisitions, including France's Adisseo, a major animal nutrition firm; the Italian tyre maker Pirelli, in which it took a controlling stake in 2015; and the German machinery maker KraussMaffei in 2016. In 2017 ChemChina completed its acquisition of the Swiss agribusiness Syngenta for about US$43 billion, the largest overseas acquisition by a Chinese company at the time.

Consolidation with Sinochem

In 2021 the Chinese government approved a restructuring bringing ChemChina and Sinochem Group under a new holding company, Sinochem Holdings Corporation Ltd., creating one of the world's largest chemical conglomerates. ChemChina's chairman Ning Gaoning, who also chaired Sinochem, led the combined group.
