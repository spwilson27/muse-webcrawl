Telecommunications in Singapore

Telecommunications in Singapore is highly developed: the city-state has one of the world's highest mobile and broadband penetration rates, nationwide fibre coverage, and serves as a major Asian hub for submarine cables and data centres.

History and liberalisation

Telecom services were long provided by the government-linked Singapore Telecommunications (Singtel), corporatised in 1992 and partly listed on the stock exchange. The market was fully liberalised in 2000, earlier than originally scheduled, bringing in StarHub and M1 as facilities-based competitors; a fourth operator, TPG Telecom (later SIMBA Telecom), entered after a 2016 spectrum auction.

Infrastructure

Singapore's Next Generation Nationwide Broadband Network delivers fibre to nearly all premises, and mobile networks progressed rapidly through 4G to standalone 5G coverage across most of the island from the early 2020s. The sector is regulated by the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA). Singapore's location has made it a landing point for numerous submarine cable systems linking Asia with Europe and Oceania.
