Buddhanusmrti

Buddhānusmṛti (Sanskrit; Pali: buddhānussati) is the Buddhist practice of recollection of the Buddha — meditating on his qualities, virtues, and life as a means of cultivating faith, mindfulness, and concentration. It is the first of the ten recollections (anussati).

In Mahāyāna Buddhism the practice developed into nianfo (Japanese nembutsu), the recitation of the name of Amitābha Buddha, central to Pure Land Buddhism, where it is believed to ensure rebirth in the Pure Land of Sukhāvatī.

Classical texts such as the Visuddhimagga describe the meditator recalling epithets of the Buddha — 'thus gone, worthy, fully enlightened' — until joy, rapture, and one-pointedness arise.
