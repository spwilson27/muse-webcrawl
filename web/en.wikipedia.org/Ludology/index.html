Ludology

FieldGame studies
SubjectGames and play

Ludology is the study of games — including video games, board games and play in general — as systems of rules and play activity. The term derives from the Latin ludus, "game", and gained currency in game studies around the turn of the 21st century.

Scope

Ludologists study games through their formal elements — rules, mechanics, goals, and player action — rather than primarily as narratives. The so-called ludology-versus-narratology debate of the early 2000s, involving scholars such as Gonzalo Frasca, Espen Aarseth and Markku Eskelinen, was a formative discussion about whether games should be understood chiefly as rule systems or as stories; most researchers now treat the two approaches as complementary.
