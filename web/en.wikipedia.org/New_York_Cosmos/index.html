New York Cosmos

The New York Cosmos were an American professional soccer club based in New York City and its suburbs. Founded in 1970, the original team played in the North American Soccer League from 1971 to 1984 and became the league's most famous franchise.

The Pelé era

Backed by Warner Communications, the Cosmos signed world superstars including Pelé, who joined in 1975, Franz Beckenbauer, Carlos Alberto and Giorgio Chinaglia. At their peak they drew crowds of more than 70,000 to Giants Stadium and won five Soccer Bowl championships, but overspending across the league contributed to the NASL's collapse in 1985.

Revival

The club name was revived in 2010; the new Cosmos won several championships in the modern NASL from 2013 before league troubles again left the club's future uncertain.
