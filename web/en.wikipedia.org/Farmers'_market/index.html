Farmers' market

A farmers' market is a physical retail marketplace intended to sell foods directly by farmers to consumers. Farmers' markets may be indoors or outdoors and typically consist of booths or stalls where farmers sell fruits, vegetables, meats, prepared foods, and handmade crafts.

Such markets have ancient roots and remain common worldwide, from wet markets in Asia to markets in Europe and North America. They are promoted as supporting local food systems, small farms, and fresher produce with shorter supply chains. In the United States the number of farmers' markets grew markedly from the 1990s onward.
