Tabletop game

Tabletop game
Types: Board games, card games, dice games, role-playing games, wargames
Played on: A flat surface, typically a table
Examples: Chess, Monopoly, Catan, Dungeons & Dragons

A tabletop game is a game generally played on a table or other flat surface, such as a board game, card game, dice game, miniature wargame or tabletop role-playing game, as distinct from video games and sports played outdoors.

Categories

Board games range from ancient race and strategy games like Senet, Go and chess to modern hobby ("Eurogame") designs such as Catan (1995) and Ticket to Ride. Card games include traditional games like poker and bridge as well as collectible card games such as Magic: The Gathering (1993). Tabletop role-playing games began with Dungeons & Dragons (1974) and emphasise collaborative storytelling guided by a gamemaster.

Industry

The hobby has grown substantially since the 1990s, supported by conventions such as Essen Spiel and Gen Con and by crowdfunding platforms, which fund thousands of new titles each year.
