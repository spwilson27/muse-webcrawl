London

London is the capital and largest city of England and the United Kingdom, with a population of around 8.8 million. Standing on the River Thames, it has been a major settlement for nearly two millennia, founded by the Romans as Londinium.

History

Roman Londinium was founded c. 47 AD. After Roman withdrawal the city revived, endured Viking attacks, Norman conquest, plague and the Great Fire of 1666, and grew into the world's largest city in the 19th century. It endured the Blitz in World War II.

Government

Greater London is administered by the Greater London Authority, headed by the Mayor of London, alongside 32 boroughs and the City of London.

Economy and culture

London is a global financial centre, home to landmarks including the Tower of London, Westminster, Buckingham Palace and the British Museum, and institutions such as the British Library. See also United Kingdom.

Transport

The London Underground, opened 1863, was the world's first underground railway; Heathrow is among Europe's busiest airports.
