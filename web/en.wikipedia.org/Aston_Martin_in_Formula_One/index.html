Aston Martin in Formula One

The British car manufacturer Aston Martin has participated in Formula One in several periods. After a brief and unsuccessful appearance in 1959–1960, the name returned in 2021 when the Racing Point team was rebranded as the Aston Martin F1 Team under the ownership of a consortium led by Lawrence Stroll. The team is based at Silverstone, England.

First incarnation (1959–1960)

Aston Martin entered the DBR4 in 1959 with drivers Roy Salvadori and Carroll Shelby. The car, designed in the front-engined era just as mid-engined cars took over, was uncompetitive; the team's best results were a pair of sixth places. After a single outing with the DBR5 in 1960, Aston Martin withdrew to concentrate on sports car racing.

Return as Aston Martin F1 Team

In January 2020 Lawrence Stroll acquired a stake in Aston Martin Lagonda, and the Racing Point F1 team — itself descended from Jordan Grand Prix — was rebranded Aston Martin for 2021. Sebastian Vettel joined Lance Stroll in the driver line-up, and the team raced in British racing green livery. Vettel retired at the end of 2022 and was replaced by two-time world champion Fernando Alonso.

The 2023 breakthrough

The AMR23 proved a revelation at the start of 2023: Alonso scored six podiums in the first eight races, and Aston Martin finished fifth in the Constructors' Championship with 280 points, a dramatic improvement over previous seasons. A new factory and wind tunnel campus at Silverstone opened during this period.

Future plans

Aston Martin announced a works power-unit partnership with Honda beginning in 2026, and in 2024 secured the services of celebrated designer Adrian Newey, who joined as Managing Technical Partner in 2025.
