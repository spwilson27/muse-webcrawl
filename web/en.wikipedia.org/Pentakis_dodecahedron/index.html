In geometry, a pentakis dodecahedron or kisdodecahedron is a Catalan solid, the dual polyhedron of the truncated icosahedron. It can be constructed by raising a low pentagonal pyramid on each face of a regular dodecahedron (the "kis" operation), dividing each pentagonal face into five isosceles triangles meeting at a raised central point.

Properties

The solid has 60 congruent isosceles-triangle faces, 90 edges, and 32 vertices: 12 of degree 5 (above the original dodecahedron vertices) and 20 of degree 6. Because the truncated icosahedron is the shape of a classic football (soccer ball) and of the buckminsterfullerene molecule, the pentakis dodecahedron corresponds to its geodesic-like subdivision pattern; the layout of hubs in geodesic domes often follows it.
