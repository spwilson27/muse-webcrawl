Yo soy Betty, la fea ("I am Betty, the Ugly One") is a Colombian telenovela created by Fernando Gaitán and broadcast by RCN from 1999 to 2001. It is widely regarded as the most successful telenovela of all time, recognised by Guinness World Records as the most adapted telenovela in television history.

Plot

Beatriz "Betty" Pinzón Solano, a brilliant but plain economist, works as a secretary at the fashion company Ecomoda, where she becomes indispensable to its president, Armando Mendoza. A scheme involving a secret shell company, corporate intrigue, and eventual genuine love drives the story to Betty's celebrated transformation and the couple's reconciliation.

Adaptations and legacy

The format has been remade in dozens of countries, including Mexico's La fea más bella and the American series Ugly Betty. A sequel, Ecomoda, followed in 2001, and an animated spin-off, Betty Toons, was also produced. A continuation series premiered on streaming in 2024.
