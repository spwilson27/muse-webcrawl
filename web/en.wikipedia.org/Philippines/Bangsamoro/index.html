Bangsamoro

Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM)
Country: Philippines
Regional centre: Cotabato City
Established: March 29, 2019
Chief Minister: head of the Bangsamoro Transition Authority (interim)

Bangsamoro, officially the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), is an autonomous region in the southern Philippines, succeeding the former Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM). The name combines the Malay word bangsa (nation) with "Moro," the collective term for the thirteen Muslim ethnolinguistic groups of the southern Philippines.

Creation

The region was created by the Bangsamoro Organic Law (Republic Act No. 11054), signed in 2018 as part of the peace agreement between the Philippine government and the Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF), and ratified in a plebiscite in January and February 2019. The Bangsamoro Transition Authority, led largely by MILF members, serves as the interim government pending regular parliamentary elections. The region comprises the provinces of Basilan, Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao del Norte, Maguindanao del Sur, Sulu and Tawi-Tawi, plus Cotabato City and dozens of barangays in North Cotabato.

Governance and culture

The BARMM has a parliamentary system headed by a Chief Minister and a ceremonial wali. Cotabato City is the seat of government. The region's culture is shaped by Islam, sultanate history, and the foodways of Mindanao's Moro cuisines. Sulu's status was later contested in court, with the Supreme Court ruling in 2024 that Sulu is not part of the autonomous region.
