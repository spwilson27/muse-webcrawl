Cebu

Cebu
Region: Central Visayas, Philippines
Capital: Cebu City
Language: Cebuano (Bisaya)
Known for: Oldest Spanish settlement in the Philippines; lechon; Sinulog festival

Cebu is a province of the Philippines in the Central Visayas region, comprising Cebu Island and more than 160 surrounding islands and islets. Its capital, Cebu City, is the oldest city in the Philippines and the centre of Metro Cebu, the country's second most important metropolitan area after Manila.

History

Ferdinand Magellan's expedition reached Cebu in April 1521; Magellan was killed by the warriors of Lapulapu at the Battle of Mactan. In 1565 Miguel López de Legazpi established the first Spanish settlement here, making Cebu the first capital of the Spanish Philippines before the seat moved to Manila. The Basilica del Santo Niño preserves the image of the Santo Niño given in 1521, and the annual Sinulog festival honours it each January.

Economy and culture

Cebu is a hub for shipping, furniture, tourism and business process outsourcing, served by Mactan–Cebu International Airport. Culturally it is famous across the country for its lechon, widely rated the best in the Philippines, and for dried mangoes, guitars from Lapu-Lapu City, and the whitetop beaches of Bantayan and Camotes. The Cebuano language is spoken by more than twenty million people across the Visayas and Mindanao.
