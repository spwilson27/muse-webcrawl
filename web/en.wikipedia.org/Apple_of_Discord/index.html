Apple of Discord

The Apple of Discord is a golden apple from Greek mythology that sparked a dispute among the goddesses Hera, Athena and Aphrodite and ultimately led to the Trojan War. Uninvited to the wedding of Peleus and Thetis, Eris, goddess of strife and discord, threw into the feast a golden apple inscribed kallistei — "to the fairest one" — which all three goddesses claimed.

The Judgement of Paris

Zeus declined to choose and appointed Paris, prince of Troy, as judge. Each goddess offered him a bribe: Hera offered power, Athena offered victory in war, and Aphrodite offered the love of the most beautiful woman in the world, Helen of Sparta. Paris awarded the apple to Aphrodite, and his subsequent abduction of Helen provoked the Greek expedition against Troy.

Idiom

Because of the story, the phrase "apple of discord" became a common idiom in European languages for a small object or issue that provokes a large dispute.
