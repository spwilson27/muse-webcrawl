Marines

Marines, or naval infantry, are military personnel generally part of a navy who operate on both sea and land. Historically, tasks undertaken by marines have included raiding ashore in support of naval objectives, providing security aboard warships, and manning the ship's guns.

Marine forces specialise in amphibious warfare, projecting power from the sea onto a hostile shore. Well-known examples include the United States Marine Corps, the Royal Marines of the United Kingdom, and the naval infantry forces of many other nations.

The word derives from the French marine, meaning "of the sea".
