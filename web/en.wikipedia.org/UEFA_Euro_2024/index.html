UEFA Euro 2024

The 2024 UEFA European Football Championship, commonly known as UEFA Euro 2024, was the 17th UEFA European Championship, held in Germany from 14 June to 14 July 2024. Spain won the tournament for a record fourth time, defeating England 2–1 in the final at the Olympiastadion in Berlin.

Tournament summary

Germany was chosen as host in September 2018, ahead of a bid from Turkey. Twenty-four teams qualified, including debutants Georgia, who reached the round of 16. Italy, the defending champions, were eliminated by Switzerland in the round of 16. Spain won all seven of their matches, defeating Croatia, Italy, Germany (after extra time in the quarter-finals), France and England.

Final

The final took place on 14 July 2024 at the Olympiastadion, Berlin, before 65,600 spectators. Nico Williams gave Spain the lead early in the second half; Cole Palmer equalised for England, but Mikel Oyarzabal scored the winner in the 86th minute. Spain's Lamine Yamal, who turned 17 the day before the final, became the youngest player to appear in a European Championship final.

Venues

Matches were staged in Berlin, Munich, Dortmund, Stuttgart, Hamburg, Düsseldorf, Cologne, Frankfurt, Leipzig and Gelsenkirchen. The opening match, a 5–1 win for Germany over Scotland, was held at the Munich Football Arena.
