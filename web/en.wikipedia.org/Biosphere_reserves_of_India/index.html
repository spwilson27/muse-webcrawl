Biosphere reserves of India

The Indian government has established 18 biosphere reserves to protect larger areas of natural habitat than typical national parks or animal sanctuaries. Twelve of them are part of UNESCO's World Network of Biosphere Reserves.

List

The first reserve established was the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve in 1986, spanning Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka. Others include the Sundarbans, the Gulf of Mannar, Nanda Devi, Nokrek, Pachmarhi, Simlipal, Achanakmar-Amarkantak, Great Nicobar, Agasthyamala, Khangchendzonga and Panna.

Structure

Each reserve is zoned into a core area, strictly protected; a buffer zone, where limited activity is permitted; and a transition zone, where sustainable human activity is encouraged.
