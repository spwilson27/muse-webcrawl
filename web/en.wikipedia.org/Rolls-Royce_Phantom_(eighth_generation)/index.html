The Phantom VIII, flagship saloon of Rolls-Royce Motor Cars.

The Rolls-Royce Phantom is a full-size luxury saloon manufactured by Rolls-Royce Motor Cars. The eighth generation of the Phantom, flagship of the marque, was unveiled in July 2017 and entered production for the 2018 model year. It replaced the seventh-generation Phantom, which had been built from 2003 to 2016.

Design

The Phantom VIII is built on the marque's aluminium spaceframe platform, the "Architecture of Luxury", later shared with the Cullinan SUV and the second-generation Ghost. Styling by Giles Taylor retained the tall Pantheon grille and long bonnet of its predecessor while introducing laser headlights and a more sculpted profile.

Inside, the dashboard features "The Gallery", a continuous glass panel running the width of the fascia within which owners may commission bespoke artwork. The rear cabin offers reclining seats, a drinks cabinet and a starlight headliner of fibre-optic lights. Rolls-Royce claimed the car was the quietest in the world thanks to more than 130 kg of sound insulation.

Series II

A facelifted Phantom Series II was revealed in May 2022, bringing a polished horizontal line between the daytime running lights, an illuminated grille, and new wheel and interior options, including a fabric rear-seat trim made of bamboo fibres.

Powertrain

The Phantom is powered by a 6.75-litre twin-turbocharged V12 engine (N74B68) producing 563 hp (420 kW) and 900 N·m of torque, driving the rear wheels through an eight-speed ZF automatic transmission with satellite-aided shifting. Self-levelling air suspension and four-wheel steering are standard.
