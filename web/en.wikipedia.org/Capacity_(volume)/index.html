Capacity (volume)

Capacity
The maximum amount a container can hold; measured in litres, gallons, etc.

Capacity, in volume terms, is the quantity of three-dimensional space a container can hold, as distinct from the volume of material the container itself occupies. It is commonly used for liquids and gases and expressed in units such as litres, millilitres, pints, and gallons.

Units

The SI-derived unit of capacity is the litre (L), defined as one cubic decimetre. Traditional units include the imperial and US gallons, quarts, and fluid ounces, which differ between the two systems.

Usage

Engine displacement, fuel tank size, cargo space, and cooking measures are common applications. In science, capacities are usually stated in litres or cubic metres.
