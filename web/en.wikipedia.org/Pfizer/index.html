Pfizer

Pfizer Inc. is an American multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1849, it is one of the world's largest pharmaceutical companies, known for products including Lipitor (atorvastatin), Viagra (sildenafil), and the Pfizer–BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine.

History

Pfizer was founded by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles F. Erhart in Brooklyn in 1849, initially producing the antiparasitic santonin and later citric acid. The company grew through mass production of penicillin during the Second World War and by major acquisitions, including Warner-Lambert (2000), Pharmacia (2003), and Wyeth (2009).

COVID-19 vaccine

In 2020 Pfizer, with German partner BioNTech, developed Comirnaty, one of the first mRNA vaccines authorised against COVID-19, which became the best-selling pharmaceutical product in the world in 2021 and 2022.
