Ensemble learning

Ensemble learning is a machine learning approach that combines multiple models to obtain better predictive performance than any single constituent model.

Methods

Bagging trains models on bootstrap samples, as in random forests. Boosting, including AdaBoost and gradient boosting, sequentially focuses on errors. Stacking learns a meta-model over base predictions, while voting and averaging combine outputs directly.

Rationale

Ensembles reduce variance, bias or both, and are among the most successful methods in practical classification and regression competitions and applications.
