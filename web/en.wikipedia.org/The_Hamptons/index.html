The Hamptons

[Placeholder image: The_Hamptons.png]

The Hamptons are a group of seaside communities and villages on the South Fork of Long Island, New York, within the towns of Southampton and East Hampton. Long known as a summer retreat for affluent New Yorkers, the area includes Southampton, East Hampton, Sag Harbor, Bridgehampton, Amagansett, and Montauk.

Culture

The Hamptons are noted for beaches, historic shingle-style houses, art institutions such as the Parrish Art Museum, and some of the most expensive residential real estate in the United States.
