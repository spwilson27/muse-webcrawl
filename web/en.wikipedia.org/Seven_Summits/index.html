Seven Summits

The Seven Summits are the highest mountains of each of the seven traditional continents. Climbing to the summit of all seven is regarded as a premier mountaineering achievement. The list was first proposed and completed in the 1980s; American businessman and climber Dick Bass became the first person to climb all seven in 1985.

The two lists

Two versions of the list exist. The Bass list counts Mount Kosciuszko (2,228 m) as Australia's highest, whereas the Messner list substitutes the more technically demanding Carstensz Pyramid in New Guinea for the Australia–Oceania region. Reinhold Messner was the first to complete his version, in 1986.

Records

Hundreds of climbers have since completed the Seven Summits. Notable records include Junko Tabei, the first woman to complete the feat (1992), and Jordan Romero, who finished at age 15 in 2011.
