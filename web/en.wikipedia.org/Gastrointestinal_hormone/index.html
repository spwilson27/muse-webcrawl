Gastrointestinal hormone

Image placeholder: the gastrointestinal tract, source of the gut hormones

Gastrointestinal hormones (or gut hormones) are a group of hormones secreted by enteroendocrine cells in the stomach, pancreas, and small intestine that control various functions of the digestive organs and communicate with the central nervous system. They coordinate digestion by regulating gastric acid secretion, enzyme release, bile flow, gut motility, and appetite. Later studies demonstrated that most gastrointestinal hormones also act outside the digestive tract.

Major gastrointestinal hormones

  • Gastrin — secreted by G cells of the stomach antrum; stimulates secretion of hydrochloric acid by parietal cells and aids gastric motility.
  • Secretin — secreted by S cells of the duodenum in response to acid; stimulates the pancreas to release bicarbonate-rich fluid. Secretin was the first hormone ever discovered (Bayliss and Starling, 1902).
  • Cholecystokinin (CCK) — secreted by I cells of the small intestine in response to fats and amino acids; stimulates gallbladder contraction, pancreatic enzyme secretion, and satiety.
  • Gastric inhibitory polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) — incretin hormones that stimulate insulin release after eating; GLP-1 analogues are now widely used to treat type 2 diabetes and obesity.
  • Motilin — secreted during fasting; initiates the migrating motor complex that sweeps the gut between meals.
  • Ghrelin — secreted mainly by the stomach; stimulates hunger, and is often called the "hunger hormone".
  • Somatostatin — secreted by D cells throughout the gut; broadly inhibits the release of other gastrointestinal hormones and acid secretion.

Enteroendocrine cells

The enteroendocrine cells that secrete these hormones are scattered among the mucosal cells of the gut and are collectively considered the largest endocrine organ in the body. They sense nutrients and other luminal contents and release hormones into the blood or act locally on neighbouring cells and nerves.
