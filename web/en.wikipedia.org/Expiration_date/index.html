Expiration date

An expiration date or expiry date is a date after which a product should not be used or sold, because it may have deteriorated in quality or safety. Expiration dates are most commonly associated with food, medicines, cosmetics and chemicals, though the term is also used for documents and contracts such as driver's licenses and credit cards.

Food labelling

Food date labels vary by jurisdiction. A "best before" or "best by" date generally indicates the period during which the manufacturer expects the food to retain its intended quality, whereas a "use by" date is a safety limit after which perishable food should not be consumed. In the United States there is no uniform federal dating system for most foods — infant formula is a notable exception — and dates are largely set by manufacturers. The United Kingdom and the European Union require "use by" dates on highly perishable foods and "best before" dates on most others.

Because date labels are conservative and inconsistently understood, they are widely blamed for contributing to food waste: consumers frequently discard food that is still safe. Campaigns by waste-reduction organisations encourage people to rely on their senses for "best before" products while continuing to respect "use by" dates.

Pharmaceuticals

For medicines, the expiration date is the final day on which the manufacturer guarantees the full potency and safety of the product when stored as directed. Regulators such as the U.S. Food and Drug Administration require stability testing to support the date. Government stockpile studies, such as the Shelf-Life Extension Program, have found that many solid-dose drugs retain potency well beyond their labelled dates under controlled storage, but consumers are advised not to use expired medication.

Other uses

Cosmetics carry period-after-opening symbols, chemicals degrade or become hazardous with age, and sterile medical supplies lose their guaranteed sterility. Outside products, an expiration date terminates the validity of licenses, passports, payment cards, options contracts and promotional offers.
