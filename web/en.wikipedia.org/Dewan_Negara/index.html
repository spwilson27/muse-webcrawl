The Dewan Negara (Jawi: ديوان نڬارا‎; English: Senate, literally "State Council") is the upper house of the bicameral Parliament of Malaysia, the other being the Dewan Rakyat (House of Representatives). Established under the Constitution of Malaysia, the Dewan Negara was modelled on the House of Lords of the United Kingdom and forms part of the federal legislature together with the Yang di-Pertuan Agong (King of Malaysia).

Composition

The Dewan Negara consists of 70 senators. Under Article 45 of the Federal Constitution, 26 members are elected by the State Legislative Assembly of each of Malaysia's 13 states (two per state), while 44 members are appointed by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong on the advice of the Prime Minister. Appointed senators include representatives of the federal territories of Kuala Lumpur (two), Labuan (one) and Putrajaya (one), as well as persons who have rendered distinguished public service or who represent minority communities, including the Orang Asli and the indigenous peoples of Sabah and Sarawak.

Unlike members of the Dewan Rakyat, senators need not be elected, and a senator's term is three years, renewable once. A person may be appointed a senator regardless of whether he or she is a member of a state assembly, provided he or she is a Malaysian citizen of at least 30 years of age.

Powers and role

The Dewan Negara functions mainly as a revising chamber. Most legislation must be passed by both houses before being presented for royal assent, although the Senate's power over money bills is limited: if the Dewan Rakyat passes a money bill and the Senate does not pass it within one month, the bill may be presented to the King directly. For other bills, the Senate may delay but not ultimately block legislation, as the Dewan Rakyat can bypass the Senate after a prescribed period. Constitutional amendments, however, require the support of two-thirds of the total membership of each house, giving the Dewan Negara a real veto over constitutional change.

The President of the Dewan Negara presides over its sittings. Senators are styled Yang Berhormat Senator.
