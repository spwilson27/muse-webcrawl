Mount San Antonio, commonly known as Mount Baldy, is a 3,069-metre peak in the San Gabriel Mountains in Los Angeles and San Bernardino counties, California. It is the highest point in Los Angeles County and a prominent landmark visible across the Los Angeles Basin on clear days.

Geography

The treeless, rocky summit gave rise to the name "Baldy". The mountain rises above San Antonio Creek and the resort community of Mt Baldy Village. The Mount Baldy ski area on its southern slopes, opened in 1952, is one of the oldest ski areas in California.

Recreation

Popular hiking routes include the Ski Hut (Baldy Bowl) trail, the Devil's Backbone trail from the ski lifts, and the long Bear Canyon trail. The exposed summit draws many hikers, and falls and exposure in winter conditions have caused recurring accidents and rescues.
