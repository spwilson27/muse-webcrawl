Bodhipakkhiyādhammā

Bodhipakkhiyādhammā (Pali; Sanskrit bodhipakṣa dharma) are "qualities conducive to enlightenment" — seven sets of virtues comprising 37 items, frequently enumerated in the Pali Canon and later Buddhist literature as the core practices leading to awakening (bodhi).

The seven sets

The 37 qualities comprise: the four foundations of mindfulness (satipaṭṭhāna); the four right efforts (sammappadhāna); the four bases of power (iddhipāda); the five spiritual faculties (indriya); the five powers (bala); the seven factors of enlightenment (bojjhaṅga); and the Noble Eightfold Path (ariya aṭṭhaṅgika magga).

Significance

In the Mahāparinibbāna Sutta the Buddha is said to declare that the teaching will endure so long as these qualities are cultivated. Commentaries such as Buddhaghosa's Visuddhimagga organise meditation practice around them, and they remain a standard summary of the path across Theravāda and Mahāyāna traditions.
