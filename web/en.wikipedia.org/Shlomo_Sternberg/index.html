Shlomo Sternberg

Born: 1936
Field: Mathematics
Institutions: Harvard University
Known for: Differential geometry, Lie groups, symplectic geometry

Shlomo Zvi Sternberg (born 1936) is an American mathematician known for his work in geometry, particularly differential geometry, Lie groups and symplectic geometry, and for influential textbooks. He was a professor of mathematics at Harvard University.

Career

Sternberg received his PhD from Johns Hopkins University in 1957, with a dissertation in the theory of differential equations and dynamical systems. He spent most of his career at Harvard, where he helped shape the teaching of advanced mathematics and mathematical physics.

Work

His research covered normal forms in dynamical systems, the structure of Lie algebras, and the geometry of gauge theory. His books include Lectures on Differential Geometry, Group Theory and Physics, and Curvature in Mathematics and Physics, works widely used by graduate students. He also wrote on Jewish thought and the interface of science and religion.
