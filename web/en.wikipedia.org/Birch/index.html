Birch

A birch is a thin-leaved deciduous hardwood tree of the genus Betula, in the family Betulaceae, which also includes alders, hazels and hornbeams. Birches are widespread in the temperate and boreal climates of the Northern Hemisphere.

Description

Birches are typically small to medium-sized trees or shrubs. Many species are noted for their distinctive bark, which in species such as the paper birch peels off in thin papery strips. The leaves are simple, serrated and borne alternately.

Uses

Birch wood is pale, fine-grained and widely used for plywood, furniture and flooring. Birch sap, bark and leaves have traditional medicinal uses, and birch tar and oil are used in flavouring and leather treatment.
