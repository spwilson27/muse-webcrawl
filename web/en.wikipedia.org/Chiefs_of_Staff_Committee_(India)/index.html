Chiefs of Staff Committee (India)


Chiefs of Staff Committee

The Chiefs of Staff Committee (COSC) is an administrative forum of the senior-most military leaders of the Indian Armed Forces, which advises the Government of India and the Minister of Defence on all military matters. It is headed by the Chief of Defence Staff, who is its permanent chairman; before the creation of that post in 2019, the committee was chaired by the senior-most serving chief of staff on a rotational basis, with the title Chairman, Chiefs of Staff Committee.

Composition

The committee comprises:

  • The Chief of Defence Staff (Chairman)
  • The Chief of the Army Staff
  • The Chief of the Naval Staff
  • The Chief of the Air Staff
  • The Chief of Integrated Defence Staff (member-secretary)

History

The Chiefs of Staff Committee was established on 15 August 1947, modelled on the British Chiefs of Staff Committee, to provide joint military advice to the government of the newly independent Dominion of India. For most of its history, the chairman was the longest-serving service chief, an arrangement criticised as weakening joint planning because the chairmanship was rotational and the committee lacked a permanent secretariat.

Following the Kargil War of 1999, the Kargil Review Committee and a Group of Ministers recommended the creation of a Chief of Defence Staff. The post was finally created in December 2019, with General Bipin Rawat becoming the first CDS and permanent chairman of the COSC on 1 January 2020. After Rawat's death in a helicopter crash in December 2021, the post lay vacant until Lieutenant General (ret.) Anil Chauhan was appointed in September 2022.

Functions

The COSC deals with policy matters concerning joint operations, inter-service coordination, defence planning, and the formulation of military advice to the cabinet. Its work is supported by the Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff.
