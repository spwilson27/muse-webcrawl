List of tallest buildings in New York City

New York City, the most populous city in the United States, is home to more than 7,000 high-rise buildings, and over 100 skyscrapers taller than 600 feet (183 m) — more than any other city in the Western Hemisphere. The tallest is One World Trade Center, completed in 2014 at 1,776 feet (541 m) including its spire, a symbolic height matching the year of American independence. New York's skyline clusters in Midtown and Lower Manhattan, with growing clusters in Downtown Brooklyn and Long Island City.

The supertall "Billionaires' Row" towers south of Central Park — Central Park Tower, 111 West 57th and 432 Park Avenue — are extraordinarily slender residential skyscrapers built on small footprints. Historic landmarks on the list include the Empire State Building (the world's tallest for over four decades), the Art Deco Chrysler Building, the Woolworth Building (1913) and 40 Wall Street. Heights are given to the architectural top; by pinnacle height, several spires rise higher.
