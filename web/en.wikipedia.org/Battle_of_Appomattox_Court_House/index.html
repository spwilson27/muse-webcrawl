Battle of Appomattox Court House

The Battle of Appomattox Court House was fought in Virginia on the morning of April 9, 1865, one of the last battles of the American Civil War. Confederate General Robert E. Lee surrendered his Army of Northern Virginia to Union General Ulysses S. Grant that afternoon.

Background

After the fall of Richmond and Petersburg, Lee's retreat west was cut off by Union cavalry and infantry at Appomattox Court House.

Surrender

Lee and Grant met in the McLean House; terms allowed Confederate soldiers to return home on parole, effectively ending the war, though other armies surrendered over following weeks.

Legacy

Appomattox is remembered as the symbolic end of the Confederacy and the beginning of national reunification.
