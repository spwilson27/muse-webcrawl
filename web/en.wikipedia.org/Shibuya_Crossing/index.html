Shibuya Crossing

Shibuya Crossing, also known as the Shibuya Scramble Crossing (渋谷スクランブル交差点), is a famous pedestrian scramble crossing in front of Shibuya Station in Tokyo, Japan. When the lights turn red for vehicles, pedestrians cross simultaneously in every direction, including diagonally.

Usage and fame

At peak times, as many as 3,000 people cross during a single green phase, and estimates put daily crossings in the hundreds of thousands. Surrounded by giant video screens and neon advertising on buildings such as the QFRONT tower, the crossing has become an emblem of modern Tokyo and appears in films including Lost in Translation and The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift, in news broadcasts, and as a gathering place for Halloween and New Year's Eve crowds.

The crossing lies beside the Hachikō statue plaza and serves several of Japan's busiest rail and subway lines.
