Harajuku

Harajuku is a district in Shibuya, Tokyo, Japan, internationally known as a centre of Japanese youth culture and street fashion. It lies between Shinjuku and Shibuya, around Harajuku Station and Takeshita Street.

Culture

From the 1970s onward, young people gathered near Harajuku on Sundays in elaborate costumes, giving rise to styles such as Lolita, Decora, and Visual Kei. The pedestrianised streets and the magazine FRUiTS helped spread Harajuku fashion worldwide.

Landmarks

Nearby attractions include Meiji Shrine, Yoyogi Park, Omotesandō avenue, and Laforet Harajuku shopping building. Takeshita Street is lined with boutiques, crepe stands, and vintage clothing shops popular with teenagers. See also Shibuya.
