Young Money Entertainment

Young Money Entertainment is an American record label founded by rapper Lil Wayne in 2005. The label is an imprint of Cash Money Records and is distributed by Republic Records. Young Money became one of the most commercially successful labels in hip hop during the late 2000s and 2010s, largely through the careers of Drake and Nicki Minaj, whom Lil Wayne signed and mentored.

History

Lil Wayne founded Young Money Entertainment in 2005 as an offshoot of Cash Money Records, the New Orleans label where he began his career. The label's early roster included artists such as Curren$y, Mack Maine, Gudda Gudda, Tyga, and Jae Millz. Its first major releases included the group album We Are Young Money (2009), which produced the hit single "BedRock" and introduced much of the roster to a mainstream audience.

The signings of Canadian rapper Drake in 2009 and Trinidadian-American rapper Nicki Minaj in 2009 transformed the label. Drake's albums, beginning with Thank Me Later (2010), and Minaj's, beginning with Pink Friday (2010), made Young Money, alongside Cash Money, a dominant force in popular music. Other successful signings included Tyga, whose single "Rack City" was a major hit in 2011.

Legal disputes

From 2015 Lil Wayne was embroiled in a legal dispute with Cash Money Records and its co-founder Birdman over unpaid royalties and the delayed release of his album Tha Carter V. The parties settled in 2018; Lil Wayne received a settlement reported at over $10 million, Tha Carter V was released, and Wayne gained full ownership of Young Money, ending the label's joint-venture arrangement with Cash Money.

Artists

Notable artists signed to Young Money have included Lil Wayne, Drake (until his move to his own OVO Sound arrangements), Nicki Minaj (until 2019), Tyga, Mack Maine, Gudda Gudda, Lil Twist, Shanell, and Christina Milian.
