Venus Williams

Venus Ebony Starr Williams (born 17 June 1980) is an American professional tennis player. A former world No. 1 in both singles and doubles, Williams has won seven Grand Slam singles titles — five at Wimbledon and two at the US Open — and is widely regarded as one of the greatest tennis players of all time.

Career

Williams turned professional in 1994 and first reached world No. 1 in February 2002. Together with her younger sister Serena Williams, she transformed women's tennis with a powerful, athletic style of play. The sisters won 14 Grand Slam doubles titles together and three Olympic doubles gold medals; Venus also won Olympic singles gold at Sydney 2000 and a silver medal in mixed doubles at Rio 2016.

Later career and advocacy

In 2011 Williams revealed she had been diagnosed with Sjögren's syndrome, an autoimmune disease. She has been a prominent advocate for equal prize money for women in tennis, and is also known for her fashion label EleVen and interior design work.
