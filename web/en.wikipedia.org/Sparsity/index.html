Sparsity

FieldMathematics, computer science, statistics
RelatedSparse matrix, sparse approximation, compressed sensing

Sparsity is the property of a structure, such as a vector, matrix, or model, in which most of its entries or parameters are zero or inactive. Exploiting sparsity allows efficient storage and computation and is a central assumption in modern statistics and signal processing.

Sparse matrices

In numerical analysis, a sparse matrix is one with enough zero entries that storing only the nonzeros saves memory and time. Special storage formats such as compressed sparse row support fast arithmetic in scientific computing and graph algorithms.

In modelling

Sparse models are preferred in statistics because they are interpretable and generalise well. Regularisation methods such as the LASSO produce sparse solutions by shrinking most coefficients exactly to zero. In neuroscience and biology, sparse coding describes representations in which only a small fraction of units are active at once.
