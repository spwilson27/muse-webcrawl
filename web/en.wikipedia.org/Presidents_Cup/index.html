Presidents Cup

The Presidents Cup is a biennial series of men's golf matches between a team representing the United States and an International Team representing the rest of the world except Europe (European players compete in the Ryder Cup). It is organized by the PGA Tour and was first held in 1994.

Format

Each team has twelve players led by a non-playing captain. Over four days, matches are played in foursomes, fourball, and singles formats in match play, with points awarded for each match won, modeled on the Ryder Cup format.

History

The event was created to give international players outside Europe a team competition comparable to the Ryder Cup. The United States has dominated the series, while the International Team won in 1998 in Melbourne and tied in 2003 in South Africa. Proceeds support charitable causes in host communities.
