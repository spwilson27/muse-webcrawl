Kenneth Ring (13 December 1935 – June 2025) was an American psychologist and researcher of near-death experiences (NDEs). A professor emeritus of psychology at the University of Connecticut, he was a co-founder of the International Association for Near-Death Studies (IANDS) and editor of the Journal of Near-Death Studies.

Research

His 1980 book Life at Death reported one of the first systematic interview studies of people who had nearly died, and Heading Toward Omega (1984) examined the long-term transformative after-effects he attributed to the experience. Ring also studied NDEs reported by blind people in Mindsight (1999, with Sharon Cooper), a work frequently cited in debates about whether the experience can be purely physiological.
