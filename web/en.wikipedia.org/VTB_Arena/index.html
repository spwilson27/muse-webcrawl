VTB Arena is a multi-purpose sports complex in Petrovsky Park, Moscow, Russia, built on the site of the historic Dynamo Stadium. It combines the Central Dynamo Stadium football ground and an indoor arena under one roof.

History

The original Dynamo Stadium, opened in 1928, was demolished in 2011 to make way for the new complex. After years of redevelopment, the football stadium, named after goalkeeper Lev Yashin, held its first match in May 2019, while the indoor arena hosts ice hockey and basketball.

Facilities

The football stadium seats about 26,000 spectators; the indoor arena holds roughly 12,000 for hockey and more for concerts. The complex also includes retail, offices, and a museum, and is sponsored by VTB Bank, which holds the naming rights.
