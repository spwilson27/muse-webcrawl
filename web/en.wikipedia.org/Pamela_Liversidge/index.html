Dame Pamela Liversidge, DBE, FREng (born 23 May 1946) is a British mechanical engineer and businesswoman. In 1997 she became the first woman president of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE), one of the major British engineering institutions, and she has been a prominent advocate for engineering education and for women in engineering.

Early life and career

Liversidge trained as a mechanical engineer and worked in industry in Yorkshire, including in the electricity supply and engineering sectors, before moving into business roles. She became managing director of Quest Investments, a Sheffield-based investment business, and held a series of public and charitable appointments in South Yorkshire.

Institution of Mechanical Engineers

Elected a Fellow of the IMechE, Liversidge served as the institution's president in 1997–98, the first woman to hold the office in its 150-year history. Her presidency emphasised the professional status of engineers and outreach to schools. She was later elected a Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering.

Public service and honours

Liversidge served as High Sheriff of South Yorkshire for 2007–08 and has been a Deputy Lieutenant of the county. She was appointed a Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire (DBE) and has been involved with universities in the Sheffield region and with organisations promoting science and engineering careers for young people.
