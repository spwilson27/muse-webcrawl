Maestà (Duccio)

ArtistDuccio di Buoninsegna
Year1308–1311
MediumTempera on wood
LocationMuseo dell'Opera, Siena

The Maestà is a large double-sided altarpiece painted by the Sienese artist Duccio di Buoninsegna for Siena Cathedral between 1308 and 1311. It is regarded as one of the masterpieces of early Italian painting and a landmark in the development of the polyptych.

Description

The front panel depicts the Virgin and Child enthroned in majesty (Maestà), surrounded by angels and saints including Siena's patron saints. The reverse carries 26 scenes from the Passion of Christ, while the predella and pinnacles showed episodes from the lives of Christ and the Virgin. An inscription on the throne asks the Virgin to grant peace to Siena and life to Duccio for painting her thus.

History

On its completion in 1311 the panel was carried in solemn procession through Siena to the cathedral. The altarpiece was dismantled in the 18th century; most panels remain in Siena's Museo dell'Opera, with some dispersed to museums abroad.
