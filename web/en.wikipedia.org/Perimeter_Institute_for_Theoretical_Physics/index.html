Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics

Established: 1999
Location: Waterloo, Ontario, Canada
Founder: Mike Lazaridis
Field: Theoretical physics
Type: Independent research institute

The Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics (PI) is an independent research centre in foundational theoretical physics located in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. It was founded in 1999 by Mike Lazaridis, co-founder of Research In Motion (BlackBerry), with an initial donation of $100 million — at the time the largest private donation to scientific research in Canadian history.

Research

Perimeter's research programme covers quantum foundations, quantum information science, quantum gravity, string theory, cosmology, particle physics, condensed matter and complex systems. The institute hosts a large community of resident faculty, postdoctoral fellows and graduate students, as well as long- and short-term visiting researchers from around the world. Its building, opened in 2004 on the shore of Silver Lake in Waterloo, and its 2011 Stephen Hawking Centre expansion, are designed to encourage informal collaboration, with blackboards throughout and a bistro open around the clock.

Training and outreach

Perimeter runs the Perimeter Scholars International master's programme and extensive educational outreach, including the International Summer School for Young Physicists and teacher training. It also operates the Perimeter Institute Recorded Seminar Archive (PIRSA), an open repository of recorded seminars and conferences. The institute is supported by a combination of private donations and funding from the governments of Canada and Ontario.
