The Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (文部科学省, Monbu-kagaku-shō), abbreviated MEXT, is one of the eleven ministries of the Government of Japan. It was created in 2001 by merging the former Ministry of Education with the Science and Technology Agency.

Responsibilities

MEXT oversees Japan's education system at all levels, authorizes school textbooks, administers national universities and the MEXT Scholarship for foreign students, and promotes culture and the arts through the Agency for Cultural Affairs. It is also responsible for science and technology policy, including support for institutions such as JAXA, research funding through competitive grants (kakenhi), and nuclear energy research oversight after the Fukushima disaster.

Organization

External agencies include the Agency for Cultural Affairs, the Japan Sports Agency, and affiliated independent administrative institutions such as the National Institutes for Cultural Heritage and the Japan Student Services Organization.
