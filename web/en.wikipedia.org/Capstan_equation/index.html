Capstan equation

The capstan equation, or belt friction equation, also known as the Euler–Eytelwein formula, describes the relationship between the load force and the hold force when a flexible line is wound around a cylinder such as a capstan, bollard or winch.

Tload = Thold · eμθ

where Tload is the tension applied on the load side, Thold the tension on the holding side, μ the coefficient of friction between the line and the cylinder, and θ the total angle of contact in radians.

Applications

The exponential relationship explains why a sailor can hold a huge load with a small force by taking several turns around a capstan, and why rock climbers' belay devices work. It also applies to belt drives, band brakes and rope rescue systems.
