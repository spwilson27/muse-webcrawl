The New Progressive Party (Spanish: Partido Nuevo Progresista, PNP) is a political party in Puerto Rico that advocates for statehood of Puerto Rico within the United States. Founded in 1967 by dissidents from the Statehood Republican Party, including Luis A. Ferré, the party won power the following year, ending decades of Popular Democratic Party dominance and making Ferré governor (1969–1973).

History

The PNP has alternated in power with the pro-commonwealth Popular Democratic Party (PPD) ever since, supplying governors including Carlos Romero Barceló, Pedro Rosselló, Luis Fortuño, Ricardo Rosselló, Pedro Pierluisi, and Jenniffer González-Colón, who won the 2024 election. The party's members in Washington have generally caucused with Republicans or Democrats according to individual preference; its resident commissioners have included members of both national parties.

Status politics

The PNP campaigns for Puerto Rico to become the 51st U.S. state, arguing that statehood would deliver equal citizenship rights, federal funding parity, and a permanent resolution of the island's territorial status. PNP-backed governments have held several status referendums and plebiscites (notably in 2012, 2017, 2020, and 2024), in which statehood won majorities or pluralities amid disputes over turnout, boycotts, and ballot design. The party also generally favours market-oriented economic policies and close ties with the United States.
