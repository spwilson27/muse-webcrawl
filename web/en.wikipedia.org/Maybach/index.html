Maybach

Maybach is a German car brand that today exists as a sub-brand of Mercedes-Benz. It was founded in 1909 by Wilhelm Maybach and his son Karl Maybach, initially making engines for Zeppelin airships and later luxury automobiles in the 1920s and 1930s.

History

Maybach Motorenbau built engines for tanks and aircraft during the Second World War and ceased car production afterward. Daimler-Benz acquired the brand in 1960 and revived it in 2002 with the Maybach 57 and 62 limousines; poor sales led to its discontinuation as a standalone marque in 2012.

Mercedes-Maybach

Since 2015 the name has been used for ultra-luxury versions of Mercedes-Benz models, beginning with the Mercedes-Maybach S-Class, and later including GLS and EQS SUV variants.
