Project portfolio management

PPM balances a portfolio of projects against strategy and capacity.

Project portfolio management (PPM) is the centralized management of the processes, methods, and technologies used by project managers and project management offices to analyze and collectively manage current or proposed projects based on their characteristics. The objectives of PPM are to determine the optimal mix and sequencing of proposed projects to best achieve the organization's overall goals, subject to constraints of funding, resources, and risk.

Relation to project management

Where project management focuses on delivering a single project well ("doing the project right"), portfolio management concerns selecting which projects to undertake at all ("doing the right projects"). Typical PPM activities include maintaining an inventory of projects, scoring and prioritizing proposals against strategic criteria, balancing risk and resource capacity across the portfolio, and periodically reviewing, re-prioritizing, or terminating underperforming projects.

Methods

Borrowed from financial portfolio theory, PPM uses techniques such as scoring models, bubble diagrams plotting value against risk, and stage-gate reviews. Guidance is provided by standards including PMI's Standard for Portfolio Management and the UK government's Management of Portfolios (MoP).
