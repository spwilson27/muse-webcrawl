Oude IJssel

LengthAbout 82 km
SourceNear Raesfeld, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany
MouthIJssel at Doesburg, Netherlands
German nameIssel

The Oude IJssel (Dutch for "Old IJssel"; in Germany simply the Issel) is a river about 82 kilometres long that rises in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, flows through the Dutch region of the Achterhoek, and joins the IJssel at Doesburg in the province of Gelderland.

Course

From its source near Raesfeld the river flows northwest past Bocholt and Isselburg, crosses the Dutch border near Gendringen, and passes Ulft, Terborg, and Doetinchem before reaching Doesburg. It is navigable for small vessels over its lower course.

History

The name "Old IJssel" reflects the river's history: in Roman times and the early Middle Ages it followed the course of the present IJssel valley more directly, and a channel cut in the early Middle Ages diverted the Rhine's water into what became the Gelderse IJssel, leaving this stream as the older, smaller branch. The river valley later supported an iron industry using local bog ore, remembered in the DRU ironworks at Ulft.
