Gmail

Gmail is an email service developed by Google. Launched on 1 April 2004, it is one of the world's most widely used email services, with more than 1.5 billion users reported by 2018. It is accessible via web browsers and official mobile apps, and also through third-party clients using POP and IMAP protocols.

Gmail
Type: Webmail
Launched: 1 April 2004
Owner: Google (Alphabet)
Storage: 15 GB free (shared with Drive and Photos)

History

Gmail was developed by Paul Buchheit and a small team, drawing on Google's search technology. Its launch offer of 1 gigabyte of free storage — hundreds of times more than competing webmail services such as Hotmail and Yahoo! Mail then offered — together with a fast, search-based interface built with AJAX, transformed expectations for webmail. Initially available by invitation only, it opened to the public in February 2007 and left beta status in July 2009. Free storage grew progressively to 15 GB shared across Gmail, Google Drive and Google Photos.

Features

Gmail organises mail with labels rather than folders, powerful full-text search, automatic spam filtering, and conversation view, which groups messages into threads. The 2013 redesign introduced tabbed inboxes (Primary, Social, Promotions) that sort incoming mail automatically. Later additions include "Smart Reply" and "Smart Compose" suggestions, email scheduling and confidential mode, nudges to follow up on messages, and integration with Google Meet, Chat and Workspace. Gmail supports multiple accounts, keyboard shortcuts, and, in the mobile apps, support for third-party email accounts.

Privacy and advertising

At launch, Gmail funded the free service with contextual advertisements matched to the content of messages, a practice that drew privacy criticism and litigation. Google announced in 2017 that it would stop scanning Gmail messages for ad personalisation. Gmail traffic is encrypted in transit, and the service offers two-step verification and security alerts; Google has also campaigned against phishing, warning users about suspicious messages.
