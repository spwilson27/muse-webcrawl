Chris Carter (born 1956) is an American television and film producer, director, and screenwriter, best known as the creator of the science fiction drama series The X-Files, one of the most successful and influential television shows of the 1990s.

Career

Carter worked as a journalist and editor at Surfing magazine before moving into television, writing for Walt Disney Studios productions in the 1980s. In 1992 he created The X-Files, following FBI agents Fox Mulder and Dana Scully as they investigate paranormal cases; the show ran for nine seasons, spawned two feature films, and was revived for two further seasons in 2016 and 2018.

Other work

Through his company Ten Thirteen Productions, Carter also created the series Millennium, Harsh Realm, and The Lone Gunmen. His production company's name derives from his birthday, October 13.
