Jaguar Land Rover

Jaguar Land Rover Automotive PLC (JLR) is a British multinational automobile manufacturer which produces luxury vehicles and SUV. JLR is a wholly owned subsidiary of Tata Motors, part of the Tata Group, and was formed in 2008 from Tata's acquisition of the Jaguar and Land Rover businesses from the Ford Motor Company.

History

Jaguar and Land Rover had previously been brought together under Ford's Premier Automotive Group. Ford purchased Jaguar Cars in 1989 and Land Rover from BMW in 2000. In March 2008 Tata Motors announced it would buy both marques from Ford for about US$2.3 billion; the deal was completed in June 2008. In January 2013 the two businesses were merged as a single company, Jaguar Land Rover Automotive PLC, with its headquarters at Whitley, Coventry, England.

Products

The company sells vehicles under two brands. Jaguar models include the XE, XF, F-Type, E-Pace, F-Pace and the all-electric I-Pace. Land Rover models include the Defender, Discovery, Discovery Sport, Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, Range Rover Evoque and Range Rover Velar.

Operations

JLR has manufacturing plants in the United Kingdom (Solihull, Castle Bromwich, Halewood, and the Engine Manufacturing Centre near Wolverhampton), as well as plants in China, India, Slovakia, Brazil, and Austria (contract manufacturing). In 2021 the company announced its "Reimagine" strategy, under which Jaguar would become an all-electric brand and Land Rover would launch six electric variants.
