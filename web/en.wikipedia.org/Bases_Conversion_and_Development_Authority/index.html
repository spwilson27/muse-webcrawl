Bases Conversion and Development Authority

The Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA) is a government-owned and controlled corporation in the Philippines created under Republic Act No. 7227, signed on 13 March 1992. Its mandate is to convert former United States military bases and Philippine military reservations into productive civilian use and to raise funds for the modernisation of the Armed Forces of the Philippines.

New Clark City in Capas, Tarlac, one of BCDA's flagship developments.

History and projects

The BCDA was established following the Philippine Senate's rejection of a new bases treaty in 1991 and the withdrawal of US forces from Clark Air Base and Subic Bay Naval Base. It oversees special economic zones including the Clark Freeport and Special Economic Zone, the Subic-Clark corridor, the John Hay Special Economic Zone in Baguio, and the Poro Point Freeport Zone in La Union.

In Metro Manila, the BCDA developed former military land at Fort Bonifacio into Bonifacio Global City, a major business district, and McKinley Hill. Its flagship recent project is New Clark City, a planned smart city in Tarlac province adjacent to Clark, which hosted venues for the 2019 Southeast Asian Games. The BCDA also supervises the Subic–Clark–Tarlac Expressway (SCTEX) and participates in the development of Clark International Airport.
