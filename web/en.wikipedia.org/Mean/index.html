Mean

A mean is a measure of central tendency of a set of values. The most common is the arithmetic mean, the sum of the values divided by their number. Other means include the geometric mean, harmonic mean, and the Pythagorean means.

Types

The arithmetic mean of numbers x1…xn is (x1+…+xn)/n. The geometric mean takes the n-th root of their product, useful for growth rates; the harmonic mean is the reciprocal of the mean of reciprocals, suited to rates and speeds. In statistics, the population mean is distinguished from the sample mean.

Properties

The mean is sensitive to outliers, unlike the median. It is central to probability theory, where the expected value generalises the mean to random variables.
