Cadillac Mountain

Cadillac Mountain is a mountain located on Mount Desert Island, within Acadia National Park, in Hancock County, Maine. With an elevation of 1,530 feet (466 m), it is the highest point on the island and the highest summit on the Atlantic coast of the United States north of Rio de Janeiro.

Geography

The mountain is composed of pink granite formed by ancient igneous activity and sculpted by glaciers. Its bare summit offers panoramic views of Bar Harbor, Frenchman Bay, the Porcupine Islands, and the Cranberry Isles.

Sunrise

From early October to early March, Cadillac Mountain is among the first places in the United States to receive the rays of the rising sun, a distinction it shares seasonally with other eastern summits.

History

The mountain was formerly called Green Mountain. A cog railway and later a summit road, built in the early 20th century, made it accessible to visitors. It was renamed in 1918 for Antoine Laumet de La Mothe, sieur de Cadillac, the French explorer who claimed Mount Desert Island.

Access

A paved 3.5-mile summit road climbs from the Park Loop Road. Hiking trails including the North Ridge, South Ridge, Gorge Path, and Cadillac-West Face trails also reach the top.
