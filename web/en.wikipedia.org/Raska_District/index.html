The Raška District (Serbian: Рашки округ, Raški okrug) is one of the administrative districts of Serbia, located in the southwestern part of the country. Its administrative centre is the city of Kraljevo.

Municipalities

The district comprises the cities of Kraljevo and Novi Pazar and the municipalities of Raška, Tutin, and Vrnjačka Banja. It takes its name from the historic region of Raška, the medieval core of the Serbian state, and includes the Kopaonik mountain resort, the spa town of Vrnjačka Banja, and medieval monuments such as the monasteries of Studenica and Sopoćani and the old town of Ras, UNESCO-listed sites.
