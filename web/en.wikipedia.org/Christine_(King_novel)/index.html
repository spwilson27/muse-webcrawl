Christine (King novel)

AuthorStephen King
Published1983
PublisherViking Press
GenreHorror

Christine is a horror novel by American author Stephen King, published in 1983. It tells the story of a 1958 Plymouth Fury possessed by a malevolent supernatural force, and of the obsessive bond between the car and its teenage owner, Arnie Cunningham.

Plot

Arnie Cunningham, a shy, bullied high-school student in the fictional town of Libertyville, Pennsylvania, buys a dilapidated 1958 Plymouth Fury named Christine from the sinister Roland D. LeBay. As Arnie restores the car, his personality darkens: he becomes arrogant, defiant and increasingly detached from his best friend Dennis Guilder and his girlfriend Leigh Cabot. Christine, meanwhile, repairs herself, plays 1950s rock and roll on her radio, and drives herself to murder Arnie's tormentors one by one. Dennis and Leigh eventually confront the car and crush it in a junkyard, though the novel's ending hints that Christine may be rebuilding herself again.

Themes

The novel explores teenage obsession, the corrupting lure of status and freedom embodied by a first car, and the toxic side of nostalgia for the 1950s. The spirit inhabiting Christine is linked to her previous owner, LeBay, whose personality gradually overwrites Arnie's own.

Adaptation

The novel was adapted into a film directed by John Carpenter, also released in 1983, starring Keith Gordon as Arnie. The film has become a cult classic and is credited with boosting the collectability of 1958 Plymouth Fury cars.
