Singham is a 2011 Indian Hindi-language action film directed by Rohit Shetty and produced by Reliance Entertainment. A remake of the 2010 Tamil film Singam, it stars Ajay Devgn as Bajirao Singham, an upright police inspector, opposite Kajal Aggarwal in her Hindi film debut, with Prakash Raj reprising his role as the antagonist Jaikant Shikre.

Plot

Bajirao Singham, an honest inspector in the border village of Shivgad in Maharashtra, dispenses justice through community mediation. When he crosses the powerful Goa-based criminal-turned-politician Jaikant Shikre, he is transferred to Goa and framed through bureaucratic pressure, but ultimately exposes Jaikant's crimes with the support of the public and his colleagues.

Reception and legacy

Singham was a major commercial success, earning over ₹150 crore worldwide and becoming one of the highest-grossing Hindi films of 2011. It launched Rohit Shetty's "Cop Universe": the sequel Singham Returns (2014), followed by crossovers with Simmba (2018) and Sooryavanshi (2021), and the ensemble film Singham Again (2024). Devgn's portrayal of the incorruptible, lion-hearted ("singham" means lion) officer became one of his signature roles.
