Urho Kekkonen

Urho Kaleva Kekkonen (1900-1986) was a Finnish politician who served as President of Finland from 1956 to 1982, the country's longest-serving president. He led Finland through the Cold War with a policy of neutrality toward the Soviet Union.

Early life

Kekkonen was a champion athlete, lawyer and parliamentarian, serving as prime minister several times and as minister of justice and foreign affairs.

Presidency

His foreign policy, continuing the Paasikivi line, balanced Western ties with accommodation of Soviet security concerns — a stance critics called Finlandization. He hosted the 1975 Helsinki CSCE conference.

Legacy

Kekkonen dominated Finnish politics for decades; his era brought stability and prosperity but also concentrated presidential power.
