The Nagarakretagama, also known by its older title Desawarnana, is an Old Javanese eulogy (kakawin poem) to Hayam Wuruk, king of the Majapahit Empire. It was written by the court poet Mpu Prapanca in 1365 and is one of the most important primary sources for the history of medieval Java.

Content

The poem describes the Majapahit capital in remarkable detail — its palaces, temples, markets, and ceremonies — as well as the king's royal progress through East Java in 1359 and the vast sphere of tributary regions claimed by the empire, which the text portrays as extending across much of the Indonesian archipelago. It also gives an account of the Bubat incident of 1357.

Survival and significance

A single manuscript survived at the royal palace of Lombok and was recovered by Dutch forces in 1894; it is now held in Leiden. The text famously contains an early use of the phrase "Bhinneka Tunggal Ika" ("Unity in Diversity"), later adopted as Indonesia's national motto. UNESCO inscribed the manuscript in the Memory of the World Register.
