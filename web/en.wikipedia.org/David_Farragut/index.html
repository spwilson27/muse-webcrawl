David Farragut

David Glasgow Farragut (5 July 1801 – 14 August 1870) was a flag officer of the United States Navy during the American Civil War. He was the first rear admiral, vice admiral and admiral in the history of the US Navy, ranks created in large part to honour him.

Civil War

Farragut captured New Orleans in April 1862 after running his fleet past the forts below the city, opened the Mississippi River campaigns, and in August 1864 won the Battle of Mobile Bay, where he is remembered for the order popularly rendered "Damn the torpedoes, full speed ahead!" — referring to the naval mines (then called torpedoes) filling the channel. He had gone to sea as a midshipman at age nine and served in the War of 1812.
