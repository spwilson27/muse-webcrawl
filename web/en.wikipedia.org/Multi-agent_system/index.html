Multi-agent system

FieldArtificial intelligence, distributed systems
ComponentsMultiple interacting intelligent agents

A multi-agent system (MAS) is a system composed of multiple interacting intelligent agents that can solve problems difficult or impossible for an individual agent. Agents may cooperate, compete, or both, and the system as a whole can exhibit self-organisation and emergent behaviour.

Characteristics

Agents in a multi-agent system are typically autonomous, partially informed, and decentralised — there is no single point of control. Communication and coordination mechanisms, such as message passing, blackboards, auctions and norms, are central research topics. Applications include robotics (swarm systems), simulation of social and economic behaviour, online trading, and distributed sensor networks.

Frameworks

Development platforms such as JADE, Repast and NetLogo support the construction and simulation of multi-agent systems.
