Justin Trudeau

Justin Pierre James Trudeau
Born: December 25, 1971, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Office: 23rd Prime Minister of Canada (2015–2025)
Party: Liberal Party of Canada
Father: Pierre Elliott Trudeau

Justin Pierre James Trudeau (born December 25, 1971) is a Canadian politician who served as the 23rd prime minister of Canada from 2015 to 2025 and as leader of the Liberal Party from 2013 to 2025. The eldest son of former prime minister Pierre Elliott Trudeau, he is the second child of a prime minister to hold the office himself.

Early life and career

Born in Ottawa while his father was in office, Trudeau was educated at Collège Jean-de-Brébeuf in Montreal and studied literature at McGill University and education at the University of British Columbia. He worked as a teacher in Vancouver and gained national attention delivering the eulogy at his father's state funeral in 2000. He was elected to Parliament for the Montreal riding of Papineau in 2008.

Premiership

Trudeau led the Liberals from third place to a majority government in the 2015 federal election. His government's major initiatives included the Canada Child Benefit, a national carbon-pricing framework, the legalization of recreational cannabis in 2018, medical assistance in dying legislation, and an expanded immigration policy. His government managed Canada's response to the COVID-19 pandemic and renegotiated NAFTA into the USMCA. His tenure was also marked by ethics controversies, including the SNC-Lavalin affair and the WE Charity scandal, and the Liberals were reduced to minorities in 2019 and 2021.

Resignation

Amid declining poll numbers and internal party pressure, Trudeau announced his intention to resign as Liberal leader and prime minister on January 6, 2025. He was succeeded by Mark Carney in March 2025.
