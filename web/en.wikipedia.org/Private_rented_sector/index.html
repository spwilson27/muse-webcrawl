Private rented sector

The private rented sector (PRS) is a classification of housing tenure in which properties are owned by private landlords and let to tenants, as distinct from owner-occupied housing and social housing provided by councils or housing associations. The term is used particularly in the United Kingdom, where the size and regulation of the sector are major topics of housing policy.

History in England

In the early twentieth century most households in Britain rented privately, but the sector shrank for decades as owner occupation and council housing grew, and rent controls reduced returns for landlords. By the late 1980s private renting accounted for under ten percent of households in England. The Housing Act 1988 introduced the assured shorthold tenancy and deregulated rents on new lettings, and from the mid-1990s the availability of buy-to-let mortgages encouraged small investors to become landlords. The sector roughly doubled, housing around a fifth of English households by the 2010s — some 4.5–4.7 million households.

Characteristics

The sector is dominated by small landlords owning one or a few properties, alongside a growing "build to rent" segment of purpose-built blocks owned by institutional investors. Private renters tend to be younger and more mobile than other tenures, and on average spend a higher share of income on housing costs. The sector houses many students, young professionals and lower-income households, including many claiming housing benefit or the housing element of Universal Credit.

Regulation and debate

Tenancies in England are governed mainly by the Housing Acts 1988 and 2004, deposit protection schemes, and safety requirements for gas, electricity and energy efficiency. Debates focus on security of tenure (including proposals to abolish "no-fault" section 21 evictions), rent levels, property conditions and the balance of rights between landlords and tenants. Scotland reformed its private tenancy regime in 2017 with open-ended private residential tenancies and rent pressure zones.
