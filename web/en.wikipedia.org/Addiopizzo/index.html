Addiopizzo

Addiopizzo (Italian for "goodbye, pizzo") is a grassroots movement established in Palermo, Sicily, in 2004 to resist the pizzo, the protection money extorted by the Sicilian Mafia from businesses. Its slogan is "An entire people who pays the pizzo is a people without dignity."

History

The movement began when a group of young people, planning to open a bar, began thinking about the pizzo they would be expected to pay. On the night of 29 June 2004 they covered Palermo with stickers reading "An entire people who pays the pizzo is a people without dignity." The campaign encouraged consumers to support businesses that refused to pay.

Activities

Addiopizzo maintains a list of pizzo-free businesses, promotes ethical consumerism and tourism, and supports shopkeepers who report extortion. Hundreds of businesses and thousands of consumers have joined the initiative.
