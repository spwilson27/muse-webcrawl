Google Play Protect

Developer: Google

Initial release: 2017

Platform: Android

Type: Antivirus software

Google Play Protect is a security service and malware protection system developed by Google for devices running the Android operating system. Announced at Google I/O in May 2017, it unified and rebranded existing Android security features, including Verify Apps, under a single visible brand.

Operation

Play Protect scans apps installed on a device, including those installed from outside the Google Play Store, and checks them against known malware signatures and behavioral heuristics. Google reported that it scans over 200 billion apps daily. If a potentially harmful app is found, the service can warn the user, disable the app, or remove it automatically.

The service also includes Safe Browsing protections in Chrome, Find My Device functionality for locating or wiping a lost phone, and warnings about unverified developers. In later updates, Play Protect added real-time scanning of app code and on-device machine learning analysis for sideloaded apps.

Effectiveness

Independent testing organizations have evaluated Play Protect's detection rates; results have historically been lower than those of some third-party antivirus products, though it serves as the default protection for the majority of Android devices worldwide.
