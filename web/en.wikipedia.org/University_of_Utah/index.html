University of Utah

The University of Utah is a public research university in Salt Lake City, Utah. Founded in 1850, it is the flagship institution of the Utah System of Higher Education.

History

Founded as the University of Deseret, it closed and reopened in the 1860s and adopted its present name in 1892.

Academics

Known as 'the U', it is noted for medicine, genetics, computer graphics, law and business. Pioneers of computer graphics and the ARPANET had Utah ties.

Athletics

The Utes compete in the Big 12 Conference and play football at Rice-Eccles Stadium, a 2002 Winter Olympics venue.
