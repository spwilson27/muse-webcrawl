Chautauqua Lake

Chautauqua Lake is a lake in Chautauqua County in western New York, United States. About 17 miles (27 km) long and up to 2 miles wide, it drains south via the Chadakoin River and the Allegheny River system.

The lake is a centre for boating, fishing and summer tourism, and gave its name to the Chautauqua Institution on its northwestern shore, founded in 1874 as an adult education and cultural movement.
