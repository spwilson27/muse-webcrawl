Russell Ira Crowe (born 7 April 1964) is a New Zealand-born Australian actor. He achieved international fame for his role as the Roman general Maximus Decimus Meridius in Ridley Scott's historical epic Gladiator (2000), for which he won the Academy Award for Best Actor. He received consecutive Oscar nominations for The Insider (1999) and A Beautiful Mind (2001), the latter winning him a BAFTA and a Golden Globe.

Russell Crowe became a leading Hollywood actor in the late 1990s.

Early life and career

Born in Wellington to film caterers Jocelyn and John Crowe, he moved to Australia as a child and appeared on Australian television as a child actor. His breakthrough came with Romper Stomper (1992), playing a violent skinhead, and The Sum of Us (1994). Hollywood success followed with L.A. Confidential (1997) and The Insider (1999), in which he played tobacco whistleblower Jeffrey Wigand.

Later work

Major roles include Captain Jack Aubrey in Master and Commander: The Far Side of the World (2003), boxer James J. Braddock in Cinderella Man (2005), Robin Hood in Robin Hood (2010), Javert in Les Misérables (2012), Jor-El in Man of Steel (2013), and Noah in Noah (2014). He made his directorial debut with The Water Diviner (2014). Outside acting he has fronted rock bands including 30 Odd Foot of Grunts and co-owned the South Sydney Rabbitohs rugby league team.
