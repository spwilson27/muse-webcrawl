National Fire Protection Association

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) is a United States-based international nonprofit organisation devoted to eliminating death, injury, property and economic loss due to fire, electrical and related hazards. Founded in 1896, it is best known for developing and publishing consensus codes and standards used worldwide.

Codes and standards

The NFPA publishes more than 300 codes and standards, including NFPA 1 (Fire Code), NFPA 70 (the National Electrical Code), NFPA 101 (Life Safety Code) and NFPA 921 (guide for fire and explosion investigations). These documents are developed through an open, consensus-based process involving committees of volunteers.

Other work

The association conducts research, provides training and certification, and runs public education campaigns. Its mascot, Sparky the Fire Dog, created in 1951, is widely recognised in fire-safety education for children. The NFPA also maintains the "NFPA 704" diamond system for identifying hazardous materials.
