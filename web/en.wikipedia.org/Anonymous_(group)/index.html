Anonymous (group)

Formationc. 2003–2004
TypeDecentralised hacker collective / movement
PurposeHacktivism, online protest
SymbolGuy Fawkes mask; "headless suit" emblem

Anonymous is a decentralised international activist and hacktivist collective and movement, widely known for cyberattacks against governments, corporations and other institutions. Originating around 2003–2004 on imageboards such as 4chan, where users posted under the default name "Anonymous", the collective has no formal leadership or membership structure; anyone may act under its banner.

Identity and imagery

Participants adopted the Guy Fawkes mask popularised by the film V for Vendetta as a visual symbol, along with slogans such as "We are Legion. We do not forgive. We do not forget. Expect us." Its emblem depicts a suited figure with a question mark in place of a head over a globe.

Major campaigns

Anonymous first attracted wide attention with Project Chanology (2008), a protest campaign against the Church of Scientology combining online attacks with worldwide street demonstrations. During 2010–2011, operations in support of WikiLeaks ("Operation Payback") targeted payment companies, while splinter groups such as LulzSec carried out high-profile breaches. Anonymous-branded actions have since accompanied numerous protest movements worldwide, and offshoots and imitators have continued to claim attacks during geopolitical conflicts, including the Russo-Ukrainian War.

Assessment

Commentators describe Anonymous variously as a protest movement, a brand, and a meme; the openness of the name means actions attributed to it can be the work of loosely connected individuals rather than a coordinated organisation. Law-enforcement operations in the early 2010s led to the arrest of several prominent participants.
