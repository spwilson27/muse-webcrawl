The Eclipse Stakes is a Group 1 flat horse race in Great Britain open to horses aged three years or older, run at Sandown Park over a distance of about 1 mile 2 furlongs (2,002 metres) each year in early July. It is Britain's first major mid-season clash between the Classic generation of three-year-olds and older horses at middle distances.

History

The race was established in 1886 and named after Eclipse, the 18th-century champion racehorse and foundational sire. At its inauguration it was the richest race ever run in Britain, with prize money of £10,000 put up to attract the best horses. It quickly became a championship event and has been sponsored for many years by Coral bookmakers, as the Coral-Eclipse.

Notable winners

The roll of honour includes many of the sport's greats: Isinglass, Buchan, Polyphontes, Brigadier Gerard (1972), Wollow, Dancing Brave, Nashwan (1989), Giant's Causeway, Sea the Stars (2009), Golden Horn (2015), Enable (2019) and City of Troy (2024). Lester Piggott is among the leading jockeys with seven wins, and Aidan O'Brien among the leading trainers.

Records

The race record is just under two minutes for the Sandown course. Three-year-olds receive a weight allowance from their elders, making the Eclipse a test of the Classic crop against established stars of the division en route to races such as the International Stakes and the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe.
