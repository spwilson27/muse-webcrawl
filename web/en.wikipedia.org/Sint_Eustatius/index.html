Sint Eustatius

Sint Eustatius, known locally as Statia, is an island in the Caribbean. It is a special municipality (officially a public body) of the Netherlands, part of the Caribbean Netherlands along with Bonaire and Saba. The island lies in the northern Leeward Islands, southeast of the Virgin Islands.

The capital is Oranjestad. The island has an area of 21 square kilometres (8.1 sq mi) and a population of roughly 3,000. Its dominant landmark is the Quill, a dormant stratovolcano whose crater contains a rainforest.

In the 18th century, Sint Eustatius was a major free port and trading centre — the 'Golden Rock' — and in 1776 it became the first foreign territory to formally acknowledge the independence of the United States, an act known as the 'First Salute'.
