Luc Plamondon

Luc Plamondon (born 2 March 1942) is a French-Canadian lyricist and music executive. He is best known for his work with singer Diane Dufresne, and for writing the book and lyrics of the rock operas Starmania and Notre-Dame de Paris.

Career

Plamondon began writing songs in the 1970s for Quebec artists including Diane Dufresne, Robert Charlebois and Ginette Reno. His collaboration with French composer Michel Berger produced Starmania (1978), one of the most successful French-language musicals ever staged. In 1998 he joined composer Riccardo Cocciante to create Notre-Dame de Paris, based on Victor Hugo's novel, which became an international success and launched the hit song "Belle".

Honours

Plamondon has received numerous Félix Awards, was inducted into the Canadian Songwriters Hall of Fame, and holds a star on Canada's Walk of Fame.
