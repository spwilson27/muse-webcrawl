Affordable housing

Affordable housing is housing deemed affordable to households with low or median incomes. A common benchmark is that housing costs should consume no more than about 30 percent of a household's gross income; households spending more are considered cost-burdened.

Policy approaches

Governments promote affordability through public or social housing, rent subsidies and vouchers, inclusionary zoning, tax credits such as the US Low-Income Housing Tax Credit, and by easing land-use rules to increase housing supply.

Debates

Discussion centres on the balance between market supply, subsidies and regulation, and on causes of unaffordability including restrictive zoning, land costs and stagnant incomes. Shortages of affordable housing are linked to homelessness and long commutes.
