Wings of Fire

GenreChildren's / middle-grade fantasy

Wings of Fire is a series of children's fantasy novels written by Tui T. Sutherland and published by Scholastic Press. Set in the dragon-inhabited continents of Pyrrhia and Pantala, the books follow young dragons drawn into wars and prophecies among the dragon tribes. The series has sold millions of copies worldwide and has expanded into graphic novels and companion books.

Structure

The main sequence is organised into arcs of five books, each narrated by a different dragon protagonist. The first arc (2012–2015) follows five dragonets raised to fulfil a prophecy ending the War of SandWing Succession; the second arc centres on students at Jade Mountain Academy; the third takes place largely on Pantala. Standalone companion volumes, the Winglets short stories, and the Legends novels fill in the world's history.

World and tribes

Pyrrhia is home to seven tribes — MudWings, SandWings, SkyWings, SeaWings, IceWings, RainWings, and NightWings — each with distinctive abilities and kingdoms; Pantala is home to the SilkWings, HiveWings, and LeafWings. Themes of friendship, prejudice, and resistance to prophecy recur throughout the series.

Adaptations

Graphic novel adaptations illustrated by Mike Holmes began in 2018. An animated series adaptation was announced for development in the 2020s.
