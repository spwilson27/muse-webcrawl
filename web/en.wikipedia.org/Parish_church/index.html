Parish church
Role: Religious centre of a parish
Traditions: Roman Catholic, Anglican, Lutheran, Orthodox and others

A parish church is a church which serves as the religious centre of a parish, the basic pastoral and administrative unit in many Christian denominations. It is the church attended by the local community, where baptisms, marriages and funerals are held and recorded.

Role

Historically, the parish church was supported by tithes and served a defined geographic area; every resident belonged to a parish. Parish registers of baptisms, marriages and burials, kept for centuries, are a primary source for genealogists and historians. In England there are thousands of medieval parish churches, many of great architectural importance.
