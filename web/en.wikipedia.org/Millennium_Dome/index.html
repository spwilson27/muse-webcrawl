Millennium Dome

LocationGreenwich Peninsula, London, England
Opened1 January 2000
ArchitectRichard Rogers
Current useThe O2 entertainment district

The Millennium Dome is a large dome-shaped building on the Greenwich Peninsula in south-east London, England, built to house the Millennium Experience exhibition celebrating the beginning of the third millennium.

Structure

Designed by architect Richard Rogers, the dome is one of the largest of its type in the world: a fabric canopy 365 metres in diameter supported by twelve 100-metre yellow steel masts, symbolizing the days and months of the year.

Millennium Experience

The exhibition inside, divided into themed "zones" about the body, mind, work, and the environment, ran throughout 2000 and attracted about 6.5 million visitors — fewer than projected, and the project became controversial for its cost and management.

The O2

After years of uncertainty, the dome was redeveloped and reopened in 2007 as The O2, containing a major arena, cinemas, and restaurants, while keeping the original dome structure.
