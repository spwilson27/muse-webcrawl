Aare

The Aare is the longest river that both rises and ends entirely within Switzerland. Its total length is about 288–295 kilometres (179–183 mi), and it drains an area of nearly 17,800 square kilometres, almost half the country. Rising in the Aare Glaciers of the Bernese Alps, it flows through Lakes Brienz and Thun, past the federal city of Bern, and through Lake Biel before joining the Rhine at Koblenz, opposite Waldshut in Germany.

The river makes a distinctive loop around the old town of Bern, and its turquoise waters are a popular swimming location in summer. Tributaries include the Reuss and the Limmat, which join it shortly before its confluence with the Rhine.
