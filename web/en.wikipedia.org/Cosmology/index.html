Cosmology

Cosmology is a branch of physics and metaphysics dealing with the nature of the universe — its origin, structure, evolution, and ultimate fate. Physical cosmology is the scientific study of the universe's origin, its large-scale structures and dynamics, and its eventual destiny, as well as the scientific laws that govern these realities. The prevailing model is the Big Bang theory embodied in the ΛCDM model, in which the universe began in a hot, dense state about 13.8 billion years ago and has been expanding and cooling ever since.

History

Mythological and philosophical cosmologies — Babylonian, Greek, Hindu, and others — long sought to explain the world as a whole. The scientific discipline began with the Copernican revolution and matured when Einstein's general relativity (1915) permitted dynamical models of the universe. Friedmann and Lemaître derived expanding-universe solutions; Hubble's observations of galactic redshifts (1929) confirmed expansion. The Big Bang model prevailed over the rival steady-state theory after the discovery of the cosmic microwave background by Penzias and Wilson in 1964.

The standard model

In ΛCDM, an early period of cosmic inflation smoothed and flattened the universe; nucleosynthesis in the first minutes fixed the abundances of hydrogen, helium, and lithium; and the cosmic microwave background was released at recombination, some 380,000 years after the Big Bang. Gravity then assembled dark matter and gas into galaxies and large-scale structure. Since the late 1990s, observations of distant supernovae have shown the expansion is accelerating, attributed to dark energy (the Λ of the model, the cosmological constant). Precision measurements by the WMAP and Planck satellites have determined the universe's age and composition to percent-level accuracy.

Open questions

The nature of dark matter and dark energy, the physics of inflation, the Hubble tension between early- and late-universe measurements of the expansion rate, and the initial singularity all remain active research problems.
