Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival

The Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival is an annual winter festival that takes place in Harbin, Heilongjiang, China. It is the largest winter festival in the world, featuring massive ice and snow sculptures displayed across the city, most famously at Sun Island and the Ice and Snow World.

History and attractions

The festival originated in Harbin's traditional ice lantern show and garden party, held since 1963, and was formally established in 1985. It typically begins in late December or early January and lasts about a month, weather permitting. Artists carve blocks of ice taken from the frozen Songhua River into enormous illuminated buildings, slides, bridges and sculptures, some reaching the size of full-scale palaces and cathedrals.

Other winter activities in Harbin during the festival include alpine skiing at Yabuli, winter swimming in the Songhua River, ice sailing, speed skating and dog sledding. The festival attracts millions of visitors each year and is one of China's major tourist events.
