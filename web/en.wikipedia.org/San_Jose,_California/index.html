San Jose is a city in the U.S. state of California, in the Santa Clara Valley at the southern end of San Francisco Bay. It is the largest city in Northern California by population and the centre of Silicon Valley, the global hub of the technology industry.

History

San Jose was founded on 29 November 1777 as the Pueblo de San José de Guadalupe, the first civilian town in Spanish California, to supply food to the presidios at San Francisco and Monterey. It briefly served as California's first state capital in 1849–1851. For much of its history the valley around it was intensely agricultural — the "Valley of Heart's Delight" — famous for orchards and canneries, before post-war suburbanisation and the rise of the electronics industry transformed it.

Economy

Major technology companies with headquarters or large campuses in and around San Jose include Adobe, Cisco, PayPal, Zoom and eBay. The city consistently ranks among the wealthiest large cities in the United States, though its housing costs are also among the nation's highest. San Jose International Airport and the region's VTA light rail and Caltrain services connect it to the wider Bay Area.

Culture and sport

Notable sites include the Winchester Mystery House, the Tech Interactive museum, the Rosicrucian Egyptian Museum and the SAP Center, home of the NHL's San Jose Sharks. The MLS club San Jose Earthquakes plays at PayPal Park.
