Fédération Aéronautique Internationale

The Fédération Aéronautique Internationale (FAI; English: World Air Sports Federation) is the world governing body for air sports and for certifying aviation and spaceflight world records. It was founded in Paris on 14 October 1905 and is headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland.

Activities

The FAI defines standards and rules for aeronautical and astronautical records, sanctions international competitions in disciplines including aerobatics, gliding, ballooning, parachuting, aeromodelling, hang gliding and paragliding, and awards diplomas and medals such as the FAI Gold Air Medal. Its definition of the boundary of space, the Kármán line at 100 km altitude, is widely used for record purposes.

Membership

Member organisations are the national aero clubs and air sports associations of more than 100 countries.
