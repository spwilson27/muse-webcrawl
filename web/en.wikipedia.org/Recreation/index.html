Recreation

Recreation is an activity of leisure, undertaken for enjoyment, amusement, or pleasure during free time. Recreational activities are often done for relaxation, physical exercise, social interaction, or personal fulfillment, and they are regarded as an important contributor to health and quality of life.

Forms

Recreation includes indoor and outdoor pursuits: sports and games, hiking, fishing, camping, gardening, reading, music, arts and crafts, travel, and social gatherings. Organized recreation is provided by parks and recreation departments, community centers, and clubs, while much recreation is informal and self-directed.

History and significance

The growth of industrial society, with its distinction between work and leisure, expanded opportunities and institutions for recreation. In the late 19th and 20th centuries, movements for playgrounds, public parks, and adult recreation developed in many countries. Public health research links regular recreational activity with reduced stress and lower risk of chronic disease.
