Warhammer Age of Sigmar is a miniature wargame produced by Games Workshop that simulates battles between armies in a high-fantasy multiverse known as the Mortal Realms. Launched in July 2015, it replaced Warhammer Fantasy Battle (1983–2015) as the company's flagship fantasy game, following an apocalyptic storyline, the End Times, which destroyed the old Warhammer World.

Setting

The game is set in the Mortal Realms, eight interconnected magical realms (including Azyr, the Realm of Heavens; Aqshy, Fire; Ghyran, Life; and Shyish, Death) formed from the winds of magic after the destruction of the World-That-Was. The god-king Sigmar, who survived the old world's end, forged the golden warriors known as the Stormcast Eternals and launched the Realmgate Wars to reclaim the realms from the forces of Chaos. The setting has developed through narrative campaign books and "ages" of lore, including the Soul Wars against Nagash, god of death, and the Era of the Beast.

Gameplay

Players field armies of miniatures organized into four Grand Alliances — Order, Chaos, Death, and Destruction — each containing multiple factions. Compared with its predecessor, Age of Sigmar uses simpler, looser rules: models move individually rather than in ranked blocks, units are described on individual "warscrolls", and games are commonly played with points-based matched play lists in the game's General's Handbook. A second edition in 2018 and third edition in 2021 expanded the core rules, adding features such as endless spells and refined command abilities. Skirmish and narrative offshoots include Warcry (2019).

Reception

The launch was controversial among long-time players: the End Times destroyed decades of lore, and the original rules — just four pages, with some humorous rules mocked by veterans — replaced the rank-and-flank complexity of Warhammer Fantasy Battle. Over time, community organizations, points systems, and subsequent editions rebuilt goodwill, and Age of Sigmar has become commercially successful, with a large and growing model range. A real-time strategy video game, Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Realms of Ruin, was released in 2023, alongside other licensed games and novels from Black Library.
