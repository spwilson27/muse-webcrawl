Summit

A mountain summit — the highest point relative to its surroundings.

A summit is a point on a surface that is higher in elevation than all points immediately adjacent to it. The topographic terms acme, apex, peak and zenith are synonymous.

Definitions

Whether a point counts as a summit depends not only on elevation but on criteria such as prominence (height relative to surrounding terrain) and isolation. A mountain's highest point is its true summit, while subsidiary high points on its flanks may be classed as sub-peaks; mountaineering lists such as the Munros, the Alpine four-thousanders and the eight-thousanders apply minimum prominence rules to decide which summits count. A summit is typically also a watershed divide point in many ranges.

Other senses

The word is also used figuratively and institutionally: a "summit meeting" or simply "summit" is a high-level meeting of heads of state or government, such as G7 or UN climate summits; "summit" can denote the highest level of an organisation or achievement more generally. Reaching the summit of a mountain ("summiting") is the goal of mountaineering ascents, and in several traditions summits are sacred places.
