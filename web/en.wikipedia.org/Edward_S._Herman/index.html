Edward Samuel Herman (7 April 1925 – 11 November 2017) was an American economist, media scholar and social critic. He was Professor Emeritus of Finance at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

Media analysis

With Noam Chomsky, Herman wrote Manufacturing Consent: The Political Economy of the Mass Media (1988), which set out the propaganda model — five filters (ownership, advertising, sourcing, flak and anti-communist ideology) said to shape news content in democratic societies without direct censorship. His other works include The Political Economy of Human Rights (1979, with Chomsky), The Real Terror Network (1982) and The Global Media (1997, with Robert McChesney).

Other work

In finance, Herman wrote on corporate control and governance. A frequent contributor to Z Magazine and other left publications, he remained an active commentator on foreign policy and media into his nineties.
