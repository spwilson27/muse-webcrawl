Naoshima

Naoshima (Japanese: 直島) is an island town in the Seto Inland Sea of Japan, in Kagawa Prefecture. An administrative town covering Naoshima and neighbouring small islands, it is internationally known as a centre for contemporary art, home to the Benesse Art Site Naoshima project.

Art sites

From the late 1980s the Benesse Corporation, under the direction of Soichiro Fukutake and architect Tadao Ando, developed museums and installations across Naoshima and the nearby islands of Teshima and Inujima. Major venues include the Benesse House Museum (1992), the Chichu Art Museum (2004) — built mostly underground and housing works by Monet, James Turrell and Walter De Maria — and the Lee Ufan Museum. Outdoor icons include Yayoi Kusama's polka-dot pumpkins on the shore. The Art House Project has converted vacant houses in Honmura village into artworks. Since 2010 the islands have hosted the Setouchi Triennale art festival.

Industry

The northern part of the island hosts a Mitsubishi Materials copper smelter and refinery, long the island's main industry, alongside fishing and tourism.
