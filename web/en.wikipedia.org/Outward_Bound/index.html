Outward Bound

Founded1941, Aberdyfi, Wales
FoundersKurt Hahn, Lawrence Holt
TypeOutdoor education organisation

Outward Bound is an international network of outdoor education schools that run expeditionary courses designed to build character, resilience, and leadership through challenging experiences in nature. The name comes from the nautical term for a ship leaving safe harbour.

History

The first school was opened in 1941 at Aberdyfi, Wales, by the educator Kurt Hahn and shipping magnate Lawrence Holt, to train young merchant seamen in the hardiness and teamwork needed to survive when their ships were sunk during the Battle of the Atlantic. After the Second World War the programme was adapted for young people generally.

Approach

Courses typically involve multi-day expeditions — sailing, mountaineering, canoeing, or desert travel — in which participants must cooperate, navigate, and solve problems with limited resources, following Hahn's philosophy of "training through the body" and service to others. Schools now operate in more than thirty countries, including the United States, where the first school opened in Colorado in 1962.
