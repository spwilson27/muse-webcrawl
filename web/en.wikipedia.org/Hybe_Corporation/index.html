Hybe Corporation is a South Korean multinational entertainment company founded by Bang Si-hyuk in 2005 as Big Hit Entertainment. It rebranded as Hybe in March 2021, restructuring into labels, solutions and platform divisions, the latter built around the fan platform Weverse.

Growth with BTS

Big Hit's fortunes were transformed by the boy group BTS, which debuted in 2013 and became a global phenomenon, topping the Billboard charts and addressing the United Nations. The company's 2020 initial public offering on the Korea Exchange was one of South Korea's largest, and it acquired Ithaca Holdings in the United States in 2021, adding ties to acts managed by Scooter Braun.

Labels and disputes

Hybe's multi-label system includes Big Hit Music, Pledis Entertainment, Source Music, ADOR, KOZ Entertainment and Belift Lab, home to groups such as Seventeen, Tomorrow X Together, Enhypen, Le Sserafim and NewJeans. In 2024 a public dispute with ADOR's leadership over control of the label drew intense attention in the Korean entertainment industry.
