The PlayStation Store is a digital media store available to users of Sony's PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5 home video game consoles through the PlayStation Network. The store offers downloadable content including full games, add-on content, playable demos, themes, and trailers, and has become one of the largest digital distribution platforms for console games.

History

The store launched alongside the PlayStation 3 on 11 November 2006 in Japan. Early versions were accessed as a web-based page through the console browser; a native application and several redesigns followed, notably a major visual overhaul in 2012. Versions for the PlayStation Portable and PlayStation Vita were later discontinued. In 2021 Sony announced the closure of the PS3, PSP, and Vita storefronts but reversed the PS3 and Vita closures after criticism, while restricting purchases to direct console access and pre-funded wallets.

Features

Users purchase content with credit or debit cards, PayPal, or prepaid PlayStation Store gift cards, with funds held in a PSN wallet. The store runs frequent sales and offers additional discounts and monthly downloadable games to subscribers of PlayStation Plus. Purchases are tied to the user's account and can be re-downloaded, subject to regional store differences, age ratings, and licensing. A web and mobile version of the store allows remote purchases that queue downloads to consoles.

2011 outage

The PlayStation Network outage of April–May 2011, caused by an external intrusion that compromised personal data of roughly 77 million accounts, took the Store offline for several weeks. Sony offered free games under its "Welcome Back" programme when service was restored, and the incident prompted hearings and regulatory scrutiny in several countries.
