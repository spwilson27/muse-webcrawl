Aviemore
Scottish Gaelic: An Aghaidh Mhòr
Country: Scotland
Council area: Highland
Location: Cairngorms National Park, on the River Spey

Aviemore (Scottish Gaelic: An Aghaidh Mhòr) is a town and tourist resort, situated within the Cairngorms National Park in the Highlands of Scotland. It lies on the River Spey and is a centre for hill walking and skiing, serving the Cairngorm ski area. The town is on the Highland Main Line railway and the A9 road, which links Inverness with Perth and the Central Belt.

History

Aviemore grew significantly with the arrival of the railway in the 19th century, when it became an important junction. From the 1960s it developed as one of Scotland's first skiing and outdoor-activity resorts, with the construction of the Aviemore Centre. Although the resort declined for a time, redevelopment in the 2000s revitalised the town.

Recreation

Aviemore is a popular base for exploring the Cairngorms, Britain's largest national park, offering walking, climbing, mountain biking, and skiing at Cairn Gorm. Nearby attractions include the Strathspey Railway, a preserved heritage line, and the ancient Caledonian pinewoods of Rothiemurchus. The town is also known for its annual sled-dog rally, held each winter.
