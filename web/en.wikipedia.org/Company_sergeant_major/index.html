Company sergeant major

A company sergeant major (CSM) is the senior non-commissioned soldier of a company in the British Army and armies modelled on it, including those of Commonwealth nations. The appointment is normally held by a warrant officer class 2 (WO2), who is addressed as "Sir" or "Ma'am" by subordinates and "Sergeant Major" by officers.

Role

The CSM assists the company commander (usually a major), and is responsible for discipline, administration, welfare, training standards, dress and drill within the company, and for advising the commanding officer on matters affecting the soldiers. In combat the CSM typically oversees the company's logistics in the rear echelon, including ammunition resupply and casualty evacuation.

The equivalent appointment at battalion level is the regimental sergeant major (RSM), held by a WO1. In artillery batteries the appointment is battery sergeant major (BSM), and in armoured and engineer squadrons, squadron sergeant major (SSM).
