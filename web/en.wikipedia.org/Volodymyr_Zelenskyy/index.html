Volodymyr Zelenskyy

Volodymyr Oleksandrovych Zelenskyy (born 25 January 1978) is a Ukrainian politician and former comedian and actor who has served as the sixth president of Ukraine since 2019.

Early life and entertainment career

Zelenskyy was born in Kryvyi Rih to a Jewish family; his father was a professor of cybernetics and his mother an engineer. He studied law but went into comedy, founding the Kvartal 95 troupe and production company. He became famous across the Russian-speaking world and starred in romantic comedies and in the television satire Servant of the People (2015–2019), in which he played a schoolteacher who unexpectedly becomes president of Ukraine — a role that foreshadowed his real political career.

Presidency

Running as an anti-corruption outsider for a party named after his TV show, Zelenskyy defeated incumbent Petro Poroshenko in the 2019 presidential election with about 73% of the second-round vote. His programme promised to end the war in Donbas, tackle oligarchic influence and digitise government.

When Russia launched a full-scale invasion in February 2022, Zelenskyy refused evacuation offers from Kyiv — reportedly replying "I need ammunition, not a ride" — and became the international face of Ukrainian resistance, addressing parliaments worldwide by video link and rallying military and financial support. Ukraine has remained under martial law, and elections due in 2024 were postponed in accordance with the constitution. Time named him Person of the Year for 2022.
