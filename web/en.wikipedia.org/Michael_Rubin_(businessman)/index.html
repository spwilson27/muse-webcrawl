Michael Rubin (businessman)

BornJuly 21, 1972, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Known forFounder and CEO of Fanatics, Inc.

Michael G. Rubin (born July 21, 1972) is an American businessman, founder and chief executive officer of Fanatics, Inc., a global digital sports platform and licensed sports merchandise company. He previously built GSI Commerce into a major e-commerce provider, sold to eBay in 2011 for about .4 billion.

Career

Rubin began in business as a teenager selling ski equipment in Pennsylvania. After the eBay sale of GSI Commerce, he retained and combined its sports-merchandise businesses into Fanatics, which grew through exclusive licensing deals with major leagues and expansion into trading cards, sports betting and live events. He has also held ownership stakes in the Philadelphia 76ers and New Jersey Devils.

Philanthropy

Rubin co-founded the REFORM Alliance, which works on probation and parole reform in the United States, and Fanatics has run large-scale charitable initiatives including the ALL IN Challenge during the COVID-19 pandemic.
