World War II, or the Second World War, was a global conflict between two coalitions: the Allies and the Axis powers. Nearly all of the world's countries participated, with many investing all available economic, industrial and scientific capabilities in pursuit of total war. Tanks and aircraft played major roles, and the war saw strategic bombing of cities, the Holocaust, and the only use of nuclear weapons in an armed conflict. It was the deadliest conflict in history, causing an estimated 70 to 85 million deaths.

Background

The war is generally considered to have begun on 1 September 1939 with Nazi Germany's invasion of Poland, after which Britain and France declared war on Germany. Its roots lay in the unresolved grievances of the First World War, the Great Depression, and the rise of fascism and militarism in Germany, Italy and Japan. Many historians also treat Japan's invasion of China (1937, or Manchuria in 1931) as part of the same conflict.

Course of the war

Germany's blitzkrieg rapidly overran Poland, Denmark, Norway, the Low Countries and France in 1939–40. Britain survived the Battle of Britain and the Blitz. In June 1941 Germany invaded the Soviet Union (Operation Barbarossa); in December 1941 Japan attacked Pearl Harbor, bringing the United States into the war. Turning points included the Soviet victory at Stalingrad (1943), the Allied landings in Normandy (D-Day, 6 June 1944), and US island-hopping victories in the Pacific such as Midway and Iwo Jima.

Germany surrendered in May 1945 after the fall of Berlin and Hitler's suicide. Japan surrendered on 2 September 1945, following the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and the Soviet declaration of war.

Aftermath

The war redrew the world's political map: the United States and Soviet Union emerged as superpowers, setting the stage for the Cold War; the United Nations was founded; European colonial empires began to dissolve; and Germany and Korea were divided. Nazi leaders were tried at Nuremberg for war crimes, including the genocide of six million Jews in the Holocaust.
