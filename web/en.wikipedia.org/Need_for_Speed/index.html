Need for Speed

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Need for Speed (NFS) is a racing game franchise published by Electronic Arts and developed by various studios including Criterion Games, Ghost Games, and EA Black Box. The series generally centres around illicit street racing and tasks players with racing various types of cars, sometimes involving police pursuits. It is one of the best-selling video game franchises of all time, having sold over 150 million copies.

History

The series began with The Need for Speed in 1994, developed with Road & Track magazine to simulate real exotic cars. Later landmarks include Need for Speed III: Hot Pursuit (1998), the tuner-culture titles Underground (2003) and Underground 2, Most Wanted (2005), the simulation-leaning Shift, Criterion's Hot Pursuit (2010), and modern entries such as Heat (2019) and Unbound (2022). A feature film adaptation was released in 2014.

Gameplay

Across entries, gameplay blends arcade handling, extensive car customisation, story-driven campaigns, and open-world cities. Police chases, drifting, and night street-racing cultures recur as signature elements. See also Electronic Arts.
