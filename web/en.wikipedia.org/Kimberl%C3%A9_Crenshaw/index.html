Kimberlé Williams Crenshaw (born 1959) is an American civil rights advocate and legal scholar. A professor at the UCLA School of Law and Columbia Law School, she is best known for introducing the concept of intersectionality in her 1989 essay "Demarginalizing the Intersection of Race and Sex", arguing that the experiences of Black women could not be captured by treating race and gender discrimination as separate categories.

Work

Crenshaw was a founding figure of critical race theory and co-edited the foundational volume Critical Race Theory: The Key Writings That Formed the Movement (1995). She co-founded the African American Policy Forum and launched the #SayHerName campaign to raise awareness of Black women victims of police violence. Her later work, including the 1991 essay "Mapping the Margins", extended intersectionality to questions of identity politics and violence against women of colour.
