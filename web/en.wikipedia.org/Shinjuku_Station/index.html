Shinjuku Station

Shinjuku Station (新宿駅, Shinjuku-eki) is a major railway station in Shinjuku and Shibuya wards in Tokyo, Japan. It is recognised by Guinness World Records as the busiest transport hub in the world, used by an average of about 3.5 million passengers per day.

Lines and layout

The station complex is served by JR East lines including the Yamanote, Chūō, Saikyō, and Shōnan-Shinjuku lines, plus the Odakyu Odawara Line, Keio Line and Keio New Line, and three subway lines (Marunouchi, Shinjuku, and Ōedo). Dozens of platforms spread through multiple interconnected station buildings and extensive underground concourses with hundreds of exits, linking department stores, bus terminals (Busta Shinjuku), and the office towers of Nishi-Shinjuku.

History

Opened in 1885 on the line that became the Yamanote, the station grew as private railways built terminals beside it and as Shinjuku developed into one of Tokyo's principal subcentres after the 1923 Great Kantō earthquake shifted commerce westward.
