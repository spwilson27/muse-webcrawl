Art music

Art music (also called serious music or erudite music) is music considered to be of high aesthetic value. It generally implies advanced structural and theoretical considerations and a written musical tradition, and is contrasted with popular music and folk music. In Western contexts the term overlaps substantially with classical music.

Characteristics

Art music is typically composed in advance, notated, and performed from a score, with the work — rather than any single performance — regarded as the enduring object. It is associated with institutions such as conservatories, concert halls, and state or elite patronage, and with ideals of autonomy: music valued for its own sake rather than for dance, worship, or entertainment functions alone.

Criticism of the term

Musicologists have criticised the hierarchy implied by "art music", noting that traditions outside the Western canon — such as Indian classical music, Chinese literati music, or Persian art music — possess their own sophisticated theories and canons, while popular genres can display comparable complexity.
