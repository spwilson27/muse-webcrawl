Charlie Brooker

Charlton "Charlie" Brooker (born 3 March 1971) is an English writer, television presenter, producer and satirist. He is best known as the creator of the science fiction anthology series Black Mirror, which explores the dark side of technology and society.

Career

Brooker began as a cartoonist and video games journalist, contributing to PC Zone magazine and creating the satirical TV listings website TVGoHome. For The Guardian he wrote the "Screen Burn" column of acerbic television criticism. He wrote the comedy series Nathan Barley (2005) with Chris Morris and the horror drama Dead Set (2008), and presented the review programmes Screenwipe, Gameswipe and Newswipe, along with the annual Wipe specials.

Black Mirror

Black Mirror debuted on Channel 4 in 2011 and moved to Netflix in 2016, winning multiple Emmy Awards, including for the episodes "San Junipero" and "USS Callister", and for the interactive film Bandersnatch (2018). Brooker also co-wrote the police procedural parody A Touch of Cloth and the mockumentary Cunk on Earth with Diane Morgan.
