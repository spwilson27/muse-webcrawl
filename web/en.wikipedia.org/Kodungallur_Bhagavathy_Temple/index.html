Kodungallur Bhagavathy Temple

Sree Kurumba Bhagavathy Temple, commonly known as the Kodungallur Bhagavathy Temple, is a Hindu temple at Kodungallur in Thrissur district, Kerala, India. It is dedicated to the goddess Bhadrakali, a form of Mahakali, worshipped here as Kurumba Bhagavathy.

Tradition and festivals

The temple is one of the oldest in Kerala and is associated in tradition with Kannaki, heroine of the Tamil epic Silappathikaram, and with the ancient port of Muziris. It is famous for the Bharani festival, held in the Malayalam month of Meenam, and the Thalappoli festival in Makaram, which draw large numbers of devotees, including oracles known as velichappadu.

Setting

The temple stands in the centre of Kodungallur, with the deity facing north. Rituals preserve distinctive local traditions linked to the region's Shakti worship.
