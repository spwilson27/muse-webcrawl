UICC

The UICC unites cancer organizations in over 170 countries.

The Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) is a membership-based non-governmental organization dedicated to uniting and supporting the cancer community to reduce the global cancer burden, promote greater equity, and ensure cancer control continues to be a priority in the world health and development agenda. Founded in Paris in 1933, the UICC is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, adjacent to the World Health Organization.

Activities

The UICC has more than 1,100 member organizations in over 170 countries and territories, including cancer societies, research institutes, treatment centres, hospitals, ministries of health, and patient groups. It organizes the World Cancer Congress and the World Cancer Leaders' Summit, leads World Cancer Day on 4 February each year, and maintains the widely used TNM Classification of Malignant Tumours for cancer staging, in cooperation with the American Joint Committee on Cancer.
