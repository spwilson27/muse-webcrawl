Minsk II
Signed12 February 2015, Minsk, Belarus
PartiesUkraine, Russia, France, Germany (Normandy Format); OSCE
PurposeCeasefire and settlement of the war in Donbas

Minsk II, formally the Package of Measures for the Implementation of the Minsk Agreements, was an agreement signed on 12 February 2015 in Minsk, Belarus, intended to end the war in the Donbas region of eastern Ukraine. It followed the failed Minsk Protocol (Minsk I) of September 2014.

Background and signing

After fighting escalated around Debaltseve in early 2015, the leaders of Ukraine (Petro Poroshenko), Russia (Vladimir Putin), France (François Hollande), and Germany (Angela Merkel) — the "Normandy Format" — negotiated through the night of 11–12 February. The resulting package was signed by the Trilateral Contact Group (Ukraine, Russia, and the OSCE) with representatives of the separatist "people's republics" of Donetsk and Luhansk, and endorsed by UN Security Council Resolution 2202.

Terms

The 13 points called for an immediate ceasefire from 15 February 2015, withdrawal of heavy weapons to create a security zone, OSCE monitoring, dialogue on local elections in the separatist-held areas, pardons and amnesty, exchange of hostages and prisoners, humanitarian aid, restoration of economic ties, constitutional reform in Ukraine with decentralisation and a special status for parts of Donbas, and ultimately the restoration of Ukrainian control over its border with Russia.

Implementation and failure

The ceasefire reduced but never ended the fighting; Debaltseve fell to separatist forces days after the agreement. The core political provisions were never implemented: Ukraine and Russia disputed the sequencing — Kyiv insisting on border control and security first, Moscow on elections and special status first — and the special-status and amnesty laws remained contested in Ukrainian politics. The agreement froze rather than resolved the conflict until Russia recognised the separatist republics and launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, rendering Minsk II defunct.
