Unit Structures

Unit Structures is a studio album by American jazz pianist Cecil Taylor, recorded in 1966 and released on Blue Note Records. It is widely regarded as one of the landmark recordings of free jazz and of Taylor's career.

Music

The album features Taylor's septet, including Eddie Gale on trumpet, Jimmy Lyons on alto saxophone, Ken McIntyre on reeds, Henry Grimes and Alan Silva on basses, and Andrew Cyrille on drums. Its long pieces — "Steps", "Enter, Evening (Soft Line Structure)", "Unit Structure/As of the Now/Section", and "Tales (8 Whisps)" — combine densely composed ensemble structures with intense collective improvisation, reflecting Taylor's conception of the ensemble as a set of interlocking "unit structures". The album was produced by Alfred Lion, with liner notes reflecting Taylor's poetic, architectural approach to improvisation.

Reception

Unit Structures is frequently listed among the greatest jazz albums of the 1960s and is a cornerstone of the avant-garde jazz canon, notable for bringing Taylor's challenging music to a major label.
