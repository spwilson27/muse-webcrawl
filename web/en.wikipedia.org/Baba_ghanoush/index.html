Baba ghanoush

Baba ghanoush is a Levantine appetizer of mashed cooked eggplant mixed with tahini, olive oil, lemon juice and seasonings. The eggplant is traditionally roasted or grilled over an open flame, giving the dish a characteristic smoky flavour.

Preparation

Whole eggplants are charred until the skin blackens and the flesh softens, then peeled and mashed or blended with tahini, garlic, lemon juice and salt. It is served cold or at room temperature as a dip with pita bread, drizzled with olive oil and often garnished with parsley or pomegranate seeds.

Regional variations

In Lebanon and Syria it is commonly made with tahini; a related dish, mutabbal, is sometimes distinguished by the use of yogurt. Variants across the Middle East and the eastern Mediterranean add tomatoes, peppers or walnuts.
