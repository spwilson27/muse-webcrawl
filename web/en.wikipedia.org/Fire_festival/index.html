Fire festival

Fire festivals are seasonal celebrations involving bonfires, torches or fireworks, found in many cultures and often tied to solstices, equinoxes or agricultural turning points. In the Celtic calendar, Samhain, Imbolc, Beltane and Lughnasa were traditionally marked with fire.

Examples

Well-known fire festivals include Beltane fires in Scotland and Ireland; Up Helly Aa in Shetland, where a Viking galley is burned; Las Fallas in Valencia, where large satirical effigies are set alight; Guy Fawkes Night bonfires in Britain; and the burning of the Zozobra effigy in Santa Fe.

Interpretation

Folklorists have linked fire festivals to purification, protection of crops and livestock, and the symbolic renewal of the sun, though specific historical origins are often uncertain and many modern events are conscious revivals.
