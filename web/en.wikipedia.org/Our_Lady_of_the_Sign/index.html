Our Lady of the Sign

TypeIconographic type (Theotokos)
DepictsVirgin Mary in orans pose, Christ Emmanuel on her breast
Notable exampleIcon of Novgorod (12th century)

Our Lady of the Sign is a type of icon of the Theotokos (Virgin Mary) in Eastern Orthodox tradition. Mary is shown facing the viewer in the orans posture of prayer, with hands raised, and a medallion on her breast containing the image of Christ Emmanuel. The name refers to the prophecy of Isaiah 7:14: "the Lord himself shall give you a sign; behold, a virgin shall conceive…"

Meaning

The icon presents Mary as the temple and vessel of the Incarnation: Christ is depicted within her as the fulfilment of the prophetic sign. The type is related to the ancient image of the Virgin in the apse of early Christian churches.

The Novgorod icon

The most famous example is the twelfth-century icon of Novgorod the Great, credited in tradition with protecting the city during the siege of 1170, when an arrow struck the icon and it wept. Icons of the Sign were widely copied, including the Kursk Root Icon discovered in 1295.
