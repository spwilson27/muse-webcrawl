Lytton, British Columbia

Province: British Columbia
Location: Confluence of the Fraser and Thompson rivers
Nickname: Canada's hot spot

Lytton is a village in British Columbia, Canada, lying at the confluence of the Thompson River and Fraser River on the east side of the Fraser. It sits on the traditional territory of the Nlaka'pamux Nation, whose community there is known as Camchin.

Climate and 2021 fire

Lytton is known for Canada's highest recorded temperatures. On 29 June 2021, during the western North America heat wave, it reached 49.6 °C (121.3 °F), the highest temperature ever recorded in Canada. The next day a wildfire destroyed most of the village, forcing evacuation; rebuilding has proceeded since.
