Oligospermia

Oligospermia
DefinitionLow sperm concentration in semen
ThresholdBelow about 15 million sperm per millilitre (WHO)
FieldAndrology, reproductive medicine

Oligospermia, also called oligozoospermia, is a condition of male infertility characterised by a low concentration of sperm in the semen. Reference values from the World Health Organization place the lower limit of normal sperm concentration at about 15 million sperm per millilitre of ejaculate.

Causes

Causes include varicocele, hormonal disorders of the hypothalamic–pituitary–gonadal axis, genetic conditions such as Klinefelter syndrome and Y-chromosome microdeletions, infections, obstruction of the genital tract, certain medications, exposure to heat or toxins, and lifestyle factors. In many cases no cause is found (idiopathic oligospermia).

Diagnosis and treatment

Diagnosis is by semen analysis, usually repeated, with hormonal and genetic testing as indicated. Treatment addresses the underlying cause where possible; assisted reproductive techniques such as intrauterine insemination, IVF, and intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) can overcome even severe cases, while complete absence of sperm is termed azoospermia.
