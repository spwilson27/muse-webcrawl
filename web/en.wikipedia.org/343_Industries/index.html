343 Industries is an American video game developer located in Redmond, Washington, part of Xbox Game Studios. Founded in 2007, the studio was created by Microsoft to oversee development of the Halo science fiction franchise after the original creator, Bungie, became independent.

History

The studio, named after the Halo character 343 Guilty Spark, initially coordinated multimedia projects and supervised outside developers, including the anniversary remaster Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary (2011). Its first full in-house title was Halo 4 (2012), which began the "Reclaimer Saga." It followed with Halo 5: Guardians (2015), the real-time strategy game Halo Wars 2 (2017) with Creative Assembly, and Halo Infinite (2021), which launched alongside the Xbox Series X/S.

Recent developments

After layoffs and a leadership change in 2022–2023, the studio restructured, and in October 2024 it announced it would rebrand as Halo Studios and move future development from its proprietary Slipspace Engine to Unreal Engine 5, with multiple new Halo projects in development.
