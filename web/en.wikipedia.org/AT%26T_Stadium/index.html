AT&T Stadium

AT&T Stadium
Location: Arlington, Texas, U.S.
Opened: May 27, 2009
Owner: City of Arlington
Capacity: 80,000 (expandable to ~105,000)

AT&T Stadium is a retractable roof stadium in Arlington, Texas, United States. It serves as the home of the Dallas Cowboys of the National Football League (NFL) and was completed on May 27, 2009. Nicknamed "Jerry World" after Cowboys owner Jerry Jones, it is one of the largest domed stadiums in the world.

Features

The stadium is known for its enormous center-hung video board, once the largest high-definition video screen in the world, suspended 90 feet above the field. Its retractable roof and huge end-zone doors can open or close in about 12 minutes.

Events

AT&T Stadium hosted Super Bowl XLV in 2011, the 2010 NBA All-Star Game, college football's Cotton Bowl Classic, the first College Football Playoff National Championship in 2015, WrestleMania events, and numerous major concerts and soccer matches.
