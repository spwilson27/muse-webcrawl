Watch

A watch is a portable timepiece intended to be carried or worn by a person. It is designed to keep a consistent movement despite the motions caused by the person's activities. A wristwatch is worn on the wrist; a pocket watch is carried in a pocket.

History

Portable spring-driven clocks appeared in 15th-century Europe, evolving into early watches in the 16th century. The wristwatch became popular after the First World War. In the 1970s quartz watches, using a vibrating quartz crystal and battery, largely displaced mechanical movements, before smartwatches emerged in the 2010s.

Types

Watches are broadly mechanical (manual or automatic winding), quartz, or digital/smart. Complications are extra functions beyond timekeeping, such as a chronograph, calendar, moon phase or tourbillon.
