Vasyugan Swamp

Location: West Siberian Plain, Russia
Coordinates: approx. 57°N 78°E
Area: about 53,000 km²
Regions: Tomsk, Novosibirsk, Omsk oblasts; Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug

The Vasyugan Swamp (Russian: Васюганские болота, Vasyuganskiye bolota) is one of the largest swamps in the world, situated in the West Siberian Plain of Russia between the Ob and Irtysh rivers. Covering some 53,000 square kilometres — an area larger than Switzerland — it is the largest peatland complex in the northern hemisphere.

Geography and ecology

The swamp formed over roughly the last 10,000 years as bogs spread across the flat interfluve, and it continues to expand. It is the source of numerous rivers, including the Vasyugan, and stores enormous quantities of peat and carbon, making it significant for the global carbon balance and methane emissions. It hosts diverse taiga wetland wildlife, including moose, bear, sable, mink and many bird species. Part of the swamp is protected as the Great Vasyugan Mire nature reserve. Oil and gas fields lie around its margins.
