The Liga Portugal 2, also known as Liga Portugal 2 Meu Super for sponsorship reasons and previously as the Segunda Liga, is the second-highest division of the Portuguese football league system. It is administered by the Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional (LPFP), which also runs the top-flight Primeira Liga.

History

A national second division existed from 1934 within the old Segunda Divisão structure, but the modern professional second tier was created in 1990 as the Segunda Divisão de Honra, with UD União de Leiria among its early champions. The league was renamed Segunda Liga in 1999 and Liga Portugal 2 in 2020 as part of a rebranding of the professional competitions. The number of teams has varied between 16 and 24; it has been fixed at 18 since 2023–24.

Format

Eighteen teams, including several B teams of major clubs (such as Benfica B and Porto B, which are ineligible for promotion), play a double round-robin of 34 matches. The top two teams are promoted to the Primeira Liga, with the third-placed team entering a promotion playoff against the 16th-placed top-flight team. The bottom two are relegated to Liga 3, with the 16th-placed club contesting a relegation playoff. Clubs also enter the Taça de Portugal and Taça da Liga.
