Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions

AbbreviationBDS
Launched2005
TypeNonviolent protest movement

Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions (BDS) is a Palestinian-led movement promoting boycotts, divestment, and economic sanctions against Israel. Launched in 2005 by a coalition of Palestinian civil-society organizations, it models itself in part on the international anti-apartheid movement against South Africa.

Goals

The movement's stated demands are: ending Israel's occupation and colonization of Arab lands and dismantling the West Bank barrier; full equality for Arab-Palestinian citizens of Israel; and recognition of the rights of Palestinian refugees to return to their homes and properties, as asserted under UN General Assembly Resolution 194.

Tactics and response

Campaigns have targeted consumer goods, cultural and academic exchanges, and institutional investments. Supporters describe BDS as a nonviolent human-rights campaign; critics, including the Israeli government and several other states, characterize it as discriminatory and antisemitic, and a number of jurisdictions have adopted anti-BDS legislation. The movement remains a subject of substantial international debate.
