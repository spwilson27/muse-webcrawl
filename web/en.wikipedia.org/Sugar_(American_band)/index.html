Sugar (American band)

Sugar, Bob Mould's power-pop trio of the early 1990s.

Sugar was an American alternative rock band active from 1992 to 1995. Formed in Washington, D.C., it was led by guitarist/vocalist Bob Mould, formerly of Hüsker Dü, with bassist David Barbe (formerly of Mercyland) and drummer Malcolm Travis (formerly of Human Sexual Response).

Career

The band's debut album, Copper Blue (1992), released on Rykodisc in the US and Creation Records in the UK, combined Mould's trademark dense guitars with strong pop melodies; it was named album of the year by the NME and produced the singles "Helpless", "If I Can't Change Your Mind" and "A Good Idea". The darker EP Beaster followed in 1993, drawn from the same sessions' contrasting material. A second studio album, File Under: Easy Listening (1994), included the single "Your Favorite Thing" but sold less well amid friction during recording.

Sugar disbanded in 1995, after which Mould resumed his solo career. Copper Blue has since been widely reissued and celebrated, and is frequently ranked among the best albums of the 1990s.
