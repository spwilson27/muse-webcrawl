Raipur is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Chhattisgarh, in central India. Located on the eastern bank of the Mahanadi River region in the fertile Chhattisgarh plain, it became the state capital when Chhattisgarh was carved out of Madhya Pradesh on 1 November 2000.

History

Raipur's history is traced to the Kalchuri dynasty, whose branch at Ratanpur ruled the region from about the 9th century; a stone inscription and local tradition associate the city's founding with King Brahmdeo Rai. The city later came under Maratha rule in the 18th century and British control in the 19th century, developing as a trading centre for grain and forest produce.

Economy and transport

Raipur is a major commercial and industrial centre for central India, with rice mills, cement plants, and steel and aluminium industries; the nearby Bhilai Steel Plant anchors the regional steel economy. The planned capital city of Nava Raipur (Atal Nagar) is being developed nearby. Raipur is served by Swami Vivekananda Airport and lies on the Mumbai–Howrah railway line.

Landmarks

Sights include the Mahant Ghasidas Memorial Museum (founded 1875), the Swami Vivekananda statue and Marine Drive along the Kharun riverfront, and the ancient temples of nearby Rajim and Sirpur.
