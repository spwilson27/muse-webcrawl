The Pro D2 is the second division of professional rugby union in France, sitting below the Top 14 in the French league system. It is administered by the Ligue Nationale de Rugby (LNR) and features 16 clubs competing in a home-and-away league format followed by play-offs.

Competition format

Each season, the 16 teams play a regular season of 30 rounds. The top two teams at the end of the regular season advance directly to the semi-finals of the promotion play-offs, while teams finishing third through sixth contest barrage matches. The play-off winner is promoted to the Top 14, and a second promotion place is decided by an accession match against the 13th-placed Top 14 club. The bottom two teams are relegated to the Nationale.

History

The league was created in 2000 as part of the professionalisation of French rugby, replacing the former second division of the French championship. It has since become one of the best-attended and most competitive second-tier rugby competitions in the world, regularly drawing crowds and television audiences comparable to top divisions in other countries.
