List of ARM processors

This is a list of central processing units (CPUs) implementing the ARM instruction set architecture, together with processor cores designed by ARM Holdings and by licensees. ARM processors are ubiquitous in mobile phones, embedded systems, and increasingly in laptops and servers.

Licensee implementations

Licensees have produced many custom implementations, including Apple's Cyclone through Avalanche and Everest cores (Apple silicon A-series and M-series), Qualcomm's Scorpion, Krait, Kryo and Oryon, Samsung's Mongoose and Exynos cores, NVIDIA's Denver and Carmel, Marvell and Cavium ThunderX server processors, Fujitsu's A64FX, and Amazon's Graviton family. Earlier generations included DEC/Intel StrongARM and XScale, Texas Instruments OMAP, and Freescale i.MX devices.
