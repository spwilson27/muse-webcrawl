Twente
Country: Netherlands
Province: Overijssel
Largest city: Enschede

Twente (Dutch Low Saxon: Tweante) is a region in the eastern Netherlands, in the province of Overijssel. It borders Germany to the east, the Achterhoek region to the south, and the Salland region to the northwest. Its principal cities are Enschede, Hengelo and Almelo.

History

Historically part of the Oversticht of the Bishopric of Utrecht, Twente was long a rural, relatively isolated region. In the 19th and early 20th centuries it became a major centre of the Dutch textile industry, earning Enschede the nickname of a textile city; the industry declined sharply after the 1960s.

Culture and language

The local dialect, Tweants, belongs to the Dutch Low Saxon group and is still widely spoken. The region is home to the University of Twente, founded in 1961, and to the football club FC Twente, based in Enschede.
