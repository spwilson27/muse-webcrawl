Valerian and the City of a Thousand Planets (French: Valérian et la Cité des mille planètes) is a 2017 English-language French space opera film written and directed by Luc Besson, based on the French science fiction comics series Valérian and Laureline.

Theatrical release poster.

Plot

In the 28th century, spatio-temporal agents Valerian (Dane DeHaan) and Laureline (Cara Delevingne) are assigned to the vast intergalactic metropolis Alpha — a city of a thousand planets — to uncover a dark force threatening the peaceful station. Their mission exposes a decades-old cover-up tied to the destroyed paradise planet Mül and its gentle inhabitants, the Pearls.

Production

The film was among the most expensive European productions ever made, with extensive visual effects by Weta Digital and ILM. Besson, a lifelong fan of the comic, had acquired the rights in the 1990s. Principal photography took place in France in 2015–2016.

Reception

Critics praised the film's visuals while finding its screenplay thin and the leads' chemistry unconvincing. It underperformed commercially against its large budget, though it later found a cult audience among science-fiction fans and admirers of the source comics.
