The Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews

The Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews is one of the oldest golf clubs in the world, based in St Andrews, Fife, Scotland — the "home of golf." Founded in 1754 as the Society of St Andrews Golfers, it received royal patronage in 1834.

Role in the game

For over two centuries the club was golf's governing authority. In 2004 its governance functions passed to a separate body, The R&A, which jointly with the USGA maintains the Rules of Golf and organizes The Open Championship and other championships. The club itself is now a private members' club with about 2,400 members worldwide; it admitted its first women members in 2014.

Setting

Its clubhouse overlooks the Old Course, where golf has been played since the 15th century.
