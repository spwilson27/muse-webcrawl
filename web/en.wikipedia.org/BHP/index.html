BHP

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

BHP Group Limited, known as BHP and formerly BHP Billiton, is an Australian multinational mining and metals company headquartered in Melbourne. It is one of the world's largest mining companies, producing iron ore, copper, metallurgical coal, nickel and potash, with major operations in Australia and the Americas.

History

The Broken Hill Proprietary Company was founded in 1885 to mine a rich silver-lead-zinc deposit at Broken Hill, New South Wales, and expanded into steelmaking and later petroleum. In 2001 it merged with the Anglo-Dutch Billiton to form BHP Billiton, then the world's largest diversified miner. A dual-listed structure was unified under the Australian parent in 2022, and the company shortened its name to BHP in 2018. Its petroleum business was merged into Woodside in 2022.

Operations

BHP's Western Australia Iron Ore division in the Pilbara is among the lowest-cost and largest iron-ore producers globally. Its copper assets include Escondida in Chile, the world's largest copper mine, and Olympic Dam in South Australia; the Jansen potash project in Canada is a major growth investment. In 2015 it demerged several non-core assets as South32, and in 2024 it jointly acquired Filo Corp. for copper exploration in South America; a proposed takeover of Anglo American in 2024 was withdrawn.

Controversies

The 2015 Samarco dam collapse in Brazil, at a joint venture with Vale, killed 19 people and caused extensive environmental damage, leading to major settlements. The Ok Tedi mine in Papua New Guinea, from which BHP exited in 2002, remains a prominent case in debates over mining's environmental legacy.
