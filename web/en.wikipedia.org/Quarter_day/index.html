Quarter day

Quarter days are the four days traditionally dividing the year in parts of the British Isles, on which rents were due, servants were hired, and leases began. In England and Wales the traditional quarter days are Lady Day (25 March), Midsummer Day (24 June), Michaelmas (29 September), and Christmas Day (25 December).

Scotland and Ireland

Scotland historically used different term days — Candlemas (2 February), Whitsunday (15 May in the legal calendar sense), Lammas (1 August), and Martinmas (11 November). The Irish quarter days were similar to the Scottish ones. Quarter days survive in some commercial leases and in the rhythm of the legal and academic years; dates close to the quarter days are still common for rent payments under modern tenancy agreements.
