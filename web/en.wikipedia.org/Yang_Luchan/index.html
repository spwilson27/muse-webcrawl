Yang Luchan

Born1799, Guangping, Hebei, China
Died1872, Beijing
StyleFounder of Yang-style tai chi

Yang Luchan (1799–1872), also known as Yang Fukui, was a Chinese martial artist and the founder of Yang-style tai chi, today the most widely practised style of tai chi in the world.

Life

According to family tradition, Yang learned tai chi in Chenjiagou from Chen Changxing of the Chen family. Returning to his home region and later moving to Beijing, he taught members of the imperial court and the banner guard, gradually modifying the art into a softer, more even-paced form suited to a broad public.

Legacy

Yang earned the nickname "Yang the Invincible" for his skill. His sons Yang Banhou and Yang Jianhou, and grandson Yang Chengfu, spread and standardised the Yang style, whose large, flowing frame became the basis of the modern simplified forms taught worldwide.
