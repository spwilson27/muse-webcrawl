Saint-Denys Garneau

BornHector de Saint-Denys Garneau, 13 June 1912, Montreal, Quebec
Died24 October 1943, Sainte-Catherine-de-la-Jacques-Cartier, Quebec
OccupationPoet, painter
Notable workRegards et jeux dans l'espace (1937)

Hector de Saint-Denys Garneau (1912–1943) was a French-Canadian poet regarded as a founder of modern poetry in Quebec. A great-grandson of the historian François-Xavier Garneau, he published only one collection in his lifetime.

Work

Regards et jeux dans l'espace (1937) broke with the traditional verse of Quebec letters, using free verse and introspective imagery. Discouraged by its reception, Garneau withdrew from literary life; his posthumously published journals and complete poems revealed the depth of his spiritual and artistic searching.

Legacy

Garneau died of a heart attack while canoeing in 1943, aged 31. His work profoundly influenced the poets of the Hexagone movement and modern Quebec literature.
