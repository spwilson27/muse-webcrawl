Ernst & Young

Ernst & Young Global Limited, trade name EY, is a multinational professional services partnership headquartered in London, England. It is one of the Big Four accounting firms, providing assurance, tax, consulting, and strategy and transactions services.

History

The firm traces its roots to 1849. The modern firm was formed in 1989 by the merger of Ernst & Whinney and Arthur Young & Co., themselves descendants of firms founded by Alwin C. Ernst and Arthur Young. It rebranded as EY in 2013.

Services

EY member firms offer audit, tax planning, business consulting, technology, and transaction advisory services to clients in more than 150 countries, employing around 400,000 people worldwide.
