Chain lock
TypeBicycle / motorcycle lock
ComponentsHardened steel chain, padlock or integrated lock
UseSecuring vehicles and gates

A chain lock is a security device consisting of a length of hardened steel chain whose ends are joined by a padlock or an integrated locking mechanism. Chain locks are widely used to secure bicycles, motorcycles, gates, and other property to fixed objects.

Design

The chain is usually made of hardened alloy steel links, often encased in a nylon or fabric sleeve to protect painted surfaces from scratching. Security depends on link thickness, the hardening of the steel, and the quality of the lock itself; thicker chains resist bolt cutters and saws better but are heavier to carry.

Use on bicycles

For bicycles, a chain lock is typically threaded through the frame and one or both wheels and around an immovable rack or post. Compared with a U-lock, a chain offers more flexibility in what it can be fastened around, at the cost of greater weight.
