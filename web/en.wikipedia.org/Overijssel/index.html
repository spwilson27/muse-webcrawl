Overijssel

Overijssel is a province of the Netherlands located in the central-eastern part of the country. Its capital is Zwolle, and its largest city is Enschede. The name means "across the IJssel", the river forming much of its western border.

Geography

The province includes the Salland, Twente and Kop van Overijssel regions. Twente in the southeast is the most urbanised area, historically a textile industry centre. The Weerribben-Wieden National Park is one of the largest wetlands in northwestern Europe.

History

Overijssel formed part of the Oversticht under the bishops of Utrecht before passing to Habsburg rule and joining the Dutch Republic. Hanseatic cities such as Deventer, Kampen and Zwolle flourished from medieval river trade.
