Multi-function printer

FunctionsPrint, copy, scan, fax, email
Also calledMFP, all-in-one (AIO)

A multi-function printer (MFP), also marketed as an all-in-one device, is an office machine that combines the functionality of several devices in one unit, typically printing, scanning, photocopying and faxing, often with the ability to email scanned documents and store files.

Types

Consumer and SOHO devices are usually inkjet- or laser-based units aimed at home offices, while enterprise MFPs are networked floor-standing machines with finishing options such as stapling and booklet-making, user authentication and document-workflow software.

Considerations

Because networked MFPs contain storage and process sensitive documents, their security — hard-drive encryption, secure printing and timely firmware patching — is an information-security concern.
