Public forum debate

Public forum debate is a widespread form of competitive debate in American high schools, sponsored by the National Speech & Debate Association. Designed to be accessible to a general audience, it features two-person teams arguing a current-events resolution that changes monthly, with an emphasis on persuasive delivery and clash understandable to lay judges.

Format

A coin flip determines sides and speaking order. Speeches alternate between pro and con teams: constructive speeches, rebuttals, summaries and final focus speeches, interspersed with crossfire periods in which debaters question each other directly, including a "grand crossfire" involving all four speakers. Preparation time is limited, typically three minutes per team.

Topics and style

Recent resolutions have addressed foreign policy, economics and social issues. Public forum prizes clear communication and real-world argumentation, though as competition has intensified its style has grown faster and more technical at national-circuit levels.
