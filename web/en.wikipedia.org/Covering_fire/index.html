Covering fire

Covering fire, or cover fire, is the use of weapons fire to protect personnel advancing or withdrawing, or to suppress an enemy so friendly forces can manoeuvre. It is a core element of fire and movement tactics.

Tactics

One element fires while another moves, then they alternate — a practice often summarised as "I'm up, he sees me, I'm down." Suppressive fire need not hit targets directly; volume and accuracy force opponents to keep their heads down.
