Le Mans

Le Mans is a city in northwestern France, on the Sarthe River, and the prefecture of the Sarthe department in the Pays de la Loire region. With a population of roughly 145,000 in the city proper and over 300,000 in its metropolitan area, it is best known internationally for the 24 Hours of Le Mans, the world's oldest active endurance sports-car race, held annually since 1923.

History

Le Mans was the Roman city of Vindunum, capital of the Aulerci Cenomani tribe, and retains one of the most complete Roman walls in the former empire. In the Middle Ages it was the capital of the county of Maine and a contested prize between the French crown and the dukes of Normandy and Anjou. Geoffrey Plantagenet, Count of Anjou, married Matilda, daughter of Henry I of England, at Le Mans in 1128; their son Henry II of England was born in the city in 1133. During the French Wars of Religion and again during the Vendée uprising of 1793, the city saw heavy fighting. In 1871, during the Franco-Prussian War, the French Second Army of the Loire was defeated at the Battle of Le Mans.

The old town

The historic centre, Cité Plantagenêt, is a well-preserved district of cobbled streets, half-timbered houses, and Renaissance mansions, overlooked by the great Gothic Cathedral of Saint-Julien, noted for its flying buttresses and medieval stained glass. The old town is a frequent film location for period dramas.

Motorsport

Motor racing at Le Mans dates to the 1906 French Grand Prix, held on a circuit of public roads near the city. The 24 Hours of Le Mans, organised by the Automobile Club de l'Ouest, runs on the Circuit de la Sarthe, a 13.6 km course combining permanent track and closed public roads, including the famous Mulsanne Straight. The race is the centrepiece of the FIA World Endurance Championship and one leg of the informal Triple Crown of Motorsport.

Economy and transport

Le Mans is a centre for insurance (it is home to the MMA group), agriculture, and engineering. The TGV connects the city to Paris in under an hour. The city also has a tramway system, opened in 2007.
