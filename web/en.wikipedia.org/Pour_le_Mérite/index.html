Pour le Mérite

The Pour le Mérite is an order of merit formerly awarded by the Kingdom of Prussia. Established in 1740 by King Frederick II of Prussia, it honoured extraordinary military and later civilian achievement. The military class became famous in the First World War as the "Blue Max" (German: Blauer Max).

History

Frederick II modelled the order on the French Ordre du Mérite; its name and inscription were in French, the language of the Prussian court. A civilian class for the arts and sciences was added by King Frederick William IV in 1842, with Alexander von Humboldt as its first chancellor.

Modern era

The military order became extinct with the death of its last holder in 1994, while the civilian order continues today under the patronage of the President of Germany, with a maximum of 40 living German members.
