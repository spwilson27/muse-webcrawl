FX (TV channel)

FX is an American pay television channel owned by FX Networks, LLC, a subsidiary of the Disney Entertainment business segment and division of The Walt Disney Company. It is based at the Fox Studios lot in Century City, California. FX originally launched on June 1, 1994.

Sister channelsFXX, FXM

Programming

FX's programming consists largely of original scripted dramas and comedies, including series such as The Shield, Sons of Anarchy, Justified, Fargo, American Horror Story, and Atlanta. The network became known for edgy, auteur-driven series distinct from broadcast network fare, and its originals are streamed under the "FX on Hulu" banner.
