Wolpertinger

In Bavarian folklore, the Wolpertinger is a legendary hybrid animal said to inhabit the alpine forests of Bavaria and southern Germany. It is typically depicted as having the body of a rabbit or hare combined with antlers, wings, and fangs, assembled from parts of several different animals.

Description

Representations vary, but a common form joins a hare's head and body with a deer's antlers, a bird's wings and feet, and prominent canine teeth. Related composite beasts appear throughout German-speaking folklore under names such as Rasselbock and Raurakl.

In culture

Taxidermied "wolpertingers" stitched together from pieces of real animals are displayed in taverns and hunting museums across Bavaria, where they serve as jokes played on tourists in much the same spirit as the American jackalope. The creature has become a familiar mascot of Bavarian folk humour.
