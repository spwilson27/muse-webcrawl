Duck curve

The duck curve is a graph of power production over the course of a day that shows the timing imbalance between peak demand and solar power generation. It was popularised by the California Independent System Operator (CAISO) around 2012–2013 and is named for its resemblance to the profile of a duck.

Description

As solar capacity grows, midday net load (total demand minus wind and solar generation) falls into a deep "belly", while the evening decline of solar output coincides with rising demand, producing a steep "neck" requiring rapid ramping of other generators. The pattern creates risks of overgeneration at midday and steep evening ramps.

Mitigation

Proposed responses include energy storage, demand response, time-of-use pricing, regional interconnection, and curtailing excess solar generation.
