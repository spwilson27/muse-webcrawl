Arsenal F.C.

Founded: 1886 (as Dial Square)
Ground: Emirates Stadium
Nickname: The Gunners
Manager: Mikel Arteta

Arsenal Football Club is a professional football club based in Islington, London, England. It competes in the Premier League, the top flight of English football. Founded in 1886 by workers at the Royal Arsenal armaments factory in Woolwich as Dial Square, the club turned professional in 1891 and joined the Football League in 1893.

History

Under manager Herbert Chapman in the 1930s, Arsenal won five league titles and revolutionized English football tactics with the WM formation. The club won its first league and FA Cup double in 1970–71. Arsène Wenger, appointed in 1996, transformed the club with modern training and scouting methods, winning three Premier League titles and seven FA Cups; his 2003–04 "Invincibles" squad went an entire league season unbeaten, an unmatched achievement in the Premier League era.

Honours

Arsenal has won 13 league titles, a record 14 FA Cups, 2 League Cups, and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1994. The club moved from Highbury to the Emirates Stadium in 2006.

Rivalries

Arsenal's principal rivalry is the North London derby against Tottenham Hotspur. The club also has historic rivalries with Manchester United and Chelsea.
