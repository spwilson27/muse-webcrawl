Projection screen

A projection screen is an installation consisting of a surface and a support structure used for displaying a projected image for viewing by an audience. Projection screens may be permanently installed in cinemas or home theatres, wall-mounted or pull-down in classrooms and boardrooms, or portable for temporary use.

Types and materials

Screens are commonly made from vinyl or woven fabrics, in matte white or grey finishes; grey ("high contrast") screens improve perceived black levels in ambient light. Specialised types include rear-projection screens, acoustically transparent screens placed in front of loudspeakers, high-gain screens that concentrate reflected light toward the audience, and ambient-light-rejecting screens. Screen performance is characterised by gain — the ratio of brightness reflected to that of a standard white surface — and by viewing angle.

Formats

Screens are sized by diagonal and aspect ratio: 4:3 for older video, 16:9 for HDTV, and wider 2.35:1/2.39:1 "scope" formats for cinema presentation.
