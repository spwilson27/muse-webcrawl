Founded: 1971
Founder: George Lucas
Headquarters: Presidio, San Francisco, California

Lucasfilm Ltd. LLC is an American film and television production company founded by filmmaker George Lucas in 1971 in San Rafael, California. It is best known for creating the Star Wars and Indiana Jones franchises.

History

After the success of Star Wars (1977), Lucasfilm grew into a leader in special effects through its Industrial Light & Magic division, and in sound design through Skywalker Sound. In 2012, The Walt Disney Company acquired Lucasfilm for about $4.05 billion, with Kathleen Kennedy becoming president. Subsequent productions include the Star Wars sequel trilogy and streaming series such as The Mandalorian.
