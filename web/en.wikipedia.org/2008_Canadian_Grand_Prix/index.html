The 2008 Canadian Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held on 8 June 2008 at the Circuit Gilles Villeneuve in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. It was the seventh race of the 2008 Formula One World Championship and was won by Robert Kubica of BMW Sauber — the first and only Formula One victory for both Kubica and the BMW Sauber team, exactly one year after Kubica's heavy accident at the same circuit.

Race report

Lewis Hamilton started from pole position and led until a safety car period bunched the field. As the pit lane opened, Hamilton failed to see the red light at the pit exit and crashed into the back of Kimi Räikkönen's Ferrari, eliminating both cars. Kubica, who had narrowly avoided the incident, went on to lead a BMW Sauber one-two finish with Nick Heidfeld second and David Coulthard third for Red Bull.

The win moved Kubica into the lead of the Drivers' Championship. The race was the last Canadian Grand Prix until 2010, as the event was omitted from the 2009 calendar.
