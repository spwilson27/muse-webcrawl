Euroclear is a Belgium-based financial services company that specializes in the settlement of securities transactions, as well as the safekeeping and asset servicing of these securities. It operates the Euroclear System, the world's largest international central securities depository (ICSD), and also runs the national central securities depositories of several European countries, including those of Belgium, France, Ireland, the Netherlands, Sweden, Finland, and the United Kingdom (through Euroclear UK & International, formerly CREST).

History

The Euroclear System was created in 1968 by the Brussels office of Morgan Guaranty Trust to solve the paperwork chaos of the growing Eurobond market, in which physical certificates were constantly shipped between banks. Ownership passed in the 1970s to Euroclear Clearance System, a cooperative owned by its users; the operating company Euroclear Bank was established in 2001 following restructuring, with Euroclear plc later becoming the group's parent in London. In 2022, the London Stock Exchange Group and other investors acquired stakes as the group's ownership broadened.

Role in sanctions

Euroclear became central to international financial sanctions after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022: roughly €190 billion or more of Russian Central Bank assets immobilized in the EU are held at Euroclear in Belgium. The interest generated on these frozen assets — billions of euros per year — was tapped by the EU beginning in 2024 to support Ukraine, including as backing for a G7 loan package, making Euroclear one of the most geopolitically significant financial utilities in the world.

Services

Beyond settlement and custody, Euroclear provides collateral management (the Collateral Highway), fund services, and issuer services. Alongside Clearstream in Luxembourg, it forms the duopoly of ICSDs serving international capital markets.
