Digital Cinema Initiatives, LLC (DCI) is a joint venture of major motion picture studios, formed in 2002 to establish a standard architecture for digital cinema systems. Its members have included Disney, Paramount, Sony Pictures, Universal Pictures, and Warner Bros.

Specification

In 2005 DCI published the Digital Cinema System Specification, which defines how films are packaged, encrypted, and projected digitally. The spec underlies the Digital Cinema Package (DCP): JPEG 2000 image compression at 2K or 4K resolution, MXF wrapping, and strong encryption with per-screen keys (KDMs) to combat piracy.

Impact

The DCI standard made large-scale conversion from 35mm film projection commercially viable, and virtually all commercial digital cinemas worldwide now conform to it. DCI also runs compliance testing for projectors and servers.
