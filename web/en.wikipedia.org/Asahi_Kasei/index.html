Asahi Kasei Corporation (旭化成株式会社, Asahi Kasei Kabushiki-gaisha) is a multinational Japanese chemical company headquartered in Tokyo. Its main products are chemicals and materials science, including fibres, plastics, electronic materials, pharmaceuticals and medical devices, as well as homes and construction materials. It is a constituent of the Nikkei 225 index.

History

The company traces its origins to 1922, when engineer Shitagau Noguchi began ammonia production using the Casale process, and to the founding of Asahi Bemberg Silk in 1931, which produced Bemberg cupro rayon. Over the following decades the group expanded from fibres into petrochemicals, plastics, housing (through its Hebel Haus brand), and electronics. The lithium-ion battery separator business builds on work by Akira Yoshino, an Asahi Kasei researcher who shared the 2019 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the development of lithium-ion batteries.

Business segments

Asahi Kasei operates through three main segments: Material (fibres, chemicals, and electronics, including Hipore lithium-ion battery separators and electronic compass components), Homes (Hebel Haus houses and construction materials), and Health Care (pharmaceuticals and medical devices such as dialyzers and apheresis products, including through its subsidiary ZOLL Medical, acquired in 2012). In 2020 it acquired the American pharmaceutical company Veloxis.
