Ballymoney

Ballymoney (from Irish Baile Monaidh, meaning "townland of the bog") is a small town in County Antrim, Northern Ireland. Historically part of the Route district, it stands a short distance southeast of Coleraine and had a population of roughly 11,000 at recent censuses.

History

The town grew around a market settlement and was badly affected by the rebellion of 1798; much of it was burned in the aftermath of the Battle of Ballymoney. In the 19th century it developed as a market and linen town, and the railway arrived in 1855, linking it to Belfast and Derry.

Notable associations

Ballymoney is the home town of the Dunlop family of motorcycle road racers, including Joey Dunlop, five-time world champion and 26-time winner at the Isle of Man TT, whose memorial garden stands in the town. The nearby Dark Hedges, an avenue of beech trees, has become a popular visitor attraction.
