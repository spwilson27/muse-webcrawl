Ascot Racecourse is a British racecourse located in Ascot, Berkshire, England, used for thoroughbred horse racing. Founded in 1711 by Queen Anne, it is one of the leading racecourses in the United Kingdom and hosts Royal Ascot, the most famous meeting in British racing, each June.

Royal Ascot

The Royal Meeting, attended by the monarch and members of the royal family who arrive each day in a horse-drawn procession, features several Group 1 races including the Queen Anne Stakes, the King's Stand Stakes, the St James's Palace Stakes, the Prince of Wales's Stakes and the Gold Cup, the meeting's traditional highlight on "Ladies' Day".

Other racing

Ascot also stages the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes in July, one of Europe's most prestigious weight-for-age races, British Champions Day in October, and National Hunt racing in winter including the Clarence House Chase. The course was closed for a major redevelopment between 2004 and 2006, when a new grandstand was built.
