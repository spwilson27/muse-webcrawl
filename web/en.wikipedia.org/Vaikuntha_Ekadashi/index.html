Vaikuntha Ekadashi

Vaikuntha Ekadashi is a Hindu festival observed on the eleventh lunar day (Ekadashi) of the fortnight of the waxing moon in the month of Margashirsha (roughly December–January). It is dedicated to the god Vishnu and is especially significant in South India.

Observance

Devotees fast, keep vigil, and visit Vishnu temples. In major temples such as Srirangam and Tirupati, a special entrance called the Paramapada Vasal or Vaikuntha Dvaram — the "gate to Vaikuntha", Vishnu's abode — is opened on this day, and passing through it is believed to confer liberation.

Legend

According to tradition, Vishnu opened the gates of Vaikuntha to two demons despite their opposition to him, and granted that whoever visits a Vishnu temple on this day and passes through the gate would attain Vaikuntha. The festival spans 21 days of celebrations at Srirangam, known as Pagal Pathu and Ra Pathu.
