Niños Héroes

The Niños Héroes ("Boy Heroes", or "Heroic Cadets") were six Mexican teenage military cadets who were killed in the defence of Mexico City during the Battle of Chapultepec, one of the last major battles of the Mexican–American War, on 13 September 1847.

The six cadets

The cadets — Juan de la Barrera, Juan Escutia, Agustín Melgar, Vicente Suárez, Francisco Márquez, and Fernando Montes de Oca — were aged between 13 and 20. According to tradition, when the castle's position became untenable, the cadets refused orders to fall back; Juan Escutia is said to have wrapped himself in the Mexican flag and leapt from the Chapultepec Castle roof rather than let it fall into enemy hands.

Commemoration

The cadets are honoured at the Altar a la Patria monument in Chapultepec Park, Mexico City, and 13 September is observed in Mexico with official ceremonies. Their images have appeared on Mexican currency, and streets, schools, and a Mexico City Metro station bear the name Niños Héroes.
