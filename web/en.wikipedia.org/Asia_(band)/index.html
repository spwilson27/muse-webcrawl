Asia is an English rock supergroup formed in London in 1981. The original line-up consisted of vocalist and bassist John Wetton (formerly of King Crimson and U.K.), guitarist Steve Howe (of Yes), keyboardist Geoff Downes (of Yes and the Buggles) and drummer Carl Palmer (of Emerson, Lake & Palmer) — four veterans of 1970s progressive rock. Their sound streamlined progressive rock into polished, radio-friendly hard rock with strong choruses.

History

The band's self-titled debut album Asia (1982), featuring cover art by Roger Dean, was an enormous commercial success: it reached number one in the United States and was the best-selling album of 1982 there, driven by the hit singles "Heat of the Moment" and "Only Time Will Tell". The follow-up Alpha (1983) sold less well amid internal tensions over songwriting and direction; Wetton briefly left and was replaced, and guitarist Steve Howe departed after the third album, Astra (1985). The original band effectively dissolved in 1986.

Downes revived the Asia name in the 1990s with a shifting cast of musicians, releasing albums such as Aqua (1992) and Silent Nation (2004). In 2006 the four original members reunited for a 25th-anniversary tour and recorded the albums Phoenix (2008), Omega (2010) and XXX (2012). Howe retired from the band in 2013 to focus on Yes and was replaced by Sam Coulson. John Wetton died of cancer on 31 January 2017; the band continued with Billy Sherwood, and later with new frontman Harry Whitley and drummer Virgil Donati, releasing the live album Asia in Asia and studio material including Gravitas (2014, with the earlier line-up).
