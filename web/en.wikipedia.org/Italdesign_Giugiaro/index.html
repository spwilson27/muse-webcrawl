Italdesign Giugiaro

Italdesign is famous for its wedge-shaped car designs

Italdesign Giugiaro S.p.A. is a design and engineering company based in Moncalieri, near Turin, Italy, founded in 1968 by designer Giorgetto Giugiaro and engineer Aldo Mantovani. It is best known for automobile styling, though it also undertakes industrial design, engineering and prototyping.

History

Founded as Studi Italiani Realizzazione Prototipi, the firm quickly became one of the most influential car design houses in the world. Giugiaro's folded-paper, wedge styling defined 1970s sports cars and hatchbacks. Since 2010–2015 the company has been wholly owned by the Volkswagen Group through Lamborghini, while the Giugiaro family retained the separate Giugiaro Architettura venture (later GFG Style).

Notable designs

Landmark Italdesign productions include the Lotus Esprit, DeLorean DMC-12, BMW M1, Maserati Bora, Volkswagen Golf Mk1, Fiat Panda, Lancia Delta, Saab 9000, Subaru SVX and the first-generation Fiat Punto. Giugiaro was named "Car Designer of the Century" in 1999.

Activities today

The company continues to provide design, engineering, pre-series production and ultra-limited special vehicles (such as the Zerouno) for manufacturers and private clients under Volkswagen Group ownership.
