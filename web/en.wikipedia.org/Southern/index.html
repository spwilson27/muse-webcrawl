Southern

This article is about the general term. For other uses, see Southern (disambiguation).

Southern is a directional and regional descriptor meaning of, from, or relating to the south of a place. It is used in the names of countless countries, regions, institutions, and companies — for example the Southern Hemisphere, the Southern United States, Southern Europe, and Southern Africa — and in proper names such as Southern Railway and Southern Airways.

Regional uses

In the United States, "the South" or "the Southern states" typically refers to the Census Bureau's South region or the states of the former Confederacy, and "Southern" describes their shared dialect (Southern American English), cuisine, and culture. In England, "the South" denotes the regions around and below London, contrasted with the North in the North–South divide. In astronomy and geography, the southern celestial hemisphere and the Southern Ocean take their names from the direction.

Transport companies

Southern has been the name of several railway operators: the Southern Railway in the United Kingdom (1923–1947) and its modern successor train operating company Southern, which runs services between London and the south coast; and the Southern Railway in the United States, a predecessor of Norfolk Southern.
