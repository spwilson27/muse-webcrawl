Purple Rain (film)

DirectorAlbert Magnoli
StarringPrince, Apollonia Kotero, Morris Day
Released27 July 1984
Box officeApprox. $70 million

Purple Rain is a 1984 American romantic drama musical film directed by Albert Magnoli and starring Prince in his acting debut. Developed to showcase Prince's talents, it features many of his concert sequences filmed at the First Avenue club in Minneapolis.

Plot

Prince plays "the Kid", the talented but troubled frontman of the band the Revolution, who struggles with a violent home life, rivalry with Morris Day and his group the Time, and a romance with the aspiring singer Apollonia. The story culminates in an emotional performance of the title song, dedicated to the Kid's father.

Reception and legacy

Made for about $7.2 million, the film grossed more than $70 million at the box office and won the Academy Award for Best Original Song Score. It is credited with establishing Prince as a superstar and is frequently listed among the greatest musical films. Its soundtrack album became one of the best-selling soundtracks of all time.
