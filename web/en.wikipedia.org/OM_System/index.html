OM System is a brand of digital imaging products owned by OM Digital Solutions Corporation, the company formed when the imaging division of Olympus Corporation was transferred to Japan Industrial Partners in 2021. The brand was introduced in October 2021 and gradually replaced the Olympus name on cameras and lenses.

History

Olympus had produced cameras since the 1930s and was a co-founder, with Panasonic, of the Micro Four Thirds mirrorless camera system. After the divestiture, OM Digital Solutions announced the OM System brand as a nod to the classic Olympus OM film SLR line designed by Yoshihisa Maitani in the 1970s.

Products

The first camera launched under the new brand was the OM-1 (2022), a flagship Micro Four Thirds body with computational photography features and high-speed stacked sensor. The smaller OM-5 followed. The line also continues the Tough series of rugged compact cameras, M.Zuiko lenses (later rebranded OM System), binoculars and voice recorders.
