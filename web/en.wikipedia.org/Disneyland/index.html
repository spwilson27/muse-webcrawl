Disneyland

Location: Anaheim, California, United States
Opened: July 17, 1955
Owner: Disney Parks (The Walt Disney Company)
Theme: Fairy tales and Disney characters

Disneyland Park, originally Disneyland, is an amusement park in Anaheim, California, owned and operated by the Walt Disney Company through its Parks, Experiences and Products division. It is the only theme park designed and built to completion under the direct supervision of Walt Disney, opening on July 17, 1955.

Disney initially envisioned building a tourist attraction adjacent to his studios in Burbank to entertain fans who wished to visit; however, he soon realized that the proposed site was too small for the ideas he had. After hiring the Stanford Research Institute to perform a feasability study determining an appropriate site, Disney bought a 160-acre (65 ha) site near Anaheim in 1953. The park was designed by a creative team hand-picked by Walt from internal and outside talent. Construction began in 1954 and the park was unveiled during a special televised press event on the ABC Television Network on July 17, 1955.

History

The concept for Disneyland began when Walt Disney was visiting Griffith Park in Los Angeles with his daughters Diane and Sharon. While watching them ride the merry-go-round, he came up with the idea of a place where adults and their children could go and have fun together. His early concepts grew into a "Mickey Mouse Park" and then into the far more ambitious project that opened in 1955, built on former orange groves and walnut trees.

Opening day, later dubbed "Black Sunday" by Disney executives, was marred by problems: rides broke down, the park was overcrowded due to counterfeit tickets, and a heat wave melted the freshly laid asphalt. The park nevertheless grew rapidly in popularity and became the prototype for the modern theme park industry.

Lands

Disneyland comprises nine themed "lands":

  • Main Street, U.S.A. — modeled after a small American town of the early 1900s
  • Adventureland — jungle-themed, home to the Jungle Cruise and Indiana Jones Adventure
  • Frontierland — the American frontier, with Big Thunder Mountain Railroad
  • Fantasyland — behind Sleeping Beauty Castle; Peter Pan's Flight and "it's a small world"
  • Tomorrowland — retro-futuristic visions of the future; Space Mountain
  • New Orleans Square — Pirates of the Caribbean and the Haunted Mansion
  • Critter Country — Splash Mountain (later rethemed)
  • Mickey's Toontown — a cartoon neighborhood
  • Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge — opened 2019, anchored by Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run and Rise of the Resistance

Attendance

Disneyland is one of the most visited theme parks in the world, regularly drawing well over 15 million visitors in a typical year, and its cumulative attendance since opening exceeds 750 million guests. Walt Disney's dedication speech declared: "To all who come to this happy place: Welcome. Disneyland is your land."
