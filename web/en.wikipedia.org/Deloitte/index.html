Deloitte

Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Limited, commonly referred to as Deloitte, is a British multinational professional services network and one of the "Big Four" accounting organisations, alongside EY, KPMG and PwC. It is the largest professional services network by revenue and number of employees, with roughly 460,000 staff worldwide and annual revenues above 65 billion US dollars in recent years.

History

The firm traces its origins to 1845, when William Welch Deloitte opened an office in London; other founding lines include George Touche's firm of 1899 and the Japanese Tohmatsu firm of 1968. Through a series of mergers — notably Deloitte Haskins & Sells with Touche Ross in 1989 — the modern network took shape. Deloitte member firms operate as separate legal entities coordinated by the UK-domiciled parent.

Services

Deloitte provides audit and assurance, tax and legal services, consulting, financial advisory and risk advisory. Its consulting arm has grown to rival dedicated strategy firms, and the network is a major auditor of listed companies and a sponsor of sporting and cultural events.

Criticism

Like its peers, Deloitte has faced regulatory fines and litigation over audits of companies that later collapsed, as well as scrutiny of the conflicts between auditing and consulting work.
