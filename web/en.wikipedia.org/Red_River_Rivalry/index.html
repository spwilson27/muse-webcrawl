Red River Rivalry

The Red River Rivalry is an American college football rivalry game played annually between the Oklahoma Sooners and the Texas Longhorns. The game takes its name from the Red River, which forms the boundary between the states of Oklahoma and Texas.

Venue and tradition

Since 1932 the game has been played at the Cotton Bowl stadium in Dallas, Texas, roughly midway between the two campuses, during the State Fair of Texas. The stadium is split at the 50-yard line between Oklahoma and Texas fans. The winner receives trophies including the Golden Hat, a gold cowboy hat, and the Red River Rivalry trophy.

History

The teams first met in 1900 and have played over 115 times. The game regularly has conference and national championship implications; both schools joined the Southeastern Conference in 2024, continuing the rivalry within the SEC. It is consistently ranked among the greatest rivalries in college football.
