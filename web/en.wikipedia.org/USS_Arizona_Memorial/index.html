USS Arizona Memorial

USS Arizona Memorial
Location: Pearl Harbor, Honolulu, Hawaii
Dedicated: May 30, 1962
Architect: Alfred Preis
Part of: Pearl Harbor National Memorial

The USS Arizona Memorial, at Pearl Harbor in Honolulu, Hawaii, marks the resting place of 1,102 of the 1,177 sailors and Marines killed on USS Arizona during the attack on Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941.

Design

Designed by Austrian-born architect Alfred Preis, the memorial is a white concrete structure 184 feet (56 m) long that spans the sunken hull of the battleship without touching it. Its sagging center and raised ends have been interpreted as representing initial defeat and ultimate victory.

Visiting

The memorial is accessible only by boat from the Pearl Harbor visitor center and is administered by the National Park Service as part of the Pearl Harbor National Memorial. Oil still leaks from the wreck, often called the "tears of the Arizona."
