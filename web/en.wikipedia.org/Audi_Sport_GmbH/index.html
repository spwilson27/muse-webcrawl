Audi Sport GmbH

Audi Sport GmbH, formerly known as quattro GmbH, is the high-performance subsidiary of the German automobile manufacturer Audi, itself a member of the Volkswagen Group. Based in Neckarsulm, Germany, the company develops and produces Audi's highest-performance road cars, sold under the RS and R8 designations, as well as customer racing programmes and personalised vehicles through the Audi exclusive programme.

Audi Sport produces the RS and R8 model lines.

History

The subsidiary was founded in 1983 as quattro GmbH, initially producing accessories and special editions. Its first complete car was the Audi S6 Plus of 1996, followed by the RS 4 Avant in 1999, developed from the line begun with the Porsche-assembled RS2 Avant. In 2016 the company was renamed Audi Sport GmbH to align it with Audi's motorsport division, Audi Sport, whose racing activities it also came to house for customer teams.

Products

The RS range includes high-performance versions of Audi's mainstream models, from the RS 3 to the RS Q8, as well as the RS 6 Avant and RS 7 Sportback. The Audi R8 mid-engine sports car, produced at Böllinger Höfe in Heilbronn alongside the RS models, was manufactured from 2006 until 2024. Audi Sport also produces the electric RS e-tron GT.

Motorsport

Audi Sport GmbH supports customer racing with cars such as the R8 LMS GT3 and RS 3 LMS touring car, while Audi's factory motorsport heritage includes multiple victories at the 24 Hours of Le Mans with prototype cars developed under the Audi Sport name.
