Nintendo DS

Manufacturer: Nintendo
Type: Handheld game console
Release: November 21, 2004 (NA)
Units sold: 154.02 million
Best-selling game: New Super Mario Bros.

The Nintendo DS is a foldable handheld game console produced and released by Nintendo. Released in 2004, it introduced distinctive features including two LCD screens working in tandem — the lower of which is a touchscreen — a built-in microphone, and support for wireless connectivity.

History

Announced in January 2004 as a "third pillar" alongside the GameCube and Game Boy Advance, the DS (an initialism for "Dual Screen" and "Developers' System") launched first in North America. Its backward compatibility with Game Boy Advance titles and strong sales made it the best-selling handheld console of all time and the second-best-selling video game console overall, behind only the PlayStation 2.

Models

The original model was followed by the slimmer Nintendo DS Lite (2006), and later the DSi and DSi XL (2008/2009), which added cameras and downloadable software. The line was succeeded by the Nintendo 3DS in 2011.

Software

Notable titles include New Super Mario Bros., Mario Kart DS, Pokémon Diamond and Pearl, Brain Age, and Nintendogs. The system's touchscreen and dual-screen layout encouraged novel game designs that broadened the gaming audience.
