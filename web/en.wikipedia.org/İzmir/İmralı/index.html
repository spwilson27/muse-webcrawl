İmralı is a small Turkish island in the south of the Sea of Marmara, west of the Armutlu-Bozburun peninsula within Bursa Province. For decades it has served as a maximum-security prison island.

History

A prison was established on the island in 1935. Its most notable inmate was Adnan Menderes, the former prime minister overthrown in the 1960 coup, who was held on İmralı and executed there in 1961. In 1999 the island became the place of imprisonment of Abdullah Öcalan, the captured leader of the PKK, and the other prisoners were transferred away, leaving the island's high-security F-type prison built around his detention.

Geography

The island measures roughly 8 kilometres long and 3 kilometres wide, with its highest point at Türk Tepesi, about 217 metres above sea level. Civilian access is restricted and the island lies within a military forbidden zone.
