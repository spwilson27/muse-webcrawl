Super Bowl LII

Super Bowl LII was an American football game played on February 4, 2018, at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis, Minnesota, to determine the champion of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2017 season. The NFC champion Philadelphia Eagles defeated the AFC champion New England Patriots, 41–33, winning their first Super Bowl and their first NFL championship since 1960.

Game summary

The game set a Super Bowl record for total combined yards (1,151) and featured only one punt. Eagles backup quarterback Nick Foles, who had taken over after an injury to Carson Wentz late in the season, threw for 373 yards and three touchdowns and caught a touchdown pass on the famous trick play known as the "Philly Special" just before halftime. Foles was named Super Bowl MVP.

Tom Brady threw for a Super Bowl-record 505 passing yards for the Patriots, but was strip-sacked by Brandon Graham late in the fourth quarter, sealing Philadelphia's victory.

Entertainment

The halftime show was headlined by Justin Timberlake, and Pink performed the national anthem.
