Homeland security

Homeland security is an American national security term for the national effort to ensure the homeland is safe, secure, and resilient against terrorism and other hazards. It encompasses border security, immigration enforcement, cybersecurity, disaster response, and protection of critical infrastructure.

United States

After the September 11 attacks in 2001, the Homeland Security Act of 2002 created the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), consolidating 22 federal agencies including the Coast Guard, Secret Service, FEMA, Customs and Border Protection, and the Transportation Security Administration.

Scope and debate

The concept spans federal, state, local, tribal, and private-sector actors. Scholars and officials debate the breadth of the term, its overlap with traditional defense and law enforcement, and its balance with civil liberties.
