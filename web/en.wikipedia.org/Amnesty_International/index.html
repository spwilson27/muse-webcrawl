Amnesty International

Amnesty International (also referred to as Amnesty or AI) is an international non-governmental organisation with its headquarters in the United Kingdom focused on human rights.

Amnesty International
Founded1961, London
FounderPeter Benenson
FocusPrisoners of conscience, anti-torture, death penalty abolition
AwardNobel Peace Prize 1977

Work

Amnesty campaigns for those imprisoned for their beliefs, against torture and the death penalty, and for refugee rights, documenting conditions in China, including the Tiananmen crackdown and Xinjiang camps. Its logo is a candle circled in barbed wire.
