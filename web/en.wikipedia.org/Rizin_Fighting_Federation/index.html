Rizin Fighting Federation (stylised RIZIN FF) is a Japanese mixed martial arts promotion founded in 2015 by Nobuyuki Sakakibara, the former president of Pride Fighting Championships. It is based in Tokyo, Japan.

History

Rizin held its first events in December 2015 at the Saitama Super Arena, reviving the tradition of major year-end MMA shows in Japan. Its rules allow soccer kicks and knees to a grounded opponent in some bouts, and fights take place in a ring rather than a cage. The promotion gained international attention in 2018 when undefeated boxing champion Floyd Mayweather Jr. fought kickboxer Tenshin Nasukawa in an exhibition bout. Rizin has also collaborated with Bellator MMA on crossover events.
