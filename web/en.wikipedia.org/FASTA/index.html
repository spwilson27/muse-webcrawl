FASTA

FASTA is a DNA and protein sequence alignment software package first described by David J. Lipman and William R. Pearson in 1985. Its legacy is the FASTA format, which is now a ubiquitous text-based format for representing nucleotide or peptide sequences.

Original authorsDavid J. Lipman, William R. Pearson
Initial release1985
TypeBioinformatics

History

The original FASTP program was designed for protein sequence similarity searching. The FASTA package is available for download from the University of Virginia and the European Bioinformatics Institute.

Function

FASTA takes a given nucleotide or amino acid sequence and searches a corresponding sequence database by using local sequence alignment to find matches of similar database sequences. The FASTA program follows a largely heuristic method which contributes to the high speed of its execution.
