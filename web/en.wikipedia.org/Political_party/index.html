Political party

A political party is an organization that coordinates candidates to compete in a country's elections. Members hold similar political views and seek to influence public policy by winning government power, usually through elections.

Parties typically adopt a platform, nominate candidates, and organize voters and legislators. Systems may be single-party, two-party or multi-party depending on electoral rules and social cleavages. Modern mass parties developed in the 19th century alongside expanding suffrage.
