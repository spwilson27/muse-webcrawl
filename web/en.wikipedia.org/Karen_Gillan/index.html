Karen Sheila Gillan (born 28 November 1987) is a Scottish actress and filmmaker. She gained recognition for her work in British film and television, particularly for playing Amy Pond, a primary companion to the Eleventh Doctor in the BBC series Doctor Who (2010–2013), for which she received several awards and nominations.

Career

Gillan studied acting at the Italia Conti Academy in London and appeared in British television dramas before her breakthrough as Amy Pond opposite Matt Smith. She subsequently achieved international fame as Nebula in the Marvel Cinematic Universe films, beginning with Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) and continuing through Avengers: Infinity War (2018), Avengers: Endgame (2019) and Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023), and as Ruby Roundhouse in Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle (2017) and its sequel.

Other roles include the horror film Oculus (2013), the sitcom Selfie (2014), The Big Short (2015) and the action film Gunpowder Milkshake (2021). She made her feature directorial debut with The Party's Just Beginning (2018), which she also wrote and starred in, drawing on the high suicide rate among young men in her native Highlands.
