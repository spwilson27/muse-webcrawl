Indiana Hoosiers

The Indiana Hoosiers are the intercollegiate sports teams and players of Indiana University Bloomington, the flagship campus of Indiana University. The Hoosiers compete in NCAA Division I and are members of the Big Ten Conference. The school's colours are cream and crimson, and its teams are named for the demonym of the state of Indiana.

Men's basketball

Indiana is best known for its men's basketball program, one of the most storied in college basketball. The Hoosiers have won five NCAA national championships (1940, 1953, 1976, 1981, and 1987), tied for third in the history of the tournament. The 1976 team, coached by Bob Knight, completed the last undefeated season in Division I men's basketball. Knight coached the program from 1971 to 2000, winning three national titles and becoming famous for his motion offense and intense style. The team plays at Simon Skjodt Assembly Hall, renowned for its steep balconies and deafening crowds.

Football

The Hoosiers football team plays at Memorial Stadium. Historically one of the weaker programs in the Big Ten, the team has enjoyed notable high points, including a share of the 1967 Big Ten title and a trip to the 1968 Rose Bowl, and a resurgence under coach Tom Allen, who led Indiana to winning seasons and bowl appearances in 2019 and 2020. Under coach Curt Cignetti, hired in 2024, the Hoosiers achieved a breakthrough season, reaching the College Football Playoff.

Other sports

Indiana has a distinguished swimming and diving tradition, producing Olympic champions such as Mark Spitz under legendary coach Doc Counsilman. The men's soccer team has won multiple NCAA championships, and the women's basketball program has grown into a consistent national contender.
