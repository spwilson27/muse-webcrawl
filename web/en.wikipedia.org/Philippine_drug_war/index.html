Began1 July 2016
Launched byPresident Rodrigo Duterte
Key operationsOplan Tokhang; Oplan Double Barrel

The Philippine drug war, known locally as Oplan Tokhang, is the intensified anti-drug campaign launched by the Philippine government under President Rodrigo Duterte, who took office on 30 June 2016 promising to eradicate the illegal drug trade, particularly crystal methamphetamine (shabu).

Conduct and toll

The campaign combined police operations against suspects with house-to-house "knock and plead" visits urging drug users and pushers to surrender. Official figures recorded more than 6,000 deaths in police operations; human rights organizations estimated the total number of killings, including those by unidentified gunmen, at between 12,000 and 30,000. The deaths, disproportionately among the urban poor, drew widespread international condemnation.

Legal and political consequences

The campaign became the subject of a preliminary examination and later an investigation by the International Criminal Court into alleged crimes against humanity; the Philippines withdrew from the ICC in 2019, and in March 2025 Duterte was arrested and surrendered to the Court in The Hague. Domestically, the policy remained popular with much of the public during Duterte's presidency, though critics in the Senate and abroad pressed for accountability.
