Nilgiri tahr

The Nilgiri tahr (Nilgiritragus hylocrius) is an ungulate endemic to the Nilgiri Hills and the southern Western Ghats of India. A stocky, sure-footed mountain goat relative, it inhabits open montane grasslands interspersed with cliffs (shola-grassland mosaic) at elevations of roughly 1,200 to 2,600 metres.

Description and behaviour

Adults have short, coarse coats — grey-brown in females and juveniles, darker in mature males, which develop a silvery "saddle" on the back. Both sexes have curved horns. Tahrs graze mainly in the mornings and evenings, retreating to cliffs for safety. They are preyed on by leopards and dholes.

Conservation

Hunting and habitat loss reduced the species severely; it is listed as Endangered, with a population of a few thousand protected in reserves such as Eravikulam National Park in Kerala, where large herds are a famous sight. The Nilgiri tahr is the state animal of Tamil Nadu.
