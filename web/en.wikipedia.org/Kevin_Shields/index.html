Kevin Patrick Shields (born 21 May 1963) is an American-born Irish musician, singer-songwriter, composer, and producer, best known as the vocalist and guitarist of the band My Bloody Valentine. His pioneering guitar techniques and production methods defined the shoegaze genre, most famously on the album Loveless (1991).

Early life

Shields was born in Queens, New York, to Irish parents and moved to Dublin as a child. He formed My Bloody Valentine in Dublin in 1983 with drummer Colm Ó Cíosóig, relocating with the band to London in the mid-1980s.

My Bloody Valentine

After signing to Creation Records, the band released Isn't Anything (1988) and then Loveless (1991), an album whose two-year recording process and enormous cost became legendary. Shields layered guitars run through reverse reverb and pitch-shifting, using the tremolo arm to bend notes continuously — his "glide guitar" technique — producing a swirling, androgynous sound. Loveless is now routinely ranked among the greatest albums of all time. A follow-up stalled amid label problems and Shields's perfectionism and personal difficulties, and the band went on hiatus in the 1990s.

Other work

Shields contributed to Primal Scream's albums around 2000, remixed numerous artists, and composed music for Sofia Coppola's film Lost in Translation (2003), earning a BAFTA nomination. My Bloody Valentine reformed in 2007 for live shows and finally released their third album, m b v, in 2013. In 2018 Shields collaborated with Brian Eno on the track "Only Once Away My Son."
