Boss Corporation

Boss Corporation is a Japanese manufacturer of electronic musical equipment, best known for its effects pedals for guitarists and bassists. Founded in 1973 as a division of the Roland Corporation, Boss is headquartered in Hamamatsu, Japan.

Compact pedals

In 1977 Boss introduced its iconic compact pedal series, beginning with models such as the OD-1 Over Drive and PH-1 Phaser. The compact pedals share a standardized rugged metal enclosure with a large footswitch area and have become an industry standard form factor. Notable models include the DS-1 Distortion, the CE-2 Chorus, the DD series of digital delays, and the TU-3 chromatic tuner.

Other products

Beyond compact pedals, Boss produces multi-effects units, guitar synthesizers, loop stations (the RC series), amplifiers such as the Katana series, drum machines and rhythm products under the Dr. Rhythm line, and recording equipment. The company also pioneered the stompbox format that shaped modern guitar effects design.
