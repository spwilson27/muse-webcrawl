Image registration

Image registration
FieldComputer vision, medical imaging, remote sensing
GoalAlign two or more images of the same scene
MethodsFeature-based, intensity-based, transform optimisation

Image registration is the process of transforming different sets of data into one coordinate system. Data may be multiple photographs, data from different sensors, times, depths, or viewpoints. It is used in computer vision, medical imaging, and compiling and analysing images and data from satellites.

Algorithm taxonomy

Registration algorithms may be classified by the transformation model they use (rigid, affine, projective, or deformable), and by whether they compare image intensities directly, such as cross-correlation and mutual information methods, or extracted features such as points, lines, and surfaces.

Applications

In medical imaging, registration aligns scans from different modalities (for example CT and MRI) or different times to track disease and guide treatment. In remote sensing it aligns satellite images for change detection and map updating, and in computer vision it underpins panorama stitching and motion stabilisation.
