The Lower Silesian Voivodeship (Polish: województwo dolnośląskie) is a province in southwestern Poland, created in 1999 from the former Wrocław, Legnica, Wałbrzych and Jelenia Góra voivodeships. Its capital and largest city is Wrocław.

Geography

The province borders Germany to the west and the Czech Republic to the south. The Sudetes mountains, including the Karkonosze range with Śnieżka (1,603 m), Poland's highest peak outside the Tatras, dominate its southern edge. The Oder River flows through the region.

History

The territory formed part of historic Silesia, ruled over the centuries by Piast dukes, Bohemia, Austria, Prussia and Germany before becoming part of Poland after 1945.
