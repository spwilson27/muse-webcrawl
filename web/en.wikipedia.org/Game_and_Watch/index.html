Game & Watch

The Game & Watch is a series of handheld electronic games developed, manufactured, released, and marketed by Nintendo from 1980 to 1991. It was created by game designer Gunpei Yokoi, who observed a bored businessman idly playing with a calculator on a train and conceived of a small game that also functioned as a watch.

Design

Each Game & Watch unit played a single game on a segmented liquid-crystal display and included a clock and alarm function. The series used Yokoi's philosophy of "lateral thinking with withered technology" — mature, cheap technology used in novel ways. Later models introduced the cross-shaped directional pad (D-pad) in Donkey Kong (1982), and the dual-screen Multi Screen series, designs that influenced the Nintendo DS decades later.

Popularity and legacy

Around 43.4 million units were sold worldwide. Titles included Ball (the first), Manhole, Octopus, Oil Panic, Green House and versions of Donkey Kong and The Legend of Zelda. The monochrome character Mr. Game & Watch later became a fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series. Nintendo revived the line with special-edition colour units celebrating the 35th anniversaries of Super Mario Bros. (2020) and The Legend of Zelda (2021).
