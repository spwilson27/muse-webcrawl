Ket people

The Ket people are an Indigenous Siberian people living along the middle Yenisei River in Krasnoyarsk Krai, Russia. Numbering roughly one thousand, they speak the Ket language, the last surviving member of the Yeniseian language family.

Culture

Traditionally semi-nomadic hunter-fishers, the Ket practised shamanism and lived in conical tents and earth dwellings. Their language's proposed links to Na-Dene languages of North America (the Dene–Yeniseian hypothesis) attract scholarly interest.
