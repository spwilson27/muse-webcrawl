Bâlea Lake (Romanian: Lacul Bâlea) is a glacier lake situated at 2,034 metres elevation in the Făgăraș Mountains, in Cârțișoara, Sibiu County, Romania. It is one of the most visited lakes in Romania and lies near the summit of the Transfăgărășan road, one of the country's most famous mountain passes.

Geography

The lake covers an area of about 46,508 square metres and is 360 metres long. It formed in a glacial cirque carved into the Făgăraș range, the highest mountains of the Southern Carpathians.

Tourism

The lake is accessible by car on the Transfăgărășan during the summer months and by cable car from Bâlea Cascadă (Bâlea Waterfall) in winter. Two chalets stand on its shores, and in 2006 the first ice hotel in Eastern Europe was built nearby, reconstructed annually from ice blocks cut from the frozen lake.
