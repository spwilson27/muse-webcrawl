David Tennant

David Tennant (born David John McDonald; 18 April 1971) is a Scottish actor. He is best known for playing the Tenth Doctor in the BBC science fiction series Doctor Who (2005–2010), a role that made him one of Britain's most popular actors, and which he reprised in anniversary specials in 2013 and 2022. His other prominent roles include Alec Hardy in Broadchurch, Kilgrave in Jessica Jones, and Crowley in Good Omens.

Early life

Tennant was born in Bathgate, West Lothian, Scotland, the son of a Church of Scotland minister. A Doctor Who fan from childhood, he decided to become an actor at the age of three. He trained at the Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama in Glasgow and joined the Royal Shakespeare Company, performing roles including Touchstone, Romeo and Hamlet — his 2008 Hamlet for the RSC was widely acclaimed.

Career

Tennant's breakthrough television roles came in the BBC dramas Blackpool (2004) and Casanova (2005). Cast as the Doctor in 2005, he starred in three full series and a run of specials, winning numerous awards and regularly being voted the most popular Doctor. He left the role at the start of 2010. Subsequent work has included the ITV crime drama Broadchurch (2013–2017), the Netflix Marvel series Jessica Jones (2015–2019), the Amazon/BBC fantasy comedy Good Omens (2019–), extensive stage work, and voice roles including Scrooge McDuck in the DuckTales reboot.

Personal life

Tennant is married to actress Georgia Tennant, whom he met while filming Doctor Who; they have five children.
