Coachella Valley

The Coachella Valley is an arid rift valley in the Colorado Desert of Southern California, in Riverside County. It extends for about 45 miles (72 km) southeast from the San Bernardino Mountains to the northern shore of the Salton Sea.

Communities

The valley contains nine cities, including Palm Springs, Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage, Indian Wells, La Quinta, and Indio. It is a major resort and retirement destination known for golf courses, mid-century modern architecture, and warm winters.

Events and agriculture

The valley hosts the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival and the Stagecoach Festival in Indio, and the BNP Paribas Open tennis tournament in Indian Wells. Irrigated agriculture, supplied by the Coachella Canal from the Colorado River, produces dates, grapes, citrus, and vegetables; the area grows most of the dates produced in the United States.

Geography

The valley is bounded by the San Jacinto and Santa Rosa Mountains to the west and the Little San Bernardino Mountains to the east, and is traversed by the San Andreas Fault.
