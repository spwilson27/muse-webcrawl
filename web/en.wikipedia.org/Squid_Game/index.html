Squid Game

Squid Game (Korean: 오징어 게임) is a South Korean survival drama television series created by Hwang Dong-hyuk for Netflix. The series revolves around a secret contest where 456 players, all of whom are in deep financial debt, risk their lives to play a series of children's games for the chance to win a ₩45.6 billion prize. It was released worldwide on 17 September 2021.

Plot

Seong Gi-hun, a divorced and indebted chauffeur, is invited to compete in a series of children's playground games against 455 other players for a life-changing cash prize. The players soon discover that losing a game means death, and each death adds money to the prize fund. Gi-hun allies with other players, including his childhood friend Cho Sang-woo and North Korean defector Kang Sae-byeok, as the games grow deadlier.

Reception and impact

Squid Game became Netflix's most-watched series at launch, reaching number one in 94 countries and attracting more than 142 million member households in its first four weeks. It received critical acclaim and won numerous awards, including Emmy Awards for its lead actor Lee Jung-jae and director Hwang Dong-hyuk. The series has been credited with boosting global interest in Korean drama and was followed by further seasons and a reality competition spin-off.
