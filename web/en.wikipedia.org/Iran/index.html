Iran, officially the Islamic Republic of Iran and historically known in the West as Persia, is a country in West Asia. It borders Iraq and Turkey to the west, Azerbaijan and Armenia to the northwest, the Caspian Sea to the north, Turkmenistan to the northeast, Afghanistan to the east, Pakistan to the southeast, and the Gulf of Oman and Persian Gulf to the south. With a population of around 89 million, it is the 17th-largest country in the world by both area and population.

History

Iran is home to one of the world's oldest continuous major civilisations. The Medes unified the region in the seventh century BC, and Cyrus the Great founded the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BC — the largest empire the world had yet seen. Iran was conquered by Alexander the Great, ruled by the Parthian and Sasanian empires, and Islamised after the Arab conquest of the seventh century AD, while retaining the Persian language and much of its culture. Successive Persianate dynasties, the Safavid adoption of Twelver Shi'a Islam in 1501, and the Qajar and Pahlavi eras shaped the modern state. The Iranian Revolution of 1979 overthrew Shah Mohammad Reza Pahlavi and established the Islamic Republic under Ayatollah Khomeini; the Iran–Iraq War (1980–1988) followed.

Politics

Iran's system combines elected institutions — a president and the Majlis (parliament) — with clerical oversight under the doctrine of velayat-e faqih. The Supreme Leader, who heads the state and commands the armed forces, is selected by the Assembly of Experts; the Guardian Council vets candidates and legislation. Iran's nuclear programme and regional policies have brought it into prolonged conflict with the United States and its allies, including extensive international sanctions.

Economy and culture

Iran holds some of the world's largest reserves of oil and natural gas and has a diversified though sanction-constrained economy including petrochemicals, agriculture, manufacturing and a significant scientific sector. Persian culture — its poetry (Rumi, Hafez, Ferdowsi's Shahnameh), miniature painting, carpets, architecture and cinema — has influenced a vast region from Anatolia to Central and South Asia.
