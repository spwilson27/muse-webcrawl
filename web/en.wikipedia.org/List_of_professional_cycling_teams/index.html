List of professional cycling teams

Professional road cycling teams are ranked by the UCI into WorldTeams, ProTeams and Continental teams.

UCI WorldTeams (examples)

  • Ineos Grenadiers — Great Britain
  • Visma–Lease a Bike — Netherlands
  • UAE Team Emirates — UAE
  • Soudal Quick-Step — Belgium
  • Lidl–Trek — United States
  • Movistar Team — Spain
  • Groupama–FDJ — France
  • EF Education–EasyPost — United States
  • Bora–Hansgrohe / Red Bull — Germany
  • Alpecin–Deceuninck — Belgium

Below the WorldTour sit UCI ProTeams such as Lotto and Israel–Premier Tech, and hundreds of Continental teams worldwide. Women's teams have a parallel structure headed by UCI Women's WorldTeams.
