Telugu literature

[Placeholder image: Telugu_literature.png]

Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, primarily associated with the Telugu-speaking people of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana in India. It consists of poems, short stories, novels, plays, and songs, and forms part of the broader corpus of South Indian literature.

History

The earliest Telugu literature dates to the 11th century, when Nannaya began the Telugu translation of the Sanskrit Mahabharata. The Prabandha period of the Vijayanagara Empire, under Krishnadevaraya, produced celebrated poets known as the Ashtadiggajas. In the modern era, Gurajada Apparao and Kandukuri Veeresalingam pioneered new literary forms, and Viswanatha Satyanarayana won the Jnanpith Award for his novel Veyi Padagalu.
