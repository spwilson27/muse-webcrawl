Bounding overwatch

One element covers while another moves forward

Bounding overwatch (also known as leapfrogging or fire and movement at the smallest scale) is a military infantry tactic in which one element of a unit remains in a position of cover and observation — the overwatch position — ready to provide covering fire, while another element advances ("bounds") to the next position. The roles then reverse, and the movement repeats.

Technique

The technique ensures that part of the unit is always stationary, set, and able to engage the enemy, protecting the element that is exposed while moving. Bounds are kept short enough that the moving element remains within effective supporting range of the overwatch element's weapons. Variants include successive bounds, where both elements move to the same line in turn, and alternate bounds, where elements pass through each other.

Use

Bounding overwatch is employed from the fireteam and squad level up to larger formations, including armoured vehicles, especially when contact with the enemy is expected. It is slower than travelling overwatch, in which both elements move but at different speeds, but provides greater security.
