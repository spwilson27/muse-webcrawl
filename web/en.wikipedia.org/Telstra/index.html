Telstra

Telstra Group Limited
Type: Public (ASX: TLS)
Industry: Telecommunications
Headquarters: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
Founded: 1975 (as Telecom Australia)

Telstra Group Limited is an Australian telecommunications company that builds and operates telecommunications networks and markets related products and services. It is Australia's largest telecommunications company by market share and operates the country's largest mobile network. Telstra is listed on the Australian Securities Exchange and is a constituent of the S&P/ASX 20 index.

History

Australia's postal and telecommunications services were originally administered by the Postmaster-General's Department. In 1975, the functions were split, creating the Australian Telecommunications Commission, trading as Telecom Australia, and Australia Post. Telecom Australia rebranded as Telstra in 1993, initially for its international operations. The Australian government began privatizing Telstra in stages from 1997, in tranches commonly known as T1, T2 and T3, and the company became fully privatized by 2011 (with the government's remaining stake sold into the Future Fund and progressively divested).

During the rollout of the National Broadband Network (NBN), Telstra agreed in 2010–2011 to progressively decommission its copper access network and cable broadband network in NBN areas and migrate customers to the NBN, in exchange for payments valued at about A$11 billion.

Operations

Telstra provides fixed-line and mobile telephony, mobile devices, internet access, wholesale services, and pay television through its part-ownership of Foxtel (with News Corp Australia). Its retail brands have included Telstra, Belong (a budget brand launched in 2013), and previously Boost Mobile licensing arrangements. Telstra's mobile network covers the largest geographic area of any Australian carrier, a point of emphasis in its marketing in regional and rural areas. The company also operates international submarine cable networks and data centres through Telstra International.

Corporate affairs

Telstra's headquarters are at the Telstra Corporate Centre in Melbourne. In 2022 the group completed a legal restructure splitting the company into Telstra Group and subsidiaries including InfraCo, which holds its infrastructure assets. Successive chief executives have included David Thodey (2009–2015), Andy Penn (2015–2022), and Vicki Brady, who became CEO in September 2022.
