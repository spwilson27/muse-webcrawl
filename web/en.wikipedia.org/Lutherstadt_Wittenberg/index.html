Lutherstadt Wittenberg

State: Saxony-Anhalt, Germany
District: Wittenberg
Population: about 45,000

Lutherstadt Wittenberg is a town in Saxony-Anhalt, Germany, on the River Elbe. It is famed as the centre of the Protestant Reformation: Martin Luther is said to have posted his Ninety-five Theses on the door of the Castle Church in 1517.

History

First mentioned in 1180, Wittenberg became a residence of the Electors of Saxony. Its university, founded in 1502, employed Luther and Philipp Melanchthon and taught the young prince in Shakespeare's Hamlet in fiction. The Lutherhaus, Melanchthonhaus, Town Church and Castle Church are UNESCO World Heritage Sites as part of the Luther Memorials.
