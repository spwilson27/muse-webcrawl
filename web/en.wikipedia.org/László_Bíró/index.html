László Bíró

László József Bíró
Born: 29 September 1899, Budapest, Hungary
Died: 24 October 1985, Buenos Aires, Argentina
Known for: inventing the modern ballpoint pen

László József Bíró (1899–1985) was a Hungarian-Argentine inventor who patented the first commercially successful ballpoint pen. In Britain the pen is still commonly called a "biro" after him.

Invention

Working as a journalist, Bíró noticed that newspaper ink dried quickly and smudge-free. With his brother György, a chemist, he developed a viscous ink delivered by a freely rotating ball in a socket, filing patents in the 1930s and a key patent in Argentina in 1943 after emigrating there.

Commercialisation

The pen was licensed for aircrew use by the Royal Air Force, and post-war companies such as Eversharp and Bic made the ballpoint a mass-market product. Bíró's birthday, 29 September, is celebrated in Argentina as Inventor's Day.
