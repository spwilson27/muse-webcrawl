Ballade (forme fixe)

The ballade is one of the three formes fixes of medieval and Renaissance French poetry and music, alongside the rondeau and the virelai. It was the most popular of the fixed forms in the 14th and 15th centuries and is closely associated with Guillaume de Machaut.

Form

A literary ballade typically consists of three stanzas of seven or eight lines, each ending with the same refrain line, followed by a short envoi addressed to a patron or prince. In music, the ballade sets the poetic form with a structure of repeated musical sections corresponding to the stanza pattern.

Composers and poets

Machaut composed numerous musical ballades; notable literary practitioners include Eustache Deschamps, Christine de Pizan, and François Villon, whose "Ballade des pendus" is among the most famous examples.
