Belcarra

Belcarra is a village on the shore of Indian Arm, a side inlet of Burrard Inlet, in British Columbia, Canada, within Metro Vancouver. It is a predominantly residential community accessible by road through Port Moody or by water.

History

The area is the traditional territory of the Tsleil-Waututh Nation. The village was incorporated in 1979 and takes its name from Belcarra in Ireland.

Geography and recreation

Belcarra Regional Park, including Belcarra Bay and the trail to Jug Island Beach, is a popular destination for picnicking, kayaking and hiking, while nearby White Pine Beach lies on Sasamat Lake.
