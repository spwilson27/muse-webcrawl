Uummannaq

Uummannaq
Country: Greenland
Municipality: Avannaata
Population: ~1,400
Named for: The heart-shaped mountain

Uummannaq is a town on Uummannaq Island in northwestern Greenland, in the Avannaata municipality. The town takes its name—"heart-like" in Greenlandic—from the distinctive heart-shaped mountain that rises steeply behind it.

History and life

Founded as a Danish colony in the 18th century, Uummannaq served as a trading and hunting post. The area is known for traditional Inuit culture, dog sledding, and a children's home famous for its music programme, featured in international media. The mummies of Qilakitsoq, an important Inuit archaeological find, were discovered nearby in 1972.

Economy

Fishing (notably Greenland halibut) and hunting underpin the economy, with tourism growing in importance.
