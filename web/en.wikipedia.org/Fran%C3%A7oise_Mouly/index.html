Françoise Mouly

Françoise Mouly (born 24 October 1955) is a French-born American designer, editor and publisher. She is best known as the co-founder, with her husband Art Spiegelman, of the avant-garde comics anthology RAW (1980–1991), and as the art editor of The New Yorker since 1993, where she oversees the magazine's covers. She is also the founder of TOON Books, a publisher of hardcover comics for early readers.

Career

Mouly studied architecture in Paris before moving to New York in 1974. With Spiegelman she founded RAW, which serialised Spiegelman's Maus and introduced many European and alternative cartoonists to American readers. As art editor of The New Yorker, she has commissioned thousands of covers, including memorable post-9/11 and political covers, bringing numerous cartoonists and illustrators into the magazine's pages.

TOON Books

In 2008 Mouly launched TOON Books to publish high-quality comics designed for young children learning to read, with contributions from artists including Spiegelman, Renée French and Eleanor Davis.
