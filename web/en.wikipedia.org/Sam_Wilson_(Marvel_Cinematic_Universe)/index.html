Sam Wilson (Marvel Cinematic Universe)

Sam Wilson, portrayed by Anthony Mackie, as the Falcon and later Captain America.

Samuel Thomas "Sam" Wilson is a fictional character portrayed by Anthony Mackie in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) media franchise, based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. He is initially known by the alias the Falcon and later succeeds Steve Rogers as Captain America.

Appearances

Wilson debuted in Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014), as a former US Air Force pararescueman who flies with an experimental winged jetpack and befriends Steve Rogers. He subsequently appeared in Avengers: Age of Ultron, Ant-Man, Captain America: Civil War, Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, in which the elderly Rogers passes his shield to Wilson.

Captain America

The Disney+ series The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021) follows Wilson wrestling with the legacy of the shield — including the government appointing John Walker as a replacement Captain America — before ultimately accepting the mantle. Wilson headlines as Captain America in the film Captain America: Brave New World (2025), wearing a suit incorporating Wakandan technology.

Characterisation

Wilson is depicted as empathetic and grounded, working as a counsellor for veterans, and his storyline engages with race, patriotism and the meaning of the Captain America symbol in modern America.
