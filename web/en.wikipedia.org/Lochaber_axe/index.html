Lochaber axe

The Lochaber axe is a type of Scottish polearm originating in the Lochaber region of the Highlands. It was used by Scottish infantry from roughly the 16th to the 18th century.

Description

The weapon consists of a shaft about 1.5 to 1.8 metres long, topped by a broad, cleaver-like blade, often with a fluted or curved edge, and a hook at the back that could be used to pull riders from horses or catch at opponents. Its design may derive from earlier agricultural tools and from continental polearms such as the bardiche and voulge.

Use

Lochaber axes were carried by Highland clansmen and by town and burgh guards in Scotland. Edinburgh's city guard was famously equipped with them, and the weapon survived in ceremonial use long after it left the battlefield following the Jacobite era.
