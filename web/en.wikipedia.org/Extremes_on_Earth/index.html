Extremes on Earth

This article lists extreme locations and records on Earth, including the highest and lowest points, temperature records, and the most remote places on the planet.

Elevation

The highest point above sea level is the summit of Mount Everest at 8,848.86 m. The lowest land point is the shore of the Dead Sea, about 430 m below sea level, while the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench is the deepest point in the oceans at roughly 10,935 m below sea level.

Temperature

The highest reliably recorded air temperature is 56.7 °C at Furnace Creek Ranch, Death Valley, California (1913). The lowest is −89.2 °C at Vostok Station, Antarctica (1983).

Remoteness

Point Nemo in the South Pacific is the oceanic point farthest from land; the Eurasian pole of inaccessibility lies in northwestern China.
