Lorimer Burst

The Lorimer Burst (FRB 010724) was the first fast radio burst (FRB) to be discovered. It was found in 2007 by astrophysicist Duncan Lorimer and his student David Narkevic while re-analysing archival data from the Parkes radio telescope in Australia, recorded on 24 July 2001.

The event was a bright, dispersed radio pulse lasting less than 5 milliseconds, with a dispersion measure far exceeding what could be produced within the Milky Way, indicating an extragalactic origin at a distance of up to several billion light-years and an enormous implied energy release. Its discovery opened the field of FRB astronomy; hundreds have since been detected, with sources linked to magnetars and other extreme astrophysical environments.
