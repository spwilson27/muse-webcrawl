Baltic Exchange

The Baltic Exchange is a membership organisation for the maritime industry based in London, best known for publishing the Baltic Dry Index and other freight market indices used worldwide as benchmarks for shipping rates. Founded in the mid-18th century among shipowners and merchants meeting in London coffee houses, it takes its name from the Baltic trade.

Indices

The Baltic Dry Index (BDI), launched in 1985, tracks rates for dry bulk carriers across Capesize, Panamax, and Supramax segments and is watched as a bellwether of global trade. The Exchange also publishes tanker, gas, and container assessments.

History

The Exchange's historic building at 24–28 St Mary Axe was destroyed by an IRA bomb in 1992 — the site is now occupied by 30 St Mary Axe ("the Gherkin"). In 2016 the Baltic Exchange was acquired by the Singapore Exchange (SGX).
