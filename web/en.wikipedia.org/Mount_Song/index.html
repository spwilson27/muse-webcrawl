Mount Song

Mount Song (Song Shan) is a mountain in Henan Province, China, near Dengfeng, and one of the Five Great Mountains of China — the central mountain of the five. Its highest peak, Junji Peak, rises to about 1,512 m.

Sites

The mountain's foothills are dense with historic monuments: the Shaolin Monastery, famed as the birthplace of Chan (Zen) Buddhism and Shaolin kung fu; the Songyue Pagoda (523 CE), among China's oldest brick pagodas; the Zhongyue Temple, a major Taoist temple; and some of China's oldest astronomical observatories. The "Historic Monuments of Dengfeng in 'The Centre of Heaven and Earth'" were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2010.
