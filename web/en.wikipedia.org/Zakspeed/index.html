Zakspeed is a German motor racing team and engineering company founded in 1968 by Erich Zakowski in Niederzissen, near the Nürburgring. It is best known for its close association with Ford in European touring car and sports car racing, and for its spell as a Formula One constructor in the late 1980s.

Touring and sports cars

Zakspeed rose to prominence in the 1970s running Ford Escorts and the spectacular turbocharged Capri in the Deutsche Rennsport Meisterschaft, winning numerous races and championships. It later campaigned the Ford C100 in Group C sports car racing and ran factory-backed programmes in DTM with Ford and later Opel, as well as building cars such as the Chrysler Viper GTS-R programme for Oreca in the late 1990s.

Formula One

Zakspeed entered Formula One in 1985 as one of the few teams building both its own chassis and its own turbocharged engine — a 1.5-litre four-cylinder unit derived from a Ford block. Results were modest; the team's best finish was fifth place for Martin Brundle at the 1987 San Marino Grand Prix. After the turbo era ended, underpowered cars led to repeated failures to pre-qualify in 1989, and Zakspeed withdrew at the end of that season.

Later activities

Under Zakowski's son Peter, the company continued in series including Superleague Formula and the NASCAR Whelen Euro Series, alongside automotive engineering and event work at the Nürburgring.
