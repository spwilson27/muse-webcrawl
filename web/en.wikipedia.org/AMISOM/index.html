AMISOM

Full nameAfrican Union Mission in Somalia

The African Union Mission in Somalia (AMISOM) was a peacekeeping mission operated by the African Union, with the approval of the United Nations Security Council, in Somalia from 2007 until 2022.

Mandate and deployment

AMISOM was created in January 2007 to support Somalia's transitional government, protect key installations, and fight the insurgent group al-Shabaab. Ugandan troops formed the first contingent; Burundi, Kenya, Ethiopia, Djibouti, and Sierra Leone later contributed forces, and the mission grew to more than 20,000 personnel.

Transition

AMISOM forces helped government troops retake Mogadishu in 2011 and other major towns, though al-Shabaab remained active. On 1 April 2022 AMISOM was reconfigured as the African Union Transition Mission in Somalia (ATMIS), with a phased drawdown plan.
