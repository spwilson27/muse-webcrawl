Kansas City Southern Railway
Reporting mark: KCS
Headquarters: Kansas City, Missouri
Founded: 1887 (as Kansas City Suburban Belt Railway)
Successor: Canadian Pacific Kansas City (2023)

The Kansas City Southern Railway Company (reporting mark KCS) was an American Class I railroad owned by Kansas City Southern. Founded in 1887, it operated a network linking the central United States with ports on the Gulf of Mexico and, through its Mexican subsidiary, with ports on both coasts of Mexico.

History

The line originated with Arthur E. Stilwell's plan to build a railroad from Kansas City south to the Gulf of Mexico at Port Arthur, Texas. Over the twentieth century KCS grew through acquisitions including the Louisiana and Arkansas Railway.

Merger

In 2021 Canadian Pacific Railway agreed to acquire Kansas City Southern; after regulatory review the merger closed in April 2023, creating Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC), the first single-line railway connecting Canada, the United States and Mexico. See also Canadian Pacific Kansas City.
