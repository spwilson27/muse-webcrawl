Karl Wendlinger

Karl Wendlinger
Born: 20 December 1968, Kufstein, Austria
Nationality: Austrian
Formula One: 1991–1995 (March, Sauber)
Best finish: 4th (1992 Canadian GP)

Karl Wendlinger (born 1968) is an Austrian former racing driver who competed in Formula One between 1991 and 1995. A protégé of Mercedes-Benz's junior programme alongside Michael Schumacher and Heinz-Harald Frentzen, he first raced in sports cars before stepping up to Formula One with March.

Sauber and Monaco accident

Wendlinger joined the new Sauber team in 1993. At the 1994 Monaco Grand Prix he crashed heavily at the Nouvelle Chicane during practice and was left in a coma for several weeks. He returned to racing in 1995 but never fully regained his earlier form in Formula One.

Later career

He went on to a successful career in sports car and touring car racing, winning the FIA GT Championship in 1999 and the German Formula Three Championship in 1989. See also Sauber.
