Wind River Range

LocationWestern Wyoming, United States
Highest peakGannett Peak, 13,810 ft (4,209 m)
Lengthabout 100 miles (160 km)
Parent rangeRocky Mountains

The Wind River Range is a mountain range of the Rocky Mountains in western Wyoming, United States, running roughly northwest to southeast for about 100 miles (160 km). It contains Gannett Peak, the highest point in Wyoming, and more than 40 peaks above 13,000 feet.

Geography

The range forms part of the Continental Divide and holds some of the largest glaciers in the American Rockies, including Gannett Glacier and Dinwoody Glacier. Much of the range is protected in the Bridger-Teton and Shoshone National Forests and three wilderness areas. The Cirque of the Towers is a celebrated destination for climbers and backpackers, and the range is home to grizzly bears, moose, and high alpine lakes.
