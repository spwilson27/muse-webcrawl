Afghanistan, officially the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan, is a landlocked country located at the crossroads of Central Asia and South Asia. It is bordered by Pakistan to the east and south, Iran to the west, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan and Tajikistan to the north, and China to the northeast. Occupying about 652,867 square kilometres, the country is predominantly mountainous, dominated by the Hindu Kush range.

History

Human habitation in Afghanistan dates back to the Middle Paleolithic era. The region has been a crossroads of empires: it formed part of the Achaemenid Empire, was conquered by Alexander the Great, and later saw Greco-Bactrian, Kushan, Hephthalite, and Islamic influences. In 1747, Ahmad Shah Durrani founded the Durrani Empire, often regarded as the founding of the modern Afghan state.

During the 19th century, Afghanistan became a buffer state between the British and Russian empires in the "Great Game". The country gained independence from British influence in 1919 under King Amanullah Khan. A monarchy lasted until 1973, followed by republics, a communist period, the Soviet–Afghan War (1979–1989), civil war, and Taliban rule from 1996 to 2001. After the US-led invasion of 2001, the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan was established, but the Taliban returned to power in August 2021.

Geography

Afghanistan's terrain consists mostly of rugged mountains, with plains in the north and southwest. The Amu Darya forms part of the northern border, and the Wakhan Corridor extends to China in the northeast. The climate is arid to semi-arid, with cold winters and hot summers.

Demographics

The population comprises numerous ethnic groups, including Pashtuns, Tajiks, Hazaras, Uzbeks, Aimaks, Turkmens and Baloch. Pashto and Dari (Afghan Persian) are the official languages; Islam is the predominant religion, professed by the vast majority of the population.

Economy

Afghanistan's economy is largely agricultural, with wheat, fruits, nuts and livestock as important products. The country is rich in natural resources, including lithium, copper, iron ore and rare-earth elements, though extraction remains limited.
