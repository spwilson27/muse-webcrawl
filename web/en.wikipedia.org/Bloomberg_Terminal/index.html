Bloomberg Terminal

Bloomberg Terminal
Developer: Bloomberg L.P.
Released: 1982
Users: Financial professionals

The Bloomberg Terminal is a computer software system provided by Bloomberg L.P. that enables professionals in finance and other industries to access real-time market data, news, prices and analytics, and to trade via its electronic platform.

History

Launched in 1982 as the Bloomberg Market Master terminal, it grew into the industry's dominant workstation, identifiable by its dual monitors and distinctive colour-coded keyboard.

Business

Terminals are leased on subscription, historically around $2,000 per month per user, and account for the majority of Bloomberg L.P.'s revenue, funding its news operation.
