Audemars Piguet

Founded: 1875
Founders: Jules Louis Audemars and Edward Auguste Piguet
Headquarters: Le Brassus, Switzerland

Audemars Piguet is a Swiss manufacturer of luxury mechanical watches founded in 1875 by Jules Louis Audemars and Edward Auguste Piguet in Le Brassus, in the Vallée de Joux. It remains one of the few major Swiss watch houses still owned by its founding families.

History

The company became known for complicated movements, including minute repeaters and perpetual calendars. In 1972 it launched the Royal Oak, designed by Gérald Genta, the first luxury steel sports watch, whose octagonal bezel and integrated bracelet became an icon of watch design.

Products

Collections include the Royal Oak, Royal Oak Offshore, Millenary and Code 11.59. The manufacture is noted for hand finishing and for producing its movements in-house.
