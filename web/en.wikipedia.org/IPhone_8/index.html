The iPhone 8 and iPhone 8 Plus are smartphones designed, developed, and marketed by Apple Inc. They were announced on 12 September 2017 at the Steve Jobs Theater in Apple Park, alongside the iPhone X, and released on 22 September 2017, succeeding the iPhone 7 and iPhone 7 Plus.

The design of the iPhone 8 was largely a refinement of the iPhone 6-era form factor, with the addition of a glass back to enable Qi inductive wireless charging. Internally, it added the A11 Bionic system-on-chip with a "Neural Engine", improved cameras with 4K video at 60 fps and 1080p slow motion at 240 fps, and a True Tone Retina HD display.

Reception

Reviewers praised the performance, cameras and wireless charging but criticized the dated design relative to the edge-to-edge iPhone X released weeks later. The iPhone 8 was nonetheless a strong seller, especially at its price point. It was discontinued in April 2020 following the announcement of the second-generation iPhone SE, which reused the 8's chassis with updated internals.
