South Pars (Persian: پارس جنوبی) is the Iranian portion of the South Pars/North Dome field, the world's largest natural gas field, located in the Persian Gulf. The field is shared between Iran and Qatar; the Qatari portion is known as the North Dome or North Field. The Iranian sector lies offshore from the port of Assaluyeh in Bushehr Province.

Development

Development of South Pars has proceeded in numbered phases, each typically comprising offshore platforms, pipelines to shore, and onshore gas processing trains at Assaluyeh. The field also yields large volumes of gas condensate. International sanctions on Iran repeatedly delayed phases and led foreign partners to withdraw, leaving development largely to Iranian firms.

Reserves

The combined field holds estimated recoverable reserves on the order of 1,800 trillion cubic feet of natural gas, making it by far the largest gas accumulation known. The Iranian share is commonly estimated at about 14 trillion cubic metres of gas in place, along with billions of barrels of condensate.
