Trade association

A trade association, also known as an industry trade group, business association, sector association, or industry body, is an organisation founded and funded by businesses that operate in a specific industry. An industry trade association participates in public relations activities such as advertising, education, publishing, lobbying, and political donations, but its focus is generally collaboration between companies and standardisation.

Functions

Trade associations collect and publish industry statistics, set technical standards, run trade shows and conferences, provide training and certification, and represent the industry before legislators and regulators. Many also develop codes of practice and coordinate responses to public issues affecting the sector.

History

Modern trade associations descend in part from medieval guilds and nineteenth-century chambers of commerce. In the United States, thousands of national associations are based in Washington, D.C., and similar umbrella bodies exist in most industrialised countries.

Criticism

Critics note that associations can facilitate lobbying against regulation, and competition authorities watch for any coordination that shades into price-fixing or market allocation, which is illegal in most jurisdictions.
