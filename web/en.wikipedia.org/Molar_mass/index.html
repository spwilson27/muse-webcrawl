Molar mass

In chemistry, the molar mass (M) of a chemical compound is the mass of a sample divided by the amount of substance in that sample, measured in moles. It is expressed in g/mol.

Molar mass

SI unit: kg/mol (commonly g/mol)
Symbol: M

Calculation

Molar mass is the sum of the relative atomic masses of constituent atoms from the periodic table, e.g. water H2O ≈ 18.015 g/mol. It links laboratory masses to the mole: n = m / M. Molar mass combined with density gives molar volume.

Measurement

Mass spectrometry gives precise molecular masses; colligative properties and vapour density provide classical estimates.
