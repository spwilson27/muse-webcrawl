Pierre Morad Omidyar (born 21 June 1967) is a French-born American billionaire entrepreneur and philanthropist of Iranian descent, best known as the founder of the online auction site eBay.

eBay

Omidyar, a computer programmer, launched "Auction Web" over the Labor Day weekend in 1995 as one part of his personal website; the first item sold was a broken laser pointer. The site grew explosively, was renamed eBay in 1997, and went public in 1998, making Omidyar a billionaire at age 31. He served as the company's chairman until 2015.

Philanthropy and media

With his wife Pam, Omidyar founded the Omidyar Network in 2004, an investment and philanthropic organization supporting microfinance, property rights, and civic technology. In 2013 he founded First Look Media, publisher of The Intercept, after considering purchasing The Washington Post. He has signed the Giving Pledge, committing most of his wealth to charitable causes.
