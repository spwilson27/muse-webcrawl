Estadio Corona is a football stadium in Torreón, Coahuila, Mexico. It is the home of Club Santos Laguna of Liga MX and forms the centerpiece of the Territorio Santos Modelo, a large sports, entertainment, and religious complex. The stadium is named for its sponsor, the Corona beer brand of Grupo Modelo.

History

The stadium replaced the original Estadio Corona (built in 1970) as part of an ambitious complex inaugurated on 11 November 2009. The opening event featured a friendly between Santos Laguna and Santos FC of Brazil, attended by Pelé, who performed the ceremonial kickoff. Facilities in the wider complex include training pitches, the club's offices, a parish church, restaurants, and a hotel.

Facilities

The stadium holds about 30,000 spectators with notably steep stands close to the pitch, generating an intense atmosphere; it is often called "La Casa del Dolor Ajeno" (The House of Others' Pain) in club lore, reflecting Santos' strong home record. In 2011 the stadium was the scene of a shooting outside the venue during a match against Morelia, which caused the game to be abandoned amid a stampede; no one inside was reported injured, and the incident drew international attention to security during Mexico's drug-war years.

International matches

Estadio Corona has hosted matches of the Mexico national team and was a venue for the 2011 FIFA U-17 World Cup, held in Mexico.
