uKhahlamba-Drakensberg Park

LocationKwaZulu-Natal, South Africa
Areaabout 2,428 km²
StatusUNESCO World Heritage Site (2000)

The uKhahlamba-Drakensberg Park is a protected area in the Drakensberg mountains of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, along the border with Lesotho. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2000 as a mixed cultural and natural site (the Maloti-Drakensberg Park).

Nature

The park protects dramatic basalt escarpments, golden sandstone cliffs, deep valleys and high grasslands rising to over 3,400 metres. It is home to eland, oribi, mountain reedbuck and the bearded vulture (lammergeier), as well as many endemic plants.

Rock art

The park contains the largest and most concentrated group of rock paintings in Africa south of the Sahara, some 40,000 individual images in hundreds of shelters, created by the San people over about 4,000 years.
