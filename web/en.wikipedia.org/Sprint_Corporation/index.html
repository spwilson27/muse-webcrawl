Sprint Corporation was an American telecommunications company. Before its acquisition by T-Mobile US on April 1, 2020, it was the fourth-largest mobile network operator in the United States, serving 54.3 million customers as of June 30, 2019.

History

The company traced its origins to the Brown Telephone Company founded in 1899 in Abilene, Kansas. The name Sprint was an acronym for "Southern Pacific Railroad Internal Networking Telephony", derived from its origins with the Southern Pacific railroad. Sprint became a major long-distance carrier in the 1980s, merged with Nextel Communications in 2005, and was acquired by Japan's SoftBank in 2013.

Merger with T-Mobile

After a merger announced in 2018 and approved by regulators, Sprint merged into T-Mobile US in 2020; the Sprint brand was retired and its network was shut down in stages by 2022.
