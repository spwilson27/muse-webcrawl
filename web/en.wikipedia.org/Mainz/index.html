Mainz (German: [maɪnts]) is the capital and largest city of the German state of Rhineland-Palatinate, with around 220,000 inhabitants. The city lies on the left (west) bank of the River Rhine, opposite the confluence with the River Main and the city of Wiesbaden. Mainz was founded by the Romans in the late 1st century BCE as the legionary fortress of Mogontiacum and became one of the most important cities north of the Alps. It is famous as the home of Johannes Gutenberg, inventor of printing with movable type in Europe, and for its thousand-year-old cathedral and exuberant carnival celebrations.

History

Roman Mogontiacum was established around 13/12 BCE by Drusus as a military base guarding the Rhine frontier and the route into Germania. In the Middle Ages Mainz became a powerful archbishopric: its archbishops were archchancellors of the Holy Roman Empire and among the prince-electors. Saint Boniface, the "Apostle of the Germans", became bishop of Mainz in 745. In the 1450s Gutenberg's workshop in Mainz produced the Gutenberg Bible, launching the European printing revolution; the city remained a major printing centre. Mainz's medieval Jewish community, one of the ShUM cities, was a leading centre of Ashkenazi scholarship before suffering massacres during the Crusades and later persecutions. The city endured French revolutionary and Napoleonic occupation, heavy bombing in the Second World War — which destroyed much of the old town — and subsequent rebuilding; it hosted French occupation forces until 1949 and beyond as a garrison town.

Sights and culture

Mainz Cathedral (Mainzer Dom), consecrated in 1009 and repeatedly rebuilt, dominates the market square; nearby stands the Gutenberg Museum with two original Gutenberg Bibles. The city is celebrated for its Rose Monday carnival parade, one of Germany's largest. The broadcaster ZDF is headquartered in Mainz, and the pharmaceutical company BioNTech, developer with Pfizer of a COVID-19 vaccine, was founded there in 2008. The city's football club, 1. FSV Mainz 05, long associated with manager Jürgen Klopp, plays in the Bundesliga.

Wine

Mainz is a member of the Great Wine Capitals network and gateway to Rheinhessen, Germany's largest wine-producing region, known for Riesling and Silvaner.
