Lift net


Lift nets are a method of fishing using net meshes which are submerged and then lifted vertically out of the water to trap fish above them.

Lift nets, also called lever nets, are a method of fishing using square net meshes which are submerged at a certain depth and then lifted vertically out of the water as fish pass over them. Fish are attracted over the net, often by lights or bait, and are trapped when the net is raised.

Types

Small hand lift nets are operated from banks and boats; large shore-operated lift nets are mounted on fixed frames or platforms; and boat-operated lift nets, such as the Vietnamese and Indonesian bagan, are suspended from outriggers and used at night with lamps to catch squid and small pelagic fish.

History

Lift nets have been used traditionally worldwide since prehistoric times. The Chinese and Indonesian fisheries developed particularly elaborate forms, and the method remains common in artisanal fisheries across Asia.
