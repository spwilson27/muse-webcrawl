Bhubaneswar

Bhubaneswar is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Odisha, in eastern India. Nicknamed the "Temple City", it is famed for the hundreds of Hindu temples of its old town, built in the Kalinga style between the 7th and 13th centuries, and it forms a "golden triangle" of heritage tourism with Puri and Konark.

History

The area has a history of more than 3,000 years; nearby Dhauli is associated with the Kalinga War and an edict of the emperor Ashoka, and the twin hills of Khandagiri and Udayagiri contain Jain rock-cut caves of the 2nd century BCE. The city's greatest temple, the Lingaraja Temple dedicated to Shiva, dates to the 11th century. In 1948 Bhubaneswar replaced Cuttack as the capital of Odisha, and the German architect Otto Königsberger prepared the plan for the modern city, one of the first planned cities of independent India.

Modern city

Bhubaneswar has grown into a centre for education and information technology, hosting institutions such as IIT Bhubaneswar, AIIMS Bhubaneswar and numerous universities, alongside IT parks. It regularly ranks among India's leading "smart city" projects and hosted the 2018 Men's Hockey World Cup. Its population is around one million.
