Hales Trophy

The Hales Trophy is a four-foot-high silver trophy awarded to the fastest regular passenger vessel crossing the Atlantic Ocean. It was commissioned in 1933 by Harold K. Hales, a British shipowner and Member of Parliament, to formalise the traditional competition for the Blue Riband of the North Atlantic.

History

Early winners included the Italian liner Rex (1933), the French Normandie (1935), and Cunard's Queen Mary, which held the record from 1938. The last ocean liner to win, the SS United States in 1952, set a crossing record that has never been beaten by a conventional liner. Since then the trophy has passed among fast catamaran ferries, including Hoverspeed Great Britain (1990) and the Incat-built Catalonia and Cat-Link V in the late 1990s.
