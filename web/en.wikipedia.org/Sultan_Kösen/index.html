Sultan Kösen

Sultan Kösen, the tallest living man, at 251 cm (8 ft 3 in).

Sultan Kösen (born 10 December 1982) is a Turkish farmer who holds the Guinness World Record for tallest living male at 251 cm (8 ft 2.82 in). His growth resulted from the condition acromegaly, caused by a tumour affecting his pituitary gland. Because of his condition, he uses crutches to walk.

Life

Kösen, of Kurdish ethnicity, was born in Alibey, a village near Mardin in southeastern Turkey, and grew up in an ordinary farming family; his siblings are of normal height. His rapid growth began around age ten. He was first measured by Guinness World Records in 2009 at 246.5 cm, taking the title from Bao Xishun, and re-measured in 2011 at his full height of 251 cm after treatment in the United States, including gamma-knife surgery on the tumour in 2010 and medication, succeeded in stabilising his growth in 2012.

He also holds records for the largest hands (28.5 cm) and among the largest feet of a living person. Kösen has used his fame to travel widely and speak publicly; he married Merve Dibo in 2013 (they later divorced) and has appeared in film and at record-holder gatherings, including meetings with the world's shortest man, Chandra Bahadur Dangi, in 2014.
