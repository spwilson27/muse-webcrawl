Takoyaki

Takoyaki
OriginOsaka, Japan
TypeSnack
Main ingredientsBatter, octopus, tempura scraps, pickled ginger, green onion

Takoyaki is a ball-shaped Japanese snack made of a wheat flour-based batter and cooked in a special moulded pan. It is typically filled with minced or diced octopus (tako), tempura scraps, pickled ginger and green onion.

Preparation

Batter is poured into the hemispherical moulds of a cast-iron takoyaki pan; a piece of octopus is placed in each, and the balls are turned with picks until uniformly round and crisp outside. They are served brushed with takoyaki sauce (similar to Worcestershire sauce), mayonnaise, aonori seaweed flakes and bonito flakes.

History

Takoyaki was created in Osaka in the 1930s by street vendor Tomekichi Endo, evolving from earlier snacks such as akashiyaki and rajioyaki. It became an emblematic Osaka street food, sold at festivals and specialist shops throughout Japan and abroad.
