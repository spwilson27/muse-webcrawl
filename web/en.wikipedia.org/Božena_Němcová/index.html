Božena Němcová

Božena Němcová (1820–1862) was a Czech writer of the Czech National Revival movement, considered one of the founders of modern Czech prose. Her best-known work is the novel The Grandmother (Babička, 1855), a beloved classic depicting rural life in north-eastern Bohemia through the figure of an wise, kindly grandmother.

Born Barbora Panklová, she grew up partly at Ratibořice near the castle of the Duchess of Sagan, whose household inspired elements of her fiction. Unhappily married to Josef Němec, a customs official, she lived much of her life in poverty while writing stories, fairy tales, and ethnographic sketches of Czech and Slovak folk culture.

Němcová also wrote the novellas Divá Bára and Karla, and her collected fairy tales remain staples of Czech children's literature. She died in Prague in 1862, aged 41.
