Shah Rukh Khan (born 2 November 1965), also known by the initials SRK and popularly as the "King of Bollywood" or "King Khan", is an Indian actor and film producer who works in Hindi cinema. One of the most successful film stars in the world by audience size and earnings, he has appeared in more than 100 films and won numerous awards, including 14 Filmfare Awards. The Government of India honoured him with the Padma Shri in 2005, and France awarded him the Legion of Honour in 2014.

Early life and career beginnings

Khan was born in New Delhi to Mir Taj Mohammed Khan, an independence activist from Peshawar, and Lateef Fatima. He studied at St. Columba's School and Hansraj College, Delhi University, and began his career on television with serials such as Fauji (1989) and Circus. He made his film debut in 1992 with Deewana, and gained wide attention playing anti-hero roles in Baazigar and Darr (both 1993).

Rise to stardom

Khan's star-making romantic role came in Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995), one of the longest-running films in Indian cinema history, which established him as the leading romantic hero of his generation. A string of defining hits followed, including Dil To Pagal Hai (1997), Kuch Kuch Hota Hai (1998), Devdas (2002), Kal Ho Naa Ho (2003), Veer-Zaara (2004) and Chak De! India (2007). He won widespread critical acclaim for playing a man with Asperger syndrome in My Name Is Khan (2010).

Later career and production

After a run of mixed commercial results in the 2010s and a hiatus following Zero (2018), Khan returned in 2023 with the action films Pathaan and Jawan, both among the highest-grossing Indian films ever made, followed by the comedy-drama Dunki. With his wife Gauri Khan he co-founded the production company Red Chillies Entertainment in 2002, which has produced many of his films and major visual-effects work.

Business ventures

Khan is co-owner of the Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) franchise of the Indian Premier League, one of the most valuable teams in world cricket, and of affiliated T20 franchises in other leagues. Media outlets have repeatedly described him as among the wealthiest actors in the world. He has been a prolific brand endorser and stage-show performer, and his sea-facing Mumbai residence, Mannat, is a well-known landmark for fans.
