List of Allied World War II conferences

The List of Allied World War II conferences enumerates the strategic meetings of the Allied powers between 1941 and 1945, at which leaders and chiefs of staff co-ordinated military strategy and post-war planning.

Major conferences

  • Atlantic Conference, Placentia Bay, 1941 — Atlantic Charter
  • Casablanca Conference, 1943 — unconditional surrender policy
  • Cairo and Tehran Conferences, 1943
  • Bretton Woods and Dumbarton Oaks, 1944
  • Yalta Conference, February 1945
  • Potsdam Conference, July–August 1945

Many meetings were known by code names such as Arcadia, Trident, Quadrant, Octagon and Terminal, and involved Franklin D. Roosevelt, Winston Churchill and Joseph Stalin, the "Big Three", alongside their combined chiefs of staff.
