Eyjafjallajökull

Eyjafjallajökull is one of the smaller ice caps of Iceland, north of Skógar and west of Mýrdalsjökull. The ice cap covers the caldera of a volcano with a summit elevation of 1,651 metres (5,417 ft). The volcano has erupted relatively frequently since the Last Glacial Period, most recently in 2010.

Eyjafjallajökull
Elevation: 1,651 m
Location: Southern Region, Iceland
Last eruption: 2010

2010 eruption

The 2010 eruptions caused enormous disruption to air travel across Europe: between 15 and 23 April, ash from the explosive phase closed airspace over much of northern and western Europe, cancelling over 100,000 flights and stranding millions of passengers.

Name

The name means "island-mountain glacier" and is noted internationally for its difficulty of pronunciation for non-Icelandic speakers.
