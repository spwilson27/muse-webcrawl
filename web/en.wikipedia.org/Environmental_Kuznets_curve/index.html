Environmental Kuznets curve

The environmental Kuznets curve (EKC) hypothesises an inverted U-shaped relationship between economic development and environmental degradation: pollution first rises with income, then falls after a turning point.

Origins

The idea adapts Simon Kuznets's curve for inequality and was popularised by studies in the early 1990s, notably Grossman and Krueger's work related to NAFTA.

Evidence and criticism

Evidence is mixed: some local air pollutants show the pattern, while carbon dioxide emissions generally have not declined with income in the same way. Critics point to outsourcing of pollution, measurement issues and the risk of irreversible damage before any turning point.
