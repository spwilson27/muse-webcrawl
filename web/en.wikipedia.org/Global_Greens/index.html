The Global Greens is an international network of political parties and movements that work to implement the Global Greens Charter, a statement of green politics adopted at the first Global Greens Congress in Canberra, Australia, in 2001. Member parties belong to four regional federations: the European Green Party, the Federation of Green Parties of Africa, the Federation of the Green Parties of the Americas, and the Asia-Pacific Greens Federation.

Charter

The Global Greens Charter sets out six core principles: ecological wisdom, social justice, participatory democracy, nonviolence, sustainability, and respect for diversity. It commits member parties to action on climate change, peace, human rights and democratic reform. The Charter was substantially revised at the 2012 congress in Dakar.

Congresses

Global Greens congresses have been held in Canberra (2001), São Paulo (2008), Dakar (2012) and Liverpool (2017, held jointly with the European Green Party). Between congresses the network is coordinated by a committee drawn from the regional federations and by a small secretariat.
