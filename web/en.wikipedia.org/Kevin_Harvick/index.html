Kevin Harvick

Kevin Michael Harvick (born December 8, 1975) is an American former professional stock car racing driver and current racing commentator. He competed full-time in the NASCAR Cup Series from 2001 to 2023, winning the championship in 2014, and is regarded as one of the most successful drivers of his generation.

Career

Harvick, from Bakersfield, California, began racing go-karts as a child and progressed through late models and the NASCAR Truck Series. In February 2001, following the death of Dale Earnhardt at the Daytona 500, Harvick was unexpectedly promoted to Earnhardt's Richard Childress Racing car, renumbered 29. Three weeks later he won his first Cup race at Atlanta, edging Jeff Gordon in one of the closest finishes in NASCAR history.

Harvick won 23 races for RCR, including the 2007 Daytona 500, before moving to Stewart-Haas Racing in 2014. In his first season with the team he won five races and the Cup Series championship under the new elimination playoff format. Nicknamed "The Closer" and "Happy Harvick", he accumulated 60 Cup Series victories — among the top ten all-time — along with the 2001 and 2006 Busch (Xfinity) Series championships.

Retirement

Harvick retired from full-time driving after the 2023 season and joined the Fox Sports NASCAR broadcast team in 2024. He also founded Kevin Harvick Incorporated, a driver management and motorsport business, with his wife DeLana.
