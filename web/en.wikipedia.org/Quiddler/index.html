Quiddler

Quiddler is a card game, played with a proprietary deck, created by Marsha J. Falco and published by Set Enterprises in 1998. The object is to form words from the letters on one's cards, in the manner of rummy crossed with a word game; its tagline calls it "the short word game".

Gameplay

The deck contains 118 cards bearing single letters (and a few digraphs such as QU, IN, and CL) with varying point values; common letters are plentiful and worth fewer points. Players progress through eight rounds, being dealt three cards in the first round and one additional card in each successive round, up to ten. On a turn a player draws and discards; a player who can meld all but one card as words "goes out", after which others get a final turn. A bonus is awarded for the longest word and for the most words each round. Unused cards subtract their value from a player's score.
