FACEIT

FACEIT is an esports platform for online multiplayer video game competitions, which was founded in London, United Kingdom, in 2012. The company has administered leagues for games such as Counter-Strike 2, League of Legends, Rocket League, and Dota 2.

History

FACEIT was founded in 2012 by Italian entrepreneurs Niccolo Maisto and Michele Attisani. It grew from a third-party matchmaking service into one of the largest competitive gaming platforms in the world, hosting tens of millions of users across numerous titles.

The FACEIT CS:GO platform became known for its high-level matchmaking, in-house anti-cheat, and the FACEIT Pro League (FPL), which features professional players. In 2022, the Savvy Games Group, backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, acquired FACEIT and ESL for a combined total of about US$1.5 billion.

Tournaments

FACEIT has organized major tournaments including the FACEIT Major: London 2018, the first Counter-Strike: Global Offensive Major held in the United Kingdom, won by Astralis. The platform also runs the Esports Championship Series (ECS) league.
