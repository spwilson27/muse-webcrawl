Esalen Institute

Founded: 1962
Location: Big Sur, California, US
Founders: Michael Murphy, Dick Price
Type: Nonprofit retreat and educational centre

The Esalen Institute, commonly called Esalen, is a not-for-profit American retreat centre and intentional community in Big Sur, California, which focuses on humanistic alternative education. Founded in 1962 by Michael Murphy and Dick Price, it became a centre of the Human Potential Movement.

History

Esalen sits on land with natural hot springs long used by the Esselen people. In the 1960s it hosted workshops by figures such as Abraham Maslow, Fritz Perls (who developed Gestalt therapy there), Aldous Huxley, and Alan Watts. Encounter groups, massage, meditation, and psychedelic research were prominent in its early decades; "Esalen massage" became a recognised bodywork style.

Activities

Today Esalen offers hundreds of workshops each year in psychology, spirituality, ecology, and the arts, together with long-term residential programmes. Its Center for Theory & Research sponsors conferences and scholarly initiatives. The site is also known for its oceanfront hot springs baths.
