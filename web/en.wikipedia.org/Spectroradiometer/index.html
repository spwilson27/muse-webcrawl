Spectroradiometer

TypeOptical instrument
MeasuresSpectral radiance and irradiance
UsesRemote sensing, lighting, display calibration

A spectroradiometer is an instrument that measures the spectral distribution of light — the power of electromagnetic radiation as a function of wavelength — in terms of radiance or irradiance. It combines a spectrometer with radiometric calibration against a known standard.

Operation

Light enters through an input optic, is dispersed by a grating or prism onto a detector array, and the resulting spectrum is corrected using calibration data from a traceable lamp standard. Measurements are reported in units such as watts per square metre per nanometre.

Applications

Spectroradiometers are used to calibrate satellite and airborne remote-sensing instruments, characterise lamps, LEDs, and displays, measure solar radiation, and assess plant responses to light.
