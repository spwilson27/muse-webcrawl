FFP mask

An FFP2 filtering facepiece respirator.

An FFP mask is a filtering facepiece respirator certified under the European standard EN 149. FFP stands for "filtering facepiece". The standard classifies masks by their filtration efficiency and maximum total inward leakage.

Classes

FFP1 masks filter at least 80% of airborne particles and are used mainly as dust masks. FFP2 masks filter at least 94% and are broadly comparable to the American N95 and Chinese KN95 standards; they are widely used against fine dusts, aerosols, and respiratory pathogens. FFP3 masks filter at least 99% and provide the highest protection of the three classes, being used against toxic dusts, asbestos, and in healthcare settings with high-risk aerosol-generating procedures.

Use

Protection depends critically on face fit: leakage around the seal can greatly reduce effectiveness, and facial hair can compromise the seal. Some models include an exhalation valve, which improves comfort but does not filter exhaled air, making valved masks less suitable for source control. During the COVID-19 pandemic, FFP2 masks were mandated on public transport and in shops in several European countries, including Austria and Germany.

Equivalents

Equivalent respirator classifications include N95/N99/N100 under the U.S. NIOSH system, KN95 under China's GB2626 standard, and KF94 in South Korea.
