Relay race

A relay race is a competition in which members of a team take turns completing parts of a course or performing a certain action. In track and field, relay races consist of four runners per team, each running a leg and handing a baton to the next runner within a marked changeover zone.

Track relays

The standard Olympic relay events are the 4 × 100 metres relay and the 4 × 400 metres relay, with a mixed-gender 4 × 400 m added at the Tokyo 2020 Games. In the sprint relay the baton must be passed within a 30-metre takeover zone; dropping the baton or passing outside the zone results in disqualification. Other formats include the medley relay, distance medley and the road-based ekiden, popular in Japan.

Other sports

Relays are contested in swimming (freestyle and medley relays, with exchange judged by the departing swimmer leaving after the incoming swimmer touches), cross-country skiing, biathlon, triathlon and mountain biking. Swimming exchanges allow a small reaction-time tolerance for the flying start, producing the fastest splits of the race.
