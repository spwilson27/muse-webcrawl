The SRO Motorsports Group is an international motorsport organisation founded by Stéphane Ratel that promotes and sanctions GT racing series around the world. It is best known for organising the GT World Challenge series on several continents and the Intercontinental GT Challenge, as well as the CrowdStrike 24 Hours of Spa.

History

Ratel founded the Stéphane Ratel Organisation in 1995 to promote the BPR Global GT Series. The group went on to run the FIA GT Championship and later the Blancpain GT Series. In 2018 the organisation rebranded as SRO Motorsports Group.

Series

SRO sanctions GT World Challenge Europe, America, Asia and Australia, the British GT Championship, the FFSA French GT Championship, and numerous one-make and touring car categories. Its events typically feature GT3 and GT4 machinery with Bronze-rated amateur and professional drivers sharing cars under Balance of Performance rules.
