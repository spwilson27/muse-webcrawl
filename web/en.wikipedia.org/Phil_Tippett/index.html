Phil Tippett (born September 27, 1951) is an American film director and visual effects supervisor and producer, who specializes in creature design, stop-motion and computerized character animation. Over his career he has assisted ILM and DreamWorks, and in 1983 formed his own company, Tippett Studio.

Career

Tippett contributed to the original Star Wars films, animating the holochess sequence and the tauntauns and AT-AT walkers of The Empire Strikes Back. He pioneered "go motion", a variant of stop motion using computer-controlled movements to add motion blur, on Dragonslayer (1981). For Jurassic Park (1993) he initially prepared stop-motion dinosaurs before the production switched to computer graphics; he remained as dinosaur supervisor and won an Academy Award for the film's visual effects. He also won an Oscar for Return of the Jedi.

Mad God

Tippett spent some three decades making the stop-motion feature Mad God, released in 2021 to critical acclaim.
