Mortal Kombat is an American media franchise centered on a series of fighting video games originally developed by Midway Games. The first game, released in arcades in 1992, was created by Ed Boon and John Tobias and distinguished itself with digitized sprites of live actors and unusually graphic violence, including the finishing moves known as Fatalities.

Gameplay and story

The series centers on a tournament in which warriors from Earthrealm defend their world against invaders from realms such as Outworld, ruled by the emperor Shao Kahn. Iconic characters include Scorpion, Sub-Zero, Liu Kang, Raiden, Sonya Blade, and Johnny Cage. Gameplay emphasizes special moves, combos, and finishing moves such as Fatalities and, in later games, Brutalities and stage interactions.

Controversy and legacy

The original game's violence was a central subject of the 1993 United States congressional hearings on video game violence, which contributed to the creation of the Entertainment Software Rating Board (ESRB) in 1994. The franchise has sold tens of millions of copies and expanded into films (including the 1995 film Mortal Kombat and a 2021 reboot), television, comics, and merchandise. After Midway's bankruptcy, the series continued under Warner Bros. and NetherRealm Studios, with titles such as Mortal Kombat (2011), Mortal Kombat X (2015), Mortal Kombat 11 (2019), and Mortal Kombat 1 (2023).
