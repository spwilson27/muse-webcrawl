Mean time between failures

Mean time between failures (MTBF) is the predicted elapsed time between inherent failures of a mechanical or electronic system during normal operation. It is a key metric in reliability engineering, calculated as the arithmetic mean time between failures of a repairable system.

Definition

MTBF is related to mean time to failure (MTTF) and mean time to repair (MTTR): MTBF = MTTF + MTTR for the uptime portion of a cycle. It assumes a roughly constant failure rate, the flat middle of the bathtub curve.

Use and limitations

Manufacturers quote MTBF for components such as hard drives and power supplies, but figures derived from accelerated testing can be misleading if read as a guaranteed lifespan for an individual unit.
