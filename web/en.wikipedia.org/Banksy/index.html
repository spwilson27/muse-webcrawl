Banksy

Banksy is a pseudonymous England-based street artist, political activist and film director whose real identity remains unconfirmed. Active since the 1990s, he is known for satirical, stencilled graffiti combining dark humour with anti-authoritarian, anti-war and anti-capitalist messages.

Career

Banksy began as a graffiti artist in Bristol in the early 1990s, part of the Bristol underground scene. He adopted stencilling to work faster and avoid arrest, citing the influence of French artist Blek le Rat.

Notable works

Famous images include Girl with Balloon, the Flower Thrower, kissing policemen, and works on the West Bank barrier wall. His 2010 documentary Exit Through the Gift Shop was nominated for an Academy Award. In 2018 his Girl with Balloon partially shredded itself immediately after selling at auction for £1.04 million; retitled Love is in the Bin, it resold in 2021 for £18.6 million.

Identity

Despite extensive speculation, Banksy has maintained anonymity, managed through the authentication body Pest Control.
