List of Formula One World Constructors' Champions

The Formula One World Constructors' Championship (WCC) is awarded by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) to the most successful Formula One constructor over the course of a season, as determined by a point scoring system based on Grand Prix results. It was first awarded in 1958 (as the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers) to Vanwall.

Sixteen different constructors have won the championship. Ferrari is the most successful, the only team to have competed in every season and the record holder for titles. Counting rules changed over time: until 1978 only the best-placed car of each constructor scored points at each race, and for some eras only a selection of results counted toward the total.
