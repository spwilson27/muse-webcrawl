LGBT movements

LGBT movements are social and political movements that advocate for the full acceptance and equal rights of lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender people in society.

History

Early organisations included Magnus Hirschfeld's Scientific-Humanitarian Committee (1897) in Germany and, in the United States, the Mattachine Society (1950) and Daughters of Bilitis (1955). The Stonewall riots of June 1969 in New York catalysed gay liberation groups such as the Gay Liberation Front.

Campaigns

Movements have campaigned against criminalisation of homosexuality, for anti-discrimination laws, recognition of same-sex relationships and marriage, adoption rights, protection for transgender people, and effective responses to HIV/AIDS through groups such as ACT UP.

Opposition and internal debates

LGBT movements face organised opposition in many countries and debate strategy, assimilation, intersectionality and priorities internally.
