M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System

Type: Tracked multiple launch rocket system
Origin: United States
In service: 1983–present
Armament: 12 × 227 mm rockets or 2 × ATACMS missiles
Chassis: Derived from Bradley Fighting Vehicle

The M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System (MLRS) is an American armoured, self-propelled multiple launch rocket system developed by Lockheed Martin Missiles and Fire Control (originally Vought).

Design

The tracked M269 launcher loader module carries two six-round pods of 227 mm rockets (including GPS-guided GMLRS with ranges beyond 70 km) or two MGM-140 ATACMS tactical ballistic missiles. A crew of three operates from an armoured cab.

Service

First used in combat in the 1991 Gulf War, the M270 serves with the US Army and many allies including the United Kingdom, Germany, France and Japan. Upgraded M270A2 variants add a new engine, improved armoured cab and common fire control. Systems were supplied to Ukraine from 2022.
