Prabhas (born 23 October 1979) is an Indian actor who works predominantly in Telugu cinema. One of the highest-paid actors in Indian cinema, he rose to pan-Indian fame playing the title role in S. S. Rajamouli's epic action films Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017), the latter among the highest-grossing Indian films of all time.

Early life

Prabhas was born in Madras (now Chennai) to film producer Uppalapati Surya Narayana Raju and Siva Kumari. He is the nephew of Telugu actor Krishnam Raju, and was educated in Hyderabad.

Career

He made his acting debut with the drama Eeswar (2002) and gained recognition with the action films Varsham (2004) and Chatrapathi (2005), his first collaboration with Rajamouli. After a string of successful Telugu action films including Mirchi (2013), he devoted several years exclusively to the two-part Baahubali epic, whose success made him a star across India. Subsequent big-budget releases include Saaho (2019), Radhe Shyam (2022), Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire (2023) and Kalki 2898 AD (2024).

Public image

Prabhas was the first South Indian actor to have a wax figure installed at Madame Tussauds in Bangkok. He has appeared in Forbes India's Celebrity 100 list and is noted for a reserved public persona.
