Pose portrays the ballroom houses of 1980s New York.

Pose is an American drama television series about New York City's ballroom culture, an LGBTQ subculture in the African-American and Latino community, throughout the 1980s and 1990s. Created by Ryan Murphy, Brad Falchuk, and Steven Canals, the series premiered on FX on 3 June 2018 and concluded on 6 June 2021 after three seasons and 26 episodes.

Premise

Set beginning in 1987, the series follows Blanca Rodriguez, a trans woman who leaves the House of Abundance to found her own house, the House of Evangelista, taking in the dancer Damon and the runaway Angel. The characters compete in underground balls, navigate the fashion and nightlife of Manhattan, and face the impact of the HIV/AIDS crisis. The second season advances to 1990 and the third to 1994.

Cast and significance

The ensemble includes Michaela Jaé Rodriguez, Dominique Jackson, Indya Moore, Angelica Ross, and Billy Porter as the ball emcee Pray Tell. Pose made history with the largest cast of transgender actors in series-regular roles for a scripted series. Porter won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series in 2019, and Rodriguez became the first transgender performer to win a Golden Globe for a leading television role for the final season.

Reception

The series was acclaimed by critics for its writing, performances, and portrayal of ballroom culture, which had earlier been documented in the film Paris Is Burning (1990).
