Vasant Panchami

Vasant Panchami (also rendered Basant Panchami or Saraswati Puja) is a Hindu festival that marks the preparation for the arrival of spring, observed on the fifth day of the bright half of the Hindu month of Magha (about January–February). It is dedicated especially to Saraswati, the goddess of knowledge, learning, music, and the arts.

Observances

The colour yellow (basanti) dominates the festival — symbolising mustard fields in bloom, ripening crops, and prosperity. Devotees wear yellow garments and offer yellow flowers and sweets. In educational institutions, students worship Saraswati, place books and instruments before her image, and refrain from studying that day. Young children may be initiated into learning by writing their first letters (Aksharabhyasa / Vidya Arambham).

In Punjab, the day is celebrated with kite flying as Basant. In Bengal it is one of the major Saraswati Puja celebrations, while at the Kumbh Mela sites, Vasant Panchami is an important bathing day. The festival also has associations with Kamadeva, the god of love, and with the marriage of Shiva and Parvati traditions in some regions.
