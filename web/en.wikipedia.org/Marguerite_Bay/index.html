Marguerite Bay is an extensive bay on the west coast of the Antarctic Peninsula, lying between Adelaide Island to the north and Alexander Island and the George VI Sound to the south. It contains numerous islands, including Pourquoi Pas Island, Horseshoe Island and Lagotellerie Island.

History

The bay was discovered and charted by the French Antarctic Expedition of 1908–1910 under Jean-Baptiste Charcot, who named it for his wife, Marguerite. The British Graham Land Expedition (1934–1937) under John Rymill surveyed the area in detail, and the Falkland Islands Dependencies Survey established a base on Horseshoe Island (Base Y) in 1955, now preserved as a historic site. The United States' East Base on Stonington Island also stood on the bay's shores.
