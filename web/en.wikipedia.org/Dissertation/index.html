Dissertation

A dissertation, also known as a thesis, is a document submitted in support of candidature for an academic degree or professional qualification presenting the author's research and findings. In some contexts, the word thesis or a cognate is used for part of a bachelor's or master's course, while dissertation is normally applied to a doctorate. This complexity can partly be attributed to scholarly traditions which have their roots in European universities.

Etymology

The term thesis comes from the Greek θέσις, meaning "something put forth", and refers to an intellectual proposition. Dissertation comes from the Latin dissertātiō, meaning "discussion". Aristotle was the first philosopher to define the term thesis.

Structure and presentation style

A thesis (or dissertation) is normally divided into chapters, and contains a title page, an abstract, a table of contents, an introduction, a literature review, a methodology chapter, results, discussion, and a bibliography or reference section. Empirical theses typically report on research that follows an established scientific method.

Dissertations normally report on a research project or study, or an extended analysis of a topic. The structure of a thesis therefore depends on the discipline and institution, but in general presents an ordered argument supported by evidence.

Submission and defence

The candidate typically defends the work before a committee of examiners in an oral examination, known as a viva voce in the United Kingdom and Commonwealth systems. Universities often require that the final accepted dissertation be deposited in the institutional library, and many are indexed by commercial databases such as ProQuest Dissertations & Theses.

By degree level

  • Doctoral dissertation — required for the Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) and other research doctorates; must make an original contribution to knowledge.
  • Master's thesis — required for many master's degrees; demonstrates mastery of research methods and the literature of a field.
  • Undergraduate dissertation — an extended project or long essay undertaken in the final year of a bachelor's degree.
