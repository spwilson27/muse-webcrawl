Peter Zoller

Peter Zoller (born 16 September 1952) is an Austrian theoretical physicist and professor at the University of Innsbruck. He is a pioneer of quantum computing and quantum optics, particularly known for proposing, with Ignacio Cirac, the trapped-ion quantum computer architecture in 1995.

Research

The Cirac–Zoller proposal showed how laser-cooled ions in a trap could implement quantum logic gates, and it became one of the leading architectures for quantum computers. Zoller's work also spans cold atoms in optical lattices, quantum simulation, and quantum communication. He shared the 2013 Wolf Prize in Physics with Ignacio Cirac, and has received honours including the Max Planck Medal and the Dirac Medal.
