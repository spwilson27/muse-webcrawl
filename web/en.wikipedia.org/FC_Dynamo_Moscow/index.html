FC Dynamo Moscow

FC Dynamo Moscow is a Russian professional football club based in Moscow. Founded in 1923 as part of the Dynamo sports society — the sports organisation of the Soviet security services — it is one of Russia's oldest and most historically successful football clubs, and was a dominant force in Soviet football.

Dynamo Moscow's home, the VTB Arena

History

Dynamo won eleven Soviet Top League championships, the first in 1936, and six Soviet Cups. Its goalkeeper Lev Yashin, nicknamed the "Black Spider", is regarded as one of the greatest goalkeepers in the history of the sport and remains the only goalkeeper to have won the Ballon d'Or. In 1972 the club reached the final of the European Cup Winners' Cup, losing to Rangers. After the dissolution of the Soviet Union, Dynamo won the Russian Cup in 1995 but has not won a league title in the Russian era; it suffered a first-ever relegation from the Russian Premier League in 2016, returning immediately as first-division champions. The club plays at the VTB Arena, built on the site of the historic Dynamo Stadium.
