Museum

A museum is an institution that cares for a collection of artifacts and other objects of artistic, cultural, historical, or scientific importance, and makes them available for public viewing through exhibits that may be permanent or temporary. The word derives from the Latin museum, in turn from the Greek mouseion, a seat of the Muses — famously the Mouseion of Alexandria, the ancient centre of scholarship.

History

Collecting long predates the public museum: royal treasuries, temple collections and Renaissance cabinets of curiosities assembled objects for private study and prestige. The modern public museum emerged in the Enlightenment — the Ashmolean at Oxford opened in 1683 and the British Museum in 1759, whose founding statute promised free admission to all "studious and curious persons." The Louvre opened as a public museum in revolutionary Paris in 1793. The nineteenth and twentieth centuries saw explosive growth of national, municipal, university and specialist museums across the world.

Types and functions

Museums include art museums and galleries, history and archaeology museums, natural history museums, science museums and centres, ethnographic collections, maritime, military, railway and other transport museums, children's museums, and open-air museums of relocated historic buildings. Their core professional functions are collecting, conservation, research, exhibition and education. The International Council of Museums (ICOM) adopted a new definition in 2022 describing museums as not-for-profit, permanent institutions in the service of society that research, collect, conserve, interpret and exhibit tangible and intangible heritage, operating ethically and with community participation.

Issues

Modern museums face debates over the repatriation of objects acquired under colonialism — such as the Benin Bronzes and the Parthenon Marbles — funding and commercialisation, the display of human remains, and how to represent contested histories. There are estimated to be more than 100,000 museums worldwide, receiving hundreds of millions of visits a year.
