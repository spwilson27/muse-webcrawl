Battle of Amiens (1918)

The Battle of Amiens, also known as the Third Battle of Picardy, was the opening phase of the Allied offensive known as the Hundred Days Offensive that ultimately led to the end of the First World War. It began on 8 August 1918.

Battle

British, Australian, Canadian and French forces, with surprise, massed tanks and aircraft, advanced up to 12 miles on the first day — described by Ludendorff as "the black day of the German Army."

Outcome

The victory demonstrated combined-arms warfare and began Germany's continuous retreat until the Armistice of 11 November 1918.
