The Sugarcubes

[Placeholder image: The_Sugarcubes.png]

The Sugarcubes (Icelandic: Sykurmolarnir) were an Icelandic alternative rock band formed in 1986 in Reykjavík. Best known for launching the career of singer Björk, the band also featured Einar Örn Benediktsson and Sigtryggur Baldursson. Their debut album, Life's Too Good (1988), included the international hit 'Birthday' and brought them acclaim in the UK and US alternative press.

Later work

The band released two further albums, Here Today, Tomorrow Next Week! (1989) and Stick Around for Joy (1992), before disbanding; Björk went on to a major solo career.
