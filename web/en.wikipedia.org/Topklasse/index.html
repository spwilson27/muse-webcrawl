Topklasse

Topklasse
Country: Netherlands
Founded: 2010
Folded: 2016
Level on pyramid: 3
Replaced by: Tweede Divisie

The Topklasse (Dutch for "Top Class") was a football league in the Netherlands that served as the third tier of Dutch football and the highest level of amateur football in the country from the 2010–11 season until its abolition in 2016.

Structure

The league consisted of two divisions, each of 16 clubs: a Saturday division and a Sunday division, reflecting the Netherlands' traditional division between clubs that played on Saturdays (often on religious grounds) and those that played on Sundays. The champions of each division met in a play-off to decide the overall amateur champion, though promotion to the professional Eerste Divisie was voluntary and initially declined by all Topklasse champions.

Dissolution

As part of a restructuring of the Dutch football pyramid, the Topklasse was replaced in 2016 by the revived Tweede Divisie as the third tier, with the Topklasse clubs dropping into the new structure — the former Topklasse level becoming the Derde Divisie.
