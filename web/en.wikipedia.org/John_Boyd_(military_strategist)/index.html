John Boyd (military strategist)

John Richard Boyd (January 23, 1927 – March 9, 1997) was a United States Air Force fighter pilot and military strategist.

A legendary dogfighter nicknamed "Forty Second Boyd", he co-developed energy–maneuverability theory, influenced the design of the F-15 and F-16, and led the "Fighter Mafia" reform movement. His briefings on patterns of conflict introduced the OODA loop (observe–orient–decide–act), now influential across military strategy, business, and law enforcement.
