Juan Valdez Café

Juan Valdez Café is a multinational coffeehouse chain based in Colombia. It is owned by the National Federation of Coffee Growers of Colombia (FNC) through its company Procafecol S.A., and is named after Juan Valdez, the fictional coffee farmer created in 1958 to promote Colombian coffee.

History

The first Juan Valdez Café opened in December 2002 at El Dorado International Airport in Bogotá. The chain was created to capture more of the value of coffee for Colombia's roughly 500,000 coffee-growing families, who are shareholders in the venture, by selling branded coffee directly to consumers rather than exporting only green beans. The cafés serve premium Colombian coffee and sell packaged beans and branded merchandise.

Expansion

The chain expanded across Colombia and internationally, with stores in countries including the United States, Spain, Chile, Ecuador, Mexico, and others in Latin America, Europe, and the Middle East, growing to several hundred locations. Juan Valdez Café positions itself as a premium brand representing the quality of Colombian coffee and the welfare of its growers.
