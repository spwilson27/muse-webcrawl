List of UK Singles Chart Christmas number ones

In the United Kingdom, the Christmas number one is the single at the top of the UK Singles Chart in the week including Christmas Day — a much-publicised annual contest since the chart began in 1952.

Notable Christmas number ones

  • 1952: "Here in My Heart" — Al Martino (the first UK chart-topper)
  • 1973: "Merry Xmas Everybody" — Slade; 1975 and 1980: "Bohemian Rhapsody" — Queen (the only song to be Christmas number one twice by the same artist)
  • 1984: "Do They Know It's Christmas?" — Band Aid, later re-recorded by Band Aid II (1989) and Band Aid 20 (2004)
  • 1994–1996: Spice Girls achieved three consecutive Christmas number ones
  • 2000s: several winners of TV talent shows (Shayne Ward, Leona Lewis, Alexandra Burke) took the spot, prompting the 2009 campaign that made Rage Against the Machine's "Killing in the Name" number one
  • 2018–2022: LadBaby achieved five consecutive Christmas number ones with sausage-roll-themed parodies, surpassing the Beatles' four
  • 2023: "Last Christmas" — Wham! reached Christmas number one 39 years after release
