The Yu-Gi-Oh! Trading Card Game (Japanese: 遊☆戯☆王オフィシャルカードゲーム) is a collectible card game developed and published by Konami, based on the fictional game of Duel Monsters from Kazuki Takahashi's Yu-Gi-Oh! manga. Launched in Japan in 1999 and in North America in 2002, it became one of the best-selling trading card games in the world, recognised by Guinness World Records in 2009 as the top-selling trading card game, with over 22 billion cards sold at that time.

Gameplay

Two players, called duelists, each begin with 8,000 Life Points and a deck of 40 to 60 cards. Players summon Monster cards in attack or defence position and use Spell and Trap cards to attack the opponent's monsters and Life Points; a player loses when their Life Points reach zero or they cannot draw a card. Later mechanics added Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum and Link Monsters, summoned from an Extra Deck using specific materials and zones.

Organised play

Konami sanctions tournaments from local shops to the Yu-Gi-Oh! Championship Series and an annual World Championship. A frequently updated Forbidden & Limited List restricts powerful cards in official play, and separate card pools and rules historically divided the Asian "OCG" from the international "TCG".

Related media

The card game is closely tied to the Yu-Gi-Oh! anime series, in which characters duel using the game's cards, and it has been adapted into numerous video games, including the digital title Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel (2022).
