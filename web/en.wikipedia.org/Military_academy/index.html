Military academy

A military academy or service academy is an educational institution which prepares candidates for service in the officer corps of the military. It normally provides education in a military environment, the exact training varying by country and service branch.

Types

Academies may be at secondary level (military schools), undergraduate level (such as the United States Military Academy at West Point, the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst, and Saint-Cyr in France), or postgraduate staff colleges for senior officers.

Curriculum

Cadets typically combine academic study leading to a degree with military training, physical conditioning, leadership exercises, and strict codes of conduct and honor. Graduates are commissioned as officers in the army, navy, air force, or other services.

History

The earliest dedicated military academies were founded in the 18th century as warfare professionalized; the Royal Military Academy at Woolwich (1741) and the École Militaire in Paris (1750) are early examples.
