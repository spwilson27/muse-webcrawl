William Caplin

[Image: William Caplin]

William Earl Caplin (born 1947) is an American music theorist and historian, known for his theory of formal functions in classical music. He is James McGill Professor in the Schulich School of Music at McGill University in Montreal, Canada.

Work

Caplin's book Classical Form: A Theory of Formal Functions for the Instrumental Music of Haydn, Mozart, and Beethoven (1998) systematised the analysis of themes and movements in the classical style, distinguishing sentence and period structures and formal functions such as presentation, continuation and cadential phrases. The book became a standard reference in North American music theory pedagogy. His subsequent work, including Analyzing Classical Form (2013), extended and taught the approach in a textbook format.

Career

Caplin studied at Carleton College and the University of Chicago, where he earned his PhD. He served as president of the Society for Music Theory (2005–2007) and received awards for his publications and teaching.
