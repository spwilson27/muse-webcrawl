List of Lost episodes

Lost is an American drama television series created by Jeffrey Lieber, J. J. Abrams and Damon Lindelof, which aired on ABC from September 22, 2004, to May 23, 2010. The series follows the survivors of Oceanic Airlines Flight 815, which crashed on a mysterious tropical island. A total of 121 episodes were broadcast over six seasons.
