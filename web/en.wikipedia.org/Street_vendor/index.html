Street vendor

SectorInformal economy
GoodsStreet food, produce, clothing, household goods
Also calledHawker, peddler, costermonger

A street vendor is a person who sells goods or services in public places such as streets, pavements and markets, typically from a stall, cart or portable display. Street vending is a major part of the informal economy worldwide, providing livelihoods for millions and affordable goods and street food in cities across Asia, Africa and Latin America as well as in Western cities.

Regulation

Vendors' legal status varies widely: many municipalities licence and zone vending, while unlicensed vendors may face confiscation or eviction. Organisations of vendors campaign for recognition and secure trading spaces, and some countries, such as India with its Street Vendors Act of 2014, have enacted national protections.
