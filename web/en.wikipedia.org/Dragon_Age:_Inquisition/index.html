Dragon Age: Inquisition is a 2014 action role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts. The third major game in the Dragon Age franchise, it is the sequel to Dragon Age: Origins (2009) and Dragon Age II (2011).

Plot

The player character, known as the Inquisitor, bears a mysterious mark — the "Anchor" — that can close rifts in the Veil called the Breach, torn open by an explosion at the Conclave of the Divine. Leading the reborn Inquisition, the player must restore order in Thedas amid a civil war between mages and templars and stop the Elder One, Corypheus, an ancient darkspawn magister.

Gameplay

Gameplay combines real-time action with a tactical pause mode. The Inquisitor explores large semi-open regions across Ferelden and Orlais, recruits companions and agents, and makes choices from the War Table at Skyhold fortress. Character creation allows different races (human, elf, dwarf, qunari) and classes (warrior, rogue, mage).

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, praising its world, characters and combat, and it won numerous Game of the Year awards, including at the inaugural Game Awards in 2014. Its final downloadable expansion, Trespasser (2015), was widely acclaimed. A sequel, Dragon Age: The Veilguard, was released in 2024.
