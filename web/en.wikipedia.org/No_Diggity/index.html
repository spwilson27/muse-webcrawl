"No Diggity" is a song by the American R&B group Blackstreet, featuring Dr. Dre and Queen Pen. Released in 1996 as the first single from the group's second album, Another Level, it became one of the defining R&B and hip-hop hits of the decade.

Composition and success

The track, produced by Teddy Riley, is built around a sample of "Grandma's Hands" by Bill Withers and combines Riley's new jack swing and G-funk influences with rap verses. It reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100, ending the long chart run of "Macarena", and won the Grammy Award for Best R&B Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocals in 1998.

Legacy

"No Diggity" has remained a staple of 1990s popular culture, frequently licensed in films and covered or reworked by later artists, including a notable version by Chet Faker that charted in Australia in the 2010s.
