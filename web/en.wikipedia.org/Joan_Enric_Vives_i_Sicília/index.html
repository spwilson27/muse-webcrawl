Joan Enric Vives i Sicília

Joan Enric Vives i Sicília (born 24 July 1949) is a Spanish Catholic bishop who served as Bishop of Urgell from 2003 to 2025 and, in that capacity, as ex officio Co-Prince of Andorra, sharing the headship of state with the President of France.

Born in Barcelona, he was ordained in 1974, became auxiliary bishop of Barcelona in 1993, and succeeded Joan Martí Alanis at Urgell in 2003, receiving the personal title of Archbishop in 2010. He retired in 2025 and was succeeded by Josep-Lluís Serrano Pentinat.
