Chile verde

Chile verde ("green chile") is a Mexican and Mexican-American stew or sauce made from green chiles — commonly roasted poblano, Anaheim, Hatch, jalapeño, or serrano peppers — and tomatillos, typically simmered with pork. It is a signature dish of New Mexican cuisine and of the Mexican states of the north and centre.

Preparation

Green chiles are roasted until their skins blacken, then peeled and chopped or blended with tomatillos, onion, garlic, and spices such as cumin and oregano. Cubed pork shoulder is browned and simmered in the sauce until tender. Chile verde is served as a stew, as a filling for burritos and enchiladas, or as a smothering sauce.

Regional variations

In New Mexico the dish centres on New Mexico chile and often includes potatoes; Colorado versions frequently thicken the stew and serve it over burritos. It should not be confused with the fresh green table sauces known as salsa verde, though the two share ingredients.
