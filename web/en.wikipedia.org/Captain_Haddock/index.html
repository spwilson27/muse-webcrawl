Captain Haddock

Captain Archibald Haddock (French: Capitaine Haddock) is a fictional character in The Adventures of Tintin, the comics series by Belgian cartoonist Hergé. A bearded, hard-drinking merchant marine captain, he is Tintin's best friend and one of the series' most beloved characters, famous for his creative insults such as "Billions of blue blistering barnacles!" and "Thundering typhoons!"

Character

Haddock first appears in The Crab with the Golden Claws (1941) as a weak, alcoholic captain manipulated by his first mate Allan. He later reforms — mostly — and becomes Tintin's loyal, if irascible, companion. In The Secret of the Unicorn and Red Rackham's Treasure, treasure found through his ancestor Sir Francis Haddock makes him wealthy, and he buys Marlinspike Hall, where he settles with Tintin and Professor Calculus. Haddock is President of the Society of Sober Sailors, a role in comic tension with his love of Loch Lomond whisky.

Development

Hergé progressively developed Haddock from a comic foil into a co-protagonist whose emotional range balances Tintin's relative blandness. His vocabulary of pseudo-curses, drawn from obscure technical and scientific words, became a hallmark of the series.
