Tread Lightly!

Tread Lightly! promotes staying on designated routes to protect fragile landscapes.

TypeNonprofit organisation
Founded1990 (programme launched 1985 by the U.S. Forest Service)
HeadquartersCenterville, Utah, United States
FocusResponsible outdoor recreation and stewardship ethics

Tread Lightly! is a nonprofit organisation based in Utah, United States, whose mission is to promote responsible outdoor recreation through stewardship and ethics education. It began in 1985 as a public awareness programme of the United States Forest Service, aimed at reducing the impact of motorised recreation on public lands, and was launched as an independent nonprofit in 1990 with support from industry partners, land-management agencies and user groups.

The T.R.E.A.D. principles

The organisation teaches outdoor ethics through its T.R.E.A.D. principles:

  • Travel responsibly — stay on designated roads, trails and areas.
  • Respect the rights of others — including private landowners, other trail users and campers.
  • Educate yourself — obtain maps, permits and equipment before you go.
  • Avoid sensitive areas — such as meadows, lakeshores, wetlands and wildlife habitat.
  • Do your part — pack out litter, leave sites clean and volunteer for stewardship projects.

Activities

Tread Lightly! runs training courses for "Tread Trainers" and Master Tread Trainers, produces public-service campaigns, and organises trail restoration projects with clubs and manufacturers of off-highway vehicles, boats and other recreation equipment. Its message has expanded from four-wheel driving to cover motorcycling, ATV riding, snowmobiling, mountain biking, boating, hunting, fishing and camping. It is frequently compared with Leave No Trace, the complementary programme focused primarily on non-motorised recreation.
