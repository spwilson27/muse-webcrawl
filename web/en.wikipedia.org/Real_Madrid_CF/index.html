Real Madrid CF

Real Madrid Club de Fútbol, commonly referred to as Real Madrid, is a Spanish professional football club based in Madrid. Founded in 1902 as Madrid Football Club, it received the title Real ("Royal") from King Alfonso XIII in 1920. The club plays in La Liga, the top flight of Spanish football, and has never been relegated from it.

History

Real Madrid rose to dominance in the 1950s, winning the first five European Cups (1956–1960) with a team built around Alfredo Di Stéfano, Ferenc Puskás, and Francisco Gento. The club won further European titles in 1966 and then, after a long drought, in 1998, 2000, and 2002 with the Galácticos era of president Florentino Pérez, who signed stars such as Luís Figo, Zinedine Zidane, Ronaldo, and David Beckham. Under coaches including Vicente del Bosque, Carlo Ancelotti, and Zinedine Zidane, the club won three consecutive UEFA Champions League titles in 2016–2018 and added further titles in 2022 and 2024, taking its record total to fifteen European Cups/Champions Leagues.

Honours and identity

Real Madrid is the most successful club in the European Cup/Champions League and has won a record number of La Liga championships (36 as of 2024) along with numerous Copa del Rey titles. FIFA named it the best club of the 20th century. The club is owned by its members (socios) and is one of the world's highest-earning and most widely supported football clubs. Its fiercest rivalry is with FC Barcelona, contested as El Clásico, and its local rivalry with Atlético Madrid is the Madrid derby.
