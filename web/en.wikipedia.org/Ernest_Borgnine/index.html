Ernest Borgnine

Born: 24 January 1917, Hamden, Connecticut, US
Died: 8 July 2012, Los Angeles, California
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 1947–2012

Ernest Borgnine (born Ermes Effron Borgnino; January 24, 1917 – July 8, 2012) was an American actor whose career spanned over six decades. He won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his performance in Marty (1955) and was widely recognised as the lead of the 1960s sitcom McHale's Navy.

Early life

Born in Connecticut to Italian immigrant parents, Borgnine served ten years in the United States Navy, including during the Second World War. After his discharge he took up acting on his mother's suggestion, studying at the Randall School of Drama in Hartford before moving to New York.

Career

Borgnine played heavies in films such as From Here to Eternity (1953) before his acclaimed turn as the gentle butcher Marty Piletti. Later roles included The Dirty Dozen (1967), The Wild Bunch (1969), The Poseidon Adventure (1972), and Airwolf on television. From 1999 he voiced Mermaid Man on the animated series SpongeBob SquarePants.

Death and legacy

Borgnine died of kidney failure in Los Angeles in 2012, aged 95. He received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame and numerous lifetime achievement honours.
