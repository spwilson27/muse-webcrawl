King cake

A king cake is a cake associated with the feast of Epiphany (Twelfth Night) and, in many places, with pre-Lenten carnival celebrations such as Mardi Gras. Versions are found across Europe and the Americas, often containing a small hidden token—a bean, coin, or figurine—whose finder is crowned "king" or is expected to host the next gathering.

Variations

In northern France the galette des rois is a puff-pastry cake filled with frangipane; in southern France and Spain the gâteau des rois and roscón de reyes are ring-shaped brioche decorated with candied fruit. In Louisiana, the king cake is a braided, cinnamon-flavoured pastry iced in the Mardi Gras colours of purple, green, and gold, with a small plastic baby hidden inside, eaten throughout the carnival season from Epiphany to Fat Tuesday.
