St-Viateur Bagel

St-Viateur Bagel
Founded: 1957
Founder: Myer Lewkowicz
Headquarters: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Industry: Bakery

St-Viateur Bagel is a famous Montreal-style bagel bakery founded in 1957 by Myer Lewkowicz, a Polish Jewish immigrant and Holocaust survivor, on Saint-Viateur Street in the Mile End neighbourhood of Montreal, Quebec.

Montreal-style bagels

The bakery produces Montreal-style bagels, which are hand-rolled, boiled in honey-sweetened water, and baked in a wood-fired oven. They are smaller, denser, and sweeter than New York-style bagels, and are typically coated in sesame or poppy seeds.

History

Lewkowicz had worked in a Montreal bagel bakery after arriving in Canada before opening his own shop. St-Viateur has since expanded to several locations in Montreal and ships bagels widely, remaining an icon of the city's food culture.
