World Happiness Report
First published2012
PublisherWellbeing Research Centre, University of Oxford, with partners
Data sourceGallup World Poll

The World Happiness Report is an annual publication ranking countries by self-reported life evaluations, based principally on the Cantril ladder question in the Gallup World Poll. It was first published in 2012 in connection with the UN High-Level Meeting on Happiness and Well-being.

Rankings

Nordic countries consistently head the rankings, with Finland ranked first for many consecutive years. Rankings use six explanatory variables: GDP per capita, social support, healthy life expectancy, freedom to make life choices, generosity, and perceptions of corruption.

Criticism

Critics question whether a single ladder question captures well-being across cultures, and note that rankings can be sensitive to sampling and translation; editors respond that life evaluations are a robust, widely comparable summary measure.
