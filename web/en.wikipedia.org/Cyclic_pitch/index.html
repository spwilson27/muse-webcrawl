Cyclic pitch

Illustration: Cyclic pitch

Cyclic pitch is the control input by which a helicopter pilot tilts the rotor disc using the cyclic stick, varying each rotor blade's pitch angle as it rotates so that lift is greater on one side of the disc than the other.

Operation

Through the swashplate, the pilot's cyclic input raises blade pitch at one point of rotation and lowers it opposite. Because of gyroscopic precession, the resulting flapping occurs roughly 90 degrees later in the rotation, tilting the disc—and with it the thrust vector—in the desired direction.

Function

Moving the cyclic forward pitches the nose down and accelerates the helicopter; lateral inputs roll it into turns. Cyclic pitch contrasts with collective pitch, which changes all blades' pitch together to control total lift.
