Stephen King bibliography

Stephen King is an American author of horror, supernatural fiction, suspense, crime, science-fiction, and fantasy novels. His books have sold more than 350 million copies. King has published over 60 novels, including seven under the pen name Richard Bachman, and five non-fiction books. He has also written approximately 200 short stories, most of which have been published in book collections.

The Dark Tower series

King's multi-volume epic The Dark Tower (1982–2004, with a later interquel in 2012) follows gunslinger Roland Deschain and links many of King's other works into a shared multiverse.

Richard Bachman books

Writing as Richard Bachman, King published Rage (1977), The Long Walk (1979), Roadwork (1981), The Running Man (1982), Thinner (1984), The Regulators (1996), and Blaze (2007).

Collections and non-fiction

Story collections include Night Shift (1978), Different Seasons (1982), Skeleton Crew (1985), and Just After Sunset (2008). His memoir-craft book On Writing (2000) is widely recommended to aspiring authors.

Collaborations

King has collaborated with Peter Straub (The Talisman, Black House), his sons Joe Hill and Owen King, and Richard Chizmar (the Gwendy trilogy).
