Elias Ashmole

Elias Ashmole (23 May 1617 – 18 May 1692) was an English antiquary, politician, astrologer and collector whose donation of his cabinet of curiosities to the University of Oxford founded the Ashmolean Museum, the world's first university museum.

Life

Born in Lichfield, Ashmole practised as a solicitor and supported the Royalist cause in the English Civil War. After the Restoration he held public offices including Windsor Herald. A founding member of the Royal Society, he pursued wide interests in astrology, alchemy and antiquities, and published a major history of the Order of the Garter (1672).

Collection and museum

Ashmole acquired the famous "Tradescant's Ark" collection of rarities from John Tradescant the Younger. In 1677 he offered his combined collection to Oxford on condition that a suitable building house it; the Ashmolean Museum opened in 1683.
