Snidely Whiplash is a fictional character who originally appeared as the villain in the Dudley Do-Right segments of the animated television series The Rocky and Bullwinkle Show (1959–1964). Voiced by Hans Conried, he is a parody of the melodrama villains of silent films and stage plays: green-skinned, with a long black moustache, wearing a black top hat and cape.

Character

Whiplash's schemes typically involve tying the hero's sweetheart, Nell Fenwick, to railroad tracks, foreclosing mortgages, or robbing trains, only to be foiled by the dim but earnest Canadian Mountie Dudley Do-Right. His trademark menacing laugh and habit of rubbing his hands together made him an archetype of the cartoon villain; the phrase "mustache-twirling villain" is often associated with his style.

Appearances

The character was played by Alfred Molina in the 1999 live-action film Dudley Do-Right. Snidely Whiplash has also appeared in stage melodramas and advertising, and is referenced widely in popular culture as the quintessential scheming villain.
