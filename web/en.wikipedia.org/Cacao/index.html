Cacao

Cacao may refer to:

  • Theobroma cacao, the cacao tree, whose seeds are used to make:
  • Cocoa beans (cacao beans), the dried and fully fermented seeds of the tree
  • Cocoa solids, the low-fat component of cocoa beans
  • Cocoa powder, a mixture of cocoa solids and cocoa butter
  • Cocoa butter, the fatty component of the bean
  • Chocolate, the food products made from cacao

The cacao tree

Theobroma cacao is a small evergreen tree (4–8 m tall) native to the deep tropical regions of Mesoamerica and the Amazon basin. Its seeds grow in large pods directly on the trunk (cauliflory). Cacao was domesticated at least 3,000–5,000 years ago; the Olmec, Maya and Aztec used the beans for bitter drinks and as currency. Today most production comes from West Africa — Côte d'Ivoire and Ghana lead world output — followed by Indonesia, Nigeria, Cameroon, Brazil and Ecuador.

Processing

Harvested pods are opened, and the beans fermented in their pulp for several days, then dried, roasted and winnowed to produce cocoa nibs, which are ground into cocoa mass and pressed or conched into chocolate products.
