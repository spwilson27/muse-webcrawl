Management consulting

Management consulting is the practice of providing independent advice and implementation support to organisations to help them improve their performance, solve problems, and achieve their goals. Management consultants are typically employed by specialised firms — including the so-called "Big Three" strategy firms McKinsey & Company, Boston Consulting Group, and Bain & Company — or work independently.

Services

Consulting services cover corporate strategy, operations, organisational change, marketing, information technology, human resources, and financial advisory. Engagements range from short diagnostic studies to multi-year transformation programmes.

Industry

The modern industry grew during the 20th century alongside scientific management and the growth of large corporations. It is now a global business worth hundreds of billions of dollars annually, and is also a significant employer of business-school graduates.
