Eco-Drive

Eco-Drive
Manufacturer: Citizen Watch Co.
Introduced: 1995
Power source: Light (solar cell), stored in a rechargeable cell
Claim: No regular battery replacement needed

Eco-Drive is a line of watches manufactured by the Japanese company Citizen that are powered by light. A solar cell beneath or around the dial converts natural and artificial light into electrical energy, which is stored in a rechargeable lithium-ion cell, eliminating the need for regular battery changes.

History

Citizen marketed early solar watches in the 1970s and launched the Eco-Drive brand in 1995. When fully charged, typical models run for six months or more in darkness, and some high-end models have power reserves of several years. The range includes radio-controlled and satellite-synchronised (Satellite Wave) models that receive time signals from atomic clocks or GPS satellites.

Technology

The photovoltaic cells may be hidden beneath translucent dials or arranged in a ring around the dial's edge. Some models supplement solar charging with temperature-difference or kinetic generation, though light-powered models dominate the line.
