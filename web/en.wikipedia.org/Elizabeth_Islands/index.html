Elizabeth Islands

The Elizabeth Islands are a chain of small islands extending southwest from the southern coast of Cape Cod, Massachusetts, in the United States. They lie at the outer edge of Buzzards Bay, north of Martha's Vineyard, and separate Buzzards Bay from Vineyard Sound. The chain runs for about 14 miles and consists of 16 islands, most of which belong to the town of Gosnold in Dukes County.

Geography and history

The largest island is Naushon, which is privately owned by the Forbes family and is not open to the public. Other islands include Pasque, Nashawena, and Cuttyhunk, the outermost island, which has a small year-round population and a seasonal summer community. The islands were explored by Bartholomew Gosnold in 1602, who named them after Queen Elizabeth I of England; an attempted English settlement on Cuttyhunk that year was the first in New England, though it was abandoned within weeks.

Penikese Island

Penikese Island, near the middle of the chain, was the site of the Anderson School of Natural History in the 1870s, an early marine biology summer school, and later of a school for troubled youth.
