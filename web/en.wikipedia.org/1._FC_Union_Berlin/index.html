1. FC Union Berlin is a professional German football club in Köpenick, Berlin. The club emerged in 1966 in East Berlin and became known for its working-class identity and fiercely loyal supporters.

History

Union's roots go back to 1906. In the GDR era the club lived in the shadow of the state-favoured BFC Dynamo. After reunification Union endured financial crises; supporters literally helped rebuild the Stadion An der Alten Försterei in 2008–09, contributing thousands of volunteer hours.

Recent years

Union won promotion to the Bundesliga for the first time in 2019 and rapidly established itself, qualifying for European competition and, in 2023, for the UEFA Champions League.
