Jamaica is an island country in the Caribbean Sea. Spanning 10,990 square kilometres (4,240 sq mi) in area, it is the third-largest island of the Greater Antilles and of the Caribbean, after Cuba and Hispaniola. Jamaica lies about 145 km (90 mi) south of Cuba and 191 km (119 mi) west of Haiti. The country's name derives from Xaymaca, "land of wood and water", in the language of the indigenous Taíno people.

History

The island was inhabited by the Taíno (Arawak) people before Christopher Columbus claimed it for Spain in 1494. Most of the Taíno died from disease and harsh treatment, and enslaved Africans were brought to work on sugar plantations. England captured Jamaica from Spain in 1655, and it became a major sugar-producing colony and a centre of the Atlantic slave trade. Slavery was abolished in the British Empire in 1834, with full emancipation in 1838. Jamaica gained independence from the United Kingdom on 6 August 1962 and remains a Commonwealth realm with the British monarch as head of state, represented by a governor-general.

Geography and climate

Jamaica is a mountainous island; more than half of its land lies above 300 metres. The highest point is Blue Mountain Peak at 2,256 m (7,402 ft). The climate is tropical, with a hurricane season from June to November. The island is the birthplace of Blue Mountain coffee, among the most sought-after coffees in the world.

Demographics and culture

Most Jamaicans are of African descent, with minorities of European, Chinese, Indian, Lebanese and mixed ancestry. The official language is English, though most Jamaicans speak Jamaican Patois, an English-based creole. Jamaica is famous worldwide for its music — reggae, ska, rocksteady, mento and dancehall — and for artists such as Bob Marley. It is also the birthplace of the Rastafari movement and has produced world-class sprinters, including Usain Bolt, the fastest man in recorded history. The national motto is "Out of Many, One People".

Economy

Jamaica's economy is based on services, especially tourism, along with bauxite and alumina mining, agriculture (sugar, bananas, coffee), and remittances from the large Jamaican diaspora. See also Jamaican cuisine for the country's food traditions, including jerk cooking.
