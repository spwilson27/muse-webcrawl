Corvus (constellation)

Corvus is a small constellation in the Southern Celestial Hemisphere. Its name means "crow" in Latin; its four brightest stars form a distinctive quadrilateral, the "Sail", in spring skies.

Stars

The brightest stars are Gienah Corvi (γ), Kraz (β), Algorab (δ) and Alchiba (α). In Greek myth the crow was the bird of Apollo, punished for delaying on an errand.

Deep-sky objects

Corvus contains the Antennae Galaxies (NGC 4038/4039), a famous pair of colliding galaxies, and the planetary nebula NGC 4361.
