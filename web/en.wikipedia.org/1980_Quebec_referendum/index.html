The 1980 Quebec referendum, held on 20 May 1980, was the first referendum in Quebec on the place of the province within Canada. Voters were asked whether the Government of Quebec should be given a mandate to negotiate a new constitutional agreement with the rest of Canada based on sovereignty-association — political independence combined with a continued economic association with Canada.

The referendum was called by the Parti Québécois government of Premier René Lévesque, which had been elected in 1976 on a platform that included holding such a vote. The proposal was defeated by a margin of roughly 60 percent to 40 percent.

The question

The referendum question was long and carefully worded. It asked for a mandate to negotiate sovereignty-association, specifying that any resulting change in Quebec's political status would require approval in a second referendum. Critics in the federalist camp attacked the question as vague and designed to soften the meaning of independence; the provincial Liberal leader Claude Ryan campaigned for the "Non" side, while Prime Minister Pierre Trudeau intervened late in the campaign, promising a renewed federalism if Quebec voted No.

Campaign

The campaign was one of the most intense in Canadian history, with a turnout of 85.6 percent. The "Oui" campaign drew heavily on francophone nationalism and Lévesque's personal popularity; the "Non" campaign combined provincial Liberals, federal politicians and much of the business community. Voting split largely along linguistic lines: a majority of francophone voters supported Yes, while anglophone and allophone communities voted overwhelmingly No.

Aftermath

Lévesque conceded defeat on referendum night and urged his supporters to wait "until the next time" (à la prochaine fois). The federal government subsequently proceeded with the patriation of the Canadian Constitution, culminating in the Constitution Act, 1982, which Quebec's National Assembly did not endorse. The sovereignty question returned fifteen years later in the much closer 1995 Quebec referendum.
