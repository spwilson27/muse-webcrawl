Matt Reeves (born 27 April 1966) is an American filmmaker. He first gained recognition for creating, with J. J. Abrams, the television drama series Felicity (1998–2002), and for directing the found-footage monster film Cloverfield (2008). He went on to direct the acclaimed final two films of the Planet of the Apes reboot trilogy, Dawn of the Planet of the Apes (2014) and War for the Planet of the Apes (2017), and the superhero crime film The Batman (2022).

Early life and career

Reeves was born in Rockville Centre, New York, and raised in Los Angeles. He began making short films as a teenager and studied film at the University of Southern California, where he befriended J. J. Abrams. His early work included the screenplay for Under Siege 2: Dark Territory (1995) and directing the romantic comedy The Pallbearer (1996).

Breakthrough and major films

After co-creating Felicity, Reeves directed Cloverfield (2008), produced by Abrams, which became a surprise box-office hit and launched a franchise. He followed it with Let Me In (2010), an English-language remake of the Swedish vampire film Let the Right One In. Taking over the Apes series from its second instalment, Reeves brought an emphasis on character-driven, Caesar-centred storytelling; both of his entries were critical and commercial successes.

The Batman and later work

Reeves wrote and directed The Batman (2022), a noir-influenced reboot starring Robert Pattinson, which grossed over $770 million worldwide and was widely praised. He subsequently developed the spin-off series The Penguin (2024) for HBO as executive producer, expanding his standalone "Batman Epic Crime Saga," with a sequel film in development.
