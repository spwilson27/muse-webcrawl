Desertification

Desertification is a type of land degradation in drylands in which fertile areas become increasingly arid, losing bodies of water, vegetation and wildlife. It is caused by a combination of climate change and unsustainable human activities such as overgrazing, deforestation and poor irrigation.

Effects

Desertification threatens food security for hundreds of millions of people, particularly around the Sahara (the Sahel), in Central Asia and in northern China.

Responses

The United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (1994) coordinates responses, including projects such as Africa's Great Green Wall and China's shelterbelt programmes, along with sustainable land management and drought-resistant farming.
