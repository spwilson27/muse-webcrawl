China Construction Bank

FoundedOctober 1, 1954, Beijing, China
HeadquartersXicheng District, Beijing
TypePublic; state-owned commercial bank
IndustryBanking, financial services

China Construction Bank Corporation (CCB) is one of the "big four" state-owned commercial banks in China and one of the largest banks in the world by total assets and market capitalisation. It is a member of the Financial Stability Board's list of global systemically important banks.

History

CCB was founded on October 1, 1954, under the Ministry of Finance to administer funds for infrastructure and construction projects. It was transformed into a commercial bank in the 1990s as China reformed its financial system, and in 2005 it became the first of the big four banks to list shares publicly, on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, followed by a Shanghai listing in 2007.

Operations

The bank provides corporate and personal banking, treasury operations, and investment banking through thousands of branches in China and subsidiaries in major financial centres worldwide, with particular strength in infrastructure lending, mortgages, and construction finance.
