LocationPrimorsky Krai, Russian Far East
AreaAbout 179 km²
Established1925 (formalised)

Kedrovaya Pad («Кедровая Падь», "Korean Pine Valley") is a strict nature reserve (zapovednik) in Khasansky District of Primorsky Krai, in the Russian Far East near the border with China and North Korea. It is one of Russia's oldest nature reserves and is famous as a refuge of the critically endangered Amur leopard.

Flora and fauna

The reserve protects mixed forests of Korean pine, Mongolian oak and Manchurian fir, together with a rich southern Far Eastern fauna: Amur leopards, Amur tigers, Asiatic black bears, musk deer and many bird and insect species found nowhere else in Russia. Since 2012 the reserve has been administered together with the surrounding Land of the Leopard National Park.
