List of World War I flying aces

The following is a list of World War I flying aces. History's first ace was Frenchman Adolphe Pégoud, credited with five aerial victories. A flying ace is a military aviator credited with shooting down five or more enemy aircraft during aerial combat. The standards for confirming victories differed among nations, so totals are approximate.
