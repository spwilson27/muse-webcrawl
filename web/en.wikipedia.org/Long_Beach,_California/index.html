Long Beach, California

Long Beach
City in Los Angeles County, California
Incorporated: 1897
Area: approx. 133 km²
Population: approx. 466,000 (2020)

Long Beach is a coastal city in southeastern Los Angeles County, California, United States. Located on the Pacific coast about 20 miles south of downtown Los Angeles, it is the seventh-most populous city in California.

History and economy

The area was home to the Tongva people, with the village of Povuu'ngna nearby. Spanish, Mexican and American ranchos followed; the city grew with oil discoveries in 1921 at Signal Hill. The Port of Long Beach, adjacent to the Port of Los Angeles, forms one of the busiest container port complexes in the world.

Landmarks

The RMS Queen Mary is permanently moored as a hotel and museum. Other attractions include the Aquarium of the Pacific, the Long Beach Grand Prix IndyCar street race, Shoreline Village, Naples canals, and California State University, Long Beach.
