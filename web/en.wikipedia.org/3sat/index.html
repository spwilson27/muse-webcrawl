3sat

3sat is a free-to-air German-language television channel jointly operated by the public broadcasters of Germany (ZDF and ARD), Austria (ORF), and Switzerland (SRG SSR). Launched on 1 December 1984, it is a cultural channel focusing on arts, culture, science, documentaries, theatre, and film, with no commercial advertising.

History

3sat began as a satellite channel of ZDF, ORF, and the Swiss broadcaster SRG; Germany's ARD joined the partnership in 1993 after its own satellite channel, EinsPlus, closed. The name refers to the three participating countries and to satellite broadcasting. The channel is coordinated from ZDF's headquarters in Mainz, with each partner contributing programming from its own output and archives.

Programming

The schedule includes cultural magazines such as Kulturzeit, the science magazine nano, news bulletins from the partner broadcasters, documentary strands, recorded stage productions, concerts, and feature films. Annual theme days and live coverage of events such as the Ingeborg Bachmann Prize readings are fixtures of the schedule. 3sat is distributed across Europe by satellite, cable, and streaming, and is aimed at audiences throughout the German-speaking world.
