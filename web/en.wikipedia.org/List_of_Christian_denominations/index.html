List of Christian denominations

A Christian denomination is a distinct religious body within Christianity that comprises all church congregations of the same kind, identifiable by traits such as a name, particular history, organisation, leadership, theological doctrine, worship style and founder. This article lists denominations by the major branches of Christianity.

Catholicism

The Catholic Church, headed by the Pope in Rome, is the largest Christian body, alongside the Eastern Catholic Churches in communion with it.

Eastern Christianity

The Eastern Orthodox Church, Oriental Orthodox Churches, and the Church of the East tradition each comprise autocephalous churches such as the Greek, Russian and Coptic Orthodox churches.

Protestantism

Protestant denominations include Lutherans, Anglicans, Reformed/Presbyterians, Baptists, Methodists, Adventists, Pentecostals, and many independent and non-denominational churches, generally tracing their origins to the 16th-century Reformation or later revival movements.

Restorationism

Restorationist traditions include the Jehovah's Witnesses, the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and Christadelphians.
