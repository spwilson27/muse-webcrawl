DeepL Translator

DeveloperDeepL SE
Launched2017
HeadquartersCologne, Germany
TypeNeural machine translation

DeepL Translator is a neural machine translation service launched in August 2017 by the German company DeepL SE (formerly Linguee). It gained wide attention on release for producing translations that reviewers and blind tests judged more fluent and natural than those of competing services such as Google Translate.

Technology

DeepL's models are trained on the company's large bilingual corpus gathered from the web and its Linguee search engine, refined with curated data. The service uses neural network architectures trained on supercomputers in Iceland, and supports dozens of languages, chiefly European languages plus Japanese and Chinese.

Products

Besides the free web translator, DeepL offers a subscription Pro service with an API, document translation for Word and PowerPoint files, desktop applications, and DeepL Write, an AI writing assistant that improves grammar and style. The company became one of Europe's most valuable AI startups, reaching a valuation of about billion in 2024.

Reception

DeepL has been praised for handling idiom and register well, though its language coverage remains narrower than that of its largest competitors.
