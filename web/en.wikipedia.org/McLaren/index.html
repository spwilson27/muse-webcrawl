McLaren Group Limited is a British holding company based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, England. It oversees McLaren Racing, one of the most successful teams in Formula One history, and McLaren Automotive, a manufacturer of high-performance sports cars. The group traces its origins to the racing team founded by New Zealand driver Bruce McLaren in 1963.

History

Bruce McLaren founded Bruce McLaren Motor Racing in 1963 and entered Formula One in 1966, winning its first Grand Prix at Spa in 1968. After Bruce McLaren's death in a testing accident at Goodwood in 1970, the team was led by Teddy Mayer and then merged with Ron Dennis's Project Four in 1980. Under Dennis, McLaren won numerous World Championships with drivers including Niki Lauda, Alain Prost, Ayrton Senna, Mika Häkkinen and Lewis Hamilton.

McLaren Racing

McLaren Racing competes in Formula One as the McLaren Formula 1 Team, and also fields entries in IndyCar (Arrow McLaren), Formula E and Extreme E at various times. The team has won multiple Constructors' and Drivers' Championships; its dominant 1988 season with the Honda-powered MP4/4, driven by Senna and Prost, won 15 of 16 races. McLaren returned to the top of Formula One in the 2020s, winning the Constructors' Championship in 2024, its first since 1998.

McLaren Automotive

McLaren Automotive was relaunched as a standalone road-car company in 2010, building on the legacy of the 1992 McLaren F1 supercar. Its model range has included the MP4-12C, 650S, 720S, P1 hybrid hypercar, Senna, Speedtail, Artura and W1, all built in carbon fibre at the McLaren Production Centre in Woking.
