Dhanbad

Dhanbad is a city in the Indian state of Jharkhand and the administrative headquarters of Dhanbad district. It is widely known as the "Coal Capital of India" because of the extensive coal mining in and around the city, which lies in the Damodar Valley coalfields.

Economy

Coal dominates the economy: Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL), a subsidiary of Coal India, has its headquarters in Dhanbad, and Tata Steel and other companies operate mines in the surrounding Jharia coalfield. The city is also a major railway division of Indian Railways and an important trading and industrial centre for the region.

Education

Dhanbad is home to the Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad — founded in 1926 as the Indian School of Mines and converted to an IIT in 2016 — one of India's leading institutions for mining, petroleum, and earth sciences.

Geography

The city lies on the Chota Nagpur Plateau near the Damodar River. The surrounding region is marked by coal pits, washeries, and the long-burning underground fires of the Jharia coalfield.
