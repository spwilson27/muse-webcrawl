Miguel McKelvey

Miguel McKelvey
Born: 1974, Eugene, Oregon, U.S.
Education: University of Oregon (architecture)
Known for: Co-founding WeWork

Miguel McKelvey (born 1974) is an American entrepreneur and designer best known as the co-founder, with Adam Neumann, of the coworking company WeWork, where he served as chief culture officer and led the company's design and construction operations.

Early life and career

McKelvey grew up in Eugene, Oregon, and studied architecture at the University of Oregon. After graduating he worked for an architecture firm in Portland on retail rollouts, and later moved to New York, where he worked on the design of American Apparel's stores. There he met Adam Neumann, a fellow tenant in the same Brooklyn office building.

WeWork

In 2008 McKelvey and Neumann founded Green Desk, an eco-friendly coworking space in Brooklyn, and in 2010 they sold it and launched WeWork in New York's SoHo district. McKelvey, drawing on his architectural training, built out the company's in-house design, architecture and construction teams, which at their peak numbered hundreds and delivered the brand's signature glass-walled, communal aesthetic worldwide. As chief culture officer he also fronted the company's community ethos and its "We" rebrand, which expanded into co-living (WeLive) and education (WeGrow). Following WeWork's failed 2019 IPO and Neumann's departure, McKelvey announced in June 2020 that he would leave the company.

Later ventures

After WeWork, McKelvey joined the venture firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) as a partner working with its American Dynamism and cultural initiatives, departing in 2022. He has since been involved in new ventures in the built environment and community space.
