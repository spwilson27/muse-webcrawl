International Association for Near-Death Studies

The International Association for Near-Death Studies (IANDS) is a non-profit organization founded in 1981 to promote responsible, multidisciplinary exploration of near-death experiences (NDEs) and related phenomena, and to support people who have had such experiences.

History and activities

IANDS grew out of the research community around figures such as Kenneth Ring and Bruce Greyson. It publishes the peer-reviewed Journal of Near-Death Studies (founded as Anabiosis), holds an annual conference, maintains archives of experience accounts, and supports local discussion and support groups. Greyson developed the widely used Greyson NDE Scale under its auspices.
