Derrick May (musician)

Derrick May (born April 6, 1963), also known as Mayday and Rhythim Is Rhythim, is an American electronic musician from Belleville, Michigan, United States. May is credited with pioneering techno music in the 1980s along with collaborators Juan Atkins and Kevin Saunderson, together known as the Belleville Three.

Career

May formed friendships with Atkins and Saunderson at Belleville High School. Under the name Rhythim Is Rhythim, he released influential tracks including "Nude Photo" (1987) and "Strings of Life" (1987), the latter made with pianist Michael James and becoming one of the most famous tracks in techno history. In 1988 he founded the label Transmat Records, which released music by artists such as Carl Craig and Stacey Pullen.

Musical style

May famously described techno as "like Detroit… a complete mistake, it's like George Clinton and Kraftwerk are stuck in an elevator with only a sequencer to keep them company". His productions combined Chicago house and European electronic influences with the funk of Parliament-Funkadelic.

Legacy

Although his recorded output largely ceased after the early 1990s, May remained an active international DJ, and the Belleville Three are regarded as the originators of Detroit techno's first wave.
