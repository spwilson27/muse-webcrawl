Plane Train

The Plane Train is an automated people mover system at Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. It connects the domestic terminal and all seven concourses, A through F, via an underground tunnel, and is one of the busiest airport people mover systems in the world.

Operation

The system opened in 1980. Trains run in a loop along a line about 4.5 kilometres long, stopping at each concourse roughly every two minutes at peak times. Passengers reach the stations by escalators and moving walkways in the transportation mall beneath the airport.

Expansion

The line was extended to serve the Maynard H. Jackson Jr. International Terminal and Concourse F, which opened in 2012. The fleet uses rubber-tyred automated vehicles guided along a central guideway.
