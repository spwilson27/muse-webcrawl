Planet Labs

Planet Labs PBC is an American public Earth imaging company based in San Francisco, California. Its stated mission is to image the entirety of the Earth's landmass every day to monitor changes and identify trends. The company operates the largest fleet of Earth-imaging satellites in history, consisting of hundreds of small "Dove" CubeSats, and sells imagery and derived data to customers in agriculture, forestry, mapping, finance, and government.

History

Planet was founded in 2010 as Cosmogia by former NASA scientists Chris Boshuizen, Will Marshall, and Robbie Schingler. The founders sought to democratise access to satellite imagery using inexpensive, mass-produced small satellites built from consumer electronics. The first Doves were launched in 2013, and large batches ("flocks") followed, including a record-setting launch of 88 Doves on an Indian PSLV rocket in February 2017.

The company acquired BlackBridge (operator of the RapidEye constellation) in 2015 and Google's Terra Bella satellite business, with its higher-resolution SkySat satellites, in 2017. In December 2021 Planet became a public company through a merger with a special-purpose acquisition company, dMY Technology Group IV, listing on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker PL.

Satellites and data

Dove satellites are 3U CubeSats weighing about 5 kg, imaging at roughly 3 metres per pixel in sun-synchronous orbit, enabling daily global coverage. The SkySat constellation provides sub-metre imagery and video on demand. Planet's archive — billions of square kilometres of imagery — is used to track deforestation, crop health, urban growth, natural disasters, and military activity; its imagery has been widely cited by journalists, including in coverage of conflicts and environmental change.
