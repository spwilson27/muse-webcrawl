Sriharikota

Sriharikota
State: Andhra Pradesh
District: Tirupati
Country: India
Known for: Satish Dhawan Space Centre

Sriharikota is a barrier island off the Bay of Bengal coast in Andhra Pradesh, India. It separates the Pulicat Lake from the Bay of Bengal and is home to the Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC SHAR), India's primary orbital launch site operated by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

Geography

The island lies in the Tirupati district (formerly Nellore district) of Andhra Pradesh, near the town of Sullurpeta. It is roughly 80 kilometres north of Chennai. Pulicat Lake, one of India's largest brackish-water lagoons, lies to its west.

Space centre

The Sriharikota Range was established in 1969 and operational from 1971. Renamed the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in 2002 after the former ISRO chairman, it has two launch pads from which vehicles including the PSLV and GSLV/LVM3 families are launched.
