Mumbai Indians

The Mumbai Indians are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, that compete in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Founded in 2008, the team is owned by India's biggest conglomerate, Reliance Industries, through its subsidiary Indiawin Sports. They play their home matches at the Wankhede Stadium.

History

Mumbai Indians won their first IPL title in 2013, defeating Chennai Super Kings in the final. Under the captaincy of Rohit Sharma they went on to win further championships in 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020, making them one of the most successful franchises in the league's history. Notable players have included Sachin Tendulkar, Lasith Malinga, Kieron Pollard and Jasprit Bumrah.

Brand

The team's primary colours are blue and gold. Mumbai Indians have consistently been ranked among the most valuable IPL franchises and field sister teams in other leagues, such as MI Cape Town and MI New York.
