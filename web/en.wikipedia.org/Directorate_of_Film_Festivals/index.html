Directorate of Film Festivals

The Directorate of Film Festivals (DFF) was an organisation of the Government of India, functioning under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, set up in 1973 with the objective of promoting Indian cinema. It was most widely known as the organising body of the International Film Festival of India (IFFI), the National Film Awards and the Indian Panorama section. In 2022 the Directorate was merged, along with several other media units, into the National Film Development Corporation (NFDC) as part of a rationalisation of the ministry's film bodies.

Functions

The Directorate organised film festivals in India and coordinated Indian participation in film festivals abroad. It ran the annual International Film Festival of India, held in Goa since 2004, and administered the selection of films for the Indian Panorama — a curated selection of feature and non-feature Indian films screened at IFFI and sent to festivals worldwide. It also organised, on behalf of the Government, the National Film Awards, including the selection process by juries and the presentation ceremony at which the awards, including the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, are conferred by the President of India.

Other activities included organising national and international film weeks, cultural exchange programme screenings, and maintaining a collection of films and festival catalogues. India's official entry to the Academy Awards for Best International Feature Film is selected by the Film Federation of India rather than the Directorate, though the Directorate supported such participations in festival contexts.

Merger

In December 2020 the Union Cabinet approved the merger of four film media units — the Directorate of Film Festivals, the Films Division, the National Film Archive of India and the Children's Film Society — into the National Film Development Corporation. The merger was implemented in 2022, with the Directorate's functions, staff and events such as IFFI and the National Film Awards passing under the NFDC umbrella.
