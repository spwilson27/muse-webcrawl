Saint Stephen's Day

Date26 December (Western); 27 December (Eastern)
HonoursSaint Stephen, first Christian martyr
Also known asBoxing Day; Wren Day (Ireland)

Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian saint's day commemorating Saint Stephen, the first martyr (protomartyr) of Christianity, stoned to death as recorded in the Acts of the Apostles. It falls on 26 December in the Western Church and 27 December in the Eastern Church.

Customs

The day is a public holiday in many European countries. In Ireland it is known as Wren Day (Lá an Dreoilín), traditionally involving processions with a (now usually artificial) wren. In Britain and Commonwealth countries, 26 December is widely observed as Boxing Day. In Catalonia, the day (Sant Esteve) is marked with family gatherings.
