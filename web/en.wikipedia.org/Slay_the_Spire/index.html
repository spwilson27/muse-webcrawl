Slay the Spire

Slay the Spire is a roguelike deck-building video game developed by the American studio Mega Crit and published by Humble Bundle. It entered early access in 2017 and was fully released for Windows, macOS, and Linux in January 2019, with console and mobile versions following. It is widely credited with popularizing the roguelike deck-builder genre.

Gameplay

The player chooses one of several characters, each with unique card pools, and climbs a procedurally generated spire across multiple acts, choosing paths through nodes of battles, elite fights, shops, campfires, and random events. Combat is turn-based and card-driven: each turn the player draws cards and has limited energy to play attacks, skills, and powers. Winning battles adds a card to the deck, and relics provide persistent passive abilities. Death ends the run, but it unlocks new cards and relics for future attempts, and high “Ascension” levels increase the difficulty.

Reception and influence

The game sold millions of copies and received widespread acclaim for its depth and replayability, with praise for how its card and relic synergies interlock. It spawned many imitators and a board game adaptation, and a sequel, Slay the Spire II, was announced in 2024.
