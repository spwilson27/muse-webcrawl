Deportivo Toluca Fútbol Club, commonly known as Toluca, is a Mexican professional football club based in Toluca, State of Mexico. Nicknamed Los Diablos Rojos (The Red Devils) for their red kit, the club plays in Liga MX and is one of the most successful teams in Mexican football.

History

The club was founded in 1917. Toluca enjoyed a golden era in the late 1990s and 2000s under coaches such as Enrique Meza, winning multiple league titles with squads built around Paraguayan striker José Saturnino Cardozo, one of the league's all-time top scorers.

Honours

Toluca has won the Mexican Primera División/Liga MX championship numerous times (among the most of any club, behind only América and Guadalajara) and has also won the CONCACAF Champions' Cup. Its home ground, Estadio Nemesio Díez, is known as one of Mexico's oldest and most atmospheric stadiums.
