Lucre Basin

LocationCusco Region, Peru
Key sitePikillacta
CulturesWari, Inca

The Lucre Basin is a lake basin and valley in the Cusco Region of the Peruvian Andes, southeast of the city of Cusco, centered on Lake Huacarpay and the town of Lucre.

Archaeology

The basin is best known for Pikillacta, a large planned administrative center built by the Wari (Huari) Empire around the 6th–9th centuries CE, one of the most important Wari sites outside their Ayacucho heartland. Later, the Inca developed the area with estates and agricultural terraces, and the basin also contains the colonial-era and pre-Inca site of Tipón nearby. The lake and its wetlands remain an important habitat for Andean birdlife.
