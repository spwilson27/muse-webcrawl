Mars, Incorporated

Mars, Incorporated is an American multinational manufacturer of confectionery, pet food, and other food products and a provider of animal care services, with US$45 billion in annual sales in 2022. It is entirely owned by the Mars family and is headquartered in McLean, Virginia.

History

The company was founded by Franklin Clarence Mars, who began making and selling butter cream candies in Tacoma, Washington, in 1911. In Minneapolis he and his son Forrest Mars developed the Milky Way bar (1923) and the Mars bar (1932, in Britain). Forrest Mars later built a major confectionery business in Europe and, after reconciling with the family firm, merged his operations with it.

Mars is known for brands including M&M's, Snickers, Twix, Skittles, Dove/Galaxy chocolate, and Pedigree and Whiskas pet foods. Its 2008 acquisition of the Wrigley Company for about $23 billion made it the world's largest confectionery company. In 2017 it acquired VCA Inc., a chain of animal hospitals, expanding its pet care business. In 2024 Mars announced an agreement to acquire Kellanova (the snack business split from Kellogg's) for about $35.9 billion.

Divisions

Mars operates principally through Mars Wrigley (confectionery), Mars Petcare, Mars Food & Nutrition, and Mars Veterinary Health. The company is noted for its private, family-owned status and a corporate culture built around its "Five Principles": Quality, Responsibility, Mutuality, Efficiency and Freedom.
