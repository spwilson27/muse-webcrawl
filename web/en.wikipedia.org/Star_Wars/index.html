Star Wars

The Star Wars franchise logo.

Star Wars is an American epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas, which began with the film Star Wars (later subtitled Episode IV – A New Hope) in 1977 and quickly became a worldwide pop culture phenomenon. The franchise follows the adventures of characters in a galaxy "a long time ago in a galaxy far, far away", centred on the struggle between the Jedi, who use the mystical Force for good, and the Sith, who turn to its dark side.

Films

The main "Skywalker saga" comprises three trilogies: the original trilogy (1977–1983) following Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa and Han Solo against the Galactic Empire; the prequel trilogy (1999–2005) chronicling Anakin Skywalker's fall to become Darth Vader; and the sequel trilogy (2015–2019), including The Last Jedi, following Rey and the Resistance against the First Order. Standalone films include Rogue One (2016) and Solo (2018).

Expanded media and ownership

The franchise expanded into television series such as The Clone Wars, Rebels and the live-action The Mandalorian, plus novels, comics and video games. Lucasfilm was acquired by The Walt Disney Company in 2012 for about $4 billion. The films have grossed more than $10 billion worldwide, making Star Wars one of the highest-grossing media franchises of all time.

Cultural impact

Star Wars reshaped the film industry with its advances in special effects through Industrial Light & Magic, its John Williams scores, and its merchandising empire. Its themes draw on mythology, including Joseph Campbell's hero's journey, as well as Westerns, samurai films and Second World War aerial combat.
