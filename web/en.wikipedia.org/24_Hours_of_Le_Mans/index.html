24 Hours of Le Mans

"Le Mans" redirects here. For the city, see Le Mans. For other uses, see Le Mans (disambiguation).

The 24 Hours of Le Mans (French: 24 Heures du Mans) is the world's oldest active sports car endurance race, held annually since 1923 near the town of Le Mans, France. It is one of the most prestigious automobile races in the world and, together with the Monaco Grand Prix and the Indianapolis 500, forms the informal Triple Crown of Motorsport.

History

The race was first held in May 1923, organised by the Automobile Club de l'Ouest (ACO), as a test of the endurance of both car and driver. Unlike fixed-distance races, Le Mans awards the win to the car that covers the greatest distance in 24 hours. The circuit is a mix of closed public roads and a dedicated racing section, and has been lengthened and modified several times; it currently measures 13.626 km (8.467 miles), including the famous Mulsanne Straight, now interrupted by two chicanes.

The race has been won by many of the great names of motorsport. Porsche holds the record for overall victories with 19, followed by Audi with 13. The all-time individual record is held by Danish driver Tom Kristensen with nine wins. The distance record, set in 2010, is 5,259.8 km (3,268 miles) covered in 380 laps.

The race

Three drivers typically share each car, with no driver permitted to drive for more than 14 hours in total or more than four hours in any six-hour period. The race starts in the afternoon (traditionally 16:00 local time, moved to 15:00 in some years) and runs through the night to the following afternoon. Cars must be able to cover a minimum distance and run at night with working headlights; mechanical repairs are permitted only with tools carried on board or in the pit.

Classes

The modern race is run for several classes at once: Hypercar (the top class, including Le Mans Hypercars and LMDh prototypes), LMP2 prototypes, and LMGT3 grand tourers. In earlier eras the field included Group C, Group 5, and other prototype and GT categories.

Records

  • Most overall wins (manufacturer): Porsche — 19
  • Most overall wins (driver): Tom Kristensen — 9 (1997, 2000–2005, 2008, 2013)
  • Greatest distance: 5,259.8 km (2010, Audi R15+ TDI)
  • Fastest race lap: 3:17.297 (2017, Toyota TS050 Hybrid)
