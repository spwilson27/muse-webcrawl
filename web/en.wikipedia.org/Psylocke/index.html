Psylocke

Psylocke is the codename of two fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, commonly associated with the X-Men. The name was first used by Elizabeth "Betsy" Braddock, a British mutant telepath, and later by the Japanese assassin Kwannon, whose body Braddock long inhabited.

Publication history

Betsy Braddock first appeared in Captain Britain #8 (1976), created by Chris Claremont and Herb Trimpe, as the twin sister of Brian Braddock. She joined the X-Men in Uncanny X-Men #213 (1987). In a controversial 1989 storyline her mind was placed in the body of the assassin Kwannon, and she thereafter appeared as a Japanese-bodied ninja telepath, fighting with a "psychic knife". In 2018 the two women were restored to their original bodies; Kwannon subsequently took the Psylocke name herself in the Krakoan era, while Braddock became Captain Britain.

Powers and abilities

Psylocke is a telepath and telekinetic, traditionally able to focus her psionic energy into a psychic knife — described as "the focused totality of her telepathic powers" — that can disrupt a target's nervous system. The ninja incarnation is also a highly trained martial artist.

In other media

The character appeared in the film X-Men: Apocalypse (2016), played by Olivia Munn, and in various animated series and video games.
