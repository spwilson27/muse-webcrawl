Blót

Blót is the term for a blood sacrifice in Norse paganism offered to the gods and other supernatural beings. The term is cognate with English "blood" and relates to Old Norse blóta, "to sacrifice, worship".

Practice

Historical sources such as the Heimskringla describe blóts held at seasonal festivals, in which animals were slaughtered, their blood sprinkled on altars and participants, and the meat consumed at a communal feast accompanied by ritual toasts.

Modern practice

Modern Heathen movements have revived the blót as a ritual, usually replacing blood sacrifice with offerings of mead or food.
