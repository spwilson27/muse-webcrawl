Internet exchange point

An Internet exchange point (IXP) is a physical infrastructure through which Internet service providers (ISPs) and content delivery networks exchange Internet traffic between their networks (peering). IXPs reduce the portion of an ISP's traffic that must be delivered through upstream transit providers, lowering cost and latency.

Operation

Participants connect to shared Ethernet switches in colocation facilities and establish BGP sessions, often multilaterally via a route server. Among the largest exchanges by traffic are DE-CIX in Frankfurt, AMS-IX in Amsterdam, and LINX in London. Many IXPs are non-profit or association-owned; commercial, for-profit exchanges also exist, chiefly in the United States.
