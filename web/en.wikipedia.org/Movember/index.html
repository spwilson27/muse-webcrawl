Movember (a portmanteau of "mo", slang for moustache, and "November") is an annual event involving the growing of moustaches during the month of November to raise awareness of men's health issues, such as prostate cancer, testicular cancer, and men's suicide. It is run by the Movember Foundation, a charity headquartered in Melbourne, Australia.

The movement began in 2003 when a group of friends in Melbourne — often credited to Travis Garone and Luke Slattery — decided to revive the moustache as a fundraiser; in 2004 the campaign raised funds for the Prostate Cancer Foundation of Australia. Since then it has grown into a global campaign operating in more than 20 countries, and the Foundation has raised over a billion Australian dollars cumulatively, funding more than a thousand men's health projects.

How it works

Participants, known as "Mo Bros" and "Mo Sisters", register to grow a moustache for the 30 days of November, starting clean-shaven, and gather sponsorship. Many also take part in "Move for Movember", running or walking 60 km during the month — for the 60 men lost to suicide every hour globally. The Foundation is regularly ranked among the top non-government organisations in the world.

Ambassadors and partners

Notable supporters have included sports teams and celebrities across rugby, cricket, ice hockey and football. The Movember Foundation also funds the mental-health initiative and research programs in prostate and testicular cancer survivorship.
