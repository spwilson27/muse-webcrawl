End of World War II in Asia

World War II in Asia ended in August–September 1945 with the surrender of Imperial Japan following the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, the Soviet declaration of war and invasion of Manchuria, and Emperor Hirohito's announcement accepting the Potsdam Declaration.

Final months

By mid-1945 Allied forces had taken Iwo Jima and Okinawa and blockaded Japan. On August 6 the United States dropped an atomic bomb on Hiroshima, and on August 9 on Nagasaki, the same day Soviet forces invaded Manchukuo. Hirohito's radio address on August 15 announced Japan's acceptance of the Allied terms, though some units fought on.

Surrender and aftermath

The formal instrument of surrender was signed aboard USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay on September 2, 1945. Japan was occupied under General Douglas MacArthur; remaining Japanese forces surrendered across Asia, and prisoners, war-crimes trials, and the division of Korea and other territories shaped the postwar order.
