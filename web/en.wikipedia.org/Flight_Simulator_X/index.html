Microsoft Flight Simulator X

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Microsoft Flight Simulator X (abbreviated FSX) is a 2006 flight simulation video game developed by Aces Game Studio and published by Microsoft Game Studios for Microsoft Windows. It is the tenth major version of the Microsoft Flight Simulator series and was released in October 2006 in standard and deluxe editions.

Features

FSX covered the entire Earth with scenery generated from geographic data, more than 24,000 airports, and selectable aircraft ranging from the de Havilland Beaver floatplane to the Boeing 747-400. New features included a mission system (search and rescue, air racing at Reno, test-pilot flights), shared-sky multiplayer with air traffic control roles, wildlife and road traffic in the scenery, and support for DirectX 10 effects in later updates. The Acceleration expansion pack (2007) added aircraft such as the F/A-18 Hornet and the P-51D Racer and additional missions including carrier operations.

Development and legacy

FSX was the last simulator built by Aces Studio, which Microsoft closed in 2009. The game engine was licensed for Lockheed Martin's Prepar3D training simulator. In 2014 Dovetail Games re-released FSX on Steam as Flight Simulator X: Steam Edition. A large third-party add-on ecosystem — scenery, aircraft, weather engines — kept FSX in wide use among hobbyist pilots until and beyond the release of Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020).
