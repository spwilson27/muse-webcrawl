Extended vocal technique

Extended vocal techniques are vocal techniques that go beyond conventional singing, used in experimental and contemporary classical music, avant-garde performance, and some popular music genres. They include techniques such as overtone singing, throat singing, growling, screaming, falsetto extremes, sprechgesang, inhaled phonation, and vocal percussion.

Techniques

Overtone singing produces two pitches simultaneously by shaping the vocal tract resonances. Tuvan and Mongolian throat singing are among the best-known traditional forms. Other techniques include subharmonics, multiphonics, ululation, and microtonal inflection.

Notable practitioners

Performers associated with extended vocal techniques include Demetrio Stratos, Diamanda Galás, Meredith Monk, Mike Patton, and Joan La Barbara.
