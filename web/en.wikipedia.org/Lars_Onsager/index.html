Lars Onsager

Lars Onsager (November 27, 1903 – October 5, 1976) was a Norwegian-American physical chemist and theoretical physicist. He won the 1968 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the reciprocal relations bearing his name, a fundamental result in the thermodynamics of irreversible processes.

Work

Onsager's reciprocal relations (1931) state that certain cross-coefficients in transport equations are equal. He also gave the exact solution of the two-dimensional Ising model in zero field (1944), a landmark of statistical mechanics, and worked on electrolytes, liquid crystals, turbulence and the theory of ice.

Career

Born in Kristiania (Oslo), he emigrated to the United States, working at Johns Hopkins and Brown before settling at Yale University, where he spent most of his career.
