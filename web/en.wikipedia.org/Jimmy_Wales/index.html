Jimmy Wales

Jimmy Donal Wales (born August 7, 1966), also known by the pseudonym Jimbo, is an American Internet entrepreneur, former financial trader, and political activist. He is a co-founder of the online non-profit encyclopedia Wikipedia and the for-profit web hosting company Fandom (formerly Wikia). He has also worked on other online projects, including Bomis, Nupedia, WikiTribune (later Wikitribune Social, relaunched as Trust Café), and WT Social.

Early life and career

Wales was born in Huntsville, Alabama, and educated at Auburn University and the University of Alabama, studying finance. He worked as a research director at the Chicago futures and options firm Chicago Options Associates before founding the web portal Bomis in 1996.

Wikipedia

In 2000 Wales launched Nupedia, an expert-reviewed free encyclopedia, with Larry Sanger as editor-in-chief. On January 15, 2001, Sanger and Wales launched Wikipedia as a feeder project using wiki software; it rapidly outgrew Nupedia and became one of the world's most-visited websites. Wales co-founded the Wikimedia Foundation in 2003 and served as its board chair until 2006, thereafter holding the title of chair emeritus and the board's founder seat. His role in defining Wikipedia's open, community-governed editorial model has been recognised by Time magazine, which named him one of the world's most influential people in 2006.
