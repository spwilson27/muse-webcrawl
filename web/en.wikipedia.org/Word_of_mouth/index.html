Word of mouth

Word of mouth is the passing of information from person to person by oral communication, and, in marketing, the unpaid spread of favourable opinions about products and services between consumers. It is consistently found to be among the most trusted forms of product information.

Word-of-mouth marketing

Marketers seek to stimulate positive word of mouth through referral programmes, influencer seeding, and memorable customer experiences. Negative word of mouth spreads readily as well and can damage reputations quickly.

Electronic word of mouth

Online reviews, ratings, and social media posts extend personal communication to large audiences. Research on electronic word of mouth examines valence, volume, and reviewer credibility, as well as the industry of fabricated reviews.

Measurement

Metrics such as the Net Promoter Score are used to estimate customers' likelihood of recommending a business to others.
