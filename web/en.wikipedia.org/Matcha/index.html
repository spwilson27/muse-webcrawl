Matcha

TypePowdered green tea
OriginChina; developed in Japan
ColourVivid green

Matcha is a finely ground powder of specially grown and processed green tea leaves, traditionally consumed in the Japanese tea ceremony. The tea plants are shade-grown for several weeks before harvest, increasing chlorophyll and amino acid content, and the steamed leaves (tencha) are stone-ground into powder.

Preparation

Matcha is whisked with hot water using a bamboo whisk (chasen) in a bowl (chawan), producing a frothy drink. Two preparations are common: thin tea (usucha) and thick tea (koicha).

Uses

Beyond the tea ceremony, matcha flavours sweets, noodles, ice cream, and lattes, and has gained worldwide popularity as a health drink rich in caffeine and L-theanine.
