Hesse

Country: Germany
Capital: Wiesbaden
Largest city: Frankfurt am Main
Area: 21,115 km²
Population: ~6.3 million

Hesse (German: Hessen) is a state (Land) of the Federal Republic of Germany, located in west-central Germany with its capital at Wiesbaden and its largest city at Frankfurt am Main. Covering about 21,100 square kilometres, it has a population of roughly 6.3 million. Hesse borders North Rhine-Westphalia, Rhineland-Palatinate, Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, Thuringia, and Lower Saxony.

History

The state takes its name from the Chatti, a Germanic tribe. In the Middle Ages the territory was ruled by the Landgraviate of Hesse (from 1264), which was divided in 1567 into Hesse-Kassel, Hesse-Darmstadt, and smaller lines after the death of Philip the Magnanimous; Hessian soldiers hired out to Britain in the American Revolutionary War gave English the word "Hessians." The modern state was created in 1945–46 in the American occupation zone (initially as "Greater Hesse") by merging Hesse-Darmstadt with the Prussian province of Hesse-Nassau.

Geography and economy

The landscape is one of wooded low mountain ranges — the Taunus, Westerwald, Rhön, and Odenwald — crossed by the Rhine, Main, Fulda, and Lahn rivers. The Frankfurt Rhine-Main region is one of Germany's economic engines: Frankfurt hosts the European Central Bank, the Deutsche Bundesbank, the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, and one of Europe's busiest airports. Chemical and pharmaceutical companies (Merck in Darmstadt) and the automotive industry (Opel in Rüsselsheim) are also significant.

Culture

Kassel is home to the quinquennial documenta exhibition of contemporary art. Regional specialities include apple wine (Apfelwein), Handkäse cheese, and green sauce (Grüne Soße).
