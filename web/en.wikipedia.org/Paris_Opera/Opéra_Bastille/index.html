Opéra Bastille
Location: Place de la Bastille, Paris
Architect: Carlos Ott
Inaugurated: 13 July 1989
Capacity: 2,723 (main auditorium)

The Opéra Bastille is a modern opera house in Paris, France, located on the Place de la Bastille. It is the main home of the Paris Opera, together with the older Palais Garnier, and was inaugurated on 13 July 1989, the eve of the bicentenary of the storming of the Bastille.

History

The opera house was one of President François Mitterrand's grands projets. The design, by Uruguayan-Canadian architect Carlos Ott, was chosen in an international competition in 1983. Its curved glass and granite facade and huge backstage facilities were intended to create a "popular and modern" opera house.

Facilities

The main auditorium seats 2,723 people. The building contains vast workshops and rehearsal spaces allowing complete sets to be built, stored and moved on stage, so several productions can alternate in the same week.
