Paris Opera Ballet
Founded: 1669
Home: Palais Garnier, Paris
Dancers: ~150

The Paris Opera Ballet (Ballet de l'Opéra national de Paris) is the ballet company of the Paris Opera and the oldest national ballet company in the world, founded in 1669 under Louis XIV as part of the Académie Royale de Musique.

History

The company grew out of the court ballet of the French monarchy and Louis XIV's Académie Royale de Danse of 1661. It was at the Paris Opera that the Romantic ballet flourished in the 19th century with works such as Giselle (1841). In the 20th century, Serge Lifar and later Rudolf Nureyev, director from 1983 to 1989, shaped its modern profile.

Structure

Dancers are ranked in a strict hierarchy from quadrilles up to the handful of étoiles (stars). Most are trained at the Paris Opera Ballet School, founded in 1713 and now based in Nanterre. The company performs mainly at the Palais Garnier.
