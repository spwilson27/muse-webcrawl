Red wine

Red wine is a type of wine made from dark-coloured grape varieties. Its colour comes from anthocyanin pigments in the grape skins, which are fermented together with the juice — unlike white wine, where skins are removed before fermentation. Colour ranges from deep violet in young wines to brick red in mature wines.

Major red varieties include Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot noir, Syrah and Tempranillo. Fermentation typically runs one to two weeks with regular mixing of the skin cap; many reds are aged in oak barrels, which add tannin structure and flavour. Red wine has been produced for at least 6,000 years, originating in the Caucasus region.
