Ourém

Ourém is a municipality in the Santarém District of central Portugal, in the Centro region. It is known for its medieval castle and its proximity to Fátima, one of the most important Catholic pilgrimage sites in the world, which lies within the municipality.

History

The hilltop castle of Ourém dates to the Moorish period and was taken during the Portuguese Reconquista. The town received charters in the 12th and 13th centuries. The castle and old town were badly damaged by the 1755 Lisbon earthquake and later restored.

Geography

The municipality covers around 416 square kilometres and had a population of roughly 45,000 in recent censuses. It is divided into civil parishes including Nossa Senhora da Piedade, where the modern town centre lies below the castle hill.
