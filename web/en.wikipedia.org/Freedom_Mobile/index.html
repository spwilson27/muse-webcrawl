Freedom Mobile Inc. is a Canadian wireless telecommunications provider owned by Quebecor. Founded in 2008 as Wind Mobile by Globalive, it launched service in 2009 as a low-cost challenger to Canada's three national carriers, Rogers, Bell, and Telus.

History

Wind Mobile began offering service in the Greater Toronto Area in December 2009 and expanded to other cities in Ontario, British Columbia, and Alberta. In 2016, Shaw Communications acquired the company for $1.6 billion and rebranded it as Freedom Mobile in November of that year. Following the Rogers–Shaw merger, Freedom Mobile was sold to Quebecor's Vidéotron in 2023 as a condition of regulatory approval, in a transaction valued at $2.85 billion.

Network and services

Freedom operates its own wireless network in urban areas of Ontario, Alberta, and British Columbia, and provides nationwide coverage through roaming agreements. It is known for low-cost plans and was an early Canadian adopter of unlimited data offerings with throttled speeds after a fair-use threshold.
