Brewer's yeast

Brewer's yeast is a kind of yeast cultivated specifically for use in brewing beer. The two principal species are Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the warm-fermenting "ale" or top-fermenting yeast, and Saccharomyces pastorianus, the cold-fermenting "lager" or bottom-fermenting yeast. During fermentation these yeasts convert the sugars of malted grain wort into ethanol and carbon dioxide, while producing flavour compounds — esters, higher alcohols, and phenols — that give different beer styles much of their character.

Species and strains

Saccharomyces cerevisiae ferments best at relatively warm temperatures (roughly 15–24 °C) and rises to the surface as it ferments, hence "top-fermenting". It is also the species used as baker's yeast and in winemaking. Saccharomyces pastorianus, a hybrid of S. cerevisiae and the cold-tolerant S. eubayanus, ferments at cooler temperatures (roughly 7–13 °C) and settles to the bottom. Brewers maintain hundreds of distinct strains, each selected for attenuation, flocculation, alcohol tolerance, and flavour profile.

Use in brewing

Yeast is "pitched" into cooled, oxygenated wort, where it multiplies and then ferments anaerobically. At the end of fermentation the yeast may be harvested ("cropped") from the top or bottom of the fermenter and repitched into subsequent batches, a practice that makes brewery yeast cultures effectively continuous over many generations. Some styles, such as Belgian ales and German wheat beers, depend heavily on yeast-derived flavours, while wild yeasts of the genus Brettanomyces are used deliberately in lambics and some modern craft beers.

Nutritional use

Spent brewer's yeast, deactivated and dried, is sold as a nutritional supplement rich in B-complex vitamins, protein, and trace minerals such as chromium and selenium. Debit­tered brewer's yeast is used in spreads and flavourings; yeast extract products such as Marmite are derived from it. Because it is a by-product of brewing, it is inexpensive and widely available, though supplements made from it contain little or no vitamin B12 unless fortified.
