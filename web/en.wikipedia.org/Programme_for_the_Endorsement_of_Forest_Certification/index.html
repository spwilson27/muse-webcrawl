The Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC) is an international, non-profit, non-governmental organisation which promotes sustainable forest management through independent third-party certification. Founded in 1999 in Paris (originally as the Pan-European Forest Certification scheme), it is the world's largest forest certification system by certified area.

How it works

Rather than imposing a single global standard, PEFC endorses national forest certification systems that meet its sustainability benchmarks, developed through multi-stakeholder processes. It also certifies chain of custody, tracking forest-based products from the forest to the final product so they can carry the PEFC label. More than 280 million hectares of forest are PEFC-certified worldwide.
