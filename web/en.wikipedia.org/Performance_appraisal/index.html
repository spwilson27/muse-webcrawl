Performance appraisal

A performance appraisal, also referred to as a performance review or evaluation, is a regular assessment of an employee's job performance and overall contribution to an organisation, typically conducted by the employee's manager.

Methods

Common methods include graphic rating scales, management by objectives, behaviourally anchored rating scales, and 360-degree feedback, which gathers input from supervisors, peers, and subordinates. Appraisals are used for decisions on pay, promotion, and training, and for documenting underperformance.

Criticism

Performance appraisals have been criticised for rater bias (including halo and recency effects), subjectivity, and the anxiety and competition they can create; some organisations have moved toward continuous feedback and calibration processes instead of a single annual review.
