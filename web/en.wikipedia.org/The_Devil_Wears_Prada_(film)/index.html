The Devil Wears Prada is a 2006 American comedy-drama film directed by David Frankel, based on the 2003 novel by Lauren Weisberger.

Plot

Andrea "Andy" Sachs, an aspiring journalist, lands a job as junior assistant to Miranda Priestly, the powerful and demanding editor-in-chief of the fashion magazine Runway. Andy initially scorns the fashion industry but is gradually transformed by the job, straining her relationships and values, until she finally rejects Miranda's world at the height of Paris Fashion Week.

Reception

Meryl Streep's portrayal of Miranda — widely understood to be inspired by Vogue editor Anna Wintour — earned widespread acclaim and Academy Award and Golden Globe nominations, while Patricia Field's costume design was also Oscar-nominated. The film was a major commercial success.
