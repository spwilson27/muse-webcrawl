Culavamsa

Culavamsa
Language: Pali
Period covered: 4th century CE to 1815
Companion work: Mahavamsa
First part attributed to: Dhammakitti (13th c.)

The Culavamsa ("Lesser Chronicle") is a historical record in Pali of the kings of Sri Lanka, continuing the Mahavamsa from the 4th century CE to the fall of Kandy in 1815.

Contents

Compiled in parts by Buddhist monks over centuries, it narrates reigns including Parakramabahu I and the events around Anuradhapura and later capitals, supplementing the Mahavamsa's accounts of figures such as Dutugamunu and Elara.

Significance

Together with the Mahavamsa it is a primary source for South Asian history and for Sinhala Buddhist identity, though historians read its religious and dynastic framing critically. Wilhelm Geiger translated it into English in the early 20th century.
