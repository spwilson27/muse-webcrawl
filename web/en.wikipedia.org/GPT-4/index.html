GPT-4 (Generative Pre-trained Transformer 4) is a large multimodal language model developed by OpenAI and released on 14 March 2023. Unlike its predecessors, GPT-4 accepts both text and image inputs and produces text outputs. OpenAI described it as more reliable, creative, and capable of handling much more nuanced instructions than GPT-3.5, while declining to disclose its architecture, parameter count, or training details, citing competitive and safety considerations.

Capabilities

GPT-4 demonstrated substantially improved performance on professional and academic benchmarks, scoring in the top 10% of a simulated bar examination (compared with the bottom 10% for GPT-3.5) and performing strongly on examinations such as the SAT, GRE, and AP tests. It has a context window of 8,192 tokens in the base model, with a 32,768-token variant offered through the API. Its multimodal abilities let it describe images, explain charts, and interpret memes and diagrams.

Training and safety

The model was pre-trained on a large corpus of text and code and fine-tuned with reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). OpenAI published a "system card" describing risks such as hallucination, bias, and misuse, and reported that the model was less likely to produce disallowed content than GPT-3.5 while remaining vulnerable to adversarial prompts.

Deployment

GPT-4 was launched as the engine of the paid ChatGPT Plus service and of Microsoft's Bing Chat (later Copilot), and made available to developers through an API. It powered applications including Duolingo Max, Khan Academy's Khanmigo tutor, and the Be My Eyes accessibility tool. In May 2024 OpenAI released GPT-4o as its flagship multimodal model, and GPT-4 was progressively retired from ChatGPT in 2025 in favour of newer models.
