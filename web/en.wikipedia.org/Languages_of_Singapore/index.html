Languages of Singapore

Official: English, Malay, Mandarin, Tamil
National language: Malay
Working language: English

Singapore has four official languages: English, Malay, Mandarin Chinese, and Tamil. Malay is the national language, while English is the main working language of government, business, and education.

Policy

The bilingual education policy requires students to study English and their "mother tongue." The constitution recognises Malay in Roman script as the national language; the national anthem, "Majulah Singapura," is sung in Malay. Government campaigns such as the Speak Mandarin Campaign (from 1979) and the Speak Good English Movement promote standard varieties.

Languages in use

Census data show English increasingly the language most spoken at home, followed by Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil; Chinese varieties such as Hokkien, Teochew, and Cantonese, once widespread, have declined under Mandarin promotion. Singlish, an English-based creole, is widely used informally.
