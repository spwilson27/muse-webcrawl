Mayor of Los Angeles

The mayor of Los Angeles is the head of the executive branch of the government of Los Angeles, California, and the chief executive of the city. The office is officially nonpartisan, and the mayor serves a four-year term, limited to two terms.

Powers

The mayor proposes the city budget, appoints department heads and commissioners (subject to council approval), and can veto ordinances of the Los Angeles City Council. The first mayor under the American administration was Alpheus P. Hodges, elected in 1850.

Recent mayors

Notable modern mayors include Tom Bradley, the city's longest-serving mayor (1973–1993), Richard Riordan, Antonio Villaraigosa, Eric Garcetti, and Karen Bass, elected in 2022 as the city's first female mayor.
