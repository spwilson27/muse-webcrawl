Atlanta

Atlanta is the capital and most populous city of the U.S. state of Georgia. It is the seat of Fulton County and extends into DeKalb County. With a population of about 510,000 (2020) and a metropolitan population of over 6 million, Atlanta is a major transportation, media, and business hub of the American Southeast.

Downtown Atlanta skyline.

History

Founded in 1837 as the terminus of a state railroad — first called Terminus, then Marthasville — the city was named Atlanta in 1845. It was burned during Sherman's March to the Sea in the Civil War, rebuilt rapidly, and became the "capital of the New South". Atlanta was a centre of the civil rights movement, the home of Martin Luther King Jr. and the Southern Christian Leadership Conference, and hosted the 1996 Summer Olympics.

Economy and transport

Atlanta hosts the headquarters of Coca-Cola, Delta Air Lines, UPS, The Home Depot, and CNN. Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport has long been ranked the world's busiest airport by passenger traffic. The city is served by MARTA rail and a major interstate crossroads.

Culture

Sites include the Georgia Aquarium, the World of Coca-Cola, the Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park, and the Center for Civil and Human Rights. Atlanta is a major centre for film and television production, earning the nickname "Hollywood of the South", and a capital of hip-hop music through labels and artists based in the city.
