Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace

Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace AS (KDA) is one of the main business areas of Norway's Kongsberg Gruppen, supplying defence and space-related systems to the Norwegian Armed Forces and export customers.

Products

Its best-known products include the NASAMS air-defence system (developed with Raytheon), the Naval Strike Missile (NSM) and its air-launched variant the Joint Strike Missile (JSM) for the F-35, the Protector remote weapon station family, and components for satellites and launch vehicles. The company has operations in Norway, the United States, Australia and several other countries.

History

The business descends from Norway's state armaments works at Kongsberg, founded in 1814, and grew through the Cold War and afterwards as an exporter of high-technology weapons systems.
