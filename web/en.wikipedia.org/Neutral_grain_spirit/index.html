Neutral grain spirit

Neutral grain spirit, also known as rectified spirit, pure grain alcohol or ethyl alcohol of agricultural origin, is highly concentrated ethanol distilled from a grain-based mash to a very high proof — in the United States, at least 190 proof (95% alcohol by volume), and in the European Union at least 96% ABV.

Production

It is produced by continuous distillation in column stills, which strip out nearly all congeners, leaving a spirit that is almost flavourless and odourless. Common feedstocks include corn, wheat, rye and barley.

Uses

Neutral grain spirit is the base for vodka and gin, is blended into liqueurs and fortified wines, and is used industrially and medically as a solvent and antiseptic. Because of its high strength it is sold at retail under brands such as Everclear and is subject to restrictions in some jurisdictions.
