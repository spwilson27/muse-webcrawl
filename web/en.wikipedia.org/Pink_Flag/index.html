Pink Flag

Pink Flag is the debut studio album by the English rock band Wire, released in November 1977 by Harvest Records. Composed of 21 short, sharp songs—many barely a minute long—the album stripped rock music down to terse, minimalist statements and is considered a landmark of punk rock and a foundational influence on post-punk, hardcore, and alternative rock.

Style and reception

Produced by Mike Thorne, Pink Flag combined the speed and aggression of punk with an art-school sensibility: tracks like "Reuters", "Field Day for the Sundays", "Ex-Lion Tamer", "Mannequin", and "12XU" delivered oblique lyrics and abrupt endings. Initial sales were modest, but critical reputation grew steadily; the album has appeared on numerous "greatest albums" lists and its songs were covered by bands including Minor Threat ("12XU") and R.E.M. ("Strange").

Track listing highlights

Side one opens with "Reuters" and includes "Three Girl Rhumba", later famously echoed in Elastica's "Connection". The closing run of "Mr Suit", "Being Sucked in Again", "106 Beats That", and "Pink Flag" ends the album in under 36 minutes total.

Legacy

Pink Flag is frequently cited alongside the first albums by the Ramones and the Sex Pistols as a defining statement of 1977 punk, distinguished by its wit, economy, and deliberate avoidance of rock clichés.
