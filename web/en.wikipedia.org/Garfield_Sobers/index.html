Sir Garfield St Aubrun Sobers (born 28 July 1936), often known as Garry Sobers, is a Barbadian former cricketer who captained the West Indies and is widely regarded as one of the greatest all-rounders in cricket history. He batted left-handed, fielded brilliantly and could bowl several styles with the left arm.

Career

Sobers made his Test debut as a teenager in 1954. In 1958 he scored 365 not out against Pakistan, then the highest individual score in Test cricket. He later captained West Indies, played English county cricket for Nottinghamshire and became famous in 1968 for hitting six sixes in one over from Malcolm Nash in a first-class match.

Statistics and style

In 93 Tests Sobers scored 8,032 runs at an average above 57 and took 235 wickets. His value lay in combining elite batting, versatile bowling and athletic fielding, allowing selectors unusual balance in the side.

Honours

Sobers was knighted in 1975 and later named a National Hero of Barbados. The Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy was awarded to the ICC Cricketer of the Year.
