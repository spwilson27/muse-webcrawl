Monetary Policy Committee (United Kingdom)

Established1997
ParentBank of England
Members9
RoleSetting the official bank rate

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) is a committee of the Bank of England responsible for deciding the official interest rate in the United Kingdom. It was created in 1997 when the Labour government granted the Bank operational independence over monetary policy.

Remit

The committee's primary objective is to meet the government's inflation target, set at 2 percent for the Consumer Prices Index, while supporting growth and employment subject to that aim. If inflation deviates from the target by more than one percentage point, the Governor must write an open letter to the Chancellor of the Exchequer explaining why.

Membership and meetings

The MPC has nine members: five Bank officials, including the Governor, and four external members appointed by the Chancellor. It meets eight times a year, and votes and minutes are published with the decision.
