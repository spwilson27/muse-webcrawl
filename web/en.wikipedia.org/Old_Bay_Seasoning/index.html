Old Bay Seasoning is a blend of herbs and spices marketed in the United States by McCormick & Company. It is traditionally used to season crab and shrimp, and is an iconic ingredient in the cuisine of the Chesapeake Bay region, especially Maryland steamed blue crabs. The blend is a mixture of celery salt, black pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, paprika, and other spices, and is sold in a distinctive yellow, red, and blue tin.

History

Old Bay was created by Gustav Brunn, a German Jewish immigrant who fled Nazi Germany and settled in Baltimore, where in 1939 he founded the Baltimore Spice Company. Brunn named the blend after the Old Bay Line, the passenger steamship service that ran between Baltimore and Norfolk. The seasoning was at first used mainly by local crab houses; its popularity grew as crab feasts became a Maryland institution. McCormick acquired the brand in 1990.

Uses

Beyond seafood boils and steamed crabs, Old Bay is used in Maryland crab soup and crab cakes, and has spread to popcorn, french fries, fried chicken, potato chips, and even ice cream and beer in regional novelty products. In Maryland it functions as a cultural symbol as much as a condiment. McCormick also sells related variants, including a lower-sodium version and Old Bay Hot Sauce (launched 2020).
