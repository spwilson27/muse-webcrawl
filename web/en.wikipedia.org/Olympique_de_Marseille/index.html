Olympique de Marseille

Olympique de Marseille (French pronunciation: [ɔlɛ̃pik də maʁsɛj]; Occitan: Olimpic de Marselha), also known simply as Marseille or by the abbreviation OM, is a French professional football club based in Marseille. Founded in 1899, the club has played in Ligue 1, the top flight of French football, for most of its history and is one of the most successful and widely supported clubs in France. Marseille are the only French club to have won the UEFA Champions League, lifting the trophy in 1993.

History

The club was founded by René Dufaure de Montmirail as a multi-sports club and adopted the name Olympique de Marseille in 1899. OM won its first Coupe de France in 1924 and became a leading club of the professional era, collecting league titles in 1937, 1948, 1971 and 1972. The most successful period came under owner Bernard Tapie from 1986: with players such as Jean-Pierre Papin, Chris Waddle, Didier Deschamps and Marcel Desailly, Marseille won five consecutive league championships (1989–1993, one later stripped) and beat AC Milan 1–0 in Munich in the 1993 European Cup final — still the only Champions League title won by a French club.

The 1993 season ended in scandal: the Valenciennes match-fixing affair led to the club's 1993 league title being revoked, relegation to Ligue 2 in 1994 and Tapie's imprisonment. Marseille recovered, reached UEFA Cup finals in 1999 and 2004 and a Europa League final in 2018, and won another Ligue 1 title in 2010 under Deschamps. American businessman Frank McCourt bought the club in 2016.

Rivalries and supporters

OM's fiercest rivalry is Le Classique against Paris Saint-Germain, France's biggest fixture. The club commands passionate support at the Stade Vélodrome, one of Europe's most atmospheric stadiums, renovated for Euro 2016.

Honours

Marseille have won nine Ligue 1 titles (recognised), ten Coupes de France, three Coupes de la Ligue, the 1993 European Cup and the 2005 UEFA Intertoto Cup.
