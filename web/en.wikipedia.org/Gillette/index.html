Gillette is an American brand of safety razors and other personal care products, including shaving supplies, owned by the multi-national corporation Procter & Gamble (P&G). Based in Boston, Massachusetts, it was one of the world's leading suppliers of men's and women's razors and other grooming products.

History

The company was founded in 1901 by King Camp Gillette, who had the idea of a safety razor with a thin, inexpensive, disposable blade of stamped steel — an idea realised with the help of engineer William Nickerson. Sales began in 1903 and grew explosively; the razor-and-blades business model Gillette pioneered, selling the handle cheaply and profiting from the blades, became a textbook marketing strategy. The company supplied razors to U.S. soldiers in the First World War, entrenching the habit of wet shaving at home.

Products and later years

Successive innovations included the twin-blade Trac II (1971), the pivoting Atra (1977), the Sensor (1990), the triple-blade Mach3 (1998), and the five-blade Fusion (2006), as well as the Venus line for women. The Gillette Company, which had diversified into products such as Oral-B toothbrushes, Braun appliances, and Duracell batteries, was acquired by Procter & Gamble in 2005 in a deal valued at about US$57 billion. Its slogan, "The Best a Man Can Get", introduced in 1989, was refreshed in 2019 as "The Best Men Can Be".
