Bucharest

Bucharest is the capital and largest city of Romania, with a population of about 1.7 million in the city proper and over 2.3 million in the metropolitan area. It is the country's cultural, industrial, and financial centre.

First mentioned in 1459 as a residence of Vlad the Impaler, Bucharest grew into a cosmopolitan city known in the early 20th century as 'Little Paris' for its elegant architecture and boulevards. Under Nicolae Ceaușescu in the 1980s, much of the historic centre was demolished for the monumental Civic Centre and the Palace of the Parliament, the world's heaviest building.

Today Bucharest is a major IT and services hub in Eastern Europe, home to the Romanian Athenaeum, the Village Museum, and a large Old Town nightlife district.
