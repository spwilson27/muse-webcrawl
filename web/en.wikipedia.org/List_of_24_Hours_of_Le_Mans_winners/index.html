List of 24 Hours of Le Mans winners

[Image: Cars racing at the Circuit de la Sarthe at dusk]
The 24 Hours of Le Mans, held annually near Le Mans, France.

The 24 Hours of Le Mans is an endurance motor race held annually since 1923 at the Circuit de la Sarthe near Le Mans, France. Overall victory is awarded to the car that covers the greatest distance in 24 hours. This list records the overall winning cars, drivers and manufacturers for selected eras of the race.

Records

Porsche is the most successful manufacturer with 19 overall victories, followed by Audi with 13 and Ferrari with 11. The most successful driver is Tom Kristensen with nine wins, ahead of Jacky Ickx with six. Mazda's 1991 victory remains the only win by a Japanese manufacturer and by a rotary-engined car.
