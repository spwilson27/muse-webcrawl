Monday Night Wars

PeriodSeptember 4, 1995 – March 26, 2001
PromotionsWWF and WCW
ProgramsRaw and Nitro

The Monday Night Wars were a period of intense ratings competition between the World Wrestling Federation's Raw and World Championship Wrestling's Monday Nitro, which aired head-to-head on Monday nights from 1995 to 2001.

Background

WCW president Eric Bischoff was granted a prime-time slot on TNT directly opposite the WWF's flagship USA Network program. Nitro debuted live and often gave away the taped results of Raw on air, forcing the WWF to air Raw live more frequently.

Course of the war

Powered by the New World Order storyline, WCW defeated the WWF in the Nielsen ratings for 83 consecutive weeks beginning in 1996. The WWF responded with the "Attitude Era," built around stars such as Stone Cold Steve Austin, The Rock, and D-Generation X, and reclaimed the ratings lead in 1998.

End

Declining ratings and the AOL–Time Warner merger led to the sale of WCW's assets to the WWF in March 2001, ending the war. The final Nitro aired on March 26, 2001.
