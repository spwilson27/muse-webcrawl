Remaster

A remaster is a newly prepared version of a previously released recording, film, or video game, created from the original master material with the aim of improving quality or adapting the work to new formats.

Audio

In music, remastering involves returning to the original master tapes and applying modern equalisation, noise reduction, and level adjustment. Remastered albums became common with the compact disc era and again for high-resolution digital releases. Remastering is distinct from remixing, which recombines the individual recorded tracks.

Film and games

Film remasters are produced by rescanning the original negatives at higher resolutions and correcting damage and colour. Video game remasters update visuals, audio, and performance for newer hardware while largely preserving the original design, whereas remakes rebuild a game more extensively.
