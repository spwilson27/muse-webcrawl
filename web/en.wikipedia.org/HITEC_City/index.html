HITEC City (Hyderabad Information Technology and Engineering Consultancy City) is a technology township in Madhapur, in the western part of Hyderabad, India. Developed from the late 1990s by the Andhra Pradesh government with private partners such as L&T, HITEC City anchored Hyderabad's emergence as a global information-technology and business-process outsourcing hub, earning the surrounding districts the nickname "Cyberabad".

Development

The township's first landmark, the ten-storey Cyber Towers with its distinctive four quadrants, was inaugurated in November 1998 and quickly became the visual symbol of India's IT boom. It was followed by Cyber Gateway, Cyber Pearl, and large campuses of multinational firms, together with software technology parks, hotels and convention facilities such as the Hyderabad International Convention Centre and Hitex exhibition centre in the wider Madhapur–Gachibowli corridor.

Economy and transport

HITEC City hosts offices of major technology companies, financial-services firms and startups, employing hundreds of thousands of people in the wider Financial District and Gachibowli area. The neighbourhood is served by the Hyderabad Metro (Raidurg and HITEC City stations), the MMTS suburban railway and the Outer Ring Road to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport.
