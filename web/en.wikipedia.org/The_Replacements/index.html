The Replacements

The Replacements were an American rock band formed in Minneapolis, Minnesota, in 1979. Initially a punk band, they developed into one of the defining acts of American alternative rock, led by songwriter Paul Westerberg. Their chaotic, self-sabotaging live performances became legendary, and though they achieved only modest commercial success, their influence on alternative and indie rock was enormous.

History

Brothers Bob and Tommy Stinson and drummer Chris Mars recruited Westerberg after hearing him play through a window. After raw early records on Twin/Tone, the band's songwriting matured dramatically on Let It Be (1984), widely ranked among the greatest rock albums ever made, followed by Tim (1985) and Pleased to Meet Me (1987) on Sire Records. Bob Stinson, whose substance abuse worsened, was fired in 1986 and died in 1995. The band dissolved in 1991. Westerberg and Tommy Stinson reunited the band in 2012 for a successful run of reunion shows ending in 2015.
