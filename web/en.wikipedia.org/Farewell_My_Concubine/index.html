Farewell My Concubine

Farewell My Concubine is a 1993 Chinese epic drama film directed by Chen Kaige, starring Leslie Cheung, Zhang Fengyi, and Gong Li. Adapted from the novel by Lilian Lee, it spans decades of 20th-century Chinese history through the lives of two Peking opera actors.

Cheng Dieyi, trained to play female roles, and Duan Xiaolou, who plays male leads, perform the opera Farewell My Concubine together from childhood, through war, revolution, and the Cultural Revolution. The film shared the Palme d'Or at the 1993 Cannes Film Festival and is regarded as a landmark of Fifth Generation Chinese cinema.
