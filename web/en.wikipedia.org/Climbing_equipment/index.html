Climbing equipment

Climbing equipment refers to the gear used in the various disciplines of climbing, including rock climbing, mountaineering, ice climbing and sport climbing. It ranges from protective hardware that arrests falls to footwear and clothing designed for rock and cold environments.

Core equipment

  • Ropes — dynamic kernmantle ropes for lead climbing; static ropes for abseiling and hauling.
  • Harness — a waist belt and leg loops distributing fall forces.
  • Protection — carabiners, quickdraws, nuts, hexes and spring-loaded camming devices placed in cracks, plus bolts on sport routes.
  • Belay and descender devices — such as tube devices and assisted-braking devices used to control the rope.
  • Helmet and shoes — climbing shoes with sticky rubber soles; helmets protect against rockfall.
  • Chalk and chalk bag — magnesium carbonate for grip.

Standards bodies such as the UIAA and CEN certify much of this equipment for strength and performance.
