Félix Leclerc

Félix Leclerc (2 August 1914 – 8 August 1988) was a French-Canadian singer-songwriter, poet, writer, actor, and Québecois political activist. He is regarded as a founding figure of the contemporary chanson tradition in Quebec and was hugely popular in France as well.

Félix Leclerc
Born: 2 August 1914, La Tuque, Quebec
Died: 8 August 1988, Saint-Pierre-de-l'Île-d'Orléans
Occupation: Singer-songwriter, poet

Career

Discovered in Paris in 1950, Leclerc recorded classics such as "Moi, mes souliers" and "Le petit bonheur." His songs celebrating rural Quebec and its people helped shape Quebec cultural identity in the decades before and during the Quiet Revolution.

Legacy

Quebec's music awards, the Félix Awards (ADISQ gala), are named in his honour.
