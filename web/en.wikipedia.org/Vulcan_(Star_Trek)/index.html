Vulcan (Star Trek)


Homeworld: Vulcan
Notable characters: Spock, Sarek, T'Pol, Tuvok, Surak
Philosophy: Logic; IDIC ("Infinite Diversity in Infinite Combinations")
Greeting: "Live long and prosper"

Vulcans are a fictional extraterrestrial humanoid species in the Star Trek universe. Native to the planet Vulcan, they are known for their dedication to logic and reason, the suppression of emotion, pointed ears, upswept eyebrows and green, copper-based blood. The most famous Vulcan is Spock, science officer of the starship Enterprise, played by Leonard Nimoy.

Culture and history

Vulcan society was once as violent and emotional as any; some two thousand years before the series' setting, the philosopher Surak led a reform movement teaching that logic and emotional control were the path to survival, a transformation known as the Time of Awakening. Those who rejected Surak's teachings — "those who march beneath the Raptor's wings" — left the planet and eventually became the Romulans, as established in later series. Vulcans practice telepathy, most famously the mind meld, follow disciplines such as kolinahr (the purging of all emotion), and undergo the mating drive pon farr every seven years. The Vulcan salute, accompanied by the words "Live long and prosper", was devised by Nimoy from a Jewish priestly blessing.

Role in the Federation

In the franchise's chronology, Vulcans made first contact with humanity in 2063, following Zefram Cochrane's first warp flight, and were founding members of the United Federation of Planets in 2161. In the 2009 film Star Trek, the planet Vulcan is destroyed by the Romulan Nero, leaving the Vulcans an endangered species — a storyline continued in the "Kelvin timeline" films.
