Cairngorm Mountain Railway

The Cairngorm Mountain Railway is a funicular railway in the Cairngorms National Park in Highland, Scotland, serving the Cairn Gorm ski area. At 1,097 metres (3,599 ft), its top station, the Ptarmigan, is the highest railway station in the United Kingdom accessible to the public, and the line is Scotland's only funicular.

History

The railway opened in December 2001, replacing a chairlift system, to provide year-round access for skiers and visitors. It climbs about 450 metres over a length of roughly 2 kilometres from the Coire Cas base station.

Closure and repair

Concerns about the structural integrity of the concrete viaduct led to closure in September 2018. A major reinforcement programme followed, and the railway returned to service in January 2023, only to face further remedial works.

Environmental conditions

Because the summit plateau is a protected environment, summer visitors arriving by funicular generally remain within the Ptarmigan building and viewing terrace rather than walking onto the fragile mountain plateau.
