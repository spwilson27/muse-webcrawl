Derek Walcott

Sir Derek Alton Walcott KCSL OBE OCC (23 January 1930 – 17 March 2017) was a Saint Lucian poet and playwright. He received the 1992 Nobel Prize in Literature and was Professor of Poetry at the University of Essex from 2010 to 2013.

Life

Walcott was born in Castries, Saint Lucia, and educated at St Mary's College and the University of the West Indies in Jamaica. In 1959 he founded the Trinidad Theatre Workshop. He later taught at Boston University, where he founded the Boston Playwrights' Theatre, before retiring in 2007.

Works

His works include the Homeric epic poem Omeros (1990), widely considered his masterpiece, and the collections In a Green Night (1962), The Arkansas Testament (1987) and White Egrets (2010), which won the T. S. Eliot Prize. His plays include Dream on Monkey Mountain (1967) and The Joker of Seville. Walcott's writing explores Caribbean identity, colonial history and the interplay between European literary forms and West Indian vernacular.

Honours

Besides the Nobel Prize, Walcott received an OBE in 1972 and a knighthood in 2016. He died at his home near Gros Islet, Saint Lucia, in March 2017.
