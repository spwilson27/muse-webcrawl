Coastal Pacific

Route: Picton – Christchurch
Distance: 347 km (216 mi)
Operator: Great Journeys New Zealand (KiwiRail)
Line: Main North Line

The Coastal Pacific is a long-distance passenger train in New Zealand that runs daily between Picton and Christchurch on the South Island's Main North Line. Operated by KiwiRail's Great Journeys New Zealand division, the service travels the length of the Kaikōura coast, where the Seaward Kaikōura Range meets the Pacific Ocean, and is marketed principally as a scenic tourist journey, connecting at Picton with the Interislander ferries to Wellington.

History

Passenger trains have run between Christchurch and Picton since the Main North Line was completed in 1945. The modern tourist service was launched as the TranzCoastal in 1987 using refurbished panorama cars and renamed the Coastal Pacific in 2011, when new AK-class carriages with large windows, skylight panels, and an open-air viewing car entered service across KiwiRail's scenic trains. Following the November 2016 Kaikōura earthquake, which buried sections of the line under landslides, the service was suspended; the line reopened to freight in 2017 and the Coastal Pacific resumed in December 2018 with a summer-season timetable.

Route and journey

The journey of about 347 kilometres takes roughly five and a half hours. From Picton the train crosses the Marlborough wine country, calls at Blenheim and Seddon, then hugs the coast through Lake Grassmere, Ward, and Kaikōura — where passengers may spot fur seals, dolphins, and albatrosses — before turning inland through the Hundalee Hills and Cheviot, and finally across the Canterbury Plains via Rangiora to Christchurch. Southbound and northbound departures are timed to connect with Interislander sailings at Picton.

Rolling stock

The train typically comprises AK-class scenic carriages with panoramic windows, a café car serving New Zealand food and wine, and an open-air viewing carriage. Locomotives are drawn from KiwiRail's DX class pool. Audio commentary in several languages is provided through headsets at each seat.
