The German Wikipedia is the German-language edition of Wikipedia, the free online encyclopedia. Created in March 2001, it was the second language edition founded after the English Wikipedia and is among the largest editions by article count, with close to three million articles.

Characteristics

German Wikipedia is known for relatively strict inclusion criteria, a preference for comprehensiveness in retained articles, and early adoption of flagged revisions (“sighted versions”) — edits by new users must be reviewed before being shown by default. It draws contributors mainly from Germany, Austria and Switzerland and covers German-speaking culture extensively.

History

The edition grew rapidly in 2004–2006, aided by a German CD/DVD distribution of Wikipedia content. Wikimedia Deutschland, founded in 2004, was the first national Wikimedia chapter. In March 2019 the German Wikipedia (along with several other editions) blacked out for a day in protest at the EU Copyright Directive.
