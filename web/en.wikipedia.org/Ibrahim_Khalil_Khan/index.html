Ibrahim Khalil Khan

Reign 1759–1806
Died 1806
Title Khan of Karabakh

Ibrahim Khalil Khan Javanshir (1732–1806) was the second khan of the Karabakh Khanate, ruling from 1759 until his death in 1806. He was the son of Panah Ali Khan, the founder of the khanate and of the city of Shusha.

Reign

Ibrahim Khalil Khan consolidated his father's realm in the Karabakh region of the Caucasus, with Shusha as its capital and fortress. His rule coincided with intensifying rivalry between Qajar Persia and the Russian Empire for control of the Caucasus.

Relations with Persia and Russia

He resisted the campaigns of Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar, who sacked Shusha in 1795. In 1805 Ibrahim Khalil Khan signed the Treaty of Kurakchay with Russia, placing the khanate under Russian suzerainty. In 1806, amid a renewed Persian offensive, he was killed near Shusha by Russian troops under disputed circumstances.

Legacy

Under his long reign Shusha developed into an important political and cultural centre of the region.
