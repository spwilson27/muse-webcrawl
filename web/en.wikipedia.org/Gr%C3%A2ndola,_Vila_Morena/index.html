Grândola, Vila Morena

"Grândola, Vila Morena" is a Portuguese folk song composed by José Afonso, first recorded in 1971. It celebrates the town of Grândola in the Alentejo and its fraternal, egalitarian spirit, with the refrain "Grândola, vila morena / terra da fraternidade / o povo é quem mais ordena / dentro de ti, ó cidade" ("Grândola, brown town, land of fraternity; the people are the ones who command, within you, oh city").

The song became famous as the signal for the Carnation Revolution: its broadcast on Rádio Renascença in the early hours of 25 April 1974 was the second and confirming password for the Armed Forces Movement to begin the coup that ended Portugal's Estado Novo dictatorship. It remains a symbol of the revolution and of popular protest in Portugal.
