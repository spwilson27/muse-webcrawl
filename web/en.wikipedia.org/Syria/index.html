Syria

Syria, officially the Syrian Arab Republic, is a country in West Asia located in the Eastern Mediterranean and the Levant. It is bordered by Turkey, Iraq, Jordan, Israel and Lebanon, with a Mediterranean coastline.

History

Syria hosts some of the world's oldest cities, including Damascus and Aleppo. After Ottoman rule and a French mandate, it became independent in 1946. The Ba'ath Party ruled from 1963; Hafez al-Assad seized power in 1970 and his son Bashar al-Assad succeeded him in 2000.

Civil war and fall of Assad

Protests in 2011 escalated into a civil war involving government forces, opposition groups, Kurdish forces, Islamic State, and foreign powers, displacing over half the population and creating millions of refugees. In December 2024 a rebel offensive led by Hayat Tahrir al-Sham captured Damascus and Assad fled to Russia, ending 53 years of Assad family rule and beginning a transitional government.
