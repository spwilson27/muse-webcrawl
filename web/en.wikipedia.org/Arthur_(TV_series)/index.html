Arthur (TV series)

Genre: Children's animated series

Created by: Marc Brown

Original release: 7 October 1996 – 21 February 2022

Seasons: 25

Episodes: 253

Network: PBS Kids

Arthur is an animated children's television series created by Marc Brown and based on his Arthur book series. The show follows the adventures of Arthur Read, an eight-year-old anthropomorphic aardvark, his family and his friends in the fictional Elwood City. It aired on PBS from 1996 to 2022, becoming one of the longest-running children's animated series in American television history.

Premise and characters

Each episode deals with everyday issues facing children and families, such as asthma, dyslexia, bullying, sibling rivalry, friendship and diversity. Regular characters include Arthur's younger sister D.W., his parents David and Jane Read, and friends including Buster Baxter, Francine Frensky, Muffy Crosswire, Alan "the Brain" Powers and Binky Barnes. The show's theme song, "Believe in Yourself", was performed by Ziggy Marley and the Melody Makers.

Production and reception

The series was produced by WGBH Boston with Cookie Jar Group (later DHX Media/WildBrain) and, in later seasons, Oasis Animation. It won multiple Daytime Emmy Awards and a Peabody Award. The final season aired in February 2022, concluding with the episode "All Grown Up", which depicts the main characters as adults; Marc Brown appears in animated form in the finale.
