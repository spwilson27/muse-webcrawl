The UEFA Europa Conference League (renamed the UEFA Conference League from 2024) is an annual football club competition organised by UEFA, ranking third in the European club hierarchy below the Champions League and the Europa League.

History

The competition was introduced in the 2021–22 season to give clubs from more member associations the chance to play European group-stage football. Roma won the inaugural edition, beating Feyenoord 1–0 in the 2022 final in Tirana; West Ham United won in 2023 and Olympiacos in 2024.

Format

Teams qualify through their domestic leagues and cups according to the UEFA coefficient system; the winner earns a place in the following season's Europa League. From 2024–25 the group stage was replaced by a single league phase of 36 teams.
