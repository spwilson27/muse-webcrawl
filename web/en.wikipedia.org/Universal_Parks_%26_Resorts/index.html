Universal Parks & Resorts

Industry: Theme parks
Founded: 1964
Parent: NBCUniversal (Comcast)
Headquarters: Orlando, Florida

Universal Parks & Resorts, now branded as Universal Destinations & Experiences, is the theme park division of NBCUniversal, a subsidiary of Comcast. The company operates theme parks and resorts around the world based on properties from Universal Pictures and other media franchises.

History

The business began with the Universal Studios Hollywood studio tour in 1964, when tram tours of the working film lot were opened to the public. The concept expanded with Universal Studios Florida in 1990, Islands of Adventure in 1999, and international parks in Japan (2001), Singapore (2010) and Beijing (2021).

Properties

Major resorts include Universal Orlando Resort, Universal Studios Hollywood, Universal Studios Japan, Universal Studios Singapore and Universal Beijing Resort. Popular lands include The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Super Nintendo World and Jurassic World areas. Universal Epic Universe opened in Orlando in 2025 as the company's fourth Florida park.
