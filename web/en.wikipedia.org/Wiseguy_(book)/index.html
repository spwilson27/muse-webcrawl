Wiseguy (book)

Wiseguy: Life in a Mafia Family is a 1985 non-fiction book by crime reporter Nicholas Pileggi that tells the story of Henry Hill, an associate of New York's Lucchese crime family who became an FBI informant. The full title is Wiseguy: Life in a Mafia Family, and the book was published by Simon & Schuster.

Content

Based on extensive interviews with Hill, the book traces his life from his childhood in Brooklyn, where he idolised the mobsters in his neighbourhood, through his years running scams, hijackings, and thefts with figures such as Jimmy Burke and Tommy DeSimone, and his participation in crimes including the Lufthansa heist of 1978. It ends with Hill's arrest on narcotics charges and his decision to enter the federal Witness Protection Program and testify against his former associates.

Adaptation

Pileggi co-wrote the screenplay with director Martin Scorsese for the book's film adaptation, Goodfellas (1990), starring Ray Liotta as Hill. The film, widely regarded as one of the greatest crime films ever made, follows the book closely and draws some of its dialogue directly from Pileggi's reporting.
