Lov Grover

Lov Kumar Grover (born 1961) is an Indian-American computer scientist best known as the inventor of Grover's algorithm, a quantum search algorithm offering a quadratic speedup over classical unstructured search.

Work

Grover developed the algorithm in 1996 while at Bell Labs, where he spent much of his career after earning his PhD in electrical engineering from Stanford University and undergraduate degree from IIT Delhi. A database of N entries can be searched in roughly √N quantum operations instead of the classical N. He has also worked on other quantum algorithms, including applications to median estimation and pattern matching.
