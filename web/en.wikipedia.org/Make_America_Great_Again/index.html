Make America Great Again

"Make America Great Again" (often shortened to MAGA) is an American political slogan popularized by Donald Trump in his 2016 presidential campaign. It became the rallying phrase of his political movement and is frequently seen on campaign merchandise, especially red baseball caps.

Earlier uses

Similar phrases appeared in earlier campaigns: Ronald Reagan used "Let's Make America Great Again" in 1980, and Bill Clinton used the phrase in speeches in the 1990s. Trump trademarked the slogan after the 2012 election and launched his 2015 campaign with it.

Meaning and reception

Supporters interpret the slogan as a call to restore economic strength, secure borders and American prestige. Critics argue it appeals to nostalgia for a less inclusive past. The phrase and the "MAGA" label are also used broadly, by supporters and opponents alike, to describe Trump's political movement and its adherents.

In popular culture

The slogan has been widely parodied, adapted and referenced in media, protest art and counter-slogans.
