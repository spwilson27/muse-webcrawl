The Appraisal Foundation

The Appraisal Foundation is a United States non-profit organization, founded in 1987, dedicated to the advancement of professional valuation. It is best known as the source of the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP), the generally accepted standards for real estate appraisal in the United States.

Role

Following the savings and loan crisis, the Financial Institutions Reform, Recovery, and Enforcement Act of 1989 recognized the Foundation's Appraisal Standards Board as the setter of appraisal standards and its Appraiser Qualifications Board as the setter of minimum qualifications for state-licensed and certified appraisers.

Structure

The Foundation is governed by a board of trustees and sponsored by professional appraisal organizations. Separate boards maintain USPAP and qualification criteria, with exposure drafts and public comment periods preceding changes; the Appraisal Subcommittee of the Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council oversees the system at the federal level.
