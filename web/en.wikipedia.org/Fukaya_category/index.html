Fukaya category

In mathematics, specifically in symplectic geometry, the Fukaya category of a symplectic manifold is an A-infinity category whose objects are Lagrangian submanifolds of the manifold and whose morphisms are Lagrangian Floer chain groups. It is named after Kenji Fukaya, who introduced a similar notion in the context of Morse homology.

Definition

For two Lagrangian submanifolds, the morphism complex is generated by their intersection points, and the differential counts pseudoholomorphic strips between them. Higher compositions count pseudoholomorphic polygons whose edges lie on a sequence of Lagrangians, generally requiring virtual perturbation techniques to define rigorously.

Mirror symmetry

The Fukaya category is the central object on the symplectic ("A-model") side of Kontsevich's homological mirror symmetry conjecture, which predicts an equivalence with the derived category of coherent sheaves on a mirror complex manifold (the "B-model"). Variants include the wrapped Fukaya category for noncompact manifolds.
