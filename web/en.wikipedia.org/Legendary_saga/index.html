Legendary saga

A legendary saga (Old Norse: fornaldarsaga, "saga of times past") is a type of Norse saga set in the distant, largely mythical past before the settlement of Iceland, often featuring heroes, dragons, magic and ancient kings of Scandinavia and the wider Germanic world.

Examples

Examples include Völsunga saga, Hervarar saga, Örvar-Odds saga and Hrólfs saga kraka. Unlike the Icelandic family sagas, legendary sagas freely mix history with folklore and myth and were often composed for entertainment.
