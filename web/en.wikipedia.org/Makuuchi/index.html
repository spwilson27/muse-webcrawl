Makuuchi

Makuuchi (幕内), or makunouchi, is the top division of professional sumo wrestling in Japan. It is the highest of the six divisions, comprising 42 wrestlers at its top ranks.

Ranks

From highest to lowest, makuuchi ranks are yokozuna (grand champion), ōzeki, sekiwake, komusubi, and the numbered maegashira ranks. Wrestlers in the top two divisions (makuuchi and jūryō) are known as sekitori and receive salaries, in contrast to the lower divisions.

Tournaments

Makuuchi wrestlers fight once a day over the 15 days of each grand tournament (honbasho), of which six are held each year. The wrestler with the best record wins the championship (yūshō) and the Emperor's Cup. Promotion and demotion between divisions follows each tournament based on results.
