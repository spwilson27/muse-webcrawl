Amazing Grace (Aretha Franklin album)

Amazing Grace
Live album by Aretha Franklin
Released: 1 June 1972
Recorded: 13–14 January 1972, New Temple Missionary Baptist Church, Los Angeles
Genre: Gospel

Amazing Grace is a live album by American singer Aretha Franklin, released on 1 June 1972 by Atlantic Records. Recorded over two nights at the New Temple Missionary Baptist Church in Los Angeles, with the Reverend James Cleveland and the Southern California Community Choir, it returned Franklin to the gospel music of her youth.

Recording and reception

The sessions were produced by Franklin, Jerry Wexler and Arif Mardin. The double album features extended performances of standards including "Amazing Grace" (over ten minutes long), "Mary, Don't You Weep", "Wholy Holy" and "Never Grow Old". It became the best-selling album of Franklin's career and the best-selling live gospel album of all time, selling over two million copies, and won the Grammy Award for Best Soul Gospel Performance. Critics regard it as one of the greatest live albums ever made.

Concert film

Warner Bros. filmed the performances under director Sydney Pollack, but technical problems synchronising picture and sound shelved the footage for decades. The completed film, Amazing Grace, premiered in 2018 to wide acclaim following Franklin's death.
