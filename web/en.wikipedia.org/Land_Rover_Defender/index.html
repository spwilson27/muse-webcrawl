The Land Rover Defender is a series of British off-road vehicles produced by Land Rover. The name was introduced in 1990 for the long-running Ninety and One Ten models, direct descendants of the original 1948 Land Rover, and was revived in 2020 for an all-new model line.

Classic Defender (1983–2016)

The coil-sprung Ninety (90) and One Ten (110), renamed Defender 90 and 110, combined a rugged ladder chassis and aluminium body panels with permanent four-wheel drive. They served farmers, utilities, explorers, and militaries worldwide. Production of the classic design ended at Solihull on 29 January 2016 after 68 years of continuous Land Rover production in that lineage.

New Defender (2020– )

The L663 Defender, unveiled in 2019, uses unibody construction, independent suspension, and modern petrol, diesel, and plug-in hybrid powertrains while retaining short overhangs and serious off-road capability. It is offered as the 90, 110, and longer 130, and is built in Slovakia.
