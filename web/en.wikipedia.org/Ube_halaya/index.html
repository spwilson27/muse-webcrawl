Ube halaya

Ube halaya
Alternative names: Ube jam, halayang ube
Place of origin: Philippines
Type: Dessert / jam
Main ingredients: Purple yam (ube), coconut milk, sugar, butter

Ube halaya or halayang ube is a Philippine dessert made from boiled and mashed purple yam (Dioscorea alata, locally known as ube). It is recognizably vivid violet in color and is eaten on its own, spread on bread, or used as a component of other desserts.

Preparation

Grated or mashed ube is cooked with coconut milk or condensed milk, sugar, and butter, stirred constantly until thick. It is traditionally served in a llanera mold topped with latik (coconut curds).

Uses

Ube halaya is a key ingredient of halo-halo and a popular filling or flavoring for ice cream, cakes, pastries, and modern fusion desserts.
