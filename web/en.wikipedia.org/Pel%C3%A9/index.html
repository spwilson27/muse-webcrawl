Pelé

Edson Arantes do Nascimento (23 October 1940 – 29 December 2022), better known by his nickname Pelé, was a Brazilian professional footballer who played as a forward. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, he is the only player to have won three FIFA World Cups, with Brazil in 1958, 1962 and 1970.

Pelé
Born: 23 October 1940, Três Corações, Brazil
Died: 29 December 2022, São Paulo, Brazil
Position: Forward
Clubs: Santos; New York Cosmos
National team: Brazil (92 caps, 77 goals)

Club career

Pelé spent almost his entire club career with Santos, joining at fifteen and winning numerous state, national and continental titles, including two Copa Libertadores and two Intercontinental Cups in 1962 and 1963. He came out of retirement in 1975 to play for the New York Cosmos, helping popularize soccer in the United States.

International career

Debuting for Brazil at sixteen, Pelé starred at the 1958 World Cup in Sweden as a seventeen-year-old, scoring a hat-trick in the semi-final and two goals in the final. Injuries limited him in 1962 and 1966, but he led the celebrated 1970 team in Mexico, widely considered the greatest ever, and won the Golden Ball.

Legacy

Credited with over 1,200 goals in all matches including friendlies, Pelé became a global ambassador for football and for Brazil, was named Athlete of the Century by the International Olympic Committee, and served as Brazil's Minister of Sports in the 1990s.
