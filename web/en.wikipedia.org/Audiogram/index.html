Audiogram

An audiogram is a graph that displays the results of a pure-tone audiometry hearing test. It plots hearing threshold in decibels hearing level (dB HL) against frequency, typically from 125 Hz to 8,000 Hz, for each ear.

Reading an audiogram

Air-conduction thresholds are conventionally marked with circles for the right ear and crosses for the left; bone-conduction thresholds use bracket symbols. The pattern and degree of loss help distinguish conductive, sensorineural and mixed hearing loss.

Classification

Degrees of loss are commonly described as mild, moderate, severe or profound based on the average threshold across speech frequencies.
