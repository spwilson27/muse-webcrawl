Milken Institute
Founded: 1991
Founder: Michael Milken
Headquarters: Santa Monica, California
Type: Nonprofit, nonpartisan think tank

The Milken Institute is an American nonprofit, nonpartisan economic think tank founded in 1991 by financier Michael Milken. Based in Santa Monica, California, with offices in Washington, DC and internationally, it works to improve lives by accelerating medical research, expanding access to capital and building human capital.

Activities

The institute conducts research on capital markets, regional economics, health and education, and convenes leaders in business, finance, government and philanthropy. Its flagship event is the annual Milken Institute Global Conference in Beverly Hills, which draws thousands of participants for panels on the economy, technology and health. Centres within the institute focus on areas such as public health, financial markets, the future of aging and the "FasterCures" initiative to speed medical breakthroughs.

History

Michael Milken, the former Drexel Burnham Lambert banker known for developing the high-yield bond market, established the institute after his release from prison following securities-law convictions; the institute is separate from his family's Milken Family Foundation and the Prostate Cancer Foundation he also founded. The Milken Institute School of Public Health at George Washington University, opened in 2014, bears the family name following a major gift.
