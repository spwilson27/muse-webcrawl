World Heritage Site

A World Heritage Site is a landmark or area selected by UNESCO for cultural, historical, scientific or other significance of outstanding universal value, and legally protected by an international convention. Sites are inscribed on the World Heritage List, established under the 1972 World Heritage Convention.

Selection

States nominate sites within their territory; the World Heritage Committee, advised by ICOMOS and IUCN, decides on inscription. Criteria range from masterpieces of human creative genius to superlative natural phenomena. Sites facing threats may be placed on the List of World Heritage in Danger.
