Olympiacos F.C.

Founded: 1925
Ground: Karaiskakis Stadium, Piraeus
Nickname: Thrylos (The Legend)
Colours: Red and white

Olympiacos F.C. is a Greek professional football club based in Piraeus, part of the wider Olympiacos multi-sport club founded in 1925. It is the most successful club in Greek football history, having won a record number of Super League Greece titles and Greek Cups.

History

The club was founded on 10 March 1925. Olympiacos dominated Greek football in several eras and developed a fierce rivalry with Panathinaikos, known as the Derby of the Eternal Enemies. In 2024 the club won the UEFA Europa Conference League, the first European trophy for a Greek club, and its youth team won the UEFA Youth League the same season.
