Athens

Athens is the capital and largest city of Greece, and one of the world's oldest cities, with a recorded history spanning more than 3,400 years. Its classical era remains influential: the city-state of Athens was a centre of philosophy, theatre, democracy and the arts, home to figures such as Socrates, Plato and Pericles.

Landmarks

The Acropolis, crowned by the Parthenon, dominates the city. Other noted sites include the Ancient Agora, the Temple of Olympian Zeus, and the Panathenaic Stadium. Modern Athens hosted the first modern Olympic Games in 1896 and again in 2004.

Geography

The city lies in the Attica basin, surrounded by mountains including Hymettus and Pentelicus, and extends toward the port of Piraeus on the Saronic Gulf.
