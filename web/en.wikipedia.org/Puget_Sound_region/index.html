Puget Sound

Puget Sound is a complex estuarine system of interconnected marine waterways and basins in the U.S. state of Washington, part of the Salish Sea. The Puget Sound region surrounding it — centred on Seattle, Tacoma, Everett and Bellevue — is home to roughly two-thirds of Washington's population.

Geography

The sound was carved by glacial action during the Pleistocene and includes the main basin, Hood Canal, Whidbey Basin and the Tacoma Narrows. It connects to the Strait of Juan de Fuca and the Pacific Ocean.

Economy and transport

The region hosts the ports of Seattle and Tacoma (together the Northwest Seaport Alliance), major aerospace and technology industries, and the Washington State Ferries system, the largest ferry system in the United States.
