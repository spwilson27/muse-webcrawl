Vigo

Vigo is a city and municipality in the province of Pontevedra, within the autonomous community of Galicia, in northwestern Spain. Situated on the southern shore of the Ría de Vigo, it is the most populous city in Galicia, with around 290,000 inhabitants in the municipality and nearly 480,000 in the metropolitan area.

Economy

Vigo is one of Europe's leading fishing ports and home to a major Stellantis automobile plant, shipbuilding, and the European Fisheries Control Agency. Its natural harbour on the Atlantic has shaped its history since Roman times.

History

The area was raided by Sir Francis Drake in the 1580s and was the scene of the Battle of Vigo Bay in 1702 during the War of the Spanish Succession. The Cíes Islands, at the mouth of the ria, form part of the Atlantic Islands of Galicia National Park.
