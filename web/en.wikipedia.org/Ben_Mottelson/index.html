Ben Mottelson

Ben Roy Mottelson (July 9, 1926 – May 13, 2022) was an American-Danish nuclear physicist. He shared the 1975 Nobel Prize in Physics with Aage Bohr and James Rainwater for work on the structure of the atomic nucleus.

Life

Born in Chicago, Mottelson served in the US Navy, studied at Purdue University and earned his PhD from Harvard in 1950. He moved to Denmark, working at the Niels Bohr Institute and Nordita in Copenhagen, and became a Danish citizen.

Work

With Aage Bohr, he developed a unified model of the nucleus combining collective motion (rotation and vibration of a deformed nucleus) with individual nucleon motion, explaining spectra that the shell model alone could not.

Honours

Besides the Nobel Prize, he received the Atoms for Peace Award and was a member of the Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters.
