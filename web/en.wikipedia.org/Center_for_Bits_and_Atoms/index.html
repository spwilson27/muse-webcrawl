The Center for Bits and Atoms (CBA) is an interdisciplinary research centre at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, associated with the MIT Media Lab. Founded in 2001 with National Science Foundation support and directed by Neil Gershenfeld, it studies the interface between information and physical systems.

Research and fab labs

CBA developed the Fab Lab concept — community workshops equipped with laser cutters, CNC mills, 3D printers and electronics benches — which spread to more than two thousand sites worldwide. Research spans digital fabrication, programmable materials, microfluidics and personal manufacturing.

Education

Its Fab Academy and the course How To Make (Almost) Anything teach rapid prototyping globally. See Fab lab and MIT Media Lab.
