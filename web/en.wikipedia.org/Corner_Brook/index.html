Corner Brook is a city on the west coast of the island of Newfoundland in the province of Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It lies on the Bay of Islands at the mouth of the Humber River and is the province's second-largest city by metropolitan population.

History

The area was surveyed by Captain James Cook in 1767. Settlement grew around fishing and lumbering, and in the 1920s a large pulp and paper mill was built, becoming the economic anchor of the community. The modern city was formed in 1956 by amalgamating four towns: Corner Brook, Corner Brook East, Corner Brook West and Curling.

Economy and education

The Corner Brook Pulp and Paper mill remains a major employer. The city hosts Grenfell Campus, a campus of Memorial University of Newfoundland, and serves as the service centre for western Newfoundland, with Marble Mountain ski resort nearby at Steady Brook.
