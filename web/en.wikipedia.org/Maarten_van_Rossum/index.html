Maarten van Rossum

Maarten van Rossum
Born: c. 1478, Zaltbommel
Died: 7 June 1555, Antwerp
Role: Field marshal of Guelders

Maarten van Rossum (c. 1478 – 1555) was a Dutch military commander who, for more than thirty years, served the dukes of Guelders in their wars against the Habsburgs. He is also known by his nickname "the Dutch Goliath".

Career

Van Rossum conducted daring campaigns across the Low Countries, raiding The Hague in 1528 and threatening Antwerp and Brussels. His guerrilla tactics and sieges made him feared throughout the region.

Later life

After Guelders fell to Charles V in 1543, van Rossum entered imperial service. He died in Antwerp in 1555.
