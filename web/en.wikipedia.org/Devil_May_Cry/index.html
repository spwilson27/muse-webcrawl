Devil May Cry is a series of action-adventure video games developed and published by Capcom. Created by Hideki Kamiya, the series centers on Dante, a wisecracking demon hunter and the son of the legendary demon Sparda, who runs the "Devil May Cry" detective agency and battles demonic invasions of the human world.

Gameplay

The games emphasize fast, acrobatic combat in which players chain together sword attacks, firearms, and aerial maneuvers while avoiding damage. A style ranking system grades the player's performance during fights—rising from Dull through Cool, Blast, Absolute, and Stylish—rewarding varied and flamboyant play. This emphasis on expressive combat made the series a defining example of the "character action" genre.

Games

The first Devil May Cry (2001), originally begun as a Resident Evil prototype, was followed by numbered sequels culminating in Devil May Cry 5 (2019), which was widely acclaimed and became the series' best-selling entry. A reboot, DmC: Devil May Cry (2013), was developed by Ninja Theory. The franchise has also expanded into anime, novels, manga, and stage plays.

Characters and story

Recurring characters include Dante's twin brother and rival Vergil, the demon-hunting Lady, Trish, and the younger hunter Nero, who takes a leading role in later games. The stories blend Gothic settings, heavy-metal aesthetics, and themes of family and demonic heritage.

Reception

The series has sold tens of millions of copies and is regarded as one of Capcom's flagship franchises, influential across the action genre for its combat depth and stylish presentation.
