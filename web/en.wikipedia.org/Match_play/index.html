Match play

SportGolf
ScoringHoles won
Notable eventsRyder Cup; WGC Match Play

Match play is a form of scoring in golf in which a player or team competes directly against an opponent, hole by hole, rather than against the entire field. Each hole is won, lost, or halved, and the match is won by the side that is up by more holes than remain to be played.

Rules and strategy

Scores are expressed as up or down and results as combinations such as 3 and 2 (three holes ahead with two to play). Concessions of strokes, holes, or matches are permitted, giving match play a distinctive strategic and psychological character.

Events

Match play is used in the Ryder Cup, Solheim Cup, and Presidents Cup team competitions, and in the WGC Match Play championship.
