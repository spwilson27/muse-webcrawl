U.S. Route 41

U.S. Route 41

US 41 crosses the Upper Peninsula of Michigan.

Length: 1,990 mi (3,200 km)
Existed: 1926–present
South end: Miami, Florida
North end: Fort Wilkins, Michigan

U.S. Route 41 (US 41) is a major north–south United States Numbered Highway that runs from Miami, Florida, to the Upper Peninsula of Michigan. It passes through Florida, Georgia, Tennessee, Kentucky, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, and Michigan, largely paralleling Interstate 75 to its west.

Route description

In Florida, US 41 includes the Tamiami Trail across the Everglades between Tampa and Miami. It continues through Atlanta, Chattanooga, Nashville, and Evansville, then through the Chicago area (where it follows Lake Shore Drive as part of its historic routing), Milwaukee, and Green Bay before crossing into Michigan's Upper Peninsula and ending at Fort Wilkins near Copper Harbor.

History

Established in 1926, US 41 was once one of the main routes from the Midwest to Florida before the Interstate Highway System; its southern sections remain important regional corridors and scenic drives.
