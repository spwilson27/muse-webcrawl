The Riigikogu (Estonian: [ˈriːɡikoɡu]; from Estonian riigi-, "of the state", and kogu, "assembly") is the unicameral parliament of Estonia. In addition to approving legislation, the Riigikogu appoints high officials, including the prime minister and chief justice of the Supreme Court, and elects the president, alone or, if necessary, together with representatives of local government.

History

The Riigikogu was first convened following the elections of November 1920 under the first constitution of the Republic of Estonia. During the Soviet occupation the parliament was replaced by the Supreme Soviet of the Estonian SSR. Following the restoration of independence, elections to the restored Riigikogu were held in September 1992.

Elections

The 101 members of the Riigikogu are elected by proportional representation for a four-year term. Elections are held across twelve multi-member electoral districts, and Estonia is notable for its internet voting system, first used in parliamentary elections in 2007.

Seat

The Riigikogu meets in Toompea Castle in Tallinn, in a chamber built in the Expressionist style in 1922 within the medieval castle complex.
