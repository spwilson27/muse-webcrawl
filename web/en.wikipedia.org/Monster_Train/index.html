Monster Train is a roguelike deck-building video game developed by American studio Shiny Shoe and published by Good Shepherd Entertainment in 2020. In the game, Hell has frozen over, and the player escorts the last burning heart of Hell — the Pyre — aboard a train descending through Hell's rings to rekindle it, defending the Pyre against the invading forces of Heaven.

Gameplay

Combat takes place on a train of four vertical floors. Enemies enter on the bottom floor and climb one floor each turn; if they reach the top floor they attack the Pyre, whose health persists across a run. The player deploys monster units and casts spells from a deck, positioning units across floors and building synergies within and between two allied clans (Hellhorned, Awoken, Umbra, Melting Remnant, and Stygian Guard, with the Wurmkin added in the expansion The Last Divinity). Between battles, the train stops at stations where the player upgrades cards and units, gains artefacts, and chooses branching paths. Runs are structured as a map-based campaign against successive bosses, culminating in the flying fortress Seraph.

Reception

The game was released for Windows in May 2020 and later ported to Xbox, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, and mobile platforms. It received generally favourable reviews, with critics praising its multi-floor twist on the deck-builder formula popularised by Slay the Spire, its depth of clan combinations, and its replayability. A sequel, Monster Train 2, was released in 2025.
