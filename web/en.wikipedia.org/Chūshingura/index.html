Chūshingura

Subject: The forty-seven rōnin

Forms: Kabuki, bunraku, film, literature

Chūshingura (“Treasury of Loyal Retainers”) is the name given to fictionalized retellings of the Akō incident, in which forty-seven rōnin avenged their lord Asano Naganori by killing Kira Yoshinaka in 1703 and were then ordered to commit seppuku.

The story

After Asano attacked Kira in Edo Castle and was executed, his retainers, led by Ōishi Kuranosuke, endured nearly two years of apparent disgrace before their night attack. Names and details were altered in dramatizations because the Tokugawa authorities restricted direct depiction of contemporary events.

Cultural significance

The puppet play and kabuki Kanadehon Chūshingura (1748) became classics, and the tale has been retold in countless films and books as a meditation on loyalty, honour, law, and revenge.
