Dragonslayer

The dragon Vermithrax Pejorative.

Dragonslayer is a 1981 American fantasy film directed by Matthew Robbins, from a screenplay by Robbins and Hal Barwood, co-produced by Paramount Pictures and Walt Disney Productions. Set in a fictional medieval kingdom, it follows a young sorcerer's apprentice, Galen, who is called upon to kill the dragon Vermithrax Pejorative, to which maidens are sacrificed by lottery.

Production

The film starred Peter Macon, Caitlin Clarke and Ralph Richardson as the sorcerer Ulrich. Its dragon, realised through "go motion" animation developed at Industrial Light & Magic, was widely praised and remains influential; the film received Academy Award nominations for its visual effects and for Alex North's score. Though only a modest success on release—its dark tone surprising audiences expecting a family film—Dragonslayer has grown in reputation as one of the finest fantasy films of its era.
