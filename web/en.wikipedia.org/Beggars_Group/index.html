Beggars Group is a British independent record label group and the largest and most influential independent group of labels in the world. It is composed of several of the most successful independent labels of the past four decades: 4AD, Matador Records, Rough Trade Records, XL Recordings and Young.

History

The group grew out of the Beggars Banquet record shops and label founded by Martin Mills in 1974–77 during the punk era. Through partnerships and acquisitions it assembled its current roster of labels while leaving them creatively autonomous. Beggars Group was also a major shareholder in the streaming-era independent licensing network Merlin and a co-founder of AIM (the Association of Independent Music) and the Worldwide Independent Network.

Artists

Artists released through Beggars labels include Adele (XL), Radiohead's later catalogue partnerships, The National and Interpol (Matador), Vampire Weekend (XL), Cocteau Twins and Pixies (4AD), The Strokes and The Libertines (Rough Trade) and many others. The group is frequently cited as the leading example of scale achieved while remaining independent of the three major label groups. See also XL Recordings and 4AD.
