Spengler Cup

The Spengler Cup is an annual invitational ice hockey tournament held in Davos, Switzerland. First held in 1923, the Spengler Cup is often cited as the oldest invitational ice hockey tournament in the world. The event is hosted by the Swiss team HC Davos and played each year in Davos between Christmas and New Year's Day, from 26 to 31 December.

History

The tournament was founded by Dr Carl Spengler, a Davos physician, to promote contacts through sport between nations formerly at war in the First World War. HC Davos and Team Canada have been among the most successful participants, each winning the tournament many times.

Format

Six teams compete: host HC Davos, a Canada national select team, and four invited club or select teams from Europe and beyond. Teams are divided into two groups named after Swiss hockey legends (Torriani and Cattini), followed by knockout rounds.

Venue

Games are played at the Vaillant Arena (formerly Eisstadion Davos), an arena celebrated for its wooden roof structure and capacity of about 7,000 spectators.
