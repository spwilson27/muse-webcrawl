Corannulene

FormulaC20H10
ClassPolycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon
ShapeBowl-shaped
First synthesised1966

Corannulene is a polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon with the formula C20H10, consisting of a central five-membered ring fused with five benzene rings. Unlike most aromatic hydrocarbons, it is curved, adopting a bowl shape, and is sometimes called a buckybowl.

Structure and dynamics

Corannulene can be viewed as a fragment of buckminsterfullerene (C60): it is the polar cap of the fullerene. The bowl is not rigid — it undergoes rapid bowl-to-bowl inversion at room temperature, passing through a planar transition state with a modest energy barrier.

Synthesis

Corannulene was first synthesised in 1966 by Barth and Lawton in a lengthy multi-step synthesis. More practical routes using flash vacuum pyrolysis and solution-phase methods were developed from the 1990s, enabling study of its derivatives in materials chemistry and its ability to accept multiple electrons on reduction.
