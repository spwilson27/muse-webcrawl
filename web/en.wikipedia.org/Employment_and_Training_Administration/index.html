Employment and Training Administration

The Employment and Training Administration (ETA) is an agency of the United States Department of Labor that provides job training, employment services, labour market information and income maintenance services, principally through state and local workforce development systems.

The ETA administers federal grants for employment and training under the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA), funds the nationwide network of American Job Centers, and oversees programmes including Job Corps, apprenticeships, the Trade Adjustment Assistance programme and the federal-state unemployment insurance system.

The agency also issues labour certifications for foreign workers through the Office of Foreign Labor Certification and publishes labour market data in cooperation with the Bureau of Labor Statistics. It is headed by an Assistant Secretary of Labor for Employment and Training, appointed by the President and confirmed by the Senate.
