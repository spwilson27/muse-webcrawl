Jerry Maguire


Jerry Maguire is a 1996 American romantic sports comedy-drama film written and directed by Cameron Crowe, starring Tom Cruise.

Jerry Maguire is a 1996 American romantic sports comedy-drama film written, produced, and directed by Cameron Crowe, and starring Tom Cruise, Cuba Gooding Jr., and Renée Zellweger. It was inspired by sports agent Leigh Steinberg, who acted as a technical consultant on the film.

Plot

Jerry Maguire is a successful sports agent at Sports Management International. After a crisis of conscience, he writes a mission statement urging fewer clients and more personal attention — and is fired for it. He leaves to start his own agency, keeping only one client, flamboyant NFL wide receiver Rod Tidwell, and one employee, single mother Dorothy Boyd, who has long admired him. As Jerry struggles to keep his business alive and his relationship with Tidwell grows into genuine friendship, he and Dorothy fall in love, marry on impulse, separate, and reconcile.

Reception and legacy

The film grossed more than $273 million worldwide against a budget of about $50 million. It was nominated for five Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Actor for Cruise; Cuba Gooding Jr. won Best Supporting Actor. The film popularised several catchphrases that entered the language, including "Show me the money!" and "You had me at 'hello'," and "You complete me." The American Film Institute ranked it among the greatest sports films and love stories in American cinema.
