The GelreDome is a football stadium and multi-purpose events venue in Arnhem, Netherlands. Opened on 25 March 1998, it is the home of football club SBV Vitesse. The stadium was pioneering in Europe for combining a retractable roof with a sliding retractable pitch that can be moved outside the stadium so the grass can grow in natural conditions and the interior can be used for concerts and other events.

Events

The GelreDome was a venue for UEFA Euro 2000, co-hosted by the Netherlands and Belgium, and has hosted Dutch national team matches. Outside football it is one of the Netherlands' largest indoor concert venues, regularly hosting major touring acts, dance events, and motorsport shows such as Monster Jam; the roof and climate control make it usable year-round.
