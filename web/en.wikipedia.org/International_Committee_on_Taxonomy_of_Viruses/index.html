International Committee on Taxonomy of Viruses

The International Committee on Taxonomy of Viruses (ICTV) is a committee of the International Union of Microbiological Societies charged with the official classification and naming of viruses. It maintains the universal taxonomy of viruses from realm and order down to species.

Classification

The ICTV system complements the Baltimore classification, grouping viruses by genome type and replication strategy. Its hierarchy now includes the ranks realm, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus and species, and it formally ratifies species names — such as Severe acute respiratory syndrome-related coronavirus.

Publications

The committee issues regular taxonomy releases and comprehensive reports describing recognised virus families and genera, serving as the reference point for virologists worldwide.
