Johan Ernst Gunnerus

Johan Ernst Gunnerus
Born26 February 1718, Christiania, Norway
Died25 September 1773, Kristiansund, Norway
OccupationBishop, naturalist, botanist

Johan Ernst Gunnerus (1718–1773) was a Norwegian bishop and naturalist. Appointed Bishop of Trondheim in 1758, he combined his pastoral duties with pioneering scientific study of the natural history of northern Norway.

Science

Gunnerus co-founded the Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters in Trondheim in 1760, one of Norway's earliest learned societies. He corresponded with Carl Linnaeus, described numerous species — including the Greenland shark — and wrote Flora Norvegica (1766–1776), the first flora of Norway.

Legacy

The Gunnerus Library in Trondheim, among Norway's oldest scientific libraries, is named for him, as is the plant genus Gunnera (named by Linnaeus, though it commemorates him by association in popular accounts).
