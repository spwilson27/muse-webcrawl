Under the Dome

Under the Dome is a science fiction novel by American author Stephen King, published in November 2009. King had attempted versions of the story in the 1970s and 1980s before completing the 1,000-page work.

The novel is set in the small town of Chester's Mill, Maine, which is suddenly sealed off from the outside world by an invisible, indestructible dome of unknown origin. As resources dwindle and the air inside degrades, the town's corrupt second selectman, "Big Jim" Rennie, seizes power, while army veteran Dale "Barbie" Barbara and newspaper editor Julia Shumway lead the resistance. The story is told through a large ensemble cast, in King's characteristic portrayal of small-town social breakdown under pressure.

The novel was generally well received and was adapted into a CBS television series (2013–2015) developed by Brian K. Vaughan, which departed substantially from the book's plot.
