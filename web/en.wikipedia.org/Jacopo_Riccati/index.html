Jacopo Riccati

Jacopo Francesco Riccati (1676 – 1754) was an Italian mathematician and jurist from Venice, best known for the Riccati equation, a first-order ordinary differential equation that is quadratic in the unknown function.

Born28 May 1676, Venice, Republic of Venice
Died15 April 1754, Treviso
FieldsMathematics, physics, law
Known forRiccati equation

Life and work

Educated in law at Padua, Riccati devoted himself to mathematics and hydraulics, advising the Venetian Republic on canals and flood control. He studied differential equations arising in geometry and mechanics, corresponding with leading mathematicians of his day, and his work on the equation y' = a(x) + b(x)y + c(x)y² influenced the Bernoullis and Euler.

Legacy

The Riccati equation remains important in control theory, where its matrix form underlies the linear-quadratic regulator, as well as in differential geometry and quantum mechanics. His sons Vincenzo and Giordano also became notable scientists.
