Dock jumping

Dock jumping, also known as dock diving, is a dog sport in which dogs compete in jumping for distance or height from a dock into a body of water. The sport emerged in the United States in 1997 when it appeared at the Incredible Dog Challenge, an event sponsored by a pet food company.

Events

In the distance event, often called Big Air, a handler throws a toy into the water and the dog leaps from a dock, typically 40 feet long; the distance is measured from the end of the dock to where the base of the dog's tail breaks the water. In Extreme Vertical, dogs jump for height to grab a bumper suspended above the water.

Records

Top dogs jump distances of more than 30 feet. Organizations such as DockDogs, North America Diving Dogs, and Ultimate Air Dogs sanction competitions and maintain records.
