Rio Grande Games

Rio Grande Games is an American publisher of board games, founded by Jay Tummelson. The company is credited with introducing European-style ("Euro") designer board games to the United States market from the late 1990s onward by producing English-language editions of acclaimed German and European titles.

Rio Grande brought many European designer games to English-speaking audiences.

History

Tummelson began by importing German games, then in 1998 founded Rio Grande Games to publish English editions with identical components to the originals, timed to coincide with the original releases. Early successes included English editions of games such as Tigris and Euphrates, Puerto Rico—long among the highest-rated games on BoardGameGeek—Carcassonne, Power Grid and Agricola.

Notable titles

Rio Grande publishes the deck-building game Dominion (2008) by Donald X. Vaccarino, winner of the 2009 Spiel des Jahres and a foundational title of the deck-building genre, together with its many expansions. Other well-known releases include Race for the Galaxy, El Grande, Bohnanza, Torres and Concordia.
