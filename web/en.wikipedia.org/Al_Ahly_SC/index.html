Al-Ahly Sporting Club (Arabic: النادي الأهلي), commonly known as Al Ahly, is an Egyptian professional sports club based in Cairo. Founded in 1907, it is best known for its football team, which competes in the Egyptian Premier League and is one of the most successful football clubs in Africa and the world.

History

Al Ahly was founded on 24 April 1907 by Omar Lotfy Bey, who proposed the establishment of a club for Egyptian students and graduates. The name "Al Ahly" means "The National". The club grew to become a symbol of Egyptian nationalism during the period of British occupation.

Throughout the twentieth century, Al Ahly dominated Egyptian football, accumulating a record number of league and cup titles. Its fierce rivalry with Zamalek SC, known as the Cairo Derby, is one of the most intense in world football.

Honours

Al Ahly has won the Egyptian Premier League a record number of times, the Egypt Cup, and the CAF Champions League more times than any other African club, earning the title "Club of the Century" in African football from CAF in 2001. The club has also represented Africa at the FIFA Club World Cup on multiple occasions.

Stadium

The team plays its home matches at the Cairo International Stadium, with a capacity of around 75,000. The club's own stadium project has been planned for many years.
