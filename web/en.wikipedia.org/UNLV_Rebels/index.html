The UNLV Rebels are the intercollegiate athletics teams representing the University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV). The Rebels compete in NCAA Division I and are members of the Mountain West Conference.

Men's basketball

The Runnin' Rebels men's basketball team is the school's most famous program. Coached by Jerry Tarkanian from 1973 to 1992, the team won the 1990 NCAA championship, defeating Duke 103–73, and reached the Final Four several times. The 1990–91 team went 34–1, losing only in the national semifinal to Duke.

Football

The Rebels football team plays at Allegiant Stadium, shared with the Las Vegas Raiders of the NFL. The program has appeared in several bowl games.

Other sports

UNLV fields teams in baseball, softball, soccer, swimming, track and field, and other sports. The baseball team plays at Earl E. Wilson Stadium.
