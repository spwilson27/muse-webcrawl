Cream cheese

Cream cheese is a soft, usually mild-tasting fresh cheese made from milk and cream. It is not naturally matured and is meant to be consumed fresh, in contrast with aged cheeses such as Brie or cheddar. Stabilizers such as carob bean gum and carrageenan are often added in industrial production. Cream cheese is a defining ingredient of the American bagel-and-cream-cheese combination and of cheesecake.

History

Recipes for cheeses made by draining lactic curds in cloth appear in England as early as the 16th century, and in France soft cream-enriched cheeses were made in Normandy. The modern American product is credited to William A. Lawrence, a dairyman of Chester, New York, who in 1872–73 began producing a richer version of Neufchâtel-style cheese by adding cream. Lawrence marketed it under the Philadelphia brand after 1880, the name associating the product with the high-quality dairy goods for which Philadelphia was then known. By the early 20th century, cream cheese was a New York delicatessen staple, commonly spread on bagels and black bread.

Production and standards

Under U.S. Food and Drug Administration standards, cream cheese must contain at least 33% milk fat and no more than 55% moisture, with a pH between 4.4 and 4.9. Pasteurized milk and cream are inoculated with lactic acid bacteria, coagulated, and the whey is drained away; the curd is then salted and stabilized, and may be whipped with incorporated air for a lighter spread.

Uses

Cream cheese is spread on bagels — the classic pairing, often combined with lox — crackers, and toast; it is the base of cheesecake and cream cheese frosting, a common dip ingredient, and a filling for pastries, sushi rolls (as in the Philadelphia roll), and jalapeño poppers. Flavored varieties include scallion (chive), vegetable, strawberry, and smoked salmon.

Related products

Neufchâtel, in its American form a lower-fat relative, and mascarpone, an Italian acid-set cream cheese used in tiramisu, are close cousins. "Schmear" is the Yiddish-derived term for a spread of cream cheese, popularized by New York bagel shops.
