The Ultimates

The Ultimates

Publisher: Marvel Comics (Ultimate Marvel)

First appearance: 2002

Creators: Mark Millar, Bryan Hitch

Team: Ultimate version of the Avengers

The Ultimates is a superhero comic book series published by Marvel Comics under its Ultimate Marvel imprint, a modernised re-imagining of the Avengers. Written by Mark Millar with art by Bryan Hitch, the first volume ran from 2002 and was noted for its widescreen, cinematic art and its politically charged, morally ambiguous treatment of its heroes.

Story

General Nick Fury of S.H.I.E.L.D. assembles a government superhuman team — Captain America, Iron Man, Thor, the Wasp, Giant-Man, and later Hawkeye and others — to defend the United States against superhuman threats, including the Hulk's rampage in Manhattan and the shape-shifting Chitauri aliens.

Influence

The series heavily influenced the Marvel Cinematic Universe: the Chitauri invasion, Samuel L. Jackson's Nick Fury (modelled on the character's Ultimate design), and the tone of The Avengers (2012) all derive from it. Later volumes were written by Jeph Loeb and others.
