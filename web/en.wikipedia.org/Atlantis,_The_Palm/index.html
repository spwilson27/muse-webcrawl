Atlantis, The Palm

Atlantis, The Palm is a luxury hotel resort located at the apex of the Palm Jumeirah in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It was the first resort to be built on the island and is themed on the myth of Atlantis, with distinct Arabian elements. The resort opened on 24 September 2008.

Atlantis, The Palm on the crescent of Palm Jumeirah, Dubai.

Design and facilities

The resort comprises two accommodation towers linked by the Royal Bridge Suite, with 1,544 rooms and suites. Attractions include the Aquaventure Waterpark, the Lost Chambers Aquarium — home to some 65,000 marine animals — and Dolphin Bay. Its signature underwater suites look directly into the Ambassador Lagoon aquarium.

Opening and events

The opening party, reportedly costing around US$20 million, featured a performance by Kylie Minogue and one of the largest fireworks displays ever staged. A fire weeks before opening damaged the lobby but the launch proceeded on schedule. In 2023 a sister resort, Atlantis The Royal, opened adjacent to it with an extensively covered "fire and water" design.
