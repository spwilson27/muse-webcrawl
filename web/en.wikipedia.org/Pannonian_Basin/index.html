Pannonian Basin

The Pannonian Basin, or Carpathian Basin, is a large sedimentary basin in Central Europe, bounded by the Carpathian Mountains, the Alps, the Transdanubian Mountains and the Dinaric Alps. It is drained principally by the Danube and Tisza rivers.

Geology

The basin formed in the Miocene as the Pannonian Sea, an arm of the Paratethys, filled with sediments; its thin crust produces high geothermal gradients and numerous thermal springs.

Geography

It encompasses all of Hungary and parts of Austria, Slovakia, Ukraine, Romania, Serbia, Croatia and Bosnia and Herzegovina, and includes the Great Hungarian Plain (Alföld).
