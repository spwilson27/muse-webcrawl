The National Oceanography Centre (NOC) is the United Kingdom's centre for ocean research, technology development and sea-level science. It operates from waterfront campuses in Southampton and Liverpool and runs major national facilities on behalf of the UK marine science community.

History

The centre was formed in 2010 by merging the Natural Environment Research Council's National Oceanography Centre, Southampton (itself opened in 1995) with the Proudman Oceanographic Laboratory in Liverpool. In November 2019 the NOC became an independent charitable company, while continuing to deliver national capability funded by UK Research and Innovation.

Facilities and research

The NOC operates the research ships RRS Discovery and RRS James Cook, hosts the British Oceanographic Data Centre, the National Ocean Library and the Permanent Service for Mean Sea Level, and develops autonomous underwater vehicles such as the Autosub and Boaty McBoatface family. Its science addresses ocean circulation, climate change, sea-level rise, marine ecosystems and the sustainable use of ocean resources.
