Expo 2010

The China Pavilion, nicknamed the "Oriental Crown", was the centrepiece of Expo 2010 in Shanghai.

Expo 2010 was a world exposition held in Shanghai, China, from 1 May to 31 October 2010 under the theme "Better City, Better Life". It was the largest world's fair ever staged, with 246 participating countries and international organisations and a record 73 million visitors, spread across a 5.28 km² site on both banks of the Huangpu River.

Highlights

The China Pavilion, a towering red inverted pyramid built on traditional dougong bracket forms, displayed the animated scroll painting Along the River During the Qingming Festival and later became the China Art Museum. National pavilions drew enormous queues — Saudi Arabia's "moon boat" and Japan's purple silkworm-island pavilion among them — and the mascot Haibao, a blue figure based on the Chinese character for "person", became ubiquitous. The event was widely seen as a showcase of China's economic rise two years after the Beijing Olympics.
