Luxembourgish

Lëtzebuergesch
Native to: Luxembourg and border regions
Family: Indo-European — Germanic — West Germanic

Luxembourgish is a West Germanic language spoken mainly in Luxembourg, where it is the national language and one of three administrative languages alongside French and German.

History

A Moselle Franconian dialect, Luxembourgish was long primarily a spoken language, with French and German used in writing. It was recognised as the national language in 1984 and has a standardised orthography, though French and German remain dominant in many official contexts.

Features

Luxembourgish shares much vocabulary with German dialects but has borrowed many French words. About 400,000 people speak it.
