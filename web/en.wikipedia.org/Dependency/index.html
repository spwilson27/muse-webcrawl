Dependency

Captive dependencies in the broader discussion of dependence relations

Dependency most often refers to a relationship in which one entity relies on another. The term is used across many fields:

  • Grammar: dependency grammar organizes sentence structure around head–dependent relations between words.
  • Computer science: a dependency is a relationship in which one software component requires another to function; dependency injection and package dependency graphs are standard topics.
  • Economics and politics: dependency theory analyzes how resources flow from poorer "periphery" states to wealthy "core" states; a dependency is also a territory governed by another state.
  • Medicine and psychology: dependence describes compulsive reliance on a substance or behaviour, and dependent personality patterns.
  • Data: functional dependency is a key concept in database normalization.

This article is a general overview of the concept across these domains.
