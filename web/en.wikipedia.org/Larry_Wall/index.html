Larry Wall (born September 27, 1954) is an American computer programmer, linguist and author, best known as the creator of the Perl programming language. He also developed the rn newsreader and the patch utility, and later worked on Raku (formerly Perl 6).

Perl

Wall released Perl 1.0 in 1987 as a Unix scripting language combining features of C, shell, awk and sed. Its philosophy is summarised by the motto 'There's more than one way to do it' and Wall's three virtues of a programmer: laziness, impatience and hubris.

Other work

Wall, trained in linguistics, worked on Bible translation support and at O'Reilly Media. His 'Programming Perl', co-authored with Tom Christiansen and Jon Orwant, is the language's best-known book.
