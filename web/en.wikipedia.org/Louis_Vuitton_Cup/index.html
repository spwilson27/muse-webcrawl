Louis Vuitton Cup

The Louis Vuitton Cup was a yachting competition serving as the challenger selection series for the America's Cup, sponsored by the French luxury house Louis Vuitton. First held in 1983 in Newport, Rhode Island, it determined which challenger syndicate would face the defender of the America's Cup.

History

The 1983 edition was won by Australia II, which went on to win the America's Cup itself, breaking the New York Yacht Club's 132-year winning streak. Subsequent editions were held in Fremantle (1987), San Diego (1992), Auckland (2000, 2003), Valencia (2007) and San Francisco (2013). The sponsorship ended after 2013, and later challenger series took other names, though Louis Vuitton returned as sponsor of the challenger selection series for the 2024 America's Cup in Barcelona.

Winners

Notable winning syndicates include Australia II (1983), Stars & Stripes (1987), Il Moro di Venezia (1992), Team New Zealand (1995, 2003, 2007, 2013) and Luna Rossa (2000).
