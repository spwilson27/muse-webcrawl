Mukherjee–Samarth family

OriginBengal / Maharashtra, India
FieldIndian cinema (Bollywood)
Notable membersShobhna Samarth, Tanuja, Kajol, Rani Mukerji

The Mukherjee–Samarth family is a prominent Indian film family with roots in both Bengali and Marathi cinema, whose members have been active in Bollywood for four generations. The family links the Samarth line of actress Shobhna Samarth with the Mukherjee family of filmmaker Sashadhar Mukherjee.

Overview

Shobhna Samarth's daughters Nutan and Tanuja became leading actresses; Tanuja's daughters Kajol and Tanishaa continued the tradition, with Kajol becoming one of Hindi cinema's most acclaimed stars. The Mukherjee side includes filmmakers Joy, Deb and Romu Mukherjee, and actress Rani Mukerji, a leading star of the 2000s.
