John Bowlby

Edward John Mostyn Bowlby (26 February 1907 – 2 September 1990) was a British psychologist, psychiatrist, and psychoanalyst known for his pioneering work in attachment theory.

Working at the Tavistock Clinic in London, Bowlby studied children separated from their parents and published the WHO report Maternal Care and Mental Health (1951) and his trilogy Attachment and Loss (1969–1980). He argued that infants have an innate need to form a close attachment to caregivers, whose disruption causes lasting harm — ideas developed experimentally by his collaborator Mary Ainsworth.
