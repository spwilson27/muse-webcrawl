British Champions Series
Sport: Flat horse racing
Founded: 2011
Races: 35 Group races in five categories
Finale: QIPCO British Champions Day, Ascot

The British Champions Series is a series of 35 top-class flat horse races held in Britain, launched in 2011 to give the British flat racing season a clearer narrative structure and a climactic finale. The series is sponsored by QIPCO and culminates in British Champions Day at Ascot Racecourse each October, the richest single day of racing in Britain.

Structure

The races are divided into five categories: Sprint, Mile, Middle Distance, Long Distance, and Fillies & Mares. The series begins with the 2000 Guineas at Newmarket in May and takes in the Derby and Oaks at Epsom, Royal Ascot, the Eclipse Stakes, the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes, Glorious Goodwood, the Ebor Festival at York and the St Leger at Doncaster.

British Champions Day itself features four Group 1 races: the Queen Elizabeth II Stakes, the Champion Stakes, the British Champions Fillies' and Mares' Stakes and the British Champions Sprint Stakes, together with the Balmoral Handicap. The day is famous for Frankel's farewell victory in the 2012 Champion Stakes, which concluded his unbeaten 14-race career.
