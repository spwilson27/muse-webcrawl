Immunity (album)

Artist: Clairo
Released: 2 August 2019
Genre: Indie pop, bedroom pop, soft rock
Label: Fader Label
Producer: Rostam Batmanglij, Clairo

Immunity is the debut studio album by American singer-songwriter Clairo (Claire Cottrill), released on 2 August 2019 by the Fader Label. Produced largely with former Vampire Weekend member Rostam Batmanglij, the album marked a move from the lo-fi bedroom recordings that had made Clairo a viral success toward a fuller, band-based sound.

Background and singles

Cottrill rose to prominence with the 2017 viral video "Pretty Girl". On Immunity she wrote about her experiences with juvenile rheumatoid arthritis, mental health, and her sexuality; she came out as bisexual in 2018. The album was preceded by the singles "Bags", "Closer to You", and "Sofia" — the last of which later became a sleeper hit on TikTok and her first song to chart on the Billboard Hot 100.

Reception

The album received generally positive reviews, with critics praising its intimate songwriting and understated production. It appeared on several year-end best-album lists and established Clairo as a leading figure in indie pop, ahead of her second album Sling (2021).
