CONSORT

CONSORT (Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials) is an evidence-based, minimum set of recommendations for reporting randomised trials, offering a standard way for authors to prepare reports of trial findings and facilitating their complete and transparent reporting.

Statement and checklist

The CONSORT Statement comprises a 25-item checklist of essential items to include when reporting a randomised trial, together with a flow diagram documenting the progress of participants through the phases of the trial: enrolment, intervention allocation, follow-up and analysis. First published in 1996, the statement was revised in 2001 and 2010.

Adoption and extensions

CONSORT is endorsed by hundreds of medical journals and by editorial organisations, and is part of the wider EQUATOR Network of reporting guidelines. Extensions cover specialised designs and interventions, including cluster trials, non-inferiority trials, pragmatic trials, harms, abstracts and social and psychological interventions.

Studies have found that journal endorsement of CONSORT is associated with more complete reporting of trials.
