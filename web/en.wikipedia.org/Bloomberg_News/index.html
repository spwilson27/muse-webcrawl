Bloomberg News
Founded: 1990
Parent: Bloomberg L.P.
Headquarters: New York City

Bloomberg News (originally Bloomberg Business News) is an international news agency founded in 1990 by Michael Bloomberg and Matthew Winkler. It produces financial and general news distributed through Bloomberg Terminals, television, radio, magazines and websites.

Operations

The agency employs thousands of journalists in bureaus around the world. Its reporting is a core selling point of the Bloomberg Terminal subscription service used by financial professionals.

Related outlets

Associated properties include Bloomberg Television, Bloomberg Radio, Bloomberg Businessweek and Bloomberg Markets magazine.
