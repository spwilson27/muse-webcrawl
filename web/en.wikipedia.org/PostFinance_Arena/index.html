The PostFinance Arena is an indoor arena in Bern, Switzerland. It is the home of SC Bern of the National League and is the largest ice hockey arena in Switzerland and one of the largest in Europe, with a capacity of 17,031 spectators for hockey, including its famous standing-room terrace, the Stehrampe, which alone holds more than 8,000 fans.

History

The arena opened in 1967 as the Eisstadion Allmend and was renamed in 2002 when PostFinance acquired naming rights. It underwent major renovation between 2007 and 2009 ahead of the 2009 IIHF World Championship, which it co-hosted. SC Bern regularly leads Europe in attendance, often averaging over 16,000 spectators per home game.

Events

Besides ice hockey, the arena hosts concerts, exhibitions and other sporting events, including curling championships and gymnastics.
