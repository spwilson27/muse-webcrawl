List of missions to Mars

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of missions to Mars covers the robotic spacecraft sent to the planet Mars by space agencies including NASA, the Soviet/Russian program, the European Space Agency, Japan, India, China, and the United Arab Emirates. Roughly half of all Mars missions historically ended in failure, particularly early Soviet attempts in the 1960s, earning the planet a reputation for difficulty.

Early missions (1960s–1970s)

MissionAgencyLaunchResult
Mariner 4NASA1964First successful flyby; returned first close-up photos of Mars
Mariner 9NASA1971First spacecraft to orbit another planet

Viking 1 & 2NASA1975First successful landers; conducted biology experiments
