The 2008 Singapore Grand Prix (formally the 2008 Formula 1 SingTel Singapore Grand Prix) was a Formula One motor race held on 28 September 2008 at the Marina Bay Street Circuit in Singapore. The fifteenth round of the 2008 World Championship, it was the first Formula One race ever held at night, staged under floodlights in the city-state's downtown core. Fernando Alonso won for Renault — a victory later revealed to have been engineered by his team-mate deliberately crashing, in the affair known as "Crashgate".

The race

Felipe Massa qualified on pole for Ferrari and led the early laps. On lap 14, Renault's Nelson Piquet Jr. crashed at Turn 17, bringing out the safety car just after Alonso — who had qualified only 15th after a fuel-rig failure — had made an unusually early pit stop. Because the pit lane closed under the safety car and refuelling rules bunched the field, Alonso inherited the lead once the leaders served penalties and stops. Massa's race unravelled when Ferrari released him from his pit stop with the fuel hose still attached, and he finished 13th. Alonso won ahead of Nico Rosberg's Williams and championship leader Lewis Hamilton's McLaren.

Crashgate

In 2009, after Renault dismissed him, Piquet revealed that team principal Flavio Briatore and engineering director Pat Symonds had instructed him to crash deliberately to trigger the safety car and benefit Alonso. An FIA investigation followed; Renault pleaded no contest, Briatore and Symonds were banned from FIA events (bans later partly overturned in French court), and the scandal became one of the gravest cases of race-fixing in the sport's history. Alonso, who denied knowledge of the plan, was cleared of wrongdoing.
