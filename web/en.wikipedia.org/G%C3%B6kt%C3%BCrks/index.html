Göktürks

The Göktürks, also known as the Turks of the Celestial Mountains or the Ashina Turks, were a nomadic confederation of Turkic peoples in medieval Inner Asia who founded the First Turkic Khaganate in the 6th century CE.

History

Under Bumin Qaghan, the Ashina clan overthrew the Rouran Khaganate in 552 and established an empire stretching across the Eurasian steppe from Manchuria to the Black Sea. After civil war, the khaganate split into eastern and western wings around 603. A Second Turkic Khaganate was restored by Ilterish Qaghan in 682 and endured until 744.

Legacy

The Göktürks left the earliest substantial texts in a Turkic language, the 8th-century Orkhon inscriptions, which use the word Türk prominently; the modern name of Turkey and the Turkic peoples derives from this tradition.
