Levuka

Levuka
Country: Fiji
Island: Ovalau
Division: Eastern Division

Levuka is a town on the eastern coast of Ovalau Island in Fiji. It was the capital of Fiji from 1874 until 1877, when the capital moved to Suva because Levuka's cliff-bound site left little room for expansion. The town was an important port and trading centre in the nineteenth century.

History

European traders and beachcombers settled at Levuka from the early 1800s, attracted by bêche-de-mer, sandalwood and later copra. Fiji's first newspaper, banks, schools and hotels were established there, and the Kingdom of Fiji under Seru Epenisa Cakobau signed the Deed of Cession to Britain at Levuka in 1874.

Heritage

The historic port town of Levuka was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2013, recognised for its well-preserved colonial-era wooden buildings along Beach Street and its role as an early Pacific port town.
