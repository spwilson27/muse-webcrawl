Delta Air Lines

Delta Air Lines, Inc. is a major American airline headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, at Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport, the world's busiest airport by passenger traffic. Delta is one of the largest airlines in the world by revenue and passengers carried, operating thousands of daily flights with its regional partners under the Delta Connection brand.

History

Delta began in 1925 as Huff Daland Dusters, a crop-dusting operation in Macon, Georgia, and started passenger service in 1929. It grew through acquisitions including Chicago and Southern Air Lines, Northeast Airlines, Western Airlines and the Atlantic routes of Pan Am, emerging from a 2005–2007 Chapter 11 restructuring to merge with Northwest Airlines in 2008, creating what was then the world's largest airline.

Operations

Delta maintains hubs including Atlanta, Detroit, Minneapolis/St. Paul, Salt Lake City, New York–JFK and LaGuardia, Los Angeles, Seattle and Boston, and is a founding member of the SkyTeam alliance. It holds equity stakes in partners such as Air France–KLM, Aeroméxico, LATAM and Virgin Atlantic. Its loyalty programme is SkyMiles, and its fleet mixes Airbus and Boeing aircraft, including a large order of Airbus widebodies.
