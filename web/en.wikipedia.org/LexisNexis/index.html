LexisNexis is an American corporation that provides computer-assisted legal research (CALR), business research, and risk management services. It is a subsidiary of RELX Group (formerly Reed Elsevier) and is headquartered in New York City.

History

LexisNexis traces its origins to 1970, when the Mead Corporation began developing the LEXIS full-text legal search system, launched commercially in 1973. The NEXIS news and business information service followed in 1979. Reed Elsevier acquired the LexisNexis business from Mead in 1994.

Products and services

The Lexis+ platform provides access to case law, statutes, regulations, law reviews, and legal news for attorneys and legal professionals. LexisNexis Risk Solutions, a separate division, offers data analytics for insurance, financial services, healthcare, and government, including identity verification and fraud prevention products.

Operations

The company serves customers in more than 150 countries and holds vast databases of legal and public records documents, numbering in the tens of billions. Its principal competitors in legal research include Westlaw, published by Thomson Reuters, and Bloomberg Law.
