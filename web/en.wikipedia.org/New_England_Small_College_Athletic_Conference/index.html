New England Small College Athletic Conference

The New England Small College Athletic Conference (NESCAC) is an intercollegiate athletic conference that competes in the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division III, made up of highly selective liberal arts colleges and one research university in New England and New York. The eleven member schools are sometimes informally known as the "Little Ivies."

Members

Full members are Amherst College, Bates College, Bowdoin College, Colby College, Connecticut College, Hamilton College, Middlebury College, Trinity College, Tufts University, Wesleyan University and Williams College.

History

The conference traces its origins to agreements among New England colleges in the mid-20th century and was formally founded in 1971 as an extension of earlier scheduling arrangements. For many years NESCAC schools restricted participation in NCAA postseason team championships, a policy gradually liberalised from the 1990s onwards; its football teams still do not compete in the NCAA playoffs.

Sports and academics

NESCAC sponsors championships in a wide range of men's and women's sports and is widely regarded as one of the strongest Division III conferences athletically while its members are also among the most academically selective colleges in the United States. The conference emphasises the student-athlete ideal, with shorter seasons and limits on out-of-season practice compared with other divisions.
