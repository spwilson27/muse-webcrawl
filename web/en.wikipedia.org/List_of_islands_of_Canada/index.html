List of islands of Canada

Canada has an estimated 62,000 islands; the Arctic Archipelago alone contains some of the largest islands in the world. Baffin Island is Canada's largest island and the fifth-largest island on Earth.

By population

The most populous Canadian island is the Island of Montreal, home to nearly two million people, followed by Vancouver Island and Newfoundland.
