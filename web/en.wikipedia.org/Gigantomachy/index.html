Gigantomachy

The Gigantomachy (from Greek gigas, "giant", and machē, "battle") is the mythical war between the Olympian gods and the Giants (Gigantes), offspring of Gaia (Earth) and Uranus (Sky). In Greek myth it was one of the defining cosmic conflicts, in which the Olympians' order triumphed over the forces of chaos — but only with the aid of a mortal hero, Heracles, whose arrows, according to an oracle, were necessary for the gods' victory because the Giants could not be killed by gods alone.

The myth

The Giants, often depicted in later art with serpent legs, attacked Olympus hurling rocks and burning oaks. Zeus summoned the gods to battle; Athena, Apollo, Artemis, Dionysus and others each slew individual Giants, with Heracles finishing them off. Among the most famous episodes: Athena flaying the giant Pallas (whose skin became her aegis), Athena crushing Enceladus beneath the island of Sicily, Poseidon breaking off part of the island of Kos to bury Polybotes (creating the island of Nisyros), and the Fates clubbing giants to death with bronze clubs. The defeated Giants were said to lie buried beneath volcanoes, whose eruptions were their stirrings.

In art

The Gigantomachy was one of the most popular subjects in Greek art from the 6th century BC onward, symbolising the triumph of civilisation over barbarism and order over chaos. It decorated the east metopes of the Parthenon, the treasury of the Siphnians at Delphi, and — most famously — the great frieze of the Pergamon Altar (2nd century BC), a masterpiece of Hellenistic sculpture commissioned under the Attalid dynasty, in which the struggle of gods and giants served as an allegory of Pergamon's victories over the Galatians. The theme was revived in Renaissance and Baroque art and architecture.

Distinction from the Titanomachy

The Gigantomachy is sometimes confused with the earlier Titanomachy, the war in which Zeus and his siblings overthrew the Titans. The Gigantomachy came later in mythic chronology: Gaia, angered at the imprisonment of the Titans, bore the Giants to avenge them.
