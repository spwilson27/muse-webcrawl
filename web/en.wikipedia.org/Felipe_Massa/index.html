Felipe Massa (born 25 April 1981) is a Brazilian racing driver who competed in Formula One from 2002 to 2017. He drove for Sauber, spent eleven seasons with Ferrari, and finished his Formula One career with Williams. Massa won 11 Grands Prix and is best remembered for narrowly losing the 2008 World Championship to Lewis Hamilton by a single point.

Formula One career

Massa joined Ferrari in 2006 alongside Michael Schumacher and won his first races that year. In the dramatic finale of the 2008 season at Interlagos, he won his home race and was champion for a few seconds, until Hamilton overtook Timo Glock on the final lap to take the title. In 2009 Massa suffered a serious head injury at the Hungarian Grand Prix when a spring from Rubens Barrichello's car struck his helmet; he recovered and returned to racing the following season, serving as Fernando Alonso's team-mate until moving to Williams in 2014.

Later career

After Formula One, Massa raced in Formula E for the Venturi team and later in the Brazilian Stock Car Pro Series. He also served as president of the FIA's drivers' commission and has been involved in a legal challenge concerning the 2008 Singapore Grand Prix "Crashgate" scandal and its effect on the 2008 championship.
