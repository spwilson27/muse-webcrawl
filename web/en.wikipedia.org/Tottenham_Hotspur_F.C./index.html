Tottenham Hotspur Football Club, commonly known as Spurs, is a professional football club in Tottenham, North London, England, competing in the Premier League. Founded in 1882, the club's motto is Audere est Facere ("To Dare Is to Do").

History

Tottenham won the FA Cup in 1901 while still a Southern League club — the only non-League side to do so since the Football League's formation. Under Bill Nicholson, Spurs became the first British club to achieve the league and FA Cup Double in the 20th century, in 1960–61, and in 1963 the first British club to win a European trophy, the Cup Winners' Cup. Later successes included FA Cups in 1981, 1982 and 1991 and the League Cup in 2008.

In 2019, under Mauricio Pochettino, Spurs reached the UEFA Champions League final for the first time. The club moved into the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, a 62,000-seat venue on the site of the old White Hart Lane, in April 2019.

Rivalries

The club's principal rivalry is the North London derby against Arsenal. It also contests London derbies with Chelsea and West Ham United.
