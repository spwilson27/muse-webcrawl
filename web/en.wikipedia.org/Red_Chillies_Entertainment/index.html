Red Chillies Entertainment

Red Chillies Entertainment is an Indian film production and distribution company based in Mumbai, founded in 2002 by actor Shah Rukh Khan and his wife Gauri Khan. The company took over from the earlier Dreamz Unlimited partnership.

Productions

Red Chillies has produced many of Shah Rukh Khan's films, including Main Hoon Na (2004), Om Shanti Om (2007), My Name Is Khan (2010, co-production), Ra.One (2011), Chennai Express (2013), Dear Zindagi (2016), Jawan (2023) and Dunki (2023). Its subsidiary Red Chillies VFX is one of India's leading visual effects studios.

Other ventures

The company holds stakes in the Kolkata Knight Riders of the Indian Premier League and other T20 franchises through the Knight Riders Group, and has expanded into streaming content for Netflix and other platforms.
