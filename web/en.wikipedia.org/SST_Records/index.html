SST Records

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

SST Records is an American independent record label formed in 1966 in Long Beach, California, by Greg Ginn, initially as Solid State Transmitters to sell radio equipment. It became one of the most important labels in American underground music after Ginn relaunched it in 1978 to release records by his band Black Flag.

History

SST grew out of the Southern California hardcore punk scene. Frustrated that no label would release Black Flag's music, Ginn built his own label and a pioneering do-it-yourself touring network of independent promoters and venues across the United States. The label's roster expanded through the 1980s to include the Minutemen, Meat Puppets, Hüsker Dü, Bad Brains, Sonic Youth, Dinosaur Jr., and Soundgarden, making SST central to the development of American alternative and indie rock.

Legacy

SST's relentless touring circuit and mail-order distribution helped create the infrastructure of American independent music. The label's later years were marked by disputes with former artists over royalties and reissues, and several major acts left for major labels, but its 1980s catalogue remains highly influential.
