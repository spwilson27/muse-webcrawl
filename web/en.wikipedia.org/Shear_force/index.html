Shear force

In solid mechanics, shearing forces are unaligned forces acting on one part of a body in a specific direction, and another part of the body in the opposite direction. When the forces are collinear (aligned with each other), they are called tension forces or compression forces.

An example is an airplane wing in flight: the air is pushed down while the wing experiences lift upward, creating shear. In structural engineering, shear force at a section of a beam is the algebraic sum of the vertical forces acting on one side of the section, and is commonly plotted in a shear force diagram.

Shear force is related to shear stress, which is the component of stress coplanar with a material cross section, and to shear strain, the deformation of the material.
