The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India, contested annually by ten franchise-based teams representing Indian cities. Founded by the BCCI in 2007, its first season was played in 2008. It is the most-watched Twenty20 league in the world and ranks among the richest sporting leagues by revenue and media rights value.

History

The league was conceived after India won the inaugural ICC World Twenty20 in 2007. The BCCI announced the IPL in September 2007, and eight city franchises were sold at auction in January 2008 for a combined base price of about US$724 million. Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians became its most successful early franchises. The league expanded to ten teams in 2022 with the addition of Gujarat Titans and Lucknow Super Giants.

Format

Teams play a double round-robin group stage, with the top four advancing to playoffs consisting of two qualifiers and an eliminator, culminating in a final. Matches are Twenty20, with each side batting for up to 20 overs. Players are acquired through a mega-auction held periodically and annual mini-auctions, with squads mixing Indian and overseas players; only four overseas players may appear in a playing XI.

Economics

Media rights for 2023–2027 were sold for over US$6 billion across television and digital platforms, making the IPL one of the most valuable sports properties per match. Franchise valuations have risen sharply, and the league has transformed cricket economics, player salaries, and the global Twenty20 calendar.

Champions

Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings have each won five titles. Other winners include Kolkata Knight Riders, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Rajasthan Royals (inaugural champions in 2008), Gujarat Titans, and Royal Challengers Bengaluru, who won their first title in 2025.
