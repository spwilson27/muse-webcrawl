Paper craft

Paper craft is a collection of crafts using paper or card as the primary artistic medium for the creation of two or three-dimensional objects. Paper has been used for craft purposes since its invention in China in the 2nd century AD, and techniques range from simple cutting and folding to complex sculptural work.

Types

Paper crafts include origami (the art of paper folding), papercutting, quilling (paper filigree), papier-mâché, card making, scrapbooking, bookbinding, paper sculpture, and decoupage. Many of these traditions developed independently in different cultures: Chinese and Scherenschnitte papercutting, Japanese origami, and Mexican papel picado are among the best known.

Materials

Paper crafts typically require paper or cardstock, scissors or a craft knife, adhesives such as glue or tape, and sometimes paint or other decorative materials. Compared with crafts using wood, metal, or clay, paper is inexpensive and widely available, which makes paper craft popular in education and as a hobby.
