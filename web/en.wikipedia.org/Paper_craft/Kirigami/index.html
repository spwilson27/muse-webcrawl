Kirigami

Kirigami is a variation of origami, the Japanese art of paper folding, that includes cutting of the paper rather than folding only. Its name combines kiru ("to cut") and kami ("paper").

Techniques and uses

Kirigami is used to make pop-up cards and books, architectural models and intricate symmetrical designs. It typically starts from a folded base from which cuts are made and then unfolded to reveal the finished pattern.

Scientific applications

Engineers have adapted kirigami patterns to create deployable structures, stretchable electronics and metamaterials whose properties derive from patterned cuts.
