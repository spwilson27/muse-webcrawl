Electoral district

An electoral district, also known as an election district, legislative district, voting district, constituency, riding, or ward, is a territorial subdivision created for the purpose of electing members to a legislative body.

Districts may be single-member, electing one representative — common in plurality systems such as those of the United States House of Representatives and the British House of Commons — or multi-member, as in proportional representation systems.

Apportionment and boundaries

District boundaries are periodically redrawn to reflect population changes, a process called redistricting or redistribution. Deliberate manipulation of boundaries for partisan advantage is known as gerrymandering. Principles of districting include population equality, contiguity, compactness, and respect for communities and administrative boundaries.
