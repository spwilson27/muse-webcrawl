Tatuus Racing S.p.A. is an Italian manufacturer of racing cars based near Monza. Founded in 1980 by Artico Sandonà, the company is one of the world's leading suppliers of chassis for junior single-seater categories, particularly Formula 4 and Formula Regional.

History and products

Tatuus began by building cars for Formula Ford and Formula 3 in Italy before expanding internationally. It supplied the chassis for the Formula Renault 2.0 categories raced across Europe, and from 2014 became the dominant constructor in FIA Formula 4 championships worldwide with its F4-T014 and second-generation F4-T421 cars, both built around a carbon-fibre monocoque with halo protection in the later design.

Formula Regional and other series

The Tatuus T-318 and its successor the T-326 serve as the standard chassis of the Formula Regional European Championship and sister series in the Americas, Asia, Oceania, and the Middle East. Tatuus also built cars for the Toyota Racing Series in New Zealand, the USF2000 Championship in the United States (the USF-17), and the W Series. In 2021 the company merged its operations with Autotecnica Motori under the ATM-Tatuus group structure.
