Trekking pole

Trekking poles (also known as hiking poles, hiking sticks, or walking poles) are poles used by hikers to assist their balance and provide support while walking over rough terrain. Modern trekking poles are usually made of aluminium or carbon fibre, are telescopically adjustable in two or three sections, and feature cork or foam grips, wrist straps, and carbide tips with removable rubber feet and snow baskets.

Benefits and use

Poles transfer part of the body's load from the legs to the arms and shoulders, reduce impact on the knees during descents, improve stability on slippery or uneven ground, aid river crossings, and help establish a walking rhythm. Studies have found they can reduce compressive force on the knees by up to about 25 percent on descents. They are standard equipment in hiking, trekking, Nordic walking, and ski touring, where similar poles are used for propulsion.
