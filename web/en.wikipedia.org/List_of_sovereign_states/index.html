List of sovereign states

The following is a list providing an overview of sovereign states around the world with information on their status and recognition of their sovereignty. The list is divided into two parts. The first part contains the 193 member states of the United Nations and the two observer states, the Holy See and the State of Palestine. The second part contains states that claim sovereignty but are not universally recognised.

Membership in the United Nations system is based on the principle of the sovereign equality of all its members. The criteria for statehood under customary international law are generally taken from the 1933 Montevideo Convention: a permanent population, a defined territory, a government, and the capacity to enter into relations with other states.

United Nations member states

There are 193 United Nations member states, from Afghanistan to Zimbabwe. The newest member is South Sudan, which joined in 2011 following its independence from Sudan. Each member state has one vote in the General Assembly.

Observer states

The Holy See (Vatican City) and the State of Palestine hold non-member observer state status at the United Nations, allowing them to participate in the proceedings of the General Assembly without voting rights.
