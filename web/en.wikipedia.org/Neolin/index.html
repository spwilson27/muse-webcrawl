Neolin

Neolin (fl. 1760s) was a Lenape (Delaware) religious prophet in the Ohio Country who preached a message of purification and resistance to European colonization. His teachings were a major influence on Pontiac, the Ottawa leader whose war against the British followed the French and Indian War.

Teachings

Around 1761 Neolin described a visionary journey to the Master of Life, who instructed Native peoples to abandon the goods, alcohol, and practices adopted from Europeans and to return to traditional ways, uniting in abstinence and self-sufficiency; only then would the game return and the colonists be driven out. The message combined moral reform with political resistance and spread widely among Great Lakes and Ohio peoples.

Legacy

Neolin's movement is an early example of the prophetic revitalization movements that recurred in Native American history, later echoed by Tenskwatawa, the Shawnee Prophet, in the early 19th century.
