List of tallest structures

The Burj Khalifa (828 m) is the tallest human-made structure in the world.

This is a list of the tallest structures in the world, past and present. The tallest human-made structure is the Burj Khalifa in Dubai, at 828 metres (2,717 ft), completed in 2010. Structures include self-supporting towers, skyscrapers, guyed masts, chimneys, bridges, offshore platforms and other constructions; heights are given to the architectural or structural top, as applicable.

Tallest by category

The tallest guyed mast is the KVLY-TV mast in North Dakota, at 629 m (1963). The tallest chimney is that of the Ekibastuz GRES-2 power station in Kazakhstan (420 m). The tallest bridge tower is on the Millau Viaduct in France (343 m), and the tallest offshore platform is the Petronius platform in the Gulf of Mexico, extending 610 m from seabed to top. Historically, the Great Pyramid of Giza (147 m) was the world's tallest structure for more than 3,800 years, a record no successor has approached in duration.

Measurement

The Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH) maintains criteria for measuring buildings, using height to architectural top as its principal standard. Guyed masts, held up by cables, are usually listed separately from self-supporting structures, a distinction that affects rankings (the Warsaw radio mast, 646 m, was the tallest structure ever until its collapse in 1991).
