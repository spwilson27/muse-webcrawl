SC Bern

Schlittschuh Club Bern (Skating Club Bern), commonly known as SC Bern, SCB, or simply Bern, is a professional ice hockey team based in Bern, Switzerland. The club plays in the National League (NL), the top tier of the Swiss league system, and is the most popular ice hockey club in Switzerland.

History

SC Bern was founded on 3 November 1930 and began playing in 1931. The club has won the Swiss championship numerous times — among its titles are those of 1959, 1965, 1974, 1975, 1977, 1979, 1989, 1991, 1992, 1997, 2004, 2010, 2013, 2016, 2017, and 2019 — making it one of the most successful clubs in Swiss hockey history.

Arena and attendance

The team plays at the PostFinance Arena, which has a capacity of over 17,000 and is famous for its steep standing-room terrace, the Stehrampe, one of the largest in world hockey. SC Bern regularly leads European ice hockey in attendance, drawing average crowds of more than 16,000 per game — figures unmatched by any other European club.

Identity

The club's colors are black, yellow, and red, and its crest features a bear, the heraldic animal of the city and canton of Bern. Notable players who have worn the SCB jersey include NHL stars during lockout seasons, as well as long-serving Swiss internationals. The club's principal rivals include HC Fribourg-Gottéron and the ZSC Lions.
