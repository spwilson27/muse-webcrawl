The 2000 Summer Paralympic Games were held in Sydney, Australia, from 18 to 29 October 2000. They were the eleventh Summer Paralympic Games and followed the 2000 Summer Olympics, using many of the same venues at Sydney Olympic Park.

Participation

A total of 121 nations took part, represented by 3,843 athletes competing in 561 medal events across 18 sports. Australia topped the medal table, followed by Great Britain and Canada.

Highlights

The Games were widely praised for record crowds and media coverage, with more than a million tickets sold. Australian swimmer Siobhan Paton won six gold medals, and British wheelchair racer Tanni Grey-Thompson won four golds. The event is credited with significantly raising the profile of Paralympic sport.

Controversy

The Games were later overshadowed by a scandal in which members of the gold-medal-winning Spanish basketball team for athletes with intellectual disabilities were found not to have disabilities, leading to the removal of intellectual-disability events from subsequent Paralympics for a time.
