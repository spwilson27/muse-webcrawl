Eddie Bauer, LLC is an American clothing store chain headquartered in Bellevue, Washington. Founded in 1920 in Seattle by outdoorsman Eddie Bauer (1899–1986), the company sells outdoor apparel, active wear, footwear, and gear through retail stores, outlets, a catalogue, and e-commerce.

An Eddie Bauer retail store

History

Eddie Bauer opened his first shop, Eddie Bauer's Sport Shop, in Seattle in 1920, initially repairing and selling tennis rackets and fishing tackle. In 1936, after nearly dying of hypothermia on a fishing trip, he developed the Skyliner, the first quilted down jacket patented in America (1940), which the U.S. Army Air Corps later adopted in large numbers as flight gear during the Second World War. The company pioneered mail-order sales of outdoor equipment and outfitted major mountaineering expeditions, including the first American ascent of Mount Everest in 1963.

Later developments

The company was sold to General Mills in 1971 and then to Spiegel in 1988, shifting emphasis toward casual apparel. After Spiegel's bankruptcy, Eddie Bauer emerged as a standalone company in 2005, but filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy itself in 2009 and was acquired by Golden Gate Capital. In 2021 it was bought by Authentic Brands Group and SPARC Group. The brand continues to market technical outerwear, including its First Ascent line for mountaineers, alongside mainstream clothing.
