Elections in Ireland

Elections in Ireland cover elections to Dáil Éireann, Seanad Éireann, local authorities, the presidency, and the European Parliament, as well as referendums on constitutional amendments.

Most elections use proportional representation by the single transferable vote (PR-STV) in multi-seat constituencies, a system enshrined in the Constitution of Ireland for Dáil and local elections. Voters rank candidates in order of preference.

Dáil elections

General elections must be held at least every five years. Teachtaí Dála (TDs) are elected from constituencies returning three to five members. Major parties include Fianna Fáil, Fine Gael, and Sinn Féin.

Presidential elections are held every seven years; the President is elected by instant-runoff form of STV in a single national constituency.
