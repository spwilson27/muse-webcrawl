Marina Beach
LocationChennai, Tamil Nadu, India
CoastBay of Bengal (Coromandel Coast)
Lengthabout 13 km

Marina Beach is a natural urban beach in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, running along the Bay of Bengal from near Fort St. George in the north to Besant Nagar in the south. At roughly 13 kilometres it is the longest natural urban beach in India and one of the longest in the world.

Features

The wide sandy beach is lined by a promenade developed in the 1880s under Governor Mountstuart Elphinstone Grant Duff. Landmarks along it include the statues of Tamil scholars and leaders, the MGR and Anna memorials, Chennai Lighthouse, and the Senate House of the University of Madras. Bathing and swimming are officially prohibited because of strong currents, but the beach draws huge crowds of walkers, vendors and visitors, especially at sunrise and on weekends.

Events

Marina Beach has hosted major public gatherings, including the large-scale pro-jallikattu protests of January 2017.
