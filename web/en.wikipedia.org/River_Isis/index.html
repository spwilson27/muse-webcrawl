The Isis is the name given to the part of the River Thames above Iffley Lock which flows through the English university city of Oxford, past Christ Church Meadow and the Oxford University boat houses.

Name and rowing

The name derives from Tamesis, the Latin name for the Thames, interpreted as a combination of "Thame" and "Isis". The Isis is central to rowing at the University of Oxford: college crews train and race on it, including in the bumps races of Eights Week and Torpids, and it hosts the reserve Boat Race between Isis (Oxford's second boat) and Goldie (Cambridge's). The stretch below Folly Bridge is used by the Oxford University Boat Club.
