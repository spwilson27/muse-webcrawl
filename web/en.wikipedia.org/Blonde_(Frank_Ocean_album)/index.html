Blonde (Frank Ocean album)

Artist: Frank Ocean
Released: August 20, 2016
Genre: R&B, pop, avant-garde
Length: 60 minutes
Label: Boys Don't Cry (self-released)

Blonde (stylised Blond on the cover) is the second studio album by American singer Frank Ocean. It was released on August 20, 2016, as an Apple Music exclusive, a day after the surprise visual album Endless. The two releases allowed Ocean to fulfil and exit his contract with Def Jam Recordings; Blonde was issued independently through his own Boys Don't Cry imprint.

Background and release

Following the acclaim of Channel Orange (2012), Ocean spent several years recording in studios in Los Angeles, London, and elsewhere, repeatedly delaying the album's expected release. Contributors include Beyoncé, Kanye West, Kendrick Lamar, André 3000, Tyler, the Creator, and producer Jamie xx, though many contributions are uncredited or subtly woven into the arrangements.

Music and reception

The album is characterised by sparse, guitar- and keyboard-led arrangements, pitch-shifted vocals, and a loosely narrative structure dealing with masculinity, memory, sexuality, and fame. Blonde debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200 and was met with widespread critical acclaim; it has since been ranked among the greatest albums of all time in critics' lists, including a high placement on Rolling Stone's revised 500 Greatest Albums list.

Track listing

Tracks include "Nikes", "Ivy", "Pink + White", "Be Yourself", "Solo", "Skyline To", "Self Control", "Good Guy", "Nights", "Pretty Sweet", "White Ferrari", "Seigfried", and "Godspeed".
