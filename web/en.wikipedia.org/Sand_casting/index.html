Sand casting

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Sand casting

Process: Metal casting

Mold material: Sand (green sand or chemically bonded sand)

Products: Engine blocks, pump housings, machine bases, bells, sculpture

Characteristics: Low tooling cost, any size from grams to hundreds of tonnes

Sand casting, also known as sand molded casting, is a metal casting process characterised by using sand known as casting sand as the mold material. Well over half of all metal castings by weight are produced by sand casting. The mold is made by packing specially prepared sand around a pattern of the desired part; after the pattern is withdrawn, molten metal is poured into the cavity, where it solidifies; the sand mold is then broken away to release the casting. Sand casting is comparatively inexpensive, can use nearly any alloy, and accommodates parts from a few grams to hundreds of tonnes, though with rougher surface finish and looser tolerances than processes such as investment casting or die casting.

The process

The two-part mold is formed in a box called a flask: the upper half is the cope and the lower the drag. “Green sand” — a mixture of silica sand, bentonite clay and water — is rammed around the split pattern; channels (the sprue, runners and gates) are formed to feed metal, and risers act as reservoirs to compensate for shrinkage. Cores of bonded sand create internal cavities. After pouring and cooling, the casting is shaken out of the sand, the gating is cut off, and the part is cleaned, shot-blasted and, if necessary, machined. The sand is largely reclaimed and reused.

Applications and history

Sand casting is the classic process for engine blocks and cylinder heads, large pump and valve bodies, machine-tool bases, railway components, bells and bronze sculpture. Sand molds descend from antiquity: bronze casting in sand and clay molds was practised in ancient China and the Mediterranean world, and the process remains the backbone of the foundry industry because new tooling consists of little more than a pattern.
