George William Russell (born 15 February 1998) is a British racing driver who competes in Formula One for Mercedes. Russell won the 2017 GP3 Series and the 2018 FIA Formula 2 Championship back-to-back, and is a member of the Mercedes Junior Team. He serves as a director of the Grand Prix Drivers' Association.

Russell joined Mercedes as a full-time Formula One driver in 2022.

Career

After a successful karting career, Russell won the BRDC Formula 4 Championship in 2014 and the McLaren Autosport BRDC Award. He took the GP3 title with ART Grand Prix in 2017 and the Formula 2 title in 2018, again with ART — matching the feat of winning both feeder series in consecutive years.

Formula One

Russell debuted in Formula One with Williams in 2019. Despite driving one of the slowest cars, he out-qualified his team-mates in every session of his rookie year. In December 2020 he stood in for Lewis Hamilton (absent with COVID-19) at Mercedes at the Sakhir Grand Prix, qualifying second and leading most of the race before a pit-stop error and a puncture dropped him to ninth; he nevertheless scored his first championship points. He took his first podium with Williams at the rain-shortened 2021 Belgian Grand Prix, having qualified second.

Promoted to Mercedes for 2022 alongside Hamilton, Russell took his first pole position in Hungary and his maiden Grand Prix victory at São Paulo in November 2022, leading a Mercedes one-two. He finished ahead of Hamilton in the 2022 championship. Further wins followed, including the 2024 Austrian and Las Vegas Grands Prix, and he became Mercedes' senior driver after Hamilton's move to Ferrari for 2025.
