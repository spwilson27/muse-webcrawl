Jho Low


Low Taek Jho (born 4 November 1981), known as Jho Low, is a Malaysian businessman and international fugitive.

Low Taek Jho (born 4 November 1981), often referred to as Jho Low, is a Malaysian businessman and international fugitive wanted by authorities in Malaysia, Singapore, and the United States for his central role in the 1Malaysia Development Berhad (1MDB) scandal, in which billions of dollars were allegedly embezzled from a Malaysian state investment fund.

Early life

Low was born in George Town, Penang, into a wealthy Malaysian Chinese family with business interests through the MWE group. He was educated at the Harrow School in England and the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, where he cultivated connections with students from Middle Eastern royal families that later became central to his deal-making.

1MDB scandal

Prosecutors allege that Low was the mastermind of a scheme, beginning in 2009, that diverted an estimated US$4.5 billion from 1MDB into shell companies and accounts used to buy luxury real estate, art, a superyacht (the Equanimity), a private jet, and to finance the film The Wolf of Wall Street through Red Granite Pictures. In 2018, the U.S. Department of Justice indicted Low on money-laundering and bribery conspiracy charges; Malaysia and Singapore also issued warrants. Low has denied wrongdoing, claiming he was merely an intermediary, and his whereabouts have remained unknown, with reports placing him in China.

Fallout

The scandal contributed to the 2018 electoral defeat of Malaysian prime minister Najib Razak, who was later convicted of corruption in connection with 1MDB funds. Goldman Sachs, which raised much of the money for the fund, paid billions in fines. Low's story is recounted in the book Billion Dollar Whale (2018) by Tom Wright and Bradley Hope.
