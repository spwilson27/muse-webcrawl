Colleges of the University of Oxford

The University of Oxford is made up of 39 self-governing constituent colleges and a number of permanent private halls. Colleges are independent institutions with their own fellows, students, finances and buildings, federated within the university.

History

University College (founded 1249), Balliol (1263) and Merton (1264) are among the oldest. Teaching, tutorials, accommodation and much pastoral care are provided by colleges, while the central university awards degrees and runs examinations. The newest college, Reuben College, was founded in 2019.

Life

Intercollegiate rivalry is expressed in sport, notably the summer rowing bumps races (Eights Week), and in the informal Norrington Table ranking exam results.
