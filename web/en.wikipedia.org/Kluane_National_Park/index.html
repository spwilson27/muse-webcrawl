Kluane National Park and Reserve


Kluane National Park and Reserve, Yukon, Canada. Established 1972; area about 22,000 km².

Kluane National Park and Reserve (French: Parc national et réserve de parc national de Kluane) is a Canadian national park in the southwestern corner of Yukon, near the borders with Alaska and British Columbia. It was established in 1972 and covers roughly 22,000 square kilometres (8,500 sq mi), making it one of the largest national parks in Canada.

Geography

The park contains Mount Logan, at 5,959 metres (19,551 ft) the highest mountain in Canada and the second-highest peak in North America, within the Saint Elias Mountains. Much of the park is covered by vast icefields — the largest non-polar icefield in the world — feeding glaciers such as the Kaskawulsh Glacier. The eastern portion of the park is lower and greener, with forests and lakes including Kluane Lake, the largest lake in Yukon.

Recognition

In 1979 Kluane was designated, together with Wrangell–St. Elias and Glacier Bay national parks in Alaska, a UNESCO World Heritage Site (later extended to include Tatlatui and Alsek parks), recognized for its spectacular glacier and icefield landscapes and habitat for grizzly bears, caribou and Dall sheep.

The park is a popular destination for mountaineering, hiking and rafting, particularly on the Alsek River. The gateway community is Haines Junction.
