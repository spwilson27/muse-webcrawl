Cyclic guanosine monophosphate

Structure of cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP).

Cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP) is a cyclic nucleotide derived from guanosine triphosphate (GTP). Like its better-known counterpart cyclic AMP, cGMP acts as a second messenger in signal transduction, notably in the nitric oxide (NO) pathway that relaxes vascular smooth muscle, and in phototransduction in the retina.

FormulaC10H12N5O7P
Synthesised byGuanylate cyclase
Degraded byPhosphodiesterases (esp. PDE5, PDE6)
Main effectorProtein kinase G (PKG)

Synthesis and signalling

cGMP is produced by guanylate cyclases. The soluble enzyme (sGC) is activated when nitric oxide binds its haem group — the discovery of this pathway earned Robert Furchgott, Louis Ignarro and Ferid Murad the 1998 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. Particulate (membrane-bound) guanylate cyclases are instead activated by natriuretic peptides such as ANP and BNP. Once formed, cGMP activates protein kinase G, opens or closes cyclic nucleotide–gated ion channels, and modulates phosphodiesterases. It is broken down by phosphodiesterases to 5′-GMP.

Physiological roles

Vasodilation

In vascular smooth muscle, cGMP-driven kinase activity lowers intracellular calcium and relaxes the cell, dilating blood vessels and lowering blood pressure. Organic nitrates such as nitroglycerin act by supplying NO to this pathway.

Vision

In rod and cone photoreceptors, cGMP keeps sodium channels open in the dark. Light activates the enzyme phosphodiesterase 6 (PDE6) via transducin, cGMP falls, channels close, and the cell hyperpolarises — the first electrical event of vision. Mutations in this pathway cause forms of retinitis pigmentosa.

Other roles

cGMP also participates in platelet inhibition, intestinal fluid secretion (the target of the drug linaclotide), bone growth and some forms of synaptic plasticity.

Pharmacology

Inhibiting the cGMP-degrading enzyme PDE5 raises cGMP levels; sildenafil (Viagra), tadalafil and related drugs exploit this to treat erectile dysfunction and pulmonary arterial hypertension. The sGC stimulator riociguat likewise targets the pathway. Combining PDE5 inhibitors with nitrates can cause dangerous hypotension because both raise cGMP.
