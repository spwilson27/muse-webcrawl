Dominion (card game)

Cards from the base set of Dominion.

Dominion is a deck-building card game created by Donald X. Vaccarino and published by Rio Grande Games in 2008. It is widely credited with popularising the deck-building mechanic, in which players construct their own decks during the course of play rather than bringing pre-built decks to the game. Dominion won the Spiel des Jahres award in 2009 and has been followed by numerous expansions.

Gameplay

Each player begins with an identical small deck of ten cards: seven Coppers (currency) and three Estates (victory points). On a turn, a player plays Action cards, buys new cards from a shared supply of Kingdom cards, and discards their hand, drawing five new cards. Purchased cards go into the player's discard pile and are shuffled into the deck, progressively strengthening it. The game ends when the Province pile or any three supply piles are exhausted; the player whose deck contains the most victory points wins.

Expansions

Expansions include Intrigue, Seaside, Alchemy, Prosperity, Cornucopia, Hinterlands, Dark Ages, Guilds, Adventures, Empires, Nocturne, Renaissance, Menagerie, Allies, and Plunder, together with several promo cards. A second edition of the base game and Intrigue appeared in 2016, replacing some cards.

Reception and legacy

Dominion's success spawned an entire genre of deck-building games, including Ascension, Thunderstone, and numerous digital adaptations. The game is noted for its combinatorial variety: the base set's 25 Kingdom card types allow millions of distinct setups.
