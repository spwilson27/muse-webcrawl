Kingdom of Romania

The Kingdom of Romania was a constitutional monarchy in southeastern Europe from 13 March 1881, when the Romanian principality was proclaimed a kingdom under Carol I, until 1947, when the monarchy was abolished and the Romanian People's Republic proclaimed.

History

Romania had won independence from the Ottoman Empire in the war of 1877–78. Under Carol I and Ferdinand I the country fought in the Second Balkan War and, from 1916, the First World War; victory brought union with Transylvania, Bessarabia, Bukovina and Banat, creating Greater Romania in 1918–1920.

Second World War and end

Losses of territory in 1940 preceded Carol II's abdication and the dictatorship of Ion Antonescu under King Michael I, who in August 1944 overthrew Antonescu and switched Romania to the Allied side. Under Soviet pressure Michael abdicated on 30 December 1947, ending the kingdom.
