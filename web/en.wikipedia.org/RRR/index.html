RRR (subtitled in Telugu as Ranam Roaran Rudhiram and in Hindi as Rise Roar Revolt) is a 2022 Indian Telugu-language epic action drama film directed by S. S. Rajamouli, who co-wrote it with V. Vijayendra Prasad. The film is a fictionalised account of the friendship between two real Indian revolutionaries of the 1920s, Alluri Sitarama Raju (Ram Charan) and Komaram Bheem (N. T. Rama Rao Jr.), imagining that they met and fought together against the British Raj.

Plot

In 1920, tribal leader Komaram Bheem travels to Delhi to rescue Malli, a young Gond girl abducted by the British governor's wife. Police officer A. Rama Raju, secretly a revolutionary seeking to arm his people, is assigned to hunt the unknown rescuer. The two meet saving a boy from a derailment and become close friends, unaware of each other's missions. When their identities are revealed, Raju captures Bheem; wracked with guilt, he later frees him at the cost of his own freedom, and the two reunite to storm the governor's residence, ignite a revolt and rescue Malli.

Reception and legacy

Made on a budget reported around ₹550 crore, then the most expensive Indian film ever, RRR became a global phenomenon, especially after streaming release in the West. It won wide critical acclaim for its direction, performances, action set-pieces and music. The song "Naatu Naatu" won the Academy Award and Golden Globe for Best Original Song — a first for an Indian feature film — and the film won the National Film Award for Best Popular Film, among many honours.
