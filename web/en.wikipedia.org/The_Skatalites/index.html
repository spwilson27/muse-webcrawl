The Skatalites

The Skatalites are a Jamaican band that founded the ska genre. Formed in Kingston in 1964 from Kingston's top session musicians, they backed nearly every major Jamaican artist of the era and defined ska's horn-driven sound.

History

Core members included trombonist Don Drummond, saxophonists Tommy McCook and Roland Alphonso, trumpeter Johnny "Dizzy" Moore, pianist Jackie Mittoo, bassist Lloyd Brevett and drummer Lloyd Knibb. They recorded for producers Coxsone Dodd (Studio One) and Duke Reid, cutting classics like "Guns of Navarone" and backing Bob Marley, Peter Tosh, Prince Buster and Toots and the Maytals.

Later years

The original band broke up in 1965 after Drummond was institutionalized for murdering his partner; they reformed in 1983 and have toured for decades since, profoundly influencing the British 2 Tone movement and third-wave ska. See also The Sopranos.
