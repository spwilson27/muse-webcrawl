Jacob Bernoulli

Born6 January 1655, Basel, Switzerland
Died16 August 1705 (aged 50), Basel
FieldsMathematics
Known forBernoulli numbers; law of large numbers; Ars Conjectandi

Jacob Bernoulli (also spelled Jacques or James; 6 January 1655 – 16 August 1705) was a Swiss mathematician, one of the most prominent members of the Bernoulli family of mathematicians. He was an early proponent of Leibnizian calculus and made foundational contributions to probability theory; his posthumous Ars Conjectandi (1713) contains the first proof of the law of large numbers.

Life

Born in Basel, Jacob studied philosophy and theology at his father's insistence but secretly pursued mathematics and astronomy. Travelling through Europe, he met leading scientists including Robert Boyle and Robert Hooke. In 1687 he became professor of mathematics at the University of Basel, a chair he held until his death. He taught and later famously quarrelled with his younger brother Johann Bernoulli, also a gifted mathematician; their rivalry over problems such as the brachistochrone spurred the development of the calculus of variations.

Contributions

Bernoulli introduced the Bernoulli numbers in connection with sums of powers, studied the Bernoulli distribution and Bernoulli trials, and discovered the approximate value of the constant e through a problem in compound interest. He made important contributions to the theory of differential equations, the catenary, the lemniscate of Bernoulli, and polar coordinates. His Ars Conjectandi ("The Art of Conjecturing"), published eight years after his death, systematised probability as a mathematical measure of uncertainty and proved the weak law of large numbers for Bernoulli trials.

Legacy

Bernoulli was so fond of the logarithmic spiral — which he called the spira mirabilis, "marvellous spiral" — that he asked for it to be carved on his tombstone with the motto Eadem mutata resurgo ("Although changed, I arise the same"). The stonemason mistakenly carved an Archimedean spiral instead. The lunar crater Bernoulli is named for him and his brother Johann.
