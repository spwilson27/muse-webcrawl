Rajneesh (born Chandra Mohan Jain; 11 December 1931 – 19 January 1990), also known as Acharya Rajneesh, Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh, and later simply Osho, was an Indian godman, mystic and founder of the Rajneesh movement. During his lifetime, he was viewed as a controversial new religious movement leader and a mystic guru.

Teachings

Rajneesh's teachings emphasised the importance of meditation, mindfulness, love, celebration, courage, creativity and humour — qualities he viewed as being suppressed by adherence to static belief systems, religious dogma and social conditioning. He advocated a more open attitude towards human sexuality, earning him the nickname "sex guru" in the Indian and later the international press.

Rajneeshpuram

In 1981, Rajneesh's followers established an intentional community at Rajneeshpuram in Oregon, United States. The commune became the focus of intense conflict with local residents and the state government, and a series of legal battles limited its development. In 1984, several of Rajneesh's senior followers were discovered to have committed a bioterror attack in The Dalles, Oregon, in an effort to influence local elections. Rajneesh denied knowledge of the crimes; he was arrested in 1985, pleaded guilty to immigration fraud, and was deported from the United States.

Later years

After his deportation, Rajneesh eventually returned to Pune, India, where his ashram became the Osho International Meditation Resort. He adopted the name "Osho" in 1989 and died in January 1990. His books, compiled from thousands of recorded discourses, remain widely read.
