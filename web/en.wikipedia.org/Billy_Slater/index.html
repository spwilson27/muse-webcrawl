Billy Slater (born 18 June 1983) is an Australian former professional rugby league footballer and coach. A fullback, he played his entire club career with the Melbourne Storm in the National Rugby League (NRL) and is widely regarded as one of the greatest fullbacks in the sport's history.

Playing career

Slater debuted for Melbourne in 2003 and won the Dally M Medal in 2011. He was part of Storm premiership-winning teams and won the Clive Churchill Medal in 2009 and 2017. For Queensland he played 31 State of Origin matches and was instrumental in the Maroons' era of dominance, and he represented Australia in 30 Tests, winning the Golden Boot Award in 2008.

Records

Slater retired at the end of 2018 as the NRL's second-highest try-scorer of his era, with 190 career tries for the Storm, a club record.

Coaching

In 2022 Slater became head coach of the Queensland State of Origin team, leading the Maroons to series victories in his first two years in charge.
