Geek Pride Day is an initiative to promote geek culture, celebrated annually on 25 May. The date commemorates the release of the first Star Wars film, A New Hope, on 25 May 1977. The celebration originated in Spain in 2006 as Día del orgullo friki, promoted by blogger Germán Martínez, and spread via the internet to other countries.

Observance

Events include parades, costume gatherings, role-playing games, and readings. The day coincides with two other fandom observances: Towel Day, honouring Douglas Adams, author of The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, and the Glorious 25th of May from Terry Pratchett's Discworld series. A frequently circulated "bill of rights" for geeks accompanies the celebration, asserting rights such as the right to be even more geeky and the right not to like football or any other sport.
