The Limits to Growth


The Limits to Growth is a 1972 report commissioned by the Club of Rome, modelling the consequences of exponential population and economic growth with finite resources.

The Limits to Growth (LTG) is a report that was published in 1972. It was commissioned by the Club of Rome and first presented at the St. Gallen Symposium. Its authors — Donella Meadows, Dennis Meadows, Jørgen Randers and William Behrens III — used the World3 system dynamics model to simulate interactions between population, industrial capital, food production, resource depletion and pollution.

Conclusions

The study concluded that if the growth trends of the time continued unchanged, the limits to growth on the planet would be reached within the next hundred years, most probably resulting in a rather sudden and uncontrollable decline in both population and industrial capacity. It also argued that a sustainable state of ecological and economic stability was achievable through deliberate policy changes.

Reception

The book sold millions of copies and sparked intense debate; critics questioned its assumptions and aggregate modelling. Updated editions appeared in 1992 (Beyond the Limits) and 2004 (a 30-year update).
