Unlockable (video gaming)

In video gaming, an unlockable is content — such as a character, level, weapon, costume or mode — that is initially unavailable and becomes accessible after the player meets conditions such as completing levels, earning points or entering a code.

Types

Common unlockables include playable characters, bonus stages, difficulty modes, concept art galleries and alternative endings.

Design

Unlockables reward progression and extend replay value, though modern games sometimes sell equivalent content as downloadable content or microtransactions.

History

Early arcade and console games used passwords and cheat codes; achievements and online profiles later formalised unlock systems.
