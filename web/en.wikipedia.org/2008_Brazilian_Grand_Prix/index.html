The 2008 Brazilian Grand Prix (formally the Grande Prêmio do Brasil 2008) was a Formula One motor race held on 2 November 2008 at the Autódromo José Carlos Pace, Interlagos, in São Paulo, Brazil. It was the eighteenth and final round of the 2008 Formula One World Championship and produced one of the most dramatic championship finishes in the sport's history: Lewis Hamilton of McLaren won the Drivers' Championship by a single point, overtaking Timo Glock on the final corner of the final lap.

The race

Hamilton arrived seven points ahead of home favourite Felipe Massa and needed to finish fifth or better if Massa won. Massa qualified on pole and, in a race interrupted by rain at the start and finish, drove flawlessly to victory. For most of the closing laps Hamilton ran in the required fifth place, but late rain forced an extra pit stop for intermediate tyres, dropping him to sixth behind Sebastian Vettel. As Massa crossed the line to win, the Ferrari garage began celebrating the championship.

On the last lap, Toyota's Timo Glock — who had gambled on staying on dry-weather tyres — was struggling for grip on the wet final ascent. At the last corner, Junção, Hamilton passed Glock to regain fifth place and with it the title, 98 points to Massa's 97. The Ferrari celebrations gave way to disbelief in an image broadcast worldwide. Hamilton, at 23, became the youngest World Champion at that time, and the first Black driver to win the title. Fernando Alonso finished second and Kimi Räikkönen third; Ferrari won the Constructors' Championship.
