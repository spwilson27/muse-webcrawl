Crack cocaine

Crack cocaine, commonly known as crack, is the freebase form of cocaine that can be smoked. It is made by processing cocaine with baking soda or ammonia and water, forming small rocks.

Effects

Smoking crack delivers cocaine rapidly to the brain, producing an intense, short euphoria lasting about 5–15 minutes, with high potential for addiction, cardiovascular harm and psychosis.

History

Crack appeared in major U.S. cities in the early 1980s and was associated with the crack epidemic, violent crime and controversial sentencing disparities between crack and powder cocaine, partly addressed by the Fair Sentencing Act of 2010.
