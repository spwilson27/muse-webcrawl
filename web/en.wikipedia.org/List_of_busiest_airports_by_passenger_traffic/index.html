List of busiest airports by passenger traffic

The busiest airports by passenger traffic are measured by total passengers (enplaned and deplaned). Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport has been the world's busiest airport by passenger traffic for most years since 1998, except during the COVID-19 pandemic, when Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport briefly took the top spot in 2020.
