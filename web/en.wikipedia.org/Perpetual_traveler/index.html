Perpetual traveler

A perpetual traveler (also "perpetual tourist" or PT) is a person who moves between countries so as not to be considered a tax resident of any jurisdiction, or to live under more favourable legal regimes. The idea is associated with the "flag theory" popularised by investor Harry D. Schultz.

Flag theory

Flag theory suggests planting different aspects of one's life — citizenship, residence, business, assets and recreation — in different countries ("flags") chosen for their advantages. Practitioners may rely on tourist visas, rotating stays before residency thresholds are met.

Criticism and legality

Tax authorities have tightened residency and exit-tax rules, and citizenship-based taxation (as in the United States) limits the strategy. Critics question its practicality, cost and ethics; proponents frame it as lawful jurisdictional arbitrage. The modern digital nomad movement overlaps with, but is distinct from, the PT lifestyle.
