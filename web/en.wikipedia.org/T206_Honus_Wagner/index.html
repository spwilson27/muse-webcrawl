T206 Honus Wagner

The T206 Honus Wagner is a baseball card depicting Pittsburgh Pirates shortstop Honus Wagner, issued by the American Tobacco Company between 1909 and 1911 as part of the T206 series. It is among the rarest and most valuable sports cards in the world.

Rarity

Production of the card was halted early — legend holds that Wagner objected either to his likeness promoting tobacco to children or to his compensation — so only an estimated 50 to 200 examples were distributed, and roughly 60 are known today.

Record sales

The finest known example, the "Gretzky T206", and other copies have repeatedly set auction records for sports cards, with one copy selling for over 7 million US dollars in 2022.

Significance

The card is a cultural touchstone of collecting, frequently cited as the "Mona Lisa of baseball cards".
