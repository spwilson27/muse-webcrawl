Portaledge

A portaledge is a deployable hanging tent system designed for rock climbers spending multiple days and nights on a big wall climb where a flat surface is not available. It consists of a fabric-covered platform surrounded by a metal frame that hangs from a single anchor point and has adjustable suspension straps.

Construction

Most portaledges are made from an aluminium or steel tube frame with a heavy-duty nylon deck. They are designed to be assembled mid-air and to support the weight of two climbers plus gear. Many can be fitted with a storm fly to protect occupants from weather.

Use

Portaledges are used extensively on multi-day ascents in places such as Yosemite National Park, where climbers may spend a week or more on a wall such as El Capitan.
