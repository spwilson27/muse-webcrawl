The American Voter is a 1960 book by Angus Campbell, Philip E. Converse, Warren E. Miller, and Donald E. Stokes, based on data from the Survey Research Center at the University of Michigan. It is a foundational work of American political science, arguing that most voters have low levels of political knowledge and that party identification is the primary long-term force shaping vote choice.

Influence

The book introduced the 'funnel of causality' model and shaped decades of research on voting behaviour, though later scholars criticised its portrayal of voter competence.
