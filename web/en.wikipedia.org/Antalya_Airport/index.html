Antalya Airport (IATA: AYT, ICAO: LTAJ) is a major international airport located 13 km (8.1 mi) northeast of the city centre of Antalya, Turkey. It is a gateway to the resorts of the Turkish Riviera on the Mediterranean coast and is among the busiest airports in Europe during the summer season.

Facilities

The airport has two international terminals, a domestic terminal and a general aviation/CIP terminal. It was built to relieve congestion at a time of rapidly growing charter traffic to Mediterranean resorts. A major expansion programme in the 2020s is enlarging terminal capacity and airfield infrastructure, with the airport aiming to serve up to 80 million passengers annually when the works are completed.

Traffic

Antalya handled more than 31 million passengers in 2019 and recovered to exceed 35 million in 2023, the great majority of them international tourists, historically led by visitors from Russia and Germany. The airport is a base or focus city for carriers including SunExpress, Corendon Airlines and Pegasus Airlines, and is operated by a Fraport–TAV consortium.

Ground transport

The airport is connected to Antalya by road and by the city's tram system, whose airport branch serves the terminals, in addition to bus and taxi services.
