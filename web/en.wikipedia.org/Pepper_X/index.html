Pepper X

Pepper X is a cultivar of the chili pepper Capsicum chinense, bred by Ed Currie of the PuckerButt Pepper Company in South Carolina, who also created the Carolina Reaper. In 2023, Guinness World Records recognised Pepper X as the world's hottest chili pepper, with an average rating of about 2.69 million Scoville Heat Units, surpassing the Carolina Reaper's 1.64 million.

Development

Currie developed Pepper X over roughly a decade of crossbreeding, selecting for extreme capsaicin content. The pepper is a greenish-yellow, knobbly fruit. Its heat was verified by testing at Winthrop University in South Carolina.

Use

Pepper X features in extremely hot sauces, notably "The Last Dab" on the interview show Hot Ones. Because of its potency it is consumed only in tiny quantities.
