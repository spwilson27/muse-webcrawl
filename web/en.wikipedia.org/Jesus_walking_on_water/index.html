Jesus walking on water

Jesus walking on water, also called Jesus walking on the Sea of Galilee, is one of the miracles of Jesus recounted in the New Testament, in Matthew 14, Mark 6, and John 6.

After feeding the five thousand, Jesus sends his disciples ahead by boat while he prays alone. During the night the boat is battered by waves, and Jesus comes to them walking on the sea. The disciples are terrified, thinking him a ghost, but he reassures them. In Matthew's account Peter also walks on the water briefly before his faith falters and he begins to sink.
