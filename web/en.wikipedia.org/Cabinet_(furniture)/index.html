Cabinet (furniture)

A cabinet is a box-shaped piece of furniture with doors and/or drawers for storing miscellaneous items. Some cabinets are stand-alone while others are built into a wall or are attached to it like a medicine cabinet. Cabinets are typically made of wood, coated steel, or synthetic materials.

Types

Common types include kitchen cabinets, bathroom cabinets, filing cabinets for offices, display cabinets with glazed doors, and specialized forms such as the china cabinet and the cabinet of curiosities. A cabinet with shelves and drawers used in a dining room may be called a sideboard or buffet; a tall narrow cabinet may be termed a cupboard or armoire depending on region and use.

Construction

Traditional cabinets were made by cabinetmakers using framed panel construction and joinery such as dovetails and mortise and tenon joints. Modern mass-produced cabinets often use particleboard or medium-density fibreboard with laminate or veneer finishes, assembled from flat-packed components. Built-in cabinets are constructed on site or prefabricated and installed as part of a room's fittings.

Cabinetmaking

Cabinetmaking is the craft of making fine wooden furniture, historically distinct from carpentry and joinery and associated with high-quality work in hardwoods, marquetry, and veneers. The term "cabinet" derives from the small room, or cabinet, in which valuables were kept, and came to be applied to the furniture within.
