Planet of the Apes

Planet of the Apes is a science fiction media franchise consisting of films, books, television series, comics, and other media. The franchise is based on French author Pierre Boulle's 1963 novel La Planète des singes, translated into English as Planet of the Apes or Monkey Planet. The 1968 film adaptation, Planet of the Apes, was a critical and commercial hit, initiating a series of sequels, two television series and a remake.

Premise

The series follows a world in which humans and intelligent apes clash for control. In the original film, astronaut George Taylor (Charlton Heston) lands on a strange planet ruled by apes, in a society divided among chimpanzee intellectuals, gorilla soldiers and orangutan administrators, with humans reduced to mute animals. The famous twist ending reveals the planet to be a future, post-apocalyptic Earth.

Film series

The original series comprised five films between 1968 and 1973: Planet of the Apes, Beneath the Planet of the Apes, Escape from the Planet of the Apes, Conquest of the Planet of the Apes and Battle for the Planet of the Apes. Tim Burton directed a remake in 2001. A reboot series began with Rise of the Planet of the Apes (2011), followed by Dawn of the Planet of the Apes (2014), War for the Planet of the Apes (2017) and Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes (2024).

Legacy

The franchise has been widely praised for its social commentary, makeup effects by John Chambers (who received an honorary Academy Award), and its influence on science fiction cinema.
