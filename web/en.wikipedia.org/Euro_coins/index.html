Euro coins

Euro coins
Issued1 January 2002
Denominations1c, 2c, 5c, 10c, 20c, 50c, €1, €2
Common side designerLuc Luycx

Euro coins are issued in eight denominations, from one cent to two euros, and entered circulation on 1 January 2002. Every coin has a common European side, designed by Belgian artist Luc Luycx, showing a map of Europe, and a national side chosen by the issuing country, so dozens of designs circulate interchangeably across the euro area.

Denominations

The 1, 2 and 5 cent coins are copper-coated steel and show a globe highlighting Europe; the 10, 20 and 50 cent coins are made of a gold-coloured "Nordic gold" alloy and depict member states or the union; the €1 and €2 coins are bimetallic. The €2 coin's edge lettering is national. Some countries have effectively rounded cash payments to the nearest five cents, reducing use of the smallest coins.

Commemorative issues

Euro area states may issue commemorative €2 coins in limited numbers, celebrating events and anniversaries; several pan-eurozone commemorative coins have also been issued jointly, such as for the 50th anniversary of the Treaty of Rome. Microstates including Monaco, San Marino, Vatican City and Andorra mint their own euro coins under monetary agreements.
