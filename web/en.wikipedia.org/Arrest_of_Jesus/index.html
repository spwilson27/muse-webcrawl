Arrest of Jesus

The arrest of Jesus was a pivotal event in Christianity recorded in the four canonical gospels. It took place in the Garden of Gethsemane at night, shortly after the Last Supper, and led to Jesus's trial and crucifixion.

Gospel accounts

According to the gospels, Judas Iscariot led a party of armed men — in John's account a detachment of soldiers and officials — to the garden, where he identified Jesus with a kiss. In the synoptic accounts an ear of a servant of the high priest was cut off during the scuffle; John's gospel names the servant as Malchus and the swordsman as Simon Peter, and records Jesus healing or rebuking the violence. Jesus submitted to arrest, and his disciples fled.

Aftermath

Jesus was taken first to the house of the high priest Caiaphas for a night hearing before the Sanhedrin, then to Pontius Pilate the following morning. The scene, often called the Agony in the Garden in its preceding prayer, is a frequent subject in Christian art.
