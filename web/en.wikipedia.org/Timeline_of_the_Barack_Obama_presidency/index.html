Timeline of the Barack Obama presidency

This timeline of the Barack Obama presidency records the major events of the administration of Barack Obama, the 44th president of the United States, which began with his inauguration on 20 January 2009 and ended on 20 January 2017.

First term (2009–2013)

Key events included the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act stimulus package of 2009, the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act of 2010, the end of the Iraq War, the operation that killed Osama bin Laden in May 2011, and Obama's re-election in November 2012 over Mitt Romney.

Second term (2013–2017)

The second term included the Iran nuclear agreement (JCPOA) of 2015, restoration of diplomatic relations with Cuba, United States participation in the Paris climate agreement, the Supreme Court ruling legalising same-sex marriage nationwide in 2015, and continuing conflict with a Republican-controlled Congress over immigration and health care.
