Rogozen Treasure

The Rogozen Treasure (Bulgarian: Рогозенско съкровище) is a Thracian silver treasure discovered in 1985 near the village of Rogozen in Vratsa Province, northwestern Bulgaria. It is the largest Thracian treasure known, consisting of 165 silver vessels, many gilt, with a total weight of about 20 kilograms.

Discovery and contents

A tractor driver unearthed the first vessels while digging in his garden; archaeologists subsequently recovered the full hoard, buried in two groups. The collection includes phialai, jugs and goblets dating from the late 6th to the mid-4th century BC, decorated with mythological scenes, animals and geometric ornament. Inscriptions on some vessels name Thracian rulers and towns of the Triballi tribe. The treasure is exhibited at the Regional Historical Museum in Vratsa, with parts displayed in the National Historical Museum in Sofia.
