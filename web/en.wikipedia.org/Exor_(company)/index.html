Exor (company)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Exor N.V. is a Dutch-incorporated investment holding company controlled by Italy's Agnelli family. It is the successor to the family's earlier holding vehicles and took the name Exor in 2009; in 2016 it redomiciled from Turin to the Netherlands, while remaining listed on the Italian stock exchange in Milan (and later moving its primary listing to Euronext Amsterdam). Exor describes itself as a builder of companies, holding long-term stakes in firms it helps to develop.

Holdings

Exor's principal investments have included controlling or anchor stakes in the carmaker Stellantis (formed by the merger of Fiat Chrysler Automobiles and Groupe PSA), the agricultural and construction machinery maker CNH Industrial, the truck maker Iveco Group, the luxury sports-car manufacturer Ferrari, the football club Juventus, the publishing group GEDI (owner of La Repubblica and La Stampa), and a major stake in the reinsurance company PartnerRe, which it acquired in 2016 and sold to Covéa in 2022. It also holds a stake in The Economist Group and has invested in healthcare, luxury goods, and technology ventures.

The Agnelli family

The company is controlled through the family's holding partnerships by descendants of Giovanni Agnelli, who founded Fiat in Turin in 1899. John Elkann, great-great-grandson of the founder, has served as Exor's chairman and chief executive and is also chairman of Stellantis and Ferrari.
