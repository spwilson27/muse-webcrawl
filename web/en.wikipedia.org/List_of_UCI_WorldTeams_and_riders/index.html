List of UCI WorldTeams and riders

A UCI WorldTeam is the highest tier of professional road cycling teams registered with the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI). WorldTeams are guaranteed entry to all UCI WorldTour events, including the three Grand Tours. Recent seasons have featured 18 WorldTeams.
