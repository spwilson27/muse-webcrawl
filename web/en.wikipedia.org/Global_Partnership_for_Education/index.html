Global Partnership for Education

The Global Partnership for Education (GPE) is an international partnership and funding platform dedicated to strengthening education systems in lower-income countries. Founded in 2002 as the Education for All Fast Track Initiative and renamed in 2011, it brings together governments, donors, international organisations, civil society, teachers and the private sector.

GPE supports partner countries to develop education sector plans and provides grants for their implementation, with a focus on basic education, girls' education, and learning in fragile and conflict-affected states. Its board is chaired in recent years by former Tanzanian president Jakaya Kikwete, and its replenishment conferences have raised billions of dollars for education financing.
