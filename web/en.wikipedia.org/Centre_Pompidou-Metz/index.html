Centre Pompidou-Metz

Centre Pompidou-Metz
Location: Metz, France
Opened: 12 May 2010
Architect: Shigeru Ban

The Centre Pompidou-Metz is a museum of modern and contemporary art in Metz, France, and a satellite of the Centre Pompidou in Paris. Opened in 2010, it was the first decentralisation of a major French national museum to a regional city.

Architecture

Designed by Shigeru Ban and Jean de Gastines, the building is famed for its sweeping woven-timber roof, inspired by a Chinese bamboo hat, rising around a 77-metre spire. Beneath it are three stacked gallery tubes and a large nave, with no permanent collection of its own — exhibitions draw on the Paris museum's holdings.

Programme

Large thematic exhibitions have made it one of France's most visited museums outside Paris. See Centre Pompidou and Metz.
