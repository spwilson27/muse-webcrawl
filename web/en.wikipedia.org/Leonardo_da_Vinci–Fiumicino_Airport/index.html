Leonardo da Vinci–Fiumicino Airport

Leonardo da Vinci–Fiumicino Airport (IATA: FCO) is Italy largest international airport, serving Rome from the coastal town of Fiumicino some 35 kilometres southwest of the capital. It is the main hub of ITA Airways.

History

The airport opened in 1961, replacing the smaller Ciampino as Rome international gateway, and grew with Italian aviation through successive terminals. A rail link, the Leonardo Express, connects it to Roma Termini station in about half an hour.

Operations

Fiumicino handles tens of millions of passengers a year across its terminals, with long-haul routes to the Americas, Asia and Africa. It is named for the Renaissance artist and inventor Leonardo da Vinci, whose flying-machine studies feature in its branding.
