Battle Creek, Michigan

Battle Creek is a city in Calhoun County, Michigan, United States, at the confluence of the Kalamazoo and Battle Creek rivers. It is nicknamed "Cereal City."

History

Battle Creek became famous as the home of the Kellogg Company and Post Consumer Brands, founded around John Harvey Kellogg's sanitarium and the breakfast cereal industry pioneered by W. K. Kellogg and C. W. Post.

Economy

Food manufacturing remains important, alongside the Hart–Dole–Inouye Federal Center and other employers.

Culture

The city hosts the World's Longest Breakfast Table event and cereal heritage attractions.
