Second Battle of Swat

The Second Battle of Swat, also known as Operation Rah-e-Rast, was a Pakistani military offensive against Taliban militants in the Swat Valley of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, launched in May 2009 during the War in North-West Pakistan.

Background

An earlier operation in 2007–2008 had failed to dislodge the Tehrik-i-Taliban forces of Maulana Fazlullah, who imposed a harsh rule over the valley. After the collapse of a peace agreement in 2009, the Pakistan Army, supported by the Pakistan Air Force, moved to retake Mingora and the surrounding districts.

Outcome

By late May 2009 government forces had secured Mingora and most of Swat, though the offensive displaced around two million civilians, one of the largest internal displacements of the period. Most residents returned by the end of the summer, while Fazlullah escaped to Afghanistan.
