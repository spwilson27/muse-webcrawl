The International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN) is a global civil society coalition working to promote adherence to and implementation of the United Nations Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons. Launched in 2007, it grew to hundreds of partner organisations in over one hundred countries.

Treaty campaign

ICAN advocateds reframed nuclear weapons as a humanitarian issue, helping build support for the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons, adopted at the United Nations in July 2017 and entering into force in January 2021.

Nobel Peace Prize

In 2017 ICAN received the Nobel Peace Prize "for its work to draw attention to the catastrophic humanitarian consequences of any use of nuclear weapons and for its ground-breaking efforts to achieve a treaty-based prohibition of such weapons".
