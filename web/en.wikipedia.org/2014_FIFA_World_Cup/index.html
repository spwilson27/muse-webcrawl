The 2014 FIFA World Cup was the 20th edition of the FIFA World Cup, held in Brazil from 12 June to 13 July 2014. It was the second World Cup hosted by Brazil, after 1950. Thirty-two teams competed in twelve stadiums across the country.

Tournament

Defending champions Spain were eliminated in the group stage. The tournament is remembered above all for the semi-final in Belo Horizonte in which Germany defeated hosts Brazil 7–1, a result known in Brazil as the Mineirazo, echoing the trauma of 1950. Germany's Miroslav Klose became the World Cup's all-time leading scorer with 16 goals.

Final

At the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro on 13 July, Germany beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time, Mario Götze scoring the winning goal in the 113th minute. Germany became the first European team to win the World Cup in the Americas. The Netherlands finished third, defeating Brazil 3–0 in the play-off.
