Ames Research Center

Established1939
LocationMoffett Field, Mountain View, California
AgencyNASA
NamesakeJoseph Sweetman Ames

The Ames Research Center (ARC) is a major NASA research center at Moffett Field in California's Silicon Valley. Founded in 1939 as the second laboratory of the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA), it was named for Joseph Sweetman Ames, a physicist and NACA co-founder.

Research

Ames has led research in aeronautics, exploration technology, and science, including wind-tunnel testing, arc jet facilities for thermal protection systems, supercomputing (the Pleiades supercomputer), astrobiology, and small satellites. It managed the Pioneer and Kepler missions; the Kepler space telescope, overseen from Ames, discovered thousands of exoplanets. The landmark Hangar One, built in 1933 for the dirigible USS Macon, stands at Moffett Field.

NASA Research Park

Ames hosts the NASA Research Park, a shared-use research campus hosting technology companies and universities, and partners with firms in nearby Silicon Valley.
