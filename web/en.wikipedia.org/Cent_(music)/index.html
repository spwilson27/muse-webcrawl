Cent (music)

Cent
Unit: Musical interval
Definition: 1/100 of a semitone
Per octave: 1200 cents

A cent is a logarithmic unit of measure for musical intervals, equal to one hundredth of an equal-tempered semitone, or 1/1200 of an octave. It was proposed by Alexander John Ellis in the 19th century and is used to compare tuning systems and measure intonation precisely.

Calculation

The size in cents of an interval with frequency ratio r is 1200 × log2(r). A just perfect fifth (3:2) is about 702 cents, versus 700 cents in equal temperament — a difference trained listeners and tuners can detect with instruments, though a single cent is below normal pitch discrimination.

Uses

Cents are standard in ethnomusicology, electronic tuners and microtonal music. See Semitone and Equal temperament.
