Design for disassembly

Design for disassembly (DfD) is the process of designing products so that they can be easily taken apart at end of life for repair, remanufacturing, recycling or safe disposal of their components and materials.

Principles

Strategies include using reversible fasteners (screws rather than adhesives), standardised components, modular construction, minimal material variety and clear labelling of materials.

Context

DfD is part of design for the environment and the circular economy, and in architecture it envisions buildings whose layers can be separated and reused rather than demolished.
