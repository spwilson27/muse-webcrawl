Leofric, Earl of Mercia

Leofric (died 1057) was Earl of Mercia under kings Cnut, Edward the Confessor and their successors, one of the three great earls who dominated 11th-century England alongside Godwin of Wessex and Siward of Northumbria.

Life

Leofric held Mercia from the early 1030s until his death at Bromley in Staffordshire in 1057. He was a benefactor of monasteries, founding or enriching houses at Coventry, Much Wenlock and elsewhere, and generally a stabilising counterweight to the Godwin family.

Lady Godiva

His wife was Godgifu — Lady Godiva of legend, said to have ridden naked through Coventry to persuade Leofric to lift a heavy tax. The story appears in chronicles two centuries later; the historical couple jointly founded Coventry monastery in 1043.
