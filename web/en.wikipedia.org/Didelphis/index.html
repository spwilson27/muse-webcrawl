GenusDidelphis

Didelphis is a genus of New World marsupials in the order Didelphimorphia, commonly known as the large American opossums. The genus includes the Virginia opossum (D. virginiana), the only marsupial native to North America north of Mexico.

Species

Recognised species include the white-eared opossum (D. albiventris), the big-eared opossum (D. aurita), the Guianan white-eared opossum (D. imperfecta), the common opossum (D. marsupialis), the Andean white-eared opossum (D. pernigra), and the Virginia opossum (D. virginiana).

Characteristics

Members of the genus are robust, solitary, nocturnal omnivores with prehensile tails, opposable thumbs on the hind feet, and a well-developed marsupium in females. They are noted for playing dead (thanatosis) when threatened and for a low body temperature that makes them unlikely hosts for rabies.
