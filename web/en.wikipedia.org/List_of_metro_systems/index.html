List of metro systems

A metro (or rapid transit system) is an electric railway, usually underground or elevated, providing high-capacity urban transport on exclusive rights of way. The world's first metro was London's Metropolitan Railway, opened in 1863. By the 2020s, well over 200 cities in more than 60 countries operated metro systems. This list highlights the largest and historically most significant networks (figures approximate, from memory).

Definitions

Lists differ according to whether light rail, monorails, and suburban rail are counted. The UITP and most references require full grade separation, high frequency, and electric traction for a system to qualify as a metro.
