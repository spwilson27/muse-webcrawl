European Golden Shoe

The European Golden Shoe, also known as the European Golden Boot, is an award presented each season to the player with the most points in league matches among the top-division European leagues. The award was introduced in 1968 by the French magazine L'Équipe as the Soulier d'Or, and since the 1996–97 season it has been awarded by European Sports Media.

Scoring system

Since 1996–97, goals are weighted by league strength: goals scored in the top five European leagues (per UEFA coefficients) are multiplied by 2, goals in leagues ranked 6th to 22nd by 1.5, and goals in weaker leagues by 1. This system means the winner is not always the raw top scorer; prolific strikers in smaller leagues dominated the earlier unweighted era.
