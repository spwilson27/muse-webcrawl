Fishbourne is a village and civil parish on the Isle of Wight, England. It lies on the north-east coast of the island, about two miles south-east of Ryde, on the shore of Wootton Creek.

Ferry terminal

Fishbourne is best known as the island terminus of the Wightlink car ferry service to Portsmouth on the English mainland. The route across the Solent is one of the main gateways to the island for vehicles and passengers, and the terminal dominates the village's waterfront.

Features

The village is home to the Royal Victoria Yacht Club, founded in 1845, and to Quarr Abbey, a Benedictine monastery built in the early twentieth century near the ruins of the medieval Cistercian Quarr Abbey. Fishbourne's sheltered creek and wooded shoreline are noted for wildlife, including red squirrels.
