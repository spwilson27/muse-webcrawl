Doctor Sleep is a 2019 American supernatural horror film written, directed, and edited by Mike Flanagan. It is based on the 2013 novel of the same name by Stephen King and is a sequel to Stanley Kubrick's 1980 film The Shining, itself based on King's 1977 novel. The film stars Ewan McGregor as Dan Torrance, a man with psychic powers ("the shining") and a drinking problem, who struggles with childhood trauma from the events at the Overlook Hotel.

Plot

Years after the events of The Shining, Dan Torrance has become an alcoholic drifter suppressing his shining. After settling in Frazier, New Hampshire, and joining Alcoholics Anonymous, he works as a hospice orderly, using his gift to comfort dying patients — earning the nickname "Doctor Sleep". Meanwhile a cult of quasi-immortal beings called the True Knot, led by Rose the Hat (Rebecca Ferguson), feed on the "steam" released by children with the shining when they are tortured and killed. Dan forms a telepathic bond with Abra Stone (Kyliegh Curran), a young girl whose shining is exceptionally powerful, and the two confront the True Knot in a climax that returns Dan to the abandoned Overlook Hotel.

Production

Flanagan faced the task of reconciling King's novel sequel with Kubrick's film, which deviates significantly from King's source. With King's approval, the film functions as a sequel to both the novel and Kubrick's adaptation, recreating the Overlook Hotel sets in meticulous detail. A three-hour director's cut was released on home media in 2020.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews from critics, who praised its performances and emotional depth, though its box office gross of $72.4 million was considered disappointing against expectations. It has since been reassessed favorably by many horror audiences.
