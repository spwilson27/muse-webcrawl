Siberian High

Type: Anticyclone
Region: Northeastern Siberia
Season: Winter

The Siberian High (or Siberian anticyclone) is a massive semi-permanent area of high atmospheric pressure centred over northeastern Siberia during the winter months. It is one of the most important centres of action in Northern Hemisphere weather.

Formation

Intense radiational cooling of the snow-covered continental interior in autumn and winter produces a shallow dome of very cold, dense air. Central pressure often exceeds 1,040 hPa, among the highest recorded on Earth.

Effects

The high drives cold, dry continental air outward, shaping the East Asian winter monsoon and bringing severe cold waves to China, Korea and Japan. Blocked flow associated with it can also influence European winter weather. In summer the system disappears, replaced by low pressure over the heated continent.
