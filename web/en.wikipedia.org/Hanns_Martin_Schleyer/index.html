Hanns Martin Schleyer

Hanns Martin Schleyer (1 May 1915 – 18 October 1977) was a German business executive, employers' association leader, and former SS officer who was kidnapped and murdered by the Red Army Faction (RAF) during the German Autumn of 1977.

Career

Schleyer joined the Nazi Party and the SS in the 1930s. After the war he worked for Daimler-Benz and became president of the Confederation of German Employers' Associations and of the Federation of German Industries.

Kidnapping and death

On 5 September 1977 RAF members ambushed his car in Cologne, killing his driver and three police escorts, and held Schleyer hostage demanding the release of imprisoned RAF leaders. After the imprisoned leaders died in Stammheim Prison, Schleyer was shot; his body was found in Mulhouse, France. See also Red Army Faction and German Autumn.
