Freixenet (pronounced [fɾəʃəˈnɛt]) is a Spanish producer of cava and other sparkling and still wines, based in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia in Catalonia. Founded in 1914, it became one of the world's largest producers of traditional-method sparkling wine.

History

The company grew out of the union of two wine families, the Ferrers and the Salas, and took its name from the family estate La Freixeneda. Its best-known product, Carta Nevada, was launched in 1941, followed in 1974 by the distinctive black-bottled Cordón Negro, which drove the company's international expansion. In 2018 the German group Henkell (part of the Oetker Group) acquired a majority stake, creating the Henkell Freixenet group.

Products

Freixenet's cavas are made principally from the Macabeo, Xarel·lo, and Parellada grape varieties and are produced by the traditional method with a second fermentation in the bottle. The brand's Christmas television advertisements, featuring celebrities since the 1970s, are a well-known fixture of the Spanish festive season.
