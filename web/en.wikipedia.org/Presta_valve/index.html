Presta valve

The Presta valve (also called the Sclaverand valve or French valve) is a valve commonly found in high-pressure road-style bicycle inner tubes and on tubeless setups. It comprises a valve stem with a small external screw and a captive nut on the thread.

Description

The Presta valve is narrower (6 mm) than the Schrader valve (8 mm), allowing smaller holes in rims; this makes it suitable for narrow, high-pressure rims. Before inflation, the small lock nut at the tip must be unscrewed; the tip is then tapped briefly to free the seal. Presta-specific pumps or an adapter are required.

Comparison

Unlike Schrader valves, Prestavalves have no valve-core spring; internal pressure seals the valve.
