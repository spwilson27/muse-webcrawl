99942 Apophis is a near-Earth asteroid and potentially hazardous object with a diameter of about 370 metres (1,210 feet). It caused a brief period of concern in December 2004 when initial observations indicated a probability of up to 2.7% that it would hit Earth on 13 April 2029.

The 2029 approach

Additional observations ruled out an impact in 2029, but Apophis will pass extremely close: on 13 April 2029 it will come within about 31,000 km of Earth's surface, closer than geosynchronous satellites, and will be visible to the naked eye from parts of Europe, Africa, and Asia. Radar observations in March 2021 ruled out any impact risk for at least the next 100 years.

Exploration

NASA's OSIRIS-REx spacecraft, renamed OSIRIS-APEX, was retargeted after its Bennu sample return to rendezvous with Apophis shortly after the 2029 flyby and study how Earth's gravity affects the asteroid.
