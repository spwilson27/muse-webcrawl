1953 Ionian earthquake

Date12 August 1953
Magnitude7.2 Ms
EpicentreIonian Islands, Greece
Deaths476–800

The 1953 Ionian earthquake struck the southern Ionian Islands in Greece on 12 August 1953, with a magnitude of 7.2. It devastated the islands of Cephalonia (Kefalonia) and Zakynthos, levelling most buildings outside the northern peninsula of Kefalonia.

Damage and casualties

The earthquake and its foreshocks and aftershocks, beginning on 9 August, killed between roughly 476 and 800 people and injured thousands. The town of Sami and the capital Argostoli were almost completely destroyed, and fires broke out amid the rubble. On Zakynthos only two buildings in the town survived intact.

Aftermath

An international relief effort followed, with Britain, the United States, Norway, Sweden, and other countries sending aid. The islands were largely rebuilt in the following decade under strict anti-seismic building codes.
