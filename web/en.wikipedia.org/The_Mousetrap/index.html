The Mousetrap
Written by: Agatha Christie
Characters: Mollie Ralston, Giles Ralston, Det. Sgt. Trotter, Christopher Wren, Mrs. Boyle, Major Metcalf, Miss Casewell, Mr. Paravicini
Date premiered: 6 October 1952 (Nottingham)
Place premiered: Theatre Royal, Nottingham
Original language: English
Genre: Murder mystery / whodunit

The Mousetrap is a murder mystery play by Agatha Christie. It opened in London's West End in 1952 and has been running continuously since, making it the longest-running play in history, with well over 29,000 performances in London alone. The play is famous for its twist ending, which audiences are traditionally asked not to reveal after leaving the theatre.

Plot

At Monkswell Manor, a guest house run by Mollie and Giles Ralston, a group of guests is snowed in during a blizzard. News arrives of a murder in London connected to a childhood abuse case at a nearby farm. Detective Sergeant Trotter arrives on skis to warn that the killer may be among them; when one of the guests is murdered, the survivors realize the killer is already inside. Suspicion passes from guest to guest until the murderer's identity is revealed in the final scenes. By long-standing tradition, this article's source tradition asks readers to keep the ending secret.

History

The play began as a radio drama, Three Blind Mice, broadcast in 1947 as a birthday tribute to Queen Mary. Christie adapted it for the stage, renaming it after the play-within-a-play in Shakespeare's Hamlet. The production premiered at the Ambassadors Theatre in 1952 (after a pre-London tour) with Richard Attenborough and Sheila Sim, moved to St Martin's Theatre in 1974 without a break in its run, and passed its 25,000th performance in 2012. The run paused for the first time in 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic and reopened in May 2021.

Reception

Though early reviews were mixed, the play's longevity has made it a theatrical institution and a major tourist attraction. Christie, who initially expected it to run only a few months, gave the rights to her grandson Mathew Prichard as a ninth-birthday present.
