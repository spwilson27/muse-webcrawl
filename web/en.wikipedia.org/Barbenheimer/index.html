Barbenheimer

Barbenheimer is a portmanteau of Barbie and Oppenheimer, two American films released theatrically on the same day, July 21, 2023: Greta Gerwig's fantasy comedy Barbie and Christopher Nolan's biographical thriller Oppenheimer. The simultaneous release of two big-budget films with sharply contrasting tones became an Internet phenomenon and a major cultural and box-office event, with many audiences watching the two films as a double feature.

Phenomenon

The contrast between the two films — a colorful comedy about the Barbie doll and a somber biopic of J. Robert Oppenheimer, the "father of the atomic bomb" — inspired memes, merchandise, and fan art. Rather than cannibalizing each other's audiences, the two films appeared to boost one another.

Box office

Together the films grossed more than 2.4 billion dollars worldwide: Barbie became the highest-grossing film of 2023, while Oppenheimer became the highest-grossing biopic of all time at the time of release and went on to win the Academy Award for Best Picture.
