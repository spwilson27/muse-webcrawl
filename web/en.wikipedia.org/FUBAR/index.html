FUBAR

FUBAR is an acronym that originated in the United States Army during World War II. It commonly expands to "fucked up beyond all recognition" (or "beyond all repair"), and describes a situation or piece of equipment that is irreparably damaged or chaotic. A milder rendering, "fouled up beyond all recognition", appears in official contexts.

Origin and usage

The term belongs to a family of cynical military acronyms of the era, alongside SNAFU ("situation normal: all fucked up") and SUSFU. FUBAR spread from the armed forces into general slang and popular culture, and has been used as the title of films, comic books, and a Netflix action-comedy series starring Arnold Schwarzenegger.
