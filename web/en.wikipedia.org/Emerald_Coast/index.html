Emerald Coast

The Emerald Coast is an unofficial name for the coastal area in the US state of Florida on the Gulf of Mexico that stretches about 100 miles (160 km) through five counties, Escambia, Santa Rosa, Okaloosa, Walton, and Bay. It includes the beach communities of Pensacola Beach, Navarre Beach, Fort Walton Beach, Destin, and Panama City Beach, among others.

Name and tourism

The term "Emerald Coast", coined in 1983, refers to the emerald-green waters of the Gulf along its white quartz-sand beaches. The region is a major tourist destination, particularly popular with visitors from the southeastern United States, and is also home to large military installations including Eglin Air Force Base and Naval Air Station Pensacola.
