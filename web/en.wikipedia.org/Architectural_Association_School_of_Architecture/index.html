The Architectural Association School of Architecture (the AA) in London is the oldest independent school of architecture in the United Kingdom and one of the most prestigious and competitive in the world. It attracts students from more than 60 countries.

History

The Architectural Association was founded in 1847 by two articled pupils, Robert Kerr and Charles Gray, as a self-help association of draughtsmen rebelling against exploitative pupillage. It established its own school, which became known for avant-garde teaching and debate.

Influence

Notable alumni and teachers include Zaha Hadid, Rem Koolhaas, Richard Rogers, and members of Archigram. The school's unit system and its AA Publications and exhibitions have made it a centre of experimental architectural thought.
