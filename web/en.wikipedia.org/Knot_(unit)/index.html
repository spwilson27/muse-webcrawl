Knot (unit)

The knot (symbol: kn) is a unit of speed equal to one nautical mile per hour, exactly 1.852 km/h. It is used worldwide in meteorology and in maritime and air navigation.

Origins

The term derives from the practice of measuring a ship's speed with a log line knotted at regular intervals, thrown overboard and counted as it ran out over a fixed time measured by a sand glass.

Usage

Vessel and aircraft speeds are conventionally given in knots, as are tidal streams and wind speeds in marine forecasts. The knot is not an SI unit but is accepted for use with the SI.
