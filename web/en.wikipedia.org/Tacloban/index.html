Tacloban

Country: Philippines
Region: Eastern Visayas
Province: Leyte (geographically)
Status: Highly urbanized city
Airport: Daniel Z. Romualdez Airport

Tacloban, officially the City of Tacloban, is a highly urbanized city on the island of Leyte in the Eastern Visayas region of the Philippines. It is the regional centre of Eastern Visayas and the principal port on Leyte, situated at the head of Tacloban Bay near the San Juanico Strait, which separates Leyte from Samar.

History

Known in the colonial era as Kankabatok, the town grew as a trading port and became the capital of Leyte province. During the Second World War, Tacloban briefly served as the seat of the Philippine Commonwealth government after General Douglas MacArthur's forces landed on Leyte in October 1944.

Typhoon Haiyan

On 8 November 2013 Tacloban was devastated by Typhoon Haiyan (Yolanda), one of the strongest tropical cyclones ever recorded at landfall. The storm surge inundated much of the city and thousands of residents died, drawing a large international relief operation. The city has since rebuilt, with new housing, sea defences and a focus on disaster resilience; the annual visit of survivors and commemorations mark the anniversary.
