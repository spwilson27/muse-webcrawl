Thomas the Tank Engine is a fictional anthropomorphised steam locomotive created by the Reverend W. Awdry for The Railway Series books, first appearing as title character of the second volume in 1946. Thomas is a blue 0-6-0 tank engine, number 1 on the North Western Railway of the Island of Sodor, where he runs his own branch line from Knapford to Ffarquhar.

Character

Thomas is cheeky, eager, and sometimes overconfident, frequently getting into trouble before learning to be a "Really Useful Engine." His closest friends include Percy the Small Engine, Toby the Tram Engine, and his coaches Annie and Clarabel. The Fat Controller (Sir Topham Hatt) directs the railway's operations.

In other media

Thomas became the star of the television series Thomas & Friends (1984–2021), in which he was the central character of most episodes, and he is one of the most recognisable children's characters worldwide, anchoring a franchise of books, toys, films, and theme-park attractions.
