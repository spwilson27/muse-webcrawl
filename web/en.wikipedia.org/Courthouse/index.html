Courthouse

A courthouse is a building that is home to a local court of law and often the regional county government as well, although this is not the case in some larger cities. The term is common in North America.

United States

In the United States, county courthouses are often architecturally prominent, frequently built in Classical Revival, Neoclassical or Art Deco styles, and located on a central town square. They contain courtrooms, judges' chambers, clerk offices and sometimes a jail.

Other countries

Equivalent buildings are called courts, palaces of justice, or law courts in other jurisdictions.
