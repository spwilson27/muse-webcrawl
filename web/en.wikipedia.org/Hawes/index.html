Hawes is a market town and civil parish in North Yorkshire, England, at the head of Wensleydale in the Yorkshire Dales. It is one of the main settlements of the Yorkshire Dales National Park and a popular base for walkers; the Pennine Way passes through the town.

History and economy

Hawes received its market charter in 1699 and grew as a centre for the surrounding farming dale, with lead mining and hand-knitting also important historically. Today its best-known product is Wensleydale cheese, made at the Wensleydale Creamery, which rescued the local cheese-making tradition in the 1990s and now produces the cheese associated with the Wallace and Gromit films.

Attractions

The Dales Countryside Museum, housed in the former Hawes railway station on the Wensleydale Railway, displays the working life of the Dales. Nearby are Hardraw Force, one of England's highest single-drop waterfalls, and the Buttertubs Pass road to Swaledale.
