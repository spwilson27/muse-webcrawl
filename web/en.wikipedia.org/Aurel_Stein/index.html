Aurel Stein

Born: 26 November 1862, Budapest
Died: 28 October 1943, Kabul
Occupation: Archaeologist and explorer

Sir Marc Aurel Stein (1862–1943) was a Hungarian-born British archaeologist and explorer, famous for expeditions to Central Asia and western China along the Silk Road.

Expeditions

On his second expedition (1906–1908) Stein obtained thousands of manuscripts, paintings and artefacts from the Mogao Caves near Dunhuang, including the Diamond Sutra, the world's earliest dated printed book. The collections, now largely in the British Museum and British Library, remain controversial in China.

Later work

Stein also surveyed sites in Persia, Baluchistan and the Indus region, contributing to knowledge of Alexander the Great's campaigns and of Gandharan civilisation.
