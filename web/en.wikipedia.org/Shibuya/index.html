Shibuya

Shibuya (渋谷区, Shibuya-ku) is a special ward of Tokyo, Japan, and one of the capital's major commercial and entertainment districts. Centred on Shibuya Station, it is famous worldwide for the Shibuya Crossing, often called the world's busiest pedestrian scramble.

Landmarks

Outside the station's Hachikō exit stands the statue of Hachikō, the loyal Akita dog who waited daily for his deceased owner, now Tokyo's best-known meeting point. Youth fashion and culture concentrate in areas such as Center Gai, Shibuya 109, Harajuku and Omotesandō (both within the ward), and the more recent Miyashita Park and Shibuya Sky developments. The ward also contains Yoyogi Park, the Meiji Shrine (in neighbouring grounds), Ebisu, and Daikanyama.

Overview

As a special ward, Shibuya has a population of roughly 230,000 and hosts numerous technology companies, embassies, and the United Nations University. Major redevelopment around the station through the 2010s and 2020s added skyscrapers such as Shibuya Scramble Square.
