Norges Bank

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Norges Bank is the central bank of Norway. Founded on 14 June 1816, shortly after Norway's union with Sweden and constitution at Eidsvoll, it is responsible for monetary policy, financial stability, and the issue of the Norwegian krone. The bank is wholly owned by the Norwegian state.

Functions

The Executive Board sets the policy rate (the sight deposit rate) with the aim of keeping inflation close to the target of 2 percent over time, under the inflation-targeting regime adopted in 2001. Norges Bank also holds and manages Norway's foreign exchange reserves and promotes robust payment systems.

Investment management

Through Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM), the bank manages the Government Pension Fund Global — the Norwegian sovereign wealth fund built from petroleum revenues — which is one of the world's largest investment funds, holding stakes in thousands of companies worldwide. The fund's management is governed by a mandate from the Ministry of Finance and by ethical guidelines overseen by the Council on Ethics.
