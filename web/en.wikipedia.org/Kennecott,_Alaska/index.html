Kennecott, Alaska

Kennecott, also known as Kennicott and Kennecott Mines, is an abandoned mining camp in the Wrangell Mountains of Alaska, now within Wrangell–St. Elias National Park and Preserve. It was the centre of the Kennecott copper mines, among the richest copper deposits ever found.

After prospectors found extraordinarily rich chalcocite ore in 1900, the Kennecott Copper Corporation (financed by the Guggenheim and Morgan interests) built the camp and the Copper River and Northwestern Railway to Cordova. The mines operated from 1911 to 1938, producing over $200 million in copper before closure left a ghost town. The preserved 14-storey red mill building is a National Historic Landmark, accessible from McCarthy via the Kennicott Glacier area.
