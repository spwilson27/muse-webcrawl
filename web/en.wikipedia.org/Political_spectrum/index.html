Political spectrum

A political spectrum is a system of classifying political positions in relation to one another. The most familiar is the left–right spectrum, originating in the French Revolutionary assemblies, where supporters of the king sat on the right and supporters of revolution on the left.

Modern models add dimensions for economic and social issues, or use two-axis charts such as the Nolan Chart and Political Compass separating economic and authoritarian–libertarian axes. Positions commonly described include far-left, left, centre-left, centre, centre-right, right and far-right.
