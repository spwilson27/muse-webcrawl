FC Bayern Munich

Fußball-Club Bayern München e. V., commonly known as FC Bayern or Bayern Munich, is a German professional sports club based in Munich, Bavaria. It is best known for its professional men's association football team, which plays in the Bundesliga, the top tier of the German football league system. Bayern is the most successful club in German football and club football history, having won a record number of national titles and cups, including numerous Bundesliga championships and DFB-Pokals, and six European Cup/UEFA Champions League titles.

History

Bayern was founded in 1900 by members of a Munich gymnastics club. The club's golden era of the mid-1970s, led by Franz Beckenbauer, Gerd Müller, and Sepp Maier, won three consecutive European Cups (1974–1976). Bayern won further Champions League titles in 2001 and, in 2013 and 2020, completed continental trebles. The 2019–20 season under Hansi Flick culminated in a sextuple, matching FC Barcelona's achievement of 2009. Bayern has dominated the Bundesliga in the modern era, winning the title eleven consecutive times from 2013 to 2023 before Bayer Leverkusen ended the streak in 2024.

Honours

Bayern has won over 30 Bundesliga titles, 20 DFB-Pokals, six European Cup/Champions League titles, one UEFA Cup, one European Cup Winners' Cup, and two Intercontinental Cups plus two FIFA Club World Cups.

Club structure

Bayern is majority-owned by its members under German football's 50+1 rule; the professional football company, FC Bayern München AG, has minority stakes held by Adidas, Audi, and Allianz. With several hundred thousand members, it is among the largest sports clubs in the world by membership. The club also fields teams in basketball, handball, and other sports.

Rivalries

Bayern's main rivals are Borussia Dortmund (Der Klassiker), and locally 1860 Munich in the Munich derby; a historic rivalry also exists with 1. FC Nürnberg.
