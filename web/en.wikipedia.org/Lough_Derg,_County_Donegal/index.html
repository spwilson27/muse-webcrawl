Lough Derg, County Donegal

Lough Derg (Irish: Loch Dearg) is a small lake in County Donegal, Ireland, near the village of Pettigo and the border with County Fermanagh. It should not be confused with the much larger Lough Derg on the River Shannon.

The lake is famous for Station Island, site of St Patrick's Purgatory, a pilgrimage destination dating to the Middle Ages. Pilgrims undertake a three-day programme of fasting, prayer and a sleepless vigil, traditionally arriving by boat from the mainland shore. The pilgrimage season runs through the summer months and has drawn visitors from across Ireland and the wider Catholic world for centuries.
