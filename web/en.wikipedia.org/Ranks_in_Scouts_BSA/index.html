Ranks in Scouts BSA

The ranks in Scouts BSA are the advancement system of Scouts BSA, the flagship programme of Scouting America (formerly the Boy Scouts of America) for youth aged 11 to 17. There are seven ranks, earned in order: Scout, Tenderfoot, Second Class, First Class, Star, Life, and Eagle.

The ranks

Scout is the joining rank. Tenderfoot, Second Class, and First Class develop Scoutcraft skills, fitness, citizenship, and first aid; a Scout who earns First Class is considered to have mastered the basic Scouting skills. Star and Life require merit badges, leadership service, and service projects. Eagle Scout, the highest rank, requires 21 merit badges, sustained leadership, and the planning and completion of a significant community service project; only a small percentage of Scouts attain it.

History

The rank structure evolved from the original 1910 programme. Girls became eligible for the ranks when Scouts BSA opened to them in 2019, with the inaugural class of female Eagle Scouts recognised in 2021.
