Sanrio

Sanrio Company, Ltd. (株式会社サンリオ) is a Japanese entertainment company founded in 1960 by Shintaro Tsuji. It designs, licenses, and sells products focusing on the kawaii ("cute") segment of Japanese popular culture. Its most famous character is Hello Kitty, a cartoon cat created in 1974, who has become one of the highest-grossing media franchises in the world.

History

The company began as the Yamanashi Silk Company in 1960, selling silk and later rubber sandals decorated with flowers. Tsuji noticed that adding cute designs to products increased sales, and the firm pivoted to character merchandise, taking the name Sanrio in 1973. Hello Kitty debuted on a vinyl coin purse in 1975 and became a global phenomenon, later joined by characters such as My Melody, Keroppi, Badtz-maru, Cinnamoroll, Kuromi, PomPomPurin, and the lazy egg Gudetama (2013). The company also co-created the aggressive red panda Aggretsuko, popularized by Netflix animated series.

Business

Sanrio's business rests on character licensing across toys, stationery, apparel, video games, and food, as well as its own retail stores. It operates two theme parks in Japan — Sanrio Puroland in Tama, Tokyo, and Harmonyland in Ōita — and has subsidiaries and licensees worldwide. Its corporate philosophy is often summarized by the motto "Small gift, big smile," reflecting Tsuji's belief that small gifts foster friendship and communication.
