Nicolás Maduro Moros (born 23 November 1962) is a Venezuelan politician who has served as the president of Venezuela since 2013. A former bus driver and trade union leader, he rose through the ranks of the movement founded by Hugo Chávez, serving as Minister of Foreign Affairs from 2006 to 2013 and as vice president from 2012 to 2013. Following Chávez's death in March 2013, Maduro won a special presidential election by a narrow margin over Henrique Capriles.

Early life and career

Maduro was born in Caracas into a working-class family. He worked as a bus driver for the Caracas Metro and became active in the transport workers' union. He was a founding member of the Fifth Republic Movement and was elected to the National Assembly in 2000, later serving as its president. As foreign minister under Chávez he promoted the Bolivarian Alliance for the Peoples of Our America (ALBA) and closer ties with Cuba, Russia, China and Iran.

Presidency

Maduro's presidency has been dominated by a severe economic crisis marked by hyperinflation, shortages of food and medicine, the collapse of oil production, and the emigration of several million Venezuelans. His government has faced sustained domestic protests and international condemnation over human rights and the erosion of democratic institutions. The 2018 presidential election, boycotted by much of the opposition, was not recognized by many countries; in January 2019 National Assembly president Juan Guaidó declared himself interim president, triggering a prolonged political standoff that ended with the interim government dissolving in late 2022. Maduro won another term in the July 2024 election, whose official results were disputed by the opposition and several foreign governments.
