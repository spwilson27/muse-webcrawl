Tokyo Story

Tokyo Story
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Released: 1953
Starring: Chishū Ryū, Setsuko Hara

Tokyo Story (東京物語, Tōkyō Monogatari) is a 1953 Japanese drama film directed by Yasujirō Ozu. It tells of an elderly couple from the port town of Onomichi who travel to Tokyo to visit their adult children, only to find themselves gently but firmly set aside by their busy offspring.

Plot

Shūkichi and Tomi Hirayama visit their son, a doctor, and their daughter, who runs a hair salon; both plead work and pass their parents off to a noisy spa at Atami. The only warmth they receive comes from Noriko, the widow of their son who was killed in the war. After returning home, Tomi falls ill and dies, and the family briefly reunites before dispersing again, leaving the widowed father alone.

Reception

Shot in Ozu's signature low "tatami" camera style with minimal movement, the film was long considered too Japanese for export and was released in the West only in the 1970s. It is now routinely ranked among the greatest films ever made, topping the directors' poll in Sight and Sound's 2012 survey.
