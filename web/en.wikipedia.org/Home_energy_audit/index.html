Home energy audit

A home energy audit is an assessment of a home's energy use and efficiency, identifying how and where energy is consumed and what measures can reduce consumption and cost. In the United States, professional audits are often conducted under standards such as those of the Building Performance Institute or RESNET.

Procedure

Auditors inspect insulation levels, heating and cooling systems, lighting, and appliances, and commonly perform diagnostic tests. A blower door test depressurizes the house to locate air leaks, often combined with infrared thermography to reveal missing insulation and thermal bridges. Duct leakage testing may also be performed.

Recommendations

Typical recommendations include air sealing, adding insulation, upgrading to efficient heating and cooling equipment, installing LED lighting, and sealing ducts. A simplified do-it-yourself audit can identify obvious problems, checking insulation, looking for air leaks around windows and doors, and reviewing past energy bills.
