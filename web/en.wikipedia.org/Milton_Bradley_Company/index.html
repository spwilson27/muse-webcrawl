Milton Bradley Company

Founded1860, Springfield, Massachusetts
FounderMilton Bradley
FateAcquired by Hasbro (1984); brand retired 2009

The Milton Bradley Company (MB) was an American board game manufacturer established by Milton Bradley in Springfield, Massachusetts, in 1860. It became one of the world's best-known game companies before being acquired by Hasbro in 1984.

History

Bradley began in lithography; his first game, The Checkered Game of Life (1860), was a success. During the American Civil War the company produced game kits for soldiers. In the 20th century MB published classics such as Candy Land, Yahtzee, Twister, The Game of Life, Battleship, and Mouse Trap.

Later years

MB expanded into electronic games with Simon (1978) and the Microvision handheld console (1979). After the Hasbro acquisition it operated as a division until the Milton Bradley brand was discontinued in 2009, its games continuing under Hasbro.
