Gay

Gay is a term primarily referring to a homosexual person or to homosexuality. It is most often used to describe men who are attracted to other men, though it may also serve as an umbrella term for lesbian, gay, and bisexual (LGB) people. The term originally meant "carefree", "cheerful", or "bright and showy".

Etymology and history of usage

From the 17th century, "gay" acquired connotations of immorality and later of prostitution. By the early 20th century it was used in homosexual subcultures as a code word, and from the 1960s onward it became the preferred self-designation of the gay rights movement, displacing clinical terms such as "homosexual".

Usage

Style guides and organizations such as GLAAD generally recommend "gay" as an adjective (e.g., "gay people") rather than as a noun. In informal youth slang of the late 20th century, "gay" was also used pejoratively to mean "lame" or "rubbish", a usage widely criticized as homophobic.
