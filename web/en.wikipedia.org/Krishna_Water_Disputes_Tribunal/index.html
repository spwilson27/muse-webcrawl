Constituted1969 (KWDT I); 2004 (KWDT II)
Legal basisInter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956
Basin statesMaharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana
KWDT II chairmanJustice Brijesh Kumar

The Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal (KWDT) is a tribunal constituted by the Government of India under the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956, to adjudicate the sharing of the waters of the Krishna River and its tributaries among the riparian states of Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh (and, after 2014, Telangana).

First tribunal (KWDT I)

The first tribunal, chaired by Justice R. S. Bachawat, was constituted in 1969 and gave its award in 1973 (notified in 1976). It allocated the river's assessed 2,060 TMC of water at 75% dependability: 560 TMC to Maharashtra, 700 TMC to Karnataka and 800 TMC to Andhra Pradesh, with review possible after the year 2000.

Second tribunal (KWDT II)

The second tribunal under Justice Brijesh Kumar was constituted in 2004 and delivered its award on 30 December 2010, allocating 2,578 TMC at 65% dependability — 666 TMC to Maharashtra, 911 TMC to Karnataka and 1,001 TMC to Andhra Pradesh — and raising the height of the Almatti Dam. Following the creation of Telangana in 2014, the tribunal's terms were extended to apportion water between Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, a process that remained contentious, and a further tribunal (KWDT III) was announced by the Union government in 2023.
