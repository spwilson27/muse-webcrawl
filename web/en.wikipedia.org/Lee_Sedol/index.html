Lee Sedol (born 2 March 1983) is a South Korean former professional Go player ranked 9 dan. Widely regarded as one of the strongest and most creative players of his generation, he won 18 international titles, second only to Lee Chang-ho among Korean players.

AlphaGo match

In March 2016 Lee played a five-game match in Seoul against DeepMind's AlphaGo program, losing 1–4. His victory in game four — the only game a human won against AlphaGo in its mastery phase — featured a celebrated "divine move" (move 78) and is considered a landmark in both Go and artificial intelligence history.

Retirement

Lee retired from professional play in 2019, stating that AI had become an entity that could not be defeated. His career is the subject of the documentary AlphaGo (2017).
