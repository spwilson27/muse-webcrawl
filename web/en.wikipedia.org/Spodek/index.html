Spodek

Spodek (meaning "saucer" in Polish) is a multipurpose arena complex in Katowice, Poland, opened in 1971. Its distinctive flying-saucer-shaped main hall is one of the most recognisable buildings in Poland and an early example of a tensegrity-style roof structure of its scale.

Architecture

The arena was designed by Maciej Gintowt and Maciej Krasiński, with structural engineer Wacław Zalewski. The main hall is a truncated cone with an inclined roof held by a cable structure. Construction was controversial because of the risk of mining subsidence in the region; the building in fact survived a notable subsidence event in the 1970s. The complex also contains an ice rink, a gymnasium and a hotel.

Events

Spodek has hosted European and world championships in ice hockey, volleyball, basketball, handball and weightlifting — including matches of the 2014 Volleyball World Championship won by Poland — as well as concerts by major international artists. It is particularly known in esports as the long-time home of the Intel Extreme Masters Katowice tournaments, among the largest esports events in the world.
