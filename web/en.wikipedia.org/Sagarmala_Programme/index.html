Sagarmala Programme

The Sagarmala Programme is an initiative of the Government of India to promote port-led development along the country's roughly 7,500 km coastline and 14,500 km of potentially navigable waterways. Approved by the Union Cabinet in March 2015, it is administered by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.

Objectives

The programme aims to reduce the logistics cost of export–import and domestic trade by modernising existing ports, building new ports, improving port connectivity by road, rail and inland waterways, and promoting coastal shipping and industrial clusters near ports. Its components include port modernization, port connectivity enhancement, port-linked industrialization, and coastal community development.

Implementation

Sagarmala Development Company Limited was incorporated in 2016 to support implementation. Hundreds of projects were identified across India's 12 major ports and numerous non-major ports, with investment planned over several years.
