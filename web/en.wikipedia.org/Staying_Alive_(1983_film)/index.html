Staying Alive (1983 film)

Directed by: Sylvester Stallone
Starring: John Travolta, Cynthia Rhodes, Finola Hughes
Music by: Bee Gees
Release date: July 15, 1983
Box office: $127 million

Staying Alive is a 1983 American musical drama film and the sequel to Saturday Night Fever (1977). It was directed, co-produced and co-written by Sylvester Stallone, and stars John Travolta reprising his role as Tony Manero, a former disco king now working as a dance instructor and waiter in Manhattan while seeking success on Broadway.

Plot

Six years after the events of Saturday Night Fever, Tony Manero auditions for a Broadway production, "Satan's Alley", and becomes caught between his girlfriend Jackie, a dancer and singer, and the show's wealthy lead dancer Laura. Through ambition and persistence he works his way from a chorus place into the lead role.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing about $127 million worldwide, aided by a soundtrack featuring new songs by the Bee Gees including "The Woman in You". Critical reception was sharply negative, however; it has frequently appeared on lists of the worst sequels ever made, in marked contrast to the acclaim for its predecessor.
