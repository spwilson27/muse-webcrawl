Ancient astronauts

The ancient astronauts hypothesis, also called the ancient aliens hypothesis, is a pseudoscientific and pseudohistorical claim that extraterrestrial beings visited Earth in antiquity and prehistory and made contact with humans, influencing early cultures, religions, and technologies. Proponents suggest that monuments such as the Egyptian pyramids, the Nazca Lines, and Moai of Easter Island were built with extraterrestrial assistance or inspiration.

History of the idea

The idea has roots in early 20th-century speculation and was popularized by Erich von Däniken's 1968 bestseller Chariots of the Gods?, followed by works of Zecharia Sitchin and others. The television series Ancient Aliens (2010– ) brought the claims to a wide audience.

Criticism

Historians, archaeologists, and scientists reject the hypothesis as unsupported by evidence, noting that the monuments cited are well explained by documented human techniques and that the claims often rest on misinterpretation of artefacts and texts. Critics also argue the hypothesis can be dismissive of the ingenuity of ancient and Indigenous peoples.
