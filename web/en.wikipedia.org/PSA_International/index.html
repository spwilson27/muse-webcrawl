PSA International

PSA International Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based global port operator, one of the world's largest, and the owner of the flagship Port of Singapore terminals. It originated as the Port of Singapore Authority, established in 1964, and was corporatised as PSA Corporation in 1997; it is wholly owned by Temasek Holdings.

Operations

PSA operates dozens of terminals across Asia, Europe and the Americas, handling tens of millions of TEUs (twenty-foot equivalent units) of containers annually. In Singapore it runs the terminals at Tanjong Pagar, Kepong, Brani and Pasir Panjang, and is consolidating operations at the Tuas mega-port, which began opening in phases from 2022 and is intended to be the world's largest fully automated container terminal. The company also invests in inland terminals, logistics parks and digital supply-chain platforms.
