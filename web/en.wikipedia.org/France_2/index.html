France 2

France 2 is a French public national television channel. It is part of the state-owned France Télévisions group, along with France 3, France 4, and France 5. France 2 is the largest channel of the group and one of the most-watched television networks in France.

History

The channel began on 21 December 1963 as RTF Télévision 2, the second channel of French public broadcasting, and initially broadcast in black and white before becoming the first French channel to switch to colour in October 1967. It became part of the ORTF, then was separated in 1975 as Antenne 2. In September 1992, Antenne 2 was renamed France 2 as part of the reunification of public television under the France Télévision umbrella. Since 5 January 2009, France 2 has carried no commercial advertising after 8 p.m., a reform extended to a full advertising ban on public channels in later years.

Programming

France 2 broadcasts a generalist schedule of news (its flagship 13 Heures and 20 Heures bulletins), French drama series, films, entertainment shows, and major sporting events including the Tour de France and the French Open in shared arrangements. Its news programmes compete directly with those of the private channel TF1.
