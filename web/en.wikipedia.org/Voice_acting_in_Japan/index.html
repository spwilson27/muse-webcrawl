Voice acting in Japan

Voice acting in Japan is an industry in which actors provide voice-overs for a wide range of media, including anime, video games, audio dramas, dubbing of foreign films and television, and narration. Japanese voice actors, known as seiyū (声優), train at specialized schools and are usually represented by talent agencies.

Industry

The growth of anime from the 1970s onwards, and especially the "seiyū boom" of the 1990s, turned leading voice actors into public personalities with fan followings, music careers and live events. Recording for anime is typically done after animation is produced (a practice known as afureko), with the cast recording together in a studio. Competition for roles is intense, and many aspirants attend voice-acting academies or agency-run training schools. Notable agencies include Aoni Production and 81 Produce; the industry's performers are honoured at the annual Seiyu Awards.
