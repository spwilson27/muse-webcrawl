Pininfarina

Pininfarina S.p.A. is an Italian car design firm and coachbuilder based in Cambiano, near Turin. Founded in 1930 by Battista "Pinin" Farina, it is famous for long-standing design partnerships, above all with Ferrari, and for styling many of the most celebrated automobiles of the 20th century. Since 2015 the firm has been majority-owned by the Indian Mahindra Group.

History

Battista Farina founded Carrozzeria Pinin Farina in 1930 to build special car bodies. Post-war designs such as the Cisitalia 202 (1947) brought international acclaim. From 1952 the firm's association with Ferrari produced icons including the 250 GT, the Daytona, the Testarossa, and the F40. Sergio Pininfarina, the founder's son, led the company for decades and expanded it into engineering and contract manufacturing, building complete cars for clients including Cadillac, Fiat, and Alfa Romeo.

Design work

Beyond Ferrari, Pininfarina styled the Peugeot 504, the Alfa Romeo Spider, the Fiat Dino, and many Maseratis. The firm also designs trains, yachts, aircraft interiors, and consumer products. In 2018 the separate Automobili Pininfarina company launched the all-electric Battista hypercar, named for the founder.
