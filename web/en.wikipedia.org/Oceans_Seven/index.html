Oceans Seven

The Oceans Seven is a marathon swimming challenge consisting of seven open-water channel swims. It was devised in 2008 by Irish swimmer Stephen Redmond, and is considered the open-water swimming equivalent of the Seven Summits mountaineering challenge.

The seven swims

  • North Channel between Ireland and Scotland
  • Cook Strait between the North and South Islands of New Zealand
  • Molokai Channel between Oahu and Molokai in Hawaii
  • English Channel between England and France
  • Catalina Channel between Santa Catalina Island and Southern California
  • Tsugaru Strait between the Japanese islands of Honshu and Hokkaido
  • Strait of Gibraltar between Spain and Morocco

Swimmers

Stephen Redmond became the first person to complete all seven swims, finishing in 2012. Since then a small number of swimmers, including Anna-Carin Nordin, Michelle Macy, Darren Miller, Adam Walker, Kimberley Chambers, Antonio Argüelles, Ion Lazarenco, Rohan More, Abhejali Bernardová, and others, have completed the challenge, which demands years of preparation and tolerance of cold water, jellyfish, strong currents and shipping traffic.
