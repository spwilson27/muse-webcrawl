The Ministry of Economic Warfare (MEW) was a British government department formed on 3 September 1939, at the outbreak of the Second World War, to coordinate the economic blockade of Germany and the other Axis powers.

Role

The ministry administered the contraband control system, inspecting neutral shipping and issuing navicerts to approved cargoes, maintained blacklists of firms trading with the enemy, and negotiated war trade agreements with neutral countries to limit supplies reaching Germany. It gathered economic intelligence on the Axis war economy, assessing targets and blockade effectiveness.

Special Operations Executive

In July 1940 the ministry's minister, Hugh Dalton, established the Special Operations Executive (SOE) to conduct sabotage and support resistance in occupied Europe; SOE remained under the ministry's political direction even as it operated independently.

End of the war

The ministry was wound up in 1945 as hostilities ended, its remaining functions passing to the Foreign Office and Board of Trade.
