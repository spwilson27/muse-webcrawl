Factory acceptance test

A factory acceptance test (FAT) is a test conducted at the manufacturer's premises to verify that equipment or a system meets the requirements specified in the purchase contract before it is shipped to the purchaser's site. The test is typically witnessed by the customer or a third-party inspector.

Purpose

The FAT checks functionality, workmanship, and documentation, and is intended to catch defects while the supplier still has full facilities to correct them. A successful FAT is often a contractual milestone triggering shipment and payment.

Related tests

After installation, a site acceptance test (SAT) repeats or extends functional verification under actual site conditions. FAT procedures are common in process automation, power generation, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and defence procurement.
