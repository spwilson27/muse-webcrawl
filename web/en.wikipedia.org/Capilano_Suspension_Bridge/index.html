Capilano Suspension Bridge

Capilano Suspension Bridge
Location: North Vancouver, British Columbia
Length: 140 m
Height above river: 70 m
First built: 1889

The Capilano Suspension Bridge is a simple suspension bridge crossing the Capilano River in the District of North Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. The current bridge is 140 metres (460 ft) long and 70 metres (230 ft) above the river, and is one of Vancouver's most popular tourist attractions.

History

The bridge was originally built in 1889 by George Grant Mackay, a Scottish civil engineer, from hemp ropes and cedar planks. It was replaced with a wire cable bridge in 1903 and rebuilt in steel in 1956.

The park

The surrounding private park includes rainforest trails, totem poles, the Treetops Adventure canopy walkways, and the Cliffwalk opened in 2011.
