John Anthony Allan

John Anthony Allan (2 January 1937 – 15 April 2021) was a British geographer and water resource scientist, a professor at King's College London and at the School of Oriental and African Studies (SOAS), University of London. He is best known for developing the concept of virtual water.

Virtual water

Allan introduced the idea of virtual water in the early 1990s: the water embedded in the production of goods and services, which is effectively traded internationally when those goods are traded. He argued that water-scarce regions, notably the Middle East, had quietly solved their water deficits by importing virtual water in food — especially grain — rather than through war or megaprojects, an insight he called "the water is already flowing" through trade. He published widely on the political economy of water, including The Middle East Water Question (2000) and Virtual Water (2011).

Recognition

In 2008 Allan received the Stockholm Water Prize for the virtual water concept. He founded and advised water policy research groups at SOAS and King's and influenced international policy debates on water security and trade.
