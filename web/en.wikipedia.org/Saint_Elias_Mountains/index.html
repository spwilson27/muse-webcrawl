Saint Elias Mountains

Highest peakMount Saint Elias (5,489 m / 18,009 ft)
CountriesUnited States (Alaska), Canada (Yukon, British Columbia)
Protected areasWrangell–St. Elias and Kluane national parks; Glacier Bay

The Saint Elias Mountains are a mountain range spanning southeastern Alaska, southwestern Yukon and the far northwest of British Columbia. They form part of the Pacific Coast Ranges and include some of the highest coastal mountains on Earth.

Geography

Mount Saint Elias, at 5,489 metres, is the second-highest peak in both the United States and Canada, rising dramatically from tidewater at Icy Bay. The range also includes Mount Logan, Canada's highest mountain. Extensive ice fields and glaciers — among them the Malaspina and Hubbard glaciers — cover much of the range.

Protection

Together, Kluane, Wrangell–St. Elias, Glacier Bay and Tatlatui parks form a UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of the largest internationally protected wilderness areas in the world.
