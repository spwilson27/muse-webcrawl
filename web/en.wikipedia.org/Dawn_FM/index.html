Dawn FM is the fifth studio album by Canadian singer-songwriter the Weeknd, released on January 7, 2022, through XO and Republic Records. It is a concept album framed as a radio broadcast hosted by actor Jim Carrey, presenting a purgatory-like state in which the narrator awaits the light at the end of the tunnel.

Background

The Weeknd began teasing the album during the rollout of his previous record, After Hours (2020), describing it as a sonic universe. Production was handled primarily by the Weeknd, Oneohtrix Point Never, and Max Martin, with contributions from Calvin Harris and Swedish House Mafia.

Music and themes

Dawn FM draws on dance-pop, synth-pop, city pop, and funk influences from the 1980s. Its radio-station concept intersperses narration by Jim Carrey with tracks exploring regret, mortality, and transcendence. The cover art depicts an aged version of the Weeknd, reflecting the album's themes of aging and death.

Singles and reception

The album was supported by singles including "Take My Breath," "Sacrifice," "Out of Time," and "Less than Zero." Critics praised its cohesiveness and production. It debuted at number two on the US Billboard 200 and topped charts in several countries. An alternate version, Dawn FM (Alternate World), added remixes and additional tracks.
