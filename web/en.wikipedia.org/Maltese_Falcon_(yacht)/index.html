Maltese Falcon (yacht)

Launched2006
Length88 m (289 ft)
RigDynaRig, three masts, 15 sails
BuilderPerini Navi

Maltese Falcon is a full-rigged sailing yacht built by Perini Navi in Turkey and commissioned by American venture capitalist Tom Perkins. At 88 metres (289 ft), she was among the largest privately owned sailing yachts in the world when launched in 2006.

Design

The yacht uses the DynaRig system: three rotating carbon-fibre masts, each 58.6 m (192 ft) tall, carry 15 square sails totalling about 2,400 m² (25,800 sq ft) of sail area, which can be furled into the masts in minutes. The sails are controlled by computer, allowing the vessel to sail at speeds over 20 knots.

Ownership

Perkins sold the yacht in 2009; she has since been used for luxury charter under successive owners.
