List of eggplant cultivars

The eggplant (Solanum melongena), also called aubergine or brinjal, is grown worldwide in a wide range of cultivars differing in shape (globular, oval, elongated, snake-like), colour (purple, black, green, white, striped), and size. The following table lists well-known cultivars and regional types.
