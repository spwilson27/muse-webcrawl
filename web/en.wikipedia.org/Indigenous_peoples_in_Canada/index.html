Indigenous peoples in Canada

Indigenous peoples in Canada comprise the First Nations, Inuit, and Métis peoples. According to the 2021 census, there were about 1.8 million Indigenous people in Canada, roughly 5% of the population. Section 35 of the Constitution Act, 1982 recognizes and affirms their existing Aboriginal and treaty rights. Indigenous peoples have inhabited what is now Canada for at least 12,000 years.

Groups

First Nations is the term used for the Indigenous peoples who are neither Inuit nor Métis; there are more than 630 recognized First Nations governments or bands, speaking over 50 distinct languages. The Inuit are the Indigenous people of the Arctic, living in Inuit Nunangat, which comprises Nunavut, the Inuvialuit Settlement Region, Nunavik in northern Quebec, and Nunatsiavut in northern Labrador. The Métis are a distinct people of mixed First Nations and European ancestry whose ethnogenesis centred on the Red River region of Manitoba in the 18th and 19th centuries.

History

European contact from the 16th century onward brought trade, alliances, and eventually colonization. Between 1763 and 1923 the Crown signed a series of treaties with First Nations, many of which remain contested. The Indian Act of 1876 imposed a pervasive regime of federal control, and the residential school system removed generations of children from their families; the Truth and Reconciliation Commission (2008–2015) concluded that the schools constituted a policy of cultural genocide. The last federally run residential school closed in 1996. Key legal milestones include the Calder decision (1973), section 35 of the Constitution Act, 1982, the Delgamuukw decision (1997), and the creation of Nunavut in 1999.

Contemporary issues

Ongoing themes include land claims and self-government agreements, the crisis of missing and murdered Indigenous women and girls, gaps in health, housing, and clean water on reserves, and language revitalization. Canada formally adopted the UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples in 2016 and passed implementing legislation in 2021.
