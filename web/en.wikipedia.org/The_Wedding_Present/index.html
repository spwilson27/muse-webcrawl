The Wedding Present are a British indie rock group formed in 1985 in Leeds, England, from the ashes of The Lost Pandas. The band's music has evolved from fast-paced indie rock rooted in the C86 scene to more varied forms, and singer David Gedge remains its only constant member and chief songwriter.

History

Their debut album George Best (1987), named after the footballer, was followed by Bizarro (1989) and the Steve Albini-recorded Seamonsters (1991). In 1992 the band famously released a single every month, equalling Elvis Presley's record of twelve UK Top 30 hits in a year; the singles were compiled as the Hit Parade albums. After Watusi (1994) and Saturnalia (1996), Gedge put the band on hold and formed Cinerama. The Wedding Present returned in 2004 with Take Fountain, followed by albums including El Rey (2008), Valentina (2012), and Going, Going... (2016).
