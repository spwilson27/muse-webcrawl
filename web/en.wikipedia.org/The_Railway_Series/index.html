The Railway Series is a series of British children's books about railways on the fictional Island of Sodor. The first 26 books were written by the Reverend Wilbert Awdry between 1945 and 1972; a further 16 were written by his son Christopher Awdry between 1983 and 2011.

Content

The stories centre on anthropomorphic steam locomotives — most famously Thomas the Tank Engine — and their work on the North Western Railway under the Fat Controller (Sir Topham Hatt). Awdry, a railway enthusiast, strove for realism in operations, illustrations, and Sodor's geography and history.

Adaptations

The books were adapted into the television series Thomas the Tank Engine & Friends (from 1984), which became a worldwide children's franchise.
