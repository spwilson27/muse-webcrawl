Burl Ives

Burl Icle Ivanhoe Ives (June 14, 1909 – April 14, 1995) was an American folk singer, musician, actor and author. With his warm bass-baritone voice and genial presence, he became one of the best-known interpreters of traditional American folk songs and ballads.

Career

Ives began as an itinerant singer in the 1930s and launched his own radio show, The Wayfaring Stranger, in 1940. His recordings of songs such as "Blue Tail Fly", "Big Rock Candy Mountain" and "Froggie Went A-Courtin'" sold widely. He narrated and sang in the 1964 Christmas special Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer, voicing Sam the Snowman and singing "A Holly Jolly Christmas".

Acting

On screen Ives won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for The Big Country (1958) and played Big Daddy in the film of Cat on a Hot Tin Roof the same year. He originated the role of Big Daddy on Broadway as well.
