CentrePort Wellington

CentrePort Wellington is the port company that owns and operates the Port of Wellington in New Zealand. Located at the southern tip of the North Island on Wellington Harbour, it handles containers, bulk cargo, vehicles, and inter-island ferry traffic across Cook Strait.

History

The port developed from the Wellington harbour facilities established in the nineteenth century. Following port reform in New Zealand in the late 1980s, the port company was formed, with ownership held regionally. The 2016 Kaikōura earthquake caused significant damage to port infrastructure, requiring a major rebuild programme.

Facilities and operations

CentrePort operates container terminals, log and bulk berths, and the ferry terminals used by Cook Strait services linking Wellington with Picton in the South Island. It is a key node in New Zealand's domestic freight network because of its central location between the two main islands.
