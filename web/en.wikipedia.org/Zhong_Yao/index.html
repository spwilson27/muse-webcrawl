Zhong Yao

Zhong Yao (151–230 AD), courtesy name Yuanchang, was a Chinese calligrapher and politician of the late Eastern Han dynasty and the Cao Wei state during the Three Kingdoms period. He served under the warlord Cao Cao and the first Wei emperors, rising to the highest ministerial ranks.

Career

Zhong Yao played a key role in stabilizing the Guanzhong region for Cao Cao, persuading the warlord Ma Teng to submit and coordinating supplies during the northern campaigns. After the founding of Cao Wei in 220, he served as Chancellor and later Grand Chancellor, and he advocated the restoration of Han-era penal practices in famous court debates.

Calligraphy

Zhong Yao is celebrated as a master of calligraphy and is credited with a central role in the development of the regular script (kaishu), earning the honorific title "father of the standard script". His surviving attributed works, known through later copies and stone engravings, influenced calligraphers for centuries.
