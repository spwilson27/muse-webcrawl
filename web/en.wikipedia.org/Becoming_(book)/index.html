Becoming

AuthorMichelle Obama
Published13 November 2018
PublisherCrown Publishing Group
GenreMemoir

Becoming is the memoir of former First Lady of the United States Michelle Obama, published in 2018. It became one of the best-selling memoirs of all time, selling more than ten million copies within months of release and over seventeen million worldwide.

Contents

The book is divided into three sections — "Becoming Me," "Becoming Us," and "Becoming More" — tracing her childhood on Chicago's South Side, her education at Princeton and Harvard Law School, her meeting and marriage to Barack Obama, and her years in the White House.

Reception

Critics praised the memoir's candour and narrative warmth. The audiobook, narrated by Obama herself, won the Grammy Award for Best Spoken Word Album in 2020, and a companion Netflix documentary film appeared the same year.
