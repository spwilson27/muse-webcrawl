Opera Australia

Opera Australia
Founded: 1956 (as Australian Elizabethan Theatre Trust Opera Company)
Headquarters: The Opera Centre, Surry Hills, Sydney
Principal venue: Sydney Opera House

Opera Australia is the principal opera company in Australia. Based in Sydney, its performance season at the Sydney Opera House runs for approximately eight months of the year, with the remainder of its time spent at the Arts Centre Melbourne. It is one of the world's busiest opera companies, presenting hundreds of performances annually to audiences in capital city, metropolitan, regional, and interstate venues.

History

The company traces its origins to 1956, when the Australian Elizabethan Theatre Trust established an opera company that first toured with Mozart productions. In 1970 the company became the Australian Opera, and in 1996 it merged with the Victoria State Opera to form Opera Australia. The company's repertoire ranges from Baroque and bel canto works to standard repertory by Verdi, Puccini, and Wagner, as well as contemporary and Australian compositions.

Productions and outreach

Opera Australia is known for large-scale events such as Handa Opera on Sydney Harbour, an annual outdoor production on a floating stage, and its long-running production of La bohème. The company tours nationally, runs education programs, and maintains the Opera Australia Orchestra and chorus. Notable artistic directors have included Moffatt Oxenbould, Simone Young, and Lyndon Terracini.
