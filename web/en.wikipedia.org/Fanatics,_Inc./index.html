Fanatics, Inc.

Founded: 1995
Headquarters: Jacksonville, Florida, U.S.
Founder / CEO: Michael Rubin
Industry: Sports merchandise, trading cards, sports betting

Fanatics, Inc. is an American manufacturer and online retailer of licensed sportswear, sports collectibles, trading cards, and gambling products. Headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, it operates e-commerce sites and retail for most major North American leagues and hundreds of teams and colleges, and manufactures licensed apparel under vertical "v-commerce" model — designing, producing and selling directly to consumers.

History

The company began in 1995 as a Jacksonville sports retail business. Michael Rubin acquired it into his GSI Commerce group, selling GSI to eBay in 2011 while retaining and expanding Fanatics. It became the official merchandise partner of leagues including the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and NASCAR, and in 2020–2021 raised large funding rounds at valuations in the tens of billions. Fanatics acquired the Topps trading card business in 2022, securing exclusive future licensing rights with major leagues, bought the PointsBet U.S. sports-betting business, and acquired Italian retailer EPI. Its rapid expansion has drawn both praise for its vertical model and criticism over product quality and licensing monopolies.
