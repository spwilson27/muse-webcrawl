The EuroLeague is the top-tier European professional basketball club competition. Founded by FIBA in 1958 as the European Champions Cup, it was reorganised in 2000 when leading clubs broke away to form Euroleague Basketball, and since 2016 it has operated largely as a closed, round-robin league of licensed clubs.

Format

In recent seasons 18 teams have played a double round-robin regular season, followed by play-in games, best-of-five playoffs and a single-venue Final Four that decides the champion. The second-tier competition is the EuroCup.

History and clubs

Real Madrid is the most successful club in the competition's history, with eleven titles, followed by CSKA Moscow and Panathinaikos. Other multiple champions include Maccabi Tel Aviv, Olympiacos, Fenerbahçe and Anadolu Efes. The league's modern era has been marked by disputes with FIBA over control of European club basketball and by the commercial growth of clubs from Spain, Turkey, Greece and elsewhere.
