Netflix

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Netflix, Inc. is an American subscription video on-demand streaming service and production company based in Los Gatos, California. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, California, Netflix began as a DVD-by-mail rental service before launching streaming in 2007. It is among the world's largest entertainment companies, with well over 260 million paid memberships in more than 190 countries.

History

The company's original business model let subscribers rent DVDs by post with no late fees, directly challenging video rental chains such as Blockbuster. In 2007 Netflix introduced streaming media, and in 2011 it controversially attempted to split the DVD business off as "Qwikster", a plan abandoned after customer backlash. International expansion began with Canada in 2010 and reached most of the world by 2016.

Original programming

Netflix began producing original content in earnest with Lilyhammer (2012) and the acclaimed House of Cards and Orange Is the New Black (2013). Its original films and series — including Stranger Things, The Crown, Squid Game and Wednesday — have won numerous Academy Awards, Emmys and other honours. The release of whole seasons at once popularised "binge-watching" and reshaped television production and distribution.

Business

Netflix earns revenue chiefly from subscriptions, offering tiers that include an advertising-supported plan introduced in 2022. In the 2020s it moved to curb password sharing, expanded into mobile gaming, and experimented with live events. It competes with services from Disney, Amazon, Warner Bros. Discovery and others in the so-called streaming wars. See also streaming media and video on demand.
