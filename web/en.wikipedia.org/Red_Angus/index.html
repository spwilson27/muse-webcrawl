Red Angus

Country of originScotland / United States
UseBeef production
CoatSolid red

Red Angus is a breed of beef cattle derived from the Aberdeen Angus of Scotland, distinguished by its solid red coat rather than the black coat typical of Angus cattle. Red-coated calves occur naturally in Angus herds because the red colour is recessive, and breeders selected for the trait.

History

Although red animals had long appeared in Angus herds in Britain, breed organizations in some countries initially excluded them from registration. In the United States, the Red Angus Association of America was founded in 1954, making Red Angus one of the first breeds to emphasize performance records in registration.

Characteristics

Red Angus are naturally polled (hornless), noted for maternal ability, calving ease, fertility, and good carcass quality. The red coat is considered advantageous in hot climates, reflecting more sunlight and reducing heat stress compared with black cattle.
