Rhodes Must Fall

Rhodes Must Fall was a protest movement that began in March 2015 at the University of Cape Town, South Africa, demanding the removal of a statue of the British colonialist Cecil Rhodes from the campus. It broadened into a movement for the decolonisation of education and became a catalyst for related student protests, including the Fees Must Fall campaign.

Origins

On 9 March 2015 student Chumani Maxwele threw human waste at the Rhodes statue, sparking occupation of university offices and sustained protest. On 9 April 2015, following a university council vote, the statue was removed. The movement criticised institutional racism, the Eurocentric curriculum, and the lack of transformation more than two decades after apartheid.

Spread

The campaign inspired Rhodes Must Fall in Oxford, where students at the University of Oxford campaigned to remove a statue of Rhodes from Oriel College; the college initially declined, though debate continued for years. The movement influenced wider global debates about colonial monuments and memorials.
