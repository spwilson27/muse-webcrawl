Izaak Walton

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Izaak Walton (baptised 21 September 1593 – 15 December 1683) was an English writer. Known as the author of The Compleat Angler (1653), he also wrote a series of short biographies, published as Walton's Lives.

Life

Born at Stafford, Walton worked as an ironmonger in London and was a friend of the poet and clergyman John Donne, whose life he wrote. A Royalist during the English Civil War, he retired in later life to fishing on the River Dove and to Winchester, where he died.

The Compleat Angler

The Compleat Angler, or the Contemplative Man's Recreation is a celebration of the art and spirit of fishing in prose and verse, in the form of a dialogue. It has been reprinted hundreds of times and is one of the most enduring works of English literature. Walton's Lives — of Donne, Wotton, Hooker, Herbert, and Sanderson — are landmarks of English biography.
