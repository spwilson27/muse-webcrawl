Chinese yuan
Unit: Renminbi (RMB)
Symbol: ¥ / CN¥
Code: CNY
Issuer: People's Bank of China
Subunit: 1 yuan = 10 jiao = 100 fen

The Chinese yuan (yuán) is the principal unit of the renminbi, the official currency of the People's Republic of China. In common usage 'yuan' and 'renminbi' are often used interchangeably, though renminbi is the currency's name and yuan its primary unit.

History

The renminbi was first issued in 1948 by the People's Bank of China during the Chinese Civil War. Earlier yuan-denominated currencies circulated in the Qing dynasty and Republic of China, following the introduction of silver dollar-like coins in the 19th century.

Use and internationalisation

The yuan is managed against a basket of currencies. An offshore version trades in Hong Kong as CNH. In 2016 the renminbi joined the IMF's Special Drawing Rights basket. A digital version, the e-CNY, has been piloted since 2020.
