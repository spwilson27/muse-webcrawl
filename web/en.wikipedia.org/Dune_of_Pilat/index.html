Dune of Pilat

The Dune of Pilat (French: Dune du Pilat) is the tallest sand dune in Europe, located in La Teste-de-Buch in the Arcachon Bay area of southwestern France, on the Atlantic coast about 60 km from Bordeaux.

Description

The dune is roughly 2.7 to 2.9 km long and around 500–600 m wide, with a height that varies around 100–115 metres above sea level. It moves inland by a few metres per year, gradually engulfing the surrounding Landes forest. It faces the Banc d'Arguin at the entrance to Arcachon Bay and attracts more than a million visitors annually; it is a popular paragliding site.
