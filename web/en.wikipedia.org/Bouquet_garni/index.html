Bouquet garni

The bouquet garni (French for "garnished bouquet") is a bundle of herbs added to soups, stocks, and stews during cooking to impart flavor, and removed before serving. It is a classic component of French cuisine.

Composition

The traditional bouquet garni consists of parsley, thyme, and bay leaf. The herbs may be tied together with kitchen string, wrapped inside a leek leaf, or enclosed in a small cheesecloth sachet. Depending on the dish, additions can include basil, celery leaves, chervil, rosemary, tarragon, peppercorns, or a clove-studded onion in regional variations.

Use

The bouquet is simmered with the dish so the herbs release their aroma gradually, then lifted out at the end. A dried form, sometimes sold ready-made in mesh bags like a tea bag, is a common store-bought convenience. Similar herb bundles exist in other cuisines, such as the Italian mazzetto and the spice bags used in Chinese and Indian cooking.
