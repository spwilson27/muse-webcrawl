Field theory (mathematics)

Field theory is the branch of mathematics that studies fields — algebraic structures with addition, subtraction, multiplication and division — and their extensions.

Central topics include field extensions, splitting fields, algebraic and transcendental extensions, finite fields, and Galois theory, which links field extensions to groups of automorphisms and explains the solvability of polynomial equations.

Examples

The rational numbers, real numbers and complex numbers are fields; finite fields GF(p^n) underpin coding theory and cryptography.
