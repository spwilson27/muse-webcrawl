Destiny 2

Destiny 2 is a free-to-play online-only multiplayer first-person shooter video game developed by Bungie. It was originally released as a pay-to-play game in 2017 for PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and Microsoft Windows. The game is the sequel to 2014's Destiny and its expansions. Set in a "mythic science fiction" world, Destiny 2 features a multiplayer "shared-world" environment with elements of role-playing games.

Synopsis

The original campaign, the Red War, follows the Guardians after the Last City falls to Dominus Ghaul and his Red Legion, who sever the Guardians' connection to the Light. Subsequent expansions and seasons expanded the story to include the return of the Darkness, the Witness, and the conclusion of the "Light and Darkness Saga" in The Final Shape (2024).

Gameplay

Like the original, activities are divided into player versus environment and player versus player game types. Players create a Guardian of one of three classes — Titan, Hunter, or Warlock — and pursue story missions, strikes, dungeons, raids, and competitive Crucible matches, earning new weapons and armour.

Development and business changes

In January 2019, Bungie split from publisher Activision and began self-publishing the franchise. In October 2019 the game moved to a free-to-play model under the title New Light. Sony Interactive Entertainment acquired Bungie in 2022 for $3.6 billion. Major annual expansions after launch include Forsaken (2018), Shadowkeep (2019), Beyond Light (2020), The Witch Queen (2022), Lightfall (2023), and The Final Shape (2024).
