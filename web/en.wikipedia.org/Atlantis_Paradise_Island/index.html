Atlantis Paradise Island is an ocean-themed resort on Paradise Island, Nassau, in the Bahamas. It is built around the myth of the lost city of Atlantis and is one of the largest resort properties in the Caribbean.

Development

The site was first developed as a resort in the 1960s. South African developer Sol Kerzner acquired and transformed it, opening the Royal Towers in 1998 with the iconic Bridge Suite connecting the two towers — long marketed as one of the most expensive hotel suites in the world. Kerzner later sold the property to Brookfield Asset Management in 2011–2012.

Attractions

The resort features Aquaventure, a 154-acre waterscape with slides (including the Leap of Faith through a shark tank), river rides and pools; the Dig, an aquarium exhibit themed as the ruins of Atlantis containing tens of millions of gallons of marine habitat; Dolphin Cay; a casino; a marina village; golf; and dozens of restaurants. The Marine Habitat is among the largest open-air aquariums in the world.

In popular culture

The resort has hosted events such as the Miss Universe pageant and appears in films and television, including the James Bond film Casino Royale (2006).
