Muscle dysmorphia

Muscle dysmorphia is a subtype of body dysmorphic disorder in which a person becomes preoccupied with the idea that their body build is too small or insufficiently muscular. It is sometimes informally called "bigorexia" or reverse anorexia.

Characteristics

Affected individuals — most often men, particularly bodybuilders and gym users — may train compulsively, follow rigid diets, check mirrors repeatedly or avoid situations where their body might be seen, despite appearing muscular to others. Muscle dysmorphia is associated with elevated rates of anabolic steroid misuse, depression, anxiety and eating disorders.

Classification and treatment

The DSM-5 classifies muscle dysmorphia as a specifier of body dysmorphic disorder. Treatment typically involves cognitive behavioural therapy and, in some cases, selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors. Research attention has grown as fitness and social media culture have intensified body-image pressures.
