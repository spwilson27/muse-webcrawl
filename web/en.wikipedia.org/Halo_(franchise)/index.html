Halo (franchise)

Halo is a military science fiction media franchise, originally created by Bungie and now managed and developed by Halo Studios (formerly 343 Industries), part of Xbox Game Studios. The series centres on an interstellar war between humanity in the 26th century, led by the United Nations Space Command (UNSC), and an alliance of alien races known as the Covenant, later joined by threats such as the parasitic Flood and the machines of the ancient Forerunners.

Games

The franchise began with Halo: Combat Evolved (2001), a launch title for the Xbox that became a landmark first-person shooter and popularised the genre on consoles. Sequels Halo 2 (2004) and Halo 3 (2007) completed Bungie's original trilogy; Bungie also made the prequel Halo: Reach (2010) and the side story Halo 3: ODST (2009). After Bungie's departure, 343 Industries developed Halo 4 (2012), Halo 5: Guardians (2015) and Halo Infinite (2021). Spin-offs include the real-time strategy games Halo Wars (2009) and Halo Wars 2 (2017). The central protagonist of most mainline games is the super-soldier Master Chief (John-117), usually accompanied by the AI Cortana.

Other media

The franchise has expanded into dozens of novels (beginning with Eric Nylund's The Fall of Reach in 2001), comics, the animated anthology Halo Legends, web series such as Forward Unto Dawn, and a live-action television series that aired on Paramount+ from 2022 to 2024.

Reception and impact

The Halo games have sold more than 81 million copies worldwide, and the franchise has grossed billions of dollars across media. The series is credited with popularising online console multiplayer through Xbox Live, and its ringworld setting, music (by Martin O'Donnell and Michael Salvatori), and multiplayer design have been widely influential.
