Zombi 2 is a 1979 Italian zombie film directed by Lucio Fulci. Marketed in Italy as a sequel to George A. Romero's Dawn of the Dead (released there as Zombi), it is in fact an unrelated story: a group of travellers journeys to a Caribbean island where a mysterious disease is raising the dead. The film was released in English-speaking countries as Zombie, Zombie Flesh Eaters and Woodoo.

Plot

An apparently abandoned yacht drifts into New York Harbor carrying a zombie. The owner's daughter, Anne Bowles (Tisa Farrow), travels with reporter Peter West (Ian McCulloch) to the island of Matul in the Antilles, where her father's colleague Dr. Menard (Richard Johnson) is fighting an epidemic that revives the dead — including the long-buried Spanish conquistadors. The film climaxes with the survivors fleeing as the zombies march on New York.

Notable scenes and reception

The film is famous for two set pieces: a zombie fighting a live shark underwater, and the graphic "splinter in the eye" scene. Banned or heavily cut in several countries on release — it appeared on the British "video nasties" list — it has since been reassessed as one of the defining Italian horror films and the work that established Fulci's reputation as a master of gore.
