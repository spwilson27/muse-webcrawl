Boulder, Colorado

Boulder is a city in and the county seat of Boulder County, Colorado, United States. It lies at the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, about 25 miles northwest of Denver, at an elevation of roughly 5,430 feet. The population was about 105,000 at the 2020 census.

Landmarks and character

The city is known for the Flatirons, the slanted sandstone rock formations that rise just west of town, and for extensive protected open space surrounding it. Pearl Street Mall, a four-block pedestrian mall in the downtown historic district, is a central gathering place. Boulder is consistently ranked among the healthiest and most outdoor-oriented cities in the United States, with hundreds of miles of trails and a strong cycling culture; it hosts the annual Bolder Boulder 10K road race.

Economy and education

Boulder is home to the University of Colorado Boulder, the flagship campus of the University of Colorado system, founded in 1876. Federal laboratories, including NIST and NOAA facilities, and a significant technology and natural-products industry are major employers. The city is also known for its liberal politics and early environmental protections, including a voter-approved tax in 1967 to purchase open space — the first of its kind in the nation.
