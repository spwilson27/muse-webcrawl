Cockatrice

A cockatrice is a mythical beast, essentially a two-legged dragon or serpent-like creature with a rooster's head. It was described in medieval bestiaries and folklore, and is closely related to the basilisk.

Legend

According to legend, a cockatrice is hatched from a cockerel's egg incubated by a toad or serpent. Its glance, breath, or touch was said to be deadly, able to kill at a distance. Protection included carrying a weasel — its mortal enemy — or causing the creature to see its own reflection. The crowing of a rooster was also said to be fatal to it.

In heraldry and literature

The cockatrice appears in English heraldry and in literature from the early modern period onward. In modern fantasy games and books it typically retains the power to petrify or poison its victims.
