Aerial firefighting

Aerial firefighting is the use of aircraft to combat wildfires, dropping water or fire retardant and supporting ground crews with reconnaissance and transport.

Aircraft

Air tankers range from single-engine agricultural aircraft to converted airliners such as the DC-10 Air Tanker and the 747 Global SuperTanker of earlier years. Scooper aircraft like the Canadair CL-415 refill by skimming lakes, while helicopters use buckets or internal tanks.

Tactics

Retardant lines slow a fire's spread ahead of its front so ground crews can build containment lines; lead planes mark drop runs and bird-dog aircraft coordinate the air attack. See also Wildfire and Fire retardant.
