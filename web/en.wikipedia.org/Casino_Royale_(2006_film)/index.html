Casino Royale is a 2006 spy film, the twenty-first in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions, and the first to star Daniel Craig as the fictional MI6 agent James Bond. Directed by Martin Campbell and written by Neal Purvis, Robert Wade, and Paul Haggis, it is based on Ian Fleming's 1953 novel of the same name and reboots the franchise, showing Bond at the beginning of his career as a 00 agent.

Plot

Newly licensed to kill, Bond is sent to bankrupt Le Chiffre (Mads Mikkelsen), a banker to terrorists, by beating him in a high-stakes Texas hold 'em poker game at the Casino Royale in Montenegro. Bond is accompanied by Treasury agent Vesper Lynd (Eva Green), with whom he falls in love. After winning the game and surviving torture, Bond discovers Vesper has stolen the winnings to save her blackmailed lover; she drowns in Venice as a building collapses, and a hardened Bond tracks down the man behind the scheme.

Production and reception

The film was shot in the Czech Republic, the Bahamas, Italy, and the United Kingdom. It received widespread critical acclaim for Craig's performance and its grittier, more realistic tone, and grossed over $616 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing Bond film at the time. Its theme song, "You Know My Name," was performed by Chris Cornell.
