Stadio Olimpico

Stadio Olimpico
Location: Rome, Italy
Opened: 1911 (current form 1953)
Capacity: about 68,000
Tenants: Lazio, Roma, Italy national team

The Stadio Olimpico is the largest sports facility in Rome, Italy, located in the Foro Italico sports complex. It is the home stadium of Serie A clubs Lazio and Roma and the Italian national football team.

History

The stadium was rebuilt several times on the site; its current form dates from 1953 and it hosted the opening and closing ceremonies and athletics of the 1960 Summer Olympics. It has since hosted the 1990 FIFA World Cup final and multiple European Cup/Champions League finals.

Events

Beyond football, it hosts the Golden Gala athletics meeting and Italy's Six Nations rugby matches, plus major concerts.
