The Procter & Gamble Company (P&G) is an American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. It was founded in 1837 by William Procter, a candlemaker, and James Gamble, a soapmaker, who were brothers-in-law. Its products are sold in over 180 countries.

Brands

P&G's portfolio includes many household-name brands across fabric and home care, baby and family care, beauty, grooming, and health care, including Tide, Pampers, Ariel, Gillette, Head & Shoulders, Pantene, Crest, Oral-B, Olay, Always, Dawn, Fairy, and Mr. Clean.

History

The company grew supplying soap and candles to the Union Army during the American Civil War. In 1879 it launched Ivory soap, marketed as pure and floating. P&G became known for pioneering brand management in the 1930s and for sponsoring radio and television serial dramas ("soap operas"). In 2005 it acquired Gillette for $57 billion.
