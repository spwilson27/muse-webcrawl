Southern Lord Records

Southern Lord Records
Founded: 1998
Founder: Greg Anderson
Location: Los Angeles, California
Genres: doom metal, drone metal, sludge metal, hardcore punk, black metal

Southern Lord is an American independent record label based in Los Angeles, California, founded in 1998 by Greg Anderson, guitarist of Goatsnake and co-founder of the drone metal band Sunn O))). The label specialises in heavy music — doom metal, drone, sludge, stoner metal, hardcore punk and black metal — and is known for lavish vinyl packaging and limited editions.

Roster

Artists associated with the label include Earth, Boris, Electric Wizard (licensing), Sleep, High on Fire, Pelican, Wolves in the Throne Room, Burning Witch, Khanate, Om, Power Trip, and Anderson's own projects Sunn O))), Goatsnake and Engine Kid. Southern Lord was instrumental in the 2000s revival of doom and drone metal and in bringing Japanese band Boris to Western audiences.

Southern Lord Europe

The label also operated a European division and has organised tours and festivals presenting its roster. In 2017 the label released the comeback material of Power Trip–era crossover thrash acts alongside reissues of classic hardcore records, reflecting its twin roots in metal and punk.
