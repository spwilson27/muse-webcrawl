List of Tom and Jerry shorts

Tom and Jerry is an American animated series of comedy short films created in 1940 by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera for Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer, centred on the rivalry between the cat Tom and the mouse Jerry.

Hanna–Barbera era (1940–1958)

The original MGM run produced 114 shorts, beginning with "Puss Gets the Boot" (1940), in which the cat was named Jasper. Seven of the shorts won the Academy Award for Best Short Subject (Cartoons), including "The Yankee Doodle Mouse", "The Cat Concerto" and "Johann Mouse".

Later eras

Gene Deitch directed 13 shorts in Prague (1961–1962), and Chuck Jones produced 34 more (1963–1967). Dozens further shorts and films have followed under Warner Bros., including direct-to-video features and the 2021 live-action/animation film.
