McLaren F1 GTR

The McLaren F1 GTR is a racing variant of the McLaren F1 sports car, produced from 1995 to 1997. Originally reluctant to race the F1, designer Gordon Murray developed the GTR at customers' urging for the BPR Global GT Series.

Racing success

The GTR famously won the 1995 24 Hours of Le Mans outright on its debut, finishing first, third, fourth, fifth and thirteenth, defeating purpose-built prototypes. Powered by a BMW 6.1-litre V12 restricted to about 600 horsepower, it featured revised aerodynamics, a stripped interior, and racing suspension and brakes.

Variants

For 1996 the car was updated with lighter bodywork, and for 1997 the Longtail version lengthened the bodywork for greater downforce. Twenty-eight F1 GTRs were built in total, and several were later converted for road use.
