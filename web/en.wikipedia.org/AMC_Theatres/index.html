AMC Theatres

AMC Theatres
Industry: Entertainment (movie theaters)
Founded: 1920
Headquarters: Leawood, Kansas, United States

AMC Theatres (originally an abbreviation for American Multi-Cinema, often known simply as AMC) is an American movie theater chain headquartered in Leawood, Kansas. It is the largest movie theater chain in the world, operating roughly 900 theatres and more than 10,000 screens across the United States and Europe.

History

The company traces its origins to 1920, when Maurice, Edward, and Barney Dubinsky founded a small theater operation in Kansas City, Missouri. The Dubinsky brothers later changed their surname to Durwood, and the business became Durwood Theatres. Stanley Durwood, Edward's son, is credited with pioneering the multiplex concept: in 1963 he opened the Parkway Twin in Kansas City, among the first theaters with two screens under one roof, after realizing he could double revenue without doubling staff. The company was renamed American Multi-Cinema in 1968.

AMC went public in 1983. It introduced many industry firsts, including the cup-holder armrest and stadium seating in megaplexes such as the AMC Grand 24 in Dallas, opened in 1995. In 2012, the Chinese conglomerate Wanda Group acquired a controlling stake in AMC. In 2016 AMC expanded into Europe by acquiring Odeon & UCI Cinemas, and it also acquired Carmike Cinemas, then the fourth-largest U.S. chain, making AMC the largest theater chain in the United States.

Meme stock episode

In January 2021, AMC's stock became a prominent "meme stock" during a short squeeze driven by retail investors coordinating online, alongside GameStop. The company used the elevated share price to raise capital and reduce its debt load, helping it survive the COVID-19 pandemic, which had shuttered theaters worldwide in 2020.

Operations

AMC operates several brands, including AMC, AMC Dine-In, and AMC Classic, and premium formats including Dolby Cinema, IMAX, and its own AMC Prime. Its loyalty program, AMC Stubs, includes the A-List subscription tier launched in 2018, which allows members to see multiple movies per week for a monthly fee.
