Dumuzi

Dumuzi (Sumerian: "faithful son"), later known by the West Semitic name Tammuz, was a Mesopotamian god associated with shepherds and fertility, and the consort of the goddess Inanna (Ishtar). He is also listed in the Sumerian King List as a legendary antediluvian ruler of Bad-tibira and as an early king of Uruk.

Mythology

In the myth of Inanna's Descent into the Underworld, Dumuzi is delivered to the underworld as his wife's substitute, but is permitted to spend half of each year there, with his sister Geshtinanna taking his place for the other half — an early dying-and-rising god motif linked to the agricultural seasons. His death was mourned in annual lamentation rituals, and the Babylonian month Tammuz was named after him.
