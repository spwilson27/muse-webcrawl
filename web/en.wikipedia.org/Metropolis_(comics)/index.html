Metropolis (comics)

Metropolis is a fictional city appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, best known as the home of Superman. First named in Action Comics #16 in 1939, the city is depicted as a prosperous, futuristic East Coast metropolis, often modelled on New York City, and is located in the state of Delaware in many modern continuities.

Overview

Metropolis is home to the Daily Planet newspaper, where Clark Kent and Lois Lane work, and to LexCorp, the corporation of Superman's arch-enemy Lex Luthor. Famous locations include Suicide Slum, the Hob's Bay waterfront district, and the Science Spire. The city is frequently contrasted with Gotham City, the darker home of Batman.

In other media

The city has appeared in numerous adaptations, including the Superman film series, Lois & Clark, Smallville and the DC Extended Universe, where New York- and Chicago-inspired skylines have been used to represent it.
