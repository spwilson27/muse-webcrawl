Decommissioning

Decommissioning is the process of taking a facility, structure, vehicle, or system out of service and rendering it safe, typically at the end of its useful life. The term is most commonly used for nuclear installations, offshore oil and gas platforms, ships, and industrial plants.

Nuclear decommissioning

Nuclear decommissioning involves removing radioactive material, decontaminating structures, and dismantling a reactor or fuel facility so the site can be released for other uses. Strategies include immediate dismantling, deferred dismantling after a period of safe enclosure, and entombment. The process can take decades and is typically funded by levies collected during the plant's operating life.

Offshore oil and gas

When offshore fields are exhausted, operators are generally required to remove platforms and plug wells, though in some jurisdictions structures may be converted to artificial reefs under "rigs-to-reefs" programmes.

Ships and weapons

Naval vessels are decommissioned in a formal ceremony, struck from the naval register, and then scrapped, sunk as targets or reefs, or preserved as museum ships. The term is also applied to the retirement of weapons systems and to the end-of-life disposal of spacecraft.
