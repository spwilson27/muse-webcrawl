NTT

Nippon Telegraph and Telephone Corporation (日本電信電話株式会社), commonly known as NTT, is a Japanese telecommunications holding company headquartered in Tokyo. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world by revenue, and the Japanese government retains a stake of roughly one-third, as required by the NTT Law.

NTT was established in 1952 as a public corporation holding a monopoly on Japanese telephony, and was privatized in 1985. A major reorganization in 1999 split its domestic fixed-line operations between NTT East and NTT West, while NTT Docomo became the group's mobile arm — Japan's largest mobile operator — and NTT Data its systems integration business. In 2020 NTT took Docomo fully private in one of Japan's largest tender offers.

Research and business

Beyond telecom services, the NTT Group operates global data centres, IT services and network infrastructure through companies including NTT Ltd. and NTT Data. Its laboratories are known for long-running research in photonics, cryptography and communications, and the group has promoted its IOWN (Innovative Optical and Wireless Network) initiative as a next-generation infrastructure concept.
