Blue Jacket

Blue Jacket (c. 1743 – c. 1810), known in Shawnee as Weyapiersenwah, was a war chief of the Shawnee people, known for his leadership in the Northwest Indian War against the expanding United States in the Ohio Country.

Life

Blue Jacket rose to prominence during and after the American Revolutionary War, in which the Shawnee largely sided with the British. He was a leading figure in the Western Confederacy of Native nations and commanded warriors at the Battle of Fallen Timbers in 1794.

Later years

Following the defeat at Fallen Timbers and the Treaty of Greenville (1795), Blue Jacket advocated a more accommodationist stance toward the United States. A popular legend that he was a white captive named Marmaduke Van Swearingen is now regarded by historians as unfounded.
