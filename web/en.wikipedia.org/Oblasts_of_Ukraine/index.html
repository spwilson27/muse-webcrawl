Oblasts of Ukraine

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Administrative map of Ukraine's oblasts

An oblast (Ukrainian: область) in Ukraine is the primary administrative division of the country. Ukraine is subdivided into 24 oblasts, together with the Autonomous Republic of Crimea and two cities with special status, Kyiv and Sevastopol. Most oblasts are named after their administrative centres.

List

The 24 oblasts are: Cherkasy, Chernihiv, Chernivtsi, Dnipropetrovsk, Donetsk, Ivano-Frankivsk, Kharkiv, Kherson, Khmelnytskyi, Kirovohrad, Kyiv, Luhansk, Lviv, Mykolaiv, Odesa, Poltava, Rivne, Sumy, Ternopil, Vinnytsia, Volyn, Zakarpattia, Zaporizhzhia, and Zhytomyr. The largest by population include Dnipropetrovsk, Kharkiv, and Lviv oblasts; Kyiv Oblast surrounds but does not include the capital city.

Administration

Each oblast is governed by an oblast council (rada) elected by residents and by a state administration whose head is appointed by the President of Ukraine. Following decentralisation reforms begun in 2014, oblasts are divided into enlarged raions (districts), which in turn consist of hromadas (municipal communities).

Status of Crimea and the east

Russia annexed Crimea in 2014 and, following its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, claimed to annex Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson, and Zaporizhzhia oblasts after disputed referendums; these annexations are not recognised by Ukraine or most of the international community.
