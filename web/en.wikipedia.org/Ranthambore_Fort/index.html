Ranthambore Fort

LocationSawai Madhopur district, Rajasthan, India
Part ofHill Forts of Rajasthan (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
Builtc. 10th century onwards

Ranthambore Fort is a historic fortress in the Sawai Madhopur district of Rajasthan, India, standing on a steep hill within what is now Ranthambore National Park. It is one of the six Hill Forts of Rajasthan inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2013.

History

The fort's origins are attributed to the Chauhan dynasty around the 10th century. It was famously besieged by Alauddin Khalji in 1301, when its ruler Hammir Dev Chauhan died defending it, and later passed between the Delhi Sultanate, the rulers of Mewar and Bundi, the Mughals under Akbar, and the Maharajas of Jaipur. Within its walls are temples, palaces, stepwells, and cenotaphs.

Present day

The fort is a major tourist attraction, often combined with tiger safaris in the surrounding national park, and contains an important Ganesh temple visited by large numbers of pilgrims.
