Piaggio & C. SpA is an Italian motor vehicle manufacturer based in Pontedera, best known for the Vespa scooter. Founded by Rinaldo Piaggio in 1884, the company began in timber and railway equipment, moved into aircraft production, and after the Second World War created the vehicle that made it famous.

The Vespa

Designed by aeronautical engineer Corradino D'Ascanio and launched in 1946, the Vespa ("wasp") offered enclosed bodywork, a step-through frame, and small wheels, making motorized transport cheap and accessible in postwar Italy. It became a global cultural icon and has remained in continuous production in evolving forms.

Group and products

The Piaggio Group also owns Aprilia, Moto Guzzi, and Derbi, producing motorcycles, scooters, and light commercial vehicles such as the Ape three-wheeler and the Porter van. The company is listed on the Milan stock exchange and controlled by the Colaninno family's Immsi holding.
