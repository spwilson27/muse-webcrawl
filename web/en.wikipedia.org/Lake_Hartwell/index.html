Lake Hartwell is a man-made reservoir bordering Georgia and South Carolina on the Savannah, Tugaloo, and Seneca rivers. It is one of the largest reservoirs in the southeastern United States, built by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers between 1955 and 1963 as part of the Hartwell Dam project for flood control, hydropower, and recreation.

Geography

At full pool the lake covers about 56,000 acres with 962 miles of shoreline, extending up the Tugaloo and Seneca arms toward Clemson, South Carolina, whose stadium and campus sit on its shore. The lake is named for Nancy Hart, a Revolutionary War heroine of Georgia frontier lore.

Recreation

Lake Hartwell is a major recreation destination for boating, camping, swimming, and fishing — especially striped bass, largemouth bass, and crappie — and hosts major bass fishing tournaments, including the Bassmaster Classic.
