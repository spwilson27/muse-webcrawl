ChaCha20-Poly1305

ChaCha20-Poly1305 is an authenticated encryption with associated data (AEAD) algorithm combining the ChaCha20 stream cipher with the Poly1305 message authentication code. It was standardised in RFC 8439 and is widely used in protocols such as TLS 1.3 and WireGuard.

Construction

ChaCha20, designed by Daniel J. Bernstein, generates a keystream from a 256-bit key, a nonce, and a counter, using only addition, rotation, and XOR operations, making it fast in software without hardware AES acceleration. The first block of keystream derives a one-time Poly1305 key; the ciphertext and associated data are then authenticated with Poly1305.

Adoption

Google adopted ChaCha20-Poly1305 for TLS connections to mobile devices, and it is one of the three AEAD suites permitted in TLS 1.3. It is also the sole cipher of the WireGuard VPN protocol and is used in SSH and QUIC.
