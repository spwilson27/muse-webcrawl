Flevoland

Flevoland
Country: Netherlands
Capital: Lelystad
Largest city: Almere
Established: 1 January 1986
Area: 1,388 km² (land)

Flevoland is the youngest province of the Netherlands, established in 1986. It consists almost entirely of land reclaimed from the Zuiderzee, including the Noordoostpolder, Eastern Flevoland and Southern Flevoland polders.

History

The polders were created as part of the Zuiderzee Works: the Noordoostpolder (drained 1942) and the Flevopolder (completed 1968). Flevoland became the Netherlands' twelfth province on 1 January 1986.

Geography

The province lies below sea level and is surrounded by the IJsselmeer, Markermeer, and randmeren (border lakes). It is notable for the Oostvaardersplassen nature reserve.

Municipalities

Flevoland has six municipalities: Almere, Dronten, Lelystad, Noordoostpolder, Urk, and Zeewolde.
