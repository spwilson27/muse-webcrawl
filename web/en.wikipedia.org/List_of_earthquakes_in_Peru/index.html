List of earthquakes in Peru

Peru lies in a seismically active zone where the Nazca Plate subducts beneath the South American Plate. The country has suffered many destructive earthquakes, several of which generated tsunamis affecting its long Pacific coastline.

Risk and preparedness

Peru's National Institute of Civil Defense (INDECI) coordinates preparedness, and national earthquake drills are held annually.
