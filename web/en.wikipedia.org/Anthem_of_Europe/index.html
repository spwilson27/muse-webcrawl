Anthem of Europe

The Anthem of Europe is an anthem used by the Council of Europe and the European Union to represent Europe as a whole. It is based on the "Ode to Joy" movement from Beethoven's Ninth Symphony, composed in 1823, setting Friedrich Schiller's 1785 poem "Ode to Joy".

Adoption

The Council of Europe adopted the melody as its anthem in 1972, with an arrangement commissioned from Herbert von Karajan, who produced versions for piano, wind ensemble and orchestra. In 1985 it was adopted by the heads of state and government of the European Community as the official anthem of the European Union.

Lyrics and use

To avoid favouring any one European language, the anthem is officially instrumental only and has no fixed lyrics, although Schiller's German text is frequently sung. It is performed at official events of the EU and the Council of Europe, and it expresses the European ideals of freedom, peace and solidarity.
