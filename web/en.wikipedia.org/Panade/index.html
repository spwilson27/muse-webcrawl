Panade

A panade is a mixture of starch and liquid — commonly bread or breadcrumbs soaked in milk — used in cooking as a binder and to keep ground-meat preparations such as meatballs and meatloaf moist.

Use

When starch granules in the panade absorb liquid and gelatinise during cooking, they form a gel that traps moisture inside the meat and interferes with the cross-linking of meat proteins, yielding a tender texture. Panades are also the basis of some soups and stuffings in French and other European cuisines, and chocolate panade is a confectionery intermediate made from sugar syrup and cocoa mass.
