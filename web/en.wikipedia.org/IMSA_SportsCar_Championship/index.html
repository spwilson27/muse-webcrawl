The IMSA SportsCar Championship, currently known as the IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship under sponsorship, is a sports car racing series based in the United States and Canada and organized by the International Motor Sports Association (IMSA). It resulted from a merger between two existing North American sports car racing series, the American Le Mans Series and the Rolex Sports Car Series, and began with the 2014 season opener, the 24 Hours of Daytona.

History

On 5 September 2012 it was announced that the Grand-Am Road Racing sanctioning body would merge with the American Le Mans Series, unifying top-level North American sports car racing for the first time since the 1990s. The combined series debuted in January 2014 as the Tudor United SportsCar Championship, taking WeatherTech as title sponsor from 2016. The championship's marquee races — Daytona, Sebring, Watkins Glen and Petit Le Mans — also form the Michelin Endurance Cup.

Classes

  • GTP (Grand Touring Prototype) — the top class, from 2023 using LMDh hybrid prototypes built to rules shared with the FIA World Endurance Championship, alongside Acura, BMW, Cadillac, Lamborghini, and Porsche entries.
  • LMP2 — cost-capped Le Mans Prototype 2 cars for pro-am teams.
  • GTD Pro and GTD — GT3-specification grand touring cars, with GTD reserved for line-ups including amateur (Bronze/Silver) drivers.
