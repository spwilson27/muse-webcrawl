Pete Shelley

Pete Shelley (born Peter Campbell McNeish; 17 April 1955 – 6 December 2018) was an English singer, songwriter and guitarist, best known as the frontman of the punk band Buzzcocks, which he co-founded in Manchester in 1976 with Howard Devoto.

Pete Shelley
Born: 17 April 1955, Leigh, Lancashire
Died: 6 December 2018, Tallinn, Estonia
Occupation: singer-songwriter, guitarist
Associated act: Buzzcocks

Career

Buzzcocks pioneered pop-infused punk and the independent-label model with the self-released Spiral Scratch EP (1977). Shelley wrote most of their best-known songs, including "Ever Fallen in Love (With Someone You Shouldn't've)" (1978), "What Do I Get?" and "Everybody's Happy Nowadays". After the band split in 1981 he had a solo hit with "Homosapien" and worked in electronic music; Buzzcocks reformed in 1989 and toured for decades.

Legacy

Shelley's songs of adolescent longing and bisexual openness, delivered in a distinctive high tenor, influenced generations of punk and indie bands. He died of a suspected heart attack in Tallinn, where he had been living.
