Politics of Iraq

The politics of Iraq take place in the framework of a federal parliamentary republic established by the Constitution of 2005, adopted after the 2003 invasion that overthrew Saddam Hussein's Ba'ath regime.

The Council of Representatives elects the President, who nominates the Prime Minister. Power-sharing conventions allocate the premiership to a Shia Arab, the presidency to a Kurd, and the speakership to a Sunni Arab, reflecting Iraq's sectarian and ethnic composition. The Kurdistan Region enjoys substantial autonomy.
