The Open Group

The Open Group
Type: Consortium
Founded: 1996
Headquarters: San Francisco, California, US
Key people: Steve Nunn (CEO)
Website: opengroup.org

The Open Group is a global consortium that enables the achievement of business objectives through technology standards and open source initiatives by fostering a culture of collaboration, inclusivity, and mutual respect among its diverse membership of more than 900 organizations worldwide.

It is best known as the former owner of the UNIX trademark and for publishing the Single UNIX Specification, as well as for the TOGAF enterprise architecture framework.

History

The Open Group was formed in 1996 by the merger of X/Open and the Open Software Foundation (OSF). X/Open had been founded in 1984 by European computer manufacturers to promote open systems based on Unix. The OSF was founded in 1988 by US vendors including IBM, DEC and Hewlett-Packard as an alternative to AT&T and Sun Microsystems' Unix International.

Standards and certification

The Group owns the UNIX trademark and administers the Single UNIX Specification certification programme. Products certified as conforming include macOS, AIX, HP-UX and others. It also publishes POSIX-related standards inherited from earlier work.

Forums and frameworks

Major activities include TOGAF, ArchiMate, the Open Agile Architecture standard, the Future Airborne Capability Environment (FACE) Consortium, and work on security, semiconductors and sustainability.
