Food

Food is any substance consumed to provide nutritional support for an organism. Food is usually of plant, animal, or fungal origin and contains essential nutrients such as carbohydrates, fats, proteins, vitamins, and minerals, which are ingested and assimilated by the organism's cells for energy, growth, and maintenance of life.

Food sources

Most food derives from agriculture: cereals such as wheat, rice and maize supply most calories worldwide, alongside livestock, dairy, and fisheries. Fungi including mushrooms are also important foods.

Nutrients

Macronutrients — carbohydrates, proteins, and fats — provide energy, while micronutrients (vitamins and minerals) support metabolic functions. A varied diet is required to avoid nutritional deficiencies.

Cuisine and culture

Food is central to culture: cuisines reflect local ingredients, religion, trade, and history, from staple breads to festive dishes and global fast food.

Food safety

Food production, processing, and storage are regulated to prevent contamination and foodborne illness. The FAO and WHO maintain international standards through the Codex Alimentarius.
