Peroxisomal disorder

Peroxisomal disorders are a class of metabolic disorders in which peroxisome function is impaired. They are generally divided into peroxisome biogenesis disorders and single peroxisomal enzyme deficiencies.

Examples

The biogenesis disorders form the Zellweger spectrum, encompassing Zellweger syndrome, neonatal adrenoleukodystrophy, and infantile Refsum disease, caused by mutations in PEX genes. Single enzyme defects include X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy (defective ABCD1 transporter), adult Refsum disease, and rhizomelic chondrodysplasia punctata.

Features

Clinical features vary but often include developmental disability, hypotonia, seizures, liver dysfunction, and accumulation of very long chain fatty acids. Diagnosis uses biochemical testing of plasma fatty acids and genetic sequencing; treatment is largely supportive.
