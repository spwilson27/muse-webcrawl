Johannes Hendrikus Hubert "John" de Mol Jr. (born 24 April 1955) is a Dutch billionaire television producer and media mogul. He co-founded the production company Endemol and created reality television formats — most famously Big Brother and The Voice — that were sold around the world.

Career

De Mol, the son of singer John de Mol Sr., began in Dutch radio and television in the 1970s, founding his own production company in 1979. In 1994 he merged it with Joop van den Ende's company to form Endemol, which in 1999 launched Big Brother in the Netherlands; the format became a global phenomenon and redefined reality TV.

Later ventures

After Endemol was sold to Telefónica, de Mol founded Talpa Media. In 2010 he created The Voice of Holland, whose blind-audition format was franchised to dozens of countries, including the United States. He also launched the Dutch channel Talpa (later Tien) and acquired media businesses including the news agency ANP and broadcasters in the SBS group.

Personal life

De Mol is one of the wealthiest people in the Netherlands. His sister is the television presenter and actress Linda de Mol.
