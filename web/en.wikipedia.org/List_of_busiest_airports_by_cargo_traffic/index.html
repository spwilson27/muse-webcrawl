List of busiest airports by cargo traffic

The following is a list of the world's busiest airports by cargo traffic, ranked by total cargo (freight and mail, loaded and unloaded) in tonnes, based on Airports Council International statistics.

Hong Kong and Memphis have traded the top ranking over the past two decades; express integrator hubs (FedEx in Memphis, UPS in Louisville) rank unusually highly in cargo terms because of their dedicated freighter operations.
