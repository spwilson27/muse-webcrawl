Latent Dirichlet allocation

Latent Dirichlet allocation (LDA) is a Bayesian generative probabilistic model for collections of discrete data such as text corpora, introduced by David Blei, Andrew Ng and Michael Jordan in 2003. It models each document as a mixture of latent topics, and each topic as a distribution over words, with Dirichlet priors on the mixtures.

Use

LDA is widely used in topic modelling, information retrieval, recommender systems and bioinformatics to uncover hidden thematic structure. Inference is usually by variational Bayes or collapsed Gibbs sampling.

Model

For each document, a topic distribution θ is drawn from Dirichlet(α); for each word, a topic is sampled from θ and then a word from that topic's word distribution φ, itself drawn from Dirichlet(β).
