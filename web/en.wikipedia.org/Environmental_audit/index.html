Environmental audit

An environmental audit is a systematic evaluation of an organisation's environmental performance, compliance and management systems against legal requirements, standards or internal policies.

Types

Audits may assess regulatory compliance, waste and emissions, site contamination (as in property transactions), or the effectiveness of an environmental management system such as ISO 14001.

Process

A typical audit involves planning, document review, site inspection, interviews and sampling, followed by a report with corrective actions. Audits can be internal, conducted by regulators, or voluntary third-party assessments.
