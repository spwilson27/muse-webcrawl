Babar the Elephant

Babar the Elephant is a fictional character who first appeared in the French children's book Histoire de Babar (1931) by Jean de Brunhoff. Babar, a young elephant whose mother is killed by a hunter, travels to the city, learns civilised ways, and returns to become king of the elephants.

History

The story originated as a bedtime tale told by Cécile de Brunhoff to her sons; Jean de Brunhoff illustrated and published it. After his death in 1937, his son Laurent continued the series, which has grown to dozens of volumes translated worldwide.

Adaptations

Babar has appeared in animated television series (1989 and Babar and the Adventures of Badou, 2010), films, and Francis Poulenc's musical setting L'Histoire de Babar for narrator and piano.
