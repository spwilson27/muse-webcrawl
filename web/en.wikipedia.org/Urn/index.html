Urn

An urn is a vase with a tall, rounded body, often on a pedestal foot, typically with a lid. Urns have been used since antiquity for storing liquids and dry goods, for decoration, and—most famously—as containers for cremated remains, known as cinerary or cremation urns.

History

The ancient Greeks and Romans used urns for funerary purposes and for drawing lots in elections. In many cultures, burial urns were placed in tombs or columbaria. The word derives from the Latin urna, a water jar or voting vessel.

Types

Funerary urns hold ashes after cremation. Decorative garden urns are used as planters and ornaments. The tea urn and the large metal coffee urn are vessels for serving hot drinks. In probability theory, "urn models" describe random sampling experiments.
