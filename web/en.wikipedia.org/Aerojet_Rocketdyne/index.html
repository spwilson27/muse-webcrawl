Aerojet Rocketdyne

Aerojet Rocketdyne is an American manufacturer of rocket and hypersonic propulsion products, headquartered in El Segundo, California. It was formed in 2013 by the merger of Aerojet and Pratt & Whitney Rocketdyne, and was acquired by L3Harris Technologies in 2023.

Aerojet Rocketdyne
Founded: 2013 (merger)
Headquarters: El Segundo, California
Parent: L3Harris Technologies
Products: Rocket engines, missile propulsion

Products

The company's engines include the RS-25 (the Space Shuttle main engine, reused for the Space Launch System), the RL10 upper-stage engine, and the RS-68. It also produces propulsion for missiles such as the THAAD and Patriot systems and electric propulsion systems for spacecraft.

History

Aerojet traced its origins to 1936, when Caltech researchers including Theodore von Kármán founded a company to build jet-assisted takeoff rockets. Rocketdyne, formed as a division of North American Aviation, built engines for the Atlas, Saturn V (F-1 and J-2), and Space Shuttle programs. The combined company was purchased by L3Harris for about $4.7 billion in 2023.
