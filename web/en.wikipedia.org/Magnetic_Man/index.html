Magnetic Man

OriginCroydon, London, England
GenresDubstep, electronic
Years active2007–present
MembersSkream · Benga · Artwork

Magnetic Man is an English electronic music project and dubstep supergroup consisting of producers Skream (Oliver Jones), Benga (Adegbenga Adejumo) and Artwork (Arthur Smith). The three were central figures in the development of dubstep in Croydon, South London, and came together as a live act in the late 2000s.

Career

The group released their self-titled debut album, Magnetic Man, in October 2010 on Columbia Records. It reached number 5 on the UK Albums Chart and featured the singles "I Need Air", "Fire" (featuring Ms. Dynamite), "Perfect Stranger" (featuring Katy B), "Getting Nowhere" (featuring John Legend) and "Crossover" (featuring Katy B). The album helped bring dubstep from underground clubs to mainstream audiences.

Live performances

Magnetic Man's live shows were notable for their elaborate production, including a large cube-shaped LED rig, and the members performing behind laptops and controllers. They headlined festivals across Europe as dubstep peaked in popularity in the early 2010s.
