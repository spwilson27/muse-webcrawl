Underdrawing

Underdrawing is a preparatory drawing done on a painting ground before paint is applied. It can be used by the artist to plan the composition, place contours, and indicate areas of light and shadow before beginning to paint.

Materials and methods

Historical underdrawings were made with charcoal, metalpoint, ink, or diluted paint applied with a brush, sometimes transferred from a full-scale cartoon by pouncing or tracing. Artists frequently revised the drawing as they painted; such changes are called pentimenti.

Study

Art historians and conservators reveal underdrawings using infrared reflectography, which passes through many paint layers while being absorbed by carbon-based drawing materials. Study of underdrawings helps attribute works and reconstruct workshop practice.
