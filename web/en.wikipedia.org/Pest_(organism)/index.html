Pest (organism)

A pest is any organism that is harmful, damaging or troublesome to humans, their crops, livestock or property. The concept is anthropocentric: an organism is a pest because of where it occurs and what it does in a human context, not because of any intrinsic property. Pests include insects, mites, nematodes, fungi, weeds, rodents, birds and other animals.

Types of pests

Agricultural pests damage crops in the field and stored products in granaries; locusts, aphids, boll weevils and grain weevils are notorious examples. Household pests include cockroaches, bed bugs, termites and clothes moths. Disease vectors such as mosquitoes and ticks are pests because they transmit pathogens. Invasive species can become pests when introduced to new regions lacking natural enemies.

Control

Pest control methods range from chemical pesticides to biological control with natural predators and parasites, cultural practices such as crop rotation, traps and barriers, and genetic approaches. Integrated pest management combines these methods, using monitoring and economic thresholds to minimise pesticide use and its environmental side effects, including harm to pollinators and the evolution of pesticide resistance.
