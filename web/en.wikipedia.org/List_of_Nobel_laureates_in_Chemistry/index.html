List of Nobel laureates in Chemistry

The Nobel Prize in Chemistry is awarded annually by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences to scientists in the field of chemistry. It was established by the will of Alfred Nobel in 1895 and first awarded in 1901 to Jacobus Henricus van 't Hoff. The following is a selected list of laureates.
