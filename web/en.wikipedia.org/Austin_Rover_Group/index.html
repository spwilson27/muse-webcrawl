Austin Rover Group

Industry: Automotive
Founded: 1982
Defunct: 1989 (renamed Rover Group)
Parent: British Leyland / BL plc

The Austin Rover Group was a British motor manufacturer formed in 1982 as the mass-market car division of British Leyland. It produced cars under the Austin, Rover, MG and Morris marques.

History

Under chairman Michael Edwardes and later Graham Day, the group launched models including the Austin Metro, Maestro and Montego, and the Rover 200 and 800 series, the latter developed with Honda in a collaboration that shaped the company's 1980s range.

Renaming

In 1989 the group was renamed the Rover Group as the Austin badge was phased out, and in 1994 it was sold to BMW.
