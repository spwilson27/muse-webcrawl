Robert Besley
Born: 1794
Died: 1876
Occupation: type founder; Lord Mayor of London

Robert Besley (1794–1876) was an English type founder and politician who served as Lord Mayor of London.

Type founding

Besley was a partner in the Fann Street Foundry in London, one of the principal British type foundries of the 19th century. In 1842 the foundry issued what is often credited as the first Clarendon typeface, designed under Besley's direction; Clarendon — a bold slab-serif intended for display use alongside ordinary text type — became one of the most widely used display faces of the Victorian era and remains influential.

Public life

Besley served the City of London as a sheriff and alderman and became Lord Mayor of London in 1869. He is commemorated by the Besley memorial fountain in Postman's Park in the City.
