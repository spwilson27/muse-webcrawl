Electronic filter

Electronic filters are circuits that remove unwanted frequency components from an electrical signal, enhancing desired ones or reducing interference. They are fundamental to radio, audio and signal processing.

Categories

Low-pass filters pass frequencies below a cutoff and attenuate those above; high-pass filters do the reverse. Band-pass filters pass a range of frequencies, while band-stop (notch) filters reject a range. Filters may be passive, built from resistors, capacitors and inductors, or active, using amplifiers such as operational amplifiers.

Designs

Classic response types include Butterworth (maximally flat), Chebyshev (steeper roll-off with ripple) and Bessel (linear phase). Digital filters perform the same functions on sampled signals.

Applications

Filters are used in power supplies, radio receivers, crossover networks in loudspeakers, anti-aliasing in data converters and many other systems.
