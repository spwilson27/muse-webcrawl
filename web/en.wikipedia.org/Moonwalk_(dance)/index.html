Moonwalk (dance)

The moonwalk, also known as the backslide, is a popping dance move in which the performer glides backwards while their body actions suggest forward motion. It became a global sensation after Michael Jackson performed it during "Billie Jean" on the television special Motown 25: Yesterday, Today, Forever in 1983, and it became Jackson's signature move.

TypePopping / street dance move
Popularised byMichael Jackson (1983)
Earlier namesBackslide

Technique

The dancer shifts weight onto one leg with the heel raised, then slides the flat supporting foot backward while lowering the raised heel and raising the other, alternating legs to create the illusion of walking forward while travelling backward.

History

Similar sliding steps appear in the work of earlier performers, including Cab Calloway, Bill Bailey, Marcel Marceau's mime routines, and James Brown. Dancers and choreographers such as Jeffrey Daniel performed the backslide on television before Jackson, who learned and refined the move before his 1983 performance.
