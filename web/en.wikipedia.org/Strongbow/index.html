Strongbow
Type: Cider
Introduced: 1960
Maker: H. P. Bulmer / Heineken

Strongbow is a dry cider produced by H. P. Bulmer in Hereford, England, now owned by Heineken. Launched in 1960 and named after the Norman knight Richard de Clare, 2nd Earl of Pembroke, nicknamed "Strongbow", it became one of the world's best-selling cider brands.

Product

Strongbow is made from a blend of bittersweet cider apples and culinary apples, fermented to about 5% ABV in the UK version. Variants have included Strongbow Dark Fruit, Cloudy Apple and low-alcohol versions, sold in cans, bottles and on draught.

Marketing

Long associated with Herefordshire apple orchards, the brand has been a major sponsor of British sport and festivals. It is distinct from the Anglo-Norman historical figure Richard "Strongbow" de Clare, who led the Norman invasion of Ireland in 1170.
