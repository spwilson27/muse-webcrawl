The Conjuring is a 2013 American supernatural horror film directed by James Wan. It stars Vera Farmiga and Patrick Wilson as Ed and Lorraine Warren, paranormal investigators who help a family terrorised by a dark presence in their farmhouse.

Plot

In 1971, Roger and Carolyn Perron move with their five daughters into a farmhouse in Harrisville, Rhode Island, where they experience escalating paranormal disturbances. The Warrens investigate and discover the house's history of witchcraft and death, culminating in an unauthorised exorcism to save Carolyn from possession by the spirit of Bathsheba Sherman.

Reception and franchise

The film was a major critical and commercial success and launched the Conjuring Universe franchise, including sequels The Conjuring 2 (2016) and The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It (2021), and spin-offs such as Annabelle and The Nun.
