Vincenzo Bellini

Vincenzo Salvatore Carmelo Francesco Bellini (3 November 1801 – 23 September 1835) was an Italian opera composer, famed for his long-flowing melodic lines, which earned him the nickname "the Swan of Catania". His most famous works are Norma, La sonnambula and I puritani.

Career

Born in Catania, Sicily, into a musical family, Bellini studied in Naples. His breakthrough came with Il pirata (1827) at La Scala, Milan, beginning his collaboration with librettist Felice Romani. La sonnambula and Norma (both 1831) established him across Europe; Norma's aria "Casta diva" became one of the most celebrated soprano showpieces.

Bellini died in Puteaux, near Paris, aged 33, shortly after the triumph of I puritani (1835). Along with Rossini and Donizetti, he is a central figure of bel canto opera, and his influence on Chopin and Wagner was considerable.
