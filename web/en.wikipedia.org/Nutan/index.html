Nutan Samarth Bahl (4 June 1936 – 21 February 1991), known mononymously as Nutan, was an Indian actress who worked in Hindi films. One of the finest actresses in the history of Indian cinema, she appeared in more than 70 films over a career spanning four decades, and held the record for the most Filmfare Best Actress Awards (five) for nearly thirty years.

Career

The daughter of actress Shobhna Samarth and director Kumarsen Samarth, Nutan made her debut as a teenager and won the Miss India title in 1952. Her breakthrough came with Seema (1955), which earned her first Filmfare Award. She won further Best Actress awards for Sujata (1959), Bandini (1963) — widely regarded as one of her greatest performances — Milan (1967), and Main Tulsi Tere Aangan Ki (1978). Her other celebrated films include Anari (1959), Chhalia (1960), Tere Ghar Ke Samne (1963) with Dev Anand, and Saraswatichandra (1968). In later years she moved to strong character roles in films such as Meri Jung (1985), for which she won a Filmfare Best Supporting Actress Award, and Karma (1986). She was awarded the Padma Shri in 1974.

Personal life and death

Nutan married naval officer Rajnish Bahl in 1959; their son Mohnish Bahl became an actor, and her granddaughter Pranutan Bahl later debuted in films. Nutan died of breast cancer in February 1991, aged 54, and remains a benchmark for naturalistic acting in Hindi cinema.
