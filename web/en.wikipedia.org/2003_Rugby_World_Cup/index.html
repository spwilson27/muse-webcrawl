The 2003 Rugby World Cup was the fifth Rugby World Cup, the quadrennial international championship of rugby union. It was hosted by Australia and contested by 20 national teams from 10 October to 22 November 2003.

Final

England defeated the defending champions and hosts Australia 20–17 in the final at Stadium Australia in Sydney. With the scores level in the final minute of extra time, fly-half Jonny Wilkinson kicked a drop goal to win the match and give England its first Rugby World Cup, making it the first northern-hemisphere team to win the tournament.

Tournament

The 20 teams were divided into four pools of five. New Zealand, France and South Africa were among the other semi-finalists and quarter-finalists. England's captain was Martin Johnson and the coach was Clive Woodward. The tournament set attendance records, with nearly two million spectators across the 48 matches.
