Acute (medicine)

In medicine, acute describes a disease or condition of rapid onset and/or a short course, in contrast to a chronic condition, which develops slowly and lasts a long time. The term can also imply a condition that is severe or sudden in its presentation.

Usage

"Acute" forms part of the name of many conditions, such as acute respiratory distress syndrome, acute myocardial infarction and acute lymphoblastic leukaemia. It does not by itself indicate severity: an acute condition may be mild, while chronic conditions can be life-threatening.

Related terms

A subacute condition falls between acute and chronic in onset and duration. An acute exacerbation is a sudden worsening of a chronic condition, as in an asthma attack in a person with chronic asthma.
