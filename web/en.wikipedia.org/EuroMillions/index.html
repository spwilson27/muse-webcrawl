EuroMillions

First draw: 13 February 2004
Format: 5 numbers from 1–50 plus 2 "Lucky Stars" from 1–12
Participating countries: Austria, Belgium, France, Ireland, Luxembourg, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland, United Kingdom

EuroMillions is a transnational lottery that requires seven correct numbers to win the jackpot. It was launched on 7 February 2004 by France's Française des Jeux, Spain's Loterías y Apuestas del Estado, and the United Kingdom's Camelot Group. The first draw was held on 13 February 2004 in Paris. Initially only the United Kingdom, France, and Spain participated, but lotteries from additional European countries joined shortly thereafter.

Play and prizes

Players pick five main numbers from 1 to 50 and two different "Lucky Star" numbers from 1 to 12. Draws take place every Tuesday and Friday evening in Paris. Matching all five main numbers and both Lucky Stars wins the jackpot, which starts at €17 million and rolls over when unwon, up to a cap that has risen over time to €240 million. There are thirteen prize tiers in total.

Records

EuroMillions is known for some of the largest lottery prizes in European history, with record jackpots reaching the €240 million cap. Superdraws, in which the jackpot is boosted to a guaranteed amount, are held periodically.
