Political prisoner

A political prisoner is a person imprisoned for their political beliefs, activities or identity, or where political motives underlie the prosecution. Definitions vary; Amnesty International uses the related category “prisoner of conscience” for those jailed for beliefs who have not used or advocated violence.

Famous political prisoners have included Nelson Mandela in apartheid South Africa, Václav Havel in Czechoslovakia and Aung San Suu Kyi in Myanmar. Human rights organizations monitor such cases and campaign for release.
