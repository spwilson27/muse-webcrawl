Quantico is a town in Prince William County, Virginia, United States, located on the Potomac River near the unincorporated community of Quantico Base. The town is almost completely surrounded by Marine Corps Base Quantico, one of the largest United States Marine Corps installations in the world. As such, the town's life is closely tied to the base, and access to it is through base grounds.

History

The area was settled in colonial times along Quantico Creek, a navigable tributary of the Potomac, and the town developed as a small shipping and fishing village. In 1917, during World War I, the Marine Corps leased land near the town and established Marine Barracks, Quantico — later Marine Corps Base Quantico. The town was incorporated in 1927. The construction of Interstate 95 and the growth of the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area have shaped the modern community.

Quantico as a name

The name "Quantico" is widely associated with the federal and military installations nearby: Marine Corps Base Quantico hosts the FBI Academy, the FBI Laboratory, the DEA Training Academy, the Marine Corps University, and The Basic School for Marine officers, as well as the headquarters of Marine Corps Combat Development Command. The television series Quantico (2015–2018) took its name from the FBI Academy there.

Geography and transport

Quantico lies in northern Virginia, roughly 35 miles southwest of Washington, D.C. The town is served by the Quantico station on Amtrak's Northeast Regional and Virginia Railway Express's Fredericksburg Line, one of the few stations located within a military installation's grounds.
