Environmental anthropology

Environmental anthropology is a subfield of anthropology studying the relationships between human cultures and their environments, past and present.

Development

It grew from cultural ecology, associated with Julian Steward, and from work on how societies adapt to ecosystems. Later approaches include political ecology, which examines power and inequality in resource access, and studies of traditional ecological knowledge.

Contemporary topics

Environmental anthropologists work on climate change adaptation, conservation and its effects on local communities, environmental justice, disasters and sustainability.
