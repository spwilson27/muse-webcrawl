World Environment Day

World Environment Day
Date5 June
Established1972 (first observed 1974)
OrganiserUnited Nations Environment Programme

World Environment Day is celebrated annually on 5 June and is the United Nations' principal vehicle for encouraging awareness and action for the protection of the environment. It was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972, coinciding with the Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment, and first observed in 1974.

Celebrations

Each year a theme and host country are chosen; recent themes have addressed air pollution, ecosystem restoration, and plastic pollution. Activities include clean-up campaigns, tree planting, conferences, and public pledges by governments and businesses.

Impact

The day is observed in more than 150 countries and is used by the United Nations Environment Programme to launch major reports and global campaigns.
