The United Nations Association of the United States of America (UNA-USA) is a nonprofit membership organisation dedicated to building public understanding of and support for the work of the United Nations among people in the United States. It is part of the United Nations Foundation and is affiliated with the World Federation of United Nations Associations. The association operates through a network of local chapters and campus chapters across the country.

History

UNA-USA traces its origins to the American Association for the United Nations, founded in 1943 to mobilise American public support for the creation of a postwar international organisation. Eleanor Roosevelt was among its leading figures and served on its board, travelling widely to promote the new United Nations after its founding in 1945. The organisation took its present name following a merger in 1964. In 2011, UNA-USA became part of the United Nations Foundation, founded by Ted Turner in 1998 with a $1 billion gift to support UN causes.

Programmes

The association's activities include:

  • Model United Nations —upport for school and university simulations of UN bodies, including the Global Classrooms programme for underserved schools
  • Advocacy — campaigns urging US policymakers to fund UN peacekeeping and agencies and to maintain US engagement with the UN
  • UN Day — annual public events each 24 October marking the anniversary of the UN Charter's entry into force
  • Young professionals and campus networks — chapters in cities and universities across the United States
