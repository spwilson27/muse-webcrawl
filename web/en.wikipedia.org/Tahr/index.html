Tahr
FamilyBovidae
SubfamilyCaprinae
GeneraHemitragus, Arabitragus, Nilgiritragus

Tahrs are large hoofed mammals of the subfamily Caprinae, related to wild goats, inhabiting rugged mountain country in Asia. Three species are recognised: the Himalayan tahr, the Arabian tahr, and the Nilgiri tahr.

Species

The Himalayan tahr (Hemitragus jemlahicus) lives in the southern ranges of the Himalayas and has been introduced to New Zealand, South Africa and elsewhere, where it is hunted. The Arabian tahr (Arabitragus jayakari) inhabits the Al Hajar Mountains of Oman and the UAE and is endangered. The Nilgiri tahr (Nilgiritragus hylocrius) is found in the southern Western Ghats of India.

Characteristics

Tahrs are stocky, sure-footed climbers with rubbery hooves adapted to steep rock. Males of the Himalayan species grow long shaggy manes. They graze grasses and browse shrubs, gathering in herds that vary with season.
