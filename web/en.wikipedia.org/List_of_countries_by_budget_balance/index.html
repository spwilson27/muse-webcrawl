List of countries by budget balance

This is a list of countries by government budget balance, expressed as a percentage of gross domestic product (GDP). A positive figure indicates a budget surplus; a negative figure indicates a deficit. Figures are drawn largely from the CIA World Factbook and the IMF.

Concepts

The budget balance differs from government debt, which is the accumulated stock of past deficits. Cyclically adjusted balances attempt to remove the effects of the business cycle.
