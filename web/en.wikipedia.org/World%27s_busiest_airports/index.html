World's busiest airports

The world's busiest airports are ranked by Airports Council International (ACI) according to total passenger traffic, international passenger traffic, cargo traffic, and aircraft movements. The rankings are dominated by major hub airports in the United States, China, and Europe.

Passenger traffic

Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport has been the busiest airport by total passengers for most years since 1998, exceeding 100 million passengers annually before the COVID-19 pandemic. Other consistently top-ranked airports include Beijing Capital, Los Angeles International, Dubai International, Tokyo Haneda, Chicago O'Hare, London Heathrow, Hong Kong International, Shanghai Pudong, and Dallas/Fort Worth.

International traffic

By international passengers alone, Dubai International has ranked first in recent years, followed by London Heathrow and Hong Kong International.

Cargo

Hong Kong International and Memphis International (the FedEx superhub) lead rankings by freight tonnes handled.
