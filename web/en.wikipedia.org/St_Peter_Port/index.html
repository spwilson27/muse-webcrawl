St Peter Port
Island: Guernsey, Channel Islands
Status: Capital and main port
Population: about 19,000 (parish)

St Peter Port is a town and parish on the east coast of Guernsey in the Channel Islands. It is the capital of the Bailiwick of Guernsey and its principal port and commercial centre.

Geography and landmarks

The town climbs in terraces above a natural harbour guarded by Castle Cornet, a fortress on a breakwater that has defended the anchorage for centuries. Hauteville House, where Victor Hugo lived in exile and wrote Les Misérables, is now a museum.

Economy

Finance and tourism dominate the local economy. Ferries connect St Peter Port with England, France and the other Channel Islands.
