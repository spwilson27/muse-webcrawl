Denning report

The Denning report was an official inquiry by Lord Denning, Master of the Rolls, into the Profumo affair, published in September 1963. It investigated the security implications of the relationship between John Profumo, Secretary of State for War, and Christine Keeler, who was also involved with a Soviet naval attaché.

Findings

Denning concluded there had been no breach of security, while criticising the lax moral climate and the handling of the matter. The report became a bestseller, unusual for an official publication, and is remembered for its readable, narrative style.
