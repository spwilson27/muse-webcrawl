Bharti Airtel

Bharti Airtel Limited is an Indian multinational telecommunications company headquartered in New Delhi. It operates mobile and fixed-line services in India and in more than a dozen countries in Africa, as well as Sri Lanka, and ranks among the world's largest mobile network operators by subscriber numbers.

History

The company traces its roots to Bharti Enterprises, founded by Sunil Bharti Mittal in 1976 to manufacture bicycle parts, which diversified into telecom equipment and, in 1995, launched mobile services in Delhi under the Airtel brand. Airtel pioneered the low-cost "minutes factory" model of outsourcing network operations. In 2010 it acquired Zain's African operations for about US$10.7 billion, transforming it into a major international operator; in 2018 it absorbed Telenor India and Tata Teleservices' consumer mobile business.

Operations

Airtel provides 4G and 5G mobile services, broadband (Airtel Xstream), direct-to-home television, enterprise services and digital payments through Airtel Payments Bank. It competes primarily with Reliance Jio in the Indian market. Sunil Mittal remains chairman of the company, which is part of the Bharti group.
