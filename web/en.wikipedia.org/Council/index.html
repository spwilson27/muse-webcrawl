Council

A council is a group of people who come together to consult, deliberate, or make decisions. A council may function as a legislature, especially at a town, city or county level, but most legislative bodies at state, national or international level are not considered councils.

Types

Examples include municipal councils, county councils, ecumenical councils in churches, workers' councils, school councils, and bodies such as the United Nations Security Council and the Council of the European Union. Historically councils have been tribal, royal, and ecclesiastical assemblies.

This article is about deliberative bodies in general. For specific bodies, see their own articles.
