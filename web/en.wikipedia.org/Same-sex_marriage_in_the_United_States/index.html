Same-sex marriage in the United States

Same-sex marriage has been legal nationwide in the United States since 26 June 2015, when the Supreme Court ruled in Obergefell v. Hodges that the fundamental right to marry is guaranteed to same-sex couples by the Due Process and Equal Protection Clauses of the Fourteenth Amendment. The ruling required all states to license and recognise same-sex marriages.

Before national legalisation

Massachusetts became the first state to legalise same-sex marriage in 2004, following the state supreme court's decision in Goodridge v. Department of Public Health. Over the following decade, more states legalised it through courts, legislatures and — for the first time in 2012 — popular referendums, while others adopted constitutional bans. Federal recognition had been barred by the Defense of Marriage Act (DOMA) of 1996 until the Supreme Court struck down DOMA's key provision in United States v. Windsor (2013). By the time of Obergefell, 36 states and the District of Columbia already allowed same-sex marriages.

Obergefell v. Hodges

The consolidated cases arose from the Sixth Circuit, which had upheld state bans. Justice Anthony Kennedy's 5–4 majority opinion held that marriage is a fundamental right inherent in individual autonomy, that it supports a two-person union unlike any other, safeguards children and families, and is a keystone of social order — and that same-sex couples could not be denied it. The four dissenters argued the question should be left to democratic processes.

Aftermath

In 2022, following concerns that Obergefell might be revisited after Dobbs, Congress passed the Respect for Marriage Act, requiring the federal government and states to recognise valid out-of-state marriages regardless of the spouses' sex, race or ethnicity, while including protections for religious organisations. Public support for same-sex marriage, about 27% in 1996, rose to roughly 70% in Gallup polling of the early 2020s. Census estimates put the number of married same-sex couples in the United States at over 700,000.
