Endless (Frank Ocean album)

Released: August 19, 2016

Label: Def Jam

Endless is a visual album by American singer Frank Ocean. It was released on August 19, 2016, as an Apple Music exclusive, one day before his second studio album Blonde. The video depicts Ocean building a spiral staircase in a warehouse while new music plays.

Background and release

Endless fulfilled Ocean's contractual obligations to Def Jam Recordings, clearing the way for Blonde to be released independently on his own label, Boys Don't Cry. The visual album consists of a continuous 45-minute video of Ocean constructing a staircase, set to lo-fi, ambient-influenced R&B tracks.

Reception

Critics praised Endless for its atmosphere and experimentation, though many regarded it as a companion piece to Blonde rather than a standalone work. In 2017, Ocean made limited vinyl, CD, and VHS editions available through his website.
