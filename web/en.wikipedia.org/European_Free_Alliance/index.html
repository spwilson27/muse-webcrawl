The European Free Alliance (EFA) is a European political party that brings together regionalist and nationalist parties from across Europe that advocate self-government — ranging from autonomy to independence — for their regions or stateless nations, including parties from Scotland, Wales, Catalonia, the Basque Country, Flanders, Corsica and elsewhere.

History

Founded in Brussels in 1981 as a loose cooperation of regional parties, the EFA registered as a European political party in 2004. Its member parties span the political spectrum from centre-right to centre-left, united by support for self-determination, decentralisation, linguistic diversity and a "Europe of the peoples". Prominent members have included the Scottish National Party (until Brexit), Plaid Cymru, the New Flemish Alliance (N-VA) — its largest member for a period — and Catalan and Basque nationalist parties.

In the European Parliament

Since 1999 the EFA's MEPs have sat in a joint group with the European Green Party, the Greens–European Free Alliance (Greens/EFA) group, although some member parties' MEPs have sat with other groups, notably the European Conservatives and Reformists.
