Watcher (angel)

A Watcher (Aramaic: ʿiyr; Greek: egrēgoroi) is a type of angel mentioned in the Book of Daniel and developed extensively in the apocryphal Book of Enoch. Watchers were angels sent to earth to observe humankind.

Book of Enoch

In the Book of the Watchers (1 Enoch 1–36), two hundred Watchers led by Semjaza descend to Mount Hermon, take human wives, and father giant offspring, the Nephilim. They also teach forbidden arts — metalworking, cosmetics, sorcery and astrology — corrupting humanity and provoking the Flood. The archangels bind them in the desert and valleys of the earth until judgement.

Daniel

In Daniel 4, Nebuchadnezzar sees a "watcher, a holy one" descend from heaven in a dream to announce his humbling. Second Temple and later Jewish and Christian literature variously treated Watchers as fallen angels.
