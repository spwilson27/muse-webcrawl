Zechariah (New Testament figure)

Zechariah (or Zacharias) is a figure in the New Testament, described in the Gospel of Luke as a Jewish priest of the division of Abijah and the father of John the Baptist. His wife was Elizabeth, a relative of Mary, the mother of Jesus.

Narrative in Luke

According to Luke, while Zechariah was burning incense in the Temple in Jerusalem, the angel Gabriel appeared and announced that his elderly, barren wife would bear a son who would prepare the way for the Lord. When Zechariah asked for a sign, he was struck mute until the prophecy was fulfilled. After the child's birth, his speech returned and he uttered the prophecy known as the Benedictus ("Blessed be the Lord God of Israel").

Veneration

Zechariah is venerated as a saint in Roman Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, and other traditions, and is also honoured in Islam as the prophet Zakariya, guardian of Maryam (Mary).
