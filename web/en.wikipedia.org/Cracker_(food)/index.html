Cracker (food)

A cracker is a flat, dry baked biscuit typically made with flour. Flavourings or seasonings such as salt, herbs, seeds or cheese may be added to the dough or sprinkled on top before baking.

Types

Common types include saltine (soda) crackers, water crackers, cream crackers, graham crackers, oyster crackers and cheese crackers such as Ritz and Goldfish. Crackers are eaten alone as snacks or with cheese, soup and dips.

History

Precursors include hardtack and ship's biscuit, valued for long storage. Commercial cracker baking developed in 19th-century New England.
