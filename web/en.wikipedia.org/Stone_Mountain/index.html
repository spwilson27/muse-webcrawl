Stone Mountain

LocationDeKalb County, Georgia, United States
Elevation1,686 ft (514 m)
TypeQuartz monzonite dome (monadnock)

Stone Mountain is a quartz monzonite dome monadnock near the city of Stone Mountain in DeKalb County, Georgia, east of Atlanta. Rising some 825 feet above the surrounding terrain, it is the centerpiece of Stone Mountain Park, one of Georgia's most visited attractions.

The carving

The north face of the mountain bears the largest bas-relief sculpture in the world, depicting Confederate leaders Jefferson Davis, Robert E. Lee and Stonewall Jackson on horseback. Conceived in the early twentieth century, the carving was worked on intermittently by sculptors including Gutzon Borglum and was completed in 1972. The memorial has long been controversial because of its association with the Confederacy and the Ku Klux Klan, which held ceremonies at the site.

Geology

Stone Mountain is a pluton formed from magma that intruded the surrounding rock roughly 300-350 million years ago during the Alleghanian orogeny and was later exposed by erosion. Its dome is composed largely of quartz monzonite.
