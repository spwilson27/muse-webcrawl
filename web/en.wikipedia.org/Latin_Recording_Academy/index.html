Latin Recording Academy

The Latin Recording Academy presents the Latin Grammy Awards

The Latin Recording Academy, formally the Latin Academy of Recording Arts & Sciences, is a multinational membership-based organization composed of Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking music creators and professionals. It is best known as the organization behind the annual Latin Grammy Awards.

History

The Academy was established in 1997 as the Latin music branch of the United States National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences (now the Recording Academy), in response to the growth of Latin music worldwide. It organized the first Latin Grammy Awards in 2000.

Activities

Beyond the awards ceremony, the Academy runs educational and charitable programmes, including the Latin Grammy Cultural Foundation, which supports music education and scholarships for young Latin music creators, and initiatives to preserve Latin musical heritage.
