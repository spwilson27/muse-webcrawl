Fars province

Fars province (Persian: استان فارس) is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, in the south of the country. Its capital is Shiraz. In ancient times the region was known as Persis, the homeland of the Persians, from which the name Persia derives.

Fars contains major archaeological sites including Persepolis, Pasargadae, and Naqsh-e Rostam, associated with the Achaemenid Empire. The province's population is about 4.8 million. Persian is the predominant language, with Qashqai and Luri minorities.
