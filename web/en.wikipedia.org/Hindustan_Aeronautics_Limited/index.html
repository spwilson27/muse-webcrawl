Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) is an Indian public sector aerospace and defence company headquartered in Bengaluru. Governed by the Ministry of Defence, it is one of the oldest and largest aerospace and defence manufacturers in the world and the principal aircraft manufacturer for the Indian Armed Forces. HAL designs, manufactures, overhauls and upgrades aircraft, helicopters, aero-engines, avionics and accessories.

History

The company was founded on 23 December 1940 as Hindustan Aircraft Limited in Bangalore by the industrialist Walchand Hirachand, in association with the princely state of Mysore, to manufacture aircraft in India. The Government of India became a shareholder in 1942 and took management control during the Second World War, when the factory overhauled Allied aircraft. In 1964 Hindustan Aircraft Limited merged with Aeronautics India Limited to form Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.

HAL's first indigenous design to reach production was the HT-2 trainer (1951). Under licence it has built aircraft including the Mikoyan-Gurevich MiG-21, MiG-27, SEPECAT Jaguar and Sukhoi Su-30MKI, the last becoming the backbone of the Indian Air Force. Early indigenous combat designs include the HF-24 Marut fighter-bomber (1961), designed with a team led by Kurt Tank.

Products

  • HAL Tejas — single-engine, delta-wing light combat aircraft, in service with the IAF; the enlarged Tejas Mk2 is under development.
  • Dhruv — advanced light helicopter, exported to several countries; weaponised variant Rudra and the Prachand light combat helicopter derive from it.
  • HJT-16 Kiran and HTT-40 trainers; the HJT-36 Sitara intermediate jet trainer.
  • Dornier 228 — licence-built light transport, also offered for civil use.
  • Aero-engines, avionics, and structures for space launch vehicles in cooperation with ISRO.

Organisation

HAL operates divisions across India, including complexes at Bengaluru, Nashik (aircraft), Koraput (engines), Hyderabad (avionics), Lucknow (accessories) and Kanpur (transport aircraft). It was granted Navratna status by the Government of India in 2007 and listed on Indian stock exchanges in 2018, with the government retaining a majority stake. HAL regularly ranks among the world's top defence companies by revenue.
