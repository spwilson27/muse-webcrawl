The Grand Illusion (album)

ArtistStyx
Released7 July 1977
GenreProgressive rock, hard rock
LabelA&M

The Grand Illusion is the seventh studio album by the American rock band Styx, released on 7 July 1977 (7/7/77) by A&M Records. It became the band's breakthrough album, reaching triple-platinum status in the United States.

Content

The album explores themes of fame, ambition, and disillusionment, contrasting outward success with inner emptiness. It includes some of the band's best-known songs, notably "Come Sail Away", written by Dennis DeYoung, which became a top-ten single, and "Fooling Yourself (The Angry Young Man)", written by Tommy Shaw.

Legacy

The Grand Illusion established Styx as one of the most successful American arena-rock acts of the late 1970s and paved the way for the even bigger commercial success of Pieces of Eight (1978) and Paradise Theatre (1981).
