Creation and annihilation operators

Creation and annihilation operators
Field: Quantum mechanics, quantum field theory
Also called: Ladder operators
Key relation: [a, a†] = 1 (bosons)

Creation and annihilation operators are mathematical operators that increase or decrease the number of particles in a quantum state by one. They are central to the quantum harmonic oscillator, second quantization, and quantum field theory, where particles themselves are excitations created and destroyed by field operators.

Harmonic oscillator

For the quantum harmonic oscillator, the annihilation operator a lowers the energy by one quantum ħω, while the creation operator a† raises it. Acting on the ground state, repeated application of a† generates the full ladder of number states. For bosons the operators obey the commutation relation [a, a†] = 1; for fermions, anticommutation relations enforce the Pauli exclusion principle, so a state can hold at most one particle.

Applications

In quantum field theory, creation and annihilation operators for each momentum mode build the Fock space of multi-particle states. They also appear in quantum optics, condensed matter physics (phonons, magnons), and the algebraic solution of angular momentum.
