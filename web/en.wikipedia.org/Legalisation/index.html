Legalisation

Legalisation has two principal meanings. In international law and consular practice, it is the process of authenticating a document so it will be recognised abroad — for example by an apostille under the 1961 Hague Convention, which abolished the older chain of consular legalisation among member states.

Other sense

More generally, legalisation means making something lawful that was previously prohibited, as in debates over the legalisation of a drug or activity; in that sense it is closely related to, but distinct from, decriminalisation, which removes criminal penalties without a full legal regulatory framework.
