Morocco, officially the Kingdom of Morocco, is a country in the Maghreb region of North Africa. It overlooks the Mediterranean Sea to the north and the Atlantic Ocean to the west, and has land borders with Algeria to the east and the disputed territory of Western Sahara to the south. Morocco also claims the Spanish exclaves of Ceuta, Melilla and Peñón de Vélez de la Gomera. Its capital is Rabat and its largest city is Casablanca.

History

The region has been inhabited since prehistoric times and was home to Berber (Amazigh) peoples for millennia. In 788, Idris I founded the first Moroccan state. A series of dynasties — including the Almoravids, Almohads, Marinids, Saadians, and Alaouites — ruled the country. Under the Alaouite dynasty, which came to power in the 17th century and still reigns today, Morocco resisted Ottoman expansion and remained independent while much of North Africa was absorbed into the Ottoman Empire. In 1912 Morocco became a French and Spanish protectorate; it regained independence in 1956.

Geography

Morocco's geography spans Atlantic and Mediterranean coastlines, the Rif Mountains in the north, the Atlas Mountains running through its centre, and the Sahara Desert in the south. The climate ranges from Mediterranean along the coast to arid and desert in the interior south. Major cities include Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakesh, Fez, Tangier, and Agadir.

Government and society

Morocco is a constitutional monarchy in which the king retains wide executive and religious authority as "Commander of the Faithful." Islam is the state religion; the population is predominantly Arab and Amazigh, and Arabic and Amazigh are official languages, with French widely used in administration, business, and education.

Economy

The economy rests on agriculture, phosphates (Morocco holds the world's largest phosphate reserves), tourism, manufacturing including automobiles and aeronautics, and remittances. Modern infrastructure projects include the Tanger Med port and the Al Boraq high-speed railway, Africa's first, opened in 2018.
