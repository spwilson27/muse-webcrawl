Google Home is a line of smart speakers developed by Google under the Google Nest brand. The devices enable users to speak voice commands to interact with services through Google Assistant, the company's virtual assistant. Both in-house and third-party services are integrated, allowing users to play media, control smart home devices, and get information.

History

The first Google Home speaker was announced in May 2016 and released in the United States in November 2016. Smaller and larger variants followed, including the Google Home Mini (2017) and Home Max. In 2019 the product line was folded into the Google Nest brand, with subsequent speakers sold as the Nest Mini and Nest Audio.

Features

Google Home supports voice control of compatible lights, thermostats, and other connected devices, multi-room audio, Chromecast streaming, hands-free calling in some regions, and routines that chain actions to a single command. Privacy concerns have been raised over always-listening microphones and the retention of voice recordings.
