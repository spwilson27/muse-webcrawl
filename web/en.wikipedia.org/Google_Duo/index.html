Google Duo was a video chat mobile app developed by Google, available on Android and iOS, and on the web. It was announced at Google's developer conference on 18 May 2016 and began its worldwide release on 16 August 2016. It was distinguished by very simple, high-quality one-to-one video calling based on phone numbers.

Features

Duo's signature feature, "Knock Knock", showed a live video preview of the caller before the recipient answered. The app used end-to-end encryption, adaptive quality that switched between Wi-Fi and mobile data without dropping calls, and later gained group calling, audio-only calls, and low-light mode.

Merger with Google Meet

In 2022 Google announced that Duo would merge with Google Meet; the Duo app was rebranded as Google Meet, and the standalone Duo service was discontinued as the features were consolidated into Meet.
