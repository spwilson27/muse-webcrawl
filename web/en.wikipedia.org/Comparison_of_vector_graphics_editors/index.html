Comparison of vector graphics editors

Vector graphics editors create images from geometric primitives — points, lines, curves, and shapes — which scale without loss of quality. They are used for logos, illustration, typography, and technical drawing. Editors are compared by platform, price and licence, native file format, SVG support and standards compliance, and features such as tracing (bitmap-to-vector conversion), gradients and meshes, and print colour support.
