Dragon Quest (video game)

Developer: Chunsoft
Publisher: Enix
Designer: Yuji Horii
Artist: Akira Toriyama
Composer: Koichi Sugiyama
Release: 1986 (Famicom/NES)

Dragon Quest, titled Dragon Warrior in its initial North American releases, is a 1986 role-playing video game developed by Chunsoft and published by Enix for the Nintendo Entertainment System. It is the first game in the long-running Dragon Quest series.

Gameplay

The player controls the descendant of the legendary hero Erdrick, who must rescue Princess Gwaelin and defeat the evil Dragonlord in the kingdom of Alefgard. The game features a top-down overworld, random encounters fought in turn-based first-person battles, and experience points and levels — elements that became the template for the Japanese role-playing game genre.

Development and legacy

The game was created by Yuji Horii, with character and monster designs by Akira Toriyama and music by Koichi Sugiyama. Inspired by Western RPGs such as Wizardry and Ultima, Dragon Quest simplified their systems for a console audience. It was a major commercial success in Japan and is widely credited with establishing the conventions of the JRPG; its influence on subsequent titles, including Final Fantasy, was profound.
