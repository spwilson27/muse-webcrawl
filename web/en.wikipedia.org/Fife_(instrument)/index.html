Fife (instrument)

A fife is a small, high-pitched, transversely blown flute similar to the piccolo. The fife originated in medieval Europe and is typically made of wood. It has six finger holes and an embouchure hole. It is strongly associated with military bands.

History

Fifes were used in Landsknecht armies and spread through European militaries in the 16th century. In colonial America the fife-and-drum corps became iconic in the Revolutionary War; tunes such as "Yankee Doodle" were fife standards.

Military use

Paired with snare drums, fifes played reveille, tattoo, and marches for daily camp routine. Fife and drum traditions continue in New England corps and in Basel's carnival Fasnacht.
