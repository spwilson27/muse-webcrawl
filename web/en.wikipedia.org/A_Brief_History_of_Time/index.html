A Brief History of Time

A Brief History of Time: From the Big Bang to Black Holes is a book on theoretical cosmology by the physicist Stephen Hawking, first published in 1988. It became a bestseller and sold more than 25 million copies, making Hawking one of the most famous scientists in the world.

The book explains topics in cosmology — including the Big Bang, black holes, light cones, and superstring theory — in terms accessible to the non-specialist reader. Hawking aimed to give an overview of the subject but also attempted to explain some complex mathematics; at the editor's suggestion, only one equation, E = mc², was included. An updated and expanded edition, A Briefer History of Time, was published in 2005.
