Spermatheca

The spermatheca (plural: spermathecae) is an accessory organ of the female reproductive tract in some invertebrates, including insects, arachnids, and some molluscs and worms, that stores sperm received from the male during mating.

Function

Sperm stored in the spermatheca may remain viable for long periods — in honeybee queens, for years — and is released to fertilise eggs as they pass through the reproductive tract. This allows a female to fertilise many eggs after a single mating and gives her some control over the timing of fertilisation.

Structure

The spermatheca is typically a sac or tube connected to the reproductive tract by a duct, often accompanied by a gland that secretes fluids nourishing the stored sperm. Some species have multiple spermathecae.
