Ambiorix

Ambiorix (fl. 54–53 BCE) was a prince of the Eburones, a Belgic tribe in northeastern Gaul, who led a major revolt against Julius Caesar during the Gallic Wars. Together with Cativolcus he ruled the Eburones; his destruction of a Roman legion in 54 BCE was Caesar's worst defeat in Gaul and nearly changed the course of the conquest.

The revolt

As recounted in Caesar's Commentarii de Bello Gallico, after a poor harvest Caesar dispersed his legions in winter quarters among the Belgic tribes, including one and a half legions under Sabinus and Cotta among the Eburones. Ambiorix attacked their camp, then lured the Romans out with a false offer of safe passage and annihilated the column in a valley ambush — some 6,000 men. He then incited the Nervii and other tribes to attack Quintus Cicero's winter camp, which Caesar relieved only after a desperate siege.

Aftermath

Caesar responded with a campaign of devastation against the Eburones, who effectively disappear from history as a people. Ambiorix himself escaped across the Rhine and was never captured. In the 19th century he was rediscovered as a Belgian national hero — a statue of him stands in Tongeren, Belgium's oldest town — and he appears as a character in the Asterix comics' Belgian adventure.
