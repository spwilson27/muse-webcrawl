Mission Inn Hotel & Spa

The Mission Inn Hotel & Spa is a historic landmark hotel in downtown Riverside, California. Beginning as a modest adobe boarding house opened by Frank Miller in the 1870s, it grew over decades of eclectic additions into an elaborate, sprawling complex mixing Mission Revival, Spanish Gothic Revival, Moorish, and many other architectural styles.

History

Miller continually expanded the inn, adding cloisters, a rotunda wing, catacombs-like corridors, chapels, and a vast collection of art, bells, and religious artefacts. The hotel hosted numerous U.S. presidents and celebrities; Richard and Pat Nixon were married there, and Ronald and Nancy Reagan honeymooned there.

Today

Designated a National Historic Landmark in 1977, the Mission Inn is famous for its annual Festival of Lights, one of the largest holiday lighting displays in the United States.
