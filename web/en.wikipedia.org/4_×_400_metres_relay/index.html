The 4 × 400 metres relay is a track and field relay race in which four athletes per team each run one 400-metre lap, handing a baton to the next runner within a designated zone. Because it combines sprinting speed with endurance and tactics, it is customarily scheduled as the closing event of championship meetings.

Race description

Teams line up in lanes with a three-turn stagger. The lead-off runner completes a full lap in lanes; the second runner breaks for lane one after the first bend. Subsequent exchanges are usually made with runners jostling for position in a physical, tactical race to the finish, with the anchor leg frequently deciding medals.

Notable performances

Highlights in Olympic history include the British men's upset victory at Berlin 1936, the United States' long periods of dominance, the Bahamas' win at London 2012, and the women's rivalry between the United States, Jamaica, and earlier the Soviet Union and East Germany.
