Canyoning
Descending canyons using abseiling, jumping, sliding, and swimming

Canyoning (known as canyoneering in the United States) is a sport that involves travelling down canyons using a variety of techniques including walking, scrambling, climbing, jumping, abseiling (rappelling), and swimming.

Techniques and equipment

Participants typically wear wetsuits, helmets, and harnesses, and carry ropes for descending waterfalls. Descents range from easy walks through non-technical slot canyons to committing multi-pitch routes with strong currents.

Risks

Flash floods are the principal hazard, particularly in desert slot canyons of the American Southwest; weather checks and escape routes are essential.
