Pivotal Ventures is an American investment and incubation company founded in 2015 by philanthropist Melinda French Gates. Based in Kirkland, Washington, the firm invests in funds, nonprofit organizations, and for-profit ventures, with a focus on advancing social progress and expanding women's power and influence in the United States and around the world.

History

French Gates established Pivotal Ventures as the private office through which she carries out much of her philanthropic and investment work outside the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. The organization has made investments and grants across areas including women's economic empowerment, caregiving, adolescent mental health, civic engagement, and increasing diversity in technology and venture capital.

Activities

Pivotal Ventures invests through a combination of venture funding, philanthropic grants, and advocacy. It has backed investment funds led by women and people of colour, supported organizations working on paid family and medical leave and the care economy, and launched initiatives aimed at closing gender gaps in computing and engineering fields.

In 2024, following her departure from the Gates Foundation, French Gates committed substantial additional resources through Pivotal Ventures to causes supporting women and families.
