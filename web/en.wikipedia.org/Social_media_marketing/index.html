Social media marketing

Social media marketing is the use of social media platforms and websites to promote a product or service, build brand awareness, engage customers, and drive traffic and sales. It encompasses organic activities — posting text, images, and video to owned profiles — and paid social advertising targeted using platform data, together with influencer marketing, social listening, and community management.

Practices

Marketers develop content strategies tailored to each platform's formats and algorithms (short-form video on TikTok and Reels, professional content on LinkedIn, visual posts on Instagram and Pinterest), set objectives such as reach, engagement, leads, or conversions, and measure performance with analytics metrics including impressions, click-through rates, follower growth, and return on ad spend. Influencer marketing partners brands with creators whose audiences trust their recommendations; social listening monitors mentions and sentiment to inform strategy and customer service. Campaigns exploit shareable and interactive content — hashtags, challenges, polls, user-generated content — to gain earned distribution.

History

The discipline emerged in the late 2000s as Facebook, Twitter (now X), and YouTube accumulated mass audiences and launched advertising products; Facebook's Pages and ad platform (2007 onward) let brands maintain official presences, and subsequent targeting, retargeting, and shopping features made social a central channel in digital marketing budgets. By the 2020s social platforms were among the largest advertising media globally.

Criticism and regulation

Social media marketing has drawn criticism over undisclosed advertising, children's exposure to marketing, data privacy in targeting, and the amplification of misleading claims. Regulators such as the U.S. Federal Trade Commission and the UK Advertising Standards Authority require clear disclosure of paid endorsements (for example, #ad), and platforms provide branded-content tools and ad libraries for transparency.
