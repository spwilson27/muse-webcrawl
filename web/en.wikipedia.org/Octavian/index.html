Augustus as Octavian

After the assassination of Julius Caesar in 44 BC, the eighteen-year-old Octavian raised an army, allied with Mark Antony and Lepidus in the Second Triumvirate, defeated Caesar's assassins at Philippi, and after Antony's defeat at Actium in 31 BC became the undisputed master of the Roman world. His rise took place against the last generation of the Roman Republic, whose armies included officers of every rank known simply as an officer.
