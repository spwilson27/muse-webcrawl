The Steam Deck is a handheld gaming computer developed by Valve Corporation and released on 25 February 2022. It runs a custom AMD accelerated processing unit and Valve's SteamOS, allowing users to play games from their Steam library, including Windows games via the Proton compatibility layer.

Hardware

The device features a 7-inch LCD touchscreen flanked by gamepad controls: two analog sticks with capacitive touch, a D-pad, ABXY buttons, dual trackpads, analog triggers and four assignable grip buttons. A custom AMD APU ("Aerith") combines a quad-core Zen 2 CPU with an RDNA 2 GPU rated at 1.6 TFLOPS. A 40 watt-hour battery provides a claimed two to eight hours of gameplay depending on load. An OLED model with a larger 7.4-inch HDR display, bigger battery and faster Wi-Fi was released in November 2023.

Software

Steam Deck runs SteamOS 3.0, based on Arch Linux with the KDE Plasma desktop available in desktop mode. Windows titles run through Proton, a fork of Wine. Valve operates a "Deck Verified" programme categorising games as Verified, Playable, Unsupported or Unknown. The device is open: users can install other operating systems, third-party stores and emulators without modification.

Reception

The Steam Deck was praised for its performance, value and build quality, and credited with popularising the handheld PC form factor, spurring competitors such as the ASUS ROG Ally and Lenovo Legion Go. Supply was initially constrained by a reservation queue, which was cleared in October 2022.
