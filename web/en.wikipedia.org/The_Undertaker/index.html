The Undertaker

The Undertaker

Real name: Mark William Calaway

Born: 24 March 1965, Houston, Texas

Debut: 1987; WWF debut 1990

Retired: 2020

Finisher: Tombstone Piledriver

Mark William Calaway (born 1965), better known by his ring name The Undertaker, is an American retired professional wrestler widely regarded as one of the greatest of all time. He spent most of his career in the WWF/WWE, where his "Deadman" persona — an undead, supernatural mortician-like figure managed by Paul Bearer — became one of wrestling's most enduring characters.

Career

Debuting at Survivor Series in 1990, the Undertaker won multiple world championships and feuded with Hulk Hogan, Mankind, Kane (his storyline half-brother), and Triple H. His Hell in a Cell match with Mankind at King of the Ring 1998 is famous for Mankind being thrown from the top of the cell.

The Streak and retirement

At WrestleMania, the Undertaker compiled a 21–0 winning streak, "The Streak", before losing to Brock Lesnar in 2014. He retired after his cinematic Boneyard match at WrestleMania 36 in 2020 and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2022.
