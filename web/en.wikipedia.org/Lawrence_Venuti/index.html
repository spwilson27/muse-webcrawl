Lawrence Venuti

Lawrence Venuti (born 1953) is an American translation theorist, historian and translator, professor emeritus at Temple University. His book The Translator's Invisibility: A History of Translation (1995) reshaped translation studies by arguing that fluent, "invisible" translation domesticates foreign texts to target-culture values.

Concepts

Venuti contrasts domestication — smoothing a text into familiar style — with foreignization, which retains signs of the source culture and makes the translator visible. Drawing on Schleiermacher and poststructuralism, he advocates resistant translation as an ethical and political act against the marginal status of translators and Anglo-American cultural dominance.

Work

His other books include The Scandals of Translation (1998) and Translation Changes Everything (2013); he has translated extensively from Italian, including works by Iginio Ugo Tarchetti and Dino Buzzati, and edited The Translation Studies Reader.
