National Science Board

The National Science Board (NSB) is the governing body of the United States National Science Foundation (NSF) and an independent adviser to the President and Congress on science and engineering policy. It was created by the National Science Foundation Act of 1950.

Membership and role

The Board has 25 members appointed by the President and confirmed by the Senate for six-year terms; the NSF Director serves ex officio as a voting member. The NSB sets NSF policy within the framework of applicable legislation, approves major awards and new programmes, and oversees the Foundation's budget proposals.

Science and Engineering Indicators

Every two years the Board publishes Science and Engineering Indicators, a congressionally mandated report presenting quantitative data on the state of U.S. and global research and development, education, workforce and innovation. The Board also issues policy reports and statements on issues such as STEM education, research security and the skilled technical workforce.
