Wiki

A wiki is a website or collection of web pages that users can create and edit collaboratively, usually through a web browser. The first wiki, WikiWikiWeb, was created by Ward Cunningham in 1995; the name comes from the Hawaiian word wiki, meaning “quick.”

Features

Wikis typically provide simple markup or visual editing, internal hyperlinks between pages, revision history, recent-changes pages and discussion spaces. Access may be public or restricted. These features make wikis useful for encyclopedias, documentation, community knowledge bases and project coordination.

Examples

Wikipedia is the best-known wiki, but wikis are also used inside companies, schools and software projects. The social norms of a wiki community — such as assuming good faith and resolving disputes by discussion — can be as important as the software.
