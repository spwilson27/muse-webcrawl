Dewan Rakyat

The Dewan Rakyat (Malay for "House of Representatives") is the lower house of the Parliament of Malaysia. Together with the Dewan Negara (Senate) and the Yang di-Pertuan Agong (King), it forms the federal legislature of Malaysia.

Composition

The Dewan Rakyat consists of 222 members, called Members of Parliament (MPs), elected from single-member constituencies in federal general elections using the first-past-the-post system. Members serve terms of up to five years. To be eligible, a candidate must be a Malaysian citizen of at least 18 years of age (lowered from 21 by a 2019 constitutional amendment).

Powers

The Dewan Rakyat is the dominant chamber of Parliament. Money bills originate there, and the government is formed from the party or coalition commanding its confidence; the Prime Minister is normally a member of the Dewan Rakyat. Legislation passed by both houses receives royal assent, although the Dewan Negara's veto can in most cases only delay a bill.

Officers

The chamber is presided over by the Speaker of the Dewan Rakyat, elected by members from among themselves or from outside the house. Sittings are held in the Malaysian Houses of Parliament building in Kuala Lumpur.
