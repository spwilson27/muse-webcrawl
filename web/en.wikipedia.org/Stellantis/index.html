Stellantis

Founded: 16 January 2021
Headquarters: Hoofddorp, Netherlands
Industry: Automotive
Formed from: Merger of Fiat Chrysler Automobiles and Groupe PSA

Stellantis N.V. is a multinational automotive manufacturing corporation formed in 2021 from the merger of the Italian-American conglomerate Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (FCA) and the French PSA Group. The company is headquartered in Hoofddorp, Netherlands.

Stellantis is among the world's largest automakers by sales. Its portfolio comprises fourteen brands, including Abarth, Alfa Romeo, Chrysler, Citroën, Dodge, DS Automobiles, Fiat, Jeep, Lancia, Maserati, Opel, Peugeot, Ram, and Vauxhall.

History

FCA and PSA announced their intention to merge in October 2019, presenting it as a merger of equals. Shareholders of both companies approved the deal in January 2021, and Stellantis shares began trading in Milan, Paris, and New York that month. The name Stellantis derives from the Latin verb stello, "to brighten with stars". Carlos Tavares, formerly PSA's chief executive, led the merged group until his resignation in December 2024.

Operations

The company manufactures vehicles at plants across Europe, North and South America, and Asia, with major operations in France, Italy, the United States, Germany, Spain, and Brazil. Stellantis has invested heavily in electrification, launching dedicated electric vehicle platforms, and also owns the Free2move mobility service and the Comau robotics business.
