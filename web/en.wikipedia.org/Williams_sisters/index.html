Williams sisters

[Image: Williams sisters]

The Williams sisters are American professional tennis players Venus Williams (born June 17, 1980) and Serena Williams (born September 26, 1981). Trained by their parents, Richard Williams and Oracene Price, on public courts in Compton, California, the sisters transformed women's tennis with their power, athleticism and longevity, dominating the sport from the late 1990s into the 2020s.

Achievements

Together the sisters won 30 Grand Slam singles titles — 23 by Serena and 7 by Venus — and 14 Grand Slam doubles titles as a pair, never losing a major doubles final together. They also won four mixed doubles titles between them and four Olympic gold medals: each has a singles gold (Venus in 2000, Serena in 2012), and they won three doubles golds together (2000, 2008, 2012). Both reached the world No. 1 ranking in singles and doubles.

Rivalry and impact

Venus and Serena met professionally 31 times, with Serena winning 19 of the encounters, including nine Grand Slam finals. Their success as Black women from a working-class background reshaped the demographics and culture of tennis, and both became major figures in business, fashion and advocacy for equal pay and gender equity in sport. Serena retired from professional tennis in 2022 after the US Open.
