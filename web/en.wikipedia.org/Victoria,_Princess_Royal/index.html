Victoria, Princess Royal

Victoria, Princess Royal (Victoria Adelaide Mary Louisa; 21 November 1840 – 5 August 1901) was German Empress and Queen of Prussia as the wife of Frederick III, German Emperor. She was the eldest child of Queen Victoria of the United Kingdom and Prince Albert of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha.

Life

Highly intelligent and liberal in outlook, Victoria married Prince Frederick William of Prussia in 1858. She became German Empress in March 1888 when her husband succeeded as Frederick III, but he reigned for only 99 days before dying of throat cancer. Thereafter known as the Empress Frederick, she was a patron of education, nursing and the arts, and founded institutions including hospitals and schools in Germany.

Family

Her eldest son became Wilhelm II, the last German Emperor. Her often strained relationship with Wilhelm, and with the Chancellor Otto von Bismarck, reflected the political divide between her liberal views and the conservative Prussian establishment. She died at Kronberg in 1901, shortly after her mother.
