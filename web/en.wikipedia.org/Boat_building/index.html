Boat building

Boat building is the design and construction of boats and their systems. It has been practised since prehistoric times, from dugout canoes and reed boats to modern vessels of steel, aluminium and fibre-reinforced composite.

Methods

Traditional wooden construction techniques include carvel and clinker (lapstrake) planking over frames, strip planking, and plywood stitch-and-glue construction. Modern production boats are commonly moulded in fibreglass, while racing yachts use carbon-fibre composites.

Process

Building typically proceeds from a design and lofting of full-size lines, through construction of the hull, installation of structure and systems, and finishing. Amateur boat building remains a popular hobby, supported by kits and published plans.
