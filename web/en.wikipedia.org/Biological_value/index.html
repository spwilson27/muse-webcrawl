Biological value

Biological value (BV) is a measure of the proportion of absorbed protein from a food which becomes incorporated into the proteins of the organism's body. It captures how readily the digested protein can be used in protein synthesis in the cells of the organism. Proteins are the major source of nitrogen in food; BV assumes protein is the only source of nitrogen and measures the proportion of this nitrogen absorbed by the body which is then excreted.

Determination

BV is determined by measuring nitrogen intake and nitrogen excretion in urine and faeces. Whole egg protein was historically assigned a value of 100 as a reference, with whey protein sometimes measured slightly above this on relative scales. Foods with a high proportion of essential amino acids in the pattern the body requires score highest.

Limitations

The method depends on test conditions — including the protein level fed and the fasting state — and does not account well for digestibility differences in normal diets. Related measures include net protein utilization and the protein digestibility corrected amino acid score (PDCAAS).
