BBC News (known as BBC News 24 from 1997 to 2008) is a British free-to-air television news channel owned and operated by the BBC. It was launched as BBC News 24 on 9 November 1997 as part of the BBC's foray into digital domestic television channels, becoming the first competitor to Sky News, which had been running since 1989.

History

The channel was renamed BBC News in April 2008 as part of a creative review of BBC News branding. It broadcasts rolling news 24 hours a day, with hourly bulletins, sports and weather updates, and simulcasts with BBC One and the BBC World News service overnight. In April 2023, the domestic and international versions of the channel were merged into a single service headquartered in London, with opt-outs for UK audiences during major domestic stories.

Programming

Regular strands include BBC News at One (simulcast), Newsnight coverage, Sportsday, The Context, and BBC Verify fact-checking segments. The channel is known for breaking news coverage, live press conferences, and coverage of Parliament and major national events.
