Referee (association football)

The referee is the official in association football who enforces the Laws of the Game during a match. The referee has full authority to enforce the Laws, acts as timekeeper, keeps a record of the match, and may stop or abandon play for infringements or outside interference.

The referee is assisted by two assistant referees, a fourth official and, in top competitions, a video assistant referee (VAR). Sanctions include free kicks, penalty kicks, and yellow and red cards for cautionable and sending-off offences. Refereeing decisions on facts connected with play are final.

The office developed in English football in the late 19th century, replacing the earlier system of team umpires. See also Laws of the Game and VAR.
