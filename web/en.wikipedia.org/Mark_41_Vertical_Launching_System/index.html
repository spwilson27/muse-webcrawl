Mark 41 Vertical Launching System

The Mark 41 Vertical Launching System (Mk 41 VLS) is a shipborne missile canister launching system providing a rapid-fire launch capability against hostile threats. Developed by Martin Marietta (now Lockheed Martin), it entered service with the US Navy in 1986 aboard Ticonderoga-class cruisers.

Missiles are stored in canisters in vertical eight-cell modules below deck and can include the Standard Missile family, Tomahawk cruise missiles, ESSM, and VL-ASROC anti-submarine rockets.

The system is used by the navies of many allied nations and underlies the Aegis combat system ships' striking power.
