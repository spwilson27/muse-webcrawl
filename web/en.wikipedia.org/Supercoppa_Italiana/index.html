Supercoppa Italiana

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Supercoppa Italiana (English: Italian Super Cup) is an annual super cup tournament in Italian football. Founded in 1988, it was traditionally contested between the winners of Serie A and the Coppa Italia in a single match, usually played before or during the league season.

History and format

The first edition, in 1988, saw AC Milan defeat Sampdoria. For most of its history the cup was a one-off match; if the same club won both league and cup, the Coppa Italia runners-up took part. The match has frequently been staged abroad for commercial reasons, including in the United States, China, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia. From the 2023 edition, the format expanded to a four-team mini-tournament — a "Final Four" — featuring the top two Serie A clubs and the two Coppa Italia finalists, played in Saudi Arabia.

Records

Juventus are the most successful club in the competition's history, with nine titles, followed by Inter Milan and AC Milan. The trophy is regarded as the least prestigious of Italy's major domestic honours, but it is valued as the first silverware of the season and for its growing television and hosting revenues.
