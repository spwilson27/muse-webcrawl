Winchester College is a public school (an independent fee-charging boarding school) for boys aged 13–18 in Winchester, Hampshire, England. Founded in 1382 by William of Wykeham, Bishop of Winchester, it is one of the oldest schools in England and has operated continuously on its present site for over 600 years.

History

Wykeham founded the college as a feeder for New College, Oxford, providing for 70 poor scholars alongside commoners. The school was one of the nine "Clarendon schools" investigated in the 1860s and retains many medieval buildings, including its 14th-century chapel and Chamber Court. It has educated numerous notable alumni, known as Old Wykehamists, and began admitting girls into its sixth form in the 2020s.
