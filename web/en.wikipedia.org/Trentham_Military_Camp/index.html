Trentham Military Camp

LocationTrentham, Upper Hutt, New Zealand
Established1914 (as a training camp)
OperatorNew Zealand Army
HostsHeadquarters Joint Forces New Zealand; Defence College

Trentham Military Camp is a New Zealand Defence Force facility in the suburb of Trentham in Upper Hutt, near Wellington. It is one of the New Zealand Army's principal camps and hosts Headquarters Joint Forces New Zealand, the New Zealand Defence College, and a number of training and support units.

History

The site became a major mobilisation and training camp during the First World War, when thousands of men of the New Zealand Expeditionary Force trained at Trentham before embarking overseas. The camp also operated as a hospital and convalescent centre; during the 1918 influenza pandemic an emergency hospital at Trentham treated soldiers and the nearby Featherston and Trentham camps were severely affected. Training continued on a large scale in the Second World War, when Trentham again served as a reinforcement training depot.

Facilities and units

The modern camp houses headquarters and training establishments, including the Defence College, trade and specialist training schools, the Military Police, and support services for the Wellington region. Trentham Racecourse, adjacent to the camp, is a well-known thoroughbred racing venue and has historically been used for military purposes as well. The camp's memorials and historic buildings commemorate the soldiers who trained there in both world wars.
