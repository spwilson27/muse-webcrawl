21st Century Cures Act

The 21st Century Cures Act is a United States law enacted by the 114th United States Congress in December 2016 and signed by President Barack Obama on December 13, 2016. It authorized .3 billion in funding, mostly for the National Institutes of Health, and was designed to accelerate the discovery, development, and delivery of new medical treatments and cures.

The Act funded the NIH's BRAIN Initiative, the Cancer Moonshot, and the Precision Medicine Initiative, reauthorized funding to combat the opioid epidemic, and included provisions intended to streamline Food and Drug Administration approval processes and reform mental health care. It passed the House 392–26 and the Senate 94–5.
