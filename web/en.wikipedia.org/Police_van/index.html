Police van

TypePolice vehicle
UsesPrisoner transport, patrol support, public order
Also known asPaddy wagon, Black Maria (historical slang)

A police van is a type of vehicle operated by police forces. Police vans are usually employed for the transport of prisoners inside a specially adapted cell in the vehicle, or for the rapid transport of several officers to an incident.

Types

Prison vans have a secure cell area, often subdivided into individual cubicles, with reinforced doors and mesh-covered windows. Larger public-order (riot) vans carry teams of officers with protective screens and equipment storage. Mini and medium vans are also used as mobile custody suites, dog units, and forensic vehicles.

History and nicknames

Horse-drawn prisoner wagons of the 19th century were nicknamed "paddy wagons" and "Black Marias"; the terms carried into the motor era in North America. The modern police van evolved from converted commercial vans to purpose-built vehicles with separate driving and cell compartments and video monitoring.
