Temuco

[Placeholder image: Temuco.png]

Temuco is a city and commune, capital of the Cautín Province and of the Araucanía Region in southern Chile. Founded in 1881 during the Occupation of Araucanía, it lies about 670 kilometres south of Santiago. The city is a commercial and agricultural centre for the surrounding region and is noted for its markets selling Mapuche crafts.

Geography and climate

Temuco is situated on the banks of the Cautín River and has a temperate oceanic climate with a dry summer. Notable sites include the Museo Regional de la Araucanía and the football stadium Estadio Germán Becker.
