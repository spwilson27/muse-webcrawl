Foodborne illness (also foodborne disease and food poisoning) is any illness resulting from the consumption of food contaminated by pathogenic bacteria, viruses, parasites or toxins. More than 250 different foodborne hazards have been described. Norovirus accounts for the largest share of cases in high-income countries and is a leading cause of outbreaks in institutional settings such as hospitals, schools, cruise ships and restaurants, where it can also spread secondarily from person to person and via contaminated surfaces and aerosolized vomit.

Causes and vehicles

Each pathogen has characteristic food vehicles: Campylobacter with poultry; Salmonella with eggs, poultry and fresh produce; Shiga toxin–producing E. coli with ground beef and leafy greens; Listeria monocytogenes with refrigerated ready-to-eat foods; hepatitis A virus and norovirus with shellfish and food handled by infected workers. Because many pathogens can contaminate the same foods and cause similar symptoms, identifying the agent in an outbreak typically requires stool culture or molecular testing plus epidemiological interviews of those who ate common meals.

Incubation periods

The interval between eating and symptoms helps distinguish causes: preformed staphylococcal toxin acts within 1–6 hours; Clostridium perfringens within 6–24 hours; norovirus within 12–48 hours; Salmonella and Campylobacter within one to several days; and hepatitis A after several weeks. Longer incubation periods make outbreak investigation harder because recall of foods eaten is less reliable.

Control along the supply chain

Modern food safety is built on preventive controls at every step "from farm to fork": good agricultural and manufacturing practices, supplier verification, sanitation standard operating procedures, HACCP plans, temperature control, traceability systems enabling rapid recalls, and surveillance networks that detect clusters of illness. Irradiation, high-pressure processing and antimicrobial washes are additional interventions for specific products.
