Replevin

Hero image (placeholder):

In the common law, replevin is a legal action to recover personal property that has been wrongfully taken or detained, together with damages for its detention. The claimant seeks the return of the goods themselves rather than merely their monetary value.

Procedure

Historically, replevin arose in England where a landlord had distrained a tenant's goods, and it developed into a general remedy for wrongful taking. In modern practice in the United States and other jurisdictions, a plaintiff may obtain prejudgment seizure of the property by posting a bond, subject to due-process requirements of notice and a hearing. Replevin is distinguished from trover and conversion, which award money damages, and from detinue, a related action for wrongful detention.
