The Guardians of the Galaxy are a team of superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The name was first used for a 31st-century team created in 1969 by Arnold Drake and Gene Colan. The modern incarnation, formed by Dan Abnett and Andy Lanning during the Annihilation: Conquest storyline of 2008, brought together Star-Lord (Peter Quill), Gamora, Drax the Destroyer, Rocket Raccoon and Groot as a proactive cosmic strike force.

Film adaptations

Marvel Studios' 2014 film Guardians of the Galaxy, directed by James Gunn, transformed the obscure team into one of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's most popular franchises, praised for its humour and 1970s pop soundtrack. Chris Pratt, Zoe Saldaña, Dave Bautista, Bradley Cooper and Vin Diesel starred. Sequels followed in 2017 and 2023, and the team appeared in Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame.

Members

Peter Quill, a half-human abducted from Earth as a child, leads the team. Gamora, adopted daughter of Thanos, is "the deadliest woman in the galaxy". Drax seeks vengeance for his family; Rocket is a genetically engineered raccoon bounty hunter; Groot is a tree-like Flora colossus whose vocabulary is limited to "I am Groot". Later additions include Mantis and Nebula.
