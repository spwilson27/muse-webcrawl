Krasnoyarsk
Country: Russia
Founded: 1628
Population: over 1.1 million

Krasnoyarsk (Russian: Красноярск) is the largest city and administrative centre of Krasnoyarsk Krai, Russia, located on the Yenisei River in Siberia. It is a major junction of the Trans-Siberian Railway.

History

Founded in 1628 as the fort (ostrog) of Krasny Yar, the settlement grew with the Siberian gold rush and the arrival of the Trans-Siberian Railway in 1895. In the Soviet era it became a centre of aluminium smelting, machinery and defence industry; the huge Krasnoyarsk Dam and hydroelectric station upriver was completed in 1972.

Landmarks

Landmarks include the Paraskeva Pyatnitsa Chapel depicted on the 10-ruble note, the Stolby nature reserve of dramatic rock pillars just outside the city, and bridges over the Yenisei such as the Communal Bridge. The city hosted the 2019 Winter Universiade.
