Bell Media

Bell Media Inc. (French: Bell Média inc.) is a Canadian media conglomerate that is the mass-media subsidiary of BCE Inc. (also known as Bell Canada Enterprises), Canada's largest telecommunications company. Headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, it owns television, radio, digital media, and outdoor advertising assets across Canada in both English and French.

History

The company originated as CTVglobemedia, formed in 2007 when BCE regained control of the CTV television network and acquired the assets of CHUM Limited. In September 2010 BCE announced it would acquire full control of CTVglobemedia's broadcasting assets; the transaction was approved by the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC) and completed on April 1, 2011, whereupon the company was renamed Bell Media. In 2013 Bell Media acquired Astral Media for C$3.38 billion, substantially expanding its specialty channels, French-language television, and radio holdings, though regulators required the divestiture of some stations.

Assets

Bell Media's television properties include the CTV and CTV 2 networks, the Noovo French-language network, and numerous specialty channels such as TSN (The Sports Network), RDS, Discovery Channel Canada, CTV News Channel, CP24, Much, E!, and Crave (which includes the streaming service of the same name). Its radio division, built largely on the iHeartRadio brand under licence in Canada, operates dozens of stations including Toronto's CHUM-FM and CFRB, and Montreal's CJAD. Bell Media also owns the Astral Out-of-Home advertising network.
