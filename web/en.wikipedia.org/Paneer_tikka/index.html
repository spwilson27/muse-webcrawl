Paneer tikka

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Paneer tikka is an Indian dish made from chunks of paneer — a fresh, unaged cheese — marinated in yogurt and spices and then grilled, traditionally in a tandoor (clay oven). It is a vegetarian alternative to chicken tikka and is widely served across India as a starter or snack, as well as in wraps and sandwiches. Popular variants include paneer tikka masala, in which the grilled paneer is served in a spiced gravy.

Preparation

Cubes of paneer are coated in a marinade of thick yogurt (curd), ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, chaat masala, cumin, lemon juice and mustard oil, together with pieces of capsicum (bell pepper) and onion. After marinating, the pieces are threaded onto skewers and roasted at high heat until the edges char slightly. The dish is commonly garnished with coriander, a squeeze of lemon and onion rings, and served with mint chutney. Home versions are prepared on a grill, in an oven, or in a pan.

Variations

Regional and restaurant variants include hariyali paneer tikka (with a green herb marinade), achari paneer tikka (with pickling spices), and malai paneer tikka (with cream and cheese, milder in heat). Paneer tikka is also used as a filling in kathi rolls and pizzas in contemporary Indian fast food.
