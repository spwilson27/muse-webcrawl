German occupation of Czechoslovakia

Occupation of Czechoslovakia
Period1938/1939 – 1945
Key datesMunich Agreement, Sept 1938; occupation of Bohemia and Moravia, 15 March 1939; liberation, May 1945
StructuresSudetenland annexed to the Reich; Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia; Slovak State (client state)

The German occupation of Czechoslovakia began with the annexation of the Sudetenland under the Munich Agreement of September 1938 and was completed on 15 March 1939, when German troops occupied the Czech lands of Bohemia and Moravia in defiance of that agreement. The country ceased to exist as an independent state until its restoration in 1945.

From Munich to the Protectorate

After Munich, a weakened Second Czechoslovak Republic lost its border fortifications and much industry. In March 1939, under intense German pressure, President Emil Hácha signed an agreement in Berlin placing his country under German "protection"; Slovakia declared itself an independent client state, and Hungary annexed territory in the south and east. Bohemia and Moravia were reorganised as a Reich Protectorate, nominally autonomous but ruled in practice by a Reich Protector — first Konstantin von Neurath, then, from 1941, the SS leader Reinhard Heydrich.

Resistance and repression

Czechoslovak resistance operated at home and through a government-in-exile in London under Edvard Beneš, recognised by the Allies. In May 1942 Czechoslovak agents trained in Britain assassinated Heydrich in Prague (Operation Anthropoid); the Nazis retaliated by destroying the villages of Lidice and Ležáky and murdering their inhabitants. Czech industry was integrated into the German war economy, and the country's Jews were deported, many through the Theresienstadt (Terezín) ghetto-camp, to extermination camps.

Liberation

The Slovak National Uprising of 1944 and the Prague uprising of May 1945 accompanied the final collapse of the occupation as Soviet and American forces liberated the country. Czechoslovakia was restored in 1945, and the Munich Agreement was declared void by the Allies.
