Tom and Jerry is an American animated media franchise and series of comedy short films created in 1940 by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera. Best known for its 161 theatrical short films produced by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer, the series centers on the rivalry between the titular characters of a cat named Tom and a mouse named Jerry, whose comic chases and slapstick battles form the basis of most plots.

History

Hanna and Barbera, animators at MGM's cartoon studio, introduced the pair in the short Puss Gets the Boot (1940), in which the cat was named Jasper and the mouse was unnamed. The short was a hit and was nominated for an Academy Award. In a studio contest the characters received their permanent names, Tom and Jerry, and the team went on to produce 114 shorts for MGM between 1940 and 1958. Seven of these won the Academy Award for Best Animated Short Film, tying the series with Walt Disney's Silly Symphonies as the most-awarded cartoon series in that category.

After MGM closed its animation studio in 1957, the series was revived by Gene Deitch's Rembrandt Films in Prague (1961–1962) for 13 shorts, and then by Chuck Jones at Sib Tower 12 Productions (1963–1967) for 34 more. Later incarnations include television series by Hanna-Barbera and Filmation, the feature film Tom and Jerry: The Movie (1992), direct-to-video films, and the live-action/animation hybrid film Tom and Jerry (2021).

Characters

Tom (full name Thomas D. Cat) is a blue-grey Russian Blue cat who usually lives a pampered life in a suburban house. Jerry (full name Gerald Jinx Mouse) is a small brown house mouse who lives in a hole in the same house and consistently outwits Tom. Despite the violent slapstick of their conflicts — involving mousetraps, explosives, kitchen implements and elaborate contraptions — the characters rarely speak, and the comedy is carried by expressive animation and music. Supporting characters include the bulldog Spike, his son Tyke, the kitten's rival Butch, and Tom's housekeeper, Mammy Two Shoes.

Music

The MGM shorts are noted for their scores by Scott Bradley, which combined classical excerpts, jazz and popular songs into tightly synchronized accompaniment that is frequently cited as a high point of cartoon music composition.

Legacy

Tom and Jerry remains one of the most widely distributed and recognizable cartoon series in the world. The original shorts continue to air internationally, and the franchise has generated extensive merchandising, comic books and video games. The characters are cultural icons, referenced across popular media worldwide.
