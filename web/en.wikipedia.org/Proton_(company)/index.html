Proton (company)

Proton AG is a Swiss technology company founded in 2014 that provides privacy-focused online services. Its flagship product, Proton Mail, is an end-to-end encrypted email service founded by scientists who met at CERN. The company's services also include Proton VPN, Proton Calendar, Proton Drive, and Proton Pass. Proton is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, and operates under Swiss privacy law.

History

Proton Mail was launched in 2014 after a crowdfunding campaign that raised more than $550,000. It was founded by Andy Yen, Jason Stockman, and Wei Sun, who were motivated by revelations about mass surveillance and sought to build email that was private by default, using end-to-end encryption so that even the provider cannot read users' messages. In 2017, the company launched Proton VPN. Beginning in 2020 it expanded into a broader ecosystem with Proton Calendar, Proton Drive (2022), and Proton Pass (2023). In 2024, Proton transitioned to a structure in which the nonprofit Proton Foundation became the primary shareholder, intended to lock in the company's privacy mission.

Products

  • Proton Mail — end-to-end encrypted email with over 100 million accounts claimed across Proton services.
  • Proton VPN — a virtual private network service with a free tier and a no-logs policy, audited by third parties.
  • Proton Drive — end-to-end encrypted cloud storage and file sharing.
  • Proton Calendar — an encrypted calendar service.
  • Proton Pass — a password manager with built-in email aliases.

Business model and structure

Proton follows a freemium model: basic services are free, and revenue comes primarily from paid subscriptions rather than advertising or data collection. The company has positioned itself as an alternative to the surveillance-based business models of large technology firms. Its open-source clients and published security audits are central to its trust model, and its infrastructure is located in Switzerland and Germany, with servers in hardened facilities including a former Swiss military bunker.
