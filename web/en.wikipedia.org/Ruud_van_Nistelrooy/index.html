Rutgerus Johannes Martinus van Nistelrooy (born 1 July 1976), known as Ruud van Nistelrooy, is a Dutch professional football coach and former player, widely regarded as one of the most clinical strikers of his generation. He is Manchester United's record European goalscorer and one of the top scorers in UEFA Champions League history.

Club career

Van Nistelrooy rose through Den Bosch and Heerenveen before two prolific seasons at PSV Eindhoven, twice winning the Eredivisie golden boot. After recovering from a serious knee injury, he joined Manchester United in 2001 for a British record £19 million. In five seasons he scored 150 goals in 219 appearances, won the Premier League, FA Cup, and League Cup, and was voted PFA Players' Player of the Year. Moving to Real Madrid in 2006, he won two La Liga titles and the Pichichi Trophy, before later spells with Hamburger SV and Málaga.

International career and coaching

Van Nistelrooy earned 70 caps for the Netherlands, scoring 35 goals and playing at Euro 2004, the 2006 World Cup, and Euro 2008. After retiring in 2012 he moved into coaching with the Dutch national setup and PSV, where he became head coach in 2022 and won the KNVB Cup and Johan Cruyff Shield. He joined Manchester United as assistant coach in 2024 and served briefly as interim head coach.
