Doha

Doha (Arabic: الدوحة, romanized: ad-Dawḥah) is the capital city and main financial hub of Qatar. Located on the coast of the Persian Gulf in the east of the country, north of Al Wakrah and south of Al Khor and Lusail, it is home to most of the country's population and to the seat of government.

History

Doha was founded in the 1820s as an offshoot of Al Bidda, and it became the capital when Qatar gained independence from Britain in 1971. Once a small pearling and fishing town, it grew rapidly with the development of Qatar's oil and natural gas wealth.

Economy and landmarks

Doha is home to the government headquarters, QatarEnergy, the Al Jazeera media network, and Education City. Landmarks include the Museum of Islamic Art, the National Museum of Qatar, Souq Waqif, the Pearl Island, and the West Bay skyline. The city hosted the 2006 Asian Games, the 2022 FIFA World Cup final tournament matches, and the Doha Round of world trade negotiations is named after it.

Climate

Doha has a hot desert climate, with long, extremely hot summers where temperatures frequently exceed 40 °C (104 °F), and mild winters.
