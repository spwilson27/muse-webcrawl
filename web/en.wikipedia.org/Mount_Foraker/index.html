Mount Foraker

Mount Foraker is a mountain in the central Alaska Range in Denali National Park, Alaska. At 17,400 feet (5,304 m) it is the second highest peak in the Alaska Range and the third highest in the United States. Its Dena'ina name is Sultana, meaning "the woman", a companion to nearby Denali ("the husband").

History

The mountain was named in 1899 by Lt. J.S. Herron after Joseph B. Foraker, then a U.S. senator from Ohio. The first ascent was made in 1934 by Charles Houston, T. Graham Brown and Chychele Waterston. Its steep north face and severe weather make it a serious undertaking; notable routes include the Infinite Spur, first climbed in 1977 by Michael Kennedy and George Lowe.
