Butane

Butane is an alkane with the formula C4H10. It exists as two structural isomers: n-butane and isobutane (methylpropane). Butane is a colourless, highly flammable gas at room temperature that liquefies readily under modest pressure.

Uses

Liquefied butane is widely used as a portable fuel in lighters, camping stoves, and blowtorches, and as a component of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) together with propane. It also serves as a propellant in aerosol sprays, a refrigerant (isobutane, R-600a), and a feedstock in the petrochemical industry, including for the production of butadiene used in synthetic rubber.

Safety

Butane forms explosive mixtures with air. Inhalation abuse of butane gas ("huffing") is dangerous and can cause sudden death through cardiac arrhythmia and asphyxiation. Because butane vapour is heavier than air, leaks can accumulate in low-lying spaces.
