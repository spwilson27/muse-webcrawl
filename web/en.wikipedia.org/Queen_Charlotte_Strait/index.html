Queen Charlotte Strait

Queen Charlotte Strait is a strait between Vancouver Island and the mainland of British Columbia, Canada. It connects Queen Charlotte Sound to the south with Johnstone Strait to the east and forms part of the Inside Passage shipping route along the British Columbia coast.

Geography

The strait lies between the northeastern end of Vancouver Island and the mainland coast, among a maze of islands including Malcolm Island and Cormorant Island. Communities on its shores include Port McNeill, Alert Bay, and Sointula. Its waters are important for commercial fishing, aquaculture, and marine traffic, including BC Ferries routes.

Name

The strait was named in the 18th century after Queen Charlotte, consort of King George III, in the same series of namings as Queen Charlotte Sound and the Queen Charlotte Islands (now Haida Gwaii).

Ecology

The strait and surrounding waters support salmon migration routes, kelp forests, and marine mammals including orcas and humpback whales. The area is the traditional territory of Kwakwaka'wakw First Nations.
