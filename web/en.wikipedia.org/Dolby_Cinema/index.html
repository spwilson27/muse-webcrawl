Dolby Cinema

Dolby Cinema is a premium cinema concept created by Dolby Laboratories that combines Dolby proprietary technologies such as Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, as well as other signature entrance and intrinsic design features. The technology competes with IMAX and other premium large formats.

Technology

Dolby Cinema uses the Dolby Vision projection system, which uses dual modular laser projectors capable of high dynamic range with enhanced color and contrast capable of reaching high brightness levels and a contrast ratio far exceeding conventional projection. Sound is delivered through Dolby Atmos, an object-based surround sound system, and auditoria are designed to a specification covering seating, acoustics, and sightlines.

History

The first Dolby Cinema installations opened in late 2014 in the Netherlands, and the format expanded through partnerships with exhibitors including AMC Theatres in the United States. The design includes a signature entrance with a video wall themed to the film being shown.
