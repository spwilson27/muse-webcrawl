Tymshare

Tymshare, Inc.
Founded: 1964
Headquarters: Cupertino, California
Known for: Tymnet network; time-sharing services

Tymshare, Inc. was an American time-sharing computer services company founded in 1964 by Thomas O'Rourke and David Schmidt. Based in California, it sold interactive computing time on mainframes and minicomputers to businesses, universities and engineers across the United States.

Tymnet

To connect customers to its data centres cheaply, Tymshare built Tymnet, one of the first public packet-switched data networks, which grew to serve hundreds of cities internationally and was later used by many other service providers. Tymnet's virtual-circuit architecture was a contemporary of the ARPANET.

Later history

Tymshare was acquired by McDonnell Douglas in 1984; its network operations passed through later owners including British Telecom. The company is remembered as a pioneer of commercial time-sharing and public data networking.
