Paramount Television

Paramount Television was the television production division of Paramount Pictures. The original company operated from the late 1960s until 2006, producing many of American television's most successful series; the name was revived in 2013 as Paramount Television Studios, producing series for streaming platforms and cable networks.

History

Paramount entered television production by absorbing Desilu Productions in 1967, inheriting studios and series including Star Trek and Mission: Impossible. As Paramount Television it produced hits such as Happy Days, Laverne & Shirley, Cheers, Family Ties, MacGyver, Star Trek: The Next Generation, and Frasier. After the 2005 Viacom split, the library and ongoing production moved to CBS Paramount Television under CBS Corporation.

Revival

Paramount Television Studios relaunched the brand in 2013, producing series including 13 Reasons Why, Jack Ryan, The Alienist, Reacher, and Station Eleven.
