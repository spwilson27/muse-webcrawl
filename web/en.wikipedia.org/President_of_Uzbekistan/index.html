President of Uzbekistan

The President of the Republic of Uzbekistan is the head of state of Uzbekistan. The office was established on 24 March 1990 as the President of the Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic, shortly before Uzbekistan declared independence from the Soviet Union on 31 August 1991.

Islam Karimov, the former head of the Communist Party of Uzbekistan, became the country's first president and ruled for 25 years, until his death in office on 2 September 2016. Karimov's presidency was characterized by authoritarian governance and widespread human rights concerns reported by international organizations.

Shavkat Mirziyoyev

Prime Minister Shavkat Mirziyoyev served as interim president following Karimov's death and was elected president in December 2016. His administration launched a program of economic liberalization, currency reform, improved relations with neighboring Central Asian states, and the release of some political prisoners. Mirziyoyev was re-elected in October 2021 and again in July 2023, following a constitutional referendum that extended presidential terms from five to seven years.

Powers

The president is elected by popular vote, appoints and dismisses the Prime Minister subject to approval by the Oliy Majlis, heads the executive branch, and serves as commander-in-chief of the armed forces. The president may also issue decrees, resolutions and orders binding throughout the country.
