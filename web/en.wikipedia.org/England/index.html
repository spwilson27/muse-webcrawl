England

Capital: London

Population: ~57 million

Part of: United Kingdom

England is a country that is part of the United Kingdom. It is located on the island of Great Britain, sharing land borders with Scotland to the north and Wales to the west, and is the largest and most populous of the UK's constituent countries, home to London, its capital and the UK's capital.

History

Settled since prehistory, England was unified in the 10th century from Anglo-Saxon kingdoms. The Norman Conquest of 1066 transformed its language and aristocracy. England developed parliamentary government, underwent the Reformation, and in 1707 united with Scotland to form Great Britain. It was the birthplace of the Industrial Revolution, and the English language and legal system spread worldwide through the British Empire.

Geography

Low rolling countryside dominates the south and midlands, with uplands in the north (Pennines, Lake District) and west. Major rivers include the Thames, Severn and Trent. The climate is temperate maritime.

Culture and government

England has no separate devolved government; it is governed directly by the UK Parliament at Westminster. Its culture — Shakespeare, the Church of England, association football, cricket — has had global influence. Its national library is the British Library.
