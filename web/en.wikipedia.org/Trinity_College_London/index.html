Trinity College London

Formation1872
TypeExamination board / charity
HeadquartersLondon, England

Trinity College London (TCL) is an examination board based in London, England, which offers graded and diploma qualifications across a range of disciplines in the performing arts and English language learning and teaching. It is distinct from Trinity College, Cambridge and Trinity College Dublin. Trinity College London is a registered charity, and its qualifications are regulated by Ofqual and equivalent bodies. It conducts examinations in over 60 countries.

History

Trinity was founded in 1872 as part of the Church Choral Society for the reform of church music teaching, and held its first examinations in 1877, making it one of the oldest music examination boards. It began as the examinations arm of Trinity College of Music, from which it later became a separate institution.

Qualifications

Trinity offers graded music examinations (Grades 1–8) and performance diplomas in instruments, singing, and theory; graded examinations in drama, speech, and musical theatre; the "Rock & Pop" syllabus launched in 2012; and English-language qualifications including the Integrated Skills in English (ISE) and Graded Examinations in Spoken English (GESE), as well as the CertTESOL and DipTESOL teaching qualifications. Its Secure English Language Tests have been used for UK visa and immigration purposes.
