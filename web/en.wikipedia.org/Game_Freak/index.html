Game Freak

Game Freak Inc.
Founded: April 26, 1989
Founders: Satoshi Tajiri, Ken Sugimori, Junichi Masuda
Headquarters: Setagaya, Tokyo, Japan
Known for: Pokémon series

Game Freak Inc. is a Japanese video game developer and co-owner of the Pokémon franchise. Founded in 1989 by Satoshi Tajiri, Ken Sugimori and Junichi Masuda, the company began as a self-published video game fanzine of the same name, written by Tajiri and illustrated by Sugimori in the early 1980s.

History

Game Freak's first game was the puzzle game Mendel Palace (1989) for the Famicom/NES. The studio is best known for developing the main series of Pokémon role-playing games, beginning with Pokémon Red and Green in 1996, which grew into one of the highest-grossing media franchises in history. Game Freak co-owns The Pokémon Company together with Nintendo and Creatures Inc.

Other works

Outside Pokémon, Game Freak has developed titles such as Drill Dozer (2005), HarmoKnight (2012), Tembo the Badass Elephant (2015), Giga Wrecker (2017) and Little Town Hero (2019), partly through its "Gear Project" initiative encouraging developers to pitch original ideas.
