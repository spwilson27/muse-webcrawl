Lynx

Scientific classification
Kingdom: Animalia
Family: Felidae
Genus: Lynx

A lynx is any of the four species in the genus Lynx: the Eurasian lynx, Canada lynx, Iberian lynx and bobcat. They are medium-sized wild cats characterised by short tails, tufts of black hair on the tips of their ears and large padded paws.

Species

The Eurasian lynx is the largest, ranging across European and Siberian forests. The Iberian lynx, once the world's most endangered cat, has recovered from near extinction through captive breeding and reintroduction. The bobcat and Canada lynx inhabit North America.

Behaviour

Lynx are solitary predators hunting hares, deer and other mammals, relying on keen hearing and eyesight in forest and mountainous habitats.
