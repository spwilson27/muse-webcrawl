La Paz, officially Nuestra Señora de La Paz, is the seat of government of the Plurinational State of Bolivia and the administrative capital of the La Paz Department. With an estimated population of around 750,000, it is Bolivia's third-most populous city; its metropolitan area, including El Alto, exceeds two million. (Sucre is Bolivia's constitutional capital.)

Geography

La Paz is the world's highest seat of government, at an average elevation of about 3,640 metres (11,975 ft) above sea level. The city occupies a dramatic canyon carved by the Choqueyapu River below the Altiplano, with the snow-capped triple peak of Illimani (6,438 m) as its backdrop. The higher, poorer districts climb toward El Alto on the canyon rim, while affluent neighbourhoods lie lower, where the air is richer in oxygen.

Transport and culture

Since 2014 the city has been served by Mi Teleférico, the world's largest urban cable-car network, linking La Paz with El Alto. Sights include the Witches' Market (Mercado de las Brujas), selling traditional Aymara ritual goods; the colonial San Francisco Church; Plaza Murillo with the presidential palace; and the eroded pinnacles of the Valle de la Luna ("Valley of the Moon"). La Paz was founded by the Spanish conquistador Alonso de Mendoza in 1548.
