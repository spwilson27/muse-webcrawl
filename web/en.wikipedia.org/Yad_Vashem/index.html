Yad Vashem

Yad Vashem (Hebrew: 'a memorial and a name', from Isaiah 56:5) is Israel's official memorial to the victims of the Holocaust, established in 1953 by the Knesset and located on Mount Herzl in Jerusalem.

Site and work

The complex includes the Holocaust History Museum (opened 2005), the Hall of Names — which collects 'Pages of Testimony' recording millions of victims — the Hall of Remembrance, the Avenue and Garden of the Righteous Among the Nations honouring non-Jews who rescued Jews, and extensive archives, research and education institutes.

Significance

Yad Vashem is one of the most visited sites in Israel and a customary stop for visiting heads of state. Its databases of victims' names and deportation records are central resources for Holocaust remembrance and scholarship.
