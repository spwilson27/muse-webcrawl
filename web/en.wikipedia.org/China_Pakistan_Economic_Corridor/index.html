China–Pakistan Economic Corridor

The China–Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC) is a collection of infrastructure projects across Pakistan, launched in 2015 as a flagship of China's Belt and Road Initiative. It links Kashgar in Xinjiang with the Arabian Sea port of Gwadar.

Investments, initially valued at about $46 billion and later expanded, cover motorways, railways, energy plants and special economic zones, aiming to modernise Pakistan's infrastructure and give China a shorter route for Middle Eastern energy imports.
