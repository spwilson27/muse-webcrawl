USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center
Location: Flushing Meadows–Corona Park, Queens, New York City
Opened: 1978
Main stadium: Arthur Ashe Stadium (23,771 seats)

The USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center is a stadium complex in Flushing Meadows–Corona Park in Queens, New York City, home of the US Open tennis tournament, held every August and September.

Facilities

The complex includes Arthur Ashe Stadium, the largest tennis stadium in the world with a retractable roof added in 2016; Louis Armstrong Stadium; the Grandstand; and dozens of outdoor courts.

History

The center opened in 1978 when the US Open moved from Forest Hills. It was renamed in 2006 in honor of Billie Jean King, the champion and equal-rights advocate.
