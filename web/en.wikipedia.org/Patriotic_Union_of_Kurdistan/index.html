Patriotic Union of Kurdistan

The Patriotic Union of Kurdistan (PUK; Kurdish: یەکێتیی نیشتمانیی کوردستان, romanized: Yekîtiya Nîştimanî ya Kurdistanê) is a political party active in Kurdistan Region and the disputed territories in Iraq. It is traditionally considered socialist and left-leaning, and has been a member of the Socialist International. The PUK describes its goals as self-determination, human rights, democracy, and peace for the Kurdish people of Iraq.

History

The party was founded on 1 June 1975 in Damascus by Jalal Talabani and others following the collapse of the Kurdish revolt under Mustafa Barzani. It united several Kurdish factions and launched an armed insurgency against the Ba'athist regime. After the 1991 Gulf War, the PUK shared power in the new Kurdistan Region with its rival, the Kurdistan Democratic Party (KDP), although the two fought a civil war in the mid-1990s. Talabani served as President of Iraq from 2005 to 2014.

Organisation

The PUK's stronghold is the eastern part of the Kurdistan Region, centred on Sulaymaniyah, and its armed wing is part of the Peshmerga. Following Talabani's death in 2017, leadership passed through a period of contention before Bafel Talabani emerged as party president.
