The Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine (FRECA) is a Formula Regional racing series held across Europe. Launched in 2019 by the Automobile Club d'Italia (ACI Sport) and later co-organised with Alpine, it sits between Formula 4 and Formula 3 in the FIA's single-seater ladder, awarding Super Licence points to its leading finishers.

[Image: Formula Regional cars on the grid at a European circuit — hero image placeholder]

Background

The FIA's Formula Regional concept, introduced in 2018, created an intermediate formula with cars more powerful than F4 but cheaper than international F3. The European championship adopted the Tatuus F3 T-318 chassis, initially with Alfa Romeo-badged engines and, from 2021, Alpine-badged Renault units. In 2021 the series merged with the Formula Renault Eurocup, taking the "by Alpine" name and a calendar of ten rounds supporting major European events.

Champions

Early champions include Frederik Vesti (2019), Gianluca Petecof (2020), Grégoire Saucy (2021), Dino Beganovic (2022), Andrea Kimi Antonelli (2023) and Rafael Câmara (2024). Many graduates have moved on to FIA Formula 3 and Formula 2.

Format

A typical season visits circuits such as Spa-Francorchamps, Monza, the Red Bull Ring, Zandvoort and Mugello, with two races per weekend. Italian team Prema Racing has been the dominant force for much of the championship's history.
