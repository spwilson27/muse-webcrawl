Renaissance art

Renaissance art (1350–1620) is the painting, sculpture, architecture and decorative arts of the period of European history known as the Renaissance, which emerged as a distinct style in Italy in about AD 1400, in parallel with developments in philosophy, literature, music, science and technology. Renaissance art took the art of the preceding Gothic period as its foundation, but was inspired by classical antiquity and by new knowledge of nature and the human body.

Early Renaissance

The Early Renaissance (c. 1400–1495) began in Florence. Brunelleschi rediscovered linear perspective; Donatello revived naturalistic sculpture; Masaccio's frescoes in the Brancacci Chapel (1420s) gave figures weight and emotion. Botticelli's mythologies (Primavera, The Birth of Venus) embodied the Neoplatonic culture of Medici Florence. In the North, Jan van Eyck mastered oil paint and microscopic detail.

High Renaissance

The High Renaissance (c. 1495–1527) centred on Rome and is epitomised by three masters: Leonardo da Vinci (The Last Supper, Mona Lisa), Michelangelo (the Sistine Chapel ceiling, David) and Raphael (The School of Athens). Their works combined classical balance, idealised anatomy and harmonious composition. In Venice, Giorgione and Titian developed a colourito-rich painting of atmosphere and sensuality.

Late Renaissance and Mannerism

After the Sack of Rome (1527), artists such as Pontormo, Parmigianino and Tintoretto distorted proportion, space and colour in the self-conscious style called Mannerism. Northern art saw Bruegel's landscapes and peasant scenes, and architecture across Europe adopted classical orders from treatises by Serlio and Palladio.

Techniques and themes

Innovations included single-point perspective, foreshortening, sfumato and chiaroscuro; media ranged from fresco, tempera and oil on panel to bronze and marble. Subjects combined Christian iconography with classical mythology, portraiture, allegory and landscape. Giorgio Vasari's Lives of the Artists (1550/1568) canonised the period's story.
