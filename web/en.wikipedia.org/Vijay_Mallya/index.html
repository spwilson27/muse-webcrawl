Vijay Vittal Mallya (born 18 December 1955) is an Indian businessman and former Member of Parliament. He is the son of Vittal Mallya and the former chairman of the United Breweries Group, a conglomerate with interests in brewing, aviation, real estate and fertilisers. Mallya became widely known for his flamboyant lifestyle and for founding Kingfisher Airlines in 2005.

Early life and education

Mallya was born in Kolkata to a Konkani-speaking family originally from Karnataka. He was educated at La Martinière Calcutta and St. Xavier's College, Kolkata, where he graduated with a Bachelor of Commerce degree. He began working for the family business in the 1970s and interned with Hoechst AG in the United States.

Business career

After his father's death in 1983, Mallya became chairman of the United Breweries Group at the age of 28. Under his leadership, the group grew into one of India's largest alcohol companies, with the Kingfisher beer brand becoming a household name. The group acquired Shaw Wallace in 2005 and Whyte & Mackay in 2007. Mallya also owned the Force India Formula One team and the Royal Challengers Bangalore cricket team in the Indian Premier League.

Kingfisher Airlines collapse and legal cases

Kingfisher Airlines, launched in 2005, accumulated heavy losses and ceased operations in 2012. Mallya left India in March 2016 amid investigations into loan defaults of over ₹9,000 crore owed to a consortium of Indian banks. He was declared a fugitive economic offender by an Indian court in 2019, and Indian authorities have pursued his extradition from the United Kingdom.

Politics

Mallya served two terms as an independent member of the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of the Indian Parliament, representing Karnataka from 2002 to 2016, when he resigned following the initiation of ethics proceedings.
