1957 German Grand Prix

The 1957 German Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held on 4 August 1957 at the Nürburgring Nordschleife in West Germany. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest races in the sport's history, owing to Juan Manuel Fangio's extraordinary comeback drive to victory in a Maserati 250F.

Date4 August 1957
CircuitNürburgring Nordschleife (22.8 km)
Race distance22 laps, 501.8 km
WinnerJuan Manuel Fangio (Maserati)
SecondMike Hawthorn (Ferrari)
ThirdPeter Collins (Ferrari)

Race summary

Fangio, aged 46, led the early laps but a disastrous pit stop for fuel and tyres cost him around 48 seconds, dropping him far behind the Ferraris of Mike Hawthorn and Peter Collins. In what he later called the drive of his life, Fangio broke the lap record repeatedly — by as much as 15 seconds in a lap — hunting the Ferraris down on the demanding 22.8-kilometre circuit. He caught and passed both in the final laps, winning by just over three seconds over Hawthorn.

Significance

The victory was Fangio's 24th and last Formula One win, and it secured his fifth World Championship, a record that stood for nearly half a century until Michael Schumacher surpassed it. Fangio himself said he had never driven so hard, that he had taken risks beyond his usual limits, and that he had been in a kind of trance for the final laps.
