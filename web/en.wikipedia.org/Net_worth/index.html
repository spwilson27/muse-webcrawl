Net worth

Net worth is the value of all the non-financial and financial assets owned by an individual or institution minus the total of all its outstanding liabilities. Since financial assets minus outstanding liabilities equals net worth, it can also be conveniently thought of as the total assets minus total liabilities.

Calculation

Net worth is calculated by subtracting total liabilities (such as mortgages, credit card debt, loans and other obligations) from total assets (such as cash, investments, property and vehicles). If liabilities exceed assets, net worth is negative. For companies, net worth is known as book value or shareholders' equity.

Individuals

In personal finance, tracking net worth over time is a common way to measure financial progress. Wealth reports, such as those produced by central banks and statistical agencies, aggregate household net worth and study its distribution across populations.

Businesses and governments

For a business, net worth is reported on the balance sheet as equity. For governments, net worth is computed from the public sector balance sheet, comparing state-owned assets against public debt and other liabilities.
