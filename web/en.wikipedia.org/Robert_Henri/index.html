Robert Henri

Robert Henri
Born: 24 June 1865, Cincinnati, Ohio
Died: 12 July 1929, New York City
Movement: Ashcan School

Robert Henri (born Robert Henry Cozad; 1865–1929) was an American painter and teacher, a leading figure of the Ashcan School of American realism and an organiser of the exhibition of "The Eight" in 1908.

Life and work

Henri studied at the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts and in Paris. As a teacher in New York he urged his students to paint the life of the city around them — its streets, saloons, theatres and ordinary people — rather than academic subjects. In 1908 he helped organise an exhibition of eight painters at the Macbeth Galleries in protest against the conservatism of the National Academy of Design.

Influence

Henri's teaching, recorded by his students in The Art Spirit (1923), influenced a generation of American artists including George Bellows and Edward Hopper. His portraits and urban scenes helped define American realist painting in the early 20th century.
