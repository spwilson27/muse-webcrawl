Dorothy C. Stratton

Born: March 24, 1899, Brookfield, Missouri, U.S.
Died: September 17, 2006 (aged 107), West Lafayette, Indiana, U.S.
Known for: First director of the SPARS; coined the name "SPARS"

Dorothy Constance Stratton (March 24, 1899 – September 17, 2006) was an American naval officer and academic. She was the first director of the SPARS, the United States Coast Guard Women's Reserve during World War II, and coined their popular name — an acronym from the Coast Guard's motto, Semper Paratus — Always Ready. Over 10,000 enlisted women and 1,000 commissioned officers served as SPARS during the war.

Early life and academic career

Stratton was born in Brookfield, Missouri, the daughter of a Baptist minister. She earned a bachelor's degree from Ottawa University in 1920, a master's degree from the University of Chicago, and a doctorate from Columbia University. From 1933 to 1942 she was dean of women and associate professor of psychology at Purdue University.

Coast Guard service

In 1942 Stratton took a leave of absence from Purdue and was commissioned a lieutenant in the Naval Reserve's WAVES. Within months she was transferred to the Coast Guard to organize its women's reserve, which was created by an act signed by President Franklin D. Roosevelt on November 23, 1942. She was appointed its first director and promoted to commander and later captain — the first woman to hold that rank in the Coast Guard. Under her leadership the SPARS grew to more than 11,000 women who performed shore duties, freeing men for sea service. She retired in January 1946 and was awarded the Legion of Merit.

Later life

After the war Stratton returned briefly to Purdue, then served as director of personnel of the International Monetary Fund (1947–1950) and as national executive director of the Girl Scouts of the USA (1950–1960). She lived to the age of 107, dying in West Lafayette, Indiana, in 2006.

The U.S. Coast Guard named the National Security Cutter USCGC Stratton (WMSL-752) in her honour; it was christened in 2010 by First Lady Michelle Obama, the ship's sponsor. The Dorothy C. Stratton Award for leadership is presented by the Coast Guard's Women's Leadership Initiative.
