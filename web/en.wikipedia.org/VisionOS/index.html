VisionOS

visionOS is a mixed reality operating system developed by Apple for its Apple Vision Pro mixed-reality headset. Announced on June 5, 2023, at the company's Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC23), the operating system was released alongside the Vision Pro on February 2, 2024, in the United States. It is derived from Apple's other platforms, sharing frameworks with iOS and macOS, and marketed around "spatial computing".

Interface

Users interact with visionOS using eye tracking, hand gestures, and voice: looking at an element selects it, and a pinch gesture "taps" it, replacing the need for controllers. Floating windows can be placed and resized in the wearer's physical space, while "Environments" replace the surroundings with virtual scenes. A feature called EyeSight displays a rendering of the wearer's eyes on the headset's outer display. The system supports virtual Mac displays, FaceTime with spatial video, and compatibility with most iPad and iPhone apps.

Development

Applications are built with SwiftUI, RealityKit, and ARKit using Xcode, and a dedicated App Store distributes spatial apps. The system's persona feature generates a photorealistic avatar from a scan of the user's face for use in video calls. Version updates — visionOS 2 in 2024 and later releases — added features such as ultrawide Mac virtual display, spatial photo conversion, and improved guest-user modes.

Reception

Critics praised the display quality and interface sophistication of the platform while noting the headset's high price and limited app library. visionOS is Apple's first entirely new major operating system family since watchOS in 2015.
