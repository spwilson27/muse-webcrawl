Ras Laffan Industrial City (Arabic: راس لفان) is an industrial hub located 80 kilometres (50 mi) north of Doha, Qatar. Administered by QatarEnergy, it covers an area of approximately 295 square kilometres and serves as the onshore base for the processing of natural gas from the North Field, the world's largest non-associated natural gas field, which Qatar shares with Iran.

History

The city was developed beginning in the 1990s to host liquefied natural gas (LNG) plants and related industries. The first LNG train, operated by Qatargas, began production in 1996. Ras Laffan Port, commissioned in the same period, has grown into one of the largest artificial harbours and LNG export facilities in the world.

Industry

Ras Laffan hosts the LNG trains of Qatargas and RasGas (now integrated into QatarEnergy LNG), the Pearl GTL plant operated by Shell—one of the largest gas-to-liquids facilities in the world—and the Laffan Refinery, which processes condensate. Other facilities include the Barzan Gas Plant, the Dolphin Gas Project processing plant, and a shipyard operated by Nakilat. The city also has a dedicated desalination and power plant supplying utilities to its industrial tenants.

Port

Ras Laffan Port has multiple berths for LNG, liquefied petroleum gas, condensate, petroleum products and dry cargo, and is the primary export terminal for Qatari LNG, making Qatar one of the world's leading LNG exporters.
