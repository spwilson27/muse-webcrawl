Detinue

Historical writs in the action of detinue

Detinue is a common law action to recover personal property wrongfully withheld, or its value, together with damages for its detention. It lies against a person who, having come lawfully into possession (for example by bailment or finding), refuses to return the goods on demand.

History

Detinue is one of the oldest forms of action in English law, alongside debt, covenant, and later trover and replevin. Historically a defendant could wage his law, and judgment was in the alternative for the goods or their value.

Modern status

In England and Wales, detinue was abolished by the Torts (Interference with Goods) Act 1977, which created the statutory tort of wrongful interference with goods. The action survives in some other common law jurisdictions.
