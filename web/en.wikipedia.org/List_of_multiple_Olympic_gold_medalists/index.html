List of multiple Olympic gold medalists

This list ranks athletes who have won multiple gold medals at the Olympic Games (Summer and Winter combined). American swimmer Michael Phelps holds the record with 23 gold medals — more than double the next competitor. The table below shows the leading multiple gold medalists (counts from memory).
