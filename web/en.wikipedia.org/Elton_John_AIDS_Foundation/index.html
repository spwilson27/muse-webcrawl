Elton John AIDS Foundation

The Elton John AIDS Foundation (EJAF) is a nonprofit organization established by musician Elton John in 1992 in the United States, and in 1993 in the United Kingdom, to support innovative HIV prevention, education programs, direct care and support services to people living with HIV. It is one of the largest independent AIDS grant-making organizations in the world, having raised hundreds of millions of dollars to support programs in dozens of countries.

Work

The foundation funds programs addressing HIV testing, treatment, stigma reduction, and harm reduction, with a focus on marginalized communities at elevated risk. It is known for its annual Academy Awards viewing party in Los Angeles, a major fundraising event held since 1993, and its work advocating for policy change and increased government funding for the global AIDS response.
