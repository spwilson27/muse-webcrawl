Snap, crackle and pop

Snap, Crackle and Pop are the cartoon mascots of Rice Krispies, a brand of breakfast cereal marketed by Kellogg's. The three gnome-like characters take their names from the onomatopoeic sounds made by the cereal when milk is added. Snap first appeared in 1933, with Crackle and Pop joining shortly after, drawn originally by illustrator Vernon Grant.

Physics usage

In physics, "snap, crackle and pop" are also used informally as names for the fourth, fifth and sixth derivatives of position with respect to time, after displacement, velocity, acceleration and jerk. The fourth derivative is snap (or jounce), the fifth is crackle, and the sixth is pop — a playful naming inspired by the cereal mascots.

Characters

Snap wears a chef's hat, Crackle a red stocking cap, and Pop a yellow band leader's cap; the trio have featured in decades of advertising, singing the jingle "Snap! Crackle! Pop! Rice Krispies!" In some markets overseas, translations and names vary.
