Ian James Thorpe (born 13 October 1982) is an Australian retired freestyle swimmer who won five Olympic gold medals, the most by an Australian at the time of his retirement. Nicknamed the "Thorpedo," he dominated middle-distance freestyle in the early 2000s.

Career

Thorpe became the youngest male world champion in history when he won the 400 m freestyle at the 1998 World Championships aged 15. At the Sydney 2000 Olympics he won three gold and two silver medals; at Athens 2004 he won the celebrated "Race of the Century" in the 200 m freestyle ahead of Pieter van den Hoogenband and Michael Phelps. He held the 400 m freestyle world record for nearly a decade.

Later life

Thorpe announced his retirement in 2006, made a brief comeback attempt in 2011 that ended without selection for the London 2012 team, and has since worked in broadcasting and charity, including youth mental-health advocacy.
