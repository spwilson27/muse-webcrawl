Mitsubishi Motors

Native name: 三菱自動車工業株式会社

Industry: Automotive

Founded: 22 April 1970

Headquarters: Minato, Tokyo, Japan

Mitsubishi Motors Corporation is a Japanese multinational automobile manufacturer headquartered in Minato, Tokyo, Japan. In 2011, Mitsubishi Motors was the sixth-largest Japanese automaker and the 19th-largest worldwide by production. Since October 2016, Mitsubishi has been one-third (34%) owned by Nissan, and included in the Renault–Nissan–Mitsubishi Alliance.

History

Mitsubishi's automotive origins date to 1917, when the Mitsubishi Shipbuilding Co. built the Model A, Japan's first series-production automobile. The modern company was formed in 1970 when the automobile division of Mitsubishi Heavy Industries was spun off. Mitsubishi vehicles were first sold in the United States in 1971 through an arrangement with Chrysler, which took a 15 percent stake in the company.

Well-known models have included the Pajero (Montero/Shogun) SUV, the Lancer Evolution sports sedan, the Outlander crossover, and the i-MiEV, one of the first mass-produced highway-capable electric vehicles. The brand achieved considerable motorsport success, winning multiple World Rally Championships with the Lancer Evolution and numerous Dakar Rally victories with the Pajero.

Fuel economy scandal

In April 2016, Mitsubishi admitted it had falsified fuel-economy test data for mini-cars built for itself and Nissan. The revelation cut the company's market value sharply and led Nissan to acquire its controlling stake later that year.
