Library of Congress

The Library of Congress (LC or LOC) is the research library that officially serves the United States Congress and is the de facto national library of the United States. Founded in 1800, it is among the largest libraries in the world.

Established24 April 1800
LocationWashington, D.C., USA

LibrarianLibrarian of Congress

History

After British troops burned the Capitol in 1814, Thomas Jefferson sold his personal library to restart the collection. The Thomas Jefferson Building opened in 1897. See library.

Collections and services

Holdings include books in hundreds of languages, manuscripts, maps, photographs, recordings and the U.S. Copyright Office. Its classification system is used worldwide.

Buildings

The library occupies the Jefferson, Adams and Madison buildings on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C.
