Blockade runners of the American Civil War

Blockade runners
Period: 1861 – 1865
Purpose: Evading the Union blockade
Bases: Nassau, Bermuda, Havana

Blockade runners of the American Civil War were fast steamships that evaded the Union Navy's blockade of Confederate ports to export cotton and import arms, ammunition and manufactured goods the Confederacy could not produce.

Operations

Runners operated from neutral ports such as Nassau in the Bahamas, Bermuda and Havana, making night runs into Wilmington, Charleston and Galveston. Purpose-built vessels were low, sleek and painted grey to reduce visibility.

Impact

Although the majority of attempts early in the war succeeded, the tightening blockade steadily reduced traffic. Blockade running supplied vital materiel but could not sustain the Confederate war economy.
