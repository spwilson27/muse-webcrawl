James Mills (ship owner)

Born30 July 1847, Wellington, New Zealand
Died23 January 1936, Dunedin, New Zealand
OccupationShipping magnate, politician
Known forFounder of the Union Steam Ship Company

Sir James Mills (30 July 1847 – 23 January 1936) was a New Zealand businessman and politician who founded the Union Steam Ship Company, which grew into the dominant shipping line of New Zealand and one of the largest in the Southern Hemisphere.

Career

Mills joined the shipping firm of Johnny Jones in Dunedin as a young man. In 1875 he founded the Union Steam Ship Company of New Zealand, building a fleet that dominated coastal trade and trans-Tasman routes to Australia, later extending services across the Pacific.

Politics and honours

Mills represented Port Chalmers in the New Zealand Parliament from 1887 to 1893. He was knighted in 1907 and appointed KCMG in 1909, the first New Zealand-born businessman to receive such honours. The company he founded was eventually absorbed into P&O in the 1970s.
