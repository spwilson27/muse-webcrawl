Ulster Volunteer Force

Ulster Volunteer Force (UVF)
Dates active: 1966–present (ceasefire since 1994)
Ideology: Ulster loyalism
Area: Northern Ireland
Status: Proscribed terrorist organisation (UK)

The Ulster Volunteer Force (UVF) is an Ulster loyalist paramilitary group based in Northern Ireland. Formed in 1966, it took its name from the original Ulster Volunteers of 1912. Its stated purpose was to combat Irish republicanism, particularly the Provisional IRA.

The Troubles

During the Troubles the UVF carried out bombings and sectarian murders, and was responsible for roughly 500 deaths, the majority of them Catholic civilians. Its attacks included the 1971 McGurk's Bar bombing and involvement in the 1974 Dublin and Monaghan bombings.

Ceasefire

The UVF declared a ceasefire in 1994 and in 2007 announced it was ending its armed campaign and putting weapons beyond use, though splinter criminal activity persisted.
