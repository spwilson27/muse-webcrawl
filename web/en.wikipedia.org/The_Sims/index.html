The Sims
Genre: Life simulation
Developer: Maxis
Publisher: Electronic Arts
Creator: Will Wright
First release: The Sims — February 4, 2000
Latest release: The Sims 4 — September 2, 2014

The Sims is a series of life simulation video games developed by Maxis and published by Electronic Arts. Created by Will Wright, the franchise has sold over 200 million copies worldwide and is one of the best-selling video game series of all time.

Gameplay

Players create virtual people called Sims, place them in houses, and direct their lives — meeting needs such as hunger, comfort and social interaction, pursuing careers and relationships. The games are largely sandbox, without defined goals, and Sims speak the fictional language Simlish.

Games

The main entries are The Sims (2000), The Sims 2 (2004), The Sims 3 (2009) and The Sims 4 (2014), each supported by numerous expansion and stuff packs. Spin-offs include The Sims Online, MySims and mobile titles.

Reception and legacy

The original game surpassed Myst as the best-selling PC game of its time, appealed to a large female audience unusual for the era, and influenced the life-simulation genre. A next-generation Sims project, codenamed Project Rene, has been announced.
