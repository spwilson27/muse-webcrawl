Nettuno

Nettuno is a town and comune of the Metropolitan City of Rome in the Lazio region of central Italy, on the Tyrrhenian Sea coast. It has a population of roughly 50,000 and lies immediately south of the neighbouring town of Anzio, with which it forms a continuous coastal settlement about 60 kilometres south of Rome.

History

The area was inhabited in antiquity; nearby stood the Volscian and later Roman coastal settlement associated with Antium (modern Anzio). The medieval town grew around a castle and the church of San Giovanni. Nettuno's historic centre, the Borgo Medievale, preserves narrow streets and a seafront fortress. The Forte Sangallo, a Renaissance fortress built in the early 16th century to designs associated with Antonio da Sangallo the Elder, guards the harbour.

Sights and events

Nettuno is a popular seaside resort with sandy beaches, a marina and a busy promenade. It is also known for the Sicily–Rome American Cemetery and Memorial just north of the town, where thousands of American servicemen who died in the Italian campaign of the Second World War are buried. The town is associated with Saint Maria Goretti, who was born in Corinaldo but lived and was fatally attacked near Nettuno in 1902; her shrine is in the town.

Sport

Nettuno is a historic centre of Italian baseball; its club, Nettuno Baseball Club, founded in the wake of the American presence after the war, is among the most successful in Italian baseball history.
