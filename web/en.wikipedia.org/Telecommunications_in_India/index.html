Telecommunications in India

Telecommunications in India is one of the largest telecom markets in the world, with well over a billion telephone subscriptions, the overwhelming majority of them mobile. India also has among the cheapest mobile data prices globally and one of the largest internet user bases.

History

Telegraphy began in India in 1851 and telephones followed in 1882, with services run as a government monopoly after independence through the Department of Telecommunications. Liberalisation began in the 1990s: the National Telecom Policy of 1994 admitted private operators, and the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) was established in 1997. Mobile subscriptions grew explosively in the 2000s.

Market

The market is dominated by Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel and Vodafone Idea (Vi), with state-owned BSNL and MTNL also operating. Jio's 2016 launch of cheap 4G data transformed usage, making India the largest consumer of mobile data. India began rolling out 5G services in 2022.
