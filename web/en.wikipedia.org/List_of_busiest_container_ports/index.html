List of busiest container ports

This is a list of the world's busiest container ports, ranked by container throughput measured in twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU). Asian ports dominate the rankings: the Port of Shanghai has been the world's busiest since 2010, handling over 47 million TEU annually in recent years, followed by Singapore, Ningbo-Zhoushan and Shenzhen. Throughput figures vary slightly between sources (such as Lloyd's List and the American Association of Port Authorities) depending on counting methods for transshipment traffic.

Outside Asia, the largest ports are Rotterdam in Europe (around 13–14 million TEU, long Europe's busiest) and Los Angeles/Long Beach in North America (roughly 9–10 million TEU each at the adjacent San Pedro Bay complex). Singapore's volume is overwhelmingly transshipment — containers transferred between ships — whereas Chinese ports handle large volumes of domestic exports and imports. Historically, Hong Kong and Singapore alternated at the top through the 1990s and 2000s before mainland Chinese ports surged with China's manufacturing growth.
