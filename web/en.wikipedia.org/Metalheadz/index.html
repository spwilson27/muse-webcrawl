Metalheadz

Metalheadz is a drum and bass record label based in the United Kingdom. It was founded in 1994 by Kemistry & Storm and Goldie. The label is widely regarded as one of the most influential in drum and bass, known for a dark, futuristic and often experimental sound, meticulous production values and distinctive artwork.

History

Goldie had come to prominence with the 1994 single "Inner City Life" and the 1995 album Timeless. Metalheadz began as an outlet for like-minded producers at a time when the jungle scene was fragmenting. Its famous Sunday sessions at the Blue Note club in Hoxton Square, London (1995-1997) became legendary: DJs including Goldie, Kemistry & Storm, Doc Scott, Grooverider, Fabio and Photek played there, and the night is credited with shaping the "intelligent" and techstep directions of drum and bass.

Artists and releases

Releases have come from Photek, Dillinja, Source Direct, J Majik, Adam F, Ed Rush & Optical, Total Science, Spirit, Digital, Commix, Lenzman, Ulterior Motive, Blocks & Escher and many others. The Platinum Breakz compilation series (from 1996) showcased the roster, and the label has continued through sub-labels and series such as Metalheadz Platinum and the METH catalogue, remaining active into the 2020s.

Legacy

Metalheadz is routinely cited in histories of UK electronic music for taking drum and bass production seriously as an album and artist medium, and for its visual identity, which drew on Goldie's background in graffiti art.
