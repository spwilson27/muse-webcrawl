Resurrection

The resurrection of the dead is a belief, held in the Abrahamic religions and Zoroastrianism, that the dead will be brought back to life at the end of time. In Christianity it is linked to the resurrection of Jesus and to a general resurrection and final judgment; in Judaism it appears in the Book of Daniel and rabbinic teaching; in Islam, Yawm al-Qiyāmah is the Day of Resurrection and Judgment.

Concepts

Beliefs differ over whether resurrection is of the body, the soul, or both, and whether it is universal or reserved for the righteous. Philosophers and theologians have long debated the identity problem: how a reassembled or recreated body could be the same person. Some traditions instead teach reincarnation, in which the soul is reborn in a new body.

Individual resurrections

Scriptures also describe individual returns from death, such as Lazarus and the daughter of Jairus in the New Testament, distinct from the eschatological general resurrection.
