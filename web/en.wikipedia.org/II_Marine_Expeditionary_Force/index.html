The II Marine Expeditionary Force (II MEF) is a Marine Air-Ground Task Force of the United States Marine Corps, headquartered at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune, North Carolina. It is one of three Marine expeditionary forces and provides forces principally to the U.S. European, Central, and Southern commands.

Subordinate units

II MEF comprises the 2nd Marine Division, the 2nd Marine Aircraft Wing, the 2nd Marine Logistics Group, and the 22nd, 24th, and 26th Marine Expeditionary Units, which deploy aboard U.S. Navy amphibious ships.

History

Elements of II MEF have served in operations including Desert Storm, the Iraq War — where II MEF commanded Multi-National Force West in Anbar Province — and the war in Afghanistan.
