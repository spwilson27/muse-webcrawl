Brand

Branding distinguishes products in the marketplace

A brand is a name, term, design, symbol, or any other feature that distinguishes one seller's goods or services from those of other sellers. Brands are used in business, marketing, and advertising for recognition and to create and store value as brand equity for the object branded, to the benefit of the brand's customers, its owners, and shareholders.

History

The practice originates in branding livestock with a hot iron to indicate ownership; the word derives from the Old Norse brandr, "to burn". Marks identifying makers appear on ancient goods from Egypt, China, and Rome. Modern brand management emerged in the 19th and 20th centuries with mass-produced packaged goods and companies such as Procter & Gamble.

Concepts

Key concepts include brand identity, brand image, brand awareness, brand loyalty, and brand equity — the commercial value derived from consumer perception of the brand. A brand is protected through trademarks. "Branding" also extends to people (personal branding), places, and organisations.
