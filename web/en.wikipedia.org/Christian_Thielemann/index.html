Christian Thielemann

Christian Thielemann (born 1959) is a German conductor noted for interpretations of Wagner, Strauss and Bruckner.

Career

Born in Berlin, Thielemann assisted Herbert von Karajan and Daniel Barenboim, became Generalmusikdirektor of the Deutsche Oper Berlin and then of the Munich Philharmonic, and served as chief conductor of the Staatskapelle Dresden from 2012 to 2024. He is a regular at Bayreuth and Salzburg and frequently conducts the Vienna Philharmonic, including its New Year's Concerts.

Style

His conducting is associated with the German Romantic tradition, weighty sonority and long-arching phrasing.
