Minato Mirai 21

CityYokohama, Kanagawa, Japan
Areaabout 186 hectares
LandmarkYokohama Landmark Tower

Minato Mirai 21 (みなとみらい21) is a seaside urban district in central Yokohama, Japan, developed on reclaimed land and former shipyards and rail yards along the waterfront; its name means "harbor of the future in the 21st century".

Development

Construction began in 1983 as a master-planned project to revitalize Yokohama's port area. The district combines office towers, shopping malls, hotels, museums, and convention facilities, notably the Pacifico Yokohama complex.

Landmarks

The Yokohama Landmark Tower (296 m), completed in 1993, was Japan's tallest building until 2014 and contains a fast elevator, hotel, and observation deck. Other sights include the Cosmo World Ferris wheel, the Red Brick Warehouse shopping complex, the Nippon Maru museum ship, and Yokohama Museum of Art.
