The Raška District (Serbian: Рашки округ, Raški okrug) is one of the administrative districts of Serbia, located in the southwestern part of the country within the Šumadija and Western Serbia statistical region. Its administrative centre is the city of Kraljevo.

Municipalities

The district comprises the cities of Kraljevo and Novi Pazar and the municipalities of Raška, Tutin, and Vrnjačka Banja. It is named after the historical Raška region and the medieval Serbian state of Rascia.

Demographics

According to recent censuses, the district has roughly 185,000–200,000 inhabitants. Serbs form the majority overall, while Bosniaks constitute a large share — and in Novi Pazar and Tutin a majority — of the population. The district contains some of Serbia's most important medieval monuments, including the monasteries of Studenica, Sopoćani, and Žiča, and the spa town of Vrnjačka Banja.
