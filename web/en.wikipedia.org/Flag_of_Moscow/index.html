Flag of Moscow

Proportion2:3
Adopted1 February 1995
DesignDark red field with Saint George slaying the dragon in the centre

The flag of Moscow, the capital and federal city of Russia, is a dark red banner of arms: it depicts the city's heraldic emblem, Saint George in silver armour and a blue cloak, mounted on a silver horse and slaying a black dragon with a golden spear.

Symbolism and history

Saint George has been associated with Moscow since the medieval period, appearing on the seals of its princes from the fourteenth century onward, and the rider slaying the serpent became the emblem of Muscovy before being incorporated into the arms of the Russian Empire. The modern flag, based on the city's coat of arms, was adopted by law on 1 February 1995.
