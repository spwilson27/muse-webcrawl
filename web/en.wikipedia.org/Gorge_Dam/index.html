Gorge Dam

The Gorge Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the Cuyahoga River in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio, United States, within the Gorge Metro Park between Akron and Cuyahoga Falls. Built in 1911–1912 by the Northern Ohio Traction and Light Company, the dam stands about 17 metres (57 feet) high and created a reservoir that supplied cooling water and hydroelectric power.

The dam became a focus of environmental debate because it blocks fish passage and traps contaminated sediments from the river's industrial past, while the reservoir above it largely hides the natural gorge and waterfalls behind it. Plans developed in the 2010s and 2020s to remove the dam, manage the accumulated sediment and restore free flow of the Cuyahoga River through the gorge.
