Gómez Palacio, Durango

Gómez Palacio
State: Durango, Mexico
Founded: 1885
Named for: Francisco Gómez Palacio
Part of: La Laguna metropolitan area

Gómez Palacio is a city and municipality in the north-eastern part of the Mexican state of Durango. Together with Torreón and Matamoros in Coahuila and Lerdo in Durango, it forms the La Laguna metropolitan area, one of the largest urban agglomerations in northern Mexico.

History

The city was founded in 1885 on lands of the Hacienda de Santa Rosa, growing rapidly with the arrival of the railway and the cotton boom of the Comarca Lagunera. It was named for Francisco Gómez Palacio, a former governor of Durango and jurist. During the Mexican Revolution the region was a major theatre of operations.

Economy

Gómez Palacio is an industrial and agro-processing centre, historically associated with cotton and today with dairy production, textiles, and manufacturing in its industrial parks. The Nazas River, whose waters irrigate the Laguna district, passes nearby.
