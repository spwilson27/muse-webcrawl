Chuck E. Cheese is an American chain of family entertainment centres and restaurants, operated by CEC Entertainment. The centres combine a pizza restaurant with arcade games, amusement rides and animatronic stage shows, and are a popular venue for children's birthday parties. The chain's mascot is Chuck E. Cheese, an anthropomorphic mouse (originally a rat).

History

The first location, Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre, opened in San Jose, California in 1977. It was founded by Nolan Bushnell, co-founder of Atari, who wanted a place to showcase arcade games to families and developed the concept of animatronic entertainment using technology descended from his Pizza Time Theatre venture. After bankruptcy in 1984, the chain was bought by its chief rival, ShowBiz Pizza Place, which eventually converted all locations to the Chuck E. Cheese brand.

Format

Guests historically bought tokens for arcade and redemption games, exchanging prize tickets for toys; most locations have since shifted to card-based systems. Animatronic bands such as Munch's Make Believe Band performed at many restaurants for decades; beginning with a 2017 remodel programme, the company removed animatronics from most locations in favour of dance floors, a change completed as the chain modernised. CEC Entertainment filed for bankruptcy in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic and emerged later that year.
