The Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional (Portuguese Professional Football League), commonly known as Liga Portugal or LPFP, is the governing body responsible for administering and organising the professional football competitions in Portugal. Founded in 1978, it is headquartered in Porto and is an associate member accountable to the Portuguese Football Federation.

Competitions

The LPFP organises the Primeira Liga (Liga Portugal Betclic), the top tier of Portuguese football, and the Liga Portugal 2, the second tier. In 2007–08 it introduced a third competition, the Taça da Liga (Allianz Cup), a league cup open to clubs in the two professional divisions.

Governance

The league is run by a president elected by member clubs; notable presidents have included Valentim Loureiro, Hermínio Loureiro, Mário Figueiredo and Pedro Proença, a former international referee elected in 2015. The body negotiates central television and sponsorship contracts, regulates club licensing and financial fair play for the professional game, and appoints referees for its competitions through the federation's arbitration council.
