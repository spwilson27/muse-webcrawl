Chlorhexidine

Chlorhexidine
Class: Antiseptic / disinfectant (bisbiguanide)
Uses: Skin disinfection, mouthwash, surgical scrub
WHO: On the List of Essential Medicines

Chlorhexidine is a disinfectant and antiseptic used for skin disinfection before surgery, sterilisation of surgical instruments, wound care and dental hygiene. It is effective against a wide range of bacteria and some fungi and viruses.

Uses

Chlorhexidine gluconate mouthwash is used to treat gingivitis and reduce dental plaque. In hospitals it is used in hand scrubs, patient bathing to reduce infections, and impregnated dressings and catheters. Its residual activity—binding to skin and oral tissues—prolongs its effect.

Safety

Side effects include tooth staining and altered taste with oral use, skin irritation, and rare but serious allergic reactions including anaphylaxis. It is harmful to the eyes and middle ear and is inactivated by some soaps and organic matter.
