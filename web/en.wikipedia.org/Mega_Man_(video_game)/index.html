Mega Man (video game)

Developer: Capcom
Publisher: Capcom
Release: December 17, 1987 (JP)
Platform: Nintendo Entertainment System
Genre: Action, platform

Mega Man, known as Rockman in Japan, is a 1987 action-platform video game developed and published by Capcom for the Nintendo Entertainment System. It was the first game in the original Mega Man series and the entire Mega Man franchise. The plot follows the humanoid robot Mega Man and his quest to save the world from mad scientist Dr. Wily, who has reprogrammed six Robot Masters to aid in his plans for world domination.

Gameplay

The player can attempt any of six stages in any order, each ending in a boss fight with a Robot Master — Cut Man, Guts Man, Ice Man, Bomb Man, Fire Man and Elec Man. Defeating a boss grants Mega Man its special weapon, and each boss is weak to another's weapon, giving the game a rock-paper-scissors structure. After all six, the player proceeds through Dr. Wily's fortress in a set sequence. The game is noted for its high difficulty and its distinctive cover art in North America, which inaccurately depicts Mega Man.

Development and legacy

The game was designed by Akira Kitamura with character design by Keiji Inafune. Although initial sales were modest, it spawned a sequel, Mega Man 2 (1988), which became a major success; the franchise grew to dozens of games across multiple subseries, making Mega Man one of Capcom's flagship characters.
