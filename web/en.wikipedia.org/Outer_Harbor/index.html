Outer Harbor

Outer Harbor is a suburb of Adelaide, South Australia, located at the northern tip of the LeFevre Peninsula on the eastern shore of Gulf St Vincent. It is the location of the main container terminal and passenger shipping facilities for the Port of Adelaide.

History and facilities

The deep-water harbor was opened in 1908 to accommodate larger steamships that could not navigate the shallower Port River. An outer harbor breakwater and wharves were constructed, and the suburb was connected to Adelaide by rail; the Outer Harbor railway line still serves a station near the passenger terminal. The Osborne berths nearby host shipbuilding, notably Australia's Collins-class submarine construction at Osborne.

Port today

Operated within Flinders Ports' network, Outer Harbor handles container traffic (Flinders Adelaide Container Terminal), bulk grain, vehicles, and cruise ship visits at the Outer Harbor passenger terminal. The surrounding area includes the Torrens Island power station across the Port River, marinas, and beachside housing on the peninsula.
