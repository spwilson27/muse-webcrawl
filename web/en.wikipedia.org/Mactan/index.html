Mactan

Mactan is a densely populated island located a few kilometres east of Cebu Island in the Philippines. It is part of Cebu Province and is divided into the city of Lapu-Lapu and the municipality of Cordova. The island is connected to Cebu by bridges spanning the Mactan Channel, and it hosts Mactan–Cebu International Airport, one of the busiest airports in the Philippines.

History

Mactan is famous as the site of the Battle of Mactan on 27 April 1521, in which warriors led by the chieftain Lapulapu defeated the Spanish expedition of Ferdinand Magellan, who was killed in the fighting. The battle is commemorated at the Mactan Shrine in Lapu-Lapu City, and Lapulapu is celebrated as one of the earliest Filipino heroes to resist foreign invasion.

Economy and tourism

The island is known for beach resorts, diving, and island-hopping tourism along its eastern coast, as well as for export-processing zones, furniture making, and the manufacture of guitars and ukuleles. Its proximity to Cebu City makes it part of the Metro Cebu metropolitan area.
