SAVE Britain's Heritage is a British conservation organisation and pressure group that campaigns to save historic buildings from demolition and decay. It was founded in 1975, European Architectural Heritage Year, by the journalist and architectural historian Marcus Binney and a group of architects, journalists and planners.

Activities

SAVE publishes reports, catalogues and a regularly updated register of buildings at risk, highlighting endangered country houses, churches, mills, civic buildings and other structures. It campaigns through the media, public inquiries and the courts, often proposing alternative schemes that reuse threatened buildings rather than demolishing them.

Notable campaigns

The organisation's early campaigns helped save the Barlaston Hall in Staffordshire and campaigned against the demolition of historic buildings across Britain. It has campaigned for the preservation of country houses, industrial heritage and post-war architecture, and against proposals it considers damaging to historic city centres, including in Liverpool, London and elsewhere.
