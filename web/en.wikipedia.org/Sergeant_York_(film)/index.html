Sergeant York (film)

Directed byHoward Hawks
StarringGary Cooper, Walter Brennan, Joan Leslie
Released1941
StudioWarner Bros.

Sergeant York is a 1941 American biographical film directed by Howard Hawks about the life of Alvin C. York, the highly decorated American soldier of the First World War. Gary Cooper stars as York, a Tennessee farmer and conscientious objector who, during the Meuse–Argonne offensive of 1918, single-handedly captured 132 German soldiers.

Reception

Released shortly before the United States entered the Second World War, the film was the highest-grossing picture of 1941 and was seen as an encouragement to enlistment. Cooper won the Academy Award for Best Actor, and the film received eleven Oscar nominations, also winning for Best Film Editing.
