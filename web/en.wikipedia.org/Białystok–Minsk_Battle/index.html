Battle of Białystok–Minsk

Date22 June – 9 July 1941
LocationPolish–Belarusian border region, USSR
Part ofOperation Barbarossa, Eastern Front
ResultGerman victory; double encirclement of Soviet forces

The Battle of Białystok–Minsk was a major German encirclement battle in the opening phase of Operation Barbarossa, the invasion of the Soviet Union, fought from 22 June to 9 July 1941.

Course of the battle

Panzer groups of Army Group Centre under Heinz Guderian and Hermann Hoth broke through the Soviet frontier defences and closed pincers first around Białystok and then around Minsk, trapping much of the Soviet Western Front in two pockets. Despite fierce resistance, the pockets were eliminated by early July.

Outcome

The Red Army suffered catastrophic losses, with several hundred thousand soldiers captured and thousands of tanks and artillery pieces destroyed. The victory opened the road towards Smolensk and Moscow, though the scale of Soviet resistance and reserves would soon slow the German advance.
