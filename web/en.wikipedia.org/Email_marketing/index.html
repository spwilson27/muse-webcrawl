Email marketing

Email marketing is the act of sending a commercial message, typically to a group of people, using email. In its broadest sense, every email sent to a potential or current customer could be considered email marketing. It involves using email to send advertisements, request business, or solicit sales or donations, and is meant to build loyalty, trust, or brand awareness.

Types and practice

Email marketing can be carried out through newsletters, promotional campaigns, transactional messages (such as order confirmations) and automated sequences triggered by customer behaviour. Marketers typically seek the consent of recipients (permission marketing), and laws such as the US CAN-SPAM Act and the European GDPR regulate unsolicited bulk email. Performance is measured through metrics such as open rates, click-through rates and conversions.
