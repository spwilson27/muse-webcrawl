CNN

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

CNN (Cable News Network) is an American multinational news channel and website headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. Founded on June 1, 1980, by media proprietor Ted Turner and Reese Schonfeld, it was the first television channel to provide 24-hour news coverage and the first all-news television channel in the United States.

History

CNN gained prominence with its live coverage of events such as the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster (1986) and especially the Gulf War of 1991, when its correspondents reported live from Baghdad during the opening bombing — cementing the "CNN effect" on global politics and diplomacy. CNN International launched in 1985, and sister channels such as Headline News (HLN) followed.

Organisation and reach

The network is a division of Warner Bros. Discovery. It operates bureaus around the world, and its US channel is available in tens of millions of pay-television households, while CNN International reaches hundreds of millions of households globally. Its digital properties are among the most-visited news websites.

Reception

CNN has won numerous journalism awards, including Peabody and Emmy Awards, while also facing recurring criticism and debates over perceived bias, ratings competition with Fox News and MSNBC, and the pressures of the 24-hour news cycle.
