Philokalia

A manuscript of the Philokalia on Mount Athos

The Philokalia is the great anthology of Orthodox spirituality, spanning authors from the Egyptian desert of the 4th century to the hesychasts of 14th-century Byzantium. Its title means "love of the beautiful" — the beauty being the divine life into which prayer leads.

Compilation

The collection was assembled on Mount Athos by Saint Nicodemus the Hagiorite, working with Saint Macarius of Corinth, from manuscripts preserved in Athonite libraries; it was printed at Venice in 1782 through the patronage of John Mavrogordato.

Contents

Among its most influential selections are the writings On Watchfulness and the Prayer of Jesus, the Centuries of Maximus the Confessor, the works of Peter of Damascus, and the treatises of Gregory Palamas defending the vision of the uncreated light. Its teaching shaped the Orthodox revival of the 18th–20th centuries and the practice of the Jesus Prayer worldwide.
