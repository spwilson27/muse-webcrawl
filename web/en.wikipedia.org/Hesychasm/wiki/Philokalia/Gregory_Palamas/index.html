Gregory Palamas

Saint Gregory Palamas, defender of the hesychasts

Gregory Palamas (c. 1296 – 1359), Athonite monk and Archbishop of Thessalonica, is remembered as the saint who articulated the theology of the uncreated energies and of the Tabor Light. Summoned from Mount Athos to answer Barlaam of Calabria's attacks on the hesychasts, he produced the Triads in Defence of the Holy Hesychasts, the classic statement of Orthodox mystical theology.

Doctrine

His central claim is that the distinction between God's essence and energies is real but does not divide God: the saints participate in the energies — the divine life itself — while the essence remains imparticipable. This grounds the doctrine of theosis and the possibility of seeing the uncreated light of the Transfiguration with the bodily eyes, transfigured by grace.

Veneration

Canonised in 1368, Palamas is commemorated on 14 November and on the Second Sunday of Lent, when the Orthodox Church celebrates the triumph of his teaching.
