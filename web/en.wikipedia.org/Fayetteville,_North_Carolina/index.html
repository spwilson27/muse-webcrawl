Fayetteville, North Carolina

Fayetteville is a city in and the county seat of Cumberland County, North Carolina, United States. Its population was about 209,000 at the 2020 census. It is best known as the home of Fort Liberty (formerly Fort Bragg), one of the largest US Army installations in the world.

Settled in the 18th century at the head of navigation on the Cape Fear River, Fayetteville was named in 1783 for the Marquis de Lafayette — the first US city named for him. North Carolina's constitutional convention met there in 1789 and ratified the US Constitution.
