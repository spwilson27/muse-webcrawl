Circular mil

A circular mil is a unit of area used chiefly in the United States and Canada to specify the cross-sectional area of electrical wire and cable. It is defined as the area of a circle whose diameter is one mil (one thousandth of an inch, 0.001 in). One circular mil is approximately 5.067 × 10−4 mm².

Use

The area of a round wire in circular mils is simply the square of its diameter in mils, which avoids any calculation involving π. Wire sizes in the American wire gauge system are commonly tabulated in circular mils. For large conductors, sizes are given in thousands of circular mils, abbreviated kcmil (formerly MCM); standard building-wire sizes range up to 1,000 kcmil and beyond.

Conversion

The area in square millimetres is obtained by multiplying the circular-mil value by about 0.0005067. Conversely, one square millimetre equals about 1,973 circular mils.
