Drishyam (transl. Visual) is a 2013 Indian Malayalam-language crime thriller film written and directed by Jeethu Joseph and starring Mohanlal. It tells the story of Georgekutty, a cable-TV operator and film buff in a rural Kerala village, whose family becomes entangled in the disappearance of Varun Prabhakar, the son of a senior police officer; drawing on plot devices absorbed from a lifetime of movies, Georgekutty constructs an elaborate alibi to protect them.

Reception and legacy

Drishyam was a major critical and commercial success, becoming at the time the highest-grossing Malayalam film ever, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest Indian thrillers. It inspired an exceptional number of remakes: in Telugu (Drushyam), Tamil (Papanasam, with Kamal Haasan), Kannada (Drishya), Hindi (Drishyam, 2015, with Ajay Devgn), Sinhala, and Chinese (Sheep Without a Shepherd). A sequel, Drishyam 2, was released on Amazon Prime Video in 2021 to further acclaim, and itself spawned remakes.
