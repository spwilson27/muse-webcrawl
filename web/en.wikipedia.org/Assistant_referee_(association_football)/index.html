Assistant referee (association football)

In association football, an assistant referee (also known as a linesman or lineswoman) is an official empowered to assist the referee in enforcing the Laws of the Game during a match. Two assistant referees are appointed to each match, patrolling opposite touchlines, with duties defined under Law 6 of the Laws of the Game.

Duties

The assistant referee indicates, using a flag, when the ball has left the field of play and which team is entitled to the restart; when an offside offence has occurred; when a substitution is requested; and when misconduct or incidents have occurred out of the view of the referee. Assistants also help with penalty-kick monitoring, standing at the intersection of the goal line and penalty area.

Other officials

Modern professional matches may also appoint a fourth official, an additional assistant referee on each goal line (in some competitions), and a video assistant referee (VAR) team. The assistant referee's decisions are advisory; the referee remains the final authority.

History

Linesmen originally focused on touchline decisions and were often supplied by the competing clubs. They became neutral assistant referees with broader duties, and the title was formally changed from linesman to assistant referee in 1996.
