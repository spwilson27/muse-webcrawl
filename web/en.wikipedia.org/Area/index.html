Area

Area is the measure of a region's size on a surface. The area of a plane region or plane area refers to the area of a shape or planar lamina, while surface area refers to the area of an open surface or the boundary of a three-dimensional object.

Units

Area is measured in square units, such as square metres (m²) in the International System of Units. Other units include the hectare, the acre, and the square kilometre.

Formulas

For a rectangle, area equals length times width; for a triangle, half the base times the height; for a circle, π times the radius squared. The areas of irregular shapes can be found by decomposition, by the method of exhaustion developed in ancient Greek mathematics, or by integration in calculus.

History

The concept arose from practical needs such as land measurement in ancient Egypt and Babylon. Archimedes computed areas bounded by parabolas and circles with remarkable precision, anticipating integral calculus.
