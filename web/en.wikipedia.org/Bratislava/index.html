Bratislava

Bratislava is the capital and largest city of Slovakia, with a population of roughly 480,000. It lies in southwestern Slovakia on the Danube, bordering Austria and Hungary — the only national capital that borders two sovereign states. Historically known as Pressburg (German) and Pozsony (Hungarian), it was the coronation city of the Kingdom of Hungary for nearly 300 years.

History

The site has been inhabited since the Neolithic. Under Hungary, Pressburg became the capital and coronation town from 1563 to 1830, with eleven Hungarian kings and queens crowned in St. Martin's Cathedral. After the First World War it joined Czechoslovakia, and in 1993 it became the capital of independent Slovakia.

Landmarks

Bratislava Castle dominates the Old Town. Other landmarks include Devín Castle at the confluence of the Danube and Morava, the Slovak National Theatre, the UFO-shaped tower of the Most SNP bridge, and the Blue Church.

Economy and transport

Bratislava is Slovakia's political, cultural, and economic centre, with major automotive plants (notably Volkswagen) and one of the highest GDP per capita figures in Central Europe. It is served by M. R. Štefánik Airport and is close to Vienna International Airport.
