Toad Suck Daze

Toad Suck Daze is an annual community festival held in downtown Conway, Arkansas, United States, typically over a weekend in late April or early May. First held in 1982, it is one of Arkansas's largest free-admission festivals and raises scholarship money for local students.

Name and history

The festival takes its name from Toad Suck, a locality on the Arkansas River west of Conway. According to local legend, riverboat crews in the steamboat era would dock at the shallow crossing and frequent a nearby tavern, "sucking on the bottle" until they swelled like toads. The ferry at the site operated until a bridge replaced it in the 1970s, and the nearby lock and dam and park retain the name.

Events

Popular features include live music on multiple stages, arts and crafts vendors, carnival rides, the World Championship Toad Races — in which children and local officials race pet toads — and the Stuck on a Truck contest, in which contestants keep a hand on a pickup truck for days to win it. Festival proceeds fund scholarships administered through the Toad Suck Daze Scholarship Foundation.
