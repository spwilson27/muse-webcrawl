The King George VI Chase is a Grade 1 National Hunt steeplechase horse race in Great Britain, open to horses aged four years or older. It is run at Kempton Park over a distance of about three miles (4,828 metres), with eighteen fences to be jumped, and is held each year on 26 December — Boxing Day — as the centrepiece of Kempton's Christmas Festival.

History

The race was first run in February 1937 and was named in honour of the new king, George VI. It was moved to its now-traditional Boxing Day slot after the Second World War. The event is second in prestige among British staying chases only to the Cheltenham Gold Cup, and horses that win both in the same season achieve a celebrated double.

Notable winners

Desert Orchid, the popular grey, won the race a record four times (1986, 1988, 1989, 1990). Kauto Star surpassed even that achievement with five victories (2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2011), his 2009 winning margin of 36 lengths being a race record; he also completed the King George–Gold Cup double in 2006–07. Other multiple winners include Wayward Lad (three) and Edredon Bleu, One Man, See More Business, and Clan Des Obeaux (two each). In recent years the race has also been won by Bravemansgame (2022) and Hewick (2023).

Sponsorship

The race has been sponsored since 2006 by the bookmaker Ladbrokes (previously by firms including William Hill and Stan James), and is officially titled the Ladbrokes King George VI Chase.
