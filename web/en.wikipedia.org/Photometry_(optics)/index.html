Photometry (optics)

Photometry is the science of the measurement of light, in terms of its perceived brightness to the human eye. It is distinct from radiometry, which measures radiant energy in absolute terms; photometric quantities are weighted by the luminosity function, a model of the eye's sensitivity.

Quantities

Key photometric quantities include luminous flux (measured in lumens), luminous intensity (candela), illuminance (lux) and luminance. The candela is one of the seven base units of the International System of Units.

Applications

Photometry underlies lighting design, display calibration and astronomical measurements of star brightness.
