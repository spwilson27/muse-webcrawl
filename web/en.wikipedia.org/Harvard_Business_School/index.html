Harvard Business School (HBS) is the graduate business school of Harvard University, located in Allston, Boston, across the Charles River from Harvard's main campus in Cambridge. Founded in 1908, it offered the world's first Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree and pioneered the case method of teaching, in which students discuss real business decisions drawn from HBS's own library of hundreds of thousands of written cases. Its publishing arm produces the Harvard Business Review (founded 1922) and most of the business-school cases used worldwide.

History

The school began in 1908 with 59 students under dean Edwin Gay, initially modelled on scientific-management ideas associated with Frederick Winslow Taylor. A transformative gift of $5 million from banker George F. Baker in 1924 funded the Georgian campus whose library bears his name. Under Wallace Donham, dean from 1919 to 1942, the case method became the school's signature pedagogy. Women were admitted to the full MBA programme in 1963, and the school progressively globalised, opening research centres around the world and launching the HBX (now HBS Online) digital platform in 2014.

Programmes

HBS enrolls roughly 2,000 MBA students, with about 930 in each class, taught almost entirely by discussion of some 500 cases over the two-year programme; grading includes mandatory class participation. It also offers doctoral programmes (PhD and DBA) run with Harvard's Graduate School of Arts and Sciences and a large portfolio of executive-education courses. Its alumni include heads of state, Fortune 500 chief executives and founders; the school's endowment is among the largest of any business school.

Campus

The 40-acre residential campus in Allston centres on Baker Library (1927), with its landmark bell tower, and Aldrich and Morgan halls; newer buildings include Tata Hall (2013), named for alumnus Ratan Tata, and Klarman Hall (2018). The school owns the Harvard Business Review and Harvard Business School Publishing, whose case sales to other institutions are a major revenue source.
