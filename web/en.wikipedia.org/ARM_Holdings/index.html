Arm Holdings

Arm Holdings plc
Industry: Semiconductors (IP licensing)
Founded: 27 November 1990
Headquarters: Cambridge, England
Parent: SoftBank Group (majority)

Arm Holdings plc is a British semiconductor and software design company based in Cambridge, England. Its primary business is designing the ARM architecture family of central processing units and licensing the designs — rather than manufacturing chips itself — to hundreds of partners including Apple, Qualcomm, Samsung, and Nvidia. Arm-based chips power nearly all smartphones and a vast range of embedded devices.

History

Arm was founded in 1990 as Advanced RISC Machines, a joint venture between Acorn Computers, Apple Computer, and VLSI Technology, to develop the processor line that began with the Acorn Archimedes. The ARM architecture's low power consumption made it dominant in mobile phones from the late 1990s onward.

Japan's SoftBank Group acquired Arm in 2016 for about £24.3 billion. A planned sale to Nvidia, announced in 2020 at $40 billion, was abandoned in February 2022 amid regulatory opposition in the US, UK, and EU. Arm listed on the Nasdaq in September 2023 in the year's largest IPO, with SoftBank retaining a majority stake.

Business model

Arm licenses processor core designs and the ARM instruction set architecture, earning an upfront license fee plus a royalty on each chip shipped. Partners may design their own cores under an architecture license, as Apple does for its A- and M-series chips. Arm's designs have expanded into laptops, servers, and automotive systems.
