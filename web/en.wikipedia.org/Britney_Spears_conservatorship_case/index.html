Britney Spears conservatorship case

Spears conservatorship
Began1 February 2008
Ended12 November 2021
CourtLos Angeles County Superior Court

From 2008 to 2021, American singer Britney Spears was placed under a conservatorship controlled primarily by her father, Jamie Spears, who acted as conservator of her person and estate following a series of public mental-health crises in 2007–08. During the arrangement Spears continued to record and perform, including a lucrative Las Vegas residency, while lacking control over her finances and personal decisions.

#FreeBritney and termination

A fan-led movement, #FreeBritney, alleged the conservatorship was abusive; a 2021 documentary intensified scrutiny. In June 2021 Spears testified publicly against the arrangement. Her father was suspended as conservator in September 2021, and Judge Brenda Penny terminated the conservatorship entirely on 12 November 2021. The case prompted reforms to conservatorship law in California.
