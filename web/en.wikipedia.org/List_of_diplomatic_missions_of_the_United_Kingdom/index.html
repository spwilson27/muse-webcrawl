List of diplomatic missions of the United Kingdom

The British Embassy in Washington, D.C., one of the UK's largest diplomatic missions.

This is a list of diplomatic missions of the United Kingdom. The United Kingdom maintains one of the world's largest diplomatic networks, administered by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO). British missions in fellow Commonwealth realms are called high commissions rather than embassies, and their heads are high commissioners.

Overview

The UK has embassies and high commissions in most world capitals — including Washington, D.C., Paris, Berlin, Moscow, Beijing, New Delhi, Canberra, Ottawa and Tokyo — together with consulates-general in major commercial cities such as New York, Los Angeles, Hong Kong, Dubai, Istanbul and São Paulo. The United Kingdom is also represented to international organizations, with permanent missions to the United Nations in New York, Geneva and Vienna, to the European Union in Brussels, and to NATO.

History

Britain's resident diplomatic service developed from the sixteenth century onward. The Foreign Office was created in 1782, and the Diplomatic Service expanded with the British Empire, producing a global network that was reorganized after the Second World War and again with the merger of the Foreign and Commonwealth Office and the Department for International Development in 2020.
