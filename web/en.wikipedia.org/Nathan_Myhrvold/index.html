Nathan Myhrvold

Born: 3 August 1959, Santa Monica, California
Known for: Microsoft CTO; founder of Intellectual Ventures; Modernist Cuisine

Nathan Paul Myhrvold (born 3 August 1959) is an American computer scientist, technologist, mathematician and physicist. He was chief technology officer and co-founder of Microsoft's research division, and in 2000 co-founded Intellectual Ventures, a patent portfolio developer and licensor. He is also known as the author of the encyclopedic cookbook Modernist Cuisine.

Education and early career

A prodigy, Myhrvold graduated from UCLA at 19 with degrees in mathematics and geophysics/space physics, then earned master's and doctoral degrees at Princeton, including a PhD in theoretical and mathematical physics, and did postdoctoral work with Stephen Hawking at Cambridge on quantum field theory in curved spacetime. He founded Dynamical Systems Research, which Microsoft acquired in 1986.

Microsoft and Intellectual Ventures

At Microsoft he founded Microsoft Research (1991) and served as chief strategist and CTO until 1999. Intellectual Ventures, which he founded with Edward Jung, amassed tens of thousands of patents; its model of patent licensing ("patent assertion") has been both defended as monetising invention and criticised as patent trolling.

Other pursuits

Myhrvold is a prize-winning nature and wildlife photographer, a dinosaur enthusiast who has funded palaeontology research and published statistical analyses of dinosaur growth, and a chef: his lab's Modernist Cuisine (2011) applied scientific method to cooking in six lavish volumes. He has also written on climate engineering and on mosquito control by laser.
