Tūheitia

Kīngi Tūheitia, seventh Māori monarch
Reign21 August 2006 – 30 August 2024
PredecessorTe Arikinui Dame Te Atairangikaahu
SuccessorNgā Wai Hono i te Pō
Born21 April 1955, Huntly, New Zealand
Died30 August 2024, Hamilton, New Zealand

Tūheitia Pōtatau Te Wherowhero VII (21 April 1955 – 30 August 2024), crowned as Kīngi Tūheitia, was the seventh Māori king of New Zealand. He succeeded his mother, Te Arikinui Dame Te Atairangikaahu, on her death in August 2006, and was crowned at Tūrangawaewae Marae in Ngāruawāhia on 21 August 2006, the day of her tangihanga (funeral).

Tūheitia was the eldest son of Te Atairangikaahu and Whatumoana Paki. Before his accession he worked in a variety of jobs, including as a truck driver, and served as a cultural adviser. As king, he acted as a figurehead and unifying figure for the Kīngitanga (Māori King Movement), hosting national hui, engaging in political advocacy on issues such as water rights and Māori representation, and representing Māori at events in New Zealand and abroad, including the coronation of Charles III in 2023.

He suffered from ill health, including diabetes and cancer, and underwent a kidney transplant in 2014. Tūheitia died on 30 August 2024, shortly after celebrating the 18th anniversary of his coronation. After his tangihanga at Tūrangawaewae, his youngest child and only daughter, Ngā Wai Hono i te Pō, was chosen to succeed him as the eighth Māori monarch.
