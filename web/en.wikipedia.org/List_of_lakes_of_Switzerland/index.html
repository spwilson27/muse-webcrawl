List of lakes of Switzerland

Switzerland has more than 7,000 lakes; this is a list of the lakes of Switzerland with a surface area of more than 1 km², or otherwise notable. The two largest lakes wholly or partly within Switzerland are Lake Geneva (Lac Léman) and Lake Constance (Bodensee), both shared with neighbouring countries. Lakes cover about 4% of Switzerland's surface area when foreign shares are included.
