ISO (disambiguation)


The ISO emblem of the International Organization for Standardization.

ISO may refer to:

Organisations and standards

  • International Organization for Standardization (ISO), the international standard-setting body composed of representatives from national standards organisations, publisher of standards such as:
    • ISO 9000 — quality management systems
    • ISO 9001 — quality management requirements
    • ISO 14000 — environmental management
    • ISO 14644 — cleanrooms and associated controlled environments
    • ISO 3166 — country codes; ISO 4217 — currency codes; ISO 8601 — date and time format
  • Infrared Space Observatory, a European Space Agency space telescope launched in 1995.

Science and technology

  • ISO image (disk image), an archive file of an optical disc, named after the ISO 9660 file system.
  • Film speed, measured in ISO units (from the ISO 5800 standard), often called simply "ISO" in photography.
  • Iso, a prefix in chemistry and taxonomy indicating an isomer or equal form (e.g., isopropyl, isotope).
  • Isometric drawing or exercise, abbreviated "iso".

Other uses

  • .iso, the filename extension for ISO disk images.
  • ISO, a class of container in shipping derived from ISO container standards.
  • Insurance Services Office, a U.S. provider of insurance data and analytics (now part of Verisk).
