New England is a region comprising six states in the Northeastern United States: Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, and Vermont. It is bordered by the state of New York to the west and south, and by the Canadian provinces of New Brunswick to the northeast and Quebec to the north.

The region was named by English explorer John Smith in 1616. It was one of the earliest sites of English colonisation in North America: the Pilgrims founded Plymouth Colony in 1620, and Puritan settlers established the Massachusetts Bay Colony in 1630. New England's town meetings, Congregational churches, and maritime trade shaped its distinctive political culture.

History

Indigenous peoples, including the Abenaki, Massachusett, Narragansett, Pequot, and Wampanoag, inhabited the region for thousands of years before European contact. Epidemics in the early 17th century devastated coastal populations. Colonial New England became a centre of shipbuilding, fishing, and transatlantic commerce, and its merchants and intellectuals played a leading role in the American Revolution — the first shots were fired at Lexington and Concord in April 1775.

In the 19th century New England led the United States in industrialisation, with textile mills powered by its rivers, and in abolitionism and literary culture (the Transcendentalists Emerson and Thoreau). As manufacturing later declined, the region's economy shifted toward education, medicine, finance, and technology.

Geography and climate

The region includes the Appalachian ranges of the White Mountains, Green Mountains, and Berkshires, a glaciated coastline, and numerous lakes and rivers such as the Connecticut River. New England has a humid continental climate with cold, snowy winters and warm summers; its fall foliage is a major tourist attraction.

Culture

New England is known for its regional dialect, seafood cuisine (clam chowder, lobster rolls), covered bridges, and prestigious universities, including the Ivy League institutions Harvard, Yale, Brown, and Dartmouth. Boston is its cultural and economic hub.
