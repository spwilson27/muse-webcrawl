Natural disaster

A natural disaster is the very harmful impact on a society or community following a natural hazard event. Examples include floods, hurricanes, earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, heat waves, wildfires, droughts and tsunamis.

Natural disaster
TypesGeological, hydrological, meteorological, biological
ResponseEmergency management, humanitarian aid

Classification

Geological disasters include earthquakes and volcanoes; hydrological include floods and tsunamis; climatological include drought, which can contribute to famine. Biological disasters include epidemics.

Impacts

Disasters disproportionately harm poor communities with weak infrastructure. Between 2000 and 2019, UNDRR recorded about 7,348 disaster events claiming over a million lives. Climate change is increasing the frequency and intensity of many weather-related hazards.

Response

Relief is coordinated nationally and internationally by bodies such as OCHA and the Red Cross and Red Crescent.
