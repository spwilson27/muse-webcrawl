José Saturnino Cardozo

José Saturnino Cardozo Otazú (born 19 March 1971) is a Paraguayan former professional footballer who played as a striker, and is one of the greatest goalscorers in the history of Mexican football. Nicknamed El Príncipe Guaraní ("The Guaraní Prince") and El Diablo Mayor, he spent the peak of his career with Toluca in Mexico's Primera División.

Club career

Cardozo began his career in Paraguay with River Plate Asunción and Olimpia, then played in Switzerland for St. Gallen before moving to Mexico. After a spell with Universidad Católica in Chile, he joined Toluca in 1995 and became the club's all-time top scorer, winning four league titles and finishing as the league's top scorer on several occasions. In the Apertura 2002 tournament he scored 29 goals, at the time one of the highest totals ever recorded in a short tournament worldwide. He ended his career with Olimpia in 2006.

International career

Cardozo earned over 80 caps for the Paraguay national team, scoring 25 goals. He represented Paraguay at the 1998 and 2002 FIFA World Cups and won a silver medal at the 2004 Athens Olympics as an overage player. After retiring, he moved into management, coaching clubs including Olimpia, Toluca, Chiapas, and Chivas.
