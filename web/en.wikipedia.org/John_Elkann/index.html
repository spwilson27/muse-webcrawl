John Philip Jacob Elkann (born April 1, 1976) is an Italian-American industrialist and the designated heir of Gianni Agnelli, his grandfather, to the leadership of the Agnelli family's business empire. He is chairman of Stellantis, the multinational automaker formed by the merger of Fiat Chrysler Automobiles and PSA Group, and chief executive of Exor, the family's holding company, whose investments include Ferrari, Juventus Football Club, and stakes in media and industry.

Early life and education

Elkann was born in New York City to Margherita Agnelli and the writer Alain Elkann. Educated in Paris and Turin, he graduated in engineering from the Polytechnic University of Turin in 2000. He gained experience across the family businesses, including at General Electric's corporate audit programme, before taking on senior roles within Fiat.

Leadership of Fiat and Stellantis

Following the deaths of his grandfather Gianni Agnelli in 2003 and great-uncle Umberto Agnelli in 2004, Elkann became the family's leading figure. He became chairman of Fiat in 2010, working closely with chief executive Sergio Marchionne on the acquisition of Chrysler and the group's global expansion. After Marchionne's death in 2018, Elkann steered Fiat Chrysler into the 2021 merger with PSA that created Stellantis, one of the world's largest carmakers, and became its chairman.

Other roles

Elkann is also chairman of Ferrari and of GEDI Gruppo Editoriale, and founded the venture firm Lingotto Investment Management under Exor. He is married to Lavinia Borromeo, with whom he has three children.
