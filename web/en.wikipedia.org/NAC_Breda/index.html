NAC Breda

NAC Breda, often simply known as NAC, is a Dutch professional football club based in Breda, North Brabant. Founded in 1912 from a merger of the clubs NOAD and ADVENDO, its name is an acronym of the two founding clubs' mottoes: NOAD (Nooit Opgeven Altijd Doorzetten — "Never give up, always persevere") and ADVENDO (Aangenaam Door Vermaak En Nuttig Door Ontspanning), plus C for Combinatie (combination).

Founded: 19 September 1912

Ground: Rat Verlegh Stadion, Breda

Capacity: ~19,000

History

NAC won its only Dutch national championship in 1921 and the KNVB Cup in 1973, defeating N.E.C. in the final. The club has spent most of its modern history moving between the Eredivisie and the second-tier Eerste Divisie, earning a reputation for passionate evening home matches — the famous Avondje NAC ("an evening at NAC") — in which an intimidating atmosphere helps the team against stronger opponents.

Supporters and identity

NAC's supporters are known for their loyalty through relegations and financial crises, including supporter-led initiatives that helped stabilise the club. Its strongest rivalries are with Willem II of Tilburg and Feyenoord.
