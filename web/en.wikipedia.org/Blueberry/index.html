Blueberry

Blueberries are perennial flowering plants with blue or purple berries, classified in the section Cyanococcus of the genus Vaccinium, which also includes cranberries, bilberries and huckleberries. Species native to North America, such as the northern highbush blueberry (Vaccinium corymbosum), form the basis of the commercial crop.

Cultivation

Blueberries are grown commercially in North and South America, Europe, Asia and Australasia. They require acidic soil and are harvested for fresh consumption and processing into juice, jams, muffins and dried fruit.

Nutrition

Blueberries are low in calories and contain vitamin C, vitamin K, manganese and anthocyanin pigments that give the fruit its colour. They are frequently marketed as a "superfruit" for their antioxidant content.
