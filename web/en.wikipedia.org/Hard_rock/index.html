Hard rock

Hard rock or heavy rock is a loosely defined subgenre of rock music typified by aggressive vocals, distorted electric guitars, and emphatic rhythms, rooted in 1960s garage rock, blues rock, and psychedelic rock.

History

Bands such as Led Zeppelin, Deep Purple, Black Sabbath, Aerosmith, AC/DC, and Van Halen defined the sound in the late 1960s and 1970s, emphasising guitar riffs, solos, and powerful live performance.

Characteristics

Hard rock typically uses a verse-chorus structure, electric guitar as the lead instrument, and a strong backbeat. It overlaps with heavy metal, which generally takes a heavier and more intense approach. See also Heavy metal.
