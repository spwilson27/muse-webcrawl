Reinforcement learning from human feedback

Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) is a technique for training machine learning models in which a reward model is learned from human evaluations of model outputs and then used as the reward signal for reinforcement learning. It is widely used to align large language models with human preferences.

Method

Human annotators rank or compare several outputs produced by a model for the same prompt. These comparisons are used to train a reward model that predicts the score a human would assign to a new output. The language model is then optimised against that reward model, commonly with the Proximal Policy Optimization algorithm, while a penalty keeps it from drifting too far from the original model.

History and impact

The approach was developed in research at OpenAI and DeepMind in the late 2010s and applied at scale in InstructGPT and ChatGPT, where it substantially improved the helpfulness and harmlessness of model responses.
