Characters per minute

CPM was used to rate teleprinter speeds

Characters per minute (cpm) is a measure of the speed of data entry, typing, or printing, counting the number of characters produced or transmitted in one minute. It is closely related to words per minute (wpm); by a common convention, one "word" is reckoned as five characters including spaces, so a speed in wpm multiplied by five gives an approximate cpm figure.

Usage

The measure was historically applied to teleprinters and telegraph systems, where speeds were also expressed in baud or words per minute. In reading research and subtitling, characters per minute (and characters per second) are used to gauge reading speeds and to set display rates for captions and subtitles in different languages.
