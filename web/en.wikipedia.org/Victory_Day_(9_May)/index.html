Victory Day (9 May)

Observed byRussia, Belarus, and other post-Soviet states
Date9 May
SignificanceCommemorates the victory of the Soviet Union over Nazi Germany in the Great Patriotic War

Victory Day (Russian: День Победы) is a holiday that commemorates the victory of the Soviet Union over Nazi Germany in 1945. The German Instrument of Surrender was signed late in the evening of 8 May 1945 in Berlin, which was already 9 May in Moscow time, so the holiday is observed on 9 May in Russia and most former Soviet republics, while Western Europe marks Victory in Europe Day on 8 May.

Observance

In Russia the day is marked by a major military parade on Red Square in Moscow, fireworks, and the laying of wreaths at war memorials. Since 2012 the Immortal Regiment marches, in which people carry photographs of relatives who served in the war, have become a prominent part of the observance in Russia and abroad. The St George's ribbon is a widely worn symbol of the holiday.
