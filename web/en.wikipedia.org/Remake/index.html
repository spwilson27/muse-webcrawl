Remake

A remake is a film, television series, video game or other work of entertainment that is based on an earlier work and tells the same story, or a substantially similar one, with updated production values, technology, or cultural context.

In film

Remakes have been common throughout cinema history, from silent-era stories refilmed with sound to Hollywood remakes of foreign films, such as The Magnificent Seven (from Seven Samurai) or The Departed (from Infernal Affairs). Remakes differ from sequels and reboots: a reboot restarts a franchise with a new continuity, while a remake retells the original narrative. Critics and audiences often debate whether remakes improve on or merely imitate their sources.

In video games

Game remakes rebuild an older title from scratch with new graphics and mechanics, distinct from remasters, which are lighter enhancements of the original code and assets.
