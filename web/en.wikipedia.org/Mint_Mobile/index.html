Mint Mobile is an American mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) that sells prepaid wireless service on T-Mobile's network. Founded in 2015 as Mint SIM, it is known for selling service in bulk multi-month packages at discounted rates and for its heavily promoted association with actor Ryan Reynolds, who became a part-owner in 2019.

Business model

Mint sells plans in 3-, 6- and 12-month bundles paid in advance, primarily online, rather than through retail stores — a model the company credits for its low prices. Reynolds's celebrity-fronted advertising campaigns, including humorous low-budget video ads, became central to the brand's identity.

Acquisition

In March 2023 T-Mobile US announced it would acquire Mint's parent, Ka'ena Corporation, for up to about US$1.35 billion. The deal closed on 1 May 2024, with T-Mobile pledging to retain Mint's pricing model, and Reynolds remaining in a creative role.
