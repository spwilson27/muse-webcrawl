Giro d'Italia

The Giro d'Italia (Italian for "Tour of Italy") is an annual multiple-stage bicycle race held primarily in Italy, usually in May, and is one of professional cycling's three Grand Tours, alongside the Tour de France and the Vuelta a España.

History

The race was first organized in 1909 by the newspaper La Gazzetta dello Sport to boost sales. Its leader wears the pink jersey (maglia rosa), matching the newspaper's pink pages. Riders such as Alfredo Binda, Fausto Coppi, and Eddy Merckx have each won the Giro five times.

Classifications

Besides the general classification (pink jersey), the race awards the points classification (ciclamino jersey), the mountains classification (blue jersey), and the young rider classification (white jersey). Stages typically include Alpine and Dolomite mountain passes such as the Passo dello Stelvio and the Passo di Gavia.
