Minganie is a regional county municipality in the Côte-Nord region of Quebec, Canada. It includes several municipalities on the north shore of the Gulf of St. Lawrence as well as Anticosti Island. Its regional seat is Havre-Saint-Pierre.

Geography

Minganie stretches along the northern shore of the Gulf of St. Lawrence and is one of the largest regional county municipalities in Quebec by area. The territory includes the Mingan Archipelago, known for its striking limestone monoliths sculpted by erosion, which form part of the Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve. Anticosti Island, famous for its white-tailed deer population, also lies within the municipality.

Communities

The municipality includes the towns and villages of Aguanish, Baie-Johan-Beetz, Havre-Saint-Pierre, L'Île-d'Anticosti, Longue-Pointe-de-Mingan, Natashquan, Rivière-Saint-Jean and Rivière-au-Tonnerre, as well as Innu communities such as Ekuanitshit (Mingan) and Nutashkuan.

Economy

The local economy historically depended on fishing, forestry and fur trading. In the twentieth century, ilmenite mining near Havre-Saint-Pierre, operated by Rio Tinto Fer et Titane, became a major employer, with ore transported by the Romaine River railway to the port.
