Ketosis

Ketosis is a metabolic state characterised by elevated levels of ketone bodies in the blood or urine, produced by the liver from fatty acids when glucose availability is low, such as during fasting, prolonged exercise, or a ketogenic diet.

Physiology

The ketone bodies acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate and acetone serve as an alternative fuel for brain and muscle. Nutritional ketosis is distinct from diabetic ketoacidosis, a dangerous condition of uncontrolled ketone and acid production, usually in type 1 diabetes.
