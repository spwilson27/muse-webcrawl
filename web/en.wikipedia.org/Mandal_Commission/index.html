Mandal Commission

Official name: Socially and Educationally Backward Classes Commission
Chairman: B. P. Mandal
Report submitted: 1980

The Mandal Commission, formally the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes Commission (SEBC), was set up by the Government of India in 1979 under Prime Minister Morarji Desai, chaired by former Bihar chief minister B. P. Mandal, to identify socially and educationally backward classes in India.

Recommendations

The commission's 1980 report identified 3,743 castes as socially and educationally backward, estimated them at about 52% of the population, and recommended reserving 27% of government jobs and seats in public educational institutions for Other Backward Classes (OBCs).

Implementation

Prime Minister V. P. Singh's decision to implement the job reservation in 1990 triggered widespread protests and reshaped Indian politics. The Supreme Court upheld the reservation, subject to a 50% overall ceiling and exclusion of the "creamy layer", in the Indra Sawhney case (1992).
