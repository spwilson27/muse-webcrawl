Alpine F1 Team

Full name: BWT Alpine F1 Team

Base: Enstone, Oxfordshire, England

Team principal: Flavio Briatore (executive advisor, de facto head from 2024)

Chassis: A525

Engine: Renault

First entry: 2021 Bahrain Grand Prix

Alpine F1 Team, competing as BWT Alpine F1 Team, is a Formula One constructor that debuted at the start of the 2021 season, rebranded from the Renault F1 Team to promote Renault's sports car brand Alpine. The team is based at Enstone in England, whose lineage traces back through Renault, Lotus F1 and Benetton to the Toleman team of 1981, while its engines are built at Viry-Châtillon in France.

History

The team's first victory came at the 2021 Hungarian Grand Prix, where Esteban Ocon held off Sebastian Vettel and Lewis Hamilton to win in wet-dry conditions. Fernando Alonso, who had returned to the sport with the team, took third in Qatar that year. Alonso departed for Aston Martin in 2023, replaced by Pierre Gasly, giving Alpine an all-French driver line-up.

The team slumped in 2024, beginning the season at the back of the field, though a double podium for Ocon and Gasly in a rain-hit São Paulo Grand Prix lifted it to sixth in the constructors' championship. Long-serving executive advisor Flavio Briatore returned to lead the team, and Alpine announced it would end its works engine programme after 2025 to become a Mercedes customer team from 2026.

Predecessors

The Enstone operation previously won constructors' titles as Benetton (1995) and Renault (2005, 2006). Alpine's sister brand achieved success in rallying and endurance racing, including overall victory at the 1978 24 Hours of Le Mans.
