List of largest giant sequoias

The giant sequoia (Sequoiadendron giganteum) is the world's most massive tree species. The largest giant sequoias are ranked by trunk volume. All grow in groves on the western slopes of California's Sierra Nevada.

The General Sherman Tree is the largest known living single-stem tree on Earth by volume, standing about 83.8 m tall with a trunk diameter of 7.7 m. Many of the largest trees were named in the 19th and early 20th centuries; some historical giants, such as the Mother of the Forest, were destroyed by logging or exhibition.
