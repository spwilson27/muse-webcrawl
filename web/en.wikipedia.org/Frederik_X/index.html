Frederik X

Frederik X (born 26 May 1968) is King of Denmark. He acceded to the throne on 14 January 2024, following the abdication of his mother, Queen Margrethe II, after her 52-year reign — the first voluntary abdication of a Danish monarch in nearly nine centuries.

Early life and education

Frederik André Henrik Christian was born at Rigshospitalet in Copenhagen, the elder son of Princess Margrethe and Prince Henrik. He studied political science at Aarhus University and completed a master's degree there in 1995, having spent a year at Harvard University under the name Frederik Henriksen. He received extensive military training in all three services, including service as a frogman in the Royal Danish Navy's elite Frømandskorpset, where he earned the nickname "Pingo."

Crown Prince

As Crown Prince he represented Denmark abroad, served on the International Olympic Committee from 2009 to 2021, and became known for sporting pursuits including the Royal Run and expeditions in Greenland and Mongolia. On 14 May 2004 he married Australian Mary Donaldson, whom he had met during the 2000 Sydney Olympics; they have four children, including Christian, Prince of Denmark, the heir apparent.

Reign

Frederik was proclaimed king from the balcony of Christiansborg Palace by Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen on 14 January 2024. His motto is "Forbundet, forpligtet, for Kongeriget Danmark" ("United, committed, for the Kingdom of Denmark").
