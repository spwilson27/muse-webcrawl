Rogers Communications Inc. is a Canadian communications and media company operating primarily in the fields of wireless communications, cable television, telephony and mass media. It is one of Canada's largest telecommunications providers, alongside Bell and Telus.

History

The company traces its origins to 1925, when Edward S. "Ted" Rogers Sr. founded a radio station and developed a batteryless radio receiver. His son, Ted Rogers, built the modern company from 1960 onward through cable television, then wireless telephony. Rogers acquired the Toronto Blue Jays baseball team and the Rogers Centre stadium, and owns media properties including Citytv and Sportsnet. In 2023 it completed a merger with Shaw Communications, significantly expanding its western Canadian footprint.
