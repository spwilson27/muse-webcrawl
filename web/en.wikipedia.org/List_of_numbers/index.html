List of numbers

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of numbers collects notable integers and other numbers, organized by size and kind, together with links to articles about them. It includes natural numbers with special mathematical properties or cultural significance, named large numbers, and important irrational and complex constants.

Large numbers

Named large numbers include the million (106), billion (109 in the short scale), trillion (1012), and beyond, up to centillions and specially coined terms such as the googol (10100) and googolplex (10googol). Very large numbers arise in combinatorics and computer science, e.g. Graham's number.

Irrational and transcendental numbers

  • π (pi) ≈ 3.14159 — ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter
  • e ≈ 2.71828 — base of the natural logarithm
  • φ (golden ratio) ≈ 1.61803
  • √2 ≈ 1.41421 — length of the diagonal of a unit square
