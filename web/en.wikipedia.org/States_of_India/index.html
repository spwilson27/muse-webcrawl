States and union territories of India

India
28 states
8 union territories
Largest state: Rajasthan (area), Uttar Pradesh (population)

India is a federal union comprising 28 states and 8 union territories. The states and union territories are further subdivided into districts and smaller administrative divisions. The states have their own elected governments, while union territories are governed directly by the Government of India, some with legislatures of their own.

History

At independence in 1947, British India was divided into provinces, alongside more than 500 princely states that were integrated into the new union. The States Reorganisation Act of 1956 redrew state boundaries largely along linguistic lines. Further states were created in later decades: Maharashtra and Gujarat in 1960, several northeastern states in the 1970s and 1980s, Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand, and Jharkhand in 2000, and Telangana in 2014. In 2019 the state of Jammu and Kashmir was reorganised into the union territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.

Government

Each state has a governor appointed by the president, a chief minister heading a council of ministers, and a legislative assembly (some also have a legislative council). The Constitution of India divides powers between the Union and the states through the Union, State, and Concurrent Lists of the Seventh Schedule. The largest state by population is Uttar Pradesh, and the largest by area is Rajasthan.
