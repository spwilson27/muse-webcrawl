African art

African art includes the visual arts of native Africans and the African diaspora, spanning sculpture, painting, pottery, textiles, masks, and personal decoration. African art often has religious, utilitarian, and ceremonial functions, and its forms vary widely among the continent's many cultures.

Sculpture and masks

Well-known traditions include the naturalistic bronze and terracotta heads of Ife, the Benin Bronzes of the Kingdom of Benin, Akan goldweights, Yoruba and Igbo sculpture, and the masks of the Dan, Chokwe, and Bamum peoples. Sculpture in wood, bronze, brass, terracotta, and ivory predominates.

Influence

In the early twentieth century, African masks and sculpture profoundly influenced European modernists, notably Picasso and the Cubists. Much African art was removed during colonial rule and is held in Western museums; restitution of works such as the Benin Bronzes has become a major issue in the twenty-first century.
