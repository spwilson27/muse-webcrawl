University of Central Arkansas

Type: Public university
Established: 1907
Location: Conway, Arkansas, U.S.
Students: ~10,000

The University of Central Arkansas (UCA) is a public university in Conway, Arkansas. Founded in 1907 as Arkansas State Normal School to train teachers, it became Arkansas State Teachers College in 1925, State College of Arkansas in 1967, and a university under its present name in 1975.

Academics

UCA offers degrees through colleges of arts, humanities and social sciences; business; education; health and behavioral sciences; and natural sciences and mathematics, together with the Schedler Honors College, one of the oldest honours programmes in the state. It is particularly known for health sciences, including nursing and physical and occupational therapy.

Athletics

The Central Arkansas Bears and Sugar Bears compete in NCAA Division I as members of the ASUN Conference (United Athletic Conference for football). Scottie Pippen, the NBA Hall of Famer, played basketball at UCA in the 1980s.
