Panj Pyare

The Panj Pyare, the "Five Beloved Ones", are the five Sikhs who, in 1699, responded to Guru Gobind Singh's call for volunteers willing to offer their lives, and were instead initiated as the first members of the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib on Vaisakhi.

The five

They were Bhai Daya Singh, Bhai Dharam Singh, Bhai Himmat Singh, Bhai Mohkam Singh and Bhai Sahib Singh, drawn from different regions and castes, symbolising the Khalsa's break with caste hierarchy.

Role today

In modern Sikh practice, five initiated Sikhs acting as Panj Pyare administer amrit, lead processions, and represent the community at major religious functions.
