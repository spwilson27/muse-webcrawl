JD Vance

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

James David Vance (born James Donald Bowman; August 2, 1984) is an American politician, author, and former venture capitalist who has served as the 50th vice president of the United States since 2025. A member of the Republican Party, he represented Ohio in the U.S. Senate from 2023 to 2025.

Early life and memoir

Vance was born in Middletown, Ohio, and raised partly in Kentucky's Appalachian region. After serving in the U.S. Marine Corps, including a deployment to Iraq, he graduated from Ohio State University and Yale Law School. His 2016 memoir Hillbilly Elegy, about his upbringing and the social problems of Appalachia, became a bestseller and was adapted into a film.

Political career

After working in venture capital, Vance won election to the U.S. Senate in 2022. In 2024 Donald Trump selected him as his running mate; the ticket won the election, and Vance took office as vice president on January 20, 2025.
