Martial arts

Martial arts are codified systems and traditions of combat practised for reasons including self-defence, military and law-enforcement application, competition, physical and mental development, entertainment and the preservation of cultural heritage. The term derives from Latin and means "arts of Mars", the Roman god of war.

History

Organised fighting systems are ancient worldwide: wrestling and boxing appear in Egyptian and Greek art, Chinese traditions trace roots to the Shaolin Monastery, and battlefield arts developed in Japan, Korea, India, Europe and elsewhere. In the modern era, Asian martial arts spread globally after the Second World War, and judo, taekwondo and karate have become Olympic sports.

Types

Martial arts are often categorised as striking arts (karate, taekwondo, boxing, muay Thai), grappling arts (judo, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, wrestling), weapon-based arts (kendo, fencing, eskrima) and internal or meditative arts (tai chi, aikido). Mixed martial arts (MMA) combines techniques across these categories in full-contact competition.
