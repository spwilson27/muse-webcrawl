A British citizen is a person holding the primary class of British nationality, which carries the right of abode in the United Kingdom, the right to live and work there without immigration control, and the right to vote in UK elections. The status was created by the British Nationality Act 1981, in force from 1 January 1983.

Acquisition

Citizenship may be acquired by birth in the UK to a citizen or settled parent, by descent from a citizen parent, by registration (including for children and certain other nationality holders), or by naturalisation after a period of residence and meeting requirements of good character, language and knowledge of life in the UK.
