Warp (record label)

Warp Records (commonly known simply as Warp) is a British independent record label founded in Sheffield in 1989 by Steve Beckett and Rob Mitchell. It is one of the most influential labels in electronic music, pioneering the "intelligent dance music" (IDM) movement in the early 1990s and later expanding into experimental rock, hip-hop, and film.

History

The label grew out of the FON record shop in Sheffield, and the name Warp is said to derive from "Warped Records," which was hard to pronounce over the telephone. Early releases such as LFO's "LFO" and Nightmares on Wax's work defined the bleep techno sound. The compilation Artificial Intelligence (1992) helped establish Warp's roster of album-oriented electronic artists.

Artists

Warp's signature artists include Aphex Twin, Autechre, Boards of Canada, Squarepusher, and LFO, along with later signings such as Flying Lotus, Danny Brown, Battles, Grizzly Bear, and Oneohtrix Point Never. The label co-founded Warp Films in 1999, which produced features including Dead Man's Shoes and Four Lions, and in 2009 celebrated its 20th anniversary with the Warp20 box set and concerts.
