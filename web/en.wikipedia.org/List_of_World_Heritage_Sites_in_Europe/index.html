List of World Heritage Sites in Europe

UNESCO designates World Heritage Sites of outstanding universal value. Europe (Western, Northern, Southern and Eastern, in UNESCO's regional scheme) holds the largest share of sites of any region, several hundred in total.

Countries with most sites

Italy leads the world overall with around 60 sites, followed within Europe by Germany, France, Spain and the United Kingdom. Well-known sites include:

  • Italy: Historic Centre of Rome, Venice and its Lagoon, Florence, Pompeii, the Amalfi Coast, Cinque Terre.
  • France: Palace of Versailles, Chartres Cathedral, Mont-Saint-Michel, the Banks of the Seine in Paris, Lascaux cave paintings (Vézère Valley).
  • Spain: Alhambra (Granada), Works of Antoni Gaudí, Santiago de Compostela, Historic Centre of Córdoba.
  • United Kingdom: Stonehenge and Avebury, Tower of London, St Kilda, the English Lake District, Jodrell Bank.
  • Germany: Aachen and Cologne Cathedrals, Bauhaus sites, the Wadden Sea (shared), Upper Middle Rhine Valley.
  • Greece: Acropolis of Athens, Delphi, Olympia, Meteora, Mount Athos.
