Deutsche Börse

Frankfurt Stock Exchange, operated by Deutsche Börse

Deutsche Börse AG is a German multinational corporation that operates the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and other marketplaces for trading shares and securities. Founded in 1992 and headquartered in Eschborn, near Frankfurt am Main, it is one of the world's largest exchange organizations.

Business areas

The group operates the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and the Xetra electronic trading platform, the Eurex derivatives exchange, the Clearstream post-trading and custody business, and index provider business including STOXX and DAX indices. It also provides market data, analytics, and technology services.

History

Deutsche Börse has repeatedly pursued consolidation, including an attempted merger with the London Stock Exchange Group blocked by the European Commission in 2017. It is a constituent of the DAX index itself.
