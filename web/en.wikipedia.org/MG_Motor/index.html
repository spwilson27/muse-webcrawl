MG Motor

Founded2006 (as MG Motor UK)
ParentSAIC Motor
HeadquartersMarylebone, London, UK
ProductsPassenger cars, electric vehicles

MG Motor is an automotive company owned by the Shanghai-based Chinese state-owned carmaker SAIC Motor. It sells cars under the historic British MG marque, which SAIC acquired through its purchase of Nanjing Automobile, the company that had bought the assets of the collapsed MG Rover Group in 2005.

History

Production of MG cars restarted in China in 2007, and limited assembly of the MG TF resumed at the Longbridge plant in Birmingham in 2008. MG Motor UK relaunched the brand in Britain with the MG6 in 2011, followed by the MG3 supermini and the GS and ZS crossovers. UK assembly at Longbridge ended in 2016, though design and engineering work continued there. The brand expanded rapidly with value-priced SUVs and then with electric vehicles.

Electric era and expansion

MG became one of Europe's fastest-growing car brands in the early 2020s on the strength of affordable EVs such as the MG ZS EV and the MG4 EV hatchback, and it re-entered the sports car segment with the electric Cyberster roadster in 2023. MG Motor also operates in India (as a brand of the SAIC–JSW venture), Australia, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America, frequently ranking among the top-selling Chinese-owned brands abroad.
