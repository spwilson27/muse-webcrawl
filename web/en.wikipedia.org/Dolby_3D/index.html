Dolby 3D

Dolby 3D is a marketing name for a system from Dolby Laboratories used for showing three-dimensional motion pictures in digital cinemas. Unlike competing systems that rely on polarized light, Dolby 3D uses wavelength multiplexing based on technology licensed from Infitec.

Dolby 3D
DeveloperDolby Laboratories
Type3D cinema projection system
TechnologyWavelength multiplex (interference filter)

Technology

A filter wheel in the projector separates the image into slightly different sets of red, green, and blue wavelengths for the left and right eyes. Viewers wear passive glasses with interference-filter lenses that transmit only the wavelengths intended for each eye, producing full-color 3D on a standard white screen — unlike polarization systems, which require a silver screen. The glasses are more expensive than polarized ones and are typically collected and reused by the cinema.

Dolby Cinema

Dolby 3D is also used in premium Dolby Cinema auditoriums, paired with Dolby Vision laser projection and Dolby Atmos sound.
