The 2008 Italian Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held at the Autodromo Nazionale Monza on 14 September 2008. It was the fourteenth race of the 2008 World Championship and produced one of the sport's landmark results: Sebastian Vettel, aged 21 years and 73 days, became the youngest driver in history to win a Formula One Grand Prix — and to take pole position — driving for the small Scuderia Toro Rosso team.

Race summary

The weekend was dominated by rain. In a wet qualifying session Vettel mastered the treacherous conditions to claim pole position, while championship leader Lewis Hamilton was caught out and qualified only 15th. The race began behind the safety car in standing water.

Vettel controlled the race from the front with a maturity that belied his age, building a lead and managing his one-stop strategy as the track slowly dried. He won by 12.5 seconds from Heikki Kovalainen's McLaren, with Robert Kubica third for BMW Sauber after starting 11th. Hamilton recovered to seventh, one place behind title rival Felipe Massa.

Significance

It was Toro Rosso's first — and, as Toro Rosso/AlphaTauri/RB, for many years only — victory, achieved at the team's home race with a Ferrari engine in a car designed in Faenza. Vettel's records for youngest polesitter and winner stood until Max Verstappen's emergence in 2015–16, and the performance confirmed Vettel as the sport's outstanding emerging talent ahead of his move to Red Bull Racing in 2009.
