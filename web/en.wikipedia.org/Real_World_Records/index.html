Real World Records is a British record label founded in 1989 by musician Peter Gabriel, growing out of the WOMAD (World of Music, Arts and Dance) festival organisation. Based at Gabriel's Real World Studios in Box, Wiltshire, the label was created to record and release music by artists from around the world with high production standards and fair presentation, at a time when "world music" was a marginal marketing category.

Artists and releases

The label's catalogue includes albums by Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Youssou N'Dour, Geoffrey Oryema, Sheila Chandra, the Afro Celt Sound System, Maryam Mursal, and Papa Wemba, as well as the collaborative project Big Blue Ball (2008). Recording "weeks" at Real World Studios brought together musicians from many traditions to collaborate in the studio, a model that produced several label releases.

Significance

Real World Records is credited with raising the profile of international artists in Western markets and with demonstrating that such music could be marketed with the same design and production values as mainstream rock and pop.
