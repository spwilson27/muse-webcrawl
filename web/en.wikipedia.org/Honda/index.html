Honda

Honda Motor Co., Ltd. is a Japanese multinational conglomerate founded in 1948 by Soichiro Honda and Takeo Fujisawa. It is the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles and a major producer of automobiles, power equipment and aircraft.

The Honda Super Cub is the best-selling motor vehicle in history.

History

Soichiro Honda began by fitting small engines to bicycles after the Second World War and incorporated Honda Motor in 1948. The Super Cub, launched in 1958, became the most-produced motor vehicle ever, passing 100 million units. Honda entered the US market in 1959 and automobile production in 1963 with the T360 truck and S500 sports car.

Automobiles

The Civic (1972) and Accord (1976) established Honda in North America, and the Acura luxury brand followed in 1986. Honda was a pioneer of fuel-efficient engines (the CVCC met US emissions rules without a catalyst) and of hybrid cars with the Insight (1999). It also builds the HondaJet business aircraft through a US subsidiary.

Motorsport

Honda has competed in Formula One as a constructor and engine supplier, powering championship-winning teams including McLaren in the late 1980s and Red Bull Racing in the 2020s, and is the dominant engine maker in MotoGP motorcycle racing.
