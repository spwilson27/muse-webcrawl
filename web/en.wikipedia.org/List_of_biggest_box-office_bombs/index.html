List of biggest box-office bombs

In the film industry, a box-office bomb, or box-office flop, is a film that is regarded as highly unsuccessful or unprofitable during its theatrical run. This list ranks some of the biggest box-office failures of all time, ordered by the estimated amount of money lost by the studio, taking into account the estimated budgets and grosses of the films.

Causes

Common causes of box-office failure include excessive production budgets, weak marketing, poor critical reception, and competition from other releases. Some films, such as Cutthroat Island, contributed to the bankruptcy of their studios.
