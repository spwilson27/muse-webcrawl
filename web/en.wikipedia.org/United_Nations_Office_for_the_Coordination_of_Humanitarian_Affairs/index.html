United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs

The United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) is a United Nations body established in December 1991 to strengthen the UN response to complex emergencies and natural disasters.

OCHA
Founded1991 (as DHA; OCHA from 1998)
HeadquartersNew York and Geneva
HeadUnder-Secretary-General for Humanitarian Affairs

Functions

OCHA coordinates humanitarian assistance, advocacy, policy, and information management, manages the Central Emergency Response Fund (CERF), and publishes appeals for crises in countries such as Syria, Sudan and Ukraine. It runs ReliefWeb.

History

It succeeded the Office of the UN Disaster Relief Coordinator, becoming the Department of Humanitarian Affairs in 1992 and OCHA in 1998.
