Nonlinear gameplay

Nonlinear gameplay is a video game design approach in which the player is given freedom in how to progress, rather than being guided through a predetermined sequence of challenges. Challenges can usually be completed in a number of different sequences or by different methods, and story events may branch depending on player choices.

Forms

Nonlinearity ranges from branching storylines and multiple endings, through open-world games in which a large environment can be explored in any order (as in The Elder Scrolls or Grand Theft Auto series), to sandbox games with no fixed goals at all. In level-based games it may take the form of multiple routes, optional objectives and emergent solutions. The opposite approach, linear gameplay, offers a single fixed path and is favoured for tightly scripted narratives.
