University of Ottawa

The University of Ottawa is a bilingual public research university in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1848 as the College of Bytown, it is the largest bilingual (English-French) university in the world.

History

The university was founded by Oblates of Mary Immaculate and received university status in 1866. It was secularised in 1965.

Academics

Faculties include law (common and civil law), medicine, social sciences, science, engineering and arts, with instruction in both official languages.

Campus

The main campus lies in Sandy Hill, near Parliament Hill and the Rideau Canal.
