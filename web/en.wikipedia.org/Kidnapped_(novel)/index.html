Kidnapped (novel)

AuthorRobert Louis Stevenson
Published1886
GenreHistorical novel, adventure
SettingScotland, 1751

Kidnapped is a historical fiction adventure novel by the Scottish author Robert Louis Stevenson, first published in 1886. Set in Scotland in 1751, in the aftermath of the Jacobite rising of 1745, it follows young David Balfour, who is cheated of his inheritance by his uncle Ebenezer and kidnapped aboard the brig Covenant.

Plot

Shipwrecked on the island of Erraid, David makes his way across the Highlands, befriends the Jacobite Alan Breck Stewart, and becomes entangled in the Appin murder—the real-life killing of the government factor Colin Roy Campbell, "the Red Fox", in 1752. The novel ends with David confronting his uncle and reclaiming his inheritance, while Alan escapes to France. Stevenson wrote a sequel, Catriona (also known as David Balfour), published in 1893.

Legacy

The novel was first serialised in the magazine Young Folks in 1886 and has remained in print ever since. It has been adapted many times for film and television, including a 1971 film and several BBC productions, and is considered one of Stevenson's finest works.
