Japan Railways Group

Japan Railways Group

Native name: JRグループ

Founded: 1 April 1987

Predecessor: Japanese National Railways

Industry: Rail transport

The Japan Railways Group, more commonly known as the JR Group (JRグループ), is a group of railway companies that were created in 1987 from the privatization of the government-owned Japanese National Railways (JNR). The JR Group lies at the heart of Japan's railway network, operating almost all major intercity rail services.

Constituent companies

The group comprises six regional passenger railway companies — JR Hokkaido, JR East, JR Central, JR West, JR Shikoku and JR Kyushu — together with the nationwide JR Freight, the Railway Technical Research Institute and other shared bodies. The three largest passenger companies, JR East, JR Central and JR West, are fully privatized and listed on stock exchanges, while JR Hokkaido, JR Shikoku and JR Freight remain under state-related ownership structures.

Services

JR companies operate the Shinkansen high-speed network, extensive commuter networks in metropolitan areas such as Tokyo and Osaka, long-distance limited express trains and local services. The companies share common ticketing conventions and jointly issue passes such as the Japan Rail Pass.
