Process group

In a POSIX-conformant operating system, a process group denotes a collection of one or more processes. Process groups are used to control the distribution of signals: a signal directed to a process group is delivered to each process that is a member of the group.

Details

Each process group has a unique integer identifier, the process group ID (PGID), equal to the PID of the process that created the group (the group leader). Processes form new groups or join existing ones with the setpgid() system call. Process groups are further collected into sessions, used for job control on terminals; the foreground process group of a terminal receives keyboard-generated signals such as SIGINT.
