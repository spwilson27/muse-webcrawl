The Shining (novel)

The Shining is a 1977 horror novel by American author Stephen King. It was King's third published novel and his first hardcover bestseller, establishing him as a preeminent horror writer.

Plot

Recovering alcoholic writer Jack Torrance takes a job as winter caretaker of the isolated Overlook Hotel in Colorado, bringing his wife Wendy and five-year-old son Danny, who has psychic abilities — the "shining." The hotel's malevolent presence, fed by past murders, exploits Jack's alcoholism and drives him to attempt killing his family. Chef Dick Hallorann, who also shines, helps Danny and Wendy escape as the hotel's neglected boiler explodes and destroys it — a climax differing from Stanley Kubrick's 1980 film, which King famously disliked.

Sequel

King published a sequel, Doctor Sleep (2013), following the adult Danny. The novel draws on King's own struggles with alcohol. See also The Green Mile.
