Uniform Bar Examination

Uniform Bar Examination
Administrator: National Conference of Bar Examiners
First administered: 2011
Components: MBE, MEE, MPT

The Uniform Bar Examination (UBE) is a bar examination prepared by the National Conference of Bar Examiners and adopted by the majority of US jurisdictions. Because scores are portable between participating jurisdictions, an attorney who passes in one UBE state may transfer the score to another, subject to each jurisdiction's own character, education, and supplementary requirements.

Components

The UBE comprises the Multistate Bar Examination (MBE), a 200-question multiple-choice test; the Multistate Essay Examination (MEE), six essays; and the Multistate Performance Test (MPT), two practical lawyering tasks. It is administered over two days each February and July. Missouri was the first state to adopt the UBE in 2011; New York adopted it in 2016.
