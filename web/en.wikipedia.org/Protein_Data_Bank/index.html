Protein Data Bank

The Protein Data Bank (PDB) is a database for the three-dimensional structural data of large biological molecules, such as proteins and nucleic acids, obtained by X-ray crystallography, NMR spectroscopy and cryo-electron microscopy.

Protein Data Bank

Founded: 1971
Organisation: Worldwide PDB (wwPDB)

History

Established in 1971 at Brookhaven National Laboratory with just seven structures, the PDB grew to hundreds of thousands of entries. It is managed by the Worldwide Protein Data Bank consortium (RCSB PDB, PDBe, PDBj, BMRB).

Contents and formats

Each entry has a four-character PDB ID and atomic coordinates in PDB or mmCIF format, plus experimental metadata and validation reports. Predicted models from AlphaFold are distributed in a separate database.
