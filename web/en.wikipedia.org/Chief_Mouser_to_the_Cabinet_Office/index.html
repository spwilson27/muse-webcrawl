Chief Mouser to the Cabinet Office

The Chief Mouser to the Cabinet Office is the title of the official resident cat of 10 Downing Street, the residence and office of the Prime Minister of the United Kingdom. The cat is employed as a mouser and pet, and the title, though informal, has been officially recognized.

History

Cats have kept the seat of government free of mice since the reign of Henry VIII; records of a cat on the Treasury or Home Office payroll date to the 19th century. Notable mousers include Peter, Munich Mouser, Nelson (Winston Churchill's cat), Wilberforce — who served under four prime ministers — Humphrey, Sybil, Freya, and Larry, who took up residence in 2011 and became a popular media figure.

Duties and status

The Chief Mouser's duties are described jokingly by the government as greeting guests, inspecting security defences, and testing antique furniture for napping quality. The cat is cared for by Downing Street staff, and its maintenance has occasionally prompted parliamentary questions and freedom-of-information requests.
