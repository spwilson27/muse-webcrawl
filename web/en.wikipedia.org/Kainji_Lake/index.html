Kainji Lake

Kainji Lake
Location: Niger State / Kebbi State, Nigeria
Coordinates: approx. 10.4°N 4.6°E
Surface area: about 1,243 km²
Created: 1968 (Kainji Dam)

Kainji Lake, in north-central Nigeria, is a reservoir on the Niger River formed by the Kainji Dam. It lies between Niger State and Kebbi State and is one of the largest lakes in Nigeria, with a surface area of roughly 1,243 square kilometres and a maximum depth of about 12 metres.

Creation

The lake was created in 1968 when the Kainji Dam, a major hydroelectric project, was completed across the Niger River. The filling of the reservoir displaced tens of thousands of people, who were resettled in new communities around the lake. The dam's power station was a cornerstone of Nigeria's electricity supply for decades.

Ecology and economy

Kainji Lake supports an important fishery, though catches have declined over time due to overfishing and falling water levels. Part of the lake and its surroundings fall within Kainji Lake National Park, established in 1978, which combines the former Borgu and Zugurma game reserves and protects savanna wildlife including lions, elephants, hippos, and antelopes. Fishing, farming along the drawdown zone, and livestock grazing are the main livelihoods of lakeside communities.
