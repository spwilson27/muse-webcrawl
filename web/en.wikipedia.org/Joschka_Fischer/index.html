Joschka Fischer

Joseph Martin "Joschka" Fischer (born 12 April 1948) is a German politician of the Alliance 90/The Greens party. He served as foreign minister and vice chancellor of Germany in the federal government of Gerhard Schröder from 1998 to 2005. Fischer has been a leading figure in the West German Greens since the party's founding and was one of the most popular politicians in Germany during his tenure.

Early activism and Green politics

Fischer was active in the student and extra-parliamentary movements of the late 1960s and 1970s in Frankfurt, associated with the "Sponti" scene. A founder of the Hessian Greens, he became the first Green state minister in Germany in 1985 — famously sworn in wearing sneakers as Hesse's environment minister. He led the "Realo" (realist) wing of the Greens toward governing pragmatism.

Foreign minister

As foreign minister, Fischer supported German participation in the NATO intervention in Kosovo in 1999 — the Bundeswehr's first combat deployment since 1945 — and German involvement in Afghanistan, while opposing the 2003 invasion of Iraq, declaring Germany's "No" to the war alongside Schröder. He championed European integration and enlargement. After leaving office in 2005 he took up academic posts and consultancy work.
