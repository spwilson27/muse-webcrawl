Kentucky Oaks

The Kentucky Oaks is a Grade I American thoroughbred horse race for three-year-old fillies, held annually at Churchill Downs in Louisville, Kentucky, on the Friday before the Kentucky Derby. Run over 1⅛ miles (9 furlongs) on dirt, it is the premier race for fillies in the United States.

First run in 1875, the same year as the Derby, the Oaks winner receives a garland of lilies — "Lilies for the Fillies" — and a silver trophy. Notable winners include Rachel Alexandra (2009), who later beat males in the Preakness, Monomoy Girl (2018) and Thorpedo Anna (2024). The event also promotes breast-cancer awareness, with its "Pink Out" and Survivors Parade.
