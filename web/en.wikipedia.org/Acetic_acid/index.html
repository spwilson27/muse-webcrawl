Acetic acid

Acetic acid
Formula: CH₃COOH
Molar mass: 60.05 g/mol
pKa: 4.76
Principal use: Vinegar, chemical feedstock

Acetic acid (systematically ethanoic acid) is an organic compound with the chemical formula CH₃COOH. It is a colorless liquid with a distinctive sour taste and pungent smell, best known as the main component of vinegar apart from water.

Properties

Acetic acid is a weak monoprotic acid with a pKa of 4.76. The pure compound, called glacial acetic acid, freezes at 16.6 °C. It is miscible with water and many organic solvents.

Production

Worldwide production is dominated by methanol carbonylation, which accounts for roughly 75% of output. Acetic acid is also produced by oxidation of acetaldehyde and by bacterial fermentation in vinegar making.

Uses

Major uses include the manufacture of vinyl acetate monomer, acetic anhydride, and acetate esters used in paints, adhesives, and films. Dilute solutions serve as vinegar for food, pickling, and household cleaning.
