Professional development

Professional development, also known as professional education, is learning that helps individuals develop and maintain professional credentials and competencies. It encompasses formal coursework leading to qualifications and certifications, as well as conferences, workshops, mentoring, coaching, and informal learning in practice.

Approaches

Approaches to professional development include case study methods, consultation, coaching, communities of practice, lesson study, mentoring, reflective supervision, and technical assistance. Many regulated professions require continuing professional development (CPD) or continuing education units to maintain licensure, as in medicine, law, accounting, teaching, and engineering.

In education

In schools, professional development refers to training intended to improve teachers' knowledge and classroom practice, and research suggests effective programmes are sustained, content-focused, collaborative, and embedded in teachers' actual work rather than delivered as one-off workshops.
