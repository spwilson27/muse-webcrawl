Pebble (watch)

Pebble was a line of smartwatches developed by Pebble Technology Corporation. The original Pebble was funded through the crowdfunding platform Kickstarter in 2012, raising over US$10 million — at the time the most money raised for any product on the platform. More than one million units were sold.

Features

Pebble watches used a low-power e-paper (memory LCD) display that remained always on and was readable in sunlight, giving the watch a battery life of about a week — far longer than contemporary LCD or OLED smartwatches. The watches connected to Android and iOS smartphones via Bluetooth, displaying notifications, controlling music, and running downloadable watch faces and apps written with the Pebble SDK. Later models included the Pebble Steel (2014), the colour-display Pebble Time (2015, which raised over $20 million on Kickstarter), Pebble Time Steel, Pebble 2, and the round Pebble Time Round.

Closure

In December 2016, Pebble announced it was shutting down; its intellectual property, software assets, and some personnel were acquired by Fitbit for a reported sum of under $40 million. Production of Pebble watches ceased, and official servers were turned off in June 2018. The community-run Rebble project has since maintained replacement web services, keeping remaining watches functional.
