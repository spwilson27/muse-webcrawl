Glenfinnan

Glenfinnan (Scottish Gaelic: Gleann Fhionnain) is a village and glen in the Lochaber area of the Scottish Highlands, at the northern head of Loch Shiel, about 17 miles (27 km) west of Fort William.

History

Glenfinnan is famous as the place where Charles Edward Stuart ("Bonnie Prince Charlie") raised his standard on 19 August 1745, beginning the Jacobite rising of 1745. The Glenfinnan Monument, a tall column surmounted by a statue of a lone Highlander erected in 1815, commemorates the event.

Viaduct and tourism

The Glenfinnan Viaduct, a 21-arch concrete railway viaduct completed in 1898 for the West Highland Line, curves above the glen and has become one of Scotland's most photographed structures, widely known from its appearances in the Harry Potter films, in which the Jacobite steam train crosses it as the Hogwarts Express. The village has a station, a visitor centre managed by the National Trust for Scotland, and is a stop on the West Highland Way corridor of routes.
