Magnum Photos

Founded: 1947, Paris / New York
Founders: Robert Capa, Henri Cartier-Bresson, George Rodger, David "Chim" Seymour
Type: Photographers' cooperative agency

Magnum Photos is an international photographic cooperative owned by its photographer-members, founded in 1947 after the Second World War.

History

Robert Capa proposed an agency giving photographers independence, copyright ownership and editorial control. Founders included Henri Cartier-Bresson, George Rodger and David Seymour. Magnum photographers documented major conflicts, cultures and events of the 20th and 21st centuries.

Membership and work

Membership is by rigorous election, progressing from nominee to associate to full member. Members have included Eve Arnold, Elliott Erwitt, Mary Ellen Mark, Steve McCurry, Josef Koudelka and Susan Meiselas. The agency maintains offices in New York, Paris, London and Tokyo and a vast archive licensed worldwide.
