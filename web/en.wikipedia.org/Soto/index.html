Soto

Soto may refer to several different things:

Religion

  • Sōtō (曹洞宗, Sōtōshū), one of the three traditional sects of Zen Buddhism in Japan — see Soto Zen.

People

  • Hernando de Soto (c. 1500–1542), Spanish explorer and conquistador who led the first European expedition deep into the modern United States and crossed the Mississippi River.
  • Domingo de Soto (1494–1560), Spanish Dominican friar and theologian of the School of Salamanca.
  • Numerous modern athletes and public figures bear the Spanish surname Soto, including baseball player Juan Soto.

Places and other uses

  • Soto, a traditional Indonesian soup (such as soto ayam), found throughout Indonesia and the Malay world.
  • Soto del Real, a municipality in the Community of Madrid, Spain.
  • De Soto, a defunct American automobile marque produced by Chrysler (1928–1961).

For the Zen Buddhist school, see Soto Zen.
