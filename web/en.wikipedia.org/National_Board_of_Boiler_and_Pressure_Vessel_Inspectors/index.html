National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors

The National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors (NBBI) is an American organisation founded in 1919, composed of chief boiler inspectors from US states, territories, Canadian provinces and municipalities that have adopted boiler and pressure vessel safety laws. It is headquartered in Columbus, Ohio.

Purpose and activities

The National Board was created to promote uniform safety standards after decades of catastrophic boiler explosions in the steam era. It commissions boiler and pressure vessel inspectors, administers examinations and training through its National Board Inspection Code (NBIC) — which governs installation, inspection, and repair and alteration of pressure equipment — and maintains registration records of boilers and pressure vessels built to the ASME code, identified by National Board numbers. Manufacturers data reports are registered with the Board, creating a permanent construction record of the equipment.

Stamps and commissions

The Board issues the "R" stamp for organisations performing repairs and alterations, and commissions Authorized Inspectors and Authorized Inspector Supervisors who oversee code construction inspections, typically employed by insurance companies or jurisdictions.
