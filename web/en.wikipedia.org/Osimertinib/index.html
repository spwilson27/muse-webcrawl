Osimertinib

Osimertinib, sold under the brand name Tagrisso, is a medication used to treat non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with specific mutations in the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) gene. Developed by AstraZeneca, it is a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor.

Medical uses

Osimertinib is approved for metastatic NSCLC whose tumours have EGFR exon 19 deletions or the exon 21 L858R mutation, and for cancers that developed the T790M resistance mutation after earlier EGFR inhibitors. It irreversibly binds the mutant receptor while sparing the wild-type form more than earlier drugs.

History

The drug received accelerated approval from the U.S. FDA in November 2015. The FLAURA trial later showed improved survival as first-line therapy, leading to expanded approvals. Common side effects include diarrhoea, rash, dry skin, and nail toxicity; it is taken orally once daily.
