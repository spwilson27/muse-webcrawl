U.S. Route 101 in California

U.S. Route 101 in California is the California segment of U.S. Route 101, a major north–south highway running roughly 790 miles (1,270 km) from Los Angeles to the Oregon border, largely following the historic El Camino Real and the Pacific coast.

Route

The highway begins in downtown Los Angeles, passes through the San Fernando Valley and Ventura, follows the Gaviota Coast to San Luis Obispo and Salinas, crosses San Francisco via Van Ness Avenue and the Golden Gate Bridge, and continues through Redwood country — including the Avenue of the Giants — to Crescent City and Oregon.

History

Commissioned in 1926, Route 101 was gradually upgraded to freeway standards through the twentieth century; its southernmost portion in Los Angeles is among the oldest freeways in the United States. It remains a vital commuter and freight corridor, complementary to Interstate 5 inland.
