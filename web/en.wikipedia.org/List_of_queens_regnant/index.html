List of queens regnant

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A queen regnant is a female monarch who reigns in her own right, as opposed to a queen consort, who is the wife of a reigning king. This list records notable queens regnant (and female monarchs with equivalent titles, such as reigning empresses) throughout history. Because many succession systems preferred males, queens regnant were historically uncommon, though some realms—such as Egypt, England, Spain, and several African and Asian kingdoms—had celebrated female sovereigns.

Ancient world

  • Hatshepsut — pharaoh of Egypt (c. 1479–1458 BC), one of ancient Egypt's most successful rulers
  • Cleopatra VII — last active pharaoh of Egypt (51–30 BC)
  • Artemisia I of Caria — 5th-century BC ruler and naval commander
  • Wu Zetian — the only woman to rule China as emperor in her own name (690–705 AD)
  • Theodora and Irene — reigning empresses of Byzantium; Irene ruled alone 797–802
  • Seondeok and Jindeok — queens of Silla, Korea (7th century)

Medieval and early modern Europe

  • Urraca of León and Castile (1109–1126)
  • Petronilla of Aragon (1137–1164)
  • Margaret I of Denmark — ruler of Denmark, Norway, and Sweden (Kalmar Union), d. 1412
  • Jadwiga of Poland — crowned "king" of Poland (1384–1399)
  • Isabella I of Castile (1474–1504), co-ruler with Ferdinand II of Aragon
  • Mary I of England (1553–1558) and Elizabeth I of England (1558–1603)
  • Mary, Queen of Scots (1542–1567)
  • Christina of Sweden (1632–1654)
  • Anne of Great Britain (1702–1714)
  • Maria Theresa — ruler of the Habsburg lands (1740–1780)
  • Catherine the Great of Russia (1762–1796)

Modern era

  • Victoria of the United Kingdom (1837–1901)
  • Wilhelmina (1890–1948), Juliana (1948–1980), and Beatrix (1980–2013) of the Netherlands
  • Elizabeth II of the United Kingdom (1952–2022) — the longest-reigning British monarch
  • Margrethe II of Denmark (1972–2024)
  • Liliʻuokalani — last sovereign of the Kingdom of Hawaii (1891–1895)
  • Ranavalona I of Madagascar (1828–1861)
