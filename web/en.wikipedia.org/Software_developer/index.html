Software developer

A software developer is a person who designs, builds, and maintains computer software. The role overlaps with software engineer and programmer, though "developer" usually implies a broader remit that includes requirements, design, testing, and deployment, while "programmer" emphasises writing code.

Work

Developers work in nearly every industry, producing applications, operating systems, embedded software, games, and services. They collaborate with product managers, designers, quality-assurance specialists, and other developers, commonly working with version control, issue trackers, and continuous-integration pipelines.

Specialisations

Common specialisations include front-end, back-end, and full-stack web development; mobile development; data engineering; DevOps; and machine-learning engineering. Seniority levels commonly run from junior through mid-level and senior to staff and principal roles.
