Jonathan Howsmon Davis (born January 18, 1971), also known as JD, is an American singer, songwriter and musician. He is best known as the lead vocalist and frontman of the nu metal band Korn, which he co-founded in 1993 and which is regarded as a pioneer of the genre.

Early life

Davis grew up in Bakersfield, California. His father played keyboards professionally and his stepmother's hostility, asthma, and bullying at school shaped the anguished themes of his later lyrics. Trained as a mortician's assistant, he worked in a morgue before music — experiences he has cited as influences. Unusually for a metal singer, he learned the bagpipes, which he plays on several Korn recordings.

Career

Korn's self-titled debut (1994), Life Is Peachy (1996), and the chart-topping Follow the Leader (1998) and Issues (1999) established the band internationally, with Davis's scat-like vocalisations, anguished singing and songs such as "Blind", "Freak on a Leash" and "Falling Away from Me". He contributed vocals to the Queen of the Damned soundtrack and released a solo album, Black Labyrinth, in 2018, having worked on it for over a decade. Davis has also performed as a DJ under the name JDevil.
