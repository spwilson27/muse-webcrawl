Sergeant

Sergeant (abbreviated Sgt) is a rank of non-commissioned officer in many military forces, police forces and other uniformed organisations worldwide. The term derives from the Latin serviens, "one who serves", via the French sergent. Sergeants typically command a squad or section, or serve as senior instructors and administrators.

Usage

In the US Army and Marine Corps, sergeant (E-5) is the first grade of the NCO corps with several higher sergeant grades (staff sergeant, sergeant first class, master sergeant, sergeant major). In the British Army, a sergeant (three chevrons) usually serves as a platoon second-in-command. In police forces in the UK, Commonwealth and many US departments, sergeant is the first supervisory rank above constable or officer.
