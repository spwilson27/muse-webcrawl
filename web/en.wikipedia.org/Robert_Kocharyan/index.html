Robert Kocharyan

Robert Sedraki Kocharyan (born 31 August 1954) is an Armenian politician who served as the second president of Armenia from 1998 to 2008 and as the first president of the Republic of Artsakh (Nagorno-Karabakh) from 1994 to 1997. He previously served as prime minister of Armenia from 1997 to 1998.

Early life and Karabakh career

Kocharyan was born in Stepanakert, in the Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast of Soviet Azerbaijan. He worked as an engineer before entering politics during the Karabakh movement. He became a leader of the Nagorno-Karabakh Armenian leadership during the First Nagorno-Karabakh War, serving as head of the State Defense Committee and then as the first president of the unrecognized Republic of Nagorno-Karabakh.

Presidency of Armenia

After serving as Armenia's prime minister under President Levon Ter-Petrosyan, Kocharyan won the 1998 presidential election following Ter-Petrosyan's resignation, and was re-elected in 2003 amid opposition allegations of fraud. His presidency oversaw continued economic growth driven by construction and remittances, alongside persistent tensions with Azerbaijan over Nagorno-Karabakh. His term ended in 2008, when he was succeeded by Serzh Sargsyan; the post-election period was marked by violent clashes in March 2008 in which ten people died.

Later life

In 2018, following the Armenian revolution, Kocharyan was arrested and charged in connection with the March 2008 crackdown. He later returned to politics as leader of the Armenia Alliance, which placed second in the 2021 parliamentary elections.
