Ontario Highway 401

King's Highway 401, commonly referred to as Highway 401 and also known as the Macdonald–Cartier Freeway, is a controlled-access 400-series highway in the Canadian province of Ontario. It stretches 828 kilometres from Windsor in the west to the Ontario–Quebec border in the east, where it continues as Autoroute 20.

The section through Toronto is among the widest and busiest highways in the world, with over 18 lanes in places and daily traffic exceeding 400,000 vehicles on some segments. A portion near Trenton is designated the Highway of Heroes, honouring fallen Canadian soldiers repatriated via CFB Trenton.

History

Construction began in the 1940s; the highway was completed in stages and fully opened by 1968. The tolled Highway 407 was built to relieve congestion to its north. Major widening and collector-express systems characterise the Toronto and Mississauga sections.
