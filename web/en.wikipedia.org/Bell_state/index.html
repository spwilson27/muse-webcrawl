Bell state

The Bell states or EPR pairs are specific quantum states of two qubits that represent the simplest and maximal examples of quantum entanglement. They form an orthonormal basis known as the Bell basis.

Definition

The four Bell states are superpositions such as (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2 and (|00⟩ − |11⟩)/√2, together with the two corresponding states built from |01⟩ and |10⟩.

Applications

Bell states are central to quantum teleportation, superdense coding and tests of Bell's theorem that rule out local hidden-variable theories.
