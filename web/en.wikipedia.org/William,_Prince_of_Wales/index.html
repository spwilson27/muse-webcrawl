William, Prince of Wales (born 21 June 1982), is the heir apparent to the British throne. He is the elder son of King Charles III and Diana, Princess of Wales, and the elder brother of Prince Harry, Duke of Sussex.

Early life and education

William was born at St Mary's Hospital, London, the first child of the then Prince of Wales. He was educated at Wetherby School, Ludgrove School, and Eton College, and studied geography at the University of St Andrews in Scotland, where he met his future wife, Catherine Middleton. After graduating in 2005 he trained at the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst and served in the British Armed Forces, qualifying as a search and rescue helicopter pilot with the RAF and later flying with the East Anglian Air Ambulance from 2015 to 2017.

Family and role

William married Catherine Middleton at Westminster Abbey on 29 April 2011. They have three children: Prince George, Princess Charlotte, and Prince Louis. On the accession of his father in September 2022 he became Prince of Wales and Duke of Cornwall. His public work focuses on mental health (through the Royal Foundation and the Heads Together campaign), conservation (the Earthshot Prize, launched in 2020), and homelessness.
