Kowloon

Kowloon is an urban area in Hong Kong comprising the Kowloon Peninsula and New Kowloon. Bounded by Victoria Harbour to the south, it lies opposite Hong Kong Island and is one of the most densely populated places on Earth.

History

The name, Cantonese for "nine dragons", is said to refer to eight hills and an emperor. Kowloon south of Boundary Street was ceded by Qing China to Britain under the Convention of Peking (1860), and New Kowloon was leased in 1898 as part of the New Territories. The area was occupied by Japan from 1941 to 1945.

Districts and landmarks

Kowloon includes districts such as Tsim Sha Tsui, Yau Ma Tei, Mong Kok, Sham Shui Po, Kowloon City and Kwun Tong. It is known for Nathan Road, the Temple Street Night Market, the former Kai Tak Airport, and the Kowloon Walled City, demolished in 1993–94 and now a park. Major transport links include the MTR, West Kowloon station for high-speed rail, and the harbour crossings to Hong Kong Island.
