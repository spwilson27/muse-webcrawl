Eye of Ra

The Eye of Ra or Eye of Re, usually depicted as the sun god Ra's right eye, is a being in ancient Egyptian mythology that functions as a violent, feminine counterpart to Ra and a subduing force that protects him. The eye is personified by a number of goddesses, including Hathor, Sekhmet, Bastet, and Wadjet.

Mythology

In the myth of the "Destruction of Mankind," Ra sends his eye, as Hathor, to punish rebellious humans; when she becomes the bloodthirsty Sekhmet, the gods flood the land with beer dyed red to pacify her. The eye also sails with Ra in his solar barque, defending him against the serpent Apep.

Symbolism

The symbol is associated with royal power, protection, and the sun's fierce heat, and is related to, but distinct from, the Eye of Horus.
