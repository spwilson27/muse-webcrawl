Ken (doll)

Ken, full name Kenneth Sean Carson, is a fashion doll introduced by Mattel in 1961 as the male counterpart and boyfriend of Barbie. Named after the son of Mattel founders Ruth and Elliot Handler, Ken debuted at the American International Toy Fair wearing red swim trunks, with flocked hair that soon proved to rub off, leading to moulded plastic hair.

Ken's appearance, outfits and careers have been updated repeatedly over six decades, mirroring changing fashions. He gained renewed cultural prominence through Ryan Gosling's portrayal in the 2023 film Barbie, whose song "I'm Just Ken" became a hit.
