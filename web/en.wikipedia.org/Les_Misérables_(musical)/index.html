Les Misérables, colloquially known as Les Mis, is a sung-through musical with music by Claude-Michel Schönberg, French lyrics by Alain Boublil and Jean-Marc Natel, English lyrics by Herbert Kretzmer, adapted from Victor Hugo's 1862 novel of the same name. Set in early 19th-century France, it follows the ex-convict Jean Valjean, pursued relentlessly by the police inspector Javert, amid student revolutionaries, the innkeeper Thénardiers and the orphan Cosette.

History

The original French concept album appeared in 1980 and a stage production opened at the Palais des Sports in Paris that year. Producer Cameron Mackintosh mounted the English-language version, developed with the Royal Shakespeare Company, at the Barbican Theatre in London on 8 October 1985; it transferred to the Palace Theatre and then the Queen's Theatre (now Sondheim Theatre), becoming the longest-running musical in West End history. The Broadway production opened in 1987, won eight Tony Awards including Best Musical, and ran for 6,680 performances.

Synopsis

Released on parole after 19 years for stealing bread, Valjean breaks parole, is shown mercy by a bishop, and reinvents himself as a factory owner and mayor. He rescues the child Cosette from the Thénardiers, raising her in Paris while Javert hunts him. Years later, Cosette falls in love with the student Marius, who fights at the barricades in the June Rebellion of 1832; Valjean saves Marius's life, confronts Javert — who, unable to reconcile mercy with the law, takes his own life — and dies reconciled, having revealed his past.

Notable songs

  • "I Dreamed a Dream" (Fantine)
  • "On My Own" and "A Little Fall of Rain" (Éponine)
  • "Bring Him Home" and "Who Am I?" (Valjean)
  • "Stars" and "Javert's Suicide" (Javert)
  • "One Day More" and "Do You Hear the People Sing?" (company)
  • "Empty Chairs at Empty Tables" (Marius)

Film and other adaptations

A film adaptation directed by Tom Hooper, starring Hugh Jackman and Russell Crowe, was released in 2012 and won three Academy Awards. The musical has been produced in dozens of countries and languages and seen by well over 100 million people worldwide.
