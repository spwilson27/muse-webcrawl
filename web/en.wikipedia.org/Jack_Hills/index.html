Jack Hills

The Jack Hills are a range of hills in Mid West Western Australia. They are best known as the source of the oldest material of terrestrial origin found to date: Hadean zircons that have been dated to around 4.4 billion years old.

LocationMid West region, Western Australia

Geology

The hills lie within the Narryer Gneiss Terrane of the Yilgarn Craton. Detrital zircons from metasedimentary rocks there have yielded uranium–lead ages up to about 4.404 billion years, only slightly younger than the formation of the Earth itself. Oxygen isotope ratios in these zircons have been interpreted as evidence that liquid water existed at the surface surprisingly early.

Mining

The Jack Hills iron ore mine, operated by Mitsubishi Development and Crosslands Resources, shipped direct-shipping hematite ore from Geraldton before being placed into care and maintenance.
