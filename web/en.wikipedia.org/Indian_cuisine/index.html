Indian cuisine

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Indian cuisine consists of a wide variety of regional and traditional cuisines native to the Indian subcontinent. Given the diversity in soil, climate, culture, ethnic groups, and religions, these cuisines vary substantially and use locally available spices, herbs, vegetables, and fruits. Indian food is also heavily influenced by religious and cultural choices: a large proportion of Indians practise vegetarianism in some form, and Hindu, Jain, Buddhist, Muslim, and Christian traditions have all shaped regional cooking.

Ingredients and seasoning

Indian cooking is noted for its sophisticated use of spices, including cumin, coriander, turmeric, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, mustard seed, fenugreek, and asafoetida, as well as fresh and dried chillies, introduced from the Americas in the 16th century. Spice mixtures such as garam masala and curry powders vary by region and household. Staple grains include rice in the south and east and wheat — as roti, naan, and other flatbreads — in the north; lentils, chickpeas, and other pulses are universal protein sources.

Regional cuisines

  • North India: tandoor-baked breads, rich tomato-and-cream curries, kebabs, and Mughlai dishes such as biryani; butter chicken and dal makhani are famous exports.
  • South India: rice-based dishes including dosa, idli, sambar, and rasam, flavoured with curry leaves, tamarind, and coconut.
  • East India: Bengali fish curries, mustard-based sauces, and sweets such as rasgulla and sandesh.
  • West India: Gujarati vegetarian thalis, Goan seafood and vindaloo (shaped by Portuguese rule), and Maharashtrian street food.
  • Northeast India: fermented foods, smoked meats, and bamboo-shoot preparations.

Global influence

Through migration, Indian cuisine has spread worldwide; the British dish chicken tikka masala and the global popularity of curry houses testify to its reach. Indian restaurants abroad often adapt dishes to local tastes, producing hybrid traditions such as British Indian and Indo-Chinese cuisine.
