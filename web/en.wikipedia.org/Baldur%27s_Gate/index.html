Baldur's Gate

Baldur's Gate is a role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published by Interplay Entertainment in 1998. Set in the Forgotten Realms campaign setting of Dungeons & Dragons, it follows the ward of Gorion in the city of Candlekeep as an iron crisis grips the Sword Coast.

Gameplay

The game uses the Infinity Engine with real-time-with-pause combat and an isometric view, adapting the Advanced Dungeons & Dragons 2nd edition ruleset. Players recruit a party of companions and explore the city of Baldur's Gate and surrounding wilderness.

Reception and legacy

Baldur's Gate was a critical and commercial success credited with revitalizing the computer role-playing genre. It spawned an expansion, Tales of the Sword Coast (1999), and a sequel, Baldur's Gate II: Shadows of Amn (2000). An Enhanced Edition appeared in 2012.
