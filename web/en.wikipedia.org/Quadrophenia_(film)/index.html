Quadrophenia is a 1979 British drama film, loosely based on The Who's 1973 rock opera of the same name. It was directed by Franc Roddam in his feature directing debut and stars Phil Daniels as Jimmy, a young London mod in 1964 whose life of scooters, amphetamines and seaside clashes with rockers leads to disillusionment.

Plot

Jimmy works a dull job in an advertising office and lives for his mod lifestyle: tailored clothes, his Lambretta scooter, and all-night parties. Travelling to Brighton for a bank-holiday confrontation with rockers, he is arrested, and the idols and friendships he trusted collapse around him, ending with the theft and destruction of his idol Ace Face's scooter.

Reception and legacy

The film is regarded as a classic of British youth cinema and a defining depiction of the mod subculture. It launched the career of Phil Daniels and featured early appearances by Sting, Toyah Willcox and Ray Winstone.
