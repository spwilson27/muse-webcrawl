A Beautiful Mind (film)

A Beautiful Mind
Directed by: Ron Howard
Starring: Russell Crowe, Jennifer Connelly
Released: December 21, 2001
Box office: $316.8 million

A Beautiful Mind is a 2001 American biographical drama film directed by Ron Howard and written by Akiva Goldsman, based on Sylvia Nasar's 1998 biography of the mathematician John Nash. Russell Crowe stars as Nash, a brilliant but asocial Princeton mathematician who makes a breakthrough early in his career and then struggles with paranoid schizophrenia.

Plot

In 1947, John Nash arrives at Princeton University and develops his revolutionary concept of governing dynamics (the Nash equilibrium). After work at MIT and secretive code-breaking for a mysterious handler, Nash's behavior turns erratic; he is diagnosed with schizophrenia, and the film reveals that his handler, his roommate, and other figures are hallucinations. With the support of his wife Alicia (Jennifer Connelly), Nash gradually learns to manage his illness without relying solely on medication, returns to teaching, and in 1994 receives the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences.

Reception and awards

The film grossed over $316 million worldwide. It won four Academy Awards: Best Picture, Best Director, Best Actress in a Supporting Role (Connelly), and Best Adapted Screenplay. Critics praised Crowe's performance, though the film was criticized for sanitizing and fictionalizing aspects of Nash's life and illness.
