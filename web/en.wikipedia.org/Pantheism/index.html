Pantheism

Pantheism is the philosophical and religious belief that reality, the universe and nature are identical to divinity, or that everything composes an all-encompassing, immanent God.

History

The term was popularised in the early 18th century, though pantheistic ideas appear in earlier traditions. Baruch Spinoza's philosophy in the Ethics (1677), identifying God with Nature, is one of its most influential expressions.

Varieties

Forms range from classical pantheism, which emphasises God's identity with the cosmos, to naturalistic pantheism, a reverential attitude toward nature and the universe without supernatural claims.
