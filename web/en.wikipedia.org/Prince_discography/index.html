Prince discography

The discography of American musician Prince (1958–2016) consists of 39 studio albums released during his lifetime, plus live albums, compilations, and numerous side projects under various names and with associated acts such as The Revolution and The New Power Generation.

Studio albums

Prince released his debut album For You in 1978. His commercial breakthrough came with 1999 (1982) and the worldwide success of Purple Rain (1984), the soundtrack to his film of the same name, which spent 24 weeks at number one on the Billboard 200. Subsequent acclaimed albums include Parade (1986), Sign o' the Times (1987), Lovesexy (1988), Diamonds and Pearls (1991), and the symbol-titled Love Symbol Album (1992). In the 1990s he famously changed his stage name to an unpronounceable symbol during a dispute with Warner Bros. His final albums, HITnRUN Phase One and Phase Two, appeared in 2015.

Singles

Prince achieved five US Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles: "When Doves Cry", "Let's Go Crazy", "Kiss", "Batdance", and "Cream". Other signature hits include "Little Red Corvette", "Raspberry Beret", "1999", and "The Most Beautiful Girl in the World".
