Equity (economics)

Equity

Accounting identity
Equity = Assets − Liabilities
Forms
Shareholders' equity, owner's equity, home equity

Equity is an economic concept that appears in several related senses. In accounting and finance, equity is the residual interest in the assets of an entity after deducting its liabilities: it represents what the owners would be left with if all assets were sold and all debts paid. For a company this is shareholders' equity (share capital plus retained earnings); for a homeowner it is the market value of the property minus the outstanding mortgage.

Equity as fairness

In economics and public policy, equity also denotes fairness in the distribution of resources, income, or opportunities, contrasted with equality (identical treatment) and with efficiency. Debates about taxation, welfare, and education funding often turn on trade-offs between equity and efficiency. Horizontal equity requires that people in similar circumstances be treated similarly; vertical equity concerns how treatment should differ across people with different income or need.

Uses in finance

"Equities" is also a synonym for stocks or shares, representing ownership stakes traded on securities markets, and private equity denotes investment in companies not publicly listed.
