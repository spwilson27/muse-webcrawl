Christmas traditions

Observed: Late November–January, varying by region

Centred on: Christmas Day, 25 December

Christmas traditions are the customs associated with celebrating Christmas around the world. They combine Christian liturgy and Nativity observances with older winter festivals, regional folklore, family practices, food, music, decoration, and gift giving.

Religious observance

Churches hold Advent services, carol singing, midnight Mass, Nativity plays, and Christmas Day liturgies. Eastern traditions may observe a longer fast and celebrate on different calendar dates.

Home and community customs

Common practices include decorating trees, wreaths, lights, cards, stockings, markets, charitable giving, and gatherings for special meals. Figures such as Santa Claus, Father Christmas, Saint Nicholas, and regional gift bringers occupy a central place in many children's celebrations.

Food and music

Roast meats, puddings, fruitcakes, gingerbread, mince pies, and spiced drinks vary widely by country, as do carols, popular songs, dances, and public festivals. Modern media and migration continually reshape local traditions.
