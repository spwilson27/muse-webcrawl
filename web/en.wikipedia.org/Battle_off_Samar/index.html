Battle off Samar
Date25 October 1944
LocationPhilippine Sea, east of Samar Island
ResultAmerican victory
BelligerentsUnited States; Empire of Japan
Part ofBattle of Leyte Gulf, Pacific War

The Battle off Samar was the centremost action of the Battle of Leyte Gulf, fought on the morning of 25 October 1944. A small American task unit of escort carriers, destroyers and destroyer escorts — "Taffy 3", commanded by Rear Admiral Clifton Sprague — found itself facing Vice Admiral Takeo Kurita's powerful Center Force, including the battleship Yamato, which had slipped through San Bernardino Strait unopposed.

The battle

Outmatched in every respect, Taffy 3's destroyers made repeated torpedo and gun attacks at close range while the escort carriers launched every available aircraft and fled south under smoke and rain squalls. The destroyers Johnston, Hoel and Heermann and the destroyer escort Samuel B. Roberts pressed attacks that confused the Japanese formation; Johnston, Hoel, Samuel B. Roberts and the escort carrier Gambier Bay were sunk.

Aftermath

Mistaking the escort carriers for fleet carriers and believing American reinforcements were near, Kurita broke off the action just as he was in reach of the Leyte invasion shipping, and retired through San Bernardino Strait. The stand of Taffy 3 is celebrated as one of the finest hours in the history of the United States Navy.
