Ti-6Al-4V

MaterialTitanium alloy
CompositionTi, 6% Al, 4% V
ClassificationAlpha–beta alloy
UNS numberR56400

Ti-6Al-4V (also written TC4 or Ti64) is an alpha–beta titanium alloy with high specific strength and excellent corrosion resistance. It is one of the most commonly used titanium alloys and accounts for a large share of all titanium mill product, finding wide application in the aerospace, marine and biomedical industries.

Composition

The alloy consists of titanium alloyed with 6% aluminium and 4% vanadium, with minor amounts of iron, oxygen and other elements. Grade 5 is the standard commercial form; Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI, extra low interstitial) is a higher-purity variant used for surgical implants.

Properties

Ti-6Al-4V has a density of about 4.43 g/cm³, roughly 60% that of steel, while offering comparable tensile strength in the annealed condition. It retains useful strength to temperatures above 400 °C and resists seawater corrosion, but it is difficult to machine and to weld without inert-gas shielding.

Applications

Typical uses include jet-engine compressor blades and discs, airframe components, pressure vessels, orthopaedic and dental implants, and high-performance automotive parts.
