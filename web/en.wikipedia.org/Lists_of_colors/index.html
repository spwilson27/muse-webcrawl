Lists of colors

A colour spectrum and assorted named colours.

These articles contain lists of colors. Named colors are catalogued in several Wikipedia lists, organized alphabetically and by hue: lists covering colors A–F, G–M and N–Z give names, hex triplets and RGB values for hundreds of shades, from abalone and absolute zero through shades of red, green, blue, cyan, magenta, yellow, pink, purple, orange, brown, black, gray and white.

Systems

Colors may be specified in many systems: the RGB and hexadecimal values used in web colors; the HSV and HSL colour models; CMYK for printing; and proprietary or standard sets such as the ISCC–NBS system, Crayola crayon colors, Pantone matching colors, and the X11 color names adopted by web browsers.
