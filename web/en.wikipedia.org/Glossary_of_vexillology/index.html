Glossary of vexillology

Vexillology is the study of flags. The term was coined in 1957 by the American scholar Whitney Smith, from the Latin vexillum, a kind of small flag carried by Roman cavalry. Vexillologists use a specialised vocabulary to describe the parts, patterns and uses of flags.

Terms

banner
Strictly, a flag bearing a coat of arms; loosely, any flag.
canton
The quarter in the upper hoist corner of a flag, as in the blue field of stars on the flag of the United States.
charge
An emblem or device placed on the field of a flag.
civil ensign
A flag flown by civilian (non-government) vessels.
defaced
Describes an ensign with an additional emblem added to the fly or field, as on many British colonial ensigns.
ensign
A flag used at sea to indicate nationality; also a national flag variant for marine use.
field
The background colour of a flag.
fimbriation
A narrow stripe separating two larger areas of colour, used to keep contrasting colours apart.
fly
The half of a flag farthest from the pole; also the horizontal length of a flag.
gonfalon
A flag hanging from a horizontal crossbar, often carried in processions.
guidon
A small swallow-tailed flag used by military units.
half-mast (half-staff)
Flying a flag partway down its pole as a sign of mourning.
hoist
The half of a flag nearest the pole; also the vertical dimension of a flag.
jack
A flag flown at the bow of a ship, typically a national flag or part of one (e.g., the Union Jack).
pall
A Y-shaped division of the field, as on the flag of South Africa.
pennant (pennon)
A long, tapering flag.
saltire
A diagonal cross (X shape), as on the flags of Scotland and Jamaica.
standard
A long tapering heraldic flag bearing badges and mottoes; also a cavalry flag.
triband
A flag of three stripes; horizontal (triband proper) or vertical, including the tricolour.
vexilloid
A flag-like object used as a standard where the material is hung vertically from a crossbar, as in Roman vexilla.
vexillophile
A flag enthusiast or collector; a vexillologist studies flags scientifically.
war flag
A national flag variant used by a country's armed forces on land.
