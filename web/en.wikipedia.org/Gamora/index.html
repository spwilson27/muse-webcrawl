Gamora Zen Whoberi Ben Titan is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-artist Jim Starlin, she first appeared in Strange Tales #180 (1975). The last of her species, the Zehoberei, after their extermination, Gamora was adopted and raised by the supervillain Thanos as an assassin — becoming known as "the deadliest woman in the galaxy" — before rebelling against him and joining the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Fictional biography

Thanos found the infant Gamora after the Badoon (in some versions, the Universal Church of Truth) destroyed her people, and raised her through time-travel manipulations to serve as a weapon against the Magus. As an adult she discovered Thanos's plan to destroy all life and defected, allying with Adam Warlock, Pip the Troll and later the Infinity Watch. Gamora has wielded the Time Gem, founded the Graces (a group of female warriors), and been romantically linked with Warlock, Richard Rider (Nova), and, in film continuity, Peter Quill (Star-Lord). Her adoptive sister is the cybernetic warrior Nebula.

Powers and abilities

Gamora possesses superhuman strength, speed, agility and a rapid healing factor, augmented by Thanos's enhancements. She is among the Marvel Universe's most skilled martial artists, proficient with virtually all weapons; her favoured sword is the blade called Godslayer.

In other media

Zoe Saldaña portrays Gamora in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) and appearing in its sequels and the Avengers films Infinity War (2018) — in which Thanos sacrifices her to obtain the Soul Stone — and Endgame (2019), which introduced a 2014 version of the character who remained in the present timeline and returned in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023).
