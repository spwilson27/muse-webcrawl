R Adams Cowley

R Adams Cowley (1917–1991), founder of the Shock Trauma Center in Baltimore.

R Adams Cowley (July 25, 1917 – October 27, 1991) was an American surgeon who is widely regarded as the father of trauma medicine and the pioneer of the modern trauma center. He founded the Maryland Institute for Emergency Medical Services Systems and the trauma center in Baltimore that now bears his name, the R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center.

Cowley coined the expression the "golden hour" to describe the critical first sixty minutes after a traumatic injury, during which prompt medical treatment has the greatest chance of preventing death. Drawing lessons from military medicine in the Korean and Vietnam wars, he argued that critically injured civilians should be transported rapidly — often by helicopter — directly to specialised centres staffed around the clock by trauma surgeons.

Early life and career

Cowley was born in Layton, Utah, and studied medicine at the University of Utah before completing his surgical training and serving as a surgeon in the United States Army in the European theatre of the Second World War. After the war he joined the faculty of the University of Maryland School of Medicine in Baltimore, where he spent the rest of his career.

Shock Trauma

In the 1960s Cowley persuaded the University of Maryland to open a small clinical research unit for the study of shock, which grew into a dedicated trauma hospital. Against considerable institutional resistance — conventional practice was to take patients to the nearest hospital, not the best-equipped one — he built a statewide system: in 1973 Maryland established the first statewide emergency medical services system in the United States, with state police helicopters delivering patients to the Baltimore center. Mortality among patients reaching the centre fell dramatically, and the Maryland model became the template for trauma systems worldwide.

Legacy

The Shock Trauma Center at the University of Maryland Medical Center was renamed in his honour in 1988. Cowley's concepts of the golden hour, regionalised trauma care, and integrated emergency medical services are now standard practice in emergency medicine.
