Cumberland County, North Carolina

Cumberland County is a county located in the U.S. state of North Carolina. As of the 2020 census, its population was 334,728, making it the fifth-most populous county in the state. Its county seat is Fayetteville.

History

The county was formed in 1754 from Bladen County and named for Prince William, Duke of Cumberland. Fayetteville, first settled as Cross Creek, became an important inland trading centre on the Cape Fear River.

Geography and communities

Located in the Sandhills and Coastal Plain region of south-central North Carolina, the county covers about 658 square miles. Communities include Fayetteville, Hope Mills, Spring Lake, Eastover, Falcon, Godwin, Linden, Stedman, and Wade. A large portion of Fort Liberty (formerly Fort Bragg), one of the largest U.S. Army installations, lies in the county.

Economy and transport

The military presence at Fort Liberty dominates the local economy, alongside healthcare, education, and retail. Interstate 95 and U.S. routes 13, 301, and 401 cross the county.
