Downtown Las Vegas

Fremont Street in downtown Las Vegas.

Downtown Las Vegas is the central business district and historic centre of Las Vegas, Nevada. It was the original townsite and the gambling district of the city prior to the Strip, centring on Fremont Street, home to classic casinos such as the Golden Nugget, Binion's and the Four Queens.

History

Las Vegas was founded as a railroad town in 1905, and downtown grew around Fremont Street, which became the first paved street in the city and the site of Nevada's first gaming licence. After gambling focus shifted to the Strip from the 1950s onward, downtown declined, but redevelopment from the 1990s—including the Fremont Street Experience canopy, opened in 1995—revitalised the area.

Attractions

Beyond the casinos, downtown includes the Neon Museum, the Mob Museum, the Arts District, and the Downtown Container Park. The area has attracted tech and start-up investment through revitalisation initiatives in the 2010s.
