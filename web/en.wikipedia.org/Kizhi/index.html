Kizhi

Kizhi is an island in Lake Onega in the Republic of Karelia, Russia, famous for its ensemble of historic wooden architecture, now an open-air museum and UNESCO World Heritage Site (the Kizhi Pogost).

The pogost

The enclosure contains two 18th-century wooden churches and a bell tower: the 37-metre Church of the Transfiguration (1714), crowned by 22 onion domes and built without nails using traditional Russian carpentry, and the Church of the Intercession (1764).

Museum

The Kizhi State Open-Air Museum, established in the 1960s, has gathered dozens of wooden houses, chapels, barns and windmills from around Karelia, preserving a record of northern Russian peasant building.
