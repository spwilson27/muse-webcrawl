Pokémon Detective Pikachu is a 2019 fantasy film based on the Pokémon franchise and the 2016 video game Detective Pikachu. Directed by Rob Letterman, it was the first live-action Pokémon film, blending live actors with computer-animated Pokémon characters.

Plot

Former Pokémon trainer Tim Goodman travels to Ryme City, where humans and Pokémon live side by side, after learning of his detective father's apparent death. There he meets his father's partner, a wisecracking amnesiac Pikachu whom only Tim can understand. Together they investigate a conspiracy involving a mysterious gas that drives Pokémon into a frenzy.

Reception

The film received mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for its visual effects and Ryan Reynolds's voice performance as Pikachu. It grossed around $450 million worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing video game film adaptations of its time.
