Arm Ltd.

Arm Limited (styled arm) is a British semiconductor and software design company based in Cambridge, England. Rather than manufacturing chips itself, Arm licenses its processor designs and instruction set architecture to other companies, which fabricate their own chips. Arm's energy-efficient designs dominate the mobile device market and are used in the overwhelming majority of smartphones, as well as in tablets, embedded systems, servers, and laptop and desktop computers such as Apple's M-series machines.

History

Arm was founded in 1990 as Advanced RISC Machines, a joint venture between Acorn Computers, Apple Computer, and VLSI Technology, to develop the ARM architecture first created at Acorn for the BBC Micro and Archimedes computers. The company grew rapidly with the rise of mobile phones in the 1990s and 2000s. It was listed on the London Stock Exchange and NASDAQ until 2016, when Japan's SoftBank Group acquired it for about £24 billion. A proposed sale to Nvidia, announced in 2020, was abandoned in 2022 amid regulatory opposition. Arm returned to the public markets with an IPO on NASDAQ in September 2023.

Business model

Arm earns revenue from licence fees and per-chip royalties paid by licensees such as Apple, Qualcomm, Samsung, MediaTek, and Amazon. Its architectures include the Armv8 and Armv9 families and the Cortex, Neoverse, and Mali product lines.
