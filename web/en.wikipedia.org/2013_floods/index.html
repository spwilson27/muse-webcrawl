2013 floods

2013 floods may refer to several major flooding events that occurred around the world in 2013:

Events

  • 2013 European floods — severe flooding in Central Europe in late May and June 2013, affecting Germany, Austria, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary and neighbouring countries, with the Danube and Elbe rivers reaching record levels in places.
  • 2013 North India floods — devastating floods and landslides in the North Indian state of Uttarakhand in June 2013, caused by a cloudburst and heavy monsoon rains, killing several thousand people and destroying towns and pilgrimage routes.
  • 2013 Alberta floods — flooding in the Canadian province of Alberta in June 2013, among the costliest natural disasters in Canadian history, forcing the evacuation of much of downtown Calgary.
  • 2013 Colorado floods — flooding along Colorado's Front Range in September 2013 after days of heavy rain, damaging thousands of homes.
  • 2013 Southern Thailand floods and other regional events in Asia and Australia.

Overview

The year 2013 saw an unusual concentration of severe flood disasters across multiple continents, prompting debate about flood defences, land-use planning and the influence of a changing climate on extreme rainfall events.
