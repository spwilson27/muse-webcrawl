Jerk sauce

Jerk sauce is a spicy sauce and marinade from Jamaica used in jerk cooking. It is built on the characteristic combination of allspice (pimento) and Scotch bonnet peppers, which give it both its aromatic warmth and its intense heat.


Jamaican jerk sauce

Ingredients

A typical jerk sauce blends Scotch bonnet peppers, ground allspice, fresh thyme, scallions, garlic, ginger, onion, brown sugar, soy sauce, lime juice or vinegar, and warm spices such as cinnamon and nutmeg. The ingredients are blended into a paste-like marinade.

Use

Jerk sauce is primarily used as a marinade for meats before grilling — especially chicken and pork — and as a table sauce served alongside the finished dish. It is also applied to seafood, sausages and vegetables. Commercial bottled versions are produced in Jamaica and abroad and are a staple export of Jamaican cuisine.
