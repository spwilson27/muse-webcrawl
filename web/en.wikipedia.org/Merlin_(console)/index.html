Merlin (console)

Merlin

Manufacturer: Parker Brothers

Release date: 1978

Inventor: Bob Doyle

Type: Handheld electronic game

Merlin, also known as Merlin the Electronic Wizard, is a handheld electronic game released by Parker Brothers in 1978. The console was invented by former NASA employee Bob Doyle and his wife Holly.

Gameplay

Merlin's array of 3×3 touch-sensitive buttons with red LEDs supports six different games, including Tic-Tac-Toe, a music-making mode, an echo/memory game similar to Simon, and Blackjack 13. It was powered by batteries and included a speaker for simple tones.

Reception and legacy

Merlin was one of the most popular toys of the 1978 Christmas season, selling over 700,000 units. A redesigned version, Merlin: The 10th Quest, appeared in the 1990s, and the design is remembered as a landmark in early handheld electronic gaming.
