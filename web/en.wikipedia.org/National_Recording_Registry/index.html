National Recording Registry

The National Recording Registry is a list of sound recordings deemed "culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant" by the Librarian of Congress for preservation in the United States Library of Congress.

Background

The registry was established under the National Recording Preservation Act of 2000. Each year the Librarian of Congress, advised by the National Recording Preservation Board and public nominations, selects 25 recordings that are at least ten years old for inclusion.

Selections

Selections range across music, spoken word, radio broadcasts, and historical recordings, from early Edison cylinders to landmark albums. Recordings are preserved in high-quality formats to safeguard America's recorded sound heritage.
