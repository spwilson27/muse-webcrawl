List of Fields Medalists

The Fields Medal, often described as the Nobel Prize of mathematics, is awarded every four years at the International Congress of Mathematicians to mathematicians under 40 years of age.

Maryam Mirzakhani (2014) was the first woman to receive the medal; Maryna Viazovska was the second, in 2022.
