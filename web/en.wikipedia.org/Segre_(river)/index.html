Segre (river)

The Segre (Catalan: Segre; French: Sègre) is a river in the northeast of the Iberian Peninsula, a major tributary of the Ebro. It is about 265 kilometres long.

Course

The Segre rises in the French Pyrenees near the Pic del Segre, crosses the Cerdanya valley, and enters Spain, passing through La Seu d'Urgell, Balaguer, and Lleida. It joins the Ebro at Mequinenza in Aragon. Important tributaries include the Valira, Noguera Pallaresa, Noguera Ribagorçana, and Cinca, the last joining close to the confluence with the Ebro.

Use

The river's waters are extensively used for irrigation and hydroelectric generation, with major reservoirs such as Oliana and Rialb on its course and the large Mequinenza reservoir near its mouth.
