Escape from Alcatraz (film)

Escape from Alcatraz is a 1979 American prison thriller film directed by Don Siegel and starring Clint Eastwood. It dramatises the June 1962 prisoner escape from Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary by Frank Morris and brothers John and Clarence Anglin.

Plot

Frank Morris, an intelligent convict, is transferred to Alcatraz. He befriends fellow inmates and meticulously plans an escape: digging through cell walls with spoons, crafting dummy heads from soap and hair to fool guards, and building a raft from raincoats. On the night of 11 June 1962 the men vanish; their fate remains unknown.

Cast

Clint Eastwood as Frank Morris, Patrick McGoohan as the Warden, Fred Ward as John Anglin, Jack Thibeau as Clarence Anglin, and Danny Glover in an early role.

Reception

The film was a box-office success and is regarded as one of the finest prison films.
