Spanish Civil War


Combat during the Spanish Civil War

The Spanish Civil War (Spanish: Guerra Civil Española) was fought in Spain between the Republicans, loyal to the left-leaning Popular Front government of the Second Spanish Republic, and the Nationalists, a rebel group of the Spanish Republican Armed Forces led by a military junta under General Francisco Franco. The war lasted from 17 July 1936 to 1 April 1939 and ended with the Nationalist victory and the establishment of Franco's dictatorship, which lasted until his death in 1975.

Background

Spain in the early 1930s was bitterly divided between left and right: landowners, the Catholic Church, and much of the officer corps stood against republican reformers, socialists, communists, and anarchists. The victory of the Popular Front in the elections of February 1936 intensified polarization, street violence, and political assassinations. A military coup began in Spanish Morocco on 17 July 1936 and spread to the mainland; it failed to seize the whole country, and Spain split into rival zones.

The war

Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy supplied Franco with aircraft, troops, and weapons, including the German Condor Legion, which bombed the Basque town of Guernica in April 1937 — an atrocity immortalized by Picasso's painting. The Soviet Union sold arms to the Republic, and some 40,000 foreign volunteers fought in the International Brigades. Britain and France pursued non-intervention. Major campaigns included the siege of Madrid, the battles of Jarama, Brunete, Teruel, and the Ebro (1938), and the Nationalist drive that split the Republican zone in two. Barcelona fell in January 1939; Franco declared victory on 1 April 1939.

Consequences

Estimates of the dead range from 350,000 to over 500,000, including victims of repression in both zones — the Nationalist "White Terror" and the Republican "Red Terror". Hundreds of thousands of Republicans fled into exile. The war is widely regarded as a prelude to the Second World War, in which rival ideologies and new weapons were tested.
