Smart glass

Smart glass, also called switchable glass or privacy glass, is glass whose light-transmission properties change when voltage, light, or heat is applied. The glass can switch between transparent and translucent or opaque states.

Technologies

The main technologies are electrochromic coatings, suspended-particle devices (SPD), polymer-dispersed liquid crystal (PDLC) films, and thermochromic and photochromic materials. Electrochromic windows tint gradually and hold their state with little power; PDLC films switch almost instantly between clear and frosted.

Applications

Smart glass is used in architectural windows and skylights for glare and solar control, in privacy partitions in offices and hospitals, in aircraft windows (such as those of the Boeing 787), and in automotive sunroofs and mirrors.
