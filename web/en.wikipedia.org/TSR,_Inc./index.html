TSR, Inc.

TSR, Inc. was an American game publishing company, best known as the original publisher of Dungeons & Dragons (D&D), the first and most influential tabletop role-playing game. Founded in 1973 in Lake Geneva, Wisconsin, as Tactical Studies Rules by Gary Gygax and Don Kaye, the company published D&D in 1974, written by Gygax and Dave Arneson, and grew rapidly during the game's late-1970s and early-1980s boom.

History

After Don Kaye's death in 1975, the company was reorganised as TSR Hobbies, Inc., with investment from Brian and Kevin Blume. Gygax lost control of the company in 1985 to Lorraine Williams, under whose management TSR expanded into novels — including the bestselling Dragonlance and Forgotten Realms series — board games, and the magazine Dragon. By the mid-1990s the company was heavily indebted.

Acquisition

In 1997 the financially troubled TSR was acquired by Wizards of the Coast, publisher of Magic: The Gathering, which itself was bought by Hasbro in 1999. The TSR name was retired as a brand, although it was briefly revived as a trademark by unrelated companies in the 2010s and 2020s.

Products

Beyond D&D and its Advanced Dungeons & Dragons line, TSR published games including Gamma World, Star Frontiers, Marvel Super Heroes and Boot Hill, and settings such as Greyhawk, Ravenloft, Dark Sun and Planescape.
