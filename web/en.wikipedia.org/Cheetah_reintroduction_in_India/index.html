Cheetah reintroduction in India

The cheetah reintroduction in India is a programme to restore cheetahs to India, where the native Asiatic cheetah was declared extinct in 1952 after the last individuals were shot in 1948.

Under "Project Cheetah", eight African cheetahs from Namibia arrived in September 2022 and twelve from South Africa in February 2023. They were released into Kuno National Park in Madhya Pradesh, the site selected after earlier proposals involving Iran's cheetahs failed.

The project, overseen by the National Tiger Conservation Authority, has seen successes including cubs born in India, but also several adult deaths from various causes, prompting debate among conservationists about habitat, prey density, and the suitability of African cheetahs as substitutes for the Asiatic subspecies.
