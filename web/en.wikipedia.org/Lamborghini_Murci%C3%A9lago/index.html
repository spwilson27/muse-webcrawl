The Lamborghini Murciélago is a sports car produced by Lamborghini between 2001 and 2010. The flagship V12 model of its period, it succeeded the Diablo and was the first new model launched after Audi took ownership of the company. Its name, Spanish for "bat", honours a legendary fighting bull.

Design

Styled by Luc Donckerwolke, the Murciélago features scissor doors, a low wedge profile, and active air intakes that rise from the rear bodywork at speed. The original car used a 6.2-litre V12 with 572 hp; the 2006 LP 640 update enlarged the engine to 6.5 litres and 631 hp.

Variants

A Roadster version, the limited Reventón (which previewed Lamborghini's later design language), racing R-GT and racing versions, and the final lightweight LP 670-4 SuperVeloce rounded out production. Total production was 4,099 cars before the Aventador took over in 2011.
