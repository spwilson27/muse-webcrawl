Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is a state in southern India, bordered by Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, the Bay of Bengal and the Indian Ocean. Its capital and largest city is Chennai. It is the home of the Tamil people and Tamil language, one of the world's oldest living languages.

History

The region was ruled by the Chera, Chola and Pandya dynasties, famed for temple architecture, literature and maritime trade. Later it came under Vijayanagara, Nayak, Maratha, Mughal and British rule, forming much of the Madras Presidency. After independence it became Madras State, renamed Tamil Nadu in 1969.

Culture

Tamil Nadu is known for Dravidian temple architecture, Bharatanatyam dance, Carnatic music, Pongal festival and Tamil literature such as the Tirukkural. Cuisine includes dosa, idli, sambar and filter coffee.

Economy

One of India's most industrialised and urbanised states, with strengths in automobiles around Chennai, textiles, leather, IT and renewable energy.
