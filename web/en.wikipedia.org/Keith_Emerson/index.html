Keith Noel Emerson (2 November 1944 – 11 March 2016) was an English keyboardist, songwriter and composer who played a founding role in the progressive rock bands the Nice and Emerson, Lake & Palmer (ELP). Widely regarded as one of the greatest keyboard players of the rock era, he was famous for his virtuoso Hammond organ and Moog synthesiser playing and flamboyant stage performances.

Career

With the Nice in the late 1960s Emerson pioneered the fusion of rock with classical and jazz elements. In 1970 he formed ELP with Greg Lake and Carl Palmer; albums such as Tarkus and Brain Salad Surgery made them one of the most successful progressive rock acts of the decade. Emerson was the first artist to tour with a Moog synthesiser and composed a piano concerto and several film scores.

Death

Emerson died in 2016. In later years he had suffered from depression and a degenerative nerve condition affecting his hands.
