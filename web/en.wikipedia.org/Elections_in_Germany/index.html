Elections in Germany

Elections in Germany include elections to the Bundestag (federal parliament), to the parliaments of the sixteen Länder (states), to local councils, and to the European Parliament.

Federal elections

The Bundestag is elected every four years by mixed-member proportional representation. Each voter casts two votes: a first vote for a constituency candidate and a second vote for a party list. Parties must pass a five-percent threshold (or win three constituencies) to enter the Bundestag. Citizens aged 18 and over may vote.

The federal election determines the composition from which the Chancellor is elected. Major parties include the CDU/CSU, SPD, Alliance 90/The Greens, FDP, AfD, and Die Linke.

Administration

Elections are overseen by the Federal Returning Officer and state returning officers under the Basic Law and federal electoral law.
