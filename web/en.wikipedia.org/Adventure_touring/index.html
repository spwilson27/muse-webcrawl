Adventure touring

Adventure touring refers to long-distance travel — most commonly by bicycle or motorcycle — undertaken for recreation and exploration, often on mixed or unpaved surfaces and with the traveler carrying their own equipment. Bicycle adventure touring overlaps with bicycle touring and bikepacking, while motorcycle adventure touring uses large dual-sport or adventure motorcycles.

Adventure touring
Modes: Bicycle, motorcycle
Gear: Panniers, luggage, camping equipment
Related: Touring, overlanding

Bicycle adventure touring

Cyclists travel self-supported, carrying gear in panniers or frame bags, often following established routes such as the TransAmerica Trail or the Great Divide Mountain Bike Route. Trips range from overnight excursions to multi-year journeys around the world.

Motorcycle adventure touring

Adventure motorcycles such as the BMW GS series are designed for long distances on both paved and unpaved roads. Long-distance rides, including circumnavigations popularized by books and television series, are a prominent part of the culture.
