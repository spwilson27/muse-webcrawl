Ludovico Ariosto

Born8 September 1474, Reggio Emilia, Italy
Died6 July 1533, Ferrara, Italy
OccupationPoet, courtier
Notable workOrlando Furioso

Ludovico Ariosto (1474–1533) was an Italian Renaissance poet, best known as the author of the romance epic Orlando Furioso (1516, final edition 1532), a continuation of Matteo Maria Boiardo's Orlando Innamorato.

Life and work

Ariosto spent most of his career in the service of the Este family in Ferrara, as a courtier of Cardinal Ippolito d'Este and later of Duke Alfonso I. Orlando Furioso, written in ottava rima, weaves together chivalric adventure, love, magic and irony around the paladin Roland (Orlando), driven mad by love, and is regarded as one of the masterpieces of Italian literature. Ariosto also wrote comedies, satires and lyric poetry, and served as governor of the Garfagnana region.
