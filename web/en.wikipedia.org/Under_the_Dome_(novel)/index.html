Under the Dome is a science fiction novel by American author Stephen King, published in 2009. It tells the story of the small town of Chester's Mill, Maine, which is suddenly cut off from the rest of the world by an invisible, semipermeable barrier.

Plot

As supplies dwindle and air quality deteriorates, corrupt selectman "Big Jim" Rennie consolidates power, while ex-army drifter Dale "Barbie" Barbara and journalist Julia Shumway lead the resistance and try to uncover the dome's origin.

Adaptation

The novel was adapted into a CBS television series that ran for three seasons (2013–2015).
