Amatrice

Amatrice is a town and comune in the province of Rieti, in the Lazio region of central Italy, set in the Apennines near the borders with Abruzzo, Umbria, and Marche. It gives its name to the pasta sauce amatriciana, made with guanciale, pecorino, and tomato.

2016 earthquake

On 24 August 2016, a magnitude 6.2 earthquake struck central Italy with its epicentre near Accumoli; Amatrice was among the worst-hit towns, with much of its historic centre destroyed and the majority of the disaster's roughly 300 deaths occurring in the area. Reconstruction of the town has proceeded slowly since.
