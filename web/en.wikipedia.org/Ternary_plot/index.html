Ternary plot

A ternary plot, also called a ternary graph, triangle plot, simplex plot or de Finetti diagram, is a barycentric plot on three variables which sum to a constant. It graphically depicts the proportions of three variables as positions inside an equilateral triangle, where each corner represents 100% of one component.

Reading the plot

The proportion of each component is read along the axis opposite its corner: a point at the apex is pure in that component, and points along the opposite edge contain none of it. Grid lines drawn parallel to the sides mark constant percentages of each variable.

Uses

Ternary plots are widely used in geology for soil texture and rock composition, in chemistry for phase diagrams and flammability ranges, in metallurgy, and in other fields where three-part compositions (compositional data) must be displayed.
