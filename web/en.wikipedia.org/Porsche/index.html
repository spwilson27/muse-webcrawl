Porsche

Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, usually shortened to Porsche, is a German automobile manufacturer specialising in luxury, high-performance sports cars, SUVs, and sedans, headquartered in Stuttgart, Baden-Württemberg. The company was founded by Ferdinand Porsche in 1931 as an engineering consultancy; its first production car, the Porsche 356, appeared in 1948 under his son Ferry Porsche.

Models

The company's defining model is the rear-engined 911, in production since 1964 and developed through successive generations. Other lines include the mid-engined Boxster and Cayman (718), the Panamera grand tourer, the Taycan electric sports car, and the Macan and Cayenne SUVs, the latter becoming major profit centres. Limited hypercars have included the Carrera GT and the hybrid 918 Spyder.

Motorsport

Porsche is the most successful marque in endurance racing, with a record number of overall victories at the 24 Hours of Le Mans, and a long history in rallying, touring cars, and Formula One as an engine supplier.

Corporate

Porsche AG was the subject of a celebrated takeover battle with Volkswagen in 2008–2009, ending with Volkswagen acquiring the sports-car business while the Porsche-Piëch family holding, Porsche SE, became Volkswagen Group's controlling shareholder. Porsche AG was relisted on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in September 2022 in one of Europe's largest IPOs.
