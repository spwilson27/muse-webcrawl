UEFA Nations League

The UEFA Nations League is a biennial international football competition contested by the senior men's national teams of the 55 member associations of UEFA. It was inaugurated in 2018, in part to replace international friendly matches with more competitive fixtures and to give every national team meaningful matches against similarly ranked opponents.

Format

Teams are divided into four leagues (A, B, C and D) based on their UEFA national team coefficient ranking, with promotion and relegation between leagues after each edition. League A group winners advance to the Nations League Finals, a knockout tournament of semi-finals, a third-place play-off and a final held in one of the participating countries. The competition also provides an alternative route into play-offs for the UEFA European Championship and FIFA World Cup qualification.

History

UEFA's congress approved the competition in March 2014, and the first edition began in September 2018. Portugal won the inaugural Finals on home soil in June 2019, beating the Netherlands 1–0. France won the second edition in 2021, defeating Spain in Milan, and Spain won the third in 2023, beating Croatia on penalties in Rotterdam. From the 2024–25 edition, the format was expanded with quarter-finals for League A and promotion/relegation play-offs between leagues.

Background

The idea of a "league of nations" tournament was championed by UEFA president Michel Platini and modelled partly on league structures in club football; supporters argued it would raise the standard of international football outside the major confederations' calendars and reduce one-sided friendlies.
