P. Adams Sitney

P. Adams Sitney (born 9 August 1944) is an American film historian and critic, best known as the author of Visionary Film: The American Avant-Garde (1974), the standard history of American experimental cinema. He taught at Princeton University for many years.

Work

Sitney co-founded the journal Film Culture circle and Anthology Film Archives in New York, where he helped define the canon of the New American Cinema. In Visionary Film he coined the term "structural film" for the work of Michael Snow, Hollis Frampton, Paul Sharits and others, and analysed the "lyrical film" of Stan Brakhage. His later books include Modernist Montage (1990) and Eyes Upside Down (2008), on visionary film and modernist literature.
