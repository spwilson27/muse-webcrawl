Media literacy

Media literacy is the ability to access, analyse, evaluate, create and act using all forms of communication. It encompasses the practices that allow people to critically engage with media messages, from print and broadcast to digital and social media.

Key concepts

Media literacy education teaches that all media messages are constructed, use creative languages with their own rules, are experienced differently by different people, embed values and points of view, and are mostly organized to gain profit or power. Scholars and organizations such as the National Association for Media Literacy Education promote core principles and competencies for analysing media.

Development

The field grew from media education movements in the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia in the mid-20th century and gained prominence with the spread of television and, later, the internet. Concerns about misinformation and "fake news" have renewed interest in media literacy curricula in schools worldwide.
