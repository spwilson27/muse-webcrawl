Compsognathus

KingdomAnimalia
CladeDinosauria; Theropoda
PeriodLate Jurassic, about 150 million years ago

Compsognathus is a genus of small, bipedal, carnivorous theropod dinosaur. It lived during the Late Jurassic period and, at roughly the size of a turkey, was long considered the smallest known dinosaur.

Discovery

Fossils come from the Solnhofen limestone of Germany and from France. One specimen preserves the remains of a small lizard in its stomach, showing its diet of small prey.

Description

Compsognathus had a long tail, slender hind limbs, and small forelimbs with two functional digits. Its relationship to Archaeopteryx, found in the same deposits, informed early debates about the origin of birds.
