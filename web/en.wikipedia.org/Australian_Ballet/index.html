The Australian Ballet is the largest classical ballet company in Australia. It was founded in 1962 by the English dancer and administrator Dame Peggy van Praagh, and gave its first performance, Swan Lake, at Her Majesty's Theatre in Sydney on 2 November 1962.

History

The company grew out of the earlier Borovansky Ballet. Rudolf Nureyev was a frequent guest artist in the 1960s and 1970s and created productions for the company. The Australian Ballet is based in Melbourne, where it performs at the State Theatre of Arts Centre Melbourne, and tours extensively nationally and internationally.

Repertoire

The repertoire combines the nineteenth-century classics — including The Sleeping Beauty, The Nutcracker and Giselle — with works by choreographers such as Graeme Murphy, whose Nutcracker – The Story of Clara became a signature production, and contemporary Australian creators.

School

The Australian Ballet School, founded in 1964, is the company's official training institution and one of Australia's leading dance schools.
