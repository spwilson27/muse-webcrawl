Lockheed Martin C-130J Super Hercules

The Lockheed Martin C-130J Super Hercules is an American four-engine turboprop military transport aircraft, the newest and only current production model of the C-130 Hercules family, in service since the late 1990s.

Design

The J-model introduced Rolls-Royce AE 2100 engines with composite six-blade propellers, a glass cockpit with head-up displays, and digital avionics that reduced the flight-deck crew to two pilots. It offers improved range, speed and short-field performance over earlier C-130s.

Variants and service

Variants include the stretched C-130J-30, the KC-130J tanker, the HC-130J and MC-130J special-operations models, and the LM-100J civil version. Operators include the United States Air Force, the Royal Air Force and many other air forces worldwide.
