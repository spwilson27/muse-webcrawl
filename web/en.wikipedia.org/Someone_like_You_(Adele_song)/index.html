"Someone like You" is a song recorded by English singer-songwriter Adele for her second studio album, 21 (2011). Written with Dan Wilson, it is a piano-led ballad in which the narrator comes to terms with a former lover's new relationship.

Success

Following Adele's performance at the 2011 Brit Awards, the song rose to number one in the United Kingdom, where it became the best-selling single of the year. It also topped the US Billboard Hot 100 — the first piano-and-voice ballad to do so — and reached number one in more than twenty countries. It is one of the best-selling digital singles of all time.

Composition

Set in A major with a simple recurring piano figure, the song builds from a restrained verse to a soaring chorus ("Never mind, I'll find someone like you"). Critics praised its emotional directness and Adele's vocal performance, and it won numerous year-end accolades.
