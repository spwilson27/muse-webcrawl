Emma (novel)

Emma is a novel written by English author Jane Austen. It is set in the fictional country village of Highbury and the surrounding estates of Hartfield, Randalls, and Donwell Abbey, and involves the relationships among people from a small number of families. The novel was first published in December 1815, with its title page listing a publication date of 1816.

Plot

Emma Woodhouse, "handsome, clever, and rich", has a talent for matchmaking but a habit of misreading the affections of others. After the marriage of her governess Miss Taylor, she takes Harriet Smith under her wing and attempts to arrange her romances, with consequences that unfold through a series of misunderstandings, including those involving Mr. Elton, Frank Churchill, and Jane Fairfax. Only the corrections of her friend and brother-in-law, Mr. Knightley, eventually lead Emma to recognise her own feelings and errors.

Reception and adaptations

Emma is regarded as one of Austen's finest achievements, notable for its use of free indirect discourse and its comedy of manners. It has been adapted many times for film and television, including the 1995 film Clueless, a modern retelling set in Beverly Hills, and a 2020 film adaptation.
