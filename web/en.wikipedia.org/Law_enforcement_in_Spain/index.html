Law enforcement in Spain

Law enforcement in Spain is divided between national, autonomous-community and local forces under the 1978 Constitution and the 1986 Organic Law on Security Forces.

National forces

The National Police Corps (Cuerpo Nacional de Policía), a civilian force, polices cities and handles criminal investigation, immigration, identity documents and counterterrorism support. The Civil Guard (Guardia Civil), a gendarmerie founded in 1844, polices rural areas, highways, coasts, borders, firearms and environmental crime, and has both military and civilian roles.

Regional and local

Some autonomous communities have their own police with broad powers: Catalonia's Mossos d'Esquadra, the Basque Ertzaintza, Navarre's Policía Foral and the Canary Islands police. Municipalities maintain local police (Policía Municipal/Local) for traffic and local ordinances. Specialist agencies include Customs Surveillance and prison and traffic services.
