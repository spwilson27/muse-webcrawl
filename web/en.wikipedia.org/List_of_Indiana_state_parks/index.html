List of Indiana state parks

The U.S. state of Indiana operates a system of state parks managed by the Indiana Department of Natural Resources. The system began when McCormick's Creek and Turkey Run became the first state parks in 1916, the state park system's centennial gift from the people of Indiana.

Parks

Well-known parks include Brown County (the largest, famed for autumn foliage), Turkey Run and Shades (sandstone gorges), Indiana Dunes (on Lake Michigan, adjacent to the national park), Clifty Falls, Spring Mill (with its pioneer village and limestone caves), Pokagon (with a toboggan run), McCormick's Creek, Fort Harrison, Ouabache, and White River.

Administration

Indiana also operates state recreation areas and state forests separately from the roughly two dozen state parks.
