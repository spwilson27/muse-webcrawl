Lily Dale is a hamlet in the town of Pomfret in Chautauqua County, New York, United States, on the east shore of Cassadaga Lake. Developed from 1879 as a camp meeting ground for Spiritualists, it is home to the Lily Dale Assembly, and describes itself as the world's largest centre for the science, philosophy and religion of Spiritualism. The gated community requires, for residence, membership in a Spiritualist church, and is notable for its population of registered mediums.

History

Modern Spiritualism began nearby in western New York — the Fox sisters' rappings at Hydesville in 1848 launched the movement, and the region (the "Burned-over district") was a hotbed of religious innovation. In 1879 the Cassadaga Lake Free Association was organised and purchased land at what was then called "The Dale"; it was renamed the City of Light and, in 1906, formally Lily Dale. Prominent visitors over the years included suffragists and reformers — Susan B. Anthony spoke at Lily Dale — reflecting the overlap between Spiritualism and the women's rights movement.

The Assembly today

About 275 people live in Lily Dale year-round, but during the summer season (late June to early September) the community hosts tens of thousands of visitors — commonly estimated at around 22,000 — who attend workshops, lectures, church services, public demonstrations of mediumship at the Inspiration Stump and the Forest Temple, and private readings with the community's roughly fifty registered mediums, who must pass tests administered by the Assembly. Sites in the hamlet include the Lily Dale Museum (housed in a former schoolhouse), a Spiritualist library, the Maplewood Hotel (a centre of activity said by believers to be haunted), and the original farmhouse associated with the community's founders.

The hamlet's Victorian cottages, many painted in bright colours, and its quiet, car-limited streets preserve the atmosphere of a 19th-century camp meeting. Lily Dale has featured in documentaries and television programmes about mediumship, including the HBO documentary No One Dies in Lily Dale (2010).
