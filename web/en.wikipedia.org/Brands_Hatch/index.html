Brands Hatch is a motor racing circuit near West Kingsdown in Kent, England. Set in a natural amphitheatre, it is one of Britain's best-known circuits and hosted the Formula One British Grand Prix 12 times between 1964 and 1986, alternating with Silverstone.

Layouts and history

The venue was used for grass-track motorcycle racing from the 1920s before a paved circuit opened in 1950. The longer Grand Prix loop, added in 1960, threads through woodland beyond the short Indy circuit, which occupies the natural bowl and gives spectators views of nearly the whole track. Famous corners include Paddock Hill Bend, Druids hairpin, and Clearways.

Events

Brands Hatch hosts rounds of the British Touring Car Championship, British Superbike Championship, and GT racing, and it welcomed American series including Champ Car (2003) and NASCAR's European series. The circuit also served as a venue for the 2012 Paralympic road cycling events. It is operated by MotorSport Vision.
