Boston Celtics

Founded: 1946
Arena: TD Garden, Boston
League: NBA (Eastern Conference)
Championships: 18 (NBA record)
Colours: Green, white, gold, black

The Boston Celtics are an American professional basketball team based in Boston. The Celtics compete in the National Basketball Association (NBA) as a member of the Atlantic Division of the Eastern Conference. Founded in 1946, they are one of only two chartered BAA franchises still playing in their original city.

Dynasty years

Under coach Red Auerbach and centre Bill Russell, the Celtics won an unmatched eleven championships in thirteen seasons (1957–1969), including eight consecutive titles from 1959 to 1966 — a record in North American professional sports. Later eras led by Larry Bird won titles in 1981, 1984 and 1986.

Modern era

The "Big Three" of Paul Pierce, Kevin Garnett and Ray Allen won the 2008 championship. In 2024, led by Jayson Tatum and Finals MVP Jaylen Brown, the Celtics won their 18th NBA title, breaking their tie with the Los Angeles Lakers for the most championships in league history.
