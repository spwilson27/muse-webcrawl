David Frankfurter

David Frankfurter (9 July 1909 – 19 July 1982) was a Croatian Jewish medical student who assassinated Wilhelm Gustloff, the Swiss leader of the Nazi Party's foreign organisation branch (NSDAP/AO), in Davos, Switzerland, on 4 February 1936, in protest against Nazi persecution of Jews.

Aftermath

Frankfurter surrendered to police immediately and was sentenced by a Swiss court to 18 years in prison. The assassination was exploited by Nazi propaganda — the regime named the ship Wilhelm Gustloff after the victim — but also resonated internationally as an act of resistance. Frankfurter was pardoned and expelled from Switzerland in 1945, after which he emigrated to Mandatory Palestine, later Israel.
