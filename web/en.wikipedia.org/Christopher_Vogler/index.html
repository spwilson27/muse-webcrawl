Christopher Vogler

Christopher Vogler (born 1949) is an American screenwriter and story consultant best known for The Writer's Journey.

The Writer's Journey

As a story analyst at Disney, Vogler wrote a memo distilling Joseph Campbell's monomyth — the hero's journey — into a practical screenwriting framework. Expanded into the book The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers (1992), it describes twelve stages from the Ordinary World to the Return with the Elixir and character archetypes such as Mentor, Threshold Guardian and Shadow.

Influence

The framework influenced films including The Lion King and countless screenplays, and remains a standard, if sometimes debated, tool in Hollywood story development.
