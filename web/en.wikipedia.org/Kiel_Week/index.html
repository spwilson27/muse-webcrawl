Kiel Week

Kiel Week (German: Kieler Woche) is an annual sailing event and festival held in Kiel, Germany, in the last full week of June. It is the largest sailing event in the world, drawing millions of visitors, thousands of boats, and sailors from dozens of countries.

History and events

The first Kiel Week regatta was held on 23 June 1882, when twenty racing yachts took part; Kaiser Wilhelm II was an enthusiastic early patron. Races are held on the Kiel Firth and at the Olympic harbour of Schilksee, built for the 1972 Summer Olympics. Alongside the racing, the event includes a large public festival with concerts, food stalls, and the traditional windjammer parade, in which tall ships and historic sailing vessels sail up the fjord.

The closing fireworks display over the harbour traditionally ends the festival on the final Sunday.
