Panchamrita

Panchamrita is a mixture of five foods used in Hindu worship and puja, especially for abhisheka (ritual bathing of a deity). Its name derives from the Sanskrit words pancha (five) and amrita (nectar of immortality).

Composition

The five ingredients are typically milk, curd (yogurt), honey, sugar and ghee, though regional variations substitute ingredients such as banana or coconut water. After the ritual the mixture is distributed to devotees as prasada.
