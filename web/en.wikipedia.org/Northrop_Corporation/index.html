Northrop Corporation was a leading United States aircraft manufacturer from its formation in 1939 until its 1994 merger with Grumman to form Northrop Grumman. The company is known for its development of the flying wing concept — embodied in designs such as the YB-49 and, most famously, the B-2 Spirit stealth bomber — and for successful production aircraft including the F-5/T-38 family and the P-61 Black Widow night fighter.

History

Jack Northrop founded the Northrop Corporation in 1939 in Hawthorne, California, after earlier ventures including the Avion Corporation and a previous Northrop company that had become part of Douglas Aircraft. During the Second World War the company produced the P-61 Black Widow, the first American military aircraft designed specifically as a night fighter, as well as components for other manufacturers.

Northrop's long-running fascination with the flying wing produced the propeller-driven XB-35 and the jet-powered YB-49 in the late 1940s. Although neither entered production — a decision surrounded by controversy and allegations of political pressure — the concept was vindicated four decades later when Northrop won the contract for the B-2 Spirit stealth bomber, which drew directly on the earlier work, and Jack Northrop was reportedly shown the still-classified B-2 design shortly before his death in 1981.

Post-war aircraft

The company's most commercially successful designs were the T-38 Talon supersonic trainer, first flown in 1959 and still in US Air Force service, and its sibling the F-5 Freedom Fighter/Tiger II, a lightweight, low-cost fighter exported to dozens of allied nations. Northrop also built the F-89 Scorpion interceptor, the F/A-18 Hornet in partnership with McDonnell Douglas (as subcontractor for carrier-based production), and the Tacit Blue experimental stealth demonstrator, whose technology fed into the B-2 programme.

Merger

In 1994, Northrop acquired Grumman Corporation for $2.17 billion after a bidding contest with Martin Marietta, creating Northrop Grumman, which became one of the world's largest defence contractors.
