St. Louis, Iron Mountain and Southern Railway

St. Louis, Iron Mountain and Southern Railway
Headquarters: St. Louis, Missouri
Locale: Missouri, Arkansas, Louisiana, Texas
Successor: Missouri Pacific Railroad

The St. Louis, Iron Mountain and Southern Railway was an American railroad operating in Missouri, Arkansas, Louisiana and Texas. It originated in lines chartered in the 1850s to connect St. Louis with the iron ore region around Pilot Knob and Iron Mountain, Missouri.

History

The company was formed by consolidation in 1874 and came under the control of financier Jay Gould. It absorbed the Cairo and Fulton Railroad, extending its system south to Texarkana and into Texas and Louisiana.

Merger

In 1917 the railway was merged into the Missouri Pacific Railroad, ending its independent corporate existence while its lines remained core routes of the Missouri Pacific system.
