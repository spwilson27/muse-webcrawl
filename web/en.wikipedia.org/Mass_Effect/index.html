Mass Effect

Mass Effect is a military science fiction media franchise created by Casey Hudson and developed by BioWare, and published by Electronic Arts. The franchise centres on a series of third-person role-playing shooter video games in which the player character, Commander Shepard, leads efforts to save the Milky Way galaxy from the Reapers, a race of ancient synthetic-organic starships that periodically exterminate advanced organic life.

The original trilogy follows Commander Shepard's fight against the Reapers.

Setting

The series is set in the late 22nd century. Humanity has joined a galactic community centred on the Citadel after discovering Prothean technology on Mars and the mass relays, immense devices that enable faster-than-light travel via the "mass effect" — the manipulation of dark energy by element zero. Players explore the galaxy aboard the starship Normandy, recruiting a diverse squad of human and alien companions.

Gameplay

The games combine squad-based third-person shooting with role-playing elements: character classes, dialogue trees, and a morality system (Paragon and Renegade) that tracks the player's choices. Decisions made in one game carry over into sequels through save-game imports, shaping characters' fates and the state of the galaxy — a system widely praised, although the ending of Mass Effect 3 proved controversial and prompted BioWare to release an extended cut.

Games and other media

The original trilogy (2007–2012) was critically acclaimed and sold millions of copies; Mass Effect 2 in particular is frequently listed among the greatest video games of all time. A fourth game, Mass Effect: Andromeda (2017), shifted the setting to the Andromeda galaxy and received a mixed reception. Mass Effect Legendary Edition (2021) remastered the trilogy. The franchise also includes novels, comics, and an animated film.
