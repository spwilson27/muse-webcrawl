Expo 2015 was a Universal Exposition hosted by Milan, Italy, from May 1 to October 31, 2015. It was the second time Milan hosted a world's fair, after the Milan International of 1906. The exposition site, located in the municipalities of Rho and Pero northwest of the city, covered about 1.1 km². Milan defeated the Turkish city of İzmir in the 2008 Bureau International des Expositions vote, 86 to 65.

Theme and content

The theme, "Feeding the Planet, Energy for Life," embraced technology, innovation, culture, traditions, and creativity in relation to food and diet. A total of 145 countries participated, alongside international organizations and corporate pavilions, organized partly into thematic "clusters" devoted to commodities such as rice, coffee, cocoa, and spices. The Italian pavilion, Palazzo Italia, and the Tree of Life — a 37-metre illuminated wooden-and-steel structure that became the expo's symbol — anchored the central Cardo and Decumano avenues. The Milan Charter, a document on food rights and sustainability presented as the expo's legacy, was signed by participating countries and organizations.

Organization and controversies

Preparations were marked by delays, cost overruns, and corruption investigations that led to arrests of officials and business figures in 2014. Anti-expo protests culminated in clashes during the opening day demonstration in Milan. Attendance recovered strongly in the final months, and total visits exceeded 21 million, broadly meeting organizers' targets.

Legacy

After closure, the site was redeveloped as MIND — the Milano Innovation District — hosting the new Galeazzi hospital, the Human Technopole research institute, and university facilities of the University of Milan.
