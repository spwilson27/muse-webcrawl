C. J. Chenier

C. J. Chenier (born Clayton Joseph Thompson, September 28, 1957, in Port Arthur, Texas) is an American zydeco accordionist and singer, the son of Clifton Chenier, the "King of Zydeco".

Career

Raised in Louisiana Creole musical culture, Chenier played saxophone and keyboards before taking up the accordion. In 1987 he joined his father's Red Hot Louisiana Band; after Clifton's death that year he took over the band and carried its blend of zydeco, blues, R&B and rock to international audiences.

Recordings

His albums include Let Me in Your Pocket (1990), Too Much Fun (1995) and The Desperate Chase (1998). He received a Grammy nomination for his work with the Red Hot Louisiana Band and was inducted into the Louisiana Music Hall of Fame.
