Puerto Rico (Spanish for "rich port"), officially the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, is a Caribbean island and unincorporated territory of the United States. It is located in the northeast Caribbean Sea, approximately 1,000 miles (1,600 km) southeast of Miami, Florida, between the Dominican Republic and the U.S. Virgin Islands. The archipelago consists of the main island and smaller islands including Vieques, Culebra and Mona.

History

Puerto Rico was inhabited for centuries by the Indigenous Taíno people before Christopher Columbus landed in 1493. Spain colonised the island, founding San Juan in 1521 — one of the oldest European settlements in the Americas — and ruled for more than 400 years. Following the Spanish–American War, Spain ceded Puerto Rico to the United States under the Treaty of Paris of 1898.

The Jones–Shafroth Act of 1917 granted U.S. citizenship to Puerto Ricans. In 1952 a constitution established the island as a commonwealth in association with the United States. Puerto Rico's political status — statehood, independence, or continuation of the commonwealth — remains the central question of its politics, the subject of several non-binding referendums.

Geography and economy

The main island is mountainous, with a tropical marine climate and a central cordillera; it is vulnerable to hurricanes, most devastatingly Hurricane Maria in 2017. The economy, historically based on sugar, shifted to manufacturing — especially pharmaceuticals and medical devices — encouraged by federal tax incentives, alongside tourism and services. Puerto Rico underwent a prolonged debt crisis in the 2010s, addressed through the federal PROMESA law of 2016, which imposed a fiscal oversight board.

Demographics and culture

Spanish is the dominant language, with English also official. Puerto Rican culture blends Taíno, Spanish and African influences, expressed in music (salsa, reguetón), cuisine (mofongo, lechón) and festivals. A large diaspora lives on the U.S. mainland, where Puerto Ricans outnumber those on the island; New York City has historically been its centre.
