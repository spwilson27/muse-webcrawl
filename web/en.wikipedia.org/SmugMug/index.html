SmugMug

Founded2002
FoundersDon MacAskill, Chris MacAskill
HeadquartersMountain View, California, U.S.
IndustryPhoto sharing and hosting

SmugMug is a paid image sharing, image hosting and online video platform that allows users to upload photos and videos, display them in customisable galleries, and sell prints and digital downloads. The company was founded in 2002 by Don MacAskill and his father Chris MacAskill, and has remained independent and bootstrapped for most of its history.

History

SmugMug positioned itself as a service for serious amateurs and professional photographers, emphasising customisation, unlimited storage and e-commerce tools rather than advertising. In April 2018, SmugMug acquired Flickr from Verizon's Oath division, pledging to keep it as a separate service while investing in its community.

Features

Plans offer custom domains, themes, watermarking, print fulfilment through partner labs, and privacy controls. The service is widely used by event, wedding and sports photographers to deliver and sell client galleries.
