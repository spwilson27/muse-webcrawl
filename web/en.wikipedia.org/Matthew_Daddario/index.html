Matthew Daddario

Matthew Quincy Daddario (born October 1, 1987) is an American actor. He is best known for his role as Alec Lightwood on the Freeform television series Shadowhunters (2016–2019), for which he won several Teen Choice Awards.

Early life

Daddario was born and raised in New York City, the son of Christina, a lawyer, and Richard Daddario, a prosecutor and former head of the NYPD counterterrorism unit. He is the younger brother of actress Alexandra Daddario. He studied business at Indiana University Bloomington, graduating in 2010.

Career

He made his film debut in Breathe In (2013) and appeared in When the Game Stands Tall (2014) and the remake of Cabin Fever (2016). His breakthrough came with Shadowhunters, based on Cassandra Clare's The Mortal Instruments novels. He later starred in the second season of Why Women Kill (2021).
