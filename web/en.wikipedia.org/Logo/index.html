Logo

A logo (abbreviation of logotype, from the Greek logos, "word", and typos, "imprint") is a graphic mark, emblem or symbol used to aid and promote public identification and recognition of a company, organisation, product or brand. It may be purely graphic (a symbol or icon), or composed of the name of the organisation in a distinctive lettering style (a logotype or wordmark).

History

Pictorial marks have ancient precedents in seals, coins, heraldic crests and makers' marks. The modern logo emerged with mass printing and advertising in the late 19th century; the Bass red triangle, registered in 1876, was the first trademark registered in the United Kingdom. Twentieth-century modernism favoured simple geometric marks, and designers such as Paul Rand established corporate identity as a discipline.

Design

Effective logos are distinctive, memorable, legible at small sizes and reproducible in one colour. They form the centrepiece of a wider brand identity system covering colour, typography and layout. Logos are commonly protected as trademarks, and periodic redesigns ("rebrands") balance recognition against modernisation.
