Belgium

Belgium, officially the Kingdom of Belgium, is a country in Northwestern Europe bordered by the Netherlands, Germany, Luxembourg, France and the North Sea. Its capital and largest city is Brussels.

Geography and people

Belgium has a population of about 11.7 million in a densely populated area. It has three official languages: Dutch, spoken by the Flemish in the north; French, spoken by Walloons in the south; and a small German-speaking community in the east.

Politics

Belgium is a federal constitutional monarchy. Brussels hosts the headquarters of the European Union and NATO. The country became independent from the Netherlands in 1830.

Economy and culture

A highly developed economy, Belgium is known for chocolate, beer, waffles and comics, and for medieval towns such as Bruges and Ghent. See Brussels.
