Bashar al-Assad and the Baʼath Party

Bashar al-Assad served as president of Syria from 2000 until his government's fall in December 2024, succeeding his father Hafez al-Assad. He was also the regional secretary of the Syrian branch of the Arab Socialist Baʼath Party, which ruled Syria from 1963.

Rule

Initially greeted by hopes of reform during the brief "Damascus Spring", Assad's rule hardened into authoritarianism. The Syrian civil war, beginning in 2011 after the violent suppression of protests, devastated the country. In December 2024, a rebel offensive captured Damascus and Assad fled to Russia, ending more than five decades of Assad family rule under the Baʼath Party.
