Baʼath Party and the Iran–Iraq War

During the Iran–Iraq War (1980–1988), Baʼathist Syria under Hafez al-Assad sided with Iran against Baʼathist Iraq, an unusual alignment that deepened the Syrian–Iraqi split within the Baʼath movement.

Syrian policy

Syria closed Iraq's oil pipeline across its territory in 1982, provided Iran with intelligence and some matériel, and received discounted Iranian oil in return. Syria's stance reflected its rivalry with Saddam Hussein's Iraq, competition for leadership of the Arab world, and Assad's opposition to Iraq's challenge to Syrian interests in Lebanon. The war ended in 1988 with a return to the status quo ante bellum.
