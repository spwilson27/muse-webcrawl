Baʼath Party and the Gulf War

Syria under the Baʼath Party and Hafez al-Assad joined the US-led coalition against Iraq in the Gulf War of 1990–91, despite Iraq also being ruled by a rival branch of the Baʼath Party.

Background

The Syrian and Iraqi branches of the Baʼath Party had been bitter rivals since the 1966 split in the party, and Syria had supported Iran during the Iran–Iraq War. When Iraq invaded Kuwait in August 1990, Syria condemned the invasion and contributed roughly 14,500 troops to the coalition in Saudi Arabia. Participation improved Syria's relations with the United States and the Gulf states and helped consolidate Syrian predominance in Lebanon.
