Luciano Moggi

Luciano Moggi (born 10 July 1937) is a former Italian football administrator, best known as general director of Juventus from 1994 to 2006, a period in which the club won numerous Serie A titles, the UEFA Champions League (1996) and other trophies, signing players such as Zinedine Zidane and Pavel Nedvěd.

Calciopoli

In 2006 Moggi was the central figure of the Calciopoli scandal, after intercepted telephone calls suggested he had exercised influence over referee appointments. Juventus were relegated to Serie B and stripped of two league titles, and Moggi was banned from football for life by the Italian federation; related criminal proceedings ended with the charges extinguished by the statute of limitations.
