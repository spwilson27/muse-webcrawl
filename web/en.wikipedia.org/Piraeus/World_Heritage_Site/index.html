World Heritage Site

A World Heritage Site is a landmark or area selected by UNESCO (the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization) as having cultural, historical, scientific or other significance of outstanding universal value, and legally protected by the World Heritage Convention of 1972.

Selection

Sites are nominated by states parties and evaluated against ten criteria by advisory bodies (ICOMOS for cultural sites, IUCN for natural sites) before inscription by the World Heritage Committee. There are more than 1,200 listed sites across over 160 countries, classed as cultural, natural or mixed.

Protection

Listing brings international recognition and access to the World Heritage Fund, but also obligations to preserve the site; properties under serious threat are placed on the List of World Heritage in Danger, and three sites have been delisted entirely.
