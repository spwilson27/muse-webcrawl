Starbucks Corporation is an American multinational chain of coffeehouses and roastery reserves headquartered in Seattle, Washington. It is the world's largest coffeehouse chain.

History

Starbucks was founded in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl and Gordon Bowker at Seattle's Pike Place Market, initially selling roasted coffee beans. Under Howard Schultz, who joined in 1982, it pivoted to espresso-based drinks inspired by Italian coffee bars and expanded rapidly across the United States and internationally from the 1990s.

Products and culture

The company sells coffee drinks, teas, and food under its own name, and is known for writing customers' names on cups, its customised drinks menu, and its siren logo. It faces ongoing debates over trade practices, labour relations and market saturation.
