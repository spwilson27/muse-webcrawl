PUBG: Battlegrounds (originally PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds) is a battle royale game developed by PUBG Studios and published by Krafton. Designed by Brendan Greene — the "PlayerUnknown" of the title, who had created battle royale mods for ARMA games — it was released into Steam early access in March 2017.

Gameplay

Up to one hundred players parachute onto an island map such as Erangel or Miramar, scavenge for weapons and equipment, and fight as an encroaching electric field shrinks the safe area. The last player or team alive wins the match, greeted with the phrase "Winner Winner Chicken Dinner!" Matches support solo, duo, and four-player squad play in first- or third-person perspective.

Impact

PUBG sold tens of millions of copies on PC and consoles and popularised the battle royale genre, paving the way for Fortnite Battle Royale and Apex Legends. Its mobile version, PUBG Mobile, became one of the highest-grossing mobile games in the world. The PC and console versions became free-to-play in January 2022.
