Thriller is the sixth studio album by the American singer and songwriter Michael Jackson, released on November 29, 1982, by Epic Records. Produced by Quincy Jones, it became the best-selling album of all time, with estimated sales of over 70 million copies worldwide.

Background and recording

Following the success of Off the Wall (1979), Jackson reunited with Jones to record an album in which, as Jackson put it, every song was a "killer". Recording took place in Los Angeles in 1982 with a budget of $750,000. The album blends pop, rock, funk, R&B, and disco.

Singles and legacy

Seven of its nine tracks were released as singles, including "Billie Jean", "Beat It", and the title track "Thriller", whose 14-minute music video directed by John Landis transformed the medium. The album won a record eight Grammy Awards in 1984 and helped break racial barriers on MTV and in pop radio. It was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame and preserved in the US National Recording Registry.
