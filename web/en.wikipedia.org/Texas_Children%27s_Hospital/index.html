Texas Children's Hospital

Texas Children's Hospital is a nationally ranked, freestanding children's hospital in Houston, Texas, located in the Texas Medical Center. Affiliated with Baylor College of Medicine, it is one of the largest pediatric hospitals in the United States and is regularly ranked among the top children's hospitals by U.S. News & World Report.

History

The hospital opened in 1954 with 106 beds, championed by Houston philanthropists including Leopold Meyer. It has expanded repeatedly, adding the Pavilion for Women in 2012 and the Legacy Tower for critical care in 2018, and operates additional campuses in the Houston area including West Campus and The Woodlands.

Care and research

Texas Children's is known for its heart center, cancer center, and fetal surgery program, and hosts one of the largest pediatric residency and fellowship programs in the country. Its global health network, Texas Children's Global Health, works extensively in sub-Saharan Africa on pediatric HIV and other conditions.
