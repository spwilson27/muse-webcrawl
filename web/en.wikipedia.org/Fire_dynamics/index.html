Fire dynamics

Fire dynamics is the study of how chemistry, fire science, material science and mechanics influence how a fire starts, spreads and develops. It underpins fire protection engineering, fire investigation and firefighter training.

Concepts

Key ideas include the fire triangle (fuel, oxygen, heat) and fire tetrahedron, heat release rate, fire growth through the stages of ignition, growth, flashover, fully developed fire and decay, and the difference between fuel-controlled and ventilation-controlled burning. Compartment fire behaviour, smoke movement and the effects of suppression are modelled analytically and with computational tools such as zone models and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) field models like the Fire Dynamics Simulator (FDS).
