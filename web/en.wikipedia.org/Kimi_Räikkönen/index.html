Kimi Räikkönen

Kimi-Matias Räikkönen (born 17 October 1979), nicknamed "the Iceman", is a Finnish racing driver who competed in Formula One from 2001 to 2021. He won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 2007 with Ferrari, and remains Ferrari's most recent drivers' champion.

Career

Räikkönen entered Formula One with Sauber in 2001 despite minimal single-seater experience, then moved to McLaren in 2002, finishing championship runner-up in 2003 and 2005. Joining Ferrari in 2007, he won the title by a single point in his first season. After leaving F1 at the end of 2009, he competed in the World Rally Championship and NASCAR before returning with Lotus in 2012. A second Ferrari stint (2014–2018) brought a final victory at the 2018 United States Grand Prix — 113 races after his previous win, a record gap. He finished his career with Alfa Romeo in 2021.

Records and style

Across 349 starts Räikkönen achieved 21 wins, 103 podiums, and 46 fastest laps. Known for laconic radio messages — including the famous "leave me alone, I know what I'm doing" at the 2012 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, which he won — he was immensely popular with fans for his blunt, unpretentious manner and formidable natural speed, especially in wet and changing conditions.

Personal life

Räikkönen was born in Espoo, Finland. He is married to Minttu Räikkönen and has three children. His older brother Rami was also a racing driver. Since retiring from Formula One he has served as team principal of the Kawasaki Racing Team in the MXGP motocross world championship and has made occasional rallying appearances.
