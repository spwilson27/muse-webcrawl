Denver is the capital and most populous city of the U.S. state of Colorado. Located in the South Platte River Valley on the western edge of the High Plains, just east of the Front Range of the Rocky Mountains, Denver is nicknamed the "Mile High City" because its official elevation is exactly one mile (5,280 feet) above sea level.

History

Denver was founded in November 1858 as a mining town during the Pike's Peak Gold Rush. It was named after Kansas Territorial Governor James W. Denver. The city grew rapidly with the arrival of the railroad in 1870 and became the state capital. Silver booms and busts shaped its early economy; in the 20th century it became a regional centre for energy, defence, and telecommunications.

Geography and climate

Denver has a semi-arid, continental climate with four distinct seasons and roughly 300 days of sunshine per year. Its location at the foot of the Rockies makes it a gateway to mountain recreation, including skiing in resorts such as Aspen and Vail.

Culture and sports

The city is home to the Denver Broncos (NFL), Denver Nuggets (NBA), Colorado Rockies (MLB), Colorado Avalanche (NHL), and Colorado Rapids (MLS). Landmarks include Red Rocks Amphitheatre, the Denver Art Museum, Union Station, and the Colorado State Capitol, whose 13th step marks one mile above sea level. Denver International Airport, opened in 1995, is one of the largest airports in the world by land area.
