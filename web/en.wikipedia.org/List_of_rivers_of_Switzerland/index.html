List of rivers of Switzerland

Switzerland is the source of several major European rivers. Its rivers drain to the North Sea via the Rhine, to the Mediterranean via the Rhône, to the Adriatic via the Po system, and to the Black Sea via the Inn and Danube.

  • Rhine — 375 km within Switzerland in total course
  • Aare — longest river entirely in Switzerland, about 295 km
  • Rhône — about 264 km in Switzerland
  • Reuss
  • Limmat
  • Inn (En)
  • Ticino
  • Thur
  • Saane / Sarine
  • Doubs
  • Linths and Landquart

The full list is organised by drainage basin.
