Morris Day (born 13 December 1957) is an American singer, songwriter, and actor best known as the lead singer of the funk band the Time (later known as Morris Day and the Time), a group closely associated with Prince and the Minneapolis sound.

Career

Day grew up in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and attended high school with Prince. In 1981 Prince assembled the Time as an opening act and outlet for his funk compositions, with Day as its flamboyant frontman. The band released albums including The Time (1981), What Time Is It? (1982), and Ice Cream Castle (1984), which featured the hits "Jungle Love" and "The Bird".

Day played a fictionalized version of himself as Prince's rival in the 1984 film Purple Rain and its 1990 sequel Graffiti Bridge. As a solo artist he released albums such as Color of Success (1985) and Daydreaming (1987), the latter including the R&B hit "Fishnet". He has continued to tour with the Time for decades.

Style

Day's stage persona — vain, sharp-dressed, and comically self-absorbed, often checking himself in a mirror held by his valet Jerome Benton — became one of the signature images of 1980s funk.
