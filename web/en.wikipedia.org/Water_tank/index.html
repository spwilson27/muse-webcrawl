Water tank

A water tank is a container for storing water for purposes such as drinking water supply, irrigation, fire suppression, and industrial processes. Tanks range from small household cisterns to reservoirs holding millions of litres.

Types

Water tanks may be ground-level, underground, or elevated (such as water towers) to provide pressure by gravity. Construction materials include plastic (polyethylene), fibreglass, concrete, steel, and traditional stone or earthenware.

History

Storage of water is ancient: Indus Valley civilisation houses had water tanks around 3000 BC, and castles and hilltop settlements relied on cisterns. Modern municipal systems use tanks to balance supply and demand and maintain pressure.
