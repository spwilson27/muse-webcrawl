Aston Martin F1

Aston Martin is a British car manufacturer that has participated in Formula One in various forms. The current Formula One team, the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team, is based in Silverstone, England, and is owned by Lawrence Stroll, having been rebranded from Racing Point for the 2021 season.

The Aston Martin AMR23 in the team's green livery.

History

Aston Martin first entered Formula One in 1959 with the DBR4, with drivers including Roy Salvadori and Carroll Shelby, but scored no championship points and withdrew after 1960. The name returned in 2021 when the Racing Point team — itself descended from Jordan Grand Prix through Midland, Spyker, and Force India — was rebranded following Stroll's investment in Aston Martin Lagonda.

Modern era

Sebastian Vettel joined for 2021–2022. Fernando Alonso replaced him in 2023 and scored a string of early podiums, lifting the team to fifth in the constructors' championship. The team opened a new factory and wind tunnel campus at Silverstone, signed Adrian Newey as Managing Technical Partner, and agreed a works Honda power-unit partnership for the 2026 regulations. Its other seat is held by Lance Stroll, the owner's son.
