Meat-shaped Stone

The Meat-shaped Stone (Chinese: 肉形石) is a piece of jasper carved into the shape of a piece of Dongpo pork, a popular Chinese braised pork dish. It is held in the National Palace Museum in Taipei, Taiwan, and is one of the museum's most famous treasures.

Description

Carved during the Qing dynasty, the stone's natural banded layers were shaped, textured and stained so that its surface resembles layers of fat, lean meat and glistening pork skin. Measuring about 5.7 cm high, it is often displayed alongside the museum's Jadeite Cabbage, the pair forming a playful celebration of humble food rendered in precious stone.
