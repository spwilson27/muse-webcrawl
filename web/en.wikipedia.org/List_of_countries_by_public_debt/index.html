List of countries by public debt

Public debt — general government gross debt as a percentage of GDP — is a key fiscal indicator compiled by the IMF. Figures vary by year and definition (gross vs net).

Highest debt ratios

  • Japan — the highest among major economies, around 250% of GDP gross
  • Greece, Italy, Lebanon, Sudan, Venezuela, Singapore, United States, France, Belgium, Spain, Canada — ratios commonly above 100% of GDP in recent IMF data (Singapore's gross figure reflects CPF borrowing, offset by assets)
  • United States — federal/general government gross debt around 120% of GDP

Lowest debt ratios

Countries such as Brunei, Liechtenstein, Kuwait, Turkmenistan, and several microstates and resource-rich states have very low public debt, in some cases under 5% of GDP, and some hold net asset positions through sovereign wealth funds (notably Norway).
