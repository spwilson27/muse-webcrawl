FIFA Puskás Award

The FIFA Puskás Award is an award established by FIFA on October 20, 2009, to be given to the player, male or female, judged to have scored the most aesthetically significant, or "most beautiful," goal of the calendar year. It is named in honour of former Hungarian striker Ferenc Puskás, captain of the Hungarian national team of the 1950s and star of Real Madrid.

Criteria and selection

Criteria include that the goal should be aesthetically beautiful, scored without the result of luck or a mistake by the opposing team, and in support of fair play; originally the goal had to be scored in top-level football. FIFA compiles a shortlist, and winners were initially chosen by a fan vote on FIFA's website; later editions combined fan voting with a panel of experts, and the award is presented at The Best FIFA Football Awards ceremony.

Cristiano Ronaldo was the first recipient, for his long-range goal against Porto in the 2008–09 UEFA Champions League. In 2022, Marcin Oleksy became the first amputee footballer to win, for a spectacular scissor-kick in Polish amputee football. From 2024, FIFA introduced a separate award, the FIFA Marta Award, for the best goal in women's football, named for Brazilian forward Marta.
