Navan Fort (Irish: Emain Macha) is an ancient ceremonial monument near Armagh in Northern Ireland. In Irish mythology it was the royal seat of the kings of Ulster and the home of the Red Branch Knights of the Ulster Cycle.

Archaeology

The site is a circular hilltop enclosure. Excavations revealed a remarkable sequence culminating in a huge timber structure built around 95 BC, which was filled with stones, burned, and covered with turf—interpreted as a ritual act rather than destruction in war.

Interpretation

Despite its name, Navan Fort appears to have been a ceremonial and ritual centre rather than a defensive fortification. A visitor centre interprets the site's archaeology and mythology.
