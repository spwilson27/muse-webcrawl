Chinon AOC

Chinon AOC
Region: Loire Valley, France
Main grape: Cabernet Franc
Styles: Red, rosé, white (Chenin Blanc)
AOC established: 1937

Chinon is an Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée (AOC) for wine in the Loire Valley around the town of Chinon, on the Vienne river. It is best known for red wines made almost entirely from Cabernet Franc.

Wines

Chinon reds range from light, fruity wines from sandy and gravelly soils near the river—suited to early drinking—to fuller, structured wines from limestone and clay slopes that can age for decades. Small amounts of rosé and white wine (from Chenin Blanc) are also produced.

Geography

Vineyards span communes on both banks of the Vienne near its confluence with the Loire. The tuffeau limestone cellars of the region, many carved into cliffs, provide natural storage.
