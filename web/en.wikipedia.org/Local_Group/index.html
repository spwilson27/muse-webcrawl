Local Group

The Local Group is the galaxy group that includes the Milky Way, the Andromeda Galaxy and more than 80 other galaxies, most of them dwarfs. It spans about 10 million light-years and forms part of the Virgo Supercluster.

Members

The two dominant members are the spiral galaxies Milky Way and Andromeda (M31), each with systems of satellite dwarf galaxies including the Magellanic Clouds and M33, the Triangulum Galaxy, is the third-largest member.

Dynamics

Andromeda and the Milky Way are approaching each other and are expected to collide and merge in roughly 4–5 billion years. The group's barycentre lies between the two giants.

Neighbourhood

The Local Group sits on the edge of the Local Sheet and is part of the larger Laniakea Supercluster.
