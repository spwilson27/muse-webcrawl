Responsory

A responsory is a type of chant in Western plainchant (Gregorian chant) sung responsorially: a soloist (or small group) sings verses, and the choir or congregation responds with a repeated refrain (the respond). Responsories are a major component of the Divine Office and, in one form, of the Mass.

Forms

In the Office, great responsories (responsoria prolixa) follow the lessons at Matins and are long, florid chants with elaborate melismas; the short responsory (responsorium breve) is a brief, syllabic chant used at the other hours. At Mass, the Gradual and Alleluia evolved from responsorial chants sung after the readings.

Structure

A great responsory consists of the respond, a verse (often from a psalm), and usually a repetition of part of the respond; at major feasts the verse may be followed by the doxology. Famous examples are associated with Advent and Holy Week, including the Tenebrae responsories.
