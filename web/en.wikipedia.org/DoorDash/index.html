DoorDash


DoorDash operates an online food ordering and delivery platform.

DoorDash, Inc. is an American company that operates an online platform for food delivery and convenience goods. Founded in 2013 in Palo Alto, California, by Tony Xu, Stanley Tang, Andy Fang, and Evan Moore, it is headquartered in San Francisco. DoorDash became the largest food delivery company in the United States, holding the majority share of the market ahead of competitors such as Uber Eats and Grubhub.

History

The company began as PaloAltoDelivery.com before passing through the Y Combinator accelerator in 2013 and rebranding as DoorDash. It expanded rapidly through venture funding, acquiring Caviar in 2019 and the Finnish delivery company Wolt in 2022 for about $8.1 billion. DoorDash went public on the New York Stock Exchange in December 2020 under the ticker DASH, one of the largest IPOs of that year.

Operations

Customers order through the DoorDash app or website; independent contractors known as "Dashers" pick up and deliver the orders. The company earns revenue from commissions charged to merchants, delivery and service fees, and its DashPass subscription program. Like other gig-economy platforms, DoorDash has faced scrutiny over worker classification, tipping practices, and restaurant commissions.
