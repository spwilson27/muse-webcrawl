National Transitional Council

The National Transitional Council (NTC) was the de facto government of Libya during the Libyan Civil War of 2011, formed by anti-Gaddafi forces to act as the political face of the revolution against Muammar Gaddafi.

Formation

The council was announced in Benghazi on 27 February 2011, shortly after the uprising began, and formally declared itself Libya's sole legitimate governing body on 5 March 2011. It was chaired by Mustafa Abdul Jalil, a former justice minister.

International recognition

France became the first country to recognise the NTC in March 2011; many other states followed, and in September 2011 the United Nations recognised the council as Libya's representative.

Transition

After the fall of Tripoli and Gaddafi's death in October 2011, the NTC declared Libya liberated and governed during the transition. In August 2012 it handed power to the newly elected General National Congress, the country's first peaceful transfer of power in decades.
