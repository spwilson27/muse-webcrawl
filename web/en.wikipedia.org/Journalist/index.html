Journalist

A journalist is an individual who collects, writes, or distributes news and other information to the public. A journalist's work is called journalism. Journalists work in a variety of media, including newspapers, magazines, radio, television, and online outlets, and may specialise in areas such as politics, business, sport, science, or investigative reporting.

Roles and duties

Journalists research stories, interview sources, verify facts, and prepare reports for publication or broadcast. Their roles range from beat reporters who cover a specific topic or institution, to foreign correspondents, investigative reporters who pursue in-depth inquiries over months, columnists and commentators who offer opinion, and editors who supervise and refine the work of others. Photojournalists and video journalists document events visually.

Ethics and standards

Professional codes of journalism commonly emphasise truthfulness, accuracy, objectivity, impartiality, fairness, and public accountability. Journalists are expected to verify information before publication, to distinguish news from opinion and advertising, to protect confidential sources, and to correct errors. Press freedom — the freedom of journalists to report without censorship or restraint by the state — is protected in many democracies and is enshrined in instruments such as the First Amendment to the United States Constitution and Article 19 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

History

The profession emerged with the growth of newspapers in the 17th and 18th centuries and was transformed by the telegraph, the penny press, radio, television, and finally the internet, which enabled citizen journalism and social-media reporting while disrupting traditional business models. By the early 21st century many journalists worked as freelancers or for digital-native outlets.

Working conditions and safety

Journalists may face irregular hours, deadline pressure, and, in conflict zones or under repressive governments, threats of imprisonment, violence, or death. Organisations such as the Committee to Protect Journalists and Reporters Without Borders monitor press freedom and document attacks on journalists worldwide.
