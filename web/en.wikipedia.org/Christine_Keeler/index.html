Christine Keeler

Christine Margaret Keeler (1942–2017) was an English model and showgirl at the centre of the Profumo affair.

The affair

In 1963 it emerged that Keeler had had relationships with both John Profumo, Secretary of State for War, and Yevgeny Ivanov, a Soviet naval attaché — a Cold War security scandal that forced Profumo's resignation and damaged Harold Macmillan's government. Her friend Stephen Ward, who had introduced her to both men, was prosecuted and died by suicide.

Later life

Keeler served a short prison sentence for perjury in an unrelated case and published several memoirs maintaining her account of events. The affair has been dramatised in film and television, including The Trial of Christine Keeler.
