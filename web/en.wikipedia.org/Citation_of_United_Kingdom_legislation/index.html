Citation of United Kingdom legislation

The citation of United Kingdom legislation refers to the systems used to identify Acts of Parliament and other legislation of the United Kingdom and its constituent jurisdictions. An Act may be cited by its short title, by its regnal year and chapter number (for older Acts), or by calendar year and chapter number (for Acts since 1963).

Primary legislation

Each Act receives a chapter number according to the order in which it receives royal assent within a session or year. Public General Acts are numbered in one series; Local Acts use lower-case Roman numerals and Provisional Order Confirmation Acts a separate series. Since the Acts of Parliament Numbering and Citation Act 1962, Acts are cited by calendar year — for example, "2020 c. 1". Earlier Acts are cited by the regnal year(s) of the monarch and chapter, such as "24 & 25 Vict. c. 95". Acts of the devolved legislatures have their own series, e.g. Acts of the Scottish Parliament ("asp") and Acts of Senedd Cymru ("asc").

Secondary legislation

Statutory instruments are numbered in an annual series and cited as "SI 2020/123". Other forms, such as statutory rules in Northern Ireland, have their own numbering schemes.

Short titles

From the mid-nineteenth century, Acts were given short titles for convenient reference, and the Short Titles Act 1896 retrospectively assigned short titles to many earlier Acts.
