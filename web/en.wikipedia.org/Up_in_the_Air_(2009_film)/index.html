Up in the Air (2009 film)

Up in the Air is a 2009 American comedy-drama film directed by Jason Reitman and starring George Clooney, Vera Farmiga and Anna Kendrick. It adapts Walter Kirn's 2001 novel about a corporate downsizer who travels constantly.

Plot

Ryan Bingham lives out of a suitcase firing people for a living and pursuing ten million frequent-flyer miles, until a young colleague's video-conferencing plan and two relationships challenge his detached life.

Reception

The film was acclaimed for its screenplay and performances and was nominated for six Academy Awards, including Best Picture.

Themes

It explores job loss during the Great Recession, loneliness and human connection.
