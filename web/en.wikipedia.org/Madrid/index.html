Madrid

Madrid is the capital and most populous city of Spain, with about 3.3 million inhabitants in the municipality and roughly 6.8 million in the metropolitan area. It is the second-largest city in the European Union by population within city limits after Berlin. Located on the Manzanares River on the central Meseta plateau at about 650 metres elevation, Madrid has been Spain's capital since 1561 and is the country's political, financial, and cultural centre.

History

The site was a Muslim fortress, Mayrit, founded in the 9th century by Emir Muhammad I of Córdoba to guard the approaches to Toledo, on a site with older settlement. It passed to Castile in 1083. Philip II moved the royal court to Madrid in 1561, and apart from a brief period (1601–1606) in Valladolid it remained the seat of the Spanish monarchy. Under the Habsburgs and Bourbons the city grew around the Royal Palace, the Plaza Mayor, and later the grand axis of the Paseo del Prado. Madrid was a central theatre of the Peninsular War (the Dos de Mayo uprising of 1808) and, during the Spanish Civil War (1936–39), endured a long siege as the Republican capital before falling to Franco's forces.

Culture

Madrid's "Golden Triangle of Art" groups three world-class museums along the Paseo del Prado: the Prado (Velázquez, Goya, El Greco), the Reina Sofía (home of Picasso's Guernica), and the Thyssen-Bornemisza. The city is famed for its late-night culture, tapas bars, and neighbourhoods such as Malasaña and Chueca; the movida madrileña countercultural movement flourished after Franco's death. Retiro Park, the Royal Palace, and the Sunday El Rastro flea market are major landmarks.

Economy and transport

Madrid is Spain's principal financial and corporate centre, headquarters of companies such as Telefónica, Iberia, and Banco Santander (nearby), and a major European hub for Latin American business. Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport is among Europe's busiest, and the Madrid Metro, opened in 1919, is one of the world's largest subway systems. The city hosts two famous football clubs, Real Madrid and Atlético Madrid.
