The International Forum of Sovereign Wealth Funds (IFSWF) is a voluntary organisation of sovereign wealth funds (SWFs). It is a non-profit organisation established in 2009 with a permanent secretariat in London. IFSWF represents sovereign wealth funds from across a range of countries; its members collectively manage many trillions of US dollars in assets.

The Santiago Principles

The Forum grew out of the International Working Group of Sovereign Wealth Funds, convened with the International Monetary Fund in 2008 amid concern in recipient countries about the transparency and motives of large state-owned investors. The working group produced the Generally Accepted Principles and Practices for sovereign wealth funds, known as the Santiago Principles—24 voluntary principles covering legal framework, governance, transparency, investment policy and risk management. IFSWF members commit to applying the principles and undertake periodic self-assessments and peer reviews of their implementation.

Activities

IFSWF holds an annual meeting hosted by a member fund, publishes research on long-term investment and governance, and runs knowledge-sharing programmes. It works to strengthen understanding of sovereign wealth funds among governments, markets and the public, and to demonstrate that SWFs invest on an economic and financial basis.
