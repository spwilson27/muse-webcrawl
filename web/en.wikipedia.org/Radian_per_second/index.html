Radian per second

One radian per second corresponds to a frequency of 1/(2π) hertz.

The radian per second (symbol: rad·s−1 or rad/s) is the unit of angular velocity (angular frequency) in the International System of Units (SI). One radian per second is defined as the change in the orientation of an object by one radian every second. Since the radian is a dimensionless unit, the radian per second is dimensionally equivalent to the hertz, though the two should not be confused: hertz expresses cycles per second, so angular frequency ω = 2πf, where f is the ordinary frequency in hertz.

Conversions

One revolution per second equals 2π rad/s (≈ 6.283 rad/s). One radian per second equals 1/(2π) Hz ≈ 0.159 Hz, or about 9.549 revolutions per minute. The unit radian per second squared (rad/s²) is the corresponding unit of angular acceleration.
