Spearman's rank correlation coefficient

FieldStatistics
InventorCharles Spearman
Symbolρ (rho)
Range−1 to +1

Spearman's rank correlation coefficient, often denoted by the Greek letter ρ (rho), is a nonparametric measure of rank correlation: the statistical dependence between the rankings of two variables. It assesses how well the relationship between two variables can be described using a monotonic function.

Definition

The coefficient is computed as the Pearson correlation coefficient between the rank variables: each observation is replaced by its rank within its variable, and the usual correlation formula is applied. A value of +1 indicates a perfect monotone increasing relationship, −1 a perfect monotone decreasing one, and 0 no monotone association.

Uses

Because it uses ranks, Spearman's coefficient is robust to outliers and does not assume a linear relationship or normally distributed data. It is widely used in the social sciences and for ordinal data.
