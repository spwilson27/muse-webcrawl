List of things named after Barack Obama

Numerous places, schools, species, and other things have been named after Barack Obama, the 44th president of the United States (2009–2017). Naming followed his rapid rise to prominence after 2004 and continued through and after his presidency; many schools in the United States bear his name, as do streets and institutions abroad, particularly in Kenya, where his father was born.

Schools

Dozens of American schools have been named or renamed for Obama, including the Barack Obama Elementary School in Hempstead, New York (renamed in 2009, among the first), the Barack and Michelle Obama Elementary School in St. Paul, Minnesota, the Barack Obama Academy of International Studies in Pittsburgh, and Barack Obama High School in Milwaukee. In Kenya, the Senator Obama Kogelo Primary School and Mama Sarah Obama Secondary School in his father's home village of Kogelo bear family names.

Species named after Obama

Scientists have named a notable number of organisms for Obama, including:

  • Caloplaca obamae — a lichen, the first species named for him (2009).
  • Etheostoma obama — the spangled darter, a fish from Tennessee.
  • Obamadon gracilis — an extinct lizard from the late Cretaceous.
  • Baracktrema obamai — a parasitic flatworm of turtles.
  • Tosanoides obama — a coral-reef basslet from Hawaiʻi.
  • Syllipsimopodi bideni was named for his successor; Obama's tally of eponymous species (over a dozen) is unusually high for a president.

Other

Kenya declared a public holiday on 5 November 2008 following his first election victory. Aircraft, cocktails, and numerous newborn children (in Kenya and elsewhere) have also carried his name; the informal name "Obamacare" for the Affordable Care Act became so widespread that Obama himself adopted it.
