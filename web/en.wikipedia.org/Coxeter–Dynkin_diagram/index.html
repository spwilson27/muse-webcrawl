Coxeter–Dynkin diagram

A Coxeter–Dynkin diagram is a graph with numerically labelled edges representing a Coxeter group, giving a concise description of the reflections and symmetry of polytopes and Lie algebras.

History

Named for H. S. M. Coxeter and Eugene Dynkin. Dynkin diagrams classify semisimple Lie algebras and root systems into families An, Bn, Cn, Dn and exceptional types E6, E7, E8, F4, G2.
