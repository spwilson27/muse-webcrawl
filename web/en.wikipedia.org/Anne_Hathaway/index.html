Anne Hathaway

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Anne Jacqueline Hathaway (born November 12, 1982) is an American actress. One of the world's highest-paid actresses, she has received numerous accolades, including an Academy Award, a Golden Globe Award and a Primetime Emmy Award, and her films have grossed over $6.8 billion worldwide.

Career

Hathaway made her breakthrough as Mia Thermopolis in the Disney comedy The Princess Diaries (2001). She transitioned to adult roles with Brokeback Mountain (2005) and the comedy-drama The Devil Wears Prada (2006). She earned an Oscar nomination for Rachel Getting Married (2008), played Catwoman in The Dark Knight Rises (2012), and won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for her portrayal of Fantine in the musical Les Misérables (2012). Later roles include the science fiction film Interstellar (2014), Ocean's 8 (2018) and the series WeCrashed (2022).

Public image and activism

Hathaway supports numerous charities and serves as a UN Women goodwill ambassador, advocating for gender equality and paid parental leave. She is married to businessman Adam Shulman, with whom she has two sons.
