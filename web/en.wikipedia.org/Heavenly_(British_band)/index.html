Heavenly are an English twee pop band formed in Oxford in 1989. They were closely associated with the legendary indie label Sarah Records and with the international indiepop scene, and they are noted for pairing bright, jangling melodies with lyrics that sometimes addressed serious subjects such as sexual politics and bereavement.

History

Singer-guitarist Amelia Fletcher and guitarist Peter Momtchiloff had previously played in Talulah Gosh, one of the original "shambling" C86 bands. With Rob Pursey (bass), Cathy Rogers (keyboards and vocals) and Amelia's brother Mathew Fletcher (drums), Heavenly released four studio albums: Heavenly vs. Satan (1991), Le Jardin de Heavenly (1992), The Decline and Fall of Heavenly (1994) and Operation Heavenly (1996), along with a run of singles including "P.U.N.K. Girl" and "Atta Girl". Their sound grew harder on later records, influenced by riot grrrl, and they toured the United States on Calvin Johnson's K Records scene circuit.

Break-up and reunion

The band ended in 1996 following the death of drummer Mathew Fletcher. The surviving members continued in other projects, notably Marine Research and later Tender Trap, while Amelia Fletcher also became known as an economist and competition-policy official. In 2023 Heavenly re-formed — with Ian Button on drums — for reunion concerts and festival appearances, and subsequently released new material, returning to widespread affection within the indiepop community.
