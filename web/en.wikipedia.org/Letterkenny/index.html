Letterkenny

Letterkenny is a Canadian television sitcom created by Jared Keeso and Jacob Tierney, based on the web series Letterkenny Problems. Set in the fictional small rural Ontario town of Letterkenny, it follows the feuds and fast-talking banter of "hicks", skids, hockey players and other locals.

Premise

Wayne (Keeso), the town's "toughest guy", his sister Katy, and friends Daryl and "Squirrelly" Dan defend their way of life through wordplay-heavy dialogue, rapid-fire jokes and occasional dust-ups. The show is known for its distinctive slang, including Wayne's catchphrase "Pitter patter, let's get at 'er."

Reception

Letterkenny won multiple Canadian Screen Awards, including Best Comedy Series, and developed a devoted international audience through Hulu. A spin-off, Shoresy, premiered in 2022.
