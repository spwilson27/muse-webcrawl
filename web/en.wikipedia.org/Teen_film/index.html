Teen film

[Placeholder image: Teen_film.png]

A teen film or teen movie is a film genre targeted at teenagers, youth and young adults in which the plot is based upon the lives, problems, and coming of age of teenagers and young adults, such as dating, school, rebellion against parents, and identity formation.

History

The genre emerged in the 1950s with films such as Rebel Without a Cause (1955) and the beach party films of the 1960s. John Hughes popularised the modern teen film in the 1980s with Sixteen Candles, The Breakfast Club, and Ferris Bueller's Day Off. The 1990s saw a revival with Clueless, American Pie, and 10 Things I Hate About You.

Subgenres

Teen films include teen dramas, romantic comedies, horror films, and dystopian films such as The Hunger Games series.
