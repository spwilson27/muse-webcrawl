Israel

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Israel, officially the State of Israel, is a country in West Asia situated in the Southern Levant of the Middle East. It is bordered by Lebanon to the north, Syria to the northeast, Jordan to the east, Egypt to the southwest, and the Mediterranean Sea to the west. Israel also controls the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, territories of the State of Palestine. Its capital and largest city is Jerusalem, and its financial and technological centre is Tel Aviv.

History

The region has been inhabited since prehistoric times and was the heartland of the ancient kingdoms of Israel and Judah. After centuries of rule by successive empires — Persian, Hellenistic, Roman, Byzantine, Arab, Crusader, Mamluk and Ottoman — the area came under British administration after the First World War as part of Mandatory Palestine. The Zionist movement, seeking a Jewish homeland, promoted immigration during this period. In 1947 the United Nations proposed partitioning the land into Jewish and Arab states; the Jewish leadership accepted and proclaimed the State of Israel on 14 May 1948, while neighbouring Arab states invaded, beginning a conflict that has shaped the region ever since.

Geography

Despite its small area of about 20,770 square kilometres (22,072 including annexed territories), Israel contains varied landscapes: the coastal plain along the Mediterranean, the central hills, the Jordan Rift Valley — including the Dead Sea, the lowest point on land — and the Negev desert in the south, which makes up more than half the land area. The Sea of Galilee and the Jordan River are its principal freshwater sources.

Demographics and culture

Israel has a population of roughly ten million. The majority are Jews, many of them immigrants or the descendants of immigrants from Europe, the Middle East, North Africa, Ethiopia and the former Soviet Union; about a fifth of citizens are Arabs, mostly Muslim, alongside Druze, Christian and other minorities. Hebrew is the official language, and Arabic has a special status. The country is the world's only Jewish-majority state and a major centre of Jewish culture and religion, holding sacred sites for Judaism, Christianity and Islam.

Economy

Israel has an advanced, market-based economy noted for its high-technology sector, which has earned it the nickname "Start-up Nation". Major industries include software, pharmaceuticals, defence equipment, agriculture and tourism. It is a member of the OECD and has free trade agreements with the United States and the European Union.
