Hamas

Emblem of Hamas

Hamas (Arabic: حماس, romanised: Ḥamās), officially the Islamic Resistance Movement, is a Palestinian nationalist Sunni Islamist political organisation with a military wing, the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades. Founded in 1987 during the First Intifada by Sheikh Ahmed Yassin and others as an offshoot of the Muslim Brotherhood, Hamas published a charter calling for the establishment of an Islamic Palestinian state. Its 2017 document of general principles accepted the 1967 borders as a formula for national consensus while not recognising Israel.

History

Hamas won the 2006 Palestinian legislative election and, after a conflict with Fatah in 2007, took control of the Gaza Strip, while the Palestinian Authority under Fatah continued to govern parts of the West Bank. Hamas has fought several wars with Israel, including in 2008–09, 2012, 2014, and 2021. On 7 October 2023, Hamas-led militants attacked Israel, killing around 1,200 people and taking some 250 hostages, triggering a large-scale Israeli military campaign in Gaza.

Designation

Hamas is designated as a terrorist organisation by Israel, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, and several other countries; some states designate only its military wing. It maintains a political bureau, historically based abroad, and has been supported by Iran, Qatar, and Turkey to varying degrees.

Governance and social services

Before the 2023 war, Hamas administered Gaza's government, security forces, and a network of charities, schools, and mosques that built its popular base.
