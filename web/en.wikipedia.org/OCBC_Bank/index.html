OCBC Bank

Oversea-Chinese Banking Corporation Limited, abbreviated as OCBC, is a multinational banking and financial services corporation headquartered in OCBC Centre, Singapore. OCBC Bank has total assets of more than S$580 billion, making it the second largest bank in Southeast Asia by assets and among the largest in the Asia-Pacific region.

TypePublic
IndustryBanking, financial services
Founded31 October 1932
HeadquartersSingapore

History

OCBC was formed in 1932 from the merger of three local banks: the Chinese Commercial Bank (1912), the Ho Hong Bank (1917), and the Oversea-Chinese Bank (1919), during the Great Depression. It grew to become one of Singapore's "big three" local banks, alongside DBS and UOB.

Operations

The bank has a network of more than 400 branches and offices in 19 countries and regions, with key subsidiaries including Great Eastern Holdings (insurance), Bank of Singapore (private banking), and PT Bank OCBC NISP in Indonesia. In 2024 OCBC moved to take full ownership of Great Eastern.
