Tokyo International Forum


The Glass Hall atrium

The Tokyo International Forum (東京国際フォーラム) is a multi-purpose exhibition and convention centre in Marunouchi, Chiyoda, Tokyo, Japan. Located between Tokyo Station and Yūrakuchō Station on the site of the former Tokyo City Hall, it is one of the city's best-known pieces of contemporary architecture.

The centre was designed by the Uruguayan-American architect Rafael Viñoly, who won an international competition for the commission in 1989; it opened in 1997. Its centrepiece is the Glass Hall, a vast boat-shaped atrium of glass and steel some 207 metres long and 60 metres high, whose roof trusses are supported by just two massive columns. The building also contains several halls, including Hall A, which with about 5,000 seats is one of the largest theatre-style venues in Japan, plus exhibition space, conference rooms, shops and restaurants.

The Forum hosts concerts, musicals, conventions and exhibitions. During the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games it served as the venue for weightlifting. Its spacious plaza, planted with trees and dotted with sculpture, is a popular public space in the Marunouchi business district.
