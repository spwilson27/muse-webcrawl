Beg (title)

Beg, also spelled bey, begh or bek, is a Turkic title for a chieftain, and a royal and noble title in Central Asian cultures and the Ottoman sphere. It is still used as a surname and honorific in parts of Turkey, Central Asia and South Asia.

Etymology and use

The title appears in Old Turkic inscriptions and spread widely with Turkic peoples. In the Ottoman Empire, bey denoted rulers of small principalities (beyliks) and was later a general honorific.

Variants

Forms include Uzbek and Uyghur beg, Azerbaijani bəy, and the South Asian baig, borne notably by Mirza and Mughal families. The female equivalent is begum. See Bey and Begum.
