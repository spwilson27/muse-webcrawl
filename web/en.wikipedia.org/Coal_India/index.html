Coal India

Founded: 1975
Headquarters: Kolkata, India
Owner: Government of India
Industry: Coal mining

Coal India Limited (CIL) is an Indian state-owned coal mining and refinery company headquartered in Kolkata. It is the largest producer of coal in the world and a Maharatna public sector undertaking controlled by the Government of India under the Ministry of Coal.

History

Coal India was formed in 1975 following the nationalisation of India's coal mines in the early 1970s. It operates through several wholly owned subsidiaries, including Eastern Coalfields, Bharat Coking Coal, Central Coalfields, Western Coalfields, South Eastern Coalfields, Northern Coalfields, and Mahanadi Coalfields.

Operations

The company produces the great majority of India's coal output — on the order of 700 million tonnes annually in recent years — supplying power generation, steel, cement, and other industries. It employs several hundred thousand people and operates hundreds of mines, both opencast and underground.
