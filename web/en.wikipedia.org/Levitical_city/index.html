Levitical city

Levitical cities were 48 towns in ancient Israel set apart in the Hebrew Bible for the tribe of Levi, which received no territorial inheritance of its own because of its priestly and religious duties. The cities are listed in Joshua 21 and 1 Chronicles 6.

Biblical account

According to Numbers 35, each of the other tribes contributed cities from its territory, together with surrounding pastureland for the Levites' livestock. Among the 48 were the six cities of refuge — such as Hebron, Shechem, Kedesh, Golan, Ramoth-Gilead and Bezer — where a person who had killed unintentionally could flee from the avenger of blood until trial.

Organisation

The cities were distributed among the Levitical clans: the Kohathites received cities mainly in the south and centre, the Gershonites in the north, and the Merarites in Transjordan and the north-east. The arrangement gave the Levites a presence throughout the land.
