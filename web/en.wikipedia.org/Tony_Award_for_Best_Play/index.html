Tony Award for Best Play

The Tony Award for Best Play is an annual award honouring the best new non-musical play produced on Broadway. It is presented by the American Theatre Wing and the Broadway League at the Tony Awards ceremony in New York.

History

The category dates to the earliest Tony Awards; the first winner was Mister Roberts (1948). The award goes to the playwright and the producers of the winning production. Acclaimed winners have included A Raisin in the Sun, Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf?, Amadeus, Angels in America, Doubt, The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night-Time, Harry Potter and the Cursed Child and Stereophonic.

Eligibility

To be eligible, a play must open on Broadway during the season and be classified as a new work rather than a revival; revivals compete in the separate Best Revival of a Play category.
