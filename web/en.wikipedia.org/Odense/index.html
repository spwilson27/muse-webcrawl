Odense

Country: Denmark
Region: Southern Denmark
Island: Funen
Population: approx. 180,000 (city)

Odense (Danish: [ˈoðˀn̩sə]) is the third largest city in Denmark and the largest city on the island of Funen. The city lies on the Odense River, about 167 kilometres west of Copenhagen. Its name derives from Odin's vi, "Odin's shrine", referring to a sanctuary of the Norse god Odin.

History

Odense is one of Denmark's oldest cities, celebrating its thousandth anniversary in 1988; it was first mentioned in a letter of 988. Saint Canute IV was murdered in the city's priory church in 1086 and canonised, making Odense a medieval pilgrimage centre. The city prospered as a market town and later as an industrial centre with shipbuilding and food processing.

Culture

Odense is best known as the birthplace of the fairy-tale writer Hans Christian Andersen (1805–1875), whose childhood home and the Hans Christian Andersen Museum are major attractions. It is also the birthplace of composer Carl Nielsen. The city is home to Odense Zoo, the Funen Village open-air museum, and the University of Southern Denmark, and is a centre for robotics research and industry.

Transport

The city is a rail and motorway hub on the route between Jutland and Zealand, linked to both by the Great Belt Fixed Link to the east. A light rail line, the Odense Letbane, opened in 2022.
