David Holmgren

David Holmgren (born 1955) is an Australian environmental designer, ecological educator and writer. With Bill Mollison he is co-originator of the permaculture concept, first published in Permaculture One (1978).

Holmgren was born in Fremantle, Western Australia, and studied environmental design at the Tasmanian College of Advanced Education, where he met Mollison. Their collaboration applied systems thinking and ecology to the design of sustainable human settlements and perennial agricultural systems.

Work

While Mollison popularized permaculture globally, Holmgren developed its theoretical foundations, most fully in Permaculture: Principles and Pathways Beyond Sustainability (2002), which set out twelve design principles and the permaculture flower diagram of domains of action. His earlier book Melliodora: Hepburn Permaculture Gardens documented the property in Hepburn Springs, Victoria, where he lives and demonstrates cool-temperate permaculture.

In Future Scenarios (2009) and RetroSuburbia (2018), Holmgren addressed energy descent — the contraction of fossil fuel availability — and strategies for household-level resilience and suburban retrofit.
