Hahnenkamm, Kitzbühel

The Hahnenkamm is a mountain in the Austrian state of Tyrol, rising to 1,712 metres above the town of Kitzbühel. It is world-famous as the venue of the annual Hahnenkamm Races, the most prestigious event on the FIS Alpine Ski World Cup calendar, first held in 1931.

The Streif

The downhill race is run on the Streif course, widely considered the most demanding on the World Cup circuit. It features gradients of up to 85 percent at the Mausefalle jump, the steep Steilhang, and high-speed traverses where racers reach around 140 km/h. A slalom on the neighbouring Ganslernhang completes the traditional weekend, and the combined winner receives the Hahnenkamm trophy.

Access

The summit is reached by the Hahnenkammbahn gondola lift from Kitzbühel; in summer the mountain is a popular hiking and mountain-biking area.
