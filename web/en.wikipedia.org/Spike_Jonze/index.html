Spike Jonze

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Jonze in 2013

Spike Jonze (born Adam Spiegel, October 22, 1969) is an American filmmaker, photographer and actor. He began his career creating skateboarding videos and distinctive music videos for artists including the Beastie Boys ("Sabotage"), Björk, Weezer and Fatboy Slim, before moving into feature films.

Career

Jonze's feature directorial debut, Being John Malkovich (1999), written by Charlie Kaufman, earned him an Academy Award nomination for Best Director. He reteamed with Kaufman for Adaptation (2002) and later directed Where the Wild Things Are (2009), adapted from Maurice Sendak's picture book. His film Her (2013), a science-fiction romance about a man who falls in love with an artificial intelligence, won him the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.

Jonze is also a co-creator of the MTV stunt series Jackass and its films, and a co-founder of Directors Label. He has acted occasionally under the name Richard Koufey and appeared in films including Three Kings (1999).
