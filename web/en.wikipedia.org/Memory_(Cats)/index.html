Memory (Cats)

"Memory" is a show tune from the musical Cats (1981), composed by Andrew Lloyd Webber with lyrics by Trevor Nunn based on T. S. Eliot's poems "Preludes" and "Rhapsody on a Windy Night". It is sung by the character Grizabella, a formerly glamorous cat who has fallen on hard times, as a lament for her lost youth and an appeal to be accepted back by the other cats.

The song

The melody had earlier been considered by Lloyd Webber for other projects, and its main theme has often been noted for its similarity to passages in classical music. "Memory" is the emotional climax of Cats and its best-known song. Elaine Paige, who originated the role of Grizabella in London, released it as a single that reached No. 6 in the UK in 1981.

Recordings and legacy

The most commercially successful recording is by Barbra Streisand (1981), which became a standard; the song has been recorded by hundreds of artists including Barry Manilow, Celine Dion and Susan Boyle. Jennifer Hudson performed it in the 2019 film adaptation of Cats. "Memory" remains one of the most famous songs of the musical theatre repertoire.
