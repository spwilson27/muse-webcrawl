The Rumble in the Jungle

The Rumble in the Jungle was a heavyweight boxing championship fight between undefeated champion George Foreman and Muhammad Ali, held on 30 October 1974 at the Stade du 20 Mai in Kinshasa, Zaire (now DR Congo). It is widely considered the greatest boxing match of the 20th century.

The fight

Promoted by Don King with $5 million purses funded by dictator Mobutu Sese Seko, the bout was preceded by the Zaire 74 music festival. Ali, a heavy underdog against the fearsome punching Foreman, employed his famous "rope-a-dope" strategy — leaning on the ropes and letting Foreman exhaust himself — before knocking him out in the eighth round to regain the title.

Legacy

An estimated one billion people watched worldwide. The event was chronicled in Norman Mailer's The Fight and the Oscar-winning documentary When We Were Kings (1996). See also The Shadow Brokers.
