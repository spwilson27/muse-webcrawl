Dallara Automobili is an Italian manufacturer of racing cars and engineering consultancy founded by Gian Paolo Dallara in 1972 in Varano de' Melegari, near Parma. The company is the world's largest producer of racing car chassis and supplies cars to series including IndyCar, FIA Formula 2, FIA Formula 3, Super Formula and numerous sports-car championships.

History

Gian Paolo Dallara had previously worked for Ferrari, Maserati, Lamborghini and De Tomaso, where he was involved with the Lamborghini Miura. His company began by building sports prototypes and hillclimb cars, then entered Formula Three in 1978, where its cars came to dominate the category in Italy and abroad. In 1988 the company entered Formula One as chassis constructor for Scuderia Italia (BMS), competing until 1992, and later built the chassis for the Hispania Racing Team in 2010. Dallara also designed the Haas F1 Team's chassis from the team's debut in 2016.

IndyCar and spec racing

Dallara entered the Indy Racing League in 1997 and became its sole chassis supplier; the Dallara DW12, introduced in 2012 and named in honour of Dan Wheldon, remains the basis of the IndyCar chassis. Dallara is likewise the sole supplier to the FIA Formula 2 and Formula 3 championships, Indy NXT, and Japanese Super Formula, and built the chassis for all cars in the racing video-game-inspired e-series of single-make categories.

Engineering and road cars

Beyond racing, Dallara performs contract engineering for manufacturers including Alfa Romeo, Bugatti, Ferrari, KTM and Maserati, and was responsible for the carbon-fibre chassis of several road cars. In 2017 it launched its first car under its own name, the Dallara Stradale, a lightweight sports car. The company operates facilities in Italy, the United States (Indianapolis) and Japan, and runs the Dallara Academy museum and educational centre, opened in 2018.
