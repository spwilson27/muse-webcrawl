Mammootty.

Mammootty (born 7 September 1951 as Muhammad Kutty Panaparambil Ismail) is an Indian actor and film producer who works predominantly in Malayalam cinema. In a career spanning over five decades, he has appeared in more than 400 films, mainly in Malayalam and also in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada. Widely regarded as one of the greatest actors in Indian cinema, he is noted for his versatility and longevity, and is affectionately called "Mammukka" by fans. He is one of the twin pillars of Malayalam cinema alongside Mohanlal.

Career

Mammootty made his debut as a junior artist in Anubhavangal Paalichakal (1971) but his first credited lead role came in Vilkkanundu Swapnangal (1980). He rose to prominence in the 1980s with films such as New Delhi (1987). His collaborations with directors like Adoor Gopalakrishnan, K. G. George, and Hariharan produced landmark performances: Mathilukal (1990), Vidheyan (1994), and Oru Vadakkan Veeragatha (1989), which demonstrated his range across tragedy, historical drama, and psychological roles. He has also played real-life figures, notably B. R. Ambedkar in the English-language film Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar (2000).

Awards and recognition

Mammootty has won three National Film Awards for Best Actor — for Oru Vadakkan Veeragatha and Mathilukal (1989), for Vidheyan and Ponthan Mada (1993), and for Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar (1999) — a record he shares with Kamal Haasan and Ajay Devgn, along with numerous Kerala State Film Awards and Filmfare Awards South. He was honoured with the Padma Shri in 1998 and received honorary doctorates from the University of Kerala and the University of Calicut. In later years he continued to headline critically acclaimed films such as Peranbu (2018, Tamil), Unda (2019), Bheeshma Parvam (2022), Nanpakal Nerathu Mayakkam (2022), and Kaathal – The Core (2023).

Other work

Through his production company Mammootty Kampany, he has produced several of his recent films. He has served as chairman of Malayala Manorama's television channel and as a goodwill ambassador for various social causes in Kerala. His son Dulquer Salmaan is also a leading actor.
