Boeing F

Boeing F may refer to several aircraft produced by the Boeing Airplane Company and designated with the letter F in the United States Navy and Marine Corps designation systems, or to Boeing's early fighter designs.

Naval fighters

Under the pre-1962 US Navy designation system, Boeing fighters included the FB series and, most famously, the F4B — a 1930s carrier-based biplane fighter that was also exported and produced for the US Army Air Corps as the P-12. The F4B/P-12 was one of the most widely used American fighters of the interwar period, noted for its manoeuvrability and sturdy construction.
