The Boeing EA-18G Growler is an American carrier-based electronic warfare aircraft, a specialized version of the two-seat Boeing F/A-18F Super Hornet. The EA-18G replaced the Northrop Grumman EA-6B Prowlers in service with the United States Navy. The Growler's electronic warfare capability is provided mainly by Northrop Grumman.

The aircraft combines the airframe of the Super Hornet with a suite of electronic attack equipment, including ALQ-218 receiver pods on the wingtips and ALQ-99 jamming pods, along with communications receiver and jamming equipment. It retains the ability to carry air-to-air missiles and AGM-88 HARM anti-radiation missiles.

Operational history

The Growler first deployed in 2010–2011 and has flown electronic attack missions in support of operations in Libya, Iraq, and Syria. The Royal Australian Air Force operates a fleet of EA-18Gs alongside its Super Hornets. The Growler is planned to receive the Next Generation Jammer to replace the ALQ-99 pods.
