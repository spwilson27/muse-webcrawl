The British Red Cross Society is the United Kingdom's national Red Cross society, part of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement. Founded in 1870 as the British National Society for Aid to the Sick and Wounded in War, it received its royal charter in 1908.

Work

The charity provides emergency response to crises in the UK and abroad, first aid training, refugee support, mobility aids and community services. It is an auxiliary to the public authorities in the humanitarian field and is bound by the movement's fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality and neutrality.
