Uğur Şahin

Born1965, İskenderun, Turkey
OccupationPhysician, immunologist, entrepreneur
Known forCo-founding BioNTech; COVID-19 vaccine

Uğur Şahin (born 1965) is a German physician, immunologist and entrepreneur of Turkish origin. Together with his wife Özlem Türeci he co-founded the biotechnology company BioNTech in 2008 and serves as its chief executive officer.

Career

Şahin studied medicine at the University of Cologne and built his career at the University of Mainz, researching cancer immunotherapy and mRNA-based individualised cancer treatments. Earlier he co-founded Ganymed Pharmaceuticals, acquired by Astellas Pharma in 2016.

COVID-19 vaccine

In January 2020 Şahin redirected BioNTech's mRNA research to develop a vaccine against COVID-19; the resulting vaccine, produced with Pfizer, became the first mRNA vaccine authorised for general use. Şahin and Türeci have received numerous honours, including Germany's Order of Merit.
