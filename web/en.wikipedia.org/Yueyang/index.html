Yueyang (simplified Chinese: 岳阳; traditional Chinese: 岳陽; pinyin: Yuèyáng) is a prefecture-level city in northeastern Hunan province, China, on the southern shore of Dongting Lake near the Yangtze River. It is one of Hunan's major port cities and a historic cultural centre.

History

The area has been settled since ancient times and was known historically as Baling and Yuezhou. The city is famous across China for the Yueyang Tower, one of the Three Great Towers of southern China, which overlooks Dongting Lake. The Northern Song statesman Fan Zhongyan's essay "Memorial to Yueyang Tower" (岳阳楼记), written in 1046, made the tower a symbol of public-spiritedness; its famous line puts concern for the world's welfare before personal joy.

Geography and economy

Yueyang administers districts and counties along the lake and the Yangtze, including the Chenglingji port, Hunan's main outlet to the river. Its economy includes petrochemicals (a large Sinopec complex), paper, food processing and tourism. Junshan Island in Dongting Lake is noted for producing Junshan Yinzhen yellow tea.

Transport

The city is served by the Beijing–Guangzhou railway and high-speed line, expressways, Yangtze shipping through Chenglingji, and Yueyang Sanhe Airport, opened in 2018.
