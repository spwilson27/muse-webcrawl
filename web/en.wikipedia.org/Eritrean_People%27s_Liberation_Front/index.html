Eritrean People's Liberation Front

The Eritrean People's Liberation Front (EPLF) was an armed organisation that fought for the independence of Eritrea from Ethiopia. Founded in 1970 as a breakaway from the Eritrean Liberation Front (ELF), it became the dominant liberation movement and won the Eritrean War of Independence in 1991.

History

The EPLF emerged from factional splits within the ELF and built a highly disciplined guerrilla army, supported by an extensive network of underground facilities in the mountains around Nakfa. After defeating Ethiopian forces and entering Asmara in May 1991, the EPLF formed a provisional government. A referendum in 1993 confirmed Eritrean independence with overwhelming support.

Transformation into the PFDJ

In 1994 the EPLF renamed itself the People's Front for Democracy and Justice (PFDJ), which has remained the sole legal political party in Eritrea under President Isaias Afwerki, a former EPLF leader. The movement's wartime ideals of social justice and self-reliance continue to shape the country's politics.
