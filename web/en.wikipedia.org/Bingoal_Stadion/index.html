Bingoal Stadion

The Bingoal Stadion is a multi-use stadium in The Hague, Netherlands, and the home ground of Eredivisie club ADO Den Haag. Completed in 2007 at a cost of about €28 million, it replaced the club's former Zuiderpark Stadion and has a capacity of around 15,000 spectators.

History

The stadium opened on 28 July 2007. It was initially known simply as the ADO Den Haag Stadium, then from 2010 as the Kyocera Stadion under a naming-rights deal, later the Cars Jeans Stadion, and from 2022 the Bingoal Stadion after the Belgian betting company Bingoal acquired the naming rights. The stadium is noted for extensive security features built into its design, reflecting concern about football hooliganism at the time of construction, and it hosted matches of the 2014 Hockey World Cup alongside the GreenFields Stadium.
