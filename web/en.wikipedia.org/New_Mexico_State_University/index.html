New Mexico State University

New Mexico State University (NMSU) is a public land-grant research university in Las Cruces, New Mexico. Founded in 1888 as Las Cruces College, it is the state's land-grant institution and the flagship of the NMSU system.

Academics

The university offers bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees through colleges including Agricultural, Consumer and Environmental Sciences; Arts and Sciences; Business; Engineering; and Health, Education and Social Transformation. Its astronomy programme operates the Apache Point Observatory, and it hosts a Physical Science Laboratory and the Southwest Indian Polytechnic connections to regional research.

Campus and athletics

The main campus spans about 900 acres against the Organ Mountains. The Aggies compete in NCAA Division I, and the university is a Hispanic-serving institution with a large proportion of first-generation students.
