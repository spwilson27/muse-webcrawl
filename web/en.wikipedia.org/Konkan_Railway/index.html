Konkan Railway

The Konkan Railway is a railway line operated by the Konkan Railway Corporation linking India's western coast through Maharashtra, Goa and Karnataka, from Roha near Mumbai to Thokur near Mangalore, a distance of about 756 kilometres.

Construction

Built through the rugged Western Ghats terrain of the Konkan coast, the line required more than 2,000 bridges and about 91 tunnels, including the 6.5 km Karbude tunnel. Led by chief engineer E. Sreedharan, the project was completed in 1998 and dramatically shortened travel between Mumbai and Mangalore. Innovations included anti-collision devices and sky-bus experiments, and the line is noted for scenic monsoon-season journeys and for roll-on/roll-off truck services.
