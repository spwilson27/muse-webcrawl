Makuhari Messe (幕張メッセ) is a Japanese convention centre in Mihama-ku, Chiba City, within the Makuhari new city area on Tokyo Bay. Despite its name, it is not in the Makuhari district proper but in the nearby Nakase district.

Facilities

The complex comprises the International Exhibition Hall (halls 1–11), the International Conference Hall, and the Makuhari Event Hall. It hosts major trade fairs such as the Tokyo Game Show, Tokyo Auto Salon, and Jump Festa, as well as concerts and corporate events.

Events

Makuhari Messe served as a venue for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games, hosting fencing, taekwondo, wrestling, and goalball. It is one of Japan's largest convention facilities.
