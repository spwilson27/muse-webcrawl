St. James's Gate Brewery

St. James's Gate Brewery
Location: Dublin, Ireland
Opened: 1759
Owner: Diageo
Known for: Guinness stout

St. James's Gate Brewery is a brewery founded in 1759 by Arthur Guinness at St. James's Gate, Dublin, Ireland. It is the home of Guinness stout and one of Ireland's best-known industrial landmarks.

History

Arthur Guinness signed a famous 9,000-year lease on the site in 1759 at £45 per year. The brewery grew to become, for a time, the largest in the world, and Guinness became Ireland's largest exporter.

Guinness Storehouse

The Guinness Storehouse, a visitor attraction in a former fermentation building opened in 2000, tells the story of the brand and is one of Ireland's most visited tourist attractions.
