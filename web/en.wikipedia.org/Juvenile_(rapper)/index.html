Juvenile (rapper)

Terius Gray (born March 25, 1975), better known by his stage name Juvenile, is an American rapper from New Orleans, Louisiana. He rose to national fame as a member of the Hot Boys and a leading artist on Cash Money Records in the late 1990s, and is best known for the hit singles "Back That Azz Up", "Ha" and the number-one single "Slow Motion".

Career

Juvenile began recording bounce music as a teenager and released his debut album Being Myself in 1995. Signing with Cash Money, he broke through with 400 Degreez (1998), which sold over four million copies and produced "Ha" and "Back That Azz Up" (also titled "Back That Thang Up"). With B.G., Lil Wayne and Turk he formed the Hot Boys, whose album Guerrilla Warfare (1999) reached number five on the Billboard 200.

Solo success and later work

After leaving Cash Money, Juvenile topped the Billboard Hot 100 in 2004 with "Slow Motion" featuring Soulja Slim, from the album Juve the Great. He later returned to Cash Money and Atlantic for further albums including Cocky & Confident (2009) and Just Another Gangsta (2019) with Birdman. His 2023 Tiny Desk Concert performance brought renewed attention to his catalogue.
