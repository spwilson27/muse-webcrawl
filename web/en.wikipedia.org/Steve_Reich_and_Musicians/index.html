Steve Reich and Musicians

Steve Reich and Musicians is a musical ensemble founded by the American composer Steve Reich in 1966 to perform his works. It became one of the leading ensembles of minimal music, premiering many of Reich's landmark compositions and touring them internationally.

History

Reich formed the group after early performances of pieces such as It's Gonna Rain (1965) and Come Out (1966). The ensemble provided the precision and stamina required by his phasing and pattern-based works, including Piano Phase (1967), Drumming (1970–71), Music for Mallet Instruments, Voices and Organ (1973) and Music for 18 Musicians (1974–76). The flexible lineup typically combines percussion (marimbas, xylophones, drums), keyboards, winds, strings and voices.

Performances and recordings

The ensemble recorded Music for 18 Musicians for ECM Records in 1978, a release that brought Reich's music to a wide audience. It has since premiered works including Tehillim (1981), The Desert Music (1984) and Three Tales (2002), and continues to perform with Reich as artistic director. Many of its musicians, including members who also founded other new-music groups, have been associated with the ensemble for decades.
