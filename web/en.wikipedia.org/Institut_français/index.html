Institut français
Formation2011
TypePublic agency (EPIC)
PurposePromotion of French culture and language abroad
HeadquartersParis, France

The Institut français is a French public agency responsible for promoting French culture and the French language around the world. Created in 2011 under the supervision of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, it replaced the earlier association CulturesFrance.

Activities

The agency supports the network of Instituts français and French cultural centres abroad, organises festivals and touring exhibitions, promotes French books, cinema and performing arts, and supports the teaching of French as a foreign language. It also runs cultural seasons and residencies for artists.

Network

The Institut français works with more than ninety institutes worldwide, alongside the Alliances françaises network, to present France's cultural diplomacy and foster exchanges between French and foreign artists and audiences.
