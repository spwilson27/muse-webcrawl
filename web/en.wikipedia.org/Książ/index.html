Książ

Książ (German: Fürstenstein) is a castle near Wałbrzych in Lower Silesian Voivodeship, Poland. It is the largest castle in Silesia and the third-largest in Poland, after Malbork and Wawel.

History

The original stronghold was built in the late 13th century by Bolko I the Strict, Duke of Świdnica. The castle passed through Bohemian, Hungarian and local hands, and from 1509 was the seat of the Hochberg family, who rebuilt it in Baroque and later styles. Hans Heinrich XV von Hochberg, Prince of Pless, and his English wife Daisy, Princess of Pless, made it a lavish residence before the First World War.

During the Second World War the castle was seized by Nazi Germany and incorporated into the vast underground Project Riese tunnel complex; tunnels beneath the castle remain a subject of legend. After 1945 it lay in Poland and served various uses before restoration as a museum and hotel.
