Chamber of Deputies (Mexico)

The Chamber of Deputies (Cámara de Diputados) is the lower house of the Congress of the Union, the bicameral legislature of Mexico, the upper house being the Senate of the Republic. It sits in the Legislative Palace of San Lázaro in Mexico City.

Composition and elections

The Chamber has 500 deputies: 300 elected by plurality in single-member districts and 200 by proportional representation from regional lists. Deputies serve three-year terms; consecutive re-election has been permitted since reforms in the 2010s, ending Mexico's long-standing no-re-election principle in practice.

Powers

The Chamber has exclusive powers including approving the federal budget and overseeing public accounts. Together with the Senate it enacts federal legislation and can amend the constitution with state legislatures' approval.
