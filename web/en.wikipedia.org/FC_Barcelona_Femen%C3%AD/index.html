FC Barcelona Femení

Futbol Club Barcelona Femení, commonly known as Barça Femení, is a Spanish professional women's football team based in Barcelona, Catalonia. It is the women's football section of FC Barcelona. The club is one of the most successful women's football teams in Spain and Europe.

Founded1988
GroundEstadi Johan Cruyff
LeagueLiga F

History

Originally founded in 1970 as a supporters' club team, the women's section was officially incorporated into FC Barcelona in 2002. The team turned fully professional in 2015 and went on to dominate Spanish women's football.

Honours

Barça Femení won the UEFA Women's Champions League in 2021, 2023 and 2024, and completed a continental treble in the 2020–21 season. Players such as Alexia Putellas and Aitana Bonmatí have won the Ballon d'Or Féminin while at the club.
