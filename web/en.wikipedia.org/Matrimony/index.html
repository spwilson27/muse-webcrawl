Matrimony

Redirect note — see Marriage

Matrimony is a synonym for marriage: the culturally and legally recognised union, usually between two people, that establishes rights and obligations between the spouses, their children and their wider families. The word, from Latin matrimonium (derived from mater, "mother," plus the suffix denoting a condition), emphasises the state or institution of being married, and is especially used in religious contexts — in the Roman Catholic Church, Holy Matrimony is one of the seven sacraments, signifying the union of Christ and the Church and conferring grace on the couple, who are held to minister the sacrament to each other.

For the full treatment of the institution — its history, legal forms around the world, ceremonies and social functions — see the main article on marriage.
