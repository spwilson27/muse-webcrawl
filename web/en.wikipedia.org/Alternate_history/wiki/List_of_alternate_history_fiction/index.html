List of alternate history fiction

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of notable works of alternate history fiction, organized by their point of divergence. The genre explores worlds in which history unfolded differently from our own.

Early works

  • Arsène Lupin's era novellas and Geoffrey Trease's works preceded the modern genre.
  • L. Sprague de Camp, Lest Darkness Fall (1939) — a modern man in Ostrogothic Rome.
  • Murray Leinster, "Sidewise in Time" (1934) — an early parallel-worlds story.

American Civil War divergences

  • Ward Moore, Bring the Jubilee (1953)
  • Harry Turtledove, The Guns of the South (1992) and the Southern Victory series

Second World War divergences

  • Philip K. Dick, The Man in the High Castle (1962)
  • Robert Harris, Fatherland (1992)
  • Len Deighton, SS-GB (1978)

Other notable works

  • Kingsley Amis, The Alteration (1976)
  • Kim Stanley Robinson, The Years of Rice and Salt (2002)
  • Philip Roth, The Plot Against America (2004)
  • Michael Chabon, The Yiddish Policemen's Union (2007)
