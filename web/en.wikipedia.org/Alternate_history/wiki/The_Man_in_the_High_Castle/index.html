The Man in the High Castle

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Man in the High Castle is a 1962 alternate history novel by American author Philip K. Dick. It depicts a world in which the Axis powers won the Second World War: fifteen years after the war, Imperial Japan and Nazi Germany have divided the defeated United States between them, with a neutral buffer zone in the Rockies.

Plot

The novel interweaves the lives of several characters in Japanese-occupied San Francisco and the neutral zone, many of them guided by the I Ching. A banned novel within the novel, The Grasshopper Lies Heavy by the reclusive Hawthorne Abendsen — the "man in the high castle" — imagines an Allied victory, though one different from our own history.

Reception and adaptation

The book won the Hugo Award for Best Novel in 1963 and is regarded as a landmark of the genre. Amazon adapted it as a television series running from 2015 to 2019.
