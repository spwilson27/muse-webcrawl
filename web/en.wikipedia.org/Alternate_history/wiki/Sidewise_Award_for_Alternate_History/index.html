Sidewise Award for Alternate History

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Sidewise Award for Alternate History is an annual literary award recognizing the best works of alternate history fiction published in the previous year. It was established in 1995 and first presented in 1996. The name comes from Murray Leinster's 1934 story "Sidewise in Time", a pioneer of parallel-worlds fiction.

Categories

The award is given in categories for long-form works (novels and series) and short-form works (stories and novellas). Winners have included Stephen Baxter, Ken MacLeod, Ian R. MacLeod, and Mary Robinette Kowal, among others.

Judging

A panel of judges drawn from writers, historians, and critics selects the winners, which are usually announced at the World Science Fiction Convention or a similar event.
