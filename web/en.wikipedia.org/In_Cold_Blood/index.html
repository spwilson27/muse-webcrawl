In Cold Blood
Author: Truman Capote
Published: 1966
Genre: Non-fiction novel / true crime

In Cold Blood is a non-fiction novel by American author Truman Capote, first published in 1966. It recounts the 1959 murders of the four members of the Clutter family in the small farming community of Holcomb, Kansas, and the capture, trial and execution of the killers, Perry Smith and Richard Hickock.

Background

Capote read a short newspaper item about the murders and travelled to Kansas with his friend Harper Lee. Over several years he interviewed residents, investigators and the two killers, producing some 8,000 pages of notes.

Publication and reception

The book was serialised in The New Yorker in 1965 and became an immediate bestseller. It is credited as a pioneering work of the non-fiction novel and of New Journalism, though questions have been raised about the literal truth of some scenes.

Adaptations

The book was adapted into a noted 1967 film directed by Richard Brooks, and the story of its writing was dramatised in the films Capote (2005) and Infamous (2006).
