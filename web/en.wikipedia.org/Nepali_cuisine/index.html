Nepali cuisine

Nepali cuisine comprises the diverse foods of Nepal, shaped by the country's geography — Himalayan, hill, and Terai regions — and its ethnic diversity. The staple meal is dal bhat tarkari: lentil soup, steamed rice, and curried vegetables, eaten twice daily across much of the country.

Regional and ethnic dishes

Newari cuisine of the Kathmandu Valley includes dishes such as yomari, chatamari, and bara. Thakali cuisine is famed for its dal bhat sets with buckwheat and millet breads. Momos — Tibetan-style dumplings — are ubiquitous street food. Gundruk, a fermented leafy green, and dhindo, a thick porridge of millet or buckwheat flour, are traditional hill foods.

Ingredients

Common spices include cumin, coriander, turmeric, timur (Szechuan pepper), and jimbu, a Himalayan herb. Achar (pickle) accompanies most meals.
