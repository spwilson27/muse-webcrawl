County Antrim

County Antrim (Irish: Contae Aontroma) is one of the six counties of Northern Ireland and one of the traditional thirty-two counties of Ireland. It is in the province of Ulster, in the north-east of the island, with a population of over 650,000, including much of Belfast.

Geography

The county town is Antrim. The Antrim Coast and Glens include the Giant's Causeway, Glens of Antrim, Carrick-a-Rede rope bridge and Rathlin Island. Lough Neagh borders the south-west.
