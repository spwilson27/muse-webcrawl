The Ohio History Connection, formerly the Ohio Historical Society, is a nonprofit organisation devoted to the history of the U.S. state of Ohio. Founded in 1885, it is headquartered in Columbus at the Ohio History Center museum.

Activities

The organisation operates or partners in the management of more than 50 historic sites and museums across Ohio, including Serpent Mound, Fort Ancient, the National Afro-American Museum and Cultural Center, and the homes of Presidents Warren G. Harding and William Howard Taft interests in its network. It serves as the state's historic preservation office liaison, maintains the state archives in partnership, publishes history materials, and administers the Ohio History Fund grant programme.

History

The society grew from earlier antiquarian efforts and was chartered in 1885 with Archaeological and Historical Society of Ohio origins; it adopted the name Ohio History Connection in 2014 to reflect a broader public mission. It holds in trust extensive collections of artifacts, documents, photographs and natural history specimens.
