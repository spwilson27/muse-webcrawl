Mercury-Redstone 2

Mercury-Redstone 2

Mission type: Test flight

Operator: NASA

Launch date: 31 January 1961

Passenger: Ham (chimpanzee)

Apogee: 251 km (156 mi)

Mercury-Redstone 2 (MR-2) was the third uncrewed (but carrying a live passenger) flight of the Mercury-Redstone Launch Vehicle in NASA's Project Mercury. It carried Ham, a chimpanzee, on a suborbital flight on 31 January 1961.

Flight

The mission launched from Cape Canaveral, Florida. During the flight, the capsule reached an apogee higher than planned because of a thrust regulator malfunction, and it splashed down in the Atlantic Ocean after a flight of about 16.5 minutes. Ham survived the flight in good condition, suffering only a bruised nose, and his successful performance of lever-pulling tasks demonstrated that an astronaut could operate controls in space.

Significance

The flight was the final test before Alan Shepard's Mercury-Redstone 3 mission, the first American human spaceflight. Ham's capsule is displayed at the California Science Center.
