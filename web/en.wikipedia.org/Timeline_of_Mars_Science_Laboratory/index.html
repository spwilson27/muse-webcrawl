Timeline of Mars Science Laboratory

The Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) is a NASA mission that landed the Curiosity rover in Gale Crater on Mars on 6 August 2012 UTC. This timeline summarises the mission's launch, cruise, landing and surface operations, during which Curiosity has investigated Mars's climate and geology and assessed whether Gale Crater ever offered conditions favourable for microbial life.

Launch and landing

MSL was launched from Cape Canaveral on 26 November 2011 aboard an Atlas V rocket. After an eight-month cruise, the spacecraft performed the novel "sky crane" landing, lowering the rover to the surface on cables at Bradbury Landing site on 6 August 2012.

Surface mission

During its primary mission Curiosity found evidence of an ancient freshwater lake environment in Yellowknife Bay and detected organic molecules and fluctuating methane in the atmosphere. The rover then travelled to the base of Mount Sharp (Aeolis Mons), studying sedimentary layers, and continued climbing into sulfate-bearing terrain. The mission has been extended repeatedly, with the rover operating for well over a decade beyond its planned two-year lifetime.
