Extraterrestrials in fiction

Extraterrestrials in fiction are fictional portrayals of life originating elsewhere than on Earth, a staple of science fiction. From H. G. Wells's invading Martians in The War of the Worlds to the benevolent visitors of later film and television, alien beings have served as mirrors for human hopes, fears, and questions about intelligence and otherness.

Themes

Common motifs include first contact, alien invasion, alien abduction, and the exploration of alien societies. Portrayals range from near-human "rubber-forehead" aliens of television budgets to radically non-humanoid entities such as the heptapods of Arrival.

History

Early speculative voyages to the Moon featured inhabitants centuries before modern science fiction; the genre expanded greatly in the pulp magazines of the twentieth century and in franchises such as Star Trek and Star Wars.
