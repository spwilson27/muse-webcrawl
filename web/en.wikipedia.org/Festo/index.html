Festo SE & Co. KG is a German multinational industrial control and automation company based in Esslingen am Neckar. Founded in 1925 by Gottlieb Stoll and Albert Fezer, the company began in woodworking machinery (the name combines Fezer and Stoll) and introduced its first pneumatic products in the 1950s, becoming one of the world's leading suppliers of pneumatic and electric automation technology.

Business

Festo's catalogue includes pneumatic cylinders, valves and valve terminals, servo and stepper motor controllers, vacuum technology, sensors, and complete handling and assembly systems used in automotive, electronics, food and packaging, and process industries. Through Festo Didactic, the company is also a major provider of technical training equipment and education services for industrial automation.

Bionic Learning Network

Festo is widely known to the public for its Bionic Learning Network, a research program that builds robotic models inspired by biology. Demonstrators have included the BionicKangaroo, which recovers energy on each jump; the AirPenguin and AirRay flying robots; the BionicOpter dragonfly robot; robotic elephant-trunk manipulators (the Bionic Handling Assistant); and ant-like cooperative robots (BionicANTs). These projects showcase energy efficiency, lightweight construction, and adaptive control principles that feed back into product development.

Scale

The family-owned company employs on the order of twenty thousand people and operates in dozens of countries, with annual revenues of several billion euros. It remains a prominent example of the German Mittelstand.
