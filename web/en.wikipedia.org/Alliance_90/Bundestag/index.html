The Bundestag (German for "Federal Diet") is the directly elected federal parliament of Germany and the country's principal legislative body. It sits in the Reichstag building in Berlin.

Elections

Members are elected for four-year terms by mixed-member proportional representation: voters cast a first vote for a constituency candidate and a second vote for a party list, and parties must clear a five-percent threshold. A 2023 electoral reform fixed the chamber at 630 seats, ending the growth caused by overhang and balance seats.

Functions

The Bundestag elects the Federal Chancellor, passes federal legislation together with the Bundesrat, approves the budget, and oversees the executive through committees, question times, and committees of inquiry. Its president is the second-highest-ranking official in the German order of precedence, after the Federal President.
