The Greens (Germany)

The Greens (German: Die Grünen) is a green political party in Germany, known in full since 1993 as Alliance 90/The Greens (Bündnis 90/Die Grünen). It was formed in 1980 in West Germany from environmental, anti-nuclear, peace and alternative movements, and in 1993 it merged with the East German citizens' movement Alliance 90.

History

The party entered the Bundestag in 1983 and became a major force in German politics, overcoming early internal conflict between its fundamentalist ("Fundi") and realist ("Realo") wings. From 1998 to 2005 it governed federally in a "red-green" coalition with the Social Democratic Party under Chancellor Gerhard Schröder, with Joschka Fischer as foreign minister and vice chancellor. The Greens have led several state governments, including Baden-Württemberg under Winfried Kretschmann from 2011.

Recent role

In the 2021 federal election, led by Annalena Baerbock, the party achieved its best-ever federal result and joined the "traffic light" coalition with the SPD and FDP, taking the foreign, economy and climate ministries; Robert Habeck became vice chancellor and Annalena Baerbock foreign minister. The coalition collapsed in late 2024. The party's programme centres on climate protection, the energy transition, social justice and a pro-European foreign policy.
