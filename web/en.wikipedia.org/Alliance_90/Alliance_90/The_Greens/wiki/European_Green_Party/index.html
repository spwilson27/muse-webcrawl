European Green Party

The European Green Party (EGP), also called the European Greens, is a transnational federation of green political parties in Europe and a European political party. It was founded on 21 February 2004 in Rome, succeeding the European Federation of Green Parties (founded in 1993). The EGP has dozens of member parties from EU member states and other European countries, and its headquarters are in Brussels.

Politics

The party advocates environmental protection, climate action, social justice, gender equality, human rights, participatory democracy, nonviolence and a more democratic and federal European Union. In the European Parliament its members sit in the Greens/European Free Alliance group, which it shares with the European Free Alliance and independent MEPs. The EGP chooses lead candidates (Spitzenkandidaten) for European elections; prominent figures associated with it include Ska Keller and Bas Eickhout.

Organisation

The party's highest body is its congress, held at least every five years, with a council meeting between congresses. It is led by two co-chairs, one female and one male, in line with the gender-balance practices common to green parties. The EGP is part of the Global Greens network of green parties worldwide.
