Global Greens

Founded: 2001
Headquarters: Brussels, Belgium
Ideology: Green politics
Members: Green parties in around 90 countries

The Global Greens is an international network of political parties and movements that work to implement the Global Greens Charter, a common statement of core values adopted at the first Global Greens Congress in Canberra, Australia, in 2001. The network brings together Green parties from the regional federations of Europe, Africa, the Americas, and Asia-Pacific, coordinating campaigns on climate change, democracy, biodiversity, and social justice.

The Charter

The Global Greens Charter identifies six core principles: ecological wisdom, social justice, participatory democracy, nonviolence, sustainability, and respect for diversity. Member parties, including Germany's Alliance 90/The Greens, endorse the Charter and send delegates to Global Greens Congresses, held roughly every five years.

Organisation

The Global Greens is governed by a steering committee drawn from its four regional federations and is supported by a small secretariat. It maintains close ties with groups such as the Federation of Young European Greens and campaigns internationally for strong climate agreements, including within the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change.
