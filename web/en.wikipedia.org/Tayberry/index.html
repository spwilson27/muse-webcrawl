Tayberry

The tayberry (Rubus fruticosus × R. idaeus) is a cultivated shrub in the genus Rubus of the family Rosaceae, a hybrid between a blackberry and a red raspberry. It was patented in 1979 and is named after the River Tay in Scotland.

Description and cultivation

The tayberry produces long, conical aggregate fruits that ripen to a deep purplish-red and are larger than raspberries and more aromatic than loganberries, a similar hybrid. The plant was bred by Derek Jennings at the Scottish Crops Research Institute in Dundee. Fruits are soft and best picked by hand, which limits commercial production; they are used in jams, desserts and liqueurs. Plants are vigorous, thorny, and usually trained on wires.
