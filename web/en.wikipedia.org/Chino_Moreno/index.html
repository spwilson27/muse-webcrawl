Chino Moreno
Born: Camillo Wong Moreno, 20 June 1973, Sacramento, California
Genres: Alternative metal, nu metal, experimental rock
Instruments: Vocals, guitar
Groups: Deftones, Crosses, Palms, Team Sleep

Chino Moreno (born Camillo Wong Moreno) is an American musician best known as the lead vocalist and contributing guitarist of the alternative metal band Deftones, which he co-founded in Sacramento in 1988.

Career

With Deftones, Moreno developed a style shifting between ethereal, melodic singing and aggressive screams, on albums including White Pony (2000), Diamond Eyes (2010) and Ohms (2020). White Pony is widely regarded as a landmark of nu metal and alternative metal.
