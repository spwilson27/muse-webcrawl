Drishyam 2 is a 2022 Indian Hindi-language crime thriller film directed by Abhishek Pathak. A remake of the 2021 Malayalam film of the same name by Jeethu Joseph, it is the sequel to the 2015 film Drishyam and again stars Ajay Devgn as Vijay Salgaonkar, alongside Tabu, Shriya Saran, and Akshaye Khanna as the new investigating officer.

Plot

Years after the events of the first film, Vijay has prospered, owning a cinema hall and working on producing a film of his own. The police, still smarting from their earlier failure, have kept the family under quiet surveillance and run an undercover operation in the village for years, planting officers near the family and tapping their conversations. A witness who once saw Vijay at a construction site on the night of the original crime surfaces, and a manuscript arising from Vijay's own film project — a crime story eerily matching the real events — becomes a weapon in the renewed investigation.

Arrested and brought to trial, Vijay's case turns on manipulated evidence and a swapped set of exhumed remains: the body finally produced for forensic testing proves not to be Varun's, devastating the prosecution and the Deshmukhs' hope of closure. Vijay is acquitted, remaining a step ahead to the end.

Reception

The film opened to positive reviews praising its second half and performances, and became one of the highest-grossing Hindi films of 2022, earning over ₹340 crore worldwide and cementing the Drishyam franchise's reputation for intricately plotted thrillers.
