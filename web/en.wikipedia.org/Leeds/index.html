Leeds

Leeds is a city in West Yorkshire, England, on the River Aire. It is one of the largest cities in the United Kingdom and the principal centre of the Leeds City Region.

History

Leeds grew from a manorial borough into a major mill town during the Industrial Revolution, built on the woollen cloth trade, engineering and printing. Its population expanded rapidly in the 19th century.

Today

Modern Leeds is a major centre for finance, legal services, retail and higher education, home to the University of Leeds and Leeds Beckett University, and to Leeds United Football Club at Elland Road.
