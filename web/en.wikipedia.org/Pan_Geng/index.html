Pan Geng

Pan Geng was a king of the Shang dynasty of ancient China, traditionally dated to around the 13th century BC. He is best known for relocating the Shang capital to Yin, near modern Anyang in Henan province, an event from which the dynasty is sometimes called the Yin dynasty.

Reign

According to traditional histories, Pan Geng moved the capital across the Yellow River despite resistance from aristocrats and commoners alike. Chapters attributed to him in the Book of Documents record speeches justifying the move. The relocation is credited with reviving the dynasty's fortunes, and Yin remained the capital for the rest of the Shang period, later becoming famous for its oracle bone inscriptions.
