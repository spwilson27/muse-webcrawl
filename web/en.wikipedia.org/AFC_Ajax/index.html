Amsterdamsche Football Club Ajax, also known as AFC Ajax, Ajax Amsterdam, or simply Ajax, is a Dutch professional football club based in Amsterdam, playing in the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football. The club is named after the mythological Greek hero Ajax.

History

Founded in 1900, Ajax is the most successful club in the Netherlands, with 36 Eredivisie titles and 20 KNVB Cups. Under coach Rinus Michels and star player Johan Cruyff, Ajax won three consecutive European Cups from 1971 to 1973 playing "Total Football". A young side coached by Louis van Gaal won the UEFA Champions League again in 1995.

Youth academy

Ajax is internationally renowned for its youth academy, De Toekomst, which has produced players such as Johan Cruyff, Marco van Basten, Frank Rijkaard, Dennis Bergkamp and Wesley Sneijder. The club traditionally plays in a red and white kit with a distinctive broad red vertical stripe.

Rivalries

Ajax's matches against Feyenoord of Rotterdam are known as De Klassieker (The Classic), while the rivalry with PSV Eindhoven pits the country's two other most successful clubs against Ajax in the "Big Three".
