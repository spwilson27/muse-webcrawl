Washer (mechanical)

In mechanical engineering contexts, the term washer may refer to components of machinery beyond the common hardware fastener washer — including thrust washers and spring elements used within mechanisms.

Thrust washers

A thrust washer is a flat bearing in the form of a ring that supports axial loads between rotating and stationary parts, for example in gearboxes and transmissions. They are often made of bronze, steel, or low-friction polymers.

Spring washers

Belleville washers and wave washers act as springs within assemblies, maintaining preload and compensating for thermal expansion or wear in bolted joints and bearings.
