Staple gun

A staple gun or powered stapler is a hand-held machine used to drive heavy metal staples into wood, plastic or masonry. Staple guns are used for many applications, such as attaching roofing felt, insulation, upholstery, and house wrap.

Types

Manual staple guns rely on a spring mechanism released by squeezing a lever. Electric and pneumatic models drive staples with greater force and speed, and are common in construction and furniture making. Hammer tackers drive staples by being swung like a hammer.

Staples

Staple guns use heavier wire staples than desktop staplers, sized by wire gauge and crown width. Proper selection of staple length is important to hold materials without protruding through them.
