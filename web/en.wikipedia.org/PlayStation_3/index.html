PlayStation 3

The PlayStation 3 (PS3) is a home video game console developed by Sony Computer Entertainment. It was first released in Japan and North America in November 2006 and in Europe in March 2007, succeeding the PlayStation 2 and competing with Microsoft's Xbox 360 and Nintendo's Wii.

Hardware

The PS3 introduced the Cell Broadband Engine processor, developed with Toshiba and IBM, and was the first console to use Blu-ray Disc as its primary storage medium, helping Blu-ray win the high-definition format war against HD DVD. Its high launch price and complex hardware initially slowed sales, but slimmer, cheaper revisions in 2009 and 2012 revived the platform.

Software and sales

Notable games include The Last of Us, Uncharted, Metal Gear Solid 4, LittleBigPlanet and Gran Turismo 5. The console also launched the PlayStation Network's free online play and the PlayStation Store. The PS3 sold more than 87 million units worldwide before production ended in 2017.
