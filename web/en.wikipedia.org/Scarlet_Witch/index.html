The Scarlet Witch (Wanda Maximoff) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, she first appeared in The X-Men #4 (1964). She debuted as a reluctant villain in Magneto's Brotherhood of Evil Mutants alongside her twin brother, Quicksilver, before reforming and becoming a long-standing member of the Avengers.

Publication history and characterization

Originally presented as a mutant whose "hex" power altered probability, Wanda's abilities were later redefined as the manipulation of chaos magic, making her one of the most powerful beings in the Marvel Universe. Her parentage was retconned several times; for decades she was presented as the daughter of Magneto, though later stories revised this. Landmark storylines include her marriage to the android Vision, the loss of her children, and the events of Avengers Disassembled and House of M (2005), in which, suffering a breakdown, she reshaped reality and then uttered the words "No more mutants," depowering most of mutantkind.

In other media

Elizabeth Olsen portrays Wanda Maximoff in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015) and starring in the Disney+ series WandaVision (2021), which earned critical acclaim, and in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022).
