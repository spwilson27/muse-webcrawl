Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty

An Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty (AONB; now also branded as a National Landscape) is an area of countryside in England, Wales, or Northern Ireland that has been designated for conservation due to its significant landscape value. The designation was created under the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949.

Designation

AONBs enjoy levels of protection from development comparable to national parks, though they lack their own planning authorities. There are 46 AONBs, covering about 18% of the English countryside, including the Cotswolds (the largest), the North Pennines, and the Gower Peninsula in Wales — the first area so designated, in 1956.

Rebranding

In November 2023 all AONBs in England and Wales were renamed "National Landscapes" to reflect their national importance, though the legal designation remains AONB.
