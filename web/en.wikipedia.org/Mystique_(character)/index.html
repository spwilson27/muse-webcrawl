Mystique (character)

Mystique (Raven Darkhölme) is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by artist David Cockrum and writer Chris Claremont, she first appeared in Ms. Marvel #16 (1978). A mutant, Mystique is a shapeshifter who can mimic the appearance and voice of any person with exquisite precision.

Alter ego: Raven Darkhölme

Species: Human mutant

Abilities: Shapeshifting, enhanced agility, slowed aging

Affiliations: Brotherhood of Mutants, X-Men, X-Factor

Fictional character biography

Born with blue skin and yellow eyes, Raven Darkhölme spent much of a life extending over a century working as a covert operative, terrorist, and assassin. She founded the second Brotherhood of Evil Mutants and led the government-sponsored Freedom Force. In modern continuity she is depicted as the wife of the mutant Destiny (Irene Adler) and the mother of Nightcrawler and the adoptive mother of Rogue.

In other media

Mystique was portrayed by Rebecca Romijn in the first X-Men film trilogy and by Jennifer Lawrence in the prequel films beginning with X-Men: First Class (2011).
