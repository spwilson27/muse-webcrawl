The 2 euro coin is the highest-denomination circulation coin of the euro, the common currency of the eurozone. It has been in circulation since 2002, when euro coins and banknotes were introduced. The coin is bimetallic, with a three-layer nickel brass centre (copper, zinc and nickel) set into a cupronickel ring — the reverse arrangement of the 1 euro coin.

Design

The common reverse side, designed by Luc Luycx, shows a map of Europe and the denomination. The obverse side is chosen by each issuing country and features national designs, such as monarchs, coats of arms, or cultural figures. Since 2004, eurozone states have also issued commemorative 2 euro coins — the only denomination permitted for commemoratives — each limited in number and legal tender throughout the eurozone.

Security features

The bimetallic construction, lettered edge and electromagnetic signature make the coin difficult to counterfeit. Its edge inscription varies by country, often repeating a motif such as "2 **" or a national motto.
