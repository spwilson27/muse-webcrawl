Manchester United F.C.

Manchester United Football Club, commonly referred to as Man United (often stylised as Man Utd) or simply United, is a professional football club based in Old Trafford, Greater Manchester, England. They compete in the Premier League, the top tier of English football. Nicknamed the Red Devils, they were founded as Newton Heath LYR Football Club in 1878, but changed their name to Manchester United in 1902. After a spell playing in Clayton, Manchester, the club moved to their current stadium, Old Trafford, in 1910.

History

United won their first league title in 1908 and the FA Cup in 1909. Under Matt Busby, appointed in 1945, the club built the famous "Busby Babes"; the team won league titles in 1956 and 1957 before eight players were killed in the Munich air disaster of 6 February 1958. Busby rebuilt the side around George Best, Denis Law and Bobby Charlton, and in 1968 United became the first English club to win the European Cup. The club declined after Busby's retirement until Alex Ferguson arrived in 1986. In 26 years Ferguson won 38 trophies, including 13 Premier League titles, five FA Cups and two UEFA Champions League titles, with the Treble of 1998–99 — league, FA Cup and Champions League — the pinnacle.

Honours

Manchester United are among the most successful clubs in English football, with a record 20 league titles, 13 FA Cups, 6 League Cups, and three European Cups/Champions Leagues, plus the UEFA Europa League, Cup Winners' Cup, Intercontinental Cup and FIFA Club World Cup. They were the first club to win the Premier League three seasons in a row (1998–2001), a feat they repeated in 2006–09.

Rivalries and support

The club's fiercest rivalries are with Liverpool, Leeds United and Manchester City. United are one of the most widely supported teams in the world and regularly rank among the richest football clubs by revenue; the Glazer family's leveraged takeover in 2005 and Ineos's acquisition of a minority stake and football operations control in 2024 are continuing topics of contention among supporters.
