PDF/X

PDF/X is a subset of the PDF standard designed to facilitate graphics exchange in professional printing workflows, defined by the ISO 15930 family of standards. The "X" stands for "eXchange".

Requirements

PDF/X files must be complete and self-contained: all fonts must be embedded, images must be included at sufficient resolution, and colour must be specified in print-appropriate spaces such as CMYK or spot colours, with an output intent characterising the intended printing condition. Features unsuitable for print, such as transparency in early versions, encryption, and external references, are prohibited.

Versions

Variants include PDF/X-1a (CMYK and spot only), PDF/X-3 (colour-managed workflows), PDF/X-4 (supports live transparency and layers), and PDF/X-6. Files are commonly verified by preflight software before being sent to print.
