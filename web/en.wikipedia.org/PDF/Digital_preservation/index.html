Digital preservation

Digital preservation refers to the series of managed activities necessary to ensure continued access to digital materials for as long as necessary, beyond the limits of media failure or technological and organisational change.

Strategies

Common strategies include migration (converting files to current formats, such as PDF/A, the archival subset of PDF standardized in ISO 19005), emulation (recreating obsolete hardware and software environments), refreshing (copying data to new media), and bit-level preservation with fixity checking using checksums.

Standards and models

The Open Archival Information System (OAIS) reference model (ISO 14721) provides the dominant conceptual framework. File formats such as PDF are widely used as preservation formats because of their ubiquity and self-contained rendering, though long-term risks include dependence on specific software and undocumented features.
