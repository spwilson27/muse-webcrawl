Barrage (dam)

A barrage is a type of low-head dam, typically consisting of a series of large gates that can be opened or closed to control the amount of water passing through. Unlike a conventional dam, a barrage regulates river flow and level rather than storing a large reservoir.

Uses

Barrages are built for irrigation diversion, navigation, flood control and tidal power. Notable examples include the barrages of the Nile Delta, the Sukkur Barrage on the Indus in Pakistan, and the proposed tidal barrages in the Severn Estuary. The word derives from the French barrer, "to bar".
