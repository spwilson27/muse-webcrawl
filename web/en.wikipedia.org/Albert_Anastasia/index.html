Albert Anastasia (born Umberto Anastasio; September 26, 1902 – October 25, 1957) was an Italian-American mobster who became boss of the New York crime family that later became known as the Gambino family. He was one of the founders of the contract-killing organization known as Murder, Inc.

Rise in the Mafia

Born in Calabria, Anastasia arrived in the United States as a teenager and worked on the Brooklyn waterfront. He rose through the ranks under Joe Masseria and then aligned with Lucky Luciano, helping eliminate the old-guard bosses during the Castellammarese War.

Boss and downfall

After the 1951 murder of Vincent Mangano, Anastasia took control of the family. His violent temper and high-profile killings, including that of garment worker Arnold Schuster, alienated other bosses. On October 25, 1957, Anastasia was shot dead by two gunmen while seated in a barber's chair at the Park Sheraton Hotel in Manhattan — one of the most famous mob murders in American history.
