Ceneri Base Tunnel

Location: Ticino, Switzerland
Length: 15.4 km (9.6 mi)
Status: Operational
Opened: 4 September 2020

The Ceneri Base Tunnel (Italian: Galleria di base del Monte Ceneri) is a Swiss railway base tunnel in the canton of Ticino. It passes under Monte Ceneri between Camorino in the Magadino Plain and Vezia near Lugano; it bypasses the steep grades of the Monte Ceneri Pass route.

At 15.4 kilometres (9.6 mi) long, the twin single-track tunnel is the third tunnel of the New Railway Link through the Alps (NRLA) project, after the Lötschberg and Gotthard base tunnels. Together with the Gotthard Base Tunnel it forms a flatter, faster north–south axis across the Alps, shortening the journey between Zurich and Milan.

History

Construction began in 2006. The main breakthrough in the western bore was achieved in January 2016, and the last breakthrough took place later that year. The tunnel was officially opened on 4 September 2020, and scheduled services began in December 2020, completing the NRLA project.

Operation

The tunnel allows freight trains to avoid the steep ramps of the old Monte Ceneri line and cuts travel time between Bellinzona and Lugano to about 12 minutes for passenger trains. It is used by long-distance trains on the Zurich–Milan corridor and by the Ticino regional S-Bahn network.
