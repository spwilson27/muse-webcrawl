Chișinău is the capital and largest city of the Republic of Moldova, and its main industrial and commercial centre. It lies in the middle of the country on the Bâc River, a tributary of the Dniester.

History

First mentioned in 1436 as a village in the Principality of Moldavia, Chișinău came under Ottoman suzerainty and then, in 1812, was ceded to the Russian Empire, becoming capital of Bessarabia. It joined Romania after the First World War, was annexed by the Soviet Union in 1940, suffered heavy destruction in the Second World War and a major 1940 earthquake, and became capital of independent Moldova in 1991.

Cityscape and culture

The city is known for its green boulevards and parks, Ștefan cel Mare Central Park, the Nativity Cathedral, the Triumphal Arch and Soviet-era architecture. It is Moldova's political, economic and educational centre.
