Sockpuppet

Sockpuppet
MeaningA deceptive secondary online identity
Named afterA puppet made from a sock
Related termsAstroturfing; meatpuppet; troll

A sockpuppet is a deceptive identity created and used by a person to manipulate opinion, evade scrutiny, or simulate independent support — most commonly an extra account or persona controlled by someone already participating under another name. The term derives from the simple hand puppet made from a sock, ventriloquised by its operator.

Usage

Sockpuppetry is used to praise or defend oneself (as in disputed book reviews attributed to authors reviewing their own work), to create false consensus in online discussions, to circumvent bans, and in organised propaganda and astroturfing campaigns by firms, political groups, and state-sponsored troll operations. On Wikipedia and many other community sites, covert sockpuppetry is prohibited and investigated through "sockpuppet investigations" and technical CheckUser evidence.

Related concepts

A "meatpuppet" is a real second person recruited to act on someone's behalf. Legitimate uses of alternate accounts — privacy, security testing, clearly disclosed role accounts — are generally distinguished from deceptive sockpuppetry. Laws and platform policies increasingly address undisclosed paid advocacy and coordinated inauthentic behaviour.

Etymology

The metaphor was in use in Usenet and early web communities by the mid-1990s and spread widely with the growth of forums and social media.
