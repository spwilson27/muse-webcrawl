Firestop

A firestop is a passive fire protection component used to seal openings and joints in fire-rated walls and floors — for example where pipes, cables or ducts penetrate them — restoring the barrier's fire resistance. Systems are tested as assemblies to standards such as ASTM E814 / UL 1479.

Materials

Firestops use intumescent sealants, collars and wraps that expand in heat to close around melting plastic pipes, silicone foams, mortars, pillows and batts. Each penetration type requires a listed system matched to pipe material and size.

Importance

Unsealed penetrations were implicated in the rapid smoke spread of several major fires, and inspection of firestopping is now a required special inspection in many jurisdictions' building codes.
