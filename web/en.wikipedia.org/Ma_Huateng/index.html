Ma Huateng

Ma Huateng (Chinese: 马化腾; born 29 October 1971), also known as Pony Ma, is a Chinese billionaire businessman and the co-founder, chairman, and chief executive of Tencent, one of the largest technology and entertainment companies in the world. He is regarded as one of China's most influential entrepreneurs.

Early life and education

Ma was born in Chaoyang District, Shantou, Guangdong. His father worked at a port authority in Shenzhen, and the family moved there when Ma was a teenager. He studied computer science at Shenzhen University, graduating in 1993, and worked in software development for paging systems at China Motion Telecom Development.

Tencent

In November 1998 Ma co-founded Tencent in Shenzhen with Zhang Zhidong and other partners. Its first product, the instant-messaging service OICQ (later renamed QQ after a trademark dispute with ICQ), grew explosively. Tencent listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2004. Under Ma's leadership the company expanded into online games, social networking, payments, and cloud services; its super-app WeChat, launched in 2011, surpassed one billion users. Ma has at times been ranked the richest person in China and among the wealthiest people in the world.

Other roles

Ma has served as a deputy to the National People's Congress. Known for a low public profile compared with other Chinese tech founders, he is noted for a product-focused management style and for large philanthropic pledges, including a major donation of Tencent shares to charitable causes.
