Accenture

Accenture plc is a global multinational professional services company specialising in information technology (IT) services and consulting. A Fortune Global 500 company, it is incorporated in Dublin, Ireland, and reports revenues of around US$65 billion (fiscal 2024), employing roughly 750,000 people serving clients in more than 120 countries. Accenture's current clients include 97 of the Fortune Global 100 and more than three-quarters of the Fortune Global 500.

History

Accenture began as the business and technology consulting division of the accounting firm Arthur Andersen in the early 1950s. In 1989 the consulting division separated as Andersen Consulting. Following an arbitration ruling in 2000, Andersen Consulting severed all contractual ties with Arthur Andersen and adopted the new name "Accenture" — a portmanteau of "accent on the future" — on 1 January 2001. The company held its initial public offering in July 2001 and moved its place of incorporation to Ireland in 2009.

Services

Accenture organises its services into areas including Strategy & Consulting, Technology, Operations, Industry X (digital engineering and manufacturing) and Song (its creative and customer-experience arm, formerly Accenture Interactive). The company provides consulting, systems integration, application and infrastructure outsourcing, cloud services and artificial intelligence solutions, and has grown substantially through a long series of acquisitions.

Organisation

Julie Sweet has served as chief executive officer since September 2019 and as chair since 2021. Accenture is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker ACN and is a component of the S&P 500 index.
