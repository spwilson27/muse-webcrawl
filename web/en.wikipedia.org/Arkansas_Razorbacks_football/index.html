The Arkansas Razorbacks football program represents the University of Arkansas in American football at the NCAA Division I FBS level. The Razorbacks compete in the Southeastern Conference, which they joined in 1991 after decades in the Southwest Conference.

History

The program began in 1894. The team adopted the Razorbacks name in 1910. Under coach Frank Broyles (1958–1976), Arkansas reached national prominence, winning a national championship in 1964. Notable coaches since include Lou Holtz, Ken Hatfield, Houston Nutt, and Bobby Petrino.

Traditions

Fans famously "call the Hogs" with the chant "Woo Pig Sooie". Home games are played primarily in Fayetteville, with some games historically held at War Memorial Stadium in Little Rock.

Rivalries

Arkansas's rivals include LSU (for the Golden Boot trophy), Texas, Texas A&M, and Ole Miss.
