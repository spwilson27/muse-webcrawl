Species distribution

FieldBiogeography, ecology
SynonymGeographic range
MethodsSpecies distribution modelling, occurrence records

Species distribution is the manner in which a biological taxon is spatially arranged. The geographic limits of a particular taxon's distribution is its range, often represented as shaded regions on a map.

Patterns

Distributions may be cosmopolitan, endemic to a restricted area, or disjunct, with populations separated by wide gaps. Patterns are shaped by climate, habitat, dispersal ability, competition, and historical events such as glaciation and continental drift.

Modelling

Species distribution models combine occurrence records with environmental variables to predict suitable habitat, and are used to forecast range shifts under climate change and to plan conservation reserves.
