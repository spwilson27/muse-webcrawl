The San Francisco Seals were a minor league baseball team in the Pacific Coast League that played in San Francisco from 1903 until 1957, when the arrival of the major league Giants from New York displaced them. One of the most successful franchises in PCL history, the Seals won 14 league championships.

History

The Seals played at Recreation Park and, from 1931, at Seals Stadium in the Mission District. The club developed numerous major leaguers, most famously Joe DiMaggio, whose 61-game hitting streak with the 1933 Seals remains a landmark of minor league history. His brothers Vince and Dom DiMaggio also played for the team.

Legacy

After the 1957 season the franchise relocated to Phoenix. Seals Stadium briefly hosted the Giants before being demolished; the Seals name survives in local nostalgia and was revived by later independent teams.
