Ambrox

Ambrox, also sold as Ambroxan, is a synthetic fragrance ingredient that reproduces the prized ambergris note in perfumery. It is a tricyclic ether derived semi-synthetically from sclareol, a compound obtained from clary sage.

Use in perfumery

Natural ambergris, produced in the digestive system of sperm whales, was for centuries one of the most valuable perfume materials, valued as both an odorant and a fixative. Ambrox, developed in the mid-20th century, offers a consistent, animal-free alternative with a warm, woody-amber scent and exceptional tenacity, and it is now a backbone ingredient of modern fine fragrance and functional perfumery.
