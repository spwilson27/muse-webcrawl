Maria I of Portugal

Maria I
Reign: 24 February 1777 – 20 March 1816
Born: 17 December 1734, Lisbon
Died: 20 March 1816 (aged 81), Rio de Janeiro
Spouse: Pedro III of Portugal
House: Braganza

Maria I (Maria Francisca Isabel Josefa Antónia Gertrudes Rita Joana; 17 December 1734 – 20 March 1816) was Queen of Portugal from 1777 until her death, the first undisputed queen regnant of Portugal. Deeply pious, she was known as "Maria the Pious" in Portugal and, because of her later mental illness, "Maria the Mad" in Brazil.

Reign

Maria succeeded her father Joseph I and ruled jointly with her husband and uncle, Pedro III. Her first act was to dismiss the powerful Marquis of Pombal, her father's chief minister, though she retained much of his administrative legacy. Her reign saw the foundation of the Academy of Sciences of Lisbon, charitable and educational institutions, and refuge given to French clergy fleeing the Revolution.

Illness and Brazil

From 1792, as her mental state deteriorated — today often attributed to porphyria or severe depression following the deaths of her husband, son and confessor — her son João acted as regent. In 1807, fleeing Napoleon's invading army, the royal family famously transferred the court to Rio de Janeiro, the first time a European monarch resided in a colony. Maria died there in 1816 and was succeeded by João VI.
