Ministry of Jal Shakti

The Ministry of Jal Shakti (lit. "Ministry of Water Power") is a ministry under the Government of India, formed in May 2019 by merging the Ministry of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation with the Ministry of Drinking Water and Sanitation. The ministry is responsible for policy and programmes relating to water resources, drinking water, sanitation, and the rejuvenation of the Ganga and its tributaries.

The creation of the unified ministry was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, with the aim of consolidating all water-related functions under a single authority given India's growing water stress. Gajendra Singh Shekhawat became its first minister.

Functions

The ministry oversees development, conservation and management of water resources; inter-state river disputes; the National Mission for Clean Ganga (Namami Gange); rural drinking water supply under the Jal Jeevan Mission, which aims to provide tap water connections to all rural households; and sanitation programmes including the Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin).

Organisations

Attached and subordinate organisations include the Central Water Commission, the Central Ground Water Board, the National Water Development Agency (which studies interlinking of rivers), the WAPCOS consultancy, and the Namami Gange authority (National Mission for Clean Ganga).

Programmes

  • Jal Jeevan Mission — functional household tap connections for rural India
  • Namami Gange — cleaning and rejuvenation of the river Ganga
  • Jal Shakti Abhiyan — a people's movement for water conservation and rainwater harvesting
  • Atal Bhujal Yojana — community-led groundwater management
