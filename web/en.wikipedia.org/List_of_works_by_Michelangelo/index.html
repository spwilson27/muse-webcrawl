List of works by Michelangelo

Michelangelo di Lodovico Buonarroti Simoni (1475–1564) was an Italian Renaissance sculptor, painter, architect, and poet, regarded as one of the greatest artists of all time. This list presents his principal works in sculpture, painting, and architecture.

Architecture

  • Laurentian Library and Medici Chapel, San Lorenzo, Florence (from 1519/1524)
  • Piazza del Campidoglio redesign, Rome (from 1536)
  • Palazzo Farnese completion, Rome
  • St. Peter's Basilica — chief architect from 1546; designed the dome, completed after his death by Giacomo della Porta
  • Porta Pia and Santa Maria degli Angeli conversion, Rome

Poetry and drawings

Michelangelo wrote over 300 sonnets and madrigals, many addressed to Tommaso dei Cavalieri and Vittoria Colonna, and left celebrated presentation drawings and studies, including the Battle of Cascina cartoon (lost) and numerous red-chalk life studies.
