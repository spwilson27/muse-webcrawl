List of United States Marine Corps acronyms and expressions

The United States Marine Corps has a rich body of acronyms, slang, and traditional expressions, reflecting its naval heritage and expeditionary culture. Many terms are shared across the US armed forces, while others are distinctively Marine. This list gives a selection of the most common.

Traditions

Marines celebrate the Corps' birthday on 10 November 1775, the date of its founding in Tun Tavern, Philadelphia. The hymn "The Marines' Hymn" ("From the Halls of Montezuma…") and the motto Semper Fidelis anchor many expressions. Rank and occupational slang evolves with each generation of service.
