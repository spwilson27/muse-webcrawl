Billboard (magazine)

Billboard is an American music magazine and website. It provides music charts, news, video, opinion, reviews, events and style related to the music industry. It is known for its record charts, including the Billboard Hot 100 and Billboard 200, tracking the most popular singles and albums in the United States.

History

Founded in Cincinnati in 1894 by William Donaldson and James Hennegan as Billboard Advertising, it originally covered bill posting and outdoor entertainment such as circuses, fairs and burlesque. It gradually focused on music, jukeboxes and radio, and by the 1960s concentrated exclusively on the music business.

Charts

Billboard published its first hit parade in 1936. The Hot 100 was launched in 1958, combining sales, radio airplay and later streaming. The Billboard 200 ranks albums by equivalent album units. Chart methodology has evolved with formats from sheet music and vinyl to downloads and on-demand streams, overseen in partnership with Luminate.

Other activities

Billboard operates international editions and licensing, conferences such as Billboard Latin Music Week, and award coverage. Ownership passed through Nielsen/VNU, Prometheus Global Media, Eldridge Industries and Penske Media.
