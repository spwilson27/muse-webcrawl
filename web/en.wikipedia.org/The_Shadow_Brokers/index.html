The Shadow Brokers

The Shadow Brokers (TSB) is a hacker group that first appeared in August 2016, leaking hacking tools and exploits attributed to the Equation Group, widely believed to be the U.S. National Security Agency's Tailored Access Operations unit.

Leaks

The group attempted to auction "cyber weapons" for bitcoin, then released passwords in April 2017 that unlocked a cache including the EternalBlue SMB exploit. EternalBlue was soon used to spread the WannaCry ransomware (May 2017) and the NotPetya attack (June 2017), causing billions of dollars of damage worldwide.

Identity

The group's identity remains unknown; speculation has included foreign intelligence services and NSA insiders. The leaks triggered a major debate over the U.S. government's Vulnerabilities Equities Process and stockpiling of exploits. See also The Rumble in the Jungle.
