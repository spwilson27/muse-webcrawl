Sasol

Sasol Limited is a South African integrated energy and chemicals company, founded in 1950 as the South African Coal, Oil and Gas Corporation. Its name derives from the Afrikaans Suid-Afrikaanse Steenkool-, Olie- en Gas Korporasie. Sasol is world-renowned for commercializing the Fischer–Tropsch process to convert coal and natural gas into liquid fuels and chemicals.

Operations

The company's giant Secunda complex in Mpumalanga is the world's largest coal-to-liquids facility and one of the largest single-point sources of greenhouse gas emissions on Earth. Its original plant at Sasolburg dates to 1955. Sasol also operates gas-to-liquids plants in Qatar (Oryx GTL) and has chemical operations in the United States, including the Lake Charles Chemicals Project in Louisiana, as well as businesses in mining, solvents, polymers, and waxes.

History

Sasol was created as a state enterprise to reduce South Africa's dependence on imported oil — a goal that became critical under apartheid-era sanctions. It was listed on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange in 1979 and on the New York Stock Exchange in 2003. In recent years the company has faced pressure over its emissions and has announced transition plans involving green hydrogen and sustainable feedstocks.
