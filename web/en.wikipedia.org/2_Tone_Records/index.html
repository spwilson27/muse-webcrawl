2 Tone Records

2 Tone Records was an English record label that released popular ska and reggae-influenced music in the late 1970s and early 1980s. Founded in Coventry in 1979 by Jerry Dammers of the Specials, the label gave its name to the 2 Tone genre, a fusion of Jamaican ska and rocksteady with punk rock and new wave.

History

Dammers set up the label with support from Chrysalis Records. Its first release, a split single by the Specials and the Selecter, reached the UK charts in 1979. The label went on to release records by Madness (its first album only), the Beat, the Selecter, the Bodysnatchers and others.

Style and imagery

2 Tone was known for its black-and-white chequerboard artwork and the "Walt Jabsco" logo, a figure based on a photograph of reggae musician Peter Tosh. The label's multiracial bands and message of unity stood out amid the social tensions of late-1970s Britain.

Legacy

Although the label effectively wound down by the mid-1980s, 2 Tone reshaped British pop music and influenced later ska revivals worldwide. Its story is commemorated by the 2-Tone Village in Coventry.
