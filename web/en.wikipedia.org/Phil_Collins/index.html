Phil Collins

Phil Collins performing; he is among the world's best-selling music artists.

Philip David Charles Collins (born 30 January 1951) is an English musician, singer, songwriter, drummer, record producer, and actor. He was the drummer and later lead singer of the rock band Genesis and is also a successful solo artist. Between 1982 and 1990, Collins achieved more top-40 singles on the US Billboard Hot 100 as a solo artist, with Genesis, and with other collaborations than any other artist.

Career

Collins began as a child actor before taking up the drums. He joined Genesis in 1970 as drummer and, following Peter Gabriel's departure in 1975, became the band's lead vocalist on the album A Trick of the Tail (1976). With Collins fronting a trio completed by Tony Banks and Mike Rutherford, Genesis achieved major commercial success with albums such as Duke (1980), Abacab (1981), Genesis (1983), and Invisible Touch (1986).

His parallel solo career produced a series of international hits including "In the Air Tonight" (whose gated-reverb drum sound became hugely influential), "Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)", "Sussudio", "One More Night", "A Groovy Kind of Love", "Two Hearts", and "Another Day in Paradise". His solo albums include Face Value (1981), No Jacket Required (1985), and ...But Seriously (1989). Collins left Genesis in 1996, reunited with the band for the 2007 Turn It On Again Tour, announced retirement in 2011, and returned with the solo Not Dead Yet Tour in 2017. Genesis's final concerts, on The Last Domino? Tour, concluded in March 2022, with Collins performing seated due to long-standing health issues with his back and hands.

Recognition

Collins is one of only three artists (with Paul McCartney and Michael Jackson) to have sold over 100 million records both as a solo artist and as a member of a band. His awards include eight Grammy Awards, six Brit Awards, two Golden Globe Awards, and an Academy Award for Best Original Song for "You'll Be in My Heart" from Disney's Tarzan (1999). He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Genesis in 2010.
