Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street is a 1979 musical with music and lyrics by Stephen Sondheim and a book by Hugh Wheeler, adapted from Christopher Bond's 1970 play. It tells of a Victorian barber who murders his customers and, with his accomplice Mrs. Lovett, disposes of the bodies in meat pies.

Plot

Barber Benjamin Barker returns to London after unjust transportation to Australia by Judge Turpin, who coveted Barker's wife. Renaming himself Sweeney Todd, he seeks revenge, descending into indiscriminate killing, while Mrs. Lovett's pie shop thrives on the proceeds. The story ends in tragedy as Todd unknowingly kills his own wife and is killed in turn.

Reception

The original Broadway production won eight Tony Awards including Best Musical. Often described as a "musical thriller", it contains songs such as "The Worst Pies in London", "Pretty Women" and "A Little Priest". Tim Burton's film adaptation appeared in 2007.
