Falls in older adults

Falls in older adults are a major cause of injury, disability, and death among people aged 65 and over. About one in three older adults living in the community falls each year, and falls are the leading cause of fatal and non-fatal injuries in this age group, including hip fractures and traumatic brain injuries.

Risk factors

Risk factors include muscle weakness, gait and balance problems, vision impairment, vitamin D deficiency, medications such as psychoactive drugs, arthritis, depression, and environmental hazards in the home. A history of a previous fall is among the strongest predictors of a future fall.

Prevention

Evidence-based prevention includes strength and balance exercise programmes, review and reduction of fall-risk-increasing medications, vision correction, vitamin D supplementation in deficient individuals, and home safety assessment and modification. Fear of falling can itself lead to reduced activity and further loss of strength.
