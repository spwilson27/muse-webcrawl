Group GT4

Group GT4 is a category of grand touring racing cars maintained by the SRO Motorsports Group. Introduced in the late 2000s, GT4 sits below the GT3 category in the SRO's performance pyramid and is aimed at amateur and semi-professional drivers, using production-based sports cars with only limited modifications permitted compared with their road-going counterparts.

Regulations

GT4 cars are based closely on production vehicles. Aerodynamic aids are minimal, engines remain close to standard specification, and costs are controlled through homologation and a Balance of Performance (BoP) process that adjusts weight, power and ride height to equalise competing models. Typical eligible cars have included the Porsche Cayman GT4 Clubsport, McLaren 570S GT4, Audi R8 LMS GT4, BMW M4 GT4, Mercedes-AMG GT4, Aston Martin Vantage AMR GT4, Alpine A110 GT4 and Toyota GR Supra GT4.

Series

The class is used in numerous championships worldwide, including the GT4 European Series, the British GT Championship, GT4 America Series, the FFSA GT Championship in France, and as a class within multi-category events such as the Bathurst 12 Hour. The standalone European GT4 Cup began in 2007 and evolved into the GT4 European Series.
