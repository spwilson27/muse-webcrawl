Mensa Select

The Mensa Select award is given annually by American Mensa, the United States branch of Mensa International, to board games that are judged by Mensa members at the Mind Games event. The winners are the top five games of the competition, and the award recognizes games that are original, creative, challenging, and well designed.

History

Mind Games has been held annually since 1990. Mensa members gather over a weekend to play and rate dozens of newly released games, scoring them on aesthetics, instructions, originality, play appeal, and play value. The five highest-rated games receive the Mensa Select seal.

Notable winners

Past winners have included Set, Qwirkle, Forbidden Island, Dominion, and Ticket to Ride. The Mensa Select seal is frequently displayed on game packaging as an endorsement of the game's quality and intellectual challenge.
