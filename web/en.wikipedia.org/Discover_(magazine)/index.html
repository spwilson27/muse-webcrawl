Discover (magazine)

Discover is an American general audience science magazine launched in October 1980 by Time Inc. It was founded amid a surge of public interest in science, in the same period that saw the launch of the television series Cosmos.

History

The magazine was created to present science news and research in an accessible style for educated lay readers. Its first editor was Leon Jaroff, formerly of Time. Over the decades it passed through several owners, including the Walt Disney Company and Bob Guccione Jr., before being acquired by Kalmbach Publishing in 2010. Kalmbach later moved the magazine's offices to Wisconsin, alongside its other science title Astronomy.

Content

Discover covers topics across the sciences, including physics, astronomy, biology, medicine, neuroscience, environment and technology. It is known for features such as its annual list of the top science stories of the year and for columns on health, the brain and the environment. Longtime contributors have included science writers covering topics from cosmology to evolution.
