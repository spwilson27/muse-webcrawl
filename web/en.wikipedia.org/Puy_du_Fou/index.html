Puy du Fou

Puy du Fou is a historical theme park located in Les Epesses in the Vendée department of western France. Rather than rides, the park is built around large-scale live spectacle shows re-creating episodes from French history, from the Roman era to the twentieth century, performed in purpose-built arenas and reconstructed period villages. With more than two million visitors a year, it is one of France's most visited theme parks, and its night-time show, the Cinéscénie, performed by thousands of local volunteers, is among the largest in the world.

History

The park traces its origin to 1978, when Philippe de Villiers created the Cinéscénie, a night-time sound-and-light spectacle staged around the ruins of the Puy du Fou castle with volunteer performers from local communities. The daytime park, the Grand Parc, opened in 1989 and grew steadily, adding major shows such as the Viking attack spectacle, the Roman stadium games, the birds of prey show Le Bal des Oiseaux Fantômes, the Richelieu musketeer production Mousquetaire de Richelieu, and the immersive WWI show Les Amoureux de Verdun. A Spanish sister park, Puy du Fou España, opened near Toledo in 2019, followed by a full park in 2021.

Shows and attractions

Major productions include Le Signe du Triomphe (Roman circus games), Les Vikings, Le Secret de la Lance (medieval), Les Chevaliers de la Table Ronde, Le Dernier Panache (Vendée wars), and the naval spectacle Le Mystère de La Pérouse. The park also contains four period villages, themed hotels, and nature areas. Its shows have won international industry recognition, including the Thea Award for outstanding achievement, and Puy du Fou was the first French park named the world's best park in the Applause Award voting by industry professionals in 2012.

Organisation

The park is operated commercially while the Cinéscénie is produced by an association of volunteers — some 4,000 people take part across the season. This volunteer model, rooted in the local identity of the Vendée, is frequently cited as central to the park's character and economics.
