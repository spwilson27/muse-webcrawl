Dudley Do-Right

Dudley Do-Right is a fictional Canadian Mountie cartoon character created by Jay Ward, Alex Anderson, and Chris Hayward. He first appeared in 1961 as a segment of The Rocky and Bullwinkle Show, parodying early 20th-century melodrama and silent-film serials.

Characters and format

Dudley is a cheerful but dim-witted officer of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police who invariably foils the schemes of the villainous Snidely Whiplash, usually rescuing Nell Fenwick, the daughter of his superior Inspector Fenwick, from being tied to railway tracks. His trusty Horse is often smarter than he is. Episodes are narrated in breathless melodrama style.

Later appearances

The character was revived in The Dudley Do-Right Show and a 1999 live-action film starring Brendan Fraser.
