Skolt Sámi

Skolt Sámi (sääʹmǩiõll) is a Sámi language spoken by the Skolts, an indigenous Sámi people. It is spoken in the northeastern part of Finland, mainly in Sevettijärvi and along the Näätämö river, and in the villages of Nellim and Keväjärvi in the municipality of Inari, as well as by some speakers in Russia.

The language has roughly 300 speakers in Finland and is classified as severely endangered. It uses a Latin-based alphabet with additional characters such as ǩ, ǧ, and ʒ to represent palatalized and affricate sounds.

Skolt Sámi differs significantly from the more widely spoken Northern Sámi, with complex consonant gradation and vowel changes. Revitalization efforts include language nests (immersion preschools) and teaching in schools.
