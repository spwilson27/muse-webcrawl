Yuji Horii

Image placeholder: Yuji Horii at a game awards event

Yuji Horii (堀井 雄二, Horii Yūji, born January 6, 1954) is a Japanese video game designer and scenario writer best known as the creator of the Dragon Quest series of role-playing games. He also supervised and wrote the scenario for Chrono Trigger.

Career

Horii began as a freelance writer and won Enix's 1982 Game Hobby Program Contest with a tennis game. Inspired by Wizardry and Ultima, he designed Dragon Quest (1986) with character designs by Akira Toriyama and music by Koichi Sugiyama, establishing conventions of the console RPG genre in Japan. He founded the studio Armor Project, which manages the series jointly with Square Enix.

Works

His credits include the Dragon Quest mainline series, the adventure game The Portopia Serial Murder Case (1983), and Chrono Trigger (1995).
