Leon County, Florida

Leon County is a county in the Florida Panhandle, named for the Spanish explorer Juan Ponce de León. Its seat and largest city is Tallahassee, the state capital, and it is home to Florida State University and Florida A and M University.

History

Created in 1824, Leon County was antebellum Florida richest cotton county, worked by enslaved labour on plantations whose houses still ring the city. Tallahassee was chosen as territorial capital as a midpoint between Pensacola and St. Augustine.

Geography

Unlike most of peninsular Florida, the county has red clay hills, canopy oak roads and rolling terrain. Lake Jackson and Lake Lafayette are notable sinkhole lakes that periodically drain into the Floridan aquifer.
