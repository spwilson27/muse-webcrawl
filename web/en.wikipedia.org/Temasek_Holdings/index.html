Temasek Holdings (Private) Limited, often simply Temasek, is a Singaporean state-owned multinational investment firm. Incorporated on 25 June 1974, Temasek has a multinational staff of more than 800 people, and offices across the Americas, Asia, and Europe. It is wholly owned by the Singapore Minister for Finance, and its portfolio spans financial services, telecommunications, media, transportation, energy, and life sciences.

History

Temasek was established to hold and manage commercially the shares in companies previously held by the Singapore government — including Singapore Airlines, DBS Bank, Singtel, and Keppel Corporation — so that the Ministry of Finance could focus on policy. Its name derives from Temasek, an early name for Singapore recorded in Malay annals.

Governance and portfolio

Temasek operates on commercial principles, pays taxes and dividends, and issues its own bonds with top credit ratings. Its net portfolio value has grown from S$354 million at inception to several hundred billion Singapore dollars. Notable long-held stakes include Singtel, DBS, Singapore Airlines, CapitaLand, and Standard Chartered.
