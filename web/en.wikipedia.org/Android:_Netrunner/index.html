Android: Netrunner is an asymmetric two-player Living Card Game designed by Richard Garfield and published by Fantasy Flight Games from 2012 to 2018. Set in the dystopian Android universe, it pits a megacorporation (the Corp) against a hacker (the Runner) in a contest of hidden information, bluffing and resource management.

Gameplay

The Corp installs cards face-down in servers protected by "ice" (intrusion countermeasures), advancing agendas to score them. The Runner builds a rig of programs, hardware and resources and makes "runs" on the Corp's servers, breaking ice with icebreakers and attempting to access and steal agendas. The first player to score seven agenda points wins; the Corp also wins if the Runner is "flatlined" by taking too much damage.

History

The game revived Garfield's 1996 collectible card game Netrunner, re-set in Fantasy Flight's Android setting and distributed in fixed, non-randomized expansions. It developed a strong competitive scene, but production ended in 2018 when the license from Wizards of the Coast was not renewed. A fan organization, Null Signal Games (formerly NISEI), has continued producing compatible cards and organizing play.
