The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker is an action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the GameCube. It is the tenth installment in The Legend of Zelda series and is set on a group of islands in a vast ocean known as the Great Sea.

Gameplay

Players control Link as he sails between islands aboard a talking boat, the King of Red Lions, using a magical baton called the Wind Waker to control the wind. The game is notable for its cel-shaded, cartoon-like visual style, sometimes called "toon shading".

Plot

Link sets out to rescue his sister Aryll from the Forsaken Fortress and becomes entangled in a struggle involving the pirate captain Tetra and the return of Ganondorf, in a world that lies above the flooded kingdom of Hyrule.

Legacy

An HD remaster, The Wind Waker HD, was released for the Wii U in 2013. The game received two direct sequels on the Nintendo DS: Phantom Hourglass and Spirit Tracks.
