The IndyCar Series, currently known as the NTT IndyCar Series for sponsorship reasons, is the highest class of American open-wheel racing and the premier championship of American Championship car racing. Its premier event is the Indianapolis 500, held each Memorial Day weekend at Indianapolis Motor Speedway.

The series was founded in 1994 by Indianapolis Motor Speedway owner Tony George as the Indy Racing League, beginning competition in 1996 as a rival to the established CART championship. The resulting "split" in American open-wheel racing lasted twelve years and damaged both series before reunification in 2008 under the IndyCar banner.

History

American championship racing traces its lineage to AAA-sanctioned events from 1905, continuing under USAC and then CART (from 1979). The Indy Racing League emphasised oval tracks and lower costs; CART (later Champ Car) emphasised road and street courses. After Champ Car's bankruptcy in 2008, its teams joined the IRL, reunifying the sport. The series has since been owned by Penske Entertainment, part of Roger Penske's organisation, which purchased the series and Indianapolis Motor Speedway in 2019.

Cars

Since 2012 all teams have used the Dallara DW12 chassis, named for the late Dan Wheldon, fitted since 2018 with a universal aero kit. Twin-turbocharged 2.2-litre V6 engines from Chevrolet and Honda produce roughly 550–700 horsepower depending on the circuit, with a hybrid power unit introduced in 2024. Cars reach speeds above 230 mph (370 km/h) at Indianapolis, making IndyCar among the fastest circuit racing series in the world.

Calendar and drivers

The season of about 17 races mixes superspeedways, short ovals, permanent road courses, and temporary street circuits such as Long Beach and St. Petersburg. Notable tracks include Indianapolis, Laguna Seca, Road America, and the Streets of Toronto. Champions have included Scott Dixon (six titles), Josef Newgarden, Will Power, and Álex Palou, while Indianapolis 500 winners such as Helio Castroneves (four victories) are among the sport's best-known figures.
