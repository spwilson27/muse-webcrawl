Jerry R. Ehman

Jerry R. Ehman

Born: 1939, United States

Alma mater: University of Michigan

Fields: Radio astronomy

Known for: Discovering the Wow! signal (1977)

Jerry R. Ehman is an American astronomer and former professor who detected the famous "Wow! signal" in 1977 while reviewing data collected by Ohio State University's Big Ear radio telescope as part of a SETI (search for extraterrestrial intelligence) project.

The Wow! signal

On 15 August 1977, the Big Ear recorded a strong, narrowband radio signal from the direction of the constellation Sagittarius, bearing the expected hallmarks of an artificial extraterrestrial transmission. Reviewing the computer printout days later, Ehman circled the intensity sequence "6EQUJ5" — a 72-second burst peaking far above background noise — and wrote "Wow!" in the margin, giving the event its name. Despite repeated follow-up observations, the signal never recurred and its source remains unexplained.

Career

Ehman taught astronomy at Ohio State University and worked on the SETI programme at Big Ear until the telescope's closure in 1998. He has written about the signal and its investigation, maintaining scientific caution about its origin while noting that no terrestrial explanation has fully accounted for it.
