Avengers: Infinity War

Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo
Produced by Kevin Feige
Release date April 27, 2018
Running time 149 minutes
Budget $325–400 million
Box office $2.052 billion

Avengers: Infinity War is a 2018 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team the Avengers. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures, it is the sequel to The Avengers (2012) and Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), and the 19th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).

Plot

Having acquired the Power Stone from Xandar, Thanos and his lieutenants intercept the spaceship carrying the survivors of Asgard's destruction. After defeating Thor and subduing the Hulk, Thanos extracts the Space Stone from the Tesseract, kills Loki, and flees. The Avengers and the Guardians of the Galaxy respond in separate groups, racing to keep the remaining Infinity Stones from Thanos, who intends to use them to wipe out half of all life in the universe. Despite their efforts, Thanos collects all six stones, snaps his fingers, and half of all life disintegrates.

Cast

The ensemble cast includes Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark / Iron Man, Chris Hemsworth as Thor, Mark Ruffalo as Bruce Banner / Hulk, Chris Evans as Steve Rogers / Captain America, Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow, Benedict Cumberbatch as Doctor Strange, Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man, Chadwick Boseman as T'Challa / Black Panther, and Josh Brolin as Thanos.

Production

The film was announced in October 2014 as Avengers: Infinity War – Part 1. The Russo brothers came on board to direct in April 2015, and Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely signed on to write the screenplay. Filming took place back-to-back with its sequel, Avengers: Endgame, beginning in January 2017 at Pinewood Atlanta Studios.

Reception

The film received praise for Brolin's performance, the visual effects, action sequences, and emotional weight, although the runtime was criticized. It grossed over $2 billion worldwide, becoming the fourth-highest-grossing film of all time at the time of its release.
