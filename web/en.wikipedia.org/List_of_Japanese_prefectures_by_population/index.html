List of Japanese prefectures by population

Japan is divided into 47 prefectures. This article ranks them by population according to census figures.

Largest prefectures

Tokyo Metropolis is by far the most populous prefecture, with roughly 14 million inhabitants, followed by Kanagawa (about 9.2 million), Osaka (about 8.8 million), Aichi (about 7.5 million) and Saitama (about 7.3 million). Together the Greater Tokyo Area prefectures — Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama and Chiba — hold over 36 million people.

Smallest prefectures

The least populous prefectures include Tottori (the smallest, around 550,000), Shimane, Kochi, Yamanashi and Fukui, reflecting long-term rural depopulation and ageing in Japan.
