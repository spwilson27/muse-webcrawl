List of Swedish Navy ships

This is a list of ships of the Swedish Navy, past and present. Swedish warships carry the prefix HMS (Hans/Hennes Majestäts Skepp — His/Her Majesty's Ship).

Modern fleet

The modern fleet centres on stealth corvettes of the Visby class and Göteborg class, submarines of the Gotland and Södermanland classes (with the Blekinge class under construction), mine countermeasure vessels of the Koster class, and patrol and support ships.

Historical ships

Historic vessels include the warship Vasa, which sank on her maiden voyage in 1628 and is now a museum ship in Stockholm, the armoured ships (pansarskepp) of the coastal defence fleet, and the destroyers and cruisers, such as HSwMS Göta Lejon, of the mid-20th century.
