Diocese of Norwich

The Diocese of Norwich is a Church of England diocese covering most of the county of Norfolk and part of Suffolk in eastern England. Its seat is Norwich Cathedral, and its bishop is the Bishop of Norwich.

History

The see traces its origins to the Anglo-Saxon diocese of the East Angles, founded around 630 at Dunwich and later seated at Elmham and Thetford. Bishop Herbert de Losinga moved the see to Norwich in 1094 and began building the cathedral the following year.

Organisation

The diocese is divided into archdeaconries (Norwich, Norfolk and Lynn) and contains several hundred parishes. Suffragan bishops of Thetford and Lynn assist the diocesan bishop. The diocese is part of the Province of Canterbury.
