Larry Page

Lawrence Edward Page (born March 26, 1973) is an American computer scientist and businessman who co-founded Google with Sergey Brin while a PhD student at Stanford University. With Brin he invented PageRank, the link-analysis algorithm at the heart of Google's search engine.

Career

Page was Google's first CEO, serving until 2001 and again from 2011 to 2015, when he became CEO of parent company Alphabet Inc.; he stepped down in 2019 but remains a board member and controlling shareholder. He has invested in flying-car and space ventures including Kitty Hawk and Planetary Resources.

Early life

Born in East Lansing, Michigan, to computer science professors, Page studied computer engineering at the University of Michigan before Stanford.
