São Paulo Grand Prix

The São Paulo Grand Prix (Portuguese: Grande Prêmio de São Paulo) is a Formula One motor racing event held at the Autódromo José Carlos Pace, better known as Interlagos, in São Paulo, Brazil. The race was previously run under the name Brazilian Grand Prix; it adopted the São Paulo name in 2021 after a new promotional agreement.

History

The Brazilian Grand Prix was first held at Interlagos in 1972 (non-championship) and joined the World Championship in 1973. From 1978 to 1989 it was mostly staged at Jacarepaguá in Rio de Janeiro before returning permanently to a shortened Interlagos in 1990. The circuit, built on hilly ground between two reservoirs, is noted for its anti-clockwise layout, elevation changes, and unpredictable weather.

Memorable races

Interlagos has hosted many title deciders, including Ayrton Senna's emotional home wins in 1991 and 1993, Lewis Hamilton's last-lap championship victory in 2008, and Sebastian Vettel's comeback title in 2012. Since the rebranding, the event has featured Formula One's sprint format on several occasions, and Max Verstappen's 2024 win from 17th on the grid in the wet is widely regarded as one of the great drives.
