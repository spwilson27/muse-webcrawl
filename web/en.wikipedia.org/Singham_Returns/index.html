Singham Returns is a 2014 Indian Hindi-language action film written and directed by Rohit Shetty and produced by Ajay Devgn Films. A sequel to the 2011 film Singham — itself a remake of the Tamil film Singam — it stars Ajay Devgn reprising his role as Deputy Commissioner of Police Bajirao Singham, alongside Kareena Kapoor Khan.

Plot

Now posted in Mumbai, Singham investigates the death of one of his officers, found with bags of unaccounted cash, and uncovers a nexus between a corrupt godman, Satyaraj "Babaji" Chander, and politicians controlling black money. Public outrage and political pressure mount as Singham fights to clear his team's name and expose the network.

Reception

The film opened on Indian Independence Day 2014 and was a major commercial success, becoming one of the highest-grossing Hindi films of the year. It forms part of Shetty's "Cop Universe", continued in crossovers such as Simmba (2018), Sooryavanshi (2021) and Singham Again (2024).
