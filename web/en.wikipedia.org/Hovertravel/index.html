Hovertravel

Hovertravel is a ferry company operating hovercraft services across the Solent between Southsea in Portsmouth, England, and Ryde on the Isle of Wight. Founded in 1965, it is the world's longest-running commercial hovercraft operator and the last remaining scheduled passenger hovercraft service in Britain.

Services

The crossing takes about ten minutes, making it one of the fastest routes to the Isle of Wight, and connects with Ryde Esplanade railway station. Hovertravel operates a fleet of twin-engine hovercraft; in 2016 it introduced two 12000TD craft, Solent Express and Island Flyer, built by Griffon Hoverwork.

History

The company began operations in 1965 using SR.N6 hovercraft developed by Saunders-Roe, and at its peak also ran services elsewhere on the south coast. While larger hovercraft ferry routes such as the cross-Channel services ended in 2000, the short Solent route proved durable, and Hovertravel has also operated charter and rescue-support work.
