Bad Brains

Bad Brains are an American rock band formed in Washington, D.C., in 1977. They are widely regarded as pioneers of hardcore punk, famed for playing extremely fast, virtuosic punk while also performing reggae songs — a fusion reflecting their Rastafari faith.

History

The classic line-up of vocalist H.R., guitarist Dr. Know, bassist Darryl Jenifer and drummer Earl Hudson began as a jazz-fusion group called Mind Power before converting to punk. Their 1982 self-titled cassette on ROIR Records, and songs like "Banned in D.C.", "Sailin' On" and "Pay to Cum", defined early hardcore.

Style and influence

Bad Brains mixed breakneck hardcore with dub and reggae interludes and lyrics promoting "PMA" (positive mental attitude). Despite an erratic career marked by break-ups and H.R.'s unpredictability, albums such as I Against I (1986) influenced generations of punk, metal and alternative bands, from Minor Threat to the Beastie Boys.

Legacy

The band was nominated for the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame and remains a touchstone of the Washington, D.C. hardcore scene. See also Washington, D.C. hardcore.
