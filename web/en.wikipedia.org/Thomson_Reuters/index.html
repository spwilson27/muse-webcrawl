Thomson Reuters Corporation is a Canadian multinational information conglomerate. The company was founded in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, and is headquartered there; it was created by the Thomson Corporation's purchase of the British company Reuters Group on April 17, 2008, and is majority-owned by the Woodbridge Company, the investment firm of the Thomson family.

Business

Thomson Reuters provides information, software, and data analytics for professionals in the legal, tax and accounting, and media industries. Its principal offerings include the Westlaw legal research service, Practical Law, ONESOURCE and Checkpoint tax products, and the Reuters news agency, one of the world's largest international multimedia news providers. The company's professional-information business grew out of the Thomson family's Canadian newspaper and publishing empire built by Roy Thomson, 1st Baron Thomson of Fleet.

History

The Reuters side of the business traces its origins to Julius Reuter's news agency, founded in London in 1851, which pioneered the use of the telegraph for transmitting news and stock prices. After the 2008 merger, Thomson Reuters sold a majority stake in its Financial & Risk unit (renamed Refinitiv) to Blackstone in 2018; Refinitiv was subsequently acquired by the London Stock Exchange Group in 2021, leaving Thomson Reuters concentrated on legal, tax, and news.
