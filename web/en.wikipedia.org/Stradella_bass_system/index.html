Stradella bass system

Used onPiano accordion (left hand)
RowsSix: counterbass, fundamental bass, major, minor, dominant 7th, diminished 7th
SizesCommonly 12 to 120 bass buttons

The Stradella bass system is the standard arrangement of left-hand buttons on a piano accordion, named after the Italian town of Stradella, a centre of accordion manufacture. It provides bass notes and preset chords so the left hand can supply a complete harmonic accompaniment.

Layout

The buttons are arranged in six rows. The fundamental bass row is organised in a circle of fifths, with the C button marked by a dimple or groove; adjacent rows give the major third above each bass note (counterbass), then major, minor, dominant seventh and diminished seventh chords built on each root. Full-size instruments have 120 bass buttons spanning twenty roots.
