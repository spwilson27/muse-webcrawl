The Imperium of Man is a fictional human empire in the Warhammer 40,000 tabletop game universe created by Games Workshop. It is a vast, authoritarian, and technologically stagnant galactic empire spanning over a million worlds, ruled in the name of the immortal Emperor of Mankind.

Setting

Founded ten thousand years before the game's "present" during the Great Crusade, the Imperium fell into decay after the civil war known as the Horus Heresy. The Emperor, mortally wounded, is sustained on the Golden Throne on Terra, while the empire is administered by the Adeptus Terra and defended by the Imperial Guard, the Space Marines, and other military orders.

Themes

The Imperium is a central element of the setting's grimdark tone, depicting a dystopian theocracy in perpetual war against alien species and the forces of Chaos.
