Holika

Holika is a demoness in Hindu mythology, sister of the demon king Hiranyakashipu and aunt of Prahlada. Her attempted killing of Prahlada and her own death by fire are commemorated on the eve of the festival of Holi.

Legend

According to the Puranas, Holika possessed a boon or cloak that made her immune to fire. At her brother's command she sat in a pyre with young Prahlada, a devotee of Vishnu, on her lap, intending to burn him. By divine intervention the protection passed to Prahlada, Holika burned to death, and Prahlada emerged unharmed.

Holika Dahan

On the night before Holi, bonfires called Holika Dahan are lit in many parts of India to symbolize the victory of good over evil and the burning away of negativity before spring.
