Neonatology

Neonatology is a subspecialty of pediatrics that consists of the medical care of newborn infants, especially ill or premature newborns. It is a hospital-based specialty, practised principally in neonatal intensive care units (NICUs).

Scope

Neonatologists manage premature birth, low birth weight, intrauterine growth restriction, congenital malformations, sepsis, pulmonary hypoplasia, and birth asphyxia. Care may include mechanical ventilation, parenteral nutrition, and therapeutic hypothermia.

History

The field developed in the mid-20th century with advances in infant incubators, respiratory support, and parenteral feeding, dramatically improving survival of very premature infants.
