Toner

Toner is a powder mixture used in laser printers and photocopiers to form printed text and images on paper. The powder is transferred to the page by an electrostatically charged drum and then fused onto the paper by heated rollers.

Composition and process

Early toner was little more than carbon powder; modern toner consists of finely ground polymer resin with colourants, charge-control agents and additives, in particles roughly 5–10 micrometres across. In the xerographic process the toner adheres to a latent electrostatic image, is transferred to paper, and is fixed by heat and pressure in the fuser unit.

Cartridges and handling

Toner is supplied in cartridges, which may be refilled or remanufactured. Though generally of low toxicity, fine toner dust can irritate the respiratory system, and spills are best cleaned with a vacuum rated for fine particles rather than swept. The word "toner" is also used for astringent skincare products, an unrelated sense.
