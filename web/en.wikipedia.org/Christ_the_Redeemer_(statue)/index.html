Christ the Redeemer (statue)

Christ the Redeemer (Cristo Redentor) is an Art Deco statue of Jesus Christ atop Corcovado mountain in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, overlooking the city from 700 metres above sea level.

Construction

Designed by Brazilian engineer Heitor da Silva Costa with French sculptor Paul Landowski, the statue was built in France from reinforced concrete clad in soapstone tiles and assembled in Brazil, opening on 12 October 1931. It stands 30 metres tall, 38 metres with its pedestal, with arms stretching 28 metres wide.

Symbol

One of the New7Wonders of the World, it is a symbol of Rio and of Brazilian Christianity, maintained against lightning strikes and weathering by regular restoration.
