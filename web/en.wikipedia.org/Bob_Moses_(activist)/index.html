Bob Moses (activist)

Robert Parris Moses (23 January 1935 – 25 July 2021) was an American educator and civil rights activist. A key figure in the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee (SNCC), he led voter registration efforts in Mississippi during the 1960s and was a central organiser of the 1964 Freedom Summer.

Later work

After earning a doctorate in philosophy from Harvard, Moses founded the Algebra Project in 1982, a programme promoting mathematics literacy as a civil rights issue for underserved students. He received a MacArthur "Genius" Fellowship in 1982.

Philosophy

Moses advocated grassroots organising and shared decision-making, famously describing leadership as working so communities could speak and act for themselves.
