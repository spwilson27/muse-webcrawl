Guitarrón

The guitarrón is a very large, deep-bodied Mexican six-string acoustic bass guitar, the bass instrument of the mariachi ensemble. Its name is an augmentative of guitarra, "large guitar".

Description

The guitarrón is about the size of a cello, with a short, fretless neck and an exceptionally deep body (up to about 45 cm) whose back is strongly convex. It has six thick strings, usually nylon or gut with the lower strings metal-wound, tuned A–D–G–C–E–A. Because of its huge resonating body it needs no amplification to balance trumpets, violins and guitars in a mariachi band.

Playing technique

The instrument is held upright-ish against the body and played with a powerful plucking technique, often sounding two strings in octaves to reinforce the bass line. The player supplies the rhythmic and harmonic foundation of rancheras, sones and other mariachi repertoire, working with the smaller five-string vihuela and the standard guitar to form the ensemble's rhythm section, the armonía.
