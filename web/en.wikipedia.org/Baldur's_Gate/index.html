Baldur's Gate is a role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published in 1998 by Interplay Entertainment. It is set in the Forgotten Realms campaign setting of Dungeons & Dragons, on the Sword Coast of Faerûn.

Gameplay

The game uses real-time-with-pause combat based on the Advanced Dungeons & Dragons 2nd edition rules. The player creates a protagonist, the ward of the sage Gorion at Candlekeep, and recruits a party of up to five companions, including the ranger Minsc and his hamster Boo.

Plot

After an assassination attempt, the protagonist flees Candlekeep and uncovers a conspiracy behind an iron shortage afflicting the Sword Coast, eventually confronting Sarevok, a fellow offspring of Bhaal, the dead god of murder.

Reception and legacy

Baldur's Gate was a critical and commercial success, credited with revitalizing the computer role-playing game genre. It spawned the expansion Tales of the Sword Coast (1999) and a sequel, Baldur's Gate II: Shadows of Amn (2000). An Enhanced Edition appeared in 2012, and Larian Studios' Baldur's Gate 3 followed in 2023.
