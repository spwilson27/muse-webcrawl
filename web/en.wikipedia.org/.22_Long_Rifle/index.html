The .22 Long Rifle or simply .22 LR ( metric designation 5.6×15 mmR) is a long-established variety of .22 caliber rimfire ammunition originating in the United States. It is by far the most common ammunition in the world by units sold.

History

Introduced in 1887 by the J. Stevens Arms & Tool Company, the cartridge combined the case of the .22 Long with the bullet of the .22 Extra Long. Its low cost, low recoil and low noise made it the standard round for target shooting, training and small-game hunting.

Use

The .22 LR is used in rifles, pistols and revolvers, and in Olympic and other target-shooting disciplines. Variants include subsonic, standard, high-velocity and hyper-velocity loadings, and shot shells.
