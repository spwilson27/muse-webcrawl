The San Francisco Giants are an American professional baseball team based in San Francisco, California. They compete in Major League Baseball as a member of the National League West Division. Founded in 1883 as the New York Gothams and long known as the New York Giants, the franchise moved to San Francisco in 1958.

History

The New York Giants won five World Series and featured players such as Christy Mathewson, Mel Ott and Willie Mays. After moving west, the team played at Candlestick Park from 1960 until 1999, then moved to its waterfront ballpark, now Oracle Park. Managed by Bruce Bochy, the Giants won three World Series in five seasons — 2010, 2012 and 2014 — ending a 56-year championship drought.

Rivalries

The Giants' rivalry with the Los Angeles Dodgers, dating to their shared New York origins, is among the oldest and fiercest in baseball. Barry Bonds, who holds the all-time home run records, spent his final 15 seasons with San Francisco.
