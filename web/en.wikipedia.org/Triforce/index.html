Triforce

SeriesThe Legend of Zelda
First appearanceThe Legend of Zelda (1986)
CreatorShigeru Miyamoto

The Triforce (Japanese: トライフォース) is a fictional sacred relic from Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda series of video games. It consists of three golden triangles, each conferring one of three virtues: the Triforce of Power, the Triforce of Wisdom, and the Triforce of Courage. Left by the three goddesses who created the world of Hyrule, the Triforce is said to grant any wish to the one who touches it, provided that person's heart balances all three virtues; otherwise it splits into its three pieces, which come to rest in the individuals who most embody each virtue.

Pieces and bearers

In most games the pieces are associated with the series' three central figures: the Triforce of Power with the villain Ganon, the Triforce of Wisdom with Princess Zelda, and the Triforce of Courage with the hero Link. The struggle to assemble or protect the Triforce drives the plot of many entries, beginning with the original The Legend of Zelda (1986) and Zelda II: The Adventure of Link (1987), and developed most fully in Ocarina of Time (1998).

Design and legacy

The triangular emblem resembles the crest of the Japanese Hōjō clan (three triangles in a triangular arrangement). The Triforce has become one of the most recognizable symbols in video gaming, appearing on merchandise and in other media.
