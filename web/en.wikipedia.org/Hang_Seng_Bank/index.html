Hang Seng Bank Limited is a Hong Kong-based banking and financial services company, and one of the largest banks in Hong Kong. It is a majority-owned subsidiary of HSBC Holdings. The bank lends its name to the Hang Seng Index, one of the principal stock market indices of Hong Kong.

History

Hang Seng was founded in 1933 in Hong Kong as a money-changing and gold-trading business. It weathered the turmoil of the Second World War and grew into a full-service bank. During a bank run in 1965, HSBC acquired a controlling 51 percent stake, rescuing the bank; HSBC's ownership later increased to over 60 percent.

Hang Seng Index

The bank's subsidiary launched the Hang Seng Index on 24 November 1969. The index tracks the largest and most liquid companies listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange and is widely used as a barometer of the Hong Kong market.

Operations

Hang Seng provides retail banking, wealth management, commercial banking, and treasury services through an extensive branch and ATM network in Hong Kong, with a growing presence in mainland China through Hang Seng Bank (China) Limited.
