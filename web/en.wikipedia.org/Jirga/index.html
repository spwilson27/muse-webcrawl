Jirga

A jirga is a traditional assembly of leaders, usually Pashtun tribal elders, that makes decisions by consensus under the Pashtunwali code in Afghanistan and Pakistan.

Jirgas settle disputes, dispense justice, and address matters affecting the whole community. A loya jirga (grand council) has been convened at turning points in Afghan history, including to approve constitutions and select heads of state. Decisions are binding and enforced communally.
