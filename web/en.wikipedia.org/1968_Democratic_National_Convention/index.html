1968 Democratic National Convention

Date26–29 August 1968
VenueInternational Amphitheatre, Chicago, Illinois
NomineesHubert Humphrey / Edmund Muskie

The 1968 Democratic National Convention was held August 26–29 at the International Amphitheatre in Chicago, Illinois, to select the Democratic Party's nominees for president and vice president in the 1968 United States presidential election.

Protests

The convention took place during a year marked by the assassinations of Martin Luther King Jr. and Robert F. Kennedy and deep division over the Vietnam War. Thousands of anti-war demonstrators gathered in Chicago, and violent clashes with police outside the convention hall — later officially described as a "police riot" — were broadcast live on television.

Outcome

Vice President Hubert Humphrey, who had not contested the primaries, won the nomination, defeating anti-war candidates Eugene McCarthy and George McGovern. Humphrey went on to lose the general election to Republican Richard Nixon.
