Object Management Group

Object Management Group (OMG)
Founded: 1989
Type: Standards consortium
Headquarters: Boston, Massachusetts, United States
Focus: Modelling and distributed computing standards

The Object Management Group (OMG) is a computer industry standards consortium. OMG task forces develop enterprise integration standards for a range of technologies. Founded in 1989, OMG is known for creating and maintaining specifications such as CORBA and the Unified Modeling Language (UML).

History

OMG was founded in 1989 by eleven companies, including Hewlett-Packard, IBM, and Sun Microsystems, to promote the object model as the basis for distributed software. Its Common Object Request Broker Architecture (CORBA) specification, first released in 1991, became a leading middleware standard of the 1990s.

Standards

  • UML — Unified Modeling Language, adopted in 1997.
  • BPMN — Business Process Model and Notation.
  • SysML — Systems Modeling Language.
  • DDS — Data Distribution Service for real-time systems.
  • Model Driven Architecture (MDA) — a software design approach based on models.

OMG specifications are developed through a vendor-neutral process and are publicly available; several have become ISO standards.
