Paris Métro Line 15

Line 15
System: Paris Métro / Grand Paris Express
Type: Automated driverless metro (orbital)
Length: ~75 km (full loop)
Opening: Phased, from 2026 (south section)

Paris Métro Line 15 is an automated rapid transit line under construction in the suburbs of Paris, France, as the flagship line of the Grand Paris Express project. When complete it will form a roughly 75-kilometre orbital loop around the capital, the longest metro line in France.

Project

Line 15 is built entirely outside the city of Paris, running through the inner suburbs and linking the three surrounding departments. It is divided into southern, western and eastern sections, with the southern section between Pont de Sèvres and Noisy–Champs the most advanced. Trains will be driverless, running on standard-gauge track in deep tunnels.

Stations

The line will serve 36 stations, providing interchanges with most existing Métro lines, RER lines, tramways and the future Lines 16, 17 and 18 of the Grand Paris Express.
