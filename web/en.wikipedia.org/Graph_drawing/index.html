Graph drawing

Graph drawing is an area of mathematics and computer science combining methods from geometric graph theory and information visualisation to derive two- or three-dimensional depictions of graphs arising in applications such as social networks, software engineering, databases and systems biology.

Drawing conventions include placing vertices as points and edges as straight segments, polylines or orthogonal paths. Quality criteria include minimising edge crossings, symmetry, uniform edge lengths and angular resolution. Standard algorithms include force-directed placement (such as the Fruchterman–Reingold algorithm), layered drawing for directed acyclic graphs (Sugiyama's method), orthogonal layouts and spectral methods. The annual Graph Drawing Symposium has been held since 1992.
