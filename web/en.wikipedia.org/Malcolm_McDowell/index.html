Malcolm McDowell

Malcolm McDowell
Born: 13 June 1943, Horsforth, Yorkshire, England
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 1964–present

Malcolm McDowell (born Malcolm John Taylor) is an English actor known for his roles in provocative and influential films. His breakthrough came with Lindsay Anderson's If.... (1968), playing the rebellious schoolboy Mick Travis.

Career

McDowell achieved international fame as Alex DeLarge in Stanley Kubrick's A Clockwork Orange (1971). He continued the Mick Travis character in O Lucky Man! (1973) and Britannia Hospital (1982), and played the title role in Caligula (1979).

Later work

Later roles include the villain in Star Trek Generations (1994), Dr Loomis in Rob Zombie's Halloween films, and extensive television and voice work, including Entourage, Mozart in the Jungle and animated series. His career spans more than five decades.
