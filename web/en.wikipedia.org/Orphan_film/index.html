Orphan film

An orphan film is a motion picture that has been abandoned by its owner or copyright holder. The term, coined by archivists in the 1990s, encompasses home movies, educational films, industrial films, newsreels, experimental works, and other non-commercial or neglected footage of historical and cultural value.

Preservation

Orphan films are often the only surviving record of everyday life, minority communities, and regional history. Because they lack commercial owners, they are especially vulnerable to decay, particularly works on nitrate and acetate stock.

Orphan Film Symposium

Since 1999, the Orphan Film Symposium, founded by Dan Streible at New York University, has brought together archivists, scholars, and filmmakers to screen and study such works. The U.S. National Film Preservation Foundation has supported the rescue of many orphan films.
