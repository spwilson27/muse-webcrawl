Michael Christopher White (born 28 June 1970) is an American filmmaker and actor. He wrote the films Chuck & Buck (2000), The Good Girl (2002), School of Rock (2003), and Nacho Libre (2006), co-created the HBO series Enlightened (2011–2013) with Laura Dern, and created the acclaimed HBO anthology series The White Lotus (2021– ), which he writes and directs in full.

Career

White began in television as a writer and producer on Dawson's Creek and Freaks and Geeks, in which he also acted. His screenplays blend comedy with uncomfortable explorations of class, longing, and self-delusion. School of Rock, starring Jack Black, became a major commercial success and was later adapted into a Broadway musical.

The White Lotus

Filmed at resort locations in Hawaii, Sicily, and Thailand, The White Lotus satirizes wealthy vacationers and the staff who serve them, each season framing its social satire around a mysterious death. The series won numerous Emmy Awards, including for White's writing and directing.

Reality television

A self-described reality TV enthusiast, White competed on The Amazing Race with his father Mel White and was runner-up on Survivor: David vs. Goliath (2018), experiences he credits with shaping his understanding of character under pressure.
