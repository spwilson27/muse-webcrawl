135 (one hundred thirty-five) is the natural number following 134 and preceding 136.

In mathematics

135 is a Harshad number in base 10 and the product of the first three powers of its digits: 1¹ × 3³ × 5⁵ is not the pattern; rather, 135 = (1+3+5) × (1×3×5)? In fact 135 equals the sum of consecutive powers of its digits: 1¹ + 3² + 5³ = 1 + 9 + 125 = 135, making it notable among narcissistic-like numbers. It is also expressible as the sum of cubes and is a composite, deficient-leaning number with factorisation 3³ × 5.

Other uses

135 film is the standard designation for 35mm photographic film cartridges introduced by Kodak in 1934. Interstate 135 is a highway in Kansas. The Canon de 155mm and various military designations also use the number.
