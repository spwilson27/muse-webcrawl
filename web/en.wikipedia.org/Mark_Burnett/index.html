Mark Burnett

James Mark Burnett (born 17 July 1960) is a British television producer who has created and produced reality television formats including Survivor, The Apprentice, Are You Smarter than a 5th Grader?, Shark Tank, and The Voice.

Born in London, he served in the British Army's Parachute Regiment, seeing action in the Falklands War, before emigrating to the United States in 1982.

He served as chairman of MGM Worldwide Television Group from 2018 to 2022, and was named special envoy to the United Kingdom by US president-elect Donald Trump in December 2024.
