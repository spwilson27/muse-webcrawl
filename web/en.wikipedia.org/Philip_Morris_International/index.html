Philip Morris International

Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI) is an American multinational tobacco company, one of the largest in the world, with products sold in over 180 countries. It was spun off from Altria Group in March 2008: Altria retained the Philip Morris USA business, while PMI took the international operations. Its most famous brand is Marlboro, the world's best-selling cigarette brand, alongside L&M, Chesterfield, Parliament, and local brands such as Sampoerna in Indonesia and Fortune in the Philippines. The company has its operational headquarters in Lausanne, Switzerland, and corporate headquarters in Stamford, Connecticut.

History

The company traces its origins to Philip Morris, a London tobacconist who opened a shop on Bond Street in 1847; the business expanded into the United States and was incorporated in New York in 1902. Through the 20th century it grew into the tobacco giant that became part of the Altria conglomerate before the 2008 spin-off.

Smoke-free products

Since 2016 PMI has publicly committed to a "smoke-free future", investing heavily in heated tobacco products — principally its IQOS system, which heats rather than burns tobacco — and other nicotine products. Its 2022 acquisition of Swedish Match added the ZYN nicotine pouch brand. The strategy remains controversial with public-health advocates, who point to the company's continued global cigarette sales.
