Larisa Semyonovna Latynina (born 27 December 1934) is a Soviet artistic gymnast from Ukraine who won 18 Olympic medals — nine gold, five silver, and four bronze — between 1956 and 1964. Her total stood as the record for any athlete, male or female, for 48 years, until swimmer Michael Phelps surpassed it at the 2012 London Olympics; she remains the most decorated female Olympian in history and the gymnast with the most Olympic medals.

Career

Latynina made her international debut at the 1954 World Championships in Rome, winning the team gold. At the 1956 Melbourne Olympics she won four gold medals (team, all-around, vault, and floor exercise). She repeated as world all-around champion in 1958 — famously competing while pregnant — and again led the Soviet team at Rome 1960 (three golds) and Tokyo 1964 (two golds), by then nearly 30 years old, an advanced age for a gymnast of the era. Between World, Olympic, and European competition she amassed 46 gold medals in total, winning the individual all-around at every major championship from 1956 to 1962.

Later life

After retiring in 1966, Latynina became head coach of the Soviet national gymnastics team (1967–1977), presiding over a further era of dominance, and later helped organise gymnastics at the 1980 Moscow Olympics. She was inducted into the International Gymnastics Hall of Fame in 1998.
