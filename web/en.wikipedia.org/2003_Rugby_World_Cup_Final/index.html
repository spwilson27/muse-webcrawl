The 2003 Rugby World Cup Final was the final match of the 2003 Rugby World Cup, played on 22 November 2003 at Telstra Stadium in Sydney between hosts and defending champions Australia and England. England won 20–17 after extra time to become the first northern hemisphere team to win the Rugby World Cup.

Match summary

Australia struck first through a try by Lote Tuqiri, but England built a 14–5 half-time lead through a Jason Robinson try and the boot of Jonny Wilkinson. Elton Flatley kicked Australia back into contention with a series of penalties, levelling the scores at 14–14 near the end of normal time to force extra time.

In extra time the teams exchanged penalties to reach 17–17. With less than a minute remaining, a break by Matt Dawson set up field position from which Wilkinson kicked a drop goal with his weaker right foot, sealing a 20–17 victory. England captain Martin Johnson lifted the Webb Ellis Cup.

Legacy

The victory sparked huge celebrations in England, including a victory parade in London attended by hundreds of thousands of people. Wilkinson's drop goal is regarded as one of the most famous moments in English sporting history.
