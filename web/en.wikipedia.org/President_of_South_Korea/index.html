The president of the Republic of Korea (Korean: 대한민국 대통령) is the head of state and head of government of South Korea. The president directs the executive branch, is commander-in-chief of the Republic of Korea Armed Forces, and conducts the country's foreign relations. The office was established upon the founding of the Republic of Korea in 1948, with Syngman Rhee as its first holder.

Election and term

Under the constitution of the Sixth Republic (1987), the president is elected by direct popular vote in a single-round plurality election. The term is five years and the president may not be re-elected — a provision designed to prevent the prolonged rule associated with the country's authoritarian era. A candidate must be a South Korean citizen at least forty years old. If the presidency falls vacant, the prime minister acts in the office until a new president is elected within sixty days.

Powers

The president appoints the prime minister (with the consent of the National Assembly), ministers, and other senior officials; issues decrees; grants pardons; and may declare a state of emergency or martial law, subject to legislative oversight. The president is immune from criminal prosecution while in office except for insurrection or treason. Constitutional amendments require both a two-thirds vote of the National Assembly and approval in a national referendum.

History

Korea's presidential history has been turbulent. Syngman Rhee (1948–1960) resigned after rigged elections sparked the April Revolution. Park Chung Hee seized power in a 1961 coup and ruled until his assassination in 1979; his Yushin Constitution gave the presidency near-total control. Chun Doo-hwan, who took power after another coup, presided over the violent suppression of the 1980 Gwangju Uprising. The June Democratic Struggle of 1987 forced the restoration of direct elections, inaugurating the Sixth Republic.

Democratically elected presidents since 1987 include Roh Tae-woo, Kim Young-sam, Kim Dae-jung (Nobel Peace Prize laureate and author of the Sunshine Policy toward North Korea), Roh Moo-hyun, Lee Myung-bak, Park Geun-hye, Moon Jae-in, and Yoon Suk Yeol. Several have faced prosecution after leaving office; Park Geun-hye was impeached and removed in 2017, and Yoon was impeached in December 2024 after his brief declaration of martial law, with the Constitutional Court upholding his removal in April 2025.

Residence

The Blue House (Cheong Wa Dae) in Seoul served as the presidential office and residence from 1948 until 2022, when the office was moved to the Ministry of National Defense building in Yongsan District and the Blue House grounds were opened to the public.
