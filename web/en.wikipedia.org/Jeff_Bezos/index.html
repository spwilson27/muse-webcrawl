Jeff Bezos

Jeffrey Preston Bezos

Born: 12 January 1964, Albuquerque, New Mexico, U.S.

Alma mater: Princeton University

Known for: Founding Amazon and Blue Origin

Jeffrey Preston Bezos (born January 12, 1964) is an American businessman, media proprietor and commercial astronaut. He is the founder, executive chairman and former president and CEO of Amazon, the world's largest e-commerce and cloud computing company, and the founder of the spaceflight company Blue Origin. He is among the wealthiest people in the world.

Early life and career

Born in Albuquerque and raised in Houston and Miami, Bezos graduated from Princeton University in 1986 with degrees in electrical engineering and computer science. He worked on Wall Street, becoming a senior vice president at the hedge fund D. E. Shaw, before leaving in 1994 to found an online bookstore.

Amazon and other ventures

Founded in 1994 in Bellevue, Washington, Amazon grew from bookselling into a global technology and logistics empire, including the cloud platform Amazon Web Services. Bezos stepped down as CEO in 2021, becoming executive chairman. He founded Blue Origin in 2000, flew to space aboard its New Shepard vehicle in July 2021, and in 2013 purchased The Washington Post for $250 million. Through his Bezos Earth Fund he has pledged $10 billion to address climate change.
