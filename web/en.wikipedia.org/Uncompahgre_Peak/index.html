Uncompahgre Peak

Uncompahgre Peak is the highest summit of the San Juan Mountains in southwestern Colorado, United States, and the sixth-highest summit of the Rocky Mountains. Its elevation is 14,321 feet (4,365 metres), making it one of Colorado's "fourteeners".

Geography

The peak lies in the Uncompahgre Wilderness in Hinsdale County, within the Uncompahgre National Forest, northeast of Lake City and west of the mining town area near the headwaters of Henson Creek. The mountain takes its name from the Ute word Uncompahgre, referring to the warm springs and red water of the region ("dirty water" or "red water spring").

Climbing

The standard route ascends from the Nellie Creek trailhead via the south ridge and is generally regarded as a walk-up with some scrambling near the summit cliffs. The peak's broad, flat summit plateau contrasts with the rugged surrounding San Juan range.
