Pronking

Pronking (also called stotting) is a gait in which a quadruped leaps into the air with all four legs held stiff and the back arched, landing on all fours together. It is famously performed by gazelles and springbok, and by young deer, goats, and sheep.

Explanations

Proposed functions include signaling fitness to predators (an honest signal that pursuit is futile), alerting the herd, confusing predators, and play or exuberance in juveniles. Amotz Zahavi's handicap-principle account treats conspicuous pronking as an advertisement of quality. Rats and some other animals also stot, and similar bouncing behaviour appears in domestic animals at play.
