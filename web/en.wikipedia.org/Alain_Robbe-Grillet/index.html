Alain Robbe-Grillet

Alain Robbe-Grillet (18 August 1922 – 18 February 2008) was a French writer and filmmaker, the leading figure of the nouveau roman ("new novel") movement of the 1950s.

Writing

Trained as an agronomist, Robbe-Grillet published his first novel, The Erasers (Les Gommes), in 1953. The Voyeur (1955) and Jealousy (La Jalousie, 1957) exemplified his style: obsessive, precise descriptions of objects and surfaces with minimal psychological narration. His essays were collected in For a New Novel (1963).

Film

He wrote the screenplay for Alain Resnais's Last Year at Marienbad (1961) and later directed his own films, including Trans-Europ-Express (1966). He was elected to the Académie française in 2004, though he never took his seat.
