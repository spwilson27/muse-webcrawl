Diageo

Diageo plc is a British multinational alcoholic beverage company headquartered in London, England. It is one of the world's largest producers of spirits and beer, selling its products in more than 180 countries.

History

Diageo was formed in 1997 from the merger of Grand Metropolitan and Guinness plc, creating a food and drinks conglomerate; the company subsequently sold off its food businesses, including Burger King and Pillsbury, to focus on premium drinks. Its name combines the Latin dies ("day") and the Greek geo ("world"), suggesting "celebrating life, every day, everywhere".

Brands

Major brands include Johnnie Walker, Crown Royal, J&B, Buchanan's, and Windsor whiskies; Smirnoff, Ketel One, and Cîroc vodkas; Captain Morgan rum; Don Julio and Casamigos tequilas; Tanqueray and Gordon's gins; Baileys liqueur; and Guinness stout. Diageo also owns a major stake in Moët Hennessy drinks interests.

Operations

The company is listed on the London Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange. Its largest markets include the United States, where it is the leading spirits supplier. Diageo has faced continuing scrutiny over alcohol marketing, taxation, and responsible drinking, and promotes moderation campaigns alongside its commercial activities.
