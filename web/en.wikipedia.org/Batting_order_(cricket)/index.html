Batting order (cricket)

The batting order in cricket is the sequence in which batters play through their team's innings. Teams usually develop a settled order, with specialist openers facing the new ball and lower-order batters expected to defend or score quickly as circumstances demand.

Structure

The two opening batters face the new ball; the "top order" (numbers 1–3) and "middle order" (4–7) follow, with the "tail" of bowlers batting last. Captains may promote a pinch hitter or send in a nightwatchman near the close of play to protect more accomplished batters. Because a team has only ten wickets, the order balances scoring ability against the risk of losing wickets cheaply.
