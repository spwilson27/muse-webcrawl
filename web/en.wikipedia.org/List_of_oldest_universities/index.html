List of oldest universities

The oldest universities in continuous operation are mostly in Europe, founded in the medieval period, with older institutions of higher learning elsewhere in the world.

  • University of Bologna — 1088, Italy
  • University of Oxford — teaching from 1096, England
  • University of Paris — mid-12th century, France
  • University of Cambridge — 1209, England
  • University of Salamanca — 1218, Spain
  • University of Padua — 1222, Italy
  • University of Naples Federico II — 1224, Italy
  • University of Siena — 1240, Italy
  • University of Valladolid — 1241, Spain
  • University of Coimbra — 1290, Portugal

The University of al-Qarawiyyin in Fez, founded in 859, and al-Azhar in Cairo are often cited as the oldest existing educational institutions, recognised by UNESCO and Guinness in that category.
