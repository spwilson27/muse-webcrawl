Dana Katherine Scully is a fictional character in the Fox science fiction television series The X-Files, portrayed by Gillian Anderson. Scully is a Federal Bureau of Investigation special agent and a medical doctor who is assigned to work with Fox Mulder on the X-Files — unsolved cases involving paranormal phenomena.

Character

Trained as a physician and forensic pathologist, Scully serves as the skeptic and scientific counterweight to Mulder's belief in the paranormal, applying reason and the scientific method to their investigations. As the series progresses, her own experiences — including an abduction, a cancer linked to an implant, and encounters she cannot explain — gradually complicate her skepticism, though she remains grounded in faith and science alike. She and Mulder develop a deep partnership that becomes romantic in later seasons; they have a son, William.

Reception and legacy

Scully is widely regarded as one of television's most influential female characters. The so-called "Scully effect" describes the documented phenomenon of the character inspiring women to pursue careers in science, medicine, and law enforcement.
