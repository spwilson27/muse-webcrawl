Mountain pass

Mountain pass
SynonymsCol, gap, notch, saddle, bealach, bwlch
Related termSaddle (landform)

A mountain pass is a navigable route through a mountain range or over a ridge. Since mountain ranges form major barriers to travel, passes have played a key role in trade, war and migration throughout history, and are often the only practical routes through a range.

Formation and function

Passes are usually found just above the source of a river, forming a natural crossing at the drainage divide between two valleys. They may be created by erosion, the action of glaciers, or faults and folds in the Earth's crust. At higher elevations passes may be snowbound for much of the year.

Notable passes

Famous passes include the Khyber Pass between Afghanistan and Pakistan, the Brenner and Great St Bernard passes in the Alps, the Khardung La in Ladakh (one of the world's highest motorable passes), and Independence Pass in Colorado. In sport, high passes such as the Col du Tourmalet are celebrated in cycle racing.
