La Quebrada Cliff Divers

The La Quebrada Cliff Divers (Spanish: Clavadistas de La Quebrada) are a group of professional divers who perform daily shows at La Quebrada in Acapulco, Mexico, diving from cliffs about 35 metres (115 ft) high into a narrow inlet of the Pacific Ocean.

History

The tradition began in 1934, when local youths began diving for dares and tips; it was formalised as an organised attraction in the 1940s and 1950s and became one of Acapulco's signature tourist spectacles, featured in films and the Wide World of Sports television broadcasts.

The dive

Divers climb the cliff, pray at a shrine to the Virgin of Guadalupe at the top, and time their plunge to the incoming waves, which briefly deepen the water in the cove to a safe depth. Night shows feature divers carrying torches.
