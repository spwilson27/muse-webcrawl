Pokémon Trading Card Game

Publisher: The Pokémon Company
Release date: 20 October 1996 (Japan); 9 January 1999 (North America)
Players: 2
Skills: Card playing, strategy, arithmetic

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (abbreviated PTCG or Pokémon TCG) is a collectible card game based on Pokémon, first published in October 1996 by Media Factory in Japan. In the US, it was first published by Wizards of the Coast. In June 2003, Nintendo transferred all rights to The Pokémon Company, which has since published the game. As of March 2023, the game has sold over 52.9 billion cards worldwide.

The game saw a surge in popularity and sales during the COVID-19 pandemic, and is today considered one of the most popular trading card games in the world, alongside Magic: The Gathering and Yu-Gi-Oh!.

Gameplay

The Pokémon Trading Card Game is a strategy-based card game in which two players, taking on the role of Pokémon Trainers, use decks of 60 cards featuring Pokémon with abilities and attacks. Players battle their Pokémon against one another; when a Pokémon is Knocked Out, the opposing player takes a Prize card. A player wins by taking all six of their Prize cards, by Knocking Out all of the opponent's Pokémon in play, or if the opponent cannot draw a card at the start of their turn.

Cards come in several types: Basic and Evolution Pokémon cards, Energy cards used to power attacks, and Trainer cards (including Item, Supporter and Stadium cards) that provide various effects.

Collectibility and market

Pokémon cards are also widely collected, and rare cards have achieved record prices at auction. In 2022, a PSA Grade 10 Pikachu Illustrator card was sold for a world record $5.275 million. First edition holographic cards from the original 1999 Base Set, particularly Charizard, are among the most sought-after cards in the hobby.

Competitive play

Organised play is administered through Play! Pokémon, which runs league events, regional championships and the Pokémon World Championships, held annually. A digital version, Pokémon Trading Card Game Live, allows online play.
