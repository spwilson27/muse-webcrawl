Claudio Simonetti

Claudio Simonetti (born 19 February 1952) is an Italian musician and film composer. He is the keyboard player of the progressive rock band Goblin, best known for its iconic scores to the horror films of Dario Argento, including Deep Red (Profondo rosso, 1975), Suspiria (1977) and Tenebrae (1982).

Early life

Simonetti was born in São Paulo, Brazil, to Italian parents; his father, Enrico Simonetti, was a well-known composer, conductor and television personality. The family returned to Italy when Claudio was a child, and he studied piano at the Conservatorio Santa Cecilia in Rome.

Career

In 1972 Simonetti co-founded the band Oliver, which after line-up changes and a spell in London became Goblin. Recruited by Argento in 1975 to replace Giorgio Gaslini on Deep Red, Goblin's driving, synthesiser-led score was a huge commercial success in Italy. Simonetti wrote or co-wrote most of the band's film music and also scored films by other directors, including Lamberto Bava's Demons (1985). In the 1980s he had success as an Italo disco producer and performer. After periods with successor groups such as Daemonia, he re-formed Goblin and later toured internationally as "Claudio Simonetti's Goblin", performing the classic scores live to film.

Legacy

Simonetti's work with Goblin is widely cited as an influence on electronic, metal and horror soundtrack music, and the Suspiria score in particular is regarded as one of the defining works of Italian progressive rock.
