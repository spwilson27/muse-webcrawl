Charlottesville

Charlottesville is an independent city in the U.S. state of Virginia, and the county seat of Albemarle County, which surrounds it. It is named after Queen Charlotte and had a population of about 46,000 at the 2020 census.

History

Charlottesville was formed by an act of the Virginia General Assembly in 1762. Thomas Jefferson was born nearby at Shadwell and built his home, Monticello, on a hill overlooking the city. James Monroe's home, Highland, and James Madison's Montpelier are also nearby.

Education

The city is home to the University of Virginia, founded by Jefferson in 1819; its Academical Village, together with Monticello, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Recent history

In August 2017 the city was the site of the Unite the Right rally, during which a counter-protester was killed, drawing national attention to debates over Confederate monuments.
