Blowgun

A blowgun (also called a blowpipe or blow tube) is a simple ranged weapon consisting of a long narrow tube for shooting light projectiles such as darts by blowing into it. Blowguns have been used for hunting and warfare by many peoples, particularly in Southeast Asia, the Amazon rainforest and Central America.

Construction and use

Traditional blowguns are made from bamboo, cane, palm wood or other hollow plant stems, sometimes several metres long. Darts are tipped with plant fibres or kapok to form a seal, and in hunting are often coated with poisons such as curare or frog toxins.

Modern use

Modern sporting blowguns are usually made of aluminium or plastic and are used for target shooting; some jurisdictions regulate or prohibit them. Veterinarians and biologists also use blowpipe systems to deliver tranquilliser darts to animals.
