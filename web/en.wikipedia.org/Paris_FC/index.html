Paris FC

Paris Football Club Founded: 1969
Ground: Stade Charléty, Paris
Capacity: approx. 19,000
Colours: Blue and dark blue
Notable development: Majority stake acquired by the Arnault family (LVMH) group with Red Bull minority stake, 2024

Paris Football Club (commonly referred to as Paris FC or simply PFC) is a French professional football club based in Paris. The club was founded in 1969 and merged with Stade Saint-Germain to form Paris Saint-Germain in 1970; the two clubs split in 1972, with Paris FC retaining the first-division place and professional squad, while PSG were relegated to the third tier — a division of fortunes that was soon reversed, as Paris FC were relegated in 1974 while PSG rose to become the dominant Parisian club.

History

Paris FC spent most of the following decades in the lower divisions of French football, playing at the Stade Charléty in the 13th arrondissement. After spells in the amateur Championnat National and a brief amalgamation under the name Paris Racing in the 1980s, the club stabilised as a well-run Ligue 2 side in the 2010s, reaching the promotion play-offs repeatedly and building a reputation for youth development; its women's team competes at the top of Division 1 Féminine and in European competition, winning the Coupe de France féminine in 2025.

In 2024 the club entered a new era when Agache, the holding company of Bernard Arnault and the Arnault family (of LVMH), agreed to acquire a majority stake, with Red Bull taking a minority share — a move intended to establish a genuine Parisian rival to PSG. The men's team won promotion to Ligue 1 in 2025, returning the club to the top flight for the first time since 1979 and setting up the first top-division Paris derby in decades.

Stadium

The club has played most of its recent history at the Stade Charléty, a multi-use stadium south of central Paris. In 2025 Paris FC moved to the Stade Jean-Bouin, directly across the street from Paris Saint-Germain's Parc des Princes, under a long lease with the city — placing the two rival clubs within metres of one another.
