Iqbal Academy Pakistan

Established1951 (reorganised 1962)
HeadquartersAiwan-e-Iqbal, Lahore, Pakistan
PurposeStudy and promotion of the works of Muhammad Iqbal
Parent bodyGovernment of Pakistan

The Iqbal Academy Pakistan (Urdu: اقبال اکادمی پاکستان) is a statutory body of the Government of Pakistan, established to promote and disseminate the study of the works and philosophy of Muhammad Iqbal (1877–1938), the poet-philosopher regarded as the spiritual founder of Pakistan.

History

An Iqbal academy was first founded in 1951; the present institution was established in 1962 under the Iqbal Academy Ordinance. It is an autonomous body working under the federal government's cultural and heritage administration, with its head office in the Aiwan-e-Iqbal complex in Lahore.

Activities

The Academy publishes critical editions and translations of Iqbal's poetry and prose in Urdu, Persian, English, and other languages, produces scholarly journals such as Iqbal Review (Iqbaliyat), maintains a specialised library and website with digital texts, and organises conferences, seminars, and Iqbal Day observances on 9 November. It also awards a Presidential Iqbal Award for outstanding research on Iqbal studies and cooperates with Iqbal academies, chairs, and societies abroad.

Objectives

Its statutory aims include promoting the understanding of Iqbal's message in Pakistan and internationally, encouraging research and publication, and coordinating the work of Iqbal-related institutions. The Academy's publications range from the collected works (Kulliyat) to bibliographies, concordances of Iqbal's poetry, and translations such as that of the Javid Nama and the Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam.
