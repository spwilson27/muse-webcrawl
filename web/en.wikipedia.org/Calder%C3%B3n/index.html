Calderón

Calderón is a Spanish surname. In Spanish it literally means "large cauldron" (from caldera, "cauldron" or "caldera"). It is borne by numerous notable people in the Spanish-speaking world, in fields including literature, politics, sport, and the arts.

Notable people

  • Pedro Calderón de la Barca (1600–1681), Spanish dramatist, poet, and writer of the Spanish Golden Age, author of Life Is a Dream (La vida es sueño).
  • Felipe Calderón (born 1962), Mexican politician, president of Mexico from 2006 to 2012.
  • José Manuel Calderón (born 1981), Spanish professional basketball player who played in the NBA.
  • Mercedes Calderón, Cuban volleyball player.
  • Ramón Calderón (born 1951), Spanish lawyer and former president of Real Madrid.
  • Abdón Calderón (1804–1822), Ecuadorian independence hero.
  • Paul Calderón (born 1959), Puerto Rican actor.
  • Cristian Calderón (born 1997), Mexican footballer.

Other uses

Calderón is also a parish and town in the canton of Quito, Ecuador, famous for its marzipan figurines (masapanes), and a common place-name element in Spain and Latin America.
