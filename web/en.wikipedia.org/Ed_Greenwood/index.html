Ed Greenwood (born July 21, 1959) is a Canadian fantasy writer and the creator of the Forgotten Realms campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons fantasy role-playing game. He began writing about the Realms in 1967 as a child, began running it as a game world in 1975, and sold the setting to TSR in 1986, where it became one of the game's signature worlds.

Career

Greenwood contributed extensively to Dragon magazine, including the "Pages from the Mages" articles, and wrote or co-wrote numerous game sourcebooks. His best-known fictional character is the wizard Elminster, featured in novels including Elminster: The Making of a Mage (1994) and Spellfire (1988). He has written dozens of novels and worked as a librarian in Ontario. In 2015 he launched new work through the Onder Librum imprint, including Hellmaw. Greenwood is a frequent guest at gaming conventions, recognizable for appearing in the persona of Elminster.
