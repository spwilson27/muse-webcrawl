Pando (tree)

Pando (Latin for "I spread"), also known as the trembling giant, is a colony of a single quaking aspen (Populus tremuloides) tree located in the Fishlake National Forest in south-central Utah, United States. Every stem in the colony is genetically identical and connected by one massive root system, making Pando a single genetic individual and one of the largest and oldest known living organisms.

Description

The colony covers about 43 hectares (106 acres) and comprises roughly 47,000 stems, with a combined estimated mass of about 6,000 tonnes, making it the heaviest known organism. Individual stems average about 130 years old; the root system's age is much greater and estimates derived from genetic mutation rates and local pollen records suggest it may be many thousands of years old, sometimes estimated at up to 14,000 years or more.

Reproduction and decline

Pando reproduces primarily by asexual root suckering, sending up new stems from its roots. Studies have found the colony in decline: browsing by deer and livestock suppresses young stems, and few middle-aged stems are replacing dying older ones. Fenced exclosures and conservation projects have been established to protect regeneration.
