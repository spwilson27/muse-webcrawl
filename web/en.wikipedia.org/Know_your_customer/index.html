Know your customer

Know your customer (KYC)
PurposeIdentity verification; anti-money laundering compliance
SectorsBanking, finance, crypto-assets, telecoms
RelatedCustomer due diligence (CDD), AML, CFT

Know your customer (KYC) refers to the processes by which banks and other regulated businesses verify the identity of their clients and assess the risks of illegal intentions, such as money laundering and terrorist financing. KYC is a core element of anti-money laundering (AML) regulation worldwide.

Components

A typical KYC programme includes a customer identification programme (checking government ID and address), customer due diligence (understanding the nature and purpose of the relationship, and identifying beneficial owners of companies), and ongoing monitoring of transactions against the customer's expected profile. Higher-risk customers require enhanced due diligence.

Legal basis

Requirements derive from laws such as the US Bank Secrecy Act and USA PATRIOT Act, the EU Anti-Money Laundering Directives, and recommendations of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF). Failures have produced fines of billions of dollars against major banks.

Digital KYC

Electronic identity verification (eKYC) uses document scans, selfies with liveness checks and database verification to onboard customers remotely. Supporters cite efficiency; critics raise privacy, data-security and financial-exclusion concerns for people lacking standard documents.

Related concepts

"Know your customer's customer" (KYCC) and transaction monitoring extend the principle deeper into financial networks.
