Christophany

Field: Christian theology

Related: Theophany, Angel of the Lord

A Christophany is an appearance of Christ before his incarnation, especially an episode in the Hebrew Bible interpreted by Christians as a manifestation of the pre-incarnate Son. The term is also used more broadly for a visible appearance of Christ.

Interpretation

Passages proposed as Christophanies include the Angel of the Lord speaking with divine authority, the visitor to Abraham, the figure who wrestles Jacob, and the commander of the Lord's army before Joshua. Theologians debate whether each is Christ, an angel, or a literary representation of God's presence.

Theological significance

The idea supports continuity between the Old and New Testaments and reflects doctrines of Christ's pre-existence. Interpretations differ among traditions, and Jewish readings do not identify these figures with Jesus.
