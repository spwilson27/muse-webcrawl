Borrego Springs, California

Borrego Springs is a census-designated place in San Diego County, California, United States. It is a desert resort community completely surrounded by Anza-Borrego Desert State Park, the largest state park in California. The population was around 3,000 at recent counts, swelling seasonally with winter visitors.

Geography and climate

Borrego Springs lies in the Borrego Valley in the Colorado Desert, west of the Salton Sea. It has a hot desert climate with very hot summers, mild winters, and low rainfall. Average summer highs exceed 100 °F (38 °C), while winter days are typically in the upper 60s °F.

Attractions

The community is known for its dark skies: in 2009 it was designated an International Dark-Sky Community, the first in California. Large metal sculptures by artist Ricardo Breceda, depicting prehistoric creatures and other figures, are scattered across the surrounding desert floor and are a popular attraction. Nearby attractions include the badlands formations, palm oases, and wildflower blooms in wet winters.

The name "Borrego" comes from the Spanish word for lamb or sheep, referring to the bighorn sheep found in the area.
