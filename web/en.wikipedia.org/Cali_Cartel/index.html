Cali Cartel

Cali Cartel
Founded: 1970s, Cali, Colombia
Founders: Gilberto Rodríguez Orejuela, Miguel Rodríguez Orejuela, José Santacruz Londoño, Hélmer "Pacho" Herrera
Territory: Colombia, United States, Europe
Activities: Cocaine trafficking, money laundering
Status: Defunct (mid-1990s)

The Cali Cartel (Spanish: Cartel de Cali) was a drug cartel based in southern Colombia, around the city of Cali and the Valle del Cauca department. It was founded by the brothers Gilberto and Miguel Rodríguez Orejuela, José Santacruz Londoño, and Hélmer Herrera Buitrago. At its height in the mid-1990s, following the death of Medellín Cartel leader Pablo Escobar, the Cali Cartel was estimated to control as much as 80 percent of the world's cocaine market and was described by the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration as one of the most powerful criminal organizations in history.

History

The cartel's founders began in the 1970s with a group known as "Las Chemas," initially involved in kidnapping and marijuana trafficking before moving into cocaine. Unlike the violent Medellín organization, the Cali group cultivated a businesslike image, investing heavily in legitimate businesses — including the Drogas La Rebaja pharmacy chain and the América de Cali football club — and in bribery and intelligence networks rather than open confrontation with the state.

Operations

The cartel pioneered sophisticated trafficking methods, including concealing cocaine in hollowed-out concrete posts, frozen vegetables, and other cargo, and it ran extensive money-laundering operations. It maintained a counter-intelligence apparatus that monitored communications in Cali, and it cultivated ties with politicians; the cartel's financing of Ernesto Samper's 1994 presidential campaign produced the Proceso 8000 scandal.

Downfall

In 1995 Colombian authorities arrested Gilberto Rodríguez Orejuela and, months later, Miguel Rodríguez Orejuela. José Santacruz Londoño escaped prison but was killed by police in 1996, and Pacho Herrera was murdered in prison in 1998. The Rodríguez Orejuela brothers were extradited to the United States in 2004–2005 and pleaded guilty in 2006 to cocaine trafficking charges, forfeiting billions in assets.

In popular culture

The Cali Cartel is depicted prominently in the third season of the Netflix series Narcos.
