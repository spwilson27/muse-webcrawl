McClellan–Kerr Arkansas River Navigation System

The McClellan–Kerr Arkansas River Navigation System (MKARNS) is a 445-mile (716 km) inland waterway system in Oklahoma and Arkansas, in the United States. Named for Senators John L. McClellan of Arkansas and Robert S. Kerr of Oklahoma, it was authorised by the Rivers and Harbors Act of 1946 and completed in 1970.

Description

The system follows the Verdigris River, Arkansas River, and Canadian River, connecting the Tulsa Port of Catoosa — one of the most inland US river ports — to the Mississippi River via a series of locks and dams maintained by the US Army Corps of Engineers. The navigation channel is maintained at a depth of about 9 feet.

Use

Barges carry petroleum products, fertiliser, steel, grain, and other bulk commodities. The system also supports hydroelectric power, flood control, water supply, and recreation along its reservoirs.
