Global optimization

Global optimization is the branch of applied mathematics and numerical analysis concerned with finding the globally best solution of an optimisation problem, in contrast to local optimisation, which finds only a nearby optimum. Global optimisation is essential where objective functions are non-convex or multimodal and local methods can become trapped at suboptimal points.

Approaches include deterministic methods such as branch and bound, interval analysis and Lipschitz optimisation, and stochastic or heuristic methods such as simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, particle swarm optimisation and differential evolution. Applications include molecular conformation, engineering design, finance and machine-learning hyperparameter tuning.
