Cirrus Aircraft

Cirrus Aircraft Corporation
Founded: 1984
Headquarters: Duluth, Minnesota, U.S.
Products: SR20, SR22, Vision SF50

Cirrus Aircraft Corporation is an American aircraft manufacturer based in Duluth, Minnesota. Founded in 1984 by brothers Alan and Dale Klapmeier, the company is best known for the SR20 and SR22 piston single-engine aircraft—the best-selling general aviation airplanes of the 21st century—and the Vision SF50 single-engine jet.

Products

Cirrus aircraft are built largely of composite materials and are notable for the Cirrus Airframe Parachute System (CAPS), a ballistic parachute designed to lower the entire aircraft to the ground in an emergency. The SR22 has been the world's best-selling general aviation aircraft since 2003. The Vision Jet SF50, certified in 2016, is the first single-engine personal jet.

History

The company began with the VK-30 kit aircraft before developing the certified SR series, first delivered in 1999. After financial difficulties during the Great Recession, Cirrus was acquired in 2011 by the China Aviation Industry General Aircraft Company (CAIGA).
