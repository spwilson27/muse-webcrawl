David Croft

David John Croft OBE (born David John Shared; 7 September 1922 – 27 September 2011) was an English television comedy writer, producer and director. With Jimmy Perry he co-created Dad's Army (1968–1977), drawing on his own experience in the Home Guard and the Royal Artillery, and with Jeremy Lloyd he co-wrote Are You Being Served? and 'Allo 'Allo!.

Career

Croft produced and directed most of his series himself, an unusual degree of control at the BBC. His partnership with Perry also produced It Ain't Half Hot Mum and Hi-de-Hi!. His work is noted for ensemble casts, catchphrases and gentle period settings, and Dad's Army remains among the most repeated British sitcoms.
