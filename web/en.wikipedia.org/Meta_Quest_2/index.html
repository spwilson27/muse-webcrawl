Meta Quest 2

Meta Quest 2
Manufacturer: Meta (Reality Labs)
Released: 13 October 2020 (as Oculus Quest 2)
Chipset: Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2
Display: 1832 × 1920 per eye, up to 120 Hz
Launch price: US$299

The Meta Quest 2 (originally Oculus Quest 2) is a standalone virtual reality headset developed by Meta's Reality Labs division. Released in October 2020, it succeeded the original Quest with a lighter body, faster Snapdragon XR2 processor, sharper displays and a lower starting price of $299, and became the best-selling VR headset in history, with sales reported above ten million units within its first year and approaching twenty million by 2023.

Hardware and software

The headset tracks position and the two Touch controllers with four onboard cameras (inside-out tracking), requires no PC or external sensors, and can also connect to a gaming PC via Link cable or wireless Air Link. Storage options were 64, 128 and 256 GB; a controversial August 2022 price increase raised prices by $100. Its library runs through the Quest Store, with hits such as Beat Saber, Resident Evil 4 VR and Superhot VR, alongside social platform Horizon Worlds.

Controversies and rebranding

The initial requirement to log in with a Facebook account drew sustained criticism and regulatory scrutiny in Germany; Meta dropped the requirement in 2022 in favour of Meta accounts. Following Facebook's corporate rebrand to Meta, the Oculus name was retired and the headset renamed Meta Quest 2 in 2022. It was succeeded by the Meta Quest 3 in October 2023, and discontinued in 2024 as the Quest 3S took over the entry-level position.
