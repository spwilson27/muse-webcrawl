Bar Standards Board

The Bar Standards Board (BSB) is the regulatory body for barristers in England and Wales. It was established in 2006 as the independent regulatory arm of the Bar Council, following the separation of regulatory and representative functions recommended by the Clementi review and later enacted in the Legal Services Act 2007.

Functions

The BSB sets the standards of conduct and training for barristers, authorises individuals and entities to practise, maintains the Bar Register, sets the education and training requirements for qualification (including the Bar training courses and pupillage), and handles complaints and disciplinary matters against barristers. Disciplinary findings of professional misconduct are typically dealt with by independent disciplinary tribunals convened by the Bar Tribunals and Adjudication Service.

Structure

The BSB is governed by a board with a lay majority, chaired by a lay chair, in keeping with the principle of independent regulation overseen by the Legal Services Board. Its offices are in High Holborn, London.
