P. O. Ackley

Parker Otto Ackley (May 25, 1903 – August 23, 1989) was an American gunsmith, barrel maker, author, columnist, and wildcat cartridge developer. He is best known for the Ackley Improved family of wildcat rifle cartridges.

Career

Ackley was born in Granville, New York. He began his gunsmithing career in the 1930s and operated shops in Trinidad, Colorado, and later in Salt Lake City, Utah. He contributed shooting and reloading columns to publications such as Shooting Times and Guns & Ammo, and authored the influential two-volume Handbook for Shooters and Reloaders (1962).

Ackley Improved cartridges

His "Improved" designs typically consist of a standard factory cartridge fire-formed in a chamber with less body taper and a sharper shoulder angle, increasing case capacity and velocity. Well-known examples include the .257 Roberts Ackley Improved, the .280 Ackley Improved (later standardized by Nosler), and the .22-250 Ackley Improved.

Ackley died in Salt Lake City in 1989.
