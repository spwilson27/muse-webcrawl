Khanate

A khanate or khaganate was a political entity ruled by a khan, typical of peoples of the Eurasian Steppe. Khanates succeeded the Mongol Empire across Central Asia, Siberia and Eastern Europe.

Examples

Notable khanates include the Golden Horde's successors at Kazan, Astrakhan and Crimea, the Khanates of Bukhara, Khiva and Kokand in Central Asia, and the Kazakh Khanate. Many survived into the 18th–19th centuries before absorption by Russia or Qing China.
