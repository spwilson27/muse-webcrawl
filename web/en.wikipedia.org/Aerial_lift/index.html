Aerial lift

An aerial lift is a cable transport system in which cabins, chairs or other carriers are suspended from a continuously moving cable loop, used to move people across terrain where roads are difficult, especially at ski resorts and tourist sites.

Types

Types include gondola lifts, chairlifts, aerial tramways with large cabins shuttling on fixed track cables, and surface lifts such as T-bars and drag lifts. Modern gondolas may carry bikes and wheelchairs and serve as urban transit, as in Medellin and La Paz.

Safety

Lifts are fitted with evacuation systems, wind limits and braking safeguards, and are inspected under national standards. See also Gondola lift, Chairlift and Cable car.
