List of universities in Denmark

This is a list of universities in Denmark. Higher education in Denmark is dominated by public universities funded by the state; tuition is free for students from the EU/EEA. The oldest institution, the University of Copenhagen, was founded in 1479.

Universities

  • University of Copenhagen (1479) — Denmark's oldest and largest university.
  • Aarhus University (1928) — large comprehensive university in Jutland.
  • University of Southern Denmark (1966) — campuses in Odense and other cities.
  • Roskilde University (1972) — known for interdisciplinary, problem-oriented learning.
  • Aalborg University (1974) — known for its problem-based learning model.
  • Technical University of Denmark (DTU) (1829) — engineering university at Lyngby.
  • Copenhagen Business School (1917).
  • IT University of Copenhagen (1999).

Other institutions

Denmark also has university colleges (professionshøjskoler) offering professionally oriented bachelor's degrees, business academies, and specialised art and music academies such as the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts and the Royal Academy of Music.
