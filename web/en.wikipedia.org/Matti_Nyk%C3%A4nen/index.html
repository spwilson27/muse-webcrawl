Matti Nykänen

Matti Ensio Nykänen (17 July 1963 – 4 February 2019) was a Finnish ski jumper who is widely regarded as one of the greatest ski jumpers of all time. He won five Olympic medals, including three golds, nine World Championship medals, and the overall World Cup four times. He is the only ski jumper to have won all five of the sport's major events: the Winter Olympics, the World Ski Championships, the Ski Flying World Championships, the World Cup overall, and the Four Hills Tournament.

Career

Nykänen dominated ski jumping in the 1980s. At the 1988 Calgary Winter Olympics he won all three ski jumping events — the normal hill, large hill, and the inaugural team event. He set several ski flying world records and won a then-record 46 individual World Cup competitions.

Later life

After retiring, Nykänen's life was marked by alcohol problems, financial difficulties, a brief career as a pop singer, and several criminal convictions that made him a fixture of Finnish tabloid media. He died in 2019 at the age of 55.
