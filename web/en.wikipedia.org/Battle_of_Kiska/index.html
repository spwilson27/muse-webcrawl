Battle of Kiska

The Battle of Kiska, also known as Operation Cottage, was the Allied recapture of Kiska Island in the Aleutians in August 1943 during the Second World War — notable because the Japanese garrison had already secretly evacuated.

Events

Japanese forces had occupied Kiska since June 1942. Under cover of fog, the Imperial Japanese Navy evacuated the entire garrison on 28 July 1943. Unaware, Allied forces bombarded the island for weeks and landed over 34,000 troops on 15 August. In the confusion, friendly-fire incidents and booby traps caused several hundred Allied casualties despite the absence of any enemy.
