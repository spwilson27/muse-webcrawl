Elementary mathematics

Elementary mathematics consists of the mathematical topics most frequently taught at the primary and secondary school levels. It forms the foundation on which more advanced mathematics is built.

Topics

Strands of elementary mathematics include number sense and counting; arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication and division) on whole numbers, fractions and decimals; basic geometry of shapes, measurement and spatial reasoning; introductory algebra with variables, equations and functions; and elementary data handling, statistics and probability.

Teaching

Curricula typically introduce these topics progressively, moving from concrete manipulation to abstract representation. Mastery of elementary mathematics is considered essential for everyday numeracy and for further study in science and mathematics.
