Big Ten Network

The Big Ten Network (BTN) is an American sports network based in Chicago, Illinois. The channel is dedicated to coverage of collegiate sports sanctioned by the Big Ten Conference, including live and recorded event telecasts, news, analysis programs, and other content focusing on the conference's member schools. It is a joint venture between the conference and Fox Sports, and was launched on August 30, 2007.

History

BTN was the first American cable network devoted to a single college athletic conference, and its launch was a landmark in the growth of conference-owned media. Early carriage disputes with major cable providers were eventually resolved, and the network became highly profitable, distributing substantial revenue to member schools. Its success inspired similar ventures, including the Pac-12 Network and the SEC Network. BTN's programming includes football and men's and women's basketball games not carried by national partners, along with Olympic sports, studio shows, and original documentaries such as The Journey.

Programming

The network broadcasts hundreds of live events per year across sports including football, basketball, volleyball, wrestling, ice hockey, baseball and softball, supplemented by studio analysis programs and campus-focused shows. Content is also streamed through the B1G+ service and the Fox Sports app.
