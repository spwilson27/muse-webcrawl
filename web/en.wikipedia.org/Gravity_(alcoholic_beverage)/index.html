Gravity (alcoholic beverage)

Gravity, in the context of alcoholic beverages, refers to the specific gravity of the wort or must at various stages of fermentation, used to estimate the alcohol content of beer and wine.

Original gravity

Original gravity (OG) is a measure of the fermentable and unfermentable substances in the wort before fermentation. It is measured with a hydrometer or refractometer and is typically around 1.030–1.070 for beer.

Final gravity

Final gravity (FG) is the specific gravity measured once fermentation is complete. The difference between OG and FG is used to calculate the alcohol by volume (ABV) of the finished beverage, since yeast converts sugars into alcohol and carbon dioxide, lowering the density.

Scales

Gravity may also be expressed in degrees Plato, Brix or Balling, which measure the extract content of the solution as a percentage by weight.
