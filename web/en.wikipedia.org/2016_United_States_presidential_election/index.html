2016 United States presidential election

The 2016 United States presidential election was the 58th quadrennial presidential election, held on Tuesday, November 8, 2016. The Republican ticket of businessman Donald Trump and Indiana governor Mike Pence defeated the Democratic ticket of former secretary of state Hillary Clinton and US senator Tim Kaine in the Electoral College, despite losing the popular vote.

Background

President Barack Obama was ineligible to seek a third term. Clinton prevailed over Senator Bernie Sanders in the Democratic primaries, while Trump defeated a large field of Republican candidates, winning the nomination despite initial opposition from much of the party establishment.

Campaign

The campaign was marked by controversies including the investigation into Clinton's use of a private email server, the release of hacked Democratic emails attributed to Russian interference, and the Access Hollywood recording. US intelligence agencies concluded that Russia interfered in the election to favour Trump.

Results

Clinton won the national popular vote by about 2.9 million votes, but Trump carried the key swing states of Michigan, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin by narrow margins and won 304 electoral votes to Clinton's 227 after faithless electors were counted.

Aftermath

Trump was inaugurated as the 45th president on January 20, 2017. The election prompted debates about the Electoral College, polling accuracy, foreign interference and the role of social media in campaigns.
