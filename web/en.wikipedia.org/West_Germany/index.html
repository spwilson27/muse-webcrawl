West Germany is the common English name for the Federal Republic of Germany from its formation on 23 May 1949 until its reunification with East Germany on 3 October 1990. During the Cold War, the western portion of Germany and the associated territory of West Berlin were parts of the Western Bloc. The Federal Republic was created during the Allied occupation of Germany after the Second World War, established from eleven states formed in the three Allied zones of occupation held by the United States, the United Kingdom, and France. Its provisional capital was the city of Bonn.

The Federal Republic claimed an exclusive mandate for all of Germany, regarding East Germany (the German Democratic Republic) as an illegally constituted state. Under Chancellor Konrad Adenauer, West Germany integrated into Western institutions: it joined NATO in 1955, co-founded the European Economic Community in 1957, and pursued the Hallstein Doctrine of breaking relations with states that recognised East Germany.

Economic miracle

Under Economics Minister Ludwig Erhard, the social market economy and the 1948 currency reform produced the Wirtschaftswunder — rapid industrial growth, full employment, and rising living standards through the 1950s and 1960s. West Germany became one of the world's largest exporters and absorbed millions of ethnic German expellees and, later, Gastarbeiter (guest workers) from Turkey, Italy, and elsewhere.

Ostpolitik and reunification

From 1969 Chancellor Willy Brandt pursued Ostpolitik, normalising relations with the East — treaties with the Soviet Union and Poland, the Basic Treaty with East Germany (1972), and mutual entry into the United Nations in 1973, recognised with the Nobel Peace Prize. In 1989, as the Eastern Bloc crumbled, East Germans fled westward and the Berlin Wall fell on 9 November. Chancellor Helmut Kohl negotiated reunification; on 3 October 1990 the East German states acceded to the Federal Republic, and "West Germany" ceased to be a separate political reality — the Federal Republic continuing as the reunified Germany, with Berlin as capital.
