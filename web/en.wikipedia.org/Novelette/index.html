BetweenShort story and novella

A novelette is a prose fiction work longer than a short story but shorter than a novella. The Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers Association (SFWA) defines it for Nebula Award purposes as a work of between 7,500 and 17,500 words; the Hugo Awards use the same range.

Usage

The term has shifted over time. In the 19th and early 20th centuries "novelette" was sometimes used dismissively for short, light or sentimental novels — often romantic fiction aimed at women. In genre fiction publishing it settled into its modern technical sense as an intermediate length category, with its own Hugo and Nebula award categories.

Characteristics

Novelettes allow more development of character and subplots than a short story while remaining readable in a single sitting. Famous examples in science fiction include works that anchor anthology and magazine publishing, where the length has historically suited periodical formats.
