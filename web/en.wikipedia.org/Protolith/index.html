Protolith

A protolith is the original, unmetamorphosed rock from which a given metamorphic rock was formed. Identifying the protolith is a central task in metamorphic petrology, because a rock's mineral assemblage and texture depend both on its starting composition and on the pressure and temperature conditions of metamorphism.

Examples

  • Shale (sedimentary protolith) → slate, phyllite, schist, gneiss with increasing grade
  • Limestone → marble
  • Sandstone → quartzite
  • Basalt or gabbro (igneous protolith) → greenschist, amphibolite, or eclogite

Identification

Geologists infer the protolith from bulk chemical composition, relict textures or structures preserved from the original rock, and geological context. Names of metamorphic rocks sometimes preserve the protolith with the prefix "meta-", as in metabasalt or metapelite.
