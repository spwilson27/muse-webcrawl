The Marina Bay Street Circuit (also known as the Singapore Street Circuit) is a street circuit around Marina Bay, Singapore, encompassing the planning areas of Downtown Core and Kallang. It is the venue for the Singapore Grand Prix of Formula One.

History

The circuit hosted the first night race in Formula One history on 28 September 2008, won by Fernando Alonso. The event became famous in part for the "Crashgate" scandal, in which Alonso's teammate Nelson Piquet Jr. was later revealed to have crashed deliberately to aid Alonso's victory.

Characteristics

Running anti-clockwise past landmarks such as the Singapore Flyer, the Padang and the Anderson Bridge, the layout is physically demanding: high humidity, bumpy public roads, and relentless corner sequences give drivers little rest. A revised layout introduced in 2023 bypassed the former floating-platform section, shortening the lap and reducing the turn count from 23 to 19.

Events

Besides Formula One, the circuit hosts support series and concerts as part of the Grand Prix weekend, a major event in Singapore's tourism calendar.
