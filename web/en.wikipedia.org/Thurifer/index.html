Thurifer

A thurifer is an acolyte or altar server who carries and swings a thurible, the metal censer in which incense is burned during religious services. The role is found in Roman Catholic, Anglo-Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, and some Lutheran liturgies.

Role in the liturgy

The thurifer leads processions, swinging the thurible so that the incense smoke rises continuously, and presents it to the celebrant when the altar, offerings, Gospel book, or congregation are to be incensed. The thurifer is often assisted by a boat bearer, who carries the "boat" containing grains of incense. In large celebrations, the famous Botafumeiro of Santiago de Compostela Cathedral is swung by a team of men called tiraboleiros.
