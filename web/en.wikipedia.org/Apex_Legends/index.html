Apex Legends

Developer: Respawn Entertainment
Publisher: Electronic Arts
Release: 4 February 2019
Genre: Battle royale, hero shooter

Apex Legends is a free-to-play battle royale hero shooter developed by Respawn Entertainment and published by Electronic Arts. It was released without prior announcement on 4 February 2019 for PlayStation 4, Windows and Xbox One, with later versions for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S and mobile devices.

Gameplay

Set in the same universe as Respawn's Titanfall series, the game pits squads of three players — each controlling a "Legend" with unique abilities — against other squads on an island map. Players scavenge weapons and equipment while a shrinking ring forces encounters; the last squad standing wins. The game introduced a widely praised non-verbal "ping" communication system, later adopted by other shooters.

Reception

Apex Legends attracted 25 million players within a week of launch and 50 million within a month. It won awards for its multiplayer design and has continued to be supported with seasonal content, new Legends, maps and limited-time modes, becoming one of EA's most successful live-service titles.
