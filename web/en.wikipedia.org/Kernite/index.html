Kernite

Kernite, also known as rasorite, is a hydrated sodium borate hydroxide mineral with the formula Na2B4O6(OH)2·3H2O. It is colourless to white, crystallising in the monoclinic system.

Occurrence

It was discovered in 1926 in Kern County, California, in the Mojave Desert borate deposits, from which it takes its name. Kernite is a major ore of boron, mined for borax production, and alters to tincalconite on exposure to air.
