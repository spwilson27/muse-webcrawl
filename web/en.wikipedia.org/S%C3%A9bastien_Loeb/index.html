Sébastien Loeb (born 26 February 1974) is a French professional rally and racing driver. Competing for Citroën in the World Rally Championship (WRC), he won nine consecutive world drivers' titles between 2004 and 2012, making him the most successful driver in WRC history, with 80 rally victories.

Career

Loeb began rallying in the late 1990s and won the Junior WRC in 2001. Joining Citroën's main team, he narrowly missed the 2003 title before dominating the championship for nine seasons with co-driver Daniel Elena. After retiring from full-time WRC competition, he made successful part-time returns, winning rallies as late as 2022 with M-Sport Ford.

Other disciplines

Beyond rallying, Loeb has competed in the World Touring Car Championship with Citroën, won races in rallycross, set a record at the Pikes Peak International Hill Climb in 2013, and contested the Dakar Rally and Extreme E. He is also a multiple winner of the Race of Champions.
