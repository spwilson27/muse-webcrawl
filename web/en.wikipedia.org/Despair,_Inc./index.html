Despair, Inc. is an American company based in Austin, Texas, best known for its line of Demotivators—satirical posters that parody the glossy motivational posters produced by companies such as Successories. Each Demotivator pairs a striking photograph with a cynical or pessimistic caption, inverting the format's usual message of optimism and teamwork.

History

The company was founded in 1998 by brothers Justin and Jef Sewell together with E. L. Kersten, a former academic. Its early products spread rapidly by word of mouth and through the early commercial internet, and the Demotivators became a popular office joke gift. The company later expanded into calendars, mugs, T-shirts, and other novelty merchandise.

Products and style

Typical Demotivator slogans include "Mistakes: It could be that the purpose of your life is only to serve as a warning to others" and "Failure: When your best just isn't good enough." The parody relies on faithfully imitating the visual language of motivational products—black borders, serif titles, and inspirational nature photography—while subverting the message.

Trademark dispute

Despair, Inc. became known in internet culture for registering a trademark on the "frowny" emoticon, :-(, and issuing a mock press release in 2001 announcing plans to sue internet users who employed it. The company later acknowledged the announcement was a parody intended to satirize trademark overreach.
