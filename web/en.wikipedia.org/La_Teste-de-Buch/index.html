La Teste-de-Buch

La Teste-de-Buch is a commune in the Gironde department of Nouvelle-Aquitaine in southwestern France, on the southern shore of the Arcachon Basin (Bassin d'Arcachon). It is known for oyster farming and for containing the Dune of Pilat, the tallest sand dune in Europe, within its territory.

Geography

The commune covers a large area including the Île aux Oiseaux in the basin, the resort of Pyla-sur-Mer, and part of the Landes forest. The Dune du Pilat, over 100 metres high, draws around two million visitors a year and is gradually migrating inland.

Economy

Oyster culture in the Arcachon Basin has been centred on the ports of La Teste since the 19th century, alongside tourism, boatbuilding and a French Air and Space Force base (BA 120 Cazaux).
