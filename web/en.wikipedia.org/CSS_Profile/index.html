CSS Profile

The CSS Profile, short for the College Scholarship Service Profile, is an online financial aid application administered by the College Board and used by roughly 200 colleges, universities and scholarship programs in the United States to award institutional (non-federal) financial aid.

Unlike the federal FAFSA, which is free, the CSS Profile charges a fee per institution (with fee waivers for low-income applicants) and asks more detailed questions. It collects information the FAFSA does not, such as home equity, the income and assets of a noncustodial parent, and family medical or private-school expenses. Schools use its methodology—sometimes called institutional methodology—to calculate a family's expected contribution toward their own aid packages, which often makes the Profile's estimate of need lower than the federal one.

History

The College Scholarship Service was founded in 1954 by a group of private colleges seeking a common, consistent way to assess financial need; the paper form it produced eventually became the online CSS Profile, launched in its modern form in the 2000s.
