List of colleges in Canada

In Canada, "college" generally refers to a post-secondary institution offering diplomas, certificates, and applied degrees, distinct from research universities. Ontario's 24 Colleges of Applied Arts and Technology, Quebec's CEGEPs, and similar institutions in other provinces form the college system. Notable colleges by province are listed below.
