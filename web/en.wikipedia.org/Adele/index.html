Adele Laurie Blue Adkins (born 5 May 1988), known mononymously as Adele, is an English singer-songwriter. She is one of the world's best-selling music artists, with sales of over 120 million records worldwide, and is known for her powerful mezzo-soprano vocals and emotionally direct songwriting, much of it drawn from her own relationships.

After graduating from the BRIT School in 2006, Adele signed to XL Recordings after a demo posted on MySpace attracted attention. Her debut album 19 (2008) produced the hit "Chasing Pavements" and won her the Grammy Awards for Best New Artist and Best Female Pop Vocal Performance. Her second album, 21 (2011), featuring "Rolling in the Deep", "Someone like You" and "Set Fire to the Rain", became one of the best-selling albums of all time and won six Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year.

Later career

She recorded the theme song "Skyfall" for the 2012 James Bond film, winning an Academy Award and a Golden Globe. Her third album, 25 (2015), led by the single "Hello", broke first-week sales records in the UK and US and again won Album of the Year at the Grammys. Her fourth album, 30 (2021), included the number-one single "Easy on Me". In 2022 she began a Las Vegas residency, "Weekends with Adele", at Caesars Palace.

Artistry and legacy

Adele cites the Spice Girls, Lauryn Hill, Mary J. Blige and the Cure among her influences. Critics praise the emotional authenticity of her ballads and her live singing; she is frequently ranked among the greatest vocalists of her generation and one of the most influential British cultural figures.
