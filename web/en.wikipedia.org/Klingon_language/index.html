Klingon language

Created by: Marc Okrand
Date: 1984
Setting: Star Trek
ISO 639: tlh

The Klingon language (Klingon: tlhIngan Hol) is a constructed language spoken by the Klingons, a fictional alien warrior race in the Star Trek franchise. It was developed by linguist Marc Okrand for the 1984 film Star Trek III: The Search for Spock.

History

Actor James Doohan devised a few guttural phrases for Star Trek: The Motion Picture (1979); Okrand expanded these into a full language with consistent grammar for the third film. Okrand published The Klingon Dictionary in 1985. The language has an unusual object–verb–subject word order, agglutinative morphology, and sounds chosen to feel alien to English speakers.

Speakers and culture

A small community of enthusiasts speaks Klingon fluently; the Klingon Language Institute, founded in 1992, promotes its study. Works such as Hamlet and the Epic of Gilgamesh have been translated into Klingon, and annual conferences (qep'a') are held. The language has its own ISO 639 code, tlh.
