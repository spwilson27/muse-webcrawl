Nosso Lar (Portuguese for "Our Home") is a 1944 novel by the Brazilian medium Francisco Cândido "Chico" Xavier, presented within Spiritism as the psychographed account of the spirit André Luiz. It is the first and best known of the Life in the Spirit World series and one of the most widely read Spiritist books in Brazil, describing life after death in a spiritual colony suspended above Rio de Janeiro.

Plot

André Luiz, a physician who lived self-indulgently, dies and wakes in a dark, desolate region of the spirit world known as the Umbral, where he suffers for eight years. Rescued by the benevolent spirit Clarêncio, he is taken to Nosso Lar, an organised colony of the afterlife with ministries for tasks such as regeneration, communication, and enlightenment. There he recovers, studies, works, and learns the principles of reincarnation and moral progress, eventually earning the right to visit his earthly family.

Reception and adaptations

The book became a phenomenon in Brazil, where Spiritism counts millions of adherents, and has been translated into several languages, including an English edition titled Nosso Lar: Life in a Spiritist Colony. A film adaptation, Nosso Lar (also released as Astral City: A Spiritual Journey), directed by Wagner de Assis, premiered in 2010 and became one of the highest-grossing Brazilian films of its era; a sequel, Nosso Lar 2: Os Mensageiros, followed in 2024.
