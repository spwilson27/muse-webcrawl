Boracay

Location: Malay, Aklan, Western Visayas, Philippines

Area: about 10.3 km²

Known for: White Beach; powdery white sand

Access: via Caticlan airport and jetty port

Boracay is a small resort island in the Philippines, about 0.8 kilometres off the northwest coast of Panay Island in the municipality of Malay, Aklan. Renowned for the four-kilometre White Beach, lined with resorts, restaurants, and bars, it has repeatedly been ranked among the world's best islands by travel publications.

Tourism development

Tourism took off in the 1970s and 1980s, and by the 2010s the island received around two million visitors a year. Bulabog Beach on the eastern side became a centre for kiteboarding and windsurfing, hosting the Asian Windsurfing Tour.

2018 closure and rehabilitation

In April 2018 President Rodrigo Duterte, calling the island a "cesspool" because of untreated sewage and environmental violations, ordered Boracay closed to tourists for six months. A rehabilitation programme repaired sewerage, demolished illegal structures, imposed a visitor cap and new rules (including bans on single-use plastics and beach drinking). The island reopened in October 2018 with a stricter, sustainability-oriented regime.
