Prester John

Prester John was a legendary Christian patriarch and king said to rule over a vast, wealthy Christian kingdom in the East, beyond the Islamic world. Tales of Prester John circulated in Europe from the 12th to the 17th century and spurred exploration and diplomacy.

Origins

The legend emerged around 1145, when Bishop Hugh of Jabala reported to the papal court that a Christian king-priest named John had defeated the Persians. A famous forged "Letter of Prester John" (c. 1165) described his fabulous realm of wonders, including rivers of gold and the Fountain of Youth.

Search for the kingdom

At various times the kingdom was located in Central Asia, India, or Ethiopia. European explorers and missionaries sought Prester John as an ally against Muslim powers; Portuguese expeditions to Ethiopia in the 15th and 16th centuries were partly motivated by the search. As geographical knowledge grew, the legend faded.
