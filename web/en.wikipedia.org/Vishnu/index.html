Vishnu

Vishnu is one of the principal deities of Hinduism, the preserver in the Trimurti with Brahma the creator and Shiva the destroyer. He is the supreme being in Vaishnavism.

AffiliationTrimurti, Deva
AbodeVaikuntha
ConsortLakshmi
VehicleGaruda
SymbolsShankha (conch), Sudarshana Chakra, Kaumodaki (mace), Padma (lotus)

Avatars

Vishnu descends to restore dharma as avatars, traditionally ten (Dashavatara): Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama (hero of the Ramayana), Krishna (central to the Mahabharata and Bhagavad Gita), Buddha (in many lists) and Kalki, yet to come.

Iconography and worship

He is shown blue-skinned, four-armed, reclining on the serpent Shesha or standing with Lakshmi. Major temples include Tirupati Venkateswara, Srirangam and Badrinath. Festivals include Vaikuntha Ekadashi and Janmashtami for Krishna.
