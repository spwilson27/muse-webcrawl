Meccano

Meccano is a brand of model construction system created in 1901 by Frank Hornby in Liverpool, England. The system consists of reusable metal strips, plates, angle girders, wheels, axles and gears, with nuts and bolts to connect the pieces.

History

Hornby patented his invention, initially called "Mechanics Made Easy", in 1901; the name Meccano was registered in 1907. Meccano Ltd. manufactured the sets in Liverpool until 1979. Production later moved to France under successive owners, and the brand was acquired by the Canadian company Spin Master in 2013.

The system

Meccano parts use a standard ½-inch hole spacing and Whitworth-thread screws, allowing models ranging from simple cranes to complex clocks and working machinery. Related systems include the American Erector Set and the Czech Merkur. Meccano inspired generations of engineers and remains in production with both classic-style and plastic "Meccano Junior" sets.
