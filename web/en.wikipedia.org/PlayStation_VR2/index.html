The PlayStation VR2 (PS VR2) is a virtual reality headset for the PlayStation 5 developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Released on 22 February 2023 as the successor to the 2016 PlayStation VR, it connects to the console with a single USB-C cable and requires no external camera or processor unit.

Hardware

The headset uses Fresnel lenses in front of OLED panels giving 2000×2040 resolution per eye, a field of view of about 110 degrees, and refresh rates of 90 or 120 Hz. Four cameras on the headset provide inside-out tracking, while an infrared camera tracks the user's eyes, enabling gaze-based interaction and foveated rendering. Headset feedback — a subtle vibration motor — and the accompanying Sense controllers, which incorporate adaptive triggers and haptic feedback adapted from the DualSense, add tactile effects. The Sense controllers are tracked by the headset without the PlayStation Camera used by its predecessor.

Software

Launch titles included Horizon Call of the Mountain, and updates added VR modes to games such as Gran Turismo 7 and Resident Evil Village. The headset is not backward compatible with original PS VR games, a decision Sony attributed to its different tracking and rendering architecture. In August 2024 Sony released a PC adapter allowing the headset to be used with SteamVR on Windows, though several features such as eye tracking and adaptive triggers were not supported on PC at launch.

Reception and sales

Critics praised the display quality, eye tracking and simplified setup, while criticising the high price and limited exclusive library. Sales started slowly relative to Sony's expectations and production was reportedly paused in early 2024 amid unsold inventory; price reductions followed in several markets.
