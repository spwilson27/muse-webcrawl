Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi (born 1 September 1945) is a Yemeni politician and former military officer who served as President of Yemen from 2012 until 2022. He previously served as Vice President from 1994 to 2012 under President Ali Abdullah Saleh.

Hadi was born in Thukain in the Abyan Governorate of southern Yemen. He received military training in South Arabia, Egypt, and the Soviet Union, and rose through the ranks of the armed forces of South Yemen before unification in 1990. During the 1994 civil war he sided with the north, and Saleh appointed him Vice President in October 1994.

Presidency

Following the 2011 Yemeni revolution, Saleh transferred power to Hadi under a Gulf Cooperation Council-brokered agreement. Hadi was confirmed as president in a February 2012 referendum in which he was the sole candidate. His government faced an insurgency by the Houthi movement (Ansar Allah), which seized the capital, Sanaa, in September 2014. Hadi resigned in January 2015 under house arrest, later escaping to Aden and then to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. A Saudi-led coalition intervened militarily in March 2015 to restore his government, beginning a prolonged civil war.

Hadi's internationally recognised government operated largely from Aden and from exile in Riyadh. In April 2022, he transferred power to a new Presidential Leadership Council headed by Rashad al-Alimi.
