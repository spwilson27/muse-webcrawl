We Have Always Lived in the Castle (film)

We Have Always Lived in the Castle is a 2018 American mystery thriller film directed by Stacie Passon and based on the 1962 novel of the same name by Shirley Jackson. It stars Taissa Farmiga, Alexandra Daddario, Crispin Glover, and Sebastian Stan.

Plot

Director: Stacie Passon
Based on: We Have Always Lived in the Castle by Shirley Jackson
Starring: Taissa Farmiga, Alexandra Daddario, Crispin Glover, Sebastian Stan
Release: 2018 (LA Film Festival); 2019 (wide)

Sisters Mary Katherine "Merricat" Blackwood (Farmiga) and Constance Blackwood (Daddario) live in isolation in their family's mansion with their wheelchair-bound Uncle Julian (Glover), six years after the rest of the family died of arsenic poisoning—a crime for which Constance was acquitted but which the local villagers still hold against them. Their fragile seclusion is disrupted by the arrival of their cousin Charles (Stan), whose charm masks an interest in the family fortune, with violent consequences.

Production and release

The adaptation was written by Mark Kruger. The film premiered at the Los Angeles Film Festival in September 2018 and was released in theaters and on video on demand in May 2019.

Reception

The film received generally favorable reviews, with critics praising its gothic atmosphere and the performances of Farmiga and Daddario, though some noted it softened the novel's strangeness.
