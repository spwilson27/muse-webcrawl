Gatwick Airport (IATA: LGW), also known as London Gatwick, is a major international airport near Crawley, West Sussex, England, around 47 km south of central London. It is the second-busiest airport in the United Kingdom by total passenger traffic, after Heathrow, and was for many years the world's busiest single-runway airport.

History

The site began as an aerodrome in the late 1920s beside a racecourse. It was requisitioned by the Royal Air Force during the Second World War and rebuilt as a commercial airport in the 1950s, reopening in 1958. The North Terminal opened in 1988, and a second terminal's expansion has steadily increased capacity.

Operations

Gatwick is a major base for easyJet and also serves British Airways, TUI Airways, Norwegian and many other carriers, with a strong focus on European short-haul and leisure routes alongside long-haul services. The airport is connected to London by the Gatwick Express and other rail services from its own railway station.
