Procter & Gamble

Procter & Gamble (P&G) is an American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candle-maker William Procter and soap-maker James Gamble, it is one of the world's largest makers of household and personal care products.

Brands

Major brands include Pampers, Tide, Ariel, Gillette, Oral-B, Crest, Pantene, Olay, Head & Shoulders, Bounty, Charmin, Febreze, and Dawn. The company divested its food brands, selling Pringles to Kellogg's in 2012.

History

P&G grew supplying soap and candles to the Union Army, pioneered brand management in the 1930s, sponsored radio "soap operas," and expanded globally through acquisitions including Richardson-Vicks (1985), Tambrands (1997), and Gillette (2005). It is a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average.
