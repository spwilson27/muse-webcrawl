Nirmal Hriday

Nirmal Hriday (Bengali for "pure heart"), formerly known as Mother Teresa's Kalighat Home for the Dying, is a hospice for the sick, destitute, and dying established by Mother Teresa in Kalighat, Kolkata, India, in 1952.

History

The home occupies a former rest house attached to the Kalighat Kali Temple, offered to Mother Teresa by the municipal authorities. It was the first home founded by the Missionaries of Charity, and its early work among the dying collected from Kolkata's streets drew both admiration and criticism regarding the standard of medical care provided.

Work

Run by Missionaries of Charity sisters and volunteers, the home provides shelter, food, basic nursing, and palliation to people who would otherwise die alone on the streets. Since its founding, tens of thousands of men, women, and children have been taken in, roughly half of whom died there.
