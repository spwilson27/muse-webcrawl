Resolute, Nunavut

Resolute (Inuktitut: ᖁᓯᖅᑑᖅ, Qausuittuq, "place with no dawn") is an Inuit hamlet on Cornwallis Island in the Qikiqtani Region of Nunavut, Canada. It is one of the northernmost communities in Canada and a key staging point for travel and research in the High Arctic, including expeditions to the North Pole.

History

The community is named after HMS Resolute, an Arctic exploration vessel abandoned in the ice nearby in 1854. A Royal Canadian Air Force base and weather station were established in 1947. In 1953, Inuit families from Inukjuak, Quebec, and Pond Inlet were relocated to Resolute (and Grise Fiord) by the Canadian government during the High Arctic relocation, an episode for which the government formally apologised in 2010.

Transport

Resolute Bay Airport, with a gravel runway, links the hamlet to Iqaluit and southern Canada and serves as a hub for charter flights across the High Arctic. The community is supplied annually by sealift during the short summer.
