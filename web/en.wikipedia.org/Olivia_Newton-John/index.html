Dame Olivia Newton-John AC DBE (26 September 1948 – 8 August 2022) was a British-Australian singer, songwriter and actress. One of the best-selling music artists of all time, she won four Grammy Awards and had numerous hit singles, including "Physical", "I Honestly Love You", and "Magic".

Career

Raised in Melbourne, Newton-John first found success in Britain and represented the United Kingdom at the 1974 Eurovision Song Contest. She became a major star in the United States with soft-rock and country hits before starring opposite John Travolta in the film adaptation of Grease (1978), one of the most successful movie musicals ever. Her 1981 single "Physical" spent ten weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100.

Activism and later life

After a breast cancer diagnosis in 1992, she became a prominent advocate for cancer research and founded the Olivia Newton-John Cancer Wellness & Research Centre in Melbourne. She died of metastatic breast cancer in 2022.
