Wroclaw is the spelling, without the Polish diacritic "ł", of Wrocław, a city in southwestern Poland on the Oder River. The plain-ASCII rendering is commonly used in international and technical contexts where diacritics cannot be reproduced.

Overview

Wrocław is the capital of the Lower Silesian Voivodeship and the largest city in Silesia, with a population of roughly 674,000. Founded over a thousand years ago, it has been part of Poland, Bohemia, Austria, Prussia, and Germany — under the name Breslau — before returning to Poland in 1945.

Highlights

The city is known for its vast Market Square, the Gothic cathedral on Ostrów Tumski island, the UNESCO-listed Centennial Hall, and the Panorama of the Battle of Racławice. It hosted matches of UEFA Euro 2012 and was European Capital of Culture in 2016. Its universities, including the University of Wrocław (founded 1702), make it a major academic centre.
