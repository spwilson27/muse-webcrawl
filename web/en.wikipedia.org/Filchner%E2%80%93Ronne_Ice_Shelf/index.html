Filchner–Ronne Ice Shelf

LocationWeddell Sea, Antarctica
Area≈ 422,000 km²
Named forWilhelm Filchner; Edith Ronne

The Filchner–Ronne Ice Shelf, also known as the Ronne–Filchner Ice Shelf, is an Antarctic ice shelf bordering the Weddell Sea. It is the second largest ice shelf in the world after the Ross Ice Shelf, covering roughly 422,000 square kilometres.

Geography

The shelf is divided by Berkner Island into the eastern Filchner Ice Shelf and the larger western Ronne Ice Shelf. It is fed by several large ice streams draining the East and West Antarctic ice sheets, including the Recovery, Slessor, and Rutford ice streams.

Research

The shelf is a focus of climate research because warm ocean water intruding beneath it could accelerate melting and sea-level rise. Several seasonal research stations have operated on or near it, including the former Filchner Station.
