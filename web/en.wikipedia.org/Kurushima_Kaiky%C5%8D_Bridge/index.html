Kurushima Kaikyō Bridge
LocationImabari, Ehime, Japan
Total length4,105 m (three linked bridges)
Opened1999

The Kurushima Kaikyō Bridge is a suspension bridge structure spanning the Kurushima Strait in Japan's Seto Inland Sea, linking the island of Ōshima with Shikoku at Imabari, Ehime. It consists of three successive suspension bridges — the First, Second, and Third Kurushima-Kaikyō Bridges — with a combined length of about 4.1 km.

Construction

Opened in 1999 as part of the Honshū–Shikoku Bridge Project's Nishiseto Expressway, it was the world's longest suspension bridge structure at completion. The strait's strong tidal currents required extensive marine engineering. The expressway, popular with cyclists as part of the Shimanami Kaidō, includes lanes for pedestrians and bicycles.
