The 2022 Formula One World Championship was the 73rd season of Formula One motor racing, contested over twenty-two Grands Prix. Max Verstappen defended his Drivers' Championship in dominant fashion, clinching the title at the Japanese Grand Prix with four races to spare, while Red Bull Racing won the Constructors' Championship for the first time since 2013.

New regulations

The season introduced the most sweeping technical rule changes in a generation: ground-effect aerodynamics returned, wheels grew to 18 inches, and a budget cap was tightened, all intended to promote closer racing. Ferrari began strongly, Charles Leclerc winning two of the first three races, but a combination of Red Bull's development pace, Ferrari reliability failures and strategy errors swung the championship decisively to Verstappen, who won a record fifteen races.

Season highlights

The calendar included the debut Miami Grand Prix. George Russell took his first victory in Brazil, Mercedes' only win of the season, while Kevin Magnussen scored a shock pole position for Haas at the same event. The championship confirmation at Suzuka was briefly confused by the awarding of full points for a race shortened by heavy rain — the result of a loophole in the new points rules — before officials confirmed Verstappen as champion.

Cost cap controversy

In October 2022 the FIA announced that Red Bull had committed a minor breach of the 2021 financial regulations; the team accepted a $7 million fine and a reduction in aerodynamic testing allowance. The 2022 season was also the last for four-time champion Sebastian Vettel, who retired at the end of the year.
