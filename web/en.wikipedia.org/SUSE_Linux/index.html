SUSE Linux is a family of Linux distributions built on the Linux kernel and developed by the German company SUSE. The name originally stood for Software- und System-Entwicklung ("software and systems development"). Its mascot is a green chameleon named Geeko.

Products

The flagship commercial product is SUSE Linux Enterprise Server (SLES), widely used on mainframes, servers and in SAP environments. The community distribution openSUSE — in its rolling-release Tumbleweed and point-release Leap forms — is developed openly and shares much of its codebase with the enterprise products. SUSE is also known for the YaST configuration tool and the Open Build Service.

History

Founded in Nuremberg in 1992, SUSE released its first Linux distribution in 1994, initially based on Slackware and later on Jurix. The company was acquired by Novell in 2003, later passed to Attachmate and Micro Focus, and became independent again in 2019 before listing on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in 2021.
