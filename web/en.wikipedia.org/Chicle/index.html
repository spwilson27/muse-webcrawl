Chicle

Chicle is a natural latex gum obtained from the sapodilla tree (Manilkara zapota) of Central America, tapped much like rubber. It was the traditional base of chewing gum.

Thomas Adams commercialised chicle gum in the United States in the 19th century after importing it from Mexico, where it had been chewed since Maya times. Most modern gum bases are synthetic, but chicle has seen a revival in natural products.
