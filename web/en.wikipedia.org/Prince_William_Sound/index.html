Prince William Sound

LocationGulf of Alaska, south coast of Alaska, USA
Areaapprox. 9,000 km² of water
SettlementsValdez, Cordova, Whittier

Prince William Sound is a sound of the Gulf of Alaska on the south coast of the U.S. state of Alaska. It is located on the east side of the Kenai Peninsula, bounded by the Chugach Mountains, and includes numerous islands, fjords, and tidewater glaciers such as Columbia Glacier.

History

The sound was named by George Vancouver in 1794 for Prince William, later King William IV. James Cook had entered it in 1778. The port of Valdez is the southern terminus of the Trans-Alaska Pipeline.

Exxon Valdez oil spill

On 24 March 1989, the oil tanker Exxon Valdez ran aground on Bligh Reef, spilling roughly 11 million US gallons of crude oil — one of the most devastating environmental disasters in maritime history. The spill harmed wildlife populations across the sound, and recovery efforts continued for decades.
