Golmaal (film series)

Golmaal is an Indian Hindi-language comedy film series directed by Rohit Shetty and produced by Shetty together with Dhilin Mehta. The series stars Ajay Devgn, Arshad Warsi, Tusshar Kapoor and Shreyas Talpade in recurring ensemble roles, and is among the most commercially successful comedy franchises in Indian cinema.

Films

The series began with Golmaal: Fun Unlimited (2006), in which four friends take shelter in the home of a blind couple while fleeing a creditor. It was followed by Golmaal Returns (2008); Golmaal 3 (2010), one of the highest-grossing Indian films of its year and the first Hindi film to cross the ₹100 crore mark in its initial run in the franchise's style of slapstick ensemble comedy; and Golmaal Again (2017), which added a supernatural horror-comedy angle and became one of the biggest hits of the year in India. A fifth film, Golmaal 5, has been announced.

Characters and style

The films revolve around the exploits of Gopal (Devgn), Madhav (Warsi), Lucky (Kapoor) — a character who is mute in the early films and communicates in comical sounds — and Laxman (Talpade and others). Kareena Kapoor Khan, Kunal Khemu, Tabu and Parineeti Chopra have also appeared. The series is known for slapstick humour, colourful sets, action sequences involving cars, and Rohit Shetty's signature style.

Other media

An animated series, Golmaal Jr., based on the child versions of the characters, premiered on Sonic Nickelodeon in 2019.
