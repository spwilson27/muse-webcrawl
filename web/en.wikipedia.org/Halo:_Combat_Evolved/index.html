Halo: Combat Evolved is a 2001 first-person shooter video game developed by Bungie and published by Microsoft Game Studios. It was released as a launch title for the Xbox console on November 15, 2001. The game is set in the 26th century, with the player controlling Master Chief, a cybernetically enhanced supersoldier, assisted by the artificial intelligence Cortana, as humanity fights the alien Covenant on the mysterious ringworld Halo.

Gameplay

Halo popularised many features that became standard in console shooters, including a two-weapon carry limit, recharging energy shields, grenades and melee on dedicated buttons, and drivable vehicles. Its enemy artificial intelligence and open-ended encounters were widely praised.

Development and release

The project began as a real-time strategy game for Mac and Windows before evolving into a third-person and then first-person shooter. Microsoft acquired Bungie in 2000, and Halo became the flagship title demonstrating the Xbox hardware.

Reception and legacy

The game was a critical and commercial success, selling millions of copies and launching the Halo franchise, which expanded into sequels, novels, films, and other media. A high-definition remake, Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary, was released in 2011, and the original was later included in Halo: The Master Chief Collection.
