Hornsea Wind Farm is a series of offshore wind farms in the North Sea, off the east coast of England, developed by Ørsted. Hornsea 1 and Hornsea 2 were each, on completion, the largest offshore wind farms in the world.

Hornsea 1

Located about 120 km off the Yorkshire coast, Hornsea 1 comprises 174 Siemens Gamesa 7 MW turbines with a capacity of 1,218 MW, covering some 407 km². It generated first power in 2019 and was fully commissioned in 2020, supplying over a million homes.

Hornsea 2

Hornsea 2, adjacent to the first phase, has 165 turbines and 1,386 MW capacity; fully operational in August 2022, it surpassed Hornsea 1 as the world's largest offshore wind farm. Operations are run from a hub at Grimsby.

Hornsea 3 and 4

Hornsea 3 won a UK Contract for Difference in 2022 and received consent for up to about 2.9 GW, which would make it among the largest wind farms of any kind. A fourth zone, Hornsea 4, has also been consented.
