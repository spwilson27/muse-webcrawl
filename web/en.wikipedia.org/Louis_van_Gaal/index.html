Louis van Gaal

Aloysius Paulus Maria van Gaal (born 8 August 1951) is a Dutch former football manager and player. Regarded as one of the most accomplished managers of his era, he won league titles in the Netherlands, Spain and Germany.

Managerial career

Van Gaal rose to prominence with Ajax, winning three Eredivisie titles and the 1995 UEFA Champions League with a famously young side. He then managed Barcelona (winning La Liga twice), AZ Alkmaar (Eredivisie champions in 2009), Bayern Munich (Bundesliga champions in 2010), and Manchester United (FA Cup winners in 2016).

He managed the Netherlands national team in three separate spells; his third spell took the Dutch to the quarter-finals of the 2022 FIFA World Cup, after which he stepped down.
