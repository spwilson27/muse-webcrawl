Google Stadia

Developer: Google

Launch date: November 19, 2019

Discontinued: January 18, 2023

Type: Cloud gaming service

Stadia was a cloud gaming service developed and operated by Google. Known in development as Project Stream, the service debuted through a closed beta running Assassin's Creed Odyssey in October 2018. Stadia publicly launched on November 19, 2019, in 22 countries initially, and received features for real-time streaming games at 60 frames per second.

Service

Because games ran on Google's data center hardware, users needed only a compatible screen — a television with Chromecast Ultra, a computer running Chrome, or a supported phone — rather than a local console or gaming PC. The Stadia controller connected directly over Wi-Fi to Google's servers to reduce latency.

Closure

Google announced on September 29, 2022, that Stadia would shut down, citing a failure to gain traction with users; the service closed on January 18, 2023. Google refunded all hardware and game purchases made through its store, and released a Bluetooth update for the Stadia controller so it could be used with other devices.
