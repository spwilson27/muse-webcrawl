The award honours the top first-year player in the NBA.

The National Basketball Association's Rookie of the Year is an annual award given since the 1952–53 season to the top rookie of the regular season. The winner receives a trophy renamed in 2022 for Wilt Chamberlain, himself a winner in 1960 — one of two players, along with Wes Unseld, to win Rookie of the Year and Most Valuable Player in the same season.

Selection

A panel of sportswriters and broadcasters from the United States and Canada votes by ranked ballot, with points awarded for first-, second- and third-place selections. The winner is the player with the most total points. In 2002–03 Amare Stoudemire became the first player drafted directly out of high school to win the award, and several winners were selected outside the draft lottery, including Malcolm Brogdon, a second-round pick who won in 2017. A number of international players have won, among them Pau Gasol, Luka Dončić and Victor Wembanyama, the 2024 winner.

Notable winners

Winners who became all-time greats include Oscar Robertson, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Larry Bird, Michael Jordan, Shaquille O'Neal, Tim Duncan, LeBron James and Kevin Durant. Co-winners have been declared three times, most recently in 2000 with Elton Brand and Steve Francis.
