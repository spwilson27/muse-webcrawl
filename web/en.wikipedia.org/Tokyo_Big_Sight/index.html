Tokyo Big Sight (東京ビッグサイト, Tōkyō Biggu Saito), officially known as the Tokyo International Exhibition Center, is a convention and exhibition center in Tokyo, Japan, and the largest such venue in the country. Opened in April 1996, it is located in the Ariake district of Kōtō Ward on Tokyo Bay. Its most iconic feature is the visually distinctive Conference Tower, composed of four inverted pyramids mounted on huge supports.

Construction and facilities

The venue was commissioned by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Bureau of Finance and built on reclaimed land in Tokyo Bay. The center includes the Conference Tower, East and West Exhibition Halls, and (from 2019) the South Exhibition Halls, plus the nearby Ariake GYM-EX facility converted from the Olympic gymnastics arena. The total exhibition area exceeds 100,000 square metres.

Events

Tokyo Big Sight hosts major trade fairs and public events, most famously Comiket (Comic Market), the world's largest dōjinshi fair, which moved to the venue in 1996 and draws hundreds of thousands of visitors. Other events include the Tokyo Motor Show (held here from 2011), Tokyo Game Show in earlier years, Design Festa, trade exhibitions, and concerts.

2020 Olympics

During the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games (held in 2021), the venue served as the International Broadcast Centre and Main Press Centre. The gymnastics events were held at the adjacent Ariake Gymnastics Centre.

Access

The center is served by Tokyo Big Sight Station on the Yurikamome line and Kokusai-tenjijō Station on the Rinkai Line.
