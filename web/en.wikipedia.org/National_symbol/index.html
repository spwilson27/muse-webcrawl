National symbol

A national symbol is a symbol of any entity considering and manifesting itself to the world as a national community: the sovereign states but also nations in loose meaning of ethnic or cultural groups, whether or not sovereign. National symbols help to unite people by creating visual, verbal, or iconic representations of the national people, values, goals, or history.

Common types

Official national symbols commonly include the national flag, coat of arms or national emblem, national anthem, and national motto. Many nations also recognize national animals (such as the bald eagle of the United States or the kangaroo of Australia), national birds, national flowers or trees, national colours, and national personifications (such as Marianne for France or Britannia for Britain).

Role and significance

National symbols are displayed at ceremonies, sporting events, and official occasions, and are protected to varying degrees by law. They communicate identity and continuity, and may become focal points of patriotic sentiment or political contest — for example, in debates over flag desecration or the adoption of new symbols after political change.
