Krasnodar Krai
Country: Russia
Administrative centre: Krasnodar
Area: about 75,500 km²
Population: about 5.8 million

Krasnodar Krai (Russian: Краснодарский край) is a federal subject of Russia in the North Caucasus, on the Black Sea coast. It is the most populous krai in Russia and the third most populous federal subject overall.

Geography

The krai covers the Kuban lowland north of the western Caucasus Mountains and a narrow Black Sea coastal strip. It enclaves the Republic of Adygea. Major cities include Krasnodar, the Black Sea resorts of Sochi and Anapa, the port of Novorossiysk, and Armavir.

Economy

Krasnodar Krai is a major agricultural region (grain, sunflowers, wine), a transport and oil-refining hub, and Russia's principal domestic tourism region. Sochi hosted the 2014 Winter Olympics.
