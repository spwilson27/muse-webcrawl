Derby della Mole

The Derby della Mole, also known as the Turin derby (Italian: Derby di Torino), is the local football derby in Turin, Italy, between Juventus and Torino. It is named after the Mole Antonelliana, one of the city's best-known landmarks. The fixture is among the oldest derbies in Italian football, with roots in the class division of the city: Juventus were historically supported by the middle classes and associated with the Agnelli family and the FIAT industry, while Torino drew support from the working class and older, locally born residents.

History

Torino were founded in 1906 by dissidents from Juventus, giving the rivalry a personal origin. The first official match between the clubs was played in January 1907. Torino dominated the rivalry in the 1940s with the Grande Torino side, whose players were killed in the Superga air disaster of 1949. Juventus, by far the more successful club nationally, have generally held the upper hand in the derby since the 1990s, though matches remain fiercely contested.

Recent decades

Torino's relegation battles meant the derby was not played in several seasons, but it remains one of Italy's most storied city rivalries, regularly filling the Juventus Stadium or the Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino.
