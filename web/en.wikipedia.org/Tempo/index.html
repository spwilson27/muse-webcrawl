Tempo

In music, tempo (Italian for "time") is the speed or pace of a given composition. Tempo is typically indicated by a metronome marking in beats per minute (BPM), or by a traditional tempo marking, often an Italian word such as allegro or andante.

Measurement

Since the early 19th century, tempo has commonly been specified with Mälzel's metronome, invented around 1815 and popularized by Beethoven, the first major composer to publish metronome markings. Terms such as ritardando (slowing) and accelerando (speeding up) describe changes of tempo within a piece.
