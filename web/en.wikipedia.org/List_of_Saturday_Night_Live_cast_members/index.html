List of Saturday Night Live cast members

Saturday Night Live (SNL) is an American late-night live sketch comedy and variety show created by Lorne Michaels, premiering on NBC on October 11, 1975. The show's cast is divided into repertory players and featured players. Kenan Thompson is the longest-tenured cast member in the show's history.
