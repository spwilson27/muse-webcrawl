An imageboard (or image board) is a type of Internet forum that focuses on the posting of images, often alongside text and discussion. Imageboards emerged in Japan in the late 1990s and are characterised by anonymous posting and threads that are deleted as new content pushes them out.

History

The concept developed from Japanese textboards such as 2channel and imageboards such as Futaba Channel (2chan), whose software and layout inspired the English-language 4chan, founded in 2003.

Characteristics

Most imageboards do not require user registration, identify posters only by thread-specific IDs or not at all, and organise discussion into topic boards. Their culture has been influential in the spread of Internet memes, and controversial because of weak moderation and anonymity.
