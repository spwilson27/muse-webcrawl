SystemImperial / US customary

Exact value0.9144 metres; 3 feet; 36 inches

The yard (symbol: yd) is an English unit of length in both the British imperial and US customary systems of measurement. Since 1959 it has been defined internationally as exactly 0.9144 metres, and it equals 3 feet or 36 inches.

History

The yard's origins are uncertain; it may derive from a measure based on the human body or from early standards kept in medieval England. Henry I is traditionally said to have fixed it as the distance from his nose to his thumb. Successive legal standards were maintained in London until the international agreement of 1959 aligned the imperial and US yards.

Current use

The yard remains common in the United States for fabric, landscaping, and sports — American football fields are measured in yards — and appears in the United Kingdom in some contexts, though the metre is the official unit in most countries.
