The Greens–European Free Alliance (Greens/EFA) is a political group of the European Parliament. It brings together two distinct European political alliances: the green parties of the European Green Party and the autonomist and regionalist parties of the European Free Alliance, alongside a number of independent MEPs and parties such as the European Pirate Party.

History

The group was formed in 1999, when the European Green Party's MEPs, previously sitting in the Green Group, joined with the European Free Alliance, which had sat in the European Radical Alliance. The merger gave the combined group greater weight in the Parliament and it has been maintained through successive terms since.

The group reached its largest size after the 2019 European elections, with 74 MEPs, buoyed by strong results for green parties in Germany, France and elsewhere. In the 2024 elections the group shrank, to 53 seats, amid a wider decline in green support.

Positions

Greens/EFA campaigns on climate action and environmental protection, social justice, civil liberties and digital rights, transparency in EU institutions, and the cultural and political rights of stateless nations and regions. Its co-presidents are elected from its ranks of MEPs and have included Daniel Cohn-Bendit, Rebecca Harms, Philippe Lamberts, Ska Keller, Terry Reintke and Bas Eickhout.

Composition

Member parties have included the German Alliance 90/The Greens, the French Europe Écologie, the Scottish National Party (until Brexit removed its MEPs in 2020), the Catalan and Corsican autonomists, and Pirate parties from several member states.
