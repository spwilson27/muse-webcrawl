Battlefield 1942

DeveloperDigital Illusions CE (DICE)
PublisherElectronic Arts
Release2002
GenreFirst-person shooter

Battlefield 1942 is a first-person shooter video game developed by DICE and published by Electronic Arts in 2002. Set in the Second World War, it launched the Battlefield series and popularised large-scale multiplayer battles combining infantry combat with tanks, aircraft, and ships.

Gameplay

Matches pit Axis against Allied teams on maps recreating theatres such as Wake Island, El Alamein, Iwo Jima, and Stalingrad. Players choose classes including assault, medic, engineer, and anti-tank, and fight to control spawn points in the signature Conquest mode, supporting up to 64 players.

Legacy

Two expansion packs, The Road to Rome and Secret Weapons of WWII, followed, along with a large modding community. The game received critical acclaim and numerous awards, and its formula defined the series for two decades.
