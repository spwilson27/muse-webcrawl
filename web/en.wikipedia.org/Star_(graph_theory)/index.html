Star (graph theory)

In graph theory, a star Sk is the complete bipartite graph K1,k: a tree with one internal node and k leaves (for k ≥ 2). A star with 3 edges is called a claw.

Properties

The star Sk has k + 1 vertices and k edges. Its centre has degree k and every leaf has degree 1. Stars are trees, unit distance graphs, and have graph diameter 2. A graph is a star if and only if it is a tree with maximum degree equal to its number of vertices minus one.

Related concepts

Claw-free graphs — graphs containing no induced claw subgraph — have been widely studied. Star networks in computing take their topology from the star graph, with a central hub connected to all nodes.
