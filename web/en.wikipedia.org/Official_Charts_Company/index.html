The Official Charts Company, often abbreviated to Official Charts, is a British inter-professional organisation that compiles various official record charts in the United Kingdom, including the UK Singles Chart, the UK Albums Chart, the UK Singles Downloads Chart, and genre and music video charts. It is jointly owned by the British Phonographic Industry (BPI) and the Entertainment Retailers Association (ERA). Chart data are collected from retailers, digital services, and streaming platforms, with the charts traditionally published on Friday afternoons and broadcast in association with BBC Radio 1.

The Official Charts Company compiles the United Kingdom's record charts.

History

Chart compilation in the UK was historically performed by bodies including the British Market Research Bureau and later Gallup, which introduced computerized compilation in the 1980s. The organisation became the Chart Information Network in 1990 and the Official UK Charts Company in 2001, before shortening its name to the Official Charts Company. In 2014, audio streaming data were incorporated into the UK Singles Chart, and video streams were added in 2018, reflecting changing consumption habits.

Chart methodology

Sales and streaming data are gathered daily from thousands of retailers and digital platforms. Streaming figures are converted into "chart sales" using ratios that differ for premium and ad-funded streams, and rules govern matters such as the number of tracks from one album that may appear in the singles chart. The company also administers the Official Chart rules in consultation with the industry, and publishes an archive of chart history, including every UK number one single since 1952.
