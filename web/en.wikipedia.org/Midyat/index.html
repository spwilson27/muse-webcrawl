Midyat

Midyat (Syriac: ܡܕܝܕܬ, romanized: Mëdyat; Kurdish: Midyad) is a municipality and district of Mardin Province in southeastern Turkey. It is an ancient city in the historical Tur Abdin region, long a centre of Syriac Christianity.

History and culture

Midyat's old town is renowned for its ornate stone houses carved from local limestone, its Syriac Orthodox churches and monasteries, and its silversmiths' filigree work known as telkari. The population historically included Syriac Christians (Assyrians), Kurds, Arabs, and Turks, and the town continues to host one of the largest remaining Syriac communities in Turkey. Nearby stands the Mor Gabriel Monastery, founded in 397 AD and among the oldest surviving monasteries in the world.
