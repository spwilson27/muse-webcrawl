Kellogg Company

Kellogg Company, doing business as Kellogg's, was an American multinational food company headquartered in Battle Creek, Michigan, famous for breakfast cereals including Corn Flakes, Frosted Flakes (Frosties), Rice Krispies, Special K and Froot Loops, as well as snacks such as Pringles and Pop-Tarts.

Founded by Will Keith Kellogg in 1906 from the corn-flake process developed with his brother John Harvey Kellogg at the Battle Creek Sanitarium, the company became a global cereal leader, with mascots Tony the Tiger and Snap, Crackle and Pop.

Split

In 2023 Kellogg Company split into two public companies: WK Kellogg Co (North American cereals) and Kellanova (global snacking, international cereals and noodles), the latter agreeing in 2024 to be acquired by Mars, Incorporated.
