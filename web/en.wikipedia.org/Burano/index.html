Burano

Burano is an island in the Venetian Lagoon, northern Italy, about 7 km from Venice, known for its brightly coloured fishermen's houses and its centuries-old lace-making tradition.

Legend holds that the houses were painted in vivid colours so fishermen could find their homes in fog; today the colours are regulated by the municipality. Burano lace (punto in aria) was prized in European courts from the 16th century, and a lace school founded in 1872 revived the craft.

Sights include the leaning campanile of San Martino church, the Lace Museum, and the house of painter Baldassare Galuppi, the island's famous composer son. Burano is connected by vaporetto to Venice and neighbouring Murano and Torcello.
