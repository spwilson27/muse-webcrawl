Ilya Prigogine (25 January 1917 – 28 May 2003) was a Belgian physical chemist of Russian origin. He received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1977 "for his contributions to non-equilibrium thermodynamics, particularly the theory of dissipative structures".

Work

Prigogine studied at the Université libre de Bruxelles, where he spent most of his career. His research showed that systems far from thermodynamic equilibrium can spontaneously develop ordered structures, which he called dissipative structures, maintained by flows of energy and matter.

Legacy

His ideas influenced the study of self-organisation and complex systems, and he also founded a research centre at the University of Texas at Austin. With Isabelle Stengers he wrote the popular book Order Out of Chaos (1984).
