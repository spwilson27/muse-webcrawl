Court of Quebec

The Court of Quebec (French: Cour du Québec) is a court of first instance in the province of Quebec, Canada, with jurisdiction in civil, criminal, penal, and youth matters. It was created in 1988 by merging the Provincial Court, the Court of Sessions of the Peace, and the Youth Court.

The court is organised into divisions and chambers, including the Civil Division (with a small claims division for claims up to C$15,000), the Criminal and Penal Division, and the Youth Division. Its roughly 300 judges are appointed by the Government of Quebec and are led by a chief judge and senior associate chief judge.

Jurisdiction

In civil matters the court generally hears claims of less than C$85,000, along with municipal and administrative matters assigned by statute. Appeals from its decisions generally go to the Superior Court of Quebec or, for many matters, the Court of Appeal of Quebec.
