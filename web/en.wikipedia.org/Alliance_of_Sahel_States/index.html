Alliance of Sahel States

The Alliance of Sahel States (French: Alliance des États du Sahel, AES) is a confederation formed between Mali, Niger, and Burkina Faso. It originated as a mutual defense pact created on 16 September 2023 following the 2023 Nigerien crisis, in which the three military juntas pledged to defend one another against armed rebellion or external aggression.

History

The three member states had each experienced military coups in the early 2020s and came into conflict with the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS), which threatened military intervention to restore the deposed Nigerien president Mohamed Bazoum after the July 2023 coup. The founding Liptako-Gourma Charter established the alliance as a collective defense framework. The three countries subsequently expelled French forces, drew closer to Russia for security assistance, and in January 2024 jointly announced their withdrawal from ECOWAS.

Confederation

On 6 July 2024, at a summit in Niamey, the leaders of the three states signed a treaty transforming the alliance into a confederation, with plans for deeper economic integration, a common investment bank, coordinated diplomacy, and joint military forces to fight the jihadist insurgencies affecting the central Sahel.
