Warrant officer

A warrant officer (WO) is a rank category in the armed forces of many countries. Warrant officers are highly skilled specialists who receive authority by a warrant from a commissioned officer or a secretary of a military department. In different countries the term covers different positions: in some forces warrant officers form a separate rank class between commissioned officers and non-commissioned officers, while in others they are the senior non-commissioned ranks.

United States

In the United States Armed Forces, warrant officers are technical leaders and specialists who hold ranks from W-1 (warrant officer 1) through CWO-5 (chief warrant officer 5). They are appointed by warrant at the entry grade and commissioned by the President of the United States as chief warrant officers. They serve in fields such as aviation (army helicopter pilots are largely warrant officers), intelligence, engineering and maintenance. The United States Air Force discontinued new warrant officer appointments for decades but announced the reintroduction of the ranks in 2024 for certain technical fields.

United Kingdom and Commonwealth

In the British Armed Forces, warrant officers are the senior non-commissioned ranks: Warrant Officer Class 2 (WO2) and Warrant Officer Class 1 (WO1). The most senior WO1 appointments include the Regimental Sergeant Major and the Academy Sergeant Major at Sandhurst. Similar systems are used in Canada, Australia and New Zealand, where warrant officer ranks also include Chief Warrant Officer and Master Warrant Officer in the Canadian system.
