The Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug (Russian: Яма́ло-Не́нецкий автоно́мный о́круг), also known as Yamalia, is a federal subject of Russia located in northwestern Siberia. It is administratively part of Tyumen Oblast while remaining a separate federal subject. Its territory includes the Yamal Peninsula and lands around the lower Ob River and the Gulf of Ob.

Geography

The okrug lies mostly north of the Arctic Circle, in tundra and forest-tundra zones dotted with lakes and vast wetlands. The capital, Salekhard, sits almost exactly on the Arctic Circle on the Ob River. Winters are long and extremely cold.

Economy

Yamalia is one of the world's most important natural gas regions. Enormous fields around Novy Urengoy, Yamburg and the Yamal Peninsula supply a major share of Russia's gas production; the Yamal LNG project at Sabetta ships liquefied natural gas along the Northern Sea Route. The region has among the highest average incomes in Russia, though much of its workforce consists of rotational (fly-in/fly-out) workers.

People

The indigenous Nenets people, after whom the okrug is partly named, are renowned as the world's largest group of reindeer herders, many still following nomadic migration routes with their herds across the tundra. Russians form the majority of the settled population, alongside Ukrainians, Tatars, Khanty and Selkups.
