Bangsamoro

Official name: Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM)
Country: Philippines
Regional center: Cotabato City
Established: 2019

Bangsamoro, officially the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), is an autonomous region in the southern Philippines, replacing the former Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (ARMM) in 2019. The name combines the Malay/Tagalog word bangsa ("nation") with Moro, the Spanish-derived term for the Muslim peoples of the southern Philippines.

History

The region's Muslim sultanates, such as Sulu and Maguindanao, resisted Spanish and later American colonization for centuries. In the 20th century, grievances over land and marginalization fueled a separatist insurgency led by the Moro National Liberation Front and later the Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF). After decades of conflict, the Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro was signed in 2014, and the Bangsamoro Organic Law was enacted in 2018. A plebiscite in January and February 2019 ratified the law and established the BARMM.

Government and territory

The region is governed by the Bangsamoro Transition Authority, an interim parliament, pending regular elections, with a chief minister as head of government. It comprises the provinces of Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao del Norte, Maguindanao del Sur, Basilan, Sulu, and Tawi-Tawi, along with Cotabato City and several municipalities in North Cotabato. Following a 2024 Supreme Court ruling, Sulu was excluded from the region, though its future status remains a matter of political negotiation.
