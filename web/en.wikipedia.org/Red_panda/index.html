Red panda

The red panda (Ailurus fulgens), also called the lesser panda, is a small mammal native to the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China. Slightly larger than a domestic cat, it has dense reddish-brown fur, a long bushy ringed tail, and a false thumb — an extended wrist bone — for gripping bamboo.

Despite its name it is not closely related to the giant panda; it is the sole living member of the family Ailuridae. It feeds almost entirely on bamboo leaves but is classified in Carnivora. Largely arboreal and nocturnal, it is endangered because of habitat loss and fragmentation, with fewer than 10,000 mature individuals estimated in the wild.
