Killaloe, County Clare

Killaloe (Irish: Cill Dalua) is a small town in east County Clare, Ireland, on the western shore of Lough Derg, where the River Shannon leaves the lake. It faces Ballina, County Tipperary, across a historic bridge.

History

Killaloe was an important early Christian and medieval centre, associated with St Flannan and its 13th-century cathedral. Nearby stood Kincora, seat of the High King Brian Boru in the 11th century. The Shannon made it a river port and later a hub for boating and tourism on Lough Derg.
