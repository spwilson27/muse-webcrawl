Khilafat Movement

The Khilafat Movement (1919–1924) was a campaign by Indian Muslims, led by the Ali brothers, to pressure the British government to preserve the Ottoman Caliphate after the First World War.

Course

Mahatma Gandhi allied the movement with his Non-cooperation Movement, creating a notable period of Hindu–Muslim unity. The movement faded after Mustafa Kemal Atatürk abolished the caliphate in 1924.
