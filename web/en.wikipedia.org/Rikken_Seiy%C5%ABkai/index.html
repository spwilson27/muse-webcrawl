The Rikken Seiyūkai (立憲政友会, Friends of Constitutional Government Association) was one of the main political parties in the pre-war Empire of Japan. It was founded on September 15, 1900, by Itō Hirobumi as an alliance of bureaucrats and former members of the Kenseitō party, and it dominated Japanese politics through much of the Taishō and early Shōwa periods.

History

The Seiyūkai served as a pro-government party supporting Itō's cabinet and later supplied many prime ministers, including Saionji Kinmochi, Yamamoto Gonnohyōe, Hara Takashi, Takahashi Korekiyo, Tanaka Giichi and Inukai Tsuyoshi. Hara's appointment in 1918 made him the first commoner and first party politician to become prime minister of Japan.

Decline and dissolution

After the assassination of Prime Minister Inukai in the May 15 Incident of 1932, party cabinets ended. The Seiyūkai split over support for the military, and in 1940 it dissolved itself as part of the formation of the Imperial Rule Assistance Association.
