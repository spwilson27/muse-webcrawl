Terry Gene Bollea (11 August 1953 – 24 July 2025), better known by his ring name Hulk Hogan, was an American professional wrestler and media personality. Widely regarded as the most recognised wrestling star worldwide and the most popular wrestler of the 1980s, he was the face of the World Wrestling Federation's "Hulkamania" boom and later a founding figure of the New World Order (nWo) in WCW.

Career

Bollea began wrestling in Florida in 1977 and, after stints in the AWA and Japan, joined the WWF in 1983. On 23 January 1984 he defeated The Iron Sheik to win the WWF Championship, launching Hulkamania: headlining the first WrestleMania and eight of the first nine, feuding with André the Giant (WrestleMania III, 1987), Randy Savage and The Ultimate Warrior. After leaving for WCW in 1994, he reinvented himself in 1996 as the villainous "Hollywood" Hogan, leader of the nWo, a central storyline of the Monday Night Wars. He returned to WWE in 2002, winning a sixth WWE title, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2005 (and again in 2020 as part of the nWo).

Outside wrestling

Hogan starred in films including Rocky III (1982, as Thunderlips), No Holds Barred and Mr. Nanny, and the reality series Hogan Knows Best. His 2013 lawsuit against Gawker Media over a leaked sex tape ended in a $140 million judgment (later settled) that bankrupted the site in 2016. He died of cardiac arrest in Clearwater, Florida, in July 2025.
