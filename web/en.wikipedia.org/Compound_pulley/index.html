Compound pulley

A compound pulley is a system combining fixed and movable pulleys so that a load can be lifted with an effort smaller than its weight. Arrangements of pulleys and rope are traditionally called a block and tackle.

Mechanical advantage

The ideal mechanical advantage equals the number of rope segments supporting the movable block. Doubling the supporting segments halves the required effort but doubles the rope that must be pulled, conserving work.

Uses

Compound pulleys are used in cranes, sailboat rigging, elevators, and rescue systems, where compact manual lifting of heavy loads is needed.
