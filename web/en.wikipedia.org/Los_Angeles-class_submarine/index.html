Los Angeles-class submarine

The Los Angeles class (also known as the 688 class) is a class of nuclear-powered fast attack submarines (SSN) of the United States Navy. With 62 boats built between 1972 and 1996, it has been the most numerous nuclear-powered submarine class in the world and formed the backbone of the US submarine force for decades.

The boats displace about 6,900 tonnes submerged, are powered by an S6G reactor, and are armed with torpedoes and, in later boats, Tomahawk cruise missiles launched from vertical launch tubes. Later "688i" (improved) boats are quieter and fitted for under-ice operations. From the 2010s the class has been progressively replaced by the Virginia class.
