Minamoto no Yoritomo

BornMay 9, 1147
DiedFebruary 9, 1199
TitleSei-i Taishōgun (from 1192)
SpouseHōjō Masako

Minamoto no Yoritomo (源 頼朝, 1147–1199) was the founder and first shōgun of the Kamakura shogunate of Japan, which he ruled from 1192 until his death in 1199, beginning nearly 700 years of military government.

Rise to power

Exiled to Izu Province as a youth after his father's defeat in the Heiji Rebellion (1160), Yoritomo raised an army in 1180 against the ruling Taira clan, launching the Genpei War. Although defeated at first, he built a power base at Kamakura with his own vassals and administrators.

Shogunate

After his half-brother Yoshitsune destroyed the Taira at Dan-no-ura in 1185, Yoritomo turned on Yoshitsune and secured imperial recognition of his authority, receiving the title of shōgun in 1192. He established the bakufu institutions — the Samurai-dokoro, Mandokoro, and Monchūjo — that governed alongside the imperial court. His widow Hōjō Masako and her family dominated the shogunate after his death.
