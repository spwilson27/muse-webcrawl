Cruelty-free

Cruelty-free is a label for products or activities that do not harm or kill animals. It is most commonly applied to cosmetics, personal-care and household products that are not tested on animals, though definitions and certification standards vary.

Certification

Independent programmes such as Leaping Bunny and PETA's Beauty Without Bunnies certify brands whose finished products and ingredients are not tested on animals by the company or its suppliers. The familiar rabbit logos denote these standards; "cruelty-free" does not necessarily mean vegan, as certified products may still contain animal-derived ingredients.

Regulation

The European Union banned the sale of cosmetics tested on animals in 2013, and several other jurisdictions have adopted partial or full bans. Because the term is loosely regulated in some markets, critics warn of misleading claims, sometimes called "cruelty-free washing".
