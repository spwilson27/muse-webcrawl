European Economic Area

The European Economic Area (EEA) was established by the EEA Agreement signed in 1992 and entered into force in 1994. It links EU member states and three EFTA states — Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway — into an internal market governed by the four freedoms.

Membership

The EEA extends the EU single market to participating EFTA states, which adopt much EU legislation concerning the free movement of goods, services, persons and capital.
