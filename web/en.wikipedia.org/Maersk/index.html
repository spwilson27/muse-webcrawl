Maersk

Full name: A.P. Møller – Mærsk A/S
Founded: 1904, Copenhagen, Denmark
Industry: Container shipping, logistics, terminals
Headquarters: Copenhagen

Maersk (A.P. Møller – Mærsk A/S) is a Danish integrated shipping and logistics company, for decades the world's largest container ship operator.

History

Founded by Arnold Peter Møller and his father Peter Mærsk Møller in 1904, the company grew from steamships into container shipping with Maersk Line, terminal operator APM Terminals, and logistics services. It pioneered very large container ships with the E-class and Triple E class vessels.

Operations

Maersk operates hundreds of vessels calling at ports worldwide, plus inland logistics, warehousing and supply-chain services. It has invested in methanol-fuelled ships as part of decarbonisation goals. Its 2M alliance with MSC was dissolved in 2025, replaced by the Gemini Cooperation with Hapag-Lloyd.
