Kingdom of Kandy

The Kingdom of Kandy was a monarchy in the central highlands of present-day Sri Lanka, founded in the late 15th century. It was the last independent kingdom of the island, resisting Portuguese, Dutch and British colonial power for more than three centuries.

History

From its capital at Kandy (Senkadagalapura), the kingdom used mountainous terrain and guerrilla tactics to repel invasions of its lowland-colonising neighbours. Kings such as Rajasinghe II sought Dutch help against the Portuguese, only to face Dutch encroachment in turn. The Nayakkar dynasty from South India ruled from 1739.

Fall

In 1815 the Kandyan Convention deposed King Sri Vikrama Rajasinha and ceded the kingdom to Britain, uniting the whole island under one administration for the first time in centuries. The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy remains a major Buddhist pilgrimage site.
