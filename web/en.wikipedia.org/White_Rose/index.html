White Rose

The White Rose was a non-violent resistance group in Nazi Germany centred on students at the University of Munich, including Hans and Sophie Scholl and Christoph Probst. In 1942 and 1943 the group produced and distributed leaflets denouncing Nazi crimes and calling on Germans to resist.

Leaflets and arrest

The leaflets appealed to conscience, cited German cultural and religious traditions, and reported atrocities on the Eastern Front. In February 1943 Hans and Sophie Scholl were arrested after scattering leaflets at the university. They and Probst were tried by the People's Court and executed.

Memory

Other members were later imprisoned or executed. The White Rose is now commemorated in schools, memorials and public spaces across Germany as an example of moral courage under dictatorship.
