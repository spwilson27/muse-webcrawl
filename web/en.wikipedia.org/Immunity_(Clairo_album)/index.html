Immunity is the debut studio album by American singer-songwriter Clairo (Claire Cottrill), released on 2 August 2019 by the Fader Label. Co-produced with Rostam Batmanglij, formerly of Vampire Weekend, the album followed her viral success with "Pretty Girl" and the EP Diary 001 (2018).

Music and themes

The album blends bedroom pop, indie pop, and soft rock, with lyrics about adolescence, first relationships, and vulnerability; its title and several songs reflect Cottrill's experience of juvenile rheumatoid arthritis, diagnosed in her teens.

Singles and reception

Singles included "Bags", "Closer to You", and "Sofia", the last of which later became a major streaming hit. Immunity received generally favourable reviews, praised for its intimate songwriting, and reached the top 40 of the US Billboard 200.

Track listing

Tracks include "Alewife", "Impossible", "Bags", "North", "Sofia", "White Flag", "Feel Something", "Sinking", and "I Wouldn't Ask You".
