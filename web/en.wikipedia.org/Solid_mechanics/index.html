Solid mechanics

Solid mechanics is the branch of continuum mechanics that studies the behaviour of solid materials, especially their motion and deformation under forces, temperature changes, and other external or internal agents. It underlies civil, mechanical, aerospace, and materials engineering.

Topics

Core topics include stress and strain; elasticity (linear and non-linear); plasticity and yielding criteria; viscoelasticity; fracture mechanics and fatigue; and the mechanics of beams, plates, and shells. Constitutive equations relate stress to strain for each material model.

Methods

Classical analytical solutions exist for simple geometries; most practical problems are solved numerically, especially with the finite element method. Experimental techniques such as strain gauges and photoelasticity validate models.
