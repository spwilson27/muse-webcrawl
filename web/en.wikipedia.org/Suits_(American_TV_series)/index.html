Suits is an American legal drama television series created by Aaron Korsh that aired on USA Network from 2011 to 2019. Set at a fictional New York City law firm, it follows talented college dropout Mike Ross, who talks his way into a job as an associate for top closer Harvey Specter despite never having attended law school — a secret that drives much of the series' tension.

Premise and cast

Mike Ross (Patrick J. Adams) has an eidetic memory and dreams of being a lawyer like his grandmother urged, but was expelled from college after selling a math test. Fleeing a botched drug delivery, he stumbles into Harvey Specter's (Gabriel Macht) associate interviews and impresses him into a hire. At the firm — successively named Pearson Hardman, Pearson Darby, Pearson Specter, and finally Specter Litt — the cast includes managing partner Jessica Pearson (Gina Torres), rival partner Louis Litt (Rick Hoffman), paralegal Rachel Zane (Meghan Markle), and Harvey's secretary Donna Paulsen (Sarah Rafferty).

Production and reception

Though set in Manhattan, the series filmed in Toronto. It was one of USA Network's flagship "blue sky" dramas and earned award nominations, including Screen Actors Guild recognition for Adams. Gina Torres departed after season 6 and later headlined the short-lived spin-off Pearson (2019); Markle and Adams left after season 7, and the show concluded with season 9 in 2019. The franchise was revived with the spin-off Suits LA in 2025.

Streaming resurgence

In 2023, years after its finale, Suits became a surprise streaming phenomenon on Netflix and Peacock, topping Nielsen's U.S. streaming charts for a record run of weeks and becoming the most-streamed acquired series of that year — a revival of interest that led directly to the LA spin-off.
