Lucas sequence

In mathematics, the Lucas sequences Un(P,Q) and Vn(P,Q) are certain constant-recursive integer sequences satisfying the recurrence xn = P·xn−1 − Q·xn−2, named after the French mathematician Édouard Lucas.

Examples

The Fibonacci numbers are Un(1,−1), and the Lucas numbers are Vn(1,−1). Pell numbers, Jacobsthal numbers and Mersenne numbers are other special cases.

Properties

Lucas sequences have closed forms using the roots of x² − Px + Q, satisfy divisibility properties used in primality testing, and generalize many identities of the Fibonacci sequence. They underlie the Lucas–Lehmer primality test for Mersenne numbers.
