Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players on a field at the centre of which is a 22-yard (20.12 m) pitch with a wicket at each end, each comprising three stumps topped by two bails. The batting side scores runs by striking the ball bowled at one wicket with the bat and running between the wickets, while the bowling and fielding side tries to prevent runs and dismiss each batter. It is the world's second most popular spectator sport after association football, with its strongest following in South Asia, Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, South Africa, Zimbabwe, and the Caribbean.

History

Cricket originated in south-east England, with a definite reference to the game at Guildford in 1550, and it developed during the 17th and 18th centuries, when the Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC), founded in 1787 at Lord's, became the custodian of the Laws of Cricket. The game spread through the British Empire: the first international match was played between the United States and Canada in 1844, and Test cricket began in 1877 between England and Australia, inaugurating the Ashes rivalry. The Imperial Cricket Conference (now the International Cricket Council) was founded in 1909.

Formats of the game

  • Test cricket — the longest format, played over up to five days with two innings per side, contested by the ICC's full members wearing whites with a red ball.
  • One Day Internationals (ODIs) — limited-overs matches of 50 overs per side, popularised from the 1970s; the first Cricket World Cup was held in England in 1975.
  • Twenty20 (T20) — a short format of 20 overs per side lasting about three hours, introduced in England in 2003 and propelled globally by franchise leagues such as the Indian Premier League (founded 2008).

Essentials of play

The bowler delivers the ball with a straight arm from one end of the pitch toward the striker at the other. The striker may score by hitting the ball to the boundary (four runs, or six if it crosses on the full) or by running between the wickets. Batters can be dismissed in several ways, most commonly bowled, caught, leg before wicket (LBW), run out, and stumped. An innings ends when ten batters are dismissed, a set number of overs has been bowled, or the batting captain declares. Matches are officiated by two on-field umpires, with a third umpire assisting on video reviews through the Decision Review System (DRS).

Women's cricket and global growth

Women's cricket has been played since the 18th century; the first Women's Cricket World Cup was held in 1973, two years before the men's. The game has expanded rapidly in the 21st century through T20 leagues and ICC development programmes, and cricket is scheduled to return to the Olympic programme at Los Angeles 2028 in T20 form.
