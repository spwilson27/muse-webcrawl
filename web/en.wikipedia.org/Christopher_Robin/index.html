Christopher Robin

Christopher Robin is a character who appears in A. A. Milne's Winnie-the-Pooh stories and poems. He is the young human boy around whom the adventures of Winnie-the-Pooh, Piglet, Eeyore, Tigger, Rabbit, Owl, Kanga and Roo revolve in the Hundred Acre Wood. The character was named after Milne's son, Christopher Robin Milne, whose stuffed toys inspired the animal cast.

In the books

Christopher Robin appears in Winnie-the-Pooh (1926) and The House at Pooh Corner (1928), as well as in the poetry collections When We Were Very Young (1924) and Now We Are Six (1927). He is the animals' leader and problem-solver, and the stories often end with his return home. The final chapter of The House at Pooh Corner, in which Christopher Robin prepares to leave the forest to go to school, is a famous meditation on the end of childhood.

Real-life counterpart

Christopher Robin Milne (1920–1996) grew up in the public eye because of the books' fame, a prominence he found difficult. He later ran a bookshop in Dartmouth and wrote memoirs about his childhood and his complicated relationship with his father and the Pooh legacy.

Adaptations

The character appears in Disney's animated Winnie the Pooh films and television series, and as an adult protagonist, played by Ewan McGregor, in the 2018 live-action film Christopher Robin. The 2017 biographical film Goodbye Christopher Robin dramatises the creation of the stories.
