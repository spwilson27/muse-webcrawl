Chennai Super Kings (CSK) is an Indian professional cricket franchise based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Founded in 2008, the team plays its home matches at the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium and is one of the most successful and valuable franchises in the league.

History

CSK was bought by India Cements at the inaugural franchise auction, and MS Dhoni — bought at the first player auction as the most expensive player — captained the side from 2008 until 2023, becoming the defining figure of the franchise. The team reached the play-offs in each of its first eight seasons and won back-to-back titles in 2010 and 2011.

Suspension and return

In 2015 CSK was suspended for two seasons (2016–2017) following the 2013 IPL betting scandal involving a team official. On its return in 2018 the "Dad's Army" squad immediately won the title. Further titles followed in 2021 and 2023, the latter in a rain-shortened final against Gujarat Titans.

Rivalries and culture

CSK's rivalry with Mumbai Indians is the IPL's most prominent, and its matches with Royal Challengers Bengaluru form the "Southern derby". The franchise is noted for its loyal "Yellow Army" fanbase, the whistle podu slogan, and a strategy of backing experienced players and squad continuity.
