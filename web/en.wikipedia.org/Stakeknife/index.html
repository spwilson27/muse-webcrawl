Stakeknife
Codename of: British agent within the IRA
Allegedly: Freddie Scappaticci
Context: The Troubles, Northern Ireland
Inquiry: Operation Kenova

Stakeknife is the codename of a high-ranking British intelligence agent who allegedly infiltrated the Provisional Irish Republican Army during the Troubles in Northern Ireland. The agent is widely alleged to have been Freddie Scappaticci, a Belfast republican who was involved in the IRA's internal security unit.

Allegations

Reports in 2003 named Scappaticci, who denied the claims. The agent was said to have operated for the Army's Force Research Unit while vetting suspected informers, raising questions about whether murders were allowed to proceed to protect his cover.

Operation Kenova

The multi-year Operation Kenova investigation, led by Jon Boutcher, examined the agent's activities and related cases; its interim report was published in 2024. Scappaticci died in 2023 without being prosecuted as the agent.
