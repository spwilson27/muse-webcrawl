Francis I (1708–1765) was Holy Roman Emperor from 1745 until his death and the husband of Maria Theresa, Archduchess of Austria and Queen of Hungary and Bohemia. Born Duke of Lorraine, he founded the House of Habsburg-Lorraine, which ruled Austria until 1918.

Early life and marriage

Francis Stephen of Lorraine was raised partly at the imperial court in Vienna, where he met the young Maria Theresa. Under the settlement ending the War of the Polish Succession he gave up Lorraine to France, receiving the Grand Duchy of Tuscany in compensation. He married Maria Theresa in 1736.

Emperor

After the death of Emperor Charles VII, Francis was elected Holy Roman Emperor in 1745, restoring the imperial title to the Habsburg line in the male person, while real power in the Austrian hereditary lands remained with Maria Theresa. Francis managed the dynasty's finances with skill, amassing a large private fortune, and took an interest in natural history and Freemasonry.

Death and succession

Francis died suddenly at Innsbruck in 1765 while celebrating the wedding of his son Leopold. He was succeeded as emperor by his eldest son, Joseph II, who had already been made co-regent of the Austrian lands by Maria Theresa.
