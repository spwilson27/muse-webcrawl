James Alexander Maynard (born 10 June 1987) is a British mathematician working in analytic number theory, particularly on prime numbers. He is a professor at the University of Oxford and received the Fields Medal in 2022.

Work

Maynard independently developed a refinement of the sieve methods of Zhang Yitang's breakthrough on bounded gaps between primes, showing that there are infinitely many pairs of primes differing by at most 600, and proving stronger results on prime tuples. With Dimitris Koukoulopoulos he resolved the Duffin–Schaeffer conjecture, a long-standing problem in Diophantine approximation.

Honours

He has received the SASTRA Ramanujan Prize, the Whitehead Prize, and the Cole Prize in Number Theory. He was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society in 2023.
