Diablo Lake

LocationNorth Cascades, Whatcom County, Washington, U.S.
TypeReservoir
Primary inflowsSkagit River
Surface elevation1,201 ft (366 m)

Diablo Lake is a reservoir in the North Cascade mountains of northern Washington state, United States. Located in the Ross Lake National Recreation Area, the lake was created by Diablo Dam on the Skagit River and is famed for its intense turquoise color.

Geography

The lake's striking green-blue hue comes from glacial flour — fine rock particles ground by surrounding glaciers and suspended in the water, which reflect sunlight. Diablo Lake lies between Ross Lake and Gorge Lake along the Skagit River hydroelectric project operated by Seattle City Light.

Tourism

The Diablo Lake Overlook on the North Cascades Highway (State Route 20) is a popular stopping point, offering views of Colonial Peak and the surrounding mountains. Boat launches, campgrounds, and the North Cascades Environmental Learning Center sit on its shores.

Not to be confused with the video game Diablo.
