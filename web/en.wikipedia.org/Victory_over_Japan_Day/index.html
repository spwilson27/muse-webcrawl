Victory over Japan Day

Victory over Japan Day (V-J Day, also written VJ Day) is the day on which Imperial Japan surrendered in World War II, in effect bringing the war to an end. Japan announced its acceptance of the Potsdam Declaration on 14 August 1945 (15 August in Japan); the formal surrender ceremony took place aboard the battleship USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay on 2 September 1945.

Dates

Different countries commemorate different dates: the United Kingdom observes V-J Day on 15 August, while the United States has historically marked 2 September, the date of the formal signing (the US federal holiday, observed 1946–1975, was on 2 September, except in Rhode Island, which retains a state holiday in August). Japan itself observes 15 August as the memorial day for the end of the war. Alfred Eisenstaedt's photograph V-J Day in Times Square is an enduring image of the celebrations.
