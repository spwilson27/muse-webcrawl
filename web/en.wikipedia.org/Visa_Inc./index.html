Visa Inc.

A Visa payment card

Type: Public

Founded: 1958 (as BankAmericard)

Headquarters: San Francisco, California, U.S.

Industry: Financial services

Visa Inc. is an American multinational payment card services corporation headquartered in San Francisco, California. It facilitates electronic funds transfers throughout the world, most commonly through Visa-branded credit cards, debit cards, and prepaid cards. Visa is one of the world's most valuable companies and operates one of the largest electronic payments networks.

History

Visa's origins date to 1958, when Bank of America launched the BankAmericard credit card program in Fresno, California. In 1976 the program was rebranded as Visa, a name chosen because it was short, easy to pronounce in many languages, and evoked universal acceptance. Dee Hock, the first CEO of the Visa association, structured it as a decentralized member-owned organization. Visa Inc. was formed in 2007 by combining regional associations, and its 2008 initial public offering was one of the largest in U.S. history.

Operations

Visa does not issue cards, extend credit, or set rates for consumers; instead, it provides financial institutions with payment products and the VisaNet network, which can process tens of thousands of transaction messages per second. Revenue comes mainly from service, data processing, and international transaction fees. Visa Europe operated separately until Visa Inc. acquired it in 2016.
