8K resolution

Width7680 pixels
Height4320 pixels
Total pixels33.2 megapixels
Aspect ratio16:9

8K resolution refers to a horizontal display resolution of approximately 8,000 pixels. The most commonly used 8K resolution is 7680 × 4320 pixels, which has four times the total pixels of 4K resolution and sixteen times that of Full HD (1080p).

History

The Japanese public broadcaster NHK began research into ultra-high-definition television in 1995, and demonstrated the Super Hi-Vision system with an 8K camera at Expo 2005. The first 8K displays targeted at consumers appeared in the mid-2010s, and NHK launched the world's first 8K television channel, BS8K, in December 2018.

Standards

The ITU-R Recommendation BT.2020 defines 8K UHDTV with a resolution of 7680 × 4320, progressive scan, and frame rates up to 120 Hz. The Digital Cinema Initiatives define an 8K DCI container of 8192 × 4320.

Adoption

Adoption has been limited by cost, the scarcity of native 8K content, and the minimal perceived benefit over 4K at typical viewing distances. Broadcasters in Japan and South Korea have led experimentation with 8K production, particularly for sporting events.
