Better World Campaign

The Better World Campaign (BWC) is a United States non-profit organization that advocates for a strong partnership between the United States and the United Nations. It is a sister organization of the United Nations Foundation and works to educate policymakers, the media, and the American public about the role of the UN in addressing global challenges.

History

The Better World Campaign was launched in 1999 by the United Nations Foundation, which had been created in 1998 following a one billion dollar gift from media entrepreneur Ted Turner to support United Nations causes. The campaign was established to build support in the United States for the UN at a time when American arrears in UN dues were a major point of contention between Washington and the organization.

Mission and activities

The campaign works to strengthen the US–UN relationship through advocacy, outreach, and education. Its activities include:

  • Advocating for full and timely payment of United States dues and peacekeeping assessments to the United Nations.
  • Organizing briefings and travel for members of the United States Congress and their staff.
  • Publishing research and polling on American public attitudes toward the UN.
  • Supporting US engagement with UN agencies working on global health, humanitarian relief, and peacekeeping.

The Better World Campaign has been a prominent voice in debates over US funding for UN peacekeeping operations and for agencies such as the World Health Organization and the UN Population Fund.
