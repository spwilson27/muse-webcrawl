JT International SA (JTI) is the international tobacco division of Japan Tobacco Inc. (JT), headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland. It is one of the largest tobacco companies in the world, selling its products in more than 130 countries and employing tens of thousands of people.

History

JTI was formed in 1999 when Japan Tobacco acquired the non-US tobacco operations of R.J. Reynolds for about $7.8 billion, one of the largest overseas acquisitions by a Japanese company at the time. In 2007 JTI greatly expanded with the £9.4 billion acquisition of the British tobacco company Gallaher Group, adding brands such as Benson & Hedges (outside the US) and Silk Cut. In 2018 the group acquired the tobacco business of Russia's Donskoy Tabak and the Bangladeshi company Akij Group's tobacco unit, strengthening its position in key markets.

Brands and products

JTI's global flagship brands include Winston and Camel (both outside the United States), Mevius (formerly Mild Seven), LD, and Sobranie, along with regional and local brands. The company has also invested in reduced-risk products, including the Ploom heated tobacco device and the Logic e-cigarette brand.

Operations

Russia was historically one of JTI's largest markets. The company operates factories and offices across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas. Like the wider industry, JTI faces declining cigarette volumes in many developed markets, plain-packaging and tax regulation, and litigation and reputational pressures related to the health effects of smoking.
