Pakistan, officially the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, is a country in South Asia. It is the fifth-most populous country in the world, with a population of over 240 million, and has the second-largest Muslim population after Indonesia. Pakistan is bordered by India to the east, Afghanistan to the west, Iran to the southwest, China to the northeast, and the Arabian Sea and Gulf of Oman to the south.

History

The territory of modern Pakistan was home to the ancient Indus Valley Civilisation, centred on cities such as Mohenjo-daro and Harappa, and was later ruled by empires including the Achaemenids, Mauryas, Mughals and the British Raj. Pakistan emerged as an independent dominion on 14 August 1947, as a result of the Pakistan Movement led by Muhammad Ali Jinnah and the partition of British India. Its eastern wing seceded in 1971 to become Bangladesh. Pakistan adopted its current constitution in 1973 and became a declared nuclear-weapon state in 1998.

Geography

Pakistan's landscape varies from the coastal plains of the Arabian Sea to the Indus River plain, the deserts of Sindh and Balochistan, and the northern highlands where the Karakoram, Hindu Kush and western Himalaya ranges meet. K2, at 8,611 m, is the world's second-highest mountain. The Indus, one of Asia's great rivers, flows the length of the country and sustains its agriculture.

Politics and government

Pakistan is a federal parliamentary republic with a bicameral legislature, the Majlis-e-Shoora (National Assembly and Senate). The head of state is the President; the head of government is the Prime Minister. The country is divided into four provinces — Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan — along with the Islamabad Capital Territory and the territories of Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir. The military has played a powerful role throughout Pakistan's history, ruling directly for several extended periods.

Economy

Pakistan has a developing, semi-industrialised economy centred on textiles, agriculture (notably cotton, rice and wheat), remittances from a large overseas diaspora, and a growing services sector. Karachi is the financial and commercial capital. Pakistan is a member of the United Nations, the Commonwealth of Nations, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation.

Demographics and culture

Urdu is the national language and lingua franca, while English is widely used in government and commerce; major regional languages include Punjabi, Pashto, Sindhi, Saraiki and Balochi. Islam is the state religion, practised by about 96 per cent of the population. Pakistan's culture blends South Asian, Persian, Central Asian and Islamic influences, visible in its music (including qawwali), poetry, cuisine and architecture such as Lahore's Badshahi Mosque. Cricket is the most popular sport.
