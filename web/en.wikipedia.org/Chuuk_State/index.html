Chuuk State

Chuuk State is one of the four states of the Federated States of Micronesia in the western Pacific, centred on Chuuk Lagoon, one of the world's largest lagoons.

Geography and history

Its capital is Weno. The lagoon's islands were a major Japanese naval base during the Second World War; in February 1944 the US Operation Hailstone sank dozens of ships and aircraft, whose wrecks now make Chuuk a famous wreck-diving destination, sometimes called Truk Lagoon from the older name.

People

Chuuk is the most populous FSM state, with around 50,000 people speaking Chuukese and other languages. Fishing and copra are mainstays, and out-migration to Guam and the United States is common.
