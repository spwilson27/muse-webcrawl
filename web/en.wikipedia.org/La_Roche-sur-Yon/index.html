La Roche-sur-Yon

La Roche-sur-Yon is a commune in western France, the prefecture (capital) of the Vendée department in the Pays de la Loire region, with a population of roughly 55,000.

History

An older town around a medieval castle was largely destroyed during the Wars of the Vendée. In 1804 Napoleon chose the site as a new prefecture and ordered a planned, grid-pattern town, originally named Napoléon; its name changed several times with regimes before settling again on La Roche-sur-Yon.

Landmarks

The pentagonal old centre is centred on Place Napoléon, with its equestrian statue and restored mechanical animals inspired by Les Machines de l'île.
