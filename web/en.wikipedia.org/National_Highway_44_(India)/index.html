National Highway 44 (India)

National Highway 44 (NH 44) is a major north–south national highway in India, running from Srinagar in Jammu and Kashmir to Kanyakumari in Tamil Nadu. At roughly 4,100 kilometres, it is the longest national highway in the country.

Route

The highway passes through the union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the states of Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu. Major cities on the route include Jammu, Ludhiana, Delhi, Agra, Gwalior, Jhansi, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Salem and Madurai.

History

NH 44 was formed in the 2010 renumbering of the national highway network, largely from the former NH 7 (then the longest highway) together with sections of the old NH 1, NH 2, NH 3, NH 26 and NH 75. Much of its length forms part of the North–South Corridor of the National Highways Development Project.
