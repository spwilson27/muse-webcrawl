List of tallest buildings in the Paris region

The Paris region's tallest buildings are concentrated in La Défense, the purpose-built business district west of the city proper in the Hauts-de-Seine department. Strict height limits within Paris itself — imposed in the 1970s after controversy over the Tour Montparnasse and relaxed only modestly since — have kept most tall development outside the historic core. The tallest structure in the region remains the Eiffel Tower (330 m), which is not a building.

Context

La Défense was launched in 1958 by the EPAD development authority along the historic axis extending west from the Louvre through the Arc de Triomphe. Its Grande Arche, completed in 1989, is a hollow cube 110 m tall. Within Paris, newer projects such as the Tours Duo and the Tour Triangle (180 m, under construction in the 15th arrondissement) mark a cautious return of tall construction to the city itself.
