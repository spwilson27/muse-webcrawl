House plan

A house plan is a set of construction or working drawings (sometimes called blueprints) that define all the construction specifications of a residential house, such as the dimensions, materials, layouts, installation methods and techniques. The principal element is the floor plan, a scaled drawing showing the arrangement of rooms, walls, doors, windows, and fixtures as seen from above.

A floor plan is the central drawing in a set of house plans.

Contents of a house plan set

  • Floor plans for each level, showing room sizes and layout.
  • Elevations, drawings of the exterior faces of the house.
  • Sections, cut-through views showing construction details.
  • Foundation and roof plans.
  • Site plan, showing the position of the house on its lot.
  • Electrical, plumbing, and structural details as required.

Design considerations

House plans reflect the needs and budget of the occupants, local building codes, climate, and lot constraints. Common layout concepts include open-plan living areas, en-suite bathrooms, and the orientation of rooms for sunlight and ventilation. Historically, house plans circulated through pattern books and mail-order catalogues — most famously the Sears Modern Homes programme in the early 20th century — and are today distributed widely as stock plans online, with computer-aided design (CAD) and 3D visualisation standard in professional practice.
