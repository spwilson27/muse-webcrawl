Central Pacific Hurricane Center

AgencyNational Weather Service (NOAA)
HeadquartersHonolulu, Hawaii
Area of responsibilityCentral North Pacific, 140°W to the International Date Line

The Central Pacific Hurricane Center (CPHC) is the United States National Weather Service office in Honolulu, Hawaii, designated by the World Meteorological Organization as the Regional Specialized Meteorological Center for the central North Pacific tropical cyclone basin.

Responsibilities

The center issues forecasts, watches and warnings for tropical cyclones between 140°W longitude and the International Date Line, the region that includes the Hawaiian Islands. It works with the National Hurricane Center in Miami, which covers the Atlantic and eastern Pacific basins.

Operations

During the central Pacific hurricane season, which runs from June 1 to November 30, CPHC forecasters monitor satellite imagery, radar and model guidance, naming storms from separate Hawaiian name lists. Notable storms tracked by the center include Hurricane Iniki (1992) and Hurricane Lane (2018).
