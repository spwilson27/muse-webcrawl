Wheelhouse
Also calledPilothouse; bridge (on modern ships)
PurposeShelter for the ship's wheel and helmsman; navigation station

A wheelhouse is a structure on a ship or boat that houses the ship's wheel and provides shelter for the helmsman and, traditionally, the ship's navigation equipment. On small vessels the terms pilothouse and wheelhouse are used interchangeably; on larger modern ships the wheelhouse forms part of the bridge, the raised platform from which the vessel is commanded.

Historically, the wheelhouse protected the steering station on steamships and paddle steamers, often placed high to give the pilot a clear view ahead and over the paddle boxes. In riverboats and tugs it remains a distinct raised cabin with windows on all sides. The term is also used in other senses: on a watermill or mill building for the structure housing the waterwheel, and in sport (baseball) metaphorically for a batter's strongest hitting zone — "in his wheelhouse."
