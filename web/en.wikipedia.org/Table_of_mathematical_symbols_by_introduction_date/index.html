Table of mathematical symbols by introduction date

The following table lists many specialized symbols commonly used in modern mathematics, ordered by their introduction date. Mathematical notation developed gradually: early texts used words where modern writers use symbols, and many glyphs were introduced by individual mathematicians whose choices then became standard.
