The 2032 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XXXV Olympiad and branded as Brisbane 2032, are a forthcoming international multi-sport event scheduled to take place in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia, from July 23 to August 8, 2032. Brisbane was awarded the Games on July 21, 2021, at the 138th IOC Session in Tokyo, becoming the first host selected under the International Olympic Committee's new targeted dialogue process.

Selection

Under reforms introduced with Olympic Agenda 2020, the IOC's Future Host Commission engaged in continuous dialogue with interested cities rather than a competitive bidding race. Brisbane, the only city taken into targeted dialogue for 2032, was confirmed with a vote of 72–5. It will be the third Summer Olympics held in Australia, after Melbourne 1956 and Sydney 2000.

Venues and preparations

The event model is regional, with events spread across South East Queensland, including the Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast. Venue planning has been politically contentious: proposals to rebuild the Gabba cricket ground as the main stadium were abandoned in 2024 after a cost review, and in 2025 the Queensland government announced a new 63,000-seat stadium at Victoria Park for athletics and ceremonies. Planning emphasizes legacy infrastructure, including rail projects such as Cross River Rail, intended to serve the growing region long after the Games.
