Association Sportive Vita Club, commonly known as AS Vita Club or simply Vita Club, is a Congolese football club based in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo. Founded in 1935, it is one of the country's most successful clubs and plays in Linafoot, the top division of Congolese football. Its home ground is the Stade des Martyrs, and its traditional colors are green and black.

History

The club was established in Kinshasa (then Léopoldville) during the Belgian colonial period. Vita Club rose to continental prominence in the 1970s, winning the African Cup of Champions Clubs in 1973 — the club's greatest honor — and reaching the final again in 1981. Domestically it has won numerous league titles and Coupe du Congo trophies, contesting a fierce Kinshasa derby with Daring Club Motema Pembe.

In 2014, Vita Club again reached the final of the CAF Champions League, losing on away goals to Algeria's ES Sétif. The club has also won the CAF Super Cup-era domestic honors and remains a regular participant in CAF interclub competitions.
