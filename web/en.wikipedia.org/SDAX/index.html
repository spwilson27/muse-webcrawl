SDAX

The SDAX (German abbreviation for Small-Cap-Deutscher Aktienindex) is a German stock index which tracks the performance of 70 small-cap companies in terms of order book volume and market capitalisation. It is based on prices from the electronic trading system Xetra.

It is the small-cap equivalent of the MDAX and DAX, which track mid-sized and large companies respectively. Together, the DAX, MDAX, TecDAX and SDAX form the Prime Standard index group of Deutsche Börse. The SDAX was introduced on 24 June 1999. Its base date is 30 December 1988, with a base value of 1,000 points.

Composition

The index comprises the 70 companies ranking below the 90 companies included in the MDAX in terms of market capitalisation and trading volume. Constituents are reviewed twice a year. Since September 2021, the SDAX has contained 70 companies, having been expanded from its previous size of 50.
