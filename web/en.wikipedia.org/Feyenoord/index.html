Feyenoord

De Kuip, Feyenoord's home stadium

Feyenoord Rotterdam is a Dutch professional football club based in Rotterdam, South Holland. Founded in 1908 as Wilhelmina, it is one of the country's most successful clubs and, with Ajax and PSV Eindhoven, one of the "big three" that have dominated Dutch football. Feyenoord play their home matches at De Kuip, one of the most famous stadiums in the Netherlands.

History and honours

Feyenoord have won the Eredivisie title 16 times, most recently in 2023 under Arne Slot, along with numerous KNVB Cups. Their greatest triumph came in 1970, when they won the European Cup, defeating Celtic in the final, and went on to win the Intercontinental Cup the same year. They also won the UEFA Cup in 1974 and 2002.

Rivalries and support

The club's fiercest rivalry is with Ajax of Amsterdam, contested as De Klassieker. Feyenoord are traditionally regarded as a working-class club with a loyal, passionate support known as Het Legioen ("The Legion").
