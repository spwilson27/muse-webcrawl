First Philippine Republic

The First Philippine Republic (Spanish: República Filipina), also known as the Malolos Republic, was established in Malolos, Bulacan, during the Philippine Revolution against Spain. It is the first republic in Asia founded by revolution against a colonial power.

Background

Led by the Katipunan, the revolution began in 1896. Emilio Aguinaldo proclaimed independence on 12 June 1898 at Kawit, Cavite.

Malolos Constitution

The revolutionary congress at Barasoain Church, Malolos, drafted the Malolos Constitution (drafted largely by Apolinario Mabini), promulgated on 21 January 1899; the Republic was inaugurated two days later.

Philippine–American War

After the Treaty of Paris ceded the Philippines, fighting with the United States began in February 1899. Aguinaldo's capture at Palanan in March 1901 ended the Republic, though resistance continued.
