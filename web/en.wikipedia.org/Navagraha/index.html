Navagraha

The Navagraha are nine heavenly bodies and deities that influence human life in Hindu astrology (Jyotish). The word derives from Sanskrit nava ("nine") and graha ("grasping" or "seizing"), referring to the celestial powers believed to seize or influence the destinies of people. The nine comprise the Sun, the Moon, the five visible planets, and the two lunar nodes, Rahu and Ketu.

Worship

Many Hindu temples, especially in Tamil Nadu, contain a Navagraha shrine where the nine deities are arranged in a square with Surya at the centre. Devotees circumambulate the shrine, often nine times, and perform remedies (parihara) to mitigate the adverse effects attributed to a malefic graha in their horoscope. A cluster of nine temples in the Kaveri delta region, the Navagraha temples of Tamil Nadu, assigns one temple to each graha.

In astrology

Each graha rules signs, houses and periods (dashas) of life. The Vimshottari dasha system, the most widely used in Vedic astrology, divides life into periods ruled successively by the nine grahas, beginning with the lord of the nakshatra occupied by the Moon at birth.
