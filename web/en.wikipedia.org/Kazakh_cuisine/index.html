Kazakh cuisine

Kazakh cuisine is the traditional food of the Kazakh people of Central Asia, shaped by a nomadic herding culture centred on meat and dairy products, supplemented in modern times by grains, vegetables and fruits. Horse, mutton and beef are the principal meats, and fermented dairy drinks are staples of hospitality.

Traditional dishes

The national dish is beshbarmak — boiled meat (traditionally horse or mutton) served over wide flat noodles with onion broth, eaten communally by hand. Other staples include kazy, a sausage made from horse ribs; shashlik skewers; lagman noodles; and baursak, fried dough balls served with tea.

Drinks

Kumis (fermented mare's milk), shubat (fermented camel's milk) and strong black tea with milk are traditional drinks, offered to guests as a mark of welcome.
