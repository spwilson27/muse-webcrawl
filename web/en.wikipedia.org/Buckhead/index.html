Buckhead

Buckhead
Location: Atlanta, Georgia, United States
Known for: Upscale shopping, dining and residential district

Buckhead is the uptown commercial and residential district of Atlanta, Georgia, comprising approximately the northernmost fifth of the city. Buckhead is a major commercial and financial center of the Southeast, and it is the third-largest business district within the Atlanta metropolitan area, behind Downtown and Midtown.

The district is known for its upscale malls, Lenox Square and Phipps Plaza, its shopping streets, hotels, restaurants and nightlife, as well as large single-family homes in wooded residential neighbourhoods. It is anchored by the Buckhead Village and the Peachtree Road corridor.

History

The name is said to derive from a tavern established in the 1830s by Henry Irby, who mounted the head of a buck deer he had killed. The area remained largely rural until the early 20th century, when wealthy Atlantans built country estates there. Buckhead was annexed by the city of Atlanta in 1952. Commercial development accelerated with the opening of Lenox Square in 1959.

Transportation

Buckhead is served by MARTA rail stations including Buckhead, Lenox and Lindbergh Center, and by Georgia State Route 400 and Peachtree Road.
