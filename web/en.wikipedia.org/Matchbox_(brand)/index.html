Matchbox (brand)

Product typeDie-cast toy vehicles
Introduced1953
OwnerMattel

Matchbox is a popular toy brand introduced by Lesney Products in 1953 and now owned by Mattel. The brand was so named because the original die-cast toy vehicles were sold in boxes similar in style and size to those used for matches.

History

Lesney's first major success was a miniature of the Queen's coronation coach; the 1-75 series of small vehicles launched in 1953 became a worldwide phenomenon. Matchbox was acquired by Mattel in 1997, alongside its longtime rival Hot Wheels.

Products

Matchbox vehicles are typically modelled at around 1:64 scale and have increasingly emphasised realistic working vehicles alongside collector lines.
