Kumiho

A kumiho (Korean: 구미호, literally "nine-tailed fox") is a creature that appears in the folktales and legends of Korea. Similar to the Japanese kitsune and the Chinese huli jing, the kumiho is a fox that can freely transform, commonly into a beautiful woman, to seduce people — often with harmful or deadly intent.

Folklore

Unlike many fox spirits in neighbouring traditions, the kumiho is typically portrayed as malicious. According to legend, a fox that lives for a thousand years becomes a kumiho, gaining the ability to shapeshift. Tales often describe the kumiho eating the hearts or livers of its victims, or seeking to become fully human by abstaining from killing and eating people for a set period — a condition it usually fails to meet.

In popular culture

The kumiho remains a popular figure in modern South Korean media, appearing in television dramas such as My Girlfriend Is a Gumiho (2010) and Tale of the Nine Tailed (2020), as well as in games, comics and webtoons, where its portrayal is sometimes softened into a sympathetic or romantic character.
