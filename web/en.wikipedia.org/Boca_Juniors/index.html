Club Atlético Boca Juniors is an Argentine professional football club based in the La Boca neighbourhood of Buenos Aires. Founded in 1906 by Italian immigrants, it is one of the most successful and popular clubs in South America.

History

Boca has won numerous Primera División titles and six Copa Libertadores trophies, most recently in 2007, along with three Intercontinental Cups. Its fierce rivalry with River Plate, the Superclásico, is one of the most famous derbies in world football.

Stadium and colours

Boca plays at the Alberto J. Armando Stadium, universally known as La Bombonera ("the chocolate box") for its steep, compact shape, and wears blue shirts with a horizontal gold band—a design adopted in 1913.
