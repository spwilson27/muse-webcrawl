Doctor Sleep is a 2019 American supernatural horror film written, directed, and edited by Mike Flanagan. It is based on the 2013 novel by Stephen King, itself a sequel to King's 1977 novel The Shining, and the film serves as a sequel both to the novel and to Stanley Kubrick's 1980 film adaptation — a reconciliation Flanagan negotiated with King's approval.

Plot

Decades after the events at the Overlook Hotel, Dan Torrance (Ewan McGregor) is a traumatised alcoholic who suppresses his psychic gift, "the shining". Working as a hospice orderly — where his gift earns him the nickname "Doctor Sleep" — he is drawn into protecting Abra Stone (Kyliegh Curran), a teenager whose shining is far stronger, from the True Knot, a cult of psychic vampires led by Rose the Hat (Rebecca Ferguson) who feed on the "steam" of murdered children. The climax returns to the Overlook Hotel, where Dan confronts the ghosts of his past.

Production and release

Flanagan recreated scenes from Kubrick's film and cast lookalike actors for Jack Torrance and other original characters, while also restoring elements of King's novel that Kubrick had omitted, including the redemptive ending for Dan. The film received generally positive reviews, with praise for its performances and emotional depth, but underperformed at the box office, grossing about $72 million against reported expectations of a franchise launch. A three-hour director's cut was released on home media in 2020 and is often preferred by critics.
