The National Broadcasting Company (NBC) is an American commercial broadcast television and radio network serving as the flagship property of the NBC Entertainment division of NBCUniversal, a subsidiary of Comcast. Founded in 1926 by the Radio Corporation of America (RCA), NBC is the oldest of the traditional "Big Three" American television networks and is sometimes referred to as the "Peacock Network", in reference to its stylized peacock logo, introduced in 1956 to promote the company's innovations in early colour broadcasting.

History

Radio

NBC began as a radio network in 1926, owned by RCA with partners General Electric and Westinghouse. It operated two networks, the Red Network and the Blue Network; the Blue Network was divested in the early 1940s under federal antitrust pressure and became the American Broadcasting Company (ABC) in 1945. NBC Radio was a leader in early broadcasting, with stars such as Jack Benny and the NBC Symphony Orchestra under Arturo Toscanini.

Television

NBC began regular television broadcasts in 1939 from New York. It pioneered colour television in the 1950s, and by 1965 nearly all of its prime-time schedule was in colour. Long-running and landmark programmes include The Tonight Show (launched 1954), Saturday Night Live (1975–present), the Today show, and hit series such as Cheers, Seinfeld, Friends, ER, Law & Order, and The Office. Its "Must See TV" Thursday-night line-up dominated American ratings in the 1990s.

Operations

NBC broadcasts from 30 Rockefeller Plaza ("30 Rock") in New York City, home of Studio 8H where Saturday Night Live is produced. The network reaches viewers through more than 200 affiliated stations across the United States. Programming divisions include NBC News, NBC Sports — which holds rights to the Olympic Games and Sunday Night Football — and NBC Entertainment.

Ownership

RCA was acquired by General Electric in 1986. In 2004 NBC merged with Vivendi Universal's entertainment assets to form NBCUniversal; Comcast acquired a controlling stake in 2011 and full ownership in 2013.
