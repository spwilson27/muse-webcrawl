FC Porto

Futebol Clube do Porto, commonly known as FC Porto, is a Portuguese professional sports club based in Porto. It is best known for its professional football team playing in the Primeira Liga. Founded in 1893, Porto is one of the "Big Three" teams in Portugal, together with Lisbon-based rivals Benfica and Sporting CP.

History

FC Porto was founded by wine merchant António Nicolau de Almeida. The club's golden era in European football came under José Mourinho, who led Porto to the UEFA Cup in 2003 and the UEFA Champions League in 2004. Porto also won the European Cup in 1987 under Artur Jorge.

Honours

Porto has won numerous Primeira Liga titles, Portuguese Cups, and two Intercontinental Cups, and is among the most successful Portuguese clubs in international competitions.
