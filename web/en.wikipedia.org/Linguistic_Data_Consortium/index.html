Linguistic Data Consortium

The Linguistic Data Consortium (LDC) is an open consortium of universities, libraries, corporations and government research laboratories, founded in 1992 and hosted by the University of Pennsylvania.

The LDC creates, collects and distributes speech and text databases, lexicons and other language resources for research in human language technology, including corpora used in DARPA evaluations, the Switchboard corpus and many multilingual datasets catalogued by member access or licence.
