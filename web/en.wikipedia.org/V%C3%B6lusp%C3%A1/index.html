Völuspá

Völuspá ("Prophecy of the Seeress") is the first and best-known poem of the Poetic Edda, the Old Norse collection of mythological verse. In it, a völva (seeress), summoned by Odin, recounts the history of the world from its creation to its destruction at Ragnarök and its rebirth.

Content

The poem describes the shaping of the cosmos from the void Ginnungagap, the creation of the first humans, Ask and Embla, the death of Baldr, and the chain of events leading to the final battle, in which gods and giants perish and the world sinks into the sea before rising green and renewed.

Sources

Völuspá is preserved principally in the Codex Regius manuscript (c. 1270) and in Hauksbók, and is quoted extensively by Snorri Sturluson in the Prose Edda.
