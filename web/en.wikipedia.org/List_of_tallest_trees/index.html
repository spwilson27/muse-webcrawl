List of tallest trees

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of the tallest trees presents the tallest known tree species and individual specimens, measured by modern techniques including laser rangefinders and climber-deployed tape drops. The world's tallest trees are found in the temperate rainforests of California and Tasmania and, for tropical species, in the dipterocarp forests of Borneo.

Tallest species by group

  • Coast redwood — tallest conifer and tallest tree overall
  • Mountain ash (Eucalyptus regnans) — tallest flowering plant (angiosperm); historical specimens were reported near 130 m, though unverified
  • Yellow meranti (Shorea faguetiana) — tallest tropical tree
  • Giant sequoia — slightly shorter than coast redwood but far more massive
  • Sitka spruce and Douglas-fir — historically among the tallest, with 19th-century Douglas-firs credibly reported above 100 m

Measurement controversies

Many 19th- and early 20th-century height records were exaggerated by unreliable methods, such as measuring felled trees incorrectly or estimating from shadows. Modern standards require direct measurement, and only a handful of species have verified living specimens over 100 m: coast redwood, giant sequoia, mountain ash, yellow meranti, and a few others. Theoretical maximum tree height is limited by the physics of lifting water to the crown, to roughly 120–130 m.
