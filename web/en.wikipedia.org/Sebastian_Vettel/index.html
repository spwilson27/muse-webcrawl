Sebastian Vettel (German pronunciation: [zeˈbasti̯a(ː)n ˈfɛtl̩]; born 3 July 1987) is a German former racing driver who competed in Formula One from 2007 to 2022 for BMW Sauber, Toro Rosso, Red Bull, Ferrari, and Aston Martin. Vettel is one of the most successful drivers in Formula One history, having won four consecutive World Drivers' Championship titles from 2010 to 2013 with Red Bull. He ranks third in all-time Grand Prix victories (53) and podium finishes, and fourth in pole positions.

Vettel started his Formula One career as a test driver for BMW Sauber in 2006 and made his race debut in 2007. Part of the Red Bull Junior Team, he appeared for Toro Rosso later that year and was kept as a driver for 2008. In his second race of that season, at a rain-soaked Monza, he became the youngest driver at the time to win a Grand Prix, aged 21. Promoted to Red Bull for 2009, he finished runner-up to Jenson Button before dominating the following era: his 2013 season, with nine consecutive wins, matched a record held by Alberto Ascari and Michael Schumacher. Winning the 2010 title at the final race in Abu Dhabi aged 23 years and 134 days made him the youngest world champion in the sport's history.

Ferrari and Aston Martin years

Vettel joined Ferrari in 2015, hoping to emulate his hero Michael Schumacher. He won 14 races for the team and was championship runner-up in 2017 and 2018, but errors in both campaigns and a deteriorating relationship with the team — culminating in being outpaced by new teammate Charles Leclerc in 2019 — led to his departure at the end of 2020. He spent two seasons with Aston Martin (2021–2022), scoring the team's first podium in Azerbaijan, before retiring at the end of 2022 to focus on family and environmental activism, an increasingly prominent part of his public life in his later career.

Personal life and legacy

Vettel is a private person by the standards of modern sport, long avoiding social media and famously naming his cars (including "Kinky Kylie" and "Gina"). He is married with three children and lives in Switzerland. Since retirement he has campaigned on sustainability, appearing at events such as COP climate summits and driving demonstration runs in his own historic cars on synthetic fuel.
