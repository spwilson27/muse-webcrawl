Star Trek: The Next Generation (TNG) is an American science fiction television series created by Gene Roddenberry. It originally aired from September 28, 1987, to May 23, 1994, in syndication, spanning 178 episodes over seven seasons. The third series in the Star Trek franchise, it was inspired by Star Trek: The Original Series. Set in the last third of the 24th century, when Earth is part of the United Federation of Planets, it follows the adventures of a Starfleet starship, the USS Enterprise-D, in its exploration of the Alpha quadrant and Beta Quadrant in the Milky Way Galaxy.

Premise

The series follows the adventures of the crew of the USS Enterprise-D, a Galaxy-class starship commanded by Captain Jean-Luc Picard. Episodes generally follow an episodic format, though several multi-part stories and season-long character arcs appear throughout the run. Many episodes explore themes of diplomacy, the human condition, and artificial intelligence.

Cast and characters

The ensemble cast includes Patrick Stewart as Captain Jean-Luc Picard; Jonathan Frakes as Commander William Riker; LeVar Burton as Lieutenant Geordi La Forge; Michael Dorn as Lieutenant Worf; Gates McFadden as Dr. Beverly Crusher; Marina Sirtis as Counselor Deanna Troi; Brent Spiner as Lt. Commander Data; Wil Wheaton as Wesley Crusher; and Denise Crosby as Lieutenant Tasha Yar in the first season.

Production

Roddenberry initially resisted a continuation of the franchise after the cancellation of The Original Series, but Paramount pursued the project in the mid-1980s. Rather than sell to a network, the studio distributed the series in first-run syndication, which gave the creative team unusual freedom. The series established the 24th-century setting used by subsequent series including Deep Space Nine and Voyager.

Legacy

The Next Generation is widely regarded as the most popular Star Trek series, and it won numerous awards including a Peabody Award and multiple Primetime Emmy Awards. Four feature films followed: Star Trek Generations (1994), Star Trek: First Contact (1996), Star Trek: Insurrection (1998), and Star Trek: Nemesis (2002). The sequel series Star Trek: Picard premiered in 2020.
