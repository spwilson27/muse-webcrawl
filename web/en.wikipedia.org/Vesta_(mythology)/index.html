Vesta (mythology)

Vesta was the Roman virgin goddess of the hearth, home, and family, and one of the twelve Dii Consentes. She is the Roman counterpart of the Greek goddess Hestia.

Worship

Vesta's sacred fire in the Temple of Vesta in the Roman Forum was tended by the Vestal Virgins, and was believed to protect the city of Rome. The Vestalia, her chief festival, was celebrated each June. Because the goddess was associated with virginity and the sanctity of the home, she was depicted veiled and almost never shown in scenes of conflict.

Legacy

The name survives in the asteroid 4 Vesta, in the Vestal Virgins of the Catholic order, and in the phrase "vestal virgin" denoting ritual chastity.
