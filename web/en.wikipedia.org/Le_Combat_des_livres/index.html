Le Combat des livres

Le Combat des livres is a French-language Canadian literary game show broadcast by Radio-Canada. Modelled on the CBC's English-language programme Canada Reads, the show invites prominent francophone personalities to champion a Canadian book in French, debating over several episodes to persuade panel and public alike of their chosen work's merits until one title is declared the winner.

Format

Each season, five panellists each select a book to defend. Over the course of the series the champions debate, and one book is eliminated per round until a single winner remains. The programme has highlighted works by Quebec and other French-Canadian authors, contributing to public discussion of francophone literature in Canada. The show has been hosted by figures including Marie-Louise Arsenault and Christiane Charette in various incarnations.

History

Le Combat des livres was launched in 2004 as the French counterpart to Canada Reads, which had begun two years earlier. It aired for a number of seasons on Radio-Canada radio and television, was revived in new formats in later years, and its winning titles frequently saw increased sales and readership across French Canada.
