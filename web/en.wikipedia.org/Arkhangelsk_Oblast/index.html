Arkhangelsk Oblast
Country: Russia
Federal district: Northwestern
Administrative centre: Arkhangelsk
Area: approx. 587,400 km² (incl. Nenets AO and Arctic islands)

Arkhangelsk Oblast is a federal subject of Russia (an oblast) located in the north of European Russia. Its administrative centre is the city of Arkhangelsk, a historic port on the Northern Dvina River near the White Sea.

Geography

The oblast borders Karelia, Vologda Oblast, Kirov Oblast, and the Komi Republic. It includes the Nenets Autonomous Okrug and the Arctic archipelagos of Franz Josef Land and Novaya Zemlya, as well as the Solovetsky Islands in the White Sea. Major rivers include the Northern Dvina, Mezen, Pechora (partly), and Onega.

History

Arkhangelsk, founded in 1584, was Russia's first and for a time only seaport for trade with Western Europe. The oblast in its present form was established in 1937. The Plesetsk Cosmodrome, a major Russian spaceport, is located in the oblast near Mirny.

Economy

Timber and pulp-and-paper production, fishing, shipbuilding at Severodvinsk (including nuclear submarines), and diamond mining are key industries.
