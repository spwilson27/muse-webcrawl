Nintendo eShop

The Nintendo eShop is a digital distribution service operated by Nintendo for its video game consoles. It launched on the Nintendo 3DS in June 2011 via a system update, and later appeared on the Wii U (2012) and Nintendo Switch (2017). Through the eShop, users can purchase and download games, downloadable content, demos, and applications, and view trailers and reviews.

Features

The eShop offers full retail games, digital-only indie titles, downloadable content (DLC), free demos, and (on 3DS and Wii U) Virtual Console re-releases of classic games. Purchases were historically tied to individual consoles; with the Switch and the unified Nintendo Account system, purchases became account-based. On Switch, the eShop is closely integrated with the Nintendo Switch Online subscription service, which offers cloud saves, online play, and a library of classic games.

Closures

Nintendo closed the eShop for Wii U and Nintendo 3DS to new purchases in March 2023, ending the sale of hundreds of digital-only and Virtual Console titles on those platforms, though previously purchased software can still be re-downloaded.
