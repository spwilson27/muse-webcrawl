Net.jokes (rec.humor)

net.jokes was an early Usenet newsgroup for humorous posts, created in the early 1980s as part of the original net.* hierarchy. In the Great Renaming of 1987 it became rec.humor, with spun-off groups including rec.humor.funny (moderated) and rec.humor.oracle.

History

The group was among the most-read on early Usenet. rec.humor.funny, moderated by Brad Templeton, became one of the first widely read moderated comedy publications on the net and published annual joke collections. The newsgroups helped shape early internet humour conventions, FAQs, and netiquette debates.

Legacy

As the web displaced Usenet, humour migrated to websites and forums, but archived net.jokes and rec.humor posts remain a record of pre-web internet culture.
