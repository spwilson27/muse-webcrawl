Slope rating

Slope rating is a number that describes the relative difficulty of a golf course for a bogey golfer in comparison to a scratch golfer. It is used, together with the course rating, in handicap systems such as the World Handicap System to allow golfers of different abilities to compete equitably on courses of differing difficulty.

Calculation

Under the United States Golf Association system, slope rating is calculated from the difference between the bogey rating and the course rating, multiplied by a constant (5.381 for men, 4.24 for women). Slope ratings range from 55 to 155, with 113 considered the standard or average difficulty.

Use in handicaps

A player's course handicap for a given set of tees is computed by scaling their handicap index by the slope rating divided by 113, adjusted for the difference between course rating and par. The steeper the slope, the more strokes a higher-handicap golfer receives relative to a scratch golfer.
