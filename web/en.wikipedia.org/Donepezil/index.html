Donepezil

Donepezil, sold under the brand name Aricept among others, is a medication used to treat Alzheimer's disease. It is a reversible acetylcholinesterase inhibitor that increases levels of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine in the brain by blocking its breakdown.

Medical uses

Donepezil is used for the symptomatic treatment of mild, moderate, and severe Alzheimer's disease. It does not cure or slow the progression of the underlying disease, but it can modestly improve cognition, memory, and daily functioning in some patients. It is taken once daily by mouth.

Side effects

Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, insomnia, vomiting, muscle cramps, fatigue, and loss of appetite. Less commonly it can cause bradycardia and gastrointestinal bleeding. It was first approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in 1996.
