Kingscote, South Australia

Kingscote is the largest town on Kangaroo Island in South Australia, and the island's administrative and commercial centre. It is located on the shore of Nepean Bay on the island's north-east coast.

History

Kingscote was founded on 27 July 1836 by the South Australian Company, making it the first formal European settlement in South Australia, predating Adelaide. The settlement was soon eclipsed by the mainland capital, but the town developed as a port and service centre for the island's farmers and fishers.

Today

The town has a population of roughly two thousand and is known for its tidal swimming pool, pelican feeding, historic buildings and as a base for exploring Kangaroo Island's wildlife and national parks.
