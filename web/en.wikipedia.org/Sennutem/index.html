Sennutem

Sennutem (also rendered Sennofri or Senneferi in some sources) was an ancient Egyptian artisan who lived under the reigns of Seti I and Ramesses II during the 19th Dynasty of the New Kingdom. He lived in the workers' village of Deir el-Medina on the west bank of the Nile opposite Thebes.

Life

Sennutem held the title "Servant in the Place of Truth", meaning he worked on the excavation and decoration of the royal tombs in the Valley of the Kings. He was the son of Khabekhnet and Tahaynu. He was married to Iyneferti, and the couple had several children.

Tomb

Sennutem was buried together with his wife Iyneferti in tomb TT71's associated burial chamber, TT1, at Deir el-Medina. The small chapel and burial chamber are famous for their well-preserved painted decoration, including an astronomical ceiling and a celebrated scene of the couple ploughing and harvesting in the Field of Reeds of the afterlife. A large number of funerary objects from the tomb are now in museums around the world.
