Eyehategod

Eyehategod (also abbreviated as EHG) is an American sludge metal band from New Orleans, Louisiana, formed in 1988. They are widely regarded as pioneers of the sludge metal genre, combining doom metal and hardcore punk with blues influences and feedback-laden guitar work.

Eyehategod
Origin: New Orleans, Louisiana, US
Genres: Sludge metal, doom metal, hardcore punk
Years active: 1988–present

History

The band's early albums, including In the Name of Suffering (1990) and Take as Needed for Pain (1993), helped define the New Orleans metal sound alongside Crowbar and Acid Bath. Vocalist Mike Williams's lyrics and the band's abrasive style became highly influential.

Discography

Studio albums include Dopesick (1996), Confederacy of Ruined Lives (2000), Eyehategod (2014), and A History of Nomadic Behavior (2021).
