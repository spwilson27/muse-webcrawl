Silvopasture

Silvopasture (from Latin silva, 'forest') is the practice of integrating trees, forage, and the grazing of domesticated animals in a mutually beneficial way. It is one of the oldest forms of agriculture and a recognized form of agroforestry.

In a silvopasture system, livestock graze among trees that provide shade and shelter while producing timber, fruit, nuts, or other products. The forage understory is managed for grazing, and the system can improve animal welfare, diversify farm income, and sequester carbon.

Examples include the dehesa systems of Spain and Portugal, where cattle and pigs graze among oak trees, and pine-based systems in the southeastern United States.
