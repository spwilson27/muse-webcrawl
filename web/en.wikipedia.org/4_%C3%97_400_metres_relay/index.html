The 4 × 400 metres relay or long relay is an athletics track event in which teams of four runners each complete 400 metres, one full lap of the track. It is traditionally the final event of a track meet and is known for dramatic anchor-leg finishes.

Rules

The race begins from a staggered start; the first leg is run entirely in lanes, and runners break for the inside after the first bend of the second leg (a three-turn stagger). The baton must be exchanged within a 20-metre zone measured by lines. Unlike the 4 × 100 metres relay, the exchange is visual, with the outgoing runner watching the incoming teammate.

History

The men's event appeared at the 1912 Stockholm Olympics and the women's at Munich 1972. A mixed-gender 4 × 400 metres relay was introduced at the 2019 World Championships and the Tokyo 2020 Olympics. The event requires both sprint speed and endurance, and anchor runners often produce memorable duels, such as the Bahamian victory over the United States at London 2012.
