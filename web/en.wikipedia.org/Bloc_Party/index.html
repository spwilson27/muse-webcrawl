Bloc Party are an English rock band formed in London in 1999 by lead singer and rhythm guitarist Kele Okereke and lead guitarist Russell Lissack. The band's music has been compared to the Cure, Gang of Four, and Franz Ferdinand, combining angular guitar rock with dance and electronic influences.

History

Okereke and Lissack, who had known each other at school in Essex, recruited bassist Gordon Moakes and drummer Matt Tong, settling on the name Bloc Party in 2003. Their debut album Silent Alarm (2005), released on Wichita Recordings, was critically acclaimed — named album of the year by NME — and included the singles "Helicopter," "Banquet," and "So Here We Are." A Weekend in the City (2007) reached number two in the UK and produced the top-ten single "The Prayer." The band experimented with electronic production on Intimacy (2008), rushed out as a surprise download release.

After a hiatus during which Okereke pursued solo work, the band returned with Four (2012). Moakes and Tong subsequently departed, replaced by Justin Harris and Louise Bartle, and the reconfigured line-up released Hymns (2016) and Alpha Games (2022).

Musical style

Bloc Party's sound pairs Okereke's urgent vocals and Lissack's effect-laden guitar lines with a rhythm section influenced by dance music; later work drew increasingly on electronic textures, gospel, and R&B.
