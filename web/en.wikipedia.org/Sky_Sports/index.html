Sky Sports

Sky Sports revolutionised televised football in Britain from 1992

Sky Sports is a group of British subscription sports channels operated by Sky Group. Launched in 1990 as the Sports Channel and rebranded in 1991, it became a dominant force in British sports broadcasting after securing rights to the newly formed Premier League in 1992, a deal credited with transforming the finances of English football.

Channels and coverage

The service comprises a family of channels including Sky Sports Main Event, Premier League, Football, Cricket, Golf, F1, Tennis and News. Sky Sports broadcasts live football (Premier League, EFL and Scottish football), Formula One, international and domestic cricket, golf's majors and Ryder Cup, darts, boxing and NFL American football, among many other sports.

Impact

Sky's huge investment in broadcast rights reshaped British sport: kick-off times were scheduled for television, clubs' revenues grew dramatically, and sports presentation adopted innovations such as Monday Night Football analysis, interactive red-button coverage and extensive use of statistics. Sky Sports content is also offered through streaming via Sky Go and NOW.
