Let's Move!

Let's Move! was a public health campaign in the United States launched by First Lady Michelle Obama in February 2010, dedicated to solving the problem of childhood obesity within a generation. Its slogan encouraged children to be active and families to make healthier choices.

Goals and initiatives

The campaign worked on five pillars: creating a healthy start for children, empowering parents, providing healthy food in schools, improving access to healthy and affordable food, and increasing physical activity. Related efforts included the White House Kitchen Garden, the Chefs Move to Schools programme and updated school meal standards under the Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act of 2010.

Reception

Supporters credited the campaign with raising national attention on child nutrition and prompting food companies to cut calories and marketing changes; critics questioned the role of government in personal food choices.
