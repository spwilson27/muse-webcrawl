FC Utrecht
Founded: 1 July 1970
Ground: Stadion Galgenwaard, Utrecht (capacity 23,750)
League: Eredivisie
Nickname: Utreg

Football Club Utrecht is a Dutch professional football club based in Utrecht, founded on 1 July 1970 through the merger of three clubs from the city: DOS, Elinkwijk and Velox. The club plays in the Eredivisie and has been a fixture of the Dutch top flight for most of its existence, being one of the few clubs outside the traditional "big three" never to have been relegated from the Eredivisie for much of its history.

History

Predecessor club DOS won the Dutch championship in 1958. FC Utrecht's principal honours are three KNVB Cups (1985, 2003 and 2004) and the Johan Cruyff Shield in 2004. The club has regularly qualified for European competition, reaching the UEFA Cup/Europa League group stages on several occasions. Famous players include Dirk Kuyt, who began and ended his career in the Netherlands with Utrecht and Feyenoord, and Sébastien Haller, who played for the club before moving to Germany.

Stadium and supporters

Utrecht's home is Stadion Galgenwaard, opened in its modern form in 2004 on the site of the old Galgenwaard. The Bunnikside stand houses the club's most fanatical supporters. The club's main rivalry is with Ajax of Amsterdam, reflecting the proximity and historical tension between the two cities.
