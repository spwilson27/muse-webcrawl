Larian Studios is a Belgian video game developer and publisher founded in 1996 by Swen Vincke and headquartered in Ghent. The studio is best known for the Divinity series of role-playing games and for developing Baldur's Gate 3 (2023), which won numerous Game of the Year awards. Larian maintains studios in several countries, including Ireland, Spain, Malaysia, England, Canada, and Poland.

History

Swen Vincke founded Larian in 1996; the studio's name derives from his dog, Lar. Early projects included the educational game The Lady, the Mage and the Knight, which was cancelled, and contract work such as LED Wars (1997). The studio's breakthrough came with the action role-playing game Divine Divinity (2002), followed by Beyond Divinity (2004). After Divinity II (2009) and the strategy spin-off Dragon Commander (2013), Larian turned to crowdfunding.

Divinity: Original Sin (2014), funded partly through a successful Kickstarter campaign, was a critical and commercial success for its cooperative multiplayer and systemic, physics-driven combat. Its sequel, Divinity: Original Sin II (2017), released after an early access period, was acclaimed as one of the best role-playing games of its decade. Wizards of the Coast then entrusted Larian with Baldur's Gate 3, which launched in early access in 2020 and fully released in August 2023 to widespread acclaim, winning Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2023 and the Golden Joystick Awards, and later the Hugo Award for Best Game or Interactive Work.

Following Baldur's Gate 3, Vincke announced that Larian would not develop downloadable content or a sequel for it, and would move away from the Dungeons & Dragons license to work on new projects, returning to self-publishing and creative independence. The Chinese conglomerate Tencent holds a minority stake in the studio.
