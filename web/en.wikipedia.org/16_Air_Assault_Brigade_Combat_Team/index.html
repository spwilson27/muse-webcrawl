16 Air Assault Brigade Combat Team, known simply as 16 Air Assault Brigade until 2021, is a formation of the British Army based at Colchester in Essex. It is the Army's airborne rapid reaction force and, from 2021, was redesignated as a brigade combat team as part of the Future Soldier reforms. The brigade specialises in air assault operations using helicopters and parachute insertion.

History

The brigade was formed on 1 September 1999 by the amalgamation of 5 Airborne Brigade and 24 Airmobile Brigade, bringing together the Parachute Regiment and airmobile units. It deployed to Macedonia in 2001, Iraq in 2003, and repeatedly to Helmand Province, Afghanistan, including the major Operation Herrick deployments of 2006, 2008, and 2010–11.

Composition

Its core units include battalions of the Parachute Regiment, supported by aviation from the Army Air Corps, artillery, engineers, signals, and logistics units. Its soldiers wear the maroon beret associated with British airborne forces.
