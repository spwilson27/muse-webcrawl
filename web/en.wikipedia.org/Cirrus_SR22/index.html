The Cirrus SR22 is a single-engine, four- or five-seat, composite light aircraft built by Cirrus Aircraft of Duluth, Minnesota. Introduced in 2001 as a higher-performance development of the SR20, it has been the world's best-selling general aviation piston aircraft for most years since 2003. The type is built in successive "generations" (G1 through G7), with turbocharged versions designated SR22T.

Design

The SR22 has a Continental IO-550-N engine of 310 hp, fixed tricycle landing gear, a glass cockpit (Cirrus Perspective by Garmin in later models) and side-stick controls. Its signature safety feature is the Cirrus Airframe Parachute System (CAPS), a whole-aircraft ballistic parachute fitted as standard, credited with saving many lives. Cruising at about 183 knots, the aircraft is popular with owner-pilots for personal transport.
