Mrs. America is an American historical drama television miniseries created by Dahvi Waller, produced by FX and originally distributed through FX on Hulu. It premiered on April 15, 2020. The series dramatises the political movement to pass the Equal Rights Amendment (ERA) and the unexpected backlash led by conservative activist Phyllis Schlafly in the 1970s.

Plot and characters

Told through the eyes of women on both sides of the fight, the series follows Schlafly (Cate Blanchett), who built a grassroots movement of homemakers that helped defeat ratification of the ERA, and the feminist leaders arrayed against her: Gloria Steinem (Rose Byrne), Betty Friedan (Tracey Ullman), Shirley Chisholm (Uzo Aduba), Bella Abzug (Margo Martindale), and Jill Ruckelshaus (Elizabeth Banks), along with Schlafly's ally Alice Macray (Sarah Paulson), a composite character. Each episode centres on a different woman's perspective on the era's battles over abortion rights, gay rights, and the role of women in American life.

Reception

The series received widespread critical acclaim for its performances, writing, and direction, and was nominated for multiple Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Limited Series. Uzo Aduba won the Emmy for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited Series for her portrayal of Shirley Chisholm, the first Black woman elected to Congress and a 1972 presidential candidate. Some family members and former associates of Schlafly criticised the series' portrayal of her.
