Kh-35

The Kh-35 (Russian: Х-35; NATO reporting name AS-20 "Kayak") is a Soviet/Russian turbojet subsonic anti-ship cruise missile developed by Zvezda-Strela, sometimes nicknamed the "Harpoonski" for its resemblance to the American Harpoon.

Design

Entering service in the 2000s, the Kh-35 can be launched from ships, aircraft, helicopters and coastal Bal batteries. It flies at sea-skimming altitude with active radar homing, carrying a warhead of about 145 kg, with a range of roughly 130–260 km depending on variant.
