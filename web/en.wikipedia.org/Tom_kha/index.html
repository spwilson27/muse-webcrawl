Tom kha

Tom kha (Thai: ต้มข่า, literally "boiled galangal") is a family of Thai soups made by simmering ingredients in coconut milk with galangal as the defining aromatic. The best-known member of the family is tom kha gai, made with chicken, but the base technique is applied to mushrooms, seafood, pork and tofu as well.

Ingredients and preparation

The soup is built on coconut milk seasoned with galangal (kha), lemongrass, makrut lime leaves, lime juice, fish sauce and chilies. Unlike tom yum, which is usually a clear, sharply sour broth, tom kha is rich and milky, with the galangal giving a piney, citrus-like flavour quite different from ginger. The soup is typically finished rather than boiled hard once the protein is added, to keep the coconut milk from splitting.

Variations

Common variations include tom kha gai (chicken), tom kha het (mushroom), tom kha thale (mixed seafood) and tom kha mu (pork). Vegetarian versions substitute soy sauce or salt for fish sauce. The dish is served with steamed jasmine rice and is a standard item on Thai restaurant menus worldwide.
