Stadion Galgenwaard

Stadion Galgenwaard (Dutch pronunciation: [ˈstaːdijɔn ˈɣɑlɣə(n)ʋaːrt]) is a football stadium in Utrecht, Netherlands. It has been the home of the football club FC Utrecht since 1970. The stadium, which underwent a renovation starting in 2000, has a capacity of 23,750 spectators.

History

The name Galgenwaard refers to the gallows field (galgenveld) that stood on the site in earlier centuries. The original stadium was opened in 1936 and served several Utrecht clubs. From 1970 it became the home ground of the newly merged FC Utrecht. The stadium was rebuilt almost entirely around the turn of the twenty-first century, with work completing in phases in the early 2000s, giving it four modern stands and a markedly larger capacity than the old ground.

International matches and events

The Galgenwaard has hosted matches of the Netherlands national football team, including World Cup qualifiers, and was one of the venues of the 2005 FIFA World Youth Championship held in the Netherlands, staging group matches and knockout fixtures including a semi-final. The stadium also hosts concerts and other events, and its facilities include hospitality areas and the FC Utrecht museum.
