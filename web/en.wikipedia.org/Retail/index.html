Retail

Retail is the sale of goods and services to consumers for personal or household use, as opposed to wholesale, which sells to businesses and institutions. Retailers purchase products from manufacturers or wholesalers and sell them in smaller quantities through physical stores, e-commerce, mail order, and other channels.

Formats

Retail formats include department stores, supermarkets, convenience stores, specialty shops, discount and warehouse clubs, pop-up shops, and online marketplaces. The industry is characterised by the "four Ps" of marketing — product, price, place, and promotion — and by thin margins offset by high sales volume in sectors such as grocery.

Trends

Since the 1990s, e-commerce has reshaped retail, driving omnichannel strategies, the decline of many shopping malls, and growth in direct-to-consumer brands. Retail is one of the largest private-sector employers in many economies, and consumer spending data from the sector is a key economic indicator.
