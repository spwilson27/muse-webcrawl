Hunter Mountain (New York)

Hunter Mountain is a mountain in the town of Hunter in Greene County, New York, within the Catskill Mountains. At about 4,040 feet (1,231 m) above sea level, it is the second-highest peak in the Catskills, after Slide Mountain, and the highest that is not in a wilderness area. The mountain is best known as the site of the Hunter Mountain ski area.

Ski area

The Hunter Mountain ski resort opened in 1959, developed by the Slutzky brothers, local construction contractors who built the original trails. It was one of the first ski areas in the eastern United States to invest heavily in snowmaking, and it grew into one of the closest major ski resorts to New York City, roughly two hours' drive away. The resort offers dozens of trails across multiple faces, terrain parks, and summer attractions; it was acquired by Peak Resorts in 2015 and subsequently became part of Vail Resorts through its 2019 acquisition of Peak Resorts.

Fire tower and hiking

A steel fire tower near the summit, erected in 1917, is among the highest in New York State and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places; it is maintained by volunteers and open to hikers in season. Trails to the summit include routes from Spruceton Valley and the Becker Hollow trail, the shortest and steepest approach.

Events

The mountain and the village of Hunter host summer festivals, including German Alps and Celtic festivals, and the Mountain Jam music festival was held at the resort for many years.
