Founded10 March 1925

NicknameThrylos (The Legend)

Olympiacos F.C. is a Greek professional football club based in Piraeus, part of the multi-sport club Olympiacos CFP founded in 1925. It is the most successful club in Greek football history, having won a record number of league championships (over 45) and Greek Cups, and it is famous for its intense rivalry with Panathinaikos, the "derby of the eternal enemies".

History

The club was formed by merchants, military officers and workers of Piraeus and quickly became identified with the port city's working class. Olympiacos has played at the Karaiskakis Stadium for most of its history. In Europe, the club's greatest triumph came in 2024, when it won the UEFA Europa Conference League — the first European trophy for a Greek club — defeating Fiorentina in the final in Athens. Its youth team also won the UEFA Youth League the same season.

Support

Olympiacos' organised supporters, Gate 7, take their name from the stadium section where 21 fans died in a crush at Karaiskakis Stadium in 1981, the worst disaster in Greek football.
