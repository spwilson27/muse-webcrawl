List of universities in Israel

Higher education in Israel is overseen by the Council for Higher Education. Israel has ten recognised universities, along with dozens of accredited academic colleges. Several Israeli universities rank among the world's leading research institutions, and the country has among the highest shares of population holding tertiary degrees in the OECD.

Academic colleges

Beyond the universities, more than fifty academic colleges — such as the Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design, the Jerusalem Academy of Music and Dance, Shenkar College, and regional colleges in the Galilee and Negev — offer accredited bachelor's and (in some cases) master's degrees, though they generally do not award research doctorates.
