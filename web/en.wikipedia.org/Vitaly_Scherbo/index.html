Vitaly Scherbo

Vitaly Venediktovich Scherbo (born 13 January 1972) is a Belarusian former artistic gymnast. He is one of the most successful gymnasts of all time and the only male gymnast ever to have won a world title in all eight events. At the 1992 Summer Olympics in Barcelona, competing for the Unified Team, he won six gold medals — a record for a single gymnastics competition at one Games — in the team, all-around, pommel horse, rings, vault, and parallel bars events.

Career

Born in Minsk, then in the Soviet Union, Scherbo rose through the Soviet gymnastics system and won his first world titles in 1991. His dominance at Barcelona 1992 included a near-sweep of the individual apparatus finals. Representing Belarus at the 1996 Atlanta Olympics, he won four bronze medals despite injuries and personal upheaval. His career total of 23 world championship medals stood among the highest in the sport's history. Several skills in the Code of Points bear his name.

Personal life and later years

In 1995 robbers broke into Scherbo's home, stealing valuables including his Olympic medals (later recovered) and attacking his wife Irina, who survived a period in a coma. Scherbo emigrated to the United States, settling in Las Vegas, Nevada, where he opened a gymnastics school, Vitaly Scherbo's School of Gymnastics. In 2017 he was accused by former teammate Tatiana Gutsu of having raped her in 1991; Scherbo denied the allegation. He was inducted into the International Gymnastics Hall of Fame in 2009.
