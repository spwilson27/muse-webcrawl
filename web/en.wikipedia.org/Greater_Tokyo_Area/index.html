The Greater Tokyo Area is the metropolitan area centered on Tokyo, Japan, and is the most populous metropolitan area in the world. Depending on the definition used, it encompasses Tokyo Metropolis and surrounding prefectures, most commonly the "One Metropolis, Three Prefectures" of Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba, and Saitama.

Definitions

The Kantō Major Metropolitan Area, as defined by the Statistics Bureau of Japan, covers a wide commuting zone with a population of roughly 38 million. Broader definitions that include the entire southern Kantō region push estimates above 40 million. The region includes the major cities of Yokohama (Japan's second-largest city), Kawasaki, Chiba, and Saitama, as well as the ports of Tokyo Bay.

Economy and transport

Greater Tokyo generates a gross regional product comparable to that of large national economies and hosts the headquarters of most major Japanese corporations, the Tokyo Stock Exchange, and two international airports, Haneda and Narita. Its dense railway network—including the Yamanote Line, Shinkansen terminals, and multiple private railways—carries tens of millions of passengers daily.
