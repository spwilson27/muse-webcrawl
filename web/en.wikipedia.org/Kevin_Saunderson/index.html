Kevin Maurice Saunderson (born 5 September 1964) is an American electronic music producer and DJ. Along with Juan Atkins and Derrick May — the so-called Belleville Three — he is credited with originating Detroit techno in the 1980s. His group Inner City, formed with singer Paris Grey, brought techno-influenced dance music to the international pop charts.

Early life

Saunderson was born in Brooklyn, New York, and moved to Belleville, Michigan, at age nine. At Belleville High School he befriended Atkins and May; the three were exposed to the electronic sounds of Kraftwerk and the radio mixes of the Electrifying Mojo, and began experimenting with synthesizers and drum machines.

Career

In 1987 Saunderson founded KMS Records, one of Detroit techno's foundational labels, and released influential tracks under names including Reese, whose bassline on "Just Want Another Chance" (1988) became a template for UK hardcore and jungle. With vocalist Paris Grey he formed Inner City, whose singles "Big Fun" and "Good Life" (both 1988) were worldwide hits and remain dancefloor standards; the group sold millions of records and released albums including Paradise (1989).

Later work

Saunderson has continued to DJ internationally, remixed artists from New Order to the Pet Shop Boys, and mentored a second generation of Detroit producers. He has also worked with the Detroit Electronic Music Festival (Movement), and performs with his sons in the project The Saunderson Brothers.
