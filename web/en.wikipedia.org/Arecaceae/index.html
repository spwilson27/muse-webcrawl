The Arecaceae is a family of perennial flowering plants in the monocot order Arecales, commonly known as the palm family. Palms are among the most recognizable plant families, typically with a single unbranched trunk crowned by large evergreen leaves that are either palmately ("fan") or pinnately ("feather") divided.

Importance

Palms are immensely important to humans: the coconut, date palm, and oil palms are staple crops, and many species provide timber, rattan, fibre, and ornamental plantings. The date palm is among the oldest cultivated trees, and palms feature prominently in the symbolism of many cultures.

Diversity

Most palms grow in tropical and subtropical climates, especially in the Americas and Southeast Asia. They range from the tiny Dypsis species of Madagascar to the wax palms of the Andes, the tallest monocots in the world.
