Theft

Theft is the act of taking another person's property or services without that person's permission or consent, with the intent to deprive the rightful owner of it. It is a common-law criminal offence in most jurisdictions, distinct from related offences such as robbery (theft by force), burglary (unlawful entry with intent), embezzlement and fraud.

Elements

Theft generally requires an act of appropriation of property belonging to another, done dishonestly and with the intention of permanently depriving the owner. In England and Wales the offence is defined by the Theft Act 1968; in the United States theft laws vary by state, often graded by the value of the property taken.

Related offences

Related concepts include larceny, shoplifting, joyriding, identity theft and theft of services.
