Trimurti

The Trimurti is the triple deity of supreme divinity in Hinduism, in which the cosmic functions of creation, preservation and destruction are personified by Brahma the creator, Vishnu the preserver, and Shiva the destroyer.

Concept

The three gods are regarded as aspects of a single divine reality. The concept developed in the Puranic period and is famously depicted in the Trimurti sculpture at the Elephanta Caves near Mumbai, and at Prambanan in Central Java, where three temples face the main Shiva temple.

Roles

  • Brahma — creation of the universe.
  • Vishnu — preservation; descends as avatars such as Rama and Krishna.
  • Shiva — destruction and regeneration, completing the cycle.

Feminine counterparts are Saraswati, Lakshmi and Parvati, together sometimes called the Tridevi. See also Hindu temple.
