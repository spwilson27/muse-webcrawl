HMS Warspite (03)

HMS Warspite was one of five Queen Elizabeth-class battleships built for the Royal Navy during the early 1910s. Completed during the First World War in 1915, she took part in the Battle of Jutland, where she was heavily damaged.

Service

Extensively modernised between the wars, Warspite served throughout the Second World War: in the Norwegian campaign, the Mediterranean — including Calabria, Cape Matapan and Crete — escorting convoys, supporting the Salerno landings (where she was crippled by a Fritz X guided bomb), and bombarding shore targets during the Normandy landings in 1944.

Fate

One of the most decorated British warships, she earned the most battle honours of any Royal Navy ship. She ran aground in 1947 while under tow to the breakers in Mount's Bay, Cornwall, and was scrapped in place.
