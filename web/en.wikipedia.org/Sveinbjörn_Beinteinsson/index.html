Sveinbjörn Beinteinsson

Sveinbjörn Beinteinsson (1924–1993), Icelandic poet and religious leader.

Sveinbjörn Beinteinsson (4 July 1924 – 23 December 1993) was an Icelandic poet and prominent religious leader. He was the founder of the Icelandic Ásatrúarfélagið (Ásatrú Fellowship), the organization that revived the practice of Norse paganism (Ásatrú) in Iceland and achieved official state recognition for it in 1973. Sveinbjörn served as the fellowship's first allsherjargoði (high priest) until his death.

Life and work

Sveinbjörn was a sheep farmer and a respected practitioner of the traditional Icelandic rímur chanting style, preserving and recording old chants and verses. His album "Edda" (1990), setting poems from the Poetic Edda to traditional chant, brought the rímur tradition to a wider audience, and he also collaborated with members of the band Current 93.

Legacy

Under his leadership the Ásatrú Fellowship grew from its founding, and Iceland became one of the first modern states to recognize a revived pre-Christian religion. A memorial stone to Sveinbjörn stands in Reykjavík, and he remains a central figure in the modern Heathen movement.
