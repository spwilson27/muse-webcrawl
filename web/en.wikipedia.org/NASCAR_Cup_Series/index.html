The NASCAR Cup Series is the top racing series of the National Association for Stock Car Auto Racing (NASCAR). It is named for its current title sponsor and has been known under various names through its history, including the Strictly Stock Division, Grand National Series, Winston Cup Series, Nextel Cup Series, Sprint Cup Series, and Monster Energy NASCAR Cup Series.

History

The series began in 1949 as the Strictly Stock Division, with the first race held at Charlotte Speedway in North Carolina. Jim Roper won the inaugural event. In 1950 the series became the Grand National Series, a name it held for two decades. In 1972, tobacco company R.J. Reynolds' Winston brand became title sponsor, ushering in the modern era: the schedule was trimmed, points were standardized, and the Daytona 500 grew into the sport's flagship event.

Richard Petty, nicknamed "The King," won a record 200 races and seven championships, a title mark later matched by Dale Earnhardt and Jimmie Johnson. The series expanded nationally in the 1990s, adding tracks in California, Texas, Las Vegas, and Kansas, and television audiences peaked in the early 2000s.

Championship format

Since 2004 the series has used a playoff-style format. The current system, introduced in 2014, admits 16 drivers to the NASCAR Playoffs based largely on race wins during the 26-race regular season. Four drivers are eliminated after every three playoff races, leaving four to contest the championship in a winner-take-all finale, where the highest finisher among them wins the title.

Cars

Cup Series cars are purpose-built stock cars with a silhouette resembling production models from Chevrolet (Camaro), Ford (Mustang), and Toyota (Camry). The Next Gen car, introduced in 2022, standardized many components, including a single-lug wheel, independent rear suspension, and a sequential five-speed gearbox, powered by V8 engines producing roughly 670 horsepower at most tracks.

Notable races

The Daytona 500, held each February at Daytona International Speedway, is the series' most prestigious race. Other crown-jewel events include the Coca-Cola 600 at Charlotte, the Southern 500 at Darlington, and the Bristol night race. See also Daytona 500 and NASCAR.
