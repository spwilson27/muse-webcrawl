Five Boroughs of the Danelaw

The Five Boroughs were the five main towns of the Viking settlement of the Danelaw in the East Midlands of England: Derby, Leicester, Lincoln, Nottingham and Stamford.

The boroughs guarded the main routes into the Danish-ruled east.

History

Following the Treaty of Wedmore (878) and later agreements, the armies of the Danish "Great Heathen Army" settled and fortified these burhs, minting coins and trading.

Organisation

Each borough formed the centre of an administrative territory and was associated with Danish law and jarls. The five acted as a confederation and were treated jointly in later royal administration.

Reconquest

Edward the Elder and Æthelflæd recovered Derby and Leicester in 917; the remaining boroughs were taken by the 940s under Edmund I and celebrated in a poem of the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle.
