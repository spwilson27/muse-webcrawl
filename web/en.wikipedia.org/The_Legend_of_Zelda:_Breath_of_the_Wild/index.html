The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild is a 2017 action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch and Wii U. The player controls Link, who awakens from a hundred-year slumber to a ruined kingdom of Hyrule and must regain his memories, free the four Divine Beasts, and defeat Calamity Ganon before he escapes Hyrule Castle and destroys the world.

Gameplay

Breaking with the series' traditional linear structure, the game presents a vast seamless open world governed by consistent physics and chemistry systems, encouraging experimentation with climbing, gliding, cooking, and improvised combat. Shrines scattered across Hyrule offer puzzles and trials in place of large conventional dungeons.

Reception and legacy

Breath of the Wild won numerous Game of the Year awards, is one of the best-selling Switch games with around 30 million copies sold, and is frequently cited among the greatest video games ever made. Its design influenced open-world games broadly. A sequel, Tears of the Kingdom, was released in 2023.
