iPhone X

Manufacturer: Apple Inc.
Released: 3 November 2017
Display: 5.8 in Super Retina OLED, 2436×1125
Chip: Apple A11 Bionic
Discontinued: 12 September 2018

The iPhone X (pronounced "iPhone Ten") is a smartphone designed and marketed by Apple Inc. Announced on 12 September 2017 alongside the iPhone 8, it marked the tenth anniversary of the iPhone and introduced a major redesign: an edge-to-edge OLED display with a sensor "notch", the removal of the Home button and Touch ID, and Face ID facial recognition.

Design and features

The phone has a stainless steel frame and glass front and back enabling Qi wireless charging and IP67 water resistance. Its TrueDepth camera projects over 30,000 infrared dots to map the user's face for Face ID and Animoji. Navigation relies on gestures, such as swiping up to return home. The A11 Bionic chip included Apple's first in-house GPU and a Neural Engine for machine learning tasks.

Reception and legacy

Reviewers praised the display and Face ID but criticised the $999 starting price and the notch. The iPhone X set the design template for subsequent iPhones for years, and its gesture interface and facial authentication became industry standards.
