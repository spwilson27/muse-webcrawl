Monopoly (game)

PublisherHasbro (Parker Brothers)
First published1935
Players2–8
Playing time60–375 minutes

Monopoly is a multiplayer economics-themed board game in which players roll dice to move around a board, buying, trading, and developing properties, and charging opponents rent with the aim of driving them into bankruptcy.

History

The game descends from The Landlord's Game, patented by Lizzie Magie in 1904 to demonstrate the ideas of Henry George. Charles Darrow sold a version to Parker Brothers in 1935, which became a worldwide success; it is now published by Hasbro and licensed in more than 100 countries, with localised editions naming streets of cities around the world.

Gameplay

Players collect £200 (or $200) each time they pass Go, buy properties to complete colour monopolies, and build houses and hotels to raise rents. Chance and Community Chest cards, taxes, and jail add variation. The standard London board runs from Old Kent Road to Mayfair.
