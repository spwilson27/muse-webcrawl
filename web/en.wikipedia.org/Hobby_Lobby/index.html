Hobby Lobby

Hobby Lobby Stores, Inc.
Industry: Retail
Founded: 1972
Headquarters: Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, United States
Founder: David Green

Hobby Lobby Stores, Inc., formerly Hobby Lobby Creative Centers, is an American retail company. It operates a chain of arts and crafts stores with a volume of locations exceeding 900 in 2024 alone, mainly in the United States. It was founded in 1972 by David Green in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.

History

David Green and his family grew a small picture-frame business in Oklahoma City into Hobby Lobby, opening the first retail store in 1972. The chain expanded across the Midwest and South, selling crafts, fabrics, home decor, seasonal goods, and hobby supplies.

Business model and controversy

The company is privately held and closely associated with the conservative Christian views of the Green family. It was the named plaintiff in Burwell v. Hobby Lobby Stores, Inc. (2014), in which the U.S. Supreme Court ruled that closely held for-profit corporations could claim a religious exemption from a federal requirement to provide certain contraceptive coverage in employee health plans.
