Lamar University

Type: Public research university
Established: 1923
Location: Beaumont, Texas, U.S.
Mascot: Cardinals

Lamar University is a public research university in Beaumont, Texas. Founded in 1923 as South Park Junior College, it is a member of the Texas State University System and is named after Mirabeau B. Lamar, the second president of the Republic of Texas.

History

The institution became Lamar College in 1932, a four-year school as Lamar State College of Technology in 1949, and gained university status in 1971. It is classified among doctoral research universities and enrols roughly 18,000 students.

Academics and athletics

Lamar offers programmes through colleges of engineering, business, education and human development, arts and sciences, and fine arts and communication. The Cardinals compete in NCAA Division I as members of the Southland Conference. The campus is home to the Spindletop-Gladys City Boomtown Museum commemorating the 1901 Texas oil boom.
