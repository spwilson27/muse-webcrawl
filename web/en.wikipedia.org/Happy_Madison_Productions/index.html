Happy Madison Productions is an American film and television production company founded in 1999 by actor and comedian Adam Sandler. The company's name combines the titles of two of Sandler's early star vehicles, Happy Gilmore and Billy Madison.

History

The company's first production was Deuce Bigalow: Male Gigolo (1999). It went on to produce most of Sandler's comedy films, including Big Daddy, Little Nicky, Mr. Deeds, 50 First Dates, The Longest Yard, Click, Grown Ups, and Just Go with It, many distributed by Columbia Pictures.

Netflix era

From 2015, Happy Madison became a major supplier of films to Netflix under a multi-picture deal, producing titles such as The Ridiculous 6, Murder Mystery, Hubie Halloween, and Hustle. Several became among the platform's most-watched films.

Other work

The company has also produced films starring Sandler's collaborators, such as Rob Schneider and David Spade, and television series including Rules of Engagement and The Goldbergs (with Adam F. Goldberg). Its logo features Sandler's late father, Stanley, and the greeting "Terrific!".
