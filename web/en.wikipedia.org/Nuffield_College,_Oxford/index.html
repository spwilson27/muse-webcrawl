Nuffield College is a graduate constituent college of the University of Oxford specialising in the social sciences, particularly economics, politics and sociology. It was founded in 1937 with an endowment from the motor manufacturer William Morris, Lord Nuffield.

History and buildings

Construction of the college buildings on the New Road site was delayed by the Second World War and completed in the 1950s; the college is noted for its tower, the first built in Oxford for 200 years. Nuffield was the first Oxford college to admit both men and women as students and fellows.

Research

The college hosts research centres in economics and political science and runs the well-known Nuffield election studies of British general elections.
