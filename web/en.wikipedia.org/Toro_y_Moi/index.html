Chaz Bear (born Chazwick Bradley Bundick, 7 November 1986), known professionally as Toro y Moi, is an American singer, songwriter, record producer and graphic designer. He is frequently cited as a key figure of the chillwave movement of the early 2010s, alongside artists such as Washed Out and Neon Indian, though his later work has ranged across indie rock, house music, funk and jazz-inflected styles.

Career

Bundick, who is of Filipino and African-American descent, formed the indie rock band The Heist and The Accomplice while in high school in Columbia, South Carolina, and studied graphic design at the University of South Carolina. After signing to Carpark Records in 2009, he released his debut studio album, Causers of This (2010), whose sample-based, hypnagogic pop was associated with the emerging chillwave genre. Subsequent albums include Underneath the Pine (2011), the funk- and soft-rock-influenced Anything in Return (2013), the guitar-driven What For? (2015), Boo Boo (2017), Outer Peace (2019), Mahal (2022) and Hole Erth (2024). He also records dance music under the name Les Sins.

Style

Toro y Moi's music is characterised by shifting genre allegiances between albums, layered production, and influences from French house, 1990s R&B and psychedelic rock. His work as a designer — he has produced artwork and merchandise through his own studio — is integral to his public identity, and he is regarded as one of the most prominent independent artists to emerge from the blog-era music economy.
