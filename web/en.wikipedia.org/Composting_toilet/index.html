Composting toilet

A composting toilet is a type of dry toilet that treats human waste by a biological process called composting. It uses little or no water and converts waste into a humus-like material through aerobic decomposition.

Operation

Waste is collected in a chamber where ventilation, moisture control, and carbon-rich bulking material such as sawdust support microorganisms that break down pathogens and odour compounds.

Applications

Composting toilets are used in cabins, boats, roadside rest areas, and eco-buildings where sewer or septic connections are impractical, and they reduce water consumption substantially compared with flush toilets.
