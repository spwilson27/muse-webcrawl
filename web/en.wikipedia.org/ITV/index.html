ITV

Type: Free-to-air television network
Country: United Kingdom
Launched: 22 September 1955
Owner: ITV plc (most regions); STV Group (Scotland)

ITV is a British free-to-air public broadcast television network, launched in 1955 as Independent Television to provide commercial competition to the BBC. Originally a federation of regional franchise companies — including Granada, Thames, London Weekend Television, and Yorkshire Television — it produced landmarks such as Coronation Street, Upstairs, Downstairs, and Brideshead Revisited.

History

The Television Act 1954 created the network; the first broadcasts came from London's Associated-Rediffusion. Franchises were periodically re-awarded, reshaping the map, and a national breakfast franchise began in 1983. Deregulation under the Broadcasting Act 1990 and subsequent mergers consolidated the English and Welsh franchises into ITV plc by 2004, while STV serves Scotland and UTV Northern Ireland. Channel 3 branding varies by region, with ITV1 used in most areas.

Programming and services

ITV's schedule centres on soaps, drama, entertainment formats such as The X Factor and I'm a Celebrity..., sport, and national news from ITN. Streaming moved from ITV Hub to ITVX in 2022. Sister channels include ITV2, ITV3, ITV4, and ITVBe.
