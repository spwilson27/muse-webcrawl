National Institute of Standards and Technology

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is a physical sciences laboratory and non-regulatory agency of the United States Department of Commerce. Founded in 1901 as the National Bureau of Standards, its mission is to promote American innovation and industrial competitiveness by advancing measurement science, standards and technology.

Work

NIST maintains the national standards of measurement for the United States, operates laboratories in Gaithersburg, Maryland, and Boulder, Colorado, and provides Standard Reference Materials and calibration services. It is widely known in computing for its cryptographic standards — including the AES, SHA and post-quantum cryptography standards — and for the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. Its scientists have won multiple Nobel Prizes in Physics.

Organisation

Programs include the Hollings Manufacturing Extension Partnership, the Baldrige Performance Excellence Program, and the CHIPS for America research and development office created under the CHIPS and Science Act of 2022.
