Australia national cricket team

The Australian team, nicknamed the Baggy Greens, is one of the most successful sides in international cricket.

The Australia men's national cricket team represents Australia in men's international cricket. It is the joint oldest team in Test cricket history, having played in the first ever Test match in 1877 against England. The team is administered by Cricket Australia and is a full member of the International Cricket Council (ICC) with Test, One Day International (ODI) and Twenty20 International (T20I) status.

Australia is the most successful team in ODI cricket history, having won the Cricket World Cup a record six times (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015 and 2023). The team has also won the ICC T20 World Cup once, in 2021, and the ICC Champions Trophy twice. In Test cricket, Australia has spent long periods as the world's top-ranked side and contests the Ashes against England, the oldest rivalry in the sport.

History

The first Test match, played at the Melbourne Cricket Ground in March 1877, was won by Australia by 45 runs. The Ashes rivalry began in 1882, after Australia's victory at The Oval led a mock obituary in The Sporting Times to declare that English cricket had died and "the ashes" would be taken to Australia. The return series in Australia was billed as the quest to regain the Ashes.

Donald Bradman, who played from 1928 to 1948, is widely regarded as the greatest batsman of all time, finishing his Test career with an average of 99.94. Later eras of dominance included the teams of Richie Benaud, Ian Chappell, Allan Border, Mark Taylor, Steve Waugh and Ricky Ponting. Under Waugh and Ponting, Australia set a record of 16 consecutive Test wins and won three consecutive World Cups.

Tournament record

Australia has appeared in every Cricket World Cup since the tournament began in 1975, winning six of the thirteen editions. The team won the World Test Championship in 2023, defeating India in the final at The Oval. In the shorter formats, Australia won its first T20 World Cup in Dubai in 2021 under Aaron Finch.

Colours and kit

In Test cricket the team wears white with the baggy green cap, which has become an iconic symbol of Australian cricket. In limited-overs cricket the team traditionally wears a canary yellow and green uniform, colours drawn from Australia's sporting palette.
