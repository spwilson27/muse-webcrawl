Tall ship
TypeLarge traditionally rigged sailing vessel
Typical rigsShip, barque, brig, brigantine, schooner
Modern useSail training, festivals, racing

A tall ship is a large, traditionally rigged sailing vessel. Popular modern tall ship rigs include topsail schooners, brigantines, brigs and barques. "Tall ship" can also be defined more specifically by an organization, such as for a race or festival. Traditional rigging may include square rigs and gaff rigs, usually with separate topmasts and topsails.

Sail training

Many tall ships today are operated by sail training organizations that take trainees, often young people, to sea to learn traditional seamanship and teamwork. International races such as the Tall Ships' Races, organized by Sail Training International, bring fleets together in ports around the world.

Terminology

The term was coined in the mid-20th century and popularized by the Tall Ships' Races beginning in 1956. It does not describe a specific rig; vessels of many rigs qualify when they meet size and traditional-rigging criteria.
