LinkedIn is an American business- and employment-oriented social networking service, operated through websites and mobile apps. Founded in Mountain View, California, and launched on 5 May 2003 by Reid Hoffman and co-founders, it allows workers and employers to create profiles, build professional networks, post and search for jobs, and share business content.

History

The company grew steadily through the 2000s, held an initial public offering in May 2011, and acquired services including the presentation platform SlideShare and the learning company Lynda.com (later LinkedIn Learning). In June 2016 Microsoft announced it would acquire LinkedIn for $26.2 billion; the deal closed that December, and LinkedIn continued to operate as a distinct subsidiary.

Services and membership

LinkedIn reports over one billion members worldwide. Revenue comes from premium subscriptions, recruitment solutions (such as LinkedIn Recruiter), and advertising. The platform has faced criticism and study over data scraping, privacy, and its role in professional self-presentation, but remains the dominant professional networking service.
