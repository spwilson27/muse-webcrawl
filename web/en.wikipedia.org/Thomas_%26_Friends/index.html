Thomas & Friends (originally Thomas the Tank Engine & Friends) is a British children's television series created by Britt Allcroft, based on The Railway Series books by the Reverend W. Awdry. It follows Thomas, a cheerful blue steam locomotive, and his engine friends on the fictional Island of Sodor.

Format and production

The first twelve series were filmed with live-action models on detailed sets, giving the show its distinctive look; from series 13 the production switched to CGI animation. Early series were narrated by a single storyteller — Ringo Starr for the first two series, followed by Michael Angelis in the UK and George Carlin and Alec Baldwin in the US.

Legacy

The series ran for 24 series and 584 episodes between 1984 and 2021, spawned feature-length specials, a theatrical film (Thomas and the Magic Railroad, 2000), and one of the world's most successful preschool merchandising franchises. A 2D animated reboot, Thomas & Friends: All Engines Go, premiered in 2021.
