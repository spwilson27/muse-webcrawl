Erin Hunter

Erin Hunter is a pen name used by a team of authors — originally Victoria Holmes, Kate Cary, Cherith Baldry and Tui T. Sutherland, later joined by others — who write the popular children's fantasy series Warriors, Seekers, Survivors, Bravelands and Bamboo Kingdom, published by HarperCollins.

Origin

The pseudonym was created so that the multi-author Warriors series — first published in 2003 with Into the Wild — would be shelved together in bookshops and libraries under a single name. Victoria Holmes, an editor at Working Partners, devised the world of feral cat Clans and plotted the series, while Cary and Baldry alternated writing the novels; Sutherland wrote early volumes and field guides before leaving to write her own series.

The series

Warriors follows clans of feral cats — ThunderClan, RiverClan, WindClan, ShadowClan and later SkyClan — and has grown to many six-book arcs, super editions, novellas, manga and field guides, selling tens of millions of copies worldwide. Seekers (2008–) follows bears, Survivors (2012–) dogs, Bravelands (2017–) African savanna animals, and Bamboo Kingdom (2021–) pandas.
