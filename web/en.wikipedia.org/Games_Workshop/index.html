Games Workshop Group PLC is a British manufacturer of miniature wargames, based in Nottingham, England. Its best-known products are Warhammer Age of Sigmar and Warhammer 40,000. Founded in 1975 by John Peake, Ian Livingstone, and Steve Jackson, the company began by making wooden boards for traditional games and importing American role-playing games, including Dungeons & Dragons, into the United Kingdom.

History

Games Workshop opened its first shops in the late 1970s and launched the magazine White Dwarf in 1977. Through its subsidiary Citadel Miniatures it built a business in metal (later plastic) miniatures; Warhammer Fantasy Battle (1983) and Warhammer 40,000 (1987) became dominant properties. The company floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1994 and grew into a vertically integrated business designing, manufacturing, and retailing through its own Warhammer-branded stores.

Licensing and growth

Games Workshop licenses its Warhammer settings extensively to video games — including the Total War: Warhammer trilogy and Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 — and its Black Library fiction line. A period of high growth in the late 2010s and early 2020s made it one of the FTSE 250's standout performers, with royalties and a planned Amazon adaptation of Warhammer 40,000 associated with Henry Cavill adding to its profile.
