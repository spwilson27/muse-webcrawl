Founded2015
FounderYuri Milner
FocusSearch for life beyond Earth
ProgrammesBreakthrough Listen, Breakthrough Starshot, Breakthrough Watch

The Breakthrough Initiatives are a suite of scientific and technological programmes, funded by Yuri Milner and announced in 2015, aimed at searching for evidence of life elsewhere in the universe and developing the means to reach other stars. The initiatives are overseen with advice from prominent scientists and were launched alongside Stephen Hawking at the Royal Society in London.


A Starshot lightsail concept.

Breakthrough Listen

Breakthrough Listen is a ten-year, $100 million search for extraterrestrial intelligence. It uses major radio telescopes — including the Green Bank Telescope in West Virginia and the Parkes Observatory in Australia — together with optical facilities, to survey nearby stars and galaxies for artificial signals, and it releases its data publicly.

Breakthrough Starshot

Announced in 2016, Breakthrough Starshot is a research and engineering programme to develop gram-scale "nanocraft" carrying miniature cameras and sensors, pushed by a ground-based laser array acting on lightweight lightsails to a significant fraction of the speed of light. The goal is a flyby mission to the Alpha Centauri system, including the planet Proxima Centauri b, within a generation.

Breakthrough Watch and Message

Breakthrough Watch seeks to identify and characterise nearby Earth-like exoplanets using ground-based instruments, while the Breakthrough Message competition invited designs for a message representing humanity, though there are no plans to transmit without broad consultation.
