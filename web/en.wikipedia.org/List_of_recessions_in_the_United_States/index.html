List of recessions in the United States

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of recessions in the United States records the periods of significant decline in economic activity identified by the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER), the official arbiter of U.S. business cycle dates since the mid-20th century. The NBER defines a recession as a significant decline in economic activity spread across the economy, lasting more than a few months—not simply two consecutive quarters of falling GDP, a common rule of thumb.

Earlier downturns

Before 1948 the United States experienced frequent and often severe contractions, including the Panic of 1837, the Long Depression of the 1870s, the Panic of 1893, the Panic of 1907, the Depression of 1920–21, and the Great Depression of 1929–1933—the deepest downturn in U.S. history, during which unemployment reached roughly 25 percent.
