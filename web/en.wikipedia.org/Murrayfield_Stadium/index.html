Murrayfield Stadium

Murrayfield Stadium is a rugby stadium in the Murrayfield area of Edinburgh, Scotland. It is the home of the Scottish Rugby Union and the Scotland national rugby union team, and with a capacity of about 67,000 it is the largest stadium in Scotland.

History

The stadium opened on 21 March 1925, when Scotland defeated England to win a Grand Slam. The current stadium was largely rebuilt in the 1990s, with new stands completed in 1994. Murrayfield hosts Scotland's home matches in the Six Nations Championship and autumn internationals.

Other uses

Beyond rugby, Murrayfield has hosted association football (including Hearts home matches and cup games), American football, rugby league and major concerts by artists such as U2, Madonna and Taylor Swift. It also hosted matches during the 2007 Rugby World Cup.
