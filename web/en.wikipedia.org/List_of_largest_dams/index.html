List of largest dams

This article lists the world's largest dams according to several measures: structural volume, reservoir capacity, and height. The largest dam by structural volume is the Tarbela Dam in Pakistan, an earth-fill embankment dam on the Indus River. The largest reservoir by volume is impounded by the Kariba Dam between Zambia and Zimbabwe. The tallest dam in the world is the Nurek Dam in Tajikistan, an embankment dam 300 metres high.

Tallest dams

The Nurek Dam (300 m) in Tajikistan is the tallest dam in the world, followed by the Baihetan Dam (289 m) and the Jinping-I Dam (305 m arch dam) in China — Jinping-I is the tallest concrete dam. The Three Gorges Dam on the Yangtze River, while not the tallest, hosts the world's largest power station with an installed capacity of 22,500 MW.
