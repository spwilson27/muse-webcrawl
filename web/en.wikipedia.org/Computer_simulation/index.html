Computer simulation

A computer simulation is the use of a computer to represent the dynamic responses of one system by the behaviour of another system modelled after it. Simulations imitate real-world processes over time using mathematical models.

Types

Discrete-event, continuous, agent-based, and Monte Carlo simulations serve different purposes, from queuing systems to weather forecasting and molecular dynamics.

Applications

Simulation is used in science, engineering design, flight training, video games, and policy analysis, allowing experiments that would be costly, dangerous, or impossible to perform on the real system.
