Dulle Griet

Dulle Griet (Mad Meg), also known as Dull Gret, is an oil painting by Pieter Bruegel the Elder, painted around 1563. It depicts a peasant woman, from Flemish folklore, leading an army of women to pillage Hell. The painting is held by the Museum Mayer van den Bergh in Antwerp.

Description

Griet appears in the foreground in armour, sword in hand, striding toward the mouth of Hell amid a chaotic, Bosch-like landscape of demons, monsters, and burning cities. The figure refers to a Flemish proverb about a woman so fierce she could plunder in front of Hell without fear. The work is notable for its apocalyptic imagery and dense detail.
