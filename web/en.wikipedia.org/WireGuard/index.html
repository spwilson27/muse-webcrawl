WireGuard

Original authorJason A. Donenfeld
Initial release2016
LicenseGPLv2 (kernel module)
PurposeVirtual private network (VPN) tunnelling protocol

WireGuard is a free and open-source communication protocol and software that implements encrypted virtual private network (VPN) techniques. Designed by Jason A. Donenfeld, it is noted for its small codebase — about 4,000 lines in the Linux kernel implementation, compared with hundreds of thousands for IPsec or OpenVPN — which eases auditing, and for high performance.

Design

WireGuard uses modern cryptography: Curve25519 for key exchange, ChaCha20 for encryption, Poly1305 for authentication, and BLAKE2s for hashing, arranged in a Noise-based handshake. It is a layer-3 tunnel over UDP, associates peers with public keys (a concept Donenfeld calls "cryptokey routing"), and is largely silent when not sending data, reducing attack surface. Roaming is supported natively, since a peer is identified by key rather than address.

Adoption

WireGuard was merged into the Linux kernel in version 5.6 (2020); Linus Torvalds praised its design. Implementations exist for all major operating systems, and many commercial VPN services and products such as Tailscale build on it.
