Super Bowl LIX

Super Bowl LIX was an American football game played on February 9, 2025, at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana, to determine the champion of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2024 season. The NFC champion Philadelphia Eagles defeated the AFC champion Kansas City Chiefs, 40–22, denying Kansas City a third consecutive championship.

Game summary

Philadelphia's defence dominated, sacking quarterback Patrick Mahomes six times without blitzing, and cornerback Cooper DeJean returned an interception for a touchdown on his 22nd birthday. The Eagles built a 34–0 lead in the third quarter. Quarterback Jalen Hurts accounted for three touchdowns and was named Super Bowl MVP.

Entertainment

The halftime show was headlined by Kendrick Lamar, featuring guest appearances including SZA, and Jon Batiste performed the national anthem. The game was broadcast by Fox in the United States.

Background

It was the eighth Super Bowl hosted at the Superdome and the eleventh in New Orleans overall. The Chiefs had won the previous two Super Bowls, LVII and LVIII, the former also against the Eagles.
