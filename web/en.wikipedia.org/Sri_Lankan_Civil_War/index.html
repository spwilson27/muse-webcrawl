Sri Lankan Civil War
Date: 23 July 1983 – 18 May 2009
Location: Sri Lanka
Belligerents: Sri Lankan government vs LTTE
Deaths: estimated 80,000–100,000+

The Sri Lankan Civil War was a civil war fought in Sri Lanka from 1983 to 2009 between the government and the Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam (LTTE), who sought an independent Tamil state (Tamil Eelam) in the north-east.

Origins

Post-independence tensions over language — the 1956 Sinhala Only Act favouring Sinhala — education and jobs alienated the Tamil minority. Anti-Tamil violence in July 1983 ("Black July") triggered full-scale war.

Course

The war saw LTTE suicide bombings and assassinations, an Indian peacekeeping intervention (1987–1990), ceasefires including a Norwegian-brokered truce (2002–2006), and a final government offensive. LTTE leader Velupillai Prabhakaran was killed in May 2009.

Aftermath

The United Nations has alleged war crimes by both sides in the final phase; reconciliation, missing persons and political settlement remain unresolved.
