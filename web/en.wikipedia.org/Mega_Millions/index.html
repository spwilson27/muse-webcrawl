Mega Millions

Mega Millions (originally known as The Big Game in 1996 and renamed, temporarily, The Big Game Mega Millions six years later) is an American multi-jurisdictional lottery game; it has been played in 45 states plus the District of Columbia and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Drawings are held on Tuesday and Friday evenings in Atlanta, Georgia.

Format

Players choose five numbers from a pool of 70 and one Mega Ball number from a pool of 25 (in the format introduced in 2017; earlier versions used different matrices). Matching all six numbers wins the jackpot, which starts at a set minimum and grows with rollovers until won. Prizes may be taken as an annuity paid over 30 years or as a smaller lump-sum cash option. An optional Megaplier feature multiplies non-jackpot prizes.

History

The first drawing for The Big Game took place on September 6, 1996, with six states participating. The game was renamed Mega Millions in May 2002, and its first drawing under the new name produced a jackpot won in Illinois. Over the years the matrix and price changed several times; the ticket price rose to $2 in 2017, and in 2025 the price increased to $5 with a built-in multiplier and revised odds.

Record jackpots

Mega Millions has produced some of the largest lottery prizes in history, including a $1.537 billion jackpot won by a single ticket in South Carolina in October 2018, and a $1.58 billion jackpot won in Florida in August 2023.
