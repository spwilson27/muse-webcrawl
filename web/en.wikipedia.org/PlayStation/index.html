PlayStation

PlayStation is a video gaming brand owned by Sony Interactive Entertainment, a subsidiary of Sony. It consists of home consoles, handheld consoles, the PlayStation Network online service, and games and media services. The brand was introduced with the original PlayStation console, launched in Japan on 3 December 1994.

History

The original PlayStation, using CD-ROM games, became the first console to ship over 100 million units, popularising 3D gaming with titles such as Gran Turismo, Final Fantasy VII and Tekken. The PlayStation 2 (2000) is the best-selling home console of all time, with over 160 million units sold. It was followed by the PlayStation 3 (2006), PlayStation 4 (2013) and PlayStation 5 (2020), as well as the handheld PlayStation Portable and PlayStation Vita.

Services

The PlayStation Network provides online multiplayer, digital game sales and the PlayStation Plus subscription service. The brand's controllers, from the DualShock to the DualSense, are among its most recognisable products.
