Nico Erik Rosberg (born 27 June 1985) is a German-Finnish former racing driver who won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 2016 with Mercedes. The son of 1982 world champion Keke Rosberg, he competed for Williams (2006–09) and Mercedes (2010–16), winning 23 Grands Prix. Five days after clinching his title, he announced his immediate retirement from the sport, one of the most surprising retirements in F1 history.

Career

Rosberg won the inaugural GP2 Series championship in 2005 and graduated to F1 with Williams, setting the fastest lap on his debut in Bahrain in 2006. His first podium came at the 2008 Australian Grand Prix. Joining the new Mercedes works team in 2010, he outscored team-mate Michael Schumacher over three seasons and took Mercedes' first win of the modern era at the 2012 Chinese Grand Prix.

From 2013 Rosberg was partnered with Lewis Hamilton, his former karting team-mate and friend. As Mercedes dominated the hybrid era from 2014, their rivalry intensified: Hamilton won the 2014 and 2015 titles, while Rosberg, after a run of victories at the end of 2015 and start of 2016, beat Hamilton to the 2016 championship by five points, sealed with second place at the Abu Dhabi finale. He and Keke remain one of only two father-son pairs to win the title (after Graham and Damon Hill).

After racing

Rosberg has since worked as a television pundit, sustainability entrepreneur and investor, and founder of the Greentech Festival; his Rosberg X Racing team won the inaugural Extreme E championship in 2021.
