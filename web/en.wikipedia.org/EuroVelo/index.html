EuroVelo is a network of long-distance cycle routes criss-crossing Europe, coordinated by the European Cyclists' Federation (ECF). It consists of 17 routes, numbered EV1 to EV17, which combine existing national and regional cycle paths with new links.

Routes

The best-known routes include EV1, the Atlantic Coast Route from Norway's North Cape to Portugal; EV6, the "Rivers Route", which follows the Danube from the Atlantic to the Black Sea and is one of the most popular cycle-touring itineraries in the world; EV7, the Sun Route from the North Cape to Malta; EV8 along the Mediterranean; EV12, the North Sea Cycle Route; and EV15 along the Rhine. Some routes, such as EV2 (the Capitals Route from Galway to Moscow) and EV13 (the Iron Curtain Trail), carry a strong historical theme.

Development

Launched in the late 1990s, EuroVelo is developed by national coordinators and partners in each country, with standards for surfacing, signage and services. Large portions are complete, though completion varies by route and country; the network promotes cycle tourism and sustainable transport.
