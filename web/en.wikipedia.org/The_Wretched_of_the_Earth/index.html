The Wretched of the Earth

The Wretched of the Earth (French: Les Damnés de la Terre) is a 1961 book by the Martinican psychiatrist and philosopher Frantz Fanon, published shortly before his death. It is a foundational work of decolonisation theory and postcolonial studies, with a preface by Jean-Paul Sartre.

AuthorFrantz Fanon
Published1961
Original titleLes Damnés de la Terre
SubjectDecolonisation, colonialism

Content

Fanon analyses the dehumanising effects of colonisation on individuals and nations, arguing that decolonisation is inherently a violent process in which the colonised reclaim their humanity. The book examines national culture, the pitfalls of national consciousness, and the psychiatric effects of colonial war, drawing on Fanon's clinical work in Algeria during its war of independence.

Influence

The book influenced liberation movements worldwide and remains a central text in critical theory, though its discussion of violence remains controversial.
