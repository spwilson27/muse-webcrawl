Eric Harris and Dylan Klebold

Eric David Harris (April 9, 1981 – April 20, 1999) and Dylan Bennet Klebold (September 11, 1981 – April 20, 1999) were two American high school seniors who carried out the Columbine High School massacre in Littleton, Colorado, on April 20, 1999. They murdered 12 students and one teacher, wounded 24 others, and then died by suicide in the school library.

Background

Harris and Klebold met in middle school and became close friends. Both worked part-time at a local pizza shop and shared interests in video games, computers, and music. In the year before the attack they planned the massacre extensively, keeping journals and making videotapes in which they discussed their intentions.

The attack

The pair had originally planned a bombing of the school cafeteria, but the propane bombs they planted failed to detonate. They then began shooting outside the school and inside its halls and library. The attack lasted about 49 minutes before the pair took their own lives.

Motives and aftermath

Investigative reports and analyses by the FBI suggested Harris displayed traits consistent with psychopathy while Klebold was severely depressed and suicidal. The massacre prompted national debates about gun control, school security, bullying, and the influence of violent media, and led to widespread changes in police response tactics for active shooter situations.
