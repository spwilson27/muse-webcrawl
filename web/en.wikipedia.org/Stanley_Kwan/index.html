Stanley Kwan

Born9 October 1957, Hong Kong
OccupationFilm director, producer

Stanley Kwan Kam-pang (born 9 October 1957) is a Hong Kong film director and producer associated with the Hong Kong New Wave. He is known for films centred on women, memory, and Hong Kong identity.

Career

Kwan began at TVB and served as assistant director to Ann Hui before directing Women (1985). His acclaimed works include Rouge (1988), starring Anita Mui and Leslie Cheung; Actress (also known as Center Stage, 1992), a biopic of silent-film star Ruan Lingyu; and Hold You Tight (1998).

Style

His films blend melodrama, documentary elements and self-reflexive commentary on cinema history, and he is one of the few openly gay directors in Chinese-language cinema.
