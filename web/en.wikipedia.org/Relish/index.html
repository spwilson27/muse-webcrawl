Relish

A relish is a cooked and pickled product made of chopped vegetables, fruits, or herbs, used as a condiment to enhance the flavour of a dish. Relishes are typically pungent, sweet, sour, or spicy, and are eaten in small amounts alongside plainer staple foods.

Varieties

North American pickle relish is made from finely chopped pickled cucumbers with sugar, vinegar, and spices and is a standard topping for hot dogs and hamburgers. In Britain, pickles such as Branston pickle accompany cheese and cold meats. South Asian cuisines have a wide range of relishes and chutneys, from mango chutney to lime pickle, and Korean kimchi is sometimes classed as a relish.

Preparation

Ingredients are chopped, combined with vinegar, sugar, and spices, and either cooked briefly and jarred or fermented. The acidity preserves the relish and gives it its characteristic tang.
