Rainbow jersey
Jersey worn by reigning cycling world champions
White with green, red, black, yellow and blue bands

The rainbow jersey is the distinctive cycling jersey worn by the reigning world champion in a cycling discipline. The jersey is predominantly white with five horizontal bands in the traditional colours of the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI) around the chest — from top to bottom: green, red, black, yellow and blue, echoing the colours of the Olympic rings.

Use

World champions in road racing, track cycling, cyclo-cross, mountain biking and BMX may wear the jersey in competition in the same discipline, category and format of their title for the year following their victory. Former world champions may wear rainbow piping on the collar and cuffs of their regular jerseys.

History and prestige

The jersey has been awarded since the first UCI world championships in the 1920s. Winning it is considered one of the highest honours in cycling. A sometimes-discussed "curse of the rainbow jersey" refers to the misfortunes — crashes, illness or loss of form — said to befall champions in their title year, though statistical analyses suggest the effect is largely regression to the mean and increased scrutiny.
