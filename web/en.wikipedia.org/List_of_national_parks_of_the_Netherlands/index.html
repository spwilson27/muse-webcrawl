The Netherlands has 21 national parks in its European territory, established to protect characteristic Dutch landscapes including dunes, heathland, forests, wetlands, tidal flats, and river floodplains. The first, Veluwezoom, was designated in 1930. Additional parks exist in the Caribbean Netherlands, such as Bonaire National Marine Park. Management involves the state forestry service (Staatsbosbeheer), nature organizations such as Natuurmonumenten, provinces, and private landowners.
