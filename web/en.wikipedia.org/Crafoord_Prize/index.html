Crafoord Prize

The Crafoord Prize is an annual science prize established in 1980 by Swedish industrialist Holger Crafoord and his wife Anna-Greta Crafoord. It is awarded by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences.

Fields

The prize promotes research in disciplines not covered by the Nobel Prizes: mathematics and astronomy, geosciences, biosciences, and polyarthritis research, rotating annually, with polyarthritis awarded when a significant advance warrants it. Laureates receive a gold medal and a substantial monetary award.
