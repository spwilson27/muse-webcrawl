Cherokee Phoenix

The Cherokee Phoenix was the first newspaper published by Native Americans in the United States, issued bilingually in Cherokee and English from New Echota, capital of the Cherokee Nation, beginning in 1828.

Edited by Elias Boudinot and printed with type cast for Sequoyah's Cherokee syllabary, it carried laws, news and editorials defending Cherokee sovereignty. Publication was disrupted by Georgia's campaign against the Nation and the paper ceased in 1834; a modern Cherokee Phoenix continues today.
