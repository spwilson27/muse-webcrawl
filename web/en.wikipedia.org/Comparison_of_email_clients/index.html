Comparison of email clients
Subject: software for accessing electronic mail
Clients compared: Outlook, Thunderbird, Apple Mail, eM Client, Mutt and others
Criteria: protocols, encryption, platforms, licensing

This article compares notable email clients—programs used to access and manage electronic mail—across features such as supported protocols, security, operating systems and cost.

General information

Major desktop clients include Microsoft Outlook (part of Microsoft 365), Mozilla Thunderbird (free and open source), Apple Mail (bundled with macOS and iOS) and eM Client. Text-based clients such as Mutt and Alpine persist on Unix systems. Webmail interfaces are treated separately from installable clients.

Protocol and feature support

Comparisons typically cover IMAP, POP3 and Exchange/ActiveSync support; message filtering; calendaring; and integration with address books. Most modern clients support TLS encryption in transit.

Security features

Support for end-to-end encryption via S/MIME and OpenPGP varies: Thunderbird integrates OpenPGP natively, while Outlook emphasises S/MIME in enterprise deployments. Phishing protection, sandboxing of attachments and spam filtering are also compared.
