Pentagram

A pentagram (sometimes known as a pentalpha, pentangle, or star pentagon) is a regular five-pointed star polygon, drawn with five straight strokes. It is the simplest regular star polygon and contains a pentagon at its centre, whose diagonals exhibit the golden ratio.

History

Pentagrams were used in ancient Greece and Babylon, and appear as a symbol in many cultures and religions. The Pythagoreans used it as a symbol of health and mutual recognition. It has served as a symbol in Christianity, Neopaganism (notably Wicca), occultism, and as a military and state insignia, including on the flags of Morocco and Ethiopia.

Geometry

The pentagram is the {5/2} star polygon. Its line segments are in golden ratio proportion to one another, a property that fascinated Greek mathematicians and that links it to the pentagon and the dodecahedron.
