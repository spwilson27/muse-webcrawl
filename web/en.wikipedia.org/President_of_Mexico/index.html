The President of Mexico (Spanish: Presidente de México), officially the President of the United Mexican States, is the head of state and head of government of Mexico and the supreme commander of the Mexican Armed Forces. The office is defined by the Constitution of 1917, under which the president is elected by direct popular vote for a single six-year term, called a sexenio, with re-election absolutely prohibited.

Powers

The president promulgates and executes laws, appoints and removes cabinet secretaries and other senior officials, directs foreign policy and negotiates treaties (subject to Senate ratification), commands the army, navy, and air force, proposes the federal budget, and may grant pardons. Although formally checked by Congress and the judiciary, the Mexican presidency was historically dominant during the long rule of the Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI) from 1929 to 2000, when outgoing presidents effectively chose their successors (the dedazo).

Election and term

Candidates must be Mexican citizens by birth, at least 35 years old, and resident in the country. Elections are held on the first Sunday of June of the election year, and the president takes office on 1 October (changed from 1 December by a 2014 reform, first applied in 2024). The winner is determined by plurality — Mexico has no runoff. The constitution's ban on re-election, rooted in the revolutionary slogan "effective suffrage, no re-election" associated with Francisco I. Madero, extends to anyone who has held the presidency even briefly or on an interim basis.

History

The first president, Guadalupe Victoria, took office in 1824. The nineteenth century saw frequent turnover, the long rule of Porfirio Díaz (1876–1911), and the Mexican Revolution (1910–1920), which produced the Constitution of 1917. The PRI and its predecessors then held the presidency continuously for 71 years until Vicente Fox of the PAN won in 2000. Andrés Manuel López Obrador (Morena) won in 2018, and Claudia Sheinbaum, elected in June 2024, took office on 1 October 2024 as Mexico's first woman president.

Recent presidents

Recent holders include Vicente Fox (2000–2006), Felipe Calderón (2006–2012), Enrique Peña Nieto (2012–2018), Andrés Manuel López Obrador (2018–2024), and Claudia Sheinbaum (from 2024).
