Long Island City

Long Island City (LIC) is a neighbourhood on the western tip of the borough of Queens in New York City, at the mouth of Newton Creek where it meets the East River, opposite Manhattan.

History

Long Island City was an independent city from 1870 until it was consolidated into New York City in 1898, including the villages of Astoria, Hunters Point, Ravenswood, Steinway and Blissville. Long an industrial and transport hub — with the Long Island Rail Road and the Queensboro Bridge (1909) — it has transformed since the 2000s into one of the fastest-growing residential areas in the United States, with high-rise development around Court Square and Hunters Point.

Culture

The neighbourhood is home to MoMA PS1, the Noguchi Museum, Socrates Sculpture Park, and the Silvercup Studios. The Pepsi-Cola sign in Gantry Plaza State Park is a landmark. Amazon selected LIC, alongside Arlington, Virginia, for its HQ2 in 2018 before cancelling the New York site in 2019 after local opposition.
