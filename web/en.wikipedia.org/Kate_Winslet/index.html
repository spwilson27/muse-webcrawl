Kate Elizabeth Winslet (born 5 October 1975) is an English actress. Known for her work in independent films and period dramas, she has received numerous accolades, including an Academy Award, a Grammy Award, two Primetime Emmy Awards, five BAFTA Awards, and five Golden Globe Awards.

Career

Winslet made her film debut in Peter Jackson's Heavenly Creatures (1994) and gained recognition for Sense and Sensibility (1995), earning her first Academy Award nomination. Global stardom followed with James Cameron's epic romance Titanic (1997), then the highest-grossing film of all time. She subsequently favoured critically acclaimed smaller productions, including Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), Finding Neverland (2004), Little Children (2006), and Revolutionary Road (2008).

She won the Academy Award for Best Actress for playing a former Nazi camp guard in The Reader (2008). Her later work includes the Divergent series, Steve Jobs (2015), and James Cameron's Avatar: The Way of Water (2022). On television she won Emmy Awards for Mildred Pierce (2011) and the crime drama Mare of Easttown (2021).

Honours

Winslet was appointed a Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in 2012. She is known for refusing to conform to Hollywood beauty standards and for speaking publicly about body image and the pressures on young actors.
