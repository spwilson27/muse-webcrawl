Transcription into Chinese characters

Transcription into Chinese characters is the use of Chinese characters to represent the sound of words from other languages, chiefly names of people, places and organisations. Because Chinese characters are logographic, transcription selects characters whose pronunciation approximates the foreign sound, often disregarding their literal meanings.

Principles

Standard transcription systems are published by official bodies — in mainland China, the Xinhua News Agency's reference service standardises renderings for major languages — and are based on Mandarin pronunciation in simplified characters. Cantonese, Taiwanese and historical transcriptions can differ markedly, producing variant names for the same foreign entity.

Character choice

Transcribers prefer characters with neutral or auspicious connotations and avoid characters with strongly negative meanings. Some transcriptions are partly semantic: country names such as Měiguó ("beautiful country") for the United States are admired, while brand names are often crafted to combine sound with attractive meanings, such as Kěkǒu Kělè for Coca-Cola.
