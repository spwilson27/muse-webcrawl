The Game & Watch is a series of handheld electronic games developed, manufactured, released, and marketed by Nintendo from 1980 to 1991. Created by game designer Gunpei Yokoi, who was inspired by watching a bored businessman idly pressing buttons on a calculator on a bullet train, each Game & Watch features a single game to be played on an LCD screen in addition to a clock and an alarm.

Series and design

The line grew through several formats: the Silver and Gold series, Wide Screen, the clamshell Multi Screen — whose dual-screen, folding design foreshadowed the Nintendo DS — Tabletop and Panorama models, and the New Wide Screen series. The D-pad control cross, introduced on the Game & Watch version of Donkey Kong in 1982, became a lasting standard for game controllers.

Legacy

The Game & Watch sold roughly 43 million units worldwide and established Nintendo in handheld gaming, paving the way for the Game Boy. Its mascot-like generic character became Mr. Game & Watch in the Super Smash Bros. series, and Nintendo revived the format with anniversary handhelds for Super Mario Bros. (2020) and The Legend of Zelda (2021), as well as the Game & Watch Gallery compilations.
