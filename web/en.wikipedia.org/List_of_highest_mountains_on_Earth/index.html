List of highest mountains on Earth

[Image: Mount Everest from Kala Patthar]
Mount Everest, the highest mountain on Earth above sea level.

There are at least 108 mountains on Earth with elevations of 7,200 metres (23,622 ft) or greater above sea level. All are located in central and southern Asia, in the Himalaya, Karakoram, Hindu Kush, Pamir, Hengduan and Kunlun ranges. The very highest are the fourteen "eight-thousanders", peaks above 8,000 metres.
