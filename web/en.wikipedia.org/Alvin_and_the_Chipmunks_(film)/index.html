Alvin and the Chipmunks (film)

Alvin and the Chipmunks is a 2007 American live-action/animated musical comedy film directed by Tim Hill, based on the characters created by Ross Bagdasarian Sr. It stars Jason Lee as songwriter Dave Seville, with Justin Long, Matthew Gray Gubler, and Jesse McCartney voicing the singing chipmunks Alvin, Simon, and Theodore.

Plot and reception

Struggling songwriter Dave Seville takes in three singing chipmunks whose tree is cut down and brought to Los Angeles; their rise to fame under an exploitative record executive (David Cross) strains the new family. Reviews were largely negative, but the film was a major commercial success and launched a franchise with sequels in 2009, 2011, and 2015.
