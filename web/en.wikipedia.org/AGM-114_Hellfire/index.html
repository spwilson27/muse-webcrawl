AGM-114 Hellfire

TypeAir-to-surface missile
OriginUnited States
In service1984–present
GuidanceSemi-active laser homing; millimeter-wave radar (Longbow)
Range≈ 8 km (varies by variant)

The AGM-114 Hellfire is an American air-to-ground missile system developed primarily for anti-armour use. It was first produced in 1984 and is used by the United States and allied forces from helicopters, fixed-wing aircraft, and unmanned aerial vehicles.

Design

Most Hellfire variants home on laser energy reflected from a target designated by the launch aircraft or a ground observer. The Longbow Hellfire (AGM-114L) uses a millimeter-wave radar seeker for fire-and-forget operation. Warheads include shaped-charge anti-tank, blast-fragmentation, and thermobaric types.

Use

The missile is carried by the AH-64 Apache, MQ-1 Predator and MQ-9 Reaper drones, and many other platforms. The AGM-114R9X variant, fitted with pop-out blades instead of explosives, has been reported in targeted strikes.
