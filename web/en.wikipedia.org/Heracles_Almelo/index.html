Heracles Almelo is a Dutch professional football club based in Almelo, founded on 3 May 1903 and named after Heracles, the son of Zeus in Greek mythology. The club plays its home matches at the Asito Stadion (formerly Erve Asito and Polman Stadion) and has spent much of its modern history moving between the top-flight Eredivisie and the second-tier Eerste Divisie.

History

Heracles were Dutch national champions twice in the amateur era, in 1927 and 1941. In the professional era the club's honours are modest: it won the Eerste Divisie in 1985, 2005 and 2023, earning promotion to the Eredivisie on each occasion. Its best modern Eredivisie finish came in 2015–16, when sixth place and victory in the European play-offs brought qualification for the UEFA Europa League — the club's first European campaign, ended by Portuguese side Arouca in the third qualifying round. Heracles also reached the KNVB Cup final in 2012, losing to PSV Eindhoven. The club was relegated in 2022 but returned to the Eredivisie at the first attempt as Eerste Divisie champions in 2023; a further relegation followed in 2025. The club's traditional rivals are FC Twente, with whom it contests the Twentse derby.
