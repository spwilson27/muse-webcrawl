CERT Coordination Center

The CERT Coordination Center (CERT/CC) is a computer security incident response centre at the Software Engineering Institute of Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. It was created in November 1988 by the US Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) in response to the Morris worm, the first major worm to spread across the Internet.

Role

CERT/CC studies software vulnerabilities, coordinates disclosure between researchers and vendors, publishes advisories and the Vulnerability Notes Database, and develops tools and practices for incident response. It was the first computer security incident response team (CSIRT) and helped inspire the worldwide network of CERTs and the Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams (FIRST).

Name

"CERT" originated as an acronym for "computer emergency response team". The CERT Division of the SEI continues the centre's work in cybersecurity research, training and vulnerability analysis.
