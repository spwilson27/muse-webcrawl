21 (Adele album)

21 is the second studio album by English singer-songwriter Adele, released on 24 January 2011 in Europe by XL Recordings and on 22 February 2011 in North America by Columbia Records. The album was named after the age of the singer during its production.

Background and recording

Following the success of her debut album 19, Adele began writing 21 after separating from her partner. The album draws on soul, blues, country and roots music, with production by Rick Rubin, Paul Epworth, Ryan Tedder, Dan Wilson and others.

Singles

The album spawned the hit singles "Rolling in the Deep", "Someone like You" and "Set Fire to the Rain", all of which reached number one in numerous countries. "Someone like You" gained enormous exposure after Adele's performance at the 2011 Brit Awards.

Commercial performance

21 became one of the best-selling albums of all time, with sales of over 31 million copies worldwide. It topped charts in more than 30 countries and was the world's best-selling album of both 2011 and 2012. In the United States it spent 24 weeks atop the Billboard 200.

Accolades

At the 54th Grammy Awards in 2012, the album won six awards, including Album of the Year, and "Rolling in the Deep" won Record and Song of the Year.
