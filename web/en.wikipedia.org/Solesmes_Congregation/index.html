Solesmes Congregation

The Solesmes Congregation is an association of Benedictine monasteries centred on the Abbey of Saint-Pierre de Solesmes in Sarthe, France, founded by Dom Prosper Guéranger, who refounded monastic life at Solesmes in 1833 after its suppression during the French Revolution.

History

Guéranger was appointed first abbot when Pope Gregory XVI raised Solesmes to an abbey in 1837 and made it head of a French Benedictine congregation. The congregation is notable for its restoration of Gregorian chant: monks of Solesmes, including Dom Joseph Pothier and Dom André Mocquereau, developed the rhythmic interpretation and published the chant books that underpinned the Church's official editions.

Member houses

The congregation today includes monasteries in France, England (Quarr and other houses), Spain, the Netherlands, Canada, and the United States (including Clear Creek Abbey in Oklahoma), as well as communities of nuns. It forms part of the worldwide Benedictine Confederation.
