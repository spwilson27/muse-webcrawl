County town

A county town in Great Britain and Ireland is the principal town of a county, usually its administrative centre and historically the seat of assizes, county councils and other county institutions.

Characteristics

The county town is not always the largest settlement — for example Lancaster is the county town of Lancashire while Preston hosts administration and Manchester and Liverpool are larger. Examples include York for Yorkshire historically, Chelmsford for Essex, Maidstone for Kent, and Enniskillen for County Fermanagh. The term is also used in some other former British-influenced contexts.
