Joseph Christian Leyendecker (23 March 1874 – 25 July 1951) was a German-American illustrator, regarded as one of the preeminent American illustrators of the early 20th century. He is best known for his poster, book, and advertising illustrations, including more than 400 covers for The Saturday Evening Post.

Career

Born in Germany, Leyendecker emigrated with his family to Chicago as a child and studied at the Art Institute of Chicago and in Paris. Between 1899 and 1943 he painted 322 covers for The Saturday Evening Post, popularising images that became fixtures of American culture, including the New Year's Baby, the plump red-suited Santa Claus, and Mother's Day flowers. His Arrow Collar Man advertisements for menswear were among the most famous advertising images of the era.

Influence

Leyendecker's bold, sculptural style — built on extensive use of live models, including his partner Charles Beach — strongly influenced Norman Rockwell, who began his own Post career in Leyendecker's shadow and served as a pallbearer at his funeral.
