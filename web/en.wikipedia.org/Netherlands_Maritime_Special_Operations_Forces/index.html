Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces

The Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces (NLMARSOF, also simply called MARSOF) is the special operations force of the Netherlands Marine Corps (Korps Mariniers) of the Royal Netherlands Navy. It was established in 2013 by merging earlier specialist units, including the frogmen of the Kikvorsmannen and the Unit Intervention Maritime (UIM).

Organisation

NLMARSOF consists of two operational squadrons. C-Squadron (Mountain Leader and special reconnaissance elements) specialises in reconnaissance and operations in mountainous and arctic terrain, building on the Marine Corps' long cooperation with the British Royal Marines' Mountain Leader cadre. M-Squadron (Maritime Counter Terrorism) is trained for maritime counter-terrorism, including boarding of ships and offshore installations, and incorporates the former UIM.

Selection and training

Personnel are drawn from experienced marines who undergo demanding selection and specialist training in diving, parachuting, sniping, demolitions, and close-quarters battle. The unit works closely with the army's Korps Commandotroepen under the Netherlands Special Operations Command (NLD SOCOM), established in 2018.
