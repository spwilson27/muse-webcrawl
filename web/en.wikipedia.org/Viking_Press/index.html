Viking Press

Viking Press (formally Viking Penguin, also listed as Viking Books) is an American publishing company owned by Penguin Random House. It was founded in New York City on March 1, 1925, by Harold K. Guinzburg and George S. Oppenheim.

History

The firm's name and logo — a Viking ship drawn by Rockwell Kent — were chosen to evoke exploration in publishing. Viking published important works by John Steinbeck, including The Grapes of Wrath (1939), as well as authors such as Jack Kerouac, Saul Bellow, James Joyce (Finnegans Wake), Thomas Pynchon and Stephen King.

Viking merged with Penguin Books in 1975 and is now an imprint of Penguin Random House, continuing to publish literary fiction, non-fiction and the Viking Children's Books line.
