Hot Boys

The Hot Boys (stylized as Hot Boy$) are an American hip hop group from New Orleans, Louisiana, formed in 1997 by the Cash Money Records label. The group consisted of rappers B.G., Lil Wayne, Juvenile and Turk, all of whom also released solo albums on the label. They are regarded as one of the most influential groups in Southern hip hop, helping to bring bounce music and New Orleans rap to a national audience in the late 1990s.

Career

The group debuted with the album Get It How U Live! (1997), which sold regionally despite receiving little radio play. Their second album, Guerrilla Warfare (1999), was a major commercial success, debuting at number five on the Billboard 200 and eventually certified platinum; it featured the singles "We on Fire" and "I Need a Hot Girl". The phrase "bling bling" was popularised by B.G.'s 1999 solo single of the same name.

The group fragmented in the early 2000s as Turk and Juvenile left Cash Money over financial disputes; only Lil Wayne remained with the label, later becoming one of the best-selling rappers in the world. A third album, Let 'Em Burn (2003), was assembled from unreleased recordings after the group's breakup. The members have reunited for occasional performances, including at Lil Wayne's Lil Weezyana Fest in New Orleans, where the surviving members performed together in the 2020s.
