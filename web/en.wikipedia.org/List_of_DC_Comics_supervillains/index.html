List of DC Comics supervillains

DC Comics, one of the largest American comic book publishers, has created a large roster of supervillains who oppose its superheroes, including Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman and the Justice League.

Major villains

  • The Joker — Batman's archenemy, a criminal mastermind with a clown-like appearance.
  • Lex Luthor — Superman's nemesis, a brilliant billionaire industrialist.
  • Darkseid — tyrannical ruler of Apokolips, seeking the Anti-Life Equation.
  • Catwoman, Penguin, Riddler, Two-Face, Bane, Ra's al Ghul, Poison Ivy, Scarecrow, Mr. Freeze — members of Batman's rogues gallery.
  • Sinestro, Reverse-Flash (Eobard Thawne), Cheetah, Ares, Black Adam, Brainiac, Doomsday, Deathstroke, Harley Quinn, Gorilla Grodd, Captain Cold, Black Manta, Ocean Master, Circe, Vandal Savage, the Anti-Monitor and many others.

Teams

Villainous teams include the Legion of Doom, the Injustice League, the Secret Society of Super Villains, the Suicide Squad (often coerced), and the Crime Syndicate of America from Earth-3.
