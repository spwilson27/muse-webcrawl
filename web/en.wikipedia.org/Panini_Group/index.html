Panini Group

The Panini Group is an Italian company headquartered in Modena, founded in 1961 by the Panini brothers, best known worldwide for its collectible stickers and trading cards.

Stickers and publishing

Panini's football sticker albums, especially those produced for the FIFA World Cup since 1970, are a cultural phenomenon in many countries. The group also publishes comics — including licensed Marvel and Disney titles in several markets — books and magazines.

Operations

The company has subsidiaries and distributors across Europe and the Americas, printing billions of stickers each year.
