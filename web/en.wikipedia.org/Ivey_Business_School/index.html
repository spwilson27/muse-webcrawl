Ivey Business School

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Ivey Business School is the business school of Western University in London, Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1922, it is one of Canada's oldest business schools and is known for its case-method teaching, modelled on that of Harvard Business School.

History and programs

The school is named for Richard G. Ivey. It offers an undergraduate Honors Business Administration (HBA) program, an MBA, an Executive MBA, MSc and PhD programs. In 1998, Ivey opened the Cheng Yu Tung Management Institute in Hong Kong, becoming the first North American business school with a campus in Asia. Ivey Publishing is a major distributor of business case studies.

Rankings and alumni

Ivey's MBA and HBA programs are consistently ranked among the best in Canada, and its alumni include numerous Canadian business and political leaders.
