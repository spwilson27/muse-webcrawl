LE Audio

LE Audio is a set of audio specifications within Bluetooth Low Energy, announced by the Bluetooth Special Interest Group (SIG) at CES in January 2020. It introduces the LC3 codec, multi-stream audio, support for hearing aids, and Auracast broadcast audio, which allows one transmitter to share audio with an unlimited number of nearby receivers.

Features

LE Audio runs on the Bluetooth Low Energy radio rather than Bluetooth Classic. Its Low Complexity Communications Codec (LC3) delivers higher audio quality than the older SBC codec at roughly half the bitrate, extending battery life. Multi-stream support enables independent, synchronised streams to each earbud, improving true wireless stereo.

Auracast

Auracast broadcast audio, finalised in 2022, lets venues such as airports, gyms, theatres and places of worship broadcast audio streams that visitors can join with their own earbuds or hearing devices.
