Lycanthropy

Lycanthropy is the mythological ability of a human to transform into a wolf (or werewolf), either purposely or after being placed under a curse, often in connection with the full moon.

Folklore

Werewolf beliefs are widespread in European folklore, with trials of accused werewolves occurring in early modern Europe alongside witch trials. In Greek myth, Lycaon was transformed into a wolf by Zeus — the origin of the term.

Clinical lycanthropy

In psychiatry, clinical lycanthropy is a rare syndrome in which a person believes they can transform, or have transformed, into an animal. Proposed historical explanations for werewolf reports include rabies, hypertrichosis and ergot poisoning.
