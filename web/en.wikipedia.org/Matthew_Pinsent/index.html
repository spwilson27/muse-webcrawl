Matthew Pinsent

Born: 10 October 1970, Holt, Norfolk, England
Sport: Rowing
Olympic golds: 4 (1992, 1996, 2000, 2004)

Sir Matthew Clive Pinsent (born 10 October 1970) is an English rower and broadcaster. During his rowing career, he won 10 world championship gold medals and four consecutive Olympic gold medals, three of them in coxless pairs with Steve Redgrave. He is one of the most successful British Olympians of all time.

Rowing career

Educated at Eton College and St Catherine's College, Oxford, Pinsent began his partnership with Redgrave in 1990. They won Olympic gold in the coxless pair at Barcelona 1992 and Atlanta 1996. After Redgrave switched to the coxless four, Pinsent won gold again at Sydney 2000, and at Athens 2004 he led the coxless four to a famous victory over Canada by eight hundredths of a second. He retired from international rowing after the Athens Games.

Later life

Pinsent was knighted in the 2005 New Year Honours. He has worked as a sports broadcaster for the BBC, covering Olympic Games and rowing events, and has been a member of the International Olympic Committee and the British Olympic Association. In 2004 he was awarded the Thomas Keller Medal by FISA for his outstanding career in rowing.
