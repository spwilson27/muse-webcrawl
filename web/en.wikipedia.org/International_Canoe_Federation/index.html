International Canoe Federation

The International Canoe Federation (ICF) is the umbrella organization of all national canoe organizations worldwide. It is headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland, and administers all aspects of canoe sport worldwide.

History

The ICF was founded in 1924 in Copenhagen, Denmark, as the Internationale Repräsentantenschaft für Kanusport (IRK). It adopted its current name in 1946. Canoe sprint became an Olympic sport at the 1936 Berlin Games, and canoe slalom joined the Olympic programme at Munich in 1972.

Disciplines

The ICF governs canoe sprint, canoe slalom, canoe marathon, canoe polo, wildwater canoeing, canoe freestyle, paracanoe, dragon boat, stand up paddling, and other paddling disciplines. It organizes world championships and the qualification systems for the Olympic and Paralympic Games in cooperation with the International Olympic Committee.
