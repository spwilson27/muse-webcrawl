Emomali Rahmon

Emomali Rahmon (born 5 October 1952) is a Tajik politician who has served as President of Tajikistan since 1994, and previously as head of state from 1992. He is one of the longest-serving leaders in the former Soviet Union.

Born: 5 October 1952, Danghara, Tajik SSR, Soviet Union
Office: President of Tajikistan (1994–present)
Party: People's Democratic Party

Early life and career

Rahmon was born Emomali Sharipovich Rakhmonov in Danghara. He worked in collective farming and local government before becoming chairman of a state farm and a deputy in the Supreme Soviet.

Presidency

He came to power during the Tajikistani Civil War (1992–1997), which ended with a peace agreement with the United Tajik Opposition. He has won successive elections in 1999, 2006, 2013, and 2020, which international observers criticized as not free and fair. His government emphasizes secularism, has banned certain religious practices, and maintains close relations with Russia and China. In 2015 he was given the title Leader of the Nation, granting him lifelong immunity and privileges after leaving office.
