World Rowing, formerly the Fédération Internationale des Sociétés d'Aviron (FISA), is the international governing body for the sport of rowing. Founded in Turin in 1892, it is the oldest international sports federation in the Olympic movement.

History

FISA was created by rowing federations from France, Switzerland, Belgium, Italy, and the Adriatic clubs to standardise rules, race distances, and boat classes. It organised the first European championships in 1893 and the first World Rowing Championships in 1962 at Lucerne.

Events

World Rowing oversees the World Rowing Championships, the World Rowing Cup series, junior and under-23 championships, coastal rowing, and indoor rowing, and works with the IOC on the Olympic rowing programme. It rebranded from FISA to World Rowing in 2020.
