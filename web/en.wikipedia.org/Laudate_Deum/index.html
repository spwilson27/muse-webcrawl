Laudate Deum

Laudate Deum ("Praise God") is an apostolic exhortation by Pope Francis, published on 4 October 2023, the feast of St Francis of Assisi. It is a follow-up to his 2015 encyclical Laudato si' on care for the environment.

Content

The document warns that responses to the climate crisis in the eight years since Laudato si' have been insufficient, reviews climate science, criticises climate denial and the "technocratic paradigm", and calls for effective international agreements ahead of COP28 in Dubai. It closes by grounding ecological action in faith: "Praise God for all his creatures".
