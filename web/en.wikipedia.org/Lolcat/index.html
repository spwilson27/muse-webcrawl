Lolcat

A lolcat is an image macro or captioned photograph of a cat, usually with humorous text in a idiosyncratic, deliberately misspelled form of English known as lolspeak or kitty pidgin. The word is a portmanteau of "lol" (laughing out loud) and "cat".

History

Captioned cat pictures circulated as early as the 1870s photographs of Harry Pointer and in early-2000s forums, but the genre exploded in 2007 with the website I Can Has Cheezburger?, named after a famous lolcat of a British Shorthair apparently asking for a cheeseburger. "Caturday" posting traditions on forums such as 4chan helped spread the format.

Style

Lolspeak captions use playful misspellings, unusual grammar and verb forms ("I can has", "do not want"), often presented as the cat's own speech in a bold sans-serif font. Related phenomena include lolrus images, ceiling cat and basemant cat memes, and the LOLCODE programming language based on lolspeak.

Legacy

Lolcats are regarded as one of the earliest widespread internet meme formats, and have been the subject of exhibitions, books and linguistic study of their pseudo-pidgin.
