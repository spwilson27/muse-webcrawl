AB Bofors

Founded1646 (ironworks); company 1873
HeadquartersKarlskoga, Sweden
IndustryDefence, steel
ProductsArtillery, anti-aircraft guns

AB Bofors is a Swedish arms manufacturer founded in Karlskoga, best known for the Bofors 40 mm anti-aircraft gun, one of the most widely used air-defence weapons of the twentieth century.

History

The Bofors works began as an ironworks in 1646 and were incorporated in 1873. Alfred Nobel owned the company from 1894 until his death in 1896 and shifted its focus from steel toward armaments. During the 1930s Bofors developed the 40 mm automatic gun that armed navies and armies on both sides of the Second World War.

Later years

The company was involved in the Bofors scandal in India in the 1980s concerning howitzer sales. Its defence business passed through Celsius and Saab before the land-weapons division became part of BAE Systems Bofors; the name survives within BAE Systems AB.
