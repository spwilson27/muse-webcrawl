Zumbi

Zumbi (1655 – 20 November 1695), also known as Zumbi dos Palmares, was a Brazilian quilombola leader and one of the most famous figures of resistance to slavery in colonial Brazil. He was the last king of the Quilombo dos Palmares, a large settlement of fugitives from slavery in the captaincy of Pernambuco (in present-day Alagoas), and is celebrated in Brazil as a symbol of Afro-Brazilian resistance and freedom.

Palmares

The Quilombo dos Palmares was a federation of settlements founded around 1605 by people escaping Portuguese sugar plantations; at its height it may have housed 11,000–20,000 inhabitants and controlled a substantial territory in the Serra da Barriga. It survived repeated Portuguese and Dutch military expeditions for most of the 17th century. Palmares was organized with a king, councils, agriculture, crafts, and African-derived political and religious practices.

Life

Zumbi was born free in Palmares, a nephew of its king Ganga Zumba. Captured as an infant in a raid, he was given to a Portuguese priest, baptized Francisco, and educated in Portuguese and Latin; at about fifteen he escaped and returned to Palmares, where he became a renowned war leader. When Ganga Zumba accepted a peace treaty with the Portuguese in 1678 requiring relocation and the return of future fugitives, Zumbi rejected the terms, deposed his uncle, and continued armed resistance. After years of attacks, Portuguese forces under the bandeirante Domingos Jorge Velho destroyed the capital, Macaco, in 1694. Zumbi escaped but was betrayed, ambushed, and killed on 20 November 1695; his head was displayed in Recife to dispel the belief that he was immortal.

Legacy

The date of Zumbi's death, 20 November, is observed in Brazil as Black Consciousness Day (Dia da Consciência Negra), a public holiday in many states and municipalities. Zumbi is a national hero of Brazil, honoured in monuments, schools, music, and the name of an airport in Maceió; scholars continue to debate the details of his life, much of which rests on thin colonial records.
