The canton of Basel-Stadt (German: Kanton Basel-Stadt) is one of the 26 cantons forming the Swiss Confederation. It is composed of three municipalities: Basel, Riehen and Bettingen, situated where the Swiss, French and German borders meet. Traditionally it is considered a "half-canton", the other half being Basel-Landschaft, its rural partner canton; the division dates to 1833, when the former canton of Basel split after political conflict between city and countryside.

Basel-Stadt is the smallest canton by area but among the most densely populated. The city of Basel is a major centre of the pharmaceutical and chemical industries, home to Novartis and Roche, and of banking and trade fairs, including Art Basel and Baselworld. The canton joined the Swiss Confederation in 1501. Its constitution dates, in its current form, from 2005.

Basel-Stadt is the seat of Switzerland's oldest university, founded in 1460, and of the Bank for International Settlements. The canton's best-known cultural event is the Basler Fasnacht, its carnival.
