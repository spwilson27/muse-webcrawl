The Prodigy are an English electronic dance music band formed in Braintree, Essex, in 1990 by producer, keyboard player and songwriter Liam Howlett. They are credited as pioneers of the breakbeat and big beat genres, achieving worldwide mainstream success in the 1990s.

Along with the Chemical Brothers and Fatboy Slim, the Prodigy helped bring rave culture into the charts and arenas.

History

Howlett formed the band with dancers Keith Flint and Leeroy Thornhill after meeting at an acid house night. Their debut album Experience (1992) captured UK rave; Music for the Jilted Generation (1994) broadened their sound. The Fat of the Land (1997), featuring Flint's vocals on "Firestarter" and "Breathe", topped charts in many countries.

Later career

Later albums include Always Outnumbered, Never Outgunned (2004), Invaders Must Die (2009), The Day Is My Enemy (2015) and No Tourists (2018). Flint died in March 2019; the band later returned to touring with Maxim and Howlett at its core.

Musical style

Their music blends breakbeats, punk attitude, samples and aggressive synthesisers, with live shows noted for their energy.
