Variable data printing

Variable data printing (VDP) is a form of digital printing, including on-demand printing, in which elements such as text, graphics, and images may be changed from one printed piece to the next, using information drawn from a database, without stopping or slowing the printing process.

Applications

VDP is widely used in direct marketing, where mail pieces are personalised with the recipient's name, address, purchase history, or tailored offers; in transactional documents such as bills and statements (sometimes called transpromo); and for tickets, labels, and certificates with unique serial numbers or barcodes. It enables mass customisation and one-to-one marketing communications.

Technology

The workflow combines a database, page-layout software, and a digital press. Data and business rules determine which variable elements appear in each document; output languages and formats such as PPML, VPS, and PDF/VT were developed to stream variable content efficiently to high-speed printers.
