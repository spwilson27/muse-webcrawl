Equatorial Guinea competed at the 2000 Summer Olympics in Sydney, Australia, sending a delegation of four athletes competing in athletics and swimming. The team received worldwide attention through the swimmer Eric Moussambani, who became famous for his struggle to complete the 100 metres freestyle.

Swimming

Eric Moussambani Malonga, nicknamed "Eric the Eel", had learned to swim only months before the Games and had never seen an Olympic-size pool. When his two competitors in the heat false-started and were disqualified, he swam alone, finishing in 1 minute 52.72 seconds — the slowest time in Olympic history — to sustained cheering from the crowd. Paula Barila Bolopa competed in the women's 50 metres freestyle, also drawing attention for her very slow heat time.

Athletics

Equatorial Guinea's track athletes competed in sprint events but did not progress beyond the heats. The country's participation continued a tradition, begun at the 1984 Los Angeles Games, of Olympic involvement despite very limited sporting infrastructure.
