Resident Evil (film series)

Resident Evil is an action horror film series loosely based on Capcom's video-game franchise of the same name. The best known sequence, released from 2002 to 2016, follows Alice, an original character played by Milla Jovovich, as she fights the Umbrella Corporation and the biological weapons and undead produced by the T-virus.

Paul W. S. Anderson wrote the six films and directed four. The plots diverge substantially from the games, although characters including Jill Valentine, Claire Redfield, Chris Redfield, Leon S. Kennedy, Ada Wong, and Albert Wesker appear. The series combines gun-centred action, martial arts, science fiction, and zombie horror.

Reception and box office

Reviews were generally mixed to negative, especially for plotting and fidelity to the games, but the films were commercially successful. Together they grossed more than US$1.2 billion worldwide, for a time making Resident Evil the highest-grossing film series based on video games.

Reboot

Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City (2021), written and directed by Johannes Roberts, rebooted the continuity and adapted elements of the first two games more directly. It follows Claire and Chris Redfield, Jill Valentine, Leon Kennedy, and other residents during the 1998 Raccoon City outbreak. A separate live-action television series premiered on Netflix in 2022 and was cancelled after one season.
