Félix Tshisekedi

Félix Antoine Tshisekedi Tshilombo (born 13 June 1963) is a Congolese politician who has served as the fifth president of the Democratic Republic of the Congo since January 2019. He is the son of the veteran opposition leader Étienne Tshisekedi and led the Union for Democracy and Social Progress (UDPS), the country's oldest and largest opposition party.

Presidency

Tshisekedi's victory in the December 2018 general election, though disputed, led to the first peaceful transfer of power in the country's history. He was re-elected in December 2023. His tenure has focused on the conflict in the eastern provinces, relations with Rwanda, and economic reforms.
