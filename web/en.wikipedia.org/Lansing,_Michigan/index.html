Lansing is the capital city of the U.S. state of Michigan. Located mostly in Ingham County, in the south-central part of the Lower Peninsula at the confluence of the Grand and Red Cedar rivers, it was chosen as the state capital in 1847. The wider metropolitan area includes East Lansing, home of Michigan State University.

History

Lansing was named after Lansing, New York. It grew with the automobile industry: Ransom E. Olds founded Oldsmobile there in 1897, and General Motors remains a major employer.

Government and economy

As state capital, government is a major sector, alongside education, insurance and manufacturing. Landmarks include the Michigan State Capitol building, completed in 1879.
