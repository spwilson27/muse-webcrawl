Bipin Rawat

General Bipin Rawat (16 March 1958 – 8 December 2021) was an Indian military officer who served as the first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of the Indian Armed Forces from January 2020 until his death in a helicopter crash in December 2021. He previously served as the 57th Chairman of the Chiefs of Staff Committee and as the 27th Chief of the Army Staff of the Indian Army.

Career

Rawat was commissioned into the 5th battalion of the 11 Gorkha Rifles in 1978. He commanded forces along the Line of Actual Control with China and in counter-insurgency operations in Jammu and Kashmir and the northeast. As Army Chief from 2016 to 2019 he oversaw the 2016 cross-Line of Control strikes and the Army's posture during the Doklam standoff. As CDS he was charged with creating the Department of Military Affairs and driving joint theatre commands and integration among the three services.

Death

Rawat, his wife Madhulika and 11 others died on 8 December 2021 when their Indian Air Force Mi-17V5 helicopter crashed near Coonoor, Tamil Nadu. He was posthumously awarded the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian honour.
