The Amazing Spider-Man 2

The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (released in some markets as The Amazing Spider-Man 2: Rise of Electro) is a 2014 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Spider-Man. Directed by Marc Webb, it is the sequel to The Amazing Spider-Man (2012) and the fifth theatrical Spider-Man film overall. Andrew Garfield stars as Peter Parker / Spider-Man, alongside Emma Stone, Jamie Foxx, Dane DeHaan, Campbell Scott, Embeth Davidtz, Colm Feore, Paul Giamatti and Sally Field.

Plot

Peter Parker struggles to keep his promise to the late Captain George Stacy to stay away from his daughter Gwen, even as they graduate from high school. Oscorp electrical engineer Max Dillon, an ignored Spider-Man admirer, suffers an accident that transforms him into the electricity-wielding Electro. Meanwhile Peter's childhood friend Harry Osborn, dying of the same retroviral disease that killed his father Norman, believes Spider-Man's blood can save him; refused, he injects himself with refined spider venom and becomes the Green Goblin. In the climax at a power plant, Spider-Man and Gwen defeat Electro, but the Goblin arrives; Gwen falls from a clock tower and dies when Peter's web line stops her fall too abruptly. Months later, a grieving Peter returns to heroism after watching Gwen's graduation speech.

Reception and aftermath

The film grossed $709 million worldwide — the lowest of the Spider-Man films at the time — and received mixed reviews criticizing its crowded plot. Sony cancelled the planned sequels and spin-offs; in February 2015 Sony and Marvel Studios announced Spider-Man would join the Marvel Cinematic Universe, with Tom Holland debuting in Captain America: Civil War (2016). Garfield later reprised his role in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021).
