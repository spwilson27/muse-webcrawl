Outer Banks

The Outer Banks are a 200-mile (320 km) string of barrier islands off the coast of North Carolina and southeastern Virginia, separating the Atlantic Ocean from mainland North Carolina's sounds. They are a major tourist destination known for beaches, state parks and historic sites.

History

The islands were the site of the Roanoke Colony (1585–1590), England's first attempt at settlement in the Americas, whose disappearance remains a mystery. The Wright brothers made the first powered flights at Kill Devil Hills near Kitty Hawk in 1903. The treacherous shoals offshore earned the nickname "Graveyard of the Atlantic".

Geography

Major islands and communities include Bodie Island, Hatteras Island (with Cape Hatteras lighthouse), Ocracoke, and Roanoke Island with the town of Manteo. Much of the area lies within Cape Hatteras and Cape Lookout National Seashores. The islands are low, narrow and vulnerable to hurricanes and sea-level rise.
