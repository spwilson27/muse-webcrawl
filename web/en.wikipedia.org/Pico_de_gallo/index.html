Pico de gallo

Pico de gallo (Spanish for "rooster's beak"), also called salsa fresca or salsa cruda, is a fresh, chunky Mexican salsa made from chopped tomato, onion and serrano or jalapeño peppers, with salt, lime juice and cilantro. Its red, white and green colours echo the Mexican flag, and it is also known as salsa mexicana.

Use

Unlike blended salsas, pico de gallo is uncooked and coarse-textured, with little liquid. It is served as a topping or accompaniment for tacos, grilled meats, molletes, eggs and many other dishes, and as a dip with tortilla chips.

Name

The origin of the name is uncertain; explanations include that it was eaten by pinching between thumb and forefinger like a rooster pecking, or that the chopped peppers resemble feed. Regional varieties add ingredients such as cucumber, radish, mango or other fruit.
