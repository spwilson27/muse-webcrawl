Ganon

Ganon is a fictional character and the principal antagonist of Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda video game series. He is depicted in two forms: a monstrous, boar-like demon known as Ganon, and a human Gerudo man named Ganondorf. Created by Shigeru Miyamoto and first appearing in The Legend of Zelda (1986), Ganon is the archenemy of the hero Link and a recurring threat to the kingdom of Hyrule and Princess Zelda.

Character

Ganon is characterised by an insatiable lust for power, above all for the Triforce, the sacred relic that grants the wishes of whoever possesses it. In the series' mythology he is the reincarnating embodiment of the hatred of the demon king Demise. As Ganondorf, he is the king of the Gerudo, a tribe of desert-dwelling people, and is typically portrayed as a tall, imposing sorcerer and warlock of great physical and magical strength. His beast form, Ganon, is a giant boar-like monster, sometimes wielding a trident.

Appearances

Ganon or Ganondorf has appeared in most mainline entries in the series, including A Link to the Past, Ocarina of Time, The Wind Waker, Twilight Princess, and Tears of the Kingdom, in which he is voiced by Matthew Mercer in English. In Breath of the Wild he appears as the monstrous Calamity Ganon. The character is frequently cited among the greatest villains in video game history, and the human portrayal in The Wind Waker is often singled out for giving him a more sympathetic motivation.
