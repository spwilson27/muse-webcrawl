Elevon

Elevons are aircraft control surfaces that combine the functions of the elevator (pitch control) and the aileron (roll control) into one. The name is a blend of "elevator" and "aileron".

Operation

Elevons are placed on the trailing edge of each wing. When moved together in the same direction they act as elevators, changing pitch; when moved differentially — one up, one down — they act as ailerons, rolling the aircraft. A mechanical or electronic mixer combines the pilot's pitch and roll inputs.

Applications

Elevons are essential on tailless aircraft and delta-wing designs, which lack a separate horizontal tailplane. Famous examples include the Avro Vulcan bomber, the Concorde supersonic airliner, the Space Shuttle orbiter and many flying-wing and stealth aircraft such as the B-2 Spirit.
