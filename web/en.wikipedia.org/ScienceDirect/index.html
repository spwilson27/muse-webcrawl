ScienceDirect

ScienceDirect is a website that provides access to a large bibliographic database of scientific and medical publications of the Dutch publisher Elsevier. Launched in March 1997, it hosts over 18 million pieces of content from more than 4,000 academic journals and 30,000 e-books. The articles are grouped in four main sections: Physical Sciences and Engineering, Life Sciences, Health Sciences, and Social Sciences and Humanities.

Access

Most content on ScienceDirect is available only by subscription or on a pay-per-view basis; individual users typically gain access through their university or institutional library. A minority of journals and articles are open access. The platform supports searching, browsing by journal, alerts, and linking through DOIs, and it provides usage statistics to subscribing institutions.

Usage and criticism

ScienceDirect is one of the largest scholarly literature platforms in the world, recording well over a billion article downloads. Elsevier's pricing and bundling practices for platforms like ScienceDirect have been criticized by libraries and researchers and were a major factor in the rise of the open access movement; several university systems have publicly negotiated or temporarily cancelled their Elsevier subscriptions.
