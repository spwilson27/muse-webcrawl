Dodge Challenger

The Dodge Challenger is the name of three different automobiles produced by Dodge. The best known is the pony car introduced for the 1970 model year to compete with the Ford Mustang and Chevrolet Camaro, and the retro-styled muscle car revived in 2008.

First generation (1970–1974)

Sharing its E-body platform with the Plymouth Barracuda, the original Challenger offered engines from a slant-six to the 426 Hemi V8. The high-performance Challenger T/A competed in Trans-Am racing. Sales faded with the fuel crisis and the model was dropped after 1974.

Later models

The Challenger name returned in 1978 on a rebadged Mitsubishi Galant Lambda coupe, sold until 1983. The modern Challenger debuted in 2008 with styling closely echoing the 1970 car, built on Chrysler's LX platform. Performance versions escalated through the SRT8, the 707-horsepower Hellcat (2015), the drag-focused Demon (2018, 840 hp) and the Demon 170 (2023, over 1,000 hp on E85). Production of the modern car ended in December 2023 as Dodge moved toward electrified muscle cars.
