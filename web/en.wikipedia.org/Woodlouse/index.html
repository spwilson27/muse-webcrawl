Woodlouse

A woodlouse (plural woodlice) is a terrestrial crustacean belonging to the suborder Oniscidea within the isopods. Despite the name, woodlice are not insects and are not closely related to lice; they are more closely related to shrimps and crabs. Over 3,500 species are known worldwide.

KingdomAnimalia

OrderIsopoda
SuborderOniscidea

Description

Woodlice have a segmented, flattened exoskeleton, seven pairs of legs, and two pairs of antennae. Many species — the "pill woodlice" such as Armadillidium — can roll into a ball (conglobation) when threatened. They breathe through feathery gills and so require damp environments.

Ecology

Woodlice feed mainly on decaying plant matter and are important decomposers in soils and leaf litter. They shelter under stones, bark, and logs, emerging mostly at night.
