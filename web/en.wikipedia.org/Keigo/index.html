Keigo

Keigo is the system of honorific speech in the Japanese language, used to convey respect, humility and social distance. It is essential to business and formal interaction in Japan.

Categories

Keigo is traditionally divided into sonkeigo (respectful language elevating the listener or subject), kenjōgo (humble language lowering the speaker), and teineigo (polite language, including the desu/masu forms). Honorifics involve special verbs — for example irassharu (to be/go/come, respectful) and itasu (to do, humble) — as well as respectful prefixes o- and go-.

Mastery of keigo is taught in schools and corporate training; misuse can cause offence, and elaborate keigo remains a hallmark of customer service in Japan.
