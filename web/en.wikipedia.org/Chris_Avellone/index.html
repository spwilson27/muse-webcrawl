Chris Avellone (born 1971) is an American video game designer and comic book writer known for his narrative work on role-playing games. He was a senior designer at Interplay's Black Isle Studios and a co-founder of Obsidian Entertainment, where he served as creative director.

Career

At Black Isle, Avellone was the lead designer of Planescape: Torment (1999), widely praised for its writing, and worked on the Fallout and Icewind Dale series. At Obsidian he contributed to Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II, Neverwinter Nights 2, Alpha Protocol, and as lead designer of several Fallout: New Vegas downloadable content packs, as well as Pillars of Eternity.

Later work

After leaving Obsidian in 2015, Avellone worked as a freelance narrative designer on titles including Prey (2017), Divinity: Original Sin II, Pathfinder: Kingmaker, and Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order. He has also written comic books set in the Star Wars and Fallout universes.
