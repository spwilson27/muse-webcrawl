Libra (constellation)

Libra
Symbolism: the Balance / Scales
Brightest star: Zubeneschamali (β Lib)
Zodiac sign: Libra

Libra is a constellation of the zodiac, representing a balance scale. It lies between Virgo to the west and Scorpius to the east. Its brightest stars form a quadrangle; the two brightest, Alpha and Beta Librae, are named Zubeneschamali ("northern claw") and Zubenelgenubi ("southern claw"), recalling a time when the constellation was considered part of Scorpius's claws.

History

The Babylonians associated the stars with scales, and in Roman tradition Libra was linked with justice and the goddess Astraea. It is the only zodiac constellation representing an inanimate object rather than a person or animal. The Sun passes through Libra in late October and November, and the September equinox once lay within it.

Notable objects

Libra contains the globular cluster NGC 5897 and the star Gliese 581, a red dwarf once studied for possibly habitable-zone planets.
