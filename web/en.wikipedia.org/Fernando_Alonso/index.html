Fernando Alonso Díaz (born 29 July 1981) is a Spanish racing driver who competes in Formula One for Aston Martin. He won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 2005 and 2006 with Renault, ending Michael Schumacher's run of titles and becoming, at the time, the youngest double world champion in the sport's history.

Formula One career

Alonso debuted in Formula One with Minardi in 2001 and joined Renault as a race driver in 2003, winning his first Grand Prix in Hungary that year. His back-to-back championships followed in 2005 and 2006. He spent a turbulent single season at McLaren in 2007, finishing third in a four-way title fight, returned to Renault for 2008–2009, and then drove for Ferrari from 2010 to 2014, finishing runner-up in the championship three times, twice losing the title at the final race.

A less successful second stint at McLaren (2015–2018) preceded a two-year sabbatical from Formula One. He returned with Alpine in 2021, scoring a podium in Qatar, and moved to Aston Martin for 2023, where a string of early-season podiums made him one of the year's standout performers despite being the oldest driver on the grid.

Other racing

Alonso won the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 2018 and 2019 with Toyota and the FIA World Endurance Championship for the 2018–19 season. He competed in the Indianapolis 500 in 2017 and 2019, leading laps on his debut, and contested the Dakar Rally in 2020. His pursuit of victory at Indianapolis forms part of an attempt at the informal Triple Crown of Motorsport.
