Icarus (film)

Directed by Bryan Fogel
Released 2017
Genre Documentary

Icarus is a 2017 American documentary film by Bryan Fogel. It chronicles Fogel's exploration of illegal doping in sports, which leads him to Grigory Rodchenkov, the head of Russia's anti-doping laboratory, and into the exposure of Russia's state-sponsored doping programme.

Plot

Fogel, an amateur cyclist, sets out to test performance-enhancing drugs on himself in order to expose weaknesses in sports drug testing. His experiments bring him to Rodchenkov, whose revelations about systematic doping of Russian athletes turn the film into an account of one of the largest scandals in sporting history.

Reception and awards

The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival, where it won a special jury prize, and went on to win the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature at the 90th Academy Awards.
