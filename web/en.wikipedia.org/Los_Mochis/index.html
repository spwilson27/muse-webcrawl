Los Mochis

Los Mochis is a coastal city in the northern part of the Mexican state of Sinaloa, in the municipality of Ahome, of which it is the seat. Founded in 1893 around sugar-cane agriculture promoted by American entrepreneur Benjamin F. Johnston, it grew into a major agricultural and commercial centre of the fertile Fuerte River valley.

The city is a gateway to the Copper Canyon via the Ferrocarril Chihuahua al Pacífico (El Chepe) railway, which begins at Los Mochis and climbs into the Sierra Tarahumara. Its port, Topolobampo, lies about 20 km away on the Gulf of California. Its name derives from the Cahita word for a local plant or turtle.
