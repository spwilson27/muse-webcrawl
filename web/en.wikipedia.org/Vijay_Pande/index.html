Vijay Pande

Vijay S. Pande is a Trinidadian-American scientist and venture capitalist. A former professor of chemistry, structural biology and computer science at Stanford University, he is best known for founding Folding@home, the distributed computing project that uses volunteers' computers to simulate protein folding.

Career

Pande received his bachelor's degree from Princeton and his PhD in physics from MIT in 1995. At Stanford he led the Pande Lab, applying large-scale computation and, later, machine learning to problems in biophysics and drug discovery. In 2015 he joined the venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) as a general partner leading its bio fund, investing in companies at the intersection of biology, computation and engineering.
