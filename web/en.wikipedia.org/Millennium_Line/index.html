The Millennium Line is the second line of the SkyTrain rapid transit system in the Metro Vancouver region of British Columbia, Canada. It is operated by TransLink and runs from VCC–Clark station in Vancouver through Burnaby and Port Moody to Lafarge Lake–Douglas station in Coquitlam.

History

The first phase opened in 2002, connecting Waterfront station (sharing tracks with the Expo Line) to Commercial Drive and onward through Burnaby. The Evergreen Extension to Port Moody and Coquitlam opened on December 2, 2016.

Route

The line is largely elevated, using the same driverless SkyTrain technology as the Expo Line, and interchanges with it at Commercial–Broadway and Lougheed Town Centre stations. A further extension west along Broadway to Arbutus is under construction.
