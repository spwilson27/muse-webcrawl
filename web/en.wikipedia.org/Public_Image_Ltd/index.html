Public Image Ltd (abbreviated as PiL) is an English post-punk band formed by lead vocalist John Lydon (previously Johnny Rotten of the Sex Pistols), guitarist Keith Levene, bassist Jah Wobble, and drummer Jim Walker in May 1978, following the breakup of the Sex Pistols. The band's membership has rotated frequently; Lydon is the sole constant member.

PiL's early work—particularly the albums First Issue (1978) and Metal Box (1979)—is widely regarded as among the most challenging and innovative of the post-punk era, fusing dub reggae basslines, avant-garde noise, krautrock rhythms, and Lydon's confrontational vocals. Metal Box, originally packaged in a film-canister-style metal box, is frequently cited as one of the greatest albums of its time.

Career

The band's debut single, "Public Image" (1978), reached the UK top ten. After Jah Wobble's departure in 1980 and Levene's in 1983, Lydon continued PiL as a more fluid project. The album This Is What You Want... This Is What You Get (1984) produced the dance hit "This Is Not a Love Song"; Album (1986), featuring musicians including Steve Vai and Ginger Baker, yielded the hit "Rise". Later records included Happy? (1987), 9 (1989), and That What Is Not (1992), after which the band went on hiatus.

Lydon re-formed PiL in 2009 with Lu Edmonds, Bruce Smith, and Scott Firth, touring widely and releasing the albums This Is PiL (2012), What the World Needs Now... (2015), and End of World (2023).

Legacy

Public Image Ltd are regarded as pioneers of post-punk, influencing alternative rock, industrial, and dance music. Their disregard for rock convention, corporate-style presentation (PiL was literally incorporated as a company), and integration of dub and disco anticipated much of the experimental popular music that followed.
