Spelthorne

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


The River Thames at Staines, in Spelthorne

Spelthorne is a local government district with borough status in Surrey, England. Its council is based in Staines-upon-Thames; other towns in the borough include Ashford, Sunbury-on-Thames, Shepperton, and Stanwell. Much of the borough lies within the Metropolitan Green Belt and the M25 motorway corridor, and it borders London (Hillingdon and Hounslow) to the east.

History

The name derives from the Spelthorne hundred, an Anglo-Saxon administrative division (recorded in the Domesday Book as Spelethorne). The borough was formed in 1974 from the urban districts of Staines and Sunbury-on-Thames, transferred from the former county of Middlesex to Surrey; the borough's inclusion in Surrey remains locally debated. The area includes part of Heathrow Airport's vicinity (Stanwell), the reservoirs of the Thames valley, and Shepperton Studios.
