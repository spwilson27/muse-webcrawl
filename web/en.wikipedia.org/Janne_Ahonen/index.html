Janne Petteri Ahonen (born May 11, 1977) is a Finnish former ski jumper widely regarded as one of the greatest in the sport's history. Over a career spanning more than two decades he won five Four Hills Tournament titles — a record — two World Cup overall crystal globes (2003–04 and 2004–05) and multiple World Championship gold medals, though an individual Olympic gold eluded him.

Career

Ahonen debuted in the World Cup in 1992 as a teenager and won his first World Championship team gold in 1995. Known for his stoic demeanour and immaculate, nearly expressionless style, he dominated the mid-2000s, equalling and then breaking records for podium finishes. He retired in 2008, returned for the 2010 Vancouver Olympics, retired again, and made further comebacks, competing into his forties in national events.

Personal life

Ahonen is also an accomplished drag racer, competing in Top Fuel dragsters, and worked as a commentator for Finnish television after his jumping career.
