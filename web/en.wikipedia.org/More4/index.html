More4

Launched: 10 October 2005
Owner: Channel Four Television Corporation
Country: United Kingdom
Sister channels: Channel 4, E4, Film4, 4seven

More4 is a British free-to-air television channel owned and operated by the Channel Four Television Corporation. It launched on 10 October 2005 and is aimed primarily at an adult, upmarket audience, with programming including documentaries, drama, lifestyle shows, and repeats of Channel 4 content.

Programming

At launch, More4 carried a nightly news programme and documentary strands, positioning itself as a more "grown-up" complement to E4's youth focus. Its schedule has included imported American dramas such as The West Wing and ER, British property and lifestyle programmes such as Grand Designs and Location, Location, Location, and popular factual entertainment including The Great British Bake Off repeats and Come Dine with Me. The channel also broadcast the satirical programme The Last Leg in early series and various Channel 4 news and documentary repeats.

Availability

More4 is available on Freeview, Freesat, Sky, and Virgin Media in the United Kingdom and Ireland, and streams via Channel 4's on-demand service. A one-hour timeshift channel, More4 +1, has also operated.
