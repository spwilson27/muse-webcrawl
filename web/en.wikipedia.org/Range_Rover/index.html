Range Rover

ManufacturerLand Rover (Jaguar Land Rover)
Production1970–present
ClassLuxury SUV
LayoutFront-engine, four-wheel drive

The Range Rover is a luxury sport utility vehicle produced by Land Rover, a marque of Jaguar Land Rover. Launched in 1970, it combined the off-road capability of the Land Rover with car-like comfort, effectively creating the luxury SUV segment.

Generations

The first-generation "Classic" (1970–1996) began as a two-door with a V8 engine and permanent four-wheel drive. Successive generations — P38A (1994), L322 (2002), L405 (2012), and L460 (2022) — progressively added luxury, technology, and aluminium construction. The Range Rover name also heads a sub-brand including the Sport, Evoque, and Velar models.

Significance

An early Range Rover was exhibited at the Louvre as an example of industrial design. The model remains a status symbol and a benchmark for luxury off-road vehicles.
