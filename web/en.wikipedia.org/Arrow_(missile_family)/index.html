Arrow (missile family)

Type: Anti-ballistic missile system

Place of origin: Israel / United States

In service: 2000–present (Arrow 2); 2017–present (Arrow 3)

Designer: Israel Aerospace Industries (MLM), Boeing

Variants: Arrow 1, Arrow 2, Arrow 3

The Arrow (Hebrew: חֵץ, Ḥetz) is a family of anti-ballistic missiles designed to fulfill an Israeli requirement for a theater missile defense system. Jointly funded and produced by Israel and the United States, development of the system began in 1986 and has continued since. The Arrow system consists of the joint production hypersonic anti-missile interceptors Arrow 2 and Arrow 3, the Elta EL/M-2080 "Green Pine" early-warning AESA radar, the Elisra "Golden Citron" ("Citron Tree") C3I center, and the Israel Aerospace Industries "Brown Hazelnut" ("Hazelnut Tree") launch control center.

Background

The need for a dedicated anti-ballistic missile system arose from the threat of Iraqi Scud missiles and later from Iran's ballistic missile programme. The Arrow programme was undertaken as part of the US Strategic Defense Initiative cooperation, and is one of the most advanced missile defense programmes in existence.

Arrow 2

The Arrow 2 is a two-stage interceptor using a blast-fragmentation warhead that destroys incoming ballistic missiles in the upper atmosphere (endo-atmospheric and low exo-atmospheric intercepts). It was first deployed in 2000. The Arrow 2 achieved its first operational intercept in combat conditions on 30 October 2023, when it destroyed a ballistic missile launched from Yemen during the Gaza war — widely reported as the first instance of space warfare.

Arrow 3

The Arrow 3 is designed for exo-atmospheric interception, using a hit-to-kill kinetic vehicle capable of intercepting ballistic missiles in space, including potential intercontinental ballistic missiles carrying weapons of mass destruction. It was declared operational on 18 January 2017. On 9 November 2023, Arrow 3 made its first operational interception, destroying a ballistic missile launched toward Israel.

Deployment and export

The Arrow system forms the top tier of Israel's multi-layered air and missile defense, above Iron Dome and David's Sling. In 2023 Germany agreed to purchase the Arrow 3 system, the largest defence export deal in Israel's history at the time (approximately US$3.5 billion).
