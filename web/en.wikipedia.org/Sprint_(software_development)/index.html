Sprint (software development)

A sprint is a time-boxed period during which a Scrum team works to complete a set amount of work. Sprints are at the heart of the Scrum and agile methodologies, typically lasting between one week and one month, with two weeks being the most common length.

Structure

Each sprint begins with sprint planning, in which the team selects items from the product backlog and sets a sprint goal. Daily Scrum meetings coordinate work during the sprint. At the end, a sprint review demonstrates the increment to stakeholders and a sprint retrospective examines how the team can improve its process.

Principles

During a sprint, the scope negotiated with the product owner is intended to remain stable so the team can focus; the sprint backlog is the team's plan for delivering the goal.
