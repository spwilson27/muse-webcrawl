Yenisei

Yenisei (Russian: Енисе́й) most commonly refers to the Yenisei River, the great river system flowing north through Siberia to the Arctic Ocean — the largest river draining into the Arctic and the fifth-longest river system in the world. The name may also refer to several related subjects.

Uses

  • Yenisei River — the river in Russia, formed at Kyzyl in Tuva by the Great and Little Yenisei, flowing through Krasnoyarsk to the Kara Sea.
  • Yenisei Gulf — the long estuary where the river meets the Kara Sea.
  • Yeniseian languages — a small Siberian language family named after the river, whose only surviving member is Ket; the family has been linked by Edward Vajda to the Na-Dene languages of North America in the proposed Dene–Yeniseian family.
  • Yenisei Kyrgyz — a Turkic people and khaganate of the upper Yenisei in the medieval period.
  • Yenisei Governorate — a province of the Russian Empire (1822–1925) centred on Krasnoyarsk.
  • BC Enisey and Yenisey-STM — basketball and rugby clubs from Krasnoyarsk.
  • Yenisei — a Soviet/Russian photograph reconnaissance satellite series (Zenit variants) and various ships of the Russian navy.
