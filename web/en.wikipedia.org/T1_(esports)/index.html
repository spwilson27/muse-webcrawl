T1 is a South Korean esports organisation, originally founded as SK Telecom T1, best known for its League of Legends team, the most successful in the game's history.

History

The team rose to prominence after signing mid laner Lee Sang-hyeok, known as Faker, in 2013, winning the World Championship that year and again in 2015 and 2016 — the only back-to-back titles of their era.

Later titles

After several near misses, T1 won the World Championship again in 2023 and 2024, completing a second back-to-back run with the same core roster, an unprecedented feat.

Other divisions

T1 has fielded teams in games including Valorant, StarCraft, and Dota 2.
