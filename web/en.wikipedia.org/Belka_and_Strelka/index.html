Belka and Strelka

Belka ("Squirrel" or "Whitey") and Strelka ("Little Arrow") were Soviet space dogs who spent a day in space aboard Korabl-Sputnik 2 (Sputnik 5) on 19 August 1960 before safely returning to Earth.

The flight

They were the first Earth-born creatures to go into orbit and return alive, accompanied by mice, rats, flies and plants. Their success helped pave the way for Yuri Gagarin's human flight in 1961.

Aftermath

Strelka later had puppies, one of which, Pushinka, was given to U.S. President John F. Kennedy's family. The dogs became celebrated figures in Soviet popular culture.
