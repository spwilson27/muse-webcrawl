Deportation of the Crimean Tatars

The deportation of the Crimean Tatars (Crimean Tatar: Qırımtatar sürgünligi) was the ethnic cleansing and forced transfer of the entire Crimean Tatar population by the Soviet Union in May 1944, ordered by Joseph Stalin on accusations of collaboration with Nazi Germany.

Events

Beginning 18 May 1944, NKVD troops rounded up some 190,000 Crimean Tatars and deported them in cattle trains, mainly to the Uzbek SSR. Many thousands died from starvation, disease and harsh conditions during transit and in exile.

Aftermath

Crimean Tatars campaigned for decades for the right to return; mass return began only in the late 1980s. Ukraine and several other states have recognised the deportation as a genocide.
