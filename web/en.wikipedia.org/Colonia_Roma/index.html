Colonia Roma

Colonia Roma, also called La Roma, is a district located in the Cuauhtémoc borough of Mexico City just west of the city's historic center. It was planned as an upper-class Porfirian-era suburb in the early 1900s and is now known for its Art Deco and Art Nouveau architecture, tree-lined avenues, galleries, restaurants, and nightlife.

History

The colonia was built on land that had been the village of La Romita and potreros belonging to the Countess of Miravalle. Development began around 1903 under Porfirio Díaz. After the 1985 Mexico City earthquake damaged many buildings, the district declined, then revived from the 2000s as a cultural and gastronomic hub.

Landmarks

Notable sites include Plaza Río de Janeiro with its replica of Michelangelo's David, Avenida Álvaro Obregón, Casa Lamm, the Museo del Objeto del Objeto, and numerous galleries. The 2018 film Roma by Alfonso Cuarón, set in the district, brought renewed international attention.

Divisions

Roma is generally divided into Roma Norte and Roma Sur, separated by Avenida de los Insurgentes area avenues.
