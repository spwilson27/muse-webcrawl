Bellagio (resort)

Bellagio is a resort, luxury hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada. It is owned and operated by MGM Resorts International and was built on the site of the demolished Dunes hotel and casino. Inspired by the Lake Como town of Bellagio in Italy, the resort is famous for the Fountains of Bellagio, a dancing water feature set in an 8.5-acre lake in front of the hotel.

Attractions

The resort opened in 1998 under developer Steve Wynn at a cost of about $1.6 billion, at the time the most expensive hotel ever built. Its attractions include the Fountains of Bellagio, the Conservatory and Botanical Gardens, the Bellagio Gallery of Fine Art, and the Cirque du Soleil aquatic production O. The hotel's main tower has 36 floors and nearly 3,000 rooms, supplemented by the Spa Tower.

In popular culture

The Bellagio is prominently featured in the 2001 film Ocean's Eleven, in which its vault is the target of a heist.
