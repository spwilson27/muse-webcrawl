Lushootseed

dxʷləšucid
Region: Puget Sound, Washington
Family: Salishan — Coast Salish

Lushootseed (dxʷləšucid) is a Coast Salish language historically spoken by many Indigenous peoples around Puget Sound in present-day Washington State, including the Duwamish, Suquamish, Snohomish, Swinomish, Puyallup and Nisqually peoples.

Dialects

Northern and Southern dialects are traditionally distinguished. The language is written in a Latin-based orthography using characters such as č, š, ƛ̕ and ʔ for sounds not found in English.

Revitalization

After generations of suppression, tribal language programs — notably the work of linguist Vi Hilbert (taqʷšəblu) of the Upper Skagit — have documented stories and taught Lushootseed in schools and universities. Chief Seattle (Si'ahl) was a Lushootseed speaker.
