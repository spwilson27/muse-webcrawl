Baahubali: The Beginning is a 2015 Indian epic action film directed by S. S. Rajamouli and produced by Arka Media Works. The first of two cinematic parts, the film was shot simultaneously in Telugu and Tamil and follows Sivudu (Shiva), a tribal youth who learns of his royal heritage and sets out to rescue the imprisoned queen Devasena, while the story of Amarendra Baahubali is told in flashback.

Plot

Raised by a tribal community at the foot of a great waterfall, the strong and curious Shiva repeatedly climbs toward the summit and encounters the warrior Avantika, whose rebel group seeks to free Devasena, the former queen of Mahishmati, held captive by the tyrannical king Bhallaladeva. Shiva infiltrates the kingdom and is hailed by its people, prompting the slave-commander Kattappa to recount the story of Shiva's father, Amarendra Baahubali — and the film ends on the revelation that Kattappa himself killed Baahubali.

Reception

The film was the most expensive Indian production of its time and became one of the highest-grossing Indian films ever on release, praised for its scale, visual effects, and Rajamouli's direction. It won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film. Its sequel, Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017), became the highest-grossing Indian film of all time at that point, and the two films together established a pan-Indian model for large-scale regional cinema.
