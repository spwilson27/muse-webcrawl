Oasis (band)

Oasis are an English rock band formed in Manchester in 1991. Led by brothers Liam Gallagher (vocals) and Noel Gallagher (lead guitar, songwriting), they became the most successful act of the Britpop era, selling more than 75 million records worldwide before splitting in 2009 amid the brothers' long-running feud — and sensationally reuniting in 2024 for a stadium tour.

History

Their debut album Definitely Maybe (1994) became the fastest-selling debut in UK history at the time. The follow-up, (What's the Story) Morning Glory? (1995), containing "Wonderwall", "Don't Look Back in Anger", and "Champagne Supernova", sold over 22 million copies and made them global stars. In August 1996 they played to 250,000 people over two nights at Knebworth. Later albums, from Be Here Now (1997) to Dig Out Your Soul (2008), met declining reviews but continued commercial success.

Breakup and reunion

The band broke up in August 2009 when Noel quit hours before a Paris festival performance after an altercation with Liam. Both brothers pursued solo projects — Liam with Beady Eye and then solo, Noel with Noel Gallagher's High Flying Birds. In August 2024, Oasis announced a reunion tour for 2025, with tickets selling out amid enormous demand.

Style and legacy

Oasis's guitar-driven sound draws on the Beatles, the Stone Roses, and glam and punk rock, and their public rivalry with Blur — the "Battle of Britpop" — defined mid-1990s British popular culture.
