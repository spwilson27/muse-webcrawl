Joey Dunlop

William Joseph Dunlop OBE (25 February 1952 – 2 July 2000) was a motorcycle racer from Ballymoney, Northern Ireland, regarded as the greatest road racer of all time.

Dunlop won the Isle of Man TT a record 26 times, including three hat-tricks, and won the Ulster Grand Prix 24 times. Known for his quiet, self-effacing manner and his humanitarian aid convoys to Eastern European orphanages, he was killed in a crash while racing in Tallinn, Estonia, in 2000. His record of TT wins stood for over two decades.
