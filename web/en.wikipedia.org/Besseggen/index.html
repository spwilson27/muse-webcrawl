Besseggen

Besseggen is a mountain ridge in Vågå Municipality in Innlandet county, Norway, within Jotunheimen National Park. The ridge runs between the green, glacial lake Gjende and the blue lake Bessvatnet, and its highest point reaches 1,743 metres above sea level. The hike across Besseggen is one of the most popular mountain walks in Norway, attracting tens of thousands of hikers each year.

The hike

The classic route runs between the Gjendesheim and Memurubu tourist cabins, a walk of roughly 14 kilometres that typically takes six to eight hours. Many hikers take the boat across Gjende to Memurubu and walk back over the ridge. The narrow crest of the ridge, with Gjende lying some 400 metres below on one side and Bessvatnet on the other, provides the striking contrast between the milky green water of Gjende — coloured by glacial flour — and the clear blue of Bessvatnet.

In literature

Besseggen is famous in Norwegian culture through Henrik Ibsen's play Peer Gynt (1867), in which Peer boasts of riding a reindeer buck along the "Gjendin ridge" — a scene inspired by a hunter's tale from the area. The ridge is also crossed by the historical figure of the local guide Jo Gjende.
