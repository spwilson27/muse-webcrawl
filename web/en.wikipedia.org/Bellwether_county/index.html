Counties tracked as presidential bellwethers.

A bellwether county in United States politics is a county that reliably votes for the winner of presidential elections, and is therefore watched as an indicator of national political trends. The term derives from the bellwether sheep that leads a flock.

Notable bellwethers

Vigo County, Indiana voted for the winning presidential candidate in every election from 1956 to 2016, before backing the loser in 2020. Valencia County, New Mexico picked the winner in every election from 1952 to 2016. Analysts attribute such streaks to demographics that closely mirror the nation, though changing populations and polarisation have broken many long-running streaks in recent cycles; statistically, some counties will match long sequences by chance alone.

Bellwether states

The related concept of a bellwether state applies to states such as Ohio, which voted for the winner in every election from 1964 to 2016, and Missouri, once so reliable that its name was synonymous with the phenomenon before its streak ended in 2008.
