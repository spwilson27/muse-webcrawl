ORF (German: Österreichischer Rundfunk, lit. 'Austrian Broadcasting') is Austria's national public broadcaster. Funded primarily by a licence fee, it operates four national television channels (ORF 1, ORF 2, ORF III and ORF Sport +), a number of radio stations including Ö1, Ö3 and FM4, and regional studios in each of the nine federal states.

History

Radio broadcasting in Austria began in 1924 with RAVAG. The modern ORF was established in 1955, and television broadcasts began shortly afterwards. A legal reform in 1974 restructured the organisation as a public-law institution. ORF held a monopoly on broadcasting in Austria until private radio and television were licensed in the 1990s.

Programming

ORF produces news (the flagship Zeit im Bild), drama, sport and cultural programming, and is a major co-producer of German-language film and television. Its orchestras and the annual broadcast of the Vienna Philharmonic's New Year's Concert are internationally known.
