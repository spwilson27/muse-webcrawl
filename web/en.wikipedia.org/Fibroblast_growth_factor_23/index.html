Fibroblast growth factor 23

SymbolFGF23
Chromosome12p13.32 (human)
FunctionPhosphate and vitamin D regulation

Fibroblast growth factor 23 (FGF23) is a protein in humans that is encoded by the FGF23 gene. It is a member of the fibroblast growth factor family and acts as a hormone produced mainly by osteocytes in bone. FGF23 regulates phosphate homeostasis and vitamin D metabolism by acting on the kidneys.

Function

FGF23 decreases the reabsorption of phosphate in the proximal tubule of the kidney, promoting urinary phosphate excretion, and suppresses the activation of vitamin D by inhibiting 1α-hydroxylase. Its signalling requires the co-receptor α-Klotho together with FGF receptors.

Clinical significance

Mutations in FGF23 that make the protein resistant to cleavage cause autosomal dominant hypophosphatemic rickets. Excess FGF23 also drives tumour-induced osteomalacia and X-linked hypophosphatemia. The antibody burosumab, which blocks FGF23, is used to treat these conditions. Elevated levels are also associated with chronic kidney disease.
