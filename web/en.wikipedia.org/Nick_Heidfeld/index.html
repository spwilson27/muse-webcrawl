Nick Lars Heidfeld (born 10 May 1977) is a German racing driver. He competed in Formula One from 2000 to 2011, driving for Prost, Sauber, Jordan, Williams, BMW Sauber and Renault, and holds the record for the most podium finishes without a Grand Prix victory (13). Nicknamed "Quick Nick", he was regarded as one of the most consistent points scorers of his era.

Early career

Heidfeld rose through German Formula Ford and Formula 3, winning the German F3 championship in 1997, and then the International Formula 3000 title in 1999 as a McLaren junior driver.

Formula One career

He debuted with the Prost team in 2000, then moved to Sauber (2001–03), Jordan (2004) and Williams (2005), where he took his only pole position, at the 2005 European Grand Prix, and three podiums. From 2006 to 2009 he drove for BMW Sauber, finishing a career-best fifth in the 2007 championship and recording a memorable one-two finish behind team-mate Robert Kubica in Canada in 2008. After BMW's withdrawal, Heidfeld became Mercedes' test driver and Pirelli's tyre tester before returning to race with Sauber in late 2010. In 2011 he replaced the injured Kubica at Renault, scoring a podium in Malaysia, before leaving the team mid-season.

Later career

Heidfeld subsequently raced in the FIA World Endurance Championship with Rebellion Racing, including class podiums at Le Mans, and in Formula E with Mahindra Racing, scoring multiple podiums in the electric series.
