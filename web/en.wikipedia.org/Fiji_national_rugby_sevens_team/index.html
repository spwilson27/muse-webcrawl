Fiji national rugby sevens team

The Fiji national rugby sevens team represents Fiji in men's international rugby sevens. One of the most successful teams in the history of the sport, Fiji won the gold medal at the 2016 Summer Olympics in Rio de Janeiro — Fiji's first ever Olympic medal — and retained the title at the Tokyo 2020 Games, before taking silver at Paris 2024.

History

Fiji has been a dominant force in sevens since the format's early days, famous for a free-flowing, offloading style of play. The team has won the Hong Kong Sevens many times — its most successful tournament — and has won the World Rugby Sevens Series championship multiple times. Fiji also won the Rugby World Cup Sevens in 1997 and 2005.

Olympic Games

Coached by Ben Ryan, Fiji defeated Great Britain 43–7 in the final of the inaugural Olympic sevens tournament in 2016, sparking celebrations across the country, where rugby is the national sport. Under Gareth Baber, Fiji beat New Zealand 27–12 in the Tokyo 2020 final to defend the gold. At Paris 2024, Fiji reached the final again but lost to hosts France.

Style and significance

Fiji's players, drawn largely from domestic competitions and the country's military and police teams, are renowned for instinctive offloading, one-handed carries, and improvised passing out of contact. Sevens success is a source of immense national pride for Fijians, for whom the team is a unifying symbol.
