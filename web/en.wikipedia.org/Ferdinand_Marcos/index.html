Ferdinand Marcos

Ferdinand Emmanuel Edralin Marcos Sr. (11 September 1917 – 28 September 1989) was a Filipino politician and kleptocrat who served as the tenth president of the Philippines from 1965 to 1986. He ruled under martial law from 1972 until 1981, and his authoritarian regime was marked by corruption, extravagance, and human rights abuses before he was deposed by the People Power Revolution of 1986.

Early life and political career

Marcos was born in Sarrat, Ilocos Norte, to Mariano Marcos, a politician, and Josefa Edralin. He studied law at the University of the Philippines and topped the 1939 bar examinations while under indictment — later overturned by the Supreme Court — for the murder of his father's political rival Julio Nalundasan. During the Second World War he claimed an extensive guerrilla record that later investigations found to be largely fabricated. He served in the House of Representatives from 1949 and in the Senate from 1959, becoming Senate President in 1963.

Presidency and martial law

Marcos won the 1965 presidential election against Diosdado Macapagal and was re-elected in 1969, the first Philippine president to win a second term. Facing unrest, a communist insurgency, and a constitutional bar on a third term, he declared martial law on 21 September 1972 (announced 23 September) under Proclamation No. 1081. He dissolved Congress, arrested opposition figures including Benigno Aquino Jr., shut down media outlets, and ruled by decree, presiding over a crony-dominated economy that eventually collapsed into deep recession.

Downfall and legacy

The assassination of Aquino on his return from exile in August 1983 galvanised opposition. After a disputed snap election against Aquino's widow Corazon Aquino in February 1986, mass protests and military defections forced Marcos and his family to flee to Hawaii on 25 February 1986. He died in Honolulu in 1989. Courts and the Philippine Presidential Commission on Good Government have sought to recover billions of dollars in ill-gotten wealth accumulated by the Marcos family. His son, Ferdinand "Bongbong" Marcos Jr., was elected president in 2022.
