Electric circuit

An electric circuit is an interconnection of electrical components — such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, switches, and voltage or current sources — forming a closed path through which electric current can flow.

Analysis

Circuit behavior is described by Kirchhoff's circuit laws: the current law (currents at a node sum to zero) and the voltage law (voltages around a loop sum to zero), together with component relations such as Ohm's law, V = IR. Elements may be arranged in series, sharing the same current, or in parallel, sharing the same voltage.

Direct current (DC) circuits reach a steady state, while alternating current (AC) circuits are analyzed with phasors and impedance. Lumped-element models apply when physical dimensions are small compared with the wavelength of the signals involved.
