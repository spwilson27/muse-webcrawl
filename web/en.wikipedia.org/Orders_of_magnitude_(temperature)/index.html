Orders of magnitude (temperature)

Temperature is a physical quantity that expresses the degree of hotness or coldness of a body. This article lists temperatures across many orders of magnitude, expressed in kelvin, from the coldest conditions achievable in laboratories to the hottest events in the universe.

Scales

The kelvin is the SI base unit of thermodynamic temperature. The Celsius and Fahrenheit scales are defined by offsets and degree sizes relative to it; the Rankine scale is the absolute counterpart of Fahrenheit.
