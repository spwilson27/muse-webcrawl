Alyx Vance

Alyx Vance is a fictional character in Valve's Half-Life video game series. She first appeared in Half-Life 2 (2004) as the deuteragonist and companion of Gordon Freeman, and is the protagonist of the virtual-reality game Half-Life: Alyx (2020). She is voiced by Merle Dandridge, and by Ozioma Akagha in Half-Life: Alyx.

Role in the series

Alyx is the daughter of former Black Mesa scientist Eli Vance and a leading member of the human resistance against the alien Combine, which occupies Earth. Skilled in hacking, engineering, and combat, she accompanies the player through much of Half-Life 2 and its episodes, assisted by her robotic pet, Dog. Critics have widely praised Alyx as one of the most believable companion characters in gaming, noting her expressive animation and supportive AI.
