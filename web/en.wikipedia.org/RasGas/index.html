RasGas

IndustryOil and gas (LNG)
Founded1993
Defunct2018 (merged into Qatargas)
HeadquartersDoha, Qatar

RasGas Company Limited was a liquefied natural gas (LNG) producing company in Qatar, headquartered in Doha. It operated production facilities in Ras Laffan Industrial City, drawing gas from Qatar's North Field, the world's largest non-associated natural gas field.

Operations

RasGas operated seven LNG trains with a combined capacity of about 37 million tonnes per annum, supplying markets in Asia and Europe, and also managed the Al Khaleej Gas project supplying domestic pipeline gas. Shareholders included Qatar Petroleum (now QatarEnergy) and ExxonMobil.

Merger

In 2018 RasGas was integrated into Qatargas, creating a single LNG operating company as part of a restructuring of Qatar's energy sector.
