Koduri Marakathamani Keeravani (born 4 July 1961), professionally known as M. M. Keeravani, is an Indian composer, playback singer and lyricist who works predominantly in Telugu cinema, and also in Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi films. Credited as M. M. Kreem in Hindi films and Maragathamani in Tamil cinema, he is one of the most prolific Indian film composers, with a career spanning more than three decades and over 200 films.

Keeravani achieved global recognition for his work with his cousin, director S. S. Rajamouli, composing the scores of the Baahubali films (2015, 2017) and RRR (2022). The RRR song "Naatu Naatu" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song and the Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song in 2023, a first for an Indian feature film production. He was awarded the Padma Shri, India's fourth-highest civilian honour, in 2023.

Early life

Keeravani was born in Kovvur, Andhra Pradesh, into a family with deep film-industry ties: his father, Koduri Siva Shakthi Datta, was a painter and screenwriter, and his brother Kalyani Malik also became a composer. He began learning the violin at the age of four and travelled with music troupes as a child. Before his break as a composer he worked as an assistant to veteran music directors including Chakravarthy.

Career

Keeravani's first film as an independent composer was Manasu Mamatha (1990). He rose to prominence with Ram Gopal Varma's Kshana Kshanam (1991) and the devotional hit Annamayya (1997), which won him a National Film Award for Best Music Direction. His long collaboration with Rajamouli began with Student No: 1 (2001) and includes Simhadri, Magadheera (2009), Eega (2012), the two Baahubali films — among the highest-grossing Indian films ever made — and RRR.

Under the name M. M. Kreem he composed for Hindi films including Criminal, Zakhm, Jism, Paheli and Sur: The Melody of Life, winning Filmfare recognition. His style is noted for blending Indian classical and folk melody with large-scale orchestral writing, and for his integration of background score with a film's emotional structure.

Awards

Keeravani has won a National Film Award, an Academy Award, a Golden Globe Award, a Critics' Choice Award and numerous Nandi Awards and Filmfare Awards South. "Naatu Naatu," performed by Rahul Sipligunj and Kaala Bhairava (his son), became a global viral phenomenon in 2022–23.
