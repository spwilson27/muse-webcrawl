Lands of Sweden

The lands of Sweden (Swedish: landsdelar) are the three traditional regions of the country: Norrland in the north, Svealand in the central region, and Götaland in the south. Unlike counties and municipalities, the lands have no administrative function but remain in common use geographically, culturally and in weather reporting.

Götaland

Götaland, the land of the Geats, comprises the southern provinces including Scania, Småland, Öland, Gotland, Västergötland and Östergötland.

Svealand

Svealand, the land of the Swedes proper, lies in south-central Sweden and includes Stockholm, Uppsala, Värmland and Dalarna.

Norrland

Norrland covers roughly 60 percent of Sweden's land area in the north, but is sparsely populated, and includes Lapland, Västerbotten, Ångermanland and other northern provinces.
