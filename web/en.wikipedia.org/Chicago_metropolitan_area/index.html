Chicago metropolitan area

The Chicago metropolitan area, often called Chicagoland, is the metropolitan area associated with the city of Chicago, Illinois, and its suburbs. It spans northeastern Illinois and extends into northwest Indiana and southeast Wisconsin. With roughly 9.5 million inhabitants, it is the third-largest metropolitan area in the United States.

Composition

The U.S. Office of Management and Budget defines the Chicago–Naperville–Elgin metropolitan statistical area across fourteen counties. The wider combined statistical area adds further counties in Indiana and Wisconsin, including the Milwaukee-Racine area's neighbours to the south. Major suburban cities include Naperville, Aurora, Evanston, Schaumburg, Joliet and Gary, Indiana.

Transportation and economy

The region is one of North America's great transportation hubs, centred on O'Hare International Airport, Midway Airport, an extensive freight-rail network, and the Metra commuter rail system. Its diversified economy includes finance, manufacturing, logistics, healthcare and technology.
