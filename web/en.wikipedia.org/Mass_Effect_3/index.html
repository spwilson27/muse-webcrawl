Mass Effect 3

Developer: BioWare
Publisher: Electronic Arts
Released: 6 March 2012
Genre: Action role-playing, third-person shooter
Series: Mass Effect

Mass Effect 3 is a 2012 action role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts, the final chapter of the original Mass Effect trilogy.

Plot and gameplay

Commander Shepard must unite the galaxy's species and build military assets against the Reapers, machine intelligences invading Earth and the Milky Way. Choices imported from earlier games shape missions and outcomes; gameplay blends squad-based shooting, dialogue and exploration, with a cooperative multiplayer mode.

Reception and ending controversy

The game was critically acclaimed but many fans criticised its endings as insufficiently reflecting player choices, prompting protests and a free Extended Cut DLC clarifying the endings. It sold millions of copies and received multiple awards.
