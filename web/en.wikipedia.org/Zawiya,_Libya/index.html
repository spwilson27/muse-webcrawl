Zawiya, Libya

Zawiya (also spelled Az Zawiyah) is a city in northwestern Libya, on the Mediterranean coast about 47 kilometres west of Tripoli. It is the capital of the Zawiya District and one of Libya's most important oil refining centres.

Economy

Zawiya hosts one of Libya's largest oil refineries, operated by the Zawiya Oil Refining Company, with a capacity of about 120,000 barrels per day, connected by pipeline to southwestern oil fields. The surrounding coastal plain is also an agricultural area producing olives, onions, and livestock fodder.

Recent history

During the Libyan Civil War of 2011, Zawiya was an early centre of revolt against Muammar Gaddafi and was heavily fought over, retaken by government forces in March and recaptured by rebels in August. The city saw further fighting in later years of conflict.
