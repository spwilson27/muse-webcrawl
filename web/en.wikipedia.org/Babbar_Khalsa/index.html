Babbar Khalsa

Babbar Khalsa International (BKI) is a Sikh militant organisation that aims to create an independent Sikh state of Khalistan in the Indian state of Punjab. It is designated as a terrorist organisation by India, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Japan and the United States.

History

The group traces its origins to the Babbar Akali movement of the 1920s; the modern organisation emerged in 1978 after clashes between Sikhs and the Nirankari sect. Babbar Khalsa was among the most active and organised groups during the Punjab insurgency of the 1980s and early 1990s.

Activities

Members of the organisation were convicted in connection with the 1985 bombing of Air India Flight 182, the deadliest act of aviation terrorism before 2001. Its operational strength in India declined after police campaigns of the 1990s, though support networks abroad persisted.
