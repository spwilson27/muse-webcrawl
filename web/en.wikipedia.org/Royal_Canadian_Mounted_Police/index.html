The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP; French: Gendarmerie royale du Canada) is the national and federal police service of Canada. It provides law enforcement at the federal level and, under contract, serves as the provincial and municipal police force in eight provinces, the three territories, and many municipalities. Members are instantly recognizable by the Red Serge tunic, Stetson hat and mounted Musical Ride.

History

The force descends from the North-West Mounted Police, founded in 1873 to establish law and order in the North-West Territories and famous for the March West of 1874. It became the Royal North-West Mounted Police in 1904 and merged with the Dominion Police in 1920 to form the RCMP. The force has since served in both world wars, provided security for the Canadian state, and became an enduring symbol of Canada abroad.

Duties

Federal duties include enforcement of federal statutes, counter-terrorism, organized crime and border integrity, protective policing for the prime minister and visiting dignitaries, and international peacekeeping support. The RCMP's forensic, identification and informatics services support police forces across Canada.
