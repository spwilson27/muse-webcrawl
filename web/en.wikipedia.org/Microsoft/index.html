Microsoft

Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Founded by Bill Gates and Paul Allen on April 4, 1975, it develops, manufactures, licenses, supports, and sells computer software, consumer electronics, personal computers, and related services.

Microsoft Corporation
Founded: April 4, 1975, Albuquerque, New Mexico
Founders: Bill Gates, Paul Allen
Headquarters: Redmond, Washington, U.S.
CEO: Satya Nadella (since 2014)
Products: Windows, Office, Azure, Xbox, Surface

History

Microsoft rose to dominance with the MS-DOS operating system in the 1980s, followed by Microsoft Windows. Its 1986 initial public offering created several billionaires among employees. In the 2000s the company faced major antitrust cases in the United States and Europe.

Recent era

Under CEO Satya Nadella, who succeeded Steve Ballmer in 2014, Microsoft pivoted toward cloud computing with its Azure platform, acquired LinkedIn (2016) and GitHub (2018), and became a leader in artificial intelligence through its partnership with OpenAI. In 2023 it completed the acquisition of Activision Blizzard. Microsoft has repeatedly been among the world's most valuable companies by market capitalization, exceeding $3 trillion in 2024.

Products

Major products include the Windows operating system, the Microsoft 365 productivity suite, the Azure cloud platform, the Xbox gaming division, Surface devices, and the Bing search engine.
