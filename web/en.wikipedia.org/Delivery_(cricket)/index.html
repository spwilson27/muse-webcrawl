Delivery (cricket)

A bowler delivering the ball in cricket

In cricket, a delivery, or ball, is a single action of bowling a cricket ball toward the batter. A series of six legal deliveries constitutes an over. Deliveries are classified by type, including fast, swing, seam, spin (such as leg break, off break, and googly), and slower balls.

Legal delivery

A delivery must be bowled, not thrown; the Laws of Cricket govern arm action, and an umpire may call a no-ball for an illegal action, overstepping the crease, or dangerous bowling. A wide ball is one bowled out of the batter's reasonable reach.

Tactics

Bowlers vary pace, line, length, and movement to deceive batters; notable named deliveries include the yorker, bouncer, doosra, and carrom ball.
