The Balkan Wars were two conflicts fought in the Balkan Peninsula in 1912 and 1913 that largely expelled the Ottoman Empire from Europe and redrew the map of southeastern Europe on the eve of the First World War.

Background

By 1912 the Ottoman Empire's European provinces (Rumelia) were coveted by the newly independent Balkan states, each claiming Macedonian and Thracian territory on ethnic grounds. Encouraged by Russia, Serbia, Bulgaria, Greece and Montenegro formed the Balkan League in 1912. The Ottoman Empire was simultaneously weakened by its war with Italy over Libya.

First Balkan War (1912–1913)

Montenegro declared war on 8 October 1912, followed by the other League members. The League's armies won rapid victories: Bulgarian forces advanced through Thrace and besieged Adrianople (Edirne); Serbian forces won at Kumanovo and took much of Macedonia and Kosovo; Greek forces captured Thessaloniki (Salonika) and advanced in Epirus. By the armistice of December 1912 the Ottomans retained only Constantinople and a small strip of eastern Thrace. The Treaty of London (30 May 1913) ceded nearly all Ottoman territory west of the Enos–Midia line to the League, and the Great Powers mandated the creation of an independent Albania.

Second Balkan War (1913)

Quarrelling over the division of Macedonia, Bulgaria attacked its former allies Serbia and Greece on 29 June 1913. Romania invaded Bulgaria from the north, and the Ottoman Empire retook Adrianople. Bulgaria was quickly defeated and sued for peace. By the Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913), Bulgaria lost most of its gains: Serbia and Greece divided most of Macedonia, Romania took Southern Dobruja, and the Ottomans recovered eastern Thrace.

Consequences

The wars doubled Serbia's territory, left Bulgaria resentful and revisionist, and produced enormous civilian suffering — atrocities against civilians were documented by an international Carnegie Endowment commission. The resulting tensions, especially between Austria-Hungary and an emboldened Serbia, formed part of the background to the outbreak of the First World War in 1914.
