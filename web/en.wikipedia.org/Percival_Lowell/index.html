Percival Lowell

Percival Lowell (March 13, 1855 – November 12, 1916) was an American businessman, author, mathematician, and astronomer who founded the Lowell Observatory in Flagstaff, Arizona.

Career

A member of the Boston Lowell family, he spent time in Japan and Korea and wrote books about East Asia before turning to astronomy. In 1894 he founded his observatory to study Mars; he popularised the theory that the "canals" reported on Mars were the works of an intelligent civilisation, a view later discredited.

Planet X

Lowell also predicted a trans-Neptunian "Planet X" from supposed irregularities in the orbits of Uranus and Neptune. The search he began led Clyde Tombaugh to discover Pluto at Lowell Observatory in 1930; Pluto's symbol (♇) reflects Lowell's initials.
