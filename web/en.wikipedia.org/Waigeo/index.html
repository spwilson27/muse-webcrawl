Waigeo

Waigeo is an island in Southwest Papua province, eastern Indonesia, and the largest of the four main islands of the Raja Ampat archipelago, off the northwestern tip of New Guinea. The island has an area of approximately 3,155 square kilometres and is separated from Halmahera by the Pitt Strait.

Geography and wildlife

Waigeo is rugged and forested, with deeply indented bays such as Mayalibit Bay nearly dividing it in two. It is noted for exceptional biodiversity and forms part of the Coral Triangle marine region. The island is a stronghold for endemic birds including the Wilson's bird-of-paradise and red bird-of-paradise, and Alfred Russel Wallace collected specimens there during his explorations of the Malay Archipelago.

People

The principal settlement is Waisai, the capital of Raja Ampat Regency, on the island's southern coast. Inhabitants include speakers of Papuan and Austronesian languages such as Ma'ya and Biak. Tourism, centred on diving and wildlife, has grown since the 2000s.
