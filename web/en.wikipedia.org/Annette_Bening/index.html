Annette Bening

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Annette Carol Bening (born May 29, 1958) is an American actress. With a career spanning stage and screen over four decades, she has received numerous accolades, including two Golden Globe Awards and a BAFTA Award, along with five Academy Award nominations.

Career

Bening began on stage, earning a Tony Award nomination for Coastal Disturbances (1987). Her film breakthrough came with The Grifters (1990), followed by Bugsy (1991), American Beauty (1999), Being Julia (2004) and The Kids Are All Right (2010) — each bringing her an Oscar nomination. She played Carolyn Burnham in American Beauty, winner of the Academy Award for Best Picture. Later roles include 20th Century Women (2016), Film Stars Don't Die in Liverpool (2017), Captain Marvel (2019) and the swimming biopic Nyad (2023), for which she received her fifth Oscar nomination, portraying long-distance swimmer Diana Nyad.

Personal life

Bening has been married to actor and filmmaker Warren Beatty, her Bugsy co-star, since 1992; they have four children. She has served on the board of trustees of the Actors Fund and is active in arts education.
