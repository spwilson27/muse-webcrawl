Chichester is a cathedral city and civil parish in West Sussex, England. It is the county town of West Sussex and the only city in the county. The city has a long history as a settlement, going back to Roman times, when it was the civitas capital of Noviomagus Reginorum.

History

Chichester retains much of its Roman street plan, with North, South, East and West streets radiating from the medieval Market Cross. The city walls are among the most intact in Southern England. Chichester Cathedral, founded in 1075, is notable for its free-standing bell tower and modern artworks, including a stained-glass window by Marc Chagall.

Culture

The city is home to the Chichester Festival Theatre, founded in 1962, one of the United Kingdom's flagship regional theatres, and the Pallant House Gallery. The nearby Goodwood estate hosts the Festival of Speed and Glorious Goodwood horse racing.
