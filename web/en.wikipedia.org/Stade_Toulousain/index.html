Stade Toulousain
Founded: 1907
Location: Toulouse, France
Stadium: Stade Ernest-Wallon
League: Top 14

Stade Toulousain is a French professional rugby union club based in Toulouse. Founded in 1907, it is one of the most successful clubs in European rugby, with a record number of French championships and European Cup/ Champions Cup titles.

History

The club won its first French title in 1912 and became a dominant force from the 1980s onward. Toulouse won the inaugural European Cup in 1996 and has added several more European titles since, producing generations of French internationals.

Colours and stadium

The team plays in red, black and white at the Stade Ernest-Wallon, which holds around 19,500 spectators.
