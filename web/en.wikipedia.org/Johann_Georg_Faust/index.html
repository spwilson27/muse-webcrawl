Johann Georg Faust

Johann Georg Faust (c. 1480 or 1466 – c. 1541), also known as Doctor Faustus, was a German itinerant alchemist, astrologer, and magician of the German Renaissance.

Expelled from towns and denounced by churchmen as a fraud and sorcerer in league with the devil, Faust died apparently in an alchemical explosion at Staufen im Breisgau. Within decades his life became legend in the Historia von D. Johann Fausten (1587), the source for Marlowe's Doctor Faustus and Goethe's Faust, giving rise to the archetype of the scholar who sells his soul to Mephistopheles.
