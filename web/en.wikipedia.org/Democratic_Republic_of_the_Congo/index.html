The Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC), also known as Congo-Kinshasa and formerly as Zaire, is a country in Central Africa. It is the second-largest country in Africa by area and among the most populous, with more than 100 million inhabitants and over 200 ethnic groups. It is bordered by nine countries and has a short Atlantic coastline. The capital and largest city is Kinshasa.

Geography

Most of the country lies in the basin of the Congo River, the world's second-largest river by discharge, covered by the second-largest rainforest on Earth. The east is mountainous, along the Albertine Rift, with active volcanoes near Lake Kivu; the southeast (Katanga) holds immense mineral deposits. The DRC is one of the world's richest countries in natural resources — including copper, cobalt (the majority of world supply), coltan, gold, diamonds, and timber — yet remains among the poorest by income per head.

History

The region was home to the Kingdom of Kongo and other states before colonisation. From 1885 to 1908 the Congo Free State was the personal property of Belgium's King Leopold II, whose rubber regime killed millions through violence, disease, and exhaustion — one of the great atrocities of the colonial era. The Belgian Congo followed (1908–1960). Independence in 1960 brought the Congo Crisis: the secession of Katanga, the murder of first prime minister Patrice Lumumba, and eventually the 1965 coup of Mobutu Sese Seko, who ruled the renamed Zaire as a kleptocratic one-party state until 1997. Mobutu's overthrow by Laurent-Désiré Kabila triggered the First and Second Congo Wars (1996–1997 and 1998–2003), the latter drawing in nine African states and causing millions of deaths, mostly from disease and malnutrition. Armed conflict, driven partly by control of minerals, has persisted in the eastern provinces, with the M23 rebellion resurging in the 2020s.

Politics and economy

The DRC is a semi-presidential republic. The 2018 election produced the country's first peaceful transfer of power, to Félix Tshisekedi, who was re-elected in 2023. The economy depends on mining exports and is hampered by weak infrastructure, corruption, and insecurity; the vast majority of Congolese work in the informal sector or subsistence agriculture. The Congo Basin rainforest is a globally significant carbon store and reservoir of biodiversity, home to okapis, bonobos, and mountain gorillas.
