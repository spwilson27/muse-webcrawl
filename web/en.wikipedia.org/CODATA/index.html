CODATA

The Committee on Data for Science and Technology (CODATA) is an interdisciplinary committee of the International Science Council. It was established in 1966 to promote global collaboration in improving the quality, management and accessibility of scientific data.

Fundamental constants

CODATA is best known for its Task Group on Fundamental Constants, which periodically publishes internationally recommended values of the fundamental physical constants, such as the speed of light, the Planck constant and the elementary charge, based on a least-squares adjustment of all relevant measurements. Major adjustments appeared in 2018, underpinning the 2019 redefinition of the SI base units.

Other activities

CODATA also works on data policy, open science, FAIR data principles, and data education, coordinating task groups and a biennial conference. Its executive office is in Paris.
