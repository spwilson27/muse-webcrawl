India has a coastline of about 7,500 km and a port system comprising a dozen major ports administered by the central government and more than 200 notified non-major ports under state governments. Roughly 95% of India's trade by volume moves by sea. Ports are governed under the Indian Ports Act and, for major ports, the Major Port Authorities Act 2021, overseen by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.

Major ports

  • Western coast: Deendayal (Kandla), Mumbai, Jawaharlal Nehru (Nhava Sheva — India's largest container port under public ownership), Mormugao, New Mangalore, Cochin.
  • Eastern coast: Kolkata (Syama Prasad Mookerjee, including Haldia), Paradip, Visakhapatnam, Kamarajar (Ennore), Chennai, V. O. Chidambaranar (Tuticorin), and Port Blair in the Andaman Islands.

Non-major ports and development

State and private ports such as Mundra (Adani) in Gujarat — India's largest commercial port by cargo — Hazira, Pipavav, Krishnapatnam and Gangavaram have grown rapidly and now handle close to half of India's port traffic. The Sagarmala programme promotes port-led development, modernisation and coastal shipping, while new deep-water projects include Vadhavan (Maharashtra) and Galathea Bay (Great Nicobar).
