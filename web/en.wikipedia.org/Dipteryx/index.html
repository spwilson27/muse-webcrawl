Dipteryx

KingdomPlantae
OrderFabales
FamilyFabaceae
GenusDipteryx Schreb.

Dipteryx is a genus of shrubs and trees in the legume family, Fabaceae, native to South and Central America and the Caribbean. The genus includes the tonka bean tree, Dipteryx odorata.

Species and uses

Recognised species include D. alata (baru), D. charapilla, D. micrantha, D. odorata and D. oleifera. Several are valued timber trees: D. oleifera and D. micrantha yield extremely dense, durable hardwood sold as cumaru or Brazilian teak.

Tonka beans

The seeds of D. odorata, tonka beans, contain coumarin and are used in perfumery and as a flavouring, though coumarin content has led to restrictions on their use in food in some countries. The baru nut of D. alata is an edible seed from the Brazilian cerrado.

Conservation

Logging for timber and habitat loss threaten several species; Dipteryx species have been listed in CITES Appendix II to regulate international trade.
