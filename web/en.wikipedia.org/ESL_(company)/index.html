ESL (formerly the Electronic Sports League) is a German esports organizer and production company that produces video game competitions worldwide. It is one of the largest and oldest esports organizations, known for flagship tournament series such as Intel Extreme Masters (IEM) and ESL One.

ESL's Intel Extreme Masters event in Katowice, Poland, is one of the world's biggest esports tournaments.

History

The Electronic Sports League was founded in 2000 in Cologne, Germany, growing out of earlier online gaming leagues. It expanded into large arena events, most notably the Intel Extreme Masters series, launched in 2006, whose Katowice events became showcase tournaments for games such as Counter-Strike and StarCraft II. The Swedish media company Modern Times Group acquired a majority stake in ESL's parent, Turtle Entertainment, in 2015. In 2022 Savvy Gaming Group, backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, acquired ESL and merged it with the FACEIT platform to form the ESL FACEIT Group.

Competitions

ESL operates leagues and tournaments across many titles, including the ESL Pro League and Intel Extreme Masters for Counter-Strike, ESL One events for Dota 2, and national championships in numerous countries. It also runs the DreamHack festival brand following a merger with DreamHack in 2020.
