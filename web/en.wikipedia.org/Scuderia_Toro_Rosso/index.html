Scuderia Toro Rosso

Scuderia Toro Rosso was an Italian Formula One racing team owned by the Austrian drinks company Red Bull, competing from 2006 to 2019. Its name is Italian for "Red Bull Stable/Team" — literally "Team Red Bull". Based in Faenza, Italy, at the factory of its predecessor Minardi, Toro Rosso served as the junior team to Red Bull Racing, developing young drivers from the Red Bull Junior Team programme. From 2020 the team was rebranded Scuderia AlphaTauri, and later became RB Formula One Team.

History

Red Bull bought the struggling Minardi team in late 2005 and renamed it for the 2006 season. Toro Rosso initially ran a restricted V10 engine under a controversial concession, then customer Ferrari V8s from 2007. In the rain at the 2008 Italian Grand Prix, the team's 21-year-old driver Sebastian Vettel took pole position and won the race — Toro Rosso's only Grand Prix victory and, at the time, a win for the youngest driver in Formula One history. Vettel graduated to Red Bull Racing for 2009 and won four world championships there.

Driver development role

The team's purpose within the Red Bull organisation was to test young talent in a competitive environment. Drivers developed at Toro Rosso include Daniel Ricciardo, Max Verstappen (who debuted at 17 in 2015 and was promoted to Red Bull Racing early in 2016), Carlos Sainz Jr., Pierre Gasly and Alex Albon. The Faenza team was correspondingly known for rapid driver turnover, with mid-season swaps a recurring feature.

Later years and rebranding

Toro Rosso switched to Honda power in 2018, paving the way for Red Bull Racing's own Honda partnership. Under team principal Franz Tost, who led the team throughout its existence, further highlights included podium finishes for Daniil Kvyat in Germany and Gasly in Brazil in 2019. The Toro Rosso name disappeared after 2019 as Red Bull promoted its AlphaTauri fashion brand; the Faenza outfit's victory at Monza in 2020, under the AlphaTauri name with Gasly driving, echoed its famous 2008 win.
