Reynolds American, Inc. is an American tobacco company based in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, and a wholly owned subsidiary of British American Tobacco since 2017. Before the acquisition it was the second-largest tobacco company in the United States.

History

Reynolds American was formed in 2004 by the merger of the US operations of Brown & Williamson with R. J. Reynolds Tobacco Company, founded by Richard Joshua Reynolds in 1875. Its operating companies included R.J. Reynolds, maker of Camel, Newport, Pall Mall and Natural American Spirit (via Santa Fe Natural Tobacco), and American Snuff Company. In 2015 it acquired Lorillard, gaining the Newport menthol brand while divesting several brands to Imperial Tobacco to satisfy regulators.

BAT acquisition

In 2017 British American Tobacco, which had held a 42 percent stake since 2004, acquired the remaining 58 percent for about 9 billion, creating the world's largest listed tobacco company at the time.
