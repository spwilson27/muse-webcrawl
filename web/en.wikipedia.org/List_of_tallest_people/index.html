List of tallest people

This is a list of the tallest people in recorded history for whom reliable evidence exists. Extreme height in humans is usually caused by gigantism or acromegaly resulting from a tumour of the pituitary gland, and is often accompanied by serious health problems. The tallest person ever reliably measured was Robert Pershing Wadlow of Alton, Illinois (1918–1940), who reached 2.72 m (8 ft 11.1 in). The tallest living man recognised by Guinness World Records is Sultan Kösen of Turkey, at 2.51 m (8 ft 2.8 in). The tallest woman in recorded history was Zeng Jinlian of China (1964–1982), 2.48 m (8 ft 1.5 in); the tallest living woman is Rumeysa Gelgi of Turkey, about 2.15 m.

Tallest women

Zeng Jinlian (2.48 m) heads the historical list, followed by Anna Haining Bates (2.41 m, 1846–1888, Canada), whose husband Martin van Buren Bates was also exceptionally tall, and Jane Bunford of England (2.41 m, 1895–1922). Yao Defen of China measured 2.33 m before her death in 2012.

Verification

Many historical claims (including various "giants" of 2.7–2.9 m exhibited in circuses) lack reliable measurement and are excluded. Guinness World Records re-verifies living title-holders under standardised conditions; heights can vary during the day and with posture and spinal curvature, so careful measurement matters. Twins Adam and David Rainer — the latter the only person recorded as both a dwarf and a giant — illustrate the complexity of some cases.
