Spectre is a 2015 spy film and the twenty-fourth in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions. Directed by Sam Mendes and starring Daniel Craig in his fourth appearance as Bond, the film pits the agent against the shadowy criminal organisation Spectre and its leader, Franz Oberhauser, played by Christoph Waltz.

Plot

Following an unauthorised mission in Mexico City, Bond follows a trail from Rome to Austria and Morocco, uncovering Spectre's plan to seize control of global surveillance through the "Nine Eyes" programme, while M battles political pressure to shut down the 00 section.

Reception

The film grossed over $880 million worldwide. Its theme song, "Writing's on the Wall" by Sam Smith, won the Academy Award for Best Original Song.
