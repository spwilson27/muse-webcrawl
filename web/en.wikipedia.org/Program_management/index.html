Program management

Program management is the process of managing several related projects, often with the intention of improving an organization's performance. A program is a group of projects managed in a coordinated way to obtain benefits and control not available from managing them individually.

Comparison with project management

While project management focuses on delivering defined outputs within scope, time, and budget, program management is oriented toward strategic outcomes and benefits realisation over a longer horizon. Program managers coordinate dependencies between projects, manage program-level risks and stakeholders, and align the portfolio of work with organisational strategy. Portfolio management, one level higher, concerns selecting and prioritising programs and projects.

Standards

Major frameworks include the Project Management Institute's Standard for Program Management and its Program Management Professional (PgMP) certification, and the UK Cabinet Office's Managing Successful Programmes (MSP).
