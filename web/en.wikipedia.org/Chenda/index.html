Chenda

The chenda is a cylindrical percussion instrument used in the Indian state of Kerala and in coastal Karnataka (where it is also used in Yakshagana and Tulu traditions). It is a key instrument in temple ensembles and art forms such as Kathakali, Theyyam, Panchavadyam and Chenda Melam.

The drum is about two feet long, with a wooden body (often jackwood) and heads made of animal hide laced with leather straps that control tension and pitch. It is hung vertically and played with two sticks, producing a loud, sharp, resonant sound.

Chenda Melam is a large percussion ensemble of chenda drums with cymbals (elathalam), kombu and kuzhal, performed at temple festivals. Famous exponents include Mattannoor Sankarankutty Marar.
