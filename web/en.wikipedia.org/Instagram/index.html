Instagram

Instagram is an American photo and video sharing social networking service owned by Meta Platforms. The app allows users to upload media that can be edited with filters, organized by hashtags, and associated with locations. Posts can be shared publicly or with preapproved followers. Users can browse content by tags and locations, view trending content, like photos, and follow other users.

Instagram, launched in 2010, grew into one of the world's largest social networks.

History

Instagram was created by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger and launched on 6 October 2010 for iOS. It gained two million users in two months and 10 million within a year. In April 2012, shortly after launching on Android, it was acquired by Facebook (now Meta) for approximately US$1 billion in cash and stock. The service reached 1 billion monthly users in 2018 and about 2 billion by 2022.

Features

Beyond photo posts, Instagram added video (2013), Stories — ephemeral posts lasting 24 hours, copied from Snapchat (2016) — direct messaging, shopping, and Reels, a short-form video feature launched in 2020 to compete with TikTok. The main feed, originally chronological, became algorithmically ranked in 2016, a perennial subject of user debate.

Impact and criticism

Instagram reshaped photography, marketing, celebrity culture and the "influencer" economy, and its filters and square format became visual signatures of the 2010s. It has also faced sustained criticism over effects on teenagers' mental health — highlighted by the 2021 Facebook Files disclosures — as well as concerns about misinformation, privacy and its role in elections.
