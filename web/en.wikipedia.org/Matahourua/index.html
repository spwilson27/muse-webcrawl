Matahourua

TypeVoyaging canoe (waka)
CommanderKupe
TraditionMaori oral tradition

The Matahourua (also written Matawhaorua) was the voyaging canoe (waka) of Kupe, the legendary Polynesian navigator credited in Maori oral tradition with the discovery of Aotearoa (New Zealand). According to tradition, Kupe pursued a giant octopus, Te Wheke-o-Muturangi, across the Pacific before reaching New Zealand.

Traditions

Accounts describe Kupe sailing from Hawaiki, naming places along the New Zealand coast, including Aotearoa itself, and later returning to Hawaiki with sailing directions for later migrants. The Matahourua was later refitted and, as some traditions hold, used by Kupe's descendants in the migration to New Zealand.
