Trench knife
Type: Combat knife
Wars: World War I, World War II

A trench knife is a combat knife designed to kill or gravely injure an enemy soldier at close quarters, as might be encountered in a trenchline or other confined area. It was developed in response to the need for a close combat weapon for soldiers conducting assaults and raids on enemy trenchlines during the First World War.

An example was the United States M1917 trench knife, with a triangular stiletto blade and a wooden grip equipped with a knuckle guard. Later designs such as the M1918 combined a knuckle-duster guard with a conventional blade, allowing the weapon to be used for striking as well as stabbing.

History

Early in the war, ordinary infantry were poorly equipped for the hand-to-hand fighting of trench raids. Improvised clubs, sharpened entrenching tools and privately purchased knives gave way to officially issued patterns in France, Germany, Britain and the United States.
