Model figure

A model figure is a scale model representing a person, animal, or fictional character, typically assembled from a kit or purchased pre-painted, and used for display, wargaming, role-playing games, dioramas, or as part of a larger scale model scene. Model figures are a major branch of the scale modelling hobby and range from tiny 6 mm wargaming miniatures to large display statues a metre or more tall.

Materials and production

Figures have traditionally been cast in metal — first lead alloys, later white metal and pewter — or injection-moulded in polystyrene plastic. Resin casting is common for small-run and garage-kit figures, and PVC is used for many pre-painted collectible figures. Modern figures may also be produced by 3D printing, either commercially or by hobbyists from digital files.

Scales

Common figure scales include 1:6 (about 30 cm), 1:12, 1:24, and the railway- and armour-modelling scales 1:35, 1:48, 1:72 and 1:87 (HO scale). Wargaming figures are usually described by height, such as 15 mm, 25/28 mm and 54 mm.

Painting and collecting

Figure painting — priming, base coating, shading, highlighting and basing — is a hobby in its own right, with competitions such as the Golden Demon awards. Pre-painted figures, including Japanese anime figures and designer toys, form a large collectors' market.
