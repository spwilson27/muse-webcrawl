Columbus, Ohio

Columbus is the capital and most populous city of the U.S. state of Ohio. Founded in 1812 at the confluence of the Scioto and Olentangy rivers, it was purpose-built as the state capital and named for Christopher Columbus. The city's population was about 906,000 at the 2020 census, making it the 14th-most populous city in the United States, with a metropolitan area of over two million people.

Economy and institutions

Columbus has a diversified economy based on government, education, insurance, banking, retail and logistics, and is headquarters to companies such as Nationwide Mutual Insurance, Huntington Bancshares and retailer L Brands' successors. It is home to Ohio State University, one of the largest universities in the United States, whose main campus lies just north of downtown.

Geography

The city lies in central Ohio and has grown through extensive annexation to cover about 580 km² across Franklin, Delaware and Fairfield counties. Its flat terrain was formed by glaciers, and the Scioto and Olentangy rivers run through the city.
