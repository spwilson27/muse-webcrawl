Aso Rock

LocationAbuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria
Elevation936 m (3,071 ft)
TypeGranitic monolith (inselberg)

Aso Rock is a large granitic rock outcrop — an inselberg or monolith — on the outskirts of Abuja, the capital city of Nigeria. Rising to a peak of 936 metres (3,071 ft) above sea level, it is one of the city's most conspicuous landmarks. The name comes from the Asokoro ("the people of victory") who historically inhabited the area.

Presidential complex

"Aso Rock" is also used metonymically for the Nigerian seat of government: the Aso Rock Presidential Villa, located at the foot of the rock, contains the residence and offices of the President of Nigeria, the vice-presidential complex, and State House facilities. Nearby landmarks include the National Assembly complex, the Supreme Court, Eagle Square and the Nigerian National Mosque and National Christian Centre.

Geology and surroundings

The rock is part of the basement complex of the Nigerian shield and stands amid the planned districts of the federal capital, which was moved from Lagos to Abuja in 1991. Vegetation around the rock is Guinea savanna, and the area remains largely undeveloped for security and ceremonial reasons.
