Oklahoma! (musical)

Oklahoma! is the first musical written by the duo of Rodgers and Hammerstein. The musical is based on Lynn Riggs' 1931 play, Green Grow the Lilacs and is set in farm country outside the town of Claremore, Indian Territory, in 1906. It tells the story of farm girl Laurey Williams and her courtship by two rival suitors, cowboy Curly McLain and the sinister and frightening farmhand Jud Fry. A secondary romance concerns cowboy Will Parker and his flirtatious fiancée, Ado Annie.

Background and premiere

The original Broadway production opened on March 31, 1943, directed by Rouben Mamoulian and choreographed by Agnes de Mille, whose "dream ballet" integrated dance into the storytelling. The show ran for an unprecedented 2,212 performances and is credited with launching the "golden age" of the American musical by fully integrating songs, book, and dance into a coherent story.

The title song became the state song of Oklahoma in 1953. A 1955 film version starred Gordon MacRae and Shirley Jones. Acclaimed revivals include the 1998 Royal National Theatre production and the darker 2019 Broadway revival directed by Daniel Fish, which won the Tony Award for Best Revival of a Musical.
