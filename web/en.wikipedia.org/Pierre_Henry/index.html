Pierre Henry

Pierre Henry (9 December 1927 – 5 July 2017) was a French composer and a pioneer of musique concrète, music composed from recorded sounds. With Pierre Schaeffer he co-founded the field of electroacoustic music in Paris around 1949–50.

Work

Henry's Symphonie pour un homme seul (1950, with Schaeffer) was a landmark of tape music. His later works include Variations pour une porte et un soupir (1963) and the electronic ballet score Messe pour le temps présent (1967), whose "Psyché Rock" became widely known and was later sampled and adapted, notably as the basis of the Futurama theme. Henry built a famous home studio in Paris where he gave concerts of his works on loudspeaker orchestras.

Legacy

Henry's manipulation of everyday sounds influenced electronic, rock and hip-hop musicians, and he is regarded alongside Schaeffer as a founder of sampling culture.
