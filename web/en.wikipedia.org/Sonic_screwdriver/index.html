Sonic screwdriver

First appearanceFury from the Deep (1968)
Created byVictor Pemberton (script), Sydney Newman (series)
GenreScience fiction
FunctionMultipurpose tool using sound waves

The sonic screwdriver is a fictional multifunctional tool in the British science fiction television programme Doctor Who and its spin-offs, used by the Doctor. Like many of the Doctor's tools and technologies, its function varies by episode and writer, but it commonly opens locks, scans objects and environments, repairs or disrupts machinery, and interfaces with computers.

History in the series

The device first appeared in the 1968 serial Fury from the Deep, where the Second Doctor used it literally as a screwdriver. Its capabilities expanded through the 1970s until producer John Nathan-Turner had it largely written out in the 1982 serial The Visitation, fearing it was a too-convenient plot device. It returned in the 1996 television film and became central to the revived series from 2005, with each Doctor's incarnation using a distinct design. A recurring joke notes that it "doesn't do wood", and it is famously useless against "deadlock seals".

Other sonic devices

The series has also featured a sonic blaster, sonic lipstick (used by Sarah Jane Smith), sonic sunglasses, and a sonic cane. River Song carries a future version of the screwdriver.

Cultural impact

The sonic screwdriver is among the most recognisable props in British television; replicas are popular merchandise, and engineers have experimented with real devices inspired by it that manipulate objects with ultrasound.
