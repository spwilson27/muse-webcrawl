Huascarán

Huascarán is a mountain in the Peruvian province of Yungay, in the Cordillera Blanca range of the Andes. At 6,768 metres above sea level, its snow-capped summit is the highest point in Peru and the fourth-highest mountain in the Western Hemisphere. The mountain has two main summits: Huascarán Sur (south), the higher, and Huascarán Norte (north).

Huascarán National Park

The mountain is protected within Huascarán National Park, established in 1975 and declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1985. The park contains most of the Cordillera Blanca, with hundreds of glaciers and turquoise glacial lakes, and wildlife including spectacled bears and Andean condors.

Disasters

Debris avalanches from Huascarán have caused two of Peru's worst natural disasters. An earthquake in 1962 triggered an ice avalanche that buried Ranrahirca, and the great Ancash earthquake of 31 May 1970 dislodged a mass of ice and rock that buried the town of Yungay, killing tens of thousands across the region.
