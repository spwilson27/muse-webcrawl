Adrian Bowyer

Adrian Bowyer is a British engineer and mathematician best known as the founder of the RepRap project, the open-source initiative to create a self-replicating 3D printer.

RepRap

Working at the University of Bath, Bowyer launched RepRap in 2005 under the GNU General Public License. The project produced designs such as Darwin and Mendel whose printed plastic parts could make much of a copy of the machine itself, driving down the cost of fused filament fabrication printers.

Background

Bowyer's earlier research included computational geometry and the Bowyer-Watson algorithm for Delaunay triangulations.
