Halkomelem

Halkomelem (Halq̓eméylem / Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓ / hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓) is a language of the Central Salish branch of the Salishan language family, traditionally spoken by Indigenous peoples in the Fraser Valley and on southeastern Vancouver Island in British Columbia, Canada, around what are now Vancouver and the lower Fraser River.

Dialects

Three dialects are recognised: Halq̓eméylem, the upriver dialect of the Stó:lō; hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓, the downriver dialect of the Musqueam, Tsawwassen, and neighbouring nations; and Hul̓q̓umín̓um̓, the island dialect spoken around Nanaimo and Chemainus. The language is severely endangered, with few fluent first-language speakers remaining, but it is the focus of active revitalisation, including immersion programmes, university courses, and official recognition of hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓ names in public places around Vancouver.
