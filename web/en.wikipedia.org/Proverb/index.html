Proverb

A proverb is a simple, traditional saying that expresses a perceived truth based on common sense or experience. Proverbs are usually metaphorical, use formulaic language, and are passed down through generations as part of a culture's oral and literary heritage — examples include "a stitch in time saves nine" and "actions speak louder than words."

Characteristics

Proverbs are short, memorable, and often employ parallelism, alliteration, or rhyme. They offer advice, moral instruction, or observations about human nature. Because they distill general wisdom, different cultures often have equivalent proverbs, and some contradict each other ("look before you leap" versus "he who hesitates is lost"), reflecting their situational use.

Collections and study

Famous collections include the biblical Book of Proverbs and the adages gathered by Erasmus. The scholarly study of proverbs is called paremiology. Pieter Bruegel the Elder's 1559 painting Netherlandish Proverbs famously illustrates over a hundred Flemish sayings in a single scene.
