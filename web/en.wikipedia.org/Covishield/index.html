Covishield

Covishield is the brand name used in India for the Oxford–AstraZeneca COVID-19 vaccine (AZD1222), manufactured under licence by the Serum Institute of India. It is a viral-vector vaccine using a modified chimpanzee adenovirus (ChAdOx1) encoding the spike protein of SARS-CoV-2.

Covishield was granted emergency use authorisation in India on 3 January 2021, alongside Covaxin, and formed the backbone of India's vaccination campaign, which began on 16 January 2021; it accounted for the large majority of doses administered in the country. The vaccine was also supplied internationally, including through the COVAX facility, to countries in Asia and Africa.

History

The underlying vaccine was developed by the University of Oxford's Jenner Institute with AstraZeneca. The Serum Institute, the world's largest vaccine manufacturer by volume, produced Covishield at its Pune facilities. During the Delta-variant wave in spring 2021 India suspended vaccine exports to meet domestic demand, which significantly affected COVAX supply schedules worldwide.
