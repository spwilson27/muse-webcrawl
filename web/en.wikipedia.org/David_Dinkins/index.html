David Dinkins

David Norman Dinkins (10 July 1927 – 23 November 2020) was an American politician who served as the 106th mayor of New York City from 1990 to 1993, the first African American to hold the office. He previously served as Manhattan borough president.

Mayoralty

Dinkins defeated Rudolph Giuliani in 1989, describing New York as a "gorgeous mosaic". His administration expanded police numbers under the Safe Streets, Safe City programme, after which crime began its long decline; cleaned up Times Square; and kept libraries open despite a fiscal crisis. He lost re-election to Giuliani in 1993 and later taught at Columbia University.
