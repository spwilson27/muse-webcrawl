Blackwater (waste)

Blackwater
Type: Wastewater
Source: Toilets
Contrast: Greywater

Blackwater in sanitation is wastewater from toilets, which carries faeces, urine and flushwater, as distinct from greywater from baths, sinks and washing machines. Because it may contain pathogens, blackwater requires treatment before discharge or reuse.

Treatment

Conventional treatment uses sewers and wastewater plants. On-site options include septic tanks and composting toilets, which convert blackwater into compost with minimal water use.

Health and environment

Untreated blackwater is a major vector of waterborne disease. Modern sanitation separates, conveys and treats it to protect drinking water sources.
