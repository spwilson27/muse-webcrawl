Air Force Global Strike Command

Air Force Global Strike Command (AFGSC)
Founded: 7 August 2009
Country: United States
Branch: United States Air Force
Headquarters: Barksdale AFB, Louisiana
Role: Nuclear deterrence and global strike

The Air Force Global Strike Command (AFGSC) is a major command of the United States Air Force, headquartered at Barksdale Air Force Base, Louisiana. AFGSC is responsible for the nation's three intercontinental ballistic missile wings and the Air Force's entire bomber force.

Mission

The command provides combat-ready forces for nuclear deterrence and global strike operations in support of U.S. Strategic Command. Its forces include the Minuteman III ICBM fleet and the B-1B Lancer, B-2 Spirit, and B-52H Stratofortress bomber fleets, organised principally under Eighth Air Force (bombers) and Twentieth Air Force (ICBMs).

History

AFGSC was activated on 7 August 2009 following a decision to consolidate oversight of Air Force nuclear forces, prompted by incidents in 2007–2008 that exposed lapses in nuclear weapons handling. It assumed control of ICBM forces in December 2009 and of bomber forces in February 2010. The command is also developing the LGM-35 Sentinel ICBM and the B-21 Raider bomber as successors to its current systems.
