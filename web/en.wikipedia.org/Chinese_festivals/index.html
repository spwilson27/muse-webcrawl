Chinese festivals

Traditional Chinese festivals
Calendar: Chinese lunisolar calendar
Major festivals: Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn, Dragon Boat, Qingming

Chinese festivals are traditional holidays celebrated in China and by Chinese communities worldwide, most dated by the Chinese lunisolar calendar. They combine seasonal, agricultural, ancestral and mythological themes and are occasions for family reunion.

Major festivals

The Spring Festival (Chinese New Year) is the most important, marked by family dinners, red decorations and hongbao. The Lantern Festival closes the New Year period. Qingming is devoted to tomb sweeping and ancestor remembrance.

Other festivals

The Dragon Boat Festival features zongzi and boat races, traditionally linked to the poet Qu Yuan. The Mid-Autumn Festival celebrates the harvest and full moon with mooncakes. The Double Ninth Festival honours the elderly, and Qixi is a lovers' festival.
