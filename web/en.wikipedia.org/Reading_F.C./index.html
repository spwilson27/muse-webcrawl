Reading Football Club is a professional football club based in Reading, Berkshire, England. Founded in 1871, it is one of the oldest teams in England, though it did not join the Football League until 1920. The club is nicknamed the Royals after the Royal County of Berkshire, and plays its home matches at the Madejski Stadium, which opened in 1998.

History

Reading spent most of its history in the lower divisions. Under manager Steve Coppell, the club won the Football League Championship in 2005–06 with a record 106 points, earning its first promotion to the Premier League, where it finished eighth in 2006–07. Relegated the following season, Reading returned to the top flight as Championship champions in 2011–12 but went straight back down. Financial difficulties in the 2020s under owner Dai Yongge brought points deductions and relegation to League One in 2023, amid sustained supporter protests.

Records

The club's 106-point season remains an English professional record for a league campaign. Reading's record attendance was 33,042 at Elm Park, its home from 1896 to 1998.
