The Stade Sébastien Charléty, commonly known as Stade Charléty, is a multi-purpose stadium in the 13th arrondissement of Paris, France. Owned by the City of Paris, it is home to the football club Paris FC and hosts athletics meetings and other events.

History

The original stadium was inaugurated in 1939 on land near the Cité Internationale Universitaire. It was largely demolished and rebuilt to designs by architect Henri Gaudin, reopening in 1994 with a distinctive undulating roofline. The stadium is named after Sébastien Charléty, a rector of the University of Paris.

Uses

Charléty has hosted the Paris athletics meeting (part of the Diamond League in some years), French cup finals in various sports, rugby matches, and was a venue of the 2019 FIFA Women's World Cup training infrastructure; Paris Saint-Germain's women's team played there for several seasons, and the Stade Français rugby club used it as a temporary home. During the 2024 Paris Olympic Games period it served as a base for the French team.

Location

The stadium sits along the Boulevard Kellermann at the southern edge of Paris, served by the T3a tram line and nearby RER B stations.
