Vada pav

Vada pav
Place of originMumbai, Maharashtra, India
Main ingredientsBatata vada, pav (bread roll), chutneys

Vada pav, sometimes spelled wada pav, is a vegetarian fast food dish native to Mumbai, India. It consists of a deep-fried spiced potato dumpling (batata vada) placed inside a bread roll (pav), served with chutneys and a green chilli.

History

The dish is widely credited to Ashok Vaidya, who began selling vada pav outside Dadar railway station in the 1960s. Cheap and filling, it became an iconic street food of Mumbai and a symbol of the city's working-class culture.

Preparation

Mashed potatoes are seasoned with mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric, garlic, and chillies, coated in chickpea-flour batter, and deep fried. The vada is served in a pav with dry garlic chutney and often tamarind or green chutney. Variants include cheese, schezwan, and grilled versions.
