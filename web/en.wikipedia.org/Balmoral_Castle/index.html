Balmoral Castle

Location: Aberdeenshire, Scotland
Completed: 1856
Style: Scots Baronial
Owner: The monarch (private)

Balmoral Castle is a large estate house in Aberdeenshire, Scotland, and a residence of the British royal family. It is near the village of Crathie, 9 miles (14 km) west of Ballater and 50 miles (80 km) west of Aberdeen, on the banks of the River Dee within the Cairngorms National Park.

History

The estate was purchased for Queen Victoria in 1852 by her husband Prince Albert, and the present castle was completed in 1856, designed by architect William Smith of Aberdeen in the Scots Baronial style, with contributions by Albert himself. Unusually for a royal residence, Balmoral is private property of the monarch, not part of the Crown Estate. The estate has grown to about 50,000 acres (20,000 ha) of grouse moors, forestry, and farmland.

Royal residence

Balmoral has been the summer holiday residence of the sovereign since the reign of Victoria. Queen Elizabeth II spent much of each summer there; she died at Balmoral on 8 September 2022, the first monarch to die in Scotland since the 16th century. Members of the royal family traditionally attend church at nearby Crathie Kirk during their stay. Parts of the grounds and the castle ballroom are open to the public seasonally.
