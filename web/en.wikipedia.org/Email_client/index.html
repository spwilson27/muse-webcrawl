Email client

An email client, email reader or mail user agent (MUA) is a computer program used to access and manage a user's email. These applications allow users to compose, send, receive and organize messages, usually connecting to mail servers using protocols such as SMTP for sending and IMAP or POP3 for receiving.

Types

Email clients may be desktop applications, such as Microsoft Outlook, Mozilla Thunderbird or Apple Mail; mobile apps; or web-based interfaces (webmail) such as Gmail and Outlook.com, which are accessed through a browser. Many clients also integrate calendars, contacts, tasks, spam filtering, message search, rules and encryption support.
