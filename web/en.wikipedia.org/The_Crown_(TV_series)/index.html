The Crown (TV series)

The Crown
Genre: Historical drama
Created by Peter Morgan
Starring Claire Foy, Matt Smith, Olivia Colman, Tobias Menzies, Imelda Staunton, Jonathan Pryce
Country: United Kingdom / United States
Original network: Netflix
Original release: 4 November 2016 – 14 December 2023
Seasons: 6; Episodes: 60

The Crown is a historical drama television series about the reign of Queen Elizabeth II, created and principally written by Peter Morgan and produced by Left Bank Pictures and Sony Pictures Television for Netflix. Morgan developed it from his drama film The Queen (2006) and his stage play The Audience (2013). The series ran for six seasons of ten episodes each, from 2016 to 2023, with the principal cast replaced roughly every two seasons to reflect the passage of time.

Overview

The first season covers the period from Elizabeth's marriage to Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, in 1947 to the disintegration of her sister Princess Margaret's engagement in 1955, with Claire Foy as the Queen. Olivia Colman took the role for the third and fourth seasons, covering 1964 to 1990, including Margaret Thatcher's premiership and the marriage of Charles and Diana. Imelda Staunton played Elizabeth in the final two seasons, which depict the 1990s — the "annus horribilis", the death of Diana, Princess of Wales — and end in 2005 with the wedding of Charles and Camilla. The sixth season was released in two parts in November and December 2023.

Production

The series was among the most expensive ever made for streaming, noted for its lavish production design, location filming across the United Kingdom and internationally, and extensive historical research, though Morgan freely dramatized private conversations. Each season was written largely by Morgan with a small writing team.

Reception

The Crown received widespread critical acclaim, particularly for its acting, direction, and production values, winning numerous awards including multiple Primetime Emmy Awards; its fourth season swept all seven drama categories at the 2021 Emmys. It also drew criticism and calls for a fiction disclaimer over its depiction of living or recently deceased royals, particularly in the later seasons covering Charles and Diana's marriage.
