Beijing National Aquatics Center

The Beijing National Aquatics Center, officially the National Aquatics Centre and nicknamed the Water Cube, is a swimming centre at the Olympic Green in Beijing, built for the 2008 Summer Olympics.

Design

Designed by PTW Architects with Arup, its steel space frame is clad in ETFE cushions resembling soap bubbles and water molecules. Michael Phelps won eight gold medals there in 2008.

Later use

After the Games it became a water park and multi-purpose venue. For the 2022 Winter Olympics it was converted for curling and nicknamed the Ice Cube.
