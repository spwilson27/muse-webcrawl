Satya Narayan Goenka (1924–2013), commonly known as S. N. Goenka, was an Indian teacher of Vipassanā meditation. Born into an Indian business family in Burma (Myanmar), he learned Vipassanā from the Burmese teacher Sayagyi U Ba Khin and became the most influential figure in spreading the technique worldwide.

Life

Goenka was a successful businessman suffering from severe migraines when he met U Ba Khin in 1955. He trained under him for fourteen years and was authorised to teach. In 1969 he moved to India and began conducting ten-day meditation retreats, first for relatives and later for the public.

Teaching

Goenka's courses are ten-day residential retreats taught free of charge, funded by donations from past students. Participants observe noble silence, meditate for about ten hours a day, and are guided through recorded instructions: first developing concentration by observing the breath (ānāpāna), then practising Vipassanā, the observation of bodily sensations with equanimity, and finally loving-kindness (mettā). He emphasised that the technique is non-sectarian and universal. His organisation grew to nearly two hundred meditation centres worldwide, and he addressed the Millennium World Peace Summit at the United Nations in 2000.
