Club de Fútbol Pachuca is a Mexican professional football team based in Pachuca, Hidalgo. Founded in 1892 by Cornish miners, it is the oldest football club in Mexico and one of the founding members of the Mexican Primera División.

History

British miners from Cornwall brought football to Pachuca in the late 19th century. After decades of modest results, the club enjoyed its golden era from the late 1990s, winning multiple Liga MX championships and becoming the most successful Mexican club internationally of its time.

Honours

Pachuca has won the CONCACAF Champions Cup/League several times — including titles in 2002, 2007, 2008, 2010, 2017 and 2024 — and in 2006 became the first Mexican club to win a South American tournament, the Copa Sudamericana. The club is renowned for its youth academy, which has produced many Mexican internationals, and for the Universidad del Fútbol, a football university.
