Kilowatt-hour

Unit ofEnergy
SymbolkW⋅h or kWh
SI equivalent3.6 MJ (megajoules)

The kilowatt-hour (symbolised kW⋅h or kWh) is a unit of energy equal to one kilowatt of power sustained for one hour. It equals 3.6 megajoules in SI units. The kilowatt-hour is the standard unit used for measuring and billing electrical energy delivered to consumers by electric utilities.

Usage

A heater rated at 1,000 watts (1 kilowatt) running for one hour uses one kilowatt-hour of energy. Electricity bills are typically charged per kilowatt-hour consumed. Related units include the megawatt-hour (MWh), gigawatt-hour (GWh), and terawatt-hour (TWh) used for generation-scale energy. Although not an SI unit, the kilowatt-hour is widely accepted for use with the SI system because of its practical convenience in the energy sector.
