Tommy (The Who album)

ArtistThe Who
Released17 May 1969
GenreRock opera, hard rock
LabelTrack / Decca

Tommy is the fourth studio album by the English rock band the Who, released in 1969. A double album largely composed by guitarist Pete Townshend, it tells the story of Tommy Walker, a "deaf, dumb and blind kid" who becomes a pinball champion and a messianic figure. It is generally regarded as the first rock opera and a landmark of rock music.

Story and songs

The narrative unfolds through songs including "Overture", "Amazing Journey", "Christmas", "Cousin Kevin", "Acid Queen", "Pinball Wizard", "Go to the Mirror!" and "We're Not Gonna Take It", which ends with the anthemic "See Me, Feel Me". "Pinball Wizard" became one of the band's signature singles.

Legacy

Tommy was adapted into a 1975 film directed by Ken Russell and a successful Broadway musical in 1993. The album has been inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame and appears on many lists of the greatest albums of all time; it helped establish the Who as one of the defining bands of the era.
