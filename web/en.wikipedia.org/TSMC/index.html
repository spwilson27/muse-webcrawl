Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC) is a Taiwanese semiconductor contract manufacturing and design company, the world's largest dedicated independent ("pure-play") semiconductor foundry and one of the world's most valuable semiconductor companies. It manufactures chips designed by customers such as Apple, AMD, Nvidia, Qualcomm, and Broadcom.

History

TSMC was founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, with backing from the Taiwanese government and Philips, pioneering the pure-play foundry model in which the company fabricates only other firms' designs and never sells products under its own brand. The model enabled the rise of "fabless" chip designers and reshaped the global electronics industry.

Technology and scale

TSMC has repeatedly led the industry in introducing leading-edge process nodes, including volume production at 7 nm, 5 nm, and 3 nm, using extreme ultraviolet lithography. Its fabs are concentrated in Taiwan, with additional plants in China, the United States (Arizona), Japan, and Germany under construction or in production. Because a large share of the world's most advanced logic chips is made in its fabs, TSMC is regarded as strategically vital to the global technology supply chain.

Business

The company earns revenue from wafer fabrication and related services across hundreds of products for computing, smartphones, automotive, and Internet-of-things applications. By market capitalization it has ranked among the ten most valuable companies in the world.
