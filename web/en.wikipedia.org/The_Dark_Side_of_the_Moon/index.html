The Dark Side of the Moon is the eighth studio album by the English rock band Pink Floyd, released in March 1973. A concept album about the pressures of modern life — conflict, greed, time, death, and insanity — it is one of the best-selling albums of all time, with estimated sales of over 45 million copies.

Music

The album's tracks flow together as a continuous piece, bookended by a heartbeat. It includes the singles "Money" and "Us and Them", the instrumental "On the Run", the wordless vocal tour de force "The Great Gig in the Sky" featuring Clare Torry, and incorporates tape loops, synthesisers, and spoken-word fragments recorded from interviews with studio staff and band members.

Artwork and legacy

The iconic cover, designed by Storm Thorgerson of Hipgnosis with George Hardie, shows a beam of white light refracted through a prism into a spectrum. The album spent a record-breaking total of over 900 weeks on the Billboard 200 chart in the United States.
