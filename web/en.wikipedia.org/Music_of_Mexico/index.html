Music of Mexico

The music of Mexico is very diverse and features a wide range of musical genres and performance styles. It has been influenced by a variety of cultures, most notably Indigenous peoples of Mexico and European, especially Spanish, traditions.

Traditional genres

The best-known style is mariachi, originating in Jalisco, performed by ensembles of violins, trumpets, guitars, vihuela and guitarrón, with singers in embroidered charro suits; it was inscribed on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2011. Other regional genres include ranchera, son jarocho from Veracruz (famous for "La Bamba"), banda from Sinaloa, norteño from the northern border, corridos (narrative ballads) and the bolero.

Modern music

Mexico has a major Spanish-language popular music industry spanning rock en español, pop, cumbia and regional Mexican styles that top charts across Latin America and the United States. Classical composers such as Carlos Chávez and Silvestre Revueltas drew on Indigenous themes.
