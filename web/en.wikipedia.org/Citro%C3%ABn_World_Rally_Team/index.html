The Citroën World Rally Team was the official World Rally Championship team of the French manufacturer Citroën, run by its motorsport division Citroën Racing (formerly Automobiles Citroën's competition department and Citroën Sport). It was one of the most successful teams in WRC history, winning eight consecutive manufacturers' championships between 2003 and 2012 (interrupted only in 2006–2007, when it competed semi-privately through the Kronos team while developing a new car).

History

Citroën had a rallying heritage dating back to the 1950s and 1960s with the DS, and returned to the fore in the 1990s with the ZX Rallye Raid in cross-country rallying and the Xsara Kit Car, which famously won two WRC events outright in 1999. The team entered the WRC full-time with the Xsara WRC in 2001–2002. From 2003, with Sébastien Loeb as lead driver, Citroën dominated: Loeb won nine consecutive drivers' championships (2004–2012) driving the Xsara WRC, C4 WRC, and DS3 WRC. Citroën withdrew from full-time WRC competition at the end of the 2019 season after Sébastien Ogier's return for that year.

Cars

The team's principal World Rally Cars were the Xsara WRC (2001–2006), C4 WRC (2007–2010), DS3 WRC (2011–2016), and C3 WRC (2017–2019).
