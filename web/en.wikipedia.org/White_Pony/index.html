White Pony is the third studio album by the American alternative metal band Deftones, released on 20 June 2000. Widely regarded as the band's most experimental and accomplished work, it incorporated elements of shoegaze, trip hop, and post-rock into the band's heavy sound.

Background and recording

The album was produced by Terry Date and recorded in California and Vancouver. Singer Chino Moreno took up guitar on the record, and turntablist Frank Delgado became a full member, expanding the band's palette with atmospheric textures. Maynard James Keenan of Tool co-wrote and sang on the track "Passenger".

Reception

The singles "Change (In the House of Flies)" and "Back to School (Mini Maggit)" received heavy airplay; "Elite" won the Grammy Award for Best Metal Performance in 2001. The album debuted at number three on the Billboard 200 and was later certified platinum in the United States. It frequently appears on lists of the greatest metal albums of all time.

Legacy

In 2020 the band released Black Stallion, a remix companion album, for the 20th anniversary of White Pony, featuring remixes by artists including Robert Smith, Mike Shinoda, and Squarepusher.
