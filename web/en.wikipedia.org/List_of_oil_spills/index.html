List of oil spills

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of oil spills records major accidental releases of crude oil and petroleum products into the environment, especially marine oil spills from tankers, offshore platforms, and wells. Volumes are commonly reported in tonnes or barrels; estimates for the largest spills vary considerably.

Notable land spills

The Lakeview Gusher in California (1910–1911) was among the largest accidental oil releases in history, flowing uncontrolled for about 18 months with an estimated 9 million barrels spilled, much of it recovered or burned off. Pipeline ruptures and storage failures have caused many further large spills on land.

Regulation

Major spills drove regulatory change: after the Exxon Valdez spill, the United States passed the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 and required double hulls on tankers; international rules under MARPOL were likewise tightened. Tanker spill volumes have generally declined since the 1970s despite growing oil trade.
