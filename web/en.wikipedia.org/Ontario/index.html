Ontario

Ontario is the southernmost province of Canada. Located in Central Canada, it is the country's most populous province, home to over 16 million people, about 38–39 percent of Canada's population. Its capital is Toronto, Canada's largest city, while Ottawa, Canada's capital, is also in Ontario.

Ontario is bordered by Manitoba to the west, Hudson Bay and James Bay to the north, Quebec to the east, and the U.S. states of Minnesota, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania and New York to the south, largely across the Great Lakes and the St. Lawrence River. With an area of about 1,076,395 km², it is Canada's fourth-largest province.

History and economy

Indigenous peoples inhabited the region for thousands of years before French and British colonisation. Ontario was formed in 1867 as one of the four founding provinces of Confederation, from the former Province of Canada's Canada West. Its economy is Canada's largest, driven by finance in Toronto, manufacturing, technology, mining in the Canadian Shield, agriculture in the south, and hydroelectric power.

Geography

Geography ranges from the densely settled Golden Horseshoe around western Lake Ontario, to farmland, the boreal forests and lowlands of the north, and some 250,000 lakes including the Great Lakes — Superior, Huron, Erie and Ontario — containing a fifth of the world's fresh surface water.
