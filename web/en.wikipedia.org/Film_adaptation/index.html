Film adaptation

A film adaptation is the transfer of a work — most commonly a novel, play, short story, comic or video game — into a feature film. Adaptation involves reinterpreting narrative, character and theme for a visual, time-limited medium.

Adaptations dominate commercial cinema; studios value pre-existing audiences and intellectual property. Fidelity varies from close transposition to loose inspiration, and adaptations are a regular category at major awards, including the Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay.
