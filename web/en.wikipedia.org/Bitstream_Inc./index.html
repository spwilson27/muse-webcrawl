Bitstream Inc.

Bitstream Inc. was an American type foundry and software company headquartered in Marlborough, Massachusetts. Founded in 1981 by Matthew Carter, Mike Parker and others, it was the first independent digital type foundry. Bitstream developed and licensed fonts and font-rendering technology for desktop publishing, embedded systems and mobile devices.

History

Bitstream built a large library of typefaces, including original designs and licensed versions of classic faces, and pioneered retail font sales with products such as the Bitstream Font Navigator. Its Font Fusion rendering engine was licensed for use in mobile phones, set-top boxes and printers.

Acquisition

In 2012 Bitstream's font business was acquired by Monotype Imaging for approximately $50 million. The remaining company, which held the Pageflex variable-data publishing business, renamed itself Marlborough Software Development Holdings.

Products

Well-known Bitstream typefaces and technologies include Charter (designed by Carter), the Vera font family released under an open license, Thunderbird, and the BOLT mobile browser technology.
