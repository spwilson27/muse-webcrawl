Bloomberg L.P.

Founded: October 1, 1981
Founder: Michael Bloomberg
Headquarters: New York City, US
Industry: Financial software, mass media

Bloomberg L.P. is a privately held financial, software, data, and media company headquartered in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. It was founded in 1981 by Michael Bloomberg, Thomas Secunda, Duncan MacMillan, and Charles Zegar, initially as Innovative Market Systems, a provider of financial data terminals to Wall Street firms.

Products and services

The company's core product is the Bloomberg Terminal, a subscription computer system that gives finance professionals real-time market data, news, analytics, and trade execution. Terminal subscriptions account for the majority of the company's revenue. Bloomberg also operates the Bloomberg News wire service, Bloomberg Television and Radio, and publishes Bloomberg Businessweek and Bloomberg Markets magazines.

History and ownership

The firm launched its first terminal installations at Merrill Lynch in the early 1980s and grew rapidly as electronic data became central to financial markets. Bloomberg bought back Merrill Lynch's stake in 2008. Michael Bloomberg retains a controlling majority interest in the company, which employs tens of thousands of people in offices around the world.
