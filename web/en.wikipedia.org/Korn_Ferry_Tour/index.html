The Korn Ferry Tour is the developmental tour for the U.S.-based PGA Tour. It features professional golfers who have not yet reached the PGA Tour, or who have done so but failed to win enough FedEx Cup points to retain their cards, and it is the primary route by which players earn promotion to the main tour.

History

The tour was launched in 1990 as the Ben Hogan Tour, sponsored by the Ben Hogan golf equipment company. It subsequently carried the names Nike Tour, Buy.com Tour, Nationwide Tour and Web.com Tour before Korn Ferry, a global organisational consulting firm, became title sponsor in June 2019.

Format and promotion

The season consists of roughly two dozen tournaments, mainly in the United States with events in Latin America and elsewhere. At the end of the regular season, the top players on the points list earn PGA Tour cards directly; further cards are awarded through the Korn Ferry Tour Finals, a series of season-ending events. Notable graduates who went on to win major championships include Zach Johnson, Bubba Watson, Jason Dufner and Scottie Scheffler, who was the tour's Player of the Year in 2019.

Korn Ferry Tour Finals

The Finals, introduced in 2013, conclude the season with events such as the Nationwide Children's Hospital Championship, the Albertsons Boise Open and the Korn Ferry Tour Championship.
