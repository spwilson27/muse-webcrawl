Lazarus Group

The Lazarus Group (also tracked as Hidden Cobra, APT38 and other aliases) is a cybercrime and espionage group attributed by the United States and allied governments to North Korea's Reconnaissance General Bureau, active since at least 2009.

Notable operations

Attributed campaigns include the 2014 destructive attack on Sony Pictures Entertainment, linked to the film The Interview; the 2016 theft of US$81 million from Bangladesh Bank via the SWIFT network; the 2017 WannaCry ransomware worm; and extensive cryptocurrency exchange thefts used, investigators say, to fund the regime. Members have been indicted in the US, and the group is under international sanctions.
