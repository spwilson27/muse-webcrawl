OAI-PMH

Full nameOpen Archives Initiative Protocol for Metadata Harvesting
Version2.0 (2002)
TransportHTTP, XML responses
Baseline formatDublin Core

The Open Archives Initiative Protocol for Metadata Harvesting (OAI-PMH) is a protocol for harvesting metadata descriptions of records in digital repositories. Developed by the Open Archives Initiative to support interoperability among scholarly archives and digital libraries, it allows service providers to collect metadata from many data providers into searchable central indexes.

Design

The protocol defines six requests ("verbs") — including Identify, ListRecords, ListIdentifiers and GetRecord — issued over HTTP with XML responses. Repositories may group records into sets, support selective harvesting by date range, and must be able to supply unqualified Dublin Core metadata as a common baseline format.

Adoption

OAI-PMH became a standard mechanism for institutional repositories, journal platforms and aggregators such as BASE and CORE to expose and collect open-access metadata.
