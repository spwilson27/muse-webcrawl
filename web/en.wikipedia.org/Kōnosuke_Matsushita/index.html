Kōnosuke Matsushita (1894–1989) was a Japanese industrialist who founded Matsushita Electric Industrial Co., now Panasonic, in 1918. Starting from a small workshop in Osaka selling an improved electric light socket of his own design, he built the company into one of the world's largest electronics manufacturers.

Philosophy

Matsushita is revered in Japan as a "god of management". His philosophy emphasised service to society, employee welfare, and the elimination of poverty through mass production — famously predicting that electrical goods would become as cheap as tap water. In 1980 he founded the Matsushita Institute of Government and Management to train future political leaders.
