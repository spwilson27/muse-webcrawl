Depositional environment

A depositional environment or sedimentary environment describes the combination of physical, chemical and biological processes associated with the deposition of a particular type of sediment, and hence the rock types formed.

Types

Environments are grouped as terrestrial (fluvial, aeolian, glacial, lacustrine), transitional (deltaic, beach, tidal) and marine (shallow shelf, reef, deep-sea fan). Each leaves characteristic sedimentary structures and facies.

Importance

Recognising depositional environments in ancient rocks lets geologists reconstruct past geography and predict the distribution of reservoirs, aquifers and fossil deposits.
