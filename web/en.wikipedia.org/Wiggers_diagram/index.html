Wiggers diagram

A Wiggers diagram is a standard chart in cardiovascular physiology that aligns events of the cardiac cycle on a common time axis. Named after physiologist Carl J. Wiggers, it plots the electrocardiogram alongside aortic, ventricular and atrial pressures, ventricular volume, heart sounds and valve events.

Reading the diagram

By comparing the traces, students can see when the mitral and aortic valves open and close, why pressure rises during isovolumetric contraction, and how ejection and filling change ventricular volume. The jugular venous pulse may also be shown.

Use

The diagram is widely used in medical and physiology teaching because it integrates electrical and mechanical events that are otherwise taught separately. Variants exist for the right side of the heart and for disease states.
