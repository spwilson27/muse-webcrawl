List of the busiest airports in the Nordic countries

This is a list of the busiest airports in the Nordic countries — Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden — ranked by total passenger traffic. Copenhagen Airport is by a wide margin the largest airport in the region and serves as the principal hub for Scandinavian Airlines. Stockholm Arlanda and Oslo Gardermoen follow, with Helsinki-Vantaa and Keflavík International completing the group of major international gateways.

Regional patterns

Norway's extensive domestic network, driven by its fjords and mountainous terrain, supports a large number of smaller airports with scheduled services. Sweden and Finland likewise maintain regional airports for remote communities in the north. Passenger numbers across the region collapsed in 2020–2021 during the COVID-19 pandemic and recovered substantially in the following years.
