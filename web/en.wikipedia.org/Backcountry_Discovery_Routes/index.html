Backcountry Discovery Routes

Backcountry Discovery Routes (BDR) is a nonprofit organization based in the United States that creates and publishes off-highway routes for adventure and dual-sport motorcycle travel. Founded in 2010, the organization develops state-based routes that cross public lands primarily on unpaved roads, and provides free GPS tracks, maps, and trip-planning resources.

Routes

Beginning with the Washington Backcountry Discovery Route (WABDR), the organization has published a network of routes in states including Idaho, Utah, Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico, Oregon, California, Nevada, and Wyoming, together with regional routes in the Mid-Atlantic, the Northeast, and the Southeast. Each route is typically several hundred miles long, is scouted and documented by the BDR team, and is accompanied by an expedition documentary film and a printed map produced with Butler Motorcycle Maps.

Mission

BDR states that its goals include promoting responsible use of public lands, supporting rural communities along its routes through motorcycle tourism, and advocating for the preservation of OHV access. The organisation also participates in rider education and trail-stewardship initiatives.
