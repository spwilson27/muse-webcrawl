List of dams in India

India has more than 5,000 large dams, ranking third in the world after China and the United States. Dams in India serve irrigation, hydroelectric power generation, flood control, and drinking water supply. The Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi River is the tallest dam in India.

History

Ancient India had notable hydraulic works, such as the Grand Anicut (Kallanai) on the Kaveri River, built in the Chola era and still in use. Modern dam building accelerated after independence in 1947, when Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru described dams as the "temples of modern India".
