Chelsea Market

Chelsea Market is a food hall, shopping mall and office building in the Chelsea neighborhood of Manhattan, New York City. It occupies the former Nabisco factory complex between 9th and 10th Avenues and 15th and 16th Streets, where the Oreo cookie was invented and first produced.

The complex was redeveloped in the 1990s, preserving its industrial character. It houses dozens of food vendors, restaurants and shops, and offices — Google, which bought the building in 2018 for $2.4 billion, is a major tenant, as were YouTube and the Food Network.

The market sits alongside the High Line elevated park and attracts millions of visitors annually.
