Professional Bull Riders, Inc. (PBR) is an international professional bull riding organization based in Pueblo, Colorado, United States. It is the largest bull riding league in the world, sanctioning hundreds of events each year in the United States, Canada, Brazil, Australia, and Mexico.

History

The PBR was founded in 1992 by 20 bull riders, including Ty Murray and Tuff Hedeman, who broke away from the Professional Rodeo Cowboys Association to create a stand-alone bull riding tour. Each founder contributed $1,000. The organization grew rapidly; its premier series is now the Unleash the Beast tour, culminating in the PBR World Finals, where the world champion is crowned based on season points. In 2015, PBR was acquired by Endeavor (then WME-IMG).

Competition

Riders attempt to stay aboard a bucking bull for eight seconds, holding a braided rope with one hand. Scores out of 100 are split evenly between the rider's performance and the bull's difficulty. PBR also runs the Pendleton Whisky Velocity Tour and the team-based PBR Team Series, launched in 2022.
