AIR Studios

Founded1965 (company); Lyndhurst Hall opened 1992
FounderGeorge Martin and partners
LocationHampstead, London, England
IndustryRecording studios

AIR Studios (Associated Independent Recording) is a group of recording studio facilities founded in London in 1965 by producer George Martin and his partners after they left EMI. Its main facility since 1992 has been Lyndhurst Hall, a converted church in Hampstead.

History

AIR's first studios opened on Oxford Street in 1970; a second facility, AIR Montserrat, operated in the Caribbean from 1979 until Hurricane Hugo forced its closure in 1989. Artists who recorded at AIR include the Beatles' members as solo artists, Pink Floyd, and many film composers.

Lyndhurst Hall

The main hall is one of the largest orchestral recording rooms in the world and is heavily used for film and television scores and classical recordings.
