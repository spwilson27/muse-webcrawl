Cosmetics

Cosmetics, commonly known as make-up, are substances or products used to enhance or alter the appearance of the face or body. They include foundation, lipstick, mascara, eyeshadow, blush, and many other products, and have been used for thousands of years.

History

The use of cosmetics dates back to ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia, where kohl was used to line the eyes and pigments coloured the skin. Cosmetics were also important in ancient Greece, Rome, China, and Japan. In the 20th century, the modern cosmetics industry developed alongside film and photography, and make-up became a mass-market consumer product.

Types

Make-up products are generally categorised by the area of application: face products such as primer, foundation, concealer and powder; eye products such as mascara, eyeliner and eyeshadow; lip products such as lipstick and gloss; and products for cheeks and nails.

Regulation and safety

Cosmetics are regulated in many jurisdictions, such as by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the European Union's Cosmetics Regulation, which set rules on ingredients, labelling, and safety testing.
