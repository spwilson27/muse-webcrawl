Sebastian Stan

Sebastian Stan (born 13 August 1982) is a Romanian-American actor. Born in Constanța, Romania, he emigrated to the United States as a child and studied acting at Rutgers University. He gained worldwide recognition for playing Bucky Barnes / the Winter Soldier in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with Captain America: The First Avenger (2011) and continuing through films including Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014) and the Disney+ series The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021).

Career

Early roles included Carter Baizen in the television series Gossip Girl and the Mad Hatter in Once Upon a Time. He earned critical praise for playing Jeff Gillooly in I, Tonya (2017) and Tommy Lee in the miniseries Pam & Tommy (2022), which brought an Emmy nomination. In 2024 he won the Silver Bear for Best Leading Performance at the Berlin International Film Festival for A Different Man and portrayed Donald Trump in The Apprentice, earning an Academy Award nomination for Best Actor.
