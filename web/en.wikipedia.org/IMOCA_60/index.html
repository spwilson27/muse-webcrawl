IMOCA 60

ClassOcean racing monohull
Length18.28 m (60 ft)
Governing bodyIMOCA
Key racesVendée Globe, The Ocean Race

The IMOCA 60 is a class of 60-foot (18.28 m) racing monohull sailboats governed by the International Monohull Open Class Association (IMOCA). Designed for single-handed and crewed ocean racing, the boats compete in events such as the solo non-stop Vendée Globe circumnavigation.

Design

IMOCA 60s are development-class boats within a box rule: canting keels, water ballast, and — since the mid-2010s — hydrofoils that lift the hull and dramatically increase speeds. Masts, keels, and some safety equipment are standardized, while hull shapes and foils remain areas of intense design competition.

Racing

Besides the Vendée Globe, held every four years, the class races in the Route du Rhum, Transat Jacques Vabre, and The Ocean Race. Boats are sailed solo, double-handed, or fully crewed depending on the event.
