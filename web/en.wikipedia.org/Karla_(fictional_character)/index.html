Karla (fictional character)

Karla
Created by: John le Carré
First appearance: Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (1974)
Role: Head of Soviet foreign intelligence (the Thirteenth Directorate)
Antagonist of: George Smiley

Karla is a fictional character created by John le Carré, the spymaster who heads Soviet foreign intelligence and serves as the nemesis of George Smiley in the "Karla trilogy": Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (1974), The Honourable Schoolboy (1977) and Smiley's People (1979).

Character

Karla's real name is never revealed; the codename derives from his first known operation. A fanatical and ruthless intelligence chief, he runs the mole Gerald inside British intelligence and is protected by Smiley's opposite number in Moscow Centre. Smiley had met him once, in a Delhi prison in 1955, and failed to persuade him to defect.

Downfall

In Smiley's People, Smiley exploits Karla's one human weakness — his secret devotion to his mentally ill daughter, whom he has hidden in a Swiss clinic using embezzled funds — and blackmails him into defecting to the West in Berlin. See also George Smiley.
