Abdel Fattah Saeed Hussein Khalil el-Sisi (born 19 November 1954) is an Egyptian politician and former military officer who has served as the sixth president of Egypt since 2014. A career army officer, he rose to become director of military intelligence and then commander-in-chief of the armed forces and minister of defence in 2012.

Rise to power

Following mass protests against President Mohamed Morsi, el-Sisi, as head of the armed forces, issued an ultimatum and on 3 July 2013 removed Morsi from power in a military coup. A crackdown on the Muslim Brotherhood followed, including the dispersal of pro-Morsi sit-ins in August 2013 in which hundreds were killed. El-Sisi resigned from the military in March 2014 and won the presidential election held that May, taking office on 8 June 2014.

Presidency

El-Sisi was re-elected in 2018 and again in 2023, in elections critics described as neither free nor fair, and constitutional amendments in 2019 extended presidential terms and his potential tenure to 2030. His government has pursued large infrastructure projects — including the expansion of the Suez Canal, a new administrative capital east of Cairo, and extensive road building — while facing recurring economic crises, inflation and foreign-debt pressures. Human rights organisations accuse his government of widespread repression of dissent.
