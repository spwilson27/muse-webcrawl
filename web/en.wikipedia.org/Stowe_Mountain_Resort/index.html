Mount Mansfield, Vermont's highest peak, at Stowe

Stowe Mountain Resort is a ski area in the northeastern United States, near the town of Stowe in northern Vermont. It comprises two mountains — Mount Mansfield, the highest peak in Vermont at 4,393 feet (1,339 m), and Spruce Peak — and is one of the oldest and best-known ski resorts in the eastern United States.

History

Skiing on Mount Mansfield dates to the 1930s, when the Civilian Conservation Corps cut early trails; the first chairlift, then the longest in the world, opened in 1940. The resort was owned for decades by the American International Group before being acquired by Vail Resorts in 2017.

Terrain and facilities

Stowe offers about 116 trails across 485 acres, served by gondolas and high-speed lifts, including the famous steep "Front Four" trails — Starr, Goat, National, and Liftline. The resort includes the Spruce Peak village with hotels, a performing arts center, and an adventure center, and connects to Smugglers' Notch by cross-country trails.

Summer activities

In summer the resort operates the Auto Toll Road up Mount Mansfield, a gondola sky ride, and mountain recreation, drawing visitors for hiking and foliage.
