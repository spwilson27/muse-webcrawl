Sustainable living

Sustainable living describes a lifestyle that attempts to reduce an individual's or society's use of the Earth's natural resources and one's personal resources. Practitioners aim to reduce their ecological footprint by altering methods of transportation, energy consumption, housing and diet.

Practices

Common practices include growing food at home, using renewable energy, reducing waste through reuse and recycling, choosing public transport, cycling or walking, and favouring locally produced goods. Concepts such as permaculture, the zero-waste movement and voluntary simplicity are closely related.

Housing and energy

Sustainable housing emphasises insulation, passive solar design and efficient appliances. Small houses, cohousing and ecovillages are social expressions of the same goals.

Criticism

Critics argue that individual lifestyle change is insufficient without systemic and policy change, while supporters see personal practice as both intrinsically valuable and politically catalytic.
