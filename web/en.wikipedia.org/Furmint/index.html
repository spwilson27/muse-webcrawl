Furmint

SpeciesVitis vinifera
OriginHungary
Notable regionsTokaj
Notable winesTokaji aszú

Furmint is a white Hungarian wine grape variety grown most famously in the Tokaj wine region, where it is the principal grape of the renowned sweet Tokaji aszú wines. It is also cultivated in Slovakia, Austria (as Zapfner), Slovenia, and Croatia.

Characteristics

Furmint is late-ripening, with high natural acidity and thin skins that make it susceptible to noble rot (Botrytis cinerea), which concentrates sugars for sweet wine production. Dry Furmint wines, increasingly popular since the 2000s, are typically steely and mineral with notes of quince, pear, and smoke, reflecting Tokaj's volcanic soils.
