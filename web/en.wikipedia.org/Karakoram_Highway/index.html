Karakoram Highway

The Karakoram Highway (KKH) is a 1,300 km (810 mi) national highway linking Hasan Abdal in Pakistan's Punjab province with Kashgar in China's Xinjiang region, crossing the Karakoram mountain range via the Khunjerab Pass. One of the highest paved international roads in the world, it is often called the "Eighth Wonder of the World" and is a celebrated symbol of Pakistan–China friendship.

Route

From Hasan Abdal the highway runs north through Abbottabad, Mansehra, Besham and Chilas along the Indus River, then follows the Gilgit and Hunza valleys past Gilgit, Karimabad and Sust. It crosses into China at the Khunjerab Pass (4,693 m), among the highest border crossings in the world, and descends through Tashkurgan to Kashgar. In Pakistan it is designated National Highway N-35; in China it forms part of National Highway G314.

Construction

The road was built jointly by the governments of Pakistan and China beginning in 1959, largely following the alignment of an ancient branch of the Silk Road. Pakistani and Chinese engineers and labourers worked in extreme terrain and weather for two decades; hundreds of workers on both sides died, mostly in landslides and falls, and memorial cemeteries along the route — notably the Chinese cemetery at Gilgit — commemorate them. The highway was completed in 1979 and opened to foreign travellers in 1986.

Trade and tourism

The highway is a principal artery for overland trade between Pakistan and China and forms part of the China–Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC), under which sections have been widened and realigned. It passes some of the world's most dramatic mountain scenery, including views of Nanga Parbat and Rakaposhi, and serves as the main access route for mountaineering expeditions to the Karakoram. Seasonal closures occur at the Khunjerab Pass in winter because of snow.

Hazards

The road crosses an active seismic and landslide zone. In January 2010 a massive landslide at Attabad blocked the Hunza River, creating a large lake that submerged a long section of the highway; a new alignment with tunnels, built with Chinese assistance, reopened the route in 2015.
