Lake Bled (Slovene: Blejsko jezero) is a glacial lake in the Julian Alps of the Upper Carniolan region of northwestern Slovenia, adjoining the town of Bled. It is one of the country's most famous landmarks and tourist destinations.

Bled Island

The lake surrounds Bled Island, the only natural island in Slovenia. The island has several buildings, the main one being the pilgrimage church dedicated to the Assumption of Mary, built in its current form near the end of the 17th century and decorated with remains of Gothic frescos. The church has a 52 m (171 ft) free-standing bell tower and a famous "wishing bell"; tradition holds that visitors who ring it will have a wish granted. Visitors reach the island in traditional wooden boats called pletna.

Bled Castle and tourism

Overlooking the lake from a cliff is Bled Castle, the oldest Slovenian castle, first mentioned in 1011. The lake is also a world-class rowing venue, having hosted the World Rowing Championships several times, and is known for the original Bled cream cake (kremšnita).
