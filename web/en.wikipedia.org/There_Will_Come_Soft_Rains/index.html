There Will Come Soft Rains

"There Will Come Soft Rains" is a short story by Ray Bradbury, first published in 1950 and included in The Martian Chronicles. Its title is taken from the 1918 poem of the same name by Sara Teasdale. The story depicts an automated house in Allendale, California, continuing its routines in August 2026 after its human inhabitants have been killed in a nuclear war.

AuthorRay Bradbury
Published1950
GenreScience fiction short story

Plot

The house announces the date, prepares breakfast and reads poetry to an empty room, while outside only ruins and a lone dog — which soon dies — remain. A falling tree branch starts a fire that the house's systems cannot contain, and by dawn only one wall still stands, repeating the date announcement.

Themes

The story contrasts enduring nature with fragile human technology and is among Bradbury's most anthologised works.
