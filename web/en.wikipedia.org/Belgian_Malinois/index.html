A Belgian Malinois.

The Belgian Malinois (pronounced "MAL-in-wah") is a medium-to-large breed of herding dog named after the Belgian city of Mechelen (Malines). It is one of four varieties of the Belgian Shepherd, distinguished by its short fawn coat with a black mask. Highly intelligent, energetic and trainable, the Malinois is in demand worldwide as a military and police working dog.

Characteristics

Malinois are athletic, intense and strongly bonded to their handlers, with high prey and work drives. They excel at obedience, agility, tracking and protection sports, but their energy and sensitivity make them demanding pets for inexperienced owners.

Working roles

The breed serves in armed forces and police units for detection, patrol and search and rescue. A Malinois named Cairo accompanied the US Navy SEAL team in the 2011 raid that killed Osama bin Laden, and another, Conan, was honoured after the 2019 raid on Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi.
