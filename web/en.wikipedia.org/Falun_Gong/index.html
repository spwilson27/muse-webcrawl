Falun Gong

Falun Gong, also known as Falun Dafa, is a new religious movement founded in China by Li Hongzhi in the early 1990s. It combines meditation and qigong exercises with a moral philosophy centred on truthfulness, compassion and forbearance.

TypeNew religious movement
FounderLi Hongzhi
Founded1992, Changchun, China

Persecution

After an estimated 10,000 practitioners gathered at Zhongnanhai on 25 April 1999, the Chinese government banned the movement, launched a media crackdown and carried out mass arrests. Amnesty International and governments report imprisonment and forced labour; practitioners allege forcibly mandated transplants, claims Beijing denies.

Outside China

Falun Gong is practised worldwide; Li Hongzhi settled in the United States, and media outlets linked to the movement have expanded internationally.
