Empress Elisabeth of Austria

Elisabeth (24 December 1837 – 10 September 1898), nicknamed Sisi or Sissi, was Empress of Austria and Queen of Hungary as wife of Emperor Franz Joseph I. Born a Bavarian duchess, she married at sixteen and became an iconic, restless figure of the Habsburg court.

Life

Elisabeth struggled with court etiquette and her mother-in-law Archduchess Sophie. She cultivated her famed beauty, long hair, and slim figure, traveled extensively, learned Hungarian, and supported the Austro-Hungarian Compromise of 1867. The deaths of her daughter Sophie and her son Crown Prince Rudolf in the Mayerling incident (1889) deepened her withdrawal and melancholy.

Assassination and legacy

Italian anarchist Luigi Lucheni stabbed her in Geneva in 1898. She remains a cultural icon in films, musicals, and literature about Austria-Hungary.
