Lenta.ru

Lenta.ru is a Russian-language online newspaper based in Moscow, founded in 1999 by Anton Nossik. It became one of Russia's most popular news websites, known for fast news updates and, for a period, relatively independent editorial coverage.

History

In 2014 its editor-in-chief Galina Timchenko was dismissed and replaced, prompting much of the staff to leave and found Meduza. Lenta.ru is owned by Rambler Media Group and continues as a major Russian news portal covering politics, society, culture and sport.
