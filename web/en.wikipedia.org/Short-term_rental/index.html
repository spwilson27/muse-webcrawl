Short-term rental

Typical duration: Under 30 days
Major platforms: Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com
Issues: Housing supply, noise, tax, regulation

A short-term rental (STR) is the renting out of a furnished home, apartment or room for a short period, typically fewer than 30 consecutive days, as an alternative to hotels. The market expanded rapidly in the 2010s with online platforms such as Airbnb and Vrbo, which allow property owners and managers to list spaces and guests to book them.

History

Renting rooms to travellers is centuries old, but internet platforms transformed it into a mass market. Airbnb, founded in 2008, grew into the sector's best-known company, with millions of listings worldwide, joined by vacation-rental specialists such as Vrbo and hotel platforms offering homes.

Regulation and debate

Many cities regulate short-term rentals through registration, night caps, zoning and taxes, citing effects on housing affordability and availability, neighbourhood disturbance, and competition with hotels. Supporters point to income for hosts and tourism benefits. Rules vary widely, from permissive regimes to near-bans in some city centres.
