Warp Records

Founded1989, Sheffield, England
FoundersSteve Beckett, Rob Mitchell, Robert Gordon
GenreElectronic, IDM, experimental

Warp Records (or simply Warp) is a British independent record label founded in Sheffield in 1989. It became one of the most influential labels in electronic music, pioneering the bleep techno sound and later the genre known as intelligent dance music (IDM).

History

Warp grew out of the FON record shop in Sheffield. Early releases by Nightmares on Wax, LFO and Sweet Exorcist defined the "bleep and bass" sound. The 1992 compilation Artificial Intelligence showcased artists including Aphex Twin, Autechre and Richie Hawtin, and marketed electronic music for home listening.

Artists

Long-standing Warp artists include Aphex Twin, Autechre, Boards of Canada, Squarepusher, Flying Lotus, and Broadcast. The label also runs Warp Films, founded in 1999.
