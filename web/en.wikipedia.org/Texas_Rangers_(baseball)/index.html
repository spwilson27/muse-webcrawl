Texas Rangers (baseball)

The Texas Rangers are an American professional baseball team based in Arlington, Texas, in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex. The Rangers compete in Major League Baseball (MLB) as a member club of the American League (AL) West Division. The franchise was founded in 1961 as the Washington Senators, an expansion team in Washington, D.C., and relocated to Texas for the 1972 season, taking the name of the historic Texas Ranger Division of the Texas Department of Public Safety.

History

After a decade of losing seasons in Washington, owner Bob Short moved the Senators to Arlington, where they played at Turnpike Stadium (renamed Arlington Stadium). The Rangers reached the postseason for the first time in 1996 behind American League MVP Juan González, winning division titles in 1996, 1998 and 1999, but did not win a playoff series until 2010.

Under manager Ron Washington, the Rangers won consecutive American League pennants in 2010 and 2011, losing the 2010 World Series to San Francisco and the 2011 World Series to St. Louis in seven games — coming within one strike of the championship twice in Game 6.

2023 World Series championship

Led by manager Bruce Bochy and shortstop Corey Seager, the 2023 Rangers defeated the Arizona Diamondbacks in five games to win the first World Series title in franchise history. Seager was named World Series MVP for the second time in his career.

Ballparks

The Rangers played at Arlington Stadium (1972–1993), then at the Ballpark in Arlington (later Globe Life Park, 1994–2019), and since 2020 at the retractable-roof Globe Life Field.

Notable players

  • Nolan Ryan — Hall of Fame pitcher and later club president; threw his seventh no-hitter as a Ranger.
  • Iván Rodríguez — Hall of Fame catcher.
  • Adrián Beltré — Hall of Fame third baseman who collected his 3,000th hit with Texas.
  • Michael Young, Josh Hamilton, Juan González, Rafael Palmeiro.
