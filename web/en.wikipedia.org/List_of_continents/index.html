List of continents

A continent is any of several large geographical regions. Continents are generally identified by convention rather than any strict criteria. A continent could be a single landmass or a part of a very large landmass. This article gives a list of the continents under the most common seven-continent model.

Different models

Models vary from four to seven continents: some combine Europe and Asia as Eurasia, or the Americas as a single continent; Olympic rings represent five inhabited continents. Geologists treat continents as large areas of continental crust and also recognise submerged landmasses such as Zealandia. By the geological definition, continents have moved over time through plate tectonics; past supercontinents include Pangaea, Gondwana and Laurasia.
