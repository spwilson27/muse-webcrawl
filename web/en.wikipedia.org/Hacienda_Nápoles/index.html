Hacienda Nápoles

Hacienda Nápoles is a 7.7-square-mile (20 km²) estate in Puerto Triunfo, Antioquia, Colombia, built by drug lord Pablo Escobar and the Medellín Cartel. The estate included a colonial house, a sculpture park, a private zoo with exotic animals from around the world, an airstrip, a bullring, and a go-kart track. At the entrance arch stands a replica of the Piper PA-18 Super Cub airplane that flew Escobar's first shipment of cocaine to the United States.

History

After Escobar's death in 1993, the estate fell into disrepair and much of it was taken over by the Colombian government. The private zoo's animals were largely relocated, though a population of hippopotamuses escaped and established a feral herd in the surrounding rivers and lakes, becoming the largest population of hippos outside Africa.

Present day

The property was later developed into a theme park open to the public, with water attractions, animal exhibits, and a museum about Escobar's life and the cartel era.
