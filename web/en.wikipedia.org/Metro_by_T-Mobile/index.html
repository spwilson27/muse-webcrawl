Metro by T-Mobile is an American prepaid wireless service provider and brand owned by T-Mobile US. It was previously an independent company, MetroPCS Communications, Inc., the fifth-largest facilities-based mobile network operator in the United States, before merging with T-Mobile in a reverse merger completed on May 1, 2013. The brand was relaunched in 2018 as Metro by T-Mobile.

History

MetroPCS was founded in 1994 and launched service in Miami, Florida, in 2002, pioneering flat-rate unlimited prepaid plans in the U.S. market. It built a CDMA network covering selected metropolitan areas and later added LTE. In 2012, T-Mobile USA and MetroPCS announced a merger; MetroPCS shareholders received cash and a stake in the combined company, which adopted the T-Mobile name. The MetroPCS network's CDMA service was shut down in 2015 as customers migrated to T-Mobile's GSM/LTE network.

Service

Metro by T-Mobile offers prepaid plans with no annual contracts, sold through thousands of authorised dealer stores and its own retail locations, positioned as the prepaid flank brand of T-Mobile alongside its postpaid offerings.
