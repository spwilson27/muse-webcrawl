Getting Things Done
Author: David Allen
Published: 2001
Subject: Personal productivity, time management
Publisher: Viking Press

Getting Things Done (GTD) is a personal productivity system developed by David Allen and described in his 2001 book of the same name. Its premise is that a person frees mental capacity by recording all tasks and commitments in a trusted external system rather than relying on memory — achieving what Allen calls "mind like water".

The workflow

GTD has five stages: capture everything that has your attention into inboxes; clarify what each item means and whether it is actionable; organise outcomes into lists of projects, next actions (grouped by context), waiting-for items, and a calendar; reflect by reviewing the system, including a weekly review; and engage by choosing what to do. Actions requiring less than two minutes should be done immediately when clarified.

Reception and influence

The book became a bestseller and GTD a widely adopted framework, spawning software tools and a large community of practitioners. A substantially revised edition appeared in 2015.
