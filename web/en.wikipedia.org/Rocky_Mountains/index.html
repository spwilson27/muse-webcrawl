The Rocky Mountains, also known as the Rockies, are a vast mountain range in western North America stretching more than 4,800 kilometres from northern British Columbia in Canada to New Mexico in the United States. They form the largest mountain system in North America and the eastern part of the North American Cordillera, separated from the Pacific ranges by interior plateaus and trenches. The mountains take their name from their rugged rock terrain — itself the product of the rock cycle at work over hundreds of millions of years.

Geology

The Rockies rose during the Laramide orogeny (roughly 80–55 million years ago), when the Farallon Plate subducted at an unusually shallow angle beneath North America, uplifting basement-cored ranges far inland. Their core consists of ancient Precambrian metamorphic and igneous rock overlain — and often faulted up through — thick sequences of Palaeozoic and Mesozoic sedimentary rock. Glaciers repeatedly sculpted the high ranges during the Pleistocene.

Geography and ecology

The range includes all of Colorado's famous "fourteeners"; its highest summit is Mount Elbert (4,401 m). The Continental Divide runs along the crest, separating rivers flowing to the Pacific from those flowing to the Atlantic and Arctic. Major rivers including the Colorado, Missouri, Rio Grande and Columbia head here. Ecosystems range from prairie foothills through montane and subalpine forests of pine, spruce and fir to alpine tundra; wildlife includes elk, moose, bighorn sheep, grizzly and black bears, and wolves.

Human history and recreation

Indigenous peoples have lived in and around the Rockies for at least 11,000 years. European exploration intensified after the Louisiana Purchase (1803) and the Lewis and Clark Expedition; fur trappers ("mountain men"), miners and the transcontinental railroads followed. Today the range hosts famous national parks — Banff and Jasper in Canada, Yellowstone, Glacier and Rocky Mountain in the US — and major ski resorts, and its scenery anchors western North American identity.
