No pasarán

"¡No pasarán!" ("They shall not pass") is a slogan used to express determination to defend a position against an enemy. It gained fame during the Battle of Verdun in the First World War in the French form "Ils ne passeront pas", associated with General Robert Nivelle's order of the day in 1916.

Spanish Civil War

The Spanish version became iconic during the Spanish Civil War, when it was used by Republican speakers — most famously Dolores Ibárruri, "La Pasionaria", in her 1936 radio speech as Nationalist forces advanced on Madrid: "¡No pasarán! Fascism will not pass." The besieged capital held until 1939, and the slogan became a rallying cry of anti-fascist movements worldwide; the International Brigades adopted it as a motto. It has since been reused by resistance, protest, and political movements in many countries.
