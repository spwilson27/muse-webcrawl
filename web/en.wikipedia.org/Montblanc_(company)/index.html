Montblanc (company)

Montblanc International GmbH
Founded: 1906, Hamburg, Germany
Industry: luxury goods
Products: writing instruments, watches, leather goods, jewellery, fragrances
Parent: Richemont

Montblanc is a German manufacturer of luxury goods, headquartered in Hamburg and owned by the Swiss Richemont group. The company is best known for its fountain pens — above all the Meisterstück ("Masterpiece") line introduced in 1924 — and for its white six-pointed star emblem, representing the snow-capped peak of Mont Blanc, the highest mountain in the Alps.

History

The company was founded in 1906 as the Simplo Filler Pen Co. by Hamburg banker Alfred Nehemias and Berlin engineer August Eberstein, and adopted the Montblanc name in 1909. Its pens became status symbols during the twentieth century. From the 1990s the brand diversified into leather goods, watches (through its Montblanc Montre subsidiary in Le Locle, Switzerland), jewellery, eyewear and fragrances.

Products

Alongside the Meisterstück, Montblanc produces limited-edition "Patron of Art" and "Writers Edition" pens honouring historical and literary figures. Its watch collections include the 1858, TimeWalker and Star Legacy lines, and the Summit smartwatch series launched in 2017.
