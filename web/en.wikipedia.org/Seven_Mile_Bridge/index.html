Seven Mile Bridge

The Seven Mile Bridge carrying the Overseas Highway

The Seven Mile Bridge is a famous bridge in the Florida Keys, in Monroe County, Florida, United States. It connects Knight's Key (part of the city of Marathon) in the Middle Keys to Little Duck Key in the Lower Keys. At 35,862 feet (10.93 km) in total length, it is among the longest bridges in the world. The bridge carries the Overseas Highway (U.S. Route 1) across the open water between the two keys.

History

The original bridge was built between 1909 and 1912 as part of Henry Flagler's Florida East Coast Railway's Key West Extension, often called the Overseas Railroad. After the railroad was badly damaged by the Labor Day Hurricane of 1935, the bridge was converted for automobile use. That older structure, with its narrow roadway built over the railway girder, served drivers for decades.

The modern bridge

The present bridge, a segmental concrete box-girder structure, was constructed from 1978 to 1982 at a cost of about $45 million. The old bridge alongside it was largely closed to traffic; sections survive as a pedestrian and fishing pier giving access to Pigeon Key, and it hosts the annual Seven Mile Bridge Run each April, when the highway bridge is closed for a footrace across its full length.

In popular culture

The bridge has appeared in numerous films and television series, including True Lies, Licence to Kill, 2 Fast 2 Furious and Burn Notice, thanks to its long run over open tropical water.
