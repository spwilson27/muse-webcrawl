Mister Fantastic (Dr. Reed Richards) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, he first appeared in The Fantastic Four #1 (November 1961) and is the leader of the Fantastic Four.

Fictional biography and powers

Richards, a child prodigy and polymath scientist, led an unauthorized experimental spaceflight whose crew — Richards, pilot Ben Grimm, Susan Storm, and her brother Johnny — were bombarded by cosmic rays. Richards gained the ability to stretch, deform, and reshape his body to extreme lengths and forms, and took the name Mister Fantastic. Regarded as one of the most intelligent characters in the Marvel Universe, he is married to Susan Storm (the Invisible Woman), with whom he has two children, Franklin and Valeria. His scientific preoccupation frequently strains his family life, and his arch-rival is Doctor Doom, his former college acquaintance Victor von Doom.

In other media

The character has appeared in animated series and films, played by Ioan Gruffudd in the 2005 and 2007 Fantastic Four films, by Miles Teller in the 2015 reboot, and by John Krasinski as an alternate-universe variant in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022).
