Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh
Capital: Lucknow
Population: ~200 million (most populous Indian state)
Major cities: Kanpur, Agra, Varanasi, Prayagraj

Uttar Pradesh is a state in northern India and the most populous state in the country—if it were a country it would rank among the world's five largest by population. It lies across the fertile Indo-Gangetic Plain, watered by the Ganges and Yamuna rivers.

History and significance

The region is central to Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain traditions: Varanasi is one of the world's oldest living cities, Ayodhya is revered as Rama's birthplace, and Sarnath is where the Buddha first taught. Under Mughal rule Agra became an imperial capital and site of the Taj Mahal. The state was a heartland of the 1857 rebellion and of the Indian independence movement.

Economy

Agriculture (wheat, sugarcane) dominates, alongside growing industry and services in the western corridor near Delhi, including Noida.
