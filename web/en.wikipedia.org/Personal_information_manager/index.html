Personal information manager

A personal information manager (PIM) is software that helps users organise personal information such as contacts, calendars, tasks, notes and reminders. PIMs began as electronic organisers in the 1980s and became a core function of personal digital assistants and smartphones.

Features

Typical functions include an address book, calendar with appointments and alarms, to-do lists, note-taking, and synchronisation with other devices or cloud services. Examples include Microsoft Outlook, Apple Calendar and Contacts, and Google Calendar.

History

Handheld devices such as the Psion Organiser and PalmPilot popularised PIM functions in dedicated hardware before they were absorbed into general-purpose phones and web services. Personal information management is also studied as an academic field concerning how people acquire, organise and retrieve information.
