Nuffield Foundation

The Nuffield Foundation is a charitable trust in the United Kingdom, established in 1943 by William Morris, Lord Nuffield, the founder of Morris Motors. It funds research and innovation in education and social policy.

Areas of work

The Foundation's main domains are education, welfare and justice. Well-known projects include the Nuffield Council on Bioethics (founded 1991), the Nuffield Family Justice Observatory, and long-running research programmes on adolescence and social change. It also supported the development of Nuffield science and mathematics curricula in schools.

Funding

The Foundation is endowed and independent of government, awarding grants to universities, research institutes and charities across the UK.
