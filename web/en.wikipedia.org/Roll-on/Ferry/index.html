Roll-on/roll-off ferry

A roll-on/roll-off ferry (ro-pax when carrying passengers) is a ferry designed to carry wheeled cargo — cars, buses, trucks and trailers — which drive on and off the ship via bow, stern or side ramps. Vehicle decks run the length of the vessel, with passenger accommodation usually above.

Operations and safety

Ro-ro ferries serve short sea crossings worldwide, such as the English Channel, the Baltic, and Japanese domestic routes. Their large, open vehicle decks and low freeboard when bow doors are open make watertight integrity critical; the losses of the Herald of Free Enterprise (1987) and Estonia (1994) led to stricter rules on door indicators, subdivision and stability standards.
