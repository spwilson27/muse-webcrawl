Lift-on/lift-off

Lift-on/lift-off (LoLo) describes cargo ships whose freight is loaded and unloaded vertically using cranes, in contrast to roll-on/roll-off (RoRo) ships whose wheeled cargo rolls on and off via ramps. Most container ships and general cargo ships are LoLo vessels.

Comparison with roll-on/roll-off

LoLo ships require port crane infrastructure but use hull volume more efficiently and can carry non-wheeled cargo, while RoRo ships load quickly with minimal port equipment but devote substantial space to ramps and vehicle lanes. Some vessels combine both principles, carrying containers on deck and vehicles below, or using their own geared cranes for self-loading in smaller ports.
