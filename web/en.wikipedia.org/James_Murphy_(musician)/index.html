James Jeremiah Murphy (born February 4, 1970) is an American musician, DJ, singer, songwriter, and record producer. He is best known as the frontman of LCD Soundsystem and co-founder of the dance-punk label DFA Records.

Career

Murphy played drums in several indie bands in the 1980s and 1990s and worked as a recording engineer. In 2001 he co-founded DFA Records with Tim Goldsworthy and Jonathan Galkin in New York. LCD Soundsystem debuted in 2002 with the single "Losing My Edge", a wry, self-deprecating anthem that became a signature song. The band's albums — LCD Soundsystem (2005), Sound of Silver (2007), and This Is Happening (2010) — won wide critical acclaim, blending dance-punk, disco, and electronic music with lyrics about aging, scenes, and sincerity.

Later projects

Murphy disbanded LCD Soundsystem in 2011 with a celebrated farewell concert at Madison Square Garden, then reunited the band in 2015; the 2017 album American Dream reached number one in the United States and won a Grammy for the song "Tonight". He has also worked as a producer and remixer, composed film scores, and pursued side ventures including a New York wine bar and a proposed musical redesign of the New York City subway turnstile sounds.
