Continental IO-550

The Continental IO-550 is a family of large-bore, fuel-injected, air-cooled, horizontally opposed six-cylinder aircraft engines produced by Continental Motors. Developed from the IO-520 series, the IO-550 powers high-performance single-engine aircraft such as the Cirrus SR22, Beechcraft Bonanza G36, and Cessna 206 Stationair.

IO-550

Type: Six-cylinder, air-cooled, horizontally opposed piston engine
Manufacturer: Continental Motors
Displacement: 552 cu in (9.05 L)
Power output: 280–315 hp depending on variant
Fuel system: Fuel injection

Design

The IO-550 shares the IO-360's basic architecture but with a much larger displacement achieved through increased bore and stroke. Naturally aspirated variants such as the IO-550-N (310 hp, Cirrus SR22) and IO-550-G (280 hp, Bonanza) are common; the turbocharged TSIO-550 series serves pressurised and high-altitude aircraft like the Cessna 400/Corvalis and Mooney Acclaim.

Applications

Besides the SR22 and Bonanza, the engine family powers the Columbia/Lancair twins of designs, the Comp Air and various homebuilt aircraft. Factory-remanufactured and overhaul support through Continental and independent shops keeps the type central to premium general aviation.
