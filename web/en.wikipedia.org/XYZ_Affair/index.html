XYZ Affair

The XYZ Affair was a diplomatic incident in 1797–1798 between the United States and Revolutionary France that led to the undeclared Quasi-War at sea.

Events

President John Adams sent envoys Charles Cotesworth Pinckney, John Marshall and Elbridge Gerry to negotiate after French seizures of American shipping. French foreign minister Talleyrand's agents — later labelled X, Y and Z in published dispatches — demanded a large bribe and a loan before talks could begin. The envoys refused; Pinckney's reported reply was 'No! No! Not a sixpence!', popularised as 'Millions for defense, but not one cent for tribute!'

Consequences

Publication of the dispatches in 1798 caused outrage in America, strengthened the Federalists, prompted naval expansion, and opened two years of undeclared naval war, ended by the Convention of 1800.
