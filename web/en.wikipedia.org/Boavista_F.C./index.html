Boavista Futebol Clube, commonly known as Boavista, is a Portuguese professional football club based in Porto. Founded in 1903, it is one of Portugal's oldest clubs and is distinctive for its black-and-white chequered shirts.

History

Boavista's greatest success came in the 2000–01 season, when it won the Primeira Liga title—only the second club outside the traditional "Big Three" to do so. The club also won the Taça de Portugal five times and reached the semi-finals of the UEFA Cup in 2002–03.

Stadium

The club plays at the Estádio do Bessa Século XXI, rebuilt for UEFA Euro 2004.
