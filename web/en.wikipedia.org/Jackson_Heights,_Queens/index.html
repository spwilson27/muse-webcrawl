Jackson Heights, Queens

Jackson Heights is a neighbourhood in the northwestern portion of the borough of Queens in New York City. It is known for its garden apartment complexes — among the first in the United States — and for one of the most ethnically diverse populations in the world.

History

Developed from 1909 by the Queensboro Corporation as a planned community for middle-income families, Jackson Heights pioneered cooperative garden apartments with interior courtyards. Much of the neighbourhood is now a designated historic district.

Diversity

Since the 1960s Jackson Heights has become a major centre for South Asian, Latin American and other immigrant communities; Roosevelt Avenue and 74th Street are lined with South Asian shops and restaurants, and the area hosts one of New York's largest LGBTQ communities in Queens.
