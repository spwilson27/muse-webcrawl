Intersex

Intersex people are individuals born with sex characteristics — including chromosomes, gonads, hormones or genitals — that do not fit typical binary notions of male or female bodies. Intersex is an umbrella term for a range of natural variations, sometimes grouped medically as differences of sex development.

Variations

Examples include androgen insensitivity syndrome, congenital adrenal hyperplasia, Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY), Turner syndrome (45,X), and variations in genital development.

Medical practices and rights

Historically, infants and children underwent surgeries to make bodies conform to male or female norms. Human rights bodies and intersex advocates have criticised non-consensual interventions and called for bodily autonomy, legal recognition and protection from discrimination.

Terminology

Activism since the 1990s promoted 'intersex' over older medical terms. Estimates of frequency depend on definitions used.
