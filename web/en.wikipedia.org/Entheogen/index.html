Entheogen

An entheogen is a psychoactive substance used in religious, shamanic or spiritual contexts to induce altered states of consciousness. The term, coined in 1979, means "generating the divine within".

Examples

Traditional entheogens include peyote, psilocybin mushrooms, ayahuasca, iboga, cannabis in some traditions, and the ancient soma and kykeon whose identities are debated.

Legal and cultural status

Some jurisdictions exempt sacramental use by recognised churches, such as the Native American Church's use of peyote, while most entheogens are controlled substances elsewhere. Research into therapeutic uses has revived in the 21st century.
