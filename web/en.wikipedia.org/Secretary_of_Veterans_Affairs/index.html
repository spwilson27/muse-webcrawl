The United States Secretary of Veterans Affairs is the head of the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, the department that administers benefits, health care, and national cemeteries for military veterans and their families. The secretary is a member of the Cabinet of the United States and is seventeenth in the line of succession to the presidency.

History

The position was created when the Veterans Administration was elevated to a cabinet-level department by the Department of Veterans Affairs Act, signed by President Ronald Reagan in 1988 and effective March 15, 1989. Ed Derwinski, who had been Administrator of Veterans Affairs, became the first secretary.

Duties

The secretary oversees the Veterans Health Administration, the Veterans Benefits Administration, and the National Cemetery Administration. The department is the second-largest in the federal government by number of employees, after the Department of Defense, operating hundreds of medical facilities and regional benefit offices.

List of secretaries

Secretaries have included Ed Derwinski, Jesse Brown, Togo West, Anthony Principi, Jim Nicholson, James Peake, Eric Shinseki, Robert McDonald, David Shulkin, Robert Wilkie, and Denis McDonough.
