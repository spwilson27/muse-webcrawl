Legalization

Legalization (British English: legalisation) is the process of removing a legal prohibition against something which is currently not legal, replacing it — usually — with a system of laws regulating the activity.

Discussion

Legalization is debated for subjects such as drugs, prostitution, gambling and abortion. Supporters argue it brings activity under regulation, taxation and quality control and reduces criminal markets; opponents argue it increases harm or availability. It differs from decriminalization, which removes criminal penalties while the activity remains formally illegal or subject to civil fines.
