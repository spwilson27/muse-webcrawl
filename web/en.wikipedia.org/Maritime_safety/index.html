Maritime safety

Maritime safety encompasses the protection of life and property at sea through regulation, management, and technology. It includes the safety of ships, their crews and passengers, and the prevention of marine pollution.

The International Maritime Organization (IMO) develops global standards, including the SOLAS convention (International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, first adopted in 1914 after the sinking of the Titanic), the STCW convention on crew training, and COLREGs governing collision avoidance.

National coast guards and maritime administrations enforce safety inspections, vessel traffic services, and search and rescue.
