Ursula Gertrud von der Leyen (née Albrecht; born 8 October 1958) is a German politician and physician who has served as President of the European Commission since 1 December 2019. A member of the Christian Democratic Union (CDU), she previously held three cabinet posts in the governments of Angela Merkel, culminating as Federal Minister of Defence from 2013 to 2019 — the first woman to hold that office in Germany.

Early life and career

Von der Leyen was born in Brussels, where her father, Ernst Albrecht, worked for the European institutions; he later became Minister-President of Lower Saxony. She studied economics at Göttingen and at the London School of Economics (under the name "Rose Ladson" in 1978, amid security concerns related to the Red Army Faction) and then medicine at Hannover Medical School, qualifying as a physician and earning a doctorate in 1991. She spent a period in the United States at Stanford while her husband, the physician Heiko von der Leyen, taught there. She has seven children.

Political career in Germany

Entering Lower Saxony politics, she became state minister for social affairs in 2003, then Federal Minister of Family Affairs (2005–2009), where she expanded parental-leave benefits (Elterngeld) and childcare, and Minister of Labour and Social Affairs (2009–2013). As Defence Minister from 2013 she pushed to modernize the Bundeswehr, though her tenure was marked by procurement problems and a consultancy scandal investigated by a Bundestag committee.

European Commission President

In July 2019 she was nominated by the European Council and narrowly confirmed by the European Parliament as the first female President of the European Commission. Her first Commission launched the European Green Deal, coordinated the EU's joint COVID-19 vaccine procurement and the €750 billion NextGenerationEU recovery fund, and responded to Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine with sanctions and support for Kyiv. Following the 2024 European elections, she was re-elected by the European Parliament for a second term, which began on 1 December 2024.
