University of Reading

The University of Reading is a public research university in Reading, Berkshire, England. It traces its origins to 1892 and received its royal charter in 1926.

History

Reading began as an extension college of the University of Oxford. It developed strengths in agriculture, meteorology and typography.

Academics

The university is known for Henley Business School, meteorology, agriculture and food science, and the Museum of English Rural Life.

Campuses

Its main Whiteknights campus is set in parkland, with additional campuses at London Road and Greenlands near Henley-on-Thames.
