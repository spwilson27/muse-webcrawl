Minsk Protocol

The Minsk Protocol (Minsk I) is an agreement signed on 5 September 2014 in Minsk, Belarus, seeking to halt the war in Donbas in eastern Ukraine. It was signed by representatives of Ukraine, Russia, the OSCE, and the self-proclaimed Donetsk and Luhansk people's republics, following talks under the Trilateral Contact Group.

Provisions

The twelve points included an immediate bilateral ceasefire, OSCE monitoring, decentralisation of power, early local elections in parts of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts, withdrawal of illegal armed groups, and humanitarian measures.

Failure and Minsk II

Fighting continued, notably around Donetsk airport and Debaltseve. A follow-up package, Minsk II, was negotiated in February 2015 by Ukraine, Russia, France and Germany in the Normandy Format. Neither agreement was fully implemented, and Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022 ended the process.
