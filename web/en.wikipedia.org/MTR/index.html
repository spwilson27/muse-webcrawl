MTR

The MTR (Mass Transit Railway) is a major public transport network serving Hong Kong. It consists of heavy rail, light rail, and feeder bus services, and is operated by the MTR Corporation, a company listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in which the Hong Kong government is the majority shareholder.

Network

The first line of the system, a modified initial system between Kwun Tong and Shek Kip Mei, opened in October 1979. Today's heavy-rail network comprises roughly 100 stations across lines including the Kwun Tong, Tsuen Wan, Island, Tuen Ma, East Rail, Tung Chung, and South Island lines, plus the Airport Express and the Hong Kong section of the high-speed rail link to mainland China (the Express Rail Link, opened 2018). The MTR carries about five million passengers on a typical weekday.

Rail plus property model

The MTR is famous for its "Rail + Property" financing model: the government grants the corporation development rights above and around stations, and the profits from property development help fund railway construction, allowing the system to operate without direct operating subsidies while maintaining high reliability and frequency.

International operations

Through subsidiaries and joint ventures, MTR Corporation has operated or built railways in mainland China (Beijing, Shenzhen, Hangzhou), Macau, Australia (Melbourne and Sydney), Sweden (Stockholm), and the United Kingdom (including London's Elizabeth line under the TfL Rail concession).
