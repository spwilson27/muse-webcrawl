World Athletics, formerly known as the International Association of Athletics Federations (IAAF), is the international governing body for the sport of athletics, covering track and field, cross country running, road running, race walking, mountain running, and ultra running. Founded in 1912 in Stockholm, it is headquartered in Monaco and includes 214 member federations.

History

The body was founded as the International Amateur Athletic Federation on 17 July 1912, immediately after the Olympic Games in Stockholm, by representatives of 17 national federations. "Amateur" was dropped from the name in 2001, and in 2019 the organization rebranded itself as World Athletics. It has been led by presidents including Sigfrid Edström, David Burghley (Lord Burghley), Adriaan Paulen, Primo Nebiolo, Lamine Diack, and, since 2015, Sebastian Coe.

Competitions

World Athletics organizes the World Athletics Championships, first held in Helsinki in 1983 and now staged every two years; the World Indoor Championships; the World Cross Country Championships; the World Athletics Relays; the World Race Walking Team Championships; and world championships for under-20 athletes. It also sanctions the Diamond League circuit and maintains the world rankings and world records lists.

Governance and issues

The federation sets the rules of competition, certifies facilities and timing, runs anti-doping programs (in cooperation with the Athletics Integrity Unit, established in 2017), and adjudicates eligibility questions that have included rules on testosterone in female categories and the suspension of the Russian federation following state-sponsored doping revelations.
