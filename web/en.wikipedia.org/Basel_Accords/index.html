Basel Accords

The Basel Accords are a set of banking supervision agreements set by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, based at the Bank for International Settlements in Basel, Switzerland. They provide recommendations on capital requirements and bank regulation.

Basel I, II and III

Basel I (1988) introduced minimum capital ratios. Basel II (2004) added risk-sensitive pillars. Basel III, agreed after the 2007–08 financial crisis, strengthened capital, leverage and liquidity requirements.

Purpose

The accords aim to ensure banks hold enough capital to absorb losses and to promote financial stability internationally.
