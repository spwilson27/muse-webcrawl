List of tomato cultivars

There are more than 10,000 cultivated varieties (cultivars) of the tomato (Solanum lycopersicum), bred for flavour, size, colour, disease resistance and climate. They range from tiny cherry tomatoes to beefsteak fruits weighing over a kilogram.

Well-known cultivars

  • Brandywine — a large pink Amish heirloom beefsteak prized for flavour.
  • San Marzano — an Italian plum tomato protected by DOP status, favoured for sauces.
  • Roma — a standard paste tomato with dense, low-moisture flesh.
  • Cherokee Purple — a dusky purple-brown heirloom from Tennessee.
  • Green Zebra — a green-striped tomato bred by Tom Wagner.
  • Sungold — a very sweet orange cherry hybrid.
  • Black Krim — a dark Crimean heirloom.
  • Gardener's Delight — a prolific red cherry variety.
  • Moneymaker — a reliable British greenhouse variety.
  • Alicante — a classic cordon variety for salads.

Types

Cultivars are grouped as beefsteak, plum (paste), cherry, grape, campari and oxheart, and by growth habit as determinate (bush) or indeterminate (cordon) plants. Heirloom varieties are open-pollinated lines maintained for generations, while F1 hybrids are bred commercially for uniformity and resistance.
