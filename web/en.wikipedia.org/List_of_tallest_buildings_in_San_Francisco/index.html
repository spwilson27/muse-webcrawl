List of tallest buildings in San Francisco

San Francisco, California, has more than 490 high-rise buildings, concentrated in the Financial District and the South of Market (SoMa) area. The city's skyline was long dominated by the Transamerica Pyramid (1972), but the Salesforce Tower, completed in 2018 at 1,070 feet (326 m), surpassed both it and 555 California Street to become the tallest building in San Francisco and the second-tallest west of the Mississippi River at its completion. Height limits, Proposition M office caps, seismic requirements (following the 1906 and 1989 earthquakes) and neighbourhood opposition historically constrained construction, with a building boom in the 2010s adding several towers over 800 feet.

The Millennium Tower became notorious for sinking and tilting after completion, requiring a costly perimeter-pile remediation project. Other notable structures include the Russ Building (1927), for many years the city's tallest, and the de Young Museum and California Academy of Sciences in nearby Golden Gate Park — low-rise but architecturally celebrated.
