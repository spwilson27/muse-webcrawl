The GP3 Series was a single-seater motor racing series launched in 2010 as a feeder class to the GP2 Series (later FIA Formula 2) and the Formula One World Championship. Organised by Bruno Michel's GP2 Motorsport organisation, it raced on the Formula One support bill at European Grand Prix weekends, using a spec Dallara chassis and a normally aspirated engine of about 400 horsepower.

History

The series debuted at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya in May 2010 with ten teams and thirty cars. Mexican driver Esteban Gutiérrez won the inaugural championship for ART Grand Prix. The first-generation car, the Dallara GP3/10, was replaced by the GP3/13 for 2013, and a third-generation car, the GP3/16, arrived in 2016 with a Mecachrome V6.

GP3 produced several Formula One drivers, including Valtteri Bottas (2011 champion), Daniil Kvyat (2013), Charles Leclerc (2016), George Russell (2017) and Esteban Ocon. The final season, in 2018, was won by French driver Anthoine Hubert for ART Grand Prix. For 2019 GP3 merged with the FIA Formula 3 European Championship to form the FIA Formula 3 Championship.
