BP

BP p.l.c. (formerly British Petroleum) is a British multinational oil and gas company headquartered in London, England. It is one of the world's oil and gas supermajors, operating in all areas of the industry, including exploration and extraction, refining, distribution, marketing, power generation and trading.

History

The company was founded in 1909 as the Anglo-Persian Oil Company, exploiting oil discovered in Persia (Iran) the previous year. It became the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company in 1935 and was renamed British Petroleum in 1954. The British government was a major shareholder until privatisation in stages between 1979 and 1987. In 1998 BP merged with the American company Amoco to form BP Amoco, later shortened back to BP, and it acquired ARCO and Burmah Castrol in 2000.

Deepwater Horizon

In April 2010 an explosion on the BP-operated Deepwater Horizon drilling rig in the Gulf of Mexico killed eleven workers and caused the largest marine oil spill in history. The disaster cost BP tens of billions of dollars in cleanup, fines and compensation, and severely damaged its reputation.

Operations

BP runs a global network of service stations under the BP and Aral brands and has in recent years announced, and partly scaled back, plans to shift investment toward low-carbon energy while remaining a major producer of oil and gas.
