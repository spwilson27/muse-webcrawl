Vrbo

Vrbo
Founded: 1995
Founder: David Clouse
Parent: Expedia Group
Industry: Vacation rentals

Vrbo (originally an abbreviation of Vacation Rentals by Owner) is an American online marketplace for vacation rental properties. Founded in 1995, it lists whole homes, condos and cabins offered for short-term rental by property owners and managers.

History

Vrbo began as a simple website listing vacation rentals and grew with the rise of online travel. It was acquired by HomeAway in 2006, and HomeAway was in turn acquired by Expedia Group in 2015 for about .9 billion. In 2019, HomeAway's brands were consolidated under the Vrbo name.

Business model

Unlike services emphasising shared spaces, Vrbo concentrates on entire homes, positioning itself toward families and groups. Revenue comes from subscription and commission fees charged to hosts and service fees charged to travellers.
