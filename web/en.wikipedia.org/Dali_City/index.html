Dali City

Dali
Location: Yunnan, China
Prefecture: Dali Bai Autonomous Prefecture
Features: Erhai Lake, Cangshan mountains, Three Pagodas

Dali City is a county-level city in and the seat of Dali Bai Autonomous Prefecture in western Yunnan, China, situated on the shore of Erhai Lake at the foot of the Cangshan mountains.

History

The area was the centre of the Nanzhao kingdom (738–937) and then the Dali Kingdom (937–1253), conquered by the Mongols under Kublai Khan. The old town preserves Ming-era walls and gates.

Attractions

Landmarks include the Three Pagodas of Chongsheng Temple, Dali Old Town, and the lakeside town of Shuanglang. The city is a major domestic tourism destination and a hub for the Bai people, known for tie-dye crafts and the March Fair festival.
