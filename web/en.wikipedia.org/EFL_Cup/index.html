EFL Cup

The EFL Cup, known as the League Cup and currently the Carabao Cup for sponsorship reasons, is an annual knockout football competition in English football organised by the English Football League (EFL). First held in 1960–61, it is open to all 92 clubs of the Premier League and EFL.

Format

The competition is played over seven rounds of single-leg ties, with the final held at Wembley Stadium, traditionally in late February. Clubs involved in European competition receive byes to later rounds. The winners qualify for the UEFA Conference League play-off round (unless already qualified for Europe through the league).

History

The League Cup was introduced by Football League secretary Alan Hardaker to give clubs additional revenue under floodlights and to compensate for declining league attendances. Sponsorship has given it a succession of names, including the Milk Cup, Littlewoods Cup, Coca-Cola Cup, Worthington Cup, Carling Cup and Capital One Cup.

Records

Liverpool are the most successful club, with ten titles; Manchester City won four consecutive finals from 2018 to 2021. Ian Rush and Geoff Hurst share the competition's all-time scoring record.
