UCL (University College London) is a public research university in London, England, and a member institution of the federal University of London. Founded in 1826, it was the first university institution to be established in London and the first in England to admit students regardless of religion, and among the first to admit women on equal terms with men.

Academics

UCL has more than 40,000 students and is consistently ranked among the top universities in the world. Its alumni and staff include numerous Nobel laureates, and it is known for strengths across medicine, law, engineering, architecture (the Bartlett) and the humanities.

Campus

The main campus is in Bloomsbury, central London, centred on the neoclassical Wilkins Building and its portico and dome; the university has expanded with additional sites including UCL East on the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park.
