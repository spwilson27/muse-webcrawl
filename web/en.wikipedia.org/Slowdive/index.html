Slowdive are an English rock band formed in Reading in 1989. The core lineup consists of vocalists and guitarists Neil Halstead and Rachel Goswell, guitarist Christian Savill, bassist Nick Chaplin and drummer Simon Scott. They are regarded as one of the defining acts of the shoegaze genre, known for layered, effects-drenched guitars and ethereal vocals.

History

Signed to Creation Records, Slowdive released their debut album Just for a Day in 1991. Their second album, Souvlaki (1993), was poorly received by parts of the British music press at the time but is now widely considered a classic of the genre. After the more ambient Pygmalion (1995), the band were dropped by Creation and split; Halstead, Goswell and drummer Ian McCutcheon went on to form Mojave 3.

Reunion

Slowdive reformed in 2014 to wide acclaim, playing major festivals and releasing a self-titled fourth album in 2017. A fifth album, Everything Is Alive, followed in 2023. Their reputation has grown substantially since the 1990s, with a new generation of listeners discovering the band through streaming.
