Daily Kos
Type: Political blog / news site
Founded: 2002
Founder: Markos Moulitsas

Daily Kos is a group blog and internet forum focused on the Democratic Party and progressive politics in the United States. Founded in 2002 by Markos Moulitsas, its name derives from his nickname, "Kos".

Content

The site combines front-page articles by staff and community diaries by registered users, election analysis, and polling through its Elections arm. It has been influential in netroots fundraising and activism.

Events

Daily Kos sponsors the annual Netroots Nation convention, which grew out of the Yearly Kos gathering begun in 2006.
