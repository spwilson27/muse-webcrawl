ShowBiz Pizza Place

ShowBiz Pizza Place, often shortened to ShowBiz Pizza or ShowBiz, was an American family entertainment center and restaurant pizza chain founded in 1980 as a competitor to Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre. It was founded by Robert L. Brock and the Creative Engineering company, founded by Aaron Fechter, who produced the chain's animatronic show, The Rock-afire Explosion.

History

Brock, a Holiday Inn franchisee, originally signed a franchise agreement with Pizza Time Theatre Inc. to open Chuck E. Cheese locations, but after seeing Creative Engineering's animatronic technology, he withdrew from the agreement and instead partnered with Fechter to create ShowBiz Pizza Place. The first location opened on March 3, 1980, in Kansas City, Missouri. At its peak in the mid-1980s, the chain operated more than 200 locations across the United States.

The Rock-afire Explosion

The chain's entertainment centerpiece was The Rock-afire Explosion, an animatronic band created by Aaron Fechter's Creative Engineering. The band featured characters including Billy Bob Brockali (a bear who played guitar), Fatz Geronimo (a gorilla keyboardist), Mitzi Mozzarella (a mouse cheerleader), Dook LaRue (a dog drummer), Beach Bear, Rolfe DeWolfe with his puppet Earl Schmerle, and Looney Bird. The characters performed musical shows with synchronized movements and dialogue.

Merger with Chuck E. Cheese

ShowBiz Pizza Place and Pizza Time Theatre both struggled financially during the video game crash of 1983. In 1984, Pizza Time Theatre filed for bankruptcy, and ShowBiz purchased the company, merging the two chains. By 1992, all ShowBiz locations had been converted to the Chuck E. Cheese brand under a process called "Concept Unification", in which the Rock-afire Explosion characters were retrofitted with Chuck E. Cheese costumes and programming.

Legacy

The Rock-afire Explosion has maintained a cult following. In the 2000s, surviving animatronic bands in private collections gained renewed attention through online videos in which the characters performed contemporary songs. A documentary film about the band and its fans was released in 2008.
