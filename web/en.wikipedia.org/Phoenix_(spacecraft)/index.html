Phoenix (spacecraft)

Phoenix was an uncrewed space probe that landed on Mars on 25 May 2008 and operated until 2 November 2008. Phoenix was NASA's sixth successful landing on Mars and the first in its polar regions. The lander completed its mission in August 2008 and made a last brief communication with Earth on 2 November as falling solar illumination made further operation impossible.

Mission

Scientists conducting the mission used instruments aboard the lander to search for environments suitable for microbial life on Mars and to research the history of water there. Its robotic arm dug into the icy soil of the northern plains, and its Thermal and Evolved Gas Analyzer confirmed the presence of water ice, which sublimated away when exposed.

Findings

Phoenix found evidence of perchlorate salts in the soil, alkaline conditions, and observed falling snow from clouds using its lidar. The mission concluded in November 2008 as the Martian winter closed in; NASA declared it complete on 10 November 2008.
