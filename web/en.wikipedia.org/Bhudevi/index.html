Bhudevi

Bhudevi (Sanskrit: "Earth Goddess"), also known as Bhumi, is the Hindu goddess of the Earth and a consort of the god Vishnu. She personifies the earth, fertility, patience and abundance.

Mythology

In the Varaha avatar legend, Vishnu takes the form of a boar to rescue Bhudevi from the demon Hiranyaksha, who had dragged the earth to the bottom of the cosmic ocean. Bhudevi is also the mother of the demon Narakasura, whose defeat by Krishna is celebrated during Diwali in some traditions. In another well-known tale, she is the mother of Sita, who is found in a furrow in the earth.

Worship

Bhudevi is typically depicted alongside Vishnu and the goddess Lakshmi (as Sridevi), notably in South Indian temple bronzes and in the Sri Vaishnava tradition, where she represents forbearance and the devotee's dependence on divine grace.
