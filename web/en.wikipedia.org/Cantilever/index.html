Cantilever
Structural element projecting horizontally, supported at only one end

A cantilever is a rigid structural element that extends horizontally and is unsupported at one end. Typically it extends from a flat vertical surface such as a wall, to which it must be firmly attached.

Applications

Cantilevers are used in bridge construction (for example the Forth Bridge in Scotland), in building overhangs and balconies, in aircraft wings fixed to the fuselage, and in microelectromechanical systems such as atomic force microscope probes.

Mechanics

A load at the free end creates both shear stress and a bending moment at the support, which must resist the resulting tension in the upper fibres and compression in the lower fibres.
