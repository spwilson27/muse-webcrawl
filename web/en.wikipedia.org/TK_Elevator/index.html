TK Elevator

TK Elevator, formerly ThyssenKrupp Elevator, is a German multinational company specialising in the manufacture, installation, and maintenance of elevators, escalators, moving walkways, and related mobility systems. Headquartered in Düsseldorf, it is one of the world's largest elevator companies, alongside Otis, Schindler, and Kone.

History

The company's elevator business grew out of the German steel and industrial conglomerate ThyssenKrupp. In 2020, ThyssenKrupp sold its elevator division to a consortium of Advent International, Cinven, and the RAG foundation for €17.2 billion, one of the largest private-equity buyouts in German history. The business was relaunched under the independent brand TK Elevator in February 2021, with the "TKE" logo.

Products

TK Elevator's products include passenger and freight elevators, escalators, boarding bridges, and accessibility lifts. Its notable innovations include the TWIN system, in which two independent cabins operate in a single shaft, and the ropeless MULTI system, which uses linear motors to move cabins horizontally as well as vertically and was conceived with high-rise and metro applications in mind. Test facilities include the 246-metre Rottweil test tower in Germany. The company employs tens of thousands of people and services well over a million units worldwide.
