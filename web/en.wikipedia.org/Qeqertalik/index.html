Qeqertalik (Greenlandic: Kommune Qeqertalik) is a municipality of Greenland, created on 1 January 2018 from the northern part of the former Qaasuitsup municipality. Its name means "the place with islands" in Greenlandic. The administrative seat is Aasiaat on Disko Bay.

Geography and settlements

The municipality surrounds Disko Bay in western Greenland and includes Disko Island (Qeqertarsuaq). Its towns are Aasiaat, Kangaatsiaq, Qasigiannguit, and Qeqertarsuaq, accompanied by smaller settlements such as Ikamiut, Akunnaaq, and Kitsissuarsuit. The landscape is dominated by mountains, fjords, and the Ilulissat Icefjord lies just north of the municipal boundary in neighboring Avannaata.

Economy

Fishing and fish processing — especially shrimp and Greenland halibut — dominate the economy, alongside hunting of seals and other marine mammals. Tourism based on whale watching, dog sledding, and the Disko Bay icebergs is growing. Like all Greenlandic municipalities, Qeqertalik depends heavily on the block grant from Denmark and on Royal Greenland's processing plants.

Governance

The municipal council is elected every four years; Greenland's 2018 division replaced the huge Qaasuitsup municipality with the two municipalities of Avannaata and Qeqertalik to bring administration closer to residents. Greenlandic is the official language, with Danish also widely used.
