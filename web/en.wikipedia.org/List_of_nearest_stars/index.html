List of nearest stars

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of nearest stars gives the star systems closest to the Solar System, measured in light-years. After the Sun, the closest stars are the three components of the Alpha Centauri system, about 4.2–4.4 light-years away. Most nearby stars are dim red dwarfs invisible to the naked eye; the list also includes sub-stellar brown dwarfs in extended versions.

Measurement

Distances are determined primarily from stellar parallax—the apparent shift of a star against distant background objects as the Earth orbits the Sun—measured with high precision by space observatories such as Hipparcos and Gaia. Within about 25 light-years there are more than 100 known stars and brown dwarfs, and new faint neighbors continue to be discovered, so rankings below the closest few systems change as surveys improve.
