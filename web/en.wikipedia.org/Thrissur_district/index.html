Thrissur district (Malayalam: തൃശ്ശൂർ ജില്ല) is one of the 14 districts of Kerala state in southern India, with its headquarters at the city of Thrissur. Formed on 1 July 1949, it is often called the cultural capital of Kerala because of its temples, festivals and academies of art and literature, and the gold capital of India because of the concentration of jewellery manufacture and trade in the district.

Geography

The district stretches from the Arabian Sea coast in the west to the Western Ghats in the east, covering coastal plains, midland laterite hills and forested high ranges. Rivers including the Bharathappuzha, Karuvannur and Chalakudy flow through it, and the Athirappilly Falls on the Chalakudy River are a major tourist attraction. The Kole wetlands are an important rice-growing and bird habitat area.

History

The region was part of the ancient Chera kingdom and later of the Kingdom of Cochin. Thrissur city was developed in the 18th century by Shakthan Thampuran, the ruler of Cochin, who cleared the teak forest around the Vadakkunnathan Temple and laid out the circular Swaraj Round, making it a commercial and cultural centre. After Indian independence the district formed part of Travancore-Cochin state before becoming part of Kerala in 1956.

Culture and festivals

The district is famous for the Thrissur Pooram, Kerala's largest temple festival, held at the Vadakkunnathan Temple with rival processions of caparisoned elephants, percussion ensembles and fireworks. It hosts the Kerala Sahitya Akademi and Kerala Sangeetha Nataka Akademi (at Thrissur city), the Kerala Kalamandalam at Cheruthuruthy — a premier school of Kathakali and other classical arts — and the ancient drama tradition of Koodiyattam. Guruvayur, with its major Krishna temple, is one of India's most visited pilgrimage centres.

Economy

Thrissur is a major centre of gold jewellery production and banking; several of India's large gold loan and jewellery firms originated here. Textiles, tile-making around Ollur, coir, and remittances from Gulf migrants also contribute significantly to the district economy.
