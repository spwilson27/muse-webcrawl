Basmati

Basmati is a variety of long, slender-grained aromatic rice traditionally grown in the Indian subcontinent, in the Himalayan foothills regions of India and Pakistan.

Characteristics

Basmati is known for its fragrance, delicate flavour and elongation on cooking. Its aroma comes in part from the compound 2-acetyl-1-pyrroline.

Cultivation and trade

India is the largest producer and exporter. Basmati has geographical indication status in India and is the subject of international trade disputes.

Cuisine

It is the rice of choice for biryani, pilaf and other South Asian and Middle Eastern dishes.
