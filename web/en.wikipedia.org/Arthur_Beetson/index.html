Arthur Beetson

Born: 21 January 1945, Roma, Queensland

Died: 1 December 2011, Gold Coast, Queensland

Position: Prop

Arthur Beetson OAM (1945–2011) was an Australian rugby league footballer and coach, widely regarded as one of the finest front-row forwards in the game's history and a pioneering Indigenous Australian sportsman. He was the first Indigenous Australian to captain his country in any sport.

Career

Beetson played for Balmain, Eastern Suburbs, Parramatta and Hull Kingston Rovers, winning premierships with Easts in 1974 and 1975. In 1980 he captained Queensland in the first State of Origin match, becoming an enduring symbol of the series. He later coached Queensland, the Sydney Roosters and Hull KR.

Legacy

Beetson was named in the Australian Rugby League Team of the Century and inducted into the Sport Australia Hall of Fame. The Arthur Beetson Foundation supports Indigenous youth through sport and education.
