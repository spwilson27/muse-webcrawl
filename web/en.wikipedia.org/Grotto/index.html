Grotto

A grotto is a natural or artificial cave associated with modern, historic or prehistoric use by humans. Natural grottoes are often small caves near water that are flooded or liable to flood at high tide.

History

Grottoes were popular features of ancient Greek and Roman sanctuaries, and artificial grottoes became fashionable in Renaissance and Baroque gardens, notably in Italy and France, where they were decorated with shells, pebbles, statues and fountains. The word derives from the Italian grotta.

Religious grottoes

Many grottoes are sacred sites, including rock temples in China and India and Catholic shrine grottoes such as that of Lourdes in France, where an apparition of the Virgin Mary was reported in 1858.
