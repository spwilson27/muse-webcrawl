Landlord

Related concepts: Tenancy, lease, rent, landlord and tenant law

A landlord is the owner of a house, apartment, condominium, land or real estate which is rented or leased to an individual or business, who is called a tenant (also a lessee or renter). When a juristic person is in this position, the term landlord is used. Other terms include lessor, housing provider and owner.

Duties and rights

Landlords typically provide and maintain the property in a habitable condition, carry out repairs, and comply with housing and safety codes. In return they receive rent. The relationship between landlord and tenant is usually governed by a lease agreement and by landlord–tenant law, which varies by jurisdiction.

History

The term derives from the lord of a manor in the feudal system, who held land granted by a monarch and let it to tenants in return for rents and services. Modern usage covers any property owner who lets property, from individual owners of a single flat to large institutional investors.
