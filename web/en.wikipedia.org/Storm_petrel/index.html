Storm petrel

KingdomAnimalia
OrderProcellariiformes
FamiliesHydrobatidae and Oceanitidae

Storm petrels are small seabirds in the order Procellariiformes, comprising the northern storm petrels (family Hydrobatidae) and the southern storm petrels (family Oceanitidae). They are among the smallest of all seabirds, feeding on planktonic crustaceans and small fish picked from the ocean surface, often while pattering across the water with their feet.

Life history

Storm petrels are pelagic outside the breeding season and come ashore only to breed on remote islands, nesting in burrows or rock crevices and visiting colonies at night to avoid predators. A single egg is laid and incubated by both parents. The European storm petrel is famous to sailors; the name "petrel" is traditionally said to derive from St Peter, who walked on water.
