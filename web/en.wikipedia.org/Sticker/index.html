Sticker

A sticker is a type of label: a piece of printed paper, plastic, vinyl, or other material with pressure-sensitive adhesive on one side. Stickers are affixed to objects for identification, decoration, instruction, or expression.

Uses

Common kinds include bumper stickers conveying political or humorous messages, price and barcode labels in commerce, warning and safety labels, decorative stickers for children and collectors, fruit stickers bearing PLU codes, and inspection or registration decals on vehicles. Sticker art and "slaps" are a medium of street art.

History

Self-adhesive labels became practical with R. Stanton Avery's invention of the first self-adhesive label in 1935, using his company Avery Dennison's technology. Pre-gummed and lickable labels preceded them in the 19th century.

Collecting

Sticker collecting and trading — from album stickers issued by companies such as Panini to laptop stickers in technology culture — is a widespread hobby.
