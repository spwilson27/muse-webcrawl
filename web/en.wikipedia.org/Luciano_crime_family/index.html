Luciano crime family

The Luciano crime family, today known as the Genovese crime family, is one of the "Five Families" of the American Mafia in New York City. It traces its origins to the Morello crime family and was reorganised by Charles "Lucky" Luciano in 1931 after the Castellammarese War.

History

Luciano modernised the organisation, helped create the Commission of the Mafia, and built rackets in labour unions, narcotics and gambling. After Luciano's deportation in 1946, the family was led by Frank Costello and then Vito Genovese, whose name it took. Later bosses included Vincent "the Chin" Gigante, who feigned mental illness for decades to evade prosecution. The family has long been considered among the most powerful and secretive in American organised crime.
