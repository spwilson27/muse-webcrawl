Bobby Bowden

Born8 November 1929, Birmingham, Alabama
Died8 August 2021 (aged 91)
Known forHead coach, Florida State Seminoles (1976–2009)

Robert Cleckler Bowden (1929–2021) was an American college football coach. He was head coach of the Florida State Seminoles from 1976 to 2009, building the program into a national power and winning national championships in 1993 and 1999.

Career

Bowden previously coached at West Virginia (1970–1975). At Florida State his teams finished in the top five of the AP poll for fourteen consecutive seasons (1987–2000) and won twelve ACC championships after joining the conference in 1992.

Legacy

He retired with 377 career wins at the major-college level and was inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame in 2006.
