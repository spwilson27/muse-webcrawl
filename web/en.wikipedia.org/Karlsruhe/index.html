Karlsruhe
State: Baden-Württemberg, Germany
Founded: 1715
Population: approx. 310,000
Known for: Seat of the Federal Constitutional Court; radial "fan" city plan

Karlsruhe is a city in southwestern Germany, in the state of Baden-Württemberg near the Franco-German border and the Rhine. Founded in 1715 by Margrave Charles III William of Baden-Durlach, it is famous for its fan-shaped street plan radiating from the palace tower.

History

According to legend, Charles III William dreamed the design of the city while asleep on a hunting trip. Karlsruhe became the capital of the Grand Duchy of Baden. It later emerged as a centre of technology, home to the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), one of Germany's oldest technical universities.

Judicial capital

Karlsruhe hosts Germany's highest courts: the Federal Constitutional Court and the Federal Court of Justice, earning it the nickname "Residence of the Law". See also Baden-Württemberg.
