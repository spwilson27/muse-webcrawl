Tones on Tail

Tones on Tail
Origin: Northampton, England
Years active: 1982–1984
Genres: Post-punk, gothic rock
Members: Daniel Ash, Glenn Campling, Kevin Haskins

Tones on Tail was a British post-punk band formed in 1982 by guitarist Daniel Ash as a side project while he was a member of Bauhaus. He was joined by Bauhaus roadie and bassist Glenn Campling and, after Bauhaus split in 1983, drummer Kevin Haskins. The group became Ash's full-time project until its dissolution in late 1984.

History

The band took its name from a phrase describing the calibration of a tape machine. Tones on Tail released a series of EPs and one studio album, Pop (1984), blending skeletal guitar textures, dub influences and dance rhythms. Their best-known song, "Go!", built around a propulsive bassline and wordless chanting, became an enduring club favourite and was later sampled by Moby for his 1990 single "Go".

Aftermath

In late 1984 Ash and Haskins reunited with former Bauhaus bassist David J to form Love and Rockets. Interest in Tones on Tail has persisted; the band's catalogue was collected on the compilation Everything! (1998), and Ash has occasionally performed the material live.
