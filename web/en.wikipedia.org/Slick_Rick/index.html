Slick Rick


Slick Rick, English-American rapper and record producer

Richard Martin Lloyd Walters (born January 14, 1965), better known by his stage names Slick Rick and Rick the Ruler, is an English-American rapper and record producer. He rose to prominence with Doug E. Fresh & the Get Fresh Crew in the mid-1980s. Their songs "The Show" and "La Di Da Di" are considered early hip hop classics. "La Di Da Di" is one of the most sampled songs in history.

In 1986, Slick Rick became the third artist signed to Def Jam Recordings. He has released five studio albums: The Great Adventures of Slick Rick (1988), The Ruler's Back (1991), Behind Bars (1994), The Art of Storytelling (1999), and Victory (2025). His music has been sampled and interpolated over 1,000 times, in dozens of songs by artists including Eminem, Beyoncé, Mariah Carey, Beastie Boys, TLC, Nas, Miley Cyrus, Kanye West, Black Star, The Notorious B.I.G., Snoop Dogg, and Color Me Badd. In the process, Slick Rick has collaborated with a number of these performers.

Early life

Richard Walters was born in the South Wimbledon district of London to Jamaican immigrant parents. As a 5-year-old, he was blinded in his right eye by broken glass, and consequently often wears an eye patch. In 1976, he and his family immigrated to the United States, settling in the Baychester area of the Bronx, New York City. At Fiorello H. LaGuardia High School, he majored in visual art. There he met Dana Dane during his teenage years, with whom he later formed a rapper duo known as the Kangol Crew.

Career

Walters first gained success in the music industry alongside Doug E. Fresh & the Get Fresh Crew in 1985. Their recording "The Show" (with the B-side "La Di Da Di") became classics in the hip hop genre. His debut album, The Great Adventures of Slick Rick, reached No. 1 on Billboard's R&B/Hip Hop chart, and featured the singles "Children's Story", "Mona Lisa", "Teenage Love", and "Hey Young World". He is widely regarded as one of the greatest storytellers in hip hop.

In 2013, Slick Rick was honored at the BET Hip Hop Awards. In October 2016, he became a United States citizen. On April 23, 2024, he received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.

Legacy

Slick Rick is regarded as one of the most influential rappers of all time. His smooth delivery, narrative storytelling, and distinctive British-tinged accent set a template followed by generations of MCs. "La Di Da Di" and "Children's Story" rank among the most sampled and referenced recordings in hip hop history.
