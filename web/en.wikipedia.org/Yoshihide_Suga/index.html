Yoshihide Suga (born 6 December 1948) is a Japanese politician who served as Prime Minister of Japan from September 2020 to October 2021. He was previously Chief Cabinet Secretary under Shinzo Abe from 2012 to 2020, making him the longest-serving holder of that post.

Early life and career

Suga was born in Yuzawa, Akita Prefecture, the son of a strawberry farmer. He worked his way through Hosei University in Tokyo and entered politics as a secretary to a Diet member before winning election to the Yokohama City Council in 1987 and to the House of Representatives in 1996.

Prime Minister

After Abe resigned for health reasons in 2020, Suga won the Liberal Democratic Party leadership and became prime minister. His government focused on the COVID-19 response, administrative reform, digitalisation, and carbon neutrality by 2050. Amid falling approval ratings he declined to seek re-election as party leader in 2021 and was succeeded by Fumio Kishida.
