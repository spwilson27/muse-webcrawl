Emperor Tenji

Emperor Tenji (626 – 672), also known as Emperor Tenchi, was the 38th emperor of Japan. As Prince Naka no Ōe he helped overthrow the Soga clan in 645 and drive the Taika Reforms; he reigned formally from 668 to 672.

Rise

With Nakatomi no Kamatari he assassinated Soga no Iruka in the Isshi Incident. As crown prince under Empress Saimei he was the effective ruler; after Japan's defeat aiding Baekje at the Battle of Baekgang (663), he strengthened defenses and moved the capital to Ōtsu in Ōmi Province in 667.

Reign

He promulgated the Ōmi Code (668), among Japan's earliest legal codes, and compiled a household register. His death in 672 triggered the Jinshin War between his son Prince Ōtomo and his brother Prince Ōama (Emperor Tenmu).
