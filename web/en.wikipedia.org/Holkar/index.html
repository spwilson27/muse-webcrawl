The Holkar dynasty was a Maratha clan that rose to rule Indore State in central India, one of the most powerful houses of the Maratha Confederacy. Its founder, Malhar Rao Holkar, began as a cavalry officer under the Peshwa and was granted territory around Indore in the 1730s.

Ahilyabai Holkar

After the deaths of Malhar Rao and his son, rule passed to his daughter-in-law Ahilyabai Holkar (1767–1795), celebrated for just administration, public works and temple building across India, including the reconstruction of the Kashi Vishwanath Temple at Varanasi. Indore prospered under her rule from the capital at Maheshwar and then Indore.

Later history

Yashwant Rao Holkar fought the British in the Second Anglo-Maratha War, winning notable engagements, but the dynasty was defeated in the Third Anglo-Maratha War (1817–1818) and Indore became a princely state under British paramountcy. The Holkars ruled until Indore acceded to India in 1948.
