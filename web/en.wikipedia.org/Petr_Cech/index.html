Petr Čech (born 20 May 1982) is a Czech former professional footballer who played as a goalkeeper. Widely regarded as one of the greatest goalkeepers of all time, he holds the record for the most clean sheets in Premier League history, with 202.

Club career

Early career

Čech began his career at Chmel Blšany before joining Sparta Prague and then French club Rennes in 2002, where his performances attracted Europe's biggest clubs.

Chelsea

Čech signed for Chelsea in 2004 for around £7 million. In his first season he kept a record 24 league clean sheets as Chelsea won the Premier League, conceding only 15 goals. In October 2006 he suffered a depressed skull fracture in a collision with Reading's Stephen Hunt; he returned to action wearing a protective rugby-style headguard that became his trademark. With Chelsea he won four Premier League titles, four FA Cups, three League Cups, the UEFA Europa League, and the 2012 UEFA Champions League, in which he saved penalties in the semi-final and final and in the shoot-out against Bayern Munich.

Arsenal

In 2015 Čech moved across London to Arsenal, winning the FA Cup in 2017. He retired in 2019 after the Europa League final against Chelsea, then returned to Chelsea in a technical and performance advisor role.

International career

Čech earned 124 caps for the Czech Republic between 2002 and 2016 — the country's most-capped player — and starred at Euro 2004, where the Czechs reached the semi-finals and he was named in the team of the tournament.

Records and honours

He holds records for the most clean sheets in Premier League history (202), the most clean sheets for a single club (162, Chelsea), and the fewest goals conceded and most clean sheets in a Premier League season (2004–05). After football, he also played ice hockey as a goaltender in British semi-professional leagues.
