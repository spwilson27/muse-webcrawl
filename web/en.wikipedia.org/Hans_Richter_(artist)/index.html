Hans Richter (artist)

Hans Richter (6 April 1888 – 1 February 1976) was a German Dada artist, filmmaker, and writer, a pioneer of abstract film.

Career

Born in Berlin, Richter was wounded in the First World War and joined the Zurich Dada movement. With Viking Eggeling he made some of the earliest abstract films, including Rhythmus 21 (1921). He later made the surrealist film Dreams That Money Can Buy (1947) in the United States, collaborating with Marcel Duchamp, Man Ray, and others.

Legacy

Richter taught at the City College of New York and wrote histories of Dada, including Dada: Art and Anti-Art (1964). See also Dada.
