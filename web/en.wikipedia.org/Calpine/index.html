Calpine Corporation is an American Fortune 500 company based in Houston, Texas. It is the largest generator of electricity from natural gas and geothermal resources in the United States, with operations in competitive power markets.

History

Calpine was founded in 1984 in San Jose, California, by Peter Cartwright and four partners. The company's name is a combination of "California" and "Alpine", a reference to both its home state and the mountainous terrain that suggests the heights the founders aspired to reach. The company grew rapidly through the 1990s by developing natural gas-fired and geothermal power plants.

Calpine owns and operates The Geysers in northern California, the largest complex of geothermal power plants in the world. In 2005 the company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection amid high debt and the downturn in power markets following the California electricity crisis; it emerged from bankruptcy in 2008. In 2017, Calpine agreed to be acquired by an investor consortium led by Energy Capital Partners for about $5.6 billion, and the company was taken private in 2018.

Operations

The company operates a fleet of natural gas-fired combined-cycle plants and geothermal facilities across the United States and Canada, selling wholesale power into markets including California, Texas and the Northeast. It also operates retail energy businesses under brands such as Champion Energy Services.
