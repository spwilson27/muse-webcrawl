Sea mile

Unit of: Length

System: Navigation

Approximate value: One minute of arc of latitude

The sea mile is a unit of length historically used in navigation, defined as the length of one minute of arc along a meridian of the Earth. Because the Earth is not a perfect sphere, the length of a minute of latitude varies from about 1,843 metres at the equator to about 1,862 metres at the poles.

Relation to the nautical mile

The sea mile differs from the international nautical mile, which was fixed by agreement in 1929 at exactly 1,852 metres — approximately the average value of the sea mile. The sea mile (sometimes called the geographical mile in British usage, though that term has other meanings) remained in navigational texts during the transition to the standardised unit.

Use

Navigators traditionally measured distances on charts in minutes of latitude, making the sea mile a natural unit: a vessel's speed in knots corresponded to sea miles travelled per hour.
