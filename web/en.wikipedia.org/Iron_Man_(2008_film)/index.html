Iron Man (2008 film)

Directed by: Jon Favreau

Starring: Robert Downey Jr., Terrence Howard, Jeff Bridges, Gwyneth Paltrow

Release date: May 2, 2008

Budget: $140 million

Box office: $585.8 million

Iron Man is a 2008 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Paramount Pictures, it is the first film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Directed by Jon Favreau from a screenplay by Mark Fergus and Hawk Ostby, and Art Marcum and Matt Holloway, the film stars Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark / Iron Man alongside Terrence Howard, Jeff Bridges, Shaun Toub, and Gwyneth Paltrow.

Plot

Billionaire engineer Tony Stark, CEO of weapons manufacturer Stark Industries, is ambushed and captured in Afghanistan by the Ten Rings terrorist group after a weapons demonstration. Gravely wounded, he secretly builds a powered suit of armour with fellow captive Ho Yinsen and escapes. Returning home, Stark announces his company will stop making weapons and refines the armour, but his mentor Obadiah Stane steals the technology to build a larger "Iron Monger" suit. Stark defeats Stane, and at a press conference publicly admits: "I am Iron Man." A post-credits scene introduces Samuel L. Jackson as Nick Fury, proposing the "Avenger Initiative".

Reception and legacy

The film received praise for its acting (particularly Downey's), direction, and humour, grossing over $585 million worldwide. It is credited with launching the Marvel Cinematic Universe, and was selected for preservation in the US National Film Registry in 2022. Two sequels followed: Iron Man 2 (2010) and Iron Man 3 (2013).
