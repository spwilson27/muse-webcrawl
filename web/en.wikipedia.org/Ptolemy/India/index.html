India, officially the Republic of India, is a country in South Asia. It is the seventh-largest country by area and the most populous country in the world, having surpassed China in population in 2023. Bounded by the Indian Ocean to the south, the Arabian Sea to the south-west and the Bay of Bengal to the south-east, it shares land borders with Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar. The subcontinent appears in the geography of the ancient world — Ptolemy's Geography describes India within and beyond the Ganges — and sustained one of the world's oldest civilisations along the Indus River.

History

The Indus Valley Civilisation flourished around 3300–1300 BC, followed by the Vedic period, during which the foundations of Hinduism were laid. The Mauryan Empire under Ashoka (3rd century BC) united most of the subcontinent; the Gupta Empire (4th–6th century AD) is remembered as a golden age of science and art. Medieval India saw the Delhi Sultanate and then the Mughal Empire, which ruled most of the region in the 16th–18th centuries. European trading companies, above all the British East India Company, progressively colonised India, which became the British Raj after 1858. The independence movement led by Mahatma Gandhi achieved independence in 1947, accompanied by Partition into India and Pakistan.

Government and economy

India is a federal parliamentary republic and the world's largest democracy by electorate. Since economic liberalisation in 1991 it has become one of the fastest-growing major economies, with leading sectors in information technology, pharmaceuticals, textiles, agriculture and space technology.

Culture

India is the birthplace of Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism and Sikhism, and home to extraordinary linguistic and religious diversity. Its cultural heritage includes classical dance and music traditions, yoga, a major film industry centred in Mumbai (Bollywood), and festivals such as Diwali and Holi.
