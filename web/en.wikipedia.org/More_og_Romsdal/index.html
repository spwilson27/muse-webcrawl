Møre og Romsdal

Møre og Romsdal is a county in the northernmost part of Western Norway. It borders the counties of Trøndelag, Innlandet and Vestland, and its coastline faces the Norwegian Sea. The county town and largest city is Molde, while Ålesund is the largest urban area by population.

Geography

The county is traditionally divided into three districts: Sunnmøre in the south, Romsdal in the centre, and Nordmøre in the north. Deep fjords, including the famous Geirangerfjord (a UNESCO World Heritage Site), cut into the mountainous coast.

Economy

Fisheries, shipbuilding and maritime industries, furniture manufacture, and petroleum-related activity are central to the county's economy. Tourism is drawn by the fjord landscape and the Atlantic Road.
