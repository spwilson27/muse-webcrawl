The FIA Formula 3 Championship is a third-tier international single-seater racing series organised by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). Launched in 2019 from the merger of the GP3 Series and the FIA Formula 3 European Championship, it races on the support bill of Formula One Grands Prix and forms the third step of the FIA Global Pathway, below Formula 2 and Formula One.

Format

Thirty drivers, three per team across ten teams, contest a season of about ten rounds. Each weekend includes practice, a qualifying session, a sprint race with a partially reversed grid drawn from qualifying results, and a feature race on Sunday starting in qualifying order. Points are awarded on a scale similar to Formula One's, with additional points for pole position and fastest laps.

Car

All teams use the Dallara F3 2019 chassis powered by a Mecachrome 3.4-litre naturally aspirated V6 producing around 380 horsepower, with Pirelli tyres and a Drag Reduction System (DRS). The car is a spec design intended to keep costs controlled and emphasise driver skill.

Champions

Early champions included Robert Shwartzman (2019), Oscar Piastri (2020), Dennis Hauger (2021) and Victor Martins (2022). Piastri's rapid progression — Formula 3, Formula 2 and then McLaren's Formula One team — is frequently cited as the pathway working as designed.
