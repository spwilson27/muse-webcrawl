Conwy Valley line

The Conwy Valley line is a railway line in North Wales, running from Llandudno to Blaenau Ffestiniog. It is part of the Wales & Borders franchise and is operated by Transport for Wales Rail.

The line runs along the valley of the River Conwy through Betws-y-Coed, passing beneath the mountains of Snowdonia (Eryri) in the long Ffestiniog Tunnel before reaching Blaenau Ffestiniog, where it connects with the Ffestiniog Railway. The section from Llandudno Junction to Betws-y-Coed opened in 1868 as part of the Conway and Llanrwst Railway, and the extension to Blaenau Ffestiniog, built by the London and North Western Railway, opened in 1879.

Services and stations

Stations include Deganwy, Llandudno Junction, Glan Conwy, Tal-y-Cafn, Dolgarrog, North Llanrwst, Llanrwst, Betws-y-Coed, Pont-y-Pant, Dolwyddelan, Roman Bridge, and Blaenau Ffestiniog. Services are infrequent — typically six trains per day each way — and the line has repeatedly suffered flood damage from the River Conwy, most notably closures in 2015, 2019 and 2020, after which sections were rebuilt with flood-resilient works.
