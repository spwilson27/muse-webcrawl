Laudato si'

Author: Pope Francis
Published: 18 June 2015
Subject: Environment, climate change, social justice

Laudato si' ("Praise Be to You") is the second encyclical of Pope Francis, promulgated on 18 June 2015. Subtitled "on care for our common home", it addresses consumerism, irresponsible development, environmental degradation and climate change, and calls for worldwide action.

Title and themes

The title is taken from the "Canticle of the Creatures" of Saint Francis of Assisi, which begins "Laudato si', mi' Signore" ("Praise be to you, my Lord"). The encyclical develops the concept of "integral ecology", linking care for the environment with concern for the poor.

Reception

The document was widely discussed in the run-up to the 2015 Paris climate conference, drawing praise from many environmental and religious leaders and criticism from some political and business figures.
