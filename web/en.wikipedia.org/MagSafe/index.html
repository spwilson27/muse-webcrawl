MagSafe

MagSafe is a series of proprietary magnetically attached power connectors developed by Apple Inc. It was first introduced in 2006 for MacBook laptops, using magnets to hold the charging plug in place so that the cable detaches safely if tugged, rather than pulling the computer off a surface. The name was later revived in 2020 for a magnetic wireless charging and accessory system for the iPhone, based on an array of magnets around a Qi wireless charging coil.

Laptop connectors

Three generations of laptop MagSafe were produced: the original rectangular connector (2006), the slimmer MagSafe 2 (2012), and MagSafe 3 (2021), which returned after Apple temporarily replaced it with USB-C charging. The connectors are magnetically reversible in some versions and incorporate pins for power and signalling.

iPhone system

For the iPhone 12 and later, MagSafe supports up to 15 W wireless charging (later increased on newer models) and a snap-on ecosystem of wallets, cases, and mounts aligned by magnets.
