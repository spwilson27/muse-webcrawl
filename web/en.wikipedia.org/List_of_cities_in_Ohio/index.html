List of cities in Ohio

Ohio is a state in the Midwestern United States. Under Ohio law, a municipality with 5,000 or more residents at the last census is a city, while smaller incorporated places are villages. The following is a list of cities in Ohio, with the largest settlements by population (approximate, 2020 census).
