Death of Osama bin Laden

DateMay 2, 2011 (Pakistan time)
LocationAbbottabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan
OperationOperation Neptune Spear
Conducted byU.S. Navy SEALs (DEVGRU), CIA

Osama bin Laden, founder of the militant Islamist organization al-Qaeda and mastermind of the September 11 attacks, was killed on May 2, 2011 (local time) in a covert operation by United States Navy SEALs at his compound in Abbottabad, Pakistan.

The search

Years of intelligence work focused on bin Laden's courier network. In 2010, analysts identified a compound in Abbottabad with unusual security features, including high walls and residents who burned their trash. Surveillance built confidence that bin Laden lived there, and President Barack Obama authorized a raid in late April 2011.

The raid

In the early hours of May 2, two dozen SEALs of Naval Special Warfare Development Group flew in stealth helicopters from Afghanistan. One helicopter crash-landed in the compound without casualties. The SEALs fought through the buildings, killed several occupants including bin Laden's son Khalid, and shot bin Laden on the third floor. The operation lasted about 40 minutes.

Aftermath

Bin Laden's identity was confirmed by facial recognition and DNA testing, and his body was buried at sea from the USS Carl Vinson within 24 hours, in accordance with Islamic practice. President Obama announced the death in a televised address; celebrations erupted in Washington and New York. Pakistan faced questions about how bin Laden had hidden near its military academy, and the raid strained U.S.–Pakistani relations.
