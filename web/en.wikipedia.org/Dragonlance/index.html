Dragonlance

Cover art from the Dragonlance Chronicles.

Dragonlance is a shared fantasy universe created by Laura and Tracy Hickman and popularised by TSR, Inc. for the Dungeons & Dragons role-playing game. Set mainly on the continent of Ansalon on the world of Krynn, it combines game modules with a long-running series of novels.

Novels

The franchise began in 1984 with Dragons of Autumn Twilight by Margaret Weis and Tracy Hickman, the first volume of the Chronicles trilogy, which follows the companions—including Tanis Half-Elven, Raistlin Majere, Sturm Brightblade and Tasslehoff Burrfoot—through the War of the Lance against the Dragon Highlords of Takhisis. The sequel Legends trilogy centres on Raistlin and his twin Caramon. Hundreds of Dragonlance novels by many authors have since been published, making it one of the largest shared fantasy worlds in print.

Setting

Krynn is shaped by the balance between good, neutral and evil gods, its moons governing arcane magic, and features kender, gully dwarves, draconians and the Knights of Solamnia.
