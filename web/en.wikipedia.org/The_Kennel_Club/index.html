The Kennel Club

The Kennel Club (KC) is the official kennel club of the United Kingdom and the oldest recognized kennel club in the world. Founded on 4 April 1873 by Sewallis Shirley and others, it maintains pedigree records for dogs and licenses dog shows, trials and activities in the UK.

Activities

The Kennel Club maintains the UK's register of pedigree dogs, recognizes over 200 breeds grouped into seven groups (Hound, Gundog, Terrier, Utility, Working, Pastoral, Toy), and owns Crufts, the world's largest dog show, held annually in Birmingham. It also runs the Good Citizen Dog Scheme and campaigns on canine health and welfare.

Controversy

The 2008 BBC documentary Pedigree Dogs Exposed criticized breed standards for harming health; the Kennel Club subsequently revised many standards and introduced health schemes. See also The Open Championship.
