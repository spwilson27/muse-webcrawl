List of states with nuclear weapons

Nine states are generally considered to possess nuclear weapons. Five are recognised as nuclear-weapon states under the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT): the United States, Russia, the United Kingdom, France and China. Four others — India, Pakistan, Israel and North Korea — possess weapons outside or after withdrawing from that framework.

Other states

India tested a device in 1974 and weaponised tests in 1998; Pakistan followed in 1998. Israel is widely believed to possess weapons but maintains deliberate ambiguity and has never publicly tested. North Korea withdrew from the NPT and has conducted six announced tests since 2006. South Africa built six devices and dismantled them before joining the NPT in 1991, the only state to give up weapons it had itself developed. Belarus, Kazakhstan and Ukraine returned Soviet-era weapons stationed on their territory to Russia in the 1990s.

Stockpiles

Global inventories peaked at roughly 70,000 warheads in the mid-1980s and have since fallen to roughly 12,000, the great majority held by Russia and the United States.
