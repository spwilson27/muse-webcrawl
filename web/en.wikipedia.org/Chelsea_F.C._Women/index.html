Chelsea F.C. Women

Chelsea Football Club Women, commonly referred to as Chelsea Women, is an English professional women's football club based in Fulham, London, affiliated with Chelsea F.C. Founded in 1992, the team plays in the Women's Super League (WSL), the top tier of English women's football.

Under manager Emma Hayes (2012–2024), Chelsea became the dominant force in English women's football, winning multiple WSL titles — including five in a row from 2019–20 to 2023–24 — as well as FA Cups and League Cups, and reaching the UEFA Women's Champions League final in 2021.

The team plays home matches at Kingsmeadow and occasionally Stamford Bridge. Notable players have included Sam Kerr, Fran Kirby, Millie Bright and Pernille Harder. Sonia Bompastor succeeded Hayes in 2024.
