Kwasi Alfred Addo Kwarteng (born 1975) is a British politician and historian who served as Chancellor of the Exchequer under Prime Minister Liz Truss from September to October 2022. A member of the Conservative Party, he was Member of Parliament for Spelthorne from 2010 to 2024.

Early life and career

Born in London to Ghanaian immigrant parents — an economist and a barrister — Kwarteng was educated at Eton and Trinity College, Cambridge, where he earned a PhD in economic history and was part of a winning University Challenge team. He worked as a financial analyst and wrote books including Ghosts of Empire (2011) and, with other free-market Conservative MPs, Britannia Unchained (2012).

Chancellorship and the mini-budget

After ministerial roles in the business department, Kwarteng became Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy in 2021 and Chancellor on 6 September 2022. On 23 September he delivered a "mini-budget" of large unfunded tax cuts — including abolishing the 45p top income-tax rate. The statement triggered a sharp fall in the pound, a surge in gilt yields, and emergency Bank of England intervention in pension funds. The top-rate cut was reversed, and Kwarteng was dismissed on 14 October 2022 after 38 days — the second-shortest tenure as Chancellor in modern British history. Truss resigned days later.

Later life

Kwarteng stood down as an MP at the 2024 general election and has since worked in broadcasting, writing and advisory roles.
