Sonic Corporation, founded by Troy Smith, opened its first location in Shawnee, Oklahoma, in 1953. It operates as Sonic Drive-In (stylized as SONIC), an American drive-in fast-food restaurant chain owned by Inspire Brands, the parent company of Arby's, Dunkin' Donuts, Buffalo Wild Wings, Jimmy John's, and Baskin-Robbins, among others.

History

Troy Smith began with a root beer stand called Top Hat in 1953. After visiting a Louisiana drive-in that used speakers for ordering, he adopted the drive-in format with canopied stalls and carhops — famously, in some locations, delivering food on roller skates. The name was changed to Sonic in 1959, with the slogan "Service with the Speed of Sound". Franchising expanded the chain across the American South and Midwest. In 2018, Sonic was acquired by Inspire Brands in a deal valued at about $2.3 billion.

Menu and format

Sonic is known for burgers, hot dogs, tater tots, onion rings, and an extensive drink menu of soft-drink combinations, slushes, and milkshakes, along with the Cherry Limeade. Customers order from their cars through a speaker system and are served by carhops; many locations also have drive-thrus and patios.

Operations

The chain has roughly 3,500 locations, primarily in the central and southern United States.
