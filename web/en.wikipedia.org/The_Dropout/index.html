The Dropout is an American biographical drama miniseries created by Elizabeth Meriwether, based on the ABC News podcast of the same name hosted by Rebecca Jarvis. It dramatizes the rise and fall of Elizabeth Holmes (played by Amanda Seyfried) and her company Theranos, which claimed to have revolutionized blood testing before being exposed as a fraud. The series premiered on Hulu on March 3, 2022, and ran for eight episodes.

Synopsis

The series follows Holmes from her upbringing and brief time at Stanford University — which she left at 19 to found Theranos — through the company's ascent to a $9 billion valuation, its secretive corporate culture, and her relationship with chief operating officer Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani. It depicts the whistleblowing of employees Tyler Shultz and Erika Cheung, the reporting of Wall Street Journal journalist John Carreyrou that exposed the company's deceptions in 2015, and Holmes's eventual criminal trial.

Cast

Amanda Seyfried stars as Elizabeth Holmes, with Naveen Andrews as Sunny Balwani. The ensemble includes Stephen Fry as chemist Ian Gibbons, William H. Macy as Richard Fuisz, Laurie Metcalf as professor Phyllis Gardner, and Sam Waterston as George Shultz. Young Holmes is played by Dylan Minnette in early episodes' flashback material.

Reception

The series received widespread critical acclaim, particularly for Seyfried's performance, which won her the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series and a Golden Globe Award. The series itself was nominated for the Emmy for Outstanding Limited or Anthology Series. Reviewers praised its portrayal of ambition and self-deception in Silicon Valley culture.
