Request for Comments

A Request for Comments (RFC) is a publication in a series from the principal technical development and standards-setting bodies for the Internet, most prominently the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF). RFCs document Internet standards, protocols, procedures, and concepts.

History

The series began in 1969 with memos exchanged by researchers on the ARPANET; RFC 1, "Host Software", was written by Steve Crocker. Jon Postel served as RFC Editor for nearly three decades. RFCs include standards-track specifications as well as informational, experimental, and humorous documents — such as RFC 1149, "IP over Avian Carriers".

Status

Not all RFCs are standards. Those approved as Internet Standards receive an STD number, while the Best Current Practice subseries carries BCP numbers. RFCs are numbered sequentially and are never revised in place; corrections appear in errata or new documents.
