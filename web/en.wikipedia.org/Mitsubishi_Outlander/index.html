Mitsubishi Outlander

Mitsubishi Outlander

Manufacturer: Mitsubishi Motors

Production: 2001–present

Class: Compact crossover SUV

Assembly: Japan; Russia; Thailand; China

The Mitsubishi Outlander (Japanese: 三菱・アウトランダー, Hepburn: Mitsubishi Autorandā) is a compact crossover SUV manufactured by Japanese automaker Mitsubishi Motors since 2001. It was originally known as the Mitsubishi Airtrek when it was introduced in Japan.

The original Airtrek name was chosen to "describe the vehicle's ability to transport its passengers on adventure-packed journeys in a 'free-as-a-bird' manner", and was "coined from Air and Trek to express the concept of footloose, adventure-filled motoring pleasure." The Outlander nameplate was chosen for use in North America, where the vehicle's larger size and altered styling distinguished it from the Japanese model.

Generations

The first generation (2001–2006) was based on the Mitsubishi ASX concept shown at the 2001 North American International Auto Show. The second generation (2006–2012), introduced in markets worldwide, used the company's GS platform and offered V6 power and a larger body. The third generation (2012–2021) introduced a plug-in hybrid variant, the Outlander PHEV, which became one of the world's best-selling plug-in hybrids. The fourth generation, launched for the 2022 model year, is closely related to the Nissan Rogue/X-Trail as part of the Renault–Nissan–Mitsubishi Alliance, and is offered with a standard 2.5-litre four-cylinder engine; a plug-in hybrid version followed.

Outlander PHEV

The plug-in hybrid electric vehicle (PHEV) version debuted at the 2012 Paris Motor Show and went on sale in Japan in January 2013. It combines a petrol engine with two electric motors and a battery pack, offering all-electric driving range alongside conventional hybrid operation, and uses Mitsubishi's Super All-Wheel Control (S-AWC) system.

Motorsport

Mitsubishi campaigned modified Outlanders in endurance and rally-raid events, and the Outlander PHEV competed in events such as the Australasian Safari and the Baja Portalegre 500.
