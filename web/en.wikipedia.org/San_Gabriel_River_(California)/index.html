San Gabriel River (California)

San Gabriel River
LocationLos Angeles and Orange counties, California
Lengthabout 93 km (58 mi)
SourceSan Gabriel Mountains
MouthPacific Ocean at Seal Beach / Long Beach

The San Gabriel River is a mostly urban waterway flowing about 58 miles southward through Los Angeles County and the northwestern corner of Orange County, California. It rises in the San Gabriel Mountains from the confluence of its West and East Forks and empties into the Pacific Ocean at the border of Long Beach and Seal Beach.

Course and dams

Flood-control works built after devastating floods include San Gabriel Dam, Morris Dam and Cogswell Dam in the mountains, and Santa Fe and Whittier Narrows dams on the valley floor. Much of the lower river flows in a concrete-lined channel, and its basin is a major source of groundwater recharge.

Recreation

The East Fork is a popular area for hiking, fishing and camping in the Angeles National Forest, and the San Gabriel River Bike Trail runs along the channel from Azusa to the ocean.
