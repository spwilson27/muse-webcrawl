List of Saturday Night Live guests

Saturday Night Live (SNL), the NBC late-night sketch comedy show created by Lorne Michaels in 1975, features a different host and musical guest each episode. This list covers the guests by season.

Frequent hosts

Alec Baldwin has hosted the most times (17), followed by Steve Martin (16) and John Goodman; the informal "Five-Timers Club" recognises those who have hosted five or more times, including Tom Hanks, Christopher Walken, Drew Barrymore, Tina Fey, Scarlett Johansson, Paul Rudd and Emma Stone.

Notable firsts

  • First host (1975): George Carlin; first musical guest pairing included Billy Preston and Janis Ian.
  • Betty White hosted in 2010 at age 88 after a Facebook campaign, the oldest host at the time.
  • Hosts have included politicians, athletes, musicians and actors, with musical guests ranging from Paul Simon and The Rolling Stones to contemporary pop and hip-hop acts.
