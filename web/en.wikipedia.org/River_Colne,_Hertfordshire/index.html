The River Colne is a river in England that rises near North Mymms in Hertfordshire, flows south-west through Watford and Rickmansworth, and joins the River Thames at Staines in Surrey. It should not be confused with the River Colne in Essex or the Colne in West Yorkshire.

Course

Near Rickmansworth the Colne is joined by the Chess and the Gade, and the Grand Union Canal shares its valley. Downstream the river spreads into a braided course through the Colne Valley lakes, a chain of flooded gravel pits forming the Colne Valley Regional Park, before reaching the Thames via distributaries including the Colne Brook and the Wraysbury River.

Environment

The river is a chalk stream in its upper reaches and supports fisheries and wetland habitats, although it has historically suffered from abstraction and pollution. Its valley is crossed by major infrastructure, including the M25 and the route of High Speed 2.
