Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu

Country: India
Type: Union territory
Capital: Daman
Formed: 26 January 2020 (merger)

Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is a union territory of India, created on 26 January 2020 through the merger of the former union territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. The territory consists of four distinct geographical areas on India's western coast and interior: Dadra, Nagar Haveli, Daman, and the island of Diu. The capital is Daman, and the territory comprises three districts.

History

All four areas were formerly part of Portuguese India. Daman and Diu were annexed by India in 1961, while Dadra and Nagar Haveli were liberated from Portuguese rule in 1954 and formally incorporated into India in 1961. The two union territories were administered separately for decades; the merger, enacted by an act of Parliament in 2019, was intended to improve administration and reduce duplication.

Geography and economy

Dadra and Nagar Haveli lie inland between Gujarat and Maharashtra, while Daman sits on the Gujarat coast and Diu is an island off the southern coast of the Kathiawar peninsula. The territory's economy includes manufacturing — encouraged by tax concessions that drew many factories to Silvassa — as well as agriculture and tourism, particularly beach tourism in Diu and Daman.
