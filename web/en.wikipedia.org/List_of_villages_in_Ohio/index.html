List of villages in Ohio

Granville, a village in Licking County, Ohio.

A village is an incorporated municipality in the US state of Ohio, typically with fewer than 5,000 residents; incorporated places of 5,000 or more are generally classed as cities. Ohio has several hundred villages — around 680 — spread across its 88 counties. This article lists them, generally in alphabetical order with their counties and populations.

Examples

  • Ada — Hardin County
  • Amberley — Hamilton County
  • Antwerp — Paulding County
  • Archbold — Fulton County
  • Bexley-area villages and enclaves around Columbus
  • Chagrin Falls — Cuyahoga County
  • Granville — Licking County
  • Mariemont — Hamilton County, a planned community
  • Put-in-Bay — Ottawa County, on South Bass Island
  • Yellow Springs — Greene County
  • Zoar — Tuscarawas County, a historic communal village

Village government in Ohio is typically headed by a mayor and a six-member council. Some villages have voted to dissolve or surrender their charters in recent decades, reverting to township governance.
