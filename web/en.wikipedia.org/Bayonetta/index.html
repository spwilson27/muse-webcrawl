Bayonetta

Bayonetta (Japanese: ベヨネッタ, Hepburn: Beyonetta) is a character created by Hideki Kamiya, designed by Mari Shimazaki, and voiced by Hellena Taylor (in English, for the first two games). She is the protagonist of the Bayonetta video game series, developed by PlatinumGames and published variously by Sega and Nintendo.

Concept and creation

Bayonetta is a coquettish and mysterious Umbra Witch, and the main protagonist of the Bayonetta series. Kamiya conceived the character as a modern witch who fights with guns attached to her hands and high-heeled shoes, uses her hair as both clothing and a conduit for summoning demons, and embodies a stylish, over-the-top aesthetic. Her design went through roughly a year of iterations, with Shimazaki refining her beehive hairstyle, glasses, and slender proportions.

Appearances

In the first Bayonetta (2009), the witch awakens after a 500-year slumber with no memory of her past and travels to the European city of Vigrid in search of the "Eyes of the World." In Bayonetta 2 (2014), she journeys to the sacred mountain Fimbulventr to rescue the soul of her friend Jeanne from Inferno. In Bayonetta 3 (2022), she faces the Homunculi and the entity Singularity across a multiverse of alternate Bayonettas. A prequel, Bayonetta Origins: Cereza and the Lost Demon (2023), depicts her as a young girl named Cereza. Bayonetta also appears as a playable fighter in Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.

Reception

Bayonetta has been well received by critics, with praise for her confident personality, distinctive design, and the fluid combat she anchors. She has frequently appeared on lists of notable video game characters, though her highly sexualized presentation has also drawn discussion and criticism. She was voted into Super Smash Bros. as the winner of a fan ballot among realizable characters.
