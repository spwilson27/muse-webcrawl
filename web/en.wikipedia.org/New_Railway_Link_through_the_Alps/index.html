The New Railway Link through the Alps (NRLA; German: Neue Eisenbahn-Alpentransversale, NEAT) is a Swiss infrastructure project to build fast north–south railway axes across the Swiss Alps, intended to shift freight traffic from road to rail and shorten passenger journey times between northern and southern Europe.

Axes and tunnels

The project comprises three base tunnels on two axes. The Lötschberg Base Tunnel (34.6 km) opened in 2007; the Gotthard Base Tunnel (57 km, the longest railway tunnel in the world) opened in 2016; and the Ceneri Base Tunnel (15.4 km), completing the flat Gotthard axis, opened in 2020.

Background

The project was approved by Swiss voters in referendums in the 1990s, driven in part by environmental concerns over truck traffic through Alpine valleys, and was financed largely through a dedicated fund fed by a heavy vehicle fee and fuel taxes.
