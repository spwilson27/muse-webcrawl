Gesso

Gesso is a white paint mixture consisting of a binder mixed with chalk, gypsum, or another inert white substance, traditionally applied to wood panels, canvas, and other surfaces to prime them for painting.

Use

In traditional panel painting and gilding, layers of gesso — first coarse gesso grosso, then smooth gesso sottile — are applied and sanded to produce a hard, slightly absorbent, brilliant white ground for egg tempera or oil paint. Modern "acrylic gesso" is a primer made with acrylic polymer emulsion, calcium carbonate, and titanium dioxide, widely used to prepare canvas and board.
