Hannes Bok

Hannes Bok, pseudonym of Wayne Francis Woodard (2 July 1914 – 11 April 1964), was an American artist and writer of science fiction and fantasy, celebrated for his distinctive illustrative style.

Career

Born in Duluth, Minnesota, Bok moved to New York and became a prominent cover artist and interior illustrator for pulp magazines including Weird Tales, Planet Stories, and Famous Fantastic Mysteries. He was mentored by Ray Bradbury, who helped him enter the field.

Writing

Bok wrote fantasy novels including The Sorcerer's Ship (1942) and completed A. Merritt's The Black Wheel. His painting style, influenced by Maxfield Parrish, is noted for luminous colour and intricate detail. See also Weird Tales.
