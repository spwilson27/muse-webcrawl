Chora Church

The Chora Church, or Church of the Holy Saviour in Chora, is a medieval Byzantine church in Istanbul, Turkey, famed for its mosaics and frescoes.

History

"Chora" means "in the country", reflecting its original position outside Constantinople's early walls. The present building largely dates to 1077–1081, with its celebrated decoration added in 1316–1321 under the patron Theodore Metochites, depicting cycles of the lives of Christ and the Virgin Mary.

Later use

Converted into the Kariye Mosque after the Ottoman conquest, its images were covered, then uncovered and restored in the 20th century when it became a museum. It was reconverted to a mosque in 2020.
