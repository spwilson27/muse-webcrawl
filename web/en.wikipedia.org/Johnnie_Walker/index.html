Johnnie Walker is a brand of Scotch whisky owned by Diageo, originating in Kilmarnock, Scotland. It is the world's best-selling Scotch whisky brand, sold in nearly every country, and is known for its colour-coded labels and the "Striding Man" logo.

History

The brand began with grocer John Walker, who from 1820 sold blended tea and whisky in his Kilmarnock shop. His son Alexander developed the blending business, introducing in 1867 "Old Highland Whisky" and the distinctive square bottle and slanted label. Alexander II and George Paterson expanded the range with the colour names that became Red Label and Black Label in 1909, together with the Striding Man figure symbolizing forward motion and the slogan "Keep Walking".

Blends

The core range runs from Red Label and the twelve-year-old Black Label through Double Black, the fifteen-year-old Green Label malt blend, Gold Label Reserve and the ultra-premium Blue Label, alongside limited and experimental editions. The blends draw on stocks from dozens of Scottish distilleries, including Cardhu, Caol Ila and Talisker.

Cultural presence

Johnnie Walker is a fixture of global popular culture, frequently referenced in film, literature and music, and a major sponsor of sporting events including Formula One.
