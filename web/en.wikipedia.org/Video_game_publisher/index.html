Video game publisher

A video game publisher is a company that publishes video games that have been developed either internally by the publisher or externally by a game developer. As with book and film publishing, the publisher typically finances development and marketing, manufactures or arranges digital distribution, and takes a share of revenue.

Role

Publishers may control licensing, localisation, quality assurance, marketing campaigns and release scheduling. Major publishers include Nintendo, Sony Interactive Entertainment, Microsoft Gaming, Electronic Arts, Activision Blizzard, Ubisoft, Take-Two Interactive and Bandai Namco.

Business models

Traditional publishers paid developers advances against royalties. Digital distribution and self-publishing have allowed independent developers to bypass publishers, though publishers remain important for funding, discoverability and console certification.
