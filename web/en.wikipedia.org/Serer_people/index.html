Serer people

RegionsSenegal, the Gambia, Mauritania
LanguagesSerer, Cangin languages, Wolof, French
ReligionsIslam, Christianity, Serer religion (A ƭat Roog)

The Serer people are a West African ethnoreligious group native to the Senegambia region, the third-largest ethnic group in Senegal. They founded the precolonial kingdoms of Sine and Saloum and are known for their resistance to Islamisation in the 11th century, their matrilineal inheritance traditions, and a rich religious heritage centred on the supreme deity Roog.

Culture and history

Serer society historically combined matrilineal (tim) and patrilineal (kurcala) descent. The Serer are associated with the megalithic stone circles of Senegambia, including Wassu and Wanar, now UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Serer religious festivals such as the xooy divination ceremony remain important, and the saltigue priests traditionally preside over them.
