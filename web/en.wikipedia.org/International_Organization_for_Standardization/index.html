International Organization for Standardization

The International Organization for Standardization (ISO) is an independent, non-governmental international organisation based in Geneva, Switzerland, founded in 1947. It develops and publishes voluntary international standards for technology, manufacturing, management and services.

Name

Because the name would differ across languages, the short form ISO was chosen, from Greek isos meaning equal.

Standards

Well-known standards include ISO 9001 for quality management, ISO 14001 for environmental management, ISO 3166 country codes, ISO 8601 dates, and many computing and engineering standards developed jointly with IEC.

Membership

Members are national standards bodies, one per country. Standards are drafted by technical committees of experts and approved by consensus.
