John Wendell Thompson (born April 24, 1949) is an American technology executive. He served as chief executive officer of Symantec Corporation from 1999 to 2009, transforming it into a leading security software company, and later became chairman of the board of Microsoft.

Career

Thompson grew up in Florida and earned a bachelor's degree from Florida A&M University and a master's degree from MIT's Sloan School of Management. He spent 28 years at IBM, rising to general manager of IBM Americas. In 1999 he joined the relatively small security firm Symantec as CEO and grew it through acquisitions, including the 3.5 billion purchase of Veritas Software, into one of the world's largest software companies.

Later roles

After leaving Symantec, Thompson became CEO of the privately held Virtual Instruments corporation. In February 2014 he succeeded Bill Gates as chairman of Microsoft's board of directors, a position he held until 2021. He has also served on the boards of numerous companies and as a trustee of educational institutions.
