MPEG-2

MPEG-2
Type: Video/audio compression standard
Published: 1995
Standard: ISO/IEC 13818

MPEG-2 (formally ISO/IEC 13818) is a standard for the generic coding of moving pictures and associated audio information. It describes a combination of lossy video compression and lossy audio data compression methods, which permit storing and transmitting movies using available storage media and transmission bandwidth.

Applications

MPEG-2 is widely used as the format of digital television signals broadcast by terrestrial, cable, and direct-broadcast satellite systems, and as the video format of DVD-Video discs.

Video coding

The video part (H.262) uses inter-frame prediction with I-, P- and B-frames, discrete cosine transform coding, and chroma subsampling, achieving large compression ratios at broadcast quality.
