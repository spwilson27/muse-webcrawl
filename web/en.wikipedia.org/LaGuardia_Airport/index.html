LaGuardia Airport

LaGuardia Airport (IATA: LGA) is an airport in East Elmhurst, Queens, New York City, serving the New York metropolitan area. Operated by the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, it is the third-busiest airport in the New York system after JFK and Newark, handling primarily domestic flights.

History

The airport opened in 1939 and was named for former New York mayor Fiorello La Guardia, who championed its construction. A federal "perimeter rule" limits most nonstop flights to about 1,500 miles, with exceptions including Denver and Saturday services.

Redevelopment

A roughly $8 billion rebuilding programme begun in 2016 replaced the fragmented old terminals with new Terminal B (completed 2022) and Terminal C concourses, earning international design awards and reversing the airport's long-standing poor reputation.
