Gemini (chatbot)

Gemini is a generative artificial intelligence chatbot developed by Google, based on the Gemini family of large language models. It was initially launched in March 2023 as Bard, built on the LaMDA and later PaLM models, and was rebranded as Gemini in February 2024.

History

Google announced Bard in February 2023 in response to the rapid rise of OpenAI's ChatGPT, releasing it publicly the following month. Initial reviews were mixed, and an error in an early promotional demo — about the James Webb Space Telescope — briefly dented Alphabet's share price. In December 2023 Google introduced the Gemini model family (Ultra, Pro, and Nano), which progressively powered the chatbot; the Bard name was retired in February 2024 and a paid tier, Gemini Advanced, was launched within Google One subscriptions.

Features

Gemini answers questions, drafts and summarises text, writes code, translates languages, and analyses uploaded images and documents. It is integrated into Google products including Search (AI Overviews), Gmail, Docs, Android (as a replacement for Google Assistant in many tasks), and Chrome. More capable versions include "Deep Research" agentic reporting and multimodal "Gemini Live" voice conversation.

Incidents and criticism

In February 2024 Google paused Gemini's image generation of people after the model produced historically inaccurate depictions (for example, racially diverse Nazi-era soldiers), prompting public criticism and an apology; the feature returned with modifications later that year. Gemini, like other chatbots, has also been criticised for hallucinations and political bias.
