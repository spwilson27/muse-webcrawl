List of Polish cities

Poland has over 1,000 towns and cities with official city status. This list presents them, usually ranked by population within each voivodeship.

Warsaw, the capital, has roughly 1.8 million inhabitants in the city proper. Other major urban centres include Białystok, Gdynia, Częstochowa, Radom, Toruń, Rzeszów, Kielce, Olsztyn and Opole.
