Dyson sphere

A Dyson sphere is a hypothetical megastructure that encompasses a star, capturing a large proportion of its radiated energy. The concept was proposed by physicist Freeman Dyson in a 1960 paper in Science.

Concept

Dyson suggested that as civilizations grow, their energy demands would eventually lead them to harvest the output of their star. He proposed searching for artificial infrared radiation — waste heat from such structures — as a way to detect advanced extraterrestrial civilizations.

Variants

The original concept was a loose swarm of independent collectors (a Dyson swarm) rather than a solid shell, which would be mechanically implausible. Other variants include the Dyson ring, the Dyson bubble (held in place by radiation pressure) and partial swarms.

Searches

Astronomers have searched infrared survey data, such as from the WISE telescope, for Dyson sphere signatures. Unusual stars like Tabby's Star (KIC 8462852) briefly attracted speculation, but natural explanations have been favoured. The idea remains a staple of science fiction and SETI research.
