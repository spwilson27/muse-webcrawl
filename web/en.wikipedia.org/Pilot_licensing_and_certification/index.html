FrameworkICAO standards
Main gradesStudent, Private, Commercial, Airline Transport

Pilot licensing and certification is the system by which national aviation authorities authorise individuals to fly aircraft. Licences are issued under international standards set by the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) and entitle the holder to act as pilot of aircraft registered in the issuing state, with privileges depending on the licence grade and ratings held.

Licence grades

The usual progression runs from a student pilot certificate, through the private pilot licence (PPL), which permits flying for pleasure but not for hire, to the commercial pilot licence (CPL) and finally the airline transport pilot licence (ATPL), required to command airliners. Additional qualifications include the instrument rating, multi-engine and type ratings, and instructor ratings. Applicants must pass written examinations, flight tests and aviation medical examinations.

International framework

The FAA regulates licensing in the United States under Part 61 of the Federal Aviation Regulations; in Europe, EASA issues Part-FCL licences. ICAO Annex 1 standards allow licences to be recognised and validated between member states.
