Elon Musk

Elon Reeve Musk (born June 28, 1971) is a businessman and investor known for his leadership of Tesla, Inc., SpaceX, and the social media platform X (formerly Twitter). He was born in Pretoria, South Africa, and later became a citizen of Canada and the United States.

Musk co-founded Zip2 and the online payment company that became PayPal, earning a fortune from its sale to eBay in 2002. He then founded SpaceX, which developed reusable rockets and became the first private company to send astronauts to the International Space Station, and joined Tesla as an early investor and later chief executive, popularising electric vehicles.

He has also founded or co-founded Neuralink, the Boring Company and xAI, and acquired Twitter in 2022, rebranding it as X. His ventures in spaceflight, electric vehicles and artificial intelligence have made him one of the world's richest people and a prominent, frequently controversial public figure.
