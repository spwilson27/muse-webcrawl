Banavie

Banavie (Scottish Gaelic: Banbhaidh) is a small settlement near Fort William in the Highland region of Scotland. It lies at the southern end of the Caledonian Canal, where the canal meets Loch Linnhe, and is overlooked by Ben Nevis, the highest mountain in the British Isles.

Neptune's Staircase

Banavie is best known for Neptune's Staircase, a flight of eight locks on the Caledonian Canal designed by Thomas Telford and completed in the 1820s. The staircase raises boats 19 metres (62 ft) over a quarter-mile and is the longest staircase lock in Scotland, taking vessels about 90 minutes to pass through.

Transport

Banavie railway station lies on the West Highland Line between Glasgow and Mallaig, and a swing bridge carries the railway over the canal. The village also has a pier station, Banavie Pier, formerly used by canal steamers.
