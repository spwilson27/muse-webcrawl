Alfa Romeo

Alfa Romeo Automobiles S.p.A.

Founded: 24 June 1910, Milan, Italy

Founder: Alexandre Darracq (A.L.F.A.); Nicola Romeo

Headquarters: Turin, Italy

Parent: Stellantis Europe

Industry: Automotive

Alfa Romeo is an Italian luxury car manufacturer founded in Milan in 1910. The company began as A.L.F.A. (Anonima Lombarda Fabbrica Automobili, "Lombard Automobile Factory Joint-Stock Company"), established by French businessman Alexandre Darracq, and was renamed Alfa Romeo in 1920 after industrialist Nicola Romeo took control during the First World War.

History

The firm's first car, the 1910 24 HP, was designed by Giuseppe Merosi and immediately entered competition, contesting the Targa Florio in 1911. Under Romeo's ownership the company diversified into aero engines, munitions and railway stock during the war years, before returning to car production and racing in the 1920s. Alfa Romeo's racing department was managed in the 1930s by Enzo Ferrari's Scuderia Ferrari, running the famous P2, P3 and 8C racing cars.

After the Second World War, Alfa Romeo won the first two Formula One World Championships in 1950 and 1951 with Giuseppe Farina and Juan Manuel Fangio driving the Alfetta 158/159. Mass production followed with the Giulietta (1954), Giulia (1962) and Alfasud (1971). Financial troubles led to a takeover by Fiat in 1986–87, and the marque later became part of the Fiat Chrysler Automobiles group and then Stellantis in 2021.

Motorsport

Enzo Ferrari once remarked that he had "a particular admiration for Alfa" and wept at his first victory over it. The marque's racing successes include the first World Grand Prix Championship in 1925, eleven Mille Miglia victories, ten Targa Florio wins, and dominance of early Formula One. The Quadrifoglio (four-leaf clover) badge, first painted on Ugo Sivocci's car in 1923, remains the emblem of its high-performance models.

Design

Alfa Romeo styling is centred on the scudetto triangular grille and has been shaped by coachbuilders including Zagato, Touring, Bertone and Pininfarina. Notable models include the 8C 2900, the Giulia Sprint GTA, the Montreal, the 156, the 8C Competizione, the Giulia Quadrifoglio, the Stelvio SUV and the Tonale.
