Lost Crops of Africa

Lost Crops of Africa is a series of reports published by the US National Research Council (National Academies Press) examining underutilised African food plants with potential to improve food security. The volumes cover grains (1996), vegetables (2006) and fruits (2008).

"Lost" refers not to extinction but to crops long cultivated locally yet neglected by research and policy in favour of introduced staples. Featured species include sorghum, pearl millet, fonio, teff and African rice among grains; baobab, marula, tamarind and safou among fruits; and numerous indigenous leafy vegetables. The reports argue these hardy, locally adapted crops could improve nutrition and resilience in African agriculture.
