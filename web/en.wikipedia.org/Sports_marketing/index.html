Sports marketing

Sports marketing is a subdivision of marketing which focuses both on the promotion of sports events and teams as well as the promotion of other products and services through sporting events and sports teams. In the first case the service marketed is the sport itself; in the second, products or brands are marketed by association with sport.

Elements

Sports marketing draws on sponsorship, licensing, advertising, endorsements by athletes, broadcasting partnerships, and fan engagement through digital and social media. The marketing mix for sport differs from other services because consumption is social, outcomes are uncertain, and fans identify emotionally with teams.

Industry

The sports industry is a major global market encompassing professional leagues, collegiate sport, and events such as the FIFA World Cup and the Olympic Games, both of which generate billions in media and sponsorship revenue.
