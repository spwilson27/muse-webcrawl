NetHack

NetHack
Developer: NetHack DevTeam
First release: 1987
Genre: Roguelike
Platform: Cross-platform

NetHack is a single-player roguelike video game originally released in 1987, descended from Hack (1982) and ultimately from Rogue (1980). The player explores a procedurally generated dungeon to retrieve the Amulet of Yendor from the lowest level and offer it to their god — "ascending" the character.

Gameplay

The game traditionally renders the dungeon in ASCII characters, though graphical tile sets exist. Its depth is legendary: hundreds of monsters, items, and interactions — captured in the community maxim "The DevTeam has thought of everything." Permanent death, hunger, and polymorphing make runs unpredictable.

Development and legacy

NetHack is open-source and still maintained; version 3.6.6 was released in 2020 after the long-lived 3.4.3 era (2003–2015). It is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made and inspired numerous variants such as SLASH'EM.
