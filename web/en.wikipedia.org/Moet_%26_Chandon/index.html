Moët & Chandon

Moët & Chandon is a French fine winery and part of the luxury group LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton SE. Founded in 1743 by Claude Moët in Épernay, in the Champagne region of France, it is one of the world's largest and best-known champagne houses.

History and products

The house popularised champagne across the courts of Europe; its name became Moët & Chandon after Pierre-Gabriel Chandon de Briailles joined the firm in the nineteenth century. Its flagship cuvée is Moët Impérial, and its prestige vintage is Dom Pérignon, named for the Benedictine monk traditionally credited with pioneering champagne-making techniques. The house holds a royal warrant and owns extensive vineyards across the Champagne region.
