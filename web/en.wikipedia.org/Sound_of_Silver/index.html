Sound of Silver

Sound of Silver is the second studio album by the American rock band LCD Soundsystem. It was released on March 12, 2007, by DFA and Capitol Records. Produced by the DFA and written by frontman James Murphy, the album continued the band's blend of dance-punk, electronic music, and post-punk influences.

The album was recorded at Long View Farm in Massachusetts and DFA Studios in New York. It includes the singles "North American Scum", "All My Friends", "Someone Great", and "Time to Get Away". The track "All My Friends" was widely acclaimed and frequently ranked among the best songs of the 2000s; the album closes with "New York, I Love You but You're Bringing Me Down", a ballad about Murphy's ambivalence toward his home city.

Reception

Sound of Silver received widespread critical acclaim, appearing on numerous year-end and decade-end best-album lists, and was nominated for the Grammy Award for Best Electronic/Dance Album. It built on the success of the band's 2005 debut and preceded This Is Happening (2010). Retrospective assessments regard it as one of the defining albums of 2000s indie and dance music.
