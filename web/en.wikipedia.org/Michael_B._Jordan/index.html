Michael B. Jordan

Michael Bakari Jordan (born February 9, 1987) is an American actor and producer. He is known for his roles as Wallace in the HBO series The Wire (2002), Vince Howard in Friday Night Lights (2009–2011), Oscar Grant in the drama Fruitvale Station (2013), boxer Adonis Creed in the Creed films (2015–2023), and Erik Killmonger in the Marvel film Black Panther (2018). The "B." stands for Bakari; he is not related to basketball player Michael Jordan.

Career

Jordan began as a child model and actor, appearing in The Sopranos and taking over the role of Reggie Montgomery on All My Children (2003–2006). His early television roles in The Wire and Friday Night Lights brought critical attention. His breakthrough came with director Ryan Coogler's Fruitvale Station (2013), portraying Oscar Grant, a young man killed by transit police. He and Coogler reunited for Creed (2015), in which Jordan played Apollo Creed's son; he directed and starred in Creed III (2023), his directorial debut. As Killmonger in Black Panther (2018) he played one of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's most acclaimed villains.

Producing and other ventures

Through his company Outlier Society, founded in 2016, Jordan has produced films and series including Just Mercy (2019), in which he starred as attorney Bryan Stevenson, and has championed inclusion riders in production. He became a part-owner of English football club AFC Bournemouth in 2022. He was named one of Time's 100 most influential people in 2020 and People's Sexiest Man Alive the same year.
