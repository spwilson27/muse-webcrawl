Byurakan Observatory

Byurakan Astrophysical Observatory
Location: Mount Aragats, Armenia
Founded: 1946
Founder: Victor Ambartsumian
Main telescope: 2.6 m Cassegrain reflector

The Byurakan Astrophysical Observatory is an astronomical observatory in Armenia, located on the slopes of Mount Aragats near the village of Byurakan, at an elevation of about 1,400 metres. It is operated by the Armenian Academy of Sciences.

History

The observatory was founded in 1946 by the astrophysicist Victor Ambartsumian, who directed it for decades and made it a leading centre for the study of stellar associations and unstable phenomena in galaxies. Markarian's survey, conducted here with a 1-metre Schmidt telescope, catalogued galaxies with ultraviolet excess and remains a standard reference.

Instruments

The principal telescope is a 2.6-metre Cassegrain reflector, commissioned in 1976, supplemented by Schmidt telescopes of 1 m and 0.5 m used for surveys, including the First and Second Byurakan Surveys of faint blue objects and quasars.

Legacy

Byurakan hosted major international conferences, including the 1966 conference on extraterrestrial communication, and remains the principal astronomical institution of Armenia.
