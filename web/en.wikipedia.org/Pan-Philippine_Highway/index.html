Pan-Philippine Highway

The Pan-Philippine Highway, also known as the Maharlika Highway, is a network of roads, bridges and ferry services that connects the islands of Luzon, Samar, Leyte and Mindanao in the Philippines. It forms part of the Asian Highway Network as route AH26.

Route

The highway runs roughly 3,380 kilometres (2,100 mi) from Laoag in the north of Luzon through Metro Manila, Bicol, and the Visayas ferry crossings to Davao City and Zamboanga in Mindanao.

History

The route was conceived in 1965 and progressively upgraded; the San Juanico Bridge between Samar and Leyte, opened in 1973, is its most famous structure.
