Brad Feld

Brad Feld (born December 1, 1965) is an American entrepreneur, author and venture capitalist based in Boulder, Colorado. He is a co-founder of the venture capital firm Foundry and of the startup accelerator Techstars, and is widely known for his writing on startups, venture capital and entrepreneurial communities.

Career

Feld earned bachelor's and master's degrees in management science from MIT. While a student he co-founded Feld Technologies, a custom software development firm that was acquired by AmeriData Technologies in 1993. He then became an active angel investor and co-founded the venture firm Mobius Venture Capital before co-founding Foundry (formerly Foundry Group) in 2006, which has backed companies across the United States.

Techstars and startup communities

In 2006–2007 Feld co-founded Techstars with David Cohen, David Brown and Jared Polis, helping to pioneer the mentorship-driven accelerator model. His book Startup Communities (2012) articulated the "Boulder Thesis" — that entrepreneurial ecosystems must be led by entrepreneurs, take a long-term view, be inclusive and engage the whole community — and became a widely cited framework for regional startup development. He co-founded the Startup Weekend movement and has written or co-written the Startup Revolution series, including Venture Deals with Jason Mendelson.

Writing and advocacy

Feld has blogged at Feld Thoughts since 2004, discussing entrepreneurship, technology and mental health; he has written openly about his own experience with depression and obsessive-compulsive disorder and advocates for mental-health awareness among founders.
