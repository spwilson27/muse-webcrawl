Fire protection

Fire protection is the study and practice of mitigating unwanted fires through prevention, detection, suppression and safe egress. It combines fire engineering, building regulation, firefighting services and public education.

Components

Active systems respond to a fire: alarms, sprinklers, extinguishers and smoke control. Passive measures are built into the structure: compartmentation, fire doors, firestopping and protected escape routes. Organisational measures include fire risk assessments, drills and maintenance regimes.

Regulation and standards

Codes such as the NFPA standards in the United States and the Building Regulations in the UK govern design and management. Fire protection engineers model fire growth and occupant evacuation to demonstrate safety, increasingly using performance-based design.
