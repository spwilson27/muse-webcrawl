Zebra Technologies

Zebra Technologies Corporation is an American mobile computing company based in Lincolnshire, Illinois, that produces marking, tracking, and computer printing technologies. Its products include thermal barcode label and receipt printers, RFID readers and smart labels, rugged mobile computers and tablets, barcode scanners, and software for asset tracking and workforce management, sold to the retail, logistics, manufacturing, transportation, and healthcare industries.

History

The company was founded in 1969 as Data Specialties Inc. by Ed Kaplan and Gary Cless, initially producing high-speed electromechanical products such as paper tape punches and readers. It adopted the Zebra name in the 1980s as it pivoted to on-demand thermal printing for barcoding and personal identification, and held its initial public offering in 1991. The zebra-stripe logo reflects its barcode heritage.

Acquisitions

Zebra grew substantially by acquisition, buying printer maker Eltron International in 1998 and numerous smaller AIDC firms. Its defining deal came in 2014, when it acquired Motorola Solutions' enterprise business — including the Symbol Technologies scanner and mobile computing lines — for about $3.45 billion, roughly doubling the company and making it the largest player in enterprise asset intelligence. Later acquisitions included Xplore Technologies (rugged tablets, 2018), Reflexis Systems (workforce software, 2020), and Fetch Robotics (autonomous mobile robots, 2021).

Products and markets

Zebra's hardware and software are used to identify, track, and manage goods and people along supply chains: warehouse scanning and picking, retail inventory and price checking, patient identification in hospitals, and baggage and asset tracking in transport. The company is a member of the S&P 500 and reports annual revenue in the range of US$4.5–5.5 billion.
