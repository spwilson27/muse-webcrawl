Lake Ashi (芦ノ湖, Ashi-no-ko), also known as Hakone Lake or Ashinoko Lake, is a scenic crater lake in the Hakone area of Kanagawa Prefecture in Honshu, Japan. It is famous for its views of Mount Fuji, its numerous hot springs, and the Hakone Shrine.

Geography and formation

The lake lies in the caldera of Mount Hakone, a volcano that last erupted about 3,000 years ago; that eruption blocked the Haya River and formed the lake. Its shores are mostly undeveloped, lined with cedar forests. The floating torii gate of Hakone Shrine stands in the lake near Moto-Hakone.

Tourism

Sightseeing cruises, including replica pirate ships, cross the lake between Togendai and the towns of Hakone-machi and Moto-Hakone, connecting with the Hakone Ropeway, which offers celebrated views of Mount Fuji on clear days. The lake is also stocked for fishing, notably with black bass and trout.
