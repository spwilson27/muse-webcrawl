Royal Leamington Spa, commonly known as Leamington Spa, is a spa town in Warwickshire, England. Originally a small village called Leamington Priors, it grew into a fashionable resort in the 19th century after the supposed medicinal qualities of its saline spa waters were publicised.

History

The town expanded rapidly in the Georgian and Victorian eras, acquiring elegant Regency terraces, the Royal Pump Rooms (opened 1814) and Jephson Gardens. Queen Victoria granted the "Royal" prefix in 1838. The spa declined in the later 19th century as sea bathing became fashionable, but the town reinvented itself as a residential and commercial centre.

Today

Leamington is known for its wide boulevards and Regency architecture, and is a significant hub for the video game industry, sometimes nicknamed "Silicon Spa". It lies adjacent to Warwick and near the University of Warwick.
