CNC router

A CNC router is a computer-controlled cutting machine related to the hand-held router, used for cutting various materials including wood, composites, aluminium, steel, plastics and foams. Guided by computer numerical control (CNC), it automates the tasks of a traditional router table with greater precision and repeatability.

Operation

A design created in CAD software is converted by CAM software into toolpaths, typically in G-code, which the machine's controller follows. A high-speed spindle carrying router bits moves over a gantry in three axes (X, Y, Z) above a work bed where the material is clamped or held by vacuum. Bit diameter, feed rate and spindle speed are chosen according to the material being cut.

Applications

CNC routers are used in sign making, furniture and cabinet production, mould making, musical instrument manufacture, aerospace and prototyping. Machines range from small desktop units for hobbyists to industrial routers that process full plywood sheets in production lines; many can be fitted with automatic tool changers and multiple spindles.
