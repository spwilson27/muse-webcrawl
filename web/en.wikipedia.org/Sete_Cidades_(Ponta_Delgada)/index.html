Sete Cidades (Ponta Delgada)

The twin lakes in the Sete Cidades caldera

Sete Cidades is a civil parish in the municipality of Ponta Delgada on the island of São Miguel in the Portuguese archipelago of the Azores. The parish lies within the massive volcanic caldera of the Sete Cidades Massif, on the western end of the island, and takes its name ("Seven Cities") from a legend of seven settlements said to have existed or vanished in the crater.

Geography

The caldera, about five kilometres across, contains two large crater lakes, Lagoa Azul (Blue Lake) and Lagoa Verde (Green Lake), which according to legend were formed from the tears of a shepherd and a princess. The parish village sits on the shore of Lagoa Azul. Viewpoints on the caldera rim, notably Vista do Rei, offer the classic panoramic view of the twin lakes. The area is a protected landscape and one of the most visited natural attractions of the Azores.

The volcano

The Sete Cidades volcano is an active polygenetic volcano; its caldera was formed in stages by major eruptions over the last tens of thousands of years, and historical eruptions have occurred offshore nearby. The floor of the caldera contains several small volcanic cones and villages, including Sete Cidades and Mosteiros on the coast.

The parish

The parish church, dedicated to Saint Nicholas, was built in the nineteenth century in neo-Gothic style. The local economy is based on agriculture, particularly dairy cattle grazing on the caldera floor, and on tourism.
