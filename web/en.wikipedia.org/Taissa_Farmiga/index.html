Taissa Farmiga

Taissa Farmiga (born August 17, 1994) is an American actress. She is the younger sister of actress Vera Farmiga. She began her career in her sister's directorial debut film Higher Ground (2011) and rose to prominence as a regular in the anthology horror series American Horror Story.

Career

Farmiga made her acting debut playing the younger version of Vera Farmiga's character in Higher Ground. Later in 2011 she was cast as Violet Harmon in American Horror Story: Murder House, the first season of Ryan Murphy's anthology series. She returned for subsequent seasons including Coven, Roanoke, and Apocalypse, becoming one of the show's most recognizable performers.

On film, she appeared in Sofia Coppola's The Bling Ring (2013), the time-loop thriller The Final Girls (2015), and Warren Beatty's Rules Don't Apply (2016). She gained a new generation of fans playing Sister Irene in the horror films The Nun (2018) and The Nun II (2023), part of The Conjuring Universe — a franchise in which her sister Vera plays Lorraine Warren. She also starred as Nancy in the HBO period drama The Gilded Age.

Personal life

Farmiga was born in New Jersey to Ukrainian immigrant parents. She is the youngest of seven children. In 2020 she married screenwriter and director Hadley Klein.
