Grace Patricia Kelly (12 November 1929 – 14 September 1982), also known as Grace of Monaco, was an American actress and Princess of Monaco as the wife of Prince Rainier III. After beginning her film career in the early 1950s, she became one of Hollywood's leading stars, winning the Academy Award for Best Actress for The Country Girl (1954) and starring in three Alfred Hitchcock films: Dial M for Murder, Rear Window (both 1954) and To Catch a Thief (1955).

Film career

Kelly made her film debut in Fourteen Hours (1951) and gained attention in High Noon (1952). She won a Golden Globe and an Oscar nomination for Mogambo (1953), then the Best Actress Oscar the following year. Her cool, elegant screen persona made her one of Hitchcock's archetypal leading ladies.

Princess of Monaco

At the height of her career she married Prince Rainier III on 19 April 1956, in what the press called "the wedding of the century", and retired from acting. The couple had three children, including Albert II, the reigning Prince of Monaco. As princess she undertook extensive charitable and cultural work.

Death

On 13 September 1982 Kelly suffered a stroke while driving, and her car left the road on the Grande Corniche above Monaco. She died of her injuries the following day, aged 52.
