Ministry of National Defense of the People's Republic of China

Ministry of National Defense
CountryPeople's Republic of China
Formed1954
HeadquartersBeijing
RelationUnder the State Council; the PLA is directed by the Central Military Commission

The Ministry of National Defense (国防部) is the second-ranked executive department under the State Council of the People's Republic of China. Unlike defence ministries in many countries, it does not exercise operational command over the armed forces; command authority over the People's Liberation Army rests with the Central Military Commission.

Functions

The ministry handles military diplomacy and liaison with foreign armed forces, administers matters such as conscription and mobilization frameworks through state organs, and serves as the public face of China's military in international exchanges. Its spokespersons issue statements on defence policy and military activities.

The minister

The Minister of National Defense is customarily a senior PLA general and a member of the Central Military Commission. The post is largely diplomatic and ceremonial in function despite its holder's high military rank, a structure that has occasionally caused confusion abroad about who wields real authority in a crisis.
