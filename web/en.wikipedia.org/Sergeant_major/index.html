Sergeant major

Sergeant major is a senior non-commissioned rank or appointment in many militaries around the world. In Commonwealth forces, it is an appointment held by a warrant officer at unit level; in the United States armed forces, it is a distinct enlisted rank above first sergeant.

History

The title dates to the 16th century, when the sergeant major was a senior officer responsible for drill, training, and the arrangement of an army on the march—the "major" element reflecting staff duties. Over time the role passed to senior non-commissioned personnel.

Modern roles

Today sergeant majors typically serve as the senior enlisted advisor to a commanding officer, responsible for discipline, welfare, and standards. The United States Army maintains the office of Sergeant Major of the Army, the service's senior enlisted representative.
