Korn (stylised as KoЯn) is an American nu metal band from Bakersfield, California, formed in 1993. The band is notable for pioneering the nu metal genre and bringing it into the mainstream.

History

Korn's lineup includes founding members James "Munky" Shaffer (guitar), Reginald "Fieldy" Arvizu (bass), Brian "Head" Welch (guitar), and Jonathan Davis (vocals), with Ray Luzier on drums since 2007. Their self-titled debut album (1994) featured the singles "Blind" and "Shoots and Ladders". The band achieved mainstream success with Follow the Leader (1998), which debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and included "Freak on a Leash" and "Got the Life".

Style and influence

Korn's sound combines downtuned seven-string guitars, slap bass, hip hop rhythms and Davis's scat singing and emotionally raw lyrics addressing abuse, alienation and addiction. The band has sold more than 40 million records worldwide and won two Grammy Awards.
