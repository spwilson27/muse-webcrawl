List of deaths in rock and roll

The sudden deaths of rock and roll musicians — particularly at young ages and in accidents — have long been a subject of public fascination and mythology. The following is a selective list of notable deaths of rock and popular musicians, ordered chronologically. (Entries below reflect well-known historical facts.)

The 27 Club

The recurring deaths of prominent musicians at age 27 — Jones, Hendrix, Joplin, Morrison, Cobain, and Winehouse among them — gave rise to the cultural notion of the "27 Club", though statistical studies find no unusual mortality peak at that age for musicians generally.
