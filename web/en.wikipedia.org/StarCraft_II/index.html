StarCraft II

StarCraft II is a military science fiction video game created by Blizzard Entertainment as the sequel to the highly successful StarCraft (1998). Set in a fictitious timeline of the 26th century, the game centers around three species fighting for dominance across the Milky Way: the terrans, human exiles from Earth; the zerg, a race of insectoids obsessed with assimilating other species; and the protoss, a technologically advanced race with psionic abilities.

Release structure

The game was released as a trilogy: the base game Wings of Liberty (2010), focusing on the terrans; the expansion Heart of the Swarm (2013), centered on the zerg; and the standalone expansion Legacy of the Void (2015), following the protoss. Each part features a single-player campaign with branching missions and between-mission exploration, alongside shared competitive multiplayer through Battle.net.

Gameplay

StarCraft II is a real-time strategy game in which players harvest minerals and vespene gas, construct bases and armies, and attempt to destroy their opponents' structures. Its three asymmetric factions are finely balanced, and the game became a flagship esport, particularly in South Korea, with professional leagues, large prize pools and a ladder system. In 2017 the base game became free-to-play. The editor shipped with the game enabled an extensive custom-map and modding community.

Reception

The games received widespread critical acclaim for their campaign design, multiplayer balance and production values, and sold millions of copies, with Wings of Liberty selling over 1.5 million copies in its first two days. StarCraft II is frequently cited as one of the greatest real-time strategy games of all time.
