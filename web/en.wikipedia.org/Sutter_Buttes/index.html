Sutter Buttes

The Sutter Buttes are a small circular complex of eroded volcanic lava domes rising abruptly from the flat agricultural plains of the Sacramento Valley in Sutter County, California. Sometimes called the "world's smallest mountain range", they cover about 75 square miles.

Geology

The buttes formed about 1.6 million years ago from volcanic activity associated with the Cascade Range. A ring of rhyolitic and andesitic domes surrounds a central core; the highest point, South Butte, reaches about 648 metres (2,126 feet).

Significance

The range is sacred to the Maidu and Wintun peoples, who know it as a place of spirits. Much of the land is privately held ranchland, with a portion protected as state park land, and guided hikes are offered by local conservancies.
