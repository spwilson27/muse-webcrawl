List of Pokémon Trading Card Game sets

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) was first released in Japan in 1996 by Media Factory and internationally from 1999 by Wizards of the Coast, and later by The Pokémon Company International. Cards are released in themed expansion sets.

Early sets

The international game launched with the Base Set (1999), followed by Jungle, Fossil, Team Rocket and the Gym Heroes/Gym Challenge sets, and the Neo series tied to Pokémon Gold and Silver.

Later series

Subsequent generations — eX, Diamond & Pearl, Black & White, XY, Sun & Moon, Sword & Shield and Scarlet & Violet — each introduced new mechanics such as Pokémon-ex, Mega Evolutions, GX, V/VMAX and ex cards, with dozens of expansions in each era.
