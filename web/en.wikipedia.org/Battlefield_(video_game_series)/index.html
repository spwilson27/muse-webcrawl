Battlefield (video game series)

Battlefield is a first-person shooter video game franchise developed by Swedish studio DICE and published by Electronic Arts (EA). The series is known for large-scale multiplayer battles with up to 128 players, combined-arms combat using infantry, tanks, aircraft and naval vessels, squad-based play and destructible environments. The first game, Battlefield 1942, was released in 2002.

Gameplay

Battlefield games centre on team-based multiplayer matches on large maps, classically in the Conquest mode, where teams capture and hold control points while draining the enemy's tickets. Players choose soldier classes with distinct roles and can crew vehicles ranging from jeeps and tanks to jets and helicopters. Later entries added extensive environmental destruction ("Levolution" in Battlefield 4), single-player campaigns, and modes such as Rush, Operations and battle-royale variants.

History

The series began with Battlefield 1942 (2002), set in the Second World War, followed by Battlefield Vietnam (2004) and the modern-warfare Battlefield 2 (2005). The Bad Company spin-offs (2008, 2010) introduced the Frostbite engine and fully destructible environments. Battlefield 3 (2011) and Battlefield 4 (2013) returned to modern settings; Battlefield 1 (2016) took the series to the First World War and Battlefield V (2018) back to the Second. Battlefield 2042 (2021) moved to a near-future setting with 128-player matches, launching to a mixed reception before extensive post-launch updates.

Reception

The franchise has sold tens of millions of copies worldwide and is regarded as one of the defining multiplayer shooter series, frequently positioned as a large-scale alternative to Call of Duty.
