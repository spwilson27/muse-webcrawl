Chemosphere

The chemosphere is the region of the upper atmosphere, roughly from 30 to 100 km altitude, in which photochemical reactions driven by solar ultraviolet radiation dominate atmospheric chemistry. It overlaps the upper stratosphere and the mesosphere.

Processes

In the chemosphere, intense ultraviolet light dissociates molecules such as oxygen and water vapour, producing reactive species including atomic oxygen, hydroxyl radicals and ozone. These reactions govern the formation and destruction of the ozone layer and the chemistry of the mesosphere.

Terminology

The term was introduced in the mid-20th century as atmospheric scientists distinguished the chemically active upper atmosphere from the lower, weather-dominated troposphere and lower stratosphere.
