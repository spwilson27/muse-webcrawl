Lennon–McCartney

Lennon–McCartney is the songwriting partnership of John Lennon and Paul McCartney of the Beatles, one of the most successful and influential in popular music. Under an agreement made before the band's fame, songs written by either or both were credited jointly to Lennon–McCartney.

The partnership

Lennon and McCartney met in Liverpool in 1957 and wrote many early Beatles songs together, "eyeball to eyeball". As the 1960s progressed they increasingly wrote separately while still contributing ideas to each other's compositions. Their catalogue includes classics such as "A Day in the Life", "Yesterday", "Hey Jude", "Let It Be" and "Strawberry Fields Forever", and their contrasting styles helped define the Beatles' sound.
