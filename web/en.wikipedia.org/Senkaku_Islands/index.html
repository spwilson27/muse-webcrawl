Senkaku Islands

The Senkaku Islands are a group of uninhabited islands in the East China Sea, administered by Japan and also claimed by the People's Republic of China (which calls them the Diaoyu Islands) and by Taiwan (as the Diaoyutai Islands).

Geography

The group comprises five volcanic islands and three rocky outcrops, lying about 170 km northeast of Taiwan and 330 km southeast of mainland China, with a total land area of roughly 7 km². The largest island is Uotsurijima.

Dispute

Japan incorporated the islands in 1895, maintaining they were terra nullius; China contends they were part of its territory ceded to Japan by the Treaty of Shimonoseki and should have reverted after the Second World War. The dispute intensified after Japan nationalised three of the islands in 2012, prompting regular patrols by Chinese coast guard vessels and making the islands a persistent flashpoint in Sino-Japanese relations.
