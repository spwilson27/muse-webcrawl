Red Bull

Red Bull is an energy drink created and owned by the Austrian company Red Bull GmbH. Launched in Austria in 1987, it is the best-selling energy drink in the world, with billions of cans sold annually in more than 170 countries. A standard 250 ml can contains about 80 mg of caffeine — roughly the same as a cup of coffee — together with taurine, B-group vitamins, and sugars (sugar-free versions use sweeteners).

History

The drink was created by Austrian entrepreneur Dietrich Mateschitz, who encountered the Thai energy drink Krating Daeng ("red bull" in Thai) while travelling in Asia in 1982 and found that it relieved his jet lag. Mateschitz partnered with Krating Daeng's creator, Chaleo Yoovidhya, adapting the formula and marketing for Western markets. The two families have retained ownership of the private company, headquartered in Fuschl am See, Austria.

Marketing and sport

Red Bull is famous for its marketing slogan "Red Bull gives you wings" and for building its brand through extreme sports and event ownership rather than conventional advertising alone. The company owns or sponsors Formula One teams (Red Bull Racing and Racing Bulls), football clubs including RB Leipzig, FC Red Bull Salzburg, and the New York Red Bulls, ice hockey teams, and events such as the Red Bull Air Race, the Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series, and the 2012 Red Bull Stratos stratospheric jump by Felix Baumgartner. It also operates media businesses, including Red Bull TV and the Red Bulletin magazine.
