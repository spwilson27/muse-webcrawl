The Philippine Navy is the naval warfare service branch of the Armed Forces of the Philippines. It has an estimated personnel strength of about 25,000, including the Philippine Marine Corps, and is responsible for defending the maritime territory of the Philippine archipelago.

History

A revolutionary navy was improvised during the Philippine Revolution in 1898. The modern service descends from the Offshore Patrol established in 1939 and reconstituted after the Second World War. For much of the postwar period the fleet consisted of ex-American patrol vessels used for internal security and transport.

Modernization

Since 2016 the Navy has commissioned its largest-ever warships, the Tarlac-class landing platform docks built in Indonesia and the José Rizal-class guided missile frigates built in South Korea, alongside new corvettes and offshore patrol vessels, as the Philippines responds to maritime disputes in the South China Sea (West Philippine Sea).
