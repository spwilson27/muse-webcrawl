Integrated device manufacturer

An integrated device manufacturer (IDM) is a semiconductor company that designs, manufactures and sells its own integrated circuit products. This contrasts with fabless companies, which design chips but outsource fabrication to foundries, and with merchant foundries, which manufacture chips designed by others.

Characteristics

IDMs control the whole production chain, from design through wafer fabrication, assembly and test. This integration can allow close optimisation of design and process technology, but it requires enormous capital expenditure to build and maintain leading-edge fabrication plants ("fabs").

Examples and trends

Classic IDMs include Intel, Samsung, Texas Instruments, Micron and SK Hynix. Over time many former IDMs, such as AMD, spun off their manufacturing arms and became fabless, concentrating fabrication in a few large foundries such as TSMC. Some IDMs also sell foundry services, blurring the categories.
