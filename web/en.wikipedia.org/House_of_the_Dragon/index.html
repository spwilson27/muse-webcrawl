House of the Dragon

House of the Dragon is an American fantasy drama television series created by Ryan Condal and George R. R. Martin for HBO. A prequel to Game of Thrones (2011–2019), it is based on parts of Martin's 2018 novel Fire & Blood and depicts the beginning of the end of House Targaryen, culminating in the civil war known as the Dance of the Dragons.

The series chronicles the Targaryen civil war, the Dance of the Dragons.

Premise

Set almost 200 years before the events of Game of Thrones and about 170 years after the Targaryen conquest of Westeros, the series follows the reign of King Viserys I Targaryen and the succession crisis that follows his decision to name his daughter Rhaenyra as heir, pitting her supporters (the Blacks) against those of her half-brother Aegon II (the Greens).

Cast and production

The ensemble cast includes Paddy Considine as Viserys I, Emma D'Arcy and Milly Alcock as Rhaenyra Targaryen, Matt Smith as Daemon Targaryen, Olivia Cooke and Emily Carey as Alicent Hightower, and Rhys Ifans as Otto Hightower. The first season premiered in August 2022 to strong ratings and won the Golden Globe Award for Best Television Series – Drama. A second season premiered in June 2024, with further seasons planned to conclude the story of the Dance.

Reception

The series was praised for its performances, production values, and visual effects, and became one of HBO's most-watched premieres.
