Christian Coleman (born 1996) is an American sprinter who competes in the 60 metres, 100 metres, and 200 metres. He won the 100 m gold medal at the 2019 World Championships in Doha and is a former world record holder in the indoor 60 m.

Career

Coleman starred at the University of Tennessee, sweeping the NCAA indoor and outdoor sprint titles in 2017. Turning professional, he set the 60 m world record of 6.34 seconds in 2018, breaking Maurice Greene's longstanding mark. At the 2019 World Championships he won the 100 m in 9.76 seconds and anchored the United States to gold in the 4 × 100 m relay.

Suspension and return

In 2020 Coleman received a two-year ban, later reduced to 18 months, for whereabouts failures under anti-doping rules, causing him to miss the Tokyo Olympics. He returned to competition in 2022, winning another world 4 × 100 m relay title with the US team in 2023.
