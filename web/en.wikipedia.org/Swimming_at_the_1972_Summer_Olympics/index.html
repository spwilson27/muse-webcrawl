Swimming at the 1972 Summer Olympics

Image placeholder: the Munich Olympic swimming hall, 1972.

The swimming competition at the 1972 Summer Olympics in Munich, West Germany, took place from 28 August to 4 September 1972 at the Olympia Schwimmhalle. A total of 29 events were contested — 15 for men and 14 for women — and 532 swimmers from 52 nations participated.

Mark Spitz

The Games are best remembered for the performance of American swimmer Mark Spitz, who won seven gold medals, each in world-record time: the 100 m and 200 m freestyle, the 100 m and 200 m butterfly, and three relays. His seven golds at a single Olympics stood as a record until Michael Phelps won eight in 2008.

Other highlights

Australian teenager Shane Gould won three gold medals, a silver and a bronze in women's freestyle events. Sweden's Gunnar Larsson won both individual medley events; his 400 m medley victory over American Tim McKee by two thousandths of a second led to the elimination of thousandths-of-a-second timing in swimming. East German and Soviet swimmers also emerged strongly, and Roland Matthes of East Germany successfully defended both of his backstroke titles. American Rick DeMont won the 400 m freestyle but was disqualified after testing positive for ephedrine contained in his asthma medication.
