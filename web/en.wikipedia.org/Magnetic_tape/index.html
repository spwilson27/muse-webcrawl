Magnetic tape

Type: Magnetic storage medium
Introduced: 1928 (audio, Fritz Pfleumer)
Uses: Audio, video and data recording

Magnetic tape is a medium for magnetic storage made of a thin, magnetizable coating on a long, narrow strip of plastic film. It was developed in Germany in 1928, based on earlier magnetic wire recording, and revolutionised audio recording, broadcasting, video and computer data storage.

Formats

Tape has been packaged as open reels, compact cassettes, VHS and other video cassettes, and data cartridges such as LTO. Audio tape recording was pioneered commercially by Bing Crosby's investments in Ampex in the United States after the Second World War.

Data storage

Although access is sequential, magnetic tape remains in use for archival data storage because of its low cost per bit, durability and high capacity, particularly in the Linear Tape-Open (LTO) format.
