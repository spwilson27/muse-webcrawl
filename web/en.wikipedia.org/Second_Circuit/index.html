United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit

The United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit (the Second Circuit) is one of the thirteen United States courts of appeals. It hears appeals from the federal district courts in Connecticut, New York and Vermont, and from certain federal agencies.

Jurisdiction and seat

The court sits principally at the Thurgood Marshall United States Courthouse in Manhattan, New York City. Its jurisdiction covers the District of Connecticut, the Eastern, Northern, Southern and Western Districts of New York, and the District of Vermont. Congress has authorised thirteen active judgeships.

Significance

Because the Southern District of New York covers Manhattan's financial district, the Second Circuit is especially influential in securities law, banking, copyright and other commercial fields. Famous judges of the court have included Learned Hand, Henry Friendly and Thurgood Marshall, who served on it before becoming Solicitor General and then a Supreme Court justice.
