Aberdeen Bestiary

The Aberdeen Bestiary is a 12th-century English illuminated manuscript bestiary that was first documented in 1542 in the Royal Library at the Palace of Westminster. It is now held by the University of Aberdeen (MS 24) and is considered one of the finest surviving examples of a medieval bestiary.

The manuscript contains descriptions and moralised interpretations of animals, birds, snakes, stones, and plants, drawing on the tradition of the Physiologus. Its richly gilded miniatures include a famous series of Creation scenes and depictions of creatures both real and fabulous, from the lion and elephant to the unicorn and dragon.
