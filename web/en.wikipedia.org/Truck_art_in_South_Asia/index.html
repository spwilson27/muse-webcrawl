Truck art in South Asia

Truck art
RegionPakistan, India, Bangladesh
MediumPainted trucks and buses
FeaturesFloral patterns, calligraphy, landscapes, portraits

Truck art in South Asia is a popular form of regional decoration found on trucks and buses in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh, famous for its vibrant colours, intricate floral patterns, poetry and calligraphy, depictions of landscapes, animals, religious symbols and portraits.

Pakistani truck art

Pakistan's decorated trucks — sometimes described as moving art galleries — are painted by specialist workshops, notably in Karachi, and often carry ornate wooden prows above the cab (the taj or crown), reflective stickers, chains and bells believed to ward off the evil eye. Owners invest heavily in decoration as a matter of pride and to attract business.

Influence

The style has influenced fashion, advertising and gallery art internationally, and decorated buses are iconic in cities such as Peshawar and in Afghan transport.
