Uruguay
Capital: Montevideo
Population: ~3.4 million
Currency: Uruguayan peso (UYU)
Official language: Spanish

Uruguay, officially the Oriental Republic of Uruguay, is a country in the southeastern region of South America, bordered by Argentina to the west and southwest, Brazil to the north and northeast, and the Atlantic Ocean and Río de la Plata to the south.

History

Uruguay was contested between Spain and Portugal in the colonial era, then invaded by Brazil and annexed as the Cisplatina Province. Independence was achieved in 1828 after the Cisplatine War, mediated by Britain. The country is known for early 20th-century social reforms under José Batlle y Ordóñez and for winning the first FIFA World Cup in 1930 and again in 1950.

Economy and society

Uruguay has a high-income economy based on agriculture (beef, soy, cellulose), services, and a growing software sector. It consistently ranks highly in Latin America for democracy, low corruption, and social welfare.
