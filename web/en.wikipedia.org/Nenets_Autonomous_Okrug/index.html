Nenets Autonomous Okrug

The Nenets Autonomous Okrug (Russian: Не́нецкий автоно́мный о́круг), also known as Nenetsia, is a federal subject of Russia and an autonomous okrug of Arkhangelsk Oblast. Its administrative centre is Naryan-Mar. With an area of about 177,000 km² and a population of roughly 45,000, it is one of Russia's least densely populated regions.

Geography

The okrug lies in the northeast of European Russia on the Barents Sea coast, largely within the Arctic tundra of the Pechora lowland and the Timan Ridge. It includes the Kanin Peninsula and Kolguyev and Vaygach islands.

People and economy

The indigenous Nenets, traditionally reindeer herders, make up a substantial minority of the population; ethnic Russians form the majority. The economy is dominated by oil and gas extraction — the okrug sits atop major Timan-Pechora petroleum fields — which gives it one of the highest gross regional products per capita in Russia, alongside persistent questions about revenue sharing and the impact of extraction on nomadic herding and tundra ecology. A 2020 proposal to merge the okrug into Arkhangelsk Oblast was abandoned after protests.
