The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is a 2023 action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. It is a sequel to The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild (2017) and is set in the land of Hyrule, expanded with floating sky islands and an underground region known as the Depths.

Gameplay

The game retains the open-world exploration of its predecessor while adding new abilities for Link, including Ultrahand, which lets players attach objects together to build vehicles and devices; Fuse, which combines weapons with materials; Recall, which rewinds an object's motion; and Ascend, which allows Link to pass through ceilings.

Plot

After an event known as the Upheaval, Link and Princess Zelda confront an ancient evil connected to Ganondorf, the Demon King. Link embarks on a journey across the surface, skies, and depths of Hyrule to restore the Master Sword and aid the sages of Hyrule's regions.

Reception

Tears of the Kingdom received universal acclaim from critics and sold more than ten million copies in its first three days. It won several awards, including Best Action/Adventure Game at The Game Awards 2023.
