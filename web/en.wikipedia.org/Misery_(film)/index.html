Misery (film)

Directed byRob Reiner
Screenplay byWilliam Goldman
Based onMisery by Stephen King
StarringJames Caan; Kathy Bates
Release date1990
GenrePsychological thriller

Misery is a 1990 American psychological thriller film directed by Rob Reiner, based on Stephen King's 1987 novel of the same name. James Caan plays romance novelist Paul Sheldon, who crashes his car in a Colorado snowstorm and is rescued by Annie Wilkes (Kathy Bates), a former nurse who describes herself as his "number one fan." On learning that Sheldon has killed off her beloved character Misery Chastain in his latest book, Wilkes holds him captive in her isolated home and forces him to write a new novel resurrecting the character — escalating to infamous acts of violence when he resists or tries to escape.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success, praised as one of the finest Stephen King adaptations. Kathy Bates won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her performance, the only Oscar ever won by a film based on a King novel. The "hobbling" scene, in which Wilkes breaks Sheldon's ankles with a sledgehammer, has become one of the most notorious sequences in popular cinema, and the film is regularly ranked among the greatest thrillers.
