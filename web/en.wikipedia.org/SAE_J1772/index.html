SAE J1772

TypeElectric vehicle charging connector
StandardSAE J1772
RegionNorth America, Japan
Nickname"J plug"

SAE J1772 is a North American standard for the electrical connectors used to charge plug-in electric vehicles. The round five-pin connector defined by the standard, often called the J plug or Type 1 connector, is used for AC Level 1 (120 V) and Level 2 (240 V) charging.

Design

The connector carries two AC power pins, a protective earth pin, a proximity detection pin and a control pilot pin. The pilot signal communicates the available current to the vehicle and ensures the connector is not energised until safely latched; a latch and proximity switch prevent driving away while plugged in. The standard supports charging currents up to 80 A on 240 V, providing up to about 19.2 kW.

Combo extension

The Combined Charging System (CCS) "Combo 1" connector adds two large DC pins below the J1772 inlet, enabling DC fast charging through the same vehicle inlet. From the mid-2020s many automakers in North America began adopting Tesla's connector, standardised as SAE J3400 (NACS), as an alternative.

Adoption

Virtually all mass-market electric cars sold in North America and Japan before the NACS transition, including the Nissan Leaf and Chevrolet Volt, were equipped with a J1772 inlet for AC charging.
