Ōedo Line

The Toei Ōedo Line (都営地下鉄大江戸線, Toei Chikatetsu Ōedo-sen) is a subway line in Tokyo, Japan, operated by the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Transportation (Toei). It is the newest and deepest of Tokyo's subway lines, forming a loop line (with a branch to Hikarigaoka) that encircles central Tokyo. The line colour is magenta, and its stations carry the prefix "E".

History and characteristics

The first section opened in 1991 between Hikarigaoka and Nerima, and the full line was completed in December 2000. Because much of the route runs deep below existing infrastructure — in places more than 40 metres underground, with Daimon station among the deepest in Tokyo — construction was expensive, and the line was built with linear-motor trains running on a narrow profile to reduce tunnel size. The name "Ōedo" means "Great Edo", referring to Tokyo's former name, and was chosen by public competition.
