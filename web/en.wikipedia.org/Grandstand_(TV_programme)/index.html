Grandstand (TV programme)

The Grandstand studio, home of BBC Saturday sport for nearly five decades

Grandstand was a British television sport programme that was broadcast by the BBC from 1958 to 2007. One of the longest-running sports shows in the world, it was transmitted on Saturday afternoons on BBC One, presenting live coverage and highlights of football, horse racing, rugby, and many other sports, concluding with the classified football results.

History

Grandstand debuted on 11 October 1958, created by Paul Fox and presented in its early years by Peter Dimmock. Its format — a rolling multi-sport magazine mixing live outside broadcasts with studio presentation — became a fixture of British Saturday afternoons. Presenters over the decades included David Coleman, Frank Bough, Desmond Lynam, and Steve Rider. Its innovations included the "vidiprinter" teleprinter that typed out football results as they came in, and Final Score, the results segment that later became a programme in its own right.

Theme music and rivalry

The programme's theme, "News Scoop" by Len Stevens, is one of the most recognisable in British television. Grandstand competed throughout its life with ITV's World of Sport until that programme ended in 1985. The loss of key sports rights in the 1990s and 2000s eroded the programme's content; the BBC announced its end in 2006, and the final edition aired on 28 January 2007, after nearly 3,500 episodes.

Legacy

Grandstand defined televised sport presentation for generations of British viewers and launched the careers of many of the country's best-known sports broadcasters and commentators.
