Perioeci

The perioeci or perioikoi (Greek: περίοικοι, "those dwelling around") were inhabitants of ancient Sparta's territory who were free but lacked full Spartan citizenship. They lived in autonomous communities in Laconia and Messenia, around the Spartan citizen body (the Spartiates).

Status and role

Unlike the helots, who were state-owned serfs, the perioeci were personally free and governed their own local affairs, but they had no vote in Spartan policy and were subject to Spartan foreign control. They served in the Spartan army, increasingly in the hoplite ranks, and were responsible for much of the trade, crafts and manufacturing that Spartan citizens were barred from pursuing.

Origins

Their origins are debated: they may descend from earlier inhabitants subordinated during the Dorian settlement and Spartan expansion. By the classical period there were dozens of perioecic towns, including Gythium, Sparta's port.
