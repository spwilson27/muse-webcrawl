2010 Winter Paralympics

The 2010 Winter Paralympics, officially the X Paralympic Winter Games, were held in Vancouver and Whistler, British Columbia, Canada, from 12 to 21 March 2010, following the 2010 Winter Olympics. About 506 athletes from 44 countries competed in 64 events across five sports: alpine skiing, biathlon, cross-country skiing, ice sledge hockey, and wheelchair curling. The motto, shared with the Olympics, was "With Glowing Hearts".

Opening ceremony at BC Place, Vancouver.

Highlights

The Games were the first Winter Paralympics hosted by Canada. Canadian skier Lauren Woolstencroft won five gold medals, and her compatriot Brian McKeever, a visually impaired cross-country skier who had also been selected for Canada's Olympic team, won three golds. The United States won the ice sledge hockey gold medal, defeating Japan in the final, while Canada won wheelchair curling. Germany topped the medal table by gold medals; Russia won the most medals overall.

Organisation

The Games were organised by VANOC under the slogan-in-spirit of the Inuit inukshuk mascot Sumi, the first Paralympic mascot for a joint Olympic-Paralympic host, an animal guardian figure with the wings of a thunderbird, legs of a black bear, and the hat of an orca. Television coverage was broader than at any previous Winter Paralympics, with the host broadcaster producing coverage of every sport. The closing ceremony was held in Whistler on 21 March 2010, and the Paralympic flag was handed over to Sochi, host of the 2014 Games.
