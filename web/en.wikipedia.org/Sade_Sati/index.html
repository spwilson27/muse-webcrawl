Sade Sati

Sade Sati (Hindi: साढ़े साती, from sāṛhe sāt, "seven and a half") is a period of approximately seven and a half years in Vedic astrology (Jyotish) during which Saturn (Shani) transits the zodiac sign of a person's natal Moon, the sign before it, and the sign after it.

Calculation

Saturn takes about two and a half years to cross each sign. Sade Sati begins when Saturn enters the sign preceding the Moon sign in the Vedic birth chart and ends when Saturn leaves the sign following the Moon sign — three signs at roughly 2.5 years each, totalling about 7.5 years.

Interpretation

In traditional interpretation, the period is associated with challenges, delays, hard work and life lessons, although effects are said to vary by the individual's chart, Saturn's strength and concurrent planetary periods (dashas). Astrologers commonly divide the phase into three parts of rising, peak and setting influence. Remedies traditionally suggested include charity, discipline and worship of Hanuman or Shani.
