Lipogram

A lipogram is a kind of constrained writing or word game consisting of writing paragraphs or longer works in which a particular letter or group of letters is avoided.

Famous examples omitting the letter e, the commonest letter in English, include Ernest Vincent Wright's novel Gadsby (1939) and Georges Perec's A Void (La Disparition, 1969), translated into English without using e. Ancient Greek lipograms are attributed to Lasus of Hermione and others, and the form is a favourite exercise of the Oulipo group.
