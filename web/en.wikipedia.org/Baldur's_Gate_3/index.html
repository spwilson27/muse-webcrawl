Baldur's Gate 3

Baldur's Gate 3 is a 2023 role-playing video game developed and published by Larian Studios. It is the third main game in the Baldur's Gate series, itself based on the Dungeons & Dragons tabletop role-playing system. The game uses a turn-based combat system derived from the Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition ruleset.

Plot

The player character awakens aboard a mind flayer nautiloid after being infected with a parasitic tadpole that threatens to transform them into a mind flayer. Gathering a party of fellow infected companions — including the wizard Gale, the cleric Shadowheart, the vampire spawn Astarion, the warlock Wyll, the barbarian Karlach and the githyanki fighter Lae'zel — the player journeys across the Sword Coast toward the city of Baldur's Gate, confronting the cult of the Absolute and the machinations of the Dead Three.

Gameplay

The game features both single-player and cooperative multiplayer modes, a branching narrative driven by player choice, dice-roll-based skill checks, and extensive character customisation across the classes and races of Dungeons & Dragons. Nearly all quests and encounters can be approached through combat, stealth, dialogue or creative use of the environment.

Reception

Baldur's Gate 3 was released for Windows in August 2023, followed by PlayStation 5, macOS and Xbox Series X/S versions. It received universal acclaim for its narrative, player freedom and production values, winning numerous Game of the Year awards including at The Game Awards 2023, the Hugo Award for Best Game, and the BAFTA Games Awards.
