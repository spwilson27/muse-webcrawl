Princess Zelda is a fictional character in Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda video game series. Created by Shigeru Miyamoto and named after the writer Zelda Fitzgerald, she is the princess of the kingdom of Hyrule and a central figure in the series alongside the hero Link and the antagonist Ganon. Although the games bear her name, Zelda was long portrayed primarily as a damsel in distress; later instalments have expanded her role substantially.

Role in the series

In most games Zelda is a mortal incarnation of the goddess Hylia and bearer of the Triforce of Wisdom, one of three parts of the sacred Triforce. She possesses prophetic dreams, sealing magic, and in some titles fights under alter egos: as the ninja-like Sheik in Ocarina of Time (1998) and as the pirate captain Tetra in The Wind Waker (2002). In Spirit Tracks (2009) she accompanies Link as a spirit, and in Breath of the Wild (2017) and Tears of the Kingdom (2023) she is a scholar-princess whose research and sacrifice drive the story.

Playable appearances

Zelda has been a playable fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series since Melee (2001) and in the Hyrule Warriors spin-offs. In Echoes of Wisdom (2024), she became the protagonist of a mainline Zelda game for the first time, using the Tri Rod to summon echoes of objects and monsters to rescue Link and Hyrule.

Reception

Zelda is regarded as one of the most recognisable female characters in video games. Critics praised her more active characterisation in Breath of the Wild, and her theme, "Zelda's Lullaby", is among the series' best-known melodies.
