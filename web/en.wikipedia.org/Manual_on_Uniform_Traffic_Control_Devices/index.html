Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices

Publisher: Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)
First published: 1935
Current edition: 11th edition (2023)
Country: United States

The Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD) is a document issued by the Federal Highway Administration of the United States Department of Transportation that specifies the standards by which traffic signs, road surface markings and signals are designed, installed and used in the United States.

History

The first edition appeared in 1935, as motoring spread and the need grew for consistent signing across state lines. Successive editions followed the expansion of the Interstate Highway System, and the manual acquired the force of federal regulation: compliance is required on all public roads open to travel. The 11th edition was published in December 2023, the first major revision since 2009, with new provisions for bicycle facilities, crosswalks and automated vehicles.

Contents

The MUTCD is organised into parts covering general provisions, signs, markings, highway traffic signals, traffic control for school areas, railroad and light rail grade crossings, bicycle facilities, temporary traffic control (such as work zones), and toll plaza control. It defines the shapes, colours, lettering and placement of devices — for example, the octagonal red stop sign and the warning-sign diamond — so that drivers encounter a uniform visual language nationwide.

State supplements

States may adopt the national manual as-is or issue their own manuals and supplements in substantial conformance with it; California and several other states publish their own editions.
