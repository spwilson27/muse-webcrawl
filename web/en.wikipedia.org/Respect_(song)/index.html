Respect (song)

"Respect" is a song written and originally released by American singer-songwriter Otis Redding in 1965. It became a signature hit for Aretha Franklin, whose 1967 version — with its rearranged verses, the spelled-out "R-E-S-P-E-C-T" hook, and the "sock it to me" refrain — reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and became an anthem of the civil rights and feminist movements.

Versions

Redding's original is a plea from a man demanding respect from his partner. Franklin inverted its perspective: her recording demands respect for a woman and, by extension, for Black Americans, giving the song its enduring cultural force. Franklin won two Grammy Awards for the recording, and it was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame.

Legacy

In 2021, Rolling Stone ranked Franklin's "Respect" number one on its list of the 500 Greatest Songs of All Time. The recording is preserved in the United States National Recording Registry.
