Irwindale Speedway, also operated as the Irwindale Event Center, is a motorsports facility in Irwindale, California, in the San Gabriel Valley of Los Angeles County. It features a half-mile (0.8 km) paved oval, a one-third-mile oval and a one-eighth-mile drag strip, and has hosted stock car racing, drifting and demolition events since it opened in 1999.

History

The speedway opened on 27 March 1999 and quickly became a prominent venue for NASCAR-sanctioned short-track racing on the US West Coast, hosting events such as the annual Turkey Night Grand Prix for midget cars (moved from its traditional venues) and NASCAR weekly series racing. Its drag strip hosted street-legal drag racing popular in Southern California car culture, and its drift course became a signature venue for Formula Drift, earning the nickname "House of Drift".

The track's future was repeatedly uncertain: the property owners filed demolition plans in 2015 amid redevelopment proposals for the former quarry site, but the track continued operating under new management from 2018. In October 2024 it was announced the facility would close at the end of the 2024 season, and Irwindale held its final events in December 2024, ending 25 years of operation.

Events

Signature events included Formula Drift rounds, NASCAR-sanctioned late model and modified racing, the "Night of Destruction" shows with trailer races and figure-8 events, and Thursday-night drag racing.
