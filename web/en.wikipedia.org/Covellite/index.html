Covellite

Covellite (also known as covelline) is a rare copper sulfide mineral with formula CuS. It is known for its distinctive iridescent indigo-blue colour.

Properties

Covellite crystallises in the hexagonal system, has Mohs hardness 1.5–2, is very soft and sectile, and shows perfect basal cleavage. It occurs in copper deposits as a secondary mineral in the supergene enrichment zone, associated with chalcocite, bornite and chalcopyrite. It was named in 1832 for Italian mineralogist Niccolò Covelli, who discovered it at Mount Vesuvius.
