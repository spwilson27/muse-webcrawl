Renault

Founded: 25 February 1899
Founders: Louis Renault, Marcel Renault, Fernand Renault
Headquarters: Boulogne-Billancourt, France
Industry: Automotive
Parent: Renault Group

Renault is a French multinational automobile manufacturer established in 1899. The company produces a range of cars and vans and has a long history in motorsport, including Formula One. Renault is part of the Renault–Nissan–Mitsubishi Alliance, one of the world's largest automotive groups by sales.

Renault's diamond logo has represented the brand since the 1920s.

History

Louis Renault built his first car, the Voiturette, in 1898, demonstrating its direct-drive gearbox to friends in Paris; with his brothers Marcel and Fernand he founded Société Renault Frères in 1899. The company grew rapidly, supplying taxis — some 1,100 Renault taxis famously carried French troops to the First Battle of the Marne in 1914 — and building the FT light tank during the First World War. After the Second World War, the company was nationalized by the French state following Louis Renault's death in prison in 1944 while awaiting trial on collaboration charges. The postwar 4CV, Dauphine, Renault 4, and Renault 5 became symbols of mass motoring in France. Renault was privatized in 1996, formed its alliance with Nissan in 1999, and later controlled Romania's Dacia and Korea's Samsung Motors.

Electric vehicles

Renault was an early leader in mass-market electric cars with the Zoe, launched in 2012, which for several years was Europe's best-selling electric vehicle. Under the "Renaulution" strategic plan announced by CEO Luca de Meo in 2021, the group reorganized around electrification, reviving the Renault 5 as an electric model and spinning off its electric vehicle activities into the Ampere subsidiary.

Motorsport

Renault has competed in Formula One both as a constructor and as an engine supplier, winning constructors' and drivers' championships in 2005 and 2006 with Fernando Alonso, and powering Red Bull Racing to four consecutive titles from 2010 to 2013. The team was rebranded as Alpine in 2021.
