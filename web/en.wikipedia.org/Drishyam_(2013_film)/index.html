Drishyam (2013 film)

Drishyam (transl. Visual) is a 2013 Indian Malayalam-language crime thriller film written and directed by Jeethu Joseph. It stars Mohanlal as Georgekutty, a cable-TV operator of modest education whose family becomes entangled in the disappearance of a police officer's son, alongside Meena, Ansiba Hassan, Esther Anil, Kalabhavan Shajohn, Asha Sharath and Siddique.

Plot

Georgekutty, a movie-loving orphan who runs a cable network in the village of Rajakkad, lives happily with his wife Rani and daughters Anju and Anu. When Varun, the spoiled son of Inspector-General Geetha Prabhakar, blackmails Anju with a compromising video filmed at a school camp, a confrontation ends in Varun's death. The family buries the body and Georgekutty, drawing on plots from the many films he has watched, methodically constructs an alibi: the family "repeats" a trip to Thodupuzha on the relevant dates, rehearsing receipts, tickets and witnesses until their false account becomes indistinguishable from a real memory. Despite intense police interrogation, no evidence is found, and the case closes — though Georgekutty quietly confesses to the new sub-inspector that he will protect his family at any cost.

Reception and legacy

The film was a critical and commercial success, becoming one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films of its time and running for over 150 days in theatres. It won several Kerala State Film Awards and was widely praised for its screenplay. Drishyam was remade in several Indian languages, including in Tamil as Papanasam (2015) and in Hindi as Drishyam (2015), as well as internationally, including a Chinese adaptation. A sequel, Drishyam 2, was released in 2021.
