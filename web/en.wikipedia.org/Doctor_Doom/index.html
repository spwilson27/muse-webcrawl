Doctor Doom

Doctor Doom (Victor von Doom) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in The Fantastic Four #5 in July 1962 and serves as the archenemy of the Fantastic Four.

Fictional character biography

Victor von Doom was born in the fictional European nation of Latveria to a Romani healer mother and a physician father. A scientific genius, he studied at Empire State University in New York, where a failed experiment to contact his dead mother's soul scarred his face. Blaming Reed Richards for the accident, he forged a suit of powered armor and a metal mask, becoming Doctor Doom, and seized control of Latveria.

Powers and abilities

Doom is one of the most intelligent figures in the Marvel Universe, a master of both advanced science and sorcery. His titanium armor grants superhuman strength and durability and is equipped with energy weapons, force fields, and flight systems. He is also a skilled diplomat and ruler who commands the resources of Latveria.

In other media

The character has appeared in numerous animated series, video games, and films; he was portrayed by Julian McMahon in the 2005 and 2007 Fantastic Four films.
