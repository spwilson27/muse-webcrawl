Australia at the 2000 Summer Olympics was the nation's participation as host of the Games of the XXVII Olympiad, held in Sydney from 15 September to 1 October 2000. Australia fielded one of its largest teams ever, with 617 athletes competing across 34 sports, and finished fourth on the medal table.

Medal summary

Australia won 58 medals in total: 16 gold, 25 silver and 17 bronze. Swimming was the standout sport, led by Ian Thorpe, who won three gold medals, and Grant Hackett, who won the 1500 metre freestyle. Cathy Freeman's victory in the women's 400 metres, days after she lit the Olympic flame, became one of the defining images of the Games.

Highlights

The Australian men's 4 × 100 metre freestyle relay team famously defeated the United States in the opening night of swimming competition. Other gold medallists included the women's water polo team, beach volleyball pair Natalie Cook and Kerri Pottharst, and cyclist Brett Aitken and Scott McGrory in the Madison.

Legacy

The Sydney Games are widely regarded as among the most successful in Olympic history, and Australia's medal haul as host remains its largest at a Summer Olympics.
