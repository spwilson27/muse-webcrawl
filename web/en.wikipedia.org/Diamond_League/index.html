Diamond League

SportTrack and field
Founded2010
OrganizerWorld Athletics
PredecessorIAAF Golden League

The Diamond League is an annual series of elite track and field athletics competitions comprising fourteen of the best invitational athletics meetings. The series sits in the top tier of World Athletics' one-day meeting competitions and was inaugurated in 2010, succeeding the IAAF Golden League.

Format

Athletes earn points at each meeting by placing in their discipline, and the top performers qualify for a season final, where the winner of each event is crowned Diamond League champion and receives a diamond trophy. Meetings are held across Europe, Asia, Africa, and North America, typically from May to September.

History

The Diamond League was created to enhance the worldwide appeal of athletics by going outside Europe for the first time and by staging head-to-head competition between the world's best athletes across a season-long series rather than at a single championship.
