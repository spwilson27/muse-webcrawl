Olive Oyl is a cartoon character created by E. C. Segar in 1919 for his comic strip Thimble Theatre. She is the main female character of the Popeye franchise, best known as the love interest of the sailor Popeye.

Character

Tall, thin, and black-haired, with her hair in a bun and large feet, Olive Oyl predates Popeye by a decade: she was originally the girlfriend of Ham Gravy in Thimble Theatre. When Popeye joined the strip in 1929 he soon displaced Ham, and Olive became Popeye's famously fickle sweetheart, frequently kidnapped by Bluto and rescued after Popeye downs a can of spinach.

In other media

Olive Oyl appeared in the Fleischer and Famous Studios theatrical cartoons, voiced most famously by Mae Questel, and was played by Shelley Duvall in Robert Altman's 1980 live-action film Popeye.
