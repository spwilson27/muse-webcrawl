Liner notes

Liner notes (also sleeve notes) are the writings found on the sleeves of LP record albums and in booklets accompanying CDs, cassettes and digital releases. They typically include credits, lyrics, photographs and essays about the music or performers.

The name derives from the paper lining of early album sleeves, on which manufacturers printed information. Writers such as Nat Hentoff became noted for jazz liner notes, and the Grammy Award for Best Album Notes has been presented since the 1960s.

Digital era

Streaming reduced printed notes, though deluxe reissues and digital booklets have revived detailed annotation and session information.
