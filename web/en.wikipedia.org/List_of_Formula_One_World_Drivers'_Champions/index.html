List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions

Formula One World Drivers' Championship
The title has been awarded since 1950

The Formula One World Drivers' Championship is awarded by the FIA to the most successful Formula One driver over a season, based on points scored in Grands Prix. It was first awarded in 1950 to Giuseppe Farina. Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton share the record with seven titles each, followed by Juan Manuel Fangio with five. The championship has been won by drivers of many nationalities, with British drivers the most successful collectively.

Records

  • Most titles: 7 — Michael Schumacher (1994, 1995, 2000–2004) and Lewis Hamilton (2008, 2014, 2015, 2017–2020).
  • Most consecutive titles: 5 — Schumacher (2000–2004).
  • Youngest champion: Sebastian Vettel, aged 23 years and 134 days in 2010.
  • Oldest champion: Juan Manuel Fangio, aged 46 in 1957.
  • Two champions died during their title season: Jochen Rindt (1970, awarded posthumously) — the only posthumous champion — and others such as Senna (1994) died while reigning as a former champion during a season.
