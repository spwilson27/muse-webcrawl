Phyllite

Phyllite is a type of foliated metamorphic rock formed from slate through further regional metamorphism. It is composed mainly of quartz, sericite mica and chlorite, and its aligned micaceous minerals give it a characteristic silky or satiny sheen known as phyllitic luster.

Formation

Phyllite represents a grade of metamorphism between slate and schist. The original sedimentary rock—usually shale or mudstone—is recrystallised under directed pressure and moderate heat, growing mica crystals large enough to impart sheen but not yet visible as individual flakes.

Characteristics

Phyllite is typically grey, green or black, splits along wavy foliation planes, and is sometimes used as a decorative stone.
