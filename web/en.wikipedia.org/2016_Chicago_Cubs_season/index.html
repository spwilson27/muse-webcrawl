The 2016 Chicago Cubs season was the 145th season of the Chicago Cubs franchise, the 141st in the National League and the Cubs' 101st season at Wrigley Field. Managed by Joe Maddon, the Cubs finished the regular season with a major-league-best 103–58 record.

Postseason

The Cubs defeated the San Francisco Giants in the Division Series and the Los Angeles Dodgers in the National League Championship Series, winning the franchise's first pennant since 1945. In the World Series they faced the Cleveland Indians.

World Series

After trailing the Series three games to one, the Cubs won three straight, culminating in a dramatic 8–7 victory in ten innings in Game 7 at Cleveland, following a rain delay. The win ended the Cubs' 108-year championship drought, the longest in North American professional sports, and broke the so-called "Curse of the Billy Goat". Third baseman Kris Bryant won the National League MVP award, and Ben Zobrist was named World Series MVP.
