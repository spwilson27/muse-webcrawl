Louhi

Louhi is a powerful witch-queen in Finnish mythology and a principal antagonist of the Kalevala, the Finnish national epic compiled by Elias Lönnrot. She is the mistress of Pohjola, the cold northern land, and a formidable sorceress capable of shapeshifting and commanding plagues and beasts.

Louhi commissions the smith Ilmarinen to forge the miraculous Sampo in return for her daughter's hand, then becomes the heroes' adversary when Väinämöinen, Ilmarinen and Lemminkäinen steal the Sampo; in the ensuing sea battle the Sampo is shattered. In revenge she sends diseases and a great bear against Kalevala and steals the sun and moon, which the heroes eventually compel her to release.
