Lars Porsena

Lars Porsena (Etruscan: Pursenas) was an Etruscan king of the city of Clusium in the late 6th century BC. According to Roman tradition he marched on Rome around 508 BC in an attempt to restore the exiled king Tarquin the Proud.

War with Rome

Livy's account tells of Horatius Cocles defending the Sublician Bridge, Mucius Scaevola's failed assassination attempt and burnt hand, and the hostage Cloelia's escape across the Tiber. Impressed by Roman bravery, Porsena is said to have made peace and withdrawn. Other sources suggest he may in fact have taken the city.

Legacy

His reputed monumental tomb at Clusium was described by Pliny the Elder quoting Varro. The story was retold in Macaulay's Lays of Ancient Rome (1842).
