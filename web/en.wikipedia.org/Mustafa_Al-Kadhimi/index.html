Mustafa Al-Kadhimi (born 1967) is an Iraqi politician and former journalist and intelligence chief who served as the prime minister of Iraq from May 2020 to October 2022. He previously headed the Iraqi National Intelligence Service (INIS) from 2016 to 2020.

Early life and career

Al-Kadhimi was born in Baghdad in 1967. He opposed the regime of Saddam Hussein and lived in exile in Iran, Germany and the United Kingdom. He worked as a journalist and editor, contributing to publications covering Iraqi affairs, and served as executive director of the Iraq Memory Foundation, which documented crimes of the Ba'ath regime. After returning to Iraq, he wrote a regular column for Al-Monitor's Iraq Pulse.

Intelligence chief

In June 2016, Prime Minister Haider al-Abadi appointed Al-Kadhimi director of the Iraqi National Intelligence Service. In that role he oversaw intelligence operations during the war against the Islamic State and maintained working relationships with both Western and regional intelligence services.

Premiership

Following the resignation of Adil Abdul-Mahdi amid the 2019–2021 Iraqi protests, and after two other nominees failed to form governments, President Barham Salih designated Al-Kadhimi as prime minister. His cabinet won a vote of confidence on 7 May 2020. His government pledged early elections, held in October 2021, economic reform under a "white paper" programme, and accountability for violence against protesters. In November 2021 he survived an assassination attempt when explosive-laden drones struck his Baghdad residence. After a year of political deadlock following the 2021 election, he was succeeded by Mohammed Shia' Al Sudani in October 2022.
