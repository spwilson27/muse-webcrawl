CRRC Corporation Limited is a Chinese state-owned and publicly traded rolling stock manufacturer. It is the world's largest rolling stock manufacturer in terms of revenue, ahead of Alstom. CRRC was formed on 1 June 2015 through the merger of CNR and CSR, the two previously competing state-owned train builders.

The merger was intended to end destructive price competition between CNR and CSR in overseas markets and to create a single national champion able to compete with Siemens, Bombardier Transportation (later Alstom) and Japanese builders for export orders. CRRC supplies the great majority of China's huge domestic market, including Fuxing Hao high-speed trains, metro systems across the country, locomotives and freight wagons.

International business

CRRC has exported trains to more than 100 countries and regions. Notable projects include metro cars for Boston, Chicago and Los Angeles in the United States, trains for the Jakarta–Bandung high-speed railway in Indonesia, and passenger stock across Africa, Latin America, Southeast Asia and Europe. Its expansion has attracted security scrutiny in some markets, including in the United States, where legislation restricts the use of federal funds for Chinese-built transit cars.

Subsidiaries

Major manufacturing subsidiaries include CRRC Changchun, CRRC Zhuzhou, CRRC Qingdao Sifang, CRRC Tangshan and CRRC Nanjing Puzhen.
