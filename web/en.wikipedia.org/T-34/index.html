T-34

TypeMedium tank
Place of originSoviet Union
In service1940–present (limited)
Main armament76.2 mm F-34 gun (later 85 mm)

The T-34 is a Soviet medium tank introduced in 1940 that had a profound effect on tank design. Its combination of sloped armour, a powerful gun, wide tracks, and a diesel engine gave it an effective balance of firepower, protection, and mobility.

Development

Designed at the Kharkov locomotive works under Mikhail Koshkin, the T-34 replaced the BT and T-26 series. When it met German forces in 1941 it outclassed most opposing armour despite early problems with radios, optics, and two-man turrets.

Variants

The principal variants were the T-34-76 and the upgunned T-34-85 of 1944, with a larger three-man turret mounting an 85 mm gun. Some 84,000 were built in total, making it one of the most-produced tanks in history.

Legacy

The T-34 served in dozens of armies for decades and remains a symbol of the Soviet war effort on the Eastern Front.
