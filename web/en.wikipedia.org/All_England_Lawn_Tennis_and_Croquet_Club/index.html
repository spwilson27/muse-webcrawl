All England Lawn Tennis and Croquet Club

The All England Lawn Tennis and Croquet Club, also known as the All England Club, is a private members' club at Aorangi Park, Wimbledon, London, England. It is best known as the venue for the Wimbledon Championships, the only Grand Slam tennis event still held on grass.

All England Club
Founded: 23 July 1868
Location: Wimbledon, London, England
Venue for: The Championships, Wimbledon
Surface: Grass (outdoor)

History

The club was founded in 1868 as the All England Croquet Club and adopted lawn tennis in the 1870s, staging the first Wimbledon championship in 1877. In 1882 the word "croquet" was dropped from the name (it was restored in 1899, though tennis remained dominant). The club moved to its present site at Church Road in 1922.

Facilities

The grounds contain 18 tournament grass courts, including Centre Court (with a retractable roof since 2009) and No. 1 Court (retractable roof since 2019), as well as practice and clay courts. Membership is limited to a few hundred full members, and the club's patrons have included members of the British royal family.
