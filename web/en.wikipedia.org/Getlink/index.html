Getlink SE, known until 2017 as Groupe Eurotunnel, is the company that holds the concession to operate the Channel Tunnel between France and the United Kingdom until 2086. It runs the Eurotunnel Le Shuttle vehicle-carrying trains through the tunnel and owns other transport and energy infrastructure businesses.

History

The tunnel was built by TransManche Link under the Franco-British concession awarded in 1986 and opened in 1994. The Eurotunnel group accumulated heavy debts from construction cost overruns and lower-than-expected traffic, undergoing major financial restructurings, including a safeguard procedure in 2006–2007 that sharply reduced its debt. In November 2017 Groupe Eurotunnel renamed itself Getlink to reflect a broader strategy beyond the tunnel itself.

Activities

Through Eurotunnel, the group operates passenger and freight shuttle trains carrying cars, coaches and trucks between Coquelles (near Calais) and Folkestone, and receives track-access charges from Eurostar high-speed passenger trains and through freight trains. Its subsidiaries include Europorte, a French rail freight operator, and ElecLink, a 1 GW electricity interconnector through the tunnel connecting the British and French grids, which entered service in 2022.
