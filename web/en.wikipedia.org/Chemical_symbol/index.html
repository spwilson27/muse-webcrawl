Chemical symbol

A chemical symbol is a one- or two-letter abbreviation for a chemical element, such as H for hydrogen, O for oxygen and Fe for iron (from Latin ferrum). Symbols are used in formulas and equations worldwide.

The system was systematised by Jöns Jacob Berzelius in the early 19th century. In a formula, a subscript number after a symbol shows how many atoms of that element are present.
