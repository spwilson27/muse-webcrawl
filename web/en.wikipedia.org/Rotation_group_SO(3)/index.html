Rotation group SO(3)

In mathematics, the rotation group SO(3), also called the special orthogonal group in three dimensions, is the group of all rotations about the origin of three-dimensional Euclidean space. Its elements can be represented by 3×3 orthogonal matrices with determinant +1, with matrix multiplication as the group operation.

Properties

SO(3) is a compact, connected, non-abelian Lie group of dimension three. By Euler's rotation theorem, every element is a rotation through some angle about some axis. The group is not simply connected: its fundamental group is Z/2, a fact reflected in the "belt trick" and in the double covering of SO(3) by the spin group SU(2), which is fundamental to the quantum mechanics of spin.

Parametrisations

Rotations can be parametrised by Euler angles, by axis–angle (Rodrigues) vectors, or by unit quaternions. As a topological space, SO(3) is homeomorphic to real projective 3-space.
