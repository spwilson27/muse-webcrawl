RuPaul Andre Charles (born 17 November 1960), known mononymously as RuPaul, is an American drag queen, television host, singer and actor. He is best known for creating and hosting the reality competition series RuPaul's Drag Race, which has spawned dozens of international editions and won numerous Primetime Emmy Awards.

Early life and career

Born in San Diego and raised partly in Atlanta, RuPaul studied at the Atlanta Northside School of Performing Arts and performed in the Atlanta underground scene before moving to New York City, where he became a fixture of the club scene. His breakthrough came with the 1993 dance single "Supermodel (You Better Work)", which became a pop hit and led to appearances in films and the first MAC Cosmetics Viva Glam campaign by a drag queen.

Drag Race

Premiering in 2009, RuPaul's Drag Race challenges drag performers in design, comedy, acting and lip-sync competitions, closing each episode with RuPaul's signature phrases such as "Shantay, you stay" and "If you can't love yourself, how in the hell you gonna love somebody else?" The franchise is credited with bringing drag culture into the mainstream.

Other work

RuPaul has released numerous dance albums, hosted talk shows and radio programmes, acted in series including AJ and the Queen, and written memoirs including Lettin It All Hang Out (1995) and The House of Hidden Meanings (2024). He has won multiple Emmy Awards for Outstanding Host and received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2018.
