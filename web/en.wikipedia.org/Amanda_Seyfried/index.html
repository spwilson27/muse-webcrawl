Amanda Seyfried

Amanda Michelle Seyfried (born December 3, 1985) is an American actress. She made her film debut as Karen Smith in the comedy Mean Girls (2004) and gained wide recognition as Sophie in the musical Mamma Mia! (2008) and its sequel.

BornDecember 3, 1985, Allentown, Pennsylvania
OccupationActress
Years active1996–present

Career

Born in Allentown, Pennsylvania, Seyfried began as a child model and appeared on the soap operas As the World Turns and All My Children before her film breakthrough. Her later work includes Jennifer's Body (2009), Les Misérables (2012), and an Academy Award–nominated performance as Marion Davies in Mank (2020). She won a Primetime Emmy Award for portraying Elizabeth Holmes in the miniseries The Dropout (2022).
