Hasbro

Hasbro, Inc. is an American multinational toy manufacturing and entertainment holding company founded on December 6, 1923, by Henry and Helal Hassenfeld in Providence, Rhode Island. Hasbro owns the trademarks and products of Kenner, Milton Bradley, Parker Brothers, and Wizards of the Coast, among others. Its products include Transformers, G.I. Joe, Power Rangers, My Little Pony, Monopoly, Nerf, Play-Doh, and Dungeons & Dragons.

History

The Hassenfeld brothers began in textiles and pencil cases before moving into school supplies and, in the 1940s, toys. The company produced Mr. Potato Head from 1952 and G.I. Joe from 1964, which coined the term "action figure". Hasbro grew through acquisitions, purchasing Milton Bradley in 1984 and Parker Brothers in 1991, thereby gaining Monopoly and other classic board games. In 1999 it acquired Wizards of the Coast, publisher of Magic: The Gathering and owner of Dungeons & Dragons.

Entertainment

Hasbro has pursued a strategy of turning its brands into films, television, and digital games, including the commercially successful Transformers film franchise. In 2019 it acquired Entertainment One (eOne) for about $4 billion, gaining children's properties such as Peppa Pig, before selling most of eOne's film and television assets to Lionsgate in 2023. Its gaming subsidiary Wizards of the Coast has become one of the company's most profitable segments.
