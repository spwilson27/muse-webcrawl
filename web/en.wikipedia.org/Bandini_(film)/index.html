Bandini (film)

Bandini is a 1963 Indian Hindi-language drama film directed and produced by Bimal Roy. It stars Nutan, Ashok Kumar, and Dharmendra. Based on the Bengali novel Tamasi by Charu Chandra Chakraborty, the film tells the story of Kalyani, a woman imprisoned for murder, and is considered one of Bimal Roy's finest works and a landmark of Indian cinema.

Plot

Kalyani, a prisoner serving a sentence for murder, recounts her past to the jail doctor Deven, who falls in love with her. Her story follows her life in a Bengal village, her romance with the freedom fighter Bikash, his disappearance, and the circumstances that led to her crime. The film is notable for its sensitive portrayal of a woman caught between love, duty, and the freedom movement.

Awards

The film won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Hindi and six Filmfare Awards, including Best Film, Best Director, and Best Actress for Nutan, whose performance is regarded as one of the greatest in Indian cinema. Its songs, composed by S. D. Burman, include "Mere Sajan Hain Us Paar" and "Mora Gora Ang Lai Le".
