The Robe is a 1953 American biblical epic film starring Richard Burton, Jean Simmons and Victor Mature, directed by Henry Koster. It tells the story of Marcellus Gallio, the Roman tribune who wins Christ's robe in a dice game at the Crucifixion and is transformed by it.

It was the first film released in the widescreen process CinemaScope and pioneered the biblical epic cycle of the 1950s.

Plot

Marcellus, exiled to Palestine, supervises the Crucifixion and wins the robe, then suffers torment and seeks out the early Christians, including his slave Demetrius and the apostle Peter, ultimately converting and facing martyrdom under Caligula.

Production and reception

Adapted from Lloyd C. Douglas's 1942 bestselling novel, the film was a major box-office success and won Academy Awards for art direction and costume design. A sequel, Demetrius and the Gladiators (1954), followed.
