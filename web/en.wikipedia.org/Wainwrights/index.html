Wainwrights

The Wainwrights are the 214 fells (hills and mountains) in the English Lake District described in Alfred Wainwright's seven-volume Pictorial Guide to the Lakeland Fells (1955–66). Climbing all of them — "Wainwright bagging" — is a popular goal among hillwalkers, and the list is one of the best-known British hill lists.

The list

Wainwright selected the fells for his hand-lettered, hand-illustrated guides by his own criteria rather than a strict height threshold; every fell of 1,000 feet is included, along with a few lower but distinctive tops. The highest is Scafell Pike, England's highest mountain. He later added a supplementary volume, The Outlying Fells of Lakeland (1974), covering 116 more hills, which form a separate list sometimes called the Outlying Fells.

Bagging

There is no official register, but long-distance walkers' associations record completers; thousands have finished the round. The fastest known continuous rounds have been completed in under a week. Wainwright's guides, continuously in print and later revised by Chris Jesty and Clive Hutchby, remain the standard companion to Lakeland walking.
