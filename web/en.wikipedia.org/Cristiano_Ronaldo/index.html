Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born 5 February 1985) is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward for Saudi Pro League club Al-Nassr and captains the Portugal national team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, he has won five Ballon d'Or awards and four European Golden Shoes, and holds the records for most goals in the UEFA Champions League and in men's international football.

Club career

Ronaldo began his senior career with Sporting CP before signing for Manchester United in 2003, where he won three consecutive Premier League titles, the Champions League and his first Ballon d'Or. In 2009 he transferred to Real Madrid for a then-world-record fee of £80 million. In nine seasons in Madrid he became the club's all-time top scorer, winning four Champions League titles and forming a famous rivalry with Lionel Messi. He moved to Juventus in 2018 for €100 million, returned to Manchester United in 2021, and joined Al-Nassr in January 2023 after his United contract was terminated.

International career

Ronaldo debuted for Portugal in 2003 and has earned more caps than any other male player. He captained Portugal to victory at UEFA Euro 2016 — the country's first major trophy — and to the inaugural UEFA Nations League title in 2019. He is the first male player to score at five World Cups and the top scorer in European Championship history.

Legacy and off the pitch

Ronaldo is one of the most marketable athletes in the world and was the first person to reach over 600 million followers on Instagram. His rivalry with Messi defined a generation of football. He has been named FIFA World Player of the Year and The Best FIFA Men's Player multiple times, and is the sport's first billionaire by career earnings.
