Boyz n the Hood

Boyz n the Hood is a 1991 American coming-of-age hood drama film written and directed by John Singleton in his feature directorial debut. It stars Cuba Gooding Jr. as Tre Styles, who is sent to live with his father (Laurence Fishburne) in South Central Los Angeles, alongside Ice Cube as Doughboy and Morris Chestnut as Ricky, whose lives are shaped by gang violence, poverty, and the hope of escape.

Singleton, at 23, became the youngest person and the first African American nominated for the Academy Award for Best Director. The film was a critical and commercial success and is credited with launching the 1990s cycle of hood films.

In 2002 it was selected for preservation in the National Film Registry by the Library of Congress as culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant.
