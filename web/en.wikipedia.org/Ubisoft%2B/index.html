Ubisoft+

Ubisoft+
Type: Video game subscription service
Developer: Ubisoft
Launched: September 3, 2019 (as Uplay+)
Platforms: Windows, Xbox, PlayStation, Amazon Luna

Ubisoft+ (formerly Uplay+) is a subscription-based video game service from Ubisoft offering access to a library of over 100 games from Ubisoft's catalogue, including new releases, downloadable content, and premium editions, for a monthly fee.

Tiers

The service has been offered in tiers including Ubisoft+ Premium, giving day-one access to new releases, and Ubisoft+ Classics, a curated selection bundled with some PlayStation Plus tiers.

Availability

The service launched on PC in 2019 and later expanded to cloud and console platforms, including Amazon Luna and Xbox consoles.
