King of Bohemia

The King of Bohemia was the ruler of the Kingdom of Bohemia, a state in Central Europe centred on Bohemia (with Moravia and Silesia forming the Lands of the Bohemian Crown), whose kings ranked as electors of the Holy Roman Empire.

History

Bohemian dukes of the Přemyslid dynasty gained a hereditary royal title by 1212 under Ottokar I. Great medieval kings included Ottokar II and Charles IV, who made Prague an imperial capital. After 1526 the crown passed to the Habsburgs; the Bohemian revolt of 1618, which opened the Thirty Years' War, was crushed at White Mountain in 1620 and the crown became hereditary in the Habsburg line. The monarchy ended in 1918 with the creation of Czechoslovakia, the last king being Charles I of Austria.
