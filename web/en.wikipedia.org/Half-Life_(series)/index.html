Half-Life is a series of first-person shooter games developed and published by Valve. The games follow the theoretical physicist Gordon Freeman, who battles alien forces after a scientific experiment at the Black Mesa Research Facility opens a portal to the alien world of Xen, and later fights the interdimensional Combine empire that conquers Earth. The series is named after the scientific concept of half-life, and its logo is the Greek letter lambda (λ), which in physics denotes the decay constant.

Main games

Half-Life (1998)

Valve's debut title, released for Windows in November 1998 and published by Sierra Studios. Built on a heavily modified version of the Quake engine known as GoldSrc, it was praised for its seamless, scripted single-player narrative presented entirely in first person without cutscenes. See the main article on the Half-Life video game.

Half-Life 2 (2004)

Released in November 2004 on Valve's new Source engine and distributed through its Steam platform, Half-Life 2 is set about twenty years later in the dystopian City 17, where Freeman joins a resistance against the Combine. It introduced advanced physics-based gameplay and won numerous Game of the Year awards. It was followed by the episodic sequels Episode One (2006) and Episode Two (2007); a planned third episode was never released.

Half-Life: Alyx (2020)

A virtual reality prequel released in March 2020, starring Alyx Vance in the years before Freeman's return. It was widely acclaimed as a landmark VR title.

Expansions and related titles

Gearbox Software developed the expansions Opposing Force (1999), Blue Shift (2001) and Decay (2001), which retell the Black Mesa incident from other perspectives. The wider series shares its universe with the Portal games. The fan-made remake of the first game, Black Mesa, was released commercially with Valve's approval in 2020.

Story overview

An experiment with an anomalous crystal sample triggers a "resonance cascade" at Black Mesa, tearing open portals to Xen whose creatures invade the facility. Freeman's efforts to close the rift draw the attention of the mysterious G-Man, who places him in stasis. Awakening decades later, Freeman finds Earth ruled by the Combine, an empire that crossed into Earth during the Seven Hour War, and becomes a symbol of the human resistance led by former colleagues including Eli and Alyx Vance, Dr. Judith Mossman and the administrator-turned-collaborator Wallace Breen.

Legacy

The series has sold tens of millions of copies and is regarded as one of the most influential in the medium, credited with advancing narrative design, physics in games, and digital distribution. The long absence of a third numbered game became a famous running joke in gaming culture, making the announcement of Alyx a major industry event.
