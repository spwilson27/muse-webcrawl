Kennedy Space Center

The John F. Kennedy Space Center (KSC) is NASA's primary launch center for human spaceflight, located on Merritt Island, Florida, adjacent to Cape Canaveral Space Force Station. It has been the departure point for every NASA crewed mission since 1968.

Named for President John F. Kennedy after his 1963 assassination, the center includes Launch Complex 39 — with the enormous Vehicle Assembly Building and pads 39A and 39B used by Apollo, the Space Shuttle, SpaceX Falcon launches and the Space Launch System. Apollo 11 lifted off from KSC on 16 July 1969. Its visitor complex displays the space shuttle Atlantis and a Saturn V rocket.
