TORRO scale

The TORRO tornado intensity scale (or T-Scale) is a scale measuring tornado intensity developed by Terence Meaden of the Tornado and Storm Research Organisation (TORRO) in the United Kingdom.

Scale

The scale runs from T0 (light tornado, wind speeds of 61–86 km/h) to T11 (super tornado, above 480 km/h), with each step derived from a formula relating the T number to wind speed, extending Beaufort-scale wind values.

Use

TORRO rates tornadoes in Britain and elsewhere in Europe primarily from damage surveys, since direct wind measurements in tornadoes are rare. T-Scale ratings are published in TORRO's records of British tornadoes dating back centuries.

Comparison

The scale is analogous in purpose to the Fujita and Enhanced Fujita scales used in North America, though its gradations and wind-speed ranges differ.
