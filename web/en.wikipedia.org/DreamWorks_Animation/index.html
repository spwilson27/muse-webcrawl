DreamWorks Animation

Founded: 1994
Headquarters: Glendale, California, U.S.
Parent: Universal Pictures (NBCUniversal)
Industry: Animated films and television

DreamWorks Animation LLC is an American animation studio owned by Universal Pictures, a division of NBCUniversal. The studio has released numerous animated feature films, including the Shrek, Madagascar, Kung Fu Panda, and How to Train Your Dragon franchises, and has won multiple Academy Awards.

History

DreamWorks Animation originated as the animation division of DreamWorks Pictures, founded in 1994 by Steven Spielberg, Jeffrey Katzenberg, and David Geffen. Early successes included Antz (1998) and The Prince of Egypt (1998), followed by the blockbuster Shrek (2001), which won the first Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. The division was spun off into a separate publicly traded company in 2004. In 2016, Comcast's NBCUniversal acquired DreamWorks Animation for approximately $3.8 billion.

Productions

The studio produces films at its Glendale campus and through Oriental DreamWorks/Pearl Studio partnerships, and also produces animated television series for streaming and broadcast, many based on its film franchises.
