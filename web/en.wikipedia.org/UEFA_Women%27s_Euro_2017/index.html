UEFA Women's Euro 2017

HostNetherlands
Dates16 July – 6 August 2017
Teams16
ChampionsNetherlands

The 2017 UEFA Women's Championship was held in the Netherlands from 16 July to 6 August 2017. Expanded to sixteen teams for the first time, the tournament was won by the host nation.

Tournament

The Netherlands, coached by Sarina Wiegman and inspired by players such as Lieke Martens, Vivianne Miedema and Shanice van de Sanden, won all their matches. In the final in Enschede they defeated surprise package Denmark 4–2, in front of a crowd of over 28,000.

Legacy

The tournament set attendance records for women's football in Europe and greatly boosted the sport's popularity in the Netherlands; England's Jodie Taylor finished as top scorer with five goals.
