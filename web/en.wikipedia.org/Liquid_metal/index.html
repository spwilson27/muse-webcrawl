Liquid metal

A liquid metal is a metal or metal alloy which is liquid at or near room temperature. Mercury is the only elemental metal liquid at standard conditions; gallium-based alloys such as galinstan, and sodium–potassium alloy, are also widely used.

Liquid metals combine high electrical and thermal conductivity with fluidity, finding applications in coolants for reactors, thermal interface materials, stretchable electronics, antennas and soft robotics. Some, notably mercury, are toxic and are being phased out where alternatives exist.
