Sam Altman

Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI.

Samuel Harris Altman (born April 22, 1985) is an American entrepreneur and investor best known as the chief executive officer of OpenAI, the artificial intelligence company behind ChatGPT. He was previously president of the startup accelerator Y Combinator.

Early life and career

Altman grew up in St. Louis, Missouri, and studied computer science at Stanford University before dropping out in 2005 to co-found Loopt, a location-based social networking app backed by Y Combinator. Loopt was acquired in 2012. Altman joined Y Combinator as a partner in 2011 and became its president in 2014, expanding the accelerator's scale and launching funds for later-stage and hard-tech companies.

OpenAI

Altman co-founded OpenAI in 2015 with Elon Musk and others, and became CEO in 2019 as the organisation shifted toward a capped-profit model and a major partnership with Microsoft. Under his leadership OpenAI released GPT-3, DALL-E and ChatGPT, whose November 2022 launch triggered a worldwide wave of interest in generative AI.

Removal and reinstatement

In November 2023 OpenAI's board abruptly removed Altman, citing a lack of candour; after days of turmoil and pressure from employees and investors, he was reinstated as CEO with a reconstituted board. Altman has also been an active investor in companies including Helion Energy and World (formerly Worldcoin), and advocates for technology-driven economic growth and AI safety governance.
