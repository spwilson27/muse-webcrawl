Transalpina (DN67C)

Length148 km
Highest pointUrdele Pass, 2,145 m
LocationParâng Mountains, Romania

The Transalpina or DN67C is a 148 km national road in the Parâng Mountains group, Southern Carpathians of Romania. It is the highest road in Romania, reaching 2,145 metres above sea level at the Urdele Pass, and connects Novaci in Gorj County with Sebeș in Alba County.

History

The route follows an old Roman and shepherd's path over the mountains. It was rebuilt under King Carol II in the 1930s — hence its nickname "the King's Road" — and paved and modernised in the 2010s. Because of snow at the highest sections, the road is generally closed in winter and opens only in the warmer months.

Tourism

The Transalpina is a popular scenic driving route, passing the Rânca ski resort and offering wide views over the Parâng and Lotru mountains, rivalling the better-known Transfăgărășan.
