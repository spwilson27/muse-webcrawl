Enduro World Series

Enduro World Series
Sport: Mountain bike enduro racing
Founded: 2013
Headquarters: Peebles, Scotland
Organizer: ESO (Enduro Sports Organisation)

The Enduro World Series (EWS) was the premier international series for enduro mountain bike racing, founded in 2013 by Chris Ball through the Enduro Sports Organisation. It brought together the leading enduro races from around the world into a single championship.

Format

Enduro races consist of multiple timed downhill-oriented stages linked by untimed (but time-limited) liaison stages that riders must pedal. The rider with the lowest cumulative stage time wins. Events typically run over one or two days on technical natural terrain.

History

The series began in 2013 with rounds in France, Italy, Canada, the United States, and Scotland. In 2023, the competition was integrated into the UCI Mountain Bike World Series as the UCI Enduro World Cup, organized under Warner Bros. Discovery Sports.
