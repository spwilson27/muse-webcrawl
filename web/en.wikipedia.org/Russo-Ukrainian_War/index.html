LocationUkraine; spillover into western Russia

The Russo-Ukrainian War is an ongoing war between Russia and Ukraine that began in February 2014. Following Ukraine's Revolution of Dignity, Russia annexed Crimea from Ukraine and supported pro-Russian separatists fighting the Ukrainian military in the Donbas war in eastern Ukraine. In February 2022, Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine and began occupying more of the country, starting the biggest conflict in Europe since World War II. The war has caused tens of thousands of deaths and displaced millions.

Background

After the pro-Western Euromaidan protests and the flight of President Viktor Yanukovych in February 2014, unmarked Russian forces seized Crimea, which Russia annexed following a disputed referendum. In April 2014, armed groups backed by Russia seized territory in the Donbas (Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts); fighting there continued for eight years despite the Minsk agreements, killing some 14,000 people.

Full-scale invasion

After a massive military buildup and demands that NATO bar Ukrainian membership, Russia invaded on 24 February 2022 from the north, east, and south. The initial drive on Kyiv failed and Russian forces withdrew from northern Ukraine in April 2022, concentrating on the Donbas and the south, where they occupied much of Kherson, Zaporizhzhia, Donetsk, and Luhansk oblasts; Russia proclaimed the annexation of those four regions in September 2022, a move not recognized internationally. Ukrainian counteroffensives retook Kharkiv Oblast and the city of Kherson in autumn 2022. The war evolved into attritional trench and artillery combat, extensive drone and missile strikes on infrastructure, and large-scale Western military aid to Ukraine, alongside sanctions on Russia. Casualty estimates for both armies by Western sources reached into the hundreds of thousands killed and wounded, and millions of Ukrainians fled abroad. Investigations documented alleged war crimes, including the Bucha killings and deportations of Ukrainian children, for which the International Criminal Court issued an arrest warrant for Russian President Vladimir Putin in March 2023.
