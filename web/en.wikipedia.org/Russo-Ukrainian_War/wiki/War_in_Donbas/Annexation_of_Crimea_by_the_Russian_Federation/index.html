Annexation of Crimea by the Russian Federation

The annexation of Crimea by the Russian Federation took place in February–March 2014 during the Russo-Ukrainian War, when Russia invaded and seized the Crimean Peninsula from Ukraine and incorporated it as two federal subjects — the Republic of Crimea and the federal city of Sevastopol. It was the first forcible annexation of territory in Europe since the Second World War and remains unrecognised by most of the international community, which continues to regard Crimea as Ukrainian territory under Russian occupation.

Background

Crimea had been transferred from the Russian SFSR to the Ukrainian SSR in 1954 and remained in Ukraine on the dissolution of the USSR, an arrangement Russia confirmed in the 1994 Budapest Memorandum and the 1997 Treaty on Friendship. The peninsula hosted Russia's Black Sea Fleet at Sevastopol under leasing agreements extended in 2010. After Ukraine's Euromaidan revolution drove President Viktor Yanukovych from power in February 2014, Moscow moved to secure Crimea.

The takeover

Beginning on 27 February 2014, unmarked Russian special forces — the "little green men" — seized Crimea's parliament, airports, and Ukrainian military bases. A hastily installed pro-Russian regional government announced a referendum, held on 16 March under military occupation without credible international observation, which official results said favoured joining Russia. On 18 March President Vladimir Putin signed a treaty of accession in Moscow. Ukrainian forces withdrew within days.

Reactions and consequences

The UN General Assembly adopted Resolution 68/262 affirming Ukraine's territorial integrity and declaring the referendum invalid. The United States, the European Union, and others imposed sanctions on Russia, which was suspended from the G8. Within Crimea, Crimean Tatars and pro-Ukrainian residents faced repression, including the banning of the Tatars' representative body, the Mejlis. The annexation set the template for the war in Donbas and, ultimately, Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.
