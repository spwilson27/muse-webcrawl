Standards organization

A standards organization, standards body, or standards developing organization (SDO) is an organization whose primary function is developing, coordinating, promulgating, revising, and publishing technical standards intended to address the needs of a group of affected adopters.

Types

Standards organizations may be international (such as the International Organization for Standardization, ISO), regional, or national (such as ANSI in the United States or BSI in the United Kingdom). Others are industry consortia, such as the W3C for the World Wide Web, or professional societies such as IEEE.

Standardization process

Standards are usually developed by consensus among interested parties — manufacturers, users, governments, and experts — and may become de facto requirements through market adoption or be incorporated into regulation.
