Salsa verde

Salsa verde (lit. "green sauce") is a type of spicy, green sauce in Mexican cuisine based on tomatillo and green chili peppers. It is one of the most common table sauces in Mexico and is used as a condiment and as a cooking sauce for dishes such as chilaquiles, enchiladas verdes and chile verde.

TypeSauce
Place of originMexico
Main ingredientsTomatillos, green chiles, onion, cilantro, garlic

Preparation

Tomatillos are husked and rinsed to remove their sticky coating, then either roasted, broiled or boiled until soft. They are blended with green chiles (commonly serrano or jalapeño), white onion, garlic, cilantro and salt. Raw (cruda), roasted (asada) and fried (frita) versions exist; in the fried version the blended sauce is cooked briefly in oil to deepen the flavour. The resulting sauce is tangy and herbaceous, its heat varying with the chiles chosen.

Other green sauces

The name "salsa verde" is also applied to unrelated green sauces elsewhere: an Italian sauce of parsley, capers and anchovies served with boiled meats, and various herb-based sauces in Spain, France (sauce verte) and South America, where chimichurri occupies a similar role. The Mexican tomatillo sauce is distinct from all of these.
