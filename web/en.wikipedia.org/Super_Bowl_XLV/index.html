Super Bowl XLV

Super Bowl XLV was an American football game between the American Football Conference (AFC) champion Pittsburgh Steelers and the National Football Conference (NFC) champion Green Bay Packers to decide the National Football League (NFL) champion for the 2010 season. The Packers defeated the Steelers 31–25 on February 6, 2011, at Cowboys Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

Super Bowl XLV
Date: February 6, 2011
Stadium: Cowboys Stadium, Arlington, Texas
Result: Green Bay Packers 31, Pittsburgh Steelers 25
MVP: Aaron Rodgers (Packers, quarterback)
Attendance: 91,060

Game summary

Green Bay raced to an 18-point lead, helped by Nick Collins's interception return for a touchdown. Pittsburgh rallied behind Ben Roethlisberger to cut the deficit to 28–25 in the fourth quarter, but a forced fumble by Clay Matthews halted the Steelers' momentum. Quarterback Aaron Rodgers threw for 304 yards and three touchdowns and was named MVP; a late Steelers drive stalled near midfield, sealing the Packers' fourth Super Bowl title.

Broadcast

The game was televised in the United States by Fox and drew an average audience of about 111 million viewers, then a record for American television.
