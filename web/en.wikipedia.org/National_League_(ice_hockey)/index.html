National League (ice hockey)

Founded: 1937 (as Nationalliga A)

Country: Switzerland

Teams: 14

Most titles: HC Davos

The National League (NL) is the top professional ice hockey league in Switzerland. It is widely regarded as one of the strongest leagues in Europe, regularly ranking among the leaders in attendance for European hockey. The league was known as Nationalliga A from its founding until 2017, when it adopted its current English name. Its champion is determined by playoffs following the regular season, and the league sits above the Swiss League in the Swiss ice hockey league system.

Format

The league's 14 teams play a regular season of about 52 games, facing each opponent multiple times. The top teams qualify for the playoffs, contested as best-of-seven series, culminating in the Swiss championship. The lowest-ranked teams face a playout series, and the loser may face relegation against the champion of the second-tier Swiss League in a league qualification series, subject to licensing criteria.

History and profile

Swiss club hockey traces its championship to 1909, with the Nationalliga A era beginning in 1937. HC Davos is the most successful club historically, while SC Bern has set European attendance records, averaging more than 16,000 spectators per game. The league permits a limited number of import players per team on the ice, which has helped attract notable foreign players, and its clubs compete in the Champions Hockey League.
