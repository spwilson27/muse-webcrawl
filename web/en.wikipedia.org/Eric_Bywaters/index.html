Eric Bywaters

Eric Bywaters is a New Zealand politician and a member of the New Zealand House of Representatives. He was elected to Parliament in the 2023 general election as a list MP for the New Zealand Labour Party after contesting the election in the Taranaki region.

Background

Before entering national politics, Bywaters worked in the education sector and was involved in union advocacy, including roles connected with secondary teachers' representation. He also served in local community organisations in the Taranaki area.

Parliamentary career

Bywaters entered Parliament following the 2023 election, in which Labour lost government to the National Party-led coalition. As an opposition list MP, he has been associated with portfolios relating to education and regional development, speaking on issues affecting provincial New Zealand.
