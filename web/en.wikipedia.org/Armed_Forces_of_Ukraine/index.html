Armed Forces of Ukraine

Armed Forces of Ukraine
Founded: 1991
Headquarters: Kyiv
Supreme commander: President of Ukraine
Branches: Ground Forces, Air Force, Navy, Air Assault Forces, Marine Corps, Special Operations Forces, Territorial Defense Forces

The Armed Forces of Ukraine are the military forces of Ukraine. They were formed from the Ukrainian units of the Soviet Armed Forces after Ukraine declared independence from the Soviet Union in 1991.

History

Ukraine inherited one of the largest military forces in Europe, including nuclear weapons, which it gave up under the 1994 Budapest Memorandum in exchange for security assurances. Chronic underfunding reduced the force over the following two decades. After Russia's annexation of Crimea and the outbreak of war in the Donbas in 2014, Ukraine began a major rebuilding program with Western assistance.

Russo-Ukrainian War

Since Russia's full-scale invasion began on 24 February 2022, the Armed Forces of Ukraine have fought to defend the country, mobilizing large numbers of personnel and receiving extensive military aid from NATO members and other partners.

Structure

The Commander-in-Chief leads the armed forces under the authority of the President, who is Supreme Commander. The General Staff, headquartered in Kyiv, directs operations.
