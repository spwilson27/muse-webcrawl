Queensland rugby league team

The Queensland rugby league team, nicknamed the Maroons after the colour of their jerseys, represents the Australian state of Queensland in rugby league football. The team has existed since the sport arrived in Queensland in 1908, but it is best known for its participation since 1980 in the annual State of Origin series against the New South Wales Blues, one of Australian sport's fiercest rivalries.


The Maroons at Lang Park, Brisbane

History

Queensland first played New South Wales in 1908, but for most of the 20th century the interstate contest was lopsided: Queensland's best players routinely signed with wealthy Sydney clubs and, under residency rules, turned out for New South Wales against their own state. The introduction of "state of origin" selection in 1980 — players representing the state where they first played senior football — transformed the contest. Queensland won the first Origin match at Lang Park under captain-coach Arthur Beetson, whose selection became a founding legend of the series.

The Origin era

Champion five-eighth Wally Lewis, "the King", dominated the 1980s, and later generations — Mal Meninga, Allan Langer, Darren Lockyer, Johnathan Thurston, Cameron Smith and Billy Slater — sustained an extraordinary dynasty. Between 2006 and 2017 Queensland won eleven of twelve series, including a record eight consecutive series victories from 2006 to 2013. The Maroons' home ground is Lang Park (Suncorp Stadium) in Brisbane.
