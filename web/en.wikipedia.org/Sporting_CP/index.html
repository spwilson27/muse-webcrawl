Sporting Clube de Portugal, otherwise referred to as Sporting CP or simply Sporting, and colloquially known outside Portugal as Sporting Lisbon, is a Portuguese sports club based in Lisbon. Founded on 1 July 1906, it is one of the "Big Three" clubs in Portugal that have never been relegated from the Primeira Liga, along with rivals SL Benfica and FC Porto.

History

Sporting have won numerous Primeira Liga titles and Taça de Portugal trophies, and won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1964. The club's academy at Alcochete is renowned for producing world-class players, including Cristiano Ronaldo and Luís Figo. Sporting's home ground is the Estádio José Alvalade, opened in 2003.

Other sports

Beyond football, Sporting fields teams in athletics, futsal, handball, basketball and many other sports, and is one of the most decorated multi-sport clubs in Europe.
