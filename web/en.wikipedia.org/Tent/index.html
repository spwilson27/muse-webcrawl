Tent

A tent is a shelter consisting of sheets of fabric or other material draped over, attached to, or suspended from a frame of poles or a supporting rope. Tents range from small one-person bivouac shelters to enormous circus tents capable of seating thousands of people.

History

Tents have been used as nomadic dwellings since prehistory; the Roman army used leather tents, and nomadic peoples of Central Asia developed the felt-covered yurt. Traditional tents include the ridge tent, the bell tent, and the tipi of the North American Plains peoples.

Modern tents

Modern recreational tents are usually made of lightweight waterproof fabrics such as nylon or polyester, supported by flexible poles of aluminium or fibreglass. Common designs include dome tents, tunnel tents, and geodesic tents. Tents are also used for military shelter, refugee housing, festivals, and events.
