Essay mill

Essay mills market custom-written assignments to students

An essay mill (also called a paper mill) is a business that allows customers to commission an original piece of writing on a particular topic so that they may commit academic dishonesty by passing the work off as their own. The work is typically a term paper, essay, coursework assignment, or a higher-education dissertation. Essay mills are a form of contract cheating.

History and operation

Paper mills existed by the mid-twentieth century, selling research papers from shops near university campuses. The rise of the internet greatly expanded the industry: customers place orders online specifying topic, length, academic level, and deadline, and freelance writers — often in countries with lower labour costs — produce the work for a fee. Some companies also operate "essay bank" websites offering free pre-written essays in exchange for uploads, which serve both as marketing and as sources of searchable text.

Legal and institutional responses

Using a purchased essay is treated as academic misconduct by universities and can lead to expulsion. Several jurisdictions have moved against the providers: England made it an offence to provide or advertise contract-cheating services under the Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022, and Australia criminalised the provision and advertising of academic cheating services in 2020. New Zealand and Ireland have similar laws. Countermeasures include plagiarism-detection software, though bespoke essays are written to order and are not copied, making them harder to detect; assessment redesign and oral examinations are also used.

Research paper mills

The term "paper mill" is also used for organisations that produce fabricated or low-quality scientific manuscripts for sale to researchers seeking publications, a practice linked to mass retractions by academic publishers in the 2020s.
