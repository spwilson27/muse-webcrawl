Bitter lemon

Bitter lemon is a bitter-tasting, carbonated soft drink flavoured with lemon and quinine. It is a variant of tonic water, typically made with lemon juice and pith combined with sweetened carbonated water.

Description

The drink's bitterness comes primarily from quinine, while the lemon component includes juice and oils from the peel, giving it a sharper citrus taste than lemonade. It is consumed on its own or as a mixer, for example with gin or vodka.

History

Bitter lemon was popularised in the mid-20th century, with Schweppes among the first major manufacturers to market it commercially in the 1950s.
