Wings (band)

OriginLondon, England
Years active1971–1981
GenresRock, pop rock, soft rock
Key membersPaul McCartney, Linda McCartney, Denny Laine

Wings, also known as Paul McCartney and Wings, were a British–American rock band formed in 1971 in London by Paul McCartney, his wife Linda McCartney (keyboards), session drummer Denny Seiwell, and former Moody Blues guitarist Denny Laine. The band went through several line-up changes but became one of the most commercially successful groups of the 1970s.

Career

After a low-key debut with Wild Life (1971) and Red Rose Speedway (1973), Wings achieved major success with Band on the Run (1973), recorded in Lagos, Nigeria, which topped charts on both sides of the Atlantic and included the hit singles "Jet" and the title track. Subsequent albums included Venus and Mars (1975) and Wings at the Speed of Sound (1976), the latter promoted by the Wings over the World tour. The single "Mull of Kintyre" (1977) became one of the best-selling singles in UK history.

Break-up and legacy

Following McCartney's 1980 drug arrest in Japan and John Lennon's murder the same year, Wings effectively dissolved in 1981. Denny Laine died in 2023. The band's catalogue remains widely popular; a comprehensive documentary and anthology projects in the 2020s renewed interest in the group's history.
