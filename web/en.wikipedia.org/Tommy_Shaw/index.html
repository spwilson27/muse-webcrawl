Tommy Roland Shaw (born 11 September 1953) is an American guitarist, singer and songwriter best known as a member of the rock band Styx, with whom he wrote and sang hits including "Renegade", "Blue Collar Man (Long Nights)", "Fooling Yourself (The Angry Young Man)" and "Too Much Time on My Hands". He was also a member of the supergroup Damn Yankees and records in the duo Shaw Blades with Jack Blades.

Career

Shaw was born in Montgomery, Alabama, and played in local bands including The Smoke Ring and MSFunk. In late 1975 he was invited to audition for the Chicago band Styx after the departure of guitarist John Curulewski, joining in time for the album Crystal Ball (1976), which included his song "Mademoiselle". Shaw's contributions became central to Styx's commercial peak on The Grand Illusion (1977), Pieces of Eight (1978) and Cornerstone (1979); his "Renegade" became one of the band's signature songs.

After Styx's hiatus in the mid-1980s, Shaw released solo albums including Girls with Guns (1984), whose title track was a modest hit. In 1989 he formed Damn Yankees with Ted Nugent, Jack Blades and Michael Cartellone, scoring hits with "High Enough" and "Coming of Age". In 1995 he released Influence with Jack Blades as Shaw Blades, and from 1996 he rejoined Styx, with whom he has toured and recorded ever since, including the concept albums The Mission (2017) and Crash of the Crown (2021). His bluegrass-leaning solo album The Great Divide appeared in 2011. In 2025 Styx were announced as inductees into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame's orbit of long-eligible acts; Shaw remains the band's co-lead vocalist and guitarist alongside James "JY" Young.
