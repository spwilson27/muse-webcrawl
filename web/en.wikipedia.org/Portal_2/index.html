Portal 2 is a 2011 puzzle-platform video game developed and published by Valve for Windows, Mac OS X, Linux, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360. The sequel to Portal (2007), it was released as a standalone retail product and is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made.

Gameplay

The player controls Chell in the single-player campaign and one of two robots, ATLAS and P-body, in the cooperative campaign. Using the Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device, the player creates two linked portals on suitable surfaces and uses them — together with momentum conservation — to solve test chambers. Portal 2 adds new mechanics including tractor beams (Excursion Funnels), light bridges, lasers, and mobility gels (Propulsion, Repulsion and Conversion gels) that change how surfaces behave. A level editor and Steam Workshop support were added after release.

Plot

Awakened long after the first game by the personality core Wheatley (voiced by Stephen Merchant), Chell accidentally reactivates the rogue AI GLaDOS (Ellen McLain). After Wheatley betrays Chell and seizes control of the Aperture Science facility, the two descend into its abandoned mid-century depths, uncovering the history of founder Cave Johnson (J. K. Simmons). The cooperative campaign follows two robots testing under GLaDOS's direction.

Development and reception

Valve expanded the concept of the short original into a full-length game, hiring the DigiPen students behind Tag: The Power of Paint for the gel mechanics. The game received universal acclaim for its puzzles, writing, voice acting and dark humour, won multiple awards including BAFTAs, and sold over four million copies in its first year. Its songs, including Jonathan Coulton's "Want You Gone", became popular in their own right.
