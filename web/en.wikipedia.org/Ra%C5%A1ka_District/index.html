Raška District

The Raška District (Serbian: Рашки округ, Raški okrug) is one of the administrative districts of Serbia, located in the southwestern part of the country. It encompasses the municipalities of Kraljevo, Novi Pazar, Raška, Vrnjačka Banja, and Tutin. The administrative centre of the district is the city of Kraljevo. The district has a population of roughly 300,000 and covers an area of about 3,900 square kilometres.

Geography and demographics

The district takes its name from the historical Raška region and the Raška river, a tributary of the Ibar. The population is mixed: Serbs form a majority overall, while Bosniaks are a majority in Novi Pazar and Tutin. Novi Pazar is the cultural centre of the Sandžak region.

Culture and tourism

The district contains some of Serbia's most important medieval monuments, including the Studenica Monastery, a UNESCO World Heritage Site founded by Stefan Nemanja, and the monuments of Stari Ras and Sopoćani near Novi Pazar. The spa town of Vrnjačka Banja is one of Serbia's best-known health resorts, and the Kopaonik mountain area near Raška supports winter tourism.
