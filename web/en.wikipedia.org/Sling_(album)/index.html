Sling is the second studio album by American singer-songwriter Clairo (Claire Cottrill), released on 16 July 2021. Produced by Clairo with Jack Antonoff and recorded largely at Allaire Studios in upstate New York, the album marked a turn from the bedroom pop of her debut Immunity (2019) toward 1970s-influenced folk and soft rock.

Background

Clairo wrote much of the album during the COVID-19 pandemic, reflecting on fame, mental health, domesticity and her dog Joanie, who inspired the title and much of the album's imagery. The lead single, "Blouse", was released in June 2021 and performed on The Tonight Show.

Music and themes

The arrangements — piano, strings, woodwinds and close harmonies — recall artists such as Carole King and the Carpenters. Songs including "Amoeba", "Wade", "Harbor" and "Joanie" explore anxiety, the desire for a quieter life, and ambivalence about a career in music. Lorde contributed backing vocals to several tracks.

Reception

Sling received generally positive reviews, with critics praising its warmth, craftsmanship and maturity; it debuted at number 17 on the US Billboard 200. Some reviewers found the album's restraint muted compared with her earlier work, but it consolidated Clairo's reputation as a songwriter and preceded her third album, Charm (2024).

Track listing

Highlights include "Bambi", "Blouse", "Harbor", "Just for Today", "Amoeba", "Partridge", "Zinnias", "Wade", "North", "Joanie", "Reaper" and "Management".
