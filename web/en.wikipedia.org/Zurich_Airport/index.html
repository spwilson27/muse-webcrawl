Zurich Airport (IATA: ZRH, ICAO: LSZH) is the largest international airport of Switzerland and the principal hub of Swiss International Air Lines. It is located 13 kilometres (8 mi) north of central Zurich, in the municipalities of Kloten, Rümlang, Oberglatt, Winkel and Opfikon, all in the canton of Zurich.

Zurich Airport, Switzerland's largest international gateway.

History

The first foreign airlines began flying to Switzerland in 1921 near Dübendorf, which served as Zurich's civil and military airfield until a dedicated civil airport was planned in the 1940s. Construction at Kloten began in 1946, and the west runway opened on 14 June 1948. The airport has since been expanded in several stages, most notably with the Dock B and Dock E (midfield) terminals and the Airside Center.

Terminals and transport

Passenger facilities consist of Terminals 1 and 2 with docks A, B/D and E, connected by the underground Skymetro people mover to the satellite Dock E. The airport railway station beneath the terminal complex offers direct trains to Zurich Hauptbahnhof in about ten minutes, as well as long-distance services across Switzerland. In 2019 the airport handled a record 31.5 million passengers.
