Fees Must Fall

#FeesMustFall was a South African student protest movement that began in October 2015.

Fees Must Fall (stylised as #FeesMustFall) was a student-led protest movement that began in mid-October 2015 in South Africa. The goals of the movement were to stop increases in student fees as well as to increase government funding of universities. Protests started at the University of the Witwatersrand and spread to the University of Cape Town, Rhodes University, and campuses across the country.

Background

The movement followed the Rhodes Must Fall campaign earlier in 2015, which had secured the removal of a statue of Cecil Rhodes at the University of Cape Town and raised broader demands for the decolonisation of education. When universities announced fee increases of around 10–12% for 2016 amid stagnant student funding, students mobilised, blockading campuses and marching on Parliament in Cape Town and the Union Buildings in Pretoria.

Outcome

On 23 October 2015, President Jacob Zuma announced that there would be no fee increase for 2016. Protests flared again in 2016 over demands for free, decolonised higher education, with some campuses closed for extended periods and clashes between students, private security, and police. In December 2017, Zuma announced free higher education for poor and working-class students, to be phased in from 2018. The movement is regarded as one of the most significant post-apartheid protest movements in South Africa.
