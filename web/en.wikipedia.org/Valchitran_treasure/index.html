Valchitran treasure
Discovered1924, near Valchitran, Bulgaria
PeriodLate Bronze Age (c. 1300 BCE)
MaterialGold

The Valchitran treasure is a Thracian gold hoard discovered in 1924 near the village of Valchitran in Pleven Province, northern Bulgaria. It is the largest known gold treasure from Bronze Age Bulgaria.

Discovery and contents

Workmen ploughing a vineyard uncovered 13 vessels of high-purity gold with a combined weight of about 12.5 kilograms. The hoard includes large and small cups, lids, and a distinctive three-part vessel possibly used for ritual libations or mixing liquids.

Dating and significance

The treasure is dated to the late Bronze Age, around 1300 BCE, and attests to the wealth and craftsmanship of Thracian elites. It is exhibited at the National Archaeological Museum in Sofia, with replicas shown in regional museums.
