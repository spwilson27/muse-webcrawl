Jeremy McGrath (born November 19, 1971) is an American former professional motocross and supercross racer, widely regarded as one of the most popular and influential riders in the history of the sport. Nicknamed the "King of Supercross" and "Showtime", he won seven AMA Supercross championships during the 1990s and holds the all-time record for supercross main-event wins with 72.

Career

McGrath began riding in Southern California and turned professional in 1989. After winning two 125cc West regional supercross titles, he moved to the 250cc class with Honda in 1993 and immediately won the championship, beginning a run of four consecutive titles. After a difficult 1997 season with Suzuki, he joined Yamaha and won three more championships from 1998 to 2000. His smooth, BMX-influenced style and showmanship—including the "nac-nac" trick he popularised—helped bring supercross to a mainstream audience in the 1990s.

Later career

McGrath retired from full-time competition in 2003 but continued racing selected events, later competing in off-road truck racing and supermoto, and making appearances at invitational events. He was inducted into the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame.
