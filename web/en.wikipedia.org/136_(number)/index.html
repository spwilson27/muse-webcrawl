136 (number)

136 (one hundred [and] thirty-six) is the natural number following 135 and preceding 137.

In mathematics

136 is a composite number with divisors 1, 2, 4, 8, 17, 34, 68 and 136; it factorises as 23 × 17. It is the 16th triangular number: the sum of the first 16 natural numbers is 136. It is also a centred triangular number and a centred nonagonal number, and it is a refactorable number, since it is divisible by the number of its divisors (8).

The sum of its digits in the decimal representation pattern is notable in that 1 + 3 + 6 = 10. Its cube, 1363 = 2,515,456, plays no special role, but 136 is sometimes cited in recreational mathematics because the last three digits of 1362 (18,496) end in ...496. In the Eddington number context, 136 was once the hypothesised value of the reciprocal of the fine-structure constant before more precise measurement settled near 137.036.

In other fields

136 is the atomic number of the yet-unsynthesised element unhexhexium. It is also the number of the United States Interstate highway I-136 is not in use, but Illinois Route 136 and numerous other highways carry the designation.
