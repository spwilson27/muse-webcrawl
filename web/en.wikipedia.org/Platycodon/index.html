Platycodon

Platycodon grandiflorus, commonly called the balloon flower or Chinese bellflower, is a species of herbaceous perennial flowering plant in the bellflower family, Campanulaceae. It is the only member of the genus Platycodon and is native to East Asia, including China, Korea, Japan and the Russian Far East.

Description

The plant grows to about 60 cm tall with broad, lance-shaped leaves. Its flower buds inflate like small balloons before opening into bell-shaped blue, violet, pink or white flowers, giving the species its common name.

Uses

Platycodon is widely grown as an ornamental. In Korea its root, called doraji, is eaten as a vegetable and used in traditional medicine; the root is rich in saponins and is also used in Chinese herbal medicine (as jié gěng), mainly for coughs and respiratory complaints.
