Roosevelt family

The Roosevelt family is an American political and business family of Dutch origin, descended from Claes Maartenszen van Rosenvelt, who arrived in New Amsterdam in the 1640s. From estates at Oyster Bay, Long Island, and Hyde Park in the Hudson Valley, the family produced two presidents of the United States.

Branches

The two branches descend from the sons of Isaac Roosevelt. The Oyster Bay branch produced Theodore Roosevelt (1858–1919), the 26th president, and his niece Eleanor Roosevelt, diplomat and First Lady. The Hyde Park branch produced Eleanor's husband and Theodore's fifth cousin, Franklin D. Roosevelt (1882–1945), the 32nd president, who led the country through the Great Depression and most of the Second World War.

Prominence

Roosevelts married into other notable families, served in Congress, the military and diplomacy, and built fortunes in banking, real estate and trade. Family homes — Sagamore Hill, Springwood and Val-Kill — are preserved as national historic sites. See also Theodore Roosevelt and Franklin D. Roosevelt.
