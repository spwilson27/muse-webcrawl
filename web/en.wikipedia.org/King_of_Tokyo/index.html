King of Tokyo

King of Tokyo is a tabletop board game designed by Richard Garfield and first published in 2011 by Iello. Players control giant monsters, mutants and robots battling for control of Tokyo, rolling dice to attack rivals, heal, gain energy and score victory points.

Designer: Richard Garfield
Publisher: Iello
Players: 2–6
Playing time: about 30 minutes

Gameplay

On a turn a player rolls six custom dice up to three times, keeping chosen results. Dice show numbers for victory points, claws for attacks, hearts for healing and lightning bolts for energy cubes. A monster in Tokyo attacks all others but is attacked by all in return; yielding Tokyo lets another monster take the space. The game is won by reaching 20 victory points or being the last monster standing.

Expansions and legacy

Expansions including Power Up! and Halloween added evolution cards and new monsters. A sequel, King of New York, appeared in 2014. The game's accessible push-your-luck dice play made it a popular family gateway game.
