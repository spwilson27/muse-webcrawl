Qaanaaq

Qaanaaq is a town in the Avannaata municipality in northwestern Greenland, and one of the northernmost permanent settlements in the world. It had about 600–650 inhabitants in recent years. The town was founded in 1953 when the Inughuit population of the old settlement of Thule (Uummannaq) was forcibly relocated to make way for the expansion of the United States Thule Air Base (now Pituffik Space Base).

Geography

Qaanaaq sits on the northern shore of the Inglefield Fjord (Inglefield Bredning) on the Hayes Peninsula. The surrounding district, sometimes called Avanersuaq or the Thule region, is home to the Inughuit, the northernmost Greenlandic Inuit group, whose traditional hunting culture centers on narwhal, seal, walrus, and polar bear. The settlement of Siorapaluk, further north, is among the northernmost inhabited places on Earth, and the now-abandoned Etah was a starting point for historic Arctic expeditions.

Transport

Qaanaaq Airport links the town to Upernavik and to Pituffik; supply ships call during the short ice-free season.
