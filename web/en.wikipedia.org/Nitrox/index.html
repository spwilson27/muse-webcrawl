Nitrox

Nitrox refers to any gas mixture composed of nitrogen and oxygen. In recreational and technical scuba diving the term usually means enriched air nitrox (EAN), a breathing gas with a higher oxygen fraction than air — most commonly 32% or 36% oxygen (EAN32 and EAN36).

Use in diving

Reducing the nitrogen fraction lowers nitrogen uptake, extending no-decompression limits and shortening surface intervals. The trade-off is a shallower maximum operating depth, because the higher oxygen partial pressure raises the risk of oxygen toxicity; divers must analyse their gas, mark the cylinder with the blend and maximum depth, and plan accordingly.
