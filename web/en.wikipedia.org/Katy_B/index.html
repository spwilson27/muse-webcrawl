Kathleen Anne Brien (born 8 May 1989), better known as Katy B, is an English singer and songwriter. She released her debut album, On a Mission, in 2011, which spawned two top-five singles, "Katy on a Mission" and "Lights On". Her work helped bring UK underground club styles — dubstep, garage, and funky — into the pop mainstream.

Early life

Katy B was born in Peckham, south London, and attended the BRIT School for Performing Arts and Technology in Croydon. She began singing on pirate radio and collaborating with producers in the UK funky and dubstep scenes, including features on tracks by DJ NG and Magnetic Man.

Career

Signed to the Rinse label, she released "Katy on a Mission" (produced by Benga) in 2010, reaching number five on the UK Singles Chart. Her debut album On a Mission (2011) was nominated for the Mercury Prize. She followed with the EP Danger (2012), the album Little Red (2014) — which reached number one in the UK and included the number-one single "Crying for No Reason" — and the collaborative project album Honey (2016), featuring producers and artists such as Kaytranada, Craig David, and Major Lazer. Her fourth album, Peace and Offerings, appeared in 2021.
