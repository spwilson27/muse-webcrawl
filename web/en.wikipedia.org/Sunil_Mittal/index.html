Sunil Mittal

Sunil Bharti Mittal (born 23 October 1957) is an Indian billionaire businessman and philanthropist. He is the founder and chairman of Bharti Enterprises, whose interests span telecom, media, financial services, and space communications. Its flagship company, Bharti Airtel, is one of the largest mobile network operators in India and Africa.

Early life and education

Mittal was born in Ludhiana, Punjab, the son of Sat Paul Mittal, a Member of Parliament. He graduated from Panjab University with a bachelor's degree in economics and political science.

Career

Mittal began his entrepreneurial career in 1976 making bicycle parts in Ludhiana, later moving into importing and manufacturing, including push-button telephones in the 1980s. In 1995 Bharti Cellular Limited won a licence to operate mobile services in Delhi, launching the Airtel brand. Under his leadership Airtel expanded across India and into Africa, pioneering low-cost, high-volume business models including outsourcing network operations and pioneering minute-factory style tariff pricing.

He has served as president of the Confederation of Indian Industry and has held leadership roles in international telecom bodies, including chairman of the GSM Association. In 2024 his company took a major stake in the UK telecom group BT.

Philanthropy and honours

Through the Bharti Foundation he has supported education for underprivileged children in rural India, notably through the Satya Bharti School Program. Mittal received the Padma Bhushan, India's third-highest civilian award, in 2007, and in 2025 became the first Indian citizen to receive an honorary knighthood (KBE) from King Charles III, awarded in recognition of services to UK–India business relations.
