The M142 High Mobility Artillery Rocket System (HIMARS) is an American light multiple rocket launcher mounted on a wheeled truck chassis. Developed by Lockheed Martin, it carries a single launch pod containing either six GPS-guided rockets or one ATACMS tactical ballistic missile — the same ordnance as the heavier tracked M270 MLRS, but on a lighter, air-transportable vehicle.

Design

HIMARS is mounted on a 5-ton Medium Tactical Vehicle truck with an armoured cab for a crew of three. Its wheeled chassis gives it road mobility far beyond that of tracked launchers, and it can be carried by a C-130 transport aircraft. Fire control is automated, allowing a mission to be executed in minutes before the launcher displaces to avoid counter-battery fire.

Operational history

The system entered United States service in 2010 and saw combat use in Afghanistan and against the Islamic State in Iraq and Syria. From 2022, HIMARS launchers supplied to Ukraine gained prominence for precision strikes against Russian command posts, ammunition depots and logistics, substantially affecting the war's course. Numerous allied nations have since ordered the system.
