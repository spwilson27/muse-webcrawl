Death of Diana, Princess of Wales

In the early hours of 31 August 1997, Diana, Princess of Wales, died from injuries sustained in a car crash in the Pont de l'Alma tunnel in Paris. Her companion Dodi Fayed and their driver, Henri Paul, were also killed; bodyguard Trevor Rees-Jones survived with serious injuries. Diana was 36 years old.

The crash

Diana and Fayed had left the Ritz Paris shortly after midnight, pursued by paparazzi photographers. Their Mercedes-Benz S280, driven by Ritz security chief Henri Paul, entered the Alma underpass at high speed — estimated well above the 50 km/h limit — lost control, struck a Fiat Uno, and collided head-on with a tunnel pillar. Fayed and Paul died at the scene. Diana was taken to the Pitié-Salpêtrière Hospital, where she died of internal injuries, principally a torn pulmonary vein, despite surgery.

Investigations

A French investigation (1999) concluded that Paul, found to have a blood alcohol level well above the legal limit and traces of prescription drugs, lost control while speeding to escape photographers; the paparazzi were cleared of manslaughter. The British Metropolitan Police's Operation Paget report (2006) reached essentially the same conclusion, and the 2007–08 British inquest returned a verdict of unlawful killing, citing the grossly negligent driving of Paul and the pursuing paparazzi, the lack of seatbelts, and the impairment of the driver. Despite the official findings, conspiracy theories — promoted especially by Mohamed Al-Fayed — have persisted.

Reaction and funeral

The death provoked an extraordinary outpouring of public grief in Britain and worldwide. Criticism of the royal family's initial reserve ("Show us you care," ran one headline) led Queen Elizabeth II to broadcast live to the nation on 5 September. Diana's funeral at Westminster Abbey on 6 September 1997 was watched by an estimated 2.5 billion people; Elton John performed a rewritten "Candle in the Wind 1997," which became the best-selling single of all time. She was buried on an island at Althorp, her family home.
