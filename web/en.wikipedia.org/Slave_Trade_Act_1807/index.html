Slave Trade Act 1807

The Slave Trade Act 1807, officially an Act for the Abolition of the Slave Trade, was an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom prohibiting the slave trade in the British Empire. It was passed on 25 March 1807 and came into effect on 1 May 1807.

The Act abolished the trade in enslaved people but did not abolish slavery itself, which continued in most British colonies until the Slavery Abolition Act 1833. The Royal Navy's West Africa Squadron was established to enforce the ban by intercepting slave ships.

The Act was the culmination of a long campaign led by figures including William Wilberforce, Thomas Clarkson, and the Committee for the Abolition of the Slave Trade, founded in 1787, alongside resistance by enslaved people themselves in the colonies.
