List of Formula One World Championship races

Formula One, abbreviated F1, is the highest class of open-wheeled auto racing defined by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The World Championship season consists of a series of races, known as Grands Prix, held around the world on purpose-built circuits and closed public roads.

History

The first World Championship race was the 1950 British Grand Prix at Silverstone. Since then, dozens of differently named Grands Prix have appeared on the calendar, including the Monaco, Italian, British, Belgian and German Grands Prix.

Notable races

The Italian Grand Prix at Monza and the British Grand Prix have been held in nearly every season. Races are typically named after the host country or city, such as the Japanese, Brazilian, Las Vegas and Miami Grands Prix. The Indianapolis 500 counted toward the championship from 1950 to 1960.
