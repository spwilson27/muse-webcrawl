Dennis DeYoung (born 18 February 1947) is an American singer, songwriter, keyboardist and record producer. He is a founding member of the rock band Styx, for which he served as primary lead vocalist and keyboardist from 1970 until 1999, and wrote many of the group's best-known songs.

Styx

DeYoung formed the band that became Styx in Chicago with brothers Chuck and John Panozzo and later James Young and Tommy Shaw. His compositions include the ballad "Lady" (1973), the band's first hit; "Come Sail Away" (1977); the chart-topping "Babe" (1979); and "Mr. Roboto" (1983), the centrepiece of the rock-opera concept album Kilroy Was Here, which DeYoung conceived. Creative tensions over his theatrical direction led to his departure from Styx in 1999.

Solo career

His debut solo album Desert Moon (1984) produced the top-ten single of the same name. He also composed the musical The Hunchback of Notre Dame (1997). Since leaving Styx he has toured performing the band's music with his own group and released the albums 26 East, Vol. 1 (2020) and Vol. 2 (2021), the latter presented as his final studio album.
