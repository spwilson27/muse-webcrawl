Navy Midshipmen

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Navy Midshipmen are the athletic teams that represent the United States Naval Academy in Annapolis, Maryland. The Midshipmen compete in NCAA Division I; most teams play in the Patriot League, while the football team competes as a member of the American Athletic Conference. Navy fields varsity teams in some thirty sports, more than most service academies.

Football

Navy football is best known for its fierce rivalry with Army, culminating each December in the Army–Navy Game, first played in 1890. The Midshipmen have produced two Heisman Trophy winners — Joe Bellino (1960) and Roger Staubach (1963) — and were named national champions for 1926. Under coach Paul Johnson and his successor Ken Niumatalolo, Navy became a fixture in bowl games running the triple-option offense.

Other sports and traditions

Navy traditionally dominates the Army–Navy series in many Olympic sports and contests the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy with Army and Air Force. The school's mascot is Bill the Goat, and its colors are navy blue and gold. See also United States Naval Academy and Army–Navy Game.
