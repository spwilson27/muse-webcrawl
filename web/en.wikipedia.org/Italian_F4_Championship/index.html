The Italian F4 Championship (Italian: Campionato Italiano F4), officially the Italian F.4 Championship powered by Abarth, is a single-seater racing series based in Italy, run to FIA Formula 4 regulations. Inaugurated in 2014 as the first national championship launched under the FIA's new Formula 4 framework, it replaced Formula Abarth and quickly became one of the most competitive junior formulae in the world, serving as a proven stepping stone towards Formula Regional, Formula 3, and ultimately Formula One.

History

The championship was created by the Automobile Club d'Italia (ACI) through its sporting arm ACI Sport, adopting Gerhard Berger and the FIA's vision of an affordable entry-level category above karting. The inaugural 2014 season was won by Canadian Lance Stroll, who went on to race in Formula One. Large grids — often exceeding 30 cars — and races at classic Italian venues such as Monza, Imola, Mugello, Vallelunga, and Misano made the series a magnet for international karting graduates.

Format and car

Each event typically comprises three races across a weekend, with qualifying sessions setting the grids and championship points awarded on the Formula One scale for the top ten finishers in each race. Since its inception the series has used Tatuus chassis powered by Abarth-branded 1.4-litre turbocharged engines producing around 160 horsepower. For 2022 the championship adopted the second-generation Tatuus F4-T421 chassis, incorporating the halo cockpit protection device and improved safety standards.

Notable champions and graduates

Champions include Lance Stroll (2014), Ralf Aron (2015), Marcos Siebert (2016), Marcus Armstrong (2017), Enzo Fittipaldi (2018), Dennis Hauger (2019), Gabriele Minì (2020), Oliver Bearman (2021), Rafael Câmara (2022), Kacper Sztuka (2023), and Freddie Slater (2024). Hauger, Bearman, and Andrea Kimi Antonelli — who dominated Italian junior racing in 2022 — illustrate the championship's role in the modern driver development ladder; Bearman and Antonelli both reached Formula One in 2025.

Related series

The championship sits alongside the F4 Eurocup/Euro 4 Championship, also organised by ACI Sport and WSK Promotion, launched in 2023 to provide additional high-level Formula 4 racing in Italy and neighbouring countries.
