Machairodontinae
Common name: Sabre-toothed cats
Family: Felidae
Range: Miocene to Pleistocene (extinct)

Machairodontinae is an extinct subfamily of carnivoran mammals of the cat family Felidae, known as sabre-toothed cats, characterised by their extremely long, flattened upper canine teeth. They flourished from the Miocene until the end of the Pleistocene, about 10,000 years ago.

Genera

The subfamily includes famous genera such as Smilodon (the sabre-toothed tiger), Homotherium, Machairodus and Megantereon.

Hunting

Despite their name, machairodonts were not closely related to modern tigers. Their forelimbs were powerful, suggesting they grappled large prey before delivering a shearing bite with the sabre canines.
