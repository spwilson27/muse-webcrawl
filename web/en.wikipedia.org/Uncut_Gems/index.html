Uncut Gems

Uncut Gems is a 2019 American crime thriller film directed by Josh and Benny Safdie, who co-wrote the screenplay with Ronald Bronstein. It stars Adam Sandler as Howard Ratner, a charismatic, gambling-addicted jeweller in New York City's Diamond District.

Set in 2012, the film follows Howard as he obtains a rare uncut black opal from an Ethiopian mine and tries to auction it to pay off his escalating debts—while making a series of increasingly reckless sports bets, including on Boston Celtics star Kevin Garnett, who plays himself and becomes obsessed with the opal. The cast includes Lakeith Stanfield, Julia Fox in her film debut, Idina Menzel, and Eric Bogosian, with a score by Daniel Lopatin (Oneohtrix Point Never).

The film premiered at the Telluride Film Festival in August 2019 and was released by A24 in the United States and by Netflix internationally. It was acclaimed for its frenetic direction and for Sandler's dramatic performance, widely considered among the best of his career, and it became one of A24's highest-grossing films at the time. Sandler won the Independent Spirit Award for Best Male Lead.
