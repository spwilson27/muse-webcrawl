Eduard Hoffmann

Eduard Hoffmann may refer to several notable people. This page summarises the principal bearers of the name in history, including figures in engineering, commerce, and the arts from German-speaking Europe.

Notable bearers

Eduard Hoffmann (1825–1895) was a Swiss entrepreneur and railway pioneer associated with the development of alpine transport infrastructure in the nineteenth century. Industrialists of the Hoffmann name were likewise active in the Thurgau and St. Gallen machinery trades during the period of Swiss industrialisation.

In the arts, Eduard Hoffmann is recorded among nineteenth-century German theatre and choral administrators, part of the generation that professionalised regional opera companies across the German Confederation and later the German Empire.

Name

Hoffmann is one of the most common German surnames, historically denoting a steward or manager of a farm or manor (Hof). Eduard, a German form of Edward, was a popular given name in the nineteenth century, and the combination occurs frequently in German, Austrian, and Swiss records of the period.
