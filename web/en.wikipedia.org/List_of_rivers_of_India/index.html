List of rivers of India

India's rivers fall broadly into the Himalayan rivers — perennial, fed by snowmelt and monsoon rain — and the peninsular rivers, which are largely seasonal. The great Himalayan systems drain into the Bay of Bengal and the Arabian Sea through the Indus, Ganges and Brahmaputra basins; the largest peninsular rivers, such as the Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri, flow eastward across the Deccan to the Bay of Bengal, while the Narmada and Tapti flow west through rift valleys.

Major tributaries

  • Ganges system: Yamuna, Gomti, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Son, Damodar, Chambal (via Yamuna).
  • Indus system: Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej (the five rivers of Punjab).
  • Brahmaputra system: Subansiri, Dibang, Lohit, Teesta, Manas.
  • Godavari system: Manjira, Pranhita, Indravati, Wainganga.
  • Krishna system: Bhima, Tungabhadra, Koyna, Ghataprabha.

West-flowing peninsular rivers

Besides the Narmada and Tapti, shorter west-flowing rivers include the Sabarmati, Mahi, Luni (which ends in the Rann of Kutch), and the steep Western Ghats streams of Kerala and Karnataka such as the Periyar and Sharavati.
