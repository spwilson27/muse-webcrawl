Okayama International Circuit

Okayama International Circuit (岡山国際サーキット), formerly known as TI Circuit Aida, is a 3.703 km (2.301 mi) racing circuit in Mimasaka, Okayama Prefecture, Japan. Opened in 1990 as a private test and racing facility of the Tanaka International company (hence "TI"), it hosted the Formula One Pacific Grand Prix in 1994 and 1995.

History

The circuit was built as a private playground for wealthy businessmen and opened in November 1990. It was added to the Formula One calendar as the Pacific Grand Prix; Michael Schumacher won both races held there, in 1994 and 1995, the latter clinching his second World Championship. The remote location and modest facilities made the event short-lived on the F1 calendar.

Events

The circuit regularly hosts Super GT, Super Formula, and Superbike races, and in 2008 hosted a round of the World Touring Car Championship. It also runs the annual "Okayama" rounds of Japanese national series and historic racing festivals.
