Orbia Advance Corporation, S.A.B. de C.V., known as Orbia (formerly Mexichem), is a Mexican multinational company headquartered in Mexico City. It is one of the world's largest producers of PVC piping and a major player in the fluorochemicals, precision irrigation, and data communications industries.

History

The company traces its origins to 1953, when it was founded as Cables Mexicanos. Through decades of acquisitions it grew into a diversified industrial group under the name Mexichem. In 2017 Mexichem acquired a controlling stake in Netafim, the Israeli drip-irrigation pioneer, in a deal valued at about US$1.5 billion. In September 2019 the company rebranded as Orbia, framing its strategy around addressing global challenges in food and water security, urbanization, and connectivity.

Business groups

Orbia operates through five business groups: Building and Infrastructure (Wavin), Polymer Solutions (Vestolit and Alphagary), Fluor and Energy Materials (Koura), Precision Agriculture (Netafim), and Connectivity Solutions (Dura-Line). The company employs tens of thousands of people and operates in more than 50 countries.
