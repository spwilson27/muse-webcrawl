Keccak


Illustration: Keccak

Keccak is a family of cryptographic hash functions based on the sponge construction. It was designed by Guido Bertoni, Joan Daemen, Michaël Peeters and Gilles Van Assche, building on earlier designs such as RadioGatún.

SHA-3

In 2012 Keccak won the NIST hash function competition to become SHA-3, standardised in FIPS 202 in 2015. Unlike SHA-1 and SHA-2, which share the Merkle–Damgård structure, SHA-3 uses Keccak's sponge construction, giving it a completely different internal design as a hedge against future cryptanalysis.

Design

The Keccak-f permutation operates on a 5×5 array of 64-bit lanes (a 1600-bit state) through rounds of the step mappings θ, ρ, π, χ and ι. The sponge absorbs input into the state and squeezes out digest bits, supporting arbitrary output lengths and extendable-output functions such as SHAKE128 and SHAKE256.
