Dunkerque-class battleship

The Dunkerque class was a pair of fast battleships built for the French Navy in the 1930s: Dunkerque and Strasbourg. They were France's first new battleships in two decades, designed to counter German pocket battleships, and were the first French capital ships built after the Washington Treaty era.

Design

The ships displaced about 26,500 tons standard and carried eight 330 mm guns in two quadruple turrets mounted forward, an unusual all-forward main armament, with a top speed of about 29.5 knots. Strasbourg had somewhat heavier armour than her sister.

Service

Both ships were present at Mers-el-Kébir in July 1940, where Dunkerque was damaged in the British attack; Strasbourg escaped to Toulon. Both were scuttled at Toulon in November 1942 to prevent capture by German forces.
