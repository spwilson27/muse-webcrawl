The Fundamentals of Engineering (FE) exam, also referred to as the Engineer in Training (EIT) exam, is the first of two examinations that engineers in the United States must pass to become licensed as a Professional Engineer (PE). It is administered by the National Council of Examiners for Engineering and Surveying (NCEES) and is generally taken by students near the end of, or shortly after, an accredited engineering degree programme.

Structure

Since 2014 the FE has been a computer-based test offered year-round at Pearson VUE centres. It consists of 110 multiple-choice questions over a six-hour appointment, divided into discipline-specific versions: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical and Computer, Chemical, Industrial and Systems, Environmental, and an "Other Disciplines" option. All examinees have access to the searchable NCEES FE Reference Handbook during the exam.

Licensure pathway

Passing the FE and graduating from an ABET-accredited programme allows registration as an Engineer Intern (EI) or Engineer in Training (EIT) with a state licensing board. After accumulating typically four years of qualifying work experience under a licensed PE, the candidate may sit the Principles and Practice of Engineering (PE) exam to obtain full licensure.
