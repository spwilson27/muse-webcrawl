Water potential

Water potential is the potential energy of water per unit volume relative to pure water at atmospheric pressure and ambient temperature. It is denoted Ψ (psi) and usually measured in megapascals (MPa). Water moves from regions of higher water potential to regions of lower water potential.

Components

Total water potential is the sum of components including solute (osmotic) potential Ψs, pressure potential Ψp, gravitational potential Ψg, and matric potential Ψm. Solute potential is always negative; pressure potential in plant cells arises from turgor pressing against the cell wall.

Importance

The concept explains water uptake by roots, transport through xylem, turgor maintenance, and transpiration in plants, and is widely used in plant physiology, soil science and agriculture.
