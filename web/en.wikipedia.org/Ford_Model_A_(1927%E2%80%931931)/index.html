Ford Model A (1927–1931)

The Ford Model A (also colloquially called the A-Model Ford) is a car produced by the Ford Motor Company from October 1927 to March 1932, succeeding the long-running Model T.

ManufacturerFord Motor Company
Production1927–1932 (model years 1928–1931)
PredecessorFord Model T
Engine3.3 L (201 cu in) L-head inline-four

Development

After nearly two decades, Ford shut down Model T production in 1927 to retool for the Model A, named to restart the company's model alphabet. The new car offered a conventional three-speed sliding-gear transmission, four-wheel mechanical brakes, safety glass, and a 40-horsepower four-cylinder engine, capable of about 65 mph.

Production and legacy

More than 4.8 million Model As were built in a wide range of body styles, from Tudor sedans and roadsters to trucks. It was also produced under license abroad, including in the Soviet Union by GAZ. The Model A remains one of the most popular collector cars, supported by a large restoration community.
