Fine dining

A plated course at a fine dining restaurant.

Fine dining is a restaurant experience that is generally more sophisticated, unique, and expensive than at other restaurants. The décor of such restaurants features higher-quality materials, and establishments usually have certain rules of dining that visitors must follow, often including a dress code.

Characteristics

Fine dining restaurants are typically full-service restaurants offering specific dedicated meal courses. Meals are commonly served over multiple courses — such as appetisers, entrées, main courses, and desserts — and may be offered as a set tasting menu chosen by the chef or as an à la carte selection. Ingredients are generally of the highest quality, sourced carefully, and dishes are prepared with considerable technical skill and presented artfully on the plate.

Service is formal and attentive: tables are set with fine tableware and stemware, staff are trained in etiquette and in detailed knowledge of the menu and wine list, and a sommelier may advise on wine pairings. Fine dining establishments commonly accept reservations well in advance and may require a deposit or prepayment for tasting menus.

History

The conventions of fine dining descend from French haute cuisine, codified in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries by chefs such as Auguste Escoffier, whose brigade system and organisation of the professional kitchen remain influential. In the 1960s and 1970s, nouvelle cuisine emphasised lighter, more delicate dishes and artistic presentation. Restaurant guides, most famously the Michelin Guide with its star ratings, and later lists such as The World's 50 Best Restaurants, have played a major role in defining the top tier of fine dining.

In the twenty-first century, fine dining has diversified: chef's counters, omakase formats, and restaurants built around local and seasonal produce — such as the New Nordic cuisine movement — have broadened the model beyond French classical cooking, while prices and demand for the most celebrated restaurants have continued to rise.
