Golden Ray


The capsized Golden Ray in St. Simons Sound.
Type: Vehicle carrier (PCTC)
Launched: 2017
Owner/Operator: Hyundai Glovis
Fate: Capsized 8 September 2019; wreck removed 2021

MV Golden Ray was a 200-metre vehicle carrier built in 2017 by Hyundai Mipo Dockyard and operated by Hyundai Glovis under the flag of the Marshall Islands. She became famous after capsizing in St. Simons Sound, Georgia, in 2019.

Capsizing

In the early hours of 8 September 2019, shortly after departing the Port of Brunswick with about 4,200 vehicles aboard and 24 crew, the ship heeled heavily to port and capsized onto her side. All crew were rescued, the last four — trapped in the engine room for over 30 hours — being freed by drilling through the hull. Investigation attributed the casualty to incorrect calculation of stability and ballast water levels following discharge of cargo at the previous port.

Wreck removal

The wreck, too large to refloat, was cut into eight sections in place using the heavy-lift catamaran VB-10000 and a diamond-cutting chain, in an operation that ran from late 2020 to October 2021. Fires and oil spills complicated the work; the operation was one of the largest and most expensive wreck removals in US history, costing over $800 million in total insured losses. Most of the vehicles aboard were total losses.
