In general usage, "Chief series" may refer to several distinct topics, most notably in vexillology and design: a chief is the horizontal band at the top of a heraldic shield, and series of flags, coins, and locomotive classes have borne the name "Chief".

Heraldic chief

In heraldry, a chief is a charge on a coat of arms that takes the form of a band running horizontally across the top edge of the shield. Writers of heraldry treat the chief as one of the honourable ordinaries. A "chief" can be plain or charged with geometric shapes or animals, and its lower edge may be straight, indented, wavy, or otherwise varied.

Usage

The term heads several named series in transport and numismatics, including the Santa Fe Chief passenger trains (the Chief, Super Chief, and San Francisco Chief) and various "Chief" portrait coin and note series.
