Order of the Red Banner of Labour

The Order of the Red Banner of Labour was a Soviet civil order of merit established on 28 December 1920 to honour great deeds and services in production, science, culture, literature, art, education, health, and other spheres of labour activity.

History

Modelled on the military Order of the Red Banner, it was initially an award of the Russian SFSR before becoming an all-Union order in 1928. An early recipient was Nikita Khrushchev, decorated in 1939 for work on the Moscow Metro. Over its history more than 1.2 million awards were made.

Design

The badge shows a red banner inscribed "Proletarians of all countries, unite!", the emblem of the USSR, and a cogwheel surrounding scenes of industrial and agricultural labour, with the inscription "For Valour in Labour"-related wording varying between versions. It ceased to be awarded with the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991.
