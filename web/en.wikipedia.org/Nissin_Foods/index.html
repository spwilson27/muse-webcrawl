Nissin Foods

Nissin Foods Co., Ltd. (Japanese: 日清食品株式会社) is a Japanese food company that specialises in the production and sale of convenience food and instant noodles. It was founded in 1958 in Osaka by Momofuku Ando, the inventor of instant ramen, who launched the world's first instant noodle product, Chicken Ramen, that year. Nissin's Cup Noodles (1971), sold in a foam cup with dried vegetables, became a global success and a cultural icon.

History

Ando developed his flash-frying method for preserving noodles after observing post-war food shortages in Japan. Nissin expanded overseas early, establishing Nissin Foods USA in 1970 and introducing Cup Noodles to the American market in 1973. The company now operates factories and sells products in dozens of countries, with major brands including Top Ramen, Demae Ramen (Demae Itcho), UFO yakisoba, and Donbei noodles.

Legacy

The Momofuku Ando Instant Ramen Museum in Ikeda, Osaka, commemorates Ando's invention; in a 2000 Japanese poll, instant ramen was voted Japan's most important 20th-century invention. Nissin's Space Ramen was developed for astronaut Soichi Noguchi's 2005 space shuttle mission.
