Discord is a social platform providing voice over IP (VoIP), instant messaging, and video calls, developed by Discord Inc. Users communicate in private chats or as part of communities called "servers" — collections of persistent text channels and voice channels that are joined by invitation. Originally created for video game players, it has grown into a general platform for communities of all kinds, with hundreds of millions of registered users.

History

Discord was conceived by Jason Citron, founder of the social gaming platform OpenFeint, and programmer Stanislav Vishnevskiy, after their studio's mobile game struggled while their voice-chat tool proved popular. The service launched publicly in May 2015 and spread through esports and Twitch communities as a free, low-latency alternative to TeamSpeak and Skype. During the COVID-19 pandemic Discord expanded beyond gaming, rebranding in 2020 with the slogan "Your Place to Talk." Microsoft reportedly held acquisition talks valuing the company at around $10 billion in 2021, which did not proceed. In 2023 Discord replaced its four-digit discriminator username system with unique lowercase usernames.

Features

Core features include servers with role-based permissions, direct messages, group chats, voice and video calls, screen sharing and live streaming ("Go Live"), bots and integrations, and threads and forums. A subscription service, Discord Nitro, offers larger uploads, higher-quality streaming, and profile customization. Discord is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and in web browsers, and later came to PlayStation and Xbox consoles.
