Egypt Cup

The Egypt Cup is the main knockout cup competition in Egyptian football, organised by the Egyptian Football Association. First held in 1921, it is the oldest football competition in Egypt and one of the oldest in Africa and the Arab world. Al Ahly is the most successful club in the competition's history, ahead of their Cairo rivals Zamalek.

History

The cup began in the 1921–22 season, in the early years of organised Egyptian football, and has been contested in most seasons since, with occasional interruptions for war and political upheaval. Finals have traditionally been played in Cairo, for many years at the Cairo International Stadium.

Format

The competition is a single-elimination tournament open to clubs from the Egyptian league system; ties are generally decided in a single match, with extra time and penalties if required.

Winners

Al Ahly and Zamalek have dominated the cup, together winning the great majority of editions. Other winners include Al Ittihad Alexandria, Tersana, Ismaily, ENPPI, Haras El Hodoud and ZED FC, who won a surprise title in the 2020s.
