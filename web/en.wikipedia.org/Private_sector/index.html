Private sector

The private sector is the part of the economy which is owned by private individuals or businesses, usually for the purpose of making a profit. It forms one part of a three-sector economy alongside the public sector (government) and the voluntary or third sector (nonprofit organizations).

Composition

The private sector encompasses sole traders, partnerships, privately held companies, and publicly traded corporations owned by shareholders, as well as private households as employers. In most free-market economies, the private sector employs the majority of the workforce and generates the bulk of economic output.

Relationship to the state

Businesses operate within legal and regulatory frameworks set by the state. Debates about the proper boundary between sectors include privatization (transferring state assets or services to private ownership), nationalization (the reverse), and public–private partnerships for infrastructure and services.
