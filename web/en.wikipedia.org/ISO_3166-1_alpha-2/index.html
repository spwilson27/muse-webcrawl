ISO 3166-1 alpha-2

ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes are two-letter country codes defined in ISO 3166-1, part of the ISO 3166 standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO). They represent countries, dependent territories, and special areas of geographical interest.

Uses

The alpha-2 codes are the basis for Internet country code top-level domains (such as .de for Germany and .jp for Japan), with the notable exception of the United Kingdom, whose code is GB while its ccTLD is .uk. They are also used in vehicle registration codes, currency and language code systems, and as the first element of ISO 3166-2 subdivision codes.

Assignment

Codes are assigned by the ISO 3166 Maintenance Agency following changes in the United Nations country lists. Some codes are exceptionally reserved, including UK, EU, and UN.
