Barbie

Barbie is a fashion doll line manufactured by the American toy company Mattel and launched on 9 March 1959. Created by Ruth Handler, who named the doll after her daughter Barbara, Barbie was inspired by the German Bild Lilli doll. The brand has grown into a multibillion-dollar entertainment franchise.

History

Handler noticed her daughter projecting adult roles onto paper dolls and proposed an adult-bodied doll. Debuted at the American International Toy Fair in New York, Barbie was marketed as a teenage fashion model; her boyfriend Ken followed in 1961.

Careers and diversity

Barbie has held hundreds of careers, from astronaut (1965) to US presidential candidate. Mattel introduced dolls of varied body types, skin tones and abilities from 2016 with the Fashionistas line, responding to long-running criticism about unrealistic proportions.

In film

After many animated features, the live-action film Barbie (2023), directed by Greta Gerwig and starring Margot Robbie, grossed over $1.4 billion worldwide, the highest-grossing film of its year.
