Medley relay

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The medley relay is a swimming event in which teams of four swimmers each swim a different stroke. In long-course Olympic competition the event is swum over 4×100 metres. The order of strokes in the medley relay differs from the individual medley: backstroke is swum first (because it begins in the water), followed by breaststroke, butterfly and freestyle.

Order of strokes

  1. Backstroke
  2. Breaststroke
  3. Butterfly
  4. Freestyle (any stroke other than backstroke, breaststroke or butterfly)

Rules

Each swimmer must finish their leg in accordance with the rules of their stroke, and takeovers are judged by touchpads and officials. A team is disqualified for an early takeover, an incorrect stroke or an illegal turn.

History

The men's medley relay has been an Olympic event since 1960 and the women's since 1960 as well, with a mixed 4×100 m medley relay added at the Tokyo 2020 Games.
