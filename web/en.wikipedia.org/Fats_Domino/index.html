Fats Domino

Antoine Dominique Domino Jr. (February 26, 1928 – October 24, 2017), known as Fats Domino, was an American pianist, singer, and songwriter from New Orleans. A pioneer of rock and roll, his boogie-woogie piano style and relaxed vocals produced hits including "Ain't That a Shame", "Blueberry Hill", "Blue Monday", and "I'm Walkin'".

Domino sold more than 110 million records and was among the first musicians inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1986. He remained in New Orleans throughout his career, surviving Hurricane Katrina in 2005.
