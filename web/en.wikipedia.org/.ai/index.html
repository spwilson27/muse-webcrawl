.ai

.ai is the country code top-level domain (ccTLD) for Anguilla, a British Overseas Territory in the Caribbean. Delegated in 1995, the domain is administered under the authority of the Government of Anguilla. Since the 2010s it has become extremely popular with artificial intelligence companies, because "AI" is the common abbreviation for artificial intelligence, turning the small territory's domain into an unexpectedly valuable source of revenue.

History

The .ai domain was delegated in 1995, with Vince Cate, an American computer scientist resident in Anguilla, involved in its early administration. For many years registrations were modest, reflecting the island's small population of roughly 15,000.

Registration figures began climbing as machine learning startups adopted .ai addresses for branding. The surge accelerated sharply after the launch of ChatGPT in late 2022: .ai registrations more than doubled within a year, and revenue from domain fees came to represent a substantial share of Anguilla's government income, with reported figures reaching tens of millions of dollars annually.

Registrations

Anyone may register a .ai domain; there are no residency requirements. Registrations are typically for terms of two years. Second-level domains such as .com.ai, .net.ai, .org.ai and .off.ai have also been made available. Registration volume grew from around 140,000 domains in 2022 to well over 350,000 by 2024, with projections of continued growth tied to the expansion of the AI industry.

Economic significance

Domain registration revenue has become a pillar of Anguilla's public finances, funding infrastructure and helping stabilize the economy of the tourism-dependent island. The windfall also drew comparisons with Tuvalu's earnings from leasing rights connected with the .tv domain.
