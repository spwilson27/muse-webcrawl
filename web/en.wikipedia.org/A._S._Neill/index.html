A. S. Neill

Alexander Sutherland Neill (17 October 1883 – 23 September 1973) was a Scottish educator and author who founded Summerhill School, the oldest existing "democratic school", which he ran for over fifty years and wrote about in a series of some twenty books. In 1921 Neill founded Summerhill, in Suffolk, England, as a school where children could be free from adult authority.

His work centered on the belief that children learn best when free from coercion, and that a child's happiness should be the paramount consideration in their upbringing. Summerhill students decide for themselves whether to attend lessons, and the school is governed by a weekly meeting in which pupils and staff have equal votes. Neill's 1960 book Summerhill became an international bestseller and influenced alternative education worldwide.
