France national football team

Les Bleus — World Cup winners in 1998 and 2018

The France national football team (French: Équipe de France de football) represents France in men's international football and is controlled by the French Football Federation (FFF). Nicknamed Les Bleus (The Blues), the team plays its home matches at the Stade de France in Saint-Denis, near Paris.

History

France was one of four European teams to compete at the inaugural FIFA World Cup in 1930, and striker Lucien Laurent scored the tournament's first goal. The team enjoyed a golden period in the 1980s under Michel Hidalgo, winning Euro 1984 with Michel Platini's famous midfield carré magique, and again from the late 1990s: captained by Didier Deschamps and inspired by Zinedine Zidane, France won the 1998 World Cup on home soil and Euro 2000. Under Deschamps as manager, France won the 2018 World Cup in Russia and reached the 2022 final, losing to Argentina on penalties.

Honours

  • FIFA World Cup: winners 1998, 2018; runners-up 2006, 2022.
  • UEFA European Championship: winners 1984, 2000.
  • UEFA Nations League: winners 2021.
  • FIFA Confederations Cup: winners 2001, 2003.
  • Olympic gold medal: 1984.

Notable players include Just Fontaine, Michel Platini, Zinedine Zidane, Thierry Henry (long the record scorer), Hugo Lloris (record appearance holder), and Kylian Mbappé.
