List of NHL records (individual)

This is a list of individual records recognised by the National Hockey League (NHL), covering regular season and playoff achievements by players and goaltenders.

Scoring records

Wayne Gretzky holds most of the league's principal scoring records, including most career goals (894 regular season), most assists (1,963) and most points (2,857), as well as most goals in a season (92, in 1981–82) and most points in a season (215, in 1985–86). In 2025 Alex Ovechkin passed Gretzky's regular-season goals mark.

Other records

Notable records include most seasons and games played (Gordie Howe and Chris Chelios), most career wins by a goaltender (Martin Brodeur, 691), and most Stanley Cups by a player (Henri Richard, 11).
