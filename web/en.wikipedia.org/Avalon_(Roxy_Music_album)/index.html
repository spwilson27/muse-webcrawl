Avalon (Roxy Music album)

The sleeve of Avalon (1982), Roxy Music's eighth and final studio album.

Avalon is the eighth and final studio album by the English rock band Roxy Music, released on 28 May 1982 by E.G. Records and Polydor. Recorded at Compass Point Studios in Nassau and the Power Station in New York, it marked the culmination of the band's movement from the experimental art rock of their early 1970s work toward a smooth, atmospheric sophisti-pop sound.

Background and recording

Roxy Music had re-formed in 1978 after a three-year hiatus, releasing Manifesto (1979) and Flesh + Blood (1980). For Avalon, the core trio of Bryan Ferry, Andy Mackay and Phil Manzanera worked with producer Rhett Davies and a large cast of session musicians. Ferry later recalled hearing the haunting backing vocal of Yanick Étienne, a Haitian singer working in the studio, on the title track; her improvised performance over the coda became one of the album's signature moments.

Music and reception

The album's singles were "More Than This" — a UK top ten hit — "Avalon" and "Take a Chance with Me". Avalon reached number one in the UK and became the band's best-selling album, earning platinum certification in the United States. Critics praised its polished, nocturnal mood: a seamless sequence of romantic, melancholic songs carried by Ferry's croon, Mackay's saxophone and oboe, and spacious arrangements. Retrospectively it is frequently ranked among the finest albums of the 1980s and a defining record of the sophisti-pop and new romantic era.

Aftermath

Roxy Music supported the album with an extensive tour, after which the band dissolved in 1983; Ferry pursued the sound further on his solo album Boys and Girls (1985). The group reunited for tours in 2001 and 2011 and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2019, but released no further studio album, leaving Avalon as their farewell statement.

Track listing

Side one: "More Than This", "The Space Between", "Avalon", "India", "While My Heart Is Still Beating". Side two: "The Main Thing", "Take a Chance with Me", "To Turn You On", "True to Life", "Tara".
