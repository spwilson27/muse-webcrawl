Timeline of human evolution

This timeline of human evolution outlines the major events in the evolutionary lineage of the modern human species, Homo sapiens, from the divergence of hominins from other apes to the present. Human evolution is characterized by a number of morphological, developmental, physiological, and behavioral changes, including bipedalism, increased brain size, and the development of language and complex culture.

Key developments

Bipedalism preceded large brains by millions of years; the australopithecines walked upright while retaining ape-sized brains and climbing adaptations. Stone tools appear by about 3.3 million years ago (Lomekwi), predating genus Homo. Control of fire, perhaps by ~1 million years ago, enabled cooking and expanded diet. Anatomically modern humans interbred with Neanderthals and Denisovans, leaving 1–4% Neanderthal ancestry in most non-African genomes today.
