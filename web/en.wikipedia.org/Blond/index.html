Blond

Blond
Trait: Hair colour
Cause: Low eumelanin
Most common: Northern and Eastern Europe

Blond (or blonde for women) hair is a human hair colour characterized by low levels of the dark pigment eumelanin. Shades range from pale platinum to dark golden brown. Natural blond hair is most common among people of Northern and Eastern European descent.

Distribution and genetics

Blondness in adults is associated with variants in genes such as KITLG. Children born blond often darken with age as melanin production increases. Blond hair also occurs among some Indigenous Australians and peoples of the Solomon Islands through a distinct genetic variant.

Culture

Blond hair has been prized or stereotyped in many cultures, from ancient Greece and Rome, where it was artificially imitated, to modern media tropes.
