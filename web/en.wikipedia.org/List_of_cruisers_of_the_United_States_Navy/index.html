List of cruisers of the United States Navy

This is a list of cruisers of the United States Navy. The Navy operated protected cruisers, armoured cruisers, heavy cruisers, light cruisers, and guided-missile cruisers. The Ticonderoga-class guided-missile cruisers are the only cruisers remaining in active service.

Hull classifications

Cruiser hull symbols include C (cruiser), ACR (armoured cruiser), CA (heavy cruiser), CL (light cruiser), CG (guided-missile cruiser), and CGN (nuclear-powered guided-missile cruiser).
