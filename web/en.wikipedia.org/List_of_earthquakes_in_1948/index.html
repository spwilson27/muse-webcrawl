List of earthquakes in 1948

This is a list of earthquakes in 1948. Only earthquakes of magnitude 6.0 or greater, or earthquakes that caused significant damage or casualties, are included. The deadliest earthquake of the year was the Ashgabat earthquake in Turkmenistan.

The Ashgabat earthquake of 1948 remains among the deadliest seismic events of the 20th century, striking the capital of Turkmenistan at night and destroying most of the city's buildings.
