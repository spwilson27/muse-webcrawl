Abortion in Florida

Abortion in Florida is currently illegal after 6 weeks of gestation, with exceptions for rape, incest, human trafficking, fatal fetal abnormalities, and to save the life of the pregnant woman. The six-week limit took effect on 1 May 2024, following a ruling by the Supreme Court of Florida that the state constitution's privacy clause does not protect abortion rights.

Florida previously allowed abortion up to 24 weeks under Roe v. Wade, and state law reduced the limit to 15 weeks in 2022 before the six-week law was allowed to take effect. In November 2024, voters considered Amendment 4, which would have enshrined abortion rights until viability in the state constitution; it won about 57% of the vote but failed to reach the 60% threshold required for adoption.
