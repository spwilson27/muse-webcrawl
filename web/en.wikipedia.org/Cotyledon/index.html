Cotyledon

A cotyledon is a significant part of the embryo within the seed of a plant, and is defined as the embryonic leaf in seed-bearing plants, one or more of which are the first to appear from a germinating seed.

Classification

Flowering plants are divided by number of cotyledons: monocotyledons (monocots) have one, dicotyledons (dicots) have two. Gymnosperms vary. Cotyledons may remain below ground (hypogeal) storing food, or emerge above ground (epigeal) and become photosynthetic.

Function

They store nutrients or begin photosynthesis to support the seedling until true leaves develop.
