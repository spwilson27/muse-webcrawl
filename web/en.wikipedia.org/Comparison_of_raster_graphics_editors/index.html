Comparison of raster graphics editors

Raster graphics editors create and edit images as grids of pixels (bitmaps). They are used for photo retouching, digital painting, and web graphics. Editors are typically compared by platform, price and licence, colour model support (RGB, CMYK, Lab, HDR), bit depth, layers and masking, scripting, and file-format support.

Features compared

Comparisons commonly note that Photoshop and Affinity Photo offer full CMYK and soft-proofing for print, that GIMP supports high bit depths since version 2.10, and that painting-oriented programs such as Krita and Procreate emphasise brush engines and stabilisation over photo manipulation.
