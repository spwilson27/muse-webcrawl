HAROPA Port is a French public port authority created on 1 June 2021 by merging the ports of Le Havre, Rouen and Paris into a single establishment. Its name and strategy express the ambition of a single integrated port system along the Seine axis, from the English Channel to the Paris region, competing with the great ports of the Northern Range such as Rotterdam and Antwerp.

Components

Le Havre, at the mouth of the Seine, is France's leading container port and a major oil-import harbour with deep-water terminals (Port 2000). Rouen, far upriver, is a leading European grain-export port and handles bulk and general cargo. The Port of Paris is France's largest inland and river port system, handling construction materials, containers and urban logistics across the Île-de-France.

Activities and strategy

HAROPA Port handles on the order of 100 million tonnes of maritime and river traffic a year and around 3 million TEU of containers. Its development strategy emphasises decarbonisation of logistics, rail and river connections to the Paris basin, industrial land along the Seine, and energy-transition cargoes and industries.

Governance

HAROPA Port is a state-owned public establishment headquartered in Le Havre, with territorial directorates for each of the three historic ports.
