Supernatural horror film

A supernatural horror film is a subgenre of horror film in which the threat arises from phenomena beyond the laws of nature — ghosts, demons, curses, possession, hauntings, or occult rituals — rather than from human or natural antagonists.

Themes and history

Drawing on Gothic literature, the subgenre appeared early in cinema with films such as Nosferatu (1922) and developed through haunted-house pictures, the occult cycle of the late 1960s and 1970s (Rosemary's Baby, 1968; The Exorcist, 1973; The Omen, 1976), and later waves including Japanese horror and its remakes and the Conjuring franchise of the 2010s.

Characteristics

Supernatural horror typically relies on atmosphere, unseen presences, and violations of everyday reality to generate dread, often centering on a haunted location or an object or rite that unleashes the entity.
