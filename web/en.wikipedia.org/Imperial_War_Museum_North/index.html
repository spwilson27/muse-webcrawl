Imperial War Museum North (sometimes referred to as IWM North) is a museum in the Metropolitan Borough of Trafford in Greater Manchester, England. One of five branches of the Imperial War Museum, it explores the impact of modern conflicts on people and society.

Building

The museum was designed by architect Daniel Libeskind and opened in July 2002. Its striking aluminium-clad form represents a globe shattered by conflict, composed of three interlocking "shards" representing air, earth, and water.

Exhibitions

The main exhibition space presents a chronological timeline of British and Commonwealth involvement in conflicts from the First World War to the present, supplemented by hourly immersive "Big Picture Show" projections and temporary exhibitions.
