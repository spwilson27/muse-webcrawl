Colin Newman

Colin John Newman (born 16 September 1954) is an English musician, record producer and record label owner. He is a member of the post-punk band Wire, serving as singer and guitarist.

Career

Wire formed in London in 1976 and released the influential albums Pink Flag (1977), Chairs Missing (1978) and 154 (1979). Newman also released solo albums including A-Z (1980), Provisionally Entitled the Singing Fish (1981) and Not To (1982). With his wife Malka Spigel he founded the swim ~ label and recorded as part of Githead and Immersion.
