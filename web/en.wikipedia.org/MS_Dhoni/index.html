MS Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni
Born 7 July 1981, Ranchi, Bihar (now Jharkhand), India
Role: wicket-keeper batsman
National side: India (2004–2019)
Teams: Chennai Super Kings, Jharkhand, Rising Pune Supergiant

Mahendra Singh Dhoni (born 7 July 1981) is an Indian professional cricketer who captained the Indian national team in limited-overs formats from 2007 to 2017 and in Test cricket from 2008 to 2014. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest captains and wicket-keeper batters in the history of the game.

International career

Dhoni made his one-day international debut against Bangladesh in December 2004. Appointed captain in 2007, he led India to victory in the inaugural ICC World Twenty20 in South Africa, then to the 2011 Cricket World Cup on home soil — sealing the final against Sri Lanka in Mumbai with a six that became one of the defining images of Indian sport — and to the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy in England, making him the only captain to win all three major ICC limited-overs trophies. Under him India also reached the number one position in the ICC Test rankings in 2009.

Renowned as a finisher, Dhoni scored 10,773 ODI runs at an average above 50 and effected a record number of stumpings. He retired from Test cricket in 2014 and from international cricket in August 2020.

IPL career

In the Indian Premier League, Dhoni has captained the Chennai Super Kings to five titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, and 2023), becoming the league's most iconic figure. Off the field he is an honorary lieutenant colonel in the Indian Territorial Army and one of India's most decorated sportsmen, receiving the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, the Padma Shri, and the Padma Bhushan.
