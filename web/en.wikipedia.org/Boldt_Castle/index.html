Boldt Castle

Boldt Castle is a major landmark and tourist attraction on Heart Island in the Thousand Islands region of New York State, near Alexandria Bay. The 120-room Rhineland-style castle was commissioned by hotel magnate George C. Boldt, proprietor of the Waldorf-Astoria Hotel, as a summer home and tribute to his wife Louise.

History

Construction began in 1900 with some 300 workers. In January 1904 Louise Boldt died suddenly, and the heartbroken Boldt telegraphed the island to stop all work; he never returned, and the unfinished castle stood abandoned and vandalised for over 70 years. The Thousand Islands Bridge Authority acquired Heart Island in 1977 and, using admission revenues, has restored the castle and its outbuildings — including the Power House, Alster Tower and a drawbridge.

Today

Boldt Castle is open to visitors seasonally, accessible by boat from Alexandria Bay and nearby marinas, and is a popular wedding venue. Its restoration is ongoing, funded entirely by visitor fees.
