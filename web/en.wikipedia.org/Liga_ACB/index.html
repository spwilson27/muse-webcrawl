The Liga ACB, known as the Liga Endesa for sponsorship reasons, is the top professional basketball division of the Spanish basketball league system. Administered by the Asociación de Clubs de Baloncesto (ACB), it is widely regarded as one of the strongest domestic leagues in Europe.

History

The league was founded in 1983, taking over from the earlier Liga Nacional run by the Spanish Basketball Federation since 1957. The ACB introduced a modern club-run structure, playoffs, and extensive television coverage, helping Spanish basketball flourish. Real Madrid and FC Barcelona have historically dominated the competition, though clubs such as Baskonia, Unicaja Málaga, Valencia Basket and Joventut Badalona have also won titles.

Format

Eighteen teams play a double round-robin regular season of 34 games. The top eight qualify for the playoffs, which culminate in a best-of-five final series. The bottom two teams are relegated to the LEB Oro. Spanish clubs also compete in European competitions such as the EuroLeague and EuroCup, and the league's champions contest the Copa del Rey and Supercopa each season.

Players

The ACB has been an important development league for international talent, including Spanish stars Pau Gasol and Ricky Rubio and many future NBA players who began their European careers in Spain.
