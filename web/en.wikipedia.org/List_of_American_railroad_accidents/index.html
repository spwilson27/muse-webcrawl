List of American railroad accidents

The Great Train Wreck of 1856 in Pennsylvania was one of the deadliest early American rail disasters.

This is a list of American railroad accidents, organized chronologically. Railroad accidents in the United States have occurred since the earliest days of rail transport in the 1830s, and their investigation led to the creation of bodies such as the Interstate Commerce Commission and, later, the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB).

Investigation

Since 1967, major rail accidents in the United States have been investigated by the National Transportation Safety Board, an independent federal agency. The Federal Railroad Administration regulates rail safety and collects accident statistics.
