The São Paulo Grand Prix is a Formula One motor race held at the Autódromo José Carlos Pace, better known as Interlagos, in São Paulo, Brazil. The event was renamed from the Brazilian Grand Prix beginning with the 2021 season, reflecting the city's increased role in promoting the race.

History

A Brazilian Grand Prix had been part of the Formula One calendar since 1973, mostly at Interlagos, with a period at Jacarepaguá in Rio de Janeiro. After uncertainty over the race's future and the cancellation of the 2020 event due to the COVID-19 pandemic, a new contract was signed keeping the race at Interlagos under the São Paulo Grand Prix name.

The circuit

Interlagos is a short, undulating, anti-clockwise circuit of about 4.3 km, famous for its unpredictable weather, dramatic championship deciders and passionate crowds. Notable corners include the Senna S and the long uphill run to the finish line. The race has hosted several title showdowns, including those of 2007, 2008 and 2012 under the Brazilian Grand Prix name.

Winners

Early editions under the new name were won by Lewis Hamilton (2021), George Russell (2022) and Max Verstappen (2023 and 2024).
