Tsui Hark (born Tsui Man-kong, 1950) is a Hong Kong filmmaker. A key figure of the Hong Kong New Wave, he is renowned for reinvigorating the wuxia and action genres with kinetic camerawork, special effects and fast editing, both as a director and through his production company Film Workshop.

Career

After early films such as The Butterfly Murders (1979) and Zu Warriors from the Magic Mountain (1983), Tsui produced John Woo's A Better Tomorrow and The Killer and directed the Once Upon a Time in China series with Jet Li, Peking Opera Blues and the blockbuster fantasy Detective Dee films.

Later work

He also directed in Hollywood (Double Team, Knock Off) and co-directed major mainland Chinese productions such as The Battle at Lake Changjin (2021), among the highest-grossing films worldwide.
