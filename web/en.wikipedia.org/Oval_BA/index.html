The Oval BA, officially the Oval Lingotto, is a multi-purpose indoor arena in Turin, Italy. It was built to host the speed skating events of the 2006 Winter Olympics and stands in the Lingotto district, near the historic Fiat factory of the same name.

Design and construction

Designed by architects including Arata Isozaki's collaborator practice (Studio Zoppini), the building is a large, flexible pavilion about 210 metres long. Its structure allows the interior to be reconfigured for exhibitions, trade fairs, concerts, and sporting events. During the Games it enclosed a 400-metre speed skating oval with seating for roughly 8,500 spectators. The 2006 Olympic speed skating events held there produced several world and Olympic records, helped by the fast ice and the arena's controlled indoor climate.

Post-Olympic use

After the Games the oval was converted primarily to exhibition and trade-fair use within the Lingotto Fiere complex, retaining its name and distinctive long, flat profile. Like many purpose-built Olympic venues, its legacy program centres on events rather than permanent ice sport, though the building's design was praised for its adaptability.
