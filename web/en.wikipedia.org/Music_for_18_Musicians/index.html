Music for 18 Musicians

Music for 18 Musicians is a work of minimalist music composed by Steve Reich between 1974 and 1976. It is scored for a large ensemble including pianos, marimbas, xylophones, metallophone, voices, strings, clarinets and bass clarinets, and lasts about an hour.

Structure

The piece is built on a cycle of eleven chords. Sections explore each chord, with melodic patterns gradually lengthening and shortening as if "breathing"; human breath determines the phrasing of the wind and vocal parts, while the pulsing percussion provides a rhythmic grid. A full cycle of the chords frames the work.

Reception

Premiered in New York in 1976 and recorded for ECM in 1978, Music for 18 Musicians was Reich's breakthrough to a wide audience and remains one of the landmark works of minimalism, admired for its shimmering, harmonically rich sound world.
