The Strokes are an American rock band formed in New York City in 1998. They were a leading group of the early-2000s garage rock and post-punk revivals, and their debut album Is This It (2001) is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of all time.

History

The members met at school in New York and Switzerland. Their EP The Modern Age (2001), released on Rough Trade, sparked a major-label bidding war. Is This It won universal acclaim for its lean, melodic songs; follow-ups Room on Fire (2003) and First Impressions of Earth (2005) followed. After a hiatus, the band returned with Angles (2011) and Comedown Machine (2013), and won the Grammy Award for Best Rock Album for The New Abnormal (2020). Frontman Julian Casablancas also leads The Voidz.
