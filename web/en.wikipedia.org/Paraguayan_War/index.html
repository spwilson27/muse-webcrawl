Paraguayan War

The Paraguayan War, also known as the War of the Triple Alliance, was a South American war fought from 1864 to 1870 between Paraguay and the Triple Alliance of Argentina, the Empire of Brazil, and Uruguay. It is considered the deadliest conflict in South American history.

Causes and course

The war began after Paraguayan president Francisco Solano López intervened in the Uruguayan Civil War and then declared war on Brazil (1864) and Argentina (1865). After initial Paraguayan advances, the allies pushed into Paraguay itself. Major engagements included the naval Battle of Riachuelo (1865), the Battle of Tuyutí (1866) — the largest battle in South American history — and the siege of Humaitá. Asunción was occupied in 1869, and López was killed at the Battle of Cerro Corá on 1 March 1870, ending the war.

Consequences

Paraguay suffered catastrophic losses; estimates suggest it lost a large fraction of its pre-war population, possibly more than half, along with territory to Brazil and Argentina. The war remains central to Paraguayan national memory and continues to be debated by historians.
