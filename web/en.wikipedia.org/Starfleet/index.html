Starfleet

Starfleet is a fictional organisation in the Star Trek franchise. Within the franchise universe, Starfleet is a uniformed space force maintained by the United Federation of Planets ("the Federation") as its principal means of interstellar exploration, defence, and diplomacy.

Role

Starfleet's mandate, expressed in the starship Enterprise's mission statement, is exploration: "to explore strange new worlds, to seek out new life and new civilizations". Its vessels also conduct scientific research, humanitarian aid, and, when necessary, military defence of the Federation.

Organisation

Headquartered at Starfleet Headquarters in San Francisco, with training at Starfleet Academy, Starfleet uses a naval rank structure and operates starbases and starships such as the Enterprise, Voyager and Discovery. Its guiding directive is the Prime Directive of non-interference with less-developed cultures.
