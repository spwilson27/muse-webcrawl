Sea Life Melbourne Aquarium

Sea Life Melbourne Aquarium is a public aquarium located on the banks of the Yarra River in central Melbourne, Victoria, Australia, opposite Flinders Street station. It is operated by Merlin Entertainments as part of the Sea Life chain.

History

The aquarium opened in January 2000. A major redevelopment completed in 2013 added new themed zones. The building spans several levels, descending from Antarctic exhibits to ocean tanks.

Exhibits

Displays are organised around Australian aquatic habitats, including Antarctic penguins (king and gentoo penguins), a large oceanarium with sharks and rays viewed through an underwater tunnel, mangrove and billabong habitats, seahorses, jellyfish, and a crocodile exhibit featuring a large saltwater crocodile. The aquarium has participated in rescue, rehabilitation and breeding programmes, and in shark research.

Incidents

In 2008 legionella bacteria were detected at the site and the aquarium was temporarily closed for cleaning after cases of illness were linked to the building's water systems.
