Alla Rakha

Born29 April 1919, Phagwal, Jammu, British India
Died3 February 2000, Mumbai, India
InstrumentTabla
Known forAccompanying Ravi Shankar; Punjab gharana tabla

Ustad Alla Rakha Qureshi (29 April 1919 – 3 February 2000), popularly known as Alla Rakha, was an Indian tabla player who specialised in Hindustani classical music. He was a frequent accompanist of sitar player Pandit Ravi Shankar and helped popularise the tabla in the West.

Career

Born in Jammu, Alla Rakha ran away from home at 12 to learn tabla and became a disciple of Mian Kader Baksh of the Punjab gharana. He worked at All India Radio and composed music for Hindi films under the name A. R. Qureshi. From the 1960s his partnership with Ravi Shankar brought him international fame, with performances at the Monterey Pop Festival (1967), Woodstock (1969), and the Concert for Bangladesh (1971).

Legacy

Alla Rakha was awarded the Padma Shri in 1977 and the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award in 1982. His sons Zakir Hussain, Fazal Qureshi, and Taufiq Qureshi are also noted percussionists.
