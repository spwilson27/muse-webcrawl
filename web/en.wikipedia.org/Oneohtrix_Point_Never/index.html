Oneohtrix Point Never (often abbreviated OPN) is the stage name of American experimental electronic musician Daniel Lopatin (born 1982). Known for sample-based sound collages, fractured synthesizer compositions, and work at the intersection of ambient, noise, and pop, Lopatin became a central figure in 2010s experimental music and an acclaimed film composer.

Career

Lopatin, raised in Massachusetts by Russian-Jewish immigrant parents, began releasing synthesizer music in the mid-2000s; the name Oneohtrix Point Never derives from the Boston radio station Magic 106.7 and a mishearing of "never." Early cassette and CD-R releases were compiled on Rifts (2009). As Chuck Person, he released Eccojams Vol. 1 (2010), made of looped, slowed fragments of pop songs, a foundational work of the vaporwave genre.

Replica (2011), built from chopped samples of television advertisements, was followed by his Warp Records debut R Plus Seven (2013), the collage-like Garden of Delete (2015), the more vocal-oriented Age Of (2018), and Magic Oneohtrix Point Never (2020), co-executive-produced by the Weeknd. His album Again appeared in 2023.

Film and production work

Lopatin's scores for the Safdie brothers' films Good Time — which won the Soundtrack Award at the 2017 Cannes Film Festival — and Uncut Gems (2019) brought him wider recognition. He has produced and written for artists including the Weeknd (co-producing much of Dawn FM), Charli XCX, FKA twigs, and Anohni, and served as musical director for the Weeknd's Super Bowl LV halftime show.
