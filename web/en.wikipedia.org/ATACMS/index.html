ATACMS
Type: Tactical ballistic missile
Place of origin: United States
Manufacturer: Lockheed Martin
In service: 1991–present

The MGM-140 Army Tactical Missile System (ATACMS, pronounced "a-TACK-ums") is an American short-range ballistic missile system developed and manufactured by Lockheed Martin. It is launched from the M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System (MLRS) and the M142 HIMARS, and can strike targets well beyond the range of conventional artillery.

Variants

Early Block I missiles carried hundreds of M74 anti-personnel/anti-materiel submunitions over ranges of about 128–165 km. Later variants introduced GPS guidance and unitary warheads, with ranges extending up to roughly 300 km depending on the model.

Service

ATACMS was first used in combat during Operation Desert Storm in 1991 and later in Iraq and Afghanistan. The system has been exported to several U.S. allies, and it gained renewed prominence after being supplied to Ukraine. Its designated successor is the Precision Strike Missile (PrSM).
