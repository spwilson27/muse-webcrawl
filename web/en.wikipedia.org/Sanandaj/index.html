Sanandaj (Persian: سنندج; Kurdish: Sine) is a city in northwestern Iran and the capital of Kurdestan Province. With a population of about 413,000 at the 2016 census, it is one of the largest Kurdish-majority cities in Iran and a centre of Kurdish culture and music.

History

The modern city took shape in the seventeenth century under the Ardalan emirs, who ruled the region as a semi-autonomous principality under Persian suzerainty until the nineteenth century. Historic mansions and the Asef Vaziri House (now the Kurdistan anthropology museum) survive from this period.

Geography and culture

Sanandaj sits in a mountainous valley at roughly 1,500 metres elevation, with the Abidar mountain park overlooking the city. The population speaks mainly the Sanandaji dialect of Sorani Kurdish. In 2019 UNESCO designated Sanandaj a Creative City of Music.
