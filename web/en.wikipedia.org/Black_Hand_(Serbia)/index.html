The Black Hand (Serbian: Crna ruka), formally Unification or Death (Ujedinjenje ili smrt), was a secret military society formed in 1911 by officers in the army of the Kingdom of Serbia. It sought the liberation and unification of Serb-inhabited territories, including those under Austro-Hungarian and Ottoman rule.

Assassination of Franz Ferdinand

Members of the Black Hand supplied weapons, bombs, and training to the Young Bosnia activists who assassinated Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 — the event that precipitated the July Crisis and the outbreak of the First World War. Whether the Serbian government directed or merely tolerated the plot remains debated.

Suppression

In 1916–17 the Serbian government-in-exile turned on the society. Its leader, Colonel Dragutin Dimitrijević, known as Apis, was tried at Salonica on charges of plotting against the state and executed in June 1917.
