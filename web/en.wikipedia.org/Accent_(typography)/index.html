Accent (typography)

In typography and orthography, an accent is a diacritical mark placed above, below, or through a letter to alter its pronunciation or meaning. Accents are also known more generally as diacritics. Common accents include the acute accent (´), the grave accent (`), the circumflex (ˆ), the tilde (˜), the diaeresis or umlaut (¨), the macron (¯), and the cedilla (¸, placed below a letter).

Usage

Accents serve several distinct functions: marking stress (as in Spanish café), distinguishing vowel quality, indicating nasalisation, differentiating otherwise identical words (French ou "or" versus où "where"), and marking tone in tonal languages such as Vietnamese. In digital typography, accented letters are usually represented either as precomposed characters or as a base letter followed by a combining diacritical mark in Unicode.
