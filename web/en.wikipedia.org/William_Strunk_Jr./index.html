William Strunk Jr. (July 1, 1869 – September 26, 1946) was an American professor of English at Cornell University and the author of The Elements of Style (1918), the brief writing guide later revised and popularized by his former student E. B. White as "Strunk and White."

Life and work

Strunk taught at Cornell for four decades, specializing in Shakespeare and English literature and editing editions of Dryden and Shakespeare. In 1918 he privately printed The Elements of Style for his students, urging concise, vigorous prose — most famously, "Omit needless words." After his death, White's 1957 New Yorker essay revived the book, and the expanded 1959 edition became one of the best-selling style guides in American publishing.
