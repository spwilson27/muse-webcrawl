Inishowen

Inishowen (Irish: Inis Eoghain, meaning "Eoghan's island") is a peninsula in the north of County Donegal, Ireland. It is the largest peninsula in Ireland and one of the country's most northerly points: Malin Head, at its tip, is the most northerly point of mainland Ireland. Inishowen is bounded by Lough Foyle to the east and Lough Swilly to the west, and connected to the rest of Donegal to the south.

Geography and settlements

The peninsula's main towns include Buncrana on Lough Swilly, Carndonagh, Moville, and Greencastle. The landscape is rugged and scenic, with hills such as Slieve Snaght, sandy beaches, and dramatic Atlantic coastline. The area has a population of roughly 40,000.

History

Inishowen takes its name from Eoghan, a son of the fifth-century king Niall of the Nine Hostages. It was historically the territory of the Mac Lochlainn clan and later came under the influence of the O'Dohertys, whose strongholds included castles at Inch, Elagh, and Carrickabraghy. The Grianán of Aileach, a restored stone ringfort near Burt, is among the peninsula's most famous ancient monuments. Sir Cahir O'Doherty's rebellion of 1608, launched from Inishowen, was one of the triggers of the Plantation of Ulster.

Tourism

The "Inishowen 100" scenic drive loops around the peninsula, passing Malin Head (a filming location for Star Wars: The Last Jedi), the Dunree military museum, and numerous beaches and heritage sites. The peninsula is also a noted location for viewing the aurora borealis.
