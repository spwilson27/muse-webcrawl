Mad (magazine)

Mad
Founded: 1952
Founder: Harvey Kurtzman; William Gaines
Publisher: EC Comics / DC Comics
Mascot: Alfred E. Neuman

Mad is an American humour magazine founded in 1952 by editor Harvey Kurtzman and publisher William Gaines. Launched as a comic book series before becoming a magazine, it is widely credited with influencing American satire and comedy.

Features

Signature features include movie and TV parodies, Spy vs. Spy, the Mad Fold-In, and the gap-toothed mascot Alfred E. Neuman with his motto "What, me worry?" Its writers and artists were known as "The Usual Gang of Idiots".

History

Mad survived the 1950s comics crackdown by switching to magazine format in 1955. After decades of monthly publication, regular new issues largely ceased in 2019, with the magazine continuing mainly through reprint specials.
