Stockfish

Stockfish is a free and open-source chess engine, widely regarded as one of the strongest chess engines in the world. It is available for desktop and mobile platforms and is integrated into many chess websites and analysis tools.

History

Stockfish began in 2008 as a fork of the engine Glaurung by Tord Romstad; Marco Costalba became its lead developer, and the project grew into a large community effort. Unusually, Stockfish is developed through distributed testing on the "Fishtest" framework, in which volunteers contribute computing power to test code changes over millions of games before they are accepted.

Strength

Stockfish has repeatedly topped computer chess rating lists and has won numerous seasons of the Top Chess Engine Championship (TCEC) and the Chess.com Computer Chess Championship. Since 2020 it has combined classical alpha–beta search with an efficiently updatable neural network evaluation (NNUE), a technique borrowed from shogi engines, which further increased its playing strength. Its Elo rating is estimated to exceed 3500, far above the best human players.
