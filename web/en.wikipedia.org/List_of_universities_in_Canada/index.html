List of universities in Canada

Universities in Canada are established and governed by provincial and territorial governments; there is no national ministry of education. As of recent counts, Canada has over 100 public universities enrolling more than a million students in undergraduate and graduate programs. Degrees are quality-assured through provincial bodies and the institutions' membership in Universities Canada.

Language and research

Most Canadian universities teach in English; Quebec and some other institutions teach in French, and several (such as the University of Ottawa) are officially bilingual. The research-intensive U15 group accounts for a large share of sponsored research. The oldest institution with degree-granting roots is Université Laval, descended from the Séminaire de Québec founded in 1663.
