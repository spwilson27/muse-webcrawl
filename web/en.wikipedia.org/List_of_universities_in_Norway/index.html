List of universities in Norway

This is a list of universities in Norway. Public higher education in Norway is free of tuition fees for all students. The country's oldest university is the University of Oslo, founded in 1811 when Norway was under Danish rule.

Universities

  • University of Oslo (1811) — Norway's oldest and largest university.
  • University of Bergen (1946).
  • Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) (1996, roots to 1760) — Trondheim; Norway's main technical university.
  • University of Tromsø – The Arctic University of Norway (1968).
  • University of Stavanger (2005).
  • University of Agder (2007) — Kristiansand and Grimstad.
  • Nord University (2016) — Bodø and other campuses.
  • University of South-Eastern Norway (2018).
  • Oslo Metropolitan University (OsloMet) (2018).
  • Inland Norway University of Applied Sciences and other university colleges.

Specialised institutions

Specialised institutions include the Norwegian School of Economics (NHH) in Bergen, BI Norwegian Business School (private), the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU) at Ås, and the Oslo School of Architecture and Design.
