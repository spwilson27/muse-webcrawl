Petsamo

Petsamo (Finnish: Petsamo; Russian: Pechenga) is a historical region in the far north of Europe, on the coast of the Barents Sea, in present-day Murmansk Oblast, Russia. It gave Finland access to the Arctic Ocean between 1920 and 1944.

History

Under the 1920 Treaty of Tartu, Soviet Russia ceded Petsamo to Finland, which developed the ice-free harbour of Liinahamari and a nickel mine at Kolosjoki. Finland lost part of the region after the Winter War (1940), and ceded the remainder in the 1944 Moscow Armistice and the 1947 Paris Peace Treaty.

Legacy

The area remains part of Russia's Pechengsky District; the nickel industry at Nikel (formerly Kolosjoki) continued under Soviet and Russian operation.
