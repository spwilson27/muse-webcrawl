Śūnyatā

Śūnyatā (Sanskrit: शून्यता), often translated as "emptiness" or "voidness", is a Buddhist concept central to Mahayana Buddhism: phenomena are empty of inherent, independent existence (svabhāva).

Development

The concept developed in the Prajñāpāramitā sutras and was philosophically systematised by Nāgārjuna in the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā, where emptiness is identified with dependent origination (pratītyasamutpāda): because things arise only in dependence on causes and conditions, they lack any fixed essence.

Interpretations

In Madhyamaka, emptiness itself is empty — it is not a thing or an absolute. In Yogācāra and later traditions, śūnyatā is also treated as an object of meditative realisation, the direct insight into which leads to liberation.
