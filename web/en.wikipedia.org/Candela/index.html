Candela
Symbol: cd
SI base unit of luminous intensity

The candela (symbol: cd) is the unit of luminous intensity in the International System of Units (SI). It measures luminous power per unit solid angle emitted by a light source in a particular direction.

Definition

Since 2019 the candela is defined by taking the fixed numerical value of the luminous efficacy of monochromatic radiation of frequency 540 × 1012 Hz, Kcd, to be 683 lm/W. One candela is roughly the luminous intensity of an ordinary candle.

History

The candela was adopted in 1948, replacing earlier units such as the international candle and the Hefner lamp. It is one of the seven SI base units.
