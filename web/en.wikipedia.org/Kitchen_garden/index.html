Kitchen garden

A kitchen garden (called a potager in French) is a garden or part of a garden devoted to growing vegetables, fruits and herbs for household use, traditionally kept separate from ornamental flower gardens.

History

Great houses in Europe maintained walled kitchen gardens supplying produce year-round, with glasshouses, cold frames and trained fruit trees on the walls. Monasteries, cottages and allotments maintained smaller versions. The French potager tradition, exemplified by Villandry, treats vegetables ornamentally in formal patterns.

Design

Kitchen gardens are usually laid out in rectangular beds with paths for access, sited in full sun near the kitchen. Succession sowing, crop rotation and companion planting are typical practices to keep a continuous supply.
