Canoe sprint

Canoe sprint is a water sport in which athletes race canoes or kayaks on calm water over set distances. The sport is governed by the International Canoe Federation (ICF) and has been part of the Summer Olympic Games since 1936.

Events and craft

Races are held in kayaks (K), propelled with a double-bladed paddle while seated, and canoes (C), propelled with a single-bladed paddle while kneeling. Olympic distances are 200, 500 and 1000 metres, in singles, doubles and fours, raced in lanes on flatwater courses.

History

Competitive paddling grew out of 19th-century canoe clubs in Europe and North America. Canoe sprint world championships were first held in 1938. It is distinct from canoe slalom, which is raced on white water.
