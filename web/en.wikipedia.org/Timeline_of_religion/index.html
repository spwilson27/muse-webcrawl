Timeline of religion

This timeline of religion outlines major events in the history of religious belief and practice, from prehistoric burial customs and the world's oldest known temple complexes, through the rise of organised religions, to the present day.

Prehistory and the ancient world

Deliberate burial of the dead appears among early humans by roughly 100,000 years ago. Monumental ritual architecture such as Göbekli Tepe dates to about 9500 BCE. Writing in Mesopotamia and Egypt recorded elaborate pantheons by the 3rd millennium BCE, and the earliest texts of Hindu traditions were composed in the 2nd millennium BCE. The Axial Age (roughly 800–200 BCE) saw the emergence of Buddhism, Jainism, Confucianism, Taoism and Greek philosophy, alongside the consolidation of monotheism in Judaism.

Common era

Christianity spread through the Roman Empire in the 1st–4th centuries CE, Islam arose in Arabia in the 7th century and expanded rapidly, and Sikhism was founded in the Punjab in the 15th century. The modern era brought the Protestant Reformation, missionary movements, new religious movements such as the Baháʼí Faith, and growing religious unaffiliation in many countries.
