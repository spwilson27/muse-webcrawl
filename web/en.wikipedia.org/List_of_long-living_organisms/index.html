List of long-living organisms

This is a list of long-living organisms — the individual organisms and clonal colonies with the longest known lifespans. The longest-lived individual (non-clonal) organisms are certain trees, notably bristlecone pines approaching 5,000 years old. Clonal organisms, which reproduce asexually from a single ancestor, can be far older: some estimates for seagrass meadows and fungal colonies run to tens or even hundreds of thousands of years. The longest-lived known animals include the Greenland shark (up to about 500 years) and the ocean quahog clam (over 500 years).

Longest-lived humans of note

  • Jeanne Calment of France (1875–1997) — the oldest verified human lifespan, 122 years and 164 days.
  • Kane Tanaka of Japan (1903–2022) — 119 years, second-oldest verified.
  • Sarah Knauss of the United States (1880–1999) — 119 years.
