British American Tobacco p.l.c. (BAT) is a British multinational tobacco company headquartered in London. Founded in 1902 as a joint venture between the UK's Imperial Tobacco Company and the American Tobacco Company of the United States, it is one of the world's largest tobacco companies and a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index.

History

The 1902 agreement divided markets: the two parent companies kept their home territories while BAT handled trade in the rest of the world, growing rapidly in Asia, Africa and Latin America. In 1911 the American Tobacco trust was dissolved by the US Supreme Court, and BAT became independent. The company acquired Brown & Williamson in the United States in 1927 and for many years was known in the US through that subsidiary.

In 1999 BAT merged its insurance businesses (as Zurich Financial Services) out and acquired Rothmans International. In 2017 it completed a $49.4 billion takeover of the remainder of Reynolds American, becoming the largest listed tobacco company by revenue and gaining full control of brands such as Newport and Camel in the US.

Products and strategy

BAT's cigarette brands include Dunhill, Kent, Lucky Strike, Pall Mall, Rothmans, Kool and Newport. Since the 2010s the company has invested heavily in "new category" products: the Vuse (Vype) vapour brand, the glo tobacco-heating device, and Velo nicotine pouches, as cigarette volumes decline in developed markets. Like other major tobacco companies, BAT has faced extensive litigation and regulation over the health effects of smoking and its historical marketing practices.
