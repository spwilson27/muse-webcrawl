Skyfall (song)

“Skyfall” is a song recorded by English singer Adele for the 2012 James Bond film Skyfall. It was written by Adele and producer Paul Epworth, with orchestration by J. A. C. Redford, and released in October 2012 to coincide with the fiftieth anniversary of the Bond film series.

Composition and reception

Epworth and Adele aimed to write a dramatic ballad in the tradition of Shirley Bassey's Bond themes, incorporating the classic Monty Norman James Bond chord progression. Recorded at Abbey Road Studios with a 77-piece orchestra, the song runs just under five minutes. It was a worldwide hit, reaching number one in many countries and the top ten in the United Kingdom and United States.

Awards

“Skyfall” became the first Bond theme to win the Academy Award for Best Original Song, at the 85th Academy Awards in 2013. It also won the Golden Globe for Best Original Song, the Brit Award for British Single of the Year, and the Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media.
