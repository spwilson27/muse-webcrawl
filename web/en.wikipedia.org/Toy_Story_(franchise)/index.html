Toy Story (franchise)

Created byPixar Animation Studios

Toy Story is an American media franchise that started in 1995 with the release of the animated feature film of the same name, produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. The franchise is based on the anthropomorphic concept that all toys are secretly alive and possess human-like personalities. It focuses primarily on the characters of cowboy Woody and space ranger Buzz Lightyear, and the boy who owns them, Andy.

Films

The main series consists of Toy Story (1995), Toy Story 2 (1999), Toy Story 3 (2010), and Toy Story 4 (2019), with a fifth film in development. Toy Story 3 and Toy Story 4 each won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. The spin-off film Lightyear (2022) tells the story of the fictional character who inspired the Buzz Lightyear toy. The franchise has grossed more than $3.3 billion worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing animated franchises of all time.

Characters

Principal characters include Sheriff Woody (voiced by Tom Hanks), Buzz Lightyear (Tim Allen), Jessie (Joan Cusack), Mr. Potato Head (Don Rickles), Slinky Dog, Rex, Hamm, Bo Peep, and, introduced in the fourth film, Forky. The toys' stories revolve around loyalty, growing up, and being outgrown by their owners.

Other media

The franchise includes the TV specials Toy Story of Terror! and Toy Story That Time Forgot, short films, video games, the Disney+ series Forky Asks a Question, and themed lands (Toy Story Land) at Disney parks in Florida, Paris, Hong Kong, and Shanghai.
