Protected areas of South Australia

Protected areas of South Australia comprise a network of reserves administered under the state's National Parks and Wildlife Act 1972 and related legislation, forming part of Australia's National Reserve System. They include national parks, conservation parks, regional reserves, game reserves, and wilderness protection areas, together covering roughly a fifth of the state's land area, in addition to a system of marine parks.

Categories and examples

Notable areas include Flinders Ranges (Ikara-Flinders Ranges National Park), the Coorong National Park, Kangaroo Island parks such as Flinders Chase, and the vast desert reserves of the far north, including the Witjira and Kati Thanda-Lake Eyre national parks. Co-management arrangements with Aboriginal traditional owners apply to several parks, and Indigenous Protected Areas add further large areas of the Anangu Pitjantjatjara Yankunytjatjara lands to the reserve system.

Marine protected areas

South Australia established a network of 19 marine parks in 2012, covering waters such as Spencer Gulf, Gulf St Vincent, and the Encounter Bay region.
