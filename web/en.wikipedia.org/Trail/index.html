Trail

A trail is a path, track or unpaved lane or road for travel, typically through natural terrain. Trails are used for recreation — hiking, cycling, horse riding and trail running — and, in many parts of the world, remain everyday transport routes.

Types

Long-distance trails such as the Appalachian Trail and Pacific Crest Trail in the United States run for thousands of kilometres. National trail systems designate scenic, historic and recreational routes, while local networks serve mountain biking, cross-country skiing and equestrian use. Trail surfaces range from bare earth and boardwalk to gravel and stone pitching.

Construction and impact

Trail design considers gradient, drainage and erosion control; switchbacks reduce steepness on hillsides. Heavy use can damage fragile environments, so land managers apply permits, seasonal closures and leave-no-trace education.
