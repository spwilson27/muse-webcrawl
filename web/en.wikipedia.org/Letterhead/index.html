Letterhead

A letterhead is the heading at the top of a sheet of letter paper, typically consisting of a name, address, contact details and often a logo or graphic design, used by businesses, organisations and individuals for formal correspondence. The term is also applied to the pre-printed stationery itself.

Design and use

Letterheads developed with modern printing in the 19th century, when engraved and lithographed headings became a mark of commercial respectability. A letterhead's design is usually part of an organisation's corporate identity: it may occupy the top margin alone or extend to side margins and footers, and may include registration numbers, a list of directors (historically required on British company stationery), and other legally mandated information. Letter-sized paper is standard in North America, while A4 is used in most of the rest of the world. With the decline of paper correspondence, digital letterhead templates in word processors and email serve the same identifying function.
