History Trust of South Australia

Established: 1981

Type: Statutory authority

Location: Adelaide, South Australia

Parent agency: Government of South Australia

The History Trust of South Australia is a statutory authority of the South Australian Government established under the History Trust of South Australia Act 1981. Its role is to encourage the research, appreciation and enjoyment of South Australian history and to safeguard the state's movable cultural heritage collections.

Museums

The Trust operates three museums in Adelaide:

  • The Migration Museum, opened in 1986, which explores the history of migration and settlement in South Australia; it was the first migration museum in Australia.
  • The National Motor Museum at Birdwood in the Adelaide Hills, which holds one of the largest collections of motor vehicles in Australia.
  • The South Australian Maritime Museum at Port Adelaide, which preserves the state's maritime heritage and manages historic vessels and lighthouses.

The Trust also manages the Centre for Democracy in Adelaide, in partnership with the State Library of South Australia.

Collections and programs

The Trust holds the State History Collection, comprising more than 40,000 objects relating to South Australian history. It supports community history through grants, advice, the Community Museums Program and the annual South Australia's History Festival (originally About Time), one of the largest community history festivals in Australia. It also maintains a network of registered and accredited community museums across the state.
