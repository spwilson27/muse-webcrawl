Paris Métro Line 15

Paris Métro Line 15 is a rapid transit line under construction as part of the Grand Paris Express project in Île-de-France, France. It will form a ring line through the inner suburbs of Paris, the longest metro line in France when complete.

Route and construction

The fully underground line will be about 75 kilometres long with 36 stations, running from Noisy–Champs in the east through Champigny, Créteil, Villejuif, Bagneux, Boulogne-Billancourt, Saint-Cloud, La Défense, Saint-Denis, and Rosny-sous-Bois. It is divided into three sections: Line 15 South (Pont de Sèvres to Noisy–Champs), Line 15 West, and Line 15 East. Construction began in 2015; the southern section is scheduled to open first, around 2026, with the remaining sections following in the early 2030s.

Operation

The line will be fully automated and driverless, using wide trains on standard-gauge track, unlike much of the older Paris Métro. It is expected to carry around a million passengers a day and to relieve congestion on radial lines by enabling suburb-to-suburb journeys without passing through central Paris.
