Paris Métro Line 14

Line 14
Opened: 15 October 1998
Type: Automated driverless metro
Termini: Saint-Denis–Pleyel — Aéroport d'Orly

Paris Métro Line 14 is a fully automated, driverless line of the Paris Métro, opened in 1998 and originally known by its project name METEOR (Métro Est-Ouest Rapide). It was the first Paris line built with platform screen doors and trains with no driving cab.

History

The line was built to relieve the saturated RER A across central Paris. It was extended south to Olympiades in 2007, north to Mairie de Saint-Ouen in 2020, and in June 2024 — in time for the Paris Olympic Games — both northwards to Saint-Denis–Pleyel and southwards to Orly Airport, making it the longest line of the network at the time.

Operation

Trains are rubber-tyred MP 89 and MP 14 stock, running at high frequency with headways under 90 seconds in peak hours.
