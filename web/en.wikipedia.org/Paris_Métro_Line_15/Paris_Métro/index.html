Paris Métro

Paris Métro
Opened: 19 July 1900
Lines: 16
Stations: 300+
Length: ~245 km
Operator: RATP

The Paris Métro is the rapid transit system of Paris and its metropolitan area. Opened in 1900 for the Exposition Universelle, it is known for its dense coverage of the city, its Art Nouveau station entrances designed by Hector Guimard, and its short distances between stations.

Network

The system has 16 lines, numbered 1 to 14 plus the shuttle lines 3bis and 7bis, and more than 300 stations. Lines 1 and 14 are fully automated and driverless. The Métro carries over four million passengers per day, making it one of the busiest metro systems in Europe.

Expansion

The Grand Paris Express project is adding four new automated lines (15–18) and extending lines 11 and 14, roughly doubling the network in the suburbs.
