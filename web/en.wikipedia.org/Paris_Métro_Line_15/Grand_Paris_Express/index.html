Grand Paris Express
Location: Île-de-France, France
New lines: 15, 16, 17, 18 plus extensions of 11 and 14
Length: ~200 km; 68 stations

The Grand Paris Express is a group of new automated rapid transit lines being built in the Paris region of France. It consists of four new lines (15, 16, 17 and 18) around the capital and extensions of existing Métro lines 11 and 14, totalling about 200 kilometres of track and 68 stations.

Purpose

The project aims to connect the suburbs directly to one another without passing through central Paris, relieving the saturated RER and Métro lines and supporting the development of the Grand Paris metropolis. It is managed by the Société du Grand Paris.

Lines

Line 15, an orbital line of about 75 km, is the backbone of the system. Lines 16 and 17 serve the eastern and northern suburbs, including Charles de Gaulle Airport and Le Bourget, while Line 18 serves the southern plateau of Saclay. Openings are phased from the mid-2020s into the 2030s.
