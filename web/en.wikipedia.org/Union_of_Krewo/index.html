Union of Krewo

The Union of Krewo (also spelled Kreva; Polish: unia w Krewie) was a set of prenuptial agreements made at Kreva Castle on 14 August 1385 by Grand Duke Jogaila of Lithuania in exchange for marriage to Queen Jadwiga of Poland and the Polish crown.

Union of Krewo
Signed14 August 1385, Krewo Castle
PartiesJogaila of Lithuania; Polish lords on behalf of Jadwiga
EffectJogaila's baptism, marriage, and Polish–Lithuanian union

Terms

Jogaila promised to: accept Catholic baptism with his brothers, nobles and people; marry Jadwiga and be crowned King of Poland; pay compensation to William of Austria, Jadwiga's former betrothed; release Polish prisoners; recover lost Polish and Lithuanian lands; and — in the famous applicare clause — attach his Lithuanian and Ruthenian lands to the Crown of Poland in perpetuity.

Implementation and debate

Jogaila was baptised Władysław on 15 February 1386, married Jadwiga on 18 February, and was crowned on 4 March. Historians long debated whether applicare meant full incorporation of Lithuania into Poland or a looser personal union; Lithuania in practice retained separate institutions, army, treasury and law, deepened later by the Union of Lublin (1569) into the Commonwealth.
