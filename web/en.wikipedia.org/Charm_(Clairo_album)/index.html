Charm (Clairo album)

Charm is the third studio album by American singer-songwriter Clairo, released on 12 July 2024. It was co-produced by Clairo and Leon Michels and recorded with live instrumentation drawing on soft rock, jazz and soul of the 1970s.

Background

Following Sling (2021), Claire Cottrill recorded Charm largely live to tape in New York studios, working with Michels of El Michels Affair. The album was self-released after her departure from Republic Records.

Singles and reception

Singles included "Sexy to Someone" and "Nomad". Critics praised its warm, understated arrangements. The album debuted in the top ten of the Billboard 200 and was nominated for Best Alternative Music Album at the Grammy Awards.

Track listing

Tracks include "Nomad", "Sexy to Someone", "Second Nature", "Slow Dance", "Thank You", "Terrapin", "Juna", "Add Up My Love", "Echo", "Glory of the Snow" and "Pier 4".
