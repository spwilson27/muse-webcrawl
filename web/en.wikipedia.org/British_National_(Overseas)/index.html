British National (Overseas), abbreviated BN(O), is a class of British nationality created by the Hong Kong Act 1985 for Hong Kong residents who were British Dependent Territories citizens. Registration was voluntary and took place between 1987 and the handover of Hong Kong to China in 1997; around 3.4 million people hold the status.

Rights

BN(O) status alone does not confer the right of abode in the United Kingdom, but holders may travel visa-free as visitors. In January 2021, following the imposition of the Hong Kong national security law, the UK opened a BN(O) visa route allowing status holders and their dependants to live, work and study in the UK, with a path to settlement and citizenship.
