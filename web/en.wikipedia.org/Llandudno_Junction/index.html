Llandudno Junction (Welsh: Cyffordd Llandudno) is a town in Conwy County Borough, Wales. It grew up around the railway junction where the branch line to Llandudno leaves the North Wales Main Line, close to the historic town of Conwy and the River Conwy estuary.

History

The settlement developed after the Chester and Holyhead Railway opened in the 1840s. A station at the junction, with engine sheds and sidings, made the town an important railway centre in North Wales; the railway works and depot were major local employers through the steam era.

Transport and community

Llandudno Junction station is served by trains on the North Wales Main Line between Crewe and Holyhead and by services up the Conwy Valley line to Blaenau Ffestiniog. The town adjoins Deganwy and Conwy, and the A55 North Wales Expressway passes nearby.
