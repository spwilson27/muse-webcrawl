The Air Force Reserve Command (AFRC) is a major command of the United States Air Force, headquartered at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia. It administers the Air Force Reserve, the federal reserve component of the Air Force, comprising tens of thousands of Citizen Airmen.

Mission

AFRC provides combat-ready forces across the full spectrum of Air Force missions, including airlift, aerial refueling, fighter and bomber operations, space and cyber support, and aeromedical evacuation. Reservists serve part-time in peacetime but can be mobilized to active duty, and many fly the same aircraft types as active-duty units, often in formal associate arrangements.

History

The Air Force Reserve traces its origins to 1948 and was reorganized over the decades; in 1997 it gained the status of a major command as Air Force Reserve Command, reflecting the Reserve's growing operational role after the Cold War.
