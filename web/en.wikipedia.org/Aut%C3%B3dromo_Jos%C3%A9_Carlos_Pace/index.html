The Autódromo José Carlos Pace, better known as Interlagos, is a motor racing circuit in the Interlagos district of São Paulo, Brazil. It has hosted the Brazilian (now São Paulo) Grand Prix of Formula One for most years since 1973 and is named after Brazilian driver José Carlos Pace, who died in 1977.

Layout

The modern Grand Prix layout is about 4.3 km long, runs anticlockwise, and is renowned for its flowing infield section, the Senna S curves at the start, and the long uphill run to the finish line. The circuit sits at roughly 800 metres above sea level, affecting engine performance.

History

The circuit opened in 1940 on land between two reservoirs, which gave it the name Interlagos ("between lakes"). Shortened and upgraded for its return to the Formula One calendar in 1990, it has witnessed many championship deciders, including title wins by Kimi Räikkönen (2007), Lewis Hamilton (2008) and Jenson Button (2009).
