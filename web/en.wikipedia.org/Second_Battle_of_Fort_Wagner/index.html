Second Battle of Fort Wagner

Date18 July 1863
LocationMorris Island, South Carolina
ResultConfederate victory
Notable unit54th Massachusetts Infantry Regiment

The Second Battle of Fort Wagner, also known as the Second Assault on Morris Island, was fought on 18 July 1863 during the American Civil War. Union Army troops commanded by Brigadier General Truman Seymour assaulted the Confederate stronghold of Fort Wagner on Morris Island, guarding Charleston Harbor.

The 54th Massachusetts

The assault was led by the 54th Massachusetts Infantry Regiment, one of the first major American military units made up of Black soldiers. Its commander, Colonel Robert Gould Shaw, was killed in the attack, along with many of his men. Though the assault failed and the fort was abandoned by the Confederates only after a siege months later, the regiment's bravery helped spur further enlistment of African American soldiers.

Legacy

The battle was depicted in the 1989 film Glory.
