Launch Entry Suit

The Launch Entry Suit (LES) was a partial-pressure suit worn by NASA Space Shuttle crews from STS-5 in 1982 until its replacement by the Advanced Crew Escape Suit in 1994. Nicknamed the "pumpkin suit" for its bright international orange colour, it was introduced after early Shuttle flights had used only flight suits.

Design

The LES provided pressure protection up to about 30 km altitude, an oxygen supply, a parachute harness, flotation equipment and survival gear for bailout during launch or re-entry — phases in which full pressure suits were judged unnecessary but some protection was wanted after early unprotected flights. It included a helmet with communications, anti-g suit trousers, and a life raft pack.

Replacement

Following the Challenger disaster, requirements tightened, and the LES evolved into the full-pressure Advanced Crew Escape Suit (ACES), modelled on high-altitude reconnaissance suits, which flew from 1994 to the end of the Shuttle programme in 2011.
