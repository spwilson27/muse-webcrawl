USS Pennsylvania (BB-38)

USS Pennsylvania
Class: Pennsylvania-class battleship
Launched: March 16, 1915
Commissioned: June 12, 1916
Fate: Scuttled after atomic tests, 1948

USS Pennsylvania (BB-38) was the lead ship of her class of super-dreadnought battleships built for the United States Navy in the 1910s. She was the third Navy ship named for the state of Pennsylvania.

World War II

Pennsylvania was in drydock at Pearl Harbor during the Japanese attack on December 7, 1941, and escaped major damage. She subsequently supported amphibious operations across the Pacific, including at Attu, Makin, Kwajalein, Saipan, Guam, Peleliu, and Lingayen Gulf. A torpedo hit on August 12, 1945 — days before the war's end — killed 20 crewmen and left her the last surviving battleship damaged in the war.

Postwar

Badly damaged and obsolete, she was used as a target ship in the Operation Crossroads atomic tests at Bikini Atoll in 1946 and was scuttled off Kwajalein in 1948.
