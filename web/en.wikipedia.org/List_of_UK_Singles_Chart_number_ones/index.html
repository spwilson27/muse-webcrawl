List of UK Singles Chart number ones

The UK Singles Chart is compiled by the Official Charts Company and ranks the best-selling singles in the United Kingdom. The first number one, in November 1952, was "Here in My Heart" by Al Martino. Well over a thousand songs have topped the chart since.
