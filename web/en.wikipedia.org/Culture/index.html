Culture

Culture is an umbrella term encompassing the social behaviour, institutions, and norms found in human societies, as well as the knowledge, beliefs, arts, laws, customs, capabilities, and habits of the individuals in these groups. Culture is often originated from or attributed to a specific region or location.

Culture
Studied by: Anthropology, sociology, cultural studies
Key concepts: Norms, values, symbols, language
Related: Civilisation, society, tradition

Definitions

Anthropologist Edward Burnett Tylor defined culture in 1871 as "that complex whole which includes knowledge, belief, art, morals, law, custom, and any other capabilities and habits acquired by man as a member of society." UNESCO describes culture as the set of distinctive spiritual, material, intellectual, and emotional features of a society or social group.

Elements

Cultures share elements including language, religion, cuisine, social habits, music, and arts. Material culture comprises physical objects such as technology and architecture, while non-material culture includes intangible ideas, values, and worldviews. Cultural change occurs through invention, diffusion, and contact between groups.

Cultural studies

Modern study of culture draws on anthropology, archaeology, cultural geography, and sociology. Concepts such as cultural relativism, ethnocentrism, acculturation, and cultural appropriation frame debates about how cultures interact and change, especially under globalisation.
