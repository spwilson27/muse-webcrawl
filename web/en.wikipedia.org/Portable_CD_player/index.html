Portable CD player

A portable CD player is a portable audio player used to play compact discs. The first portable CD player, released by Sony in November 1984, was the D-50 (marketed as the Discman in some regions). The Discman name became synonymous with portable CD players in general.

Features

Basic units offer play/pause, stop, and track skipping. More expensive models offered additional features such as programmable playback, bass boost, and anti-skip protection (electronic skip protection buffer memory). Most ran on AA batteries or a rechargeable battery pack, and output sound through headphones or a line-out jack.

Decline

Portable CD players were largely displaced in the 2000s by MP3 players and later smartphones, though they retain a niche following.
