Virginia Beach is an independent city in southeastern Virginia, United States. With a population of 459,470 at the 2020 census, it is the most populous city in Virginia and the 43rd-largest in the United States. It is part of the Hampton Roads metropolitan area and lies where Chesapeake Bay meets the Atlantic Ocean.

History

The Chesepian people inhabited the area before English colonists first landed at Cape Henry in April 1607 en route to founding Jamestown. The modern city was formed in 1963 by the consolidation of the small resort city of Virginia Beach with Princess Anne County. It grew rapidly as a suburb and resort destination in the second half of the 20th century.

Tourism

Virginia Beach is a classic American resort city, with miles of beaches, hundreds of hotels and a three-mile concrete boardwalk along the oceanfront. Guinness World Records lists it as having the longest pleasure beach in the world. Attractions include the Virginia Aquarium and Marine Science Center, First Landing State Park — Virginia's most visited state park — the Cape Henry Lighthouse, and the annual North American Sand Soccer Championships and Neptune Festival.

Military

The city hosts several military facilities, including Naval Air Station Oceana, a master jet base; Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek–Fort Story; and Dam Neck Annex, home to special warfare training commands.
