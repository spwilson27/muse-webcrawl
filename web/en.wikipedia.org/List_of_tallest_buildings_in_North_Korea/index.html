List of tallest buildings in North Korea

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of the tallest buildings in North Korea ranks skyscrapers in the Democratic People's Republic of Korea by height. Nearly all of the country's tall buildings are in the capital, Pyongyang, and many are residential towers built as prestige projects. The country's tallest structure is the pyramid-shaped Ryugyong Hotel, which remained unfinished for decades.

Ryugyong Hotel

Construction of the Ryugyong Hotel began in 1987 with the aim of opening for the 1989 World Festival of Youth and Students, but work halted in 1992 amid the country's economic crisis, leaving a bare concrete shell that dominated Pyongyang's skyline for 16 years. An Egyptian firm, Orascom, resumed work in 2008 and completed the glass exterior by 2011, but the hotel has never opened.
