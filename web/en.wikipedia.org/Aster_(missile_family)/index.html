Aster (missile family)

TypeSurface-to-air missile family
ManufacturerMBDA (Eurosam consortium)
OriginFrance / Italy
VariantsAster 15, Aster 30

The Aster family of surface-to-air missiles is a Franco-Italian series developed by MBDA through the Eurosam consortium. Vertically launched and guided by an active radar seeker, Aster missiles provide naval and land-based air defence against aircraft, drones, cruise missiles, and ballistic missiles.

Variants

The Aster 15 is a short-to-medium-range missile (over 30 km) used for point and local area defence, while the Aster 30 is a longer-range system (over 100 km, with anti-ballistic capability in later versions) employed in area defence. Both share a two-stage design: a solid booster that separates after launch and a dart-like terminal stage, whose small lateral thrusters ("PIF-PAF" system) give exceptional manoeuvrability for hit-to-kill interception.

Service

Aster missiles arm many modern European warships, including the British Type 45 destroyers (as Sea Viper), French and Italian Horizon-class and FREMM frigates, and the French carrier Charles de Gaulle. The land-based SAMP/T system, built around the Aster 30, serves with the French and Italian armed forces and has been exported and deployed in active air-defence operations.
