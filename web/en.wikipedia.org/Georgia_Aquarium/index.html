Georgia Aquarium is a public aquarium in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. When it opened in 2005 it was the world's largest aquarium, holding more than 10 million gallons of fresh and salt water and housing tens of thousands of animals representing several hundred species. It was funded largely by a $250 million donation from Home Depot co-founder Bernie Marcus.

Exhibits

The centrepiece gallery, Ocean Voyager, features a 6.3-million-gallon tank viewed through a 100-foot underwater tunnel and one of the world's largest acrylic viewing windows. It is one of the few aquariums outside Asia to house whale sharks, which share the exhibit with manta rays and thousands of other fish. Other galleries include Cold Water Quest (beluga whales, penguins, sea otters), Southern Company River Scout (freshwater species and albino alligators), Tropical Diver (coral reef), and a dolphin presentation theatre. A major expansion, Sharks! Predators of the Deep, opened in 2020.

Research and conservation

The aquarium conducts research on whale sharks, beluga whales, and coral restoration, and operates the Correll Center for Aquatic Animal Health. It is accredited by the Association of Zoos and Aquariums and is a popular tourist attraction in downtown Atlanta's Centennial Olympic Park area, adjacent to the World of Coca-Cola.
