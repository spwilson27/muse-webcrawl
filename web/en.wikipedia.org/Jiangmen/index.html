Jiangmen is a prefecture-level city in Guangdong Province, China. Located on the western side of the Pearl River Delta, it administers a large area that includes the districts of Pengjiang, Jianghai and Xinhui, as well as several county-level cities including Taishan, Kaiping, Enping and Heshan.

History

The port of Jiangmen was opened to Western trade in 1904. The region is famous as the ancestral home of many overseas Chinese, particularly in North America, and is known for its diaolou — fortified multi-storey towers, especially in Kaiping, which are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Geography and economy

Jiangmen lies along the Xi River. Its economy includes manufacturing, agriculture and tourism, and it is connected by bridge and rail to Guangzhou, Zhuhai and other Pearl River Delta cities. The area is part of the Sze Yup region, whose dialect differs from standard Cantonese.

Culture

The city and its surrounding counties are noted for Chenpi (aged tangerine peel) from Xinhui, ancestral halls, and a large emigrant heritage reflected in remittance architecture and clan associations abroad.
