Marina Abramović (born 1946) is a Serbian performance artist whose work, spanning more than five decades, explores the relationship between performer and audience, the limits of the body, and the possibilities of the mind. She has described herself as the "grandmother of performance art".

Work

Her early Rhythm series (1973–1974) included Rhythm 0, in which she stood passively for six hours beside 72 objects the audience could use on her as they wished. From 1976 she collaborated for over a decade with the German artist Ulay; their final joint work involved walking from opposite ends of the Great Wall of China to meet in the middle. Her 2010 retrospective at the Museum of Modern Art in New York featured The Artist Is Present, in which she sat silently across from museum visitors for a total of 736 hours.
