Lofi Girl is a French YouTube channel and music label known for its continuous livestreams of lo-fi hip hop music accompanied by an animated character — a girl studying or relaxing at a desk. The channel, created in 2015 as ChilledCow and rebranded in 2021, became a defining symbol of the lo-fi streaming culture and of online study culture.

The stream and character

The flagship stream, "beats to relax/study to", began in 2017 and ran for years with only brief interruptions, including a mistaken copyright takedown in 2020 and a false DMCA claim in 2022 that ended a stream after more than 20,000 hours. The anime-style character was drawn by artist Juan Pablo Machado, inspired by a scene from the Studio Ghibli film Whisper of the Heart. In 2023 the channel introduced a companion character, Synthwave Boy, for its synthwave stream.

Impact

Lofi Girl has accumulated tens of millions of subscribers, released hundreds of albums and compilations from lo-fi producers, and has been widely parodied and referenced as an internet cultural icon, including official collaborations with universities, game studios and space agencies.
