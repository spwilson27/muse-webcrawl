The 2002 Winter Paralympics, the eighth Winter Paralympics, were held in Salt Lake City, Utah, United States, from 7 to 16 March 2002. They followed the 2002 Winter Olympics and were the first Winter Paralympics held in North America since the movement's early years.

Events

About 416 athletes from 36 nations competed in 92 events across five sports: alpine skiing, biathlon, cross-country skiing, ice sledge hockey and sledge racing. Norway topped the medal standings, with the United States and Germany also performing strongly on home and near-home snow.

Highlights

The Games featured record attendance for a Winter Paralympics and extensive television coverage in the United States. The host nation's sledge hockey team won the gold medal, defeating Norway in the final.
