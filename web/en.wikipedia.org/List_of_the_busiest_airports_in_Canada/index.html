List of the busiest airports in Canada

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of the busiest airports in Canada ranks Canadian airports by total passenger traffic (enplaned and deplaned passengers) using Statistics Canada and airport authority figures. Toronto Pearson International Airport is by far the country's busiest airport and a major North American hub; it alone handles well over a third of Canada's air passengers.
