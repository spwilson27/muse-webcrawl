Box office: Over ₹1,800 crore worldwide

Baahubali 2: The Conclusion is a 2017 Indian epic action film directed by S. S. Rajamouli, who co-wrote it with V. Vijayendra Prasad. It is the sequel to Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and the second part of the Baahubali franchise. Simultaneously shot in Telugu and Tamil, the film stars Prabhas, Rana Daggubati, Anushka Shetty, and Tamannaah.

Plot

The film continues the story of the kingdom of Mahishmati. Through an extended flashback, Shiva learns of his father Amarendra Baahubali's past: his rise as the beloved crown prince, his love for the warrior princess Devasena, his fall through the scheming of Bhallaladeva and Bijjaladeva, and his death at the hands of the loyal slave-warrior Kattappa — answering the famous question left by the first film, "Why did Kattappa kill Baahubali?" In the present day, Shiva avenges his father and frees his mother Devasena.

Reception

The film became the highest-grossing Indian film of all time on release, earning over ₹1,800 crore (about US$250 million) worldwide. It was praised for its scale, visual effects, and performances, and won several National Film Awards, including Best Popular Film.
