The Reformed Political Party (Dutch: Staatkundig Gereformeerde Partij, SGP) is a conservative Calvinist political party in the Netherlands. Founded in 1918, it is the oldest political party in the country still in existence and is known for advocating governance according to biblical principles.

History

The SGP was founded by orthodox Reformed Protestants who opposed women's suffrage and the direction of the existing Anti-Revolutionary Party. It has held seats in the House of Representatives almost continuously since 1922, typically winning two or three seats with a stable base of support in the Dutch Bible Belt.

Ideology and policies

The party supports theocracy in principle, opposes abortion, euthanasia, and same-sex marriage, and defends traditional observance of Sunday rest. Historically it resisted women's participation in politics; following court rulings, it formally opened party membership and candidacy to women in the 2010s.
