2024 aluminium alloy

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

2024 aluminium alloy is widely used in aircraft structures.

2024 aluminium alloy is an aluminium alloy, with copper as the primary alloying element. It is used in applications requiring a high strength-to-weight ratio and good fatigue resistance. It is weldable only through friction welding, and has average machinability. Due to poor corrosion resistance, it is often clad with aluminium or Al-1Zn for protection, although this may reduce fatigue strength. In older systems of nomenclature, 2xxx series alloys were known as duralumin, and 2024 is sometimes still called by that term.

Properties

2024 aluminium alloy has a density of 2.78 g/cm³, an electrical resistivity of 5.7 μΩ·m, and a thermal conductivity of 121 W/(m·K). Its composition typically includes 3.8–4.9% copper, 1.2–1.8% magnesium, 0.3–0.9% manganese, with small amounts of silicon, iron, zinc, titanium, and chromium, the remainder being aluminium.

The mechanical properties of 2024 depend greatly on the temper of the material. In the T3 temper (solution heat treated, cold worked, and naturally aged), it has an ultimate tensile strength of about 470 MPa and a yield strength of about 325 MPa, with an elongation at break of roughly 18%. Its fatigue strength is notably good for an aluminium alloy, which accounts for its popularity in cyclic-loading applications.

Tempers

2024 is produced in a number of tempers, including:

  • 2024-0 — annealed, with low strength but good formability.
  • 2024-T3 — solution heat treated, cold worked, and naturally aged; the most common aircraft temper.
  • 2024-T4 — solution heat treated and naturally aged.
  • 2024-T6 — solution heat treated and artificially aged.
  • 2024-T351 — stress-relieved by stretching, widely used for plate.
  • 2024-T851 — used for extrusions and forgings requiring high strength.

Uses

Due to its high strength and good fatigue resistance, 2024 is widely used in aircraft structures, especially wing and fuselage structures under tension. It is also used in military vehicles, hydraulic manifolds, computer chassis, and high-quality bicycle frames in some applications. It is commonly supplied in sheet, plate, and extrusion forms; sheet material is frequently "Alclad", coated with a thin layer of high-purity aluminium for corrosion protection.

Corrosion

The copper content that gives 2024 its strength also makes it susceptible to intergranular corrosion. For this reason, sheet products are commonly clad, and parts are usually anodised or painted in service. It should not be used unprotected in marine environments.
