Sky News

Sky News broadcasts rolling news from studios in London

Sky News is a British free-to-air television news channel and organisation. Launched on 5 February 1989, it was the first 24-hour television news channel in the United Kingdom and a pioneer of rolling news coverage in Europe. It is owned by Sky Group, a division of Comcast.

History

Sky News began as part of the four-channel Sky Television service launched by Brian Haynes and backed by Rupert Murdoch's News International. It gained prominence for live, unscripted coverage of breaking events, including the fall of the Berlin Wall and the Gulf War. The channel has won numerous Royal Television Society and BAFTA awards for its journalism.

Services

In addition to the UK channel, Sky News operates international versions and sister services including Sky News Australia (now under separate ownership) and Sky News Arabia. Its content is distributed widely online, through radio bulletins supplied to commercial stations, and via streaming platforms. Sky News is regulated by Ofcom and is known for a style generally more fast-paced than the BBC News channel, its main domestic rival.
