Asterix

Asterix (French: Astérix) is a French comic album series about a Gaulish village in 50 BCE which, thanks to a magic potion that gives superhuman strength, resists Roman occupation. The series was created by writer René Goscinny and illustrator Albert Uderzo, first appearing in the French magazine Pilote on 29 October 1959. It has become one of the most popular Franco-Belgian comics in the world, translated into more than 100 languages and dialects.

Premise

Set in 50 BCE, a year after the Roman conquest of Gaul, the stories centre on a small coastal village in Armorica (Brittany) whose inhabitants refuse to submit to Julius Caesar. Their druid, Getafix, brews a magic potion that grants the drinker temporary superhuman strength. The small, cunning warrior Asterix and his large, kind-hearted friend Obelix — who fell into the potion as a baby and is permanently strong — undertake adventures across the ancient world, foiling Roman schemes and foreign tyrants alike.

Characters

Principal characters include Chief Vitalstatistix, the proud village leader; Cacofonix, the tone-deaf bard; Geriatrix, the aged villager; and Dogmatix, Obelix's tiny dog. Recurring adversaries include Julius Caesar himself, Roman camp commanders around the village, and pirates whose ship is invariably sunk.

Publication history

Goscinny and Uderzo produced 24 albums together before Goscinny's death in 1977; Uderzo continued alone as writer and artist for ten more. Since 2013 the series has been written by Jean-Yves Ferri with art by Didier Conrad, and from 2023 by Fabcaro. By the 2020s more than 400 million copies had been sold worldwide.

Adaptations

Asterix has been adapted into numerous animated and live-action films, including Asterix & Obelix: Mission Cleopatra (2002), video games, and the Parc Astérix theme park near Paris, opened in 1989.

Legacy

The series is renowned for wordplay, puns on classical and modern themes, and gentle satire of national stereotypes. Asterix became a symbol of French cultural identity — France's first satellite, launched in 1965, was named Astérix.
