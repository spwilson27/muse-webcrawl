Mount Kirs

Elevation2,725 m (8,940 ft)
LocationShusha District, Azerbaijan
RangeKarabakh Range, Lesser Caucasus

Mount Kirs (Great Kirs; Azerbaijani: Böyük Kirs dağı) is a peak in the Karabakh Range of the Lesser Caucasus, in the Shusha District of Azerbaijan. At 2,725 metres it is among the highest summits of the range, and its long ridge dominates the skyline south of the city of Shusha, the historic cultural centre of Karabakh.

Geography

The mountain forms part of the watershed between the Kura and Aras river systems. Its high pastures have traditionally been used as summer grazing by communities of the surrounding valleys, and springs on its slopes feed the streams that supply Shusha — the city's famous Isa spring lies in this upland area. In winter the summit ridge is snow-covered for months.

Cultural associations

Kirs is a recurring image in the poetry and music of Shusha, which produced many of Azerbaijan's best-known composers and singers. The annual Khari Bulbul music festival, named for a flower of the Shusha uplands, is closely associated with the landscape around the city and its backdrop of the Kirs ridge. The region's control was contested during the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict; Azerbaijan restored control over Shusha and its surroundings in November 2020.
