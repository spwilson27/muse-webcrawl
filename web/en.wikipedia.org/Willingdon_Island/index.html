Willingdon Island is one of the largest artificial islands in India, located in Kochi, Kerala. It was created in the 1930s from soil dredged while deepening the backwaters to build the modern port of Kochi, under the direction of harbour engineer Robert Bristow.

History and features

The island is named after Lord Willingdon, then Governor of Madras Presidency. It hosts the Port of Kochi's administrative offices, a naval base — home to the Indian Navy's Southern Naval Command facilities at INS Venduruthy — the Cochin Port Trust, customs and maritime offices, hotels, and the old railway station (Ernakulam Terminus). For decades it was also the site of Kochi's airport before civil operations moved to Nedumbassery (Cochin International Airport).

The island is connected to the mainland by the Mattancherry Bridge and the Venduruthy Bridge. Following the commissioning of the International Container Transshipment Terminal at Vallarpadam, much container traffic moved off the island, and redevelopment of its land for tourism and business use has been periodically proposed.
