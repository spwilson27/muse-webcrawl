Lech Poznań is a Polish professional football club based in Poznań, Greater Poland Voivodeship. Founded in 1922 in the district of Dębiec, the club takes its name from Lech, the legendary founder of the Polish nation. Its nickname, Kolejorz ("the Railwayman"), reflects its long historical association with the Polish State Railways (PKP), which supported the club for much of the twentieth century.

Honours

Lech has won the Polish championship (Ekstraklasa) multiple times, with title-winning seasons including 1983, 1984, 1992, 1993, 2010, 2015 and 2022, and has lifted the Polish Cup several times. The club is known for its strong academy, which has produced numerous Polish internationals.

Supporters and stadium

The club plays at Stadion Poznań, built for UEFA Euro 2012, and draws some of the largest crowds in Polish football. Lech supporters are famous for the "Poznań celebration" — turning their backs to the pitch, linking arms and bouncing in unison — which was adopted by fans of Manchester City and others abroad. Its fiercest rivalry is with Legia Warsaw.

In Europe

Lech has enjoyed notable European campaigns, including reaching the knockout stages of the UEFA Cup/Europa League and a run to the quarter-finals of the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2022–23.
