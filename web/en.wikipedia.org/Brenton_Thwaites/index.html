Brenton Thwaites (born 10 August 1989) is an Australian actor. He came to prominence for his portrayal of Luke Gallagher in the Fox8 teen drama SLiDE (2011) and later played Stu Henderson in the soap opera Home and Away. Beginning a film career in the United States, he starred as Jonas in The Giver (2014) and Henry Turner in Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales (2017).

Early life

Thwaites was born in Cairns, Queensland, the son of Fiona and Peter Thwaites. He studied acting at the Queensland University of Technology, graduating in 2010.

Career

Thwaites's early Australian work included the independent feature Charge Over You (2010), the teen drama SLiDE, and a recurring role in Home and Away. His American breakthrough came with roles in Oculus (2013), The Signal (2014) and the dystopian adaptation The Giver (2014), alongside Jeff Bridges and Meryl Streep. He played the Egyptian god-slayer Bek in Gods of Egypt (2016), then joined the Pirates of the Caribbean franchise as Henry Turner, son of Will Turner and Elizabeth Swann.

Titans

From 2018 to 2023 Thwaites starred as Dick Grayson / Robin, later Nightwing, in the DC Universe/Max superhero series Titans, leading the ensemble across four seasons.

Personal life

Thwaites has been in a relationship with Chloe Pacey since 2015; they have children together and live in Australia.
