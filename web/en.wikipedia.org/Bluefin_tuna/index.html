Bluefin tuna

Bluefin tuna is a common name for three species of large tuna in the genus Thunnus: the Atlantic bluefin (T. thynnus), the Pacific bluefin (T. orientalis) and the southern bluefin (T. maccoyii). Bluefins are among the largest bony fish, with Atlantic specimens exceeding 600 kg, and are capable of sustained high speeds and regional endothermy.

Fisheries

Bluefin tuna are highly prized in the sushi and sashimi markets, especially in Japan, where single fish have sold for record prices at Tokyo's New Year auctions. Heavy fishing pressure led to severe stock declines in the late 20th and early 21st centuries.

Conservation

Management by regional fisheries bodies and catch quotas has allowed some stocks, particularly the Atlantic population, to show signs of recovery, though the species remain conservation concerns.
