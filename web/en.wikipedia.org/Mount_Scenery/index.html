Mount Scenery

Mount Scenery is a potentially active volcano on the Caribbean island of Saba, a special municipality of the Netherlands. Its summit, 887 metres (2,910 ft) above sea level, is the highest point of both Saba and the entire Kingdom of the Netherlands.

The mountain dominates the island and is crowned by an elfin cloud forest within Mount Scenery National Park.

Geology

Mount Scenery is an andesitic stratovolcano whose summit is a lava dome. The most recent eruption occurred around 1640, producing pyroclastic flows on the northern side of the island. The volcano is monitored and considered potentially active.

Hiking

The Mount Scenery Trail climbs from the village of Windwardside to the summit via more than a thousand concrete steps, passing through rainforest and mahogany plantings into cloud forest at the top. On clear days the neighbouring islands of Sint Eustatius, St. Kitts, Saint Martin and St. Barths can be seen. A communications tower stands near the summit.
