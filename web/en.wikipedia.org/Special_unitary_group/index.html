Special unitary group

FieldMathematics, Lie theory
NotationSU(n)
DefinitionUnitary matrices of determinant 1
Physics roleGauge groups of the Standard Model

In mathematics, the special unitary group SU(n) is the Lie group of n×n unitary matrices with determinant 1. It is a compact, connected Lie group whose Lie algebra consists of traceless skew-Hermitian matrices.

Properties

SU(1) is trivial, SU(2) is diffeomorphic to the 3-sphere and double-covers the rotation group SO(3), and SU(3) is an eight-dimensional simply connected group. The groups SU(n) are simply connected for all n, making them the universal covers of their projective counterparts.

Physics

Special unitary groups are the gauge symmetry groups of the Standard Model of particle physics: SU(3) for quantum chromodynamics, SU(2) for the weak interaction, while SU(5) and SU(10) appear in grand unified theories.
