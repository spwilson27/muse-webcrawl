Pomorie is a town and seaside resort in southeastern Bulgaria, situated on a narrow rocky peninsula in Burgas Bay on the southern Bulgarian Black Sea Coast. It lies within Burgas Province, about 20 km from the city of Burgas.

History

Pomorie was founded by the Ancient Greeks under the name Anchialos, a colony of Apollonia (present-day Sozopol). It later passed through Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman hands, and was the site of the Battle of Achelous in 917, a major Bulgarian victory over the Byzantine Empire nearby. The modern name Pomorie, from Greek po- and more (sea), was adopted in 1934.

Economy

The town is known for salt production in its lagoon pans, wine making, and tourism. Lake Pomorie is a protected area noted for its therapeutic mud and birdlife along the Via Pontica migration route.
