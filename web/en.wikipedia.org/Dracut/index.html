Dracut, Massachusetts

Dracut is a town in Middlesex County, Massachusetts, United States, in the Greater Lowell area of the Merrimack Valley. The population was about 32,600 at the 2020 census. Dracut is primarily a suburban and agricultural community, bordered by Lowell to the south across the Merrimack River, and by Pelham and Hudson, New Hampshire, to the north. It is one of the largest towns in Massachusetts by population to still use an open town meeting form of government.

History

Before European settlement the area was inhabited by the Pennacook people, whose central village was at Pawtucket Falls on the Merrimack. English colonists from Chelmsford acquired the land in the later 17th century; Dracut was incorporated as a town on February 26, 1701, from land north of the Merrimack River. Part of Dracut was annexed by Lowell in the 19th century as that mill city grew. Farming dominated the town's economy for centuries, with dairy and market gardens supplying Lowell and Boston.

Geography and villages

Dracut covers about 21 square miles. Its historic villages include Dracut Center, East Dracut, Collinsville, the Navy Yard district along the river, and Kenwood near Lake Mascuppic. Beaver Brook flows through the town to the Merrimack.

Government and education

The town is governed by an open town meeting, a five-member Board of Selectmen and a town manager. Dracut High School (home of the Middies) serves local students, and the town is also part of the Greater Lowell Technical High School district.
