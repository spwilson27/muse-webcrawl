Mecha

In science fiction, mecha are large piloted robots or machines, typically bipedal and humanoid, originating in Japanese manga and anime. The term derives from the English word "mechanical".

History

Early examples include the remote-controlled Tetsujin 28-go (Gigantor, 1956) and Mazinger Z (1972), whose hero piloted the robot from inside. Mobile Suit Gundam (1979) launched the "real robot" subgenre, treating mecha as military hardware, while "super robot" series emphasised heroic, fantastic machines.

Influence

Mecha became central to franchises such as Macross, Evangelion, and Code Geass, and influenced Western works including Pacific Rim and BattleTech. Japan has also built real moving life-size Gundam statues.
