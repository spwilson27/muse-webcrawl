Dhaka

Dhaka
Country: Bangladesh
Status: Capital city
Population: over 10 million (city); over 23 million (metropolitan area)
Nickname: City of Mosques; Rickshaw Capital of the World

Dhaka is the capital and largest city of Bangladesh. Located on the eastern banks of the Buriganga River in the Ganges Delta, it is the country's political, economic and cultural centre and one of the largest and fastest-growing megacities in the world, with a metropolitan population exceeding 23 million.

History

Dhaka rose to prominence as a Mughal provincial capital in the early 17th century, when it became renowned for its muslin trade; its name may derive from the dhak tree or the goddess Dhakeshwari, whose temple stands in the old city. Under British rule it was eclipsed by Calcutta, though it briefly served as capital of the province of Eastern Bengal and Assam (1905–1912). It became the capital of East Pakistan in 1947 and of independent Bangladesh after the Liberation War of 1971.

Geography and climate

The city occupies a flat, low-lying floodplain threaded by rivers and canals, and is highly vulnerable to monsoon flooding despite extensive embankments. Dhaka has a tropical monsoon climate with a hot, wet rainy season from June to October.

Economy and transport

Dhaka is the hub of Bangladesh's garment industry, the world's second-largest exporter of ready-made clothing, alongside pharmaceuticals, jute and services. The city is famous for its estimated hundreds of thousands of cycle rickshaws, and suffers some of the world's worst traffic congestion; its first metro line, MRT Line 6, opened in December 2022. Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport is the country's main gateway.

Culture

Landmarks include Lalbagh Fort, the pink Ahsan Manzil palace, the National Parliament House (Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban) designed by Louis Kahn, and the Liberation War Museum. Dhaka University, founded in 1921, is the country's oldest university. The city is often called the City of Mosques for its hundreds of mosques, including the Baitul Mukarram national mosque.
