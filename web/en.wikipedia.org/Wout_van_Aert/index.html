Wout van Aert (born 15 September 1994) is a Belgian professional cyclist who rides for UCI WorldTeam Visma–Lease a Bike. He is a three-time cyclo-cross world champion (2016, 2017, 2018) and one of the most versatile road riders of his generation.

Van Aert won the points classification at the 2022 Tour de France.

Career

Van Aert emerged from cyclo-cross, where his rivalry with Mathieu van der Poel defined the sport for a decade. On the road he has won Milan–San Remo (2020), Strade Bianche (2020), Gent–Wevelgem, Omloop Het Nieuwsblad, the E3 Saxo Bank Classic, and multiple stages of the Tour de France, including victories on sprint, mountain, and time-trial stages. At the 2022 Tour he won the green points jersey.

Major results

He won silver medals in the road race at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics and at the World Championships, and has stood on the podium of both the Tour of Flanders and Paris–Roubaix. His 2024 season was interrupted by crashes, including a heavy fall at Dwars door Vlaanderen.
