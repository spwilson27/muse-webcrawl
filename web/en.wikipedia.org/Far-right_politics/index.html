Far-right politics

Far-right politics is a term used to describe political positions that lie further on the right of the left–right spectrum than the standard political right. Far-right politics typically involve strong emphasis on nationalism, traditional social hierarchies, and opposition to immigration and multiculturalism, though definitions vary.

Historically, far-right movements have included fascism and Nazism, which came to power in Italy and Germany in the interwar period. Contemporary far-right parties and movements in various countries emphasize national sovereignty, law and order, and cultural identity.

Characteristics

Scholars commonly cite ultranationalism, authoritarianism, nativism, and populism as recurring features. The term is contested and is used differently across political contexts; some groups reject the label.
