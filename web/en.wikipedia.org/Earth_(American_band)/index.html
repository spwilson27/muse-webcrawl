Earth is an American rock band based in Seattle, Washington, formed in Olympia in 1989 and led by guitarist Dylan Carlson. The band pioneered drone metal, a style built from extremely slow, repetitive, heavily distorted guitar tones, most famously on the 1993 album Earth 2: Special Low Frequency Version, widely cited as the first drone metal record.

History

Carlson formed Earth in Olympia in 1989; an early lineup included Slim Moon and Greg Babior, and Kurt Cobain — Carlson's close friend — sang on the early demo recording. Earth signed to Sub Pop and released the EP Extra-Capsular Extraction (1991) and the debut album Earth 2 (1993), whose glacial, minimalist drones influenced later acts such as Sunn O))) and Boris. Pentastar: In the Style of Demons (1996) moved toward a more conventional heavy rock sound.

Later work

After a hiatus marked by Carlson's personal difficulties, Earth returned with a reinvented sound on Hex; Or Printing in the Infernal Method (2005), drawing on country and western and the work of Ennio Morricone. Subsequent albums, including The Bees Made Honey in the Lion's Skull (2008), the two-part Angels of Darkness, Demons of Light (2011–2012), Primitive and Deadly (2014) and Full Upon Her Burning Lips (2019), feature drummer Adrienne Davies as the band's other core member and explore a sparse, meditative Americana-inflected heaviness.

Style and influence

Earth's music emphasizes sustain, repetition and tone over rhythm and riff, treating the electric guitar as a source of slowly evolving texture. The band's early work is foundational to drone metal and drone music generally, while its later records bridge metal, folk and minimalism.
