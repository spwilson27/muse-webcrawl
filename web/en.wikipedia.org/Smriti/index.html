Smriti

Smriti (Sanskrit: "that which is remembered") is a body of Hindu texts usually attributed to an author, traditionally written down but constantly revised, in contrast to the Śruti ("that which is heard"), which are considered authorless revelations. Smriti literature includes the epics, Puranas, Dharmashastras and other texts.

Contents

The category includes the Itihasas — the Mahabharata and the Ramayana — the eighteen major Puranas, the Dharmaśāstra law books such as the Manusmriti (Laws of Manu), and various sutras and commentarial traditions. Smriti texts are considered secondary in authority to Śruti where the two conflict, though in practice they have shaped much of lived Hindu law and custom.

Role

Smriti functions as the remembered tradition that interprets and applies revelation to social and ritual life, covering dharma (duty, law), festivals, rites of passage and ethics.
