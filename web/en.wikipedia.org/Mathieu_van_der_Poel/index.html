Mathieu van der Poel

Mathieu van der Poel
Born: 19 January 1995, Kapellen, Belgium
Nationality: Dutch
Team: Alpecin–Deceuninck
Disciplines: Road, cyclo-cross, mountain biking
Major wins: World Road Race Champion (2023); multiple Tour of Flanders and Paris–Roubaix titles; Milan–San Remo (2023)

Mathieu van der Poel (born 19 January 1995) is a Dutch professional cyclist who races for UCI WorldTeam Alpecin–Deceuninck. Considered one of the most versatile and explosive riders of his generation, he has won world championships in three disciplines — cyclo-cross, road racing and gravel — and is a multiple winner of cycling's Monuments, including the Tour of Flanders, Paris–Roubaix and Milan–San Remo. He is the son of former professional Adrie van der Poel and the grandson of French cycling legend Raymond Poulidor.

Career

Van der Poel first dominated cyclo-cross, winning multiple elite world titles and building a celebrated rivalry with Wout van Aert. On the road he announced himself with a stunning comeback victory at the 2019 Amstel Gold Race. He won the Tour of Flanders in 2020, 2022 and 2024, Paris–Roubaix in 2023 and 2024, and Milan–San Remo in 2023. In August 2023 he won the UCI World Road Race Championships in Glasgow with a solo attack, surviving a late crash to take the rainbow jersey. At the 2021 Tour de France he won a stage and wore the yellow jersey for six days, dedicating the ride to his late grandfather. His long-range attacks and technical skill — honed in cyclo-cross and mountain biking, where he also raced at the Tokyo Olympics — have made him one of the sport's biggest stars.

Riding style

Van der Poel is noted for immense short-duration power, bike handling on cobbles and descents, and an instinctive attacking style. His duels with Wout van Aert and Tadej Pogačar at the spring classics have defined the era's one-day racing.
