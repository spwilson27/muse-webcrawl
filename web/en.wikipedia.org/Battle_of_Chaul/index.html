Battle of Chaul

The Battle of Chaul was a naval battle fought in March 1508 in the harbour of Chaul, on the western coast of India, between the Portuguese fleet of Lourenço de Almeida and a combined Mamluk Egyptian and Gujarati fleet.

Significance

The Portuguese squadron, caught at anchor and outnumbered, was defeated and Lourenço de Almeida, son of the Viceroy Francisco de Almeida, was killed. It was the first Portuguese naval defeat in the Indian Ocean. Francisco de Almeida avenged his son the following year at the Battle of Diu (1509), destroying the combined fleet and securing Portuguese dominance of Indian Ocean trade routes.
