Eugene Borisovich Dynkin (11 May 1924 – 14 November 2014) was a Soviet and American mathematician who made major contributions to probability theory and algebra. He studied at Moscow State University under Andrei Kolmogorov and later taught at Cornell University after emigrating to the United States in 1977.

Work

As a young researcher Dynkin introduced the diagrams now named after him to organise the classification of semisimple Lie algebras, simplifying the exposition of root systems. In probability, he laid foundational work on Markov processes — including Dynkin's formula and the characteristic operator — and the Dynkin system (λ-system) theorem used in measure theory. His later research dealt with super-Brownian motion and the interplay of probability with partial differential equations.

He received the Leroy P. Steele Prize for Lifetime Achievement in 1993 and was a member of the United States National Academy of Sciences.
