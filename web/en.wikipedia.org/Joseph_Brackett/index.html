Joseph Brackett

Joseph Brackett Jr.
Born: 6 May 1797, Cumberland, Maine
Died: 4 July 1882, Canterbury, New Hampshire
Known for: "Simple Gifts"

Joseph Brackett Jr. (6 May 1797 – 4 July 1882) was an American songwriter, author and elder of the United Society of Believers in Christ's Second Appearing, better known as the Shakers. He is best known for composing the Shaker dancing song "Simple Gifts".

Life

Brackett joined the Shaker community at Gorham, Maine, helped found the community at Poland Hill, and later became an elder and head of the society at Canterbury, New Hampshire. He wrote several books of Shaker theology and hymns.

Simple Gifts

Composed in 1848 while Brackett was at Alfred, Maine, "Simple Gifts" ('Tis the gift to be simple...) remained little known outside Shaker communities until Aaron Copland used its melody in Appalachian Spring (1944). The tune was later adapted as "Lord of the Dance".
