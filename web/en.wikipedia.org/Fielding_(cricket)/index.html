Fielding (cricket)

Fielding in the sport of cricket is the action of fielders in collecting the ball after it is struck by the striker, to limit the number of runs scored or to get a batter out.

Basics

The fielding side has eleven players on the field. The bowler and wicket-keeper have specialised roles; the remaining fielders are placed by the captain in named positions around the ground — slip, gully, point, cover, mid-off, mid-on, square leg, fine leg, third man and others — balancing defensive and attacking aims. Fielding involves stopping and returning the ball, catching to dismiss batters, and effecting run outs by hitting the stumps.

Restrictions and standards

Limited-overs cricket imposes fielding restrictions (the powerplay) limiting how many fielders may stand outside the inner circle. Fielding standards and athleticism have risen markedly in the modern game, and fielding is regarded as a specialist skill requiring dedicated coaching.
