1970 Huascarán earthquake (Ancash earthquake)

Date31 May 1970
Magnitude7.9 Mw
EpicentreOff the coast of Ancash, Peru
DeathsAbout 70,000

The 1970 Ancash earthquake occurred on 31 May 1970 off the coast of Peru in the Pacific Ocean. Together with the massive landslides it triggered from Mount Huascarán, it is the most catastrophic natural disaster in Peruvian history.

Huascarán debris avalanche

The shaking dislodged an enormous mass of ice and rock from Huascarán Norte, the highest peak in Peru. The debris avalanche travelled at speeds of up to 280 km/h and buried the towns of Yungay and Ranrahirca, killing most of their inhabitants. The survivors of Yungay were those who had climbed to the cemetery hill above the town.

Toll

Around 70,000 people died, some 50,000 were injured, and hundreds of thousands were left homeless. The city of Huaraz was largely destroyed.
