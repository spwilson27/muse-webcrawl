Outlaw

An outlaw is a person declared outside the protection of the law. In pre-modern legal systems, outlawry removed a person's legal rights, allowing anyone to pursue or kill them with impunity. The term comes from Old Norse útlagi.

History

Outlawry was used in medieval England, Iceland and Germany for those who fled justice. Famous legendary outlaws include Robin Hood. In the American Old West, figures such as Jesse James and Billy the Kid were romanticised as outlaws.

Modern usage

Formal outlawry has been abolished in most legal systems, and the word now generally denotes a habitual criminal or fugitive, and figuratively someone who defies convention.
