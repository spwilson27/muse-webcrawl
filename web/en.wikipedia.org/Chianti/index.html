Chianti

Chianti is an Italian red wine produced in the Chianti region of central Tuscany, made principally from the Sangiovese grape. Chianti Classico comes from the historic core zone between Florence and Siena.

Once sold in straw-covered fiasco bottles, Chianti is now a quality-controlled DOCG wine, typically dry and medium-bodied with cherry fruit and food-friendly acidity, suited to Tuscan cuisine.
