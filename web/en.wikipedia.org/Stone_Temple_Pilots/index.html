Stone Temple Pilots

OriginSan Diego, California, United States
GenresAlternative rock, grunge, hard rock
Years active1989-2003, 2008-present

Stone Temple Pilots is an American rock band formed in San Diego, California, in 1989. Originally consisting of vocalist Scott Weiland, guitarist Dean DeLeo, bassist Robert DeLeo and drummer Eric Kretz, the band became one of the most commercially successful acts of the 1990s alternative rock and grunge era.

Career

Their debut album Core (1992) produced the hits "Plush", "Creep" and "Sex Type Thing", and won a Grammy Award for "Plush". Purple (1994) debuted at number one and included "Interstate Love Song" and "Vasoline". Weiland's struggles with addiction led to hiatuses and the band's 2003 breakup. They reunited in 2008; after Weiland's dismissal in 2013 he was replaced briefly by Chester Bennington, and following Weiland's death in 2015, Jeff Gutt became lead vocalist in 2017.
