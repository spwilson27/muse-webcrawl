Panathenaic Stadium

The Panathenaic Stadium, also known as Kallimarmaro, is a multi-purpose stadium in Athens, Greece. Built entirely of marble, it stands on the site of the ancient stadium that hosted the Panathenaic Games.

History

The original stadium was built around 330 BC and rebuilt in marble by Herodes Atticus in AD 144. Excavated in the 19th century, it was refurbished to host the opening and closing ceremonies of the first modern Olympic Games in 1896.

Modern use

The stadium hosted archery and the marathon finish at the 2004 Athens Olympics and remains the finishing point of the annual Athens Classic Marathon.
