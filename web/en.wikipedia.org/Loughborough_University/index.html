Loughborough University

Loughborough University is a public research university in Loughborough, Leicestershire, England. It has been a university since 1966, but its history goes back to 1909, when Loughborough Technical Institute began offering courses in skills and knowledge for local industries. It is organised into ten academic schools and is especially renowned for sport-related subjects and sports science.

History

The institution grew from the technical institute through Loughborough College and its Division of Technology, which became a College of Advanced Technology. In 1966 it received its Royal Charter as Loughborough University of Technology, later dropping "of Technology" in 1996. In 2015 the university opened a postgraduate campus on the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park in London, known as Loughborough University London.

Campus and sport

The university has one of the largest single-site campuses in the United Kingdom, of about 523 acres (212 ha). Loughborough is famous for sport: it has been ranked the world's best university for sports-related subjects in the QS rankings for many consecutive years, hosts national performance centres for sports including swimming, athletics and triathlon, and its students dominate the British Universities and Colleges Sport (BUCS) championships.

Reputation

Loughborough consistently places in the top ten of British university league tables and has won the Times Higher Education "Student Experience" honours multiple times. Its alumni include Olympic athletes, engineers, designers, and business leaders.
