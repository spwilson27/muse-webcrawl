Google Pixel

Manufacturer: Google

Type: Smartphones, tablets, earbuds, smartwatches

Release date: October 20, 2016

Operating system: Android

The Google Pixel is a brand of consumer electronic devices developed by Google that run either ChromeOS or the Android operating system. The main line of Pixel products consists of Android-powered smartphones, which have been produced since October 2016 as the replacement of the older Nexus line of products.

History

The first Pixel phone, the Pixel and Pixel XL, was announced on October 4, 2016, and released on October 20, 2016. It was praised for its camera, which used computational photography techniques such as HDR+. Subsequent generations added features such as Night Sight, the Titan security chip, and Google's custom Tensor system-on-a-chip, first used in the Pixel 6 in 2021.

Products

The Pixel line includes flagship phones (such as the Pixel 8 and Pixel 9 series), mid-range "a" series phones, the foldable Pixel Fold and Pixel 9 Pro Fold, the Pixel Tablet, Pixel Buds wireless earbuds, and the Pixel Watch smartwatch line.

Reception

Pixel phones have been noted for their camera quality and software features, including AI-powered tools such as Magic Eraser and Call Screen, though the line holds a relatively small share of the global smartphone market compared with Samsung and Apple devices.
