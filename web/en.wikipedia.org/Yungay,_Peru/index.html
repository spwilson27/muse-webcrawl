Yungay is a town in the Ancash Region of Peru, capital of Yungay Province, located in the Callejón de Huaylas valley beneath the Cordillera Blanca. The modern town stands near the site of the original city destroyed in 1970.

1970 disaster

On 31 May 1970, the Ancash earthquake (magnitude 7.9) triggered a massive landslide from the north peak of Huascarán. A wall of ice, rock, and mud travelled at enormous speed and buried the city of Yungay and neighbouring villages within minutes, killing an estimated 20,000 people in one of the deadliest natural disasters in Peruvian history.

Today

The buried city is preserved as a memorial and cemetery, marked by a hilltop cemetery hill (Cerro Huansakay) where some residents survived, a white Christ statue, and the wreckage of a bus and cathedral towers. A new Yungay was rebuilt nearby and serves as a gateway to the Llanganuco lakes and Huascarán National Park.
