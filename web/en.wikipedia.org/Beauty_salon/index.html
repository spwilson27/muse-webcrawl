Beauty salon

A beauty salon or beauty parlor is an establishment that provides cosmetic treatments for people. Other variations of this type of business include hair salons, spas and medical spas. Services typically include hair cutting, colouring and styling, manicures and pedicures, facials, waxing, massage and other skin and body treatments.

History

Beauty treatments have ancient roots, but the modern salon emerged in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, when entrepreneurs such as Martha Matilda Harper opened salon chains and pioneered franchising, and figures like Helena Rubinstein and Elizabeth Arden built beauty empires. Innovations such as the permanent wave and hair dyes expanded the range of services, and salons became important social spaces, particularly for women. In many communities, barbershops and salons also function as centres of local life and conversation.

Services and industry

Practitioners — including cosmetologists, hairdressers, estheticians and nail technicians — are licensed in many jurisdictions after formal training. The industry is a major employer worldwide, dominated by small businesses and self-employed stylists who may rent chairs within a salon. Nail salons, day spas and specialised brow and lash studios are common variants. In the United States, the beauty services sector generates tens of billions of dollars in annual revenue.
