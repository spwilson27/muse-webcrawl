Citizen Watch

IndustryElectronics, watches
Founded1918 (as Shokosha Watch Research Institute), Tokyo, Japan
HeadquartersNishitokyo, Tokyo, Japan

Citizen Watch Co., Ltd. is a Japanese electronics company, best known as one of the world's largest manufacturers of wristwatches. Citizen is the core company of the Citizen Group, which also produces electronic components, calculators and machine tools, and owns brands including Bulova, Alpina and Frederique Constant.

History

The company traces its origins to the Shokosha Watch Research Institute, founded in Tokyo in 1918; its first pocket watch, produced in 1924, was named "Citizen" by the mayor of Tokyo in the hope that watches would become affordable to ordinary citizens. Citizen was among the pioneers of quartz watches and in 1976 introduced the first watch with a solar cell. Its Eco-Drive line, launched in the 1990s, is powered by light and seldom needs a battery change. The company also makes the Miyota line of watch movements used by many other brands.
