Piste

A piste is a marked ski run or trail down a mountain, prepared for skiing and snowboarding. The word comes from French, meaning a track or trail. Pistes are groomed by snowcats that produce the ridged "corduroy" surface, and are classified by difficulty.

Difficulty ratings

In Europe, runs are colour-coded: blue for easy, red for intermediate and black for difficult. In North America, green circles, blue squares and black diamonds are used instead. Off-piste skiing takes place on unmarked, unpatrolled terrain, with greater avalanche and navigation risks.
