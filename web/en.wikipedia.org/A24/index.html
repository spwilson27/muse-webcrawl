A24

A24 is an American independent entertainment company based in New York City, founded in 2012 by Daniel Katz, David Fenkel, and John Hodges. It specialises in film and television production and distribution, and is known for critically acclaimed, director-driven films.

History

The company took its name from the Italian A24 highway its founders drove on while deciding to start the business. Early successes included Spring Breakers, Ex Machina, and Room. In 2017 Moonlight won the Academy Award for Best Picture, and in 2023 Everything Everywhere All at Once swept the Oscars, winning seven awards including Best Picture — making A24 the first studio to win all six major categories in a single night.

Notable films

  • Ex Machina (2014)
  • Lady Bird (2017)
  • Hereditary (2018)
  • Uncut Gems (2019)
  • Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022)
  • The Whale (2022)

A24 also produces television, including the series Euphoria and Beef, and has become a distinctive brand with a devoted following among younger audiences.
