Blizzard Entertainment

Founded: 1991 (as Silicon & Synapse)
Headquarters: Irvine, California, US
Industry: Video games
Parent: Activision Blizzard (Microsoft)

Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. is an American video game developer and publisher based in Irvine, California. Founded in 1991 as Silicon & Synapse by Michael Morhaime, Frank Pearce, and Allen Adham, the company adopted the Blizzard name in 1994 and became known for highly polished, genre-defining titles.

History

After early work porting games for other studios, Blizzard developed the real-time strategy series Warcraft (1994), which together with StarCraft (1998) made it a dominant force in PC gaming. The action role-playing game Diablo (1997) and its sequels established another long-running franchise. In 2004 Blizzard released World of Warcraft, a massively multiplayer online role-playing game that became the most commercially successful title of its kind, peaking at over twelve million subscribers.

Later releases include the digital card game Hearthstone (2014) and the hero shooter Overwatch (2016). In 2008 Blizzard's parent combined with Activision to form Activision Blizzard, which was acquired by Microsoft in October 2023.

Games

Major franchises include Warcraft, StarCraft, Diablo, Overwatch, and Hearthstone. Blizzard also operated the Battle.net online service, launched in 1996.
