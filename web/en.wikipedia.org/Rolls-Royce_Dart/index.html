Rolls-Royce Dart

TypeTurboprop aero engine
ManufacturerRolls-Royce
First run1946
Major applicationsVickers Viscount, Fokker F27, Hawker Siddeley HS 748

The Rolls-Royce RB.53 Dart is a British turboprop aircraft engine designed and built by Rolls-Royce. First run in 1946, it became one of the most successful turboprops ever produced, powering the Vickers Viscount — the world's first turboprop airliner — as well as the Fokker F27 Friendship, the Hawker Siddeley HS 748, the Grumman Gulfstream I and the NAMC YS-11.

Design and service

The Dart is a single-shaft engine with a two-stage centrifugal compressor, producing from about 1,500 to over 3,000 equivalent horsepower in later marks. More than 7,000 were built over a production run exceeding 40 years; the last examples were manufactured in the late 1980s, and Darts remained in service decades later. Its distinctive high-pitched whine was a hallmark of early turboprop travel.
