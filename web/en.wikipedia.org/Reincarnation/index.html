Reincarnation

Reincarnation, also known as rebirth or transmigration, is the religious or philosophical belief that the non-physical essence of a living being begins a new life in a different physical form or body after biological death. It is a central tenet of the Indian religions — Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism and Sikhism — and appears in various forms in other traditions, including some pagan religions, Spiritism, and the beliefs of various Indigenous peoples.

Indian religions

In Hindu thought, the eternal soul (ātman) transmigrates through successive lives in the cycle of saṃsāra, its condition determined by karma, the accumulated consequences of actions. Liberation (mokṣa) from the cycle is the ultimate goal. Buddhism teaches rebirth without a permanent soul, through the doctrine of anattā (non-self), in which a stream of consciousness continues from life to life. Jainism holds that each soul is bound by karmic matter and seeks liberation through non-violence and asceticism. The concepts developed in the early Upanishads and were systematised in texts such as the Bhagavad Gita.

Other traditions

Reincarnation-like ideas appeared in ancient Greek philosophy — Pythagoras, Empedocles and Plato all taught forms of metempsychosis, and Plato's Myth of Er describes souls choosing new lives. Orphism and later esoteric traditions carried similar notions. In the 19th and 20th centuries, Theosophy and the Spiritist doctrine codified by Allan Kardec popularised reincarnation in the West. Some early Christian Gnostic groups entertained rebirth doctrines, though mainstream Christianity and Islam generally teach resurrection rather than reincarnation.

Research and criticism

In the 20th century, psychiatrist Ian Stevenson investigated thousands of cases of children who claimed to remember past lives, publishing his findings in works such as Twenty Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation. Critics attribute such reports to cultural expectation, cryptomnesia and selective reporting, and the mainstream scientific consensus is that there is no empirical evidence for reincarnation. Surveys nonetheless indicate that belief in reincarnation is held by a substantial minority of people in Western countries.
