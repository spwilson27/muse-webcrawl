The Blitz
Dates: 7 September 1940 – 11 May 1941
Attacker: Nazi Germany (Luftwaffe)
Target: United Kingdom cities

The Blitz was a German bombing campaign against the United Kingdom during the Second World War, from September 1940 to May 1941. The Luftwaffe attacked London for 56 of 57 consecutive nights and bombed ports and industrial centres including Coventry, Liverpool, Birmingham and Belfast.

Course

Having failed to win air superiority in the Battle of Britain, Germany turned to night bombing of cities to break British morale and war production. More than 40,000 civilians were killed.

Outcome

The campaign failed to cripple British industry or force surrender, and in May 1941 most air units were redeployed east for the invasion of the Soviet Union.
