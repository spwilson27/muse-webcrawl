La Création du monde

La Création du monde, Op. 81, is a ballet in six scenes by French composer Darius Milhaud, premiered in Paris in 1923 by the Ballets suédois.

Creation

The scenario by Blaise Cendrars drew on African creation mythology; designs were by Fernand Léger, with cubist-inspired costumes and sets. Milhaud, who had heard jazz in Harlem in 1922, scored the work for a small orchestra including saxophone, using jazz and blues idioms alongside polytonality.

Music

Lasting about fifteen minutes, the ballet is an early and influential fusion of jazz and classical concert music, opening with a saxophone melody over ostinato rhythms before a fugue-like section and a lyrical finale.
