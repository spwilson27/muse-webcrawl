The Integral Coach Factory (ICF) is an Indian manufacturer of railway coaches and a production unit of Indian Railways, located at Perambur in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Established in 1955 in collaboration with Swiss Car and Elevator Manufacturing Company, it is one of the largest railway coach manufacturers in the world by volume.

Products

ICF originally produced the all-steel, integral-design "ICF coach" that became standard on Indian Railways for decades. It later adopted stainless-steel LHB coach designs. In recent years it has designed and built the Vande Bharat Express trainsets, India's indigenous semi-high-speed trains, as well as coaches for metro systems and export markets.

Scale

The factory employs roughly ten thousand people and has produced well over fifty thousand coaches since its founding, including thousands of self-propelled vehicles such as electric multiple units.
