Drizzt Do'Urden

Drizzt Do'Urden is a fictional character in the Forgotten Realms campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons fantasy role-playing game. A drow (dark elf) ranger who has forsaken the evil society of his people, he is the protagonist of numerous novels by R. A. Salvatore, beginning with The Crystal Shard (1988), and one of the most popular characters in the D&D franchise.

Character

Drizzt was born in Menzoberranzan, the great drow city of the Underdark, as a prince of House Do'Urden. Trained as a fighter and mentored by the weapons master Zaknafein—later revealed as his father—he rejected the cruelty and worship of the spider goddess Lolth that governed drow society and escaped to the surface world. There he became a ranger, befriended the barbarian Wulfgar, the dwarf king Bruenor Battlehammer, the halfling Regis, and the archer Catti-brie, the group known as the Companions of the Hall.

Abilities and equipment

Drizzt is a master of two-weapon fighting, wielding the scimitars Twinkle and Icingdeath, and is accompanied by Guenhwyvar, a magical black panther summoned from the Astral Plane by an onyx figurine. His lavender eyes and moral character set him apart from other drow in the fiction.

Reception

The Drizzt novels have been New York Times bestsellers, and the character has appeared in video games including the Baldur's Gate series and as the star of the action game Dungeons & Dragons: Dark Alliance (2021).
