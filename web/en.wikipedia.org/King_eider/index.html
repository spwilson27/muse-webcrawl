King eider

Scientific nameSomateria spectabilis
FamilyAnatidae
RangeArctic coasts of North America, Europe, and Asia

The king eider (Somateria spectabilis) is a large sea duck that breeds along Arctic coasts of the Northern Hemisphere. The drake is unmistakable, with a powder-blue head, greenish cheeks, a coral-orange frontal shield, and black-and-white body plumage; the female is mottled brown.

Behaviour and habitat

King eiders breed on tundra near the coast and winter at sea in subarctic and northern temperate waters, often in large flocks. They dive for benthic prey such as molluscs, crustaceans, and echinoderms. Vast numbers migrate each spring past Point Barrow, Alaska. Unlike the common eider, the king eider is not a major source of commercial eiderdown, though its feathers are collected locally.
