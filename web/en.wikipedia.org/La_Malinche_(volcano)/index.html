La Malinche (volcano)

La Malinche, also known as Matlalcueitl or Malintzin, is a dormant stratovolcano on the border of the Mexican states of Tlaxcala and Puebla, rising to about 4,462 metres (14,636 ft).

Geography and park

It is the centrepiece of La Malinche National Park, established in 1938, whose pine, fir and oak forests protect watersheds supplying nearby cities. The mountain is a popular, non-technical climb from a trailhead resort, though altitude and weather demand care.

Name

The volcano shares its name with La Malinche, the Nahua interpreter of Hernán Cortés during the Spanish conquest of the Aztec Empire.
