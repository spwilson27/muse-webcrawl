Complication (horology)

In horology, a complication is any feature of a mechanical watch beyond the simple display of hours, minutes, and seconds. Complications add functions and greatly increase the difficulty of design and manufacture.

Types

Common complications include calendars, moon-phase displays, chronographs, alarms, and power-reserve indicators. The most prestigious are the tourbillon, minute repeater, and perpetual calendar.

Grande complication

A watch containing several major complications is called a grande complication. Such timepieces, made by houses such as Patek Philippe and Vacheron Constantin, may contain hundreds of parts and rank among the most expensive watches ever sold.
