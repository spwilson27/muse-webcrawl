The Flaming Lips are an American psychedelic rock band formed in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, in 1983. The band consists of Wayne Coyne (vocals, guitar), Steven Drozd (multi-instrumentalist), and others. Their elaborate live shows feature costumes, balloons, puppets, confetti, and Coyne's signature man-sized plastic bubble.

History

The band released a string of independent albums in the 1980s before signing to Warner Bros. Their 1993 single "She Don't Use Jelly" became a surprise hit. The experimental four-disc album Zaireeka (1997), designed to be played simultaneously on four stereos, was followed by the critically acclaimed The Soft Bulletin (1999) and Yoshimi Battles the Pink Robots (2002), the latter containing "Do You Realize??", later named Oklahoma's official rock song. Subsequent albums include Embryonic (2009), The Terror (2013), and American Head (2020).
