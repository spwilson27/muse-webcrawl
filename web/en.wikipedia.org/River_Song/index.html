River Song

River Song is a fictional character created by Steven Moffat and played by Alex Kingston in the British science fiction television series Doctor Who. She is a companion, romantic interest and eventual wife of the Doctor, the series' protagonist. Because she is a time traveller herself, her meetings with the Doctor occur out of chronological order — a running motif summarised by her catchphrase "Spoilers!" when refusing to reveal future events.

Appearances

River Song first appeared in the 2008 episodes "Silence in the Library" and "Forest of the Dead", in which she arrived already knowing the Tenth Doctor intimately while he did not know her at all. Subsequent stories with the Eleventh Doctor revealed her identity as Melody Pond, the daughter of companions Amy Pond and Rory Williams, conceived aboard the TARDIS and thus part-Time Lord. Kidnapped as a baby by the Silence and raised to kill the Doctor, she instead saved him, married him in "The Wedding of River Song" (2011), and spent much of her life as an archaeologist.

Character arc and reception

The character's non-linear relationship with the Doctor — the two move through each other's lives in opposite directions — became one of the defining story arcs of the Moffat era. River's final chronological appearance opposite the Twelfth Doctor came in the 2015 Christmas special "The Husbands of River Song", before her recorded consciousness returned briefly in later stories. Kingston's performance was widely praised, and the character has continued in Big Finish audio dramas.
