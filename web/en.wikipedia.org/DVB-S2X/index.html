DVB-S2X

DVB-S2X
Standard: ETSI EN 302 307-2
Introduced: 2014
Predecessor: DVB-S2

DVB-S2X is an extension of the DVB-S2 satellite broadcasting standard, published in 2014, offering finer modulation and coding granularity and improved efficiency, especially for professional and high-throughput applications.

Features

S2X adds additional MODCODs including higher-order modulations up to 256APSK, finer FEC code rates, channel bonding, super-frame formats, and improved performance at low signal-to-noise ratios for mobile and maritime services.

Applications

It is used for contribution links, VSAT networks, broadband satellite services, and ultra-high-definition television distribution.
