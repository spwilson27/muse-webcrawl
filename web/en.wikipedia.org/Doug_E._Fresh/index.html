Douglas Davis (born September 17, 1966), known professionally as Doug E. Fresh, is a Barbadian-born American rapper, record producer and beatboxer, widely nicknamed "The Human Beat Box". He pioneered American beatboxing, imitating drum machines and effects with his mouth, and is regarded as one of hip hop's foundational performers.

Career

Fresh first recorded in 1983 with Spoonie Gee and DJ Spivey. His breakthrough came in 1985 with the Get Fresh Crew and MC Ricky D (later Slick Rick), releasing "The Show" and "La Di Da Di"—the latter becoming one of the most sampled songs in hip hop history. His albums include Oh, My God! (1986) and The World's Greatest Entertainer (1988).

Legacy

"La Di Da Di" has been sampled or interpolated by hundreds of artists, from Snoop Dogg to Miley Cyrus. Fresh remains an active performer and mentor, and has been honoured for his contributions to hip hop culture, including recognition connected with the genre's 50th anniversary.
