Overthrow of the Hawaiian Kingdom

Date17 January 1893
LocationHonolulu, Kingdom of Hawaii
ResultProvisional Government established; monarchy abolished

The overthrow of the Hawaiian Kingdom was accomplished by a group of American and European businessmen and residents, organised as the Committee of Safety, who deposed Queen Liliʻuokalani on 17 January 1893. Supported by the landing of United States forces from USS Boston, the conspirators proclaimed a Provisional Government under Sanford B. Dole.

Background

American sugar planters had grown politically dominant in Hawaii, and the "Bayonet Constitution" of 1887 had sharply limited royal authority. When Liliʻuokalani attempted to promulgate a new constitution restoring power to the monarchy and Native Hawaiian voters, the Committee of Safety moved against her.

Aftermath

President Grover Cleveland's Blount Report concluded the overthrow was illegal, but the Provisional Government refused to yield power and proclaimed the Republic of Hawaii in 1894. Hawaii was annexed by the United States in 1898 during the Spanish–American War. In 1993, the U.S. Congress passed the Apology Resolution acknowledging the role of U.S. agents in the overthrow.
