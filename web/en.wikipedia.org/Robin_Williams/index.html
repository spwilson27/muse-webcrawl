Robin Williams

Robin Williams
Born: 21 July 1951, Chicago, Illinois
Died: 11 August 2014, Paradise Cay, California
Occupations: actor, comedian

Robin McLaurin Williams (1951–2014) was an American actor and comedian, celebrated for his improvisational skill and the wide variety of characters he created on stage and screen.

Career

Williams rose to fame playing the alien Mork in the sitcom Mork & Mindy (1978–1982). His film career ranged from comedy and family films — Good Morning, Vietnam (1987), Aladdin (1992, as the voice of the Genie), Mrs. Doubtfire (1993) and Jumanji (1995) — to acclaimed dramas including Dead Poets Society (1989) and Good Will Hunting (1997), for which he won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor.

Death and legacy

Williams died by suicide in 2014; he had been diagnosed with Parkinson's disease, and an autopsy found diffuse Lewy body dementia. He is remembered as one of the greatest comedians and screen actors of his generation.
