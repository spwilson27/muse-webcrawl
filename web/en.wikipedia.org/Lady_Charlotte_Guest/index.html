Lady Charlotte Elizabeth Guest (née Bertie; 19 May 1812 – 15 January 1895), later Lady Charlotte Schreiber, was an English linguist and businesswoman best known as the first publisher in modern print format of The Mabinogion, the earliest prose literature of Britain.

Life

Born into the aristocracy, she married Josiah John Guest, owner of the Dowlais Iron Company in Merthyr Tydfil, Wales, in 1833. She taught herself Welsh and, after moving to Wales, translated the medieval Welsh tales of the Mabinogion, published in parts between 1838 and 1849 with scholarly notes — a landmark work that introduced Welsh medieval literature to a wide audience.

Business career

On her husband's death in 1852 she took over the management of the Dowlais ironworks, then among the largest in the world, running it successfully for several years and concerning herself with the welfare and education of the workforce, founding schools for workers' children.

Later life

In 1855 she married Charles Schreiber and devoted herself to collecting English porcelain, fans, and playing cards, collections later bequeathed to the Victoria and Albert Museum and the British Museum. Her extensive diaries are an important source for the history of the Welsh iron industry.
