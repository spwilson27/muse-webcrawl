A Star Is Born (1954 film)

A Star Is Born
Directed by: George Cukor
Starring: Judy Garland, James Mason
Production company: Transcona Enterprises
Distributed by: Warner Bros.
Release date: September 29, 1954

A Star Is Born is a 1954 American musical drama film directed by George Cukor, written by Moss Hart, and starring Judy Garland and James Mason. It is the second version of the original 1937 film, and was later remade in 1976 and 2018.

Plot

Alcoholic leading man Norman Maine discovers talented singer Esther Blodgett and helps her become movie star Vicki Lester. As her career rises and his collapses into alcoholism, their marriage disintegrates; Maine ultimately drowns himself so as not to burden her, and she returns to the stage, introducing herself as "Mrs. Norman Maine".

Production and legacy

The film marked Garland's comeback after several difficult years. Warner Bros. cut the film substantially after its premiere; a 1983 restoration recovered much of the lost footage with still photographs filling the gaps. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest musical films, and Garland's performance — including "The Man That Got Away" — is considered among her finest.
