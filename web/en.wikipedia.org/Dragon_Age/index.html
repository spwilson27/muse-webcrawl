The Dragon Age fantasy role-playing series by BioWare

Dragon Age is a media franchise centred on a series of fantasy role-playing video games created by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts. The franchise takes place on the fictional continent of Thedas and follows the experiences of its various protagonists. The main games are Dragon Age: Origins (2009), Dragon Age II (2011), Dragon Age: Inquisition (2014) and Dragon Age: The Veilguard (2024). The series has also spawned novels by writers including David Gaider, comic books, an animated film and series, and tabletop games.

Setting

Thedas is a world of nations including Ferelden, Orlais, Tevinter and Antiva, threatened periodically by "Blights" — invasions of monstrous darkspawn led by an Archdemon (a corrupted dragon god) — which are opposed by the order of Grey Wardens. Magic users (mages) are overseen by the Chantry and its templars, creating a central political and religious conflict; elves, dwarves and the horned qunari peoples populate the world, and the dream realm called the Fade connects to magic and demons.

Games

Origins follows a Grey Warden recruit ending the Fifth Blight in Ferelden and won wide acclaim as a spiritual successor to Baldur's Gate. Dragon Age II tells the story of Hawke, a refugee who becomes Champion of the city-state Kirkwall over a decade. Inquisition casts the player as the Inquisitor, leader of a restored Inquisition closing a tear in the sky; it won numerous Game of the Year awards in 2014. The Veilguard (2024), originally announced as Dreadwolf, follows Rook's team against the elven gods released by the Dread Wolf Solas.

Reception

The series is praised for its writing, characters, player choice and world-building, and has sold millions of copies. It is noted for its inclusive character writing and its emphasis on companion relationships.
