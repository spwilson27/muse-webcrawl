Eravikulam National Park

Eravikulam National Park
Location: Idukki district, Kerala, India
Area: 97 km²
Established: 1978
Nearest town: Munnar

Eravikulam National Park is a 97 km² national park located along the Western Ghats in the Idukki and Ernakulam districts of Kerala, India. It is the first national park in Kerala and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site as part of the Western Ghats. The park is famous for its population of the endangered Nilgiri tahr.

Geography and flora

The park consists of a high rolling plateau with an average elevation of about 2,000 metres, covered with shola-grassland mosaic. Anamudi, at 2,695 metres the highest peak in South India, lies within the park. Every twelve years the hillsides flower en masse with the Neelakurinji shrub (Strobilanthes kunthiana), most recently in 2018, drawing large numbers of visitors.

Fauna

Eravikulam holds the largest surviving population of Nilgiri tahr, estimated at several hundred animals. Other mammals include lion-tailed macaques, gaur, Indian muntjac, sambar deer and predators such as leopards and dholes. The park also hosts numerous endemic birds and butterflies of the southern Western Ghats.
