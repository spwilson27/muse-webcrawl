PROMESA

The Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, and Economic Stability Act (PROMESA) is a United States federal law enacted in 2016 that established a financial oversight board and a process for restructuring the debts of Puerto Rico, which faced a debt crisis with roughly $70 billion in outstanding obligations.

Provisions

Signed by President Barack Obama on June 30, 2016, PROMESA created the Financial Oversight and Management Board for Puerto Rico, an entity with authority over the territory's budgets and fiscal plans. Title III of the act provides a court-supervised, bankruptcy-like debt adjustment process, while Title V expedites approvals for critical infrastructure projects.

Debt restructuring

In May 2017 the oversight board filed for Title III proceedings, the largest public debt restructuring in United States history. A plan of adjustment for the central government's debt was confirmed in January 2022.
