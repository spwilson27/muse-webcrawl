Queen Mary 2


RMS Queen Mary 2 at sea

RMS Queen Mary 2 (QM2) is a British ocean liner built for and operated by Cunard Line. Named by Queen Elizabeth II in 2004 after the earlier RMS Queen Mary of 1936, she was the largest passenger ship ever built at her launch and remains the only purpose-built ocean liner in regular scheduled transatlantic service, sailing between Southampton and New York.

Design

QM2 was designed as a true liner, with a long, fine hull, deep draft and about 40% of her volume devoted to machinery space to maintain high North Atlantic speeds. She is propelled by four azimuthing podded drives — the first fitted to a liner — powered by a combined diesel-electric and gas turbine plant. Her interiors, designed under Carnival's Stephen Payne, include the largest library and only planetarium at sea, the Canyon Ranch spa, and the Britannia, Princess Grill and Queens Grill restaurants reflecting Cunard's class-graded accommodation.

Service

She took over the transatlantic role of QE2 in 2004 and has since also undertaken annual world voyages and cruises. A major 2016 remastering refit added staterooms and refreshed public spaces. Her hull's "RMS" designation reflects her contract to carry Royal Mail.
