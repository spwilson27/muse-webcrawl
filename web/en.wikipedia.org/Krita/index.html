Krita is a free and open-source raster graphics editor designed primarily for digital art and 2D animation. It is developed by the Krita Foundation with the KDE community. Krita is available for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and ChromeOS.

History

Krita originated in 1998–1999 as KImageShop/Krayon within the KOffice suite, was renamed Krita (from the Swedish word for crayon and the Sanskrit word for "created") and released with KOffice in 2005. From 2009 the project refocused on digital painting rather than general image manipulation. Successful crowdfunding campaigns funded full-time development, and the Krita Foundation oversees development and fundraising. The software gained wider attention when institutions such as the Paris-Sorbonne University art school adopted it over proprietary alternatives.

Features

Krita offers natural media brush engines (including smudge, chalk and spray brushes), brush stabilisers, layer and mask management, HDR painting, vector and text tools, animation tools with onion skinning, and support for graphics tablets. Its mascot is Kiki the Cyber Squirrel.
