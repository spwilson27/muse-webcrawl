The sign in the median of Las Vegas Boulevard, south of the Strip.

The Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign is a Las Vegas landmark funded in May 1959 and erected soon after by Western Neon. The sign was designed by Betty Willis at the request of Ted Rogich, a local salesman, who sold it to Clark County, Nevada. It stands in the median at 5100 Las Vegas Boulevard South, in Paradise, just south of the historic Las Vegas Strip.

Design

The sign is a horizontal white elongated oval with a red starburst at the top, modelled in the Googie architecture style prevalent at the time. The words "Welcome" appear in red inside blue starbursts, "to Fabulous" in blue, and "Las Vegas" in large red letters, with "Nevada" in blue beneath. The back of the sign reads "Drive Carefully" and "Come Back Soon". The circles behind the word "Welcome" were intended to portray silver dollars, a reference to Nevada's nickname as the "Silver State".

History and recognition

Designer Betty Willis never trademarked the design, considering it her gift to the city. The sign was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2009, on its 50th anniversary. Following the 2017 Las Vegas shooting, the sign became a site of memorial crosses and vigils. A parking area built in 2008 allows visitors to photograph the sign safely.
