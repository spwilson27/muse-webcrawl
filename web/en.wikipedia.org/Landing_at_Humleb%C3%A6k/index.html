Landing at Humlebæk
Date4 August 1700
LocationHumlebæk, Zealand, Denmark
Part ofGreat Northern War

The Landing at Humlebæk was a Swedish military landing operation carried out on 4 August 1700 during the opening phase of the Great Northern War. Swedish forces under King Charles XII landed near Humlebæk, north of Copenhagen on Zealand, Denmark, forcing Denmark–Norway out of the war by the Peace of Traventhal.

Background

Denmark–Norway had joined an alliance with Saxony and Russia against Sweden. Charles XII decided to strike Denmark first, escorted by Swedish, English and Dutch naval forces.

Landing

Swedish troops went ashore largely unopposed despite the presence of Danish cavalry, secured the beachhead, and advanced toward Copenhagen.

Aftermath

Denmark withdrew from the war, allowing Sweden to turn against Russia and Saxony, leading to the Battle of Narva later that year.
