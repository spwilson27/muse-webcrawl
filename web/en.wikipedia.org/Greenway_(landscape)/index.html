Greenway (landscape)

A greenway is usually a shared-use path along a strip of undeveloped land, in an urban area or along natural features such as rivers, ridgelines or converted rail corridors, set aside for recreational use or environmental protection.

Definition

Greenways often link parks, nature reserves, cultural features and historic sites with populated areas. They may follow natural land or water features — riverbanks, valleys, canals — or converted rights-of-way such as disused railways, utility corridors or dikes.

Purposes

Greenways serve recreation, non-motorized transport (walking and cycling), wildlife movement between habitat fragments, flood mitigation and water-quality protection. Well-known examples include the East Coast Greenway in the United States and numerous urban river trails worldwide.
