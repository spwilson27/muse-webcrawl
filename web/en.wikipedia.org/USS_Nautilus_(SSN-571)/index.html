USS Nautilus (SSN-571)

USS Nautilus (SSN-571) was the world's first operational nuclear-powered submarine and the first submarine to complete a submerged transit of the North Pole. She was launched in 1954, sponsored by First Lady Mamie Eisenhower, and commissioned that September. On 17 January 1955 her first commanding officer, Commander Eugene P. Wilkinson, signalled the historic message "Underway on nuclear power."

Powered by the Submarine Thermal Reactor, Nautilus could operate submerged for weeks at high speed, shattering existing submarine records and transforming undersea warfare. In August 1958, during Operation Sunshine, she passed under the polar ice cap and reached the geographic North Pole, commanded by William R. Anderson.

She served until decommissioning in 1980, having travelled more than 500,000 miles. After her reactor was removed, Nautilus was preserved as a museum ship at the Submarine Force Library and Museum in Groton, Connecticut, and designated a National Historic Landmark.
