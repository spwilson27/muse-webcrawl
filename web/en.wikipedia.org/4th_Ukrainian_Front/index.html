The 4th Ukrainian Front was a Front (army group) of the Soviet Red Army during the Second World War. It was created on 20 October 1943 by renaming the Southern Front under General Fyodor Tolbukhin.

First formation

The front advanced along the northern coast of the Sea of Azov and the Black Sea, conducted the Melitopol operation, isolated German forces in Crimea by reaching the Perekop Isthmus, and in spring 1944 carried out the Crimean Offensive, which liberated Sevastopol in May 1944. On 31 May 1944 the front headquarters was disbanded to the Reserve of the Supreme High Command.

Second formation

The front was re-formed on 5 August 1944 under General Ivan Petrov to operate in the Carpathians. It fought through the East Carpathian and West Carpathian offensives, advancing across Slovakia in difficult mountain terrain, and later took part in the Moravia–Ostrava Offensive and the Prague Offensive of May 1945 under General Andrey Yeryomenko. It was disbanded on 25 August 1945 and its headquarters used to form the Carpathian Military District.
