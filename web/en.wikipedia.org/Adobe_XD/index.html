Adobe XD (Adobe Experience Design) is a vector-based user experience design tool developed by Adobe Inc. for designing and prototyping websites and mobile apps. It lets designers create wireframes, screen layouts, interactive prototypes with transitions, and design systems with components and shared libraries.

History

Adobe announced the tool in 2015 under the name "Project Comet" as a response to Sketch, then dominant in interface design. A public preview for macOS followed in March 2016, with a Windows version later that year; the product left beta in 2017 and was included in Creative Cloud plans.

Decline

Adobe XD lost ground to the web-based rival Figma. In 2022 Adobe announced it would acquire Figma for about 0 billion, and XD was placed in maintenance mode; the acquisition was abandoned in December 2023 after regulatory opposition, and Adobe subsequently ended new sales of XD while continuing limited support.
