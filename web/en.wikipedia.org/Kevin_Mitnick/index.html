Kevin Mitnick

Kevin David Mitnick (6 August 1963 – 16 July 2023) was an American computer security consultant and author, once one of the FBI's most-wanted hackers for computer and wire fraud.

Life and career

Arrested in 1995 after a widely publicised pursuit, he served five years in prison. After release he founded a security consultancy, wrote books including The Art of Deception and the memoir Ghost in the Wires, and became known for popularising social engineering.
