Capcom

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Capcom Co., Ltd. is a Japanese video game company. It has created a number of multi-million-selling game franchises, with its most commercially successful being Resident Evil, Monster Hunter, Street Fighter, Mega Man, and Devil May Cry.

History

Founded in 1979 as I.R.M. Corporation in Osaka by Kenzo Tsujimoto, the company adopted the name Capcom — a contraction of "Capsule Computers" — in the early 1980s and began as a manufacturer of arcade machines. Its first arcade title was Vulgus (1984). Breakthrough successes included the arcade hits 1942 and Ghosts 'n Goblins, and the 1987 releases Mega Man and the original Street Fighter.

Major franchises

Street Fighter II (1991) ignited the fighting-game boom of the 1990s. Resident Evil (1996) popularised the survival horror genre. Monster Hunter became a phenomenon in Japan and, with Monster Hunter: World (2018), Capcom's best-selling title. Other series include Ace Attorney, Dead Rising, Dragon's Dogma, and Marvel vs. Capcom.

Structure

Headquartered in Chūō-ku, Osaka, Capcom maintains subsidiaries in North America and Europe and operates divisions covering consumer games, arcade operations, pachislo, and licensing of its characters in film and merchandise.
