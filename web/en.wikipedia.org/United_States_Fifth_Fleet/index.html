The United States Fifth Fleet is a numbered fleet of the United States Navy. Its area of responsibility covers approximately 2.5 million square miles of water in the Middle East, including the Persian Gulf, Red Sea, Arabian Sea, Gulf of Oman, and parts of the Indian Ocean. The fleet is headquartered at Naval Support Activity Bahrain in Manama, Bahrain, and its commander is dual-hatted as commander of United States Naval Forces Central Command (NAVCENT), the naval component of U.S. Central Command.

History

The Fifth Fleet was first established on 26 April 1944 during the Second World War, when the Central Pacific Force was renamed. Under Admirals Raymond Spruance and William Halsey — the designation alternating between Third Fleet (Halsey) and Fifth Fleet (Spruance) for the same forces — it conducted the great carrier campaigns of the Pacific, including the Battle of the Philippine Sea and the operations at Iwo Jima and Okinawa. The fleet was deactivated in January 1947.

For most of the Cold War and early post-Cold War periods, naval operations in the Middle East were conducted by the Middle East Force and, after 1983, by NAVCENT. On 1 July 1995, the Fifth Fleet was reactivated to take operational control of naval forces in the region, with its commander also serving as NAVCENT commander.

Operations

Since its reactivation, the Fifth Fleet has supported operations including the enforcement of sanctions on Iraq, Operation Enduring Freedom, Operation Iraqi Freedom, and counter-piracy patrols off the Horn of Africa through Combined Task Force 151. It oversees the Combined Maritime Forces, a multinational partnership of more than 40 nations headquartered alongside it in Bahrain.

The fleet's task forces include Task Force 50 (battle force), Task Force 51 (amphibious), Task Force 52 (mine warfare), Task Force 53 (logistics), Task Force 55 (surface combatants), Task Force 56 (expeditionary), Task Force 57 (patrol and reconnaissance), and Task Force 59, established in 2021 to integrate unmanned systems and artificial intelligence — the Navy's first such task force.
