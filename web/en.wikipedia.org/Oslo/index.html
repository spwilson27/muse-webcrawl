Oslo

Oslo is the capital and most populous city of Norway, with a population of about 700,000 in the municipality and around 1.5 million in the metropolitan area. It sits at the head of the Oslofjord, surrounded by forested hills.

History

Oslo was founded around 1048 by King Harald Hardrada. From 1624 to 1925, following a rebuilding after fire under King Christian IV, it was known as Christiania (later Kristiania). It is Norway's seat of government, home to the Royal Palace, the Storting (parliament), and the Nobel Peace Prize ceremony held each year at Oslo City Hall.

Economy and culture

Oslo is a centre for maritime industries, finance, and technology. Landmarks include the Oslo Opera House, the MUNCH museum, Akershus Fortress, and the Vigeland sculpture park. The city hosted the 1952 Winter Olympics.
