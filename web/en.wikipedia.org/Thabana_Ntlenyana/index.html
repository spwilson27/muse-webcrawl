Thabana Ntlenyana

Thabana Ntlenyana is the highest mountain in Lesotho and the highest point in southern Africa south of Kilimanjaro. Its summit rises to 3,482 metres (11,424 feet) in the Maloti range of the Drakensberg escarpment, near Lesotho's border with South Africa. The name means "beautiful little mountain" in Sesotho.

Elevation3,482 m (11,424 ft)
RangeMaloti / Drakensberg
LocationMokhotlong District, Lesotho

Geography and climbing

The peak is a broad, rounded high point on the basalt plateau of the Drakensberg, formed by lava flows some 180 million years old. It is usually climbed from South Africa via the Sani Pass and the village of Sani Top, or on multi-day treks through the remote Mokhotlong highlands. The summit commands views across Lesotho, a country notable as the only state in the world entirely above 1,000 metres.

Environment

The surrounding alpine grasslands support grazing herds, bearded vultures, and unique high-altitude flora. Snow falls regularly in winter, and the peaks feed headwaters of the Orange–Senqu river system.
