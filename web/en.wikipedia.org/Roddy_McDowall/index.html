Roddy McDowall

Roddy McDowall
Born: Roderick Andrew Anthony McDowall, 17 September 1928, Herne Hill, London
Died: 3 October 1998, Los Angeles, California
Occupation: Actor, photographer, director
Years active: 1938–1998

Roderick Andrew Anthony McDowall (17 September 1928 – 3 October 1998) was a British-American actor, photographer, and film director. He began his acting career as a child in England, and later in America, to become an accomplished adult actor. He was known for his roles in How Green Was My Valley (1941), Lassie Come Home (1943), and the Planet of the Apes series (1968–1973).

Early life and child acting

McDowall was born in Herne Hill, London, and won a modeling contest as a baby. He appeared in several British films as a child before his family moved to the United States in 1940 to escape the Blitz. His breakthrough came as Huw Morgan in John Ford's How Green Was My Valley, followed by leading roles in My Friend Flicka (1943) and Lassie Come Home (1943), which established him as one of the most famous child actors of his generation.

Adult career

As an adult, McDowall worked extensively on stage, winning a Tony Award for Jean Anouilh's The Fighting Cock in 1960, and on television, winning an Emmy Award for Sunday Showcase. He played Cornelius and Caesar in the Planet of the Apes films and Galen in the 1974 television series. Other notable roles included Octavian in Cleopatra (1963) and Peter Vincent in Fright Night (1985).

Photography

McDowall was also an accomplished portrait photographer and published several books of his photographs of fellow actors. He was a lifelong film enthusiast and preservation advocate, serving on the board of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences.
