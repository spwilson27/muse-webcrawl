François Édouard Anatole Lucas (4 April 1842 – 3 October 1891) was a French mathematician. He is known for his study of the Fibonacci sequence and the related Lucas sequences, and for devising methods of testing prime numbers.

Work

Lucas developed the Lucas primality test, later refined by Derrick Henry Lehmer into the Lucas–Lehmer test still used to verify Mersenne primes. In 1876 he proved that the Mersenne number 2127 − 1 is prime, the largest prime verified by hand calculation.

Recreational mathematics

Lucas invented the Tower of Hanoi puzzle, published under the anagrammatic pseudonym "N. Claus de Siam", and the game of Dots and Boxes. His multi-volume Récréations mathématiques became a classic of recreational mathematics.
