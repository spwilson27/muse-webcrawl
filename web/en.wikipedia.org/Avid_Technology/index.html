Avid Technology

Avid Technology, Inc. is an American technology and multimedia company founded in 1987 by Bill Warner, based in Burlington, Massachusetts. It develops software and hardware for video and audio production, media asset management and broadcast newsroom systems.

Products

Avid's best-known products are the Media Composer video editor, the Pro Tools digital audio workstation, and Sibelius music notation software. Through acquisitions — including Digidesign (Pro Tools), Pinnacle Systems and Blue Order — the company assembled a broad media production portfolio, and its Interplay/MediaCentral platforms became common in television newsrooms.

History

Avid went public in 1993 and was a central company in the digitalisation of film editing in the 1990s. After years of restructuring it was taken private in 2023, when an affiliate of STG Partners acquired the company in a deal valued at about $1.4 billion.
