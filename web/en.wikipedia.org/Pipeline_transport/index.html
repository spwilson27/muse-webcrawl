Pipeline transport

Pipeline transport is the movement of liquids, gases or finely divided solids through a system of pipes, most commonly used for crude oil, refined petroleum products, natural gas and water. Pipelines are among the most economical and safest means of moving large volumes of fluids over long distances.

Systems

Gathering pipelines collect output from wells, transmission pipelines carry products long distances to distribution hubs, and distribution pipelines deliver to end users. Pumping or compressor stations maintain pressure along the route, and major networks include the Trans-Alaska Pipeline and extensive grids in Russia, North America and Europe.

Safety and environment

Leaks and ruptures, though infrequent per unit of volume moved, can cause major pollution and explosions; operators use inline inspection tools ("smart pigs"), leak-detection systems and corrosion control. Pipeline projects also attract public debate over land use, indigenous rights and climate policy.
