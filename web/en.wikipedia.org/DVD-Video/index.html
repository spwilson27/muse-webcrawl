DVD-Video

DVD-Video is a consumer video format used to store digital video on DVD discs, employing MPEG-2 video compression (and occasionally MPEG-1) with audio in formats such as Dolby Digital (AC-3), DTS, or PCM.

Structure

Content is stored in a VIDEO_TS directory as VOB files, with navigation data in IFO files and backups in BUP files. Discs support menus, multiple audio tracks, subtitles, chapters, and multiple camera angles.

Restrictions

Commercial discs typically use CSS encryption and regional playback codes dividing the world into six main regions, which restrict copying and cross-region playback.

Resolution

Standard-definition resolutions include 720×480 for NTSC and 720×576 for PAL.
