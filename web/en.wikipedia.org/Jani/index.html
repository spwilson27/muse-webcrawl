Jani

Jani is a given name and surname found in several cultures. It is a Finnish and Hungarian masculine given name, a form of John (from Hebrew Yochanan, "God is gracious"), and also appears as a feminine name in some contexts. In India, Jani is a surname found among Gujarati and other communities, historically associated with scholars and court poets attached to royal households.

Given name

Notable people with the given name Jani include Finnish racing driver Jani Nevala, and it is common in Finland as a diminutive of Juhani or Jan. In Hungarian usage it is an affectionate form of János.

Surname

As a surname, Jani is borne by Gujarati families; in medieval Gujarat and Rajasthan, a "Jani" was a learned Brahmin retained by kings to compose genealogies and praise poetry. The surname also occurs in Albania and elsewhere in the Balkans.

Other uses

Jani may also refer to places and titles in various languages, and appears in compound names across South Asia, the Middle East and Europe.
