Mall of America (MOA) is a large shopping mall located in Bloomington, Minnesota, United States, in the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area. Opened on August 11, 1992, it is one of the largest malls in the United States and among the largest in the world by total floor area. The mall is owned by the Triple Five Group of Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, which also owns the West Edmonton Mall.

History

The mall was built on the site of Metropolitan Stadium, the former home of the Minnesota Twins baseball team and the Minnesota Vikings football team, which had stood on the site until 1981; a plaque and a preserved red stadium seat in the amusement park mark the location of home plate. The project was developed by the Ghermezian family's Triple Five Group together with the Simon Property Group and Melvin Simon & Associates. Ground was broken in June 1989, and the mall opened in August 1992 with more than 330 stores.

An expansion known as Phase II added a Radisson Blu hotel, an office tower, additional retail, and the JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America, which opened in 2015. Further phases have been proposed over the years, including an indoor water park.

Attractions

At the center of the mall is Nickelodeon Universe, a seven-acre indoor theme park — originally opened as Camp Snoopy — featuring roller coasters such as the Fairly Odd Coaster and SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge. Other attractions include:

  • Sea Life Minnesota Aquarium, an aquarium with a 300-foot underwater tunnel.
  • A Lego Store with large-scale Lego models.
  • The Moose Mountain Adventure Golf miniature golf course.
  • A comedy club, movie theaters, and the FlyOver America flight simulation ride.

The mall also hosts hundreds of events each year, including concerts, charity walks, and book signings, and has been a filming location and a venue for professional wrestling and mixed martial arts events.

Scale and visitors

Mall of America is large enough to fit seven Yankee Stadiums inside it, and its walking distance around one level is about 0.57 miles (0.92 km). It employs roughly 11,000 people year-round, rising during peak seasons. The mall reports around 40 million visitors annually, making it one of the most visited tourist destinations in the United States; a substantial share of visitors are tourists from outside the region, drawn in part by Minnesota's lack of sales tax on clothing and shoes.

Transportation

The mall is a major transit hub. The Metro Blue Line light rail connects it to downtown Minneapolis and Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport, and the Metro Red Line bus rapid transit serves communities to the south. Large parking ramps on the east and west sides provide more than 12,000 parking spaces.
