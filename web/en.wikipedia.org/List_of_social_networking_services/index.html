List of social networking services

Social networking services allow users to build profiles, connect with others and share content. This list includes major current and defunct services.

  • Facebook — founded 2004, Meta
  • YouTube — video sharing, 2005
  • WhatsApp and Instagram — Meta
  • WeChat and QQ — Tencent, China
  • TikTok / Douyin — ByteDance
  • Snapchat
  • X (formerly Twitter)
  • Reddit
  • Pinterest
  • LinkedIn — professional networking, Microsoft
  • Telegram
  • Discord

Earlier services such as MySpace, Friendster, Orkut, Google+, Vine and Bebo are also listed, many now defunct.
