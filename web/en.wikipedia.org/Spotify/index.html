Spotify

Spotify is a Swedish audio streaming and media service provider founded on 23 April 2006 by Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon. It is one of the largest music streaming service providers, with hundreds of millions of monthly active users, including paying subscribers. Spotify offers digital copyright-restricted recorded audio content, including more than 100 million songs and numerous podcasts, from record labels and media companies.

History

Spotify was founded in Stockholm, Sweden, and launched publicly in October 2008. The service expanded to the United States in 2011. Spotify Technology S.A. was listed on the New York Stock Exchange in April 2018 through a direct public offering. Founder Daniel Ek has served as chief executive since the company's founding.

Features

As a freemium service, basic features are free with advertisements and limited control, while additional features, such as offline listening and ad-free playback, are offered via paid subscriptions known as Spotify Premium. Users can search for music based on artist, album or genre, and can create, edit and share playlists. Spotify's recommendation features include Discover Weekly, a personalised playlist updated each Monday, and algorithmic mixes.

Business model and criticism

Spotify pays royalties to rights holders based on the number of artist streams as a proportion of total songs streamed. The size of per-stream payments to artists has been a recurring subject of criticism from musicians and songwriters, who argue that the streaming model undervalues recorded music.
