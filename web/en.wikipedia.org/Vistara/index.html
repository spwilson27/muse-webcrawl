Vistara was an Indian full-service airline, based in Gurgaon, with its hub at Indira Gandhi International Airport in Delhi. The carrier, a joint venture between Tata Sons and Singapore Airlines, commenced operations on 9 January 2015 with its inaugural flight between Delhi and Mumbai. The name Vistara is derived from the Sanskrit word vistaar, meaning "limitless expanse".

History

The venture marked Tata's return to aviation decades after Air India's nationalisation, and Singapore Airlines' entry into the Indian market following the relaxation of foreign investment rules in 2012. Vistara was the first Indian carrier to introduce a premium economy class on domestic routes and built a fleet of Airbus A320neo, A321neo, and Boeing 787-9 aircraft.

Merger with Air India

In November 2022, Tata Sons and Singapore Airlines announced that Vistara would be merged into Air India as part of the consolidation of Tata's airline businesses. The merger was completed on 12 November 2024, with Vistara's flights thereafter operated by Air India under the AI flight code with a "2" prefix series, and Singapore Airlines taking a 25.1% stake in the enlarged Air India group.
