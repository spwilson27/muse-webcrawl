Donald Kinder

Donald R. Kinder
Nationality: American
Field: Political psychology
Institution: University of Michigan
Known for: Research on racial resentment and public opinion

Donald R. Kinder is an American political scientist and political psychologist at the University of Michigan, known for influential research on public opinion, voting behavior, and the role of racial attitudes in American politics.

Research

With Lynn Sanders, Kinder wrote Divided by Color (1996), which developed the concept of racial resentment (or modern/symbolic racism) as a predictor of white Americans' policy preferences. With David Sears and others, he studied how group interests, prejudice, and political information shape citizens' opinions. He has also contributed to the American National Election Studies and to work on how voters use candidate traits and group cues when forming judgments.
