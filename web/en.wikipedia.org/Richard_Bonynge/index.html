Richard Bonynge


Richard Bonynge, Australian conductor and pianist

Richard Bonynge AC CBE (born 29 September 1930) is an Australian conductor and pianist, celebrated for his championship of French and Italian bel canto opera and for his long artistic partnership with his wife, the soprano Dame Joan Sutherland.

Early life and career

Bonynge was born in Epping, Sydney, and studied piano at the New South Wales Conservatorium of Music before travelling to London to study with Herbert Fryer at the Royal College of Music. Originally a pianist and vocal coach, he worked with Sutherland from early in her career, guiding her transformation from a dramatic soprano into one of the leading coloratura sopranos of the 20th century. The couple married in 1954.

Conducting

Bonynge made his conducting debut in Rome in 1962 and his Metropolitan Opera debut in 1966 with Lucia di Lammermoor. He became a leading exponent of the neglected French and Italian repertoire of the early 19th century, conducting and recording operas by Donizetti, Bellini, Rossini, Massenet, Delibes, Adam and others, and reviving much 19th-century French ballet music. He served as artistic director of the Vancouver Opera and as music director of the Australian Opera (1975–1986), and made a vast discography, mostly for Decca, frequently with Sutherland in the leading roles.

Honours

He was appointed a Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in 1977 and a Companion of the Order of Australia (AC) in 2012, and was made a Commandeur de l'Ordre des Arts et des Lettres by France. Sutherland died in 2010; Bonynge has continued to conduct and to promote bel canto repertory in his later years.
