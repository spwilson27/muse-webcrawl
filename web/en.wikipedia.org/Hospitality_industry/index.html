Hospitality industry

The hospitality industry is a broad category of fields within the service industry that includes lodging, food and drink service, event planning, theme parks, travel, and tourism. It is a multibillion-dollar industry that depends heavily on the availability of leisure time and disposable income.

Sectors

The principal sectors are accommodation (hotels, motels, hostels, resorts, and short-term rentals), food and beverage (restaurants, bars, catering, and cafes), travel and tourism (airlines, travel agencies, tour operators, and cruise lines), and entertainment and recreation (casinos, theme parks, and events). Hospitality businesses are often organised through franchising and management contracts, in which brand companies provide names, standards, and reservation systems while local owners operate the properties.

Characteristics

The industry is labour-intensive and characterised by perishable inventory — an unsold hotel room or airline seat cannot be stored for later sale. Quality depends on delivering consistent service experiences, and the sector is highly sensitive to economic cycles, seasonality, and disruptive events such as pandemics.
