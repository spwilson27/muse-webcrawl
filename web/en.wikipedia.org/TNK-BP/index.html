TNK-BP

Founded2003
Defunct2013
HeadquartersMoscow, Russia
IndustryOil and gas

TNK-BP was a major vertically integrated Russian oil company created in 2003 by the merger of BP's Russian oil and gas assets with those of the Alfa, Access/Renova (AAR) consortium. It was Russia's third-largest oil producer.

History

The joint venture combined the Tyumen Oil Company (TNK) with BP's holdings, including a stake in the Kovykta gas field. Relations between the partners were often strained, notably during a 2008 shareholder dispute over strategy and management.

Sale to Rosneft

In 2013 the state-controlled company Rosneft acquired TNK-BP in a deal worth about 55 billion US dollars, with BP taking a significant minority stake in Rosneft and AAR receiving cash.
