Vindesine

Class: Vinca alkaloid
Use: Chemotherapy
Route: Intravenous

Vindesine, also known as desacetyl vinblastine amide, is a semi-synthetic vinca alkaloid derived from vinblastine. It is an antineoplastic (chemotherapy) drug used in the treatment of various cancers, including acute lymphocytic leukaemia, blast crisis of chronic myeloid leukaemia, malignant melanoma, and lung and breast cancers.

Mechanism of action

Like other vinca alkaloids (vinblastine, vincristine, vinorelbine), vindesine binds to tubulin and inhibits microtubule assembly, arresting cell division in metaphase and leading to apoptosis of rapidly dividing cells.

Adverse effects

Side effects include bone marrow suppression (leucopenia, thrombocytopenia), nausea and vomiting, constipation, peripheral neuropathy, hair loss, and jaw pain. Its neurotoxicity is generally less than that of vincristine, and it may be used where vincristine resistance has developed.
