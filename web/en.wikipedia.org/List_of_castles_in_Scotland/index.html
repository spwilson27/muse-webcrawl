List of castles in Scotland

Scotland has been estimated to possess some 2,000 to 3,000 castles, from great royal fortresses to small tower houses, built from the 12th century onward. Many survive as ruins, others are restored and inhabited. This list presents a selection of the most notable examples by region.

History

Castle-building in Scotland began with Norman-style motte-and-bailey and stone keeps in the 1100s, followed by great curtain-walled enclosures, then the characteristic tower houses of the 15th–17th centuries built by lairds and clan chiefs amid a lawless border society. After the Union of 1707 and the Jacobite risings, fortification declined; the 19th century revived castles as romantic country houses in the Scots Baronial style.
