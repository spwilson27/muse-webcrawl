Cetshwayo kaMpande

Cetshwayo kaMpande (c. 1826 – 8 February 1884) was the last great king of the independent Zulu Kingdom. The son of King Mpande, he ruled from 1873 until his capture during the Anglo-Zulu War of 1879, and was partially restored to a reduced throne in 1883.

Reign and war

Cetshwayo consolidated power after defeating his brother Mbuyazi at the Battle of Ndondakusuka in 1856, and was crowned in 1873. Faced with an ultimatum from Sir Bartle Frere designed to provoke war, his armies won a famous victory over British forces at Isandlwana in January 1879, but were decisively defeated at Ulundi in July. Cetshwayo was captured and exiled to Cape Town and later London, where he petitioned Queen Victoria.

Restoration and death

In 1883 he was restored to part of his former kingdom, but civil war with rival chief Zibhebhu destroyed his capital and he died the following year, possibly by poison. His son Dinuzulu succeeded him.
