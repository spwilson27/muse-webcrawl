Yas Island (Arabic: جزيرة ياس) is a man-made leisure and entertainment island in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, developed from the mid-2000s at a cost of some US$40 billion. Covering about 25 km², it is one of the emirate's flagship tourism projects.

Attractions

The island hosts the Yas Marina Circuit, home of the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix since 2009, and Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, whose Formula Rossa is among the world's fastest roller coasters. Other attractions include Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi, SeaWorld Abu Dhabi (opened 2023), the Yas Mall, the du/Etihad Arena, and Yas Beach. The W Abu Dhabi hotel famously straddles the circuit.

Development and events

Yas Island was built on a largely empty coastal site east of Abu Dhabi city, near Zayed International Airport. Beyond motorsport it hosts concerts, UFC events at Etihad Arena, and golf at Yas Links. A significant residential and hotel district continues to expand.
