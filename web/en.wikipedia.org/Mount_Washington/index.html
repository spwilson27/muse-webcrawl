Mount Washington

Mount Washington is the highest peak in the northeastern United States at 6,288.2 ft (1,916.6 m), located in the Presidential Range of the White Mountains of New Hampshire. The Abenaki name is Agiocochook. It is famous for its dangerously erratic weather: the summit observatory recorded a wind gust of 231 mph (372 km/h) on April 12, 1934, long the world record for a surface station.

Access and features

The summit, within Mount Washington State Park, is reached by the Mount Washington Auto Road (opened 1861), the Cog Railway (1869, the world's first mountain-climbing cog railway), and hiking trails including the Appalachian Trail. Its combination of severe cold, high winds and rime ice has earned it the slogan "Home of the World's Worst Weather".
