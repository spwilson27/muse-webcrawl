Second Polish Army

The Second Polish Army (Polish: Druga Armia Wojska Polskiego) was a Polish field army formed in the Soviet Union in 1944 as part of the Polish Armed Forces in the East, fighting alongside the Red Army in the final campaigns of the Second World War.

History

The army was organised in the summer of 1944 under General Karol Świerczewski, drawing on Polish divisions formed in the USSR. It entered combat in 1945 during the offensives into Germany, fighting in the Battle of Bautzen in April 1945 — its bloodiest engagement, in which it suffered heavy casualties against German counterattacks — and then advanced into Czechoslovakia, taking part in the Prague Offensive in May 1945.

Postwar

After the war the Second Army was disbanded and its units used in border security and operations against Ukrainian insurgents in south-eastern Poland. A new Second Army was later formed in the Polish People's Army during the Cold War era.
