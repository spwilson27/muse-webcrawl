Polyacrylamide

Polyacrylamide (IUPAC poly(2-propenamide)) is a polymer formed from acrylamide subunits. It can be synthesized as a simple linear-chain or cross-linked structure, is highly water-absorbent, and forms a soft gel.

Uses include flocculants in water treatment, soil conditioning, enhanced oil recovery, cosmetics, and in molecular biology laboratories as the medium for polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (PAGE) to separate proteins and nucleic acids. While the polymer is considered non-toxic, residual acrylamide monomer is a neurotoxin, so products are regulated.
