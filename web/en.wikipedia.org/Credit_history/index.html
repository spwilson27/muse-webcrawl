Credit history

Credit history
Record of: Borrowing and repayment
Compiled by: Credit bureaus
Used for: Loans, renting, employment

A credit history is a record of a borrower's responsible repayment of debts. It is summarized in a credit report maintained by credit bureaus and is a primary factor lenders use to judge creditworthiness.

Contents

A credit history typically includes identifying information; credit accounts (cards, mortgages, auto and student loans) with limits, balances, and payment timeliness; public records such as bankruptcies and judgments; and inquiries made by lenders. Late payments, defaults, and collections weigh negatively, and most adverse items age off after about seven years, bankruptcies after up to ten.

Use and building credit

People with little or no history — young adults, recent immigrants — may be "credit invisible" and struggle to borrow. Secured cards, credit-builder loans, and becoming an authorized user are common ways to establish a record.
