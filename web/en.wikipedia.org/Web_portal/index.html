Web portal

A web portal is a specially designed website that brings information from diverse sources, like emails, online forums, and search engines, together in a uniform way. Portals were among the most popular destinations of the early World Wide Web, acting as starting points or "gateways" for users going online.

History and types

General consumer portals such as Yahoo!, AOL, MSN, and Excite offered search, news, weather, stock quotes, email, and directories on a single page, and were key players in the dot-com era. Specialized portals include enterprise (corporate) portals for employees, government portals for public services, and personal portals customizable with widgets.

Features

Portals typically provide single sign-on, personalized content, and integration of multiple applications and data sources. Although the standalone consumer portal declined as search engines and social media grew, the portal model survives in intranets, dashboards, and aggregated service sites.
