Filmmaking

Filmmaking or film production is the process of creating a motion picture. It normally consists of an initial story, idea, or commission, continuing through screenwriting, casting, pre-production, shooting, sound recording, post-production, and screening.

Stages

The five stages are development (financing and script), pre-production (casting, locations, crew), production (principal photography), post-production (editing, visual effects, sound design, scoring), and distribution (theatrical release, streaming, home video).

Technology

Filmmaking evolved from silent 35mm film through sound and colour to today's digital cameras, CGI, and virtual production stages.
