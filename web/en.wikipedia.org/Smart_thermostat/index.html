Smart thermostat

A smart thermostat is a Wi-Fi-enabled device that automatically adjusts heating and cooling temperature settings in a home for optimum performance. Smart thermostats that feature geofencing can automatically adjust when no one is home, and learning models can adapt temperature schedules to occupants' behaviour, reducing energy use.

Features

Typical features include remote control via smartphone apps, occupancy and humidity sensors, integration with voice assistants and smart-home platforms, energy-use reports, and demand-response participation with utilities. The Nest Learning Thermostat, launched in 2011, popularised the category; competitors include Ecobee and Honeywell Home devices.

Energy savings

Manufacturers and independent studies generally estimate heating and cooling savings of roughly 8–15 percent from features such as adaptive scheduling and home/away detection, though results vary with climate, housing stock and prior thermostat behaviour.
