YG Entertainment Inc. is a South Korean entertainment company founded in 1996 by Yang Hyun-suk, a former member of the pioneering K-pop group Seo Taiji and Boys. The letters "YG" stand for Yang-goon, a nickname of the founder. Long counted among the "big three" Korean record labels alongside SM Entertainment and JYP Entertainment, the company specializes in hip-hop-influenced pop music and operates as a record label, talent agency, production company and more.

History

After early success with groups such as 1TYM and solo artist Seven, YG rose to the top of the industry with the boy band BigBang (debuted 2006) and girl group 2NE1 (2009). In 2012, the company's artist Psy achieved global fame with "Gangnam Style". The girl group Blackpink, debuted in 2016, became one of the world's most popular acts, headlining major festivals and setting numerous streaming records. More recent acts include Winner, iKon, Treasure and Babymonster.

Business and controversies

Yang Hyun-suk stepped down from his positions in 2019 amid a series of scandals connected to the Burning Sun nightclub affair and other allegations. The company has since undergone restructuring while remaining a major force in K-pop, with income from concerts, music sales, merchandise and acting management.
