The Foundations of Arithmetic

The Foundations of Arithmetic (German: Die Grundlagen der Arithmetik, 1884) is a book by Gottlob Frege investigating the philosophical foundations of arithmetic. In it, Frege argues against psychologism and defends logicism — the view that arithmetic is reducible to logic — while formulating the context principle: 'never to ask for the meaning of a word in isolation, but only in the context of a proposition.'

Influence

The work is a landmark of analytic philosophy, introducing themes developed further in Frege's later writings and in Bertrand Russell's logicist programme, despite the blow dealt by Russell's paradox to Frege's later system in the Grundgesetze.
