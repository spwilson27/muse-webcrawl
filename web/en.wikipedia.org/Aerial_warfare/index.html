Aerial warfare

Aerial warfare is the use of military aircraft and other flying machines in war, encompassing air-to-air combat, bombing, reconnaissance and airlift conducted by air forces, naval aviation and army aviation.

History

Balloons were used for observation from the 1790s; aeroplanes entered combat in 1911 and transformed warfare in the First World War through dogfighting, bombing and reconnaissance. The Second World War added strategic bombing, carrier warfare and airborne operations.

Modern era

Jet fighters, precision-guided munitions, airborne early warning, electronic warfare and unmanned aerial vehicles dominate modern air power, whose first objective is usually air superiority. See also Air force, Dogfight and Strategic bombing.
