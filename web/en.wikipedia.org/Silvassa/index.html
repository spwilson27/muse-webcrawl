Silvassa

Silvassa is a city and the headquarters of the Dadra and Nagar Haveli district of the Indian union territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. Under Portuguese rule it was known as Vila de Paço d'Arcos. Once a quiet tribal town, it grew into an industrial hub after tax incentives attracted factories in textiles, plastics, and chemicals.

History

The region passed between Maratha and Portuguese control in the 18th century and remained part of Portuguese India until 1954, when local volunteers liberated Dadra and Nagar Haveli; it formally joined India in 1961. Silvassa became the administrative capital of the territory.

Economy and attractions

Excise exemptions made Silvassa a manufacturing centre with hundreds of industrial units. Nearby attractions include the Dudhni lake resort, the Tribal Cultural Museum, lion and deer safari parks at Vasona and Khanvel, and the Church of Our Lady of Piety, built by the Portuguese in the 1880s.
