Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018

ParliamentParliament of India
Enacted2018
StatusIn force

The Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018 is an Act of the Parliament of India that provides for the confiscation of properties of economic offenders who have fled the country to avoid facing criminal prosecution. It applies to offences involving amounts of ₹100 crore or more.

Provisions

Under the Act, an individual against whom an arrest warrant has been issued for a scheduled economic offence and who has left India to avoid prosecution can be declared a "fugitive economic offender" by a special court. Once declared, the government may confiscate all of the person's properties, including benami properties, and the offender is barred from pursuing civil claims in Indian courts.

Notable cases

The Act was prompted by high-profile flight cases such as those of Vijay Mallya and Nirav Modi. It was passed by Parliament in July 2018 and received presidential assent shortly afterwards.
