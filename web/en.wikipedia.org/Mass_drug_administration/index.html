Mass drug administration

AbbreviationMDA
FieldPublic health
TargetsNeglected tropical diseases

Mass drug administration (MDA) is the administration of medication to entire populations or defined groups, regardless of individual infection status, as a public health strategy to control or eliminate infectious diseases. It is a cornerstone of efforts against neglected tropical diseases.

Programmes

MDA campaigns distribute medicines such as ivermectin, albendazole, and azithromycin to combat onchocerciasis, lymphatic filariasis, trachoma, and soil-transmitted helminths, often reaching hundreds of millions of people annually.

Considerations

Programmes must monitor coverage, drug resistance, and adverse events, and are usually combined with vector control, sanitation, and health education.
