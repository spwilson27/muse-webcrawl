Molar volume

The molar volume (Vm) is the volume occupied by one mole of a substance at a given temperature and pressure, equal to its molar mass divided by its density.

Molar volume

SI unit: m³/mol
Ideal gas at STP: ≈22.7 L/mol (100 kPa, 273.15 K)

Ideal gases

For an ideal gas Vm = RT/p, the same for all gases — a consequence of Avogadro's law. At 0 °C and 1 atm it is 22.414 L/mol; the modern IUPAC STP (100 kPa) gives 22.711 L/mol.

Solids and liquids

Molar volumes of condensed phases are much smaller and element-specific, reflecting atomic radius and packing; silicon's molar volume is used in precise determinations of the Avogadro constant.
