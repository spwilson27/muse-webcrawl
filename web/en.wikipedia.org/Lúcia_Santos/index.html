Lúcia Santos

Sister Lúcia of the Immaculate Heart
Born: 28 March 1907, Aljustrel, Portugal
Died: 13 February 2005, Coimbra, Portugal
Known for: eldest of the three children who reported the Fátima apparitions

Lúcia de Jesus Rosa dos Santos (1907–2005), known as Sister Lúcia, was a Portuguese Discalced Carmelite nun who, with her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto, reported visions of the Virgin Mary at Fátima in 1917.

The Fátima apparitions

Between May and October 1917 the three shepherd children reported a series of apparitions of Our Lady, culminating in the "Miracle of the Sun" witnessed by a large crowd on 13 October 1917. Francisco and Jacinta died in the influenza pandemic shortly after; Lúcia lived on for nearly 88 more years.

Life and writings

Lúcia entered religious life, eventually joining the Carmelites at Coimbra. Her memoirs, written under obedience, are the principal record of the events and of the "three secrets" of Fátima. She died in 2005, and her cause for canonisation has been opened.
