Lemmatisation

Lemmatisation (or lemmatization) in linguistics and natural language processing is the process of grouping together the inflected forms of a word so they can be analysed as a single item, identified by its lemma, or dictionary form. For example, better lemmatises to good, and walked, walking and walks to walk.

Methods

Lemmatisers use dictionaries, morphological analysis and part-of-speech context to choose the correct lemma, unlike stemming, which crudely truncates endings. Lemmatisation is used in information retrieval, text mining, lexicography and language learning tools.
