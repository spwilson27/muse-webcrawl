Auk

An auk is a bird of the family Alcidae, which also includes puffins, guillemots, murres and auklets. Auks are pelagic seabirds of northern oceans, black and white in colour, that swim and dive using their wings.

Characteristics

Most auks are strong fliers, though the extinct great auk was flightless. They breed colonially on coastal cliffs and islands and feed on fish and krill. Because of their upright posture and colouration they are sometimes compared to penguins, to which they are not closely related — an example of convergent evolution.

Species

Living examples include the razorbill, little auk and the auks of the genus Alca. The great auk, the largest species, was hunted to extinction by the mid-19th century.
