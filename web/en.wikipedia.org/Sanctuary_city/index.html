Sanctuary city

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A sanctuary city is a municipality that limits its cooperation with national government efforts to enforce immigration law. Leaders of sanctuary cities say they want to reduce fear of deportation and possible family break-up among people who are in the country illegally, so that such people will be more willing to report crimes, use health and social services, and enroll their children in school. The term has no single legal definition and is applied to a wide range of policies in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

History

The sanctuary movement in the United States began in the 1980s, when churches and synagogues offered refuge to Central American asylum seekers fleeing civil wars whom the federal government declined to recognize as refugees. Cities such as San Francisco adopted "City of Refuge" ordinances in that decade. The concept expanded in the 2000s and 2010s as municipalities adopted policies limiting local police inquiries into immigration status and restricting compliance with federal immigration detainers.

Policies

Typical sanctuary policies include prohibiting police or city employees from questioning a person's immigration status, declining to hold individuals past their release date solely on the basis of an immigration detainer, and limiting information-sharing with federal immigration authorities. Supporters argue such policies improve public safety and community trust; critics argue they obstruct federal law enforcement and shield people who have committed crimes. The policies have been the subject of extensive litigation and political debate, particularly during periods when the federal government has threatened to withhold funding from sanctuary jurisdictions.

Outside the United States

In the United Kingdom, the City of Sanctuary movement, founded in 2005, promotes a culture of welcome for asylum seekers and refugees, though it does not involve limiting cooperation with immigration enforcement. In Canada, cities including Toronto and Hamilton have declared themselves sanctuary cities, offering municipal services regardless of immigration status.
