Strahd von Zarovich

Count Strahd von Zarovich is a fictional character originally appearing in the Dungeons & Dragons adventure module Ravenloft (1983), written by Tracy and Laura Hickman and published by TSR. A vampire lord and the tyrannical ruler of the valley of Barovia from his seat at Castle Ravenloft, Strahd became one of the best-known villains in the role-playing game and the centre of the Ravenloft campaign setting, which helped define the gothic horror genre within Dungeons & Dragons.

Origins and story

According to his backstory, Strahd was a conquering warlord and middle-aged man who, having settled in Barovia, fell in love with Tatyana, the fiancée of his younger brother Sergei. On Sergei and Tatyana's wedding day, Strahd murdered his brother and confessed his love; Tatyana threw herself from the castle walls rather than accept him. Strahd was killed by his own guards but rose as a vampire, bound to the valley by a pact with dark powers. The Dark Powers arranged for Tatyana to be endlessly reincarnated within Barovia, so that Strahd is doomed to pursue and lose her forever. His tale deliberately echoes Bram Stoker's Count Dracula and other gothic fiction.

Appearances

Strahd returned in the sequel module Ravenloft II: The House on Gryphon Hill (1986) and became the anchor of the Ravenloft boxed set (1990). He is the central antagonist of the fifth-edition adventure Curse of Strahd (2016), a reimagining of the original module that was widely praised. He also appears in novels including Vampire of the Mists by Christie Golden and I, Strahd: The Memoirs of a Vampire by P. N. Elrod, which retells his story in the first person.

Reception

Strahd is frequently ranked among the greatest villains in Dungeons & Dragons history, notable for being a villain with a full tragic backstory and a lair — Castle Ravenloft — integrated into the adventure as an explorable, mapped dungeon.
