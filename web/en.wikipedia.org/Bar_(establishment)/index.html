Bar (establishment)

A bar, also known as a saloon, a tavern or tippling house, or sometimes as a pub or club, is a retail business establishment that serves alcoholic beverages, such as beer, wine, liquor, cocktails, and other beverages such as mineral water and soft drinks. Bars often also sell snack foods, such as crisps or peanuts, at the counter.

Description

The term "bar" derives from the counter, typically of wood or other material, across which drinks are served by a bartender to patrons seated on stools. Bars are often subject to licensing laws governing serving hours, age limits, and responsible service.

Types

Varieties include cocktail bars and lounges, wine bars, beer bars and brewpubs, dive bars, sports bars, speakeasies (historically, illegal establishments during Prohibition in the United States), and themed or hotel bars. In many countries, pubs and taverns are related but distinct traditions of drinking establishments.
