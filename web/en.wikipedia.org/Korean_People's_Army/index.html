Korean People's Army

The Korean People's Army (KPA) is the military force of North Korea (the Democratic People's Republic of Korea). Founded on 8 February 1948, it is among the largest militaries in the world by personnel, with roughly 1.3 million active troops and several million reservists and paramilitary personnel.

Structure

The KPA comprises the Ground Force, the Navy, the Air Force, the Strategic Force (responsible for ballistic missiles), and Special Operations forces, all under the State Affairs Commission and, historically, guided by the songun ("military-first") policy. It operates large numbers of artillery, armoured vehicles, submarines and ballistic missiles, and North Korea has developed nuclear weapons in defiance of international sanctions.

History

The KPA fought the Korean War (1950–53) against South Korea and a United States-led United Nations command, a conflict ended by armistice but no peace treaty, leaving the peninsula technically at war.
