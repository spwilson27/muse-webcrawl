Cream (band)

Cream were a British rock supergroup formed in London in 1966, comprising Eric Clapton (guitar, vocals), Jack Bruce (bass, vocals) and Ginger Baker (drums). They are regarded as the first successful supergroup and pioneers of blues rock and hard rock.

Career

Albums include Fresh Cream (1966), Disraeli Gears (1967) with "Sunshine of Your Love" and "Strange Brew", and Wheels of Fire (1968), a double album that topped US charts and included "White Room". Internal tensions led to their breakup in 1968 after a farewell tour. They were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1993 and reunited for concerts in 2005.
