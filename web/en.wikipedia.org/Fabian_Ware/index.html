Fabian Ware

Major-General Sir Fabian Arthur Goulstone Ware KCVO KBE CB CMG (20 June 1869 – 28 April 1949) was a British educator, journalist, and the founder of the Imperial War Graves Commission (now the Commonwealth War Graves Commission).

Born20 June 1869, Clifton, Bristol
Died28 April 1949, Amberley, Gloucestershire
Known forFounding the Imperial War Graves Commission

Life and career

Ware worked as an assistant master, examiner, and journalist, including as editor of the Morning Post. Too old for active service at the outbreak of the First World War, he commanded a mobile unit of the British Red Cross in France, where he began recording and caring for the graves of the fallen.

War graves work

His unit's work was taken over by the Army in 1915, and in 1917 the Imperial War Graves Commission was chartered. Ware served as its vice-chairman and driving force, establishing the principles of permanent commemoration, uniform headstones, and no repatriation of bodies.
