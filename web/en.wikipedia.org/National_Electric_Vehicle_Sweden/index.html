National Electric Vehicle Sweden

National Electric Vehicle Sweden AB (NEVS) was a Swedish electric vehicle manufacturer based in Trollhättan. It acquired the main assets of the bankrupt Saab Automobile in 2012 and set out to build electric cars, initially based on the Saab 9-3 platform.

History

NEVS was founded in 2012 by a consortium led by Chinese-Swedish businessman Kai Johan Jiang. After buying Saab's assets, it briefly restarted production of petrol 9-3s before focusing on an electric version, the NEVS 9-3, produced in Trollhättan and later in Tianjin, China. The company lost the right to use the Saab name in 2014–2016 and thereafter marketed vehicles under the NEVS brand.

Decline

From 2021 the company was drawn into the liquidity crisis of its owner, the Chinese property group Evergrande, whose automotive arm held a majority stake. NEVS entered a period of "hibernation" with mass layoffs in 2023 and was placed in reorganisation, effectively ending car production.
