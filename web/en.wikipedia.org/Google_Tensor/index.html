Google Tensor is a series of ARM64-based system-on-a-chip (SoC) processors designed and marketed by Google for its Pixel devices. The first-generation Tensor chip debuted in the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro in October 2021, marking Google's shift from Qualcomm Snapdragon processors to its own silicon co-developed with Samsung's semiconductor division.

Design

Tensor chips emphasize on-device machine learning. Each includes a Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) for AI workloads such as speech recognition, computational photography, live translation, and image processing, alongside CPU and GPU clusters based on ARM designs and a Titan M2 security coprocessor in later generations.

Generations

Successive generations — Tensor G2 (Pixel 7, 2022), Tensor G3 (Pixel 8, 2023), and Tensor G4 (Pixel 9, 2024) — improved efficiency and AI performance. Later generations moved fabrication from Samsung Foundry toward TSMC.
