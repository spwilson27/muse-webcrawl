The London Borough of Lewisham is a London borough in south-east London, England. It forms part of Inner London. The principal settlement of the borough is Lewisham, with other districts including Catford, its administrative centre, Deptford, New Cross, Forest Hill, Sydenham, Bellingham, Brockley, Ladywell, Hither Green, Lee, Blackheath (partly) and Downham.

History

The borough was formed in 1965 by the London Government Act 1963 from the former Metropolitan Boroughs of Lewisham and Deptford, both previously part of the County of London, with Deptford historically associated with Kent and Surrey borders and a long maritime history on the Thames.

Geography

The borough is bordered by the Royal Borough of Greenwich to the east, Bromley to the south, Southwark to the west, and across the Thames, Tower Hamlets. The Rivers Ravensbourne and Quaggy flow through it. Hills at Forest Hill and Sydenham offer views over central London.

Transport

Lewisham station is a major interchange for Southeastern rail and the Docklands Light Railway. Other stations include Catford, Forest Hill, Sydenham, New Cross and Deptford, with London Overground services on the East London line.

Places of interest

The Horniman Museum and Gardens in Forest Hill, Mountsfield Park, Ladywell Fields, Beckenham Place Park (partly in the borough), and the Broadway Theatre in Catford are notable. Lewisham has a diverse population and a large street market.
