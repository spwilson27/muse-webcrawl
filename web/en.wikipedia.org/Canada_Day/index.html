Canada Day
DateJuly 1

Canada Day (French: Fête du Canada) is the national day of Canada, celebrated annually on July 1. It commemorates the anniversary of Canadian Confederation, which occurred on July 1, 1867, when the British North America Act united the Province of Canada, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick into a single dominion within the British Empire.

History

The holiday was established in 1879 and was long known as Dominion Day. It was renamed Canada Day in 1982, the year the Canadian constitution was patriated with the Canada Act. Major celebrations have marked milestone anniversaries, including the centennial in 1967 and Canada 150 in 2017.

Celebrations

Canada Day is a statutory holiday in all provinces and territories. Events typically include parades, concerts, citizenship ceremonies, picnics and fireworks, with the largest official celebrations held on Parliament Hill in Ottawa. In Quebec the day coincides with the traditional moving day for leases. Canadian expatriates also hold celebrations abroad, notably in Trafalgar Square, London.
