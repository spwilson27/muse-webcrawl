Airline

An airline is a company that provides air transport services for passengers and freight. Airlines use aircraft to supply these services and may form partnerships or alliances with other carriers.

Types

Major categories include legacy (full-service) carriers, low-cost carriers, regional airlines, charter airlines and cargo airlines. Many airlines are organised into three global alliances: Star Alliance, SkyTeam and Oneworld.

Operations

Airlines operate under an air operator's certificate issued by national regulators and typically use hub-and-spoke or point-to-point networks. The industry is capital-intensive and sensitive to fuel prices, economic cycles and regulation; it has been collectively unprofitable over much of its history despite carrying billions of passengers a year.
