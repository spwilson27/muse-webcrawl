Staircase

Staircase
Components: Treads, risers, stringers, handrail
Forms: Straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, spiral
Purpose: Vertical circulation in buildings

A staircase (or stairway) is a construction designed to bridge a vertical distance by dividing it into smaller distances in the form of steps. Staircases may be straight, round, or consist of several flights at angles.

Components

Each step consists of a horizontal tread and a vertical riser. Supporting members called stringers carry the steps, while handrails and balustrades provide safety. Flights are separated by landings.

Types

Types include straight runs, L- and U-shaped stairs with landings, spiral and helical stairs, and grand ceremonial staircases in palaces. Building codes regulate riser height, tread depth and headroom for safety.
