Council of Ministers of the Soviet Union

The Council of Ministers of the USSR was the highest executive and administrative body of the Soviet Union from 1946 to 1991. It replaced the Council of People's Commissars.

Structure

Headed by a Chairman — the premier — it included deputy chairmen, ministers in charge of ministries, and heads of state committees. It was formed by the Supreme Soviet, coordinated economic planning through Gosplan, and implemented laws and party policy. Premiers included Joseph Stalin, Georgy Malenkov, Nikita Khrushchev, Alexei Kosygin, Nikolai Ryzhkov and Valentin Pavlov.

It was dissolved in 1991 as the USSR collapsed.
