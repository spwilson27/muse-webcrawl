Chen Qun

Chen Qun (died 237), courtesy name Changwen, was an official of the Cao Wei state during the Three Kingdoms period of China. Serving Cao Cao and his successors, he is best known for instituting the Nine-Rank System for selecting officials.

The system graded candidates into nine ranks by local evaluators. Chen Qun also contributed to revising Wei law. He rose to high office as Minister of Works and was enfeoffed as Marquis of Yingyin.
