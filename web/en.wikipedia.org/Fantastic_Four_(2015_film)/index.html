Fantastic Four (stylized as FANT4STIC) is a 2015 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics team of the same name. It is the third theatrical Fantastic Four film and a reboot of the series. The film was directed by Josh Trank, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jeremy Slater and Simon Kinberg. It stars Miles Teller, Michael B. Jordan, Kate Mara, Jamie Bell, and Toby Kebbell.

Plot

Childhood friends Reed Richards and Ben Grimm work together on a prototype teleporter, which attracts the attention of Franklin Storm, director of the Baxter Foundation. Reed joins a team including Storm's children Sue and Johnny and the brilliant but reclusive Victor von Doom to build the Quantum Gate. A trip to the alternate dimension "Planet Zero" goes wrong: the team returns transformed — Reed can stretch, Sue can turn invisible and project force fields, Johnny can engulf himself in flame, and Ben becomes a rocky superhuman. Doom, left behind and fused with his environment suit, returns with destructive powers and must be stopped from opening a portal that would destroy Earth.

Production and reception

Production was troubled, with reports of clashes between Trank and the studio, and extensive reshoots reshaped the film's third act. Upon release, Fantastic Four was panned by critics, holding a 9% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes, and underperformed at the box office, grossing $167.9 million worldwide against a budget of $120–155 million. It won three Golden Raspberry Awards, including Worst Picture and Worst Director. A planned sequel was cancelled, and the film rights later reverted to Marvel Studios following Disney's acquisition of 21st Century Fox.
