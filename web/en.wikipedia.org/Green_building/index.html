Green building
Also: sustainable building
Rating systems: LEED, BREEAM, DGNB, Green Star
Focus: Energy, water, materials, health

Green building (also known as green construction, sustainable building or eco-friendly building) refers to both a structure and the application of processes that are environmentally responsible and resource-efficient throughout a building's life cycle: planning, design, construction, operation, maintenance, renovation and demolition.

Goals

Green buildings reduce energy and water use, use non-toxic and recycled materials, protect occupant health, cut waste and pollution, and preserve surrounding ecosystems. Techniques include passive solar design, insulation, daylighting, rainwater harvesting, green roofs and efficient HVAC.

Certification

LEED (US), BREEAM (UK, the oldest, from 1990) and other systems rate buildings. Critics note certification can reward points over measured performance — the 'performance gap'.

Costs and benefits

Upfront costs may be higher, but operating savings, health and productivity gains, and higher asset values are often reported.
