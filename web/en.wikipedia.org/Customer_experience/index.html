Customer experience

Illustration: Customer experience

Customer experience (CX) is the sum of a customer's perceptions and feelings resulting from interactions with a brand's products, services, employees, and channels across the whole customer journey, from awareness and purchase to use and support.

Management

Customer experience management (CXM or CEM) is the discipline of designing and reacting to interactions to meet or exceed expectations and increase satisfaction, loyalty, and advocacy. Firms measure CX with metrics such as Net Promoter Score (NPS), Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT), and Customer Effort Score (CES).

Distinctions

CX is broader than customer service, which covers individual support encounters, and includes digital experience on websites and apps, product design, pricing, and delivery. Journey mapping and voice-of-the-customer programmes are common tools.
