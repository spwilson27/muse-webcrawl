Fox Sports is the sports programming division of the Fox Corporation responsible for sports broadcasts carried by the Fox broadcast network and its affiliated national sports cable channels in the United States. Launched in 1994 with Fox's acquisition of broadcast rights to the National Football League, the division grew into one of the dominant sports media brands in North America.

History

Fox Sports began in 1994 when the Fox network, then a young fourth network, outbid CBS for the rights to televise NFL games. The move gave Fox instant credibility with viewers and affiliates. The division subsequently added Major League Baseball, NASCAR, college football and basketball, soccer including the FIFA World Cup, and championship boxing.

Channels and properties

Its cable properties include FS1 and FS2 (launched in 2013 as replacements for Speed and Fuel TV), the Spanish-language Fox Deportes, and the Fox Sports Radio network. Fox Sports has televised the World Series every year since 2000 and the Super Bowl on a rotating basis with other NFL broadcasters.

Notable coverage

The division is known for its NFL pregame show Fox NFL Sunday, its baseball coverage led for many years by Joe Buck, and its broadcasts of the FIFA World Cup in 2018 and 2022, which drew record English-language soccer audiences in the United States.
