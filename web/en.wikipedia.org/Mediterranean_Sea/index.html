The Mediterranean Sea is a sea connected to the Atlantic Ocean, surrounded by the Mediterranean Basin and almost completely enclosed by land: on the north by Southern Europe and Anatolia, on the south by North Africa, and on the east by the Levant in West Asia. It covers about 2.5 million km². Its name derives from Latin mediterraneus, "inland" or "in the middle of the land".

Geography

The Mediterranean is connected to the Atlantic through the 14 km-wide Strait of Gibraltar. The Strait of Sicily divides it into western and eastern basins. Its major subdivisions include the Balearic, Ligurian, Tyrrhenian, Adriatic, Ionian, Aegean, and Levantine seas. Large islands include Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica, Cyprus, Crete, Mallorca, and Malta. Major inflowing rivers are the Nile, Ebro, Rhône, and Po. The Suez Canal (1869) joins it to the Red Sea, enabling "Lessepsian migration" of Red Sea species.

Geology

The sea is a remnant of the ancient Tethys Ocean, compressed as Africa converges with Eurasia. About 5.97 million years ago, during the Messinian salinity crisis, the Mediterranean largely dried out, depositing vast salt layers, before the Zanclean flood refilled it through Gibraltar about 5.33 million years ago. The region remains tectonically and volcanically active (Etna, Vesuvius, Santorini).

Climate and ecology

The surrounding lands give their name to the Mediterranean climate of hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. The sea is relatively nutrient-poor but rich in endemic species — about 17,000 marine species in total. Its waters are warming faster than the global ocean average, and overfishing and invasive species are major pressures.

History

The Mediterranean was the cradle of some of the earliest human civilisations — Egypt, Phoenicia, Greece, and Rome, whose empire made it Mare Nostrum ("Our Sea"). It remained a crossroads of trade and conflict through the Byzantine, Arab, Crusader, and Ottoman eras, and the opening of the Suez Canal restored its centrality to world shipping; around a third of world merchant shipping transits it annually.
