Copper River Highway

RouteAlaska Route 10
Length~49.5 mi (79.7 km)
FromCordova, Alaska
TowardChilds Glacier / Million Dollar Bridge

The Copper River Highway, designated Alaska Route 10, extends from Cordova, Alaska, following the former route of the Copper River and Northwestern Railway toward the Million Dollar Bridge near Childs Glacier. Much of the road follows the old railbed along the lower Copper River delta.

History

The railway was built in the early 1900s to carry copper ore from the Kennecott mines to the port at Cordova. After the railroad ceased operations in 1938, the right-of-way was gradually converted to a highway, with construction advancing in stages from the 1940s through the 1960s.

Damage and closures

The 1964 Alaska earthquake damaged the Million Dollar Bridge, dropping one span; it was later repaired with a temporary ramp arrangement. In 2011, flooding washed out approaches at one of the river crossings, cutting off road access to the far portion of the highway, and repairs have remained a long-standing issue.
