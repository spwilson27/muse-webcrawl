List of Star Trek races

The Star Trek franchise, created by Gene Roddenberry in 1966, features hundreds of fictional alien species across its television series and films. This article lists notable races.

Federation species

Humans, Vulcans (logical, pointed-eared people of Vulcan), Andorians, Tellarites, Betazoids, Trill and Bajorans are prominent United Federation of Planets members.

Other major powers

The Klingon Empire (warrior culture), the Romulan Star Empire, the Cardassian Union, the Ferengi Alliance (commerce-obsessed), the Dominion (ruled by the Founders/Changelings, with Jem'Hadar and Vorta), and the cybernetic Borg Collective are central recurring powers.

Other species

Other notable species include the Q Continuum, Breen, Gorn, Tribbles, Species 8472 (Undine), Xindi and the Tholians.
