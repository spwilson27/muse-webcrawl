ADCC Submission Fighting World Championship

The ADCC Submission Fighting World Championship is a submission grappling competition organized by the Abu Dhabi Combat Club. Founded in 1998 by Sheikh Tahnoun bin Zayed Al Nahyan, the tournament is widely regarded as the most prestigious competition in submission grappling (no-gi grappling).

Competitors fight in weight classes under a ruleset that rewards aggressive submission hunting in the finals, with points scored for dominant positions in the second half of matches. Champions such as Marcelo Garcia, Gordon Ryan, and André Galvão are among the most decorated athletes in the sport's history. The championship is held biennially, with trials staged on every inhabited continent.
