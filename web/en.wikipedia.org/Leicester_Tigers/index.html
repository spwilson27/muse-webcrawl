Leicester Tigers

Leicester Tigers (officially Leicester Football Club) are an English professional rugby union club based in Leicester. Founded in 1880, they play in Premiership Rugby and at Welford Road stadium, wearing green, scarlet and white.

History

The Tigers are one of England's most successful clubs, winning multiple league titles — including a record run in the professional era — numerous domestic cups, and the European Cup twice, in 2001 and 2002. Famous players include Martin Johnson, Neil Back, Austin Healey and Manu Tuilagi. The nickname Tigers derives from the club's early association with the Leicestershire Regiment and its tiger emblem.

Honours

Premiership champions on multiple occasions, Anglo-Welsh Cup winners, and European champions 2000–01 and 2001–02.
