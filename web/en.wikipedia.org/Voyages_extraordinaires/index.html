Voyages extraordinaires

Voyages extraordinaires (with a lowercase "e") is an alternate capitalisation of the title of Jules Verne's celebrated series of adventure novels, the Voyages Extraordinaires. In French typographic convention, only the first word of a title and proper nouns are capitalised, so French sources frequently write the series name as Voyages extraordinaires.

About the series

The series, published by Pierre-Jules Hetzel from 1863, comprises fifty-four novels by Jules Verne, including Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas, Around the World in Eighty Days and Journey to the Centre of the Earth. Its goal was to present the scientific and geographical knowledge of the nineteenth century in narrative form.
