Doukas

The Doukas family was one of the great aristocratic houses of Byzantium

The Doukas family (Greek: Δούκας, pl. Δούκαι; feminine Δούκαινα), Latinised as Ducas, was one of the most prominent aristocratic families of the Byzantine Empire, active from the 9th century to the fall of Constantinople and beyond. The name derives from the Latin title dux ("leader", "general"). The family produced generals, governors and, in the 11th century, a line of emperors: Constantine X Doukas (r. 1059–1067) and his son Michael VII Doukas (r. 1071–1078).

Origins and rise

The Doukai first appear in the mid-9th century. Constantine Doukas, son of the general Andronikos Doukas, attempted to seize the throne in 913. In the 10th century members of the family served as domestikoi ton scholon (commanders-in-chief) and governors of themes. Bardas Doukas and his sons Nikephoros and Constantine were leading generals under the Macedonian dynasty.

Imperial dynasty

Constantine X ascended the throne in 1059 after the abdication of Isaac I Komnenos. His reign saw military retrenchment, and after his death his widow Eudokia Makrembolitissa ruled as regent before marrying Romanos IV Diogenes. Michael VII proved unable to halt Norman and Turkish advances; he was deposed in 1078. The Doukas family thereafter remained intermarried with the ruling Komnenos dynasty — Irene Doukaina was the wife of Alexios I Komnenos — and Doukai continued to hold the highest offices, such as sebastokrator and protostrator, throughout the Komnenian and Palaiologan periods.

Later branches

A branch of the family, the Komnenos Doukai, ruled the Despotate of Epirus in the 13th century under Michael I Komnenos Doukas and his successors. Other members founded lines in Thessaly and served in the Empire of Nicaea. The surname survives in modern Greek families.

Notable members

  • Andronikos Doukas (9th–10th c.), general
  • Constantine X Doukas, emperor 1059–1067
  • Michael VII Doukas, emperor 1071–1078
  • Irene Doukaina, empress consort of Alexios I
  • Michael I Komnenos Doukas, founder of the Despotate of Epirus
