Manmohan Singh

Born26 September 1932, Gah, Punjab, British India
Died26 December 2024, New Delhi, India
OfficePrime Minister of India (2004–2014)

Manmohan Singh (26 September 1932 – 26 December 2024) was an Indian economist and politician who served as the 13th prime minister of India from 2004 to 2014. A member of the Indian National Congress, he was the first Sikh prime minister of India and the first since Jawaharlal Nehru to be re-elected after completing a full five-year term.

Early life and education

Singh was born in Gah, in the Punjab province of British India (now in Pakistan), and migrated to India during the Partition of 1947. He studied economics at Panjab University, the University of Cambridge, and earned a DPhil from the University of Oxford, where his thesis examined India's export performance.

Economic reforms

As finance minister in the government of P. V. Narasimha Rao, Singh was the architect of the 1991 economic liberalisation programme, which dismantled much of the Licence Raj, devalued the rupee and opened India to foreign investment amid a balance-of-payments crisis.

Prime ministership

His two terms (2004–2014) saw high economic growth, the India–United States civil nuclear agreement, and major social programmes such as the rural employment guarantee scheme (MGNREGA) and the Right to Information Act. His second term was clouded by corruption scandals and slowing growth.
