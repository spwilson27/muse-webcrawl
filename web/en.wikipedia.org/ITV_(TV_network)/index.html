ITV (TV network)

ITV, legally known as Channel 3, is a British free-to-air public broadcast television network. It was launched in 1955 as Independent Television to provide competition to BBC Television (established in 1936). ITV is the oldest commercial television network in the UK. For decades it was a network of separate, regionally based companies that shared a common schedule and franchise obligations; today most of the network is owned and operated by ITV plc.

History

ITV was created by the Television Act 1954 and regulated initially by the Independent Television Authority (ITA). The first station, Associated-Rediffusion in London, began broadcasting on 22 September 1955. Regional franchises — among them Granada in the North West, ATV in the Midlands and ABC in the North — produced a mix of local and networked programmes. The network became famous for drama such as Coronation Street (1960–present), light entertainment, and current affairs programmes like World in Action.

Franchise rounds in 1968, 1980 and 1991 reshaped the network. The Broadcasting Act 1990 replaced the IBA with the Independent Television Commission and introduced a controversial franchise auction, which cost several established companies — including TV-am, Thames and TVS — their licences from 1993. Thereafter, successive mergers consolidated ownership: Granada and Carlton merged in 2004 to form ITV plc, which gradually acquired most remaining Channel 3 licensees, including Channel Television (2011) and UTV (2016). STV, serving Scotland, remains separately owned but closely affiliated.

Programming and channels

The main ITV channel in England, Wales and the Channel Islands is branded ITV1 (previously simply "ITV"). ITV plc also operates ITV2, ITV3, ITV4 and ITVBe, plus the children's channel CITV until its closure as a linear channel in 2023. The network is funded almost entirely by advertising. Its news is provided by ITN, and it is required as a public service broadcaster to carry news, current affairs, regional programming and other quotas.
