Glossary of sumo terms

The following is a glossary of terms used in sumo, the traditional Japanese full-contact wrestling sport. Terms are given in romanised Japanese with their usual meanings in professional sumo as governed by the Japan Sumo Association.

Selected terms

TermMeaning
banzukeThe ranking list of wrestlers, published before each tournament.
bashoA sumo tournament; six honbasho (official tournaments) are held each year.
dohyōThe raised clay ring in which bouts take place, about 4.55 metres in diameter.
dohyō-iriThe ceremonial ring-entering ritual performed by top-division wrestlers.
gyōjiThe referee who officiates bouts and calls the action.
heyaA stable where wrestlers live and train communally.
kachi-koshiA winning record — more wins than losses in a tournament.
kimariteThe winning technique used to decide a bout; dozens are officially recognised.
make-koshiA losing record in a tournament.
mawashiThe thick belt worn by wrestlers, the main target for grips.
ōzekiThe second-highest rank in sumo.
rikishiA professional sumo wrestler.
shikiriThe pre-bout ritual of crouching, stamping and psychological preparation.
shikoThe leg-stomping exercise and ritual used to drive evil spirits from the ring.
tachiaiThe initial charge at the start of a bout.
yokozunaThe highest rank in sumo, "grand champion", whose holders are expected to retire rather than be demoted.
yorikiriA common winning technique: forcing the opponent out of the ring while maintaining a grip on the mawashi.
yūshōA tournament championship.
