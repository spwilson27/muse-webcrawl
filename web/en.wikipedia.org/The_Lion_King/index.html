The Lion King is a 1994 American animated musical drama film produced by Walt Disney Feature Animation and released by Walt Disney Pictures. It is the 32nd Disney animated feature film and tells the story of Simba, a young lion prince of the Pride Lands in Africa.

Plot

Simba, heir of King Mufasa, is manipulated by his uncle Scar into believing he caused Mufasa's death and flees into exile. Raised by the meerkat Timon and the warthog Pumbaa under the philosophy of "hakuna matata", the adult Simba eventually returns to challenge Scar and take his place in the Circle of Life.

Music

The film's songs, including "Circle of Life", "I Just Can't Wait to Be King", "Hakuna Matata", and "Can You Feel the Love Tonight", were written by Elton John and Tim Rice. Hans Zimmer's score won the Academy Award for Best Original Score, and "Can You Feel the Love Tonight" won Best Original Song.

Legacy

The Lion King became the highest-grossing animated film of its time and spawned a Broadway musical, sequels, television series, and a 2019 photorealistic remake.
