Campeones Cup


Placeholder image: The Campeones Cup trophy

The Campeones Cup is an annual super cup match in North American soccer, contested between the champions of Major League Soccer (MLS) and the winner of the Campeón de Campeones of Liga MX. The competition was established in 2018 as part of a broader partnership between the two leagues.

Format

The match is a single game hosted by the MLS champion. Tigres UANL won the inaugural edition in 2018, defeating Toronto FC in Toronto. Subsequent winners have included Atlanta United, the Columbus Crew, New York City FC and Tigres again. The competition is related to, but distinct from, the Leagues Cup, the joint MLS–Liga MX tournament expanded in 2023.
