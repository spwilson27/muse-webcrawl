Mihrimah Sultan (Ottoman Turkish: مهرماه سلطان; c. 1522 – 25 January 1578) was an Ottoman princess, the only daughter of Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent and his wife Hürrem Sultan. She was one of the most powerful women of the Ottoman Empire and a prominent figure of the period often called the Sultanate of Women.

Her name, Mihrimah (Persian: مهر ماه), means "sun and moon". She was said to be her father's favourite child and corresponded with him throughout his campaigns, at one point even accompanying him as far as the empire's eastern frontiers in her youth.

Marriage and political influence

In 1539 Mihrimah married Rüstem Pasha, the governor of Diyarbakır, who later twice served as Grand Vizier. The marriage allied the grand vizier's faction closely with her mother Hürrem's circle at court. After Hürrem's death in 1558, Mihrimah effectively assumed her mother's position as her father's closest confidante and the senior woman of the dynasty; Suleiman is reported to have consulted her on matters of state.

During the succession struggle among Suleiman's sons, Mihrimah supported her full brothers Selim and Bayezid in turn; after Selim II ascended the throne in 1566 she became the wealthiest woman in the empire, lending money to the state treasury and acting as a link between the sultan and the administration. She also maintained diplomatic correspondence, including exchanges with the king of Poland.

Patronage

Mihrimah was a major patron of architecture. The imperial architect Mimar Sinan built two mosque complexes in her name in Istanbul: the Mihrimah Sultan Mosque at Üsküdar (completed 1548) and the Mihrimah Mosque at Edirnekapı (completed about 1565), notable for its exceptionally light-filled interior. She also endowed caravanserais, baths, schools and charitable foundations, and her immense personal fortune funded building works as far away as Mecca, where she financed water-supply repairs.

Death

Mihrimah Sultan died in Constantinople on 25 January 1578, during the reign of her nephew Murad III. She was buried beside her father in the mausoleum of the Süleymaniye Mosque.
