BBC Radio 1Xtra

First air date 16 August 2002
Format Black music and rhythmic contemporary
Owner BBC

BBC Radio 1Xtra is a British digital radio station owned and operated by the BBC. Launched on 16 August 2002 as a sister station to BBC Radio 1, it broadcasts black British and urban music, including hip hop, grime, Afrobeats, dancehall, R&B, drum and bass, and garage, and is aimed primarily at listeners aged 15–30.

History

The station, originally codenamed "Network X", was launched as part of the BBC's expansion into digital radio. It was created to serve audiences and genres that were under-represented on other BBC stations, and has played a major role in bringing UK underground genres — particularly grime and UK garage — to a national audience.

Programming

1Xtra's schedule mixes music shows with specialist programming and news tailored to younger audiences. Many of its presenters and DJs share programmes with Radio 1, and the station simulcasts some Radio 1 output. It also stages its own events, including 1Xtra Live.

Availability

The station is available on DAB digital radio, digital television, online, and through BBC Sounds. It does not broadcast on FM or AM.
