Royal Parks of London

LocationLondon, England
Managed byThe Royal Parks (charity)
Number of parks8
Total areaapprox. 5,000 acres (2,000 ha)

The Royal Parks of London are lands that were originally owned by the monarchy of the United Kingdom for the recreation — mostly hunting — of the royal family. They are part of the hereditary possessions of the Crown. The eight Royal Parks cover almost 2,000 hectares (4,900 acres) of land in London and are managed by The Royal Parks, a charity established in 2017 which took over from the Royal Parks Agency.

The eight parks

The Royal Parks are:

  • Bushy Park — in the London Borough of Richmond upon Thames, famous for its deer herds and the Diana Fountain.
  • Green Park — a peaceful triangle of mature trees and grassland adjoining Buckingham Palace.
  • Greenwich Park — the oldest enclosed Royal Park, home to the Prime Meridian, the Royal Observatory and herds of deer.
  • Hyde Park — one of the largest central London parks, famous for Speakers' Corner and the Serpentine lake.
  • Kensington Gardens — adjoining Hyde Park, containing Kensington Palace, the Albert Memorial and the Diana Memorial Playground.
  • Regent's Park — home to London Zoo, an open-air theatre, and sports facilities.
  • Richmond Park — the largest Royal Park, a National Nature Reserve with ancient trees and herds of red and fallow deer.
  • St James's Park — the oldest Royal Park in central London, lying in front of Buckingham Palace and known for its pelicans.

History

Most of the parks were enclosed as royal hunting grounds in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. Henry VIII acquired and enclosed several areas, including St James's Park and Hyde Park. Public access grew gradually; Richmond Park was opened to the general public by court decision in the eighteenth century, and by the nineteenth century the parks had become valued public open spaces. The parks are protected by legislation, including the Crown Lands Act 1851, and provide habitats for wildlife as well as major venues for national celebrations and ceremonies.
