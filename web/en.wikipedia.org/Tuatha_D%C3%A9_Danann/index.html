The Tuatha Dé Danann ("the tribes of the goddess Danu") are a supernatural race in Irish mythology, representing the main deities of pre-Christian Gaelic Ireland. They appear in the Mythological Cycle, especially the Lebor Gabála Érenn ("Book of Invasions"), as the fifth group to settle Ireland.

Mythology

According to legend, the Tuatha Dé arrived in Ireland in dark clouds or ships, bringing four magical treasures: the Lia Fáil (Stone of Destiny), the sword of Nuada, the spear of Lugh and the cauldron of the Dagda. They defeated the Fir Bolg at the First Battle of Mag Tuired and the Fomorians at the Second.

Later tradition

Defeated in turn by the human Milesians, the Tuatha Dé withdrew into the sídhe (fairy mounds) and became the Aos Sí of later folklore, ruled underground while humans held the surface world.
