Michelle Yeoh Choo Kheng (born 6 August 1962) is a Malaysian actress. Rising to fame in 1980s and 1990s Hong Kong action films as a performer who executed her own stunts, she gained international recognition in the James Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies (1997) and Ang Lee's Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000). In 2023 she became the first Asian performer to win the Academy Award for Best Actress, for Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022).

Early life and career

Yeoh was born in Ipoh to a Malaysian Chinese family and trained as a ballet dancer in England, where a spinal injury ended her dance ambitions. Crowned Miss Malaysia in 1983, she began acting in Hong Kong under the name Michelle Khan, starring in films such as Yes, Madam (1985), Magnificent Warriors (1987), and Police Story 3: Supercop (1992) alongside Jackie Chan. After a brief retirement following her marriage to Dickson Poon, she returned to acting in the early 1990s.

International success

Her role as Wai Lin in Tomorrow Never Dies and as Yu Shu Lien in Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon established her in the West. Subsequent credits include Memoirs of a Geisha (2005), Sunshine (2007), The Lady (2011), in which she played Aung San Suu Kyi, Crazy Rich Asians (2018), Star Trek: Discovery, Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings (2021), and Wicked (2024) as Madame Morrible.

Everything Everywhere and honours

For playing laundromat owner Evelyn Wang in Everything Everywhere All at Once, Yeoh won the Academy Award, Golden Globe, and Screen Actors Guild Award, among many others. She is a Goodwill Ambassador for the United Nations Development Programme and was elected a member of the International Olympic Committee in 2023. In France she has been made a Commander of the Legion of Honour.
