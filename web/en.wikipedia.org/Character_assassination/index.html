Character assassination

Character assassination (CA) is a deliberate and sustained effort to damage the reputation or credibility of an individual or group. Such attacks may involve exaggeration, misleading half-truths, or manipulation of facts to present a false picture of the target, and can occur in politics, business, academia and personal relationships.

Methods

Common techniques include spreading rumours, innuendo and smears, guilt by association, selective quotation, and raising irrelevant personal matters. In politics the practice overlaps with "negative campaigning" and the work of opposition researchers. When conducted by state actors it may form part of propaganda or kompromat.

Study and law

Character assassination has become a recognised field of academic study, with scholars examining historical and contemporary cases. Legally, false statements of fact that damage reputation may be actionable as defamation, though public figures in many jurisdictions must meet a higher standard of proof.
