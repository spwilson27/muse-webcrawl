Light verse

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Light verse is poetry on light or playful subjects, characterized by wit, technical dexterity, and usually flawless form. Although its subject matter is trivial or humorous, successful light verse is often technically demanding, requiring control of meter, rhyme, and wordplay. The term distinguishes such work from "serious" poetry while acknowledging that craft is independent of subject.

History

Light verse has ancient antecedents in Greek and Latin epigrams and the playful poems of Catullus. In English, John Skelton, the metaphysical poets' lighter pieces, and Jonathan Swift's satirical verse kept the mode alive; the 19th century produced Edward Lear's nonsense verse and limericks, Lewis Carroll's verse in the Alice books, W. S. Gilbert's Bab Ballads and comic-opera lyrics, and the "vers de société" of writers such as Frederick Locker-Lampson. In the early 20th century, light verse flourished in magazines like Punch and The New Yorker, with practitioners including G. K. Chesterton, Hilaire Belloc, Dorothy Parker, Ogden Nash (famous for deliberately irregular line lengths), Phyllis McGinley, and John Betjeman. W. H. Auden, who edited the anthology The Oxford Book of Light Verse (1938), argued that light verse flourishes when poets share a common social and linguistic world with their audience.

Forms

Forms particularly associated with light verse include the limerick, the clerihew, the double dactyl, the triolet, parodies of famous poems, and comic ballads. Comic effects commonly exploit puns, feminine and multi-syllable rhymes, and deliberate bathos. Later practitioners include Wendy Cope, whose parodies and "strugnell" poems renewed the mode in Britain, and the American poets X. J. Kennedy and J. Patrick Lewis.

Assessment

Critics have debated whether light verse is a minor entertainment or a genuine branch of poetry; Kingsley Amis, editor of The New Oxford Book of English Light Verse (1978), held that the label describes subject, not quality, and that the best light verse gives pleasures comparable to serious lyric.
