Pal (4 June 1940 – 18 June 1958) was a male Rough Collie who became the first dog to portray the fictional heroine Lassie on screen, starring in the MGM film Lassie Come Home (1943) and six sequels, as well as the pilot episodes of the long-running television series.

Career

Pal was brought as a young dog to animal trainer Rudd Weatherwax because his owners could not break him of chasing motorcycles. MGM, casting for Lassie Come Home, initially passed Pal over for the lead in favour of a show collie — but when the chosen dog balked at a flooded-river scene, Pal performed it in one take, swimming the river and delivering a convincing performance, and the studio recast the role. Although Lassie is female, male collies were preferred for the part because of their fuller coats and larger size.

Pal starred in seven MGM Lassie films between 1943 and 1951 and filmed the two pilots for the CBS television series in 1954 before retiring. His descendants, trained by the Weatherwax family, have played Lassie ever since — every dog to portray the character has been a male descendant of Pal. Weatherwax was famously devoted to him, and Pal died of natural causes in 1958, aged eighteen.
