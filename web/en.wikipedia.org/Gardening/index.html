Gardening

Gardening is the practice of growing and cultivating plants in a garden, yard, allotment, container or other managed space. It ranges from subsistence food production and kitchen gardens to ornamental planting, lawns, community gardens and large designed landscapes.

Activities

Gardeners prepare soil, sow seeds, transplant seedlings, water, fertilise, prune, mulch, weed and manage pests and diseases. Seasonal timing, local climate, soil texture, sunlight and drainage shape what can be grown successfully.

Types

Common forms include vegetable gardening, flower gardening, herb gardens, raised beds, container gardening, hydroponics, xeriscaping and wildlife-friendly planting. Public and botanical gardens also serve education, conservation and recreation.

Benefits and impacts

Gardening can supply fresh food, support pollinators and provide exercise and mental-health benefits. It can also consume water and inputs, spread invasive species or expose soil if poorly managed, so composting and integrated pest management are often encouraged.
