Najib Razak

Mohammad Najib bin Tun Haji Abdul Razak (born 23 July 1953) is a Malaysian politician who served as the sixth Prime Minister of Malaysia from 2009 to 2018. The son of second prime minister Abdul Razak Hussein, he held numerous cabinet posts, including finance minister, defence minister and deputy prime minister, and led the United Malays National Organisation (UMNO) and the Barisan Nasional coalition.

Premiership

Najib promoted economic liberalisation under the slogan "1Malaysia" and initiatives such as the Government Transformation Programme. His administration was overwhelmed by the 1Malaysia Development Berhad (1MDB) scandal, in which billions of dollars were alleged to have been misappropriated from the state investment fund; the United States Department of Justice described it as the largest kleptocracy case of its time. Barisan Nasional lost power in the 2018 general election — the first change of government in Malaysia's history.

Conviction

After investigations under the new government, Najib was arrested and charged with multiple counts of corruption and money laundering. In July 2020 he was convicted in the SRC International case and sentenced to 12 years' imprisonment and a large fine; the conviction was upheld on appeal, and he began serving his sentence in August 2022. In 2024 his sentence was halved by the Pardons Board.
