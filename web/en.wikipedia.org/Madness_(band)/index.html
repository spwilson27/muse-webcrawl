Madness (band)

OriginCamden Town, London, England
GenresSka, 2 Tone, pop, new wave
Years active1976–1986, 1992–present
Labels2 Tone, Stiff, Zarjazz

Madness are an English ska and pop band from Camden Town, north London, formed in 1976. They were one of the most prominent groups of the late-1970s and early-1980s Two Tone ska revival, and remain one of the most successful British chart acts, with a string of UK top ten singles including "Baggy Trousers", "It Must Be Love" and the number one hit "House of Fun".

History

The band formed from the North London Invaders and took their name from a song by Jamaican ska artist Prince Buster, whose "One Step Beyond" they covered for their 1979 debut single and album. Their nutty, music-hall-influenced image and comic videos made them favourites on British television. Keyboardist Mike Barson left in 1984, and the band split in 1986 before reforming in 1992.

Legacy

Madness have continued to record and tour, releasing albums such as The Liberty of Norton Folgate (2009) and performing at major events including the 2012 London Olympics closing ceremony.
