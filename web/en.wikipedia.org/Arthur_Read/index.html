Arthur Timothy Read is the protagonist of the book series Arthur by Marc Brown and the titular main character of the PBS Kids animated television series Arthur. He is an eight-year-old anthropomorphic aardvark in the third grade at Lakewood Elementary School in the fictional city of Elwood City.

Character

Arthur is a sweet, well-meaning, and somewhat anxious boy who wears round glasses, a yellow sweater over a white shirt, and blue jeans. He enjoys reading, playing the piano, and spending time with his best friend Buster Baxter. Through everyday challenges — homework, sibling rivalry, friendship conflicts, and school life — Arthur typically learns a lesson by the end of each story.

Family

Arthur lives with his father David, a caterer; his mother Jane, an accountant who works from home; his preschool-aged younger sister Dora Winifred "D.W." Read, who frequently teases and embarrasses him; his baby sister Kate; and his dog Pal. His grandmother Thora and grandfather Dave also appear regularly.

In the books and series

Marc Brown introduced Arthur in the 1976 picture book Arthur's Nose; in early books Arthur had a long aardvark snout, which gradually shortened over the series as the art style evolved. The animated series premiered in 1996 and ran for 25 seasons, ending in 2022 as one of the longest-running children's animated series in American television history.

Cultural impact

Arthur became a widely recognized cultural figure; a still of Arthur's clenched fist from the episode "Arthur's Big Hit" became a popular internet meme, and in 2022 Arthur appeared in a PBS farewell special addressing the show's legacy.
