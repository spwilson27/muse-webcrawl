The Falling Soldier

[Placeholder image: The_Falling_Soldier.png]

The Falling Soldier (originally titled Loyalist Militiaman at the Moment of Death, Cerro Muriano, September 5, 1936) is a photograph by Robert Capa, taken during the Spanish Civil War. It shows a soldier of the Iberian Federation of Libertarian Youth falling backwards, reputedly at the moment of death after being shot.

Authenticity debate

The photograph became an iconic image of war photography, but its authenticity has been debated since the 1970s, with some researchers arguing it was staged and taken at a different location than claimed.
