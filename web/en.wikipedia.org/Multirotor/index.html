Multirotor

TypeRotorcraft (helicopter)
Common formsQuadcopter, hexacopter, octocopter

A multirotor or multicopter is a rotorcraft with more than two lift-generating rotors. Multirotors are widely used for unmanned aerial vehicles (drones) because their fixed-pitch rotors and electronic flight control make them mechanically simple and highly manoeuvrable.

Design

A quadcopter has four arms with motors spinning fixed-pitch propellers, arranged in counter-rotating pairs to cancel torque. A flight controller adjusts individual motor speeds many times per second to control pitch, roll, yaw and altitude. Hexacopters (six rotors) and octocopters (eight) add payload capacity and redundancy against motor failure.

Applications

Multirotors are used for aerial photography and cinematography, surveying, agriculture, inspection, search and rescue, and racing.
