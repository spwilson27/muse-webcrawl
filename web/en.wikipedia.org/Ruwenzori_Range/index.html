The Ruwenzori Range, often called the Rwenzori Mountains and historically identified with the legendary "Mountains of the Moon", is a mountain range on the border between Uganda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Unlike most other high African mountains, it is not volcanic but an uplifted block of ancient crystalline rock.

Geography and ecology

The range extends about 120 km and includes Mount Stanley, whose Margherita Peak at 5,109 m is Africa's third-highest summit. Despite lying almost on the Equator, the highest peaks carry permanent snow and glaciers, which have retreated markedly since the early 20th century. The mountains are famed for their extraordinary vegetation zones, with giant groundsels and lobelias in the afro-alpine zone, and feed streams flowing toward the Semliki River and the Nile system.
