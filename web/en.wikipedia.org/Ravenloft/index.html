Ravenloft is a campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons role-playing game, centred on gothic horror adventures in the Demiplane of Dread. It began with the 1983 adventure module Ravenloft (I6), written by Tracy and Laura Hickman, which introduced the vampire Count Strahd von Zarovich and his castle in the land of Barovia. The module is frequently ranked among the greatest Dungeons & Dragons adventures of all time.

The Demiplane of Dread

In the expanded setting (1990), Ravenloft became a demiplane composed of domains, each ruled by a Darklord imprisoned by the mysterious Dark Powers as punishment for some great evil. Strahd, the first Darklord, rules Barovia from Castle Ravenloft, eternally pursuing the reincarnations of his lost love Tatyana.

Publications

The setting spawned a boxed set, numerous supplements, and a line of novels including I, Strahd by P. N. Elrod and Christie Golden's Vampire of the Mists. The adventure was revisited for fifth edition in Curse of Strahd (2016), and the setting was expanded in Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft (2021).
