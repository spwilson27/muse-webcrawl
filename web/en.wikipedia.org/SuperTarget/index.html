SuperTarget

SuperTarget and Target Greatland are discount/general merchandise stores of the American retail corporation Target, offering a full-service grocery department alongside Target's general merchandise selection. The SuperTarget format was introduced in 1995 with a store in Omaha, Nebraska, combining a conventional Target with a complete supermarket including fresh produce, meat, dairy, and a bakery and deli.

SuperTarget stores are substantially larger than standard Target locations — historically around 175,000 square feet (16,300 m²) — and many include additional services such as a Target Pharmacy, optical centre, and Starbucks. The format was part of a wave of hypermarket expansion by American discount retailers competing with Walmart Supercenters.

Later development

In the 2010s Target largely stopped distinguishing the SuperTarget name, as full grocery departments were incorporated into many standard stores under the "PFresh" and later remodel programs; new stores were no longer opened under the SuperTarget banner, and existing locations were gradually rebranded simply as Target. The related Target Greatland format, launched in 1990, was a larger-than-average Target without a full grocery store; those stores have likewise been converted or rebranded.
