XMM-Newton

XMM-Newton, also known as the High Throughput X-ray Spectroscopy Mission, is an X-ray space observatory launched by the European Space Agency on 10 December 1999. Its name combines X-ray Multi-Mirror with a tribute to Isaac Newton.

Design

The spacecraft carries three X-ray telescopes with gold-coated nested mirrors — the largest X-ray mirror modules of their time — feeding EPIC cameras, reflection grating spectrometers and an optical monitor. Its highly elliptical 48-hour orbit allows long, uninterrupted observations.

Science

XMM-Newton has studied galaxy clusters, active galactic nuclei, X-ray binaries, supernova remnants and exoplanet atmospheres, producing large source catalogues used across astrophysics. Originally a 10-year mission, it has been extended repeatedly into the 2020s.
