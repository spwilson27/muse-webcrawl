Dylan Carlson

Dylan Carlson (born 1968) is an American musician, best known as the founder, lead guitarist and sole constant member of the drone metal band Earth.

Career

Carlson formed Earth in Seattle, Washington, in 1989. The band's early albums, including Earth 2: Special Low Frequency Version (1993), pioneered the drone doom style with extremely slow, heavily distorted, repetitive guitar passages. After a hiatus, Carlson re-formed Earth in the 2000s in a cleaner, more minimalist style influenced by country and jazz, beginning with Hex; Or Printing in the Infernal Method (2005).

Personal history

Carlson was a close friend of Nirvana frontman Kurt Cobain, who contributed vocals to an early Earth release. His struggles with addiction and health problems interrupted his career in the 1990s, before his return with a reconstituted Earth lineup. He has also released solo work and collaborated with artists including Adrienne Davies, who became Earth's drummer.
