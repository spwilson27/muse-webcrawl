The Festival Interceltique de Lorient (Inter-Celtic Festival of Lorient) is an annual festival held every August in the city of Lorient, Brittany, France. Founded in 1971, it celebrates the traditional and contemporary culture of the Celtic nations — Brittany, Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Cornwall, the Isle of Man, Galicia, and Asturias, as well as Celtic diaspora communities.

Program

Over ten days, the festival presents thousands of performers and attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors. Its central events include the Grand Parade of the Celtic Nations, in which pipe bands (bagadoù) and dance troupes march through the city, and the National Breton Bagad Championship finals. Concerts range from traditional music to Celtic rock and fusion, alongside dance competitions, masterclasses, exhibitions, and a large Celtic marketplace.

Significance

The festival is one of the largest Celtic cultural gatherings in the world and a major showcase for Breton culture. Each edition traditionally highlights one Celtic nation as guest of honor. Notable performers over the years have included Alan Stivell, The Chieftains, and Gilles Servat.
