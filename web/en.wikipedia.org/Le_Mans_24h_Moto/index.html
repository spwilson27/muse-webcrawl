Le Mans 24h Moto

Le Mans 24h Moto
Venue: Bugatti Circuit, Le Mans, France
First held: 1978
Series: FIM Endurance World Championship

The Le Mans 24h Moto (24 Heures Motos) is an annual motorcycle endurance race held at the Bugatti Circuit in Le Mans, France. First run in 1978, it is one of the most prestigious events in motorcycle endurance racing and traditionally opens the FIM Endurance World Championship season.

Format

Teams of three riders share a single motorcycle over 24 hours of continuous racing, with pit stops for fuel, tyres and rider changes. Machines compete in classes including Formula EWC, Superstock and experimental categories. The race starts with the famous running start across the track, in the tradition of the 24 Hours of Le Mans car race.

History

Endurance racing at Le Mans dates to the Bol d'Or era of the 1960s; the standalone 24-hour motorcycle race was established in 1978. Japanese manufacturers Honda, Suzuki, Kawasaki and Yamaha have dominated the event, and the race regularly draws crowds of more than 100,000.
