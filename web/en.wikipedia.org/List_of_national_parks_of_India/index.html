List of national parks of India

India has more than 100 national parks, covering about 44,000 km² (roughly 1.3% of the country's geographical area). The first national park was established in 1936 as Hailey National Park, now Jim Corbett National Park in Uttarakhand. National parks are designated under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, and many of the tiger reserves managed under Project Tiger are centred on national parks. This list gives the principal national parks by state.
