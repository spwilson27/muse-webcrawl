Christine (film)

Directed by: John Carpenter

Release: 1983

Genre: Supernatural horror

Christine is a 1983 American supernatural horror film directed by John Carpenter and adapted from Stephen King's novel of the same name. It stars Keith Gordon as Arnie Cunningham, a shy teenager whose personality changes after he buys and restores a dilapidated 1958 Plymouth Fury named Christine.

Plot

The car proves to possess a malevolent, jealous intelligence. As Arnie becomes obsessed with Christine, the vehicle repairs itself, pursues those who threaten their relationship, and draws its owner into isolation and violence. Friends and family attempt to destroy the car in a climactic confrontation at a garage.

Production and reception

Carpenter combined practical automotive effects, expressive lighting, and an electronic score to give the automobile a predatory presence. Released in December 1983, the film was a commercial success and has become one of the best-known adaptations of King's work, admired for its atmosphere and treatment of adolescent obsession.

Legacy

Christine helped define the haunted-vehicle subgenre and remains a cult favourite. Surviving Plymouth Furys prepared for filming are prized by collectors.
