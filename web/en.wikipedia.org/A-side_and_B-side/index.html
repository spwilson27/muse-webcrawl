A-side and B-side

The A-side and B-side are the two sides of phonograph records and cassettes. The terms derive from 78 rpm records, and were later applied to 45 rpm vinyl singles and cassette tapes. The A-side usually featured the recording that the artist, record producer, or company intended to receive the initial promotional effort and radio airplay and hopefully become a hit record. The B-side (or "flip-side") is the secondary recording.

Some B-sides themselves became famous — such as "I Am the Walrus" and "Revolution" by the Beatles — and the term "B-side" has since entered wider usage metaphorically for something secondary or less prominent. A "double A-side" single is one in which both songs are promoted as potential hits.
