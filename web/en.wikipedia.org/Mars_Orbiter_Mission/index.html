Mars Orbiter Mission

The Mars Orbiter Mission (MOM), unofficially known as Mangalyaan, was a space probe orbiting Mars developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). It was launched on 5 November 2013 and entered Mars orbit on 24 September 2014, making India the first nation to succeed on its maiden attempt and ISRO the fourth space agency to reach Mars orbit.

The mission's objectives were technological demonstration and scientific study of the Martian atmosphere and surface with five instruments, including a colour camera and a methane sensor.

It operated far beyond its planned six-month life, until contact was lost in 2022. Its low cost of about US$73 million was widely noted.
