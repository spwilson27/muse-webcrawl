AI safety

AI safety is the field concerned with ensuring that artificial intelligence systems behave as intended and do not cause unintended harm. It includes technical research on aligning AI systems with human goals and values, as well as governance, monitoring, and robustness work.

Topics

Central topics include specification problems (such as reward hacking), robustness to distribution shift, interpretability of neural networks, scalable oversight of systems more capable than their evaluators, and the alignment of advanced systems whose goals might diverge from those of their designers.

Long-term concerns

Some researchers argue that sufficiently advanced AI could pose existential risks if its objectives are misaligned, a concern popularized by writers such as Nick Bostrom and Eliezer Yudkowsky. Others dispute the likelihood or framing of such risks while supporting nearer-term work on bias, misuse, and accidents.

Institutions

Research is conducted at universities, AI laboratories, nonprofits such as the Machine Intelligence Research Institute, and government bodies including the UK AI Safety Institute founded in 2023.
