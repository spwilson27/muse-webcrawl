Bill Gaither

William J. Gaither (born March 28, 1936) is an American singer and songwriter of Southern gospel and contemporary Christian music. He has written numerous popular Christian songs with his wife Gloria; he is also known for performing as part of the Bill Gaither Trio and the Gaither Vocal Band, and for the Gaither Homecoming series of concerts, albums, and videos that helped revive interest in Southern gospel music.

Career

Gaither was born in Alexandria, Indiana, and formed the Bill Gaither Trio in 1956 while at Anderson College. With Gloria Sickal, whom he married in 1962, he wrote more than 700 songs, including "He Touched Me" (1963), "Because He Lives" (1971), "The King Is Coming", and the children's musical "Let's Just Praise the Lord". "He Touched Me" was recorded by Elvis Presley, who won a Grammy Award for his 1972 album of that name.

In 1981 Gaither founded the Gaither Vocal Band; the Homecoming series launched in 1991 grew into a multimillion-selling franchise of videos and tours featuring veteran gospel artists. Gaither has won multiple Grammy Awards and Dove Awards and was inducted into the Gospel Music Hall of Fame. His Gaither Music Group, based in Alexandria, Indiana, remains a major force in gospel music recording and publishing.
