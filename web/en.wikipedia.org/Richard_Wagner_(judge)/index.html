Richard Wagner (judge)

Richard Wagner (born April 2, 1957) is a Canadian jurist who has served as the 18th Chief Justice of Canada since December 18, 2017. He previously served as a judge of the Quebec Court of Appeal and as a puisne justice of the Supreme Court of Canada from 2012.

Early life and legal career

Wagner was born in Montreal, Quebec, the son of former Quebec MNA Claude Wagner. He studied law at the University of Ottawa and was called to the Bar of Quebec in 1980. He practised litigation in Montreal, notably with Lavery, de Billy, and served as president of the Bar of Montreal.

Judicial career

Appointed to the Superior Court of Quebec in 2004 and elevated to the Court of Appeal in 2011, Wagner was named to the Supreme Court of Canada by Prime Minister Stephen Harper in October 2012. He succeeded Beverley McLachlin as Chief Justice in 2017, appointed by Prime Minister Justin Trudeau.

Administrator of Canada

In 2021, following the resignation of Governor General Julie Payette, Wagner served as Administrator of the Government of Canada, the longest-serving in the role, until the appointment of Mary Simon.
