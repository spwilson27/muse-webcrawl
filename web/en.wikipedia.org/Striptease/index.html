Striptease
Type: Erotic performance
Settings: Burlesque, clubs

A striptease is an erotic or exotic dance in which the performer gradually undresses, usually to music, in a manner intended to be sexually appealing. The emphasis is traditionally on the tease — the slow, suggestive removal of clothing — rather than nudity itself.

History

Elements appear in ancient ritual and in 19th-century Parisian revues and American burlesque, where performers such as Gypsy Rose Lee made striptease a witty theatrical art. Famous venues included the Moulin Rouge and American burlesque theatres.

Modern forms

From the mid-20th century, striptease moved largely to dedicated clubs, and pole dancing became a common athletic form. Neo-burlesque revivals since the 1990s have reclaimed the theatrical, costume-rich style, and male revues perform for largely female audiences.
