Dajti National Park

Dajti National Park
Location: Tirana County, Albania
Nearest city: Tirana
Area: about 293 km² (after 2006 expansion)
Established: 1966

Dajti National Park (Albanian: Parku Kombëtar i Dajtit) is a national park in central Albania, established in 1966 and greatly expanded in 2006. It covers the Dajti Mountain massif east of the capital Tirana and is sometimes called the "natural balcony of Tirana".

Geography and nature

The park centres on Mount Dajti, which rises to 1,613 metres. Its slopes are covered with pine, oak and beech forest, with canyons, waterfalls, caves and a lake. Wildlife includes wild boar, red fox, European hare and, more rarely, brown bear and grey wolf.

Access

Since 2005 the Dajti Ekspres cable car, one of the longest in the Balkans, has carried visitors from the outskirts of Tirana to a recreation area near the summit, which offers restaurants, an adventure park and views over the city and, on clear days, the Adriatic Sea.
