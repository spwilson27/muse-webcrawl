The Association of Spanish Language Academies (Spanish: Asociación de Academias de la Lengua Española, ASALE) is the entity that brings together the 23 academies of the Spanish language around the world. Founded in Mexico in 1951, it works to promote the unity, integrity and development of the Spanish language while respecting its regional varieties.

Work

ASALE coordinates the academies' joint production of the reference works of Spanish: the Diccionario de la lengua española, the Nueva gramática de la lengua española (2009) and the Ortografía de la lengua española (2010), all produced under a pan-Hispanic policy in which all academies share authorship and authority. The association convenes the International Congress of the Spanish Language and awards prizes including the Real Academia Española Prize.

Members

Members include the Royal Spanish Academy (founded 1713) and academies in every Spanish-speaking country, the North American Academy of the Spanish Language, the Philippine Academy, and, since 2016, the Academia Ecuatoguineana, with Judeo-Spanish represented by the National Authority of Ladino in Israel.
