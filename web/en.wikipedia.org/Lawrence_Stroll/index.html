Lawrence Stroll

Lawrence Sheldon Strulović Stroll (born 11 July 1959) is a Canadian billionaire businessman and investor, best known in fashion for building brands such as Ralph Lauren Europe, Tommy Hilfiger and Michael Kors with partner Silas Chou, and in sport as owner of the Aston Martin Formula One team and a major shareholder and executive chairman of Aston Martin Lagonda.

Motorsport

In 2018 Stroll led a consortium that bought the Force India F1 team, rebranded Racing Point and then, from 2021, Aston Martin, based at Silverstone. His son Lance Stroll drives for the team. Stroll has invested heavily in a new factory and wind tunnel and hired star designer Adrian Newey. Earlier he owned Circuit Mont-Tremblant in Quebec.

Personal life

Born in Montreal to a fashion-importer father, Stroll is also a noted collector of vintage Ferraris and owns the superyacht Faith. His fortune, built on taking fashion brands public, is estimated in the billions of US dollars by Forbes.
