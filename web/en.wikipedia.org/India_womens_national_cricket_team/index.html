India women's national cricket team

The India women's national cricket team, nicknamed the Women in Blue, represents India in international women's cricket. The team is governed by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and is a full member of the International Cricket Council with Women's Test, One Day International and Twenty20 International status.

History

Women's cricket in India was organised under the Women's Cricket Association of India from the early 1970s; India played its first Women's Test match against the West Indies in 1976 and took part in the Women's Cricket World Cup from 1978, hosting that year's tournament. The women's association merged with the BCCI in 2006, bringing the women's game under the main board's administration.

Performances

India have twice reached the final of the Women's Cricket World Cup, losing to Australia in 2005 and in a celebrated close final to England at Lord's in 2017. They reached the final of the Women's T20 World Cup in 2020 and won the silver medal at the 2022 Commonwealth Games. Prominent players have included Mithali Raj, long-time captain and one of the leading run-scorers in women's ODIs, fast bowler Jhulan Goswami, and more recent figures such as Harmanpreet Kaur and Smriti Mandhana. The launch of the Women's Premier League in 2023 marked a major step in the professionalisation of the women's game in India.
