Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways

The Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways is a ministry of the Government of India responsible for the formulation and administration of rules, regulations and laws relating to ports, shipping and inland waterways. It was known as the Ministry of Shipping until November 2020, when its remit over waterways was reflected in a new name.

The ministry administers India's major ports, the merchant shipping fleet, shipbuilding, coastal shipping, and the development of national waterways for inland navigation. Sarbananda Sonowal has served as the cabinet minister in recent years.

Scope

India has a coastline of over 7,500 km and a network of declared national waterways exceeding 100 routes. The ministry's responsibilities include the twelve major ports administered by the central government, non-major ports in coordination with maritime states, shipbuilding and ship repair, dredging, maritime training and seafarer welfare, lighthouses, and the Sagarmala programme for port-led development.

Key programmes

  • Sagarmala — port modernisation, port connectivity, coastal community development and new port development
  • Jal Marg Vikas — capacity augmentation of National Waterway 1 (the Ganga)
  • Maritime India Vision 2030 and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 — long-term sector roadmaps

Organisations

Major bodies under the ministry include the Inland Waterways Authority of India, the Directorate General of Shipping, the Shipping Corporation of India, Cochin Shipyard, the Dredging Corporation of India, the Directorate General of Lighthouses and Lightships, and the port authorities of the major ports such as Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata (Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port), Visakhapatnam and Jawaharlal Nehru Port.
