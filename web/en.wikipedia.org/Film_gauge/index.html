Film gauge

Film gauge is the physical width of photographic or motion picture film stock, measured in millimetres. Standard gauges include 8 mm, Super 8, 9.5 mm, 16 mm, 35 mm, and 65/70 mm.

35 mm became the dominant theatrical gauge for most of the 20th century; 16 mm served television, education and independent film, while 70 mm offered higher resolution for prestige productions and IMAX.
