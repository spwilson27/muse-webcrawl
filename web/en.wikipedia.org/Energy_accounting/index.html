Energy accounting

Energy accounting is a system used to measure, analyze, and report the energy consumption of an organization, process, or economy. It extends conventional accounting principles to physical flows of energy, expressed in units such as joules, kilowatt-hours, or tonnes of oil equivalent.

Purpose

Energy accounting identifies where energy is used and wasted, supports energy-efficiency investment decisions, and provides baselines for targets and carbon reporting. It underlies techniques such as energy audits, energy management systems (e.g., ISO 50001), and embodied energy analysis of products.

Related concepts

Related fields include energy economics, life-cycle assessment, material flow accounting, and the study of energy returned on energy invested (EROEI), which compares the useful energy delivered by a process to the energy consumed in delivering it.
