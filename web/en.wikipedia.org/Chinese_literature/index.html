Chinese literature

Chinese literature spans more than three thousand years, from oracle-bone inscriptions and the Confucian classics to Tang poetry, Ming and Qing novels and modern fiction.

Classical literature

The Shijing (Classic of Poetry) and Shujing founded the tradition. Tang dynasty poets Li Bai and Du Fu are regarded as China's greatest. The Four Great Classical Novels — Romance of the Three Kingdoms, Water Margin, Journey to the West and Dream of the Red Chamber — crown the pre-modern novel.

Modern literature

The May Fourth Movement promoted vernacular baihua writing. Lu Xun is the founder of modern Chinese literature. In 2000 Gao Xingjian and in 2012 Mo Yan won the Nobel Prize in Literature.
