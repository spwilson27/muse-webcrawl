Per mille

Per mille (from Latin per mille, "in each thousand") is a unit of proportion indicating parts per thousand. It is denoted by the symbol ‰, which resembles a percent sign (%) with an additional zero. One per mille equals one thousandth (0.1 percent).

Uses

Per mille notation is used where quantities are small and percent values would involve awkward decimals. Common applications include:

  • Salinity of seawater, historically expressed in grams of salt per kilogram of water (parts per thousand).
  • Railway and road gradients, in many European countries expressed in per mille rather than percent.
  • Alcohol content of blood in legal limits in much of Europe.
  • Isotope geochemistry, where delta values (δ) are reported in per mille relative to a standard.
  • Property tax rates in parts of the United States (millage) and birth and death rates in demography, often stated per 1,000 population, which is equivalent to per mille.

Related units

Per mille belongs to a family of proportional notations that includes percent (per hundred), per ten thousand (basis points in finance, denoted ‱), parts per million (ppm) and parts per billion (ppb). The symbol ‰ has the Unicode code point U+2030.
