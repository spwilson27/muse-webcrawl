First French Empire

The First French Empire, officially the French Empire (French: Empire français), was the empire ruled by Napoleon Bonaparte, who established French dominance over much of continental Europe at the beginning of the 19th century.

Origins

Napoleon, First Consul since the coup of 18 Brumaire (1799), was proclaimed Emperor of the French in 1804 and crowned at Notre-Dame. His Napoleonic Code reshaped French law.

Napoleonic Wars

Victories at Austerlitz (1805), Jena and Friedland (1807) gave France hegemony; the Continental System blockaded Britain. The disastrous invasion of Russia (1812) began the decline.

Fall

Defeated at Leipzig (1813), Napoleon abdicated in 1814 and was exiled to Elba. He returned for the Hundred Days, ending with defeat at Waterloo (18 June 1815).
