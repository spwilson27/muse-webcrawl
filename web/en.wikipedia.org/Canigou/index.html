Canigou

Canigou
Elevation: 2,784.66 m (9,136 ft)
Location: Pyrénées-Orientales, France

Canigou (Catalan: Canigó) is a mountain in the Pyrenees in southern France, in the department of Pyrénées-Orientales. Rising to 2,784 metres, it is a symbolic mountain for Catalan identity and is visible from across the plains of Roussillon and, on clear days, from Marseille.

Traditions

On the eve of the summer solstice (St John's Eve, 23 June), the Flame of Canigou is lit at the summit and carried to bonfires throughout the Catalan lands. The abbey of Saint-Martin-du-Canigou sits on its slopes.

Climbing

The summit is reachable by hiking trails from Vernet-les-Bains and Casteil, and a rough track leads to the Chalet des Cortalets refuge.
