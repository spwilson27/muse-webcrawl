Robert Pershing Wadlow (1918–1940), known as the Alton Giant, was an American man who is the tallest person in recorded history for whom there is irrefutable evidence. He reached 2.72 m (8 ft 11.1 in) and was still growing when he died aged 22.

[Image placeholder: Robert Wadlow standing beside his father in 1936]

Life

Wadlow was born in Alton, Illinois, of normal size, but grew at an extraordinary rate: he was 1.83 m (6 ft) tall by age eight and taller than his father by nine. His growth resulted from hypertrophy of the pituitary gland, causing abnormally high levels of human growth hormone — a condition never treated in his lifetime. He graduated from Alton High School in 1936 and toured the United States with the Ringling Bros. Circus and later as a goodwill ambassador for the International Shoe Company, which supplied his size-37AA shoes free of charge. He required leg braces to walk and had little sensation in his legs and feet.

Death and legacy

In July 1940 a blister on his ankle, caused by a poorly fitting brace, became infected; despite transfusions and emergency surgery he died in his sleep on 15 July 1940. His coffin was 3.28 m long and was carried by twelve pallbearers. Nearly 27,000 people attended his funeral. A life-size statue stands in Alton, and his record — nearly nine feet — remains unmatched by any verified case.
