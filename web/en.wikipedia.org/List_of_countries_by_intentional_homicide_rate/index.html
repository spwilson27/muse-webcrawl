List of countries by intentional homicide rate

The intentional homicide rate is the number of unlawful deaths deliberately inflicted per 100,000 population per year. Data are compiled by the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC). The global average is around 5-6 per 100,000.

Regional patterns

  • Highest rates: parts of Latin America and the Caribbean — Jamaica, Saint Kitts and Nevis, South Africa, Honduras, Trinidad and Tobago, Mexico, Colombia, Brazil and others have recorded rates above 25-40 per 100,000 in various years. El Salvador's rate fell dramatically in the early 2020s.
  • United States: roughly 6-8 per 100,000 in recent years — higher than other wealthy nations.
  • Europe: typically 0.5-1.5 per 100,000; Russia and the Baltic states somewhat higher.
  • Lowest rates: Japan, Singapore, Qatar, Bahrain and several small states record rates below 0.3 per 100,000.

UNODC notes definitional and reporting differences between countries; the full table lists the most recent available year for each country and territory.
