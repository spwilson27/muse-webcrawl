Bowl Championship Series

The Bowl Championship Series (BCS) was a selection system in American college football that operated from 1998 to 2013. It was designed to match the top two teams in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision in a national championship game, and to place other highly ranked teams in prestigious bowl games.

Rankings were produced by a formula combining human polls (the Coaches Poll and Harris Interactive Poll) with computer rankings. The BCS bowls were the Rose Bowl, Fiesta Bowl, Sugar Bowl, Orange Bowl, and, from 2006, a standalone BCS National Championship Game.

The system was persistently controversial for excluding undefeated teams from smaller conferences and for its opaque formula. It was replaced after the 2013 season by the College Football Playoff, a four-team tournament later expanded to twelve teams.
