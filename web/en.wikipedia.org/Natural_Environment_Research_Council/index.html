Natural Environment Research Council

The Natural Environment Research Council (NERC) is the United Kingdom's main agency for funding and managing research, training, and knowledge exchange in the environmental sciences. Since 2018 it has been one of the councils within UK Research and Innovation (UKRI).

Scope

NERC funds research across the full range of environmental science, including atmospheric, Earth, biological, terrestrial, and aquatic sciences. It supports university research through grants and maintains national research capability through its wholly owned and associated research centres, including the British Antarctic Survey, the British Geological Survey, the National Oceanography Centre, and the UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology.

Facilities

NERC operates major research infrastructure, including the royal research ships RRS Sir David Attenborough and RRS Discovery, research aircraft, and Antarctic research stations such as Rothera and Halley. Its science addresses questions including climate change, biodiversity loss, natural hazards, and the sustainable management of natural resources.
