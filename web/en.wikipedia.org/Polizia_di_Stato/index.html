Polizia di Stato

The Polizia di Stato (State Police) is one of the national police forces of Italy, primarily a civilian force under the Ministry of the Interior, responsible for patrol highways, railways, airports, customs together with the Guardia di Finanza, and general law enforcement in cities.

Headed by the Chief of Police – Director General of Public Security, it operates through questure in each province. Specialized units include the traffic police (Polizia Stradale), railway and postal police, and anti-mafia and counterterrorism units. It works alongside the military Carabinieri.
