John Joseph Mearsheimer (born December 14, 1947) is an American political scientist and international relations scholar at the University of Chicago, best known for his theory of offensive realism, which holds that great powers relentlessly seek to maximise their power in an anarchic international system.

Career

Mearsheimer graduated from West Point in 1970 and served five years in the U.S. Air Force before earning his doctorate from Cornell University. He joined the University of Chicago faculty in 1982.

Offensive realism

In The Tragedy of Great Power Politics (2001) Mearsheimer argues that the structure of the international system — anarchy, military capabilities, and uncertainty about intentions — compels states to seek regional hegemony and prevent rivals from doing the same, making great-power conflict a recurring tragedy.

Other work and controversy

His books include Conventional Deterrence (1983), The Israel Lobby and U.S. Foreign Policy (2007, with Stephen Walt), and The Great Delusion (2018), which criticises liberal hegemony. His arguments about the Israel lobby and about NATO enlargement and Russia's war against Ukraine have drawn wide attention and criticism.
