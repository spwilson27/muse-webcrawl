Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse is a 2018 American animated superhero film featuring the Marvel Comics character Miles Morales / Spider-Man, produced by Columbia Pictures and Sony Pictures Animation in association with Marvel Entertainment. It was directed by Bob Persichetti, Peter Ramsey, and Rodney Rothman from a screenplay by Phil Lord and Rothman. Shameik Moore stars as the voice of Miles, with Jake Johnson, Hailee Steinfeld, Mahershala Ali, Brian Tyree Henry, Lily Tomlin, Luna Lauren Vélez, John Mulaney, Kimiko Glenn, Nicolas Cage, and Liev Schreiber in supporting roles.

Plot

Brooklyn teenager Miles Morales is bitten by a radioactive spider and gains abilities similar to those of Spider-Man, Peter Parker. When crime lord Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin, activates a supercollider to access parallel universes, Parker is killed and Miles must learn to become Spider-Man with the help of versions of the hero from other dimensions, including an older, jaded Peter B. Parker, Gwen Stacy / Spider-Woman, Spider-Man Noir, Peni Parker, and Spider-Ham.

Production

The film was dedicated to Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, the co-creators of Spider-Man, who both died in 2018. The animation combined computer animation with traditional hand-drawn comic book techniques, requiring up to 140 animators, the largest crew used by Sony Pictures Animation.

Reception

The film grossed $384 million worldwide against a $90 million budget. It was praised for its animation, characters, story, voice acting, and humor, and won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 91st Academy Awards, the first non-Disney/Pixar film to win the award since Rango (2011). A sequel, Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, was released in 2023.
