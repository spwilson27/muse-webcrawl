Aston Martin DB4

ManufacturerAston Martin
Production1958–1963 (1,204 built)
Engine3.7 L Tadek Marek straight-six
SuccessorAston Martin DB5

The Aston Martin DB4 is a grand tourer produced by the British manufacturer Aston Martin from 1958 to 1963. A landmark model, it introduced the Italian-styled Superleggera body construction and a new straight-six engine that defined the company's cars for a generation.

Design and engineering

The DB4's aluminium body panels were mounted on a tubular steel frame by Carrozzeria Touring of Milan under its patented Superleggera system, giving the car an elegant fastback profile. Its 3.7-litre twin overhead-camshaft six, designed by Tadek Marek, produced about 240 hp in standard form, propelling the car to roughly 140 mph — exceptional performance for a four-seat touring car of the period.

Variants

The model went through five series of detail changes and spawned notable variants: a convertible, the shorter, lighter DB4 GT (including the rare Zagato-bodied DB4 GT Zagato), and the high-performance Vantage. Its successor, the DB5 of 1963, became famous as James Bond's car in Goldfinger.
