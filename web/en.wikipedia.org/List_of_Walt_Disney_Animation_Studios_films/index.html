List of Walt Disney Animation Studios films

Walt Disney Animation Studios is an American animation studio headquartered in Burbank, California. Its feature films form the Disney Animated Canon, beginning with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), the first American animated feature.

Golden and Silver Ages

Early classics include Pinocchio and Fantasia (1940), Dumbo (1941), Bambi (1942), Cinderella (1950), Sleeping Beauty (1959) and One Hundred and One Dalmatians (1961).

Renaissance and modern era

The Disney Renaissance (1989–1999) produced The Little Mermaid, Beauty and the Beast, Aladdin and The Lion King. The CGI era brought Tangled (the 50th film), Wreck-It Ralph, Frozen and its sequel, Zootopia, Moana and Encanto.
