Dietrich Mateschitz

Dietrich Mateschitz
Born: 20 May 1944, Sankt Marein im Mürztal, Austria
Died: 22 October 2022 (aged 78)
Occupation: Co-founder of Red Bull GmbH

Dietrich Mateschitz (20 May 1944 – 22 October 2022) was an Austrian entrepreneur best known as the co-founder of the energy drink company Red Bull. He held a 49 percent share of the company and was for many years Austria's richest person.

Early life and career

Mateschitz studied marketing at the Vienna University of Economics and Business. Working for the consumer goods company Blendax, he travelled internationally; in Thailand in 1982 he discovered Krating Daeng, a tonic drink popular with workers and drivers, which relieved his jet lag.

Red Bull

In 1984 Mateschitz founded Red Bull GmbH with the Thai producer Chaleo Yoovidhya and his son, adapting the drink for Western markets. Launched in Austria in 1987, Red Bull created the modern energy drink category. Mateschitz built the brand through sponsorship of extreme sports, Formula One (Red Bull Racing), and football clubs including RB Leipzig and Red Bull Salzburg.

Death and legacy

Mateschitz died on 22 October 2022 after a long illness. His stake passed to his son, Mark Mateschitz.
