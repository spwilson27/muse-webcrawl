Leo Abse

Leo Abse (22 April 1917 – 19 August 2008) was a Welsh Labour politician, solicitor and social reformer. He served as a Member of Parliament for Pontypool, and later Torfaen, from 1958 to 1987.

Career

Abse, from Cardiff, was a prominent backbench campaigner whose private member's legislation contributed to the decriminalisation of homosexual acts in England and Wales in 1967 and to reform of divorce law. Flamboyant in dress and interested in psychoanalysis, he wrote several books blending biography and psychological interpretation.
