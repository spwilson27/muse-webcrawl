Charles François du Périer Dumouriez (1739–1823) was a French general during the French Revolutionary Wars. A soldier from the age of nineteen, he served in the Seven Years' War and on secret missions for Louis XV before rising to prominence after the Revolution.

Revolutionary career

Dumouriez briefly served as Minister of Foreign Affairs in 1792 and then commanded the Army of the North. Together with Kellermann he won the pivotal Battle of Valmy (20 September 1792), which halted the Prussian advance on Paris, and followed it with victory over the Austrians at Jemappes (6 November 1792), conquering the Austrian Netherlands.

Defection

Defeated at Neerwinden in March 1793, and increasingly at odds with the revolutionary government in Paris, Dumouriez arrested the commissioners sent to investigate him and defected to the Austrians in April 1793 — a sensational betrayal that pushed the Revolution toward the Terror. He spent the rest of his life in exile, dying in England in 1823.
