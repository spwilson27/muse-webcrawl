It (2017 film)

It became the highest-grossing horror film of all time on release.

It (titled onscreen as It Chapter One) is a 2017 American supernatural horror film directed by Andy Muschietti and written by Chase Palmer, Cary Fukunaga and Gary Dauberman. It is the first of a two-part adaptation of Stephen King's 1986 novel It, previously adapted as a 1990 television miniseries.

Plot

In the town of Derry, Maine, in 1989, a group of bullied children — the self-styled "Losers' Club" — are terrorised by a shape-shifting evil that emerges from the sewers every 27 years, most often taking the form of Pennywise the Dancing Clown (Bill Skarsgård). After Pennywise murders young Georgie Denbrough, his brother Bill and the other Losers confront their fears and drive the creature back into hibernation, vowing to return if it ever does.

Reception

The film stars Jaeden Lieberher as Bill, with Sophia Lillis, Finn Wolfhard, Jack Dylan Grazer and others as the Losers. Made for about $35 million, it grossed over $700 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing horror film at the time, and received generally positive reviews. Its sequel, It Chapter Two, was released in 2019.
