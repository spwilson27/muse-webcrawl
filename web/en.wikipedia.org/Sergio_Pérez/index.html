Sergio Pérez

Sergio Michel Pérez Mendoza (born 26 January 1990), nicknamed "Checo", is a Mexican racing driver who competed in Formula One from 2011 to 2024. He drove for Sauber, McLaren, Force India/Racing Point, and Red Bull Racing, winning six Grands Prix and becoming the most successful Mexican driver in Formula One history.

Early career

Pérez began karting at age six and moved to Europe as a teenager to pursue single-seater racing, competing in Formula BMW, British Formula 3 (winning the National Class in 2007), and GP2, where he finished runner-up to Pastor Maldonado in 2010 with the Barwa Addax team. He was a member of the Ferrari Driver Academy and was backed by the Mexican telecommunications magnate Carlos Slim's Escudería Telmex programme.

Formula One career

Pérez debuted with Sauber in 2011 and took three podiums in 2012, notably second place in Malaysia and Italy. A difficult single season at McLaren in 2013 followed, before a long and successful stint with Force India (later Racing Point) from 2014, where he earned a reputation for tyre management and opportunistic podiums. At the 2020 Sakhir Grand Prix he took his first victory, coming from last place after a first-lap incident — Racing Point's only win — shortly before leaving the team.

Signed by Red Bull Racing for 2021, Pérez won in Baku that year and famously defended against Lewis Hamilton in Abu Dhabi to aid teammate Max Verstappen's title. He added wins at Monaco and Singapore in 2022 and in Saudi Arabia and Azerbaijan in 2023, finishing a career-best second in the 2023 championship as Red Bull dominated. After a slump in 2024 he and the team parted ways by mutual agreement at the end of the season, ending his Formula One career (at least for the time being) with six wins and 39 podiums.
