Khao Kheow Open Zoo (Thai: สวนสัตว์เปิดเขาเขียว) is a large zoo in Si Racha District, Chonburi Province, Thailand. It is an "open zoo," meaning that many animals roam in large naturalistic enclosures rather than cages, and visitors travel through much of the park by vehicle or tram.

History and layout

The zoo opened in 1978 on land at the foot of Khao Kheow ("Green Mountain") and has expanded to roughly 800 hectares of forested terrain, of which a portion is developed for visitors. It is administered by the Zoological Park Organization of Thailand, which also runs zoos in Bangkok (formerly Dusit), Chiang Mai, Songkhla, and Nakhon Ratchasima.

Animals and attractions

The zoo keeps several thousand animals of hundreds of species, including elephants, giraffes, zebras, white lions, tigers, hippos, and a large walk-through aviary. Attractions include animal shows, a night safari, and an Eld's deer park where visitors can feed animals. In 2024 the zoo's pygmy hippopotamus calf Moo Deng became an international social-media sensation, sharply increasing visitor numbers.
