Combinatorial group theory

Combinatorial group theory is the theory of free groups and of presentations of groups by generators and relations, developed from the work of Max Dehn in the early 20th century.

Main problems

Dehn posed three fundamental decision problems: the word problem, the conjugacy problem and the isomorphism problem. In the 1950s Novikov and Boone independently proved the word problem unsolvable in general. Key tools include van Kampen diagrams, small cancellation theory, Tietze transformations and the Nielsen method.

Later developments

The field merges into geometric group theory, pioneered by Mikhail Gromov, which studies groups through their Cayley graphs and actions on metric spaces.
