Waymo LLC is an American autonomous driving technology company headquartered in Mountain View, California. It is a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., the parent company of Google. Waymo operates a commercial self-driving taxi service, Waymo One, in several U.S. metropolitan areas, and is regarded as a leader in the development of autonomous vehicle technology.

History

The company began in 2009 as the Google Self-Driving Car Project, led by Sebastian Thrun, co-inventor of Google Street View. Its test cars — initially modified Toyota Priuses and later a custom-built pod-like prototype nicknamed "Firefly" — accumulated millions of miles on public roads. In October 2015 the project completed the first fully driverless ride on public roads, carrying a blind passenger in Austin, Texas. In December 2016 the project was spun out and rebranded as Waymo.

Service

In 2018 Waymo launched Waymo One in the Phoenix, Arizona area, the first commercial self-driving service, and in 2020 began offering fully driverless rides to the public there. The service has since expanded to San Francisco, Los Angeles, Austin and other cities, reaching hundreds of thousands of paid rides per week. Its vehicles, including the Jaguar I-Pace and Zeekr-based models, use a sensor suite of lidar, radar and cameras together with the "Waymo Driver" software system.

Technology and safety

Waymo combines on-road testing with large-scale simulation, in which billions of virtual miles are driven to expose the system to rare scenarios. The company publishes safety data and has partnered with insurers to analyse crash rates, reporting substantially lower rates of injury-causing crashes than human-driver benchmarks in its operating areas.
