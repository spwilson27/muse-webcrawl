Price

A price is the quantity of payment or compensation expected, required, or given by one party to another in return for a unit of a good or service. In modern economies, prices are usually expressed in units of some form of currency.

Function

Prices act as signals that coordinate the decisions of buyers and sellers: a rising price indicates relative scarcity and encourages increased production and conservation, while a falling price indicates relative abundance. Economists study how prices are formed through the interaction of supply and demand.

Related concepts

Price differs from value and cost: value is the worth a consumer assigns to a good, while cost is the expense of producing it. Businesses set prices using strategies such as cost-plus pricing, value-based pricing, and dynamic pricing, and consumers compare prices through measures like the consumer price index.
