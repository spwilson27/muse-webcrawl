FIA Formula 2 Championship

The FIA Formula 2 Championship is a single-specification motorsport championship sanctioned by the FIA and regarded as the primary feeder series to Formula One. The championship was created in 2017 as a rebranding of the long-running GP2 Series, itself a descendant of the original Formula 2 category (1948–2012).

Format

Rounds are held as support races at Formula One Grand Prix weekends. Each weekend comprises practice, qualifying, a shorter Sprint Race (with a partially reversed grid) and a longer Feature Race with a mandatory pit stop. All teams use identical Dallara chassis and 3.4-litre turbocharged V6 engines supplied by Mecachrome.

Champions

Early champions included Charles Leclerc (2017), George Russell (2018), Nyck de Vries (2019), Mick Schumacher (2020), Oscar Piastri (2021), Felipe Drugovich (2022) and Théo Pourchaire (2023)—several of whom graduated to Formula One.
