FIA Formula 2 Championship

The FIA Formula 2 Championship is a second-tier single-seater racing championship organised by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The championship was introduced in 2017 as a rebranding of the long-running GP2 Series, and it serves as the penultimate step on the FIA Global Pathway to Formula One, racing on Formula One Grand Prix weekends as a support series. All teams use an identical Dallara chassis and a Mecachrome turbocharged V6 engine, making it a spec series designed to emphasise driver skill.

History

Formula 2 was a historic category of the post-war decades; the modern championship was revived when the FIA rebranded GP2 — the series that had produced Lewis Hamilton, Nico Rosberg, and many others — for 2017. Charles Leclerc won the inaugural title and graduated directly to Formula One. Subsequent champions include George Russell (2018), Nyck de Vries (2019), Mick Schumacher (2020), Oscar Piastri (2021), Felipe Drugovich (2022), Théo Pourchaire (2023), and Gabriel Bortoleto (2024). Champions are barred by rule from continuing in the series, ensuring turnover.

Format

Each round features a sprint race (with a partially reversed grid) and a longer feature race with a mandatory pit stop, held at around fourteen Formula One events, mostly in Europe and the Middle East. The Dallara F2 2018 car served from 2018 to 2023; a new Dallara F2 2024 chassis, closer in appearance to contemporary F1 cars and designed to accommodate a wider range of drivers, debuted in 2024. Points towards an FIA Super Licence are among the highest available outside Formula One.
