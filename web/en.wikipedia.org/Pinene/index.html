Pinene

Chemical formulaC₁₀H₁₆
Isomersα-pinene, β-pinene
AppearanceColourless liquid with a pine-like odour

Pinene is a collection of unsaturated bicyclic monoterpene chemical compounds. Two structural isomers are found in nature: α-pinene and β-pinene. As the name suggests, both forms are important constituents of pine resin; they are also found in the resins of many other conifers and in a variety of non-coniferous plants.

Occurrence and uses

α-Pinene is the most widely encountered terpenoid in nature and is highly repellent to insects. It is the principal component of turpentine, obtained from pine resin. Industrially, pinene is converted into fragrances and flavour compounds, and it serves as a feedstock for the synthesis of camphor and other chemicals. Pinene is also emitted in large quantities by forests and contributes to atmospheric aerosol formation.

Biological effects

Pinene is a bronchodilator in humans at low exposure levels and has shown antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activity in studies. It is one of the compounds responsible for the characteristic aroma experienced in conifer forests.
