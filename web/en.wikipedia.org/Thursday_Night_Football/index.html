Thursday Night Football (often abbreviated as TNF) is the branding used for broadcasts of National Football League (NFL) games played on Thursday nights. The package debuted in 2006 on the NFL Network with a partial late-season schedule and expanded to a full season in 2012.

History

From 2014, games were simulcast in partnership with broadcast networks, first CBS and then NBC, and later Fox from 2018 to 2021. Beginning with the 2022 season, Amazon Prime Video became the exclusive home of Thursday Night Football under an eleven-year agreement, making it one of the first NFL packages carried primarily by a streaming service.

Criticism

The short rest between Sunday and Thursday games has drawn criticism from players and commentators over injury risk and quality of play, though the league has kept the package as a prominent primetime showcase.
