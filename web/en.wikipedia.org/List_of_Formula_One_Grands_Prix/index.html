List of Formula One Grands Prix

[Image: Formula One cars at the start of a Grand Prix]
A Grand Prix start.

A Grand Prix is a motor race forming part of the Formula One World Championship, held since 1950. The following table lists selected Grands Prix that have formed part of the championship, with their circuits and the number of times held.

French Grand PrixCircuit Paul Ricard1906 (F1 from 1950)

The British and Italian Grands Prix are the only events to have been held in every championship season since 1950. The Indianapolis 500 counted toward the championship from 1950 to 1960, though few Formula One regulars contested it.
