Chamberlin (disambiguation)

Chamberlin may refer to:

People

Thomas Chrowder Chamberlin (1843–1928), American geologist and educator, founder of the Journal of Geology, known for the Chamberlin–Moulton planetesimal hypothesis of the origin of the Solar System and for his "method of multiple working hypotheses". Edward Hastings Chamberlin (1899–1967), American economist at Harvard University who developed the theory of monopolistic competition, published in 1933. Clarence Duncan Chamberlin (1893–1976), American aviator who made the second transatlantic flight, to Germany, in 1927 shortly after Charles Lindbergh's crossing. William Henry Chamberlin (1897–1969), American journalist and historian of the Soviet Union. Joseph Chamberlin and others bear the surname in politics, science and the arts.

Places

Chamberlin is the name of settlements and features in the United States, including communities in Texas and elsewhere, and of Chamberlin Hall and other buildings named for the geologist at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and the University of Chicago. Mount Chamberlin in Alaska's Brooks Range is among the highest peaks in the range.

Other uses

The Chamberlin is an electro-mechanical keyboard instrument invented by Harry Chamberlin in California in the 1940s–1950s, using tape loops of recorded instruments; it is the direct predecessor of the British Mellotron. Chamberlin is also a surname of Norman origin, originally an occupational name for an official who looked after the lord's chamber (compare chamberlain).
