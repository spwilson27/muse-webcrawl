Tax Court of Canada

The Tax Court of Canada (French: Cour du canadienne de l'impôt) is a federal superior court in Canada that hears appeals relating to federal tax matters, principally under the Income Tax Act, the Excise Tax Act (GST/HST), and employment insurance and Canada Pension Plan rulings.

History and procedure

The court was established in 1983, succeeding the Tax Review Board and the Tax Appeal Board. It sits in cities across the country, with its main office in Ottawa. Cases proceed under either an informal procedure, for smaller amounts in dispute, or the general procedure. Appeals from the Tax Court go to the Federal Court of Appeal and, with leave, to the Supreme Court of Canada. The Chief Justice of the court is appointed by the Governor in Council.
