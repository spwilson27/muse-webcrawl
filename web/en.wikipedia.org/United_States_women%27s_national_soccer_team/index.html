United States women's national soccer team

The United States women's national soccer team (USWNT) represents the United States in international women's soccer. It is the most successful team in the history of the women's game, governed by the United States Soccer Federation.

Achievements

The team won the first FIFA Women's World Cup in China in 1991 and went on to win the tournament again in 1999 — famously beating China on penalties before a crowd of over 90,000 at the Rose Bowl — and in 2015 and 2019. It has also won five Olympic gold medals in women's soccer (1996, 2004, 2008, 2012, and 2024). Stars of successive generations have included Mia Hamm, Abby Wambach, Carli Lloyd, Megan Rapinoe, and Alex Morgan.

Equal pay campaign

The team became a prominent voice in the movement for gender equality in sport; after years of litigation and campaigning, it reached a landmark 2022 agreement with US Soccer that equalised pay and prize money with the men's national team, including pooling of World Cup bonuses.
