Chamber of Deputies (Italy)

The Chamber of Deputies (Camera dei Deputati) is the lower house of the bicameral Italian Parliament, the other being the Senate of the Republic. It meets at Palazzo Montecitorio in Rome.

Composition and elections

Following constitutional reform approved in 2020, the Chamber has 400 deputies (reduced from 630), elected for five-year terms by universal suffrage; some seats are reserved for Italians abroad. Deputies must be at least 25 years old, and voters must be at least 18.

Role

Italy has a system of perfect bicameralism: both chambers have equal powers in legislation and in granting confidence to the government. Laws must be approved in identical form by both houses before promulgation by the President of the Republic.
