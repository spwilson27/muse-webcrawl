List of secretaries of the treasury of the United States

The secretary of the treasury is the head of the United States Department of the Treasury and the chief financial officer of the federal government. The first secretary was Alexander Hamilton, appointed by President George Washington in 1789, who established the nation's financial system. The longest-serving secretary was Albert Gallatin (1801–1814), under Presidents Jefferson and Madison. The secretary's signature appears on United States paper currency.
