Alexander Lebed

Alexander Ivanovich Lebed (April 20, 1950 – April 28, 2002) was a Russian military officer and politician. A charismatic paratroop general, he came third in the 1996 Russian presidential election and later served as governor of Krasnoyarsk Krai.

BornApril 20, 1950, Novocherkassk, Russian SFSR
DiedApril 28, 2002, Krasnoyarsk Krai, Russia
OfficeGovernor of Krasnoyarsk Krai (1998–2002)

Military career

Lebed served as a paratrooper in Afghanistan and commanded airborne units in several late-Soviet conflicts. As commander of Russia's 14th Army in Moldova in 1992, he intervened to halt fighting in Transnistria, gaining national fame for his blunt, plainspoken style.

Politics

In the 1996 presidential election Lebed won about 14.5% of the vote in the first round. He endorsed Boris Yeltsin in the runoff and was appointed Secretary of the Security Council, in which role he negotiated the Khasavyurt Accord ending the First Chechen War. Dismissed by Yeltsin months later, he was elected governor of Krasnoyarsk Krai in 1998.

Death

Lebed died in a helicopter crash in the Sayan Mountains on April 28, 2002, while still in office.
