Mukherjee–Samarth family


Origin: Bengal and Maharashtra, India
Occupation: Film acting, directing, production
Notable members: Shobhna Samarth, Kajol, Rani Mukerji, Tanuja

The Mukherjee–Samarth family is a prominent Indian film family whose members have been involved in Hindi cinema (Bollywood) and Bengali cinema for several generations. It descends from the union of the Mukherjee family of Bengal and the Samarth family of Maharashtra, connected through the marriage of actress Shobhna Samarth and filmmaker Kumarsen Samarth, and later through the marriage of actress Tanuja (a Samarth) to director Shomu Mukherjee.

Overview

The family's presence in cinema dates to the 1930s and 1940s. Shobhna Samarth was a celebrated actress known for playing Sita in Ram Rajya (1943). Her daughters include Nutan, regarded as one of the greatest actresses in Indian cinema history, and Tanuja. Tanuja's daughters Kajol and Tanishaa Mukerji became actors in the 1990s.

The Mukherjee branch

Shomu Mukherjee, Tanuja's husband, was the son of Sashadhar Mukherjee, a founder of Filmalaya studios and a major producer. The wider Mukherjee clan includes producer Joy Mukherjee and is connected to the Ganguly family (of Ashok Kumar and Kishore Kumar). Actress Rani Mukerji belongs to this branch, being the daughter of director Ram Mukherjee.

Notable members

  • Nutan (1936–1991) — five-time Filmfare Best Actress winner.
  • Tanuja — leading actress of the 1960s and 1970s.
  • Kajol — one of the most successful Hindi film actresses of the 1990s onward; married to Ajay Devgn.
  • Rani Mukerji — acclaimed actress, married to producer-director Aditya Chopra.
  • Ayan Mukerji — director, son of actor Deb Mukherjee.
