Nicolas Hülkenberg (born August 19, 1987) is a German racing driver who has competed in Formula One for several teams including Williams, Force India, Renault, Racing Point, Aston Martin, Haas and Sauber. He won the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 2015 with Porsche in his first attempt at the race.

Hülkenberg won championships in Formula BMW, the A1 Grand Prix series for Team Germany, the Formula 3 Euro Series and the GP2 Series in 2009 before making his Formula One debut with Williams in 2010, taking a surprise pole position at the Brazilian Grand Prix in his rookie season.

Formula One career

Despite consistent performances, Hülkenberg for many years held the record for the most Formula One race starts without a podium finish, a streak that lasted 239 races. The record finally ended at the 2025 British Grand Prix at Silverstone, where, driving for Sauber, he finished third—achieving his first podium nearly fifteen years after his debut, in a result widely celebrated across the paddock.

He is noted for his qualifying pace, his one-lap speed in mixed conditions, and his role as a dependable development driver for midfield teams.
