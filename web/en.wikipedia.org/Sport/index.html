Sport

Sport is a physical activity or game, often competitive and organised, that maintains or improves physical ability and skills. Sport may provide enjoyment to participants and entertainment to spectators. The number of participants in a sport can vary from single contestants to hundreds of players, competing either as individuals, in teams, or against opponents.

Definition

Definitions generally require that sport be physical, competitive and governed by rules or customs. Bodies such as the Council of Europe and SportAccord have set criteria distinguishing sports from other pastimes; activities such as chess and poker are recognised as "mind sports" by some organisations.

History

Sport has ancient roots: wrestling and athletics appear in ancient Egyptian and Greek art, and the Olympic Games of ancient Greece date from 776 BC. The modern codification of sports rules began in 19th-century England, spreading worldwide. Professionalism, international federations, and the revived Olympic Games (from 1896) shaped modern sport.

Organisation and economics

International federations govern most sports worldwide. Professional sport is a major entertainment industry, with revenue from broadcasting rights, sponsorship, ticketing and merchandising, while mass participation is promoted for public health.
