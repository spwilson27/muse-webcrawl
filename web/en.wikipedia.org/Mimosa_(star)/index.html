Mimosa (star)

ConstellationCrux
Bayer designationBeta Crucis (β Cru)
Apparent magnitude1.25
Distanceabout 280 light-years

Mimosa is the second-brightest star in the constellation of Crux (the Southern Cross) and the 20th-brightest star in the night sky. Its Bayer designation is Beta Crucis.

Characteristics

Mimosa is a binary system whose primary is a massive blue giant or subgiant of spectral type B, a Beta Cephei variable that pulsates slightly in brightness. The system lies roughly 280 light-years from the Sun and is a member of the Scorpius–Centaurus association of young stars.

Name

The name Mimosa, approved by the International Astronomical Union in 2016, derives from the mimosa plant. Together with Acrux, Mimosa forms the long axis of the Southern Cross used for navigation in the southern hemisphere.
