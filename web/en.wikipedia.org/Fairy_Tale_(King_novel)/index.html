Fairy Tale is a dark fantasy novel by American author Stephen King, published on September 6, 2022, by Scribner. The novel follows 17-year-old Charlie Reade, who inherits keys to a hidden, otherworldly realm after the death of an elderly neighbour, and discovers a kingdom in peril where good and evil are locked in a struggle for survival.

Plot summary

Charlie Reade is a high-school student in Illinois whose mother died when he was seven and whose father subsequently struggled with alcoholism before recovering. Charlie befriends Howard Bowditch, a reclusive old man living in a decaying house on a hill with his elderly German Shepherd, Radar. After Bowditch suffers a serious fall, Charlie cares for him and for Radar, and is rewarded with a cassette tape explaining Bowditch's secret: in the shed behind his house is a portal to another world, a decaying fairy-tale kingdom called Empis, where a magical sundial can restore youth — and where an evil force known as the Flight Killer, Elden, has seized power.

Determined to use the sundial to restore the ageing Radar, Charlie descends into Empis, is drawn into the kingdom's rebellion, is imprisoned in the Deep Maleen, and eventually confronts the monstrous power behind the realm's curse before returning home.

Background and reception

King wrote much of the novel during the COVID-19 pandemic, asking himself what he could write that would make him happy; the result was a deliberate homage to the fairy tales and adventure stories of his youth, with echoes of H. P. Lovecraft, Ray Bradbury and the Oz books. The novel debuted at number one on The New York Times Best Seller list and received generally favourable reviews, with critics praising its storytelling warmth while noting its length. A film adaptation was announced with Paul Greengrass attached, later developing toward a television series format.
