Akshaye Khanna
Born: 28 March 1975, Bombay (Mumbai), India
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 1997–present
Parent: Vinod Khanna

Akshaye Khanna (born 28 March 1975) is an Indian actor who primarily works in Hindi cinema. The son of actor-politician Vinod Khanna, he made his debut in Himalay Putra (1997) and gained recognition the same year in J. P. Dutta's war film Border.

Career

Khanna earned critical acclaim and a Filmfare Award for his performance in Farhan Akhtar's Dil Chahta Hai (2001), a landmark coming-of-age film. He balanced mainstream comedies such as Hungama (2003) and Hulchul (2004) with praised dramatic turns in Gandhi, My Father (2007), in which he played Harilal Gandhi.

After a quieter period, he returned to prominence with antagonist and supporting roles in films such as Mom (2017), Ittefaq (2017), and Section 375 (2019), and later appeared in Drishyam 2 (2022).
