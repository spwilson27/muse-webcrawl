Åndalsnes is a town in Rauma Municipality in Møre og Romsdal county, Norway. Åndalsnes is the administrative center of Rauma Municipality. The town sits at the mouth of the Rauma River, at the shores of the Romsdalsfjord, surrounded by steep mountains including Romsdalshorn and the Troll Wall (Trollveggen), the tallest vertical rock face in Europe.

Transport and tourism

Åndalsnes is the terminus of the Rauma Line railway, which runs from Dombås through the Romsdalen valley and is considered one of Norway's most scenic rail journeys. The town is a popular base for hiking, climbing and fishing, and a port of call for cruise ships. The Norwegian Mountaineering Centre (Norsk Tindesenter) is located in the town.

History

Åndalsnes was heavily bombed during the Second World War, when British forces landed there in April 1940 during the Norwegian Campaign. The town was granted town status in 1996.
