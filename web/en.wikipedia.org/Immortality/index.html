Immortality

Immortality
ConceptEndless life; exemption from death
ContextsReligion, philosophy, mythology, science
RelatedEternal life, afterlife, life extension

Immortality is the concept of eternal life. Some modern species such as the jellyfish Turritopsis dohrnii may possess biological immortality. Throughout history, many religions, mythologies, and philosophies have featured beliefs in an immortal soul or an afterlife, and the pursuit of physical immortality appears in legends such as the Fountain of Youth and the elixir of life.

Religious views

Most religious traditions hold that some aspect of the person—typically the soul or spirit—survives bodily death, whether through resurrection, reincarnation, or continued existence in an afterlife.

Life extension and transhumanism

Modern life-extension research studies the biology of ageing and senescence, while transhumanist thinkers speculate about technologies such as cryonics and mind uploading that might one day extend human life indefinitely.
