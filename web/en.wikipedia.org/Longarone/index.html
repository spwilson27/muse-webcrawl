Longarone is a town and comune in the province of Belluno, in the Veneto region of north-east Italy, situated on the River Piave near the Vajont valley in the Dolomites foothills.

Vajont disaster

On the night of 9 October 1963, a massive landslide from Monte Toc fell into the reservoir behind the Vajont Dam, displacing a huge volume of water that overtopped the dam and destroyed Longarone and neighbouring villages within minutes. Around 2,000 people were killed, among them most of Longarone's population below the flood line; the town was almost entirely destroyed even though the dam itself survived largely intact.

Reconstruction

Longarone was rebuilt on the same site in the years after the disaster, with a modernist layout and a new church designed by Giovanni Michelucci. The town hosts an annual international gelato exhibition (MIG, Mostra Internazionale del Gelato Artigianale), reflecting the tradition of ice-cream makers from the Belluno valleys who emigrated across Europe.
