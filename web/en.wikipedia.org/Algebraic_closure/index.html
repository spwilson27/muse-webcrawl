Algebraic closure

AreaField theory
NotationKalg, K̄
Key propertyEvery non-constant polynomial has a root

In mathematics, particularly abstract algebra, an algebraic closure of a field K is an algebraic extension of K that is algebraically closed. It is one of many closures in mathematics.

Using Zorn's lemma or the weaker ultrafilter lemma, it can be shown that every field has an algebraic closure, and that the algebraic closure of a field K is unique up to an isomorphism that fixes every member of K. Because of this essential uniqueness, we often speak of the algebraic closure of K, rather than an algebraic closure of K.

Examples

The fundamental theorem of algebra states that the algebraic closure of the field of real numbers is the field of complex numbers. The algebraic closure of the field of rational numbers is the field of algebraic numbers. There are many countable algebraically closed fields within the complex numbers, and containing the rational numbers; these are the algebraic closures of finite extensions of the rationals.

The algebraic closure of the finite field of order q, for q a prime power, is a countably infinite field that contains a copy of the field of order qn for each positive integer n.

Properties

For any field K, the algebraic closure of K has the same cardinality as K if K is infinite, and is countably infinite if K is finite. Every field has an algebraic extension which is an algebraic closure; among all such extensions there is a minimal one, and all algebraic closures of K are isomorphic as K-algebras. The algebraic closure of a field of characteristic zero is a model of the theory of algebraically closed fields of that characteristic, a fact used in model theory.

Separable closure

An algebraic closure Kalg of K contains a separable closure Ksep consisting of all (algebraic) separable extensions of K within Kalg. The separable closure is a Galois extension of K, and the quotient of Kalg by Ksep is a purely inseparable extension. For a perfect field (for instance, any field of characteristic zero or any finite field), the separable closure equals the algebraic closure.
