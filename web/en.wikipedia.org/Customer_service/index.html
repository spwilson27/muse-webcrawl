Customer service

Customer service is the assistance and advice provided by a company to those who buy or use its products or services, before, during and after a purchase. It is considered a key part of customer relationship management and a major influence on customer satisfaction, loyalty and repeat business.

Delivery

Service may be delivered in person, by telephone through call centres, by email and live chat, through social media, or by self-service systems such as help centres, FAQs, automated phone menus and chatbots. Organisations commonly set service-level targets for response and resolution times and monitor quality through satisfaction surveys, complaint handling and metrics such as Net Promoter Score.

History

Systematic customer service grew with telephone support in the twentieth century and expanded rapidly with the call-centre industry and, later, the internet. Outsourcing of service operations to specialised firms and offshore centres became widespread from the 1990s, while recent decades have seen increasing automation and AI-assisted support.

Automation

Automated customer service uses interactive voice response, knowledge bases and artificial intelligence to answer routine enquiries, reserving human agents for complex or sensitive issues. Poorly designed automation is a frequent source of consumer frustration.
