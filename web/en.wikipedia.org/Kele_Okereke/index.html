Kele Okereke

Kelechukwu "Kele" Okereke (born 13 October 1981) is a British singer, songwriter and musician. He is best known as the lead singer and rhythm guitarist of the indie rock band Bloc Party.

Early life

Okereke was born in Liverpool to Nigerian Igbo parents and grew up in East London. He met guitarist Russell Lissack in 1998 and, with bassist Gordon Moakes and drummer Matt Tong, formed the band that became Bloc Party in 1999.

Career

Bloc Party's debut album, Silent Alarm (2005), was a critical and commercial success, with Okereke's lyrics addressing politics, urban life and identity. The band followed it with A Weekend in the City (2007), Intimacy (2008), Four (2012), Hymns (2016) and Alpha Games (2022).

Okereke released his first solo album, The Boxer, in 2010, an electronic-leaning record, followed by Trick (2014), Fatherland (2017) — a folk-influenced album recorded under his full name and reflecting on fatherhood — and 2042 (2019). He has also written and performed music for theatre, including the musical Leave to Remain (2019) with Matt Jones.

Personal life

Okereke is gay, having come out publicly in 2010, and has spoken about race, sexuality and representation in rock music. He is a father, a theme explored on Fatherland.
