Counter-Strike: Global Offensive

Counter-Strike: Global Offensive (CS:GO) is a 2012 multiplayer tactical first-person shooter developed by Valve and Hidden Path Entertainment. It is the fourth game in the Counter-Strike series.

Gameplay

Two teams, Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists, compete in objective-based rounds such as bomb defusal and hostage rescue. Players earn money for actions to buy weapons and equipment. Modes include Competitive, Casual, Deathmatch and Arms Race. The game became free-to-play in 2018 and built a major esports scene with Major championships.

Legacy

CS:GO was succeeded in 2023 by Counter-Strike 2, which replaced it on Steam while retaining player inventories.
