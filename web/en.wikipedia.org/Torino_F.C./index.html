Torino Football Club


The club crest and maroon colours

Torino Football Club, commonly known as Torino or simply Toro, is an Italian professional football club based in Turin, Piedmont. Founded in 1906, the club plays in Serie A and wears distinctive maroon (granata) shirts.

Torino's greatest era was the 1940s, when the team known as the Grande Torino — captained by Valentino Mazzola — won five consecutive league titles (1943 and 1946–1949) and dominated Italian football. The team was wiped out on 4 May 1949 in the Superga air disaster, when the plane carrying the squad home from a friendly in Lisbon crashed into the retaining wall of the Basilica of Superga, killing all 31 people on board. The tragedy remains one of the defining events in Italian sporting history.

The club won its seventh (and most recent) scudetto in 1975–76 and has won the Coppa Italia five times. After bankruptcy in 2005 the club was refounded under current owner Urbano Cairo. Torino's home ground is the Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino, and its fierce local rivalry with Juventus is contested in the Derby della Mole.
