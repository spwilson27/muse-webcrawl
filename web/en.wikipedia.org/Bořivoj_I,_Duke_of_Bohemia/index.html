Bořivoj I, Duke of Bohemia

Bořivoj I (c. 852 – c. 889) was the first historically documented Duke of Bohemia and progenitor of the Přemyslid dynasty. He ruled from about 867 until his death.

According to tradition recorded in the chronicle of Cosmas of Prague, Bořivoj was baptised, together with his wife Ludmila, by Saint Methodius during a visit to the court of Svatopluk I of Great Moravia around 883, an event traditionally regarded as the introduction of Christianity to Bohemia. Ludmila was later canonised as Saint Ludmila, grandmother of Saint Wenceslaus.

Bořivoj founded churches at Levý Hradec and at Prague, where he established his seat at what became Prague Castle, moving the ducal residence from Levý Hradec. He was succeeded by his sons, first Spytihněv I and then Vratislaus I.
