Audi R8

Manufacturer: Audi (quattro GmbH / Audi Sport GmbH)

Production: 2006–2024

Class: Sports car (S)

Layout: Mid-engine, all-wheel drive

The Audi R8 is a mid-engine, two-seater sports car manufactured by the German automobile manufacturer Audi. It was introduced at the Paris Motor Show in 2006 and is based on the Lamborghini Gallardo platform in its first generation, sharing its aluminium space frame construction. The car is built by Audi's high-performance subsidiary, originally quattro GmbH and later Audi Sport GmbH, at a specially constructed facility in Neckarsulm, Germany.

First generation (2006–2015)

The first-generation R8 was launched with a 4.2-litre V8 engine producing 420 PS (309 kW), coupled to a six-speed manual or the R tronic automated manual transmission. A 5.2-litre V10 variant followed in 2009, sharing its engine architecture with the Lamborghini Gallardo LP560-4. The R8 became Audi's flagship performance model and served as the basis for the successful R8 LMS GT3 racing car.

Second generation (2015–2024)

The second-generation R8, unveiled at the 2015 Geneva Motor Show, was offered exclusively with the 5.2-litre V10 engine in standard (540 PS) and plus (610 PS) forms, later updated to performance quattro specification with up to 620 PS. A rear-wheel-drive variant, the R8 RWS and later R8 RWD, was also produced. Production of the R8 ended in 2024 after more than 44,000 examples across both generations.

Motorsport

The R8 LMS and its successors became among the most successful GT3 customer racing cars, winning multiple editions of the 24 Hours Nürburgring, the Spa 24 Hours, and the Bathurst 12 Hour, as well as numerous championships worldwide.
