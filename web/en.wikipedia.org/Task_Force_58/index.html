Task Force 58

The Fast Carrier Task Force was the principal striking arm of the US Pacific Fleet.

Task Force 58 (TF 58), known as the Fast Carrier Task Force, was the main striking force of the United States Navy during the second half of the Pacific War. When operating under Admiral William Halsey's Third Fleet it was designated Task Force 38; under Admiral Raymond Spruance's Fifth Fleet it was Task Force 58. The force was built around large fleet aircraft carriers screened by fast battleships, cruisers, and destroyers.

Operations

The Fast Carrier Task Force was established in January 1944 under Vice Admiral Marc Mitscher. It first struck the Japanese base at Truk in Operation Hailstone in February 1944, and thereafter supported the advance across the Central Pacific: the Marshall Islands, the Marianas, the Battle of the Philippine Sea in June 1944, the Palaus, the Philippines, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa. In 1945 its aircraft attacked the Japanese home islands.

Organization

At its peak in 1945 the task force comprised more than a dozen fleet and light carriers organized into task groups, with over 1,000 aircraft, supported by a fast replenishment train that allowed the force to remain at sea for extended periods — a logistical innovation central to its effectiveness.
