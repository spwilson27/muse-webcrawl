Astronomy is a monthly American magazine about astronomy, published since August 1973. Targeting amateur astronomers, it contains articles on the practice of observing the night sky, telescope equipment, astrophotography, and general-interest articles on astronomy and space science.

History

Astronomy was founded in 1973 by Stephen A. Walther, a graduate of the University of Wisconsin–Stevens Point. The magazine's first issue, dated August 1973, appeared the same year. Walther later sold the magazine to Astromedia Corporation, and in 1985 it was acquired by Kalmbach Publishing of Waukesha, Wisconsin, which also published the competing magazine Sky & Telescope after acquiring it in 2019.

Content

Regular features include a monthly sky chart, observing tips, telescope and product reviews, and articles by contributors such as Stephen James O'Meara, Phil Harrington, and Bob King. David J. Eicher served as editor for many years. The magazine also organizes star parties and astronomy tours.
