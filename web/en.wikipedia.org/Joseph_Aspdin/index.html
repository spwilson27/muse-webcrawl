Joseph Aspdin

Joseph Aspdin
Born: 1778, Leeds, England
Died: 20 March 1855, Wakefield
Known for: Inventing Portland cement

Joseph Aspdin (1778 – 20 March 1855) was an English bricklayer, businessman and inventor who obtained the patent for Portland cement on 21 October 1824.

Portland cement

Aspdin's patent described heating a mixture of limestone and clay and grinding it to a powder. He named the product Portland cement because the set material resembled Portland stone, a popular building stone from the Isle of Portland. His son William Aspdin later developed an improved, higher-temperature process closer to modern cement.

Life

Aspdin worked as a bricklayer in Leeds, experimenting with cement in his kitchen. He set up manufacturing in Wakefield, where the family business continued under William.

Legacy

Portland cement became the most widely used cement type worldwide and a fundamental material of modern construction.
