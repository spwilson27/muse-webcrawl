Sven Magnus Øen Carlsen (born 30 November 1990) is a Norwegian chess grandmaster widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. He was World Chess Champion from 2013 to 2023, a five-time World Rapid Chess Champion and multiple-time World Blitz Chess Champion, and has held the No. 1 position in the FIDE world rankings since 2011.

Career

Carlsen became a grandmaster in 2004 at age 13 and first reached world No. 1 in 2010. He won the World Championship in 2013 by defeating Viswanathan Anand and defended the title against Anand (2014), Sergey Karjakin (2016), Fabiano Caruana (2018), and Ian Nepomniachtchi (2021). In 2022 he announced he would not defend the title again, citing diminished motivation, and the crown passed to Ding Liren in 2023. His peak rating of 2882, achieved in 2019, is the highest in chess history.

Playing style

Carlsen is noted for his endgame technique, positional mastery, and ability to grind down opponents from seemingly equal positions, as well as exceptional performance in rapid and blitz formats and online chess.
