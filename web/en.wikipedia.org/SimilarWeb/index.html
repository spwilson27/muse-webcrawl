Similarweb Ltd. is an Israeli web analytics and digital intelligence company founded in 2007 by Or Offer. The company provides web analytics services and offers its customers information on their clients' and competitors' website traffic and performance.

Similarweb gathers data from a panel of web users, internet service providers, direct measurement from websites and apps, and partnerships, and uses it to estimate traffic, engagement metrics, audience demographics, and traffic sources for websites and mobile apps worldwide. Its free website ranking pages, which list the top sites by country and category, became a widely cited reference for web popularity.

History

The company raised multiple rounds of venture capital and grew through acquisitions of related analytics firms. In May 2021, Similarweb completed an initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol SMWB. The company maintains that it is headquartered both in Givatayim, Israel, and in New York City, with offices in several other countries.

Criticism

Like other panel-based measurement services, Similarweb's estimates for smaller websites have been criticised as imprecise, since they are modelled rather than directly measured.
