Ahom–Mughal conflicts

The Ahom–Mughal conflicts were a series of wars in the 17th century between the Ahom kingdom of Assam and the Mughal Empire, fought mainly over control of the Brahmaputra valley. They culminated in the Ahom victory at the Battle of Saraighat in 1671.

Course

Mughal expansion eastward brought repeated invasions from 1615 onward. The Mughal commander Mir Jumla occupied the Ahom capital Garhgaon in 1662 and imposed the Treaty of Ghilajharighat, but disease and guerrilla resistance forced a withdrawal.

Saraighat and aftermath

Under the general Lachit Borphukan, the Ahoms defeated a large Mughal force in the naval Battle of Saraighat (1671) near present-day Guwahati. After the Battle of Itakhuli (1682), the Ahoms recovered Kamrup and fixed the boundary at the Manas River, ending Mughal ambitions in Assam.
