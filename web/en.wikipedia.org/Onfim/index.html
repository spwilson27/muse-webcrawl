Onfim

Lived13th century, Novgorod
Known forBirch-bark drawings and school exercises
AgeEstimated six or seven years old

Onfim (Old Novgorodian: Онфиме) was a boy of about six or seven who lived in Novgorod, in modern Russia, in the thirteenth century. He is known from his homework exercises and drawings, which he inscribed on birch bark — the common writing material of medieval Novgorod — and which were preserved in the city's clay soil. Seventeen of Onfim's birch-bark items were excavated in 1956.

The writings

Onfim's pieces include alphabet practice, syllable exercises, psalm fragments, and a note from one Gavrila to his brother — probably a classmate's work. Alongside the lessons, Onfim drew pictures: a knight on horseback striking down an enemy, labelled with his own name, apparently depicting himself as a warrior; beasts; and a scene often interpreted as himself in the form of a fantastic animal. One drawing shows a man hanging from a portage-like structure with a caption identifying the figure as a classmate, "Danilo."

Significance

Birch-bark manuscripts demonstrate that literacy in medieval Novgorod extended beyond clergy and merchants to children and laypeople. Onfim's casual sketches are among the most famous examples, giving an unusually personal glimpse of a child's life and schooling in the medieval Rus'. The originals are held in Novgorod's museum collections.
