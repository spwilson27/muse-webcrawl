Jaguar Cars

Jaguar Cars is a British luxury vehicle brand, now part of Jaguar Land Rover, owned by Tata Motors since 2008. Founded in 1922 as the Swallow Sidecar Company by William Lyons and William Walmsley, the company originally made motorcycle sidecars before moving into car bodies and, eventually, complete automobiles. The Jaguar name first appeared in 1935, and the company was renamed Jaguar Cars in 1945.

History

The SS Jaguar models of the 1930s established the marque's reputation for stylish, affordable performance. After the Second World War, Jaguar produced a string of celebrated sports cars: the XK120 (1948), then the fastest production car in the world; the C-Type and D-Type racing cars, which won the 24 Hours of Le Mans five times in the 1950s; and the E-Type (1961), famously praised by Enzo Ferrari as the most beautiful car ever made.

The XJ saloon, launched in 1968, defined the brand's luxury line for decades. Jaguar merged into British Motor Holdings in 1966 and later British Leyland, was privatised in 1984, and was acquired by Ford in 1989. Ford sold Jaguar and Land Rover to Tata Motors in 2008.

Recent years

Under Jaguar Land Rover, the range expanded to include the XE and XF saloons, the F-Type sports car, and the F-Pace, E-Pace and all-electric I-Pace SUVs. In 2021 JLR announced that Jaguar would become an all-electric luxury brand, and in late 2024 the brand unveiled a controversial rebrand and the Type 00 concept car ahead of a new generation of electric vehicles.

Motorsport

Beyond Le Mans success in the 1950s, Jaguar returned to top-level racing in Formula E with the Jaguar TCS Racing team, and previously competed in Formula One from 2000 to 2004 as Jaguar Racing, the team that later became Red Bull Racing.
