The VT220 is a computer terminal introduced by Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC) in November 1983. It succeeded the widely used VT100 and became one of the most emulated terminal designs in computing.

Features

The VT220 featured a compact keyboard with a design that influenced later PC keyboards, a tilt-and-swivel monitor, and support for downloadable character sets, allowing user-defined glyphs. It supported ANSI escape sequences and offered multinational character sets, making it popular in international markets.

Variants

Related models included the VT240, which added graphics capability, and the VT241 colour variant. The VT220's escape sequences and behaviour form the basis of the "vt220" terminal type still referenced by terminal emulators today.
