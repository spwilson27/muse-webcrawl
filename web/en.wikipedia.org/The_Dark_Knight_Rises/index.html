The Dark Knight Rises is a 2012 superhero film directed by Christopher Nolan, who co-wrote the screenplay with his brother Jonathan. It is the concluding instalment of Nolan's The Dark Knight trilogy, following Batman Begins (2005) and The Dark Knight (2008).

Plot

Eight years after the death of Harvey Dent, Bruce Wayne has retired as Batman. When the masked mercenary Bane seizes control of Gotham City and threatens it with a nuclear fusion reactor, Wayne returns to confront him, aided by cat burglar Selina Kyle and young police officer John Blake. Broken and imprisoned by Bane, Wayne must escape, reclaim Gotham, and make a final sacrifice to save the city.

Reception

The film received positive reviews and grossed over $1 billion worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of all time on its release.
