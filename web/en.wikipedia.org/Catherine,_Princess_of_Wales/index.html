Catherine, Princess of Wales

Catherine, Princess of Wales (born Catherine Elizabeth Middleton; 9 January 1982) is a member of the British royal family. She is married to William, Prince of Wales, heir apparent to the British throne, making her a likely future queen consort.

Early life and education

Catherine Middleton was born in Reading, Berkshire, and raised in Bucklebury. She was educated at St Andrew's School, Marlborough College, and the University of St Andrews, where she studied history of art and met Prince William in 2001. Before her marriage she worked in retail and for her family's business, Party Pieces.

Marriage and family

The couple's engagement was announced in November 2010, and they married on 29 April 2011 at Westminster Abbey. They have three children: Prince George (born 2013), Princess Charlotte (born 2015), and Prince Louis (born 2018). Upon the accession of Charles III in September 2022, Catherine became Princess of Wales.

Public work

Her patronages and campaigning focus on early childhood development, mental health, addiction, and the arts. She founded the Royal Foundation Centre for Early Childhood and the "Heads Together" mental health initiative with William and Prince Harry. In 2024 she announced she was undergoing treatment for cancer and later reported being in remission.
