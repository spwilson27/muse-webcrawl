History of Microsoft

Founded4 April 1975, Albuquerque, New Mexico
FoundersBill Gates and Paul Allen
HeadquartersRedmond, Washington
Key productsMS-DOS, Windows, Office, Azure

The history of Microsoft began on 4 April 1975, when Bill Gates and Paul Allen founded the company in Albuquerque, New Mexico, to sell a BASIC interpreter for the Altair 8800 microcomputer. Its decisive break came in 1980, when IBM contracted Microsoft to provide an operating system for the IBM PC; Microsoft acquired and adapted 86-DOS, released as MS-DOS, and retained the right to license it to other manufacturers.

Windows era

Microsoft launched Windows in 1985 as a graphical shell for DOS; Windows 3.0 (1990) and Windows 95 made it dominant on personal computers, while Microsoft Office became the leading productivity suite. The company went public in 1986 and faced a landmark U.S. antitrust case, United States v. Microsoft, decided in 2001.

Internet, mobile, and cloud

After initially underestimating the internet, Microsoft pivoted with Internet Explorer and later entered gaming with Xbox (2001) and cloud computing with Azure (2010). Under CEO Satya Nadella (from 2014) the company refocused on cloud services and cross-platform software, acquired LinkedIn and GitHub, and became one of the world's most valuable companies, with heavy investment in artificial intelligence.
