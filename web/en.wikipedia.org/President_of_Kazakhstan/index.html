President of Kazakhstan

The President of the Republic of Kazakhstan is the head of state of Kazakhstan and the commander-in-chief of the country's armed forces. The president is the highest office in Kazakhstan and is granted special powers by the constitution that establishes a strongly presidential system of government.

The office was established on 24 April 1990, a year before the dissolution of the Soviet Union, when the Supreme Soviet of the Kazakh Soviet Socialist Republic created the post and elected Nursultan Nazarbayev as the first president. Nazarbayev went on to lead the independent republic from 1991 until his resignation in March 2019, winning five successive elections, several of them criticized by international observers.

History

Nazarbayev's successor, Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, initially served as interim president before winning the June 2019 presidential election. Following the January 2022 unrest, Tokayev pursued constitutional reforms that limited the president to a single seven-year term, a change ratified by referendum in June 2022. Tokayev was re-elected in November 2022 for a seven-year term.

Election and powers

The president is elected by direct popular vote for a single seven-year term and may not be re-elected. The president appoints the Prime Minister with the consent of the Mazhilis (the lower house of parliament), determines the government's structure, and may dissolve parliament under specified conditions. The president also conducts foreign policy, negotiates international treaties, and issues decrees and orders having the force of law.

Residence

The official residence is the Akorda Presidential Palace, located on the left bank of the Ishim River in the capital, Astana. The palace, completed in 2004, is notable for its blue and gold dome topped by a spire with a golden sun.
