Cullercoats

Illustration: Cullercoats

Cullercoats is a coastal village in the borough of North Tyneside, Tyne and Wear, England, historically within Northumberland. It lies between Tynemouth and Whitley Bay and is served by Cullercoats station on the Tyne and Wear Metro.

History

Cullercoats was long a fishing community, famous for its fishwives who carried creels of fish for sale and featured in many paintings; the American artist Winslow Homer lived and worked in the village from 1881 to 1882, producing celebrated watercolours of the fisherfolk. Coal was also exported from a small harbour in earlier centuries.

The bay and lifeboat

Cullercoats Bay, a sandy beach sheltered by two piers, is popular with bathers. A Royal National Lifeboat Institution lifeboat station was established in the village in 1852. The Watch House (1879) and the Dove Marine Laboratory of Newcastle University are notable landmarks.
