Marvel UK

Marvel UK was a British comic book publisher, an imprint of Marvel Comics founded in 1972 to reprint American Marvel material for the British market. It later became known for original British-created series.

History

Marvel UK launched weekly black-and-white reprint titles such as The Mighty World of Marvel and Spider-Man Comics Weekly. In the late 1970s, under editor Dez Skinn, the company began producing original material, including Captain Britain Weekly, Hulk Comic and the long-running Doctor Who Weekly/Monthly.

Original creations

Original Marvel UK properties include Captain Britain (later developed by Alan Moore and Alan Davis), Death's Head, the Knights of Pendragon, Motormouth and Warheads. In the 1990s Marvel UK expanded with its "Genesis" line of titles aimed at the American market, but the line was cancelled amid the comics industry downturn of the mid-1990s. The remaining publishing operations were absorbed into Panini Comics, which continues to publish Marvel material in the UK under licence.
