Stamford Bridge (stadium)

Stamford Bridge is a football stadium in Fulham, adjacent to the borough of Chelsea in West London. It is the home of Premier League club Chelsea. With a capacity of 40,173, it is the ninth largest venue for the 2024–25 Premier League season.

History

Opened in 1877, the stadium was initially used by the London Athletic Club for athletics meetings. In 1905 the ground was offered to Fulham, who turned it down; instead, the new Chelsea Football Club was formed to play there, and it has been Chelsea's home ever since. The name derives from a local stream, Stanford Creek, and a bridge over it on the Fulham Road.

The ground once formed a vast oval bowl with a record attendance of 82,905 in 1935. Redevelopment in the 1990s converted it into a compact rectangular all-seater stadium. It hosted FA Cup finals in the early 1920s and has also staged cricket, rugby, speedway, greyhound racing and American football. Plans for a major expansion or rebuild of Stamford Bridge have been discussed for many years.
