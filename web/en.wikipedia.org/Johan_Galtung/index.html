Johan Galtung

Johan Vincent Galtung (24 October 1930 – 17 February 2024) was a Norwegian sociologist regarded as the principal founder of peace and conflict studies.

Galtung founded the Peace Research Institute Oslo (PRIO) in 1959 and the Journal of Peace Research in 1964. He coined concepts including structural violence, direct violence, and the distinction between negative and positive peace, and developed the TRANSCEND method of conflict mediation. He received the Right Livelihood Award in 1987.
