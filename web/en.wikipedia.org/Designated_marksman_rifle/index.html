Designated marksman rifle

A designated marksman rifle used by infantry squads

A designated marksman rifle (DMR) is a high-precision rifle used by a designated marksman within an infantry squad, intended to fill the range gap between standard service rifles and dedicated sniper rifles — typically engaging targets at 300–800 metres.

Characteristics

DMRs are usually semi-automatic, magazine-fed rifles chambered in intermediate or full-power cartridges (such as 7.62×51mm NATO), fitted with magnified optics and often bipods and match-grade barrels. Examples include the Mk 14 EBR, the M110 SASS, the SVD Dragunov, and the L129A1.

Role

The designated marksman remains embedded with the squad and also performs ordinary infantry tasks, unlike a sniper who operates in a specialized team.
