Grease (film)

StarringJohn Travolta, Olivia Newton-John, Stockard Channing, Jeff Conaway
Released1978
Based onGrease, the 1971 stage musical by Jim Jacobs and Warren Casey

Grease is a 1978 American musical romantic comedy film directed by Randal Kleiser, based on the 1971 stage musical of the same name. It stars John Travolta as Danny Zuko and Olivia Newton-John as Sandy Olsson, two teenagers from different worlds who fall in love during the summer of 1958 and unexpectedly meet again at Rydell High School.

Plot

Greaser gang leader Danny must reconcile his tender summer romance with Australian transfer student Sandy against his tough image with the T-Birds, while Sandy is befriended by the Pink Ladies. The film follows the school year through dances, a drag race and a carnival finale, ending with Danny and Sandy reconciled and flying off in a levitating car.

Music

The soundtrack was a phenomenon, spending weeks at number one and producing hit singles including "You're the One That I Want", "Hopelessly Devoted to You", "Sandy" and the title song "Grease", written by Barry Gibb and sung by Frankie Valli. Songs from the stage show such as "Summer Nights" and "Greased Lightnin'" became standards.

Reception and legacy

Grease was the highest-grossing film of 1978 and for decades the highest-grossing movie musical ever made. Although some critics questioned its sanitized nostalgia, it became a lasting cultural touchstone, spawning a 1982 sequel, the prequel series Grease: Rise of the Pink Ladies (2023), and countless sing-along re-releases. In 2020 it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry.
