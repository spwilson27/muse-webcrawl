Mini Countryman

Manufacturer: Mini (BMW)

Production: 2010–present

Class: Subcompact crossover SUV

Layout: Front-engine, front- or all-wheel drive

The Mini Countryman, also called the Mini Countryman Cooper, is a subcompact crossover SUV manufactured and marketed by Mini. Launched at the 2010 Geneva Motor Show, it was the first Mini crossover and the first five-door model of the modern BMW-owned era, substantially larger than the Mini hatch.

Generations

The first generation (R60) was built by Magna Steyr in Austria and offered with petrol and diesel engines and optional ALL4 all-wheel drive; a plug-in hybrid followed. The second generation (F60, 2017) moved to BMW's UKL platform shared with the BMW X1. The third generation (U25, 2023) grew again and added a fully electric version built in Leipzig, Germany, alongside petrol models.

Motorsport

A competition version, the Mini John Cooper Works WRC, campaigned in the World Rally Championship from 2011, and privately entered Countryman-based cars achieved multiple victories in the Dakar Rally during the 2010s and early 2020s.
