Pierre Schaeffer

Pierre Schaeffer (14 August 1910 – 19 August 1995) was a French composer, writer and broadcasting engineer who founded musique concrète, composing with recorded sounds rather than written notes.

Musique concrète

Working at French radio (RTF), Schaeffer presented the Cinq études de bruits in 1948, including the "Étude aux chemins de fer" built from train sounds. He founded the Groupe de Recherche de Musique Concrète and collaborated with Pierre Henry on the Symphonie pour un homme seul (1950).

Theory

In his treatise Traité des objets musicaux (1966) Schaeffer proposed the "sound object" and modes of reduced listening (écoute réduite), hearing sounds apart from their sources — ideas foundational to acousmatic music and sound studies.
