Adblock Plus

Adblock Plus (ABP) is a free and open-source browser extension for content filtering and ad blocking. It is developed by Eyeo GmbH, a German software company. The extension is available for major browsers and Android, and has been one of the most popular browser add-ons since its release.

History

Adblock Plus grew out of the earlier Adblock extension for Mozilla Firefox. Wladimir Palant rewrote the code and released Adblock Plus 0.6 in 2006, introducing an improved interface and filter subscription features. Versions for Chrome, Safari, Opera, Internet Explorer, and Microsoft Edge followed. Filtering is driven by community-maintained filter lists, most prominently EasyList, which specify rules matching advertising and tracking resources.

Acceptable Ads controversy

In 2011 Adblock Plus introduced its "Acceptable Ads" programme, which by default allows advertisements deemed non-intrusive, and under which larger publishers pay Eyeo a fee for whitelisting. The programme has been criticised as a conflict of interest and praised by supporters as a compromise encouraging less intrusive advertising. Users can disable the feature.
