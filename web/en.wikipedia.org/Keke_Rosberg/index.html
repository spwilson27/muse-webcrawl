Keijo Erik "Keke" Rosberg (born 6 December 1948) is a Finnish former racing driver and the 1982 Formula One World Champion. Known for his spectacular, sideways driving style and heavy moustache, he was the first Finn to win the Formula One championship, and with his son Nico Rosberg, champion in 2016, forms one of only two father-son pairs to have both won the title.

Early career

Rosberg was born in Solna, Sweden, where his Finnish parents were studying, and grew up in Finland. He started in karting and worked his way through Formula Vee and Formula Super Vee — winning European titles — and Formula 2, funding his racing partly through his own team management. He won the 1978 International Trophy at a rain-soaked Silverstone in the uncompetitive Theodore, which helped him secure regular Formula One drives.

Formula One

After difficult seasons with Theodore, ATS, Wolf, and Fittipaldi, Rosberg joined Williams in 1982. In a chaotic season marked by the FISA–FOCA war and the deaths of Gilles Villeneuve and Riccardo Paletti, his consistency and a single victory — at the Swiss Grand Prix at Dijon — earned him the championship with 44 points, despite winning only one race. He stayed with Williams through the turbo era, winning in Detroit, Dallas, and Adelaide, before a final season with McLaren in 1986.

Later career

After Formula One, Rosberg raced sports cars with Peugeot and touring cars with Opel in the DTM, where he also ran his own team. He managed the careers of Finnish drivers including Jyrki Järvilehto (JJ Lehto), Mika Häkkinen, and his son Nico, and worked as a television commentator in Finland and Germany.
