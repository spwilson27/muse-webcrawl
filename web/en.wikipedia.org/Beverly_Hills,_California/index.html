Beverly Hills is a city in Los Angeles County, California, United States, surrounded almost entirely by the city of Los Angeles. Famous worldwide as a home of movie stars and for its luxury shopping district, it is one of the most affluent communities in the United States; its ZIP Code, 90210, became a pop-culture byword for glamour.

History

The land was part of a Spanish-era rancho and was farmed in lima beans into the early 20th century. Oil prospector Burton Green and his partners bought the land hoping to find oil; finding water instead, they developed it as a planned residential city named after Beverly Farms in Massachusetts. The city was incorporated in 1914, and film stars Douglas Fairbanks and Mary Pickford built their estate, Pickfair, in 1919, beginning the association with Hollywood wealth. The Beverly Hills Hotel, opened in 1912, predates the city itself.

Geography and landmarks

Beverly Hills is roughly triangular. South of Santa Monica Boulevard lie "the Flats", with wide palm-lined streets; north of it, homes climb into the hills. The commercial heart, the "Golden Triangle", contains Rodeo Drive, celebrated for designer boutiques. Other landmarks include the Beverly Gardens Park with its cactus garden and rose gardens, the Greystone Mansion (1928), and the palm canyon estate district along Canon Drive.

Economy and culture

The city's economy centres on luxury retail, hotels, and entertainment-industry offices; talent agencies such as William Morris Endeavor are headquartered there. Beverly Hills has its own police and fire departments, school district, and the Beverly Hills Public Library. It appears frequently in film and television, from Pretty Woman to the teen drama Beverly Hills, 90210.
