Tanuja

Tanuja Samarth (born 23 September 1943), known mononymously as Tanuja, is an Indian actress who works primarily in Hindi films. A member of the celebrated Mukherjee–Samarth family, she is the daughter of actress Shobhna Samarth and the younger sister of actress Nutan. She is the mother of actresses Kajol and Tanishaa Mukerji.

Career

Tanuja began acting as a child and made her adult debut in Chhabili (1960), produced by her mother. She gained recognition with Memdidi (1961) and Baharen Phir Bhi Aayengi (1966), and won praise for her vivacious, natural screen persona in films such as Jewel Thief (1967), Jeene Ki Raah (1969) — for which she won the Filmfare Award for Best Supporting Actress — Haathi Mere Saathi (1971), and Anubhav (1971).

She also acted in Bengali cinema, notably opposite Uttam Kumar in films such as Deya Neya (1963) and Anthony Firingee (1967). From the late 1970s she moved into character and mother roles, and later appeared in Marathi films and television, including a turn as a judge on family reality programming with her daughter Kajol.

Personal life

Tanuja married filmmaker Shomu Mukherjee in 1973; they had two daughters, Kajol and Tanishaa. She comes from a family central to Indian cinema across four generations, stretching from her grandmother Rattan Bai to her daughters' generation.
