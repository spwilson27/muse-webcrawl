Red Cloud

Red Cloud (Lakota: Maȟpíya Lúta; c. 1822 – December 10, 1909) was a leader of the Oglala Lakota from 1868 to 1909, and one of the most capable Native American opponents the United States Army faced in the western territories.

Red Cloud's War

He led a successful campaign known as Red Cloud's War (1866–1868) over control of the Powder River Country in present-day Wyoming and Montana. Following the Fetterman Fight of 1866, the United States agreed to the Treaty of Fort Laramie (1868), abandoning its Bozeman Trail forts — a rare negotiated victory for a Native American leader.

Later life

Red Cloud afterwards advocated for his people through diplomacy, leading delegations to Washington and settling at the Pine Ridge Agency in South Dakota. He clashed with government agents and with rival Lakota leaders, and remained a prominent figure until his death in 1909. The town of Red Cloud, Nebraska, and the Red Cloud Indian School are named for him.
