Channel Four Television Corporation is a British publicly owned media company that operates the television channels Channel 4, E4, More4, Film4, 4seven, and related streaming services. Unusually, it does not produce programmes itself: it commissions or buys all of its content from independent production companies, under a statutory remit to innovate and serve diverse audiences.

History

Channel 4 began broadcasting on 2 November 1982, initially subsidised by the ITV companies in exchange for advertising sales. Under the Broadcasting Act 1990 it became a statutory corporation in 1993, selling its own advertising while remaining in public ownership. Proposals to privatise the broadcaster were abandoned by the government in 2023.

Operations

The corporation is funded entirely by advertising and commercial revenue, at no cost to the taxpayer, while reinvesting its surplus in content. Its "nations and regions" strategy moved hundreds of jobs to a national headquarters in Leeds and creative hubs in Glasgow and Bristol. Channel 4 News, Film4 productions, and Paralympics coverage are signature outputs.
