List of Resident Evil media

Resident Evil, known in Japan as Biohazard, is a Japanese horror franchise created by Capcom, beginning with the 1996 video game Resident Evil. The franchise spans video games, films, animation, novels and comics.

Video games

The main series includes Resident Evil (1996), Resident Evil 2 (1998), Resident Evil 3: Nemesis, Code: Veronica, Resident Evil 4 (2005), Resident Evil 5, Resident Evil 6, Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (2017) and Resident Evil Village (2021), plus the Revelations sub-series and remakes of the early titles.

Films

The live-action film series directed chiefly by Paul W. S. Anderson (2002–2016) starred Milla Jovovich as Alice, followed by the reboot Welcome to Raccoon City (2021). Computer-animated films include Degeneration, Damnation, Vendetta and Death Island.
