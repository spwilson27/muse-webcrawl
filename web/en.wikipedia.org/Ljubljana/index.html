Ljubljana

Ljubljana is the capital and largest city of Slovenia, with a population of roughly 300,000. It lies in the Ljubljana Basin on the Ljubljanica River, between the Alps and the Karst, beneath a medieval castle.

History

The Roman city of Emona stood here from the first century AD. The medieval town grew under Habsburg rule, and was known in German as Laibach. After an earthquake in 1895 it was rebuilt partly in the Vienna Secession style; between the wars the architect Jože Plečnik reshaped it with the Triple Bridge, river embankments, the market arcades and the National and University Library, giving the centre its distinctive character. Ljubljana became the capital of socialist Slovenia within Yugoslavia and, from 1991, of independent Slovenia.

Today

The pedestrianised old town, castle hill, Tivoli Park and café-lined riverbanks anchor a compact university and government city. It was named European Green Capital in 2016, and Plečnik's works were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2021.
