Russian invasion of Ukraine

Date24 February 2022 – present
LocationUkraine (with spillover into Russia)
BelligerentsRussia (with Belarus support) vs Ukraine
ContextRusso-Ukrainian War (begun 2014)

On 24 February 2022, Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine, in a major escalation of the Russo-Ukrainian War that had begun in 2014 with Russia's annexation of Crimea and the war in Donbas. It is the largest conventional war in Europe since the Second World War. Russian president Vladimir Putin announced a "special military operation", citing the protection of Russian speakers, the "demilitarisation" and "denazification" of Ukraine, and opposition to NATO enlargement; these justifications have been widely rejected internationally.

Course of the war

The initial offensive attacked from the north (via Belarus, toward Kyiv), the east and the south (from Crimea). The drive on Kyiv failed amid Ukrainian resistance and Russian logistical problems, and Russia withdrew from northern Ukraine in April 2022, refocusing on the Donbas and consolidating a land corridor along the Sea of Azov after the siege of Mariupol. Evidence of mass killings of civilians in Bucha and other liberated towns drew global condemnation and war-crimes investigations.

Ukraine's counteroffensives retook much of Kharkiv Oblast in September 2022 and the city of Kherson that November. In September 2022 Russia announced the annexation of four Ukrainian oblasts, a move condemned as illegal by the UN General Assembly. The front then largely stabilised into attritional warfare, including the prolonged battle for Bakhmut, captured by Russian and Wagner Group forces in May 2023, and a Ukrainian counteroffensive in summer 2023 that made limited gains. In August 2024 Ukraine launched a surprise incursion into Russia's Kursk Oblast. Drone and missile strikes on cities, infrastructure and shipping in the Black Sea have been persistent features, as has fighting around strongpoints such as Avdiivka and Pokrovsk in 2024–2025.

Impact

The war has caused tens of thousands of civilian deaths, displaced millions of Ukrainians (the largest refugee crisis in Europe since 1945), and killed or wounded hundreds of thousands of soldiers on both sides. It triggered sweeping Western sanctions on Russia, large-scale military aid to Ukraine, Finland's and Sweden's accession to NATO, and global disruptions to food and energy supplies. The International Criminal Court issued an arrest warrant for Vladimir Putin in March 2023 over the deportation of Ukrainian children. Diplomatic efforts to end the war have continued without a settled peace agreement.
