Interstellar

Interstellar is a 2014 epic science fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan, who co-wrote the screenplay with his brother Jonathan Nolan. Set in a future where Earth is becoming uninhabitable, it follows a team of explorers who travel through a wormhole near Saturn in search of a new home for humanity. The film stars Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Jessica Chastain and Michael Caine.

Plot

In the mid-21st century, crop failures and dust storms threaten humanity's survival. Former pilot Joseph Cooper is recruited for a secret mission: a wormhole discovered near Saturn leads to a distant galaxy with potentially habitable planets, scouted earlier by the Lazarus missions. Cooper leaves his children, including his daughter Murph, to pilot the spacecraft Endurance through the wormhole. Time dilation near the black hole Gargantua means hours on a planet's surface cost years back on Earth, and the story interweaves the explorers' journey with Murph's decades-long effort to solve the equation of gravity needed to evacuate Earth's population.

Production and science

Physicist Kip Thorne served as executive producer and scientific consultant; his equations for the black hole's appearance drove new visual-effects rendering techniques and led to published scientific papers. The film was shot on 70mm and IMAX film, with extensive practical sets and miniatures. Hans Zimmer's organ-driven score became one of his most celebrated works.

Reception

Interstellar grossed over $700 million worldwide and won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects. It was praised for its ambition, emotional core and depiction of relativistic effects, and has grown in reputation since release, frequently appearing on lists of the best science fiction films.
