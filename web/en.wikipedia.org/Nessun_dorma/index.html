Nessun dorma

"Nessun dorma" (Italian: "None shall sleep") is an aria from the final act of Giacomo Puccini's opera Turandot (1926), sung by the character Calaf. It is one of the best-known tenor arias in all of opera.

Context and text

Calaf, having answered Turandot's three riddles, offers her a chance to escape the marriage if she can discover his name by morning. Confident of victory, he sings of the coming dawn: "Nessun dorma… Tu pure, o Principessa… Vincerò! I will be victorious!"

In popular culture

Luciano Pavarotti's recording became a global hit after it was used as the theme for the BBC's coverage of the 1990 FIFA World Cup in Italy; a recording by the Three Tenors further cemented its fame. The aria has since been performed and recorded by numerous tenors and crossover artists.
