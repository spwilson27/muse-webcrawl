Startup India

Startup India is the Government of India's flagship entrepreneurship initiative.

Startup India is an initiative of the Government of India, launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 16 January 2016, aimed at promoting innovation, building a strong ecosystem for startups, and fostering sustainable economic growth and large-scale employment opportunities. The initiative is administered by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Key features

Recognized startups receive benefits including self-certification under labour and environmental laws, a three-year income tax holiday, exemptions from certain compliance and capital gains provisions, fast-tracked patent examination with rebates on fees, and easier public procurement norms. The programme also created a Fund of Funds for startups, managed through SIDBI, to provide venture capital, and supports incubators, research parks, and innovation challenges across the country.

Impact

India has developed one of the world's largest startup ecosystems, with well over one hundred thousand DPIIT-recognized startups and more than a hundred unicorns (startups valued above US$1 billion), concentrated in hubs such as Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, and Mumbai.
