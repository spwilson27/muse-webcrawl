Integrated Defence Staff
Formed23 November 2001
HeadquartersNew Delhi, India
RoleTri-service coordination of the Indian Armed Forces

The Integrated Defence Staff (IDS) is an organisation responsible for fostering coordination and enabling prioritisation across the different branches of the Indian Armed Forces. It was created on 23 November 2001 following the recommendations of the Group of Ministers that reviewed national security after the Kargil War.

Role and structure

Headed for many years by the Chief of Integrated Defence Staff to the Chairman Chiefs of Staff Committee (CISC), the IDS brings together officers of the Army, Navy and Air Force for joint planning, intelligence, medical services, training and doctrine. Following the creation of the post of Chief of Defence Staff in 2019 and the Department of Military Affairs, the IDS was reorganised as part of the new joint structures supporting the CDS.
