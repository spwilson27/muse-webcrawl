Linux Foundation

The Linux Foundation (LF) is an American non-profit technology consortium founded in 2000 through the merger of Open Source Development Labs and the Free Standards Group, to standardise, promote and support Linux and collaborative open-source development.

The Foundation employs core kernel developers including Linus Torvalds, hosts hundreds of projects such as Kubernetes (via the CNCF), Hyperledger, the Yocto Project and LF Energy, runs training and certification, and organises events including Open Source Summit.
