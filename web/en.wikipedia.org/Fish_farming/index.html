Fish farming

Fish farming or pisciculture involves commercially raising fish, most commonly for food, in fish tanks or artificial enclosures such as fish ponds. It is a particular type of aquaculture.

Methods

Systems range from extensive ponds and rice-fish culture, through cage and net-pen farming in lakes and coastal waters, to intensive land-based recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS).

Major farmed species

The most farmed fish include carps, Atlantic salmon, tilapia, catfish, rainbow trout, and milkfish. China is the largest producer.

Environmental issues

Concerns include nutrient pollution, sea lice, disease spread to wild stocks, escapes, and the use of fishmeal from forage fish.
