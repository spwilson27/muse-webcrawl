Median

The median of a finite list of numbers is the middle value when the list is sorted: half the values lie above it and half below. For an even number of values, the median is usually the mean of the two middle values.

Properties

Unlike the arithmetic mean, the median is robust to outliers and skewed distributions, making it preferred for income and house-price statistics. It is a special case of a quantile — the second quartile — and can be generalised to probability distributions, where a median m satisfies P(X ≤ m) ≥ ½ and P(X ≥ m) ≥ ½.

Comparison

Together with the mean and mode, the median is one of the standard measures of central tendency; their relationship reveals the skewness of a distribution.
