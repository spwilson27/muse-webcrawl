Mass Effect 2

2010 action role-playing video game by BioWare

Mass Effect 2 is a 2010 action role-playing video game developed by BioWare and published by Electronic Arts. The sequel to Mass Effect (2007) and the second entry in the Mass Effect series, it was released for Windows and Xbox 360 in January 2010, with a PlayStation 3 version following in 2011. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games of all time.

Plot

Two years after the attack on the Citadel, Commander Shepard — killed in an ambush by the mysterious Collectors and rebuilt by the pro-human organisation Cerberus — is sent to investigate the abduction of entire human colonies. Working for Cerberus's leader, the Illusive Man, Shepard assembles a team of specialists for a "suicide mission" through the Omega-4 relay to the Collectors' base. Player choices, including decisions imported from the first game, shape the story and determine which characters survive the final mission.

Gameplay

Compared with its predecessor, the game streamlined inventory and exploration in favour of cover-based third-person shooting, interrupt-driven dialogue choices, and character loyalty missions. A remastered version was included in Mass Effect Legendary Edition (2021).

Reception

The game won numerous game-of-the-year awards and praise for its character writing, voice acting and the structure of its climactic mission; it holds scores in the mid-90s on review aggregators.
