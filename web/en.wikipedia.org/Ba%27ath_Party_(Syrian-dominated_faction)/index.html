Ba'ath Party (Syrian-dominated faction)

The Arab Socialist Ba'ath Party – Syria Region, commonly the Ba'ath Party (Syrian-dominated faction), was the Syrian branch of the Ba'ath movement after its 1966 split from the Iraqi-dominated faction. Based in Damascus, it ruled Syria from 1963 until the fall of Bashar al-Assad in December 2024.

Arab Socialist Ba'ath Party – Syria Region
Founded: 7 April 1947 (unified party)
Ideology: Ba'athism, Arab nationalism, Arab socialism (Neo-Ba'athism 1963–1970)

Historical leaders: Michel Aflaq, Salah al-Din al-Bitar, Hafez al-Assad, Bashar al-Assad

Origins

The Ba'ath Party was founded in Damascus in 1947 by Michel Aflaq, Salah al-Din al-Bitar, and Zaki al-Arsuzi's followers, merging Arab nationalist and socialist currents. It came to power in Syria through the military coup of 8 March 1963.

The 1966 split and Neo-Ba'athism

An internal struggle between the party's historic civilian leadership and a radical military committee culminated in the coup of 23 February 1966, which expelled Aflaq and Bitar and installed Salah Jadid's faction. The Iraqi branch rejected the new Damascus leadership, splitting the Ba'ath into rival Syrian- and Iraqi-dominated factions, each with its own "National Command." The Syrian regime of 1966–1970, often called Neo-Ba'athist, pursued radical socialist policies and a more military, sectarian-tinged politics than the original party doctrine.

The Assad era

Defense Minister Hafez al-Assad seized power in the 1970 "Corrective Movement," softening some radical policies while consolidating an authoritarian system in which the Ba'ath was constitutionally the "leading party in society and the state." His son Bashar al-Assad succeeded him in 2000. The party dominated Syrian political life through the National Progressive Front, the People's Assembly, and mass organizations, until the regime collapsed in December 2024 amid a renewed civil war offensive, after which the party's activities were suspended and its assets seized by the transitional authorities.
