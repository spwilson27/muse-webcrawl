Crime prevention

Crime prevention refers to strategies and measures that seek to reduce the risk of crimes occurring and their potential harmful effects, as an alternative or complement to punishment and enforcement. It encompasses government and community efforts aimed at would-be offenders, places, and potential victims.

Approaches

Major approaches include situational crime prevention, which alters environments to increase the effort and risks of offending (such as improved lighting, locks, and surveillance); social or developmental prevention, which addresses risk factors through early-childhood, school, and community programmes; and community policing and problem-oriented policing strategies.

Evidence

Evaluations suggest that focused interventions — such as hot spots policing, early family interventions, and certain situational measures — can reduce crime without simply displacing it, though effectiveness varies by context and implementation.
