Washer (hardware)

A washer is a thin plate (typically disk-shaped, but sometimes square) with a hole (typically in the middle) that is normally used to distribute the load of a threaded fastener, such as a bolt or nut.

Functions

Washers spread clamping load over a larger area, protect surfaces from damage by the fastener, and can prevent loosening. Some types also act as spacers, springs, or seals.

Types

Common types include plain (flat) washers, spring washers, split lock washers, toothed lock washers, tab washers, and fender washers with a large outside diameter. They are usually made of steel, stainless steel, brass, nylon or rubber, and standardised under specifications such as DIN and ISO.
