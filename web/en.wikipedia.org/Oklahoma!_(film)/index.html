Oklahoma! (film)

Oklahoma! is a 1955 American musical film based on the 1943 stage musical by Richard Rodgers and Oscar Hammerstein II, itself drawn from Lynn Riggs's 1931 play Green Grow the Lilacs. Directed by Fred Zinnemann and distributed by Magna Corporation and 20th Century Fox, it stars Gordon MacRae as Curly, Shirley Jones as Laurey (in her film debut), Rod Steiger as Jud Fry, Gloria Grahame as Ado Annie, and Gene Nelson as Will Parker.

Production

The film is noted as the first feature photographed in Todd-AO, a 70 mm widescreen process developed by Mike Todd. Because of studio caution about the new format, every scene was shot twice — once in Todd-AO at 30 frames per second and once in CinemaScope at 24 frames per second — creating two subtly different versions of the film. Choreographer Agnes de Mille recreated her celebrated stage dances, including the dream ballet.

Plot and reception

Set in Oklahoma Territory in 1906, the story follows cowboy Curly McLain and farm girl Laurey Williams, whose courtship is threatened by the brooding hired hand Jud Fry, against a backdrop of the farmers-and-cattlemen conflict. The film was a major commercial success, and its title song later became the state song of Oklahoma. In 2007 it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry.
