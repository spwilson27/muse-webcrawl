ALS Association

The ALS Association is an American nonprofit organisation founded in 1985 that fights amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS, also known as Lou Gehrig's disease) by funding research, providing care services and support to people living with the disease, and advocating for public policy.

Activities

The association operates a network of chapters and certified centres across the United States, offering support groups, equipment loans, and multidisciplinary clinics. Its research programme funds laboratory and clinical studies and has supported work on genetics, biomarkers, and clinical trials for ALS.

Ice Bucket Challenge

The association gained worldwide attention in the summer of 2014 through the Ice Bucket Challenge, a viral social-media campaign in which participants dumped buckets of ice water over themselves and donated. The campaign raised about $115 million for the association in a few months and funded research that contributed to identifying the gene NEK1 as associated with ALS. It remains one of the most successful viral fundraising campaigns ever.
