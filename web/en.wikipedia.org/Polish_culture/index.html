Culture of Poland

The culture of Poland is the product of its geography and distinct historical evolution, closely connected with its intricate thousand-year history. Many regard its unique character as having developed as a result of its geography at the confluence of various European regions.

Poland's culture has been shaped by Latin and Byzantine influences, Catholic traditions, and periods of partition and occupation that reinforced national identity through language, literature and the arts. Major contributions include the music of Fryderyk Chopin, the scientific work of Nicolaus Copernicus and Marie Curie, and a rich literary tradition.

Arts and traditions

Polish folk traditions vary by region, with distinctive costumes, dances such as the polonaise and mazurka, and crafts including paper cutouts (wycinanki) and painted eggs (pisanki) for Easter. Cuisine features pierogi, bigos, żurek and kiełbasa.
