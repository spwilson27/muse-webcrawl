Center for Atmospheric Sciences

The Center for Atmospheric Sciences is a research centre at Hampton University in Hampton, Virginia, United States. It conducts research and education in atmospheric and planetary science, with particular emphasis on satellite remote sensing of the atmosphere and climate.

Research

The centre's work includes the study of aerosols, radiation balance, and atmospheric chemistry using data from NASA Earth-observing satellites, as well as the analysis of weather systems in the context of the celestial and terrestrial observing frameworks used by modern meteorology.

Education and outreach

As part of Hampton University, a historically Black university, the centre supports graduate training in atmospheric science and works to broaden participation in the geosciences. Its scientists collaborate with NASA Langley Research Center, located nearby, on instrument development and mission science.
