RTÉ

Raidió Teilifís Éireann (RTÉ)
Type: Public service broadcaster
Country: Ireland
Founded: 1 June 1960 (as Radio Éireann authority)
Headquarters: Donnybrook, Dublin

Raidió Teilifís Éireann (RTÉ; "Radio [and] Television of Ireland") is the national public service broadcaster of Ireland. It produces and broadcasts programmes on television, radio, and online in English and Irish. RTÉ is financed primarily by a television licence fee supplemented by advertising revenue.

Services

Its television channels include RTÉ One and RTÉ2, the RTÉ News channel, and RTÉjr; radio services include RTÉ Radio 1, RTÉ 2FM, RTÉ lyric fm, and the Irish-language RTÉ Raidió na Gaeltachta. The RTÉ Player provides streaming and catch-up content. Long-running programmes include The Late Late Show (since 1962) and the soap opera Fair City.

History

Irish radio broadcasting began in 1926 with 2RN under the Department of Posts and Telegraphs. The Radio Éireann authority was established in 1960, and television began on 31 December 1961. A 2023 scandal over undisclosed payments to presenter Ryan Tubridy prompted major governance reviews and funding reform debates.
