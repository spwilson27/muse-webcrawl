Chixoy Dam

Chixoy Dam
Location: Chixoy River, Alta Verapaz, Guatemala
Type: Embankment dam with hydroelectric plant
Capacity: About 300 MW
Completed: 1983

The Chixoy Dam (Planta Hidroeléctrica Chixoy) is a hydroelectric dam on the Chixoy River in Alta Verapaz, Guatemala. Its power station, among the country's largest, supplies a substantial share of Guatemala's electricity via a long tunnel to turbines at Quixal.

Construction and displacement

Built with World Bank and Inter-American Development Bank financing during Guatemala's civil war, the project displaced Maya Achi communities of the Río Negro area. Between 1980 and 1982, hundreds of villagers who resisted resettlement were killed in a series of massacres by the military and paramilitaries, known as the Río Negro massacres.

Reparations

After decades of advocacy, Guatemala adopted a reparations plan in 2014 for communities harmed by the dam's construction, providing compensation and development projects.
