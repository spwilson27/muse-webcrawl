Fire whirl

A fire whirl or fire devil is a whirlwind induced by a fire, composed of flame, ash and burning debris. Rising hot air and turbulent winds create a rotating column that intensifies combustion and can spread embers.

Large fire whirls in wildfires and firestorms — such as during the 1923 Great Kantō earthquake and the 2018 Carr Fire in California — can reach tornado-like intensity and cause severe damage.
