Pierre Christin

Pierre Christin (born 27 July 1938) is a French writer of comics and novels, best known as co-creator, with artist Jean-Claude Mézières, of the science-fiction comics series Valérian and Laureline (from 1967), one of the most influential European comics series.

Career

Christin studied at the Sorbonne and taught in the United States, where he reunited with childhood friend Mézières; their collaboration began in Pilote magazine. Valérian combined space opera with political and social themes and influenced generations of creators; Luc Besson adapted it as the film Valerian and the City of a Thousand Planets (2017).

Other work

With artists such as Enki Bilal, Christin created politically charged graphic novels including The Black Order Brigade and The Hunting Party (Partie de chasse). He also wrote novels, screenplays and founded a journalism school programme in Bordeaux.
