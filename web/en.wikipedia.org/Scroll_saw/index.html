Scroll saw

A scroll saw is a small electrically or pedal-operated saw used to cut intricate curves in wood, metal or other materials. Its fine, reciprocating blade allows tight turns and delicate internal cutouts that would be impossible with most other saws, making it the tool of choice for fretwork, marquetry, intarsia and wooden jigsaw puzzles.

Description

The blade is held in tension between two arms and moves rapidly up and down while the workpiece rests on a small table that can be tilted for bevel cuts. Blades are thin with fine teeth and can be unhooked and threaded through a drilled hole in the workpiece to make internal cuts. Variable-speed motors, blade guides and air blowers that clear sawdust from the cutting line are common features.

Uses

Scroll sawing is a popular hobby craft, used for ornaments, signs, toys and decorative panels. Before powered scroll saws, the same work was done with a hand fretsaw or treadle machine.
