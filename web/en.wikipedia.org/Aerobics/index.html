Aerobics

Aerobics is a form of physical exercise that combines rhythmic aerobic exercise with stretching and strength training routines with the goal of improving all elements of fitness (flexibility, muscular strength, and cardiovascular fitness). It is usually performed to music and may be practiced in a group setting led by an instructor.

Aerobics
Type: Physical exercise
Popularized: 1968, Kenneth H. Cooper
Variations: Step aerobics, water aerobics, dance aerobics

History

The term and method were developed by Dr. Kenneth H. Cooper, whose 1968 book Aerobics promoted exercise for cardiovascular health. In the 1980s, classes popularized by figures such as Jane Fonda and Richard Simmons turned aerobics into a mass fitness phenomenon, spawning styles such as step aerobics.

Forms

Variations include dance aerobics, step aerobics, water (aqua) aerobics, and sport aerobics, a competitive discipline combining choreography with gymnastic elements governed at the international level by the FIG.
