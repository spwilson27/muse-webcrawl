Orthogonal polynomials

In mathematics, an orthogonal polynomial sequence is a family of polynomials such that any two different polynomials in the sequence are orthogonal to each other under some inner product, typically an integral with a weight function over an interval.

Classical families

The most widely used families are the Legendre polynomials, the Chebyshev polynomials of the first and second kind, the Hermite polynomials, and the Laguerre polynomials. The Jacobi polynomials generalise several of these.

Properties

Orthogonal polynomials satisfy a three-term recurrence relation and a second-order differential equation. Their roots are real, distinct, and lie within the interval of orthogonality, which makes them central to Gaussian quadrature. They arise throughout physics, for example in solving Laplace's equation in spherical coordinates and in quantum mechanics.
