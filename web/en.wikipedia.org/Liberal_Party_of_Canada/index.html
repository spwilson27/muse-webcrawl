Liberal Party of Canada

The Liberal Party of Canada (French: Parti libéral du Canada) is a federal political party in Canada. Founded in 1867, it is the oldest active federal political party in the country and has dominated Canadian politics for much of its history, earning the nickname "Canada's natural governing party".

History

The party descends from the Clear Grits of Canada West and the Parti rouge of Canada East. Liberal governments under leaders such as Alexander Mackenzie, Wilfrid Laurier, William Lyon Mackenzie King, Louis St. Laurent, Lester B. Pearson, and Pierre Trudeau shaped Canadian institutions including medicare, the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, official bilingualism, and multiculturalism.

Ideology

The party occupies the centre to centre-left of Canadian politics, advocating liberalism, fiscal responsibility balanced with social programmes, and a strong federal state. Prominent later leaders include Jean Chrétien, Paul Martin, and Justin Trudeau, who led the party back to government in 2015.
