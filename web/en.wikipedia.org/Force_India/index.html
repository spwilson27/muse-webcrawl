Force India Formula One Team Limited, commonly known as Force India and later Sahara Force India, was a Formula One racing team and constructor that competed from 2008 to 2018. The team was based in Silverstone, England, at the former Jordan Grand Prix factory, and raced under an Indian licence. It was founded by Indian businessman Vijay Mallya and Dutch businessman Michiel Mol after they bought the Spyker F1 team in 2007.

The Force India VJM10 of 2017, in its distinctive pink livery.

History

Force India scored its first points and a surprise pole position with Giancarlo Fisichella at the 2009 Belgian Grand Prix, where Fisichella finished second. The team steadily improved, finishing fourth in the Constructors' Championship in both 2016 and 2017 despite one of the smallest budgets on the grid. Notable drivers included Adrian Sutil, Paul di Resta, Nico Hülkenberg, Sergio Pérez, and Esteban Ocon; Pérez delivered several podium finishes, including third places at the 2014 Bahrain and 2016 Monaco and European Grands Prix.

Administration and sale

In July 2018, amid Mallya's legal and financial troubles, the team entered administration. Its assets were purchased by a consortium led by Lawrence Stroll, and the team re-entered the 2018 championship as Racing Point Force India before becoming Racing Point and, later, Aston Martin F1 Team—still operating from the same Silverstone base.
