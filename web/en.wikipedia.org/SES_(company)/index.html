SES (company)

SES S.A. is a Luxembourgish communications satellite operator supplying video and data connectivity worldwide to broadcasters, content and internet service providers, mobile and fixed network operators, governments and institutions. Headquartered in Betzdorf, Luxembourg, SES operates a fleet of more than 70 satellites in geostationary orbit (GEO) and medium Earth orbit (MEO), reaching over a billion people worldwide and delivering video services to more than 350 million households.

History

SES was founded in 1985 as Société Européenne des Satellites (SES) under the backing of the Luxembourg government. Its first satellite, Astra 1A, was launched in December 1988 and began delivering television channels to European homes from the 19.2° East orbital position, pioneering direct-to-home satellite television in Europe. The company expanded globally through acquisitions and new ventures, including SES Americom, SES New Skies and a stake in O3b Networks, whose medium Earth orbit constellation it fully acquired in 2016.

Services

SES provides satellite capacity and managed services for video distribution, government applications, maritime and aeronautical connectivity, and fixed data networks. Its two business units are SES Video and SES Networks. In 2024 SES announced an agreement to acquire rival operator Intelsat, creating one of the largest satellite operators in the world.
