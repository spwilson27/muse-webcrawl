Lenin Mausoleum

Lenin Mausoleum is the resting place of Vladimir Lenin, whose embalmed body has lain on public display in Red Square, Moscow, since shortly after his death in 1924. The stepped granite pyramid by architect Alexey Shchusev became the ceremonial centre of the Soviet state.

History

A temporary wooden mausoleum was erected within days of Lenin death; the stone structure followed in 1930. Stalin body joined Lenin from 1953 until 1961, when de-Stalinisation removed it to the Kremlin Wall Necropolis. Leaders reviewed parades from the mausoleum rostrum, and an honour guard stood watch until 1993.

The body

A dedicated laboratory maintains the embalmed remains through periodic re-embalming and constant temperature and humidity control. Since 1991 the mausoleum future — burial for Lenin or preservation — has been periodically debated in Russia, but the display continues.
