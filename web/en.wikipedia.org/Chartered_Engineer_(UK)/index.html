Chartered Engineer (CEng) is a professional registration grade and protected title in the United Kingdom, awarded by the Engineering Council through its licensed professional engineering institutions. Chartered Engineers are characterised by their ability to develop solutions to engineering problems using new or existing technologies, through innovation, creativity and change.

Requirements

Registration requires demonstration of competence and commitment as set out in the UK Standard for Professional Engineering Competence and Commitment (UK-SPEC). Candidates typically hold an accredited integrated master's degree (MEng) or equivalent knowledge and understanding, together with responsible professional experience, and must pass a professional review interview conducted by a licensed institution such as the Institution of Civil Engineers, the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, or the Institution of Engineering and Technology.

Related grades

The Engineering Council also registers Incorporated Engineers (IEng) and Engineering Technicians (EngTech). Unlike some countries, the practice of engineering is not itself restricted by law in the UK, but the CEng title is legally protected. The title is internationally recognised through agreements such as the Washington Accord and the International Professional Engineers Agreement, and Chartered Engineers may also register as European Engineers (Eur Ing).
