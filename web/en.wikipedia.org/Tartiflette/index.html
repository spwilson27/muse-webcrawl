Tartiflette

Tartiflette is a dish from Savoy in the French Alps, made with potatoes, reblochon cheese, lardons and onions, often with a splash of white wine. Despite its rustic image, it was popularised in the 1980s by the Syndicat Interprofessionnel du Reblochon to boost sales of the cheese at ski resorts.

Preparation

Sliced or cubed potatoes are combined with fried lardons and onions, seasoned, and baked in a dish whose top is covered with a wheel or wedge of reblochon, rind side up, until the cheese melts and browns. Variants include croziflette, made with crozets pasta, and versions using other washed-rind cheeses. The name derives from the Savoyard word tartiflâ, meaning potatoes.
