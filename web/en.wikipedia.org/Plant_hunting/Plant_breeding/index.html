Plant breeding

Plant breeding is the manipulation of plant species to create new varieties with desirable traits. Farmers have selected seed from the best-performing plants for thousands of years, domesticating crops such as wheat, maize and rice from wild ancestors.

Classical and modern breeding

Classical breeding relies on crossing and selection over many generations. Modern programmes add hybrid seed production, tissue culture, mutation breeding, molecular markers and transgenic or gene-edited lines to speed progress and introduce traits unavailable within a species.

Importance

Breeding underpins global food security, delivering varieties resistant to diseases such as wheat rust, tolerant of drought and flooding, and adapted to local soils and climates.
