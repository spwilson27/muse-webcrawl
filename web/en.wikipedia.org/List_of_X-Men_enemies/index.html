List of X-Men enemies

Magneto, the X-Men's first and most enduring adversary.

The X-Men, a team of mutant superheroes in Marvel Comics, have faced a large rogues' gallery of enemies since their debut in The X-Men #1 (1963), created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Their adversaries include rival mutants, anti-mutant human organizations, cosmic entities and robotic Sentinels.

Major enemies

  • Magneto — Erik Lehnsherr, the "Master of Magnetism", founder of the Brotherhood of Mutants and the X-Men's very first foe; his stance has shifted between villain and ally over the decades.
  • Apocalypse — En Sabah Nur, an ancient mutant who seeks to judge mutantkind through "survival of the fittest".
  • Mister Sinister — Nathaniel Essex, a geneticist obsessed with the Summers bloodline.
  • Mystique — Raven Darkhölme, shapeshifter and frequent leader of the Brotherhood.
  • Sabretooth — Victor Creed, Wolverine's brutal nemesis.
  • Juggernaut — Cain Marko, stepbrother of Professor X, empowered by the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak.
  • The Sentinels — giant mutant-hunting robots created by Bolivar Trask.
  • Hellfire Club — a wealthy secret society whose Inner Circle, including Sebastian Shaw and Emma Frost, has opposed the team.
  • William Stryker — anti-mutant crusader and leader of the Purifiers.
  • Dark Phoenix — the corrupted cosmic form of Jean Grey.
  • Brotherhood of Mutants — a recurring rival mutant team.
  • Onslaught — a psionic entity formed from the minds of Professor X and Magneto.
