Brian Patrick Herbert (born 29 June 1947) is an American author, best known for continuing the Dune universe created by his father, Frank Herbert. In partnership with Kevin J. Anderson, he has written numerous prequel, sequel, and interquel novels set in the Dune fictional universe, as well as managing the literary estate and franchise adaptations of his father's work.


Brian Herbert, author of the expanded Dune series.

Career

Herbert's early books included the humorous novel Classic Comebacks (1981) and the biography Dreamer of Dune: The Biography of Frank Herbert (2003), based on extensive journals he kept of his father's life. After Frank Herbert's death in 1986, Brian discovered his father's notes and outlines for the conclusion of the Dune saga.

Dune collaborations

Beginning with Dune: House Atreides (1999), Herbert and Anderson produced the Prelude to Dune trilogy, the Legends of Dune trilogy about the Butlerian Jihad, and two novels — Hunters of Dune (2006) and Sandworms of Dune (2007) — completing the original series from Frank Herbert's outline. Further series include Heroes of Dune, Great Schools of Dune, and the Caladan Trilogy. The books have been commercially successful, though some fans and critics differ over their fidelity to the original novels' style.

Other work

With Anderson he also co-created the Hellhole trilogy. Herbert oversees Dune licensing for film, television, comics, and games, and was an executive producer on the 2021 film adaptation Dune.
