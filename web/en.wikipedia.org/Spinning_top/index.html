Spinning top

A spinning top, or simply a top, is a toy with a squat body and a sharp point at the bottom, designed to spin rapidly on the ground, the motion of which causes it to remain precisely balanced on its tip due to its rotational inertia. Such toys have existed since antiquity and are found in cultures around the world.

History

The earliest tops date to roughly 5500 to 4000 BCE and have been found in Mesopotamia and elsewhere. Clay and stone tops, animal bones, and nuts have been recovered archaeologically. Tops appear in ancient literature and in children's games from Greece and Rome through China and Japan.

Physics

When spun fast enough, gyroscopic effect keeps the top's axis stable. As friction slows the rotation, the axis begins to precess and wobble until the top falls. Variants include the whip top, the dreidel, the tippe top, and the rattleback, some of which display counterintuitive behavior studied in classical mechanics.

Types

Plunger tops, throwing tops, supported tops, and magnetic levitating tops are among the many designs. Competitions with battling tops, such as Beyblade, became popular in the early 21st century.
