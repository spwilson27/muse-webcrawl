First Constitutional Era

The First Constitutional Era (Turkish: Birinci Meşrutiyet Devri) of the Ottoman Empire was the period of constitutional monarchy from the promulgation of the Qanun-i Esasi (Ottoman constitution of 1876) on 23 December 1876 until 14 February 1878.

Background

The constitution was driven by the reformist Young Ottomans, especially Midhat Pasha, amid the financial crisis and the Great Eastern Crisis.

The constitution and parliament

The constitution proclaimed Ottoman-citizen equality and created a bicameral General Assembly with an elected Chamber of Deputies. The first parliament opened in March 1877.

Suspension

Citing the Russo-Turkish War, Sultan Abdul Hamid II prorogued parliament in February 1878 and ruled autocratically for 30 years, until the Young Turk Revolution of 1908 restored the constitution (Second Constitutional Era).
