REI

Recreational Equipment, Inc.
Industry: Retail (outdoor recreation)
Founded: 1938, Seattle, Washington
Headquarters: Kent, Washington, U.S.
Type: Consumer cooperative

Recreational Equipment, Inc., doing business as REI, is an American retail and outdoor recreation services corporation. It is organized as a consumers' co-operative. Founded in Seattle in 1938 by Lloyd and Mary Anderson, REI sells sporting goods, camping gear, travel equipment, and clothing, and also offers services such as outdoor-oriented vacations and courses.

History

Lloyd Anderson, an employee of Seattle City Light and a mountaineer, founded REI in 1938 after struggling to find affordable quality climbing equipment in the United States. He ordered an ice axe directly from Austria for $3.50, compared with $16.50–$20 locally, and fellow members of The Mountaineers asked him to order for them as well. The Andersons incorporated the cooperative with 21 other climbers, each paying a one-dollar lifetime membership fee.

Co-operative structure

REI is a consumer cooperative: members pay a one-time fee and receive an annual dividend based on a share of their purchases, typically around ten percent of eligible purchases. The company has tens of millions of members and has long ranked among the largest consumer co-operatives in the United States.

Operations

REI operates well over 150 stores across the United States, along with distribution centers and its REI Adventures travel business. It is known for its "Opt Outside" campaign, launched in 2015, in which stores close on Black Friday and employees are given the day off.
