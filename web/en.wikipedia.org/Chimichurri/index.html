Chimichurri

Chimichurri is an uncooked sauce used both as a table condiment and as a marinade for grilled meat. Originating in Argentine and Uruguayan cuisine, it is made of finely chopped flat-leaf parsley, minced garlic, olive oil, oregano, and red wine vinegar, often with red pepper flakes.

Varieties

The common green version is chimichurri verde; a red version, chimichurri rojo, adds tomato and red pepper. In Argentina the sauce is the standard accompaniment to asado (barbecued meats) and to the choripán sausage sandwich.

Name and origins

The origin of the name is uncertain; folk etymologies link it to Basque settlers' tximitxurri ("a mixture of several things") or to an English phrase such as "give me the curry" supposedly used by British prisoners in the 19th century. None is firmly documented.
