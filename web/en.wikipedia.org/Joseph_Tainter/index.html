Born: 1949

Known for: The Collapse of Complex Societies

Joseph Anthony Tainter (born 1949) is an American anthropologist and historian known for his 1988 book The Collapse of Complex Societies, which studies the collapse of the Roman Empire, the Maya civilisation and other societies.

Collapse theory

Tainter argues that societies increase complexity to solve problems, but complexity is subject to diminishing marginal returns: eventually the costs of maintaining complexity exceed its benefits, making a society vulnerable to collapse — a rapid loss of complexity that can be an economically rational adjustment.

Career

Tainter has taught at the University of New Mexico and Utah State University, where he was professor of environment and society, and has worked on sustainability and energy, co-editing works on resource depletion and long-term social change.
