Bank of America

The Bank of America Corporation is an American multinational investment bank and financial services holding company headquartered at the Bank of America Corporate Center in Charlotte, North Carolina. Founded in San Francisco in 1870, it is the second-largest banking institution in the United States, after JPMorgan Chase, and one of the "Big Four" American banks.

History

The bank traces its roots to the Bank of Italy, founded by Amadeo Giannini in San Francisco in 1904 to serve working-class immigrants. Renamed Bank of America in 1930, it grew through acquisitions, merging with NationsBank of Charlotte in 1998, and later acquiring FleetBoston Financial, MBNA, Countrywide Financial, and Merrill Lynch during the 2008 financial crisis.

Operations

Bank of America serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, and large corporations with banking, investing, asset management, and risk management products. Its wealth management arm includes Merrill (formerly Merrill Lynch) and Bank of America Private Bank. The company holds a significant share of United States deposits and operates thousands of retail financial centers and ATMs.
