Eberron

Eberron
Designer: Keith Baker
First published: 2004
Genre: Fantasy, pulp adventure
Game: Dungeons & Dragons

Eberron is a campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons role-playing game, created by Keith Baker and first published by Wizards of the Coast in 2004 after winning the company's Fantasy Setting Search competition. The setting blends fantasy with pulp adventure and noir elements, featuring widespread low-level magic used as everyday technology.

Setting

The main continent, Khorvaire, is recovering from the Last War, a century-long conflict between the Five Nations that ended two years before the default start of play with the destruction of the nation of Cyre, which became the haunted Mournland. Magic powers lightning rails, airships and communication networks operated by dragonmarked houses — great mercantile families bearing hereditary magical sigils.

Features

Eberron introduced new races such as the warforged (living constructs built for the war), shifters, changelings and kalashtar, and the artificer class of magical engineers. Alignment is deliberately blurred: priests of good faiths may be corrupt, and monsters are not inherently evil.

Publications

The setting was supported for the 3.5 and 4th editions of D&D and updated for 5th edition in Eberron: Rising from the Last War (2019). It is also the setting of the MMORPG Dungeons & Dragons Online.
