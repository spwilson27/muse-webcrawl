Automatic meter reading

Automatic meter reading (AMR) is a technology for automatically collecting consumption, diagnostic, and status data from water, gas, and electricity meters and transferring it to a central database for billing and analysis, without a reader physically inspecting each meter.

Technology

AMR systems range from handheld receivers used by a walking or driving meter reader passing nearby, to fixed networks in which meter transmitters send readings by radio to collectors and onward to the utility. One-way systems transmit readings only, while two-way systems, often called advanced metering infrastructure (AMI), also accept commands such as remote disconnection and can record consumption at short intervals.

Benefits and concerns

Utilities use AMR to reduce labor costs, eliminate estimated bills, detect leaks and outages, and support time-of-use pricing. Deployment has raised questions about radio-frequency emissions, data privacy, and cost recovery, though regulators in many regions have approved large rollouts.
