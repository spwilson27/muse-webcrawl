Eduard

Eduard is a male given name, the German, Dutch, and several other European languages' form of the English name Edward. It derives from Old English Ēadweard, meaning "wealthy guardian" (from ēad, riches or fortune, and weard, guardian). Bearers of the name include royalty of historical German states, artists, and scientists.

People

Notable people named Eduard include Eduard Bernstein (1850–1932), German socialist theorist; Eduard Mörike (1804–1875), German poet; Eduard Buchner (1860–1917), Nobel laureate chemist; Eduard Hanslick (1825–1904), influential music critic; Eduard Shevardnadze (1928–2014), Soviet foreign minister and later President of Georgia; and Eduard Strauss (1835–1916), Austrian composer of the Strauss dynasty. In modern usage the name appears across Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Scandinavia, the Czech Republic, Estonia, and Russia.

Other uses

Eduard is also the name of a Czech manufacturer of plastic model kits and photo-etched accessories, founded in 1989, well known among scale modellers. "Eduard" may also refer to the protagonist of Goethe-related literature and to various minor places and institutions named after historical figures.
