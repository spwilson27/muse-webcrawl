The Human Comedy (novel)

[Placeholder image: The_Human_Comedy_(novel).png]

The Human Comedy is a 1943 novel by William Saroyan. Set in the fictional town of Ithaca, California, during World War II, it follows fourteen-year-old Homer Macauley, who works as a telegraph messenger delivering news of soldiers' deaths to their families, and his younger brother Ulysses.

Adaptations

The story began as a screenplay for MGM; the 1943 film The Human Comedy, starring Mickey Rooney, was released alongside the novel and won the Academy Award for Best Story.
