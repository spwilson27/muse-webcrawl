Navy Day (India)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Navy Day in India is celebrated on 4 December every year to recognise the achievements and role of the Indian Navy. The date commemorates Operation Trident, the Indian Navy's audacious attack on Karachi harbour during the Indo-Pakistani War of 1971.

Operation Trident

On the night of 4/5 December 1971, a strike group of the Indian Navy — missile boats of the 25th Killer Squadron, including INS Nipat, INS Nirghat and INS Veer, escorted by corvettes — approached Karachi and sank the Pakistani destroyer PNS Khaibar, the minesweeper PNS Muhafiz and the merchant vessel MV Venus Challenger, while severely damaging the destroyer PNS Shah Jahan and setting Karachi's fuel storage ablaze. The operation, followed days later by Operation Python, is celebrated as one of the most successful naval strikes after World War II and was achieved without loss to the Indian force.

Celebrations

Navy Week is observed in the run-up to Navy Day, with open-ship visits, a Beating Retreat and tattoo ceremony, an operational demonstration, and a naval ball. Recent celebrations have been hosted in different coastal cities, including Visakhapatnam, Mumbai, and Sindhudurg. See also Indian Navy.
