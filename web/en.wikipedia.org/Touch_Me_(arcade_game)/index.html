Touch Me is an arcade game released by Atari in 1974. It is an audio memory game in which the machine plays a sequence of tones, each associated with a button, and the player must reproduce the sequence; the sequence grows longer with each successful round until the player makes a mistake.

Legacy

Touch Me is best remembered as the inspiration for Ralph Baer's handheld electronic game Simon, released by Milton Bradley in 1978. Baer saw Touch Me at a trade show and, finding its gameplay compelling but its presentation and harsh sounds unattractive, redesigned the concept with larger, colourful buttons and more pleasant tones. Simon became a best-selling toy and a pop-culture icon. Atari later produced its own handheld version of Touch Me in 1978 to compete with Simon.
