Concacaf Champions Cup

RegionNorth America, Central America, Caribbean
Founded1962
OrganizerConcacaf

The Concacaf Champions Cup is the premier annual club football competition for teams from North America, Central America, and the Caribbean, organized by Concacaf. It was known as the Concacaf Champions League from 2008 to 2023.

Format

Clubs qualify through their domestic leagues and regional cups. The knockout tournament culminates in a final, and the winner represents the confederation at the FIFA Club World Cup.

History

Mexican clubs have dominated the competition, with Club América among the most successful teams. MLS clubs from the United States and Canada have won only occasionally, notably Seattle Sounders FC in 2022.
