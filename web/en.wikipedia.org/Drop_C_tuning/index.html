Drop C tuning

Drop C tuning is an alternative guitar tuning in which the strings are tuned, from low to high, to C–G–C–F–A–D. It is achieved by tuning a guitar down a whole step (D standard) and then lowering the sixth string a further whole step to C, so the three lowest strings form a C power chord when played open.

Characteristics

Like other drop tunings, drop C lets the player form power chords on the lowest strings with a single finger (a barre), enabling fast, heavy riffs, while the lowered pitch gives a darker, heavier sound. It is most commonly used in heavy metal, metalcore, and hard rock, by bands such as System of a Down (on several albums), Killswitch Engage, and Bullet for My Valentine.

Related tunings

Drop C is related to drop D (only the sixth string lowered from standard tuning) and to drop B and drop A, which extend the same concept to lower pitches, often on baritone or seven-string guitars.
