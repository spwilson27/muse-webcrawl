Rutger Oelsen Hauer (23 January 1944 – 19 July 2019) was a Dutch actor. He became internationally famous for his role as the replicant Roy Batty in Ridley Scott's science-fiction film Blade Runner (1982), in which his partly improvised "Tears in rain" monologue became one of the most celebrated speeches in cinema history.

Hauer as Roy Batty in Blade Runner (1982).

Career

Hauer rose to fame in the Netherlands in the television series Floris (1969) and in Paul Verhoeven's films Turkish Delight (1973), Soldier of Orange (1977), and Spetters (1980). His American breakthrough came with Nighthawks (1981) opposite Sylvester Stallone, followed by Blade Runner. He played the sinister hitchhiker in The Hitcher (1986), won a Golden Globe for the television film Escape from Sobibor (1987), and appeared in films including Ladyhawke (1985), Buffy the Vampire Slayer (1992), Confessions of a Dangerous Mind (2002), Sin City (2005), Batman Begins (2005), and Hobo with a Shotgun (2011).

Personal life and death

An environmental activist, Hauer founded the Rutger Hauer Starfish Association to raise awareness of HIV/AIDS. He died at his home in Beetsterzwaag on 19 July 2019, aged 75, after a short illness. By coincidence, 2019 was the year in which his character Roy Batty dies in Blade Runner, prompting widespread tributes quoting the "tears in rain" speech.
