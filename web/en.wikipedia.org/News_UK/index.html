News UK

News UK is a British print and digital media company owned by Rupert Murdoch's News Corp. It publishes The Times, The Sunday Times, The Sun and The Sun on Sunday, and operates the Times Radio station and other media brands in the United Kingdom.

History

The company grew from Murdoch's News Ltd purchases of The Sun in 1969 and The Times and Sunday Times in 1981. As News International it moved its print operations to Wapping in 1986, provoking a year-long industrial dispute. Rebekah Brooks served as chief executive from 2009 to 2011.

Phone hacking scandal

In 2011 revelations that journalists at its News of the World tabloid had hacked the phones of celebrities, politicians and the murdered schoolgirl Milly Dowler led to the paper's closure, the Leveson Inquiry into press ethics, and extensive litigation and settlements. The company rebranded as News UK in 2013, when News Corporation split its publishing and entertainment assets.

Today

News UK is based at The News Building in London Bridge. The Times and Sunday Times operate subscription websites; The Sun remains one of Britain's best-selling tabloids and runs a large free website.
