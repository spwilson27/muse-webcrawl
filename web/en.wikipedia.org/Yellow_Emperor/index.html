Yellow Emperor

Chinese黃帝 (Huángdì)
Traditional reign2698–2598 BC
Known forLegendary ancestor of the Han Chinese; culture hero

The Yellow Emperor, or Huangdi, is a legendary Chinese sovereign and culture hero considered the ancestor of the Han Chinese people (the Huaxia) and a founder of Chinese civilisation. Tradition credits him with inventions ranging from writing and the calendar to medicine, boats, and the compass chariot.

Legend

According to accounts such as Sima Qian's Records of the Grand Historian, Huangdi defeated the rival ruler Chiyou at the Battle of Zhuolu and the Flame Emperor at Banquan, uniting the tribes of the Yellow River plain. His wife Leizu is credited with discovering sericulture.

Works and worship

The Huangdi Neijing (Yellow Emperor's Inner Canon), the foundational text of traditional Chinese medicine, is cast as a dialogue with him, though it was compiled centuries later. Mausoleums attributed to him stand in Shaanxi and elsewhere, and he has been venerated as an ancestral deity; the traditional Chinese calendar counts years from his reign.
