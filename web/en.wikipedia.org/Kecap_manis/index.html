Kecap manis

Kecap manis
TypeCondiment (sweet soy sauce)
Place of originIndonesia
Main ingredientsFermented soybeans, palm sugar

Kecap manis is an Indonesian sweet soy sauce with a thick, syrupy consistency and a deep, sweet flavour derived from added palm sugar. Darker and sweeter than regular soy sauce (kecap asin), it is a defining ingredient of Indonesian cooking and accounts for the largest share of kecap production in Indonesia.

Production and flavour

Kecap manis is brewed from fermented soybeans and sweetened with palm sugar, often flavoured with star anise, galangal, lemongrass or garlic. The result is a molasses-like sauce used in marinades, glazes and stir-fries.

Uses

It is essential in dishes such as nasi goreng (fried rice), mie goreng, satay marinades and semur stews, and is served as a dipping sauce, sometimes mixed with sliced shallots and chilli.
