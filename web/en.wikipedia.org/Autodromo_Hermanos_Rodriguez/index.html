The Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez is a motor racing circuit in the Magdalena Mixhuca Sports City in Mexico City. Named after the racing brothers Ricardo and Pedro Rodríguez, it has hosted the Mexican Grand Prix of Formula One in three eras: 1963–1970, 1986–1992, and from 2015 to the present.

Layout

The current layout, redesigned by Hermann Tilke for Formula One's return, is about 4.3 km long and runs through the Foro Sol baseball stadium, creating a distinctive stadium section. At an altitude of over 2,200 metres it is the highest circuit on the Formula One calendar, and the thin air reduces engine power and downforce.

History

The circuit opened in 1959 and held a non-championship Formula One race in 1962, during which Ricardo Rodríguez was killed in practice. The Grand Prix returned in 2015 to large crowds, and the event has since been renamed the Mexico City Grand Prix.
