Public service

A public service is any service intended to address specific needs pertaining to the aggregate members of a community. Public services include services provided by a government to people living within its area, whether through the public sector directly or by financing private provision. They are associated with fundamental human rights such as the right to water.

Examples

Common public services include healthcare, education, law enforcement, fire services, public transport, postal services, social services, public broadcasting, water supply, waste management and defence.

Characteristics

A public service may sometimes have the characteristics of a public good (being non-rivalrous and non-excludable), but most are services which may, according to prevailing social norms, be under-provided by the market. Public services are often funded through taxation and delivered free at the point of use.

Administration

The terms public service and civil service are often used interchangeably, though public service can also refer to the body of government employees — the public servants — who deliver services on behalf of the state.
