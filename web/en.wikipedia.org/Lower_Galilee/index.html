Lower Galilee

RegionGalilee
CountryIsrael
Highest pointMount Tabor (575 m)

The Lower Galilee is a region within the Galilee in northern Israel. It is bounded by the Jezreel Valley to the south, the Sea of Galilee and the Jordan Valley to the east, and the Beit Netofa and Sakhnin valleys to the north, with the Mediterranean coastal plain to the west.

Geography

Unlike the mountainous Upper Galilee, the Lower Galilee is characterized by relatively low hills, rarely exceeding 500 metres, separated by wide fertile valleys. Mount Tabor, Mount Precipice near Nazareth, and the hills around the Beit Netofa Valley are among its best-known features. The climate is Mediterranean, with hot dry summers and mild wet winters.

Settlements

Major towns include Nazareth, Afula, Tamra and Sakhnin. The region is home to a mixed population of Jews, Muslim and Christian Arabs, Druze and Bedouin communities.
