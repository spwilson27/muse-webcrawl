Stykkishólmur

Stykkishólmur is a town and municipality in western Iceland, located on the northern side of the Snæfellsnes peninsula facing Breiðafjörður bay. With a population of roughly 1,200, it serves as a centre of commerce, fishing, and tourism for the surrounding region.

History

The site was an important trading post from the 16th century onward, owing to its sheltered natural harbour. Many of the town's oldest houses date from the Danish trade era of the 19th century and are carefully preserved.

Economy and transport

Fishing and fish processing remain important, alongside growing tourism. A car ferry, Baldur, crosses Breiðafjörður to Brjánslækur in the Westfjords, calling at the island of Flatey. The town is also known for environmental initiatives, having pursued sustainability certification and extensive recycling.

Landmarks

Notable buildings include the Norwegian House regional museum, the modern Stykkishólmskirkja church, and the Library of Water art installation by Roni Horn.
