Marc Brown

Born25 November 1946, Erie, Pennsylvania, USA
OccupationAuthor and illustrator

Marc Tolon Brown (born 25 November 1946) is an American children's book author and illustrator, best known as the creator of the Arthur series of books and the long-running animated television series of the same name.

Career

Brown grew up in Erie, Pennsylvania, and studied at the Cleveland Institute of Art. The first Arthur book, Arthur's Nose, appeared in 1976; its hero, Arthur Read, is an eight-year-old aardvark living in the fictional town of Elwood City. The PBS animated series Arthur premiered in 1996 and ran for 25 seasons until 2022, becoming one of the longest-running children's animated series in American television. Brown also worked on the spin-off Postcards from Buster.

Awards

The television series won multiple Daytime Emmy Awards and a Peabody Award, and Brown has been honoured for his contribution to children's literacy.
