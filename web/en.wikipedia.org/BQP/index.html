BQP

In computational complexity theory, BQP (bounded-error quantum polynomial time) is the class of decision problems solvable by a quantum computer in polynomial time, with an error probability of at most 1/3 for all instances. It is the quantum analogue of the classical complexity class BPP.

Relation to other classes

BQP is known to contain P and BPP, and to be contained in PSPACE. Problems believed to lie in BQP but not in P include integer factorisation, solved in polynomial time by Shor's algorithm, and the discrete logarithm problem.

Definition

Formally, a language is in BQP if there exists a polynomial-time uniform family of quantum circuits that accepts inputs in the language with probability at least 2/3 and rejects inputs not in the language with probability at least 2/3.
