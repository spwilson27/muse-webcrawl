River Taff

The River Taff (Welsh: Afon Taf) is a river in Wales. It rises as two rivers in the Brecon Beacons — the Taf Fechan and the Taf Fawr — which join near Merthyr Tydfil and flow south for about 67 km (42 miles) through Pontypridd and Cardiff, joining the Severn Estuary at Cardiff Bay.

History

The Taff valley was central to the Industrial Revolution in South Wales, carrying iron and coal from Merthyr Tydfil and the Rhondda (whose river, the Rhondda, joins the Taff at Pontypridd) to the docks at Cardiff. Once heavily polluted, the river's water quality has greatly improved since the late 20th century, and salmon and other migratory fish have returned. The Taff Trail walking and cycling route follows much of its course, and the river passes the Principality Stadium in central Cardiff.
