Volkswagen Golf Mk1

Volkswagen Golf Mk1
Manufacturer: Volkswagen
Production: 1974–1983 (to 2009 in South Africa)
Designer: Giorgetto Giugiaro
Class: Compact car

The Volkswagen Golf Mk1 is the first generation of the Volkswagen Golf, a compact family car produced from 1974 to 1983 in Europe. Designed by Giorgetto Giugiaro at Italdesign, it replaced the ageing rear-engined Beetle with a modern front-wheel-drive hatchback layout.

Development

Volkswagen needed a successor to the Beetle, whose sales were declining. The Golf's transverse-engine, front-wheel-drive design, styled with sharp, folded-paper lines, set the template for the modern European hatchback. It was sold in North America as the Volkswagen Rabbit.

Variants

Alongside the standard three- and five-door hatchbacks, the Mk1 spawned a convertible, the Jetta saloon, the Caddy pickup and the performance GTI model. In South Africa, a facelifted version called the Citi Golf remained in production until 2009.

Significance

The Golf became one of the best-selling cars of all time, and the Mk1 established the nameplate that continues today. The GTI variant in particular created the hot hatch segment.
