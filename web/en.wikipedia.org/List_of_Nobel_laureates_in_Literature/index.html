List of Nobel laureates in Literature

[Image: The Nobel Prize in Literature diploma]
The Literature prize is awarded by the Swedish Academy.

The Nobel Prize in Literature is awarded annually by the Swedish Academy to an author who, in the words of Alfred Nobel's will, has produced "in the field of literature the most outstanding work in an ideal direction". It was first awarded in 1901 to the French poet Sully Prudhomme.

French authors have won the prize most often. The prize has occasionally been controversial, and Jean-Paul Sartre remains the only laureate to have voluntarily declined it.
