The Indian Navy is the maritime branch of the Indian Armed Forces. It is a blue-water navy operating around 150 ships and submarines and some 300 aircraft, including two aircraft carriers, and is among the largest navies in the world. Its stated role is to safeguard India's maritime borders and interests, deter aggression at sea, and protect the sea lanes on which most of India's trade and energy imports depend.

History

The navy's origins lie in the East India Company's Bombay Marine of 1612 and its successor, the Royal Indian Navy. At independence the small force was split between India and Pakistan. The navy's defining combat action came in the 1971 war: its carrier INS Vikrant enforced a blockade of East Pakistan, while missile boats executed Operation Trident, a daring night strike on Karachi harbour on 4–5 December 1971 that sank several Pakistani vessels — still celebrated as Navy Day.

Fleet and organisation

The navy operates through three commands: Western Naval Command at Mumbai, Eastern Naval Command at Visakhapatnam, and the training-oriented Southern Naval Command at Kochi, with the fleets assigned to the first two. The fleet centres on the carriers INS Vikramaditya and the indigenously built INS Vikrant (commissioned 2022), Kolkata- and Visakhapatnam-class destroyers, stealth frigates, and a submarine arm mixing conventional boats with nuclear-powered attack and ballistic-missile submarines — the Arihant class, which completes India's nuclear triad. The naval air arm flies MiG-29K and (on order) Rafale-M fighters, maritime patrol aircraft and helicopters; the MARCOS special forces and a growing network of coastal radar and bases, from Karwar to the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, extend its reach.

Role

With India's trade overwhelmingly seaborne, the navy emphasises sea-lane security across the Indian Ocean, anti-piracy patrols in the Gulf of Aden (continuous since 2008), humanitarian assistance to littoral states, and a strategy of being a "net security provider" in the region. Large-scale indigenous shipbuilding — most warships in service or on order are built in Indian yards — is a central policy goal.
