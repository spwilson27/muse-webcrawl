Skolts

The Skolts or Skolt Sámi are an indigenous Sámi ethnic group. They live primarily in the municipality of Inari in northern Finland, around the villages of Sevettijärvi, Keväjärvi, and Nellim, and in the Murmansk region of Russia near the Pechenga area.

Traditionally Orthodox Christian — converted in the 16th century through the mission of Saint Tryphon of Pechenga — the Skolts differ religiously from the predominantly Lutheran Finnish Sámi. Their traditional livelihoods include reindeer herding, fishing, hunting, and gathering.

After the Second World War, Skolts from the ceded Petsamo region were resettled in Finland. The Skolts have their own language, Skolt Sámi, and are recognized as an indigenous people under Finnish law, with representation through the Sámi Parliament.
