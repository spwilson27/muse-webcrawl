Duathlon

Highest governing bodyWorld Triathlon
DisciplinesRunning, cycling
FormatRun–bike–run

Duathlon is an athletic event that consists of a running leg, followed by a cycling leg and then another running leg, in a format similar to triathlons. World Triathlon sanctions international duathlon events, including the World Duathlon Championships.

Distances

Standard-distance duathlons consist of a 10 km run, a 40 km cycle and a 5 km run. Sprint events halve those distances, while long-distance events such as Powerman races extend the legs considerably. Off-road duathlons replace the road cycling leg with mountain biking and the runs with trail running.

History

The sport grew out of run–bike–run training events in the 1980s, partly as an alternative for athletes in climates where open-water swimming was impractical. The first World Duathlon Championships were held in 1990 in Palm Springs, California.

Notable events

Powerman Zofingen in Switzerland is the most prestigious long-distance duathlon. National federations hold annual championships, and duathlon also features in multisport festivals alongside triathlon and aquathlon.
