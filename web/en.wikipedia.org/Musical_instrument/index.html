Musical instrument

Instruments from the string, wind, and percussion families

A musical instrument is a device created or adapted to make musical sounds. In principle, any object that produces sound can be considered a musical instrument; the term is generally reserved for objects purpose-built or adapted for music.

History

Instruments are among the oldest human artefacts: bone flutes from the Swabian Jura caves date to roughly 40,000 years ago. Ancient civilisations developed lyres, harps, drums, and trumpets, and instruments appear in ritual, court, and folk music across every culture.

Classification

The most widely used scholarly system, devised by Erich Moritz von Hornbostel and Curt Sachs (1914), classifies instruments by how they produce sound: idiophones (the body itself vibrates), membranophones (a stretched membrane), chordophones (strings), aerophones (air), and later electrophones. The Western orchestra uses the simpler division into strings, woodwind, brass, and percussion, with keyboards spanning several principles.
