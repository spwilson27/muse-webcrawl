Volcanic Seven Summits

The Volcanic Seven Summits are the highest volcanoes on each of the seven continents, a mountaineering challenge analogous to the Seven Summits. The list comprises volcanoes that are both the highest on their continent and still classed as volcanic features.

Ojos del Salado, on the Chile–Argentina border, is the highest volcano on Earth and the second-highest summit in the Western Hemisphere. Mount Sidley, in the Executive Committee Range of Marie Byrd Land, is the most remote of the seven and was not climbed until 1990. Definitions vary slightly: some lists substitute other peaks where the volcanic nature or continental assignment of a summit is disputed.
