Extreme weather

Extreme weather includes unexpected, unusual, severe, or unseasonal weather events that fall at the extremes of the historical distribution for a place — typically a range that has been seen only rarely, such as the most unusual ten percent. Extreme weather events include heat waves, cold waves, floods, droughts, tornadoes, and tropical cyclones.

Causes

Extreme events arise from natural climate variability, but attribution science examines how human-caused climate change alters their frequency and intensity; warmer air holds more moisture and adds energy to storm systems.

Impacts

Extremes damage infrastructure, agriculture, and ecosystems, and pose direct risks to human health, particularly heat stress among vulnerable populations.
