The Smile (band)

The Smile

Origin: England

Formed: 2020

Members: Thom Yorke, Jonny Greenwood, Tom Skinner

Genres: Art rock, post-punk, math rock

The Smile are an English rock band comprising Thom Yorke and Jonny Greenwood of Radiohead with the jazz drummer Tom Skinner of Sons of Kemet. Formed during the COVID-19 lockdowns, the band debuted with a surprise performance streamed from Glastonbury Festival in May 2021.

Music

The Smile's sound combines the songwriting of Yorke and Greenwood with Skinner's nimble, jazz-inflected drumming, drawing on post-punk, progressive rock and Afrobeat. Their debut album, A Light for Attracting Attention (2022), produced by Nigel Godrich, was widely acclaimed. A second album, Wall of Eyes, followed in 2024, along with Cutouts the same year.

Discography

Studio albums: A Light for Attracting Attention (2022), Wall of Eyes (2024), Cutouts (2024). The band's name comes from the Ted Hughes poem "The Smile".
