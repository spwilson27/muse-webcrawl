Emirates Stadium

The Emirates Stadium (known as Ashburton Grove prior to sponsorship, and as Arsenal Stadium for UEFA competitions) is a football stadium in Holloway, London, England, and the home of Arsenal Football Club. With a current capacity of 60,704, it is the fifth-largest football stadium in England by capacity.

History

The stadium opened in July 2006, replacing Arsenal's former home, Highbury, which the club had occupied since 1913. It was named under a sponsorship deal with Emirates Airline, which also became the club's shirt sponsor. The project cost around £390 million, financed in part by the redevelopment of the Highbury site into housing. The stadium features a four-tiered bowl design and has hosted international fixtures, concerts and other events in addition to Arsenal matches.
