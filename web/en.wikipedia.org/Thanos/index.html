Thanos is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-artist Jim Starlin, he debuted in 1973 and is regarded as one of the most powerful villains in the Marvel Universe, frequently battling the Avengers and the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Fictional biography

Thanos was born on Saturn's moon Titan, a mutant among the Eternals carrying Deviant genes that gave him his purple, ridged skin. Obsessed with Death — personified in Marvel's universe as Mistress Death — he sought to court her through acts of destruction, including killing his own family and much of Titan. His most famous scheme, in the 1991 storyline The Infinity Gauntlet, assembled the six Infinity Gems, granting him omnipotence with which he erased half of all life in the universe before being defeated.

Powers and abilities

Thanos possesses superhuman strength, durability and longevity, energy projection, telepathy and telekinesis, and is a master strategist and hand-to-hand combatant with vast scientific knowledge.

In other media

Portrayed by Josh Brolin through motion capture, Thanos was the central antagonist of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's "Infinity Saga", appearing in The Avengers (2012, cameo), Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019), where his snap of the Infinity Gauntlet — "the Blip" — became a major cultural touchstone. Critics praised the film version as one of the great screen villains of the decade.
