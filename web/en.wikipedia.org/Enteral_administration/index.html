Enteral administration

Enteral administration is the delivery of drugs or nutrition through the gastrointestinal tract, in contrast to parenteral routes that bypass it.

Routes

Oral administration is the most common enteral route; others include sublingual, rectal, and tube feeding via nasogastric, gastrostomy or jejunostomy tubes for patients unable to swallow.

Considerations

Enteral drugs are subject to first-pass metabolism in the liver, variable absorption and food interactions, but the route is generally safer, cheaper and more convenient than injection.
