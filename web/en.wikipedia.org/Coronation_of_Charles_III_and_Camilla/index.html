Coronation of Charles III and Camilla

Date: 6 May 2023

Location: Westminster Abbey, London

Participants: Charles III, Queen Camilla, clergy and congregation

The coronation of Charles III and his wife, Camilla, as king and queen of the United Kingdom and the other Commonwealth realms, took place on Saturday, 6 May 2023, at Westminster Abbey. Charles had acceded to the throne on 8 September 2022 upon the death of his mother, Elizabeth II. It was the first coronation held in Britain since 1953 and the fortieth to take place at the abbey since 1066.

Ceremony

The service, conducted by the Archbishop of Canterbury, Justin Welby, followed the traditional structure of recognition, oath, anointing, investiture, crowning, and homage, but was shorter and smaller than its predecessor, with about 2,200 guests. Charles was anointed with holy oil consecrated in Jerusalem and crowned with St Edward's Crown; Camilla was crowned with Queen Mary's Crown. The ceremony included representatives of other faiths and the other Commonwealth realms, and music ranged from Handel and Byrd to new commissions.

Processions and celebrations

The King and Queen travelled from Buckingham Palace in the Diamond Jubilee State Coach and returned in the Gold State Coach, followed by a balcony appearance and flypast. Street parties, a concert at Windsor Castle, and the "Big Help Out" volunteering day marked the bank-holiday weekend.
