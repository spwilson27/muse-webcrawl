Kenya

Kenya, officially the Republic of Kenya, is a country in East Africa. With an estimated population of more than 55 million, it is bordered by Ethiopia to the north, Somalia to the east, Tanzania to the south, Uganda to the west, South Sudan to the northwest, and the Indian Ocean to the southeast. Its capital and largest city is Nairobi.

History

Kenya was inhabited by hunter-gatherers and later by Cushitic, Nilotic and Bantu-speaking peoples. Arab traders frequented the coast from the first century AD. Kenya was colonised by the British Empire, becoming a protectorate in 1895 and a colony in 1920. It gained independence on 12 December 1963 under Jomo Kenyatta.

Geography

Mount Kenya, the second-highest peak in Africa, gives the country its name. The Great Rift Valley runs through the country. Kenya's climate varies from tropical on the coast to arid in the north.

Economy and culture

Agriculture, including tea and coffee, tourism centred on safaris and wildlife reserves such as the Maasai Mara, and services dominate the economy. Swahili and English are official languages. Kenya is renowned for long-distance runners.
