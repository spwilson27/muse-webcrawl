Image processing

Image processing is the use of algorithms to perform operations on digital images — to enhance them, extract information, or prepare them for analysis or display. Digital image processing grew out of 1960s work at laboratories such as JPL, where techniques were developed to correct images from space probes.

Basic operations

Point operations adjust brightness, contrast, and colour; spatial filters blur, sharpen, or detect edges (e.g. Sobel operators); geometric transforms scale, rotate, and warp; and frequency-domain methods use the Fourier transform for filtering and compression.

Analysis

Further tasks include segmentation, morphological operations, feature detection, image registration, and restoration of degraded images. These underpin computer vision, medical imaging, remote sensing, and industrial inspection.

Software

Tools range from editors such as Photoshop and GIMP to libraries such as OpenCV, scikit-image, and MATLAB toolboxes.
