List of most-visited websites

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of the most-visited websites on the World Wide Web, based on estimates of total monthly visits published by traffic-measurement services such as SimilarWeb. Rankings change over time, but the top positions have been dominated for years by Google Search, YouTube, and major social media platforms.

Methodology

Traffic estimates are compiled from panels of users, data partnerships, and other measurement sources; figures are approximations and may differ between providers. Lists typically count visits to the web domain rather than use of mobile apps, which can understate services used mainly through apps.
