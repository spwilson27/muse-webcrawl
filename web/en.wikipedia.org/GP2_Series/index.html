GP2 Series

The GP2 Series was a form of open-wheel motor racing introduced in 2005 by Bernie Ecclestone and Flavio Briatore, following the discontinuation of the Formula 3000 championship. Sanctioned by the FIA and racing on Formula One support bills, it served as the premier feeder category to F1 until it was rebranded as the FIA Formula 2 Championship in 2017.

Format

GP2 used a single Dallara chassis with a Renault-badged Mecachrome V8 engine and control tyres. Weekends featured a long Saturday feature race with a mandatory pit stop and a shorter Sunday sprint race with a partially reversed grid — a format Formula 1's support categories retain today.

Champions and graduates

The inaugural 2005 champion was Nico Rosberg, followed by Lewis Hamilton in 2006 — both later Formula One world champions. Other champions included Timo Glock, Romain Grosjean and Stoffel Vandoorne, and most F1 drivers of the era passed through the series.

GP2 Asia Series

A winter GP2 Asia Series ran from 2008 to 2011, visiting circuits in the Middle East and Asia before being merged into the main championship.
