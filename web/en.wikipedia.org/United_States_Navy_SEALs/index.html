The United States Navy Sea, Air, and Land (SEAL) Teams, commonly known as Navy SEALs, are the U.S. Navy's primary special operations force and a component of the Naval Special Warfare Command. SEALs are trained to operate in all environments — sea, air, and land — conducting missions including direct action raids, special reconnaissance, counter-terrorism, unconventional warfare, hostage rescue, and foreign internal defence.

History

The SEALs trace their lineage to the Second World War's Naval Combat Demolition Units and Underwater Demolition Teams (UDTs), the "frogmen" who cleared beach obstacles at Normandy and in the Pacific. President John F. Kennedy, recognizing the need for unconventional warfare capabilities, directed the creation of the SEAL teams; SEAL Team One and SEAL Team Two were commissioned on 1 January 1962.

SEALs served extensively in the Vietnam War, advising South Vietnamese forces and conducting ambushes and reconnaissance in the Mekong Delta, becoming one of the most decorated units of the war. They later operated in Grenada, Panama, the Gulf War, Somalia, and the Balkans. After 2001, SEALs deployed continuously in Afghanistan and Iraq. In May 2011, SEALs of the Naval Special Warfare Development Group (DEVGRU, popularly "SEAL Team Six") conducted the raid in Abbottabad, Pakistan, that killed Osama bin Laden.

Selection and training

Candidates must pass a demanding screening and then Basic Underwater Demolition/SEAL (BUD/S) training at Coronado, California — a six-month course famous for "Hell Week", five and a half days of near-continuous training with minimal sleep. Historically, around 70–80 percent of candidates fail to complete BUD/S. Graduates proceed to SEAL Qualification Training (SQT), parachute school, and cold-weather and specialised courses before assignment to a team.

Organization

There are eight numbered SEAL teams (odd-numbered teams based on the West Coast at Coronado, even-numbered on the East Coast at Little Creek, Virginia), plus SEAL Delivery Vehicle teams and support units. The Naval Special Warfare Development Group is the Navy's premier counter-terrorism unit and a component of Joint Special Operations Command (JSOC).
