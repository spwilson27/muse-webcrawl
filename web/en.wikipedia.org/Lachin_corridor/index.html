Lachin corridor

The Lachin corridor (Azerbaijani: Laçın dəhlizi; Armenian: Լաչինի միջանցք) is a mountain road in the South Caucasus that was, until 2023, the only road connecting Nagorno-Karabakh with Armenia, passing through the Azerbaijani district of Lachin.

After the First Nagorno-Karabakh War, the corridor came under the control of the self-proclaimed Republic of Artsakh. Under the 2020 ceasefire agreement ending the Second Nagorno-Karabakh War, the corridor — 5 km wide — remained open under the control of Russian peacekeepers, with a new route bypassing the town of Lachin (Berdzor) opened in 2022. From December 2022 Azerbaijanis blockaded the corridor, causing a humanitarian crisis in Nagorno-Karabakh. Following Azerbaijan's military offensive in September 2023, the region's Armenian population fled through the corridor to Armenia, and the corridor ceased to function in its former role.
