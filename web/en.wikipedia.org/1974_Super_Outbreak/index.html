1974 Super Outbreak

Date3–4 April 1974
Tornadoes148 confirmed
Affected area13 US states and Ontario, Canada
Deaths319

The 1974 Super Outbreak was the second-largest tornado outbreak on record for a single 24-hour period, just behind the 2011 Super Outbreak. From 3 to 4 April 1974, 148 tornadoes touched down across 13 American states and the Canadian province of Ontario.

Intensity

Thirty of the tornadoes were rated F4 or F5 on the Fujita scale, including seven F5s — a record for a single outbreak. The town of Xenia, Ohio, was struck by an F5 tornado that killed 32 people, and the community of Guin, Alabama, was nearly obliterated.

Aftermath

The outbreak killed 319 people and injured more than 5,000. It spurred major improvements in tornado forecasting, warning dissemination, and storm research in the United States.
