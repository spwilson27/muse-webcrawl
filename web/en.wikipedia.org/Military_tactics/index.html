Military tactics
Level: Organisation and employment of forces in battle
Related levels: Strategy (theatre/national), operational art (campaign)

Military tactics are the science and art of organising and employing fighting forces on or near the battlefield. Tactics involve the disposition and manoeuvre of units in combat, the use of terrain, weapons and timing, and sit between strategy, which concerns the overall conduct of a war, and the operational level, which links battles into campaigns.

Elements

Core tactical concepts include fire and movement, concentration of force, flanking and envelopment, ambush, defence in depth, reserves, reconnaissance and combined arms — the coordination of infantry, armour, artillery, engineers and air power so that each covers the others' weaknesses. Small-unit tactics govern squads and platoons using cover, sectors of fire and bounding overwatch, while larger formations plan attacks, withdrawals and river crossings.

Historical development

Tactics evolved with technology: the phalanx and legion of antiquity, the shock cavalry charge and longbow of the middle ages, the linear tactics of the musket era, and the infiltration tactics, tanks and close air support of the twentieth century. Modern doctrine adds networked sensors, precision fires, drones and electronic warfare, and has been reshaped by counter-insurgency and urban combat experience.
