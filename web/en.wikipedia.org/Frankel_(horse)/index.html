Frankel (foaled 11 February 2008) is a retired champion British Thoroughbred racehorse and successful sire. Trained by Sir Henry Cecil and owned by Khalid Abdullah's Juddmonte Farms, Frankel was unbeaten in his fourteen-race career and was the highest-rated racehorse in the world from May 2011 until his retirement in October 2012.

Racing career

Named after the American trainer Bobby Frankel, the colt won all four of his races as a two-year-old in 2010, including the Dewhurst Stakes. At three he won the 2000 Guineas by six lengths with a startling front-running performance, followed by the St James's Palace Stakes, the Sussex Stakes, and the Queen Elizabeth II Stakes. As a four-year-old in 2012 he added the Lockinge Stakes, Queen Anne Stakes, a second Sussex Stakes, the Juddmonte International at York — his first win beyond a mile — and the Champion Stakes at Ascot.

Assessment and stud career

Frankel's official rating of 140 made him the highest-rated horse in the history of the World Thoroughbred Racehorse Rankings. Timeform rated him 147, the highest figure the organisation had ever awarded. Retired to Banstead Manor Stud in Newmarket, he quickly became a leading sire, with offspring including Cracksman, Oaks winner Anapurna, and 2000 Guineas winner Chaldean.
