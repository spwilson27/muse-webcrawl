Water polo

Water polo is a competitive team sport played in water between two teams of seven players each (six field players and a goalkeeper). The game consists of four quarters in which players swim, tread water, and attempt to throw the ball into the opposing goal.

History

The sport originated in Britain in the late 19th century as a form of "water rugby" played in rivers and lakes. It was among the first team sports introduced at the modern Olympic Games, appearing at Paris 1900; women's water polo joined the Olympics at Sydney 2000.

Rules

Players may touch the ball with only one hand (except the goalkeeper), advance it by swimming or passing, and shoot within a 30-second shot clock. Fouls, exclusions and penalty throws are administered by referees. Hungary, Serbia, Croatia, Italy and the United States are among the leading nations.
