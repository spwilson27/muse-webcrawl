Jet Li


Li Lianjie (born 26 April 1963), better known as Jet Li, is a Chinese martial artist and actor.

Li Lianjie (born 26 April 1963), better known by his stage name Jet Li, is a Chinese-born martial artist and actor. A five-time national wushu champion of China as a teenager, he retired from competitive wushu at 19 and became one of the most internationally recognised martial arts film stars, acclaimed for films such as Shaolin Temple, the Once Upon a Time in China series, Hero, and Fearless.

Early life and wushu career

Born in Beijing, Li began training with the Beijing Wushu Team at age eight. He won his first national championship at eleven and went on to win the Chinese national all-around wushu title five times. In 1974 he performed for U.S. President Richard Nixon on the White House lawn during a tour of the United States.

Film career

Li's debut film, Shaolin Temple (1982), was a massive hit in China and launched the kung fu boom of the 1980s. In Hong Kong cinema he became famous playing the folk hero Wong Fei-hung in Tsui Hark's Once Upon a Time in China series, and starred in Fist of Legend (1994), widely regarded as one of the finest martial arts films. His Hollywood debut came as the villain in Lethal Weapon 4 (1998), followed by leading roles in Romeo Must Die, Kiss of the Dragon, and The One. Zhang Yimou's Hero (2002) was a global success, and Fearless (2006), in which he played Huo Yuanjia, was billed at the time as his final martial arts epic.

Philanthropy

After surviving the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami in the Maldives, Li founded the One Foundation, a charitable organisation focused on disaster relief and child welfare. He became a Singaporean citizen in 2009 and has increasingly devoted himself to philanthropy and to promoting tai chi through ventures such as Taiji Zen.
