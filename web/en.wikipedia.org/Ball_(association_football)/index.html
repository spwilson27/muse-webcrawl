Ball (association football)

A football is the ball used in the sport of association football. Its size, weight, and material are specified by Law 2 of the Laws of the Game maintained by the IFAB: a spherical ball with a circumference of 68–70 cm (size 5) and a weight of 410–450 g at the start of a match.

Construction

Early footballs were made of leather panels stitched around an inflatable bladder. The classic black-and-white truncated icosahedron design was popularized by the Adidas Telstar at the 1970 World Cup. Modern match balls use thermally bonded synthetic panels with textured surfaces for grip and stable flight.

World Cup balls

Each FIFA World Cup since 1970 has had an official Adidas match ball, including the Telstar, Tango, Jabulani (2010), Brazuca (2014), and Al Rihla (2022).
