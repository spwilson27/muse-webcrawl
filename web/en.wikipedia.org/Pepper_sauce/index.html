Pepper sauce

Pepper sauce, also known as hot sauce or chili sauce, is a condiment made from chili peppers and other ingredients such as vinegar, salt, and sometimes fruit or vegetables. Varieties range from mildly piquant to extremely hot, measured on the Scoville scale.

History

Chili-based sauces originated in the Americas, where peppers were domesticated thousands of years ago. Commercial sauces appeared in the 19th century; Tabasco sauce, produced by the McIlhenny Company in Louisiana since 1868, became one of the most famous brands worldwide. Sriracha, Louisiana-style sauces, and Caribbean Scotch bonnet sauces are other popular styles.

Types

Sauces may be fermented or fresh, red or green, and thin and vinegary or thick and sweet. In the Caribbean, "pepper sauce" commonly refers to homemade Scotch bonnet or habanero sauces served as a table condiment.
