Rory Williams

Rory Williams is a fictional character in the British science fiction television series Doctor Who, played by Arthur Darvill. Introduced in 2010 as the fiancé (later husband) of Amy Pond, Rory travels with the Eleventh Doctor and is noted for his loyalty, his work as a nurse, and a story arc in which an Auton duplicate of him guards Amy for nearly two thousand years, earning him the epithet "the Last Centurion."

Appearances

Rory first appears in "The Eleventh Hour" (2010) as a nurse in the village of Leadworth and Amy Pond's boyfriend. After travelling in the TARDIS and a series of adventures, he is erased from existence by a crack in time in "Cold Blood," then returns in "The Pandorica Opens" as a Roman centurion — revealed to be an Auton duplicate who retains Rory's memories and guards the Pandorica, and Amy inside it, for 1,894 years. Restored to humanity, he marries Amy and continues travelling with the Doctor.

In the 2011 series it is revealed that Amy was pregnant; her daughter, Melody Pond, grows up to become River Song. Rory's final regular appearance was in "The Angels Take Manhattan" (2012), in which he and Amy are sent back in time by a Weeping Angel and live out their lives in the past, ending their travels with the Doctor.

Characterisation

Rory is portrayed as grounded, kind, and courageous, often acting as a moral counterweight to the Doctor's recklessness. Although initially insecure about Amy's relationship with the Doctor, he develops into a confident companion in his own right. His profession as a nurse informs his compassionate nature and is frequently drawn upon in episodes.

Reception

The character was well received by critics and audiences, with Darvill's performance praised for giving the series emotional depth. Rory's long vigil as the Last Centurion is frequently cited as one of the most memorable companion arcs of the revived series.
