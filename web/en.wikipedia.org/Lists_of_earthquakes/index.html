Lists of earthquakes

Earthquakes are recorded by magnitude, location and human impact. The largest instrumentally recorded earthquake was the 1960 Valdivia earthquake in Chile, at magnitude 9.5. Other great megathrust events include Alaska 1964 (9.2), Sumatra 2004 (9.1), Japan 2011 (9.1) and Kamchatka 1952 (9.0).

Deadliest earthquakes

The deadliest in recorded history was the 1556 Shaanxi earthquake in China, which killed an estimated 830,000 people, many in collapsed loess cave dwellings. The 1976 Tangshan earthquake killed at least 242,000; the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami around 228,000; the 2010 Haiti earthquake over 200,000 by some estimates; and the 1920 Haiyuan earthquake roughly 260,000 including landslides.

Organisation of lists

Earthquakes are catalogued by century and year, by country and region, by magnitude and by death toll. Seismological agencies such as the USGS maintain comprehensive catalogues combining instrumental records, historical accounts and palaeoseismic evidence from trenching across faults.
