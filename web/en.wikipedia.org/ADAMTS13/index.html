ADAMTS13

ADAMTS13 (a disintegrin and metalloproteinase with a thrombospondin type 1 motif, member 13), also known as von Willebrand factor-cleaving protease, is a zinc-containing metalloprotease enzyme in the blood. It cleaves von Willebrand factor (vWF), a large protein involved in clotting, into smaller pieces, thereby regulating platelet adhesion and preventing excessive clot formation.

Clinical significance

Severe deficiency of ADAMTS13 activity causes thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP), a life-threatening disorder in which uncleaved ultra-large vWF multimers trigger widespread microvascular clots. The deficiency may be inherited (Upshaw–Schulman syndrome) or acquired through autoantibodies. Measuring ADAMTS13 activity is a key diagnostic test for TTP, and treatments include plasma exchange, caplacizumab, and the recombinant ADAMTS13 enzyme adzynma.

Genetics

The ADAMTS13 gene is located on chromosome 9q34. Mutations across the gene have been identified in congenital TTP patients.
