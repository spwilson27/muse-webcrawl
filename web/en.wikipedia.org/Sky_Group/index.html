Sky Group Limited is a British media and telecommunications conglomerate, a subsidiary of Comcast since 2018. Sky operates pay television, broadband and mobile services in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Italy, and produces original content through Sky Studios. It is one of Europe's largest media companies.

History

Sky's origins lie in Satellite Television Ltd, founded in 1981, which became Sky Channel. Rupert Murdoch's Sky Television launched direct-to-home satellite broadcasting in 1989; following a competitive struggle with British Satellite Broadcasting, the two merged in November 1990 to form British Sky Broadcasting (BSkyB). BSkyB transformed British sport broadcasting by buying rights to the newly formed Premier League in 1992, and launched digital satellite television (Sky Digital) in 1998.

In 2014 BSkyB acquired Sky Deutschland and Sky Italia from 21st Century Fox and renamed itself Sky plc. In 2018, after a bidding war with Walt Disney (which was acquiring most of 21st Century Fox), Comcast agreed to acquire Sky for £29.7 billion, completing the purchase in October 2018. Dana Strong became group chief executive in 2021.

Services

Sky's flagship products include the Sky Q and Sky Glass television platforms, Sky Sports and Sky Atlantic channels, Sky Broadband, Sky Mobile, and the NOW streaming service. Sky News operates a 24-hour news channel. The company employs around 31,000 people and reported roughly 23 million customers across its European markets.
