La Seu d'Urgell

La Seu d'Urgell is a town in Catalonia, Spain, in the comarca of Alt Urgell in the province of Lleida, near the border with Andorra, at the confluence of the Segre and Valira rivers.

History

Seat of the Bishop of Urgell — who is, ex officio, co-prince of Andorra — the town grew around its cathedral. Santa Maria d'Urgell, consecrated in the 12th century, is a notable example of Catalan Romanesque architecture.

Sport

The Segre Olympic Park, built for the 1992 Barcelona Olympics, hosted canoe slalom events and remains a venue for whitewater sport.
