Le Reculet

Le Reculet is a mountain in the Jura Mountains of eastern France. At 1,717 metres (5,633 ft) above sea level, it is the second-highest summit of the Jura massif, exceeded only by the nearby Crêt de la Neige (1,720 m). It is located in the department of Ain, in the commune of Thoiry, within the Haute Chaîne du Jura national nature reserve.

Geography

Le Reculet rises in the Pays de Gex region, overlooking the Rhône plain and, on clear days, offering views across Lake Geneva towards the Alps and Mont Blanc. A large cross stands at the summit, erected in the late nineteenth century. The mountain is a popular destination for hikers and cross-country skiers, accessible from trailheads near Thoiry and the Col de la Faucille.
