Paris Saint-Germain Football Club (commonly Paris SG or PSG) is a professional football club based in Paris, France. Founded in 1970 from the merger of Paris FC and Stade Saint-Germain, the club plays in Ligue 1, the top division of French football, and has become the most successful club in French football history, with a record number of Ligue 1 titles (more than a dozen) and Coupe de France trophies.

History

PSG won its first major trophy, the Coupe de France, in 1982 and its first league title in 1986. Under Canal+ ownership in the 1990s it won a second league title (1994) and the UEFA Cup Winners' Cup in 1996 — still the only European trophy won by a French club besides Marseille's 1993 European Cup. After a leaner period in the 2000s, the club was transformed in 2011 by its takeover by Qatar Sports Investments (QSI), becoming one of the richest clubs in the world.

QSI era

Backed by Qatari investment, PSG signed stars including Zlatan Ibrahimović, Thiago Silva, Edinson Cavani and, in 2017, Neymar for a world-record €222 million fee and Kylian Mbappé. The club dominated French football, winning the league in most seasons of the era, though repeated dramatic Champions League exits became a storyline. In 2025, under coach Luis Enrique and a younger squad built around players such as Ousmane Dembélé, Désiré Doué and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, PSG finally won the UEFA Champions League, defeating Inter Milan 5–0 in the Munich final — the largest margin in a European Cup final.

Identity

The club's home colours are blue with a central red-and-white vertical stripe (the "Hechter shirt", designed by fashion designer and former club president Daniel Hechter). Its crest features the Eiffel Tower and a fleur-de-lis. PSG's fierce rivalry with Olympique de Marseille is known as Le Classique. The club is based at the Parc des Princes in the 16th arrondissement of Paris.
