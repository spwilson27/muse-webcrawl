Salekhard is a city in northern Russia, the administrative centre of the Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug. It stands on the Ob River close to the Arctic Circle, which passes through the city and is marked by a monument — Salekhard is often described as the world's only city directly on the Arctic Circle.

History

Founded in 1595 as the Cossack fort of Obdorsk, it became a trading post for furs and a place of exile in the tsarist and Soviet periods. It was renamed Salekhard in 1933, from the Nenets for "settlement on the cape." In the late 1940s Gulag prisoners built much of the Salekhard–Igarka railway, the unfinished "Dead Road" east of the city.

Economy

The city serves the vast natural-gas fields of the Yamal Peninsula and is a river port and administrative hub; there is no year-round road or rail link to the rest of Russia, though a bridge project across the Ob is long planned.
