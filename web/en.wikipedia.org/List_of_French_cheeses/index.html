List of French cheeses

France produces a wide variety of cheeses — popularly said to number several hundred types — many protected by Appellation d'origine protégée (AOP) status.

Notable cheeses

  • Brie de Meaux, Brie de Melun — soft cow's-milk cheeses from Île-de-France.
  • Camembert de Normandie — soft bloomy-rind cheese from Normandy.
  • Roquefort — blue sheep's-milk cheese aged in caves near Roquefort-sur-Soulzon.
  • Comté, Beaufort, Morbier — pressed cooked cheeses from the Jura and Alps.
  • Munster, Époisses, Maroilles, Livarot, Pont-l'Évêque — washed-rind cheeses.
  • Cantal, Salers, Saint-Nectaire, Fourme d'Ambert — from Auvergne.
  • Crottin de Chavignol, Sainte-Maure de Touraine, Valencay, Pouligny-Saint-Pierre — goat's-milk cheeses, many from the Loire Valley.
  • Reblochon, Tome des Bauges, Ossau-Iraty, Brebis and Corsican cheeses such as Brocciu.
