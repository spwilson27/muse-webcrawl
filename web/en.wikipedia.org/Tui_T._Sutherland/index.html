Tui T. Sutherland (born July 31, 1978) is an American children's book author, best known for the bestselling dragon fantasy series Wings of Fire. She has also contributed, under the shared pseudonym Erin Hunter, to the Warriors and Seekers series, and has written numerous other books for young readers.

Early life and education

Sutherland was born in Caracas, Venezuela, and grew up in Paraguay, Florida, and the Dominican Republic before settling in the United States. She graduated from Williams College in 1998 and worked in publishing in New York before becoming a full-time writer.

Career

As part of the Erin Hunter team, Sutherland helped develop the Warriors series about feral cats. Her Wings of Fire series, begun in 2012, follows young dragons in the kingdoms of Pyrrhia and Pantala and has grown to include multiple arcs, graphic novel adaptations, and companion books.
