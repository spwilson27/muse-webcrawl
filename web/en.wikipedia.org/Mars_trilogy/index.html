Mars trilogy

The Mars trilogy is a series of science fiction novels by Kim Stanley Robinson chronicling the settlement and terraforming of Mars. The books are Red Mars (1992), Green Mars (1993), and Blue Mars (1996), following the "First Hundred" colonists and their descendants over nearly two centuries.

The series explores scientific, political, and ethical questions of colonisation, with factions debating whether Mars should be preserved ("Reds") or transformed into an Earth-like world ("Greens").

Red Mars won the BSFA Award and Green Mars and Blue Mars won the Hugo Award for Best Novel.
