Erythema nodosum

Erythema nodosum (EN) is an inflammatory condition characterised by inflammation of the fat cells under the skin, resulting in tender red nodules typically on the shins. It is a form of panniculitis and is thought to be a delayed hypersensitivity reaction.

Signs and symptoms

Patients develop painful, red, raised nodules, usually symmetrically on the front of the legs, often with fever, malaise, and joint pain. The lesions evolve through bruise-like colours and resolve without ulceration over weeks.

Causes

Triggers include streptococcal infections, sarcoidosis, coccidioidomycosis, tuberculosis, inflammatory bowel disease, pregnancy, and certain drugs; in many cases no cause is found.

Treatment

Treatment is directed at the underlying cause, with rest, leg elevation, and nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs for symptoms.
