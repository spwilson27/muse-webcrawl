1982 Lebanon War

Date6 June 1982 – June 1985 (main phase 1982)
LocationLebanon
BelligerentsIsrael and allies vs. PLO, Syria, and allies

The 1982 Lebanon War, codenamed Operation Peace for Galilee by Israel, began on 6 June 1982, when the Israel Defense Forces invaded southern Lebanon. Israel sought to expel the Palestine Liberation Organization, which had used Lebanon as a base for attacks on northern Israel.

Course of the war

Israeli forces advanced rapidly to Beirut, clashing with Syrian forces in the Beqaa Valley, and besieged the capital through the summer. Under a US-brokered agreement, the PLO evacuated Beirut in August–September 1982. Following the assassination of president-elect Bachir Gemayel, Israel occupied West Beirut, during which Lebanese Phalangist militia carried out the Sabra and Shatila massacre of Palestinian refugees.

Aftermath

Israel withdrew to a "security zone" in southern Lebanon in 1985, which it held until 2000. The war's prolonged occupation helped give rise to the Shia militant movement Hezbollah.
