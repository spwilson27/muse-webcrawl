Metro station

A metro station, or subway station, is a railway station for a rapid transit system, often called a "metro", "underground", "subway" or "elevated railway". Such a station provides a means for passengers to purchase tickets, board trains, and evacuate the system in case of emergency.

Design

Metro stations may be underground, at ground level, or elevated. Underground stations typically consist of a concourse or mezzanine level containing ticket gates and access points, above or below one or more platform levels. Common platform layouts include island platforms, side platforms, and stacked arrangements in deep-level stations. Many systems build stations with distinctive architecture — the stations of the Moscow Metro and the Stockholm metro are famous examples of stations treated as public art spaces.

Facilities

Modern metro stations include ticket machines, fare gates, escalators and lifts for accessibility, ventilation and emergency exits, and increasingly platform screen doors in automated systems.
