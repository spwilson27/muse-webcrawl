Enriques–Kodaira classification

The Enriques–Kodaira classification groups compact complex surfaces into ten classes according to their Kodaira dimension and other invariants.

Overview

Begun by Federigo Enriques and completed by Kunihiko Kodaira, the classification lists rational and ruled surfaces of Kodaira dimension −∞, elliptic and related surfaces of dimension 0 including K3, Enriques, abelian and hyperelliptic surfaces, surfaces of dimension 1 (properly elliptic), and surfaces of general type with dimension 2.

Significance

It is a cornerstone of algebraic geometry, analogous in spirit to the classification of curves by genus, and a model for higher-dimensional birational classification programmes.
