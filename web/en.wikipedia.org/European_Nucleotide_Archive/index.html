The European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) is a repository providing a comprehensive public record of nucleotide sequence data produced by the global research community. It is maintained by the European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) at the Wellcome Genome Campus in Hinxton, Cambridgeshire, England.

Overview

The ENA archives raw sequencing reads, assembled sequences, and functional annotation, covering data from small-scale experiments through to large international genome projects. It accepts submissions of sequences generated by a wide range of sequencing technologies, including Sanger sequencing and high-throughput platforms.

International collaboration

The ENA is a founding member of the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC), alongside the DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ) and GenBank at the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) in the United States. The three partners exchange data daily so that the same core sequence records are available from each archive.

Data access

Data in the ENA can be searched and retrieved through a web browser interface, programmatic REST APIs, and bulk download via FTP and Aspera. Accession numbers issued by the ENA are widely cited in scientific publications to identify underlying sequence data.
