Kyrgyz cuisine

Kyrgyz cuisine is the cuisine of the Kyrgyz people of Kyrgyzstan in Central Asia, shaped by centuries of nomadic herding. It centres on meat — mutton, beef, and horse meat — and dairy products, supplemented by noodles, bread, and vegetables traded from settled neighbours.

Characteristic dishes

Beshbarmak, boiled meat served over flat noodles with onion broth, is the national dish, traditionally eaten by hand. Other staples include plov (osh), laghman (pulled noodles), manti (steamed dumplings), shashlik, and samsa. Kumis, lightly fermented mare's milk, is the traditional summer drink, alongside ayran and black tea. Horse meat dishes such as chuchuk (sausage) are reserved for feasts and honoured guests.
