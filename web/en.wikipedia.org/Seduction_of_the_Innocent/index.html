Seduction of the Innocent

Seduction of the Innocent is a book by German-American psychiatrist Fredric Wertham, published in 1954, which warned that comic books were a negative form of popular culture and a serious cause of juvenile delinquency.

Claims

Wertham argued that crime and horror comics glamorised violence and crime, and that even superhero comics contained harmful, sexual and subversive themes — famously suggesting that Batman and Robin embodied a homosexual relationship and that Wonder Woman had lesbian and bondage undertones. He linked comics to illiteracy, delinquency and moral corruption among youth.

Impact

The book fed a public moral panic, contributed to the 1954 United States Senate Subcommittee hearings on juvenile delinquency, and prompted the comics industry to adopt the self-censorship of the Comics Code Authority. Publishers of horror and crime comics, notably EC Comics, were driven out of those genres. Later scholars have criticised Wertham's methods, showing that he misrepresented, overstated and fabricated some of his evidence.
