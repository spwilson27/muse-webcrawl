United States Secretary of Veterans Affairs

The United States Secretary of Veterans Affairs is the head of the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), the cabinet department responsible for administering benefits, health care, and national cemeteries for military veterans and their families. The secretary is a member of the President's Cabinet and is 17th in the presidential line of succession.

History

The position was created when the Veterans Administration was elevated to cabinet status by the Department of Veterans Affairs Act of 1988, effective 15 March 1989. Edward Derwinski, the last Administrator of Veterans Affairs, became the first secretary. Notable later secretaries have included Jesse Brown, Anthony Principi, Jim Nicholson, James Peake, Eric Shinseki, Robert McDonald, David Shulkin, Robert Wilkie, and Denis McDonough.

Duties

The secretary oversees one of the largest federal departments, including the Veterans Health Administration's hospital system, the Veterans Benefits Administration, and the National Cemetery Administration.
