Nine lyric poets

The Nine lyric poets were a canonical group of ancient Greek poets esteemed by the scholars of Hellenistic Alexandria as worthy of critical study. In the Palatine Anthology it is said that they established the lyric song.

The nine

The list varies slightly between sources; some include Corinna in place of one of the nine. The poets wrote in a variety of metres and dialects, and their works survive mostly in fragments quoted by later authors, along with papyrus finds.
