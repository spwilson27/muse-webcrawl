Jewish Legion

The Jewish Legion was the name for five battalions of the British Army's Royal Fusiliers raised in the First World War largely from Jewish volunteers from Britain, the United States, Canada, and Palestine, championed by Ze'ev Jabotinsky and Joseph Trumpeldor.

Preceded by the Zion Mule Corps at Gallipoli, the battalions (38th–42nd Royal Fusiliers) served under General Allenby in the Palestine campaign in 1918. Veterans later formed a nucleus of Jewish self-defence organizations in Mandatory Palestine.
