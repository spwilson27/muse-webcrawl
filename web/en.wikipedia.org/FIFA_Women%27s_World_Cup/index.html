FIFA Women's World Cup

The FIFA Women's World Cup is an international association football competition contested by the senior women's national teams of the members of FIFA. It has been held every four years since the inaugural tournament in 1991, which took place in China.

History

The United States won the first tournament and is the most successful team, with four titles; Germany has won twice, and Norway, Japan and Spain once each. The 2019 tournament in France drew record television audiences and a total attendance of over one million. The 2023 tournament, hosted jointly by Australia and New Zealand, was the first with 32 teams and was won by Spain. The final is among the most-watched women's sporting events in the world.

Format

Qualification takes place through continental competitions. The finals tournament consists of a group stage followed by a knockout stage culminating in the final.
