Dummy (album)

Dummy is the debut studio album by the English band Portishead, released on 22 August 1994 by Go! Beat Records. Built around Beth Gibbons' vocals, Geoff Barrow's production, and Adrian Utley's guitar work, it became a landmark of the trip hop genre that emerged from Bristol.

Music and reception

The album blends hip hop beats, samples from film scores and jazz records, scratches, and sombre orchestration on tracks such as "Sour Times", "Glory Box", "Mysterons", and "Roads". Dummy won the 1995 Mercury Prize and has been widely acclaimed as one of the greatest albums of the 1990s, frequently appearing in critics' all-time lists.

Track listing

Standard tracks include "Mysterons", "Sour Times", "Strangers", "It Could Be Sweet", "Wandering Star", "Numb", "Roads", "Pedestal", "Biscuit", and "Glory Box".
