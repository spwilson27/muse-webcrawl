NFL Network

NFL Network is an American sports-oriented cable and satellite television network owned by the National Football League. Launched on November 4, 2003, it is dedicated entirely to American football, broadcasting games, documentaries, studio analysis shows and coverage of events such as the NFL Draft and the scouting combine.

The network began airing live regular-season games in 2006 under the Thursday Night Football banner, a package that became one of its signature properties and was later shared with broadcast and streaming partners. Its flagship studio programme is NFL Total Access, and its morning show Good Morning Football launched in 2016. Long-running documentary series include America's Game: The Super Bowl Champions and A Football Life.

Distribution history

In its early years the network fought widely publicized carriage disputes with major cable operators such as Comcast and Time Warner Cable, which resisted its price and placement demands; most disputes were settled by the early 2010s, placing the channel in tens of millions of homes. Its operations are based in studios in the Los Angeles area, including its headquarters at Hollywood Park in Inglewood adjacent to SoFi Stadium, opened in 2021.
