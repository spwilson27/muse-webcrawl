Arrakis

Created by: Frank Herbert

Genre: Science fiction

Notable features: Desert planet, source of melange

Arrakis, also known as Dune, is a fictional desert planet in Frank Herbert's Dune universe. It is the only known source of the spice melange, the most valuable substance in the Imperium, which extends life and expands consciousness and makes interstellar travel possible.

Geography and ecology

Arrakis is covered almost entirely by desert. Its native ecology centres on the giant sandworms, which produce the spice through their life cycle, and the sandtrout that encyst water beneath the surface. The Fremen, the planet's nomadic human inhabitants, have adapted to its extreme aridity with stillsuits that recycle body moisture.

Role in the series

The planet is the principal setting of Dune (1965), where House Atreides takes control of it from House Harkonnen, and of several sequels chronicling its gradual transformation under new ecological regimes.
