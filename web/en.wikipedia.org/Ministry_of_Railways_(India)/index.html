The Ministry of Railways is a ministry in the Government of India responsible for the country's rail transport. It operates through the Railway Board and administers Indian Railways, one of the largest railway networks in the world under a single management and one of the country's biggest employers.

The ministry is headed by the Minister of Railways, a cabinet-level post. For many decades the ministry presented a separate Railway Budget in Parliament, a practice dating to the colonial era; in 2017 the railway budget was merged into the general Union Budget. The ministry oversees passenger and freight operations, zonal railways, production units, and major modernisation programmes including electrification, dedicated freight corridors and station redevelopment.

Organisation

The Railway Board, chaired by the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, is the apex decision-making body. Below it, Indian Railways is divided into zones, each headed by a general manager, which are further subdivided into operating divisions. The ministry also controls several public sector undertakings involved in construction, finance, catering and tourism, and container logistics.
