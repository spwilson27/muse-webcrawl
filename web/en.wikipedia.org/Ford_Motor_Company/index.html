Ford Motor Company (commonly known as Ford) is an American multinational automobile manufacturer headquartered in Dearborn, Michigan, United States. It was founded by Henry Ford and incorporated on June 16, 1903. The company sells automobiles and commercial vehicles under the Ford brand, and previously sold luxury cars under the Lincoln brand.

History

Henry Ford's early attempts at automobile manufacturing included the Detroit Automobile Company and the Henry Ford Company, the latter of which became Cadillac after Ford's departure. In 1903 he founded the Ford Motor Company with capital raised from investors including John and Horace Dodge.

The introduction of the Model T in 1908 transformed the automobile from a luxury item into practical transportation for the middle class. In 1913 Ford introduced the world's first moving assembly line for cars, drastically reducing production time and cost; a Model T chassis could be assembled in 93 minutes. In 1914 the company announced a five-dollar daily wage, more than doubling most workers' pay, which reduced turnover and enabled employees to afford the cars they built.

Products and operations

Ford's best-known products include the F-Series pickup trucks, which have been among the best-selling vehicles in the United States for decades, the Mustang sports car introduced in 1964, and the Transit van. The company operates manufacturing facilities worldwide and has major markets in North America, Europe, China, and South America.

In the 21st century Ford has invested heavily in electric vehicles, including the Mustang Mach-E and the F-150 Lightning, and in autonomous driving technology. The Ford family retains significant control of the company through special Class B shares despite holding a minority economic stake.

Motorsport

Ford has a long history in motorsport, including victories at the 24 Hours of Le Mans with the GT40 in the 1960s, success in Formula One as an engine supplier under the Cosworth partnership, and participation in the World Rally Championship and NASCAR.
