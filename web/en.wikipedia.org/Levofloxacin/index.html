Levofloxacin

Levofloxacin, sold under the brand name Levaquin among others, is a broad-spectrum antibiotic of the fluoroquinolone class. It is used to treat bacterial infections including pneumonia, urinary tract infections, sinusitis, prostatitis, anthrax and tuberculosis.

MechanismInhibits bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV

Medical uses

Levofloxacin is the left-handed isomer of ofloxacin and is active against many Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria. It is on the World Health Organization's List of Essential Medicines, notably for drug-resistant tuberculosis regimens.

Adverse effects

Like other fluoroquinolones it carries warnings for tendon rupture, peripheral neuropathy, central nervous system effects and aortic aneurysm risk, so regulators advise reserving it for cases without safer alternatives for minor infections.
