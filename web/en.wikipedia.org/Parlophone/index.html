Parlophone

Parlophone
Founded: 1896 (as Parlophon, Germany)
Genre: Various
Parent: Warner Music Group (since 2013)
Notable act: The Beatles

Parlophone Records Limited is a record label founded in Germany in 1896 by the Carl Lindström Company as Parlophon. The British branch, established in 1923, became famous under manager and producer George Martin, who signed the Beatles in 1962; Parlophone released most of the band's recordings, including Please Please Me and Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band.

History

Initially a jazz label in Britain, Parlophone was part of EMI from 1931. George Martin took over in 1950, recording comedy acts such as Peter Sellers and the Goons before turning to pop. Later Parlophone artists have included Queen (early 1970s), Radiohead, Coldplay, Gorillaz, Lily Allen and Dua Lipa (via Warner).

Ownership

When Universal Music acquired EMI in 2012, regulators required the divestment of Parlophone, which was sold to Warner Music Group in 2013. The Beatles' catalogue remained with Universal under Apple Corps/Calderstone.
