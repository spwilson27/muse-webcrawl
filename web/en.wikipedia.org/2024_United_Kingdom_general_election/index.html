2024 United Kingdom general election

The 2024 United Kingdom general election was held on Thursday, 4 July 2024, to elect 650 members of Parliament to the House of Commons. The Labour Party, led by Keir Starmer, won a landslide victory, ending 14 years of Conservative government.

Campaign

Prime Minister Rishi Sunak announced the election on 22 May 2024. The campaign focused on the economy, public services, immigration and standards in public life following scandals under previous Conservative governments. It was the first general election contested under new constituency boundaries and the first at which voters in Great Britain were required to show photographic identification.

Results

Labour won 411 seats with 33.7% of the vote, achieving a large majority on a comparatively modest vote share. The Conservatives fell to 121 seats, their worst result in history. Reform UK won 5 seats with 14.3% of the vote, the Liberal Democrats won 72 seats, and the Scottish National Party was reduced to 9 seats.

Aftermath

Keir Starmer became prime minister on 5 July 2024. Rishi Sunak resigned as Conservative leader, and Kemi Badenoch won the subsequent leadership election.
