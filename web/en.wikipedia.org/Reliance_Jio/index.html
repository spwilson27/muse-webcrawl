Reliance Jio

Jio Platforms / Reliance Jio Infocomm
Founded: 2007 (launched 2016)
Headquarters: Navi Mumbai, India
Parent: Reliance Industries
Industry: Telecommunications

Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited, doing business as Jio, is an Indian telecommunications company and a subsidiary of Jio Platforms, part of Reliance Industries. It operates a national 4G and 5G network and is India's largest mobile network operator by subscribers.

Launch and growth

Jio launched commercially on 5 September 2016 with an extended free-services offer, followed by some of the world's cheapest mobile data tariffs. The strategy rapidly attracted hundreds of millions of subscribers and forced competitors to cut prices and consolidate, sharply increasing internet use in India.

Services

Besides mobile telephony and broadband, Jio offers fibre-to-the-home service (JioFiber), enterprise connectivity, and a suite of apps for video, music, payments, and commerce. In 2020 Jio Platforms raised large investments from companies including Meta and Google.
