I Marine Expeditionary Force

The I Marine Expeditionary Force ("I" pronounced "One") is a Marine Air Ground Task Force (MAGTF) of the United States Marine Corps, primarily based at Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, California. It is the largest of the three standing Marine Expeditionary Forces and comprises ground, air and logistics elements capable of deploying globally on short notice.

Organisation

I MEF's major subordinate elements include the 1st Marine Division, the 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing and the 1st Marine Logistics Group, together with a command element and MEF Information Group. Its standing subordinate Marine Expeditionary Units — the 11th, 13th and 15th MEUs — deploy aboard U.S. Navy amphibious ships on rotation.

History

The force traces its roots to the I Marine Amphibious Corps of the Second World War. It was activated under its present name on 8 November 1969 at Camp Pendleton. I MEF provided the bulk of Marine forces in Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm (1990–91), led the ground combat element during the 2003 invasion of Iraq and subsequent operations in Al Anbar Province, and deployed extensively to Afghanistan, including Helmand Province. It also supports humanitarian assistance and disaster relief missions in the Indo-Pacific region.
