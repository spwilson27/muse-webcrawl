The Balsams Grand Resort Hotel is a grand hotel and ski resort located in Dixville Notch in New Hampshire's White Mountains, United States. The resort is famous as the location of the first-in-the-nation midnight vote in US presidential primaries, in which the residents of Dixville Notch — registered in the hotel's Ballot Room — cast the first ballots of the New Hampshire primary just after midnight.

History

The property began in 1866 as the Dix House, a modest inn at the notch, built by George Parsons. It was renamed The Balsams in the late nineteenth century and expanded into a grand resort by the Hale family, with the Hampshire House wing added in 1918, roughly doubling its capacity. At its height the self-sufficient resort had its own post office, power plant, dairy, golf course designed by Donald Ross, and thousands of acres of forest and trails.

Dixville Notch voting

Since 1960, residents of the unincorporated township have voted at The Balsams at midnight on primary and election day, receiving extensive media coverage as the first results reported in the nation. The tradition was promoted by resort owner Neil Tillotson, who also cast a celebrated first vote in general elections.

Closure and redevelopment

The resort closed in 2011 after being sold, and its contents were auctioned. Subsequent owners, led by developer Les Otten, have pursued an ambitious redevelopment and expansion of the ski area, with portions of the project proceeding in phases amid financing and permitting delays. The main hotel buildings, listed on the National Register of Historic Places, are slated for restoration as part of the plan.
