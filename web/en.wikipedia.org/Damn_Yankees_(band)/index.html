Damn Yankees (band)

Damn Yankees, American rock supergroup

Damn Yankees was an American rock supergroup formed in Los Angeles, California, in 1989. The band consisted of guitarist and vocalist Ted Nugent, bassist and vocalist Jack Blades (of Night Ranger), guitarist and vocalist Tommy Shaw (of Styx), and drummer Michael Cartellone. The group is best known for the power ballad "High Enough", which reached number 3 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1991.

History

The band was assembled by A&R executive John Kalodner, who combined three established arena-rock acts' frontmen with drummer Michael Cartellone. Their self-titled debut album, Damn Yankees (1990), was produced by Ron Nevison and sold over two million copies in the United States, powered by the singles "Coming of Age", "Come Again", and the hit ballad "High Enough".

The follow-up album, Don't Tread (1992), included the single "Where You Goin' Now" and a cover of Grand Funk Railroad's "We're an American Band". After touring, the members returned to their other projects; a third album was recorded in the late 1990s but never officially released by the label. The members have reunited sporadically for live performances.
