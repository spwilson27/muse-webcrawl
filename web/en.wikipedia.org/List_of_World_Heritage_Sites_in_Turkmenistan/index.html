List of World Heritage Sites in Turkmenistan

Turkmenistan acceded to the UNESCO World Heritage Convention in 1994. Its inscribed sites reflect the region's ancient Silk Road and Parthian and Khorezm history.

Inscribed sites

SiteInscribedNotes
State Historical and Cultural Park "Ancient Merv"1999Oasis city of the Karakum, one of the great cities of the Islamic world and a Silk Road hub
Kunya-Urgench2005Ruins of the Khorezm capital, with mausoleums and the Kutlug-Timur minaret
Parthian Fortresses of Nisa2007Old and New Nisa, early seat of the Parthian Empire near Ashgabat
Silk Roads: Zarafshan-Karakum Corridor2023Transnational listing shared with Tajikistan and Uzbekistan
Cold Winter Deserts of Turan2023Natural transnational site shared with Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan

Tentative list

Tentative entries have included Dehistan/Mishrian, Badhyz State Nature Reserve, and other Silk Road and natural sites.
