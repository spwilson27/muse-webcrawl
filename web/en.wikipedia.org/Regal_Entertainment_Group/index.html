Regal Entertainment Group

Regal Entertainment Group, doing business as Regal Cinemas, is an American movie theater chain headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee. It is one of the largest theater circuits in the United States. In 2018 the company was acquired by the British cinema operator Cineworld Group for approximately $3.6 billion, making the combined group one of the largest cinema operators in the world.

Industry: Entertainment (movie theaters)
Founded: 1989 (as Regal Cinemas)
Headquarters: Knoxville, Tennessee, U.S.
Parent: Cineworld Group (since 2018)
Brands: Regal, United Artists Theatres, Edwards Theatres

History

Regal Cinemas was founded in 1989 in Knoxville by Mike Campbell. Through the 1990s the company grew rapidly by building large multiplexes and stadium-seating auditoriums. Overextended, Regal filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in 2001; billionaire Philip Anschutz emerged as the controlling shareholder after restructuring, combining Regal with United Artists Theatres and Edwards Theatres — both of which he also controlled — to form Regal Entertainment Group in 2002.

The merged company operated theaters under the Regal, United Artists, and Edwards names. It was a founding partner of the Digital Cinema Implementation Partners venture to finance the conversion of auditoriums to digital projection.

Cineworld acquisition and afterwards

Cineworld completed its acquisition of Regal in February 2018. In September 2022, following the COVID-19 pandemic's impact on cinema attendance, Cineworld filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the United States; it emerged from restructuring in 2023, with Regal continuing to operate as its U.S. arm. Regal has also introduced subscription services such as Regal Unlimited, premium formats including IMAX, RPX, ScreenX, and 4DX, and widespread recliner seating conversions.
