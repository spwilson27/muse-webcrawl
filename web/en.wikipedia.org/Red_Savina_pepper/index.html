Red Savina pepper

The Red Savina pepper is a cultivar of the habanero chili pepper (Capsicum chinense) developed by Frank Garcia of GNS Spices in California. It was selected for its larger, heavier and hotter fruit.

Heat

The Red Savina was recognised by Guinness World Records as the world's hottest chili pepper from 1994 to 2006, measuring up to about 577,000 Scoville Heat Units — roughly twice the heat of a typical habanero. It was surpassed in 2007 by the Bhut Jolokia (ghost pepper).

Characteristics

The fruit ripens to a bright red, is about 5 cm long, and has the fruity flavour typical of habaneros beneath its intense heat. A protectively registered variety, it was for some years grown commercially only by its developer.
