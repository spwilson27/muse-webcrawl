Icelandair

Icelandair is the flag carrier of Iceland, with its headquarters at Keflavík International Airport near Reykjavík. The airline is the principal carrier of the Icelandair Group and operates scheduled services to destinations on both sides of the Atlantic Ocean via its hub at Keflavík.

History

Icelandair traces its roots to Flugfélag Akureyrar, founded in 1937, which merged with Loftleiðir and Flugfélag Íslands in the 1970s to form the present company. Its business model exploits Iceland's mid-Atlantic location, offering one-stop transatlantic connections between North America and Europe, and encouraging passengers to take a stopover in Iceland at no additional airfare cost — a programme that greatly boosted Icelandic tourism.

Fleet and network

The fleet has long centred on Boeing 757 aircraft, progressively supplemented and replaced by Boeing 737 MAX and Airbus A321LR jets. Icelandair serves dozens of destinations across Europe and North America, along with domestic Icelandic services operated under the Air Iceland Connect brand.
