Fu Hao

Bornc. 13th century BC
Diedc. 1200 BC
SpouseKing Wu Ding
Posthumous nameMu Xin

Fu Hao (婦好; died c. 1200 BC), posthumously Mu Xin, was one of the many wives of King Wu Ding of the Shang dynasty of ancient China. She served as a military general and high priestess, an exceptional position for a woman of her era, and is known from oracle bone inscriptions and her intact tomb.

Military career

Oracle bone records credit Fu Hao with leading numerous military campaigns, commanding as many as 13,000 soldiers against neighbouring peoples such as the Tu-Fang, Ba, Yi, and Qiang. She is the best-documented female general in early Chinese history.

Tomb

Her tomb at Yinxu (Anyang), excavated in 1976, is the only Shang royal tomb found completely intact. It contained 468 bronze objects — including ritual vessels and weapons — along with jade carvings, cowry shells, and ivory artefacts, offering an unparalleled window into Shang material culture.
