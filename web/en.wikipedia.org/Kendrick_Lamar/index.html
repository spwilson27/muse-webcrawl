Kendrick Lamar

Kendrick Lamar Duckworth (born June 17, 1987) is an American rapper and songwriter from Compton, California, regarded as one of the most influential artists of his generation. Raised in Section 8 housing, he began releasing music as K.Dot before signing with Top Dawg Entertainment.

His major-label debut good kid, m.A.A.d city (2012) was followed by the landmark To Pimp a Butterfly (2015), blending jazz, funk and spoken word with themes of race and identity. DAMN. (2017) won the Pulitzer Prize for Music — the first popular-music work so honoured. Later albums include Mr. Morale & the Big Steppers (2022) and GNX (2024); his 2024 feud with Drake produced the record-breaking diss track "Not Like Us", and he headlined the Super Bowl LIX halftime show in 2025.
