Hera (space mission)

Hera

Operator: European Space Agency (ESA)

Launch: 7 October 2024, Falcon 9, Cape Canaveral

Target: 65803 Didymos binary asteroid system

Arrival: late 2026 (planned)

Companions: CubeSats Juventas and Milani

Artist's impression of Hera at Didymos

Hera is a planetary defence mission of the European Space Agency (ESA) to the binary asteroid system 65803 Didymos. It is the European contribution to the Asteroid Impact and Deflection Assessment (AIDA) collaboration with NASA, whose DART spacecraft deliberately struck Didymos's moonlet Dimorphos in September 2022, shortening its orbital period. Hera will perform the first detailed post-impact survey of the system to measure the crater, the mass of Dimorphos, and the momentum transferred by the impact — data needed to validate asteroid deflection as a planetary defence technique.

Mission

Hera was launched on 7 October 2024 aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 from Cape Canaveral. After a Mars gravity assist in March 2025, it is scheduled to rendezvous with Didymos in late 2026 and study the system for about six months. The main spacecraft carries cameras, a laser altimeter, a thermal infrared imager (provided by JAXA), and a low-frequency radar experiment.

CubeSats

Hera carries two deep-space CubeSats, a first for ESA: Juventas, which will probe the interior of Dimorphos with low-frequency radar and attempt a landing, and Milani, which will image the surface in visible and near-infrared light to characterise its composition and the dust environment. The smaller craft will communicate with Earth through Hera as a relay.
