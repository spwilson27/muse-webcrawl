Admiral of the Fleet Sir George Rooke (1650 – 24 January 1709) was an English naval officer. As a junior officer he fought at the Battle of Solebay and other engagements of the Anglo-Dutch Wars, and he rose to become one of the leading admirals of the War of the Spanish Succession.

Career

Rooke served in the Nine Years' War, commanding at the Battle of Barfleur and burning French ships at La Hogue in 1692. In 1702 he destroyed the Spanish treasure fleet at the Battle of Vigo Bay, though an earlier assault on Cádiz had failed.

Gibraltar and later life

In August 1704 Rooke's Anglo-Dutch fleet captured Gibraltar, which has remained under British sovereignty ever since. Days later he fought the French at the Battle of Málaga, the largest naval battle of the war. Criticised at home and in poor health, he retired from the sea in 1705 and died in Canterbury in 1709.
