Club Santos Laguna is a Mexican professional football club based in Torreón, Coahuila, representing the Comarca Lagunera metropolitan area. Founded in 1983, the club plays in Liga MX, the top division of Mexican football, and is owned by Grupo Orlegi. Known as Los Guerreros, Santos wear green and white hooped shirts.

History

Santos entered the top flight in 1988 and won its first league championship in the Invierno 1996 tournament. Further titles followed in the Verano 2001, Clausura 2008, Clausura 2012, Clausura 2015, and Clausura 2018 tournaments, making Santos one of the most successful Mexican clubs of its era despite its comparatively recent founding. In 2009 the club moved to the new Estadio Corona within the Territorio Santos Modelo complex. Notable players include Jared Borgetti, the club's record goalscorer, Oribe Peralta, and goalkeeper Oswaldo Sánchez.

Rivalries and identity

The club's support is concentrated in the Lagunera region spanning Torreón, Gómez Palacio, and Lerdo. Its modern era has featured a strong youth academy and, through its ownership group, a sister relationship with Atlas FC.
