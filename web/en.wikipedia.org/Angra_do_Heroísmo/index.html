Angra do Heroísmo (Portuguese pronunciation: [ˈɐ̃ɡɾɐ ðu eɾuˈiʒmu]), commonly known as Angra, is a city and municipality on Terceira Island in the Portuguese autonomous region of the Azores. The population of the municipality is approximately 35,000, making it one of the largest urban centres in the archipelago. Its historic centre was classified as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.

History

Angra was founded around 1478 by Álvaro Martins Homem and was one of the first settlements in the Azores. Its name derives from angra (cove or inlet) and the epithet "do Heroísmo" (of Heroism), granted by Queen Maria II in 1837 in recognition of the city's role in the Liberal Wars.

The city's sheltered bay made it a vital port of call for fleets sailing between Europe, Africa, and the Americas during the Age of Discovery, as well as for the Portuguese India Armadas returning laden with spices and precious goods. This strategic importance attracted attacks by pirates and corsairs, prompting the construction of extensive fortifications, including the Fortress of São João Baptista on Monte Brasil.

During the Portuguese Civil War (1828–1834), Angra served as the capital of the liberal government in exile and the base from which the Duke of Terceira launched the expedition that landed at Mindelo in 1832. The city was briefly the capital of Portugal during this period.

On 1 January 1980, a powerful earthquake (the 1980 Azores Islands earthquake) severely damaged the city, killing dozens and destroying many historic buildings. Reconstruction preserved the original urban layout, contributing to the later UNESCO designation.

Geography

Angra do Heroísmo occupies the southern coast of Terceira. The city is dominated by Monte Brasil, an extinct volcanic cone that forms a peninsula sheltering the bay. The municipality comprises 19 civil parishes (freguesias).

Landmarks

Important sights include the Sé Cathedral (begun 1570), the Church of São Salvador, the Palace of the Capitães-Generais, the Fortress of São João Baptista (the largest fortress built by Spain in the 16th century), the Fortress of São Sebastião, and the Museum of Angra do Heroísmo. The city is also known for the Sanjoaninas festivals, held each June, among the largest popular festivities in the Azores.
