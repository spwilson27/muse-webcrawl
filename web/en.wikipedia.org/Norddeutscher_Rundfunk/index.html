Norddeutscher Rundfunk (NDR; "Northern German Broadcasting") is a public radio and television broadcaster serving northern Germany. It is a member of the ARD consortium of German public broadcasters and covers the states of Hamburg, Lower Saxony, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, and Schleswig-Holstein. Headquartered in Hamburg, NDR operates regional studios in Hanover, Kiel, Rostock, and Schwerin, among others.

History

NDR's origins lie in the Nordwestdeutscher Rundfunk (NWDR), established under British occupation after the Second World War and modelled partly on the BBC. In 1955 the NWDR was split into NDR (Hamburg) and Westdeutscher Rundfunk (WDR, Cologne), effective 1 January 1956. Mecklenburg-Vorpommern joined NDR's coverage area after German reunification in 1990. A notable controversy was the 2022 scandal surrounding the director of the Kiel state broadcasting house and the director-general of RBB, which prompted reforms of governance and compliance across ARD.

Services

NDR produces the television channel NDR Fernsehen with regional opt-outs (such as Hallo Niedersachsen, Schleswig-Holstein Magazin, and the Hamburg Journal), and contributes to national channels Das Erste, KiKA, arte, and others. It produces editions of the news programme Tagesschau from Hamburg — Germany's main television news — and the political talk shows and satire of its Hamburg base. Radio services include the pop station NDR 2, classical NDR Kultur, information channel NDR Info, youth station N-JOY, regional stations NDR 1 (with separate state programmes), and the Schlager and special-interest digital channels. NDR maintains the NDR Elbphilharmonie Orchester (resident at Hamburg's Elbphilharmonie), the NDR Radiophilharmonie in Hanover, the NDR Chor, and the NDR Bigband.

Programming

Well-known NDR productions include the long-running talk show 3 nach 9, the satirical magazine extra 3, the crime series Tatort episodes from Hamburg, Kiel, and Hanover, and the nature documentary brand Expeditionen ins Tierreich. NDR also organises the German national selection for the Eurovision Song Contest in many years.
