Lucien Bouchard

Lucien Bouchard (born December 22, 1938) is a Canadian lawyer and retired politician. A former Progressive Conservative cabinet minister and ambassador to France, he broke with Brian Mulroney's government over the Meech Lake Accord and founded the Bloc Québécois, becoming Leader of the Official Opposition in Ottawa in 1993.

Premier of Quebec

Bouchard led the Yes campaign in the 1995 Quebec sovereignty referendum, which the federalist side won by a razor-thin margin, then became Premier of Quebec (1996–2001) as leader of the Parti Québécois, pursuing balanced budgets before retiring from politics.
