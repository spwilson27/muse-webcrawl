Estate planning

Estate planning is the process of arranging, during a person's life, for the management and disposal of that person's estate during their life, in the event of incapacity, and after death, in a way that minimises taxes and legal complications.

Instruments

Core documents include a will, trusts (such as revocable living trusts), powers of attorney, and advance healthcare directives. Planning may also address guardianship for minor children, beneficiary designations, and charitable giving.

Goals

Aims include ensuring assets pass to intended beneficiaries, avoiding or simplifying probate, reducing estate and gift taxes, and providing for dependants.
