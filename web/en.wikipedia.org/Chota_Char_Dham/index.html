Chota Char Dham

The Chota Char Dham is a Hindu pilgrimage circuit of four shrines in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand, India: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.

The four shrines

Yamunotri is the source of the Yamuna River, Gangotri of the Ganges, Kedarnath one of the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines of Shiva, and Badrinath a major shrine of Vishnu. Pilgrims traditionally visit in clockwise order, from west to east, during the season from late spring to autumn when the high temples are open.

Significance

The "smaller" Char Dham complements the all-India Char Dham founded by Adi Shankara. The yatra draws hundreds of thousands of devotees annually and is a mainstay of Uttarakhand's economy.
