Burma campaign

The Burma campaign was a series of battles fought in British Burma during the Second World War, principally between Commonwealth, Chinese and United States forces and the Empire of Japan, from 1941 to 1945. It was the longest continuous campaign fought by the British in the war.

Japanese conquest, 1941–42

Japan invaded Burma in December 1941, seeking its resources and to close the Burma Road supply line to China. Rangoon fell in March 1942 and Allied forces retreated to India in the longest retreat in British military history.

Allied counteroffensives

Under Field Marshal William Slim, the Fourteenth Army — the multi-ethnic "Forgotten Army" — rebuilt and, supplied increasingly by air, defeated Japanese offensives at Imphal and Kohima in 1944. In 1945 Slim's forces crossed the Irrawaddy, captured Mandalay and retook Rangoon in Operation Dracula shortly before the Japanese surrender.
