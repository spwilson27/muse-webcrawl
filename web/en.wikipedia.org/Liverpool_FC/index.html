Liverpool FC is an English professional football club based in Liverpool, Merseyside. This article presents the club under its common short-form name; the club's full stylised title is Liverpool F.C.

The club was established in 1892 by John Houlding after Everton left Anfield for Goodison Park. Liverpool won the Lancashire League in its debut season and joined the Football League Second Division in 1893, earning promotion at the first attempt. It won its first First Division title in 1900–01.

Identity

The team traditionally plays in an all-red home strip, adopted in 1964 under manager Bill Shankly, who believed the colour had a psychological impact on opponents. The club badge incorporates the Liver bird, the symbol of the city, and the Shankly Gates' inscription "You'll Never Walk Alone". Two eternal flames on the badge commemorate the victims of the Hillsborough disaster of 1989, in which 97 supporters died.

Records

Ian Rush is the club's record goalscorer with 346 goals in all competitions. Ian Callaghan holds the record for most appearances, with 857 between 1958 and 1978. The club's record signing and record sale both exceeded £100 million in the 2020s as Liverpool remained among the world's highest-earning football clubs.
