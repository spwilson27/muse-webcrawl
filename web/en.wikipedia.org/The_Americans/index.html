The Americans

The Americans is an American period spy drama television series created by Joe Weisberg that aired on FX from 2013 to 2018. Set during the Cold War in the early 1980s, it follows two Soviet KGB officers posing as a married American couple in suburban Washington, D.C.

Premise

Philip and Elizabeth Jennings (Matthew Rhys and Keri Russell) run a travel agency by day and conduct espionage, assassinations, and seductions by night, while raising two children who know nothing of their true identities. Complication arrives when FBI counterintelligence agent Stan Beeman moves in across the street.

Reception

The series was widely acclaimed as one of the finest dramas of its era, praised for its writing, acting, and moral complexity. It won the Peabody Award and, in its final season, Emmy Awards for Rhys's acting and for the writing of the finale, "START".
