Benjamin "Bibi" Netanyahu (born 21 October 1949) is an Israeli politician who has served as Prime Minister of Israel since December 2022, having previously held the office from 1996 to 1999 and from 2009 to 2021. He is the longest-serving prime minister in Israel's history. Netanyahu has chaired the Likud party since 2005, having also led it in the 1990s.

Early life and military service

Netanyahu was born in Tel Aviv to Benzion Netanyahu, a historian, and Tzila Segal. He spent part of his adolescence in the United States, near Philadelphia. Returning to Israel in 1967, he served in the Sayeret Matkal special forces unit, taking part in operations including the rescue of hostages from hijacked Sabena Flight 571 in 1972. His elder brother Yonatan was killed commanding the Entebbe rescue operation in 1976. Netanyahu studied architecture and management at MIT.

Political career

After serving as Israel's ambassador to the United Nations (1984–1988), Netanyahu entered the Knesset and became Likud leader in 1993. As prime minister in the 1990s he pursued economic liberalisation and a cautious peace policy. Returning to power in 2009, he led successive coalition governments for twelve years, emphasising security, opposition to Iran's nuclear programme, and normalisation agreements with several Arab states under the Abraham Accords of 2020. He returned to office after the November 2022 election at the head of a right-wing and religious coalition.
