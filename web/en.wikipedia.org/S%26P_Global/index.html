S&P Global Inc. is an American publicly traded corporation headquartered in New York City. Its primary businesses are financial information and analytics. The company traces its origins to the McGraw-Hill publishing and information conglomerate, founded in the early 20th century, and adopted the S&P Global name in 2016 after divesting its education division.

Business segments

The company's divisions include S&P Global Ratings, one of the world's major credit rating agencies; S&P Dow Jones Indices, provider of the S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average; S&P Global Market Intelligence, a financial data and research platform (expanded through the 2022 merger with IHS Markit); S&P Global Commodity Insights, including the Platts energy price reporting business; and S&P Global Mobility, covering automotive data.
