Glossary of equestrian terms

This is a basic glossary of equestrian terms used in horse husbandry, riding and horse sports.

Terms

bit
The metal mouthpiece of a bridle, by which the rider communicates rein aids to the horse.
bridle
The headgear, consisting of headstall, bit and reins, used to control a ridden horse.
canter
A three-beat gait, faster than a trot and slower than a gallop.
colt
A young male horse, usually under four years of age.
dressage
The systematic training of horse and rider, and the Olympic discipline in which prescribed movements are performed in an arena.
farrier
A specialist who trims horses' hooves and fits horseshoes.
filly
A young female horse, usually under four years of age.
foal
A horse in its first year of life.
gallop
The fastest natural gait of the horse, a four-beat gait with a moment of suspension.
gelding
A castrated male horse.
girth
The strap that passes under the horse's belly to hold the saddle in place (a cinch in western riding).
hand
The unit for measuring a horse's height at the withers; one hand equals four inches (10.16 cm).
mare
An adult female horse, generally four years or older.
martingale
A piece of tack that limits how high a horse can raise its head.
near side / off side
The horse's left side (the side from which it is traditionally led and mounted) and right side, respectively.
saddle
The seat fastened on the horse's back for the rider, supported by a tree and held by the girth.
stallion
An uncastrated adult male horse.
stirrup
The ring or treadle hanging from the saddle in which the rider places the foot.
tack
Collectively, the saddlery and equipment used on a horse.
trot
A two-beat diagonal gait, between a walk and a canter in speed.
walk
A slow four-beat gait, the most basic of the horse's gaits.
weanling
A foal that has been weaned from its dam but is under one year old.
withers
The ridge between the shoulder blades at the base of the neck, the point from which height is measured.
yearling
A horse between one and two years old.
