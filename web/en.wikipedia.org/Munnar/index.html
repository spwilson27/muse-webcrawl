Munnar is a hill station and town in the Idukki district of Kerala, in the Western Ghats of south-western India. Its name is said to derive from the Tamil/Malayalam words moonnu (three) and aaru (river), referring to the confluence of three mountain streams.

Geography and economy

Surrounded by rolling hills covered with tea plantations established in the late 19th century under British planters, Munnar is a major centre of tea production (including Tata Tea's estates) and one of Kerala's most popular tourist destinations. Nearby attractions include Eravikulam National Park, home to the endangered Nilgiri tahr, and the mass flowering of neelakurinji shrubs every twelve years.
