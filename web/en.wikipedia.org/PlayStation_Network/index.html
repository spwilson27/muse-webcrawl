The PlayStation Network (PSN) is a digital media entertainment service provided by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Launched in November 2006, it was originally conceived for the PlayStation video game consoles, but soon extended to encompass smartphones, tablets, Blu-ray players and high-definition televisions. The service is the account-based platform for PlayStation consoles, providing access to online multiplayer gaming, the PlayStation Store for digital content purchases, streaming media applications, and social features.

As of the mid-2020s, the PlayStation Network has more than 120 million monthly active users worldwide, making it one of the largest online gaming services in the world, competing with Microsoft's Xbox network and Nintendo's Nintendo Network.

History

Sony's first foray into online gaming was with the PlayStation 2, which supported online play through an optional network adapter, but without a unified central service. With the announcement of the PlayStation 3 in 2005, Sony revealed it would offer a free, unified online platform. The PlayStation Network launched alongside the console on November 11, 2006 in Japan and November 17, 2006 in North America.

In April 2011, the service suffered a major external intrusion that compromised the personal data of approximately 77 million accounts. Sony shut down the network for 23 days, one of the largest data breaches in history at the time. The company subsequently offered affected users free games and identity-theft protection through its "Welcome Back" program.

Services

The core of PSN is the user account, which stores a player's trophies, friends list and purchased content. PlayStation Plus, launched in 2010, is a subscription service that adds online multiplayer access on PS4 and PS5, monthly downloadable games, cloud saves and exclusive discounts. The PlayStation Store allows users to purchase and download games, add-ons, movies and themes. Other services historically offered through the network include PlayStation Now, a cloud streaming service merged into PlayStation Plus Premium in 2022, PlayStation Vue, a television streaming service discontinued in 2020, and PlayStation Home, a virtual social platform that ran from 2008 to 2015.

User accounts

Registration for a PSN account is free. Accounts are identified by an Online ID, a unique username displayed in games and on leaderboards. In 2019 Sony began allowing users to change their Online ID for the first time. Accounts are region-specific, and content purchased in one region's store generally cannot be accessed from an account registered in another region.
