Home Secretary

Secretary of State for the Home Department
Department: Home Office
First holder: Earl of Shelburne (1782)
Appointer: The monarch, on advice of the prime minister

The Secretary of State for the Home Department, more commonly known as the Home Secretary, is a senior minister of the Crown in the Government of the United Kingdom and head of the Home Office. It is one of the four Great Offices of State.

Responsibilities

The Home Secretary is responsible for internal affairs in England and Wales, including policing, national security in cooperation with the security services, immigration and borders, and citizenship. Many functions have shifted over time, with justice and prisons moving to the Ministry of Justice in 2007.

History

The office dates to 1782, when the Home Department was created from the former Southern Department. Its holders have included Robert Peel, who founded the Metropolitan Police while Home Secretary.
