Electronic payment

An electronic payment (also e-payment) is a digital transaction between two parties, in which money is exchanged through electronic means rather than cash or paper cheques. Electronic payments include transfers made with payment cards, mobile phones, and the internet.

Types

Common methods include credit and debit cards, bank transfers and direct debits via automated clearing houses, e-wallets, mobile payment services, online payment processors, and QR-code payments. One-time and recurring payments may be authorised in various ways, including PINs, signatures, and biometric authentication.

Infrastructure

Transactions are routed through payment gateways, card networks such as Visa and Mastercard, and interbank settlement systems. Standards such as EMV govern chip-card transactions, while real-time payment systems settle transfers in seconds.

Adoption and impact

Electronic payments have displaced cash in many economies, enabling e-commerce and reducing handling costs, while raising questions about privacy, fees, financial inclusion, and resilience when networks fail.
