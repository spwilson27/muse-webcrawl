Albert II (Albert Alexandre Louis Pierre Grimaldi; born 14 March 1958) is Prince of Monaco, reigning since the death of his father, Rainier III, in 2005. He is the son of Rainier III and the American actress Grace Kelly, and heads the thousand-year-old House of Grimaldi, which has ruled Monaco, with interruptions, since 1297.

Early life and sport

Educated in Monaco and at Amherst College in Massachusetts, where he studied political science, Albert served briefly in the French Navy. A keen sportsman, he competed in bobsleigh at five consecutive Winter Olympic Games between 1988 and 2002 and has been a member of the International Olympic Committee since 1985. He is also an accomplished judoka and patron of Monegasque sport, including AS Monaco football club and the Monaco Grand Prix.

Reign

Albert succeeded as sovereign prince on 6 April 2005 and was formally enthroned that November. His reign has emphasised environmental protection: he established the Prince Albert II of Monaco Foundation in 2006 to combat climate change, protect biodiversity, and manage water resources, and in 2006 he travelled to the North Pole to draw attention to global warming — later adding a South Pole journey, making him the first head of state to visit both poles. Monaco under Albert has pursued land reclamation projects such as the Mareterra eco-district and has worked to diversify its economy beyond finance and tourism.

Marriage and family

In 2011 Albert married Charlene Wittstock, a former Olympic swimmer from South Africa. Their twins, Princess Gabriella and Prince Jacques, were born in December 2014; Jacques is heir apparent under Monaco's male-preference primogeniture. The prince has also acknowledged two children born before his marriage.
