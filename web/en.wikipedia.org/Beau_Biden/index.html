Beau Biden

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Joseph Robinette "Beau" Biden III (February 3, 1969 – May 30, 2015) was an American politician, lawyer and officer in the Delaware Army National Guard. The eldest son of U.S. President Joe Biden, he served as Attorney General of Delaware from 2007 to 2015.

Life and career

Biden survived the 1972 car crash that killed his mother Neilia and sister Naomi. He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania and Syracuse University College of Law, worked as a federal prosecutor in Philadelphia, and served in Iraq in 2008–2009 as a captain in the Delaware Army National Guard, earning the Bronze Star Medal.

Death

Biden died of glioblastoma, a form of brain cancer, on May 30, 2015, aged 46, while considered a leading candidate for Governor of Delaware. The Beau Biden Foundation for the Protection of Children was established in his memory.
