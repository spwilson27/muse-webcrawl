Football Club Internazionale Milano, commonly referred to as Internazionale or simply Inter, and known as Inter Milan in English-speaking countries, is an Italian professional football club based in Milan, Lombardy. Founded in 1908 following a schism within the Milan Cricket and Football Club (now AC Milan), Inter is the only Italian side to have always competed in the top flight of Italian football since its debut in 1909.

History

The club was founded by players who wanted the Milan club to accept foreign players as well as Italians — hence the name Internazionale. Inter won its first championship in 1910. Under coach Helenio Herrera in the 1960s, the "Grande Inter" side won three Serie A titles and back-to-back European Cups in 1964 and 1965, along with two Intercontinental Cups.

In 2010, managed by José Mourinho, Inter won an unprecedented Italian treble of Serie A, the Coppa Italia, and the UEFA Champions League, defeating Bayern Munich 2–0 in the final in Madrid — the first and only treble by an Italian club. Inter's honours include 20 Serie A titles, 9 Coppa Italia trophies, 3 European Cups/Champions Leagues, 3 UEFA Cups, 2 Intercontinental Cups, and 1 FIFA Club World Cup.

Stadium and rivalry

Inter shares the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza — commonly called San Siro — with city rivals AC Milan. Matches between the two are known as the Derby della Madonnina. The club's other major rivalry is with Juventus, in the Derby d'Italia. Inter's colours are black and blue vertical stripes, giving the team the nickname Nerazzurri.
