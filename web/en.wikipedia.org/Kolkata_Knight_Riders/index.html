Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) is a professional Twenty20 cricket franchise based in Kolkata, West Bengal, that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Owned by Bollywood actor Shah Rukh Khan's Red Chillies Entertainment together with Juhi Chawla and Jay Mehta, the team plays its home matches at Eden Gardens and wears purple and gold.

History

KKR was one of the eight founding IPL franchises in 2008, with Sourav Ganguly as its icon player and first captain. After inconsistent early seasons, the team was rebuilt under captain Gautam Gambhir, winning the IPL title in 2012 (defeating Chennai Super Kings) and again in 2014 (defeating Kings XI Punjab), when it also set a then-record winning streak. Under Shreyas Iyer's captaincy, KKR won a third championship in 2024, beating Sunrisers Hyderabad in the final after a dominant league campaign.

Home ground and fanbase

Eden Gardens, one of the largest and most storied cricket stadiums in the world, gives KKR one of the league's most passionate home crowds. The team's anthem, "Korbo, Lorbo, Jeetbo Re" ("We will do, we will fight, we will win"), is a fixture of Kolkata sporting culture.

Knight Riders group

The franchise's owners have expanded the Knight Riders brand globally, with sister teams including the Trinbago Knight Riders in the Caribbean Premier League, the Abu Dhabi Knight Riders in the ILT20, and the Los Angeles Knight Riders in Major League Cricket.
