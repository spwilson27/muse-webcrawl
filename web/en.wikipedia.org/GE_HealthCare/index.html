GE HealthCare Technologies Inc. is an American medical technology company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. A longtime division of General Electric, it became an independent public company when GE spun it off in January 2023 as the first step of the conglomerate's three-way breakup.

Business

GE HealthCare operates in four segments: Imaging (CT, MRI, X-ray, molecular imaging and related equipment), Ultrasound, Patient Care Solutions (monitoring, anaesthesia and maternal care), and Pharmaceutical Diagnostics (contrast media and radiopharmaceuticals). Its equipment is installed in hospitals and clinics in more than 160 countries, and the company reports that over a billion patients a year are imaged or monitored using its technology.

History

GE's medical business traces to its X-ray work in the early twentieth century and expanded through acquisitions including Amersham, the British life sciences and contrast media firm bought in 2004. After the spin-off, GE retained a minority stake that it gradually sold down. GE HealthCare joined the S&P 500 index in 2023.

Products and research

Notable products include the Revolution CT scanner series, SIGNA MRI scanners, the Voluson and LOGIQ ultrasound lines, and the Edison digital health platform. The company invests heavily in artificial intelligence for image reconstruction and workflow, including FDA-cleared deep-learning reconstruction tools.
