Legend of the White Snake

The Legend of the White Snake is one of China's Four Great Folktales. It tells of Bai Suzhen, a white snake spirit who takes human form and falls in love with the young scholar Xu Xian.

Story

Their marriage is opposed by the monk Fahai, who reveals Bai Suzhen's true nature and ultimately imprisons her beneath Leifeng Pagoda by West Lake in Hangzhou. Versions, including Feng Menglong's Ming-dynasty story, developed the tale from an earlier cautionary story into a romance of love defying prejudice.

Adaptations

The legend is a staple of Chinese opera and has inspired many films, television series and animations.
