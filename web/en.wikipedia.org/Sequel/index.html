Sequel

A sequel is a work of literature, film, theatre, television, music, or video game that continues the story of, or expands upon, an earlier work. In the common context of a narrative work of fiction, a sequel portrays events set in the same fictional universe as an earlier work, usually chronologically following it.

Related terms

A work set earlier than a preceding work is a prequel; one occurring between previously released works is sometimes called an interquel or midquel, and a side story expanding a minor element is a spin-off. Series of three works may be called trilogies. Sequels are often numbered or subtitled.

History and commercial role

Sequels are ancient—continuations appear in classical epic and early novels—but became a dominant commercial strategy in modern publishing and especially in cinema, where successful films routinely generate franchises of sequels.
