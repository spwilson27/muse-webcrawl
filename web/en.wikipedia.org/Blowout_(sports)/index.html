Blowout (sports)

In sports, a blowout is an easy or one-sided victory in which one team or competitor outscores the other by a large margin, or in such a fashion that the game effectively becomes decided well before its end. Blowouts are common in mismatched contests, such as early rounds of knockout tournaments or professional teams playing much weaker opposition.

Context and effects

Commentators note that blowouts can diminish spectator interest and television ratings, with fans leaving venues early. Coaches of winning teams often rest their star players ("garbage time" for reserves) to reduce injury risk and, in some sporting cultures, to avoid being seen as unsporting by "running up the score" against an overmatched opponent. Conversely, some competitions use tie-breaking rules that reward large margins, encouraging teams to keep scoring.

Responses

Some leagues and youth competitions apply a "mercy rule", ending the game early once a specified lead is reached, and clock-running rules that shorten the remainder of the game. Leagues also use drafts, salary caps, scheduling, and promotion and relegation to keep competition balanced and limit blowouts.
