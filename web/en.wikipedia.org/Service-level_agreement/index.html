Service-level agreement

A service-level agreement (SLA) is a commitment between a service provider and a customer. Particular aspects of the service—quality, availability, responsibilities—are agreed between the provider and the user of the service.

Contents

SLAs commonly specify metrics such as uptime percentages, response and resolution times, and performance thresholds, together with remedies or service credits when targets are missed. Internal agreements within an organisation are sometimes termed operational-level agreements (OLAs), while contracts with external suppliers underpinning an SLA are underpinning contracts.

Use

SLAs are standard in telecommunications, IT services, cloud computing, and outsourcing, where they give customers measurable assurance about services delivered over networks they do not control.
