Joan Violet Robinson (1903–1983) was a British economist, a central figure in the Cambridge circle around John Maynard Keynes and a founder of post-Keynesian economics.

Work

Her first major book, The Economics of Imperfect Competition (1933), developed tools for analysing markets between pure competition and monopoly. She helped expound and extend Keynes's General Theory, and her The Accumulation of Capital (1956) launched theories of long-run growth and capital that fuelled the Cambridge capital controversy with American economists.

Influence

Robinson was a fierce critic of orthodox neoclassical theory and an influential teacher at the University of Cambridge. She is often cited as one of the most important economists never to receive the Nobel Prize in Economics.
