Living in Bondage: Breaking Free

Living in Bondage: Breaking Free is a 2019 Nigerian supernatural thriller film and the sequel to the landmark 1992 video film Living in Bondage, which is widely credited with launching the Nollywood industry. The sequel was directed by Ramsey Nouah in his directorial debut and produced by Charles Okpaleke of Play Network Studios.

Plot

Nnamdi Okeke, the ambitious son of Andy Okeke, seeks rapid wealth and is drawn into the same secret occult Brotherhood that destroyed his father's first family, mentored by the wealthy Richard Williams. As success and tragedy mount, Nnamdi must confront the cult and his father's past.

Reception

The film brought back original cast members including Kenneth Okonkwo as Andy and Kanayo O. Kanayo, alongside Jide Kene Achufusi (Swanky JKA) as Nnamdi and Munachi Abii. It was a box-office success in Nigeria, won several Africa Magic Viewers' Choice Awards including Best Director and Best Movie West Africa, and reached wider audiences through streaming.
