Blue Devils Drum and Bugle Corps

The Blue Devils Drum and Bugle Corps is a World Class competitive junior drum and bugle corps based in Concord, California. Founded in 1957, it is a member corps of Drum Corps International (DCI) and is among the most successful corps in the activity's history.

History

The organisation began as a drum and bell corps sponsored by a local VFW post and evolved into a drum and bugle corps in the early 1970s. The Blue Devils won their first DCI World Championship in 1976 and have accumulated more than twenty titles, the most of any corps.

Organisation

The Blue Devils organisation (BD Performing Arts) also fields the Blue Devils B and C corps, the Blue Devils International corps, and other performing ensembles.
