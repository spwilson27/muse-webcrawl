Krasnoyarsk Krai (Russian: Красноярский край) is a federal subject of Russia (a krai) located in Siberia. Its administrative centre is the city of Krasnoyarsk. It is the second-largest federal subject by area, exceeded only by the Sakha Republic, and stretches from the Sayan Mountains in the south to the Taymyr Peninsula on the Arctic Ocean.

Geography

The krai is bisected by the Yenisei River, one of the great Siberian rivers, flowing north to the Kara Sea. In 2007 the Taymyr and Evenk autonomous okrugs were merged into the krai, greatly enlarging its territory. The region includes the Putorana Plateau, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and Norilsk, one of the largest cities above the Arctic Circle.

Economy

Krasnoyarsk Krai is a major centre of mining and metallurgy: Norilsk Nickel produces much of the world's palladium and nickel near Norilsk; aluminium is smelted at Krasnoyarsk; and the Krasnoyarsk and Sayano-Shushensk hydroelectric dams on the Yenisei and its tributaries are among the largest power stations in the world.
