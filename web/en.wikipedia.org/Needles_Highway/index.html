Needles Highway
DesignationSouth Dakota Highway 87
LocationBlack Hills, South Dakota, United States
Lengthabout 14 miles (23 km)

The Needles Highway is a scenic section of South Dakota Highway 87 in the Black Hills of South Dakota, within Custer State Park. It is named for the tall, needle-like granite spires through which it winds.

Features

Completed in 1922, the road was long considered impossible to build. It passes through narrow single-lane tunnels blasted through the rock, over pigtail bridges and around hairpin turns, offering views of the Cathedral Spires and other eroded granite formations.

Use

The highway is a popular tourist drive in summer and is generally closed in winter. Its tight tunnels restrict the size of vehicles that can use it.
