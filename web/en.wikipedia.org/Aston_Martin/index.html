Aston Martin Lagonda is a British manufacturer of luxury sports cars and grand tourers. It was founded in 1913 by Lionel Martin and Robert Bamford. The company's name derives from the Aston Clinton hill climb in Buckinghamshire and the surname of co-founder Lionel Martin.

History

Steered from 1947 by David Brown, the company became associated with expensive grand touring cars in the 1950s and 1960s, and with the fictional character James Bond, whose use of the DB5 in the 1964 film Goldfinger made the marque world-famous. The company has survived seven bankruptcies and passed through many owners, including Ford Motor Company, which held it from 1994 to 2007.

Models

Well-known models include the DB series (named for David Brown), the Vantage, DB11, DBS, and the Valkyrie hypercar. The DBX, launched in 2020, was the company's first SUV. Aston Martin returned to Formula One as a works team in 2021, based at Silverstone.

Motorsport

Aston Martin won the 24 Hours of Le Mans outright in 1959 and has since competed successfully in GT racing with the Vantage.
