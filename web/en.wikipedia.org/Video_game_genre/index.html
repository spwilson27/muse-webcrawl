Video game genre

A video game genre is a category of video games defined by a common set of gameplay characteristics rather than by setting or narrative, which distinguishes game genres from literary and film genres. Major genres include action, adventure, role-playing, simulation, strategy, sports, puzzle, and idle games, each with numerous subgenres: action alone includes platformers, shooters, fighting games and beat 'em ups.

Classification

Genre labels are used by developers, retailers, journalists and players to communicate what kind of experience a game offers. Many successful games combine genres — action-adventure and action role-playing are common hybrids — and newer labels such as "roguelike", "metroidvania", "soulslike" and "battle royale" have emerged from influential individual games. Scholars note that genre boundaries are fuzzy and evolve with technology and design practice.
