Dice game

Dice games are games that use or incorporate one or more dice as their sole or central component, usually as a random device. Dice games range from simple games of pure chance to strategy games in which dice rolls are combined with decision-making.

A pair of standard six-sided dice.

History

Dice are among the oldest gaming implements known, with knucklebones and marked stones used in antiquity. Dice games were played in ancient Egypt, Greece, and Rome, and dicing was a popular pastime across medieval Europe despite periodic prohibitions. The modern casino game of craps descends from the English game of hazard.

Types of dice games

  • Banking games, in which players bet against a house or banker, such as craps and sic bo.
  • Point-scoring games, such as Yahtzee and Farkle, in which rolls are scored in categories or combinations.
  • Race games, in which dice movement determines progress around a board, as in backgammon (which combines dice with strategy).
  • Gambling and street games, such as cee-lo, played with three dice.

Probability

Because dice outcomes can be enumerated, dice games were central to the early development of probability theory: the correspondence between Blaise Pascal and Pierre de Fermat in 1654, prompted by questions about dice gambling, laid the foundations of the field.
