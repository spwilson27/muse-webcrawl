Interlagos, officially the Autódromo José Carlos Pace, is a motorsport circuit in São Paulo, Brazil, named after the Brazilian driver José Carlos Pace. Built between the city's two artificial lakes, Guarapiranga and Billings — hence "Interlagos", "between the lakes" — it opened in 1940.

Formula One

The circuit first hosted the Brazilian Grand Prix in 1973 and, after a spell at Jacarepaguá, has been its permanent home since 1990 on a shortened layout. Its anti-clockwise direction, changeable weather and elevation changes frequently produce dramatic races, and it decided several world championships, including in 2007, 2008 and 2012.
