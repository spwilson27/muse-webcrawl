WarnerMedia, LLC was an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate. It was originally formed as Time Warner in 1990 from the merger of Time Inc. and Warner Communications, and at its peak ranked among the largest media companies in the world, with operations in film, television, cable networks, and publishing. Its principal units included the Warner Bros. film and television studios, HBO and Cinemax, the Turner networks (CNN, TNT, TBS, and Cartoon Network), and — until spin-offs — Time Inc.'s magazine business and AOL.

History

The combined company endured the disastrous 2000 merger with AOL, after which it gradually slimmed down: AOL was spun off in 2009, Time Warner Cable in 2009, and Time Inc. in 2014. In October 2016 AT&T announced it would acquire Time Warner for $85.4 billion; after an antitrust trial, the deal closed in June 2018, and the company was renamed WarnerMedia. Under AT&T it launched the HBO Max streaming service in May 2020.

Merger into Warner Bros. Discovery

In May 2021 AT&T announced it would spin off WarnerMedia and merge it with Discovery, Inc. The transaction closed on April 8, 2022, creating Warner Bros. Discovery, and WarnerMedia ceased to exist as a separate company.
