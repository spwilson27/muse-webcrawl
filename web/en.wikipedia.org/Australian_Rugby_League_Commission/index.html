The Australian Rugby League Commission (ARLC) is the governing body for rugby league in Australia. It was formed on 10 February 2012, replacing the Australian Rugby League and taking over control of the National Rugby League (NRL) from the partnership between the ARL and News Limited that had run the competition since the aftermath of the Super League war.

Structure

The Commission consists of independent commissioners appointed under its constitution, with a chairman elected by the members. Its responsibilities include the elite NRL competition, the State of Origin series, the national teams (the Kangaroos and Jillaroos), and the development of the game in partnership with state leagues.

History

The creation of an independent commission was a central recommendation of reviews into the game's governance following the reunification of the sport in 1998. Its establishment in 2012 was accompanied by a record broadcast rights agreement.
