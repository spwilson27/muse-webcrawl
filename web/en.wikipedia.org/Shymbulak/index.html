Shymbulak

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Shymbulak (Kazakh: Шымбұлақ), also known as Chimbulak, is a ski resort near Almaty, Kazakhstan, in the Zailiyskiy Alatau mountain range of the Tian Shan. It is the largest ski resort in Central Asia and a popular destination for skiing and snowboarding from late November to May.

Location and facilities

The resort lies about 25 kilometres south of Almaty at elevations between roughly 2,260 and 3,163 metres above sea level, above the Medeu valley. A gondola lift connects the Medeu skating rink area with the resort base, and further lifts — modernized extensively for the 2011 Asian Winter Games — reach the Talgar Pass at over 3,100 metres. The resort offers pistes for beginners through advanced skiers, night skiing, ski schools, hotels and restaurants.

History

Skiing began at Shymbulak in the Soviet era, when it served as a training base for Soviet skiers. It hosted alpine skiing events of the 2011 Asian Winter Games, held in Astana and Almaty. The resort was part of Almaty's bid for the 2022 Winter Olympics, which narrowly lost to Beijing. In summer, the lifts carry hikers and mountain bikers into the surrounding alpine terrain.
