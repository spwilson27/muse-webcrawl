Corporate branding

Corporate branding refers to the practice of promoting the brand name of a corporate entity, as opposed to specific products or services. The activities and thinking that go into corporate branding are different from product and service branding because the scope of a corporate brand is typically vast.

Concepts

A corporate brand encompasses the company's mission, values, identity and reputation among customers, employees, investors and the public. Brand architecture describes how corporate and product brands relate: monolithic (one master brand, e.g. Virgin), endorsed, or pluralistic/freestanding strategies.

Management

Corporate branding is managed through visual identity, communications, behaviour and culture. Strong corporate brands can command price premiums, ease new product launches and aid recruitment, but a scandal can damage every product sharing the name.
