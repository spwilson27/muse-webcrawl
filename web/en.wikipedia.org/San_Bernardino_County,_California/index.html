San Bernardino County is a county in the southern portion of California. With an area of about 20,105 square miles, it is the largest county in the contiguous United States by area — larger than several U.S. states. Its population was 2,181,654 at the 2020 census, making it the fifth-most populous county in California.

Geography

The county stretches from the Los Angeles metropolitan fringe across the San Bernardino Mountains to the Mojave Desert and the Nevada and Arizona borders. Much of its land is desert, including parts of Death Valley National Park, Joshua Tree National Park and the Mojave National Preserve.

Communities

Major cities include San Bernardino, Fontana, Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Victorville, Rialto and Redlands. Vast unincorporated desert areas are administered directly by the county.

Transportation

Interstates 10, 15, 40 and 215 cross the county, which is a major freight corridor; Ontario International Airport serves its western portion.
