Dole Food Company

Founded1851 (as Castle & Cooke); Hawaiian Pineapple Company 1901
FounderJames Dole
HeadquartersCharlotte, North Carolina, US
ProductsFresh fruit and vegetables, packaged foods

Dole Food Company, Inc. is an American agricultural multinational corporation and one of the world's largest producers and marketers of fresh fruit and vegetables. It is best known for pineapples and bananas and sells its products in more than 90 countries, many under the Dole brand, sometimes written "Dole plc" following its 2021 merger with Total Produce.

History

The company's roots lie in Castle & Cooke, founded in Hawaii in 1851. James Drummond Dole, a Harvard graduate, arrived in Hawaii in 1899 and founded the Hawaiian Pineapple Company in 1901, developing large-scale pineapple cultivation and canning at Wahiawa on Oahu; canned pineapple became the company's signature product. Castle & Cooke acquired control in the 1930s, later renamed itself Dole Food Company, and expanded into bananas in Central America, competing with United Fruit and Standard Fruit.

Operations

Dole sources produce from its own plantations and independent growers in Latin America, the Philippines, and elsewhere, operates a fleet of refrigerated container ships, and markets fresh-cut salads, packaged fruit, and frozen products. In 2021 Dole merged with Total Produce of Ireland to form Dole plc, listed in New York; the fresh vegetables division was subsequently sold to Fresh Express.

Criticism

Like other tropical fruit producers, Dole has faced criticism and litigation over pesticide use (including DBCP exposure cases brought by banana workers), labour conditions, and environmental practices, and has responded with sustainability and certification programmes.
