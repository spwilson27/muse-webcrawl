Alliance française

The Alliance française is an international organisation and network of non-profit cultural institutions that promotes the French language and francophone culture outside France. Founded in Paris in 1883 as the Association nationale pour la propagation de la langue française dans les colonies et à l'étranger, its early supporters included the scientist Louis Pasteur and the writer Jules Verne.

Activities

Local Alliances françaises, which are legally independent associations run by local committees, teach French language courses, administer official French diploma examinations such as the DELF and DALF, and organise cultural events including film screenings, exhibitions, concerts and lectures. The network comprises hundreds of chapters in more than 130 countries, teaching hundreds of thousands of students annually, making it one of the largest cultural networks in the world.

Organisation

The Fondation des Alliances françaises, created in 2007, coordinates the worldwide network from Paris, while the historic Alliance française Paris Île-de-France operates one of the largest French schools for foreign students. The network is a key instrument of French cultural diplomacy, alongside the Institut français.
