Xbox Game Studios (previously Microsoft Games and Microsoft Game Studios) is an American video game publisher and division of Microsoft Gaming responsible for the development and publishing of games for Xbox consoles and Windows. It oversees a family of first-party studios including 343 Industries (later Halo Studios), The Coalition, Turn 10 Studios, Playground Games, Rare, Mojang Studios, Obsidian Entertainment, Ninja Theory, and World's Edge.

History

The division was formed in 2000 from Microsoft's internal Games Group ahead of the original Xbox launch. For years it relied on a relatively small slate of franchises, and several studios were closed in the mid-2010s. From 2018, under Phil Spencer and studio head Matt Booty, Microsoft embarked on a major acquisition spree, buying or founding numerous studios and rebranding the unit as Xbox Game Studios in February 2019.

Role within Microsoft Gaming

Following the acquisitions of ZeniMax Media (2021) and Activision Blizzard (2023), Xbox Game Studios became one of three publishing arms of Microsoft Gaming, alongside Bethesda Softworks and Activision Blizzard's labels. Its games are released day one on the Game Pass subscription service, and many also stream via Xbox Cloud Gaming.
