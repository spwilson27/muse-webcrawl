Ellington Airport (IATA: EFD, ICAO: KEFD), formerly Ellington Field, is a public and military use airport in Houston, Texas, United States. Established in 1917 as a World War I Army Air Service training field, it was named for Lieutenant Eric Ellington, an aviator killed in a training accident.

Uses

The airfield supports general aviation, NASA operations associated with the nearby Johnson Space Center — including astronaut training flights in T-38 jets — and the Texas Air National Guard's 147th Attack Wing, which operates MQ-9 Reaper drones. It is also the site of the annual Wings Over Houston airshow. A portion of the airport has been developed as the Houston Spaceport, licensed for horizontal-launch spaceflight operations.
