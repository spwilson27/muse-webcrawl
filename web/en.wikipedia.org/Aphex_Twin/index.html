Aphex Twin

Birth name: Richard David James
Born: 18 August 1971, Limerick, Ireland
Genres: IDM, ambient, acid techno, drum and bass

Richard David James (born 18 August 1971), best known by the alias Aphex Twin, is a British musician, composer and DJ. Raised in Cornwall, he became a central figure in electronic music in the 1990s, pioneering styles associated with IDM and ambient techno through work on labels including Warp Records and his own Rephlex Records.

Career

His debut album, Selected Ambient Works 85–92 (1992), is considered a landmark of ambient techno, while Selected Ambient Works Volume II (1994) explored darker, minimal soundscapes. The Richard D. James Album (1996) combined lush strings with frenetic drum programming. Singles such as "Windowlicker" (1999) and "Come to Daddy" (1997), accompanied by Chris Cunningham's videos, brought him mainstream attention.

Later work

After the album Drukqs (2001), James released little under the Aphex Twin name until Syro (2014), which won the Grammy Award for Best Dance/Electronic Album. He has since issued several EPs and performed at major festivals, and remains one of the most influential electronic musicians of his generation.
