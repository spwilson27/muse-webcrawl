Kajol Devgan (née Mukherjee; born 5 August 1974), known mononymously as Kajol, is an Indian actress who works in Hindi films. One of the most successful and highest-paid actresses of the 1990s, she has received numerous accolades, including six Filmfare Awards, tying the record for most Best Actress wins, and the Padma Shri in 2011. She is the daughter of actress Tanuja and filmmaker Shomu Mukherjee, and part of the Mukherjee–Samarth film family.

Career

Breakthrough and stardom (1992–1999)

Kajol debuted at 17 in Bekhudi (1992) and achieved success with the thriller Baazigar (1993) opposite Shah Rukh Khan. Their pairing became one of Bollywood's most beloved: Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995), among the biggest hits in Indian cinema history, Kuch Kuch Hota Hai (1998), and Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham... (2001) all won her Filmfare Best Actress awards. She also showed range in negative and dramatic roles, notably in Gupt (1997) — for which she became the first woman to win the Filmfare Award for Best Performance in a Negative Role — and Dushman (1998).

Later work

After marrying actor Ajay Devgn in 1999, Kajol worked selectively. She returned to acclaim with Fanaa (2006) and My Name Is Khan (2010), both Filmfare-winning performances. Later roles included Dilwale (2015), the Tamil film Velaiilla Pattadhari 2 (2017), Tanhaji (2020), and the Netflix drama Tribhanga (2021), followed by the legal drama series The Trial (2023).

Public image and philanthropy

Kajol is noted for her outspoken personality and for prioritising family alongside selective film work. She is involved with charities concerning children and education, including Pratham, and has served as a goodwill ambassador. With her husband she has campaigned for causes such as the education of the girl child.
