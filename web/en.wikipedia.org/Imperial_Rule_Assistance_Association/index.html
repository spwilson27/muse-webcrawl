The Imperial Rule Assistance Association (Japanese: 大政翼賛会, Taisei Yokusankai) was the para-fascist organisation of the Empire of Japan, created by Prime Minister Fumimaro Konoe on 12 October 1940, to promote the goals of his Shintaisei ("New Order") movement. It evolved into a statist ruling organisation that absorbed Japan's political parties during the Second World War.

History

In 1940 Japan's existing political parties dissolved themselves into the Association as part of the New Order movement. The IRAA mobilised the population for the war effort through neighbourhood associations (tonarigumi) and sponsored candidates in the 1942 general election.

Dissolution

As Japan's war situation deteriorated, the Association was reorganised and finally dissolved on 13 June 1945, shortly before the end of the war.
