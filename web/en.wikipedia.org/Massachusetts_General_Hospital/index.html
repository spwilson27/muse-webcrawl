Massachusetts General Hospital

AffiliationHarvard Medical School

Massachusetts General Hospital (Mass General) is a teaching hospital of Harvard Medical School located in the West End neighbourhood of Boston, Massachusetts. Founded in 1811, it is the third-oldest general hospital in the United States and one of the largest and most highly regarded medical centres in the world.

History

The hospital admitted its first patient in 1821. It is famous for the 1846 Ether Dome demonstration, the first public use of inhaled ether as a surgical anaesthetic.

Research and care

Mass General is a major research institution and trauma centre, consistently ranked among the top hospitals in the United States across numerous specialties.
