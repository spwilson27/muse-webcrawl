Johann Rupert

[Image placeholder: portrait of Johann Rupert]

Johann Peter Rupert (born 1 June 1950) is a South African billionaire businessman, the eldest son of business tycoon Anton Rupert. He is the chairman of the Swiss-based luxury-goods company Richemont and of the South African companies Remgro and Reinet Investments. Since 2023 he has been ranked among the richest people in Africa, and for a period was listed by Forbes as the wealthiest person on the continent.

Rupert was born in Stellenbosch and studied economics and company law at Stellenbosch University before leaving to pursue business. He worked in banking in New York before returning to South Africa, where he founded Rand Merchant Bank and later took over the family empire built by his father around Rembrandt and Rothmans.

Richemont

In 1988 Rupert founded Compagnie Financière Richemont AG by spinning off the international assets of the Rembrandt group. Richemont grew into one of the world's largest luxury groups, owning brands including Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Montblanc, Vacheron Constantin, Jaeger-LeCoultre, IWC, Alfred Dunhill, and Chloé. Rupert has served as the company's executive chairman and controlling shareholder.

Other interests

Rupert is a keen sportsman and conservationist: he played first-class cricket and golf, helped establish the Laureus Sport for Good Foundation, and has been involved in wildlife conservation in South Africa. He has also been a frequent commentator on South African politics and economics.
