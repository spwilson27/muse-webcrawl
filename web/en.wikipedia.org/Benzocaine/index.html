Benzocaine

Benzocaine
Chemical nameEthyl 4-aminobenzoate
FormulaC9H11NO2
UseTopical local anaesthetic

Benzocaine, sold under brand names such as Orajel, is an ester local anaesthetic commonly used as a topical pain reliever and in cough drops. It is the ethyl ester of p-aminobenzoic acid (PABA) and works by blocking voltage-gated sodium channels in neuronal membranes, preventing the transmission of pain signals.

Uses

Benzocaine is used to relieve pain from mouth ulcers, sore throat, teething, and minor skin irritation, and to numb the skin or mucosal surfaces before minor procedures such as endoscopy. It is an ingredient in many over-the-counter lozenges, gels, sprays, and ear drops, sometimes combined with other agents.

Safety

Excessive use can cause methaemoglobinaemia, a potentially serious condition in which haemoglobin is oxidised and carries less oxygen; regulators have warned against its use in children under two years of age. Allergic reactions, including contact dermatitis, also occur.
