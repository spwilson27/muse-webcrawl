Falmer Stadium, known as the American Express Community Stadium for sponsorship reasons and commonly as the Amex, is a football stadium in Falmer, near Brighton, Sussex, England. It is the home ground of Brighton & Hove Albion F.C.

History

After the club's Goldstone Ground was sold in 1997, Brighton played at Gillingham and then at the Withdean Stadium athletics venue while campaigning for a new ground. Planning permission for Falmer was granted after a lengthy public inquiry; construction began in 2008 and the stadium opened in July 2011 at a cost of about £93 million.

Design

The stadium was designed by KSS Design Group and is set into the landscape of the South Downs. It has hosted Premier League football since Brighton's promotion in 2017, Rugby World Cup matches in 2015, and UEFA Women's Euro 2022 fixtures.
