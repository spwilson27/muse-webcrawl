The Court of the Lord Lyon is a standing court of law in Scotland, based in Edinburgh, which regulates heraldry. It is presided over by the Lord Lyon King of Arms, who is both a minister of the Crown and a judge. Unlike in England, where heraldic authority rests with the College of Arms, in Scotland the bearing of arms without authority is a legal offence enforceable by the Lyon Court.

Functions

The court grants and records coats of arms in the Public Register of All Arms and Bearings in Scotland, established by Act of Parliament in 1672 and maintained ever since; it rules on rights to arms, pedigrees, and the chiefship of clans, and it prosecutes the unauthorised use of arms. The Lord Lyon also advises on flags and is responsible for state ceremonial in Scotland, including proclaiming the sovereign in Edinburgh.
