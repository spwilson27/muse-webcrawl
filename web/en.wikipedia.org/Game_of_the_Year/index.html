Game of the Year

FieldVideo games
Major awardThe Game Awards (since 2014)
Other bodiesD.I.C.E. Awards; Golden Joystick Awards; BAFTA Games Awards; numerous publications

Game of the Year (GotY) is a title awarded by various video game publications and award shows to the video game they consider the best released in a calendar year. Awards are given by media outlets, industry ceremonies, and player votes, and winning titles are frequently re-released in "Game of the Year editions" bundling downloadable content and expansions.

Major awards

The most prominent Game of the Year honor is presented at The Game Awards, hosted by Geoff Keighley since 2014; recent winners have included The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, God of War, Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, The Last of Us Part II, It Takes Two, Elden Ring, and Baldur's Gate 3. Other long-running awards include the D.I.C.E. Awards' Game of the Year, the BAFTA Best Game award, and the Golden Joystick Awards' Ultimate Game of the Year, which is decided by public vote.

Aggregation

Because dozens of outlets name their own winners, aggregator tallies count total awards per title to identify each year's most decorated game. The phrase "Game of the Year edition" also became a common marketing label for complete-edition re-releases, whether or not the game in question literally won the award.
