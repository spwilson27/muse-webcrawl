Cordoba

Cordoba (Spanish: Córdoba) is a name shared by several cities and places in the Spanish-speaking world, most of them named ultimately for Córdoba, Spain, the historic capital of Islamic al-Andalus.

Principal places

Córdoba, Spain is a city in Andalusia on the Guadalquivir River, famous for the Great Mosque–Cathedral (Mezquita) begun in 785. Under the Umayyad caliphate it became one of the largest and most advanced cities of the medieval world.

Córdoba, Argentina is the capital of Córdoba Province and Argentina's second-largest city, founded in 1573, home to one of the oldest universities in the Americas (1613) and a major industrial and student centre.

Córdoba, Veracruz is a city in Mexico founded in 1618, where the Treaties of Córdoba (1821) recognised Mexican independence. Córdoba is also a province of Colombia and a department name elsewhere in Latin America.

Other uses

The name also denotes the córdoba, the currency of Nicaragua, and appears in surnames and titles derived from the Spanish city.
