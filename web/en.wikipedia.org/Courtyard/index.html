Courtyard

A courtyard or court is a circumscribed area, often surrounded by a building or complex, that is open to the sky. Courtyards are common elements in both Western and Eastern building patterns and have been used by ancient and contemporary architects as a typical and traditional building feature.

Uses

Courtyards provide light, ventilation, privacy and outdoor living space. Examples include the Roman atrium, Islamic riad gardens, Chinese siheyuan, Spanish patios and cloisters in monasteries.
