The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is an American fantasy television series developed by J. D. Payne and Patrick McKay for Amazon Prime Video. Based on J. R. R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and its appendices, it is set in the Second Age of Middle-earth, thousands of years before the events of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings.

Premise

The series follows an ensemble cast including Galadriel, Elrond, and the ancestors of the hobbits, the Harfoots, as the peoples of Middle-earth confront the re-emergence of evil and the forging of the Rings of Power by the Dark Lord Sauron.

Production

Amazon bought the television rights for $250 million in 2017 and committed to a multi-season production, making it one of the most expensive television series ever produced. The first season was filmed in New Zealand; production later moved to the United Kingdom.
