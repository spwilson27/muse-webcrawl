Falkirk Wheel

LocationFalkirk, Scotland
Opened24 May 2002
Lift height35 m (115 ft)
Cost£17.5 million

The Falkirk Wheel (Scottish Gaelic: Cuibhle na h-Eaglaise Bige) is a rotating boat lift in Tamfourhill, Falkirk, in central Scotland, connecting the Forth and Clyde Canal with the Union Canal. It reconnected the two canals for the first time since the 1930s.

Design and operation

The wheel raises boats 24 metres (79 ft), after which a pair of locks raises them a further 11 metres, for a total lift of 35 metres. Two diametrically opposed gondolas, each carrying up to 250,000 litres of water and up to four boats, rotate 180 degrees while a system of gears keeps the gondolas level. A full rotation takes about five minutes and uses only about 22 kW of power.

History

The wheel was built as part of the Millennium Link project to restore navigation between Glasgow and Edinburgh. It was opened by Queen Elizabeth II on 24 May 2002 and has become a major tourist attraction and a landmark of modern Scottish engineering.
