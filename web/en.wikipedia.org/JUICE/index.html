JUICE

JUICE (Jupiter Icy Moons Explorer) is an interplanetary spacecraft developed by the European Space Agency (ESA) to study Jupiter and its large icy moons Ganymede, Callisto and Europa. It was launched on 14 April 2023 and is expected to arrive at Jupiter in July 2031.

OperatorEuropean Space Agency
Launch14 April 2023, Kourou
ArrivalJuly 2031 (planned)
TargetsGanymede, Callisto, Europa

Mission

After a cruise involving gravity assists at Earth, Venus and the Moon, JUICE will enter orbit around Jupiter and perform flybys of Europa and Callisto before entering orbit around Ganymede in 2034 — the first spacecraft to orbit a moon other than Earth's. Its studies focus on the subsurface oceans thought to lie beneath the icy crusts of the moons and on conditions that might support life.

Instruments

The spacecraft carries ten science instruments, including the JANUS camera, the MAJIS imaging spectrometer, the GALA laser altimeter and the RIME ice-penetrating radar, along with a radio science experiment using its communications system.
