Swarthmore College is a private liberal arts college in Swarthmore, Pennsylvania, United States. Founded in 1864 by members of the Religious Society of Friends (Quakers), it was one of the earliest coeducational colleges in the United States.

Academics

The college is known for its rigorous undergraduate programme and its Honours Program, modelled on the Oxford tutorial system, in which students take small seminars and sit externally examined assessments. It offers the bachelor of arts and bachelor of science, including an accredited engineering degree unusual among liberal arts colleges.

Campus

The 425-acre campus is an arboretum, the Scott Arboretum, with Parrish Hall as its centrepiece. Swarthmore is a member of the Tri-College Consortium with Bryn Mawr and Haverford Colleges.
