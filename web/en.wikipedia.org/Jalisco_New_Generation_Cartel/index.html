Jalisco New Generation Cartel

The Jalisco New Generation Cartel (Spanish: Cártel de Jalisco Nueva Generación, CJNG) is a Mexican criminal organisation based in Jalisco, led for many years by Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes, known as "El Mencho." It emerged around 2009–2010 from a split in the Milenio Cartel and fragments of the Sinaloa Cartel's networks after the death of Ignacio "Nacho" Coronel.

Activities

The CJNG is engaged in trafficking cocaine, methamphetamine and fentanyl to the United States and other markets, as well as extortion, fuel theft, kidnapping and money laundering. It expanded aggressively across Mexico, fighting the Sinaloa Cartel and local groups, and became notorious for high-profile violence, including the 2015 ambush of police in Jalisco and the downing of a military helicopter with a rocket-propelled grenade.

Designations and enforcement

The United States has offered a multimillion-dollar reward for El Mencho, one of the DEA's most wanted fugitives. His son, Rubén Oseguera González ("El Menchito"), was convicted in the United States in 2024, and his son-in-law was arrested in California the same year. In February 2025, the US State Department designated the CJNG as a Foreign Terrorist Organization.

The cartel's rapid rise made it, alongside the Sinaloa Cartel, one of the two dominant organisations in Mexico's criminal landscape, and a central actor in the fentanyl supply chain targeted by US-Mexico security cooperation.
