New World Order (professional wrestling)

The New World Order (commonly abbreviated nWo) was a professional wrestling stable that originally consisted of "Hollywood" Hulk Hogan, Scott Hall, and Kevin Nash. The group became one of the most influential and popular factions in wrestling history and a driving force of the Monday Night Wars between World Championship Wrestling (WCW) and the World Wrestling Federation (WWF).

Formation

In 1996, Hall and Nash, recently arrived from the WWF, appeared in WCW as "The Outsiders," implying an invasion. At Bash at the Beach on 7 July 1996, Hulk Hogan shocked fans by turning villain and joining them, announcing the "New World Order of professional wrestling." The storyline blurred reality and fiction and propelled WCW ahead in the ratings war.

Expansion and split

The nWo recruited many of WCW's stars and became a franchise in itself, with merchandise sales booming. In 1998 the group famously split into rival factions: nWo Hollywood (black-and-white, led by Hogan) and the nWo Wolfpac (red-and-black, led by Nash), before reuniting in 1999 in a storyline widely seen as declining in quality.

Later incarnations and legacy

After WCW's closure in 2001, the nWo was revived briefly in the WWF in 2002 with Hogan, Hall and Nash. The group was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2020. The nWo's black-and-white logo and "4 Life" catchphrases remain iconic in popular culture.
