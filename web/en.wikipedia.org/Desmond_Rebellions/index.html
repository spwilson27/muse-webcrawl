Desmond Rebellions

The Desmond Rebellions occurred in 1569–1573 and 1579–1583 in Munster in south-west Ireland. They were led by the Fitzgerald Earl of Desmond and his followers, the Geraldines, against the rule of Queen Elizabeth I.

Course

The first rebellion was led by James FitzMaurice FitzGerald in protest at English colonisation and centralisation. The second, bloodier rebellion drew in papal and Spanish troops, who were massacred at Smerwick (1580).

Aftermath

The Earl was killed in 1583. Devastation and famine killed a large share of Munster's population, and the confiscation of Desmond lands enabled the Munster Plantation by English settlers.
