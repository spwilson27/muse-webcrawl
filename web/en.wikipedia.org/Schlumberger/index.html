SLB, formerly Schlumberger Limited, is a global technology company serving the energy industry and the world's largest oilfield services provider. Headquartered principally in Houston, Texas, it operates in more than 100 countries and supplies technology for reservoir characterization, drilling, production and processing.

History

The company traces its origins to 1926, when brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger founded the Société de Prospection Électrique in Paris to commercialise electrical resistivity measurements of the subsurface, a technique Conrad had conceived in 1912. In 1927 their team performed the first electrical well log at Pechelbronn, France. The enterprise expanded internationally, notably to the United States, and grew through acquisitions including Fairchild Camera and Instrument's semiconductor operations, which for a time made it a major force in automatic test equipment.

Business

SLB's activities span wireline logging, drilling services, completions, production systems, subsea equipment and digital solutions for the energy sector. In 2022 the company adopted the name SLB, reflecting an expanding focus on decarbonisation, carbon capture and new energy systems alongside its traditional oil and gas services.
