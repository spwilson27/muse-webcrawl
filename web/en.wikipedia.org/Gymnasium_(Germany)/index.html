Gymnasium (Germany)

Gymnasium (plural: Gymnasien) is a type of secondary school in Germany and other European countries, providing advanced academic education and preparing students for university study. Students graduate with the Abitur qualification.

Structure

Children typically enter a Gymnasium after primary school, around age 10–12, selected by academic ability. The course lasts eight or nine years (G8 or G9), culminating in the Abitur examinations, which entitle holders to study at any German university.

Curriculum

The curriculum emphasizes languages (including Latin or Greek in humanistic Gymnasien), mathematics, sciences, history and the arts. Gymnasien form the top tier of Germany's tracked, tripartite secondary school system alongside Realschule and Hauptschule.
