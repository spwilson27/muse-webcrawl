Timeline of the Franklin D. Roosevelt presidency

This timeline of the Franklin D. Roosevelt presidency covers the administration of Franklin D. Roosevelt, the 32nd president of the United States, from his first inauguration on 4 March 1933 until his death on 12 April 1945 — the longest presidency in American history, spanning the Great Depression and most of World War II.

New Deal era (1933–1939)

The first hundred days of 1933 saw a flurry of legislation under the New Deal: bank reform, relief programmes, the Civilian Conservation Corps and the Tennessee Valley Authority. Later measures included the Social Security Act (1935) and the Fair Labor Standards Act (1938). Roosevelt was re-elected in landslides in 1936 and, breaking precedent, in 1940.

World War II (1939–1945)

After the attack on Pearl Harbor in December 1941 the United States entered World War II. Roosevelt directed the war effort with Winston Churchill and Joseph Stalin, approved the Manhattan Project, and was re-elected to a fourth term in 1944, dying in office at Warm Springs, Georgia, in April 1945, weeks before the end of the war in Europe.
