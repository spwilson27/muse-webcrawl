Mosaic Stadium is an open-air Canadian football stadium in Regina, Saskatchewan, Canada. It is the home of the Saskatchewan Roughriders of the Canadian Football League (CFL). The stadium opened in 2017, replacing the historic Mosaic Stadium at Taylor Field, which stood nearby and had hosted the Roughriders since 1936.

History

A new stadium was announced in 2012 as the centrepiece of the Regina Revitalization Initiative, on the Evraz Place exhibition grounds west of downtown. Construction began in 2014, and the stadium was built largely "roof-ready" and sunken, with a continuous bowl, a translucent spectator roof canopy around the rim, and a general-admission lounge at one end. A test event was held in October 2016, and the Roughriders played their first regular-season game there on 1 July 2017.

Events

Mosaic Stadium hosted the 109th Grey Cup in November 2022, the CFL championship game originally scheduled for 2020 but delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic. It has also hosted outdoor hockey — including the 2019 NHL Heritage Classic — concerts, and international soccer exhibitions.

Design

The stadium seats 33,350 with the possibility of temporary expansion to about 40,000 for marquee events. Its design emphasises wide concourses, a variety of premium spaces, and protection from Saskatchewan's prairie winds through its orientation and lowered playing field.
