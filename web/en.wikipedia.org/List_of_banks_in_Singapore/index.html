List of banks in Singapore

Singapore is a major international financial centre regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). Banking licences include full banks, wholesale banks, merchant banks and finance companies.

Local full banks

  • DBS Bank — largest bank in Southeast Asia
  • Oversea-Chinese Banking Corporation (OCBC)
  • United Overseas Bank (UOB)
  • Bank of Singapore (private banking arm of OCBC)

Foreign full banks with Qualifying Full Bank privileges

  • Standard Chartered, HSBC, Citibank, Maybank, State Bank of India, BNP Paribas, ICICI Bank, Bank of China, ANZ, ABN AMRO and others

Digital banks

  • GXS Bank, MariBank, Trust Bank, ANEXT Bank, Green Link Digital Bank

Other institutions

Numerous wholesale and offshore branches operate, including JPMorgan, Bank of America, UBS, Credit Suisse (now UBS), Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, Barclays, Deutsche Bank, MUFG, Mizuho and Sumitomo Mitsui. POSB, founded in 1877, operates as part of DBS.
