Concepción (volcano)

Elevation1,610 m (5,282 ft)
LocationOmetepe, Lake Nicaragua, Nicaragua
TypeStratovolcano

Concepción is one of two volcanoes, together with Maderas, that form the island of Ometepe in Lake Nicaragua. It is an active, symmetrical stratovolcano and one of Nicaragua's most active volcanoes.

Activity

Concepción has erupted frequently in historical times, producing ash plumes and occasional lava flows; notable activity occurred in the 20th and 21st centuries, periodically prompting monitoring and evacuations of nearby communities.

Geography

The cone rises steeply from the lake and is climbed from towns such as Moyogalpa and Altagracia. Alongside Maderas it defines the figure-eight shape of Ometepe, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve.
