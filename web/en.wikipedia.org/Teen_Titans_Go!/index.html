Teen Titans Go!

Teen Titans Go! is an American animated television series developed by Aaron Horvath and Michael Jelenic for Cartoon Network. It premiered on April 23, 2013, and is based on the DC Comics superhero team the Teen Titans. The series is a comedic spin-off of the 2003 Teen Titans series, featuring the characters in chibi-like designs and focusing on humorous, everyday situations rather than serialized action storylines.

Premise

The series follows Robin, Starfire, Cyborg, Beast Boy, and Raven as they live together in Titans Tower in Jump City. Episodes typically show the team's antics when they are not fighting crime, involving food, pranks, video games, and absurd adventures, with frequent self-referential humor about the show itself and its fans.

Voice cast

The principal voice cast from the 2003 series returned, including Scott Menville as Robin, Hynden Walch as Starfire, Khary Payton as Cyborg, Greg Cipes as Beast Boy, and Tara Strong as Raven.

Reception

Teen Titans Go! has been a major ratings success for Cartoon Network and has aired hundreds of episodes, making it one of the network's longest-running series. It has also been controversial among fans of the original series for its radically different, comedy-focused tone, a divide the show itself has repeatedly parodied. A feature film, Teen Titans Go! To the Movies, was released theatrically in 2018 to positive reviews.
