SUSE S.A.


Image placeholder: SUSE S.A.

SUSE S.A. is a German-based multinational open-source software company that develops and sells Linux products to business customers. Founded in 1992 and headquartered in Luxembourg (with its largest office in Nuremberg, Germany), SUSE is the developer of SUSE Linux Enterprise and the major sponsor of the openSUSE community project. The name "S.u.S.E." was originally an acronym for Software- und System-Entwicklung (Software and Systems Development).

History

SUSE was founded in 1992 in Nuremberg by Roland Dyroff, Thomas Fehr, Burchard Steinbild and Hubert Mantel, initially as a service company supporting UNIX and Linux systems and distributing Slackware. It released its own distribution, S.u.S.E. Linux, in 1994. The company was acquired by Novell in 2003 for US$210 million, then passed to The Attachmate Group in 2011, to Micro Focus in 2014, and was acquired by the Swedish private equity firm EQT Partners in 2019. SUSE listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in May 2021; EQT took the company private again in 2023. In 2020 SUSE announced the acquisition of Rancher Labs, expanding into Kubernetes container management.

Products

Principal products include SUSE Linux Enterprise Server and Desktop, SUSE Manager for infrastructure management, Rancher Prime for Kubernetes management, and NeuVector for container security. The company also maintains the openSUSE distribution (Leap and Tumbleweed) as a community project.
