Far Eastern Federal University

Far Eastern Federal University (FEFU; Russian: Дальневосточный федеральный университет) is a university located in Vladivostok, Primorsky Krai, Russia. It was established in its current form in 2010 by merging several Vladivostok higher-education institutions, with origins in the Oriental Institute founded in 1899.

Since 2012 its main campus has been on Russky Island, in facilities built for the APEC summit that year. The university offers programmes in natural sciences, engineering, medicine, humanities, economics, and Asian studies, and is a centre for Russia's educational cooperation with Asia-Pacific countries.
