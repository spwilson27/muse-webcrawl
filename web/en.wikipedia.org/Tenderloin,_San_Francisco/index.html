Tenderloin, San Francisco

The Tenderloin is a neighbourhood in downtown San Francisco, California, in the flatlands on the southern slope of Nob Hill, between the Union Square shopping district to the northeast and Civic Center to the southwest. Encompassing about fifty square blocks, it is historically a working-class neighbourhood of residential hotels, theatres, and restaurants. A portion of the neighbourhood was listed on the National Register of Historic Places as the Uptown Tenderloin Historic District in 2009.

History

The Tenderloin took shape after the 1906 earthquake and fire, when the area was rapidly rebuilt with residential hotels and apartment buildings for workers. Its name is often said to echo New York's Tenderloin district, in turn supposedly named for the bribes police officers could extract there — enough, as one captain put it, to eat tenderloin instead of chuck steak. Through the 20th century the district was known for gambling, speakeasies, jazz clubs, and later for the Blackhawk jazz club, where Miles Davis and others recorded live albums.

The neighbourhood played a role in early LGBT history: the Compton's Cafeteria riot of August 1966, in which transgender women and patrons resisted police harassment at a cafeteria at Turk and Taylor streets, preceded the Stonewall riots by nearly three years and is commemorated in the city's Compton's Transgender Cultural District, designated in 2017 as the world's first legally recognised transgender cultural district.

Character

The Tenderloin contains a large share of San Francisco's single-room-occupancy (SRO) hotels, which house many of the city's lowest-income residents, including families, immigrants, and seniors. The southeast portion of the neighbourhood, sometimes called Little Saigon, is home to a substantial Vietnamese community. At the same time, the district has long struggled with concentrated poverty, open-air drug use, and homelessness, and it became a focus of the city's response to the fentanyl crisis; in December 2021 the mayor declared a state of emergency in the Tenderloin.
