List of airports in Switzerland

This is a list of airports in Switzerland, grouped by type and sorted by location.

International airports

Switzerland's principal airports are Zurich Airport (ZRH), the country's busiest hub; Geneva Airport (GVA); and EuroAirport Basel Mulhouse Freiburg (BSL/MLH), located in France and serving Basel. Bern Airport and Lugano Airport provide smaller scheduled services, while St. Gallen–Altenrhein and Sion serve regional and charter traffic.

Other airfields

Numerous regional airfields serve general aviation, gliding and military use, including Grenchen, Lausanne, Ecuvillens, Birrfeld, and the military air bases at Payerne, Emmen and Meiringen.
