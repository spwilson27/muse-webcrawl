LOCKSS

LOCKSS (Lots of Copies Keep Stuff Safe) is an open-source, library-led digital preservation system built on the principle that multiple copies stored in different places protect content against loss and corruption.

How it works

Libraries run LOCKSS nodes that collect published content with publisher permission, store local copies, and continually poll peer nodes, comparing cryptographic hashes and repairing damaged copies from peers. The system originated at Stanford University Libraries under Vicky Reich and David S. H. Rosenthal.

Networks

The Global LOCKSS Network preserves e-journals and other scholarly content; Private LOCKSS Networks serve communities preserving specialised collections such as government documents and datasets.
