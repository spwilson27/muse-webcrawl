List of time zones by country

This is a list representing time zones by country. Countries are ranked by total number of time zones, including dependencies and overseas territories.

Countries with the most time zones

France, counting its overseas departments and territories scattered across the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans, uses 12 time zones — more than any other country. The United States and Russia follow, each spanning 11 zones in total, though Russia reduced its number from 11 to 9 in 2010 before restoring two zones in 2014. Australia uses eight zones when its external territories are included.

Single-zone large countries

China, despite spanning roughly five geographical time zones, observes a single standard time, UTC+08:00, nationwide. India likewise uses one zone, Indian Standard Time (UTC+05:30), across its whole territory.

Offsets

Standard offsets range from UTC−12:00 to UTC+14:00. Several countries use half-hour offsets, such as India and Iran, and Nepal uses the unusual UTC+05:45. Many countries observe daylight saving time in summer, shifting clocks forward by an hour.
