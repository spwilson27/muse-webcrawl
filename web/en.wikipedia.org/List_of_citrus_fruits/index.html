List of citrus fruits

Citrus is a genus of flowering trees and shrubs in the rue family, Rutaceae, producing fruit notable for their acidity and aromatic rinds. Nearly all common citrus fruits descend by hybridization from three ancestral species: the citron (Citrus medica), the pomelo (Citrus maxima) and the mandarin (Citrus reticulata). This article lists notable citrus fruits, both species and hybrids.

Ancestral species

  • Citron — one of the three original species; includes the Buddha's hand variety.
  • Pomelo — the largest citrus fruit, ancestor of the grapefruit.
  • Mandarin orange — ancestor of many sweet oranges and tangerines.
  • Papeda species — including the kaffir (makrut) lime and ichang papeda.
  • Kumquats and the trifoliate orange are close relatives sometimes included in Citrus.

Common hybrids and cultivars

  • Sweet orange (citron × mandarin/pomelo ancestry), bitter (Seville) orange
  • Lemon (citron × bitter orange), lime (several kinds: Key lime, Persian lime)
  • Grapefruit (pomelo × sweet orange), tangelo, tangor, ugli fruit
  • Clementine, satsuma, yuzu, bergamot orange, calamansi, rangpur
