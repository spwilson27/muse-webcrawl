Blue Moon (spacecraft)

ManufacturerBlue Origin
CountryUnited States
TypeLunar lander

Blue Moon is a family of lunar lander spacecraft under development by Blue Origin. First publicly unveiled in May 2019 by Jeff Bezos, the lander is designed to deliver cargo—and in a crew-rated variant, astronauts—to the surface of the Moon.

Design

The cargo version is designed to land several tonnes of payload on the lunar surface using a BE-7 liquid hydrogen/liquid oxygen engine, developed specifically for precision lunar landings. Blue Moon is intended to launch on Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket.

Artemis program

In 2023 NASA selected Blue Origin's Blue Moon Mark 2 as the second Human Landing System for the Artemis program, planned to carry astronauts to the lunar surface on the Artemis V mission.
