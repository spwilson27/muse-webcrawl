Li Qiang (Chinese: 李强; pinyin: Lǐ Qiáng; born 23 July 1959) is a Chinese politician who has served as the eighth premier of the People's Republic of China since March 2023. He is the second-ranking member of the Politburo Standing Committee of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) and heads the State Council, China's cabinet.

Early life and career

Li was born in Rui'an, Zhejiang province. He joined the CCP in 1983 and studied agricultural mechanization and later sociology and economics. He rose through local government in Zhejiang, serving in Wenzhou and as secretary-general of the provincial party committee while Xi Jinping was Zhejiang party secretary (2002–2007), making him one of Xi's close associates. He later served as governor of Zhejiang (2012–2016), party secretary of Jiangsu (2016–2017), and party secretary of Shanghai (2017–2022), overseeing Shanghai's development as a financial and technology hub and the launch of the STAR Market.

Premiership

At the 20th Party Congress in October 2022, Li was elevated to the Politburo Standing Committee as its second-ranked member, an unprecedented rise without prior service as a vice premier. He was confirmed as premier at the National People's Congress on 11 March 2023, succeeding Li Keqiang. As premier he has emphasized economic recovery, support for private enterprise, and opening to foreign investment, while presiding over challenges including weak consumer demand, a property-sector downturn, and local government debt.
