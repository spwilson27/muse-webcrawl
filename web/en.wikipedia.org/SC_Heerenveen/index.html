SC Heerenveen

Sportclub Heerenveen (Frisian: Sportklub It Hearrenfean), commonly known as sc Heerenveen, is a Dutch professional football club from Heerenveen, Friesland. The club plays in the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football, and was founded on 20 July 1920.

History

Heerenveen rose to prominence in the 1940s under striker Abe Lenstra, one of the greatest Dutch players of his era, after whom the club's stadium is named. The modern club established itself in the Eredivisie from 1993 onward, finishing as runners-up in 1999–2000 and qualifying for the UEFA Champions League the following season.

The club won its first major trophy, the KNVB Cup, in 2009, defeating FC Twente in the final. It also reached the cup final in 1993 and 1997. Heerenveen is known for its youth development and scouting, having developed or showcased players such as Ruud van Nistelrooy, Klaas-Jan Huntelaar, and Afonso Alves.

Identity

Heerenveen strongly identifies with the province of Friesland: the Frisian flag's red pompeblêden (water-lily leaves) appear on the club's blue-and-white striped shirts, and the Frisian anthem is played before home matches at the Abe Lenstra Stadion, which holds around 27,000 spectators. The club's fiercest rivalry is with SC Cambuur of Leeuwarden, contested as the Frisian derby.
