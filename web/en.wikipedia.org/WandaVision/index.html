WandaVision is an American television miniseries created by Jac Schaeffer for the streaming service Disney+, based on Marvel Comics featuring the characters Wanda Maximoff / Scarlet Witch and Vision. It was the first television series in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) produced by Marvel Studios, premiering on January 15, 2021, and concluding on March 5, 2021, with nine episodes.

Premise

Set three weeks after the events of Avengers: Endgame (2019), the series depicts Wanda Maximoff and Vision living an idyllic suburban life in the town of Westview, New Jersey, in a world styled after classic American sitcoms of successive decades. As inconsistencies accumulate, it is revealed that Wanda, overcome with grief, has used her chaos magic to create a false reality — the "Hex" — holding the town's residents captive, while the agency S.W.O.R.D. attempts to intervene.

Production

Elizabeth Olsen and Paul Bettany reprised their film roles. The early episodes were filmed in black and white before a live studio audience to replicate the sitcom format authentically, progressing through the decades of television history. Kathryn Hahn played the neighbor "Agnes," revealed as the witch Agatha Harkness, and performed the viral song "Agatha All Along."

Reception

The series received critical acclaim for its performances, sitcom homages, and exploration of grief. It earned 23 Primetime Emmy Award nominations and won three, including for "Agatha All Along." It leads directly into the film Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022), while the spin-off series Agatha All Along premiered in 2024.
