Bazooka (chewing gum)

Bazooka is an American brand of bubble gum introduced in 1947 by the Topps Company of Brooklyn, New York. The pink gum is famous for its red, white and blue packaging and for the small comic strips wrapped around each piece, featuring the character Bazooka Joe, which began appearing in 1953.

History

Topps launched Bazooka shortly after the Second World War, naming it after the bazooka weapon and the novelty musical instrument popularised by comedian Bob Burns. Sales grew rapidly, and in 1953 the company added the miniature comics starring Bazooka Joe and his gang, along with jokes, fortunes and offers for prizes redeemable with saved comics. The character's eyepatch and the comics' corny humour made them a piece of mid-century Americana, and original comics became collectible items.

Changes and legacy

Over the decades the gum was sold in many countries and flavours, and the packaging was modernised several times. In 2012 Topps announced it would replace the traditional comic format with brain-teaser puzzles, codes and other features, a change reversed in part by later "throwback" packaging that revived the classic comics. The brand has since changed hands as part of sales of Topps's confectionery business.
