Data loss

Data loss is an error condition in information systems in which information is destroyed by failures (like failed spindle motors or head crashes on hard drives) or neglect (like mishandling, or intentional deletion) in storage, transmission, or processing.

Causes

Common causes include hardware failure — the most frequent cause, especially hard disk drive crashes — accidental deletion or overwriting by users, software corruption, computer viruses and ransomware, theft, and natural disasters such as fire and flood. Studies of data loss incidents consistently attribute a large share to human error.

Prevention and recovery

Prevention centres on regular backups, ideally following the 3-2-1 rule (three copies, on two different media, one off-site), together with uninterruptible power supplies, redundancy such as RAID, and access controls. After a loss, data recovery techniques may reconstruct files from damaged media, and deleted files can often be recovered if they have not been overwritten, since deletion usually only removes directory references.
