United Spirits

United Spirits Limited (USL) is an Indian alcoholic beverages company headquartered in Bangalore, Karnataka. A subsidiary of the British multinational Diageo, it is one of the world's largest producers of spirits by volume, with a portfolio concentrated on whisky, alongside rum, brandy, vodka, and gin brands sold principally in India.

History

USL grew out of McDowell's, a trading and distilling business with roots in 19th-century Madras, and became the flagship spirits arm of Vijay Mallya's UB Group. Its brands include McDowell's No.1, Royal Challenge, Antiquity, Signature, Black Dog, Bagpiper, and Director's Special; in 2007 it acquired the Scotch whisky maker Whyte & Mackay. Diageo acquired a controlling stake in 2013–2014, and Mallya resigned as chairman in 2016 amid financial controversies.

Operations

The company operates numerous distilleries and bottling units across India and is listed on Indian stock exchanges. Following its integration with Diageo, USL has focused increasingly on premium brands.
