American Airlines Group Inc. is an American publicly traded airline holding company headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas. It was formed on December 9, 2013, by the merger of AMR Corporation, the parent company of American Airlines, and US Airways Group, the parent company of US Airways.

Operations

The group wholly owns three mainline carriers — American Airlines, Envoy Air, and PSA Airlines — and through American's hub network operates one of the largest airline networks in the world. American Airlines is a founding member of the Oneworld alliance.

History

AMR Corporation filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in November 2011. The merger with US Airways, announced in February 2013 and completed that December, created the world's largest airline at the time and allowed AMR to exit bankruptcy.
