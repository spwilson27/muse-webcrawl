Nephroid

In geometry, a nephroid (from the Greek nephros, "kidney," for its kidney-like shape) is a plane curve: an epicycloid with two cusps, traced by a point on a circle of radius a/2 rolling around a fixed circle of radius a.

Properties

The nephroid has arc length 12a and enclosed area 12πa² relative to its generating parameter. It can also be generated as the caustic by reflection of rays parallel to an axis inside a circle, or as the envelope of circles with centres on a circle and tangent to a diameter line.

Equations

Parametrically, a nephroid with cusps on the x-axis is given by x = a(3 cos t − cos 3t), y = a(3 sin t − sin 3t). It is the catacaustic of a circle for rays at infinity along its axis.
