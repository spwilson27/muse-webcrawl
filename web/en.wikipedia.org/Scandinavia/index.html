Scandinavia

Scandinavia is a subregion of Northern Europe with strong historical, cultural and linguistic ties. In its strictest and most common usage it consists of the three kingdoms of Denmark, Norway and Sweden, whose majority languages — Danish, Norwegian and Swedish — are closely related North Germanic languages descended from Old Norse and largely mutually intelligible. In broader English usage "Scandinavia" is sometimes used synonymously with the Nordic countries, which additionally include Finland, Iceland and their associated territories (Greenland, the Faroe Islands and Åland), though the Nordic countries themselves distinguish the two terms.

Geography

Norway and Sweden occupy the Scandinavian Peninsula, the largest peninsula in Europe, bordered by the Barents Sea, the Norwegian Sea and the North Sea, and by the Baltic Sea to the east. Denmark consists of the Jutland peninsula and a large archipelago to the south. The Scandinavian Mountains run down the peninsula's spine, and the region's coasts are marked by Norway's famous fjords, deeply cut by Ice Age glaciers.

History

The region's peoples enter wider history with the Viking Age (c. 793–1066), when Norse seafarers traded, raided and settled from North America to the Caspian Sea. Denmark, Norway and Sweden were united under a single monarch in the Kalmar Union (1397–1523), after which Sweden emerged as a great power while Denmark–Norway formed a rival composite state. In modern times the Scandinavian countries developed the "Nordic model" of extensive welfare states, high-trust societies and some of the world's highest measured standards of living, cooperating through the Nordic Council (founded 1952) while taking different paths on European integration: Denmark and Sweden joined the European Union, Norway did not.

Culture

Scandinavia is associated with Lutheran heritage, egalitarian social norms, minimalist design and architecture, and a strong literary tradition from the Icelandic sagas to Ibsen, Strindberg and contemporary Nordic noir fiction and television.
