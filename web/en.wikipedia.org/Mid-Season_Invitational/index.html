Mid-Season Invitational

The Mid-Season Invitational (MSI) is an annual international League of Legends tournament hosted by Riot Games in the middle of the competitive season, typically in May. It is the second most important international League of Legends tournament after the World Championship, bringing together the champions (and, in recent formats, runners-up) of the game's regional leagues.

The first MSI was held in 2015 in Tallahassee, Florida, and won by China's EDward Gaming. Subsequent editions have been hosted in cities including Shanghai, Berlin, São Paulo, Paris, Reykjavík, Busan, London, Chengdu and Vancouver. Successful teams in the tournament's history include SK Telecom T1 (T1), Royal Never Give Up — the first three-time champion — and G2 Esports.

Format

The format has evolved repeatedly: recent editions use a play-in stage followed by a bracket stage with double-elimination best-of-five series, and include two representatives from the strongest regions. Results at MSI have also been used to influence seeding and additional slots for the World Championship, linking mid-season performance to the end-of-year tournament.
