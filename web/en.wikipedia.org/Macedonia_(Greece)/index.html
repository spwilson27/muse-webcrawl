Macedonia (Greece)

Macedonia
Country: Greece
Capital: Thessaloniki
Area: 34,177 km²
Population: approx. 2.4 million

Macedonia is a geographic and former administrative region of Greece, in the southern Balkans. It is the largest and second-most-populous Greek geographic region, with its capital at Thessaloniki. Administratively it is divided among the peripheries of Western Macedonia, Central Macedonia, and part of Eastern Macedonia and Thrace.

History

The region corresponds roughly to the ancient kingdom of Macedon, homeland of Philip II and Alexander the Great. It became part of the modern Greek state after the Balkan Wars of 1912–13.

Geography

Macedonia is bounded by Mount Olympus to the south and includes the Chalkidiki peninsula and Mount Athos to the east.
