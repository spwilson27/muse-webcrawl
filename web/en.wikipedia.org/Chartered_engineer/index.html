Chartered engineer

A Chartered Engineer (CEng) is a professional engineer registered with the Engineering Council in the United Kingdom. The title is protected and demonstrates competence, commitment to professional standards, and continuing professional development.

Registration typically requires an accredited master's-level qualification (MEng or MSc) or equivalent knowledge, several years of responsible engineering experience, and a professional review interview assessed against the UK Standard for Professional Engineering Competence (UK-SPEC).

Chartered Engineers develop solutions to engineering problems using new or existing technologies, hold technical and commercial leadership, and accept responsibility for complex systems. Related titles include Incorporated Engineer (IEng) and Engineering Technician (EngTech). Equivalent designations exist in other Commonwealth countries, such as Chartered Professional Engineer (CPEng) in Australia and New Zealand.
