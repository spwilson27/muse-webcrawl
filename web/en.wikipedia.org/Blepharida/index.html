Blepharida

Blepharida
Family: Chrysomelidae
Tribe: Alticini (flea beetles)
Host plants: Anacardiaceae

Blepharida is a genus of flea beetles in the leaf beetle family Chrysomelidae. Species are found in the Americas and Africa and feed on plants in the cashew family, such as sumac and Bursera.

Ecology

Larvae of Blepharida sequester toxic compounds from their host plants and excrete them in a faecal shield carried on the back as a defence against predators. Adults have enlarged hind femora that let them jump like fleas when disturbed.

Research

The genus is studied as a model of plant–insect coevolution, particularly the association between Blepharida rhois and its sumac hosts.
