Cruise (autonomous vehicle)

Cruise LLC
Founded: 2013, San Francisco
Parent: General Motors
Industry: Self-driving vehicles

Cruise LLC is an American self-driving car company headquartered in San Francisco, majority-owned by General Motors. Founded in 2013 by Kyle Vogt and Dan Kan, it was acquired by GM in 2016 and developed autonomous vehicles based on the Chevrolet Bolt EV, later unveiling the purpose-built Cruise Origin shuttle.

Operations

Cruise began public robotaxi services in San Francisco in 2022, expanding to other US cities. Following an October 2023 incident in which a pedestrian was dragged by a Cruise vehicle after being struck by another car, regulators suspended its permits; Cruise paused operations, underwent leadership changes and restructuring, and GM refocused the programme toward personal autonomous vehicles, ending the robotaxi effort in late 2024.

Technology

Cruise vehicles use lidar, radar and cameras with machine-learning perception and prediction software to operate without a safety driver, supervised by remote assistance staff.
