The E5 European long distance path is one of the European long-distance paths designated by the European Ramblers Association. It runs for roughly 3,200 kilometres from Pointe du Raz on the Brittany coast of France, across Switzerland, Germany and Austria, over the main chain of the Alps, and down into Italy, ending at Verona (with a variant continuing to Venice).

Route

From Brittany the path crosses northern France before reaching Lake Constance. It then traverses the Allgäu and the Austrian Lechtal Alps, crossing the main Alpine ridge via high passes such as the Pitztal and Ötztal valleys, before descending through South Tyrol past Merano and Bolzano, over the Mendel Pass, and across the Venetian plain toward Verona.

Walking

The Alpine section between Oberstdorf in Germany and Bolzano in Italy is the most popular part of the E5 and is usually walked in about ten to twelve days, using mountain huts of the German and Austrian Alpine Clubs. The full route takes several months. Waymarking varies by country but the path generally follows established national and regional trails.
