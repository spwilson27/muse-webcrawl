The Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia is a British Overseas Territory on the island of Cyprus. The areas, which include British military bases and installations formerly part of the Crown colony of Cyprus, were retained by the United Kingdom under the 1960 Treaty of Establishment that granted independence to Cyprus.

Geography

Akrotiri forms the Western Sovereign Base Area on a peninsula near Limassol, housing RAF Akrotiri, a major airbase. Dhekelia forms the Eastern Sovereign Base Area near Larnaca and Famagusta, surrounded by several enclaved Cypriot villages. Together the areas total about 254 square kilometres, roughly 3% of the island's land area.

Governance

The territory is administered by an Administrator, who is the commander of British Forces Cyprus, reporting to the UK Ministry of Defence rather than the Foreign Office. It has its own legal system and police force, the Sovereign Base Areas Police. Although administered by the UK, the areas use the euro, following Cyprus's adoption of the currency in 2008.
