Bangalore

Bangalore (Bengaluru)
Country: India
State: Karnataka
Elevation: 920 m
Population: over 8 million (city, 2011); metro approx. 14 million
Nickname: Silicon Valley of India

Bangalore (officially Bengaluru) is the capital and largest city of the southern Indian state of Karnataka. Located on the Deccan Plateau at an elevation of about 900 metres, it has a population of more than eight million, making it India's third-most populous city. It is widely regarded as the centre of India's high-tech industry and is known as the "Silicon Valley of India".

History

The modern city was founded in 1537 by Kempe Gowda I, a feudatory of the Vijayanagara Empire, who built a mud fort there. Bangalore later came under the rule of the Marathas, the Mughals, and the Kingdom of Mysore under Haider Ali and Tipu Sultan, before passing to the British, who made it a major cantonment. After Indian independence in 1947 it became the capital of Mysore State (later Karnataka). Its name was officially changed from Bangalore to Bengaluru in 2014.

Economy and climate

Headquarters of major technology firms such as Infosys and Wipro, along with public-sector aerospace and research institutions (HAL, ISRO, IISc), anchor its economy. Its elevation gives it a moderate, pleasant climate year-round, and the city is noted for its parks, including Cubbon Park and Lalbagh Botanical Garden.
