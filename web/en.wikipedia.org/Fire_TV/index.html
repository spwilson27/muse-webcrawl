Fire TV

Amazon Fire TV
Developer: Amazon
Type: Digital media player / smart TV platform
First released: 2014
Operating system: Fire OS

Fire TV is a line of digital media players and smart TV software developed by Amazon, first released in 2014. Devices such as the Fire TV Stick and Fire TV Cube stream video, music and games from services including Prime Video, Netflix and YouTube over the internet to a television.

Hardware

The range began with a set-top box and expanded to compact HDMI sticks, the hands-free Cube with built-in Alexa voice control, and televisions with Fire TV built in, produced with partners such as Toshiba and Amazon's own Omni series.

Software

Fire TV runs Fire OS, a fork of Android, with an interface organised around content recommendations and Alexa voice search. It supports 4K HDR streaming, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos on higher-end models.
