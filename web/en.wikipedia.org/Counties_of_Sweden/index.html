Counties of Sweden

The counties of Sweden (Swedish: län) are the top-level geographic subdivisions of Sweden. Sweden is today divided into 21 counties.

Administration

Each county has a County Administrative Board (länsstyrelse) headed by a governor appointed by the government, and a separate Region (formerly county council) elected to run health care and public transport among other services. Stockholm is the largest county by population; Gotland is both a county and a municipality.

History

Counties were established in 1634 under Axel Oxenstierna, replacing provinces (landskap), which remain cultural and historical divisions.
