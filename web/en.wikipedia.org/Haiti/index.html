Haiti

Republic of Haiti
Capital: Port-au-Prince
Official languages: Haitian Creole, French
Population: ~11.8 million
Currency: Gourde (HTG)

Haiti, officially the Republic of Haiti, is a country on the island of Hispaniola in the Caribbean Sea, east of Cuba and Jamaica, and south of The Bahamas. It occupies the western three-eighths of the island, which it shares with the Dominican Republic. Its capital is Port-au-Prince.

History

Hispaniola was inhabited by the Taíno people before Christopher Columbus landed in 1492. Spain ruled the island until ceding its western third to France in 1697; French Saint-Domingue became the richest sugar- and coffee-producing colony in the world, worked by hundreds of thousands of enslaved Africans. In 1791 the enslaved rose in the Haitian Revolution, led successively by Toussaint Louverture, André Rigaud, and Jean-Jacques Dessalines. On 1 January 1804 Dessalines proclaimed independence, making Haiti the first independent state in Latin America and the Caribbean, the second republic in the Americas, and the only country founded by a successful slave revolt.

France, with warships in Haitian waters, extorted a huge indemnity in 1825 in exchange for recognition — a debt Haiti paid until 1947. The 19th and 20th centuries saw chronic political instability, a United States occupation (1915–1934), and the dictatorships of François "Papa Doc" Duvalier (1957–1971) and his son Jean-Claude (1971–1986). Jean-Bertrand Aristide became the first democratically elected president in 1990 but was twice removed. A catastrophic earthquake in January 2010 killed well over 150,000 people and devastated Port-au-Prince; subsequent years have been marked by cholera, hurricanes, the 2021 assassination of President Jovenel Moïse, and severe gang violence and humanitarian crisis.

Geography

Haiti is predominantly mountainous — its name derives from the Taíno Ayiti, "land of high mountains". The highest peak is Pic la Selle (2,680 m). The country includes the islands of Île de la Gonâve and Tortuga. The climate is tropical, with a hurricane season from June to November.

Demographics and culture

Haiti's population of around 11.8 million is overwhelmingly of African descent. Haitian Creole, a French-based creole, is spoken by virtually all Haitians and is co-official with French. Vodou, officially recognised as a religion in 2003, is practised alongside Roman Catholicism and Protestantism. Haitian art, music (such as compas), and literature are internationally celebrated.

Economy

Haiti is the poorest country in the Western Hemisphere, with agriculture, remittances, and light manufacturing (notably apparel) central to its economy; deforestation and soil erosion are severe environmental problems.
