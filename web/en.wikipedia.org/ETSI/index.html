Full name: European Telecommunications Standards Institute
Founded: 1988
Headquarters: Sophia Antipolis, France
Type: Standards organization

The European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI) is an independent, non-profit standardization organization in the field of information and communications technology. It is officially recognized by the European Union as a European Standards Organization, and its standards — including the GSM mobile system — have been adopted worldwide.

ETSI headquarters in Sophia Antipolis, southern France.

Work

ETSI develops standards for telecommunications, broadcasting and related ICT fields. Its best-known achievement is GSM, developed in the late 1980s and early 1990s, which became the world's dominant second-generation mobile standard. ETSI also created the DECT cordless telephone standard and the TETRA radio system for public safety, and it is a founding partner of the 3GPP project that develops specifications for 3G, 4G LTE and 5G mobile networks.

Membership and structure

ETSI has more than 900 member organizations from over 60 countries, including manufacturers, network operators, administrations, research bodies and users. Unlike many standards bodies, it allows direct participation by companies rather than through national delegations. ETSI standards are freely available for download.
