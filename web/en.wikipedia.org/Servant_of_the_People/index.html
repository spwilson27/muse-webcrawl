Servant of the People

Servant of the People has governed Ukraine since 2019

Servant of the People (Ukrainian: Слуга народу, Sluha narodu) is a liberal political party in Ukraine. The party was founded in late 2017 and registered in March 2018, taking its name from the popular Ukrainian television series Servant of the People (2015–2019), in which comedian Volodymyr Zelenskyy played a schoolteacher unexpectedly elected President of Ukraine.

History

Zelenskyy ran for the real presidency under the party's banner in the 2019 Ukrainian presidential election, campaigning on anti-corruption and anti-establishment themes, and won a landslide victory in the second round with about 73 percent of the vote against incumbent Petro Poroshenko. In the snap parliamentary election of July 2019, Servant of the People won the first single-party majority in modern Ukrainian history, taking 254 of 450 seats in the Verkhovna Rada.

In government

The party's governments pursued reforms including land market liberalization, banking reform and digitalization of public services, though they also faced criticism over the pace of anti-corruption efforts and internal discipline. Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, the party has governed under martial law as part of the wartime political landscape, with Zelenskyy remaining president.

Ideology

Servant of the People is generally described as a centrist and liberal party with a populist, anti-establishment style, drawing support across Ukraine's traditional political divides. It declares support for Ukraine's integration into the European Union and NATO.
