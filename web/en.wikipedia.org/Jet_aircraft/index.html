Jet aircraft

A jet aircraft (or simply jet) is an aircraft propelled by jet engines. Jet aircraft generally fly much faster than propeller-driven aircraft and at higher altitudes, and are more efficient at long distances.

The first practical jet aircraft was the German Heinkel He 178, flown in 1939 following the work of Hans von Ohain; Frank Whittle independently developed the turbojet in Britain. Jets transformed military aviation in the Second World War and the Korean War, and commercial aviation from the de Havilland Comet and Boeing 707 onward. Modern types use turbofan engines.
