Bracket (tournament)

A bracket is a tree diagram representing the series of games played during a tournament, typically drawn so that winners advance along lines toward a final. Brackets are used in single-elimination and double-elimination formats, where teams or players are seeded so that the strongest entrants are kept apart until the later rounds.

In a standard seeded bracket, the highest seed plays the lowest seed in the first round. Predicting the outcomes of an entire bracket — most famously for the NCAA men's basketball tournament, "March Madness" — is a popular pastime, and a perfectly predicted bracket is extraordinarily improbable.

Brackets are commonly printed in newspapers and interactive web pages, and the term has extended to any contest structured as successive head-to-head eliminations.
