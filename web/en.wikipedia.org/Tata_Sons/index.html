Tata Sons

Tata Sons Pvt. Ltd. is the parent company of the Tata Group and holds the bulk of the group's shareholding in group companies. It was established in 1917 and is headquartered at Bombay House in Mumbai, India. Tata Sons is registered as a core investment company and acts as the principal holding company and promoter of Tata companies, which operate in sectors including steel, automobiles, information technology, consumer products, chemicals, energy, and hospitality.

Ownership

About two-thirds of the equity of Tata Sons is held by philanthropic trusts endowed by members of the Tata family, principally the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and the Sir Ratan Tata Trust. These trusts use their dividends to fund charitable work in education, health, and rural development across India. The Shapoorji Pallonji Group, through Cyrus Investments and Sterling Investment Corporation, has historically been the largest individual shareholder with a stake of around 18 percent.

History

The Tata trading enterprise was founded by Jamsetji Tata in 1868. Tata Sons was created as the group's managing agency and later became its holding company. Chairmen of Tata Sons — who traditionally also chair the Tata Group — have included J. R. D. Tata, Ratan Tata, Cyrus Mistry, and Natarajan Chandrasekaran, who took office in 2017. The removal of Cyrus Mistry in 2016 led to a high-profile legal dispute between Tata Sons and the Shapoorji Pallonji group.

Role in the group

Tata Sons owns the Tata brand and trademarks, licenses them to group companies, and provides governance and strategic direction. Major operating companies associated with the group include Tata Steel, Tata Motors, Tata Consultancy Services, Tata Power, Tata Chemicals, Indian Hotels, and Titan Company.
