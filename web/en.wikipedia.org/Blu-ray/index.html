Blu-ray

Media typeOptical disc
Capacity25 GB (single layer); 50 GB (dual layer)
Developed byBlu-ray Disc Association
Released2006

Blu-ray (Blu-ray Disc or BD) is a digital optical disc data storage format designed to supersede the DVD format. It uses a blue-violet laser, which has a shorter wavelength than the red laser used for DVDs, allowing data to be stored more densely. A standard single-layer disc can hold 25 GB, and a dual-layer disc 50 GB.

History

Blu-ray was developed by the Blu-ray Disc Association, a consortium led by Sony and Philips. After a format war with Toshiba's HD DVD, Blu-ray prevailed in 2008 when Toshiba withdrew HD DVD. The format became the standard for high-definition video distribution.

Ultra HD Blu-ray

Ultra HD Blu-ray, launched in 2016, supports 4K resolution, high dynamic range and capacities up to 100 GB on triple-layer discs.
