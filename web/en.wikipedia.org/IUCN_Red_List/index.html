The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species, also known as the IUCN Red List or Red Data Book, founded in 1964, is an inventory of the global conservation status and extinction risks of biological species. A series of Regional Red Lists, produced by national and regional organisations, assess the risk of extinction to species within their political management units. The IUCN Red List is widely recognised as the most comprehensive, objective global approach for evaluating the conservation status of plant and animal species.

Categories

Species are assigned to categories based on criteria such as rate of decline, population size, geographic distribution and fragmentation:

EX Extinct EW Extinct in the Wild CR Critically Endangered EN Endangered VU Vulnerable NT Near Threatened LC Least Concern DD Data Deficient NE Not Evaluated

Species in the CR, EN and VU categories are collectively described as "threatened". The criteria (version 3.1, adopted in 2001) use quantitative thresholds A–E covering population reduction, restricted range, small population size, very small or restricted populations, and quantitative analysis such as population viability analysis.

Assessment process

Assessments are carried out by the IUCN Species Survival Commission's network of specialist groups, BirdLife International (for birds), and partner institutions, and are peer-reviewed before publication. More than 160,000 species have been assessed; of these, over 45,000 are considered threatened with extinction. The target of 160,000 assessments was reached in the 2020s, and periodic "Red List Index" updates track trends for major groups such as mammals, birds, amphibians, corals and cycads.

Uses and criticism

The list informs conservation policy, protected-area planning, CITES listings and environmental impact assessments. It has been criticised for reliance on data of uneven quality, the potential misuse of its data by collectors of rare species, and for the sensitivity of listings to economic and political pressures. The IUCN also publishes a Red List of Ecosystems and regional red lists.
