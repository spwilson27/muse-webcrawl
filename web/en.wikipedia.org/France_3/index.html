France 3

France 3 is a French public regional television channel and part of the France Télévisions group. Launched on 31 December 1972, it is distinguished by its network of regional stations that produce local news and programming for France's regions.

History

The channel began as the Troisième chaîne of the ORTF in 1972 and became France Régions 3 (FR3) in 1975 when the ORTF was broken up. FR3 was also responsible for overseas broadcasting and cinema production support. In September 1992 it was renamed France 3 as public channels were regrouped under France Télévision. During the 2018–2024 reforms the regional network was strengthened and rebranded under the "ici" identity for its local news bulletins.

Programming and regions

France 3 operates a network of regional centres — including services for Corsica (France 3 Corse Via Stella) and regional-language programming in Breton, Alsatian, Basque, Catalan, and Occitan. Its schedule mixes national programmes with regional opt-outs, notably the midday and evening regional news editions. Popular national programmes have included the detective series set in various French regions and the long-running game show Des chiffres et des lettres (until its move and later cancellation).
