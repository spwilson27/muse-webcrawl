Village

A village is a clustered human settlement or community, larger than a hamlet but smaller than a town, with a population typically ranging from a few hundred to a few thousand. Villages are often located in rural areas, though the term also applies to certain urban neighbourhoods.

Characteristics

Historically villages were the usual form of settlement for societies practising subsistence agriculture, typically centred on a church, market, green or water source, with houses clustered for defence and community. Industrialisation and urbanisation drew population to towns and cities, but villages remain home to a large share of the world's population, particularly in South Asia and Africa.

By region

In Britain a village traditionally has a church; in India villages (gram) number in the hundreds of thousands and are governed locally by gram panchayats; in North America the term often denotes a small incorporated municipality.
