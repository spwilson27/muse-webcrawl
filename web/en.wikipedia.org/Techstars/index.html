Techstars is an American startup accelerator and investment firm founded in 2006 in Boulder, Colorado. It operates mentorship-driven accelerator programs in cities around the world, investing in early-stage companies in exchange for equity.

Program

Techstars programs typically run for three months. Selected startups receive seed funding, office space, and intensive mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs and investors, culminating in a "Demo Day" presentation to potential investors. The organization's motto is "give first," emphasizing mentorship without expectation of return.

History

Techstars was founded by David Cohen, Brad Feld, David Brown, and Jared Polis. Its first program ran in Boulder in 2007 with ten companies. It expanded to Boston, Seattle, New York City, and many other cities, and has run corporate-sponsored programs with partners such as Microsoft, Disney, and Barclays. Alumni include SendGrid, DigitalOcean, PillPack, and ClassPass.
