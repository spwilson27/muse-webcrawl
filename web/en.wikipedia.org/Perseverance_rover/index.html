Perseverance, nicknamed Percy, is a car-sized Mars rover designed to explore the Jezero Crater on Mars as part of NASA's Mars 2020 mission. It was launched on 30 July 2020 and landed on 18 February 2021.

Mission

The rover searches for signs of ancient microbial life, characterizes Mars's geology and past climate, and caches samples of rock and regolith for a possible future return mission to Earth. It carried the Ingenuity helicopter, a small experimental rotorcraft that made the first powered flight on another planet in April 2021 and completed 72 flights before ending its mission in January 2024.

Instruments

Perseverance carries a suite of instruments including the Mastcam-Z cameras, the SuperCam laser spectrometer, the PIXL and SHERLOC X-ray and ultraviolet spectrometers, the MEDA weather station, and MOXIE, an experiment that produced oxygen from the carbon-dioxide atmosphere.

Design

The rover is based on the Curiosity rover's design, with upgraded wheels, a robotic arm with a coring drill for sample caching, and improved autonomous navigation. It is powered by a radioisotope thermoelectric generator using plutonium-238.
