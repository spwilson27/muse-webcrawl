List of Olympic medalists in swimming (men)

This is the complete list of men's Olympic medalists in swimming, covering freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, medley, relays, the 10 km marathon swim, and discontinued events.

Most successful swimmers

Michael Phelps (United States) is the most decorated Olympian of all time with 28 medals, 23 of them gold, won between 2004 and 2016. Other multiple champions listed include Ian Thorpe and Grant Hackett (Australia), Mark Spitz (United States, seven golds at Munich 1972), Matt Biondi, Ryan Lochte, Caeleb Dressel, and Léon Marchand (France), who won four individual golds at Paris 2024.

Events

  • Freestyle: 50 m, 100 m, 200 m, 400 m, 800 m, 1500 m
  • Backstroke: 100 m, 200 m
  • Breaststroke: 100 m, 200 m
  • Butterfly: 100 m, 200 m
  • Individual medley: 200 m, 400 m
  • Relays: 4×100 m and 4×200 m freestyle, 4×100 m medley
  • Marathon: 10 km open water
