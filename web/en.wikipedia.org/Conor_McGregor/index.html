Conor Anthony McGregor (born 14 July 1988) is an Irish professional mixed martial artist and boxer. He is a former Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) featherweight and lightweight double-champion — the first fighter to hold UFC championships in two weight classes simultaneously — and one of the biggest pay-per-view draws in the history of combat sports.

Mixed martial arts career

McGregor began his professional career in 2008 and won Cage Warriors featherweight and lightweight titles before signing with the UFC in 2013. After a rapid rise, he knocked out José Aldo in 13 seconds at UFC 194 in December 2015 to win the featherweight title, then defeated Eddie Alvarez at UFC 205 in November 2016 to add the lightweight belt. His two bouts with Nate Diaz in 2016 and his 2018 loss to Khabib Nurmagomedov at UFC 229 were among the best-selling events in UFC history.

Boxing and business

In August 2017 McGregor made his professional boxing debut against Floyd Mayweather Jr., losing by tenth-round stoppage in one of the most lucrative fights ever staged. Outside the cage he founded the whiskey brand Proper No. Twelve, sold a majority stake in 2021, and has pursued business, acting and political ambitions in Ireland, where his career has also been accompanied by repeated legal controversies.
