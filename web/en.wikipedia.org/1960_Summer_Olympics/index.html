The 1960 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XVII Olympiad, were held from 25 August to 11 September 1960 in Rome, Italy. They were the first Summer Games to be telecast widely in North America and Europe.

Highlights

Ethiopian Abebe Bikila won the marathon running barefoot, becoming the first sub-Saharan African Olympic champion. American boxer Cassius Clay (later Muhammad Ali) won the light heavyweight gold medal. Danish cyclist Knud Enemark Jensen collapsed during the team time trial and died, the first Olympic death linked to doping. The Soviet Union led the medal table ahead of the United States.

Venues

Events blended ancient and modern Rome: wrestling took place in the Basilica of Maxentius and gymnastics in the Baths of Caracalla, while the marathon finished at the Arch of Constantine.
