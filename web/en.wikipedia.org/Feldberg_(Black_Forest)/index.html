Feldberg (Black Forest)

The Feldberg is the highest mountain in the Black Forest and in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, with an elevation of 1,493 metres (4,898 ft). It is also the highest peak in Germany outside the Alps.

Geography and tourism

The Feldberg lies southeast of Freiburg im Breisgau. Its summit area hosts weather stations and a broadcasting tower, and the surrounding massif is a major centre for winter sports, with ski lifts and cross-country trails, and for summer hiking. The Feldberg nature reserve, established in 1937, is the oldest in Baden-Württemberg and protects the subalpine flora of the summit region.
