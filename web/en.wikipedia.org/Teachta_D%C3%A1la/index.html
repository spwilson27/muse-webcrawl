Teachta Dála

A Teachta Dála (plural Teachtaí Dála), abbreviated TD, is a member of Dáil Éireann, the lower house of the Oireachtas (Irish parliament). The term is Irish for "deputy of the assembly" and is loosely equivalent to "member of parliament".

Election and role

TDs are elected by proportional representation with the single transferable vote from multi-seat constituencies. The Dáil has had 158 members in recent terms, increased to 174 at the 2024 general election following a constituency review. TDs are known for a strong constituency-service role, holding local clinics for constituents. Members are addressed within the chamber through the Ceann Comhairle (chair), and cabinet ministers are drawn from among TDs (and occasionally senators).
