Half marathon

A half marathon is a road running event of 21.0975 kilometres (13 miles 192.5 yards)—half the distance of a marathon. Participation has grown enormously since the 2000s, and the event is a popular goal for recreational runners for whom a full marathon is a step too far; typical finishing times range from just over two hours for beginners to under an hour for elites.

Records and events

World Athletics ratifies world records for the distance; the men's record was lowered to 56:42 by Yomif Kejelcha in 2024, and Letesenbet Gidey set the women's record of 1:02:52 in 2021. Major half marathons include the Great North Run in England—the world's largest, with around 60,000 entrants—and races in Copenhagen, Lisbon, Valencia, and Houston. World Athletics has staged a World Half Marathon Championships since 1992.
