Polycentricity

Polycentricity refers to a structure with many centers of decision-making, authority or activity, rather than a single center. The concept is used in political science, law, urban studies and governance research.

Vincent and Elinor Ostrom developed the idea of polycentric governance for managing common-pool resources, arguing that multiple overlapping authorities can adapt to local conditions and experiments. In urban geography, polycentric regions such as the Randstad or Rhine-Ruhr have several major cities rather than one primate city.
