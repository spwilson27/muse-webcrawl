Discovery+ (pronounced "Discovery Plus"; stylized as discovery+) is a streaming service owned by Warner Bros. Discovery. It is oriented toward non-fiction and factual programming, drawing primarily on the libraries of Discovery Channel, TLC, Animal Planet, Food Network, HGTV, Investigation Discovery, and related brands, together with original series made for the platform.

History

The service first launched in India in March 2020 and rolled out in the United States on January 4, 2021, followed by launches in numerous international markets. Following the 2022 merger of Discovery, Inc. and WarnerMedia into Warner Bros. Discovery, the company launched the combined Max service in 2023, while Discovery+ has continued as a standalone offering in many markets with its own subscription tiers, including an ad-supported plan.

Content

Programming spans true crime, reality, home and food, nature documentaries, and paranormal series, including spin-offs and exclusives from franchises such as 90 Day Fiancé, Deadliest Catch, and Ghost Adventures, plus content from the Magnolia Network.
