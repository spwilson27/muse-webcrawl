Bharti Global

Bharti Global is the international investment arm of Bharti Enterprises, the Indian business group founded by Sunil Bharti Mittal. Based in London, it manages the group's strategic investments outside India, principally in telecommunications, satellite communications, and hospitality.

Investments

Bharti Global is best known for leading, together with the UK government, the 2020 rescue of the satellite broadband company OneWeb from bankruptcy, each partner committing substantial funding to relaunch the low-earth-orbit constellation business; OneWeb later merged with the French satellite operator Eutelsat in 2023, with Bharti remaining a major shareholder of the combined Eutelsat Group.

In August 2024 Bharti Global agreed to acquire a stake of about 24.5% in BT Group, the British telecommunications company, from Altice, making the Bharti group BT's largest shareholder. The investment was completed after UK regulatory approval under the National Security and Investment Act.
