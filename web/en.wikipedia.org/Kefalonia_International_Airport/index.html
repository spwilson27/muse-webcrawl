Kefalonia International Airport "Anna Pollatou" (IATA: EFL, ICAO: LGKF) is an airport on the island of Kefalonia in Greece. It is located in the southwest of the island, about 8 km south of the capital Argostoli, near the villages of Minia and Svoronata. The airport serves mainly seasonal charter and low-cost traffic bringing tourists from northern Europe during the summer, along with year-round domestic services to Athens.

History

The airport opened in 1971. In 2017 its operation was transferred, together with 13 other Greek regional airports, to Fraport Greece under a long-term concession, which subsequently renovated and expanded the terminal. The airport is named after Anna Pollatou, a rhythmic gymnast from Kefalonia who died in a car accident in 2014.
