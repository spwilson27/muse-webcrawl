Adele discography

The discography of English singer-songwriter Adele consists of four studio albums, two extended plays, 17 singles and several video releases. Adele signed with XL Recordings in 2006 and released her debut album, 19, in January 2008. Her four studio albums — each named after the age at which she wrote and recorded most of it — have made her one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with worldwide sales of over 120 million records.

Selected singles

Adele's best-known singles include "Chasing Pavements" (2008), "Rolling in the Deep" (2010), "Someone Like You" (2011), "Skyfall" (2012), the James Bond theme that won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, "Hello" (2015) and "Easy On Me" (2021). The albums 21 and 25 each won the Grammy Award for Album of the Year.
