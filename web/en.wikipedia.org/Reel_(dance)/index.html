Reel (dance)

The reel is a folk dance type and the accompanying music, originating in Scotland and Ireland and danced in 2/4 or 4/4 time. Reels are characterised by lively, running melodic figures and travelling steps in which dancers weave figures-of-eight patterns around one another.

Forms

In Scottish country dancing, reels are one of the two main dance types alongside jigs, set for two, three or four couples. In Irish step dance, the reel is danced in soft shoes by women and in a hard-shoe treble version by both sexes. Specific dances such as the Virginia reel spread through North America, and the eight-hand reel features in Scottish cèilidhs.

Music

Reel tunes are typically built of two repeated eight-bar strains played on fiddle, flute, accordion or pipes, at tempos faster than a jig's lilting compound time.
