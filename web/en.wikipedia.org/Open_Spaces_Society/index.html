Open Spaces Society

The Open Spaces Society is a campaign group that works to protect public rights of way and open spaces in the United Kingdom. Founded in 1865 as the Commons Preservation Society, it is Britain's oldest national conservation body.

The society was instrumental in saving Epping Forest, Hampstead Heath and other London commons from enclosure and development, and helped found the National Trust in 1895. Its founders and early supporters included Lord Eversley (George Shaw-Lefevre), Octavia Hill, Sir Robert Hunter and John Stuart Mill's circle.

Work

Today the society advises local authorities, landowners and the public on commons, town and village greens, and public paths, takes legal action to defend access, and campaigns on legislation. It is a statutory consultee on works on common land in England and Wales.
