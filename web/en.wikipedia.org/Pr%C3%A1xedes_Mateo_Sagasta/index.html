Práxedes Mateo Sagasta

Práxedes Mateo Sagasta (21 July 1825 – 5 January 1903) was a Spanish civil engineer and politician who served as Prime Minister of Spain on eight occasions between 1870 and 1902, always in charge of the Liberal Party, which he founded.

Career

Trained as a civil engineer, Sagasta entered politics as a progressive. He participated in the Glorious Revolution of 1868 that deposed Isabella II, and later became the chief architect, along with Antonio Cánovas del Castillo, of the Restoration settlement — the turno pacífico system under which Liberals and Conservatives alternated in power.

Legacy

Under his governments, Spain introduced universal male suffrage (1890) and endured the Spanish–American War of 1898, which cost Spain Cuba, Puerto Rico and the Philippines.
