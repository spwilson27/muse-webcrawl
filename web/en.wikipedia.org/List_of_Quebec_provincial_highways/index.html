List of Quebec provincial highways

The Quebec provincial highway network is managed by Transports Québec and consists of autoroutes (freeways, numbered below 100 and in the 400s–900s series) and national/regional routes (numbered 100 and above).

Autoroutes

Major autoroutes include A-20 (Montreal–Quebec City along the south shore and eastward), A-40 (Montreal–Quebec City via the north shore), A-15 (Montreal to the U.S. border and the Laurentians), A-10 (Montreal to the Eastern Townships), and A-73 and A-85 in the Quebec City and Bas-Saint-Laurent regions.

Provincial routes

Numbered routes such as Route 132 (the long St. Lawrence and Gaspé Peninsula route), Route 138 (north shore to the Côte-Nord), Route 117 (to Abitibi-Témiscamingue) and Route 389 (toward Labrador) form the backbone of the regional network.
