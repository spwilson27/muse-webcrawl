2011 Cricket World Cup Final

The 2011 Cricket World Cup Final was a One Day International (ODI) cricket match played between India and Sri Lanka at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai, India on 2 April 2011. India won the match by six wickets, securing their second Cricket World Cup title after their first in 1983.

Background

The 2011 tournament was co-hosted by India, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh. India defeated Pakistan in the semi-final at Mohali, while Sri Lanka beat New Zealand at Colombo. Sri Lanka captain Kumar Sangakkara won the toss and elected to bat first.

Sri Lanka innings

Sri Lanka posted 274/6 in their 50 overs, built around an unbeaten century by Mahela Jayawardene, who scored 103 not out off 88 balls. Zaheer Khan and Yuvraj Singh were among the wicket-takers for India.

India innings

India's chase began poorly with the early loss of Virender Sehwag and Sachin Tendulkar, but Gautam Gambhir's 97 and an unbeaten 91 from captain Mahendra Singh Dhoni carried India home. Dhoni finished the match with a six off Nuwan Kulasekara, sealing victory with 10 balls to spare.

Aftermath

Yuvraj Singh was named Player of the Tournament. The victory sparked nationwide celebrations across India, and Dhoni was named man of the match in the final.
