Visakhapatnam

Visakhapatnam (also known as Vizag) is the largest and most populous city in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh, lying between the Eastern Ghats and the coast of the Bay of Bengal. It is the administrative headquarters of Visakhapatnam district and the second-largest city on India's east coast. It was designated the executive capital of Andhra Pradesh under the state's three-capital plan announced in 2020.

Economy and navy

Visakhapatnam grew from a fishing village into a major industrial and port city, with steel (RINL's Visakhapatnam Steel Plant), shipbuilding (Hindustan Shipyard, est. 1941), petroleum refining, fertilisers, and a growing IT and pharmaceutical sector. Visakhapatnam Port is one of India's largest cargo ports. The city hosts the headquarters of the Eastern Naval Command of the Indian Navy, and the INS Kursura submarine museum on RK Beach commemorates the submarine that served in the 1971 war.

Tourism

Attractions include Rama Krishna Beach, the Kailasagiri hill park, the Simhachalam hill temple, the Borra Caves and the Araku Valley in the nearby Eastern Ghats. In October 2014, Cyclone Hudhud devastated the city, which was subsequently rebuilt.
