The Regional Municipality of Waterloo (Waterloo Region) is a metropolitan area and regional municipality in Southern Ontario, Canada. It comprises the cities of Cambridge, Kitchener, and Waterloo, together with the townships of North Dumfries, Wellesley, Wilmot, and Woolwich.

Government

The two-tier structure created in 1973 gives the regional government responsibility for services such as public transit (Grand River Transit and the Ion light rail), policing, public health, waste management, and arterial roads, while lower-tier municipalities handle local services.

Economy and education

The region is a major technology centre, anchored by the University of Waterloo and Wilfrid Laurier University, and home to a dense cluster of technology firms in the Toronto–Waterloo Corridor.
