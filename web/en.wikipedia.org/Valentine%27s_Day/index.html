Valentine's Day
Date14 February
ObservancesSending cards, flowers, and gifts to loved ones

Valentine's Day, also called Saint Valentine's Day, is celebrated annually on February 14. It originated as a Christian feast day honouring the martyr Saint Valentine and has become a significant cultural and commercial celebration of romance and love in many regions of the world.

History

Several martyrs named Valentine were commemorated on February 14 in early Christian tradition. The association with romantic love developed in the Middle Ages, in the circle of Geoffrey Chaucer, when courtly love flourished. By the 18th century in England, lovers exchanged flowers, confectionery, and handwritten greeting cards known as "valentines".

Customs

Modern customs include sending cards, red roses, chocolates, and gifts, and going on dates. Symbols of the day include hearts, doves, and the figure of Cupid. The holiday is observed in many countries, sometimes with local variations such as White Day in parts of East Asia.
