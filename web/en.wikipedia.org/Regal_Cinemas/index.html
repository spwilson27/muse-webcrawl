Regal Cinemas (also known as Regal Entertainment Group) is an American chain of movie theaters headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee. It is one of the largest theater circuits in the United States, operating hundreds of locations with thousands of screens, including IMAX and premium large-format auditoriums.

History

The company was founded in 1989 and grew rapidly through acquisitions during the 1990s. In 2002 it combined with United Artists Theatres and Edwards Theatres under the Regal Entertainment Group name. In 2018 the British chain Cineworld acquired Regal for about $3.6 billion, making the combined group one of the world's largest exhibitors.

Operations

Regal operates the Regal Crown Club loyalty program and the Unlimited subscription service, and many locations feature the premium RPX (Regal Premium Experience) format.
