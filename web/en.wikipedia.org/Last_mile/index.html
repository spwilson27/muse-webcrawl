Last mile

A delivery vehicle completing the last mile of a shipment

The last mile or last kilometer is the final leg of a journey or delivery, in which people or goods are moved from a transportation hub to their final destination. The term is used in supply chain management, transportation, and telecommunications.

In logistics

In supply chain management, last-mile delivery is the movement of goods from a distribution centre to the customer's home or business. It is widely considered the most expensive and inefficient part of the delivery process, often accounting for more than half of total shipping costs, largely because individual stops each serve a single customer.

Companies have experimented with parcel lockers, delivery drones, autonomous vehicles, and crowdsourced couriers to reduce last-mile costs and failed deliveries.

In telecommunications

In telecommunications, the last mile is the final leg of a network that delivers connectivity to retail end users, such as the copper telephone wires or fibre strands running from a local exchange or cabinet to a subscriber's premises. The term is also applied to electricity and water distribution networks.
