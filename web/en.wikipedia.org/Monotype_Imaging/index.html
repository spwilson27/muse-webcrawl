Monotype Imaging Holdings Inc. is an American company specialising in typeface design, font technology, and branding. Based in Woburn, Massachusetts, it descends from the historic Monotype Corporation and owns one of the world's largest libraries of typefaces, including Helvetica, Arial, Times New Roman, Gill Sans, and FF DIN.

History and business

The modern company emerged from the hot-metal typesetting businesses of Lanston Monotype and the British Monotype Corporation as those firms' font libraries and digital assets were consolidated. In the digital era Monotype supplied core fonts for operating systems, printers, and e-readers, and developed rendering and hinting technology for text on screens. It grew by acquisition, absorbing businesses such as Linotype (2006), the Chinese foundry China Type Design, Ascender Corporation, Bitstream, and FontShop (2014), operator of the FontFont library. Private equity firm HGGC took Monotype private in 2019 in a deal valued at roughly $825 million.

Products and controversies

Monotype licenses fonts to publishers, software makers, and global brands, and operates retail channels such as MyFonts. Its licensing enforcement — particularly demands over embedded or unlicensed font use — and its stewardship of ubiquitous designs inherited from acquired foundries have periodically drawn criticism from independent type designers, even as the company has also invested in new releases and revivals of classic faces.
