43 (forty-three) is the natural number following 42 and preceding 44.

In mathematics

43 is the 14th prime number. It is a twin prime with 41, a Chen prime, and a Wagstaff prime. It is the third term in the Euclid–Mullin sequence and the largest natural number that is not a McNugget number. 43 is also a Heegner number and the smallest prime that is not a Chen prime in one sense and is expressible as the sum of two, three, four, or five distinct primes in various notable ways.

43 is a centered heptagonal number and a Markov number's companion in certain solutions. In binary, 43 is 101011, a palindrome-adjacent pattern.

In science

43 is the atomic number of technetium, the lightest element whose isotopes are all radioactive. In astronomy, Messier object M43 is a star-forming nebula in the constellation Orion, part of de Mairan's Nebula.

In other fields

George H. W. Bush was the 43rd president of the United States (his son George W. Bush was the 43rd individual to hold the office, counting Grover Cleveland twice giving George W. Bush as 43rd president). In popular culture, 43 is the number of the legendary liquor Licor 43 from Spain, and the racing number famously associated with Richard Petty in NASCAR.
