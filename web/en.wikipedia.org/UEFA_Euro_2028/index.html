The 2028 UEFA European Football Championship, commonly referred to as UEFA Euro 2028 or simply Euro 2028, will be the 18th edition of the UEFA European Championship, the quadrennial international men's football championship of Europe organised by UEFA. It is scheduled to be hosted jointly by England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland in June and July 2028.

Host selection

The five football associations of the United Kingdom and Ireland — England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland — submitted the sole valid bid after Turkey withdrew to focus on Euro 2032, and UEFA confirmed them as hosts in October 2023. Matches in Northern Ireland were dropped from the schedule in 2024 after redevelopment of Casement Park in Belfast was ruled out in time for the tournament, leaving nine venues in eight cities across the remaining four host nations, including Hampden Park in Glasgow, the Principality Stadium in Cardiff, the Aviva Stadium in Dublin and Wembley Stadium in London, where the semi-finals and final are planned.

Qualification

As with recent tournaments, 24 teams will compete. Unusually for multi-nation hosts, none of the host nations receives a guaranteed automatic place; instead UEFA reserved back-stop provisions intended to ensure host representation, subject to qualifying results.
