Political correctness

Political correctness (adjectivally politically correct; PC) is a term used to describe language, policies, or measures that are intended to avoid offense or disadvantage to members of particular groups in society. Since the late 1980s the term has often been used pejoratively in debates about language and public policy.

Supporters argue inclusive language promotes respect and equality, while critics contend it can restrict free expression or become excessive. The concept became prominent in American cultural debates of the 1990s and remains contested in discussions of education, media and employment.
