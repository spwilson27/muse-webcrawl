RCA Records

RCA's "His Master's Voice" Nipper logo

RCA Records is an American record label owned by Sony Music Entertainment, a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation. Founded in 1901 as the Victor Talking Machine Company, it is one of the oldest record labels in existence and a flagship label of Sony Music, alongside Columbia Records and Epic Records.

History

Victor, maker of the Victrola phonograph and the famous "His Master's Voice" trademark featuring the dog Nipper, was acquired by the Radio Corporation of America in 1929. The label introduced the 45 rpm single in 1949 and pioneered commercial stereo recordings in the 1950s, marketing them as "Living Stereo".

Artists

RCA's catalogue spans much of 20th-century popular music: it was the label of Elvis Presley, whose contract RCA bought from Sun Records in 1955, and of artists including David Bowie, the Jefferson Airplane, Lou Reed, and Dolly Parton. In later decades its roster has included Britney Spears, Justin Timberlake, Alicia Keys, and many others.

Ownership

Following RCA's absorption into General Electric and the sale of its music division to Bertelsmann, the label passed through BMG and its joint venture with Sony before becoming wholly part of Sony Music in 2008.
