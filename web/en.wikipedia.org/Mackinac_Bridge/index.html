Mackinac Bridge

Mackinac Bridge
Location: Michigan, United States
Opened: 1 November 1957
Total length: 8,614 m (5.35 mi)
Designer: David B. Steinman

The Mackinac Bridge is a suspension bridge that connects the Upper and Lower peninsulas of the US state of Michigan. Opened in 1957, the 26,372-foot-long (4.995 miles; 8.038 km) bridge is among the world's longest suspension bridges by total length.

History

Before the bridge, travellers crossed the Straits of Mackinac by ferry. Designed by David B. Steinman, construction began in 1954; the bridge is nicknamed "Big Mac" and "Mighty Mac".

Crossing

The bridge carries Interstate 75 and is closed in extreme weather. Each Labor Day, thousands walk the bridge in the annual Mackinac Bridge Walk.
