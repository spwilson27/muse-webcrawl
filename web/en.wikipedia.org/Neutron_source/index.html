Neutron source

A neutron source is any device that emits neutrons, irrespective of the mechanism used to produce them. Neutron sources are used in physics, engineering, medicine, nuclear weapons, petroleum exploration and nuclear chemistry for neutron activation analysis and neutron scattering.

Types

Small sources include radioisotope sources based on spontaneous fission (californium-252), alpha–neutron reactions (americium–beryllium), and photoneutron sources. Medium-sized sources include plasma focus devices and neutron generators. The largest and most intense sources are nuclear research reactors and spallation sources such as ISIS, the Spallation Neutron Source in Tennessee, and the European Spallation Source.

Applications

Intensities range from tiny hand-held sources used for moisture gauging and well logging, to reactor and spallation facilities that deliver beams of thermal and cold neutrons for crystallography, materials science and fundamental physics.
