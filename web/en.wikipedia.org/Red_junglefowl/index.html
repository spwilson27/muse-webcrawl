Red junglefowl

The red junglefowl (Gallus gallus) is a tropical bird in the pheasant family Phasianidae, native to Southeast Asia. It is the primary wild ancestor of the domestic chicken.

Description

Males are strikingly coloured, with golden-orange neck hackles, a red comb and wattles, and iridescent dark tail feathers; females are drab brown for camouflage. They forage on the forest floor for seeds, insects and fallen fruit, and roost in trees at night.

Domestication

Genetic studies show the chicken was domesticated from red junglefowl, with contributions from other junglefowl species, several thousand years ago in Southeast Asia, initially perhaps for cockfighting and ritual before eggs and meat. The domestic form is classified as Gallus gallus domesticus.
