List of particles

This is a list of particles in particle physics, including the elementary particles of the Standard Model and the most important composite particles (hadrons). Elementary particles are particles with no known substructure; composite particles are bound states of quarks and antiquarks held together by the strong interaction.

Other particles and quasiparticles

  • Atoms and ions — composite bound states of nuclei and electrons; antiprotons and positrons are the antiparticles of the proton and electron.
  • Exotic hadrons — tetraquarks (e.g. X(3872), Tcc⁺) and pentaquarks (Pc states), confirmed at LHCb.
  • Glueballs — hypothetical bound states of gluons.
  • Quasiparticles in condensed matter — phonons, excitons, plasmons, magnons, polarons, holes and Cooper pairs behave like particles within materials.
  • Hypothetical particles — the graviton, axions, WIMPs, sterile neutrinos, magnetic monopoles, tachyons and the superpartners of supersymmetry (e.g. neutralinos, gluinos, squarks and sleptons), none yet observed.
  • Majorana fermions — particles that are their own antiparticles; neutrinos may be of this type.
