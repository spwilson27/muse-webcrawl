The Philippine Air Force (PAF) is the aerial warfare service branch of the Armed Forces of the Philippines. It traces its origins to the Philippine Army Air Corps, formed in 1935 under the Commonwealth, and was established as a separate service on 1 July 1947.

History

Philippine Army Air Corps pilots flew in the defence of the Philippines in 1941–42; Captain Jesús Villamor became a celebrated ace. After the Second World War the force was rebuilt with American assistance, operating fighters such as the F-86 Sabre and later the F-5, and served in counterinsurgency campaigns. In recent decades the PAF has focused on air defence, transport, disaster relief and maritime patrol.

Modernization

Under the AFP Modernization Program the PAF acquired twelve FA-50 light combat aircraft from South Korea beginning in 2015, together with new transport aircraft, helicopters and air defence radars.
