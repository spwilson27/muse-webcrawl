Midland, Michigan

Midland is a city in the U.S. state of Michigan in the Tri-Cities region of Central Michigan. It is the county seat of Midland County. The city's population was 42,547 at the 2020 census. Midland is best known as the home of Dow Inc., the multinational chemical corporation founded there by Herbert Henry Dow in 1897 to extract bromine from the region's brine; Dow remains the dominant employer.

The city is noted for its mid-century modern architecture, much of it designed by Alden B. Dow, and for attractions including the Dow Gardens, the Alden B. Dow House and Studio, and the Tridge, a three-way wooden footbridge over the confluence of the Tittabawassee and Chippewa rivers. In May 2020, Midland was severely flooded after the failures of the Edenville and Sanford dams upstream on the Tittabawassee River following heavy rain, forcing the evacuation of around 10,000 residents.
