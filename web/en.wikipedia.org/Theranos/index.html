Theranos

Theranos was an American health technology company founded in 2003 by Elizabeth Holmes, who claimed its devices could run hundreds of diagnostic tests from a few drops of blood. The claims were exposed as false, and the company dissolved in 2018 in one of the largest corporate fraud scandals in Silicon Valley history.

Founded2003
FounderElizabeth Holmes
Defunct2018
HeadquartersPalo Alto, California
Peak valuationabout $9 billion (2014)

Exposure and collapse

In October 2015, Wall Street Journal reporter John Carreyrou published investigations showing Theranos's "Edison" devices were inaccurate and that most tests were run on conventional machines. Regulators sanctioned the company, its partnership with Walgreens collapsed, and Holmes and president Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani were charged with fraud. Holmes was convicted in 2022 and sentenced to over 11 years in prison.
