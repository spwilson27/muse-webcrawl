Austin, Texas

State: Texas

County: Travis (partly Hays and Williamson)

Population (2020): 961,855

Incorporated: 27 December 1839

Nickname: Live Music Capital of the World

Austin is the capital city of the U.S. state of Texas and the county seat of Travis County. Founded in 1839 as Waterloo and renamed for Stephen F. Austin, the "Father of Texas", it became the capital of the Republic of Texas and remained the state capital after annexation. It is the eleventh-most populous city in the United States and one of its fastest growing.

Economy and culture

Austin is a major centre for technology — sometimes called "Silicon Hills" — with large operations for companies such as Dell, IBM, Apple, Google and Tesla, alongside the University of Texas at Austin, one of the largest public universities in the country. The city brands itself the "Live Music Capital of the World", hosting the South by Southwest (SXSW) and Austin City Limits festivals and an outsized number of live music venues. Its unofficial slogan, "Keep Austin Weird", celebrates local businesses and eccentricity.

Geography

The city lies in Central Texas on the Colorado River, which forms Lady Bird Lake and Lake Austin within the city, at the edge of the Texas Hill Country. Summers are hot, and the area is known for its spring-fed Barton Springs Pool and outdoor recreation.
