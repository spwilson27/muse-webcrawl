The Naval Special Warfare Command (NAVSPECWARCOM), also known as NAVSOC, is the naval component of United States Special Operations Command and the United States Navy's special operations force. Headquartered at Naval Amphibious Base Coronado in San Diego, California, it organises, trains and equips the Navy SEAL teams and Special Warfare Combatant-craft Crewmen (SWCC).

Organisation

The command oversees Naval Special Warfare Groups on both coasts of the United States and overseas detachments. Its principal units include the SEAL teams, SEAL Delivery Vehicle teams and special boat teams.

History

The command was established in 1987 following the creation of U.S. Special Operations Command, drawing its lineage from the underwater demolition teams and other naval special warfare units of the Second World War.
