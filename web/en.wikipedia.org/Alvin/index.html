Alvin

Alvin is a given name and surname. It may refer to several people, places, and other topics.

People

Notable people with the given name include Alvin Ailey (1931–1989), American dancer and choreographer who founded the Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater; Alvin Lee (1944–2013), English guitarist of Ten Years After; Alvin Toffler (1928–2016), American writer and futurist; Alvin C. York (1887–1964), American soldier and Medal of Honor recipient; Alvin Kamara (born 1995), American football running back; and Alvin Plantinga (born 1932), American philosopher.

Places

Alvin is the name of several communities in the United States, including Alvin, Texas, a city in Brazoria County; Alvin, Illinois; and Alvin, Wisconsin. Alvin is also a community in British Columbia, Canada.

Other uses

DSV Alvin is a crewed deep-ocean submersible operated by the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, famous for exploring the wreck of the Titanic and discovering hydrothermal vents. Alvin is also the lead character of Alvin and the Chipmunks, the animated singing group created by Ross Bagdasarian in 1958. Tropical Storm Alvin has been used for several Pacific hurricanes and tropical storms.
