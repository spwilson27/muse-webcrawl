Stray Kids

Stray Kids (Korean: 스트레이 키즈; abbreviated SKZ) is a South Korean boy band formed by JYP Entertainment. The group is composed of eight members: Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. Originally a nine-piece group, member Woojin left due to personal reasons in October 2019. The group was formed through the 2017 survival reality show of the same name, and is noted for its self-produced music, much of it written by the in-house production trio 3Racha (Bang Chan, Changbin and Han).

Career

Stray Kids released their pre-debut extended play Mixtape in January 2018 and officially debuted on 25 March 2018 with the EP I Am Not. Their first studio album, Go Live (2020), was followed by Noeasy (2021), their first million-seller. In 2022 they topped the US Billboard 200 with the EPs Oddinary and Maxident, and in 2023 their third studio album 5-Star became one of the best-selling albums of the year worldwide, driven by the single "S-Class". The 2023 EP Rock-Star gave them a fourth consecutive Billboard 200 number one, and their 2024 releases Ate and Hop extended the streak, making them one of the most commercially successful K-pop acts of their generation.

Artistry

The group's music blends hip hop, EDM and rock, with lyrics frequently addressing identity, anxiety and social pressure. Their fandom is known as "Stay". Stray Kids have won numerous awards in South Korea and internationally and have headlined major festivals outside Asia, including Lollapalooza Paris and BST Hyde Park.
