L.L.Bean

L.L.Bean, Inc. is an American privately held retail company founded in 1912 by Leon Leonwood Bean in Freeport, Maine. The company, headquartered in Freeport, specialises in clothing and outdoor recreation equipment, historically sold through mail-order catalogues and now also through retail stores and online.

History

Bean founded the company after designing the Maine Hunting Shoe — a waterproof boot combining leather uppers with rubber bottoms — to keep his feet dry while hunting. The "Bean Boot" became the company's signature product. The Freeport flagship store, famous for a giant replica boot outside, has traditionally stayed open 24 hours a day.

Business

L.L.Bean built its reputation on generous customer service, including a lifetime satisfaction guarantee (modified in 2018 to a one-year return policy with receipts). It remains family-owned and has expanded internationally, particularly in Japan and Canada.
