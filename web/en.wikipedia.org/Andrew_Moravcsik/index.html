Andrew Moravcsik

Andrew Maitland Moravcsik (born 1958) is an American political scientist, Professor of Politics and director of the European Union Program at Princeton University. He is known for developing liberal intergovernmentalism, a theory of European integration set out in The Choice for Europe (1998).

Work

Liberal intergovernmentalism argues integration is driven by national governments: domestic interest groups shape state preferences, governments bargain interstate, and they delegate to institutions such as the European Commission to lock in commitments. Moravcsik has also written on human rights regimes, liberal international relations theory, and transatlantic relations, and contributes commentary on European affairs.
