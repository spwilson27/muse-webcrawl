AuthorRichard Korherr
Commissioned byHeinrich Himmler
Date23 March 1943 (16-page version); April 1943 (summary)
SubjectStatistics on the "Final Solution" to the end of 1942

The Korherr Report is a statistical report on the progress of the Holocaust, prepared in early 1943 by Richard Korherr, chief statistician of the SS, at the request of Heinrich Himmler. Titled "The Final Solution of the European Jewish Question", it documented, in euphemistic bureaucratic language, the murder and deportation of millions of Jews by the end of 1942.

Contents

The report calculated that the Jewish population of Europe had declined by more than four million since the Nazis came to power, through emigration, deaths and deportations. It recorded over 1.27 million Jews "evacuated" through camps in the General Government and the Warthegau — figures matching the numbers murdered at the Operation Reinhard killing centres — and roughly 145,000 through camps in other regions, alongside the deportation of Jews from Germany, Austria, Slovakia and the occupied West.

Language

Himmler's office objected to the phrase Sonderbehandlung ("special treatment", a common SS euphemism for killing), and Korherr replaced it with wording about Jews being "transported" and "sluiced through" the camps. The surviving correspondence about this wording is important evidence that the regime's leadership understood exactly what the statistics described.

Significance

The report was later used as evidence in war-crimes proceedings and remains a key document for historians quantifying the Holocaust, corroborated by other records such as the Höfle telegram, whose figures for the Reinhard camps match Korherr's to the digit.
