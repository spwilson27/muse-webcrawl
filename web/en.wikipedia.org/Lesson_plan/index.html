Lesson plan

A lesson plan is a teacher's detailed description of the course of instruction for a single lesson. It sets out the objectives students should reach, the material and activities to be used, the sequence and timing of the lesson, and how learning will be assessed.

Lesson plan
PurposeGuide instruction for one lesson
Typical elementsObjectives, materials, activities, timing, assessment
Used byTeachers and trainee teachers

Components

A typical lesson plan states the topic, grade level and duration; learning objectives; required materials; an opening or "hook"; the main teaching and guided practice; independent work; and a closing summary with checks for understanding. Many plans also note differentiation for students of different abilities.

Approaches

Formats vary by school and country. Models such as Madeline Hunter's lesson design and the 5E model (engage, explore, explain, elaborate, evaluate) structure lessons around stages of learning, while backward design starts from the desired outcomes and plans assessment before activities.
