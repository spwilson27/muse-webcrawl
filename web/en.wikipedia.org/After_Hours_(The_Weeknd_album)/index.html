After Hours is the fourth studio album by the Canadian singer-songwriter the Weeknd, released on 20 March 2020 by XO and Republic Records. Primarily produced by the Weeknd himself alongside DaHeala, Illangelo, Max Martin and Oscar Holter, the album explores loneliness, heartbreak, escapism and self-loathing, presented through a persona with a bloodied, bandaged face that the Weeknd maintained throughout its promotional cycle.

Singles and reception

The album was supported by the singles "Heartless", "Blinding Lights" and "Save Your Tears". "Blinding Lights" became one of the most successful songs of all time, spending four weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100 and being named the greatest Hot 100 hit of all time by Billboard. After Hours debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 with 444,000 album-equivalent units and received generally positive reviews praising its production and cohesion. Its exclusion from the 63rd Grammy Award nominations provoked public controversy, with the Weeknd criticising the Recording Academy.

Track listing

Standard edition tracks include "Alone Again", "Too Late", "Hardest to Love", "Scared to Live", "Snowchild", "Escape from LA", "Heartless", "Faith", "Blinding Lights", "In Your Eyes", "Save Your Tears", "Repeat After Me (Interlude)", "After Hours" and "Until I Bleed Out".
