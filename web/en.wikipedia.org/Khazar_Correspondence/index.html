Khazar Correspondence

The Khazar Correspondence is a set of documents from the 950s–960s, consisting of letters exchanged between Hasdai ibn Shaprut, a Jewish courtier of the Caliph of Córdoba, and Joseph, king of the Khazars.

Content

Hasdai inquired about the Khazar kingdom and its adoption of Judaism; Joseph's reply describes Khazar history, geography and the conversion under King Bulan. The correspondence is a key source for Khazar history, though its authenticity has been debated.
