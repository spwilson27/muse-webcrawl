The UEFA Europa League (usually known as the Europa League) is an annual football club competition organised since 1971 by the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) for eligible European football clubs. It is the second-tier competition of European club football, ranking below the UEFA Champions League and above the UEFA Conference League.

The competition was known as the UEFA Cup from 1971 to 2009, having itself replaced the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup. Since the 2014–15 season, the winner of the Europa League qualifies for the following season's UEFA Champions League league phase, and plays the Champions League winner in the UEFA Super Cup.

History

The UEFA Cup evolved from the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup, founded in 1955. Tottenham Hotspur won the first UEFA Cup final in 1972. The competition adopted a group stage in 2004 and was relaunched as the Europa League in 2009 with a distinctive brand and anthem. Spanish clubs have dominated: Sevilla FC won a record seven titles (2006, 2007, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2020, 2023), and Atlético Madrid won three. The tournament adopted UEFA's "Swiss-style" 36-team single league phase from 2024–25.

Format

Teams qualify through their domestic league positions and cup wins, plus clubs eliminated from Champions League qualifying and league-phase play-offs. After qualifying rounds, 36 teams play eight matches in a single league phase; the top eight advance directly to the round of 16, the next sixteen contest a play-off round, and the final is played at a pre-selected neutral stadium.
