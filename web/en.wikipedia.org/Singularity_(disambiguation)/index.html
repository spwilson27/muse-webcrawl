Singularity (disambiguation)

Singularity may refer to:

Science and mathematics

  • Gravitational singularity — a point in spacetime where densities and curvature become infinite, as at the centre of a black hole or the Big Bang.
  • Mathematical singularity — a point at which a function or object is not well-behaved, e.g. where it is undefined or infinite.
  • Essential singularity, removable singularity, and pole — classes of isolated singularities in complex analysis.
  • Singular point of a curve or algebraic variety.
  • Singularity (climate) — a weather phenomenon recurring around a specific date.

Technology and futurology

  • Technological singularity — the hypothetical point at which artificial intelligence triggers runaway technological change beyond human comprehension.
  • Singularity (operating system) — a Microsoft Research experimental OS.
  • Singularity (software) — a container platform for scientific computing, now Apptainer.

Other uses

  • Singularity (2010 video game) by Raven Software.
  • The Singularity, a concept in the works of Vernor Vinge and Ray Kurzweil (The Singularity Is Near).
