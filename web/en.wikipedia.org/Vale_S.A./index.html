Vale S.A. is a Brazilian multinational mining corporation and one of the world's largest producers of iron ore and nickel. Founded in 1942 as the state-owned Companhia Vale do Rio Doce (CVRD), it was privatized in 1997.

Operations

Vale operates vast iron ore mines in the Carajás range of Pará and in Minas Gerais, together with railways, ports, and shipping. It also produces pellets, nickel, copper, cobalt, and other metals, with operations in Canada, Indonesia, and elsewhere.

Dam disasters

The company was responsible for two catastrophic tailings dam collapses in Minas Gerais: the Mariana disaster of 2015 (a joint venture with BHP) and the Brumadinho disaster of January 2019, which killed 270 people and led to major settlements and criminal proceedings.
