List of prime numbers

A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that has no positive divisors other than 1 and itself. Euclid proved around 300 BC that there are infinitely many primes. The number of primes less than or equal to n is denoted π(n); there are 25 primes below 100, 168 below 1,000, and 9592 below 100,000.

Types of prime numbers

Mathematicians study many special classes of primes:

  • Mersenne primes — primes of the form 2p − 1; the largest known primes are of this form, found mainly by the GIMPS project.
  • Twin primes — pairs differing by 2, such as (11, 13) and (17, 19); whether infinitely many exist is unproven.
  • Sophie Germain primes — primes p where 2p + 1 is also prime.
  • Fermat primes — primes of the form 22^n + 1; only five are known.
  • Factorial primes and primorial primes — primes near factorials and primorials.
  • Palindromic primes — primes that read the same backward and forward, such as 131 and 373.
  • Emirps — primes whose reversal is a different prime, such as 13 and 31.

Largest known prime

The largest known prime number, discovered by the Great Internet Mersenne Prime Search (GIMPS) in October 2024, is 2136,279,841 − 1, a number with 41,024,320 decimal digits. Such records are dominated by Mersenne primes because of the efficient Lucas–Lehmer primality test.
