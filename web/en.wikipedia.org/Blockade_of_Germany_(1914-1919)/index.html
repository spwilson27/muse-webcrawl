Blockade of Germany (1914–1919)

Blockade of Germany
Dates: 1914 – 1919
Enforced by: Royal Navy
Context: First World War

The Blockade of Germany, or Blockade of Europe, occurred from 1914 to 1919. The British Royal Navy prevented maritime trade from reaching Germany and the other Central Powers, restricting imports of food and raw materials.

Effects

The blockade contributed to severe shortages and malnutrition in Germany; the winter of 1916–17 was known as the "Turnip Winter". Historians debate the number of excess civilian deaths, commonly estimated in the hundreds of thousands.

Continuation after the armistice

The blockade was maintained after the November 1918 armistice until Germany signed the Treaty of Versailles in June 1919, a policy that drew criticism for harming civilians.
