The Long Dark Tea-Time of the Soul is a 1988 humorous fantasy detective novel by Douglas Adams. It is the second book featuring the "holistic detective" Dirk Gently, following Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency (1987).

Plot

The novel begins with an unexplained explosion at Heathrow Airport's check-in desk and involves Dirk Gently investigating a client connected to the incident. The story weaves in Norse mythology: the gods Odin and Thor are present in the modern world, and Odin has entered into a contract with a record executive and a lawyer's firm, with consequences for Asgard and humanity.

Title

The title phrase is drawn from Adams's earlier novel Life, the Universe and Everything, where the immortal Wowbagger uses it to describe the tedium of Sunday afternoons.
