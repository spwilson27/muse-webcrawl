Ségolène Royal (born 22 September 1953) is a French politician who served as president of the Poitou-Charentes regional council and as Minister for Ecology, Sustainable Development and Energy. She was the Socialist Party's candidate in the 2007 French presidential election, losing the run-off to Nicolas Sarkozy — the first woman in France to be nominated by a major party for the presidency.

Career

An adviser to President François Mitterrand in the 1980s, Royal held ministerial posts for the environment, school education, and family affairs. Her 2007 campaign used extensive online consultation and emphasised participatory democracy. She later served as France's ambassador for the Arctic and Antarctic poles.
