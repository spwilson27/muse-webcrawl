Luciano Pavarotti Cavaliere di Gran Croce OMRI (12 October 1935 – 6 September 2007) was an Italian operatic tenor who, during the late part of his career, crossed over into popular music, eventually becoming one of the most acclaimed and loved tenors of all time. He made numerous recordings of complete operas and individual arias, gaining worldwide fame for his clear, ringing tone and the charisma of his stage performances, and became one of the Three Tenors alongside Plácido Domingo and José Carreras.

Early life and debut

Pavarotti was born in Modena, in northern Italy; his father Fernando was a baker and amateur tenor who encouraged his singing. After studying with Arrigo Pola and Ettore Campogalliani, he won the Concorso Internazionale in 1961 and made his professional debut that year as Rodolfo in La bohème in Reggio Emilia. A 1963 performance with Joan Sutherland in London launched his international career; their partnership in bel canto repertoire — and his recording of Donizetti's La fille du régiment, whose aria "Ah! mes amis" contains nine high Cs — earned him the title "King of the High Cs."

Career

Pavarotti debuted at the Metropolitan Opera in New York in 1968 and at La Scala in 1965. His 1972 Met performance of La fille du régiment made him a star in America; televised concerts from Lincoln Center drew huge audiences. From 1990 the Three Tenors concerts, beginning at the Baths of Caracalla in Rome during the FIFA World Cup, took opera to stadium-sized crowds, and his signature aria, Puccini's "Nessun dorma," became a global popular anthem. He founded the Pavarotti & Friends charity concerts in Modena, collaborating with pop and rock artists to raise money for humanitarian causes.

Later years and death

Pavarotti gave his final operatic performance at the Met in 2004 and sang publicly for the last time at the opening ceremony of the 2006 Winter Olympics in Turin, performing "Nessun dorma." He died of pancreatic cancer at his home in Modena in September 2007. His funeral Mass at Modena Cathedral was attended by thousands, and the Italian air force's Frecce Tricolori performed a fly-past.
