Vukašin Mrnjavčević

Vukašin Mrnjavčević
Died: 26 September 1371
Title: King of Serbia (co-ruler)
Reign: 1365–1371
House: Mrnjavčević

Vukašin Mrnjavčević (Serbian Cyrillic: Вукашин Мрњавчевић; died 1371) was a Serbian medieval nobleman who rose from courtier to become king and co-ruler of the Serbian Empire under Emperor Stefan Uroš V. He was the most powerful magnate in the southern Serbian lands, ruling from Prilep in Macedonia.

Rise to power

Vukašin first appears as a župan in the service of Emperor Stefan Dušan and his son Uroš. In 1365 Uroš crowned him king, making him co-ruler and designated heir, a sign of the weakening of imperial authority and the rise of regional lords such as Vukašin and his brother, Despot Uglješa.

Death

In 1371 Vukašin and Uglješa marched against the expanding Ottoman Turks but were defeated and killed at the Battle of Maritsa on 26 September 1371, a turning point that opened the southern Balkans to Ottoman conquest. His son Marko Mrnjavčević, later the hero of Serbian epic poetry as Prince Marko, succeeded him at Prilep.
