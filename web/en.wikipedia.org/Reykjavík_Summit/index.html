Reykjavík Summit

The Reykjavík Summit was a meeting between United States President Ronald Reagan and Soviet General Secretary Mikhail Gorbachev, held in Reykjavík, Iceland, on 11–12 October 1986. Intended as a preparatory discussion, it unexpectedly came close to an agreement to eliminate all nuclear weapons before collapsing over the US Strategic Defense Initiative (SDI).

Negotiations

Meeting at Höfði house, the leaders and their delegations negotiated deep cuts in strategic and intermediate-range missiles. Gorbachev proposed halving strategic arsenals and, ultimately, abolishing all nuclear weapons by 2000, but insisted that SDI research be confined to laboratories. Reagan refused to abandon SDI, and the summit ended without agreement.

Significance

Though initially portrayed as a failure, Reykjavík revealed the depth of possible reductions and directly paved the way for the Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces Treaty signed in Washington in December 1987, the first agreement to eliminate an entire class of nuclear weapons.
