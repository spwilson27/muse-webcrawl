Sochi (Russian: Сочи) is a city in Russia, on the Black Sea coast of the North Caucasus in Krasnodar Krai. Stretching some 145 kilometres along the shore, Greater Sochi is sometimes described as one of the longest cities in Europe. It is Russia's largest resort city, famed for its subtropical climate, beaches, and the nearby Caucasus Mountains.

History

The area was inhabited in antiquity and was part of Circassia before Russian expansion; the modern settlement grew around a fort founded in 1838. During the Soviet period Sochi became a premier health resort, with sanatoria built along the coast, and Joseph Stalin maintained a dacha there. The Circassian population had been expelled in 1864 at the end of the Caucasian War, a history commemorated by Circassians worldwide.

2014 Winter Olympics

Sochi hosted the XXII Olympic Winter Games and the 2014 Winter Paralympics, with events split between the coastal Olympic Park in Adler and the mountain cluster at Krasnaya Polyana. The games' massive construction programme transformed the city's infrastructure. Sochi also hosted matches of the 2018 FIFA World Cup and the Russian Grand Prix in Formula One (2014–2021).

Geography and climate

Sochi has a humid subtropical climate unusual for its latitude, with mild wet winters and warm summers, protected by the mountains from cold northern air. Palm trees and tea plantations grow in the area; the city extends into Sochi National Park and the Caucasus Biosphere Reserve.
