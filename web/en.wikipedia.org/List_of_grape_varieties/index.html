List of grape varieties

This is a list of grape varieties, also called cultivars, both those grown for wine (chiefly Vitis vinifera) and those grown as table grapes or for raisins. Thousands of varieties exist; a small number dominate world wine production.

Table and raisin grapes

Thompson Seedless (Sultana) is widely grown for table use and raisins, while Concord, a Vitis labrusca variety, is used for juice and jelly in North America.
