Avatar (2009 film)

Directed by: James Cameron
Starring: Sam Worthington, Zoe Saldaña, Sigourney Weaver, Stephen Lang
Released: December 2009
Box office: over $2.9 billion worldwide

Avatar is a 2009 American epic science fiction film written and directed by James Cameron. Set in 2154 on Pandora, a lush moon orbiting a gas giant in the Alpha Centauri system, it follows paraplegic Marine Jake Sully, who operates a genetically engineered Na'vi body — an "avatar" — and is drawn into the conflict between human colonists seeking the mineral unobtanium and the indigenous Na'vi.

Plot

Jake, played by Sam Worthington, infiltrates the Na'vi at the direction of Colonel Miles Quaritch while learning their ways from Neytiri (Zoe Saldaña) and scientist Grace Augustine (Sigourney Weaver). When the mining company moves to destroy the Na'vi's sacred Hometree, Jake defects, unites the clans, and leads them to victory in a final battle for Pandora.

Production and reception

Cameron developed new performance-capture and 3D filming technology for the production, delaying the film until the tools matured. Avatar became the highest-grossing film of all time, a record it has largely retained with worldwide grosses approaching $3 billion including reissues, and it won three Academy Awards. It launched a franchise, with the sequel Avatar: The Way of Water released in 2022.
