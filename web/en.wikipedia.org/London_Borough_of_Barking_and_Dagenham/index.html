The London Borough of Barking and Dagenham is a London borough in East London. It was created in 1965 under the London Government Act 1963, largely from the former Municipal Borough of Barking and the Municipal Borough of Dagenham in Essex. The borough lies around 14 kilometres east of Charing Cross, on the north bank of the River Thames.

History

Barking was an ancient fishing and market town centred on Barking Abbey. Dagenham was transformed in the interwar years by the Becontree Estate, one of the largest public housing estates in the world, and by the Ford Dagenham motor plant, long a major employer. The borough remains more suburban and lower-rise than inner London.

Governance and places

The borough is administered by Barking and Dagenham London Borough Council. Its districts include Barking, Dagenham, Becontree, Chadwell Heath, and Rush Green. Regeneration schemes, notably at Barking Riverside on former industrial land by the Thames, are adding thousands of new homes.
