Lexical semantics

Lexical semantics (also known as lexicosemantics) is a subfield of linguistics concerned with the study of word meanings and the relationships between words. It examines how words denote objects, concepts and events, and how their meanings are structured in the mental lexicon.

Topics

Core topics include polysemy and monosemy, synonymy and antonymy, hyponymy and hyperonymy, meronymy, and semantic fields — sets of words covering related concepts, such as terms for cooking or kinship. Lexical semanticists also study how word meanings are decomposed into semantic features or primitives, and the argument structure of predicates.

Approaches

Approaches range from structuralist field theory to generative and cognitive frameworks, as well as computational resources such as WordNet, a lexical database of English organised around sets of synonyms (synsets). Lexical semantics contrasts with compositional semantics, which studies how the meanings of larger expressions are built from their parts.
