Sylver Vertical Launching System

The Sylver (Système de Lancement Vertical, "Vertical Launching System") is a vertical launching system for missiles designed and manufactured by Naval Group of France. It is used on French, British, Italian and other navies' warships, principally to launch the Aster family of surface-to-air missiles.

Variants

Sylver is produced in several lengths, designated by the maximum missile length in decimetres: the A-35, A-43, A-50 and A-70. The A-43 and A-50 are used with the Aster 15 and Aster 30 missiles respectively within the PAAMS (Principal Anti-Air Missile System) fitted to the French and Italian Horizon-class frigates and the British Type 45 destroyers. The longer A-70 can also launch the MdCN land-attack cruise missile, as fitted to French FREMM frigates.

Design

Launchers are grouped in eight-cell modules. Unlike the American Mk 41 system, each Sylver cell is dedicated to a missile type suited to its length, and the system vents exhaust through a dedicated uptake. Sylver has been exported with FREMM and other French-designed frigates to several navies, including those of Egypt, Morocco, Singapore (a variant), South Korea (in combination with other launchers) and Saudi Arabia.

Use

Aster missiles launched from Sylver have been used operationally in air defence, including engagements in the Red Sea, and the system forms the core of the air-defence capability of the Royal Navy's Type 45 destroyers.
