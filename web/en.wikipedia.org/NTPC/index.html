NTPC

NTPC Limited, formerly known as the National Thermal Power Corporation, is an Indian central public sector undertaking engaged in the generation of electricity and related activities. Headquartered in New Delhi, it is India's largest power utility, with an installed capacity exceeding 75 GW through its own stations and subsidiaries and joint ventures.

Founded in 1975, NTPC is a Maharatna company under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, which retains a majority shareholding.

History and operations

NTPC was incorporated in 1975 to accelerate power development in India, beginning with coal-fired stations at Singrauli, Korba and Ramagundam. It has since diversified into gas, hydro, solar and wind generation, nuclear power through joint ventures, coal mining, power trading, and consultancy. Its stations include some of the largest thermal power plants in the world, such as Vindhyachal (4,760 MW).

Subsidiaries and renewables

Subsidiaries and joint ventures include NTPC Green Energy Limited, which houses the company's renewable energy portfolio, and companies in distribution, services and mining. NTPC has set targets of reaching 130 GW of total capacity by 2032, with a growing share from non-fossil sources.
