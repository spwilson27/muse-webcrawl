Messier 49

Messier 49 (also known as M49 and NGC 4472) is a giant elliptical galaxy about 56 million light-years away in the equatorial constellation of Virgo. It was the first member of the Virgo Cluster of galaxies to be discovered, by Charles Messier in 1771, and it is the brightest galaxy in the cluster, with an apparent magnitude of 8.4.

Properties

M49 is one of the largest known elliptical galaxies, roughly 160,000 light-years across, containing an estimated trillion stars and an extensive system of about 6,000 globular clusters. Its core harbors a supermassive black hole of roughly 5.6 × 108 solar masses. The galaxy is interacting with the dwarf irregular galaxy UGC 7636, and X-ray observations show a plume of hot gas being stripped from it.

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in M49: SN 1969Q, discovered in June 1969, of unknown type. A possible black-hole merger in one of its globular clusters (the X-ray source XMMU J1230) has been discussed in the literature.
