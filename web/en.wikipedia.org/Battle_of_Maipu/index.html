Battle of Maipú

The Battle of Maipú was fought on 5 April 1818 near Santiago, Chile, during the Chilean War of Independence. The patriot Army of the Andes under General José de San Martín decisively defeated the Spanish royalist army of General Mariano Osorio.

Significance

Coming weeks after the patriot disaster at Cancha Rayada, the victory at Maipú secured Chilean independence and allowed San Martín to prepare his expedition to liberate Peru. The embrace between San Martín and Bernardo O'Higgins after the battle — the "Abrazo de Maipú" — is an iconic image in Chilean history, and the site is commemorated by the Museo Histórico Nacional and the Votive Temple of Maipú.
