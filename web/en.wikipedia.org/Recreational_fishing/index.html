Recreational fishing

Recreational fishing, also called sport fishing or game fishing, is fishing done for pleasure, sport, or competition rather than for subsistence or commercial profit. It is one of the most popular outdoor leisure activities in many countries, with tens of millions of participants in the United States alone.

Methods

Anglers typically use a rod, line, and baited hook or lure. Techniques include fly fishing, spinning, baitcasting, trolling, and ice fishing. Fishing may be done from shore, from piers, or from boats ranging from kayaks to charter vessels. Many anglers practice catch and release, returning fish to the water alive.

Regulation and economy

Recreational fisheries are usually regulated through licenses, seasons, size limits, and catch limits administered by wildlife agencies. License fees and tackle purchases make the sport a significant economic activity and a major funding source for conservation.
