Denham Harman

Denham Harman (February 14, 1916 – November 25, 2014) was an American medical scientist best known as the originator of the free radical theory of aging, proposed in 1956.

Career

Harman worked at the University of Nebraska Medical Center, where he spent decades researching the role of free radicals — highly reactive molecules produced during normal metabolism — in aging and disease.

Free radical theory

His theory held that accumulated damage from free radicals causes the deterioration associated with aging. It spurred research into antioxidants, though clinical hopes that antioxidant supplements would extend lifespan largely proved disappointing. Harman died in Omaha, Nebraska, aged 98.
