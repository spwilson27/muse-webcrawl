The Imam Reza shrine (Persian: حرم امام رضا) in Mashhad, Iran, is a complex which contains the mausoleum of Imam Reza, the eighth Imam of Twelver Shi'ism. It is the largest mosque in the world by area and one of the holiest sites in Shia Islam, drawing tens of millions of pilgrims each year.

History

Imam Reza died in 818 CE and was buried in the village of Sanabad, beside the tomb of the Abbasid caliph Harun al-Rashid. The settlement grew into the city of Mashhad, "place of martyrdom", and the shrine was repeatedly rebuilt and expanded, notably under the Safavid and Qajar dynasties.

The complex

The complex includes the Goharshad Mosque, a museum, a library, cemeteries, courtyards (sahn), and the golden-domed tomb chamber. It is administered by the Astan Quds Razavi foundation.
