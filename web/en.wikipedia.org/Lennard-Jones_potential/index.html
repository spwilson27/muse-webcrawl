Lennard-Jones potential

The Lennard-Jones potential is a mathematical model approximating the interaction between a pair of neutral atoms or molecules. Named after John Lennard-Jones, it is commonly written V(r) = 4ε[(σ/r)12 − (σ/r)6], where ε is the depth of the potential well and σ is the distance at which the potential is zero.

Interpretation

The r−12 term models Pauli repulsion at short distances, while the r−6 term models attractive van der Waals (London dispersion) forces. The potential is widely used in molecular dynamics simulations and computational chemistry as a simple yet effective description of soft matter and noble gases.
