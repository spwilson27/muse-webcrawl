Biscayne National Park is a United States national park in southern Florida, south of Miami. It preserves Biscayne Bay, one of the top scuba diving areas in the United States, along with offshore barrier reefs and the northernmost Florida Keys.

Ecology

About 95 percent of the park is water. It protects mangrove forests, seagrass beds, coral reefs and more than 500 species of fish, as well as habitat for manatees and sea turtles.

History

The area was authorised as Biscayne National Monument in 1968 and redesignated a national park in 1980. Sites include Stiltsville, a group of houses built on stilts in the bay.
