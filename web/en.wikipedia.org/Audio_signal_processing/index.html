Audio signal processing

Audio signal processing is a subfield of signal processing concerned with the electronic manipulation of audio signals. Applications include recording, reproduction, compression, effects, speech recognition and synthesis.

Analog and digital

Processing was originally performed by analogue circuits such as filters and compressors. Since the late 20th century, digital signal processing has predominated, using sampling, the Fourier transform and digital filters implemented on DSP chips or in software.

Applications

Common operations include equalisation, dynamic range compression, reverberation, noise reduction, perceptual audio coding such as MP3 and AAC, and spatial audio rendering for surround sound and virtual reality.
