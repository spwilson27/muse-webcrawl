Eutelsat

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Eutelsat Group is a French satellite operator headquartered in Issy-les-Moulineaux, near Paris. Founded in 1977 as an intergovernmental organisation — the European Telecommunications Satellite Organization — to develop a satellite infrastructure for Europe, it was privatised in 2001 as Eutelsat S.A. It is one of the world's largest satellite operators by revenue.

Fleet and services

Eutelsat operates a fleet of geostationary satellites at orbital positions covering Europe, Africa, the Middle East, Asia, and the Americas, including the HOT BIRD satellites at 13° East, a key neighbourhood for European television broadcasting. Its services include video distribution to cable and DTH households, broadband connectivity, data networks, and government and mobility communications.

OneWeb merger

In September 2023 Eutelsat completed its merger with the low-Earth-orbit broadband operator OneWeb, creating Eutelsat Group, the first fully integrated GEO–LEO satellite operator. The combined group aims to compete in the market for satellite broadband against constellations such as Starlink, while continuing to derive the majority of its revenue from GEO video services, a market in long-term decline.

History

Eutelsat's first satellite, ECS-1, was launched in 1983. It pioneered direct-to-home television in Europe and carried some of the first satellite television channels. The organisation's privatisation and listing on Euronext Paris in 2005 was followed by two decades of fleet expansion across emerging markets in Africa, Latin America, and Asia.
