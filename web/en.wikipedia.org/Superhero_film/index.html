Superhero film

A superhero film is a film genre centered on superheroes — characters, usually adapted from comic books, who possess extraordinary abilities and use them to fight crime or threats to humanity. The genre blends action, science fiction, and fantasy elements and became the dominant force in global box office during the 2000s and 2010s.

History

Early examples include serials of the 1940s and Superman (1978), which proved a comic-book adaptation could anchor a big-budget blockbuster, followed by Batman (1989). The modern boom began with X-Men (2000) and Spider-Man (2002), and expanded into interconnected franchises, most notably the Marvel Cinematic Universe inaugurated by Iron Man (2008), several of whose entries rank among the highest-grossing films of all time.

Characteristics

Typical elements include an origin story, a costumed identity, a supervillain antagonist, and large-scale visual-effects action sequences. Critics have debated the genre's dominance and its influence on the economics of studio filmmaking.
