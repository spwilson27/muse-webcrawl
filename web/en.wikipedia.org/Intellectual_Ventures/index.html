IndustryPatent portfolio management and invention

Intellectual Ventures is an American private equity company that centres on the development and licensing of intellectual property. Founded in 2000, it has raised several billion dollars from technology companies and institutional investors and assembled one of the world's largest patent portfolios, comprising tens of thousands of patents and applications.

Business model

The company acquires existing patents, funds its own invention work — including the invention lab Intellectual Ventures Lab in Bellevue — and licenses its portfolio to companies, sometimes through litigation. Its Global Good Fund, supported by Bill Gates, develops technologies for the developing world, including vaccine cold-chain devices and malaria-control tools.

Criticism

Critics, including many technology firms, have labelled Intellectual Ventures one of the largest "patent trolls", accusing it of extracting royalties through shell-company lawsuits without producing products. The firm responds that it creates a market for invention and protects independent inventors.
