Entablature

An entablature is the horizontal superstructure in classical architecture that rests on columns and supports the roof or pediment.

Components

From bottom to top it consists of the architrave, the frieze (often decorated, with triglyphs and metopes in the Doric order or a continuous relief in Ionic) and the cornice, which projects outward.

Orders

The proportions and ornament of the entablature are defining features of the Doric, Ionic and Corinthian orders, and were revived in Renaissance and neoclassical architecture.
