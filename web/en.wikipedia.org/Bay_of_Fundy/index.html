Bay of Fundy

LocationBetween New Brunswick and Nova Scotia, Canada
Tidal rangeUp to about 16 metres — among the highest on Earth
FeaturesHopewell Rocks, Fundy National Park

The Bay of Fundy is a bay on the Atlantic coast of North America, between the Canadian provinces of New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. It is famous for having among the highest tidal ranges in the world.

Tides

The bay's shape funnels the Atlantic tide so that the difference between high and low water can reach roughly 16 metres (52 feet), notably at Burntcoat Head in the Minas Basin. At low tide, vast mudflats and the carved sandstone stacks of the Hopewell Rocks are exposed; hours later they stand in deep water.

Ecology and economy

The nutrient-rich waters support whales, seabirds, and important fisheries, and the tides have long been eyed for tidal power — the Annapolis Royal Generating Station operated in the bay from 1984 to 2019.
