People mover

A people mover or automated people mover (APM) is a small-scale automated guideway transit system, typically driverless, used to carry passengers over short distances — within airports, between terminals, in downtown districts, at theme parks, and on university or hospital campuses.

Characteristics

People movers run on dedicated, usually elevated, guideways with short trains or individual vehicles on rubber tyres or steel wheels, or in some systems (such as the Morgantown Personal Rapid Transit) as small automated cars. They are fully automated and grade-separated, giving high frequency at modest capacity compared with conventional metro rail.

Examples

Famous installations include the Tampa International Airport airside shuttles (the first airport system, opened 1971), the ATL Skytrain and plane train at Atlanta, systems at Singapore Changi and many other hub airports, the Miami Metromover downtown loop, and the Walt Disney World monorail system — often classed with people movers. Bombardier's Innovia and Mitsubishi's Crystal Mover are common vehicle families.
