Prelude FLNG

Prelude FLNG is a floating liquefied natural gas (FLNG) facility owned by Shell, built by the Technip–Samsung consortium at the Samsung Heavy Industries yard in Geoje, South Korea. At 488 metres long and 74 metres wide, it is one of the largest floating structures ever built, displacing around 600,000 tonnes when fully loaded.

Operation

The facility is moored over the Prelude and Concerto gas fields in the Browse Basin, about 475 kilometres north-northeast of Broome, Western Australia. It extracts, liquefies, stores and offloads natural gas at sea. Prelude was towed to Australia in 2017, produced its first LNG in 2019, and has capacity of about 3.6 million tonnes per annum of LNG, together with LPG and condensate. The project partners are Shell (82.5%), Inpex (9.5%), CPC (5%) and KOGAS (3%).

Incidents

Operations have been interrupted by technical faults, a fire in December 2021 that caused an extended shutdown, and industrial action in 2022. Prelude was the world's first deployed FLNG facility, establishing the concept for developing remote offshore gas fields without a pipeline to shore.
