Kellanova

Kellanova, formerly known as the Kellogg Company, is an American multinational food manufacturing company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. Kellanova produces and markets convenience foods and snack foods, including Pringles, Cheez-It, Pop-Tarts, Rice Krispies Treats, and Eggo.

History

The company was founded by Will Keith Kellogg in 1906 in Battle Creek, Michigan, as the Battle Creek Toasted Corn Flake Company, following the development of corn flakes by Kellogg and his brother, Dr. John Harvey Kellogg. The Kellogg Company grew into one of the world's largest cereal makers, with brands such as Corn Flakes, Rice Krispies and Frosted Flakes, and its mascot Tony the Tiger.

In 2012 Kellogg acquired the Pringles brand from Procter & Gamble for $2.7 billion, greatly expanding its snack business. In 2023 the company split into two: its North American cereal business was spun off as WK Kellogg Co, while the remaining global snacking and international cereal company adopted the name Kellanova, retaining the "K" heritage and the ticker symbol K.

Acquisition

In August 2024, Mars, Incorporated announced an agreement to acquire Kellanova for approximately $36 billion, one of the largest deals in the packaged food industry, combining Kellanova's snack brands with Mars's confectionery and snacking portfolio.
