Vacheron Constantin SA is a Swiss manufacturer of luxury watches, founded in Geneva in 1755 by Jean-Marc Vacheron. It is regarded as the world's oldest watch manufacture in continuous operation and is one of the most prestigious names in haute horlogerie. The company is a subsidiary of the Richemont group.

History

Jean-Marc Vacheron opened his workshop in Geneva in 1755. In 1819, François Constantin joined the firm, and the partnership of Vacheron and Constantin adopted the motto attributed to Constantin: "Do better if possible, and that is always possible." The company adopted the Maltese cross as its emblem in 1880, inspired by a component of the barrel cover used to limit the tension of the mainspring.

Among its landmark creations is the Reference 57260, unveiled in 2015 for the company's 260th anniversary, which with 57 complications was the most complicated mechanical pocket watch ever made at the time of its introduction. Earlier celebrated pieces include the Kallania jewellery watch and the Tour de l'Île wristwatch of 2005, then the most complicated wristwatch in the world.

Collections

Principal collections include Patrimony, Traditionnelle, Overseas, Fiftysix, Historiques and the bespoke Les Cabinotiers department, which produces unique commissioned pieces. Many movements carry the Hallmark of Geneva (Poinçon de Genève), certifying their origin and finishing.
