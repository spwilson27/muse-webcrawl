RMS Carpathia

RMS Carpathia
Owner: Cunard Line
Launched: 6 August 1902
Fate: Torpedoed and sunk, 17 July 1918
Known for: Rescuing survivors of Titanic

RMS Carpathia was a Cunard Line transatlantic passenger steamship built by Swan Hunter & Wigham Richardson. She became famous for rescuing the survivors of the White Star liner RMS Titanic after it struck an iceberg and sank on 15 April 1912.

The Titanic rescue

On the night of the disaster, Carpathia was en route from New York to Fiume when her wireless operator, Harold Cottam, received Titanic's distress calls. Captain Arthur Rostron turned the ship around and drove her through the ice field at a speed beyond her rated maximum, arriving at the scene around 4 a.m. Carpathia took aboard all 705 survivors from the lifeboats and carried them to New York, where Rostron and his crew were hailed as heroes.

Later career and sinking

Carpathia continued in passenger service and, during the First World War, carried troops. On 17 July 1918 she was torpedoed by the German submarine U-55 off the east coast of Ireland and sank with the loss of five crew members. Her wreck was located in 2000.
