McDonnell Douglas CF-18 Hornet

The McDonnell Douglas CF-18 Hornet, officially designated the CF-188, is a Royal Canadian Air Force (RCAF) variant of the American McDonnell Douglas F/A-18 Hornet fighter aircraft. Canada was the first export customer for the Hornet, selecting it in 1980 under the New Fighter Aircraft program.

Service

Canada acquired 98 single-seat CF-188A and 40 two-seat CF-188B aircraft, delivered between 1982 and 1988. CF-18s are based at CFB Bagotville, Quebec, and CFB Cold Lake, Alberta. They provide Canada's contribution to NORAD air defence and have deployed on operations including the Gulf War, Kosovo, Libya in 2011 and Iraq from 2014.

Upgrades and replacement

The fleet underwent an incremental modernisation program in the 2000s adding modern radar, communications and weapons. A further Hornet Extension Project is intended to keep part of the fleet viable until the replacement F-35A fleet, selected in the Future Fighter Capability Project, is fully introduced.
