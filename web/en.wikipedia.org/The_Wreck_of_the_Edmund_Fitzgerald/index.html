"The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald"

"The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald" is a 1976 hit song written, composed and performed by Canadian singer-songwriter Gordon Lightfoot to memorialise the sinking of the bulk carrier SS Edmund Fitzgerald on Lake Superior on 10 November 1975. The ballad reached number 2 on the US Billboard Hot 100 and number 1 in Canada.

Background

The Edmund Fitzgerald, the largest ship on the Great Lakes when launched in 1958, sank in a storm with the loss of all 29 crew. Lightfoot drew on news reports of the disaster, writing in a modal folk style with a repeating melody over a slow 12/8 rhythm evocative of waves.

Legacy

The song is widely regarded as one of Lightfoot's finest works and a landmark of narrative songwriting. Lightfoot later subtly altered a lyric after investigations revised the likely cause of the sinking.
