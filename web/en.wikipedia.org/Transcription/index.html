Transcription

Transcription is the process in molecular biology of copying a segment of DNA into RNA. It is the first step of gene expression: the RNA transcript, typically messenger RNA (mRNA), carries the information needed to synthesise proteins.

Mechanism

The enzyme RNA polymerase binds to a promoter region, unwinds the DNA double helix and synthesises a complementary RNA strand in the 5′ to 3′ direction, using the template strand. Termination signals end transcription and the polymerase releases the RNA. In bacteria, transcription and translation occur simultaneously in the cytoplasm.

Regulation and processing

Gene expression is controlled by transcription factors, enhancers and epigenetic marks that govern when and how strongly genes are transcribed. In eukaryotes the initial transcript (pre-mRNA) is processed — a 5′ cap and poly-A tail are added and introns are spliced out — before export to the cytoplasm.
