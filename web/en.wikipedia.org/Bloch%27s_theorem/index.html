Bloch's theorem
Field: Condensed matter physics
Named after: Felix Bloch
Subject: Wavefunctions in periodic potentials

Bloch's theorem states that solutions to the Schrödinger equation in a periodic potential take the form of a plane wave modulated by a function with the same periodicity as the lattice. Such solutions are called Bloch functions or Bloch states.

Statement

For a potential with lattice periodicity, the wavefunction can be written as ψ(r) = eik·r u(r), where u(r) has the periodicity of the crystal lattice and k is the crystal momentum.

Significance

The theorem underlies the band theory of solids, explaining electrical conductivity in metals, semiconductors and insulators, and was introduced by Felix Bloch in 1929.
