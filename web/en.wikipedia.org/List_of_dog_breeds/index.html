List of dog breeds

[Image: Dogs of several breeds together]
Domestic dogs show enormous variation in size and form.

This is a list of well-known dog breeds, including those recognised by major kennel clubs and selected landraces. Breeds are typically classified by function into groups such as sporting, herding, working, terrier, toy, hound and non-sporting.

Selected breeds by group

GroupExample breeds
SportingLabrador Retriever, Golden Retriever, Cocker Spaniel, German Shorthaired Pointer
HoundBeagle, Bloodhound, Greyhound, Dachshund, Afghan Hound
WorkingGerman Shepherd (also herding), Boxer, Siberian Husky, Great Dane, Rottweiler
TerrierJack Russell Terrier, Scottish Terrier, Bull Terrier, Airedale Terrier
ToyChihuahua, Pomeranian, Pug, Yorkshire Terrier, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel
HerdingBorder Collie, Australian Shepherd, Pembroke Welsh Corgi, Old English Sheepdog
Non-sportingBulldog, French Bulldog, Poodle, Dalmatian, Shiba Inu

The Fédération Cynologique Internationale recognises over 350 breeds. Many more local types and crossbreeds exist worldwide. Breed standards describe the ideal characteristics of each breed, including size, coat and temperament.
