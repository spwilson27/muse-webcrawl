Timo Glock (born 18 March 1982) is a German professional racing driver who competed in Formula One for Jordan, Toyota, Virgin Racing and Marussia, and later raced in the DTM for BMW. He won the GP2 Series championship in 2007.

Early career

Glock began karting at the age of 15 and progressed through German Formula BMW, winning the BMW ADAC Junior Cup in 2000 and the championship in 2001. He raced in German Formula Three and the Formula Three Euroseries before moving to the United States, where he competed in Champ Car in 2005 for Rocketsports Racing, scoring a best finish of second at Montreal and winning the Rookie of the Year title.

Formula One

Glock made his Formula One debut with Jordan Grand Prix at the 2004 Canadian Grand Prix, scoring points in seventh place in a chaotic race — though he was later temporarily disqualified before the result was reinstated on appeal of the cars ahead. After serving as BMW Sauber's test driver in 2006 and winning the 2007 GP2 title with iSport, he returned to a race seat with Toyota in 2008.

He is best remembered for the rain-hit finale of the 2008 season at Interlagos, where, still on dry tyres on the final lap, he was passed by Lewis Hamilton at the last corner — the move that decided the World Championship in Hamilton's favour by one point over Felipe Massa. Glock scored podium finishes for Toyota at Singapore in 2008 and Malaysia in 2009 before the team withdrew. He then led the new Virgin Racing (later Marussia) team from 2010 to 2012, leaving by mutual consent before the 2013 season.

DTM and later career

From 2013 Glock raced for BMW in the Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters, winning several races including at the Red Bull Ring and Zandvoort. He has also worked as a television analyst for German Formula One coverage with Sky Sports.
