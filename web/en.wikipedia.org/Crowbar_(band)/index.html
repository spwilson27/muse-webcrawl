Crowbar (band)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Crowbar is an American sludge metal band formed in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1990. Led by guitarist and vocalist Kirk Windstein, the band is considered one of the founding acts of the New Orleans sludge metal scene, combining the slow tempos and heavy riffing of doom metal with the aggression of hardcore punk. Windstein is the only constant member throughout the band's many line-up changes.

History

The group evolved from earlier Windstein projects including Shell Shock and Aftershock before settling on the name Crowbar. Their self-titled second album, Crowbar (1993), produced by Phil Anselmo, brought them wider attention, aided by exposure on MTV's Beavis and Butt-Head and touring with Pantera. Subsequent albums such as Time's Up for Us is not an album — the band's notable releases include Odd Fellows Rest (1998), Equilibrium (2000), Lifesblood for the Downtrodden (2005), and Sever the Wicked Hand (2011).

Windstein also performed with the supergroups Down and Kingdom of Sorrow alongside his work in Crowbar. Later albums include Symmetry in Black (2014), The Serpent Only Lies (2016), and Zero and Below (2022), with the band continuing to tour internationally.

Musical style

Crowbar's sound is characterized by extremely downtuned, slow, and heavy guitars, anguished vocals, and lyrics about suffering and perseverance. The band is frequently cited as an influence on later sludge and doom acts.
