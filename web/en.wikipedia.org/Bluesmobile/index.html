Bluesmobile

The Bluesmobile is the fictional automobile driven by Jake and Elwood Blues in the 1980 film The Blues Brothers. In the film it is a 1974 Dodge Monaco sedan, formerly a Mount Prospect, Illinois police car, bought by Elwood at auction. Despite its battered appearance it displays near-supernatural performance — outrunning police fleets, jumping drawsbridges and driving through a shopping mall.

The car in the film

Elwood famously defends the car: "It's got a cop motor, a 440-cubic-inch plant. It's got cop tires, cop suspension, cop shocks." Thirteen different cars were used in production to depict the Bluesmobile. In the 1998 sequel Blues Brothers 2000, the second Bluesmobile is a 1990 Ford LTD Crown Victoria, also a former police car.

Legacy

The Bluesmobile has become an icon of American cinema; replicas tour car shows, and the film's chase sequences — among the most destructive ever filmed, wrecking over 100 vehicles — remain celebrated.
