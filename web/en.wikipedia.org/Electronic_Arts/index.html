Electronic Arts

Industry: Video games
Founded: May 27, 1982
Founder: Trip Hawkins
Headquarters: Redwood City, California, U.S.

Electronic Arts Inc. (EA) is an American video game company headquartered in Redwood City, California. Founded in May 1982 by former Apple employee Trip Hawkins, the company was a pioneer of the early home computer game industry and promoted the designers and programmers responsible for its games as "software artists".

History

EA published early successes such as Pinball Construction Set and M.U.L.E., and distributed games by independent developers. In the 1990s it grew through major sports franchises and acquisitions, becoming one of the world's largest third-party publishers.

Franchises

EA is known for Madden NFL, EA Sports FC (formerly FIFA), The Sims, Battlefield, Need for Speed, Mass Effect, Dragon Age, and Apex Legends. Its studios include BioWare, DICE, Maxis, and Respawn Entertainment.

EA Sports

The EA Sports division publishes annualised sports titles under the slogan "It's in the game", and its Ultimate Team modes have been commercially significant, drawing both revenue and controversy over microtransactions.
