Russia (Russian: Россия, Rossiya), officially the Russian Federation, is a country spanning Eastern Europe and North Asia. It is the largest country in the world by area, extending across eleven time zones and sharing land borders with fourteen countries. With a population of around 144 million, it is Europe's most populous country and the ninth-most populous in the world. Russia's capital and largest city is Moscow; Saint Petersburg is its second-largest city and cultural centre.

History

The history of Russia begins with the East Slavs and the medieval state of Kievan Rus', founded in the 9th century and centred on Kyiv and Novgorod. After the Mongol invasion of the 13th century, the Grand Duchy of Moscow rose to power, and under Ivan III and Ivan IV ("the Terrible", the first crowned Tsar, 1547) the Russian state expanded dramatically. The Romanov dynasty (from 1613) and Peter the Great's westernizing reforms created the Russian Empire (1721), which by the 19th century stretched from Poland to the Pacific and Alaska.

The revolutions of 1917 ended the monarchy; after the Russian Civil War the Bolsheviks established the Soviet Union (1922). The USSR became a superpower after its victory in the Second World War (the "Great Patriotic War") at the cost of tens of millions of lives, then contested the Cold War with the United States until its dissolution in 1991, from which the Russian Federation emerged as its legal successor. In the 21st century Russia has been ruled by Vladimir Putin for most of the period; its annexation of Crimea in 2014 and full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022 drew wide international sanctions and condemnation.

Geography

Russia's terrain ranges from the tundra of the Arctic coast through taiga forest — Siberia alone contains a fifth of the world's trees — to steppe, semi-desert, and the Caucasus mountains. Major rivers include the Volga (Europe's longest), the Ob, Yenisei, and Lena. Lake Baikal in Siberia is the world's deepest and oldest freshwater lake, holding about a fifth of the world's fresh surface water.

Economy and culture

Russia is a major producer of oil, natural gas, metals, and grain, possesses the world's largest stockpile of nuclear weapons, and maintains a permanent seat on the UN Security Council. Its cultural heritage includes writers such as Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, Pushkin, and Chekhov; composers such as Tchaikovsky and Stravinsky; and pioneering achievements in ballet, cinema, and the space program — Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space in 1961.
