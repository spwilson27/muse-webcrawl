Ecatepec de Morelos

Ecatepec de Morelos is a municipality in the State of Mexico, northeast of Mexico City, and forms part of the Mexico City metropolitan area. Its seat is San Cristóbal Ecatepec. With a population of roughly 1.6 million, it is one of the most populous municipalities in Mexico.

Geography and history

The name Ecatepec comes from Nahuatl Ehécatl (wind, and the wind god) and tépetl (hill): "hill of the wind." The suffix honours José María Morelos, the independence hero who was executed there in 1815. The area has pre-Hispanic roots as an Aztec altepetl controlling a causeway across Lake Texcoco, and it grew explosively in the late twentieth century as Mexico City expanded.

Transport and society

Ecatepec is served by Mexico City Metro Line B, suburban highways, and the Mexicable aerial gondola system, opened in 2016 as the first of its kind in Mexico. The municipality faces significant socioeconomic challenges and has frequently been in the news over public security and infrastructure issues.
