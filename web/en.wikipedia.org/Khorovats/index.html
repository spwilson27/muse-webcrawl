Khorovats

Course: Main dish
Place of origin: Armenia
Serving temperature: Hot

Khorovats (Armenian: խորոված) is an Armenian dish of barbecued or grilled meat, comparable to shashlik. It is a centrepiece of gatherings and celebrations in Armenia and the Armenian diaspora, traditionally cooked outdoors over a manghal (brazier).

Preparation

Cubes of meat — most often pork, but also lamb, beef or chicken — are marinated, commonly with onion, salt, pepper and herbs, threaded onto skewers (shampur) and grilled over charcoal or wood embers. Vegetables such as tomatoes, peppers, aubergines and onions are often grilled alongside.

Serving

Khorovats is served hot, typically wrapped or laid on lavash flatbread that absorbs the juices, accompanied by grilled vegetables, fresh herbs, onion and salads. The cook who tends the skewers, the varpet, holds an honoured role at the feast.
