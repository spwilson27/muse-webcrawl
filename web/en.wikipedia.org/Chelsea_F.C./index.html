Chelsea F.C.

Chelsea Football Club is a professional football club based in Fulham, London, founded in 1905. The club plays at Stamford Bridge and competes in the Premier League.

History

Chelsea won their first major trophy, the League championship, in 1955. The club became a cup side of note in the 1960s and 1970s, winning the FA Cup in 1970 and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1971.

Abramovich era and after

Bought by Roman Abramovich in 2003, Chelsea under José Mourinho won back-to-back league titles in 2005 and 2006, and won the UEFA Champions League in 2012 under Roberto Di Matteo. A second Champions League title followed in 2021 under Thomas Tuchel. In 2022 the club was sold to a consortium led by Todd Boehly and Clearlake Capital.

Honours

Chelsea have won six league titles, eight FA Cups, five League Cups, two Champions Leagues, two Europa Leagues and the FIFA Club World Cup.
