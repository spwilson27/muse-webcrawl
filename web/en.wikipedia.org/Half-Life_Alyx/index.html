Half-Life: Alyx

Half-Life: Alyx is a 2020 virtual reality first-person shooter developed and published by Valve. It is the fifth main instalment in the Half-Life series and the first since Half-Life 2: Episode Two in 2007. The game was released for Windows on 23 March 2020 and supports most PC-compatible VR headsets.

Plot

Set five years before the events of Half-Life 2, the game follows Alyx Vance and her father Eli as members of a resistance movement against the alien Combine occupying Earth. Working with fellow resistance member Russell, Alyx attempts to rescue Eli after his capture and to steal a Combine superweapon held in a floating vault above City 17. The ending revises the conclusion of Episode Two, with the G-Man intervening in events.

Gameplay

The game is played entirely in virtual reality. Players use motion controllers to interact with the environment — grabbing, throwing and catching objects — and use "gravity gloves" to pull distant items toward them. Combat involves Combine soldiers and headcrab zombies; weapons are wielded one-handed and upgraded at fabrication stations. Reviews praised the game's detailed physics interactions, level design and production values, and it is frequently cited as a showcase ("killer app") for virtual reality.

Development and reception

Valve developed Alyx on the Source 2 engine beginning around 2016, after concluding that a new Half-Life game should push VR forward rather than be a conventional flat-screen title. The game received universal acclaim, won Best VR/AR at The Game Awards 2020, and significantly boosted sales of the Valve Index headset, which was bundled with it.
