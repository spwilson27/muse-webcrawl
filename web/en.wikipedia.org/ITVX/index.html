ITVX

ITVX is a British over-the-top streaming service operated by ITV plc. It launched on 8 December 2022, replacing ITV Hub as the broadcaster's main online platform. ITVX offers free, advertising-supported streaming of ITV's television channels and catch-up content, together with box sets, films, live channels, and a paid premium tier that removes advertising and provides additional content including BritBox programming.

Launch and content

ITVX was announced in March 2022 as the centrepiece of ITV's digital strategy, with a plan to roughly double its investment in streaming content. At launch the service offered around 9,000 hours of programming — substantially more than ITV Hub — and a strategy of premiering certain series in full on ITVX weeks or months before their linear broadcast. Original commissions for the platform included the dramas A Spy Among Friends, Nolly and The Twelve.

The service streams all of ITV's channels (ITV1, ITV2, ITV3, ITV4, ITVBe and CITV at launch) live, and includes curated themed "FAST" channels. ITVX Premium, the subscription tier, folds in the catalogue of BritBox UK, which ITV had fully acquired in 2022; the standalone BritBox service in the UK was closed and its subscribers migrated to ITVX.

Reception and usage

ITV reported strong growth in streaming hours following the launch, crediting ITVX with significant increases in both monthly active users and digital revenue. The platform is available on smart TVs, games consoles, mobile devices, and via Freeview Play, Freesat, Sky, Virgin Media and other television platforms in the United Kingdom.
