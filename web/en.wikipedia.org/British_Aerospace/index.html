British Aerospace (BAe) was a British aircraft, munitions and defence-systems manufacturer created in 1977 by the nationalisation and merger of the British Aircraft Corporation, Hawker Siddeley Aviation, Hawker Siddeley Dynamics and Scottish Aviation. It was privatised during the 1980s.

Products

The company's products included the Hawk trainer, Harrier jump jet, Panavia Tornado and its share of the Eurofighter Typhoon, as well as airliners such as the BAe 146 and a stake in Airbus. In 1999 British Aerospace merged with Marconi Electronic Systems to form BAE Systems.
