Chicago blues

Chicago blues is a form of blues music that developed in Chicago as musicians migrating from the Mississippi Delta in the Great Migration electrified the music — amplified guitars, harmonica, piano, bass and drums — in the city's clubs and on labels such as Chess Records.

Artists including Muddy Waters, Howlin' Wolf, Little Walter, Buddy Guy and Willie Dixon shaped the sound that became a foundation of rock and roll.
