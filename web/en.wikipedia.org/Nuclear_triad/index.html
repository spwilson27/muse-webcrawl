Nuclear triad

Image placeholder: The three legs of the nuclear triad
The three components of a nuclear triad: land-based missiles, ballistic-missile submarines, and strategic bombers

A nuclear triad is a three-pronged nuclear strategy of a state armed with nuclear weapons, consisting of land-based intercontinental ballistic missiles (ICBMs), submarine-launched ballistic missiles (SLBMs) aboard ballistic missile submarines, and strategic aircraft (bombers) carrying nuclear bombs and missiles.

The purpose of having a three-pronged nuclear capability is to significantly reduce the possibility that an enemy could destroy all of a nation's nuclear forces in a first-strike attack, thereby ensuring a credible threat of a second strike, and thus increasing a nation's nuclear deterrence.

Components

Land-based missiles

Land-based ICBMs, usually stationed in hardened silos or on mobile launchers, offer high readiness and rapid launch capability but have known, fixed locations that are vulnerable to attack.

Submarine-launched missiles

Nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarines (SSBNs) patrol undetected in the oceans and are considered the most survivable leg of the triad, providing an assured retaliatory capability even after a devastating first strike.

Strategic bombers

Bombers carrying gravity bombs and air-launched cruise missiles are flexible and can be recalled after launch, but are slower to reach their targets and vulnerable to air defenses.

Triad powers

The United States and Russia maintain full nuclear triads. China has developed all three components, and India has pursued a triad based on its doctrine of credible minimum deterrence. Other nuclear-armed states, such as the United Kingdom and France, rely primarily on submarine-based deterrents.

History

The triad concept emerged during the Cold War, as the United States built up land-based missiles, Polaris missile submarines, and fleets of strategic bombers such as the B-52. The diversification was intended to complicate Soviet attack planning and preserve a retaliatory force under any scenario.
