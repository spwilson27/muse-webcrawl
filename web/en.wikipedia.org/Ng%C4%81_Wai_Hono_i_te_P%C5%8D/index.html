Ngā Wai Hono i te Pō

Ngā Wai Hono i te Pō (born 13 January 1997) is the eighth Māori monarch and the second Māori queen, head of the Kīngitanga (Māori King Movement). She succeeded her father, Kīngi Tūheitia, and was crowned at Tūrangawaewae Marae in Ngāruawāhia on 5 September 2024, the day of his funeral.

Life and accession

The youngest child and only daughter of Tūheitia, she was educated at Te Whare Ūi and the University of Waikato, where she studied te reo Māori, and has worked in roles promoting Māori language and culture, including at the university and on the Waitangi Tribunal support services. Although Māori royal succession is not strictly hereditary, a council of chiefs (Tekau-mā-rua) selected her as her father's successor. Her full name, Ngā Wai Hono i te Pō — "the waters joining in the night" — was the name of her great-grandmother Te Arikinui Te Atairangikaahu's great-grandmother.
