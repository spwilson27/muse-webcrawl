Cabinet of India

The Cabinet of India, formally the Union Cabinet, is the principal decision-making body of the Government of India. It consists of the Prime Minister and senior ministers holding major portfolios, and forms the inner core of the Council of Ministers established under Articles 74 and 75 of the Constitution of India.

Constitutional basis

Article 74 of the Constitution provides for a Council of Ministers, with the Prime Minister at its head, to aid and advise the President. The Cabinet is the highest tier of that Council, above Ministers of State and Deputy Ministers. Cabinet ministers head key ministries such as Home Affairs, Finance, Defence, and External Affairs.

Functions

The Cabinet decides major questions of national policy, approves proposals for legislation, and co-ordinates the work of the ministries. Its decisions are implemented through the ministries and departments of the Government of India. The Cabinet Secretariat, headed by the Cabinet Secretary, provides administrative support and records decisions.

Meetings and committees

The Cabinet usually meets weekly in New Delhi, chaired by the Prime Minister. Much detailed work is handled by Cabinet Committees, such as the Cabinet Committee on Security, the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, and the Appointments Committee of the Cabinet.
