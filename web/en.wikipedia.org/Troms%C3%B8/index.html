Tromsø is a city and municipality in Troms county, Norway. Located about 350 km north of the Arctic Circle, it is one of the largest cities in the Nordic countries at that latitude and a major centre for Arctic research and tourism.

Geography and climate

The city centre lies on the island of Tromsøya, connected to the mainland by the Tromsø Bridge and tunnel. Despite its latitude, its climate is moderated by the North Atlantic Current, with relatively mild winters. Tromsø experiences the midnight sun from May to July and a polar night period in winter, when it is a prime destination for viewing the aurora borealis.

History and institutions

Granted city status in 1794, Tromsø grew as a base for Arctic hunting and expeditions. It hosts the University of Tromsø (UiT – The Arctic University of Norway), the Norwegian Polar Institute, the Arctic Cathedral (1965) and the world's northernmost botanical garden.
