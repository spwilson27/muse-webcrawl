List of political parties by country

This is a list of political parties by country, organised by party system. Political systems range from multi-party systems, in which many parties compete and coalition governments are common, to two-party systems dominated by two major parties, and one-party states in which a single party is legally or practically the only party permitted to govern. Some states, such as several Gulf monarchies, have no political parties at all.

Party systems

Political scientists classify party systems using criteria such as the effective number of parties and the pattern of competition. Dominant-party systems, such as Japan under the LDP or Singapore under the PAP, permit opposition parties but see one party win most elections. Non-partisan systems — used historically in some territories and still found in a few states and in many local governments — elect individuals without party labels.

International party organisations

Parties of similar ideology often cooperate internationally, for example through the Socialist International, the International Democracy Union (conservative), Liberal International, and the Global Greens.
