Mashhad

CountryIran
ProvinceRazavi Khorasan
PopulationOver 3 million

Mashhad is the second-most-populous city in Iran and the capital of Razavi Khorasan Province, in the northeast of the country. It is best known as the site of the Imam Reza shrine, one of the holiest places in Shia Islam, which draws tens of millions of pilgrims each year.

History

Mashhad grew around the village of Sanabad, where Imam Reza, the eighth Shia Imam, was martyred in 818 CE and buried. The city later flourished under the Safavids and was briefly the capital of Iran under Nader Shah in the 18th century.

Economy and culture

Besides religious tourism, Mashhad is known for saffron, carpets, and leather goods, and for the tomb of the poet Ferdowsi at nearby Tus.
