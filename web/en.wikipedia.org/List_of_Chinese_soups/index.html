List of Chinese soups

Chinese cuisine includes a large number of soups, ranging from clear broths served throughout a meal to thickened soups and elaborate banquet dishes. This article is a list of notable Chinese soups.

Notable soups

  • Hot and sour soup — a peppery, vinegar-soured soup with wood ear mushrooms, bamboo shoots, tofu and egg.
  • Egg drop soup — beaten egg streamed into hot chicken broth, forming delicate ribbons.
  • Wonton soup — pork or shrimp wontons in a light broth.
  • West Lake beef soup — a thickened soup with minced beef, egg white and coriander.
  • Buddha Jumps Over the Wall — a luxurious Fujian soup with many premium ingredients, including shark fin and abalone, long simmered.
  • Bird's nest soup — a delicacy made from swiftlet nests.
  • Shark fin soup — a banquet soup (increasingly avoided for conservation reasons).
  • Winter melon soup — winter melon cooked in broth, sometimes in the melon shell.
  • Gingeng chicken soup (jī tāng) — slow-cooked chicken with medicinal herbs.
  • Corn soup — chicken and sweet corn soup, thickened with cornstarch.
  • Crossing-the-bridge noodles — a Yunnan rice noodle soup assembled at the table.
  • Bak kut teh — a pork rib soup in herbal broth, popular with Chinese communities in Malaysia and Singapore.
