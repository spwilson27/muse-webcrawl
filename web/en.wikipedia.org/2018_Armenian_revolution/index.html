2018 Armenian revolution

Date31 March – 8 May 2018
LocationArmenia
CauseSerzh Sargsyan's move to become prime minister
ResultSargsyan resigned; Nikol Pashinyan became PM

The 2018 Armenian revolution, most commonly known as the Velvet Revolution, was a series of anti-government protests in Armenia from April to May 2018 staged by various political and civil groups led by opposition MP Nikol Pashinyan.

Background

Protests erupted after Serzh Sargsyan, who had served two terms as president, was nominated as prime minister — a move seen as a way to retain power after constitutional changes shifted authority to the premiership. Pashinyan began a protest march from Gyumri to the capital, Yerevan, drawing growing crowds.

Outcome

Following mass demonstrations, general strikes and the defection of some soldiers to the protesters, Sargsyan resigned on 23 April 2018, famously stating that "Nikol Pashinyan was right. I was wrong." On 8 May 2018 the National Assembly elected Pashinyan as prime minister. The transition was largely peaceful, hence the name "Velvet Revolution".
