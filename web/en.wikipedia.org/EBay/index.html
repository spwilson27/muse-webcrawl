eBay Inc. is an American multinational e-commerce company based in San Jose, California, that facilitates consumer-to-consumer and business-to-consumer sales through its website. eBay was founded by Pierre Omidyar in 1995 as AuctionWeb, one of the first online auction websites, and became a notable success story of the dot-com bubble.

eBay is one of the world's largest online marketplaces.

History

Omidyar launched AuctionWeb in September 1995; one of the first items sold was a broken laser pointer, which fetched $14.83. The site was renamed eBay in 1997, after Omidyar's consulting firm, Echo Bay Technology Group. The company went public in 1998. Meg Whitman served as CEO from 1998 to 2008, overseeing rapid growth. eBay acquired the online payment company PayPal in 2002 and spun it off again in 2015.

How it works

Sellers list items either in an auction-style format, in which buyers bid over a set period, or at a fixed price using "Buy It Now". eBay charges sellers insertion and final value fees. Buyers and sellers rate each other through a feedback system that underpins trust on the platform. The site is free for buyers to use.

Scope

eBay operates localized websites in many countries and is one of the largest e-commerce platforms in the world, hosting hundreds of millions of listings across categories from collectibles and electronics to vehicles and fashion. In recent years it has emphasized fixed-price sales, authenticated luxury goods, and refurbished products.
