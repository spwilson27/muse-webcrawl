First Philippine mass (disambiguation)

First Philippine mass may refer to:

  • First Mass in the Philippines — the first Roman Catholic Mass celebrated in the Philippine islands, held on Easter Sunday, 31 March 1521, by Father Pedro de Valderrama, chaplain of Ferdinand Magellan's expedition.
  • Limasawa — the island in Southern Leyte identified by the National Historical Commission of the Philippines as the site of the 1521 Mass, the traditional identification supported by the accounts of Antonio Pigafetta.
  • Butuan — the rival claimant location, whose supporters argued that the island called "Mazaua" in the expedition chronicles lay near Butuan in Mindanao rather than at Limasawa; the controversy was formally reviewed several times in the 20th and 21st centuries, with official panels reaffirming Limasawa.

Background

The debate turns on interpretation of Pigafetta's chronicle and maps of the Magellan–Elcano circumnavigation: coordinates, island names and sailing times recorded in the sources have been read to support both sites. The question matters to regional heritage and to the commemoration of the introduction of Christianity to the Philippines, marked prominently during the 2021 quincentennial celebrations.
