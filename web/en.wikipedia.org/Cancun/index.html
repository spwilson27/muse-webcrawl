Cancun

Cancun is an English-language rendering of the name of the Mexican city of Cancún in the state of Quintana Roo on the northeast coast of the Yucatán Peninsula. In Spanish the name carries an accent on the final syllable; English-language publications, travel media, and older reference works frequently omit the accent, writing "Cancun". The city itself is treated in the main article under its Spanish name.

Usage

The unaccented form predominates in the branding of international hotel chains, airline schedules, and spring-break travel marketing aimed at the United States, where the resort is a famous destination for beach tourism. Spanish-language sources almost invariably use Cancún.
