List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions

The Formula One World Drivers' Championship (WDC) is awarded by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) to the most successful Formula One racing car driver over a season, as determined by a points system based on individual Grand Prix results. The championship has been awarded since the inaugural season in 1950.

First champions

Giuseppe Farina won the first championship in 1950 for Alfa Romeo, followed by Alberto Ascari's back-to-back titles in 1952 and 1953. Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton share the record of seven titles each.
