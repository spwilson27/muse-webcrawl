Evolution of birds

The evolution of birds began in the Jurassic Period, with the earliest birds evolving from a group of small feathered theropod dinosaurs. Birds are regarded by many palaeontologists as the only surviving lineage of dinosaurs.

Origins

Archaeopteryx, known from the Late Jurassic of Germany (~150 million years ago), is widely considered the earliest known bird, combining feathers and wings with teeth, clawed fingers and a long bony tail.

Diversification

During the Cretaceous, birds diversified into forms including the toothed Enantiornithes and Hesperornithiformes. The ancestors of modern birds (Neornithes) survived the Cretaceous–Paleogene extinction event 66 million years ago and radiated rapidly afterwards into the more than 10,000 living species.
