The 1998 Winter Olympics, officially the XVIII Olympic Winter Games, were held from 7 to 22 February 1998 in Nagano, Japan. They were the last Winter Olympics of the 20th century and the third Olympic Games held in Japan.

Highlights

Ice hockey's "Dream Team" era began as NHL professionals competed for the first time; the Czech Republic, led by goaltender Dominik Hašek, won gold. Women's ice hockey, curling, and snowboarding made their Olympic debuts. Norwegian cross-country skier Bjørn Dæhlie won three gold medals to become the most decorated Winter Olympian at the time. Fifteen-year-old Tara Lipinski became the youngest ladies' figure skating champion. Germany topped the medal table.

Controversy

After the Games it emerged that Nagano bid officials had spent lavishly entertaining IOC members and destroyed bid records, contributing to the IOC corruption scandal and subsequent reforms.
