75th Ranger Regiment

The 75th Ranger Regiment, also known as the Army Rangers, is the United States Army's premier light infantry special operations unit within the U.S. Army Special Operations Command (USASOC). Headquartered at Fort Moore, Georgia, the regiment consists of three Ranger battalions and support elements, each able to deploy within 18 hours of notification.

Mission

The regiment specializes in direct action, airfield seizure, special reconnaissance, and personnel recovery, typically in support of joint special operations. Its motto is "Rangers Lead the Way!", earned during the Normandy landings of 1944.

History

Ranger units fought in World War II — including the assault on Pointe du Hoc on D-Day — and in Korea, Vietnam, Grenada, Panama, and Somalia, where Rangers fought in the 1993 Battle of Mogadishu. The modern regiment was constituted in 1974 with the activation of its first battalions; a regimental headquarters followed in 1984. Since 2001, Ranger battalions have rotated almost continuously through Afghanistan and Iraq.

Selection and training

Volunteers must complete the Ranger Assessment and Selection Program (RASP) and meet demanding physical standards; leaders also graduate from the U.S. Army Ranger School at Fort Moore, earning the Ranger tab.
