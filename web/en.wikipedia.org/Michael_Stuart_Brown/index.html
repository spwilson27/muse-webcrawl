Michael Stuart Brown

Michael Stuart Brown (born April 13, 1941) is an American geneticist and Nobel laureate. With Joseph L. Goldstein, he discovered the LDL receptor and elucidated regulation of cholesterol metabolism, work awarded the 1985 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

Education and career

Brown studied at the University of Pennsylvania and its medical school. At UT Southwestern in Dallas, his long collaboration with Goldstein used cells from patients with familial hypercholesterolemia to reveal receptor-mediated endocytosis of low-density lipoprotein.

Research

Their work explained how cells take up cholesterol, why genetic defects raise blood cholesterol and heart-attack risk, and paved the way for statin drugs. Later research identified SREBP transcription factors controlling lipid synthesis.

Honours

Brown is a member of the National Academy of Sciences and received the National Medal of Science.
