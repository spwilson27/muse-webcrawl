Aegis Ballistic Missile Defense System

The Aegis Ballistic Missile Defense System is a United States programme, run by the Missile Defense Agency, that adapts the naval Aegis combat system to intercept ballistic missiles in their midcourse and terminal phases.

Components

The system centres on the AN/SPY-1 radar and Standard Missile interceptors, principally the SM-3, launched from Mk 41 vertical launchers on cruisers and destroyers. The shore-based Aegis Ashore variant has been deployed in Romania and Poland.

Deployments

Aegis BMD ships are operated by the US Navy and Japan's Maritime Self-Defense Force, among others, and form the sea-based leg of the European Phased Adaptive Approach. See also Aegis Combat System and Missile defense.
