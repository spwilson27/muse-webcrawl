The UEFA Conference League is an annual football competition organised by UEFA for eligible European football clubs. It is the third tier of continental club football in Europe, behind the Champions League and the Europa League. Introduced for the 2021–22 season as the UEFA Europa Conference League, it was renamed the UEFA Conference League from 2024–25.

Format and purpose

The competition was created so that clubs from lower-ranked UEFA member associations would have a more realistic chance of progressing beyond qualification rounds and playing group/league-phase football. Teams enter through their domestic league placings and cup results, and from elimination in Champions League and Europa League qualifying. The winner qualifies for the following season's Europa League league phase.

History

The inaugural final, in Tirana in May 2022, was won by Roma, who beat Feyenoord 1–0 under José Mourinho — making Mourinho the first coach to win all three current UEFA club competitions. West Ham United won in 2023, Olympiacos won the first European trophy for a Greek club in 2024 in Athens, and Chelsea won in 2025. From 2024–25 the competition adopted UEFA's new "Swiss-style" single league phase with 36 teams.
