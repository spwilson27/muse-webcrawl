President of Nigeria

The President of Nigeria is the head of state and head of government of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, and commander-in-chief of the Nigerian Armed Forces. The president is elected by popular vote for a four-year term, renewable once.

History

Nigeria became a republic in 1963 with Nnamdi Azikiwe as its first president, initially a largely ceremonial office. Periods of military rule alternated with civilian republics. Since the return to democracy in 1999, presidents have included Olusegun Obasanjo, Umaru Musa Yar'Adua, Goodluck Jonathan, Muhammadu Buhari, and Bola Tinubu, who took office in 2023.

Powers and election

To be elected, a candidate must win the most votes nationally and at least 25 percent of the vote in two-thirds of Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. The president appoints ministers, subject to Senate confirmation, and directs the executive branch.
