Garrett AiResearch was an American aerospace manufacturer founded by Cliff Garrett in Los Angeles in 1936. It became known for aircraft pressurisation and air-conditioning systems, turbochargers, auxiliary power units and small gas-turbine engines.

Development

The company expanded during the Second World War with cabin-pressure and cooling equipment for high-altitude aircraft. Postwar work included turboprop engines such as the TPE331, the GTCP family of auxiliary power units and components for military and civil aircraft.

Corporate history

AiResearch was a principal part of the Garrett Corporation. After mergers involving Signal Companies and AlliedSignal, its aerospace businesses ultimately became part of Honeywell. The Garrett name also became strongly associated with automotive turbochargers in a separate line of business.

Legacy

Garrett systems helped make high-altitude flight routine by managing cabin pressure, bleed air and onboard electrical and pneumatic power. Its APUs remain a standard feature of airliners and business jets.
