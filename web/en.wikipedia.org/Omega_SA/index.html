Omega SA is a Swiss luxury watchmaker founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt. Based in Biel/Bienne and now part of the Swatch Group, Omega is one of the most famous watch brands in the world, known for precision records, Olympic timekeeping, and its association with spaceflight and James Bond films.

History

The company adopted the name Omega in 1903 after its highly successful Omega calibre of 1894. Omega watches won numerous observatory trials for accuracy, and the brand became the official timekeeper of the Olympic Games from 1932 onward. In 1999 Omega introduced the co-axial escapement, invented by George Daniels, into mass production.

Speedmaster and the Moon

The Omega Speedmaster, launched in 1957, was chosen by NASA for manned spaceflight; Buzz Aldrin wore a Speedmaster Professional on the lunar surface during Apollo 11 in 1969, earning it the name "Moonwatch" — the first watch worn on the Moon. Omega Seamaster models have been worn by James Bond since GoldenEye (1995).
