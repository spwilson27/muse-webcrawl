Gherardini family

The Gherardini were a noble family of Florence in Tuscany.

The Gherardini family is an Italian noble family originally from Tuscany, attested in Florence from the early Middle Ages. Traditions trace their origins to the feudal lords of the Chianti region, where they held castles and lands around Montagliari and Linari.

History

Members of the family held offices in the Florentine Republic, though like other magnate families they were periodically excluded from power under the Ordinances of Justice. The family name is best known to modern audiences through Lisa Gherardini, wife of the Florentine merchant Francesco del Giocondo, who sat for Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa in the early sixteenth century.

Irish branch

A longstanding family tradition, recorded in later genealogies, holds that members of the Gherardini emigrated to Ireland in the twelfth century with the Norman invasion and became progenitors of the great Anglo-Norman FitzGerald dynasty. The claim — deriving "FitzGerald" from "figli di Gherardini" ("sons of Gherardini") — is treated cautiously by historians, though it was discussed in print as early as the seventeenth century.
