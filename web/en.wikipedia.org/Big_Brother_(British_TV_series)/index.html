Big Brother is a British reality game show in which a number of contestants, referred to as housemates, live in isolation from the outside world in a custom-built "house", competing to avoid eviction by public vote and remain to win a cash prize. It first aired on Channel 4 on 18 July 2000.

History

Presented by Davina McCall, the original Channel 4 run became a defining programme of British popular culture in the 2000s, producing celebrity editions and the spin-off Big Brother's Little Brother. After Channel 4 ended the show in 2010, it moved to Channel 5 from 2011 to 2018. ITV revived the format on ITV2 in 2023, presented by AJ Odudu and Will Best.

Format

Housemates nominate each other for eviction, and viewers vote to evict (or, in some series, to save). Tasks, secret missions, and twists such as fake evictions shape the narrative. The show's narration by Marcus Bentley, famous for his "Day X in the Big Brother house" announcements, has been a constant feature.

Cultural impact

Big Brother transformed British television, launching debates about fame, surveillance, and reality TV ethics, and several controversies — notably the 2007 Celebrity Big Brother racism row — became national news. Memorable housemates such as Jade Goody and the first winner, Craig Phillips, became household names.
