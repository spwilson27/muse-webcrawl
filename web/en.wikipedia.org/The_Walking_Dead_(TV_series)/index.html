The Walking Dead is an American post-apocalyptic horror drama television series developed by Frank Darabont for AMC, based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore and Charlie Adlard. It premiered on 31 October 2010 and ran for eleven seasons until 20 November 2022.

Overview

Former sheriff's deputy Rick Grimes (Andrew Lincoln) wakes from a coma to find civilisation destroyed by a zombie apocalypse. Leading a group of survivors through Georgia and Virginia, the group faces "walkers" and increasingly brutal human antagonists, including the Governor, the cannibals of Terminus, and Negan and the Saviors. Later stars include Norman Reedus as Daryl Dixon and Melissa McBride as Carol Peletier.

Reception and spin-offs

For several years the series was the most-watched show on American cable television. It spawned a franchise of spin-offs including Fear the Walking Dead, The Walking Dead: World Beyond, Tales of the Walking Dead, Dead City and Daryl Dixon.
