Poker is a family of comparing card games in which players wager over which hand is best according to that specific game's rules. Players bet into a central pot, and the pot is won either by the player with the best hand at showdown or by the player who makes a bet that no other player calls (folds to).

History

Poker developed in the United States in the early 19th century from earlier European games such as poque and brag. It spread via Mississippi riverboats and during the Civil War, evolving from a 20-card game to the full 52-card deck, with the draw and later stud variants. In the 1970s community-card variants rose to prominence.

Popular variants

The most popular variant today is Texas hold 'em, the main event game of the World Series of Poker, founded in 1970 in Las Vegas. Other common variants include Omaha, seven-card stud, and five-card draw. Poker experienced a global boom in the 2000s driven by televised tournaments, the 2003 World Series win by amateur Chris Moneymaker, and online poker.

Hand rankings

Standard rankings run from high card through pairs, two pair, three of a kind, straights, flushes, full houses, and four of a kind, to the straight flush and royal flush.
