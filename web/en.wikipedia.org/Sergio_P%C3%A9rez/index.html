Sergio Pérez

Born26 January 1990, Guadalajara, Mexico
NationalityMexican
CareerFormula One driver (2011–2024)

Sergio Michel Pérez Mendoza (born 1990), nicknamed "Checo", is a Mexican racing driver who competed in Formula One from 2011 to 2024. He drove for Sauber, McLaren, Force India/Racing Point and Red Bull Racing, winning six Grands Prix, including the 2020 Sakhir Grand Prix — Mexico's first F1 win in 50 years — and the prestigious Monaco Grand Prix in 2022.

Career highlights

Pérez earned a reputation for exceptional tyre management and defensive driving. With Red Bull from 2021 he helped the team win constructors' championships and finished runner-up in the 2023 drivers' championship. He and Red Bull parted ways after the 2024 season.
