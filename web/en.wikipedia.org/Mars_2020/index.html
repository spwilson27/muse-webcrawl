Mars 2020 is a NASA Mars rover mission that launched on 30 July 2020 and landed in Jezero crater on 18 February 2021. Its principal component is the Perseverance rover, which carries the Ingenuity helicopter technology demonstration and a suite of instruments to search for signs of ancient microbial life and to cache samples for possible return to Earth.

Objectives

The mission studies the geology of an ancient lake and river delta in Jezero crater, assesses past habitability, seeks biosignatures in Martian rocks, and collects sealed tubes of rock and regolith samples intended for retrieval by a future sample-return campaign. The MOXIE experiment aboard Perseverance also demonstrated producing oxygen from the carbon dioxide atmosphere.

Ingenuity

The Ingenuity helicopter made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet on 19 April 2021, eventually completing 72 flights before its mission ended in January 2024 after rotor damage.
