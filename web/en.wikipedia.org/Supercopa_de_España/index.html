Supercopa de España

The Supercopa de España (Spanish Super Cup) is a super cup tournament in Spanish football. Founded in 1982, it was originally contested between the winners of La Liga and the Copa del Rey over two legs.

Current format

Since the 2019–20 edition, the competition has been expanded to four teams — the winners and runners-up of La Liga and the Copa del Rey — playing semi-finals and a final in a single host location. Under a long-term agreement, editions have been staged in Saudi Arabia, typically in January.

History and records

The competition had predecessors including the Copa de los Campeones (1940), Copa de Oro Argentina (1945), and the Copa Eva Duarte (1947–1953). FC Barcelona is the most successful club in the tournament, followed by Real Madrid. Lionel Messi is the competition's all-time top scorer and its most decorated player.
