Naval Submarine Base New London

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Naval Submarine Base New London (SUBASE NLON) is the United States Navy's primary East Coast submarine base, located in Groton, Connecticut, on the Thames River. Known as the "Submarine Capital of the World", it is homeport to attack submarines of the Atlantic Fleet and hosts the Naval Submarine School.

History

The site began as a naval station in 1868, when Connecticut and the city of New London donated land to the Navy. It served as a coaling station before becoming a submarine base in 1915, when the first submarines, escorted by the monitor Ozark, arrived and the Submarine School was established in 1916. The base grew substantially during the two world wars and the Cold War.

Tenants and facilities

The base covers roughly 687 acres and supports more than 70 tenant commands, including Submarine Squadron 12, the Naval Submarine Medical Research Laboratory, and the Naval Submarine School, which trains enlisted sailors and officers for submarine duty. The Submarine Force Library and Museum, home of USS Nautilus (SSN-571), the world's first nuclear-powered submarine, lies just outside the base.

2005 closure threat

The base was recommended for closure by the 2005 Base Realignment and Closure (BRAC) Commission but was removed from the list, preserving thousands of jobs in southeastern Connecticut.
