Annie Wilkes

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Annie Wilkes is a fictional character created by Stephen King, the antagonist of his 1987 psychological horror novel Misery. A former nurse and obsessive fan, she rescues her favourite author, Paul Sheldon, from a car crash — then imprisons him in her remote Colorado home and forces him to write a novel reviving her beloved character, Misery Chastain.

Character

Wilkes is outwardly folksy and prudish — she dislikes profanity and says "cockadoodie" — but is violently unstable. As Paul discovers her scrapbook, he learns she is suspected of multiple murders, including infants in her care as a nurse. In the novel she amputates Paul's foot with an axe ("hobbling"); the 1990 film substituted a sledgehammer blow to the ankles.

Portrayals and reception

Kathy Bates won the Academy Award for Best Actress for playing Wilkes in Rob Reiner's film adaptation Misery (1990), the only Oscar-winning performance in a King adaptation. The character has been ranked among the greatest screen villains, and Lizzy Caplan portrayed a younger Annie Wilkes in the second season of the television series Castle Rock (2019).
