Hoh River

Hoh River
Location: Olympic Peninsula, Washington, United States
Length: about 56 miles (90 km)
Mouth: Pacific Ocean

The Hoh River is a river on the Olympic Peninsula in western Washington state in the United States. It drains a mountainous, heavily forested watershed on the west slope of the Olympic Mountains and flows into the Pacific Ocean.

Course

The river originates from glaciers on Mount Olympus, including the Hoh Glacier, and flows west through Olympic National Park and the Hoh Rainforest, one of the largest temperate rainforests in the United States.

Ecology and people

The Hoh watershed supports salmon, old-growth conifers, Roosevelt elk, and lush moss-draped forest. The lower river lies within the reservation and traditional territory of the Hoh Tribe, whose name is associated with the river.
