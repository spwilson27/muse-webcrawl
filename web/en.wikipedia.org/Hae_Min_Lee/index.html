Hae Min Lee

Hae Min Lee (이해민; born October 15, 1980) was a Korean-American high school student at Woodlawn High School in Baltimore, Maryland, who was murdered in January 1999. Her body was found the following month in Leakin Park, and she had been killed by manual strangulation. Her ex-boyfriend, Adnan Syed, was convicted of first-degree murder in 2000 and sentenced to life in prison.

Case and aftermath

The case gained renewed international attention in 2014 when it became the subject of the first season of the podcast Serial, which raised questions about the evidence and the effectiveness of Syed's legal representation. After years of appeals, a Baltimore judge vacated Syed's conviction in September 2022, citing unreliable evidence and failures to disclose exculpatory material, and prosecutors dropped the charges the following month. In subsequent proceedings, a Maryland appellate court reinstated the conviction on procedural grounds while leaving Syed free, and the case continued through further reviews.
