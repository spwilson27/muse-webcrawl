Diwan (school)

Diwan is a federation of schools in Brittany, France, that teach through the medium of the Breton language using immersion methods. The first Diwan school was founded in 1977 at Lampaul-Ploudalmézeau by Reun L'Hostis and others concerned about the decline of Breton.

History

The Diwan movement began with a small nursery school and grew into a network spanning preschool through secondary education, including a lycée at Carhaix. The schools are secular, free, and open to all, and they operate outside the French public school system, though agreements with the state have provided some public funding and recognition of teacher training.

Teaching method

Diwan schools use total immersion in Breton in the early years, introducing French progressively. The model is similar to that of the Gaelscoileanna in Ireland, the Ikastolak in the Basque Country, and Welsh-medium education in Wales.
