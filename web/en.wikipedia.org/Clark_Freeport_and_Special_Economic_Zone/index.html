Clark Freeport and Special Economic Zone

Clark Freeport Zone
Location: Pampanga and Tarlac, Philippines
Established: 1993
Administered by: Clark Development Corporation

The Clark Freeport and Special Economic Zone, often shortened to Clark Freeport Zone, is a freeport and special economic zone in the Philippines spanning parts of Angeles City, Mabalacat and Porac in Pampanga and Capas and Bamban in Tarlac. It occupies much of the former United States Clark Air Base, which was turned over to the Philippines after the eruption of Mount Pinatubo and the expiry of the bases agreement in 1991.

History

The zone was created under Republic Act No. 7227 (the Bases Conversion and Development Act of 1992) and established in 1993 under the administration of the Bases Conversion and Development Authority and its operating subsidiary, the Clark Development Corporation. Registered enterprises enjoy tax and duty-free privileges on imports and simplified business procedures.

Economy

Clark hosts hundreds of locators in manufacturing, electronics, aviation, tourism, logistics and business process outsourcing, employing well over a hundred thousand workers. It is served by Clark International Airport, which has been expanded as an alternative gateway to Metro Manila, and is the site of the New Clark City development to the north.
