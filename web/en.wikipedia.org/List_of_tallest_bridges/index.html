List of tallest bridges

This list ranks the world's tallest bridges by structural height, measured from the lowest visible point of the foundation to the highest visible point of the structure. Structural height differs from deck height: a bridge may tower high above a gorge on tall piers while its towers are comparatively short. Many of the tallest bridges are found in China, where deep river gorges in mountainous provinces such as Guizhou have prompted record-breaking designs.

Highest bridges

The highest bridges, measured by deck height above the ground or water below, are led by China's Beipanjiang and Duge bridges over the Beipan River canyon. The Duge Bridge, opened in 2016, has a deck 565 metres above the river, making it the highest bridge in the world by deck height.
