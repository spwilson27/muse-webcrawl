Track racing

Track racing is a form of motorsport that takes place on purpose-built closed circuits, as distinct from road racing on public roads or rallying on mixed surfaces. The term is also used for bicycle racing on velodromes (track cycling) and for athletics events run on a running track.

Motor racing

Circuit racing includes categories such as Formula One, touring car racing, sports car racing, and motorcycle road racing on paved circuits. Tracks vary from permanent facilities such as Silverstone and Spa-Francorchamps to temporary street circuits. Races are typically run over a set number of laps, with pit stops for fuel and tyres in longer events.

Track cycling

In cycling, track racing takes place on banked velodromes and includes sprint, keirin, pursuit, points race, scratch race, and madison events, contested on fixed-gear bicycles without brakes.

Athletics

In athletics, track events include sprints, middle- and long-distance races, hurdles, steeplechase, and relays, run on a standard 400-metre oval track.
