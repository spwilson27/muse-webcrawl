Ben Horowitz

Ben Horowitz (born June 13, 1966) is an American businessman, investor and author. He is a co-founder, with Marc Andreessen, of the venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z).

Early life and education

Horowitz was born in London and raised in Berkeley, California. He earned a bachelor's degree in computer science from Columbia University and a master's degree from UCLA.

Career

He worked at Silicon Graphics and Netscape, where he met Andreessen. He co-founded Loudcloud, which became Opsware and was sold to Hewlett-Packard for $1.6 billion in 2007. In 2009 he and Andreessen founded Andreessen Horowitz, which became one of Silicon Valley's most influential venture firms, investing in companies including Facebook, Twitter, Airbnb and Coinbase.

Books

He is the author of The Hard Thing About Hard Things (2014), about the difficulties of running a company, and What You Do Is Who You Are (2019), about organisational culture.
