Open Source Initiative

The Open Source Initiative (OSI) is an American public-benefit nonprofit organisation that actively promotes and maintains the Open Source Definition and reviews and approves software licences as conforming to it. It was founded in February 1998 by Bruce Perens and Eric S. Raymond, following the coinage of the term open source by Christine Peterson.

The OSI was created in part to present free software concepts in business-friendly terms. It maintains opensource.org, runs licence-discussion review, certifies marks, and has more recently worked on defining open source artificial intelligence (the OSAID, released in 2024).

Governance

The OSI is governed by a board of directors elected by affiliate and individual members, with corporate sponsors. Perens left in 1999; other presidents have included Raymond, Michael Tiemann, Stefano Zacchiroli and Josh Simmons.
