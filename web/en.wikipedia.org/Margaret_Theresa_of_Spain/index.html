Margaret Theresa of Spain (1651–1673) was, by marriage to her maternal uncle and paternal cousin Leopold I, Holy Roman Empress and Queen consort of Germany, Hungary and Bohemia. She was the daughter of King Philip IV of Spain and Mariana of Austria.

Life

Margaret Theresa is best known as the central figure of Diego Velázquez's masterpiece Las Meninas (1656), painted when she was five years old, and of several other court portraits showing the blonde infanta in elaborate hoop skirts. Betrothed from childhood to Leopold I, she married him in Vienna in 1666 amid splendid festivities.

Death and legacy

Weakened by repeated pregnancies and miscarriages, she died aged 21 after the birth of her fourth surviving pregnancy. Her daughter Maria Antonia later carried the Spanish Habsburg claim into the Bavarian Wittelsbach line.
