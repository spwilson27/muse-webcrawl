Le Vaillant is a French-language Catholic weekly newspaper published in Mauritius. It was founded in 1967 by the Diocese of Port-Louis and takes its name from the parish magazine tradition; Le Vaillant replaced the bulletin La Croix du Sud tradition of church publications on the island and became the principal print organ of the Mauritian Catholic Church.

The paper publishes news of the diocese and parishes, commentary on Mauritian social and political affairs from the standpoint of Catholic social teaching, and material on education, family life and interfaith relations in Mauritius's plural society. Long associated with the episcopate of Cardinal Jean Margéot, the first Mauritian cardinal, Le Vaillant played a notable role in public discussion during the decades after independence in 1968, addressing questions of development, communal harmony and social justice.

Like other church publications worldwide, the paper faced declining print circulation in the 21st century as audiences moved online, and has developed a digital presence alongside print distribution through parishes across Mauritius.
