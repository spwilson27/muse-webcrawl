Eastern Orthodoxy

Eastern Orthodoxy, officially the Orthodox Catholic Church, is the second-largest Christian communion, with roughly 220 million adherents worldwide, concentrated in Eastern Europe, the Balkans, the eastern Mediterranean and Russia. It understands itself as the one, holy, catholic and apostolic church founded by Jesus Christ, in continuity with the early church and the first seven ecumenical councils.

Organization

The church is a communion of autocephalous (self-governing) national churches — including the Ecumenical Patriarchate of Constantinople, whose patriarch holds a primacy of honour, and the patriarchates of Alexandria, Antioch and Jerusalem, together with the churches of Russia, Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Georgia, Greece and others — united in doctrine, sacraments and mutual recognition, rather than subject to a single earthly head.

History

Orthodoxy grew in the Greek-speaking east of the Roman Empire. Mounting differences with the Latin west over papal authority and the Filioque clause culminated in the East–West Schism of 1054. The church survived the fall of Constantinople in 1453, spread among the Slavs through the missions of Cyril and Methodius, and endured persecution under Ottoman rule and 20th-century communist regimes, after which it experienced a revival.

Theology and worship

Orthodox theology rests on Scripture and Holy Tradition, the decisions of the ecumenical councils and the consensus of the church fathers. Central features include the doctrines of theosis (divinization), the veneration of icons, and a rich liturgical life centred on the Divine Liturgy of St John Chrysostom, celebrated with chant, incense and processions behind the iconostasis. Monasticism, especially on Mount Athos, plays a major spiritual role, and the church keeps the feasts of the liturgical year culminating in Pascha (Easter).

Demographics

The largest Orthodox populations are in Russia, Ukraine, Romania, Greece, Ethiopia (Oriental Orthodox, a distinct communion) and the Balkans, with significant diaspora communities in Western Europe, the Americas and Australia.
