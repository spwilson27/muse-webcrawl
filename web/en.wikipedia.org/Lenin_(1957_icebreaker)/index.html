Lenin (1957 icebreaker)

Lenin was a Soviet nuclear-powered icebreaker, launched in 1957 as the world first nuclear-powered surface ship and the first nuclear-powered civilian vessel. She kept the Northern Sea Route open along Russia Arctic coast.

Design and service

Lenin was powered by three pressurised-water reactors driving steam turbines and electric motors on three shafts. She escorted convoys through ice for thirty years, covering some 654,000 nautical miles. Two reactor accidents in the 1960s led to a refit with two new reactors.

Preservation

Decommissioned in 1989, Lenin is preserved as a museum ship at Murmansk, her reactors removed and sealed, a monument to the Soviet atomic fleet she inaugurated.
