Rome (Italian and Latin: Roma) is the capital city of Italy. It is also the capital of the Lazio region, the centre of the Metropolitan City of Rome Capital, and a special municipality (comune). With 2,748,109 residents in 1,287.36 km², Rome is the country's most populated comune and the third most populous city in the European Union by population within city limits.

Rome is located in the central-western portion of the Italian Peninsula, along the shores of the Tiber River. Vatican City, an independent country inside the city boundaries of Rome, is the only existing example of a country within a city. Rome is often referred to as the City of Seven Hills due to its geographic location, and also as the "Eternal City".

History

Rome's history spans over 28 centuries. While Roman mythology dates the founding of Rome at around 753 BC, the site has been inhabited for much longer. Rome successively was the capital of the Roman Kingdom, the Roman Republic, and the Roman Empire, and is regarded by many as the first-ever Imperial City and metropolis. In the 1st century BC Rome was already the largest city in the world, with a population of around one million.

After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, Rome fell under Byzantine and later papal control; as seat of the Papacy it remained a pilgrimage and cultural centre through the Middle Ages and flourished again in the Renaissance, producing monuments such as St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel ceiling. In 1870 Rome was captured by the forces of the Kingdom of Italy, becoming its capital in 1871, and in 1946 it became capital of the Italian Republic.

Landmarks

Rome's historic centre, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, contains the Colosseum, the Roman Forum, the Pantheon, Castel Sant'Angelo, and numerous piazzas, fountains (including the Trevi Fountain), churches, and palazzi. The Vatican Museums and St. Peter's Basilica lie within Vatican City, an enclave of the Holy See.

Economy and culture

Rome is Italy's centre of government and a hub of film (Cinecittà studios), media, finance, and tourism — it is consistently among the most visited cities in the world, receiving tens of millions of visitors annually. It hosted the 1960 Summer Olympics and is home to the football clubs SS Lazio and AS Roma, who share the Stadio Olimpico.
