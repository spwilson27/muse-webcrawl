Chelmsford

Chelmsford is a city in Essex, England, and the county town of Essex, located about 30 miles northeast of London. It was granted city status in 2012 for the Diamond Jubilee of Elizabeth II.

Chelmsford is known as the "birthplace of radio": Guglielmo Marconi built his first wireless factory there in 1899, and the first entertainment radio broadcast in Britain was made from Chelmsford in 1920 featuring Dame Nellie Melba.

The city has Roman origins and a medieval market charter. Today it is a commuter city with a cathedral, Anglia Ruskin University campus, Essex County Cricket Club, and Hylands Park, host of the former V Festival. Population is about 180,000 in the district.
