5th Dalai Lama

Ngawang Lobzang Gyatso
Born1617, Chingwar Taktse, Tibet
Died1682, Lhasa
Reign1642–1682
Known as"The Great Fifth"

Ngawang Lobzang Gyatso (1617–1682) was the 5th Dalai Lama, remembered as "the Great Fifth" for unifying Tibet under the Gelug school and establishing the Dalai Lamas as the temporal as well as spiritual rulers of the country.

Unification of Tibet

With the military backing of the Mongol khan Güshi Khan, the Gelug school defeated its rivals in the Tibetan civil war, and in 1642 the Dalai Lama was enthroned as ruler of Tibet. His government, the Ganden Phodrang, endured until 1959. His regent, Sangay Gyatso, became a major political figure and later concealed the Dalai Lama's death for fifteen years.

Achievements

The Fifth ordered the construction of the Potala Palace on Marpo Ri hill in Lhasa, begun in 1645 and named after Mount Potalaka, the abode of Avalokiteśvara. He was a prolific writer on philosophy, history, and tantra, practiced traditions beyond the Gelug including Nyingma teachings, and maintained diplomatic relations with the Qing dynasty, visiting the Shunzhi Emperor in Beijing in 1652.
