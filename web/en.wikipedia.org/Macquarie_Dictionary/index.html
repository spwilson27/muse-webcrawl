First edition: 1981
Publisher: Macquarie Dictionary Publishers
Subject: Australian English

The Macquarie Dictionary is a dictionary of Australian English. It is generally held by universities and the legal profession to be the authoritative source on Australian spelling, and is published by Macquarie Dictionary Publishers, based at Macquarie University.

History

First published in 1981, the dictionary was compiled under the editorial direction of Arthur Delbridge. Regular new editions have followed, and each year the dictionary announces an Australian Word of the Year.

Influence

Its guidance underpins the Australian Government Style Manual and most Australian publishers' house styles.
