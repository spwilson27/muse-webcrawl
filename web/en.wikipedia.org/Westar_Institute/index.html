Westar Institute
Founded: 1985
Founder: Robert W. Funk
Type: Nonprofit research institute
Location: Salem, Oregon (formerly Willamette University)

The Westar Institute is a nonprofit, member-supported research and educational institute dedicated to the advancement of religious literacy. It was founded in 1985 by biblical scholar Robert W. Funk, who had previously founded the Society of Biblical Literature's publishing arm, Scholars Press. Westar is best known as the sponsor of the Jesus Seminar.

The Jesus Seminar and other seminars

The Jesus Seminar (1985–1998) brought together some 150 scholars and lay participants ("Fellows") to evaluate the historical authenticity of the sayings and deeds attributed to Jesus in the Gospels, voting with colored beads. Its 1993 report, The Five Gospels, concluded that only about 18 percent of the sayings attributed to Jesus were likely authentic — a result that attracted wide media attention and criticism from conservative scholars. Westar has since run seminars on the Acts of the Apostles, Paul, early Christianity, and "God and the human future," and publishes the magazine The Fourth R and journals through Cascade Books.
