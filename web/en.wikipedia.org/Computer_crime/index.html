Computer crime

Computer crime, or cybercrime, is a crime involving a computer or computer network, where the computer may have been used in the commission of the crime or may be its target.

Categories

Offences include unauthorized access and hacking, malware distribution, ransomware, online fraud, identity theft, and denial-of-service attacks. Crimes range from individual scams to state-sponsored espionage and sabotage of infrastructure.

Law and enforcement

Legislation such as the United States Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and the Council of Europe's Budapest Convention criminalize computer offences, but cross-border jurisdiction makes enforcement difficult.
