The Fountains of Bellagio are a free attraction at the Bellagio resort on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada. They consist of a musical fountain show performed in an 8-acre man-made lake in front of the resort, with water jets choreographed to music and light. The fountains are among the most famous landmarks in Las Vegas and one of the largest choreographed fountain systems in the world.

Design

The fountains were designed by WET Design and opened with the resort in 1998. The system uses more than 1,200 nozzles, including "shooters" that can propel water hundreds of feet into the air, and thousands of lights, all computer-controlled. Shows typically run every half hour in the afternoons and every 15 minutes in the evenings, rotating through a repertoire of songs ranging from classical and operatic pieces to Broadway and pop numbers.

In popular culture

The fountains feature prominently in the closing scene of the 2001 film Ocean's Eleven and in numerous other films, television programmes, and advertisements set in Las Vegas.
