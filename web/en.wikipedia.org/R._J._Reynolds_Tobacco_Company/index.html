The R. J. Reynolds Tobacco Company is an American tobacco manufacturer and the second-largest tobacco company in the United States, based in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. It is a subsidiary of Reynolds American Inc., itself ultimately controlled by British American Tobacco. Its best-known brands include Camel, Newport, Pall Mall and Natural American Spirit.

History

Richard Joshua Reynolds founded the company in 1875, selling chewing tobacco from a small factory in the town of Winston. In 1913 the company introduced Camel, the first nationally marketed blended cigarette and one of the most successful brands in history, followed by the menthol brand Salem in 1956 and Winston cigarettes in 1954, which lent the merged city of Winston-Salem its second name.

Controversies

Reynolds was a central defendant in the tobacco litigation of the 1990s and a signatory of the 1998 Tobacco Master Settlement Agreement. Its Joe Camel cartoon mascot, introduced in 1988, was withdrawn in 1997 amid accusations that it marketed cigarettes to children. In 2015 Reynolds American acquired Lorillard and its Newport brand, and in 2017 BAT took full control of Reynolds American in one of the largest deals in tobacco history.
