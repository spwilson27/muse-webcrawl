The Field Mice

The Field Mice were an English indie pop band from London, initially a duo of Robert Wratten and Michael Hiscock. They were among the most prominent acts on the Bristol-based Sarah Records label, and their gentle, melancholic songs made them emblematic of the twee pop sound.

History

The band formed in 1987 and debuted with the single "Emma's House" (1988). Expanding to a five-piece, they released the mini-album Snowball (1989) and the full-length albums Skywriting (1990), which experimented with dance rhythms, and For Keeps (1991). Singles such as "Sensitive", "September's Not So Far Away", and "Missing the Moon" became indie favourites. The band split in 1991 shortly before a planned tour; Wratten went on to form Northern Picture Library and later Trembling Blue Stars.
