The Magic Castle is a private clubhouse for magicians and magic enthusiasts in the Hollywood district of Los Angeles, California. It serves as the headquarters of the Academy of Magical Arts, Inc., and is open only to members and their guests.

History

The building is a châteauesque mansion built in 1909 for banker and real estate developer Rollin B. Lane. In the early 1960s it was leased and converted by brothers Bill and Milt Larsen, together with Irene Larsen, into a club for magicians, opening in January 1963.

Performances

Performances take place nightly in several intimate venues within the building, including the Palace of Mystery, the Parlour of Prestidigitation, and the Close-Up Gallery. A strict dress code is enforced, and entry is famously gained by uttering a secret phrase to a hidden door in the library.
