Elsa Lanchester

Elsa Sullivan Lanchester (28 October 1902 – 26 December 1986) was a British-American actress with a long career in theatre, film and television. She was best known for her role as the title character in Bride of Frankenstein (1935), in which she also played Mary Shelley. Her performance as the Bride, with her distinctive beehive hairstyle streaked with white, became one of the most iconic images in horror cinema.

Career

Lanchester began acting as a child and appeared in British films in the 1920s, including The Private Life of Henry VIII (1933) opposite Charles Laughton, whom she married in 1929. After moving to Hollywood, she earned Academy Award nominations for Best Supporting Actress for Come to the Stable (1949) and Witness for the Prosecution (1957). She later appeared in Disney films such as Mary Poppins (1964) and in the horror comedy Willard (1971) and Murder by Death (1976).
