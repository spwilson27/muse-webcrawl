Balaban (instrument)

The balaban is a cylindrical-bore, double-reed wind instrument about 35 centimetres long with eight finger holes and one thumb hole. It is played in Azerbaijani, Iranian, and Caucasian folk music, and is closely related to the duduk and the mey.

Construction and sound

Traditionally carved from apricot wood, the balaban uses a wide flattened reed that produces a soft, mellow, and melancholy tone well suited to lyrical melodies and to accompanying ashik singers.

Cultural significance

Balaban performance was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, and the craft of making and playing the instrument is central to Azerbaijani musical identity.
