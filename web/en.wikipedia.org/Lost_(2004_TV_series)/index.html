Lost is an American science fiction drama television series that follows the survivors of Oceanic Airlines Flight 815, which crashes on a mysterious tropical island in the South Pacific. Each episode typically interweaves the survivors' present-day story on the island with flashbacks — and later flashforwards and "flash-sideways" sequences — centred on individual characters.

Plot and mythology

The island conceals a research organisation, the DHARMA Initiative, a group of hostile inhabitants known as the Others, strange electromagnetic properties and a smoke monster. Over six seasons the narrative explores themes of faith versus science, destiny and redemption, culminating in a final season split between the island storyline and a parallel "sideways" world.

Reception

Filmed in Hawaii, Lost was one of the most expensive series of its era; its pilot was among the costliest ever made. It won the Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series in 2005 and a Golden Globe the following year, and is widely credited with reshaping serialised television and online fan culture. Its finale, "The End", drew 13.5 million viewers and sharply divided audiences.
