Grammy Museum

The Grammy Museum is an interactive museum in downtown Los Angeles, California, dedicated to the history and winners of the Grammy Awards and to the exploration of music-making. It opened in December 2008 at the L.A. Live entertainment complex, coinciding with the Grammy Awards' 50th anniversary.

Location: L.A. Live, Los Angeles, California

Opened: December 2008

Size: About 30,000 sq ft (four floors)

Affiliated: The Recording Academy

Exhibits

The museum spans four floors of exhibits, including interactive touchscreens where visitors can explore musical genres, instruments, and recording technology; artifacts such as costumes, instruments, handwritten lyrics, and Grammy trophies; and rotating special exhibitions devoted to artists such as Michael Jackson, The Beatles, Bob Marley, and Shakira. Its Clive Davis Theater hosts intimate performances, interviews, and film screenings. Permanent installations include the Grammy Hall of Fame gallery and the "In the Studio" recording experience.

Other locations and education

Affiliate museums have included the Grammy Museum Mississippi in Cleveland, Mississippi (opened 2016) and the Grammy Museum Experience at the Prudential Center in Newark, New Jersey. The museum runs extensive education programs, including Grammy Camp and workshops for students, and in 2017 the museum's foundation merged with the Grammy Foundation to expand music education advocacy.
