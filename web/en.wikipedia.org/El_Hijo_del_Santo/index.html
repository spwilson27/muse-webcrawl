El Hijo del Santo ("The Son of the Saint"; born Jorge Ernesto Guzmán Rodríguez, 2 August 1963) is a Mexican luchador enmascarado (masked professional wrestler) and the son of the legendary El Santo, whose silver mask and identity he inherited. He is regarded as one of the most successful and respected second-generation wrestlers in lucha libre history.

Career

Guzmán made his professional debut in 1982, shortly before his father's retirement. He went on to work for Mexico's major promotions, including CMLL and AAA, as well as the Universal Wrestling Association, holding numerous championships such as the UWA World Welterweight Championship and the WWA World Welterweight and Tag Team titles. His long-running rivalries and mask-versus-mask victories — including famous Luchas de Apuestas wins — made him one of the era's top stars while he steadfastly defended his own mask, which he never lost.

Outside wrestling

Like his father, he starred in adventure films and a comic book, and he has run businesses under the El Santo brand, including stores in Mexico City. He has also written a newspaper column and campaigned on environmental issues, notably for the protection of the vaquita porpoise. He announced a farewell tour in 2024–2025, with his own son wrestling as Santo Jr.
