Sylvain-Eugène Raynal

Sylvain-Eugène Raynal
Born: 6 March 1867, Bordeaux, France
Died: 13 January 1939
Known for: Command of Fort Vaux, 1916

Sylvain-Eugène Raynal (1867–1939) was a French army officer best known as the commandant of Fort Vaux during the Battle of Verdun in the First World War.

Defence of Fort Vaux

In June 1916 Raynal commanded the garrison of Fort Vaux, one of the key forts of the Verdun defensive ring. Cut off, out of water and under relentless German artillery and gas attack, the garrison resisted for about a week. Raynal sent his last messages by carrier pigeon — the famous pigeon Valiant, which delivered its message and died — before surrendering on 7 June 1916 when his men were incapacitated by thirst.

Recognition

The German Crown Prince's headquarters received Raynal with military honours, and his defence became a symbol of French endurance at Verdun. He was made a commander of the Legion of Honour and promoted, ending his career as a general.
