Guangdong

Guangdong (廣東)
Capital: Guangzhou
Largest city: Shenzhen
Population: about 127 million (2020)
Area: 179,800 km²

Guangdong is a coastal province in South China on the north shore of the South China Sea. Its capital is Guangzhou and its most populous city is Shenzhen. With about 127 million residents, it is the most populous province of China, and its economy has been the largest of any Chinese province since 1989.

Geography

The province centres on the Pearl River Delta, one of the world's largest urban areas, encompassing Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Dongguan, Foshan and neighbouring cities, and bordering the special administrative regions of Hong Kong and Macau. Northern Guangdong is hilly and forested.

History

Long on the periphery of Chinese civilisation, Guangdong became the principal gateway for China's maritime trade — Guangzhou (Canton) was the only port open to European traders under the Canton System — and the homeland of most of the Chinese diaspora in Southeast Asia and the West. After 1978, the province led China's economic reforms, hosting three of the first four special economic zones, including Shenzhen.

Culture and language

Cantonese is the prestige language of the Pearl River Delta, while Teochew and Hakka are spoken in the east and north. Cantonese cuisine (dim sum, roast meats) is among the most internationally known of Chinese cuisines.
