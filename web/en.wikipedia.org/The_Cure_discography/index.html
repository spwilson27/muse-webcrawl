The Cure discography

The discography of the English rock band The Cure consists of fourteen studio albums, live albums, compilations, extended plays and more than forty singles. Formed in Crawley in 1976 and led throughout by singer and guitarist Robert Smith, the band became central to post-punk, gothic rock and alternative rock.

Studio albums

The studio albums are: Three Imaginary Boys (1979), Seventeen Seconds (1980), Faith (1981), Pornography (1982), The Top (1984), The Head on the Door (1985), Kiss Me, Kiss Me, Kiss Me (1987), Disintegration (1989), Wish (1992), Wild Mood Swings (1996), Bloodflowers (2000), The Cure (2004), 4:13 Dream (2008) and Songs of a Lost World (2024).

Singles and success

Signature singles include "A Forest", "The Love Cats", "In Between Days", "Close to Me", "Just Like Heaven", "Lovesong", "Friday I'm in Love" and "Pictures of You". Disintegration and Wish were major international successes, the latter reaching number one in the UK and number two in the US. The band has sold over 30 million albums worldwide and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2019.

Compilations and live albums

Notable compilations include Standing on a Beach (1986), Mixed Up (1990), Galore (1997) and Greatest Hits (2001); the live albums Concert (1984), Entreat (1991) and Show and Paris (both 1993) document their celebrated concerts.
