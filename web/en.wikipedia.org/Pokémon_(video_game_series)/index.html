Pokémon (video game series)

GenreRole-playing
DeveloperGame Freak
PublisherNintendo, The Pokémon Company
CreatorSatoshi Tajiri
First releasePocket Monsters Red and Green (1996)

The Pokémon video game series is a series of role-playing games developed by Game Freak for Nintendo handheld and home consoles. Beginning with Pocket Monsters Red and Green for the Game Boy in 1996, the series introduced players to catching, training, trading, and battling creatures called Pokémon.

Core series

Each generation launches with paired versions set in a new region and introducing new Pokémon species: Kanto (Generation I), Johto (II), Hoenn (III), Sinnoh (IV), Unova (V), Kalos (VI), Alola (VII), Galar (VIII), and the open-world Paldea of Scarlet and Violet (IX). Enhanced editions, remakes, and the action role-playing game Pokémon Legends: Arceus extend each generation.

Spin-offs

Notable spin-offs include Pokémon Snap, the Mystery Dungeon series, Pokémon Stadium, Pokémon Pinball, and the augmented reality mobile hit Pokémon Go. Competitive play is organised through official Video Game Championships using the core games.

Sales

The series is among the best-selling video game franchises of all time, with the core RPGs alone selling well over 300 million units.
