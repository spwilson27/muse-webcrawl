2016 Dyn cyberattack

Date21 October 2016
TypeDistributed denial of service (DDoS)
TargetDyn DNS infrastructure
MethodMirai botnet

The 2016 Dyn cyberattack took place on 21 October 2016 and involved multiple distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks targeting systems operated by the Domain Name System (DNS) provider Dyn. The attack made major internet platforms and services unavailable to large numbers of users in Europe and North America.

Attack

The attacks were carried out using the Mirai botnet, which had infected large numbers of insecure Internet of Things devices such as webcams, DVRs and routers. Waves of traffic overwhelmed Dyn's DNS servers, disrupting access to sites including Twitter, Netflix, Reddit, GitHub, Spotify and major news outlets.

Aftermath

The incident highlighted the fragility of core internet infrastructure and the security risks posed by poorly secured connected devices. US authorities later prosecuted the creators of the Mirai botnet, who pleaded guilty in 2017.
