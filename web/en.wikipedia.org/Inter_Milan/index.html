Football Club Internazionale Milano, commonly referred to as Internazionale or simply Inter (in English Inter Milan), is an Italian professional football club based in Milan, Lombardy. Founded in 1908 after a schism from the Milan Cricket and Football Club (now AC Milan), Inter is the only Italian club to have always competed in the top flight, Serie A, since its debut.

History

The club was founded by players who favoured allowing foreign players, hence the name Internazionale. Inter won its first Scudetto in 1910. Under Helenio Herrera in the 1960s, the "Grande Inter" side won three Serie A titles and back-to-back European Cups in 1964 and 1965, popularizing the catenaccio system. The club endured a long drought before returning to dominance in the 2000s, winning five consecutive league titles from 2006 to 2010. Under José Mourinho, Inter completed a historic treble — Serie A, Coppa Italia, and UEFA Champions League — in 2009–10.

Recent years

After ownership passed to Indonesian businessman Erick Thohir (2013) and then to Chinese group Suning (2016), Inter won the Scudetto again in 2020–21 under Antonio Conte, ending Juventus's nine-year run, and in 2023–24 under Simone Inzaghi, earning the club's second star for its twentieth title. In May 2024, control passed to the U.S. fund Oaktree Capital after Suning defaulted on a loan. Inter reached the Champions League final in 2023 and again in 2025, losing to Manchester City and Paris Saint-Germain respectively.

Stadium and rivalry

Inter shares the San Siro stadium (Stadio Giuseppe Meazza) with AC Milan; the fixture between the two is the Derby della Madonnina. The club's colours are black and blue vertical stripes, and its anthem and supporters are based in the Curva Nord.

Honours

Domestically, Inter has won 20 Serie A titles, 9 Coppa Italia trophies, and 8 Supercoppa Italiana titles. Internationally, it has won three European Cup/Champions League titles, three UEFA Cups, two Intercontinental Cups, and one FIFA Club World Cup.
