Kate Millett

Kate Millett
Born: 14 September 1934, Saint Paul, Minnesota
Died: 6 September 2017, Paris
Occupation: Writer, artist, activist
Known for: Sexual Politics (1970)

Katherine Murray Millett (1934–2017) was an American feminist writer, artist and activist, best known for her 1970 book Sexual Politics, one of the foundational texts of second-wave feminism.

Sexual Politics

Based on her Columbia University doctoral dissertation, Sexual Politics argued that patriarchy is a political institution sustained through socialisation, and subjected the works of D. H. Lawrence, Henry Miller and Norman Mailer to a feminist critique. The book became an international bestseller and made Millett a prominent public figure.

Later work

Her later books include The Prostitution Papers, Flying and The Loony-Bin Trip, on her experiences of mental illness and involuntary treatment. She also founded the Women's Art Colony Farm in Poughkeepsie, New York. See also Second-wave feminism.
