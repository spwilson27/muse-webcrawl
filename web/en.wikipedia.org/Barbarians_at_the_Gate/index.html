Barbarians at the Gate

Barbarians at the Gate: The Fall of RJR Nabisco is a 1989 book by investigative journalists Bryan Burrough and John Helyar about the leveraged buyout (LBO) of RJR Nabisco in 1988 — then the largest in history at about $25 billion. It is regarded as a classic of business journalism.

The deal

RJR Nabisco CEO F. Ross Johnson proposed a management buyout, triggering a bidding war with the private equity firm Kohlberg Kravis Roberts (KKR). KKR ultimately prevailed with a bid of $109 per share, financed largely with junk bonds, on 1 December 1988.

Adaptation

The book was adapted into an acclaimed 1993 HBO television film starring James Garner as Ross Johnson, which won Emmy and Golden Globe awards.

Legacy

The RJR buyout became emblematic of 1980s corporate excess and the LBO boom; the record deal size was not exceeded for nearly two decades.
