The Glenfinnan Viaduct is a railway viaduct on the West Highland Line in Glenfinnan, Inverness-shire, Scotland, built from 1897 to 1901. Located at the northern end of Loch Shiel, it carries the line on a sweeping curve of 21 concrete arches, 380 metres (1,248 ft) long and up to 30 metres (98 ft) high, across the valley of the River Finnan.

Construction

The viaduct was designed and built by "Concrete Bob" — Sir Robert McAlpine — whose firm pioneered the use of mass concrete in large structures; at the time it was among the longest concrete railway bridges in Britain. A persistent legend holds that a horse and cart fell inside one of the piers during construction; investigations in the 1980s and 2000s found evidence of a horse skeleton within a pier at Loch nan Uamh Viaduct on the same line, but not at Glenfinnan.

In popular culture

The viaduct gained worldwide fame through the Harry Potter films, in which the Hogwarts Express crosses it, and it appears on some Scottish banknotes. The Jacobite steam train crosses it daily in the tourist season, watched by crowds gathered on the surrounding hillsides.
