Lost Villages

The Lost Villages are ten communities in Ontario, Canada, that were permanently flooded during construction of the Saint Lawrence Seaway. In 1958 the villages and hamlets — including Aultsville, Farran's Point, Dickinson's Landing, Wales, Moulinette, Mille Roches, Sheek's Island communities and Iroquois (relocated) — were inundated by the creation of Lake St. Lawrence upstream of the Moses-Saunders dam.

Some 6,500 residents were relocated, many to the new planned towns of Ingleside and Long Sault. Buildings were moved, demolished or burned before the flooding on 1 July 1958. At times of low water, foundations and roads of the old villages remain visible, and the history is commemorated at the Lost Villages Museum in Ault Park, Long Sault.
