Kras Stadion
Location: Volendam, Netherlands
Opened: 1948
Capacity: about 7,384
Tenants: FC Volendam

Kras Stadion is a multi-purpose stadium in Volendam, in the province of North Holland, Netherlands. It is the home ground of football club FC Volendam.

Opened in 1948 and named for the Kras recycling/sponsorship company (formerly Veronicastadion among earlier names), the stadium has a capacity of roughly 7,384 spectators. It is used mainly for football matches and has an artificial turf surface. The ground consists of four stands close to the pitch, typical of small Dutch stadiums.
