Born19 July 1894, Kondrovo, Russian Empire

FieldsProbability theory, number theory, queueing theory

Aleksandr Yakovlevich Khinchin (19 July 1894 – 18 November 1959) was a Soviet mathematician, one of the founders of the Soviet school of probability theory, and a member of the USSR Academy of Sciences.

Work

Khinchin made fundamental contributions to the theory of stochastic processes, including the law of the iterated logarithm for sums of independent random variables (with Kolmogorov), the theory of stationary processes, and the foundations of queueing theory — the Pollaczek–Khinchin formula gives mean waiting behaviour in an M/G/1 queue. In number theory his name is attached to Khinchin's constant, governing the geometric mean of continued-fraction coefficients for almost all real numbers. He also wrote on the mathematical foundations of statistical mechanics and information theory, proving the characterization of Shannon entropy.

Legacy

His monographs, such as Mathematical Foundations of Statistical Mechanics and Mathematical Foundations of Information Theory, remain widely cited.
