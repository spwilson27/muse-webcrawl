United Malays National Organisation

The United Malays National Organisation (Malay: Pertubuhan Kebangsaan Melayu Bersatu; abbreviated UMNO) is a nationalist political party in Malaysia and the largest component party of the Barisan Nasional coalition. Founded in 1946 to oppose the British Malayan Union proposals, UMNO led the independence movement and supplied every prime minister of Malaysia from independence in 1957 until its first-ever general election defeat in 2018.

History

UMNO was founded at Johor Bahru under Dato' Onn Jaafar after Malay protests forced Britain to abandon the Malayan Union, which would have equalized citizenship for non-Malays. Onn left in 1951 when his proposal to open the party to all races was rejected, and Tunku Abdul Rahman took over, leading the Alliance coalition (with the MCA and MIC) to victory in the 1955 elections and independence in 1957.

Dominance and policies

Under Tun Abdul Razak and especially Mahathir Mohamad (prime minister 1981–2003), UMNO dominated Malaysian politics as the champion of Malay interests, implementing the New Economic Policy's affirmative action for Malays and other indigenous groups (bumiputera). The party weathered a major split in 1987–88, when Mahathir's faction re-registered "UMNO Baru", and another after 2016 when Mahathir and Muhyiddin Yassin left to form Bersatu over the 1MDB scandal.

Decline and recent history

Corruption scandals, above all 1MDB under Najib Razak, led to Barisan Nasional's historic loss in May 2018. UMNO fragmented, many MPs defecting to other parties, but it returned to government through shifting coalitions in 2020, and after the hung parliament of 2022 joined Anwar Ibrahim's unity government, with UMNO president Ahmad Zahid Hamidi as deputy prime minister.
