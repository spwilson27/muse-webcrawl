Finnish Jägers

The Finnish Jägers (Finnish: jääkärit) were volunteers from the Grand Duchy of Finland who received military training in Germany during the First World War as the Royal Prussian 27th Jäger Battalion.

Some 2,000 men trained at Lockstedt; their return in 1918 formed the officer and NCO core of the White Army in the Finnish Civil War and of the later Finnish Defence Forces. The Jäger movement is a founding tradition of independent Finland's military.
