Cheddar, Somerset

Cheddar is a large village and civil parish in Somerset, England, situated on the southern edge of the Mendip Hills, 9 miles northwest of Wells.

Cheddar is famous for Cheddar Gorge, a limestone gorge with show caves including Gough's Cave, where Britain's oldest complete human skeleton, "Cheddar Man" (about 10,000 years old), was found in 1903. The village gave its name to Cheddar cheese, made in the area since the 12th century and now produced worldwide.

Tourism centered on the gorge and caves is the main industry, along with strawberry growing — Cheddar strawberries were famous in the railway era. The parish had a population of about 5,700.
