Sickle cell disease

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Sickle cell disease (SCD) is a group of inherited blood disorders characterized by an abnormality in the oxygen-carrying protein haemoglobin in red blood cells. The abnormal haemoglobin, haemoglobin S, causes red cells to distort into a sickle, or crescent, shape under low oxygen conditions. These rigid cells can block small blood vessels, causing pain, organ damage, and a shortened red-cell lifespan leading to anaemia.

Genetics and inheritance

SCD is caused by a point mutation in the HBB gene on chromosome 11, in which glutamic acid is replaced by valine at the sixth position of the beta-globin chain. The disease follows an autosomal recessive pattern: a person must inherit two copies of the sickle gene to have sickle cell anaemia, the most common severe form. People with one copy have sickle cell trait and are usually healthy; the trait is common in regions where malaria is or was endemic, because carriers have some protection against the disease.

Signs and symptoms

Symptoms usually begin in early childhood and include episodes of severe pain (vaso-occlusive crises), swelling of the hands and feet, frequent infections, delayed growth, vision problems, and increased risk of stroke. Complications can affect nearly every organ, including the spleen, lungs (acute chest syndrome), kidneys, liver and bones.

Diagnosis and management

Diagnosis is by blood tests, including newborn screening in many countries, confirmed by haemoglobin electrophoresis or genetic testing. Management focuses on preventing and treating complications: vaccinations and penicillin prophylaxis against infection, pain relief, hydration, blood transfusions, and hydroxyurea, which increases fetal haemoglobin and reduces crises. Bone marrow (stem cell) transplantation can be curative, and gene therapies were approved beginning in 2023.

Epidemiology

SCD is most prevalent in sub-Saharan Africa, where hundreds of thousands of affected children are born each year, and is also common in parts of India, the Mediterranean, and the Americas among people of African descent.
