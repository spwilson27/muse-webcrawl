University: Arizona State University
Location: Tempe, Arizona
Conference: Big 12 Conference
Colors: Maroon and gold
Mascot: Sparky the Sun Devil

The Arizona State Sun Devils are the athletic teams that represent Arizona State University in Tempe, Arizona. The university sponsors varsity teams in numerous men's and women's sports and has won many NCAA national championships across its programs.

History

Arizona State athletics date to the late 19th century, when the school was a territorial normal school. The teams were known as the Bulldogs before adopting the Sun Devils name in 1946. The mascot, Sparky the Sun Devil, was designed in 1946 by former Disney illustrator Berk Anthony.

Football

The Sun Devils football team plays at Mountain America Stadium (formerly Sun Devil Stadium). Legendary coach Frank Kush led the program from 1958 to 1979. The team won the 1987 Rose Bowl under coach John Cooper. In 2024 Arizona State moved from the Pac-12 to the Big 12 Conference and won the Big 12 championship in its first season, reaching the College Football Playoff.

Other sports

Arizona State has strong traditions in baseball, softball, swimming, wrestling, and golf, producing athletes such as Barry Bonds, Reggie Jackson, James Harden, and Jon Rahm-adjacent collegiate golfers of national standing.

Rivalry

The Sun Devils' chief rival is the University of Arizona Wildcats; their football meeting is known as the Duel in the Desert, played for the Territorial Cup.
