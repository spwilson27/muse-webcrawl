Thermal design power

The thermal design power (TDP), sometimes called thermal design point, is the maximum amount of heat a computer chip — typically a CPU or GPU — is expected to generate under a representative workload, and thus the amount of heat its cooling system must be able to dissipate.

Usage

Manufacturers such as Intel and AMD publish TDP figures, usually in watts, to guide heatsink and system design. TDP is not a direct measure of a chip's actual power consumption, which varies with workload, voltage and boost behaviour; modern processors may briefly exceed TDP under turbo boost.
