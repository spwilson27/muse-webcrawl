Ludovico Sforza

Ludovico Maria Sforza (27 July 1452 – 27 May 1508), called il Moro ("the Moor"), was Duke of Milan from 1494 until 1499/1500. Son of Francesco Sforza, he first ruled as regent for his nephew Gian Galeazzo, whom he displaced.

Patronage and downfall

Ludovico made Milan a brilliant Renaissance court, patronising Leonardo da Vinci — who painted The Last Supper under his rule — and the architect Bramante. His invitation to Charles VIII of France to press claims on Naples helped trigger the Italian Wars. Captured by the French at Novara in 1500, he died a prisoner at Loches in France.
