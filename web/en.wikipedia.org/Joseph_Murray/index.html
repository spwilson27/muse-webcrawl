Joseph Murray

Joseph Edward Murray
Born: 1 April 1919, Milford, Massachusetts
Died: 26 November 2012, Boston
Known for: First successful kidney transplant

Joseph Edward Murray (1 April 1919 – 26 November 2012) was an American plastic surgeon who performed the first successful human kidney transplant, between identical twins Ronald and Richard Herrick, on 23 December 1954 at the Peter Bent Brigham Hospital in Boston.

Transplant work

Murray's team went on to perform transplants between non-identical individuals using immunosuppression, laying the foundations of modern organ transplantation. He shared the 1990 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with E. Donnall Thomas.

Career

Trained at Harvard Medical School, Murray served in the US Army during World War II treating burns, which sparked his interest in skin grafting and rejection. He later became professor of surgery at Harvard and chief of plastic surgery at Boston Children's Hospital.
