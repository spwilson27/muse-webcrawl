Tasman Glacier

The Tasman Glacier is the largest glacier in New Zealand, flowing south from the Southern Alps in Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park. It is roughly 23 to 27 kilometres long and up to several kilometres wide, terminating in the proglacial Tasman Lake.

Tasman Glacier
Location: Canterbury, South Island, New Zealand
Length: approx. 23 km
Terminus: Tasman Lake

Retreat

The glacier has been retreating rapidly since the 1990s. Tasman Lake, which did not exist in 1973, grew as the ice front calved icebergs into it, and the glacier lost several kilometres of length within a few decades. The retreat is attributed to regional warming, and the growing lake accelerates calving. Boat tours operate among the icebergs on the lake.
