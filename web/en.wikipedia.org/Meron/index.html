Meron

A meron or half-instanton is a hypothetical particle related to the instanton, proposed by Russian-American physicist Alexander Polyakov. A meron can be understood as "half" of an instanton: where an instanton carries a full unit of topological charge, a meron carries half a unit.

Physics context

In non-Abelian gauge theories such as quantum chromodynamics, single merons have infinite action and so cannot appear alone in the vacuum; instead, pairs of merons, or meron–antimeron configurations, are believed to be relevant to the mechanism of quark confinement. Merons also appear in condensed matter physics in discussions of topological excitations in magnets and related systems.
