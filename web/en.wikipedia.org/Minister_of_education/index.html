Minister of education

A minister of education is a position in the governments of some countries responsible for the national education system, including schools, and in many cases universities, research, and science policy. The officeholder usually heads an education ministry and sits in the cabinet.

Roles and titles

The exact title varies: the United Kingdom's equivalent is the Secretary of State for Education, the United States has a Secretary of Education, and many countries combine the portfolio with science, culture, or youth affairs. In federal states such as Germany, Canada, and Australia, most schooling is administered at state or provincial level, so education ministers exist at both national and regional tiers.

Responsibilities

Responsibilities commonly include setting curricula and standards, funding public education, teacher certification and pay policy, administering national examinations, and overseeing higher education and vocational training. Because education is a major area of public spending and public interest, education ministers are often prominent cabinet figures.
