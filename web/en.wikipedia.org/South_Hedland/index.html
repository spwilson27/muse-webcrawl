South Hedland is a suburb and the main residential and commercial centre of the Town of Port Hedland in the Pilbara region of Western Australia. It lies about 18 kilometres (11 mi) south of the port town of Port Hedland itself and roughly 1,600 kilometres (990 mi) north of Perth.

History

South Hedland was developed from the late 1960s and 1970s to house the growing workforce of the iron ore industry, after the original townsite of Port Hedland, constrained on a narrow peninsula, could not accommodate further expansion. Planned neighbourhoods known as "radburn" suburbs were built around cul-de-sacs and pedestrian pathways.

Economy and facilities

The suburb serves the iron ore mining and port operations centred on Port Hedland, one of the world's largest bulk export ports. South Hedland contains the Hedland Health Campus hospital, shopping centres, sporting facilities including the Wanangkura Stadium, and the South Hedland town centre. The area has a hot desert climate with very high summer temperatures and occasional tropical cyclones.
