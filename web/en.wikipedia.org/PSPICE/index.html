PSPICE

PSPICE is a SPICE analog and digital circuit simulator adapted for personal computers, originally developed by MicroSim Corporation and first released in 1984. It is widely used for electronic design automation in industry and education.

History

PSPICE brought the mainframe-era SPICE simulation engine to the IBM PC, combining a text netlist input with graphical schematic capture and the Probe waveform viewer. MicroSim was acquired by OrCAD in 1998, and OrCAD in turn was acquired by Cadence Design Systems in 1999; the simulator continues as part of Cadence's OrCAD and Allegro product lines as PSpice.

Features

PSPICE supports DC, AC, and transient analyses, Monte Carlo and worst-case analysis, and mixed-signal simulation with digital primitives and extensive vendor model libraries.
