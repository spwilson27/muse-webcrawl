The British Expeditionary Force (BEF) was the contingent of the British Army sent to France in 1939 after the outbreak of the Second World War, commanded by General Lord Gort. It took up positions along the Franco-Belgian border during the "Phoney War".

Battle of France

When Germany invaded France and the Low Countries in May 1940, the BEF advanced into Belgium but was cut off by the German drive to the Channel. Most of the force was evacuated from Dunkirk in Operation Dynamo between 26 May and 4 June 1940, losing almost all of its heavy equipment but saving some 338,000 Allied troops.
