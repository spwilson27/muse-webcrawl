Labasa

Labasa is a town in Fiji, the largest urban centre on the island of Vanua Levu, situated in Macuata Province on the island's northeastern coast at the delta of the Labasa River. Its population is around 28,000.

Economy

Labasa is the service centre for northern Fiji's sugar industry; the Fiji Sugar Corporation mill at Labasa is one of the country's largest sugar mills, and cane farming dominates the surrounding plain. The town is connected by Labasa Airport and by ferry services to Viti Levu.

Community

The population includes a large Indo-Fijian community, descendants of indentured labourers brought to work the cane fields, reflected in the town's temples, mosques and festivals such as Diwali and the South Indian fire-walking ceremonies.
