Air India

Founded: 15 October 1932 (as Tata Airlines)
Hub: Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi
Owner: Tata Group (via Talace/Air India Ltd)
Alliance: Star Alliance
Mascot: The Maharajah

Air India is the flag carrier airline of India, headquartered in Gurugram and based primarily at Indira Gandhi International Airport in Delhi. It operates a fleet of Airbus and Boeing aircraft serving domestic and international destinations across Asia, Europe, North America, Africa and Australia.

History

The airline was founded by industrialist J. R. D. Tata in 1932 as Tata Air Services (later Tata Airlines), initially carrying mail between Karachi and Madras via Ahmedabad and Bombay. After the Second World War it became a public company, Air India International, and in 1953 the Government of India nationalised it under the Air Corporations Act, creating Air India to run international services and Indian Airlines for domestic routes.

For decades Air India was known for its distinctive art-filled lounges and its mascot, the Maharajah. Financial difficulties mounted in the 2000s; a 2007 merger with Indian Airlines did not revive profitability. After years of attempts, the government sold the airline back to the Tata Group in January 2022. The new owners ordered hundreds of aircraft — one of the largest orders in aviation history in 2023 — merged Vistara into Air India in November 2024, and combined AirAsia India with Air India Express, reshaping the group around two carriers.

Operations

Air India is a member of Star Alliance, which it joined in 2014. Its loyalty programme is Maharaja Club (formerly Flying Returns). Subsidiaries include the low-cost carrier Air India Express.

Incidents

The airline has suffered notable disasters, including the bombing of Flight 182 by terrorists in 1985, the deadliest aviation terrorist attack of its era, and Flight 171, a Boeing 787 that crashed shortly after take-off from Ahmedabad in June 2025.
