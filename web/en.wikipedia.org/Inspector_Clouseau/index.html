Inspector Jacques Clouseau, later Chief Inspector, is a fictional detective in Blake Edwards's Pink Panther series. He is an incompetent yet doggedly determined detective in the French Sûreté. Clouseau is best known as the character played by Peter Sellers in the original series of films, from The Pink Panther (1963) onwards.

Character

Clouseau speaks English with an exaggerated French accent and perpetrates elaborate disguises and disastrous physical comedy, yet through luck he usually ends up solving the case. His superior, Commissioner Dreyfus, is driven progressively insane by Clouseau's blunders. In the later Sellers films, Clouseau is assisted by his manservant Cato, who attacks him by surprise to keep his reflexes sharp.

Other portrayals

Alan Arkin played the role in Inspector Clouseau (1968), and Steve Martin portrayed a reimagined Clouseau in the 2006 film The Pink Panther and its 2009 sequel.
