The Lamborghini Gallardo is a sports car built by Lamborghini from 2003 to 2013. Named after a famous breed of Spanish fighting bull, it was the marque's best-selling model of its era, with 14,022 cars built, and it marked Lamborghini's return to a smaller, more accessible V10 model under Audi ownership.

Overview

The Gallardo was launched with a 5.0-litre V10 producing about 500 hp, offered with a six-speed manual or the "e-gear" automated manual, and all-wheel drive. A Spyder convertible followed in 2005, and a comprehensive 2008 update (the LP 560-4) introduced a 5.2-litre direct-injection V10 with 552 hp.

Special editions

Numerous derivatives appeared, including the lightweight Superleggera, the rear-drive LP 550-2 Valentino Balboni, and the racing-derived Super Trofeo Stradale. Gallardos also served as police cars in Italy and as the basis of the one-make Super Trofeo racing series. The model was replaced by the Huracán in 2014.
