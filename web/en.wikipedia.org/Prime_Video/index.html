Amazon Prime Video, or simply Prime Video, is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming service owned by Amazon. It is offered both as a standalone subscription and as part of the Amazon Prime membership. The service primarily distributes films and television series produced by Amazon MGM Studios and licensed content from other providers.

History

The service launched in the United States on September 7, 2006, as Amazon Unbox, a download-to-own video store. It was rebranded as Amazon Video on Demand in 2008, and in February 2011 it became a streaming benefit for Prime subscribers, giving members access to a library of titles at no additional cost. The service expanded internationally in November 2016 and is now available in most countries worldwide.

Original programming

Amazon began producing original series in 2013 through its Amazon Studios arm, later renamed Amazon MGM Studios following Amazon's 2022 acquisition of Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer. Acclaimed originals include Transparent, The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, The Boys, and the fantasy series The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, reported to be the most expensive television series ever made. The service has also moved into live sports, acquiring exclusive rights to Thursday Night Football from 2022.

Channels and rentals

Through Prime Video Channels, subscribers can add third-party services such as Paramount+ and Max to their subscription. The Prime Video Store also offers films for rental or purchase independent of a subscription. In 2024, Amazon introduced advertising on the standard Prime Video tier in several markets, with an ad-free option available for an additional fee.
