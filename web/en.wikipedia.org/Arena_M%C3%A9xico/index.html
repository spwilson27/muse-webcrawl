Arena México is an indoor arena in Mexico City primarily used for professional wrestling, or lucha libre. It is the home venue of the Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre (CMLL), the world's oldest professional wrestling promotion, and is nicknamed the "Cathedral of Lucha Libre".

History

The arena was built under promoter Salvador Lutteroth on the site of the older Arena Modelo and opened in 1956. It hosted boxing events of the 1968 Summer Olympics, where Mexico won several medals. CMLL holds its flagship Friday night shows (Viernes Espectaculares) there, including its annual Anniversary Show, the longest-running annual wrestling event in the world.
