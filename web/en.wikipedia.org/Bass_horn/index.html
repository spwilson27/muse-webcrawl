Bass horn

The bass horn was an early brass instrument, a form of upright serpent used in military and wind bands from the late 18th century into the 19th century. It was made of wood or metal, played with a cup mouthpiece, and produced the bass line in wind ensembles before the development of valved bass instruments.

History

Developed around 1800, the bass horn — known in France as the basson russe — improved on the serpent's intonation and projection. It was eventually displaced by the ophicleide and later by the tuba, which offered more reliable pitch and greater dynamic range.
