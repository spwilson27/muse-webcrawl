The Nautilus is the fictional submarine featured in Jules Verne's novels Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas (1870) and The Mysterious Island (1874). Commanded by Captain Nemo, it is powered by electricity generated from sodium batteries and is capable of diving to great depths and travelling at high speed.

Description

Verne describes the Nautilus as a long, spindle-shaped vessel about 70 metres in length, built in sections by different manufacturers and assembled on a desert island. It carries a library, a museum-like salon with large viewing windows, and a powerful searchlight, and defends itself with an electrically charged hull and a ramming prow.

Legacy

The name was later given to the USS Nautilus (SSN-571), the world's first operational nuclear-powered submarine, launched in 1954.
