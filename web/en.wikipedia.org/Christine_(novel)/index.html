Christine (novel)

Author: Stephen King

Published: 1983

Genre: Horror novel

Christine is a horror novel by Stephen King, published in 1983. It tells the story of Arnie Cunningham, an awkward Pennsylvania teenager who buys a battered 1958 Plymouth Fury and is transformed by his attachment to it.

Plot

The previous owner's history, the car's apparent ability to regenerate, and its radio's fondness for 1950s rock and roll suggest that Christine carries a possessive spirit. Arnie grows confident but cruel, while his friend Dennis Guilder and girlfriend Leigh Cabot investigate the deaths surrounding the vehicle and attempt to save him.

Themes

King uses the automobile as a symbol of adolescence, masculinity, nostalgia, and addiction. The changing narrative viewpoint deepens the portrait of friendship and loss as Dennis watches Arnie disappear into an idealized past.

Adaptation

John Carpenter directed a film adaptation released the same year as the novel. The book is longer and more explicit about the car's supernatural history and the fate of its characters.
