The Superbike World Championship (WorldSBK) is the premier world championship for production-based motorcycle road racing. Founded in 1988, it features heavily modified versions of motorcycles available for sale to the public, in contrast to the purpose-built prototypes of MotoGP.

Format

A typical round comprises a Superpole qualifying session and three races: two full-length feature races and a short Superpole Race, whose results set part of the grid for the final race and award reduced points. Points follow the standard FIM system, 25 for a win in feature races.

Motorcycles and regulations

Machines are based on homologated production models from manufacturers including Ducati, Kawasaki, Yamaha, BMW and Honda, with engine capacities around 900–1200 cc depending on configuration. Balance-of-performance measures such as rev limits are adjusted during the season to equalise competition.

Champions

Jonathan Rea is the most successful rider in championship history, winning six consecutive titles with Kawasaki from 2015 to 2020. Other multiple champions include Carl Fogarty (4 titles), Troy Bayliss (3), Álvaro Bautista and Toprak Razgatlıoğlu (2 each). Ducati is the most successful manufacturer, and riders such as Fred Merkel, Doug Polen, Colin Edwards and James Toseland helped establish the series' popularity.
