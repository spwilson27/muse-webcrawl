Worf

Image placeholder: Worf, son of Mogh, in Starfleet uniform

Worf, son of Mogh, is a fictional character in the Star Trek franchise, portrayed by actor Michael Dorn. He is the first Klingon to serve in Starfleet as a main character, appearing in Star Trek: The Next Generation, Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, several films, and Star Trek: Picard.

Character biography

Worf was orphaned when his parents were killed in the Khitomer massacre and was raised by human foster parents, Sergey and Helena Rozhenko, on the farming colony of Gault and later on Earth. He joined Starfleet, serving as a tactical and security officer aboard the USS Enterprise-D, and later as strategic operations officer on Deep Space Nine, where he married Jadzia Dax.

Development

Dorn has appeared as Worf more times than any other actor has played the same character in the franchise. Worf's arc explores the tension between his Klingon heritage and his Starfleet upbringing.
