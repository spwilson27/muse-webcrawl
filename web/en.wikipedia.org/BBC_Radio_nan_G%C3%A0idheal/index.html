BBC Radio nan Gàidheal

BBC Radio nan Gàidheal is a Scottish Gaelic-language radio station owned and operated by the BBC. It broadcasts on FM across Scotland, as well as digitally, and is based in Stornoway on the Isle of Lewis, with additional studios in Inverness and Glasgow.

Programming

The station broadcasts a broad mix of news, current affairs, music, sport, and children's and educational programming in Gaelic. Its flagship news programme is Aithris na Maidne, and it carries Gaelic-language coverage of shinty and Scottish football.

History

Gaelic radio provision by the BBC grew from short opt-out programmes on Radio Highland and other stations. A unified national Gaelic service launched in 1985, taking the name Radio nan Gàidheal, meaning "radio of the Gaels".
