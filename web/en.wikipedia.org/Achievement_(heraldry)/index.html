Achievement (heraldry)

Achievement
Field: Heraldry
Components: Shield, helmet, crest, supporters, motto
Also called: Full achievement of arms

In heraldry, an achievement, armorial achievement, or heraldic achievement (historical: hatchment) is a full display of all the heraldic components to which the bearer of a coat of arms is entitled.

Components

A complete achievement may include the shield bearing the arms, surmounted by a helmet with mantling and a crest (often on a torse), flanked by supporters, standing on a compartment, and accompanied by a motto scroll. Additional elements can include badges, standards, coronets of rank, and insignia of orders of knighthood.

Use

Full achievements are used by sovereigns, states, institutions, and armigerous individuals. Funeral hatchments — painted achievements displayed after a death — survive in many English churches.
