Sustainable transport

Sustainable transport refers to ways of transportation that are sustainable in terms of their social, economic and environmental impacts and their ability to be maintained indefinitely without depleting resources or causing severe ecological damage.

Modes

Walking and cycling are the lowest-impact modes; public transport moves many people per unit of energy and space. Sustainable policy typically prioritises these over private cars, alongside electrification of remaining vehicle fleets and compact, mixed-use urban planning.

Concepts

Related ideas include transit-oriented development, complete streets, congestion pricing, car sharing, and the "avoid–shift–improve" framework used by development agencies: avoid unnecessary trips, shift to efficient modes, improve vehicle technology.

Challenges

Transport accounts for roughly a fifth of global carbon dioxide emissions, and demand growth in aviation and freight makes the sector one of the hardest to decarbonise.
