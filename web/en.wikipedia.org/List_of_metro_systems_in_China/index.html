List of metro systems in China

China has more metro (rapid transit) systems than any other country, and the longest networks in the world. The first metro in mainland China was the Beijing Subway, opened in 1969. Since the 2000s, dozens of cities have built metro systems; by the mid-2020s more than 40 mainland cities operated metros, in addition to the systems in Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan. The Shanghai Metro, opened in 1993, is the world's longest network by route length.
