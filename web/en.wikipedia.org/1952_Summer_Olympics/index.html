The 1952 Summer Olympics, officially the Games of the XV Olympiad, were an international multi-sport event held from 19 July to 3 August 1952 in Helsinki, Finland. Helsinki had originally been awarded the 1940 Summer Olympics, which were cancelled because of World War II.

Highlights

The Soviet Union competed at the Olympic Games for the first time. Czechoslovak long-distance runner Emil Zátopek won the 5,000 metres, 10,000 metres, and the marathon, the last in his first attempt at the distance — a feat never repeated. The United States topped the medal table, followed by the Soviet Union and Hungary.

Participating nations

A total of 69 nations sent athletes, including debut appearances by the Soviet Union, Israel, China (represented by a single swimmer), and several others.
