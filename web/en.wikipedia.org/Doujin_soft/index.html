Doujin soft


Doujin soft are sold at conventions such as Comiket and online stores.

Doujin soft (同人ソフト) are video games created by Japanese hobbyist circles (doujin groups) rather than commercial studios. They are distributed at conventions, above all Comiket in Tokyo, and through specialist retailers and digital storefronts.

Characteristics

Doujin games span every genre, but the scene is especially known for bullet-hell shooters, fighting games, and visual novels. Many are derivative works based on existing intellectual property — an arrangement tolerated by Japanese rights holders — while others are wholly original. Some doujin circles have achieved wide fame: Team Shanghai Alice's Touhou Project became a multimedia phenomenon, and Type-Moon began as a doujin group before commercialising Tsukihime and Fate/stay night.

Distribution

Print runs are typically small and sold at cost-covering prices; modern distribution also uses platforms such as DLsite and Steam, which list categories of indie and doujin titles for an international market.
