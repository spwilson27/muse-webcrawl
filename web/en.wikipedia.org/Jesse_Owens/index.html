Jesse Owens

James Cleveland "Jesse" Owens (September 12, 1913 – March 31, 1980) was an American track and field athlete who won four gold medals at the 1936 Olympic Games in Berlin.

Owens specialized in the sprints and the long jump. At the Big Ten Championships in Ann Arbor on May 25, 1935, he set three world records and tied a fourth within about 45 minutes, in what has been called the greatest 45 minutes in sport. At Berlin he won gold in the 100 metres, 200 metres, long jump, and 4 × 100 metre relay, foiling Nazi propaganda about Aryan supremacy, though he was himself snubbed on his return home and struggled financially afterward.
