The Noteć (German: Netze) is a river in central Poland, about 391 kilometres long, and the largest tributary of the Warta. It flows from the Kuyavian lake area westward past Inowrocław, Nakło nad Notecią, Czarnków and Drezdenko before joining the Warta at Santok.

Navigation

The lower Noteć forms part of the historic waterway linking the Vistula and Oder river systems: via the Bydgoszcz Canal (opened 1774) boats could travel from Bydgoszcz on the Brda and Vistula westward down the Noteć to the Warta and Oder. Much of the river is canalised with locks.

Basin

The Noteć drains a largely lowland agricultural basin of about 17,000 km². Its valley, the Toruń-Eberswalde glacial valley, is a broad post-glacial channel important for wetlands and bird habitats.
