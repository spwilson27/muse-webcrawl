The Venetian Las Vegas

Location: Las Vegas Strip, Paradise, Nevada

Opened: 3 May 1999

Developer: Las Vegas Sands (Sheldon Adelson)

Rooms: Over 7,000 suites (with The Palazzo)

The Venetian Las Vegas is a luxury hotel and casino resort on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada, built on the site of the demolished Sands Hotel. Themed on Venice, Italy, it features replicas of Venetian landmarks — including St Mark's Campanile, the Doge's Palace and the Rialto Bridge — and canals with gondolas ridden by singing gondoliers.

History

Developed by Sheldon Adelson's Las Vegas Sands, the resort opened in 1999 with an all-suite hotel tower; the connected Palazzo tower, opened in 2007, made the combined property one of the largest hotels in the world. The resort includes the Grand Canal Shoppes mall and the Venetian Expo convention centre.

Features

All rooms are suites substantially larger than standard Las Vegas rooms. The resort has hosted long-running entertainments and, in 2022, Las Vegas Sands sold the property's operations to Apollo Global Management and the real estate to Vici Properties.
