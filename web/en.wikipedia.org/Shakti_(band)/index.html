Shakti (band)

Shakti
OriginInternational (UK / India)
GenresJazz fusion, Indian classical, world fusion

Key membersJohn McLaughlin, L. Shankar, Zakir Hussain, Vikku Vinayakram, R. Raghavan

Shakti is a fusion band formed by English guitarist John McLaughlin with Indian violinist L. Shankar, tabla player Zakir Hussain, and ghatam player Vikku Vinayakram, with mridangam player R. Raghavan. Active in the 1970s, the group pioneered world fusion by combining jazz improvisation with North and South Indian classical music.

Music

Shakti's largely acoustic music features long, virtuosic improvisations built on ragas and complex Indian rhythmic cycles (tala), with McLaughlin playing a custom double-neck acoustic guitar modified with sympathetic and drone strings. Albums include Shakti with John McLaughlin (1976), A Handful of Beauty (1976), and Natural Elements (1977).

Remember Shakti

McLaughlin revived the concept in the late 1990s as Remember Shakti, with Zakir Hussain and rotating Indian musicians including mandolinist U. Srinivas, guitarist Debashish Bhattacharya, and vocalist Shankar Mahadevan, who later fronted a further incarnation under the Shakti name.
