Advanced driver-assistance system

Advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) are electronic systems in vehicles that assist the driver, using sensors such as cameras, radar and lidar to detect the surroundings and warn of hazards or automate limited tasks.

Features

Common features include adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assistance, automatic emergency braking, blind-spot monitoring, parking assistance and driver drowsiness detection. Many are mandated on new cars in the European Union under the 2019 General Safety Regulation.

Automation levels

ADAS corresponds mainly to Levels 1 and 2 of the SAE automation taxonomy, in which the driver remains responsible for supervision. See also Self-driving car and Vehicular automation.
