Bessel

Bessel most commonly refers to Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel (1784–1846), a German astronomer, mathematician, physicist and geodesist. Bessel was the first astronomer to measure the distance to a star other than the Sun by parallax, determining the distance to 61 Cygni in 1838. He also systematised the mathematical functions that now bear his name.

Named after Bessel

  • Bessel functions — canonical solutions of Bessel's differential equation, widely used in physics and engineering.
  • Bessel's correction — the use of n − 1 in estimating a population variance from a sample.
  • Bessel ellipsoid — a reference ellipsoid used in geodesy.
  • Bessel filter — an electronic filter with a maximally flat group delay.
  • Bessel (crater) — a lunar impact crater in the Mare Serenitatis.
  • 1552 Bessel — an asteroid named in his honour.

People

Other notable people with the surname include Emil Artin-era mathematician Bessel van der Kolk (psychiatrist, born 1943) and the German industrialist family associated with the Bessel name.
