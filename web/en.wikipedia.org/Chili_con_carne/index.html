Chili con carne

Chili con carne — Spanish for 'chili with meat' — is a spicy stew of chili peppers, meat (usually beef), tomatoes and often beans, seasoned with cumin, garlic and oregano. It is the official state dish of Texas.

Its roots lie in the cooking of San Antonio's chili queens in the 19th century. Texas-style 'bowl of red' traditionally excludes beans, a point of enduring culinary debate; regional variants abound across the United States.
