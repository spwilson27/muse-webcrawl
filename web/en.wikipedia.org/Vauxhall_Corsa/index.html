Vauxhall Corsa

The Vauxhall Corsa is the British-market name for the supermini car sold elsewhere in Europe as the Opel Corsa. Produced since 1993 in its Vauxhall form (the Opel Corsa began in 1982), it has been one of the United Kingdom's best-selling cars for decades, frequently topping the annual sales charts.

Generations

The Corsa B (1993–2000) introduced the rounded styling that defined the model in Britain. The Corsa C (2000–2006) and Corsa D (2006–2014) successively grew in size and equipment. The Corsa E (2014–2019) was a heavy rework of the D. The current Corsa F, launched in 2019 after Groupe PSA's purchase of Opel and Vauxhall from General Motors, is based on the PSA CMP platform shared with the Peugeot 208 and was offered from launch with an all-electric variant, the Corsa-e, alongside petrol and diesel engines.

Sales and significance

The Corsa has been a fixture of the UK top-ten sellers list throughout its life and was the country's best-selling car overall in 2021. It is manufactured mainly at Zaragoza in Spain and Eisenach in Germany, and is a common first car and driving-school vehicle in Britain.
