The Provisional All-Russian Government (PA-RG), also known as the Directory or the Ufa Directory, was a short-lived anti-Bolshevik government proclaimed during the Russian Civil War. It was formed on 23 September 1918 at the State Conference in Ufa, which merged the rival anti-Bolshevik administrations of the Committee of Members of the Constituent Assembly (Komuch) in Samara and the Provisional Siberian Government in Omsk into a single authority claiming sovereignty over all Russia.

Formation

By the summer of 1918, following the revolt of the Czechoslovak Legion, two principal anti-Bolshevik governments controlled territory east of the Volga and in Siberia. The Ufa State Conference, attended by representatives of Komuch, the Siberian government, Cossack hosts, and regional governments, agreed on a five-person Directory headed by the Socialist Revolutionary Nikolai Avksentiev. The Directory was to govern until the reconvened Constituent Assembly (dissolved by the Bolsheviks in January 1918) could meet.

Overthrow

Compelled by the Red Army's advance, the Directory moved from Ufa to Omsk in October 1918, where it came under the influence of the Siberian military establishment and the war minister, Admiral Alexander Kolchak. Tensions between the Directory's socialist members and conservative officers culminated in a coup on the night of 17–18 November 1918: Cossack officers arrested Avksentiev and other Directory members, and the Council of Ministers transferred supreme power to Kolchak, who took the title of Supreme Ruler of Russia. The Directory's members were exiled abroad, and Kolchak's government in Omsk became the leading White administration in Siberia.

Legacy

The Provisional All-Russian Government represented the last attempt to unite the anti-Bolshevik movement under a nominally democratic, coalition banner. Its overthrow marked the eclipse of the Socialist Revolutionary claim to legality through the Constituent Assembly and the turn of the White movement in the east toward open military dictatorship.
