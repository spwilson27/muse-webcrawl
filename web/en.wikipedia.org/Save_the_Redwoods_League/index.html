Save the Redwoods League

Save the Redwoods League is a non-profit organisation founded in 1918 dedicated to protecting and restoring the coast redwood and giant sequoia forests of California. It was established by conservationists including John C. Merriam, Madison Grant and Henry Fairfield Osborn, alarmed by logging along the Redwood Highway.

Old-growth coast redwoods protected through the League's efforts

Work

The League raises funds to purchase redwood land and transfer it to state and national parks, and has helped protect more than 200,000 acres. Its purchases contributed to the creation of Humboldt Redwoods, Del Norte Coast Redwoods, Jedediah Smith Redwoods and Redwood National Park, among others. Memorial groves named for donors became a hallmark of its fundraising.

Modern programmes

Beyond acquisition, the League funds scientific research — including studies of redwood canopy ecology and the complete sequencing of the coast redwood and giant sequoia genomes — forest restoration of previously logged land, and education programmes connecting young people to the redwoods. It also works on climate adaptation, since redwood forests store exceptionally large amounts of carbon.
