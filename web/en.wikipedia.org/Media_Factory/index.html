Media Factory, Inc. (Japanese: メディアファクトリー) was a Japanese publisher and brand company active in magazines, light novels, manga and anime. Founded as the publishing division of Recruit in 1986 and spun off in 1991, it became known for the MF Bunko J light novel imprint and for producing animated adaptations of its properties.

History

The company began within the Recruit group and built a catalogue spanning magazines such as Monthly Comic Alive, the anime magazine and production business, and music and video distribution under labels including the MF label. Its light novel imprint MF Bunko J, launched in 2002, published series such as The Familiar of Zero, A Certain Magical Index's contemporary rivals in the market, and Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World.

Acquisition by Kadokawa

In 2011 Kadokawa Group Holdings acquired Media Factory, and in 2013 it was absorbed into Kadokawa Corporation as part of the merger of nine group companies, continuing thereafter as a brand rather than a separate corporation. Its imprints and anime production activities continued within Kadokawa, and Media Factory's producer role is credited on many anime series adapted from its publications.
