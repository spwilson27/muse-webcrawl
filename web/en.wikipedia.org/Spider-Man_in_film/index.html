Spider-Man in film

The Marvel Comics character Spider-Man has appeared in many films. The character was first portrayed in live action in the 1970s television series The Amazing Spider-Man. A planned Cannon Films production in the 1980s and a James Cameron treatment in the 1990s never materialised; the film rights were eventually acquired by Sony Pictures.

Sam Raimi trilogy

Director Sam Raimi's Spider-Man (2002), starring Tobey Maguire, was a major critical and commercial success, followed by Spider-Man 2 (2004) — widely regarded as one of the finest superhero films — and Spider-Man 3 (2007).

Marc Webb films

The series was rebooted with The Amazing Spider-Man (2012) and its 2014 sequel, starring Andrew Garfield. After the sequel underperformed, Sony reached an agreement with Marvel Studios to share the character.

Marvel Cinematic Universe

Tom Holland debuted as Spider-Man in Captain America: Civil War (2016), followed by solo films Homecoming (2017), Far From Home (2019), and No Way Home (2021), the latter uniting all three live-action Spider-Men and grossing over $1.9 billion.

Animated films

The animated Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018), centred on Miles Morales, won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature; its sequel Across the Spider-Verse followed in 2023.
