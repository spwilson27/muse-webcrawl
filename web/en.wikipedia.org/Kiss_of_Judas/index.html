Kiss of Judas

The kiss of Judas, also known as the Betrayal of Christ, is the act with which Judas identified Jesus to the authorities in the Garden of Gethsemane, leading to Jesus's arrest. It is narrated in the Synoptic Gospels: Judas approached Jesus and kissed him, saying "Rabbi", the pre-arranged sign.

Context

The arrest followed the Last Supper. According to the Gospels, Judas had agreed to hand Jesus over for thirty pieces of silver. The episode is a standard scene in Christian Passion art, depicted by artists including Giotto and Caravaggio.

Meaning

The phrase "kiss of Judas" or "Judas kiss" has become an idiom for an act of betrayal disguised as friendship or affection.
