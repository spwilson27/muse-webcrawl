English Folk Dance and Song Society

English Folk Dance and Song Society (EFDSS)
Formation: 1932
Headquarters: Cecil Sharp House, London, England
Purpose: Preservation and promotion of English folk dance and song

The English Folk Dance and Song Society (EFDSS) is an organisation that promotes English folk music and folk dance. It was formed in 1932 by the merger of the Folk-Song Society (founded 1898) and the English Folk Dance Society, which Cecil Sharp had founded in 1911.

Cecil Sharp House and the Vaughan Williams Memorial Library

The society is based at Cecil Sharp House in Camden, London, opened in 1930 as a home for the folk revival. The building houses the Vaughan Williams Memorial Library, the society's library and archive, which holds major collections of folk songs, dances, and manuscripts, including material collected by Cecil Sharp and Ralph Vaughan Williams.

Activities

EFDSS runs festivals, dances, concerts, education programmes, and publishing projects, and maintains the national folk music and dance archive of England.
