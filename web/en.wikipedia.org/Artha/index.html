Artha

Artha (Sanskrit: अर्थ) is one of the four aims of human life in Hindu philosophy, the Puruṣārthas. It refers to the pursuit of wealth, prosperity, economic security and worldly success within the bounds of dharma (righteous conduct).

In Hindu thought

Artha is considered a legitimate and necessary goal, particularly for householders, because material security supports family, charity and social order. Classical texts such as Kautilya's Arthashastra treat the acquisition and management of wealth and statecraft in detail. Artha is ranked below dharma and moksha but alongside kama, the pursuit of pleasure, among life's proper aims.

Relation to other aims

The Puruṣārthas are not seen as mutually exclusive: artha pursued without dharma is condemned as greed, while wealth rightly earned enables the fulfilment of duties and generosity.
