Chiquita Brands

Chiquita Brands International
Industry: Agribusiness
Known for: Bananas
Predecessor: United Fruit Company
Headquarters: Etoy, Switzerland (global); US operations in Florida

Chiquita Brands International S.à.r.l. is a major producer and distributor of bananas and other fresh produce, and the successor to the United Fruit Company. Its Chiquita brand, with the Miss Chiquita mascot created in 1944, is one of the best-known fruit brands in the world.

History

The company's origins lie in the United Fruit Company, founded in 1899, which dominated the banana trade in Central America. Renamed Chiquita Brands in 1990, the company later went through bankruptcy in 2001–2002 and was acquired in 2015 by Brazil's Cutrale and Safra groups and taken private.

Controversies

In 2007 Chiquita pleaded guilty in the United States to making payments to the Colombian paramilitary group AUC, designated a terrorist organisation, and paid a $25 million fine. The legacy of United Fruit also includes involvement in the 1954 Guatemalan coup.
