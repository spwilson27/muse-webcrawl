Textile arts

[Placeholder image: Textile_arts.png]

The textile arts are arts and crafts that use plant, animal, or synthetic fibres to construct practical or decorative objects. They include weaving, knitting, crochet, embroidery, quilting, felting, dyeing, and lace-making, and are among the oldest forms of human technology and artistic expression.

History

Textiles have been fundamental to human civilisation since prehistoric times; some of the earliest evidence of woven fabric dates to the Neolithic period. Textile production drove industrialisation in the 18th and 19th centuries, and contemporary fibre art treats textiles as a fine art medium.
