Kusama may refer to several people, places and things, most notably the Japanese contemporary artist Yayoi Kusama. This page lists the principal articles associated with the name.

People

Yayoi Kusama (born 1929) is a Japanese contemporary artist known for her polka-dot motifs, Infinity Mirror Rooms, large pumpkin sculptures and "infinity net" paintings. A leading figure associated with pop art, minimalism and feminist art, she has lived voluntarily at a psychiatric hospital in Tokyo since 1977 while continuing to produce work; her exhibitions are among the most attended in the world, and her museum opened in Tokyo in 2017. Kusama is also a Japanese surname borne by various other figures in sport, science and entertainment.

Other uses

Kusama is also the name of a blockchain network that serves as the experimental "canary network" for the Polkadot ecosystem, launched in 2019, where new features are tested before deployment. In Japanese, kusama (草間) is a surname written with characters meaning "grass" and "space/between". Place names and fictional characters also bear the name.
