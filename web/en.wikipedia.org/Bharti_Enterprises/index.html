Bharti Enterprises

Bharti Enterprises Limited is an Indian multinational conglomerate headquartered in New Delhi, founded in 1976 by Sunil Bharti Mittal. Its flagship business is Bharti Airtel, one of the world's largest telecommunications companies, and the group has interests spanning telecom, financial services, real estate, hospitality, agri-food and space communications.

History

Mittal began the business assembling bicycle parts in Ludhiana, moved into manufacturing telephone equipment in the 1980s — including India's first push-button telephones and fax machines — and won one of the first mobile licences for Delhi in 1992, launching services in 1995. The group's subsequent growth was driven by Airtel's expansion across India and into Africa.

Businesses

Group ventures include Bharti Airtel; Bharti AXA (insurance, later restructured with partners); Bharti Real Estate; Del Monte Pacific's Indian joint venture FieldFresh Foods; and Bharti Global, the family's investment arm, which acquired a major stake in the satellite operator OneWeb (now Eutelsat OneWeb) and other international assets. The Bharti Foundation runs the Satya Bharti school programme providing free education to underprivileged children in rural India.
