Good Will Hunting is a 1997 American drama film directed by Gus Van Sant and written by Ben Affleck and Matt Damon, who also star. It tells the story of Will Hunting, a self-taught mathematical genius working as a janitor at MIT.

Plot

Will Hunting (Damon), a 20-year-old from South Boston with a genius-level intellect and an eidetic memory, secretly solves a graduate-level mathematics problem posted by Professor Gerald Lambeau (Skarsgård). After Will is arrested for assaulting a police officer, Lambeau arranges for him to avoid jail by studying mathematics and attending therapy. After Will dismisses several therapists, Lambeau turns to Dr Sean Maguire (Robin Williams), his former college roommate, whose confrontational honesty — culminating in the famous "It's not your fault" scene — breaks through Will's defences formed by childhood abuse. Will must choose between the life mapped out for him and following his girlfriend Skylar (Minnie Driver) to California; encouraged by his best friend Chuckie (Affleck), he finally drives west to "see about a girl".

Reception and awards

The film was a critical and commercial success, earning $225 million worldwide from a budget of $10 million. It received nine Academy Award nominations and won two: Best Supporting Actor for Robin Williams and Best Original Screenplay for Affleck and Damon, then in their twenties, which launched their careers.
