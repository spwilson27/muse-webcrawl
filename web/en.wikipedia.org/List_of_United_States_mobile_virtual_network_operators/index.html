List of United States mobile virtual network operators

A mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) is a wireless communications provider that does not own the radio network infrastructure over which it provides services, instead leasing capacity from the major carriers — AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon — in the United States.

Notable MVNOs

Large American MVNOs include Tracfone and its brands (Straight Talk, Simple Mobile, Total Wireless, acquired by Verizon), Mint Mobile (acquired by T-Mobile), Visible (Verizon), Cricket Wireless and AT&T Prepaid (AT&T flanker brands), Metro by T-Mobile, Boost Mobile, Google Fi, Consumer Cellular, Xfinity Mobile and Spectrum Mobile (cable-company MVNOs using Verizon), and US Mobile.

Market

MVNOs typically compete on price, prepaid plans and niche markets such as international calling, seniors, or low-data users.
