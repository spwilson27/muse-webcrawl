List of national independence days

An independence day is an annual public holiday commemorating a nation's independence or statehood, usually after ceasing to be a colony or part of another state. Not every country has one: some celebrate a national day marking a different event, such as unification, a revolution, or a monarch's birthday. This list gives selected independence days by date; many mark independence from the British, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Ottoman or Russian empires, or from the Soviet Union.
