National Instruments Corporation, doing business as NI, is an American multinational company headquartered in Austin, Texas, that produces automated test equipment, semiconductor test systems and virtual instrumentation software. It was founded in 1976 by James Truchard, Jeff Kodosky and Bill Nowlin while they worked at the University of Texas at Austin.

Products

NI's best-known product is LabVIEW (1986), a graphical programming environment in which programs are drawn as block diagrams of "virtual instruments" (VIs); it is widely used for data acquisition, instrument control and industrial automation. Hardware lines include PXI modular instruments, CompactRIO controllers, DAQ data-acquisition devices, myRIO and USRP software-defined radios. The company's approach of "virtual instrumentation" — using general-purpose computers plus modular hardware instead of fixed standalone instruments — reshaped test and measurement engineering.

Acquisition

In April 2023 Emerson Electric announced it would acquire NI for about $8.2 billion; the transaction was completed in October 2023, and NI became part of Emerson's test and measurement business.
