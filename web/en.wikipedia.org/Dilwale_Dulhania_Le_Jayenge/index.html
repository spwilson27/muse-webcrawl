Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge

Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (transl. "The Brave-Hearted Will Take the Bride"), also known by its initialism DDLJ, is a 1995 Indian Hindi-language musical romance film written and directed by Aditya Chopra in his directorial debut and produced by his father Yash Chopra. Released on 20 October 1995, the film stars Shah Rukh Khan and Kajol as Raj and Simran, two young non-resident Indians who fall in love during a vacation through Europe with their friends.

Plot

Raj Malhotra and Simran Singh meet on a Eurail trip. Simran's strict father, Baldev Singh, has promised her hand to the son of his friend in Punjab. After the two fall in love, Raj follows Simran to India and, rather than eloping, sets out to win over her family — eventually gaining Baldev's blessing in the famous climax, in which he lets Simran board a departing train to join Raj.

Reception and legacy

The film earned ₹1.22 billion worldwide and became the highest-grossing Indian film of its time. It won 10 Filmfare Awards, including Best Film, Best Director, Best Actor and Best Actress. It is one of the longest-running films in cinema history: it has been shown continuously at the Maratha Mandir theatre in Mumbai since its release, passing 1,000 weeks in 2014 and 25 years in 2020.

DDLJ is credited with redefining Bollywood romance and with shaping the film industry's portrayal of the Indian diaspora. Its songs, composed by Jatin–Lalit with lyrics by Anand Bakshi — including "Tujhe Dekha To", "Mere Khwabon Mein", and "Mehndi Laga Ke Rakhna" — remain enduringly popular.
