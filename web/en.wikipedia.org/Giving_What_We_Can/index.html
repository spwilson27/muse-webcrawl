Giving What We Can

Giving What We Can (GWWC) is an organisation within the effective altruism movement whose members pledge to donate a significant portion of their income — typically at least 10% — to charities judged to be highly effective at improving lives. It was founded in 2009 at the University of Oxford by the philosopher Toby Ord, together with Bernadette Young and William MacAskill.

The pledge

Members of Giving What We Can sign the GWWC Pledge, committing to give at least 10% of their income for the rest of their working lives to organisations that they believe most effectively help others. A less demanding "Try Giving" option allows people to pledge a smaller percentage for a set period. Toby Ord, who lives on a modest income and donates the rest of his earnings, calculated that over his career he could save many lives through donations to cost-effective health charities, and the pledge grew out of that example.

Activities and growth

The organisation researches charity effectiveness, publishes recommendations, and supports a community of thousands of members in many countries who have collectively pledged hundreds of millions of dollars. It helped launch the modern effective altruism movement alongside organisations such as GiveWell and 80,000 Hours, and it is part of the Centre for Effective Altruism network. Some members follow the related "earning to give" idea, choosing lucrative careers in order to donate more.
