DualSense

The DualSense is a wireless game controller developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment for the PlayStation 5 console. Released in November 2020 alongside the console, it succeeded the DualShock 4 and introduced haptic feedback and adaptive triggers as headline features.

Manufacturer: Sony Interactive Entertainment
Release: November 2020
Platform: PlayStation 5
Predecessor: DualShock 4

Features

The DualSense replaces the traditional rumble motors of earlier controllers with dual voice-coil actuators that produce detailed haptic feedback, capable of simulating textures such as rain or walking on sand. Its L2 and R2 triggers are adaptive: the resistance they offer can be varied by software, for instance to simulate drawing a bow or a gun jamming. The controller retains two analog sticks, a touchpad, motion sensing, a built-in microphone and speaker, and a "Create" button replacing the DualShock 4's Share button. It charges via USB-C and connects by Bluetooth.

Design

The controller has a two-tone white-and-black shell whose texture is composed of thousands of tiny versions of the PlayStation button symbols. A premium variant, the DualSense Edge, with replaceable stick modules and remappable controls, was released in 2023.
