Chemical equation

A chemical equation is the symbolic representation of a chemical reaction, with reactants on the left and products on the right, separated by an arrow. Coefficients are chosen so the equation is balanced — the same number of atoms of each element on both sides, reflecting conservation of mass.

For example, 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O describes hydrogen burning in oxygen to form water. States of matter may be shown with (s), (l), (g) and (aq).
