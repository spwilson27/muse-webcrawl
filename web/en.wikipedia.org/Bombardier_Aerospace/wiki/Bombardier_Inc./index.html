Bombardier Inc. is a Canadian business jet manufacturer headquartered in Montreal, Quebec. Founded by Joseph-Armand Bombardier in 1942 in Valcourt, Quebec, to manufacture snowmobiles (marketed as Ski-Doo), the company grew into a multinational transportation conglomerate producing aircraft, trains, and recreational products.

Bombardier Aerospace built the CRJ regional jets, the Challenger and Global business jets, and developed the CSeries airliner, whose program was transferred to Airbus in 2018 and renamed the A220. Financial pressure led Bombardier to divest its rail division to Alstom in 2021 and its remaining commercial aviation programs, leaving the company focused on business aviation.

Business jets

Today Bombardier produces the long-range Global family, including the Global 7500 flagship, and the super-midsize Challenger 3500, along with defence and special-mission variants of its aircraft.
