Canadair

Founded1944, Montreal, Quebec, Canada
FateAcquired by Bombardier in 1986
ProductsMilitary and civil aircraft, water bombers

Canadair Ltd. was a Canadian civil and military aircraft manufacturer based in Montreal. Founded in 1944, it produced aircraft under licence and of its own design, including the North Star airliner, licence-built Sabre and Starfighter fighters, the CL-215 and CL-415 water bombers used worldwide for aerial firefighting, and the Challenger business jet family begun in the 1970s.

The Canadian government sold Canadair to Bombardier in 1986. Under Bombardier, the Challenger design was stretched into the CRJ regional jet family, while the Canadair name survived for a time in products such as the Canadair Regional Jet.

Notable aircraft

Notable Canadair types include the Canadair Sabre, the CL-28 Argus maritime patrol aircraft, the CL-84 Dynavert tilt-wing experimental aircraft, the CL-215/CL-415 water bombers, and the Challenger 600 series business jets.
