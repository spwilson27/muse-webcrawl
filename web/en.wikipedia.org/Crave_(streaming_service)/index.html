Crave (streaming service)

Crave (formerly CraveTV) is a Canadian subscription video on demand service owned by Bell Media. Launched on 11 December 2014, it is available in English and French and carries programming from HBO, Showtime (historically), Warner Bros., and Bell Media's own networks, alongside original productions.

The service began as a complement to traditional television subscriptions but became available as a standalone subscription in 2016. In November 2018 it was relaunched as Crave, absorbing The Movie Network and the original CraveTV service, and adding tiered options that included HBO and theatrical films. A French-language offering, formerly Super Écran, is integrated for francophone subscribers.

Programming

Crave is the Canadian home of HBO series such as Game of Thrones, and produces Canadian originals including the comedy Letterkenny and its spin-off Shoresy, as well as Canada's Drag Race in partnership with other broadcasters.
