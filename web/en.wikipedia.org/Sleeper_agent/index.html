Sleeper agent

A sleeper agent is a spy or operative planted in a target country or organisation who does not carry out missions immediately but remains dormant, living an apparently ordinary life, until activated months or years later. Sleeper agents are typically inserted with deep cover identities ("legends") so they can be mobilised when a crisis or specific task arises.

Sleeper agent

Type: Espionage
Cover: Deep cover / illegal
Activation: On signal from handler service

Characteristics

Because they do nothing suspicious for long periods, sleeper agents are difficult for counterintelligence to detect; the danger is that cover built over years may decay, or the agent may "go native" and refuse or fail to act when called. Soviet and Russian intelligence famously used "illegals" — officers living under false identities abroad — some exposed in the 2010 Illegals Program arrests in the United States, when ten Russian sleepers were exchanged in a spy swap.

In popular culture

The figure of the sleeper agent is a staple of espionage fiction, from The Manchurian Candidate to the television series The Americans, which dramatised a pair of Soviet illegals in suburban Washington.
