Clube de Futebol "Os Belenenses", commonly known as Belenenses, is a Portuguese sports club from the Belém parish of Lisbon. Founded in 1919, it is best known for its football team and is one of only two clubs outside the "Big Three" (Benfica, Porto and Sporting CP) to have won the Portuguese championship, triumphing in 1945–46.

History

Belenenses won the Portuguese Cup three times and played at the Estádio do Restelo, overlooking the Tagus estuary. In 2018, a split between the club and its professional football company (SAD) led the SAD to continue in the top flight as Belenenses SAD while the original club restarted in the Lisbon district leagues; the club has since climbed back through the divisions.

Other sports

The multi-sport club also fields teams in handball, basketball, futsal, athletics and rugby, and its crest features the Cross of the Order of Christ, a symbol of the Age of Discoveries that departed from Belém.
