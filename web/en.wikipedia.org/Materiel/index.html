Materiel

Military equipment and supplies

Materiel (from French matériel, "equipment") refers to the equipment, apparatus and supplies — as opposed to personnel — used by an organisation, especially a military force. In armed-forces usage it encompasses weapons, vehicles, ammunition, fuel, spare parts, rations and medical supplies: everything needed to equip, operate, maintain and support military activities short of the people themselves.

Usage

The word is carefully distinguished in military writing from "material" in the general sense of raw substance. NATO and United States doctrine use materiel for finished items of supply and equipment managed through logistics systems, and the term appears in compounds such as "materiel management," "materiel readiness" and "war materiel." The distinction — personnel versus materiel — mirrors the French usage from which English borrowed the term in the nineteenth century.

Management

Modern armed forces track materiel through elaborate supply chains, stockpile planning and lifecycle management, from acquisition and storage through maintenance, distribution and disposal. The scale of the task was famously summarised in the saying that amateurs study tactics while professionals study logistics.
