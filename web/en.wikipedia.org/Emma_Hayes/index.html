Emma Carol Hayes OBE (born 18 October 1976) is an English professional football manager who is the head coach of the United States women's national soccer team. She previously managed Chelsea Women from 2012 to 2024, building the club into the dominant force in English women's football.

Career

Hayes played as a midfielder for Arsenal's academy before an ankle injury ended her playing career. She moved into coaching in the United States, working with Long Island Lady Riders and Iona Gaels, and then became assistant coach at Arsenal Ladies during their quadruple-winning 2006–07 season. She managed the Chicago Red Stars in Women's Professional Soccer before returning to England.

Chelsea

Appointed Chelsea manager in 2012, Hayes won seven Women's Super League titles, five Women's FA Cups and two League Cups with the club, completing a domestic treble in 2020–21 and reaching the UEFA Women's Champions League final. She was named FIFA Best Women's Coach for 2021.

United States

In November 2023 it was announced Hayes would become head coach of the United States women's national team after the 2023–24 WSL season. In her first tournament, the 2024 Paris Olympics, she led the USWNT to the gold medal, defeating Brazil in the final.
