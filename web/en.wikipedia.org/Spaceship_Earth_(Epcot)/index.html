Spaceship Earth (Epcot)

LocationEpcot, Walt Disney World, Florida
OpenedOctober 1, 1982
Height180 ft (55 m)
DesignerWalt Disney Imagineering

Spaceship Earth is a geodesic sphere that serves as the symbolic structure of Epcot, a theme park at the Walt Disney World Resort in Bay Lake, Florida. It is also the name of the dark ride housed within the sphere, which traces the history of human communication from prehistoric cave paintings to the computer age.

Structure

The 180-foot-tall sphere is a complete geodesic polyhedron weighing about 16 million pounds, supported on six legs and clad in 11,324 silver triangular facets. Its design derives from the work of Buckminster Fuller, who coined the term "Spaceship Earth". Rainwater is channelled through the structure so that no water ever pours off its sides onto guests below.

The ride

Guests travel in Omnimover vehicles through scenes depicting Cro-Magnon cave paintings, Egyptian hieroglyphs, the Phoenician alphabet, the Roman road system, Gutenberg's printing press, the Renaissance, and the telecommunications era. The attraction has been narrated in successive versions by Vic Perrin, Walter Cronkite, Jeremy Irons, and Judi Dench.
