List of highest mountains

This is a list of the highest mountains on Earth, ranked by elevation above sea level. All of the world's mountains over 7,200 metres (23,600 ft) are in Asia, in the ranges of the Himalayas, the Karakoram, the Hindu Kush, the Pamirs and the Kunlun. There are 14 mountains over 8,000 metres, known as the eight-thousanders. The highest is Mount Everest at 8,848.86 metres (29,031.7 ft).
