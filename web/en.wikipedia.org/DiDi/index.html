DiDi Global Inc. is a Chinese multinational mobility technology company headquartered in Beijing. It operates one of the world's largest ride-hailing platforms, offering app-based transportation services including taxi hailing, private car services, carpooling, and chauffeur services, along with food delivery, freight, and financial services in markets across Asia, Latin America, and elsewhere.

History

The company was founded in 2012 by Cheng Wei as Didi Dache, a taxi-hailing app backed by Tencent. After a costly subsidy war with Alibaba-backed rival Kuaidi Dache, the two merged in 2015 to form Didi Kuaidi, later rebranded Didi Chuxing. In 2016 DiDi acquired Uber's China operations in a deal that gave Uber a stake in the combined company, ending their competition in the Chinese market. DiDi subsequently expanded internationally, investing in or acquiring local platforms such as Brazil's 99.

Regulatory scrutiny

DiDi listed on the New York Stock Exchange in June 2021; days later, Chinese regulators launched a cybersecurity review, ordered its apps removed from Chinese app stores over data-protection concerns, and in 2022 fined the company more than 8 billion yuan. DiDi delisted from New York in 2022 and its shares moved to over-the-counter trading; its apps were restored to Chinese app stores in early 2023.

Technology ventures

Beyond ride-hailing, DiDi has invested heavily in autonomous driving through its self-driving unit, launched robotaxi pilots in Chinese cities, and developed electric vehicles in partnership with automakers for its driver fleet.
