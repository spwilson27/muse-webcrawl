Kiruna Municipality

Kiruna Municipality (Swedish: Kiruna kommun) is a municipality in Norrbotten County in northern Sweden, the northernmost and, by area, the largest municipality in the country. Its seat is the mining town of Kiruna.

Geography

The municipality covers some 20,000 square kilometres, including Sweden's highest mountain, Kebnekaise, and Abisko National Park. Much of the population is concentrated in Kiruna town, founded around the LKAB iron ore mine, one of the world's largest underground iron ore mines.

Town relocation

Mining-induced ground deformation has required moving the centre of Kiruna several kilometres east—a multi-decade project relocating or rebuilding thousands of buildings, including the historic wooden Kiruna Church, moved intact in 2025.
