Abe Lenstra Stadion

Location: Heerenveen, Friesland, Netherlands

Opened: 20 August 1994

Capacity: about 26,100

Tenant: SC Heerenveen

The Abe Lenstra Stadion is a football stadium in Heerenveen, Netherlands, and the home ground of Eredivisie club SC Heerenveen. It is named after Abe Lenstra, the club's most famous player, whose statue stands outside the main entrance.

History

The stadium was officially opened on 20 August 1994 with a friendly match against PSV Eindhoven, which Heerenveen won 2–0 — the first goal in the new ground being scored by Erik Regtop. It replaced the club's old Noordwand/Sportpark Noord ground as Heerenveen established itself in the top flight. The venue has been expanded several times, most notably around the club's UEFA Cup campaigns of the 2000s, bringing capacity to roughly 26,100 seats, and the club has periodically proposed enlarging it further to around 32,000.

Features

The ground is fully enclosed with four covered stands and is frequently noted for its pre-match playing of the Frisian anthem, "De âlde Friezen." It has hosted Netherlands national team fixtures at youth and women's level, as well as concerts and community events, and it forms part of the Sportstad Heerenveen complex near the Thialf ice arena.
