Bungie, Inc. is an American video game company based in Bellevue, Washington. The company was established in May 1991 by Alex Seropian, who later brought in programmer Jason Jones. Initially based in Chicago, Bungie focused on games for Apple Macintosh computers and developed early successes such as the Marathon first-person shooter trilogy (1994–1996) and the Myth real-time tactics series.

Microsoft era and Halo

Microsoft acquired Bungie in 2000, and its in-development game became Halo: Combat Evolved, a launch title for the Xbox in 2001. Halo became a flagship franchise for Xbox, and Bungie produced the sequels Halo 2 (2004) and Halo 3 (2007), plus Halo 3: ODST (2009) and Halo: Reach (2010). In 2007 Bungie split from Microsoft to become an independent studio again, while Microsoft retained the Halo intellectual property, handing it to 343 Industries.

Destiny and Sony acquisition

In 2010 Bungie signed a ten-year publishing deal with Activision and developed the shared-world shooter Destiny (2014) and Destiny 2 (2017). Bungie ended the Activision partnership in 2019, retaining the Destiny rights and self-publishing. In July 2022, Sony Interactive Entertainment acquired Bungie for $3.6 billion; the studio remained a multi-platform, independently operated subsidiary. Bungie has also revived Marathon as an extraction shooter. The studio has undergone rounds of layoffs in 2023 and 2024 as part of wider industry contraction and restructuring under Sony.

Culture

Bungie is known for a strong community culture, its "Bungie Day" observances on July 7, and long-running in-jokes and elaborate alternate-reality games promoting its releases. Its mottoes and the number seven recur throughout its games.
