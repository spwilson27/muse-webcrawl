Red Hand Defenders

The Red Hand Defenders (RHD) is an Ulster loyalist paramilitary group in Northern Ireland. It was formed in 1998 by loyalists who opposed the Good Friday Agreement and the ceasefires of the main loyalist organizations, and it is closely linked to the Ulster Defence Association (UDA) and the Loyalist Volunteer Force (LVF), whose members have used it as a cover name.

Activities

The group claimed responsibility for a series of sectarian attacks, including murders of Catholic civilians and a postal worker, Daniel Coffey among the postal workers targeted in 2001, and the killing of journalist Martin O'Hagan in September 2001, the only journalist killed during the Troubles' later phase. It also carried out pipe bomb attacks.

Status

The Red Hand Defenders is designated a terrorist organization by the United Kingdom and the United States. Because the name has functioned largely as a flag of convenience for UDA members, it is unclear that it ever existed as a fully separate organization.
