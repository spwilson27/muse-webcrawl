Mario Götze (born 3 June 1992) is a German professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder. He is best known for scoring the winning goal in the 2014 FIFA World Cup final against Argentina, which gave Germany its fourth World Cup title.

Club career

Götze came through the youth academy of Borussia Dortmund and made his Bundesliga debut in 2009. Under Jürgen Klopp he became a central figure in the side that won the Bundesliga in 2010–11 and the league and cup double in 2011–12, and reached the 2013 UEFA Champions League final. In 2013 he transferred to Bayern Munich for a fee of €37 million, then a record for a German player, winning three further Bundesliga titles but struggling with injuries and form.

He returned to Dortmund in 2016, where a metabolic disorder (myopathy) was diagnosed in 2017, sidelining him for several months. After leaving Dortmund in 2020 he joined Dutch club PSV Eindhoven, winning the KNVB Cup in 2022, and then signed for Eintracht Frankfurt, where he continued his career in the Bundesliga.

International career

Götze debuted for Germany in 2010. At the 2014 World Cup in Brazil he came off the bench in the final and scored the only goal in the 113th minute, controlling André Schürrle's cross and volleying home — the first World Cup final winning goal scored by a substitute. He also represented Germany at Euro 2012 and Euro 2016 and the 2022 World Cup.
