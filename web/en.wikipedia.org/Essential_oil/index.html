Essential oil

An essential oil is a concentrated hydrophobic liquid containing volatile chemical compounds from plants. Essential oils are also known as volatile oils, ethereal oils, or simply as the oil of the plant from which they were extracted, such as oil of clove.

Production

Essential oils are generally extracted by distillation, often using steam, or by cold pressing and solvent extraction. They capture the plant's scent and flavour compounds, including terpenes and other aromatics.

Uses

They are used in perfumes, cosmetics, soaps, flavourings for food and drink, incense, and household cleaning products, and are central to aromatherapy. Some oils can be skin irritants or toxic if ingested undiluted.
