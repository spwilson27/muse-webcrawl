Battle of Grozny (1994–95)

The Battle of Grozny of 1994–95 was the Russian Army's offensive to capture the Chechen capital during the First Chechen War. The assault began with a disastrous New Year's Eve attack on 31 December 1994 and ended with Russian forces taking the ruined city in March 1995.

Course

Russian armoured columns advancing into the city centre were ambushed by Chechen fighters operating from basements and rooftops, suffering very heavy losses — the 131st Maikop Brigade was nearly destroyed. Russian forces then levelled Grozny with massed artillery and air power before occupying it. The battle is studied as a cautionary example of armoured operations in urban terrain.
