Gymnastics at the 1952 Summer Olympics was held in Helsinki, Finland, as part of the Games of the XV Olympiad. Fifteen events were contested — nine for men and six for women. The Games marked the Olympic debut of the Soviet Union, whose gymnasts immediately dominated the sport, beginning a decades-long era of Soviet pre-eminence in gymnastics.

Men's events

The outstanding male gymnast was Viktor Chukarin of the Soviet Union, who won four gold medals including the individual all-around, pommel horse, vault and team all-around, plus two silvers. Switzerland's Hans Eugster won gold on the parallel bars, and Sweden's William Thoresson took the floor exercise title. The USSR won the team competition.

Women's events

Among the women, Soviet gymnast Maria Gorokhovskaya won the individual all-around and, remarkably, a medal in every women's event — two gold and five silver medals, a record haul for a female athlete at a single Games that stood for decades. Hungarian Ágnes Keleti won the floor exercise gold, foreshadowing her greater triumphs in 1956. The women's programme included the one-off team portable apparatus event, won by Sweden.
