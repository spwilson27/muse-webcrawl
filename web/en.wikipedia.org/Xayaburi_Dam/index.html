Xayaburi Dam

The Xayaburi Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the mainstream of the Mekong River in Xayaboury Province, northern Laos. With a capacity of about 1,285 megawatts, it was the first dam built across the lower Mekong mainstream, and began operating in 2019.

Development

The project was developed by Thai interests, with most electricity sold to Thailand. Construction proceeded from 2012 despite objections from Cambodia and Vietnam under the Mekong River Commission's consultation process; the dam was redesigned with fish passages and sediment flushing in response.

Controversy

Critics warn of impacts on fisheries, sediment flow and the Mekong Delta, and on species such as the Mekong giant catfish. Supporters cite Laos's strategy of becoming the 'battery of Southeast Asia' through hydropower exports.
