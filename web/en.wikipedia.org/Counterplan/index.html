Counterplan

A counterplan is a component of argumentation in policy debate. It is an alternative course of action proposed by the negative team that competes with the affirmative plan and avoids its disadvantages.

Structure

A counterplan typically has a text, an agent, and net benefits. The negative must show it is competitive — mutually exclusive with, or producing net benefit over, doing the plan — and that it solves the affirmative harms better or avoids a disadvantage. Types include agent counterplans, exclusionary counterplans, consult counterplans and plan-inclusive counterplans (PICs). Permutations test competition by proposing doing both plan and counterplan.
