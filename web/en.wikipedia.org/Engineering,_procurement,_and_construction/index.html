Engineering, procurement, and construction

Engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) is a common form of contracting arrangement in the construction and industrial sectors. Under an EPC contract, a single contractor is responsible for designing a facility, procuring all equipment and materials, and constructing the project, delivering it to the client ready for operation — often called a "turnkey" contract.

Features

EPC contracts typically place cost and schedule risk on the contractor in exchange for a fixed price (lump-sum turnkey, or LSTK). They are widely used for power plants, refineries, petrochemical plants, and infrastructure, where performance guarantees and a single point of responsibility are important to lenders and owners.

Variants

Related models include EPCM (engineering, procurement, and construction management), in which the contractor manages the project on the owner's behalf without taking direct construction risk, and design–bid–build, which separates design and construction contracts.
