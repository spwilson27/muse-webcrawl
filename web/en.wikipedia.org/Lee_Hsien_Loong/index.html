Lee Hsien Loong (born 10 February 1952) is a Singaporean politician and former brigadier-general who served as the third Prime Minister of Singapore from 2004 to 2024, and as secretary-general of the ruling People's Action Party (PAP) over the same period. The eldest son of Singapore's founding prime minister Lee Kuan Yew, he succeeded Goh Chok Tong and in turn handed over to Lawrence Wong on 15 May 2024, remaining in cabinet as Senior Minister.

Early life and career

Lee studied mathematics at Trinity College, Cambridge, graduating with first-class honours as Senior Wrangler, and later took a Master of Public Administration at Harvard's Kennedy School. Commissioned in the Singapore Armed Forces in 1971, he rose to Brigadier-General, commanding the artillery and serving as Director of Joint Operations and Plans, before leaving the army in 1984 to enter politics as MP for Teck Ghee (later Ang Mo Kio GRC). He held the trade and industry and defence portfolios and became Deputy Prime Minister in 1990; as finance minister from 2001 he steered liberalisation of the financial sector.

Premiership

Lee's two decades in office saw Singapore's population and economy grow substantially, with the opening of the two integrated resorts (Marina Bay Sands and Resorts World Sentosa), the first Formula One night race (2008), and continued investment in education, housing renewal and transport — though rail breakdowns, immigration levels and the cost of living became persistent political issues. His government managed the 2008 financial crisis and the COVID-19 pandemic, and in 2022 repealed Section 377A, the colonial-era law criminalising sex between men, while amending the constitution to protect the definition of marriage. The PAP's vote share dipped to a record-low 61.2% in the 2020 general election before recovering to 65.6% in 2025, held after the handover. A public family dispute in 2017 over the house at 38 Oxley Road, involving his siblings Lee Hsien Yang and Lee Wei Ling, drew unusual scrutiny to the family.

Personal life

Lee is married to Ho Ching, former chief executive of Temasek Holdings. He survived lymphoma diagnosed in 1992 and prostate cancer surgery in 2015. He is known as an able programmer — he has published Sudoku solvers written in C++ — and delivers his National Day Rally in Singapore's three official languages.
