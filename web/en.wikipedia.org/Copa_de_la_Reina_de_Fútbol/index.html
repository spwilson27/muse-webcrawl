The Copa de la Reina de Fútbol (English: Queen's Cup of Football) is an annual cup competition for Spanish women's football organized by the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF). It is the women's equivalent of the men's Copa del Rey.

History

The competition began in 1983 as the Copa de la Reina, following an unofficial championship for the Copa de la Reina held by the federation in the early 1980s; it was not officially recognised by the RFEF until later. For many years the tournament was contested by a small number of teams at the end of the league season. In the 2010s, as Spanish women's football professionalised, the cup was expanded to include all teams from the top flight, the Liga F.

Format

The cup is played as a single-elimination knockout tournament, with ties generally played as single matches. The final is staged at a neutral venue. The winner traditionally qualifies for the Supercopa de España Femenina.

Successful clubs

FC Barcelona Femení is the most successful club in the competition's history, having won the cup on numerous occasions, including several titles during their dominant period in the 2010s and 2020s. Other multiple winners include Levante UD and RCD Espanyol.
