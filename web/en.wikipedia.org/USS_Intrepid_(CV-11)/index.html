USS Intrepid (CV-11)

USS Intrepid
Class: Essex-class aircraft carrier
Launched: April 26, 1943
Decommissioned: March 15, 1974
Status: Museum ship in New York City

USS Intrepid (CV/CVA/CVS-11), also known as "The Fighting I", is one of 24 Essex-class aircraft carriers built during World War II for the United States Navy. She is the fourth US Navy ship to bear the name.

Service history

Commissioned in 1943, Intrepid participated in the Pacific campaign, surviving several kamikaze strikes and a torpedo hit. Modernized in the 1950s, she served as an anti-submarine carrier and recovered the Mercury-Atlas 7 and Gemini 3 space capsules. She also served three tours off Vietnam.

Museum

Since 1982 she has been berthed at Pier 86 in Manhattan as the centerpiece of the Intrepid Sea, Air & Space Museum, which also displays the Space Shuttle Enterprise.
