The World Rally-Raid Championship (W2RC) is an international rally raid championship sanctioned jointly by the FIA for cars and trucks and the FIM for motorcycles and quads. Launched in 2022 under the promotion of the Amaury Sport Organisation, it unified the former FIA World Cup for Cross-Country Rallies and FIM Cross-Country Rallies World Championship into a single series.

Calendar

The championship is anchored by the Dakar Rally, held each January in Saudi Arabia, which opens the season. Other rounds have included the Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge, the Sonora Rally in Mexico, the Desafío Ruta 40 in Argentina, the Rallye du Maroc, and Portugal's BP Ultimate Rally-Raid. Events typically run over five days of timed special stages covering thousands of kilometres of desert and mountain terrain.

Classes

FIA categories include Ultimate (T1) prototype cars, Challenger (T3) lightweight prototypes, SSV (T4), Stock (T2), and Trucks (T5). FIM competitors race in RallyGP, Rally2, Rally3, and quad classes. World titles are awarded to drivers, co-drivers, riders, and manufacturers.

Champions

Nasser Al-Attiyah won the inaugural FIA drivers' title in 2022 and repeated in 2023, while Sam Sunderland claimed the first FIM RallyGP crown. The series has featured leading manufacturers and teams including Toyota Gazoo Racing, Audi Sport (with its hybrid RS Q e-tron), Prodrive/Bahrain Raid Xtreme, KTM, Honda, and Hero MotoSports.
