Tórshavn is the capital and largest city of the Faroe Islands. It lies on the southern part of the island of Streymoy and is named after the Norse god Thor, meaning "Thor's harbour".

History

The Viking settlers established their parliament (the Løgting) on the Tinganes peninsula in Tórshavn in 850 AD, making it one of the oldest parliamentary seats in the world. The town grew around the trading post established there.

Geography and climate

Tórshavn has a subpolar oceanic climate with mild winters and cool summers, heavily moderated by the North Atlantic. Fog and changeable weather are frequent.

Transport

The harbour serves ferries to Denmark, Iceland, and the other islands, and the city hosts the national stadium, Tórsvøllur.
