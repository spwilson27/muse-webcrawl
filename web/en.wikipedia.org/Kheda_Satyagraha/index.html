Kheda Satyagraha

The Kheda Satyagraha of 1918 was a nonviolent tax-resistance campaign in Kheda district, Gujarat, India, led by Mahatma Gandhi with Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, supporting peasants hit by famine and plague.

Course

Despite crop failure entitling them to tax remission under the revenue code, officials demanded full payment. Villagers pledged not to pay and endured confiscations; the government eventually agreed to suspend taxes for the poorest, ending the campaign in mid-1918.
