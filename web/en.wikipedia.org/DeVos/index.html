DeVos

DeVos is a Dutch surname, meaning "the fox." It is borne by several notable families and individuals, most prominently the American DeVos family of Michigan, co-founders of the multi-level marketing company Amway.

The DeVos family

Richard DeVos (1926–2018) co-founded Amway in 1959 with Jay Van Andel and became owner of the Orlando Magic NBA franchise. His son Dick DeVos served as Amway's president and was the Republican nominee for Governor of Michigan in 2006. Dick's wife, Betsy DeVos, served as the 11th United States Secretary of Education from 2017 to 2021, and is known for advocacy of school choice and charter schools.

Other people

Other notable people with the surname include Belgian cyclist and officials, South African rugby players, and Dutch academics. The name also appears in place and institution names in West Michigan, including DeVos Place convention center and the DeVos Children's Hospital in Grand Rapids.
