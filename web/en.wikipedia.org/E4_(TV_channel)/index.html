E4 is a British free-to-air public broadcast television channel owned and operated by Channel Four Television Corporation. Launched on 18 January 2001 as a pay channel companion to Channel 4, it targets a young adult audience, principally viewers aged 16 to 34, with a mix of comedy, drama, reality programming and imported American series.

History

E4 began as a subscription channel on Sky and cable platforms, built around entertainment programming and spin-offs from Channel 4 shows. It became free-to-air in May 2005 in time for the sixth series of Big Brother, whose live streaming and spin-off shows were a mainstay of the early schedule. The channel moved to free-to-air permanently and gained a timeshift service, E4 +1.

Programming

Original commissions have included the teen drama Skins, the comedy The Inbetweeners, Misfits, Fresh Meat and the reality dating series Married at First Sight UK. E4 has also been the UK home of American imports such as Friends repeats, The Big Bang Theory, Scrubs, Smallville and Brooklyn Nine-Nine. Its coverage of Big Brother spin-offs such as Big Brother's Little Brother defined its early identity.

Branding

The channel is known for its irreverent continuity announcements and distinctive idents. A sister channel, E4 Extra, replaced 4Music in 2022 and carries additional comedy and entertainment programming.
