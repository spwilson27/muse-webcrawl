Quotations from Chairman Mao Tse-tung

The "Little Red Book"

Quotations from Chairman Mao Tse-tung is a book of statements drawn from the speeches and writings of Mao Zedong, chairman of the Chinese Communist Party, published from 1964 to about 1976. Universally known in the West as the Little Red Book for its pocket-sized red vinyl cover, it is one of the most printed books in history, with official print runs exceeding a billion copies.

Contents

The book contains 427 quotations organised into 33 chapters, covering the Party, class struggle, war, culture, and personal conduct, compiled under the direction of Lin Biao for the political education of the People's Liberation Army. Its foreword urges readers to "study Chairman Mao's writings, follow his teachings and act according to his instructions".

Role in the Cultural Revolution

During the Cultural Revolution, possession and study of the book were effectively compulsory in China; Red Guards carried it everywhere, and public rituals involved waving it and reciting passages. It was translated into dozens of languages and exported worldwide, influencing Maoist movements abroad.

After Mao

Distribution ended after Mao's death in 1976, and copies were withdrawn during the reforms of Deng Xiaoping. Surviving copies are now collectors' items, and the book remains an enduring symbol of the personality cult of the Cultural Revolution era.
