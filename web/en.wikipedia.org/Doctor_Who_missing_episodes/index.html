Doctor Who missing episodes

The Doctor Who missing episodes are 97 episodes of the BBC science fiction series Doctor Who from the 1960s that are no longer held in the BBC's archives, the videotapes and film copies having been wiped or junked during the 1960s and 1970s when recordings were routinely erased for reuse.

Loss and recovery

The missing material comes from the First and Second Doctor eras (William Hartnell and Patrick Troughton); all episodes from 1970 onward survive. Since the late 1970s, copies have been recovered from overseas broadcasters — notably in Nigeria, Australia, New Zealand and Hong Kong — reducing the number missing from over 140 to 97.

Reconstructions

Complete soundtracks survive for every missing episode thanks to off-air recordings made by fans. These have been used to create telesnap reconstructions, official BBC audio releases with narration, and animated recreations of stories such as The Power of the Daleks and The Macra Terror. Entire serials such as The Enemy of the World and The Web of Fear were unexpectedly returned in 2013.
