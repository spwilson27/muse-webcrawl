Cash Money Records is an American record label founded in 1991 by brothers Bryan "Birdman" (also "Baby") Williams and Ronald "Slim" Williams in New Orleans, Louisiana. The label rose from a regional independent to one of the most successful hip hop labels in the world.

History

After years of selling records locally, Cash Money signed a landmark pressing and distribution deal with Universal Records in 1998, retaining ownership of its masters. The late-1990s roster — Juvenile, B.G., Turk, and Lil Wayne, who performed together as the Hot Boys — brought national hits such as Juvenile's "Back That Azz Up".

Young Money era

In the 2000s and 2010s, Lil Wayne became the label's star and founded the imprint Young Money Entertainment, which launched the careers of Drake and Nicki Minaj, making Cash Money dominant in commercial rap. The label has also been the subject of numerous lawsuits and public disputes over royalties and contracts, including a high-profile split and reconciliation with Lil Wayne.
