Counts of Schauenburg and Holstein

The Counts of Schauenburg and Holstein were a German noble dynasty that ruled Holstein and Stormarn from 1106, as well as Schaumburg, from 1110/1111 until 1459 in Holstein and later in Schaumburg until 1640.

History

Adolf I was enfeoffed with Holstein by Lothair of Supplinburg. His successors expanded eastward, founded Lübeck, and divided into lines including Holstein-Kiel, Holstein-Plön, Holstein-Rendsburg and Holstein-Pinneberg. Gerhard I and his descendants contended with Denmark. When Adolf VIII died childless in 1459, Holstein passed by election to Christian I of Denmark, beginning Danish rule.
