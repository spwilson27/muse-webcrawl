Webelos

Webelos is a program within Cub Scouting, part of the Boy Scouts of America (now Scouting America), for boys and girls in the fourth and fifth grades (ages 9–11). It serves as the transition between Cub Scouts and Scouts BSA.

Name and program

The name is conventionally explained as an acronym for "We'll Be Loyal Scouts". Webelos Scouts work on adventures and earn activity badges, meeting in dens led by adult leaders and increasingly taking part in outdoor activities such as camping. The program's highest award is the Arrow of Light, the only Cub Scout award that can be worn on the Scouts BSA uniform.

Structure

Fourth graders are Webelos Scouts and fifth graders work toward the Arrow of Light rank. On completing the program, typically in the spring of fifth grade, Webelos "cross over" to a Scouts BSA troop in a formal ceremony.
