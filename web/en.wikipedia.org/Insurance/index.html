Insurance

Insurance pools risk across many policyholders to protect against uncertain losses.

Insurance is a means of protection from financial loss in which, in exchange for a fee (a premium), a party agrees to guarantee another party compensation in the event of a certain loss, damage, or injury. It is a form of risk management, primarily used to protect against the risk of a contingent or uncertain loss.

History

Methods of transferring or distributing risk were practised by Chinese and Indian traders as early as the 3rd and 2nd millennia BC. Marine insurance developed in medieval Italy, and the modern insurance market is often traced to Lloyd's of London, which began as a coffee house in the late 17th century where merchants and shipowners gathered to underwrite voyages. The first life insurance policies were taken out in the early 18th century, and fire insurance expanded after events such as the Great Fire of London in 1666.

Principles

Insurance operates through the pooling of risks: many policyholders pay premiums into a fund from which the losses of the few are paid. Insurers use actuarial science to estimate expected losses and set premiums. Core principles include utmost good faith, insurable interest, indemnity, subrogation, and contribution. Contracts specify the policyholder's premium, the deductible (excess), coverage limits, and exclusions.

Types

  • Life insurance — pays a benefit on the death of the insured or after a set period.
  • Health insurance — covers medical and surgical expenses.
  • Property insurance — protects against risks to property such as fire, theft, or weather damage.
  • Liability insurance — covers legal claims against the insured.
  • Auto insurance — covers vehicles and related liability; compulsory in many jurisdictions.

Reinsurance

Insurers may themselves buy insurance — reinsurance — to spread their own exposure to large or catastrophic losses. Reinsurance markets are centred in places such as London, Bermuda, and Zurich, and firms such as Munich Re and Swiss Re are among the largest reinsurers.
