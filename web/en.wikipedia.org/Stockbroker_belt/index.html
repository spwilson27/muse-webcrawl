Stockbroker belt

RegionSurrey and the Home Counties, England
CharacterAffluent commuter towns and villages

The stockbroker belt is a loosely defined area around London, particularly in Surrey, Berkshire and Buckinghamshire, known for its large detached houses, leafy villages and affluent residents, many of whom commute to the City of London to work in finance and related professions.

Characteristics

Towns and villages associated with the stockbroker belt include Esher, Weybridge, Cobham, Virginia Water and Gerrards Cross. The area developed from the late nineteenth century as railways made commuting practical, and large houses were built for prosperous businessmen and financiers. The term is now used more broadly for any wealthy commuter settlement outside London.
