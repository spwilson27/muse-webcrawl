Gian Paolo Dallara (born 16 November 1936) is an Italian businessman and engineer, founder of the racing-car manufacturer Dallara, one of the world's largest producers of competition automobiles.

Career

After graduating in aeronautical engineering from the Polytechnic University of Milan, Dallara joined Ferrari in 1959, then moved to Maserati and, in 1963, to Lamborghini, where as chief engineer he led the design of the Espada and the mid-engined Miura. At De Tomaso he worked on Formula One and sports-racing projects.

Dallara Automobili

In 1972 Dallara founded his own company near Parma. It became dominant in Formula Three, supplied chassis to Scuderia Italia in Formula One (1988–1992), and from 1997 became the near-exclusive chassis supplier to the IndyCar Series. Dallara chassis also serve Formula 2 and numerous other single-seater categories, and the firm consults on road-car projects for manufacturers including Alfa Romeo, Bugatti and KTM.
