Theatre Royal, Drury Lane

The Theatre Royal, Drury Lane, commonly known as Drury Lane, is a West End theatre in Covent Garden, London. A theatre has stood on the site since 1663, making it the oldest theatre site in London still in use; the present building, the fourth on the site, opened in 1812.

History

The first theatre of 1663 was built by Thomas Killigrew under a royal patent from Charles II. The theatre is associated with figures such as David Garrick, Richard Brinsley Sheridan and Edmund Kean. It is reputedly one of London's most haunted theatres.

Today

The theatre, owned by Andrew Lloyd Webber's LW Theatres, primarily hosts large-scale musicals and underwent a major restoration completed in 2021.
