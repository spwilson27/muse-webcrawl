Resurrection of Jesus

The resurrection of Jesus is the Christian belief that God raised Jesus from the dead after his crucifixion, on the third day, as the first of the general resurrection of the dead. It is the central doctrine of Christian faith: as Paul wrote, "if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith" (1 Corinthians 15:14).

New Testament accounts

The earliest account is Paul's summary in 1 Corinthians 15, listing appearances to Peter, the Twelve, more than five hundred others, James, and Paul himself. The four Gospels describe the discovery of the empty tomb by women followers — Mary Magdalene foremost — and appearances of the risen Jesus in Jerusalem and Galilee. Christians commemorate the resurrection at Easter.

Interpretations

Orthodox Christian teaching affirms a bodily resurrection. Historians, applying naturalistic methods, debate the origins of the disciples' resurrection experiences and the empty tomb tradition; alternative explanations proposed include visionary experiences, grief hallucinations, and legendary development, none commanding consensus.
