Paramount Pictures Corporation is an American film production and distribution company and the flagship studio of Paramount Global. Founded in 1912, it is the fifth-oldest film studio in the world and the last major studio still headquartered in Hollywood.

History

Paramount grew out of Adolph Zukor's Famous Players Film Company and Jesse L. Lasky's Feature Play Company, distributed through W. W. Hodkinson's Paramount Pictures from 1914 and formally merged in 1916. The studio flourished in the silent and early sound eras with stars such as Mary Pickford and Rudolph Valentino, and films including Wings (1927), the first Best Picture Oscar winner. Gulf+Western acquired Paramount in 1966, and Viacom acquired it in 1994.

Franchises and notable films

Paramount's catalogue includes The Godfather, Titanic (co-production), Star Trek, Mission: Impossible, Transformers, Indiana Jones, and Top Gun, whose sequel Top Gun: Maverick became one of the highest-grossing films of 2022.

Logo

The mountain-and-stars logo, inspired by the Wasatch Range and a drawing associated with Hodkinson, is one of cinema's most enduring trademarks.
