Paramount Global is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate. It was formed on 4 December 2019 as ViacomCBS through the merger of CBS Corporation and Viacom, and was renamed Paramount Global on 16 February 2022, taking its name from the historic Paramount Pictures studio.

Assets

The company's principal assets include the CBS television network, Paramount Pictures, cable channels such as MTV, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central and BET, and the streaming services Paramount+ and Pluto TV. Its businesses are organised into TV media, filmed entertainment, and direct-to-consumer segments.

History

CBS and Viacom had previously merged in 2000 and split again in 2006, both remaining under the control of Sumner Redstone's National Amusements. Their 2019 recombination brought the broadcast network, studio, and cable brands under one company at a time of consolidation in the streaming era. In 2024 the company agreed to merge with Skydance Media, a deal expected to close in 2025.
