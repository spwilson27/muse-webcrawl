Goal (association football)

In association football, a goal is scored when the whole of the ball passes over the goal line, between the goalposts and under the crossbar, provided no offence has been committed by the scoring team. Goals are the sole method of scoring in the game, and the team scoring more goals wins the match.

The Laws of the Game (Law 10) determine the result of a match based on goals scored. Goal-line technology and the video assistant referee are used in many competitions to resolve close decisions. A player may score directly from kick-off, a free kick or a corner kick; a goal scored in a player's own net is an own goal credited to the opposing team.
