Goal (sport)


A football goal.

In sports, a goal is the physical structure or area into which a player must direct a ball or puck to score points, and by extension the score itself. Goals are central to many games including association football, hockey, lacrosse, handball, water polo and Gaelic games.

Association football

In football, a goal is scored when the whole of the ball passes over the goal line, between the goalposts and under the crossbar, provided no offence has been committed. The goal structure is 7.32 m (8 yards) wide and 2.44 m (8 feet) high. The team scoring more goals wins the match.

Ice hockey

In ice hockey a goal is scored when the puck completely crosses the goal line between the posts and beneath the crossbar of the 72-inch-wide, 48-inch-high net, typically worth one point toward a player's scoring statistics (a goal or assist).

Other sports

In Gaelic football and hurling, a goal (worth three points) is scored by sending the ball under the crossbar into the net. In Australian rules football the goal, between two tall inner posts, is worth six points. In bandy, field hockey and lacrosse goals follow similar designs. The word also extends metaphorically in sports statistics, as in "field goal" in basketball and American football — though there the scoring act, not a structure entered by the ball into a defended net, is meant.
