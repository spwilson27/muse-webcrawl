Metric signature

In mathematics, the signature of a metric tensor or quadratic form is the number of positive, negative, and zero eigenvalues of the associated symmetric matrix. For a non-degenerate form, the signature is conventionally given as a pair (p, q) counting the numbers of positive and negative directions.

Relativity

In general relativity, the spacetime metric has Lorentzian signature. Two conventions are used: the mostly-plus signature (−, +, +, +) common in particle physics, and the mostly-minus signature (+, −, −, −) common in relativity texts. The signature determines which intervals are timelike, spacelike, or null.

Sylvester's law

By Sylvester's law of inertia, the signature of a real quadratic form is invariant under change of basis, making it a fundamental invariant of the form.
