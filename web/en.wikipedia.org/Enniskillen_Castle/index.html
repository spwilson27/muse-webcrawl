Enniskillen Castle

Enniskillen Castle
Location: Enniskillen, County Fermanagh, Northern Ireland
Built: 15th–16th centuries
Occupants: Maguire clan, later British forces

Enniskillen Castle is a castle in Enniskillen, County Fermanagh, Northern Ireland, standing on the banks of the River Erne. It was built by the Maguire chieftains who ruled Fermanagh.

History

The first castle was built in the early 15th century by Hugh Maguire. It guarded a strategic ford and river crossing. Captured by English forces in the 1590s during the Nine Years' War, it was later rebuilt and served as a military barracks.

Architecture

The complex includes a keep, curtain walls and the distinctive Watergate, a turreted building added in the 17th century that has become a symbol of Enniskillen.

Museum

The castle now houses the Fermanagh County Museum and the Inniskillings Museum, devoted to the Royal Inniskilling Fusiliers and the Royal Inniskilling Dragoon Guards.
