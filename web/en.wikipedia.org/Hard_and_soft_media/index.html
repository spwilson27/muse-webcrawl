Hard and soft media

Hard media and soft media are terms used in several fields to contrast rigid or durable formats with flexible or less durable ones, notably in art materials, print media, and the classification of news content.

In news

In political communication research, "hard news" covers politics, economics, and public affairs reported in durable, factual formats, while "soft news" or soft media emphasises entertainment, human interest, and sensation. Scholars debate whether soft media can inform citizens incidentally.

In materials and art

In art and engineering contexts the distinction refers to physical properties: hard media such as stone, metal, and hardboard versus soft media such as textiles, paper, and pastels. See also Hard news.
