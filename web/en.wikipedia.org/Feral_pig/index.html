Feral pig

A feral pig is a domestic pig (Sus scrofa domesticus) that has escaped or been released into the wild and reverted to a wild state, or a descendant of such animals. Feral pigs are established on every continent except Antarctica and are among the world's most destructive invasive species, causing extensive damage to agriculture and ecosystems. In many regions they interbreed with wild boar; in the United States, escaped Eurasian boar released for hunting have produced hybrid populations commonly called feral hogs or razorbacks.

A feral pig in woodland habitat.

Characteristics

Within a few generations — and partly within a single lifetime — feral pigs develop the coarse, grizzled coat, longer snout, tusks and leaner build of wild boar, an example of phenotypic plasticity in response to environment, diet and selective pressure. They are highly intelligent, opportunistic omnivores, eating roots, tubers, invertebrates, eggs, carrion and crops. Their rooting behaviour, in which they plough up soil with their snouts, is a major source of environmental damage.

Distribution and impact

Pigs were carried around the world by European explorers and settlers — Columbus, de Soto and Captain Cook all released them — and escaped livestock have founded populations ever since. In Australia, feral pigs number in the millions and damage wetlands, spread weeds and the root-rot pathogen Phytophthora cinnamomi, and prey on native fauna. In the United States, an estimated six million or more feral hogs across at least 35 states cause agricultural and property damage estimated in the billions of dollars annually; they also carry diseases such as brucellosis and pseudorabies that threaten livestock.

Control

Management methods include trapping (the most effective technique for whole sounders), aerial and ground shooting, exclusion fencing, hunting with dogs, and toxicants. Fertility control has been researched but not widely deployed. Because sows can produce two litters a year, populations can rebound quickly, and eradication is generally feasible only on islands or in newly invaded areas.
