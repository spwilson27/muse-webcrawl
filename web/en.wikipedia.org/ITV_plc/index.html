ITV plc is a British media company that holds 13 of the 15 regional television licences that make up the ITV network (Channel 3), the oldest and largest commercial terrestrial television network in the United Kingdom. ITV plc is listed on the London Stock Exchange.

History

ITV plc was formed on 2 February 2004 by the merger of Granada plc and Carlton Communications, the two largest holders of ITV franchises, after years of consolidation in the network. The remaining independent regional licensees are STV (central and northern Scotland) and UTV in Northern Ireland (acquired by ITV plc in 2016). ITV plc operates the channels ITV1, ITV2, ITV3, ITV4, ITVBe and CITV (closed as a channel in 2023), and launched the streaming service ITVX in December 2022, replacing ITV Hub.

Operations

The company's activities are divided into two divisions. ITV Studios is one of the world's largest production and distribution groups, producing programmes such as Coronation Street, Love Island, I'm a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here! and The Voice formats, and selling content internationally. Media & Entertainment covers the broadcast channels and streaming, funded principally by advertising. Programmes for which ITV is famous include Emmerdale, Britain's Got Talent, and dramas such as Mr Bates vs The Post Office (2024), which had major real-world impact in reopening the Post Office scandal debate.
