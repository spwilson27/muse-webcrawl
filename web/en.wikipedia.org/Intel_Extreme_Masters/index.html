Intel Extreme Masters

Intel Extreme Masters (IEM) is a series of international esports tournaments held in countries around the world. Organized by ESL and sponsored by Intel, the series began in 2006 and is among the longest-running global esports circuits.

History

The first Intel Extreme Masters was held in 2006 with Counter-Strike and Warcraft III. Its flagship event became IEM Katowice in Poland, which grew into one of the largest esports events in the world, hosting Counter-Strike majors and StarCraft II world championships. Games featured over the years include Counter-Strike: Global Offensive and Counter-Strike 2, StarCraft II, League of Legends, and others.

Format

Seasons consist of qualifying events in multiple regions culminating in a world championship. In 2024, ESL announced that IEM Katowice and IEM Cologne would be elevated as marquee events; Katowice had earlier been designated a CS:GO Major in 2014 and 2019.
