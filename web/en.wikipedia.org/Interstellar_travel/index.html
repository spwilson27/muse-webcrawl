Interstellar travel

Interstellar travel is the hypothetical travel of spacecraft between star systems. Because the nearest star, Proxima Centauri, is 4.24 light-years away, interstellar flight demands either enormous speeds sustained for years, journeys lasting generations, or propulsion far beyond present technology.

Concepts

Proposed methods include nuclear pulse propulsion (Project Orion, Project Daedalus), fusion rockets, antimatter rockets, laser-driven light sails (Breakthrough Starshot aims to send gram-scale probes at a fraction of light speed), generation ships, and sleeper ships. Voyager 1, launched in 1977, is the most distant human-made object but would take tens of thousands of years to reach the distance of the nearest stars. Interstellar travel is a staple of science fiction.
