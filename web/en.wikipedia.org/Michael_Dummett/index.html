Michael Dummett

Born27 June 1925, London
Died27 December 2011, Oxford
Known forPhilosophy of language; anti-realism; works on Frege

Sir Michael Anthony Eardley Dummett (1925–2011) was an English academic philosopher who was Wykeham Professor of Logic at the University of Oxford from 1979 until retiring in 1992. He is known for his work on Gottlob Frege and for developing the philosophical position of anti-realism.

Work

Dummett's Frege: Philosophy of Language (1973) helped revive interest in Frege in the English-speaking world. Drawing on intuitionistic logic and verificationism, Dummett argued that a theory of meaning must rest on speakers' ability to manifest understanding, challenging truth-conditional realism in works such as The Logical Basis of Metaphysics (1991).

Other interests

Dummett wrote extensively on voting theory and proportional representation, campaigned against racism and for immigrant rights, and was a leading historian of tarot and card games.
