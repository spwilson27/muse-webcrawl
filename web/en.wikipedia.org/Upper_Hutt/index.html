Upper Hutt (Māori: Te Awa Kairangi ki Uta) is a city in the Wellington Region of New Zealand, located some 25 km northeast of Wellington city along the Hutt River valley. It is the smallest of the four cities of the Wellington metropolitan area by population — about 47,000 people — but the largest by land area, stretching northeast into the Tararua Range.

History

The valley was inhabited by Māori for centuries before European settlement began in the 1840s under the New Zealand Company. Trentham, now a suburb of Upper Hutt, grew around Trentham Military Camp, established in 1914 and a major training base in both world wars. Upper Hutt was declared a borough in 1926 and a city in 1966, one of the fastest-growing municipalities in New Zealand in the post-war decades.

Geography and recreation

The city is surrounded by forested hills and parks, including Kaitoke Regional Park — whose native bush stood in for Rivendell in The Lord of the Rings films — and the Akatarawa Forest. The Hutt River provides swimming, kayaking, and fishing; Harcourt Park and Maidstone Park serve as the city's main recreational grounds. Upper Hutt is also known for Brewtown, a craft-beer precinct developed in the former Dunlop tyre factory.

Transport

State Highway 2 and the Wairarapa Line railway connect Upper Hutt with Wellington; the city is the terminus of the Hutt Valley Line suburban rail services.
