Vélib'
Location: Paris, France
Launched: 15 July 2007
Type: Bicycle-sharing system
Operator: Smovengo (since 2018)

Vélib' (from vélo, bicycle, and liberté, freedom) is the public bicycle-sharing system of Paris, France. Launched in 2007 with 10,000 bicycles, it grew into one of the largest systems of its kind in the world and became a model for bike-share schemes internationally.

Operation

Users rent bicycles from automated docking stations spread across Paris and its suburbs, paying by subscription or per trip; the fleet now includes a large share of electric-assist bikes. The system was originally operated by JCDecaux in exchange for advertising rights; since 2018 it has been run by Smovengo as Vélib' Métropole.
