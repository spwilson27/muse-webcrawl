Johan Gadolin

Johan Gadolin
Born5 June 1760, Turku, Finland
Died15 August 1852, Mynämäki, Finland
FieldChemistry, mineralogy

Johan Gadolin (1760–1852) was a Finnish chemist, physicist and mineralogist, professor at the Royal Academy of Turku. He is best known for his analysis of a black mineral from Ytterby, Sweden.

Discovery of yttria

In 1794 Gadolin published his examination of the mineral later called gadolinite and identified within it a previously unknown "earth", yttria — the first rare-earth compound recognised, from which the element yttrium and many other rare earths were later isolated.

Legacy

The element gadolinium (atomic number 64) and the mineral gadolinite are named in his honour. Gadolin is often called the father of Finnish chemistry research.
