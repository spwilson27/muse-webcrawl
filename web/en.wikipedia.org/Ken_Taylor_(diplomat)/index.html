Kenneth Douglas Taylor (5 October 1934 – 15 October 2015) was a Canadian diplomat who served as Canada's ambassador to Iran from 1977 to 1980. He became famous for sheltering six American diplomats in his Tehran residence during the Iran hostage crisis and helping them escape in January 1980 — the operation known as the "Canadian Caper".

The Canadian Caper

After militants seized the US embassy in November 1979, Taylor hid six Americans who had escaped, obtained Canadian passports and false documents for them, and helped them leave Iran posing as a film crew scouting locations for a fictional science-fiction movie, Argo. The episode was dramatised in the 2012 film Argo, in which Taylor's role was somewhat reduced — a point he publicly noted.

Honours

Taylor was made a Companion of the Order of Canada and received the US Congressional Gold Medal in recognition of his actions.
