Michael Woodford (businessman)

Born1960, Liverpool, England
Known forCEO of Olympus Corporation; whistleblower in the Olympus scandal

Michael Christopher Woodford (born 1960) is a British businessman who became president and chief executive officer of the Japanese optical and electronics company Olympus Corporation in 2011, the first non-Japanese person to lead the firm.

Olympus scandal

Shortly after becoming CEO, Woodford questioned large advisory fees connected with company acquisitions and uncovered a long-running scheme to conceal investment losses of over a billion dollars using tobashi accounting arrangements. After he pressed the board for answers, he was dismissed in October 2011; he then alerted regulators and the press, becoming one of the most prominent corporate whistleblowers of the era. Olympus subsequently admitted the fraud, its share price collapsed, and several executives were prosecuted.

Aftermath

Woodford described the affair in his memoir Exposure (2012) and has since worked as a speaker and consultant on corporate governance and safety culture, including in healthcare.
