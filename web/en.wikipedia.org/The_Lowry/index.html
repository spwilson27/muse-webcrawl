The Lowry

LocationSalford Quays, Greater Manchester, England
OpenedApril 28, 2000
ArchitectMichael Wilford
Named afterL. S. Lowry

The Lowry is a theatre and gallery complex at Salford Quays in Salford, Greater Manchester, England. It is named after the artist L. S. Lowry (1887–1976), famous for paintings of industrial scenes in North West England, and houses the world's largest public collection of his work.

Facilities

The building contains two theatres — the Lyric Theatre, with about 1,730 seats one of the largest proscenium stages outside London's West End, and the smaller Quays Theatre — as well as galleries, rehearsal studios, restaurants, and bars.

History

Designed by architect Michael Wilford, the complex opened in 2000 as part of the regeneration of Salford Quays. It stands near the Imperial War Museum North and MediaCityUK, and is served by the Metrolink tram network.
