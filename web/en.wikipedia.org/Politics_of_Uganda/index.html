Politics of Uganda

The politics of Uganda take place in the framework of a presidential republic, whereby the President of Uganda is both head of state and head of government. Yoweri Museveni and the National Resistance Movement have governed since 1986.

The Parliament of Uganda is the unicameral legislature. A multi-party system was restored by referendum in 2005 after two decades of the no-party Movement system. Elections have been contested amid concerns about opposition rights, term and age limits removed by constitutional amendments.
