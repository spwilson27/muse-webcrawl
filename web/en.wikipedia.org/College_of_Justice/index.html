College of Justice

The College of Justice is the supreme civil judiciary of Scotland, founded in 1532 by King James V. It consists of the Court of Session, the High Court of Justiciary and subordinate bodies.

Composition

Its members are the Senators of the College of Justice — Scotland's supreme judges — headed by the Lord President of the Court of Session and the Lord Justice Clerk. The college also historically included the Faculty of Advocates, solicitors and court staff.

History

Established on the model of continental courts, the college was confirmed by the Treaty of Union in 1707, which preserved Scots law and its courts. It sits at Parliament House in Edinburgh.
