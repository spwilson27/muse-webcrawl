The 60 metres hurdles is a hurdles event in indoor track and field, contested at the World Athletics Indoor Championships. The race is run over five hurdles; the men's hurdles are 1.067 metres (42 inches) high, the same as the outdoor 110 metres hurdles, while the women's hurdles are 0.838 metres (33 inches) high, as in the outdoor 100 metres hurdles.

Race format

The start to the first hurdle is 13.72 metres for men and 13 metres for women, and the spacing between hurdles is 9.14 metres for men and 8.5 metres for women. Because indoor tracks are shorter, the event substitutes for the full outdoor sprint hurdles races during the winter season.

Records and notable athletes

Colin Jackson's world record of 7.30 seconds, set in 1994, stood until Grant Holloway ran 7.29 in 2021 and then 7.27 in 2024. On the women's side, Susanna Kallur's 7.68 from 2008 stood until Devynne Charlton of the Bahamas ran 7.67 and then 7.65 at the 2024 World Indoor Championships in Glasgow.
