Desiderius Erasmus

Desiderius Erasmus Roterodamus (28 October 1466 – 12 July 1536) was a Dutch Christian humanist, Catholic theologian and philosopher, regarded as one of the greatest scholars of the Northern Renaissance.

Works

Erasmus wrote In Praise of Folly (1509), a satire on theological and social abuses; published a landmark Greek New Testament with a new Latin translation (1516); and produced widely used handbooks on rhetoric and education. His Adagia collected classical proverbs.

Reformation

Though he criticised corruption in the Church and influenced reformers, Erasmus broke with Martin Luther over free will (De Libero Arbitrio, 1524) and remained Catholic. He died in Basel.
