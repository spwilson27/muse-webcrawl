Dancer in the Dark is a 2000 musical drama film directed by Lars von Trier and starring Icelandic singer Björk as Selma Ježková, a Czech immigrant in 1960s America who is slowly going blind and saving money for an operation to prevent her son suffering the same hereditary condition.

Plot and style

Selma escapes her hardships through elaborate musical daydreams filmed with multiple digital cameras, contrasting with the Dogme-influenced handheld realism of the dramatic scenes. Accused of a crime she did not commit, Selma's story ends tragically.

Reception

The film won the Palme d'Or at the 2000 Cannes Film Festival, and Björk won Best Actress there; her song "I've Seen It All" was nominated for an Academy Award. Production was famously fraught, with von Trier and Björk clashing throughout.
