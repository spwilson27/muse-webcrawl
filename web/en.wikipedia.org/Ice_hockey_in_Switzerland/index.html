Ice hockey in Switzerland

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Ice hockey in Switzerland is one of the country's most popular sports, rivalling football in attendance and media coverage. The sport is governed by the Swiss Ice Hockey Federation (SIHF), founded in 1908. Switzerland was a founding member of the International Ice Hockey Federation the same year.

League system

The top professional league is the National League (NL), which regularly records some of the highest average attendances in European hockey, often exceeding 6,000 spectators per game. Below it sit the Swiss League (second tier) and the amateur MyHockey League and regional leagues. Prominent clubs include SC Bern, ZSC Lions, HC Davos, HC Lugano, EV Zug, and Genève-Servette HC. HC Davos hosts the annual Spengler Cup, the oldest invitational ice hockey tournament in the world, held in Davos since 1923.

National teams

The Swiss men's national team is consistently ranked among the top eight in the world. Switzerland won silver medals at the IIHF World Championship in 2013 and 2018, ending long medal droughts, and the legendary Swiss teams of the 1920s and 1930s also earned multiple European titles and World Championship medals. The women's national team won bronze at the 2014 Winter Olympics in Sochi.

Swiss players abroad

Swiss players have become increasingly prominent in the National Hockey League. Roman Josi of the Nashville Predators won the Norris Trophy in 2020, and Nico Hischier was selected first overall by the New Jersey Devils in the 2017 NHL Entry Draft, the highest draft position ever achieved by a Swiss player. Goaltender David Aebischer became the first Swiss player to win the Stanley Cup, with Colorado in 2001.
