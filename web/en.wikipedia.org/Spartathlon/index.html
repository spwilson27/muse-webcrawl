Spartathlon

LocationAthens to Sparta, Greece
Distance246 km (153 mi)
Established1983
Time limit36 hours

The Spartathlon is an ultramarathon race held annually in Greece since 1983, covering the 246 kilometres between Athens and Sparta. It revives the legendary run of the Athenian messenger Pheidippides, who, according to Herodotus, was sent to Sparta to request help before the Battle of Marathon and arrived the following day.

Course

Runners follow a route over paved roads, mountain trails, and the pass of Mount Parthenion, passing through Corinth and Nemea. Strict cut-off times at checkpoints eliminate a large share of entrants; finishers are crowned with an olive wreath and given water from the river Eurotas in Sparta.

Records

The course record was set by the Greek runner Yiannis Kouros in 1984 with a time of 20 hours 25 minutes, a performance long considered one of the greatest in ultrarunning.
