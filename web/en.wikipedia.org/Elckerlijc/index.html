Elckerlijc

Elckerlijc (also spelled Elckerlyc) is a Dutch morality play written around 1495. Its full title is Den Spyeghel der Salicheyt van Elckerlijc — "The Mirror of Salvation of Everyman".

The play is generally considered the source for the English morality play Everyman. It won first prize at a rhetorician contest in Antwerp.

Plot

God, dismayed by humanity's corruption, sends Death to summon Everyman (Elckerlijc) to give an account of his life. Everyman seeks companions for his journey and is successively abandoned by Fellowship, Kindred, and Goods. Only Good Deeds, weakened by neglect, agrees to accompany him after Everyman makes confession and does penance. Knowledge, Confession, Beauty, Strength, Discretion and the Five Senses also leave him at the grave, and Everyman descends accompanied only by Good Deeds.

Authorship

The author is anonymous; the play has sometimes been attributed to Peter van Diest, a rhetorician from the Low Countries.
