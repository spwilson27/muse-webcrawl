University of Dublin

Type: Public research university
Established: 1592
Location: Dublin, Ireland
College: Trinity College Dublin

The University of Dublin (Irish: Ollscoil Átha Cliath) is a university in Dublin, Ireland. It was founded in 1592 by Queen Elizabeth I to be the "mother of a university" on the model of Oxford and Cambridge, but unlike those it developed only one college, Trinity College Dublin, which is therefore effectively synonymous with the university.

Structure

Legally the university and the college are distinct: the university awards degrees, while Trinity College provides teaching, accommodation and research facilities. The university's senate elects the Chancellor, and graduates of the university historically elected members of Seanad Éireann from the Dublin University constituency until the franchise was reformed.

Degrees and traditions

The university confers degrees in Latin at commencements held in the Public Theatre. Alumni of Trinity include Oscar Wilde, Samuel Beckett, Bram Stoker and four presidents of Ireland. The Book of Kells is held in Trinity's Old Library.
