San Mamés is an association football stadium in Bilbao, Spain, and the home of Athletic Bilbao. Inaugurated on 16 September 2013, it replaced the original San Mamés stadium (1913–2013), which stood on the adjacent site and was likewise nicknamed "La Catedral". The new stadium has a capacity of 53,289.

History

Built between 2010 and 2014 — Athletic played the 2013–14 season with one end unfinished while the old ground was demolished — the stadium was designed by architect César de Azcárate. Its exterior features a translucent facade of twisted ETFE panels that can be illuminated in the club's red and white colours.

Events

San Mamés hosted the 2018 European Rugby Champions Cup and Challenge Cup finals, the UEFA Women's Champions League final in 2024, and the 2025 UEFA Europa League final. It is one of the most highly rated stadiums in Spain for atmosphere.
