Keflavík International Airport (Icelandic: Keflavíkurflugvöllur), also known as Reykjavík–Keflavík Airport, is Iceland's largest airport and the country's main hub for international transportation. Located about 50 km southwest of Reykjavík near the town of Keflavík, it serves as the base of Icelandair and a major stopover point between Europe and North America.

History

The airfield was built by the United States military during the Second World War and later shared with the adjacent Naval Air Station Keflavik, which operated until 2006. Its passenger terminal, named after the Norse explorer Leif Erikson, opened in 1987. Traffic grew rapidly in the 2010s with Iceland's tourism boom.
