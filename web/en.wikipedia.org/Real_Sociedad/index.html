Real Sociedad de Fútbol, usually known as Real Sociedad, is a Spanish professional football club based in San Sebastián (Donostia), in the Basque Country. Founded in 1909, the club was a founding member of La Liga in 1929 and plays its home games at the Reale Arena (formerly Anoeta Stadium).

History

La Real's golden era came in the early 1980s, when a side built almost entirely from home-grown Basque players won two consecutive La Liga titles in 1980–81 and 1981–82 under coach Alberto Ormaetxea. The club has won the Copa del Rey three times, most recently the delayed 2020 final — an all-Basque derby against Athletic Bilbao played in 2021. For much of its history the club signed only Basque players; the signing of Irish striker John Aldridge in 1989 ended that unwritten policy for non-Basques, though youth development at the Zubieta academy remains central to its identity. The club's fierce rivalry with Athletic Bilbao is the Basque derby.
