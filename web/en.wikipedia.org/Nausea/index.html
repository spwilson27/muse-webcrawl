Nausea

Nausea is a diffuse sensation of unease and discomfort, sometimes perceived as an urge to vomit. It is a non-specific symptom, meaning it has many possible causes, including motion sickness, early pregnancy, gastroenteritis, migraine, medication side effects, anxiety and many other conditions.

Causes

Nausea can arise from disturbances of the gastrointestinal tract, the vestibular system of the inner ear, the central nervous system, or from hormonal and metabolic changes. Common triggers include motion, certain smells, overeating, infections and chemotherapy.

Physiology

The sensation is coordinated in part by the vomiting centre and the chemoreceptor trigger zone in the medulla oblongata of the brainstem, which respond to signals from the gut, the vestibular apparatus and the bloodstream.

Treatment

Treatment addresses the underlying cause where possible. Antiemetic medicines, hydration, rest and dietary measures such as small bland meals may relieve symptoms. Persistent nausea requires medical evaluation.
