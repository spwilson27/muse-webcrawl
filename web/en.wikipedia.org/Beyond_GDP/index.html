Beyond GDP

Beyond GDP is the name of an initiative and broader movement to develop indicators of economic, environmental and social progress that go beyond gross domestic product (GDP) alone, on the grounds that GDP measures market production but not welfare, distribution, sustainability or quality of life.

Background

Critics have long noted that GDP counts defensive and remedial spending, ignores unpaid work and environmental damage, and says nothing about inequality. In 2008 France established the Stiglitz–Sen–Fitoussi Commission, whose 2009 report recommended shifting emphasis from production to well-being and sustainability.

Alternative indicators

Proposed supplements and alternatives include the Human Development Index, the Genuine Progress Indicator, the OECD Better Life Index, Bhutan's Gross National Happiness, and dashboards of Sustainable Development Goal indicators. The European Commission's Beyond GDP initiative and the UN's "valuing what counts" work continue this agenda.
