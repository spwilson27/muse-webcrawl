The Ringbahn (German for "ring railway") is a 37.5 km (23.3 mi) long circular railway line around the centre of Berlin, Germany. It carries Berlin S-Bahn trains on dedicated tracks (services S41 clockwise and S42 counterclockwise, among others) as well as regional, long-distance and freight trains on separate tracks.

History

The ring was completed in 1877 to connect Berlin's radial main lines and serve the growing suburbs. Damaged in the Second World War and severed by the division of Berlin, the West Berlin portion saw declining use and the S-Bahn ring was fully closed in the Cold War era; the complete circle was restored in stages after reunification, with through ring services resuming in 2002. Major interchange stations include Ostkreuz, Südkreuz, Gesundbrunnen and Westkreuz.
