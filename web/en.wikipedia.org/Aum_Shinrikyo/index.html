Aum Shinrikyo

Founded: 1987 (as Aum Shinsen no Kai, 1984)
Founder: Shoko Asahara
Status: Designated terrorist organisation by several states

Aum Shinrikyo is a Japanese doomsday cult founded by Shoko Asahara. Drawing on Buddhism, Hinduism, Christianity and apocalyptic prophecy, the group carried out the Matsumoto sarin attack in 1994 and the Tokyo subway sarin attack on 20 March 1995, which killed 13 people and injured thousands.

Investigations and trials

Police raids uncovered chemical and biological weapons programmes and evidence of murders of defectors and opponents. Asahara and twelve other members were sentenced to death and executed in July 2018.

Successor groups

Aum renamed itself Aleph in 2000 and remains under official surveillance in Japan; a splinter group, Hikari no Wa, separated in 2007.
