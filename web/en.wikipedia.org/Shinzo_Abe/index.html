Shinzo Abe

Born: 21 September 1954, Tokyo
Died: 8 July 2022, Kashihara, Nara
Office: Prime Minister of Japan (2006–2007, 2012–2020)
Party: Liberal Democratic Party

Shinzo Abe (安倍 晋三; 1954–2022) was a Japanese politician who served as prime minister of Japan and president of the Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) from 2006 to 2007 and again from 2012 to 2020. He was the longest-serving prime minister in Japanese history.

Early life

Abe was born into a prominent political family: his grandfather Nobusuke Kishi and great-uncle Eisaku Satō had both been prime ministers, and his father Shintaro Abe was foreign minister. He studied political science at Seikei University and public policy briefly at the University of Southern California before working at Kobe Steel and in politics.

Premierships

Abe first became prime minister in 2006 as the youngest post-war holder of the office, but resigned after a year amid scandal and ill health. Returning in 2012, he launched "Abenomics", a programme of monetary easing, fiscal stimulus and structural reform, pursued a more assertive foreign policy, and oversaw reinterpretation of the constitution to allow collective self-defence. He resigned in 2020 because of ulcerative colitis.

Assassination

On 8 July 2022, while campaigning in Nara, Abe was shot and killed by a gunman using a homemade firearm — an event that shocked Japan and the world. He was given a state funeral in September 2022.
