The Korps Commandotroepen (KCT) is the special forces unit of the Royal Netherlands Army. It is one of the principal special operations units in the Dutch military and is deployed worldwide. The corps traces its roots to the Second World War, when Dutch volunteers trained with the British No. 2 (Dutch) Troop of the No. 10 (Inter-Allied) Commando in 1942.

Role and tasks

The KCT conducts the full spectrum of special operations, including special reconnaissance, direct action and military assistance, as well as counter-terrorism tasks in support of the Dutch police in cooperation with the Dienst Speciale Interventies. Units of the corps have served in Bosnia, Iraq, Afghanistan (as Task Force Viper and with NLD Special Operations Task Groups), Mali and other theatres.

Selection and training

Candidates undergo a demanding selection and the elementary commando course (Elementaire Commando Opleiding), culminating in the final exercise known as the "hell week" and the award of the green beret. Operators then specialise in roles such as mountain leader, combat diver, free-fall parachutist, sniper, medic or demolitions expert.
