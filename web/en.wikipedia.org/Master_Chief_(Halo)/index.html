Master Chief (Halo)

First appearanceHalo: Combat Evolved (2001)
Created byBungie
Voiced bySteve Downes

Master Chief is the protagonist of the Halo video game franchise created by Bungie and now developed by 343 Industries. A supersoldier known as a Spartan-II, his full designation is John-117. He is almost always depicted wearing green MJOLNIR powered armour and rarely removes his helmet.

Character

Abducted as a child and conscripted into the Spartan-II programme, John was augmented and trained for war, becoming humanity's foremost defender against the Covenant and later threats. His AI companion Cortana accompanies him through much of the series.

Reception

Master Chief became the mascot of the Xbox brand and one of the most recognisable characters in gaming, appearing in games, novels, and the Halo television series.
