Rally obedience

Rally obedience (also known as Rally-O) is a dog sport based on obedience training. Unlike traditional obedience, handlers proceed around a course of designated stations with their dog in heel position, performing exercises indicated by signs at each station. Handlers are encouraged to talk to and praise their dogs throughout the course.

Format

Courses typically contain 10 to 20 stations depending on the level, each sign describing an exercise such as turns, sits, downs, stays, spirals, and figure-eights. A judge designs the course and observes each team; scoring starts at 200 points (perfect) or 100 points depending on the organisation, with deductions for errors.

Organisations

Rally is offered by the American Kennel Club (AKC), the United Kennel Club, the Canadian Kennel Club, and other bodies, with levels from Novice through Advanced and Excellent to Master and the Rally Championship (RACH) in the AKC.
