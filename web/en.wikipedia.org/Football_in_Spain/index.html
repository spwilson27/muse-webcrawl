Football in Spain

Governing bodyRoyal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF)
Top leagueLa Liga
Main cupCopa del Rey
National teamSpain national football team

Football is the most popular sport in Spain, organised by the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF), founded in 1909. Spain's top clubs, Real Madrid and FC Barcelona, are among the most successful and widely followed in world football, and their rivalry, El Clásico, is one of the biggest fixtures in sport.

League system

The professional pyramid is headed by La Liga (Primera División, 20 clubs), followed by the Segunda División and the semi-professional Primera Federación. The main knockout competition is the Copa del Rey, first played in 1903, and the Supercopa de España. Spanish clubs have won more UEFA Champions League titles than those of any other country, with Real Madrid the most successful club in the competition's history.

National team

The Spain national team won the FIFA World Cup in 2010 in South Africa, and the UEFA European Championship in 1964, 2008, 2012, and 2024 — the only nation to win three consecutive major tournaments (2008–2012), a period associated with the possession-based "tiki-taka" style. Spain also won the UEFA Nations League in 2023 and Olympic gold in 1992 and 2024.

Women's football

The women's game has grown rapidly, led by Liga F and Barcelona Femení's European successes. Spain won the FIFA Women's World Cup in 2023, defeating England in the Sydney final, completing a set of world titles at senior, under-20, and under-17 levels.
