The Dark Tower (series)

The Dark Tower is a series of eight novels and a novella by American author Stephen King, blending dark fantasy, western, horror and science fiction. King considers it his magnum opus; it connects, directly or indirectly, most of his other fiction.

Story

The series follows Roland Deschain, the last of the gunslingers of Mid-World, on his quest to reach the Dark Tower, the nexus of all universes, which is slowly falling into ruin. Drawing companions (his ka-tet) from 20th-century New York — Eddie Dean, Susannah Dean and Jake Chambers, along with the billy-bumbler Oy — Roland battles the forces of the Crimson King, who seeks to topple the Tower and rule the resulting chaos.

The books

The volumes are The Gunslinger (1982, revised 2003), The Drawing of the Three (1987), The Waste Lands (1991), Wizard and Glass (1997), Wolves of the Calla (2003), Song of Susannah (2004), The Dark Tower (2004), the later interquel The Wind Through the Keyhole (2012), and the novella The Little Sisters of Eluria. King was spurred to finish the sequence after his near-fatal 1999 road accident.

Influences and adaptations

Inspirations include Robert Browning's poem "Childe Roland to the Dark Tower Came", Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings, the Arthurian legend and Sergio Leone's spaghetti westerns. Marvel Comics published extensive comic-book expansions from 2007. A 2017 film starring Idris Elba and Matthew McConaughey was a critical and commercial failure.
