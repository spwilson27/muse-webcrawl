Sheffield is a city in South Yorkshire, England, situated in the foothills of the Pennines at the confluence of five rivers — the Don, Sheaf (from which the city takes its name, "the field by the Sheaf"), Rivelin, Loxley, and Porter Brook. It is one of England's largest cities and grew from a market town famous since the Middle Ages for cutlery — Geoffrey Chaucer mentions a Sheffield knife in The Canterbury Tales — into the world's pre-eminent steel city during the Industrial Revolution.

Steel and industry

Sheffield's fast-flowing rivers powered grinding wheels, while local coal, ironstone, and gritstone for grindstones completed the recipe for metalworking. Two landmark inventions were made here: Thomas Boulsover's fused plate (Sheffield plate) in the 1740s and Benjamin Huntsman's crucible steel process (c. 1740), which for the first time allowed truly homogeneous high-quality cast steel. In 1913 Harry Brearley of the Brown-Firth research laboratories discovered stainless steel in Sheffield while seeking erosion-resistant gun-barrel alloys. The city's name became a global mark of quality for cutlery and tools, administered by the Company of Cutlers in Hallamshire, founded in 1624.

Decline and reinvention

The steel industry collapsed in the 1970s–80s as international competition and recession closed mills along the Don valley, an era dramatised in the 1997 film The Full Monty. Sheffield has since reinvented itself around higher education (the University of Sheffield and Sheffield Hallam University, together bringing tens of thousands of students), advanced manufacturing at the Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre, sport (it hosted the World Snooker Championship at the Crucible Theatre since 1977, and claims the world's oldest football club, Sheffield F.C., founded 1857, and the oldest ground, Sandygate), and music — home to Pulp, Arctic Monkeys, and Def Leppard. A third of the city lies within the Peak District National Park, and Sheffield styles itself "the Outdoor City," with more trees per person than any other city in Europe by some counts.
