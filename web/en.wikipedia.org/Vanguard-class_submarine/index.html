Vanguard-class submarine

The Vanguard-class is a class of nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarines (SSBNs) in service with the United Kingdom's Royal Navy. The class was introduced in 1994 as part of the Trident nuclear programme, and comprises four vessels: Vanguard, Victorious, Vigilant and Vengeance. They were built at Barrow-in-Furness by Vickers Shipbuilding and Engineering.

Design

The Vanguard class are the largest submarines ever built for the Royal Navy, displacing about 15,900 tonnes submerged and measuring 149.9 metres in length. Each boat carries up to 16 Trident II D5 submarine-launched ballistic missiles, forming the United Kingdom's entire nuclear deterrent. Propulsion is provided by a Rolls-Royce PWR2 pressurised water reactor driving two turbines.

Service

The class operates under the Continuous At-Sea Deterrence (CASD) posture, with at least one boat on patrol at all times since April 1969's Resolution-class patrols were succeeded in the 1990s. The boats are based at HM Naval Base Clyde (Faslane) in Scotland. The class is planned to be replaced by the Dreadnought-class submarines from the early 2030s.
