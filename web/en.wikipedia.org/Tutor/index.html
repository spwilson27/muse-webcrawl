Tutor

A tutor is a person who provides individualized instruction or guidance to one or more learners, usually outside the framework of a formal classroom. Tutoring may supplement school or university study, prepare students for examinations, or teach a specific skill or subject.

History

Private tutors educated children of wealthy families for millennia; in ancient Greece and Rome, tutors and pedagogues were household fixtures, and at the universities of Oxford and Cambridge the tutorial system — small-group teaching by a college tutor — remains a central feature.

Forms

Modern tutoring includes peer tutoring, online tutoring, test preparation, and shadow education systems such as Japan's juku and South Korea's hagwon. Research, notably Benjamin Bloom's "2 sigma problem", suggests one-to-one tutoring can produce substantially better outcomes than conventional group instruction.
