Barometer

A barometer is a scientific instrument used in meteorology to measure atmospheric pressure, also called barometric pressure. Changes in pressure indicate short-term changes in the weather: rising pressure generally signals improving weather, while falling pressure suggests deterioration.

Types

The mercury barometer, invented by Evangelista Torricelli in 1643, balances the weight of a column of mercury against the atmosphere. The aneroid barometer, invented by Lucien Vidi in 1844, uses a small flexible metal box that expands and contracts with pressure changes; aneroid mechanisms are used in altimeters, barographs, and household weather stations. Modern electronic barometers and MEMS pressure sensors are built into smartphones and weather instruments.
