Cotton Bowl (stadium)

The Cotton Bowl is an outdoor stadium in Fair Park in Dallas, Texas, United States. Opened in 1930 as Fair Park Stadium, it has a capacity of about 92,100.

History

The stadium was home to the Cotton Bowl Classic college football bowl game from 1937 to 2009, after which the game moved to Arlington. It hosted the Dallas Cowboys from 1960 to 1971, SMU Mustangs, and matches of the 1994 FIFA World Cup. The annual Red River Rivalry between Oklahoma and Texas is played there during the State Fair of Texas, as is the State Fair Classic.

Events

It has hosted concerts, soccer, and other American football games, and remains one of the largest stadiums in the United States.
