Professional wrestling in the United Kingdom

Professional wrestling in the United Kingdom has a history spanning more than a century, developing a distinctive "World of Sport" style distinct from the American product, characterised by a rounds-based format, technical catch-as-catch-can wrestling, and comic character bouts.

World of Sport era

From 1955 to 1988, ITV broadcast wrestling as part of World of Sport, drawing audiences of millions. Household names included Big Daddy (Shirley Crabtree), Giant Haystacks, Kendo Nagasaki, Mick McManus, and Jackie Pallo. Matches were governed on screen by the "Mountevans rules", named after Admiral Lord Mountevans, who had codified British wrestling rules in 1947. When ITV cancelled wrestling in 1988, the domestic scene declined sharply as American promotions such as the WWF filled the gap.

Modern revival

From the 2010s a "British wrestling renaissance" ("BritWres") saw promotions such as PROGRESS Wrestling, Revolution Pro Wrestling (RevPro), and ICW in Scotland build substantial followings. WWE launched the NXT UK brand in 2018, and AEW and other promotions recruited prominent British talent.
