Yarra Valley

LocationVictoria, Australia
RiverYarra River
Known forWine, Healesville Sanctuary

The Yarra Valley is a region of Victoria, Australia, surrounding the Yarra River east of Melbourne. It is one of Australia's premier cool-climate wine regions and a popular day-trip destination for its scenery, food, and wildlife attractions.

Wine industry

Viticulture began in the valley in the 1830s at Yering Station. After a decline in the early 20th century, winemaking revived from the 1960s; the valley is now noted for chardonnay, pinot noir, and sparkling wine, with well-known producers around Coldstream, Yarra Glen, and Healesville.

Attractions

The Healesville Sanctuary showcases native Australian animals. The valley is also the gateway to the Dandenong Ranges and to snowfields at Lake Mountain, and its towns serve Melbourne's water catchments in the upper valley.
