Occupation

This article is about the general concept. "Occupation" may refer to several distinct topics listed below.

Occupation commonly refers to:

  • Job or profession — an activity, often performed in exchange for payment, that occupies a person's time; "occupation" is the term used on official forms and censuses for a person's regular work or employment.
  • Military occupation — the effective provisional control of a territory by a hostile armed force, governed under international law by the Hague Conventions and the Fourth Geneva Convention.
  • Occupation (protest) — a form of political protest in which participants seize and hold a space, such as a building or public square.
  • Occupancy in law and real estate — the act of taking possession of premises, or the state of residing in them.

Employment sense

Statistical agencies classify occupations into standard systems, such as the International Standard Classification of Occupations (ISCO) and the U.S. Standard Occupational Classification (SOC), grouping jobs by skill level and specialisation for use in censuses and labour-market statistics.

Military sense

Belligerent occupation occurs when territory comes under the effective control of a hostile army. The occupying power does not acquire sovereignty over the territory and is obliged to administer it for the benefit of the local population while respecting existing laws as far as possible.
