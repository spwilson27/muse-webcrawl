FC Barcelona Bàsquet

FC Barcelona Bàsquet, commonly referred to as FC Barcelona, is a Spanish professional basketball club based in Barcelona, Catalonia. It is part of the FC Barcelona multi-sports club, and was one of the founding members of the top-tier level Spanish Liga ACB.

Founded1926
ArenaPalau Blaugrana

History

Founded in 1926, the basketball section is one of the most successful in Spain and Europe. The club won the EuroLeague twice, in 2003 and 2010, and has won numerous Spanish league and cup titles.

Players

Notable former players include Juan Carlos Navarro, Pau Gasol, and Šarūnas Jasikevičius, the latter also having served as head coach.
