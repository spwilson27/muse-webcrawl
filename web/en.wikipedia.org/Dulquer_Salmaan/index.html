Dulquer Salmaan

Dulquer Salmaan (born 28 July 1983) is an Indian actor, playback singer and film producer who predominantly works in Malayalam cinema, in addition to Tamil, Telugu and Hindi films. The son of actor Mammootty, he is one of the most popular leading actors in contemporary Malayalam cinema and the recipient of several awards, including Kerala State Film Awards and Filmfare Awards South.

Early life and career

Salmaan studied business management at Purdue University in the United States and worked in business before training in acting. He made his debut in Second Show (2012) and broke through with Ustad Hotel (2012), followed by hits such as Bangalore Days (2014), Charlie (2015) — for which he won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor — Kammatti Paadam (2016) and Kurup (2021), which he also produced under his banner Wayfarer Films.

Other industries

He made his Tamil debut with Vaayai Moodi Pesavum (2014), his Telugu debut playing Gemini Ganesan in the biopic Mahanati (2018), and his Hindi debut in Karwaan (2018). His Telugu romance Sita Ramam (2022) was a major commercial and critical success across India.
