Mummers play

Mummers plays are folk plays performed by troupes of amateur actors, traditionally all male, known as mummers or guisers. They were formerly performed throughout the British Isles and in some other English-speaking regions, usually in streets, public houses or private homes around Christmas, New Year or Easter.

Plot

The best-known type, the hero-combat play, features a protagonist such as Saint George who fights and kills an opponent — often a Turkish Knight or dragon — after which a comic Doctor revives the slain character with a magical cure. Other characters may include Father Christmas, Beelzebub, and a fool. Performers often wear elaborate costumes made of strips of cloth or paper.

History

Texts survive from the 18th century onward, though the customs are likely older. Mummers plays declined during the 20th century but have been revived by folk societies and morris dance sides. Related traditions include the Philadelphia Mummers Parade in the United States.
