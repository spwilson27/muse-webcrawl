Gornergrat Railway

Locale: Valais, Switzerland

Termini: Zermatt – Gornergrat

Opened: 1898

Line length: 9.34 km

Track gauge: 1,000 mm (metre gauge)

System: Abt rack railway

The Gornergrat Railway (German: Gornergratbahn, GGB) is a mountain rack railway in the Swiss canton of Valais. It links the resort village of Zermatt, situated at 1,604 m above sea level, to the summit of the Gornergrat, at 3,089 m above sea level.

History

The line opened on 20 August 1898, and it was the first electric rack railway built in Switzerland. The summit station at Gornergrat is among the highest railway stations in Europe, and offers panoramic views of the Monte Rosa massif, the Gorner Glacier — the second-largest glacier in the Alps — and the Matterhorn.

Operation

The railway uses the Abt rack system to climb gradients of up to 200‰. The journey of 9.34 km takes about 33 minutes, passing intermediate stations including Riffelalp and Riffelberg. The line operates year-round and is a major tourist attraction, carrying hundreds of thousands of passengers annually.
