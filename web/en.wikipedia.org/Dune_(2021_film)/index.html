Dune (titled on-screen as Dune: Part One) is a 2021 American epic science fiction film directed by Denis Villeneuve, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jon Spaihts and Eric Roth. It is the first of a two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel Dune, covering roughly the first half of the book.

Plot

In the far future, Duke Leto Atreides accepts control of the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of the spice melange, which extends human life and enables interstellar travel. When the treacherous House Harkonnen, backed by the Emperor's Sardaukar troops, destroys the Atreides, Duke Leto's son Paul escapes into the desert with his mother Jessica and finds refuge with the native Fremen, beginning his transformation into the prophesied Muad'Dib.

Production and release

The film was produced by Legendary Pictures and filmed in Norway, Jordan, Abu Dhabi and Hungary. It premiered at the Venice International Film Festival on 3 September 2021 and was released in the United States on 22 October 2021, simultaneously in cinemas and on HBO Max.

Reception

The film was praised for its direction, visuals, score and ambition. It won six Academy Awards, including Best Cinematography, Best Production Design and Best Original Score. The sequel, Dune: Part Two, was released in 2024.
