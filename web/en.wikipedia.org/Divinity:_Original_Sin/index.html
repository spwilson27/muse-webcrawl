Divinity: Original Sin

Divinity: Original Sin is a role-playing video game developed and published by Larian Studios, released in 2014 after a successful Kickstarter campaign. It is a prequel to the earlier Divine Divinity and part of the Divinity series.

Gameplay

The game features isometric, turn-based combat with an emphasis on environmental interaction: surfaces such as water, oil and poison can be electrified, frozen or set ablaze, and spells combine in systemic ways. Two protagonists, Source Hunters, can be played cooperatively, and the pair may agree or argue in dialogue, shaping their traits. An extensive crafting system and a modding toolkit (the Divinity Engine) shipped with the game.

Reception and sequel

Original Sin was acclaimed for its combat depth, freedom and co-operative play, and is credited with reviving the classic computer RPG genre. An Enhanced Edition followed in 2015. Its sequel, Divinity: Original Sin II (2017), became one of the most highly regarded RPGs of its decade, and Larian went on to develop Baldur's Gate 3.
