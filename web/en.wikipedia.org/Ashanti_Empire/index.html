The Ashanti Empire (also spelled Asante) was an Akan state that existed from 1701 to 1901 in what is now Ghana. Founded by Osei Tutu I with the priest Okomfo Anokye, the empire was unified around the Golden Stool (Sika Dwa Kofi), the sacred symbol of the Ashanti nation said to have descended from the sky.

History

From its capital at Kumasi, the empire expanded through military conquest and diplomacy, growing rich on gold and the slave trade with Europeans on the coast. Its bureaucracy, roads and military organization made it one of the most powerful states in West Africa.

Anglo-Ashanti Wars

The Ashanti fought five wars with the British during the 19th century. In 1874 the British burned Kumasi; in 1896 they exiled Asantehene Prempeh I. The War of the Golden Stool in 1900, led by queen mother Yaa Asantewaa, was the final resistance before annexation to the Gold Coast colony in 1901. The Ashanti monarchy was restored in 1935 and remains an influential institution in modern Ghana.
