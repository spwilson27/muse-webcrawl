Axis: Bold as Love

Axis: Bold as Love is the second studio album by the Jimi Hendrix Experience, released in December 1967 in the United Kingdom on Track Records. Recorded at Olympic Studios in London with producer Chas Chandler and engineer Eddie Kramer, it was made quickly to meet a contractual deadline while the band developed the more ambitious sound of Electric Ladyland.

Music

The album mixes psychedelic rock with soul and studio experimentation, including phasing on "Little Wing" and the whimsical "If 6 Was 9". Notable tracks include "Spanish Castle Magic", "Wait Until Tomorrow", "Little Wing" — later a standard covered by many artists — and "Bold as Love". Hendrix was dissatisfied that the master tape of "If 6 Was 9" was nearly lost and that the finished mix of side one had to be redone in a single overnight session.

Cover and reception

Its gatefold cover, painted by Roger Law from a religious poster depicting Vishnu, became iconic, though Hendrix felt it did not represent his own heritage. The album reached number five in the UK and number three in the US; it is regularly ranked among the greatest albums of all time and was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame.
