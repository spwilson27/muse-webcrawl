Postcards from Buster is an American children's television series that aired on PBS Kids from 2004 to 2012. It is a spin-off of the animated series Arthur, based on the books by Marc Brown, and follows Buster Baxter, an anthropomorphic rabbit, as he travels across North America with his father and films his journeys with a video camera.

Format

Each episode mixes animation with live-action documentary segments: Buster visits real children and families in different states, countries and cultures, learning about their lives, languages, foods and traditions, and sends video "postcards" back to his friends in Elwood City. The series was produced by WGBH Boston, Cookie Jar Entertainment (later) and Marc Brown Studios.

Reception and controversy

The show was praised for its portrayal of diverse American childhoods. A 2005 episode, "Sugartime!", featuring children of lesbian parents in Vermont, drew controversy when the U.S. Secretary of Education criticised it and PBS declined to distribute it nationally, though many stations aired it.
