Tom kha gai

TypeSoup
Place of originThailand
Main ingredientsChicken, coconut milk, galangal

Tom kha gai (Thai: ต้มข่าไก่) is a Thai coconut chicken soup flavoured with galangal. It is one of the most popular Thai dishes internationally, prized for its balance of creamy, sour, salty and gently spicy flavours.

Preparation

Coconut milk is heated with bruised galangal slices, lemongrass stalks and makrut lime leaves, then pieces of chicken and straw or oyster mushrooms are simmered in the aromatic milk. The soup is seasoned off the fiercest heat with lime juice and fish sauce so the lime stays bright, and garnished with fresh coriander and sliced chilies.

History and serving

The soup belongs to the tom kha family of coconut-galanga soups. It is eaten as part of a shared Thai meal with rice, rather than as a starter course in the Western sense, and is often recommended as a gentler alternative to the fiery tom yum for diners new to Thai food.
