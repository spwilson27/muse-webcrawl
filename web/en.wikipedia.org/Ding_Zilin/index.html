Ding Zilin

Born20 December 1936, Shanghai, China
OccupationProfessor of philosophy (retired), human rights activist
Known forTiananmen Mothers

Ding Zilin (born 20 December 1936) is a Chinese professor of philosophy and a leader of the Tiananmen Mothers, a group of relatives of victims of the 1989 Tiananmen Square protests and massacre.

Background

Ding taught philosophy at Renmin University of China in Beijing. Her 17-year-old son, Jiang Jielian, was shot and killed by People's Liberation Army troops on the night of 3 June 1989, and was among the first civilian casualties of the crackdown.

Activism

After her son's death, Ding began locating other bereaved families, forming the Tiananmen Mothers group. The group has documented the names and circumstances of victims and annually petitions the Chinese government for an official accounting, the right to mourn publicly, and accountability for those responsible. Ding has faced surveillance and periods of detention in connection with her advocacy.
