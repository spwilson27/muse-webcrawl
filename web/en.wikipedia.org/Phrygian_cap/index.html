Phrygian cap

The Phrygian cap is a soft conical cap with the apex bent over, associated in antiquity with the inhabitants of Phrygia in Anatolia. In the Roman world a similar cap, the pileus, was worn by freed slaves, and the Phrygian cap consequently became a symbol of liberty.

Symbolism

The cap was adopted by revolutionaries during the American and French Revolutions; French revolutionaries wore the red bonnet rouge, and Marianne, the personification of the French Republic, is traditionally depicted wearing one. It appears on the coats of arms and flags of several nations and states, and atop the liberty pole in early American iconography.

In popular culture

The cap is worn by the Smurfs and was part of the Paris 2024 Olympic mascot design, the Phryges.
