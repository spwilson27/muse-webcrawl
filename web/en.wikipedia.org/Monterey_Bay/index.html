Monterey Bay is a bay of the Pacific Ocean located on the coast of the U.S. state of California, south of the San Francisco Bay Area. The bay is fed by upwelling from the deep Monterey Canyon, one of the largest submarine canyons in the world, which begins close to shore near Moss Landing. The nutrient-rich upwelled waters support an exceptionally productive marine ecosystem.

Geography

The bay curves between Santa Cruz at its northern end and the Monterey Peninsula at its southern end, a shoreline of roughly 40 km as the crow flies. Rivers entering the bay include the San Lorenzo, Pajaro, and Salinas rivers. At the center of the bay's shore is Moss Landing, and the small community of Elkhorn Slough, an important estuary and bird habitat.

History

Indigenous peoples, including the Ohlone and Esselen, lived around the bay for millennia. The bay was first recorded by Europeans in 1542, when Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo sailed along the coast. Sebastián Vizcaíno explored and named the bay in 1602 in honor of the Count of Monte Rey, viceroy of New Spain. Monterey became the capital of Spanish and then Mexican Alta California.

In the late 19th and early 20th centuries, Monterey became a major fishing port; its sardine canneries on Cannery Row were immortalized by John Steinbeck's novel Cannery Row (1945). The sardine fishery collapsed in the late 1940s due to overfishing and environmental change.

Marine life and conservation

The Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary, designated in 1992, is one of the largest marine protected areas in the United States. It protects kelp forests, sea otters, harbor seals, sea lions, migrating gray whales, and vast numbers of seabirds and fish. The Monterey Bay Aquarium, opened in 1984 on the site of a former cannery, is a major research and education institution and a leading tourist attraction.

The region is also a center of oceanographic research, home to the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI), Moss Landing Marine Laboratories, and Hopkins Marine Station of Stanford University.
