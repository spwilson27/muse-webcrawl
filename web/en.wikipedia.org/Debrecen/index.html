Debrecen

The Great Church (Nagytemplom) in Debrecen

Debrecen is Hungary's second-largest city, after Budapest, and the seat of Hajdú-Bihar County. Located on the Great Hungarian Plain in eastern Hungary, it had a population of roughly 200,000 in the 2010s–2020s. It is the regional centre of the Northern Great Plain and a historic stronghold of Hungarian Calvinism, sometimes called the "Calvinist Rome".

History

Debrecen was first mentioned in 1235. It twice served as Hungary's capital: in 1849, during the Hungarian Revolution, when Lajos Kossuth proclaimed the dethronement of the Habsburgs in the Great Church, and again briefly in 1944–45. The University of Debrecen traces its roots to the Reformed College founded in 1538.

Culture

Landmarks include the Great Church, the Déri Museum, and the annual Flower Carnival (Virágkarnevál) held around 20 August.
