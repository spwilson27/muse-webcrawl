Zürcher Geschnetzeltes

Zürcher Geschnetzeltes (German for "sliced meat Zurich style") is a Swiss dish from the canton of Zurich, consisting of sliced veal (and often veal kidney) cooked in a creamy white wine and demiglace sauce with mushrooms, onions and cream. It is traditionally served with rösti, the crisp grated-potato cake.

Preparation

Strips of veal are quickly seared, then simmered briefly in a sauce of white wine, cream and meat stock with sautéed mushrooms and onions. The dish is seasoned simply and finished with parsley and a little lemon. Modern variations substitute pork, chicken or turkey for veal.

History

The dish is first documented in cookbooks in the 1940s and became emblematic of Zurich's regional cuisine in the postwar decades. Its name is protected in the sense that a dish sold as Zürcher Geschnetzeltes is expected to contain veal.
