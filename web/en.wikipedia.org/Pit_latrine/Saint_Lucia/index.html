Saint Lucia

Capital: Castries
Official language: English
Currency: East Caribbean dollar

Saint Lucia is an island country in the eastern Caribbean Sea, part of the Lesser Antilles. It is known for the twin volcanic peaks of the Pitons, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and for producing two Nobel laureates: economist Arthur Lewis and poet Derek Walcott.

History

The island was contested between France and Britain for centuries before becoming British in 1814. Saint Lucia gained independence in 1979 as a Commonwealth realm. Its culture blends African, French and British influences, and a French-based creole is widely spoken alongside English.
