Dry toilet

A dry toilet is a toilet that operates without flush water, unlike flush toilets connected to sewers or septic tanks. Human excreta are collected in a chamber or pit where they dry, decompose, or are removed for treatment and possible reuse as fertiliser.

Types

Examples include the simple pit latrine, the ventilated improved pit (VIP) latrine, composting toilets, and urine-diverting dry toilets (UDDTs), which separate urine from faeces to reduce odour and volume. Dry toilets are important where water is scarce or sewerage unavailable, but require careful management to control pathogens and flies.
