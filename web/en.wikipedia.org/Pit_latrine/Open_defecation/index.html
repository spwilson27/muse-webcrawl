Open defecation

Open defecation is the practice of defecating outdoors — in fields, bushes, forests, or bodies of water — rather than using a toilet. It is associated with the spread of diarrhoeal diseases, cholera, typhoid and intestinal worms, particularly where excreta contaminate drinking water.

Prevalence and reduction

Hundreds of millions of people, mainly in South Asia and sub-Saharan Africa, still practise open defecation. Programmes such as Community-Led Total Sanitation and national campaigns like India's Swachh Bharat have sought to end the practice by promoting latrine construction and behaviour change; Sustainable Development Goal 6 includes ending open defecation.
