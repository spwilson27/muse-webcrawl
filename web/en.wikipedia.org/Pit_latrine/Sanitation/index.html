Sanitation

Sanitation refers to public health conditions related to clean drinking water and adequate treatment and disposal of human excreta and sewage. Preventing human contact with faeces and the pathogens they carry is central to sanitation, along with handwashing and safe water supply.

Systems

Sanitation systems range from on-site options such as pit latrines and septic tanks to sewer networks with wastewater treatment plants. The WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme tracks progress using a "sanitation ladder" from open defecation up to safely managed services. Lack of sanitation remains a major cause of diarrhoeal disease worldwide.
