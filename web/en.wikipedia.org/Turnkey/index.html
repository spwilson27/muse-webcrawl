Turnkey

A turnkey project or product is one that is designed, built, supplied and installed completely by a contractor, and made ready for immediate use or operation by the purchaser — the buyer need only "turn the key" to begin. The term is widely used in construction, manufacturing and engineering procurement, where a turnkey contract places responsibility for design, construction, commissioning and delivery of a fully functioning facility on a single contractor, in contrast to traditional design-bid-build procurement where the owner contracts separately with designers and builders.

Usage

Turnkey delivery is common for industrial plants, power stations, hospitals and production lines, and in real estate, where "turnkey" describes a property fully renovated and ready for occupancy or rental. The related contracting form is often called EPC (engineering, procurement and construction). Advantages for the buyer include a single point of responsibility and a fixed price and schedule; disadvantages can include less control over design details and dependence on the contractor's choices.
