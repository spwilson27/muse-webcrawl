Demaster

Demaster is a surname of Dutch origin. It may refer to several people, most notably in the fields of sports, media and the arts.

People

Notable people with the surname include Tom DeMaster, an American former professional baseball player who appeared as a first baseman in Major League Baseball during the 1960s, and Anjali Demaster, an Indian artist and educator. The name is also borne by figures in local government and business in the Netherlands and the United States.
