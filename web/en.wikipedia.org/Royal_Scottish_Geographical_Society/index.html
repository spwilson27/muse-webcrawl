The Royal Scottish Geographical Society (RSGS) is an educational charity based in Perth, Scotland, founded in 1884. The purpose of the society is to advance the understanding of geography — in the sense of the environment, place and people — worldwide, and particularly in Scotland.

History

The society was founded in Edinburgh in 1884 as the Scottish Geographical Society, receiving its royal charter and present name soon afterwards. From its earliest days it supported exploration and promoted geographical education in schools and universities.

Activities

The RSGS runs a national programme of talks and events through local groups across Scotland, publishes the Scottish Geographical Journal (founded as the Scottish Geographical Magazine), and awards medals including the Scottish Geographical Medal and the Livingstone Medal for outstanding contributions to geography.

Collections

The society holds an extensive archive of maps, photographs and documents relating to exploration and travel, much of it digitised and made available to researchers.
