Madras

Madras was the former name of Chennai, the capital city of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu, located on the Coromandel Coast of the Bay of Bengal. The city was officially renamed Chennai in 1996, though the name Madras persists in institutions such as the University of Madras, the Madras High Court (formally the High Court of Judicature at Madras), IIT Madras, and in terms such as Madras curry and the Madras check pattern of cotton cloth.

History

Madras grew around Fort St. George, founded by the British East India Company in 1639–40 after acquiring a grant of land at Madraspatnam. It became the capital of the Madras Presidency, one of the principal administrative divisions of British India, and later of Madras State in independent India, renamed Tamil Nadu in 1969.

Use of the name

The renaming to Chennai reflected the city's Tamil name, Chennapattinam. "Madras" survives in railway and postal usage (the PIN code region), in the Madras Stock Exchange name historically, and in the names of dishes, fabrics, and the former Madras Regiment of the Indian Army.
