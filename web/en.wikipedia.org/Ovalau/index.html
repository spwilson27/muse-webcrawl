CountryFiji

AreaAbout 106 km²

Ovalau is the sixth largest island in Fiji and the main island of the Lomaiviti archipelago, lying east of Viti Levu. It is a rugged volcanic island about 13 by 10 kilometres, whose interior rises steeply from a narrow coastal plain.

Levuka

The island's town, Levuka, on the east coast, was Fiji's first capital and its main port in the nineteenth century; the instrument of cession by which Fiji became a British colony was signed there in 1874. Levuka's preserved colonial waterfront was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2013.

Economy

Fishing, including the PAFCO tuna cannery at Levuka, copra, and small-scale tourism are the main activities. Ovalau is connected by ferry to Viti Levu and has an airstrip at Bureta.
