UEFA coefficient

UEFA coefficients are statistics used for ranking and seeding teams in European club and national team football competitions organised by UEFA. There are three main types: the association (country) coefficient, the club coefficient and the national team coefficient.

Association coefficient

The association coefficient ranks UEFA's member associations according to the results of their clubs in the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League over the previous five seasons. It determines how many berths each league receives in European competitions and at which stage its clubs enter.

Club coefficient

A club's coefficient is based on its own European results over five seasons (or a share of its association coefficient, whichever is higher under past systems) and is used to seed clubs in draws. Points are awarded for wins, draws, progression through rounds and participation bonuses.
