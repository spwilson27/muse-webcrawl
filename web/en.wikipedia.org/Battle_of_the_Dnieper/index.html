Battle of the Dnieper

DateAugust – December 1943
LocationDnieper River, Ukraine, Eastern Front
ResultSoviet victory
ScaleMillions of troops on both sides

The Battle of the Dnieper was one of the largest operations of the Second World War, fought on the Eastern Front in the second half of 1943. Soviet forces advancing after the Battle of Kursk raced German armies to the Dnieper River and forced crossings along a front hundreds of kilometres wide.

The operation

Hitler ordered the river line — part of the "Ostwall" — held at all costs, but Soviet bridgeheads multiplied faster than German reserves could eliminate them. In November 1943 Soviet troops liberated Kiev, the Ukrainian capital.

Outcome

By the end of the year the Red Army had recovered most of Ukraine east of the river and established bridgeheads for the offensives of 1944, at enormous cost in lives on both sides.
