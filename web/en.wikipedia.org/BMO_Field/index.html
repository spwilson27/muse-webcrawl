BMO Field

BMO Field is an outdoor stadium located at Exhibition Place in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It is the home of Toronto FC of Major League Soccer and the Toronto Argonauts of the Canadian Football League. The stadium is owned by the City of Toronto and operated by Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment.

History

BMO Field opened on 28 April 2007 on the site of the former Exhibition Stadium, as a soccer-specific stadium built principally to house Toronto's new MLS expansion team. It was a venue for the 2007 FIFA U-20 World Cup, hosting the final, and later hosted matches at the 2015 FIFA Women's World Cup.

Expansion

A major renovation completed in 2016 added an upper tier to the east stand and a canopy roof over most seats, raising capacity to roughly 30,000 for soccer and enabling the stadium to host the Argonauts. BMO Field hosted the 2016 Grey Cup and the 2017 MLS Cup final, in which Toronto FC became the first Canadian club to win the trophy. The stadium is scheduled to host matches at the 2026 FIFA World Cup, when it will be temporarily renamed under FIFA's sponsorship rules.
