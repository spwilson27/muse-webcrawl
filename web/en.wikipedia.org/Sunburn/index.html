Sunburn

Sunburn is a form of radiation burn that affects living tissue, such as skin, resulting from overexposure to ultraviolet (UV) radiation, usually from the Sun. Common symptoms include red, hot, and sore skin that may peel after a few days; severe sunburn can cause blistering, swelling, fever, and dehydration.

Cause and mechanism

UV radiation damages DNA in skin cells, triggering inflammation and increased blood flow (erythema) as the body responds. The pigment melanin offers partial natural protection, so people with lighter skin burn more easily. Cumulative exposure and repeated burning, especially in childhood, increase the risk of skin cancers including melanoma.

Prevention and treatment

Prevention includes limiting midday sun exposure, protective clothing, and sunscreen, which should be applied generously and reapplied regularly. Treatment is symptomatic: cooling the skin, moisturising, hydration, and pain relievers; most cases resolve within days as damaged skin peels away.
