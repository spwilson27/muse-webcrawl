Cinemark Theatres

Cinemark Holdings, Inc.
Founded: 1984
Headquarters: Plano, Texas, U.S.
Industry: Motion picture exhibition

Cinemark Holdings, Inc. is an American movie theater chain that started operations in 1984 and has since grown to become one of the largest motion picture exhibitors in the world. Headquartered in Plano, Texas, it operates theatres under the Cinemark, Century Theatres, Rave Cinemas, Tinseltown, and Cinéarts brands.

Operations

Cinemark operates hundreds of theatres with several thousand screens across the United States and Latin America, where it is a leading exhibitor in countries including Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, and Colombia. Its premium large format is marketed as Cinemark XD. In 2006 the company acquired Century Theatres, expanding its presence in the western United States.

History

The company was founded by Lee Roy Mitchell. Cinemark expanded rapidly in the 1990s with stadium-seating megaplexes and grew its international division, particularly in Latin America, before an initial public offering in 2007.
