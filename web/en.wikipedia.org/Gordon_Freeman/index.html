Gordon Freeman

Gordon Freeman is the silent protagonist of the Half-Life video game series, developed by Valve. A theoretical physicist by training, Freeman is employed at the Black Mesa Research Facility when an experiment triggers the resonance cascade, an interdimensional disaster that forms the inciting incident of the first game, Half-Life (1998). He is depicted wearing the orange HEV (hazardous environment) suit and is most often associated with his signature weapon, the crowbar.

Character

Freeman never speaks and is never seen outside the player's first-person view in the original games, a design choice intended to immerse the player. According to in-game documents, he holds a PhD in theoretical physics from MIT and wrote his thesis on teleportation. The mysterious G-Man places Freeman in stasis after the Black Mesa incident and "rehires" him twenty years later in Half-Life 2 (2004), where Freeman becomes a figurehead of the human resistance against the alien Combine occupying Earth, aided by Alyx Vance and former colleagues from Black Mesa.

Reception

Freeman is consistently ranked among the greatest video game characters of all time. He won GameSpot's "All Time Greatest Video Game Hero" reader contest in 2009, narrowly defeating Mario in the final round, and his crowbar has become one of gaming's most recognisable symbols.
