Bandha (yoga)

Bandha (Sanskrit for "bond", "lock") is a body lock in Hatha yoga, a muscular contraction combined with breath retention intended to direct the flow of prana (vital energy) within the body. Bandhas are classically combined with mudras and pranayama.

The principal bandhas

The three main bandhas are mula bandha, contraction of the perineum and pelvic floor; uddiyana bandha, drawing the abdomen inward and upward after exhalation; and jalandhara bandha, lowering the chin to the chest. When all three are performed together the combination is called maha bandha, the "great lock". Modern yoga styles, especially Ashtanga vinyasa, emphasise mula and uddiyana bandha as supports for posture and core stability.

Sources

The bandhas are described in medieval Hatha yoga texts such as the Hatha Yoga Pradipika and the Gheranda Samhita, which credit them with awakening kundalini energy and preserving youth.
