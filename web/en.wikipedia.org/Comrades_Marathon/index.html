Comrades Marathon

LocationKwaZulu-Natal, South Africa
DistanceAbout 89 km (55 mi)
Established1921

The Comrades Marathon is an ultramarathon run annually in South Africa between Durban and Pietermaritzburg. It is the world's largest and oldest ultramarathon race, alternating direction each year between an up run and a down run.

History

The race was founded by Vic Clapham to commemorate soldiers killed in the First World War and first run in 1921 with 34 runners. It now attracts tens of thousands of entrants.

Course

Runners face a 12-hour cut-off and a series of famous hills. Finishing medals are named by category, with the race holding deep cultural significance in South Africa.
