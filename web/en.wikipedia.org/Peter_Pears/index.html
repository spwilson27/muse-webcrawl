Peter Pears

Sir Peter Neville Luard Pears (22 June 1910 – 3 April 1986) was an English tenor, best known for his creative partnership with the composer Benjamin Britten. Britten wrote many of his leading operatic roles and song cycles specifically for Pears's distinctive voice.

Peter PearsBorn: 22 June 1910, Farnham, Surrey
Died: 3 April 1986, Aldeburgh, Suffolk
Occupation: Operatic tenor
Partner: Benjamin Britten

Career

Pears created the title roles in Britten's operas Peter Grimes (1945), Albert Herring, and Billy Budd (as Captain Vere), and sang in the premieres of the War Requiem (1962) and Death in Venice (1973). With Britten he co-founded the Aldeburgh Festival in 1948 and the Britten–Pears School for Advanced Musical Studies at Snape Maltings.

Legacy

His recordings, many with Britten as accompanist or conductor, include definitive accounts of Schubert and Schumann song cycles as well as Britten's works. He was knighted in 1978.
