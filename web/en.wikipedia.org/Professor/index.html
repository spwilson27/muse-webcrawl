Professor

A professor is an academic rank at universities and other higher-education institutions. In most systems it is the highest academic rank, awarded to scholars recognized for expertise and research in their field.

Systems

In North America, ranks run assistant professor, associate professor, and (full) professor, typically with tenure at the senior levels; "professor" colloquially denotes any college teacher. In the United Kingdom and Commonwealth, professor is a senior title above reader and senior lecturer, often attached to a chair. In continental Europe, systems vary, many requiring a habilitation. Professors teach, supervise graduate students, conduct research, and perform institutional service. Retired professors of distinction may become professor emeritus.
