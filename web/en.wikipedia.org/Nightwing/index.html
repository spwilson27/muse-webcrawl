Nightwing

Nightwing is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The identity was first adopted by Superman in pre-Crisis stories set in Kandor, but it is most famously associated with Dick Grayson, the original Robin, who took the name after outgrowing his role as Batman's sidekick. Grayson first became Nightwing in Tales of the Teen Titans #44 (1984).

Fictional character biography

Dick Grayson was a young circus acrobat whose parents were murdered; Bruce Wayne took him in as his ward and trained him as Robin. As a young adult, after tensions with Batman and his growth as leader of the Teen Titans, Grayson relinquished the Robin identity — which passed to Jason Todd — and adopted the name Nightwing, inspired by a Kryptonian legend Superman had once told him.

As Nightwing, Grayson established himself as the protector of the crime-ridden city of Blüdhaven, working for a time as a police officer by day. He has been a core member of the Titans, the Outsiders, and the Justice League, and temporarily assumed the mantle of Batman himself following the apparent death of Bruce Wayne, mentoring Damian Wayne as his Robin.

Abilities

Nightwing is a master acrobat and martial artist, trained by Batman and other renowned fighters. He is an expert detective, tactician, and escapologist, and fights with a pair of escrima sticks.
