New London is a seaport city in southeastern Connecticut, United States, situated at the mouth of the Thames River where it meets Long Island Sound. Founded by John Winthrop Jr. in 1646, it is one of the oldest English settlements in the state.

History

New London was a major whaling port in the 19th century and an important naval base during the American Revolutionary War and later conflicts. In 1781 a British force under Benedict Arnold burned much of the town and captured Fort Griswold across the river in Groton.

Education and military

The city is home to Connecticut College, Mitchell College and the United States Coast Guard Academy, which has been located in New London since 1932. Across the Thames in Groton lies Naval Submarine Base New London, the principal submarine base of the United States Navy.
