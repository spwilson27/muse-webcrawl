Polymer

A polymer is a substance composed of very large molecules, macromolecules, made of many repeating subunits called monomers. Natural polymers include proteins, DNA, cellulose and rubber; synthetic polymers include polyethylene, PVC, nylon and polystyrene.

Polymer
Subunit: Monomer
Examples: Polyethylene, proteins, DNA
Field: Polymer chemistry / materials science

Structure

Polymers may be linear, branched or cross-linked networks; homopolymers use one monomer, copolymers two or more. Crystalline and amorphous regions determine mechanical properties — measured at small scale by nanoindentation.

Synthesis

Addition (chain-growth) and condensation (step-growth) polymerisation. Hermann Staudinger established the macromolecular hypothesis in the 1920s.

Biopolymers

Proteins are polymers of amino acids folded into structures such as the alpha helix and beta sheet.
