Caravan (band)

Caravan are an English rock band from the Canterbury area, formed in 1968 by former Wilde Flowers members Dave Sinclair, Richard Sinclair, Pye Hastings, and Richard Coughlan. Blending psychedelic rock, progressive rock, and jazz, they became central figures of the Canterbury scene.

History

Their self-titled debut appeared in 1968. The albums If I Could Do It All Over Again, I'd Do It All Over You (1970) and In the Land of Grey and Pink (1971) — the latter featuring the fan favorite "Golf Girl" and the extended suite "Nine Feet Underground" — are regarded as classics of the genre. Later albums such as For Girls Who Grow Plump in the Night (1973) and Cunning Stunts (1975) kept a loyal following, though commercial success remained elusive. The band split in 1978 but re-formed several times; Pye Hastings has led continuing versions of Caravan, releasing albums including Paradise Filter (2013) and It's None of Your Business (2021).

Style

Caravan's sound is melodic and whimsical, built around Dave Sinclair's organ and Hastings' guitar and vocals, with Richard Sinclair's distinctive bass playing and singing.
