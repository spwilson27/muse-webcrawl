Electronic circuit

An electronic circuit is a network of interconnected electronic components — such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes and transistors — arranged so that electric current flows through them to perform a useful function, such as amplification, computation or signal processing.

Types

Analogue circuits process continuous signals, while digital circuits work with discrete levels representing binary values. Many modern circuits are mixed-signal, combining both. Circuits may be built from discrete components on a printed circuit board or integrated into a single semiconductor chip as an integrated circuit.

Analysis

Circuit behaviour is described using Kirchhoff's laws, Ohm's law and network analysis techniques. Diagrams representing circuits are called schematics.

Design

Design proceeds from specification through simulation to layout and testing, increasingly with computer-aided design tools.
