Fat acceptance movement

The fat acceptance movement (also known as fat pride or fat liberation) is a social movement that seeks to eliminate discrimination against people based on body weight and size, and to change societal attitudes about fatness. It began in the United States in the late 1960s, with organisations such as NAAFA (founded 1969).

Activists campaign against weight-based discrimination in employment, healthcare, and media, and promote body positivity. The movement is debated: critics argue it may downplay health risks of obesity, while supporters argue it opposes stigma and that health behaviours matter more than weight itself.
