Rahul Gandhi (born 19 June 1970) is an Indian politician who has served as the Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha since June 2024. A member of the Indian National Congress, he is a member of the Nehru–Gandhi family, the son of former prime minister Rajiv Gandhi and Congress leader Sonia Gandhi, and the grandson of Indira Gandhi.

Rahul Gandhi at a public meeting during an election campaign.

Early life and education

Gandhi was born in New Delhi and educated at St. Stephen's College, Delhi, and later at Rollins College in Florida, where he completed a bachelor's degree in 1994, followed by an MPhil in development studies at Trinity College, Cambridge. Before entering politics he worked in management consulting in London and co-founded a technology outsourcing firm in Mumbai.

Political career

Gandhi entered politics in 2004, winning the Lok Sabha seat of Amethi in Uttar Pradesh, a constituency previously held by his parents. He became general secretary of the Congress party in 2007, overseeing the Indian Youth Congress and the National Students' Union of India, and was appointed party vice-president in 2013. He served as Congress president from December 2017 until August 2019, resigning after the party's defeat in the 2019 general election.

In 2022–23 he led the Bharat Jodo Yatra, a 4,000-kilometre march from Kanyakumari to Kashmir. In the 2024 general election he won from both Wayanad and Rae Bareli, retained Rae Bareli, and became Leader of the Opposition after Congress doubled its seat tally.

Personal life

Gandhi holds a black belt in aikido and is a certified scuba diver. He has remained unmarried, unusual for an Indian political leader of his prominence.
