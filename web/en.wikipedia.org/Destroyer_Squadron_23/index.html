Destroyer Squadron 23 (DESRON 23) is a squadron of United States Navy destroyers. It is best remembered for its service in the Solomon Islands campaign of World War II under Commodore Arleigh Burke, when its ships earned the nickname the "Little Beavers" and the squadron became one of the most decorated destroyer units of the war.

World War II

The squadron, composed of Fletcher-class destroyers, fought through the Central Solomons and Bougainville campaigns in 1943–44. Under Burke, the squadron perfected aggressive night-fighting tactics using radar, culminating in the Battle of Cape St. George in November 1943, in which the Little Beavers sank three Japanese destroyers without loss—a action Burke called an almost perfect surface engagement. The squadron's emblem, the Little Beaver character from the Red Ryder comics, was painted on its ships' torpedo tubes.

Later service

After World War II the squadron was repeatedly reorganized as the Navy's destroyer force evolved. In its modern form, DESRON 23 is based on the U.S. West Coast and provides administrative and tactical oversight for a group of guided-missile destroyers, deploying with carrier strike groups.

Legacy

Arleigh Burke's tenure with the Little Beavers made his reputation and foreshadowed his later tenure as Chief of Naval Operations; the lead ship of the Arleigh Burke class of destroyers commemorates him. The squadron's World War II record remains a celebrated episode in U.S. destroyer history.
