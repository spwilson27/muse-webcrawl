List of areas in the United States National Park System

The National Park System comprises more than 430 units administered by the National Park Service (NPS), covering more than 85 million acres in all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and U.S. territories. Units include national parks, monuments, memorials, historic sites, battlefields, recreation areas, seashores, lakeshores, rivers, parkways, and trails. The 63 congressionally designated national parks are listed below.
