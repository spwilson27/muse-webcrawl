The Tamiami Trail is the southernmost 275 miles (443 km) of U.S. Highway 41 (US 41) from State Road 60 in Tampa to US 1 in Miami, Florida. The trail's name is a portmanteau of the names of the two terminal cities, "Tampa" and "Miami". Completed in 1928, it was the first road to cross the Everglades of South Florida.

History

Construction began in 1915 and took thirteen years, requiring the dredging of a canal alongside the roadway through swamp and limestone. The trail opened to great fanfare in April 1928 and linked Florida's Gulf and Atlantic coasts.

Everglades

The eastern section crosses the Big Cypress National Preserve and skirts Everglades National Park. The road and its parallel canal long altered water flow in the Everglades, and bridge-raising projects in the 2010s restored sheet flow beneath the highway.
