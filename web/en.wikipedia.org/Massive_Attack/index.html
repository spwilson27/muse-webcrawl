Massive Attack

Massive Attack are an English electronic music group formed in Bristol in 1988 by Robert "3D" Del Naja, Adrian "Tricky" Thaws, and Andrew "Mushroom" Vowles. Their debut album, Blue Lines (1991), is credited with founding the trip hop genre, and their third album, Mezzanine (1998), is widely ranked among the greatest albums of all time. The group now centres on Del Naja and Grant "Daddy G" Marshall.

Massive Attack pioneered the downbeat Bristol sound known as trip hop.

Formation and Blue Lines

The group's members emerged from the Wild Bunch, a Bristol sound system collective. Blue Lines fused hip hop, dub, soul, and reggae sampling with guest vocals from Shara Nelson, Horace Andy, and Tricky; its single "Unfinished Sympathy" is regularly cited as one of the finest songs of its era. During the 1991 Gulf War the band briefly dropped "Attack" from its billing at its record label's request.

Protection and Mezzanine

Protection (1994) featured Tracey Thorn of Everything but the Girl and the title track's celebrated Michel Gondry video. Tricky left for a solo career, and tensions during the recording of Mezzanine (1998) — a darker record built around post-punk samples and the voice of Elizabeth Fraser, whose "Teardrop" became one of the band's best-known songs — led to Vowles's departure. The album topped charts and won wide critical acclaim.

Later work and activism

Later albums include 100th Window (2003), effectively a Del Naja solo project, and Heligoland (2010). Del Naja is also a graffiti artist and prominent political activist; the band has campaigned against the Iraq War, supported environmental causes, and commissioned research into the live-music industry's carbon footprint, pursuing low-emission touring.
