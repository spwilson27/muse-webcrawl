Office building

An office building, also known as an office block or business center, is a form of commercial building which contains spaces mainly designed to be used for offices. The primary purpose of an office building is to provide a workplace and working environment primarily for administrative and managerial workers.

A modern office tower.

Design

Office buildings are commonly divided into open-plan areas, cellular offices, meeting rooms, and support spaces such as kitchens and copy rooms. They are classified in commercial property markets as Class A, B, or C according to quality, location, and amenities. Tall office buildings — skyscrapers — became possible with steel-frame construction and the elevator in the late nineteenth century.

Workplace conditions

Indoor environmental quality in offices (lighting, ventilation, thermal comfort, and noise) affects productivity and health, and is studied by fields including occupational health psychology. The growth of remote and hybrid working since 2020 has changed demand for conventional office space in many cities.
