HD Radio

HD Radio (HDR) is a trademark for an in-band on-channel (IBOC) digital radio broadcast technology used primarily in the United States, Canada and Mexico. It allows AM and FM stations to broadcast digital audio alongside their existing analog signals.

Technology

Developed by iBiquity Digital Corporation (later acquired by DTS, then Xperi), HD Radio adds digital sidebands adjacent to the analog carrier. FM stations can multicast additional audio channels (HD2, HD3, etc.) and transmit programme-associated data such as song titles and traffic information.

Adoption

Thousands of US stations broadcast in HD Radio, and receivers are common in new cars, though the system saw little adoption outside North America, where DAB is more common.
