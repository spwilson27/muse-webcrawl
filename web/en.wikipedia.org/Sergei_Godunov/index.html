Sergei Godunov

Sergei Konstantinovich Godunov (Russian: Сергей Константинович Годунов; 17 July 1929 – 15 July 2023) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician who made foundational contributions to computational fluid dynamics and the numerical solution of hyperbolic partial differential equations.

Career

Born in Moscow, Godunov studied at Moscow State University and worked for much of his career at the Sobolev Institute of Mathematics in Novosibirsk. He was elected a full member of the Russian Academy of Sciences in 1976 and received numerous honours, including the Lenin Prize.

Contributions

Godunov is best known for Godunov's method, a conservative numerical scheme for solving the Euler equations using exact or approximate Riemann solvers at cell interfaces, and for Godunov's theorem, which proves that monotone linear schemes for convection equations can be at most first-order accurate — a result that motivated modern high-resolution, limiter-based methods. His work on the Riemann problem and on viscosity solutions for hyperbolic systems remains central to numerical analysis.
