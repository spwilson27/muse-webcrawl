Alternative metal

Alternative metal bands blended heavy metal with funk, rap, industrial and other styles.

Alternative metal is a heavy metal music genre that infuses heavy metal with influences from alternative rock and other styles not normally associated with metal, such as funk, hip hop, industrial, jazz and progressive rock. It emerged in the mid-to-late 1980s and gained major popularity in the 1990s.

History

Pioneering acts included Faith No More, Jane's Addiction, Living Colour, Soundgarden, Alice in Chains, Primus and Helmet, many of them embraced by the alternative-rock audience of the Lollapalooza era. Bands such as Rage Against the Machine fused metal with rap, while Tool and Nine Inch Nails brought in progressive and industrial elements.

Nu metal and later developments

In the mid-1990s a more commercially focused offshoot, nu metal — led by Korn, Limp Bizkit and later Linkin Park — combined down-tuned riffs with hip-hop beats, turntablism and angsty lyrics, dominating rock radio around 2000. Alternative metal bands of the 2000s and 2010s, including Deftones, System of a Down and Mastodon (in part), continued to cross genre boundaries.

Characteristics

Typical traits include heavy, often down-tuned guitar riffs, unconventional song structures, wide-ranging vocals from melodic singing to screaming, and experimentation with instrumentation and rhythm drawn from outside metal.
