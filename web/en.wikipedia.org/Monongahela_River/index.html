Monongahela River

Length209 km (130 mi)
SourceFairmont, West Virginia
MouthOhio River at Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

The Monongahela River — often called "the Mon" — is a 130-mile river on the Allegheny Plateau in north-central West Virginia and southwestern Pennsylvania. It joins the Allegheny River at Pittsburgh to form the Ohio River.

Course

Unusually for North American rivers, the Monongahela flows northward, formed by the confluence of the West Fork and Tygart Valley rivers at Fairmont, West Virginia. A system of locks and dams maintained by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers makes the river navigable for its entire length.

History

The name comes from a Lenape phrase meaning "river with high banks that fall in." The valley was a centre of the American steel industry, lined with mills at towns such as Homestead and Braddock, and remains an important barge route for coal.
