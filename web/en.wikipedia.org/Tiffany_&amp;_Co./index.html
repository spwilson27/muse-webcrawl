Tiffany & Co.

Tiffany & Co. is an American luxury jewellery and specialty design house headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1837 by Charles Lewis Tiffany and John B. Young as a stationery and fancy goods store, the company became famous for its diamond jewellery, sterling silver goods and its distinctive robin's-egg blue packaging, known as Tiffany Blue.

History

The company introduced the English standard of sterling silver in the United States and set the .950 platinum standard for jewellery. Its flagship store on Fifth Avenue, opened in 1940, was immortalised by the 1961 film Breakfast at Tiffany's. In 1886 Tiffany introduced the Tiffany Setting, a six-prong solitaire diamond ring that became an enduring symbol of engagement rings.

Acquisition

After a long-negotiated deal, the French luxury conglomerate LVMH acquired Tiffany & Co. in January 2021 for about US$15.8 billion, the largest acquisition in LVMH's history at the time.
