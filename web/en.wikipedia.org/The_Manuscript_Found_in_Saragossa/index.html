AuthorJan Potocki
Writtenc. 1794–1810

GenreFrame tale / Gothic novel

The Manuscript Found in Saragossa (French: Manuscrit trouvé à Saragosse) is a frame-tale novel by the Polish aristocrat and writer Jan Potocki (1761–1815), written in French over many years around the turn of the 19th century.

Structure

The novel is organized around sixty-six days of narration. Its frame concerns Alphonse van Worden, a young officer travelling through the Sierra Morena in Spain, who encounters supernatural events and hears a labyrinth of nested stories told by gypsies, hermits, kabbalists, and others, including the Wandering Jew.

Publication and reception

Parts were published during Potocki's lifetime, but the complete text survived in complicated manuscript and translation history; a full Polish translation appeared in 1847. The work was long neglected but is now recognized as a masterpiece of the fantastic. It was adapted into a celebrated Polish film in 1965 by Wojciech Has.
