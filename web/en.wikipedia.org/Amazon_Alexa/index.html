Amazon Alexa

Amazon Alexa is a virtual assistant technology developed by Amazon, first released in November 2014 alongside the Amazon Echo smart speaker. Alexa responds to voice commands to play music, set timers and alarms, provide weather and news, control smart-home devices, and answer questions.

Features and ecosystem

Users extend Alexa with installable "skills," and the assistant is built into Echo devices, Fire TV, and many third-party products. Alexa's far-field microphones and cloud-based speech recognition raised continuing public debate about privacy and always-listening devices. In 2025 Amazon introduced Alexa+, a generative-AI upgrade of the assistant.
