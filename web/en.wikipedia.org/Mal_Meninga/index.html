Malcolm Norman Meninga AM (born 8 July 1960) is an Australian former professional rugby league footballer and coach. A giant centre, he captained Australia and the Canberra Raiders, leading Canberra to its first premierships.

Playing career

Meninga began his career with Souths in Brisbane before a season with St Helens in England. He joined Canberra in 1986 and captained the Raiders to premierships in 1989, 1990 and 1994. He played 46 Tests for Australia and was the first player to tour England with four Kangaroo squads. Renowned for his size, strength, goalkicking and leadership, he was named in Australia's Team of the Century in 2008 and inducted as an Immortal of the game in 2018.

Coaching career

After coaching Canberra, Meninga became one of the most successful representative coaches: he led Queensland to dominance in State of Origin from 2006 and coached the Australian national team from 2016, winning World Cup and Four Nations titles.
