The United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), formerly known as the United Nations International Strategy for Disaster Reduction (UNISDR), is the focal point of the United Nations system for disaster risk reduction. It supports the implementation, follow-up, and review of the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030.

History

The office was created on 22 December 1999 under General Assembly Resolution 54/219, which established the Inter-Agency Secretariat of the International Strategy for Disaster Reduction. It succeeded the secretariat of the International Decade for Natural Disaster Reduction (1990–1999), proclaimed in 1989.

The UNISDR secretariat coordinated the development of the Hyogo Framework for Action 2005–2015, adopted at the World Conference on Disaster Reduction in Kobe, Japan, in January 2005 — weeks after the devastating Indian Ocean tsunami. Its successor, the Sendai Framework, was adopted at the third World Conference on Disaster Risk Reduction in Sendai, Japan, in March 2015. On 1 May 2019, the office changed its name from UNISDR to UNDRR to align its acronym with its full name.

Mandate

UNDRR's mandate includes:

  • Coordinating international efforts in disaster risk reduction
  • Monitoring and reporting on progress under the Sendai Framework through the Sendai Framework Monitor
  • Campaigning to raise awareness, including the Making Cities Resilient campaign
  • Convening the Global Platform for Disaster Risk Reduction, held every two to three years
  • Publishing the Global Assessment Report on Disaster Risk Reduction (GAR)

Sendai Framework

The Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030 sets four priorities: understanding disaster risk; strengthening disaster risk governance; investing in disaster risk reduction for resilience; and enhancing disaster preparedness for effective response and to "Build Back Better". It includes seven global targets, among them substantially reducing global disaster mortality and the number of affected people by 2030.

Leadership

The office is headed by the Special Representative of the Secretary-General for Disaster Risk Reduction. Past heads include Sálvano Briceño, Margareta Wahlström (the first to hold the Special Representative title, from 2008), Robert Glasser, and Mami Mizutori.
