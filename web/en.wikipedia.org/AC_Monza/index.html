Associazione Calcio Monza is a professional football club based in Monza, Lombardy, Italy. Founded in 1912, the club spent most of its history in Italy's second and third tiers — at one time holding the record for the most Serie B seasons without ever reaching the top flight — before finally winning promotion to Serie A in 2022.

History

Monza's traditional kit is a red shirt with white shorts. The club's honours include several Serie C titles and the Anglo-Italian Cup of 1976, won against Wimbledon. After bankruptcy in 2015 refounded the club in Serie D, it was purchased in 2018 by Silvio Berlusconi's Fininvest holding, with former AC Milan executive Adriano Galliani — a Monza native — as chief executive. Managed by Giovanni Stroppa, Monza won the 2021–22 Serie B play-offs, defeating Pisa to reach Serie A for the first time in its 110-year history, and finished a creditable mid-table in its debut top-flight season under Raffaele Palladino.

Stadium

The club plays at the Stadio Brianteo, opened in 1988 and known for sponsorship reasons as the U-Power Stadium, located near the famous Monza Park and the Autodromo Nazionale Monza racing circuit.
