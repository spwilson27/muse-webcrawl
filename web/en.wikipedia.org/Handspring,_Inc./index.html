Handspring, Inc.

Handspring, Inc. was an American electronics company founded in 1998 by Jeff Hawkins, Donna Dubinsky and Ed Colligan — the founders of Palm, Inc. — to develop handheld computers and smartphones. It is best known for the Visor line of Palm OS devices and the Treo smartphone.

Products

The Visor, launched in 1999, ran Palm OS and introduced the Springboard expansion slot for plug-in modules such as cameras, modems and GPS receivers, selling at lower prices than Palm's own devices. In 2002 Handspring launched the Treo line, combining a phone with a handheld organiser and a thumb keyboard; the Treo 600 of 2003 was a notable success.

Acquisition

In 2003 Handspring was acquired by PalmSource/Palm interests, merging back into Palm, Inc., where the Treo line continued as a flagship smartphone range until the market shifted to touch-screen devices later in the decade.
