Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency is a humorous detective novel by English writer Douglas Adams, first published in 1987. It has been described by the author as a "thumping good detective-ghost-horror-who dunnit-time travel-romance-mystery with musical interludes". The plot weaves together an ancient ghost, time travel, the poet Samuel Taylor Coleridge, and a bewildered computer programmer, Richard MacDuff.

Plot

Svlad Cjelli, who calls himself Dirk Gently, runs a detective agency founded on his belief in the "fundamental interconnectedness of all things"; he solves cases by following apparently random connections. The story involves the Electric Monk, a labour-saving device that believes things for its owner, a murder at a technology company, and a ghost from the planet Salaxalan whose ancient mistake threatens the origin of life on Earth. Elements of the story were recycled from Adams's Doctor Who scripts, including the serial Shada and the character of Professor Chronotis from Cambridge.

Sequels and adaptations

A sequel, The Long Dark Tea-Time of the Soul, appeared in 1988, and a third volume was left unfinished at Adams's death. The novels have been adapted for radio, stage, and television, including a 2010 BBC Four series with Stephen Mangan and a 2016 BBC America/Netflix series created by Max Landis starring Samuel Barnett and Elijah Wood.
