Polar Bears International

Founded1994 (as Polar Bears Alive)
TypeNonprofit conservation organisation
FocusPolar bear and sea ice conservation
HeadquartersBozeman, Montana, US; operations in Churchill, Manitoba

Polar Bears International (PBI) is a nonprofit organisation dedicated to the conservation of polar bears and the Arctic sea ice they depend on. It is recognised as a leading scientific and outreach authority on polar bears.

Work

PBI supports and conducts research on polar bear populations, denning, and the effects of climate warming on sea ice habitat. It operates outreach from Churchill, Manitoba — the "Polar Bear Capital of the World" — including live polar bear cameras during the autumn bear migration and the Tundra Connections educational webcasts for schools.

Campaigns

The organisation founded International Polar Bear Day, observed on 27 February, and runs programmes such as the Polar Bear Tracker, which follows satellite-collared bears. Its stated mission is to conserve polar bears by protecting their sea ice habitat, principally by advocating action on climate change.
