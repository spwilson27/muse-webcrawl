Vijayawada

Vijayawada, formerly known as Bezawada, is the second largest city in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It lies on the banks of the Krishna River, bounded by the Indrakeeladri Hills to the west, and is a major commercial, political and educational hub, historically a trade centre of the Eastern Chalukyas.

History and economy

The city's name, "Land of Victory", is traditionally linked to the goddess Durga's victory over the demon Mahishasura on Indrakeeladri Hill, site of the Kanaka Durga Temple. Vijayawada is a major railway junction and road transport hub, known for its automobile, textile and food-processing industries, and it forms part of the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region centred on nearby Amaravati.

Culture

The city is noted for Kondapalli toys, the Undavalli Caves, Bhavani Island in the Krishna, and its claim to some of the spiciest cuisine in Andhra Pradesh.
