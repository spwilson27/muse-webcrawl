Flag of Russia

The flag of Russia is a tricolour flag consisting of three equal horizontal fields: white on the top, blue in the middle, and red on the bottom. The flag was first used as an ensign for Russian merchant ships in the late 17th century and became the official national flag of the Russian Empire and later of the Russian Federation. The current version, with proportions of 2:3, was adopted on 11 December 1993, shortly after the constitutional crisis of 1993, replacing the 1991 version which had used a lighter blue stripe and 1:2 proportions.

History

The white-blue-red tricolour is traditionally associated with Tsar Peter the Great, who raised it on the frigate Oryol and adopted it for the Russian merchant fleet in 1705, drawing inspiration from the Dutch flag. In 1858, Tsar Alexander II introduced a black-yellow-white tricolour as the state flag, but the white-blue-red flag remained in popular and commercial use and was restored for official purposes in 1883; in 1896 it was confirmed as the sole national flag. After the October Revolution of 1917 the Bolsheviks replaced it with the red flag of the Soviet Union. The tricolour returned on 22 August 1991 during the August Coup attempt — a date now celebrated as the Day of the National Flag of the Russian Federation — and was formally adopted by the Russian SFSR and then the Russian Federation.

Symbolism

There is no official interpretation of the colours. Popular explanations associate white with nobility and freedom, blue with faithfulness and the Virgin Mary as protectress of Russia, and red with courage, the people, and state power. The three colours also became the pan-Slavic colours, adopted — in various arrangements — by many other Slavic nations' flags, including those of Serbia, Croatia, Slovenia, Slovakia and the Czech Republic.

Related flags

The presidential standard of Russia bears the national coat of arms on the tricolour. The naval ensign of Russia is the blue saltire of St Andrew on a white field, and the naval jack combines the tricolour with the St Andrew's cross. Federal subjects and cities of Russia have their own flags, generally registered with the State Heraldic Register.
