Low Pay Commission

The Low Pay Commission (LPC) is an independent statutory body in the United Kingdom that advises the government on the National Minimum Wage and the National Living Wage. It was established under the National Minimum Wage Act 1998, ahead of the minimum wage's introduction in April 1999.

Role

The Commission has nine commissioners — drawn from employer, worker and independent backgrounds — supported by a secretariat. Each year it consults widely, analyses earnings and employment evidence, and recommends the rates that take effect the following April, balancing pay protection for low-paid workers against employment effects. Its recommendations have historically been accepted by governments of all parties.
