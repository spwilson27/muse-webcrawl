Drishyam (transl. Visual) is a 2015 Indian Hindi-language crime thriller film directed by Nishikant Kamat. A remake of Jeethu Joseph's 2013 Malayalam film of the same name, it stars Ajay Devgn as Vijay Salgaonkar, a cable-television operator in Goa; Shriya Saran as his wife Nandini; and Tabu as Inspector General Meera Deshmukh.

Plot

Vijay, a movie-obsessed orphan with only a fourth-grade education, lives contentedly with his wife and two daughters. When the family returns from a nature camp, his elder daughter Anju is blackmailed with a secretly filmed video by Varun, the spoilt son of IG Meera Deshmukh, who has come to the house to confront her. In the struggle, Anju strikes Varun with a pipe and kills him.

Vijay buries the body and, drawing on plots absorbed from countless films, constructs an elaborate false alibi: he retraces a family trip to Panaji — re-buying tickets, meals, and film and temple visits on later dates — and coaches his family to repeat the story consistently. Under aggressive interrogation, including the beating of his young daughter, the family holds firm; the only slip reveals where a body was buried, but by then Vijay has moved it. The case against the family collapses for lack of evidence, and Vijay signs a confession of conscience to Meera and her husband, leaving them with a measure of closure but no proof.

Reception

The film was a commercial success and received generally positive reviews, with particular praise for the performances of Devgn and Tabu, the screenplay's construction, and its climax. A sequel, Drishyam 2, was released in 2022.
