Titanfall is a first-person shooter video game developed by Respawn Entertainment and published by Electronic Arts. It was released for Microsoft Windows and Xbox One in March 2014, with an Xbox 360 version following. It was the debut title of Respawn, founded by Jason West and Vince Zampella, formerly of Infinity Ward.

Gameplay

Players battle as agile, wall-running Pilots who can call in Titans — large mechanised exoskeletons that drop from orbit — and either pilot them or have them follow in an autonomous guard mode. Matches are multiplayer-only, blending human players with AI grunts in modes such as Attrition and Hardpoint. The game's emphasis on vertical movement and momentum influenced later shooters.

Reception and legacy

Titanfall won over 60 awards at its E3 2013 reveal and was praised for its movement systems and Titan mechanics, though some critics noted the thin campaign. A sequel, Titanfall 2 (2016), added a widely acclaimed single-player campaign, and the battle royale spin-off Apex Legends (2019) is set in the same universe.
