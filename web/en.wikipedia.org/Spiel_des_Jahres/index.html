Spiel des Jahres

The Spiel des Jahres (German for "Game of the Year") is an award for board and card games, created in 1978 with the stated purpose of rewarding family-friendly game design and promoting excellent German games in the general public. It is widely considered the most prestigious award in board gaming, and winning games typically see large increases in sales.

History and criteria

The award is given by a jury of German-speaking board game critics who review games released in Germany in the preceding twelve months. The criteria include game concept, game mechanics, packaging and presentation, and the clarity of the rules. The jury has changed the structure of the award over time; companion awards include the Kinderspiel des Jahres (Children's Game of the Year, from 2001) and the Kennerspiel des Jahres (Connoisseur Game of the Year, from 2011).

Notable winners

Famous winners include Hare and Tortoise (1979, the first winner), Scotland Yard (1983), The Settlers of Catan (1995), Carcassonne (2001), Ticket to Ride (2004), Dominion (2009), Codenames (2016), Azul (2018), and Cascadia (2022).

Impact

Nomination alone can multiply a game's sales several-fold, and the winner's logo, printed on the box, is a powerful marketing tool in the German market, the historical center of the modern board game hobby.
