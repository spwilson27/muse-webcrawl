The Hertz Corporation

The Hertz Corporation, known as Hertz, is an American car rental company headquartered in Estero, Florida. It operates the Hertz, Dollar and Thrifty rental brands in approximately 150 countries.

History

The company was founded in Chicago in 1918 by Walter L. Jacobs, who began renting out a dozen Model T Fords. John Hertz bought the company in 1923, and it was renamed Hertz Drive-Ur-Self System; Jacobs later rejoined as president. Hertz pioneered many features of the rental industry, including airport rental locations, and for much of the 20th century was the largest car rental company in the United States, competing principally with Avis.

After passing through several owners, including RCA and UAL, Hertz became a public company in 2006 and was acquired by private equity owners in 2005. The company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in May 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic and emerged in 2021.
