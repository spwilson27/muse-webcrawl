Kochi (surname)

Kōchi (Japanese: 高知, 河内, 幸地, or other kanji combinations) is a Japanese surname. It is also the name of Kōchi Prefecture and its capital city on the island of Shikoku, and appears as a family name in several regions of Japan, including Okinawa, where the surname Chinen is sometimes written with characters read as Kōchi.

[Image: Map of Japan highlighting Kōchi Prefecture on the southern coast of Shikoku]

People with the surname

  • Kōchi Momoko (1932–1998), Japanese actress, known for her role in the original Godzilla (1954).
  • Various Japanese athletes, politicians, and artists bear the name in its different kanji spellings.

Related names and places

The surname should not be confused with the Indian city of Kochi (Cochin) in Kerala, nor with the Gujarati Kutchi people (also romanised "Kochi" in some sources), from whom the name of the Pashtun Kochi nomads of Afghanistan also derives. The Afghan Kochi are a distinct ethnic group of nomadic pastoralists, unrelated to the Japanese surname.
