Khorog

Country: Tajikistan
Region: Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region
Elevation: about 2,123 m

Khorog (Tajik: Хоруғ) is the capital of the Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region in southeastern Tajikistan. It lies in the Pamir Mountains at the confluence of the Gunt and Panj rivers, close to the border with Afghanistan.

Geography

The town sits in a narrow valley where the Shakhdara and Gunt join the Panj, which forms the frontier with Afghanistan. Its high elevation gives it a cold, dry mountain climate, moderated in the irrigated valley floor where poplars and fruit trees grow.

History

Khorog grew around a Russian military post established in the late 19th century during the contest for influence in Central Asia known as the Great Game. In the Soviet period it became the administrative and educational centre of the Pamirs, connected by the Pamir Highway to Dushanbe and to Osh in Kyrgyzstan.

Economy and culture

The town is a market and service centre for surrounding villages, with a campus of the University of Central Asia, a regional museum, botanical garden — one of the highest in the world — and a famous bazaar across the river in Afghanistan on market days. Most residents are Pamiri peoples, many of them Ismaili Muslims, speaking Shughni and other Pamiri languages alongside Tajik and Russian.
