Historic Environment Scotland

Historic Environment Scotland (HES; Scottish Gaelic: Àrainneachd Eachdraidheil Alba) is an executive non-departmental public body of the Scottish Government, responsible for investigating, caring for and promoting Scotland's historic environment. It was established in 2015 by the merger of Historic Scotland and the Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments of Scotland (RCAHMS).

Responsibilities

HES maintains more than 300 properties in state care, including Edinburgh Castle, Stirling Castle, Skara Brae, Fort George, and the Antonine Wall sites. It designates listed buildings and scheduled monuments, maintains the Canmore database of Scotland's built heritage and archives, and provides grants and advice on conservation. Under the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act and the Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014, it also leads on Scotland's historic environment strategy, Our Place in Time.

Collections and archives

The organisation holds the National Record of the Historic Environment, an archive of photographs, drawings, and aerial imagery built up over more than a century, much of it accessible online through Canmore and the Scran learning resource.
