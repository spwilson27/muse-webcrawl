Cream

Cream is a dairy product composed of the higher-fat layer skimmed from the top of milk before homogenisation. It may also be produced by centrifuges called separators.

Types

Types vary by fat content and country: half-and-half, light cream, whipping cream, heavy cream, double cream, clotted cream (from England, heated until clots form) and crème fraîche (lightly fermented). Cream is used in cooking, coffee, desserts and whipped cream.

Production

Un-homogenised milk left standing allows fat globules to rise by gravity; industrial production uses centrifugal separation and pasteurisation.
