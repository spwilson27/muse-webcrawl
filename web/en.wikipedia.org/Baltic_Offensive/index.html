Baltic Offensive

The Baltic Offensive, also known as the Baltic Strategic Offensive, was a campaign fought between the Soviet Red Army and German Army Group North in the Baltic states from September to November 1944, during the Second World War.

Course of the campaign

Following the success of Operation Bagration, Soviet forces of the Leningrad, 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Baltic Fronts attacked through Estonia and Latvia. Tallinn was captured on 22 September 1944, and Soviet troops reached the Baltic coast near Memel in October, cutting off Army Group North in the Courland Pocket.

Outcome

The offensive liberated Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania from German occupation and re-incorporated them into the Soviet Union, while some 200,000 German troops remained besieged in Courland until May 1945.
