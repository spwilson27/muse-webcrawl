Midian

Midian (Hebrew: Midyan) is a geographical place and a people mentioned in the Hebrew Bible and the Quran, located in northwestern Arabia, on the eastern shore of the Gulf of Aqaba on the Red Sea. The Midianites are described in Genesis as descendants of Abraham through his wife Keturah, by her son Midian.

In the biblical narrative, Moses fled to Midian after killing an Egyptian and married Zipporah, daughter of the Midianite priest Jethro (Reuel), before the Exodus. Relations between Israel and Midian later turned hostile: the Book of Numbers recounts a war against Midian, and in the Book of Judges the Midianites, raiding with camels, were defeated by Gideon. The Midianites are generally understood as a tribal confederation associated with the trade in frankincense and with "Midianite ware" pottery identified by archaeologists in the region of Qurayyah and the Timna Valley copper mines.
