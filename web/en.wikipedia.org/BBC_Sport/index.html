BBC Sport

BBC Sport is the sports division of the BBC, providing national sports coverage for BBC television, radio and online services. Based at MediaCityUK in Salford since 2012, it is one of the world's best-known sports broadcasters.

Coverage

BBC Sport holds free-to-air rights to a portfolio of major events, including the FA Cup, Wimbledon, the Olympic Games, the Six Nations Championship and highlights of Premier League football on Match of the Day, a programme broadcast since 1964. Its radio coverage centres on BBC Radio 5 Live, home of 606 and extensive live commentary.

History

The BBC has covered sport since its earliest years: it broadcast its first sports programme on radio in 1927 and televised Wimbledon and the FA Cup final in the late 1930s. Flagship television brands have included Grandstand (1958–2007) and Sportsnight. The BBC Sport website, launched in the late 1990s, is among the most visited sports news sites in the United Kingdom.
