Kudymkar

Country: Russia
Federal subject: Perm Krai
First mentioned: 1579
Town status: 1938

Kudymkar (Russian: Кудымкар; Komi-Permyak: Кудымкар) is a town in Perm Krai, Russia, located on the Inva River. Until 2005 it was the administrative centre of the Komi-Permyak Autonomous Okrug, after which the district was merged into Perm Krai as the Komi-Permyak Okrug.

History

Kudymkar was first mentioned in 1579. It developed as a local trade centre of the Komi-Permyak people and received town status in 1938. Its name derives from the Komi words for the legendary hero Kudym-Osh, a figure of Komi-Permyak epic tradition.

Demographics

The town's population was about 29,000 at the 2010 census. Ethnically it is mixed Russian and Komi-Permyak, and it remains the principal cultural centre of the Komi-Permyak people, with a drama theatre performing in the Komi-Permyak language.
