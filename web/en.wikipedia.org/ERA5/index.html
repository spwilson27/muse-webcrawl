ERA5 is the fifth generation of atmospheric reanalysis produced by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) on behalf of the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) of the European Union. Reanalysis combines a weather forecast model with historical observations to produce a consistent, gridded record of the atmosphere, oceans and land surface over many decades.

Data

ERA5 provides hourly estimates of many variables — temperature, wind, humidity, pressure, precipitation, soil moisture, ocean waves and more — on a global grid of about 31 km resolution with 137 vertical levels, spanning 1940 to the present. A back extension released later extended and improved the earliest decades. Data are distributed free through the Copernicus Climate Data Store.

Uses

ERA5 succeeded ERA-Interim and is widely used in climate research, for monitoring indicators such as global average temperature, in renewable-energy planning, agriculture, hydrology and insurance. Because it is updated continuously, ERA5 monthly summaries are frequently cited in reporting on record temperatures and extreme weather.
