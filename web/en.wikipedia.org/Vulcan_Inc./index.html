Vulcan Inc.

Vulcan Inc.
Founded: 1986
Founders: Paul Allen, Jody Allen
Headquarters: Seattle, Washington, U.S.
Type: Private holding / investment company

Vulcan Inc. was a private holding company based in Seattle, Washington, founded in 1986 by Microsoft co-founder Paul Allen and his sister Jody Allen. It managed Allen's business and philanthropic ventures across technology, real estate, the arts, sports and scientific research.

Activities

Vulcan's projects included the Allen Institute research organisations, the Stratolaunch space venture, major South Lake Union real-estate development in Seattle, and ownership interests in the Seattle Seahawks, Portland Trail Blazers and Seattle Sounders FC through related entities. It also funded ocean exploration and conservation, including the research vessel Petrel.

Winding down

Following Paul Allen's death in 2018, Vulcan progressively restructured and wound down many operations, spinning out or transferring its initiatives to other entities within the Allen estate and family office.
