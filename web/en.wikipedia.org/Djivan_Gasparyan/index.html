Djivan Gasparyan

Djivan Gasparyan (12 October 1928 – 6 July 2021) was an Armenian musician and composer, widely regarded as the master of the duduk, a traditional Armenian woodwind instrument. His playing brought the duduk to international attention through concerts, recordings, and film scores.

Career

Gasparyan began playing the duduk at the age of six and performed with the Yerevan Philharmonic and folk ensembles during the Soviet era. His 1988 album I Will Not Be Sad in This World, released on Brian Eno's Opal label, introduced Western audiences to the instrument.

Collaborations and film work

He collaborated with artists including Peter Gabriel, Michael Brook, Lionel Richie, and Hans Zimmer, and his duduk playing appeared in films such as The Last Temptation of Christ, Gladiator, and Blood Diamond. He was named a People's Artist of Armenia in 1973.
