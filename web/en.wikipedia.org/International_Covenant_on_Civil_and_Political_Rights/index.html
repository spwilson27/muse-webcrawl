International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights

The International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR) is a multilateral treaty that commits nations to respect the civil and political rights of individuals, including the right to life, freedom of religion, speech, assembly, electoral rights and rights to due process and a fair trial.

Rights protected

The Covenant covers self-determination, non-discrimination, freedom of movement and expression, minority rights, and prohibitions on torture, arbitrary detention and slavery.

Monitoring

States report to the Human Rights Committee; the First Optional Protocol allows individual complaints. The UN Human Rights Council and NGOs such as Amnesty International use it in assessments.
