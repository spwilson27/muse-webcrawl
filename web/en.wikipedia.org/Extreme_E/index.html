Extreme E

Extreme E races electric SUVs in remote, climate-affected locations.

Extreme E is an off-road racing series that uses electric SUVs to race in remote parts of the world affected by climate change, aiming to raise awareness of environmental issues. The series was founded by Alejandro Agag, who also founded Formula E, together with former racing driver Gil de Ferran, and its inaugural season took place in 2021.

Format

All teams use a spec electric SUV, the Odyssey 21, built by Spark Racing Technology with a battery supplied by Williams Advanced Engineering. Each team fields one male and one female driver who share driving duties, a format intended to promote gender equality in motorsport. Events, called X-Prix, are staged in locations such as the Saudi Arabian desert, Senegal, Greenland, Sardinia, and the Atacama Desert in Chile, each highlighting a different environmental theme such as desertification, rising sea levels, or deforestation.

Environmental programme

Equipment is transported between races by the ship St. Helena, a former cargo vessel refitted as the series' floating base, to reduce air freight emissions. The series runs "legacy programmes" with local partners and scientists at each venue and operates without spectators on site, relying on broadcast coverage to reduce its footprint.

Champions

The first championship in 2021 was won by Rosberg X Racing, the team founded by former Formula One champion Nico Rosberg, with drivers Johan Kristoffersson and Molly Taylor. Subsequent seasons featured teams backed by figures including Lewis Hamilton (X44) and McLaren, which entered in 2022.
