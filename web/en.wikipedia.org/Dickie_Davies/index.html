Richard Davies (30 April 1928 – 19 February 2023), known as Dickie Davies, was a British television sports presenter, best known as the anchor of ITV's World of Sport from 1968 until the programme ended in 1985.

Career

Davies began in broadcasting with the RAF and worked as a purser and announcer before joining Southern Television. At ITV he succeeded Eamonn Andrews as host of World of Sport, becoming a familiar Saturday-afternoon presence covering football, wrestling, and other sports. His distinctive grey-white streak of hair was widely recognised.

Later life

After World of Sport ended, Davies presented snooker and darts coverage and the quiz show Going for Gold's sporting segments, and worked in radio. He was referenced in popular culture, including the Half Man Half Biscuit song "Dickie Davies Eyes".
