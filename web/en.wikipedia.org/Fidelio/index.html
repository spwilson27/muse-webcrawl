Fidelio

Fidelio, Op. 72, is the only opera by Ludwig van Beethoven. Its libretto, by Joseph Sonnleithner and later revised by Stephan von Breuning and Georg Friedrich Treitschke, is based on Jean-Nicolas Bouilly's Léonore, ou L'amour conjugal.

The opera tells of Leonore, who disguises herself as a prison guard named Fidelio to rescue her husband Florestan, a political prisoner held by Don Pizarro. It premiered in Vienna in 1805, was revised in 1806, and reached its final form in 1814.

Music

Famous numbers include the Prisoners' Chorus, Leonore's aria "Abscheulicher!", and Florestan's aria in Act 2. Beethoven wrote four overtures for it; the Fidelio Overture is now standard, while Leonore Overtures Nos. 2 and 3 are often played as concert pieces.
