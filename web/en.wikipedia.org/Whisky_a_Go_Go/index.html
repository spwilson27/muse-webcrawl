Whisky a Go Go

Whisky a Go Go is a nightclub in West Hollywood, California, on the Sunset Strip. Opened in 1964, it became one of the best-known American rock venues and is associated with the rise of go-go dancing, the Doors, and the Los Angeles music scene.

History

The club adapted the name and discotheque concept from earlier Whisky à Go-Go venues, adding live bands and dancers in elevated cages. The Doors served as a house band in 1966, and many major rock, punk and metal acts later played there. Its location at Sunset Boulevard and Clark Street made it a landmark of youth culture.

Legacy

The Whisky a Go Go was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a historic venue. It remains a working club and a symbol of the Sunset Strip's role in popular music.
