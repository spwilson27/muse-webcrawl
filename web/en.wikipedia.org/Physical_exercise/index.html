Physical Exercise

Physical exercise is any bodily activity that enhances or maintains physical fitness and overall health and wellness. It includes aerobic exercise (such as running, cycling, and swimming), anaerobic exercise (such as weight training and sprinting), and flexibility and balance training.

Health effects

Regular exercise strengthens the cardiovascular and musculoskeletal systems, helps control body weight, and reduces the risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, some cancers, and depression. Health authorities commonly recommend that adults obtain at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, plus muscle-strengthening activities.

Classification

Aerobic exercise relies on oxidative metabolism and raises heart and breathing rates over sustained periods. Anaerobic exercise involves short, intense efforts fueled without oxygen and builds strength and muscle mass. Flexibility exercises maintain joint range of motion. Exercise also produces acute benefits in mood and cognition, and excess or poorly managed training can cause injury or overtraining.
