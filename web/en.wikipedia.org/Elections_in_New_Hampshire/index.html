Elections in New Hampshire

Elections in New Hampshire are held for federal, state, and local offices. New Hampshire is known nationally for holding the first primary election in the United States presidential nomination process, the New Hampshire presidential primary, traditionally in January or February of the election year.

State offices

The Governor and the five-member Executive Council are elected every two years — New Hampshire and Vermont are the only states with two-year gubernatorial terms. The state legislature, the General Court, consists of a 400-member House of Representatives, the largest state lower house in the country, and a 24-member Senate, both elected biennially.

New Hampshire elects two members to the U.S. House of Representatives and two U.S. senators. General elections are held on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November.
