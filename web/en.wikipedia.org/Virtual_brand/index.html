Virtual brand

A virtual brand (also called a virtual restaurant brand or online-only brand) is a food-service concept that exists only on online food delivery platforms, with no physical storefront, dining room or signage of its own. Virtual brands are prepared in the kitchens of existing restaurants, or in "ghost kitchens" (also called dark kitchens or cloud kitchens) built solely for delivery.

Model

The model grew rapidly with the rise of third-party delivery apps in the 2010s and accelerated during the COVID-19 pandemic. A single kitchen can operate several virtual brands at once — for example, a burger brand, a wings brand and a dessert brand — each with its own menu, name and listing on delivery apps, allowing operators to use spare kitchen capacity and test concepts with little investment. Celebrities and restaurant chains have launched virtual brands, such as MrBeast Burger (2020), created by the YouTuber MrBeast with Virtual Dining Concepts.

Criticism

Critics note that customers may not realise several differently named "restaurants" share one kitchen, and regulators in some jurisdictions have pushed delivery platforms for clearer labelling. Quality control and brand confusion are recurring concerns, and many virtual brands launched during the pandemic closed as dining patterns normalised.
