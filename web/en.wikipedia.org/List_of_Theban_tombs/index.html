List of Theban tombs

The Theban Necropolis on the west bank of the Nile opposite modern Luxor contains hundreds of tombs of nobles, officials, and artisans of the ancient Egyptian New Kingdom and later periods. Tombs are numbered in the "TT" (Theban Tomb) series roughly in the order of their discovery, and are grouped in areas including Sheikh Abd el-Qurna, el-Assasif, el-Khokha, Dra Abu el-Naga, Deir el-Medina, and Qurnet Murai. The royal tombs of the Valley of the Kings (KV) and Valley of the Queens (QV) are numbered separately.

Numbering and study

The TT catalogue was systematized by Egyptologists including Alan Gardiner and Arthur Weigall; Porter and Moss's Topographical Bibliography remains the standard reference. More than 400 tombs are catalogued. Many were reused, looted, or inhabited in later periods; conservation projects continue to document their painted decoration, which is a primary source for New Kingdom daily life, religion, and administration.
