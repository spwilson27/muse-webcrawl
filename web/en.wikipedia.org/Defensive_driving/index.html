Defensive driving

Defensive driving is a set of driving skills and techniques that aim to reduce the risk of collision by anticipating dangerous situations, adverse conditions, and the mistakes of other road users, beyond mere compliance with traffic rules and basic vehicle control.

Principles

Defensive driving emphasises maintaining safe following distances, scanning the road ahead and checking mirrors regularly, avoiding distractions and impairment, adjusting speed for weather and visibility, and always planning an escape route. Well-known teaching frameworks include the Smith System (aim high in steering, get the big picture, keep your eyes moving, leave yourself an out, make sure they see you) and the UK-based Institute of Advanced Motorists' system of car control.

Training and benefits

Defensive driving courses are offered by safety councils, employers, and motoring organisations. Many insurers grant premium discounts to drivers who complete them, and courts in some jurisdictions allow them for traffic-ticket dismissal or point reduction. Fleet operators commonly require such training to reduce crash costs.
