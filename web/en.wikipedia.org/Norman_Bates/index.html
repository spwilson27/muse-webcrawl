Norman Bates

Norman Bates is a fictional character created by American author Robert Bloch as the main antagonist in his 1959 horror novel Psycho. He was portrayed by Anthony Perkins in Alfred Hitchcock's 1960 film adaptation and its sequels, and by Freddie Highmore in the television series Bates Motel (2013-2017).

The Bates Motel and house

Character

Bates is a shy, socially awkward young man who runs a remote motel and lives with the preserved corpse of his domineering mother, Norma, whom he murdered years earlier along with her lover. Traumatized by her emotional abuse, he develops a split personality: when attracted to a woman, his "Mother" personality takes over and kills in jealousy. His most famous victim is Marion Crane, murdered in the film's celebrated shower scene.

Inspiration and legacy

Bloch based aspects of Bates on the real-life murderer Ed Gein, who lived near Bloch's Wisconsin home. Norman Bates is widely ranked among cinema's greatest villains; Perkins's performance defined the character across four films, while Bates Motel reimagined his adolescence as a psychological drama.
