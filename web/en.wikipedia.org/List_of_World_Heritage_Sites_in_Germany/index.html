List of World Heritage Sites in Germany

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) designates World Heritage Sites of outstanding universal value. Germany has 54 sites inscribed on the list, among the most of any country. The first, Aachen Cathedral, was inscribed in 1978.
