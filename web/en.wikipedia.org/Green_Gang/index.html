The Green Gang (Chinese: 青幫; pinyin: Qīng Bāng) was a Chinese secret society and criminal organization that dominated organized crime and much of the underworld in Shanghai during the first half of the 20th century. Originating among the boatmen and laborers of the Grand Canal grain-tribute transport system, it evolved into a powerful syndicate controlling opium distribution, gambling, and dock labor.

Rise under Du Yuesheng

Under the leadership of Du Yuesheng ("Big-Eared Du"), together with Huang Jinrong and Zhang Xiaolin, the Green Gang formed the "Three Tyrants" of Shanghai. It cooperated with the French Concession authorities and, in 1927, supplied the enforcers for Chiang Kai-shek's Shanghai massacre of Communists and trade unionists, after which it received official favor and control of the opium trade monopoly.

Decline

The gang's power waned after the Second Sino-Japanese War and collapsed with the Communist victory in 1949; Du fled to Hong Kong, and the organization was suppressed in mainland China, though remnants persisted in Taiwan and overseas Chinese communities.
