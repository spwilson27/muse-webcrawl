Ma Baker (song)

"Ma Baker"
Artist: Boney M.
Released: 1977
Album: Love for Sale
Genre: Disco

"Ma Baker" is a song by the disco group Boney M., released as a single in 1977 and appearing on their second album, Love for Sale. It was the group's first single to reach number one in Germany and charted across Europe.

Background

The song tells the story of the legendary 1930s outlaw Kate "Ma" Barker, reworking the folk song "Ma Baker". It features a driving bass line and the spoken refrain delivered by producer Frank Farian and singer Liz Mitchell.

Legacy

"Ma Baker" has been widely covered and sampled, and remains one of Boney M.'s signature songs.
