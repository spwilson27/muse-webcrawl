The GP2 Asia Series was a form of open-wheel motor racing based in Asia, organised by the GP2 Series management as a winter offshoot of the main European GP2 Series. It ran from 2008 to 2011, racing mainly at circuits in the Middle East and East Asia during the European winter, and used the same Dallara chassis as the main series with a slightly detuned engine specification in its first seasons.

History

The championship was announced in 2007 and began in January 2008 with rounds supporting events such as the Speedcar Series and touring-car meetings in Dubai, Indonesia, Malaysia, China and Bahrain. The inaugural season featured many drivers from the main GP2 grid alongside Asian talent. Romain Grosjean won the first title for ART Grand Prix.

The 2008–09 season included Kamui Kobayashi's championship for DAMS. The 2009–10 season was shortened, and Davide Valsecchi took the title for iSport International. In 2011 the series staged a final abbreviated season — including a round at Imola in Italy, outside Asia — won again by Grosjean, after which the Asia Series was discontinued and merged conceptually into the main GP2 calendar, which increasingly visited Asian venues such as Sepang, Marina Bay and Yas Marina.
