Blood sausage
Main ingredients: Blood, filler (oatmeal, barley, suet)
Variants: Black pudding, morcilla, Blutwurst, boudin noir

A blood sausage is a sausage made by cooking blood with a filler until thick enough to congeal when cooled. Variants include black pudding in Britain and Ireland, morcilla in Spain, Blutwurst in Germany and boudin noir in France.

Preparation

Pig's blood is most common, mixed with oatmeal, barley, rice or breadcrumbs and seasonings, then stuffed into casings and cooked. Fillers and spices vary widely by region.

Cultural notes

Blood sausage is among the oldest forms of sausage, mentioned in Homer's Odyssey. It remains a staple of full breakfasts and of traditional slaughter festivals across Europe and beyond.
