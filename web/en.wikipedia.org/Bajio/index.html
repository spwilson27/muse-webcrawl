Bajio

Bajio may refer to the Bajío, a region of west-central Mexico comprising portions of the states of Guanajuato, Querétaro, Aguascalientes, Jalisco and Michoacán, noted for its agriculture, colonial cities, and later industrial growth.

The name comes from the Spanish bajío, meaning a low-lying area, in reference to the region's position as a fertile plateau lowland between surrounding mountain ranges.
