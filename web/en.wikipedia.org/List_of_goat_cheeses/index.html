List of goat cheeses

Goat cheese, or fromage de chèvre in French, is cheese made from goat's milk. Goat milk cheeses range from soft fresh cheeses to aged hard varieties; they are characterised by a distinctive tangy flavour arising from capric, caprylic, and caproic fatty acids. France is especially noted for its goat cheeses, many of which hold protected designation of origin (AOC/PDO) status.

Characteristics

Goat milk forms smaller curds than cow's milk and its cheeses are naturally white, since goats convert carotene to vitamin A. Fresh goat cheese is soft, spreadable, and tangy; aged versions develop firm textures and more intense, sometimes goaty, flavours. Goat cheeses pair classically with white wines such as Sauvignon blanc, notably Sancerre with Crottin de Chavignol.
