Leonardo DiCaprio

Leonardo Wilhelm DiCaprio (born November 11, 1974) is an American actor and film producer. Known for his work in biographical and period films, he has received numerous accolades, including an Academy Award and three Golden Globe Awards, and his films have grossed over $7 billion worldwide.

Born: November 11, 1974, Los Angeles, California
Occupation: Actor, producer
Years active: 1989–present
Awards: Academy Award for Best Actor (2015)

Early life

DiCaprio was born in Los Angeles, the only child of Irmelin Indenbirken, a legal secretary, and George DiCaprio, an underground comix artist and distributor. He began his career in television commercials and sitcoms such as Growing Pains.

Career

He gained critical praise for What's Eating Gilbert Grape (1993), earning his first Oscar nomination, and achieved international stardom with Romeo + Juliet (1996) and James Cameron's Titanic (1997), then the highest-grossing film of all time. Seeking serious roles, he became a frequent collaborator of Martin Scorsese, in Gangs of New York, The Aviator, The Departed, Shutter Island, The Wolf of Wall Street and Killers of the Flower Moon. Other major films include Catch Me If You Can, Inception, Django Unchained and Once Upon a Time in Hollywood.

The Revenant and activism

DiCaprio won the Academy Award for Best Actor for playing frontiersman Hugh Glass in The Revenant (2015). A committed environmentalist, he founded the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation in 1998, serves as a United Nations Messenger of Peace for climate change, and has produced documentaries such as Before the Flood.
