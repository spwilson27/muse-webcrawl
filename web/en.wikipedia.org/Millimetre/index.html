Millimetre

Unit of: Length

Symbol: mm

In SI base units: 10−3 m

Imperial equivalent: ≈ 0.03937 in

The millimetre (international spelling; American spelling millimeter) is a unit of length in the International System of Units (SI), equal to one thousandth of a metre, the SI base unit of length. Its symbol is mm.

Definition and usage

Since 1983 the metre has been defined in terms of the speed of light, so the millimetre is equivalently the distance light travels in vacuum in a tiny fraction of a second. There are ten millimetres in a centimetre and one thousand millimetres in a metre. Millimetres are the standard unit for engineering drawings, rainfall measurement, and medical imaging dimensions, and are used on rulers alongside centimetres.

Related units

Smaller metric units include the micrometre (µm, one thousandth of a millimetre) and the nanometre. Electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths around a millimetre is called millimetre-wave radiation, used in radio astronomy, security scanners, and high-bandwidth communications.
