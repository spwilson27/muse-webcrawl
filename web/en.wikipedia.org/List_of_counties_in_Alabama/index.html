List of counties in Alabama

The U.S. state of Alabama has 67 counties. Each county serves as a local level of government within the state, administered by an elected county commission. The newest county is Bullock County, created in 1866; the oldest is Washington County, created in 1800.

History

Alabama's counties were formed from Mississippi Territory lands and later subdivided as settlement expanded. County names honour prominent individuals, Native American tribes, and geographic features.
