Bombardier Global 7500

Type: Ultra long-range business jet

Manufacturer: Bombardier Aviation

First flight: 4 November 2016

Introduction: 2018

Range: 7,700 nmi (14,260 km)

The Bombardier Global 7500 is an ultra long-range business jet developed by the Canadian manufacturer Bombardier Aviation. It is a stretched and extensively redesigned derivative of the earlier Global 6000, featuring an additional cabin zone, a new wing, and new engines. On entering service in 2018 it became the largest business jet in its class, with the longest range of any purpose-built business aircraft then in production.

Development

The program was launched in 2010 as the Global 7000, promising a four-zone cabin and a range sufficient to connect city pairs such as New York and Hong Kong non-stop. The aircraft made its first flight on 4 November 2016 from Bombardier's Toronto facility. Following delays caused in part by a wing redesign, the type received Transport Canada certification in September 2018 and entered service before the end of that year. It was rebranded as the Global 7500 after flight testing showed its range had grown to 7,700 nautical miles.

Design

The Global 7500 is powered by two General Electric Passport turbofan engines, each producing about 18,920 pounds of thrust. Its maximum speed is Mach 0.925, with a typical cruise of Mach 0.85. The aircraft introduced the Nuage seat, a new cabin seating design, and the Bombardier Vision flight deck based on Rockwell Collins Pro Line Fusion avionics. The cabin is divided into four living zones including a crew rest area and a full-size kitchen.

Variants and derivatives

The Global 7500 forms the basis of the shorter Global 6500 and the stretched flagship Global 8000, announced in 2022, which extends range to 8,000 nautical miles. Bombardier has also marketed a missionized version for military roles.
