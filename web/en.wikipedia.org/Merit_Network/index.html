Merit Network

Merit Network, Inc.
Founded: 1966 (as Michigan Educational Research Information Triad)
Headquarters: Ann Arbor, Michigan
Type: Nonprofit research and education network

Merit Network, Inc. is a nonprofit organisation based in Ann Arbor, Michigan, that operates a regional research and education computer network serving universities, schools, libraries and public institutions across Michigan. Founded in 1966 as the Michigan Educational Research Information Triad by the University of Michigan, Michigan State University and Wayne State University, it is the longest-running regional network in the United States.

Early history

Merit's original network linked the mainframe computers of its three founding universities beginning in 1971, developing some of the earliest packet-switching and dial-in technology in academic computing. Its dial-in services later supported remote access for users across the state.

NSFNET

In 1987 Merit, together with IBM and MCI, won the National Science Foundation award to build and manage the NSFNET backbone, the network that linked supercomputer centres and regional networks and became a principal precursor of the modern Internet. Merit managed NSFNET operations from 1988 until the backbone was retired in 1995 as the Internet was commercialised, and it helped develop routing, network-management and routing-arbiter services that shaped early Internet operations.

Today

Merit operates a statewide fibre-optic network, provides cybersecurity services, professional development and community programs, and supports Michigan's education and public-sector institutions.
