The Adventures of Rocky and Bullwinkle and Friends

The Adventures of Rocky and Bullwinkle and Friends is an American animated television series that originally aired from November 19, 1959, to June 27, 1964, on the ABC and NBC television networks. Produced by Jay Ward Productions, it is remembered for its satirical humour, Cold War parody and appeal to both children and adults.

Format

The show starred Rocket "Rocky" J. Squirrel, a flying squirrel, and Bullwinkle J. Moose, a dim but good-natured moose, who lived in Frostbite Falls, Minnesota. Their adventures pitted them against the Pottsylvanian spies Boris Badenov and Natasha Fatale, working for the dictator Fearless Leader. Serialised "Rocky and Bullwinkle" cliffhanger episodes were interspersed with supporting segments: "Dudley Do-Right of the Mounties", "Peabody's Improbable History" with Mr. Peabody and Sherman, "Fractured Fairy Tales" narrated by Edward Everett Horton, and "Aesop and Son".

Voices and production

June Foray voiced Rocky and Natasha, Bill Scott voiced Bullwinkle and Mr. Peabody, Paul Frees voiced Boris, and William Conrad narrated. Animation was outsourced to Gamma Productions in Mexico, giving the show a limited-animation look that its witty writing overcame.

Legacy

The series pioneered satirical animation for prime-time family audiences and influenced later shows from The Simpsons onwards. It spawned the 2000 live-action/animated film The Adventures of Rocky and Bullwinkle and a 2018 Amazon streaming revival.
