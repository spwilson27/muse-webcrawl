God Help the Girl

God Help the Girl is a 2014 British musical romantic drama film written and directed by Stuart Murdoch, lead singer of the indie pop band Belle and Sebastian. It stars Emily Browning, Olly Alexander, and Hannah Murray. The film premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival, where it won a special jury prize for its ensemble cast.

Plot

Eve, a young woman being treated for an eating disorder in a Glasgow hospital, escapes into songwriting. She meets James, a shy musician working as a lifeguard, and his student Cassie; over one summer the three form a band, and Eve must choose between her musical ambitions and a possible path to recovery, as the friendship is complicated by romance.

Background

The project began as a 2009 album of the same name, for which Murdoch recruited female vocalists through open auditions to sing songs he had written from a young woman's perspective. The film, partly financed through Kickstarter, extends that concept into a feature-length musical, with songs performed by the cast alongside members of Belle and Sebastian.
