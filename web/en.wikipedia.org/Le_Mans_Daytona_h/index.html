Le Mans Daytona h

The Le Mans Daytona h (LMDh) is a sports prototype category for endurance racing, jointly developed by IMSA, the Automobile Club de l'Ouest (ACO) and the FIA. Cars built to LMDh regulations compete in the GTP class of the IMSA SportsCar Championship and, alongside Le Mans Hypercars, in the Hypercar class of the FIA World Endurance Championship, including the 24 Hours of Le Mans and the 24 Hours of Daytona.

LMDh cars are based on a standardised carbon-fibre chassis supplied by one of four constructors — Dallara, Ligier, Multimatic or Oreca — fitted with a spec hybrid system on the rear axle, a common gearbox, and manufacturer-specific internal combustion engines and bodywork styling. Manufacturers that have fielded LMDh cars include Acura, Alpine, BMW, Cadillac, Lamborghini and Porsche.

The category debuted at the 2023 24 Hours of Daytona, and convergence with the Hypercar regulations allows LMDh and LMH cars to race together under Balance of Performance adjustments.
