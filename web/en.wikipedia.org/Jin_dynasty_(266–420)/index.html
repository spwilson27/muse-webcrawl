Jin dynasty (266–420)

The Jin dynasty (266–420) was a Chinese imperial dynasty founded by Sima Yan, who completed the reunification of China in 280 by conquering Eastern Wu, ending the Three Kingdoms period.

After the War of the Eight Princes and the uprisings of the Five Barbarians, the dynasty lost the north; from 317 the Eastern Jin ruled from Jiankang (Nanjing) south of the Yangtze, alongside the Sixteen Kingdoms in the north. The Eastern Jin fell in 420 when Liu Yu founded the Liu Song dynasty.
