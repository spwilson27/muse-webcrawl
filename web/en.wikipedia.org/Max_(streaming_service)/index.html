Max (streaming service)

Max, formerly known as HBO Max, is an American subscription video-on-demand streaming service owned by Warner Bros. Discovery. It launched as HBO Max on 27 May 2020, built around the HBO premium library, and was relaunched as Max on 23 May 2023 after the WarnerMedia–Discovery merger, adding Discovery's reality and lifestyle catalogue from channels such as HGTV, Food Network, and Discovery Channel. In 2025 the service announced a return to the HBO Max name.

Content

The service offers HBO originals such as Succession, House of the Dragon, and The Last of Us, Warner Bros. films, DC and Cartoon Network libraries, and Max Originals. In 2021, Warner Bros. released its entire film slate day-and-date on the service in the United States under "Project Popcorn".

History

HBO Max consolidated earlier services HBO Go and HBO Now. The 2023 rebrand to Max broadened the catalogue and pricing tiers; international expansion followed across Latin America and Europe.
