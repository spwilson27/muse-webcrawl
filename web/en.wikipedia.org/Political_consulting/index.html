Political consulting

Political consulting is a form of consulting that consists primarily of advising and assisting political campaigns. Services include campaign strategy, media production and buying, polling, opposition research, fundraising, voter targeting and get-out-the-vote operations.

The profession developed in the United States in the 20th century with firms such as Whitaker and Baxter in California, and expanded with television advertising and later digital and social media microtargeting. Consultants may work for candidates, parties, ballot initiatives or advocacy groups.

Criticism

Critics argue consulting can increase negativity, raise campaign costs, and shift focus from policy to messaging.
