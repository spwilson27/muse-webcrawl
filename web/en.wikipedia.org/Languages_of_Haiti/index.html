Languages of Haiti

The languages of Haiti are Haitian Creole and French, both official since the 1987 constitution. Haitian Creole, a French-based creole, is spoken by virtually all Haitians, while French is spoken fluently by a minority and has historically dominated government, education and formal affairs.

Haitian Creole

Creole developed during the colonial period on the sugar plantations of Saint-Domingue. It has a largely French-derived vocabulary with influences from West African languages, Spanish and Taíno, and a standardised orthography was adopted in the late 20th century.

Education and society

The imbalance between Creole and French in schooling has long been debated; reforms have promoted Creole-medium instruction in early grades.
