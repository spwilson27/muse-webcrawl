Alejandro Agag Longo (born 18 September 1970) is a Spanish businessman and former politician, best known as the founder and chairman of Formula E, the all-electric single-seater racing championship, and of the off-road electric series Extreme E.

Early life and politics

Agag was born in Madrid, the son of a banker of Algerian descent. He studied economics and entered politics with the People's Party (Partido Popular), becoming a close aide to Prime Minister José María Aznar and, in 1999, a Member of the European Parliament — at the time its youngest Spanish member. He left politics in 2002 to pursue business.

Motorsport business

Agag acquired the television rights to Formula One in Spain and became involved in GP2 Series team ownership (Campos/Addax) and sponsorship. In 2012 he founded Formula E Holdings; the FIA Formula E Championship held its first race in Beijing in September 2014, promoting electric mobility through city-centre street racing. He served as the series' CEO before becoming chairman. In 2021 he launched Extreme E, racing electric SUVs in remote locations affected by climate change, and later the E1 Series of electric powerboat racing.
