Mushing

Mushing is a sport or transport method powered by dogs. It involves a dog team pulling a sled on snow or a rig on dry land, driven by a person called a musher — a term derived from the French command marche! The practice developed among Indigenous peoples of the Arctic and became vital during the Alaskan gold rushes.

Equipment and teams

A team is arranged in pairs along a gangline, led by lead dogs, followed by swing dogs, team dogs and powerful wheel dogs nearest the sled. Mushers stand on the sled runners, steering by voice commands such as "gee" (right) and "haw" (left), and brake with a claw brake or drag mat.

Racing

The best-known races are Alaska's Iditarod Trail Sled Dog Race (about 1,000 miles from Anchorage to Nome) and the Yukon Quest. Sprint and mid-distance racing, skijoring and dryland events are also popular. Dog welfare standards and race rules govern rest, nutrition and veterinary checks.
