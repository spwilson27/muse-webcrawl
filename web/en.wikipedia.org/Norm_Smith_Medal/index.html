Norm Smith Medal

The Norm Smith Medal is an Australian rules football award presented annually to the player adjudged best on ground in the AFL Grand Final. It was first presented in 1979 and is named in honour of Norm Smith, a former Melbourne Football Club player and coach regarded as one of the game's greatest coaches.

Voting

A panel of five voters each awards 3, 2 and 1 votes to the players they rate best, for a maximum of 15 votes. Winners from losing teams are rare; Maurice Rioli (1982), Gary Ablett Sr. (1989), Nathan Buckley (2002) and Chris Judd (2005) are notable exceptions. Dustin Martin is the only player to win the medal three times (2017, 2019, 2020); four players have won it twice, including Andrew McLeod and Luke Hodge.

History

Norm Smith coached Melbourne to six premierships between 1955 and 1964 and was named coach of the AFL's Team of the Century. The medal's first winner was Wayne Harmes of Carlton in 1979.
