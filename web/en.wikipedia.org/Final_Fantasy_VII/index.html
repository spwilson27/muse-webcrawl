Final Fantasy VII

Final Fantasy VII
Developer: Square
Publisher: Square / Sony Computer Entertainment
Platform: PlayStation, others
Released: 1997
Genre: Role-playing game

Final Fantasy VII is a 1997 role-playing video game developed and published by Square for the PlayStation. It is the seventh main installment in the Final Fantasy series and the first to use 3D computer graphics and full-motion video. The game follows Cloud Strife, a mercenary who joins the eco-terrorist group AVALANCHE to fight the megacorporation Shinra, which is draining the planet's life energy as a power source.

Gameplay

Players explore towns, fields and dungeons, engage in turn-based battles using the Active Time Battle system, and customise characters with Materia — crystallised Mako energy that grants magic and abilities when slotted into equipment.

Plot

Cloud joins AVALANCHE in the city of Midgar. The conflict escalates into a quest to stop Sephiroth, a legendary former SOLDIER who plans to summon Meteor and wound the planet so he can merge with its Lifestream. Key locations include the Gold Saucer, Cosmo Canyon, Nibelheim and the Northern Crater.

Development and legacy

Directed by Yoshinori Kitase, with character designs by Tetsuya Nomura and music by Nobuo Uematsu, the game was a landmark in popularising Japanese RPGs worldwide. It sold over 14 million copies across releases and spawned the Compilation of Final Fantasy VII. A multi-part remake project began with Final Fantasy VII Remake (2020), followed by Rebirth (2024).
