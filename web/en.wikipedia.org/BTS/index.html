BTS

BTS (Korean: 방탄소년단; Bangtan Sonyeondan, "Bulletproof Boy Scouts"), also known as the Bangtan Boys, is a South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment. The seven-member group — RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jungkook — debuted in 2013 and became one of the best-selling music acts in the world.

History

BTS began with hip-hop-influenced albums before achieving international breakthrough with the Wings era and the Love Yourself series. In 2020 their English-language single "Dynamite" topped the Billboard Hot 100, followed by "Butter" and "Permission to Dance". They addressed the United Nations General Assembly and met world leaders as cultural envoys of South Korea.

Artistry and themes

The group's lyrics frequently address youth, mental health, self-acceptance and social pressure. Members co-write and produce much of their material, and their fan community, ARMY, is noted for its scale and organisation on social media.

Hiatus and military service

From 2022 the members pursued solo projects while completing mandatory South Korean military service, with group activities planned to resume after their discharges. See also K-pop and Hybe Corporation.
