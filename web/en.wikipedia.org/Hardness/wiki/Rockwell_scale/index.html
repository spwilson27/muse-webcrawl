Rockwell scale

Rockwell hardness test
Inventors: Hugh M. & Stanley P. Rockwell, patented 1914–1919
Scales: HRA, HRB, HRC etc.
Measurement: Depth of penetration

The Rockwell scale is a hardness scale based on indentation hardness of a material. The Rockwell test measures the depth of penetration of an indenter under a large load (major load) compared to the penetration made by a preload (minor load).

Scales

Different scales use different indenters and loads: HRC uses a diamond cone and 150 kgf load for hardened steels; HRB uses a 1/16-inch ball and 100 kgf for softer metals such as aluminium and brass. Results are read directly from a dial or display, making the test fast and popular in industry.

Advantages and limits

Quick, small indentation, operator-independent reading; requires flat, adequately thick samples and correct scale choice.
