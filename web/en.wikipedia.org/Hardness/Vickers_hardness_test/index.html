Vickers hardness test

Vickers hardness test
Developed: 1921, Robert L. Smith & George E. Sandland at Vickers Ltd
Indenter: Diamond square pyramid, 136°
Scale: HV

The Vickers hardness test was developed in 1921 as an alternative to the Brinell method for measuring the hardness of materials. It uses a diamond indenter in the form of a square-based pyramid with a face angle of 136 degrees, pressed into the surface under a known load; the size of the resulting indentation is measured optically.

Formula

HV is calculated from load divided by the surface area of the indentation, approximately HV = 1.8544 × F / d², where F is load in kgf and d is the mean diagonal in mm.

Advantages

One scale covers soft to very hard materials, indentations are small, and the test suits thin sheets and microhardness testing with low loads.
