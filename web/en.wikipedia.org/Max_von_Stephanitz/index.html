Max von Stephanitz

Max Emil Friedrich von Stephanitz (30 December 1864 – 22 April 1936) was a German cavalry officer and dog breeder credited with developing the German Shepherd Dog breed, which he standardized in 1899.

Founding the breed

Stephanitz believed dogs should be bred primarily for working ability and intelligence, summarised in his motto "utility and intelligence." In 1899 he bought a herding dog, Hektor Linksrhein, renamed him Horand von Grafrath, and made him the first registered German Shepherd. The same year he founded the Verein für Deutsche Schäferhunde (S.V.), the breed's governing club.

Legacy

His breeding programme emphasised a standard type, temperament testing, and service roles in police, military, and guide work, shaping the modern German Shepherd.
