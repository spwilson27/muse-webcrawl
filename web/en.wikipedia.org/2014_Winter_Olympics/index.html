The 2014 Winter Olympics, officially the XXII Olympic Winter Games and commonly known as Sochi 2014, were held from 7 to 23 February 2014 in Sochi, Russia. They were the first Olympic Games hosted by the Russian Federation since the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and the first Winter Olympics held in a subtropical climate city.

Sochi was selected as host city in July 2007 over Salzburg and Pyeongchang. Events were split between the Coastal Cluster around the Olympic Park in Adler and the Mountain Cluster at Krasnaya Polyana. Twelve new events were added, including women's ski jumping and slopestyle competitions.

Cost and legacy

The Games became the most expensive Olympics in history, with reported costs exceeding US$50 billion, driven by extensive infrastructure construction, including a new road and rail link to the mountain venues. The event was also marred by concerns over corruption, Russia's legislation affecting LGBT people, and, after the Games, by the annexation of Crimea, which began during the Paralympics period.

Medal table

Russia originally topped the medal table, but a state-sponsored doping programme, documented by the McLaren Report, led to numerous Russian results being annulled by the IOC. Norway finished with 11 gold medals, Canada 10, and the United States won the most medals overall among unaffected nations.
