Dark Sun

TypeCampaign setting for Dungeons & Dragons
PublisherTSR (1991); Wizards of the Coast (4th edition, 2010)
DesignersTimothy Brown and Troy Denning
WorldAthas
GenresDark fantasy, post-apocalyptic fantasy, sword-and-planet

Dark Sun is a campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons role-playing game, first released by TSR in 1991. It is set on the desert world of Athas, a once-verdant planet devastated by uncontrolled "defiling" magic and ruled by tyrannical sorcerer-kings from fortified city-states. The setting is noted for its harsh survival themes, prominent psionics, scarcity of metal, and striking artwork by Gerald Brom.

The world of Athas

Under a dark red sun, Athas is a world of sand seas, silt flats, and walled cities where water and steel are precious. Traditional fantasy elements are twisted: halflings are feral cannibals, elves are nomadic desert raiders, and characters races include the insectoid thri-kreen and half-giant goliaths. Clerics worship elemental powers rather than gods, who are absent or dead, and arcane magic drains life from the land itself unless carefully "preserved."

Publication history

The original boxed set was designed by Timothy Brown and Troy Denning, accompanied by Denning's Prism Pentad novel series. A revised and expanded edition followed in 1995, and Wizards of the Coast revived the setting for the game's 4th edition in 2010.
