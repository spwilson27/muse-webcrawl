FIFA

Fédération Internationale de Football Association
Founded: 21 May 1904, Paris
Headquarters: Zurich, Switzerland
Membership: 211 national associations
President: Gianni Infantino (since 2016)
Motto: "For the Game. For the World."

The Fédération Internationale de Football Association, known by its acronym FIFA, is the international governing body of association football, futsal and beach soccer. Founded in 1904 to oversee international competition among a small group of European national associations, it has grown to 211 member associations — more than the United Nations — organised into six continental confederations.

History

FIFA was founded in Paris on 21 May 1904 by representatives of Belgium, Denmark, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden and Switzerland. Its first president was Robert Guérin. The association's growth accelerated with the staging of the first FIFA World Cup in Uruguay in 1930. Under presidents Jules Rimet, João Havelange and Sepp Blatter, FIFA expanded commercially and extended its flagship tournament to 32 teams (and 48 from 2026).

Competitions

FIFA organises the men's and women's World Cups, youth tournaments including the U-20 and U-17 World Cups, the Futsal World Cup, the Beach Soccer World Cup and the Club World Cup. The senior men's World Cup, held every four years since 1930 (except 1942 and 1946), is among the most watched sporting events on Earth.

Governance and criticism

FIFA is governed by its Congress, in which each member association has one vote, and an executive body now called the FIFA Council. The organisation has faced repeated allegations of corruption, culminating in the 2015 scandal in which United States and Swiss authorities indicted numerous officials; president Sepp Blatter resigned days after re-election and was later banned. Reforms followed, including term limits and salary disclosure. The awarding of World Cups to Russia (2018) and Qatar (2022) also drew sustained scrutiny over bidding and human rights.

Confederations

  • AFC (Asia), CAF (Africa), CONCACAF (North and Central America), CONMEBOL (South America), OFC (Oceania), UEFA (Europe)
