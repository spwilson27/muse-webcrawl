List of streaming media services

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of streaming media services catalogs major over-the-top (OTT) services that deliver video, music, and other media over the internet, bypassing cable and broadcast television. The market expanded rapidly in the 2010s as Netflix's streaming model was imitated by studios, technology companies, and broadcasters, an era sometimes called the "streaming wars."
