MediaCityUK is a 200-acre mixed-use property development on the banks of the Manchester Ship Canal at Salford Quays, Greater Manchester, England. Developed by the Peel Group, it was designed as a hub for media, digital and creative industries, and is best known as the northern home of the BBC.

Development

The site occupies part of the former Salford docks, which closed in 1982 and were redeveloped as Salford Quays. In 2006 the BBC confirmed it would move several departments from London to the new development, and staff began relocating in 2011. ITV followed, moving its northern operations and the production of Coronation Street to a new set at nearby Trafford Wharf.

Occupants and facilities

The BBC occupies three buildings housing departments including Children's, Sport, Radio 5 Live and BBC Breakfast. Dock10 operates the television studios used by many productions. The University of Salford has a campus building there, and the development also contains offices (including the Landing incubator for digital businesses), apartments, hotels, restaurants and the Lowry theatre and gallery across the water, served by the Metrolink tram system.
