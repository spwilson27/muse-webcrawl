The 1992 UEFA European Football Championship was held in Sweden from 10 to 26 June 1992. It is famous for Denmark's fairytale triumph: the Danes had originally failed to qualify but were admitted after Yugoslavia was excluded because of the Yugoslav Wars.

Tournament

Eight teams took part, including hosts Sweden, Germany, France, England, the Netherlands, Scotland and the CIS (the transitional team of the former Soviet Union). Denmark beat the Netherlands on penalties in the semi-final and defeated reigning world champions Germany 2–0 in the final in Gothenburg, with goals from John Jensen and Kim Vilfort.

Legacy

Euro 1992 was the last European Championship with eight teams and the last major tournament in which goalkeepers could pick up back-passes, a rule changed shortly afterwards.
