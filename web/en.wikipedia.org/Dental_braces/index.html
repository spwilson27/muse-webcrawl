Dental braces

Dental braces on the upper teeth

Dental braces (orthodontic braces) are devices used in orthodontics to align and straighten teeth and correct malocclusions such as crowding, overbites, and underbites. They consist of brackets bonded to the teeth connected by an archwire, sometimes with elastic ligatures or bands.

How they work

Braces apply continuous gentle pressure that remodels alveolar bone over months or years, moving teeth into desired positions. Periodic adjustments by an orthodontist change the force applied. Clear aligners are a removable alternative, and lingual braces sit behind the teeth.

Care and treatment

Treatment commonly lasts one to three years, often followed by a retainer. Patients must avoid hard and sticky foods and maintain careful oral hygiene, since braces increase the risk of plaque accumulation.
