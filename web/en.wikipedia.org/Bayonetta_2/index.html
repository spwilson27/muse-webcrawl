Bayonetta 2

Bayonetta 2 is a 2014 action-adventure hack and slash game developed by PlatinumGames and published by Nintendo for the Wii U. It is the sequel to Bayonetta (2009) and was directed by Yusuke Hashimoto.

Gameplay and reception

The game follows the witch Bayonetta as she journeys to the mountain of Fimbulventr to rescue the soul of her friend Jeanne from Inferno. It retains the original's combo-driven combat and "Witch Time" mechanic, adding the Umbran Climax ability. Bayonetta 2 received critical acclaim, with praise for its combat, visuals and pacing; a Nintendo Switch port including the original game was released in 2018.
