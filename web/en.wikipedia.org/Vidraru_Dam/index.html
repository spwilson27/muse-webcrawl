Vidraru Dam

LocationArgeș River, Romania
TypeDouble-curvature arch dam
Height166 m
Opened1966
ReservoirLake Vidraru

The Vidraru Dam is a hydroelectric arch dam on the Argeș River in Romania, completed in 1966. At 166 metres high it was among the tallest dams in Europe at the time of its construction. The dam created Lake Vidraru, a reservoir of about 465 million cubic metres used for hydroelectric power generation (the associated power station has an installed capacity of about 220 MW), flood control and irrigation.

Tourism

The dam lies along the Transfăgărășan road (DN7C) in the Făgăraș Mountains and is a popular tourist stop; a statue of Prometheus holding a lightning bolt, symbolizing electricity, stands on a nearby height overlooking the dam. Bungee jumping has been offered from the dam crest.
