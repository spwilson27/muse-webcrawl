Jeff Mills

Jeff Mills

Born: 18 June 1963, Detroit, Michigan, U.S.

Also known as: The Wizard

Genres: Techno, Detroit techno

Labels: Axis, Tresor, Purpose Maker

Jeff Mills (born June 18, 1963) is an American DJ, record producer and composer, regarded as one of the pioneers of Detroit techno. In the 1980s he performed as "The Wizard" on Detroit radio station WDRQ/WJLB, and in 1989 he co-founded the influential collective Underground Resistance with "Mad" Mike Banks and Robert Hood.

Career

Going solo in 1991, Mills founded the Axis label and its Purpose Maker sub-label, releasing a stream of stark, fast, minimal tracks such as "The Bells" (1997), one of techno's most famous anthems. His rapid, precise DJ style — often using three turntables — became legendary, documented on the Live at Liquid Room, Tokyo mix (1996).

Later work

Beyond the club, Mills has composed and performed scores for films, including a new soundtrack for Fritz Lang's Metropolis, and has performed his compositions with symphony orchestras, notably in Blue Potential with the Montpellier Philharmonic Orchestra (2006). He was made a Chevalier of the Ordre des Arts et des Lettres by France in 2017.
