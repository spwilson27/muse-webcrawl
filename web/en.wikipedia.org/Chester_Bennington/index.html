Chester Bennington

Chester Charles Bennington (March 20, 1976 – July 20, 2017) was an American singer and songwriter best known as the lead vocalist of the rock band Linkin Park. He was also the lead vocalist of Grey Daze, Dead by Sunrise and Stone Temple Pilots at different points in his career.

Chester Bennington

Born: March 20, 1976, Phoenix, Arizona, U.S.

Died: July 20, 2017, Palos Verdes Estates, California, U.S.

Occupation: Singer, songwriter

Associated acts: Linkin Park, Dead by Sunrise, Stone Temple Pilots

Career

Bennington gained fame with Linkin Park's debut album Hybrid Theory (2000), which became one of the best-selling albums of the 21st century and included the hit "In the End". The band's blend of nu metal, rap and melodic singing, anchored by Bennington's powerful voice — capable of both melodic singing and harsh screaming — defined much of 2000s rock. Further albums included Meteora (2003), Minutes to Midnight (2007) and One More Light (2017). In 2013 he joined Stone Temple Pilots, recording the EP High Rise.

Death and legacy

Bennington died by suicide on July 20, 2017, at the age of 41. His death prompted an outpouring of tributes and renewed attention to mental-health issues. He is widely regarded as one of the most distinctive voices of his generation.
