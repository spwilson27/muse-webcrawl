Council on Foreign Relations

The Council on Foreign Relations (CFR) is an American think tank specializing in U.S. foreign policy and international relations. Founded in 1921, it is headquartered in New York City, with an office in Washington, D.C.

Activities

CFR publishes the journal Foreign Affairs, hosts meetings with world leaders and scholars, and produces reports and books. Its membership includes former officials, academics, business leaders and journalists. It describes itself as nonpartisan and independent.

History

It grew out of discussions after World War I about American international engagement, involving figures such as Elihu Root.
