Newport Wetlands

Newport Wetlands is a nature reserve on the Severn Estuary near Newport in south Wales, managed by the RSPB in partnership with Natural Resources Wales. Covering about 438 hectares, it was created as compensation for the loss of the Cardiff Bay mudflats when the Cardiff Bay Barrage was built.

Habitats and wildlife

The reserve, opened in 2000, comprises reedbeds, saline lagoons, wet grassland and scrub built on the site of former power-station ash lagoons at Uskmouth. It is designated a Site of Special Scientific Interest and a national nature reserve. Breeding birds include bearded tits, Cetti's warblers, reed and sedge warblers and avocets, while wintering wildfowl and waders use the lagoons and estuary mudflats. The East Usk Lighthouse stands within the reserve near the visitor centre.
