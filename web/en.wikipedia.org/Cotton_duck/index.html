Cotton duck

Cotton duck (from Dutch doek, "linen canvas"), also simply duck, sometimes duck canvas or duck cloth, is a heavy, plain-woven cotton fabric. Duck canvas is more tightly woven than most canvas, and is available in a graded weight scale numbered from 1 (heaviest) to 12.

The fabric is exceptionally durable and naturally water-resistant, and was historically used for ship sails, tents, and work clothing. Modern uses include sandbags, hammocks, sneakers, tote bags, artist canvas (usually primed with gesso), upholstery, and military equipment.

Numbered duck

In the United States, cotton duck is classified by a numbering system in which the number is 19 minus the weight in ounces per square yard of a standard width — so No. 1 duck weighs about 18 ounces per square yard and No. 12 about 7 ounces. "Single-fill" and "double-fill" (army duck) describe whether one or two yarns are woven in the weft.
