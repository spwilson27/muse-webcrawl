Polish Radio

Polish Radio (Polskie Radio S.A.) is Poland's national public-service radio broadcaster. Founded in 1925, it began regular broadcasts on 18 April 1926 from Warsaw. It operates several nationwide channels including Program 1, Program 2, Program 3 (Trójka) and Program 4, as well as regional stations and the external service Polish Radio External Service broadcasting in multiple languages.

During World War II its Warsaw station was destroyed after the German invasion, and clandestine Polish broadcasting continued from abroad. After 1945 it became the state broadcaster of the Polish People's Republic. Following political changes in 1989 it was restructured as a public company owned by the State Treasury.

Channels

Program 1 (Jedynka) focuses on news, current affairs and popular music. Program 2 (Dwójka) is devoted to culture, literature and classical music. Program 3, founded in 1962, became known for alternative music and youth programming. Program 4 targets younger listeners with education and popular culture.
