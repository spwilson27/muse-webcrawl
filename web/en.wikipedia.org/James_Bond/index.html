James Bond

James Bond is a fictional British secret agent, codename 007, created by Ian Fleming in the 1953 novel Casino Royale. Bond works for MI6 and is known for his licence to kill, gadgets, cars and the introduction 'Bond, James Bond'.

Books and films

Fleming wrote twelve novels and two short-story collections. The Eon film series began with Dr. No (1962) and became one of the highest-grossing franchises. Actors who played Bond include Sean Connery, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig.

Character

Bond is a Royal Navy commander, a connoisseur of vodka martinis 'shaken, not stirred', and is supported by M, Q and Miss Moneypenny, facing villains such as Blofeld of SPECTRE.

Influence

Bond reshaped the spy genre and British popular culture worldwide.
