Kirk Windstein

Born: April 14, 1965, New Orleans, Louisiana
Genres: Sludge metal, doom metal
Instruments: Guitar, vocals
Associated acts: Crowbar, Down, Kingdom of Sorrow

Kirk Windstein (born April 14, 1965) is an American musician best known as the founder, guitarist, and vocalist of the sludge metal band Crowbar. He is regarded as one of the originators of the New Orleans sludge metal sound.

Career

Windstein founded Crowbar in New Orleans in 1990; the band released its debut album Obedience thru Suffering in 1991 and went on to albums such as Broken Glass (1996), Lifesblood for the Downtrodden (2005), and Zero and Below (2022). He was also a founding member of the supergroup Down, playing guitar on the band's landmark 1995 debut NOLA, and of Kingdom of Sorrow with Jamey Jasta of Hatebreed.

Solo work

In 2020 Windstein released his debut solo album, Dream in Motion, exploring softer, melodic material outside the Crowbar sound.
