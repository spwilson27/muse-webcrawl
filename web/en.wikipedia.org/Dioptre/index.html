Dioptre

A dioptre (American spelling diopter) is a unit of measurement of the optical power of a lens or curved mirror, equal to the reciprocal of the focal length measured in metres (1 dioptre = 1 m⁻¹).

Use in optometry

Prescriptions for spectacles and contact lenses are expressed in dioptres: positive values for converging (plus) lenses correcting hyperopia, negative for diverging lenses correcting myopia. A typical prescription also includes cylindrical power for astigmatism. The dioptric difference between the two eyes is relevant in fitting lenses.

History

The term was proposed by the French ophthalmologist Ferdinand Monoyer in 1872. Dioptres add linearly when thin lenses are placed in contact, which makes the unit convenient for combining lens powers.
