Topps

The Topps Company
Founded: 1938
Headquarters: New York City, United States
Industry: Collectibles, confectionery
Products: Trading cards, Bazooka gum
Parent: Fanatics, Inc. (since 2022)

The Topps Company, Inc. is an American company best known for producing sports trading cards — above all its flagship baseball card sets — along with other collectibles and confectionery such as Bazooka bubble gum and Ring Pops. Founded in 1938 by the Shorin brothers in Brooklyn, New York, Topps became synonymous with American baseball cards for generations of collectors.

History

Topps began as a chewing gum company. Under Sy Berger, often called the father of the modern baseball card, the company launched its landmark 1952 baseball set, whose scarce high-number cards — including the famous Mickey Mantle card — became blue-chip collectibles. For decades Topps held an exclusive licence from Major League Baseball.

In 2007 the company was taken private by Michael Eisner's Tornante Company and Madison Dearborn Partners. In January 2022, after MLB and the MLBPA awarded their future trading-card licences to Fanatics, Topps was acquired by Fanatics for a reported $500 million, ending its long run as an independent company.

Products

Beyond baseball, Topps has produced cards for football, basketball, hockey, soccer (including Match Attax in the United Kingdom) and entertainment franchises such as Star Wars, as well as novelty lines like Garbage Pail Kids and Wacky Packages.
