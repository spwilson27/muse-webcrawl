The Queen (2006 film)

The Queen is a 2006 British drama film directed by Stephen Frears and written by Peter Morgan, depicting the British royal family's response to the death of Diana, Princess of Wales, on 31 August 1997. Helen Mirren stars as Queen Elizabeth II, with Michael Sheen as Prime Minister Tony Blair.

Plot

As unprecedented public grief erupts following Diana's death, the royal family remains secluded at Balmoral, regarding the matter as a private affair since Diana was no longer a royal. Blair, newly elected and attuned to the public mood, presses the Queen to return to London and acknowledge the nation's feelings. The film dramatises the tension between duty and tradition on one side and a changing country on the other, culminating in the Queen's live broadcast to the nation.

Reception

The Queen premiered at the Venice Film Festival, where Mirren won the Volpi Cup for Best Actress; she went on to win the Academy Award for Best Actress. The film was nominated for Best Picture and praised for its nuanced, humanising portrayal of living figures. It began writer Peter Morgan's sustained engagement with the monarchy, later continued in the play The Audience and the series The Crown.
