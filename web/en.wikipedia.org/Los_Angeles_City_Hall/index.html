Los Angeles City Hall

Los Angeles City Hall is the centre of government of the City of Los Angeles, California, located in the Civic Center district of downtown. Completed in 1928, the Art Deco tower rises 138 metres (454 ft) with 32 floors and was the city's tallest building until 1964, thanks to a charter height exemption.

Designed by John Parkinson, John C. Austin and Albert C. Martin Sr., its concrete incorporates sand from each of California's 58 counties and water from its 21 missions. The building houses the mayor's office and the Los Angeles City Council chambers, and its observation deck offers panoramic views. It is a familiar landmark in film and television, notably associated with Dragnet and as the Daily Planet building in 1950s Superman series.
