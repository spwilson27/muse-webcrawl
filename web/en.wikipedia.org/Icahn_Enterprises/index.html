Icahn Enterprises L.P. is an American publicly traded master limited partnership and diversified holding company headquartered in Sunny Isles Beach, Florida. It is controlled by billionaire investor Carl Icahn, who serves as its chairman. The partnership holds interests across several business segments, including investment, automotive, energy, food packaging, metals, real estate, home fashion, and pharmaceuticals.

History

The company originated as American Real Property Partners and later American Property Investors. In 2007 the partnership changed its name to Icahn Enterprises to reflect its transformation into the principal investment vehicle for Carl Icahn's holdings beyond his private funds.

Business segments

Icahn Enterprises' subsidiaries and investments have included CVR Energy, the automotive parts and services businesses built around Pep Boys and Icahn Automotive, Viskase (food packaging), PSC Metals, Westport Petroleum, and the home-fashion company WestPoint Home. Its investment segment holds positions managed alongside Icahn's activist investing activities.

Activist investing

Through Icahn Enterprises and affiliated funds, Carl Icahn has pursued activist campaigns at numerous public companies, seeking board representation and strategic changes intended to increase shareholder value.
