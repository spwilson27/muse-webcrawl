The 2006 Winter Olympics, officially the XX Olympic Winter Games, were held from 10 to 26 February 2006 in Turin, Italy, with mountain events spread across the Susa Valley in venues including Sestriere, Bardonecchia, and Cesana. It was the second Winter Olympics hosted by Italy, fifty years after Cortina d'Ampezzo in 1956.

Highlights

Germany topped the medal table. In figure skating, Japan's Shizuka Arakawa won the women's singles — her country's only gold of the Games — while Russia's Evgeni Plushenko won the men's title. American speed skater Joey Cheek won gold and silver on the ice and donated his medal bonuses to the humanitarian organisation Right to Play, inspiring other athletes to do the same.

Snowboard cross and the mass-start biathlon made their Olympic debuts. Canada's women's ice hockey team won gold, and in the men's tournament Sweden defeated Finland in a famous all-Nordic final. Host Italy won eleven medals, highlighted by gold for the men's cross-country relay team on the final day.

Doping raid

The Games were marked by an Italian police raid on the Austrian cross-country and biathlon team's quarters, prompted by the presence of banned coach Walter Mayer. Several Austrian athletes fled or withdrew, and six were later banned for life by the IOC. Austrian skier Hans Knauss and others had already been implicated in earlier scandals, and the affair intensified pressure for stricter anti-doping enforcement.
