A Charlie Brown Christmas is a 1965 American animated television special. It is the first TV special based on the comic strip Peanuts, by Charles M. Schulz. Produced by Lee Mendelson and directed by Bill Melendez, it premiered on CBS on December 9, 1965.

Plot

Depressed by the commercialism of Christmas, Charlie Brown takes on the job of directing the school Christmas play. Mocked for choosing a small, withered tree, he despairs until Linus recites the Annunciation to the shepherds from the Gospel of Luke, reminding everyone of the season's meaning; the gang then decorates the little tree and celebrates.

Legacy

Though CBS executives expected it to fail, the special was a ratings triumph and won an Emmy and a Peabody Award. Vince Guaraldi's jazz score, including "Linus and Lucy" and "Christmas Time Is Here", became a holiday standard, and the special has aired annually in the United States for decades.
