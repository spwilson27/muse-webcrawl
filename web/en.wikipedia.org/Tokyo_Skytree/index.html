Tokyo Skytree (東京スカイツリー) is a broadcasting and observation tower in Sumida, Tokyo, Japan. At 634 metres (2,080 ft), it is the tallest structure in Japan and the tallest tower in the world, and the third-tallest structure overall after the Burj Khalifa and Merdeka 118. Built to provide complete terrestrial digital broadcasting coverage of the Kanto region — which the 333-metre Tokyo Tower could no longer guarantee amid surrounding skyscrapers — it opened to the public on 22 May 2012 and quickly became one of Tokyo's defining landmarks and most visited attractions.

Design and construction

The tower was designed by Nikken Sekkei with sculptor Kiichi Sumikawa, and built by Obayashi Corporation for Tobu Railway and its subsidiary Tobu Tower Skytree. Its lattice of steel tubes has a footprint that morphs from a triangle at the base to a circle at the top, incorporating the traditional Japanese concepts of sori (concave curve) and mukuri (convexity) found in temple architecture and sword blades. A central reinforced-concrete pillar, inspired by the five-storey pagoda's shinbashira, acts as a seismic damper. Construction began in July 2008; the height of 634 m was chosen because the digits 6-3-4 can be read as "Mu-sa-shi", the old name of the region. The tower topped out in March 2011, days after the Great East Japan Earthquake, which it survived undamaged, and was completed on 29 February 2012.

Facilities

Two observation decks — the Tembo Deck at 350 m and the Tembo Galleria at 450 m — offer panoramic views across the Kanto Plain to Mount Fuji on clear days. At the tower's base is Tokyo Solamachi, a large shopping and entertainment complex, and an aquarium. The tower is lit at night in alternating colour schemes, traditionally iki (pale blue) and miyabi (purple), with special illuminations for events and seasons.

Broadcasting

Skytree serves as the primary transmission site for NHK and the major Kanto commercial broadcasters for terrestrial television and FM radio, succeeding Tokyo Tower in that role. Its official mascot is Sorakara-chan.
