7, Lok Kalyan Marg

7, Lok Kalyan Marg (7 LKM) is the official residence and principal workplace of the Prime Minister of India. Located on Lok Kalyan Marg in New Delhi, the complex spans about 12 acres and comprises several buildings, including the prime minister's residence, office, and facilities for security and staff.

History

The bungalows that make up the complex were originally built as residences for senior government officials during the British colonial period. The site became the prime minister's official residence in the 1980s, when Rajiv Gandhi moved there. Earlier prime ministers had lived at other addresses, including Teen Murti Bhavan and 1, Safdarjung Road.

The road on which the complex stands was formerly called Race Course Road. In 2016 it was renamed Lok Kalyan Marg, and the address consequently changed from 7, Race Course Road to 7, Lok Kalyan Marg.

Complex

The complex consists of five bungalows. One serves as the prime minister's residence, another as the office and meeting space (including the Panchavati hall used for official functions), while the remaining buildings house the Special Protection Group and staff. The complex also includes a helipad and extensive gardens.
