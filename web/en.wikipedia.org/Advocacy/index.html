Advocacy

Advocacy is the act of pleading for, supporting or recommending a cause or policy. Advocacy groups and non-governmental organisations seek to influence public policy, budgets and social attitudes on behalf of their members or beneficiaries.

Forms

Forms include policy advocacy and lobbying, legal advocacy, health advocacy for patients, self-advocacy by disabled people, and mass-media and grassroots campaigning.

Methods

Methods range from research and litigation to protests, public education and direct engagement with legislators. Rules on lobbying registration and disclosure vary by country. See also Lobbying, Advocacy group and Activism.
