Odd Future

Odd Future Wolf Gang Kill Them All, better known as Odd Future or OFWGKTA, was an American alternative hip-hop music collective formed in Los Angeles, California, in 2007. The collective's best-known members included Tyler, the Creator, Frank Ocean, Earl Sweatshirt, Hodgy, Domo Genesis, Left Brain, and Syd, together with groups such as The Internet and MellowHype.

Odd Future in their early years in Los Angeles.

History

Odd Future built a following through free albums released on Tumblr and an unruly, skate-culture-inflected public image. Tyler, the Creator's 2011 single "Yonkers" brought the group mainstream attention, and the collective launched the sketch comedy show Loiter Squad on Adult Swim and the annual Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival festival.

Style and legacy

The group's music mixed horrorcore imagery, jazz-inflected production, and confessional lyricism, and its members went on to major solo careers in the 2010s. Although the collective became inactive as a unit, reunion performances have occasionally taken place at Camp Flog Gnaw.
