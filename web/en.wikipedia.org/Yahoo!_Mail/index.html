Yahoo Mail is an email service launched on October 8, 1997, by the American company Yahoo. It offers free email accounts with 1 terabyte of storage, plus a paid tier (Yahoo Mail Plus / Pro) with an ad-free interface and additional features.

History

Yahoo acquired the webmail company Four11 in 1997 and relaunched its RocketMail service as Yahoo Mail. For years it was the world's largest webmail provider, offering initially just a few megabytes of storage — raised to 100 MB in 2004 and 1 GB in 2005 in response to competition from Google's Gmail, then to "unlimited" storage in 2007, and standardized at 1 TB in 2013. The service has been redesigned many times and is also available through mobile apps and standard mail protocols.

Data breaches

Yahoo Mail accounts were at the centre of the largest data breaches ever disclosed: attacks in 2013 and 2014, revealed in 2016, ultimately acknowledged to have affected all 3 billion Yahoo accounts, exposing names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth and hashed passwords.
