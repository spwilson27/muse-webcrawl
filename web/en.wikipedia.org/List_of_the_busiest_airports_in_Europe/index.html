List of the busiest airports in Europe

This is a list of the busiest airports in Europe, ranked by total passenger traffic. For decades London Heathrow was Europe's busiest airport, but Istanbul Airport, opened in 2018 and steadily expanded, overtook it in the early 2020s. Traffic at Europe's major hubs collapsed in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic and recovered over the following years, with the largest airports again handling more than 60 million passengers annually by 2023–2024.

Before 2020, Moscow's Sheremetyevo and Domodedovo airports also ranked in the European top ten; Russian traffic figures were affected substantially by the airspace closures following the 2022 invasion of Ukraine.

Trends

European airport traffic is shaped by hub-and-spoke networks of the legacy airline groups (Lufthansa Group, Air France–KLM, and IAG) and by low-cost carrier growth at airports such as London Stansted and Bergamo. Istanbul Airport's growth reflects Turkish Airlines' expanding hub strategy and Turkey's position between Europe, Asia and the Middle East. Environmental policies, notably flight caps debated at Amsterdam Schiphol, and infrastructure constraints at Heathrow have become central political questions for Europe's largest hubs.
