Bharat Jodo Yatra

Date7 September 2022 – 30 January 2023
OrganiserIndian National Congress
Led byRahul Gandhi
RouteKanyakumari to Srinagar, c. 4,080 km

The Bharat Jodo Yatra ("Unite India March") was a mass movement and foot march organised by the Indian National Congress party, led by Rahul Gandhi, which travelled from Kanyakumari in the south to Srinagar in Jammu and Kashmir between September 2022 and January 2023.

Route and aims

The march covered roughly 4,080 kilometres through 12 states and two union territories over about 150 days. The Congress party presented it as a campaign against economic inequality, social polarisation and political centralisation, and as an effort to reconnect the party with voters ahead of the 2024 general election.

Reception

The yatra drew large crowds and extensive media coverage and was widely credited with improving Gandhi's public image. A successor march, the Bharat Jodo Nyay Yatra, was held in early 2024.
