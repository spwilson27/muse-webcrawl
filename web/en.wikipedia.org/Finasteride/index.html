Finasteride

Finasteride, sold as Proscar and Propecia, is a medication used to treat benign prostatic hyperplasia and male pattern hair loss. It is a 5α-reductase inhibitor that blocks conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT).

Common side effects include sexual dysfunction; it must not be handled by pregnant women due to risk to a male fetus. It was patented in 1984 and approved for medical use in 1992.
