State Administration of Foreign Exchange

AgencyState Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)
ParentPeople's Bank of China
HeadquartersBeijing, China

The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China is an administrative agency under the People's Bank of China, tasked with drafting rules and regulations governing foreign exchange market activities and managing China's foreign exchange reserves.

Functions

SAFE supervises the balance of payments, administers current- and capital-account convertibility rules, inspects foreign exchange transactions, and oversees the investment of the world's largest stock of foreign exchange reserves — over US trillion — including through its investment office in Hong Kong and other overseas centres.

History

The agency was established as China's foreign exchange control system was formalised in the late 1970s and 1980s, and its role has evolved with the gradual internationalisation of the renminbi.
