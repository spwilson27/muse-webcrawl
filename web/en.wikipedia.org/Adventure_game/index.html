Adventure game

An adventure game is a video game genre in which the player assumes the role of a protagonist in an interactive story, driven by exploration and puzzle-solving. Adventure games emphasize narrative, character interaction, and solving puzzles rather than reflex-based challenges.

Adventure game
Genre: Video game
Subgenres: Text adventure, graphic adventure, visual novel
Notable pioneers: Infocom, Sierra, LucasArts

History

The genre began with the text adventure Colossal Cave Adventure (1976) and was popularized by Infocom's text games in the 1980s. Graphical adventures from Sierra On-Line (King's Quest) and Lucasfilm Games (Maniac Mansion, The Secret of Monkey Island) defined the point-and-click style. Myst (1993) became one of the best-selling PC games of its era.

Gameplay

Players explore environments, examine objects, converse with characters through dialogue trees, collect items, and solve inventory and logic puzzles. Modern adventure games include episodic narrative titles from Telltale Games and "walking simulators".
