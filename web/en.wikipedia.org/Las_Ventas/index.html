Las Ventas is the largest bullring in Spain and one of the most famous bullfighting arenas in the world. Located in the Guindalera quarter of the Salamanca district of Madrid, it is considered the home of bullfighting and the most prestigious venue in the sport.

History

The bullring was designed by the Spanish architect José Espeliú and built in the Neo-Mudéjar style between 1922 and 1929. It was inaugurated on 17 June 1931 with a charity bullfight. The arena replaced the older Plaza de Toros de la Fuente del Berro, which had become too small for the growing city.

Las Ventas did not open regularly until 1934, and its activities were interrupted by the Spanish Civil War, during which the ring served various wartime purposes.

Architecture

The building is constructed of brick over a framework of iron girders, decorated with ceramic tile work depicting the coats of arms of the Spanish provinces. The ruedo (ring) is 61.5 metres in diameter, one of the largest in the world. The seating is divided into ten tendidos (sections) around the ring.

Events

The most important event at Las Ventas is the San Isidro fair, held in May and June, which is considered the most significant bullfighting festival in the world. The arena also hosts concerts and other sporting events, including tennis exhibitions and the Red Bull X-Fighters freestyle motocross event.
