Associazione Calcio Milan, commonly referred to as AC Milan or simply Milan, is an Italian professional football club based in Milan, Lombardy. Founded in 1899 by English expatriates including Herbert Kilpin, the club has spent its entire history in the top flight of Italian football, with the exception of the 1980–81 and 1982–83 seasons.

History

Milan won its first Italian championship in 1901. The club enjoyed its greatest European successes under coaches Arrigo Sacchi and Fabio Capello in the late 1980s and 1990s, and under Carlo Ancelotti in the 2000s. Silvio Berlusconi owned the club from 1986 to 2017, a period in which Milan won numerous domestic and international trophies.

Honours

Milan has won 19 Serie A titles, 5 Coppa Italia trophies and 7 European Cup/UEFA Champions League titles — the second most of any Italian club in Europe's premier competition — along with a UEFA Cup Winners' Cup and five UEFA Super Cups. With 49 major honours it is among the most successful clubs in world football.

Rivalries

Milan shares the San Siro stadium with city rivals Inter Milan; their meetings, the Derby della Madonnina, are among the most famous derbies in football. The club's colours are red and black, chosen to represent fire and fear.
