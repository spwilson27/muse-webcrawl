Metro Cebu

Metro Cebu is the metropolitan area centred on Cebu City, the capital of Cebu province in the Philippines. It is the country's second most populous and economically important metropolitan area after Metro Manila, with a population of over three million. The metro comprises seven cities — Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Talisay, Danao, Naga and Carcar — and six municipalities including Consolacion, Liloan, Compostela, Cordova, Minglanilla and San Fernando, stretching along the eastern coast of Cebu island and including Mactan Island.

Economy

Metro Cebu is a hub for shipping, trade, business process outsourcing, tourism, shipbuilding and furniture making, sometimes nicknamed "Ceboom" for its rapid growth. Mactan-Cebu International Airport, the country's second-busiest, and the Port of Cebu serve the region; major business districts include Cebu Business Park and Cebu IT Park. The Cebu-Cordova Link Expressway, opened in 2022, is the longest bridge in the Philippines, connecting Cebu City to Mactan.

Geography and governance

The urban corridor is hemmed between mountains and the Tañon Strait side of the island, producing congestion that has driven plans for mass transit, including a proposed bus rapid transit system. The Metropolitan Cebu Development and Coordinating Board coordinates planning among the local governments, though it lacks the statutory authority of a full metropolitan government.
