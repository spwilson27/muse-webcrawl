Ableton Live

Developer: Ableton AG, Berlin

First released: 2001

Type: Digital audio workstation

Platforms: macOS, Windows

Ableton Live is a digital audio workstation and software music sequencer developed by the Berlin-based company Ableton. Designed as an instrument for live performance as well as a tool for composing, recording, arranging, mixing and mastering, it is widely used by electronic musicians, DJs and producers.

Features

Live offers two main views: the Arrangement View, a conventional timeline, and the Session View, a grid of clips that can be triggered non-linearly in real time — the feature that made it popular for live performance after its 2001 release. The software is known for its real-time audio time-stretching and warping, built-in instruments and effects, and support for MIDI controllers. Ableton's Push hardware controller, first released in 2013, provides hands-on control of the software.

History and editions

Ableton was founded in 1999 by Gerhard Behles, Robert Henke and Bernd Roggendorf. Live is sold in three editions — Intro, Standard and Suite — with Suite including the Max for Live environment, which lets users build custom instruments and effects. Subsequent versions added features such as comping, MIDI Polyphonic Expression support, and extensive sound libraries.
