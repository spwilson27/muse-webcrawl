Dimension stone

Dimension stone is natural stone or rock that has been selected and finished (trimmed, cut, drilled or ground) to specific sizes or shapes. Color, texture, pattern, and the surface finish of the stone are also normal requirements. Another important selection criterion is durability: the time-measuring ability of dimension stone to endure and maintain its essential characteristics of strength, resistance to decay, and appearance.

Cut granite blocks at a dimension stone quarry.

Types and uses

Common dimension stones include granite, marble, limestone, sandstone, slate, and travertine. The stone is quarried in large blocks, sawn into slabs, and finished by polishing, honing, flaming, or other treatments. Uses include building facades and cladding, flooring and countertops, paving, monuments and gravestones, and sculptural work.

Industry

Major producing countries include China, India, Italy, Brazil, Turkey, Spain, and the United States. The industry is distinct from crushed stone production because blocks must be extracted intact, often using diamond wire saws and channeling machines rather than blasting. Recycling and reclamation of stone buildings are also notable features of the trade.
