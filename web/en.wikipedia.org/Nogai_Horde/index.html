Nogai Horde

The Nogai Horde was a confederation founded by the Nogais, a Turkic ethnic group, that occupied the Pontic–Caspian steppe from about 1500 until they were pushed west by the Kalmyks in the 17th century.

History

The horde emerged from the Golden Horde's fragmentation under Edigu (died 1419), founder of the Manghit dynasty whose followers took the name Nogai, after the 13th-century warlord Nogai Khan. The Nogais ranged between the Volga, Emba, and Caspian, with their centre at Saraychik on the Yaik (Ural) River. They supplied horses and livestock to Muscovy and fought as allies and adversaries of Russia, the Crimean Khanate, and the Kazakh Khanate. Kalmyk expansion in the 1600s split the horde into the Greater and Lesser Nogai hordes, and many Nogais migrated westward toward the Black Sea and later the Caucasus, where their descendants live today, principally in Dagestan and Stavropol Krai in Russia.
