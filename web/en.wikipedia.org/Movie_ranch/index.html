Movie ranch

A movie ranch is a ranch that is at least partially dedicated to being used as a set for the creation and production of motion pictures and television productions, particularly of the Western genre. They were developed in southern California in the 1920s as studios sought authentic-looking open land within reach of Hollywood, typically in the Simi Hills and Santa Monica Mountains.

Notable ranches

Famous movie ranches include Iverson Movie Ranch in Chatsworth, whose sandstone rocks appeared in hundreds of Westerns and serials; Corriganville, opened to the public by stuntman Crash Corrigan; the Paramount Ranch in Agoura Hills (largely destroyed in the 2018 Woolsey Fire); the Melody Ranch in Santa Clarita, owned by Gene Autry; and Big Sky Movie Ranch. Many have been subdivided or preserved as parkland, while a few continue in production use.
