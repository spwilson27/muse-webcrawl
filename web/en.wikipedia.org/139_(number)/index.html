139 (one hundred thirty-nine) is the natural number following 138 and preceding 140.

In mathematics

139 is the 34th prime number. It is a twin prime with 137, a Chen prime, and a regular prime. It is the sum of five consecutive prime numbers: 19 + 23 + 29 + 31 + 37 = 139. It is also a strictly non-palindromic number.

Other uses

Psalm 139 is one of the best-known psalms, beginning "O Lord, you have searched me and known me." The atomic number of the hypothetical element untriennium is 139. In astronomy, 139 Juewa is a large dark main-belt asteroid discovered in 1874, the first asteroid discovered in China.
