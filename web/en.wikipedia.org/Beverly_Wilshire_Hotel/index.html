Beverly Wilshire Hotel

The Beverly Wilshire, A Four Seasons Hotel, commonly known as the Beverly Wilshire Hotel, is a historic luxury hotel in Beverly Hills, California, located at the intersection of Wilshire Boulevard and Rodeo Drive. Completed in 1928 on the site of the former Beverly Hills Speedway, it has long been a gathering place for celebrities, royalty, and heads of state.

History

Built by real estate developer Walter G. McCarty and designed by Walker and Eisen in Italian Renaissance Revival style, the hotel is constructed of Tuscan stone and Carrara marble. A second wing, designed by Paul Revere Williams, was added in 1971. Notable long-term residents included Elvis Presley, John Lennon, and Warren Beatty, while guests have ranged from the Dalai Lama to Barack Obama.

In popular culture

The hotel achieved worldwide fame as the principal setting of the 1990 film Pretty Woman, starring Richard Gere and Julia Roberts, in which Gere's character stays in the hotel's penthouse. It also appears in the television series Entourage and numerous other productions.
