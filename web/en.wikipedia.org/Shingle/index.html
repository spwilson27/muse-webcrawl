Shingle

Shingle may refer to several different things:

Shingles — roof or wall coverings — are among the most common meanings of the term.

Building materials

A roof shingle is one of many overlapping elements used to cover a roof; materials include asphalt (bitumen) composition shingles, the most common roofing material in North America; wood shingles and shakes; slate; metal; fibre cement; and solar shingles that double as photovoltaic panels. Wall shingles similarly clad exterior walls, and are characteristic of the Shingle Style of American architecture of the 1880s.

Other uses

Shingle also denotes coarse, rounded beach gravel — a shingle beach — consisting of pebbles and cobbles larger than sand but smaller than boulders. In medicine, "shingles" is the common name of herpes zoster, a painful rash caused by reactivation of the varicella-zoster virus. A shingle can also be a small signboard, especially of a professional office, as in the expression "to hang out one's shingle".
