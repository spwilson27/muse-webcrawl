Neptune's Staircase

Neptune's Staircase
Location: Banavie, near Fort William, Scotland
Waterway: Caledonian Canal
Locks: 8
Rise: 19.5 m (64 ft)

Neptune's Staircase is a staircase lock comprising eight locks on the Caledonian Canal at Banavie, near Fort William in the Scottish Highlands. It is the longest staircase lock in Britain, raising boats 19.5 metres (64 feet) over a quarter-mile stretch.

History

The flight was built by Thomas Telford between 1803 and 1822 as part of the Caledonian Canal linking the east and west coasts via the Great Glen. The locks were originally hand-operated; today they are mechanised, and a passage takes roughly 90 minutes.

Today

The staircase is a popular tourist attraction, overlooked by Ben Nevis, and is crossed by road and rail swing bridges at its foot.
