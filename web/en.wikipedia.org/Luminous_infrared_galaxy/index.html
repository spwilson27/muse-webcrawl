Luminous infrared galaxy

Luminous infrared galaxies or LIRGs are galaxies with luminosities above 1011 solar luminosities in the far-infrared. Those above 1012 L☉ are called ultraluminous infrared galaxies (ULIRGs), and those above 1013 L☉ hyperluminous infrared galaxies (HyLIRGs).

Origin

Most LIRGs are interacting or merging gas-rich galaxies whose intense starbursts — and often an embedded active galactic nucleus — heat large quantities of dust, which re-radiates the energy in the infrared. They were discovered in large numbers by the IRAS satellite survey in the 1980s and are key to understanding galaxy evolution and the cosmic star-formation history.
