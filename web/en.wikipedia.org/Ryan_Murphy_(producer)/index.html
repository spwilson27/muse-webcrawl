Ryan Patrick Murphy (born 1965) is an American television writer, director and producer. He began his career as a journalist before creating the teen drama Popular (1999–2001) and the medical drama Nip/Tuck (2003–2010).

Career highlights

Murphy achieved major success with the musical comedy-drama Glee (2009–2015) and went on to create the anthology series American Horror Story, American Crime Story — whose first season, The People v. O. J. Simpson, won widespread acclaim — Feud, Pose, and the drama 9-1-1. In 2018 he signed a major overall deal with Netflix, producing series such as The Politician, Hollywood, Ratched and Dahmer – Monster: The Jeffrey Dahmer Story. He has won multiple Emmy Awards and a Tony Award.
