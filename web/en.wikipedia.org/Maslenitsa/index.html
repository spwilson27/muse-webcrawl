Maslenitsa

TypeSlavic festival
ObservancesEating blini; festivities before Lent
Related toGreat Lent; Mardi Gras

Maslenitsa is an Eastern Slavic religious and folk holiday celebrated during the last week before Great Lent in the Eastern Orthodox tradition. It combines pagan and Christian elements and is characterised by the eating of blini (thin pancakes), festivities, and the burning of a straw effigy representing winter.

Traditions

Each day of Maslenitsa week has its own customs, ending with the burning of the Maslenitsa doll and Forgiveness Sunday, when people ask each other for forgiveness before Lent.

Food

Blini, served with butter, sour cream, jam, or caviar, symbolise the sun and the approaching spring; dairy products are consumed in abundance before the Lenten fast.
