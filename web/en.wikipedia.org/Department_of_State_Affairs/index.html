Department of State Affairs

The Department of State Affairs may refer to government bodies concerned with the administration of state or foreign affairs in several countries. The term is most commonly associated with historical and contemporary ministries responsible for coordinating government business, protocol, and relations between central authorities and regional administrations.

North Korea

In North Korea, the State Affairs Commission is the supreme policy-oriented leadership body of the state, and its administrative apparatus has been rendered in English as a department of state affairs. The commission, chaired by the country's supreme leader, oversees defense, security, and major state policy.

Historical usage

In imperial China, the Department of State Affairs (Shangshu Sheng) was one of the three central government departments of the Three Departments and Six Ministries system, functioning as the chief executive organ that supervised the six ministries from the Sui and Tang dynasties onward. It was headed by the Chancellor and was responsible for drafting, reviewing, and implementing imperial edicts and administering the day-to-day business of the empire.
