Aeolian mode

The Aeolian mode is a musical mode corresponding to the natural minor scale. Starting on A, its pitches are A, B, C, D, E, F, G — the white keys of the piano — with the interval pattern whole, half, whole, whole, half, whole, whole.

History

The name, after the ancient Greek Aeolians, was assigned to this octave species by Heinrich Glarean in his Dodecachordon of 1547, expanding the traditional eight church modes.

Use

As the natural minor scale, Aeolian underlies much Western minor-key music, folk song and rock. It differs from harmonic and melodic minor in its unraised sixth and seventh degrees. See also Minor scale, Mode (music) and Dorian mode.
