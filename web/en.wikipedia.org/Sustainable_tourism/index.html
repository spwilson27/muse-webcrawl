Sustainable tourism

Sustainable tourism is tourism that takes full account of its current and future economic, social and environmental impacts, addressing the needs of visitors, the industry, the environment and host communities, as defined by the World Tourism Organization (UNWTO).

Principles

Its principles include making optimal use of environmental resources, respecting the socio-cultural authenticity of host communities, and ensuring viable long-term economic operations providing fairly distributed benefits. Ecotourism and responsible tourism are related, more narrowly focused approaches.

Issues

Key concerns include the carbon emissions of air travel, overtourism in cities such as Venice and Barcelona, leakage of tourism revenue away from local economies, and greenwashing in certification schemes.

Certification

Bodies such as the Global Sustainable Tourism Council set criteria for destinations and businesses claiming sustainable practice.
