Katalin Karikó

Katalin Karikó
Born: 17 January 1955, Szolnok, Hungary
Fields: Biochemistry, mRNA therapeutics
Institutions: University of Pennsylvania; BioNTech
Award: Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (2023)

Katalin Karikó (born 1955) is a Hungarian-American biochemist who specialises in RNA-mediated mechanisms, in particular in vitro transcribed mRNA for protein therapies. Her work with Drew Weissman on nucleoside modification laid the foundation for the mRNA vaccines against COVID-19.

Work

At the University of Pennsylvania, Karikó and Weissman discovered that modifying mRNA nucleosides (using pseudouridine) suppressed unwanted inflammatory responses and increased protein production, a breakthrough published in 2005. She later became a senior vice president at BioNTech.

Recognition

Karikó and Weissman shared the 2023 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine "for their discoveries concerning nucleoside base modifications that enabled the development of effective mRNA vaccines against COVID-19". See also mRNA vaccine.
