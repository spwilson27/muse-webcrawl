Types of motorcycles

Motorcycles are categorized by design and intended use
Main categoriesStandard, cruiser, sport, touring, dual-sport, off-road, scooter

There are many systems for classifying types of motorcycles, describing how the motorcycles are put to use, or the designer's intent, or some combination of the two. Six main categories are widely recognized: standard, cruiser, sports, touring, dual sport, and off-road, with scooters sometimes treated separately.

Categories

  • Standard / naked bikes — general-purpose road motorcycles with an upright riding position, e.g. the Honda CB series.
  • Cruisers — styled after American machines of the 1930s–1960s, with low seats and feet-forward riding positions; includes choppers and power cruisers such as Harley-Davidson models.
  • Sport bikes — optimized for speed, acceleration, braking and cornering, with full fairings and committed riding positions, e.g. the Yamaha YZF-R1 and Ducati Panigale.
  • Touring — large machines designed for long distances, with luggage, wind protection and passenger comfort, e.g. the Honda Gold Wing.
  • Dual-sport — street-legal motorcycles also capable off-road, including adventure (ADV) bikes such as the BMW R 1300 GS.
  • Off-road — motocross, enduro, and trials bikes not intended for public roads.
  • Scooters and mopeds — step-through designs with small wheels and often automatic transmissions, e.g. the Vespa.
