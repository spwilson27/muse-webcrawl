Robert McKee

Robert McKee (born January 30, 1941) is an American author and lecturer on screenwriting, best known for his "Story Seminar" and his 1997 book Story: Substance, Structure, Style and the Principles of Screenwriting.

Career

McKee began his career in theatre and acted and directed before turning to teaching. His Story Seminar, a multi-day course, has been attended by tens of thousands of writers, directors, and producers in cities around the world. Story sets out principles of narrative design — structure, character, inciting incidents, crisis, and climax — drawing on Aristotle's Poetics and on classical film examples.

Legacy and portrayal

McKee was fictionalised, played by Brian Cox, in the 2002 film Adaptation, in which Charlie Kaufman's character attends his seminar. He has also published Dialogue (2016) and Character (2021).
