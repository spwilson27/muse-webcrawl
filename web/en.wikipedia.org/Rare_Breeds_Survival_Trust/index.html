The Rare Breeds Survival Trust (RBST) is a British conservation charity founded in 1973 to safeguard the United Kingdom's native breeds of livestock and horses. It maintains a watchlist categorising breeds by rarity and risk of extinction.

Work

RBST monitors population numbers of native cattle, sheep, pigs, goats, poultry, and equines, supports breed societies and keepers, promotes the use of rare breeds in conservation grazing and sustainable farming, and operates a gene bank storing genetic material. Its work follows the loss of dozens of British breeds during the 20th century as agriculture specialised in a few commercial types.
