The European Patent Office (EPO) is one of the two organs of the European Patent Organisation, the other being the Administrative Council. The EPO acts as the executive body of the Organisation and is responsible for examining and granting European patents under the European Patent Convention (EPC). It is headquartered in Munich, with a large branch in Rijswijk (near The Hague) and offices in Berlin, Vienna, and Brussels.

Functions

The EPO receives around 190,000 patent applications per year. Its examiners search prior art and examine applications for novelty, inventive step, and industrial applicability. After grant, third parties may oppose a patent centrally within nine months. Decisions of the examining and opposition divisions can be appealed to the Boards of Appeal, whose Enlarged Board settles important points of law, such as those on the patentability of computer-implemented inventions and plants obtained by essentially biological processes.

The EPO also provides patent information services, notably the free Espacenet database with over 150 million patent documents, and publishes the European Patent Bulletin. Since June 2023, applicants may request a European patent with unitary effect (the "unitary patent"), registered by the EPO and enforceable before the Unified Patent Court.
