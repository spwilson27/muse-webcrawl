PlayStation Move

PlayStation Move (プレイステーションムーヴ) is a motion game controller developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment (then Sony Computer Entertainment). Initially released in 2010 for the PlayStation 3 console, the Move's motion controller wand is the principal input device; its position in three-dimensional space is tracked by the PlayStation Eye camera, while internal inertial sensors detect its orientation and acceleration. The illuminated sphere at the head of the wand can glow in any of a full range of colours, providing visual feedback and enabling the camera to track it robustly.

Hardware

The system comprises the Move motion controller, the PlayStation Eye camera, and the optional Move navigation controller, a wireless one-handed controller with an analog stick. Accessories such as the Move Sharp Shooter and racing wheel shell let the wand act as a gun or steering device. Move controllers are charged and paired via USB.

History

Sony's research on camera-based motion control dated back to the EyeToy of 2003. The Move was unveiled at E3 2009 and launched in September 2010, competing with Nintendo's Wii Remote and Microsoft's Kinect. The controllers were later supported on the PlayStation 4 (with the PlayStation Camera) and on PlayStation 5, and a pair of Move wands became a standard input method for PlayStation VR in 2016, until the release of the second-generation PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers.

Reception and sales

The Move was praised for precision but saw moderate game support on the PS3; Sony announced sales of over 15 million units by late 2012. Its second life as the standard controller for PlayStation VR extended its production for more than a decade.
