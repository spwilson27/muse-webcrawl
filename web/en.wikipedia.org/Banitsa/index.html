Banitsa

Banitsa is a traditional Bulgarian pastry made of layered filo dough with a filling — most classically whisked eggs and crumbled white brined cheese (sirene) — brushed with butter and baked. It is a central part of Bulgarian breakfasts and festive tables.

Variations

Fillings include spinach (spanachnik), leek, pumpkin with sugar (tikvenik), cabbage, minced meat, and sweet versions with apples or milk (mlechna banitsa). The pastry is often arranged in a spiral in a round pan.

Traditions

On New Year's Eve, a banitsa is baked with small charms or written fortunes (kasmeti) wrapped inside; each diner's slice is said to predict their luck for the coming year. Banitsa with boza or yoghurt is a classic Bulgarian breakfast pairing.

Regional relatives

Related layered pastries across the Balkans and Anatolia include burek, gibanica, zeljanica, and Turkish börek.
