Mr. Potato Head is an American toy brand consisting of a plastic model of a potato "head" that can be decorated with a variety of detachable plastic parts such as eyes, ears, nose, mouth, arms and accessories. It was invented by George Lerner and first manufactured and distributed by Hasbro in 1952.

History

The original 1952 set contained only the plastic parts, intended to be pushed into a real potato or other vegetable; government food-rationing concerns and complaints about rotting produce led Hasbro to include a plastic potato body in 1964. Mrs. Potato Head followed in 1953, and many themed variants and licensed versions have been produced since.

Mr. Potato Head was the first toy advertised on television and the first aimed directly at children rather than their parents. In 2021 Hasbro rebranded the line simply as "Potato Head", while the Mr. and Mrs. characters were retained.

In popular culture

The character gained renewed fame as a character in Pixar's Toy Story films, voiced by Don Rickles, with Mrs. Potato Head voiced by Estelle Harris.
