Ross County Football Club

Ross County Football Club is a Scottish professional football club based in Dingwall, Highland. Nicknamed "The Staggies", the club was founded in 1929 and plays its home matches at Victoria Park. Its badge features a caberfeidh, or stag's head, taken from the arms of Ross-shire.

History

Ross County played in the Highland Football League for most of its history, winning the league championship on several occasions, before being elected to the Scottish Football League in 1994 along with Inverness Caledonian Thistle. The club climbed the divisions and won the Scottish First Division in 2011–12, earning promotion to the Scottish Premier League for the first time.

Cup success

County reached the Scottish Cup final in 2010, defeating Celtic in the semi-final before losing to Dundee United. The club's greatest triumph came in March 2016, when it won the Scottish League Cup, beating Hibernian 2–1 in the final at Hampden Park to lift its first major national trophy.

Stadium

Victoria Park (later known under sponsorship names including the Global Energy Stadium) was extensively redeveloped for top-flight football and has a capacity of roughly 6,500.
