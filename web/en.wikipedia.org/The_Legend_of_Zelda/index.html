The Legend of Zelda is an action-adventure video game franchise created by Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka and developed and published by Nintendo. The series centres on the hero Link, who battles to save the kingdom of Hyrule — and its princess, Zelda — from the demon king Ganon (Ganondorf), protecting the sacred relic known as the Triforce.

Gameplay

Gameplay mixes exploration of an overworld, combat, puzzle-solving and dungeon crawling. The original 1986 game for the Famicom Disk System pioneered open, non-linear exploration. Ocarina of Time (1998) successfully translated the formula to 3D and is frequently cited among the greatest video games ever made. Breath of the Wild (2017) reimagined the series as a physics-driven open world and became one of the best-selling and most acclaimed games of its generation; its sequel, Tears of the Kingdom (2023), expanded it with building mechanics.

Major titles

  • The Legend of Zelda (1986), A Link to the Past (1991), Link's Awakening (1993)
  • Ocarina of Time (1998), Majora's Mask (2000), The Wind Waker (2002)
  • Twilight Princess (2006), Skyward Sword (2011)
  • Breath of the Wild (2017), Tears of the Kingdom (2023), Echoes of Wisdom (2024)

Legacy

The franchise is one of Nintendo's flagship properties and one of the best-selling video game series of all time, with lasting influence on game design, an official (much-debated) timeline, and music — notably Koji Kondo's themes — that is performed in concert worldwide.
