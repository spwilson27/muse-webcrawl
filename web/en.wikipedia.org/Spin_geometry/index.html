Spin geometry

Spin geometry is a field of differential geometry and mathematical physics concerning spin structures on manifolds and the Dirac operators defined on them. A spin structure on an orientable Riemannian manifold allows the consistent definition of spinor fields, which are the geometric objects underlying fermions in physics.

Key ideas

Spin geometry generalises the theory of the Dirac operator; the Atiyah–Singer index theorem computes the index of such operators topologically, connecting analysis, geometry, and topology. Seiberg–Witten theory and many proofs in scalar-curvature geometry rely on spin-geometric methods.
