International Workers' Day

International Workers' Day, also known as May Day, is a celebration of labourers and the working classes promoted by the international labour movement, observed on 1 May in most countries, where it is often a public holiday.

History

The date commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and the movement for the eight-hour day; in 1889 the Second International designated 1 May as a day of international demonstrations for workers' rights. Annual marches, rallies, and strikes remain central to labour movements worldwide, while the United States and Canada instead mark Labor Day in September.
