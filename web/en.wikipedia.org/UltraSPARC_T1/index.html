UltraSPARC T1

UltraSPARC T1
Designer: Sun Microsystems
Launched: November 14, 2005
Cores: up to 8, 4 threads each
Code name: "Niagara"

The UltraSPARC T1 is a multithreading, multicore CPU produced by Sun Microsystems and codenamed "Niagara". It was notable for its emphasis on throughput and energy efficiency rather than single-thread performance.

Design

The chip contains up to eight SPARC V9 cores, each able to interleave four hardware threads, giving up to 32 concurrent threads, sharing a crossbar-connected L2 cache. It implemented Sun's "CoolThreads" approach, consuming about 70 watts.

Impact

The T1 was open-sourced as the OpenSPARC T1 and powered Sun's Sun Fire T1000 and T2000 servers; it was followed by the UltraSPARC T2 in 2007.
