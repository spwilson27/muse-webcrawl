Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission

The Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO) is an organisation in the United States that provides frameworks and guidance on internal control, enterprise risk management and fraud deterrence. It was formed in 1985 to sponsor the National Commission on Fraudulent Financial Reporting (the Treadway Commission).

Frameworks

COSO's Internal Control — Integrated Framework (1992, updated 2013) defines internal control through five components — control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, and monitoring — visualised in the "COSO cube". It is the most widely used framework for compliance with the Sarbanes–Oxley Act's internal-control reporting requirements.

COSO also published an Enterprise Risk Management framework (2004, updated 2017 as ERM — Integrating with Strategy and Performance). Its sponsoring organisations are the AICPA, the Institute of Management Accountants, the Institute of Internal Auditors, the American Accounting Association and Financial Executives International.
