List of Nobel Memorial Prize laureates in Economics

The Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences, officially the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel, was established in 1968 by Sweden's central bank and first awarded in 1969 to Ragnar Frisch and Jan Tinbergen. It is administered by the Nobel Foundation and awarded by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences alongside the original Nobel Prizes. This list summarizes selected laureates by year; through the mid-2020s the prize has gone to more than ninety individuals, overwhelmingly men and predominantly affiliated with American universities.

Notes

The prize is sometimes criticized as it was not among the five established by Alfred Nobel's 1895 will, and its selection has at times been accused of ideological bias. Elinor Ostrom (2009) was the first woman laureate; Esther Duflo (2019), at 46, was the youngest. The award is presented each year on 10 December in Stockholm.
