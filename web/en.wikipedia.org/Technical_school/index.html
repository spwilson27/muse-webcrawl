Technical school

A technical school is an educational institution that emphasises practical, vocational and career-oriented training, preparing students for specific trades, technical occupations or applied professions rather than purely academic study. The term is used for institutions at both secondary and post-secondary level, depending on the country.

By country

In the United States, technical schools and technical colleges offer programmes leading to certificates, diplomas and associate degrees in fields such as healthcare, information technology, automotive technology and skilled trades. In the United Kingdom, technical schools historically formed part of the tripartite secondary system alongside grammar and secondary modern schools, though few were built. In Germany, institutions such as the Berufsschule combine part-time schooling with apprenticeships in the dual education system.

Curriculum

Technical schools combine general education subjects with hands-on workshop, laboratory or clinical instruction. Many work closely with employers through apprenticeships, internships and advisory boards so that training matches current industry needs.
