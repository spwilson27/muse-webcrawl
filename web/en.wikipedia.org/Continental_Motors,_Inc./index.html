Continental Motors, Inc.

Continental Motors, Inc. (now Continental Aerospace Technologies) is an American aircraft engine manufacturer headquartered in Mobile, Alabama. Founded in 1905 in Muskegon, Michigan, it became one of the two dominant makers of general-aviation piston engines, alongside Lycoming.

Continental Motors, Inc.

Founded: 1905
Headquarters: Mobile, Alabama, United States
Industry: Aerospace
Products: Piston and diesel aircraft engines
Parent: AVIC (since 2011)

History

Continental began as an automobile engine builder and entered aviation in the 1920s; its A-40 and the wartime engines that followed established its reputation. Post-war, the C-65/C-85/O-200, O-300, IO-360 and IO-550 families powered generations of Cessna, Piper, Beechcraft and Mooney aircraft. The company passed through several owners — Teledyne (as Teledyne Continental Motors) from 1969, then sale in 2011 to AVIC, the Chinese state aviation group.

Products

Current products include the classic avgas piston lines (O-200 through IO-550), the Titan experimental engines, and Jet-A-burning diesel (CD-series) engines derived from automotive diesel technology acquired from Thielert. Continental also provides factory overhauls, parts and the services of its repair stations.
