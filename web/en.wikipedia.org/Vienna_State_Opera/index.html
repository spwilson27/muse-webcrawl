Vienna State Opera

The Vienna State Opera (German: Wiener Staatsoper) is a historic opera house and opera company based in Vienna, Austria. The 1,709-seat Renaissance Revival venue was the first major building on the Vienna Ring Road and was built from 1861 to 1869.

History

The theatre opened on 25 May 1869 with Mozart's Don Giovanni. It was largely destroyed by Allied bombing in March 1945 and rebuilt, reopening on 5 November 1955 with Beethoven's Fidelio under Karl Böhm. The company performs around 350 performances of more than 60 operas and ballets each season.

Its orchestra's musicians form the Vienna Philharmonic in their concert role. Notable music directors have included Gustav Mahler, Richard Strauss, Herbert von Karajan and Claudio Abbado.
