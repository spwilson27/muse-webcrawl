Embraer E-Jet E2 family

ManufacturerEmbraer
First flight23 May 2016 (E190-E2)
Introduction24 April 2018 with Widerøe (E190-E2)
VariantsE175-E2, E190-E2, E195-E2

The Embraer E-Jet E2 family is a family of twin-engine, narrow-body, medium-range jet airliners developed by the Brazilian aerospace manufacturer Embraer as the successor to the first-generation E-Jet family. The programme was launched in 2013, with the E190-E2 making its first flight in May 2016 and entering service with Widerøe in April 2018.

Design

The E2 family features new, higher-aspect-ratio wings, fly-by-wire controls, and Pratt & Whitney geared turbofan engines, delivering substantial reductions in fuel burn and noise compared with the original E-Jets. The family comprises the E175-E2, E190-E2, and the stretched E195-E2, seating roughly 80 to 146 passengers.

Market

Development of the smallest variant, the E175-E2, was suspended because its weight exceeds the limits of U.S. airline scope clauses, while the E190-E2 and E195-E2 have been ordered by airlines including Azul, Air Peace, KLM Cityhopper, and Porter Airlines.
