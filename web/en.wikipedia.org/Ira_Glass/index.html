Ira Jeffrey Glass (born 1959) is an American public radio personality and producer. He is the creator, host, and executive producer of This American Life, the narrative documentary radio programme — later also a podcast — that became one of the most influential examples of storytelling in audio.

Early career

Glass began at NPR's Baltimore station at age 19 and spent 17 years at NPR in Chicago and Washington, working as a tape cutter, newscast writer, editor, and reporter for programmes including All Things Considered and Morning Edition, and co-hosting the experimental programme The Wild Room on WBEZ Chicago.

This American Life

In 1995 Glass launched This American Life (initially Your Radio Playhouse) at WBEZ. Each episode is built around a theme explored through several "acts" of reported and personal storytelling. The show won all major broadcasting awards, became a top podcast after 2006, and its production company helped launch the hit podcast Serial in 2014. Glass is known for his distinctive conversational narration and for mentoring many prominent audio producers.

Other work

Glass has worked as a story editor on films including Don't Think Twice (2016) and toured live shows based on the programme. He is a cousin of composer Philip Glass.
