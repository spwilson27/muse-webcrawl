Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways (Arabic: القطرية) is the state-owned flag carrier of Qatar, headquartered at Qatar Airways Tower in Doha and operating from Hamad International Airport. Founded in 1993 and relaunched in 1997 under chief executive Akbar Al Baker, it has grown into one of the world's largest long-haul airlines, serving over 150 destinations.

Operations

Qatar Airways is a member of the Oneworld alliance (joined 2013) and holds stakes in several carriers, including IAG, LATAM, Cathay Pacific, and Airlink. Its fleet consists of Airbus and Boeing wide-body and narrow-body aircraft, including A350s and Boeing 777s. The airline is known for its Qsuite business class product and has repeatedly been named Airline of the Year in the Skytrax awards. It was a major sponsor and official airline of the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar.

History

During the 2017–2021 diplomatic blockade of Qatar by neighbouring states, the airline lost access to much regional airspace and rerouted flights via Iran. In 2023 Badr Mohammed Al Meer succeeded Al Baker as group chief executive.
