Hololive Production
Agency: Cover Corporation
Founded: 2017 (as Hololive)
Industry: Virtual YouTubers

Hololive Production is a virtual YouTuber (VTuber) agency owned by Japanese company Cover Corporation. Its performers stream mainly on YouTube using anime-style avatars tracked by motion-capture and face-tracking technology, singing, gaming, and chatting with large international audiences.

History

Cover launched the Hololive project in 2017 with Tokino Sora as its first talent. The agency expanded into generations of Japanese performers, a male group Holostars, and overseas branches including Hololive Indonesia and Hololive English, which debuted in 2020 and drove global growth.

Activities

Hololive talents release original music, hold concerts with 3D models, sell merchandise, and appear in games and collaborations. Stars such as Gawr Gura became among the most-subscribed VTubers worldwide.
