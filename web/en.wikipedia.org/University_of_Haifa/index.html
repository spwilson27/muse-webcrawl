University of Haifa

Type: Public research university
Established: 1963
Location: Haifa, Israel
Students: ~18,000

The University of Haifa (Hebrew: אוניברסיטת חיפה) is a public research university on Mount Carmel in Haifa, Israel. Founded in 1963 as the Haifa University Institute under the academic auspices of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, it received full university accreditation in 1972.

Campus and character

The campus, designed partly by Oscar Niemeyer, sits atop Mount Carmel with views over the Mediterranean. The university has the most ethnically mixed student body in Israel, with Jewish, Arab, Druze and other students, and includes a large population of military and security-studies students.

Academics

Faculties cover humanities, social sciences, law, education, social welfare and health sciences, natural sciences and marine sciences — the Leon H. Charney School of Marine Sciences exploits the coastal location. The Hecht Museum of archaeology is located on campus, and the university library's Younes and Soraya Nazarian wing is among Israel's largest.
