The 1972 Winter Olympics, officially the XI Olympic Winter Games, were held from 3 to 13 February 1972 in Sapporo, Japan. They were the first Winter Olympic Games held outside Europe and North America.

Highlights

Soviet cross-country skier Galina Kulakova won all three women's events. Dutch speed skater Ard Schenk won three gold medals. In ski jumping, Yukio Kasaya led a Japanese sweep of the normal hill podium, to the delight of the home crowd. The Soviet Union topped the medal standings.

Controversy

Before the Games, IOC president Avery Brundage threatened to bar Austrian alpine skier Karl Schranz over professionalism; Schranz was declared ineligible days before the opening, a celebrated case in the amateurism disputes of the era.
