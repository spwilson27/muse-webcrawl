Standard Missile

Standard Missile refers to a family of American-made surface-to-air missiles developed by the United States Navy. The family includes the SM-1, SM-2, SM-3 and SM-6 variants, which form the backbone of the navy's Aegis air-defence system.

Variants

The SM-1 and SM-2 are medium- and extended-range fleet air defence missiles guided semi-actively. The SM-3 is an exoatmospheric ballistic missile defence interceptor using a kinetic warhead. The SM-6 combines an active seeker with over-the-horizon engagement capability.

Operators

Standard Missiles are deployed aboard Ticonderoga-class cruisers and Arleigh Burke-class destroyers of the US Navy and by allied navies including those of Japan, South Korea, Australia and Spain.
