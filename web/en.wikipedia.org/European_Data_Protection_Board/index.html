The European Data Protection Board (EDPB) is an independent European body established by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It began operating on 25 May 2018, replacing the Article 29 Working Party, and ensures the consistent application of data protection rules across the European Union.

Composition and tasks

The Board is composed of the heads of the national data protection authorities of the EU member states and the European Data Protection Supervisor. Its tasks include issuing guidelines, recommendations, and best practices on the interpretation of the GDPR; advising the European Commission; promoting cooperation and mutual assistance between national supervisory authorities; and adopting binding decisions in cross-border cases where national authorities disagree, under the GDPR's consistency mechanism.

Notable outputs include guidance on data protection impact assessments, international data transfers, and binding dispute-resolution decisions concerning enforcement against large technology companies, such as those relating to Meta's data transfers to the United States.
