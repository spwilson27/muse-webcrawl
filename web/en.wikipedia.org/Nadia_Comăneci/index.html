Nadia Comăneci

Nadia Elena Comăneci
Born: November 12, 1961, Onești, Romania
Sport: Artistic gymnastics
Olympic medals: 5 gold, 3 silver, 1 bronze
Known for: First perfect 10 in Olympic gymnastics (1976)

Nadia Elena Comăneci (born 1961) is a Romanian retired gymnast and a five-time Olympic gold medalist. At the 1976 Summer Olympics in Montreal, at age 14, she became the first gymnast ever awarded a perfect score of 10.0 at the Olympic Games, an achievement that transformed the sport's popularity worldwide.

Career

Comăneci was discovered by coach Béla Károlyi and trained at his school in Onești. At Montreal in 1976 she earned seven perfect 10s on the uneven bars and balance beam, winning gold in the all-around, balance beam, and uneven bars, plus team silver and floor bronze. The scoreboard, not programmed to display a 10, flashed 1.00 instead. She added two more golds at the 1980 Moscow Olympics, on balance beam and floor exercise, and won nine Olympic medals and four World Championship medals in total.

Later life

Comăneci retired in 1984. In November 1989, shortly before the Romanian Revolution, she defected to the United States. She later married American gymnast Bart Conner, the 1984 Olympic parallel bars champion; together they run a gymnastics academy in Oklahoma and are active in the Special Olympics and charitable work.
