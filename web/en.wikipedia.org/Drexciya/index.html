Drexciya

OriginDetroit, Michigan, U.S.
GenresElectro, Detroit techno
Years active1992–2002
MembersJames Stinson, Gerald Donald

Drexciya was an American electronic music duo from Detroit, Michigan, consisting of James Stinson (1969–2002) and Gerald Donald. Active in the 1990s and early 2000s, they are regarded as key figures in electro and Detroit techno, known for their fast, aquatic-sounding productions and for an elaborate fictional mythology.

Mythology

The duo's liner notes describe Drexciya as an underwater nation populated by the descendants of African women who were thrown overboard during the transatlantic slave trade and whose children adapted to breathe underwater. This Afrofuturist concept linked the duo's music to themes of diaspora, survival, and resistance, and has been studied extensively by cultural theorists.

Music

Drexciya released records anonymously on labels such as Underground Resistance, Shockwave, and Tresor, with titles referencing the sea and its creatures — Bubble Metropolis (1993), Aquatic Invasion (1995), The Quest (1997), and Neptune's Lair (1999). Their style combined electro bass, synthesizers, and drum machines with submerged, bubbling textures. After Stinson's death in 2002, Donald continued related projects including Dopplereffekt and Arpanet.
