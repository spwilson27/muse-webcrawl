Red admiral

The red admiral (Vanessa atalanta) is a well-known butterfly found throughout temperate Europe, Asia and North America. It has a wingspan of about 64–78 mm, black-brown wings with vivid red-orange bands and white spots near the forewing tips.

Red admirals are strong migrants, flying north in spring and south in autumn. Caterpillars feed chiefly on stinging nettle (Urtica dioica), each living alone in a tent folded from a leaf. Adults feed on flower nectar, sap and rotting fruit, and in mild regions may overwinter as adults.

The species is the type of the genus Vanessa, which also includes the painted lady. See also painted lady and Nymphalidae.
