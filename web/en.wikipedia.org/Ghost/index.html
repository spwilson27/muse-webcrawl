Ghost

In folklore, a ghost is the soul or spirit of a dead person or animal that is believed to be able to appear to the living. Descriptions of ghosts vary widely, from an invisible presence or a faint, wispy apparition to realistic, lifelike figures. Belief in ghosts — attempts to contact the spirits of the dead, known as necromancy or séances — is widespread across cultures and forms part of the broader belief in an afterlife and a spirit world.

Beliefs and typology

Ghosts are often thought to haunt places, objects, or people associated with their lives, and to linger because of unfinished business, violent death, or improper burial. Reported manifestations include apparitions, strange sounds, moving objects, and poltergeist phenomena. Many religions teach doctrines of the soul's survival after death, though mainstream Christian theology has generally held that apparitions of the dead are not the souls themselves. Animistic and ancestor-venerating cultures, by contrast, often incorporate spirits of the dead into everyday religious life.

History

Belief in ghosts dates to pre-literate societies and appears in ancient Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Greek, and Roman sources: the shade of Patroclus appears to Achilles in the Iliad, and Pliny the Younger recorded a haunted-house story in Athens. Ghost lore flourished in medieval Europe and in early modern literature — the ghost of Hamlet's father is among the most famous in drama. The nineteenth century saw the rise of Spiritualism, which claimed systematic communication with the dead, and psychical research, which attempted to investigate hauntings scientifically.

Scientific view

There is no scientific evidence that ghosts exist. Researchers attribute ghost experiences to sleep paralysis, suggestion, the ideomotor effect, infrasound, carbon monoxide poisoning, and the brain's tendency to detect agency and faces (pareidolia) in ambiguous stimuli. Ghost hunting and haunted locations remain popular in entertainment and tourism, including literature, film, and festivals such as Halloween and the Chinese Ghost Festival.
