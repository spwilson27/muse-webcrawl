The Javanshir clan (Azerbaijani: Cavanşirlər) was a Turkic clan of the Afshar tribe that rose to prominence in the Karabakh region of the Caucasus. Its most famous member, Panah Ali Khan Javanshir, founded the Karabakh Khanate in the mid-18th century and built the fortress-cities of Panahabad (Shusha) and Bayat.

History

The Javanshirs were one of the Qizilbash-associated clans settled in Karabakh and took their name from Javanshir, a district of the region. Under Panah Ali Khan and his son Ibrahim Khalil Khan, the khanate became the dominant power in the eastern Caucasus before being absorbed into the Russian Empire under the Treaty of Gulistan (1813) and Turkmenchay (1828).

Notable members

Descendants of the clan included the poet Khurshidbanu Natavan, daughter of the last khan of Karabakh, and numerous officers and cultural figures of the Russian imperial and later Azerbaijani periods.
