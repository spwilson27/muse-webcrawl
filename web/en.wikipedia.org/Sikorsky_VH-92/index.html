The Sikorsky VH-92 Patriot is an American helicopter developed by Sikorsky Aircraft for the United States presidential transport program. Derived from the Sikorsky S-92 medium-lift helicopter, the VH-92 was selected to replace the aging Marine One fleet of VH-3D Sea King and VH-60N White Hawk helicopters operated by Marine Helicopter Squadron One (HMX-1). When carrying the President of the United States, the aircraft uses the call sign "Marine One".

Development

The program followed the cancellation in 2009 of the earlier VH-71 Kestrel program, which had suffered major cost overruns. In May 2014, Sikorsky won the Presidential Helicopter Replacement Program contract with its S-92-based design, initially for six aircraft, with plans for a total fleet of 21 helicopters (23 including test and transition aircraft). The VH-92A first flew on 28 July 2017.

Entry into service

Testing revealed issues including scorching of the White House lawn by engine exhaust during landings. After modifications and further testing, President Joe Biden made the first presidential flight aboard a VH-92 in August 2024, travelling to Chicago.

Design

The VH-92 features an executive interior, military communications systems, defensive countermeasures, triple-redundant systems, and a rear cargo ramp. It seats up to 19 passengers and is powered by two General Electric CT7-8A turboshaft engines.
