Sweet Adelines International

Sweet Adelines International is a worldwide organisation of women singers committed to advancing the musical art form of barbershop harmony through education, competition and performance. Founded in Tulsa, Oklahoma, in 1945, it is one of the largest singing organisations for women in the world.

Activities

The organisation comprises hundreds of choruses and quartets across numerous countries, organised into regions. Annual regional competitions feed into an international convention and contest, where choruses and quartets are judged on sound, music, expression and showmanship.

Style

Members sing four-part a cappella harmony in the barbershop style — lead, tenor, baritone and bass — typically in colourful costumes with choreographed staging. Its male counterpart is the Barbershop Harmony Society.
