Canadian Army

The Canadian Army (French: Armée canadienne) is the command responsible for the operational readiness of Canada's land forces. It is one of three environmental commands of the Canadian Armed Forces, alongside the Royal Canadian Navy and the Royal Canadian Air Force. The Army comprises Regular Force and Primary Reserve units, Canadian Rangers, and civilian staff, totaling roughly 40,000–50,000 personnel.

History

Canada's land forces trace their origin to the militia of the colonial era and the permanent force created in 1871 after British garrisons withdrew. The Canadian Expeditionary Force fought in the First World War, winning renown at Vimy Ridge (1917). In the Second World War the Canadian Army grew to over 700,000 personnel, landing at Juno Beach on D-Day and liberating much of the Netherlands. Canadian soldiers later served in Korea, NATO Europe, peacekeeping missions, and the war in Afghanistan (2001–2014).

Structure

The Army is organized into divisions that are largely administrative and regional: the 2nd Canadian Division (Quebec), 3rd Canadian Division (Western Canada), 4th Canadian Division (Ontario), 5th Canadian Division (Atlantic Canada), and the Canadian Army Doctrine and Training Centre. Combat power rests in three Regular Force mechanized brigade groups (1, 2, and 5 CMBG) and ten Primary Reserve brigade groups. The Canadian Rangers patrol the Arctic and remote regions.

Equipment

Principal equipment includes Leopard 2 main battle tanks, LAV 6.0 infantry fighting vehicles, Coyote and TAPV armoured vehicles, M777 howitzers, and a range of small arms including the C7 rifle. The Army has been undergoing recapitalization of its logistics vehicles, artillery, and ground-based air defence.

Roles

The Army defends Canada, contributes to North American defence through NORAD-linked arrangements, deploys on NATO and coalition operations (including the enhanced Forward Presence battlegroup in Latvia), and supports civil authorities in domestic emergencies such as floods, ice storms, and wildfires under Operation LENTUS.
