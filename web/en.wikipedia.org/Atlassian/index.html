Atlassian Corporation is an Australian-American software company that develops products for software developers, project managers, and other software development teams. Founded in Sydney in 2002 by Mike Cannon-Brookes and Scott Farquhar, it is best known for Jira, an issue-tracking and project-management tool, and Confluence, a team collaboration and documentation platform.

History

The founders bootstrapped the company with a credit card debt of about A$10,000 rather than venture funding, growing it profitably for years before taking investment. The company's name is derived from the Titan Atlas of Greek mythology, reflecting the goal of supporting customers' work. Atlassian listed on the NASDAQ in December 2015 in one of the most successful technology IPOs of the year. Its legal domicile later moved from the United Kingdom to the United States.

Products and acquisitions

Alongside Jira and Confluence, Atlassian's products include Bitbucket (Git code hosting), Jira Service Management, Compass, and Loom (video messaging, acquired in 2023). Major acquisitions include Trello, the visual project-management app, bought in 2017 for about US$425 million, and Statuspage. The company acquired and later discontinued the chat tool HipChat, migrating users to Slack through a partnership. Atlassian has been a prominent adopter of remote work, announcing a permanent "Team Anywhere" policy in 2020 under which employees may work from home in many countries.

Business model

Atlassian is noted for selling software without a traditional commissioned sales force, relying instead on low starting prices, free tiers, word of mouth and a marketplace of third-party apps. It serves hundreds of thousands of customers, including most large enterprises.
