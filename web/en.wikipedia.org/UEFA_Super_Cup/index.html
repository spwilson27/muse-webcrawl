UEFA Super Cup

Founded1972
ContestantsWinners of the Champions League and Europa League

The UEFA Super Cup is an annual football match organised by UEFA and contested by the reigning champions of the two main European club competitions, the UEFA Champions League and the UEFA Europa League. It is generally played in August and is regarded as the curtain-raiser for the European club season.

History

The competition was created in 1972; originally the Champions League winners faced the winners of the now-defunct Cup Winners' Cup, and early editions were played over two legs. Since 1998 it has been a single match at a neutral venue, staged for many years in Monaco before rotating among host cities.

Records

AC Milan, Barcelona and Real Madrid are among the most successful clubs in the competition's history, each with multiple titles.
