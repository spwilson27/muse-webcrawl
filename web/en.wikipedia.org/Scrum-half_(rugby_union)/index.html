Scrum-half (rugby union)

Scrum-half

Sport: Rugby union

Position number: 9

Unit: Backs (half-backs)

The scrum-half is a position in rugby union played by the number 9. The scrum-half forms the link between the forwards and the backs, feeding the ball into the scrum, retrieving it from scrums, rucks and mauls, and distributing it to the fly-half or other backs to launch attacks.

Role

At the scrum, the scrum-half introduces the ball and then collects it at the base. Around the field, the scrum-half provides quick service from rucks and mauls, varying play with box kicks, sniping runs around the fringe and short passes. Defensively, the scrum-half covers kicks and organises the defensive line.

Characteristics

Scrum-halves are typically among the smallest players on the team, prized for fast, accurate passing, decision-making, a strong kicking game and agility. Famous scrum-halves include Gareth Edwards of Wales, Joost van der Westhuizen of South Africa, George Gregan of Australia and Antoine Dupont of France.
