Eritrean War of Independence

The Eritrean War of Independence was a conflict fought between Ethiopian governments and Eritrean separatist organisations from 1 September 1961 to 24 May 1991. It ended with Eritrea achieving de facto independence.

Background

Eritrea was an Italian colony from 1890 to 1941, then under British administration until 1952, when it was federated with Ethiopia under a UN resolution. Emperor Haile Selassie dissolved the federation in 1962 and annexed Eritrea as a province, sparking armed resistance led first by the Eritrean Liberation Front (ELF) and later the Eritrean People's Liberation Front (EPLF).

The war

The war continued through the Ethiopian Civil War and the rule of the Derg. Key events included the Battle of Afabet in 1988, one of the largest battles in African history, where the EPLF defeated a major Ethiopian offensive. In May 1991 the EPLF captured Asmara, the capital.

Aftermath

A referendum in April 1993 produced an overwhelming vote for independence, and Eritrea was recognised internationally on 24 May 1993, with Isaias Afwerki as its first president.
