ARD (broadcaster)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

ARD is the joint organisation of Germany's regional public broadcasters.

ARD (German: Arbeitsgemeinschaft der öffentlich-rechtlichen Rundfunkanstalten der Bundesrepublik Deutschland, "Working group of public broadcasters of the Federal Republic of Germany") is a joint organisation of Germany's regional public service broadcasters. It was founded in 1950 in West Germany to represent the common interests of the country's de-centralised public broadcasting services — in particular, to maintain a joint television network.

History

ARD was founded on 5 August 1950 by the regional broadcasting corporations of the Federal Republic of Germany. Its joint television channel, Deutsches Fernsehen (now Das Erste), began broadcasting on 25 December 1952. After German reunification in 1990, the East German broadcasters were dissolved and new regional corporations — Mitteldeutscher Rundfunk (MDR) and Ostdeutscher Rundfunk Brandenburg (ORB) — joined ARD.

Members

ARD's members are nine regional broadcasting corporations:

  • Bayerischer Rundfunk (BR), Bavaria
  • Hessischer Rundfunk (hr), Hesse
  • Mitteldeutscher Rundfunk (MDR), Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt and Thuringia
  • Norddeutscher Rundfunk (NDR), northern Germany
  • Radio Bremen (RB)
  • Rundfunk Berlin-Brandenburg (rbb)
  • Saarländischer Rundfunk (SR), Saarland
  • Südwestrundfunk (SWR), Baden-Württemberg and Rhineland-Palatinate
  • Westdeutscher Rundfunk (WDR), North Rhine-Westphalia

ARD also operates Deutsche Welle, Germany's international broadcaster, which is a member of ARD in its own right, and maintains a network of foreign studios and correspondents, one of the largest of any broadcaster.

Channels and services

ARD's flagship channel is Das Erste ("The First"), Germany's first national television channel, produced jointly by the member broadcasters. Each member also operates regional television channels (the "Dritten", or third channels) and multiple radio networks. Joint channels operated with ZDF and other partners include arte, Phoenix, KiKA, and 3sat. ARD's flagship news programme is the Tagesschau, Germany's most-watched news broadcast, produced by ARD-aktuell in Hamburg.

Funding

Like all German public broadcasting, ARD is funded primarily by the Rundfunkbeitrag, a licence fee charged per household, supplemented by limited advertising. The de-centralised structure of ARD reflects the constitutional responsibility of Germany's federal states (Länder) for broadcasting and culture.
