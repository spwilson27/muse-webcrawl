Captain (armed forces)

Captain is a commissioned officer rank in many militaries and uniformed services worldwide. The meaning of the rank varies between services: in most armies and air forces a captain (NATO code OF-2) commands a company or equivalent unit, ranking above lieutenant and below major; in most navies, a captain (OF-5) is a senior officer commanding a large ship, ranking well above the army captain.

Land forces

In the British Army and United States Army, a captain is typically the officer commanding a company of roughly 100–200 soldiers, or a specialist in a staff appointment. The insignia is commonly three pips or bars.

Naval forces

A naval captain commands a major warship such as a cruiser, carrier or large amphibious ship; the commanding officer of any vessel is also addressed as "captain" regardless of rank. The naval rank corresponds to an army colonel.
