Dermatoglyphics

Dermatoglyphics (from Greek derma, "skin", and glyphe, "carving") is the scientific study of fingerprints and patterns of ridges on the skin of fingers, palms, toes and soles.

Patterns

Fingertip patterns are classed as arches, loops and whorls. Ridges form during fetal development and remain unchanged through life, making them useful in identification.

Medical associations

Certain pattern frequencies are associated with chromosomal conditions such as Down syndrome; the term was coined by Harold Cummins in 1926. Dermatoglyphics is distinct from the discredited practice of palmistry.
