ESPN+

ESPN+ is an American over-the-top subscription video streaming service available in the United States, owned by Disney Streaming, a division of the Walt Disney Company, with Hearst Communications holding a minority interest through its stake in ESPN Inc. Launched on 12 April 2018, it was built on technology from BAMTech (later Disney Streaming Services) and was Disney's first direct-to-consumer streaming product.

Content

ESPN+ offers live events and original programming not carried on ESPN's linear channels, including out-of-market and exclusive games from leagues such as the NHL, MLB, MLS (before its move to Apple), UFC (including exclusive pay-per-view distribution in the US), PGA Tour Live, top-flight European football leagues and cups at various times, college sports, boxing and tennis. It also carries studio shows, documentaries including the 30 for 30 library, and written analysis formerly under the ESPN Insider brand. Subscribers historically could not access the main ESPN linear channels without a separate television subscription, a distinction that changed with the launch of ESPN's full direct-to-consumer service in 2025, into which ESPN+ content was folded.

Subscribers

The service grew from about 1 million subscribers in its first five months to a peak of roughly 25 million in the early 2020s, before declining as rights portfolios shifted. It has been sold both standalone and bundled with Disney+ and Hulu.
