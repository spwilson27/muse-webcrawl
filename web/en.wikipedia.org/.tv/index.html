.tv
Introduced: 18 March 1996
Status: Active
Registry: GoDaddy Registry
Intended use: Entities connected with Tuvalu
Actual use: Television and video sites worldwide

.tv is the Internet country code top-level domain (ccTLD) for Tuvalu, a Polynesian island nation in the Pacific Ocean. Because "tv" is also the abbreviation for television, the domain has been heavily marketed internationally for entertainment and streaming websites.

History

The domain was delegated in 1996. In 1998 the Tuvaluan government licensed it to a Canadian entrepreneur, and later agreements with Verisign and GoDaddy brought significant revenue to Tuvalu — at times a substantial share of government income — funding infrastructure and the country's membership of the United Nations in 2000.

Use

Any person or company worldwide may register a .tv domain. Popular users include streaming platform Twitch (twitch.tv). Revenue from leases and auctions of premium names remains an important source of income for Tuvalu, whose existence is threatened by sea-level rise.
