Ole Einar Bjørndalen (born 27 January 1974) is a retired Norwegian professional biathlete, often nicknamed the "King of Biathlon". With 13 Winter Olympic medals, he is the most decorated male Winter Olympian of all time.

Career

Bjørndalen competed at six Winter Olympics from 1994 to 2014. At the 2002 Salt Lake City Games he swept all four men's biathlon events, a feat unmatched in the sport. At Sochi 2014, aged 40, he won two further golds, surpassing Bjørn Dæhlie's record for Winter Olympic medals. He won 95 World Cup races and six overall World Cup titles, and also competed successfully in cross-country skiing, including a World Cup freestyle victory in 2006.

Later life

He retired in 2018 and later served as head coach of the Chinese biathlon team ahead of the Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics. He is married to Belarusian biathlete Darya Domracheva.
