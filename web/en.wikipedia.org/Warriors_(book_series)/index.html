Warriors (book series)

AuthorErin Hunter
PublisherHarperCollins
First bookInto the Wild (2003)
GenreChildren's fantasy

Warriors is a series of children's fantasy novels published by HarperCollins and written by authors under the collective pseudonym Erin Hunter. The series follows clans of feral cats living in a forest and later by a lake, struggling for territory, prey and survival.

Overview

The first arc, The Prophecies Begin (2003–2004), follows Rusty, a house cat who joins ThunderClan and rises to become its leader, Firestar. Subsequent arcs — The New Prophecy, Power of Three, Omen of the Stars, Dawn of the Clans, A Vision of Shadows and more — follow new generations of cats, with themes of loyalty, prophecy and war between the Clans and their starry ancestors, StarClan.

Reception

The series has sold tens of millions of copies worldwide and spawned Super Editions, novellas, manga, and a large fan community.
