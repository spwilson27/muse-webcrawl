Nilakantha Somayaji

Keḷallur Nīlakaṇṭha Somayāji (1444–1544) was an Indian mathematician and astronomer of the Kerala school, a student of Damodara and son of the tradition founded by Madhava of Sangamagrama.

Works

His Tantrasamgraha (1501) is a comprehensive treatise on mathematical astronomy in eight chapters, presenting a planetary model in which the inner planets and later planets orbit the Sun while the Sun orbits the Earth — a geo-heliocentric system similar in important respects to that later proposed by Tycho Brahe. His commentary Jyotirmimamsa discusses the rationale and testing of astronomical models, and the Aryabhatiya-bhashya is an extensive commentary on Aryabhata. Works of the Kerala school, including series for π and trigonometric functions, are presented and extended in his writings.
