Dave Smith (engineer)

David Joseph Smith (2 April 1950 – 31 May 2022) was an American engineer and founder of Sequential Circuits (later Sequential), a pioneering synthesizer company. He designed the Prophet-5 (1978), the first microprocessor-based musical instrument and the first synthesizer with fully programmable memory, and co-developed the MIDI specification.

Career

Smith founded Sequential Circuits in 1974. After the company was acquired by Yamaha in 1987, he worked at Yamaha and Korg, where he contributed to the Wavestation synthesizer. In 2002 he founded Dave Smith Instruments, producing instruments such as the Evolver and Prophet '08; the company reacquired the Sequential name in 2015 and released the Prophet-6 and the revived Prophet-5. Smith received a Grammy Award for technical achievement for his role in developing MIDI.
