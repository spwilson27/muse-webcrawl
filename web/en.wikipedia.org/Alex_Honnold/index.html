Alex Honnold

Alexander Honnold (born August 17, 1985) is an American rock climber best known for his free solo ascents of big walls, most notably the first free solo ascent of El Capitan in Yosemite National Park in 2017.

Career

Honnold began climbing in gyms as a child and dropped out of UC Berkeley to pursue climbing, living for years in his van. He first gained wide attention in 2008 with free solo ascents of the Regular Northwest Face of Half Dome and the Moonlight Buttress in Zion.

On June 3, 2017, he free-soloed the Freerider route on El Capitan (about 900 m) in just under four hours, a feat described as one of the greatest athletic achievements of any kind. The climb was documented in the film Free Solo (2018), which won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature.

With Tommy Caldwell, Honnold also set a speed record on the Nose of El Capitan in 2018, climbing it in under two hours.

Philanthropy

Honnold founded the Honnold Foundation in 2012, which supports solar energy projects for disadvantaged communities, and has pledged a substantial share of his income to it. His memoir, Alone on the Wall, was published in 2015.
