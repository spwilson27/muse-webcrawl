DreamHack is a Swedish gaming brand specializing in esports tournaments and large-scale gaming conventions, including the world's largest LAN parties. Originating as a small gathering of schoolmates swapping files in a school cafeteria in Malung, Sweden, in 1994, it grew into major festivals held across Europe, North America, and elsewhere.

History

DreamHack began in 1994 as a demoparty held in an elementary school cafeteria. It later moved to the Elmia exhibition centre in Jönköping, where DreamHack Summer and DreamHack Winter became its flagship events. DreamHack Winter 2004 was recognized as one of the largest LAN parties in the world, and subsequent events set records for network traffic and unique devices connected. In 2015 DreamHack was acquired by Modern Times Group and later merged into the ESL FACEIT Group structure following the combination of ESL and DreamHack under MTG, and subsequently the 2022 acquisition by Savvy Games Group.

Events

Events combine bring-your-own-computer (BYOC) LAN areas with professional esports competitions in titles such as Counter-Strike, StarCraft II, League of Legends, Dota 2, and fighting games, along with cosplay, concerts, expos, and speedrunning. Festivals have been held in cities including Atlanta, Dallas, Anaheim, Montreal, Leipzig, Valencia, Hyderabad, Melbourne, and Stockholm.
