LEED

LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) is a green building certification program developed by the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC). It is the most widely used green building rating system in the world.

DeveloperU.S. Green Building Council
LevelsCertified, Silver, Gold, Platinum
Launched1998 pilot / 2000

Rating system

Projects earn points across categories including sustainable sites, water efficiency, energy and atmosphere, materials and resources, indoor environmental quality and innovation. Totals determine certification level.

Versions

Major versions include LEED v2, v3 (2009), v4 and v4.1, tightening energy and materials requirements. See green building and sustainable architecture.

Criticism

Critics note certified buildings do not always perform as modelled, and point-based design can reward visible features over measured performance.
