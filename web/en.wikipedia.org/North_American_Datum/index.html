North American Datum

The North American Datum (NAD) is the horizontal datum used to define the geodetic network in North America. A datum is a formal description of the shape of the Earth together with an anchor point for coordinate systems. Successive versions include the North American Datum of 1927 (NAD 27), the North American Datum of 1983 (NAD 83), and later refinements produced with satellite geodesy and the Continuously Operating Reference Stations (CORS) network.

NAD 27

NAD 27 was based on the Clarke ellipsoid of 1866, with its origin at Meades Ranch in Kansas, a point chosen because it lay near the geographic centre of the contiguous United States. Coordinates were computed from a continent-wide triangulation network built up from surveys beginning in the 19th century. Systematic distortions accumulated in the network, so positions could be in error by tens of metres.

NAD 83

NAD 83, released in 1986, was computed from about 250,000 survey points including satellite Doppler observations. It uses the Geodetic Reference System 1980 (GRS 80) ellipsoid, which is geocentric — centred on the Earth's centre of mass — rather than anchored at a surface point. NAD 83 forms the basis of the National Spatial Reference System maintained by the National Geodetic Survey. Later adjustments, such as HARN and NAD 83 (2011), refined coordinates using GPS observations.

Differences and use

The shift between NAD 27 and NAD 83 coordinates at a given point varies across the continent and can exceed 100 metres in places. Map users converting between datums must apply a transformation such as the NADCON grids published by the National Geodetic Survey. NAD 83 coordinates are also closely related to, though not identical with, the global WGS 84 frame used by GPS; the two frames drift apart slowly because NAD 83 is fixed to the North American tectonic plate while WGS 84 follows the global frame.

Future datum

The National Geodetic Survey has planned a modernised National Spatial Reference System with a new terrestrial reference frame to replace NAD 83, designed around GPS and gravimetric geoid models, with release expected in the mid-2020s.
