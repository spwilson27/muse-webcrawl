Mediazona
Type: Online newspaper
Founded: September 2014
Founders: Maria Alyokhina and Nadezhda Tolokonnikova (Pussy Riot)
Editor: Sergey Smirnov
Focus: Russian courts, policing, and the penal system

Mediazona (Russian: Медиазона) is a Russian independent online media outlet founded in 2014 by members of the protest art group Pussy Riot. It specialises in covering Russia's judicial, law-enforcement, and penitentiary systems — trials, police violence, prison conditions, and political prosecutions.

History

Maria Alyokhina and Nadezhda Tolokonnikova founded Mediazona after their release from prison in 2013, drawing on their experience of the Russian penal system; journalist Sergey Smirnov became editor-in-chief. The outlet built a reputation for meticulous court reporting and data projects, including counts of police violence and, after 2022, documentation of Russian military casualties and trials connected with the invasion of Ukraine.

Repression

Russian authorities designated Mediazona a "foreign agent" in 2021; Smirnov was jailed for 25 days over a retweet. After Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, the site was blocked in Russia, and Smirnov and much of the team continued publishing from exile. Sister projects cover Belarus and Central Asia.
