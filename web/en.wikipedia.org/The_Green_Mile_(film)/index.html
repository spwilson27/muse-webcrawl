The Green Mile is a 1999 American fantasy drama film written and directed by Frank Darabont and based on Stephen King's 1996 serial novel of the same name. Tom Hanks stars as Paul Edgecomb, a death row corrections officer in a Louisiana penitentiary during the Great Depression, and Michael Clarke Duncan as John Coffey, a gentle, enormous prisoner convicted of a terrible crime who displays a mysterious supernatural gift for healing.

Plot

Narrated in flashback by the elderly Edgecomb, the film recounts life on E Block — nicknamed "the Green Mile" for its green linoleum floor — including Coffey's healings, the antics of the mouse Mr. Jingles, and the cruelty of guard Percy Wetmore, as Edgecomb comes to doubt Coffey's guilt.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing over 90 million worldwide, and was nominated for four Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Supporting Actor for Duncan.
