Nyad is a 2023 American biographical sports drama film about the long-distance swimmer Diana Nyad and her attempts, in her sixties, to swim from Cuba to Florida. Annette Bening plays Nyad and Jodie Foster plays her coach and friend Bonnie Stumpf; both received Academy Award nominations for their performances.

Background

The film is based on Nyad's memoir Find a Way. After a failed attempt in 1978, Nyad returned to the challenge in 2011 and, on her fifth attempt, completed the roughly 180 km swim from Havana to Key West in September 2013, at age 64 — though the swim's ratification was later debated within the marathon swimming community.

Production

Nyad was the narrative feature debut of documentary filmmakers Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi and Jimmy Chin, known for Free Solo. It premiered at the Telluride Film Festival before streaming on Netflix.
