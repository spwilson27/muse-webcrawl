Wankhede Stadium

Wankhede Stadium (Marathi: वानखेडे स्टेडियम) is an international cricket stadium in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. The stadium now has a capacity of 33,108, following renovations for the 2011 Cricket World Cup. Before the upgrade, the capacity was approximately 45,000. The stadium is owned and operated by the Mumbai Cricket Association (MCA).

History

The stadium was built in 1974 after a dispute between the MCA and the Bombay Gymkhana over ticket allocations, and is named after S. K. Wankhede, a politician and president of the MCA. It hosted its first Test match in 1975, between India and the West Indies.

Notable matches

Wankhede Stadium hosted the final of the 2011 Cricket World Cup, in which India defeated Sri Lanka, with captain M. S. Dhoni hitting the winning six. It was also the venue for Sachin Tendulkar's final international match in 2013 and Ravi Shastri's record six sixes in an over in first-class cricket in 1985. It is the home ground of the Mumbai Indians of the Indian Premier League.
