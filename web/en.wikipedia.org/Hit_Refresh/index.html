Hit Refresh: The Quest to Rediscover Microsoft's Soul and Imagine a Better Future for Everyone is a 2017 memoir by Satya Nadella, the third chief executive officer of Microsoft. The title plays on the browser refresh button as a metaphor for personal and corporate renewal: not discarding everything, but selectively updating one's beliefs and culture.

Content

The book weaves together three strands: Nadella's personal story, including his childhood in Hyderabad, India, his love of cricket, and raising a son with cerebral palsy, which he credits with teaching him empathy; his account of becoming CEO in 2014 and shifting Microsoft's culture from "know-it-alls" to "learn-it-alls"; and reflections on emerging technologies — artificial intelligence, mixed reality, and quantum computing — and their societal implications.

Reception

Hit Refresh received generally positive reviews for its candor and its emphasis on empathy and growth mindset, concepts drawn in part from psychologist Carol Dweck's work. Proceeds from the book were directed to Microsoft Philanthropies. Bill Gates contributed a foreword praising the account.
