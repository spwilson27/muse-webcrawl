List of largest power stations

This is a list of the largest power stations in the world, ranked by installed generating capacity. The largest power station in the world is the Three Gorges Dam in China, a hydroelectric station with an installed capacity of 22,500 MW. Hydroelectric stations dominate the top of the list; the largest non-hydro station is the Kashiwazaki-Kariwa nuclear power plant in Japan.

Largest by type

Nuclear

  • Kashiwazaki-Kariwa, Japan — 7,965 MW, the largest nuclear power plant in the world (largely offline since 2011).
  • Bruce Nuclear Generating Station, Canada — about 6,384 MW.
  • Hanul Nuclear Power Plant, South Korea — about 5,928 MW.
  • Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant, Ukraine — 5,700 MW, the largest in Europe.
  • Gravelines Nuclear Power Station, France — 5,460 MW.

Coal

  • Taichung Power Plant, Taiwan — 5,500 MW.
  • Bełchatów Power Station, Poland — 5,102 MW, Europe's largest coal plant.
  • Vindhyachal Thermal Power Station, India — 4,760 MW.
  • Mundra Thermal Power Station, India — 4,620 MW.
  • Tuoketuo Power Station, China — 5,400 MW (after expansion phases).

Natural gas

  • Surgut-2 Power Station, Russia — 5,657 MW, the largest gas-fired station in the world.
  • Futtsu Power Station, Japan — about 5,040 MW.
  • Kawagoe Power Station, Japan — 4,802 MW.
  • Burullus Power Plant, Egypt — 4,800 MW.

Solar and wind

  • Bhadla Solar Park, India — about 2,245 MW, among the largest solar parks in the world.
  • Pavagada Solar Park, India — about 2,050 MW.
  • Gansu Wind Farm (Jiuquan), China — the largest wind farm complex, exceeding 7,965 MW installed toward a planned 20,000 MW.
  • Hornsea Wind Farm, United Kingdom — the largest offshore wind farm, about 1,754 MW (Phases 1 and 2).
