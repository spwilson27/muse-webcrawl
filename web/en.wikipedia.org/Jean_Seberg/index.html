Jean Dorothy Seberg (1938–1979) was an American actress who became an icon of the French New Wave. Chosen from thousands of candidates by Otto Preminger to play Joan of Arc in Saint Joan (1957), she achieved lasting fame as Patricia, the pixie-haired heroine opposite Jean-Paul Belmondo in Jean-Luc Godard's Breathless (1960).

Career

Seberg divided her career between Hollywood films such as Lilith (1964) and Paint Your Wagon (1969) and European productions, becoming a muse of French cinema. She was briefly married to writer Romain Gary, with whom she had a son.

FBI targeting and death

Because of her support for civil-rights causes and the Black Panther Party, Seberg was targeted by the FBI's COINTELPRO programme, which surveilled her and planted a false press story about her pregnancy in 1970; her child died shortly after birth. Her mental health deteriorated, and she was found dead in a car in Paris in 1979, ruled a probable suicide. Her story was dramatised in the 2019 film Seberg.
