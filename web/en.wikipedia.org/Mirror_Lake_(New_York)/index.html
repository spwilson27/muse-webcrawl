Mirror Lake (New York)

LocationLake Placid, Essex County, New York
Surface area≈124 acres (50 ha)
Surface elevation≈1,854 ft (565 m)
OutflowChubb River (to Lake Placid)

Mirror Lake is a small lake in the Adirondack Mountains of northern New York, located in the village of Lake Placid in Essex County. The village centre sits on its eastern shore, and the lake takes its name from the still reflections of the surrounding mountains — including Whiteface Mountain — visible on its surface on calm days.

Mirror Lake is the centrepiece of the village of Lake Placid, host of the 1932 and 1980 Winter Olympic Games. Motorboats are prohibited, so the lake is used for swimming, paddling and, in winter, skating, dog-sled rides and tobogganing on its ice. It is the start and swim venue of the Lake Placid Ironman triathlon. The lake drains through a short outlet into the much larger neighbouring Lake Placid, the body of water from which the village actually takes its name.

Ecology

The lake's watershed is small and largely developed, making it sensitive to road-salt runoff and stormwater. Local monitoring groups track its water quality, and studies have documented rising chloride levels and efforts to reduce salt use on surrounding roads to protect the lake's cold-water ecology.
