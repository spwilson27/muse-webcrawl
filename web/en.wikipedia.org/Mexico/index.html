Mexico, officially the United Mexican States, is a country in the southern portion of North America. It is bordered to the north by the United States, to the south and west by the Pacific Ocean, to the southeast by Guatemala, Belize, and the Caribbean Sea, and to the east by the Gulf of Mexico. Mexico covers almost 2 million square kilometres and, with about 130 million inhabitants, is the world's most populous Spanish-speaking country.

History

Pre-Columbian Mexico was home to advanced civilizations including the Olmec, Maya, Zapotec, Teotihuacan, Toltec, and Aztec (Mexica), whose capital Tenochtitlan was one of the largest cities in the world. Hernán Cortés and his indigenous allies conquered the Aztec Empire in 1519–1521, beginning three centuries of rule as the Viceroyalty of New Spain. Independence was declared by Miguel Hidalgo in 1810 (the Grito de Dolores) and achieved in 1821. The 19th century saw the loss of Texas and, after the Mexican–American War (1846–1848), roughly half of Mexico's territory to the United States; the French intervention installed Maximilian I briefly (1864–1867) before Benito Juárez restored the republic. The Mexican Revolution (1910–1920) reshaped the country under the Constitution of 1917, and the Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI) governed for most of the 20th century.

Geography and culture

Mexico's terrain includes the Sierra Madre mountain ranges, the high central plateau, deserts in the north, and tropical rainforests in the south; it is one of the world's megadiverse countries. Its culture blends Indigenous and Spanish influences, expressed in cuisine (recognized by UNESCO), mariachi music, the Day of the Dead, and muralist art. Mexico has more UNESCO World Heritage Sites than any country in the Americas.

Economy and government

Mexico is a federal presidential republic of 31 states and Mexico City. It has one of the world's 15 largest economies, with major manufacturing, petroleum, tourism, and remittance sectors, and is a party to the USMCA trade agreement with the United States and Canada. Notable challenges include organized-crime violence, corruption, and income inequality.
