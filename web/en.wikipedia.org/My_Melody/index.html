My Melody

My Melody (Japanese: マイメロディ) is a fictional character produced by the Japanese company Sanrio, created in 1975. She is a white rabbit who always wears a pink or red hood, and she is one of Sanrio's most enduring characters alongside Hello Kitty.

Character

My Melody was born in the forest of Mariland. She lives with her family — her kind mother, her father, her mischievous younger brother Rhythm, and her grandparents, who told her the stories that sent her on adventures. Her best friend is a mouse named Flat. Her treasure is a handmade hood sewn by her grandmother, and she loves baking cookies and eating almond pound cake. Her birthday is January 18.

History and media

The character first appeared as a Little Red Riding Hood-inspired design and was originally marketed simply as a white rabbit; the name My Melody was adopted in 1976. She has been a mainstay of Sanrio merchandise for decades and stars in media including the anime series Onegai My Melody (2005), in which her cheerful rival Kuromi — originally an antagonist — became a popular character in her own right. My Melody and Kuromi later co-starred in the stop-motion Netflix series My Melody & Kuromi (2025).
