Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) is a private research university in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, United States. Founded in 1900 by the industrialist and philanthropist Andrew Carnegie as the Carnegie Technical Schools, the university became the Carnegie Institute of Technology in 1912 and merged with the Mellon Institute of Industrial Research in 1967 to form Carnegie Mellon University.

History

Carnegie founded the school to provide practical technical education to the children of Pittsburgh's steel workers. Over the twentieth century the institution expanded into a comprehensive research university. The merger with the Mellon Institute, founded by Andrew and Richard Mellon, consolidated its strengths in science and engineering. CMU established one of the first computer science departments in the United States (1965) and its School of Computer Science remains among the most highly ranked in the world.

Academics

The university comprises seven colleges and schools, including the College of Engineering, the School of Computer Science, the Tepper School of Business, the College of Fine Arts, the Dietrich College of Humanities and Social Sciences, the Mellon College of Science, and the Heinz College of Information Systems and Public Policy. CMU is particularly renowned for computer science, robotics, artificial intelligence, engineering, drama and design. The Robotics Institute, founded in 1979, is one of the largest robotics research organizations in the world.

CMU faculty and alumni include numerous Turing Award winners, Nobel laureates and MacArthur fellows. The university operates additional campuses and degree programmes, including a campus in Doha, Qatar (Carnegie Mellon University in Qatar, established 2004), and sites in Silicon Valley and Africa.

Campus and traditions

The main campus occupies about 157 acres in Pittsburgh's Oakland and Squirrel Hill neighborhoods, adjacent to the University of Pittsburgh. Distinctive traditions include the painting of "the Fence", Spring Carnival with its Buggy races (Sweepstakes), and the Scotty dog mascot. The university's motto, taken from Carnegie's words, is "My heart is in the work".
