German Empire

German Empire (Deutsches Kaiserreich)
1871–1918
Capital: Berlin
Emperors: Wilhelm I, Friedrich III, Wilhelm II
Chancellor (first): Otto von Bismarck
Currency: Goldmark

The German Empire (Deutsches Kaiserreich), also known as the Second Reich, was the German nation state that existed from the unification of Germany in 1871 until the November Revolution in 1918, when it changed its form of government from a monarchy to a republic.

Unification

The empire was proclaimed on 18 January 1871 in the Hall of Mirrors at the Palace of Versailles, during the Franco-Prussian War. The architect of unification was Otto von Bismarck, minister president of Prussia, whose wars against Denmark (1864), Austria (1866) and France (1870–71) brought the German states together under Prussian leadership, excluding Austria. Wilhelm I of Prussia became German Emperor (Kaiser).

The Bismarck era

Bismarck served as Imperial Chancellor until 1890. Domestically he fought the Catholic Church in the Kulturkampf and later the Social Democrats through the Anti-Socialist Laws, while simultaneously creating Europe's first modern welfare state with health, accident and old-age insurance. In foreign policy he built a web of alliances designed to isolate France and preserve peace in Europe. The empire industrialised rapidly, overtaking Britain in steel and chemicals, and after 1884 acquired colonies in Africa and the Pacific.

Wilhelm II and the road to war

Wilhelm II dismissed Bismarck in 1890 and pursued a more assertive Weltpolitik, building a battle fleet under Admiral Tirpitz that antagonised Britain. Germany's alliance with Austria-Hungary and Italy faced the Entente of France, Russia and Britain. Following the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in June 1914, Germany gave Austria-Hungary unconditional support, and the resulting July Crisis led to the First World War.

Collapse

Four years of attrition, blockade and the failure of the 1918 Spring Offensive brought military defeat and revolution. On 9 November 1918 Wilhelm II abdicated and a republic was proclaimed; the armistice followed two days later. The empire was succeeded by the Weimar Republic, and the Treaty of Versailles (1919) stripped Germany of its colonies and border territories.
