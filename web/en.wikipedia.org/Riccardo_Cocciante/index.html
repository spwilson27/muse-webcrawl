Riccardo Cocciante (born 20 February 1946), also known in French-speaking countries as Richard Cocciante, is an Italian and French singer-songwriter, composer and music-theatre writer born in Saigon, French Indochina, to an Italian father and a French mother.

Career

Raised in Rome, Cocciante achieved fame in Italy in the 1970s with songs such as "Bella senz'anima" and "Margherita", and in France with "Le coup de soleil". He won the Sanremo Music Festival in 1991 with "Se stiamo insieme". With lyricist Luc Plamondon he composed the hugely successful musical Notre-Dame de Paris (1998), based on Victor Hugo's novel, which has been staged worldwide. His later stage works include Giulietta e Romeo and Le Petit Prince.
