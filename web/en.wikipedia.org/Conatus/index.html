Conatus

Conatus is a term used in early modern philosophy for an innate inclination of a thing to continue to exist and enhance itself. The concept appears in the work of Descartes, Hobbes, Leibniz, and most famously Spinoza.

Spinoza

In Spinoza's Ethics, each thing strives (conatur) to persevere in its being, and this striving is the actual essence of the thing. Human desire is conatus conscious of itself, forming the basis of his account of the affects.

Influence

Conatus has been compared with later ideas of self-preservation and homeostasis, and it remains a central topic in Spinoza scholarship.
