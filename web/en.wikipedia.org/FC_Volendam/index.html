Football Club Volendam is a professional football club based in Volendam, North Holland, Netherlands. Founded in 1920 as Victoria, the club is famed for developing its own local talent from the fishing village of Volendam and for its many promotions and relegations between the Eredivisie and the Eerste Divisie, which earned it the reputation of a classic "yo-yo club".

History

The club was founded on 1 June 1920 and turned professional in the 1950s. Volendam reached the KNVB Cup final in 1958 and 1995, losing on both occasions. The club's academy has produced numerous internationals, and Volendam players are known for coming almost entirely from the village itself, an unusual feature in modern football. Wim Jonk, a Volendam product who later played for Ajax and Inter Milan, managed the club in the 2020s and led it to promotion to the Eredivisie in 2022.

Identity

The team plays in orange shirts, giving rise to the nickname "Het Andere Oranje" — the other Orange — in reference to the Netherlands national team. Home matches are played at the Kras Stadion, close to the harbour of the village.
