Endeavor (company)

Endeavor Group Holdings, Inc.
Type: Private
Industry: Talent agency; sports; entertainment
Founded: 2009 (as William Morris Endeavor)
Headquarters: Beverly Hills, California, U.S.
Key people: Ari Emanuel (CEO), Patrick Whitesell (executive chairman)

Endeavor Group Holdings, Inc. is an American holding company for talent and media agencies with its primary offices in Beverly Hills, California. It is best known as the parent company of the WME talent agency (formerly William Morris Endeavor) and for its former majority ownership of the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) through TKO Group Holdings.

History

Endeavor was formed in 2009 from the merger of the William Morris Agency and the Endeavor Agency, creating one of the largest talent agencies in the world. The company expanded through acquisitions, including the sports and media agency IMG in 2014. In 2016, Endeavor led a group that purchased the UFC for roughly $4 billion. In 2023, Endeavor combined the UFC with WWE to form TKO Group Holdings. In 2025, private equity firm Silver Lake took Endeavor private.

Business segments

The group's activities include talent representation, sports properties and events, licensing, and marketing. Its WME agency represents actors, musicians, writers, and athletes, while IMG manages sports media rights and events worldwide.
