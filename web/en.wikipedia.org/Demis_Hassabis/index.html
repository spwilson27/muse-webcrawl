Sir Demis Hassabis (born 27 July 1976) is a British artificial intelligence researcher and entrepreneur. He is the co-founder and chief executive of Google DeepMind, the AI company responsible for systems such as AlphaGo and the protein-structure prediction programme AlphaFold. In 2024 he shared the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for work on protein structure prediction.

Early life and career

Hassabis was a child chess prodigy and, as a teenager, worked at Bullfrog Productions, where he co-designed the video game Theme Park. He studied computer science at Cambridge and later completed a PhD in cognitive neuroscience at University College London, researching memory and imagination. In 2010 he co-founded DeepMind in London with Shane Legg and Mustafa Suleyman; Google acquired the company in 2014.

DeepMind

Under Hassabis's leadership, DeepMind developed AlphaGo, which defeated world-class Go players in 2016, and AlphaFold, whose 2020 version was recognised as solving the protein-folding problem for practical purposes. Hassabis also leads Isomorphic Labs, an Alphabet company applying AI to drug discovery.

Honours

He was appointed CBE in 2018 for services to science and technology, elected a Fellow of the Royal Society, and knighted in 2024. In October 2024 he was awarded the Nobel Prize in Chemistry jointly with John Jumper, sharing half the prize with David Baker's work on protein design.
