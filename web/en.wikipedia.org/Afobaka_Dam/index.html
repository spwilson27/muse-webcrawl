Afobaka Dam

LocationBrokopondo District, Suriname
RiverSuriname River
Construction1960–1964
Installed capacity189 MW

The Afobaka Dam is an embankment dam on the Suriname River near Afobaka in Brokopondo District, Suriname. Built between 1960 and 1964 by the Suriname Aluminum Company (Suralco), it created the Brokopondo Reservoir, one of the largest artificial lakes in the world by surface area, to supply hydroelectric power to an alumina refinery and smelter.

Construction and impact

The reservoir flooded about 1,560 square kilometres of rainforest and displaced roughly 6,000 Maroon and Indigenous inhabitants, who were relocated to transmigration villages. The filling of the reservoir prompted Operation Gwamba, an effort to rescue animals stranded by rising water. The dam's power station has an installed capacity of about 189 MW and supplies much of Suriname's electricity, including to the capital Paramaribo.
