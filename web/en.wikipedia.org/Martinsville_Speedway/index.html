Martinsville Speedway

Martinsville Speedway is a NASCAR-owned stock car racing short track in Ridgeway, Virginia, just south of Martinsville. At 0.526 miles (0.847 km) in length, it is the shortest track on the NASCAR Cup Series schedule.

History

The track was built in 1947 by H. Clay Earles and opened as a dirt track. It was one of the first paved speedways in NASCAR and has hosted Cup Series races every year since 1949. Winners receive a distinctive longcase clock manufactured locally, a tradition begun in 1964.

Characteristics

The track's layout is often described as resembling a paperclip, with long straightaways and tight, flat corners banked at only 12 degrees. Martinsville hosts annual NASCAR Cup Series events including the spring race and the penultimate playoff race in the autumn.
