List of wars

This is a list of wars — organised armed conflicts between states, or between states and organised non-state groups — arranged by period and region. The deadliest wars in history include the Second World War, with an estimated 70–85 million deaths, and earlier cataclysms such as the Taiping Rebellion and the Mongol conquests.

Lists by region and era

Wars are catalogued in separate lists for ancient, medieval, and modern periods and by continent — including lists of wars involving particular states, civil wars, and wars of independence. Definitions vary: some lists require 1,000 or more battle deaths per year (the Correlates of War threshold), while others include smaller conflicts and prolonged insurgencies.
