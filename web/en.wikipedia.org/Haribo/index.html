Haribo

Haribo is a German confectionery company founded in 1920 by Hans Riegel in Bonn. Its name derives from Hans Riegel Bonn. Haribo is famous as the inventor of the gummy bear.

History

Riegel registered the company on 13 December 1920 and in 1922 created the "Dancing Bear" fruit gum, the precursor of the Goldbears (Goldbären) launched in 1960. The company's slogan in German, "Haribo macht Kinder froh – und Erwachsene ebenso", is rendered in English as "Kids and grown-ups love it so – the happy world of Haribo".

Products

Products include Goldbears, Happy Cola bottles, Starmix, Tangfastics, and liquorice wheels. Haribo operates factories across Europe and beyond and acquired other confectionery brands, including Maoam. See also Gummy bear.
