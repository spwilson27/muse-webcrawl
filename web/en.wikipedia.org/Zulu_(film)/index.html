Zulu (film)

Zulu
Directed by: Cy Endfield
Produced by: Stanley Baker, Cy Endfield
Starring: Stanley Baker, Michael Caine, Jack Hawkins
Release date: 1964
Running time: 138 minutes

Zulu is a 1964 British epic war film directed by Cy Endfield, depicting the Battle of Rorke's Drift during the Anglo-Zulu War of 1879. It stars Stanley Baker and Michael Caine, in his breakthrough role, as Lieutenants John Chard and Gonville Bromhead, who led the defence of a mission station against a large Zulu force.

Plot

Following the Zulu victory at Isandlwana, some 4,000 Zulu warriors advance on the small depot at Rorke's Drift, held by about 150 British and colonial troops. Over a day and night of repeated assaults, the defenders hold their improvised barricades of mealie bags and biscuit boxes. Eleven Victoria Crosses were awarded for the action.

Production

The film was shot in Natal, South Africa, and at Twickenham Studios. Zulu participants and descendants advised on the production, and the film is noted for its large-scale battle scenes and John Barry's score.

Reception and legacy

Zulu was a major commercial and critical success and is regularly listed among the greatest British war films. A prequel, Zulu Dawn (1979), depicts Isandlwana.
