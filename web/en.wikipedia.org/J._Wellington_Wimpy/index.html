J. Wellington Wimpy

J. Wellington Wimpy, usually referred to simply as Wimpy, is a fictional character created by cartoonist E. C. Segar for the comic strip Thimble Theatre, where he appeared alongside Popeye. He debuted in 1931 and became one of the strip's most popular supporting characters.

Character

Wimpy is a portly, soft-spoken moocher whose life revolves around obtaining hamburgers without paying for them. His most famous line — "I'll gladly pay you Tuesday for a hamburger today" — became a widely quoted catchphrase in American culture. Though lazy and sly, Wimpy is generally good-natured and serves as a comic foil to Popeye.

Legacy

The character inspired the name of the Wimpy fast-food restaurant chain, founded in the United States in the 1930s and later widespread in the United Kingdom and elsewhere. Wimpy also appears in the animated Popeye cartoons produced by Fleischer Studios and in the 1980 live-action film Popeye, where he was played by Paul Dooley.
