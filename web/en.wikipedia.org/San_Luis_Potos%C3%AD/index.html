San Luis Potosí, commonly called SLP, is the capital and largest city of the Mexican state of the same name, located in north-central Mexico. Founded in 1592 after gold and silver were discovered in the region, it was named in homage to King Louis IX of France and in hope of rivalling the riches of Potosí in Bolivia.

History

The city became a major mining and commercial centre of New Spain and later an important railway hub. It played notable roles in the Mexican War of Independence and the Mexican Revolution, and President Francisco I. Madero drafted the Plan of San Luis Potosí there in 1910.

Economy

Today the metropolitan area is an industrial centre with automotive manufacturing, steel, and logistics, and its baroque and neoclassical historic centre was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List as part of the Camino Real de Tierra Adentro.
