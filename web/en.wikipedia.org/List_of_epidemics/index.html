List of epidemics

This is a list of epidemics and pandemics of infectious disease throughout history, including the largest and deadliest outbreaks recorded. Epidemics are listed by the pathogen or disease responsible, the location affected, the dates of the outbreak and the estimated death toll. The deadliest pandemic in recorded history was the Black Death of the 14th century, which killed an estimated 75–200 million people.

Epidemics by century

Antiquity and Middle Ages

  • Plague of Athens (430 BC) — killed an estimated 75,000–100,000 people, including Pericles.
  • Antonine Plague (165–180) — severely weakened the Roman Empire.
  • Plague of Cyprian (250–266) — a widespread epidemic in the Roman Empire.
  • Plague of Justinian (541–549) — first recorded outbreak of bubonic plague in Europe.
  • Black Death (1346–1353) — the deadliest pandemic in human history, killing 30–60% of Europe's population.

16th–19th centuries

  • Cocoliztli epidemics (1545, 1576) — devastated the indigenous population of Mexico.
  • Great Plague of London (1665–1666) — the last major plague outbreak in England.
  • Great Plague of Marseille (1720–1722) — the last major plague outbreak in Western Europe.
  • First cholera pandemic (1817–1824) — began in Bengal and spread across Asia.
  • Second cholera pandemic (1826–1837) — reached Europe and North America.
  • Third plague pandemic (1855–1960) — spread globally from Yunnan, China.
  • 1889–1890 pandemic ("Russian flu") — an influenza (or possibly coronavirus) pandemic.

20th century

  • Spanish flu (1918–1920) — the deadliest influenza pandemic.
  • Asian flu (1957–1958) and Hong Kong flu (1968–1969).
  • 1977 Russian flu — a mild H1N1 pandemic.
  • HIV/AIDS pandemic (1981–present).

21st century

  • SARS outbreak (2002–2004).
  • 2009 swine flu pandemic — H1N1, estimated 151,700–575,400 deaths in its first year.
  • Western African Ebola epidemic (2013–2016).
  • Zika virus epidemic (2015–2016) — principally in the Americas.
  • COVID-19 pandemic (2019–present) — declared a pandemic by the WHO on 11 March 2020.
  • 2022–present mpox outbreak — declared a public health emergency of international concern.
