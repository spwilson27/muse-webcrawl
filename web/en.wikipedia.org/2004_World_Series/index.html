The 2004 World Series was the championship series of Major League Baseball's 2004 season. The 100th edition of the World Series, it was a best-of-seven playoff between the American League champion Boston Red Sox and the National League champion St. Louis Cardinals. The Red Sox swept the series in four games.

Background

Boston had reached the Series after becoming the first team in MLB history to win a postseason series after trailing three games to none, defeating the New York Yankees in the American League Championship Series. The Cardinals had the best record in baseball during the regular season.

Significance

The victory ended the Red Sox's 86-year championship drought, popularly known as the "Curse of the Bambino", dating to their last title in 1918. Boston never trailed during the Series. Manny Ramírez was named the Series MVP.

Summary

Game 4, played on 27 October 2004 in St. Louis, ended 3–0 to the Red Sox; the clinching out was recorded on a ground ball back to pitcher Keith Foulke, who tossed to first baseman Doug Mientkiewicz.
