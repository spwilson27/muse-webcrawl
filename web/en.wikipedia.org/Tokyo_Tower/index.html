Tokyo Tower

Tokyo Tower is a communications and observation tower in the Shiba-koen district of Minato, Tokyo, Japan. At 332.9 metres (1,092 ft), it was the tallest structure in Japan from its completion in 1958 until the opening of the Tokyo Skytree in 2012.

Construction

Designed by Tachū Naitō and modelled on the Eiffel Tower in Paris, the lattice tower is painted white and international orange in accordance with air-safety regulations. It was built to provide a single centralised transmission site for the expanding television broadcasters of the 1950s, using steel partly salvaged from American tanks damaged in the Korean War.

Attractions

The tower has a main observatory at 150 metres and a top deck at 250 metres, and its base building, FootTown, contains museums, restaurants and shops. By night it is illuminated in seasonal schemes and remains one of Tokyo's most recognisable landmarks and a frequent symbol of the city in popular culture, even after primary broadcasting moved to the Skytree.
