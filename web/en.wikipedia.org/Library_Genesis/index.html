Library Genesis

Library Genesis (LibGen) is a file-sharing based shadow library website for scholarly journal articles, academic and general-interest books, images, comics, audiobooks, and magazines. It provides free access to content that is otherwise paywalled or not digitised elsewhere, and describes itself as a "links aggregator" for material uploaded by users.

History

The site originated around 2008 within Russian samizdat and scientific communities, building on earlier digital collections. Its holdings grew to tens of millions of books and scholarly articles, split among several collections, notably the LibGen Sci-Tech and fiction branches, and it has been closely associated with the wider open-access and shadow library movement alongside projects such as Sci-Hub.

Legal status and response

Publishers have repeatedly sued and obtained injunctions against the site, and its domains have been blocked in several countries, leading to a shifting constellation of mirror domains. Supporters frame LibGen as an instrument of free access to knowledge; critics characterise it as mass copyright infringement. See also Sci-Hub and shadow library.
