Labneh

Labneh
TypeStrained yoghurt (soft cheese)
RegionLevant; Middle East
Main ingredientsYoghurt, salt

Labneh (Arabic: لبنة, labna) is a soft cheese made from yoghurt that has been strained to remove most of its whey, resulting in a thick, tangy, spreadable consistency similar to soft cream cheese or Greek yoghurt strained further. It is a staple of Levantine cuisine in Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, Palestine and Israel, and is also found across the wider Middle East and Mediterranean.

Preparation and serving

Salted yoghurt is traditionally hung in cheesecloth for 12–24 hours until it reaches the desired firmness. For longer keeping, it can be rolled into balls (labneh jarjir) and preserved in olive oil. Labneh is served as part of a mezze spread, drizzled with olive oil and za'atar or dried mint, eaten with pita, or used as a sandwich filling. It is rich in protein and probiotic cultures, and its whey by-product is used in cooking and drinks.
