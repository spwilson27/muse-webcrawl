Raška
Country: Serbia
District: Raška District
Region: Southwestern Serbia (Sandžak area)

Raška is a town and municipality located in the Raška District of southwestern Serbia, near the border with Kosovo and Montenegro. The town lies in the valley of the Ibar River and takes its name from the medieval Serbian region and state of Raška, which formed the heartland of the early Serbian kingdom.

Geography

The municipality sits at the edge of the Kopaonik mountain range, Serbia's largest ski resort area, and the town serves as a gateway to the ski centre. The surrounding region is known for medieval monasteries, including Studenica and Sopoćani, both UNESCO World Heritage Sites, which preserve the legacy of the Raška architectural school.

History

The wider region of Raška was the core territory of the medieval Serbian state under the Nemanjić dynasty from the 12th century. The modern town developed later as a market and administrative settlement; according to recent censuses the municipality has a population of roughly twenty thousand.
