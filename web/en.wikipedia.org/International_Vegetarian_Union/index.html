International Vegetarian Union

The International Vegetarian Union (IVU) is an international non-profit organisation founded in 1908 to promote vegetarianism worldwide. It serves as an umbrella body linking vegetarian societies, groups and individuals across dozens of countries, and is best known for organising the World Vegetarian Congress.

History

The IVU was founded at the first World Vegetarian Congress, held in Dresden, Germany, in 1908. The British Vegetarian Society, established in 1847, was the oldest of the founding organisations and provided much of the early impetus for an international body.

World Vegetarian Congresses have since been held in many countries, bringing together campaigners, nutritionists and researchers. The congress has been hosted on every inhabited continent, and the IVU's membership grew steadily as vegetarian movements developed outside Europe and North America.

Activities

The IVU supports its member societies with information, networking and publicity. It maintains definitions of vegetarian and vegan practice used by member organisations, promotes World Vegetarian Day on 1 October, and keeps an extensive archive of the history of the vegetarian movement, including biographies of notable vegetarians.
