The Australia national rugby league team, nicknamed the Kangaroos, represents Australia in men's rugby league football. Administered by the Australian Rugby League Commission, the team is the most successful in Rugby League World Cup history, having won the tournament twelve times.

History

Australia played its first international match in 1908 against New Zealand. The Kangaroo Tours of Great Britain and France became a celebrated tradition of the sport. Australia dominated the World Cup from the 1970s onward, winning every tournament between 1975 and 2000, and again in 2013, 2017 and 2021 (played in 2022).

Rivalries

The Kangaroos' principal rivals are New Zealand (the Kiwis) and England/Great Britain. Matches against New Zealand feature the playing of the ANZAC Test tradition, while Ashes series against Great Britain were contested for much of the twentieth century.

Colours and emblem

The team plays in green jerseys with a gold chevron, reflecting Australia's national sporting colours of green and gold, adopted in the early twentieth century.
