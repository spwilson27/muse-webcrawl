RT (TV network)

RT (formerly Russia Today)
Type: State-controlled international news network
Launched: 10 December 2005
Headquarters: Moscow, Russia
Owner: ANO TV-Novosti (Russian government)

RT, formerly known as Russia Today, is a Russian state-controlled international news television network funded by the Russian federal government. It operates pay television and free-to-air channels directed at audiences outside Russia, broadcasting in English, Spanish, French, German, and Arabic (as RT Arabic), alongside the documentary channel RT Documentary.

History

RT was registered in 2005 as part of a Russian government effort to improve the country's image abroad, under the autonomous non-profit organization TV-Novosti, founded by the state news agency RIA Novosti. Margarita Simonyan has been editor-in-chief since its launch. The network rebranded from Russia Today to RT in 2009.

Reception and restrictions

RT describes itself as offering alternative perspectives on world events, while critics and several governments characterize it as a propaganda outlet for the Russian state. Following Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine, RT was banned across the European Union, lost its UK broadcasting licence, and was dropped by most major distributors in North America, leading RT America to cease production.
