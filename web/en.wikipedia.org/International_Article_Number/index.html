International Article Number

The International Article Number (also called a European Article Number, or EAN) is a standard describing a barcode symbology and numbering system used in global trade to identify a specific retail product type. The most common form is the thirteen-digit EAN-13.

Structure

An EAN-13 code combines a GS1 country prefix, a manufacturer code, an item reference and a check digit calculated so that the weighted sum of the digits is a multiple of ten. An eight-digit EAN-8 serves small packages.

Use

EAN codes are administered by GS1 and are closely related to the North American UPC; most scanners read both. The numbers identify products rather than individual items, and are used throughout retail supply chains for stock control and checkout.
