Jonathan Peter Wilkinson CBE (born 25 May 1979) is an English former rugby union player. A fly-half, he played for Newcastle Falcons and the French club Toulon and represented England and the British & Irish Lions. He is particularly known for scoring the winning drop goal in the last minute of extra time at the 2003 Rugby World Cup final, securing England's victory over Australia.

Career

Wilkinson made his England debut in 1998, aged 18. His metronomic goal-kicking, brave tackling and game management made him central to the England side that won the 2003 World Cup in Sydney. A long series of injuries then kept him out of Test rugby for more than three years, but he returned to help England reach the 2007 World Cup final and won his 91st and final cap in 2011.

Records and later career

Wilkinson retired as one of the highest points scorers in international rugby history, with 1,246 points for England and the Lions combined. With Toulon he won two Heineken Cups (2013, 2014) and the Top 14 title in 2014 before retiring. Since then he has worked as a kicking coach, pundit and mentor, and is remembered as one of the greatest fly-halves in the sport's history.
