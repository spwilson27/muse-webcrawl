Mastercard Inc. is an American multinational payment card services corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. It operates one of the world's largest payment processing networks, connecting consumers, merchants, banks, and governments in more than 210 countries and territories.

History

The company began in 1966 as the Interbank Card Association, founded by a group of California banks to compete with BankAmericard (later Visa). It adopted the name MasterCard in 1979 and rebranded as Mastercard in 2016, with its interlocking red and yellow circles logo.

Business

Mastercard does not issue cards or extend credit; instead, member financial institutions issue Mastercard-branded cards, while the company earns fees for processing transactions over its network.
