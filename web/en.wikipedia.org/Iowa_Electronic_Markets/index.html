Iowa Electronic Markets

The Iowa Electronic Markets (IEM) is a prediction market operated for research and teaching by the University of Iowa's Tippie College of Business. Founded in 1988, it is one of the earliest and best-known real-money prediction markets, run under a no-action letter from the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

Operation and record

Traders buy and sell contracts whose payoffs depend on outcomes such as U.S. presidential elections; stakes are limited to a few hundred dollars per trader. IEM prices have often outperformed opinion polls as election forecasts — studies found the market's vote-share predictions were closer to the result than the final polls in a majority of elections studied — making the IEM a canonical example of the "wisdom of crowds" in information aggregation.
