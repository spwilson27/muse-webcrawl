National Trust for Scotland

The National Trust for Scotland is the Scottish conservation charity that protects and promotes Scotland's natural and cultural heritage. Founded in 1931, it is independent of the National Trust covering England, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

Properties

Its care ranges from Culzean Castle and Robert Burns's birthplace cottage in Alloway to the battlefield of Culloden, the island world heritage site of St Kilda, Ben Lawers National Nature Reserve, and gardens such as Inverewe. The Trust's first property was Crookston Castle in Glasgow.

Work

The charity conserves buildings, landscapes, and wildlife, runs visitor centres and education programmes, and campaigns on planning and environmental policy in Scotland.
