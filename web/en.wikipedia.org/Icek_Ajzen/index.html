Icek Ajzen

Born 1942, Chełm, Poland
Field Social psychology
Known for Theory of planned behavior

Icek Ajzen (born 1942) is a Polish-born social psychologist and professor emeritus at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. He is best known as the developer of the theory of planned behavior.

Theory of planned behavior

Ajzen proposed the theory of planned behavior in the 1980s as an extension of the theory of reasoned action, which he had developed with Martin Fishbein. The theory holds that a person's behavior is driven by behavioral intentions, which are shaped by attitude toward the behavior, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control.

Career

Educated at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign, Ajzen spent most of his career at Amherst, where his work on attitudes and the prediction of human behavior became among the most cited in social psychology.
