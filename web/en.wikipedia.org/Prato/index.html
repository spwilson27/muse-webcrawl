Prato

Prato is a city and comune in Tuscany, Italy, with a population of around 195,000. It is the capital of the province of Prato and lies about 17 km (11 mi) northwest of Florence.

History

Prato has long been a centre of the textile industry; since the late 19th century it has specialised in wool processing and later in fast fashion and recycled textiles. The city has one of the largest Chinese immigrant communities in Italy, many working in the garment trade.

Sights

Landmarks include Prato Cathedral (Duomo), with frescoes by Filippo Lippi, the Emperor's Castle (Castello dell'Imperatore), the only Swabian castle in northern Italy, and the Luigi Pecci Center for Contemporary Art.
