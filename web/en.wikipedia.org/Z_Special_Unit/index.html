Z Special Unit

Z Special Unit was a joint Allied special forces unit established in Australia during the Second World War. It operated under the Services Reconnaissance Department and carried out clandestine reconnaissance and raiding operations behind Japanese lines in Southeast Asia and the Southwest Pacific.

Active1942–1946
CountryAustralia / Allied
TypeSpecial forces
ParentServices Reconnaissance Department

Operations

The unit's most famous mission was Operation Jaywick in September 1943, in which a small party sailed in the captured vessel Krait to Singapore Harbour and sank or damaged some 39,000 tons of Japanese shipping using limpet mines. A follow-up raid, Operation Rimau in 1944, ended in the loss of all its participants.

Composition

Personnel were drawn mainly from Australian forces, together with British, Dutch, New Zealand, and locally recruited operatives. In total more than 470 men served in operations conducted by the unit.

Legacy

Z Special Unit is regarded as a forerunner of modern Australian special operations forces, and its veterans helped shape postwar commando units.
