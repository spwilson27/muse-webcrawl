Technical university

A technical university (also called a university of technology, polytechnic university, or institute of technology) is a university whose teaching and research focus on engineering, technology, applied science and related disciplines, although many also offer programmes in natural sciences, management and architecture.

History

The model emerged in the late 18th and 19th centuries with institutions such as the École Polytechnique in Paris (1794) and the polytechnics founded across German-speaking Europe — including those at Karlsruhe, Munich, Zurich and Vienna — which campaigned successfully for equal status with traditional universities, including the right to award doctorates in engineering around 1900.

Examples

Notable technical universities include ETH Zurich, TU Munich, Delft University of Technology, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and the Indian Institutes of Technology. In many countries, former polytechnics and Fachhochschulen have been renamed universities of applied sciences or technical universities as their research role grew.
