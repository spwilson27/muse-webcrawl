Jervis Bay Territory

Jervis Bay Territory

Country: Australia

Acceptance: 1915 (from New South Wales)

Area: 67.7 km²

Population: about 400

The Jervis Bay Territory is an internal territory of Australia, accepted by the Commonwealth from the state of New South Wales in 1915 so that the Australian Capital Territory — the landlocked site of the federal capital, Canberra — would have access to the sea.

Geography and administration

The territory covers about 68 square kilometres on the southern shore of Jervis Bay, roughly 200 kilometres south of Sydney. It contains the naval base HMAS Creswell, home of the Royal Australian Naval College, the Jervis Bay airfield, and much of Booderee National Park, which is jointly managed with the Wreck Bay Aboriginal community. Most of the territory's roughly 400 residents are defence personnel and their families, together with the Wreck Bay Aboriginal community; the largest civilian settlement is Jervis Bay Village.

Governance

Although geographically within New South Wales, the territory is administered by the Commonwealth. Since 1989, most state-type services have been provided by the ACT government under arrangement, and residents vote in the ACT federal electorate of Fenner.
