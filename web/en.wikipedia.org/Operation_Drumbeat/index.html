Operation Drumbeat

Operation Drumbeat (Unternehmen Paukenschlag) was the opening phase of the German U-boat campaign against shipping off the east coast of North America in early 1942, part of the wider Second Happy Time in the Battle of the Atlantic.

Background

Following Germany's declaration of war on the United States on 11 December 1941, Admiral Karl Dönitz sent five long-range Type IX U-boats to the American coast. The first boats arrived in mid-January 1942 and found shipping sailing unescorted, unblackout coastal cities silhouetting targets, and no convoy system in place.

Outcome

The operation was a striking success for the U-boats, sinking large numbers of tankers and merchant ships with minimal losses, and it was extended by successive waves of boats. The campaign forced the United States Navy, under Admiral Ernest King, to adopt convoys and coastal blackouts, measures that by mid-1942 had pushed the U-boats toward the Caribbean and Gulf of Mexico.
