Nissan

Nissan Motor Co., Ltd. is a Japanese multinational automobile manufacturer headquartered in Yokohama. Founded in 1933, it sells vehicles under the Nissan, Infiniti, and Datsun brands and is one of the largest carmakers in the world.

History

Nissan traces its origins to 1911 and the Kwaishinsha Motor Car Works, maker of the DAT car. The Nissan name came into use in the 1930s. After World War II the company expanded internationally, selling cars in the United States under the Datsun name until the early 1980s.

Renault–Nissan Alliance

In 1999, facing financial difficulty, Nissan entered an alliance with France's Renault, under executive Carlos Ghosn, whose restructuring returned the company to profitability. The alliance later expanded to include Mitsubishi Motors. Ghosn's arrest in Japan in 2018 on financial misconduct charges — and his dramatic escape to Lebanon in 2019 — strained the partnership.

Products

Well-known models include the Altima, Maxima, the Z sports car, the GT-R, and the Leaf, launched in 2010 as one of the first mass-market all-electric cars and for years the world's best-selling electric vehicle.
