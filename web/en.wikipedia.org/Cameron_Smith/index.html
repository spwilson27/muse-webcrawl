Cameron Wayne Smith AM (born 18 January 1983) is an Australian former professional rugby league footballer who captained the Melbourne Storm, Queensland and Australia. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the sport's history, he holds the record for the most National Rugby League (NRL) games played, with 430 appearances.

Career

Smith debuted for the Melbourne Storm in 2002 and spent his entire career with the club, captaining it to multiple NRL premierships (the titles of 2007 and 2009 were later stripped for salary cap breaches; official premierships followed in 2012, 2017 and 2020). A hooker known for his game management, goal-kicking and durability, he won the Dally M Medal in 2006 and 2017 and the Golden Boot Award as the world's best player in 2007 and 2017.

Representative career

Smith captained Queensland in the State of Origin series, playing 42 matches, and represented Australia in 56 Tests, winning two Rugby League World Cups (2013 and 2017) as captain. He retired at the end of the 2020 season and was later appointed an assistant coach and selector in representative football, and works in media commentary.
