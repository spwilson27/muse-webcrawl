List of fireworks accidents and incidents

This is a list of fireworks accidents and incidents, including explosions at fireworks factories and storage facilities, accidents during public displays, and disasters caused by the transport and misuse of fireworks and other pyrotechnics. Some of the deadliest industrial accidents in history have involved fireworks and gunpowder factories.

Incidents during public displays

  • 1770 Paris, France — A crowd crush during a fireworks display celebrating the marriage of Louis XVI and Marie Antoinette killed 133 people by the official count.
  • 4 July 1984, Akron, Ohio, United States — A fireworks barge malfunction caused injuries among spectators.
  • 23 June 2013, Simi Valley, California, United States — A platform collapse launched fireworks into the crowd, injuring 39 people.
  • 31 December 2014, Cavite, Philippines — Stray fireworks caused multiple fires and injuries during New Year celebrations.
  • 1 January 2018, Houston area, United States — Illegal display fireworks caused structure fires.

Transport incidents

  • 4 August 2020, Beirut, Lebanon — The ammonium nitrate explosion at the Port of Beirut is frequently associated in reports with a warehouse confiscated cargo; fireworks stored in the same hangar were reported to have caught fire before the main explosion, which killed at least 218 people.
  • 28 February 2014, China — A truck carrying fireworks exploded on a highway, causing a bridge collapse in earlier related incidents (the 1 February 2013 Yichang bridge explosion in Henan killed at least 13).
