Marsha P. Johnson

Marsha P. Johnson (August 24, 1945 – July 6, 1992) was an American gay liberation activist and self-identified drag queen. Known as an outspoken advocate for gay rights, she was a prominent figure in the Stonewall uprising of 1969 in New York City.

With Sylvia Rivera she co-founded Street Transvestite Action Revolutionaries (STAR) in 1970, providing housing and support to homeless queer youth and sex workers.

Johnson's activism and art — including performing with the Hot Peaches troupe — made her a leading figure in the LGBTQ rights movement; her death in 1992, initially ruled a suicide, was reclassified as undetermined and remains a subject of investigation and remembrance.
