Box office

A box office is a place where tickets are sold for events such as films, plays, and concerts. By extension, "box office" also refers to the total revenue generated by ticket sales, and a film's commercial performance is described in terms of its box-office gross.

Box-office figures are usually reported as gross receipts, divided between domestic (United States and Canada) and international markets. Studios receive only a portion of the gross — roughly half domestically, and less in some foreign markets — known as rentals. Reported figures typically exclude production and marketing budgets, so a large gross does not by itself guarantee profit.

The highest-grossing films of all time include Avatar (2009), Avengers: Endgame (2019), and Titanic (1997). Tracking services such as Box Office Mojo and The Numbers publish daily estimates.
