Dynactin

Dynactin is a large multisubunit protein complex that activates the microtubule motor protein cytoplasmic dynein and links it to cargo. Its name is a contraction of "dynein activator". Dynactin is essential for nearly all dynein-driven transport in eukaryotic cells, including organelle positioning, vesicle trafficking and mitotic spindle function.

Structure

The complex is built around a short filament of the actin-related protein Arp1, capped by conventional actin and CapZ. Its largest subunit, p150glued (encoded by DCTN1), projects a side arm that binds microtubules and dynein itself; other subunits include dynamitin (p50), p62, p27, p25 and p24.

Function

Dynactin increases dynein's processivity and acts as an adaptor platform: cargo adaptors such as BICD2, Hook and RILP simultaneously bind dynein and dynactin, assembling an activated transport complex. Dynactin also anchors dynein at the cell cortex and at kinetochores during mitosis.

Clinical significance

Mutations in DCTN1 cause forms of motor neuron disease and Perry syndrome, underscoring the importance of axonal transport in neurons.
