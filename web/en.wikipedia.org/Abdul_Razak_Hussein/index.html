Abdul Razak Hussein

Tun Abdul Razak Hussein
Born: 11 March 1922, Pekan, Pahang
Died: 14 January 1976, London, England
Office: 2nd Prime Minister of Malaysia (1970–1976)

Tun Abdul Razak bin Hussein (11 March 1922 – 14 January 1976) was a Malaysian politician who served as the second prime minister of Malaysia from 1970 until his death in 1976. He also served as the first director of the National Operations Council (1969–1971), which governed the country after the 13 May incident of racial rioting.

Policies

Razak launched the New Economic Policy (NEP) in 1971, aimed at reducing poverty and restructuring society to reduce the identification of race with economic function. He founded the Barisan Nasional coalition in 1973, expanded rural development through FELDA land schemes, and was honoured posthumously as the "Father of Development" (Bapa Pembangunan).

Family and death

His son, Najib Razak, later became Malaysia's sixth prime minister. Abdul Razak died of leukaemia in London in 1976 while still in office, and is buried at the Heroes' Mausoleum (Makam Pahlawan) in Kuala Lumpur.
