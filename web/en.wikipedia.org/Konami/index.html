Konami

Konami Group Corporation is a Japanese multinational entertainment company founded in Osaka in 1969 by Kagemasa Kōzuki. It is one of the best-known video game developers and publishers, and also operates in pachinko machines, arcade games, trading cards and fitness clubs.

History

Konami began as a jukebox rental and repair business, taking its name from the syllables of its founders' names. It rose to prominence in the arcade and home console eras with series such as Gradius, Contra and Castlevania, and became famous for the "Konami Code" cheat sequence. Its subsidiary Konami Digital Entertainment publishes the Metal Gear and Silent Hill series, and the company holds the rights to the Yu-Gi-Oh! trading card game.
