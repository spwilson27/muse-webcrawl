Energy in Ireland

Energy in Ireland describes energy production, consumption, and policy in the Republic of Ireland. Ireland is highly dependent on imported fossil fuels, particularly natural gas and oil, but has become a world leader in the share of electricity generated from wind power.

Electricity

Ireland operates a single all-island electricity market with Northern Ireland. Wind farms regularly supply a large fraction of demand, at times exceeding half of generation, supported by interconnectors to Great Britain. Gas-fired stations provide much of the remaining conventional generation.

Policy

Irish climate policy targets deep cuts in greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 and net zero by 2050, with expansion of offshore wind, solar power, and grid interconnection. Peat-fired electricity generation, a historic domestic source, ended in the early 2020s.
