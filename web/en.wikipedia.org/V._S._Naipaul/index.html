V. S. Naipaul

V. S. Naipaul
Born: 17 August 1932, Chaguanas, Trinidad
Died: 11 August 2018, London
Notable works: A House for Mr Biswas, In a Free State, A Bend in the River
Awards: Nobel Prize in Literature (2001), Booker Prize (1971)

Sir Vidiadhar Surajprasad Naipaul (17 August 1932 – 11 August 2018) was a Trinidadian-born British writer of Indian descent, known for his unsparing novels and travel writing about the postcolonial world. He won the Booker Prize in 1971 for In a Free State and the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2001.

Works

His early comic novels are set in Trinidad, among them The Mystic Masseur and A House for Mr Biswas (1961), widely considered his masterpiece. Later fiction such as A Bend in the River (1979) and non-fiction including Among the Believers and the India trilogy drew both acclaim and controversy for their bleak assessments of postcolonial societies.
