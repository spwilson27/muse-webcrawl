The Running Man

The Running Man most commonly refers to the 1982 dystopian novel by Stephen King (published under the pseudonym Richard Bachman) and its film adaptations.

Novel

Set in a totalitarian America of 2025, impoverished Ben Richards enters the Games Network's deadliest show: declared an enemy of the state, he must evade professional Hunters for 30 days to win $1 billion for his sick daughter. Richards discovers the Network rigs the game and ends the story by crashing a hijacked airliner into the Games Building.

Films

The 1987 film starring Arnold Schwarzenegger reimagines the premise as an arena game show hosted by Damon Killian (Richard Dawson), diverging sharply from the novel. A more faithful adaptation directed by Edgar Wright, starring Glen Powell, was released in 2025. King wrote the novel extremely quickly — reportedly in 72 hours. See also The Green Mile.
