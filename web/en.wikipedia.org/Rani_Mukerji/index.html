Rani Mukerji (born 21 March 1978) is an Indian actress known for her work in Hindi cinema. Regarded as one of the most popular and influential actresses of her generation, she has received numerous awards, including multiple Filmfare Awards.

Career

Born into the Mukherjee-Samarth family of Bengali film industry figures, Mukerji made her breakthrough with Kuch Kuch Hota Hai (1998). She established herself with acclaimed performances in Saathiya (2002), Hum Tum (2004), Yuva (2004), Veer-Zaara (2004), and Black (2005), in which she played a deafblind woman. Later successes include No One Killed Jessica (2011), Talaash (2012), the Mardaani franchise, Hichki (2018), and Mrs Chatterjee vs Norway (2023).
