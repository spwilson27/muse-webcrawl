FASTag

FASTag is an electronic toll collection system used on national highways in India. It employs radio-frequency identification (RFID) technology to make toll payments directly from a prepaid or savings account linked to it, allowing vehicles to pass through toll plazas without stopping for cash transactions. The system is operated by the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) under the National Electronic Toll Collection programme.

A FASTag RFID sticker on a vehicle windscreen

History

FASTag was first introduced in 2014 on a pilot section of highway between Ahmedabad and Mumbai, and gradually extended across the national highway network. In December 2019 the government of India required all lanes at national highway toll plazas to accept the system, and from February 2021 FASTag became mandatory for all vehicles using national highways; vehicles without a valid tag are charged double the toll in cash.

Technology

A FASTag is a passive RFID sticker affixed to the vehicle's windscreen. Readers at toll plaza gantries scan the tag as the vehicle passes, and the applicable toll is debited automatically. Tags are issued by banks and other certified issuers, are linked to a specific vehicle registration, and can be recharged online or through mobile applications.
