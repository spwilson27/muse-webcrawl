Massachusetts (officially the Commonwealth of Massachusetts) is a state in the New England region of the Northeastern United States. It borders the Atlantic Ocean and Gulf of Maine, and is bounded by Rhode Island and Connecticut to the south, New York to the west, and Vermont and New Hampshire to the north. Boston, the state's capital and largest city, is the cultural and financial centre of New England. Massachusetts is the most populous of the six New England states and the third most densely populated in the Union.

History

The area was originally inhabited by Algonquian peoples, including the Massachusett tribe from whom the state takes its name. English colonists founded Plymouth Colony in 1620 and Massachusetts Bay Colony in 1630. The colony played a central role in the American Revolution: the Battles of Lexington and Concord, the Boston Tea Party and the Siege of Boston all took place in Massachusetts, which became the sixth state to ratify the U.S. Constitution in 1788. In the 19th century it was a centre of the abolitionist movement, textile and shoe manufacturing, and later of industrial decline and reinvention.

Economy and education

Modern Massachusetts has a highly developed economy centred on higher education, biotechnology, finance, healthcare and technology along the Route 128 corridor. It is home to Harvard University and MIT, the oldest and among the most prestigious universities in the world, and consistently ranks at or near the top of U.S. states in education attainment and per-capita income. Massachusetts was the first U.S. state to legalize same-sex marriage (2004) and enacted a landmark healthcare reform law in 2006 that became a model for the federal Affordable Care Act.

Geography

The state ranges from the sandy beaches of Cape Cod and the islands of Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket, through the rolling hills of central Massachusetts, to the Berkshire Mountains in the west, whose Mount Greylock is the state's highest point at 3,489 feet. The Connecticut River bisects the state north to south.
