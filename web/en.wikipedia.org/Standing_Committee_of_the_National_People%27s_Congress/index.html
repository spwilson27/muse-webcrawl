Standing Committee of the National People's Congress

Formation1954
Members~175
SeatGreat Hall of the People, Beijing

The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (NPCSC) is the permanent organ of the National People's Congress of China, exercising the legislative power of the state when the NPC is not in session.

Functions

The NPCSC interprets the Constitution and laws, enacts and amends statutes, supervises the work of the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and decides on the appointment of senior state officials.

Composition

The committee consists of a chairperson, vice-chairpersons, a secretary-general, and around 160 other members, elected by the NPC for five-year terms. It typically meets every two months in the Great Hall of the People in Beijing.
