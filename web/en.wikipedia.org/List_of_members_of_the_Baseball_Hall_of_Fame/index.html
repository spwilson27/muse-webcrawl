List of members of the Baseball Hall of Fame

The National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum in Cooperstown, New York, honours players, managers, umpires, executives, and pioneers who have made outstanding contributions to baseball. Members are elected primarily by the Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA), which requires a 75% vote, and by era committees that consider candidates from particular periods. The Hall inducted its first class in 1936 and has since grown to more than 340 members.

Categories of membership

Most members are former Major League players, but the Hall also includes managers (such as John McGraw and Casey Stengel), umpires (such as Bill Klem), executives and pioneers (such as Branch Rickey and Alexander Cartwright), and Negro leagues figures. In 2020–21 the Hall reclassified the Negro leagues' status, and a special election in 1971–2006 era committees inducted many Black baseball stars previously excluded.
