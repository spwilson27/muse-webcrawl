Chief mate

A chief mate, also called a chief officer, first mate, or first officer, is the head of the deck department on a merchant vessel and the second-in-command after the ship's master (captain). The chief mate is a licensed mariner who oversees cargo operations, ship stability, safety, and the deck crew.

Duties

The chief mate is responsible for loading, stowage, securing, and discharge of cargo, and for computing the vessel's stability and trim. The chief mate directs maintenance of the hull and deck equipment, supervises firefighting and lifesaving appliances, and typically stands a navigation watch (traditionally the 4-to-8 watch). In an emergency the chief mate coordinates on-scene response, and the chief mate assumes command if the master becomes incapacitated.

Licensing

Chief mates must hold certificates of competency issued under the STCW Convention after sea service as a second mate and passing examinations in navigation, cargo handling, and ship operations. On many vessels the chief mate is the ship's designated security and medical officer.
