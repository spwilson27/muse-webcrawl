Lipid panel

A lipid panel, also called a lipid profile, is a panel of blood tests used to find abnormalities in blood lipids such as cholesterol and triglycerides, to assess cardiovascular disease risk.

MeasuresTotal cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides
SynonymLipid profile

Components

The panel typically reports total cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, triglycerides, and LDL cholesterol calculated by the Friedewald equation or measured directly. See lipid profile and lipid.

Interpretation

Elevated LDL and triglycerides and low HDL are associated with atherosclerosis risk. Guidelines set target ranges based on overall risk.

Procedure

Blood is drawn, traditionally after fasting, though non-fasting panels are increasingly accepted.
