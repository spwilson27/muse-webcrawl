Concentus Musicus Wien

Concentus Musicus Wien is an Austrian period-instrument ensemble founded in 1953 by Nikolaus Harnoncourt and his wife Alice Harnoncourt. It was a pioneer of historically informed performance of Baroque and Classical music.

Work

The ensemble's recordings include the complete Bach cantatas, shared with the Leonhardt Consort, and cycles of Monteverdi, Handel, Haydn, and Mozart works performed on original instruments or copies.

Legacy

Concentus Musicus helped transform mainstream performance practice, bringing period instruments and rhetorical phrasing from a specialist pursuit into the centre of European musical life.
