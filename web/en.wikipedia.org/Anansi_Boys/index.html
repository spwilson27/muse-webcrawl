Anansi Boys

Anansi Boys is a fantasy novel by English author Neil Gaiman. In the novel, "Fat Charlie" Nancy discovers that his late father was a manifestation of the African trickster god Anansi, and that he has a brother who inherited divine powers. The book was published in 2005.

Anansi Boys
Author: Neil Gaiman
Language: English
Genre: Fantasy
Published: 2005
Awards: Locus Award / Mythopoeic Award (2006)

Plot

Charles "Fat Charlie" Nancy, a mild-mannered Londoner, learns after his father's death that his father was the spider god Anansi — a character who also appears in Gaiman's earlier novel American Gods. Meeting his brother Spider, who can manipulate reality and takes over Charlie's life, Charlie seeks help from old family friends and the other animal gods, unleashing conflict with the Tiger, the deity who once owned the stories Anansi stole.

Awards and adaptation

The novel won the Locus Award for Best Fantasy Novel and the Mythopoeic Award in 2006, and topped the New York Times Best Seller list. An Amazon television adaptation has been developed as a limited series.
