Nupedia

[Image placeholder: Nupedia]

Nupedia was an English-language online encyclopedia whose articles were written by volunteer contributors with relevant expertise and reviewed by expert editors before publication. It was founded by Jimmy Wales and underwritten by the web company Bomis, with Larry Sanger as editor-in-chief.

History

Launched in March 2000, Nupedia employed a rigorous seven-stage peer-review process modelled on academic publishing. The process produced high-quality articles but very slowly — only about two dozen completed articles in its lifetime. In January 2001 it launched a companion wiki, intended as a feeder of draft articles, called Wikipedia. The wiki grew so rapidly that it eclipsed Nupedia, which was shut down in September 2003; its content was absorbed into Wikipedia.

Legacy

Nupedia is remembered chiefly as the forerunner of Wikipedia, and its expert-review model was later echoed in Sanger's Citizendium project (2006).
