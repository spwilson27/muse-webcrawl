The Eleventh Doctor is an incarnation of the Doctor, the protagonist of the BBC science fiction television programme Doctor Who. He was played by Matt Smith, who was the youngest actor to take the role at the time of his casting, appearing in the role from 2010 to 2013 across three series and several specials, including the programme's 50th anniversary special "The Day of the Doctor" (2013).

Characterisation

Smith's Doctor is an energetic, eccentric and youthful figure with an old man's melancholy beneath: a self-described "madman with a box" fond of bow ties ("bow ties are cool"), fezzes, fish fingers and custard, and the exclamation "Geronimo!" His era under showrunner Steven Moffat featured complex time-travel story arcs, including the mystery of the Silence, the Doctor's apparent death at Lake Silencio, and the revelation of River Song's identity.

Storylines

The Eleventh Doctor regenerated after the events of "The End of Time" and crashed into the garden of young Amelia Pond, with whom — together with Rory Williams — he travelled through series 5 and 6 and the first half of series 7, until the Ponds were lost to the Weeping Angels in "The Angels Take Manhattan". He then travelled with Clara Oswald. In the 2013 Christmas special "The Time of the Doctor", having spent centuries defending the town of Christmas on Trenzalore, he regenerated into the Twelfth Doctor after receiving a new cycle of regenerations from the Time Lords.

Reception

Smith was widely praised for the performance, winning a National Television Award and receiving a BAFTA nomination. His era consolidated Doctor Who's popularity in the United States and internationally.
