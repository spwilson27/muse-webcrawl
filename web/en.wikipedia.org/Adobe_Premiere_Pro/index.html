Adobe Premiere Pro

Adobe Premiere Pro is a timeline-based, non-linear video editing software application developed by Adobe Inc. and published as part of the Adobe Creative Cloud licensing program. First launched in 2003 as a rewritten successor to Adobe Premiere (first released in 1991), it is used for editing films, television programs, commercials, and online video.

Features

Premiere Pro supports high-resolution video editing at up to 32K resolution, multi-camera editing, VR video, and a wide range of native camera formats. It integrates with other Adobe applications: After Effects for motion graphics, Audition for audio, and Photoshop and Illustrator for still assets. Features include the Lumetri colour tools, essential graphics, auto-transcription and text-based editing, and support for third-party plug-ins and hardware acceleration through the Mercury Playback Engine.

Usage

Premiere Pro has been used in the production of feature films including Deadpool, Gone Girl, and Everything Everywhere All at Once, as well as in broadcast newsrooms and by large numbers of online video creators.
