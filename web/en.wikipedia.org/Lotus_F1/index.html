Lotus F1 Team was a British Formula One team that competed from 2012 to 2015, racing from the Enstone factory of the former Renault team. Despite the name, it was not the historic Team Lotus; it took the Lotus name after Group Lotus became title sponsor of the Renault squad in 2011, and the operation was owned by Genii Capital.

History

With drivers Kimi Räikkönen and Romain Grosjean, Lotus F1 was a front-running team in 2012 and 2013: Räikkönen won the 2012 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix and the 2013 Australian Grand Prix and finished third in the 2012 drivers' championship. The team struggled in the new hybrid era in 2014, depended on pay driver Pastor Maldonado for funding, and fell into financial difficulty. In 2016 Renault bought the team back, and it raced as the Renault works team — today it competes as Alpine.
