Munzee

Munzee is a freemium scavenger hunt game where QR codes are hidden in real locations for players to find and scan with smartphones. It was developed in McKinney, Texas, and launched in 2011. The company behind the game takes its name from the German word for coin, Münze.

Gameplay

Players place (deploy) printed QR code stickers or tags called munzees in public places, and other players earn points by capturing them — scanning the code with the game's app within range of the location. Various munzee types offer different points, and virtual munzees can be deployed without a physical sticker. Special and temporary munzees appear for events and promotions.

Reception

The game is often compared with geocaching, though it uses QR codes rather than containers and logs. Munzee has been used in tourism, fundraising and local business promotion, and supports clan competitions and player-hosted events.
