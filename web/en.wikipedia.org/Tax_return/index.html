Tax return

A tax return is a form or set of forms filed with a tax authority reporting income, expenses and other pertinent tax information. Tax returns allow taxpayers to calculate their tax liability, schedule payments, or request refunds of overpaid taxes.

By country

In the United States, individuals file Form 1040 with the Internal Revenue Service, usually by 15 April. In the United Kingdom, taxpayers within self-assessment file a return with HM Revenue and Customs, while most employees pay through PAYE withholding. Many countries increasingly pre-fill returns using data reported by employers and banks, and electronic filing is now the norm in most jurisdictions.
