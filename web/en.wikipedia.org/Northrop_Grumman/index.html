Northrop Grumman Corporation is an American multinational aerospace and defence technology company. Formed by the 1994 merger of Northrop Corporation and Grumman Aerospace, it is one of the world's largest defence contractors and weapons manufacturers, with annual revenue exceeding US$36 billion and around 95,000 employees.

History

Northrop Corporation, founded by Jack Northrop in 1939, was known for its flying wing designs, culminating in the B-2 Spirit stealth bomber unveiled in 1988. Grumman, founded in 1929, built naval aircraft including the F-14 Tomcat and the Apollo Lunar Module. After the Cold War, the merged company grew through acquisitions including Litton Industries (2001), TRW (2002), and Orbital ATK (2018), which expanded its space and missile businesses.

Products and programs

Major programs include the B-21 Raider stealth bomber, the F-35 Lightning II (for which it is a principal partner, building the fuselage centre section), the E-2D Hawkeye airborne early warning aircraft, the RQ-4 Global Hawk unmanned aircraft, and aircraft carriers and submarines built by its Newport News shipyard in partnership with Huntington Ingalls. In space, Northrop Grumman built the Cygnus cargo spacecraft and major components of the James Webb Space Telescope, for which it was prime contractor.

Business sectors

The company operates four sectors: Aeronautics Systems, Defense Systems, Mission Systems, and Space Systems. It is a principal contractor for the U.S. Ground Based Strategic Deterrent (Sentinel) intercontinental ballistic missile program.
