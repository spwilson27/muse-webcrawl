E2 European long distance path

The E2 European long distance path, or E2 path, is one of the European long-distance paths designated by the European Ramblers Association (ERA). It runs approximately 4,850 kilometres (3,010 mi) from Galway in Ireland to Nice on the French Mediterranean coast, passing through the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland and France. Some sources describe a starting point at Stranraer in Scotland for the mainland section.

Route

The path begins in Galway, Ireland, and crosses the island before linking, via ferry connections, with trails in Scotland. From the Southern Uplands it follows routes including sections of the Southern Upland Way and St Cuthbert's Way into England, then uses paths such as the Pennine Way corridor and the Thames-side and North Downs routes towards the Channel ports. On the continent it passes through the Netherlands and Belgium — where it coincides in part with the GR5 — then through Luxembourg and eastern France, following GR routes down the Vosges, the Jura and the Alps (including sections shared with the GR5 and other paths) before descending to Nice on the Mediterranean.

Waymarking

As with other E-paths, waymarking is provided by the underlying national and regional trail systems, supplemented in places by E2 markers. The European Ramblers Association coordinates the network of twelve E-paths, of which the E2 is one of the longest.
