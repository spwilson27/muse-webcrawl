List of largest rock crystals and diamonds

This article lists the largest known rough diamonds by carat weight, together with examples of exceptionally large rock crystal (quartz) specimens. The Cullinan Diamond, found in South Africa in 1905, remains the largest gem-quality rough diamond ever discovered.

Large rock crystals

Notable rock crystal specimens include giant quartz clusters from Arkansas and Brazil, and the Crystal Cave (Cave of the Crystals) near Naica, Mexico, which contains selenite (gypsum) crystals up to about 12 metres long — among the largest natural crystals ever found.
