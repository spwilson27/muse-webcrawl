Yaxha

LocationPetén Department, Guatemala
CultureMaya
PeriodPreclassic to Postclassic

Yaxha is a Mesoamerican archaeological site of the Maya civilisation in the Petén region of Guatemala, on the north-east shore of Lake Yaxha. It was one of the largest Maya cities in the area, surpassed in the immediate region only by Tikal and Naranjo.

Description

The site contains more than 500 structures, including nine temple-pyramid complexes, two ball courts, palaces, and an astronomical observatory group. A sacbé (causeway) links the main groups, and the North Acropolis rises above the lake shore. Yaxha was occupied from the Middle Preclassic (c. 1000 BC) into the Postclassic period.

History

Yaxha flourished in the Classic period and was closely tied to the politics of Tikal and Naranjo. Its name, meaning "blue-green water", is also its ancient emblem glyph. The site lies within the Yaxha-Nakum-Naranjo National Park and gained wide recognition as a filming location for a season of the television series Survivor.
