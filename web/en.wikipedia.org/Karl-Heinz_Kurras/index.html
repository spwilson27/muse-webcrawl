Karl-Heinz Kurras

Karl-Heinz Kurras
Born: 1 December 1927, Bartenstein, East Prussia
Died: 16 December 2014, Berlin
Occupation: West Berlin police officer

Karl-Heinz Kurras (1927–2014) was a West Berlin police officer who shot and killed the student Benno Ohnesorg during a protest against the visit of the Shah of Iran on 2 June 1967. The killing became a turning point in the history of the West German student movement.

The shooting

Ohnesorg, a 26-year-old student attending his first demonstration, was shot in the back of the head in a courtyard near the Deutsche Oper. Kurras claimed self-defence and was acquitted in two trials, verdicts that outraged students and helped radicalise the protest movement; the date gave its name to the Movement 2 June.

Stasi revelation

In 2009, files uncovered in the Stasi archives revealed that Kurras had been an agent of the East German Ministry for State Security and a member of the SED since 1955. No evidence was found that the shooting itself was ordered by the Stasi. See also Benno Ohnesorg.
