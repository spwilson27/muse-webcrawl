EA Play (formerly EA Access on consoles and Origin Access on PC) is a subscription-based video game service from Electronic Arts. Subscribers gain access to a library of EA-published games ("The Play List"), early trial versions of upcoming releases, and a 10% discount on EA digital purchases.

EA Play offers subscribers a library of Electronic Arts games.

History

The service launched as EA Access on Xbox One in August 2014. Sony initially declined to offer it on PlayStation 4, but relented in 2019. A PC counterpart, Origin Access, launched in 2016, with a higher tier, Origin Access Premier, following in 2018 and offering new releases in full. In 2020 the services were consolidated under the EA Play name, with the premium tier renamed EA Play Pro, and the service was also made available through Steam.

Features

Members receive access to a rotating catalogue of back-catalogue titles, in-game rewards and member-only content, and "Play First" trials that typically allow ten hours of play of new games several days before their release date. EA Play is also bundled into the Xbox Game Pass Ultimate subscription at no extra cost.
