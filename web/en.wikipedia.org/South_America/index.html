Countries: 12 sovereign states

South America is a continent entirely in the Western Hemisphere and mostly in the Southern Hemisphere, bordered by the Pacific Ocean to the west, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, and North America and the Caribbean Sea to the northwest. It includes twelve sovereign states: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay and Venezuela, together with French Guiana and the Falkland Islands.

Geography

The continent is dominated by the Andes, the world's longest mountain range, running down its western edge, and by the Amazon basin, drained by the Amazon River and covered by the largest tropical rainforest on Earth. Other major features include the Brazilian Highlands, the Gran Chaco and Pampas grasslands, the Atacama Desert of northern Chile — among the driest places on Earth — and Patagonia in the far south. South America is home to Angel Falls, the world's highest waterfall, and to Lake Titicaca, the highest navigable lake.

History

South America has been inhabited for at least 15,000 years. Complex societies arose in the Andes, culminating in the Inca Empire, the largest empire in the pre-Columbian Americas. European colonisation began after 1498; Spain and Portugal divided the continent under the Treaty of Tordesillas, and independence movements led by figures such as Simón Bolívar and José de San Martín freed most of the continent in the early 19th century. The 20th century saw industrialisation, periods of military dictatorship, and a broad return to democracy from the 1980s.

Demographics and culture

The population of about 440 million is concentrated on the coasts. Portuguese-speaking Brazil accounts for roughly half the continent's people and land area; Spanish is the other dominant language, alongside hundreds of indigenous languages such as Quechua and Guarani. The population is ethnically diverse, descended from indigenous peoples, Europeans, Africans brought during the slave trade, and later immigrants from Europe and Asia. Roman Catholicism is the largest religion.

Economy

Major economies are Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia and Peru. The continent is rich in natural resources — copper, iron ore, oil, lithium and soybeans are leading exports — and regional integration is pursued through blocs such as Mercosur and the Pacific Alliance.
