Colombian Coffee Growers Federation

The Colombian Coffee Growers Federation (Spanish: Federación Nacional de Cafeteros de Colombia, often shortened to Fedecafé or FNC) is a non-profit business organization, popularly known for its marketing campaign featuring the fictional coffee farmer Juan Valdez.

History

Founded in 1927, the Federation represents over 500,000 coffee-growing families in Colombia. It provides support services including agricultural research through Cenicafé, extension services, price guarantees, infrastructure, and marketing of Colombian coffee worldwide.

Juan Valdez

Created in 1958, Juan Valdez — depicted with his mule Conchita carrying coffee sacks — became one of the most recognised advertising icons in the world, symbolising 100% Colombian coffee. The Juan Valdez Café chain, launched in 2002, operates coffee shops in Colombia and internationally.

Role

The Federation administers the National Coffee Fund, supports rural development, and promotes sustainability and quality standards for washed Arabica coffee grown in Colombia's mountainous coffee cultural landscape, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
