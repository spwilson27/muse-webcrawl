Leo (constellation)

Leo is one of the constellations of the zodiac, lying between Cancer to the west and Virgo to the east. It represents a lion and is recognisable by the "Sickle" asterism forming the lion's head, anchored by the bright star Regulus.

Stars and deep-sky objects

Leo's brightest stars include Regulus, Denebola and Algieba. The constellation contains many bright galaxies, including the Leo Triplet — M65, M66 and NGC 3628 — and the M96 Group, as well as the radiant of the Leonid meteor shower in November.
