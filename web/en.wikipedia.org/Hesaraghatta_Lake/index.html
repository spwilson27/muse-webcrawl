Hesaraghatta Lake

Hesaraghatta Lake is a humanmade reservoir located 18 km (11 mi) northwest of Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. It is a freshwater lake created in 1894 across the Arkavathy River to supply drinking water to the city of Bengaluru.

History

The lake was built under the direction of M. C. Sivaswamy Iyer, then Dewan of Mysore state, and the chief engineer M. Visvesvaraya's era of water works, as part of the "Chamarajendra Water Works" scheme. It covers an area of about 1,105 hectares (4.5 square miles) with a catchment of some 73.8 square kilometres.

Over time, siltation and reduced inflow from the Arkavathy diminished the lake's role; Bengaluru's water supply shifted largely to the Cauvery river. The lake and its surroundings are a noted birdwatching site, hosting waterbirds and raptors, and the area contains institutions such as the Indian Institute of Horticultural Research.
