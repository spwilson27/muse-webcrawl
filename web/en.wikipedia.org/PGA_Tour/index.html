The PGA Tour (stylised as PGA TOUR) is the organiser of professional golf tours in North America. It operates the main men's professional golf tour in the United States and North America, as well as developmental and senior tours. Its season consists of dozens of tournaments, mostly in the United States, culminating in the FedEx Cup playoffs.

Tournament golf for professionals in America was organised by the Professional Golfers' Association of America (PGA of America) from 1916. In 1968, touring professionals broke away to form their own organisation, the Tournament Players Division, which became the PGA Tour. The headquarters are in Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida, adjacent to TPC Sawgrass, home of The Players Championship, often described as golf's unofficial "fifth major".

Structure

The PGA Tour is a non-profit organisation; its events are mostly organised by local charitable organisations, and the tour reports that its tournaments have generated billions of dollars for charity. Players earn and retain playing privileges ("tour cards") mainly through the prior season's FedEx Cup points list, the Korn Ferry Tour (its developmental circuit), and qualifying school. The tour also runs PGA Tour Champions for golfers aged 50 and over, PGA Tour Americas, and formerly operated tours in Canada, China and Latin America.

Season and majors

Since 2013 the season has followed a wrap-around calendar beginning in the autumn. The four major championships — the Masters, PGA Championship, U.S. Open and The Open Championship — are not owned by the PGA Tour but are included in its schedule and money lists. From 2007 the season-ending FedEx Cup competition has awarded large bonuses to the leading points earners.

LIV Golf dispute

In 2022 the Saudi-backed LIV Golf league began signing prominent PGA Tour players, leading the tour to suspend defectors and prompting antitrust litigation. In June 2023 the PGA Tour, DP World Tour and Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund announced a framework agreement to combine their commercial operations, a deal whose completion remained under negotiation in subsequent years.
