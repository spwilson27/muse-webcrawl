Super Smash Bros.

Super Smash Bros. is a series of crossover fighting video games published by Nintendo, featuring characters from its own franchises and, in later entries, from third-party series. The series was created by Masahiro Sakurai, who directed every game. Rather than depleting an opponent's health bar, players win by knocking opponents off the stage; damage raises a percentage meter that makes characters fly farther when struck.

Super Smash Bros.
Genre: Fighting, platform
Developer: HAL Laboratory; Sora Ltd.; Bandai Namco Studios
Publisher: Nintendo
Creator: Masahiro Sakurai
First release: Super Smash Bros. (1999, Nintendo 64)

Games

The original game appeared on the Nintendo 64 in 1999 with 12 characters. Super Smash Bros. Melee (2001, GameCube) became a long-lived competitive title; Brawl (2008, Wii) added third-party characters such as Solid Snake and Sonic the Hedgehog; entries for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U followed in 2014; and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (2018, Nintendo Switch) included every previous playable fighter and sold over 30 million copies, making it the best-selling fighting game of all time.

Gameplay

Matches support up to eight players in later entries and mix platforming movement, items, and stage hazards with character movesets drawn from each fighter's home series.
