Ducati Motor Holding S.p.A. is an Italian motorcycle manufacturer headquartered in Bologna, Italy. The company is owned by German automotive manufacturer Audi through its Italian subsidiary Lamborghini, both part of the Volkswagen Group. Ducati is renowned for high-performance motorcycles built around desmodromic valve actuation, L-twin (and later V4) engines and trellis frames, and for its success in Superbike and MotoGP racing.

History

The company was founded in 1926 by Antonio Cavalieri Ducati and his three sons — Adriano, Marcello and Bruno — as a maker of radio components. After its factory was destroyed in the Second World War, Ducati turned to motorized bicycles, launching the Cucciolo auxiliary engine in 1946. Under engineer Fabio Taglioni, appointed in 1954, the company developed the desmodromic valve system and a string of racing successes that defined its sporting identity.

Products

Modern ranges include the Panigale superbikes, the Monster naked bikes (introduced 1993 and credited with saving the company), the Multistrada adventure tourers, the Diavel power cruiser, the retro-styled Scrambler line, and electric MotoE racers. Ducati Corse, the racing division, won the MotoGP riders' championship with Casey Stoner in 2007 and Francesco Bagnaia in 2022 and 2023, alongside numerous World Superbike titles.
