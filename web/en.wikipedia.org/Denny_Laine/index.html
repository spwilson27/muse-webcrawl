Denny Laine (born Brian Frederick Hines; 29 October 1944 – 5 December 2023) was an English musician, singer and songwriter, known as a founding member of the Moody Blues and of Paul McCartney's band Wings. He played guitar, bass and keyboards and sang lead vocals on the Moody Blues' early hit "Go Now" (1964).

Career

Laine co-founded the Moody Blues in Birmingham in 1964, leaving in 1966 as the band's style changed. After spells with his Electric String Band and the group Balls, and the solo single "Say You Don't Mind", he joined McCartney and Linda McCartney in 1971 to form Wings. He remained the only constant member besides the McCartneys throughout the band's decade-long career, co-writing songs including the hit "Mull of Kintyre" (1977), one of the best-selling UK singles of all time.

Later life

After Wings disbanded in 1981, Laine pursued a solo career, toured with tribute and classic-rock line-ups, and released numerous albums. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of the Moody Blues in 2018. He died in Florida in December 2023 of interstitial lung disease.
