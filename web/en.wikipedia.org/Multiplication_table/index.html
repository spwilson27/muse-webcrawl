Multiplication table

TypeMathematical table
OperationMultiplication

A multiplication table (sometimes called a times table) is a mathematical table used to define a multiplication operation for an algebraic system. The decimal multiplication table — products of numbers typically from 1 to 10 or 1 to 12 — is traditionally taught as an essential part of elementary arithmetic around the world.

History

The oldest known multiplication tables were used by the Babylonians about 4,000 years ago in base 60, and base-10 tables appear in Chinese bamboo strips from the Warring States period. Memorisation of the standard table by schoolchildren has a long history, though its extent remains a subject of debate in mathematics education.

Patterns

The table displays clear patterns: symmetry about the diagonal (commutativity), rows for 9 whose digits sum to 9, and square numbers on the main diagonal. Modular multiplication tables are used in abstract algebra for groups and other structures.
