Environmental impact

Environmental impact refers to the effect that human activities, projects or products have on the natural environment, including air, water, soil, ecosystems and climate.

Assessment

Environmental impact assessment (EIA) is a formal process, required by law in many countries since the US National Environmental Policy Act of 1969, that predicts and evaluates the effects of proposed developments before decisions are made.

Types of impact

Impacts may be direct or indirect, short or long term, reversible or irreversible, and include pollution, habitat loss, resource depletion and greenhouse gas emissions. Life-cycle assessment extends the analysis across a product's whole life.
