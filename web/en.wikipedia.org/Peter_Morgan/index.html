Peter Morgan

Peter Julian Robin Morgan (born 10 April 1963) is a British screenwriter and playwright. He is best known as the creator of the Netflix historical drama series The Crown, and for films exploring recent British history and politics, including The Queen (2006), Frost/Nixon (2008), and The Last King of Scotland (2006).

Career

Morgan's recurring examination of Tony Blair, Gordon Brown, and the Royal Family — across The Deal (2003), The Queen, The Special Relationship (2010), and the stage play The Audience (2013) — made him a defining dramatist of modern British political life. He received Academy Award nominations for The Queen and Frost/Nixon, and multiple Emmy and BAFTA awards for The Crown (2016–2023).
