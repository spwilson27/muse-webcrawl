Weekend Update

Weekend Update is a Saturday Night Live sketch and satirical news segment that has aired since the show's premiere on 11 October 1975. It is typically the longest-running recurring sketch on the show, featuring a news-anchor desk from which jokes about current events are delivered, often with guest appearances by cast members and celebrity hosts in character.

History

Chevy Chase was the first anchor, famous for opening with "I'm Chevy Chase, and you're not," and for his pratfalls as President Gerald Ford. Later anchors included Jane Curtin, Dan Aykroyd, Dennis Miller, Norm Macdonald, Jimmy Fallon, Tina Fey, Amy Poehler and Seth Meyers. Since 2014 the segment has been anchored by Colin Jost and Michael Che, the longest-serving duo in its history, who trade jokes in a "Joke Swap" tradition each Christmas and season finale.

Format

The segment parodies television news broadcasts. Jost and Che read one-liners about politics and popular culture, followed by character interviews — guests such as "Girl You Wish You Hadn't Started a Conversation With at a Party" or Drunk Uncle. Recurring desk pieces and cameo politicians have made the segment a barometer of American political satire; candidates frequently appear in person during election seasons.
