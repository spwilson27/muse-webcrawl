Château-Chalon AOC

TypeAppellation d'origine contrôlée (AOC)
RegionJura, France
Wine typeVin jaune (white)
GrapeSavagnin (100%)
AOC established1936

Château-Chalon is an Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée (AOC) for wine in the Jura wine region of eastern France. It is made exclusively from the Savagnin grape and solely as vin jaune, the celebrated oxidative "yellow wine" of the Jura.

Production

Under AOC rules, the grapes must come from vineyards on the limestone and grey marl slopes around the villages of Château-Chalon, Voiteur, Menétru-le-Vignoble, and Nevy-sur-Seille. The wine is aged in oak barrels for a minimum of six years and three months, during which a veil of yeast (voile), similar to the flor of sherry, develops on the surface and imparts the wine's distinctive nutty, curry-like flavours.

Vin jaune is bottled in a distinctive squat 62-centilitre bottle called a clavelin, said to represent the volume remaining from a litre after the long barrel ageing.

Reputation

Château-Chalon is widely regarded as the finest expression of vin jaune, capable of ageing for many decades. In years when the harvest does not meet the appellation's standards, producers may declassify the wine or decline to release it under the AOC.
