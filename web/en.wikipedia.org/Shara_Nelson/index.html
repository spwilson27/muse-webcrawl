Shara Nelson


Born: London, England
Genres: Trip hop, soul, electronic
Occupation: Singer, songwriter
Associated acts: Massive Attack

Shara Nelson is a British singer and songwriter best known for her work with the Bristol trip hop group Massive Attack. Her soulful vocals feature on the group's debut album Blue Lines (1991), including the hit single "Unfinished Sympathy", widely regarded as one of the greatest songs of the 1990s, and "Safe from Harm".

Career

Nelson began her career in the 1980s, releasing singles with the group The Wild Bunch's circle in Bristol. After the success of Blue Lines, she left Massive Attack to pursue a solo career. Her debut solo album, What Silence Knows (1993), produced by Mike Peden, spawned the UK top-20 single "Down That Road" and earned her a Mercury Prize nomination. A second album, Friendly Fire, followed in 1995.

Later work

Nelson has continued to record and collaborate, contributing vocals to other artists' projects and releasing further solo material, including the album End of the World (2019) with producer Charles Webster under the name Present.
