Thelema

Thelema is a religious and philosophical movement developed in the early 1900s by the British writer and ceremonial magician Aleister Crowley. Its central text is The Book of the Law (Liber AL vel Legis), which Crowley said he received in Cairo in 1904.

Beliefs

Thelema's core precepts are "Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law" and "Love is the law, love under will". Adherents, called Thelemites, seek to discover and enact their True Will.
