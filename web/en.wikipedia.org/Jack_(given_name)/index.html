Jack (given name)

Jack is a given name, traditionally a masculine name. Originally a diminutive of John, it has long been used as a name in its own right, and it can also derive as a short form of Jacob or Jackson.

History

Jack became common in medieval England: by the 13th century Jack (from Jakke, a pet form of John via Jankin) was so widespread that it became a generic term for a man or a labourer, appearing in phrases such as "jack of all trades" and in folklore figures including Jack the Giant Killer and Jack and the Beanstalk.

Popularity

Jack was among the most popular boys' names in the United Kingdom for much of the late 20th and early 21st centuries, ranking first in England and Wales in many years between 1996 and 2008. It has also been consistently popular in Ireland, Australia, and the United States.
