Ligue 1 is the top division of the French football league system, administered by the Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP). Founded in 1932 as the National championship, it took the name Division 1 and, from 2002, Ligue 1. It is one of Europe's "big five" leagues, alongside England's Premier League, Spain's La Liga, Italy's Serie A and Germany's Bundesliga.

Format

The league has consisted of 18 clubs since the 2023–24 season, reduced from 20. Each club plays the others home and away (34 matches). The top clubs qualify for the UEFA Champions League and other European competitions, while the bottom two clubs are relegated to Ligue 2 and the 16th-placed club enters a play-off against a Ligue 2 side.

History and champions

Professional football was legalised in France in 1932, and the first championship was won by Olympique Lillois. Saint-Étienne dominated the 1960s and 1970s, winning a record ten titles that stood until Paris Saint-Germain surpassed it in the 2020s. Olympique de Marseille won the European Cup in 1993, the only French club to do so. Olympique Lyonnais won seven consecutive titles from 2002 to 2008. Since the Qatari takeover of PSG in 2011, the Paris club has dominated the league, assembling star players including Zlatan Ibrahimović, Neymar and Kylian Mbappé.

Notable records

Delio Onnis is the league's all-time top scorer with 299 goals. PSG's recent titles have made them the most successful club in French league history, ahead of Saint-Étienne and Marseille.
