Health informatics

Health informatics is the interdisciplinary field concerned with the acquisition, storage, retrieval and use of information in health and medicine. It applies information technology and data science to clinical care, public health, biomedical research and health administration, with the aim of improving outcomes, safety and efficiency.

Key areas

Core applications include electronic health records (EHRs), clinical decision support systems, telemedicine, health information exchange, and standards for interoperability such as HL7 and FHIR. Subspecialties include clinical informatics (applied to patient care), nursing informatics, public health informatics and bioinformatics, which deals with molecular and genomic data.

Challenges

The field grapples with data privacy and security, poor usability that contributes to clinician burnout, inconsistent data quality, and the difficulty of exchanging information between incompatible systems. Recent work applies machine learning to diagnosis, risk prediction and hospital operations, raising further questions of validation and bias.
