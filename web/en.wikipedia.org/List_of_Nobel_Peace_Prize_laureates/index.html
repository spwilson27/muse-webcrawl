List of Nobel Peace Prize laureates

[Image: The Nobel Peace Prize medal]
The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in Oslo, Norway.

The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded annually by the Norwegian Nobel Committee to those who have done the most or the best work for fraternity between nations, the abolition or reduction of standing armies, and the holding and promotion of peace congresses. First awarded in 1901, it is selected by a committee appointed by the Norwegian Parliament, in accordance with Alfred Nobel's will.

The International Committee of the Red Cross has received the prize three times (1917, 1944, 1963), the most of any recipient. The prize was not awarded in several years, notably during the two world wars.
