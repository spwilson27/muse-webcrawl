Averøy is a municipality in Møre og Romsdal county, Norway. It is part of the Nordmøre region and consists of the island of Averøya and numerous smaller islands and islets on Norway's west coast. The administrative centre is the village of Bruhaugen.

Geography and attractions

The island is connected to the mainland by the famous Atlantic Ocean Road (Atlanterhavsveien), a scenic route of bridges and causeways opened in 1989 linking Averøy to Eide and the mainland, and to Kristiansund via the Atlantic Ocean Tunnel, opened in 2009. Older attractions include the Kvernes Stave Church, dating from the seventeenth century, and Bremsnes Cave.

Economy

Fishing and fish processing, agriculture, and tourism are the principal industries. The municipality covers about 176 square kilometres and has a population of roughly 5,800.
