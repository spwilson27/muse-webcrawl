Gamescom

Gamescom
Venue: Koelnmesse, Cologne, Germany
First held: 2009
Attendance: ~370,000 (2019)

Gamescom (stylized as gamescom) is a trade fair for video games held annually at the Koelnmesse in Cologne, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany. It is the world's largest gaming event by exhibition space and attendance, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors together with publishers, developers and media. It is organized by game – the German Games Industry Association, together with Koelnmesse.

History

Gamescom was first held in 2009, succeeding the Games Convention in Leipzig. It combines public consumer halls with a business area for trade visitors. Since 2019 the show has opened with "Opening Night Live", a streamed showcase hosted by Geoff Keighley featuring world premieres and announcements. International spin-off events have included gamescom asia in Singapore and gamescom latam in São Paulo, Brazil.

Scale

The event typically occupies most of the Koelnmesse exhibition complex, with queues for hands-on demos of upcoming titles, cosplay areas, esports stages, an indie area and a retro gaming section. The devcom developer conference takes place alongside it.
