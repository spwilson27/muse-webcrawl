The 2020 Democratic Party presidential primaries and caucuses were a series of electoral contests organized by the Democratic Party to select the roughly 3,979 pledged delegates to the 2020 Democratic National Convention. The primaries took place within all 50 U.S. states, the District of Columbia, and five U.S. territories, between February 3 and August 11, 2020.

Former vice president Joe Biden emerged as the presumptive nominee after a strong showing on Super Tuesday, when he won 10 of the 14 states holding contests. Senator Bernie Sanders of Vermont, the runner-up, suspended his campaign on April 8, 2020, making Biden the presumptive nominee. Biden formally secured the nomination in June after surpassing the required delegate threshold.

Background

Following the party's defeat in the 2016 presidential election, the Democratic field for 2020 became one of the largest in American history, with 29 major candidates declaring their candidacies at various points. The field included Senators Elizabeth Warren, Kamala Harris, Amy Klobuchar, and Cory Booker, former mayor Pete Buttigieg, businessman Andrew Yang, and billionaire Michael Bloomberg, who entered the race in November 2019.

Results and key contests

The Iowa caucuses of February 3, 2020, were marred by reporting delays caused by a malfunctioning results app, with Buttigieg and Sanders effectively tied. Sanders won the New Hampshire primary and the Nevada caucuses, briefly becoming the front-runner. Biden's decisive victory in the South Carolina primary on February 29, powered by African American voters and an endorsement from Congressman Jim Clyburn, revived his campaign days before Super Tuesday.

National convention

Because of the COVID-19 pandemic, the 2020 Democratic National Convention was held largely virtually from August 17 to 20, with its formal hub in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Delegates formally nominated Biden, who had selected Senator Kamala Harris as his running mate on August 11. The Biden–Harris ticket went on to win the November general election against incumbent president Donald Trump.
