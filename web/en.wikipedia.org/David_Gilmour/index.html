David Gilmour

David Jon Gilmour CBE (born 6 March 1946) is an English guitarist, singer and songwriter who is a member of the rock band Pink Floyd. He joined the group in 1967, shortly before the departure of founding member Syd Barrett, and became its guitarist and co-lead vocalist.

Career

Gilmour's lyrical, sustain-rich guitar playing and solos on albums such as The Dark Side of the Moon, Wish You Were Here and The Wall made him one of rock's most acclaimed guitarists. After Roger Waters left in 1985, Gilmour led Pink Floyd through further albums including A Momentary Lapse of Reason and The Division Bell. His solo albums include On an Island (2006), Rattle That Lock (2015) and Luck and Strange (2024). In 2022 he revived the Pink Floyd name with Nick Mason to release the charity single "Hey, Hey, Rise Up!" in support of Ukraine.
