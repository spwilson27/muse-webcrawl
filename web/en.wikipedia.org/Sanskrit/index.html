Sanskrit

Sanskrit
Family: Indo-European, Indo-Aryan
Earliest form: Vedic Sanskrit (~1500–500 BCE)
Script: Devanagari and other Brahmic scripts
Status: liturgical, scholarly; listed language of India

Sanskrit is a classical language of South Asia, belonging to the Indo-Aryan branch, and the sacred language of Hinduism and the liturgical language of Mahayana Buddhism and Jainism.

History

Vedic Sanskrit is the language of the Vedas, including the Rigveda. Pāṇini's grammar (c. 5th–4th century BCE) codified Classical Sanskrit, used for epics (Mahabharata, Ramayana), drama, philosophy, science and poetry for over two millennia.

Influence

Sanskrit vocabulary permeates modern Indo-Aryan, Dravidian and Southeast Asian languages, including Sinhala. Its study by European scholars helped found comparative Indo-European linguistics.
