The American Museum of Natural History (AMNH) is a natural history museum on the Upper West Side of Manhattan in New York City. Located in Theodore Roosevelt Park, across the street from Central Park, the museum complex comprises more than twenty interconnected buildings housing permanent exhibition halls, a library, and the Hayden Planetarium.

Founded in 1869, the museum is one of the largest natural history museums in the world, with a scientific staff of more than 200 and collections of over 34 million specimens of plants, animals, fossils, minerals, rocks, meteorites, human remains, and human cultural artifacts.

Exhibitions

Well-known halls include the Milstein Hall of Ocean Life, famous for its 94-foot model of a blue whale; the fossil halls displaying dinosaur skeletons such as Tyrannosaurus rex and a Titanosaur cast; the Hall of Human Origins; and the Rose Center for Earth and Space, opened in 2000, which houses the Hayden Planetarium. The Richard Gilder Center for Science, Education, and Innovation opened in 2023.

Research

The museum sponsors the Richard Gilder Graduate School, which grants doctoral degrees, and conducts field research on every continent. Its exhibits and collections have made it a prominent setting in popular culture, including the film Night at the Museum.
