Million Dollar Highway

RouteU.S. Route 550, Colorado
SectionSilverton to Ouray, about 25 miles
SummitRed Mountain Pass, 11,018 ft (3,358 m)

The Million Dollar Highway is the name given to the portion of U.S. Highway 550 in southwestern Colorado between Silverton and Ouray, traversing the San Juan Mountains via Red Mountain Pass.

Description

The road is famous for its dramatic scenery and its hazards: steep cliffs, narrow lanes, hairpin turns, and a lack of guardrails, particularly through the Uncompahgre Gorge. It passes abandoned mines and the old mining town of Ironton.

History

The route follows a toll road built in the 1880s by Otto Mears to serve the mining camps of the Red Mountain district. The name's origin is disputed — it may refer to the cost of construction or to fill dirt said to contain valuable ore.
