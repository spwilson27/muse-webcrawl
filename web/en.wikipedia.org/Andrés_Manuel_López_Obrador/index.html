Andrés Manuel López Obrador
Born: 13 November 1953, Tepetitán, Tabasco, Mexico
Office: President of Mexico (2018–2024)
Party: Morena
Nickname: AMLO

Andrés Manuel López Obrador (born 13 November 1953), commonly known by his initials AMLO, is a Mexican politician who served as the 65th president of Mexico from 1 December 2018 to 30 September 2024. A dominant figure of the Mexican left for three decades, he previously served as Head of Government of Mexico City (2000–2005) and ran unsuccessfully for the presidency in 2006 and 2012 before winning decisively in 2018.

Early career

Born in Tepetitán, Tabasco, López Obrador studied political science at the National Autonomous University of Mexico and worked in Tabasco politics within the Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI) before joining the breakaway movement that formed the Party of the Democratic Revolution (PRD) in 1989. As mayor of Mexico City he built an enduring popular base with public works such as the second level of the Periférico ring road and a widely copied old-age pension scheme.

Road to the presidency

After the disputed 2006 election, which he lost by a fraction of a percent, López Obrador declared himself the “legitimate president” and led months of protests. Following a second defeat in 2012 he left the PRD and founded the Morena movement, which became a party in 2014. Campaigning against corruption, inequality, and the political establishment he called the “mafia of power”, he won the 2018 election with about 53 per cent of the vote.

Presidency

His administration, dubbed the “Fourth Transformation”, raised the minimum wage and pensions, expanded cash-transfer programmes, and pursued austerity in government, while pressing ahead with signature projects including the Maya Train and the Dos Bocas refinery. Critics charged him with confrontations against autonomous institutions, journalists, and the judiciary, and with militarising public security; supporters credited him with reducing poverty and restoring the presidency's social mission. He left office with high approval ratings and was succeeded by his protégée, Claudia Sheinbaum, in October 2024.
