MrBeast Burger is an American virtual dining restaurant chain founded and owned by the YouTube personality Jimmy Donaldson (MrBeast), in partnership with Virtual Dining Concepts. It was launched on 19 December 2020 in Wilson, North Carolina, accompanied by a video in which Donaldson gave away food and money at a pop-up location. The concept operates as a "ghost kitchen" model: existing restaurants prepare the branded menu for delivery-app orders, allowing it to launch at hundreds of locations at once across the United States, and later in several other countries.

The menu includes items named for Donaldson's collaborators, such as the Beast Style burger, the Chandler Style, the Karl's Grilled Cheese, and crinkle-cut "Beast Style" fries. In September 2022 the first physical MrBeast Burger restaurant opened at the American Dream mall in New Jersey, drawing a crowd estimated in the thousands.

Legal dispute

In 2023, Donaldson sued Virtual Dining Concepts, alleging quality-control failures and reputational harm, seeking to end the partnership; Virtual Dining Concepts countersued. The dispute brought wide attention to the virtual-dining business model and its quality risks.
