CentOS

CentOS
Developer: The CentOS Project (affiliated with Red Hat)
OS family: Linux (Unix-like)
Initial release: 14 May 2004
Final release: CentOS Linux 8 (EOL 2021); CentOS 7 (EOL 2024)
Successor: CentOS Stream; community forks Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux

CentOS (Community Enterprise Operating System) was a discontinued Linux distribution that provided a free and open-source, community-supported computing platform functionally compatible with its upstream source, Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL). The first CentOS release was in May 2004, numbered CentOS 2.

History

CentOS was built from the publicly released source packages of RHEL, with Red Hat branding removed, and became one of the most widely used distributions for web servers and enterprise computing. In January 2014 Red Hat announced it would sponsor the CentOS Project, employing many of its core developers.

End of CentOS Linux and CentOS Stream

In December 2020 Red Hat announced that development of CentOS Linux, the downstream rebuild of RHEL, would be discontinued in favour of CentOS Stream, a rolling-release distribution positioned just upstream of RHEL. CentOS Linux 8 reached end of life at the end of 2021 and CentOS 7 in June 2024. The change prompted community members to found the Rocky Linux and AlmaLinux projects to continue providing RHEL-compatible downstream builds.
