Butter chicken

Butter chicken (Hindi: murgh makhani) is a curry of chicken in a spiced, creamy tomato and butter sauce. It is one of the best-known Indian dishes worldwide and a staple of Indian restaurants abroad.

Preparation

Chicken is marinated in yogurt and spices such as garam masala, ginger and chilli, traditionally cooked in a tandoor, then simmered in a makhani sauce of tomato, butter and cream, often finished with fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi).

History

The dish was created in Delhi in the 1950s at the Moti Mahal restaurant, where the cooks combined leftover tandoori chicken with a rich tomato-butter gravy. Its invention is credited to the restaurant's founders, including Kundan Lal Gujral; a court dispute in India has contested the rival claims of the Daryaganj restaurant chain.
