List of national parks of Uganda

[Image: Mountain gorilla in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park]
Bwindi is famous for its mountain gorillas.

Uganda has ten national parks, managed by the Uganda Wildlife Authority. Together they protect a wide range of habitats, from savanna and wetlands to montane rainforest, and include two UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

National parks

ParkArea (km²)Known for
Bwindi Impenetrable National Park331Mountain gorilla trekking; World Heritage Site
Kibale National Park776Chimpanzees and other primates
Kidepo Valley National Park1,442Remote savanna wildlife in the northeast
Lake Mburo National Park260Zebra, impala and wetlands near Kampala
Mgahinga Gorilla National Park34Gorillas and golden monkeys in the Virunga range
Mount Elgon National Park1,121Extinct volcano with a large caldera
Murchison Falls National Park3,840The Nile forced through a narrow gorge; largest park
Queen Elizabeth National Park1,978Diverse ecosystems; tree-climbing lions
Rwenzori Mountains National Park996Glaciated "Mountains of the Moon"; World Heritage Site
Semuliki National Park220Lowland rainforest and hot springs

Uganda's parks are complemented by wildlife reserves and forest reserves, and tourism centred on primate trekking is a major source of conservation funding.
