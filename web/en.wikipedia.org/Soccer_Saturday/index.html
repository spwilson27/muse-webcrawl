Soccer Saturday is a weekly television programme broadcast on Sky Sports in the United Kingdom and Ireland during the football season. The show provides live scores, results, and updates from matches across the English and Scottish league systems, presented by a host alongside a panel of pundits who watch matches on monitors and report events as they happen.

Format

The programme typically runs from midday until early evening on Saturdays. Because of the UK's Saturday 3 p.m. television blackout on live football, the show cannot broadcast live match footage; instead, studio reporters at grounds and pundits in the studio describe the action. A videprinter displays incoming scores and goals at the bottom of the screen, and the show concludes with the full classified results.

History

The programme began in 1992 as Sports Saturday, covering multiple sports, before being relaunched as Soccer Saturday in 1998. Jeff Stelling became its defining presenter, hosting for nearly three decades and becoming famous for his rapid, statistic-laden delivery and wordplay. His catchphrases and the on-screen banter with pundits such as Paul Merson, Matt Le Tissier, Phil Thompson, and Charlie Nicholas made the show a cultural fixture. Stelling stepped down at the end of the 2022–23 season and was succeeded by Simon Thomas.

Pundits and features

Regular features have included half-time analysis, the "Soccer Saturday Super 6" prediction game, and Soccer Special editions covering midweek fixtures. The panel was significantly reshaped in 2020 when several long-serving pundits departed. Chris Kamara's enthusiastic, sometimes confused reports from grounds became a beloved part of the show's identity.

Reception

The programme is widely credited with transforming the football results service into entertainment, influencing later shows in the genre. It has been parodied frequently, including references to Stelling's rivalry-flavoured jokes about Hartlepool United, the club he supports.
