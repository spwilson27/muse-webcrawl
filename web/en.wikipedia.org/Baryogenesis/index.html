Baryogenesis

In physical cosmology, baryogenesis is the physical process that took place during the early universe to produce baryonic asymmetry — the imbalance of matter (baryons) and antimatter (antibaryons) in the observed universe.

Sakharov conditions

Andrei Sakharov proposed three necessary conditions: baryon number violation, C and CP violation, and interactions out of thermal equilibrium.

Theories

Proposed mechanisms include electroweak baryogenesis and leptogenesis, in which a lepton asymmetry is converted into a baryon asymmetry.

Open problem

The Standard Model does not provide enough CP violation or a strong enough phase transition to explain the observed asymmetry, making baryogenesis a major open problem.
