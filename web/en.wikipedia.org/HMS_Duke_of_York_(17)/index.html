HMS Duke of York (17)

HMS Duke of York (pennant number 17) was a King George V-class battleship of the British Royal Navy, launched in 1940 and commissioned in 1941. Armed with ten 14-inch guns and displacing about 42,000 tons at full load, she served throughout the Second World War, most famously as the flagship that helped sink the German battleship Scharnhorst.

Service history

Duke of York escorted Arctic convoys to the Soviet Union and carried Prime Minister Winston Churchill across the Atlantic in December 1941 for the Arcadia Conference with President Franklin D. Roosevelt. On 26 December 1943, as flagship of Admiral Sir Bruce Fraser, she engaged Scharnhorst at the Battle of the North Cape, scoring the hits that doomed the German ship. In 1945 she joined the British Pacific Fleet as flagship for the final operations against Japan and was present in Tokyo Bay for the Japanese surrender. She became flagship of the Home Fleet after the war, was placed in reserve in 1949, and was broken up in 1957–58.
