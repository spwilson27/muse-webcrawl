Fedor Emelianenko

Fedor Vladimirovich Emelianenko (born 28 September 1976) is a Russian former professional mixed martial artist, sambist and judoka, widely regarded as one of the greatest heavyweight fighters of all time. Nicknamed "The Last Emperor", he was undefeated in 28 fights over a decade and reigned as PRIDE Heavyweight Champion from 2003 until the organisation's dissolution in 2007.

Career

Emelianenko rose to prominence in Japan's PRIDE Fighting Championships, defeating Antônio Rodrigo Nogueira to win the heavyweight title in 2003 and defending it against opponents including Mirko Cro Cop in their celebrated 2005 bout. After PRIDE he fought for Affliction and Strikeforce, where his long unbeaten run ended in 2010 against Fabrício Werdum.

Later career and legacy

He retired in 2012, returned in 2015, and finished his career with Bellator MMA, fighting his final bout in February 2023. A multiple-time world champion in combat sambo, Emelianenko is noted for his stoic demeanour, explosive striking and seamless transitions between striking and grappling, and is routinely ranked the greatest heavyweight in MMA history.
