Keyboard instrument

A keyboard instrument is a musical instrument played using a keyboard, a row of levers pressed by the fingers. The most common are the piano, organ and various electronic keyboards and synthesisers.

History

The earliest keyboard instrument was the ancient Greek hydraulis, a pipe organ. Harpsichords and clavichords flourished in the Baroque era before the piano, invented around 1700 by Bartolomeo Cristofori, became dominant. Modern instruments include digital pianos and MIDI controllers.
