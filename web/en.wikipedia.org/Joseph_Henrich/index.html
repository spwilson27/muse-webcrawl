Joseph Henrich
Born: 1968
Fields: Anthropology, human behavioural ecology
Known for: WEIRD psychology, cultural evolution

Joseph Patrick Henrich (born 1968) is an American anthropologist and professor of human evolutionary biology at Harvard University. He is known for research on cultural evolution and for highlighting that behavioural science subjects are often WEIRD — Western, Educated, Industrialised, Rich and Democratic.

Work

Henrich conducted fieldwork with the Machiguenga of Peru and in Fiji, studying fairness, cooperation and the transmission of norms. His books include The Secret of Our Success (2015), on how culture drove human evolution, and The WEIRDest People in the World (2020), on how medieval Western Church marriage policies shaped psychology and institutions.

WEIRD

His 2010 paper with Steven Heine and Ara Norenzayan, "The weirdest people in the world?", argued that WEIRD subjects are frequent outliers, challenging the generalisability of much psychological research.
