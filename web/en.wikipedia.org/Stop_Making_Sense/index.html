Stop Making Sense

Directed byJonathan Demme
StarringTalking Heads
Released1984
Runtime88 minutes

Stop Making Sense is a 1984 American concert film featuring a live performance by the rock band Talking Heads, directed by Jonathan Demme. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest concert films ever made, and was selected for the United States National Film Registry in 2021.

The performance

Filmed over three nights at Hollywood's Pantages Theatre in December 1983, the show builds piece by piece: David Byrne begins alone with a boombox and acoustic guitar for "Psycho Killer", and band members, instruments, and set elements are added song by song until the full nine-piece ensemble performs. Highlights include Byrne's oversized "big suit" during "Girlfriend Is Better" and performances of "Once in a Lifetime", "Burning Down the House", and "This Must Be the Place (Naive Melody)".

Reception and legacy

Critics praised Demme's unobtrusive direction and the band's theatrical staging. The accompanying live album was a major success. A restored 4K version was released in 2023, accompanied by a reunion of the band, and the film continues to top lists of the best music documentaries and concert films.
