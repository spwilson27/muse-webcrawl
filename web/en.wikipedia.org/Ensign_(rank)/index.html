Ensign (rank)

Ensign is a junior commissioned officer rank in many navies and, historically, armies. The word derives from the Latin insignia and the ensign or flag the officer once carried.

Naval use

In the United States Navy and Coast Guard, ensign (O-1) is the most junior commissioned rank, equivalent to second lieutenant in other services. Newly commissioned officers from the Naval Academy, ROTC or Officer Candidate School typically begin as ensigns.

Historical army use

In early modern European armies an ensign carried the regimental colours; the rank was later replaced by second lieutenant in the British Army in the 19th century.
