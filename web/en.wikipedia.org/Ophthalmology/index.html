Ophthalmology is the branch of medicine and surgery concerned with the diagnosis and treatment of disorders of the eye and the visual system. An ophthalmologist is a physician who specializes in eye care, trained in both medical and surgical management of eye disease, in distinction to optometrists and opticians, who focus respectively on vision testing and correction and on dispensing lenses.

Scope

Ophthalmology covers the anatomy, physiology, and diseases of the eyeball and orbit, the eyelids and lacrimal system, and the visual pathways. Subspecialties include cornea and external disease, cataract and refractive surgery, glaucoma, neuro-ophthalmology, oculoplastics, pediatric ophthalmology, and vitreoretinal surgery, which deals with the retina and vitreous.

Examination

A comprehensive eye examination typically includes measurement of visual acuity, refraction, intraocular pressure (tonometry), pupil assessment, visual field testing, and examination of the anterior segment with a slit lamp and of the retina with an ophthalmoscope or fundus camera. Modern imaging, especially optical coherence tomography (OCT), allows cross-sectional imaging of the retina and optic nerve at near-histological resolution.

Common diseases and treatment

Leading causes of vision loss worldwide include cataract — treated by surgical removal of the cloudy lens and implantation of an intraocular lens, one of the most common and successful operations in medicine — glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration, and diabetic retinopathy. Refractive errors such as myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism are corrected with glasses, contact lenses, or laser surgery such as LASIK. Many retinal diseases are now treated with intravitreal injections of anti-VEGF drugs.

History

Eye surgery is ancient: the Sushruta Samhita, an Indian text of the first millennium BCE, describes cataract couching. The discipline took modern shape in the 19th century with Helmholtz's invention of the ophthalmoscope in 1851, and advanced with the operating microscope, phacoemulsification, and laser technologies in the 20th century.
