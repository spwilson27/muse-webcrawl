List of Olympic Games medal counts

This article collects medal tables for each edition of the modern Olympic Games, Summer (from Athens 1896) and Winter (from Chamonix 1924).

All-time leaders

The United States leads the all-time Summer Olympics medal table with well over 2,600 medals, followed historically by the Soviet Union, Great Britain, China, Germany and France. In the Winter Games, Norway leads with the most gold and total medals, followed by the United States, Germany, Canada and Austria.
