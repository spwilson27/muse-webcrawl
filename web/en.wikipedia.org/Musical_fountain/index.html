Musical fountain

A musical fountain, also called a dancing fountain or fairy fountain, is an animated fountain created for entertainment purposes whose water jets move in choreographed patterns, typically synchronized with music and coloured lighting. Modern shows are controlled by computer, coordinating pumps, valves, lights and sometimes lasers and projection.

History

Early musical fountains date to the early 20th century. A landmark was the Magic Fountain of Montjuïc in Barcelona, built for the 1929 International Exposition, which still performs light-and-music shows. The genre expanded widely in the late 20th and 21st centuries as computer control made complex choreography practical.

Notable examples

The Dubai Fountain, set on the Burj Khalifa Lake, shoots water up to about 150 metres and performs to Arabic and world music. The Fountains of Bellagio in Las Vegas, opened in 1998, made the dancing fountain a famous resort attraction. Other notable installations include the fountain at the World of Colour show in California and the Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain in Seoul, which discharges water from the deck of a bridge.
