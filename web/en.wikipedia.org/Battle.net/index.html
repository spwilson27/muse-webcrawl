Battle.net is an Internet-based online gaming, social networking, digital distribution and digital rights management platform developed by Blizzard Entertainment. Launched on December 31, 1996, alongside the release of Diablo, it was one of the first integrated in-game online services and helped popularize online multiplayer gaming.

History

The service provided matchmaking, chat and ladder rankings for titles such as Diablo, StarCraft, Diablo II and Warcraft III. In 2009 Blizzard relaunched the platform as "Battle.net 2.0", tying it to World of Warcraft accounts and adding unified friends lists and cross-game communication. Following Activision Blizzard's corporate changes, it later hosted partner titles including Activision's Call of Duty series.

Features

The desktop application serves as a launcher and store for Blizzard and selected Activision games, managing purchases, downloads, updates, voice chat and social features. Regional servers operate in the Americas, Europe, Asia and (through partners) China.
