Piping and instrumentation diagram

A piping and instrumentation diagram (P&ID or PID) is a detailed diagram in the process industry showing the piping, process equipment, instrumentation and control devices of a plant, together with their interconnections. It is a foundational document for the design, construction and operation of chemical, petrochemical and power facilities.

Content and symbology

P&IDs depict major and auxiliary equipment, pipe lines with sizes and identification, valves, instruments and control loops using standardised symbols and letter codes (such as those of ISA standard 5.1), where, for example, "FIC" denotes a flow indicating controller. They build on the simpler process flow diagram (PFD), adding detail needed for detailed engineering.

Uses

The diagrams guide construction, safety reviews such as HAZOP studies, operator training and maintenance, and are kept updated as plants are modified.
