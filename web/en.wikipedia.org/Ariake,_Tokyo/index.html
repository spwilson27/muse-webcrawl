Ariake, Tokyo

Ariake
WardKōtō, Tokyo, Japan

Ariake (有明) is a district of Kōtō Ward, Tokyo, Japan, built largely on reclaimed land in Tokyo Bay. It forms part of the Tokyo Waterfront City (Odaiba–Ariake) development area and is known for its exhibition halls, sports venues, and convention facilities.

Landmarks

The district's best-known landmark is Tokyo Big Sight (the Tokyo International Exhibition Center), whose inverted-pyramid conference tower is a symbol of the waterfront. Ariake is also home to the Ariake Coliseum, a major tennis venue that hosted events of the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games, the Ariake Arena, the Ariake Garden complex, and the Tokyo International Cruise Terminal nearby. The area is served by the Yurikamome line's Tokyo International Cruise Terminal and Kokusai-tenjijō stations and by the Rinkai Line.

History

The land was created by landfill in the twentieth century and developed from the 1990s onward as part of Tokyo's waterfront subcenter plan, transforming former port and warehouse ground into a district of offices, hotels, and event venues.
