West Side Story is a 1961 American musical romantic drama film directed by Robert Wise and Jerome Robbins. It is an adaptation of the 1957 Broadway musical of the same name, itself inspired by William Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet. The story is set in the Upper West Side of New York City in the mid-1950s, where two rival teenage street gangs — the Jets, a white gang, and the Sharks, a Puerto Rican gang — battle for control of the neighbourhood. Tony, a former Jet, falls in love with Maria, the sister of the Sharks' leader Bernardo, with tragic consequences.

Plot

On the streets of the Upper West Side, the Jets, led by Riff, spar with the Sharks, led by Bernardo. At a dance at the local gym, Tony — once a Jet — meets Maria, Bernardo's younger sister, newly arrived from Puerto Rico, and they fall instantly in love. Their secret romance is set against a planned "rumble" between the gangs. Tony tries to stop the fight, but Riff is killed by Bernardo, and Tony in turn kills Bernardo. In the aftermath, a false report that Maria has been killed leads Tony to seek out the Sharks' Chino, who shoots him; Tony dies in Maria's arms as she pleads with both gangs to end the violence.

Music

Leonard Bernstein's score, with lyrics by Stephen Sondheim, includes "Maria", "Tonight", "America", "Somewhere", "I Feel Pretty", "One Hand, One Heart" and "Gee, Officer Krupke". The singing of Natalie Wood (Maria) was dubbed by Marni Nixon, and Richard Beymer's (Tony) by Jimmy Bryant.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major critical and commercial success, becoming the second-highest-grossing film of 1961. At the 34th Academy Awards it won ten Oscars, including Best Picture, Best Director (Wise and Robbins — the first shared directing award), Best Supporting Actor (George Chakiris) and Best Supporting Actress (Rita Moreno). It is consistently ranked among the greatest musical films ever made and was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry in 1997. A second film adaptation, directed by Steven Spielberg, was released in 2021.
