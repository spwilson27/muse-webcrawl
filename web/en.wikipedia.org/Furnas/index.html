Furnas

Furnas is a civil parish in the municipality of Povoação on São Miguel Island in the Azores, Portugal. It lies in the Furnas Valley, one of the three active central volcanoes of São Miguel, and is renowned for its geothermal activity — fumaroles, boiling springs, and mineral waters.

Geothermal features

Around the shores of Lagoa das Furnas and in the village itself, dozens of natural hot springs and geysers vent steam and mineral-rich water. Local cooks famously prepare cozido das Furnas, a stew of meats and vegetables buried in pots in the volcanic soil and slow-cooked by geothermal heat for several hours.

Terra Nostra Park

The Terra Nostra Botanical Garden, created in the 18th and 19th centuries around the estate of American merchant Thomas Hickling, contains a noted collection of camellias and other exotic plants and a large thermal swimming pool of warm, iron-rich brown water, a popular attraction for visitors.

Volcanology

The Furnas caldera was formed by major eruptions tens of thousands of years ago. The last significant eruption occurred in 1630, killing around 200 people. The volcano is continuously monitored because of its ongoing hydrothermal activity and seismicity.
