Magazine

A magazine is a periodical publication, generally published on a regular schedule (often weekly or monthly), containing a variety of content such as articles, essays, interviews, photography and advertising, aimed at a general readership or a specific interest group. The word derives from the Arabic makhāzin, meaning storehouses, via the French magasin.

History

Early magazines appeared in the 17th and 18th centuries; The Gentleman's Magazine (1731) in London was among the first to use the term in its modern publishing sense. The 19th and 20th centuries saw mass-circulation general-interest magazines, followed by an expansion of specialist consumer and trade titles, and, from the 1990s, digital and online editions.

Types

Magazines are commonly divided into consumer magazines, trade publications, and scholarly or literary journals, and are financed by subscriptions, newsstand sales, and advertising.
