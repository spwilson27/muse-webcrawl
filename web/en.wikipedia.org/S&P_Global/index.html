S&P Global

S&P Global Inc. (prior to April 2016 McGraw Hill Financial, Inc., and prior to 2013 The McGraw-Hill Companies, Inc.) is an American publicly traded corporation headquartered in Manhattan, New York City. Its primary areas of business are financial information and analytics. It is the parent company of S&P Global Ratings, S&P Global Market Intelligence, S&P Global Mobility, S&P Global Sustainable1, S&P Dow Jones Indices, and S&P Global Commodity Insights.

S&P Global headquarters at 55 Water Street, New York City.

History

The company traces its history to 1888, when James H. McGraw purchased the American Journal of Railway Appliances. Standard & Poor's, founded in 1860 as a publisher of financial information, merged with the McGraw-Hill Companies in 1966. In 2013 the company sold McGraw-Hill Education and renamed itself McGraw Hill Financial; in 2016 it adopted the S&P Global name. In 2022, S&P Global completed its merger with IHS Markit in an approximately $44 billion all-stock transaction, one of the largest deals in the financial data industry.

Divisions

  • S&P Global Ratings — one of the "Big Three" credit-rating agencies, alongside Moody's and Fitch.
  • S&P Global Market Intelligence — data, research and analytics for financial professionals, including the Capital IQ platform.
  • S&P Dow Jones Indices — joint venture with CME Group that maintains the S&P 500 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average.
  • S&P Global Commodity Insights — energy and commodities price assessments, including Platts benchmarks.
  • S&P Global Mobility — automotive data and forecasting.
