Urraca of León

Urraca of León
Reign: 1109–1126
Born: 24 June 1079, León
Died: 8 March 1126, Saldaña
Predecessor: Alfonso VI
Successor: Alfonso VII

Urraca (24 June 1079 – 8 March 1126), called the Reckless, was Queen of León, Castile and Galicia from 1109 until her death. She was the first European queen regnant to exercise full sovereign power in her own right in medieval Western Europe.

Reign

The daughter of Alfonso VI, Urraca inherited the throne after the death of her brother Sancho at the Battle of Uclés. Her politically arranged marriage to Alfonso I of Aragon, "the Battler," was turbulent and was annulled; the two kingdoms spent years in conflict. Urraca governed with the support of her lover, Count Pedro González de Lara, and fought to secure the inheritance of her son by her first husband Raymond of Burgundy, the future Alfonso VII.

Legacy

Urraca's reign was marked by civil war and Almoravid pressure on the frontier, but she maintained the integrity of her kingdoms and passed them intact to Alfonso VII.
