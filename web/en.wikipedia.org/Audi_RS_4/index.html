Audi RS 4

Manufacturer: Audi Sport GmbH
Production: 1999–2001; 2005–2008; 2012–2015; 2017–present
Layout: Front-engine, all-wheel drive

The Audi RS 4 is the high-performance variant of the Audi A4 range, produced by Audi Sport. Offered mainly as an Avant estate, it combines a powerful engine with quattro all-wheel drive.

Generations

The B5 RS 4 (1999) used a 2.7-litre twin-turbo V6 developed with Cosworth producing 381 PS. The B7 (2006) adopted a naturally aspirated 4.2-litre V8 revving to 8,250 rpm. The B8 used a related V8, while the B9 (2017) switched to a 2.9-litre twin-turbo V6 producing 450 PS.
