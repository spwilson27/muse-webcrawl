Summer of Soul (...Or, When the Revolution Could Not Be Televised) is a 2021 American documentary film directed by Ahmir "Questlove" Thompson about the Harlem Cultural Festival, a series of concerts held in Harlem, New York, in the summer of 1969.

The festival and the footage

The festival featured performances by Stevie Wonder, Nina Simone, Sly and the Family Stone, Gladys Knight & the Pips, Mahalia Jackson, Mavis Staples, B. B. King, and many others, and drew some 300,000 attendees. Producer Hal Tulchin filmed about 40 hours of the concerts, but the footage sat largely unseen in a basement for fifty years after no broadcaster would take it.

Reception

The film premiered at the 2021 Sundance Film Festival, winning the Grand Jury Prize and Audience Award for documentary, and went on to win the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature. Critics praised its restoration of a landmark of Black music and culture to public memory.
