Hac Sa Beach

Hac Sa Beach (Chinese: 黑沙海灘; Portuguese: Praia de Hác-Sá) is the largest natural beach in Macau, located on the southeastern shore of Coloane Island. Its name, literally "black sand", comes from the dark colour of its sand, caused by minerals in the seabed that are washed ashore; the sand appears in shades of dark grey and black mixed with the normal lighter sand.

Features

The beach is a popular destination for swimming, picnics, and camping, with barbecue areas, a swimming pool complex, and recreational facilities nearby. Because natural erosion gradually removes the black sand, the Macao government has at times supplemented the beach with ordinary yellow sand, leaving stretches of mixed colour. Nearby attractions include the Hac Sa Park, the Hac Sa Reservoir, and trails into the Coloane hills.
