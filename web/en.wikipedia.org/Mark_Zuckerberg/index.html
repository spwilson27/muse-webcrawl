Mark Zuckerberg

Mark Elliot Zuckerberg (born May 14, 1984) is an American businessman who co-founded the social media service Facebook and its parent company Meta Platforms, of which he is chairman, chief executive officer, and controlling shareholder.

Zuckerberg launched Facebook in 2004 with fellow Harvard students. Meta also owns Instagram, WhatsApp, and Threads, and develops virtual reality products through Reality Labs.

With his wife Priscilla Chan he founded the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative in 2015, pledging to give away 99% of their Facebook shares over their lifetimes.
