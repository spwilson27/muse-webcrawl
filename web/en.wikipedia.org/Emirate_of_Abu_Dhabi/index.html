The Emirate of Abu Dhabi (Arabic: إمارة أبوظبي) is one of seven emirates that constitute the United Arab Emirates (UAE). It is by far the largest emirate by area (67,340 km², about 84 percent of the country's total land area), accounting for approximately 87 percent of the UAE's oil reserves, and it has the second-largest population of the seven emirates. Its capital, Abu Dhabi city, is also the capital of the UAE and the seat of the federal government.

Geography

The emirate occupies the southwestern part of the UAE, bordering Saudi Arabia to the south and west, Oman to the east, and the emirates of Dubai and Sharjah to the northeast. Its territory includes a long coastline on the Persian Gulf and around 200 islands. The interior is largely desert, punctuated by the oasis city of Al Ain near the Omani border and the Liwa Oasis in the south. The emirate is divided into three municipal regions: Abu Dhabi (central), Al Ain (eastern), and Al Dhafra (western).

History

The area was historically inhabited by the Bani Yas tribal confederation, which settled Abu Dhabi island around 1761 after fresh water was discovered there. The Al Nahyan family of the Bani Yas has ruled the emirate since the eighteenth century. In the nineteenth century, Abu Dhabi became one of the Trucial States under British protection. Oil was discovered in commercial quantities in 1958, transforming the economy. Under Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, who became ruler in 1966, Abu Dhabi led the formation of the United Arab Emirates on 2 December 1971.

Economy

Hydrocarbons dominate the emirate's economy; Abu Dhabi holds roughly 94 billion barrels of proven oil reserves and substantial natural gas reserves. Through sovereign wealth funds such as the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA) and Mubadala, the emirate has diversified into finance, renewable energy (Masdar), aerospace, tourism, and culture, including the Louvre Abu Dhabi, opened in 2017 on Saadiyat Island. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, completed in 2007, is one of the largest mosques in the world.
