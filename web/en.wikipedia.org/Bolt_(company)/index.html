Bolt Technology OÜ, commonly known as Bolt and formerly Taxify, is an Estonian multinational mobility company headquartered in Tallinn. It offers ride-hailing, food and grocery delivery (Bolt Food), e-bike and e-scooter rental, and car-sharing services through its mobile applications, operating in over 45 countries and 500 cities, particularly across Europe and Africa.

History

Bolt was founded in 2013 by Markus Villig, then 19 years old, to aggregate Estonian taxi services into a single platform. The service expanded rapidly across Central and Eastern Europe and Africa, competing with Uber by charging lower commissions. It rebranded from Taxify to Bolt in 2019 to reflect its expansion beyond taxis into scooters and delivery.

Services

Through its "super-app" model, Bolt combines ride-hailing with micromobility rentals and food delivery. Bolt Drive offers car-sharing in selected cities, and Bolt Market operates dark stores for grocery delivery.

Funding and valuation

Bolt has raised funding from investors including Daimler/Mercedes-Benz, Didi Chuxing, Sequoia Capital, and the World Bank's IFC, reaching a valuation in the billions of euros and becoming one of Estonia's most prominent technology unicorns.
