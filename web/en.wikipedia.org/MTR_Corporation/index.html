MTR Corporation

MTR Corporation Limited
Founded: 1975 (as Mass Transit Railway Corporation)
Headquarters: Hong Kong
Industry: Public transport, property

The MTR Corporation Limited is a majority government-owned public transport operator and property developer in Hong Kong which operates the Mass Transit Railway, the territory's rapid transit system. Founded in 1975, it was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2000.

Operations

Beyond Hong Kong, MTR operates rail services in mainland China (Beijing, Shenzhen, Hangzhou), the United Kingdom (Elizabeth line concessions), Sweden, and Australia (Melbourne and Sydney metros).

Rail plus property model

The corporation's "Rail + Property" model, in which it develops property above and around stations, has been studied worldwide as a model of transit-oriented development and value capture.
