Centimetre

Unit system: SI
Unit of: Length
Symbol: cm
Conversions: 1 cm = 10 mm = 0.01 m ≈ 0.3937 in

A centimetre (international spelling) or centimeter (American spelling) (SI symbol: cm) is a unit of length in the metric system, equal to one hundredth of a metre. The prefix centi- indicates a factor of one hundredth. There are 2.54 centimetres in an inch.

Usage

The centimetre is a convenient unit for many everyday measurements, including human height in most countries, rainfall amounts, and the dimensions of common objects. It was the base unit of length in the now-deprecated centimetre–gram–second (CGS) system of units, which underlies units such as the dyne and the erg.

Equivalences

One centimetre equals 10 millimetres, 0.01 metres, or 10,000 micrometres. A cubic centimetre (cm³) is equal to one millilitre and is widely used for engine displacement and medical dosages.
