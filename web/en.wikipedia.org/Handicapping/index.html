Handicapping

Handicapping, in sport and games, is the practice of assigning a disadvantage or advantage to a competitor or team through scoring, weight, start position or conditions, in order to equalize the chances of winning.

Horse racing

In handicap horse races, horses carry different weights assigned by an official handicapper on the basis of past performance, so that in theory all runners have an equal chance. "Handicapping" also describes the punter's art of analysing form to predict winners.

Other sports

Golf handicaps allow players of different ability to compete by adjusting scores; Go uses handicap stones; sailing and motorsport use rating and ballast systems. In betting, a point spread is a form of handicap applied to a team.
