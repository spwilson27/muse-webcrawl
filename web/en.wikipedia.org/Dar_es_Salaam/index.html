Dar es Salaam (from Arabic: دار السلام, "house of peace") is the largest city and financial hub of Tanzania, located on the Swahili coast of the Indian Ocean. It was the capital of Tanzania until 1974, when the capital was officially moved to Dodoma, though many government offices and foreign embassies remain in Dar es Salaam.

History

The city was founded in the 1860s by Sultan Majid bin Said of Zanzibar near the fishing village of Mzizima. It became the capital of German East Africa in 1891 and later of British Tanganyika. After independence in 1961 it remained the seat of government of Tanzania and grew rapidly into one of Africa's fastest-growing cities.

Economy and transport

The Port of Dar es Salaam is Tanzania's principal port and a major gateway for landlocked neighbours including Zambia, Malawi, Burundi, Rwanda and the eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo. The city is served by Julius Nyerere International Airport and is the terminus of the TAZARA and Central Line railways.
