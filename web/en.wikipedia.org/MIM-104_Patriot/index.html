The MIM-104 Patriot is an American all-weather, all-altitude surface-to-air missile (SAM) system used by the United States Army and several allied states. Its name is a backronym for "Phased Array Tracking Radar to Intercept On Target".

Design

A Patriot battery consists of a phased-array radar (AN/MPQ-53/65), an engagement control station, power generation equipment, and up to eight launchers, each carrying four PAC-2 missiles or up to sixteen PAC-3 interceptors. The system can track up to 100 targets and engage several simultaneously against aircraft, cruise missiles, and tactical ballistic missiles.

Operational history

Patriot became famous during the 1991 Gulf War for intercepting Iraqi Scud missiles over Israel and Saudi Arabia, though the extent of its success was later disputed. Upgraded PAC-3 systems have since been used in Iraq, Yemen, and — from 2023 — by Ukraine against Russian ballistic and hypersonic missiles.
