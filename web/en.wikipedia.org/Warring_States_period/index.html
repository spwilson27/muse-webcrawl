Warring States period

The major states of China during the Warring States period

The Warring States period (traditional Chinese: 戰國時代; simplified Chinese: 战国时代; pinyin: Zhànguó Shídài) was an era in ancient Chinese history characterised by warfare, as well as bureaucratic and military reforms and consolidation. It followed the Spring and Autumn period and concluded with the Qin wars of conquest that saw the annexation of all other contender states, which ultimately led to the Qin state's victory in 221 BC as the first unified Chinese empire, under Qin Shi Huang.

The name for the period comes from the Record of the Warring States (Zhanguoce), compiled in the early Han dynasty.

The Seven Warring States

By the 5th century BC, the Zhou dynasty's authority had collapsed, and seven major states dominated China: Qin in the west, Chu in the south, Qi in the east, and Yan, Han, Zhao, and Wei in the north and centre. These states gradually annexed their smaller neighbours, so that by the late 3rd century BC only Qin and Chu remained as serious contenders, and Qin ultimately conquered all of them between 230 and 221 BC.

Warfare and military reform

Warfare in this period shifted from the chariot-based aristocratic combat of earlier eras to massed infantry armies equipped with iron weapons and crossbows. Cavalry became increasingly important, especially in the northern states facing steppe nomads. States adopted conscription, standardised taxation, and codified laws — reforms associated with Legalist thinkers such as Shang Yang in Qin.

Philosophy

Despite the political chaos, the period was intellectually fertile. The "Hundred Schools of Thought" flourished, including Confucianism, Taoism, Mohism, and Legalism. Thinkers such as Mencius, Zhuangzi, Han Fei, and Xunzi shaped Chinese philosophy for millennia.

Unification

Under King Zheng of Qin, a series of campaigns conquered Han (230 BC), Zhao (228 BC), Wei (225 BC), Chu (223 BC), Yan (222 BC), and Qi (221 BC). Zheng declared himself Qin Shi Huang, the First Emperor, founding the Qin dynasty and ending the Warring States period.
