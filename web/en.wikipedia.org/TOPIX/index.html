TOPIX

ExchangeTokyo Stock Exchange
Foundation1 July 1969
ConstituentsPrime Market listed companies

The Tokyo Stock Price Index, commonly known as TOPIX, is a capitalisation-weighted stock market index covering companies listed on the Prime Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

Overview

TOPIX is calculated from the free-float-adjusted market capitalisation of its constituents and is often regarded as a broader gauge of the Japanese market than the price-weighted Nikkei 225.

History

The index began in 1969 with a base value of 100. It peaked in December 1989 at the height of the Japanese asset price bubble and did not surpass that level for more than three decades.

Sub-indices

TOPIX is divided by company size (Large, Mid, and Small) and into 33 industry sector indices.
