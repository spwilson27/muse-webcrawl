Information technology

Information technology (IT) is a set of related fields within information and communications technology that encompass computer systems, software, programming languages, and data and information processing, and storage. Information technology is an application of computer science and computer engineering.

History

The term "information technology" in its modern sense first appeared in a 1958 article published in the Harvard Business Review. Humans have stored, retrieved, and communicated information since the earliest writing systems, but electronic IT developed with punched-card tabulators, early computers in the mid-20th century, the transistor, the microprocessor, and the growth of telecommunications networks culminating in the Internet.

Fields and applications

IT covers data processing and databases, computer networking, software development, systems administration, technical support, cybersecurity, and cloud computing. Businesses rely on IT departments to manage infrastructure and data; other major application areas include healthcare, education, finance, government, and entertainment. The discipline overlaps with, but is distinct from, computer science, which focuses on the theoretical foundations of computation.

Industry and employment

IT is one of the largest sectors of the world economy, employing millions in roles from help-desk technician to chief information officer. Industry reports track rapid growth in spending on cloud services, security, and artificial intelligence infrastructure.
