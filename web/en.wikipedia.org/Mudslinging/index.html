Mudslinging

Mudslinging is a metaphorical term for the practice of making malicious or scandalous allegations about an opponent, especially in politics, with the aim of damaging their reputation rather than engaging with their arguments. The phrase rests on the proverb that "if you throw enough mud, some of it will stick".

Mudslinging in a political campaign

Meaning and use

Mudslinging is closely associated with negative campaigning and smear tactics. Because allegations may be remembered even after they are disproved, the tactic can be effective regardless of their truth. Accusations of mudslinging are themselves often used as a counter-charge in political debate. The concept overlaps with defamation and the ad hominem fallacy, but usually refers to public reputational attacks through media rather than direct legal claims.
