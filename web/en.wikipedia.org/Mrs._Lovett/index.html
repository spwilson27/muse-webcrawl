Mrs. Lovett is a fictional character appearing in many adaptations of the Sweeney Todd story. She is the accomplice and landlady of the murderous barber Sweeney Todd, whose victims she bakes into meat pies sold at her pie shop in Fleet Street, London.

Character

The character first appeared in the 1846–47 penny dreadful The String of Pearls. In Stephen Sondheim and Hugh Wheeler's 1979 musical Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street, Mrs. Lovett — given the first name Nellie — is a cheerful, amoral widow in love with Todd, who proposes turning his victims into pies after tasting the "worst pies in London". The role was originated on Broadway by Angela Lansbury, who won a Tony Award for it.

Portrayals

Notable portrayals include Angela Lansbury (stage, 1979), Patti LuPone, Imelda Staunton, and Helena Bonham Carter in Tim Burton's 2007 film Sweeney Todd. Her songs in the musical include "The Worst Pies in London", "A Little Priest" and "By the Sea".
