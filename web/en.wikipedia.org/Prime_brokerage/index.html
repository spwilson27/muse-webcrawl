Prime brokerage

Prime brokerage is the generic name for a bundled package of services offered by investment banks and securities firms to hedge funds and other professional investors. A prime broker acts as the client's central intermediary for trading, financing, and custody.

Services

Core services include securities lending for short selling, leveraged trade execution and margin financing, clearing and settlement, custody of assets, and consolidated reporting. Additional services may include capital introduction (connecting funds with investors), risk management tools, office space, and consulting on fund formation.

History and significance

Prime brokerage became a major business line for firms such as Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as the hedge fund industry grew. The 2008 financial crisis highlighted counterparty risks when funds held assets at failing brokers, prompting many large funds to use multiple prime brokers ("multi-prime").
