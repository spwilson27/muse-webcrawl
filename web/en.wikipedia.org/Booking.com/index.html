Booking.com

Type: Online travel agency
Founded: 1996
Headquarters: Amsterdam, Netherlands
Parent: Booking Holdings
Website: booking.com

Booking.com is a Dutch online travel agency for lodging reservations and other travel products, and a subsidiary of Booking Holdings. Headquartered in Amsterdam, it is one of the world's largest travel marketplaces for both consumers and travel partners.

History

Booking.com was formed in 1996 by Geert-Jan Bruinsma in Enschede, Netherlands, as Bookings.nl. The company merged with Booking Online in 2000 to form Booking.com. In 2005 it was acquired by the Priceline Group (now Booking Holdings) for $133 million, a purchase later widely cited as one of the best acquisitions in internet history.

Services

The site offers reservations for hotels, apartments, holiday homes and other properties, in dozens of languages and currencies. It later expanded into flights, car rentals, airport taxis and attractions. Properties listed on the platform historically paid a commission on each booking rather than customers paying a booking fee.

Operations

Booking.com operates globally with offices in many countries and lists millions of properties. It has been subject to regulatory scrutiny in various markets over pricing practices, tax matters and its treatment of accommodation partners.
