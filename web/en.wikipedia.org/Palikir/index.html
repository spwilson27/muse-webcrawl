Palikir

Palikir is the capital city of the Federated States of Micronesia, located on the island of Pohnpei. It is a small town with a population of about 4,600 people, situated within the municipality of Sokehs on the northwestern coast of the island. Since 1989 it has served as the seat of the national government, which is housed in a purpose-built complex of buildings.

History

Pohnpei was ruled successively by Spain, Germany, and Japan before coming under United States administration as part of the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands after the Second World War. When the Federated States of Micronesia moved toward independence under a Compact of Free Association with the United States in the 1980s, Palikir was chosen as the site of the new capital, replacing the island's main town, Kolonia, which had been the administrative centre under earlier regimes.

Geography and government

Palikir lies in lush, mountainous terrain on one of the wettest islands in the world, with annual rainfall among the highest recorded anywhere. The capital complex includes the president's office, the Congress of the Federated States of Micronesia, and government departments, arranged in buildings whose design draws on traditional Micronesian meeting-house forms. The College of Micronesia's national campus is also located nearby.
