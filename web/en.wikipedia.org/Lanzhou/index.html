Lanzhou is the capital and largest city of Gansu Province in northwestern China. Situated on the upper reaches of the Yellow River, which passes through the city, it has long been a key link on the Northern Silk Road and is now a major transport hub and industrial centre.

Geography

Lanzhou lies in a narrow river valley between mountains, giving it an elongated urban form. The Zhongshan Bridge, built in 1909, was the first permanent bridge over the Yellow River.

History and culture

The city has been a strategic crossing since Han times. It is famous for Lanzhou beef noodles (lamian), a halal hand-pulled noodle soup originating with the Hui people.
