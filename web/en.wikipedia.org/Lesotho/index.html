Lesotho

Lesotho, officially the Kingdom of Lesotho, is a landlocked country entirely surrounded by South Africa. It is the only independent state in the world lying entirely above 1,000 metres in elevation, and its lowest point, at 1,400 metres, is the highest "low point" of any country.

History

The kingdom was founded in the 1820s by King Moshoeshoe I, who united Sotho peoples at the mountain stronghold of Thaba Bosiu during the upheavals of the Lifaqane. It became the British protectorate of Basutoland in 1868 and gained independence in 1966.

Geography and economy

Lesotho's rugged Maloti and Drakensberg mountains support livestock farming, diamond mining and the Lesotho Highlands Water Project, which supplies water and hydroelectric power to South Africa. Many Basotho work in South Africa, and remittances are a major source of income.
