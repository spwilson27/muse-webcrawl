Kitab-ı Bahriye

Author: Piri Reis
Completed: 1521; revised 1525–26
Language: Ottoman Turkish
Subject: Nautical atlas of the Mediterranean

The Kitab-ı Bahriye (Book on Navigation / Book of Seafaring) is a nautical atlas and portolan of the Mediterranean and Black Seas created by the Ottoman admiral and geographer Piri Reis. First completed in 1521 and revised in an expanded, illustrated version in 1525–1526, it is one of the most famous works of Ottoman cartography.

Contents

The work combines prose sailing directions with detailed charts of coasts, ports, anchorages, hazards, and fresh-water sources around the Mediterranean. A verse preface discusses winds, compass use, and mapmaking. Some 240 charts appear in the finest copies.

Author

Piri Reis, nephew of the corsair Kemal Reis, also produced the famous Piri Reis map of 1513 showing the Atlantic coasts. He presented the revised Kitab-ı Bahriye to Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent. Numerous illustrated manuscript copies survive in libraries in Istanbul and elsewhere.
