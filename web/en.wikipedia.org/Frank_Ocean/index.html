Frank Ocean

Frank Ocean (born 1987), acclaimed for the albums Channel Orange and Blonde.

Frank Ocean (born Christopher Edwin Breaux, 28 October 1987) is an American singer, songwriter and rapper. He is widely credited with pioneering alternative R&B, and his albums are frequently ranked among the greatest of the 21st century.

Career

Born in Long Beach, California, and raised in New Orleans, Ocean began his career as a ghostwriter for artists including Justin Bieber and Beyoncé, and joined the Los Angeles hip-hop collective Odd Future in 2009. His self-released mixtape Nostalgia, Ultra (2011), featuring the single "Novacane", brought him critical acclaim and a major-label recording career.

His debut studio album, Channel Orange (2012), won the Grammy Award for Best Urban Contemporary Album. In July 2012 Ocean published an open letter describing his first love, with a man — a landmark moment widely noted for its candour in hip-hop and R&B. After a long, much-anticipated gap, he released the visual album Endless and then his second studio album, Blonde (2016), independently; both topped charts, and Blonde, with songs such as "Nikes", "Ivy" and "Nights", is frequently cited among the best albums of all time. Ocean has since released singles sporadically, including "Chanel" (2017), and has largely avoided the public eye.
