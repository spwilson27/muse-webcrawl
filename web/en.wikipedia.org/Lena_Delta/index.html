Lena Delta

The Lena Delta is the vast delta of the Lena River in the Sakha Republic of northeastern Siberia, Russia, projecting into the Laptev Sea of the Arctic Ocean. Covering roughly 30,000 square kilometres, it is one of the largest deltas in the world.

Geography and wildlife

The delta is a labyrinth of channels, islands and tundra wetlands, much of it protected as the Lena Delta Wildlife Reserve and a Ramsar site. It provides crucial breeding habitat for migratory birds and supports fish, reindeer and other Arctic wildlife, while permafrost and seasonal ice shape its ever-changing channels.
