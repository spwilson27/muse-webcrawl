Alexandra Anna Daddario (born March 16, 1986) is an American actress. She had her breakthrough portraying Annabeth Chase in the Percy Jackson film series (2010–2013). She has since starred as Paige in Hall Pass (2011), Heather Miller in Texas Chainsaw 3D (2013), Blake Gaines in San Andreas (2015), Summer Quinn in Baywatch (2017), and Alexis Butler in We Summon the Darkness (2019).

She has also guest-starred in television series including White Collar, It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia, True Detective, New Girl, and American Horror Story: Hotel. In 2021, she starred in the first season of the HBO series The White Lotus, for which she received critical acclaim and an Emmy nomination for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie. In 2023 she began playing the lead role of Dr. Rowan Fielding in the AMC show Mayfair Witches, based on a series of novels written by author Anne Rice.

Early life

Daddario was born in New York City on March 16, 1986, the eldest child of Christina, a lawyer, and Richard Daddario, a prosecutor and former head of the New York City Police Department counterterrorism unit under Mayor Michael Bloomberg. She is of Italian, Irish, English, and Slovak descent. Her brother, Matthew Daddario, is also an actor, and her sister, Catharine Daddario, is also an actress. Her paternal grandfather was Emilio Q. Daddario, a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives for Connecticut from 1959 to 1971.

Daddario was raised on Manhattan's Upper East Side. She attended the Brearley School and the Professional Children's School. Daddario said she decided to be an actress at the age of 11. She attended Marymount Manhattan College before dropping out to pursue acting full-time.

Career

At age 16, Daddario transferred from the Brearley School to the Professional Children's School to focus on acting. She made her television debut at age 15, when she played victimized teen Laurie Lewis in the ABC daytime soap opera All My Children. Her first major role was as Annabeth Chase in the fantasy adventure film Percy Jackson & the Olympians: The Lightning Thief (2010).

In 2013, she starred as Heather Miller in the slasher film Texas Chainsaw 3D, and reprised her role as Annabeth Chase in Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters. In 2014, Daddario gained attention for her role as Lisa Tragnetti in the first season of the HBO series True Detective. The following year, she had a leading role as Blake Gaines in the disaster film San Andreas. In 2017, she starred as Summer Quinn in the action comedy film Baywatch.

In 2021, Daddario starred as newlywed Rachel Patton in the first season of the HBO satire series The White Lotus. The role earned her widespread critical acclaim and a nomination for the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie.

Personal life

Daddario began a relationship with producer Andrew Form in 2020. They announced their engagement in December 2021 and were married in June 2022 in New Orleans. In July 2024, Daddario announced she was pregnant with their first child.
