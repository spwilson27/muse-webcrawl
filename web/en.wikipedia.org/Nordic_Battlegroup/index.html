Nordic Battlegroup

The Nordic Battlegroup (NBG) is one of eighteen European Union battlegroups, a multinational rapid-reaction force led by Sweden as framework nation, with contributions from Finland, Norway, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, and Ireland.

Soldiers of the Nordic Battlegroup on exercise

History

The battlegroup first stood on standby in the first half of 2008 (NBG08), and again in 2011 and 2015. Sweden provides the core of the force, including the operational headquarters at Enköping, with a total strength of roughly 2,200 to 2,500 troops able to deploy within 10 days of a Council decision, for initial-entry operations lasting up to 120 days.

Composition

The force is built around a mechanized infantry battalion, supported by combat support and combat service support elements, strategic lift arrangements, and a deployable headquarters. Like all EU battlegroups, it has never been deployed on an operation, though its units have participated in numerous multinational exercises.
