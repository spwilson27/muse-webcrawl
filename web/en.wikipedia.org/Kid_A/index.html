Kid A is the fourth studio album by the English rock band Radiohead, released on 2 October 2000 by Parlophone. After the critical and commercial success of OK Computer (1997), the band deliberately moved away from guitar-driven rock toward electronic music, jazz, krautrock, and modern classical influences.

Recording and music

Recorded with producer Nigel Godrich in Paris, Copenhagen, Gloucestershire, and Oxford, the album features the ondes Martenot, programmed beats, sampling, and string and brass arrangements by Jonny Greenwood. Tracks include "Everything in Its Right Place", "The National Anthem", "How to Disappear Completely", and "Idioteque". Radiohead released no singles from the album and promoted it with short animated "blips" rather than conventional music videos.

Reception and legacy

Kid A debuted at number one in the United Kingdom and became Radiohead's first number-one album in the United States. Initially divisive, it is now widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time; it won the Grammy Award for Best Alternative Album and was preserved in the US National Recording Registry in 2024. A companion album, Amnesiac, followed in 2001, and the two were reissued together as Kid A Mnesia in 2021.
