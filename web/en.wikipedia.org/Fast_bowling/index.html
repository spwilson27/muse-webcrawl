Fast bowling


Illustration: Fast bowling

Fast bowling (also called pace bowling) is one of two main approaches to bowling in the sport of cricket. Practitioners are usually known as fast bowlers, quicks or pacemen. The chief aim is to bowl the ball at high speed so as to make it difficult for the batsman to react in time, with deliveries typically exceeding 130 km/h (81 mph) and the fastest recorded above 160 km/h.

Technique

Fast bowlers use a long run-up to build momentum, a bound and delivery stride with a straight arm, and wrist and seam positioning to generate swing, seam movement, bounce and pace variations such as the yorker, bouncer and slower ball.

Notable bowlers

Pakistan's Shoaib Akhtar recorded the fastest delivery in international cricket at 161.3 km/h. Other celebrated fast bowlers include Dennis Lillee, Malcolm Marshall, Wasim Akram, Curtly Ambrose and Dale Steyn.
