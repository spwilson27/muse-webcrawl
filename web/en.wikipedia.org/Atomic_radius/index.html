Atomic radius

The atomic radius of a chemical element is a measure of the size of its atoms, usually the mean distance from the nucleus to the outermost electron shell. Because atoms have no sharp boundary, several definitions exist: empirical, covalent, ionic, metallic and van der Waals radii.

Atomic radius

Typical range: ~30–300 pm

Periodic trends

Across a period of the periodic table, radius generally decreases as increasing nuclear charge pulls electrons inward; down a group it increases as new shells are added. Radii are measured by X-ray crystallography from interatomic distances in crystals.
