Distribution (marketing)

Distribution is the process of making a product or service available for the consumer or business user who needs it. It is one of the four elements of the marketing mix — product, price, place (distribution) and promotion. Distribution may be done directly by the producer or using indirect channels with one or more intermediaries.

Distribution channels

A distribution channel is the chain of businesses or intermediaries through which a good or service passes until it reaches the final buyer. Channels may include wholesalers, distributors, retailers and the internet. A direct channel, such as a manufacturer's own stores or website, has no intermediaries; an indirect channel may have one level (a retailer) or several (wholesaler, then retailer, then consumer).

Distribution strategies

  • Intensive distribution — placing the product in as many outlets as possible; typical of low-priced convenience goods such as soft drinks.
  • Selective distribution — using a limited number of outlets in a geographic area, common for shopping goods such as appliances and clothing brands.
  • Exclusive distribution — granting one intermediary sole rights in a territory, used for prestige and speciality products.

Functions of intermediaries

Intermediaries break bulk, hold inventory, provide local market knowledge and credit, and bring together assortments of products from many producers. Physical distribution (logistics) covers warehousing, transportation, materials handling and order processing. Modern supply-chain management integrates these functions, and e-commerce has added new models such as drop shipping, in which the retailer never handles the product.

Channel conflict

Firms must manage conflict within channels: vertical conflict between levels (for example, a manufacturer selling directly online against its own retailers), and horizontal conflict between intermediaries at the same level. Franchise systems, dual distribution and omnichannel retailing are common responses to changes in consumer buying behaviour.
