The 1974 FIFA World Cup was the tenth FIFA World Cup, held in West Germany from 13 June to 7 July 1974. A new format replaced the quarter-finals and semi-finals with a second group stage, and a new trophy, the FIFA World Cup Trophy, was introduced after Brazil permanently kept the Jules Rimet Trophy.

Final

Hosts West Germany, led by Franz Beckenbauer, defeated Johan Cruyff's Netherlands 2–1 in the final at Munich's Olympiastadion. The Dutch scored from a penalty in the second minute before the Germans had touched the ball, but goals from Paul Breitner (also a penalty) and Gerd Müller turned the match around.

Notable matches

East Germany beat West Germany 1–0 in the only meeting of the two German states at a World Cup. Poland finished third, with Grzegorz Lato as the tournament's top scorer.
