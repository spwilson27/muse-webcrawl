Transformers

Transformers is a media franchise produced by American toy company Hasbro and Japanese toy company Takara Tomy. It primarily follows the heroic Autobots and the villainous Decepticons, two alien robot factions at war that can transform into other forms, such as vehicles and animals. The franchise began in 1984 with a toy line and the animated series The Transformers, featuring characters such as Optimus Prime and Megatron.

Media

The franchise spans toys, animated series, comic books, video games, and a long-running live-action film series launched by Michael Bay's Transformers (2007), along with later films including Bumblebee (2018) and Transformers: Rise of the Beasts (2023), and the animated Transformers One (2024).

Impact

Transformers became one of the highest-grossing media franchises of all time, with the transforming-robot concept a lasting influence on toy design and popular culture. The concept drew on Takara's Diaclone and Microman toy lines, rebranded by Hasbro with a unified storyline created with Marvel Comics.
