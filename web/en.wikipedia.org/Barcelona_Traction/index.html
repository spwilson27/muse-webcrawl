Barcelona Traction

The Barcelona Traction, Light and Power Company was a Canadian utility company that operated electrical power and tramway services in Catalonia, Spain. Founded in Toronto in 1911 by the engineer Fred Stark Pearson, it became the subject of one of the most famous cases in international law.

The ICJ case

After the Spanish Civil War, Spanish courts declared the company's Spanish subsidiary bankrupt in 1948. Belgium brought a claim before the International Court of Justice on behalf of Belgian shareholders. In its 1970 judgment in Barcelona Traction, Light and Power Company, Limited (Belgium v. Spain), the Court held that Belgium lacked standing, since the company was incorporated in Canada. The judgment is notable for its influential discussion of obligations erga omnes.
