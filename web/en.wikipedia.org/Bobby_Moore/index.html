Bobby Moore

Born12 April 1941, Barking, England
Died24 February 1993 (aged 51)
PositionDefender
ClubsWest Ham United, Fulham

Robert Frederick Chelsea Moore (1941–1993) was an English professional footballer who captained West Ham United and the England national team. He led England to victory in the 1966 FIFA World Cup, lifting the trophy at Wembley.

Career

Moore played more than 500 league games for West Ham, captaining the club to FA Cup (1964) and European Cup Winners' Cup (1965) triumphs. He won 108 England caps, a national record for an outfield player at the time, and captained his country 90 times.

Legacy

Regarded as one of the greatest defenders of all time, Moore was famed for his reading of the game and composed tackling. A statue of him stands outside Wembley Stadium.
