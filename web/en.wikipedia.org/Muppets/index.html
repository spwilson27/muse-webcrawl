The Muppets are an ensemble cast of puppet characters created by Jim Henson, combining elements of marionettes and puppets — the origin of the name. Known for their absurdist, burlesque and self-referential humour, they became famous through Sesame Street (1969– ) and especially The Muppet Show (1976–1981).

History

Henson introduced early Muppets on the local television show Sam and Friends in 1955, where Kermit first appeared. After years of TV specials, commercials and appearances, The Muppet Show, produced in England, became an international hit, followed by films beginning with The Muppet Movie (1979). Disney acquired the Muppets in 2004, and the franchise has since continued with films, television series and streaming productions.

Technique

Muppets are typically built of soft materials such as foam and fleece and performed with the puppeteer's hand in the head, with rod-operated arms; performers work below frame, often watching monitors, with characters later voiced if the performer is unavailable.
