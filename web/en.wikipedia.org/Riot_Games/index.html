Riot Games, Inc. is an American video game developer, publisher and esports tournament organiser based in Los Angeles, California. It was founded in September 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill. Riot's first and best-known title, League of Legends, was released in October 2009 and became one of the most-played PC games in the world.

History

Chinese technology company Tencent acquired a majority stake in Riot in 2011 and full ownership in 2015. After a decade focused almost entirely on League of Legends, Riot released several new games around its tenth anniversary in 2019–2020, including the tactical shooter Valorant, Teamfight Tactics and Legends of Runeterra, and published third-party titles under the Riot Forge label.

Esports and media

Riot operates professional League of Legends and Valorant leagues worldwide, including the annual World Championship. In 2021 it released the animated series Arcane on Netflix, which won wide critical acclaim. The company has also faced scrutiny over workplace culture, settling a gender discrimination class-action lawsuit in 2021.
