The Bodyguard is a 1992 American romantic thriller drama film directed by Mick Jackson, written by Lawrence Kasdan, and starring Kevin Costner, Whitney Houston (in her feature film acting debut), Gary Kemp, Bill Cobbs, and Ralph Waite. The film follows a former United States Secret Service agent turned bodyguard (Costner) who is hired to protect a famous actress and singer (Houston) from an unknown stalker.

Plot

Former Secret Service agent Frank Farmer is hired to protect pop superstar Rachel Marron, who has been receiving death threats from an obsessed stalker. Initially dismissive of Farmer's rigid security measures, Rachel grows close to him as the danger escalates; after an attempt on her life at a nightclub and a tragedy at her family's lake house, Farmer discovers the would-be assassin is a professional hitman engaged by someone inside Rachel's circle. At the Academy Awards ceremony, Farmer intercepts the assassin's shot, saving Rachel, before the two part with mutual regret.

Reception and soundtrack

The film received mixed reviews from critics but was a major commercial success, grossing $411 million worldwide and becoming the second-highest-grossing film of 1992. Its soundtrack — featuring Houston's recording of Dolly Parton's "I Will Always Love You", which spent 14 weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 — became the best-selling soundtrack album of all time and won the Grammy Award for Album of the Year. The film was nominated for two Academy Awards for Best Original Song.
