IMSA GTP

GTP (Grand Touring Prototype) is the top prototype class of the IMSA SportsCar Championship in North America. The name was used for IMSA's original prototype category of the 1980s and early 1990s and was revived in 2023 for hybrid-powered cars built to LMDh and LMH regulations.

Modern era

From 2023, manufacturers including Acura, BMW, Cadillac, Lamborghini, and Porsche have fielded GTP cars based on the LMDh formula — a spec hybrid system and chassis from approved constructors — converged with the FIA World Endurance Championship's Hypercar class so the same cars can race at Le Mans and Daytona.

Original era

The first GTP era (1981–1993) featured iconic machinery such as the Porsche 962, Nissan GTP ZX-Turbo, and the Toyota Eagle MkIII, and is remembered as a golden age of American sports car racing.
