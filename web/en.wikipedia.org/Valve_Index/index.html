The Valve Index is a virtual reality headset developed and manufactured by Valve. Released in June 2019, it was Valve's first VR headset built entirely in-house, following the HTC Vive, which Valve had co-developed.

Valve Index headset and controllers.

Features

  • Displays: dual 1440×1600 LCDs with low persistence, capable of 120 Hz and experimental 144 Hz refresh rates for reduced motion blur.
  • Controllers: the Index Controllers ("knuckles") strap to the hands, track all five fingers individually, and include thumbsticks, trackpads, and force sensors.
  • Audio: near-field off-ear speakers (BME) positioned slightly away from the ears for natural spatial sound.
  • Tracking: external SteamVR 2.0 base stations using infrared laser sweeps.

Software

The Index runs through SteamVR on Windows and Linux. Its flagship title is Half-Life: Alyx (2020), designed around the Index's finger-tracking controllers.

Reception

Reviewers praised the Index's display clarity, audio, and controllers as the best in consumer VR at launch, while criticizing its price and the inconvenience of external base stations.
