List of castles in Wales

Wales has more castles per square mile than any other country in Europe, with over 600 castle sites. Many were built during the Norman invasion and the Welsh wars of Edward I.

Major castles

  • Caernarfon Castle — Edward I's great fortress, UNESCO World Heritage Site, seat of investitures of the Prince of Wales
  • Conwy Castle — UNESCO-listed Edwardian castle with intact town walls
  • Harlech Castle — UNESCO-listed, famous for the Wars of the Roses siege
  • Beaumaris Castle — last and most technically perfect of Edward's Welsh castles
  • Cardiff Castle — Norman keep with Victorian Gothic revival interiors
  • Pembroke Castle — birthplace of Henry VII
  • Caerphilly Castle — largest castle in Wales, with leaning tower
  • Raglan Castle, Chepstow Castle, Kidwelly Castle, Laugharne, Manorbier, Rhuddlan, Flint, Denbigh, Montgomery, Powis Castle, Chirk Castle, Penrhyn Castle, Castell Coch, Criccieth, Dolwyddelan, Dolbadarn, Carreg Cennen, Oxwich, Weobley

The Castles and Town Walls of King Edward in Gwynedd were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1986. Many castles are in the care of Cadw, the Welsh historic environment service.
