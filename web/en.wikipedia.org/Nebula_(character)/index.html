Nebula (character)

Nebula is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Roger Stern and artist John Buscema, the character first appeared in The Avengers #257 (July 1985). Originally introduced as a ruthless space pirate and mercenary claiming to be the granddaughter of the Titan Thanos, Nebula became a recurring adversary of the Avengers and the Silver Surfer, and later an uneasy ally of the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Fictional character biography

Nebula commanded a fleet of mercenaries and once seized control of Sanctuary II, Thanos's former flagship, during a period when the Titan was believed dead. When Thanos returned he repudiated her claim of kinship and punished her cruelly, leaving her a burned, near-mindless husk; she was eventually restored by the Infinity Gauntlet crisis, briefly wielding the Gauntlet herself before being defeated. In later storylines she joined iterations of the Guardians of the Galaxy and confronted her own history of violence.

Other media

Nebula reached her widest audience through the Marvel Cinematic Universe, where she is portrayed by Karen Gillan. Introduced as a villain in Guardians of the Galaxy (2014), the film version is the adopted daughter of Thanos, rebuilt with cybernetic implants after losing contests to her sister Gamora. Across Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2, Avengers: Infinity War, Avengers: Endgame and Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3, the character develops from antagonist into a full member of the team.

Powers and abilities

Nebula is a skilled hand-to-hand combatant and strategist. In the films, her cybernetic enhancements grant her superhuman strength, durability and a self-repairing mechanical body; in the comics she has wielded advanced weaponry and, briefly, cosmic power.
