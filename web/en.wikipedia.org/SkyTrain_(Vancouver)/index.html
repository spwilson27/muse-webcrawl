SkyTrain (Vancouver)

SkyTrain is the medium-capacity rapid transit system serving the Metro Vancouver region of British Columbia, Canada. Operated by TransLink, it is the oldest and one of the longest fully automated, driverless rapid transit systems in the world, using linear induction motor technology developed from the Toronto Scarborough RT.

Lines

The system comprises three lines. The Expo Line, opened in 1985 ahead of Expo 86, runs from downtown Vancouver to Surrey via Burnaby and New Westminster. The Millennium Line, opened in 2002, loops through Burnaby, Port Moody, and Coquitlam via the Evergreen Extension (2016). The Canada Line, opened in 2009 ahead of the Winter Olympics, connects downtown with Richmond and Vancouver International Airport; unlike the other lines it uses conventional motor traction and wider cars.

Operations

Trains run on elevated guideways, in tunnels, and at grade, at frequencies as short as about two minutes. The system carries several hundred thousand passengers daily. Extensions under construction and planning include the Broadway Subway extension of the Millennium Line and an Expo Line extension to Langley. Fares are integrated with TransLink's Compass card.
