Netrunner

Netrunner is a collectible card game designed by Richard Garfield, the creator of Magic: The Gathering, and published by Wizards of the Coast in 1996. Set in the cyberpunk world of the Cyberpunk 2020 role-playing game, it is an asymmetric game in which one player takes the role of a megacorporation (the Corp) and the other a hacker (the Runner).

Gameplay

The Corp scores points by advancing and completing agenda cards protected by defensive software known as “ice”, while the Runner builds a rig of programs and hardware to break through that ice and steal the agendas. The game is noted for its bluffing, hidden information, and the very different play styles of the two sides.

Later editions

In 2012 Fantasy Flight Games released a revised version as a Living Card Game, Android: Netrunner, set in its Android universe; it was widely acclaimed and supported with expansions until the licence ended in 2018. A fan organisation, NISEI (later Null Signal Games), continued producing compatible cards and organised play.
