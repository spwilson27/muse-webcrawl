2NE1 (pronounced "twenty-one" or "to anyone") is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment, composed of members Bom, CL, Dara, and Minzy. They debuted in 2009 with the single "Fire" and are widely credited with helping spread the Korean Wave internationally and expanding K-pop's image with a fierce, hip-hop-influenced style that contrasted with the cute concepts common at the time.

Career

The group achieved major success with songs including "I Don't Care" (2009), "Go Away" (2010), "Lonely" and "I Am the Best" (2011), "I Love You" (2012), and "Come Back Home" (2014). Their second studio album Crush (2014) reached number 61 on the U.S. Billboard 200, then a record for a K-pop album. 2NE1 won numerous awards, including Song of the Year at the Mnet Asian Music Awards twice.

Disbandment and reunion

Minzy departed in April 2016, and YG announced the group's disbandment that November; the remaining members released the farewell single "Goodbye" in January 2017. The four members reunited for a surprise performance at Coachella in 2022, and in 2024 they returned with YG for the Welcome Back concert tour celebrating their 15th anniversary.
