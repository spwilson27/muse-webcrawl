Chaman is a city in Balochistan province, Pakistan, situated just east of the border with Afghanistan's Kandahar Province. It is the administrative centre of Chaman District and one of the principal crossing points between the two countries, opposite the Afghan town of Spin Boldak.

Transport and trade

Chaman lies at the end of the Khojak Tunnel railway from Quetta, one of the longest tunnels in South Asia, and the road through the Khojak Pass links Pakistan to Kandahar. The "Friendship Gate" border crossing handles a large volume of trade and daily pedestrian traffic, though it has been closed periodically during tensions between Pakistan and Afghanistan.

Demographics

The population is predominantly Pashtun, principally of the Achakzai tribe, and Pashto is the main language. The city grew around the railway station and military cantonment established under British rule in the late 19th century.
