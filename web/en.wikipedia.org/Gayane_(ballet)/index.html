Gayane (ballet)

Gayane (Gayaneh or Gayne) is a four-act ballet with music by the Armenian composer Aram Khachaturian. It was composed in 1942 and first performed by the Kirov Ballet in Perm, Russia, on 9 February 1943, while Leningrad was under siege and the company evacuated.

Plot and music

Set on a collective farm in Armenia near the border, the ballet tells of Gayane, whose husband Giko is a smuggler and saboteur; the story combines romance, patriotism, and folk dance. The score draws heavily on Armenian folk music.

Sabre Dance

The ballet's most famous number is the "Sabre Dance" from the final act, a frenetic piece that became a popular music standard and has been widely used in films, television, and advertising. Other well-known excerpts include the Adagio, used in the film 2001: A Space Odyssey.
