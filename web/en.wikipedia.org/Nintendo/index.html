Nintendo Co., Ltd. is a Japanese multinational video game company headquartered in Kyoto. Founded in 1889 by the craftsman Fusajiro Yamauchi as a manufacturer of hanafuda playing cards, Nintendo developed into one of the most influential companies in the history of video games, creating franchises such as Mario, The Legend of Zelda, Pokémon, Donkey Kong, and Metroid, and hardware including the Nintendo Entertainment System, Game Boy, Nintendo DS, Wii, and Nintendo Switch.

History

For its first several decades Nintendo specialised in playing cards, becoming Japan's largest card manufacturer. Under Hiroshi Yamauchi, the third president, the company experimented in the 1960s with businesses including taxis, hotels, and toys before entering electronic entertainment. Its early video game successes included the arcade game Donkey Kong (1981), designed by Shigeru Miyamoto, which introduced the character who became Mario.

Consoles

The Famicom, released in Japan in 1983 and in North America as the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES) in 1985, is credited with reviving the American video game industry after the crash of 1983. The hand-held Game Boy (1989) and later the Nintendo DS (2004) made Nintendo the dominant force in portable gaming. The Wii (2006) popularised motion controls and sold over 100 million units. The hybrid Nintendo Switch (2017), usable both as a home console and a hand-held, became one of the best-selling consoles of all time, and was followed by the Nintendo Switch 2 in 2025.

Software and characters

Nintendo is known for its internally developed software and for guarding its intellectual property. Its best-selling series — Mario, Pokémon (co-owned with Game Freak and Creatures Inc.), The Legend of Zelda, Animal Crossing, Super Smash Bros., and Mario Kart — have sold hundreds of millions of copies. Designers such as Shigeru Miyamoto, Gunpei Yokoi, and Satoru Iwata (president 2002–2015) shaped the company's philosophy of "lateral thinking with withered technology": using mature, inexpensive components in novel ways.

Other ventures

Nintendo operates theme park areas (Super Nintendo World) with Universal Parks, co-produced the animated film The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023) with Illumination, and continues to sell playing cards in Japan. It is one of Japan's most valuable companies and a constituent of the Nikkei 225.
