Robert Mitchum

Robert Charles Durman Mitchum (August 6, 1917 – July 1, 1997) was an American actor, known for his antihero roles in film noir and for a laconic, sleepy-eyed screen presence that made him one of the defining stars of post-war Hollywood.

Career

Mitchum earned an Academy Award nomination for The Story of G.I. Joe (1945) and became a noir icon in Out of the Past (1947). He gave two of cinema's most chilling villain performances as the preacher Harry Powell in The Night of the Hunter (1955) and Max Cady in Cape Fear (1962). His many credits include River of No Return, Heaven Knows, Mr. Allison, The Sundowners, Ryan's Daughter, and Farewell, My Lovely. He also recorded successful country music singles, notably "The Ballad of Thunder Road".

Legacy

The American Film Institute ranked Mitchum among the greatest male stars of classic Hollywood cinema; critics praised his understated naturalism, which anticipated later generations of actors.
