This is a list of the largest shopping malls in the United States, ranked by gross leasable area (GLA), the standard measure of a mall's retail floor space. The shopping mall became a defining feature of American suburban life in the second half of the 20th century, and the largest centers grew into regional destinations combining retail, entertainment, dining, and hotels.

Overview

The largest mall in the United States by gross leasable area is the Mall of America in Bloomington, Minnesota, which opened in 1992 on the site of the former Metropolitan Stadium. It contains an indoor amusement park, an aquarium, and hundreds of stores. The American Dream mall in East Rutherford, New Jersey, which opened in stages beginning in 2019, is among the largest by total area and includes ski slopes, a water park, and an amusement park in addition to retail space.

History

The enclosed, climate-controlled shopping mall was pioneered by Austrian-born architect Victor Gruen, whose Southdale Center in Edina, Minnesota (1956) is generally considered the first fully enclosed mall. Through the 1960s and 1970s, malls spread across the country alongside suburbanization. From the 1990s onward, many smaller regional malls declined as e-commerce grew, while the very largest "super-regional" centers increasingly emphasized entertainment and experiences.
