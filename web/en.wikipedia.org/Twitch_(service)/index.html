Twitch is an American video live-streaming service focused on video game streaming, esports broadcasts, music and creative content. Launched in June 2011 as a spin-off of the general-interest streaming site Justin.tv, it grew rapidly and was acquired by Amazon in August 2014 for approximately US$970 million.

Content and community

Streamers broadcast live gameplay and commentary while interacting with viewers through integrated chat, which has its own culture of emotes and memes. Viewers support creators through subscriptions, "Bits" donations and advertising revenue; successful streamers graduate from Affiliate to Partner status. Major events include the TwitchCon convention, charity marathons such as Games Done Quick, and record-breaking streams by creators like Ninja and Kai Cenat.

Expansion and influence

Beyond games, Twitch hosts "Just Chatting", music, art and talk content, and briefly streamed television series. The platform helped turn esports into a mass spectator medium and reshaped online entertainment, though it competes with YouTube Gaming and Kick and has faced recurring debates over moderation, revenue splits and content policy.
