TypeGlobal network of academies of science, medicine and engineering

The InterAcademy Partnership (IAP) is a global network that brings together more than 140 national, regional and global academies of science, engineering and medicine. It mobilises the world's leading experts to provide independent, authoritative advice on scientific, technological and health issues to policymakers and the public, and to strengthen the capacity of academies — particularly in developing countries — to play their national advisory roles.

History

The IAP traces its origins to the InterAcademy Panel on International Issues, founded in 1993 following a population summit of science academies in New Delhi. A separate InterAcademy Medical Panel (IAMP) was created in 2000, and the InterAcademy Council (IAC), established in 2000 by national science academies as an advisory body producing reports on global issues, completed the family of organisations. In 2016 the three bodies merged their governance under the single InterAcademy Partnership umbrella, organising work into IAP for Science, IAP for Health and IAP for Policy programmes.

Activities

The IAP issues statements and reports prepared by experts nominated by member academies, on topics including food and nutrition security, climate change, health inequities, responsible research conduct, science education and the Sustainable Development Goals. It runs programmes to build academy capacity, supports young academies, and works through four regional networks: the Network of African Science Academies (NASAC), the Association of Academies and Societies of Sciences in Asia (AASSA), the European Academies' Science Advisory Council (EASAC) and the Inter-American Network of Academies of Sciences (IANAS). The IAP secretariat is hosted by The World Academy of Sciences (TWAS) in Trieste, Italy.
