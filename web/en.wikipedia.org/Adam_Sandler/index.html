Adam Sandler

Adam Richard Sandler (born September 9, 1966) is an American actor and comedian. He was a cast member on Saturday Night Live from 1990 to 1995, before starring in a string of successful Hollywood comedy films. His films have grossed billions of dollars worldwide, and he founded the production company Happy Madison Productions in 1999.

Career

Sandler was born in Brooklyn, New York, and raised in Manchester, New Hampshire. After performing stand-up comedy in Boston, he was hired as a writer and then featured player on Saturday Night Live, where characters such as Opera Man and songs like "The Chanukah Song" made him popular. His leading film roles in the 1990s included Billy Madison (1995), Happy Gilmore (1996), The Wedding Singer (1998), The Waterboy (1998), and Big Daddy (1999).

Dramatic roles and later work

Sandler has also earned critical acclaim for dramatic performances, notably in Punch-Drunk Love (2002), The Meyerowitz Stories (2017), and Uncut Gems (2019). Since 2014 he has produced and starred in a series of films for Netflix, including The Ridiculous 6, Murder Mystery, and the sports drama Hustle (2022).
