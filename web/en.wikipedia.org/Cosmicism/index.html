Cosmicism

Cosmicism is the literary philosophy developed by American writer H. P. Lovecraft, holding that humanity is an insignificant part of an indifferent cosmos, and that the universe is fundamentally incomprehensible and hostile to human concerns.

Themes

In Lovecraft's fiction and letters, cosmic horror arises from humanity's insignificance, the fragility of sanity before alien entities such as Cthulhu, and "cosmic indifferentism". The philosophy draws on early-20th-century science — astronomy, evolution and relativity — that displaced humanity from the centre of creation.

Influence

Cosmicism underlies the Cthulhu Mythos and has influenced later horror writers and philosophers exploring cosmic pessimism and nihilism.
