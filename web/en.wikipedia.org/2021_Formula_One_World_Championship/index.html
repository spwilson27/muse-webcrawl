The 2021 Formula One World Championship was the 72nd season of Formula One motor racing, contested over 22 Grands Prix. It featured one of the closest title battles in the sport's history, between defending champion Lewis Hamilton of Mercedes and Max Verstappen of Red Bull Racing.

Title battle

Hamilton and Verstappen traded the championship lead throughout the season, colliding controversially at Silverstone and Monza. They arrived at the final race, the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, exactly level on 369.5 points — the first time since 1974 that the title contenders were tied going into the finale.

Abu Dhabi finale

Hamilton led most of the race before a late safety car bunched the field. Race director Michael Masi's decision to allow only the lapped cars between Hamilton and Verstappen to unlap themselves, and to restart the race for a single final lap, allowed Verstappen — on fresh soft tyres — to pass Hamilton and win his first World Drivers' Championship. Mercedes won the Constructors' Championship for a record eighth consecutive year. The handling of the restart was widely criticised, and an FIA inquiry concluded it resulted from human error; Masi was removed from the role before the 2022 season.

Other notes

The season saw sprint qualifying trialled at three events, Esteban Ocon take his first win in Hungary, and Daniel Ricciardo's victory at Monza give McLaren its first win since 2012. It was the final season for 2007 champion Kimi Räikkönen.
