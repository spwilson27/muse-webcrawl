heute (German TV programme)

heute (German for "today") is the flagship television newscast of ZDF, Germany's second public-service broadcaster. First broadcast on 1 April 1963, the day ZDF itself launched, its main 19:00 edition is one of the most-watched news programmes in Germany, competing with the ARD's Tagesschau.

heute
Network: ZDF
First aired: 1 April 1963
Main edition: 19:00

Editions and formats

ZDF broadcasts numerous heute bulletins through the day, including heute – in Deutschland, heute – in Europa, and the late-night heute journal, an in-depth magazine format launched in 1978. The morning programme ZDF-Morgenmagazin incorporates heute news summaries. Since 2021 the main edition has been produced from a redesigned studio with an updated visual identity.

Presenters

Long-serving presenters of the main edition have included Petra Gerster and Marietta Slomka; the programme is produced by ZDF's news division in Mainz. See also Tagesschau and television in Germany.
