Jagdgeschwader 52

Jagdgeschwader 52 (JG 52) was a Luftwaffe fighter wing of the Second World War and the most successful fighter wing of all time, claiming more than 10,000 aerial victories over a period of about five years.

Service history

Formed in 1939, JG 52 fought on the Western Front during the Battle of France and the Battle of Britain before transferring to the Eastern Front in 1941, where it spent most of the war. Its pilots included the three highest-scoring fighter aces in history: Erich Hartmann (352 victories), Gerhard Barkhorn (301), and Günther Rall (275), as well as Hermann Graf and other leading Experten.

End of the war

Fighting a numerically superior Soviet air force, the wing retreated westward through Romania, Hungary, and Austria. Its remaining personnel surrendered in May 1945; many were handed over to the Soviet Union and spent years in captivity.
