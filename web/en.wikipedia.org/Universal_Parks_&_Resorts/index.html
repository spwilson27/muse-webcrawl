Universal Parks & Resorts (now branded as Universal Destinations & Experiences) is the theme park unit of NBCUniversal, a subsidiary of Comcast. It operates Universal-branded theme parks and resorts around the world, based on films and franchises from Universal Pictures and other studios, including Jurassic Park, Harry Potter, Despicable Me, The Simpsons and Nintendo properties.

Resorts

The flagship Universal Orlando Resort in Florida includes Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure and the Volcano Bay water park, with the Epic Universe park added in 2025. Universal Studios Hollywood in California began as a studio tram tour in 1964. International resorts include Universal Studios Japan in Osaka (opened 2001), Universal Studios Singapore (2010) and Universal Beijing Resort (2021).

The Wizarding World

The Wizarding World of Harry Potter lands, beginning at Islands of Adventure in 2010, transformed the parks' fortunes, driving record attendance with immersive environments such as Hogsmeade and Diagon Alley connected by a working Hogwarts Express. Super Nintendo World, developed with Nintendo, opened in Japan in 2021 and subsequently in Hollywood and Orlando.

History

Universal's park business grew from the Hollywood studio tour into full theme parks; the Orlando resort opened in 1990 as a joint venture. Comcast acquired full control through its purchase of NBCUniversal completed in 2013. The division was renamed Universal Destinations & Experiences in 2023, reflecting expansion into new ventures including a family park in Texas and a horror attraction in Las Vegas.
