Count Orlok

Count Orlok (Graf Orlok in German) is the main antagonist of the 1922 German Expressionist horror film Nosferatu: A Symphony of Horror, directed by F. W. Murnau. Played by Max Schreck, Orlok is a vampire based on Bram Stoker's Count Dracula; because the production failed to obtain the rights to Stoker's novel, names and details were changed, though the Stoker estate successfully sued and nearly destroyed the film.

Character

Orlok is a centuries-old vampire dwelling in a castle in the Carpathian Mountains. Unlike the aristocratic Dracula, he is depicted as a grotesque, rat-like figure with a bald head, sunken eyes, long claw-like fingers, and prominent front teeth. He brings plague to the fictional German town of Wisborg, travelling by ship with coffins and rats. He is destroyed when Ellen, the heroine, sacrifices herself and keeps him drinking her blood until sunrise, whose rays kill him.

Later portrayals

Klaus Kinski played the character (as Dracula in name) in Werner Herzog's 1979 remake Nosferatu the Vampyre. Willem Dafoe portrayed Max Schreck-as-Orlok in Shadow of the Vampire (2000), and Bill Skarsgård played Orlok in Robert Eggers's 2024 remake Nosferatu. Orlok has become an enduring icon of screen horror, frequently referenced and parodied in popular culture.
