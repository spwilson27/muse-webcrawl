A Chinese Ghost Story
Directed by: Ching Siu-tung
Produced by: Tsui Hark
Starring: Leslie Cheung, Joey Wong, Wu Ma
Released: 1987 (Hong Kong)

A Chinese Ghost Story (Chinese: 倩女幽魂) is a 1987 Hong Kong romantic comedy-horror film directed by Ching Siu-tung and produced by Tsui Hark. Loosely adapted from "Nie Xiaoqian," a story in Pu Songling's Strange Stories from a Chinese Studio, it stars Leslie Cheung as a hapless tax collector and Joey Wong as the ghostly heroine.

Plot

Debt collector Ning Caichen takes shelter for the night in the haunted Lantern Temple (Lan Ruo Si), where he falls in love with Nie Xiaoqian, a beautiful ghost compelled by a tree demon to lure men to their deaths. With the help of the Taoist swordsman Yan Chixia (Wu Ma), Ning attempts to free Xiaoqian's spirit so that she may be reincarnated.

Reception and legacy

The film was a major critical and commercial success, credited with popularizing the "ghost romance" genre in Hong Kong cinema and launching a wave of period fantasy films. It won awards at the Hong Kong Film Awards, including for its cinematography and art direction, and spawned two sequels (1990 and 1991), an animated version, and later remakes. Joey Wong's portrayal of Nie Xiaoqian became iconic in Chinese popular culture.
