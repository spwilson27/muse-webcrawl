Barre chord

A barre chord (also spelled bar chord) is a type of chord on a guitar or other stringed instrument played by using one or more fingers to press down multiple strings across a single fret of the fingerboard, like a bar pressing across the strings.

Technique

The index finger is usually placed across all six strings, while the other fingers form the chord shape above it. This allows chord shapes to be moved up and down the neck, transposing the chord without changing its quality.

Common forms

The most common barre chord shapes are derived from the open E and A chords, giving movable major and minor chords rooted on the sixth or fifth string.

Difficulty

Barre chords are a well-known challenge for beginners because they require finger strength and precise placement to avoid buzzing or muted strings.
