Persuasion (novel)

Persuasion is the last novel fully completed by Jane Austen. It was published on 20 December 1817, six months after her death, together with Northanger Abbey. The story concerns Anne Elliot, a young Englishwoman of 27 whose family moves to lower their expenses and reduce their debt by renting their home to an admiral and his wife.

Plot

Eight years before the novel opens, Anne Elliot was persuaded by her family and her mentor Lady Russell to break off her engagement to Frederick Wentworth, then a young naval commander without fortune. Now Wentworth has returned from the Napoleonic Wars a wealthy captain. As their paths cross again — in Uppercross, Lyme Regis, and Bath — Anne must watch him court others while concealing her renewed love. A now-famous letter from Wentworth, declaring "You pierce my soul. I am half agony, half hope," resolves the story in the lovers' reunion.

Themes

The novel is often considered Austen's most mature work. Its themes include the danger and value of persuasion, the changing social order as naval merit rises against landed rank, ageing and second chances, and constancy in love. Anne Elliot, older and quieter than Austen's earlier heroines, is a study in patience and self-command.

Reception and adaptations

Persuasion has grown steadily in critical esteem and is now regarded by many as among Austen's finest novels. It has been adapted many times for screen, including the 1995 film with Amanda Root and Ciarán Hinds and a 2022 Netflix film starring Dakota Johnson.
