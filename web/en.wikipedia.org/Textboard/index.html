Textboard

A textboard is a simple kind of Internet forum that supports anonymous posting without user registration and without image uploads. Textboards descend from Japanese BBS culture, in particular 2channel (now 5channel), founded in 1999 by Hiroyuki Nishimura.

Features

Users post text-only messages in threaded discussions, typically under the default name "Anonymous" or a Japanese equivalent. Threads are usually ordered by most recent post ("bumping"), and old threads are deleted as new ones arrive. Optional tripcodes let a poster prove identity across posts without an account.

Influence

Textboards are the text-only counterparts of imageboards such as 2chan and 4chan, which added image posting to the same thread-and-anonymity model. English-language textboards are comparatively niche, but the format shaped much of early anonymous web culture in Japan and abroad.
