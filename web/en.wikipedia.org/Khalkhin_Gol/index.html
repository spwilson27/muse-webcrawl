Khalkhin Gol

The Battles of Khalkhin Gol were the decisive engagements of the undeclared Soviet–Japanese border war of 1939, fought along the Khalkhin Gol river in Manchuria between the Soviet Union, Mongolia and Imperial Japan from May to September 1939.

Outcome

Under Georgy Zhukov, Soviet-Mongolian forces encircled and defeated the Japanese Sixth Army in August 1939. The defeat discouraged Japanese expansion northward and preceded the Soviet–Japanese Neutrality Pact of 1941.
