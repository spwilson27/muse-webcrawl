Robert J. Van de Graaff

Robert J. Van de Graaff
Born: 20 December 1901, Tuscaloosa, Alabama
Died: 16 January 1967, Boston, Massachusetts
Known for: Van de Graaff generator

Robert Jemison Van de Graaff (1901–1967) was an American physicist, noted for his design of the Van de Graaff generator, an electrostatic machine that produces very high voltages.

Career

After studying engineering at the University of Alabama and physics at the Sorbonne and Oxford, Van de Graaff developed his generator at Princeton and MIT around 1929–1931. The device transports electric charge on a moving belt into a hollow metal sphere, accumulating potentials of millions of volts. Such generators became important particle accelerators for nuclear physics research and are now familiar demonstration devices in science museums.

Legacy

Van de Graaff co-founded the High Voltage Engineering Corporation in 1946 to commercialise electrostatic accelerators. A crater on the Moon is named after him.
