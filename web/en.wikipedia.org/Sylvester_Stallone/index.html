Sylvester Gardenzio Stallone (born July 6, 1946) is an American actor and filmmaker. In a film career spanning more than fifty years, Stallone has received numerous accolades, including a Golden Globe Award and a Critics' Choice Award, as well as nominations for three Academy Awards and two BAFTA Awards. Stallone is one of only two actors in history (alongside Harrison Ford) to have starred in a box-office No. 1 film across six consecutive decades.

Struggling as an actor for a number of years after moving to New York City in 1969, Stallone had his breakthrough as a writer and actor in 1976 with Rocky, a sleeper hit that he wrote in three days after watching a boxing match between Muhammad Ali and Chuck Wepner. He went on to star in two of the most successful action film franchises of all time: the Rocky series, playing boxer Rocky Balboa, and the Rambo series, playing Vietnam War veteran John Rambo.

Early life

Stallone was born in the Hell's Kitchen neighborhood of Manhattan, New York City, the elder son of Jacqueline Stallone, an astrologer and former dancer, and Frank Stallone Sr., a hairdresser. Complications suffered by his mother during labor forced her obstetricians to use two pairs of forceps during his birth, which accidentally severed a nerve and caused paralysis in parts of Stallone's face. As a result, the left side of his lower lip, tongue, and chin have a small amount of paralysis, giving him his snarling look and slurred speech. His younger brother is actor and musician Frank Stallone.

He spent some of his childhood in foster care before rejoining his family and moving to Maryland and later Philadelphia. He attended the University of Miami on an athletic scholarship before returning to New York to pursue acting.

Career

After small roles in films such as The Lords of Flatbush (1974) and a softcore feature, Stallone wrote the screenplay for Rocky and insisted on playing the title role despite offers to sell the script outright. The film was shot on a budget of around $1 million, grossed over $225 million worldwide, and won the Academy Award for Best Picture. Stallone was nominated for Academy Awards for Best Original Screenplay and Best Actor, only the third person in history after Charlie Chaplin and Orson Welles to receive both nominations for the same film.

In 1982, Stallone portrayed Vietnam veteran John Rambo in First Blood, launching another long-running franchise. Through the 1980s he was one of the biggest box office draws in the world, with hits including Rocky III, Rambo: First Blood Part II, and Cobra. After a period of commercial disappointments in the 1990s, punctuated by a critically praised dramatic turn in Cop Land (1997), he revived both franchises with Rocky Balboa (2006) and Rambo (2008).

In the 2010s, Stallone created and starred in The Expendables ensemble action series, and earned widespread acclaim—and a Golden Globe Award plus an Academy Award nomination—for reprising the role of Balboa in Creed (2015). He later starred in the television crime drama Tulsa King (2022–present).

Personal life

Stallone has been married three times and has five children. His eldest son, Sage Stallone, who played Rocky's son in Rocky V, died in 2012 at the age of 36. He has been married to Jennifer Flavin since 1997, with whom he has three daughters.
