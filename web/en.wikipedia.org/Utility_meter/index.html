Utility meter

A utility meter is a device that measures the amount of a utility—electricity, gas, or water—supplied to a residence or business, enabling the utility company to bill customers according to consumption.

Types

Traditional electricity meters are electromechanical induction meters with a spinning aluminium disc; gas meters typically use diaphragms; water meters measure flow mechanically. Modern "smart meters" record consumption electronically at intervals and transmit readings automatically (AMI), enabling time-of-use pricing and outage detection.

Reading and billing

Meters may be cumulative registers read in units such as kilowatt-hours, cubic metres, or therms. Meter accuracy is regulated, and tampering with a meter is an offence in most jurisdictions.
