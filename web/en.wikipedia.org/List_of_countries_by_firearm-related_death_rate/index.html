List of countries by firearm-related death rate

This list ranks countries by the annual rate of deaths by firearms per 100,000 population, including homicides, suicides and accidental deaths. Data are drawn mainly from the Global Burden of Disease study, the Small Arms Survey and national statistics; years and methodologies vary.

Overview

The highest total firearm death rates are generally found in parts of Latin America and the Caribbean — including Venezuela, El Salvador, Guatemala, Colombia, Brazil and Jamaica — driven mainly by homicide. The United States has the highest rate among high-income countries, at roughly 14-15 per 100,000 in recent years, with suicides accounting for more than half of US gun deaths.

Countries such as Japan, South Korea, the United Kingdom, China and Singapore have very low rates, often below 0.1-0.3 per 100,000. Suicide by firearm dominates totals in countries such as Greenland and the United States, while homicide dominates in Central and South America.

Rates change year to year, and some countries lack reliable recent data; the underlying table in the original article lists total, homicide, suicide and unintentional rates separately.
