Celtic Colours

Celtic Colours International Festival
Location: Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia, Canada
Founded: 1997
Held: October, 9 days

Celtic Colours is an annual Celtic music and culture festival held each October across Cape Breton Island in Nova Scotia, Canada. Founded in 1997, it coincides with the island's autumn foliage and draws performers and visitors from Scotland, Ireland and other Celtic regions.

Programme

Concerts take place in community halls, churches and schools in dozens of communities, alongside workshops, ceilidhs, storytelling and cultural tours. Cape Breton fiddle and piano traditions are central.

Impact

The festival extends the tourist season, supports community organisations that host events, and has received national tourism and cultural awards.
