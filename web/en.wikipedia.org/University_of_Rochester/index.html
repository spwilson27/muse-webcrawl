University of Rochester

The University of Rochester is a private research university in Rochester, New York. Founded in 1850, it is known for the Eastman School of Music, medicine, optics and economics.

History

Founded by Baptists in 1850, the university moved to its River Campus in 1930 with support from George Eastman.

Academics

Components include Arts, Sciences and Engineering, the Eastman School of Music, Simon Business School, and the School of Medicine and Dentistry.

Research

Rochester is home to the Laboratory for Laser Energetics and a major medical centre.
