Winter Guard International

Winter Guard International (WGI), officially Winter Guard International, Sport of the Arts, is an American governing body for indoor performing arts competitions in color guard, percussion ensembles, and winds. Founded in 1977, WGI organises regional competitions across the United States and a world championship each spring, traditionally held in Dayton, Ohio.

Activities

WGI's competitive season runs through the winter and early spring, when member ensembles — school-based and independent groups — perform choreographed shows indoors, typically in gymnasiums and arenas. Color guard units combine dance with the spinning of flags, rifles, and sabres; percussion ensembles perform as marching or concert units; and the winds division, added in 2015, covers indoor marching ensembles of brass and woodwind players.

Classes and judging

Ensembles compete in classes organised by experience and achievement level, such as Scholastic and Independent classes subdivided into A, Open, and World. Performances are scored by panels of judges on criteria including equipment and movement technique, design analysis, and general effect. The WGI World Championships conclude the season with preliminary, semifinal, and final rounds in each class.
