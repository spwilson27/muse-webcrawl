David Petraeus

David Howell Petraeus (born 7 November 1952) is a retired United States Army general and public official. He commanded coalition forces in Iraq during the 2007 "surge", led US Central Command, and commanded forces in Afghanistan before serving as Director of the Central Intelligence Agency from 2011 to 2012.

Career and resignation

Petraeus co-authored the US Army's counterinsurgency field manual (FM 3-24) and was widely credited with the improved security situation in Iraq in 2007–2008. He resigned as CIA director in November 2012 after an FBI investigation revealed an extramarital affair with his biographer Paula Broadwell; in 2015 he pleaded guilty to a misdemeanour charge of mishandling classified information in connection with the affair and was sentenced to probation and a fine. He later became a partner at the investment firm KKR.
