The Players Championship is an annual golf tournament on the PGA Tour. First played in 1974, it is often regarded as the most prestigious tournament outside the four major championships and is sometimes informally called the "fifth major".

Venue

Since 1982 the tournament has been held at TPC Sawgrass in Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida, whose Stadium Course was designed by Pete Dye. Its par-3 17th hole, the famous "Island Green", is one of the most recognizable holes in golf.

Field and format

The Players offers one of the strongest fields in golf and historically one of its richest purses. Jack Nicklaus won the event three times in the 1970s; Tiger Woods won it twice, in 2001 and 2013.
