Edmund Allenby

Edmund Allenby is a common name. Most notable is Field Marshal Edmund Henry Hynman Allenby, 1st Viscount Allenby (23 April 1861 – 14 May 1936), a British soldier and administrator best known for commanding the Egyptian Expeditionary Force in the Sinai and Palestine campaign of the First World War, capturing Jerusalem in December 1917 and destroying Ottoman armies at Megiddo in 1918. He subsequently served as High Commissioner for Egypt and the Sudan (1919–1925).

Other uses

The name Allenby also appears in geography named for the Field Marshal, including Allenby Street in Tel Aviv, Allenby Square in Jerusalem, and the Allenby Bridge (King Hussein Bridge) over the Jordan River between the West Bank and Jordan.
