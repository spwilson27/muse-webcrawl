Coal Miner's Daughter (film)

Directed by Michael Apted

Starring Sissy Spacek, Tommy Lee Jones

Released 1980

Country United States

Coal Miner's Daughter is a 1980 American biographical musical film directed by Michael Apted and based on the life of country music singer Loretta Lynn. Sissy Spacek stars as Lynn, and performed her own vocals for the soundtrack. Tommy Lee Jones plays her husband, Oliver "Doolittle" Lynn, with Beverly D'Angelo as Patsy Cline and Levon Helm as Lynn's father.

Plot

The film traces Lynn's life from her childhood in the coal-mining community of Butcher Hollow, Kentucky, through her marriage at a young age, her rise to stardom in Nashville guided by her husband's promotion, her friendship with Patsy Cline, and the physical and emotional toll of touring and fame.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success. Spacek won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her performance, and the film received seven Academy Award nominations in total, including Best Picture. It is frequently cited among the finest music biopics, and in 2019 it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry.
