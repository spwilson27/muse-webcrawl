Paris Saint-Germain Football Club, commonly referred to as Paris Saint-Germain, PSG or Paris SG, is a French professional football club based in Paris. Founded in 1970 from the merger of Paris FC and Stade Saint-Germain, the club plays in Ligue 1, the top tier of French football, and has been the dominant French club of the 2010s and 2020s, winning a record number of Ligue 1 titles (12 by 2024).

History

PSG won its first major honour, the Coupe de France, in 1982 and its first league title in 1986. Under the ownership of broadcaster Canal+ in the 1990s the club won another title (1994) and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1996, still a landmark European success. In 2011 the club was acquired by Qatar Sports Investments, whose investment transformed PSG into one of the richest clubs in world football. Star signings followed, including Zlatan Ibrahimović, and in 2017 the world-record transfer of Neymar from Barcelona for €222 million, alongside the signing of Kylian Mbappé. With Lionel Messi joining in 2021, PSG fielded a celebrated attacking trio, though the UEFA Champions League title long remained elusive; the club reached the final in 2020, losing to Bayern Munich.

Stadium and identity

The club's home ground is the Parc des Princes, in the 16th arrondissement of Paris, its home since 1974. Its traditional colours are blue, red and white; the classic shirt design by Daniel Hechter features a central red vertical stripe. PSG's fiercest rivalry is with Olympique de Marseille, contested as Le Classique.

Honours

PSG is the most successful club in French football in terms of trophies, with a record haul of Ligue 1 championships, Coupe de France titles (including a record 15 by 2024), Coupe de la Ligue and Trophée des Champions titles, as well as the 1996 Cup Winners' Cup and the 2001 Intertoto Cup.
