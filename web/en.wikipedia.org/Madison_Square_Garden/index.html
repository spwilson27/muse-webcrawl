Madison Square Garden

Madison Square Garden, colloquially known as the Garden or MSG, is a multi-purpose indoor arena in New York City. Located in Midtown Manhattan between Seventh and Eighth Avenues from 31st to 33rd Streets, above Pennsylvania Station, the current building opened on 11 February 1968 and is the fourth venue to bear the name. It bills itself as "The World's Most Famous Arena".

Tenants and events

The Garden is home to the New York Knicks of the NBA and the New York Rangers of the NHL, and it formerly hosted the New York Liberty of the WNBA. It is one of the world's leading venues for boxing — it hosted the "Fight of the Century" between Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier in 1971 — as well as concerts, professional wrestling, college basketball, ice shows, and special events. Legendary performers from Elvis Presley and Led Zeppelin to Billy Joel, who holds a monthly residency, have played there.

The building

The arena seats roughly 19,500 for basketball and 18,000 for hockey. A major renovation completed in 2013 modernized the interior, adding the distinctive Chase Bridges suspended from the ceiling. The venue sits atop one of the busiest rail stations in the United States, a fact that has fuelled recurring proposals to relocate the arena as Penn Station is rebuilt.
