Temasek

Temasek Holdings (Private) Limited, or simply Temasek, is a Singaporean state-owned multinational investment firm. Incorporated on 25 June 1974, Temasek has a net portfolio value of several hundred billion Singapore dollars (around S$389 billion as of 2024), with investments spanning financial services, telecommunications, media and technology, consumer and real estate, transportation and industrials, life sciences and agri-food.

History

Temasek was created to hold and manage commercially the assets previously held by the Singapore government, so that the Ministry of Finance could focus on policymaking. Its initial portfolio of S$354 million comprised 35 companies, start-ups and joint ventures, including Singapore Airlines, DBS Bank, Singtel and the PSA port operations. It is wholly owned by the Minister for Finance (Incorporated) but operates as a commercial investment company with its own board.

The name Temasek derives from Tumasik, an early name for Singapore recorded in Malay and Javanese sources. Temasek is one of two main sovereign investment vehicles of Singapore, the other being GIC, which manages the country's foreign reserves.
