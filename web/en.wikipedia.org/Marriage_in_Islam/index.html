Marriage in Islam

Marriage in Islam (nikah) is a legal contract between a man and a woman in which both consent to marry. The groom gives the bride a gift called a mahr, agreed in the contract, and the marriage is witnessed.

Islamic tradition encourages marriage as the normative state for adults; the Quran and hadith describe rights and obligations of spouses, rules on divorce (talaq, khula), and prohibitions on marriage between close relatives (mahram).

Practices vary widely across cultures and schools of Islamic law (fiqh).
