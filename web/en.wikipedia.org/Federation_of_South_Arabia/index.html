Federation of South Arabia

The Federation of South Arabia (Arabic: اتحاد الجنوب العربي) was a British-organized federation of states in what is now southern Yemen. It existed from 1962, when the earlier Federation of Arab Emirates of the South was renamed and joined by the colony of Aden, until 1967, when it was abolished upon the independence of the People's Republic of South Yemen.

History

Britain had controlled the port of Aden since 1839, and the surrounding sheikhdoms and sultanates of the western Aden Protectorate were bound to Britain by treaties of protection. In February 1959, Britain joined six of these states into the Federation of Arab Emirates of the South, hoping to create a viable, pro-British political unit and a counterweight to Arab nationalism. On 4 April 1962 the federation was renamed the Federation of South Arabia, and on 18 January 1963 the Crown Colony of Aden joined as the State of Aden, despite strong opposition from Aden's trade unions and nationalist parties, who favored union with Yemen.

The Aden Emergency

The merger and continued British presence provoked an armed uprising beginning in October 1963, known as the Aden Emergency. The National Liberation Front (NLF), backed by Egypt and North Yemen, and the rival Front for the Liberation of Occupied South Yemen fought British forces and each other for control of the future state.

Abolition

Facing mounting casualties and political pressure, Britain withdrew in November 1967. The sultans of the federation were deposed, and on 30 November 1967 the People's Republic of South Yemen was proclaimed with the NLF in power, ending the federation. South Yemen later became the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen and in 1990 unified with North Yemen to form the present Republic of Yemen.

Member states

The federation at various times included Aden, the sheikhdoms of Aqrabi, Alawi, Dhala, Haushabi, Lahej, Lower Yafa, Maflahi, Shaib, and the sultanates of Fadhli, Lower Aulaqi, and others; the eastern states of the Aden Protectorate — Kathiri, Mahra, and Qu'aiti — remained outside as the Protectorate of South Arabia.
