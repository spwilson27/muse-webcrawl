Seiyu Awards

The Seiyu Awards (Japanese: 声優アワード, Seiyū Awādo) are an annual awards ceremony honouring outstanding voice acting in Japan. First held in 2007, they recognise performers — seiyū — working in anime, games, dubbing and narration, and are regarded as the most prominent awards in the Japanese voice-acting industry.

History and organisation

The awards were established with support from industry bodies including the Association of Japanese Animations and major production and publishing companies. Winners in the main categories are chosen by a selection committee, while some categories, such as the overseas fan award in early years, involved public voting. The ceremony is typically held each March in Tokyo, with results announced on radio and streamed online.

Categories

Core categories include Best Actor and Best Actress in Leading Roles, Best Supporting Actor and Actress, Best New Actor and Actress, Best Singing (for a voice actor or group performing as musical artists), and Best Personality for radio and hosting work. Special honours include the Lifetime Achievement Award, the Special Merit Award, and the Kei Tomiyama Award and Kazue Takahashi Award, named for pioneering performers and given for broadening the profession's reach. In 2019 the organisers restructured the leading and supporting categories to be gender-neutral in name (Best Lead Voice Actor, and so on), reflecting industry practice.

Notable winners

Early Best Actress honourees included Romi Park and Rie Kugimiya, while Mamoru Miyano, Hiroshi Kamiya and Yoshitsugu Matsuoka are among the leading-role winners on the male side. The veteran award categories have recognised figures such as Kenji Utsumi and Toshiko Fujita for lifetime contributions to the craft.
