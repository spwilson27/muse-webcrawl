Business process outsourcing

Business process outsourcing (BPO) is the practice of contracting specific business functions or processes to an external service provider. Commonly outsourced functions include customer support call centres, payroll and human resources administration, finance and accounting, data entry, and IT-related services.

Types

BPO is divided into back-office outsourcing (internal functions such as billing and purchasing) and front-office outsourcing (customer-facing services such as marketing and technical support). Work may be sent offshore to lower-cost countries, nearshore to nearby countries, or kept onshore. India and the Philippines became the leading offshore destinations, employing millions in the industry thanks to large English-speaking workforces and lower labour costs.

Advantages and criticism

Companies cite cost savings, flexibility, and the ability to focus on core competencies. Critics raise concerns about domestic job losses, service quality, data security, and working conditions. Automation and artificial intelligence are increasingly reshaping the industry, shifting providers toward higher-value "knowledge process outsourcing".
