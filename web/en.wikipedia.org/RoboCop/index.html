RoboCop

RoboCop is a 1987 American science fiction action film directed by Paul Verhoeven and written by Edward Neumeier and Michael Miner. It stars Peter Weller as police officer Alex Murphy, who is murdered and resurrected by the megacorporation Omni Consumer Products as a cyborg law enforcer in a near-future, crime-ridden Detroit.

Plot and themes

Murphy, reborn as RoboCop, patrols Detroit while fragments of his human memory resurface, leading him to pursue his own killers. Beneath the ultraviolent action, the film is a satire of corporate power, privatisation, media culture and American consumerism, delivered partly through fake news broadcasts and advertisements.

Reception and legacy

The film was a critical and commercial success, praised for its direction, special effects and thematic depth; it won an Academy Award for Best Sound Effects Editing. It spawned two sequels, a 2014 remake, television series, animated shows, comics and video games, and RoboCop has become an enduring pop-culture icon.
