Ernst Haas

Ernst B. Haas (1924–2003) was an American political scientist and founder of neofunctionalism. Born in Frankfurt, Germany, he emigrated to the United States and taught at the University of California, Berkeley. His book The Uniting of Europe (1958), a study of the European Coal and Steel Community, is a foundational text of regional integration theory.

Neofunctionalism

Haas argued that integration in one economic sector creates pressures — functional, political and cultivated spillover — for integration in others, shifting elite loyalties toward new supranational institutions. He later famously declared regional integration theory 'obsolescent' in the 1970s, before renewed interest with the Single European Act.

Later work

His later research addressed nationalism, human rights, and the management of global commons.
