In the Heights

In the Heights is a musical with music and lyrics by Lin-Manuel Miranda and a book by Quiara Alegría Hudes. Set in the largely Dominican-American neighbourhood of Washington Heights in Upper Manhattan, New York City, it follows bodega owner Usnavi and his neighbours over three days as a heatwave, a blackout, and a winning lottery ticket change their lives.

Productions

Miranda began writing the show at Wesleyan University in 1999. After workshops, it premiered Off-Broadway in 2007 and transferred to Broadway's Richard Rodgers Theatre in 2008, winning four Tony Awards including Best Musical and Best Original Score. A West End production won three Olivier Awards, and the show has been staged internationally.

Music and themes

The score fuses hip-hop, salsa, merengue, bachata, and traditional Broadway styles — represented in numbers such as "96,000," "Carnaval del Barrio," and "Paciencia y Fe." Themes include community, gentrification, immigration, and the competing pulls of home and ambition, centred on characters including Nina Rosario, Benny, Vanessa, and Abuela Claudia.

Film adaptation

A film adaptation directed by Jon M. Chu, with a screenplay by Hudes, was released in 2021, starring Anthony Ramos as Usnavi.
