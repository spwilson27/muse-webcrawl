King County, Washington

County seat: Seattle
Population: about 2.27 million
Area: 2,307 sq mi

King County is located in the US state of Washington. It is the state's most populous county and one of the most populous in the country, with about 2.27 million residents. Its county seat and largest city is Seattle.

Name

The county was originally named in 1852 for William R. King, US vice president-elect and a slaveholder; in 1986 the county council voted to re-designate the namesake as civil rights leader Martin Luther King Jr., and the county logo was later changed to his image.

Geography and economy

Stretching from Puget Sound to the Cascade crest, the county includes suburbs such as Bellevue and Redmond and rural mountain land. It hosts the headquarters of Amazon and Microsoft (in Redmond), Boeing facilities, and major ports, universities and technology employers.
