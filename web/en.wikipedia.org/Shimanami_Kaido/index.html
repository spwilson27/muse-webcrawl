Shimanami Kaido

Length: about 60 km
Route: Onomichi to Imabari
Crosses: Seto Inland Sea
Opened: 1999 (full route)

The Shimanami Kaidō (しまなみ海道) is a toll road and expressway in Japan that links the city of Onomichi in Hiroshima Prefecture, on the main island of Honshu, with Imabari in Ehime Prefecture on Shikoku. It crosses the Seto Inland Sea by way of a chain of bridges spanning six islands: Mukaishima, Innoshima, Ikuchijima, Ōmishima, Hakatajima and Ōshima.

Bridges

The route includes ten bridges, among them the Kurushima Kaikyō Bridge — at its opening the world's longest suspension bridge structure — and the Tatara Bridge, a major cable-stayed bridge. The road forms the westernmost of the three Honshu–Shikoku bridge connections.

Cycling

The Shimanami Kaido is famous as one of Japan's premier cycling routes. A dedicated bicycle and pedestrian path runs the full length of the route, with a marked blue line guiding riders, rental terminals at both ends, and gentle gradients designed for cyclists of all levels. International cycling events held on the route draw thousands of participants.
