La Brea Tar Pits

The La Brea Tar Pits are a group of natural asphalt seeps in Hancock Park, Los Angeles, California, where Ice Age animals became trapped and preserved over tens of thousands of years.

Fossils

Excavations since the early 20th century have recovered millions of specimens: dire wolves, saber-toothed cats (Smilodon), Columbian mammoths, ground sloths, bison, birds, insects and plants, providing an unusually complete record of life in the late Pleistocene.

Museum

The La Brea Tar Pits Museum (formerly the George C. Page Museum), opened in 1977, displays fossils and active excavations including Project 23. The site is a National Natural Landmark.
