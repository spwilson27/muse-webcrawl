Nightcrawler (character)

Nightcrawler (Kurt Wagner) is a superhero appearing in Marvel Comics, most often with the X-Men. Created by writer Len Wein and artist Dave Cockrum, he debuted in Giant-Size X-Men #1 (1975). A German mutant with indigo skin, pointed ears, fangs and a prehensile tail, he can teleport in a burst of brimstone-smelling smoke.

Character

Despite his demonic appearance, Kurt Wagner is cheerful, swashbuckling and devoutly Catholic. Raised in a circus, he became a core X-Man and leader of the British team Excalibur. Storylines have revealed his parentage — the mutant Mystique is his mother — and his connection to the Neyaphem and the demonic Azazel. He has died and been resurrected in major story arcs, remaining one of the team's moral centres.

In other media

Nightcrawler was played by Alan Cumming in X2 (2003) and by Kodi Smit-McPhee in later X-Men films, and appears in numerous animated series and games.
