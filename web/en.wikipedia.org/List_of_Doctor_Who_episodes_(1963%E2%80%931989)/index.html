List of Doctor Who episodes (1963–1989)

Doctor Who is a British science fiction television programme produced by the BBC. This article lists the episodes of the programme's original run, broadcast from 1963 to 1989, often referred to as the "classic series". Stories were broadcast in a serial format, each consisting of multiple episodes (usually four or six 25-minute parts). In total, 695 episodes were broadcast as part of 155 serials across 26 seasons, plus a 20th-anniversary special and one later television film (1996, usually grouped with the classic era).

Notes

Many episodes from the 1960s no longer exist in the BBC archives, having been wiped; 97 episodes remain missing, though audio recordings survive for all of them and some episodes have been recovered or animated. Landmark serials include The Daleks (1963–64), The Dalek Invasion of Earth, Genesis of the Daleks (1975), The Talons of Weng-Chiang, City of Death (1979), Earthshock (1982), The Caves of Androzani (1984) and Remembrance of the Daleks (1988). The five-Doctor special The Five Doctors aired in 1983.
