Women's football in Spain

Women's football in Spain has grown rapidly since the late 20th century. The top flight, Liga F (founded as the Superliga Femenina in 2001 and professionalised in 2021), is dominated by FC Barcelona Femení, whose matches at Camp Nou set world attendance records for women's club football in 2022.

History

Although informal matches were played as early as 1914, women's football was suppressed during the Franco regime and only received official recognition from the Royal Spanish Football Federation in 1980. The first official national championship, the Copa de la Reina, began in 1983, and a national league followed in 1988.

National team

The Spain women's national team, known as La Roja, qualified for its first FIFA Women's World Cup in 2015 and won the tournament in 2023, defeating England 1–0 in the final in Sydney with a goal by captain Olga Carmona. Spain also won the UEFA Women's Nations League in 2024. Players such as Alexia Putellas and Aitana Bonmatí have won multiple Ballon d'Or Féminin awards.
