LocationLondon, United Kingdom
OperatorTransport for London / National Rail
ValidityZones 1–9; 1-day, 7-day, monthly, annual

A Travelcard is a season ticket issued by Transport for London and National Rail that allows unlimited travel on most public transport in Greater London within the zones purchased, including the Underground, Overground, DLR, buses, trams, and National Rail services. Travelcards are sold for one day or seven days, or as monthly and annual season tickets.

History

The Travelcard was introduced in 1983 by London Regional Transport as part of a fares simplification, and is credited with significantly increasing public transport use in London. In 2004, one-day Travelcards began to be supplemented by price capping on the Oyster card, and paper one-day Travelcards were withdrawn from TfL retail in the 2010s, though they remain available from National Rail stations.

Zones and pricing

Prices depend on the zones covered (central Zone 1 through outer Zone 9) and the period. Bus travel is included across all of London regardless of zones. Off-peak day Travelcards are cheaper and valid after 09:30 on weekdays.
