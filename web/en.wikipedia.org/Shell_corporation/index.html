Shell corporation


A shell corporation typically exists only on paper, with little or no physical presence.

A shell corporation is a corporation without active business operations or significant assets. These types of corporations are not all necessarily illegal, but they are sometimes used illegitimately, such as to disguise business ownership from law enforcement or the public. Legitimate reasons for creating a shell company include holding assets, facilitating mergers and acquisitions, and conducting business in a foreign jurisdiction while reducing exposure to liability.

Uses

Shell companies may be formed before commencing business operations in order to obtain financing or investors, or they may remain dormant as the corporate shell left behind after a business has ceased trading or been taken over. A special purpose entity created for a reverse merger is often a shell corporation listed on a stock exchange. Shell companies are also used in tax havens, where lax disclosure rules can conceal the identity of the beneficial owner.

Abuse

Because of their potential for anonymity, shell corporations are a common instrument in money laundering, tax evasion, fraud, and sanctions evasion. The 2016 Panama Papers leak exposed the extensive use of shell companies formed by the law firm Mossack Fonseca on behalf of clients worldwide. Investigations such as the Paradise Papers and reporting on networks of shell firms in the former Soviet Union further documented their role in obscuring illicit financial flows.

Regulation

Many jurisdictions have introduced beneficial-ownership registers and customer due-diligence requirements in response to abuse. The United States Corporate Transparency Act of 2021 requires many companies to report their beneficial owners to FinCEN, while the European Union's anti-money-laundering directives require member states to maintain central registers of beneficial ownership. Securities regulators also police the use of publicly traded shells, requiring extensive disclosures when a shell company acquires an operating business.
