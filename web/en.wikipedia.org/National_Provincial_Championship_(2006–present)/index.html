National Provincial Championship (2006–present)

The National Provincial Championship, known for sponsorship reasons as the Bunnings NPC, is an annual rugby union competition in New Zealand. This article covers the competition from its 2006 restructuring, when the old three-division National Provincial Championship (founded 1976) was replaced by a two-tier professional format.

History

In 2006 the competition was split into the Air New Zealand Cup (later the ITM Cup and Mitre 10 Cup) for the top fourteen provincial unions, and the amateur Heartland Championship for the remaining twelve. In 2011 the top tier was divided into a Premiership of seven teams and a Championship of seven, with promotion and relegation between them. A single-division format with a championship playoff structure was later reintroduced, with all fourteen unions competing for the one title.

Teams

The fourteen unions are Auckland, Bay of Plenty, Canterbury, Counties Manukau, Hawke's Bay, Manawatū, North Harbour, Northland, Otago, Southland, Taranaki, Tasman, Waikato and Wellington. Canterbury has been the most successful union of the modern era, while Tasman — itself a merged union — won its first titles in 2019 and 2020.

Trophies

The competition's most famous prize is the Ranfurly Shield, a challenge trophy defended by the holding union in home matches, contested since 1904 and incorporated into the NPC season.
