Sir James Paul McCartney (born 18 June 1942) is an English singer, songwriter and musician who gained worldwide fame with the Beatles, for whom he played bass guitar and keyboards and shared primary songwriting and lead vocal duties with John Lennon. One of the most successful composers and performers of all time, McCartney is known for his melodic approach to bass-playing, versatile vocal range, and eclectic songwriting, which ranged from pre-rock-and-roll pop to classical, electronica, and musique concrète.

The Beatles

McCartney met John Lennon in 1957 and joined his skiffle group the Quarrymen, which evolved into the Beatles by 1960 with George Harrison and, from 1962, Ringo Starr. The Lennon–McCartney songwriting partnership became the most successful in popular music history. McCartney wrote or co-wrote many of the band's best-known songs, including "Yesterday"—one of the most covered songs ever recorded—"Hey Jude", "Let It Be", "Eleanor Rigby", "Blackbird", and "Helter Skelter". His innovations in the studio, including tape loops and orchestral experimentation, helped redefine the album as an art form on records such as Revolver (1966) and Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band (1967).

After the Beatles broke up in 1970, McCartney released the solo album McCartney and, with his wife Linda McCartney, formed the band Wings in 1971. Wings scored international hits including "Band on the Run", "Live and Let Die", "Silly Love Songs", and "Mull of Kintyre", and toured worldwide until their dissolution in 1981.

Solo career and later work

McCartney's solo career continued with albums such as Tug of War (1982), Flowers in the Dirt (1989), Flaming Pie (1997), Chaos and Creation in the Backyard (2005), and McCartney III (2020), recorded alone during lockdown. He has also composed classical works, including the Liverpool Oratorio (1991) and Ecce Cor Meum (2006), and experimented with electronic music as the Fireman with producer Youth. His concert tours have set attendance and grossing records; he headlined the Glastonbury Festival in 2022 at age 80.

McCartney is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with estimated sales of over 100 million records as a solo artist and more than 600 million with the Beatles. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame twice—as a Beatle in 1988 and as a solo artist in 1999—was knighted in 1997, and received the Kennedy Center Honors in 2010. A committed vegetarian and animal-rights campaigner since the 1970s, he co-founded the Meat Free Monday campaign.
