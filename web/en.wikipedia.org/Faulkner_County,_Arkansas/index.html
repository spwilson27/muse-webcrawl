Faulkner County, Arkansas

Faulkner County is a county in the central part of Arkansas, United States. Its county seat and largest city is Conway. The population was about 123,000 at the 2020 census, making it one of the fastest-growing counties in the state as part of the Little Rock metropolitan area.

The county was formed in 1873 and named for Confederate Colonel Archibald S. Faulkner. It contains the University of Central Arkansas and Hendrix College, both in Conway.
