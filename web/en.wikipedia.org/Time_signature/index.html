Time signature

A time signature (also known as metre signature, meter signature or measure signature) is an indication in musical notation used to specify how many note values called beats constitute one bar (measure), and which note value is equivalent to a beat. It appears at the beginning of a staff, typically as two numbers stacked vertically, after the clef and key signature.

Simple and compound time

In simple time signatures such as 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4, each beat divides into two. In compound time such as 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8, each beat is a dotted note dividing into three. 4/4 is so common it is called common time, sometimes abbreviated with a C symbol; 2/2 is "half time" or alla breve.

Complex metres

Composers also use asymmetrical or irregular signatures such as 5/4, 7/8 or mixed metres that change from bar to bar, common in folk music of the Balkans, progressive rock and contemporary classical music.
