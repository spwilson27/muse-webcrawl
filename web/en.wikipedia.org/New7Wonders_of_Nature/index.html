New 7 Wonders of Nature

Iguazu Falls on the Argentina–Brazil border, one of the seven winners.

New 7 Wonders of Nature (2007–2011) was an initiative started in 2007 to create a list of seven natural wonders chosen by people through a global vote. It was led by Canadian-Swiss Bernard Weber and organized by the New7Wonders Foundation, the same organization behind the New7Wonders of the World campaign. Voting took place by internet and telephone from an initial field of more than 440 locations, narrowed through stages to 28 finalists.

The seven winners

The provisional results were announced on 11 November 2011 (11/11/11). The seven winners were: the Amazon Rainforest and River (South America), Ha Long Bay (Vietnam), Iguazu Falls (Argentina/Brazil), Jeju Island (South Korea), Komodo (Indonesia), the Puerto Princesa Underground River (Philippines), and Table Mountain (South Africa).

Criticism

Like the earlier campaign, the initiative was criticized for being a commercial popularity contest rather than a scientific or heritage designation: UNESCO distanced itself from the project, some governments mounted expensive national voting campaigns, and critics noted that the voting process could be influenced by organized bloc voting and tourism promotion. Winners nonetheless reported substantial increases in tourism.
