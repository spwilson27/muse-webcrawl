Blood gas tension

Blood gas tension
Quantities: PaO2, PaCO2
Normal PaO2: 75–100 mmHg
Normal PaCO2: 35–45 mmHg

Blood gas tension refers to the partial pressure of gases such as oxygen and carbon dioxide in the blood. Measurement of arterial blood gases (ABG) is a standard clinical test of respiratory and metabolic function.

Oxygen tension

The partial pressure of oxygen in arterial blood (PaO2) normally ranges from about 75 to 100 mmHg at sea level. Low values indicate hypoxemia.

Carbon dioxide tension

PaCO2 normally ranges from 35 to 45 mmHg and is tightly regulated by ventilation; deviations signal respiratory acidosis or alkalosis.
