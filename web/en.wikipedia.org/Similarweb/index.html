Similarweb Ltd. is an Israeli software and data company specialising in web analytics, web traffic measurement and digital market intelligence. Founded in 2007 by Or Offer, the company provides estimates of website and mobile app traffic, audience behaviour and competitive performance used by marketers, investors and researchers. Similarweb is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker SMWB, following its initial public offering in May 2021.

History

Similarweb grew out of a browser add-on that recommended related websites. It launched its analytics platform in the early 2010s, initially offering free traffic estimates that popularised its brand, and expanded into enterprise subscriptions. The company acquired several data and mobile analytics firms, including Tapdog and Quettra, and raised substantial venture funding before listing publicly. Its head office is in Tel Aviv, with offices in the United States, Europe and Asia.

Products and methodology

The platform reports metrics such as total visits, traffic sources, engagement (bounce rate, pages per visit, visit duration), geographic distribution and audience demographics for millions of websites and apps, drawing on a mixture of first-party panel data, data from internet service providers and publishers, and public sources. Researchers and journalists frequently cite its rankings, such as lists of the most visited websites. Critics note that estimates for smaller sites can diverge significantly from directly measured analytics, a limitation the company acknowledges in describing its modelled data.
