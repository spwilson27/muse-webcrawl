Chilkoot Trail

The Chilkoot Trail is a 33-mile (53 km) trail through the Coast Mountains from Dyea, Alaska, to Bennett, British Columbia, following a Tlingit trade route. It became the main route of stampeders to the Klondike gold fields in 1897–98.

Canadian authorities required each stampeder to carry a ton of supplies over the steep Golden Stairs. Today it is a popular multi-day hike protected as a national historic site in both countries.
