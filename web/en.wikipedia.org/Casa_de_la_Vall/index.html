Casa de la Vall, historic seat of the Andorran parliament

Casa de la Vall is a historical house in Andorra la Vella, capital of the Principality of Andorra. Built in 1580 as a manor house and defensive tower for the Busquets family, it was acquired in 1702 by the Consell de la Terra, the forerunner of the General Council, and served as the seat of Andorra's parliament until 2011, when the legislature moved to a new building nearby. It is one of the most emblematic monuments of the country.

History

The house was constructed in 1580 over older foundations, with a square defensive tower and loopholes for firearms. When the Council bought it in 1702, it was adapted to house the country's institutions: the parliament chamber, the Court of Justice (Tribunal de Corts), a prison cell, a kitchen for the councillors, and the Chapel of Sant Ermengol. From 2003 it was restored, and the General Council held its final session there in 2011 before transferring to the new parliament building constructed beside it; the last traditional sessions and state ceremonies are still associated with the house.

Description

The building is of rough stone in the style of Catalan manor houses, with three storeys and a dovecote tower added in the 19th century. Inside, the Sala del Consell preserves 16th and 17th-century frescoes, the original wooden benches of the councillors, and the famous Cabinet of the Seven Keys (Armari de les Set Claus), an archive chest that could only be opened with the seven keys held by the representatives of Andorra's seven parishes; it guarded the nation's most important documents, including the Manual Digest of 1748. In the square outside stands a modern monument commemorating the 1993 constitution.

Museum

Today the Casa de la Vall is open to visitors as a museum, illustrating both aristocratic domestic life in early modern Andorra and the workings of one of Europe's smallest and oldest parliaments — the General Council traces its origins to 1419, making it among the oldest continuously existing legislatures in the world.
