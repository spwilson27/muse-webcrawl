Narra

Narra (Pterocarpus indicus) is a species of flowering tree in the legume family Fabaceae, native to Southeast Asia, northern Australasia and the western Pacific. It is the national tree of the Philippines, where it is prized as a premier timber species.

Description

Narra is a large deciduous tree reaching 30–40 metres in height, with a trunk up to 2 metres in diameter. It bears pinnate leaves and fragrant yellow-orange flowers, followed by flat, disc-shaped seed pods. The heartwood ranges from golden yellow to blood red and is known for its fine grain and durability.

Uses

The wood is used for fine furniture, cabinetry, cart wheels, musical instruments and decorative veneer; the celebrated "amboyna" burl comes from this species. Extracts of the tree have traditional medicinal uses, and a red dye can be obtained from the wood. Because of heavy logging, narra is listed as endangered in parts of its range and its trade is regulated.
