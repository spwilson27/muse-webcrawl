Being Digital

Being Digital is a non-fiction book by Nicholas Negroponte about digital technologies and their future impact. First published in 1995, it grew out of a series of articles Negroponte wrote for Wired magazine and explores how the transformation from atoms to bits would reshape communications, work and everyday life.

AuthorNicholas Negroponte

GenreNon-fiction

Content

Negroponte organises the book around the distinction between atoms and bits: information that had been delivered as physical atoms — books, newspapers, compact discs — would increasingly travel as weightless bits. He predicted the rise of personalised media, in which computers would assemble a daily digest tailored to each reader, and argued that digital networks would give individuals greater control over the media they consume. The book also predicts the growth of intelligent interfaces and wearable computing.

Reception

The book became a bestseller and helped popularise the phrase "being digital". While some predictions proved prescient, critics noted that Negroponte underestimated the social and commercial forces that would shape the Internet.
