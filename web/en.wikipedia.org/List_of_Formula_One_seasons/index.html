List of Formula One seasons

The Formula One World Championship has been contested annually since 1950, when the inaugural season was won by Giuseppe Farina for Alfa Romeo. Each season consists of a series of races, known as Grands Prix, held on purpose-built circuits and closed public roads. Two titles are awarded: the World Drivers' Championship (since 1950) and the World Constructors' Championship (since 1958).
