The LA Galaxy, originally the Los Angeles Galaxy, is an American professional soccer club based in the Greater Los Angeles area. The club competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Western Conference and began play in 1996 as one of the league's ten charter members.

History

The Galaxy have won a record six MLS Cups (2002, 2005, 2011, 2012, 2014, 2024), four Supporters' Shields and two U.S. Open Cups, and won the CONCACAF Champions' Cup in 2000. They play at Dignity Health Sports Park in Carson, one of the first soccer-specific stadiums in MLS, opened in 2003.

Designated Players and profile

In 2007 the club signed David Beckham from Real Madrid, a landmark transfer that created MLS's Designated Player Rule and raised the league's global profile. Later star signings included Robbie Keane, Landon Donovan (the club's iconic homegrown star), Steven Gerrard, Zlatan Ibrahimović and Javier "Chicharito" Hernández. The Galaxy's local rivals are Los Angeles FC, with whom they contest the El Tráfico derby.
