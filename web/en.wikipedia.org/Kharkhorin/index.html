Kharkhorin

Kharkhorin is a town in Övörkhangai Province, Mongolia, near the site of Karakorum, the 13th-century capital of the Mongol Empire founded under Genghis Khan and built up by Ögedei Khan from 1235.

History

Karakorum served as imperial capital until Kublai Khan moved it to Khanbaliq (Beijing). The Erdene Zuu Monastery, founded in 1585, was built partly from the ruined city's stones. Modern Kharkhorin is a small agricultural town at the monastery site.
