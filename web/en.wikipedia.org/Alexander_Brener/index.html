Alexander Brener

Alexander Davidovich Brener (born 1957, in Alma-Ata, Kazakh SSR) is a Russian artist and writer associated with Moscow Actionism, known for provocative performance actions directed at the art market and institutions.

Born1957, Alma-Ata, Kazakh SSR, Soviet Union
Known forPerformance art, Moscow Actionism

Career

Brener emigrated to Israel in 1989 and returned to Russia in the early 1990s, becoming a prominent figure of Moscow Actionism alongside Oleg Kulik and others. His actions often took the form of unauthorized interventions in exhibitions and public spaces.

In 1997 he spray-painted a green dollar sign on Kazimir Malevich's painting Suprematisme (1920–1927) at the Stedelijk Museum in Amsterdam, an act he framed as a protest against the commodification of art. He was convicted and served a prison sentence in the Netherlands; the painting was restored.

Writing

With Barbara Schurz, Brener has co-authored numerous books blending fiction, memoir and art criticism, published largely through small presses in Austria and Russia. Their works are noted for a deliberately raw, anti-institutional style.
