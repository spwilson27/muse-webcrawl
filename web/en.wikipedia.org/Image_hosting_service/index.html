Image hosting service
TypeInternet service
PurposeUploading and sharing digital images online
ExamplesImgur, Flickr, Photobucket, Postimage

An image hosting service allows individuals to upload images to an Internet website. The image host then stores the image on its servers, and shows the individual different types of code to allow others to view that image, such as direct links, HTML embeds, or forum BBCode.

Overview

Image hosts typically provide an upload interface in the browser, and increasingly through mobile applications or API integrations. Hosts differ in whether uploading is free, whether an account is required, what file formats and sizes are accepted, and how long images are retained.

Uses

Such services are widely used for sharing photographs on forums, online marketplaces, blogs, and social media, and for hosting images embedded in auction listings and documentation.

Business models

Free hosts are commonly supported by advertising, while paid tiers may offer larger storage, higher bandwidth, and removal of advertisements.
