Réveillon

A festive réveillon table in France.

A réveillon is a long dinner held in the evening preceding Christmas Day and New Year's Day in France, French-speaking Canada, Belgium and other Francophone communities. The name derives from réveil ("awakening"), because the celebration involves staying awake into the early hours of the morning.

Christmas réveillon

The Christmas Eve réveillon traditionally follows Midnight Mass and features rich festive dishes such as oysters, foie gras, escargots, roast turkey with chestnuts or other poultry, and, for dessert, a bûche de Noël (Yule log). In Quebec, traditional fare includes tourtière (meat pie) and other regional specialities.

New Year's réveillon

The réveillon de la Saint-Sylvestre on 31 December is likewise marked by a festive meal, champagne at midnight, dancing and the exchange of good wishes for the New Year.
