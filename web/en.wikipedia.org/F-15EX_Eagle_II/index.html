Boeing F-15EX Eagle II

The Boeing F-15EX Eagle II is an American all-weather multirole strike fighter derived from the McDonnell Douglas F-15 Eagle. It is a single-seat variant of the F-15QA developed for Qatar, featuring fly-by-wire controls, a digital cockpit, the EPAWSS electronic warfare suite, and the ability to carry up to 12 air-to-air missiles.

F-15EX Eagle II
Manufacturer: Boeing
First flight: 2 February 2021
Introduction: 2024
Primary user: United States Air Force

Development

The US Air Force procured the F-15EX to replace aging F-15C/D Eagles, particularly in Air National Guard air defence units, leveraging a mature production line to deliver capability quickly.

Design

The aircraft has a redesigned wing structure rated for 20,000 flight hours, advanced mission systems, and conformal provisions for large weapons loads, including hypersonic-sized stores on its centreline station.
