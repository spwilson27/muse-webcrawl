Juventus Football Club, colloquially known as Juve, is an Italian professional football club based in Turin, Piedmont. Founded in 1897 by a group of Torinese students, the club has worn its black-and-white striped home kit since 1903 and has played in the top flight of Italian football for most of its history. Juventus is the most successful club in Italian football, with a record 36 Serie A titles.

History

The club was founded as Sport-Club Juventus in 1897 and won its first Italian championship in 1905. The Agnelli family, owners of Fiat, took control in 1923, beginning an association that continues to this day and made Juventus Italy's first professional sporting club. The team won five consecutive league titles in the early 1930s and, under Giovanni Trapattoni in the 1970s and 1980s, claimed European honours including the 1985 European Cup — a final overshadowed by the Heysel Stadium disaster — and became the first club to win all three major UEFA competitions.

Marcello Lippi's side of the 1990s added another European Cup in 1996. In 2006 the club was relegated to Serie B for its involvement in the Calciopoli scandal and stripped of two league titles; it returned to Serie A immediately and, under Antonio Conte and then Massimiliano Allegri, won nine consecutive scudetti from 2011–12 to 2019–20.

Stadium

After decades at the Stadio Comunale and the Stadio delle Alpi, Juventus opened the Juventus Stadium in 2011 on the Delle Alpi site — the first club-owned stadium in modern Italian football.

Honours

Juventus has won a record 36 Serie A titles, a record 15 Coppa Italia trophies, and 9 Supercoppa Italiana titles. Internationally it has won two European Cups/UEFA Champions Leagues (1985, 1996), three UEFA Cups, the European Cup Winners' Cup (1984), two UEFA Super Cups, and two Intercontinental Cups.

Rivalries

The club's principal rivalries are the Derby d'Italia with Inter Milan and the Turin derby with Torino FC.
