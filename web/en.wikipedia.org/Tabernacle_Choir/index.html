Tabernacle Choir

Full name: The Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square
Founded: 1847
Home: Salt Lake City, Utah, US
Members: 360 volunteers
Broadcast: Music & the Spoken Word (since 1929)

The Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square, formerly known as the Mormon Tabernacle Choir, is a 360-member choir of volunteers from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Founded in 1847, it is based at the Salt Lake Tabernacle on Temple Square in Salt Lake City, Utah, and is one of the most famous choirs in the world.

Music & the Spoken Word

The choir's weekly radio and television broadcast, Music & the Spoken Word, began in 1929 and is the longest-running continuous network broadcast in history. The choir performs with the Orchestra at Temple Square and the Bells at Temple Square, and is accompanied by the Tabernacle's great organ.

Recordings and honours

The choir has made hundreds of recordings and won Grammy, Emmy and Peabody awards; its 1959 recording of "Battle Hymn of the Republic" won a Grammy and became a signature piece. It has performed at US presidential inaugurations, world's fairs and on international tours. The name was changed from Mormon Tabernacle Choir to The Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square in 2018.
