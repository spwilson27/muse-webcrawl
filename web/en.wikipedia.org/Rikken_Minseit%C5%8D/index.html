Rikken Minseitō

Rikken Minseitō
Constitutional Democratic Party
Founded: 1 June 1927
Dissolved: 1940
Country: Empire of Japan

The Rikken Minseitō (立憲民政党, Constitutional Democratic Party) was one of the main political parties in pre-war Imperial Japan. Founded in 1927 through the merger of the Kenseikai and the Seiyū Honnōtō, it alternated in power with the rival Rikken Seiyūkai during the era of party cabinets.

History

The party first came to power under Prime Minister Hamaguchi Osachi in 1929, pursuing fiscal austerity and ratifying the London Naval Treaty of 1930 — policies that provoked fierce opposition from the military. After Hamaguchi was shot and later died of his wounds, the party under Wakatsuki Reijirō struggled with the economic fallout of the Great Depression and the Manchurian Incident of 1931, after which party government effectively collapsed.

Dissolution

Through the 1930s the Minseitō remained the largest party in the Diet but was progressively sidelined by military-dominated cabinets. In 1940 it dissolved itself to join the Imperial Rule Assistance Association.
