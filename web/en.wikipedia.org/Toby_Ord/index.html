Toby Ord

Toby David Godfrey Ord (born 18 July 1979) is an Australian philosopher at the University of Oxford. His work focuses on ethics, in particular bioethics, existential risk and effective altruism.

Life and work

Ord studied mathematics and computer science at the University of Melbourne before moving to Oxford, where he completed a doctorate in philosophy. In 2009 he founded Giving What We Can, an organisation whose members pledge to donate at least 10% of their income to effective charities; Ord himself pledged to give away most of his income above a modest living allowance, a commitment that attracted wide public attention.

The Precipice

Ord's book The Precipice: Existential Risk and the Future of Humanity (2020) argues that humanity faces unprecedented risks from natural and engineered pandemics, artificial intelligence and other threats, and estimates a roughly one-in-six chance of existential catastrophe within the next century. He helped found the Future of Humanity Institute's research programme on global priorities and has advised governments and international bodies on AI policy.
