Languages of Switzerland

Switzerland has four national languages: German, French, Italian and Romansh. German, French and Italian are official at the federal level; Romansh is official when communicating with Romansh speakers. Most cantons are officially monolingual, though several are bilingual or trilingual.

Distribution

About 62 percent of residents speak Swiss German dialects as their main language, around 23 percent French, 8 percent Italian, and less than 1 percent Romansh, concentrated in Graubünden.

Language policy

The territoriality principle assigns language rights by region. Immigration has added Portuguese, Albanian, English and other languages to daily life.
