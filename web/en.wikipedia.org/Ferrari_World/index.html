Ferrari World

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi is an indoor amusement park located on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, themed around the Italian car manufacturer Ferrari. It opened on 4 November 2010 and is owned by the Aldar Properties company.

Overview

The park is the first Ferrari-branded theme park in the world and the largest indoor amusement park ever built. Its roof, inspired by the side profile of a Ferrari GT, covers 200,000 square metres.

Rides and attractions

Formula Rossa is the world's fastest roller coaster, reaching 240 km/h (150 mph) in 4.9 seconds. Other attractions include Flying Aces, Fiorano GT Challenge, Speed of Magic, Tyre Change, Viaggio in Italia, and the Formula One simulators. The park also hosts family rides, car exhibitions, and Ferrari factory tours.

Records

At opening, Ferrari World held the record for the largest indoor theme park and the fastest roller coaster (Formula Rossa), the latter still unbroken.
