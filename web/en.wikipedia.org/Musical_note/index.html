Musical note

Notes on a treble clef staff

In music, a note is the pitch and duration of a sound, and also its representation in musical notation. A notehead on a staff indicates a pitch determined by its vertical position and any accidentals, while its shape and stem indicate its rhythmic value relative to other notes.

Note names and pitch

In the Western tradition, the twelve pitches of the chromatic scale are named using the letters A through G, with sharps (♯) and flats (♭) for the intervening pitches. Notes separated by an octave share a name. The A above middle C (A4) is conventionally tuned to 440 Hz.

Note values

Durations form a proportional hierarchy: a whole note (semibreve) divides into two half notes (minims), four quarter notes (crotchets), eight eighth notes (quavers), and so on, with dots and ties used to extend values. Rests denote silences of corresponding durations.
