Hom functor

In mathematics, specifically category theory, Hom functors are functors that take a pair of objects in a category to the set of morphisms between them. For objects A and B, Hom(A, B) denotes the collection of all morphisms from A to B in the category.

Definitions

Fixing the first argument gives the covariant Hom functor Hom(A, –), sending each object X to Hom(A, X) and each morphism to composition. Fixing the second argument gives the contravariant Hom functor Hom(–, B). Together they form a bifunctor to the category of sets when the category is locally small.

Properties

Hom functors preserve limits in the appropriate variable, and representable functors are precisely functors isomorphic to a Hom functor. The Yoneda lemma, a cornerstone of category theory, describes natural transformations out of Hom functors. Hom-sets for modules, groups, and topological spaces underlie related constructions such as group cohomology and Ext functors.
