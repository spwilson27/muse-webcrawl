Puebla de Zaragoza, formally Heroica Puebla de Zaragoza, is the capital and largest city of the state of Puebla in east-central Mexico, and one of the five oldest colonial cities in the country. It lies southeast of Mexico City in a valley surrounded by volcanoes including Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl.

History

Founded in 1531 as Puebla de los Ángeles, the city was a planned colonial settlement on the route between Mexico City and Veracruz. It is famous for the Battle of Puebla on 5 May 1862, when Mexican forces under General Ignacio Zaragoza defeated a French army — commemorated internationally as Cinco de Mayo.

Culture

The historic centre, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is known for its colonial architecture, Talavera pottery, and cuisine, including mole poblano and chiles en nogada.
