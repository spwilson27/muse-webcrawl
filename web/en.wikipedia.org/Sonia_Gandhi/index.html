Sonia Gandhi


Sonia Gandhi, former president of the Indian National Congress

Sonia Gandhi (born Edvige Antonia Albina Maino, 9 December 1946) is an Indian politician of Italian origin. She served as President of the Indian National Congress from 1998 to 2017 and again as interim president from 2019 to 2022, becoming the party's longest-serving president. She is the widow of former Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi and a member of the Nehru–Gandhi family.

Early life and marriage

Born in Lusiana, near Vicenza, Italy, she met Rajiv Gandhi while he was studying in England and she was attending a language school in Cambridge. They married in 1968 and she moved to India, becoming an Indian citizen in 1983. After her husband's assassination in 1991, she declined appeals to enter politics and lived privately for several years.

Political career

Gandhi joined the Congress party in 1997 and became its president in 1998. She was elected to the Lok Sabha from Rae Bareli in 1999. In the 2004 general election the Congress-led United Progressive Alliance (UPA) won a surprise victory; although expected to become prime minister, she declined the post — a decision she attributed to her "inner voice" — and Manmohan Singh became prime minister. She instead chaired the UPA and the National Advisory Council, and was associated with welfare legislation including the rural employment guarantee (MGNREGA) and the Right to Information Act. The UPA won a second term in 2009.

Later years

Gandhi stepped down as Congress president in 2017 in favour of her son Rahul Gandhi, returned as interim president in 2019, and served until Mallikarjun Kharge succeeded her in 2022. In 2024 she entered the Rajya Sabha from Rajasthan.
