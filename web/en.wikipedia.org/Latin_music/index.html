Latin music

Latin music blends Iberian, African, and Indigenous influences

Latin music is a term used by the music industry to describe music in a wide variety of styles from Latin America, Spain, Portugal, and the Latino community in the United States, typically performed in Spanish, Portuguese, or the languages of Ibero-America.

Origins and influences

Latin music developed from the fusion of European (especially Spanish and Portuguese), African, and Indigenous American musical traditions during and after the colonization of the Americas. Characteristic instruments such as the guitar, conga drums, and maracas reflect these blended roots.

Genres

Major genres include salsa, merengue, bachata, cumbia, tango, bolero, mariachi and ranchera, bossa nova and samba from Brazil, Latin jazz, reggaeton, and Latin pop. Since the late 2010s, urban styles such as reggaeton and Latin trap have achieved major global chart success.

Industry

The Latin Grammy Awards, established in 2000, are the industry's principal honors. Streaming has driven rapid growth of Latin music in global markets, with artists such as Bad Bunny becoming the most-streamed musicians in the world.
