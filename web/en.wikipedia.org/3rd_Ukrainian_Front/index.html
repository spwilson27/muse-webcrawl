The 3rd Ukrainian Front was a Front (army group) of the Soviet Red Army during the Second World War. It was formed on 20 October 1943 by renaming the Southwestern Front, and operated on the southern sector of the Eastern Front.

Operations

Under General Rodion Malinovsky the front fought in the Lower Dnieper Offensive and the Dnieper–Carpathian Offensive, liberating much of right-bank Ukraine including Odessa in April 1944. Command passed to Fyodor Tolbukhin in May 1944. In August 1944 the front, together with the 2nd Ukrainian Front, carried out the Jassy–Kishinev Offensive that knocked Romania out of the Axis and destroyed the German 6th Army a second time.

The front then advanced through Bulgaria and Yugoslavia, participating in the Belgrade Offensive alongside Tito's partisans, before fighting in Hungary in the Budapest Offensive and finally in Austria, capturing Vienna in April 1945. The front was disbanded on 15 May 1945, shortly after the end of the war in Europe.
