Fan translation

A fan translation is an unofficial translation of a work — such as a video game, film, television series, comic, or novel — produced by fans rather than by licensed translators.

Forms

Common forms include fansubs (fan-subtitled videos, especially of anime), scanlations (scanned and translated comics, especially manga), and fan translations of video games distributed as patches. Fan translation communities developed on the early internet, supplying works that had no official release in the target language, or correcting perceived deficiencies in official translations.

Legal and cultural status

Fan translations generally infringe copyright, though enforcement varies and some rights holders tolerate or even recruit from fan translation communities. Several works have received official releases only after fan translations demonstrated demand; conversely, licensing announcements often lead fan groups to cease distribution.
