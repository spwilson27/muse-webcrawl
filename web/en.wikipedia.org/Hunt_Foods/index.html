Hunt Foods

Summary

Founded: 1888 (as Hunt Brothers Fruit Packing Co.)

Founders: Joseph and William Hunt

Headquarters: California, United States

Known for: Hunt's canned tomatoes and tomato products

Parent: Conagra Brands (Hunt's brand)

Hunt Foods was an American food processing company best known for the Hunt's brand of canned tomato products. The business began in 1888 when brothers Joseph and William Hunt founded a fruit packing company in Sebastopol, California, canning produce from the surrounding orchards and farms.

Growth

The company expanded into tomato processing, eventually building a major cannery in Fullerton, California, and became one of the largest tomato canners in the United States. The Hunt's brand grew to include tomato sauce, tomato paste, ketchup, canned tomatoes, and later snack puddings and other packaged foods. Advertising slogans and consistent branding made Hunt's one of the most recognised names in American grocery stores.

Corporate changes

In the mid-20th century Hunt Foods merged with other food companies, becoming part of Hunt-Wesson Foods. The enterprise passed through several owners, including Norton Simon Inc. and Beatrice Foods, before the Hunt's brand was acquired by ConAgra (now Conagra Brands) in 1990, which continues to market Hunt's tomato products, Manwich sloppy joe sauce, and related lines.
