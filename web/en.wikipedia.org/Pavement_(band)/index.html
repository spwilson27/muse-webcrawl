Pavement is an American indie rock band formed in Stockton, California, in 1989. The group achieved a significant cult following during the 1990s and became one of the most influential bands of the decade's alternative and lo-fi movements, despite modest commercial success during their initial run.

History

The band was founded by vocalist and guitarist Stephen Malkmus and guitarist Scott Kannberg (performing as Spiral Stairs). Their early EPs, recorded cheaply with drummer and producer Gary Young, established a distinctive ramshackle, low-fidelity sound influenced by bands such as The Fall and R.E.M. Drummer Steve West joined in 1993, with percussionist and vocalist Bob Nastanovich and bassist Mark Ibold completing the classic line-up.

Albums

Their debut studio album, Slanted and Enchanted (1992), is frequently cited among the greatest albums of the 1990s. Crooked Rain, Crooked Rain (1994) produced their best-known single, "Cut Your Hair," and brought wider attention. Subsequent albums included Wowee Zowee (1995), Brighten the Corners (1997) and Terror Twilight (1999), produced by Nigel Godrich. The band broke up in 1999 after a fractious final tour.

Reunions and legacy

Pavement reunited for a world tour in 2010 and again in 2022, playing festival headline shows to large audiences. Their influence is heard across generations of indie rock acts, and Malkmus's oblique, wordplay-laden lyrics and the band's deliberately unpolished aesthetic remain touchstones of the genre.
