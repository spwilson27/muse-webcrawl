Starch

Starch is a polymeric carbohydrate consisting of numerous glucose units joined by glycosidic bonds. Produced by most green plants for energy storage, it is the most common carbohydrate in human diets, abundant in potatoes, maize, rice and wheat.

Structure

Starch consists of two molecules: linear amylose and highly branched amylopectin. Plants store it in granules within plastids. Digestion by amylase enzymes breaks starch into maltose and glucose.

Uses

Beyond food, starch is used in papermaking, adhesives, textiles and bioplastics, and is fermented to ethanol. Corn starch is the most widely produced industrial starch.
