The Nederrijn ("Nether Rhine") is the Dutch part of the Rhine downstream of the bifurcation at the Pannerdens Kanaal near Huissen. It flows westward past Arnhem and other towns to Wijk bij Duurstede, where it continues as the Lek.

Course

The river is about 50 kilometres long. Weirs at Driel, Amerongen and Hagestein regulate its water level and the distribution of Rhine water between the Nederrijn-Lek and the IJssel branches.

History

In September 1944 the Nederrijn at Arnhem was the objective of Operation Market Garden, whose failure to hold the bridge there became famous as "a bridge too far."
