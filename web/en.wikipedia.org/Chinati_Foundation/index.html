Chinati Foundation
Location: Marfa, Texas, United States
Founded: 1986
Founder: Donald Judd
Type: Contemporary art museum

The Chinati Foundation (La Fundación Chinati) is a contemporary art museum in Marfa, Texas, based on the ideas of its founder, artist Donald Judd. It preserves and presents large permanent installations by a select group of artists, set in former military buildings and the surrounding Chihuahuan Desert landscape.

History

Judd began developing the site in 1979 with funding from the Dia Art Foundation, acquiring the former Fort D. A. Russell. After a dispute with Dia, Judd established the independent Chinati Foundation in 1986. The name derives from the nearby Chinati Mountains.

Collection

Permanent installations include Judd's 100 untitled works in mill aluminium and 15 untitled works in concrete, Dan Flavin's untitled (Marfa project) in coloured fluorescent light, and works by John Chamberlain, Roni Horn, Ilya Kabakov, Richard Long and Claes Oldenburg with Coosje van Bruggen.
