Anuradhapura
Country: Sri Lanka
Province: North Central
Founded: c. 4th century BCE
Status: UNESCO World Heritage Site (1982)

Anuradhapura is a major city in north-central Sri Lanka and the capital city of the North Central Province. It was the centre of the Anuradhapura Kingdom for over a millennium.

History

Founded as a city by King Pandukabhaya (c. 377 BCE), Anuradhapura received Buddhism in the 3rd century BCE in Ashoka's era, including the Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi sapling of the Bodhi Tree. Kings such as Dutugamunu, who defeated Elara here, built immense stupas — Ruwanwelisaya, Jetavanaramaya and Abhayagiri — and irrigation tanks, as recorded in the Culavamsa.

Sacred city

After invasions the capital moved in the 11th century, but Anuradhapura remains a living pilgrimage city in the Sinhala Buddhist world and a UNESCO heritage site.
