Mesaieed

Country: Qatar

Municipality: Al Wakrah

Former name: Umm Said

Known for: Mesaieed Industrial Area

Mesaieed (Arabic: مسيعيد), formerly known as Umm Said, is an industrial city in Al Wakrah Municipality in Qatar, located about 50 kilometres south of Doha on the eastern coast of the Qatar Peninsula.

History

The settlement developed after oil was discovered at Dukhan; in 1949 the first oil export terminal in Qatar was built at Umm Said, and the port handled the country's petroleum exports for decades. The town was renamed Mesaieed in 1997.

Industry

The Mesaieed Industrial Area hosts QatarEnergy facilities including refineries, petrochemical and fertilizer plants (such as QAFCO), an aluminium plant, and a major commercial port. It is one of Qatar's principal industrial zones alongside Ras Laffan.

Recreation

South of the city lie the Sealine Beach area and the Inland Sea (Khor Al Adaid), a UNESCO-recognized natural reserve popular for dune excursions and camping.
