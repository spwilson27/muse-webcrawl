Klingons are a fictional humanoid species in the Star Trek franchise.

The Klingons (Klingon: tlhIngan) are a fictional humanoid species in the science-fiction franchise Star Trek. Created by screenwriter Gene L. Coon, they first appeared in the original series episode "Errand of Mercy" (1967) as adversaries of the United Federation of Planets.

Depiction

In the original series, Klingons were depicted as swarthy humanoids with little facial prosthetics. From Star Trek: The Motion Picture (1979) onward, they were redesigned with prominent ridged foreheads, long hair and beards, giving them their now-familiar appearance. Klingon culture is centred on honour, combat and warrior tradition, drawing inspiration from sources including feudal Japan and Viking society.

The Klingon Empire is ruled from the homeworld Qo'noS (Kronos) by a Chancellor and High Council. Klingons became allies of the Federation following the Khitomer Accords depicted in Star Trek VI: The Undiscovered Country (1991), and prominent Klingon characters such as Worf served as protagonists in later series.

Language

The Klingon language (tlhIngan Hol) was developed by linguist Marc Okrand for the films, beginning with a small vocabulary devised by James Doohan. It is a complete constructed language with its own grammar, studied by some enthusiasts, and works such as Shakespeare's plays have been translated into it.
