Canoeing
Activity of paddling a canoe with a single-bladed paddle

Canoeing is an activity which involves paddling a canoe with a single-bladed paddle. In some parts of Europe the term also covers kayaking, while in North America and Britain it refers specifically to open canoes.

Recreation and sport

Recreational canoeing includes camping trips, whitewater runs, and touring on lakes and rivers. Competitive disciplines include canoe sprint and canoe slalom, both Olympic sports, as well as canoe marathon and canoe polo.

History

Canoes were developed by Indigenous peoples of the Americas — the word derives from the Taíno kenu via Spanish canoa — and by Aboriginal Australians and others worldwide. Birchbark and dugout canoes served as essential transport in North America's fur trade.
