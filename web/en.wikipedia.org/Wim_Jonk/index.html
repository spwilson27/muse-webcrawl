Wilhelmus Maria "Wim" Jonk (born 12 October 1966) is a Dutch football coach and former professional player who played as a midfielder. A technically gifted playmaker with a powerful shot, he won trophies with Ajax, Inter Milan and PSV Eindhoven and earned 49 caps for the Netherlands.

Playing career

Jonk began at his hometown club FC Volendam before joining Ajax in 1988, where he won the Eredivisie in 1990 and the UEFA Cup in 1992, scoring a memorable volley in the final against Torino. In 1993 he moved with Dennis Bergkamp to Inter Milan, winning a second UEFA Cup in 1994, in whose final he again scored. Returning to the Netherlands with PSV in 1995, he won the league in 1997 before finishing his career at Sheffield Wednesday in England, retiring in 2001 after persistent injuries. For the Netherlands he played at the 1994 and 1998 World Cups, scoring against Saudi Arabia in 1994 and the Republic of Ireland in the 1998 qualifiers.

Coaching and academy work

After retiring, Jonk became a leading figure in Dutch youth development. Working alongside Johan Cruyff, he helped design and implement the "Cruyff Plan" to reform Ajax's academy, heading the academy from 2011 until a falling-out with the club's board led to his departure in 2015. He later co-founded the Cruyff Football institute with Jordi Cruyff and served as head coach of FC Volendam from 2019 to 2023, winning promotion to the Eredivisie in 2022.
