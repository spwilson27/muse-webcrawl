World of Sport (UK TV series)

World of Sport was a British television sports programme that ran on ITV from 2 January 1965 to 28 September 1985, serving as the network's flagship Saturday afternoon sports show and ITV's answer to the BBC's Grandstand. The programme was produced by London Weekend Television for most of its run and was famously presented by Dickie Davies from 1968 until its end.

Format

The programme ran for much of Saturday afternoon and combined live outside broadcasts with studio links. Regular features included horse racing in the ITV Seven, football previews and results in On the Ball and the classified results service, and coverage of minority sports that rarely appeared elsewhere on British television.

Professional wrestling

World of Sport is especially remembered for its weekly professional wrestling slot, broadcast at 4 pm and watched by millions. It made household names of British wrestlers such as Big Daddy, Giant Haystacks, Mick McManus, Jackie Pallo, and Kendo Nagasaki. The wrestling segment continued briefly after the programme ended before being cancelled in 1988. A revival under the World of Sport Wrestling name aired on ITV in 2018.

Legacy

The programme's theme music and Dickie Davies's catchphrases became fixtures of British popular culture, and its mix of mainstream and minority sport influenced later Saturday sports programming.
