UK City of Culture

Coventry, UK City of Culture 2021.

The UK City of Culture is a designation given to a city (or a town or local area) in the United Kingdom for a period of one calendar year, during which the designated place bids for funding and stages a programme of cultural events. The programme aims to encourage the use of culture and creativity as a catalyst for regeneration, following the model of the European Capital of Culture and the success of Liverpool's 2008 tenure.

Title holders

The first holder was Derry~Londonderry in 2013, followed by Hull (2017), Coventry (2021, with events delayed into 2022 by the COVID-19 pandemic), and Bradford (2025). The competition is administered by the Department for Culture, Media and Sport, and bids have increasingly been invited from towns and regions as well as cities.

Impact

Hull's year was credited with large increases in visitor numbers, investment, and local participation in the arts, and the programme has become a flagship instrument of UK cultural policy and "levelling up" regeneration.
