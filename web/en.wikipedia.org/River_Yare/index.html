River Yare

The River Yare is a river in the English county of Norfolk. It rises south of Dereham and flows eastwards for about 83 km (52 miles), passing through Norwich — where it is joined by the River Wensum — before crossing the Broads and entering the sea at Gorleston-on-Sea near Great Yarmouth, joining the tidal Breydon Water.

Navigation and wildlife

The Yare is navigable from the sea to Norwich, historically an important route for trade into the city. Below Norwich it passes nature reserves of marshland and reedbed, including those of the RSPB at Strumpshaw Fen and Berney Marshes, noted for breeding waders and wintering wildfowl. The Wherryman's Way long-distance path follows the river.
