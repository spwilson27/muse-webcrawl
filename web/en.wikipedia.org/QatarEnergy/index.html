QatarEnergy

LNG facilities at Ras Laffan

QatarEnergy (Arabic: قطر للطاقة), formerly Qatar Petroleum, is the state-owned petroleum company of Qatar. Founded in 1974, it conducts all phases of the oil and gas industry in Qatar, including exploration, production, refining, transport and storage, and it is one of the world's largest producers of liquefied natural gas (LNG).

Operations

The company's core asset is the North Field, the world's largest non-associated natural gas field, shared with Iran (where it is known as South Pars), offshore northeast of the Qatar peninsula. Gas is liquefied at Ras Laffan Industrial City through QatarEnergy's LNG ventures (the former Qatargas and RasGas, consolidated under QatarEnergy LNG), making Qatar one of the top LNG exporters, alongside the United States and Australia. The North Field expansion projects are set to raise Qatar's LNG production capacity from 77 million tonnes per annum to 126 million tonnes by about 2027, and further expansion toward 142 million tonnes has been announced.

History

Qatar Petroleum was established in 1974 as Qatar General Petroleum Corporation and took over the state's concessions during the 1970s. In 2021 it rebranded as QatarEnergy to signal a broader energy remit, including investments in petrochemicals, renewables and international upstream ventures with partners such as ExxonMobil, Shell and TotalEnergies. The company also owns the Golden Pass LNG export terminal project in Texas, United States.
