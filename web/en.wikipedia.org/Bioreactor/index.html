Bioreactor

A bioreactor is a vessel in which a chemical process involving organisms or biochemically active substances derived from them is carried out. Bioreactors are used in the production of pharmaceuticals, food and beverages, biofuels and in wastewater treatment.

Design

A typical stirred-tank bioreactor is a cylindrical vessel fitted with an impeller, baffles, and systems to control temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen and nutrient supply. Processes may be run in batch, fed-batch or continuous mode.

Types

Types include stirred-tank, airlift, packed-bed, fluidised-bed and photobioreactors, the latter used for cultivating algae and photosynthetic organisms. Disposable single-use bioreactors have become common in biopharmaceutical manufacturing.
