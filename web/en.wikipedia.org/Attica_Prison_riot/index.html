Attica Prison riot

Date: 9–13 September 1971
Location: Attica Correctional Facility, New York, United States
Deaths: 43

The Attica Prison riot took place at the maximum-security Attica Correctional Facility in upstate New York from 9 to 13 September 1971. Sparked by demands for better living conditions and political rights, around 1,300 inmates seized control of part of the prison and took 42 staff hostage.

Uprising and negotiations

Prisoners issued a list of demands covering food, medical care, religious freedom and an end to racial discrimination. A committee of outside observers attempted to mediate with Commissioner Russell Oswald and Governor Nelson Rockefeller, who refused to come to Attica or grant amnesty.

Retaking and aftermath

On 13 September state police retook the prison by force. In total 43 people died, including 10 correctional officers and civilian employees and 33 inmates, most killed by law enforcement gunfire during the assault. The riot became a landmark in the history of prisoners' rights and American penal reform debates.
