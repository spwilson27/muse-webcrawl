James Cameron filmography

James Cameron is a Canadian filmmaker known for directing, writing and producing some of the most commercially successful films ever made. His work spans science fiction, action and documentary filmmaking, and he is noted for technical innovation in visual effects, 3D and underwater filming.

Documentaries

Cameron's documentary work reflects his interest in deep-sea exploration and science. It includes Ghosts of the Abyss (2003), filmed at the wreck of the Titanic; Aliens of the Deep (2005); the television documentary Expedition: Bismarck (2002); and Deepsea Challenge 3D (2014), about his solo dive to the bottom of the Mariana Trench in 2012.

Producer and writer credits

As a producer and writer, Cameron's credits include Ram­bo: First Blood Part II (1985, co-writer), Point Break (1991, executive producer), Strange Days (1995, writer and producer), Solaris (2002, producer), Alita: Battle Angel (2019, writer and producer) and Terminator: Dark Fate (2019, producer and story). On television he co-created the series Dark Angel (2000–2002) and produced documentary series for National Geographic.

Box office

Cameron's films have grossed billions of dollars worldwide. Avatar and Avatar: The Way of Water rank among the highest-grossing films of all time, alongside Titanic, making Cameron the only director with three films that each grossed more than $2 billion.
