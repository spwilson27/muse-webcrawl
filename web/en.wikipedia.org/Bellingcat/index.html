Bellingcat

Bellingcat is a Netherlands-based investigative journalism group that specialises in fact-checking and open-source intelligence (OSINT). It was founded by British citizen Eliot Higgins in 2014.

Notable investigations

Bellingcat gained prominence for investigating the downing of Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 over Ukraine, identifying the missile system and personnel involved, and later for work on the Syrian civil war, the poisoning of Sergei and Yulia Skripal, and the poisoning of Alexei Navalny.

Methods

Its researchers use publicly available satellite imagery, social media, geolocation and other open sources, and publish guides to spread OSINT techniques.
