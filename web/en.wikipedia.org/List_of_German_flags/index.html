List of German flags

This is a list of flags used in Germany, both currently and historically, including national flags, state flags, military flags and flags of German states and institutions.

National flags

  • Flag of Germany (1949–present) — a horizontal tricolour of black, red and gold, adopted by the Federal Republic of Germany; also used by East Germany from 1949 to 1959 before the addition of the coat of arms.
  • Flag of East Germany (1959–1990) — the black-red-gold tricolour defaced with the national emblem of the GDR (hammer, compass and wreath of rye).
  • Flag of the Weimar Republic and the early Nazi era (1919–1935) — black-red-gold tricolour; replaced by the swastika flag in 1935.
  • Flag of Nazi Germany (1935–1945) — red flag with a black swastika on a white disc.
  • Flag of the German Empire (1867–1918) — horizontal tricolour of black, white and red, originating with the North German Confederation.
  • Flag of the German Confederation / 1848 revolution — black-red-gold used by the Frankfurt Parliament.

Other flags

The article also covers the federal service flag (Bundestag and authorities' flag with the Bundesschild), naval ensigns, flags of the President, Chancellor and armed forces, flags of the sixteen Länder (such as Bavaria's lozenge flag and Berlin's bear flag), and municipal and historical banners.
