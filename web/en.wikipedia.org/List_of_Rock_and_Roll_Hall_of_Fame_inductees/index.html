List of Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductees

The Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, established in 1983 and located in Cleveland, Ohio, inducts artists, producers and other figures in several categories. Artists become eligible 25 years after the release of their first record. This article lists inductees by year and category.

Other categories

Additional categories include Early Influences (e.g., Robert Johnson, Hank Williams), Non-Performers and the Ahmet Ertegun Award (e.g., Sam Phillips, Berry Gordy Jr.), sidemen and the Award for Musical Excellence, introduced to honour musicians and producers whose work underpins recordings.
