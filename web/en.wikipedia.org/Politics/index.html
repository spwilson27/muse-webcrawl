Politics

Politics is the set of activities associated with making decisions in groups, or other forms of power relations among individuals, such as the distribution of resources or status. The academic study of politics is political science.

Aristotle defined humans as political animals. Politics operates at levels from local government to sovereign states and international organizations, through institutions including constitutions, laws, parties and elections, and through informal power and persuasion.

Approaches

Political science studies power, governance, public policy, comparative systems and international relations, using methods from history, economics, sociology and quantitative analysis.
