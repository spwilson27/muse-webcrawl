List of oldest universities in continuous operation

[Image: The Archiginnasio of Bologna]
The University of Bologna, founded in 1088.

This is a list of the oldest institutions that meet the modern definition of a university and have operated continuously since their founding. The medieval European universities head most such lists, though older centres of learning in the Islamic world, such as al-Qarawiyyin in Fez (founded 859), are recognised by UNESCO and Guinness World Records as the oldest existing educational institution.
