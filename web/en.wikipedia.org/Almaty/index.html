Almaty

Almaty (Kazakh: Алматы), formerly known as Alma-Ata, is the largest city in Kazakhstan, with a population of over two million. It served as the capital of the Kazakh Soviet Socialist Republic and then of independent Kazakhstan from 1929 until 1997, when the capital was moved to Astana. Almaty remains the country's financial, commercial, and cultural centre, set against the northern foothills of the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains in the far southeast of the country.

History

The area has been settled since the Bronze Age and lay on a branch of the Silk Road; the name derives from the Kazakh word for apple (alma), and the region is regarded as the ancestral home of the domestic apple (Malus sieversii grows wild in the surrounding hills). The modern city began as the Russian military fort of Verny in 1854. Renamed Alma-Ata in 1921, it became capital of the Kazakh ASSR and then the Kazakh SSR in 1929, growing rapidly with the arrival of the Turkestan–Siberia Railway and wartime industrial evacuation during the Second World War.

After Kazakhstan's independence in 1991, Almaty was the capital for six more years; the government then moved to Akmola (Astana) in 1997, citing earthquake risk, cramped mountain geography, and the desire to develop the centre of the country. In January 2022, Almaty was the epicentre of violent unrest that began as fuel-price protests and was suppressed with the help of CSTO troops.

Geography and climate

Almaty spreads across an alluvial fan descending from the Alatau range; its upper districts reach the mountains, where the Medeu skating rink and Shymbulak ski resort are popular destinations. The city has a humid continental climate with warm summers and cold, snowy winters, and it is prone to earthquakes (devastating quakes struck in 1887 and 1911) and mudflows, against which the Medeu Dam was built.

Culture and landmarks

Landmarks include the wooden Ascension Cathedral (Zenkov Cathedral) in Panfilov Park, the Green Bazaar, Kok Tobe hill with its television tower, and the Republic Palace. Almaty hosted the 2011 Asian Winter Games jointly with Astana and the 2017 Winter Universiade. Its universities, led by Al-Farabi Kazakh National University, and its stock exchange keep it the intellectual and financial hub of Kazakhstan.
