White House Director of Speechwriting

The White House Director of Speechwriting is a senior aide to the President of the United States who leads the team that drafts presidential speeches, major addresses and public remarks. The office works with policy staff, communications officials and the president to shape language for formal occasions and crises.

Role

The director supervises speechwriters, coordinates research and fact-checking, and helps maintain a consistent presidential voice. Major speeches such as the State of the Union address are produced through repeated drafts involving policy advisers and senior leadership.

History

Although presidents have long relied on assistants for addresses, the modern speechwriting office developed with the professional White House staff in the twentieth century. Holders of the role have often been close advisers as well as writers.
