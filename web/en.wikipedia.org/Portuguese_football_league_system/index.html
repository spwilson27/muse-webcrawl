Portuguese football league system

The Portuguese football league system is a series of interconnected leagues for association football clubs in Portugal, organised by the Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional (LPFP) and the Portuguese Football Federation (FPF). At the top of the pyramid is the Primeira Liga, Portugal's premier professional competition.

Structure

  • Level 1: Primeira Liga (18 clubs)
  • Level 2: Liga Portugal 2 (18 clubs)
  • Level 3: Liga 3 (24 clubs)
  • Level 4: Campeonato de Portugal
  • Level 5 and below: District championships organised by regional associations

Promotion and relegation

Clubs move between divisions by promotion and relegation. The bottom clubs of the Primeira Liga are relegated to Liga Portugal 2, with play-offs determining some places, and the system continues down through Liga 3, the Campeonato de Portugal and the district leagues.

Cups

The main cup competitions are the Taça de Portugal, run by the FPF, and the Taça da Liga, run by the LPFP. The winners of the Taça de Portugal qualify for European competition.
