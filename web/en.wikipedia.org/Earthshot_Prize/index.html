The Earthshot Prize is a global environmental award launched in 2020 by Prince William, Duke of Cambridge (later Prince of Wales), through the Royal Foundation. It awards £1 million annually to each of five winners whose solutions are judged most likely to make significant progress towards repairing the planet by 2030.

Background

The prize was developed over two years before its public launch on 31 December 2019, when Prince William announced the initiative in a short film. It was formally launched in October 2020. The name is a deliberate echo of United States President John F. Kennedy's "Moonshot" challenge of the 1960s, framing environmental repair as an equally ambitious collective endeavour.

Categories

The five "Earthshots" correspond to five goals: Protect and Restore Nature; Clean Our Air; Revive Our Oceans; Build a Waste-Free World; and Fix Our Climate. Fifteen finalists are selected each year, three per category, by an expert advisory panel, and the five winners are chosen by the Earthshot Prize Council, a global team of environmental and philanthropic figures.

Ceremonies

The first awards ceremony was held at Alexandra Palace in London on 17 October 2021. Subsequent ceremonies have taken place in Boston (2022), Singapore (2023), Cape Town (2024) and Rio de Janeiro (2025). Winners have included the government of Costa Rica, recognised for its payments for ecosystem services programme, and organisations working on coral farming, air pollution, ocean plastics and green hydrogen.
