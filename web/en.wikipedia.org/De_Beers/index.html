De Beers Group is a multinational corporation specializing in diamond exploration, mining, retail, and trading. Founded in 1888 by Cecil Rhodes in South Africa, the company for much of the twentieth century controlled the great majority of the world's rough diamond supply through its Central Selling Organisation.

History

Rhodes consolidated competing claims in the Kimberley diamond fields into De Beers Consolidated Mines. Under Ernest Oppenheimer in the twentieth century, De Beers operated a single-channel marketing system that managed prices by controlling supply and stockpiling diamonds. Its 1948 advertising slogan, "A Diamond Is Forever," is considered one of the most successful marketing campaigns in history.

Modern operations

De Beers operates mines in Botswana (in partnership with the government as Debswana), South Africa, Namibia, and Canada. In the 2000s the company paid antitrust settlements in the United States and Europe and shifted from supply control toward branded retail, including De Beers Jewellers and the Forevermark brand. Anglo American holds a majority stake.

Criticism

De Beers has faced criticism over its historical monopoly practices, its role under apartheid, and the issue of conflict ("blood") diamonds, which prompted the Kimberley Process certification scheme in 2003.
