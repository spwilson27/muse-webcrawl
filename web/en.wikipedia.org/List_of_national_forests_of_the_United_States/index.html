List of national forests of the United States

The United States has 154 national forests covering roughly 188 million acres, administered by the U.S. Forest Service, mainly in the West.

  • Tongass National Forest — Alaska, the largest
  • Chugach National Forest — Alaska
  • Humboldt-Toiyabe National Forest — Nevada and California
  • Salmon-Challis National Forest — Idaho
  • Shoshone National Forest — Wyoming, the first
  • Flathead, Lolo and Bitterroot — Montana
  • Superior National Forest — Minnesota, includes the Boundary Waters
  • White Mountain National Forest — New Hampshire and Maine
  • Pisgah and Nantahala — North Carolina
  • Angeles, Los Padres, Sierra and Stanislaus — California

The list is organised by state, with area for each forest.
