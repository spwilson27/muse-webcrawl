Purple Rain (album)

ArtistPrince and the Revolution
Released25 June 1984
LabelWarner Bros.
GenrePop, rock, R&B, psychedelia

Purple Rain is the sixth studio album by the American singer, songwriter, producer, and multi-instrumentalist Prince, released on 25 June 1984 by Warner Bros. Records. Credited to Prince and the Revolution, it is the soundtrack to the 1984 film of the same name and is widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time.

Music and recording

The album blends pop, rock, R&B, and psychedelic influences. Several tracks were recorded live at the Minneapolis venue First Avenue in August 1983 and later overdubbed in the studio. Its nine songs include the singles "When Doves Cry", "Let's Go Crazy", "Purple Rain", "I Would Die 4 U", and "Take Me with U".

Commercial performance and legacy

Purple Rain spent 24 consecutive weeks at number one on the Billboard 200 and has sold over 25 million copies worldwide. "When Doves Cry" was the best-selling single of 1984 in the United States. The album won Grammy Awards and an Academy Award for Best Original Song Score, and it is preserved in the Library of Congress's National Recording Registry.
