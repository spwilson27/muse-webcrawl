Fun (band)

Fun (stylized as fun.) is an American pop rock band based in New York City. The band consists of Jack Antonoff, Andrew Dost, and Nate Ruess.

History

The band formed in 2008 after Ruess's previous band, the Format, disbanded. Fun released its debut album, Aim and Ignite, in 2009 to modest commercial success but positive reviews.

Breakthrough: Some Nights

Their second album, Some Nights (2012), brought international fame. Its lead single "We Are Young", featuring Janelle Monáe, reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and won the Grammy Award for Song of the Year; the band also won Best New Artist at the 2013 Grammy Awards. The follow-up singles "Some Nights" and "Carry On" were also top-ten hits.

Hiatus and solo work

In 2015 the band announced a hiatus, emphasising it was not a breakup. Ruess pursued a solo career, Dost scored films, and Antonoff achieved major success as a songwriter and producer (including with his band Bleachers and collaborations with Taylor Swift, Lorde, and Lana Del Rey).

Discography

Studio albums: Aim and Ignite (2009), Some Nights (2012).
