Super League Grand Final

The Super League Grand Final is the championship-deciding game of Super League, the top tier of the British rugby league system. It has been held annually since 1998, when the league adopted a play-off system to determine its champion, and is staged at Old Trafford in Manchester.

Format

Following the regular season, the top teams enter a play-off series; the two surviving teams meet in the Grand Final. The winners receive the Super League Trophy and, in recent seasons, qualify for the World Club Challenge against the champions of the NRL.

History

The first Grand Final in 1998 saw Wigan Warriors defeat Leeds Rhinos. St Helens and Leeds became the most successful clubs in the event, with St Helens winning a record number of titles including three consecutive victories from 2020 to 2022. The man of the match receives the Harry Sunderland Trophy.

Venue

Old Trafford, home of Manchester United, has hosted every Grand Final except the 2020 edition, which was played behind closed doors at the KCOM Stadium in Hull due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
