International Narcotics Control Board

The International Narcotics Control Board (INCB) is an independent treaty body of the United Nations that monitors the implementation of the international drug control conventions: the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs (1961), the Convention on Psychotropic Substances (1971), and the Convention against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (1988).

Functions

Established in 1968 and based in Vienna, the INCB consists of 13 members elected by the UN Economic and Social Council. It administers a system of estimates for licit requirements of narcotic drugs, monitors precursor chemicals used in illicit manufacture, publishes an annual report, and may call attention to treaty violations, recommending remedial measures or, in extreme cases, an embargo on drug trade with a defaulting state.
