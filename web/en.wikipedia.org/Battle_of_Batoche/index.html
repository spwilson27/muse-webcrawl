Battle of Batoche

The Battle of Batoche was the decisive battle of the North-West Rebellion, fought from 9 to 12 May 1885 between Canadian government forces under Major-General Frederick Middleton and the Métis provisional government of Louis Riel at Batoche, in present-day Saskatchewan.

Outcome

Outnumbered and short of ammunition, the Métis riflemen held entrenched rifle pits for three days before a final Canadian advance overran the position. Riel surrendered on 15 May and was later tried and hanged for treason; the rebellion collapsed, consolidating Canadian control of the prairie west.
