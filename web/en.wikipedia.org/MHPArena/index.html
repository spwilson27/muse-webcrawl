The MHPArena is a football stadium in the Bad Cannstatt district of Stuttgart, Germany, and the home ground of VfB Stuttgart. Before 1993 it was called the Neckarstadion, named after the nearby river Neckar; it later bore the names Gottlieb-Daimler-Stadion and Mercedes-Benz Arena before taking its current name in 2023 under a sponsorship agreement with the management and IT consultancy MHP.

The MHPArena in Stuttgart's NeckarPark sports complex.

History

The stadium was opened in 1933 and has been extensively rebuilt several times. It hosted matches at the 1974 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 1988, and, after conversion into a football-only stadium with the removal of the athletics track, games at the 2006 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 2024. Its distinctive roof, a membrane structure supported by a steel ring, was added in the early 1990s. For the 2006 World Cup it temporarily bore the neutral name FIFA World Cup Stadium Stuttgart.

Other events

Beyond football, the stadium has hosted athletics championships — including the 1986 European Athletics Championships and the 1993 World Athletics Championships — as well as concerts and American football. It forms part of the NeckarPark complex alongside the Porsche Arena and the Hanns-Martin-Schleyer-Halle.
