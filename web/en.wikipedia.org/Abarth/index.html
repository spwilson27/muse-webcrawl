Abarth & C. S.p.A. is an Italian racing- and performance-car maker founded by Austro-Italian Carlo Abarth in 1949. Its logo is a stylized scorpion — Abarth's astrological sign — on a red and yellow shield.

History

Carlo Abarth established the company after the collapse of Cisitalia, making his name with tuning kits, sports exhausts and racing cars based largely on Fiat components. Abarth-built cars achieved more than 7,000 race victories and numerous speed records, many set by Abarth himself. Fiat acquired the company in 1971 and used the name on performance versions of its cars. In 2007 Fiat relaunched Abarth as a standalone brand within the group, centred on hot-hatch versions of the Fiat 500 (the Abarth 500/595/695) and the 124 Spider.

Modern range

Recent models include the petrol Abarth 595 and 695, the roadster-based Abarth 124 Spider, and the brand's move into electrification with the Abarth 500e and 600e performance EVs under Stellantis.
