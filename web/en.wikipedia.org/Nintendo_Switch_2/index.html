Nintendo Switch 2

Manufacturer: Nintendo
Type: Hybrid video game console
Release: June 5, 2025
Predecessor: Nintendo Switch

The Nintendo Switch 2 is a hybrid video game console developed and marketed by Nintendo. The successor to the Nintendo Switch, it was formally revealed in January 2025 and released worldwide on June 5, 2025.

Hardware

Like its predecessor, the Switch 2 can be used as a handheld, on a tabletop, or docked for television play. It features a larger 7.9-inch LCD display with 1080p resolution and HDR support, magnetic Joy-Con 2 controllers that can also function as a computer mouse, 256 GB of internal storage, and a custom Nvidia processor supporting DLSS upscaling and ray tracing. In docked mode it can output up to 4K resolution.

Software and services

The console is backward compatible with most Nintendo Switch games, both physical and digital. New features include GameChat, a built-in voice and video chat service, and GameShare, which lets one copy of a game be shared locally with other systems. Launch titles included Mario Kart World, alongside upgraded "Nintendo Switch 2 Edition" releases of earlier games.

Reception

The Switch 2 became Nintendo's fastest-selling console, selling over 3.5 million units in its first four days and more than 10 million within its first months on sale.
