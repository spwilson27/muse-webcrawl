Übermensch

The Übermensch (German for "Overman" or "Superman") is a concept in the philosophy of Friedrich Nietzsche. In his 1883 book Thus Spoke Zarathustra, Nietzsche has his character Zarathustra posit the Übermensch as a goal for humanity to set for itself.

Interpretation

The Übermensch represents a person who has overcome the morality of the herd, created their own values, and affirmed life in the face of the "death of God". Scholars debate whether the concept is an ideal of self-overcoming and creativity or a more radical figure beyond conventional good and evil.

Misuse

In the 20th century the term was distorted by Nazi ideology into a doctrine of racial superiority, an interpretation at odds with Nietzsche's hostility to nationalism and antisemitism.
