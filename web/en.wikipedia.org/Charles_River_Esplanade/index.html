Charles River Esplanade

The Charles River Esplanade is a state-owned park situated along the southern bank of the Charles River in the Back Bay area of Boston, Massachusetts. The park runs for about three miles from the Charles River Dam and Museum of Science to the Boston University Bridge.

History

The Esplanade was created as part of the Charles River Basin project in the early 20th century. Landscape architect Arthur Shurcliff designed successive improvements in the 1930s, adding lagoons, boathouses and walkways. The Hatch Memorial Shell, an outdoor concert venue opened in 1940, hosts the Boston Pops' Fourth of July concert.

Features

The park contains playgrounds, athletic fields, the Hatch Shell, community boating facilities, and memorials including a statue of Arthur Fiedler. Storrow Drive, a major parkway, runs alongside the park, a feature that has long generated debate about traffic and parkland.
