The Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP) is an independent, non-partisan, non-profit think tank dedicated to shifting the world's focus to peace as a positive, achievable and tangible measure of human well-being and progress. It was founded in 2007 by Australian technology entrepreneur Steve Killelea.

Research

The institute is best known for the annual Global Peace Index, which ranks countries by their level of peacefulness using indicators of internal and external conflict, societal safety and security, and militarisation. IEP also publishes the Global Terrorism Index, the Mexico Peace Index, and research on "Positive Peace" — the attitudes, institutions and structures that sustain peaceful societies.

Impact

Its indices are used by governments, international organisations, academics and the media, and its economic analyses estimate the global economic impact of violence each year.
