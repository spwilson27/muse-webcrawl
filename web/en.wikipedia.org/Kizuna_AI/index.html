Kizuna AI (Japanese: キズナアイ) is a Japanese virtual YouTuber (VTuber) who debuted in 2016 and is credited with coining the term "virtual YouTuber". Portrayed as a cheerful artificial intelligence girl with pink-streaked brown hair and a pink ribbon, her channels A.I.Channel and A.I.Games featured vlogs, gaming videos and music, attracting millions of subscribers and launching the VTuber industry.

Rise and activities

Produced by the Tokyo start-up Activ8, Kizuna AI combined motion-capture performance with an anonymous voice actress (later revealed to be Nozomi Kasuga, who also advised the project). She became a cultural ambassador for Japanese tourism, appeared on national television, released charting singles and held live concerts, including a 2018 birthday live and her 2022 farewell concert "hello, world 2022".

Hiatus and legacy

In February 2022 Kizuna AI went on indefinite hiatus to prepare for technology updates, with the voice actress continuing solo projects. Her success demonstrated a new model of virtual entertainment that agencies such as Hololive and Nijisanji expanded into a global phenomenon, and in 2025 the project announced a return as an AI-driven music artist under the same name.
