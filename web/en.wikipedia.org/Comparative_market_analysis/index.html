Comparative market analysis

A comparative market analysis (CMA) is an estimate of a home's value used by real estate agents to help sellers set listing prices and buyers evaluate offers. It is based on the recent selling prices of comparable properties ("comps") in the same market area, adjusted for differences between the subject property and the comparables.

Method

The agent or analyst selects recently sold properties similar in location, size, age, condition, and features, and adjusts their sale prices upward or downward for differences such as extra bedrooms, garages, lot size, or renovations. The adjusted prices indicate a value range for the subject property. Active listings and expired listings may also be considered as evidence of market conditions, though unsold properties are generally weighted less heavily.

CMA versus appraisal

A CMA is not an appraisal: appraisals are performed by licensed or certified appraisers following standards such as USPAP in the United States and are required for most mortgage lending. A CMA is an informal opinion of value, usually prepared free of charge as part of an agent's marketing or advisory service.
