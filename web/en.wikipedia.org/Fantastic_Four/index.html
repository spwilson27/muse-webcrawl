Fantastic Four

The Fantastic Four is a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, the team debuted in The Fantastic Four #1 (November 1961), ushering in the Marvel Age of Comics.

The four gained superpowers after exposure to cosmic rays in space: Reed Richards (Mister Fantastic) can stretch his body; Sue Storm (Invisible Woman) can become invisible and project force fields; her brother Johnny Storm (Human Torch) can generate fire and fly; and Ben Grimm (the Thing) has superhuman strength and a rock-like body.

History

Unlike earlier superheroes, the Fantastic Four bickered, held grudges, and lacked secret identities. They are based in the Baxter Building in New York City and have faced villains including Doctor Doom, Galactus, and the Skrulls. The team has appeared in films, animated series, and video games.
