Nippon Sheet Glass Co., Ltd. (Japanese: 日本板硝子), known as the NSG Group, is a Japanese glass manufacturing company and one of the world's largest makers of flat glass for buildings and vehicles. Founded in 1918 to produce flat glass in Japan using American technology, the company is headquartered in Tokyo and listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

In 2006 NSG acquired the British glassmaker Pilkington, famous for inventing the float glass process, roughly doubling its size and giving it manufacturing operations in dozens of countries. The combined group supplies architectural glazing, automotive original and replacement glass, and technical glass such as ultra-thin glass and glass fibre.

Business

NSG operates three main businesses: architectural glass (including energy-saving and solar-control glazing), automotive glass, and technical glass used in displays, lenses and battery separators. Its products include Pilkington brand glass, low-emissivity coatings and glass for solar energy applications.
