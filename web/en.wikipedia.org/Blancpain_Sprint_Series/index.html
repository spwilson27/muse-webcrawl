Blancpain Sprint Series

The Blancpain Sprint Series was a sports car racing series organized by the SRO Motorsports Group and sponsored by Swiss watchmaker Blancpain. It was created in 2014 as the successor to the FIA GT Series, and, together with the Blancpain Endurance Series, formed part of the overall Blancpain GT Series championship for GT3-specification cars.

Format

Seasons consisted of rounds at European circuits, each featuring two one-hour races — a qualifying race and a main race — with mandatory pit stops and driver changes. Points from both sprint and endurance events counted toward the combined Blancpain GT Series titles for drivers and teams. Manufacturers represented included Audi, Bentley, BMW, Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Mercedes-AMG, and Porsche.

History

The series ran from 2014 to 2016 under the Blancpain Sprint Series name. In 2017 it was rebranded as the Blancpain GT Series Sprint Cup, and later became the GT World Challenge Europe Sprint Cup after Blancpain's sponsorship ended following the 2019 season.
