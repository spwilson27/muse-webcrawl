Federal Territories of Malaysia

The Federal Territories (Malay: Wilayah Persekutuan) in Malaysia comprise three territories — Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and Labuan — governed directly by the federal government of Malaysia rather than by a state government. Kuala Lumpur is the national capital and largest city; Putrajaya is the administrative capital; Labuan is an offshore financial centre and island off Borneo.

TerritoriesKuala Lumpur, Putrajaya, Labuan
Administered byFederal government of Malaysia
Established1974 (Kuala Lumpur), 1984 (Labuan), 2001 (Putrajaya)

History

Kuala Lumpur was ceded by Selangor to the federation in 1974, becoming the first Federal Territory. Labuan was ceded by Sabah in 1984 and developed as an international offshore financial centre. Putrajaya, built on former plantation land in Selangor, became a Federal Territory in 2001 as the seat of the federal administration.

Administration

The territories are administered by the Ministry of Federal Territories and local authorities such as Kuala Lumpur City Hall (DBKL). Residents vote in parliamentary elections but have no state assembly; local matters are handled by appointed officials and the respective city administrations.
