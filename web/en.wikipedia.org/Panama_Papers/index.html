Panama Papers

The Panama Papers are 11.5 million leaked documents, published beginning on 3 April 2016, detailing the financial and attorney–client information of more than 214,488 offshore entities. They originated from the Panamanian law firm Mossack Fonseca.

Investigation

The files were leaked to the German newspaper Süddeutsche Zeitung and shared with the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists, which coordinated roughly 400 journalists in some 80 countries.

Impact

The revelations implicated numerous heads of state and public officials in offshore tax arrangements, led to resignations — including that of Iceland's prime minister — and prompted investigations and reforms worldwide.
