Freeskiing

Freeskiing, also known as newschool skiing, is a specific type of alpine skiing that involves tricks, jumps and terrain park features such as rails, boxes, jibs and pipes. It developed in the late 1990s as skiers adopted techniques and culture from snowboarding, using twin-tip skis that allow skiing and landing backwards.

Disciplines

Competitive freeskiing includes slopestyle, in which athletes perform tricks over a course of jumps and rails; big air, a single large jump; and halfpipe, performed in a U-shaped ramp. Freeskiing events featured in the X Games from the early 2000s, and ski slopestyle and halfpipe were added to the Winter Olympic programme at Sochi in 2014, with big air following at Beijing 2022.

Culture

Freeskiing is also closely associated with freeride and backcountry skiing, and with a film-driven culture in which professional skiers produce video segments showcasing tricks in parks, urban settings and big-mountain terrain.
