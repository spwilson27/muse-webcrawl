Colin Chapman

Anthony Colin Bruce Chapman CBE (19 May 1928 – 16 December 1982) was an English design engineer, inventor and builder in the automotive industry, and founder of Lotus Cars.

Career

Trained as a structural engineer, Chapman built his first trials car, the Lotus Mark I, in 1948. Team Lotus became one of Formula One's most innovative teams, winning seven Constructors' titles between 1963 and 1978. His innovations included the monocoque chassis, struts as suspension members, ground-effect aerodynamics and active suspension research. His philosophy — "simplify, then add lightness" — prized low weight above all.

Death

Chapman died of a heart attack in 1982, aged 54, shortly before the DeLorean scandal, in which Lotus had been implicated, came to trial.
