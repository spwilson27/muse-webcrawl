Dedham Vale


The River Stour in Dedham Vale

Dedham Vale is a designated Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty (now called a National Landscape) on the Essex–Suffolk border in east England. It comprises the area around the River Stour between Manningtree and Smallbridge Farm, east of Bures, including the village of Dedham in Essex.

Constable Country

The vale is strongly associated with the artist John Constable, who was born at East Bergholt in 1776 and painted the Stour valley throughout his career. The surrounding area is marketed as "Constable Country," and landmarks depicted in his works include Willy Lott's Cottage, Flatford Mill, and Dedham church, whose tower appears in many of his paintings, including The Hay Wain (1821).

Landscape

The valley combines ancient woodland, river-valley floodplain meadows, and historic parkland. Dedham Vale was designated an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty in 1970; the protected area covers about 90 square kilometres, and proposals to extend it have been considered by Natural England.
