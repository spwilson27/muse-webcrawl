Hound

A hound is a type of dog originally bred for hunting, using either a keen sense of smell (scent hounds) or exceptional speed and eyesight (sight hounds). Hound breeds are among the oldest types of dog, depicted in hunting scenes from ancient Egypt onward.

Scent hounds

Scent hounds follow the trail of game primarily by smell. They include the Bloodhound, renowned for mantrailing; the Beagle and Basset Hound, bred to follow small game at a pace a hunter on foot can match; and pack hounds such as the English Foxhound, traditionally used in mounted fox hunting. Many scent hounds have a distinctive baying call that allows hunters to follow them at a distance.

Sight hounds

Sight hounds, or gazehounds, hunt primarily by sight and speed, coursing game across open ground. Breeds include the Greyhound, Saluki, Afghan Hound, Borzoi, Irish Wolfhound, Whippet, and Scottish Deerhound. They are typically long-legged, deep-chested, and built for bursts of speed.

Temperament

As pets, hounds are generally good-natured but can be independent and single-minded when following a scent or sight of prey, and scent hounds are known for their baying. Their hunting instincts and stamina require regular exercise.
