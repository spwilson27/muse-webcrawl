Mekedatu

Mekedatu (Kannada: ಮೇಕೆದಾಟು, "goat's leap") is a location along the Kaveri river in Ramanagara district, Karnataka, India, near the town of Kanakapura. Here the river Kaveri flows through a deep and narrow gorge of rocky terrain; at its narrowest the channel is only a few metres wide. According to legend, a goat being chased by a tiger leapt across the gorge, while the tiger could not follow — hence the name.

Geography

Upstream, the river widens at Sangama, the confluence of the Arkavathi with the Kaveri. From Mekedatu the Kaveri continues downstream toward the Chunchi Falls and onward to Tamil Nadu. The rocky gorge and rapids make the spot scenic but dangerous, and swimming is prohibited after several drowning accidents.

Dam proposal

The Government of Karnataka has proposed a balancing-reservoir and drinking-water project at Mekedatu to supply Bengaluru. The project has been opposed by Tamil Nadu, the downstream state, and has been a subject of litigation and inter-state dispute before the Cauvery Water Disputes authorities and the Supreme Court of India.
