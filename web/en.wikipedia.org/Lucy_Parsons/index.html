Lucy Parsons

Bornc. 1851, Texas, United States
Died7 March 1942, Chicago, Illinois
OccupationLabor organizer, anarchist writer

Lucy Eldine Gonzalez Parsons (c. 1851 – 1942) was an American labor organizer, radical journalist and anarchist. She was married to Albert Parsons, one of the men executed after the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago, and campaigned for decades in his memory and for labor causes.

Activism

Parsons helped found the International Working People's Association, edited the newspaper The Alarm, and was a founding member of the Industrial Workers of the World (IWW) in 1905. A powerful orator, she led demonstrations of the unemployed in Chicago and was repeatedly arrested; Chicago police reportedly regarded her as "more dangerous than a thousand rioters". She remained active into her late eighties, speaking at May Day rallies shortly before her death in a house fire in 1942.
