Rohit Shetty's Cop Universe

The Cop Universe is an Indian media franchise and shared universe of Hindi-language action films created by filmmaker Rohit Shetty. The films centre on heroic police officers in India, and are known for elaborate action sequences, including large-scale car chases and explosions.

Films

The franchise began with Singham (2011), starring Ajay Devgn as Bajirao Singham, an honest inspector in Maharashtra, followed by Singham Returns (2014). The universe expanded with Simmba (2018), starring Ranveer Singh as Sangram "Simmba" Bhalerao, which included a cameo by Singham and introduced Akshay Kumar's character Veer Sooryavanshi in a post-credits scene. Sooryavanshi (2021) brought the three leads together, and Singham Again (2024) continued the shared storyline with an expanded ensemble, drawing parallels to the Ramayana.

Other media

The franchise includes the animated series Singham: The Animated Series and the web series Indian Police Force (2024), which Shetty created for streaming.

Reception

The Cop Universe films have been commercially successful, collectively grossing well over ₹1,500 crore worldwide, though critical reception has been mixed, with praise for action and entertainment value and criticism of plots and characterisation.
