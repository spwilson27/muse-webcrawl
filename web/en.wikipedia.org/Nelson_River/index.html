Nelson River

Nelson River
Location: Manitoba, Canada
Length: 644 km (400 mi)
Mouth: Hudson Bay

The Nelson River is a river of north-central Manitoba, Canada, flowing 644 kilometres from Lake Winnipeg to Hudson Bay. Together with the Saskatchewan River system, its watershed drains a large part of the Canadian Prairies and is among the largest in North America.

History

The river was named by Sir Thomas Button in 1612 in honour of Robert Nelson, a ship's master who died during his expedition's wintering at Port Nelson. The river was a key route in the fur trade, contested by the Hudson's Bay Company and French traders.

Hydroelectric development

The Nelson's great flow and drop have made it central to Manitoba Hydro's hydroelectric development, with major generating stations including Jenpeg, Kettle, Long Spruce, and Limestone, plus the Keeyask project.
