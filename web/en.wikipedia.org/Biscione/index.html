Biscione

The biscione ("big grass snake" in Italian), also known as the vipera, is a heraldic charge depicting a serpent in the act of swallowing or regurgitating a human figure. It is the emblem of the House of Visconti and of the city of Milan.

History

The symbol became associated with the Visconti family, rulers of Milan from the 13th century, and later passed to the Sforza dukes. Various legends explain its origin, including a tale of a Visconti ancestor slaying a child-devouring serpent.

Modern use

The biscione appears in the logos of Alfa Romeo, the football club Inter Milan, and other Milanese institutions.
