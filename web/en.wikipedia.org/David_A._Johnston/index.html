David A. Johnston

David Alexander Johnston (18 December 1949 – 18 May 1980) was an American volcanologist with the United States Geological Survey who was killed in the 1980 eruption of Mount St. Helens in Washington state. He was the first to report the eruption, radioing "Vancouver! Vancouver! This is it!" from the Coldwater II observation post about 10 km north of the volcano moments before being overwhelmed by the lateral blast.

Legacy

Johnston's monitoring work in the weeks before the eruption helped persuade authorities to keep the area closed, saving many lives. The Johnston Ridge Observatory, built on the ridge where he died and named in his honour, opened in 1997 as a visitor and monitoring centre overlooking the volcano.
