List of Formula One circuits

This is a list of circuits that have hosted a Formula One World Championship race since the championship began in 1950. More than seventy circuits have been used, ranging from permanent road courses to temporary street circuits.

Classic circuits

Silverstone (United Kingdom), Monza (Italy), Spa-Francorchamps (Belgium) and Monaco are among the longest-serving venues. Other historic tracks include the Nürburgring (Germany), Zandvoort (Netherlands), Suzuka (Japan) and Interlagos (Brazil).

Modern additions

Recent decades have added purpose-built circuits in Bahrain, China, Singapore (a night street race), Abu Dhabi, and the United States, as well as street circuits in Baku, Jeddah, Miami and Las Vegas.
