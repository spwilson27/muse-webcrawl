Magnetic quantum number

The magnetic quantum number (symbol ml) is one of a set of four quantum numbers that describe the unique quantum state of an electron. The magnetic quantum number distinguishes the orbitals available within a subshell, and defines the projection of the orbital angular momentum along a specified axis.

Values

The magnetic quantum number can have integer values from −l to +l, where l is the azimuthal quantum number. For example, when l = 2 (a d subshell), ml can be −2, −1, 0, +1 or +2, giving five d orbitals.

Effects

In the presence of an external magnetic field, the energy levels of orbitals with different ml values split apart, an effect known as the Zeeman effect. The magnetic quantum number also determines the direction of the orbital angular momentum vector in space.
