Lancia is an Italian automobile manufacturer founded in Turin in 1906 by Vincenzo Lancia. Historically one of the most innovative carmakers, Lancia pioneered the unitary body construction (Lambda, 1922), the production V6 engine (Aurelia), and five-speed gearboxes, and it remains the most successful marque in rallying history.

Motorsport

Lancia won the World Rally Championship manufacturers' title a record ten times, with cars including the Fulvia, the mid-engined Stratos — the first car designed specifically for rallying — the 037, and the Delta Integrale, which took six consecutive titles from 1987 to 1992.

Later years

After financial difficulties, Lancia passed to Fiat in 1969. Models such as the Delta, Thema, and Ypsilon sustained the brand, but from 2015 its range shrank to a single model, the Ypsilon, sold only in Italy. Under Stellantis the marque has been relaunched as a premium electric brand, beginning with a new Ypsilon in 2024 and concept cars evoking its heritage.
