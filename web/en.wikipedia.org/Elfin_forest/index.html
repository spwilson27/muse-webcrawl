Elfin forest

An elfin forest, or dwarf forest, is a rare ecosystem featuring miniature, stunted trees, often found at high elevations in tropical mountain regions enveloped by persistent cloud.

Characteristics

Constant wind, low temperatures, waterlogged acidic soils and near-permanent mist limit tree growth, so mature trees may be only a few metres tall with dense, gnarled canopies. Surfaces are carpeted with mosses, ferns and epiphytes such as bromeliads and orchids.

Distribution

Elfin forests occur in the Luquillo Mountains of Puerto Rico (including El Yunque), on high Andean ridges, in Central America, and in parts of Southeast Asia and East Africa — usually as the uppermost band of a montane cloud forest.

Ecology

These forests shelter specialised species, including tiny frogs such as the coquí, and play an important role in capturing cloud water for watersheds below.
