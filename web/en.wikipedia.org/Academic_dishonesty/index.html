Academic dishonesty
Forms: Cheating, plagiarism, fabrication
Setting: Education
Consequences: Disciplinary action

Academic dishonesty, academic misconduct, or academic fraud is any type of cheating that occurs in relation to a formal academic exercise. It can include plagiarism, fabrication of data, deception, cheating on examinations, and bribery.

Types

Common forms include plagiarism (presenting another's work as one's own), collusion, copying during examinations, contract cheating (having another person complete work), falsification of research data, and unauthorized reuse of one's own previous work.

History

Concerns over academic dishonesty date back centuries; the Chinese imperial examination system imposed severe penalties for cheating. Modern honor codes, such as those at American universities, developed in the 19th and 20th centuries.

Prevention

Institutions use honor codes, plagiarism-detection software, proctoring, and assessment design to deter misconduct. Research suggests that clear expectations and a culture of integrity reduce cheating rates.
