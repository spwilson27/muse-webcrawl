Presidential state car (United States)

The presidential state car of the United States, nicknamed "The Beast" and by the Secret Service code name Cadillac One, is the official state car of the president of the United States. The current model, introduced in 2018, is a heavily armored limousine built by General Motors on a truck chassis in the style of a Cadillac sedan.

Features

The car's specifications are classified, but reported features include armor plating, sealed cabin with an independent oxygen supply against chemical attack, run-flat tires, secure communications equipment, and medical supplies including a supply of the president's blood type. It travels with the president by cargo aircraft on overseas trips.

History

Presidential limousines date to the early 20th century; armored cars were introduced after the 1940s, and the 1961 Lincoln Continental in which John F. Kennedy was assassinated led to major redesigns with permanent roofs and heavier protection.
