Pacific oyster

The Pacific oyster (Magallana gigas, syn. Crassostrea gigas) is an oyster native to the Pacific coast of Asia. It has become the most widely introduced and commercially farmed oyster in the world.

Description

The shell is elongated, rough, and heavily sculptured, varying greatly in form with its environment. It grows quickly and tolerates a wide range of temperatures and salinities.

Aquaculture

Introduced to North America, Europe, Australia, and New Zealand for aquaculture, the Pacific oyster now dominates global oyster production. In several regions it has escaped cultivation and established wild reefs, where it is regarded as an invasive species, notably in the Wadden Sea.
