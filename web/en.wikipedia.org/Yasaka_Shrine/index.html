Yasaka Shrine

LocationGion, Higashiyama, Kyoto, Japan
ReligionShinto
DeitySusanoo-no-Mikoto
FestivalGion Matsuri (July)

Yasaka Shrine (Yasaka-jinja), once called Gion Shrine, is a Shinto shrine in the Gion district of Kyoto, Japan. Situated at the east end of Shijō-dōri, it is best known as the host shrine of the Gion Matsuri, one of Japan's most famous festivals.

History

Tradition dates the shrine's founding to 656, and it became an object of imperial patronage in the Heian period. In 869, during an epidemic, the shrine's mikoshi were paraded through the city to appease the deity Gozu Tennō — the origin of the Gion festival. The present main hall, an Important Cultural Property, dates from a 1654 rebuilding.

Gion Matsuri

Held throughout July, the festival culminates in the Yamaboko Junkō processions of enormous decorated floats on 17 and 24 July. The shrine's stage, hung with hundreds of lanterns, also hosts traditional dance performances, and the shrine is a focal point for New Year and Setsubun celebrations in Kyoto.
