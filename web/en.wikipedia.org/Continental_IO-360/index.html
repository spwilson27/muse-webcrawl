Continental IO-360

The Continental IO-360 is a family of fuel-injected, air-cooled, horizontally opposed six-cylinder aircraft engines manufactured by Continental Motors. First certified in the early 1960s, the series is widely used in general aviation aircraft including the Cessna 172R/S Skyhawk SP, Cirrus SR20, and many Mooney and Piper models.

IO-360

Type: Six-cylinder, air-cooled, horizontally opposed piston engine
Manufacturer: Continental Motors
Displacement: 360 cu in (5.90 L)
Power output: 180–210 hp depending on variant
Fuel system: Fuel injection

Design

The IO-360 designation denotes fuel injection (I) and opposed cylinders (O) with 360 cubic inches of displacement. Variants include the 180 hp IO-360-L2A of the Cessna 172 and the 200 hp IO-360-ES and turbocharged TSIO-360 versions. The engines feature a cast aluminium crankcase, individual steel cylinders with aluminium heads, and a single camshaft-in-block layout.

Variants and applications

The family spawned numerous sub-variants (IO-360-A through -K and the TSIO-360 turbocharged line) tailored for fixed- and constant-speed propellers and aerobatic use. Together with the competing Lycoming IO-360, it powers a large share of the world's light-aircraft fleet.
