The Florida House of Representatives is the lower house of the Florida Legislature, the state legislature of Florida, United States. It is composed of 120 members, each elected from a single-member district of roughly equal population for two-year terms.

Composition and powers

Members must be at least 21 years old, residents of Florida for at least two years, and residents of their district. Since a 1992 constitutional amendment, representatives are limited to four consecutive two-year terms (eight years). The House is presided over by the Speaker, elected by the chamber, and shares lawmaking power with the 40-member Florida Senate; revenue bills originate in the House.

Sessions

The Legislature meets in regular session for 60 days each spring in Tallahassee. Along with the Senate, the House passes the state budget, which the governor may sign subject to a line-item veto.
