Lebus

Lebus (Polish: Lubusz) is a town in the Märkisch-Oderland district of Brandenburg, Germany, on the west bank of the Oder River near the border with Poland.

History

Lebus gave its name to the historic Lubusz Land and was the seat of a medieval bishopric, the Diocese of Lebus, founded in the 12th century. The town and its castle hill were strategically important in contests between Polish and German rulers over the middle Oder. The bishopric later moved to Görlitz, then Fürstenwalde.

Geography

The town lies amid the Oder lowlands and hills, with nature reserves on the slopes known for continental steppe flora. The Oder-Neisse line placed Lebus on the frontier after the Second World War.
