United Nations Security Council Resolution 62

United Nations Security Council Resolution 62, adopted on 16 November 1948, concerned the conflict in Palestine during the 1948 Arab–Israeli War. Acting under its earlier truce resolutions, the Security Council called upon the parties concerned to seek agreement, through negotiations conducted either directly or through the Acting Mediator (Ralph Bunche), with a view to replacing the existing truce with an armistice.

Provisions

The resolution contemplated the establishment of armistice lines and the withdrawal and reduction of armed forces so as to facilitate the transition from the truce to a permanent peace in Palestine. It built on Resolution 61, adopted earlier that month, which had established a committee to consider sanctions if any party violated the truce.

Outcome

The resolution helped set in motion the negotiations on Rhodes that produced the 1949 Armistice Agreements between Israel and Egypt, Lebanon, Jordan, and Syria.
