Mugello Circuit

Mugello Circuit (Autodromo Internazionale del Mugello) is a race track in Scarperia e San Piero, Tuscany, Italy. Owned by Ferrari since 1988, it is used for Formula One testing and hosts the Italian motorcycle Grand Prix of MotoGP. The circuit length is 5.245 km (3.259 mi) with 15 turns.

History

Road races were held on public roads around Mugello from the 1920s. The permanent circuit opened in 1974. Historic corners include the uphill Arrabbiata 1 and 2 and the fast Bucine final turn. In 2020 Mugello hosted its first Formula One World Championship race, the Tuscan Grand Prix, won by Lewis Hamilton.
