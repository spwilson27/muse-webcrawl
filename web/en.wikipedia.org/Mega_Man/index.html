Mega Man, known as Rockman in Japan, is a Japanese science-fiction video game franchise created by Capcom, starring a character of the same name. The original game was released for the Nintendo Entertainment System in 1987, and spawned a franchise totalling more than 130 games across numerous systems.

Gameplay

The classic games are side-scrolling action-platformers. Mega Man can run, jump and fire his arm cannon, the Mega Buster. Defeating each level's boss, a Robot Master, grants Mega Man that boss's special weapon, which is strong against another boss in a rock-paper-scissors cycle. Players can typically tackle the stages in any order.

Story

In the year 20XX, the robot engineer Dr. Thomas Light creates intelligent humanoid robots. When his colleague Dr. Albert Wily turns robots to conquest, Light's helper robot Rock volunteers to be converted into the fighting robot Mega Man to stop him.

Spinoffs

The franchise includes several sub-series, including Mega Man X, Mega Man Zero, Mega Man Battle Network and Mega Man Legends, as well as animated series and comics.
