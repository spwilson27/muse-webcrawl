World Consumer Rights Day

World Consumer Rights Day is observed every year on 15 March to raise global awareness about consumer rights and needs. It is coordinated by Consumers International, the federation of consumer organisations, and has been celebrated since 1983.

Date15 March
First observed1983
OrganiserConsumers International

Origins

The date marks the anniversary of U.S. President John F. Kennedy's 1962 address to Congress setting out four basic consumer rights: the right to safety, the right to be informed, the right to choose, and the right to be heard. These were later expanded into the eight consumer rights adopted by Consumers International and reflected in the United Nations Guidelines for Consumer Protection.

Themes

Each year's observance takes a theme, which has covered topics such as tackling plastic pollution, sustainable consumption, and fair digital finance.
