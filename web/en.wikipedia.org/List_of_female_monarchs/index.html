List of female monarchs

This is a list of female monarchs — women who have reigned as queens regnant, empresses regnant, or in equivalent sovereign titles in their own right, as distinct from queens consort, who hold their title through marriage. Throughout history, female succession has been restricted in many monarchies by customs such as Salic law, which barred women from inheriting thrones in parts of Europe, yet numerous women have ruled kingdoms and empires across every inhabited continent.

Female succession today

In recent decades, several European monarchies — including Sweden, the Netherlands, Belgium, Norway, Spain, and the United Kingdom — have adopted absolute primogeniture, under which the eldest child inherits regardless of sex. Japan's imperial succession remains restricted to males, a rule that has periodically prompted public debate given the small size of the imperial family.
