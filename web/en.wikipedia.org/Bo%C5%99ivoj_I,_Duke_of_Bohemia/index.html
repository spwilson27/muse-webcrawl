Bořivoj I, Duke of Bohemia

Bořivoj I (c. 852 – c. 889) was the first historically documented Duke of Bohemia and the progenitor of the Přemyslid dynasty. According to tradition, he was baptised by Saint Methodius, becoming the first Christian ruler of the Bohemians.

Reign

Bořivoj initially ruled from Levý Hradec and later founded Prague Castle, where he built the Church of the Virgin Mary, among the earliest Christian churches in Bohemia. His baptism tied Bohemia to the mission of Saints Cyril and Methodius and to Great Moravia under Svatopluk I.

Family

He married Ludmila, later venerated as Saint Ludmila, and was succeeded by his sons Spytihněv I and Vratislaus I.
