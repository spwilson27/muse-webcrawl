Cocktail

A cocktail is a mixed drink, typically made with a distilled spirit as its base, combined with other ingredients such as liqueurs, fruit juices, syrups, bitters, or mixers, and usually served chilled with a garnish.

History

The word "cocktail" appeared in print in the United States in the early 19th century; an 1806 definition described it as a stimulating liquor composed of spirits, sugar, water, and bitters — the formula now known as the old fashioned. The 19th century saw classics such as the Sazerac, Manhattan, and martini; Prohibition in the 1920s reshaped drinking culture, and a late-20th-century craft cocktail revival restored pre-Prohibition techniques and recipes.

Classic cocktails

Well-known cocktails include the margarita, mojito, daiquiri, negroni, Manhattan, and Bloody Mary. The International Bartenders Association maintains an official list of recognised cocktails.
