Curse of the Billy Goat

The Curse of the Billy Goat was a sports-related curse supposedly placed on the Chicago Cubs Major League Baseball franchise in 1945 by Billy Goat Tavern owner William Sianis, whose pet goat was ejected from Wrigley Field during the World Series. The Cubs did not win a National League pennant for 71 years afterward.

Curse of the Billy Goat
Team: Chicago Cubs
Origin: 1945 World Series
Curse layer: William "Billy Goat" Sianis
Broken: 2 November 2016

Origin

During Game 4 of the 1945 World Series against the Detroit Tigers, Sianis's goat Murphy was asked to leave Wrigley Field because of its smell. Sianis reportedly declared, "Them Cubs, they ain't gonna win no more." The Cubs lost the Series and did not return to the World Series for decades, despite strong teams in 1969, 1984, and 2003 — the last marked by the Steve Bartman incident.

Breaking the curse

Generations of Cubs fans and media embraced the curse as an explanation for the drought, and Sianis's nephew repeatedly attempted ceremonial liftings. The curse was finally laid to rest on 2 November 2016, when the Cubs defeated the Cleveland Indians in Game 7 to win the World Series for the first time in 108 years.
