Research Triangle Park

Research Triangle Park
Location: Durham and Wake counties, North Carolina, U.S.
Founded: 1959
Area: about 7,000 acres
Operator: Research Triangle Foundation

Research Triangle Park (RTP) is one of the largest research parks in the world, located near Durham, Raleigh, and Chapel Hill in North Carolina, United States. It takes its name from the three major research universities that form the "triangle": Duke University, North Carolina State University, and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

History

The park was founded in 1959 by state and local governments, nearby universities, and business interests to attract research-based industry and slow the loss of graduates to other states. Early tenants included IBM and federal facilities of the Environmental Protection Agency and the National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences.

Economy

RTP hosts hundreds of companies and tens of thousands of employees in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, information technology, and environmental research, anchoring the wider Research Triangle region.
