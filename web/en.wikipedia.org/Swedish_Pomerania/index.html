Swedish Pomerania

Swedish Pomerania (Swedish: Svenska Pommern) was a dominion of the Swedish Crown on the southern Baltic coast from 1630 to 1815, comprising parts of Pomerania including Stralsund, Greifswald, and the island of Rügen.

History

Swedish forces occupied Pomerania during the Thirty Years' War, and the Peace of Westphalia (1648) formally awarded western Pomerania to Sweden. The territory gave Sweden a foothold in the Holy Roman Empire and votes in the Imperial Diet. Prussia acquired portions in 1720 after the Great Northern War and the remainder, including Rügen, was ceded at the Congress of Vienna in 1815, briefly passing through Danish hands in exchange for Norway.

Legacy

Greifswald's university, founded in 1456, was Sweden's oldest university for much of the period, and Swedish-era buildings remain prominent in Stralsund and Wismar (the latter a separate Swedish possession).
