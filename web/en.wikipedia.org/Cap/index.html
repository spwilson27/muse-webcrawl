Cap

A cap is a flat headgear, usually with a visor. Caps have crowns that fit very close to the head and typically have a brim at the front. They first appeared as early as 3200 BC and evolved over time into many styles.

Types

Common types include the baseball cap, with its rounded crown and stiff bill; the flat cap, associated with working-class dress in Britain and Ireland; the peaked cap worn as part of military and police uniforms; and close-fitting woollen caps such as the tuque or beanie worn for warmth. Academic, religious and sporting caps (such as the international football "cap", from the practice of awarding a physical cap per appearance) extend the word's uses.

History

Soft caps were worn in the ancient world and the Middle Ages, often indicating status or trade. The modern proliferation of cap styles followed industrial textile production in the 19th and 20th centuries.
