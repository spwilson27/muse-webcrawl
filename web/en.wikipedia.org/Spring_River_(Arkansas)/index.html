Spring River (Arkansas)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


The Spring River near Mammoth Spring

The Spring River is a 57-mile-long (92 km) river in north Arkansas and south Missouri. It originates at Mammoth Spring in Fulton County, Arkansas, one of the largest springs in the United States, discharging on the order of nine million gallons of water per hour at a constant cool temperature, then flows south and southeast through Hardy and other communities before joining the Black River near Black Rock in Lawrence County.

Recreation and ecology

The steady cold flow from Mammoth Spring sustains a year-round trout fishery stocked with rainbow and brown trout, unusual for the region, and the river is popular for canoeing, kayaking, and fishing for smallmouth bass elsewhere along its course. The Spring River's headwaters and the adjacent Mammoth Spring State Park are major local attractions.
