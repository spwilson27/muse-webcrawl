Otto Wallach

Otto Wallach (27 March 1847 – 26 February 1931) was a German chemist and recipient of the 1910 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his work on alicyclic compounds, especially terpenes.

Otto Wallach

Born: 27 March 1847, Königsberg
Died: 26 February 1931, Göttingen
Known for: terpene chemistry, Wallach rearrangement

Work

Working at Bonn under August Kekulé and later at Göttingen, Wallach systematically disentangled the confusing chemistry of essential oils, showing many named 'terpenes' were identical and establishing their structures and interconversions, including the isoprene rule's foundations. The Wallach rearrangement of azoxybenzenes bears his name.
