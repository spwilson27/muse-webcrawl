Coroot

Coroot is a village in County Antrim, Northern Ireland. It is a small rural settlement in the parish of Duneane, near Toome and Randalstown, in the valley of the River Bann. The name is thought to derive from the Irish Corr Út or a related form referring to a round hill.

Geography

Coroot lies in the low-lying countryside west of Lough Neagh, Northern Ireland's largest freshwater lake. The surrounding land is primarily used for agriculture, with small farms and hedgerow-divided fields characteristic of the area.

Local area

The village is part of the Mid and East Antrim local government district. Nearby settlements include Moneyglass, Portglenone and Toome, the latter known for eel fishing on the Lower Bann. The area has a number of small churches and a primary school serving the rural community.
