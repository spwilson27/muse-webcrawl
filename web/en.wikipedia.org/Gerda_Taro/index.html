Gerda Taro

Gerda Taro (born Gerta Pohorylle; 1 August 1910 – 26 July 1937) was a German Jewish war photographer active during the Spanish Civil War. She is regarded as the first female photojournalist to have covered the front lines of a war and to have been killed while doing so.

Life and work

Fleeing Nazi Germany for Paris, she met Endre Friedmann; together they invented the persona "Robert Capa", under whose name they sold their photographs. Taro reported on the Spanish Civil War for French publications, producing iconic images of Republican soldiers and civilians. During the Battle of Brunete in July 1937 she was crushed by a retreating tank and died of her injuries, aged 26. Her long-lost negatives, part of the "Mexican Suitcase", resurfaced in 2007 and led to a major reassessment of her independent authorship.
