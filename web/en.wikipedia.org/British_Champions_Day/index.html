British Champions Day is a thoroughbred horse race meeting held annually in October at Ascot Racecourse in Berkshire, England. Inaugurated in 2011, it is the finale of the British flat racing season and the richest single day in the British racing calendar.

Races

The card includes several Group 1 races, notably the Queen Elizabeth II Stakes, the Champion Stakes, the British Champions Fillies' and Mares' Stakes, the British Champions Sprint Stakes and the British Champions Long Distance Cup. The inaugural meeting is remembered for the unbeaten Frankel's victory in the Queen Elizabeth II Stakes.
