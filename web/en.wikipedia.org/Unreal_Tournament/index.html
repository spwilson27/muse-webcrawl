Unreal Tournament

Unreal Tournament is a first-person shooter video game developed by Epic Games and Digital Extremes, released in 1999. A fast-paced arena shooter focused on multiplayer deathmatch and team modes, it rivalled Quake III Arena.

Gameplay

Modes include Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Domination and Assault, with futuristic weapons such as the Flak Cannon and Shock Rifle.

Development

Built on Unreal Engine, it featured capable bots, extensive mod support and the UnrealEd editor.

Legacy

Sequels included Unreal Tournament 2003, 2004 and 3. The series influenced arena shooters and modding culture.
