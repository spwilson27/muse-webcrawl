Gaston Glock (1929–2023) was an Austrian engineer and businessman who founded the firearms company Glock Ges.m.b.H. His firm first made polymer and steel products such as curtain rods, knives and entrenching tools before entering the pistol market.

Glock pistol

In the early 1980s Glock designed a 9mm semi-automatic pistol for an Austrian Army competition. The Glock 17 used a polymer frame, striker-fired action, high-capacity magazine and few parts. Its success with military and police forces made polymer-framed pistols mainstream.

Company and later life

Glock kept a low public profile while the company expanded internationally, especially in the United States. A 1999 assassination attempt by a business associate left him injured but he survived. Control and ownership of the company were the subject of family and legal disputes in his later years.

Death

Gaston Glock died on 27 December 2023 at the age of 94. His name remains inseparably associated with the pistol brand used by armed forces, police agencies and civilian shooters worldwide.
