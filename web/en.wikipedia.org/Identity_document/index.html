Identity document

An identity document (also called an ID) is any document that may be used to prove a person's identity. If issued in a small, standard credit-card size form, it is usually called an identity card (IC). Some countries issue formal identity documents, while others require informal documents; many require identity cards at certain ages or for certain transactions.

National identity cards

Many countries operate compulsory or voluntary national identity card schemes, in which the card carries the holder's photograph, name, date of birth, and often biometric data. In other countries, passports and driving licences serve as the principal identity documents, and proposals for national cards have sometimes been controversial.

Uses

Identity documents are used for voting, opening bank accounts, travel, age verification, and access to public services. Modern documents incorporate security features such as holograms, chips, and machine-readable zones to resist forgery.
