Comune

A comune is the third-level administrative division of Italy, roughly equivalent to a township or municipality. Italy has several thousand comuni, varying from Rome, with millions of inhabitants, to villages of a few dozen.

Government

Each comune is led by a mayor (sindaco) and an elected council, providing services such as registry offices, local police, waste collection, and primary roads. Many comuni subdivide into frazioni, or hamlets.

History

The institution descends from the self-governing city communes of medieval Italy. Mergers of small comuni have been encouraged to improve efficiency, reducing their total number over recent decades.
