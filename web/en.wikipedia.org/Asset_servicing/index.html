Asset servicing

IndustryFinancial services
ClientsAsset managers, pension funds, insurers, banks
Core servicesCustody, fund accounting, transfer agency, securities lending

Asset servicing refers to the set of administrative and operational services that financial institutions — typically custodian banks and specialist administrators — provide to asset owners and asset managers in support of their investment portfolios.

Services

Core asset servicing activities include global custody (safekeeping of securities and settlement of trades), fund accounting and net asset value (NAV) calculation, transfer agency and investor record-keeping, processing of corporate actions, income collection and tax reclaims, foreign exchange, securities lending, and performance measurement and regulatory reporting.

Industry

The industry is highly concentrated, dominated by large global custodian banks that each safeguard tens of trillions of dollars in client assets. Because services are largely commoditised and scale-driven, providers compete on technology, geographic coverage, and price, and the sector has consolidated steadily. Asset servicing revenues typically combine asset-based custody fees with transaction and ancillary service fees.
