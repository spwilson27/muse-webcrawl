Black Flag is an American punk rock band formed in 1976 in Hermosa Beach, California. Initially called Panic, the band was established by Greg Ginn, the guitarist, primary songwriter, and sole continuous member through multiple personnel changes. They are widely considered to be one of the first hardcore punk bands, as well as one of the pioneers of post-hardcore.

History

Black Flag built a national following through relentless DIY touring and Ginn's label SST Records. Vocalists included Keith Morris, Ron Reyes, Dez Cadena, and, from 1981, Henry Rollins. The 1981 album Damaged is regarded as a landmark of hardcore. Later records such as My War (1984) slowed the tempo and drew on heavy metal and free jazz, alienating some fans while hugely influencing grunge and sludge metal.

Logo and legacy

The band's four-bars logo, designed by Raymond Pettibon, Ginn's brother, became one of the most reproduced images in punk culture. Black Flag's touring network helped create the infrastructure of American independent music. Lineup and legal disputes marked later reunions, including rival touring versions in 2013.
