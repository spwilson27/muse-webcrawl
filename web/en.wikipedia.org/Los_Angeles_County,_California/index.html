Los Angeles County, California

Los Angeles County is the most populous county in the United States, with roughly 10 million residents. Its county seat is the city of Los Angeles.

Geography

The county covers about 4,751 square miles (12,305 km²), from the Pacific coast and Channel Islands to the San Gabriel Mountains and the Mojave Desert. It contains 88 incorporated cities and many unincorporated communities.

Government

An elected five-member Board of Supervisors governs the county — long described as five "little mayors" for the size of each district. A 2024 charter reform will expand the board to nine members and create an elected county executive.

Economy and culture

The county is a global centre of entertainment, trade through the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach, aerospace, and technology. It is among the most ethnically diverse counties in the US.

History

One of California's original 27 counties (1850), it was home for millennia to Tongva and Chumash peoples before Spanish colonisation.
