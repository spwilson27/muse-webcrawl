About the term

"Light verse" denotes poetry written to entertain, characterised by wit, wordplay, regular metre and rhyme, and a playful tone, even when treating serious subjects. Practitioners include W. S. Gilbert, Hilaire Belloc, Ogden Nash, Wendy Cope and John Betjeman. The form encompasses limericks, clerihews, parodies and nonsense verse. Anthologies devoted to the genre, such as those edited by Kingsley Amis, helped preserve its popularity through the twentieth century.
