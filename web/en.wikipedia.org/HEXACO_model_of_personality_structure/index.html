HEXACO model of personality structure

The HEXACO model of personality structure is a six-dimensional model of human personality that was created by psychologists Michael C. Ashton and Kibeom Lee. It extends the famous five-factor (Big Five) model by adding a sixth dimension, Honesty-Humility. The six dimensions are Honesty-Humility, Emotionality, Extraversion, Agreeableness (versus Anger), Conscientiousness, and Openness to Experience.

Development

The model emerged from lexical studies of personality language conducted in several languages, including Dutch, German, Korean, Polish, Italian, and French. In these studies, six factors repeatedly emerged rather than five. The first five resemble the Big Five, but Emotionality and Agreeableness are rotated variants of Big Five Neuroticism and Agreeableness, shifting content such as sentimentality and anger between the factors.

The six dimensions

  • Honesty-Humility: sincerity, fairness, greed avoidance, and modesty. Low scorers tend to be manipulative, greedy, and boastful; high scorers avoid manipulating others and feel little temptation to break rules.
  • Emotionality: fearfulness, anxiety, dependence, and sentimentality.
  • Extraversion: social self-esteem, social boldness, sociability, and liveliness.
  • Agreeableness (versus Anger): forgiveness, gentleness, flexibility, and patience.
  • Conscientiousness: organization, diligence, perfectionism, and prudence.
  • Openness to Experience: aesthetic appreciation, inquisitiveness, creativity, and unconventionality.

Applications and research

Personality is measured with the HEXACO Personality Inventory (HEXACO-PI-R), available in 200-, 100-, and 60-item versions. The Honesty-Humility dimension has proven especially useful in predicting workplace delinquency, unethical behaviour, and the so-called Dark Triad traits; Ashton and Lee argue that the model better explains phenomena such as altruism and antisocial behaviour than five-factor models.
