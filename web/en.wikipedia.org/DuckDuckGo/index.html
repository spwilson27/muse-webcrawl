DuckDuckGo

DuckDuckGo is an American software company whose main product is a search engine that emphasises protecting users' privacy and avoiding personalised search results. Founded by Gabriel Weinberg in 2008 and based in Paoli, Pennsylvania, the company does not track users or store their search histories.

Features

DuckDuckGo results come from its own crawler, DuckDuckBot, and from sources including Bing and Wikipedia. Features include "!Bang" shortcuts that search other sites directly, instant answers, and a browser extension and mobile browser with tracker blocking. In 2022 the company launched a desktop browser, also called DuckDuckGo.

Reception

Usage grew steadily through the 2010s, passing 100 million searches per day in 2021, though its market share remains small compared with Google.
