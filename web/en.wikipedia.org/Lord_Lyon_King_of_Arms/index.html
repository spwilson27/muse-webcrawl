Lord Lyon King of Arms

The Lord Lyon King of Arms is the chief heraldic officer of Scotland and a Great Officer of State. The Lord Lyon heads the Court of the Lord Lyon, which regulates heraldry in Scotland, grants new coats of arms and maintains the Public Register of All Arms and Bearings in Scotland, established in 1672.

Duties

Uniquely among British heraldic officials, the Lord Lyon is also a judge: the Court of the Lord Lyon is a court of law with jurisdiction — civil and criminal — over the misuse of arms, and the Lord Lyon has power to fine offenders and seize goods bearing usurped arms. The office also has ceremonial duties, including proclamations and a role in state occasions, and authorises clan chiefs and the recognition of tartans for official purposes.

History

The office is documented from the 14th century and takes its name from the lion rampant in the royal arms of Scotland. Holders sit at New Register House in Edinburgh and are assisted by heralds and pursuivants of arms, as well as the Lyon Clerk and Keeper of the Records.
