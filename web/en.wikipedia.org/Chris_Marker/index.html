Chris Marker

Chris Marker (1921–2012), born Christian François Bouche-Villeneuve, was a French writer, photographer and documentary filmmaker, a pioneer of the essay film.

Works

His films include La Jetée (1962), a science-fiction meditation composed almost entirely of still photographs that inspired Twelve Monkeys; Sans Soleil (1983), a globe-spanning reflection on memory; and Le Joli Mai and A Grin Without a Cat. Marker, famously reclusive and fond of cats, also made CD-ROMs and installations.

Style

Marker's essayistic voice-over, montage of found images and interest in time, memory and political history influenced generations of filmmakers.
