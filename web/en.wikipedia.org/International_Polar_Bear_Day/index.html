International Polar Bear Day

International Polar Bear Day is an annual event celebrated every 27 February to raise awareness about the conservation of the polar bear and the impact of climate change on Arctic sea ice. It is organised principally by Polar Bears International.

Observance

The date coincides with the period when polar bear mothers and cubs are in their dens. Campaigns encourage actions such as lowering thermostats to reduce carbon emissions, and zoos hold educational events with enrichment activities for their bears.

Background

Polar bears depend on sea ice to hunt seals, and the species is classified as vulnerable, with declining ice seasons the principal threat. The day aims to focus public attention on that link.
