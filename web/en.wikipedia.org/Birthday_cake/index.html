Birthday cake

A birthday cake is a cake served as part of a birthday celebration. Modern birthday cakes are typically decorated with icing and topped with candles, which the guest of honour traditionally extinguishes by blowing them out while making a wish.

History

The tradition is often traced to ancient Greek offerings of round honey cakes to Artemis and to the German Kinderfest of the Middle Ages. The practice became widespread in the 19th century as sugar and refined flour became affordable.

Customs

Cakes are usually accompanied by the song "Happy Birthday to You". The number of candles often corresponds to the age being celebrated.
