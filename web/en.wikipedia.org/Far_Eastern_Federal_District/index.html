The Far Eastern Federal District (Russian: Дальневосточный федеральный округ) is the largest of the eight federal districts of Russia, but the least populated. It covers the Russian Far East, the easternmost territory of Russia and Asia, between Lake Baikal and the Pacific Ocean.

Geography and composition

The district encompasses eleven federal subjects, including the Sakha Republic (Yakutia), Primorsky Krai, Khabarovsk Krai, Kamchatka Krai, Sakhalin Oblast, Amur Oblast, Magadan Oblast, Jewish Autonomous Oblast, Chukotka Autonomous Okrug, and, since 2018, the Republic of Buryatia and Zabaykalsky Krai. Its administrative centre was moved from Khabarovsk to Vladivostok in 2018.

Economy and development

The region is rich in natural resources, including diamonds, gold, timber, fish, oil and natural gas, but suffers from a harsh climate, sparse infrastructure, and long-term population decline. The Russian government has promoted development through programmes such as the Far Eastern Hectare land grant and the annual Eastern Economic Forum in Vladivostok.
