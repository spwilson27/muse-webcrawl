Batgirl is the name of several fictional superheroines appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, depicted as female counterparts and allies of the superhero Batman. An earlier character named Betty Kane appeared as "Bat-Girl" in 1961, but the definitive version, Barbara Gordon, was created in 1967 at the request of the producers of the Batman television series, who wanted a character that would attract a female audience; she was played on screen by Yvonne Craig.

Barbara Gordon

Barbara Gordon, daughter of Gotham City police commissioner James Gordon, holds a doctorate in library science and is a skilled martial artist and computer expert. In the 1988 graphic novel Batman: The Killing Joke, she was shot and paralyzed by the Joker. Rather than retiring, she reinvented herself as Oracle, an information broker and hacker who founded the Birds of Prey team. In DC's 2011 New 52 reboot, Barbara recovered from her injuries and returned to the Batgirl role; her solo series, including the acclaimed "Batgirl of Burnside" era beginning in 2014, emphasized a younger, tech-savvy hero.

Other Batgirls

During Barbara's time as Oracle, the mantle was held by Cassandra Cain (introduced 1999), a near-silent martial artist raised as an assassin, the first Batgirl to headline her own ongoing series, and later by Stephanie Brown (2009–2011), formerly the Spoiler and Robin. In current continuity, Gordon, Cain, and Brown have all operated in Gotham's Bat-family, with Cain and Brown also using the name Orphan and Spoiler respectively.

In other media

Batgirl has appeared in numerous animated series, including Batman: The Animated Series (1992) and The New Batman Adventures, in video games such as the Batman: Arkham series and Gotham Knights (2022). A live-action Batgirl film starring Leslie Grace was filmed for HBO Max but was controversially cancelled by Warner Bros. Discovery in 2022 before release.
