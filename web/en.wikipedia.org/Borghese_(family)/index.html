Borghese (family)

Country: Italy (Siena, Rome)
Founded: 13th century
Notable member: Pope Paul V
Seat: Palazzo Borghese, Rome

The House of Borghese is a princely family of Italian noble and papal background, originating as the Borghesi or Borgese in Siena, where they were prominent in the 13th century. The family's greatness dates from the election of Camillo Borghese as Pope Paul V in 1605.

Rise in Rome

Pope Paul V lavished offices and wealth on his family, especially his nephew Cardinal Scipione Borghese, the great patron of the arts who built the Villa Borghese and assembled the Borghese Collection, commissioning works from Bernini and Caravaggio.

Later history

The Borghese were created Princes of Sulmona and acquired extensive estates around Rome. Pauline Bonaparte, sister of Napoleon, married Prince Camillo Filippo Ludovico Borghese. The family remained prominent in Roman society into the modern era; Junio Valerio Borghese, a naval commander, led a failed far-right coup attempt in Italy in 1970.
