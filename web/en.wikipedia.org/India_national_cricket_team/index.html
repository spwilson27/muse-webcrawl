India national cricket team

The India men's national cricket team, nicknamed the Men in Blue, represents India in international cricket. It is governed by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and is a full member of the International Cricket Council (ICC) with Test, One Day International (ODI), and Twenty20 International (T20I) status.

History

India played its first Test match in 1932 at Lord's against England, captained by C. K. Nayudu, and recorded its first Test victory in 1952. Under Kapil Dev, India won the 1983 Cricket World Cup at Lord's, defeating the West Indies — a triumph that transformed cricket's popularity in India. India won the inaugural ICC World Twenty20 in 2007 and, under M. S. Dhoni, the 2011 Cricket World Cup on home soil and the 2013 Champions Trophy. India has also produced era-defining players including Sunil Gavaskar, Kapil Dev, Sachin Tendulkar — the highest run-scorer in international cricket — Rahul Dravid, Virat Kohli, Jasprit Bumrah, and Rohit Sharma. India won the ICC T20 World Cup again in 2024 and the ICC Champions Trophy in 2025.

Home grounds and support

Major venues include Eden Gardens (Kolkata), Wankhede Stadium (Mumbai), M. A. Chidambaram Stadium (Chennai), and the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, the world's largest cricket stadium. Cricket is by far India's most popular spectator sport, and the BCCI is the wealthiest cricket board, driven by broadcast rights and the Indian Premier League.
