Marriott International

Marriott International, Inc. is an American multinational company that operates, franchises, and licenses lodging brands that include hotel, residential, and timeshare properties. It is headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland. The company was founded by J. Willard Marriott and his wife Alice Marriott.

History

Marriott Corporation was founded when J. Willard Marriott and his wife Alice opened a root beer stand in Washington, D.C., in 1927. The business grew into the Hot Shoppes restaurant chain and later into hotels, with the first hotel opening in 1957 in Arlington, Virginia. In 1993 the company split into Marriott International and Host Marriott Corporation.

Brands and operations

Marriott's portfolio includes luxury brands such as The Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, JW Marriott and W Hotels; premium brands such as Marriott Hotels, Sheraton and Westin; and select-service brands such as Courtyard, Fairfield and Residence Inn. In 2016 Marriott acquired Starwood Hotels and Resorts for about $13.6 billion, making it the world's largest hotel company. Its loyalty programme, Marriott Bonvoy, was launched in 2019.
