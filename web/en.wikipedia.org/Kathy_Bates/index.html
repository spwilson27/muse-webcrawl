Kathy Bates
Born: 28 June 1948, Memphis, Tennessee
Occupation: Actress
Known for: Misery (1990); Fried Green Tomatoes; American Horror Story
Award: Academy Award for Best Actress (1990)

Kathleen Doyle Bates (born 1948) is an American actress known for her powerful portrayals of strong, formidable women in film, television and theatre. She won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her performance as the obsessed fan Annie Wilkes in Misery (1990).

Career

Bates worked extensively on stage before her film breakthrough. Subsequent roles included Fried Green Tomatoes (1991), Dolores Claiborne (1995), Titanic (1997) and About Schmidt (2002). On television she won acclaim in Harry's Law and the anthology series American Horror Story, and in 2024 took the title role in the series Matlock.

Health advocacy

A survivor of ovarian and breast cancer, Bates is a spokesperson for the Lymphatic Education & Research Network, raising awareness of lymphedema. See also Misery.
