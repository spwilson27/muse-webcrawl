The World Tourism Organization (UN Tourism, formerly UNWTO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for the promotion of responsible, sustainable, and universally accessible tourism. Headquartered in Madrid, Spain, it serves as a global forum for tourism policy and a source of tourism statistics and knowledge.

History

The organization traces its origins to the International Union of Official Tourist Propaganda Organizations, founded in The Hague in 1925. After several reorganizations, the World Tourism Organization was formally established in 1975, and in 2003 it became a specialized agency of the United Nations. In 2024 it rebranded as "UN Tourism".

Activities

UN Tourism compiles the World Tourism Barometer, which tracks international tourist arrivals and receipts. It promotes the Global Code of Ethics for Tourism, supports tourism development projects in developing countries, and works on issues such as sustainability, climate action, and tourism's contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals.

Structure

The General Assembly is the supreme organ, meeting every two years. The Executive Council manages the organization's work between assemblies, and the Secretariat, headed by a Secretary-General, is based in Madrid.
