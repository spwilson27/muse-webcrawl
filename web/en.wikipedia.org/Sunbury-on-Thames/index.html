Sunbury-on-Thames

Sunbury-on-Thames, known locally as Sunbury, is a town on the north bank of the River Thames in the Borough of Spelthorne, Surrey, England. It lies about 13 miles (21 km) southwest of central London and is historically part of Middlesex. The town comprises the older Sunbury village near the river, including Thames Street and St Mary's Church, and the larger twentieth-century suburb of Sunbury Cross to the north.

History

Sunbury is recorded in Domesday Book of 1086 as Sunneberie. For centuries a riverside agricultural village on the road from London to Staines, it grew substantially in the interwar period with suburban housing and the arrival of the railway. The Kempton Park Racecourse lies to the northeast of the town, and the Kempton Park Reservoirs and the Queen Mary Reservoir, among the largest in London, are nearby. The Walled Garden in Sunbury Park is a well-known public garden.

Transport

Sunbury railway station, on the Shepperton branch line, provides services to London Waterloo, and the town sits near the M3 motorway, which begins at Sunbury Cross.
