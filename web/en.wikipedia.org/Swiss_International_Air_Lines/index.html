Swiss International Air Lines

Swiss International Air Lines AG, commonly known as SWISS, is the flag carrier of Switzerland, operating scheduled services in Europe and to destinations in North and South America, Asia and Africa from its hub at Zurich Airport and a focus city at Geneva Airport. It is a member of the Star Alliance and a subsidiary of the Lufthansa Group.

History

SWISS was formed in 2002 out of the regional airline Crossair following the collapse of Swissair, Switzerland's former flag carrier, in October 2001. The new airline took over much of Swissair's route network and fleet. After an initial period of heavy losses and restructuring, SWISS was acquired by Lufthansa in stages, becoming fully owned by the German group in 2007, while retaining its own brand, management and Zurich hub.

Fleet and services

The airline operates Airbus A220, A320neo family, A330-300, A340-300 and Boeing 777-300ER aircraft on long-haul routes and has introduced the Airbus A350. SWISS markets a premium product with four classes on long-haul flights and is known for Swiss cuisine and regional products in its catering. Its subsidiaries have included Edelweiss Air, a leisure airline, and Swiss Global Air Lines, which was merged back into the mainline in 2018.

Destinations

SWISS serves more than 100 destinations in over 40 countries. Together with its partners in the Lufthansa Group it offers connections through Zurich, one of Europe's major transfer hubs.
