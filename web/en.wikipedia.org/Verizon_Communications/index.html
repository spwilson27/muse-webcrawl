Verizon Communications

Verizon Communications Inc. is an American multinational telecommunications conglomerate headquartered in New York City. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world and a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average.

Verizon was formed on June 30, 2000, by the merger of Bell Atlantic and GTE, two companies descended from the breakup of AT&T's Bell System in 1984. The name "Verizon" combines the Latin veritas ("truth") and "horizon." Its wireless division, Verizon Wireless, was long a joint venture with Vodafone; Verizon bought out Vodafone's 45% stake in 2014 for about $130 billion, one of the largest corporate deals in history.

Operations

Verizon operates one of the largest mobile networks in the United States, along with the Fios fiber-optic broadband and television service. The company expanded into digital media by acquiring AOL in 2015 and Yahoo's operating business in 2017, combining them into a division first called Oath and later Verizon Media; Verizon sold a majority stake in the media business in 2021. The company also acquired prepaid carrier TracFone in 2021.

Verizon has been a leading developer and deployer of 5G networks in the United States, building both millimeter-wave and mid-band (C-band) services.
