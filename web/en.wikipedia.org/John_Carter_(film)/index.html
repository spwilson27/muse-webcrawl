John Carter is a 2012 American science-fiction action film directed by Andrew Stanton, based on A Princess of Mars (1912), the first book in Edgar Rice Burroughs's Barsoom series. Taylor Kitsch stars as John Carter, a Confederate veteran transported to Mars (Barsoom), where he becomes embroiled in a conflict between the planet's peoples and falls for the princess Dejah Thoris, played by Lynn Collins.

Production and reception

The film marked the live-action debut of Pixar director Stanton (Finding Nemo, WALL-E) and was developed for decades by many filmmakers. Despite a large budget and extensive visual effects, it received mixed reviews and performed poorly at the box office, prompting a major write-down for Disney and becoming one of the most famous box-office bombs in history.

Legacy

Rights to the Burroughs books later reverted to the author's estate. The film has since attracted defenders who regard it as an underrated adventure.
