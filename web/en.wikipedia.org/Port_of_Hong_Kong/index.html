The Port of Hong Kong, centred on the Kwai Tsing Container Terminals in the northwestern New Territories and on Victoria Harbour, is one of the world's major deep-water seaports. For decades it ranked as the busiest container port in the world, a position it held through much of the 1990s and early 2000s.

Facilities

The Kwai Chung–Tsing Yi basin contains nine container terminals with 24 berths, operated by companies including Hongkong International Terminals and COSCO-HIT. The port also includes mid-stream operations, river trade facilities at Tuen Mun, ferry terminals, and the Hong Kong Cruise Terminal at Kai Tak.

Decline and competition

Since the 2000s, Hong Kong has lost share to mainland rivals Shenzhen and Guangzhou, which offer lower costs and direct access to Pearl River Delta factories, though Hong Kong remains a leading transshipment hub and maritime services centre.
