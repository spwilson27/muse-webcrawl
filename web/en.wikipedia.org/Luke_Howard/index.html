Luke Howard

Luke Howard (28 November 1772 – 21 March 1864) was a British manufacturing chemist and amateur meteorologist, known as the "father of meteorology" and the "namer of clouds".

Cloud classification

In a lecture to the Askesian Society in December 1802, Howard presented his classification of cloud "modifications": simple forms — cirrus, stratus and cumulus — plus compound and intermediate combinations such as cirro-stratus and cumulostratus (nimbus). His Latin-based system, praised by Goethe, underlies the modern international cloud classification. Howard also made pioneering studies of London's urban climate, noting the city was warmer than the surrounding countryside.
