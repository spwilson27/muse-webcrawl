Peterson Space Force Base, previously Peterson Air Force Base, Peterson Field and Colorado Springs Army Air Base, is a United States Space Force base in Colorado Springs, Colorado. It shares runways with the adjacent Colorado Springs Municipal Airport. The base has been the headquarters of major North American air and space defence commands for decades.

Peterson Space Force Base, Colorado Springs

The airfield was established in 1942 during the Second World War and named for First Lieutenant Edward J. Peterson, a Colorado native killed in a crash at the field that year. During the Cold War the base hosted Air Defense Command, and later Aerospace Defense Command. It was for many years the home of North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD) headquarters functions and of Air Force Space Command, activated in 1982.

Present day

With the establishment of the United States Space Force in December 2019, the installation was renamed Peterson Space Force Base in July 2021. It hosts Space Base Delta 1, the provisional headquarters elements of U.S. Space Command, and the Peterson Air and Space Museum. Together with nearby Schriever Space Force Base and Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station, it forms the core of the Colorado Springs military space community.
