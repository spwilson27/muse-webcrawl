Tube (fluid conveyance)

Tube
ShapeHollow cylinder
Dimension basisOutside diameter (exact)
MaterialsSteel, copper, aluminium, plastic, glass

A tube, or tubing, is a long hollow cylinder used for moving fluids (liquids or gases) or to protect electrical or optical cables. Tubes are distinguished from pipes in that tube is measured by its exact outside diameter and wall thickness, whereas pipe is designated by a nominal pipe size that only roughly corresponds to its dimensions.

Manufacture and standards

Tubes are produced by extrusion, drawing, or rolling and welding, in materials including steel, stainless steel, copper, aluminium, titanium and plastics. Standards from bodies such as ASTM and ISO specify dimensions, tolerances and mechanical properties for applications from plumbing and heat exchangers to medical devices and bicycle frames.

Applications

Because tube sizes are precise, tubing is preferred where exact dimensions matter, such as hydraulic lines, instrumentation, refrigeration and structural uses. Square and rectangular tubes are also widely used in construction and manufacturing.
