Distributed.net is a worldwide distributed computing project that tries to solve large-scale problems via spare computing resources contributed by volunteers over the Internet. Coordinated by Distributed Computing Technologies, Inc. (DCTI), a non-profit organization founded in 1997, it is one of the earliest and longest-running volunteer computing efforts.

Projects

Distributed.net became famous for its assaults on RSA Laboratories' Secret-Key Challenge contests. Its volunteers found the winning keys for the RC5-56 challenge (1997, in 250 days) and RC5-64 challenge (2002, in 1,757 days), and completed the DES-II challenges. Its long-running projects include the RC5-72 key search and the search for Optimal Golomb Rulers (OGR), in which the project has found the largest known optimal rulers. Participants run the dnetc client on idle machines and are ranked individually and by team, a competitive model that influenced later platforms such as BOINC.
