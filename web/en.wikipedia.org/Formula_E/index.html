Formula E, officially the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship, is an open-wheel single-seater motorsport championship for electric cars. It was conceived in 2011 by FIA president Jean Todt and Spanish businessman Alejandro Agag, who is also the series' founder and former chairman.

History

The first season ran in 2014–15, with races on temporary street circuits in city centres. Nelson Piquet Jr. won the inaugural drivers' championship. Formula E was granted FIA World Championship status for the 2020–21 season.

Race format

Races, called ePrix, last 45 minutes plus one lap. Drivers manage a fixed energy budget; "Attack Mode" grants extra power after driving through an activation zone. Manufacturers include Porsche, Jaguar, Nissan, Mahindra, and Maserati.

Cars

All teams use a standardized Spark chassis and battery; since the Gen3 era (2022–23) cars reach about 350 km/h, with front and rear powertrains enabling regenerative braking of up to 600 kW.
