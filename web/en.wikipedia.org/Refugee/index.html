Refugee

A refugee is a person who has fled their own country because they are at risk of serious human rights violations and persecution there. Under the 1951 Refugee Convention definition, persecution grounds include race, religion, nationality, membership of a particular social group or political opinion.

Distinctions

Refugees cross borders; internally displaced persons remain inside their country; asylum seekers await status determination. The principle of non-refoulement forbids returning refugees to persecution.

Major situations

Large UNHCR-recorded populations originate from Syria, Ukraine, Afghanistan, South Sudan and Sudan.
