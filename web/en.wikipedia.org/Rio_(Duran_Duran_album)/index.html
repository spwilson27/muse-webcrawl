Rio (Duran Duran album)

Rio is the second studio album by the English pop rock band Duran Duran, released on 10 May 1982 through EMI. The album is a quintessential example of the new wave and synth-pop styles that defined early-1980s popular music, and it propelled the band to international stardom.

Background and success

Originally released in the United Kingdom, Rio was remixed by producer David Kershenbaum for the American market, where the band's glamorous music videos — including the clips for "Rio", "Hungry Like the Wolf" and "Save a Prayer" — received heavy rotation on the newly launched MTV. The singles "Hungry Like the Wolf" and "Rio" became major hits, and the album reached the top ten in both the UK and the US, eventually being certified double platinum in the United States.
