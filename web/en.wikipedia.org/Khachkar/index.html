Khachkar

A khachkar (Armenian: խաչքար, "cross-stone") is a carved memorial stele bearing a cross amid rosettes and botanical motifs, characteristic of medieval Armenian Christian art.

History

Khachkars appeared in the 9th century and flourished through the 13th–14th centuries, erected for the salvation of souls, to commemorate victories, or mark church construction. Thousands survive; the craft is inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
