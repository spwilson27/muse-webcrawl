Inner City (band)

Inner City is an American electronic music group formed in Detroit, Michigan, in 1987 by producer and DJ Kevin Saunderson and vocalist Paris Grey. One of the most commercially successful acts to emerge from Detroit techno, Inner City brought the sound of the underground to international pop charts with songs that fused house and techno production with soulful vocals.

Career

Saunderson, one of the "Belleville Three" who pioneered Detroit techno, met singer Paris Grey (born Shanna Jackson) in 1987, and their first single, "Big Fun" (1988), became a major hit in the United Kingdom and across Europe during the acid house boom. It was followed by "Good Life" (1988), which reached number 4 on the UK Singles Chart and became one of the era's defining dance anthems. Their debut album Paradise (1989; released in the US as Big Fun) consolidated their crossover success.

Subsequent albums included Fire (1990) and Praise (1992), with singles such as "Ain't Nobody Better", "Till We Meet Again" and "Pennies from Heaven". Inner City continued to record and tour in later decades, and "Good Life" has been widely sampled, remixed and covered, securing the group's place in dance music history.
