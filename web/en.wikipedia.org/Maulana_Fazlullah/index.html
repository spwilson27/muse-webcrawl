Maulana Fazlullah

Fazal Hayat (1974 – 14 June 2018), more commonly known by his pseudonym Maulana Fazlullah, was a Pakistani Islamist militant who was the leader of the Tehreek-e-Nifaz-e-Shariat-e-Mohammadi and later the second emir of the Pakistani Taliban (Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan).

Rise in Swat

Fazlullah gained prominence in the Swat Valley through broadcasts on an illegal FM radio station, earning the nickname "Mullah Radio". His movement imposed a harsh version of sharia in Swat in 2007–2009, destroying schools and flogging and executing opponents. A military operation in 2009 drove him from Swat into Afghanistan.

TTP leadership and death

In November 2013 Fazlullah was appointed head of the Pakistani Taliban after Hakimullah Mehsud was killed in a US drone strike. Under his leadership the group was blamed for attacks including the 2014 Peshawar school massacre, in which about 150 people, mostly children, were killed; responsibility was claimed by the TTP. Fazlullah was killed in a US-Afghan airstrike in Kunar Province, Afghanistan, in June 2018.
