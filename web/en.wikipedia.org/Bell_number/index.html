Bell number

In combinatorics, the Bell numbers count the possible partitions of a set. They are named after Eric Temple Bell, who wrote about them in the 1930s.

Definition

The nth Bell number Bn is the number of partitions of a set of size n. The sequence begins 1, 1, 2, 5, 15, 52, 203. Bell numbers can be computed by summing Stirling numbers of the second kind.

Properties

They satisfy a recurrence known as Bell's triangle or the Peirce triangle, and their exponential generating function involves eex−1.
