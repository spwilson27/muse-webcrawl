Marine Atlantic Inc. is a Canadian federal Crown corporation operating ferry services between the Island of Newfoundland and mainland Canada. Its routes connect Port aux Basques and Argentia in Newfoundland with North Sydney, Nova Scotia, across the Cabot Strait and Gulf of St. Lawrence.

History

The ferry link fulfils a term of Newfoundland's 1949 union with Canada, which guaranteed a freight and passenger connection; the service was previously run by Canadian National Railway and was transferred to the new corporation Marine Atlantic in 1986. The Port aux Basques route operates year-round, while the longer Argentia crossing is seasonal. The fleet, including the vessels Blue Puttees, Highlanders and Ala'suinu, carries passengers, cars and commercial freight in roll-on/roll-off fashion.
