Sarah Koenig (born 9 July 1969) is an American journalist and podcast host. She is the host and executive producer of the podcast Serial, which she co-founded in 2014. A longtime producer for the radio programme This American Life, Koenig is widely credited with helping to popularise the podcasting medium through Serial's first season, which investigated the 1999 murder of Hae Min Lee and the conviction of Adnan Syed.

Career

Koenig began her career in journalism as a reporter at the Baltimore Sun and the Concord Monitor, covering politics and crime. She moved into radio as a producer for ABC News and then for This American Life from 2004. Her 2013 radio story about a Connecticut honour killing, produced for the programme, drew attention for its narrative approach to difficult subjects.

Serial

Launched in October 2014 as a spin-off of This American Life, Serial was released in weekly episodes and became the fastest podcast to reach five million downloads on iTunes at the time. Its first season won a Peabody Award in 2015 — the first awarded to a podcast — for its innovative telling of a long-form nonfiction story. Koenig has hosted subsequent seasons examining the case of soldier Bowe Bergdahl and the ordinary workings of the criminal justice system in Cleveland, Ohio. In 2020, Serial Productions was acquired by the New York Times Company.
