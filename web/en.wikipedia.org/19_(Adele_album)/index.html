19 is the debut studio album by English singer-songwriter Adele, released on 28 January 2008 by XL Recordings. Named after the age at which she wrote most of its songs, it followed her breakthrough as the first recipient of the Brit Awards Critics' Choice prize. The album is rooted in soul, jazz and folk influences, and its lyrics largely concern a failed relationship, heartbreak and nostalgia.

Singles and songs

The album produced four singles: "Hometown Glory", written when Adele was 16 and re-released as a single in 2008; "Chasing Pavements", her breakthrough hit, which reached number two in the UK and number 21 on the U.S. Billboard Hot 100 and won the Grammy Award for Best Female Pop Vocal Performance; "Cold Shoulder", produced by Mark Ronson; and "Make You Feel My Love", a cover of the Bob Dylan song that became one of the album's most enduring tracks, reaching the UK top five in 2010. Other notable tracks include "Daydreamer", "Best for Last" and "Tired".

Reception and commercial performance

19 debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart and has been certified eight-times platinum in the United Kingdom, with worldwide sales of more than seven million copies. It received generally favourable reviews praising Adele's voice, though some critics found the songwriting uneven. At the 51st Grammy Awards in 2009, Adele won Best New Artist and Best Female Pop Vocal Performance. The album's success, amplified by the phenomenon of her second album 21 (2011), made Adele one of the best-selling music artists of her generation.
