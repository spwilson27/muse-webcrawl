Hakuhō Shō (Japanese: 白鵬 翔, born Mönkhbatyn Davaajargal) is a Mongolian-born former professional sumo wrestler, widely regarded as the greatest of all time. The 69th yokozuna, he won a record 45 top-division (makuuchi) tournament championships, surpassing Taihō's long-standing mark of 32, and recorded 1,187 career wins, also a record.

Career

Hakuhō debuted in 2001 with the Miyagino stable and rose rapidly despite his initially slight build, reaching the top division in 2004 and promotion to yokozuna in 2007. For much of his career he was the sole yokozuna, dominating the sport through technical mastery of throws and pushing techniques. He acquired Japanese citizenship in 2019 and retired in September 2021, afterwards taking the elder name Magaki and later succeeding to the Miyagino stablemaster name.
