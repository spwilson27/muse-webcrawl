The Port of Montreal is an inland port on the St. Lawrence River in Montreal, Quebec, operated by the Montreal Port Authority. Located about 1,600 kilometres from the Atlantic Ocean, it is the largest inland port in the world and the second-busiest container port in Canada.

Operations

The port handles containers, dry and liquid bulk, grain, and general cargo through terminals on the Island of Montreal and at Contrecœur on the south shore. It is a major cruise destination and the principal entry point for container trade with northern Europe serving central Canada and the U.S. Midwest.

History

Montreal's harbour commissions date to the 1830s; the modern port grew with the opening of the St. Lawrence Seaway in 1959. A large expansion at Contrecœur is planned to raise container capacity substantially in the late 2020s.
