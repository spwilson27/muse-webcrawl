Kievan Rus'

Yearsc. 880 – 1240
CapitalKyiv
GovernmentMonarchy (principality federation)
LanguagesOld East Slavic, Old Church Slavonic
ReligionOrthodox Christianity (from 988)

Kievan Rus' was the first East Slavic state and later an amalgam of principalities in Eastern Europe from the late 9th to the mid-13th century. Encompassing a variety of polities and peoples, including East Slavic, Norse, and Finnic, it was ruled by the Rurik dynasty, founded by the Varangian prince Rurik.

Kiev grew rapidly along the Dnieper trade route between the Baltic and the Black Sea. Under Vladimir the Great, the state adopted Orthodox Christianity in 988; under his son Yaroslav the Wise, it reached its cultural and military zenith.

Decline

The state disintegrated amid princely rivalries in the 12th century and collapsed under the Mongol invasions of 1237–1240. It is considered a cultural and political ancestor of Belarus, Russia, and Ukraine.
