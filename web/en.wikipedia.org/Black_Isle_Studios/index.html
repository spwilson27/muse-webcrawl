Black Isle Studios

Founded1996
Defunct2003
ParentInterplay Entertainment
Key peopleFeargus Urquhart
ProductsFallout, Planescape: Torment, Icewind Dale

Black Isle Studios was a division of the video game developer and publisher Interplay Entertainment, founded in 1996 and named after the Black Isle in Scotland, the ancestral home of founder Feargus Urquhart. The studio specialized in role-playing video games and became one of the most acclaimed RPG developers of its era.

Games

Black Isle developed Fallout (1997) and Fallout 2 (1998), published the BioWare-developed Baldur's Gate series, and created Planescape: Torment (1999) and the Icewind Dale series. Its cancelled projects included Van Buren, the original Fallout 3.

Closure and legacy

Interplay closed the studio in December 2003 amid financial difficulties. Many staff formed Obsidian Entertainment, which later developed Fallout: New Vegas (2010). Interplay briefly revived the Black Isle name in 2012.
