Palau Blaugrana

Palau Blaugrana
Location: Les Corts, Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain
Owner/Operator: FC Barcelona
Opened: October 23, 1971
Capacity: 7,585
Tenants: FC Barcelona (basketball, handball, futsal, roller hockey)

Palau Blaugrana (Catalan pronunciation: [pəˈlaw ˌblawˈɣɾanə], meaning "Blue and Garnet Palace") is an arena in Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain, belonging to FC Barcelona. The 7,585-seat arena is located in the Les Corts district, on the club's campus next to Camp Nou. It is the home venue of the FC Barcelona basketball, handball, futsal and roller hockey teams.

Opened on 23 October 1971, the arena was designed by architects Francesc Mitjans and Josep Soteras. Its distinctive reinforced-concrete dome was considered architecturally notable for its time. The Palau has hosted numerous major events, including basketball and handball competitions at the 1992 Summer Olympics and finals of European club competitions in several sports. FC Barcelona's plans for the Espai Barça redevelopment project include a new, larger Palau Blaugrana on the site of the Mini Estadi, after which the original arena is to be demolished.
