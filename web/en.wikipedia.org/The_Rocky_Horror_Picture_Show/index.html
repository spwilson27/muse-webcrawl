The Rocky Horror Picture Show

The Rocky Horror Picture Show is a 1975 musical comedy horror film directed by Jim Sharman, adapted from Richard O'Brien's 1973 stage musical The Rocky Horror Show. Tim Curry stars as Dr. Frank-N-Furter, a self-described "sweet transvestite" scientist, alongside Susan Sarandon and Barry Bostwick as the innocent couple Brad and Janet who stumble into his castle.

Plot and music

On a stormy night, Brad and Janet seek help at a castle where Frank-N-Furter is unveiling his creation, the muscular Rocky. A loving pastiche of B-movie science fiction and horror, the film features songs including "Science Fiction/Double Feature", "The Time Warp", "Sweet Transvestite", and "Touch-a, Touch-a, Touch-a, Touch Me".

Cult following

Initially a commercial failure, the film found a second life as a midnight movie from 1976 onward, developing the most famous audience-participation culture in cinema: fans dress as the characters, shout callbacks, and throw props such as rice, toast, and toilet paper at scripted moments. It holds the record for the longest-running theatrical release in film history and was selected for the US National Film Registry in 2005.
