Fischerspooner

Fischerspooner is an American electroclash duo formed in New York City in 1998. The group consists of Warren Fischer, a classically trained musician and composer, and Casey Spooner, an experimental theatre artist. Known for their elaborate, theatrical live performances that blur the line between concert, art installation and fashion show, Fischerspooner became one of the defining acts of the early-2000s electroclash movement.

History

Fischer and Spooner met while both were students at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago. After relocating to New York, they began performing at venues such as The Limelight, presenting shows that combined electronic music with choreographed dance, costume design and multimedia spectacle. Their debut album, #1 (2001), originally released on International Deejay Gigolo Records, included the underground hits "Emerge" and "The 15th" (a cover of the Wire song). The album was reissued by Capitol Records in 2003.

Their second album, Odyssey (2005), took a more pop-oriented direction and featured collaborations with Linda Perry, David Byrne and Susan Sontag, who contributed a spoken-word performance. The third album, Entertainment, followed in 2009. In 2017 the duo released Sir, produced by Michael Stipe of R.E.M., with Spooner exploring themes of homosexuality and identity. In 2019 Spooner announced the end of Fischerspooner.
