Sand Hill Road

Location: Menlo Park and Palo Alto, California

Runs from: El Camino Real / Santa Cruz Avenue area to Interstate 280

Known for: Concentration of venture capital firms

Landmarks: SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, Stanford University lands

Sand Hill Road is a road in Menlo Park and Palo Alto, California, that has become a metonym for the venture capital industry. Running from the Stanford Shopping Center area westward to Interstate 280, past the SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory and low office parks on Stanford University land, it is home to many of the world’s most prominent venture capital firms, among them Sequoia Capital, Kleiner Perkins, Andreessen Horowitz and Lightspeed Venture Partners.

History and reputation

The concentration began in the 1970s, when venture firms moved into inexpensive offices on Stanford-owned land near the university whose research and graduates supplied their investments. As Silicon Valley grew, “Sand Hill Road” acquired the same symbolic weight in technology finance that “Wall Street” has for banking, and office rents in its office parks repeatedly ranked among the highest in the United States. Deals that shaped the technology industry — early investments in Apple, Cisco, Google and Facebook among them — were negotiated in its nondescript low-rise buildings and at the nearby Rosewood Sand Hill hotel, a favoured meeting place for founders and investors.

Because the road itself is a quiet, tree-lined suburban thoroughfare passing horse country and the SLAC accelerator, commentators often remark on the contrast between its modest appearance and the sums controlled from it.
