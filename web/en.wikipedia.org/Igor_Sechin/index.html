Igor Sechin

Born 7 September 1960, Leningrad
Office CEO of Rosneft (2012– )
Previous Deputy Prime Minister of Russia

Igor Ivanovich Sechin (born 7 September 1960) is a Russian businessman and government official, serving as chief executive of the state oil company Rosneft since 2012. A close associate of Vladimir Putin since their work together in St. Petersburg in the 1990s, he is widely considered one of the most powerful figures in Russia.

Career

Sechin worked in the St. Petersburg mayor's office under Putin, followed him to Moscow, and served as deputy head of the presidential administration from 1999. As Deputy Prime Minister from 2008 to 2012 he oversaw energy policy. As chairman of Rosneft's board from 2004, he presided over the company's absorption of Yukos assets, and as CEO he led the acquisition of TNK-BP in 2013.

Sanctions

Following Russia's actions in Ukraine, Sechin was placed under sanctions by the United States and other Western governments, with further measures after the 2022 invasion.
