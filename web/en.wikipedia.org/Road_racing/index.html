Road racing

A city road race

Road racing is the sport of racing on paved surfaces, and the term is used for several distinct activities: long-distance running races held on roads, cycling road races, and motor racing on temporary circuits formed from closed public roads.

Running

In athletics, road running races are measured over standard distances including the 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon. Major world marathon majors include Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago, New York City and Tokyo. Courses are certified for accuracy, and records set on point-to-point or excessively downhill courses are not eligible as world records.

Cycling

Road bicycle racing is the most popular form of bicycle racing, encompassing one-day classics such as Paris–Roubaix and stage races such as the Tour de France.

Motor racing

Motor road racing takes place on closed public roads. Famous events include the Isle of Man TT motorcycle races and street circuits in Formula One such as Monaco and Singapore. Because run-off areas are limited, road circuits are generally more dangerous than purpose-built tracks.
