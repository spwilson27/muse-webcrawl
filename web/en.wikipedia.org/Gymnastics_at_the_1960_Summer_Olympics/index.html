Gymnastics at the 1960 Summer Olympics was held at the ancient Baths of Caracalla in Rome, Italy. Fourteen events were contested — eight for men and six for women. The Soviet Union once again led the medal table, but Japan announced itself as the rising power in men's gymnastics, beginning a rivalry that would define the following two decades.

Men's events

Soviet veteran Boris Shakhlin won the men's individual all-around and collected seven medals in total (four gold, two silver, one bronze), making him one of the most decorated athletes of the Rome Games. Japan, led by Takashi Ono, won the team all-around gold — its first of five consecutive Olympic team titles — while Ono also won the horizontal bar and vault.

Women's events

Larisa Latynina of the Soviet Union retained her all-around crown, winning three gold medals (all-around, floor exercise and team) plus two silvers and a bronze. The USSR swept the women's team title comfortably, and Eva Bosáková of Czechoslovakia won the balance beam gold.
