Grupo Televisa is a Mexican multinational mass media company headquartered in Mexico City, historically the largest producer of Spanish-language television content in the world and a dominant force in Mexican broadcasting through channels such as Las Estrellas.

History

Televisa was formed in 1973 from the merger of Televicentro and Telesistema Mexicano, under the Azcárraga family, whose involvement in Mexican broadcasting dated to XEW radio in 1930. For decades its telenovelas were exported across Latin America, Europe and Asia, and the company was closely associated with the long-ruling PRI, a relationship that drew persistent criticism.

TelevisaUnivision

In 2022 Televisa combined its content business with the US Spanish-language broadcaster Univision to form TelevisaUnivision, the largest Spanish-language streaming and broadcast content company, while the remaining Grupo Televisa retained cable (Izzi), satellite (Sky) and other assets in Mexico.
