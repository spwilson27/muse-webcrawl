The Škoda 1000 MB is a small family car produced by the Czechoslovak manufacturer AZNP Škoda from 1964 to 1969. The "MB" designation referred to the new plant at Mladá Boleslav built to manufacture it, and the 1000 to its roughly one-litre engine.

Design

The car broke with its predecessors by adopting a rear-engined, rear-wheel-drive layout with a water-cooled 988 cc four-cylinder engine producing about 37–48 hp depending on version. Its all-steel unitary body was notable for using advanced casting technology for the engine block. Over 443,000 examples were built, including the two-door 1000 MBX coupé variant.
