Leah

Leah is a figure in the Hebrew Bible, the first wife of the patriarch Jacob and the elder daughter of Laban. According to Genesis, Jacob worked seven years for Laban intending to marry Leah's younger sister Rachel, but was deceived at the wedding and married Leah instead; he then worked seven more years for Rachel.

Leah is described as tender-eyed, in contrast to the beautiful Rachel, and as less loved by Jacob. She bore Jacob six sons — Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar and Zebulun — the ancestors of six of the tribes of Israel, and a daughter, Dinah. Her maid Zilpah also bore two sons credited within the household. Leah was buried in the Cave of Machpelah in Hebron.

In Jewish and Christian tradition Leah is one of the matriarchs of Israel; through her son Judah she is an ancestress of the Davidic line.
