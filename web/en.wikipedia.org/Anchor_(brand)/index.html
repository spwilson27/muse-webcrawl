Anchor (brand)

Anchor is a brand of dairy products that was founded in New Zealand in 1886. The brand is owned by the New Zealand dairy co-operative Fonterra and is one of its main international consumer brands, covering products such as milk powder, butter, cheese, and UHT milk sold in many countries.

History

The Anchor brand traces its origins to 1886, when a dairy factory was established near Hamilton in New Zealand's Waikato region and its butter was exported under the Anchor trademark, featuring a distinctive anchor logo. New Zealand butter under the brand became especially prominent in the British market during the 20th century.

Products and markets

Today Anchor products — including whole milk powder, butter, cheese, cream, and ready-to-drink milks — are marketed across Asia, the Middle East, and the Pacific, while in New Zealand the brand is widely sold in supermarkets. The brand is one of Fonterra's flagship consumer brands alongside brands such as Mainland and Anlene.
