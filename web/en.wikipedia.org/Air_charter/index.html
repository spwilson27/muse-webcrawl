Air charter

Air charter is the business of renting an entire aircraft (chartering) as opposed to individual aircraft seats (purchasing a ticket through a traditional airline). While the airlines specialize in selling transportation by the seat, air charter companies focus on individual private aircraft and itineraries, urgent or time-sensitive cargo, air ambulance and other specialized services.

Categories

Charter services may be roughly divided into passenger charter (including business jets, group charters for sports teams and events, and air taxi services), cargo charter for freight that is urgent, oversized or hazardous, and air ambulance or medical evacuation flights. Charter flights may operate on demand or on seasonal scheduled-like programs, such as holiday charters to leisure destinations.

Regulation

In the United States, charter operations are generally conducted under Federal Aviation Regulations Part 135 for on-demand operations, while larger scheduled charter programs may fall under Part 121. In Europe, charter operators hold an Air Operator Certificate under EASA rules. Brokers who arrange charters on behalf of clients are also subject to disclosure and consumer-protection rules.
