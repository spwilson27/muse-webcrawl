The Canterbury Rugby Football Union (CRFU) is the governing body for rugby union in the Canterbury region of New Zealand and fields the Canterbury provincial team in the National Provincial Championship (Bunnings NPC). Founded in 1879, it was the first provincial rugby union established in New Zealand.

Canterbury players in the union's distinctive red-and-black hooped jerseys.

History

The union was formed in 1879, and Canterbury played a leading role in the founding of the New Zealand Rugby Football Union in 1892. Canterbury teams have traditionally worn red and black hoops. The province became one of the powerhouses of New Zealand domestic rugby, holding the Ranfurly Shield in several celebrated eras — including a long tenure in the early 1980s and another from 2000 — and producing a long line of All Blacks.

Provincial success

Canterbury won the first division of the National Provincial Championship repeatedly in the professional era, claiming a run of titles from 2008 and dominating the Mitre 10 Cup in the 2010s, including seven consecutive premiership titles from 2011 to 2017. The union is one of the five provincial unions that feed the Crusaders Super Rugby franchise, which is based in Christchurch and is the most successful team in that competition's history.

Grounds

For most of its history Canterbury played at Lancaster Park, which was fatally damaged in the February 2011 Christchurch earthquake and later demolished. The team moved to the temporary Rugby League Park in Addington, known commercially as Apollo Projects Stadium, pending construction of a new permanent stadium in the city centre.
