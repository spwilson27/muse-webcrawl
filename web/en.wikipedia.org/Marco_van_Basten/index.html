Marcel "Marco" van Basten (born 31 October 1964) is a Dutch former professional footballer and manager. Widely regarded as one of the greatest strikers of all time, he won the Ballon d'Or three times (1988, 1989 and 1992) and was named FIFA World Player of the Year in 1992.

Club career

Van Basten began his career with Ajax, scoring 128 goals in 133 league appearances and winning three Eredivisie titles and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1987. He then moved to AC Milan, where he formed a celebrated Dutch trio with Ruud Gullit and Frank Rijkaard, winning three Serie A titles and two European Cups (1989 and 1990).

International career

Van Basten starred at UEFA Euro 1988, the only major tournament won by the Netherlands, finishing as top scorer with five goals, including his famous zero-angle volley in the final against the Soviet Union.

Retirement and management

Recurring ankle injuries forced his retirement in 1995 at the age of 30. He later managed the Netherlands national team (2004–2008) and Ajax (2008–2009).
