Palmares (quilombo)

Palmares was a quilombo — a settlement of fugitive enslaved Africans — in the Portuguese colony of Brazil, located in the present-day state of Alagoas. Founded around 1605, it grew into a federation of settlements that resisted Portuguese and Dutch colonial forces for most of the 17th century.

History

At its peak, Palmares may have housed tens of thousands of inhabitants organised under kings such as Ganga Zumba. Its most famous leader, Zumbi, rejected a 1678 treaty with the Portuguese and led the resistance until Palmares was destroyed by bandeirante forces under Domingos Jorge Velho in 1694; Zumbi was killed on November 20, 1695.

Legacy

Zumbi and Palmares are enduring symbols of Afro-Brazilian resistance; November 20 is observed in Brazil as Black Consciousness Day.
