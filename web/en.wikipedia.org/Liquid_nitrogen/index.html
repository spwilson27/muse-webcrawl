Liquid nitrogen

Boiling point: −195.79 °C (77.36 K) at atmospheric pressure
Appearance: Colourless liquid
Density: 0.807 g/mL

Liquid nitrogen (LN2) is nitrogen in a liquid state at very low temperature. It is produced industrially by fractional distillation of liquid air and boils at −195.79 °C under atmospheric pressure.

Uses

Liquid nitrogen is a compact, economical source of dry nitrogen gas and an effective coolant. It is used for cryopreservation of biological samples, freezing and transporting food, cryotherapy, superconductors, and as a coolant for computers and sensitive equipment.

Safety

Contact can cause severe frostbite, and evaporating nitrogen can displace oxygen in enclosed spaces, creating an asphyxiation hazard. It is stored in vacuum-insulated Dewar flasks.
