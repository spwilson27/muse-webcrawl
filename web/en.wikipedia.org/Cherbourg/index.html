Cherbourg

Cherbourg is a port city in Normandy, northern France, at the tip of the Cotentin Peninsula; since 2016 it has formed part of the commune of Cherbourg-en-Cotentin. Its deep-water harbour, protected by one of the world's largest artificial roadsteads, has long naval importance.

The port was a major transatlantic departure point and was liberated in June 1944 after the Battle of Cherbourg, becoming a vital supply port for the Allied advance. It is known for shipbuilding, including submarines, and the Cité de la Mer maritime museum.
