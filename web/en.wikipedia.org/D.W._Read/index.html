D.W. Read

Illustration: D.W. Read

Dora Winifred "D.W." Read is a fictional character in Marc Brown's Arthur book and television series. She is the preschool-aged younger sister of Arthur Read, the show's title character.

Character

D.W. is portrayed as imaginative, outspoken, and often exasperating to her older brother, with whom she frequently argues. Her imaginary friend is Nadine, and her favourite song, "Crazy Bus", is a running gag. In the books she has been depicted at ages four and five.

In the series

D.W. was voiced in the PBS animated series by several child actors over its 25-year run (1996–2022), beginning with Michael Caloz. Episodes centred on her include "D.W.'s Imaginary Friend" and "D.W. the Picky Eater".
