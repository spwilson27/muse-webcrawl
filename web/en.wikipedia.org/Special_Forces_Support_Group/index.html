Special Forces Support Group

BranchBritish Army / Royal Marines
RoleSupport to UK Special Forces
Formed2006
BasedMOD St Athan, Wales

The Special Forces Support Group (SFSG) is a unit of the British Armed Forces that provides infantry and specialist support to the United Kingdom Special Forces. It was formally established in 2006 and is based at MOD St Athan in Wales.

Composition

The group is built around a battalion-strength core drawn largely from the Parachute Regiment, with personnel from the Royal Marines and the RAF Regiment, together with attached signals, medical, and explosive ordnance specialists.

Role

The SFSG provides a range of supporting tasks for special forces operations, including providing a cordon, additional firepower, chemical and biological protection, and acting as a training adversary. It has deployed on operations in Iraq and Afghanistan.
