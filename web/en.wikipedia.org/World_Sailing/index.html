World Sailing

Image placeholder: Sailing yachts racing at an Olympic regatta

World Sailing (WS) is the world governing body for the sport of sailing, recognized by the International Olympic Committee and the International Paralympic Committee. Founded in Paris in 1907 as the International Yacht Racing Union (IYRU), it adopted the name International Sailing Federation (ISAF) in 1996 and became World Sailing in 2015.

Role

The federation oversees the racing rules of sailing, class rules and equipment control, officials' training, and the sailing events of the Olympic Games. Its headquarters are in London, United Kingdom. It has over 140 member national authorities.

Events

World Sailing organizes the Sailing World Championships, the Sailing World Cup series, and world championships in the Olympic classes, and it has selected the equipment used at each Olympic regatta.
