Canton of Cartagena
1873–1874
Capital: Cartagena, Spain

The Canton of Cartagena was a short-lived federal republic proclaimed in the city of Cartagena, Spain, on 12 July 1873, during the Cantonal rebellion against the First Spanish Republic. Supported by most of the Spanish fleet, which had its main base in the port, it held out longer than any other canton.

History

The cantonalists seized the warships in the harbour and used them to raise funds along the Mediterranean coast. After a long siege by government forces under Generals Pavía and López Domínguez, the city surrendered on 12 January 1874; many defenders escaped aboard the frigate Numancia to Oran.
