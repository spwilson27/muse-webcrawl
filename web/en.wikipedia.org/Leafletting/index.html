Leafletting

Leafletting (also known as flyering) is the distribution of printed leaflets or flyers, typically by hand in public places or through letterboxes, to advertise a business, event, product, or political cause.

Practice

Leafletting is a cheap and direct form of marketing and campaigning. Businesses use it for local promotion, while political parties, trade unions and activist groups distribute leaflets to raise awareness and persuade voters or passers-by. In some jurisdictions, leafletting in public spaces is protected as free expression, though local ordinances may regulate littering and door-to-door distribution.

Aerial leaflets

A related practice is the dropping of propaganda leaflets from aircraft, used extensively during the Second World War and later conflicts to influence enemy troops and civilians.
