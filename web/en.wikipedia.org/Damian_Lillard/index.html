Damian Lillard

Damian Lillard
Born: 15 July 1990, Oakland, California, U.S.
Position: Point guard
College: Weber State
NBA draft: 2012, 6th overall, Portland Trail Blazers

Damian Lamonte Ollie Lillard Sr. (born July 15, 1990) is an American professional basketball player. A point guard known for his long-range shooting and clutch performances — a persona often called "Dame Time" — he was selected sixth overall by the Portland Trail Blazers in the 2012 NBA draft after playing at Weber State, and won the NBA Rookie of the Year Award in 2013.

Career

Lillard spent eleven seasons in Portland, becoming the franchise's all-time leading scorer and a multiple-time NBA All-Star and All-NBA Team selection. He is famous for two series-winning buzzer-beaters in the playoffs: a three-pointer over Houston in 2014 and a deep shot over Oklahoma City in 2019 to close the series, after which he waved goodbye to the Thunder bench. In 2023 he was traded to the Milwaukee Bucks, where he teamed with Giannis Antetokounmpo and won the NBA Cup in 2024. He was named to the NBA 75th Anniversary Team in 2021.

Music and personal life

Off the court, Lillard performs as a rapper under the name Dame D.O.L.L.A. and has released several studio albums. He is from Oakland, California, and has been noted for his loyalty to his teams and community work.
