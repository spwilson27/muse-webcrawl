Salesforce

Salesforce, Inc. is an American cloud-based software company headquartered in San Francisco, California. It provides customer relationship management (CRM) software and applications focused on sales, customer service, marketing automation, e-commerce, analytics and application development. Salesforce is a pioneer of software as a service (SaaS) and one of the world's largest enterprise software companies.

History

Salesforce was founded in February 1999 by Marc Benioff, a former Oracle executive, together with Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff and Frank Dominguez. Operating from a San Francisco apartment under the slogan "The End of Software," it delivered CRM over the web on a subscription basis — a radical alternative to installed enterprise software sold by rivals such as Siebel Systems. The company went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 2004 under the symbol CRM, and joined the Dow Jones Industrial Average in 2020.

Products and acquisitions

Its core platform includes Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud and Commerce Cloud, plus the Lightning development platform and the Einstein AI layer. Major acquisitions expanded its portfolio: Heroku (2010), ExactTarget (2013), Tableau Software (2019, about $15.7 billion) for analytics, MuleSoft (2018) for integration, and Slack (2021, about $27.7 billion), its largest deal, for workplace messaging. Its headquarters, Salesforce Tower, completed in 2018, is the tallest building in San Francisco.

Corporate culture

Benioff promoted the "1-1-1 model" of philanthropy — donating 1% of equity, 1% of product and 1% of employee time — through Salesforce.org. The company's community of users and developers is branded the "Trailblazer" community, and its annual Dreamforce conference in San Francisco is among the largest software conferences in the world.
