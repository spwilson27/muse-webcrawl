Winter Hexagon

The Winter Hexagon, also known as the Winter Circle, is an asterism appearing in the form of a hexagon with vertices at Rigel, Aldebaran, Capella, Pollux, Procyon, and Sirius. On the borders of the hexagon, smaller and fainter asterisms such as the Winter Triangle also appear.

Stars

All six vertex stars are first-magnitude stars and among the brightest visible from Earth in the winter months of the Northern Hemisphere: Sirius (in Canis Major), Procyon (Canis Minor), Pollux (Gemini), Capella (Auriga), Aldebaran (Taurus), and Rigel (Orion). The Milky Way passes through the hexagon, and the centre of the figure lies near the star Betelgeuse.

Visibility

The asterism is best seen on winter evenings in the Northern Hemisphere and in summer in the Southern Hemisphere, where it is sometimes called the Summer Hexagon.
