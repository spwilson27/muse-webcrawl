Serena Jameka Williams (born September 26, 1981) is an American former professional tennis player. Widely regarded as one of the greatest tennis players of all time, she was ranked world No. 1 in singles by the Women's Tennis Association (WTA) for 319 weeks, including a joint-record 186 consecutive weeks, and finished as the year-end No. 1 five times. Williams won 23 Grand Slam singles titles, the most by any player in the Open Era and the second-most of all time, behind Margaret Court's 24.

Early life

Williams was born in Lynwood, California, to Oracene Price and Richard Williams, and grew up in Compton. She and her older sister Venus were coached by their parents from a young age on public courts; the family later moved to Florida, where the sisters attended the academy of Rick Macci. Serena turned professional in 1995 at the age of 14.

Career

Williams won her first major singles title at the 1999 US Open, defeating world No. 1 Martina Hingis. In 2002–03 she achieved the "Serena Slam", holding all four major singles titles simultaneously across two calendar years, culminating in the 2003 Australian Open — a feat she repeated in 2014–15. Her 23rd major title, at the 2017 Australian Open — won while eight weeks pregnant — surpassed Steffi Graf's Open Era record. Together with Venus she won 14 Grand Slam doubles titles and three Olympic doubles gold medals, plus singles gold at the London 2012 Olympics.

Known for her powerful serve, groundstrokes and mental resilience, Williams dominated women's tennis across three decades and remained a contender for major titles into her late thirties, reaching four more Grand Slam finals after returning from childbirth. In September 2022 she announced she was "evolving away" from tennis, playing her final matches at the US Open, where she lost in the third round to Ajla Tomljanović.

Legacy and off-court life

Williams is credited with revolutionising women's tennis with her power game and with reshaping the sport's culture and economics; she was repeatedly the world's highest-paid female athlete. Off court she has pursued fashion (her brand S by Serena), venture capital through Serena Ventures, and philanthropy including schools in Africa. She married Reddit co-founder Alexis Ohanian in 2017; they have two daughters.
