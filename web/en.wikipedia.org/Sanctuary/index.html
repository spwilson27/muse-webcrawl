Sanctuary

Etymology: Latin sanctuarium, from sanctus (holy)

Primary senses: Sacred place; place of refuge

Related concepts: Asylum, temple, wildlife sanctuary, sanctuary city

A sanctuary, in its original meaning, is a sacred place such as a shrine, temple or altar precinct. By extension the word denotes any place of safety or refuge: in church architecture it is the holiest part of a church, around the altar; in law it was historically a place where fugitives could claim protection from arrest; and in modern usage it describes nature reserves for wildlife, places of safety for people, and jurisdictions (sanctuary cities) that limit cooperation with national immigration enforcement.

Sacred place

Ancient Greek and Roman temples enclosed a temenos, a precinct set apart for a god; the Latin sanctuarium was where sacred objects were kept. In Christian churches the sanctuary is the area at the east end containing the altar, distinguished from the nave where the congregation sits, often by rails or a chancel arch. Hindu temples, mosques and other houses of worship reserve analogous inner sanctums, in Hindu practice the garbhagriha (“womb chamber”) holding the deity’s image.

Right of asylum

From antiquity through the Middle Ages, temples and churches offered sanctuary to fugitives: the Hebrew Bible prescribes cities of refuge, Greek temples sheltered suppliants, and English common law allowed a felon who reached a church to confess and abjure the realm rather than face trial. The right was restricted under the Tudors and abolished in England in the 17th century, but the idea survives in modern asylum law, in the 20th-century sanctuary movement that sheltered Central American refugees, and in the “sanctuary city” policies adopted by many local governments.

Wildlife sanctuary

A wildlife sanctuary is a protected area where hunting and habitat destruction are prohibited and animals may live and breed undisturbed; unlike a national park, a sanctuary is usually established for particular species and may permit some human activity. Animal sanctuaries in the welfare sense rescue and house abused or unwanted domestic and exotic animals, a model used by farm-animal sanctuaries, elephant sanctuaries and primate sanctuaries worldwide.
