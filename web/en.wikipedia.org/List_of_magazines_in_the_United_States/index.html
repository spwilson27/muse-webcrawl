List of magazines in the United States

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of magazines in the United States, organized by subject category. The American magazine industry dates to the colonial period and grew enormously in the late 19th and 20th centuries, when mass-circulation titles such as Life, Time, and The Saturday Evening Post reached millions of households. Since the 2000s, print circulation has declined as readers and advertisers moved online, and many titles have become digital-first or ceased print editions.
