Olympic Green

Olympic Green (奥林匹克公园)
Location: Chaoyang District, Beijing, China
Area: 1,135 hectares (2,800 acres)
Built for: 2008 Summer Olympics
Status: Public park and venue cluster

The Olympic Green (Chinese: 奥林匹克公园, "Olympic Park") is an Olympic park in Chaoyang District, Beijing, China, constructed for the 2008 Summer Olympics and used again for the 2022 Winter Olympics, making Beijing the first city to host both editions. The park covers 1,135 hectares at the northern end of the city's central axis.

Venues

Its landmark venues include the Beijing National Stadium — the "Bird's Nest," designed by Herzog & de Meuron with Ai Weiwei — which hosted the opening and closing ceremonies, athletics and football in 2008 and ceremonies again in 2022; the Beijing National Aquatics Center, the "Water Cube," converted for 2022 into the "Ice Cube" for curling; the National Indoor Stadium; and the National Speed Skating Oval ("Ice Ribbon"), built on the former hockey and archery fields for 2022.

Design and layout

The park is bisected by a wide ceremonial promenade aligned with the meridian of the Forbidden City. Its northern section is a forest park with a large lake system, designed to restore wetlands and act as a green lung for the city. The Ling Long Pagoda broadcast tower and the Olympic Tower (completed 2015) punctuate the skyline.

Legacy

After the Games the venues have hosted concerts, exhibitions, sporting events and public recreation; the stadium offers tours and the Water Cube added a public water park. The Olympic Green remains a major tourist destination and the centrepiece of Beijing's Olympic legacy planning.
