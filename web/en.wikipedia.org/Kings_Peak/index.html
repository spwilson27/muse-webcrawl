Kings Peak

Elevation: 13,528 ft (4,123 m)
Range: Uinta Mountains
Location: Duchesne County, Utah, USA

Kings Peak is the highest point in the U.S. state of Utah, rising to 13,528 feet (4,123 m) within the High Uintas Wilderness of Ashley National Forest. It is named for Clarence King, the first director of the U.S. Geological Survey.

Climbing

The most popular route follows the Henrys Fork Trail, a long but non-technical hike and scramble of roughly 29 miles round trip. Despite its gentle profile, the peak's remoteness and rapidly changing mountain weather make the ascent a serious undertaking.
