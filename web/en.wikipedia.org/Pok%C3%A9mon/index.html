Pokémon is a Japanese media franchise consisting of video games, animated series and films, a trading card game, and other media. It is one of the highest-grossing media franchises of all time. The franchise was created by Satoshi Tajiri in 1996 and is centred on fictional creatures called Pokémon.

Concept

In the Pokémon universe, humans known as Pokémon Trainers catch and train Pokémon to battle each other for sport. The English-language slogan of the franchise is "Gotta Catch 'Em All!". The franchise began as the pair of role-playing games Pocket Monsters Red and Green for the Game Boy, developed by Game Freak.

Video games

The core role-playing games are divided into generations, each introducing new species of Pokémon and a new region. Spin-offs include Pokémon Go (2016), an augmented-reality mobile game that became a worldwide phenomenon, and Pokémon Snap, Pokémon Mystery Dungeon and many others.

Other media

The animated television series follows Ash Ketchum and his partner Pikachu in most seasons; the Pokémon Trading Card Game is one of the best-selling card games in the world. Pikachu, the franchise's mascot, is among the most recognisable fictional characters globally.
