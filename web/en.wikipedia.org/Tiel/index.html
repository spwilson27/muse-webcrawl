Tiel is a municipality and a town in the middle of the Netherlands, in the province of Gelderland. It lies in the Betuwe, a fertile fruit-growing region between the Waal and Linge rivers, and is one of the oldest towns in the country, with a history as a trading port going back to the early Middle Ages.

History

Tiel grew into an important trading centre in the 9th and 10th centuries, receiving toll rights and trading with England and the German Rhineland. It later became a member of the Hanseatic League. The town was heavily damaged during the Second World War, particularly in the fighting of 1944–45.

Present day

Today Tiel is known for fruit production and processing, and for Flipje, the mascot of a former local jam factory, who has a small museum in the town. The annual Appelpop festival, one of the largest free pop festivals in the Netherlands, is held in Tiel each September.
