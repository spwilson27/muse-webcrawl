Mondelēz International, Inc. is an American multinational food company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. It was formed in 2012 when Kraft Foods spun off its North American grocery business and renamed the remaining snack-focused company Mondelēz, a name combining the Latin-derived words for "world" and "delicious."

Brands

The company's portfolio includes Oreo, Cadbury, Milka, Toblerone, Ritz, Triscuit, Chips Ahoy!, belVita, and Sour Patch Kids. Its products are sold in about 150 countries.

History

Mondelēz inherited Kraft's international snack and confectionery businesses, including the 2010 acquisition of Cadbury. It has since made further acquisitions in baked snacks and divested its coffee holdings into the joint venture that became JDE Peet's.
