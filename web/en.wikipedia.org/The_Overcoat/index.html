The Overcoat

[Placeholder image: The_Overcoat.png]

'The Overcoat' (Russian: Шинель, Shinel) is a short story by Nikolai Gogol, published in 1842. It tells of Akaky Akakievich Bashmachkin, a poor government clerk in St. Petersburg who saves to buy a new overcoat, which transforms his life briefly before it is stolen, leading to his decline and death — after which his ghost haunts the city.

Influence

The story is considered a cornerstone of Russian realism and a touchstone for later writers; the remark 'We all came out of Gogol's Overcoat' is traditionally attributed to Dostoevsky.
