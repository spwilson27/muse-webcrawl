DeepMind


Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind Technologies Limited, trading as Google DeepMind, is an artificial intelligence research laboratory founded in London in 2010 by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and Mustafa Suleyman. It was acquired by Google in 2014 for a reported £400 million or more. In April 2023, DeepMind was merged with Google's Brain team to form Google DeepMind.

Research

DeepMind became widely known for AlphaGo, which in 2016 defeated world-class Go player Lee Sedol 4–1 in a televised match. Its programs include AlphaZero, AlphaStar, and AlphaFold, which in 2020 effectively solved the protein structure prediction problem at the CASP14 competition. Hassabis and John Jumper shared part of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AlphaFold's protein structure predictions.

More recently the laboratory has developed the Gemini family of multimodal large language models, and tools such as AlphaCode and the weather model GraphCast. DeepMind has also worked on reducing Google's data-center cooling energy and on AI safety research.
