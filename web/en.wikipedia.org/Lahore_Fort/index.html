Lahore Fort

The Lahore Fort (Punjabi/Urdu: شاہی قلعہ, Shahi Qila, "Royal Fort") is a citadel in the walled city of Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan, located in the northwestern corner of the old city opposite the Badshahi Mosque. A fort has stood on the site since antiquity, but the present structure was largely rebuilt by the Mughal emperor Akbar from 1566, and embellished by his successors Jahangir, Shah Jahan and Aurangzeb.

The trapezoidal fort covers about 20 hectares and contains celebrated Mughal monuments including the Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors), Naulakha Pavilion, Diwan-i-Khas, Moti Masjid and the monumental Alamgiri Gate. It later served the Sikh Empire of Ranjit Singh and the British. Together with the Shalimar Gardens, it was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981.
