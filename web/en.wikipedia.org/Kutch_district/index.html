Kutch district (also spelled Kachchh) is a district of Gujarat state in western India, the largest district in the country by area. Its headquarters is Bhuj. The district is bounded by the Arabian Sea and the Gulf of Kutch to the south and west, and by the salt flats of the Great and Little Rann of Kutch to the north and east.

Geography and culture

Much of Kutch is seasonally marshy salt desert; the Great Rann hosts the Rann Utsav festival. The region is known for embroidery and handicrafts, the port of Kandla, and wildlife sanctuaries protecting the Indian wild ass. Kutchi, a Sindhi-influenced language, is widely spoken.

2001 earthquake

On 26 January 2001 a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck near Bhuj, killing around 20,000 people and devastating towns including Bhuj, Anjar, and Bhachau; reconstruction transformed the district's economy and infrastructure.
