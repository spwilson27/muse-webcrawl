DVD
Media type: Optical disc
Capacity: 4.7 GB (single layer)
Released: 1996 (Japan)

The DVD (Digital Video Disc or Digital Versatile Disc) is a digital optical disc data storage format invented and developed in 1995 and released in late 1996 in Japan, then worldwide in 1997.

Capacity and formats

A standard single-layer DVD holds 4.7 GB, dual-layer 8.5 GB, with double-sided variants up to 17 GB. Formats include DVD-Video, DVD-Audio, and recordable DVD±R and rewritable DVD±RW.

History

DVD unified competing proposals from Toshiba/Time Warner and Philips/Sony. It rapidly replaced VHS for home video and became the dominant rental and sell-through format before being challenged by Blu-ray and streaming.

Decline

Sales peaked in the mid-2000s and declined with the rise of online streaming services, though DVDs remain in use for archives and regions with limited broadband.
