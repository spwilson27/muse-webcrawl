Mork & Mindy

Mork & Mindy

Genre: Sitcom, science fiction comedy
Created by: Garry Marshall, Dale McRaven, Joe Glauberg
Starring: Robin Williams, Pam Dawber
Network: ABC
Original run: September 14, 1978 – May 27, 1982 (4 seasons, 95 episodes)

Mork & Mindy is an American television sitcom that aired on ABC from September 14, 1978, to May 27, 1982. A spin-off of Happy Days, it stars Robin Williams as Mork, an alien from the planet Ork, and Pam Dawber as Mindy McConnell, the young woman who takes him in in Boulder, Colorado.

Premise

Mork is sent to Earth in an egg-shaped spacecraft to observe human behaviour and report back to his leader, Orson, whom he contacts in a closing monologue each episode ("Mork calling Orson, come in, Orson"). Struggling to understand human customs, Mork speaks in Orkian phrases such as "Na-Nu Na-Nu" (a greeting) and "Shazbot!" (an exclamation). Williams's improvisational physical comedy made the show a breakout hit in its first season, launching his career.

Cast and changes

Supporting characters included Mindy's father Fred (Conrad Janis), her grandmother Cora (Elizabeth Kerr), and the eccentric Exidor (Robert Donner). Later seasons introduced changes widely blamed for declining ratings, including Jonathan Winters as Mearth, Mork and Mindy's "child" born by Orkian ageing in reverse. Mork and Mindy married in the fourth season.

Legacy

The show was a top-ten ratings success in its first season and remains fondly remembered for Williams's manic energy and the chemistry between the leads. It established Robin Williams as a major star and is cited as a classic of 1970s American sitcoms.
