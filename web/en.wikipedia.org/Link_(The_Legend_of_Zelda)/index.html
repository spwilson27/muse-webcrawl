Link (The Legend of Zelda)

Link is a character and the protagonist of Nintendo's video game franchise The Legend of Zelda. He was created by Japanese video game designer Shigeru Miyamoto. Link was introduced as the hero of the original 1986 The Legend of Zelda video game and has appeared in a total of over twenty entries in the series. He has also featured in other Nintendo media, including his own animated television series, manga adaptations, and the Super Smash Bros. series.

Over the course of the series, Link is depicted as a child, teenager, or young adult of the Hylian race, originating from the fictional land of Hyrule. He is frequently reborn across the ages as the hero chosen to wield the Master Sword and fight the evil Ganon, while serving the kingdom alongside Princess Zelda. He is traditionally dressed in a green tunic and cap and armed with the Master Sword and Hylian Shield.

Characterization

Link is typically a silent protagonist who communicates through grunts and gestures rather than spoken dialogue, a design choice intended to let players project themselves into the character — the name "Link" signifies his role as a link between player and game world. In Breath of the Wild (2017) and its sequel Tears of the Kingdom (2023), his design was modernised with a blue Champion's Tunic. Link is regarded as one of the most iconic video game characters of all time.
