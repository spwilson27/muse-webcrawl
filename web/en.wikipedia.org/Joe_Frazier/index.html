Joe Frazier

Joseph William Frazier (January 12, 1944 – November 7, 2011), nicknamed "Smokin' Joe", was an American professional boxer who was undisputed world heavyweight champion from 1970 to 1973.

Frazier won gold at the 1964 Tokyo Olympics and handed Muhammad Ali his first defeat in the "Fight of the Century" in 1971. He lost the title to George Foreman in 1973 and fought Ali twice more, including the brutal Thrilla in Manila in 1975. Renowned for his relentless pressure and devastating left hook, he is ranked among the greatest heavyweights ever.
