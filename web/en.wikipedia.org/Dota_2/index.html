Dota 2

Developer: Valve
Release: July 9, 2013
Genre: Multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA)
Platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux

Dota 2 is a 2013 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Valve. The game is a sequel to Defense of the Ancients (DotA), a community-created mod for Blizzard Entertainment's Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. Dota 2 is played in matches between two teams of five players, with each team occupying and defending its own separate base on a square map.

Gameplay

Each player controls a powerful character called a "hero" with unique abilities and different styles of play. During a match, players collect experience points and items to defeat the opposing team's heroes in player-versus-player combat. A team wins by destroying the other side's "Ancient", a large building located within their base.

Matches typically last between 30 and 60 minutes. The map is divided into three lanes, along which computer-controlled "creeps" march, and a jungle area between them. Over 120 heroes are available, all free to play — the game's revenue comes primarily from cosmetic items.

Development

Valve hired IceFrog, the pseudonymous lead designer of DotA, in 2009, and announced Dota 2 in October 2010. After an extended beta period, the game was released on Steam in July 2013. In 2015 the game was ported to the Source 2 engine in the "Reborn" update, making it the first game built on that engine.

Esports

Dota 2 has a large esports scene, with teams from across the world playing in professional leagues and tournaments. The International, Valve's premier annual tournament, has featured the largest prize pools in esports history, crowdfunded through sales of an in-game battle pass; the 2021 edition, The International 10, exceeded US$40 million.
