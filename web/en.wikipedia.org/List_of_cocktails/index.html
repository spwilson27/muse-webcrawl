List of cocktails

A cocktail is a mixed drink typically made with a distilled spirit as its base. The International Bartenders Association (IBA) maintains a list of official cocktails.

IBA official cocktails (selection)

  • The Unforgettables: Old Fashioned, Martini, Manhattan, Negroni, Daiquiri, Margarita, Mojito, Whiskey Sour, Gin Fizz, Bloody Mary, Mai Tai, Piña Colada, Sidecar, French 75, Sazerac, Alexander, Aviation, Bee's Knees, Boulevardier, Brandy Crusta, Clover Club, Dry Martini, Hanky Panky, Last Word, Martinez, Paradise, Planter's Punch, Ramos Gin Fizz, Rusty Nail, Vieux Carré, White Lady
  • Contemporary Classics: Cosmopolitan, Espresso Martini, Bellini, Caipirinha, Champagne Cocktail, Corpse Reviver #2, Cubanito, Godfather, Godmother, Golden Dream, Grasshopper, Hemingway Special, Horse's Neck, Irish Coffee, Kir, Long Island Iced Tea, Mimosa, Mint Julep, Moscow Mule, Sea Breeze, Sex on the Beach, Singapore Sling, Tequila Sunrise, Vesper, Zombie
  • New Era Drinks: Aperol Spritz, Dark 'n' Stormy, Penicillin, Paper Plane, Porn Star Martini, Tipperary, Tommy's Margarita, Trinidad Sour, Ve.n.to, Illegal, Naked and Famous, Fernandito

Other notable cocktails

Gimlet, Tom Collins, Paloma, Pisco Sour, Batanga, El Presidente, Jungle Bird, Chartreuse Swizzle, and numerous regional and tiki drinks.
