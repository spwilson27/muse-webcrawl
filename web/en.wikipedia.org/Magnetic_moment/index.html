Magnetic moment

In electromagnetism, the magnetic moment or magnetic dipole moment is a vector quantity that characterizes the strength and orientation of a magnetic source, such as a bar magnet, a loop of electric current, or an elementary particle. The torque experienced by a magnetic dipole in an external magnetic field B is given by τ = μ × B.

Current loops

For a planar loop carrying current I with area A, the magnitude of the magnetic moment is μ = IA, directed normal to the loop by the right-hand rule. On the axis of a dipole, the field falls off with the inverse cube of distance.

Particles and atoms

Elementary particles carry intrinsic magnetic moments associated with spin; the electron magnetic moment, arising partly from orbital motion and partly from spin, underlies atomic magnetism, the Bohr magneton and the Zeeman effect.
