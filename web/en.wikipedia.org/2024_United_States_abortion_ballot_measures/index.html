2024 United States abortion ballot measures

In the 2024 United States elections, ten states held ballot measures concerning abortion rights, the most ever in a single year. The measures followed the Supreme Court's 2022 decision in Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization, which overturned Roe v. Wade and returned the regulation of abortion to the states.

Abortion-rights measures succeeded in seven of the ten states, including Missouri and Montana, while measures in Florida, Nebraska, and South Dakota failed. Florida's Amendment 4 won a majority of votes but fell short of the 60% supermajority required for passage in that state.
