BBC Radio 2

First air date 30 September 1967
Frequency FM 88.1–90.2 MHz
Format Adult contemporary, music and talk
Owner BBC

BBC Radio 2 is a British national radio station owned and operated by the BBC. It is the most listened-to radio station in the United Kingdom, broadcasting a wide range of music — principally adult contemporary and classic hits — alongside news, talk, comedy, documentaries, and specialist music programmes.

History

Radio 2 launched on 30 September 1967, taking over much of the output of the former Light Programme. Initially focused on easy listening, the station gradually broadened its playlist. Long-serving presenters such as Terry Wogan, who hosted the breakfast show for decades, made it a central part of British popular culture.

Programming

The schedule combines flagship shows — including the breakfast show and drive-time programmes — with specialist series covering genres such as jazz, blues, country, folk, soul, and musical theatre. Radio 2 also broadcasts news bulletins from BBC News and presents live music events, including Radio 2 in Concert and the BBC Proms in the Park.

Audience

The station targets listeners aged 35 and over and consistently attracts weekly audiences of around 13–14 million, the largest of any UK radio station.
