Internet forum

An Internet forum, or message board, is an online discussion platform where people hold conversations in the form of posted messages, which are archived rather than transient as in chat rooms. Forums descend from bulletin board systems (BBS) and Usenet.

Structure

Forums are typically organized into subforums by topic, containing threads (topics) made up of posts by registered members. Moderators and administrators enforce rules; common features include user ranks, private messaging, signatures, and post counts. Popular forum software has included vBulletin, phpBB, XenForo, and Discourse. Forums were a dominant form of online community in the 2000s before much activity migrated to social media and platforms such as Reddit.
