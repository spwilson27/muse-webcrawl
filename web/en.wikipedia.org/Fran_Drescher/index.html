Fran Drescher

Fran Drescher (born 1957), actress, writer, and union president

Francine Joy Drescher (born September 30, 1957) is an American actress, comedian, writer, activist, and trade union leader. She is best known for her role as Fran Fine in the television sitcom The Nanny (1993–1999), which she co-created and produced with her then-husband Peter Marc Jacobson. Her distinctive voice and comic persona made the show an international success, and she received two Emmy Award and two Golden Globe nominations for the role.

Career

Born in Queens, New York, Drescher had early film roles in Saturday Night Fever (1977) and American Hot Wax (1978), and appeared in This Is Spinal Tap (1984) and other films and series before The Nanny. She later starred in the sitcoms Living with Fran and Happily Divorced, the latter inspired by her life, and voiced Eunice in the Hotel Transylvania film series.

Advocacy and SAG-AFTRA

A uterine cancer survivor, Drescher founded the Cancer Schmancer Movement, a non-profit advocating early detection and health education, and wrote the memoir Cancer Schmancer. In 2021 she was elected president of SAG-AFTRA, the actors' union, and led it through the 2023 actors' strike, winning wide attention for her negotiation stance against the studios.
