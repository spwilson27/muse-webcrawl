Max Martin

Karl Martin Sandberg (born 26 February 1971), known professionally as Max Martin, is a Swedish record producer and songwriter. He rose to prominence in the late 1990s with hit songs for Britney Spears, the Backstreet Boys, and NSYNC, and has since written or co-written a long run of Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles — more than any songwriter except Paul McCartney and John Lennon — for artists including Katy Perry, Taylor Swift, Ariana Grande, and the Weeknd.

Career

Martin began as singer in the glam-metal band It's Alive before joining Denniz PoP's Cheiron Studios in Stockholm. After PoP's death in 1998, Martin led Cheiron until its closure, then founded production companies including Maratone and MXM. His signature style — melodic maximalism, "melodic math", and hooks in every section — has defined mainstream pop for three decades.

Awards

He has won multiple Grammy Awards, including Producer of the Year (Non-Classical) in 2015, and the ASCAP Songwriter of the Year award a record number of times.
