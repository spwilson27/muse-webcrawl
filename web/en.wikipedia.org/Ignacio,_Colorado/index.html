Ignacio, Colorado

Ignacio is a town in La Plata County, Colorado, United States. The population was 852 at the 2010 census. It is the headquarters of the Southern Ute Indian Tribe, and is located on the Southern Ute Indian Reservation in southwestern Colorado.

History

Ignacio was named after Chief Ignacio (1828–1913), a leader of the Southern Ute people. The town grew up on the Southern Ute Reservation, which was established in the late nineteenth century. The Denver and Rio Grande Railroad reached the area in the early 1900s, and the town became a trading and administrative center for the reservation.

Geography

Ignacio is located in the southeastern part of La Plata County, in the Pine River valley. Colorado State Highway 172 passes through the town. Durango, the county seat, lies about 24 miles (39 km) to the northwest, and Bayfield is about 11 miles (18 km) to the northeast. According to the United States Census Bureau, the town has a total area of 0.27 square miles (0.70 km²), all land.

Demographics

As of the census of 2010, there were 852 people living in the town. The population includes a significant number of members of the Southern Ute Indian Tribe, as well as Anglo and Hispanic residents. The town serves as the seat of government for the Southern Ute Indian Tribe, with tribal administration buildings, a cultural center, and the Sky Ute Casino Resort located nearby.
