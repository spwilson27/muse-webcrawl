The Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry is the combined medical and dental school of the University of Western Ontario (Western University) in London, Ontario, Canada. Founded as a medical school in 1881, it was renamed in 2005 following a major donation from businessman and philanthropist Seymour Schulich.

History

Medical teaching at Western began in 1881, making the school one of Canada's oldest. Dentistry was added in 1964. In 2005 Schulich's gift of C$26 million — then among the largest to a Canadian medical school — led to the combined renaming. A distributed campus in Windsor, delivered in partnership with the University of Windsor, opened in 2008 to address physician shortages in southwestern Ontario.

Programmes and research

The school offers the Doctor of Medicine (MD), Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS), graduate degrees, and residency training through affiliated teaching hospitals in London. Research strengths include medical imaging, neuroscience, musculoskeletal health and infectious diseases, supported by institutes such as the Robarts Research Institute.
