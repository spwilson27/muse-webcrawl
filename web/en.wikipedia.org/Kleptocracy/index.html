Kleptocracy

Kleptocracy (from Greek κλέπτης kléptēs, "thief", and -κρατία -kratía, "rule") is a form of government in which corrupt leaders (kleptocrats) use political power to steal the wealth of the people and land they govern, typically by embezzling or misappropriating government funds at the expense of the wider population. Kleptocracy is different from plutocracy (rule by the richest) and oligarchy (rule by a small elite).

Characteristics

In a kleptocracy, corrupt politicians enrich themselves secretly, outside the rule of law, through kickbacks, bribes, and special favors from lobbyists and corporations, or they simply direct state funds to themselves and their associates. Kleptocrats often export much of their profits to foreign nations in anticipation of losing power.

According to the Oxford English Dictionary, the expression is used to describe "a government by thieves; a ruling body devoted to personal enrichment." The term has been applied to notorious modern regimes in which leaders amassed personal fortunes estimated in the billions of dollars, including those of Mobutu Sese Seko of Zaire, Ferdinand Marcos of the Philippines, and Suharto of Indonesia.

Effects

The effects of a kleptocratic regime on a nation can be ruinous in terms of the quality of its economy, its political affairs, and civil rights. Kleptocracy often sours the prospects for foreign investment and drastically weakens the domestic market and cross-border trade. Because the stolen money is often laundered abroad or spent on luxury goods, it contributes little to domestic development, while public services, infrastructure, and institutions decay.

Kleptocracy and money laundering

Modern kleptocrats rely on international money laundering networks, shell companies, anonymous real estate purchases, and tax havens to hide and spend stolen wealth. Transparency organizations and investigative journalists have played a growing role in exposing these flows, and several governments have enacted asset-recovery and unexplained-wealth-order legislation aimed at kleptocratic elites.
