Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway

The Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway is a 1,318-kilometre high-speed railway in China connecting Beijing South and Shanghai Hongqiao, the country's two largest cities. It opened on 30 June 2011.

Route and service

The line passes through Tianjin, Jinan, Xuzhou, Bengbu, Nanjing and Suzhou. Trains designed for 350 km/h cut the journey to under five hours, and the line is among the world's busiest and most profitable high-speed routes.

Construction

Built largely on elevated viaducts, including the Danyang–Kunshan Grand Bridge, then the world's longest bridge, construction began in 2008 at a cost of about 220 billion yuan.
