The rhombic triacontahedron is a convex polyhedron with 30 rhombic faces. It is a Catalan solid, the dual of the icosidodecahedron, and has full icosahedral symmetry. Its faces are golden rhombi whose diagonal ratio equals the golden ratio φ.

Properties

The solid has 60 edges and 32 vertices — 12 where five faces meet and 20 where three meet. It is face-transitive and is also a zonohedron; it can be dissected into 20 golden rhombohedra (10 acute and 10 obtuse). The rhombic triacontahedron is the shape of the thirty-sided die (d30) used in role-playing games.

Related geometry

Its projection underlies the Penrose-like rhombic tilings studied in quasicrystal theory, and it appears as the shape of the Brillouin zone of certain quasicrystalline alloys. The compound of five cubes can be inscribed in its vertices of order three.
