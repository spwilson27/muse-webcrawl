The Searchers

The Searchers is a 1956 American Western film directed by John Ford, starring John Wayne as Ethan Edwards. Based on Alan Le May's 1954 novel, it is regarded as one of the greatest films ever made.

Plot

Confederate veteran Ethan Edwards returns to Texas in 1868. After Comanche chief Scar's raiders kill his brother's family and abduct his nieces, Ethan spends five years searching with his part-Cherokee adopted nephew Martin Pawley (Jeffrey Hunter). Ethan's obsessive hatred of Native Americans leads him to intend killing his surviving niece Debbie (Natalie Wood) for having "lived with" the Comanche — but at the climax he lifts her home instead, echoing his opening line, "Let's go home, Debbie."

Legacy

The AFI ranked it the greatest American Western; it placed in the top ten of the 2012 Sight and Sound critics' poll. Its influence runs through Star Wars, Taxi Driver and many others. See also The Spanish Tragedy.
