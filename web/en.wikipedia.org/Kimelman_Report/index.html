Kimelman Report

The Kimelman Report, formally the report of the Review Committee on Indian and Métis Adoptions and Placements, chaired by Judge Edwin Kimelman, was published in Manitoba, Canada, in 1985 under the title No Quiet Place.

Findings

Investigating the removal of Indigenous children from their families and their adoption or fostering, often outside the province and country, during the period known as the Sixties Scoop, the committee delivered a damning verdict: it concluded that "cultural genocide has been taking place in a systematic, routine manner" through child-welfare practices. The report recommended sweeping reforms, greater Indigenous control over child welfare, and an end to out-of-province placements, influencing policy across Canada.
