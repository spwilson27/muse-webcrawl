Naval Station Norfolk is a United States Navy base in Norfolk, Virginia, that supports the naval forces operating in the Atlantic Ocean, the Mediterranean Sea and the Indian Ocean. It is the world's largest naval station, supporting about 75 ships and 134 aircraft alongside numerous shore commands.

History

The base was established in 1917 on land that had hosted the Jamestown Exposition of 1907. It grew rapidly during both world wars and became the principal home port of the U.S. Atlantic Fleet.

Facilities

The station includes extensive piers, airfields (as part of Naval Air Station Norfolk on Chambers Field) and support facilities, and serves as headquarters of U.S. Fleet Forces Command.
