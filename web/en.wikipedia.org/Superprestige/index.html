Superprestige

The Superprestige is a season-long cyclo-cross competition held annually in Belgium and the Netherlands. Founded in 1982, it is the oldest of the major season-long classifications in the sport, alongside the UCI World Cup and the X²O Badkamers Trophy.

Format

The series comprises a set of races across the winter season, typically eight rounds at venues such as Diegem, Zonhoven, Gavere, and Ruddervoorde. Riders earn points at each round, and the rider with the highest total in each category — elite men, elite women, and junior categories — wins the overall classification.

History

Belgian rider Sven Nys is among the most successful competitors in the series' history, winning the overall title many times during his career. The Superprestige is a central part of the Flemish cyclo-cross season and is widely televised in Belgium.
