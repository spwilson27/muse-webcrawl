The Baja 500 is an annual Mexican off-road motorsport race on the Baja California Peninsula, sanctioned by SCORE International. It is held in early June, typically starting and finishing in Ensenada.

History

The race was first held in 1969 and was originally organized by the NORRA and later by SCORE, which took over the Baja events in the 1970s. The course is a loop of roughly 500 miles, though the exact distance and routing vary each year.

Classes and vehicles

Entrants range from motorcycles and ATVs to production-based classes and unlimited Trophy Trucks, which compete for the overall victory. Famous competitors include Ivan "Ironman" Stewart, Larry Ragland and Robby Gordon.
