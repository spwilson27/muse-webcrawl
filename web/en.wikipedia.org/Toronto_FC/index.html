Toronto FC is a Canadian professional soccer club based in Toronto, Ontario, that competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Eastern Conference. The club was founded in 2005 and began play in 2007 as the league's first Canadian franchise. Toronto FC plays its home matches at BMO Field, located at Exhibition Place on the city's waterfront.

History

The club was awarded to Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment in 2005. Toronto FC struggled in its early seasons but built a passionate supporter culture, with groups such as the Red Patch Boys and Inebriatti becoming fixtures at BMO Field. In 2014 the club made headlines by signing designated players Jermain Defoe and Michael Bradley.

The team's golden period came under coach Greg Vanney. Toronto won the Supporters' Shield and the MLS Cup in 2017, completing a domestic treble with the Canadian Championship — the first treble in MLS history. Star players Sebastian Giovinco, Jozy Altidore and Michael Bradley led the side. In 2018 Toronto reached the final of the CONCACAF Champions League, losing to Chivas Guadalajara on penalties.

Honours

Toronto FC has won the MLS Cup once (2017), the Supporters' Shield once (2017), and the Canadian Championship multiple times, making it one of the most successful Canadian clubs in the professional era.

Rivalries

The club's chief rivalries are with CF Montréal in the Canadian Classique and with the Columbus Crew in the Trillium Cup series.
