Robert Dahl

Robert Alan Dahl (December 17, 1915 – February 5, 2014) was an American political theorist and Sterling Professor of Political Science at Yale University. He was a leading authority on democratic theory and a founder of the pluralist school of political science.

Work

In Who Governs? (1961), a study of politics in New Haven, Connecticut, Dahl argued that power was dispersed among competing groups rather than held by a single elite. He developed the concept of polyarchy to describe real-world political systems approximating democracy, setting out criteria including effective participation, voting equality, enlightened understanding, control of the agenda, and inclusion of adults. His major works include A Preface to Democratic Theory (1956), Polyarchy (1971), and Democracy and Its Critics (1989).

Influence

Dahl's framework for measuring and judging democratisation remains foundational in comparative politics, and his "intuitive idea of power" — A gets B to do something B would not otherwise do — is a classic definition in the study of power.
