Cheeseburger

A cheeseburger is a hamburger topped with cheese, typically a slice of processed or natural cheese melted over a cooked beef patty in a bun, often with lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, ketchup and mustard.

Claims to its invention in the 1920s–1930s are made for several American restaurants; Lionel Sternberger of Pasadena and Kaelin's of Louisville are among the best known. It became a staple of fast-food menus worldwide.
