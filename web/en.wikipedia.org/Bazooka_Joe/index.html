Bazooka Joe

ProductBazooka bubble gum
Introduced1953 (comics)
CreatorWoody Gelman (developer); Wesley Morse (artist)
Distinguishing featureEye patch

Bazooka Joe is a comic strip character featured in small comics wrapped around Bazooka bubble gum, produced by the Topps Company. Recognisable by his eye patch, Joe appears with his gang — including Mort, who wears his turtleneck pulled over his face — in short jokes and gags.

History

The character was created in the early 1950s as Topps developed its gum packaging, with art by Wesley Morse and later contributions from animator Jay Lynch. The miniature comics also carried advertisements for novelty prizes redeemable with saved wrappers and a modest cash payment.

Legacy

Bazooka Joe became one of the most recognisable advertising mascots in American popular culture. Topps retired the comic format in 2013, replacing it with puzzles and codes, before briefly reviving the character in later promotions.
