A Day in the Life

"A Day in the Life" is a song by the English rock band the Beatles that closes their 1967 album Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band. Credited to Lennon–McCartney, the opening and closing sections were mainly written by John Lennon, with Paul McCartney primarily contributing the song's middle section.

Lennon's lyrics were mainly inspired by contemporary newspaper articles, including a report about the death of Guinness heir Tara Browne in a car crash. The recording includes two famous orchestral glissando passages performed by a 40-piece orchestra, and the final piano chord, played by Lennon, McCartney, Ringo Starr, and Mal Evans, is one of the most famous in popular music. The song is frequently ranked among the greatest of all time.
