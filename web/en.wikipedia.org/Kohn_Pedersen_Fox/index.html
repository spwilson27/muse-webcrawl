Kohn Pedersen Fox

Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates (KPF) is an international architecture firm founded in New York City in 1976 by A. Eugene Kohn, William Pedersen and Sheldon Fox. It is known for designing supertall skyscrapers and large mixed-use developments.

Founded1976, New York City
FoundersA. Eugene Kohn, William Pedersen, Sheldon Fox
FieldArchitecture, urban design

Notable projects

KPF designed the Shanghai World Financial Center, the International Commerce Centre in Hong Kong, One Vanderbilt in New York, and the Lotte World Tower in Seoul — among the tallest buildings in the world — as well as the Hudson Yards development and numerous airport terminals and university buildings.
