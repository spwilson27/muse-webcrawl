Lenzing AG

Lenzing AG is an Austrian company headquartered in Lenzing, Upper Austria, that is a leading global producer of wood-based cellulose fibres. Its brands include TENCEL (lyocell and modal fibres), LENZING viscose and VEOCEL fibres for nonwoven products.

History and operations

Founded in 1938 as a pulp and fibre producer, Lenzing developed the environmentally improved lyocell process and operates production sites in Europe, Asia and the Americas. Its fibres are used in clothing, home textiles and hygiene products, and the company emphasises sustainable forestry and closed-loop manufacturing.
