Caravan (towed trailer)

Caravan (towed trailer)
Also calledTravel trailer (North America)
UseRecreational accommodation, touring
FacilitiesSleeping, kitchen, sometimes toilet and shower

A caravan, in British and Commonwealth usage, is a trailer towed behind a road vehicle that provides a place to sleep and live, used especially for holidays. In North America the equivalent term is travel trailer. Caravans range from small two-berth models with a basic kitchen to large twin-axle units with full bathrooms.

History

Leisure caravanning grew from horse-drawn vans used by travelling showmen and by early motoring enthusiasts in the early 1900s. Purpose-built touring caravans proliferated after the Second World War, supported by caravan clubs and site networks.

Use

Touring caravans are taken from site to site, while static caravans remain on holiday parks as semi-permanent holiday homes. Towing requires attention to weight limits, nose weight, and licensing rules that vary by country.
