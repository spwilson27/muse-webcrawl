Avengers (comics)

Publisher: Marvel Comics
First appearance: The Avengers #1 (September 1963)
Created by: Stan Lee, Jack Kirby

The Avengers are a team of superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, the team made its debut in The Avengers #1 (cover-dated September 1963). Labeled "Earth's Mightiest Heroes", the original Avengers consisted of Iron Man, Ant-Man, Hulk, Thor and the Wasp.

Publication history

Captain America was discovered trapped in ice in issue #4 and joined the team, which quickly developed a rotating roster drawn from Marvel's superhero line. The Avengers fought villains including Loki, Ultron, Kang the Conqueror, and Thanos. Over the decades the franchise has expanded into numerous spin-off titles such as West Coast Avengers, New Avengers, and Mighty Avengers.

In other media

The Avengers are the central team of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, beginning with the 2012 film Marvel's The Avengers, which brought together Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye. Sequels include Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), Avengers: Infinity War (2018), and Avengers: Endgame (2019), the latter of which became one of the highest-grossing films of all time.
