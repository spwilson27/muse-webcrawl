Washington State Ferries (WSF) is a public ferry system located in Washington state in the United States. It is the largest ferry operator in the United States and among the largest in the world, serving Puget Sound and the San Juan Islands.

History

The state took over the private Puget Sound Navigation Company ("Black Ball Line") routes in 1951. The system is considered part of the state highway network, linking island and peninsula communities to the mainland.

Operations

WSF operates about twenty vessels on ten routes, carrying roughly 18–24 million passengers a year, including the busy Seattle–Bainbridge Island and Edmonds–Kingston crossings and the international route to Sidney, British Columbia (suspended in recent years).
