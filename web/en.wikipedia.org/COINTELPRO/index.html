COINTELPRO

COINTELPRO (a syllabic abbreviation of Counter Intelligence Program) was a series of covert and illegal projects conducted between 1956 and 1971 by the United States Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) aimed at surveilling, infiltrating, discrediting and disrupting American political organisations.

Targets and tactics

Targets included the Communist Party USA, socialist organisations, the civil rights movement — including Martin Luther King Jr. and the Southern Christian Leadership Conference — the Black Panther Party, the American Indian Movement and elements of the New Left. Tactics included infiltration, psychological warfare, harassment through the legal system, forged letters, and illegal force.

Exposure

The program was exposed in 1971 when activists burgled an FBI office in Media, Pennsylvania, and leaked documents to the press. Subsequent investigation by the Senate Church Committee (1975–76) documented extensive abuses and led to reforms and new guidelines restricting FBI domestic investigations.
