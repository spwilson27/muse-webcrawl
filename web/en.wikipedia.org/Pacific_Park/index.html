Pacific Park

Pacific Park

Location: Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica, California

Opened: 25 May 1996

Area: 2 acres (0.81 ha)

Signature rides: Pacific Wheel; West Coaster

Pacific Park is an oceanfront amusement park located in Santa Monica, California, on the Santa Monica Pier. Opened in 1996, it is a small, admission-free park (rides are individually ticketed or covered by unlimited wristbands) whose illuminated Ferris wheel has become one of the most recognisable symbols of the Santa Monica waterfront and of the western end of Route 66.

Attractions

The park's signature ride is the Pacific Wheel, a 130-foot (40 m) Ferris wheel carrying passengers some 85 feet above the pier with views over the Pacific Ocean and the Los Angeles coastline. Its current version, installed in 2008, is solar-powered and lit by more than 174,000 LED lights, which display animated light shows and special designs for holidays and events. The park's roller coaster is the steel West Coaster, which circles the park's perimeter. Other attractions have included a drop tower (Pacific Plunge), a swinging ship, bumper cars, a log flume-style family ride, miniature golf, midway games and an arcade.

History

Amusement piers have stood at Santa Monica since the early 20th century — Charles Looff's pier and the La Monica Ballroom drew crowds in the 1910s–20s — but the pier's amusement zone declined after the 1960s and storms in 1983 destroyed part of the structure. As part of the pier's restoration, Pacific Park was developed and opened on 25 May 1996, the first new amusement park on a pier on the US West Coast in decades. It has changed ownership several times, including purchase by EPR Properties and, in 2024, by SC Holdings.

In popular culture

Pacific Park and its wheel appear in numerous films, music videos and television programmes, and the wheel is frequently lit in support of causes and celebrations, or to mark events such as Earth Hour. It is also a prominent landmark visible from the surrounding beaches and the Palisades, and a common backdrop for photographs of the Santa Monica sunset.
