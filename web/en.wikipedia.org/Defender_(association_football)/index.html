Defender (association football)

PositionDefender
Main rolesCentre-back, full-back, wing-back, sweeper

In association football, a defender is an outfield player whose primary role is to stop opposition attacks and prevent the other team from scoring goals. Defenders play behind the midfielders and in front of the goalkeeper, forming the back line of the team's formation.

Positions

The centre-back (or central defender) guards the middle of the defence, marking opposing strikers and contesting aerial balls. Full-backs defend the flanks; in modern football they are also expected to advance and support attacks. Wing-backs, used in formations with three centre-backs, combine both duties along the whole flank. The sweeper (libero), once common, played behind the defensive line to cover through balls and distribute possession.

Skills

Defenders require tackling, marking, heading, positioning, and anticipation, as well as sufficient passing ability to begin attacks. Famous defenders include Franz Beckenbauer, Franco Baresi, Paolo Maldini, and Bobby Moore.
