AN/TPQ-37 Firefinder radar

TypeCounter-battery (weapon-locating) radar
OriginUnited States
ManufacturerHughes Aircraft (later Raytheon)
In serviceearly 1980s–present

The AN/TPQ-37 Firefinder radar is a mobile radar system developed by Hughes Aircraft (now part of RTX) that detects and tracks incoming artillery, mortar, and rocket fire to determine the point of origin for counter-battery fire.

Operation

The system uses a phased-array antenna to scan a sector of the horizon. When it detects a projectile in flight, computers extrapolate the trajectory back to the firing position, which can then be engaged by friendly artillery. The TPQ-37 is the longer-range member of the Firefinder family, complementing the shorter-range AN/TPQ-36.

Service

Introduced with the U.S. Army in the early 1980s, the TPQ-37 has been exported to numerous countries and used in conflicts including the Gulf War. It is gradually being supplemented and replaced by newer systems such as the AN/TPQ-53.
