AN/TPQ-53

TypeCounterfire target acquisition radar
OriginUnited States
ManufacturerLockheed Martin
NicknameQ-53

The AN/TPQ-53, commonly called the Q-53, is a mobile active electronically scanned array counter-battery radar system manufactured by Lockheed Martin for the United States Army.

Description

Mounted on a 5-ton truck, the Q-53 detects, classifies, and tracks incoming indirect fire — artillery, mortars, and rockets — and computes their points of origin for counterfire. It can operate in 90-degree or full 360-degree scan modes and was designed to replace the older AN/TPQ-36 and AN/TPQ-37 Firefinder radars in a single system.

Service

The system was fast-tracked to deployment in Iraq and Afghanistan in the early 2010s under the EQ-36 program before receiving its formal designation. It has since been exported, including to Ukraine.
