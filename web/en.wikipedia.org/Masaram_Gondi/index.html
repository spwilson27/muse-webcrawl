Masaram Gondi

Masaram Gondi is a script used to write the Gondi language, a Dravidian language spoken by the Gond people of central India. It was created in 1918 by Munshi Mangal Singh Masaram, a Gond from Balaghat district in present-day Madhya Pradesh.

Characteristics

Masaram Gondi is an abugida written from left to right, with characters for vowels, consonants, and conjuncts, largely inspired in appearance by other Indic scripts of the region. Unlike the older Gunjala Gondi script, which had fallen out of use, Masaram's script was taught locally and used in manuscripts and signage, and it has seen a revival movement among Gond communities.

Encoding

The script was encoded in the Unicode Standard in version 11.0 (2018), in the block U+11D00–U+11D5F, enabling its use in digital text and publishing.
