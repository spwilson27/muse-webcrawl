Tencent QQ

Tencent QQ
Developer: Tencent
Initial release: February 11, 1999
Platform: Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, Linux
Type: Instant messaging client

Tencent QQ, popularly known as QQ, is an instant messaging software service and web portal developed by the Chinese technology company Tencent. QQ offers online social games, music, shopping, microblogging, movies and group and voice chat, and was once the world's largest online community.

History

QQ was first released in February 1999 under the name OICQ, modelled on the early messaging service ICQ. After a trademark dispute with AOL, the name was changed to QQ. The service grew explosively in China in the 2000s; at its peak it recorded over 800 million monthly active accounts. Its mascot is a penguin wearing a red scarf.

Features and decline

Beyond messaging, QQ includes Qzone (a social networking and blogging platform), QQ Mail, games and the QQ Wallet payment service. Since the 2010s, QQ's role as Tencent's flagship social product has been partly eclipsed by WeChat, though QQ remains widely used, especially among younger users in China.
