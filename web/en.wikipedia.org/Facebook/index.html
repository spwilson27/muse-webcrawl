Facebook

Facebook is a social networking service owned by the American technology company Meta Platforms. Founded in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg with fellow Harvard University students Eduardo Saverin, Andrew McCollum, Dustin Moskovitz and Chris Hughes, it began as a directory for Harvard students, expanded to other universities, and opened to the general public in 2006. The name comes from the "face books" of student directories distributed by American universities. Facebook grew to become the world's largest social network, with around three billion monthly active users, and is one of the most valuable companies in the world. Users can create profiles, add friends, post text, photos and video, join groups, and follow pages; the company earns most of its revenue from advertising.

History

Zuckerberg launched "TheFacebook" from his dormitory room on 4 February 2004. The site spread rapidly to other colleges and, after opening to everyone aged 13 and over in 2006 and launching the News Feed, to explosive global growth. Facebook held its initial public offering in May 2012. The parent company acquired Instagram in 2012 and WhatsApp in 2014, and in 2021 rebranded itself as Meta Platforms, with Facebook remaining its flagship social network.

Features

The service centres on user profiles and the News Feed, an algorithmically ranked stream of posts from friends, groups and followed pages. Other features include Facebook Messenger, Groups, Marketplace for local buying and selling, Watch for video, Events, and the Like button and reactions. The platform is accessible on the web and as mobile apps in many languages.

Criticism

Facebook has been widely criticised over privacy practices, notably the Cambridge Analytica data scandal of 2018; over the spread of misinformation and hate speech, including its role in violence in Myanmar; over effects on users' mental health; and over its market power, which has drawn antitrust scrutiny in the United States and Europe. The company has responded with expanded content moderation, privacy controls and transparency reports, while disputes over its practices continue.
