Rolling in the Deep

"Rolling in the Deep"
Artist: Adele
Album: 21
Released: 29 November 2010
Genre: Soul, pop, blues
Songwriters: Adele Adkins, Paul Epworth
Producer: Paul Epworth

"Rolling in the Deep" is a song recorded by English singer-songwriter Adele for her second studio album, 21 (2011). It was released as the lead single from the album on 29 November 2010. The song was written by Adele and Paul Epworth, who also produced it. It has been described as a "dark blues-y gospel disco tune" and a breakup song driven by anger at a former lover.

Composition and reception

Written the day after Adele's breakup with her boyfriend, the song pairs a stomping piano-driven rhythm and handclaps with a vocal performance that critics widely praised. "Rolling in the Deep" received acclaim for its production and Adele's singing, and is frequently ranked among the best songs of 2011 and of the decade.

Commercial performance

The song was a global hit, reaching number one in 12 countries, including seven weeks atop the US Billboard Hot 100. It was the best-selling digital song of 2011 in the United States and became one of the best-selling digital singles of all time, with sales exceeding 20 million copies worldwide. The parent album 21 was the best-selling album of both 2011 and 2012.

Accolades

At the 54th Annual Grammy Awards in 2012, "Rolling in the Deep" won Record of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best Short Form Music Video.

Legacy and covers

The song has been covered by numerous artists, including Linkin Park, Aretha Franklin, and the cast of Glee, and a live version by rock band Greta Van Fleet and others circulated widely online. It is considered Adele's signature song along with "Someone Like You".
