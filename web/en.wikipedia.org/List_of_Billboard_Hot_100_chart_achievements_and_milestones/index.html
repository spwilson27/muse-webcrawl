List of Billboard Hot 100 chart achievements and milestones

The Billboard Hot 100 is the main song chart of the American music industry, published weekly by Billboard magazine since 1958. This article collects notable achievements and milestones by songs and artists on the chart.

Most total weeks on the chart

Glass Animals' "Heat Waves" spent 91 weeks on the Hot 100, a record at the time, followed by long runs for songs such as The Weeknd's "Blinding Lights" and Imagine Dragons' "Radioactive".

Artists with the most number-one songs

The Beatles lead with 20 number-one singles, followed by Mariah Carey with 19, and artists including Rihanna, Michael Jackson, Drake, Madonna, Elvis Presley, Whitney Houston, Taylor Swift and Stevie Wonder among those with ten or more.

Debuts at number one

Since Michael Jackson's "You Are Not Alone" in 1995, dozens of songs have debuted atop the Hot 100, a feat that became common in the streaming era with artists such as Drake, Ariana Grande, Taylor Swift and BTS.
