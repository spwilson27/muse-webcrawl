Marlboro

Marlboro is an American brand of cigarettes, currently owned and manufactured by Philip Morris USA in the United States and by Philip Morris International outside it. It is the world's best-selling cigarette brand.

The brand was introduced in 1924, originally marketed to women, and was relaunched in the 1950s with the iconic "Marlboro Man" cowboy advertising campaign created by Leo Burnett, transforming it into a symbol of rugged masculinity.

The brand was named after Great Marlborough Street in London, where its first factory was located.
