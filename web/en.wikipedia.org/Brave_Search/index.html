Brave Search

Brave Search is a search engine developed by Brave Software, Inc. and released in beta in March 2021 and out of beta in June 2022. It is the default search engine in the Brave browser in many regions and is distinguished by relying primarily on its own independent web index rather than on results from Google or Bing.

History

Brave Search grew out of Brave's acquisition of Tailcat, an open search engine developed by the team behind Cliqz. It launched publicly in beta in June 2021 and exited beta a year later with the addition of features such as "Goggles", which let users apply their own ranking rules to results.

Features

The engine offers ad-supported and premium ad-free tiers. In 2023 it added an AI-powered "Summarizer" and later "Answer with AI" features that generate concise answers with citations. Brave states that it does not track users or their searches.
