St Stephen Walbrook
Location: City of London
Architect: Christopher Wren
Built: 1672–1679
Feature: Early English dome

St Stephen Walbrook is a Church of England parish church in the City of London, near the Mansion House and the buried Walbrook river. Rebuilt by Sir Christopher Wren after the Great Fire of London, it is renowned for its dome.

Architecture

Constructed 1672–1679, the church contains one of Wren's most inventive interiors: a dome carried on an arrangement of columns and arches, considered a prototype for St Paul's Cathedral. Art historian Nikolaus Pevsner counted it among England's finest interiors.

Samaritans

In 1953 the rector, Chad Varah, founded the Samaritans telephone helpline from the church, using its phone line to offer support to people in distress.
