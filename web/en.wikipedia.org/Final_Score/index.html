Final Score

Final Score is a British television programme produced by BBC Sport. It is shown on Saturday afternoons, presenting football results as they come in from matches across the United Kingdom, along with reports from reporters at grounds, interviews, and the classified results read in full at the end of the afternoon's fixtures.

Format

The programme typically begins during the second half of the day's matches and continues until after full time. A presenter in the studio anchors coverage while reporters at stadiums around the country report goals and incidents as they happen. A scrolling on-screen graphic, historically known as the vidiprinter, displays scores and goalscorers in real time. The programme concludes with the classified football results, read aloud in full — a tradition inherited from earlier BBC results services.

History

A football results service has been part of BBC Saturday afternoon television since the late 1950s, originally as part of Grandstand. Final Score became a standalone programme when Grandstand ended in 2007. Versions of the programme are also produced for viewers in Scotland and Wales covering their domestic leagues in greater depth.
