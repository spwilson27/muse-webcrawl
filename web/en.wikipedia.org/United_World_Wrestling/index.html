United World Wrestling

United World Wrestling (UWW) is the international governing body for the sport of amateur wrestling, overseeing Greco-Roman and freestyle wrestling for men and women, as well as associated styles such as beach wrestling and grappling. It is headquartered in Corsier-sur-Vevey, Switzerland.

History

The federation was founded in 1912 and long known by its French acronym FILA. Wrestling faced a crisis in 2013 when the International Olympic Committee voted to remove it from the core Olympic program; reforms — including rule changes to reward action, more women's weight classes and governance changes under acting president Nenad Lalović — won reinstatement within the year. The organization rebranded as United World Wrestling in 2014.

Competitions

UWW sanctions the annual World Championships, continental championships, ranking series events and the wrestling tournaments of the Olympic Games in cooperation with the IOC. It also sets weight classes, rules and anti-doping standards for the sport worldwide.
