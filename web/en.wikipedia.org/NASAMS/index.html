NASAMS

NASAMS (Norwegian Advanced Surface-to-Air Missile System, also known as the National Advanced Surface-to-Air Missile System) is a distributed and networked short- to medium-range ground-based air defence system developed by Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace and Raytheon. It was the first surface-based application for the AIM-120 AMRAAM air-to-air missile.

Type: Surface-to-air missile system

Place of origin: Norway / United States

In service: 1994–present

Missiles: AMRAAM, AMRAAM-ER, AIM-9X Sidewinder

Design

A standard NASAMS battery consists of a command post (Fire Distribution Center), an AN/MPQ-64 Sentinel 3D radar, passive electro-optical sensors, and a number of canister launcher vehicles, linked as an open, networked system. The system's modular design allows launchers to be dispersed over a wide area while targets are engaged cooperatively. Later versions can fire the extended-range AMRAAM-ER and the AIM-9X Sidewinder as well.

Service

NASAMS entered Norwegian service in 1994 and has been exported widely, including to the United States, where it defends the National Capital Region around Washington, D.C. Since 2022, systems supplied by the United States and Norway have been used by Ukraine to defend cities against missile and drone attacks.
