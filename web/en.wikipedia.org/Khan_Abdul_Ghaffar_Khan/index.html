Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan

Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (c. 6 February 1890 – 20 January 1988), known as Bacha Khan and the "Frontier Gandhi", was a Pashtun independence activist from the North-West Frontier Province who campaigned against British rule in India through nonviolence.

Life

He founded the Khudai Khidmatgar ("Red Shirts") movement in 1929 and was a close ally of Mahatma Gandhi. Opposing the partition of India, he spent many years in prison under both British and Pakistani governments, and received India's Bharat Ratna in 1987.
