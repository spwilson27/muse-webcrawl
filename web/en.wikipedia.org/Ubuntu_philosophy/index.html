Ubuntu (philosophy)

Ubuntu (Zulu pronunciation: [ùɓúntʰù]) is a Nguni Bantu term meaning "humanity". It is sometimes translated as "I am because we are" (also "I am because you are"), or "humanity towards others". Ubuntu is a traditional African philosophy emphasizing communal relationships, compassion, and shared humanity.

Meaning

The concept holds that a person's humanity is expressed through their relationships with others. Maxims such as umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu — "a person is a person through other people" — capture the idea that community is foundational to personhood.

Influence

Ubuntu influenced South Africa's post-apartheid reconciliation process, including the Truth and Reconciliation Commission chaired by Archbishop Desmond Tutu, and has informed African jurisprudence, theology, and management theory.
