Maykop

Maykop is the capital city of the Republic of Adygea in southern Russia, located on the right bank of the Belaya River. Its name in Adyghe, Мыекъуапэ, means "valley of apple trees." The population is roughly 140,000.

History

The city was founded in 1857 as a Russian military fortress during the Caucasian War. Nearby is the famous Maykop kurgan, a Bronze Age burial mound excavated in 1897 which gave its name to the early Bronze Age Maykop culture of the North Caucasus (c. 3700–3000 BCE).

Economy

Maykop is a modest industrial and educational centre for the republic, with food processing, timber, and Adyghe State University among its main institutions.
