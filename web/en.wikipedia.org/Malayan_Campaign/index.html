Malayan Campaign

The Malayan Campaign was a military campaign fought by Allied and Axis forces in British Malaya from 8 December 1941 to 31 January 1942 during the Second World War. Japanese forces invaded from Thailand and advanced rapidly down the Malay Peninsula toward Singapore.

Course

Japanese troops under General Tomoyuki Yamashita landed at Kota Bharu and in southern Thailand, quickly gaining air superiority. The sinking of the British battleship Prince of Wales and battlecruiser Repulse on 10 December removed Allied naval power. Using jungle tracks, bicycles and light tanks, the Japanese outflanked successive British, Indian and Australian defensive lines.

Outcome

Allied forces withdrew across the Johor Strait to Singapore by 31 January 1942. The subsequent Battle of Singapore ended on 15 February 1942 with one of the largest surrenders in British military history. Malaya remained under Japanese occupation until 1945.
