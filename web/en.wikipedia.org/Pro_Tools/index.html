Pro Tools is a digital audio workstation (DAW) developed and released by Avid Technology for Microsoft Windows and macOS. It is used for music creation and production, sound for picture, audio post-production, mixing, and mastering, and is widely regarded as an industry standard in professional recording studios.

History

Pro Tools grew out of Sound Tools and Sound Designer, developed by Evan Brooks and Peter Gotcher, who founded Digidesign in 1984. The first version of Pro Tools, offering four tracks of digital audio recording and editing, was released in 1991. Digidesign was acquired by Avid in 1995. Successive generations introduced dedicated DSP hardware systems (HD, HDX) alongside native software versions.

Features

Pro Tools provides non-linear, non-destructive audio and MIDI editing, a mixer environment, and support for plug-ins in Avid's AAX format (and historically RTAS and TDM). Editions have included Pro Tools Ultimate for large-scale post-production and Pro Tools Intro, a free entry-level version.
