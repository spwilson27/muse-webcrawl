Michael William Krzyzewski (born 13 February 1947), known as Coach K, is an American former college basketball coach who led the Duke Blue Devils from 1980 to 2022. With 1,202 career victories he is the winningest coach in NCAA Division I men's basketball history, and his five national championships rank second only to John Wooden. He also coached the United States men's national team to three Olympic gold medals (2008, 2012, 2016).

Early life and playing career

Krzyzewski, the son of Polish immigrants, grew up in Chicago. He played point guard at West Point under Bob Knight and served as a U.S. Army officer before beginning his coaching career, first as an assistant to Knight at Indiana and then as head coach at Army.

Duke tenure

Appointed at Duke in 1980, Krzyzewski built the program into a perennial power. His teams reached 13 Final Fours, won back-to-back championships in 1991–92 behind Christian Laettner and Grant Hill, and added titles in 2001, 2010, and 2015. He became the first Division I men's coach to reach 1,000 wins in 2015 and announced his retirement effective after the 2021–22 season, succeeded by Jon Scheyer.

National team and honors

As head coach of Team USA, Krzyzewski compiled a 75–1 record, winning the 2007 FIBA Americas Championship, the 2010 and 2014 World Cups, and three Olympic golds. He was inducted into the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame in 2001.
