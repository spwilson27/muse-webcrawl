Mass Effect Legendary Edition

2021 remaster compilation of the Mass Effect trilogy

Mass Effect Legendary Edition is a 2021 compilation of remastered versions of the first three games in BioWare's Mass Effect series — Mass Effect (2007), Mass Effect 2 (2010) and Mass Effect 3 (2012) — together with nearly all of their single-player downloadable content. Developed by BioWare with assistance from Abstraction Games and Blind Squirrel Games, and published by Electronic Arts, it was released on 14 May 2021 for PlayStation 4, Windows and Xbox One.

Enhancements

The remasters feature improved textures, shaders, lighting and technical performance, with support for 4K resolution. The first game received the most extensive changes, including reworked combat and aiming, faster elevators, improved handling of the Mako ground vehicle, and a unified character creator carried across the trilogy. Save files can be imported from one game to the next, preserving the series' signature branching narrative. The multiplayer mode of Mass Effect 3 was not included, as was the Pinnacle Station downloadable content, whose source code had been lost.

Reception

The compilation was well received, with reviewers praising the preservation of the trilogy in a single package and the scale of the first game's overhaul, while noting occasional bugs and modest changes to the later entries.
