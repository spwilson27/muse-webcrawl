Stroke order

Stroke order
Used for: Chinese characters, kanji, hanja
General rule: Top to bottom, left to right

Stroke order is the order in which the strokes of a Chinese character (or derivative characters such as Japanese kanji and Korean hanja) are written. A standard stroke order promotes legibility, speed, correct proportions and ease of memorisation, and is taught to schoolchildren and calligraphers.

General principles

Common rules include: write top to bottom and left to right; horizontal strokes before vertical ones that cross them; centre before sides; enclosures closed last; and dots and minor strokes often last. National standards differ slightly between mainland China, Taiwan, Japan and Korea.

In computing

Stroke order and stroke count are used in dictionary lookup, handwriting recognition and input methods, where writing in the taught order improves recognition accuracy.
