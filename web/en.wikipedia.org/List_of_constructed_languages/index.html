List of constructed languages

The following is a list of constructed languages — languages that have been consciously devised rather than evolving naturally. They are organised by their purpose: international auxiliary languages, engineered languages (philosophical and logical), fictional languages, and others.

Auxiliary languages

  • Esperanto (1887, L. L. Zamenhof) — the most widely spoken constructed language.
  • Ido, Interlingua, Novial, Occidental (Interlingue), Volapük (1879)
  • Toki Pona (2001) — a minimalist language of about 120–140 words, also classed as philosophical.

Engineered and philosophical languages

  • Logban and Lojban — logical languages based on predicate logic.
  • Ithkuil — an extremely complex experimental language.
  • Solresol — a musical language based on the solfège syllables.
  • Ro, aUI, Wilkins's philosophical language (1668)

Fictional languages

  • Quenya and Sindarin — Elvish languages of J. R. R. Tolkien's legendarium.
  • Klingon — created by Marc Okrand for Star Trek.
  • Dothraki and High Valyrian — created by David J. Peterson for Game of Thrones.
  • Na'vi — created by Paul Frommer for Avatar.
  • Newspeak — the fictional language of Orwell's Nineteen Eighty-Four.
