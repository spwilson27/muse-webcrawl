American football

American football, known simply as football in the United States and Canada, is a team sport played by two teams of eleven players on a rectangular field 120 yards long (including two 10-yard end zones) with goalposts at each end. Teams score points by advancing an oval ball into the opposing end zone (a touchdown, worth six points) or kicking it through the goalposts (a field goal, worth three). The offense has four attempts ("downs") to advance at least ten yards; failure turns the ball over to the other team.

History

The sport evolved from association football and rugby football in the late nineteenth century at American colleges. Walter Camp of Yale, the "Father of American Football," introduced the line of scrimmage, downs, and the snap in the 1880s. The forward pass was legalised in 1906, in part to make the brutal running game safer after a spate of deaths. Professional football grew with the founding of the National Football League in 1920; the merger with the American Football League and the first Super Bowl in 1967 cemented its place as the most popular sport in the United States.

Gameplay

Each team fields separate offensive, defensive, and special-teams units. The quarterback leads the offense, either handing off to a running back or passing to receivers; the defense attempts tackles, sacks, and turnovers. Specialist plays include punts, kickoffs, extra points, and two-point conversions. Games consist of four 15-minute quarters, though stoppages make them last around three hours.

Popularity and culture

The NFL's championship game, the Super Bowl, is among the most-watched annual television events in the world, and college football commands enormous followings, especially in the South and Midwest. The sport is also played at high school level across the country, in arena and flag variants, and professionally in Canada (Canadian football is a related but distinct code). Concerns over head injuries and chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) have become a major issue for the sport in the twenty-first century.
