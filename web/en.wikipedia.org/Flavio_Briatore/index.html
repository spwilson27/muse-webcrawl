Flavio Briatore
Born: 12 April 1950, Verzuolo, Italy
Occupation: Businessman, Formula One manager

Flavio Briatore (born 12 April 1950) is an Italian businessman and former managing director of the Benetton and Renault Formula One teams.

Career

Briatore began in the fashion industry with Luciano Benetton, opening Benetton franchises, before being appointed commercial director of the Benetton Formula One team in the late 1980s.

Benetton and Renault

As team boss he oversaw Michael Schumacher's drivers' championships in 1994 and 1995 with Benetton, and Fernando Alonso's titles with Renault in 2005 and 2006.

Controversy

In 2009 Briatore received a lifetime ban from FIA-sanctioned events for his role in the 2008 Singapore Grand Prix "Crashgate" scandal, in which driver Nelson Piquet Jr. crashed deliberately to benefit Alonso. A French court overturned the ban in 2010.
