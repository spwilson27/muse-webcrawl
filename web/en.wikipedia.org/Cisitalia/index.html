Cisitalia

Cisitalia
Founded: 1946, Turin, Italy
Founder: Piero Dusio
Industry: automotive

Cisitalia was an Italian sports car and racing car manufacturer founded in Turin in 1946 by industrialist Piero Dusio. The name is an abbreviation of Compagnia Industriale Sportiva Italia.

Cars

Using Fiat 1100 components, Cisitalia produced the D46 single-seater racing car and the celebrated 202 coupé, designed by Pinin Farina and introduced in 1947. The Cisitalia 202, with its flowing one-piece bodywork, is considered a landmark of automotive design; an example is in the collection of the Museum of Modern Art in New York.

Grand Prix project and decline

Dusio commissioned Ferdinand Porsche's design office to create the ambitious mid-engined, four-wheel-drive Cisitalia 360 Grand Prix car, but the project's costs bankrupted the company. Dusio moved operations to Argentina, and Cisitalia production in Italy dwindled; the firm folded in the early 1960s.
