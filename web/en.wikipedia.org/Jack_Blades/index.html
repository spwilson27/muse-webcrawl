Jack Blades

Jack Charles Blades (born 1954) is an American rock musician best known as the bassist and vocalist of the band Night Ranger, and as a member of the supergroups Damn Yankees and Shaw Blades.

Born1954, Palm Desert, California, U.S.
GenresHard rock, AOR
InstrumentsBass guitar, vocals
Associated actsNight Ranger, Damn Yankees, Shaw Blades

Career

Blades first played in the funk band Rubicon before co-founding Ranger in San Francisco, which became Night Ranger. The band scored hits in the 1980s including "(You Can Still) Rock in America" and the power ballad "Sister Christian". In 1989 he formed Damn Yankees with Ted Nugent and Tommy Shaw, achieving success with "High Enough".

Songwriting

Blades has also written songs for other artists, including Aerosmith, Alice Cooper and Ozzy Osbourne, and released solo albums as well as recordings with Tommy Shaw under the name Shaw Blades.
