Cessna

The Cessna Aircraft Company is an American general aviation aircraft manufacturer headquartered in Wichita, Kansas. Founded in 1927 by Clyde Cessna, it became famous for its small single-engine piston aircraft and later for the Citation line of business jets. Since 2014 its aircraft have been marketed under Textron Aviation, alongside Beechcraft.

History

Clyde Cessna built his first aircraft in 1911 and founded the company in 1927. After the Second World War, Cessna dominated the light aircraft market with models such as the 140, 150, 170 and 180. The Cessna 172 Skyhawk, introduced in 1956, became the most produced aircraft in history, with more than 45,000 built. Textron acquired Cessna in 1992.

Notable aircraft

Besides the 172, well-known types include the 152 trainer, the 182 Skylane, the 208 Caravan turboprop, and the Citation family of jets, including the Citation Mustang, CJ series and Latitude.
