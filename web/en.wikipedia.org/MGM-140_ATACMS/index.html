The MGM-140 Army Tactical Missile System (ATACMS; pronounced "attack-ems") is a supersonic tactical ballistic missile designed and manufactured by the American defense company Lockheed Martin. It is fired from the M270 Multiple Launch Rocket System (MLRS) and the M142 HIMARS.

Development and service

The system grew out of the 1980s Assault Breaker program and entered service in 1991, seeing its first combat use during Operation Desert Storm, where dozens of missiles were fired against Iraqi air defenses and logistics. Variants have carried cluster submunitions (Block I, IA) or a unitary high-explosive warhead (Block IVA / M57), with ranges from about 140 km to 300 km.

Recent use

The United States supplied ATACMS to Ukraine in 2023 during the Russo-Ukrainian War, initially providing older cluster-munition variants and later longer-range unitary versions, which were used against airfields and air-defense systems in occupied territory.
