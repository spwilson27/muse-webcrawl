Election fraud

Election fraud, sometimes called electoral fraud, is illegal interference with the process of an election. Acts of fraud affect vote counts and can change an election result, whether by increasing the vote share of a favored candidate, depressing the vote share of rivals, or both.

Methods

Methods include voter impersonation, ballot stuffing, tampering with voting machines or counts, voter intimidation and coercion, vote buying, disenfranchisement through misleading information or purges, gerrymandering of districts, and falsification of results by officials. Fraud differs from broader electoral manipulation such as biased media coverage or legal but unfair rules.

Prevention

Safeguards include independent election administration, transparent counting, paper audit trails, risk-limiting audits, election observers, voter identification rules, and criminal penalties. The scale and impact of fraud is often contested in political debate.
