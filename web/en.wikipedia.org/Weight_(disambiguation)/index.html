Weight (disambiguation)

Weight most commonly refers to:

  • Weight, the force of gravity acting on a mass, in physics
  • Human body weight
  • A lump of mass used for measurement, e.g. in a balance or in weight training

Weight may also refer to:

Science and mathematics

  • Weight (representation theory), a type of function in Lie theory
  • Weight (string theory)
  • Weighted graph and weight functions in mathematics and statistics
  • Weight (typography), the thickness of strokes in a typeface
  • Specific weight, weight per unit volume

Other uses

  • "Weight" (song), by various artists
  • Weights, training equipment used in strength training and in the sport of weightlifting
  • Paperweight, a small solid object used to keep papers in place
  • Weight, the relative importance assigned to evidence or testimony in law
