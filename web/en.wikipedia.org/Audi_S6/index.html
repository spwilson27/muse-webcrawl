Audi S6

Manufacturer: Audi AG
Production: 1994–present
Class: Executive car

The Audi S6 is the sports version of the Audi A6 executive car, positioned below the RS 6. It has been produced across the C4 to C8 generations as a sedan and Avant estate with quattro all-wheel drive.

Powertrains

Early models used turbocharged five-cylinder and V8 engines. The C6 S6 featured a 5.2-litre V10 shared in concept with Lamborghini's V10. Later generations moved to a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8, and the C8 adopted a V6 — petrol in some markets and a torque-rich diesel in Europe.
