Amino acid

Amino acids are organic compounds containing amino and carboxylic acid functional groups, the building blocks of proteins. Twenty standard amino acids are encoded by the genetic code; each has a central alpha carbon bearing a distinctive side chain (R group).

Amino acid
Standard: 20 (+ selenocysteine, pyrrolysine)
Essential in humans: 9
Link: Peptide bond

Classification

By side chain: nonpolar (glycine, alanine, leucine), polar (serine, asparagine), acidic (glutamate, aspartate), basic (lysine, arginine, histidine), aromatic (phenylalanine, tryptophan, tyrosine). Nine are essential — humans cannot synthesise them.

Proteins

Amino acids link by peptide bonds into polypeptides, a type of polymer, which fold into secondary structures like the alpha helix and beta sheet.
