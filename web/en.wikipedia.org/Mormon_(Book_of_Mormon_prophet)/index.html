Mormon (Book of Mormon prophet)

In the Book of Mormon, Mormon is a Nephite prophet-historian and military leader who compiled and abridged the record that gives the book its name. According to the text, Mormon lived in the 4th and 5th centuries AD and edited the large plates of Nephi into the golden plates later delivered to his son Moroni.

Religious workBook of Mormon
RoleProphet, historian, general
SonMoroni

Narrative

The Book of Mormon states that Mormon was appointed custodian of the Nephite records as a boy by the prophet Ammaron, became a military commander at fifteen, and led the Nephite armies in their final wars against the Lamanites. Wounded and eventually killed after the great battle at Cumorah, he entrusted the abridged plates to his son Moroni, who completed and buried them.

Books attributed to him

The Words of Mormon and the Book of Mormon within the larger volume are attributed to his editorial voice, and his abridgment constitutes most of the text from Mosiah through Mormon chapter 7.
