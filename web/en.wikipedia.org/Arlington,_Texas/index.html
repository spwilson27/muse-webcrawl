Arlington is a city in Tarrant County, Texas, United States, situated between Dallas and Fort Worth in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex. It is one of the largest cities in the United States without a comprehensive public transit system.

History

Arlington was founded in 1876 when the Texas and Pacific Railway came through the area and was named after General Robert E. Lee's hometown of Arlington, Virginia. It grew from a farming community into a manufacturing centre in the 20th century, boosted by a General Motors assembly plant that opened in 1954.

Attractions

Arlington hosts the entertainment district containing AT&T Stadium (home of the Dallas Cowboys), Globe Life Field (home of the Texas Rangers of Major League Baseball), Six Flags Over Texas (opened 1961), and Hurricane Harbor water park.

Education

The University of Texas at Arlington, founded in 1895, is a major research university with tens of thousands of students.
