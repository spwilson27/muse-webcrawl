Spray painting

Spray painting is a painting technique in which a device sprays coating material (paint, ink, varnish, etc.) through the air onto a surface. The most common types employ compressed gas—usually air—to atomize and direct the paint particles.

Methods

Spray guns evolved from airbrushes; larger equipment serves industrial coating lines, automotive finishing and construction. Airless sprayers pump paint at high pressure, while electrostatic spraying charges particles to improve transfer efficiency. Aerosol cans provide a portable, propellant-driven form used for small jobs and graffiti.

Safety and environment

Overspray and solvent vapours require ventilation, respirators and booths with filtration; regulations limit volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions from spray operations.
