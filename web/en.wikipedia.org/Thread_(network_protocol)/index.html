Thread (network protocol)

Thread
TypeWireless mesh network protocol
BasisIEEE 802.15.4, 6LoWPAN, IPv6
Founded2014 (Thread Group)

Thread is an IPv6-based, low-power mesh networking technology for Internet of things (IoT) products. Thread is based on the IEEE 802.15.4 radio standard in the 2.4 GHz band and uses 6LoWPAN to carry IPv6 traffic, allowing devices to communicate directly with one another and with the internet through a border router.

Overview

Thread networks are self-healing mesh networks with no single point of failure: if a router device drops out, others take over its role. Devices can act as sleepy end devices running for years on small batteries. The protocol includes security based on AES encryption and device commissioning via passwords.

History and adoption

The Thread Group was formed in 2014 by companies including Nest Labs, Samsung, ARM, and Silicon Labs. Thread later became the networking layer underlying the Matter (formerly Project CHIP) smart-home standard, launched in 2022 by the Connectivity Standards Alliance, and is supported in products from Apple, Google, and Amazon.
