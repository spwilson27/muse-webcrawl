Lion dance

Lion dance is a form of traditional dance in Chinese culture and other Asian countries in which performers mimic a lion's movements in a lion costume to bring good luck and fortune.

Performed typically during Chinese New Year, weddings and business openings, it is accompanied by drums, cymbals and gongs. Northern lions are hairier and often appear in pairs; southern lions, prominent in Cantonese tradition, feature a horned head and perform feats such as plucking the greens (cai qing) hung above doorways. Lion dance is distinct from dragon dance, which requires many more performers holding a long body on poles.
