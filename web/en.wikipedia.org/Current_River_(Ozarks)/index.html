Current River (Ozarks)

Illustration: Current River (Ozarks)

The Current River is a spring-fed river of about 184 miles (296 km) flowing through south-eastern Missouri and north-eastern Arkansas in the Ozark Mountains. Formed by the convergence of Pigeon Creek and Montauk Spring in Montauk State Park, it is renowned for its cold, clear water.

Ozark National Scenic Riverways

In 1964 the river, together with its tributary the Jacks Fork, became the first river system protected by the National Park Service as the Ozark National Scenic Riverways. Huge first-magnitude springs such as Big Spring, Round Spring, and Alley Spring discharge into it, making it one of the finest float streams in the United States.

Recreation

Canoeing, kayaking, tubing, fishing for rainbow trout and smallmouth bass, and camping draw hundreds of thousands of visitors each year. Landmarks along its banks include Welch Spring, Akers Ferry, and the Current River State Park.
