Outdoor ethics

Outdoor ethics is the set of principles guiding behaviour that minimises environmental impact during outdoor recreation. Its best-known formulation is Leave No Trace, a set of seven principles developed in the United States.

Leave No Trace principles

Plan ahead and prepare; travel and camp on durable surfaces; dispose of waste properly; leave what you find; minimise campfire impacts; respect wildlife; and be considerate of other visitors.

Related concepts

Outdoor ethics also encompasses catch-and-release fishing, staying on trails to prevent erosion, packing out litter, observing wildlife from a distance, and respecting closures protecting sensitive habitats and cultural sites.
