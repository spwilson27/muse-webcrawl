New Caledonian crow

The New Caledonian crow (Corvus moneduloides) is a medium-sized member of the crow family native to New Caledonia. It is one of the few non-primate species known to fashion and use tools, and is famous for its problem-solving abilities.

Tool use

These crows manufacture hooked tools from pandanus leaves and twigs, and use stick tools to extract insect larvae from holes and crevices. Individuals can solve metatool problems — using one tool to obtain another — and experiments suggest they reason about hidden causes. The species also shows cumulative tool culture, with tool designs varying between populations.

Description and habitat

The crow is all black with a glossy sheen, a straight bill adapted for tool handling, and forward-facing eyes giving binocular vision. It inhabits forests across Grande Terre and has been introduced to Maré Island.
