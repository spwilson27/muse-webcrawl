Naval Station Great Lakes

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Naval Station Great Lakes (NAVSTA Great Lakes) is the home of the United States Navy's only current boot camp, located near North Chicago, in Lake County, Illinois. It is the largest military installation in Illinois and the largest training station in the Navy. The base includes 1,153 buildings situated on 1,628 acres, with a further outlying property used for its small-craft and outdoor training.

History

Approved by Congress in 1904 and opened in 1911, the station was championed by President Theodore Roosevelt as a great inland training center. It expanded enormously in both world wars: during World War I it trained some 125,000 sailors, and during World War II more than a million recruits and service-school students passed through. Architects Jarvis Hunt designed the original campus, whose 39 original buildings are a National Historic District.

Training

Since the 1990s, when the recruit training commands at Orlando and San Diego closed, all enlisted Navy recruits have attended Recruit Training Command Great Lakes for roughly ten weeks of boot camp before reporting to follow-on schools, including the Naval Service Schools also hosted at Great Lakes. Every year approximately 38,000 recruits graduate. See also United States Navy.
