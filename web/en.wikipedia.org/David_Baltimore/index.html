David Baltimore

David Baltimore (7 March 1938 – 6 September 2025) was an American biologist who shared the 1975 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Howard Temin and Renato Dulbecco for discoveries concerning the interaction between tumour viruses and the genetic material of the cell, including the discovery of the enzyme reverse transcriptase in retroviruses.

Career

Baltimore devised the Baltimore classification, which groups viruses by their genome type and mode of messenger-RNA synthesis. He was a professor at MIT, president of Rockefeller University (1990–1991) and president of the California Institute of Technology (1997–2006), and helped found the Whitehead Institute. His career included the "Baltimore affair", a scientific-misconduct controversy over a 1986 paper of which he was a co-author; the fraud findings against his co-author were eventually overturned and Baltimore was exonerated, though he resigned from Rockefeller amid the dispute.
