Sandown Park is a horse racing course and leisure venue in Esher, Surrey, England, located on the southwestern outskirts of London. Opened in 1875, it was among the first courses in Britain to charge for admission and to be fully enclosed, and it remains one of the country's premier dual-purpose (flat and jump) racecourses.

Course

The right-handed oval track is about one mile five furlongs round, with a long home straight and an uphill finish that places a premium on stamina. The steeplechase course is noted for its series of testing fences down the back straight — the Railway fences — and the Pond fence. The sprint course runs diagonally across the main track.

Racing

Sandown's flat season is headed by the Group 1 Eclipse Stakes, run over a mile and a quarter in July since 1886 and open to horses aged three and older. The National Hunt programme includes the Tingle Creek Chase in December, the Grade 1 Celebration Chase and the Bet365 Gold Cup at the season-closing Jump Finale in April, and the Scilly Isles Novices' Chase. Famous winners at the course include Brigadier Gerard, Mill Reef, Sea The Stars, Desert Orchid and Sprinter Sacre.

Other uses

Beyond racing, Sandown Park hosts exhibitions, concerts, antique fairs and an annual ski and snowboard show, and its Eclipse Pavilion overlooks both the course and the London skyline.
