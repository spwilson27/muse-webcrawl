Enos (chimpanzee)

Enos
Species: Chimpanzee
Born: 1957
Died: 4 November 1962
Mission: Mercury-Atlas 5

Enos (1957–1962) was a chimpanzee who became the first chimpanzee to orbit the Earth, flying aboard NASA's Mercury-Atlas 5 mission on 29 November 1961.

Training

Enos was purchased from a farm in Miami and trained at Holloman Air Force Base alongside other chimpanzees including Ham, who had made a suborbital flight earlier in 1961. His name means "man" in Hebrew and Greek.

Flight

Enos completed two orbits in a flight lasting about 3 hours 21 minutes, performing tasks despite a malfunction that gave him erroneous electric shocks for correct responses. He landed safely and his flight paved the way for John Glenn's orbital mission in February 1962.

Death

Enos died of shigellosis-related dysentery in 1962 while under observation.
