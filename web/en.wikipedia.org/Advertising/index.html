Advertising

Advertising is the practice and techniques employed to bring attention to a product or service, typically through paid announcements in media such as television, radio, print, billboards and digital platforms. Its aim is to influence the purchasing behaviour of a target audience.

History

Advertising dates to ancient wall and papyrus notices; it expanded with printing, mass-circulation newspapers, and the rise of advertising agencies in the 19th century, then with radio, television and, from the 1990s, the internet — now the largest medium, dominated by search and social-media advertising.

Types and regulation

Forms include display, video, native, influencer and political advertising. Most countries regulate misleading claims, and restrict advertising of products such as tobacco and alcohol, and advertising directed at children.
