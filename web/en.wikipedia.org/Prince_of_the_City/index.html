Prince of the City

Prince of the City is a 1981 American epic neo-noir crime drama film directed by Sidney Lumet. It is based on the 1978 non-fiction book by Robert Daley, and stars Treat Williams as NYPD detective Daniel Ciello, who agrees to expose corruption in the police department while refusing to implicate his partners.

Plot

Ciello, a narcotics detective in the NYPD's Special Investigative Unit, becomes an informant for federal prosecutors investigating police corruption. His cooperation unravels his career and personal life as the circle of implication widens beyond his control.

Reception

The film was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay and is regarded as one of Lumet's major works on institutional corruption in New York City, alongside Serpico.
