Pagani Automobili S.p.A. is an Italian manufacturer of sports cars and carbon-fibre components, founded in 1992 by the Argentine-Italian engineer and entrepreneur Horacio Pagani. The company is based in San Cesario sul Panaro, near Modena, Italy, in the heart of the country's "Motor Valley". Pagani is known for extremely limited-production hypercars combining advanced composite materials with highly detailed, jewellery-like craftsmanship.

History

Horacio Pagani, who had managed Lamborghini's composites department, founded Pagani Composite Research in 1988 before establishing Pagani Automobili in 1992. Advised by Formula One champion Juan Manuel Fangio, the company arranged to use Mercedes-AMG V12 engines. Its first production car, the Zonda C12, debuted at the 1999 Geneva Motor Show. The Zonda remained in production in numerous special editions until 2019.

Models

The Zonda (1999–2019) was Pagani's first model, built in coupe and roadster forms and many one-off and limited editions, including the track-focused Zonda R and the final 760 series. Its successor, the Huayra (2011– ), uses a twin-turbocharged Mercedes-AMG V12 and active aerodynamics, and spawned the Huayra BC and Huayra R variants. The third model, the Utopia, was unveiled in 2022, pairing an 864 PS twin-turbo V12 with an optional seven-speed manual gearbox.

Modena Design

Through its subsidiary Modena Design, Pagani develops and manufactures carbon-fibre composites — including its proprietary Carbo-Titanium material — for its own cars and for other manufacturers in the automotive and aerospace industries.
