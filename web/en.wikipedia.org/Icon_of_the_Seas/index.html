Icon of the Seas

Operator Royal Caribbean International
Maiden voyage 27 January 2024
Tonnage 248,663 GT

Icon of the Seas is a cruise ship operated by Royal Caribbean International and the lead ship of the Icon class. On entering service in January 2024 she became the largest cruise ship in the world by gross tonnage, at 248,663 gross tonnes and about 365 metres in length.

Features

The ship can carry roughly 5,600 passengers at double occupancy, and up to about 7,600 at maximum capacity, served by a crew of around 2,350. She is organised into themed "neighbourhoods" and carries the largest water park at sea, as well as a large indoor aqua theatre under a glass dome. Icon of the Seas is powered by liquefied natural gas.

Service

Built by Meyer Turku in Finland, the ship sails Caribbean itineraries from Miami. Her sister ship Star of the Seas followed in 2025.
