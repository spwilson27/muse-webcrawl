Blackpink

Blackpink (Korean: 블랙핑크; stylized in all caps) is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment. The group consists of four members: Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. They debuted in August 2016 with the single album Square One, which featured "Whistle" and "Boombayah". Blackpink is the highest-charting female Korean act on the US Billboard Hot 100 and Billboard 200, and has been called the "biggest girl group in the world".

Career

The group broke numerous records with songs such as "Ddu-Du Ddu-Du" (2018), "Kill This Love" (2019), and "How You Like That" (2020). Their first studio album, The Album (2020), became the best-selling album by a Korean female act, and their second, Born Pink (2022), topped the Billboard 200 and produced the single "Pink Venom". Their Born Pink World Tour became the highest-grossing concert tour by a girl group, and in 2023 they became the first Asian act to headline the Coachella festival.

Artistry and influence

Blackpink's music blends EDM, hip hop, and trap with a "girl crush" concept of confidence and glamour. The members are major fashion ambassadors for luxury houses and among the most-followed musicians on social media. Their fandom is known as "Blink".

Members

Jisoo (lead vocalist), Jennie (rapper and vocalist), Rosé (main vocalist), and Lisa (main dancer and rapper) have each also released successful solo work.
