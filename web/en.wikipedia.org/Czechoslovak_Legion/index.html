Czechoslovak Legion

The Czechoslovak Legion (Czech: Československé legie) was a volunteer armed force composed predominantly of Czech and Slovak prisoners of war and deserters from the Austro-Hungarian Army, raised during the First World War to fight for the Allies in support of an independent Czechoslovak state. Legions were formed in Russia, France, and Italy. The Russian Legion — tens of thousands strong — became famous for its revolt against the Bolsheviks in 1918 and its epic journey along the Trans-Siberian Railway to Vladivostok.

Formation and Zborov

The Czech Company formed in Kiev in 1914 evolved into a regiment and then a corps, recruiting Czech and Slovak prisoners of war who preferred fighting Austria-Hungary to internment. At the Battle of Zborov (2 July 1917), during the Kerensky Offensive, Czechoslovak riflemen broke through Austro-Hungarian lines, a victory that won Allied recognition for the Czechoslovak cause and boosted recruitment. Tomáš Garrigue Masaryk's Czechoslovak National Council negotiated the Legion's status; in France and Italy, legions drawn from POWs and émigrés fought on the Western and Italian fronts.

Anabasis in Siberia

After the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (1918), the Legion was to evacuate via Vladivostok to fight in France. Clashes with Bolshevik authorities — sparked by the Chelyabinsk incident of May 1918 — escalated into open revolt. The Legion seized much of the Trans-Siberian Railway and several cities, held the Imperial gold reserve captured at Kazan, and became a major factor in the Russian Civil War's opening phase. Following Allied agreements, the Legion handed over the gold and Admiral Kolchak to the authorities in Irkutsk and was evacuated from Vladivostok in 1920.

Legacy

The Legion's exploits materially advanced international recognition of Czechoslovakia, proclaimed independent on 28 October 1918. Legionnaires formed the core of the new state's army and its patriotic mythology — the "Anabasis" entered Czech national memory, though the Legion's legacy was suppressed under the communist regime after 1948.
