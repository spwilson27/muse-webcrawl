RELX plc is a British multinational information and analytics company headquartered in London. It was created in 1993 by the merger of the British publisher Reed International and the Dutch scientific publisher Elsevier, and was known as Reed Elsevier until adopting the name RELX in 2015.

Operations

The group operates in four market segments: Scientific, Technical & Medical, through Elsevier, one of the world's largest publishers of academic journals; Risk, through LexisNexis Risk Solutions; Legal, through LexisNexis; and Exhibitions, through RX, one of the largest organisers of trade shows. The company has progressively shifted from print publishing to electronic databases and decision tools.

Criticism

Elsevier's pricing and bundling of journals have drawn sustained criticism from academics, contributing to the open access movement and boycotts such as the 2012 "Cost of Knowledge" protest. RELX is listed on the London, Amsterdam, and New York stock exchanges and is a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index.
