Carrier Corporation

Carrier Global Corporation headquarters, Palm Beach Gardens, Florida

The Carrier Corporation, operating as Carrier Global Corporation, is an American multinational corporation that provides heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC), refrigeration, and fire and security equipment. Founded in 1915 as an independent company manufacturing and distributing HVAC systems, it takes its name from Willis Carrier, the inventor of modern air conditioning.

History

Willis Carrier invented the first electrical air conditioning unit in 1902 while working for the Buffalo Forge Company. In 1915, Carrier and six fellow engineers pooled their savings to form the Carrier Engineering Corporation in New Jersey. The company developed centrifugal refrigeration machines and large-scale air conditioning systems used in theatres, department stores, factories, and eventually homes.

In 1979, Carrier was acquired by United Technologies Corporation, under which it operated for four decades. In 2018, United Technologies announced it would spin off Carrier as an independent publicly traded company; the separation was completed in April 2020, and Carrier Global Corporation began trading on the New York Stock Exchange.

Operations

Carrier is one of the world's largest manufacturers of HVAC equipment, operating brands including Carrier, Bryant, and Payne for residential and commercial climate systems, Kidde for fire safety, and companies in transport refrigeration for trucks and shipping containers. Its products are sold in more than 160 countries.
