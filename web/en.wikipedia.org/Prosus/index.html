Prosus

Prosus N.V. is a Dutch global consumer internet group and technology investment company, listed on Euronext Amsterdam in September 2019. It is the international internet-assets arm spun out of South Africa's Naspers.

Holdings

Prosus's most valuable asset has been its stake (roughly a quarter to a third, gradually reduced) in China's Tencent, acquired by Naspers in 2001 for a small sum. Other holdings span classifieds (OLX), food delivery (Delivery Hero, iFood, Swiggy), payments and fintech (PayU), and edtech (Stack Overflow, Brainly, BYJU'S). The cross-holding structure with Naspers was simplified in 2023 via an open-ended share buyback program funded by Tencent share sales.
