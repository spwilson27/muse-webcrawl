Lufthansa Group

The Lufthansa Group (Deutsche Lufthansa AG) is a German airline group headquartered in Cologne with its main hub operations at Frankfurt and Munich. It is one of the world's largest airline groups and a founding member of the Star Alliance.

Members and business

Network airlines include Lufthansa, SWISS, Austrian Airlines, Brussels Airlines and Eurowings, along with cargo (Lufthansa Cargo), maintenance (Lufthansa Technik) and catering businesses. The group operates several hundred aircraft and carries over 100 million passengers in a typical recent year.
