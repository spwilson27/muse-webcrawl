Emergency medicine

Emergency medicine is the medical specialty concerned with the care of illnesses or injuries requiring immediate medical attention. Emergency physicians continuously assess and treat patients of all ages with undifferentiated, urgent and life-threatening presentations in the emergency department.

The specialty developed in the mid-20th century: the first emergency medicine residency programme began at the University of Cincinnati in 1970, and the American Board of Emergency Medicine was established in 1976. In the United Kingdom, casualty departments evolved into accident and emergency (A&E) medicine, formally recognised as a specialty in 1972.

Core skills include resuscitation, advanced airway management, trauma care, point-of-care ultrasound, and triage — prioritising patients by acuity using systems such as the Emergency Severity Index and the Manchester Triage System. Emergency physicians also manage disasters and coordinate pre-hospital emergency medical services.
