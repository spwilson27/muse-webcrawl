Mitsubishi Pajero

Mitsubishi Pajero

Manufacturer: Mitsubishi Motors

Production: 1981–2021

Class: Full-size SUV

Also called: Mitsubishi Montero, Mitsubishi Shogun

The Mitsubishi Pajero (Japanese: 三菱・パジェロ) is a full-size SUV (sport utility vehicle) manufactured and marketed globally by Mitsubishi over four generations, introduced in 1981 and discontinued in 2021.

The Pajero was named after Leopardus pajeros, the Pampas cat, native to the southern region of Argentina in South America. However, since pajero is a slang term for "wanker" in Spanish, alternative names were adopted for Spanish-speaking markets (Montero, meaning "mountaineer") and in the United Kingdom (Shogun). It was also marketed as the Montero in North America.

History

The first Pajero prototype was unveiled at the Tokyo Motor Show in 1973, and a second prototype followed in 1978. Series production began in 1981, with the three-door short-wheelbase model offered with petrol and diesel engines, followed by a long-wheelbase five-door version in 1983. The second generation (1991–1999) introduced the Super Select 4WD system. The third generation (1999–2006) adopted monocoque construction with independent suspension. The fourth and final generation (2006–2021) continued with refinements until production for the Japanese market ended in 2019 and global production ended in 2021.

Motorsport

The Pajero is famous for its success in the Dakar Rally, winning the event 12 times, including seven consecutive victories from 2001 to 2007 — a record for a single model. The competition Pajero Evolution models became icons of rally-raid racing.

Related models

The nameplate spawned smaller derivatives including the Pajero Mini kei car, the Pajero Junior, the Pajero iO/Pinin, and the mid-size Pajero Sport (Montero Sport/Challenger), which continued in production after the Pajero itself was discontinued.
