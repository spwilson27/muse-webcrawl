The London Marathon is a long-distance running event held annually in London, United Kingdom, generally in April. Founded in 1981 by former Olympic champion Chris Brasher and athlete John Disley, it has grown into one of the largest marathons in the world and is one of the six World Marathon Majors.

Course

The course begins in Blackheath and Greenwich, passes through south-east London, crosses the River Thames at Tower Bridge, loops around the Isle of Dogs and Canary Wharf, and finishes on The Mall in front of Buckingham Palace. Landmarks along the route include the Cutty Sark, the Tower of London and the London Eye.

Charity

The event is renowned as the largest annual fundraising event in the world; runners have raised over £1 billion for charity since 1981, and the race holds the Guinness World Record for the amount raised by a single-day annual event.

Notable performances

Paula Radcliffe's women's world record of 2:15:25, set in 2003, stood at the event for many years, and the men's course record was set by Kelvin Kiptum in 2023 with 2:01:25.
