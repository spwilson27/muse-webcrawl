The Keeper of the Plains is a 44-foot (13.4 m) Cor-Ten steel sculpture by Kiowa-Comanche artist Blackbear Bosin (1921–1980), standing where the Big and Little Arkansas rivers meet in Wichita, Kansas. Depicting a Native American man with arms raised in supplication to the Great Spirit, it has become the best-known symbol of the city.

Setting

The statue stands on a rock promontory surrounded by the Keeper of the Plains Plaza, completed in 2007, which includes fire pits known as the Ring of Fire — lit nightly for fifteen minutes, weather permitting — as well as exhibits on Plains Indian culture.
