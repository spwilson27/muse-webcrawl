Diplomatic bag

A diplomatic bag, also known as a diplomatic pouch, is a container with certain legal protections used for carrying official correspondence or other items between a diplomatic mission and its home government or other diplomatic, consular, or otherwise official entities.

Legal status

Under Article 27 of the 1961 Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations, a diplomatic bag "shall not be opened or detained" and must bear visible external marks of its character. It may contain only diplomatic documents or articles intended for official use.

Practice

The "bag" can range from a courier's pouch to crates and even shipping containers with diplomatic status. It is usually accompanied or collected by a diplomatic courier, who is also protected from arrest or detention while performing that function.

Controversies

The inviolability of bags has occasionally been abused for smuggling. In 1984, the former Nigerian minister Umaru Dikko was kidnapped and found drugged in a crate at Stansted Airport labelled as diplomatic baggage, an incident that caused a diplomatic dispute between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.
