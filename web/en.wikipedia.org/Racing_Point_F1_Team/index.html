Racing Point F1 Team

The Racing Point RP20, nicknamed the "Pink Mercedes", won the 2020 Sakhir Grand Prix.

Racing Point F1 Team, competing as BWT Racing Point F1 Team, was a British motor racing team and Constructor that competed in Formula One from 2019 to 2020. The team was based in Silverstone, England, and was formed from the assets of the former Force India team, bought by a consortium led by Canadian businessman Lawrence Stroll in August 2018.

The team briefly entered the second half of the 2018 season as Racing Point Force India F1 Team before taking the Racing Point name for 2019. Its drivers were Sergio Pérez and Lance Stroll, the owner's son.

History

Racing Point's first full season in 2019 ended with seventh place in the Constructors' Championship. In 2020 the team's RP20 car drew widespread attention — and a formal protest from Renault — for its close resemblance to the previous year's championship-winning Mercedes W10; the team was fined and docked points for illegally copying Mercedes brake ducts, but the car itself was ruled legal.

The highlight of the team's existence came at the 2020 Sakhir Grand Prix, where Sergio Pérez recovered from last place after a first-lap collision to win the race — his first Grand Prix victory and the team's only win. Racing Point finished fourth in the 2020 Constructors' Championship, narrowly missing third. In 2021 the team was rebranded as the Aston Martin F1 Team after Lawrence Stroll's investment in Aston Martin Lagonda.
