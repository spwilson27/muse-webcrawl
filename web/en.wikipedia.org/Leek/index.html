Leek

The leek (Allium ampeloprasum, often Allium porrum) is a vegetable in the onion family, related to garlic, shallots and chives. It has a mild, sweet, onion-like flavour and is eaten for its white shaft and light-green leaves.

Cultivation and use

Leeks are hardy biennials grown as annuals, earthed up to blanch the shaft. They feature in soups such as leek and potato soup, cock-a-leekie and vichyssoise, and in pies and stews.

Symbolism

The leek is a national emblem of Wales, worn on St David's Day; legend links it to a battle in which Welsh soldiers identified themselves by leeks in their caps.
