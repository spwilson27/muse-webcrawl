Cap Ferret

Cap Ferret is a headland at the southern tip of the commune of Lège-Cap-Ferret in the Gironde department of southwestern France. It forms the southern end of a narrow peninsula separating the Atlantic Ocean from Arcachon Bay, opposite the resort town of Arcachon.

Geography

The cape is known for its lighthouse (built 1947, replacing one destroyed in the Second World War), oyster-farming villages such as L'Herbe and Le Canon, and pine forests. The area is a fashionable but comparatively understated holiday destination, popular for surfing, cycling and seafood.

Transport

A narrow-gauge tourist railway, the Tramway du Cap Ferret, links the bay-side jetty at Bélisaire with the ocean beach at Horizon. Ferries cross the bay to Arcachon.
