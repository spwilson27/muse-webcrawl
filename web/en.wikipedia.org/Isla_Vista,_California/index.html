Isla Vista, California

Isla Vista is an unincorporated census-designated place in Santa Barbara County, California, United States. It is a beachside college town immediately west of the University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB) campus. With a very high proportion of student residents, it is one of the most densely populated communities in the United States.

Geography and character

Isla Vista occupies a flat mesa on the south-facing coast of Santa Barbara County, overlooking the Santa Barbara Channel. Its compact grid of apartment buildings housing UCSB students gives it an unusually youthful population and a strong bicycle culture; Del Playa Drive, on the oceanfront bluffs, is its best-known street.

History

The area was part of Rancho Dos Pueblos and was subdivided beginning in 1925, but development accelerated after UCSB moved to the adjacent former Marine air station site in 1954. Isla Vista was a center of student unrest in 1970, when riots followed antiwar protests and the Bank of America branch was burned.

2014 attacks

On 23 May 2014, a mass stabbing and shooting in Isla Vista killed six people and injured fourteen others; the perpetrator then killed himself. The attacks drew national attention and renewed debate over gun laws and misogyny.
