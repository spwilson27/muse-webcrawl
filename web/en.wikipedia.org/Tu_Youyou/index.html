Tu Youyou is a Chinese pharmaceutical chemist and malariologist. She discovered artemisinin (qinghaosu) and dihydroartemisinin, compounds that have dramatically reduced mortality from malaria worldwide. In 2015 she shared the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, becoming the first Chinese laureate in that category.

Discovery of artemisinin

Working in the secret military Project 523 during the Cultural Revolution, Tu's team screened traditional Chinese herbal remedies. Guided by a 4th-century text by Ge Hong describing cold-water extraction of sweet wormwood (Artemisia annua), she developed a low-temperature ether extraction that yielded a highly effective antimalarial in 1971–72.

Recognition

Artemisinin-based combination therapies became the WHO-recommended standard treatment for malaria. Tu received the Lasker–DeBakey Award in 2011 and is chief scientist of the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences.
