Medianoche

TypeSandwich
Place of originCuba
Main ingredientsEgg bread, roast pork, ham, mustard, Swiss cheese, pickles

A medianoche (Spanish for "midnight") is a type of sandwich originating in Cuba. It is served in many Cuban communities in the United States. It is so named because of the sandwich's popularity as a staple served in Havana's night clubs right after midnight.

Preparation

A medianoche consists of roast pork, ham, mustard, Swiss cheese and sweet pickles served on a soft, sweet egg dough bread similar to a challah. It is a close cousin to the Cuban sandwich, the chief difference being that a medianoche is made on egg bread rather than Cuban bread, and is generally smaller and sweeter.

History

The sandwich is believed to have emerged in Havana in the early twentieth century, sold in cafés near theatres and dance halls to patrons looking for a late-night meal. It spread to Cuban-American communities in Florida, particularly Miami and Tampa.
