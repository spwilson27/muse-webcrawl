Fast Carrier Task Force

CountryUnited States
BranchUnited States Navy
Active1943–1945
DesignationsTask Force 38 / Task Force 58
CommandersMarc Mitscher; John S. McCain Sr.

The Fast Carrier Task Force (TF 38 when assigned to the Third Fleet, TF 58 when assigned to the Fifth Fleet) was the main striking force of the United States Navy in the Pacific War from January 1944 until the end of the war in August 1945. The task force was made up of several separate task groups, each typically built around three to four aircraft carriers and their supporting warships.

Organisation

The task force's fast battleships, cruisers, and destroyers provided anti-aircraft defence and surface escort for the carriers, while a fleet train of oilers and supply ships enabled the force to remain at sea for extended periods — a decisive logistical innovation. The designation alternated between TF 38 and TF 58 as command of the parent fleet rotated between Admiral William Halsey and Admiral Raymond Spruance.

Operations

The Fast Carrier Task Force spearheaded the Central Pacific drive, supporting landings from the Marshall Islands through the Philippines, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa, raiding Truk, Formosa, and the Japanese home islands, and fighting at the Philippine Sea and Leyte Gulf. By 1945 it could put more than a thousand aircraft over a target.
