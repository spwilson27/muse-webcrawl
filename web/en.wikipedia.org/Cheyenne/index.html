Cheyenne

The Cheyenne are a Native American people of the Great Plains, speakers of an Algonquian language, historically allied with the Arapaho and Lakota. Their name in their own language is Tsêhéstáno.

Originally farmers in the upper Midwest, they became mounted buffalo hunters on the plains. Divided by the conflicts of the 19th century into Northern Cheyenne (in present-day Montana) and Southern Cheyenne (in Oklahoma), both are federally recognised tribal nations today.
