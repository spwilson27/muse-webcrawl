Yamanouchi, Nagano

Image placeholder: Snow monkeys in Yamanouchi's Jigokudani park

Yamanouchi (山ノ内町, Yamanouchi-machi) is a town located in Shimotakai District, Nagano Prefecture, Japan. It had an estimated population of roughly 11,000 in recent years and is known for winter sports and hot springs.

Attractions

The town includes parts of Jōshin'etsu-kōgen National Park and the Shiga Kogen ski area, a venue of the 1998 Winter Olympics and one of Japan's largest ski resorts. Its Jigokudani Monkey Park is famous for Japanese macaques ("snow monkeys") bathing in outdoor hot springs. The onsen towns of Yudanaka and Shibu Onsen lie within the municipality.

Geography

Yamanouchi is a mountainous town in northern Nagano, bordering Gunma Prefecture. The Yokoyu River runs through the town.
