Vital record

Vital records are government documents recording the vital events of a person's life, principally birth certificates, marriage licenses and certificates, divorce records and death certificates. They are maintained by civil registration systems at national, state or local level.

Uses

Vital records serve as primary legal evidence of identity, citizenship, age, parentage, marital status and death, and are required for passports, inheritance, pensions, school enrolment and genealogy research. Statistical data derived from them underpin public health and demographic planning.

History

Church registers of baptisms, marriages and burials preceded civil registration in many countries. England and Wales established general civil registration in 1837, while France introduced civil registers during the Revolution. In the United States, registration is handled by states and territories, coordinated in part through the National Center for Health Statistics model forms.
