The Lockheed Martin/Boeing F-22 Raptor is an American single-seat, twin-engine, all-weather stealth tactical fighter aircraft developed for the United States Air Force (USAF). Designed primarily as an air superiority fighter, it also has ground attack, electronic warfare, and signals intelligence capabilities. It was the first operational aircraft to combine stealth, supercruise, supermaneuverability, and integrated avionics sensor fusion.

Development

The F-22 emerged from the USAF's Advanced Tactical Fighter (ATF) program, launched in 1981 to replace the F-15 Eagle and counter new Soviet fighters such as the Su-27 and MiG-29. Lockheed's YF-22 prototype won the fly-off against Northrop's YF-23 in 1991. The first production-standard aircraft flew in 1997, and the type entered service in December 2005. Production ended in 2011 after 187 operational aircraft, with export prohibited by federal law.

Design

The airframe uses radar-absorbent materials, aligned edges, and internal weapons bays to achieve a very low radar cross-section. Two Pratt & Whitney F119 turbofans with two-dimensional thrust-vectoring nozzles provide supercruise — sustained flight above Mach 1.5 without afterburner — and exceptional agility. The AN/APG-77 active electronically scanned array radar, combined with passive sensors, feeds a fused picture to the pilot through the integrated avionics suite.

Operational history

Raptors are based principally at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson, Joint Base Langley-Eustis, Nellis AFB, Hickam AFB, and Tyndall AFB (later relocated). The type made its combat debut in September 2014 during strikes against Islamic State targets in Syria, and has flown air-defense intercepts worldwide. In February 2023 an F-22 scored the type's first air-to-air "kill" by downing a Chinese surveillance balloon off the South Carolina coast.

Specifications

Crew: 1. Length: 18.9 m. Wingspan: 13.56 m. Maximum speed: Mach 2.25. Range: over 1,740 nautical miles with external tanks. Service ceiling: above 65,000 ft. Armament includes a 20 mm M61A2 cannon, AIM-9 Sidewinder and AIM-120 AMRAAM missiles carried internally, and precision-guided bombs such as the JDAM and SDB.
