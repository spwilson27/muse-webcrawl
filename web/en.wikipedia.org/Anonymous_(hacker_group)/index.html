Anonymous (hacker group)

Anonymous is a decentralized international activist and hacktivist collective. Widely known for its various cyber attacks against several governments, government institutions and government agencies, corporations and the Church of Scientology, the group originated in 2003 on the imageboard 4chan, representing the concept of many online and offline community users simultaneously existing as an anarchic, digitized global brain.

Philosophy and imagery

Anonymous has no leader or controlling party and relies on the collective power of its individual participants. Its members, called "Anons", sometimes wear Guy Fawkes masks — popularized by the graphic novel and film V for Vendetta — when appearing in public. The group's mottos include "We are Anonymous. We are Legion. We do not forgive. We do not forget. Expect us."

Notable operations

Major actions have included Project Chanology (2008) against the Church of Scientology, Operation Payback against anti-piracy organizations, support for the Arab Spring movements, attacks on child pornography sites, and operations connected with the Russo-Ukrainian War. Law enforcement agencies in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, the Netherlands, Spain and Turkey have arrested numerous participants.
