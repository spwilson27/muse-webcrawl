Bixby Bridge, also known as Bixby Creek Bridge, is a reinforced concrete open-spandrel deck arch bridge on the Big Sur coast of California, carrying State Route 1 across Bixby Creek.

Construction

Completed in 1932, the bridge stands 260 feet (79 m) above the creek and was one of the tallest single-span concrete bridges in the world at the time. Its graceful arch and rugged setting have made it one of the most photographed bridges in California.
