Leica Camera

Leica Camera AG is a German company that manufactures cameras, photographic lenses, binoculars, rifle scopes and microscopes. Headquartered in Wetzlar, Hesse, it is famous for its rangefinder cameras and for pioneering the 35 mm still photographic format.

History

The company descends from Ernst Leitz's optical works founded in the 19th century. The name Leica combines Leitz and camera. Oskar Barnack's prototype Leica cameras of the 1910s, commercialised with the Leica I in 1925, made small, portable 35 mm photography practical and transformed photojournalism. The Leica M series, introduced in 1954 with the M3, became an icon used by many celebrated photographers.

Products

Leica's lines include the M rangefinder system, the SL mirrorless system, the Q fixed-lens cameras, compact models, and lenses renowned for optical quality. The company also cooperates with other manufacturers and licenses its name for smartphone camera systems.
