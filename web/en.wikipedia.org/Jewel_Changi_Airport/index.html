Jewel Changi Airport

LocationChangi, Singapore
Opened17 April 2019
ArchitectMoshe Safdie (Safdie Architects)
CostS$1.7 billion

Jewel Changi Airport (also known simply as Jewel) is a nature-themed entertainment and retail complex inside Changi Airport in Singapore. Linked to three of the airport's passenger terminals, it opened on 17 April 2019 and was designed by a consortium led by architect Moshe Safdie.

Design and attractions

The complex is built on the site of a former car park and is enclosed by a distinctive toroidal glass-and-steel dome. At its centre is the HSBC Rain Vortex, the world's tallest indoor waterfall at 40 metres, which pours from a roof oculus into the building amid the Shiseido Forest Valley, a terraced indoor garden of some 2,000 trees. Upper levels form Canopy Park, with gardens, mazes, slides, and sky nets. Jewel contains about 280 shops and restaurants, a hotel, and an IMAX cinema, and was conceived both as a traveller amenity and as a destination for local residents.

Reception

Jewel drew some 50 million visitors in its first year and has been credited with strengthening Changi's standing among the world's leading airports, winning design and tourism awards for its fusion of airport infrastructure, retail, and nature.
