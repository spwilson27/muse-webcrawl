The Coppa Italia (English: Italy Cup) is the annual domestic cup competition in Italian association football. First held in 1922, it is the country's premier knockout cup tournament, open principally to clubs of Serie A and Serie B, with limited places for lower-division sides.

History

The competition was first staged in 1922, when Vado won the inaugural edition, defeating Udinese in the final. After sporadic early editions, the cup was revived in the mid-1930s and, following wartime interruption, became an annual event from 1958 onward. Since the 2007–08 season the final has been played as a single match at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome.

Format

The tournament is a single-elimination knockout. Serie A clubs enter at various preliminary rounds, with the highest-seeded teams joining in the round of 16. Most rounds are single matches, though the semi-finals have traditionally been played over two legs. The winner qualifies for the UEFA Europa League and for the Supercoppa Italiana against the Serie A champions.

Records

Juventus is the most successful club in the competition's history, having won the trophy fifteen times, ahead of Roma and Inter Milan. The winners traditionally wear the coccarda, a roundel in the colours of the Italian flag, on their shirts during the following season.
