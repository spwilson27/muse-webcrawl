Marquess of Hertford

Marquess of Hertford is a title in the Peerage of Great Britain, held by the Seymour family. It was first created in 1641, became extinct, and was created again in 1793 for Francis Seymour-Conway, 1st Earl of Hertford.

The 4th Marquess, Richard Seymour-Conway, was a great art collector whose collection, inherited by his illegitimate son Sir Richard Wallace, forms the basis of the Wallace Collection in London.

The family seat is Ragley Hall in Warwickshire.
