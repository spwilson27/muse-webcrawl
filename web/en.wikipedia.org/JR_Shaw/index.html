JR Shaw

James Robert Shaw (1934 – 2020), known as JR Shaw, was a Canadian businessman who founded Shaw Communications, one of Canada's largest cable television and telecommunications companies.

Early life

Shaw was born in Brigden, a village near Sarnia in southwestern Ontario, and grew up in a farming family. He later moved west to Alberta, where he pursued opportunities in the growing cable television industry.

Shaw Communications

In 1966 Shaw founded Capital Cable Television in Edmonton, which became Shaw Communications. Under his leadership the company grew through acquisitions to serve millions of customers across western Canada with cable, internet and telephone services, and later acquired media assets through Shaw Media. His son Jim Shaw succeeded him as chief executive.

Legacy

Shaw was inducted into the Canadian Business Hall of Fame and was remembered as one of the pioneers of the Canadian cable industry. The JR Shaw School of Business at the Northern Alberta Institute of Technology bears his name.
