Fan work

A fan work is a creative work produced by fans of an existing work of fiction, rather than by its original creator or copyright holder. Common forms include fan fiction, fan art, fan films, fan music, doujinshi and fan-made games.

Culture and law

Fan works are typically created out of enthusiasm and shared non-commercially within fandom communities, online and at conventions. Their legal status varies: they often draw on copyrighted characters and settings, and holders' tolerance ranges from encouragement to prohibition. Some creators, such as Anne Rice, have historically objected to fan fiction, while others permit it. Derivative fan works have occasionally launched professional careers, and some have been reworked into commercially published original fiction.
