Frankfurt Airport

Frankfurt Airport (EDDF/FRA), Germany's busiest airport and a major European hub.

Frankfurt Airport (German: Flughafen Frankfurt Main; IATA: FRA, ICAO: EDDF) is Germany's busiest airport and one of the world's leading international hubs. Located about 12 km southwest of central Frankfurt am Main, it is operated by Fraport AG and serves as the main hub of Lufthansa and Lufthansa Cargo.

Overview

The airport opened in 1936 and grew with the adjacent American Rhein-Main Air Base, which operated until 2005. It has four runways and two main passenger terminals, with a third terminal under construction. In peak years it has handled more than 70 million passengers annually and over two million tonnes of cargo, making it one of Europe's largest airports by both measures and among the world's busiest for international traffic.

Frankfurt Airport is notable for its rail integration: the long-distance station Frankfurt Flughafen Fernbahnhof connects it to Germany's ICE high-speed rail network, and an underground regional station serves local trains. The airport city is one of the largest places of employment in Germany, with tens of thousands of workers.
