Groton, Connecticut

Groton is a town in New London County, Connecticut, United States, located on the Thames River. It had a population of about 38,000 at the 2020 census. Groton is home to Naval Submarine Base New London and to General Dynamics Electric Boat, and bills itself as the "Submarine Capital of the World."

History

Groton was settled in the mid-17th century as part of New London and was incorporated as a separate town in 1705. Its name was given in honour of Groton in Suffolk, England. During the American Revolutionary War, Fort Griswold on Groton Heights was stormed by British forces under Benedict Arnold in 1781; the Battle of Groton Heights ended with the massacre of much of the American garrison after it had surrendered, and the nearby town of New London was burned.

Submarine heritage

Electric Boat, founded in 1899, has built most of the United States Navy's submarines, including USS Holland, the Navy's first commissioned submarine, and USS Nautilus, the world's first nuclear-powered submarine, launched in 1954. Naval Submarine Base New London, established in 1868, is the Navy's principal submarine base on the East Coast. The retired Nautilus is now a museum ship at the Submarine Force Library and Museum in Groton.

Economy and geography

Besides Electric Boat and the submarine base, Groton hosts a major Pfizer research and manufacturing campus. The town includes the villages of Poquonnock Bridge, Mystic (shared with Stonington), Noank, and Long Point. It lies across the Thames River from New London, to which it is connected by the Gold Star Memorial Bridge and the Thames River Bridge.
