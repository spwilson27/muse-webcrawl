John Howard Carpenter is an American filmmaker, composer and actor, regarded as one of the most influential directors in horror and science fiction cinema. He typically composes the synthesizer scores for his own films.

Career

After the low-budget features Dark Star (1974) and Assault on Precinct 13 (1976), Carpenter directed Halloween (1978), which became one of the most successful independent films ever made and helped define the slasher genre. His 1980s run includes The Fog, Escape from New York, The Thing (1982), Christine, Starman, Big Trouble in Little China and They Live.

Later work and legacy

Later films include In the Mouth of Madness and Vampires. Since the 2010s Carpenter has focused on music, releasing the Lost Themes albums and touring, and composing for the revived Halloween films. His minimalist style and electronic scores have influenced generations of filmmakers and musicians.
