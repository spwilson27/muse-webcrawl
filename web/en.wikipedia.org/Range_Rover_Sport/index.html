Range Rover Sport

The Range Rover Sport is a mid-size luxury SUV produced by Land Rover, a marque of Jaguar Land Rover. Introduced in 2005, it sits below the flagship Range Rover in size and price while emphasising on-road performance and sportier styling, though it retains substantial off-road capability. The model has been built in Solihull, England.

First generation (L320, 2005–2013)

The first-generation Sport was previewed by the Range Stormer concept at the 2004 Detroit auto show and launched in 2005. Despite the name, it was based on the Discovery 3/LR3 platform rather than the Range Rover itself. Engines included Jaguar-derived V8 petrol units — naturally aspirated and supercharged — and V6 and V8 diesels. A facelift in 2009 updated styling, interior and engines. The supercharged model became known for strong performance, and the Sport was Land Rover's best-selling model in some markets.

Second generation (L494, 2013–2022)

Unveiled at the 2013 New York auto show, the second generation adopted an all-aluminium body shared with the Range Rover, shedding substantial weight. It offered optional seven seats, a range of V6 and V8 petrol and diesel engines, and later plug-in hybrid (P400e) variants. The high-performance SVR model, introduced in 2015 with a 550-horsepower supercharged V8, was developed by Jaguar Land Rover's Special Vehicle Operations.

Third generation (L461, 2022–present)

The third generation, revealed in May 2022, uses the MLA-Flex platform, with six-cylinder mild-hybrid petrol and diesel engines, a twin-turbo V8, plug-in hybrids with extended electric range, and a fully electric version announced to follow. It features rear-wheel steering, minimalist interior design and advanced driver assistance.
