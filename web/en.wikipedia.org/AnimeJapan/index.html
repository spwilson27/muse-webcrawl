AnimeJapan is a Japanese anime consumer convention held annually in Tokyo since 2014. It is one of the largest anime events in the world, organised under the supervision of The Association of Japanese Animations with the participation of major production companies, publishers, broadcasters and merchandise makers.

History

AnimeJapan was formed by merging two predecessor events, the Tokyo International Anime Fair and the Anime Contents Expo. The first edition was held at Tokyo Big Sight in March 2014 and drew over 110,000 visitors. Subsequent editions grew steadily, with attendance exceeding 140,000 in the late 2010s. The 2020 edition was cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic, and the 2021 edition was held online before the event returned to a physical format.

Event

The convention occupies the exhibition halls of Tokyo Big Sight for a weekend each March. It features stage presentations announcing new anime productions, exhibition booths by studios and streaming services, cosplay areas, character goods sales and family-oriented zones. The final day traditionally includes the announcement of results for industry award programmes run alongside the event.
