Blue Origin

Founded: September 8, 2000
Founder: Jeff Bezos
Headquarters: Kent, Washington, US
Industry: Aerospace

Blue Origin Enterprises, L.P. is an American aerospace company founded by Jeff Bezos in 2000 and headquartered in Kent, Washington. The company's motto is Gradatim Ferociter ("step by step, ferociously"), and it pursues reusable launch vehicles intended to lower the cost of access to space.

Vehicles

New Shepard, a fully reusable suborbital system, first flew in 2015 and began carrying paying space tourists in July 2021, with Bezos aboard the first crewed flight. The much larger New Glenn orbital rocket, powered by seven BE-4 engines, made its maiden flight in January 2025, reaching orbit. Blue Origin also develops the BE-3 and BE-7 engines and, under contract with NASA's Artemis program, the Blue Moon lunar lander.

History

Blue Origin operated quietly for its first years, testing experimental vehicles at a ranch in West Texas. It won NASA development funding under the Commercial Crew and Commercial Orbital Transportation programs, and later became a major supplier through its BE-4 engines, which also power United Launch Alliance's Vulcan Centaur rocket.
