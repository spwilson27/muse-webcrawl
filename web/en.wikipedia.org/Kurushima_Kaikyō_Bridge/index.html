Kurushima Kaikyō Bridge

The Kurushima Kaikyō Bridge (来島海峡大橋) is a suspension bridge structure in Japan linking the island of Ōshima with the main island of Shikoku. It forms part of the Nishiseto Expressway (the Shimanami Kaidō) connecting Onomichi in Honshu with Imabari in Shikoku across the Seto Inland Sea.

Structure

Opened in 1999, the structure crosses the Kurushima Strait — known for its strong tidal currents — and consists of three successive suspension bridges with a total length of about 4,105 metres. At the time of completion it was described as the world's first triple suspension bridge. Like the other bridges on the route, it carries a dedicated lane for pedestrians and cyclists, making it popular with cycle tourists.
