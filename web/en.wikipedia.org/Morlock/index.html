Morlock

The Morlocks are a fictional species of beings created by H. G. Wells for his 1895 novel The Time Machine. They are subterranean, ape-like descendants of humanity in the far future, who live underground, maintain the machinery of the surface world, and prey upon the gentle Eloi.

First appearanceThe Time Machine (1895)
CreatorH. G. Wells
TypeFictional species

Context in the novel

The Time Traveller hypothesises that the Morlocks and the Eloi are the divergent descendants of two human classes: the Eloi from the wealthy "Haves" who lived in comfort above ground, and the Morlocks from the labouring "Have-nots" who were driven underground to work the machinery. Blind in daylight and adapted to darkness, the Morlocks emerge at night to take the Eloi as food.

Interpretation

The Morlocks are often read as a commentary on Victorian class divisions and industrial labour, and the name has entered wider usage for troglodytic or subterranean creatures.

In other media

Morlocks appear in film adaptations of The Time Machine (1960 and 2002), and the name was borrowed for an underground-dwelling group of mutants in Marvel Comics' X-Men stories.
