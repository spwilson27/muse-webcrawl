William Oliver Stone (born September 15, 1946) is an American film director, producer, and screenwriter. A Vietnam War veteran, he became one of the most prominent Hollywood directors of the 1980s and 1990s, known for politically charged films.

Career

Stone won an Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay for Midnight Express (1978). His breakthrough as a director came with Salvador and Platoon (both 1986); Platoon, drawn from his own combat experience, won the Academy Award for Best Picture and Best Director. He won a second Best Director Oscar for Born on the Fourth of July (1989).

Other films

His other major works include Wall Street (1987), JFK (1991), controversial for its treatment of the Kennedy assassination, Natural Born Killers (1994), Nixon (1995), and Snowden (2016). Stone has also made documentaries and interview series, including The Untold History of the United States.
