Skepta

Skepta Birth name: Joseph Olaitan Adenuga Jr.
Born: 19 September 1982, Tottenham, London
Genres: Grime; British hip hop
Labels: Boy Better Know
Notable work: Konnichiwa (2016), Mercury Prize winner

Joseph Olaitan Adenuga Jr. (born 19 September 1982), known professionally as Skepta, is a British grime MC, rapper, record producer and fashion designer. He is a founding member of the grime collective and record label Boy Better Know, alongside his younger brother Jme. Skepta is widely credited with helping to bring grime to international audiences in the 2010s.

Career

Skepta began his career as a DJ and producer on the pirate radio station Deja Vu 105 FM and as part of the Meridian Crew, releasing his debut album Greatest Hits in 2007. After two further independent albums he rose to mainstream prominence with the singles "That's Not Me" (2014), which won an Ivor Novello Award, and "Shutdown" (2015), a track widely quoted after Kanye West brought Skepta and other grime artists on stage at the 2015 Brit Awards.

His fourth studio album, Konnichiwa (2016), released independently on Boy Better Know, won the Mercury Prize, beating David Bowie's Blackstar, and reached number two on the UK Albums Chart. The follow-up Ignorance Is Bliss appeared in 2019. Skepta has collaborated with artists including Drake, A$AP Rocky, Pharrell Williams and Playboi Carti, and in 2022 launched his fashion label Mains, first founded in 2017.

Honours

In 2018 Skepta was installed as a traditional chieftain in his parents' home town of Ode Remo, Ogun State, Nigeria, receiving the title Amuludun of Ode Remo. His work has been recognised for championing independent, artist-owned release models in UK rap.
