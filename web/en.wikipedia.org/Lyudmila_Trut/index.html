Lyudmila Trut

Lyudmila Nikolayevna Trut
Born: 6 November 1933, Yaroslavl, USSR
Died: 10 October 2024
Field: evolutionary biology, genetics
Known for: domesticated silver fox experiment

Lyudmila Nikolayevna Trut (1933–2024) was a Russian geneticist and evolutionary biologist best known for directing, from 1959, the long-running experiment begun with Dmitry Belyaev to domesticate the silver fox.

The fox experiment

At the Institute of Cytology and Genetics in Novosibirsk, Trut selected foxes solely for tameness toward humans. Within generations the foxes became eager for human contact and, unexpectedly, developed dog-like traits — floppy ears, piebald coats, wagging tails and altered reproductive timing — evidence for the "domestication syndrome".

Later work

Trut documented the experiment in international publications and popular writing, arguing that selection for behaviour destabilised developmental systems and released hidden variation. The project continued under her leadership for more than six decades and remains a landmark study of domestication.
