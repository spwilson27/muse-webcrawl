The 2002 Winter Olympics, officially the XIX Olympic Winter Games and commonly known as Salt Lake 2002, were held from 8 to 24 February 2002 in and around Salt Lake City, Utah, United States. They were the first Games held after the September 11 attacks, and security and patriotic themes were prominent throughout.

Highlights

Norway topped the medal table with 13 gold medals. Norwegian biathlete Ole Einar Bjørndalen won all four men's biathlon events, and Croatian alpine skier Janica Kostelić won three golds and a silver. In short-track speed skating, Australian Steven Bradbury famously won the 1000 metres after all his rivals crashed on the final turn. Canada won both men's and women's ice hockey gold, the men defeating the United States in the final.

Controversies

The Games were preceded by a bid scandal that led to sweeping IOC reforms, and marred during competition by a judging controversy in the pairs figure skating event that ended with duplicate gold medals being awarded to the Russian and Canadian pairs. Skeleton returned to the Olympic programme for the first time since 1948.
