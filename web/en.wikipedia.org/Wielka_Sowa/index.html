Wielka Sowa

[Image: Wielka Sowa]

Wielka Sowa ("Great Owl") is a mountain in south-western Poland, the highest peak of the Owl Mountains (Góry Sowie), a range of the Central Sudetes. It rises to 1,015 metres (3,330 ft) above sea level and lies in the Lower Silesian Voivodeship, near the towns of Pieszyce and Walim.

Features

A stone observation tower, 25 metres high, stands on the summit; it was built in 1906 and originally named after Otto von Bismarck. The tower and summit offer panoramic views over the Sudetes and the surrounding countryside. The mountain is a popular destination for hikers, with marked trails from Pieszyce and the mountain hostels on its slopes.

Surroundings

The Owl Mountains are known for the extensive underground tunnel complexes of Project Riese, built by Nazi Germany in the final years of the Second World War for unknown purposes, parts of which are open to visitors near Walim and Głuszyca.
