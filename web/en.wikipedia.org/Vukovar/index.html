Vukovar
Country: Croatia
County: Vukovar-Srijem
Population: approx. 23,000 (2021)
Rivers: Danube, Vuka

Vukovar is a city in eastern Croatia, located at the confluence of the Vuka River and the Danube, on the border with Serbia. It is the seat of Vukovar-Srijem County and the country's largest Danube river port.

History

The area has been inhabited since prehistoric times and is associated with the Vučedol culture of the Eneolithic. The modern town developed under Habsburg rule with a notable Baroque old core. Vukovar became internationally known during the Croatian War of Independence, when the 87-day Battle of Vukovar in 1991 devastated the city; it was reintegrated into Croatia peacefully in 1998 under the Erdut Agreement.

Landmarks

Landmarks include the Eltz Manor, the municipal museum and the Vukovar water tower, preserved with its war damage as a memorial symbol of the city.
