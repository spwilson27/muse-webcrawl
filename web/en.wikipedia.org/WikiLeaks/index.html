WikiLeaks

WikiLeaks is a non-profit media organisation and publisher founded in 2006 by Julian Assange. It publishes news leaks and classified or censored documents from anonymous sources, arguing that disclosure promotes transparency.

Major releases

WikiLeaks drew global attention in 2010 with the release of a video of a Baghdad airstrike, war logs from Afghanistan and Iraq, and United States diplomatic cables supplied by Chelsea Manning. Later releases included emails connected with the Democratic National Committee in 2016 and material about intelligence hacking tools in 2017.

Controversy

Supporters describe WikiLeaks as a publisher exposing wrongdoing. Governments and critics have accused it of endangering individuals, mishandling personal data and political bias. Assange's legal cases, including asylum in Ecuador's London embassy and later imprisonment in Britain, kept the organisation in public debate.
