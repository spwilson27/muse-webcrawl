AS Roma

Associazione Sportiva Roma
Founded: 7 June 1927
Ground: Stadio Olimpico, Rome
League: Serie A
Nickname: I Giallorossi (The Yellow and Reds)

Associazione Sportiva Roma (AS Roma) is a professional football club based in Rome, Italy. Founded by a merger in 1927, the club has participated in the top tier of Italian football for all but one season of its history. Roma plays its home matches at the Stadio Olimpico, which it shares with city rivals Lazio, against whom it contests the Derby della Capitale.

Honours

Roma has won Serie A three times (1941–42, 1982–83, and 2000–01), the Coppa Italia nine times, and the Supercoppa Italiana twice. In Europe, the club won the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup in 1960–61 and the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2021–22 under José Mourinho.

Identity

The club's colours are carmine red and golden yellow, and its crest features the Capitoline Wolf suckling Romulus and Remus, symbols of the city of Rome. Francesco Totti, who spent his entire career at the club, is its most famous player.
