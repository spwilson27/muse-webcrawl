Mohanlal Viswanathan (born 21 May 1960), mononymously known as Mohanlal, is an Indian actor, producer, and playback singer who predominantly works in Malayalam cinema, with notable roles in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and Kannada films. Regarded as one of the finest actors in Indian cinema and affectionately called "Lalettan" by fans, he has acted in more than 400 films over a career of over four decades and is often described as "the complete actor" for his versatility and naturalistic style.

Early life

Mohanlal was born in Elanthoor village in Pathanamthitta district, Kerala, and raised in Thiruvananthapuram, where he studied at Mahatma Gandhi College. He was a state-level wrestling champion in his school years. He made his acting debut in 1978 with Thiranottam (released only years later) and first appeared on screen as a villain in Manjil Virinja Pookkal (1980).

Career

Through the 1980s Mohanlal moved from antagonist and supporting parts to leading roles, emerging with Mammootty as one of the twin pillars of Malayalam cinema's golden era. Acclaimed performances include Namukku Parkkan Munthiri Thoppukal (1986), Thoovanathumbikal (1987), Amrutham Gamaya (1987), Kireedam (1989), His Highness Abdullah (1990), Bharatham (1991), Kamaladalam (1992), Devaasuram (1993), and Manichitrathazhu (1993). In the 2000s and 2010s he balanced character roles with mass successes such as Narasimham (2000), Thanmathra (2005), Drishyam (2013) — later remade in several languages — and the blockbuster Pulimurugan (2016) and Lucifer (2019). His performance in Vanaprastham (1999) was screened at Cannes.

Awards and honours

Mohanlal has won five National Film Awards, including two Best Actor awards (for Bharatham and Vanaprastham), nine Kerala State Film Awards, and numerous Filmfare Awards South. The Government of India honoured him with the Padma Shri in 2001 and the Padma Bhushan in 2019. He holds the honorary rank of lieutenant colonel in the Indian Territorial Army, and has hosted the Malayalam edition of the reality show Bigg Boss.

Other ventures

Beyond acting, Mohanlal has produced films under his company Pranavam Arts, run distribution and restaurant businesses, and performed as a playback singer on several soundtracks. He is among the most commercially successful and critically respected actors in Indian film history.
