Sécurité

Sécurité (French for "safety") is a procedure word used in maritime and aeronautical radio communications to indicate that the following message conveys important information about the safety of navigation or weather warnings.

Use

In marine radio, "sécurité" is spoken three times — "Sécurité, sécurité, sécurité" — before a safety message on channel 16 or other distress and calling frequencies. It ranks below "mayday" (distress) and "pan-pan" (urgency) in priority.

Content

Typical sécurité messages announce hazards such as drifting containers, inoperative navigational aids, or severe weather. After the initial call, traffic usually moves to a working channel to keep the calling channel clear.

Related terms

The word forms part of the international system of procedure words derived from French, alongside mayday (from m'aider) and pan-pan.
