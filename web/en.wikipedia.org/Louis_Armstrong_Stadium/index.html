Louis Armstrong Stadium, the second-largest venue of the US Open.

Louis Armstrong Stadium is a tennis stadium at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing Meadows–Corona Park, Queens, New York City. It is the second-largest stadium of the US Open tennis tournament, after Arthur Ashe Stadium, and is named after the celebrated jazz musician Louis Armstrong, who lived nearby in Corona, Queens, until his death in 1971.

History

The original Louis Armstrong Stadium opened in 1978 as the main stadium of the tournament, adapting the former Singer Bowl built for the 1964 New York World's Fair. It had a capacity of about 18,000, later reduced. After Arthur Ashe Stadium opened in 1997, it became the secondary court. The original stadium was partially demolished and a new 14,000-seat Louis Armstrong Stadium, with a retractable roof and natural ventilation, opened in time for the 2018 US Open on a site just to the east.
