List of countries by proven oil reserves

This is a list of countries by proven oil reserves. Proven reserves are quantities of petroleum that geological and engineering data indicate can be recovered with reasonable certainty under existing economic and operating conditions.

Notes

Reserves are not fixed: they grow with discoveries and better technology, and shrink with production. Many OPEC members' reported reserves jumped in the 1980s when quotas were tied to reserves, and are debated by analysts.
