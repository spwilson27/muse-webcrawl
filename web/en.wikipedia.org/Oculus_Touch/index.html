Oculus Touch

Oculus Touch is a line of motion controller systems developed by Oculus (later Meta Reality Labs) for its virtual reality headsets. The first-generation Touch controllers were released on December 6, 2016, for the Oculus Rift, adding tracked hand presence with a distinctive ring of infrared LEDs encircling the user's hand.

DeveloperOculus / Meta
TypeVR motion controllers
Release dateDecember 6, 2016
TrackingInfrared constellation (1st gen); inside-out (2nd gen)
PlatformsOculus Rift, Rift S, Quest, Quest 2

First-generation Oculus Touch controllers ("half-moon" design).

Design

The controllers are held one in each hand and provide an analogue thumbstick, face buttons, analogue index and grip triggers, and capacitive sensors that detect finger positions to render gestures such as pointing and thumbs-up. Haptic feedback is provided by internal actuators. The original design, codenamed "Half Moon", used the Rift's external Constellation tracking cameras.

Second generation

A redesigned version shipped with the Oculus Rift S and the original Oculus Quest in 2019, moving the tracking ring above the hand so the headsets' inside-out cameras could follow it. The Quest 2 controllers (2020) refined the design again with improved battery life and ergonomics.
