Sponsor Refugees

Sponsor Refugees is a United Kingdom community sponsorship programme and registered charity work that helps local community groups welcome and support refugee families resettled in the UK. It was launched in 2017 by Citizens UK to promote and support community sponsorship of refugees.

Community sponsorship

Community sponsorship, introduced by the Home Office in 2016 alongside the Vulnerable Persons Resettlement Scheme, allows a local group — neighbours, faith groups, schools, or businesses — to take responsibility for welcoming a refugee family: securing housing, raising funds, and helping the family access English classes, schools, healthcare, and employment during their first year in the UK.

Activities

Sponsor Refugees recruits and trains sponsor groups, matches them with local authorities and the Home Office process, and campaigns for the expansion of community sponsorship as a safe and legal route for refugees, drawing inspiration from Canada's long-running private sponsorship programme.
