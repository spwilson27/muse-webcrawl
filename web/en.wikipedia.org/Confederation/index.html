Confederation

A confederation (also known as a confederacy, league, or alliance) is a political union of sovereign states or communities united for purposes of common action. Usually created by a treaty, a confederation of states tends to be established for dealing with critical issues, such as defence, foreign relations, internal trade, or currency, with the central government being required to provide support for all its members.

Confederalism represents a main form of intergovernmentalism, defined as any form of interaction around states that takes place on the basis of sovereign independence or government.

Characteristics

The nature of the relationship among the member states constituting a confederation varies considerably. Likewise, the relationship between the member states and the general government and the distribution of powers among them is highly variable. Some looser confederations are similar in nature to international organizations, while tighter confederations may resemble federations.

Confederations are typically weaker than federations: the central authority derives its power from the member states, which retain sovereignty and often a right of secession.

Historical examples

Historical confederations include the Swiss Old Confederacy, the United States under the Articles of Confederation (1781–1789), the German Confederation (1815–1866), the Confederate States of America (1861–1865), and the Senegambia Confederation (1982–1989). Switzerland's official name, the Swiss Confederation, survives from the historical confederation, although the country has been a federation since 1848. The move bringing British North American colonies together in 1867 is likewise known as Canadian Confederation.

Confederation and federation

In a federation, the central government is sovereign and authority is divided constitutionally between levels of government; in a confederation, sovereignty remains with the constituent states, and the central body is subordinate to them. Many confederations have historically either dissolved or evolved into federations.
