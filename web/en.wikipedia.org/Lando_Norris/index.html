Lando Norris (born 13 November 1999) is a British racing driver who competes in Formula One for McLaren. One of the most successful junior drivers of his generation, he won the CIK-FIA World Karting Championship in 2014 and a string of single-seater titles before joining McLaren's young driver programme, making his Formula One debut at the 2019 Australian Grand Prix.

Career

Norris, born in Bristol to a British father and Belgian mother, began karting aged seven and won national, European and world titles. In cars he won the inaugural MSA Formula championship (2015), then both the Formula Renault Eurocup and NEC titles in 2016, and the FIA Formula 3 European Championship in 2017. He finished runner-up in the 2018 FIA Formula 2 Championship to George Russell while serving as McLaren's test and reserve driver.

Formula One

Promoted to a race seat for 2019 alongside Carlos Sainz Jr., Norris scored his first podium at the 2020 Austrian Grand Prix and his first pole position at the 2021 Russian Grand Prix, where he came close to victory before a late rain shower. He became McLaren's team leader after Daniel Ricciardo's departure, partnering Oscar Piastri from 2023. As McLaren's MCL38 became a front-running car in 2024, Norris took his maiden Grand Prix win at Miami, followed by further victories including Zandvoort and Singapore, and finished second in the World Drivers' Championship behind Max Verstappen while McLaren won its first Constructors' Championship since 1998. He is known for an unusually large online following built through streaming and social media, and founded the esports and content team Quadrant.

Personal life

Norris has spoken openly about mental-health pressures in elite sport and supports related charities. He splits his time between the United Kingdom and Monaco, as is common among Formula One drivers.
