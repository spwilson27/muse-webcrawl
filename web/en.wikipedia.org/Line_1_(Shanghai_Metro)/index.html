Line 1 is a north–south line of the Shanghai Metro, identifiable by its red colour on system maps. It was the first line of the Shanghai Metro to open, beginning service in 1993, and runs from Fujin Road in Baoshan District in the north, through Shanghai railway station and People's Square in the city centre, to Xinzhuang in Minhang District in the south.

History

Construction began in 1990. The initial southern section opened on 28 May 1993, and extensions followed through the 1990s and 2000s, reaching Fujin Road in December 2007. The line was built with German assistance and initially used rolling stock supplied by a German consortium, partly elevated and partly underground.

Route and interchanges

Key interchange stations include People's Square (Lines 2 and 8), Shanghai railway station (Lines 3 and 4), Xujiahui (Lines 9 and 11), and Shanghai Indoor Stadium. The line serves major destinations such as Shanghai South railway station and Jinjiang Park, and is among the busiest lines in the network.
