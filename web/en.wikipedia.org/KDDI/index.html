KDDI

KDDI Corporation is a Japanese telecommunications operator and one of the country's largest carriers. It provides mobile services under the au brand, fixed-line telephony, broadband and data services, and is consistently among Japan's top companies by revenue and market capitalisation.

History

KDDI was formed in October 2000 by the merger of KDD (Kokusai Denshin Denwa), Japan's international carrier founded in 1953, with the mobile operators DDI and IDO. The merged company became the main competitor to NTT, Japan's incumbent carrier. KDDI pioneered CDMA2000 mobile services in Japan and was an early leader in flat-rate mobile data and music downloads.

Business

Beyond mobile and fixed communications, KDDI has expanded into financial services (au Jibun Bank), energy (au Electric), commerce and content, pursuing a "life design" strategy around its au customer base. It also co-founded the online bank and payment ecosystem tied to its Smart Pass subscription service. In July 2022 a major multi-day network outage affected tens of millions of users and drew government scrutiny.
