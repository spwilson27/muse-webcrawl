StarCraft

StarCraft (1998), Blizzard Entertainment's landmark real-time strategy game.

StarCraft is a military science fiction media franchise created by Chris Metzen and James Phinney and owned by Blizzard Entertainment. The series began with the real-time strategy video game StarCraft, released on March 31, 1998, and its expansion pack Brood War later that year. The games are set in the Koprulu Sector of the Milky Way in the 26th century, where three species — the Terrans (exiled humans), the insectoid Zerg, and the psionic Protoss — fight for dominance.

Gameplay and impact

StarCraft was praised for balancing three radically different playable factions, and it became one of the best-selling PC games of all time. In South Korea it became a cultural phenomenon, spawning professional leagues, televised matches, and star players, and it is widely credited with helping to popularize esports. A remastered edition was released in 2017, and the sequel, StarCraft II: Wings of Liberty, appeared in 2010.

Story

The single-player campaigns follow characters including marshal-turned-rebel Jim Raynor, the former ghost Sarah Kerrigan (infested by the Zerg to become the Queen of Blades), and the Protoss executor Tassadar, against the backdrop of the fall of the Terran Confederacy and the rise of Arcturus Mengsk's Terran Dominion.
