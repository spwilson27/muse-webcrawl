HSPICE

HSPICE is a commercial circuit simulator, an enhanced derivative of the SPICE simulation program originally developed at the University of California, Berkeley. It is widely used in the semiconductor industry for analog and mixed-signal integrated circuit design.

Features

HSPICE, now developed by Synopsys, offers advanced device models (including BSIM transistor models), high accuracy and convergence performance, statistical and optimization analyses, and support for signal-integrity and RF simulation. Designs are described in netlist format specifying components, nodes and models.

Usage

It is a standard sign-off tool for verifying transistor-level performance of circuits such as amplifiers, data converters and memories before fabrication.
