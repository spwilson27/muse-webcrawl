Turul

The Turul is a mythological bird of prey — probably a large falcon — in Hungarian mythology and a national symbol of Hungary. In the origin myths of the Árpád dynasty, the Turul appears in the dream of Emese, mother of Álmos: the bird impregnated her and prophesied that her son would found a great dynasty. In another legend, a Turul guided the Hungarians on their migration, carrying the sword of Attila, and dropped it over the Carpathian Basin where the Hungarians were to settle.

Symbolism

The Turul embodies divine guidance, strength and the mythical ancestry of the Hungarians, whose ruling house claimed descent from Attila the Hun. Monumental Turul statues stand in several Hungarian cities, including the famous statue at Buda Castle and on Gellért Hill in Budapest, and a large one overlooking Tatabánya. The bird also appears on Hungarian military insignia.
