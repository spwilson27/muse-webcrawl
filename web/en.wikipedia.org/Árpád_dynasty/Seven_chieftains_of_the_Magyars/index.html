Seven chieftains of the Magyars

The seven chieftains of the Magyars (Hungarian: a hét vezér) were the leaders of the seven Hungarian tribes at the time of the Hungarian conquest of the Carpathian Basin in the late 9th century. According to the medieval chronicle tradition, the chieftains were Álmos, Előd, Ond, Kond, Tas, Huba and Töhötöm, and they are said to have sealed their alliance with a blood oath, electing Álmos and his line to lead the confederation.

Sources

The list of names is preserved in the Gesta Hungarorum of the anonymous notary of King Béla and in later illuminated chronicles. Historians treat the details as partly legendary, though the seven-tribe structure of the early Magyar confederation is broadly accepted. The seven chieftains are commemorated at the base of the Millennium Monument in Heroes' Square, Budapest, where their equestrian statues flank that of Árpád.
