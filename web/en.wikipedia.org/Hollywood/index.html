The Hollywood Sign, erected in 1923, is the emblem of the district and the industry named for it.

Hollywood is a neighbourhood and district in the central region of Los Angeles, California. It is famous worldwide as the historic centre of the American film industry, and its name is used as a metonym for that industry and the people associated with it. Many of the major film studios were founded there or nearby, and landmarks such as the Hollywood Sign, the Walk of Fame and the Dolby Theatre (home of the Academy Awards) anchor its identity.

History

Hollywood was laid out as a subdivision in 1887 by Harvey and Daeida Wilcox, who had bought 160 acres in the Cahuenga Valley; by local tradition the name came from Daeida, who liked the sound of it. It incorporated as a municipality in 1903 and voted to merge with Los Angeles in 1910, largely to secure water from the Los Angeles Aqueduct. Film-makers began arriving around 1910 — D. W. Griffith shot In Old California there, and the Nestor Film Company opened the first studio in 1911 — drawn by cheap land, varied scenery, sunlight and distance from Thomas Edison's patent enforcers on the East Coast.

The studio era and after

By the 1920s Hollywood dominated world cinema; the "major" studios (Paramount, Warner Bros., MGM, RKO, 20th Century Fox) ran a vertically integrated studio system through a Golden Age of stars and genres. The 1948 Paramount Decrees broke studio control of theatres, television eroded audiences, and production dispersed, but "Hollywood" remained the symbolic capital of filmed entertainment, through New Hollywood (1960s–70s), the blockbuster era inaugurated by Jaws (1975) and Star Wars (1977), and the globalised, franchise- and streaming-driven industry of today.

Landmarks

The Hollywood Sign (originally "Hollywoodland", an advertisement, 1923) overlooks the district from Mount Lee. Hollywood Boulevard hosts the Walk of Fame (begun 1960, now with over 2,700 stars), Grauman's Chinese Theatre (1927, now TCL Chinese Theatre) with celebrity handprints, and the Dolby Theatre. Capitol Records' tower and the Hollywood Bowl are nearby icons.
