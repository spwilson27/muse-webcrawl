British hip hop

British hip hop, also known as UK rap, is a genre of music and culture covering hip hop made in the United Kingdom. Emerging in the early 1980s through sound system culture and influence from the United States, it developed a distinctly British identity with local accents, slang and references.

Development

Early acts included the London Posse and Derek B. In the 2000s the scene diversified into grime—pioneered by artists such as Dizzee Rascal and Wiley—and UK garage-influenced styles. Artists including Roots Manuva, Stormzy, Skepta, Dave and Little Simz brought British rap to mainstream and international success, with Stormzy headlining Glastonbury Festival in 2019 and grime and drill becoming dominant youth sounds.
