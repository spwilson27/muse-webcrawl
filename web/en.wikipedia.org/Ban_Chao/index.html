Ban Chao (Chinese: 班超; pinyin: Bān Chāo; Wade–Giles: Pan Ch'ao; 32–102 CE) was a Chinese military general, explorer and diplomat of the Eastern Han dynasty. He is famous for re-establishing Chinese control over the Tarim Basin and the Western Regions.

Career in the Western Regions

Sent west in 73 CE under General Dou Gu against the Xiongnu, Ban Chao spent three decades bringing the oasis states of the Tarim Basin—Shanshan, Khotan, Kashgar and Kucha—under Han influence, often with very small forces, using diplomacy and local alliances. In 97 CE he dispatched his subordinate Gan Ying toward the Roman Empire; Gan Ying reached the Persian Gulf before turning back.

Later life

Created Marquis of Dingyuan, Ban Chao returned to Luoyang in 102 CE and died the same year. His son Ban Yong continued the family's involvement in the Western Regions.
