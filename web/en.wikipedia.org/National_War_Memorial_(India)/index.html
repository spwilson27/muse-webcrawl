National War Memorial (India)

The National War Memorial (Rashtriya Samar Smaarak) in New Delhi, India, honours Indian Armed Forces personnel killed in the wars of 1947–48, 1962, 1965, and 1971, the Kargil conflict of 1999, and counter-terrorism operations. It was inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 25 February 2019.

LocationC Hexagon, Rajpath, New Delhi
Inaugurated25 February 2019
Area~40 acres

Design

Designed by architect Yogesh Chandrahasan, the memorial is arranged as a Chakra-Vyuha (battle formation). Its centrepiece, the Tyag Chakra (circle of sacrifice), is a granite wall bearing the names of more than 26,000 fallen soldiers, surrounding an eternal flame. The Amar Jawan flame formerly under India Gate was merged with this flame in 2022. The complex includes murals of famous battles, busts of Param Vir Chakra recipients in the Param Yodha Sthal, and the Veerta Chakra gallery.

Significance

Proposals for a national memorial dated back to the 1960s; the site around India Gate — itself a memorial to Indian soldiers of the First World War — was chosen in the 2010s.
