Sunn O)))

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Sunn O))) (pronounced "sun") is an American experimental metal band from Seattle, Washington, formed in 1998. The band is known for its slow, heavy, and immersive sound that blends drone metal, doom metal, black metal, dark ambient, and noise rock, emphasising volume, timbre, and physical vibration.

History

The band was founded by Stephen O'Malley and Greg Anderson, taking its name and logo from the Sunn amplifier company. Anderson also founded the influential independent label Southern Lord Records. Early releases such as The Grimmrobe Demos (1999) and ØØ Void (2000) took inspiration from Earth, the Melvins, and Black Sabbath played at extreme slowness.

Style and collaborations

Sunn O)))'s music centres on detuned, sustained guitar drones amplified to extreme volume, often accompanied by vocals processed into chants or growls, and live performances featuring the members in hooded robes amid dense fog. Frequent collaborators include vocalist Attila Csihar of Mayhem, composer Eyvind Kang, and Oren Ambarchi. The band collaborated with Boris on Altar (2006), with Ulver on Terrestrials (2014), and with Scott Walker on Soused (2014). Later albums such as Kannon (2015) and Life Metal (2019), produced by Steve Albini, refined the approach, and the duo have toured internationally as a headline act of experimental music.
