Richard Kylea Cowie Jr. (born 19 January 1979), better known by his stage name Wiley (formerly Wiley Kat), is a British grime MC, rapper, songwriter, DJ and record producer from Bow, London. He is regarded as a pioneer of grime music and is often referred to as the "Godfather of Grime". In 2018 he received the Outstanding Contribution to Music Award at the NME Awards and was appointed MBE in the 2018 New Year Honours for services to music.

Career

Wiley began on pirate radio and in UK garage crews such as Pay As U Go Cartel before forming Roll Deep. In the early 2000s he developed the cold, angular "eskibeat" production style — named after his Eskiboy alias — exemplified by instrumentals such as "Eskimo", which became foundational to the grime sound. His debut album Treddin' on Thin Ice (2004) was released on XL Recordings.

After a period of commercial crossover success, including the 2008 UK top-five single "Wearing My Rolex" and the number-one single "Heatwave" (2012), Wiley returned to grime with albums including The Ascent (2013) and the acclaimed Godfather (2017), which reached number nine on the UK Albums Chart. Its follow-up Godfather II appeared in 2018.

Influence

Through Roll Deep and his mentorship of artists such as Dizzee Rascal, Skepta and Stormzy's generation, Wiley shaped the direction of British urban music, and his eskibeat productions remain canonical in grime history.
