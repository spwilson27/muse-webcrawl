Satellite Catalog Number

The Satellite Catalog Number (SATCAT, also known as the NORAD Catalog Number, NORAD ID, or USSPACECOM object number) is a sequential five-digit number assigned by the United States Space Command (formerly NORAD) to all artificial objects in Earth orbit in the order of their identification.

Usage

The first catalogued object, number 00001, is the rocket body of the 1957 launch of Sputnik 1; the satellite itself is number 00002. Catalog numbers are used in two-line element set (TLE) data to identify objects for orbital tracking. The public satellite catalog includes satellites, spent rocket stages, and tracked debris objects large enough to be followed by ground-based sensors, roughly 10 cm and larger.

Growing catalog

As launches increased and fragmentation events (such as satellite collisions and anti-satellite tests) created thousands of fragments, the catalog grew to tens of thousands of objects. The catalog is maintained today by the US Space Force's 18th Space Defense Squadron, and its data feed the public Space-Track.org service. The growth of the catalog has prompted work towards cataloguing in a format supporting larger numbers.
