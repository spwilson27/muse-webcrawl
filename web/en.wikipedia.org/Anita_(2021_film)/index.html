Anita (2021 film)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Anita (Chinese: 梅艷芳) is a 2021 Hong Kong biographical drama film directed by Longman Leung, about the life of the celebrated Cantopop singer and actress Anita Mui (1963–2003), one of Hong Kong's most beloved entertainers. Louise Wong stars as Mui, in her film debut, with supporting performances by Louis Koo, Gordon Lam, Fish Liew and Terrance Lau.

Plot and production

The film traces Mui's life from her childhood performing in nightclubs with her sister Ann, through her rise to stardom after winning the first New Talent Singing Awards in 1982, her celebrated stage career and close friendship with Leslie Cheung, to her final concert in 2003, performed while ill with cervical cancer, weeks before her death at age 40. The production recreated iconic costumes and concert moments, including her famous wedding-gown farewell.

Reception

Released in November 2021, Anita became one of the highest-grossing Hong Kong films of the year and was praised for Wong's performance and its evocation of the golden age of Cantopop. It received multiple nominations at the Hong Kong Film Awards, and an extended director's cut was later released as a streaming series.
