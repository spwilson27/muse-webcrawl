The Australian Football Hall of Fame was established in 1996, the centenary year of the Victorian Football League (now AFL), to recognise individuals who have made an outstanding contribution to Australian rules football.

Induction

Inductees are selected by a committee and announced at an annual ceremony. Categories include players, coaches, umpires, administrators and media representatives. A small number of inductees are further elevated to the status of "Legend of the Game"; the first Legend was Haydn Bunton Sr.

Legends

Legends of the Game have included figures such as John Coleman, Dick Reynolds, Bob Skilton, Ian Stewart, Leigh Matthews and Kevin Bartlett, reflecting the Hall's coverage of both the VFL/AFL and the state leagues of Western Australia and South Australia.
