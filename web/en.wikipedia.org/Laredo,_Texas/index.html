Laredo is a city in and county seat of Webb County, Texas, United States, on the north bank of the Rio Grande opposite Nuevo Laredo, Mexico. Founded in 1755, it is the United States' largest inland port and a principal gateway for trade with Mexico, handling a large share of cross-border truck freight.

History

Laredo was founded by Tomás Sánchez under Spanish rule. It briefly served as capital of the short-lived Republic of the Rio Grande in 1840, and after the Mexican–American War residents who wished to remain Mexican moved across the river to found Nuevo Laredo.

Economy

International trade, transportation and logistics dominate the economy. The World Trade Bridge and Colombia–Solidarity Bridge handle heavy commercial traffic.
