Museum of Scotland

The Museum of Scotland in Edinburgh tells the history of Scotland from its geological beginnings to the modern era. It opened in 1998 in a purpose-built building on Chambers Street, adjoining the former Royal Museum, with which it merged to form the National Museum of Scotland in 2006.

Collections

Exhibits range from the Lewis chessmen and the Monymusk reliquary to Mary, Queen of Scots' jewellery, the Maiden (an early form of guillotine) and Dolly the sheep, the first cloned mammal. Galleries cover Scottish land, nature, culture, industry and emigration.

Building

Designed by architects Benson & Forsyth, the sandstone building draws on Scottish architectural traditions including brochs and castles, with a distinctive tower and terraced forms. The combined museum underwent a major redevelopment completed in 2011, and further galleries opened in 2019.
