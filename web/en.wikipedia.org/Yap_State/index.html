Yap State is one of the four states of the Federated States of Micronesia (FSM), located in the western Caroline Islands of the Pacific Ocean. The state consists of the main Yap Islands (Yap Proper), made up of four closely spaced islands, and a chain of outer islands and atolls extending about 800 kilometres to the east, including Ulithi, Fais, Sorol, Woleai, Eauripik, Ifalik, Faraulep, and Lamotrek.

Stone money

Yap is famous for its Rai stones, large carved stone discs used as a traditional form of currency. The discs, some several metres in diameter, were quarried on the island of Palau and transported by canoe; their value depends on size, craftsmanship, and history. Although the US dollar is the official currency, Rai stones continue to be exchanged in traditional transactions.

Culture and governance

Yapese society retains a strong traditional caste and chiefly system, and the state constitution formally recognises the role of traditional leaders through the Councils of Pilung and Tamol. The capital, Colonia, on Yap Island, is the administrative centre and main port. Yapese is the principal language of the main islands, while the outer islands speak Ulithian, Woleaian, and Satawalese languages.
