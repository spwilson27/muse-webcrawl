Sacramento County is a county in the U.S. state of California, in the Sacramento Valley. Its county seat and largest city is Sacramento, the state capital. One of California's original 27 counties, created in 1850, it had a population of about 1.58 million at the 2020 census, making it among the most populous counties in the state.

Geography

The county covers roughly 994 square miles at the confluence of the Sacramento and American rivers, extending north to the Placer County line and south into the Sacramento–San Joaquin Delta. Incorporated cities include Sacramento, Elk Grove, Citrus Heights, Folsom, Rancho Cordova, Galt and Isleton.

Government and economy

State government is the region's dominant employer; other major sectors include health care, education and technology. The county is governed by a five-member Board of Supervisors.
