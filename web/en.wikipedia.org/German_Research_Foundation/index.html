German Research Foundation

The German Research Foundation (German: Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft, DFG) is a German research funding organization that promotes science and research at universities and publicly funded research institutions in Germany. It is the largest research funding body of its kind in Europe.

Functions

Headquartered in Bonn, the DFG is funded by the German federal and state governments. It awards competitive grants to individual researchers and collaborative projects, funds Collaborative Research Centres and Research Training Groups, supports large-scale research infrastructure, and advises parliaments and public authorities on scientific matters. Its annual budget is on the order of €4 billion.
