Claire Elizabeth Cottrill (born August 18, 1998), known professionally as Clairo, is an American singer-songwriter. Born in Atlanta, Georgia, and raised in Carlisle, Massachusetts, she began posting music on the internet at age 13. Clairo rose to prominence with the viral success of her lo-fi single "Pretty Girl" (2017), which she recorded in her bedroom; the homemade video amassed tens of millions of views on YouTube and made her a defining figure of "bedroom pop".

Career

After releasing the EP Diary 001 (2018) on the Fader Label, Clairo released her debut studio album, Immunity (2019), co-produced with Rostam Batmanglij. The album, featuring the singles "Bags", "Sofia", and "Closer to You", was acclaimed for its candid treatment of adolescence, sexuality, and her diagnosis with juvenile rheumatoid arthritis. Her second album, Sling (2021), co-produced with Jack Antonoff and recorded at Allaire Studios in upstate New York, adopted a 1970s singer-songwriter and soft-rock style and debuted in the top 20 of the Billboard 200. Her third album, Charm (2024), co-produced with Leon Michels, leaned further into soul- and jazz-inflected soft rock and received a Grammy nomination for Best Alternative Music Album.

Artistry and advocacy

Clairo's music is noted for its intimate, diaristic lyrics and quiet vocal delivery. She has spoken publicly about her bisexuality and her experiences with autoimmune disease, and has used tours to fundraise for causes including gun control and LGBTQ+ organisations.
