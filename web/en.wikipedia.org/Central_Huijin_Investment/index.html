Central Huijin Investment

Type: State-owned investment company
Founded: 2003
Headquarters: Beijing, China
Owner: China Investment Corporation

Central Huijin Investment Co., Ltd. is a Chinese state-owned investment company established in 2003 to hold the government's equity stakes in China's major state-owned financial institutions. It exercises shareholder rights on behalf of the state in the large commercial banks, including the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, the China Construction Bank, the Agricultural Bank of China, and the Bank of China.

History and role

Huijin was created during the restructuring and recapitalisation of China's state banks in the 2000s, when it injected foreign-exchange reserves into the banks ahead of their public listings. Since 2007 it has been a wholly owned subsidiary of the China Investment Corporation, the country's sovereign wealth fund, while continuing to operate separately under its own board. Huijin has also intervened in securities markets, buying shares in listed banks during periods of market turbulence to support stability.
