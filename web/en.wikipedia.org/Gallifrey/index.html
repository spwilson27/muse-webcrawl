Gallifrey is a fictional planet in the long-running British science fiction television series Doctor Who. It is the homeworld of the Time Lords and of the Doctor, the show's protagonist. The planet is located in the constellation of Kasterborous, some 250 million light years from Earth, and is protected by an impenetrable barrier called the Quantum Forcefield and a transduction barrier.

Description

Gallifrey is depicted with burnt-orange skies, silver-leafed trees, and a landscape of red grass and snow-capped mountains. Its capital city, the Capitol, is enclosed in a mighty glass dome and contains the Citadel of the Time Lords, including the Panopticon assembly hall and the Matrix, a vast repository of Time Lord knowledge and memories.

History in the series

Early Time Lord society was shaped by figures such as Rassilon and Omega, whose experiments on a black hole gave the Gallifreyans mastery over time travel. In the revived series, Gallifrey was said to have been destroyed in the Last Great Time War against the Daleks; later episodes revealed that the Doctor had saved the planet by freezing it in a pocket universe, and that it eventually returned to the universe before falling to destruction again in subsequent storylines.
