The National Aquarium in Baltimore, Maryland, is a non-profit public aquarium located at 501 East Pratt Street on Pier 3 in the Inner Harbor area of downtown Baltimore. Opened on 8 August 1981 as the centrepiece of the Inner Harbor redevelopment, it is consistently ranked among the best aquariums in the United States and is Maryland's most visited tourist attraction.

Exhibits

The aquarium's distinctive glass-and-steel pyramid buildings house around 20,000 animals of more than 750 species in some 2.2 million gallons of water. Signature exhibits include Blacktip Reef, a Indo-Pacific reef recreation; the multi-storey Atlantic Coral Reef and shark ring; the Upland Tropical Rain Forest under the glass pyramid; Australia: Wild Extremes; and the open-ocean shark tank. The adjoining pavilion formerly housed bottlenose dolphins in the Dolphin Discovery exhibit; in 2016 the aquarium announced plans to retire its dolphins to a seaside sanctuary.

Conservation

The National Aquarium runs conservation programmes including beach clean-ups, wetland restoration in Chesapeake Bay, marine animal rescue and stranding response, and sustainable seafood education.
