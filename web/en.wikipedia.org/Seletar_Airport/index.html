Seletar Airport
IATA: XSP · ICAO: WSSL
Location: Seletar, Singapore
Operator: Changi Airport Group
Elevation: 11 m (35 ft)

Seletar Airport (IATA: XSP, ICAO: WSSL) is a civil airport serving the north-east region of Singapore. It is located approximately 16 kilometres (9.9 mi) northeast of Changi Airport, Singapore's main international airport, and is managed by Changi Airport Group. The airport is used mainly by turboprop aircraft, business jets, flight training schools and aircraft maintenance firms.

History

Seletar began as a British Royal Air Force station, RAF Seletar, opened in 1928. It was Singapore's first international airport before the opening of Kallang Airport in 1937. During the Second World War the base was used by the Imperial Japanese Navy Air Service. After the war it returned to RAF control until the British military withdrawal, when it was handed over to Singapore.

Facilities and operations

The airport has a single runway, 03/21, about 1,895 metres long. A new passenger terminal, opened in late 2018, was built to accommodate scheduled turboprop services, notably Firefly's flights to Kuala Lumpur's Subang Airport, allowing those services to move from Changi Airport. The surrounding Seletar Aerospace Park hosts maintenance, repair and overhaul companies and manufacturers.
