Ferguson unrest

Protests in Ferguson, Missouri, 2014

The Ferguson unrest was a series of protests and riots in Ferguson, Missouri, following the fatal shooting of Michael Brown, an 18-year-old African American man, by police officer Darren Wilson on 9 August 2014. The unrest began the day after the shooting and lasted for more than a week, with a second wave erupting on 24 November 2014 after a grand jury declined to indict Wilson.

Background and events

Witness accounts differed as to whether Brown had his hands raised in surrender when shot, giving rise to the slogan "Hands up, don't shoot." Protests were largely peaceful but were interspersed with looting and violent confrontations. The heavily militarised police response, including tear gas and armoured vehicles, drew national criticism and debate about police tactics in the United States.

Aftermath

A U.S. Department of Justice investigation cleared Wilson of civil rights violations but issued a scathing report on the Ferguson Police Department, finding a pattern of unconstitutional policing and racial bias. The events in Ferguson gave major impetus to the Black Lives Matter movement and to nationwide debate over policing and race.
