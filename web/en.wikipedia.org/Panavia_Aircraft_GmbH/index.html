Panavia Aircraft GmbH

Panavia Aircraft GmbH is a German consortium company founded in 1969 by the United Kingdom, West Germany and Italy to develop and produce the Panavia Tornado multirole combat aircraft.

Organisation

Ownership is shared among BAE Systems (42.5%), Airbus (42.5%) and Leonardo (15%). The company manages the Tornado programme, coordinating manufacture across the partner nations and supporting in-service aircraft.

Tornado

The variable-geometry Tornado entered service in the early 1980s in strike, interceptor (ADV) and reconnaissance/ECR variants, serving with the RAF, Luftwaffe, Italian Air Force and Royal Saudi Air Force.
