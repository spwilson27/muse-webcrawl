Anil Chauhan

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

General Anil Chauhan (born 18 May 1961) is a retired Indian Army officer who serves as the second Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India, taking office on 30 September 2022. He succeeded General Bipin Rawat, the first CDS, after a gap of nearly ten months.

Military career

Chauhan was commissioned into the 11 Gorkha Rifles in 1981 after graduating from the National Defence Academy and the Indian Military Academy. He commanded a corps in the north-east and served as General Officer Commanding-in-Chief of the Eastern Command before retiring as a lieutenant general in May 2021. He also served in a United Nations mission in Angola and as Director General of Military Operations.

Chief of Defence Staff

His appointment required an amendment to service rules permitting a retired three-star officer under 62 to be recalled to the post. As CDS he heads the Department of Military Affairs and is tasked with promoting jointness among the army, navy and air force, including the planned creation of integrated theatre commands. In 2023 he was promoted to the rank of general on assuming the office.
