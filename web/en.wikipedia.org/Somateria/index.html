Somateria is a genus of large sea ducks, the eiders. The three living species breed in cold northern coastal regions of the Northern Hemisphere and winter farther south at sea.

Species

The genus contains the common eider (Somateria mollissima), the king eider (S. spectabilis), and the spectacled eider (S. fischeri). Males are striking black-and-white birds with coloured head markings; females are mottled brown for camouflage on the nest.

Eiderdown

The common eider's nest down has been harvested sustainably in Iceland and elsewhere for centuries to fill luxury quilts and clothing. The ducks dive for molluscs and other marine invertebrates in cold coastal waters.
