Lionel Andrés Messi (born 24 June 1987) is an Argentine professional footballer who plays as a forward and captains both Major League Soccer club Inter Miami and the Argentina national team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, he has won a record eight Ballon d'Or awards and holds numerous scoring records.

Club career

Messi joined FC Barcelona's La Masia academy at age 13 after the club agreed to pay for treatment for his growth hormone deficiency. He made his competitive debut in 2004 and spent 17 years with the first team, becoming the club's all-time top scorer and winning 35 trophies, including ten La Liga titles and four UEFA Champions Leagues. Financial constraints prevented a new contract in 2021, and he signed for Paris Saint-Germain, winning two Ligue 1 titles, before joining Inter Miami in 2023.

International career

Messi is Argentina's all-time leading scorer and most-capped player. After losing several finals, he led Argentina to victory at the 2021 Copa América, the 2022 Finalissima, and the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, where he won the Golden Ball.

Style of play

A prolific goalscorer and playmaker, Messi is renowned for his dribbling, vision, passing and finishing, typically operating as a right-sided forward or false nine.
