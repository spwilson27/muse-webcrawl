Pronoun

A pronoun is a word that substitutes for a noun or noun phrase. English pronouns include personal pronouns (I, you, he, she, it, we, they), possessives (mine, hers), reflexives (myself), demonstratives (this, those), interrogatives (who, what), relatives (who, which), and indefinites (someone, none).

Grammar

Pronouns often inflect for case (subjective/objective/possessive), number, person, and sometimes gender. Their reference may be anaphoric (to an antecedent), deictic (dependent on context), or generic. Languages differ widely: some drop subject pronouns (pro-drop), others encode formality (T–V distinction) or inclusive/exclusive "we." In recent decades, preferred personal pronouns and singular they have become prominent topics in English usage.
