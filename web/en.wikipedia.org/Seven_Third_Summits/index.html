Seven Third Summits

The Seven Third Summits are the third-highest mountains on each of the seven continents. Climbing all of them is a mountaineering challenge related to the better-known Seven Summits (the highest peaks) and Seven Second Summits.

Definitions

As with the other Seven Summits lists, definitions vary by which continental model is used (the Bass versus Messner lists for Oceania) and by how prominence is treated, so several versions of the list exist. The challenge attracted attention after climbers completed the Seven Second Summits, a set sometimes described as harder than the original Seven Summits because peaks such as K2 and Mount Tyree are more demanding climbs than their continents' highest points.
