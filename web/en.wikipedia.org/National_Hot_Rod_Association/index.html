National Hot Rod Association

The National Hot Rod Association (NHRA) is a drag racing governing body, founded in 1951 by Wally Parks to promote safety and standardisation in the young sport of drag racing. It organises the NHRA Mission Foods Drag Racing Series, the premier drag racing championship in North America.

History

Parks, editor of Hot Rod magazine, founded the NHRA to move illegal street racing onto organised strips. The association held its first national event in 1955 at Great Bend, Kansas. From its Southern California roots, the sport grew into a professional spectacle featuring Top Fuel dragsters and Funny Cars reaching speeds above 330 mph (530 km/h) over 1,000 feet.

Classes and events

Professional categories include Top Fuel, Funny Car, Pro Stock and Pro Stock Motorcycle; a large sportsman programme serves amateur racers. The U.S. Nationals, held near Indianapolis each Labour Day weekend since 1961, is the association's most prestigious event. The NHRA also owns and operates several racetracks and runs junior drag racing programmes.
