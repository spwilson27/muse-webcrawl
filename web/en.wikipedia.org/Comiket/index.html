Comiket

Full nameComic Market
LocationTokyo Big Sight, Tokyo, Japan
First held1975
FrequencyTwice yearly (summer and winter)

Comiket (short for Comic Market) is the world's largest fan convention devoted to dōjinshi — self-published works, especially manga. Held twice a year at Tokyo Big Sight in Ariake, Tokyo, it draws tens of thousands of exhibiting circles and hundreds of thousands of visitors over several days.

History

Comiket was founded in 1975 by critic Yoshihiro Yonezawa and fellow manga enthusiasts as a space for amateur creators outside the commercial publishing system. It grew steadily from a few hundred attendees to become a cornerstone of Japanese otaku culture, moving to Tokyo Big Sight in 1996.

Activities

Circles sell dōjinshi — fan works often based on existing anime, manga, and games, alongside original creations — as well as music, games, and other self-published goods. Cosplay is a major attraction, with designated changing areas and photography spaces. Although most material is produced by amateurs, professional publishers and game companies also exhibit, and talent scouts watch the event for promising artists, many of whom have gone on to professional careers.
