Space Marines, formally the Adeptus Astartes, are a fictional military order in the Warhammer 40,000 universe created by Games Workshop. They are genetically engineered superhuman warriors who serve as the elite shock troops of the Imperium of Man, and they are the most iconic and best-selling faction of the tabletop game.

Background

Space Marines are created by subjecting adolescent recruits to years of surgical implantation of gene-seed organs — including a second heart, a third lung and the ossmodula that fuses and strengthens their skeletons — derived from the twenty Primarchs created by the Emperor of Mankind. They wear powered armour, wield boltguns, and are organised into autonomous Chapters of about one thousand battle-brothers under the dictates of the Codex Astartes. Famous Chapters include the Ultramarines, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Space Wolves and Imperial Fists; traitor Legions fell to Chaos during the Horus Heresy.

In the game and media

Space Marines have headlined numerous boxed sets, novels in the Black Library's Horus Heresy and Warhammer 40,000 lines, and video games including Dawn of War and Space Marine (2011) and its 2024 sequel Space Marine 2, whose protagonist, Lieutenant Titus of the Ultramarines, became a popular emblem of the setting.

Primaris Marines

In the 2017 eighth-edition storyline, the resurrected Primarch Roboute Guilliman unveiled the Primaris Space Marines — taller, stronger Marines created over ten thousand years by Archmagos Belisarius Cawl — who reinforced and gradually began to replace the older "Firstborn" Marines across the Chapters.
