Amazon MGM Studios

Amazon MGM Studios is an American film and television production and distribution company owned by Amazon. It was formed following Amazon's acquisition of Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM) in March 2022 for about US.5 billion, and it absorbed the operations of Amazon Studios.

History

Amazon Studios began producing original content in 2013, earning acclaim with series such as Transparent and films including Manchester by the Sea. The purchase of MGM brought a library of some 4,000 films — including the James Bond and Rocky franchises — and thousands of television episodes under Amazon's control. The combined studio releases films theatrically and produces original programming for the Prime Video streaming service.
