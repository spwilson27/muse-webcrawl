Vehicle insurance

Vehicle insurance (also known as car insurance, motor insurance, or auto insurance) is insurance for cars, trucks, motorcycles, and other road vehicles. Its primary use is to provide financial protection against physical damage or bodily injury resulting from traffic collisions and against liability that could arise from incidents in a vehicle. Many jurisdictions make at least third-party liability cover compulsory.

Types of coverage

Common components include: liability coverage (for injury and property damage to others); collision coverage (damage to the insured vehicle in a crash); comprehensive coverage (theft, fire, weather, vandalism); personal injury protection / medical payments; and uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage. Policies typically include a deductible — the amount the policyholder pays before insurance contributes.

Pricing

Premiums depend on the driver record, age, vehicle type, location, annual mileage, and coverage limits. Insurers increasingly offer usage-based insurance priced from telematics data. Driving uninsured can result in fines, licence suspension, or personal liability for damages.
