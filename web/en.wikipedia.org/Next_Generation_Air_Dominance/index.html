Next Generation Air Dominance

Next Generation Air Dominance (NGAD) is a United States Air Force program to develop a sixth-generation fighter aircraft family, centred on a crewed fighter — designated the Boeing F-47 in 2025 — operating alongside autonomous uncrewed "Collaborative Combat Aircraft" drones and advanced sensors, weapons and networking. The program is intended to succeed the F-22 Raptor as the Air Force's premier air superiority system.

Background

Studies began in the 2010s under DARPA's Air Dominance Initiative and the Air Force's Air Superiority 2030 flight plan. NGAD emphasised a "family of systems" approach and rapid, digital engineering: a classified demonstrator flew in 2020, as disclosed by then-acquisition chief Will Roper, who promoted the idea of a "Digital Century Series" of rapidly iterated designs.

F-47 selection

Following a pause and cost review in 2024 under Air Force Secretary Frank Kendall, in March 2025 President Donald Trump announced that Boeing had won the engineering and manufacturing development contract for the crewed fighter, named the F-47 — the designation honouring both the 47th president and the Air Force's founding year of 1947. The aircraft is expected to have stealth, long range, and Mach 2-class speed, and to enter service in the 2030s. A parallel U.S. Navy program, F/A-XX, pursues a carrier-based counterpart.

Related systems

The program includes Collaborative Combat Aircraft — with designs from General Atomics and Anduril selected for the first increment — adaptive-cycle engines developed under the Next Generation Adaptive Propulsion program, and new long-range weapons and battle-management networks.
