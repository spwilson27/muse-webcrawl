3rd Belorussian Front

The 3rd Belorussian Front (Russian: 3-й Белорусский фронт) was a major formation of the Red Army during the Second World War. A "front" in Soviet terminology was roughly equivalent to an army group in other armies. The 3rd Belorussian Front was formed on 24 April 1944 from the northern part of the Western Front, in preparation for the great summer offensive against German Army Group Centre.

Operations

Under the command of General Ivan Chernyakhovsky, the front played a leading role in Operation Bagration (June–August 1944), liberating Vitebsk, Minsk, and Vilnius and advancing to the borders of East Prussia. In October 1944 its forces made the first incursion onto German soil in the Gumbinnen Operation. From January 1945 the front conducted the East Prussian Offensive, encircling and destroying German forces in the province; Chernyakhovsky was killed by shellfire near Mehlsack in February 1945 and was succeeded by Marshal Aleksandr Vasilevsky. The front stormed Königsberg in April 1945 and eliminated the remaining German pockets around the Vistula Lagoon before being disbanded in August 1945.

Composition

At various times the front included the 5th, 11th Guards, 31st, 39th, 43rd, and 50th Armies, the 1st Air Army, and the 5th Guards Tank Army, among other formations, making it one of the largest Red Army groupings of the war's final phase.
