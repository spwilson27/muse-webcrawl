Golmaal Returns is a 2008 Indian Hindi-language comedy film directed by Rohit Shetty and produced by Dhilin Mehta. It is the second instalment in the Golmaal film series, following Golmaal: Fun Unlimited (2006). The film stars Ajay Devgn, Kareena Kapoor, Arshad Warsi, Shreyas Talpade, and Tusshar Kapoor, and is loosely inspired by the 1989 Marathi comedy Pheka Pheki.

Plot

Gopal (Ajay Devgn) is trapped in a loveless misunderstanding with his suspicious wife Ekta (Kareena Kapoor), an obsessive fan of television soap operas who doubts his fidelity. After Gopal spends a night stuck on a yacht following an accident, he invents an alibi involving a fictitious friend, setting off a chain of lies, mistaken identities, and slapstick complications that entangle his friends and family, while a murder investigation runs in parallel.

Reception

Released during the Diwali holiday period, the film was a major commercial success at the box office, though reviews were mixed, with praise for the performances and comic set pieces and criticism of the script. Its success strengthened the franchise, which continued with Golmaal 3 (2010) and Golmaal Again (2017).
