Keith Richards (born 18 December 1943), sometimes credited as Keith Richard, is an English musician, singer and songwriter. He is the co-founder, guitarist, secondary vocalist and co-principal songwriter of the Rolling Stones, whose songwriting partnership with Mick Jagger is among the most successful in history.

Style

Richards is known for rhythm guitar built on open G tuning with five strings, and for riffs anchoring songs such as "(I Can't Get No) Satisfaction", "Jumpin' Jack Flash" and "Start Me Up". Rolling Stone magazine ranked him fourth on its list of the 100 greatest guitarists.

Solo work and writing

He has released solo albums including Talk Is Cheap (1988) and published the best-selling autobiography Life in 2010. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the Stones in 1989.
