Electric Ireland is the retail energy supply division of the ESB Group, Ireland's state-owned electricity company. It supplies electricity and natural gas to residential and business customers in the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland.

History

The Electricity Supply Board (ESB) was founded in 1927 to manage Ireland's electricity generation and supply, beginning with the Shannon hydroelectric scheme at Ardnacrusha. For most of the twentieth century ESB held a monopoly on electricity supply in Ireland. As the market was deregulated, the retail arm was rebranded as Electric Ireland in 2011 to compete with new entrants such as Bord Gáis Energy and Airtricity.

Services

Electric Ireland offers electricity and dual-fuel tariffs, smart-meter services, home energy upgrades, electric vehicle charging products, and green energy plans sourced partly from renewable generation.

Sponsorship

The company has sponsored Irish sporting and cultural events, including the GAA Minor Championships and the Electric Ireland Sigerson Cup in Gaelic games.
