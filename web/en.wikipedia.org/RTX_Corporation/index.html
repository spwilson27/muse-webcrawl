Industry: Aerospace and defense
Founded: 3 April 2020 (merger of Raytheon and United Technologies)
Headquarters: Arlington, Virginia, U.S.

RTX Corporation, formerly Raytheon Technologies Corporation, is an American multinational aerospace and defense conglomerate. It was formed in 2020 by the merger of the Raytheon Company with the aerospace businesses of United Technologies, creating one of the world's largest aerospace and defense companies by revenue and market capitalization.

Business units

RTX operates through three principal segments: Collins Aerospace (avionics and aircraft systems), Pratt & Whitney (aircraft engines, including the F135 for the F-35 and the geared turbofan family), and Raytheon (missiles, radars, and defense systems such as the Patriot air-defense system and Tomahawk cruise missile).

History

The merger was announced in 2019 and completed on 3 April 2020; United Technologies' Otis and Carrier businesses were spun off at the same time. The company adopted the name RTX in 2023. It is a major contractor to the United States Department of Defense and allied governments.
