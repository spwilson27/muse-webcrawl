The Socialist Revolutionary Party (Russian: Партия социалистов-революционеров, Partiya sotsialistov-revolyutsionerov; the SRs or Esers) was a democratic socialist and agrarian socialist political party in the Russian Empire and early Soviet Russia. Founded in 1902 by unifying various narodnik (populist) groups, it championed the peasantry—then the vast majority of Russia's population—and demanded the "socialisation" of the land: abolition of private ownership and distribution to those who worked it.

History to 1917

The SRs inherited the narodnik tradition and, notoriously, its use of terrorism: the party's Combat Organisation, later run by the double agent Yevno Azef, assassinated ministers including Vyacheslav von Plehve (1904) and Grand Duke Sergei Alexandrovich (1905). The party gained representation in the Dumas after 1905 and became the largest party in the country by membership. During the First World War it split between defencists, who supported the war effort, and internationalists, who opposed it.

1917 and the revolution

After the February Revolution of 1917, the SRs were the most popular party in Russia. Their member Alexander Kerensky became head of the Provisional Government, while Viktor Chernov served as Minister of Agriculture. In the elections to the Constituent Assembly in November 1917—the freest in Russia's history to that date—the SRs won a plurality of about 40% of the vote, well ahead of the Bolsheviks' roughly 24%. The party had by then split: the Left SRs allied with the Bolsheviks and joined the Soviet government, while the majority Right SRs opposed them.

Suppression

The Bolsheviks dispersed the Constituent Assembly in January 1918 after a single session. SRs supported anti-Bolshevik governments during the Civil War, including the Komuch government at Samara, but were distrusted by White military leaders. The Left SRs broke with the Bolsheviks over the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk and staged an uprising in July 1918. In 1922 the Soviet regime staged a show trial of SR leaders; by the early 1920s the party was destroyed inside Russia, surviving only among émigrés.
