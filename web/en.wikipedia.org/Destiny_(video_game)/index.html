Destiny (video game)

Destiny is an online-only multiplayer first-person shooter video game developed by Bungie and published by Activision. It was released worldwide on September 9, 2014, for the PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, Xbox 360, and Xbox One consoles. Players take on the role of a Guardian, defenders of Earth's last safe city, wielding a power called Light derived from a celestial being known as the Traveler, as they seek to protect humanity against various alien races.

Gameplay

Destiny's gameplay combines first-person shooting with role-playing and MMO elements in a "shared-world" environment. Activities are divided between player versus environment (PvE) missions, strikes, and raids, and player versus player (PvP) modes in the Crucible. Players choose from three classes — Titan, Hunter, and Warlock — each with distinct subclasses and abilities.

Development and release

The game was Bungie's first new franchise after the Halo series and the first product of its ten-year publishing deal with Activision. It received mixed reviews at launch, with criticism directed at its thin story and repetitive structure, though its gunplay and art direction were praised. Four expansions followed: The Dark Below (2014), House of Wolves (2015), The Taken King (2015), and Rise of Iron (2016). A sequel, Destiny 2, was released in 2017.

Setting

Set roughly 700 years in the future, Destiny follows humanity after a Golden Age brought on by the arrival of the Traveler, followed by a catastrophic Collapse that left the species on the brink of extinction, beset by the forces of the Darkness.
