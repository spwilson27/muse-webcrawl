Wikimedia Foundation

The Wikimedia Foundation, Inc. (WMF) is an American 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization headquartered in San Francisco, California, and registered there. It owns and operates the Wikimedia projects, including Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Wikimedia Commons, Wikisource, Wikibooks, Wikidata, Wikivoyage, Wikinews, Wikiquote, Wikispecies, Wikiversity and Wikifunctions.

History

The foundation was announced on 20 June 2003 by Jimmy Wales, co-founder of Wikipedia, as a way to fund Wikipedia and its sister projects through non-profit means. Wikipedia's servers and trademarks were transferred from Wales's company Bomis to the foundation. The foundation is funded mainly by donations from readers, along with grants from other foundations.

Role

The Wikimedia Foundation provides the technical infrastructure, legal support and funding for the Wikimedia projects, but does not write or control the content of Wikipedia, which is created by volunteer editors. The foundation employs several hundred staff and is governed by a Board of Trustees, partly elected by the Wikimedia community. Its chief executive has included Sue Gardner, Lila Tretikov, Katherine Maher, and Maryana Iskander.
