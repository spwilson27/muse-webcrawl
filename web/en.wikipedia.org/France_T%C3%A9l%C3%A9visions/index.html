France Télévisions is the French national public television broadcaster. It operates the main public channels France 2 and France 3, along with France 4, France 5, the rolling news channel France Info, and the overseas network La Première.

History

The group was created in 1992 by bringing together the France 2 and France 3 channels under a common presidency, and expanded in 2000 into the holding company France Télévisions. In 2009 France's public channels ceased carrying commercial advertising in the evening, and advertising was later largely phased out.

Services

Beyond its broadcast channels, France Télévisions runs the streaming platform france.tv, thematic digital services for children, culture, and sport, and it is a major funder of French film and television production. It carries the Tour de France, the French Open tennis, and the Olympic Games for French audiences.

Funding and governance

The broadcaster is funded mainly from public resources (historically an annual licence fee, replaced in 2022 by an allocation from VAT revenue). Its president is appointed through a process involving the French audiovisual regulator.
