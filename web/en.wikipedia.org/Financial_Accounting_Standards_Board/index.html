Financial Accounting Standards Board

The Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB) is a private, non-profit organisation, established in 1973 in Norwalk, Connecticut, that sets financial accounting and reporting standards (US GAAP) for public and private companies and non-profits in the United States.

It is recognised by the Securities and Exchange Commission and overseen by the Financial Accounting Foundation. Its international counterpart is the IASB, issuer of IFRS.
