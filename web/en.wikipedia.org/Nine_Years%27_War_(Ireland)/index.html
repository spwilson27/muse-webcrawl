Nine Years' War (Ireland)

Date1593–1603
LocationIreland
ResultEnglish victory; Treaty of Mellifont
BelligerentsIrish alliance (Tyrone, Tyrconnell) and Spain; Kingdom of England and loyalist Irish

The Nine Years' War (Irish: Cogadh na Naoi mBliana), sometimes called Tyrone's Rebellion, took place in Ireland from 1593 to 1603. It was fought between an Irish confederacy led by Hugh O'Neill, Earl of Tyrone, and Hugh Roe O'Donnell of Tyrconnell, against English rule under Elizabeth I.

Course of the war

The Irish alliance won notable victories, including the Battle of Clontibret (1595) and the Battle of the Yellow Ford (1598), the worst defeat suffered by English arms in Ireland. Spain intervened in 1601, landing troops at Kinsale, but the Spanish and Irish forces were defeated there in the Battle of Kinsale (1601–02). Lord Mountjoy's scorched-earth campaigns then ground down the rebellion in Ulster.

Settlement

O'Neill submitted shortly after Elizabeth's death, signing the Treaty of Mellifont in March 1603, which allowed him to retain his lands in return for submission to the Crown. In 1607, O'Neill and O'Donnell left Ireland in the Flight of the Earls, paving the way for the Plantation of Ulster.
