List of emperors of Japan

The emperor of Japan is the head of state of Japan and the head of the Imperial House of Japan. According to tradition, the imperial line was founded by Emperor Jimmu in 660 BC, making it the oldest continuous hereditary monarchy in the world. The following list includes the legendary emperors of the early period as recorded in the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki chronicles, followed by the historically attested sovereigns. The current emperor is Naruhito, who acceded to the throne on 1 May 2019.
