American Veterinary Medical Association
Abbreviation: AVMA
Formation: 1863
Headquarters: Schaumburg, Illinois
Type: Professional association

The American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA), founded in 1863, is a not-for-profit association representing more than 100,000 veterinarians in the United States working in private and corporate practice, government, industry, academia, and uniformed services.

Activities

The AVMA develops policy on animal welfare, public health, and veterinary practice; accredits veterinary colleges through its Council on Education; and publishes the Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association (JAVMA) and the American Journal of Veterinary Research.

History

Founded in New York in 1863 as the United States Veterinary Medical Association, the organization adopted its current name in 1898 and grew with the professionalization of veterinary medicine in the twentieth century.
