World Turtle

Image placeholder: Illustration of the World Turtle carrying the world

The World Turtle — also called the Cosmic Turtle or World-bearing Turtle — is a mytheme of a giant turtle, or tortoise, supporting or containing the world. It occurs in Hindu mythology, Chinese mythology, and the mythologies of some Indigenous peoples of the Americas.

In mythology

In Hindu mythology the world is supported by chained creatures culminating in the tortoise Akupara, on whose back stand elephants holding up the world. In Chinese mythology the legs of the sea turtle Ao were used by the goddess Nüwa to prop up the sky after a catastrophe. In several Native North American traditions, a turtle's back forms the land known as Turtle Island.

In philosophy and fiction

The regress "turtles all the way down" derives from the image of an infinite stack of turtles supporting the world. A World Turtle named Great A'Tuin carries the Discworld in Terry Pratchett's novels.
