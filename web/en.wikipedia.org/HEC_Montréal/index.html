HEC Montréal (formerly École des hautes études commerciales de Montréal) is a French-language university business school in Montreal, Quebec, Canada, affiliated with the Université de Montréal. Founded in 1907, it was the first business school established in Canada and the first in North America to hold the three major international accreditations (AACSB, EQUIS, and AMBA).

History

The school was founded by the Montreal Chamber of Commerce, modelled on European schools of commerce, and became affiliated with the Université de Montréal in 1915. It launched the first MBA program in Canada offered in French, and has grown into one of the largest business schools in the country, awarding bachelor's (BBA), master's, MBA, and PhD degrees.

Campus and programs

HEC Montréal's main building, on Chemin de la Côte-Sainte-Catherine near the university campus, opened in 1996; a downtown building, the Hélène-Desmarais pavilion, opened in 2023. The school is known for programs in management, finance, logistics, and business analytics, a multilingual BBA taught in French, English, and Spanish, and a large international exchange network. Its teaching uses the case method extensively, and it maintains a well-known case-writing centre.

Research and reputation

The school hosts numerous research chairs and centres, publishes in leading management journals, and consistently ranks among Canada's top business schools in national and international rankings.
