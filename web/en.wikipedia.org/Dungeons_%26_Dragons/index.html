Dungeons & Dragons

DesignersGary Gygax, Dave Arneson
PublisherTSR; Wizards of the Coast
Publication1974 (original)
GenresFantasy, tabletop role-playing game

Dungeons & Dragons (commonly abbreviated as D&D or DnD) is a fantasy tabletop role-playing game originally created and designed by Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson. It was first published in 1974 by Tactical Studies Rules, Inc. (TSR). It has been published by Wizards of the Coast, later a subsidiary of Hasbro, since 1997.

Players form an adventuring party who explore fantasy worlds together, embark on quests, and engage in tactical combat. A Dungeon Master serves as referee and storyteller, while dice rolls — notably the twenty-sided die — resolve actions.

Editions

Major versions include the original 1974 game, Advanced Dungeons & Dragons (1977–1979), the 3rd edition (2000), 4th edition (2008), and the 5th edition (2014), the most popular to date, which fuelled a broader resurgence of the game in popular culture.

Influence

D&D is considered the beginning of modern role-playing games and has profoundly influenced video games, fantasy literature, and popular culture.
