Paraveterinary worker

A paraveterinary worker is a professional who assists a veterinarian or performs animal health care tasks under varying degrees of supervision. Titles and legal scopes differ by country and include veterinary nurse, veterinary technician, veterinary technologist, and veterinary assistant.

Roles

Paraveterinary workers take medical histories, restrain and examine animals, collect laboratory samples, run diagnostic tests, induce and monitor anaesthesia, assist in surgery, administer medicines, provide nursing and dental care, and educate owners. In rural and developing regions, community animal health workers may deliver front-line care such as vaccination and basic treatment where veterinarians are scarce.

Training and regulation

Formal qualifications range from certificates to bachelor's degrees, and many jurisdictions register or license practitioners — veterinary nursing has been a regulated profession in the United Kingdom since 2015, and credentialed technicians are recognized in the United States and Canada. The World Organisation for Animal Health encourages standardized training of veterinary paraprofessionals as part of national veterinary services.
