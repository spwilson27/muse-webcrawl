Narendra Modi

Born: 17 September 1950, Vadnagar, Gujarat, India
Office: Prime Minister of India (2014–present)
Party: Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)

Narendra Damodardas Modi (born 17 September 1950) is an Indian politician who has served as the prime minister of India since 2014. He previously served as chief minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014 and is a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), a Hindu nationalist organisation.

Early life and career

Modi was born in Vadnagar, in present-day Gujarat, and as a child helped his father sell tea at a railway station. He joined the RSS at a young age, became a full-time organiser, and was assigned to the BJP in the 1980s. He rose through organisational roles before becoming Gujarat's chief minister in 2001.

Chief Minister of Gujarat

His tenure was marked by rapid economic growth and infrastructure development — the "Gujarat model" — but also by the 2002 Gujarat riots, in which over a thousand people, mostly Muslims, were killed. Modi was accused of failing to prevent the violence; a Supreme Court-appointed investigation found no evidence to prosecute him, though the episode remains politically contentious.

Prime Minister

Modi led the BJP to a majority in 2014 — the first single-party majority in three decades — and again in 2019. His governments pursued economic reforms, digital infrastructure (including the UPI payments system and Aadhaar-linked welfare delivery), the Goods and Services Tax, and the "Make in India" initiative. Controversial measures included the 2016 demonetisation, the 2019 revocation of Jammu and Kashmir's special status, and citizenship legislation that drew large protests. In 2024 he won a third term at the head of a coalition government.
