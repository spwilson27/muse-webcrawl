Chromebook

Type: Laptop and tablet computers

Operating system: ChromeOS

Introduced: 2011

A Chromebook is a laptop, detachable, or tablet computer running Google's ChromeOS. The first commercial models from Acer and Samsung appeared in 2011. Chromebooks emphasize web applications, cloud storage, fast startup, long battery life, and simple fleet management.

Market

They became widespread in schools, particularly in the United States, and are sold at price points from inexpensive classroom machines to premium business and consumer models. Manufacturers include Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, and Samsung.

Capabilities

Modern Chromebooks support Android and Linux applications, offline files, external displays, and touchscreen or convertible designs. Their suitability depends on connectivity, required software, and the length of automatic-update support for each model.
