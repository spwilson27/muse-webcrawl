David Kelley (designer)

David M. Kelley (born February 10, 1951) is an American engineer, designer, entrepreneur, and educator. He is the founder of the design consultancy IDEO and a professor at Stanford University, where he co-founded the Hasso Plattner Institute of Design, known as the Stanford d.school.

Kelley earned an electrical engineering degree from Carnegie Mellon University in 1973 and a master's in engineering/product design from Stanford in 1978. After working on the Boeing 747 interiors and early Apple products, he founded David Kelley Design in 1978, which merged in 1991 with firms led by Bill Moggridge and Mike Nuttall to form IDEO.

Design thinking

Kelley is closely associated with the popularization of "design thinking," a human-centered methodology emphasizing empathy with users, interdisciplinary teams, rapid prototyping, and iterative testing. Under his leadership IDEO designed landmark products such as the first Apple mouse and the Palm V. Through the d.school, founded in 2005, these methods were brought into education across business, medicine, law, and the humanities.

With his brother Tom Kelley he co-authored Creative Confidence (2013). His honors include the Edison Achievement Award and induction into the National Academy of Engineering.
