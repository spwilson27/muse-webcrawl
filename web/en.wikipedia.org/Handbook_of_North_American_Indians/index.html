Handbook of North American Indians

The Handbook of North American Indians is a series of edited scholarly volumes published by the Smithsonian Institution summarizing knowledge about the Native peoples of North America.

Overview

Initiated under editor William C. Sturtevant, the series was planned in 20 volumes organized by culture area — including the Arctic, Subarctic, Northeast, Southeast, Plains, Plateau, Great Basin, California, Southwest, and Northwest Coast — plus volumes on languages, technology, history of Indian–white relations and a biographical volume. Publication began in 1978.

Significance

The Handbook is a standard reference in anthropology and Native American studies, though some planned volumes remain unpublished and knowledge in some areas has moved on since early volumes appeared.
