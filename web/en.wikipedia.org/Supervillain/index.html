Supervillain

A supervillain is a fictional villain character, typically from comic books, who possesses extraordinary powers, intellect, or resources and serves as the antagonist of a superhero. Supervillains often display flamboyant costumes, codenames, exaggerated ambitions such as world domination, and recurring roles across a hero's stories.

History

The type emerged in pulp magazines and early comic strips and flourished in the Golden Age of Comic Books, with characters such as Lex Luthor (opponent of Superman) and the Joker (opponent of Batman) becoming cultural icons. Earlier literary figures — Professor Moriarty, Doctor Fu Manchu, and Ernst Stavro Blofeld — are often cited as precursors.

Characteristics

Supervillains function as foils who test the hero's abilities and values. Many have tragic or mirroring backstories, and some, such as Magneto, are depicted with sympathetic motivations that blur the line between villain and anti-hero.
