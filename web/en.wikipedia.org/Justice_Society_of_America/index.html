Justice Society of America

The Justice Society of America (JSA) is a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and editor Sheldon Mayer, the team first appeared in All Star Comics #3 (Winter 1940–1941), making it the first team of superheroes in comic book history.

Publication history

The original lineup included Doctor Fate, Hourman, the Spectre, Sandman, Atom, the Flash (Jay Garrick), Green Lantern (Alan Scott) and Hawkman, with Wonder Woman later joining as the team's secretary. The JSA headlined All Star Comics through the Golden Age until 1951. In the 1960s the team was revived as the heroes of the parallel world Earth-Two, beginning annual team-ups with the Justice League of America that ran until Crisis on Infinite Earths merged the continuities in 1985.

Later incarnations

A relaunched JSA series (1999–2006), written by James Robinson, David Goyer and Geoff Johns, emphasised legacy and mentorship, mixing surviving Golden Age heroes with their successors. The team has since appeared in various DC relaunches and in television series such as Stargirl.
