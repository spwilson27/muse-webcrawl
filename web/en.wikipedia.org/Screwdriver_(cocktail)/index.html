Screwdriver (cocktail)

The screwdriver is a popular highball drink made with orange juice and vodka. It is one of the simplest and best known mixed drinks, classified by the International Bartenders Association as an official cocktail.

Screwdriver
Type: Highball
Ingredients: Vodka, orange juice
Served: On the rocks, in a highball glass
Garnish: Orange slice

Preparation

A basic screwdriver is made by pouring vodka over ice in a highball glass and topping with orange juice, typically in a ratio of about one part vodka to two parts orange juice. It is stirred and often garnished with an orange slice or wedge.

History

The drink's origins are disputed. One popular story attributes it to American oil workers in the Persian Gulf in the mid-20th century, who supposedly stirred vodka secretly added to orange juice with a screwdriver. Another account traces a version to American Marines or airmen during World War II. The drink was widely promoted in the United States in the 1950s as vodka marketing expanded.

Variations

Variations substitute other spirits or juices: a Harvey Wallbanger adds Galliano, a Fuzzy Navel uses peach schnapps, and versions with Southern Comfort or amaretto exist under various names. A screwdriver made with equal measures is sometimes called an Anita Bryant, a satirical reference dating to the singer's anti-gay campaign in the 1970s.
