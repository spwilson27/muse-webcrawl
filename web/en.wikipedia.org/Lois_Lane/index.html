Lois Lane is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Jerry Siegel and artist Joe Shuster, she first appeared in Action Comics #1 (June 1938), the same issue that introduced Superman, and is his primary love interest and, in most modern continuities, his wife.

Character

Lois is an award-winning investigative reporter for the Metropolis newspaper the Daily Planet, where she works alongside Clark Kent and Jimmy Olsen under editor Perry White. She is depicted as determined, sharp-witted, and fearless, frequently pursuing dangerous stories and often deducing — or coming close to deducing — Superman's secret identity.

In other media

The character has been portrayed in numerous adaptations, including by Noel Neill and Phyllis Coates in the 1950s television series, Margot Kidder in the 1978–1987 Superman films, Teri Hatcher in Lois & Clark, Erica Durance in Smallville, and Amy Adams in the DC Extended Universe films.
