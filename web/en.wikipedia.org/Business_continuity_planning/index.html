Business continuity planning

Business continuity planning (BCP) is the process of creating systems of prevention and recovery to deal with potential threats to a company, so that it can continue operating during and after a disruptive event such as a fire, flood, cyberattack or pandemic.

Process

A business impact analysis identifies critical functions and the effects of their interruption. A risk assessment then evaluates threats, and a plan is written setting out recovery strategies, alternate sites, backup systems and communication procedures, governed by measures such as the recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO).

Testing and standards

Plans are exercised through tabletop walkthroughs and full simulations and revised as the business changes. The international standard ISO 22301 specifies requirements for business continuity management systems; related disciplines include disaster recovery, focused on IT systems, and crisis management.
