Royal Netherlands Navy

The Royal Netherlands Navy (Dutch: Koninklijke Marine) is the naval force of the Kingdom of the Netherlands. Tracing its origins to 1488, it is one of the oldest navies in the world and played a leading role in the Dutch Golden Age of the 17th century under admirals such as Michiel de Ruyter.

History

During the Anglo-Dutch Wars (1652–1784) the Dutch fleet contested control of the seas with England; De Ruyter's raid on the Medway in 1667 remains a celebrated victory. In the Second World War the navy fought in the Battle of the Java Sea (1942), where Rear Admiral Karel Doorman's squadron was destroyed. The postwar navy contributed aircraft carriers and then modern missile frigates to NATO.

Modern fleet

The navy operates De Zeven Provinciën-class air-defence and command frigates, Karel Doorman-class multipurpose frigates, Holland-class offshore patrol vessels, Walrus-class submarines (to be replaced by the Orka class), landing platform docks, and the Netherlands Marine Corps. Dutch ships are identified by the prefix HNLMS (Zr.Ms./Hr.Ms.).
