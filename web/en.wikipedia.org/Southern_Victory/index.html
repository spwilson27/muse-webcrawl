Southern Victory

The Southern Victory series, also known as the "Timeline-191" series, is a series of alternate history novels by Harry Turtledove. The series begins with the Confederate States of America winning the American Civil War — the point of divergence turns on Robert E. Lee's Special Orders 191 not being lost, so that Lee wins the Battle of Antietam and secures British and French recognition — and follows North America through a second Confederate victory in the 1880s, the Great War, an interwar period, and a Second Great War ending in 1944.

Books

The series comprises eleven novels: the standalone How Few Remain (1997), the Great War trilogy (American Front, Walk in Hell, Breakthroughs), the American Empire trilogy (Blood and Iron, The Center Cannot Hold, The Victorious Opposition), and the Settling Accounts tetralogy (Return Engagement, Drive to the East, The Grapple, In at the Death).

Setting

In the series, the rump United States allies with Imperial Germany and wins the alternate First World War, while the Confederacy, led in the 1930s by the fascist-style Freedom Party under Jake Featherston, launches a genocidal campaign against its Black population during the Second Great War, which ends with Confederate defeat and American atomic bombings of Philadelphia and other cities. The series is noted for dense parallel characterization and for mapping real twentieth-century history onto North America.
