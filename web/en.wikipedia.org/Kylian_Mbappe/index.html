Kylian Mbappé

Kylian Mbappé, French forward, among the world's best players.

Kylian Mbappé Lottin (born 20 December 1998) is a French professional footballer who plays as a forward. Regarded as one of the best players in the world, he is known for his exceptional pace, dribbling, and finishing. He starred for AS Monaco and Paris Saint-Germain before transferring to Real Madrid in 2024, and he captains the France national team.

Career

Born in Paris and raised in Bondy, Mbappé joined Monaco's academy and made his first-team debut at 16. In 2016–17 he helped Monaco win Ligue 1 and reach the Champions League semi-finals, earning a loan to Paris Saint-Germain that became a permanent transfer for a fee reported around €180 million — making him the second most expensive player ever and the most expensive teenager. With PSG he won multiple Ligue 1 titles and became the club's all-time top scorer before joining Real Madrid on a free transfer in the summer of 2024.

Internationally, Mbappé won the 2018 FIFA World Cup with France, scoring in the final against Croatia and winning the tournament's Best Young Player award — the first teenager to score in a World Cup final since Pelé. At the 2022 World Cup in Qatar he won the Golden Boot with eight goals, including a hat-trick in the final against Argentina, though France lost on penalties. His World Cup final goal tally is among the highest in history.

Playing style

Mbappé combines explosive acceleration and top speed with close control at pace, clinical finishing with his right foot, and intelligent movement off the ball; he is frequently compared with Thierry Henry and Ronaldo Nazário.
