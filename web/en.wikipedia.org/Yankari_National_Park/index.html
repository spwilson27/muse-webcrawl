Yankari National Park

LocationBauchi State, Nigeria
Area~2,244 km²
Established1962 as a game reserve; national park 1991

Yankari National Park is a large wildlife park in Bauchi State, north-eastern Nigeria. It is the country's best-known safari destination and protects one of the largest remaining populations of elephants in West Africa.

Wildlife

The park's savanna and riverine forest support elephants, lions, leopards, buffalo, roan antelope, hartebeest, baboons, and hippopotamus, along with more than 350 recorded bird species. Wildlife viewing is best in the dry season, when animals gather near the Gaji River.

Wikki Warm Spring

The park is famous for the Wikki Warm Spring, a natural spring maintaining a constant temperature of about 31°C, where visitors can swim. Other springs and the Marshall Cave systems are also found within the park.

Conservation

Management was transferred to the Bauchi State government in 2006, and conservation groups have worked with the park on anti-poaching patrols and elephant protection.
