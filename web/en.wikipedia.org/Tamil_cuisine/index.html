Tamil cuisine

Tamil cuisine is a culinary style originating in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu and neighboring regions. Rice is the staple grain, accompanied by lentils, legumes, tamarind, and a wide array of spices. Meals are traditionally served on a banana leaf and eaten by hand.

Staple dishes

Common dishes include idli (steamed rice cakes), dosa (fermented rice and lentil crêpes), sambar (a lentil and vegetable stew flavored with tamarind), rasam (a peppery soup), and pongal (a rice and dal porridge). Filter coffee, brewed strong with frothed milk, is an iconic beverage.

Regional varieties

The Chettinad region is famous for fiery meat and seafood dishes made with freshly ground spices, while temple towns developed elaborate vegetarian traditions. Meals may be categorized as sapadu, a full banana-leaf meal with multiple courses.
