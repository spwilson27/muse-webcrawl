Christian Louboutin

Born7 January 1963, Paris, France
OccupationFashion designer
Known forRed-soled stiletto shoes

Christian Louboutin (born 1963) is a French fashion designer whose high-end stiletto shoes, identifiable by their shiny red-lacquered soles, have become his signature. His eponymous company, founded in 1991, sells luxury footwear, handbags, and beauty products worldwide.

Career

Louboutin began sketching shoes as a teenager and trained at the Académie d'Art Roederer. After working as a freelance designer for houses including Chanel and Yves Saint Laurent, and a detour into landscape gardening, he opened his first boutique in Paris in 1991, with Princess Caroline of Monaco among his early clients. The red sole — reportedly inspired by an assistant's nail polish, which he used to paint a prototype's sole — debuted in 1993 and became one of fashion's most recognisable trademarks, the subject of notable trademark litigation.

Design

Louboutin's designs emphasise extremely high heels, ornamentation, and what he describes as making women feel beautiful and empowered. His shoes are worn by numerous celebrities and have become fixtures of popular culture, referenced in music and film.
