The United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP) is a United Nations peacekeeping force established by Security Council Resolution 186 in 1964 to prevent a recurrence of fighting between Greek Cypriots and Turkish Cypriots.

History and mandate

Originally deployed for three months, the force has remained ever since, making it one of the longest-running UN peacekeeping missions. After the events of 1974, UNFICYP took on the supervision of ceasefire lines and the maintenance of a buffer zone — the "Green Line" — stretching some 180 kilometres across Cyprus and through Nicosia.

Activities

UNFICYP patrols the buffer zone, facilitates humanitarian access and crossings, and supports the UN Secretary-General's good offices aimed at a negotiated settlement of the Cyprus issue.
