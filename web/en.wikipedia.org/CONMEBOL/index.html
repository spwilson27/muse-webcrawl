CONMEBOL

The South American Football Confederation (Spanish: Confederación Sudamericana de Fútbol; Portuguese: Confederação Sul-Americana de Futebol), known by its acronym CONMEBOL, is the continental governing body for association football in South America and one of FIFA's six continental confederations.

History and membership

Founded on 9 July 1916 in Buenos Aires, CONMEBOL is the oldest continental football confederation. It has ten member associations: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay and Venezuela — the fewest of any confederation. Its headquarters are in Luque, Paraguay, near Asunción.

Competitions

CONMEBOL organises the Copa América, the oldest international football tournament, first held in 1916, and the club competitions Copa Libertadores (since 1960) and Copa Sudamericana. South American teams have won ten FIFA World Cups, through Brazil (five), Argentina (three) and Uruguay (two).
