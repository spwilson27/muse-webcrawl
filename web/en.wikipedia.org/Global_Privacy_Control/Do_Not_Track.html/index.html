Do Not Track

Do Not Track (DNT) is a proposed HTTP header field and browser setting that requests that a website or web application disable tracking of the individual user. Proposed in 2009 by researchers and privacy advocates, it was implemented in major browsers, but compliance by websites was voluntary, and with no agreed definition of "tracking" the header was widely ignored.

The World Wide Web Consortium's Tracking Protection Working Group attempted to standardise DNT but closed in 2019 without consensus. The successor effort, Global Privacy Control (GPC), transmits a similar opt-out signal (Sec-GPC: 1) but is backed by enforceable privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, giving it legal effect that DNT lacked.
