List of mountains in Norway by height

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of mountains in Norway by height ranks the highest peaks of mainland Norway. The country's highest mountains lie in the Jotunheimen massif in the Scandinavian Mountains of southern Norway. There are nearly 300 peaks in Norway above 2,000 metres (6,562 ft). Norway's Svalbard archipelago, listed separately, has lower peaks, the highest being Newtontoppen (1,713 m).

Notes

Glittertind's summit includes a glacier whose thickness varies; at times its ice cap has made it rival or exceed Galdhøpiggen, whose summit is bare rock. Outside Jotunheimen, notable high areas include Rondane (Rondeslottet, 2,178 m), Dovrefjell (Snøhetta, 2,286 m), and the Lyngen Alps in the north.
