Tønsberg is a city and municipality in Vestfold county, Norway, traditionally regarded as the oldest town in Norway, founded in 871 according to saga accounts.

History

Tønsberg was an important Viking Age centre and later a Hanseatic trading port. The Oseberg ship, one of the finest surviving Viking ships, was excavated from a burial mound near the town in 1904.

Economy

Historically a whaling and shipping centre, the modern town has industries in metalworking, paper, and services, and is a popular summer destination for boating on the Oslofjord.

Landmarks

Castle Hill (Slottsfjellet) bears the ruins of a medieval fortress and a 19th-century tower that is the town's symbol.
