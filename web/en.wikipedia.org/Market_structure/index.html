Market structure

In economics, market structure refers to how different industries are classified and differentiated based on the degree and nature of competition between companies for goods and services. It is based partly on the number of firms and the nature of their products.

The main forms are perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, duopoly, and monopoly, differing in the number of sellers, barriers to entry, and pricing power.

Market structure analysis informs antitrust and competition policy.
