Liberia

Republic of Liberia
Capital: Monrovia
Official language: English
Currency: Liberian dollar
Independence: 26 July 1847

Liberia, officially the Republic of Liberia, is a country on the West African coast, bordered by Sierra Leone, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire and the Atlantic Ocean. Its population is around five million and its capital and largest city is Monrovia.

History

Liberia began in the early 19th century as a project of the American Colonization Society, which settled freed African Americans on the coast; it declared independence in 1847, making it Africa's first and oldest modern republic. Americo-Liberian settlers dominated politics until the 1980 coup of Samuel Doe. Two civil wars (1989–1997 and 1999–2003) devastated the country; peace followed under President Ellen Johnson Sirleaf, the first elected female head of state in Africa.

Geography and economy

The country has a tropical climate, mangrove coasts and dense rainforests inland. Its economy depends on iron ore, rubber, timber and shipping — Liberia operates one of the world's largest maritime registries. English is the official language, alongside numerous indigenous languages.
