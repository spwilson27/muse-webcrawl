D.I.C.E. Awards

The D.I.C.E. Awards (formerly the Interactive Achievement Awards) are an annual awards ceremony honouring the video game industry, presented by the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences (AIAS) during the D.I.C.E. Summit in Las Vegas. "D.I.C.E." stands for "Design, Innovate, Communicate, Entertain".

History

The awards began in 1998 as the Interactive Achievement Awards and were renamed the D.I.C.E. Awards for the 2013 ceremony. Winners are voted on by the Academy's membership of industry professionals, and the Game of the Year prize is the ceremony's highest honour. Past Game of the Year winners include The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, Half-Life 2, The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, God of War (2018) and Elden Ring.

Special awards

The ceremony also inducts individuals into the AIAS Hall of Fame — beginning with Shigeru Miyamoto — and presents Pioneer and Lifetime Achievement awards recognising foundational contributions to the medium.
