The University of California, Riverside (UCR or UC Riverside) is a public land-grant research university in Riverside, California. It is one of the ten campuses of the University of California system. The university traces its origins to the UC Citrus Experiment Station, established in 1907, and opened as a college of letters and science in 1954.

Academics

UCR enrolls roughly 26,000 students and is organized into colleges including the College of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences; the College of Natural and Agricultural Sciences; the Marlan and Rosemary Bourns College of Engineering; the School of Business; the School of Education; the School of Public Policy; and the School of Medicine, which opened in 2013. The university is noted for entomology, citrus research, and science-fiction studies holdings, including the Eaton Collection.

Campus

The main campus sits about three miles east of downtown Riverside near the Box Springs Mountains; its landmark is the 161-foot Bell Tower (carillon tower) built in the 1960s. Athletic teams, the Highlanders, compete in the Big West Conference.
