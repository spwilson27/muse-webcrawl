Roll Deep

OriginBow, East London, England
GenresGrime, UK garage
Years active2001–present
Notable membersWiley, Dizzee Rascal (former), Tinchy Stryder (former), Skepta (former)

Roll Deep are a British grime crew formed in Bow, East London, in the early 2000s, emerging from the UK garage scene and pirate radio. Founded by Wiley, the crew became one of the founding forces of grime music, helping to launch the careers of Dizzee Rascal, Tinchy Stryder and Skepta, among others.

History

Roll Deep grew out of the Pay As U Go Cartel garage crew and built their reputation through pirate stations such as Déjà Vu 92.3 FM, releasing white-label records and mixtapes. Their debut album In at the Deep End (2005) crossed over to the charts, and in 2010 they achieved two UK number-one singles, "Good Times" and "Green Light", adopting a more pop-oriented sound. Later releases returned to a harder grime style, and members have continued as solo artists and producers central to the genre's development.

Legacy

Roll Deep are widely regarded as one of the most influential collectives in grime, both for their own releases and for the number of major solo stars who passed through their ranks.
