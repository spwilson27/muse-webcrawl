Saravanan Sivakumar (born 23 July 1975), known professionally as Suriya, is an Indian actor and film producer who primarily works in Tamil cinema. He is one of the highest-paid actors of Indian cinema and has featured in Forbes India's Celebrity 100 list several times. His accolades include two National Film Awards, six Filmfare Awards South, and five Tamil Nadu State Film Awards.

Early life

Suriya was born in Madras (now Chennai) to actor Sivakumar and Lakshmi Sivakumar. He attended Padma Seshadri Bala Bhavan School and St. Bede's Anglo Indian Higher Secondary School in Chennai, and obtained a bachelor's degree in commerce from Loyola College, Chennai. Before entering films, he worked in a garment factory, concealing his identity as an actor's son. His younger brother Karthi also became a successful actor.

Career

Suriya made his debut in Nerukku Ner (1997). After a series of commercially unsuccessful films, he had his breakthrough as a rowdy in Bala's Nandha (2001). Major successes followed, including the action thriller Kaakha Kaakha (2003), the drama Pithamagan (2003), the psychological thriller Ghajini (2005), and the coming-of-age drama Vaaranam Aayiram (2008). He played dual roles including a conjoined twin in Maattrraan (2012), and earned acclaim for essaying contrasting characters in films such as Singam (2010) and its sequels. In the 2020s he won the National Film Award for Best Actor for Soorarai Pottru (2020), inspired by the life of airline entrepreneur G. R. Gopinath, and starred in Jai Bhim (2021), a legal drama widely praised by critics, and in the period action film Kanguva (2024). He also played the antagonist Rolex in Vikram (2022).

Production and philanthropy

Suriya founded the production company 2D Entertainment in 2013, which has produced films including 36 Vayadhinile, Pasanga 2, and his own later ventures. In 2006 he established the Agaram Foundation, which works to improve access to quality education for underprivileged students in Tamil Nadu and has supported thousands of first-generation learners through higher education.

Personal life

Suriya married actress Jyothika in 2006, after appearing with her in several films; the couple have two children. He is known for maintaining a low profile outside his film work and for his involvement in educational and environmental causes.
