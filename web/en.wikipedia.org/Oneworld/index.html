Oneworld

Oneworld (stylised as oneworld) is a global airline alliance founded on 1 February 1999. Its members include Alaska Airlines, American Airlines, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Fiji Airways, Finnair, Iberia, Japan Airlines, Qantas, Qatar Airways, Royal Air Maroc, Royal Jordanian, and SriLankan Airlines, serving more than 900 destinations in over 170 territories.

The alliance was announced in 1998 by founding members American Airlines, British Airways, Canadian Airlines, Cathay Pacific and Qantas. Its central management team, Oneworld Management Company, is based in Fort Worth, Texas, alongside American Airlines headquarters, having moved from New York City in 2022.

Benefits and tiers

Oneworld offers three elite tiers — Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald — mapped to member frequent-flyer programmes, providing priority check-in, lounge access and extra baggage allowances. Members codeshare, coordinate schedules and offer reciprocal mileage earning and redemption.

History

Canadian Airlines left after its acquisition by Air Canada in 2000. Aer Lingus left in 2007 but rejoined indirectly via IAG. Other former members include airberlin, LATAM, Malév, Mexicana and S7 Airlines, suspended in 2022 after Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Oman Air joined on 30 June 2025 as the alliance's fifteenth member.
