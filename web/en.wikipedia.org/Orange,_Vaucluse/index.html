Orange, Vaucluse

Orange is a commune in the Vaucluse department in the Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur region in southeastern France, about 21 km north of Avignon. It is famous for well-preserved Roman monuments, including its theatre and triumphal arch, both UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

History

Founded as Arausio around 35 BC for veterans of Rome's legions, Orange later gave its name to the Principality of Orange, held by the House of Orange-Nassau, whose title 'Prince of Orange' endures in the Dutch monarchy. The city passed to France by the Treaty of Utrecht (1713).

Sights

The Roman Theatre of Orange, built under Augustus, retains its massive stage wall and hosts the Chorégies d'Orange opera festival. The Triumphal Arch dates to the early 1st century AD.
