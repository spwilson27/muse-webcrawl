Legality of cannabis

The legality of cannabis for recreational and medical use varies worldwide. Cannabis is prohibited in most countries under international drug conventions, but many jurisdictions have legalised, decriminalised or tolerated some use.

Recreational use

Uruguay (2013) and Canada (2018) were the first modern states to legalise recreational cannabis nationally; others, including several US states, Germany, Malta and South Africa, have adopted legal or decriminalised regimes, while the Netherlands tolerates retail sale in coffee shops.

Medical use

Many more countries permit medical cannabis under prescription or licence, with differing controls on production and supply.
