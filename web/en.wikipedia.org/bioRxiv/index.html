bioRxiv (pronounced "bio-archive") is an open-access preprint repository for the biological sciences, co-founded by John Inglis and Richard Sever and hosted by Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory (CSHL). It launched in November 2013, modelled on the physics archive arXiv, allowing life scientists to post manuscripts before formal peer review.

Operation and growth

Submissions are screened for appropriateness and plagiarism but are not peer-reviewed; authors may post revised versions and link the preprint to its eventual journal publication. Adoption grew rapidly in the mid-2010s, and bioRxiv became a major channel for rapid dissemination in genomics, neuroscience and cell biology. Together with its sister server medRxiv (launched 2019 for the health sciences), it played a prominent role in the fast sharing of research during the COVID-19 pandemic, while also fuelling debate about media reporting of unreviewed findings.
