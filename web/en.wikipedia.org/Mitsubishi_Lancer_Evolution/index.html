The Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution, commonly called the Lancer Evo, is a sports sedan and rally car produced by Mitsubishi Motors from 1992 to 2016. Based on the Lancer compact car, ten official generations were built, designated Evolution I through Evolution X, each combining a turbocharged two-litre engine with all-wheel drive and increasingly sophisticated electronic chassis control.

History

The Evolution was created to homologate Mitsubishi's entry in the World Rally Championship, transplanting the drivetrain of the Galant VR-4 into the smaller Lancer. In rallying, Tommi Mäkinen won four consecutive WRC drivers' championships (1996–1999) in Lancer Evolutions, and Mitsubishi took the 1998 manufacturers' title. On the road, each generation raised output toward the informal Japanese "gentlemen's agreement" limit of 280 PS and beyond; later models introduced Active Yaw Control, and the final Evolution X (2007–2016) switched to a new 4B11 engine and an optional dual-clutch transmission. Production ended with the Lancer Evolution Final Edition in 2016.

Rivalry and culture

The Evolution's long-standing rivalry with the Subaru Impreza WRX STI, forged in rallying, made both cars icons of 1990s–2000s car culture, celebrated in video games and films and widely modified by enthusiasts.
