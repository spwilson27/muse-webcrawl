Upstate New York

Upstate New York is the region of the U.S. state of New York north of the New York City metropolitan area. Its exact boundary is debated, but it generally includes the Hudson Valley, Capital District, Finger Lakes, Western New York and the North Country.

Geography

Major cities include Buffalo, Rochester, Syracuse and Albany. The region contains the Adirondack and Catskill mountains and borders the Great Lakes and Canada.

Economy

Historically manufacturing along the Erie Canal powered growth; today health care, education, technology and tourism are important.

Culture

Upstate identity is often contrasted with downstate New York City and its suburbs, shaping state politics and regional pride.
