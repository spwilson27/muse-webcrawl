The Emilia Romagna Grand Prix is a Formula One motor racing event held at the Autodromo Internazionale Enzo e Dino Ferrari in Imola, Italy. The race was added to the calendar in 2020 as part of the revised schedule during the COVID-19 pandemic and takes its name from the Emilia-Romagna region in which the circuit is located. Imola had previously hosted the Italian Grand Prix in 1980 and the San Marino Grand Prix from 1981 to 2006.

History

The inaugural Emilia Romagna Grand Prix in November 2020 used an unusual two-day format and was won by Lewis Hamilton for Mercedes. Max Verstappen won the 2021 race — a wet-dry event in which Hamilton recovered from an off-track excursion to finish second — and repeated the victory in 2022, when the weekend also hosted a sprint race and torrential rain affected running.

The 2023 race was cancelled because of severe flooding in the Emilia-Romagna region, which made it impossible to hold the event safely and would have diverted emergency resources. Racing returned in 2024, when Verstappen held off a late charge from Lando Norris to win by less than a second. The event was widely known to be under pressure for its place on an expanding calendar, with its contract running through 2025; the race was not included on the 2026 calendar, its slot effectively yielding to a new event in Madrid.
