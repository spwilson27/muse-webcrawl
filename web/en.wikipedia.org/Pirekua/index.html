Pirekua

The pirekua is a traditional song form of the Purépecha (P'urhépecha) people of the Mexican state of Michoacán. Sung in the Purépecha language, often accompanied by guitar, violin and double bass, it is characterised by slow, lyrical melodies and poetic verses about love, nature, social life and the history of the Purépecha nation.

Characteristics

Pirekuas are typically performed by small ensembles known as pireris, singer-composers who improvise verses within a recognisable melodic framework. The repertoire includes both older anonymous songs and newly composed pieces commenting on contemporary events, migration and community life in the Purépecha highlands around Lake Pátzcuaro.

Recognition

In 2010 UNESCO inscribed the pirekua on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, recognising it as a living tradition that reinforces Purépecha language and identity.
