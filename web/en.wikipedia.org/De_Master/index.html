De Master

De Master is a 2012 Indian Malayalam-language action thriller film directed by Preethi Panicker and written by Johnny Antony. The film stars Mohanlal in the title role as Professor Michael, alongside Anoop Menon, Joju George, and others.

Plot

Professor Michael, known to his students as "Master", is a respected teacher at an engineering college in Kerala. Beneath his calm exterior, he wages a private war against the drug mafia that has begun to corrupt his campus and endanger his students. When the syndicate's activities turn deadly, Michael is drawn into a violent confrontation that tests his principles as both a teacher and a vigilante.

Cast

  • Mohanlal as Professor Michael ("Master")
  • Anoop Menon
  • Joju George
  • Shweta Menon

Production and reception

The film was produced under the banner of Sreeraj Productions and released in Kerala in 2012. It combined campus drama with action-thriller elements, and its central performance by Mohanlal was noted by reviewers, though the film received mixed critical responses overall.
