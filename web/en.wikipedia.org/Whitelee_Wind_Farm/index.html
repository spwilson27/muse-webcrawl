Whitelee Wind Farm is an onshore wind farm on Eaglesham Moor in Scotland, about 20 kilometres south of Glasgow. With 215 turbines and a capacity of 539 megawatts, it is one of the largest onshore wind farms in Europe and the largest in the United Kingdom.

Development

The original wind farm of 140 turbines (322 MW) was commissioned in 2009. Extensions added 75 turbines, bringing capacity to 539 MW by 2013. The site also includes a large battery storage facility and a visitor centre, and in the 2020s a green hydrogen production facility was planned alongside it.

Recreation

Whitelee has more than 130 kilometres of trails used for walking, cycling, and horse riding, making it a popular recreation area. Rangers run guided activities, and the site is notable for wildlife including hen harriers, merlins, and extensive blanket bog restoration.
