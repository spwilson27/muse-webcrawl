Channel Orange

Channel Orange (stylised channel ORANGE) is the debut studio album by American singer-songwriter Frank Ocean, released on 10 July 2012 by Def Jam Recordings. Following his 2011 mixtape Nostalgia, Ultra, Ocean recorded the album with producers including Malay and Om'Mas Keith.

Music and themes

The album blends R&B, soul, jazz-funk, psychedelia and electronic textures, frequently using unconventional song structures and interludes. Its lyrical themes include unrequited love, wealth and excess, drug use, and social class. Notable tracks include "Thinkin Bout You", "Pyramids", "Sweet Life", "Super Rich Kids" (featuring Earl Sweatshirt) and "Pink Matter" (featuring André 3000). Days before release, Ocean published an open letter describing his first love, with a man, which was widely reported.

Reception

Channel Orange received widespread critical acclaim, topped many year-end lists, and won the Grammy Award for Best Urban Contemporary Album in 2013. It is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of the 2010s.
