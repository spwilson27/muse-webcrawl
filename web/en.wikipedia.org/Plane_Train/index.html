Plane Train

The Plane Train is an automated people mover system located at Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport in Atlanta, Georgia. It connects the domestic terminal and Maynard H. Jackson Jr. International Terminal with Concourses A through F. The system, opened in 1980, is one of the busiest automated transit systems in the world, carrying more than 250,000 passengers per day; most travellers passing through the airport — the world's busiest by passenger traffic — ride it between flights.

System

The trains run in a roughly 6-kilometre (3.7-mile) loop of tunnels and guideways beneath the airport, with stations at each concourse. Vehicles are Bombardier Innovia APM 100 cars, rubber-tyred and electrically powered, running unattended under automatic control. A one-way trip the length of the system takes about 11 minutes, and trains depart every couple of minutes during peak periods.

A memorable feature of the system is its recorded announcements; from 2012, a series of celebrity and civic voices — including Atlanta mayor Kasim Reed — delivered the door warnings, accompanied by the frequently quoted phrase "Please hold on. This train is departing." Between the concourses an underground pedestrian walkway, decorated with art installations including a simulated rainforest canopy and a display of Zimbabwean sculpture, runs parallel to the train tunnel for passengers who prefer to walk.

History

The Plane Train opened with the airport's midfield terminal complex in September 1980, initially using C-100 vehicles built by Westinghouse. It was extended to the international concourse (Concourse F) in 2012. The system has carried notable figures and been referenced in popular culture as an emblem of the scale of the Atlanta airport.
