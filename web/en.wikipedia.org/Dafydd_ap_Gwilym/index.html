Dafydd ap Gwilym

Dafydd ap Gwilym
Born: c. 1315–1320
Died: c. 1350–1370
Nationality: Welsh
Occupation: Poet

Dafydd ap Gwilym (c. 1315/1320 – c. 1350/1370) is widely regarded as the greatest of the Welsh poets and among the leading poets of medieval Europe.

Life

Born probably at Brogynin near Aberystwyth in Ceredigion, into a noble family, Dafydd is traditionally said to be buried at Strata Florida Abbey. Little is known for certain of his life beyond his poetry.

Poetry

Around 170 poems survive, mainly cywyddau on love and nature, often humorous and self-deprecating. Recurring figures include his lover Morfudd and her husband, the jealous "Bwa Bach". His work blends Welsh bardic tradition with continental troubadour influences.

Legacy

His vivid, personal voice marks a high point of Welsh-language literature, and he remains a national cultural icon in Wales.
