Robustness

Robustness is the property of a system to maintain its function in the face of perturbations, errors, or changes in its environment or internal conditions. The term is used across biology, engineering, computer science, statistics and economics.

In biology

Biological robustness refers to the persistence of a trait or behaviour despite genetic mutation and environmental variation. Mechanisms include redundancy (duplicate genes and parallel pathways), modularity, and feedback control. Robustness can facilitate evolution by allowing organisms to accumulate cryptic genetic variation.

In computing and engineering

In computer science, robustness is the ability of a program to handle erroneous input and unexpected conditions gracefully. Robust statistics are methods that remain reliable when data violate standard assumptions such as normality. In engineering and control theory, robust design and robust control aim to guarantee performance despite model uncertainty and disturbances.
