Naas

Naas (/neɪs/; Irish: Nás na Ríogh or An Nás) is the county town of County Kildare in Ireland. It is situated 33 kilometres (21 mi) southwest of Dublin, on the N7 road, and had a population of over 21,000 at the 2016 census, making it the largest town in County Kildare.

History

The name Naas derives from the Irish Nás na Rígh, meaning "assembly place of the kings". The town was an important meeting place in pre-Christian Ireland and later became a market town. Naas was historically the county town of the eastern part of County Kildare, with Kilkenny-style assizes held there. The town grew substantially in the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries due to its proximity to Dublin, becoming a commuter town.

Transport

Naas is served by the M7 motorway, which bypasses the town, and by nearby Sallins and Naas railway station. Bus services connect the town to Dublin and other destinations in the region.

Sport

Punchestown Racecourse, home of the Irish National Hunt Festival, is located near the town. Local sports clubs include Naas Rugby Football Club and Naas GAA.
