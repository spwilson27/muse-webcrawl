Secretariat (racehorse)


Secretariat at the 1973 Belmont Stakes

Secretariat (March 30, 1970 – October 4, 1989), also known as "Big Red", was an American Thoroughbred racehorse who, in 1973, became the first U.S. Triple Crown winner in 25 years. He also set records that still stand in all three races of the Triple Crown: the Kentucky Derby (1:59 2/5), the Preakness Stakes, and the Belmont Stakes, which he won by an astonishing 31 lengths in 2:24 flat.

During his racing career, he won five Eclipse Awards, including Horse of the Year honors at ages two and three. He was nominated to the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame in 1974. In the Blood-Horse magazine list of the Top 100 U.S. Thoroughbred champions of the 20th century, Secretariat was second only to Man o' War.

Racing career

Trained by Lucien Laurin and ridden mainly by Ron Turcotte, Secretariat raced for Meadow Stable under owner Penny Chenery. As a two-year-old in 1972 he won seven of nine starts. At three, after losses in the Wood Memorial, he won the Kentucky Derby coming from last place, setting a track record, and the Preakness two weeks later. In the Belmont Stakes on June 9, 1973, he pulled away from the field in a performance widely regarded as one of the greatest races ever run, finishing 31 lengths ahead of Twice a Prince.

Stud career

Secretariat was syndicated for a then-record $6.08 million before his Triple Crown campaign and stood at stud at Claiborne Farm in Kentucky. Though his early crops disappointed relative to expectations, he became an outstanding broodmare sire; his daughters produced leading sires including Storm Cat and A.P. Indy, the latter a grandson who became one of the most influential sires of the modern era.

Death and legacy

Secretariat was euthanized on October 4, 1989, because of laminitis. A necropsy revealed a heart estimated at about 22 pounds, far larger than typical — popularly linked to the "x-factor" theory of inherited heart size. He is buried at Claiborne Farm. His life was dramatized in the 2010 Disney film Secretariat.
