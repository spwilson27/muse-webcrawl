Calvin Johnson (born November 1, 1962) is an American guitarist, vocalist, songwriter, music producer and disc jockey born in Olympia, Washington. Known for his uniquely deep, droning singing voice, Johnson was a founding member of the bands Cool Rays, Beat Happening, The Go Team, Dub Narcotic Sound System, and The Halo Benders. He is also the founder and owner of the influential independent record label K Records, a cornerstone of the Olympia independent music scene and of the international DIY and lo-fi movements.

Career

Johnson began at KAOS, the community radio station at The Evergreen State College, and founded K Records in 1982, initially releasing cassettes in handmade packaging. His band Beat Happening (formed 1982 with Heather Lewis and Bret Lunsford) became emblematic of lo-fi indie rock — deliberately primitive instrumentation, simple songs, and Johnson's baritone vocals. The International Pop Underground Convention he helped organise in Olympia in 1991 became a landmark of indie and riot grrrl culture.

Beyond Beat Happening, Johnson recorded with Dub Narcotic Sound System, with the Halo Benders (with Doug Martsch of Built to Spill), and as a solo artist, releasing albums such as What Was Me (2002) and Before the Dream Faded... (2005). His Dub Narcotic Studio in Olympia has recorded artists across the independent-music spectrum. (He is unrelated to the Calvin cycle of photosynthesis, which is named for the chemist Melvin Calvin.)
