Rafael Advanced Defense Systems Ltd. is an Israeli defense technology company owned by the State of Israel. It was founded in 1948 as Israel's National R&D Defense Laboratory and became an independent government-owned company in 2002. Rafael develops and produces weapons, military, and defense technologies for the Israel Defense Forces and for export customers worldwide.

An Iron Dome air defense battery, Rafael's best-known system.

Products

Rafael is best known for the Iron Dome short-range rocket defense system, developed with Israel Aerospace Industries and operational since 2011. Other products include the Spike family of electro-optical guided missiles, the David's Sling medium-range air defense system (developed jointly with Raytheon), the Trophy active protection system for armored vehicles, the Python and Derby air-to-air missiles, and the SPICE precision-guided bomb kits.

History

The organization originated in the Science Corps (HEMED) of the Haganah and later the IDF. In 1952 it became the Research and Design Directorate of the Ministry of Defense, and in 1958 it adopted the name Rafael, an acronym for the Hebrew "Authority for the Development of Armaments". Its early work included the Luz and Shafrir missiles. In 2002 Rafael was incorporated as a government-owned company to allow more commercial operation, while remaining wholly state owned.

Operations

Rafael employs roughly ten thousand people, many of them engineers and scientists, and reports annual sales in the billions of US dollars, a substantial share from exports. It maintains subsidiaries and partnerships in the United States, Europe, India, and elsewhere.
