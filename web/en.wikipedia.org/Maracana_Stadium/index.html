Maracana Stadium

Maracana Stadium most commonly refers to the Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, one of the world's most famous football stadiums, which hosted the 1950 and 2014 FIFA World Cup finals and the 2016 Olympic opening ceremony.

The name may also refer to smaller stadiums in Brazil and elsewhere named after it, including community and school grounds in the Maracanã neighbourhood of Rio de Janeiro.
