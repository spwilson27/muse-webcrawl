Microsoft 365 is a product line of subscription services offered by Microsoft. Launched in 2011 as Office 365, it combines the Microsoft Office desktop applications with cloud-based services such as Exchange Online, SharePoint Online, OneDrive and Microsoft Teams, sold in plans for consumers, businesses, enterprises, education and government.

History

Office 365 grew out of Microsoft's Business Productivity Online Suite. Consumer subscriptions launched in 2013 alongside Office 2013. In April 2020 the consumer and small-business plans were rebranded as Microsoft 365, and the name was progressively extended to most enterprise plans. From 2023 Microsoft integrated its AI assistant Copilot into the suite, later offering it as an add-on and in bundled plans.

Components

Subscribers receive installable and web versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote and (on Windows) Access and Publisher, plus cloud storage, email hosting, collaboration tools and device management features such as Intune in higher tiers. A free, reduced-functionality web version is offered as Office on the web.

Reception

The subscription model shifted Microsoft Office from perpetual licences to recurring revenue and became one of the largest SaaS businesses in the world, with hundreds of millions of users; it competes chiefly with Google Workspace.
