Harrison Ford

Harrison Ford (born July 13, 1942) is an American actor. He is widely regarded as an American cultural icon, and his films have grossed more than US$9 billion worldwide. He was the leading box-office star for much of the late 20th century, best known for playing Han Solo in the Star Wars saga and the title character in the Indiana Jones franchise. His other notable roles include Rick Deckard in Blade Runner (1982), John Book in Witness (1985), which earned him his only Academy Award nomination, Jack Ryan in Patriot Games (1992) and Clear and Present Danger (1994), and President James Marshall in Air Force One (1997).

Ford was born in Chicago, Illinois, to a former radio actress and an advertising executive. He attended Maine East High School in Park Ridge and Ripon College in Wisconsin, where he took a drama class to overcome shyness. After college he moved to Los Angeles, signed with Columbia Pictures' new-talent program, and appeared in small film and television roles. Frustrated by the work, he taught himself carpentry and became a professional cabinetmaker, a trade that famously brought him into contact with director George Lucas.

Breakthrough and stardom

Ford gained attention as Bob Falfa in Lucas's American Graffiti (1973) and as Martin Stett in Francis Ford Coppola's The Conversation (1974). Cast as Han Solo in Star Wars (1977), he became an international star, reprising the role in several sequels, most recently in Star Wars: The Force Awakens (2015). In 1981 he began playing archaeologist Indiana Jones in Raiders of the Lost Ark, returning for four sequels, concluding with Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny (2023).

His dramatic work includes Witness (1985), The Mosquito Coast (1986), Frantic (1988), Presumed Innocent (1990), The Fugitive (1993), What Lies Beneath (2000), 42 (2013), in which he played Branch Rickey, and Blade Runner 2049 (2017). In the 2020s he took leading television roles in the comedies and dramas Shrinking and 1923, and joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Thaddeus "Thunderbolt" Ross.

Personal life and aviation

Ford is a licensed private pilot of fixed-wing aircraft and helicopters and owns a ranch in Jackson, Wyoming, where he has assisted in rescue missions. He is an environmental advocate and serves as vice-chair of Conservation International. He has been married to actress Calista Flockhart since 2010.
