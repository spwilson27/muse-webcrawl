Baden (wine region)

Baden is the southernmost and third-largest of Germany's 13 official wine regions (Anbaugebiete), and one of only two in wine-growing zone B of the European Union — reflecting its warm, sunny climate. It stretches some 400 km along the Rhine from Lake Constance to the Tauber valley.

Grapes and wines

Baden is best known for Pinot varieties: Spätburgunder (Pinot noir), of which it grows more than any other German region, along with Grauburgunder (Pinot gris) and Weißburgunder (Pinot blanc). Müller-Thurgau and Riesling are also widely planted.

Districts

The region is divided into nine districts (Bereiche), including Kaiserstuhl, famous for its volcanic soils, Ortenau, Breisgau, Markgräflerland and Bodensee along Lake Constance.
