Midwifery

Midwifery is the health science and health profession that deals with pregnancy, childbirth, and the postpartum period (including care of the newborn), in addition to the sexual and reproductive health of women throughout their lives. A professional in midwifery is known as a midwife. Midwifery is distinct from obstetrics, the medical specialty, though in many health systems midwives and obstetricians work together, with midwives leading care for low-risk pregnancies.

Practice

Midwives provide antenatal care and screening (increasingly including ultrasound referral), support physiological labour and birth, recognise complications and refer to obstetric care when needed, and provide postnatal care including breastfeeding support. The midwifery model emphasises continuity of carer, informed choice and minimal intervention in normal birth. Evidence reviews associate midwife-led continuity models with fewer interventions and high satisfaction without increased adverse outcomes for low-risk women.

History

Midwifery is among the oldest professions: midwives are mentioned in ancient Egyptian and biblical texts, and Soranus's 2nd-century Gynaecology set qualifications for the "best" midwife. Childbirth care was traditionally women's work until male practitioners with forceps entered practice in the 17th–18th centuries, followed by the medicalisation and hospitalisation of birth in the 20th century. Modern midwifery was re-established as a regulated, educated profession — for example by the UK Midwives Act 1902 and through nurse-midwifery programmes elsewhere — and the WHO promotes midwives as key to reducing maternal and newborn mortality.

Education and regulation

Routes into practice vary: direct-entry university degrees, nursing followed by midwifery training, and (in the United States) credentials such as Certified Nurse-Midwife (CNM) and Certified Professional Midwife (CPM). Practice settings range from tertiary hospitals to planned home births, regulated by national bodies under international standards set by the International Confederation of Midwives.
