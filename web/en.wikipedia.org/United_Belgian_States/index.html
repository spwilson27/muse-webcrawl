United Belgian States
Period: January–December 1790
Capital: Brussels
Predecessor: Austrian Netherlands
Event: Brabant Revolution

The United Belgian States (Dutch: Verenigde Belgische Staten) was a confederal republic of the Southern Netherlands that existed briefly in 1790 during the Brabant Revolution against Habsburg rule. It was formed by the Treaty of Union of 11 January 1790, uniting most of the provinces of the Austrian Netherlands after their victory over imperial troops at Turnhout in October 1789.

Fall

The state was riven by conflict between the conservative Statists of Hendrik Van der Noot and the democratic Vonckists of Jan Frans Vonck, weakening its defence. In December 1790 Austrian forces reoccupied the territory and restored Habsburg rule. The experiment nonetheless foreshadowed Belgian independence in 1830, and its flag and the Lion of Brabant influenced later Belgian national symbols.
