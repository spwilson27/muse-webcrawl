Haute Chaîne du Jura National Nature Reserve

The Haute Chaîne du Jura National Nature Reserve (French: Réserve naturelle nationale de la Haute Chaîne du Jura) is a protected area in the Jura Mountains of eastern France, in the department of Ain. Created in 1993, it is one of the largest national nature reserves in metropolitan France, covering roughly 10,900 hectares.

Geography and wildlife

The reserve protects the high ridge of the Jura along the border with Switzerland, including peaks such as the Crêt de la Neige (1,720 m), the highest summit of the Jura range, and the Colomby de Gex. Its habitats range from subalpine grasslands and spruce forests to limestone pavements and peat bogs.

The reserve is home to chamois, lynx, capercaillie, Tengmalm's owl and a rich alpine flora. Access is regulated to protect sensitive species, and activities such as mountain biking and off-trail hiking are restricted in parts of the reserve.
