Incarnation

Incarnation, in religion, is the embodiment of a god, spirit or divine force in a human or animal form on earth. The concept is central to Christianity, where it refers to the belief that God the Son became flesh as Jesus of Nazareth, and also appears in several other traditions.

In Christianity

Christian doctrine teaches that Jesus is both fully divine and fully human, a union of two natures in one person. The event is commemorated at Christmas and announced, in the Gospels, by the Annunciation to Mary. The doctrine was defined at the Council of Chalcedon in 451.

In Hinduism

Hinduism has the related concept of the avatar, in which a deity such as Vishnu descends to earth in a visible form — for example as Rama or Krishna — to restore cosmic order.

Other traditions

Forms of incarnation belief appear in some schools of Buddhism, in ancient Egyptian religion and in various indigenous traditions.
