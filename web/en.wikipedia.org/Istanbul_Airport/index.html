Istanbul Airport (IATA: IST, ICAO: LTFM) is the larger of two international airports serving Istanbul, Turkey. Located in the Arnavutköy district on the European side of the city, it opened in 2018 and took over all scheduled passenger flights from Istanbul Atatürk Airport in April 2019.

History

The airport was built to relieve congestion at Atatürk Airport, which had no room to expand. Construction began in 2015, and the airport was inaugurated on 29 October 2018, the 95th anniversary of the Turkish Republic. The "great move" transferring operations from Atatürk took place on 5–6 April 2019.

Facilities

The main terminal, among the largest in the world under one roof at around 1.4 million square metres, was designed with a vaulted ceiling inspired by Turkish and Islamic art. The airport has multiple runways and is planned to expand in phases to a capacity of up to 200 million passengers a year. It serves as the global hub of Turkish Airlines and has ranked among the busiest airports in Europe by passenger traffic.
