€2 commemorative coins

€2 commemorative coins are special euro coins issued by the eurozone member states, legal tender throughout the euro area, whose national side commemorates a specific event or anniversary. They are issued on the €2 denomination because it is the largest and most suitable for detailed designs.

Rules

Since 2004, each eurozone country may normally issue one commemorative coin per year (later increased to two), plus occasional joint issues shared by all member states. The common European side remains unchanged. Joint issues have marked the 50th anniversary of the Treaty of Rome (2007), ten years of Economic and Monetary Union (2009), ten years of euro cash (2012), the 30th anniversary of the European flag (2015), and 35 years of the Erasmus programme (2022).

Collecting

Commemorative coins are struck both for circulation and in collector editions, and have become a popular field of numismatics, with some low-mintage issues from small states such as Monaco commanding high prices.
