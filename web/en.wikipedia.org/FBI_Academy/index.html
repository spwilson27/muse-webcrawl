FBI Academy

The FBI Academy is the Federal Bureau of Investigation's training and research centre near Quantico, Virginia, on the grounds of Marine Corps Base Quantico. Opened in 1972, the 547-acre facility trains new FBI special agents and intelligence analysts, as well as law enforcement officers from other agencies, including through the FBI National Academy programme for senior police executives.

The FBI Academy at Quantico, Virginia

Training

New agent trainees complete a course of roughly twenty weeks covering academics, case exercises, firearms, defensive tactics, and physical fitness. The campus includes classrooms, dormitories, a firing range, a mock town called Hogan's Alley used for tactical exercises, and training facilities shared with the Drug Enforcement Administration academy. The FBI Laboratory and the Behavioral Analysis Units associated with the former Critical Incident Response Group are also based in the Quantico complex.

National Academy

Since 1935 the FBI has run the National Academy, a professional course of instruction for U.S. and international law enforcement managers conducted at the academy, offering undergraduate and graduate-level coursework in subjects such as behavioral science, forensic science, and leadership.
