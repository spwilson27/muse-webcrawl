Lufthansa Flight 181

Lufthansa Flight 181 was a Lufthansa Boeing 737-230, named Landshut, hijacked on 13 October 1977 by four members of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine working with the West German Red Army Faction, during the crisis known as the "German Autumn".

Hijacking and rescue

The flight from Palma de Mallorca to Frankfurt was diverted via Rome, Cyprus, Bahrain, Dubai and Aden — where Captain Jürgen Schumann was murdered — to Mogadishu, Somalia. There, on the night of 17–18 October, West Germany's GSG 9 counter-terrorism unit stormed the aircraft in Operation Feuerzauber, freeing all 86 remaining hostages; three hijackers were killed. Hours later, imprisoned RAF leaders were found dead in Stammheim Prison.
