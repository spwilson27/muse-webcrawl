AdS/CFT correspondence

In theoretical physics, the anti-de Sitter/conformal field theory correspondence, sometimes called Maldacena duality or gauge/gravity duality, is a conjectured relationship between two kinds of physical theories. On one side are anti-de Sitter (AdS) spaces, used in quantum gravity formulated in terms of string theory or M-theory. On the other side are conformal field theories (CFT), including theories similar to the Yang–Mills theories that describe elementary particles.

Overview

The duality states that string theory on AdS5 × S5 is equivalent to N = 4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory on the four-dimensional boundary of AdS. Proposed by Juan Maldacena in 1997, it is the most successful realization of the holographic principle and provides a non-perturbative definition of quantum gravity in anti-de Sitter backgrounds.

Applications

Physicists have applied AdS/CFT to condensed matter physics (studied under the name AdS/CMT), to the quark–gluon plasma (where holographic calculations of viscosity compare favorably with experiment), and to quantum information, including relations between entanglement entropy and geometry.

Evidence

Although no complete proof exists, the correspondence has passed many nontrivial checks, including matching symmetries, correlation functions, Wilson loops, and black hole thermodynamics with field-theory calculations.
