List of mountains in Spain

Spain is one of Europe's most mountainous countries, with an average elevation around 660 metres. Its ranges include the Pyrenees along the French border, the Cantabrian Mountains in the north, the Sierra Nevada in the south, and the volcanic peaks of the Canary Islands. Ranges

The Meseta Central plateau is ringed and crossed by the Sistema Central, Montes de Toledo, Sierra Morena, and the Iberian System; the Betic Cordillera in the south includes the Sierra Nevada and Sierra de Grazalema. Mountain areas anchor most of Spain's national parks, including Ordesa y Monte Perdido, Picos de Europa, and Sierra Nevada.
