Hornby Railways

Hornby RailwaysParent: Hornby Hobbies Ltd
Founded: 1901 (Meccano Ltd, Liverpool); trains from 1920
Scale: principally OO gauge (1:76)
Base: Margate, Kent (historic)

Hornby Railways is a British model railway brand, among the best known in the United Kingdom. The Hornby name derives from Frank Hornby, inventor of Meccano, whose company produced clockwork O gauge trains from 1920 and launched the smaller Hornby Dublo system in 1938.

History

After the Second World War, Hornby Dublo offered two-rail electric OO gauge models from the Binns Road factory in Liverpool. The parent company was taken over by Tri-ang in 1964, and the merged range was marketed as Tri-ang Hornby, then Hornby Railways from 1972, with production centred at Margate, Kent. Facing competition and rising costs, manufacturing moved to China in the late 1990s.

Products

Hornby produces ready-to-run OO gauge locomotives, coaches and wagons, track systems and digital command control (including the Hornby DCC and HM7000 Bluetooth ranges), as well as starter train sets that have introduced generations of children to the hobby. The wider Hornby Hobbies group also owns Airfix, Scalextric, Corgi and Humbrol. The brand celebrated its centenary in 2020, marked by a television documentary series following the company.
