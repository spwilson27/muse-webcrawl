List of airlines of the United States

The United States has a large number of airlines, operating scheduled passenger, cargo, charter and regional services. The largest carriers by passengers carried are the major network airlines.

Major passenger airlines

  • American Airlines — largest US carrier, based in Fort Worth, Texas
  • Delta Air Lines — based in Atlanta, Georgia
  • United Airlines — based in Chicago, Illinois
  • Southwest Airlines — largest low-cost carrier, based in Dallas, Texas
  • Alaska Airlines — based in Seattle, Washington
  • JetBlue — based in New York City
  • Spirit Airlines — ultra-low-cost carrier
  • Frontier Airlines — ultra-low-cost carrier based in Denver
  • Hawaiian Airlines — based in Honolulu
  • Allegiant Air — low-cost leisure carrier
  • Avelo Airlines, Breeze Airways, Sun Country Airlines

Regional airlines

Regional carriers such as SkyWest Airlines, Republic Airways, Envoy Air, PSA Airlines, Piedmont Airlines, Endeavor Air, Mesa Airlines and Horizon Air operate flights under brands such as American Eagle, Delta Connection and United Express.

Cargo airlines

  • FedEx Express — largest cargo airline in the world
  • UPS Airlines
  • Atlas Air, Kalitta Air, Polar Air Cargo, Western Global Airlines

Defunct airlines

Historically notable defunct carriers include Pan Am, Trans World Airlines (TWA), Eastern Air Lines, Braniff International, Continental Airlines, Northwest Airlines, US Airways and Virgin America.
