Battle of Zboriv (1917)

Date1–2 July 1917
LocationZboriv, Galicia (now Ukraine)
ContextKerensky Offensive, First World War

The Battle of Zboriv was fought on 1–2 July 1917 during the Kerensky Offensive, the last major Russian attack of the First World War. It is remembered chiefly for the role of the Czechoslovak Rifle Brigade fighting with the Russian Army.

The battle

Czechoslovak legionaries, many of them former prisoners of war from the Austro-Hungarian Army, attacked Austro-Hungarian positions near the town of Zboriv in Galicia. Despite limited artillery support, they broke through several trench lines and captured thousands of prisoners, one of the few Russian successes of the offensive.

Legacy

The victory gave the Czechoslovak national movement a powerful claim to Allied support for an independent Czechoslovak state, and the battle became a founding legend of the Czechoslovak Legions.
