The Meta Quest 3 is a standalone virtual reality (VR) headset developed by Reality Labs, a division of Meta Platforms. It was announced on 1 June 2023 and released on 10 October 2023 as the successor to the Meta Quest 2.

Hardware

The Quest 3 is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2 processor, offering roughly double the graphical performance of its predecessor. Its pancake lenses enable a slimmer visor, and its dual LCD displays provide a resolution of 2064 × 2208 pixels per eye with refresh rates up to 120 Hz experimentally. Two RGB cameras support full-colour mixed reality passthrough, and the ring-free Touch Plus controllers use inside-out tracking supplemented by hand tracking.

Software

The headset runs an Android-based operating system and accesses the Meta Quest store. It is backwards compatible with the Quest 2 software library, and can also function as a PC VR headset via Quest Link or Air Link.

Reception

Reviews praised the improved passthrough, performance and comfort relative to the Quest 2, while noting the higher launch price of US$499 for the 128 GB model. A cheaper variant, the Meta Quest 3S, followed in 2024.
