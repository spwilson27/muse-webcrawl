Cusco, also spelled Cuzco, is a department and region of Peru. It is bordered by the departments of Ucayali to the north, Madre de Dios and Puno to the east, Arequipa to the south, and Apurímac, Ayacucho, and Junín to the west. Its capital is the city of Cusco, the historic capital of the Inca Empire.

Geography

The region spans terrain from high Andean peaks and intermontane valleys in the west to low Amazonian jungle in the east. The Vilcanota and Urubamba rivers drain much of the region, and the Sacred Valley of the Incas lies northwest of the capital.

History

Cusco was the heartland of the Inca Empire, and the region is dense with archaeological sites, most famously Machu Picchu in the province of Urubamba. After the Spanish conquest in the 1530s, the city became a major colonial centre blending Inca stonework with Spanish architecture.

Tourism

The region is Peru's principal tourist destination, drawing visitors to Machu Picchu, the Sacred Valley, Sacsayhuamán, and Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca).
