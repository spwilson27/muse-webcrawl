El Clásico

El Clásico is the name given to any football match between rivals FC Barcelona and Real Madrid CF.

El Clásico (Catalan: El Clàssic) is the name given to any football match between rival clubs FC Barcelona and Real Madrid CF. Originally referring only to competitions held in La Liga, the term now generally includes every match between the clubs, including in the UEFA Champions League, Copa del Rey and Supercopa de España. It is one of the most watched sporting events in the world, drawing audiences of hundreds of millions.

Rivalry

The rivalry extends beyond sport: Real Madrid is often identified with Spanish nationalism while Barcelona symbolizes Catalan identity, and the fixture acquired political overtones during the dictatorship of Francisco Franco. The clubs are the two most successful in Spanish football and among the wealthiest in the world.

History

The clubs first met in 1902, and the rivalry intensified after the founding of La Liga in 1929. Flashpoints include Alfredo Di Stéfano's disputed transfer in 1953, Luís Figo's return to Camp Nou with Real Madrid in 2000 — when a pig's head was thrown onto the pitch — and the era of Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo, whose parallel careers from 2009 to 2018 elevated the fixture to unprecedented global prominence.

Records

The two clubs have met more than 250 times in official competitions, with the head-to-head wins nearly level. Sergio Ramos and Lionel Messi are among the record appearance-makers; Messi is the fixture's all-time top scorer with 26 goals.
