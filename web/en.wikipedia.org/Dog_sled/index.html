Dog sled

A dog sled or dog sleigh is a sled pulled by one or more sled dogs, used to travel over ice and through snow. Numerous types of sleds are used, depending on their function. They can be used for dog sled racing or, traditionally, for transporting goods and people in Arctic regions.

History

Dog power has been used for hunting and travel for over a thousand years. As far back as the 10th century, dog sled teams contributed to human culture. Assembling a dog sled team involves picking lead dogs, point dogs, swing dogs, and wheel dogs. The lead dog is crucial, as is the musher, the person who drives the team.

Use

Dog sleds were vital for transportation in Arctic communities of Canada, Greenland, Siberia, and Alaska, and were used by explorers such as Roald Amundsen in polar expeditions. Today, while snowmobiles have largely replaced working dog teams, recreational mushing and races such as the Iditarod Trail Sled Dog Race remain popular.
