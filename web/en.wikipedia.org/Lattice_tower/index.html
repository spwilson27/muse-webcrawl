Lattice tower

A lattice tower or truss tower is a freestanding vertical framework tower made of metal members connected in a triangular pattern, combining high strength and stiffness with low weight and wind resistance.

Uses

Lattice towers support electricity transmission lines (pylons), radio and television antennas, observation and fire lookouts, and floodlights. Famous examples include the Eiffel Tower in Paris, the Tokyo Skytree's lattice structure, and guyed masts such as the KVLY-TV mast.

Design

Members are usually steel angle or tubular sections, galvanised against corrosion, arranged in a tapering square or triangular cross-section founded on concrete footings.
