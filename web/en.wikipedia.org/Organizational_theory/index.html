Organizational theory

Redirect to Organization theory.

Organizational theory is an alternative spelling of organization theory, the interdisciplinary study of how organizations are structured and how they function. The field encompasses classical approaches such as scientific management and Weberian bureaucracy; modern approaches such as contingency theory, institutional theory and resource dependence; and postmodern and critical perspectives on power, culture and identity in organizational life.

Overview

Scholars of organizational theory examine structure (hierarchy, centralisation, formalisation), environment, technology, strategy and culture, and seek to explain organizational effectiveness, change, survival and failure. The subject is taught widely in business schools and sociology departments and is published in journals such as Administrative Science Quarterly, Organization Science and the Academy of Management Review.
