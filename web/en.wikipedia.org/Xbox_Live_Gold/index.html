Xbox Live Gold

DeveloperMicrosoft
Launched15 November 2002 (as the paid tier of Xbox Live)
Succeeded byGame Pass Core (14 September 2023)
FeaturesOnline multiplayer, Games with Gold, discounts

Xbox Live Gold was the premium subscription tier of Microsoft's Xbox Live (later Xbox network) online service for Xbox consoles. For most of its life, a Gold subscription was required to play games online against other players, making it one of the defining — and most debated — features of the Xbox platform.

Features

Beyond online multiplayer, Gold included party chat and cloud saves. From 2013, the Games with Gold programme gave subscribers a selection of free games each month, theirs to keep or to play while subscribed. Members also received exclusive store discounts through Deals with Gold.

History

Xbox Live itself launched on 15 November 2002 for the original Xbox; the free "Silver" and paid "Gold" tiers were formalized with the Xbox 360 in 2005. Microsoft reversed a proposed 2021 price doubling after public backlash, and also dropped the Gold requirement for free-to-play games that year. On 14 September 2023, Xbox Live Gold was replaced by Game Pass Core, which retained online multiplayer and added a small catalogue of Game Pass titles.
