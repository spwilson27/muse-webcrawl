The Sandover Medal is awarded each year to the fairest and best player in the West Australian Football League (WAFL). The award was donated by Alfred Sandover, a prominent Perth hardware merchant and football benefactor, and was first presented in 1921. It is the WAFL equivalent of the Brownlow Medal in the VFL/AFL and the Magarey Medal in South Australia.

Voting

Votes are awarded by the field umpires after each home-and-away match on a 3–2–1 basis, and a player who is suspended during the season is ineligible to win. The count is traditionally held shortly before the WAFL Grand Final and is one of the highlights of the Western Australian football calendar.

Notable winners

Multiple winners include Merv McIntosh, Graham Farmer, Barry Cable, and Stephen Michael; Cable won the medal three times and also won a Brownlow Medal. Several Sandover Medallists went on to distinguished careers in the VFL/AFL, among them Brownlow Medallists Haydn Bunton Sr. (who won three Sandover Medals and three Brownlows) and Matt Priddis, who won the Sandover in 2006 and the Brownlow in 2014.
