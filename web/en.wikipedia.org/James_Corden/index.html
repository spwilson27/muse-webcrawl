James Kimberley Corden OBE (born 22 August 1978) is an English actor, comedian, writer and television presenter. He co-created and starred in the BBC sitcom Gavin & Stacey (2007–2010, with specials later), won a Tony Award for the play One Man, Two Guvnors, and hosted The Late Late Show on CBS in the United States from 2015 to 2023, where his "Carpool Karaoke" segments became a global viral phenomenon.

James Corden

Early life

Corden was born in Hillingdon, London, and raised in Hazlemere, Buckinghamshire. His father was a musician in the Royal Air Force band and later sold Bibles; his mother was a social worker. He was brought up in the Salvation Army church. He made his stage debut as a toddler and his screen debut in the 1997 film Twenty Four Seven.

Career in Britain

Corden appeared in the ITV drama Fat Friends (2000–2005) and played Jamie Rymer in Teachers. With Ruth Jones he created Gavin & Stacey, in which he played Smithy; the show won BAFTA awards and became one of Britain's best-loved sitcoms. He hosted the panel show A League of Their Own from 2010 and appeared in films including The History Boys (2006), reprising his stage role. In 2011 he starred in Richard Bean's One Man, Two Guvnors at the National Theatre, transferring to Broadway in 2012 and winning the Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play.

The Late Late Show

In March 2015 Corden succeeded Craig Ferguson as host of The Late Late Show on CBS. The programme became especially famous for Carpool Karaoke, in which Corden drove celebrity guests around Los Angeles singing along to their songs; segments with Adele, Paul McCartney and Michelle Obama drew hundreds of millions of online views. The feature was spun off into a standalone series for Apple Music. Corden hosted the Tony Awards and the Grammy Awards, and voiced roles in Trolls and The Emoji Movie, as well as acting in films such as Into the Woods (2014), Yesterday (2019) and Cats (2019).

Return to Britain

Corden stepped down from The Late Late Show in April 2023 and returned to live in the United Kingdom. Gavin & Stacey returned for a final special in 2024. He was appointed OBE in 2015 for services to drama.
