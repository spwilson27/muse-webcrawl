Amberd

Amberd is a 7th-century Armenian fortress located on the slopes of Mount Aragats in Armenia, at an elevation of about 2,300 metres, overlooking the confluence of the Arkashen and Amberd rivers. The name means "fortress in the clouds."

History

The fortress was built by the Kamsarakan noble family and later passed to the Pahlavuni dynasty, whose prince Vahram Pahlavuni rebuilt it in the 11th century and added the Vahramashen Church in 1026. Amberd was captured by the Seljuk Turks in the 1070s, later recovered and held by Armenian-Georgian forces, and finally destroyed by the Mongols in 1236. Its ruins — massive basalt walls on a rocky promontory between two gorges — remain a popular visitor site below the Aragats cosmic-ray station.
