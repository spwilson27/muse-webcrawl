Tom Perrotta

Tom Perrotta (born 13 August 1961) is an American novelist and screenwriter best known for the novels Election (1998) and Little Children (2004), both adapted into acclaimed films, and The Leftovers (2011), which he adapted with Damon Lindelof into the HBO television series.

Career

Raised in Garwood, New Jersey, Perrotta studied at Yale and earned a master's degree in creative writing from Syracuse University, where he studied with Tobias Wolff. His fiction, often set in suburbia, satirises and sympathises with characters caught between aspiration and disappointment. Election, the story of a ruthless high-school student election, was filmed by Alexander Payne in 1999; Little Children became a 2006 film for which Perrotta shared an Academy Award nomination for Best Adapted Screenplay.
