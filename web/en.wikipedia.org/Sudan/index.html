Sudan

Sudan, officially the Republic of the Sudan, is a country in Northeast Africa. It borders Egypt, Libya, Chad, the Central African Republic, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Eritrea, with a Red Sea coast. It is Africa's third-largest country by area.

History

Ancient Nubian kingdoms such as Kush ruled here. Sudan gained independence from Anglo-Egyptian rule in 1956. Long civil wars led to South Sudan's independence in 2011. Omar al-Bashir ruled from 1989 until protests forced his removal in 2019.

War from 2023

In April 2023 fighting erupted between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces, devastating Khartoum and Darfur, displacing over 10 million people and producing famine conditions declared in parts of Darfur in 2024 — described by the UN as the world's largest humanitarian crisis.
