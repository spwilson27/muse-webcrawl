Dufourspitze

The Dufourspitze is the highest peak of the Monte Rosa massif in the Pennine Alps, on the border between Switzerland and Italy. At 4,634 metres (15,203 ft) it is the highest mountain in Switzerland and the second-highest in the Alps and Western Europe, after Mont Blanc.

Climbing

The peak is named after Guillaume Henri Dufour, the Swiss general and cartographer whose maps first surveyed it. The first ascent was made on 1 August 1855 by a party led by Charles Hudson. The normal route from Switzerland climbs via the Monte Rosa Hut over the Monte Rosa Glacier, while the Italian normal route starts from the Capanna Regina Margherita, the highest refuge in Europe, situated on the nearby Signalkuppe.
