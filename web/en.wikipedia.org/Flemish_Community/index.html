Flemish Community

The Flemish Community (Dutch: Vlaamse Gemeenschap) is one of the three constitutional linguistic communities of Belgium, responsible for Dutch-speaking people and institutions. Communities in the Belgian federal system exercise powers over "personal" matters — principally culture, education, language use, health policy and assistance to individuals. The Flemish Community covers the population of the Flemish Region and the Dutch-speaking inhabitants of the bilingual Brussels-Capital Region.

Institutions

Uniquely among Belgium's federated entities, the Flemish Community and the Flemish Region merged their institutions from the start of the federal reforms: there is a single Flemish Parliament and a single Flemish Government exercising both community and regional powers. The Flemish Parliament has 124 members — 118 elected in the Flemish Region and 6 representing Dutch-speakers in Brussels — and sits in Brussels, which is also the capital of the Flemish Community even though the city lies outside the Flemish Region's territory.

Competences

The Community is responsible for Dutch-language education (in Flanders and for Dutch-language schools in Brussels), cultural affairs such as the public broadcaster VRT, libraries, heritage and sports, preventive health care, and welfare services. It also regulates the use of Dutch in administration and education. Roughly six and a half million people belong to the Flemish Community, making it the largest of Belgium's three communities, ahead of the French Community and the small German-speaking Community.

Symbols and politics

The Flemish Community celebrates its feast day on 11 July, commemorating the Battle of the Golden Spurs (1302). Its flag is the Flemish lion — a black lion rampant on a yellow field with red claws and tongue in the official version. Community powers have expanded through successive Belgian state reforms (1970, 1980, 1988, 1993, 2001 and 2011), and debate continues in Flemish politics over further transfers of authority from the federal level.
