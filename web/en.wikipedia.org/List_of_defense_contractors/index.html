List of defense contractors

A defense contractor is a business organisation or individual that provides products or services to a military or intelligence department of a government. This list covers the largest defense contractors worldwide by defense revenue.

Industry structure

The modern industry consolidated sharply after the Cold War, notably following the 1993 "Last Supper" meeting in the United States, which encouraged mergers among major suppliers.
