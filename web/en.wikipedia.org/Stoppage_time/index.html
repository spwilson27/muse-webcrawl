Stoppage time

SportAssociation football
Governed byLaws of the Game, Law 7 (IFAB)
Also calledAdded time, injury time

Stoppage time, also called added time or injury time, is the period added to the end of each half of an association football match to compensate for time lost during play. Under Law 7 of the Laws of the Game, the referee makes allowance for time lost through substitutions, injuries, time-wasting, disciplinary sanctions, drinks or cooling breaks, VAR checks and any other significant delay.

Procedure

The fourth official indicates the minimum amount of stoppage time on an electronic board near the end of each half; the referee may extend it further if additional delays occur. Matches are not stopped at exactly 45 or 90 minutes, and decisive goals in stoppage time — such as Manchester City's title-winning goal in 2012 — are among football's most famous moments.
