20th Century Studios, Inc. is an American film studio owned by the Walt Disney Studios, a division of Disney Entertainment, in turn a division of the Walt Disney Company. It is headquartered at the Fox Studio Lot in the Century City area of Los Angeles. Founded in 1935 as 20th Century Fox, it merged Twentieth Century Pictures with the Fox Film Corporation.

History

For over 80 years, the studio was one of the "Big Six" major American film studios. Its library includes the original Star Wars trilogy, Avatar, Titanic (co-produced), The Sound of Music, and franchises such as Alien, Planet of the Apes, X-Men, and Ice Age. In 2019, Disney acquired 21st Century Fox, including the studio, in a deal valued at $71.3 billion. In January 2020, Disney renamed the studio 20th Century Studios, removing the "Fox" branding to avoid confusion with the spun-off Fox Corporation.

Recent productions

Under Disney, the studio has released films such as Free Guy (2021), The King's Man (2021), Avatar: The Way of Water (2022, with Lightstorm Entertainment), The Creator (2023), and Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes (2024), alongside features from its specialty division, Searchlight Pictures.
