Ssam
Course: Main / wrap
Place of origin: Korea
Main ingredients: Leaf vegetables, rice, meat, ssamjang

Ssam (Korean: 쌈) refers, in Korean cuisine, to a dish in which leafy vegetables are used to wrap a piece of meat and condiments such as rice and ssamjang. The word literally means "wrapped".

Varieties

Common wraps include lettuce, perilla leaves, cabbage, seaweed (gim), and blanched leaves. Fillings may include grilled pork belly (samgyeopsal), bulgogi, rice, garlic, chillies, and soybean-based ssamjang. Bossam, boiled pork served with cabbage leaves, is a popular related dish.

Culture

Ssam is eaten by hand in a single bite. Wrapping food in leaves has a long history in Korean dining, linked to ideals of abundance and good fortune.
