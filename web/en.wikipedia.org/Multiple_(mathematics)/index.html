Multiple (mathematics)

FieldArithmetic, number theory
Related conceptDivisor

In mathematics, a multiple is the product of one integer and another integer. For example, 15 is a multiple of 3 and of 5, because 15 = 3 × 5. More generally, a number a is a multiple of b if there exists an integer n such that a = n × b; equivalently, b is a divisor (factor) of a.

Properties

Every integer is a multiple of 1 and of itself; 0 is a multiple of every integer. The multiples of a number form an infinite sequence (for example, the multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16, …). Numbers that are multiples of two or more numbers are called common multiples, and the smallest positive one is the least common multiple (LCM).
