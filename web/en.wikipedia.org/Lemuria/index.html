Lemuria

Lemuria is the name of a hypothetical lost land or sunken continent proposed in the 19th century to explain the distribution of lemurs and related fossils across the Indian Ocean, from Madagascar to India.

History of the idea

Zoologist Philip Sclater proposed Lemuria in 1864; Ernst Haeckel and others elaborated it, and it was later adopted into occult and Theosophical writings as a lost homeland of humanity. Modern geology and plate tectonics have superseded the hypothesis: the distributions are explained by continental drift and the ancient supercontinent Gondwana.
