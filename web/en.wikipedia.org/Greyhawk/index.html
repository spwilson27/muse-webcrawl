Greyhawk, also known as the World of Greyhawk, is a fictional world designed as a campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons fantasy roleplaying game. Created by Gary Gygax, it was the first published D&D setting and served as the default world for many early adventures. The setting takes its name from the Free City of Greyhawk, a major city-state on the continent of Oerik, on the planet Oerth.

History

Gygax developed the setting from his home campaign in the early 1970s. TSR published the World of Greyhawk folio in 1980 and an expanded boxed set in 1983. After Gygax left TSR, the setting was developed by other authors, including Carl Sargent's From the Ashes (1992), and later supported the Living Greyhawk organised play campaign under the RPGA from 2000 to 2008.

Geography

The best-detailed region is the Flanaess, the eastern portion of Oerik, home to kingdoms, free cities and wilderness areas such as the Great Kingdom, Furyondy, Nyrond, the Wild Coast and the Sea of Dust. The world is home to deities such as Pelor, Heironeous, Vecna and Saint Cuthbert, and famous wizards including Mordenkainen, Bigby and Tenser, drawn from Gygax's own player characters.

Influence

Many classic D&D modules were set in Greyhawk, including The Temple of Elemental Evil, Expedition to the Barrier Peaks and the Giants–Drow series. Though eclipsed commercially by the Forgotten Realms, Greyhawk remained the nominal default setting of the game's third edition.
