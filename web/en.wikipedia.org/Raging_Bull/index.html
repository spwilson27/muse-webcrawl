Raging Bull is a 1980 American biographical sports drama film directed by Martin Scorsese, based on the memoir of middleweight boxer Jake LaMotta. Robert De Niro stars as LaMotta, whose rage and jealousy destroy his relationships with his wife Vickie (Cathy Moriarty) and brother Joey (Joe Pesci), even as he rises to the middleweight championship.

Production

The film was shot in black and white, with cinematography by Michael Chapman and editing by Thelma Schoonmaker. De Niro famously gained about 60 pounds (27 kg) to portray the older, heavier LaMotta, a transformation that became a benchmark of method acting. The screenplay was written by Paul Schrader and Mardik Martin.

Reception and legacy

Initial reviews were mixed and the film was a modest box-office performer, but its reputation grew rapidly: De Niro won the Academy Award for Best Actor and Schoonmaker won for Best Film Editing. Raging Bull is now regularly ranked among the greatest films ever made, placing in the American Film Institute's top ten American films and in critics' polls such as Sight and Sound.
