Das Erste

Das Erste ("The First") is the flagship national television channel of the ARD consortium of public broadcasters in Germany. It began broadcasting on 25 December 1952 as NWDR-Fernsehen, became Deutsches Fernsehen, and was officially renamed Erstes Deutsches Fernsehen in 1984; since 1996 it has been branded simply as Das Erste.

Programming

The channel's schedule is produced jointly by ARD's nine regional broadcasting corporations. Well-known programmes include the flagship news bulletin Tagesschau, the crime series Tatort (produced by different regional broadcasters since 1970), the talk show formats of the regional members, and major sports coverage including football World Cups and the Olympic Games.

Audience and funding

Like other German public broadcasters, Das Erste is financed primarily through the Rundfunkbeitrag, a licence fee levied on households, supplemented by limited advertising. Its audience share is typically among the highest of German channels, with a notably older average audience.
