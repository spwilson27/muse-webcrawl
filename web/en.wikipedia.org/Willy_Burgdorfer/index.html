Wilhelm "Willy" Burgdorfer (June 27, 1925 – November 17, 2014) was a Swiss-American medical entomologist and bacteriologist who discovered the spirochete bacterium that causes Lyme disease, later named Borrelia burgdorferi in his honor.

Career

Burgdorfer trained at the University of Basel and emigrated to the United States in 1952 to work at the Rocky Mountain Laboratories in Hamilton, Montana, where he spent his career studying tick-borne diseases. In 1981, while examining deer ticks collected on Long Island, he found spiral-shaped bacteria in their midguts and, with colleagues, linked them to Lyme disease, publishing the discovery in 1982.
