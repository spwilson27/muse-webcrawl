Alexandru Ioan Cuza University (Romanian: Universitatea "Alexandru Ioan Cuza" din Iași) is a public university located in Iași, Romania. Founded in 1860 by a decree of Prince Alexandru Ioan Cuza, it is the oldest university in Romania and one of the country's leading research institutions.

History

The university was inaugurated on 26 October 1860, shortly after the union of the Danubian Principalities of Moldavia and Wallachia. It grew out of earlier institutions of higher learning in Iași, including the Academia Mihăileană founded in 1835. At its opening it had faculties of law, philosophy (letters) and theology; a faculty of medicine and a faculty of science followed in later decades.

Organisation

The university is organised into fifteen faculties, covering fields such as biology, chemistry, computer science, economics and business administration, geography and geology, history, law, letters, mathematics, philosophy and social-political sciences, physical education and sports, physics, psychology and education sciences, Orthodox theology, and Catholic theology. It enrols tens of thousands of students across bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes.

Campus

The main building, on Carol I Boulevard in the Copou district of Iași, is a notable example of late nineteenth-century academic architecture, completed in 1897. The university also maintains a botanical garden — the oldest and largest in Romania — an astronomical observatory, museums and a central university library named after the poet Mihai Eminescu.
