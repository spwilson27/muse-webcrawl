Vacation rental

A vacation rental is the renting out of a furnished apartment, house, or professionally managed resort-condominium complex on a temporary basis to tourists as an alternative to a hotel. In Europe the term villa rental or villa holiday is often used for houses, and in France rentals are called gîtes. Vacation rentals range from single rooms in a private home to entire villas and are especially common in resort areas.

Industry

The market grew rapidly with online platforms: VRBO was founded in 1995 and Airbnb in 2008; other platforms include Booking.com and Expedia. Property management companies handle booking, cleaning, and maintenance for owners. Vacation rentals are usually booked directly between guest and owner or manager, with stays from one night to several months.

Regulation

Many cities regulate short-term rentals over concerns about housing supply, noise, and neighbourhood character, requiring registration, limiting rental nights, or restricting rentals to primary residences. Rules vary widely by jurisdiction, and debates continue about effects on housing affordability and tourism.
