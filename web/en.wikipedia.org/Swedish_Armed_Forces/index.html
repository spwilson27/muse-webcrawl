Swedish Armed Forces

The Swedish Armed Forces (Swedish: Försvarsmakten) are the government agency responsible for the military defence of Sweden, comprising the Army, Navy, Air Force and the Home Guard. The Supreme Commander (Överbefälhavaren) leads the organisation under the Swedish government.

Role and history

For two centuries Sweden pursued a policy of non-alignment backed by strong national defence and conscription. Conscription was suspended in 2010 and reactivated in gender-neutral form in 2017–2018 as the security environment in the Baltic region deteriorated.

NATO membership

After Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, Sweden applied for NATO membership and became the alliance's 32nd member on 7 March 2024, ending its long tradition of military non-alignment.
