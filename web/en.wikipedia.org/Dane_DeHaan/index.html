Dane DeHaan

Dane William DeHaan (born February 6, 1986) is an American actor. He gained recognition for his roles in the found-footage superhero film Chronicle (2012), the drama The Place Beyond the Pines (2012), and as Harry Osborn / Green Goblin in The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014). He later starred as Valérian in Luc Besson's Valerian and the City of a Thousand Planets (2017) and played Robert Oppenheimer's colleague in Oppenheimer (2023) as Kenneth Nichols.

Early life

DeHaan was born in Allentown, Pennsylvania. He attended Emmaus High School and then the University of North Carolina School of the Arts, graduating in 2008. He began his career as an understudy on Broadway in American Buffalo and gained notice on television with a recurring role as Timbo in HBO's True Blood and as Jesse D'Amato in In Treatment.

Career

DeHaan's breakthrough came with Chronicle (2012), in which he played a troubled teenager who gains telekinetic powers. The same year he appeared in Lawless and Derek Cianfrance's The Place Beyond the Pines. He played Lucien Carr in Kill Your Darlings (2013), Harry Osborn in The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014), and James Dean in Anton Corbijn's Life (2015). In 2017 he headlined two major studio productions: Gore Verbinski's A Cure for Wellness and Besson's Valerian and the City of a Thousand Planets, one of the most expensive independent films ever made. Later credits include the Stephen King miniseries Lisey's Story (2021), the HBO true-crime series The Staircase (2022), and Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer (2023).

Personal life

DeHaan married actress Anna Wood in 2012; they have two children.
