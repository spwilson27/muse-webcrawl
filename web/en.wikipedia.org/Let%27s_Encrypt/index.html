Let's Encrypt

Let's Encrypt is a non-profit certificate authority that provides free TLS (SSL) certificates, enabling websites to use HTTPS encryption. It is operated by the Internet Security Research Group (ISRG) and launched publicly in 2016.

How it works

Let's Encrypt issues domain-validated certificates automatically through the ACME protocol: a website operator runs software such as Certbot, proves control of the domain, and receives a certificate without manual paperwork or fees. Certificates last 90 days, encouraging automated renewal.

Impact

By removing cost and complexity, Let's Encrypt helped drive the web's shift to HTTPS; it has issued billions of certificates and serves hundreds of millions of websites, making encrypted connections the default rather than the exception.
