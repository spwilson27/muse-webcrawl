Goldie (Cambridge boat race position)

Goldie is the name given to the reserve crew of the Cambridge University Boat Club, which races Oxford's reserve crew, Isis, on the same day as the main Boat Race between the universities of Oxford and Cambridge. The Goldie–Isis race, first held in 1965, is rowed over the Championship Course on the River Thames from Putney to Mortlake (or in recent years sometimes earlier on Boat Race day).

The Cambridge reserve boat is named after John Goldie, a 19th-century Cambridge oarsman and coach commemorated in the Goldie Boathouse on the River Cam. The women's reserve crews race as Blondie (Cambridge) against Osiris (Oxford).
