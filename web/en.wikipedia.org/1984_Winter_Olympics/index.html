The 1984 Winter Olympics, officially the XIV Olympic Winter Games, were held from 8 to 19 February 1984 in Sarajevo, Yugoslavia. They were the first Winter Games staged in a communist country.

Highlights

British ice dancers Jayne Torvill and Christopher Dean earned perfect 6.0 scores from all judges for artistic impression with their free dance to Ravel's Boléro, one of the most famous performances in Olympic history. East Germany led the medal table; Finnish skier Marja-Liisa Hämäläinen won all three women's cross-country events, and the host nation won its first Winter Olympic medal through alpine skier Jure Franko.

Legacy

Many Sarajevo venues were damaged or fell into ruin during the Bosnian War of 1992–95, when the city endured a prolonged siege.
