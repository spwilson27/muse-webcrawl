The Aso Rock Presidential Villa is the official residence and principal workplace of the president of Nigeria. Located in Abuja, the Federal Capital Territory, the complex is named after the Aso Rock, a prominent 400-metre monolith of granite that dominates the landscape of the capital city.

History

Construction of the villa began in October 1989 under the military administration of Ibrahim Babangida, as part of the planned relocation of Nigeria's capital from Lagos to Abuja. The complex was declared opened for use in November 1991, and Babangida became its first resident president when the seat of government formally moved to Abuja in December 1991.

Facilities

The villa complex contains several principal buildings, including the presidential residence, offices of the president and vice president, a banquet hall used for state functions, a presidential guest house, and facilities for administration, security, and press operations. The grounds also host a chapel and a mosque, reflecting Nigeria's religious diversity.
