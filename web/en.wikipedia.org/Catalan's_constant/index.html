Catalan's constant

Catalan's constant, usually denoted G, is a mathematical constant defined by the alternating series G = Σ (−1)n/(2n+1)2 = 1 − 1/9 + 1/25 − 1/49 + ⋯, approximately equal to 0.915965594177219. It is named after Eugène Charles Catalan and equals the value β(2) of the Dirichlet beta function at 2.

Properties

Catalan's constant appears in combinatorics, in integrals involving inverse tangent and logarithm functions, in the analysis of certain lattice sums, and in physics in problems involving Bessel functions. Unlike ζ(3) (Apéry's constant), it is not known whether G is irrational — one of the notable open problems concerning classical constants. It has been computed to hundreds of billions of decimal digits.
