Battle of Zacatecas

Date23 June 1914
LocationZacatecas, Mexico
ResultConstitutionalist victory
CommandersPancho Villa vs federal forces of Victoriano Huerta

The Battle of Zacatecas, known in Spanish as the Toma de Zacatecas ("Taking of Zacatecas"), was the bloodiest battle of the Mexican Revolution. On 23 June 1914, Pancho Villa's División del Norte decisively defeated the federal garrison defending the strategic mining city of Zacatecas.

The assault

Ignoring orders from Venustiano Carranza to halt, Villa attacked the strongly fortified hills around the city, including La Bufa and El Grillo. Coordinated artillery under Felipe Ángeles and mass infantry assaults overwhelmed some 12,000 federal troops; thousands were killed and the survivors fled in disorder.

Consequences

The victory broke the back of the Huerta regime, which collapsed within weeks, but it also deepened the rift between Villa and Carranza that soon plunged the revolution into a new phase of civil war.
