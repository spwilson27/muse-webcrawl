Laurus nobilis

Laurus nobilis, the bay tree or bay laurel, is an evergreen tree or shrub in the family Lauraceae, native to the Mediterranean region. Its aromatic leaves are the culinary herb bay leaf.

Description and uses

It grows 10–18 m tall with smooth, dark green, lanceolate leaves rich in essential oils. Dried bay leaves flavour soups, stews and sauces and are removed before eating. The tree is widely grown as an ornamental and in topiary, though it is tender to hard frost.

Culture

In Greek and Roman tradition the laurel was sacred to Apollo; wreaths of laurel crowned victors at the Pythian Games and Roman triumphs, giving rise to expressions such as "resting on one's laurels" and the title poet laureate.
