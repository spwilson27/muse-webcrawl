Tabu (actress)

Tabu
Born: Tabassum Fatima Hashmi, 4 November 1971, Hyderabad, India
Occupation: Actress
Years active: 1982–present

Tabassum Fatima Hashmi (born 4 November 1971), known professionally as Tabu, is an Indian actress who works primarily in Hindi films, alongside Telugu, Tamil and English-language productions. Regarded as one of the finest actresses of Indian cinema, she is known for playing strong, unconventional characters in independent and parallel cinema as well as in mainstream films.

Tabu made her first screen appearance as a child in Bazaar (1982) and her leading-role breakthrough came with the Telugu film Coolie No. 1 (1991). She won the National Film Award for Best Actress twice, for Maachis (1996) and Chandni Bar (2001), and holds a record number of Filmfare Critics Awards for Best Actress.

Career highlights

Acclaimed performances include Virasat (1997), Astitva (2000), Mira Nair's The Namesake (2006), Cheeni Kum (2007), Vishal Bhardwaj's Haider (2014) and Andhadhun (2018). Internationally she appeared in Ang Lee's Life of Pi (2012) and the BBC adaptation of A Suitable Boy (2020).

Honours

She was awarded the Padma Shri, India's fourth-highest civilian honour, in 2011.
