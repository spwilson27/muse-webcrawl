Pip Ivan (Chornohora)

Elevation: 2,028 m
Location: Chornohora range, Carpathian Mountains, Ukraine

Pip Ivan is a peak in the Chornohora mountain range of the Ukrainian Carpathians, rising to about 2,028 metres. It is one of the highest summits in Ukraine and is famous for the ruined astronomical and meteorological observatory on its summit.

The observatory

The observatory, nicknamed the "White Elephant" for its appearance in snow, was built by Poland in the 1930s and opened in 1938. Abandoned after the Second World War, its ruins stand on the summit. Restoration has been pursued jointly by Polish and Ukrainian institutions, and the peak — reached by hiking routes from villages such as Dzembronia — is a popular destination in the Carpathian National Nature Park.
