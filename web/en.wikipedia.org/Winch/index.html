Winch

TypeMechanical device
FunctionWinding rope or cable to pull or lift loads

A winch is a mechanical device used to pull in, let out, or adjust the tension of a rope or wire rope (cable). In its simplest form it consists of a spool (drum) attached to a hand crank; larger winches are powered by electric, hydraulic, or internal combustion motors and incorporate gears or worm drives to multiply force.

Applications

Winches are used on ships for anchors and mooring lines, on tow trucks and off-road vehicles for recovery, in construction and logging, on sailboats for sheets and halyards, and in theatres for moving scenery. A winch that hauls a load vertically is closely related to the hoist, while the capstan is a related device with a vertical axle that does not store the rope.
