Daniel Ash

Daniel Ash, guitarist of Bauhaus, Tones on Tail and Love and Rockets

Daniel Gaston Ash (born 31 July 1957) is an English musician, songwriter and singer, best known as the guitarist of the rock bands Bauhaus, Tones on Tail and Love and Rockets. His distinctive guitar style — combining feedback, echo, slide and unusual tunings — helped define the sound of gothic rock and alternative rock in the 1980s.

Career

Ash formed Bauhaus in Northampton in 1978 with Peter Murphy and brothers David J and Kevin Haskins. The band's 1979 debut single "Bela Lugosi's Dead" became a landmark of the emerging gothic rock scene. After Bauhaus split in 1983, Ash formed Tones on Tail with Kevin Haskins, then reunited with David J and Haskins as Love and Rockets, which scored a major US hit in 1989 with "So Alive".

Ash released his first solo album, Coming Down, in 1991, followed by Foolish Thing Desire (1992). He has continued to record and tour, participating in Bauhaus reunions including the band's 2022 tour and releasing further solo and collaborative work.

Style

Ash's playing is noted for its textural approach: heavy use of delay and reverb, e-bow-like sustained tones achieved with volume swells, prepared guitar and a Telecaster run through a HH amp stack. He has cited David Bowie, T. Rex and dub reggae among his influences.
