List of national and international statistical services

National statistical offices produce official statistics on population, the economy, health and society. International bodies coordinate standards and compile cross-country data.

International

  • United Nations Statistics Division
  • Eurostat — European Union
  • OECD Statistics
  • World Bank Data
  • International Monetary Fund statistics

National examples

  • United States Census Bureau and Bureau of Labor Statistics
  • Office for National Statistics — United Kingdom
  • Statistics Canada
  • Australian Bureau of Statistics
  • Statistics Bureau of Japan
  • National Bureau of Statistics of China
  • Destatis — Germany
  • INSEE — France
  • Statistics India / NSO
