Zōri

Image placeholder: A pair of zōri sandals

Zōri (草履) are flat Japanese sandals made of rice straw or other plant fibers, wood, lacquered wood, leather, rubber, or synthetic materials. They are a traditional form of thong sandal, similar in design to flip-flops.

Use

Zōri are worn with traditional Japanese clothing such as kimono; formal zōri are distinguished by materials and color — for example, vinyl or brocade straps for women and cork or leather soles for formal men's wear. They are worn with tabi socks when formal, but are generally worn barefoot. Setta are a related sandal with a leather sole.

Construction

A pair consists of a sole (dai) and a thong (hanao) passing between the big toe and second toe, secured through holes in the sole.
