Zurich

Zurich (Zürich)
Country: Switzerland
Canton: Zurich
Population: about 420,000 (city)
Language: German (Swiss German)
Location: North end of Lake Zurich, on the Limmat

Zurich (German: Zürich) is the largest city in Switzerland and the capital of the canton of Zurich. It lies at the northwestern end of Lake Zurich, where the river Limmat flows out of the lake. Zurich is a global centre for banking and finance and consistently ranks among the world's most liveable cities.

History

The area has been settled since prehistoric times; the Romans founded the customs station Turicum. In the Middle Ages Zurich became a free imperial city and joined the Swiss Confederacy in 1351. Under Huldrych Zwingli in the 1520s it became a centre of the Protestant Reformation.

Economy

Zurich is Switzerland's financial capital, home to major banks such as UBS, insurers, and the SIX Swiss Exchange. Its main station is one of Europe's busiest railway hubs, and Zurich Airport is the country's largest international gateway.

Culture and education

Landmarks include the Grossmünster, Fraumünster with its Chagall windows, and the Bahnhofstrasse shopping avenue. The University of Zurich and ETH Zurich are among Europe's leading universities; ETH was the workplace of Albert Einstein.
