Bang on a Can

Bang on a Can is a multi-faceted contemporary classical music organization based in New York City. It was founded in 1987 by composers Julia Wolfe, David Lang, and Michael Gordon. The organization commissions and performs new music and is known for its annual Marathon concert and its resident ensemble, the Bang on a Can All-Stars, formed in 1992.

History

The first Bang on a Can Marathon, held in 1987, presented an eclectic marathon-length program mixing composers from different traditions. The organization went on to found the People's Commissioning Fund, the Bang on a Can Summer Music Festival at MASS MoCA, and the Asphalt Orchestra street band project. Its aesthetic blurs boundaries between classical, jazz, rock, and experimental music, and it has commissioned works from composers including Steve Reich, Terry Riley, and Ornette Coleman.
