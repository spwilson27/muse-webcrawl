UN-Habitat

UN-Habitat, formally the United Nations Human Settlements Programme, is the United Nations programme for human settlements and sustainable urban development. It was established in 1978 as an outcome of the first United Nations Conference on Human Settlements and Sustainable Urban Development (Habitat I) in Vancouver, Canada, in 1976. UN-Habitat maintains its headquarters at the United Nations Office at Nairobi, Kenya.

The programme is mandated by the UN General Assembly to promote socially and environmentally sustainable towns and cities, with the goal of providing adequate shelter for all. Its work focuses on urban planning, housing, slum upgrading, water and sanitation, and making cities more resilient to climate change and disasters.

UN-Habitat convenes the World Urban Forum, the premier global conference on urban issues, and leads observance of World Habitat Day on the first Monday of October each year. It helped draft the New Urban Agenda, adopted at the Habitat III conference in Quito, Ecuador, in 2016, and supports implementation of Sustainable Development Goal 11, "Sustainable Cities and Communities."

Its governing structure includes the UN-Habitat Assembly, an Executive Board, and a Committee of Permanent Representatives. Executive Directors have included Anna Tibaijuka and Maimunah Mohd Sharif; Anacláudia Rossbach became Executive Director in 2024.
