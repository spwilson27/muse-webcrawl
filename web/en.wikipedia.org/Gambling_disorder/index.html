Gambling disorder

Gambling disorder (commonly referred to as problem gambling, or in severe form compulsive gambling or pathological gambling) is a behavioural addiction characterised by persistent and recurrent gambling behaviour that is maladaptive — disrupting personal, family, or vocational pursuits despite negative consequences. In the DSM-5, gambling disorder is classified in the chapter "Substance-Related and Addictive Disorders", reflecting evidence that it shares features with substance addictions.

Signs and symptoms

Diagnostic criteria include needing to gamble with increasing amounts of money to achieve excitement, restlessness or irritability when trying to stop, repeated unsuccessful efforts to control gambling, preoccupation with gambling, gambling when distressed, chasing losses, lying to conceal involvement, jeopardising relationships or employment, and relying on others for money to relieve desperate financial situations caused by gambling. Four or more criteria within 12 months indicate the disorder, graded mild, moderate, or severe.

Causes and risk factors

Risk factors include availability of gambling, early exposure, co-occurring mental health conditions (depression, anxiety, substance use, ADHD), impulsivity, and certain medications such as dopamine agonists used for Parkinson's disease. Cognitive distortions — the gambler's fallacy, illusion of control, and near-miss effects — help maintain the behaviour.

Treatment

Treatments with supporting evidence include cognitive behavioural therapy, motivational interviewing, and peer support such as Gamblers Anonymous. Self-exclusion schemes, financial counselling, and treatment of comorbid conditions are commonly part of management. No medication is specifically approved for gambling disorder, though some are used off-label.

Epidemiology

Estimates of past-year problem gambling vary by country and measure, commonly between about 0.1% and several percent of adults, with higher rates among young men and in populations with ready access to electronic gaming machines and online betting.
