Immediately dangerous to life or health

IDLH
Full nameImmediately dangerous to life or health
Defined byNIOSH (United States)
PurposeRespirator selection and workplace safety

The term immediately dangerous to life or health (IDLH) is defined by the US National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) as exposure to airborne contaminants that is "likely to cause death or immediate or delayed permanent adverse health effects or prevent escape from such an environment".

Purpose

IDLH values were developed to ensure that workers could escape from a contaminated environment if respiratory protection failed, and to define conditions under which only the most protective respirators—such as self-contained breathing apparatus—may be used.

Examples

NIOSH publishes IDLH values for hundreds of substances; carbon monoxide, hydrogen sulfide, and chlorine are among the commonly cited examples. Values are expressed as concentrations in air (usually parts per million) for a 30-minute exposure.
