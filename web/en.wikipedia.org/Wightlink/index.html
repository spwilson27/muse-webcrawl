Wightlink

Wightlink
Type: Ferry company
Founded: 1991
Headquarters: Portsmouth, England
Area served: Solent, Isle of Wight

Wightlink is a ferry company operating routes between Hampshire and the Isle of Wight in southern England. It is the largest of the three operators providing cross-Solent services, carrying millions of passengers, cars, and freight vehicles each year between the mainland and the island.

History

The company's origins lie in the ferry services of British Rail's shipping division, Sealink. Following privatisation and a management buyout, the Isle of Wight services were rebranded as Wightlink in 1991. The company subsequently passed through several owners, including the Macquarie European Infrastructure Fund, and later Balfour Beatty Infrastructure Partners and Basalt Infrastructure Partners.

Routes

Wightlink operates three routes across the Solent:

  • Portsmouth to Fishbourne — vehicle and passenger ferry
  • Lymington to Yarmouth — vehicle and passenger ferry
  • Portsmouth Harbour to Ryde Pier Head — fast passenger catamaran, connecting with trains to London Waterloo

Fleet

The fleet includes the Victoria of Wight, a hybrid battery/diesel-electric ferry introduced in 2018 as the company's flagship on the Portsmouth–Fishbourne route, along with the Wight class vessels Wight Sky, Wight Light, and Wight Sun, and the FastCat catamarans serving Ryde.
