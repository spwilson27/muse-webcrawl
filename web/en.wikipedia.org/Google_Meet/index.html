Google Meet

Developer: Google

Initial release: March 9, 2017

Platform: Android, iOS, web

Type: Video conferencing software

Google Meet is a video communication service developed by Google. It is one of two apps that replaced Google Hangouts, the other being Google Chat. It replaced the consumer-facing Google Duo in late 2022, when the Duo mobile app was renamed Meet and its features merged.

History

Google Meet was officially launched in March 2017 as an invitation-only service for up to 30 participants, positioned as a business-oriented version of Hangouts. During the COVID-19 pandemic, usage surged dramatically; Google made Meet free to all users in April 2020, and reported peak daily growth in the millions of new users.

Features

Features include multi-party video calls, screen sharing, live captions, noise cancellation, real-time translated captions, meeting recording and transcription for workspace tiers, and integration with Google Calendar and Gmail. Calls are encrypted in transit, and hosts can admit or deny entry to participants.
