The Book of Life is a 2014 American animated fantasy adventure film directed by Jorge R. Gutiérrez and produced by Guillermo del Toro. It was animated by Reel FX Creative Studios and distributed by 20th Century Fox.

Plot

Told as a story within a story by a museum tour guide, the film follows Manolo Sánchez, a young bullfighter who dreams of being a musician. He and his best friend Joaquín compete for the hand of María Posada, while the rulers of the afterlife — La Muerte of the Land of the Remembered and Xibalba of the Land of the Forgotten — wager on the outcome. Betrayed and sent to the Land of the Remembered, Manolo journeys through the afterlife on the Day of the Dead to return to the living and save his town from the bandit Chakal.

Style and reception

The film is noted for its distinctive visual style, inspired by Mexican folk art and wooden puppets. It received generally positive reviews and was nominated for the Golden Globe Award for Best Animated Feature Film.
