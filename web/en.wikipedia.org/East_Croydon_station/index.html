East Croydon station

East Croydon
Location: Croydon, Greater London
Managed by: Southern

East Croydon is a major railway station and tram stop in Croydon, Greater London, England. It is one of the busiest stations in the United Kingdom outside central London, serving as a key interchange on the Brighton Main Line.

The station is served by Southern and Thameslink trains, with frequent services to London Victoria, London Bridge, Brighton, Gatwick Airport and destinations across Sussex, Surrey and Bedfordshire. It is also an important stop on the Tramlink network.

History

The station opened in 1841 as Croydon station on the London and Brighton Railway. It was renamed East Croydon in 1846 to distinguish it from other stations in the town. Major rebuilding took place in the 1890s and again in the 1990s, when the current concourse and distinctive glass-and-steel building were completed.

In 2015, plans were announced for a major redevelopment of the station to increase capacity, including additional platforms, though the scheme has been subject to repeated revision.

Services

East Croydon has six platforms and handles fast and stopping services. Typical off-peak services include trains to London Victoria, London Bridge, Brighton, Eastbourne, Hastings, Southampton Central and Bedford via London. The station is in Travelcard Zone 5.

Tramlink

The East Croydon tram stop, adjacent to the station, is the busiest on the Tramlink network, with services to Wimbledon, Beckenham Junction, Elmers End and New Addington.
