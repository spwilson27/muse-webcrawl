Cessna 172

The Cessna 172 Skyhawk is an American four-seat, single-engine, high-wing, fixed-wing aircraft made by the Cessna Aircraft Company. First flown in 1955, more 172s have been built than any other aircraft, making it the most successful aircraft in history measured by production numbers, with over 45,000 built.

Development and design

Developed from the Cessna 170, the 172 introduced a tricycle landing gear and squared-off tail. It is powered by a piston engine driving a fixed-pitch propeller, cruises at about 226 km/h (122 knots), and is renowned for docile handling, which made it the archetypal flight-training aircraft.

Notable flights

In 1958–59 a Cessna 172 set the world endurance record, staying aloft for nearly 65 days with in-flight refuelling. In 1987, Mathias Rust flew a rented 172 from Finland to Moscow, landing near Red Square.
