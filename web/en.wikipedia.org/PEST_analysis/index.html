PEST analysis

PEST analysis (political, economic, social and technological) is a framework of macro-environmental factors used in the environmental scanning component of strategic management. It is part of an external analysis when conducting a strategic analysis or doing market research, and gives an overview of the different macro-environmental factors to be taken into consideration. It is a strategic tool for understanding market growth or decline, business position, potential and direction for operations.

Composition

FactorExamples
PoliticalGovernment policy, political stability, tax policy, trade restrictions, labour law, environmental law
EconomicEconomic growth, exchange rates, inflation rates, interest rates, disposable income, unemployment rates
SocialPopulation growth, age distribution, career attitudes, safety emphasis, health consciousness, cultural trends
TechnologicalAutomation, research and development activity, technological incentives, rate of technological change

Variants

The framework has been expanded in various forms. PESTEL or PESTLE adds legal and environmental factors; STEEPLE adds ethics; DESTEP reorders factors as demographic, economic, social, technological, ecological and political. The analysis is often combined with SWOT analysis, which covers internal strengths and weaknesses alongside external opportunities and threats identified through PEST.

Applicability

The factors will vary in importance depending on the industry and goods a company produces; for example, consumer and B2B companies tend to be affected more by social factors, while a global defence contractor would tend to be more affected by political factors. Factors more likely to change in the future or more relevant to the company carry greater weight in the analysis.
