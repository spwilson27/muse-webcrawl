Matewan massacre

Date19 May 1920
LocationMatewan, West Virginia, United States
Deaths10 (7 detectives, mayor, 2 miners)

The Matewan massacre was a gunfight in Matewan, West Virginia, on 19 May 1920, during the West Virginia coal wars. When Baldwin-Felts detectives arrived to evict striking miners' families from company housing, a confrontation with Police Chief Sid Hatfield and Mayor Cabell Testerman escalated into a shootout that left ten men dead, including two of the agency's owners, the Felts brothers.

Context

The shootout came amid efforts by the United Mine Workers to organise southern West Virginia coalfields against fierce company opposition.

Aftermath

Hatfield was acquitted of murder charges, becoming a hero to miners, but was assassinated by Baldwin-Felts agents on the McDowell County courthouse steps in 1921. The events were dramatised in John Sayles's 1987 film Matewan.
