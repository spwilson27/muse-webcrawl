Majel Barrett (born Majel Leigh Hudec; February 23, 1932 – December 18, 2008), later known as Majel Barrett-Roddenberry, was an American actress. She is best known for her roles in the Star Trek franchise, where she played Nurse (later Doctor) Christine Chapel and Lwaxana Troi, and provided the voice of most onboard computer interfaces throughout the series.

Career

Barrett appeared in the original Star Trek pilot "The Cage" as the Enterprise's first officer, Number One, before the role was recast for the series, in which she returned as Nurse Christine Chapel. She later played the flamboyant Betazoid ambassador Lwaxana Troi in Star Trek: The Next Generation and Deep Space Nine.

Computer voice

From The Next Generation through Enterprise, and in several films, Barrett voiced the Federation starship computer. Her final Star Trek performance was the computer voice in the 2009 film Star Trek, completed shortly before her death from leukemia in 2008.
