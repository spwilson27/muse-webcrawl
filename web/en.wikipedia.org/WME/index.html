WME


Full name: William Morris Endeavor Entertainment
Founded: 2009 (merger of William Morris Agency and Endeavor)
Headquarters: Beverly Hills, California
Parent: Endeavor (now part of WME Group)
Industry: Talent and literary agency

WME (William Morris Endeavor Entertainment, LLC) is an American talent agency headquartered in Beverly Hills, California. It is one of the largest and most influential agencies in the entertainment industry, representing artists across film, television, music, theatre, books and digital media.

History

WME was formed in April 2009 by the merger of the William Morris Agency — founded in 1898 and the oldest talent agency in the world — with the Endeavor Talent Agency, founded in 1995 by Ari Emanuel, Patrick Whitesell and others. The merger created a rival of comparable scale to Creative Artists Agency (CAA). In 2014 the holding company, renamed Endeavor, acquired the sports and media agency IMG for about $2.3 billion, adding representation of athletes, sports leagues and fashion events. Endeavor later acquired the UFC mixed martial arts promotion (2016) and took a controlling role in WWE, merging the two into TKO Group Holdings in 2023.

Operations

WME's agents package and negotiate deals for actors, directors, writers, musicians and authors, and the agency has major offices in New York, London, Nashville and other entertainment centres. The parent company went public in 2021 and was taken private again by Silver Lake in 2025, after which the agency business was organised under the WME Group banner.
