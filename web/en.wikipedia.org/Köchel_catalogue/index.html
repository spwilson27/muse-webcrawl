Köchel catalogue

The Köchel catalogue (German: Köchelverzeichnis) is a chronological catalogue of the compositions of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, compiled by Ludwig Ritter von Köchel and first published in 1862. Works are identified by their Köchel numbers, written as K. or KV followed by a number — for example, the Jupiter Symphony is K. 551.

Editions

Köchel's original edition listed 626 works in roughly chronological order. Later revisions — notably the third edition by Alfred Einstein (1937) and the sixth edition (1964) — corrected dates and added newly discovered works, sometimes producing numbers with letter suffixes while largely retaining the familiar numbering. A new scholarly edition, Köchelverzeichnis 2024, was released by the Mozarteum Foundation Salzburg and Breitkopf & Härtel.

Use

The catalogue remains the standard reference system for Mozart's output, cited in concert programmes, recordings and scholarship worldwide.
