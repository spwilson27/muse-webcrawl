Minamoto no Yoshitsune

Bornc. 1159
DiedJune 15, 1189
ClanMinamoto (Genji)

Minamoto no Yoshitsune (源 義経, c. 1159–1189) was a military commander of the Minamoto clan of Japan in the late Heian and early Kamakura periods, and one of the most famous samurai in Japanese history and legend.

Genpei War

Raised at Kurama temple after his father's death, Yoshitsune joined his half-brother Yoritomo in 1180. His brilliant, unconventional generalship won the decisive battles of the Genpei War: Ichi-no-Tani (1184), Yashima, and the final naval battle of Dan-no-ura (1185), which destroyed the Taira clan.

Fall and legend

Yoritomo, suspicious of his brother's popularity and independent court ties, outlawed him. Yoshitsune fled north to Fujiwara no Hidehira in Hiraizumi; after Hidehira's death his son betrayed him, and Yoshitsune died at the Battle of the Koromo River in 1189. His tragic story, retold in the Heike Monogatari and countless plays, made him the archetype of the doomed hero, and legends claimed he survived to become Genghis Khan.
