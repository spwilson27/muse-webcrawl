Chuvashia

Chuvashia (Russian: Chuvashiya; Chuvash: Chăvashiyĕ), officially the Chuvash Republic, is a republic of Russia located in the European part of the country, in the Volga Federal District. Its capital and largest city is Cheboksary. The republic covers about 18,300 square kilometres and has a population of roughly 1.2 million, the majority of them ethnic Chuvash.

Geography

Chuvashia lies on the right bank of the middle Volga River, bordered by the Mari El Republic, Tatarstan, Ulyanovsk Oblast, Mordovia and Nizhny Novgorod Oblast. The terrain is a rolling plateau cut by ravines, with forests in the north and forest-steppe in the south. The Volga and its tributary the Sura are the principal rivers.

People and language

The Chuvash are a Turkic people whose language, Chuvash, is the only surviving member of the Oghur branch of Turkic languages and is co-official in the republic alongside Russian. Most Chuvash are Orthodox Christians, though traditional beliefs persist. The region descends historically from Volga Bulgaria, whose inhabitants are considered ancestors of the Chuvash.

Economy

The republic's economy includes electrical engineering and machinery around Cheboksary, chemicals, textiles, and agriculture — the region is a major producer of hops and is known for brewing. The Cheboksary Hydroelectric Station on the Volga is a major power facility.
