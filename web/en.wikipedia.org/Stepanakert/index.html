Stepanakert (Armenian: Ստեփանակերտ) or Khankendi (Azerbaijani: Xankəndi) is a city in the Nagorno-Karabakh region of Azerbaijan. From 1991 to 2023 it was the capital of the breakaway Republic of Artsakh.

History

The settlement was known as Vararakn (after its river) and later as Khankendi, a village near the residence of the khans of Karabakh. In 1923 it was renamed Stepanakert after Armenian Bolshevik Stepan Shaumian and made capital of the Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast within Soviet Azerbaijan.

Karabakh conflict

The city was a focal point of the First Nagorno-Karabakh War (1988–94), suffering bombardment, and of the 2020 war, during which Azerbaijani forces captured nearby Shusha. In September 2023 Azerbaijan launched an offensive; the Artsakh authorities capitulated, and virtually the entire Armenian population — over 100,000 people across the region — fled to Armenia. The Republic of Artsakh formally dissolved on 1 January 2024.

Landmarks

The iconic tuff monument "We Are Our Mountains" (1967), nicknamed "Grandma and Grandpa", stands on a hill north of the city. The city also hosts Artsakh University and the Stepanakert Republican Stadium.

Geography

Stepanakert lies in the foothills of the Karabakh Range of the Lesser Caucasus, at about 813 m above sea level, in the valley of the Karkar River.
