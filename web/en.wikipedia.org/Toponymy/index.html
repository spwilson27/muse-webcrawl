Toponymy

Toponymy, or toponomastics, is the study of place names (toponyms), their origins, meanings, use and typology. It is a branch of onomastics, the study of proper names.

Scope and methods

Toponymists combine linguistics, history, geography and archaeology to explain names of settlements, rivers (hydronyms), mountains (oronyms) and other features. Place names often preserve archaic words and evidence of vanished languages and peoples — for instance, Celtic river names across Europe, or Norse settlement names in northern England ending in -by and -thorpe.

Significance

Place names can encode landscape features, ownership, saints, events and folk memory. Governments and the United Nations Group of Experts on Geographical Names standardise names for maps and administration, and naming disputes (as with seas and contested territories) can carry political weight.
