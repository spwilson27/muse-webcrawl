Aggregate (composite)

Crushed stone aggregate for use in concrete and road construction.

Aggregate is the component of a composite material that resists compressive stress and provides bulk. In construction, aggregate is a broad category of coarse- to medium-grained particulate material used in large quantities, including sand, gravel, crushed stone, slag, recycled concrete and geosynthetic materials. It is the most mined material in the world, and forms the principal ingredient of concrete and asphalt mixtures.

Role in composites

For aggregate to be effective, it must be much stronger than the matrix material that binds it. In concrete, aggregates are bound by cement paste; the aggregate typically occupies 60 to 80 percent of the volume and strongly influences the hardened concrete's strength, dimensional stability, durability and cost. Aggregates are classified as fine (generally sand, passing a 4.75 mm sieve) or coarse (gravel or crushed stone retained on that sieve). Particle size distribution, or grading, is controlled to minimize voids and the amount of cement paste required.

Sources and types

Natural aggregates are obtained from quarries, gravel pits and river or glacial deposits. Manufactured aggregates are produced by crushing rock or by processing industrial by-products such as blast-furnace slag. Recycled aggregates come from crushed demolition concrete and reclaimed asphalt pavement, reducing demand for virgin material and landfill use. Lightweight aggregates, such as expanded clay or shale, are used where reduced structural weight is important, while heavy aggregates containing minerals such as barite or magnetite are used for radiation shielding.

Properties and testing

Important properties include strength, hardness, resistance to abrasion and weathering (soundness), shape and surface texture, which affect workability of fresh concrete, and chemical inertness: certain siliceous aggregates react with alkalis in cement (alkali–silica reaction), causing expansion and cracking. Standards such as ASTM C33 specify requirements for concrete aggregates.
