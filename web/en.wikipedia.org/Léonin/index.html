Léonin

Léonin (fl. mid-to-late 12th century) was a French composer of the Notre Dame school of polyphony, the first known significant composer of polyphonic organum.

Life and work

Almost nothing is known of his life beyond his association with Notre Dame Cathedral in Paris. According to the English theorist known as Anonymous IV, Léonin compiled the Magnus liber organi (Great Book of Organum), a repertory of two-voice organa for the liturgical year, later revised by Pérotin.

Style

His organum purum style alternates sustained chant tones beneath florid upper-voice melismas with passages in discant style using rhythmic modes.
