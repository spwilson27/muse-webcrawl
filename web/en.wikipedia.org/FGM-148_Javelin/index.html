FGM-148 Javelin

The FGM-148 Javelin is an American-made, man-portable, anti-tank guided missile system in service since 1996. It is a fire-and-forget missile with lock-on before launch and automatic self-guidance, allowing the operator to move position immediately after firing.

TypeAnti-tank guided missile
Place of originUnited States
In service1996–present
DesignerRaytheon, Lockheed Martin

Design

The Javelin uses a tandem-charge high-explosive anti-tank (HEAT) warhead designed to defeat explosive reactive armour. Its soft-launch mechanism allows firing from enclosed spaces, and its top-attack flight profile strikes vehicles on their thinner roof armour.

Operational use

The system has been used extensively in the Iraq and Afghanistan wars and was supplied in large numbers to Ukraine following the 2022 Russian invasion, where it became a symbol of Ukrainian resistance.
