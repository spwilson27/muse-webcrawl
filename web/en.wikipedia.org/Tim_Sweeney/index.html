Timothy D. Sweeney (born 1970) is an American video game programmer and businessman. He is the founder and chief executive officer of Epic Games, and the creator of the Unreal Engine, one of the most widely licensed game development platforms.

Career

Sweeney founded the company in 1991 as Potomac Computer Systems in his parents' house, releasing his first game, ZZT, that year. The company, renamed Epic MegaGames and later Epic Games, achieved major success with Unreal (1998) and the Unreal Engine. Under Sweeney, Epic later created Gears of War, Infinity Blade and Fortnite, whose success after 2017 made the company one of the most valuable in the games industry and financed the Epic Games Store.

Views and interests

Sweeney is a noted critic of closed platform practices, and Epic's lawsuit against Apple over App Store fees drew worldwide attention. He is also a conservationist who has purchased large tracts of land in North Carolina to protect them from development.
