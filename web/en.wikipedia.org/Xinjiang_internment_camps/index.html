Xinjiang internment camps

The Xinjiang internment camps, officially titled Vocational Education and Training Centres by the government of China, are detention facilities in Xinjiang province that have detained Uyghurs and other ethnic minorities since 2017, mainly for alleged extremism and separatism.

History

The system expanded under party secretary Chen Quanguo as part of the Strike Hard Campaign Against Violent Terrorism. Leaks including the China Cables and Xinjiang Papers, satellite imagery, and survivor testimony documented the scale. A 2022 UN High Commissioner report found serious human rights violations that may amount to crimes against humanity.

Reactions

Some governments and legislatures have characterised the treatment of Uyghurs as genocide; China denies abuses, saying the centres counter extremism and have since closed, while researchers report forms of detention and coercive labour persisting. Human Rights Watch and Amnesty International have published extensive findings.
