Admiralty (United Kingdom)

The Admiralty was the British government department responsible for command of the Royal Navy until 1964. It was headed politically by the First Lord of the Admiralty and professionally by the First Sea Lord, with the Board of Admiralty exercising collective responsibility.

History

The office of Lord High Admiral dates to the 14th century; from 1628 it was usually in commission under a Board. The Admiralty oversaw naval operations through both world wars, including the Admiralty War Staff and Room 40 intelligence.

1964 reorganisation

In 1964 the Admiralty merged with the War Office and Air Ministry into a unified Ministry of Defence; the title Admiralty Board survives within the Defence Council.
