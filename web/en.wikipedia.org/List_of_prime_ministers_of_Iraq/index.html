List of prime ministers of Iraq

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The prime minister of Iraq is the head of government of the Republic of Iraq. The office was created in 1920 under the British Mandate. This list covers the prime ministers of the Kingdom of Iraq (1920–1958), the Iraqi Republic (1958–1968), the Ba'athist period (1968–2003), and the post-2003 federal republic, in which the prime minister is the country's chief executive, while the president serves as head of state.
