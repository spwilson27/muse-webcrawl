Budapest

Budapest is the capital and most populous city of Hungary, formed in 1873 by the unification of Buda and Óbuda on the west bank of the Danube with Pest on the east bank. Its population is about 1.7 million.

The city is famed for its thermal springs and grand bathhouses such as Széchenyi and Gellért, its Parliament Building, Buda Castle, Fisherman's Bastion, and the ruin bars of the Jewish Quarter. The Banks of the Danube and Buda Castle Quarter are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Budapest was a co-capital of Austria-Hungary until 1918, endured a devastating siege in 1944–45, and was the centre of the 1956 Hungarian Revolution. Today it is a major Central European financial, cultural, and tourism centre.
