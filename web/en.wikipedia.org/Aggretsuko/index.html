Aggretsuko

Created by: Sanrio (character design by Yeti)
Anime shorts: TBS, 2016–2018
Netflix series: 2018–2023, five seasons
Studio: Fanworks

Aggretsuko, also known in Japan as Aggressive Retsuko (Japanese: アグレッシブ烈子), is a Japanese animated comedy franchise created by Sanrio. The protagonist, Retsuko, is an anthropomorphic red panda who works in the accounting department of a Japanese trading firm and vents her workplace frustrations by singing death metal at karaoke.

Premise

Retsuko is a mild-mannered 25-year-old office worker subjected to a tyrannical boss (Director Ton, a pig), condescending superiors, and gossiping co-workers. By day she keeps her feelings bottled up; by night she goes to a karaoke bar where she unleashes her rage in full death-metal voice, complete with corpse-paint-like facial contortions. The series follows her work life, friendships, romances, and search for fulfilment.

Media

The character debuted in 2015 and first appeared in one-minute animated shorts produced by Fanworks, aired on TBS from 2016 to 2018. A Netflix original series premiered worldwide on 20 April 2018 and ran for five seasons, concluding in February 2023. The longer format expanded the cast, including Retsuko's friends Fenneko and Haida, her mother, and arcs involving idol groups, office politics, and elections.

A manga adaptation and several mobile and console tie-ins have also been released. The franchise was praised for its relatable depiction of millennial office life and work-life balance.
