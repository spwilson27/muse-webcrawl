Moo Deng

SpeciesPygmy hippopotamus
SexFemale
Born10 July 2024
ResidenceKhao Kheow Open Zoo, Chonburi, Thailand

Moo Deng (Thai: หมูเด้ง) is a female pygmy hippopotamus at the Khao Kheow Open Zoo in Thailand who became a global internet celebrity in 2024. Her name, chosen by a public poll, means "bouncy pork" — a Thai dish of seasoned pork — and she is part of a family whose members are named after pork dishes.

Moo Deng became famous for her expressive, feisty behaviour.

Viral fame

Videos and photographs of the calf — slippery-skinned, pink-cheeked, frequently photographed mid-bite or being hosed down by her keeper — spread across social media from September 2024, drawing enormous visitor numbers to the zoo and inspiring merchandise, memes and cosmetics products marketed on her glossy appearance. Saturday Night Live parodied her fame, and she was jokingly (and, in one poll, actually) tipped in coverage of the 2024 US presidential election. The zoo trademarked her image and channelled revenue into animal care, while also having to manage visitors who threw objects to get her attention.

Background

Pygmy hippos are an endangered species native to West African forests and swamps. Moo Deng's fame was credited with raising awareness of the species; her father Tony and mother Jona and siblings including Moo Toon are also kept at the zoo.
