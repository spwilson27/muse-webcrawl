Ken River

The Ken River is a river in the Bundelkhand region of central India, flowing through Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh. A tributary of the Yamuna, it runs about 427 kilometres from its source near Ahirgawan in Katni district to its confluence with the Yamuna at Chilla, near Banda.

The Ken passes through Panna National Park, where its deep gorges and the Raneh Falls — a canyon of coloured crystalline rocks — are notable features, and it supports gharial crocodiles in the Ken Gharial Sanctuary. The river is the subject of the Ken–Betwa river-linking project, intended to transfer water to the Betwa basin for irrigation, and controversial for submerging part of the Panna tiger habitat.
