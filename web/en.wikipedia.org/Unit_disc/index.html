Unit disc
Notation: D or 𝔻
Set: {z ∈ ℂ : |z| < 1} (open)
Boundary: Unit circle

In mathematics, the unit disc (or disk) is the set of points in the plane whose distance from a fixed centre point is less than (open disc) or at most (closed disc) one unit. In complex analysis the open unit disc 𝔻 = {z : |z| < 1} in the complex plane is a central object of study.

Role in analysis

The disc is the natural domain of power series with radius of convergence 1, and by Riemann's mapping theorem every simply connected proper subdomain of the plane is conformally equivalent to it. Equipped with the Poincaré metric, the unit disc forms a model of hyperbolic geometry. Its group of conformal automorphisms consists of the Möbius transformations that map the disc to itself.
