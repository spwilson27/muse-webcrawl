Kim Philby

Harold Adrian Russell "Kim" Philby (1 January 1912 – 11 May 1988) was a British intelligence officer and a double agent for the Soviet Union. Recruited by Soviet intelligence at Cambridge in the 1930s, he was the best known of the Cambridge Five spy ring.

Career and betrayal

Philby rose to senior positions in MI6, including heading its anti-Soviet section and serving as liaison in Washington — while passing secrets to Moscow that compromised numerous operations and agents, notably warning Donald Maclean and Guy Burgess to flee in 1951. Publicly cleared in 1955, he worked as a journalist in Beirut until further evidence emerged; he defected to the USSR in 1963 and lived in Moscow until his death.
