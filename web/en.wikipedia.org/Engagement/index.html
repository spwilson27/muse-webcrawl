Engagement

An engagement or betrothal is the period of time between the declaration of acceptance of a marriage proposal and the marriage itself (which is typically but not always commenced with a wedding). During this period, a couple is said to be fiancés (from the French), betrothed, intended, affianced, engaged to be married, or simply engaged.

Customs

Customs vary widely by culture. In many Western traditions the man proposes, often presenting an engagement ring, commonly set with a diamond. Engagement parties, newspaper announcements, and religious blessings may mark the commitment.

Legal aspects

Historically, a broken engagement could give rise to a legal action for breach of promise to marry; most jurisdictions have abolished or limited such claims. In some traditions the betrothal itself has a quasi-legal or religious status, and formal rites — such as the Jewish erusin — form part of the marriage ceremony.
