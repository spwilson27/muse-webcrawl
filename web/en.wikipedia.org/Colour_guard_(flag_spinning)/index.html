Colour guard (flag spinning)

A colour guard (spelled color guard in American English) is a team of performers who execute choreographed routines with flags, sabres, mock rifles and other equipment alongside a marching band, drum corps or as an independent indoor ensemble. The term originates with the military colour guard, the ceremonial detachment responsible for carrying a regiment's colours.

Performance and competition

In field shows, the guard interprets the band's music visually, spinning and tossing flags in coordination with the drill. During winter, guards compete indoors as winter guard, performing choreographed shows on tarps in gymnasiums; circuits such as Winter Guard International sanction regional and world championships with classes from scholastic to independent world.

Equipment and technique

The basic flag consists of a fabric silk attached to a pole, typically 1.5 to 1.8 metres long. Advanced performers use rifles and sabres for tosses requiring precise rotation and timing, and routines increasingly incorporate modern dance and theatrical staging.
