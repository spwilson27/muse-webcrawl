China Railway

China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., doing business as China Railway (CR), is the state-owned sole shareholder company that operates passenger and freight rail services in the People's Republic of China. It was formed in 2019 as the successor to China Railway Corporation, which itself replaced the Ministry of Railways in 2013.

Network

China Railway operates the largest railway network in Asia and the second largest in the world, exceeding 150,000 km of track by the early 2020s, including the world's largest high-speed rail network — over 40,000 km of dedicated lines served by Fuxing and Hexie trainsets branded as China Railway High-speed (CRH). Iconic routes include the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway and the Qinghai–Tibet railway, the highest railway in the world.

Operations

Day-to-day operations run through 18 regional railway bureaus (group companies) and specialized subsidiaries for container, express, and international services. Annual passenger journeys number in the billions; the Spring Festival travel season (chunyun) is the largest annual human migration. Freight operations connect to international corridors including the China–Europe Railway Express.

Reform

The 2013 abolition of the Ministry of Railways split regulatory oversight (kept by the National Railway Administration) from commercial operations. In June 2019 the corporation was renamed China State Railway Group and restructured as a company under state ownership.
