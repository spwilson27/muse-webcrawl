Washed Out

Washed Out is the stage name of American singer, songwriter, and record producer Ernest Weatherly Greene Jr. (born October 3, 1982). He is best known as a leading figure of the chillwave movement that emerged in the late 2000s, characterized by nostalgic, hazy, lo-fi electronic production and a summery, dreamlike atmosphere.

Career

Greene grew up in Georgia and earned a degree in English before working briefly as a librarian. After moving back in with his parents during the recession, he began recording songs in a bedroom studio. Two EPs released in 2009, Life of Leisure and High Times, attracted wide attention on music blogs. The track "Feel It All Around," built around a sample of Gary Low's "I Want You," became an anthem of the chillwave summer of 2009 and later served as the theme song of the television series Portlandia.

Studio albums

Signed to Sub Pop Records, Greene released his debut full-length album, Within and Without (2011), produced with Ben H. Allen. It was followed by Paracosm (2013), a lush, psychedelic-leaning record featuring more live instrumentation; Mister Mellow (2017), released with Stones Throw Records alongside a visual album; Purple Noon (2020), inspired by the Mediterranean and 1970s soft rock; and Notes from a Quiet Life (2024), recorded largely at his home studio in Athens, Georgia.

Style

Washed Out's music blends soft, reverb-drenched vocals with sampled beats, analog synthesizers, and melancholy melodies. Critics have associated the sound with nostalgia for 1980s pop and with the broader "hypnagogic pop" aesthetic.
