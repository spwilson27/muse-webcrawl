Stanley (vehicle)

Stanley is an autonomous car created by the Stanford Racing Team of Stanford University in cooperation with the Volkswagen Electronics Research Laboratory. It won the 2005 DARPA Grand Challenge, completing a 212 km (132 mi) desert course in 6 hours 53 minutes.

Technology

Built on a Volkswagen Touareg chassis, Stanley used GPS, inertial sensors, laser rangefinders (LIDAR), radar, and a video camera to perceive the terrain, running probabilistic machine-learning software to plan a safe path at speed.

Legacy

Stanley's victory demonstrated the feasibility of self-driving cars and catalysed the modern autonomous vehicle industry; members of its team went on to found or lead major self-driving projects, including Google's Chauffeur (later Waymo). The vehicle is now displayed at the Smithsonian National Museum of American History.
