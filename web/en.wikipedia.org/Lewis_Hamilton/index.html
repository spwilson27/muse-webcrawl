Sir Lewis Carl Davidson Hamilton (born 7 January 1985) is a British racing driver who competes in Formula One. He is a seven-time World Drivers' Champion, jointly holding the record with Michael Schumacher, and holds the outright records for most wins, pole positions, and podium finishes in the sport's history.

Career

Hamilton began karting at the age of eight and was signed to the McLaren young driver programme in 1998. He made his Formula One debut with McLaren in 2007, finishing runner-up in his rookie season, and won his first championship in 2008 in a dramatic final race in Brazil. In 2013 he moved to Mercedes, where he dominated the turbo-hybrid era, winning six further titles between 2014 and 2020. In 2025 he joined Ferrari, one of the most publicised transfers in the sport's history.

Records and influence

Hamilton surpassed 100 Grand Prix victories in 2021, a first in Formula One. Off track he has campaigned for diversity in motorsport, founded the Mission 44 charitable foundation and The Hamilton Commission, and was knighted in 2021.
