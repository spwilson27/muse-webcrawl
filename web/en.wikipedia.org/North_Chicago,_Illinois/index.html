North Chicago, Illinois

North Chicago is a city in Lake County, Illinois, United States, located on the shore of Lake Michigan about 35 miles north of downtown Chicago. Despite its name, it is a separate municipality, not part of the city of Chicago. Its population was about 30,000 at the 2020 census.

Naval Station Great Lakes

History and economy

The city grew in the late 19th century as an industrial suburb. It is best known as the home of Naval Station Great Lakes, the U.S. Navy's largest training installation and its only boot camp, established in 1911. Pharmaceutical company AbbVie has its global headquarters in North Chicago, and the Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science is located there. The city has faced economic challenges since the decline of local manufacturing, and much of its lakeside industry left contaminated sites that became federal Superfund cleanups.

Transportation

North Chicago is served by Metra's Union Pacific North Line and by Illinois Route 41 (Skokie Highway). The Great Lakes station serves the naval base.
