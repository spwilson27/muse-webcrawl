Vice President of India

ResidenceVice President's Enclave, New Delhi

The Vice President of India is the deputy to the head of state of the Republic of India, i.e. the president of India. The office is second in the order of precedence and first in the line of succession to the presidency. The vice president is also the ex officio chairperson of the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of the Parliament of India.

Election

The vice president is elected indirectly by an electoral college consisting of members of both houses of Parliament, by proportional representation using the single transferable vote. Unlike the presidential electoral college, state legislatures do not take part in the vice-presidential election.

Functions

The vice president acts as president in the event of the president's death, resignation, removal, or inability to discharge the functions of the office, until a new president is elected or the president resumes office. In the Rajya Sabha the vice president presides over proceedings but does not ordinarily vote, except to break a tie.
