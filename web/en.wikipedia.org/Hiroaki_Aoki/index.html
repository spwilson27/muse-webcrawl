Hiroaki Aoki (青木 廣彰, 1938–2008), known as Rocky Aoki, was a Japanese-born American restaurateur, professional wrestler, and powerboat racer. He founded the Benihana chain of teppanyaki restaurants, which introduced Japanese hibachi-style cooking as theatrical dining to the United States.

Early life and career

Aoki was born in Tokyo and was a member of Japan's wrestling team for the 1960 Rome Olympics, though he did not compete. He moved to the United States to study, sold ice cream from a truck in Harlem, and used his savings to open the first Benihana restaurant in New York City in 1964.

Benihana and other ventures

The chain grew into a well-known restaurant empire built around chefs cooking at the table with knife tricks and showmanship. Aoki was also a champion offshore powerboat racer, survived a serious crash in 1979, and twice attempted balloon crossings. He was the father of DJ and producer Steve Aoki and model Devon Aoki.
