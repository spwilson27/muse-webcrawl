Wikipedia: Neutral point of view

Neutral point of view (NPOV) is a core content policy of Wikipedia. It requires that articles represent all significant views fairly, proportionately and without editorial bias, relying on reliable sources rather than the opinions of editors.

Principles

Under NPOV, facts and opinions are distinguished, contested claims are attributed to their sources, and due weight is given according to prominence in reliable publications. The policy does not require false balance: fringe positions are not presented as equal to a settled scholarly consensus.

Application

Editors apply NPOV through wording, sourcing, article structure and the avoidance of loaded language. NPOV works together with verifiability and the prohibition on original research as one of Wikipedia's core content policies.
