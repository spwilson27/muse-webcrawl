Marquess of Bath

Marquess of Bath is a title in the Peerage of Great Britain, created in 1789 for Thomas Thynne, 3rd Viscount Weymouth. The family seat is Longleat House in Wiltshire, famous for its safari park.

Notable holders include the 1st Marquess, a politician who served as Secretary of State, and Alexander Thynn, 7th Marquess (1932–2020), an eccentric artist and author known for the murals and mazes at Longleat.

The present holder is Ceawlin Thynn, 8th Marquess of Bath.
