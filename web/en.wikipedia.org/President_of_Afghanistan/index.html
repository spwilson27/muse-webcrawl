President of Afghanistan

The President of Afghanistan was the head of state and head of government of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan, and the commander-in-chief of the Afghan Armed Forces. The office existed under the 2004 constitution until the republic collapsed in August 2021, when the Taliban seized Kabul and President Ashraf Ghani fled the country.

History

Afghanistan first became a republic in 1973 when Mohammed Daoud Khan overthrew King Mohammed Zahir Shah. After decades of upheaval, the 2004 constitution established a strong presidential system. Hamid Karzai served as the first elected president of the Islamic Republic (2004–2014), followed by Ashraf Ghani (2014–2021).

Powers

The president appointed ministers and judges (subject to parliamentary approval), commanded the armed forces, and could declare states of emergency. Presidents were elected for five-year terms and limited to two terms.
