United States Pacific Fleet

The United States Pacific Fleet (USPACFLT) is a theater-level component command of the United States Navy, headquartered at Naval Station Pearl Harbor in Hawaii. It provides naval forces to the United States Indo-Pacific Command across an area covering roughly half the Earth's surface.

Scope

The fleet's area spans from the West Coast of the United States across the Pacific and Indian Oceans to the east coast of Africa's approaches — some 100 million square miles. Its numbered fleets are the Third Fleet (eastern Pacific) and the forward-deployed Seventh Fleet in Japan.

History

The Pacific Fleet was formed in 1907 from the Pacific Squadron and Asiatic Squadron. It is indelibly associated with the attack on Pearl Harbor on 7 December 1941, after which it fought across the Pacific to victory in 1945. During the Cold War it maintained a major presence against the Soviet Pacific Fleet, and today it is central to U.S. strategy in the Indo-Pacific.

Organization

Beneath the fleet commander are type commanders for surface forces, air forces, submarines and other communities, principally based in San Diego, Pearl Harbor, Yokosuka and other Pacific ports.
