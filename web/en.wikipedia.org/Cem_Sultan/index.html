Cem Sultan

Cem Sultan
Born: 22 December 1459
Died: 25 February 1495, Naples
Father: Mehmed II
Rival: Bayezid II

Cem Sultan (1459–1495) was an Ottoman prince, son of Sultan Mehmed II, who claimed the throne after his father's death in 1481 and fought his elder brother Bayezid II. Defeated, he fled first to the Mamluks, then to the Knights Hospitaller on Rhodes.

Exile in Europe

Cem was held in France and later by Pope Innocent VIII and Alexander VI in Rome, becoming a valuable pawn in European plans for crusade against the Ottomans; Bayezid paid large sums to keep him detained. He died in Naples in 1495 while accompanying Charles VIII of France, amid rumours of poisoning.

Legacy

Cem was also a poet in Turkish and Persian. His body was eventually returned to Bursa. See Bayezid II and Ottoman Empire.
