Apolo Ohno

Born: 22 May 1982, Seattle, Washington
Sport: Short track speed skating
Olympic medals: 8 (2 gold, 2 silver, 4 bronze)

Apolo Anton Ohno (born 22 May 1982) is an American retired short track speed skater and television personality. With eight Winter Olympic medals won across the 2002, 2006 and 2010 Games, he is one of the most decorated American Winter Olympians.

Career

Ohno rose to prominence at the 2002 Winter Olympics in Salt Lake City, winning gold in the 1500 metres — after a famous crash took out most of the field and a controversial disqualification in another final — and silver in the 1000 metres. He added a gold, silver and bronze at Turin 2006, and three more medals at Vancouver 2010, which made him the most decorated American short track skater of all time.

After skating

Following retirement, Ohno won the fourth season of Dancing with the Stars, worked as an Olympic broadcaster, and became a motivational speaker, author and entrepreneur.
