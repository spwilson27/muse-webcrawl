IEEE 802.15.4

IEEE 802.15.4 is a technical standard that defines the operation of low-rate wireless personal area networks (LR-WPANs). It specifies the physical layer and media access control for low-power, low-data-rate radios and forms the basis for higher-layer specifications such as Zigbee, Thread, and WirelessHART.

Overview

The standard operates in unlicensed bands including the global 2.4 GHz ISM band, offering data rates up to 250 kbit/s over typical ranges of 10 to 100 metres. Devices can be organized in star or peer-to-peer (mesh) topologies, and the protocol supports very low duty cycles for battery-powered sensors.

Applications

IEEE 802.15.4 radios are used in home automation, industrial monitoring, smart metering, and Internet of Things devices. The 6LoWPAN adaptation layer allows IPv6 packets to run over these constrained links.
