Watermill Center

LocationWater Mill, New York, United States
Founded1992
FounderRobert Wilson

The Watermill Center is an interdisciplinary arts center located in Water Mill, New York, on Long Island. Founded in 1992 by theatre and opera director Robert Wilson, it serves as a laboratory for the arts, hosting residencies, workshops, and performances by artists from around the world.

History

Wilson purchased the site, a former Western Union laboratory, in 1992 and gradually transformed it into a campus of studios, rehearsal spaces, gardens, and galleries. The centerpiece is a large 20th-century building surrounded by landscaped grounds incorporating art installations and artefacts collected by Wilson.

Programs

The center's best-known program is its International Summer Program, begun in 1991, which brings together young and emerging artists for intensive collaborative work. Year-round residencies support the development of new performance, visual art, and design. The center also houses the Robert Wilson Archives and an extensive art collection.

Events

Watermill is known for its annual summer benefit gala, a major event in the Hamptons social calendar, and for open rehearsals and community "Discover Watermill Day" events.
