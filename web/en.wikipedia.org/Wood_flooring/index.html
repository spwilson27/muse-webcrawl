Wood flooring

Wood flooring is any product manufactured from timber that is designed for use as flooring, either structural or aesthetic. Wood is a common choice as a flooring material due to its durability, warmth, and restorability.

Types

Solid hardwood flooring is milled from a single piece of timber and can be sanded and refinished many times. Engineered wood flooring consists of a real-wood veneer bonded to layers of plywood or fibreboard, offering greater dimensional stability. Parquet consists of small pieces arranged in geometric patterns, while laminate flooring is a photographic imitation rather than real wood.

Species and installation

Common species include oak, maple, walnut, cherry, and various tropical hardwoods. Floors may be nailed, glued, or floated over a subfloor, and finished on site or pre-finished in the factory.

Maintenance

Wood floors respond to humidity, expanding and contracting with the seasons. They are maintained by sweeping, damp mopping, and periodic recoating or refinishing.
