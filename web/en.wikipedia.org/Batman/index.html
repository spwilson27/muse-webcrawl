Batman is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The character was created by artist Bob Kane and writer Bill Finger, and debuted in the 27th issue of the comic book Detective Comics on March 30, 1939. In the original stories, Batman's secret identity is Bruce Wayne, a wealthy American playboy, philanthropist, and industrialist who resides in Gotham City. His origin story features him swearing vengeance against criminals after witnessing the murder of his parents, Thomas and Martha Wayne, as a child, a vendetta tempered by a sense of justice.

Unlike most superheroes, Batman does not possess any superpowers; rather, he relies on his intellect, detective skills, physical prowess, martial arts abilities, an arsenal of high-tech gadgetry, and intimidation to fight crime. He operates with the support of allies including his butler Alfred Pennyworth, police commissioner James Gordon, and sidekicks such as Robin and Batgirl, against a gallery of villains that includes the Joker, Catwoman, the Penguin, the Riddler, Two-Face, and Ra's al Ghul.

Publication history

Batman was an immediate success, gaining his own self-titled ongoing series in 1940. Over the decades, writers including Dennis O'Neil and Neal Adams in the 1970s, Frank Miller in the 1980s (The Dark Knight Returns, "Year One"), and Grant Morrison and Scott Snyder in later years redefined the character, moving from the camp style of the 1960s toward darker, more psychological portrayals. Batman has consistently been among the most popular and commercially successful comic book characters.

In other media

Batman has been adapted extensively: the 1966 television series with Adam West; Tim Burton's and Joel Schumacher's films; Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy (2005–2012) with Christian Bale; Batman: The Animated Series (1992–1995), widely acclaimed and featuring Kevin Conroy's voice performance; the Arkham video game series; and Matt Reeves's The Batman (2022) with Robert Pattinson. The character is one of the most recognizable fictional figures in the world and a cornerstone of the DC Universe.
