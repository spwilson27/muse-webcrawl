Asobo Studio SAS is a French video game developer based in Bordeaux, founded in 2002 by a group of twelve developers. For much of its history the studio specialised in licensed games and support work, developing titles based on Pixar films such as Ratatouille, WALL-E and Toy Story 3, as well as games including Fuel (2009), the racing title notable for its very large open world, and several Kinect and HoloLens projects in partnership with Microsoft.

Breakthrough titles

Asobo achieved wide recognition with A Plague Tale: Innocence (2019), a stealth adventure game set in plague-stricken 14th-century France following siblings Amicia and Hugo de Rune, published by Focus Home Interactive. Praised for its storytelling, performances and its technically impressive swarms of rats, the game sold over a million copies. A sequel, A Plague Tale: Requiem, was released in October 2022 to further acclaim and award nominations, including at The Game Awards.

In parallel, Asobo co-developed Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) with Xbox Game Studios, recreating the entire Earth from satellite imagery and photogrammetry data streamed from the cloud. The simulator was acclaimed for its realism and technology and became one of the studio's defining projects; Asobo also supported its ongoing updates and the 2024 sequel. In 2021 the investment fund Sagard NewGen acquired a minority stake in the studio, which remains independent and headquartered in Bordeaux.
