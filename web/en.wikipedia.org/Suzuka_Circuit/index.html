Suzuka Circuit

Suzuka's figure-eight layout is unique in Formula One.

The Suzuka International Racing Course is a 5.807 km (3.608 mi) motorsport race track located in Ino, Suzuka City, Mie Prefecture, Japan and operated by Honda Mobilityland, a subsidiary of Honda Motor Co, Ltd. It has a capacity of 155,000 and is most famous as the home of the Formula One Japanese Grand Prix.

Designed by Dutchman John Hugenholtz, Suzuka opened in 1962 as a Honda test track and is notable for its figure-eight layout, in which one section of the course passes over another via a bridge — the only such layout on the Formula One calendar.

Events

Suzuka hosted the title-deciding Japanese Grands Prix of the late 1980s and early 1990s, including the famous clashes between Ayrton Senna and Alain Prost in 1989 and 1990. The circuit also hosts the Suzuka 8 Hours motorcycle endurance race, Super GT, and Super Formula events.

Track features

Famous sections include the S Curves, the Degner Curve, the Spoon Curve, and the fast 130R corner, named for its 130-metre radius.
