Paramount+

Paramount+ is an American subscription video on-demand streaming service owned by Paramount Global (formerly ViacomCBS). It launched in the United States on March 4, 2021, as a rebrand and expansion of CBS All Access, combining live and on-demand programming from CBS, cable networks such as Nickelodeon, MTV, BET, and Comedy Central, and the Paramount Pictures film library.

History

The service's predecessor, CBS All Access, launched in October 2014 as the first over-the-top offering from an American broadcast network. Following the 2019 merger of CBS Corporation and Viacom, the service was relaunched as Paramount+ with a much larger catalogue, including franchises such as Star Trek, SpongeBob SquarePants, and the films of Paramount Pictures.

Content

Original series include Star Trek: Discovery, Star Trek: Picard, Strange New Worlds, 1923, Tulsa King, and Halo. The service also streams live sports, including NFL games on CBS and UEFA Champions League football, and breaking news from CBS News.

Availability

Paramount+ has rolled out across the Americas, Europe, and Australia, partly through partnerships such as SkyShowtime in parts of Europe.
