Skagen

Skagen is Denmark's northernmost town and a port town in the North Jutland Region, situated on the east coast of the Skaw peninsula in the far north of Jutland. It has a population of around 7,500 to 8,000.

The town is famous for its distinctive light, its yellow-plastered houses with red roofs and white trim, and the Skagen Painters, a group of Scandinavian artists who gathered there in the late 19th century, including P.S. Krøyer and Michael and Anna Ancher. Their work is displayed at Skagens Museum.

Nearby is Grenen, the sandy point where the Skagerrak and Kattegat seas meet, and the sand-covered church (Den Tilsandede Kirke), abandoned in 1795 as drifting sand engulfed it. Fishing and tourism are the town's main industries.
