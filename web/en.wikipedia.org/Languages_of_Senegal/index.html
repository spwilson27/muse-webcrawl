Languages of Senegal

Senegal is a multilingual country: Ethnologue lists some 39 languages spoken there. French is the official language, used in government, education and media, while Wolof is the most widely spoken language and serves as a lingua franca across the country.

National languages

The constitution recognises languages with the status of "national languages": Wolof, Serer, Pulaar (Fula), Mandinka, Soninke and Diola (Jola) were first listed, and others — including Balanta, Bassari, Bedik, Hassaniya, Maninka, Noon, Safen and others — have been added as languages have been codified with official orthographies.

Distribution

Wolof is the mother tongue of the Wolof people and is understood by a large majority of Senegalese. Pulaar is spoken by Fula (Peul) communities, especially in the north and south; Serer in the west-centre; Diola/Jola in Casamance; Mandinka and Soninke particularly in the east and south.

Use and media

French remains dominant in formal education, though literacy in French is limited; Wolof dominates radio, popular music and everyday commerce in Dakar and other cities. Arabic is used in religious education in daaras (Qur'anic schools).
