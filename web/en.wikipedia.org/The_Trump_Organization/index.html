The Trump Organization

The Trump Organization is an American privately owned conglomerate headquartered at Trump Tower in Manhattan, New York City. It serves as the umbrella organization for the business ventures and investments of Donald Trump, who built the company from the real estate business founded by his grandmother Elizabeth Christ Trump and his father, Fred Trump.

History

Elizabeth Christ Trump founded the company in 1923 as E. Trump & Son; Fred Trump expanded it into a major builder of middle-class housing in Queens, Brooklyn and Staten Island. Donald Trump joined in 1968, took control in 1971, and renamed it The Trump Organization. He shifted its focus toward Manhattan luxury developments, hotels, casinos and licensing, building properties such as Trump Tower (1983), the Grand Hyatt New York and the Plaza Hotel.

Operations

The organization's holdings span real estate development, hotels, golf courses, residential towers, and brand licensing. When Donald Trump became president of the United States in 2017, he placed his assets in a trust managed by his sons Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump. The company has also been the subject of significant legal proceedings, including a New York civil fraud case in which it was found liable in 2023. See also Trump Tower.
