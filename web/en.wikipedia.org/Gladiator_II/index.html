Gladiator II

Gladiator II is a 2024 epic historical action film directed by Ridley Scott and written by David Scarpa, from a story by Scarpa and Peter Craig. It is the sequel to Gladiator (2000). The film stars Paul Mescal, Pedro Pascal, Connie Nielsen, and Denzel Washington. Nielsen and Derek Jacobi reprise their roles from the first film.

Plot

Sixteen years after the death of Maximus, Lucius Verus — the son of Lucilla, sent away from Rome as a child — lives in Numidia under the name Hanno. When Roman forces under General Marcus Acacius conquer his city and kill his wife, Lucius is enslaved and bought by Macrinus, a former slave turned arms dealer and political schemer, who trains him as a gladiator. In Rome, Lucius fights in the Colosseum under the twin emperors Geta and Caracalla while Macrinus manoeuvres toward power, and Lucius's identity as the grandson of Marcus Aurelius becomes the catalyst for a struggle over the future of Rome.

Production and release

A sequel to Gladiator was discussed for many years, including an earlier script by Nick Cave that was never made. The film was shot principally in Malta and Morocco in 2023–2024, with production interrupted by the 2023 Hollywood strikes. Hans Zimmer did not return; the score was composed by Harry Gregson-Williams.

Gladiator II premiered in Sydney on 30 October 2024 and was released internationally in November 2024, including in the United States on 22 November by Paramount Pictures. It grossed over US$460 million worldwide against a large production budget, and received generally positive reviews, with Washington's performance widely praised; it was nominated for two Golden Globe Awards and for the Academy Award for Best Costume Design.
