Los Angeles FC

Los Angeles Football Club, commonly known as LAFC, is an American professional soccer club based in Los Angeles, California, competing in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Western Conference. Founded in 2014, the club began play in 2018 at Banc of California Stadium (now BMO Stadium) in Exposition Park.

LAFC won the Supporters' Shield in 2019 and 2022 and MLS Cup in 2022, defeating the Philadelphia Union on penalties in one of the league's most celebrated finals. Its rivalry with the LA Galaxy is known as El Tráfico. Notable players have included Carlos Vela, the club's first signing and captain, and later arrivals such as Gareth Bale and Olivier Giroud. The 3252 supporters' union is renowned for its atmosphere.
