The Crusaders are a New Zealand professional rugby union team based in Christchurch, who compete in the Super Rugby Pacific competition. They are the most successful team in the competition's history, having won a record number of titles, and represent the Buller, Canterbury, Mid Canterbury, South Canterbury, Tasman and West Coast provincial unions.

History

The franchise was established in 1996 as one of five New Zealand teams in the inaugural Super 12. After a slow start, the Crusaders won their first title in 1998 under coach Wayne Smith and captain Todd Blackadder, beginning a dynasty: titles followed in 1999, 2000, 2002, 2005, 2006 and 2008. Under coach Scott Robertson the team won three consecutive Super Rugby titles from 2017 to 2019, plus the Super Rugby Aotearoa titles in 2020 and 2021, and further Super Rugby Pacific championships in 2022 and 2023.

Identity

The team's name and imagery evoke the medieval Crusaders, with players traditionally led onto the field by mounted horsemen in chainmail. Their colours are red and black, the traditional colours of Canterbury rugby. The team is famous for its production of All Blacks, including Richie McCaw, Dan Carter, Kieran Read and Sam Whitelock.

Home ground

The Crusaders played at Lancaster Park (Jade Stadium / AMI Stadium) until the stadium was damaged beyond repair in the 2011 Christchurch earthquake. Since 2012 they have played at the temporary stadium at Addington (Apollo Projects Stadium), pending the construction of the new One New Zealand Stadium in central Christchurch.
