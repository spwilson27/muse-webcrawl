Marc Benioff

Benioff, founder of Salesforce and pioneer of cloud-based software.

Marc Russell Benioff (born September 25, 1964) is an American internet entrepreneur and philanthropist. He is the co-founder, chairman and CEO of Salesforce, the enterprise cloud computing company that pioneered delivering customer relationship management (CRM) software as a subscription service over the internet. Benioff is also the owner of Time magazine, which he and his wife Lynne bought in 2018.

Early career

Benioff grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area; as a teenager he wrote and sold games for the Atari 8-bit computers and founded a small software company. After graduating from the University of Southern California in 1986, he joined Oracle Corporation, where he spent 13 years in sales, marketing and product development roles, becoming the company's youngest vice president and a close protégé of Larry Ellison — who later provided early funding for Salesforce.

Salesforce

In 1999 Benioff founded Salesforce (originally salesforce.com) in a rented San Francisco apartment, promoting the slogan "The End of Software" and the then-novel model of business applications delivered over the web by subscription — software as a service (SaaS). The company went public in 2004 and grew, through internal development and major acquisitions including Slack (2021, about $27.7 billion), Tableau (2019) and MuleSoft (2018), into one of the world's largest enterprise software companies. Its San Francisco headquarters, Salesforce Tower, is the tallest building in the city. Benioff embedded philanthropy in the company's founding documents through the "1-1-1 model", donating 1 per cent of equity, product and employee time to charitable causes — a model later promoted to other firms through Pledge 1%.

Philanthropy and other activities

Benioff and his wife have been major donors to children's hospitals in the Bay Area (the UCSF Benioff Children's Hospitals) and to causes including homelessness initiatives in San Francisco, ocean conservation and climate action; he is a signatory of the Giving Pledge. In 2018 the Benioffs personally purchased Time magazine from Meredith Corporation for $190 million. Benioff has written several books, including Compassionate Capitalism (2004) and Trailblazer (2019, with Monica Langley), advocating stakeholder capitalism.
