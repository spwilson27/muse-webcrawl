Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme

The Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM) is the global governing and sanctioning body for motorcycle racing. Founded in Paris in 1904 as the Fédération Internationale des Clubs Motocyclistes, it is headquartered in Mies, Switzerland, and represents national federations from well over one hundred countries.

FIM
Founded: 21 December 1904
Headquarters: Mies, Switzerland
Sport: Motorcycle racing

Role

The FIM sanctions world championship series including MotoGP (with Dorna), Superbike, Motocross, Trial, Enduro, and Speedway events, and works on road safety, touring, and environmental standards for motorcycling.
