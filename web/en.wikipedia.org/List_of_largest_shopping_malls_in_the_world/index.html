List of largest shopping malls in the world

This is a list of the world's largest shopping malls, ranked by gross leasable area (GLA). The largest shopping malls are concentrated in Asia, where several "megamalls" combine retail with entertainment complexes, hotels, and theme parks. The Iran Mall in Tehran is generally cited as the largest mall in the world by total area, while malls in China, Malaysia, and the Philippines dominate by leasable retail space.

History

The modern enclosed shopping mall emerged in the United States in the 1950s, with centres such as Southdale (1956). Edmonton, Canada's West Edmonton Mall was the world's largest mall from 1981 until 2004 and remains the largest in North America by total area alongside Mall of America, developed by the same Triple Five Group. In the 2000s, development shifted to Asia, where new megamalls in China, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East surpassed North American centres in scale.
