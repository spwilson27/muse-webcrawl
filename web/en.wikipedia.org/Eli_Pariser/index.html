Eli Pariser

Born: December 17, 1980, Lincolnville, Maine, U.S.
Occupation: Author, activist, entrepreneur
Known for: MoveOn.org, Upworthy, the "filter bubble" concept

Eli Pariser (born December 17, 1980) is an American author, activist, and entrepreneur. He became executive director of MoveOn.org in 2004 and later co-founded the viral media company Upworthy.

MoveOn and online activism

Pariser joined MoveOn during the aftermath of the September 11 attacks, organising an online petition against war in Iraq that drew half a million signatures. As executive director he helped pioneer large-scale online political organising and small-donor fundraising.

The Filter Bubble

His 2011 book The Filter Bubble: What the Internet Is Hiding from You argued that personalised search results and social feeds isolate users in ideological information bubbles, a concept that became widely cited in debates about algorithms and democracy. His TED talk on the subject has been viewed millions of times.

Later work

Pariser co-founded Upworthy in 2012 and has since worked on civic technology and the idea of "digital public infrastructure", promoting online spaces designed like public parks rather than attention-maximising feeds.
