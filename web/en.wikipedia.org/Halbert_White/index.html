Halbert White

Halbert Lynn White Jr. (19 November 1950 – 31 March 2012) was an American economist, professor at the University of California, San Diego, widely regarded as one of the most influential econometricians of his generation. He earned his PhD from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 1976.

Contributions

White is best known for the White test for heteroskedasticity and for heteroskedasticity-consistent standard errors (the "White" or "sandwich" standard errors), introduced in his 1980 Econometrica paper, which allow valid inference in regression models without assuming constant error variance. He also made major contributions to specification testing, quasi-maximum likelihood estimation, and the econometric analysis of artificial neural networks, including White's reality check for data snooping. His textbook Asymptotic Theory for Econometricians (1984) became a standard graduate reference.
