Cradle to Cradle

Cradle to Cradle
Authors: Michael Braungart, William McDonough
Published: 2002 (book)
Field: Sustainable design

Cradle to Cradle (C2C) is a sustainable design philosophy and certification system popularized by chemist Michael Braungart and architect William McDonough in their 2002 book Cradle to Cradle: Remaking the Way We Make Things. It contrasts with "cradle to grave" linear production, proposing that materials circulate indefinitely in closed loops.

Principles

The framework rests on three principles drawn from natural systems: waste equals food, use current solar income, and celebrate diversity. Materials are classified as biological nutrients, which can safely return to the environment, or technical nutrients, designed to circulate within industrial cycles without contamination.

Certification

The Cradle to Cradle Certified Products Program assesses products across material health, material reutilization, renewable energy, water stewardship, and social fairness, awarding levels from Basic to Platinum.

Criticism

Critics note that full closed-loop recycling is rarely achieved in practice and that certification can be costly for small manufacturers.
