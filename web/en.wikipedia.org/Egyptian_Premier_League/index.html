Egyptian Premier League
Founded: 1948
Country: Egypt
Confederation: CAF
Number of teams: 18
Most championships: Al Ahly (over 40 titles)

The Egyptian Premier League is the top professional association football league in Egypt and the highest division of the Egyptian football league system. Founded in 1948, it is organized by the Egyptian Football Association and is contested by 18 clubs, operating on a system of promotion and relegation with the Egyptian Second Division.

History

Football was introduced to Egypt in the late 19th century, and regional leagues existed in Cairo, Alexandria, and the Canal Zone before a national league was established in 1948. The league has been suspended or cancelled several times, including during the 1952 revolution period, the wars of 1967 and 1973, and after the Port Said Stadium riot of 2012, which led to matches being played behind closed doors for extended periods.

Dominant clubs

The league has been dominated by the two Cairo giants, Al Ahly and Zamalek, whose meetings form the Cairo derby, one of the most intense rivalries in world football. Al Ahly is the most successful club with more than forty league titles; Zamalek is second. Ismaily, Al Masry, Al Ittihad Alexandria, and Arab Contractors (Al Mokawloon) are among the other historically notable clubs.

Format

Clubs play a double round-robin schedule. The champion and runner-up qualify for the CAF Champions League, while lower-placed teams may qualify for the CAF Confederation Cup; the bottom clubs are relegated.
