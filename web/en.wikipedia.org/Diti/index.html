Diti

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Diti is a goddess in Hindu mythology, and the mother of the Daityas. She is a daughter of Daksha and a consort of the sage Kashyapa, along with her sister Aditi. Where Aditi is the mother of the Adityas (gods), Diti is the mother of the Daityas (demons), a duality that runs through many Hindu myths.

Legends

According to the Puranas, Diti's sons include the asura kings Hiranyaksha and Hiranyakashipu, whose deaths at the hands of the boar avatar Varaha and the man-lion avatar Narasimha form central episodes of Vishnu's mythology. Diti is also the mother of Maruts through a story in which Indra, fearing a powerful son, divided her unborn child into many parts; these became the Maruts, storm deities who accompany Indra. In some accounts she is also mother of the demoness Holika.

Diti is described as a woman of strong will and long penance, and her children are frequently presented as the rivals of Aditi's children, the Devas, in the recurring struggle between order and chaos in Hindu cosmology.
