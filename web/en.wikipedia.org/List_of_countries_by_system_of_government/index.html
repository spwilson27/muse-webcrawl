List of countries by system of government

This is a list of sovereign states by system of government, organised by the relationship between the head of state, the head of government and the legislature, and by whether the state is a republic or a monarchy, unitary or federal.

Categories

Presidential republics (e.g. United States, Brazil, Indonesia): the president is both head of state and head of government, elected independently of the legislature. Semi-presidential republics (e.g. France, Russia): a president shares executive power with a prime minister responsible to parliament. Parliamentary republics (e.g. Germany, India, Italy): a largely ceremonial president and a prime minister who leads the government.

Constitutional monarchies include parliamentary systems such as the United Kingdom, Spain, Japan and the Netherlands, where the monarch reigns within constitutional limits, and executive constitutional monarchies such as Jordan and Morocco. Absolute monarchies include Saudi Arabia, Oman and Eswatini. One-party states include China, Cuba, North Korea and Vietnam. Theocracies include Iran (a hybrid system) and Vatican City.

Federal and unitary

Federal states — among them the United States, Germany, India, Brazil, Canada and Australia — divide sovereignty between central and regional governments; most other states are unitary, though many devolve power regionally, as in the United Kingdom and Spain.
