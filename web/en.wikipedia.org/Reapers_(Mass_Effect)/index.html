The Reapers are a fictional race of sentient machines in BioWare's Mass Effect video game franchise. They are enormous living starships that reside in the dark space between galaxies and periodically return to the Milky Way to exterminate all advanced organic civilisation in a recurring cycle of extinction, harvesting the genetic material of the destroyed species to construct new Reapers.

Concept and design

The Reapers were designed to evoke the cosmic horror of H. P. Lovecraft's fiction: entities so ancient and powerful that they regard organic life as inconsequential. Their capital ships resemble giant cuttlefish or squid, with articulated limbs used both for locomotion on planetary surfaces and for close combat. The first Reaper encountered by the player, Sovereign, speaks with a deep, distorted voice compounded of many layered tones, reinforcing the impression of a collective machine intelligence.

Role in the series

In Mass Effect (2007), Commander Shepard discovers that Sovereign is not a Prothean warship, as first believed, but the vanguard of the Reaper fleet, working through the rogue Spectre Saren Arterius and the geth to reactivate the Citadel mass relay and admit the fleet from dark space. Shepard's victory delays the invasion but does not avert it.

In Mass Effect 2 (2010), the Collectors — revealed to be mutated Protheans serving the Reapers — abduct human colonists to build a Human-Reaper larva. In Mass Effect 3 (2012), the Reapers invade the galaxy in force, and the united fleets of the galaxy must complete the Crucible, a superweapon of uncertain function, before Earth and the other homeworlds fall. The trilogy's ending turns on Shepard's final choice concerning the Catalyst, the intelligence that controls the Reapers.

Origins

The Leviathan downloadable content for Mass Effect 3 reveals that the Reapers were created by the Catalyst, an artificial intelligence built by the Leviathans, an ancient apex species that sought to preserve life by preventing conflict between organics and the synthetics they create. The Catalyst's solution was the cycle: harvest advanced civilisations before they could destroy themselves, preserving their essence in Reaper form. The first Reaper, Harbinger, was created from the Leviathans themselves.

Reception

The Reapers have been widely praised as antagonists, noted for their scale, design and the existential threat they pose, though the explanation of their motives in Mass Effect 3 drew a mixed response. Sovereign's dialogue with Shepard on Virmire is frequently cited among the most memorable scenes in the series.
