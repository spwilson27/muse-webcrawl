Battle of Montiel

The Battle of Montiel was fought on 14 March 1369 near Montiel, La Mancha, in the Castilian Civil War, between the forces of Peter of Castile, supported by Granada and other allies, and the Franco-Castilian army of Henry of Trastámara led in the field by the French commander Bertrand du Guesclin.

Outcome

Peter's army was defeated, and the king took refuge in the castle of Montiel. Days later he was lured out by du Guesclin under pretence of negotiation and murdered by his half-brother Henry, who then became Henry II of Castile and founded the Trastámara dynasty.
