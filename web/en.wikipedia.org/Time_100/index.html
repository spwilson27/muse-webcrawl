Time 100

The Time 100 is a list published annually by the American news magazine Time naming the 100 most influential people in the world. It grew out of a one-off 1999 list of the most influential people of the 20th century and was first published as an annual list in 2004.

Format

Honorees are grouped into categories that have varied over the years, including Artists, Leaders, Icons, Pioneers and Titans. Many entries are accompanied by short tribute essays written by other notable figures. Inclusion is not ranked and is explicitly not a measure of approval: dictators and controversial figures have appeared alongside humanitarians, on the stated criterion of influence "for better or for worse".

Selection

Candidates are nominated by Time editors, former honorees and the magazine's international staff; an online reader poll also runs each year, occasionally producing mass-voting campaigns for internet personalities. The final list is chosen by Time's editors. The list is launched with a gala event, the Time 100 Summit and Gala in New York City.

Companion lists

Time has published related lists including Time Next (rising figures) and, in 2019, a list of the 100 most influential people in health and other specialist categories, along with regional editions.
