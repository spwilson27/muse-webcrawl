Song of the Sea


Theatrical poster for Song of the Sea

Song of the Sea (Irish: Amhrán na Mara) is a 2014 animated fantasy film directed and co-produced by Tomm Moore, and written by Will Collins. An Irish–Luxembourgish–Danish–Belgian–French co-production, it is the second feature from the Irish studio Cartoon Saloon, following The Secret of Kells (2009). The film draws on Irish mythology, particularly the legend of the selkie.

Plot

Ben, a young Irish boy, lives on an island with his lighthouse-keeper father Conor and his mute younger sister Saoirse. Ben blames Saoirse for the disappearance of their mother Bronagh, a selkie who vanished into the sea on the night Saoirse was born. On Halloween, Saoirse plays a shell flute given to their mother and discovers a magical coat that transforms her into a seal. The children are taken to the city by their grandmother, but flee to journey home, pursued by the owl witch Macha, who has been turning fairy folk to stone. Saoirse must sing the song of the sea to free the faeries before Samhain ends.

Reception

The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2014 and was widely acclaimed for its hand-drawn animation and emotional storytelling. It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 87th Academy Awards. The voice cast includes Brendan Gleeson, Fionnula Flanagan, and Lisa Hannigan; the score was composed by Bruno Coulais in collaboration with the Irish folk group Kíla.
