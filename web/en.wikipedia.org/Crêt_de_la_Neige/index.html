Crêt de la Neige

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Crêt de la Neige is the highest peak in the Jura Mountains, straddling the border between France and Switzerland in the French department of Ain, near the Swiss canton of Geneva. It reaches an elevation of 1,720 metres (about 5,643 feet) according to recent surveys by the French national geographic institute.

Elevation dispute

For many years Le Reculet, a neighbouring summit, was considered the highest point of the Jura at 1,718 metres, and some maps accordingly showed Crêt de la Neige as slightly lower. Improved measurements published in the early 2000s reversed the ranking, establishing Crêt de la Neige as the higher summit by roughly two metres, a dispute of interest mainly to peak-baggers and mapmakers given the gentle, rounded profiles of both mountains.

Access and landscape

The peak lies within the Haut-Jura regional natural park and close to the Haute Chaîne du Jura national nature reserve. It can be reached by hiking trails along the main Jura crest, including the Grande Traversée du Jura, and offers panoramic views towards Geneva, Lake Geneva, and, on clear days, Mont Blanc and the Alps. The summit area is characterized by limestone pavement, subalpine grassland, and sparse pine forest.
