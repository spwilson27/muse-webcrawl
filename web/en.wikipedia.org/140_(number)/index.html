140 (one hundred [and] forty) is the natural number following 139 and preceding 141. It is an abundant number and a Harshad number in base 10.

In mathematics

140 is the sum of the first twelve square numbers up to 36 in combination and is notable as a square pyramidal number: 140 = 1² + 2² + 3² + 4² + 5² + 6² + 7². It is the twelfth abundant number and has twelve divisors. 140 is also the number of ways of tiling certain boards in combinatorial enumerations.

In other fields

The atomic number of the hypothetical element unquadnilium is 140. In social media history, 140 was famously the original character limit for posts on the platform Twitter, a constraint that shaped its terse style before being doubled to 280 in 2017. Compare 134.
