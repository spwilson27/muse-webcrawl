Great Indian bustard

The great Indian bustard (Ardeotis nigriceps) or Indian bustard is a large bustard found on the Indian subcontinent. A huge, horizontal bird with a long neck and long bare legs, it is among the heaviest of the flying birds.

Description

Males stand about 1 m tall and weigh up to 15 kg, with black plumage on the crown contrasting with a pale neck and underparts. Females are smaller. The species inhabits dry grasslands and scrub.

Status

Once widespread across India and Pakistan, the bird is now critically endangered because of hunting and the loss and fragmentation of its grassland habitat, including collisions with power lines. Fewer than a few hundred individuals are thought to survive, centred on the Desert National Park in Rajasthan. It is the state bird of Rajasthan.
