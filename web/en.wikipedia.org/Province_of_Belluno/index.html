Province of Belluno

Country: Italy
Region: Veneto
Capital: Belluno
Area: about 3,610 km²
Population: about 200,000

The province of Belluno (Italian: provincia di Belluno) is a province in the Veneto region of Italy. Its capital is the city of Belluno. The province lies in the far north of the region, bordering Austria to the north, and is almost entirely mountainous, encompassing a large part of the Dolomites.

Geography

Belluno is the largest and least densely populated province in Veneto. The territory is drained by the Piave River, which rises in the Carnic Alps and flows southward through the province. Famous peaks within or on the borders of the province include the Marmolada, the Tre Cime di Lavaredo, and Monte Pelmo. Well-known resorts in the province include Cortina d'Ampezzo, host of the 1956 Winter Olympics, and Falcade.

Economy

Tourism, both summer and winter, is a major sector, thanks to the ski resorts of the Dolomites. The province is also known for eyewear manufacturing, centred on the Cadore district, where large companies such as Luxottica were founded. Agriculture, dairy farming, and wood processing are traditional activities in the valleys.

Municipalities

The province comprises around 60 municipalities. The largest towns include Belluno, Feltre, Sedico, Ponte nelle Alpi, and Cortina d'Ampezzo.
