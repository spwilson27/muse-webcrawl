Media franchise

The spelling "Pokemon" is the plain-ASCII rendering of Pokémon, the Japanese media franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri in 1996, comprising video games, an animated series, films, and a trading card game. It is a contraction of the Japanese brand name Pocket Monsters (ポケットモンスター). The accent on the "é" indicates its pronunciation.

Usage

Because many computer systems, keyboards, and older encodings could not easily produce the "é" character, "Pokemon" is widely used in informal writing, file names, and URLs. Officially, the franchise name is always written with the accent as "Pokémon", both in the singular and the plural.
