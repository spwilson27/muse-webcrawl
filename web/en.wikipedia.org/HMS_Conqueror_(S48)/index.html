HMS Conqueror (S48)

HMS Conqueror (pennant number S48) was a Churchill-class nuclear-powered fleet submarine of the British Royal Navy, commissioned in 1971. She is best known for sinking the Argentine cruiser ARA General Belgrano during the Falklands War in 1982 — the only nuclear-powered submarine ever to engage an enemy ship with torpedoes in combat, and the only warship sunk by a nuclear submarine.

Falklands War

On 2 May 1982, Conqueror, commanded by Commander Christopher Wreford-Brown, fired three Second World War–era Mark 8 torpedoes at General Belgrano; two struck, sinking the cruiser with the loss of 323 Argentine lives. The sinking occurred outside the British-declared Total Exclusion Zone, a fact that remained politically controversial. After the attack, Conqueror shadowed the Argentine fleet and later conducted reconnaissance missions, including flights-detecting patrols near the Argentine coast.

Later service

In June 1982 she returned to Faslane flying the Jolly Roger, the traditional emblem of a successful patrol. Aboard during the war were members of the Special Boat Service; the submarine also undertook operations towing a Soviet listening array in an episode long kept secret. Conqueror was decommissioned in 1990 and remains laid up awaiting disposal, her periscopes and control room fittings preserved at museums.
