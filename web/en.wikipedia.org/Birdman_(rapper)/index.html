Birdman (rapper)

Bryan Christopher Williams (born February 15, 1969), better known by his stage names Birdman and Baby, is an American rapper and record executive. He is the co-founder and public face of Cash Money Records, which he founded with his older brother Ronald "Slim" Williams in 1991. He is also one half of the duo Big Tymers with producer Mannie Fresh, and has released several solo albums.

Career

Birdman and his brother Ronald founded Cash Money Records in New Orleans in 1991, building the label into a powerhouse of Southern hip hop with artists such as Juvenile, B.G., and Lil Wayne, whom Birdman mentored from childhood. As half of Big Tymers he scored hits including "Still Fly" (2002). His solo albums include Birdman (2002), Fast Money (2005), 5 * Stunna (2007), and Priceless (2009), and he collaborated with Lil Wayne on the album Like Father, Like Son (2006). The single "Stuntin' Like My Daddy" became one of his signatures.

Business ventures

Beyond music, Birdman has been involved in ventures including the Rich Gang collective and clothing and oil exploration businesses, and has frequently appeared on lists of hip hop's wealthiest figures, though some ventures and reported figures have been disputed.

Controversies

Cash Money under Birdman has been the subject of numerous lawsuits and public disputes over unpaid royalties and contracts, including high-profile conflicts with Lil Wayne in the mid-2010s, later settled, and earlier disputes with Juvenile and Mannie Fresh.
