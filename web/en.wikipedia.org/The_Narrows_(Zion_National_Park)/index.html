The Narrows (Zion National Park)

LocationZion National Park, Utah, United States
RiverNorth Fork of the Virgin River
LengthAbout 16 miles (26 km)

The Narrows is the narrowest section of Zion Canyon in Zion National Park, Utah. It is a gorge carved by the North Fork of the Virgin River, with walls up to a thousand feet tall and a river sometimes only twenty to thirty feet wide.

Hiking

The Narrows is one of the most popular hikes in the park. Most visitors hike "bottom-up" from the Temple of Sinawava, walking largely in the river itself; the full 16-mile "top-down" route requires a wilderness permit. Hikers must be alert to the danger of flash floods and check river flow rates and weather forecasts before entering.
