Navya-Nyaya

Navya-Nyaya (Sanskrit: "New Logic") is a school of Indian philosophy that developed from about the 13th century CE as a refinement of the classical Nyaya school. It is associated especially with the philosopher Gangesha Upadhyaya, whose Tattvacintamani ("Thought-Jewel of Reality") founded the school, and with later thinkers such as Raghunatha Shiromani.

Method

Navya-Nyaya developed a highly precise technical language for logic and epistemology, defining terms with extraordinary care in order to remove ambiguity in philosophical debate. Its definitions, relational structures and treatment of negation influenced all later Indian philosophical traditions.

Centres

The school flourished in Mithila and later in Navadvipa (Nadia) in Bengal, which became a major centre of learning.
