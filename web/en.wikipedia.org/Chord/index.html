Chord

Chord may refer to several distinct concepts:

A chord in music and a chord in geometry

Music

  • Chord (music) — any combination of two or more notes sounded simultaneously, typically three or more, forming the basis of harmony in Western music. Common examples include major and minor triads and seventh chords.

Mathematics

  • Chord (geometry) — a line segment whose endpoints both lie on a curve, most commonly a circle.

Other uses

  • Chord, a component of a truss structure in engineering, running parallel to the span.
  • Chord, a peer-to-peer lookup protocol in distributed computing.
  • Chord, in zoology, a primitive feature of chordates such as the notochord.
