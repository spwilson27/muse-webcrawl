World Marathon Majors

The World Marathon Majors (WMM), currently sponsored by Abbott Laboratories and officially known as the Abbott World Marathon Majors, is a championship-style competition for marathon runners that started in 2006. It comprises a series of the largest and most renowned marathons in the world.

The Sydney Marathon was added as the seventh major in 2025. The series also includes the Olympic and World Championships marathons in the years they are held for elite scoring purposes.

Scoring and awards

Elite athletes earn points for top-five finishes in series races over a scoring period, with champions crowned in the men's, women's, and wheelchair divisions. Age-group runners can earn a Six Star Medal (later extended for the expanded series) by finishing the original six majors.

History

The series was founded in 2006 by the directors of the Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago, and New York City marathons. The Tokyo Marathon joined in 2013, and Sydney became the first new major added under an expanded evaluation programme in 2025.
