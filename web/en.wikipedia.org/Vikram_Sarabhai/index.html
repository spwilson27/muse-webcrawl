Vikram Sarabhai

Vikram Ambalal Sarabhai (12 August 1919 – 30 December 1971) was an Indian physicist and astronomer who initiated space research in India. Regarded as the father of the Indian space programme, he founded the Physical Research Laboratory in Ahmedabad and played a key role in establishing the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

Life and work

Born into the prominent Sarabhai industrial family in Ahmedabad, he studied at Cambridge and worked on cosmic rays under C. V. Raman. Sarabhai convinced the Indian government of the importance of satellites for a developing nation, leading to the launch of India's first satellite, Aryabhata, in 1975 — after his death.

He also helped found the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad. India's main spaceport-related centre in Thiruvananthapuram, the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre, is named in his honour. He received the Padma Bhushan in 1966 and the Padma Vibhushan posthumously in 1972.
