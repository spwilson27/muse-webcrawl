IWG plc

IWG plc, formerly Regus, is a British holding company. It provides serviced offices under several brand names, including Regus, Spaces, HQ and Signature by Regus. It was founded in Brussels, Belgium, in 1989 by Mark Dixon and is incorporated in Jersey. The company is listed on the London Stock Exchange and is a constituent of the FTSE 250 Index.

History

Mark Dixon founded the business in 1989 with a business centre in Brussels. By the late 1990s the company had expanded rapidly across Europe, the Americas and Asia. In 2000 Regus floated on the London Stock Exchange. The company's US business filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in 2003 following the dot-com downturn, but emerged the following year.

Operations

IWG operates thousands of locations in over 100 countries, providing flexible workspace, virtual offices, meeting rooms and coworking space to clients ranging from individual entrepreneurs to multinational corporations. Its network is generally regarded as the largest of any flexible workspace provider in the world.
