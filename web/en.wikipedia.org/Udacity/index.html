Udacity, Inc. is an American for-profit educational organization founded by Sebastian Thrun, David Stavens, and Mike Sokolsky that offers massive open online courses (MOOCs). It grew out of a Stanford University experiment in which Thrun and Peter Norvig offered the course "Introduction to Artificial Intelligence" free online in 2011, attracting some 160,000 students worldwide.

Business model

Udacity initially offered university-style courses but pivoted to vocational training for technology careers, introducing "Nanodegree" credentials — paid, project-based programmes in subjects such as data science, self-driving cars, artificial intelligence, and programming, often developed with industry partners such as Google, Amazon, and Mercedes-Benz. In 2024, Udacity was acquired by Accenture as part of its LearnVantage training business.
