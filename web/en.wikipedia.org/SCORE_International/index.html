SCORE International

SCORE International (Southern California Off Road Enterprises) is an American off-road racing sanctioning body based in Reno, Nevada. Founded in 1973 by Mickey Thompson, SCORE organises the World Desert Championship, a series of desert races held principally on Mexico's Baja California peninsula, including the legendary Baja 1000.

A trophy truck racing in the Baja 1000, SCORE's flagship event.

Races

The SCORE World Desert Championship traditionally consists of four races held in Baja California: the San Felipe 250 (in San Felipe), the Baja Sur 500, the Baja 500 and the Baja 1000, both historically starting in Ensenada. The Baja 1000, an off-road race of up to roughly 1,000 miles down the peninsula — some editions running point-to-point to La Paz or Cabo San Lucas — is widely considered the most prestigious off-road race in the world.

Vehicles and classes

SCORE races admit a wide range of classes for four-wheel and two-wheel vehicles. The premier class is the Trophy Truck (SCORE Trophy Truck), unlimited purpose-built racing trucks producing over 900 horsepower. Other notable classes include Class 1 (unlimited open-wheel buggies), Class 10, Class 5/1600 and stock classes, as well as motorcycle and ATV (quad) classes. The Volkswagen Beetle-based "Baja Bug" classes are longstanding fixtures of the series.

History

After founding SCORE in 1973, Mickey Thompson staged the first SCORE Baja races and the Riverside International Raceway off-road events. Longtime promoter Sal Fish led the organisation from 1974 until selling it to Roger Norman in 2012. SCORE races have drawn drivers from many disciplines, including IndyCar, NASCAR and rallying.
