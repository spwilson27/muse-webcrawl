List of things named after Donald Trump

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Donald Trump, businessman and 45th and 47th president of the United States, built much of his career on the licensing of his surname as a brand. Numerous buildings, products, organizations, and other things have borne the Trump name—some owned by the Trump Organization, others licensed. A number of properties removed the name after his entry into politics, while other things, including newly discovered species, have been named for him by third parties.

Buildings

  • Trump Tower — Fifth Avenue, New York City; opened 1983, headquarters of the Trump Organization
  • Trump World Tower — Manhattan residential skyscraper
  • Trump International Hotel and Tower — buildings in New York, Chicago, Las Vegas, and (formerly) Washington, D.C. (the Old Post Office building, 2016–2022)
  • Trump Plaza and Trump Taj Mahal — former Atlantic City casinos (demolished/closed)
  • Trump National and Trump International golf clubs — numerous courses in the U.S. and abroad, including Turnberry in Scotland
  • The Trump Building (40 Wall Street) — New York
  • Trump Parc, Trump Palace, Trump Park Avenue, and other New York residential buildings (some later renamed)

Businesses and products

  • Trump Airlines (Trump Shuttle), 1989–1992
  • Trump Vodka (discontinued)
  • Trump Steaks (discontinued)
  • Trump: The Game — board game (1989, relaunched 2004)
  • Trump University — real-estate seminar company (2005–2010), subject of lawsuits settled after the 2016 election
  • Trump Media & Technology Group and Truth Social — founded after his presidency

Other namings

  • Neopalpa donaldtrumpi — a moth species named in 2017, noted for its pale head scales resembling Trump's hair
  • Dermophis donaldtrumpi — a proposed name for a caecilian amphibian, auctioned in 2018
  • Trump Heights (Ramat Trump) — a planned community in the Golan Heights named by Israel in 2019
  • Donald J. Trump State Park — New York state park on land donated by Trump
