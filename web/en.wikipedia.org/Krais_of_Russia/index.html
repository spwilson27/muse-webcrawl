Krais of Russia

The krais of Russia are a type of federal subject of the Russian Federation. A krai (Russian: край, "edge" or "territory") is legally identical in status to an oblast; the distinction is historical, reflecting that krais were traditionally large frontier territories, often encompassing autonomous okrugs or oblasts within them.

List

Russia has nine krais: Altai Krai, Kamchatka Krai, Khabarovsk Krai, Krasnodar Krai, Krasnoyarsk Krai, Perm Krai, Primorsky Krai, Stavropol Krai and Zabaykalsky Krai. Perm Krai (2005), Kamchatka Krai (2007) and Zabaykalsky Krai (2008) were formed by mergers of oblasts with autonomous okrugs following referendums.

The largest, Krasnoyarsk Krai in Siberia, is by far the biggest federal subject after the Sakha Republic. Krasnodar Krai in the North Caucasus is the most populous krai.
