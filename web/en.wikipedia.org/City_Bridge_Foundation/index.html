City Bridge Foundation

City Bridge Foundation is a charitable foundation and bridge trust based in London, England. It is the owner and maintainer of five bridges crossing the River Thames in London: Blackfriars Bridge, Millennium Bridge, Southwark Bridge, Tower Bridge and London Bridge — the five "City bridges". Under its previous name, Bridge House Estates, it accumulated substantial funds over centuries from tolls, rents and bequests associated with old London Bridge.

History

The foundation's origins lie in the medieval Bridge House, established in the 12th and 13th centuries to maintain the old London Bridge. Tolls and rents from properties endowed to the bridge — including shops and houses built on the bridge itself — built up a considerable estate administered by the City of London Corporation.

Charitable grant-making

Surplus income beyond the needs of the bridges is distributed as grants to charitable causes across Greater London through the funding programme formerly known as City Bridge Trust, which became one of London's largest independent grant-makers, supporting work tackling disadvantage and inequality. In 2023 the organisation unified under the single name City Bridge Foundation.

Governance

The City of London Corporation is the sole trustee of the charity, and a committee of its Court of Common Council oversees the foundation's strategy, investments and grant-making.
