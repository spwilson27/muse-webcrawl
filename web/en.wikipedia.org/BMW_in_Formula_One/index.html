BMW in Formula One

BMW has been involved in Formula One in a number of capacities since the inauguration of the World Drivers' Championship in 1950, most notably as an engine supplier and, from 2006 to 2009, as a works team under the name BMW Sauber.

Engine supplier

BMW engines powered Brabham during the turbo era of the 1980s; the BMW M12/13 turbo engine took Nelson Piquet to the 1983 Drivers' Championship. BMW returned with Williams in 2000, winning races and finishing second in the Constructors' Championship in 2002 and 2003.

BMW Sauber

After buying the Sauber team, BMW competed as a constructor from 2006. Its sole victory came at the 2008 Canadian Grand Prix, a one-two finish led by Robert Kubica. BMW withdrew from Formula One at the end of 2009 citing economic and strategic reasons.
