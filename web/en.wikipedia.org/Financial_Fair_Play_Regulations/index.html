Financial Fair Play Regulations

The Financial Fair Play Regulations (FFP) were established by UEFA to prevent professional football clubs spending more than they earn in pursuit of success and, in doing so, getting into financial trouble which could threaten their survival.

Rules

Introduced in 2009 and first applied from the 2011–12 season, the regulations centred on a break-even requirement: over a monitoring period, a club's relevant expenses should not substantially exceed its relevant income, though a limited acceptable deviation funded by owners was allowed. Sanctions for breaches included warnings, fines, points deductions, withholding of prize money and exclusion from UEFA competitions. Similar squad-cost rules exist in several domestic leagues.

Changes

Following the financial impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, UEFA replaced the break-even rules in 2022 with the Financial Sustainability Regulations, including a squad cost rule limiting spending on wages, transfers and agents' fees to a percentage of revenue.
