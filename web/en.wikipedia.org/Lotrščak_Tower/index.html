Lotrščak Tower

Lotrščak Tower (Croatian: Kula Lotrščak) is a fortified tower in the Upper Town (Gradec) of Zagreb, Croatia. Built in the 13th century to guard the southern gate of the town walls, it is one of the city's best-known landmarks.

Its name derives from the Latin campana latrunculorum, "thieves' bell", which rang each evening before the gates closed. In the 19th century a fourth storey and windows were added, and a cannon — the Grič cannon — was installed; since 1 January 1877 a shot has been fired from the tower every day at noon, a tradition maintained today. Visitors can climb the tower for panoramic views over Zagreb.
