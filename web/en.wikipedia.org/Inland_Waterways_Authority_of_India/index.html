Inland Waterways Authority of India

The Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI) is a statutory body of the Government of India under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, responsible for the development and regulation of inland waterways for shipping and navigation. It came into existence in October 1986 under the IWAI Act, 1985, and is headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh.

IWAI develops India's national waterways, including NW-1 on the Ganga.

Functions

IWAI develops and maintains infrastructure on national waterways — fairways, navigation channels, terminals, jetties and navigational aids — and advises central and state governments on inland water transport. India's navigable waterways total about 20,236 km of rivers, canals, backwaters and creeks, of which about 17,980 km of river and 2,256 km of canal can be used by mechanized craft.

National Waterways

Major waterways include National Waterway 1 (the Ganga–Bhagirathi–Hooghly system between Prayagraj and Haldia, 1,620 km, declared in 1986), NW-2 on the Brahmaputra in Assam, and NW-3 on the West Coast Canal in Kerala. The National Waterways Act, 2016 declared 106 additional waterways, bringing the total to 111. The World Bank–assisted Jal Marg Vikas Project aims to augment navigation capacity on NW-1 with multimodal terminals at Varanasi, Sahibganj and Haldia.
