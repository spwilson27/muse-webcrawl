Wuchang Uprising

The Wuchang Uprising was an armed revolt on 10 October 1911 in Wuchang (now part of Wuhan), China, that triggered the Xinhai Revolution and led to the fall of the Qing dynasty and the founding of the Republic of China.

Events

Revolutionary societies, the Literary Society and the Progressive Association, had infiltrated the New Army in Hubei. After a bomb accidentally exploded in the Russian concession in Hankou on 9 October, exposing the plot, mutinous soldiers rose the next evening, seized the arsenal and the governor-general's offices, and forced commander Li Yuanhong to head a revolutionary military government.

Consequences

Province after province declared independence from the Qing. Sun Yat-sen returned from exile and was elected provisional president of the new republic, and the last emperor, Puyi, abdicated in February 1912. The date, Double Ten Day, is the national day of the Republic of China (Taiwan).
