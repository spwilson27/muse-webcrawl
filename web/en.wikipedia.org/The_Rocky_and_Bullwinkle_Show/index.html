The Rocky and Bullwinkle Show

The Rocky and Bullwinkle Show is the collective name for two American animated television series produced by Jay Ward Productions: Rocky and His Friends (1959–1961) and The Bullwinkle Show (1961–1964). The series stars Rocket "Rocky" J. Squirrel and Bullwinkle J. Moose, residents of Frostbite Falls, Minnesota, who are perpetually targeted by the Pottsylvanian spies Boris Badenov and Natasha Fatale.

Segments and style

Episodes combined serialised Rocky and Bullwinkle adventures with supporting features: "Fractured Fairy Tales" (narrated by Edward Everett Horton), "Peabody's Improbable History" with the time-travelling dog Mr. Peabody and his boy Sherman, "Dudley Do-Right of the Mounties", and "Aesop & Son". Despite limited animation, the writing was famously sophisticated, laced with puns, satire, and Cold War jokes aimed as much at adults as children.

Legacy

The show is celebrated as one of the most influential animated series ever made, paving the way for later prime-time and adult-oriented animation. Its characters returned in the 2000 live-action/animation film The Adventures of Rocky and Bullwinkle and a 2018 streaming reboot.
