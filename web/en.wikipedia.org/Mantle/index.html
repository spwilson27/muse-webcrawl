Mantle

Mantle may refer to:

  • Mantle (geology), the layer of a planet between its crust and core
  • Mantle (mollusc), the body wall of a mollusc that secretes the shell
  • Mantle (clothing), a loose sleeveless cloak
  • Gas mantle, the incandescent mesh used in gas lamps
  • Mantling, in heraldry, the drapery depicted around a shield
  • Mantle (API), a graphics API developed by AMD

For the geological sense, see Mantle (geology).
