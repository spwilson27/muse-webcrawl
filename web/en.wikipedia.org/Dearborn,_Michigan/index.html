Dearborn, Michigan

The Henry Ford museum complex in Dearborn

Dearborn is a city in Wayne County in the U.S. state of Michigan. At the 2020 census it had a population of about 110,000, making it one of the largest cities in Michigan and a major suburb of Detroit, with which it shares a border. (Note: the city is ordinarily spelled "Dearborn"; references to "Dearborn, Michigan" occur as a common variant rendering.)

History

Dearborn was settled in the late 18th century and named for Henry Dearborn, a Revolutionary War general and Secretary of War. It is best known as the hometown of Henry Ford and the site of the Ford Motor Company world headquarters, the River Rouge complex, and The Henry Ford museum and Greenfield Village.

Demographics

Dearborn has one of the largest Arab American communities in the United States, and its cultural institutions include the Arab American National Museum.

Economy

Ford Motor Company remains the city's largest employer; the University of Michigan–Dearborn and Henry Ford College are also located in the city.
