Austin, Texas

Austin is the capital city of the U.S. state of Texas and the seat of Travis County. Located in Central Texas on the Colorado River, it is one of the fastest-growing large cities in the United States, with a population of roughly one million in the city proper and over two million in its metropolitan area. It is nicknamed the "Live Music Capital of the World" and is known for the slogan "Keep Austin Weird".

History

The city was founded in 1839 as Waterloo and renamed for Stephen F. Austin, a founder of the Republic of Texas, whose government it was chosen to host. It became the state capital permanently after an 1872 election. The University of Texas at Austin, founded in 1883, grew into one of the largest public universities in the country.

Economy and culture

Austin developed a major technology sector — sometimes called "Silicon Hills" — with employers including Dell (founded in the city in 1984), IBM, Apple, Google, Meta and Tesla, which moved its headquarters there in 2021. The city hosts the South by Southwest (SXSW) festival, Austin City Limits, and the Formula One United States Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas. Congress Avenue Bridge shelters the largest urban bat colony in North America, a popular local attraction.
