Legal Services Board

The Legal Services Board (LSB) is the independent oversight regulator for legal services in England and Wales, created by the Legal Services Act 2007 and fully operational from 2010.

Functions

The Board oversees the approved regulators — such as the Solicitors Regulation Authority and the Bar Standards Board — promotes the regulatory objectives of the 2007 Act, including the rule of law, access to justice, consumer interests and competition, and oversees the Office for Legal Complaints, which runs the Legal Ombudsman.
