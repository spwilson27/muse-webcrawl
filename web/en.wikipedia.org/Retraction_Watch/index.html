Retraction Watch

Retraction Watch is a blog that reports on retractions of scientific papers and related topics in research integrity. It was founded in August 2010 by science writers Ivan Oransky and Adam Marcus.

Founded2010
FoundersIvan Oransky, Adam Marcus
ParentCenter for Scientific Integrity

Work

The site covers retractions, corrections and expressions of concern across all fields, investigating the reasons behind them — error, duplication, plagiarism or fraud — and highlighting weaknesses in peer review and publishing. Its founders argued that retractions were becoming more common but were poorly publicised, and that tracking them could improve the transparency of science.

Retraction database

Retraction Watch maintains a freely searchable database of tens of thousands of retractions, widely used by researchers, journals and institutions to study patterns in scientific misconduct and error. The project is run by the non-profit Center for Scientific Integrity.
