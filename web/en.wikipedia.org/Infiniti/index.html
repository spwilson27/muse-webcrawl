Infiniti is the luxury vehicle division of the Japanese automaker Nissan. It began selling vehicles in North America on 8 November 1989 and has since competed with brands such as Lexus, Acura, BMW and Mercedes-Benz, primarily in the United States, China, and the Middle East.

History

Nissan created Infiniti under a secret "Horizon" task force, launching with the Q45 flagship sedan and M30 coupe. Positioning emphasised performance and customer service; early advertising famously showed natural scenery without cars. The G35 coupe and sedan of the 2000s were critical and commercial successes that defined the brand's sporty image.

Models and naming

In 2013 Infiniti adopted a naming scheme in which cars are designated Q and SUVs QX followed by numbers denoting hierarchy — e.g. Q50 sedan and QX80 flagship SUV. In the 2020s the lineup narrowed to SUV-heavy offerings such as the QX50, QX55 and QX80 as sedan sales declined, and the brand announced a shift toward electrified models.

Motorsport and technology

Infiniti sponsored Red Bull Racing in Formula One during its championship years (2011–2015) and supported technical projects in hybrid performance. Its models have showcased technologies including steer-by-wire (Direct Adaptive Steering) and variable-compression turbo engines (VC-Turbo).
