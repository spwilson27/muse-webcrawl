Derby della Capitale

LocationRome, Italy
TeamsLazio and Roma
First meeting8 December 1929
StadiumStadio Olimpico

The Derby della Capitale (Derby of the Capital), also known as the Derby di Roma, is the local derby football match in Rome, Italy, between S.S. Lazio and A.S. Roma. It is regarded as one of the fiercest rivalries in Italian and European football, with both clubs sharing the Stadio Olimpico.

History

The rivalry began in 1927, when Roma was formed from a merger of three Roman clubs championed by the Fascist regime; Lazio, founded in 1900, was the only major Roman club to resist joining, owing largely to the influence of general Giorgio Vaccaro. The first derby was played in 1929, with Roma winning. The fixture has been marked by passionate choreography from the ultras of both clubs and, at times, by violence and controversial incidents.

Culture

Traditionally, Roma's support was associated with the city centre and working-class districts such as Testaccio, while Lazio's was associated with the more affluent northern districts, though these distinctions have blurred. The Curva Sud (Roma) and Curva Nord (Lazio) produce elaborate tifos for derby matches.
