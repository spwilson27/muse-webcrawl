Palak paneer (Hindi: पालक पनीर) is a vegetarian dish from the Indian subcontinent consisting of paneer, a fresh unsalted cheese, simmered in a thick purée of spinach (palak) seasoned with ginger, garlic, tomato, garam masala, turmeric, and other spices. It is one of the most popular dishes in North Indian and Punjabi restaurant cooking and is eaten with roti, naan, paratha, or rice.

Preparation

Spinach leaves are blanched and puréed, sometimes with green chillies. A base of onion, tomato, ginger, and garlic is fried in oil or ghee with whole and ground spices; the spinach purée is added and cooked briefly to keep its green colour. Cubes of paneer — often lightly fried first — are folded in at the end, and the dish is commonly finished with a swirl of cream. Variations include palak paneer made with methi (fenugreek) additions or a drier saag-style preparation using mixed greens.

Related dishes

Palak paneer is closely related to saag paneer, in which the greens may include mustard leaves and other leafy vegetables, and to dishes such as palak aloo (spinach and potato) and palak dal. Outside India, it is a staple of Indian restaurants worldwide and a common ready-meal and frozen product.
