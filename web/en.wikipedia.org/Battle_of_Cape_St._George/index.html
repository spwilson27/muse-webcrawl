Battle of Cape St. George

Date25 November 1943
LocationOff Cape St. George, New Ireland, Solomon Sea
ResultAmerican victory
BelligerentsUnited States Navy vs. Imperial Japanese Navy
CommandersCapt. Arleigh Burke vs. Capt. Kiyoto Kagawa

The Battle of Cape St. George was a naval battle of the Pacific War fought on 25 November 1943 between Cape St. George, New Ireland, and Buka Island in what is now Papua New Guinea. Five American destroyers under Captain Arleigh Burke intercepted five Japanese destroyers returning from a troop-transport run to Buka, sinking three of them without suffering any damage, in what is often described as a textbook destroyer action and the last surface engagement of the Solomon Islands campaign.

Background

Following the Allied landings at Cape Torokina on Bougainville, the Japanese continued to reinforce their garrison at Buka by the destroyer transport runs known to the Allies as the "Tokyo Express". On the night of 24–25 November, Captain Kiyoto Kagawa led the destroyers Ōnami, Makinami, Amagiri, Yūgiri and Uzuki to land troops and supplies and evacuate personnel. Burke's Destroyer Squadron 23 — the "Little Beavers", comprising Charles Ausburne, Claxton, Dyson, Converse and Spence — was sent to intercept.

Battle

Shortly after midnight, Burke's radar picked up the Japanese force. Burke's lead division launched a surprise torpedo attack that sank Ōnami with all hands, including Kagawa, and crippled Makinami, which was finished off by gunfire. In the ensuing chase toward Cape St. George, Yūgiri was also sunk. Japanese losses were over 600 sailors; the Americans rescued some survivors in the morning but took no casualties and suffered no damage to their ships.

Significance

The victory demonstrated how far US Navy destroyer tactics, radar doctrine and torpedo reliability had improved since the disasters of 1942. Burke's aggressive night-fighting methods, summarized in his practice of attacking at high speed, became famous, and he later served as Chief of Naval Operations. The battle effectively closed the Solomon Islands naval campaign.
