Province of Treviso

Country: Italy
Region: Veneto
Capital: Treviso
Area: about 2,477 km²
Population: about 880,000

The province of Treviso (Italian: provincia di Treviso) is a province in the Veneto region of north-eastern Italy. Its capital is the city of Treviso. The province stretches from the Venetian plain in the south to the foothills of the Dolomites in the north.

Geography

The territory is crossed by the Piave and Sile rivers. The northern part of the province is hilly and is known for the production of Prosecco wine around Conegliano and Valdobbiadene, an area recognised by UNESCO as a World Heritage Site in 2019. The Montello hill and the Querini oak woods are other notable features.

Economy

The province has a strong industrial base of small and medium-sized firms, particularly in footwear, furniture, textiles, and machinery; the sportswear district of Montebelluna is world-famous for ski boots and sports shoes. Treviso is the home city of Benetton, De'Longhi, and other major companies. The province is also the birthplace of tiramisu and a major producer of radicchio rosso.

Municipalities

The province contains 94 municipalities. The largest include Treviso, Conegliano, Castelfranco Veneto, Mogliano Veneto, Montebelluna, and Vittorio Veneto.
