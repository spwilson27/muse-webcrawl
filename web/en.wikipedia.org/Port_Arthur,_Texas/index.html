Port Arthur is a city in Jefferson County, Texas, United States, on the western shore of Sabine Lake near the Gulf of Mexico. Founded by railroad magnate Arthur Edward Stilwell in the 1890s as the terminus of the Kansas City Southern Railway, it was named for its founder's first name.

Economy

The Spindletop oil boom of 1901 transformed Port Arthur into a major refining center. It hosts some of the largest petroleum refineries in the United States, including the Motiva Port Arthur Refinery, the largest in the country. The Port of Port Arthur is one of the nation's principal seaports for petroleum and forest products.

History and culture

The city was incorporated in 1898. Notable natives include singer Janis Joplin and painter Robert Rauschenberg. Port Arthur suffered severe flooding from Hurricane Harvey in 2017, one of the costliest natural disasters in American history.
