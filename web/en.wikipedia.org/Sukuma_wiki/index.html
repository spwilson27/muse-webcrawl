Sukuma wiki

Sukuma wiki is a leafy green vegetable dish from East Africa, made from collard greens (a form of Brassica oleracea) sautéed with onions and tomatoes. Its name is Swahili: sukuma means "to push or stretch" and wiki means "week" — the dish is said to "stretch the week", helping a family make limited food supplies last.

Preparation

The greens are washed, destemmed, and finely shredded, then cooked in oil with chopped onions, tomatoes, and salt, sometimes with chilli or a squeeze of lemon. The result is a simple, inexpensive accompaniment served with staple starches such as ugali, rice, or chapati.

Cultural significance

Sukuma wiki is a daily staple across Kenya and neighbouring countries including Tanzania and Uganda, valued because collard greens grow readily, tolerate poor soils, and provide iron, calcium, and vitamins A and C in regions where meat is eaten infrequently.
