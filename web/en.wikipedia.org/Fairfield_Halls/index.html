Fairfield Halls

LocationCroydon, London, England
Opened2 November 1962
CapacityConcert Hall: 1,800

Fairfield Halls is an arts, entertainment and conference centre in Croydon, south London, England. It opened on 2 November 1962 and contains a concert hall, the Ashcroft Playhouse theatre, the Arnhem Gallery, and additional studio and community spaces.

History

The halls stand on the site of the former Fairfield, a fairground used since the 19th century. The concert hall was noted for its acoustics and hosted performances by major orchestras, rock and pop acts, and comedians. The venue closed in July 2016 for a major refurbishment and reopened in September 2019.

Venues

The Concert Hall seats about 1,800 and is home to the London Mozart Players as orchestra in residence. The Ashcroft Playhouse, named after Croydon-born actress Peggy Ashcroft, reopened as the Ashcroft Theatre. The building is a prominent local landmark and part of Croydon's cultural quarter.
