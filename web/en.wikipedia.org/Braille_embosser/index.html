Braille embosser

A Braille embosser is a printer that renders text as tactile Braille cells, embossing raised dots onto heavy paper so that blind and visually impaired people can read documents produced from a computer. Text is first converted by Braille translation software, which contracts words according to the rules of the Braille code in use.

Embossers range from portable personal models to high-speed production machines used by Braille publishing houses and libraries to produce books, textbooks, bank statements, and signage. Impact embossers strike the paper with pins or hammers; some models can also produce tactile graphics.

Together with refreshable Braille displays, embossers are a principal means of computer output in Braille.
