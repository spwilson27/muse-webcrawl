Business plan

A business plan is a formal written document setting out a business's goals, the methods for attaining them, and the time frame for their achievement. It typically describes the organisation, its products or services, market analysis, marketing and sales strategy, operations, management team, and financial projections.

Purposes

Business plans serve external purposes — attracting investors, securing loans, and recruiting partners — and internal purposes, such as aligning a team around a strategy and measuring performance against targets. Lenders and venture capitalists traditionally require a plan before committing funds.

Contents and formats

A standard plan includes an executive summary, company description, market and competitor analysis, organisation and management structure, product line, marketing plan, funding request, and financial statements with forecasts. Formats range from brief "lean" plans and pitch decks to comprehensive documents of dozens of pages. Critics in the lean startup tradition argue that elaborate plans can be poor predictors of success for novel ventures, favouring rapid experimentation instead; even so, planning remains a standard expectation in most financing contexts.
