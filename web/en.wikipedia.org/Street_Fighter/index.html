Street Fighter is a Japanese media franchise centred on a series of fighting video and arcade games developed and published by Capcom. Players compete in one-on-one combat using a roster of martial artists from around the world, each with distinctive special moves.

History

The original Street Fighter appeared in arcades in 1987, but the series became a phenomenon with Street Fighter II (1991), which popularised competitive head-to-head fighting games and features such as combos and selectable characters including Ryu, Ken, Chun-Li and Guile. Successive entries — the Alpha, III, IV, V and 6 instalments — have kept the series central to esports and the fighting game community, and it has spawned films, comics and animation.
