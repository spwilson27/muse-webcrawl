DVB-H

DVB-H (Digital Video Broadcasting – Handheld) is one of three prevalent mobile TV formats. It is a technical specification for bringing broadcast services to battery-powered handheld devices, standardised by ETSI in 2004 (EN 302 304). DVB-H technology is a superset of the terrestrial DVB-T system, with additional features to meet the requirements of handheld, battery-powered receivers.

Technology

DVB-H uses time slicing, transmitting data in bursts so the receiver can switch off between bursts and save power by up to 90 per cent, and multiprotocol encapsulation forward error correction (MPE-FEC) to improve robustness in mobile reception conditions. Services are carried as IP datacasts.

Deployment

Trial and commercial services launched in countries including Finland, Italy, and Croatia in the mid-2000s, and the European Union endorsed DVB-H as its preferred mobile broadcasting standard in 2008. However, the market ultimately favoured streaming over mobile broadband networks, and most DVB-H services were shut down by the early 2010s.
