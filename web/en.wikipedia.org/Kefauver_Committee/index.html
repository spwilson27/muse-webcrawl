Kefauver Committee

The Kefauver Committee, formally the United States Senate Special Committee to Investigate Crime in Interstate Commerce (1950–1951), was chaired by Senator Estes Kefauver of Tennessee. It investigated organized crime across the United States.

Its televised hearings — among the first Senate proceedings widely broadcast — exposed Mafia figures including Frank Costello, whose nervous hands became famous footage, and made Kefauver a national figure. The committee concluded that a nationwide crime syndicate, the Mafia, operated in America, recommended new anti-racketeering legislation, and spurred gambling tax and wire-service laws.
