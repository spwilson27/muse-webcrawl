Jacob (Lost)

Jacob is a fictional character in the American television series Lost, played by Mark Pellegrino. He is the mysterious, ageless guardian of the Island, whose conflict with his brother, the Man in Black, forms the mythological core of the show's later seasons.

Portrayed byMark Pellegrino
First appearance"The Incident" (2009)
SeriesLost (ABC)

Character biography

Born on the Island in ancient times and granted agelessness by its protector, Jacob spent centuries bringing people to the Island to prove his brother wrong about human nature, and to find a successor among "candidates" whose names he wrote in a cave and a lighthouse. His touch conferred longevity on Richard Alpert, and his unseen influence guided the survivors of Oceanic Flight 815.

Death and succession

In the season five finale, Jacob is murdered by Ben Linus at the Man in Black's instigation; his ghostly appearances in the final season guide the surviving candidates until Jack Shephard succeeds him as protector.
