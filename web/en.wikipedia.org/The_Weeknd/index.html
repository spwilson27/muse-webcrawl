Abel Makkonen Tesfaye (born February 16, 1990), known professionally as the Weeknd, is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and record producer. Known for his sonic versatility and dark lyricism, his music explores escapism, romance, and melancholia, and is often inspired by personal experiences. He has received numerous accolades, including five Grammy Awards, and is among the world's best-selling music artists, with over 75 million records sold.

Early life

Tesfaye was born in Toronto to Ethiopian immigrant parents and raised in Scarborough. He began uploading songs anonymously to YouTube in 2010 under the name "The Weeknd" — the spelling altered to avoid trademark issues with the Canadian band the Weekend. In 2011 he released three critically acclaimed mixtapes, House of Balloons, Thursday, and Echoes of Silence, which were later compiled on Trilogy (2012) following his signing with Republic Records and the founding of his own imprint, XO.

Career

His major-label debut Kiss Land (2013) was followed by Beauty Behind the Madness (2015), which produced the hits "Can't Feel My Face" and "The Hills" and won the Grammy for Best Urban Contemporary Album. Starboy (2016) continued his commercial success, led by the Daft Punk collaborations "Starboy" and "I Feel It Coming".

His fourth album, After Hours (2020), contained "Blinding Lights", which became the most-streamed song in Spotify history and one of the best-selling singles of all time, alongside "Save Your Tears" and "Heartless". In 2021 he headlined the Super Bowl LV halftime show. His fifth album, Dawn FM (2022), was a concept record framed as a purgatorial radio station. Tesfaye has also acted, co-creating and starring in the HBO series The Idol (2023).

Artistry and influence

Tesfaye's music began in alternative R&B, characterized by moody production, falsetto vocals, and lyrical themes of nightlife and excess, before expanding into synth-pop, new wave, and mainstream pop. He has cited Michael Jackson, Prince, and R. Kelly among his early influences, and his work is credited with helping to popularize alternative R&B in the 2010s.

Philanthropy

He serves as a Goodwill Ambassador for the United Nations World Food Programme and has donated millions to causes including Covid-19 relief, racial justice organizations in the United States, and humanitarian aid in Ethiopia.
