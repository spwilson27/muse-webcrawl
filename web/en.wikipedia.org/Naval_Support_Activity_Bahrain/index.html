Naval Support Activity Bahrain
LocationManama, Bahrain
OperatorUnited States Navy

Naval Support Activity Bahrain (NSA Bahrain) is a United States Navy base in Manama, Bahrain. It is the home of U.S. Naval Forces Central Command and the U.S. Fifth Fleet, and provides support to American and coalition naval forces operating in the Persian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Red Sea and Arabian Sea.

History

The U.S. Navy has maintained a presence in Bahrain since the late 1940s, using facilities of the British Royal Navy at HMS Jufair. The shore establishment expanded after the 1970s, and NSA Bahrain was formally established as the installation grew into the headquarters for naval operations in the region.

Role

The base supports forward-deployed ships, personnel and visiting units, and hosts the headquarters responsible for maritime security operations across the Middle East.
