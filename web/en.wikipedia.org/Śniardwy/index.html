Śniardwy is the largest lake in Poland, located in the Masurian Lake District in the Warmian-Masurian Voivodeship. It covers an area of about 113.8 square kilometres, measuring 22.1 kilometres in length and 13.4 kilometres in width.

Geography

The lake is a post-glacial ribbon-and-basin lake dotted with islands, eight of which are named, including Szeroki Ostrów and Czarci Ostrów. It is connected by channels to neighbouring lakes such as Łuknajno — a UNESCO biosphere reserve noted for its mute swan population — and Lake Mikołajskie.

Tourism

Śniardwy is a major centre for sailing and water tourism in Masuria, with marinas in towns such as Mikołajki and Pisz on its shores.
