Upper motor neuron

Upper motor neurons are motor neurons originating in the cerebral cortex or brainstem that carry signals to lower motor neurons in the spinal cord and cranial nerve nuclei. They control voluntary movement without directly innervating muscle.

Pathways

The main pathways are the corticospinal tract for body movement and the corticobulbar tract for face and head movement.

Lesions

Upper motor neuron lesions cause weakness with spasticity, hyperreflexia, clonus and a positive Babinski sign, without marked muscle atrophy or fasciculations.

Diseases

Conditions affecting upper motor neurons include stroke, multiple sclerosis, primary lateral sclerosis and hereditary spastic paraplegia.
