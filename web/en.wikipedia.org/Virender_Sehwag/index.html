Virender Sehwag

Virender Sehwag (born 20 October 1978) is a former Indian cricketer, widely regarded as one of the most destructive opening batsmen of all time. He played for India from 1999 to 2013, and for Delhi and Haryana in domestic cricket.

Career

Sehwag holds multiple records: he is one of only four batsmen to score two triple centuries in Test cricket (319 against South Africa in 2008, the fastest triple century in Test history, and 309 against Pakistan in 2004), and he scored a double century in ODIs (219 against West Indies, 2011). His Test strike rate of 82.23 is among the highest ever.

He was part of India's victorious 2007 T20 World Cup and 2011 Cricket World Cup squads. Known by nicknames "Viru" and the "Nawab of Najafgarh", after retirement he became a commentator and cricket administrator.
