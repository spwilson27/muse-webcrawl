Brooklyn (film)

Theatrical poster for Brooklyn

Brooklyn is a 2015 romantic period drama film directed by John Crowley and written by Nick Hornby, adapted from Colm Tóibín's 2009 novel of the same name. It stars Saoirse Ronan as Eilis Lacey, a young Irish immigrant in 1950s Brooklyn, with Emory Cohen, Domhnall Gleeson, Jim Broadbent, and Julie Walters in supporting roles. A British-Irish-Canadian co-production, it premiered at the 2015 Sundance Film Festival.

Plot

In 1951, Eilis Lacey leaves Enniscorthy, County Wexford, where prospects are few, for a job in a Brooklyn department store arranged by Father Flood. Homesick at first, she settles, studies bookkeeping at night, and falls in love with Tony Fiorello, an Italian-American plumber, whom she secretly marries. When her sister Rose dies suddenly, Eilis returns to Ireland, where a promising job and the courtship of Jim Farrell tempt her to stay — until a reminder of small-town scrutiny forces her choice, and she returns to Tony in Brooklyn.

Reception

The film was acclaimed for Ronan's performance and its restrained, luminous storytelling, grossing over $62 million on an $11 million budget. It received three Academy Award nominations — Best Picture, Best Actress for Ronan, and Best Adapted Screenplay — and won the BAFTA Award for Outstanding British Film.
