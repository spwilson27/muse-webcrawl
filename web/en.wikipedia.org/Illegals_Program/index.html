The Illegals Program was a network of Russian sleeper agents operating in the United States under non-official cover, run by Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR). The network was broken up by the FBI in June 2010, in an investigation codenamed Operation Ghost Stories.

Investigation and arrests

The FBI had monitored members of the network for years. On 27 June 2010, ten individuals were arrested in the United States on charges of acting as unregistered agents of a foreign government and conspiracy to commit money laundering. The agents had assumed false identities, formed couples, and raised families in the United States while passing information to Moscow, often using steganography and shortwave radio transmissions.

Prisoner exchange

In July 2010 the arrested agents pleaded guilty and were exchanged at Vienna International Airport for four prisoners held in Russia, in what was described as the largest US–Russian spy swap since the Cold War.
