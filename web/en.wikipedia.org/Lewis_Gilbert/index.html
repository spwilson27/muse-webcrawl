Lewis Gilbert

Lewis Gilbert (6 March 1920 – 23 February 2018) was a British film director, producer and screenwriter, best known for directing three James Bond films — You Only Live Twice (1967), The Spy Who Loved Me (1977) and Moonraker (1979) — and the acclaimed comedy-drama Alfie (1966).

Career

Gilbert began as a child actor and wartime documentary filmmaker before directing war films such as Reach for the Sky (1956) and Carve Her Name with Pride (1958). Alfie, starring Michael Caine, was nominated for five Academy Awards.

Later work

His collaborations with playwright Willy Russell produced Educating Rita (1983) and Shirley Valentine (1989), both major successes. Gilbert was appointed CBE in 2001 and directed his final film, Before You Go, in 2002.
