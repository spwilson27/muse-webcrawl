Roman Polanski (born Roman Raymond Thierry Polański, 18 August 1933) is a Polish and French film director, producer, screenwriter, and actor. He is widely considered one of the greatest directors of all time, known for films such as Knife in the Water (1962), Repulsion (1965), Rosemary's Baby (1968), Chinatown (1974), Tess (1979), The Pianist (2002), and An Officer and a Spy (2019).

Early life

Polanski was born in Paris to Polish-Jewish parents and moved with his family to Kraków in 1936. During the Second World War, his parents were sent to concentration camps: his mother died at Auschwitz, and his father survived Mauthausen. Polanski escaped the Kraków ghetto and survived by hiding with Polish Catholic families in the countryside. After the war he studied film at the National Film School in Łódź, where his short Two Men and a Wardrobe (1958) won international prizes.

Career

His feature debut, Knife in the Water, was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film and brought him to the West. In Britain he made Repulsion and Cul-de-sac; in Hollywood, Rosemary's Baby was a major critical and commercial success. In 1969, his pregnant wife, actress Sharon Tate, was murdered by members of the Manson Family at their Los Angeles home. Polanski returned to filmmaking with Macbeth (1971) and the acclaimed neo-noir Chinatown. His Holocaust drama The Pianist won the Palme d'Or at Cannes and three Academy Awards, including Best Director.

Sexual abuse case and exile

In 1977, Polanski was arrested in Los Angeles and charged with drugging and raping a 13-year-old girl. He pleaded guilty to unlawful sexual intercourse with a minor but fled the United States in 1978 before sentencing, fearing the judge would impose a prison term beyond the plea agreement. He has lived in Europe ever since, principally in France, whose citizenship protects him from extradition; he was arrested in Switzerland in 2009 but released after the Swiss government declined a U.S. extradition request. The case has remained a lasting source of controversy, and Polanski was expelled from the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences in 2018.
