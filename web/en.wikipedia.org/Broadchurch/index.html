Broadchurch is a British crime drama television series created by Chris Chibnall and broadcast on ITV. Set in a fictional small coastal town in Dorset, the first series (2013) follows detectives Alec Hardy (David Tennant) and Ellie Miller (Olivia Colman) investigating the murder of an eleven-year-old boy, Danny Latimer, and the effect on the community.

Reception and legacy

The first series was a major critical and ratings success and sparked national speculation about the killer's identity; filming locations around West Bay, Dorset, saw a surge in tourism. Second and third series aired in 2015 and 2017, and a US remake, Gracepoint, was broadcast in 2014.
