The Evergreen State College is a public liberal arts college in Olympia, Washington. Founded in 1967, it is known for its experimental, interdisciplinary curriculum and its rejection of conventional grades and required courses.

Academics

Instead of taking multiple separate courses, full-time students enrol in a single interdisciplinary "program" each quarter, taught by a team of faculty and organised around a theme. Faculty write detailed narrative evaluations of student work rather than assigning letter grades. The college offers Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Science degrees and graduate programs including a Master of Environmental Studies.

Campus and culture

The 1,000-acre campus sits in forest on the southern shore of Puget Sound and includes a beach and an organic farm. Evergreen is famous for its mascot, Speedy the Geoduck, and alumni including cartoonist Matt Groening, musician Kathleen Hanna, and rapper Macklemore.
