Farmington, New Mexico

Farmington is a city in San Juan County in northwestern New Mexico, United States. Its population was about 46,000 at the 2020 census. It lies at the confluence of the San Juan, Animas, and La Plata rivers, in the Four Corners region.

The area was settled by Ancestral Puebloans and later by Hispanic and Anglo settlers; farming arrived in the late 19th century, giving the town its name. Farmington grew with oil and natural gas development in the San Juan Basin in the 1950s and serves as a commercial hub for the surrounding region, including the nearby Navajo Nation.
