Nadir

Nadir
FieldAstronomy; celestial navigation
DefinitionPoint on the celestial sphere directly below an observer
Opposite pointZenith
EtymologyArabic naẓīr, "opposite"

The nadir is the direction pointing directly downward from a particular location — that is, the direction of gravity as defined by a plumb line at that point. In astronomy, it is the point on the celestial sphere directly beneath an observer, exactly opposite the zenith, the point directly overhead. The word derives from Arabic naẓīr as-samt, "opposite the zenith", transmitted through medieval Latin.

Uses

The nadir–zenith axis is fundamental to the horizontal coordinate system used in observational astronomy and surveying. In Earth observation, "nadir" describes the downward viewing direction of a satellite or aircraft sensor: images taken at nadir look straight down at the sub-satellite point, minimising geometric distortion, while "off-nadir" viewing images terrain at an angle. Clinicians and pharmacologists also borrow the term figuratively, as in the "nadir" of a blood cell count after chemotherapy, meaning its lowest point.

Figurative meaning

By extension, nadir commonly denotes the lowest point of anything — of a career, a market, or a person's fortunes — as the mirror image of "zenith" for the highest point.
