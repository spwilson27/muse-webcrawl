Color guard (flag spinning)

Color guard is a team of performers who perform choreographed dances and routines with equipment such as flags, sabres and mock rifles, providing additional visual interpretation of the music performed by a marching band, drum corps or indoor ensemble. The activity takes its name from the military colour guard, the detachment charged with carrying and guarding a unit's colours (flags).

Performance

During the marching season, the color guard performs outdoors with the marching band, spinning flags and other equipment in time with the music to enhance the show's visual effect. In the winter months, independent guards and school groups compete indoors as "winter guard", performing to recorded music on a gymnasium floor, governed internationally by Winter Guard International (WGI).

Equipment

The standard flag is a length of silk or synthetic fabric mounted on a metal or fibreglass pole, commonly about five to six feet long. Skilled performers also spin wooden or metal mock rifles and sabres, and may incorporate dance, theatre and props. Judging criteria include technique, synchronisation, difficulty, and general effect.
