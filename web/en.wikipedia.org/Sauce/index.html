Sauce

In cooking, a sauce is a liquid, cream, or semi-solid food served on or used in preparing other foods. Sauces add flavour, moisture, colour, and texture to dishes, and are rarely eaten by themselves.

French mother sauces

Classical French cuisine organises sauces around five "mother sauces" codified by Auguste Escoffier: béchamel, velouté, espagnole, hollandaise, and tomato. From these bases, hundreds of derivative "small sauces" are made by adding herbs, stocks, wines, and other ingredients.

World sauces

Examples from other traditions include soy sauce and hoisin in East Asia, mole in Mexico, curry sauces in South Asia, salsas in Latin America, and gravy in Britain and North America. Ketchup, mayonnaise, and mustard are among the most common condiment sauces worldwide.
