Bugatti Circuit

The Bugatti Circuit is a permanent race track located within the Circuit des 24 Heures in Le Mans, France. Built in 1965 and named for Ettore Bugatti, it uses part of the famous 24 Hours course, including the pits and the Dunlop Bridge section.

The circuit is about 4.19 km (2.60 mi) long. It hosts the French motorcycle Grand Prix, rounds of the MotoGP World Championship, the Le Mans 24 Hours for motorcycles, and rounds of the FIA European Truck Racing Championship.

The full Circuit de la Sarthe, of which the Bugatti Circuit forms a part, hosts the 24 Hours of Le Mans endurance race, first held in 1923.
