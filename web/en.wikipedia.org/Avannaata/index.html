Avannaata (Greenlandic for "Northern") is a municipality of Greenland created on 1 January 2018 from the northern part of the former Qaasuitsup municipality. Its administrative seat is Ilulissat, Greenland's third-largest town and the site of the Ilulissat Icefjord, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Geography

Avannaata is one of the largest municipalities in the world by area, spanning the northwest coast of Greenland from Disko Bay north to Qaanaaq, one of the world's northernmost towns. It includes the towns of Ilulissat, Uummannaq, Upernavik, and Qaanaaq, along with numerous settlements. The Northeast Greenland National Park is not part of any municipality, but Thule Air Base (Pituffik) lies within Avannaata's boundaries.

Economy

The economy is based largely on fishing (halibut and shrimp), hunting, and growing tourism centred on the Ilulissat icefjord and calving Sermeq Kujalleq glacier.
