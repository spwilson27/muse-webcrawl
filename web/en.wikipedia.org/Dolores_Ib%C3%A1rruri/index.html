Dolores Ibárruri

Dolores Ibárruri
Born: 9 December 1895, Gallarta, Spain
Died: 12 November 1989, Madrid, Spain
Nickname: La Pasionaria
Party: Communist Party of Spain (PCE)

Dolores Ibárruri Gómez (1895–1989), known as La Pasionaria ("The Passion Flower"), was a Spanish Republican leader and communist politician during the Spanish Civil War. She is famous for popularizing the slogan "¡No pasarán!" ("They shall not pass!") during the Siege of Madrid in 1936.

Early life and career

Born into a mining family in the Basque Country, Ibárruri joined the Communist Party of Spain and became a prominent journalist and organizer. She was elected to the Cortes (parliament) in 1936 for Asturias.

Civil War and exile

During the Civil War she was a leading voice of the Republican government, known for her powerful oratory rallying resistance to Franco's Nationalist rebels. After the Republican defeat in 1939 she went into exile in the Soviet Union, serving as general secretary of the PCE from 1942. She returned to Spain in 1977 after Franco's death and was again elected to parliament in the first democratic elections. She died in Madrid in 1989.
