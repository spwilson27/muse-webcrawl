Species dysphoria

FieldPsychology, identity studies
Related communitiesOtherkin, therian
DefinitionDistress at feeling one's body is of the wrong species

Species dysphoria is a term used in the otherkin and therian communities to describe a feeling of discomfort or distress arising from the sense that one's body is of the wrong species. People who experience it may feel that they are, in some non-physical sense, an animal or mythical creature trapped in a human body.

Usage

The term is modelled on gender dysphoria but is not a recognised medical or psychiatric diagnosis and does not appear in diagnostic manuals such as the DSM-5. It is discussed mainly within online communities and in qualitative academic studies of otherkin identity.

Interpretations

Researchers have treated reported experiences variously as metaphorical identity expression, as part of subcultural belonging, or, in rare clinical case reports, in connection with broader psychiatric conditions.
