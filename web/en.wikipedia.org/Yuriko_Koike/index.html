Yuriko Koike (born 15 July 1952) is a Japanese politician who has served as Governor of Tokyo since 2016. A former television news anchor, she previously served in the national Diet and cabinet, including as Minister of the Environment and, briefly in 2007, as Japan's first female Minister of Defense.

Early career

Koike studied Arabic and sociology, attending Cairo University, and worked as an Arabic interpreter and television news presenter before entering politics in 1992. She moved through several parties before joining the Liberal Democratic Party, holding cabinet posts under Prime Minister Junichiro Koizumi.

Governor of Tokyo

Koike won the 2016 Tokyo gubernatorial election as an independent and founded the Tomin First no Kai party, which won a majority in the 2017 metropolitan assembly election. She was re-elected in 2020 and again in 2024. Her governorship has overseen the postponed Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games and the city's COVID-19 response.
