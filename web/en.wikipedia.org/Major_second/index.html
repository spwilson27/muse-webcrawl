Major second

Semitones2
Cents (equal temperament)200
Just intonation ratio9:8 (greater tone) or 10:9 (lesser tone)

A major second is a musical interval encompassing two staff positions and spanning two semitones. It is also known as a whole step or whole tone. The major second is the interval between the tonic and supertonic of a major scale.

Properties

In twelve-tone equal temperament, a major second equals 200 cents. In just intonation, two sizes of whole tone occur: the greater tone with ratio 9:8 (about 204 cents) and the lesser tone with ratio 10:9 (about 182 cents).

Occurrence

The major second is one of the most common melodic intervals in Western music, forming the first step of the major scale (as in "do–re"). Famous melodies beginning with a major second include "Frère Jacques" (descending context) and many folk tunes.
