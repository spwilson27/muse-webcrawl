Fund Administration

Fund administration is the set of back-office services performed in support of an investment fund, such as a mutual fund, hedge fund, or private equity fund. Fund administrators are typically independent third-party firms that handle the administrative work of running a fund so that the investment manager can concentrate on portfolio management.

Services

Core services include calculating the net asset value (NAV) of the fund, maintaining the fund's books and accounting records, processing subscriptions and redemptions, maintaining the investor register as transfer agent, preparing financial statements, coordinating audits, and providing tax and regulatory reporting. Administrators also perform investor due diligence, including anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer checks, and may act as company secretary for the fund's board.

Industry

Fund administration is concentrated in financial centres such as Ireland, Luxembourg, the Cayman Islands, and the United States. Major providers include large custodian banks such as State Street, BNY, Northern Trust, and Citco. The industry's importance increased after financial scandals in which funds lacking independent administration concealed fraud, since an independent administrator verifies assets and prices on behalf of investors.
