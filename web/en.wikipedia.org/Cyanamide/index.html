Cyanamide

FormulaCH₂N₂
AppearanceWhite crystalline solid
Melting point44 °C

Cyanamide is an organic compound with the formula CN₂H₂. This white solid is widely used in agriculture and in the production of pharmaceuticals and other organic compounds. It contains a nitrile group attached to an amino group and is the parent of the cyanamide salts, most notably calcium cyanamide.

Production and reactions

Cyanamide is produced by hydrolysis of calcium cyanamide, which is itself prepared from calcium carbide and nitrogen gas. It dimerises to 2-cyanoguanidine (dicyandiamide), especially under alkaline conditions, and can be converted to urea derivatives, guanidines, and heterocycles such as melamine.

Uses

In agriculture, cyanamide is used as a fertiliser and as a plant growth regulator that breaks bud dormancy in fruit crops. It is also an intermediate in the synthesis of pharmaceuticals and is used (in derivative form, as calcium carbimide) as an alcohol-deterrent drug, since it inhibits aldehyde dehydrogenase.
