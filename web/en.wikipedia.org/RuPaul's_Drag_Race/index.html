RuPaul's Drag Race is an American reality competition television series produced by World of Wonder and hosted by RuPaul. Contestants — drag queens from across the United States — compete in weekly challenges testing their charisma, uniqueness, nerve and talent, with the winner crowned "America's Next Drag Superstar".

Format

Each season begins with contestants entering the "Werk Room". Episodes typically feature a mini-challenge and a main challenge — spanning sewing and design, comedy, acting (including the improvisational "Snatch Game"), singing and branding — followed by a runway presentation. The two lowest-ranked contestants lip-sync for their lives, after which one is eliminated with the words "Sashay away".

History

The series premiered on Logo TV in 2009 and moved to VH1 in 2017 and MTV in 2023. The judging panel has regularly included Michelle Visage, Carson Kressley and Ross Mathews, alongside celebrity guest judges. The show has won many Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Reality-Competition Program and multiple hosting awards for RuPaul.

Franchise

The format has been adapted internationally, with editions in the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, France, Spain, Thailand, the Philippines and many other territories, plus spin-offs including RuPaul's Drag Race All Stars and RuPaul's Secret Celebrity Drag Race. The franchise is widely credited with transforming drag from a subculture into a global mainstream entertainment industry.
