List of pig breeds

This is a list of pig breeds — domestic pig (Sus scrofa domesticus) breeds from around the world. There are several hundred recognised breeds, ranging from the big commercial breeds that dominate modern pork production (Large White/Yorkshire, Landrace, Duroc and Pietrain) to traditional, rare and miniature breeds. Breeds are listed with their country of origin.
