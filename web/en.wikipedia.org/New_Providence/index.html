New Providence

New Providence is the most populous island in the Bahamas, containing more than 70 percent of the country's population. It is home to the capital city, Nassau, which sprawls across the island's eastern and central areas.

History

Granted to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina in 1670, the island became notorious in the early 18th century as a base for pirates — the informal "Republic of Pirates" centred on Nassau — until Governor Woodes Rogers suppressed piracy from 1718. Its fine harbour made it a trading, blockade-running and later tourism hub.

Geography and economy

The low, flat limestone island is about 34 km long and 11 km wide. Tourism, centred on Cable Beach and the resorts of Paradise Island just offshore, together with offshore finance, dominates the economy.
