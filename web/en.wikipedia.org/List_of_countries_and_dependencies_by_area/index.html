List of countries and dependencies by area

Russia is the largest country in the world by area.

This is a list of countries and dependencies by area, ranked by total surface area, including land and internal waters. The figures are largely drawn from the United Nations Statistics Division and the CIA World Factbook. The entire land surface of the Earth is about 148.94 million square kilometres.
