Chemical Abstracts Service

Chemical Abstracts Service (CAS) is a division of the American Chemical Society based in Columbus, Ohio. Founded in 1907, it produces bibliographic and substance databases of chemical literature.

CAS maintains the CAS Registry, assigning unique CAS Registry Numbers to chemical substances — more than 200 million organic and inorganic substances have been registered. Its products include SciFinder and the CAS Common Chemistry database.

Chemical Abstracts, its print abstract journal, was published from 1907 to 2010 and was one of the most comprehensive scientific abstracting services, indexing journals, patents and conference proceedings worldwide.
