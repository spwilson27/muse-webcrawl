İmralı

İmralı, a small prison island in the Sea of Marmara.

İmralı is a small Turkish island in the south of the Sea of Marmara, west of the Armutlu-Bozburun peninsula within Bursa Province. It measures about 8 square kilometres and has served as a prison island for much of the modern Turkish period. The island is a closed military zone and access is prohibited.

Prison history

A semi-open agricultural prison was established on İmralı in the 1930s. After the 1960 coup d'état, the deposed prime minister Adnan Menderes and ministers Fatin Rüştü Zorlu and Hasan Polatkan were imprisoned and executed on the island in September 1961.

Since February 1999 the island's F-type high-security prison has held Abdullah Öcalan, the captured leader of the Kurdistan Workers' Party (PKK), who was for a decade its only inmate after other prisoners were transferred away; a small number of additional prisoners were moved there in 2009. Öcalan's conditions on İmralı have been the subject of reports by the European Committee for the Prevention of Torture and of applications to the European Court of Human Rights.
