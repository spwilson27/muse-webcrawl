Yonge–Dundas Square is a public square at the southeast corner of Yonge Street and Dundas Street East in downtown Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Opened in 2003, it was designed as a focal point of the downtown core, surrounded by major landmarks including the Toronto Eaton Centre, Ed Mirvish Theatre, and Toronto Metropolitan University.

History

The square was created as part of a redevelopment project in the late 1990s and early 2000s, replacing a block of older commercial buildings. It was officially opened to the public in May 2003 and quickly became a popular venue for public events, concerts, and festivals.

Features

The square features a granite surface, a stage, and a series of fountains whose jets rise directly from the pavement. Large video screens and billboards on surrounding buildings have led to comparisons with Times Square in New York and Piccadilly Circus in London. Renaming discussions in the 2020s concerned the street name Dundas, leading the city to consider renaming the square; in 2024 it was officially renamed Sankofa Square.
