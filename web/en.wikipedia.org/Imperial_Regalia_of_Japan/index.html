Imperial Regalia of Japan

The Imperial Regalia of Japan (三種の神器, Sanshu no Jingi, "Three Sacred Treasures") are the imperial treasures of Japan, consisting of the sword Kusanagi no Tsurugi (草薙劍), the mirror Yata no Kagami (八咫鏡), and the jewel Yasakani no Magatama (八尺瓊勾玉). According to legend, they were brought to earth by Ninigi, grandson of the sun goddess Amaterasu, and their possession legitimizes the Emperor as ruler of Japan.

The treasures

The Kusanagi sword is said to have been found by the storm god Susanoo in the tail of the eight-headed serpent Yamata no Orochi and presented to Amaterasu; it is traditionally kept at Atsuta Shrine in Nagoya. The Yata no Kagami, the mirror with which Amaterasu was lured from her cave, is enshrined at Ise Grand Shrine. The Yasakani jewel is kept at the Imperial Palace in Tokyo. Because the treasures are never publicly displayed, their locations and even their survival rest on tradition; replicas are presented during imperial enthronement ceremonies.

Role in succession

The regalia are presented to the new emperor in the kenji-tō-shōkei no gi ceremony following accession, symbolizing the transfer of imperial authority. The treasures figure prominently in Japanese mythology as recorded in the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki.
