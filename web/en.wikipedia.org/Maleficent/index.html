Maleficent

Maleficent is a fictional character and the main antagonist of Disney's animated film Sleeping Beauty (1959). A wicked fairy, she curses the infant Princess Aurora to prick her finger on a spinning wheel and die before sunset on her sixteenth birthday — softened by a good fairy to a deathlike sleep.

Character

Maleficent is depicted as an elegant, green-skinned sorceress in black and purple robes with horned headdress, accompanied by her raven Diablo. She is widely regarded as one of Disney's greatest villains. In the film she transforms into a dragon for the climax.

Live-action films

Angelina Jolie portrayed a more sympathetic Maleficent in Maleficent (2014), which retells the story from her perspective, and its sequel Maleficent: Mistress of Evil (2019). The character also appears across Disney parks, television and merchandise.

Origins

The character derives from the wicked fairy in the Sleeping Beauty tale as told by Perrault and the Brothers Grimm, combined with elements of the fairy Carabosse from Tchaikovsky's ballet.
