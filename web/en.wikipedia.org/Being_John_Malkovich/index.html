Being John Malkovich

Being John Malkovich is a 1999 American fantasy comedy-drama film directed by Spike Jonze and written by Charlie Kaufman, both in their feature-film debuts. It stars John Cusack, Cameron Diaz, Catherine Keener and John Malkovich as himself.

Plot

An unemployed puppeteer takes a filing job on the 7½th floor of an office building and discovers a portal that leads into the mind of actor John Malkovich, allowing occupants to experience and eventually control his body.

Reception

The film was acclaimed for its originality, received three Academy Award nominations, and is frequently ranked among the greatest films of the 1990s. See Charlie Kaufman.
