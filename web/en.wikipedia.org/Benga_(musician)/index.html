Benga (musician)

Benga (born Adegbenga Adejumo, 28 November 1986) is a British musician from Croydon, South London, known as one of the pioneers of dubstep.

Career

Adejumo began producing as a teenager, releasing on Big Apple Records and working in the record shop of the same name in Croydon, a hub of the early dubstep scene. With Skream he was part of the genre's formative circle.

Work

His debut album Diary of an Afro Warrior appeared in 2008. With Skream and Artwork he formed the supergroup Magnetic Man, whose 2010 debut album reached the UK top five, and he collaborated on Katy B's hit "Katy on a Mission". His second solo album Chapter II was released in 2013.

Hiatus and return

Benga retired from touring in 2014 citing mental health difficulties, and later returned to production and DJing.
