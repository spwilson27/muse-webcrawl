Llandudno

Country: Wales
Principal area: Conwy County Borough
Population: about 20,000
Known for: Victorian seaside resort, Great Orme

Llandudno is a coastal town in Conwy County Borough, Wales, situated on the Creuddyn peninsula between the Great Orme and Little Orme headlands on the Irish Sea coast. It developed as a purpose-built Victorian seaside resort and is known as the "Queen of the Welsh Resorts".

Features

The town has a long curved promenade, a pier opened in 1877 (the longest in Wales), and a cable-hauled tramway up the Great Orme, in operation since 1902. A Victorian extravaganza is held each May bank holiday.

History

The Great Orme was mined for copper in the Bronze Age. The modern resort was laid out from 1849 under the Mostyn estate, and Lewis Carroll's connection with the town is linked in local tradition to the inspiration for Alice's Adventures in Wonderland.
