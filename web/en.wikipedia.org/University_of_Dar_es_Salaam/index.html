University of Dar es Salaam

Type: Public university
Established: 1961
Location: Dar es Salaam, Tanzania
Students: ~24,000

The University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM) is a public university in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. It was established in 1961 as University College Dar es Salaam, affiliated with the University of London, became part of the University of East Africa in 1963, and gained independent university status in 1970. It is Tanzania's oldest and largest university.

History and role

In the 1960s and 1970s the university was a celebrated centre of radical scholarship and debate on African socialism under Julius Nyerere, hosting historians and economists such as Walter Rodney. The main campus, at Mlimani ("on the hill"), lies about 13 kilometres west of the city centre.

Structure

UDSM comprises colleges of engineering, natural and applied sciences, humanities, social sciences, business and health sciences, plus institutes such as the Institute of Development Studies. Constituent and affiliated colleges include the Muhimbili University of Health and Allied Sciences. Alumni include Tanzanian presidents and prime ministers.
