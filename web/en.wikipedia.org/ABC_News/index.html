ABC News is the news division of the American Broadcasting Company (ABC), owned by the Walt Disney Company. Its flagship program is the daily evening newscast ABC World News Tonight with David Muir.

Programs

ABC News produces Good Morning America, Nightline, 20/20, This Week, and the streaming channel ABC News Live. Its radio and digital operations supply news to affiliates across the United States.

History

The division traces its origins to 1945, shortly after ABC's separation from NBC. Under executives such as Roone Arledge, ABC News expanded in the 1970s and 1980s, launching World News Tonight in its modern form and the late-night program Nightline during the Iran hostage crisis.
