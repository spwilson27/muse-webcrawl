Sponsorship

Sponsorship is the act of supporting an event, activity, person, or organization financially or through the provision of products or services. The individual or group that provides the support is known as a sponsor. In marketing, sponsorship is a form of communication in which a company pays for the right to associate its name, products or services with a sponsored activity or property, in exchange for exposure and other benefits.

Types

Sponsorship is common in sport (teams, leagues, stadiums and events such as the Olympic Games), the arts, broadcasting — where sponsors may underwrite programmes — and charitable causes. Naming rights, in which a sponsor's name is attached to a venue or event, are a prominent form. Financial sponsorship of individuals, such as athletes or content creators, is also widespread.

Sponsorship and advertising

Unlike conventional advertising, sponsorship typically works through association and audience goodwill rather than direct product claims; regulations in many jurisdictions require broadcast sponsorship to be clearly identified.
