David Stewart, Duke of Rothesay

David Stewart (24 October 1378 – 26 March 1402) was the heir apparent to the throne of Scotland from 1390 and the first Duke of Rothesay from 1398. He was the eldest son of King Robert III and Annabella Drummond, and was named Duke of Rothesay in a ceremony that also created his uncle Robert Stewart Duke of Albany.

Death

David exercised power as lieutenant of the kingdom from 1399, but his conduct alienated magnates including his uncle Albany and the Earl of Douglas. In 1402 he was arrested and imprisoned in Falkland Palace, where he died — according to tradition by starvation — at the age of 23. His death under Albany's custody cast lasting suspicion on Albany and shadowed the succession of his younger brother, James I.
