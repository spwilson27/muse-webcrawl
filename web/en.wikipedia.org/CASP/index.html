CASP

CASP (Critical Assessment of Techniques for Protein Structure Prediction) is a community-wide, biennial blind experiment to test and advance methods for predicting protein structure from amino acid sequence. It has been held every two years since 1994.

How it works

Organisers collect amino-acid sequences of proteins whose experimental structures are about to be determined but not yet public. Participating groups submit predicted models, which are then compared with the experimental structures by independent assessors. Categories include template-based modelling, free modelling, refinement, and prediction of contacts and assemblies.

Impact

CASP14 in 2020 was a landmark: DeepMind's AlphaFold2 achieved accuracy competitive with experimental methods for many targets, widely described as solving the protein folding problem in its single-chain prediction form. CASP continues to benchmark progress in areas such as complexes and RNA structures.
