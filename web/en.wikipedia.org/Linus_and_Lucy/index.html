Linus and Lucy

"Linus and Lucy" is a popular jazz piano composition written by Vince Guaraldi, named after the Peanuts characters Linus and Lucy van Pelt. Guaraldi recorded it for his 1964 album Jazz Impressions of A Boy Named Charlie Brown, and it became the signature theme music of the Peanuts animated specials after its use in A Charlie Brown Christmas (1965).

Composition and reception

The piece is a brisk, swinging instrumental built on a memorable, syncopated riff, performed by the Vince Guaraldi Trio. Its bright, playful character made it instantly recognisable; it has since served as the de facto theme of the Peanuts franchise, appearing in numerous television specials, films, and commercials.

Legacy

"Linus and Lucy" is among the most widely recognised jazz pieces in American popular culture and a standard introduction to Guaraldi's work. It has been covered by many artists across genres and is frequently played by pianists as a showcase piece; the opening riff is also familiar as background music in countless media contexts.
