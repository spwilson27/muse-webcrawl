Eritrea, officially the State of Eritrea, is a country in the Horn of Africa, bordered by Sudan to the west, Ethiopia to the south, and Djibouti to the southeast, with a long coastline on the Red Sea to the east and northeast. Its capital and largest city is Asmara. The name Eritrea derives from the ancient Greek name for the Red Sea, Erythra Thalassa.

History

The highlands of Eritrea formed part of ancient kingdoms including D'mt and the Kingdom of Aksum, one of the great powers of the ancient world. Italy colonized the territory from the 1880s, making it Italian Eritrea in 1890. After the Second World War, Eritrea was federated with Ethiopia in 1952 and then annexed outright in 1962, sparking a 30-year war of independence led by the Eritrean People's Liberation Front. Eritrean forces entered Asmara in 1991, and a UN-supervised referendum in 1993 confirmed independence by an overwhelming margin.

A border war with Ethiopia from 1998 to 2000 killed tens of thousands, and a state of tension persisted until a peace agreement in 2018. Eritrea is a one-party state ruled by the People's Front for Democracy and Justice; national elections have never been held, and the government has been widely criticized by human rights organizations, including over its indefinite national service programme.

Geography and people

Eritrea ranges from cool highlands around Asmara, at about 2,300 metres, to the Danakil Depression, one of the hottest and lowest places on earth. The population comprises nine recognized ethnic groups; Tigrinya speakers form the largest group, and Christians and Muslims each account for roughly half the population. Recognized languages include Tigrinya, Tigre, Afar, Saho, Kunama, Bilen, Nara, and Arabic.

Economy

The economy rests on subsistence agriculture, which employs most of the workforce, and on mining — including gold, copper, and potash — which has grown since the opening of the Bisha mine in 2011. Remittances from a large diaspora are a major source of income. Asmara's Italian colonial-era modernist architecture was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2017.
