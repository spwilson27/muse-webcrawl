Lithuania

Lithuania, officially the Republic of Lithuania, is a country in the Baltic region of Europe, bordered by Latvia, Belarus, Poland and Russia (Kaliningrad), with a Baltic Sea coast. Its capital and largest city is Vilnius.

History

The Grand Duchy of Lithuania became one of Europe's largest states, forming the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth in 1569 with figures such as Jogaila and Kęstutis. After partitions, Lithuania was under Russian rule, independent 1918–1940, occupied by the USSR and Nazi Germany, then restored independence in 1990 as the first Soviet republic to do so.

Geography and politics

Lithuania is a semi-presidential republic, a member of the EU and NATO since 2004.

Culture

Lithuanian is a conservative Baltic language. Basketball is the most popular sport.
