Mezzanine (album)

Mezzanine is the third studio album by English electronic music group Massive Attack, released on 20 April 1998 by Circa and Virgin Records. Produced by the group with Neil Davidge, it marked a darker turn from the trip hop of their earlier work, drawing on post-punk, dub, and electronica influences.

Background and recording

The album features vocals from Horace Andy, Sarah Jay, and Elizabeth Fraser of Cocteau Twins, whose singing on "Teardrop" became the album's best-known track. Internal tensions during recording contributed to the departure of member Andrew "Mushroom" Vowles shortly after release.

Reception

Mezzanine debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart and is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of the 1990s. "Teardrop" later gained wide recognition as the theme music of the television series House. In 2019, the album was encoded into synthetic DNA for archival storage in a widely reported art project.
