Harbhajan Singh Khalsa

Harbhajan Singh Khalsa (26 August 1929 – 6 October 2004), commonly known as Yogi Bhajan, was an Indian-born American yoga teacher and spiritual leader who introduced Kundalini Yoga to the United States.

Life

Born in Gujranwala, Punjab (now in Pakistan), he studied yoga in India and worked as an Indian customs officer before emigrating to Canada and then Los Angeles in 1968. In 1969 he founded the 3HO (Happy, Healthy, Holy Organization) Foundation.

Legacy and controversy

He built a large following, an associated Sikh community, and businesses including Yogi Tea. After his death, numerous former followers made allegations of sexual abuse and misconduct, which brought renewed scrutiny to his legacy. See also Kundalini yoga.
