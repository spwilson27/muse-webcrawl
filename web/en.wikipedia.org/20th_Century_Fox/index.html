20th Century Fox was one of the "Big Five" major American film studios. It was founded in 1935 by the merger of Fox Film Corporation, founded by William Fox in 1915, and Twentieth Century Pictures, founded in 1933 by Joseph Schenck and Darryl F. Zanuck. Based at its lot in Century City, Los Angeles, the studio produced many of Hollywood's most successful films.

History and franchises

Fox was long led by Darryl F. Zanuck and was home to stars such as Shirley Temple and Marilyn Monroe. Its major franchises included Star Wars (distributed by Fox until Disney's acquisition of Lucasfilm), Die Hard, Alien, Planet of the Apes, X-Men, Ice Age and Avatar, the highest-grossing film of all time. The studio's fanfare music and searchlight logo became iconic.

Disney acquisition

In 2019, The Walt Disney Company acquired Fox's parent company, 21st Century Fox, in a deal worth about $71 billion. In January 2020 Disney renamed the studio 20th Century Studios, dropping the Fox name to disassociate it from the Fox Corporation's remaining media assets. The studio continues to release films as a label within Walt Disney Studios.
