Andrew David Lansley, Baron Lansley, (born 11 December 1956) is a British politician who served as Secretary of State for Health from 2010 to 2012 in the government of David Cameron. A member of the Conservative Party, he was Member of Parliament for South Cambridgeshire from 1997 to 2015.

Career

Lansley worked in the civil service and for the Conservative Research Department before entering Parliament. In opposition he served as Shadow Secretary of State for Health for six years, developing plans to reform the National Health Service.

Health Secretary

As Health Secretary, Lansley was responsible for the Health and Social Care Act 2012, the coalition government's major reorganisation of the NHS in England. The reforms, which increased the role of clinical commissioning and competition, proved controversial with health professionals and the public, and implementation was temporarily "paused" in 2011 for a listening exercise. Lansley moved to become Leader of the House of Commons in the 2012 reshuffle.

House of Lords

Lansley stood down as an MP at the 2015 general election and was created a life peer, as Baron Lansley of Orwell, later that year.
