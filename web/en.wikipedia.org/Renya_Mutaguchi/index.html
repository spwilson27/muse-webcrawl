Renya Mutaguchi
Born: 7 October 1888, Saga Prefecture, Japan
Died: 2 August 1966, Tokyo
Allegiance: Empire of Japan
Rank: Lieutenant General

Renya Mutaguchi (7 October 1888 – 2 August 1966) was a Japanese military officer, lieutenant general in the Imperial Japanese Army during the Second Sino-Japanese War and World War II. He is best known as the commander of the Japanese forces in Operation U-Go, the disastrous 1944 invasion of India that culminated in the Battle of Imphal.

Career

Mutaguchi was involved in the Marco Polo Bridge Incident of 1937 that helped trigger full-scale war with China. He later commanded the 18th Division in Malaya and at the Battle of Singapore.

Imphal

As commander of the Fifteenth Army in Burma, Mutaguchi drove the plan to invade India. The offensive collapsed amid starvation and disease; of some 85,000 Japanese troops, more than half were lost, one of the worst defeats in Japanese Army history. Mutaguchi relieved all three of his divisional commanders during the battle, an unprecedented step, and was himself removed from command.
