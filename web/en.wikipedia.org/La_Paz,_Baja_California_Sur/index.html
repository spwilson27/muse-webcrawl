La Paz, Baja California Sur

La Paz is the capital and largest city of the Mexican state of Baja California Sur, located on La Paz Bay on the Gulf of California, on the Baja California peninsula.

History

Hernán Cortés reached the bay in 1535, but permanent settlement came with a Jesuit mission in 1720 and later colonisation; the city grew as a port for pearling, fishing and trade, and became the state capital when Baja California Sur was created in 1974.

Geography and tourism

La Paz has a hot desert climate and is known for its malecón promenade, beaches such as Balandra, whale sharks, sea lions and the island of Espíritu Santo, a UNESCO-protected area.
