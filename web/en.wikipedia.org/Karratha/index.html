Karratha

Karratha
State: Western Australia
Established: 1968
Population: approx. 17,000
Industry: Iron ore, liquefied natural gas, salt

Karratha is a city in the Pilbara region of Western Australia, adjoining the port of Dampier. Its name comes from an Aboriginal word meaning "good country" or "soft earth". Founded in 1968 to support the processing and export of iron ore, it became a major centre for the resources industry.

Economy

Karratha serves the North West Shelf gas project, the Pluto LNG plant, iron ore operations and one of the world's largest solar salt operations at Dampier. The nearby Burrup Peninsula is noted for its vast collection of Aboriginal rock art (petroglyphs).

Geography

The city lies about 1,500 km north of Perth and has a hot desert climate with a cyclone season in summer. It is administered as part of the City of Karratha local government area. See also Pilbara.
