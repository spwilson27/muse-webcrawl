Cape Clear Island

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Cape Clear Island (Irish: Cléire), officially Oileán Chléire, is an island off the south-west coast of County Cork, Ireland, in the Celtic Sea. It is the southernmost inhabited part of Ireland, with a population of around 110, and forms part of the West Cork Gaeltacht, where Irish is spoken daily.

Features

The island is renowned for its bird observatory, established in 1959, which records migrating seabirds and rare vagrant species passing on migration. Nearby stands the Fastnet Lighthouse on its offshore rock. St Ciarán, patron saint of the island, is said to have been born there, and the ruins of a 12th-century church and a holy well bear his name.

Access

Ferries run from Baltimore and Schull on the mainland. Tourism, especially birdwatching and whale and dolphin watching, supports the island economy alongside farming and fishing.
