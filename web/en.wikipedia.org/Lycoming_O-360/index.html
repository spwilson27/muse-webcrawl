The Lycoming O-360 is a family of four-cylinder, direct-drive, horizontally opposed, air-cooled, piston aircraft engines. Engines in the O-360 series produce between 145 and 225 horsepower (109 to 168 kW). The basic O-360 was first certified in 1952 and remains in production.

The engine family has been installed in thousands of aircraft, including the Cessna 172 Skyhawk, the Piper PA-18 Super Cub, and the Grumman American AA-5 series. It is one of the most successful light aircraft engine families ever built, with the wider 360-cubic-inch family counted in the tens of thousands of units delivered.

Design and development

The O-360 was developed from the smaller Lycoming O-320 by increasing the stroke from 3.875 in (98.4 mm) to 4.375 in (111.1 mm) while retaining the 5.125 in (130.2 mm) bore, increasing displacement to 361.0 cu in (5.92 L). The engine retains the O-320's basic crankcase, cylinders and accessories, easing maintenance commonality between the two families.

Variants include the carburetted O-360, the fuel-injected IO-360, the aerobatic AEIO-360, the helicopter HIO-360, the vertical helicopter VO-360 and the turbocharged TO-360 and TIO-360 series. Compression ratios range from 7.00:1 (145 hp) through 8.50:1 (180 hp) to 8.70:1 for some 200 hp models. The most common variants, rated at 180 hp, power the Cessna 172R/S Skyhawk SP and the Piper Archer.

Variants

  • O-360 — carburetted series, 145–180 hp, widely used in the Cessna 172, Piper PA-18-150 and PA-28-180 Cherokee.
  • IO-360 — fuel-injected, 180–210 hp; powers the Cessna 172S (as IO-360-L2A), Mooney M20J (IO-360-A3B6D) and many homebuilts.
  • AEIO-360 — aerobatic fuel-injected versions with inverted oil systems.
  • HIO-360 — helicopter versions used in light piston helicopters.
  • LO-360 / LIO-360 — counter-rotating versions for twin-engine aircraft such as the Piper PA-34 Seneca and PA-44 Seminole (LTO-360).
  • TIO-360 — turbocharged fuel-injected versions, 180–210 hp.

Applications

  • Cessna 172 Skyhawk (many variants), Cessna 177 Cardinal
  • Piper PA-18 Super Cub, PA-28 Cherokee/Archer, PA-44 Seminole
  • Mooney M20 (several models)
  • Grumman American AA-5B Tiger
  • Van's Aircraft RV series and many other homebuilts
  • Robinson R22 (derated O-360-J2A in later production)
