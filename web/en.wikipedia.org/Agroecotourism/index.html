Agroecotourism

Agroecotourism
TypeNiche tourism
CombinesAgritourism and ecotourism
GoalsSustainable farm income, conservation, education

Agroecotourism is a form of tourism that combines agritourism with ecotourism: visitors travel to working farms that follow ecologically sustainable practices, participating in agricultural activities while learning about conservation, biodiversity, and rural livelihoods.

Features

Agroecotourism operations typically emphasize organic or agroecological production methods, minimal environmental impact, and direct benefit to local farming communities. Activities may include farm stays, harvesting, guided nature walks, wildlife observation, and workshops on traditional food processing.

Benefits and challenges

For farmers, agroecotourism diversifies income and can reward environmentally friendly practices. For visitors, it offers education and authentic rural experiences. Challenges include seasonality, the need for hospitality infrastructure and training, and ensuring that tourism growth does not itself harm the ecosystems and cultures it showcases. Costa Rica is frequently cited as a leading destination, where coffee and cacao farms pair production with rainforest conservation.
