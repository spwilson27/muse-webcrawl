Santa Clara County, California

Santa Clara County is a county in the U.S. state of California. Its county seat and largest city is San Jose. The county occupies much of the Santa Clara Valley at the southern end of San Francisco Bay and is the heart of Silicon Valley, home to many of the world's largest technology companies.

History

The county was one of California's original 27 counties, created in 1850, and took its name from the Spanish mission founded in 1777. Through the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries the valley was a major fruit-growing and canning region — the "Valley of Heart's Delight" — before electronics and semiconductor firms, anchored by Stanford University and companies such as Hewlett-Packard and Apple, transformed it after the Second World War.

Geography and cities

The valley floor is ringed by the Santa Cruz Mountains to the west and the Diablo Range to the east. Principal cities include San Jose, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Cupertino, Milpitas, Campbell, Los Gatos, Morgan Hill and Gilroy. Cupertino hosts Apple's headquarters, Mountain View Google's, and Palo Alto is the birthplace of Silicon Valley's garage-startup tradition.

Economy and demographics

Santa Clara County is one of the wealthiest counties in the United States, with a median household income well above national levels, but also some of its most expensive housing. Its population is highly diverse, with large Asian American and Latino communities. Major employers include Apple, Google, Cisco, Adobe, Nvidia and Stanford University and its medical centre.
