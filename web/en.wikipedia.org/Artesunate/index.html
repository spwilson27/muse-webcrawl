Artesunate

Class: Antimalarial

Derived from: Artemisinin

Routes: Intravenous, oral, intramuscular

Artesunate is a medication used to treat malaria. It is a water-soluble derivative of artemisinin, extracted originally from the plant Artemisia annua. Intravenous artesunate is the first-line treatment recommended by the World Health Organization for severe malaria.

Use

For severe malaria, artesunate is given intravenously or intramuscularly and followed by a complete oral course of an artemisinin-based combination therapy once the patient can swallow. Clinical trials in Asia and Africa showed it reduced mortality compared with quinine. It is also combined with amodiaquine or mefloquine in oral combination products for uncomplicated malaria.

Side effects

Artesunate is generally well tolerated. A recognised adverse effect is delayed haemolysis occurring one to three weeks after treatment of severe malaria, which requires monitoring of blood counts.
