Paradise is a town in Butte County, California, United States, in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada. Located about 85 miles (137 km) north of Sacramento, the town sits on a wide ridge between canyons formed by the Feather River.

History

The town grew after the arrival of the railroad in the early 20th century and became a popular retirement community. Paradise gained national attention as the site of Johnny Appleseed's reputed orchard connections and, more recently, for its apple festival.

Camp Fire

On 8 November 2018 the Camp Fire, the deadliest and most destructive wildfire in California history, swept through Paradise. The fire destroyed the great majority of structures in the town, killed 85 people across the affected area, and displaced nearly the entire population. The town has since been undergoing a long rebuilding process, with residents gradually returning and new fire-resistant building standards adopted.
