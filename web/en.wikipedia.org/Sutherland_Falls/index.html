Sutherland Falls

LocationFiordland, New Zealand
Total height580 m (1,904 ft)
Number of drops3

Sutherland Falls is a waterfall near Milford Sound in Fiordland, New Zealand. With a total drop of about 580 metres (1,904 feet) in three leaps, it is among the tallest waterfalls in New Zealand and was long claimed to be among the highest in the world.

Description

The falls are fed by Lake Quill, a small alpine lake above the Arthur Valley. The water descends in three drops of roughly 226, 248 and 103 metres. They are visible from the Milford Track, one of New Zealand's Great Walks.

History

The falls are named after Donald Sutherland, a Scottish settler and prospector who settled at Milford Sound in the 19th century and publicised the cascade.
