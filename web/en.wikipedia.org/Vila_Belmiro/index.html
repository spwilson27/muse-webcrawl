Vila Belmiro, officially the Estádio Urbano Caldeira, is a football stadium in the Vila Belmiro neighbourhood of Santos, São Paulo, Brazil. It is the home ground of Santos FC and one of the oldest football stadiums in Brazil still in use.

History

The stadium was inaugurated on 12 October 1916 with a match between Santos and Ypiranga, which Santos won 2–1. It is named after Urbano Caldeira, a former player, coach and director of the club. The stadium hosted Pelé for most of his career with Santos, and its small size and intense atmosphere earned it the nickname "the cauldron" (o caldeirão) and the chant that playing at Vila Belmiro was an ordeal for visiting teams.

Structure

The ground has been rebuilt and expanded several times; its current capacity is roughly 16,000 spectators, small by top-flight standards, which has repeatedly prompted proposals for a new arena. Outside the stadium stands a statue of Pelé, and the club's memorial museum (Memorial das Conquistas) is located within the complex.
