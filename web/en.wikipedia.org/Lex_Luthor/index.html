Lex Luthor
Publisher: DC Comics
First appearance: Action Comics #23 (1940)
Created by: Jerry Siegel, Joe Shuster

Alexander Joseph "Lex" Luthor is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster, he first appeared in 1940 and is the archenemy of Superman.

Characterisation

Luthor is depicted as a brilliant, wealthy businessman and scientist — in modern versions the CEO of LexCorp in Metropolis — whose genius-level intellect is matched by his ego and his obsession with defeating Superman, whom he portrays as a threat to humanity. Unlike most of Superman's foes he has no superpowers, relying on wealth, technology, powered armour and, in some stories, kryptonite.

In other media

The character has been portrayed in film by Gene Hackman, Kevin Spacey and Jesse Eisenberg, and voiced in animation notably by Clancy Brown. He is consistently ranked among the greatest comic book villains of all time.
