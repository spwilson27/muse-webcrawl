AC Transit (Alameda-Contra Costa Transit District) is a public transit agency that operates bus service in the East Bay region of the San Francisco Bay Area in California. It is the third-largest bus-only transit agency in California and serves parts of Alameda and Contra Costa counties, including the cities of Oakland, Berkeley, Richmond, San Leandro, Hayward, and Fremont.

History

AC Transit was established in 1960 to take over the financially struggling Key System, a privately owned transit company that had operated buses, streetcars, and commuter trains in the East Bay. Voters approved the creation of the district in 1956 and a bond issue to purchase the Key System's assets, and the district began operating buses in October 1960.

Service

AC Transit operates local routes, Transbay routes across the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge to San Francisco, All Nighter service, and school supplementary routes. Its flagship rapid service is the Tempo bus rapid transit line, which opened in 2020 along International Boulevard between San Leandro and downtown Oakland with dedicated lanes and level-boarding stations.
