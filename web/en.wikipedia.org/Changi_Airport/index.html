Changi Airport

Singapore Changi Airport (IATA: SIN, ICAO: WSSS) is the primary international airport serving Singapore and one of the largest transportation hubs in Asia. Located in Changi, about 20 kilometres northeast of the city centre, it is the home base of Singapore Airlines and is operated by the Changi Airport Group.

History

Changi opened in 1981, replacing Paya Lebar Airport, on a site partly reclaimed from the sea. It has since expanded to four terminals, with a fifth planned. The airport was built over the former RAF Changi airfield area.

Facilities and awards

Changi is renowned for its amenities, including gardens, a butterfly garden, a rooftop swimming pool, cinemas and the Jewel Changi Airport complex, opened in 2019, which features the Rain Vortex — the world's tallest indoor waterfall. The airport has repeatedly been named the "World's Best Airport" by Skytrax, winning the title a record number of times. Before the COVID-19 pandemic it handled over 68 million passengers a year.
