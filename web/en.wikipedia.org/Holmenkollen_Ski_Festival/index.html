Holmenkollen Ski Festival

The Holmenkollen Ski Festival is an annual Nordic skiing event in Oslo, Norway, held since 1892 at the Holmenkollen National Arena. It is one of the oldest and most prestigious ski competitions in the world and a fixture of the FIS World Cup calendars.

Events

The festival includes cross-country skiing, ski jumping, and Nordic combined. Its signature race is the 50 km cross-country event, traditionally skied before huge crowds camping and cheering along the course.

History

Founded by the Association for the Promotion of Skiing (Skiforeningen), the festival has hosted world championships and the ski events of the 1952 Winter Olympics. The Holmenkollen Medal is awarded to outstanding Nordic skiers in connection with the festival.
