Environmental Modification Convention

ENMOD Convention
Signed: 1977, Geneva
Effective: 5 October 1978
Full name: Convention on the Prohibition of Military or Any Other Hostile Use of Environmental Modification Techniques

The Environmental Modification Convention (ENMOD) is an international treaty prohibiting the military or hostile use of environmental modification techniques having widespread, long-lasting or severe effects.

Background

Negotiated after revelations of US weather modification (cloud seeding) during the Vietnam War, the convention was adopted by the UN General Assembly in 1976, opened for signature in Geneva in 1977 and entered into force in 1978.

Provisions

Parties undertake not to engage in hostile use of techniques that could trigger earthquakes, tsunamis, changes in weather or climate patterns, ocean currents or the ozone layer, while peaceful uses are permitted.
