List of nuclear weapons

This is a list of nuclear weapons — the principal designs of fission and thermonuclear weapons developed by the nuclear-weapon states, together with their delivery systems and approximate yields. The first nuclear weapons were developed by the United States under the Manhattan Project during the Second World War; the most powerful weapon ever detonated was the Soviet Tsar Bomba of 1961, with a yield of about 50 megatons.
