Patti LuPone

Patti Ann LuPone (born April 21, 1949) is an American actress and singer, best known for her work in musical theatre. Over a career spanning five decades she has won three Tony Awards, two Olivier Awards, and two Grammy Awards.

Career

Born in Northport, New York, LuPone trained at Juilliard and was a founding member of John Houseman's Acting Company. She rose to prominence as the original Eva Perón in Andrew Lloyd Webber's Evita on Broadway (1979), winning her first Tony Award. She created the role of Fantine in the original London production of Les Misérables (1985), winning an Olivier Award, and played Norma Desmond in the original London production of Sunset Boulevard (1993).

Later acclaimed roles include Rose in Gypsy (2008, second Tony), Helena Rubinstein in War Paint (2017), and Joanne in the gender-swapped revival of Stephen Sondheim's Company in London (2018) and on Broadway, for which she won her third Tony Award in 2022.

Screen work

Television roles include the drama Life Goes On (1989–1993) and appearances in Ryan Murphy's American Horror Story; film roles include Witness (1985), Driving Miss Daisy (1989), and Beau Is Afraid (2023).
