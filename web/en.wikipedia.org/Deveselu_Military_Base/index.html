Deveselu Military Base

Deveselu Military Base is a Romanian military installation near the village of Deveselu in Olt County, southern Romania. The base hosts the United States Navy's Aegis Ashore Ballistic Missile Defense System site for Romania, part of the European Phased Adaptive Approach to missile defence.

History

The site was originally a Romanian Air Force airfield built with Soviet assistance in the 1950s. In 2011 Romania and the United States agreed to host an Aegis Ashore facility at Deveselu, and construction began in 2013. The facility was declared operational in May 2016, becoming the first operational Aegis Ashore site in Europe.

Facility

The base is equipped with an AN/SPY-1 radar and vertical launch systems firing SM-3 interceptor missiles, intended to defend against short- and medium-range ballistic missiles from outside the Euro-Atlantic area. The site is operated by US Navy personnel alongside Romanian forces, and it is integrated into NATO's ballistic missile defence architecture.

Controversy

Russia has repeatedly objected to the Deveselu deployment, arguing that the Mk 41 launchers could in principle fire offensive cruise missiles, a claim denied by the United States and NATO, which describe the system as purely defensive.
