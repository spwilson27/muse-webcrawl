Park Jae-sang (born 31 December 1977), known professionally as Psy, is a South Korean singer, rapper, songwriter and record producer. He is known internationally for his 2012 hit single "Gangnam Style", whose music video became the first video on YouTube to reach one billion views and helped spread the Korean Wave worldwide.

Career

Psy debuted in 2001 with the album Psy from the Psycho World!, which attracted controversy in South Korea for its explicit lyrics. He built a domestic following with energetic live performances and satirical songs before achieving global fame with "Gangnam Style", the lead single from his sixth album Psy 6 (Six Rules), Part 1.

Gangnam Style and later work

"Gangnam Style", a satire of the affluent Gangnam District of Seoul, topped charts in more than 30 countries. Follow-up singles included "Gentleman" (2013) and "Hangover" (2014), featuring Snoop Dogg. In 2019 Psy founded the record label and entertainment company P Nation, signing artists such as Jessi, HyunA and Dawn.

Discography

Studio albums include Psy from the Psycho World! (2001), Ssa2 (2002), 3 Mai (2002), Ssajib (2006), PsyFive (2010), Psy 6 (Six Rules), Part 1 (2012), Chiljip Psy-da (2015), 4X2=8 (2017) and Psy 9th (2022).
