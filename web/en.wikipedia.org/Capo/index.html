Capo

Capo is an Italian word meaning "head" or "chief", and appears in several distinct contexts:

Music

A capo (short for capodastro or capotasto) is a device a musician uses on the neck of a stringed instrument, such as a guitar, to shorten the playable length of the strings, thereby raising the pitch. It allows a guitarist to play in different keys using familiar open chord shapes.

Organized crime

In the Mafia, a capo (caporegime or capodecina) is a "captain" who leads a crew of soldiers and reports to the family's underboss or boss.

Other uses

  • Da capo — a musical direction meaning "from the head", instructing the performer to repeat from the beginning.
  • Capo, a chief or headman in various Italian and Iberian contexts.
