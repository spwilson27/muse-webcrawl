Private Snafu

Written byTheodor "Dr. Seuss" Geisel, Phil Eastman, Munro Leaf
Voiced byMel Blanc
Released1943–1945

Private Snafu is the title character of a series of black-and-white American instructional adult cartoon shorts, produced between 1943 and 1945 during World War II. The shorts were created for the U.S. Army and shown only to servicemen, using racy humour and risqué material censored from civilian cartoons to teach soldiers about security, hygiene, booby traps, and venereal disease.

Production

The series was produced by Warner Bros. Cartoons under Frank Capra's Army Signal Corps unit. The name "Snafu" derives from military slang: "Situation Normal: All Fucked Up". Mel Blanc voiced the hapless private; the character's guide, Technical Fairy, First Class, granted him often-dubious wishes demonstrating each lesson.

Legacy

Long kept from the public, the cartoons entered the public domain and are now regarded as classics of wartime animation, showcasing the work of Dr. Seuss and the Warner Bros. directors.
