Lucas–Lehmer primality test

FieldNumber theory
Applies toMersenne numbers
Developed byÉdouard Lucas, Derrick Henry Lehmer

The Lucas–Lehmer test is a primality test for Mersenne numbers, that is, numbers of the form Mp = 2p − 1. The test was originally developed by Édouard Lucas in 1878 and improved by Derrick Henry Lehmer in the 1930s.

The test

For an odd prime p, define the sequence s0 = 4 and si = si−12 − 2 (mod Mp). Then Mp is prime if and only if sp−2 ≡ 0 (mod Mp).

Significance

Because of its simplicity and efficiency, the Lucas–Lehmer test has been used to discover the largest known prime numbers, almost all of which are Mersenne primes found by the Great Internet Mersenne Prime Search (GIMPS).
