BASF

BASF SE is a German chemical company and one of the largest chemical producers in the world. Its headquarters are in Ludwigshafen, Germany. The company name is an acronym of its original name, Badische Anilin- und Sodafabrik (Baden Aniline and Soda Factory), founded in 1865.

Business

BASF operates in segments including chemicals, materials, industrial solutions, surface technologies, nutrition and care, and agricultural solutions. Its portfolio ranges from basic petrochemicals and plastics to crop-protection products and battery materials.

History

Founded by Friedrich Engelhorn in Mannheim in 1865, BASF grew through dye production and later through ammonia synthesis using the Haber–Bosch process, developed with Carl Bosch. The huge Ludwigshafen Verbund site remains its largest production complex.
