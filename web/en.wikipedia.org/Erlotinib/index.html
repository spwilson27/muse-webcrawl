Erlotinib

Erlotinib, sold under the brand name Tarceva among others, is a medication used to treat non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and pancreatic cancer. It is a receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor that acts on the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR).

Medical uses

Erlotinib is indicated for NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 substitution mutations, and in combination with gemcitabine for pancreatic cancer. It is taken by mouth.

Mechanism

Erlotinib reversibly inhibits EGFR tyrosine kinase, blocking downstream signalling involved in cell proliferation.

Side effects

Common side effects include rash, diarrhoea, loss of appetite, fatigue, and dry skin. Serious effects can include interstitial lung disease and gastrointestinal perforation.

History

It was approved for medical use in the United States in 2004.
