ORCID

ORCID (Open Researcher and Contributor ID) is an alphanumeric code to uniquely identify authors and contributors of scholarly communication, as well as a non-profit organization supporting a registry of such identifiers and linking researchers to their publications, grants, and affiliations. The ORCID registry was launched in October 2012.

The identifier

An ORCID iD is a 16-digit number presented in four groups of four, compatible with the ISNI standard; the final character may be an "X" check character. Identifiers are expressed as a URI, e.g. https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1825-0097. Registration is free for individuals.

Adoption

Publishers, funders, and universities increasingly require or encourage ORCID iDs in manuscript submission, grant application, and research information systems, allowing works to be attributed reliably even when authors share names or change institutions.
