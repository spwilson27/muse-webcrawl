Komaram Bheem

Born22 October 1901, Sankepalli, Hyderabad State (present-day Telangana)
Died27 October 1940, Babijhari
Known forGond tribal rebellion against the Nizam of Hyderabad
Slogan"Jal, Jangal, Zameen" (Water, Forest, Land)

Komaram Bheem (1901–1940) was a Gond tribal (Adivasi) leader from the Adilabad region of present-day Telangana, India, who led a rebellion against the Nizam of Hyderabad in defence of tribal land, forest, and water rights. His rallying cry — Jal, Jangal, Zameen ("Water, Forest, Land") — remains a defining slogan of Adivasi movements across India.

Life and rebellion

Bheem was born in the village of Sankepalli in a Gond family. As a young man he witnessed the oppression of tribal people by forest officials, landlords, and moneylenders under the Nizam's administration, which seized tribal lands and imposed taxes and forced labour for access to forests the Gonds had used for generations under the podu (shifting cultivation) system. After killing a Nizam's official who had come to confiscate crops, Bheem fled and spent years travelling — to Assam's tea gardens and elsewhere — where he learned to read and write and encountered anti-colonial ideas.

Returning home, he organised the Gond and Kolam tribes of the Jodeghat hills into an armed resistance. By 1940 his guerrilla force controlled a string of villages and Bheem demanded an autonomous Gond kingdom. On 27 October 1940, Nizam's police forces attacked his hideout at Babijhari; Bheem and his companions were killed in the ensuing battle.

Legacy

Bheem is revered among the Gond people as a deity-like hero; his death anniversary is commemorated annually at Jodeghat, where a memorial and museum have been built. In 2016 the Government of Telangana created Komaram Bheem Asifabad district in his honour. He reached a global audience as one of the two protagonists of S. S. Rajamouli's film RRR (2022), in which he was portrayed by N. T. Rama Rao Jr.
