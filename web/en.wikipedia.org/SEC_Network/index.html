SEC Network is an American sports television network owned by ESPN Inc. and operated as part of ESPN's Charlotte-based networks. It is dedicated to coverage of the Southeastern Conference (SEC), the college athletic conference whose members are concentrated in the southern United States.

Programming

The network televises SEC football, men's and women's basketball, baseball, softball and other sports, carrying several hundred live events per year, with additional events streamed on the companion digital service SEC Network+. Studio programming includes the flagship news show SEC Now, the talk show The Paul Finebaum Show, and the Saturday football pregame show SEC Nation.

History

ESPN and the SEC announced the network in 2013 under a 20-year agreement extending their media rights partnership. It launched on 14 August 2014, reaching roughly 90 million households at launch — among the most successful cable network debuts in the United States. The channel's first live football telecast was a 2014 game between Texas A&M and South Carolina.
