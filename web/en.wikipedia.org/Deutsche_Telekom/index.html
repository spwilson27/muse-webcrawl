Deutsche Telekom AG is a German telecommunications company headquartered in Bonn. It is one of the world's largest telecommunications providers, operating fixed-line and mobile networks in Germany, elsewhere in Europe, and—through its majority stake in T-Mobile US—in the United States. The company's brands are marketed under the "T" logo, including T-Mobile and T-Systems, its IT services arm for business customers.

History

Deutsche Telekom was created in 1995 from the privatization of the telecommunications division of the state-owned Deutsche Bundespost. Its initial public offering in 1996 was one of the largest in German history and turned the "T-Aktie" into a widely held popular share. The company expanded internationally around 2000, acquiring mobile operators including VoiceStream in the United States, which became T-Mobile US.

Operations

The group serves well over 200 million mobile customers worldwide together with extensive fixed broadband and television services in Europe. T-Mobile US, which merged with Sprint in 2020, contributes the largest share of group revenue and growth. Deutsche Telekom is also a major operator of networks in countries including Poland, Greece, Austria, the Netherlands, and the Czech Republic.

Ownership

The German federal government and the state-owned KfW bank together retain a significant minority stake, while the remainder is in free float. Deutsche Telekom is a constituent of the DAX index of Germany's largest listed companies.
