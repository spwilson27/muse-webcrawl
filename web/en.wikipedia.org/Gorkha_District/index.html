Gorkha District

Gorkha District is one of the 77 districts of Nepal, located in Gandaki Province. Its administrative headquarters is the town of Gorkha (also called Prithvinarayan Municipality). The district covers an area of about 3,610 km² and had a population of roughly 252,000 in the 2021 census (about 271,000 in 2011).

History

Gorkha was the seat of the Shah dynasty's small principality from which Prithvi Narayan Shah launched the unification of Nepal in the 18th century. The old royal palace, Gorkha Durbar, stands on a hilltop above the town, near the temple of the goddess Gorakhkali and the cave associated with the sage Gorakhnath, from whom the district takes its name. The famous Gurkha soldiers of the British and Indian armies take their name from this district.

Geography

The district stretches from low hills in the south to the high Himalaya in the north, bordering Tibet (China). Its northern part includes parts of the Manaslu range — Mount Manaslu (8,163 m), the world's eighth-highest mountain, lies on its northwestern border — and much of the Manaslu Conservation Area. The Budhi Gandaki and Trishuli rivers drain the district.

2015 earthquake

The April 2015 Nepal earthquake (magnitude 7.8) had its epicentre at Barpak in Gorkha District. The quake and its aftershocks devastated villages across the district, destroyed many historic structures in Gorkha town, and killed thousands across Nepal.

Demographics and administration

The district's population includes Gurung, Magar, Brahmin, Chhetri, Newar, Tamang, and other groups; Nepali is the most widely spoken language. Gorkha is administratively divided into two municipalities and nine rural municipalities.
