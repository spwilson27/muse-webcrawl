Pedigree chart

A pedigree chart is a diagram that shows the occurrence and appearance of phenotypes of a particular gene or organism and its ancestors from one generation to the next. It is used in human genetics, genealogy and animal breeding to trace inheritance patterns and family relationships.

Pedigree chart
Used in: Genetics, genealogy, animal breeding
Symbols: Squares (male), circles (female)

Conventions

By convention, males are drawn as squares and females as circles; a horizontal line between them denotes mating, and vertical lines lead to their offspring in birth order. Filled symbols indicate individuals expressing the trait under study, half-filled symbols may denote carriers, and a slash marks deceased individuals.

Genetic analysis

By examining patterns across generations—whether a trait appears in every generation, skips generations, or affects one sex disproportionately—clinicians can infer whether a condition is autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive, X-linked or mitochondrial, and estimate recurrence risks.

Genealogy

In genealogy a pedigree chart records a person's direct ancestors, typically with the subject at the left or bottom and parents, grandparents and earlier generations branching outward, complementing the broader family tree.
