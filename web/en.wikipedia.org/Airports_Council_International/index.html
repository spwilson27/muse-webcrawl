Airports Council International

Airports Council International (ACI) is a global trade association representing the world's airports. Founded in 1991, ACI is headquartered in Montreal, Canada, with regional offices covering Africa, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East, Europe, Latin America and the Caribbean, and North America. Its members operate thousands of commercial airports worldwide, handling the great majority of global passenger and cargo traffic.

Activities

ACI promotes airport interests in safety, security, environmental sustainability, and economics, and represents airports before bodies such as the International Civil Aviation Organization. It publishes the widely cited World Airport Traffic Report ranking airports by passengers, cargo, and movements. Its Airport Service Quality (ASQ) programme benchmarks passenger satisfaction across hundreds of airports, and the Airport Carbon Accreditation programme certifies airports' progress in measuring and reducing carbon emissions. ACI also runs professional training and accreditation schemes, including its APEX safety-review programme.

History

ACI was formed in 1991 from the merger of predecessor bodies, including the Airports Operators Council International and the International Civil Airports Association, creating a single global voice for airports as the industry shifted from state-run infrastructure toward commercial, corporatized operators.
