Simone Arianne Biles Owens (born 14 March 1997) is an American artistic gymnast widely regarded as the greatest gymnast of all time. Her 11 Olympic medals and 30 World Championship medals make her the most decorated gymnast in history, male or female. Five skills in the Code of Points are named after her — two on vault, two on floor exercise, and one on balance beam — more eponymous skills than any other gymnast.

Career

Adopted by her grandparents in Spring, Texas, Biles began gymnastics at age six. She dominated the sport from her senior debut in 2013, winning three consecutive world all-around titles before the Rio 2016 Olympics, where she won four gold medals (team, all-around, vault, floor) and a bronze on beam. She returned to win further world titles in 2018 and 2019, unveiling skills of unprecedented difficulty, including the Yurchenko double pike vault.

At the Tokyo 2020 Olympics (held in 2021), Biles withdrew from most finals after experiencing the "twisties", a dangerous loss of spatial awareness, returning to win bronze on balance beam; her openness about mental health was widely credited with changing the conversation around athlete welfare. She made a celebrated comeback at the Paris 2024 Olympics, leading the U.S. team to gold and winning the all-around and vault titles plus silver on floor, bringing her Olympic medal tally to eleven.
