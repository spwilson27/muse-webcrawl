Lazarus (David Bowie song)

"Lazarus" is a song by English musician David Bowie, released on 17 December 2015 as the second single from his twenty-sixth and final studio album Blackstar. Written as Bowie faced terminal liver cancer — a fact then unknown to the public — it opens "Look up here, I'm in heaven" and became his farewell statement, its video released days before his death on 10 January 2016.

Composition and video

The nearly five-minute track blends art rock and jazz, performed with Donny McCaslin's quartet, moving from a funereal opening to an intense climax. Johan Renck's video shows a blindfolded, bandaged Bowie levitating above a hospital bed and retreating into a wardrobe, imagery widely read as a staged goodbye.

Legacy

"Lazarus" was Bowie's last single released in his lifetime, charted worldwide after his death, and shares its title with his off-Broadway musical Lazarus (2015), a sequel to The Man Who Fell to Earth. Critics rank it among his greatest late works. See also Lazarus of Bethany.
