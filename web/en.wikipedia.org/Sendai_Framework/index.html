Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction

The Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030 is an international agreement adopted at the Third United Nations World Conference on Disaster Risk Reduction, held in Sendai, Japan, in March 2015, and endorsed by the UN General Assembly. It succeeded the Hyogo Framework for Action and sets out how states and other stakeholders should reduce disaster losses.

Priorities and targets

The framework has four priorities: understanding disaster risk; strengthening disaster risk governance; investing in disaster risk reduction for resilience; and enhancing disaster preparedness for effective response and to "Build Back Better" in recovery. It sets seven global targets, including substantially reducing global disaster mortality, the number of people affected, direct economic loss relative to global GDP, and damage to critical infrastructure by 2030, and increasing the number of countries with national and local risk-reduction strategies.

Implementation

Progress is monitored through the Sendai Framework Monitor managed by the United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR). The framework emphasises that states bear primary responsibility but must share it with local government, the private sector and civil society, and it links closely with the Sustainable Development Goals and the Paris Agreement on climate change.
