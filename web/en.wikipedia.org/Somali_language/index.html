Somali (Af Soomaali) is an Afro-Asiatic language of the Cushitic branch spoken by Somalis in Greater Somalia and by the Somali diaspora. It is an official language of Somalia, a national language of Djibouti, and a working language in Ethiopia's Somali Region.

Linguistics

Somali is tonal, with pitch distinguishing grammatical and lexical items, and has a complex system of gender polarity and focus marking. The main dialect groups are Northern (standard), Benadiri, and Maay, the latter sometimes considered a separate language.

Writing

Somali was historically written in Arabic script and in invented scripts such as Osmanya, Borama, and Kaddare. In 1972 the government adopted a Latin alphabet devised by Shire Jama Ahmed, and Somali became the language of administration and primary education.
