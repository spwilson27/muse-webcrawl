Founded1997

Cyberjaya is a planned township in Sepang District, Selangor, Malaysia, created as the technology hub of the Multimedia Super Corridor. Launched in 1997, it was designed as Malaysia's "Silicon Valley", hosting technology companies, data centres, and universities alongside its administrative neighbour, Putrajaya, the federal administrative capital.

Development

Built largely on former oil palm plantation land, Cyberjaya offers purpose-built office campuses, generous green space, and fibre-optic infrastructure. Major occupants have included multinational technology and shared-services firms, as well as Multimedia University, one of Malaysia's first private universities.

Transport

The township is connected by expressways to Kuala Lumpur and Kuala Lumpur International Airport, and is served by the MRT Putrajaya Line, which opened stations in Cyberjaya in 2023.
