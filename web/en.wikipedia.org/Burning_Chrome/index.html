Burning Chrome

"Burning Chrome" is a short story by American-Canadian author William Gibson, first published in Omni magazine in 1982 and later the title story of his 1986 collection Burning Chrome. Set in Gibson's Sprawl setting, it follows two freelance computer hackers, Automatic Jack and Bobby Quine, who use a powerful Russian "icebreaker" program to rob a criminal organisation.

Significance

The story is notable for introducing the word "cyberspace" — Gibson's first use of the term he would popularise in Neuromancer (1984) — and is regarded as a foundational text of the cyberpunk genre. The collection also includes "Johnny Mnemonic" and "New Rose Hotel", both later adapted to film.
