Hannes Alfvén

Hannes Olof Göran Alfvén (30 May 1908 – 2 April 1995) was a Swedish electrical engineer and plasma physicist who won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1970 for his work on magnetohydrodynamics (MHD).

Career

Alfvén studied at Uppsala University, earning his doctorate in 1934. He worked at the Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. He predicted the existence of magnetohydrodynamic waves, now called Alfvén waves, in 1942.

Contributions

His work founded much of plasma cosmology and the study of plasma in space, including explanations of aurorae, the Van Allen radiation belts, and solar phenomena. He shared the 1970 Nobel Prize with Louis Néel. See also Magnetohydrodynamics and Plasma physics.
