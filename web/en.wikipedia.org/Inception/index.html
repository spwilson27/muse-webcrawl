Inception

Inception
Director: Christopher Nolan
Released: 2010
Starring: Leonardo DiCaprio, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Elliot Page
Genre: Science fiction / heist

Inception is a 2010 science fiction action film written and directed by Christopher Nolan. It stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, a thief who steals secrets from deep within the subconscious during the dream state and is offered one last job: to plant an idea inside a target's mind — "inception" — without being detected.

Plot

Cobb assembles a team to enter the dreams of businessman Robert Fischer and work through successive nested dream levels, each with its own faster flow of time, while Cobb's projection of his late wife threatens the mission.

Production

Nolan developed the idea over about a decade. The film was shot in six countries and is noted for practical effects, including a rotating corridor set for a zero-gravity fight scene.

Reception

Inception grossed over 800 million dollars worldwide, won four Academy Awards, and its ambiguous final shot — a spinning top that may or may not topple — became widely discussed.
