Don Carlos, Duke of Madrid (Carlos María de los Dolores Juan Isidro José Francisco Quirico Antonio Miguel Gabriel Rafael; 30 March 1848 – 18 July 1909) was the senior member of the House of Bourbon from 1887 until his death and the Carlist claimant to the throne of Spain under the name Carlos VII from 1868. He was also a Legitimist claimant to the French throne.

Third Carlist War

Carlos proclaimed his claim in exile and, during the Third Carlist War (1872–1876), crossed into Spain to lead his forces, establishing a rival court at Estella in Navarre. Despite early successes, his army could not take Bilbao, and after Alfonso XII's restoration, Carlist resistance collapsed in 1876; Carlos fled into France.

Later life

Carlos spent the rest of his life in exile, mainly in Venice, leading the Carlist movement, issuing manifestos, and organizing the party. His son Jaime succeeded him as claimant in 1909.
