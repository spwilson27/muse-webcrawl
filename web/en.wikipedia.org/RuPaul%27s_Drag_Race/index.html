RuPaul's Drag Race is an American reality competition television series produced by World of Wonder for MTV Entertainment Studios, hosted and produced by drag queen RuPaul. The series documents RuPaul in the search for "America's next drag superstar".

Format

Contestants compete in weekly challenges involving acting, comedy, singing, sewing, and improvisation, culminating in runway presentations judged by a panel. Each episode typically ends with the two lowest-scoring queens lip-syncing for their lives; the loser is eliminated.

Impact

The show has won numerous Primetime Emmy Awards, launched international editions in many countries, and is credited with bringing drag culture into the mainstream.
