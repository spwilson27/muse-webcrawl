Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace

Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace (KDA) is the defence division of Norway's Kongsberg Gruppen, headquartered in Kongsberg, Norway. It develops and manufactures missile systems, air defence, remote weapon stations and communications equipment.

ParentKongsberg Gruppen
HeadquartersKongsberg, Norway
ProductsNASAMS, Naval Strike Missile, Joint Strike Missile, Protector RWS

Products

The company's best-known systems include the NASAMS air defence system (developed with Raytheon), the Naval Strike Missile and its air-launched derivative the Joint Strike Missile, and the Protector family of remote weapon stations, widely exported to NATO countries including the United States.
