Elizabeth Ann Warren (née Herring; born June 22, 1949) is an American politician and former law professor who has served as the senior United States senator from Massachusetts since 2013. A member of the Democratic Party, Warren was a prominent scholar of bankruptcy and commercial law at Harvard Law School before entering politics.

Career

Warren chaired the Congressional Oversight Panel for the Troubled Asset Relief Program after the 2008 financial crisis and was the driving force behind the creation of the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, established by the Dodd–Frank Act in 2010. She was first elected to the Senate in 2012, defeating incumbent Scott Brown, and has been re-elected since.

2020 presidential campaign

Warren ran for the Democratic nomination for president in the 2020 election, campaigning on detailed policy proposals — summarised by the slogan "I have a plan for that" — including a wealth tax and universal childcare, before suspending her campaign in March 2020.
