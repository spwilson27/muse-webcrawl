BBC Cymru Wales

BBC Cymru Wales is a division of the BBC that is responsible for broadcasting in Wales. Headquartered in Cardiff, it operates the television services BBC One Wales and BBC Two Wales, the radio stations BBC Radio Wales and BBC Radio Cymru, and online and interactive services in English and Welsh.

Services

BBC Cymru Wales produces programming for broadcast across the United Kingdom as well as specifically for Wales, including the long-running soap opera Pobol y Cwm (made for S4C), news programmes such as Wales Today, and drama productions made at its Roath Lock studios in Cardiff Bay, home to series including Doctor Who and Casualty.

History

The BBC began broadcasting in Wales in 1923 from Cardiff. Its modern headquarters, BBC Central Square in Cardiff, opened to staff from 2019, replacing the former Broadcasting House in Llandaff.
