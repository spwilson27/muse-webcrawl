Hairdresser

A hairdresser is a person whose occupation is cutting or styling hair in order to change or maintain a person's image. This is achieved using a combination of hair colouring, haircutting, and hair texturing techniques. Many hairdressers work in salons or barbershops, though some visit clients at home or work in film, theatre, and fashion.

Work and training

Hairdressers typically complete vocational training or an apprenticeship and, in many jurisdictions, must hold a cosmetology licence. Their work includes consultations, shampooing, cutting with scissors, clippers and razors, chemical services such as colouring, perming and relaxing, and finishing styles with heat tools. Senior practitioners may specialise as colourists or stylists for editorial and runway work.

History

Hair care is an ancient trade: barbers in ancient Egypt, Greece, and Rome cut hair and shaved beards, and elaborate hairdressing flourished at the French court in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, when the title coiffeur gained prestige.
