Luxembourg City

Country: Luxembourg
Status: Capital city
Population: about 135,000

Luxembourg City (Luxembourgish: Stad Lëtzebuerg) is the capital and most populous commune of Luxembourg, situated at the confluence of the Alzette and Pétrusse rivers in the south of the country.

History

The city grew around a castle acquired by Count Siegfried in 963. Its formidable fortifications earned it the nickname "Gibraltar of the North" before most were dismantled under the 1867 Treaty of London. The old town and fortifications are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Today

Luxembourg City is a major banking and administrative centre, hosting institutions of the European Union including the Court of Justice and the European Investment Bank.
