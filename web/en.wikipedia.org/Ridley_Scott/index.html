Ridley Scott

Sir Ridley Scott (born 30 November 1937) is an English film director and producer. He directed, among other films, the science-fiction classics Alien (1979) and Blade Runner (1982), the road film Thelma & Louise (1991), the historical epic Gladiator (2000) and The Martian (2015). He is known for his atmospheric and highly concentrated visual style.

Early life and career

Scott was born in South Shields, County Durham, the son of an army officer, and studied design and film at the Royal College of Art. After working as a designer and director at the BBC, he founded the advertising production company Ridley Scott Associates, making acclaimed commercials such as the Hovis "Bike Ride" advertisement and Apple's "1984" Macintosh commercial.

Feature films

His feature debut, The Duellists (1977), won the Best Debut Film award at Cannes. Alien established him internationally, and Blade Runner, though initially divisive, is now regarded as one of the greatest science-fiction films ever made. Later works include Legend (1985), Black Rain (1989), 1492: Conquest of Paradise (1992), G.I. Jane (1997), Gladiator — winner of the Academy Award for Best Picture — Black Hawk Down (2001), Kingdom of Heaven (2005), American Gangster (2007), Prometheus (2012), The Martian (2015), The Last Duel (2021) and Napoleon (2023).

Style

Scott's films are characterised by dramatic lighting, smoke and atmosphere, dense production design and a painterly attention to period and futuristic detail, reflecting his background in art direction and advertising.
