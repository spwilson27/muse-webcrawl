Fraport AG Frankfurt Airport Services Worldwide is a German transport company which operates Frankfurt Airport, Germany's busiest airport, and holds interests in numerous airports around the world. Its name combines "Frankfurt" and "Airport". The company's shares are listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and it is a constituent of the MDAX index.

History

The company traces its origins to 1924 with the founding of Südwestdeutsche Flugverkehrs AG, and to the traffic company (Verkehrs-Aktiengesellschaft Rhein-Main, VAG) formed in 1947 to run Frankfurt's airport. Renamed Flughafen Frankfurt/Main AG (FAG) in 1954, it was partially privatised and listed in 2001, adopting the name Fraport. The State of Hesse and the City of Frankfurt remain its largest shareholders.

Operations

Besides Frankfurt, where it handles roughly 60 million passengers in a normal year, Fraport operates or holds stakes in airports including Ljubljana in Slovenia, Lima's Jorge Chávez International Airport in Peru, Antalya in Turkey (joint venture), Burgas and Varna in Bulgaria, St Petersburg Pulkovo (divested), and the 14 Greek regional airports of Fraport Greece, including Thessaloniki, Corfu and Rhodes. The group also runs airport retail, ground handling and consulting services worldwide.
