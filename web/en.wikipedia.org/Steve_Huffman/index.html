Steve Huffman (born 1983), known on Reddit by the username spez, is an American web developer and entrepreneur. He co-founded the social news site Reddit in 2005 with his college roommate Alexis Ohanian, with early funding from Y Combinator.

Career

Reddit was sold to Condé Nast in 2006. Huffman left in 2009 and co-founded the travel search site Hipmunk in 2010. He returned to Reddit as chief executive in 2015, succeeding Ellen Pao, and led the company through redesigns, mobile development, advertising growth, and its 2024 initial public offering. His tenure has included controversies over content moderation and, in 2023, API pricing changes that triggered widespread subreddit protests.
