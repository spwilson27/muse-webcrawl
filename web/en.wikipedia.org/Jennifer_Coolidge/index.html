Jennifer Coolidge (born August 28, 1961) is an American actress and comedian. Known for her distinctive voice and comic timing, she gained recognition for playing Jeanine Stifler ("Stifler's mom") in the American Pie film series (1999–2012) and Paulette Bonafonté in Legally Blonde (2001) and its sequel. She is a frequent collaborator of Christopher Guest, appearing in his mockumentaries Best in Show (2000), A Mighty Wind (2003), and For Your Consideration (2006).

Career resurgence

After a series of supporting roles, including Sophie Kaczynski on the sitcom 2 Broke Girls (2011–2017), Coolidge achieved a major career resurgence with her portrayal of the wealthy, fragile Tanya McQuoid in the HBO anthology series The White Lotus (2021–2022). The role earned her widespread critical acclaim, a Primetime Emmy Award, a Golden Globe Award, and a Screen Actors Guild Award, among other honours.

Early life

Coolidge was born in Boston and raised in Norwell, Massachusetts. She studied at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts in New York and performed with the Groundlings improv troupe in Los Angeles, making her television debut in a 1993 episode of Seinfeld.

Personal life and advocacy

Coolidge is a noted supporter of animal rights and LGBTQ causes and has long been regarded as a gay icon, something she has embraced in interviews and public appearances.
