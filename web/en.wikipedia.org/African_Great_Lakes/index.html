African Great Lakes

The African Great Lakes are a series of lakes constituting part of the Rift Valley lakes of East Africa. They include Lake Victoria (the second-largest freshwater lake in the world by area), Lake Tanganyika (the world's second deepest and a huge store of fresh water), Lake Malawi (Nyasa), Lake Turkana, Lake Albert, Lake Edward and Lake Kivu.

Geography and ecology

The lakes lie within and around the East African Rift and drain into the Nile, Congo and Zambezi systems. They are renowned for extraordinary biodiversity — the rift lakes' cichlid fish radiations are a classic case of adaptive evolution — and support dense surrounding populations and major fisheries.

Region

"African Great Lakes region" also denotes the surrounding countries: Burundi, Rwanda, Uganda, and parts of Kenya, Tanzania and the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
