Are You Experienced

Studio album by the Jimi Hendrix Experience

Released: 12 May 1967 (UK); 23 August 1967 (US)

Recorded: October 1966 – April 1967

Genre: Psychedelic rock, acid rock, hard rock, blues rock

Length: 40:12 (UK)

Label: Track (UK), Reprise (US)

Producer: Chas Chandler

Are You Experienced is the debut studio album by the Jimi Hendrix Experience, released in 1967. The album was an immediate critical and commercial success, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time. It features Jimi Hendrix's intricate songwriting, psychedelic, blues and rock elements, and innovative guitar playing.

The album was recorded after Hendrix moved to London from New York in September 1966, where he formed the Jimi Hendrix Experience with bassist Noel Redding and drummer Mitch Mitchell. Produced by Chas Chandler and recorded at Olympic, De Lane Lea and CBS studios in London, the album showcased Hendrix's innovative approach to songwriting and electric guitar playing, which soon established a new direction in psychedelic and hard rock music.

Music and lyrics

Are You Experienced displays Hendrix's mastery of several musical styles, including blues, R&B, rock, and psychedelia. Songs such as "Purple Haze", "Manic Depression", "Fire", and "The Wind Cries Mary" mix distorted guitar timbres with soulful, playful and surreal lyrics.

Release and reception

In the UK, Are You Experienced spent 33 weeks on the charts, peaking at number two. In the United States, the album, released with a different track order and cover, reached number five on the Billboard 200. In 2005, Rolling Stone ranked the album 30th on its list of the 500 greatest albums of all time, and in 2020 it was ranked 30th again in a revised list; it was also inducted into the United States National Recording Registry.
