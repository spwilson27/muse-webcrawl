Audi A6

Manufacturer: Audi AG
Production: 1994–present
Class: Executive car (E-segment)

The Audi A6 is an executive car manufactured by the German automaker Audi since 1994, succeeding the Audi 100. It is offered as a sedan and as an Avant estate, with front-wheel drive or quattro all-wheel drive.

Generations

Successive generations are designated C4 through C9. High-performance derivatives include the S6 and RS 6 Avant. Engines have ranged from four-cylinder diesels to V10 petrol units in earlier RS models, with later generations adding mild-hybrid and plug-in hybrid powertrains.

Allroad

The A6 allroad quattro, introduced in 1999, adds raised air suspension and protective cladding for light off-road use.
