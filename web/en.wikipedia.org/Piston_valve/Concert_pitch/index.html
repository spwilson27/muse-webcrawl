Concert pitch

Concert pitch is the standard reference pitch to which musical instruments are tuned. In modern practice, the A above middle C is set at 440 Hz, a standard adopted internationally in the 20th century (ISO 16).

History

Pitch varied widely by place and era; historical standards ranged from below 400 Hz to above 480 Hz, complicating performance of older music on modern instruments. Some orchestras tune slightly sharp (441–443 Hz) for brilliance, while period-instrument ensembles use lower standards such as A=415 Hz for Baroque repertoire.
