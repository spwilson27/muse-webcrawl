Rock climbing

Rock climbing is a sport in which participants ascend natural rock formations or artificial climbing walls using their hands and feet, with ropes and protective equipment for safety in most disciplines. Styles include free climbing, aid climbing, bouldering, sport climbing and traditional ("trad") climbing.

Equipment

Climbers use harnesses, ropes, belay devices, carabiners and protection such as nuts and camming devices that are placed in cracks. Climbing shoes with sticky rubber soles improve friction on small footholds.

History

Modern rock climbing developed in the late 19th century in Britain, Germany and Italy, and expanded greatly after the mid-20th century. Competition climbing became an Olympic sport at the Tokyo 2020 Games.
