Frequency

Frequency is the number of occurrences of a repeating event per unit of time. It is measured in hertz (Hz), where one hertz equals one cycle per second. The period is the reciprocal of frequency: the duration of one complete cycle.

Applications

Frequency describes sound waves (determining pitch), electromagnetic waves such as light and radio, alternating current (50 or 60 Hz in mains electricity), and mechanical oscillations. In music, doubling a frequency raises a tone by one octave.
