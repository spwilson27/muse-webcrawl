Perfect pitch

Absolute pitch, commonly called perfect pitch, is the ability to identify or produce a musical note without any external reference tone. It is a rare trait, estimated to occur in roughly one in ten thousand people, though it is more common among speakers of tonal languages and those who began musical training early in childhood.

Debate

Researchers debate how far absolute pitch is genetic versus learned during a critical period. It is distinct from relative pitch — the ability to judge intervals between notes — which is trainable and widespread among musicians.
