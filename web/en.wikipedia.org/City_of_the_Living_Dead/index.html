City of the Living Dead
Directed by: Lucio Fulci
Released: 1980
Country: Italy
Genre: Horror

City of the Living Dead (Italian: Paura nella città dei morti viventi, lit. "Fear in the City of the Living Dead") is a 1980 Italian supernatural horror film co-written and directed by Lucio Fulci. It is the first instalment of Fulci's unofficial "Gates of Hell" trilogy, followed by The Beyond (1981) and The House by the Cemetery (1981).

Plot

In the small town of Dunwich, a priest hangs himself in a church cemetery, opening one of the seven gates of Hell. A psychic and a journalist learn that the gates must be closed before All Saints' Day or the dead will rise and overwhelm the living. Their efforts are met by a series of gruesome supernatural killings as zombies begin to appear.

Production and release

The film stars Christopher George and Catriona MacColl. It was shot largely in the United States (including Savannah, Georgia, which stood in for Dunwich) and at studios in Rome. Like many Italian horror films of the era it was released under various titles internationally.

Reception

Initially controversial for its graphic gore effects, the film was caught up in the British "video nasty" era. It has since been reassessed as a cult classic and a key work of Fulci's filmography, noted for its dreamlike atmosphere and Fabio Frizzi's score.
