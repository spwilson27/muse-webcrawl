Berlin derby

The Berlin derby (German: Berliner Stadtderby) is the football rivalry between the two principal clubs of Berlin, Hertha BSC and 1. FC Union Berlin. The fixture carries the historical symbolism of the divided city: Hertha, based at the Olympiastadion, was West Berlin's leading club, while Union, based at the Stadion An der Alten Försterei in Köpenick, was a workers' club of East Berlin, though the Cold War era offered few competitive meetings between them.

History

During the division of the city, Hertha played in the West German league system while Union competed in East Germany, and their supporters often maintained a sympathetic "friendship behind the Wall": Hertha fans visited Union's ground, and Union fans followed Hertha's European matches in the East. After reunification the clubs met in the 2. Bundesliga in 2010–11 and, following Union's first promotion in 2019, in the Bundesliga, where Union won the first top-flight derby at the Alten Försterei in November 2019.

Other Berlin derbies

Historically, other Berlin clubs — including BFC Dynamo, the record champions of East Germany, Tennis Borussia, and Blau-Weiß 90 — have contested derbies with Hertha and Union.
