Chiefs of Staff Committee

The Chiefs of Staff Committee (COSC) is composed of the most senior military personnel in the British Armed Forces, who advise on military matters and the preparation of plans for military operations. The committee comprises the Chief of the Defence Staff, the Vice-Chief of the Defence Staff, and the professional heads of the Royal Navy, British Army, and Royal Air Force, together with the Commander of Strategic Command.

History

The committee was established in 1923 as a subcommittee of the Committee of Imperial Defence, giving the single-service chiefs a collective forum. It played a central role in British strategy during the Second World War, meeting daily at the height of the conflict and working alongside the American Joint Chiefs of Staff through the Combined Chiefs of Staff.

Role

Today the committee advises the Prime Minister and the Secretary of State for Defence on military operations and strategy, and its members hold executive responsibility for their respective services.
