Cigarette Smoking Man
Portrayed by: William B. Davis
Series: The X-Files
First appearance: "Pilot" (1993)

The Cigarette Smoking Man (abbreviated CSM or Cancer Man) is a fictional character and one of the primary antagonists of the American science fiction drama television series The X-Files. Portrayed by Canadian actor William B. Davis, he is a shadowy government conspirator, almost always seen smoking Morley cigarettes, who works to conceal the existence of extraterrestrials and the government's collaboration with them.

Character history

The character first appeared in the series pilot as a silent background figure. Over the course of the series he was revealed to be a member of the Syndicate, a secret consortium brokering a deal with alien colonists, and was eventually disclosed to be the father of FBI agents Fox Mulder and Jeffrey Spender. His backstory, explored in the episode "Musings of a Cigarette Smoking Man", links him to events including the assassinations of John F. Kennedy and Martin Luther King Jr.

Reception

The Cigarette Smoking Man became one of television's most recognizable villains; Davis's understated performance made the character an icon of conspiracy fiction. The character returned in the revival seasons of The X-Files in 2016 and 2018.
