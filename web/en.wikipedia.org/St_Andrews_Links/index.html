St Andrews Links

St Andrews Links in the town of St Andrews, Fife, Scotland, is regarded as the "home of golf". It is one of the oldest golf courses in the world, where the game has been played on the Links since the 15th century. The Links comprise seven public courses, including the famous Old Course, and are managed by the St Andrews Links Trust.

The Old Course

The Old Course is the most famous of the Links courses and the oldest golf course in continuous use. It features 112 bunkers and seven double greens, and its landmarks — the Swilcan Bridge, the Road Hole (the 17th) and Hell Bunker — are among the most recognisable in sport. The course has hosted The Open Championship more often than any other venue, and it is the home of The Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews.

Other courses

The other Links courses are the New Course (1895), the Jubilee Course, the Eden Course, the Strathtyrum Course, the Balgove Course and the nine-hole and par-3 facilities. All are open to the public, with tee times on the Old Course allocated partly by a daily ballot.
