Bad Blood (book)

Bad Blood: Secrets and Lies in a Silicon Valley Startup is a 2018 nonfiction book by journalist John Carreyrou, published by Knopf. It recounts the rise and collapse of Theranos, the blood-testing company founded by Elizabeth Holmes, which claimed to be able to run hundreds of diagnostic tests from a few drops of blood but whose technology did not work as advertised.

Background

Carreyrou, an investigative reporter at The Wall Street Journal, began examining Theranos in 2015 after receiving a tip from medical expert Adam Clapper and hearing concerns from former employees including Tyler Shultz. His series of articles, published despite aggressive legal pressure from the company's lawyers, exposed that Theranos's proprietary analysers could not perform the tests claimed and that most tests were run on conventional machines with diluted samples.

Reception and aftermath

The book became a bestseller and won the 2018 Financial Times and McKinsey Business Book of the Year Award. It has been adapted into the documentary The Inventor and drew further attention alongside the drama series The Dropout. Holmes was convicted of investor fraud in 2022 and sentenced to more than eleven years in prison.
