USS Fletcher (DD-445)

USS Fletcher (DD-445)
Class: Fletcher-class destroyer
Launched: 3 May 1942
Commissioned: 30 June 1942
Decommissioned: 1 August 1969

USS Fletcher (DD-445), named for Admiral Frank Friday Fletcher, was the lead ship of the Fletcher-class destroyers and served in the United States Navy during World War II and later conflicts. She was launched on 3 May 1942 by Federal Shipbuilding and Drydock Company, Kearny, New Jersey, and commissioned on 30 June 1942.

World War II service

Fletcher arrived in the South Pacific in late 1942 and took part in the Guadalcanal campaign, including the Naval Battle of Guadalcanal in November 1942. She fought in the Battle of Tassafaronga, the Solomon Islands campaign, the Battle of Kula Gulf, the landings at Leyte and Lingayen Gulf, and supported the Borneo operations. She received fifteen battle stars for World War II service.

Postwar service

Fletcher served in the Korean War, receiving five more battle stars, and continued in service through the 1960s, including antisubmarine duties. She was decommissioned and stricken on 1 August 1969 and sold for scrap in 1972.
