Väinämöinen

Väinämöinen is the central hero of Finnish mythology and the main character of the Kalevala, Finland's national epic compiled by Elias Lönnrot. A wise old sage and powerful singer-shaman, he was born already aged and wise from the primal waters.

Role in the Kalevala

Väinämöinen shapes the world with his songs, builds the first kantele (from the jawbone of a giant pike), and joins the quest for the magical mill, the Sampo. His singing is so powerful that it can sink rivals—such as the young Joukahainen—into a swamp. At the epic's end he departs Finland by boat, promising to return when needed.

Influence

Väinämöinen is a symbol of Finnish national identity and inspired figures in later fantasy, including aspects of Tolkien's Gandalf and Tom Bombadil.
