The International Science Council (ISC) is an international non-governmental organisation that brings together natural and social science bodies from around the world. It was formed in 2018 by the merger of the International Council for Science (ICSU) and the International Social Science Council (ISSC).

Role

The council acts as a global voice for science, convening members on issues such as sustainability, disaster risk reduction, open science and science policy. It sponsors research programmes, including Future Earth, and represents the scientific community in United Nations processes.

History

ICSU traced its origins to 1931 (and earlier bodies to 1919), while the ISSC was founded in 1952. Their merger reflected the growing need for integrated natural and social science responses to global challenges.
