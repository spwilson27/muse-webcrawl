Kilroy Was Here (album)

Artist: Styx
Released: 22 February 1983
Genre: Progressive rock, pop rock
Label: A&M

Kilroy Was Here is the eleventh studio album by American rock band Styx, released in 1983. A rock opera conceived by Dennis DeYoung, it tells of a future in which rock music is banned and its hero, Robert Orin Charles Kilroy, a rocker imprisoned by the Majority for Musical Morality, escapes disguised as a robot.

Singles and reception

The album produced the hit singles "Mr. Roboto" — famous for its refrain "Dōmo arigatō, Mr. Roboto" — and the ballad "Don't Let It End", and reached the top ten in the United States. The elaborate theatrical tour and the album's concept divided the band and critics, and tensions it exposed contributed to Styx's subsequent split.
