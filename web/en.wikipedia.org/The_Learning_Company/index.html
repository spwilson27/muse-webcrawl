The Learning Company (TLC) was an American educational software company founded in 1980. It became one of the best-known producers of children's learning games for home and school computers, most famously the Reader Rabbit series for early reading.

History

The company grew rapidly during the 1980s home-computer boom and went public before being acquired by SoftKey in 1995 in a hostile takeover. Through subsequent acquisitions it absorbed rival educational publishers including MECC (maker of The Oregon Trail) and Broderbund (maker of Carmen Sandiego). Mattel purchased the combined company in 1999 for about .5 billion — an acquisition widely regarded as one of the most disastrous in corporate history — and sold it the following year at a steep loss. The brand's catalogue passed through further owners, including Riverdeep and Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.

Products

Beyond Reader Rabbit, the company's labels produced titles such as Thinkin' Things, The ClueFinders, and numerous school editions of its franchises.
