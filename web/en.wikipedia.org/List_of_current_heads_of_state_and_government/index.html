List of current heads of state and government

This is a list of current heads of state and heads of government. In most parliamentary systems the two roles are separate: the head of state (a president or monarch) is the ceremonial or constitutional figurehead, while the head of government (usually a prime minister) directs the executive. In presidential systems, such as the United States, one person holds both roles; in semi-presidential systems, power is shared between a president and a prime minister. The list below gives representative examples of major states; office-holders change with elections, appointments and successions.

Long-serving and notable figures have included monarchs such as the Sultan of Brunei and kings of Thailand and Saudi Arabia (where the king is also head of government), and hereditary rulers in the Gulf states. In some states the effective ruler differs from the formal office-holders — for example, party general secretaries in one-party states, or supreme leaders in Iran, where the Supreme Leader outranks the President. Lists of this kind are maintained continuously and are frequently among the most-edited reference pages, reflecting elections and cabinet changes worldwide.
