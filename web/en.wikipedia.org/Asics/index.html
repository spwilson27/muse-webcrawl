Asics Corporation is a Japanese multinational corporation that designs and manufactures athletic footwear and sports equipment. Founded by Kihachiro Onitsuka in Kobe in 1949 as Onitsuka Co., it took the name ASICS in 1977, an acronym of the Latin phrase anima sana in corpore sano — "a healthy soul in a healthy body".

History

Onitsuka began making basketball and running shoes after the Second World War, and his Onitsuka Tiger brand gained fame when its shoes were worn by marathon and Olympic athletes. In the 1960s and early 1970s the company supplied shoes that the American firm Blue Ribbon Sports — later Nike — imported and sold in the United States, until the two companies split in a celebrated dispute. Onitsuka Tiger merged with other firms in 1977 to form ASICS.

Products and technology

Asics is best known for running shoes such as the Gel-Kayano and Gel-Nimbus lines, built around its silicone-based GEL cushioning system introduced in the 1980s. The company also makes shoes for volleyball, wrestling, tennis and other sports, and revived Onitsuka Tiger as a fashion brand.

Operations

Headquartered in Kobe, Asics sells in more than 50 countries and has been an official sponsor of Olympic and Paralympic events. It operates the Institute of Sport Science in Kobe for research into materials and biomechanics.
