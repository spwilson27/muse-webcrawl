The Opel Corsa is a supermini car manufactured and marketed by the German automobile manufacturer Opel since 1982. It has also been sold under a variety of other brands, most notably the British Vauxhall as the Vauxhall Corsa, as well as by Holden in Australasia as the Holden Barina, and Chevrolet in Latin America as the Chevrolet Corsa. The Corsa has been one of Opel's best-selling models, consistently ranking among Europe's most popular small cars.

A sixth-generation Opel Corsa.

History

The first Corsa (codenamed A) debuted in September 1982 at the Paris Motor Show, replacing the Kadett City. It was built in Zaragoza, Spain, and was notable as Opel's first entry into the supermini segment that had been transformed by cars such as the Ford Fiesta and Volkswagen Polo. The boxy hatchback was offered with petrol engines ranging from 1.0 to 1.6 litres.

Generations

Corsa B (1993–2000)

The second generation adopted a much rounder design by Hideo Kodama and became a major export success, with local production in Brazil, Mexico, India, South Africa and China. It spawned saloon, estate and pick-up derivatives in several markets.

Corsa C (2000–2006)

The Corsa C, launched in 2000, grew in size and introduced a more refined interior, improved safety and a range of ECOTEC engines, including a 1.7-litre diesel.

Corsa D (2006–2014)

Developed jointly with Fiat alongside the Grande Punto, the Corsa D shared its platform with the Italian car. A facelift followed in 2011, and the sporty OPC/VXR version offered a turbocharged 1.6-litre engine.

Corsa E (2014–2019)

The fifth generation was an extensive development of the D rather than an all-new car, with a redesigned cabin and new three-cylinder turbo petrol engines.

Corsa F (2019–present)

After Groupe PSA acquired Opel in 2017, the sixth-generation Corsa was switched to the French CMP platform shared with the Peugeot 208. It was the first Corsa offered with a fully electric variant, the Corsa-e, alongside petrol and later hybrid powertrains. Production continued under Stellantis following the 2021 merger.

Sales and recognition

The Corsa has regularly been among the best-selling cars in the United Kingdom and Germany, with total production running into many millions of units across six generations.
