Sagami Trough

The Sagami Trough (Japanese: 相模トラフ) is a submarine trench running offshore central Japan, from the Boso Peninsula area southward past Sagami Bay toward the Izu Islands. It marks the convergent plate boundary where the Philippine Sea Plate subducts beneath the Okhotsk (North American) Plate along the Suruga–Sagami system.

Seismic significance

The trough is the source of great megathrust earthquakes affecting the Tokyo region, including the 1703 Genroku earthquake and the 1923 Great Kantō earthquake, which devastated Tokyo and Yokohama and killed well over 100,000 people. Because of the dense population above it, the Sagami Trough is one of Japan's most closely studied seismic hazards, with historical evidence of ruptures recurring on timescales of a few centuries.
