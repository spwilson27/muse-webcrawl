Destiny USA

Destiny USA is a shopping and entertainment complex in Syracuse, New York, United States. It opened on November 15, 1990, as the Carousel Center, named for the antique carousel installed inside, and was expanded and renamed Destiny USA in 2012. It is among the largest shopping malls in the United States, with roughly 2.4 million square feet (220,000 m²) of retail space.

History

The mall was developed by the Pyramid Companies on a former landfill and oil-storage site along the shore of Onondaga Lake. The original Carousel Center drew shoppers from across upstate New York and Canada. A major expansion, announced in the 2000s and completed in 2012, added outlet stores, restaurants, hotels, and entertainment venues, and the complex was rebranded as Destiny USA, with an emphasis on sustainable features such as rainwater reuse.

Features

Destiny USA combines traditional mall anchors with outlet retail, dining, a comedy club, an indoor go-kart track, a ropes course, an antique carousel dating from 1909, and on-site hotels. It is a major retail destination for central New York.
