Sports merchandise

Sports merchandise consists of products bearing the names, logos, colours and imagery of sports teams, leagues and athletes, sold to fans under licence. Replica kits (jerseys), caps, scarves, trading cards and other memorabilia are major categories.

Licensing

Leagues and clubs grant licences to manufacturers and retailers, either directly or through central licensing programmes, sharing revenue from royalties. Counterfeit merchandise is a persistent problem, prompting enforcement by trademark holders and customs authorities.

Market

Sales of licensed sports merchandise are worth tens of billions of dollars annually in North America alone, with replica apparel among the most visible forms of fan identification and regional pride.
