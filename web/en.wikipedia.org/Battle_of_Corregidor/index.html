Battle of Corregidor

The Battle of Corregidor refers to two engagements for the fortified island of Corregidor at the entrance to Manila Bay in the Philippines: the Japanese conquest of the island in May 1942 and its recapture by American forces in February 1945.

1942

After the fall of Bataan, Japanese artillery and air bombardment pounded the island's forts before an amphibious landing on 5–6 May 1942 forced Lieutenant General Jonathan Wainwright to surrender the remaining US and Filipino forces in the Philippines.

1945

In February 1945, US paratroopers of the 503rd Parachute Regimental Combat Team dropped onto the island's Topside while infantry landed at Bottomside. After fierce fighting in the Malinta Tunnel network, the island was secured, reopening Manila Bay to Allied shipping.
