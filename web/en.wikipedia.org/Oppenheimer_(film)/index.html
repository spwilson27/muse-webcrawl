Oppenheimer (film)

Oppenheimer is a 2023 epic biographical thriller film written, directed and co-produced by Christopher Nolan. Based on the 2005 biography American Prometheus by Kai Bird and Martin J. Sherwin, it chronicles the life of J. Robert Oppenheimer, the theoretical physicist who led the Manhattan Project's Los Alamos Laboratory during the Second World War.

Plot and themes

The film interweaves Oppenheimer's early studies, his direction of the wartime atomic bomb programme and the Trinity test, and his later persecution in the 1954 security hearing, framed against the confirmation hearings of Lewis Strauss. It explores ambition, moral responsibility and the political destruction of a public figure.

Production

Nolan shot the film in IMAX 65 mm and large-format film, using practical effects to recreate the Trinity explosion. It was released on the same day as Barbie, a coincidence that produced the "Barbenheimer" phenomenon and boosted both films at the box office.

Reception and awards

Oppenheimer was a critical and commercial success. At the 96th Academy Awards it won seven Oscars, including Best Picture, Best Director, Best Actor for Cillian Murphy and Best Supporting Actor for Robert Downey Jr.
