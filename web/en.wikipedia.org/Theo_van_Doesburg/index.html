Theo van Doesburg

Theo van Doesburg (30 August 1883 – 7 March 1931), born Christian Emil Marie Küpper, was a Dutch artist, writer, and architect best known as the founder and leader of the De Stijl movement.

De Stijl

In 1917 van Doesburg founded the journal De Stijl, gathering artists including Piet Mondrian, Bart van der Leck, and the architect Gerrit Rietveld. The movement — also called neoplasticism — restricted art to straight lines, right angles, and primary colours plus black, white, and grey.

Elementarism and later work

In the 1920s van Doesburg introduced diagonal compositions he called Elementarism, causing a rupture with Mondrian. He taught at the Bauhaus informally, designed the Café Aubette interiors in Strasbourg, and built his own studio-house at Meudon, Paris, where he died in 1931.
