Federal districts of Russia

The federal districts (Russian: federalnyye okruga) are groupings of the federal subjects of Russia. They are not constituent units of the federation but administrative groupings created to help the federal government oversee the regions.

Overview

The districts were established by President Vladimir Putin in May 2000. Each is headed by a presidential plenipotentiary envoy (polpred) appointed by the President of Russia, whose task is to ensure that federal law and presidential policy are implemented by regional authorities.

List

The eight districts are the Central (Moscow), Northwestern (Saint Petersburg), Southern (Rostov-on-Don), North Caucasian (Pyatigorsk), Volga (Nizhny Novgorod), Ural (Yekaterinburg), Siberian (Novosibirsk), and Far Eastern (Khabarovsk) federal districts. A Crimean Federal District existed from 2014 to 2016 before being merged into the Southern district.
