Boy Better Know

Abbreviation: BBK

Type: Independent record label / collective

Founded: 2005, London, England

Founders: Jme and Skepta

Genre: Grime

Boy Better Know (BBK) is an English grime collective and independent record label founded in 2005 by brothers Jme (Jamie Adenuga) and Skepta (Joseph Adenuga) in Tottenham, north London. Emerging from pirate radio culture, BBK became one of the defining forces of grime's second wave and its international breakthrough in the 2010s.

Members and releases

The crew's core has included Wiley (in its early days), Jme, Skepta, Shorty, Solo 45, Frisco, Jammer, and DJ Maximum, among others. Notable releases include Jme's Famous? (2008) and Integrity> (2015), and Skepta's Mercury Prize-winning album Konnichiwa (2016), released through BBK. The label operated with a do-it-yourself ethos — pressing its own records, selling its own merchandise, and staging its own events — long before grime's mainstream acceptance.

Influence

BBK's independence and branding (including its distinctive logo and T-shirts) made it a model for UK underground music entrepreneurship. In 2017 the collective staged a multi-genre takeover of London's O2 Arena, and its members have collaborated with artists such as Drake, who signed to BBK publicly with a tattoo and frequent collaborations.
