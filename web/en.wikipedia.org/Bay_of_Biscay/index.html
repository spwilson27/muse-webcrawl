Bay of Biscay

LocationNortheast Atlantic, west of France and north of Spain
Major portsBordeaux, Nantes, Bilbao, Santander
Known forRough seas and severe storms

The Bay of Biscay is a gulf of the northeast Atlantic Ocean located south of the Celtic Sea, along the western coast of France and the northern coast of Spain. It is known among mariners for violent storms, especially in winter.

Geography

The bay is bounded by Brittany in the north, the French Atlantic coast in the east, and the Cantabrian coast of Spain in the south. Part of the continental shelf extends far into the bay before dropping into the deep Biscay abyssal plain. Rivers including the Loire and the Garonne flow into it.

History and ecology

The bay has been an important fishing ground for centuries and was a major theatre of naval warfare and the U-boat campaign in the Second World War. Its waters support rich fisheries and significant populations of whales and dolphins.
