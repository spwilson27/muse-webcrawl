Jira is a proprietary issue-tracking and project-management product developed by Atlassian. First released in 2002 as a bug tracker for software teams, it later expanded into agile project management with Scrum and Kanban boards, workflows, and extensive customisation.

Features

Jira organises work into projects and issues, with custom fields, workflows, permissions, dashboards, reports and automation. It integrates with other Atlassian products such as Confluence and Bitbucket, and with a large marketplace of third-party apps. Variants include Jira Software, Jira Service Management and Jira Work Management.

Deployment

Jira is offered as a cloud service and, historically, as self-hosted Server and Data Center editions; Atlassian ended Server support in 2024 as part of its cloud migration strategy. The name derives from "Gojira", the Japanese name for Godzilla.
