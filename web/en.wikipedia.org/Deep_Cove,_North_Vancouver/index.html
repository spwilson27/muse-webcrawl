Deep Cove, North Vancouver

Deep Cove is a neighbourhood and small bay in the District of North Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, located at the eastern edge of the district on the shore of Indian Arm, a branch of Burrard Inlet. The community centres on Gallant Avenue and the Panorama Park waterfront, and is known as a centre for kayaking and hiking.

Geography

Deep Cove sits where Indian Arm turns north into steep, forested fjord-like slopes. The cove is sheltered and its calm waters make it one of the most popular launching points for kayaks, canoes and paddleboards in the Vancouver area.

Recreation

The Baden-Powell Trail's eastern trailhead is in Panorama Park; from it, the hike to Quarry Rock offers a well-known viewpoint over Indian Arm. Panorama Park itself provides a beach, picnic areas and a boat launch, and the village hosts an annual regatta and other community events.

History

The area is part of the traditional territory of the Tsleil-Waututh Nation, whose main community lies nearby at Burrard Inlet. In the early twentieth century Deep Cove developed as a summer cottage destination reached by boat and later by road from North Vancouver.
