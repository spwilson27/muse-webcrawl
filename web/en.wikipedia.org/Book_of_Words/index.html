The Book of Words is a fantasy trilogy by American author Janny Wurts, first published in the 1990s beginning with The Sorcerer's Legacy. The series is set in a world where an ancient ward that held back demonic forces has weakened, and its story weaves together political intrigue, sorcery based on true names and words of power, and the intertwined fates of its protagonists.

Plot

The trilogy follows, among others, Korendir — a sorcerer bound by oath and tragedy — and Elienne, a woman drawn into the struggle over the fate of the wardstone and the realm. Central to the conflict is the Book of Words itself, a grimoire containing names of power whose misuse could unleash the demons it was created to bind.

Author

Janny Wurts (born 1953) is an American fantasy author and illustrator, best known for the long-running Wars of Light and Shadow series and for co-authoring the Empire trilogy with Raymond E. Feist. She traditionally paints the covers for her own books.

Reception

The trilogy is noted for Wurts' ornate, densely descriptive prose and complex characterisation, and it helped establish her reputation before the longer Wars of Light and Shadow sequence became her principal work.
