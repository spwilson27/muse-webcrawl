Azure Dragon

The Azure Dragon (Chinese: 青龍, Qīnglóng), also known as Blue-green Dragon, Green Dragon, or Blue Dragon, is one of the Dragon Kings who represent the mount or chthonic forces of the Five Regions' Highest Deities. It is also one of the Four Symbols of the Chinese constellations, which are the astral representations of the Wufang Shangdi. The Azure Dragon represents the east and the spring season.

Seven Mansions

As with the other Four Symbols, the Azure Dragon corresponds to seven mansions (lunar lodges) of Chinese astronomy in the eastern sky: Horn, Neck, Root, Room, Heart, Tail, and Winnowing Basket. Together the twenty-eight mansions divide the sky along the ecliptic.

Cultural significance

The Azure Dragon appears widely in East Asian culture, in temple murals, flags, and heraldry, and as a guardian deity of the east. In Japan it is known as Seiryū and is one of the four guardian spirits of Kyoto; in Korea it is called Cheongnyong. It should not be confused with the Yellow Dragon, which is associated with the centre and with the Emperor of China.
