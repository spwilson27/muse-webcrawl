Earth observation satellite

An Earth observation satellite is a satellite specifically designed to observe Earth from orbit, gathering data on the atmosphere, oceans and land surface for science, environmental monitoring, weather forecasting and mapping.

Instruments and orbits

Earth observation satellites commonly operate in Sun-synchronous low Earth orbits, imaging the surface at consistent local solar times. Instruments range from visible and infrared cameras through radar imagers and microwave sounders. Data are used in remote sensing applications such as tracking deforestation, measuring ice-sheet change, and monitoring natural disasters.

Notable systems

The Landsat programme (since 1972), ESA's Sentinel satellites under the Copernicus programme, and commercial high-revisit constellations of CubeSats are among the most widely used sources of Earth observation data.
