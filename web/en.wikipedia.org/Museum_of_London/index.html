Museum of London

The Museum of London documents the history of London from prehistoric times to the present day. It opened in 1976 at the Barbican, in the City of London, as part of the merger of the Guildhall Museum and the London Museum.

Collections

Holdings include archaeological finds from Roman Londinium, the Lord Mayor's State Coach, reconstructions of Victorian streets and displays on the Great Fire of London, the Suffragettes and the 2012 Olympics. The museum also oversees the Museum of London Docklands in Canary Wharf.

Relocation

The Barbican site closed in December 2022 ahead of a move to a new home in the former Smithfield Market buildings in Farringdon, where the museum is due to reopen under the name London Museum. The new site offers much larger gallery and storage space, including a gallery within the historic market halls.
