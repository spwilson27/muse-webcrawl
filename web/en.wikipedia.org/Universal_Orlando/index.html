Universal Orlando

Universal Orlando Resort, often shortened to Universal Orlando, is an American theme park and entertainment resort complex in Orlando, Florida. It is the flagship location and largest property operated by Universal Destinations & Experiences, a unit of Comcast's NBCUniversal. The resort is one of the most visited in the world, drawing tens of millions of guests per year across its theme parks, water park, hotels, and the CityWalk dining and entertainment district.

History

The resort opened on June 7, 1990, with Universal Studios Florida, a working studio and theme park themed to film and television. A major expansion in 1999 added Universal Islands of Adventure, the CityWalk entertainment complex, and the first on-site hotels, converting the property into a multi-day destination. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, opened in Islands of Adventure in 2010 and expanded with Diagon Alley in Universal Studios Florida in 2014, was widely credited with transforming attendance. Universal's Volcano Bay water park opened in 2017, and the fourth theme park, Universal Epic Universe, opened in May 2025.

Attractions and areas

Universal Studios Florida features areas such as Diagon Alley, Springfield (home of The Simpsons Ride), and Minion Land, while Islands of Adventure contains themed islands including Jurassic Park, Marvel Super Hero Island, Seuss Landing, and Hogsmeade. Epic Universe comprises five lands, including Super Nintendo World and the Wizarding World's Ministry of Magic. The resort is served by numerous on-site hotels operated in partnership with Loews Hotels.
