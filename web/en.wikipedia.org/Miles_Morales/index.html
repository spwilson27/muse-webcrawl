Miles Gonzalo Morales is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Brian Michael Bendis and artist Sara Pichelli, he debuted in 2011 as the Spider-Man of the Ultimate Marvel universe (Earth-1610), succeeding Peter Parker after his death, and later became a permanent part of the main Marvel continuity (Earth-616).

Fictional biography

Miles is a Brooklyn teenager of African-American and Puerto Rican descent, the son of Jefferson Davis and nurse Rio Morales. Bitten by a genetically engineered spider stolen from Oscorp by his uncle Aaron Davis (the criminal Prowler), Miles gains spider-powers plus two abilities Peter Parker lacks: a paralysing "venom strike" bio-electrical burst and near-invisibility camouflage. After witnessing Peter Parker's death fighting the Green Goblin, a guilt-stricken Miles takes up the mantle with the blessing of figures such as Spider-Woman.

Reception and media

The character's introduction attracted major media attention as a landmark in superhero diversity. Miles headlined the Academy Award-winning animated film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018), voiced by Shameik Moore, and its sequels Across the Spider-Verse (2023) and the forthcoming Beyond the Spider-Verse. He stars in Insomniac Games' Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales (2020) and appears throughout Marvel animation and publishing as a founding member of the modern Champions.
