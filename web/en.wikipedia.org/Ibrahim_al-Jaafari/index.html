Ibrahim al-Jaafari

Born 25 March 1947, Karbala
Office Prime Minister of Iraq (2005–2006)
Party Islamic Dawa Party

Ibrahim al-Eshaiker al-Jaafari (born 25 March 1947) is an Iraqi politician who served as Prime Minister of Iraq in the Iraqi Transitional Government from 2005 to 2006. A physician by training, he was a leading figure in the Islamic Dawa Party.

Early life and exile

Born in Karbala, al-Jaafari studied medicine at the University of Mosul. He joined the Dawa Party in 1968 and fled Iraq in 1980 following the Ba'athist regime's crackdown on the party, living in exile in Iran and later London, where he led the party's work abroad.

After 2003

Following the 2003 invasion of Iraq, he served as one of the two vice presidents in the interim government of 2004. After the January 2005 elections, the United Iraqi Alliance selected him as prime minister of the transitional government. He later served as Iraq's Minister of Foreign Affairs from 2014 to 2018.
