Regions of Japan

The regions of Japan are traditional geographic divisions of the country, not formal administrative units. Japan is formally divided into 47 prefectures, which are commonly grouped into eight regions.

The eight regions

From north to south the regions are: Hokkaido; Tōhoku (northern Honshu); Kantō (including Tokyo); Chūbu (central Honshu, sometimes subdivided into Hokuriku, Kōshin'etsu, and Tōkai); Kansai (also called Kinki, including Osaka and Kyoto); Chūgoku (western Honshu); Shikoku; and Kyushu (often grouped with Okinawa). Regional groupings are used by government agencies, businesses, weather services, and sports leagues for organizational purposes.
