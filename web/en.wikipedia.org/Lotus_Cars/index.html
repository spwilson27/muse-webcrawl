Lotus Cars is a British manufacturer of sports and racing cars, noted for its lightweight designs and motorsport heritage. The company was founded by engineer Colin Chapman in 1952 and has been based at Hethel, Norfolk, since 1966.

History

Chapman built his first trials car in 1948 and formalised the business as Lotus four years later. Road cars such as the Elite, Elan, Esprit and Elise made the marque's reputation for agility through low weight — summed up in Chapman's maxim "simplify, then add lightness". Financial difficulties through the 1980s and 1990s saw ownership pass through General Motors and other hands; in 2017 the Chinese group Geely took a majority stake and funded a new range, including the electric Evija hypercar, the Emira sports car and the Eletre SUV.

Racing heritage

Team Lotus, the company's racing arm, won seven Formula One Constructors' Championships and six Drivers' Championships between 1963 and 1978, with drivers including Jim Clark, Graham Hill, Jochen Rindt, Emerson Fittipaldi and Mario Andretti.
