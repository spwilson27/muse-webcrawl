The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom is a 2024 action-adventure game developed by Nintendo and Grezzo and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. It is the first mainline Legend of Zelda game with Princess Zelda as the sole playable protagonist.

Gameplay

After Link is swallowed by a mysterious rift, Zelda — aided by the fairy-like being Tri and the Tri Rod — must rescue him and save Hyrule. Zelda cannot wield a sword directly at first; instead she creates "echoes", magical imitations of objects and monsters, to solve puzzles and fight, with a temporary swordfighter form available for direct combat. The game uses the top-down perspective and diorama-like art style of the 2019 remake of Link's Awakening.

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, with praise for its inventive echo mechanics and criticism of occasional frame-rate issues and menu-heavy echo selection. It sold several million copies within its first months.
