UNICEF

UNICEF, originally the United Nations International Children's Emergency Fund and now officially the United Nations Children's Fund, is an agency of the United Nations responsible for providing humanitarian and developmental aid to children worldwide.

UNICEF
Founded11 December 1946
HeadquartersNew York City
FocusChild health, education, protection

Activities

UNICEF works in over 190 countries on immunisation, nutrition, clean water, education, HIV/AIDS, and protection from violence and exploitation. It is the world's largest buyer of vaccines and was a partner in COVAX.

History

Created to aid children after the Second World War, it became a permanent UN agency in 1953 and won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1965. Its work is guided by the Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989).
