United States Air Force

The United States Air Force (USAF) is the air service branch of the United States Armed Forces and one of the eight uniformed services of the United States. Originally established as the Army Air Forces within the US Army during World War II, it became an independent service on 18 September 1947 with the implementation of the National Security Act of 1947. It is the largest and most technologically advanced air force in the world, with over 300,000 active-duty airmen, about 5,000 crewed aircraft and hundreds of intercontinental ballistic missiles, operating under the motto Aim High.

History

The service's roots run through the Aeronautical Division of the Signal Corps (1907), the Army Air Service (1918) and the Army Air Forces (1941). USAAF strategic bombing in World War II — including the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki delivered by the 509th Composite Group — made the case for independence, achieved in 1947 with Stuart Symington as the first Secretary and General Carl Spaatz as the first Chief of Staff. The Air Force built the nuclear triad's air and missile legs, fought the air wars over Korea, Vietnam, and the Persian Gulf, and pioneered air refuelling, strategic airlift, airborne warning and control, stealth (the F-117, B-2 and F-22), precision-guided munitions and remotely piloted aircraft.

Structure and missions

The Air Force is organised into major commands, including Air Combat Command, Air Mobility Command, Air Force Global Strike Command (bombers and ICBMs), Air Force Special Operations Command, Pacific Air Forces and United States Air Forces in Europe. Its declared core missions are air superiority, global integrated intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, rapid global mobility, global strike, and command and control. In 2019, space operations were split off to form the independent United States Space Force, which remains within the Department of the Air Force.

Personnel

Airmen serve under a separate rank and grade structure; the senior enlisted grade is Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force. The Air Force Academy in Colorado Springs commissions officers, alongside ROTC and Officer Training School. The service's dress, its rank insignia and its song — "The U.S. Air Force" ("Off we go into the wild blue yonder") — distinguish it from the other branches.
