Mimasaka (美作市, Mimasaka-shi) is a city located in Okayama Prefecture, Japan, in the mountainous northeast of the prefecture, bordering Hyōgo and Tottori prefectures.

History

The modern city was established on March 31, 2005, through the merger of several towns and villages including Katsuta, Ōhara, Sakutō, Aida, and Mimasaka town. The area corresponds to part of the historical Mimasaka Province.

Geography and attractions

Mimasaka is largely rural and forested, drained by the Yoshii River. It is known as the birthplace of the swordsman Miyamoto Musashi (in Ōhara), and for hot springs such as Yunogō Onsen, one of the three famous springs of the former province.
