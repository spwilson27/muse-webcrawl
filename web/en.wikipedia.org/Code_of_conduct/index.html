Code of conduct

A code of conduct is a set of rules outlining the norms, responsibilities and proper practices of an individual party or organisation. Codes of conduct are common in workplaces, professions, schools and online communities, and may cover matters such as conflicts of interest, confidentiality, harassment, gifts and compliance with law.

Purpose and enforcement

Unlike a code of ethics, which states broad values, a code of conduct usually prescribes specific behaviours and the disciplinary consequences of violations. Organisations adopt codes to signal standards to employees, members, regulators and the public. In open-source software, community codes of conduct — such as the Contributor Covenant, first released in 2014 — set expectations for participation in project spaces.

Criticisms of codes of conduct include that they may be vaguely worded, selectively enforced, or used as public-relations instruments rather than genuine constraints.
