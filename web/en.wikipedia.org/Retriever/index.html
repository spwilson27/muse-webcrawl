Retriever

A retriever is a type of gun dog that retrieves game for a hunter. Generally gun dogs are divided into three major classifications: retrievers, flushing spaniels, and pointing breeds. Retrievers were bred primarily to retrieve birds or other prey and return them to the hunter without damage. Although spaniels and some pointing breeds routinely retrieve game, and many retrievers are skilled in finding game, retrievers are distinguished in that non-slip retrieval is their primary function.

A Labrador Retriever at work

As a result, retriever breeds are bred for a soft mouth and a great willingness to please, learn, and obey. A soft mouth refers to the willingness of the dog to carry game in its mouth without biting into it. "Hard mouth" is a serious fault in a gun dog, since it renders the game unfit for the table.

Breeds

Recognised retriever breeds include:

  • Labrador Retriever – the most popular retriever breed in many countries, in black, yellow and chocolate.
  • Golden Retriever – developed in Scotland in the 19th century.
  • Chesapeake Bay Retriever – a powerful American breed developed for waterfowl hunting in cold water.
  • Curly-Coated Retriever – one of the oldest retriever breeds, from England.
  • Flat-Coated Retriever – a cheerful British breed with a glossy black or liver coat.
  • Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever – the smallest retriever, from Canada, which lures waterfowl within range by "tolling".

Skills

Retrievers are expected to remain quietly under control ("steady"), watch where shot birds fall ("marking"), and retrieve them promptly on command. In waterfowl hunting they must swim, often in cold water, and their dense, water-repellent double coats protect them. Trained retrievers also perform "blind retrieves" of birds they did not see fall, directed by the handler's whistle and hand signals.

As pets and service dogs

Because of their temperament, retrievers — Labradors and Golden Retrievers in particular — are among the most popular family dogs and are widely used as guide dogs, assistance dogs and search-and-rescue dogs.
