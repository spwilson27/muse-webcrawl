Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde

AuthorRobert Louis Stevenson
Published1886
GenreGothic novella

Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde is an 1886 Gothic novella by the Scottish author Robert Louis Stevenson. It follows the London lawyer Gabriel John Utterson as he investigates the connection between his friend, the respectable Dr Henry Jekyll, and the brutal, dwarfish Edward Hyde.

Plot

Utterson is disturbed that Jekyll has made a will leaving everything to Hyde, who has committed acts of violence. After Hyde murders the MP Sir Danvers Carew, Jekyll withdraws from society. Utterson and the butler Poole finally break into Jekyll's laboratory to find Hyde's body — and Jekyll's confession: Jekyll had devised a potion to separate the evil side of his nature, which manifested as Hyde, but the transformations became involuntary until his supply of a key ingredient ran out.

Influence

The novella became a defining work of Gothic fiction, and "Jekyll and Hyde" entered the language as a byword for a split or dual personality.
