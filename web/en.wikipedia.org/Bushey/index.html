Bushey
Town in Hertfordshire, England
District: Hertsmere
Population: ~28,000 (with Bushey Heath)

Bushey is a town in the Hertsmere borough of Hertfordshire, England, immediately north of London and adjacent to Watford. Together with neighbouring Bushey Heath, it forms a largely residential commuter settlement.

History

Bushey was recorded in the Domesday Book as Bissei. The parish church of St James dates to the 13th century. In the 19th century the artist Hubert von Herkomer established an art school and built Lululaund castle in the village, making Bushey a centre of an artists' colony. The railway arrived at Bushey station in the 20th century, encouraging suburban growth.

Transport

Bushey railway station provides London Overground and West Midlands Trains services to London Euston and Watford Junction. The town lies close to the A41 and the M1 motorway.

Notable features

The Bushey Museum and Art Gallery preserves the legacy of Herkomer and local artists. The area was also home to film and television studios and to the Royal Masonic School for Girls in nearby Rickmansworth.
