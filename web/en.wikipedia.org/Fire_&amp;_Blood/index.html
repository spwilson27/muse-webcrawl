Fire & Blood
Author: George R. R. Martin
Published: 20 November 2018
Series: A Song of Ice and Fire (companion volume)
Illustrator: Doug Wheatley

Fire & Blood is a 2018 fantasy history book by George R. R. Martin, presented as a chronicle of House Targaryen in Westeros written by the fictional Archmaester Gyldayn. It covers events from Aegon the Conqueror's conquest through the reign of Aegon III, roughly the first 150 years of Targaryen rule.

Contents

The narrative includes the reigns of Jaehaerys I and Viserys I and culminates in the Dance of the Dragons, the Targaryen civil war between Rhaenyra and Aegon II. The book reuses and expands material previously published in the novellas The Princess and the Queen and The Rogue Prince.

Adaptation

The book serves as the principal source for the HBO series House of the Dragon (2022– ), which dramatises the Dance of the Dragons.
