Film4 is a British free-to-air television channel owned by Channel Four Television Corporation, dedicated to showing films. It launched on 1 November 1998 as a subscription service called FilmFour, and became free-to-air in July 2006 shortly before moving to Freeview.

History

The channel began as part of Channel 4's expansion into pay television. After relaunching as a free channel under the Film4 name, it built a schedule of British, independent, and world cinema alongside mainstream titles, and produces an annual season of themed programming. Film4's sister production company, Film4 Productions, has backed films such as Slumdog Millionaire, 12 Years a Slave, and The Favourite.

Programming

Film4 broadcasts daily from late morning, with curated seasons, director retrospectives, and horror strands such as FrightFest seasons. A timeshift channel, Film4 +1, is also available.
