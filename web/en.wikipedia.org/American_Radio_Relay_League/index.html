American Radio Relay League
Abbreviation: ARRL
Formation: 1914
Headquarters: Newington, Connecticut
Type: Nonprofit organization

The American Radio Relay League (ARRL) is the largest membership association of amateur radio enthusiasts in the United States. Founded in 1914 by Hiram Percy Maxim, the ARRL is a noncommercial organization of radio amateurs that promotes amateur radio, advocates for spectrum access, and represents the interests of its members before the Federal Communications Commission and international bodies.

Activities

The ARRL publishes the monthly journal QST, sponsors operating contests and awards such as the DX Century Club, runs the annual Field Day emergency-preparedness exercise, and supports the Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES). Its headquarters station, W1AW, broadcasts bulletins and code practice.

History

Maxim founded the league to organize relay networks that passed messages across the country by chains of amateur stations. The ARRL helped secure the restoration of amateur privileges after World War I and has shaped amateur regulation ever since.
