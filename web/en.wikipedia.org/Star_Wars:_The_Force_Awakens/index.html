Star Wars: The Force Awakens (also known as Star Wars: Episode VII – The Force Awakens) is a 2015 American epic space opera film produced by Lucasfilm and Bad Robot and directed by J. J. Abrams. It is the first instalment of the Star Wars sequel trilogy and the seventh in the "Skywalker saga", set 30 years after Return of the Jedi (1983).

Plot

Three decades after the fall of the Empire, the First Order has risen from its ashes and seeks to destroy the Republic. Luke Skywalker, the last Jedi, has vanished. A scavenger named Rey, a defecting stormtrooper named Finn and the droid BB-8 — carrying a map fragment to Luke's location — join Han Solo and Chewbacca in the Resistance's fight against the First Order and its dark warrior Kylo Ren, culminating in an assault on the planet-destroying Starkiller Base.

Production

The film entered development after Disney acquired Lucasfilm in 2012. Abrams emphasised practical effects and real locations alongside CGI, filming principally at Pinewood Studios in England from May to November 2014. John Williams returned to compose the score.

Reception

The film grossed over $2 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2015 and, at the time, the third-highest-grossing film of all time. Critics praised its action, performances and emotional weight, though some noted its narrative debt to the original 1977 film. It received five Academy Award nominations. Sequels The Last Jedi (2017) and The Rise of Skywalker (2019) followed.
