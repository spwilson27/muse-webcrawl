Magic Kingdom

Location: Walt Disney World Resort, Bay Lake, Florida, United States
Opened: 1 October 1971
Theme: Fairy tales and Disney characters
Icon: Cinderella Castle

Magic Kingdom Park is a theme park at the Walt Disney World Resort in Bay Lake, Florida. It opened on 1 October 1971 as the first of four theme parks built at the resort, and it was the only one completed during Walt Disney's lifetime to his direct design influence, being modelled on Disneyland in California.

Lands

The park is laid out like a wheel, with Cinderella Castle at the hub and six themed "lands" radiating outward: Main Street, U.S.A.; Adventureland; Frontierland; Liberty Square; Fantasyland; and Tomorrowland. A network of utilidor tunnels beneath the park allows staff and supplies to move unseen by guests.

Attractions

Classic attractions include Space Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, the Haunted Mansion, It's a Small World, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, and the Seven Dwarfs Mine Train. The park's icon, Cinderella Castle, stands 189 feet (58 m) tall and serves as the centrepiece for nightly fireworks shows.

Attendance

Magic Kingdom is regularly the most visited theme park in the world, drawing roughly 17 million guests in a typical recent year. It anchors the Walt Disney World Resort alongside Epcot, Disney's Hollywood Studios, and Disney's Animal Kingdom.
