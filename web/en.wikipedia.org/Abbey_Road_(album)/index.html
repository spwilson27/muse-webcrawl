Abbey Road (album)

Abbey Road
Artist: The Beatles
Released: 26 September 1969
Recorded: February–August 1969, EMI Studios, London
Genre: Rock
Label: Apple

Abbey Road is the eleventh studio album by the English rock band the Beatles, released on 26 September 1969. It is named after the London street where EMI Studios (later Abbey Road Studios) is located. Although it was the last album the group recorded together, it was released before Let It Be (1970).

Music

The album contains George Harrison's "Something" and "Here Comes the Sun", John Lennon's "Come Together", Ringo Starr's "Octopus's Garden", and a climactic 16-minute medley closing side two, ending with "The End" and the fragment "Her Majesty". Producer George Martin and engineer Geoff Emerick made prominent use of the Moog synthesiser and solid-state mixing desk.

Cover and legacy

The cover photograph of the band walking across the zebra crossing outside the studio is one of the most famous and imitated images in popular music. Abbey Road is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of all time and remains one of the best-selling albums worldwide.
