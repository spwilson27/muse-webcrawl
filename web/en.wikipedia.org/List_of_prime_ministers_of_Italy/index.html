List of prime ministers of Italy

The prime minister of Italy, officially president of the Council of Ministers, heads the government. The office began with Camillo Benso, Count of Cavour, in 1861 at unification.

  • Camillo Cavour — 1861, first
  • Giovanni Giolitti — several terms, liberal era
  • Benito Mussolini — 1922–1943
  • Alcide De Gasperi — 1945–1953
  • Aldo Moro — 1960s–1970s
  • Giulio Andreotti — several terms
  • Bettino Craxi — 1983–1987
  • Silvio Berlusconi — 1994, 2001–2006, 2008–2011
  • Romano Prodi — 1996–1998, 2006–2008
  • Matteo Renzi — 2014–2016
  • Mario Draghi — 2021–2022
  • Giorgia Meloni — from 2022
