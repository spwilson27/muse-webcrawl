The Valencian Community (Valencian: Comunitat Valenciana; Spanish: Comunidad Valenciana) is an autonomous community of Spain on the Mediterranean coast. Its capital and largest city is Valencia.

Geography and population

The community covers the provinces of Alicante, Castellón, and Valencia and has a population of about five million. Its coastline, the Costa Blanca and Costa del Azahar, is a major tourist destination, while inland areas are more mountainous.

Culture and language

Valencian, a variety of Catalan, is co-official with Spanish. The region is known for the Falles festival in Valencia, the dish paella, which originated in the Albufera area, and the historic Kingdom of Valencia founded by James I of Aragon in the 13th century.
