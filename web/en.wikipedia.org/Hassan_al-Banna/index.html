Hassan Ahmed Abdel Rahman Mohammed al-Banna (17 October 1906 – 12 February 1949), known as Hassan al-Banna, was an Egyptian schoolteacher and religious leader who founded the Muslim Brotherhood (Jamā'at al-Ikhwān al-Muslimīn) in 1928, one of the largest and most influential Islamist movements of the modern era. He served as its first Supreme Guide.

Early life

Al-Banna was born in Mahmoudiyah, northwest of Cairo, the son of a watch repairer and local imam who had studied at al-Azhar. Deeply religious from youth and influenced by Sufism, he trained as a teacher at the Dār al-'Ulūm in Cairo, graduating in 1927, and took a post teaching Arabic in Ismailia, a city on the Suez Canal dominated by the British-controlled canal company and foreign troops.

Founding the Brotherhood

In March 1928, al-Banna and six others founded the Society of the Muslim Brothers in Ismailia. The movement combined religious preaching, education, social services, and political activism, calling for a return to Islam as a comprehensive way of life and resistance to British colonial influence and Western cultural dominance. Under his charismatic leadership the Brotherhood grew explosively through the 1930s and 1940s, building schools, clinics, businesses, and a paramilitary wing, with branches across Egypt and beyond.

Ideas

Al-Banna's writings, including his collected epistles (Majmū'at Rasā'il), set out a program of gradual Islamization of society and government based on the Qur'an and Sunnah. He framed Islam as "creed and worship, homeland and nationality, religion and state, spirituality and action, Qur'an and sword."

Death and legacy

After the Brotherhood was implicated in political violence — including the assassination of Prime Minister Mahmoud El Nokrashy Pasha in December 1948 — the government dissolved the organization. Al-Banna was shot in Cairo on 12 February 1949, an assassination widely attributed to government agents. The Brotherhood survived underground and through periods of repression and participation, and movements inspired by it spread across the Muslim world.
