Pagani Utopia

Pagani Utopia
Manufacturer: Pagani
Production: 2023–present
Assembly: San Cesario sul Panaro, Italy
Designer: Horacio Pagani
Engine: 6.0 L Mercedes-AMG twin-turbo V12
Power: 864 hp (635 kW)
Predecessor: Pagani Huayra

The Pagani Utopia is a mid-engine sports car produced by the Italian manufacturer Pagani. It was developed under the codename "C10" and unveiled at the Museo Nazionale Scienza e Tecnologia Leonardo da Vinci in Milan on September 12, 2022. It is the third car model by Pagani, succeeding the Huayra, which itself succeeded the Zonda.

Unlike many modern hypercars, the Utopia eschews hybrid assistance and emphasises purity of driving: it is powered by a 5,980 cc Mercedes-AMG 6.0-litre twin-turbocharged V12 producing 864 PS (635 kW; 852 hp) at 6,000 rpm and 1,100 N⋅m of torque. Buyers may choose between a seven-speed manual transmission — a rarity in the segment — or a seven-speed automated manual by Xtrac. The monocoque chassis is built from Pagani's Carbo-Titanium HP62 G2 and Carbo-Triax materials, contributing to a dry weight of about 1,280 kg. Production of the coupé is limited to 99 units.

Utopia Roadster

An open-top Utopia Roadster followed in 2024, limited to 130 units, using a removable carbon-fibre hard top and achieving the same performance as the coupé with no weight penalty beyond a small increase.
