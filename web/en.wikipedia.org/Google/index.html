Google LLC is an American multinational technology company focusing on artificial intelligence, search engine technology, online advertising, cloud computing, computer software, quantum computing, e-commerce, and consumer electronics. It has been referred to as "the most powerful company in the world" and is one of the world's most valuable brands due to its market dominance, data collection, and technological advantages in AI.

[ Image placeholder: The Googleplex headquarters in Mountain View, California ]

Google was founded in September 1998 by American computer scientists Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were PhD students at Stanford University, developing the PageRank algorithm for ranking web pages. The company grew rapidly on the strength of Google Search, now the world's most used search engine, and expanded through products such as Gmail (2004), Google Maps (2005), and the Chrome browser (2008), and acquisitions including YouTube (2006) and Android (2005). In 2015, Google was reorganised as a wholly owned subsidiary of the holding company Alphabet Inc.

Products and services

Google's services are used by billions of people and are mostly free, funded by advertising through Google Ads. Its portfolio includes Search, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Maps, Google Earth, Google Street View, Chrome, Android, YouTube, Google Cloud Platform, and the Gemini family of AI models. Google is also the developer of the Go and Dart programming languages and a major contributor to open-source software.

History

The name "Google" is a play on "googol", the number 10100. After an initial public offering in 2004, Google's market value soared; by 2020 it was one of the "Big Five" American technology companies alongside Amazon, Apple, Meta, and Microsoft. Eric Schmidt served as CEO from 2001 to 2011, followed by Larry Page, and since 2019 by Sundar Pichai, who also leads Alphabet.

Criticism

Google has faced criticism and legal action over privacy, tax avoidance, censorship, search-result manipulation, and antitrust. In 2024 a US federal court ruled that Google held an illegal monopoly in online search, one of several major competition cases against the company worldwide.
