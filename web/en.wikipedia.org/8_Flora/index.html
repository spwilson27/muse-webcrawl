8 Flora

Flora (minor planet designation: 8 Flora) is a large, bright asteroid in the inner asteroid belt. It is the nearest large asteroid: no asteroid comes closer to the Sun with a similarly large size. It is the parent body of the Flora family of asteroids and the largest remaining piece of a battered protoplanet that formed the family through collisions.

Flora was discovered by J. R. Hind on 18 October 1847, the second asteroid he discovered. Its name was suggested by John Herschel after Flora, the Roman goddess of flowers and gardens. With a mean diameter of roughly 140 kilometres, it is one of the larger S-type asteroids and reflects a comparatively high proportion of the sunlight falling on it.
