Central Baptist College (CBC) is a private Christian liberal arts college in Conway, Arkansas. It is affiliated with the Baptist Missionary Association of America and serves students from Arkansas and surrounding states.

History

The college was founded in 1952 as Conway Baptist Bible Institute. It later became Central Baptist College and expanded its academic offerings beyond ministerial training to include a range of liberal arts and professional degree programs.

Academics and athletics

CBC offers associate and bachelor's degrees in fields such as business, education, ministry, psychology and the natural sciences. Its athletic teams, the Mustangs, compete in the NAIA, principally in the American Midwest Conference.
