The canton of Fribourg (French: Canton de Fribourg; German: Kanton Freiburg) is one of the 26 cantons of Switzerland, located in the west of the country. Its capital is the city of Fribourg.

Geography

The canton is drained by the Saane/Sarine river and borders Lake Neuchâtel. It is officially bilingual: about two-thirds of its roughly 325,000 inhabitants speak French and most of the rest German.

History

Fribourg joined the Swiss Confederation in 1481 after the Burgundian Wars. It remained a Catholic stronghold during the Reformation, and was a leading member of the Sonderbund defeated in 1847.
