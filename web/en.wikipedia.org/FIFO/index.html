FIFO

FIFO is an acronym for first in, first out, a method for organizing the manipulation of a data structure (often, specifically a data buffer) where the oldest (first) entry, or "head" of the queue, is processed first. The term is also used in accounting for inventory valuation and in other fields.

Computing

In computer science, FIFO describes the principle of a queue processing technique or servicing conflicting demands by ordering process by first-come, first-served (FCFS) behaviour: what comes in first is handled first, what comes in next waits until the first is finished, etc. FIFO queues are used in operating systems scheduling, buffering, and inter-process communication; in Unix-like systems, a named pipe is called a FIFO.

Accounting

The FIFO method of inventory valuation assumes that the first goods purchased are the first goods sold, so the ending inventory consists of the most recently purchased goods.
