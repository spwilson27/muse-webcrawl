Hapoel Hatzair

Hapoel Hatzair ("The Young Worker") was a Zionist socialist organisation founded in Ottoman Palestine in 1905, one of the founding movements of Labour Zionism.

History

Founded by young Jewish immigrants of the Second Aliyah, including A. D. Gordon, its spiritual mentor, Hapoel Hatzair advocated the "conquest of labour" — Jewish manual and agricultural work — and the revival of Hebrew culture, while opposing Marxist class-struggle doctrine in favour of national-social ideals.

Legacy

In 1930 Hapoel Hatzair merged with Ahdut HaAvoda to form Mapai, the dominant party in pre-state Jewish Palestine and early Israel. See also Labour Zionism and Mapai.
