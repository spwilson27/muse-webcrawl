List of fallacies

A fallacy is the use of invalid or faulty reasoning in the construction of an argument that may appear convincing. Fallacies are generally divided into formal fallacies, which involve errors in logical form, and informal fallacies, which involve errors in content or context.

Formal fallacies

FallacyDescription
Affirming the consequentAssuming that because the consequent is true, the antecedent must be true
Denying the antecedentAssuming that because the antecedent is false, the consequent must be false
Fallacy of four termsA syllogism that covertly uses four distinct terms

Informal fallacies

FallacyDescription
Ad hominemAttacking the person instead of the argument
Straw manMisrepresenting an opponent's position to make it easier to attack
Appeal to authorityClaiming truth because an authority says so, without other evidence
False dilemmaPresenting only two options when more exist
Slippery slopeClaiming a small step will inevitably lead to an extreme outcome
Circular reasoning (begging the question)Assuming the conclusion within the premises
Red herringIntroducing an irrelevant topic to divert attention
Bandwagon appealArguing something is true because many people believe it
False causeAssuming correlation implies causation
No true ScotsmanRedefining a group to exclude counterexamples
