Planned community

A planned community, planned city, or planned town is a settlement that was carefully planned from its inception and is typically constructed on previously undeveloped land. This contrasts with settlements that evolve in a more ad hoc fashion, growing organically over centuries. Planned communities may be built by governments — as new capitals such as Canberra, Brasília, Islamabad, and Abuja, or as British New Towns after the Second World War — or by private developers, as in the master-planned suburbs of Irvine, California, and Reston, Virginia.

Master-planned communities lay out housing, roads, commerce, and open space in advance.

History

Planned settlements are as old as civilization: the grid-planned cities of the Indus Valley, Greek colonies laid out on Hippodamian plans, Roman military towns, and the Spanish colonial cities of the Laws of the Indies all followed deliberate plans. In the modern era, Ebenezer Howard's garden city movement (from 1898) proposed self-contained towns surrounded by greenbelts, realized at Letchworth and Welwyn Garden City in England. The British New Towns Act 1946 led to the designation of more than twenty new towns, including Milton Keynes, to relieve overcrowding in London and other large cities.

Characteristics and criticism

Planned communities typically feature predetermined land-use zoning, road hierarchies, utilities, and amenities, and are often governed by master developers or homeowners' associations. Advocates cite coherent design, infrastructure efficiency, and balanced provision of housing, jobs, and green space. Critics argue that such communities can be sterile, socially homogeneous, car-dependent, and lacking the incremental adaptability of organically grown towns; others note the success of many planned cities in housing large populations quickly.

Examples

Notable examples include Chandigarh and Navi Mumbai in India, Putrajaya in Malaysia, Seaside and Celebration in Florida (associated with New Urbanism), Poundbury in England, and the purpose-built capitals listed above.
