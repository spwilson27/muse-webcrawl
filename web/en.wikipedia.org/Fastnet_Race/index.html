Fastnet Race

The Fastnet Race is a biennial offshore yacht race organized by the Royal Ocean Racing Club. First held in 1925, the course traditionally ran from Cowes on the Isle of Wight, around Fastnet Rock off southern Ireland, and back to Plymouth — about 608 nautical miles (the finish moved to Cherbourg in 2021).

The 1979 race was struck by a severe storm: 15 sailors died, 24 yachts were abandoned, and the disaster prompted major reforms in offshore safety. The race now attracts hundreds of entries, from amateur crews to professional maxi yachts, as part of the Admiral's Cup and Rolex series.
