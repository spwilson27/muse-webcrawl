Lockheed Corporation

The Lockheed Corporation was an American aerospace manufacturer founded in 1926 by Allan Lockheed. In 1995 it merged with Martin Marietta to form Lockheed Martin.

History

Based in Burbank, California, Lockheed built the Vega and Electra aircraft, then wartime types including the P-38 Lightning and the Hudson bomber. Its Skunk Works, led by Kelly Johnson, produced the U-2 spyplane, the SR-71 Blackbird and the F-117 stealth fighter.

Aircraft

Famous products include the Constellation airliner, the C-130 Hercules transport, the L-1011 TriStar wide-body airliner, and the F-104 Starfighter.

Merger

After the Cold War, defence consolidation led Lockheed to merge with Martin Marietta in 1995, creating one of the world's largest defence contractors.
