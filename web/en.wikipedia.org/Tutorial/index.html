Tutorial

A tutorial, in education, is a method of transferring knowledge that is more interactive and specific than a book or a lecture. A tutorial seeks to teach by example and supply the information needed to complete a certain task. Tutorials can take many forms: a seminar or small-group teaching session at a university, a set of instructions in a manual, or an interactive walkthrough in software.

In higher education

At universities such as Oxford and Cambridge, the tutorial (called a supervision at Cambridge) is the central teaching format: one or a few students meet regularly with a tutor to discuss essays and problems in depth. In many other universities, tutorials are small-group sessions attached to large lecture courses, in which teaching assistants lead problem-solving and discussion.

In computing

In computer software, a tutorial is a lesson or guided tour that introduces new users to a program's functions, often interactively, allowing them to practise steps as they learn. Video tutorials and step-by-step guides are widespread on the web for topics from programming to crafts.
