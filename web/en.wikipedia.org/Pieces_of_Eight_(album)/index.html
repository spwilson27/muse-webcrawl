Pieces of Eight (album)

Pieces of Eight is the eighth studio album by the American rock band Styx, released in September 1978 by A&M Records. Like the band's previous album, The Grand Illusion (1977), it was a major commercial success, reaching No. 6 on the Billboard 200 and being certified triple platinum in the United States.

Music and themes

The album mixes progressive rock with hard rock and continues the band's interest in concept themes, here touching on ambition, materialism and the pursuit of wealth — symbolised by the Spanish treasure coins of the title. It produced two hit singles written by Dennis DeYoung, "Renegade" (a Tommy Shaw song that became a signature rock anthem) and "Blue Collar Man (Long Nights)", both of which reached the top 25 of the Billboard Hot 100.

Track listing

Tracks include "Great White Hope", "I'm O.K.", "Sing for the Day", "The Message", "Lords of the Ring", "Blue Collar Man (Long Nights)", "Queen of Spades", "Renegade", "Pieces of Eight" and "Aku-Aku".
