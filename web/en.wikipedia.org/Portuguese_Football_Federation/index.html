The Portuguese Football Federation (Portuguese: Federação Portuguesa de Futebol, FPF) is the governing body of football in Portugal. Founded in 1914, it organises the national teams of Portugal, the Portuguese Cup (Taça de Portugal), and other competitions, and oversees the sport at all levels in cooperation with the professional league body, Liga Portugal, which runs the Primeira Liga.

History

The federation was founded on 31 March 1914 by the three regional associations of Lisbon, Porto, and Portalegre as the União Portuguesa de Futebol, adopting its present name in 1926. Portugal played its first international in 1921 against Spain. The FPF was a founding member of UEFA in 1954.

National teams

Portugal's senior men's team won UEFA Euro 2016 and the inaugural UEFA Nations League in 2019, and finished third at the 1966 FIFA World Cup, led by Eusébio. The federation also runs highly successful youth, women's, futsal, and beach soccer programmes; Portugal's futsal team won the FIFA Futsal World Cup in 2021, and its beach soccer team has won multiple world titles.

Cidade do Futebol

In 2016 the federation opened the Cidade do Futebol ("City of Football") training complex in Cruz Quebrada, Oeiras, near Lisbon, which serves as the headquarters and the home base of all national teams.
