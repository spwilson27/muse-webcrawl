FA Trophy

The Football Association Challenge Trophy, commonly known as the FA Trophy, is a men's knockout cup competition in English football, organised by and named after the Football Association. It is open to semi-professional clubs playing in Steps 1–4 of the National League System, broadly the fifth to eighth tiers of English football.

The FA Trophy

History

The competition was founded in 1969 to give semi-professional clubs, who at the time were not eligible for the FA Amateur Cup, a chance to compete for a trophy and play a final at Wembley Stadium. Macclesfield Town won the first final in 1970, defeating Telford United. Since the abolition of the Amateur Cup in 1974, the FA Trophy has been the premier cup competition for semi-professional clubs. The final returned to the rebuilt Wembley Stadium from 2007 onwards, and is often combined with the FA Vase final in a "Non-League Finals Day".

Records

Woking and Scarborough are among the most successful clubs in the competition, with three wins each. The competition is currently sponsored and known as the Isuzu FA Trophy.
