Sky Group

Sky Group Limited, formerly British Sky Broadcasting (BSkyB), is a British media and telecommunications conglomerate owned by Comcast. Founded in its modern form on 2 November 1990 through the merger of Sky Television and British Satellite Broadcasting, it pioneered satellite pay television in the United Kingdom, built its business on live Premier League football, and grew into one of Europe's largest broadcasting and broadband companies, serving more than twenty million customers across the UK, Ireland, Germany, Austria, and Italy.

History

The merger that created BSkyB was a rescue of the financially failing BSB by Rupert Murdoch's Sky. The company's fortunes turned on its audacious £304 million bid in 1992 for live rights to the new FA Premier League, which made its satellite dishes a fixture of British suburbs and transformed football economics. Sky launched digital satellite service Sky Digital in 1998, added broadband and telephony in the 2000s, and acquired Sky Italia and Sky Deutschland in 2014 to become a pan-European group led from London.

Comcast acquisition

In 2018, after a bidding war with Disney, Comcast acquired Sky for £30 billion, valuing the group at £29.7 billion in the final auction. Under Comcast, Sky has expanded its streaming service NOW, launched the Sky Glass smart television platform, invested in original drama through Sky Studios, and remained the dominant pay-TV sports broadcaster in its markets through channels such as Sky Sports, Sky News, and Sky Atlantic.
