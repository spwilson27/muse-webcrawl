Radio City Rockettes

The Radio City Rockettes are an American precision dance company. Founded in 1925 in St. Louis, they have performed out of Radio City Music Hall in Manhattan, New York City, since 1932. Until 2015 they also had a touring company.

They are best known for starring in the Radio City Christmas Spectacular, an annual holiday show, and for performing annually since 1925 in the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York.

History

Choreographer Russell Markert founded the troupe as the Missouri Rockets, inspired by the precision of the Tiller Girls and the Ziegfeld Follies. Renamed the Roxyettes and then the Rockettes, they became synonymous with Radio City after its opening in December 1932.

Style

The Rockettes are famed for synchronised eye-high kick lines, geometric formations and routines such as the "Parade of the Wooden Soldiers" (performed since 1933) and the "Living Nativity". Dancers must meet height requirements and master tap, jazz, ballet and modern dance.

Auditions and company

Hundreds audition annually in New York; the company performs the Christmas Spectacular in multiple casts through the holiday season.
