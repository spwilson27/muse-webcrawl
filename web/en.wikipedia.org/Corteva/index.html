Corteva

Corteva, Inc. (styled Corteva Agriscience) is a major American agricultural chemical and seed company. It was formed from the agriculture division of DowDuPont, the company created by the 2017 merger of Dow Chemical and DuPont, and became an independent publicly traded company on 1 June 2019 when DowDuPont split into three firms: Dow Inc. (materials), DuPont de Nemours (specialty products) and Corteva (agriculture). Its headquarters are in Indianapolis, Indiana.

Business

Corteva's two main segments are seed and crop protection. Its seed business sells corn, soybean and other seed under brands including Pioneer — the historic seed house founded by Henry A. Wallace — Brevant and Granular software services. The crop protection segment sells herbicides, insecticides and fungicides, along with newer biological products. The company employs roughly 20,000 people and sells in more than 140 countries, with annual revenues in the range of 17 billion US dollars.

History

The company's lineage runs through DuPont's agricultural chemicals and Pioneer's seed genetics and Dow's agrochemicals. After the spin-off, Corteva invested in digital agriculture, biologicals and seed-applied technologies, and it has faced industry-wide litigation and regulation concerning pesticide safety and the legacy of PFAS and other chemicals inherited from its parent companies, liabilities shared out among the three successor firms.
