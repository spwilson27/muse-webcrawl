TKO Group Holdings

TKO Group Holdings, Inc. is an American sports and entertainment company that owns the professional wrestling promotion WWE and the mixed martial arts organisation UFC. It was formed in September 2023 when Endeavor, the UFC's parent company, merged Endeavor's UFC business with WWE in a transaction valuing the combined company at more than $21 billion. TKO is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol TKO.

Formation

The merger was announced in April 2023, one day after WrestleMania 39, and closed on 12 September 2023. WWE shareholders owned 49 percent of the new company and Endeavor 51 percent. Vince McMahon served as executive chairman until his resignation in January 2024; Ari Emanuel is chief executive officer and Mark Shapiro president of the group.

Operations

Through WWE, TKO produces weekly television programmes including Raw and SmackDown, premium live events such as WrestleMania, and a large content library. Through UFC it promotes mixed martial arts events worldwide, including numbered pay-per-view cards and Fight Night shows. In 2025 TKO expanded further by agreeing to acquire sports assets including IMG, On Location and Professional Bull Riders from Endeavor.

Media rights

Major rights deals include WWE Raw's move to Netflix beginning in 2025 and UFC's media agreements in the United States, which the company has sought to renew at substantially higher values.
