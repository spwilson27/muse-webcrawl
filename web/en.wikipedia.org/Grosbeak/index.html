Grosbeak

Grosbeak is a form taxon containing various species of seed-eating passerine birds with large beaks. Although they all belong to the superfamily Passeroidea, they form a polyphyletic group, so "grosbeak" is not a natural taxonomic grouping.

Species

Birds called grosbeaks include the rose-breasted grosbeak and black-headed grosbeak of the cardinal family (Cardinalidae) in the Americas, and various finches (Fringillidae) such as the hawfinch and pine grosbeak. Their stout, conical bills are adapted for cracking hard seeds.

Distribution

Grosbeaks are found across North America, Europe and Asia, in woodland, forest edge and gardens, and several species are prized visitors to bird feeders.
