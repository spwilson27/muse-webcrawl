Lucky Luke

CreatorMorris
First appearanceSpirou, 1946
GenreWestern, humour

Lucky Luke is a Western comic album series created by Belgian cartoonist Morris in 1946. It follows the adventures of Lucky Luke, a lone cowboy known as the "man who shoots faster than his shadow", and his intelligent horse Jolly Jumper.

Overview

From 1955 to 1977 the series was written by René Goscinny, co-creator of Asterix, in what is regarded as its golden age. Recurring antagonists include the dim-witted Dalton brothers, and many stories feature caricatures of historical figures such as Calamity Jane, Billy the Kid and Jesse James. Each album traditionally ends with Luke riding off into the sunset singing "I'm a poor lonesome cowboy…".

Legacy

One of the best-selling comic series in Europe, Lucky Luke has been adapted into animated series and live-action films and translated into many languages.
