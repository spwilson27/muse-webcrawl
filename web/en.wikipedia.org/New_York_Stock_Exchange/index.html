New York Stock Exchange

The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) is an American stock exchange located at 11 Wall Street in Lower Manhattan, New York City. Nicknamed "the Big Board", it is the largest stock exchange in the world by market capitalisation of its listed companies.

History

The exchange traces its origins to the Buttonwood Agreement of 1792, signed by 24 stockbrokers under a buttonwood tree on Wall Street. It formally constituted itself as the New York Stock & Exchange Board in 1817 and adopted its current name in 1863. Its neoclassical trading-floor building at 18 Broad Street opened in 1903.

Trading

Unlike purely electronic exchanges, the NYSE retains a physical trading floor where designated market makers oversee trading in individual stocks, supplemented by fully electronic order flow. Trading hours are 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time, marked by the opening and closing bells. In 2013 the exchange's parent, NYSE Euronext, was acquired by Intercontinental Exchange (ICE).
