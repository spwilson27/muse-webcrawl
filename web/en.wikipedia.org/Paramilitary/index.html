Paramilitary

A paramilitary is a force or unit whose organisational structure, functions, training, subculture and equipment are similar to those of a professional military, but which is not formally part of a state's armed forces, or which serves functions other than conventional warfighting.

Types

Paramilitary forces include gendarmeries and constabularies that police rural areas or perform military-style internal security duties, border guards, coast guards organised on military lines, and state reserve forces. In some contexts the term is applied to irregular or non-state armed groups, including insurgent organisations and militias, though such usage varies.

Legal status

Depending on their legal status, members of paramilitary forces may be treated as combatants under the laws of armed conflict, as law enforcement officers, or as civilians. Many countries maintain official paramilitary forces as part of their security apparatus; India's Central Armed Police Forces and the Pakistan Rangers are examples.
