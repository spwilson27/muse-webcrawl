Dikarya

KingdomFungi
SubkingdomDikarya
PhylaAscomycota, Basidiomycota, Entomophthoromycota

Dikarya is a subkingdom of fungi that includes the phyla Ascomycota and Basidiomycota, both of which in general produce dikaryons, may be filamentous or unicellular, but are always without flagella. The Dikarya are most of the so-called "higher fungi".

Characteristics

The defining feature is the dikaryotic phase of the life cycle, in which hyphal cells contain two genetically distinct haploid nuclei (n + n) that divide simultaneously. This phase can be prolonged, dominating the life cycle in many basidiomycetes.

Phylogeny

Molecular studies support Dikarya as a clade comprising the great majority of described fungal species, including yeasts, truffles, morels, mushrooms and rusts. The group is named for the Greek for "two nuclei".
