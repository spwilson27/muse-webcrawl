Ko fight


A ko fight is a tactical battle in the game of Go over a repeating capture position.

A ko fight is a tactical and strategic phase in the board game Go, arising from the ko rule, which prohibits a player from immediately recapturing a single stone in a way that would repeat the previous board position. When a ko arises, the player who would recapture must first play a move elsewhere — a ko threat — forcing the opponent to respond, after which the recapture becomes legal.

Mechanics

The two players alternately make ko threats and answer them; the player who runs out of threats of sufficient size must concede the ko, allowing the opponent to connect or capture and resolve the position. Ko fights therefore hinge on the number and value of each side's threats, and their outcome can decide the fate of large groups of stones.

Strategy

Because a ko fight converts the rest of the board into a resource, players weigh the value of winning the ko against the cost of the threats they sacrifice. Some kos are "picnic kos", where one player has little to lose; others, in which the life of a large group is at stake, may dominate an entire game.
