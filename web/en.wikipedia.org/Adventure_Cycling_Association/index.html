Adventure Cycling Association

Adventure Cycling Association is a nonprofit organization in the United States that promotes bicycle travel. Headquartered in Missoula, Montana, it is the largest nonprofit membership cycling organization in North America, known for developing bicycle touring routes and publishing maps for long-distance cycling.

Adventure Cycling Association
Founded: 1973 (as Bikecentennial)
Headquarters: Missoula, Montana
Focus: Bicycle touring routes and maps

History

The organization was founded as Bikecentennial to organize a mass bicycle ride across the United States for the 1976 United States Bicentennial. The TransAmerica Trail, inaugurated for that event, remains its flagship route. The organization later took the name Adventure Cycling Association.

Routes and programs

Adventure Cycling maintains the Adventure Cycling Route Network, tens of thousands of miles of mapped routes including the Great Divide Mountain Bike Route and routes developed in partnership with the U.S. Bicycle Route System. It publishes Adventure Cyclist magazine and offers guided tours and educational programs.
