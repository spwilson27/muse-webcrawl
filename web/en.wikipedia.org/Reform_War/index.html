The Reform War (Spanish: Guerra de Reforma), also known as the Three Years' War, was a Mexican civil war fought from December 1857 to January 1861 between liberals and conservatives over the liberal reforms of La Reforma and the Constitution of 1857. The conflict began when conservatives, led by General Félix María Zuloaga, repudiated the constitution under the Plan of Tacubaya, and the liberal president Benito Juárez established a rival constitutional government.

Background

After the fall of Santa Anna in 1855, a liberal government enacted sweeping reforms — the Juárez Law abolishing separate military and clerical courts, the Lerdo Law forcing the sale of corporate (especially Church) lands, and the Iglesias Law limiting parish fees — and enshrined them in the liberal Constitution of 1857. The Catholic Church and conservative landowners and officers rejected the settlement; when Comonfort staged a self-coup and then fled, constitutional succession passed to Juárez, Chief Justice of the Supreme Court, and the country split into two governments: conservatives in Mexico City and liberals, eventually, at Veracruz.

Course of the war

Conservative armies under Zuloaga and then the young general Miguel Miramón won most early engagements and twice besieged Veracruz without success. Juárez, sustained by customs revenue and United States recognition under the (unratified) McLane–Ocampo Treaty, radicalised his programme from Veracruz with the Reform Laws of 1859, nationalising Church property and separating church and state. Liberal generals such as Santos Degollado and Jesús González Ortega gradually reversed the tide in the Bajío and the north, defeated Miramón at Silao and Calpulalpan in 1860, and entered Mexico City in January 1861.

Consequences

The liberal victory entrenched the Reform Laws and the 1857 Constitution, but left Mexico bankrupt and divided; conservative exiles invited European intervention, setting the stage for the French intervention and the Second Mexican Empire of Maximilian, which Juárez's republic ultimately defeated in 1867.
