Western Sahara

Western Sahara
Disputed territory, north-west Africa
Area: 266,000 km²
Administered largely by Morocco; the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic claims sovereignty
Largest city: Laayoune

Western Sahara is a disputed territory on the north-west coast of Africa. Bordered by Morocco to the north, Algeria to the north-east, Mauritania to the east and south, and the Atlantic Ocean to the west, it is one of the most sparsely populated territories in the world, consisting mainly of desert flatlands. Spain colonised the area in 1884 and administered it as Spanish Sahara until 1975.

History

After the Madrid Accords of 1975, Spain withdrew and the territory was partitioned between Morocco and Mauritania. The Polisario Front, representing the indigenous Sahrawi people, proclaimed the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic (SADR) in 1976 and fought a guerrilla war against both states. Mauritania withdrew in 1979, and Morocco extended its control to most of the territory, building a fortified sand wall — the Moroccan Western Sahara Wall or "Berm" — some 2,700 km long. A United Nations-brokered ceasefire took effect in 1991, monitored by the MINURSO mission, but the promised referendum on independence has never been held. The ceasefire effectively collapsed in November 2020.

Status

The United Nations lists Western Sahara as a non-self-governing territory. Morocco administers roughly the western 80 per cent of the territory and proposes autonomy under Moroccan sovereignty; the Polisario Front and the SADR, a member of the African Union, control the remainder east of the Berm and administer refugee camps near Tindouf, Algeria. Most United Nations member states do not recognise Moroccan sovereignty, while a growing number support Morocco's autonomy plan.

Geography and economy

The territory's population is estimated at around 600,000, concentrated in Laayoune, Dakhla and other coastal towns. Resources include phosphates (the Bou Craa mine, linked to the coast by a 100 km conveyor belt), rich Atlantic fisheries and offshore oil exploration prospects.
