Chorus

A chorus is a group of singers performing together, also called a choir when in a church or formal setting, and the term also denotes a repeated section of a song and the collective voice in Greek drama.

Musical chorus

Choruses sing in parts — soprano, alto, tenor and bass — in sacred music, opera, oratorio and secular works. Notable choirs range from cathedral and collegiate choirs to community and gospel choirs.

Greek drama

In ancient Greek tragedy and comedy, the chorus was a group of performers who sang, danced and commented on the action, representing public opinion between episodes.

Song structure

In popular music the chorus is the refrain, contrasting with verses, usually carrying the main melody and lyrical hook.
