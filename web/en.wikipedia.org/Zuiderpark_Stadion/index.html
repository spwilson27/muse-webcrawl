Zuiderpark Stadion was a football stadium in The Hague, Netherlands. It was the home ground of ADO Den Haag for most of the club's history, from its opening in 1925 until 2007, when the club moved to the newly built Kyocera Stadion (now the Cars Jeans Stadion) in the Forepark district.

History

The stadium stood in the Zuiderpark (Southern Park) in the southwest of the city. Over the decades it was repeatedly expanded and renovated; in its 1970s heyday it could hold close to 28,000 spectators, though safety regulations reduced capacity to about 11,000 by the time of its closure. ADO Den Haag played its final match at the ground in April 2007. The site was subsequently demolished and redeveloped for sport, leisure and educational facilities, although the location remains emotionally significant for the club's supporters.
