Kartagener syndrome

Kartagener syndrome
Type: Subset of primary ciliary dyskinesia
Named after: Manes Kartagener
Triad: Situs inversus, chronic sinusitis, bronchiectasis
Inheritance: Autosomal recessive

Kartagener syndrome is a rare genetic disorder that forms a subset of primary ciliary dyskinesia. It is defined by a classic triad: situs inversus (mirror-image reversal of the internal organs), chronic sinusitis and bronchiectasis.

Cause

The condition results from defects in the dynein arms of cilia, the hair-like structures that move mucus in the airways and, during embryonic development, determine left–right body asymmetry. Immotile cilia cause mucus retention, recurrent respiratory infections and, in about half of cases, situs inversus.

Diagnosis and treatment

Diagnosis involves nasal nitric oxide measurement, ciliary ultrastructure examination and genetic testing. Treatment focuses on airway clearance, antibiotics for infections and management of sinus disease. See also Primary ciliary dyskinesia.
