The Russian Premier League (Russian: Российская премьер-лига) is the top level professional association football league in Russia. It was established at the end of the 2001 season as the Russian Football Premier League and succeeded the Top Division. The league is managed by the Russian Football Union.

History

After the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991, Russia formed its own top division. In 2001 the competition was reorganised as the Russian Premier League. Historically, Spartak Moscow dominated the 1990s, winning nine of the first ten championships. In the 2000s CSKA Moscow, Rubin Kazan and Zenit Saint Petersburg became leading forces, with Zenit emerging as the dominant club of the 2010s and early 2020s.

Competition format

The league comprises 16 clubs playing a double round-robin schedule, for 30 matches per team across the season, which traditionally runs from July to May. The bottom two clubs are relegated to the Russian First League, while the 13th- and 14th-placed teams contest relegation play-offs. Following Russia's exclusion from UEFA competitions in 2022, Russian clubs did not participate in European tournaments.

Champions

Spartak Moscow and Zenit Saint Petersburg are among the most successful clubs, alongside CSKA Moscow, Lokomotiv Moscow and Rubin Kazan.
