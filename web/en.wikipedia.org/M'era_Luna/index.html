M'era Luna

The main stage at M'era Luna

Location: Hildesheim-Drispenstedt airfield, Germany

First held: 2000

Attendance: ~25,000

Genres: Gothic rock, industrial, metal, darkwave

M'era Luna is a gothic and alternative music festival held annually on the second weekend of August at the airfield of Hildesheim, Lower Saxony, Germany. First staged in 2000 — evolving out of the Zillo Festival, which had moved to the site in 1999 — it is one of the largest festivals of the "black scene" (Schwarze Szene) in Europe, drawing around 25,000 visitors, many in elaborate gothic fashion, for which the event's catwalk-like atmosphere and medieval market are celebrated features.

Music and programme

Two stages host performances spanning gothic rock, EBM and industrial, synth-pop and darkwave, and various shades of metal and folk; past headliners have included The Sisters of Mercy, Nightwish, Within Temptation, Rammstein-related projects, Marilyn Manson, Korn, and ASP. Alongside concerts the festival offers readings, a fashion show presenting gothic designers, club nights, and camping on the airfield grounds.

M'era Luna is organised by FKP Scorpio and is frequently paired in coverage with Leipzig's Wave-Gotik-Treffen as a pillar of the German gothic calendar, though M'era Luna's single-site festival format contrasts with the Treffen's city-wide programme.
