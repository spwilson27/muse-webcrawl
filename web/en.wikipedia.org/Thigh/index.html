Thigh

The thigh is the region of the lower limb between the hip (pelvis) and the knee. Its single bone, the femur, is the longest and strongest bone in the human body, articulating with the hip above and the tibia and patella at the knee below.

Anatomy

The muscles of the thigh are arranged in three compartments: the anterior compartment (quadriceps femoris — the knee extensors), the medial compartment (the adductors), and the posterior compartment (the hamstrings — knee flexors). The femoral artery, vein and nerve pass through the thigh, the nerve supplying the anterior muscles.

Clinical significance

Femoral fractures, hamstring strains and deep-vein thrombosis are common conditions affecting the thigh.
