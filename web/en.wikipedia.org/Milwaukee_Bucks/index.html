The Milwaukee Bucks are an American professional basketball team based in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, competing in the National Basketball Association (NBA) as a member of the Eastern Conference Central Division.

History

Founded as an expansion team in 1968, the Bucks won a coin flip for the first pick in the 1969 draft and selected Lew Alcindor (later Kareem Abdul-Jabbar), who led them to the 1971 NBA championship in only their third season.

Modern era

Led by two-time MVP Giannis Antetokounmpo, the Bucks won the 2021 NBA Finals against the Phoenix Suns, their first title in 50 years, rallying from a 2–0 deficit; Antetokounmpo was named Finals MVP after a 50-point performance in the deciding game.
