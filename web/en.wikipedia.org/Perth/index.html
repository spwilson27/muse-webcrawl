Perth

Perth (Nyungar: Boorloo) is the capital and largest city of the Australian state of Western Australia. It is the fourth most populous city in Australia, with a population of over two million. Perth is located on the Swan Coastal Plain along the Swan River, between the Indian Ocean and the Darling Scarp.

History

The Perth area has been inhabited by the Whadjuk Noongar people for tens of thousands of years. The city was founded by Captain James Stirling in 1829 as the administrative centre of the Swan River Colony. Gold rushes in the late 19th century spurred major growth, and a mining boom in the early 21st century made Perth one of Australia's fastest-growing cities.

Geography and climate

Perth enjoys a Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. It is one of the most isolated major cities in the world, closer to Jakarta than to Sydney. Landmarks include Kings Park, one of the world's largest inner-city parks, and the beaches of the Sunset Coast.
