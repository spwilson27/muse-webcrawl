List of Solar System probes

Voyager 1, launched in 1977, is the most distant human-made object from Earth.

The following is a list of Solar System probes — space probes that have left Earth orbit and travelled into interplanetary space, visiting the Moon, planets, asteroids, comets and other bodies of the Solar System. The list includes flyby spacecraft, orbiters, landers, rovers and sample return missions launched by various space agencies.
