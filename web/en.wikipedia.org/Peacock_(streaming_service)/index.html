Peacock (streaming service)

Peacock is an American video streaming service owned and operated by Peacock TV LLC, a subsidiary of NBCUniversal, itself a subsidiary of Comcast. Named after NBC's logo, the service launched on 15 July 2020 and features television series, films, news, and sports programming, including original content and programming from the NBCUniversal library.

History

Peacock was soft-launched to Comcast Xfinity subscribers in April 2020 before its nationwide rollout in July 2020. It initially offered free, premium and ad-supported tiers. The service became the streaming home of NBC series, Universal films, and WWE Network content in the United States from 2021. Peacock also carries live sports including Premier League football, NFL Sunday Night Football, and Olympic Games coverage.

Content

Original series on Peacock include Poker Face, Bel-Air, The Traitors (US), Twisted Metal, and Dr. Death. Its library includes long-running NBC franchises such as The Office, Law & Order, and Saturday Night Live. In 2024 Peacock streamed the Paris Olympic Games extensively, contributing to subscriber growth to around 36 million paid subscribers.
