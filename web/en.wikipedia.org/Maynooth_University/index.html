Maynooth University

Maynooth University (Irish: Ollscoil Mhá Nuad) is a public university in Maynooth, County Kildare, Ireland. It is Ireland's youngest university, established in 1997 from the secular faculties of St Patrick's College, Maynooth, which was founded in 1795 as a seminary.

Campus and students

The university shares its historic south campus with St Patrick's Pontifical University and has a modern north campus across the road. It has over 13,000 students from more than 90 countries and is a constituent university of the National University of Ireland.

Profile

Maynooth is known for humanities, social sciences, science and engineering, and for research institutes such as the Hamilton Institute and the National Centre for Geocomputation.
