DataCite

DataCite is an international non-profit organisation founded in 2009 that provides persistent identifiers (DOIs) for research data and other research outputs. Its aim is to help researchers locate, identify, cite and reuse datasets, supporting reproducible research and proper attribution of data creators.

Services

Through its member organisations — data centres, libraries, universities and publishers — DataCite registers DOIs accompanied by a standard metadata schema describing the resource. DataCite Commons offers search across DOIs registered with DataCite and other registration agencies, and its metadata is openly available under a CC0 waiver.
