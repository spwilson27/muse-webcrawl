List of Nobel laureates

The Nobel Prizes are awarded annually in Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature and Peace, with the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences added in 1968. Since 1901 more than 900 individuals and organisations have been laureates.

Overview

Marie Curie won in two sciences; the International Committee of the Red Cross won Peace three times. See Nobel Prize and Alfred Nobel.
