Most common surnames

Globally, the most common surnames are Chinese: Wang (王), Li (李), and Zhang (張) each have roughly 90–100 million bearers. In the United States, Smith, Johnson, and Williams head census lists; in the United Kingdom, Smith, Jones, and Taylor; in Spain, García, Rodríguez, and González; in Russia, Ivanov, Smirnov, and Kuznetsov.
