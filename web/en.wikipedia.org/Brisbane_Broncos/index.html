The Brisbane Broncos are an Australian professional rugby league football club based in Brisbane, Queensland. Founded in 1988, they compete in the National Rugby League (NRL) and play home games at Suncorp Stadium. The Broncos are one of the most successful and popular clubs in Australian rugby league.

History

Coached for their first 21 seasons by Wayne Bennett, the Broncos won premierships in 1992 and 1993, the 1997 Super League title, and further NRL premierships in 1998, 2000 and 2006. Legendary players include Wally Lewis, Allan Langer, Darren Lockyer and Sam Thaiday. The club endured a long premiership drought after 2006, including a first wooden spoon in 2020, before returning to the grand final in 2023.
