Newsboy cap

A newsboy cap is a casual-wear cap similar in style to a flat cap. It has the same overall shape and stiff peak in front, but the body of the cap is rounder and fuller, made from eight panels with a button on top joining them together.

History

The style became popular in Europe and North America in the late 19th and early 20th centuries among working-class men — and, in popular imagination, among the newspaper boys ("newsies") who gave the cap its name in the United States. It was also widely worn by golfers, drivers and dockworkers. The newsboy cap has repeatedly returned to mainstream fashion, and is often confused with the similar baker boy cap.
