BCE Inc., formerly Bell Canada Enterprises Inc., is a Canadian telecommunications and media holding company headquartered in Montreal, Quebec. It is the parent company of Bell Canada, Canada's largest telecommunications company, and of the media conglomerate Bell Media.

History

BCE was created in 1983 under the Bell Canada Act as the holding company for the Bell System companies in Canada, succeeding the structure in which Bell Canada had operated since 1880. It divested assets including Nortel Networks and Bell Aliant over subsequent decades, and expanded into media through the acquisition of CTVglobemedia (becoming Bell Media in 2011) and Astral Media (2013).

Operations

Through Bell Canada, BCE provides wireline and wireless telephone, internet (Fibe), and television services. Bell Media operates the CTV television network, specialty channels such as TSN and Crave, and radio stations. BCE is listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange.
