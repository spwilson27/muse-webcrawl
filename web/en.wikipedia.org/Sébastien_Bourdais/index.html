Sébastien Bourdais

Sébastien Olivier Bourdais (born 28 February 1979) is a French professional racing driver from Le Mans. He is one of the most successful drivers in American open-wheel racing, winning four consecutive Champ Car World Series championships (2004–2007), and later competed in Formula One with Toro Rosso and in the IndyCar Series and endurance racing.

Early career

Born into a racing family in the home city of the 24 Hours of Le Mans, Bourdais progressed through French Formula Renault and Formula 3, won the International Formula 3000 championship in 2002, and also took part in the Le Mans 24 Hours in sports cars.

Champ Car dominance

Joining Newman/Haas Racing in the CART/Champ Car series in 2003, Bourdais won rookie of the year, then four straight championships from 2004 to 2007, amassing 31 victories. His run made him the dominant driver of the series' final years before its 2008 merger with the Indy Racing League.

Formula One and later career

Bourdais raced for Scuderia Toro Rosso in Formula One in 2008 and part of 2009, with a best result of seventh. Returning to America, he won IndyCar races with KV and Dale Coyne Racing — notably at St. Petersburg in 2017 and 2018, near his adopted Florida home — and survived a heavy qualifying crash at Indianapolis in 2017. In sports cars he won his class at the 2016 Le Mans 24 Hours with Ford and took overall honours at the Daytona 24 Hours and Sebring 12 Hours.
