Pikachu

Pikachu is a fictional species of Pokémon created by Nintendo and Game Freak. Designed by Atsuko Nishida and Ken Sugimori, Pikachu first appeared in the 1996 video games Pokémon Red and Green (released internationally as Red and Blue) and has since become the mascot of the Pokémon franchise and one of the most recognisable fictional characters in the world.

Characteristics

Pikachu is a small, yellow, mouse-like Electric-type Pokémon with long pointed ears tipped in black, red circular cheek pouches that store electricity, and a tail shaped like a lightning bolt. It evolves from Pichu when leveled up with high friendship and evolves into Raichu when exposed to a Thunder Stone. In the animated series, a Pikachu voiced in part by Ikue Ōtani is the partner of the protagonist Ash Ketchum.

Cultural impact

Pikachu appears on countless items of merchandise, in films such as Pokémon Detective Pikachu (2019), in a giant balloon in the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, and as the face of ANA airline liveries and tourism campaigns. Its name combines the Japanese onomatopoeia for an electric spark, "pika", and the sound of a mouse squeaking, "chu".
