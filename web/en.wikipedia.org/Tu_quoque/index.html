Tu quoque

Tu quoque
Latin"You also"
TypeInformal fallacy; appeal to hypocrisy
RelatedWhataboutism, ad hominem

Tu quoque (Latin for "you also") is an informal fallacy, a type of ad hominem argument, that attempts to discredit an opponent's position by charging them with hypocrisy — asserting that the opponent has acted inconsistently with the position — without directly refuting the argument itself.

Structure

The argument takes the form: person A criticises action X; person B replies that A has also done X, so the criticism fails. Even if the charge of hypocrisy is true, it does not show the criticism to be wrong; the tactic instead diverts attention, and is closely related to the Soviet-era rhetorical technique known as whataboutism.

History

The phrase is famously associated with the dying words attributed to Julius Caesar, "Et tu, Brute?" Its use as a legal and rhetorical defence — that the accuser is guilty of the same offence — has a long history in diplomacy and courtroom argument.
