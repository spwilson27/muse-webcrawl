Photoresist

A photoresist (also known simply as a resist) is a light-sensitive material used in several processes, such as photolithography and photoengraving, to form a patterned coating on a surface. It is essential to the manufacture of semiconductors and printed circuit boards.

Positive and negative resists

In a positive resist, the portion exposed to light becomes soluble in the developer, so the exposed pattern is washed away. In a negative resist, exposure causes the material to polymerise or cross-link and become insoluble, so the unexposed portion is removed.

Use in lithography

A thin film of resist is spin-coated onto a wafer, exposed through a photomask (today usually by deep-ultraviolet or extreme-ultraviolet light), developed, and used as a mask for etching or ion implantation steps.
