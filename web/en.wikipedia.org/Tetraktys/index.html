Tetraktys

The tetraktys, a triangular figure of ten points

The tetraktys (Greek: τετρακτύς), or the tetrad, is a triangular figure consisting of ten points arranged in four rows: one, two, three, and four points in each row. It was a symbol of immense mystical and mathematical importance to the Pythagoreans, who swore oaths by it.

Symbolism

For the followers of Pythagoras, the tetraktys symbolized the organization of the cosmos. The four rows were associated with the elements (fire, air, water, earth) and with dimensions: the single point for the monad, two points for a line, three for a surface, and four for a solid. The sum 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10, the decad, was held to be a perfect number embracing the whole of arithmetic and the universe. The ratios among its rows also encode the musical consonances: 2:1 (octave), 3:2 (fifth), and 4:3 (fourth), reflecting the Pythagorean discovery of the mathematical basis of harmony.

Legacy

The symbol passed into later Western esotericism and the Kabbalah, and the fourth triangular number remains a staple of number symbolism. The Pythagoreans reportedly prayed to the tetraktys as the "fount and root of eternal nature."
