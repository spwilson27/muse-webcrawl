Red hair, also known as orange hair or ginger hair, is a human hair colour found in 1–2% of the world population, appearing more frequently (2–6%) among people of Northern or Northwestern European ancestry. It varies in hue from a deep burgundy or bright copper (auburn) through burnt orange to bright copper, strawberry blond and pale red.

Genetics

Red hair is caused by a low concentration of eumelanin and a high concentration of the reddish pigment pheomelanin in the hair shaft. It is associated primarily with variants of the melanocortin 1 receptor (MC1R) gene on chromosome 16. The trait is generally recessive: a child usually needs to inherit a variant MC1R allele from each parent to have red hair, though carriers of a single copy may show freckles, fair skin and a reddish beard. The same variants produce pale skin that tans poorly and a tendency to freckle.

Distribution

The highest proportions of redheads are found in Scotland and Ireland, where studies have estimated roughly 10% and 10–30% of the population respectively carry or express the trait, though definitions vary. Red hair also occurs across northern and eastern Europe, among Ashkenazi Jews, and in diaspora populations in North America, Australia and New Zealand. Ancient DNA has found red-hair-associated MC1R variants in remains from the Pontic steppe, suggesting the trait is thousands of years old.

Health

The fair skin associated with red hair produces vitamin D efficiently in low light but is highly sensitive to ultraviolet radiation: redheads have an elevated risk of sunburn and skin cancer, including melanoma. Studies also report altered sensitivity to pain and anaesthetics in people with red hair, linked to the role of MC1R in the processing of endorphins.

Cultural history

Red hair has attracted strong cultural associations across history, from admiration in art — the titian-red hair of Renaissance painting — to stereotyping and, at times, persecution. It remains the rarest natural hair colour in humans.

Natural red hair ranges from deep auburn to pale strawberry blond.
