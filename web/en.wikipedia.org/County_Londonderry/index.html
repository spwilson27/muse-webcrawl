County Londonderry

County Londonderry, also called County Derry, is one of the six counties of Northern Ireland and one of the traditional thirty-two counties of Ireland. It is in Ulster, on the north coast, with a population of about 252,000.

Name and history

The name is disputed: nationalists generally use Derry, unionists Londonderry, derived from the city of Derry/Londonderry and its London guild investment during the Plantation of Ulster. The county town historically was Coleraine, then Derry city. The county includes the Sperrin Mountains, River Bann and Lough Foyle.
