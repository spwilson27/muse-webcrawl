KKR & Co. Inc., formerly Kohlberg Kravis Roberts, is an American global investment company founded in 1976 that manages multiple alternative asset classes, including private equity, credit, infrastructure, real estate and hedge funds. It is one of the largest private equity firms in the world and a pioneer of the leveraged buyout (LBO).

History

Jerome Kohlberg Jr., Henry Kravis and George R. Roberts, who had worked together at Bear Stearns, founded the firm in 1976 to execute "bootstrap" acquisitions of mature companies. KKR became famous — and controversial — for the 1989 buyout of RJR Nabisco for about $25 billion (roughly $31 billion including costs), then the largest LBO in history, chronicled in the book and film Barbarians at the Gate. In 2007 KKR co-led the roughly $45 billion buyout of TXU, another landmark deal.

Business

The firm invests through regional private equity funds (Americas, Europe, Asia), growth equity, infrastructure and real estate funds, and a large credit business. KKR listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 2010 and converted from a partnership to a corporation in 2018. Kravis and Roberts stepped down as co-CEOs in 2021, succeeded by Joseph Bae and Scott Nuttall, remaining executive co-chairmen.
