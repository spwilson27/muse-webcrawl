Courtyard house

A courtyard house is a type of house—often a large house—where the main part of the building is disposed around a central courtyard. Many houses that have courtyards are not courtyard houses in this strict sense, which requires rooms to open principally onto the court.

History

Forms include Chinese siheyuan, Korean hanok arrangements, Middle Eastern and Mediterranean houses, Roman domus with atrium and peristyle, and Latin American haciendas. The form suits hot climates by shading walls and promoting airflow while ensuring privacy.
