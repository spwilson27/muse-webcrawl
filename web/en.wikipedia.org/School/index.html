School

A school is an educational institution designed to provide learning spaces and learning environments for the teaching of students, usually under the direction of teachers. Countries have systems of formal education, which are sometimes compulsory. In these systems, students progress through a series of schools that can be built and operated by both government and private organizations. The names for these schools vary by country but generally include primary school for young children and secondary school for teenagers who have completed primary education. An institution where higher education is taught is commonly called a university college or university.

History

The concept of grouping students together in a centralized location for learning has existed since classical antiquity; formal schools have existed at least since ancient Greece, ancient Rome, ancient India, and ancient China. The Byzantine Empire had an established schooling system beginning at the primary level. Islam was another culture to develop a modern school system, through the madrasa. In Europe, compulsory education began to spread in the 18th and 19th centuries, with Horace Mann and others promoting publicly funded common schools in the United States.

Levels and ownership

Schools are commonly organized in several years of age groups, corresponding to grade levels. Primary (or elementary) schools educate children roughly between ages 5 and 11; secondary schools cover the teenage years and may be subdivided into middle schools and high schools. Schools may be publicly funded (state schools), privately funded (independent or private schools, sometimes religious), or operated as charter schools. Some countries also permit homeschooling.

School life

School days are structured around a timetable of lessons in subjects such as mathematics, science, language, history, and physical education. Beyond instruction, schools provide socialization, meals, libraries, examinations, and extracurricular activities such as sports and music. Modern schools may use online and blended learning alongside classroom teaching.
