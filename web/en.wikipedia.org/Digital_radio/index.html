Digital radio

Digital radio is the use of digital technology to transmit or receive across the radio spectrum. Digital transmission by radio waves includes digital broadcasting, and especially digital audio radio services such as DAB and HD Radio.

Standards

Digital Audio Broadcasting (DAB and DAB+), developed in Europe under the Eureka 147 project, is used in many countries. In the United States, HD Radio uses in-band on-channel technology to broadcast alongside analogue FM and AM signals. Other systems include DRM for shortwave and medium wave, and satellite radio services.

Advantages

Digital radio offers more stations within the same spectrum, consistent sound quality without hiss or fading in coverage areas, and ancillary data such as station names, programme information and images displayed on receivers.

Transition

Norway became the first country to switch off national FM networks, completing the process in 2017. Other countries, including the United Kingdom and Switzerland, have promoted DAB while retaining FM for local services.
