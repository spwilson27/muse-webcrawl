Valaciclovir, also spelled valacyclovir and sold under the brand name Valtrex, is an antiviral medication used to treat infections caused by herpes simplex virus and varicella zoster virus, including genital herpes, cold sores, and shingles.

Medical uses

Valaciclovir is used for the treatment and suppression of herpes simplex outbreaks, treatment of herpes zoster (shingles), and prevention of cytomegalovirus infection after organ transplantation in some settings.

Pharmacology

It is a prodrug that is converted in the body to aciclovir, which interferes with viral DNA synthesis. Its better oral absorption allows less frequent dosing than aciclovir itself. Common side effects include headache and nausea; adequate hydration is advised to protect the kidneys.
