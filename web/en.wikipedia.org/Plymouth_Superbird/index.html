Plymouth Superbird

The Plymouth Superbird is a high-performance muscle car produced by Plymouth for the 1970 model year. Developed specifically for NASCAR racing, it was a modified version of the Plymouth Road Runner fitted with radical aerodynamic aids: a pointed nose cone and an exceptionally tall rear wing.

Development

The Superbird was Plymouth's answer to the Dodge Charger Daytona of 1969 and was designed to lure driver Richard Petty back from Ford. NASCAR's homologation rules required production versions for the street; about 1,935 were built. Engines ranged from the 440 cu in V8 to the 426 Hemi.

Legacy

Rule changes effectively outlawed the "aero cars" after 1970. Slow-selling at the time, Superbirds are now highly collectible. The car's distinctive shape inspired the character of "The King" in the Pixar film Cars, voiced by Petty himself.
