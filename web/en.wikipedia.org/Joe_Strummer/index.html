Joe Strummer

Joe Strummer (born John Graham Mellor, 21 August 1952 – 22 December 2002) was a British musician, lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist of the punk rock band The Clash.

Born in Ankara, Turkey, to a diplomat father, Strummer co-founded The Clash in London in 1976. Albums such as London Calling (1979) and Combat Rock (1982) made the band punk's most politically charged and musically adventurous act. After the Clash split, he acted, scored films, and led Joe Strummer and the Mescaleros before dying suddenly of a congenital heart defect in 2002.
