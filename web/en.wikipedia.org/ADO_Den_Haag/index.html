ADO Den Haag

ADO Den Haag is a Dutch association football club based in The Hague (Den Haag). The club was founded on 1 February 1905 as Alles Door Oefening ("Everything Through Practice") and plays in the green and yellow colours associated with the city, whose stork emblem it shares. Its home ground is the Bingoal Stadion (formerly Kyocera Stadion and Cars Jeans Stadion), opened in 2007 with a capacity of about 15,000.

History

ADO won the Dutch championship in 1942 and 1943 and the KNVB Cup in 1968 (as ADO) and 1975 (as FC Den Haag). The club merged with Holland Sport in 1971 to form FC Den Haag, reverting to ADO Den Haag in 1996. In the 1970s it enjoyed European campaigns in the Cup Winners' Cup, reaching the quarter-finals in 1976.

Recent decades

After relegation in 1992, ADO spent a decade in the second tier before returning to the Eredivisie in 2003. The club again dropped to the Eerste Divisie in 2021 following financial troubles and an ownership dispute involving its Chinese owner, United Vansen, before a takeover by American investors in 2022.

Supporters

The club's fanbase, centred on the Midden-Noord stand, has a longstanding rivalry with Ajax and Feyenoord supporters; a friendly bond exists with supporters of Legia Warsaw, Juventus, and Swansea City.
