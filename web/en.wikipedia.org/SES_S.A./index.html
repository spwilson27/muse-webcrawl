SES S.A.

SES S.A. is a Luxembourgish communications satellite operator supplying satellite communication services to governments and businesses worldwide. The company, formerly known as Société Européenne des Satellites, is headquartered at Château de Betzdorf in Luxembourg and operates a fleet of more than 70 satellites in geostationary and medium Earth orbit.

History

SES was founded in 1985 at the initiative of the Luxembourg government as Europe's first private satellite operator. Its first satellite, Astra 1A, was launched in 1988 and began delivering television channels to cable networks and direct-to-home viewers across Europe, helping to create the European satellite television market.

Through acquisitions — including GE Americom (forming SES Americom), New Skies Satellites, and later the full takeover of O3b Networks — SES expanded into a global operator serving video distribution, data networks, mobility, and government customers.

Operations

The SES fleet includes the Astra satellites at several orbital positions for European video, the SES and AMC satellites serving the Americas and other regions, and the O3b mPOWER constellation in medium Earth orbit providing high-throughput, low-latency connectivity. The company's networks reach hundreds of millions of television households and provide connectivity to ships, aircraft, remote industries, and defense users.

SES is listed on the Luxembourg Stock Exchange and Euronext Paris. In 2024 the company agreed to acquire its competitor Intelsat, one of the largest consolidations in the satellite industry.
