Loki Laufeyson is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, based on the Norse mythological god of the same name. He is the adoptive brother of the superhero Thor, and is typically depicted as a supervillain and trickster, the "God of Mischief", skilled in sorcery and illusion.

Publication history

Although a version of Loki appeared in Timely Comics in 1949, the modern character debuted in Journey into Mystery #85 (October 1962). It was Loki's scheming that brought the Avengers together in The Avengers #1 (1963). Over the decades the character has shifted between villain, antihero, and occasionally hero, and has been depicted in various forms, including a female form and a younger incarnation, Kid Loki.

In other media

Loki is portrayed by Tom Hiddleston in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, appearing in the Thor and Avengers films and headlining the Disney+ series Loki (2021–2023), one of the franchise's most popular characters.
