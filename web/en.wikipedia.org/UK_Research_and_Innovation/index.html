UK Research and Innovation

Formed2018
TypeNon-departmental public body
Budgetabout £8 billion per year

UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) is the national funding agency for science and research in the United Kingdom. Launched on 1 April 2018 under the Higher Education and Research Act 2017, it brings together the seven research councils, Innovate UK and Research England.

Structure

UKRI's councils cover arts and humanities (AHRC), biotechnology and biological sciences (BBSRC), economic and social research (ESRC), engineering and physical sciences (EPSRC), medical research (MRC), the natural environment (NERC) and science and technology facilities (STFC), alongside Innovate UK, which supports business innovation.

Role

With an annual budget of around £8 billion, UKRI is the UK's largest public funder of research, supporting universities, institutes, national facilities such as Diamond Light Source, and strategic programmes in areas from artificial intelligence to clean growth.
