Ceres

Ceres (1 Ceres)
Type: Dwarf planet
Discovered: 1 January 1801, Giuseppe Piazzi
Diameter: ~940 km
Location: Asteroid belt

Ceres is a dwarf planet in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter and the belt's largest object, containing about a third of its mass. Discovered by Giuseppe Piazzi in 1801, it was first considered a planet, then an asteroid, and reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006.

Exploration

NASA's Dawn spacecraft orbited Ceres from 2015 to 2018, revealing a rocky, icy world with bright salt deposits in Occator Crater — evidence of briny cryovolcanism — and the lone mountain Ahuna Mons.

Composition

Ceres has a differentiated interior with a rocky core and icy mantle, water ice at its poles, and traces of organic material, making it of interest for the origins of water and life. See Dwarf planet and Asteroid belt.
