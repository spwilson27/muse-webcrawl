Cotabato City

Cotabato City, officially the City of Cotabato, is an independent component city in the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao, Philippines. Located on the island of Mindanao at the mouth of the Rio Grande de Mindanao, it serves as the regional centre of Bangsamoro.

History

The area was the capital of the Sultanate of Maguindanao. Cotabato was chartered as a city on 20 June 1959. In the 2019 Bangsamoro plebiscite, voters chose to join the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region, although the city is geographically surrounded by Maguindanao del Norte.

Geography and population

According to the 2020 census it has a population of about 325,000. Languages include Maguindanaon, Tagalog, Cebuano and English. Major landmarks include the Bangsamoro Government Center, Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah Mosque (Grand Mosque), and People's Palace.
