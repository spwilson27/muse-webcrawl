Steven Tyler (born Steven Victor Tallarico, 1948) is an American singer best known as the lead vocalist of the rock band Aerosmith, in which he also plays harmonica and piano. Nicknamed the "Demon of Screamin'" for his wide vocal range and on-stage acrobatics, his flamboyant style — including scarves draped over his microphone stand — is iconic.

Aerosmith

Formed in Boston in 1970, Aerosmith rose to fame with albums such as Toys in the Attic (1975) and hits including "Dream On", "Sweet Emotion" and "Walk This Way". After a late-1970s slump amid drug abuse, the band made a celebrated comeback in the 1980s, aided by Run-DMC's 1986 cover of "Walk This Way", and scored later hits such as "Janie's Got a Gun" and "I Don't Want to Miss a Thing" (1998). Tyler also served as a judge on American Idol in 2011–2012 and released a solo country album in 2016.
