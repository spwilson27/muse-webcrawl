Expansion pack

MediumVideo games, board games, card games
RequirementOwnership of the base game (usually)
Typical contentNew levels, storylines, characters, items, mechanics
Modern successorDownloadable content (DLC), season passes

An expansion pack, or simply expansion, is an addition to an existing video game, board game, or collectible card game that extends the original with new content while generally requiring the base product to play. Expansions typically add some combination of new story campaigns, levels or maps, playable characters or factions, weapons and items, game modes, and quality-of-life features, and were traditionally sold as boxed retail products at a lower price than the original game.

History in video games

The format flourished on PC in the 1990s and early 2000s. Notable examples include StarCraft: Brood War (1998), widely regarded as one of the finest expansions ever made; Diablo II: Lord of Destruction (2001); The Sims series' many themed packs; and Blizzard's World of Warcraft expansions, beginning with The Burning Crusade (2007), which functioned as major content releases for the subscription MMO. Earlier antecedents include mission disks for flight simulators and data disks on home computers. Some expansions grew so large they blurred into sequels or stand-alone releases ("expandalones") that did not require the base game.

From expansions to DLC

With broadband distribution, the boxed expansion gave way to downloadable content: smaller, more frequent add-ons, season passes bundling future releases, and free updates supported by microtransactions. Critics debate the trade-offs — expansions offered substantial, complete packages but arrived slowly; DLC offers flexibility but can fragment content and pricing. Large, expansion-scale DLC (such as The Witcher 3's Blood and Wine or Elden Ring's Shadow of the Erdtree) shows the older model persisting in digital form. In tabletop gaming, expansions remain a physical staple, adding modules that can be mixed into the base game of titles from Catan to Dominion.
