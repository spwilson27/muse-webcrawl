Rosa Khutor

The Rosa Khutor Alpine Resort (Russian: Роза Хутор) is an alpine ski resort in Krasnodar Krai, Russia, located at the Western Caucasus near Krasnaya Polyana, east of Sochi. It is named after the Rosa Khutor plateau and the nearby farmstead established by Estonian settlers in the 19th century.

2014 Winter Olympics

Rosa Khutor was developed as the venue for the alpine skiing events of the 2014 Winter Olympics and Paralympics. Its Alpine Center hosted the downhill, super-G, giant slalom, slalom, and combined events, while the Rosa Khutor Extreme Park hosted freestyle skiing and snowboarding. The resort's slopes were designed with input from Olympic downhill champion Bernhard Russi.

The resort

The resort lies on the slopes of the Aibga Ridge along the Mzymta River, with lifts rising to the Rosa Peak at about 2,320 metres (7,610 ft). It offers dozens of kilometres of pistes, gondolas and chairlifts, hotels, and a village-style base area built for the Games. After 2014 it continued operating as one of Russia's largest ski resorts and also hosted World Cup alpine races and other international competitions.
