Lucius Aemilius Paullus Macedonicus

Lucius Aemilius Paullus (c. 229 – 160 BC), nicknamed Macedonicus, was a Roman general and statesman, twice consul. He campaigned in Hispania and Liguria, and in his second consulship (168 BC) was given command of the Third Macedonian War.

Battle of Pydna

At the Battle of Pydna (22 June 168 BC) Paullus decisively defeated King Perseus of Macedon, ending the Antigonid dynasty and bringing Macedonia under Roman control. He celebrated a magnificent three-day triumph in Rome in 167 BC. His sons included Scipio Aemilianus, adopted into the Cornelii Scipiones, the destroyer of Carthage.
