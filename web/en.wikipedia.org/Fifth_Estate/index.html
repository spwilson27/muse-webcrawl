Fifth Estate

The Fifth Estate is a socio-political term for actors in a society outside the conventional estates and estates of the realm, or beyond the Fourth Estate of the press. It has been applied at various times to different groups, including the poor and unrepresented, trade unions, and more recently to networked media, bloggers and online communities.

In the modern usage popularised by William H. Dutton, the Fifth Estate refers to individuals empowered by the internet to hold institutions, including the media itself, to account, analogous to how the press was described as a Fourth Estate checking the other three estates of clergy, nobility and commoners. The term was also the title of the 2013 film The Fifth Estate, about WikiLeaks.
