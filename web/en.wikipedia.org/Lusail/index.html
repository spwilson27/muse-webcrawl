Lusail (Arabic: لوسيل) is a planned city in Qatar, located on the coast, about 23 kilometres (14 mi) north of the capital, Doha. It is Qatar's second largest city by planned size and was constructed by the state-owned developer Qatari Diar Real Estate Investment Company.

Lusail covers an area of roughly 38 square kilometres and is designed to accommodate a population of around 450,000 people. The city is organised into a series of districts, including the Fox Hills area, the Energy City, the Entertainment City, and the waterfront Marina district. It is one of the largest sustainable development projects in the region, with extensive use of recycled water, district cooling, and an automated waste collection system.

Sport

The city's Lusail Stadium, with a capacity of about 89,000 spectators, was the largest stadium used during the 2022 FIFA World Cup and hosted the tournament final, in which Argentina defeated France. The Lusail International Circuit, a motor racing venue near the city, hosts the Qatar Grand Prix in Formula One and the Qatar motorcycle Grand Prix in MotoGP.

Transport

Lusail is connected to Doha by major highways and by the Lusail Tram, a light rail network that links with the Doha Metro at Legtaifiya station. The Katara Towers, a distinctive crescent-shaped pair of towers at the marina, have become a symbol of the city.
