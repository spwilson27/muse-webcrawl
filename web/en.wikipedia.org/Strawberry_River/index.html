Strawberry River

LocationArkansas, United States (other rivers of the name exist in Utah and elsewhere)
MouthBlack River
LengthAbout 115 mi (185 km) for the Arkansas river

The Strawberry River is the name of several rivers. The best known flows through north-eastern Arkansas in the United States, rising in Fulton County near the Missouri border and flowing generally south-east for roughly 115 miles to join the Black River near Newport. It drains a largely agricultural and forested watershed in the Ozark foothills.

Other rivers

A separate Strawberry River in Utah rises in the Uinta Mountains region and flows to the Duchesne River; it lends its name to Strawberry Reservoir, one of Utah's major fisheries and water-storage lakes. Smaller streams of the same name occur elsewhere in North America.
