Peter Sauber

Peter Sauber (born 13 October 1943) is a retired Swiss racing car designer and team principal, founder of the Sauber Formula One team. He built his career from sports car racing, entering Formula One in 1993 and running the Hinwil-based team through several incarnations, including as the BMW works team from 2006 to 2009.

Career

Sauber began building racing cars in the 1970s and achieved success in the World Sportscar Championship with Mercedes-Benz, winning the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1989. His Formula One team debuted in 1993 and became known for developing young drivers, including Kimi Räikkönen and Felipe Massa. After BMW's withdrawal, Sauber bought back the team and ran it until selling to investors in 2016; the outfit later raced as Alfa Romeo and Stake F1 Team Kick Sauber ahead of becoming the Audi works team.
