Cloud Native Computing Foundation

Founded: 2015
Parent: Linux Foundation
Focus: Cloud-native open source software

The Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) is a Linux Foundation project founded in 2015 to advance container technology and align the tech industry around its evolution. It hosts open source projects that support the development of cloud-native applications and microservices architectures.

History

The CNCF was announced together with Google's donation of Kubernetes, its container orchestration system, as a seed project. Other founding members included major technology companies. Membership grew to hundreds of organizations.

Projects

CNCF projects progress through sandbox, incubating, and graduated stages. Graduated projects include Kubernetes, Prometheus, Envoy, containerd, Helm, etcd, and others. The foundation also runs certification programs such as Certified Kubernetes Administrator and the Kubernetes Certified Service Provider program.

Events

The CNCF organizes KubeCon + CloudNativeCon conferences in North America, Europe, and China.
