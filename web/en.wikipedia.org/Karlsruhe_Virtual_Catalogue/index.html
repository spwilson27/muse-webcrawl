Karlsruhe Virtual Catalogue

The Karlsruhe Virtual Catalogue (KVK; German: Karlsruher Virtueller Katalog) is a free online meta search engine for library and bookseller catalogues. It is operated by the library of the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), formerly the University of Karlsruhe, in Germany.

Description

The KVK allows a single search to be run simultaneously against numerous online public access catalogues (OPACs), union catalogues, digital libraries, and commercial bookseller databases in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and many other countries. Results from the individual catalogues are presented grouped by catalogue, with links to the source records. The service indexes access to hundreds of millions of books and serials.

History

The service was developed at the University of Karlsruhe in the mid-1990s and launched in 1996. It was one of the earliest large-scale library meta-catalogues on the World Wide Web and remains a widely used research tool, particularly for interlibrary loan and locating copies of works held in German-speaking research libraries.
