Bislama

Bislama is an English-based creole language and one of the official languages of Vanuatu, alongside English and French. It is the first language of many urban residents and a lingua franca for most of the country's population of diverse language groups.

History

Bislama developed during the 19th century from the pidgin English used by ni-Vanuatu labourers on plantations in Queensland and Fiji, blending English vocabulary with Melanesian grammatical structures.

Features

The vocabulary is largely English-derived but includes words from French and local languages. Reduplication is common, and pronouns distinguish inclusive and exclusive "we" and singular, dual, trial and plural numbers.
