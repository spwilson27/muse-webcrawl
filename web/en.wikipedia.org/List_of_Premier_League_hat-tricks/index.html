List of Premier League hat-tricks

Since the Premier League began in 1992, many players have scored a hat-trick (three goals in one match). This list records every hat-trick, the player, club, opponent and date.

Records

  • The first Premier League hat-trick was scored by Eric Cantona for Leeds United against Tottenham Hotspur in August 1992.
  • Sergio Agüero holds the record for the most Premier League hat-tricks with 12, ahead of Alan Shearer with 11.
  • The fastest hat-trick was scored by Sadio Mané for Southampton against Aston Villa in 2015, in 176 seconds.
  • Five-goal hauls have been achieved by players including Alan Shearer, Andy Cole, Jermain Defoe, Dimitar Berbatov and Sergio Agüero.
  • Some matches have featured two hat-tricks, and Erling Haaland scored multiple hat-tricks in his debut 2022–23 season.
