Standard gravity

Symbolgn
Value9.80665 m/s²
Defined1901 (CGPM)

The standard acceleration of gravity (or standard acceleration of free fall), sometimes abbreviated as standard gravity and denoted gn, is the nominal gravitational acceleration of an object in a vacuum near the surface of the Earth.

Value

It is defined by standard as exactly 9.80665 metres per second squared (approximately 32.174 ft/s²). This value was adopted by the 3rd General Conference on Weights and Measures in 1901. It is a conventional value based on the actual acceleration at about 45.5° latitude, and is used in the definition of units such as the kilogram-force and the standard atmosphere.
