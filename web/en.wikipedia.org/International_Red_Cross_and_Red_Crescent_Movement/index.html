International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement

The International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement is a global humanitarian network of 191 national societies, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC), and the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC).

Red Cross Movement
Founded1863 (ICRC)
FounderHenry Dunant
PrinciplesHumanity, impartiality, neutrality, independence

History

Inspired by Dunant's experience after the Battle of Solferino (1859), the movement led to the Geneva Conventions, the foundation of international humanitarian law.

Components

The ICRC works in armed conflict, the IFRC coordinates disaster relief for natural disasters, and national societies provide first aid, blood services and community health.
