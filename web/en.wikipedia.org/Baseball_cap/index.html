Baseball cap

A baseball cap is a type of soft cap with a rounded crown and a stiff bill projecting in front. The front of the cap typically displays a sports team logo or other design, and the back is usually adjustable.

History

The cap evolved from the short-brimmed "Brooklyn style" caps worn by baseball teams in the mid-19th century. By the early 20th century the modern long-billed cap was standard in the sport, protecting players' eyes from the sun. In the later 20th century the baseball cap became a ubiquitous item of casual fashion worldwide, worn by people of all ages and adopted widely in popular and youth culture.
