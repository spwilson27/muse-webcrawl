Lucia di Lammermoor

Lucia di Lammermoor is a dramma tragico in three acts by the Italian composer Gaetano Donizetti. Salvadore Cammarano wrote the Italian-language libretto, loosely based on Sir Walter Scott's 1819 novel The Bride of Lammermoor. It premiered at the Teatro di San Carlo in Naples on 26 September 1835.

Synopsis and music

Lucia Ashton, forced by her brother Enrico into a marriage for political advantage, is in love with her family's enemy Edgardo di Ravenswood. Deceived into believing Edgardo unfaithful, she signs the marriage contract, goes mad on her wedding night — the celebrated "mad scene," with its famous coloratura writing and flute (originally glass harmonica) obbligato — and kills her bridegroom before dying; Edgardo then takes his own life. The sextet "Chi mi frena in tal momento" is among the most famous ensembles in opera.
