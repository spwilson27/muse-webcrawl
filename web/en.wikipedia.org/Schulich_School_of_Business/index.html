Schulich School of Business

The Schulich School of Business is the business school of York University in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Founded in 1966 as York's Faculty of Administrative Studies, it was renamed in 1995 following a major donation from Canadian business magnate and philanthropist Seymour Schulich, whose endowment helped transform it into one of Canada's leading business schools.

Programs and reputation

Schulich offers undergraduate (BBA, iBBA), MBA and specialized master's programs, executive education, and a PhD program. It was an early mover in international business education, establishing one of the first International MBAs and a campus in Hyderabad, India, and it maintains exchange partnerships with business schools worldwide. The school has frequently been ranked among the top business schools in Canada and internationally, and its Seymour Schulich Building, designed by Siamak Hariri, opened on York's Keele campus in 2004. Notable program strengths include real estate and infrastructure, business ethics, responsible business, and financial services.
