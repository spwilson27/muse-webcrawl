Papal encyclical

A papal encyclical is a letter sent by the pope to the bishops of the Roman Catholic Church — and often to all the faithful — concerning Catholic doctrine, moral questions or social teaching. Encyclicals are traditionally known by their opening Latin words.

Notable examples

Famous encyclicals include Rerum novarum (1891) on labour and capital, Pacem in terris (1963) on peace, Humanae vitae (1968) on birth control, and Laudato si' (2015) on the environment.

Authority

Encyclicals rank high among ordinary papal teaching documents, though they are distinct from solemn definitions and apostolic constitutions.
