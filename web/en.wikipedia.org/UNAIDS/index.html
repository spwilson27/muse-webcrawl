UNAIDS

Formed1996
HeadquartersGeneva, Switzerland
Full nameJoint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS

UNAIDS, the Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS, is the main advocate for accelerated, comprehensive and coordinated global action on the HIV/AIDS pandemic. It began operations in 1996 and is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.

Structure and mission

UNAIDS unites the efforts of eleven UN cosponsors — including WHO, UNICEF, UNDP and the World Bank — and works with governments, civil society and people living with HIV in more than 90 countries. It collects and publishes epidemiological data, promotes human-rights-based approaches, and mobilises political commitment and resources.

Goals

The programme has championed global targets such as the 95-95-95 testing and treatment goals and the aim of ending AIDS as a public health threat by 2030, in line with the Sustainable Development Goals.
