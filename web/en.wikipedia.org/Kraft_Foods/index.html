Kraft Foods

Kraft Foods Inc.
Founded: 1903, Chicago, Illinois
Founder: James L. Kraft
Fate: Split in 2012 into Mondelēz International and Kraft Foods Group

Kraft Foods Inc. was an American multinational food manufacturing and processing conglomerate, headquartered in Chicago. It grew from James L. Kraft's cheese wholesale business founded in 1903.

History

Kraft introduced processed cheese and products such as Kraft Dinner (macaroni and cheese), Miracle Whip, Velveeta and Philadelphia cream cheese. It merged with General Foods in the Philip Morris era and acquired Nabisco (2000) and Cadbury (2010). In 2012 the company split: its global snack business became Mondelēz International, while its North American grocery business became Kraft Foods Group, which merged with Heinz in 2015 to form Kraft Heinz.

Brands

Historic Kraft brands include Oscar Mayer, Maxwell House, Planters, Jell-O, Kool-Aid and Cadbury chocolate brands outside the United States.
