English rugby union system

The English rugby union system is the structure of league and cup competitions for rugby union clubs in England, governed by the Rugby Football Union (RFU). It is organised as a pyramid of leagues with promotion and relegation between levels.

Structure

At the top sits the fully professional Premiership, followed by the Championship, then National League 1 and National League 2, beneath which regional and county leagues extend down many levels to amateur club rugby. Clubs progress between divisions through end-of-season promotion and relegation.

Cups

Alongside the leagues, clubs compete in cup competitions including the Premiership Rugby Cup and, at lower levels, the RFU's national knock-out cups graded by league level.
