List of castles in England

There are more than 1,500 castle sites in England, ranging from Norman motte-and-bailey earthworks to grand fortified palaces. Many were built during and after the Norman Conquest of 1066; several hundred are reasonably well preserved or restored. This list includes notable castles by county.
