USS Enterprise (CV-6)

USS Enterprise (CV-6) was a Yorktown-class aircraft carrier of the United States Navy, launched in 1936 and commissioned in 1938. Nicknamed "the Big E," she was one of only three American carriers commissioned before World War II to survive the war, and she became the most decorated US naval vessel of that conflict, earning 20 battle stars.

Enterprise was narrowly spared at Pearl Harbor, arriving after the attack of 7 December 1941. Her aircraft fought at the Battle of Midway, where her dive bombers helped sink Japanese carriers; at the Eastern Solomons and Santa Cruz Islands, where she was repeatedly damaged; and through the Guadalcanal campaign, the Philippine Sea, Leyte Gulf, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa. In late 1942, with Saratoga torpedoed and Hornet sunk, a sign on her hangar deck read "Enterprise vs Japan."

She was badly damaged by kamikaze attacks in 1945 and was decommissioned in 1947. Despite preservation efforts, she was sold for scrap in 1958. Her name was carried on by the nuclear-powered USS Enterprise (CVN-65), and later by a Gerald R. Ford-class carrier, CVN-80.
