Madden NFL

Madden NFL is an American football video game series developed by EA Tiburon for EA Sports. The franchise, named after Pro Football Hall of Fame coach and commentator John Madden, was created in 1988 as John Madden Football and has released new editions annually since 1990. It is one of the best-selling video game franchises of all time.

History

The original game, designed with input from Madden himself — who insisted on realistic 11-on-11 play — appeared on home computers before moving to consoles with the Sega Genesis edition of 1990, which made the series a hit. Since 2005 EA has held the exclusive licence for NFL teams, players, and stadiums in simulation football games. John Madden appeared on the cover until 1999; thereafter star players have featured, giving rise to the light-hearted superstition of the "Madden curse" said to affect cover athletes.

Gameplay and modes

Modern editions feature exhibition play, Franchise mode, the card-collecting Madden Ultimate Team, and story modes. Player ratings, published each season, are a widely discussed part of the franchise's culture. The series has been used in televised esports competitions and by broadcasters to illustrate real plays.

Impact

With annual sales in the millions and cumulative sales well over one hundred million copies, Madden NFL has shaped how fans understand football strategy and has become a staple of American sports gaming culture.
