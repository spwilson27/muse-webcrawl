Filmi

Filmi ("film-y") refers to music composed for Indian popular films, especially Hindi-language Bollywood cinema. Filmi songs, sung by playback singers, are central to the structure and marketing of Indian films.

Blending Indian classical and folk traditions with Western orchestration and later electronic styles, filmi has produced composers such as R. D. Burman and A. R. Rahman and singers such as Lata Mangeshkar and Kishore Kumar, and it dominates South Asian popular music listening.
