Vice admiral (abbreviated as VADM) is a three-star commissioned officer rank in the United States Navy, the United States Coast Guard, the United States Public Health Service Commissioned Corps, and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps. It ranks above rear admiral (two-star) and below admiral (four-star).

Vice admiral is equivalent to the rank of lieutenant general in the other uniformed services. It is the highest permanent rank in the United States Coast Guard, whose commandant holds the rank while in office.

Appointment and tenure

In the United States armed forces, the rank of vice admiral is a temporary appointment tied to a specific position of importance and responsibility. Officers are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate for three-star positions, which typically carry a tour length of three years. Three-star billets include the commanders of numbered fleets, deputy commanders of combatant commands, directors of Navy staff elements, and the superintendents of service academies. When an officer leaves a three-star assignment without being nominated for another, they generally revert to their permanent rank of rear admiral.

Statutory limits cap the number of three-star officers on active duty in each service, and officers must retire after 38 years of commissioned service or at age 64 unless waived or appointed to a higher grade.

History

The rank of vice admiral was first established in the United States Navy in 1864, during the Civil War, when David Farragut was promoted to the newly created grade. Farragut subsequently became the Navy's first full admiral in 1866. The three-star grade was used sporadically thereafter and became a standard feature of the flag-officer structure in the twentieth century as fleet organizations grew.

Insignia

A vice admiral wears three silver stars on shoulder boards and sleeve stripes consisting of one broad gold stripe below two narrower stripes on the service dress blue uniform. The rank flag of a Navy vice admiral is a blue flag with three white stars.
