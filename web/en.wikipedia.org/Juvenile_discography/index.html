Juvenile discography

The discography of American rapper Juvenile consists of studio albums, collaboration albums, mixtapes and singles released from 1995 onward, primarily through Cash Money Records. His commercial peak came with 400 Degreez (1998), certified quadruple platinum in the United States.

Collaboration albums

With the Hot Boys, Juvenile released Get It How U Live! (1997), Guerrilla Warfare (1999) and Let 'Em Burn (2003). With Birdman he released Just Another Gangsta (2019).

Notable singles

His best-known singles include "Ha" (1998), "Back That Azz Up" (1999), "Slow Motion" featuring Soulja Slim (2004, number one on the Billboard Hot 100), "Rodeo" (2006) and "Gotta Get It" (2009).
