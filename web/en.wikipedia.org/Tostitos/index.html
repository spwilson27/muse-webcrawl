Tostitos is a brand of tortilla chips and accompanying dips produced by the Frito-Lay division of PepsiCo. Introduced in 1979, Tostitos became one of the best-selling tortilla chip brands in the United States, marketed heavily in connection with parties, sporting events, and Mexican-style cuisine.

History

Frito-Lay began test-marketing Tostitos in 1979 and launched the brand nationally in the United States by 1980. The original line included Traditional and Nacho Cheese flavors. The brand name suggests "little toasted things" in Spanish. Over time the line expanded to include restaurant-style chips, bite-size rounds, scoops-shaped chips designed for dipping, and a range of jarred salsas and queso dips.

Sponsorship

From 1995 to 2014, Tostitos was the title sponsor of the Fiesta Bowl, a major American college football bowl game, which was known during that period as the Tostitos Fiesta Bowl.
