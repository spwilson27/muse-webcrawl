Gen Con

Gen Con is the largest tabletop game convention in North America by both attendance and number of events. It was acquired by former Wizards of the Coast CEO Peter Adkison in 2002. Gen Con 2024 was held at the Indiana Convention Center and Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis, Indiana, and drew nearly 71,000 unique attendees.

History

The convention was founded in 1968 by Gary Gygax as the Geneva Convention, a gathering of about a hundred wargamers in Lake Geneva, Wisconsin — the name is also a pun on the Geneva Conventions. It became the home convention of TSR, and the annual venue for the launch of major Dungeons & Dragons products and the D&D Championship. After growth in Milwaukee, where it was held from 1985 to 2002, the convention moved to Indianapolis in 2003.

Events

Gen Con hosts thousands of scheduled gaming events, including role-playing games, board games, card games, miniatures wargames, and live-action role-playing, alongside an exhibit hall of game publishers, an auction, a costume contest, and the ENNIE Awards for role-playing game products. Since 2017 the convention has expanded into Lucas Oil Stadium to accommodate its growth.
