Pohnpei State

State of the Federated States of Micronesia
Capital: Kolonia
Largest island: Pohnpei
Population: approx. 36,000 (2010)
Official language: Pohnpeian (with English)

Pohnpei State is one of the four states of the Federated States of Micronesia (FSM), located in the western Pacific Ocean. The state consists of the island of Pohnpei — the largest and highest island in the FSM, and home to the national capital, Palikir — together with a number of outlying atolls and islands including Ant, Pakin, Mokil, and Pingelap. The state capital is Kolonia.

Geography

Pohnpei island is a lush, mountainous volcanic island of about 334 square kilometres, famous for some of the highest rainfall on Earth and for the Sokehs ridge overlooking Kolonia. Its interior rises to Mount Nanlaud, the highest point in the FSM at about 782 metres.

History

Pohnpei is best known for Nan Madol, a ruined stone city built on artificial islets in a lagoon off the island's eastern shore, which served as the ceremonial and political centre of the Saudeleur dynasty until about 1628. The island was subsequently ruled by Spain, Germany, Japan, and the United States (as part of the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands) before Micronesian independence.

Culture

Traditional society is organized in chiefdoms led by paramount chiefs (nahnmwarki) and a parallel line of priestly/noble chiefs (nahnken). Sakau (kava) ceremonies remain central to communal life.
