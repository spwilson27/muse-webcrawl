OhmyNews (Korean: 오마이뉴스) is a South Korean online news website founded in 2000 by journalist Oh Yeon-ho. It pioneered citizen journalism: alongside a small staff of professional editors, most of its articles are written by tens of thousands of registered "citizen reporters" whose submissions are fact-checked and edited before publication.

History and influence

OhmyNews launched at a moment when South Korean media were dominated by a few conservative newspapers. Its participatory model — under the slogan "Every citizen is a reporter" — attracted a young, politically engaged readership. The site is credited with helping mobilise voters in the 2002 presidential election won by Roh Moo-hyun, who granted OhmyNews his first post-election interview. At its peak the site drew millions of daily readers and was studied worldwide as a model for participatory media.

Editions and decline

An English-language international edition with citizen reporters from around the world ran from 2004 to 2010. Like many early online outlets, OhmyNews later faced financial pressure from portal aggregators and social media, and restructured; the Korean site continues to publish with its citizen-reporter model.
