Johann Tserclaes, Count of Tilly (1559–1632), was a field marshal who commanded the Catholic League's forces and later the Imperial army during the Thirty Years' War.

Campaigns

A veteran of Spanish service against the Dutch and the Ottomans, Tilly won decisive victories at White Mountain (1620), Wimpfen, Höchst, Stadtlohn and Lutter (1626), subduing Bohemia, the Palatinate and Denmark's intervention. His storming of Magdeburg in May 1631 ended in a fire that destroyed the city and killed most of its inhabitants — one of the war's worst atrocities.

Defeat and death

Tilly was defeated by Gustavus Adolphus of Sweden at Breitenfeld (1631) and again at the River Lech (Rain) in April 1632, where he was mortally wounded by a cannon shot, dying at Ingolstadt days later.
