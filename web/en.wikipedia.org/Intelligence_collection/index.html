Intelligence collection

Intelligence collection is the gathering of information by intelligence agencies and other organizations to support decision-making, typically as part of the intelligence cycle. Collection is planned against identified requirements and carried out through a range of disciplines, known as the "INTs".

Collection disciplines

Major disciplines include human intelligence (HUMINT), gathered by case officers and agents; signals intelligence (SIGINT), from intercepted communications and electronic emissions; imagery intelligence (IMINT), from satellites and reconnaissance aircraft; measurement and signature intelligence (MASINT); and open-source intelligence (OSINT), from publicly available material. Geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) integrates imagery and mapping data.

Management

Collection management matches available collection assets to priority intelligence requirements, evaluates the reporting produced, and retasks collection as needs change. Excessive or unfocused collection can overwhelm analysis, so modern doctrine emphasizes requirements-driven tasking and evaluation of source reliability.
