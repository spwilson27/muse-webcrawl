Whitney Smith

Whitney Smith
Born: February 26, 1940, Arlington, Massachusetts
Died: November 17, 2020
Occupation: Vexillologist, author
Known for: coining "vexillology"; founding the Flag Research Center

Whitney Smith Jr. (February 26, 1940 – November 17, 2020) was an American vexillologist and the leading popularizer of the systematic study of flags. He coined the term vexillology in 1957 and founded the Flag Research Center in Winchester, Massachusetts, in 1962, editing its journal The Flag Bulletin for decades.

Career

Smith studied at Harvard University and Boston University, earning a doctorate in political science. He wrote or co-authored dozens of books, including Flags Through the Ages and Across the World (1975) and The Flag Book of the United States, and contributed flag articles to reference works such as Encyclopaedia Britannica. He helped design or advise on flags including that of Guyana and worked with governments, the United Nations and the Olympic movement.

Legacy

Smith was a founder of the International Federation of Vexillological Associations (FIAV) and of the North American Vexillological Association. His personal library and archives formed one of the world's largest flag collections; portions are now held at institutions including the University of Texas at Austin.
