Svarga

Svarga (Sanskrit: स्वर्ग), also known as Indraloka or Svargaloka, is the celestial abode of the devas in Hindu cosmology, ruled by Indra, king of the gods. It is conceived as a paradise of pleasure situated on and above Mount Meru.

Description

Svarga is described in the Puranas as a realm of gardens, celestial nymphs (apsaras), divine musicians (gandharvas) and wish-fulfilling trees. Souls that have accumulated merit reside there in bliss until their karma is exhausted, after which they are reborn; Svarga is therefore not a final liberation (moksha) but a temporary reward within samsara.

In the epics

In the Mahabharata, the hero Arjuna visits Indraloka, and the Pandavas' final journey leads them toward Svarga. The capital of Svarga is Amaravati, famed for its splendour.
