The MD Helicopters MD Explorer is a twin-engine light utility helicopter developed by McDonnell Douglas Helicopter Systems and later produced by MD Helicopters. Originally designated MD 900, it is notable for the first civil application of the NOTAR anti-torque system on a production helicopter.

Design

The Explorer uses the NOTAR system, which replaces the exposed tail rotor with a fan-driven circulation-control tail boom and jet thruster, making it one of the quietest helicopters in its class and safer for ground crews. The composite airframe seats two crew and up to six passengers, and versions include the MD 900 with PW206A engines and the improved MD 902 with PW207E engines and upgraded avionics.

Operations

The type entered service in the mid-1990s and found its main market in air ambulance, police and corporate roles, where its low noise signature is valued in urban operations. Operators have included air medical services in Europe and the United States and several law-enforcement agencies.
