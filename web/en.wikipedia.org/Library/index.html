Library

A library is a collection of books and other information resources made accessible to a defined community for reference or borrowing. Libraries provide physical and digital access to books, periodicals, manuscripts, films, maps and databases, and are staffed by librarians.

TypesPublic, academic, national, special, digital

History

Early libraries include the Library of Ashurbanipal and the Great Library of Alexandria. Monasteries preserved texts in medieval Europe, and public libraries spread in the 19th century. See Library of Congress and legal deposit.

Types

Public libraries serve communities; academic libraries support universities; national libraries collect a nation's publications; special libraries serve organisations; digital libraries provide online collections.

Organisation

Materials are catalogued with systems such as Dewey Decimal Classification and Library of Congress Classification, and increasingly discovered through online catalogues.
