Game over

"Game over" is a message in video games that informs the player that their play session is finished. The phrase originated in arcade games, where it was displayed when the player had exhausted their lives and had no credits remaining. Early arcade machines such as pinball tables used illuminated lettering; the phrase became standard on video arcade cabinets in the 1970s and remains a universal convention, appearing in games such as Space Invaders and Pac-Man.

Usage

The game over screen traditionally offers the player the option to continue — originally by inserting more coins — or to enter their initials into a high-score table. In home games the message typically appears when the player character dies and no lives remain, or when a fail state is reached. Some games vary the message ("You Died" in Dark Souls) or invert the tradition humorously.

In popular culture

The phrase has passed into general usage, meaning any definitive end or failure, and appears in films, music, and political commentary. Variations such as "Game Over" screens have been widely parodied.
