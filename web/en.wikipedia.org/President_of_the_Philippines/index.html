President of the Philippines

The President of the Philippines (Filipino: Pangulo ng Pilipinas) is the head of state and head of government of the Republic of the Philippines, and commander-in-chief of the Armed Forces of the Philippines. The president is elected by direct popular vote for a single six-year term and may not seek re-election.

President of the Philippines
First holderEmilio Aguinaldo (1899)
ResidenceMalacañang Palace, Manila
Term lengthSix years, non-renewable

History

Emilio Aguinaldo led the revolutionary First Philippine Republic from 1899. Under the Commonwealth established in 1935, Manuel L. Quezon became the first president of a constitutionally elected government. Since independence in 1946, presidents have included Ferdinand Marcos, whose two-decade rule ended with the People Power Revolution of 1986, Corazon Aquino, Rodrigo Duterte, and Ferdinand Marcos Jr., who took office in 2022.

Powers

The president heads the executive branch, appoints cabinet officials with the consent of the Commission on Appointments, commands the military, and may declare martial law for up to 60 days subject to congressional review.
