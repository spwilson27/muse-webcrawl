Sweet potato pie

Image placeholder: a baked sweet potato pie.

Sweet potato pie is a traditional dessert pie in Southern United States cuisine, often served during the holidays, especially at Thanksgiving and Christmas, in place of or alongside pumpkin pie, to which it is visually and texturally similar.

Description and preparation

The pie consists of a smooth custard filling of mashed sweet potatoes, milk or cream, eggs, sugar and spices such as cinnamon, ginger and nutmeg, baked in an open pastry shell without a top crust. It is often served with whipped cream or marshmallow topping. Recipes commonly call for boiling or baking the sweet potatoes before mashing, and the filling may be flavoured with vanilla, orange zest or bourbon.

History

Sweet potato pie descends from European custard pie traditions combined with the African use of sweet potatoes and yams. Enslaved African cooks in the American South adapted the dish, and it became a central part of African-American and soul food cooking. Singer Patti LaBelle's branded sweet potato pie became a viral sensation in 2015. The pie is related to other Southern desserts in this collection such as sweet potato dishes generally.
