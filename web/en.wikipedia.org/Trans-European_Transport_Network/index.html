Trans-European Transport Network

The Trans-European Transport Network (TEN-T) is a planned network of roads, railways, airports and water infrastructure in the European Union. The TEN-T policies support the application and development of an integrated network covering all EU member states and aim to guarantee the accessibility and connectivity of all regions of the Union.

Structure

The network has a dual-layer structure: a comprehensive network, to be completed by 2050, and a core network of the most important connections, structured around nine core network corridors such as the Rhine–Alpine and Scandinavian–Mediterranean corridors. A 2024 revision of the TEN-T regulation merged the core and extended core networks and set completion for 2040.

Projects

Notable TEN-T projects include the Brenner Base Tunnel, the Fehmarn Belt fixed link, Rail Baltica, and the Lyon–Turin base tunnel. Funding comes partly from the Connecting Europe Facility.
