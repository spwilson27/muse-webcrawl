Rogers Sports & Media

Rogers Sports & Media
Parent: Rogers Communications
Headquarters: Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Division: Sports and media properties

Rogers Sports & Media is the sports and media division of Rogers Communications, the Canadian telecommunications and media company. The division groups Rogers' broadcasting assets and its stakes in major sports properties, including ownership of the Toronto Blue Jays of Major League Baseball and the Rogers Centre stadium in Toronto.

Assets

The division's principal holdings include the Sportsnet group of television channels, Sportsnet radio stations, and, following a reorganisation of Rogers' media businesses, its conventional and specialty television services such as Citytv and FX Canada, as well as radio stations across Canada and publishing remnants of the former Rogers Publishing business. Rogers Sports & Media also manages Rogers' minority stake in Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment (MLSE), owner of the Toronto Maple Leafs, Toronto Raptors, Toronto FC, and Toronto Argonauts.

Sports rights

Rogers holds major Canadian broadcast rights, most notably a long-term national NHL rights agreement with the National Hockey League (a 12-year deal announced in 2013 worth about C$5.2 billion, followed by a further 12-year extension announced in 2025), as well as rights to Blue Jays baseball, and various other leagues distributed on Sportsnet.

History

The division took its current form around 2020, when Rogers consolidated its media and sports holdings under the Rogers Sports & Media banner. In 2024 Rogers announced an agreement to acquire Bell's stake in MLSE, which would raise its ownership to a controlling majority, subject to league and regulatory approvals.
