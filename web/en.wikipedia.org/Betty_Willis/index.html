Betty Willis

Born20 May 1923, Overton, Nevada
Died19 April 2015, Mesquite, Nevada
OccupationGraphic designer, artist
Known for"Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas" sign

Betty Willis (20 May 1923 – 19 April 2015) was an American visual artist and graphic designer best known for designing the iconic "Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas" sign, erected in 1959 on the Las Vegas Strip.

The sign

Working for Western Neon, Willis designed the diamond-shaped sign with its distinctive lettering and starburst as a gift to the city; she never copyrighted the design, considering it her contribution to Las Vegas. The sign became one of the most recognisable landmarks in the United States and was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2009.

Later life

Willis worked in the neon sign industry for decades and continued to receive recognition for the sign late in life. She died in 2015 at the age of 91.
