Revell is a manufacturer of plastic scale model kits, founded in the United States in 1943 by Lewis H. Glaser. Originally a plastics company, it produced its first model kits in the early 1950s and became one of the best-known model brands in the world, famous for kits of aircraft, ships, cars and military vehicles.

History

The company began as Precision Specialties in Hollywood, California, making plastic products including toy train sets. In 1951 it released its first injection-moulded model kit, a replica of the Maxwell automobile. Revell expanded internationally, founding Revell AG in Bünde, West Germany, in 1956, which later became Revell GmbH. In 1986 Revell merged with its rival Monogram to form Revell-Monogram.

Products

Revell kits span scales such as 1:24, 1:32, 1:48 and 1:72, covering subjects from Second World War aircraft to contemporary automobiles and spacecraft. The company's EasyKit and snap-together lines target younger builders, while its detailed kits remain popular with hobbyists.
