The Port of Brunswick is a deepwater seaport in Brunswick, Georgia, on the Atlantic coast of the United States, operated by the Georgia Ports Authority. It is one of the nation's leading ports for roll-on/roll-off cargo, particularly the import and export of automobiles and heavy machinery.

Terminals

The Colonel's Island Terminal is the port's main ro-ro facility and one of the largest auto-processing terminals in the United States, serving major carmakers and machinery manufacturers. Mayor's Point Terminal handles forest products and breakbulk cargo. The port's harbor is reached through St. Simons Sound.

Incidents

In September 2019 the car carrier Golden Ray capsized in St. Simons Sound at the harbor entrance with some 4,200 vehicles aboard; the wreck removal, completed in 2021, was one of the largest and most complex salvage operations in American history.
