The Land Transport Authority (LTA) is a statutory board under the Ministry of Transport of the Government of Singapore, responsible for planning, operating, and maintaining Singapore's land transport infrastructure and systems. It was formed on 1 September 1995 through the merger of several transport agencies.

Responsibilities

The LTA plans and builds the Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) and Light Rail Transit (LRT) networks, manages roads and expressways, and regulates public transport operators and private vehicles. It administers policies that keep traffic flowing in the dense city-state, including the Certificate of Entitlement (COE) vehicle quota system and Electronic Road Pricing (ERP), the world's first electronic congestion charging scheme, introduced in 1998.

Initiatives

The authority also promotes walking, cycling, and active mobility, develops intelligent transport systems, and leads long-term land transport master plans aimed at a car-lite Singapore.
