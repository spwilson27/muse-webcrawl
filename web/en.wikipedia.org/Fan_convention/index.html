Fan convention

A fan convention (also known as a con or fan meeting) is an event in which fans of a particular topic gather to participate in and hold programs and other events, and to meet experts, famous personalities, and each other. Some also incorporate commercial activity. The term dates to 1942, and fan conventions are most often associated with science fiction and fantasy, but the genre also includes anime, gaming, comics, horror, film, television and many other fandoms.

History

The first science fiction convention is generally considered to be the gathering in Leeds, England, in 1937, and the first World Science Fiction Convention (Worldcon) was held in New York in 1939 alongside the World's Fair. Conventions multiplied after the Second World War, and media-based conventions surged in the 1970s with the popularity of Star Trek. Large modern conventions such as San Diego Comic-Con and Dragon Con draw tens or hundreds of thousands of attendees.

Typical features

  • Panels and talks with creators, actors and writers;
  • A dealers' room or exhibition hall selling merchandise;
  • Costuming and cosplay, including masquerades and costume contests;
  • Screenings, gaming rooms and art shows;
  • Autograph and photograph sessions with guests;
  • Social events, dances and fan-run parties.

Types of conventions

Conventions may be fan-run and non-profit, in the tradition of Worldcon, or commercial operations run for profit by entertainment companies. Anime conventions (such as Anime Expo), comic conventions, gaming conventions (such as Gen Con and PAX) and horror conventions each serve particular fandoms; some are invitation-only or professional in character.
