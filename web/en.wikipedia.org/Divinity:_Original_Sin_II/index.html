Divinity: Original Sin II

Divinity: Original Sin II is a role-playing video game developed and published by Larian Studios. The sequel to Divinity: Original Sin (2014), it was partially funded through a Kickstarter campaign that raised over $2 million, and was released for Windows in September 2017 after a period in early access, with console versions following in 2018.

Gameplay

Set in the fantasy world of Rivellon, the game casts the player as a "Godwoken" — a wielder of the mystical energy called Source, hunted by the Divine Order. It features turn-based tactical combat built around elemental surface interactions (fire, water, oil, poison and so on), extensive environmental manipulation, and a flexible classless character system. Players may create a custom character or choose one of several origin characters with personal storylines, and the game supports cooperative multiplayer for up to four players, as well as a Game Master mode for creating custom campaigns.

Reception

The game received widespread critical acclaim for its writing, freedom of choice and combat system, with aggregate scores on Metacritic around 93 for the PC version, and is frequently cited among the best role-playing games of its decade. An enhanced Definitive Edition was released in 2018. Larian went on to develop Baldur's Gate 3.
