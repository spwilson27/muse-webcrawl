List of prime ministers of Iceland

The prime minister of Iceland is the head of government. The office dates to home rule in 1904, first held by Hannes Hafstein.

  • Hannes Hafstein — 1904–1905, first
  • Jón Sigurðsson — independence era leader (president later)
  • Sveinn Björnsson — regent and first president
  • Ólafur Thors — served several terms in the mid-20th century
  • Bjarni Benediktsson (1908) — 1960s
  • Jóhanna Sigurðardóttir — 2009–2013
  • Sigmundur Davíð Gunnlaugsson — 2013–2016
  • Bjarni Benediktsson (born 1970) — 2017 and 2024
  • Katrín Jakobsdóttir — 2017–2024
  • Kristrún Frostadóttir — from late 2024

Coalition governments are the norm under Iceland's multi-party system.
