World Uyghur Congress
Founded: 16 April 2004, Munich
Type: Umbrella organisation
Focus: Uyghur human rights and self-determination

The World Uyghur Congress (WUC) is an international umbrella organisation of Uyghur exile groups, founded in Munich, Germany in 2004 by merging Uyghur youth and national congress organisations. It advocates for the rights, religious freedom and self-determination of Uyghurs, principally in Xinjiang, China.

Leadership and activities

Notable presidents have included Isa Yusuf Alptekin's successors, Rebiya Kadeer (2006–2017) and Dolkun Isa (elected 2017). The WUC publishes reports, lobbies governments and UN bodies, and organises demonstrations. China designates it a terrorist/separatist organisation, a characterisation the WUC rejects, stating it promotes peaceful advocacy.
