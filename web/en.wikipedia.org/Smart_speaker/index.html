Smart speaker

A smart speaker is a type of loudspeaker and voice command device with an integrated virtual assistant that offers interactive actions and hands-free activation with the help of one "hot word" (or several "hot words"). Smart speakers are designed to be used throughout the home, where they can play music and audio, answer questions, set alarms and timers, provide weather and news, and control smart home devices such as lights, thermostats and locks.

History

The category was popularized by Amazon's Echo, released widely in 2015 with the Alexa voice assistant. Google followed with Google Home (later Google Nest) using Google Assistant in 2016, and Apple launched the HomePod with Siri in 2018. Other manufacturers, including Sonos, Bose and many Chinese makers such as Xiaomi and Baidu, entered the market, and prices fell sharply as basic models became inexpensive.

Technology

Smart speakers contain a microphone array that listens for a wake word, after which speech is streamed to cloud servers for recognition and processing. Many models also serve as hubs for smart-home protocols. Because microphones are always on, the devices have raised privacy concerns; manufacturers have faced criticism over human review of recordings and have added mute switches, on-device processing features, and controls for deleting voice history.

Uses

Surveys consistently show the most common uses are playing music, asking factual questions, checking the weather and setting timers, with smart-home control and shopping by voice less frequent.
