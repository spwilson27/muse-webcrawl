Blackmail

Blackmail
Type: Criminal offence
Related: Extortion, coercion

Blackmail is an act of coercion using the threat of revealing or publicizing either substantially true or false information about a person or people unless certain demands are met. It is a criminal offence in most jurisdictions, closely related to extortion.

Etymology

The word derives from payments rendered by Scottish Highlanders and Border farmers to freebooters in return for protection from raids, "mail" being an old Scots word for rent or tribute.

Legal treatment

Modern statutes typically criminalize making unwarranted demands with menaces. Blackmail differs from extortion in that the threatened act — disclosure — may itself be lawful, whereas extortion usually involves threats of violence or other illegal acts.
