Robert Bylot

Robert Bylot was an English explorer of the early 17th century who took part in several voyages in search of the Northwest Passage through the Canadian Arctic.

Voyages

Bylot sailed as a mate on Henry Hudson's 1610–1611 voyage in the Discovery; after the crew mutinied and set Hudson adrift in Hudson Bay, Bylot helped navigate the ship back to England, where he was among those who escaped punishment. In 1615 and 1616 he commanded the Discovery himself, with William Baffin as pilot; the 1616 voyage explored and named Baffin Bay and its inlets — Smith Sound, Jones Sound and Lancaster Sound — though the explorers doubted that Lancaster Sound offered a passage west.

Legacy

Bylot Island, off the northern coast of Baffin Island in Nunavut, is named after him.
