Special forces

Special forces or special operations forces (SOF) are military units trained to conduct special operations. NATO has defined special operations as "military activities conducted by specially designated, organized, selected, trained and equipped forces using unconventional techniques and modes of employment".

Capabilities

Special forces capabilities include reconnaissance, direct action, counter-terrorism, counter-insurgency, foreign internal defence, unconventional warfare, hostage rescue, high-value target operations, intelligence operations and mobility operations. Such units typically operate in small teams, often behind enemy lines, and are selected through gruelling assessment processes.

History

Although elite and specialised units have existed throughout military history, modern special forces emerged during the Second World War, with units such as the British Commandos, the Special Air Service, the German Brandenburgers and the United States Army Rangers. During the Cold War and after, most major militaries established permanent special forces formations.

Notable units

Well-known special forces units include the British SAS and SBS, the United States' Delta Force, Navy SEALs and Green Berets, Russia's Spetsnaz formations, Israel's Sayeret Matkal, and India's MARCOS and Para SF.
