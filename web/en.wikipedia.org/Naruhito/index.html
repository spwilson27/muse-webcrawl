Naruhito

Naruhito
Reign: May 1, 2019 – present
Born: February 23, 1960, Tokyo, Japan
Predecessor: Akihito
Spouse: Masako Owada (m. 1993)
Issue: Aiko, Princess Toshi
Era name: Reiwa

Naruhito (born 1960) is the Emperor of Japan, the 126th monarch of the world's oldest continuous hereditary monarchy. He ascended the Chrysanthemum Throne on May 1, 2019, following the abdication of his father, Emperor Akihito — the first abdication by a Japanese emperor in over two centuries. His reign is designated the Reiwa era.

Early life and education

Naruhito was born in Tokyo, the eldest son of then-Crown Prince Akihito and Crown Princess Michiko. He studied history at Gakushuin University and wrote a thesis on medieval water transport, then spent two years at Merton College, Oxford, studying the history of transportation on the River Thames — an experience he described in his memoir The Thames and I. He is an accomplished viola player and an honorary president of events concerning water issues, reflecting his longstanding scholarly interest in waterways.

Family and reign

In 1993 he married Masako Owada, a former diplomat; their daughter, Princess Aiko, was born in 2001. Because Japanese law restricts succession to males, his heir is his younger brother, Crown Prince Akishino. As emperor, Naruhito performs the constitutional role of "symbol of the State and of the unity of the People," conducting state ceremonies, receiving foreign dignitaries, and presiding over events such as the opening of the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games.
