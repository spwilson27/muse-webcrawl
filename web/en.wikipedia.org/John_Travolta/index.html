John Travolta

John Joseph Travolta (born February 18, 1954) is an American actor, singer, and producer. He began acting in television before becoming a leading man in Hollywood films. Travolta rose to fame during the 1970s, appearing on the television sitcom Welcome Back, Kotter (1975–1979) and starring in the box office successes Carrie (1976), Saturday Night Fever (1977), and Grease (1978). His acting career declined throughout the 1980s, but he enjoyed a resurgence in the 1990s with his role in Pulp Fiction (1994), and he has since starred in films including Get Shorty (1995), Broken Arrow (1996), Face/Off (1997), Swordfish (2001), and Hairspray (2007).

Early life

Travolta was born in Englewood, New Jersey, the youngest of six children. His mother was an actress and singer, and his father a former semi-professional football player. He dropped out of high school at 16 to pursue acting in New York.

Comeback and later career

Quentin Tarantino's Pulp Fiction cast Travolta as hitman Vincent Vega, earning him an Academy Award nomination for Best Actor and reviving his career. He later portrayed Robert Shapiro in the television series The People v. O. J. Simpson: American Crime Story (2016), receiving a Primetime Emmy Award as a producer and a nomination for acting.

Personal life

Travolta married actress Kelly Preston in 1991; she died in 2020. He is a certified private pilot and a prominent member of the Church of Scientology.
