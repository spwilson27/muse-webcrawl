The 2022 Commonwealth Games, officially the XXII Commonwealth Games and commonly known as Birmingham 2022, were held in Birmingham, England, from 28 July to 8 August 2022. Birmingham stepped in as host after Durban, South Africa was stripped of the Games in 2017 for failing to meet commitments.

The Games

Around 5,000 athletes from 72 Commonwealth nations and territories competed in 20 sports. The programme included, for the first time at a major multi-sport event, more medal events for women than for men, and featured women's T20 cricket, 3x3 basketball and wheelchair 3x3 basketball for the first time. Esports were held as a pilot event.

Medal table and highlights

Australia topped the medal table with 67 gold medals and 178 medals overall, followed by hosts England and Canada. The opening ceremony at the rebuilt Alexander Stadium featured a giant mechanical bull that later became a popular public artwork in the city.
