CONCACAF Nations League

The CONCACAF Nations League is an international association football competition contested by the senior men's national teams of the member associations of CONCACAF, the regional governing body of North America, Central America and the Caribbean.

Format

Launched in 2019 on the model of the UEFA Nations League, the tournament divides the 41 member teams into leagues (A, B and C) with promotion and relegation. League A concludes with a finals tournament among the group winners. Results also serve as a qualifying pathway for the CONCACAF Gold Cup.

History

The United States won the first three editions, defeating Mexico in the inaugural final in June 2021 — a 3–2 win after extra time in Denver — and again in 2023 and 2024. The competition replaced many international friendlies with meaningful competitive matches for smaller Caribbean nations in particular.
