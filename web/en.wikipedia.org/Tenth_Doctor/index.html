The Tenth Doctor is an incarnation of the Doctor, the protagonist of the BBC science fiction television series Doctor Who. He is played by David Tennant, who appears in three full series of the programme as well as special episodes (2005–2010), and returned for the 50th anniversary special and the 60th anniversary specials.

Character

The Tenth Doctor is energetic, charismatic, and fast-talking, often dressed in a pinstriped suit with trainers. Beneath his cheerfulness he carries grief and anger over the Time War, and his storylines explore his loneliness, his relationships with companions Rose Tyler, Martha Jones, and Donna Noble, and his reluctance to regenerate — expressed in his final line, "I don't want to go."

Reception

Tennant's Doctor is frequently voted the most popular incarnation of the character in polls, and is credited with helping to cement the revived series' success.
