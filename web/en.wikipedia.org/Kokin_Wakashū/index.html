Kokin Wakashū

The Kokin Wakashū (古今和歌集, "Collection of Japanese Poems of Ancient and Modern Times"), usually abbreviated as Kokinshū, is an imperial anthology of Japanese waka poetry compiled in the early 10th century by order of Emperor Daigo.

Compiledc. 905 CE
Commissioned byEmperor Daigo
Chief editorKi no Tsurayuki
Poems1,111 waka in 20 books

Contents

The anthology contains 1,111 poems arranged in twenty books by topic — the seasons, love, travel, mourning and others. Its prefaces, especially Ki no Tsurayuki's Japanese preface, are foundational works of Japanese poetic criticism.

Influence

As the first of the imperially commissioned anthologies (the Nijūichidaishū), the Kokinshū set the standard for waka composition and aesthetics for centuries.
