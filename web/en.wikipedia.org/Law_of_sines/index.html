Law of sines

In trigonometry, the law of sines states that in any triangle the lengths of the sides are proportional to the sines of their opposite angles:

a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C = 2R

where R is the radius of the triangle's circumcircle.

Applications

The law is used to solve triangles — finding unknown sides and angles — in surveying and navigation, especially in the angle-side-angle case. In the side-side-angle case it can yield the "ambiguous case" of two possible triangles. A spherical law of sines applies to triangles on a sphere and was developed by medieval Islamic astronomers.
