List of World Heritage Sites in the Caribbean

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) designates World Heritage Sites of outstanding universal value to cultural or natural heritage. The Caribbean region includes inscribed sites across Cuba, Haiti, Jamaica, Saint Lucia, Barbados, Dominica, the Dominican Republic, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Puerto Rico and Curaçao, among others.

Notable sites

Cuba has the most sites in the region, including Old Havana and its fortifications, the Viñales Valley, and the historic centre of Trinidad. Other prominent inscriptions include the Pitons Management Area in Saint Lucia (2004), a natural site protecting two dramatic volcanic spires rising from the sea; the National History Park — Citadel, Sans Souci, Ramiers in Haiti; Historic Bridgetown and its Garrison in Barbados; Morne Trois Pitons National Park in Dominica; and Brimstone Hill Fortress in Saint Kitts.

Criteria

Sites are inscribed under cultural criteria (i)–(vi) or natural criteria (vii)–(x). Caribbean inscriptions reflect the region's colonial fortifications and plantation history, its historic port cities, and volcanic and marine ecosystems of global significance.
