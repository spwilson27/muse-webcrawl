Voxel

A voxel is a value on a regular grid in three-dimensional space — the 3D equivalent of a pixel, its name blending "volume" and "pixel". Voxels are used to represent volumetric data in medical imaging (CT and MRI scans), scientific visualisation, and video games.

Applications

In medicine, voxel data from scanners is rendered by volume rendering or marching-cubes surface extraction. In games, voxel engines power games such as Minecraft, whose worlds are built from large cubic blocks; true voxel rendering stores density or material values at grid points. Voxel-based modelling is also used in 3D printing and fluid simulation.

Data size

Volumetric data grows cubically with resolution, so voxel systems rely on compression, sparse octrees and level-of-detail. A sparse voxel octree stores only occupied regions, making very large worlds tractable.
