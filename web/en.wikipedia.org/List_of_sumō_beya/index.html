List of sumō beya

In professional sumo wrestling in Japan, a heya (部屋, often romanised as beya in this context) is a stable where wrestlers live and train together under a stablemaster (oyakata), a retired wrestler who holds elder stock in the Japan Sumo Association. All professional wrestlers must belong to a heya. There are roughly forty-five active stables, most located in Tokyo's Ryōgoku district near the Ryōgoku Kokugikan arena.

Organisation

Heya are grouped into five ichimon (clans): Dewanoumi, Nishonoseki, Tokitsukaze, Takasago, and Isegahama. The ichimon coordinate elder stock, elections to the Sumo Association board, and mutual support among stables. A stable's name usually derives from its founder's elder name. Only one wrestler per heya may hold the same ring surname in some cases, and stablemasters may not manage wrestlers from more than one heya.

Life in a heya

Wrestlers live communally, rising before dawn for training; the lowest-ranked wrestlers cook (the stable stew, chankonabe) and serve their seniors. Recruits typically join at age fifteen after junior high school. Foreign-born wrestlers are limited to one per stable. Stables occasionally close when a master retires without a successor, with wrestlers transferring to other heya in the same ichimon.
