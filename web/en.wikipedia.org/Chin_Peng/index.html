Chin Peng

Chin Peng (21 October 1924 – 16 September 2013), born Ong Boon Hua, was a Malayan communist politician and guerrilla leader. He was the long-time secretary-general of the Malayan Communist Party (MCP) and the leader of its armed wing, the Malayan National Liberation Army, during the Malayan Emergency (1948–1960). He remains a controversial figure in Malaysia and Singapore, variously seen as a freedom fighter against colonialism and as a terrorist.

Early life and resistance

Chin Peng was born in Sitiawan, Perak, in British Malaya, to a middle-class ethnic Chinese family that ran a bicycle and tyre business. He became involved in anti-Japanese activities and joined the Malayan Communist Party in 1940. During the Japanese occupation he served as a leading figure in the Malayan Peoples' Anti-Japanese Army (MPAJA), liaising with British Force 136 operatives who parachuted into Malaya. For his wartime service he was made an Officer of the Order of the British Empire and received two campaign medals — honours revoked after he took up arms against the British.

The Emergency

In 1947 Chin Peng became secretary-general of the MCP at only 23, after his predecessor Lai Teck absconded with party funds. Following a wave of violence, the colonial government declared a state of emergency in June 1948 and banned the party. Chin Peng led the ensuing guerrilla campaign from the jungle. The Emergency effectively ended in 1960, though the MCP's armed struggle resumed from bases along the Thai border until the Hat Yai peace agreements of 2 December 1989 formally ended the insurgency.

Later life

Chin Peng lived in exile in Thailand and China. The 1989 peace accord allowed MCP members to return to Malaysia, but despite court action and widespread debate, successive Malaysian governments refused to permit his return. His 2003 memoir, My Side of History, recorded his account of the struggle. He died in Bangkok in 2013, and the Malaysian government also barred the return of his ashes, prompting continued public controversy.
