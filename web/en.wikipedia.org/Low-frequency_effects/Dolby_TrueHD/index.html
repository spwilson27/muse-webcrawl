Dolby TrueHD

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Developed byDolby Laboratories
TypeLossless audio codec
LFE channelDiscrete, up to 120 Hz

Dolby TrueHD is a lossless multi-channel audio codec developed by Dolby Laboratories using Meridian Lossless Packing. It includes a discrete low-frequency effects (LFE) channel: the ".1" channel of 5.1 and 7.1 soundtracks, band-limited to frequencies below roughly 120 Hz and reproduced by a subwoofer.

Because TrueHD compression is mathematically lossless, the LFE channel decoded in the home is identical to the one on the studio master, preserving the level, timing, and impact of the bass effects exactly as the re-recording mixer balanced them against the main channels. Dolby Atmos mixes delivered on disc also ride within a TrueHD stream, and they likewise rely on the same discrete LFE channel for low-frequency reproduction.
