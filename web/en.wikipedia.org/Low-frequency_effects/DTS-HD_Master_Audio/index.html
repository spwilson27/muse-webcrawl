DTS-HD Master Audio

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Developed byDTS, Inc.
TypeLossless audio codec
LFE channelDiscrete, up to 120 Hz

DTS-HD Master Audio is a lossless surround sound codec developed by DTS. Like other modern cinema formats it carries a discrete low-frequency effects (LFE) channel — the ".1" of a 5.1 or 7.1 mix — which is reproduced by a subwoofer and supplies the deep bass impact of the soundtrack.

The codec's core-plus-extension design embeds a lossy DTS core that contains the full mix, including the LFE channel, so legacy equipment reproduces the complete soundtrack; the lossless extension then restores every channel, LFE included, to bit-perfect fidelity with the master. On Blu-ray the format supports LFE content at sample rates up to 96 kHz for multichannel material, far beyond the channel's 120 Hz bandwidth, ensuring the bass track is preserved exactly as mixed.
