Match of the Day

Match of the Day is a BBC Television football highlights and analysis programme, first broadcast on 22 August 1964, showing highlights of English top-flight matches. One of the BBC's longest-running shows, it is a fixture of Saturday evenings during the football season and holds a Guinness World Record as the longest-running football television programme in the world.

History

The first edition showed Liverpool's 3–2 win over Arsenal at Anfield and was watched by some 20,000 viewers — fewer than attended the match. Originally on BBC Two, it moved to BBC One in 1966 as colour television arrived. For many years it was the only way most fans could watch match highlights. The programme lost Premier League highlights to ITV between 2001 and 2004 before regaining them. Its theme tune, written by Barry Stoller in 1970, is among Britain's most recognisable.

Presenters and pundits

Presenters have included Kenneth Wolstenholme, David Coleman, Jimmy Hill (also a long-serving pundit), Des Lynam, and Gary Lineker, who hosted from 1999 until his departure in 2025. Long-running pundits and commentators include Alan Hansen, Alan Shearer, Ian Wright and John Motson, the programme's best-known commentator for decades. A Sunday companion, Match of the Day 2, launched in 2004.
