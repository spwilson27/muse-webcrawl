Sun Lutang
Born1860, Hebei, China
Died1933
StyleXingyiquan, Baguazhang, Taijiquan

Sun Lutang (1860–1933) was a renowned master of Chinese internal martial arts and the founder of the Sun style of taijiquan. Unusually, he achieved high mastery in all three principal internal arts — xingyiquan, baguazhang, and taijiquan — and was a scholar of Confucian and Daoist philosophy as well as a prolific author.

Training

Sun studied xingyiquan under Guo Yunshen, baguazhang under Cheng Tinghua, and Wu (Hao) style taijiquan under Hao Weizhen. Nicknamed "Monkey Sage" for his agility, he later synthesised his knowledge into his own taiji style, characterised by agile stepping, flowing movements, and the integration of bagua footwork.

Writings and legacy

Between 1915 and 1932 Sun published five influential books on the internal arts, among the first to set out their principles and forms for a general readership. The Sun style is now one of the five major family styles of taijiquan recognised in China.
