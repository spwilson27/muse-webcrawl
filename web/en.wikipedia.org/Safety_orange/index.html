Safety orange

Safety orange (also known as blaze orange, hunter orange and international orange in related variants) is a hue used to set objects apart from their surroundings, particularly in contrast to the blue of the sky. It is used in safety and hunting contexts to maximize visibility.

Uses

Safety orange is specified in ANSI standards (e.g. ANSI Z535 and high-visibility clothing standard ANSI/ISEA 107) for safety equipment, traffic cones, construction barriers, and high-visibility garments. Hunters in many jurisdictions are required to wear blaze orange to reduce accidental shootings, as deer and most game animals cannot distinguish the color well, while humans perceive it vividly. A brighter variant, international orange, is used for aerospace applications, including on the Golden Gate Bridge and NASA launch/entry suits in a distinct shade.

Color coordinates

Safety orange is commonly given as hex triplet #FF6700. It is distinct from school bus chrome yellow and from the deeper international orange (#FF4D00) used in engineering.
