The Chipettes are a fictional girl group of animated anthropomorphic chipmunks — Brittany, Jeanette, and Eleanor Miller — created by Janice Karman for the Alvin and the Chipmunks franchise. They are the female counterparts of the Chipmunks (Alvin, Simon, and Theodore).

Characters

Brittany, the oldest, is vain and ambitious and is the counterpart of Alvin. Jeanette, the middle sister, is intelligent, shy, and clumsy, mirroring Simon. Eleanor, the youngest, is athletic and sweet-natured, mirroring Theodore. Orphaned in Australia, the sisters emigrate to America and are adopted by the kindly Miss Miller.

Appearances

The Chipettes debuted in the 1983 animated series Alvin and the Chipmunks, starred in the 1987 film The Chipmunk Adventure, and appear in the live-action/CGI film series beginning with Alvin and the Chipmunks: The Squeakquel (2009), where Brittany, Jeanette, and Eleanor are voiced by Christina Applegate, Anna Faris, and Amy Poehler respectively.
