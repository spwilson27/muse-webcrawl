Fox William Mulder is a fictional FBI special agent and one of the two protagonists of the Fox science fiction–supernatural television series The X-Files, portrayed by David Duchovny. Mulder's peers dismiss his many theories on extraterrestrial activity as outlandish and coming from an eccentric mind, considering him a "spooky" character — a nickname also used for him, "Spooky Mulder".

Character arc

An Oxford-educated psychologist with an eidetic memory, Mulder rose quickly in the FBI as a profiler before becoming obsessed with the X-Files, unexplained cases he believes are connected to a government conspiracy concealing extraterrestrial contact. His driving motivation is the disappearance of his younger sister Samantha, whom he believes was abducted by aliens when he was twelve. Partnered with the sceptical Dana Scully, he pursues "the truth" through the series' monster-of-the-week cases and its unfolding mythology arc.

Reception

Mulder is regarded as one of television's most iconic characters; the dynamic between the believing Mulder and the sceptical Scully helped define the series and influenced later genre television. Duchovny's portrayal won critical acclaim and a Golden Globe Award.
