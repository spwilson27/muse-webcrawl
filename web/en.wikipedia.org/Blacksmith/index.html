Blacksmith
Occupation: Metalworking
Tools: Forge, anvil, hammer, tongs

A blacksmith is a metalsmith who creates objects from wrought iron or steel by forging the metal, using tools to hammer, bend and cut. Blacksmiths produce tools, agricultural implements, weapons, hardware and decorative work.

The forge

Work is heated in a forge fired by coal, charcoal or propane until it glows, then shaped on an anvil. Key techniques include drawing out, upsetting, bending, punching and forge welding.

History

Ironworking dates to the Iron Age; the smith held a central place in village life for millennia. Industrialization displaced much of the trade, but artistic blacksmithing survives as a craft and hobby.
