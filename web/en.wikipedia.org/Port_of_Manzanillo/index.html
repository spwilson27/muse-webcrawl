The Port of Manzanillo is a seaport in Manzanillo, Colima, on Mexico's Pacific coast. It is Mexico's busiest port by container traffic, handling several million TEUs annually, and the principal Pacific gateway for trade between Mexico and Asia.

Operations

The port handles containers, bulk minerals, agricultural products, vehicles, and petroleum products. Container terminals are operated by concessionaires including SSA Marine and Contecon (ICTSI). Rail and highway links connect Manzanillo to Guadalajara and Mexico City.

History

Manzanillo was granted the status of a city and port in the 19th century and grew rapidly in the late 20th century as Pacific trade expanded; major modern expansion of the container terminals took place in the 1990s and 2000s, including development of the northern zone.
