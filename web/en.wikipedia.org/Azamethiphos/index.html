Azamethiphos

Azamethiphos
Chemical formula: C9H10ClN2O5PS
Class: Organophosphate insecticide
Mode of action: Acetylcholinesterase inhibitor

Azamethiphos is an organophosphate insecticide. Like other organophosphates, it acts as a cholinesterase inhibitor, disrupting the nervous system of target insects. It is used to control flies in animal housing, and in aquaculture it has been used to control sea lice on farmed salmon.

Uses

Azamethiphos is applied as a spray or bait in livestock buildings to control houseflies and other nuisance flies. In salmon farming, it has been used as a bath treatment against parasitic sea lice (Lepeophtheirus salmonis and Caligus species). It is classified as moderately hazardous, and its use is regulated because of its toxicity to aquatic invertebrates and other non-target organisms.

Toxicology

As an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor, azamethiphos shares the toxicological profile of other organophosphates. Exposure can cause cholinergic symptoms, and precautions are required for handlers. Environmental concerns focus on effects on crustaceans and other marine life when used in fish farming.
