Science Europe

Science Europe is an association of major research funding and research performing organisations in Europe. It was established in October 2011 and is based in Brussels, Belgium. The association brings together more than 40 member organisations from over 30 countries, which together spend substantial public funds on research each year, and works to promote the conditions for scientific research in Europe.

Activities

Science Europe advocates for science in European policy-making, particularly in relation to the European Union's framework programmes for research and innovation such as Horizon Europe and its predecessor Horizon 2020. It publishes position statements, roadmaps, and practical guides on topics including open access to publications, research data management, research assessment, research integrity, and the circulation of researchers. It was closely involved in the development of Plan S and the cOAlition S initiative for open access publishing.

Membership and governance

Members include national research councils and large research performing organisations, such as the UK Research and Innovation, the German Research Foundation (DFG), CNRS of France, the Max Planck Society, and the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC). Science Europe is governed by a Governing Board elected by its General Assembly of member organisations.
