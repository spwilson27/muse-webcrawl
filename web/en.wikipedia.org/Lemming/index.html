Lemming

Lemmings are small rodents, usually found in or near the Arctic in tundra biomes. They are subfamily Arvicolinae along with voles and muskrats, with stout bodies, short legs and tails, and thick fur. Well-known species include the Norway lemming and the collared lemmings.

Behaviour and ecology

Lemming populations fluctuate dramatically in multi-year cycles, influencing predators such as snowy owls and Arctic foxes. Contrary to a popular misconception — reinforced by staged scenes in a 1958 nature film — lemmings do not deliberately commit mass suicide, though migrations can lead to accidental deaths when crossing water.
