Legal drama

A legal drama (or courtroom drama) is a genre of film, television and theatre focused on lawyers, courts, trials and the legal system, often built around a case that raises moral or social questions.

Examples

Classic examples include the films To Kill a Mockingbird and 12 Angry Men, and television series such as Perry Mason, L.A. Law, The Practice, Ally McBeal and The Good Wife. British examples include Rumpole of the Bailey and Silk.

Conventions

The genre typically compresses legal procedure for dramatic effect, featuring courtroom speeches, cross-examinations and last-minute revelations.
