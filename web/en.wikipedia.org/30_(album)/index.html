30 is the fourth studio album by the English singer-songwriter Adele, released on November 19, 2021, by Melted Stone and Columbia Records. Like her previous albums 19, 21, and 25, it is named after the age at which she began recording it. The album chronicles her divorce from Simon Konecki, her experience of motherhood, and the burden of fame.

Content and style

Working chiefly with producer Greg Kurstin alongside Inflo, Max Martin, and others, Adele incorporated soul, jazz, and gospel elements. The lead single, "Easy on Me," topped charts in more than 25 countries and broke streaming records on release. Other tracks include "My Little Love," which weaves in voice notes of conversations with her young son, and the closing ballad "Love Is a Game."

Reception

The album received widespread acclaim and was the world's best-selling album of 2021, selling over five million copies that year despite its late release date. It topped charts in over 30 countries, won British Album of the Year at the 2022 Brit Awards, and "Easy on Me" won the Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance.
