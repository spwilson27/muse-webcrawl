Gulfstream Aerospace Corporation is an American aircraft manufacturer and a wholly owned subsidiary of General Dynamics, headquartered in Savannah, Georgia. Founded in 1958 as the business-aircraft division of Grumman, it designs and builds a line of business jets that have become status symbols of corporate and private aviation.

History

The original Gulfstream I, a turboprop introduced in 1959, was followed by the jet-powered Gulfstream II in 1966. The company passed through ownership by American Jet Industries and Chrysler before General Dynamics acquired it in 1999. Successive flagships — the GIV, GV, G550 and G650 — pushed speed, range and cabin comfort; the G650ER can fly nearly 14,000 km non-stop at Mach 0.90.

Current products

The flagship G700, certified in 2024, offers the largest cabin in business aviation and a range of about 7,750 nautical miles; the longer-range G800 followed. The G500 and G600 feature the Symmetry Flight Deck with active control sidesticks, while the super-midsize G280 is built in Israel. More than 3,000 Gulfstream aircraft are in service worldwide, including military variants used as VIP transports and special-missions platforms.
