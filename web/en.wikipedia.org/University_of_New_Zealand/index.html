University of New Zealand

The University of New Zealand was a federal university system in New Zealand from 1870 to 1961. It did not teach directly but set examinations and awarded degrees through affiliated colleges that later became independent universities.

History

Established by the New Zealand University Act 1870, it was modelled on the University of London. Its colleges included Otago, Canterbury, Auckland and Victoria.

Dissolution

In 1961 the university was dissolved and its constituent institutions became independent degree-granting universities.

Legacy

Many New Zealand universities trace their origins to colleges of the federal university.
