Blackstar (album)

Blackstar (stylised as ★) is the twenty-fifth and final studio album by the English musician David Bowie. It was released on 8 January 2016, Bowie's 69th birthday, through his ISO label, Columbia Records, and RCA Records. Bowie died of liver cancer two days after its release, and the album was subsequently understood as a parting statement, filled with imagery of mortality.

Recording and style

Bowie recorded the album in New York with producer Tony Visconti and a band of local jazz musicians led by saxophonist Donny McCaslin. Drawing on art rock, jazz, and experimental influences — and artists such as Kendrick Lamar and Boards of Canada — the album features the ten-minute title track, "Lazarus" (also used in Bowie's off-Broadway musical of the same name), "'Tis a Pity She Was a Whore", and "I Can't Give Everything Away".

Reception and legacy

Blackstar received universal critical acclaim and commercial success, debuting at number one in numerous countries and becoming Bowie's first number-one album on the US Billboard 200. It won five Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year nominations recognition, and is frequently ranked among the greatest albums of the 2010s. The hidden image of a starfield inside the die-cut album sleeve became a famous design detail.
