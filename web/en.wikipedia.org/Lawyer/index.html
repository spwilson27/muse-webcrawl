Lawyer

A lawyer, also called an attorney or advocate, is a licensed professional who advises clients about the law and represents them in legal proceedings. Lawyers practise in fields including litigation, corporate law, criminal defence and prosecution, family law, real estate and intellectual property.

Training and regulation

Qualification typically requires a law degree (such as the JD in the United States or LLB in Commonwealth countries), professional examinations — notably the bar examination — and admission to the bar of a jurisdiction. Lawyers are bound by codes of professional conduct covering confidentiality, conflicts of interest and duties to the court.

Structures

In England and Wales the profession is split between solicitors, who advise clients and prepare cases, and barristers, who specialise in advocacy. Many other systems have a fused profession. Lawyers work in private firms, as in-house counsel, in government and in legal aid.
