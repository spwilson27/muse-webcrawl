21st Century Fox, Inc. was an American multinational mass media corporation based in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. It was created on June 28, 2013, as the legal successor to News Corporation, when Rupert Murdoch split that company into a publishing concern (the new News Corp) and a film and television company.

Assets

The company controlled the Fox Entertainment Group, including the 20th Century Fox film studio, the Fox Broadcasting Company, Fox Sports, FX Networks, the National Geographic Partners joint venture, and a major stake in the Indian broadcaster Star India. It also held a 39 percent stake in the European pay-TV company Sky plc until 2018.

Acquisition by Disney

In December 2017, the Walt Disney Company announced an agreement to acquire most of 21st Century Fox for $52.4 billion in stock, a bid later raised to $71.3 billion in cash and stock after a competing offer from Comcast. The acquisition closed on March 20, 2019. The assets not sold to Disney — the Fox broadcast network, Fox News, Fox Sports' national channels, and the Fox studio lot in Los Angeles — were spun off to shareholders as the new Fox Corporation.
