Super cup

A super cup is a football (soccer) competition, usually a single-match trophy contested between the winners of a country's top league championship and its main domestic cup competition. Super cups typically open the domestic season and are often regarded as prestigious curtain-raisers rather than major honours.

Format and history

The concept dates to early 20th-century charity and champions' matches, such as England's FA Charity Shield (now the FA Community Shield), first played in 1908. Many federations adopted their own versions during the later 20th century. Most are one-off matches at neutral venues, though Spain's Supercopa de España became a four-team mini-tournament in 2019.

Examples

Well-known super cups include the Supercopa de España in Spain, the Supercoppa Italiana in Italy, the Trophée des Champions in France, the DFL-Supercup in Germany, and the FA Community Shield in England. At continental level, the UEFA Super Cup pits the winners of the UEFA Champions League against the winners of the UEFA Europa League.
