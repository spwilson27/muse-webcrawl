National War Museum (Malta)

The National War Museum is a museum in Valletta, Malta, housed within Fort Saint Elmo. It documents Malta's military history, with particular emphasis on the island's experience in the First and Second World Wars. It is managed by Heritage Malta.

History

The museum was first established in 1975 in the old Drill Hall of Fort Saint Elmo, the fort that bore the brunt of the Great Siege of Malta in 1565. It closed for a major refurbishment and reopened in 2015 as part of a wider restoration of the fort, with expanded galleries occupying the fort's historic spaces overlooking the Grand Harbour.

Collections

Exhibits cover the Great Siege, the French occupation, British colonial rule and the two world wars. The most prized artefacts relate to the Second World War siege of Malta (1940–43): the George Cross awarded to the island by King George VI in April 1942, the Gloster Sea Gladiator Faith — one of the three legendary biplanes Faith, Hope and Charity — the jeep "Husky" used by General Eisenhower, and a wreck of an Italian E-boat. Displays also include uniforms, weapons, decorations and personal effects of Maltese servicemen and civilians.
