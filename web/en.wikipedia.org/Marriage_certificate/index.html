Marriage certificate

A marriage certificate (also known as a marriage cert or marriage licence record) is an official statement that two individuals have undertaken a marriage contract. The certificate typically records the names of the spouses, the date and place of the marriage, and the officiant and witnesses.

In many jurisdictions a marriage licence must be obtained before the ceremony, and the signed certificate is filed with a government registrar as legal proof of the marriage, used for matters such as name changes, inheritance, and immigration.
