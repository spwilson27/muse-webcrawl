2018 New Caledonian independence referendum

An independence referendum was held in New Caledonia on 4 November 2018. Voters were asked whether New Caledonia should gain full sovereignty and become independent from France. The referendum was held under the terms of the 1998 Nouméa Accord, which ended a period of violent conflict and set a timetable for consultations on the territory's future.

Despite predictions of a larger victory margin, the "remain" side won with 56.7% of the vote against 43.3% for independence, on a turnout of about 81% of eligible voters. The result entitled pro-independence groups to request two further referendums, which were subsequently held in 2020 and 2021.
