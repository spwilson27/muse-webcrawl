The 2022 Winter Olympics, officially the XXIV Olympic Winter Games and commonly known as Beijing 2022, were held from 4 to 20 February 2022 in Beijing and Zhangjiakou, China. Beijing became the first city to host both the Summer and Winter Olympics, reusing several venues from the 2008 Summer Games, including the National Stadium.

COVID-19 measures and boycotts

The Games operated inside a strict "closed-loop" bubble because of the COVID-19 pandemic, with no general-public ticket sales. Several countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia, staged a diplomatic boycott over China's human rights record, sending athletes but no government officials.

Highlights

Norway topped the medal table with a record 16 gold medals and 37 medals overall. Chinese-American freestyle skier Eileen Gu won two golds and a silver for the host nation. A doping case involving 15-year-old Russian figure skater Kamila Valieva overshadowed the figure skating events. Seven new events were introduced, including monobob and big air skiing.
