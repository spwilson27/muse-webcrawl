AT&T Inc. is an American multinational telecommunications holding company headquartered in Dallas, Texas. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world and the largest provider of mobile telephone services in the United States. The modern AT&T descends from the Bell System and the original American Telephone and Telegraph Company, founded in 1885.

History

The original AT&T was founded in 1885 as a subsidiary of Alexander Graham Bell's Bell Telephone Company to build the first long-distance telephone network in the United States. It grew into the "Bell System" monopoly, operating local service through its Bell Operating Companies and long-distance service under the AT&T name. Its research arm, Bell Labs, produced fundamental inventions including the transistor and information theory.

In 1982, the United States Department of Justice settled its antitrust case against AT&T with a consent decree that required the divestiture of the local Bell Operating Companies, effective 1 January 1984. The seven "Baby Bells" (Regional Bell Operating Companies) took over local service, while AT&T retained long distance, equipment manufacturing and Bell Labs.

Reconstruction and modern company

Through a series of mergers the Baby Bells reconsolidated. Southwestern Bell Corporation (SBC), one of the regionals, acquired the original AT&T Corp. in 2005 and renamed itself AT&T Inc., reviving the brand. It then acquired BellSouth in 2006, unifying ownership of Cingular Wireless. The company expanded into pay television with DirecTV (2015) and media with Time Warner (2018, renamed WarnerMedia), but later reversed course: WarnerMedia was spun off and merged with Discovery in 2022 to form Warner Bros. Discovery, and DirecTV was divested, leaving AT&T focused on 5G wireless and fibre broadband networks.

Operations

AT&T's principal business is AT&T Communications, providing wireless services (competing with Verizon and T-Mobile US), consumer fibre broadband under the AT&T Fiber brand, and services to business customers. It is frequently ranked among the largest United States corporations by revenue.
