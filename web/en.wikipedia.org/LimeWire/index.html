LimeWire


LimeWire was a free peer-to-peer file sharing client for Windows, macOS, Linux and Solaris, using the Gnutella network and BitTorrent protocol. Launched 2000; shut down 2010.

LimeWire was a free peer-to-peer file sharing client for Windows, macOS, Linux and Solaris. Released in 2000 by Lime Wire LLC, it used the Gnutella network and also supported BitTorrent. At its peak it was installed on a large share of the world's PCs and was a major channel for both legal and unauthorised music sharing.

Shutdown

On 26 October 2010, a U.S. federal court ordered LimeWire to disable the searching, downloading and file-sharing functions of its software, in the case Arista Records LLC v. Lime Group LLC. In May 2011 the company agreed to pay $105 million in damages to record labels.

Legacy

Unofficial forks such as FrostWire continued development. The brand was later revived in unrelated ventures, including an NFT marketplace announced in 2022.
