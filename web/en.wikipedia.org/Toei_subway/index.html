Toei Subway

The Toei Subway (都営地下鉄, Toei chikatetsu) is one of two rapid transit networks in Tokyo, Japan, operated by the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Transportation (Toei). Together with the Tokyo Metro network it forms the subway system of the capital. Toei operates four lines with a total length of about 109 km and 106 stations.

Lines

  • Asakusa Line (A) – Nishi-magome to Oshiage, opened 1960; through-running with Keikyu and Keisei lines to both airports.
  • Mita Line (I) – Meguro to Nishi-takashimadaira, opened 1968.
  • Shinjuku Line (S) – Shinjuku to Moto-yawata, opened 1978; through service to the Keio New Line.
  • Oedo Line (E) – a loop-plus-branch line opened fully in 2000; noted for its deep stations and linear-motor trains.

History

The Tokyo Metropolitan Government built its first line, the Asakusa Line, after the older Eidan (now Tokyo Metro) network had been established; the license for Line 1 had originally been held by the city. Toei lines use three different track gauges (standard, cape, and the 1,372 mm Keio gauge), which complicates rolling-stock sharing but enables extensive through-running with suburban railways.
