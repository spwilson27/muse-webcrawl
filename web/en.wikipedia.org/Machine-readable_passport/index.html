Machine-readable passport
Standard: ICAO 9303
Features: Machine-readable zone (MRZ); biometric chip in e-passports

A machine-readable passport (MRP) is a machine-readable travel document (MRTD) with the data on the identity page encoded in optical character recognition format. Defined by the ICAO Document 9303 standard, most passports worldwide are MRPs.

Machine-readable zone

The identity page carries two lines of OCR-B text (for passports) encoding the holder's name, nationality, date of birth, document number and check digits, allowing rapid automated reading at border control.

Biometric passports

Modern e-passports add a contactless chip storing a digital photograph and other biometric data, indicated by the camera-like biometric passport symbol on the cover.
