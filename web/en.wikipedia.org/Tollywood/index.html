Tollywood

This article is about Telugu cinema, centred in Hyderabad. The name is also historically used for the Bengali film industry of Tollygunge, Kolkata.

Tollywood is the popular name for the Telugu-language film industry, based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. The name combines "Telugu" and "Hollywood". Telugu cinema is one of the largest film industries in India by output and box office, and since the 2010s its big-budget spectacles — dubbed and released across India — have made it a dominant force in the national market.


Ramoji Film City, Hyderabad, a major production centre

History

The first Telugu feature film, Bhakta Prahlada (1931), directed by H. M. Reddy, was also among India's earliest talkies. The industry was initially based in Madras (Chennai) and shifted to Hyderabad from the 1970s onward, a move encouraged by actor-producer Akkineni Nageswara Rao. The studio era produced mythologicals and social dramas starring N. T. Rama Rao, who later became chief minister of Andhra Pradesh. Ramoji Film City, among the world's largest studio complexes, opened in 1996.

Pan-Indian era

S. S. Rajamouli's two-part epic Baahubali (2015–2017) transformed the industry's ambitions: Baahubali 2: The Conclusion became one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time. Rajamouli's RRR (2022) won international acclaim, including an Academy Award for the song "Naatu Naatu". Other major stars of the modern era include Chiranjeevi, Mahesh Babu, Allu Arjun, Ram Charan, Jr NTR and Prabhas. The industry produces several hundred films a year and its music, action choreography and large-scale productions are widely imitated.
