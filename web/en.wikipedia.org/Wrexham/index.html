Wrexham

Wrexham (Welsh: Wrecsam) is a city in north-east Wales and the administrative centre of Wrexham County Borough. Historically in Denbighshire, it lies between the Welsh mountains and the lower Dee Valley, close to the border with England, roughly 13 miles (21 km) south of Chester. With a population of around 45,000 in the built-up area, it is the largest settlement in northern Wales. Wrexham was granted city status in 2022 as part of the Platinum Jubilee Civic Honours.

History

Human activity in the area dates to the Mesolithic period, and the Romans established a settlement at nearby Plas Coch. Wrexham grew as a market town in the Middle Ages; St Giles' Church, whose tower is counted among the Seven Wonders of Wales, dates largely to the late 15th and early 16th centuries. From the 18th century the town became an early centre of the Industrial Revolution — John "Iron-Mad" Wilkinson's Bersham Ironworks stood nearby, and the surrounding coalfield supported iron, steel, brewing and leather industries. Brewing became a signature trade: Wrexham was home to Britain's first lager brewery, founded in 1882. In 1934 the Gresford Colliery disaster killed 266 men, one of the worst mining tragedies in British history.

Modern Wrexham

Manufacturing and light industry remain important, with major employers on the Wrexham Industrial Estate, one of the largest in Europe. The town is home to Wrexham University (formerly Glyndŵr University) and to Coleg Cambria. Nearby landmarks include Erddig Hall, a National Trust property, and the Pontcysyllte Aqueduct, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, in the surrounding county borough.

Sport

Wrexham A.F.C., founded in 1864, is one of the oldest professional football clubs in the world and plays at the Racecourse Ground, the world's oldest international football stadium still in use. The club drew global attention after its 2020 purchase by actors Ryan Reynolds and Rob McElhenney, documented in the series Welcome to Wrexham, and achieved successive promotions in the 2020s, returning to the EFL in 2023 and reaching the Championship in 2025.
