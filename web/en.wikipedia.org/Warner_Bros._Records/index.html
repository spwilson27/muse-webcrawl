Warner Records (formerly Warner Bros. Records) is an American record label owned by Warner Music Group and headquartered in Los Angeles. It was founded in 1958 as the recorded-music division of the Warner Bros. film studio.

History

Jack L. Warner established the label partly after the success of film star Tab Hunter's recording for rival Dot Records. Early success came with the Everly Brothers and comedian Bob Newhart. Under Mo Ostin, who led the label from 1970, Warner Bros. became known for artist-friendly management and signed acts including Jimi Hendrix (via Reprise), Van Morrison, Fleetwood Mac, Prince, and Madonna.

Rebranding

In 2019 the label was renamed Warner Records after the film studio's licence for the name expired. Its roster and catalogue span pop, rock, hip hop and alternative music.
