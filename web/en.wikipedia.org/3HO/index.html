The 3HO Foundation — the Healthy, Happy, Holy Organization — is a movement founded in 1969 by Yogi Bhajan (Harbhajan Singh Yogi) to teach Kundalini Yoga and a yogic lifestyle in the West. Its name reflects Bhajan's motto that happiness is a birthright attainable through a healthy and holy life.

History

Bhajan arrived in Los Angeles in 1968 and began teaching yoga publicly, breaking with the tradition that Kundalini Yoga be taught in secrecy. 3HO was incorporated in 1969 and grew into a network of ashrams and teaching centers across North America and Europe; its headquarters later moved to Española, New Mexico. In the 1970s many members adopted Sikh practices and dress, and the related Sikh Dharma movement developed alongside it.

Practices and events

3HO promotes Kundalini Yoga as taught by Yogi Bhajan, a vegetarian diet, early-morning sadhana practice, and white-turban dress for teachers. Its signature events include the Summer Solstice Sadhana in New Mexico and the International Women's Camp. The affiliated Kundalini Research Institute certifies teachers, and 3HO participates in United Nations consultative activities as an NGO.
