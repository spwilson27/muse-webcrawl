Norfolk Southern Railway

The Norfolk Southern Railway (reporting mark NS) is a Class I freight railroad in the United States, and the largest subsidiary of the Norfolk Southern Corporation. It operates approximately 19,500 route miles in 22 eastern states and the District of Columbia, serving every major container port in the eastern United States.

A Norfolk Southern freight train

History

The modern company was formed in 1982 by the merger of the Norfolk and Western Railway and the Southern Railway, both profitable carriers, under the holding company Norfolk Southern Corporation, headquartered initially in Norfolk, Virginia. In 1999 NS acquired a majority share of Conrail jointly with CSX Transportation, greatly expanding its network in the Northeast.

Operations

Norfolk Southern moves freight including coal, intermodal containers, automobiles, chemicals, agriculture, and metals. Its corporate headquarters moved to Atlanta, Georgia in 2021. The railway came under intense scrutiny after the February 2023 train derailment in East Palestine, Ohio, which spilled hazardous chemicals and prompted federal regulatory action and litigation.
