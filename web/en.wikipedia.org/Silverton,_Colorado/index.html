Silverton is a statutory town in and the county seat of San Juan County, Colorado, United States. Nestled high in the San Juan Mountains at an elevation of 9,318 feet (2,840 m), Silverton is a former silver mining camp that has retained much of its Victorian-era character. The town's population was 622 at the 2020 census. The entire town is designated a National Historic Landmark District, the Silverton Historic District.

History

Prospectors drawn by gold discoveries in the San Juan Mountains in the early 1870s founded the settlement that became Silverton in a high valley known as Baker's Park. The town was platted in 1874 and grew rapidly once the Denver and Rio Grande Railroad reached it in 1882, allowing ore from the surrounding mines to be shipped out economically. Silver mining, and later gold, lead, zinc and copper, sustained the town for decades; Thomas Blair is credited with laying out the town, while notorious Greene Street became lined with saloons and gambling halls.

The mining economy faltered with the silver crash of 1893, revived in part through gold production, and declined steadily through the twentieth century. The last major mine, the Sunnyside, closed after the 1978 Lake Emma disaster, in which a glacial lake burst into the mine workings.

Tourism and the narrow gauge railroad

Today Silverton depends largely on tourism. The Durango and Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad, a heritage line running along the Animas River from Durango, brings thousands of visitors each summer. The town is also a gateway to the Alpine Loop backcountry byway, off-roading, and the Silverton Mountain ski area, known for expert-only guided terrain. Much of the 1969 film Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid and other westerns used the surrounding country as locations.

Geography and climate

Silverton lies in a flat valley floor along the Animas River surrounded by peaks exceeding 13,000 feet. Its high elevation gives it an alpine climate with long, snowy winters and cool summers; frost is possible in any month. U.S. Highway 550, the "Million Dollar Highway", connects Silverton with Durango to the south and Ouray to the north over Red Mountain Pass.
