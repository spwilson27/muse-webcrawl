Habib Bourguiba

Habib Bourguiba (3 August 1903 – 6 April 2000) was a Tunisian lawyer, nationalist leader and statesman who led the country from its independence from France in 1956 to 1987. He served as prime minister and then as the first President of the Republic of Tunisia from 1957 to 1987.

Independence movement

Bourguiba founded the Neo Destour party in 1934 and led the nationalist struggle, spending years in French prisons and exile. After independence in 1956 he became prime minister and, on the proclamation of the republic in 1957, president.

Presidency

He pursued sweeping modernization: free education, women's rights under the 1956 Code of Personal Status (abolishing polygamy), land reform and a secular-leaning state. His rule became increasingly authoritarian; he was declared president for life in 1975 and was deposed by Zine El Abidine Ben Ali in November 1987 on grounds of ill health.
