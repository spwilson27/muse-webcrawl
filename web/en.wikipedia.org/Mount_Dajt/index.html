Mount Dajt (Albanian: Mali i Dajtit) is a mountain in central Albania, just east of the capital Tirana. Its highest summit reaches 1,613 metres above sea level. The mountain and its surrounding forests form Dajti National Park, sometimes called the "natural balcony of Tirana".

Geography and nature

The mountain is part of the Skanderbeg Mountains. Its slopes are covered by pine, oak and beech forest, with canyons, waterfalls and caves. The Pelumbas Cave near the Erzen river is one of Albania's largest known caves. Snow commonly covers the summit in winter.

Access and recreation

The Dajti Ekspres cable car, opened in 2005, runs from the eastern outskirts of Tirana to a plateau near the summit, where there are restaurants, an adventure park and viewpoints. Hiking trails and a road also reach the upper mountain. A television transmission tower stands near the top.
