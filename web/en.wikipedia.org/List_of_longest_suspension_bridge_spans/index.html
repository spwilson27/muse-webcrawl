List of longest suspension bridge spans

A suspension bridge carries its deck on vertical suspenders hung from main cables draped between towers and anchored at each end. Suspension bridges achieve the longest spans of any bridge type. This list ranks completed suspension bridges by main span length. The longest is the 1915 Çanakkale Bridge in Turkey, with a main span of 2,023 metres — a figure chosen to commemorate the year 2023, the centenary of the Turkish Republic.

Historical progression

The Brooklyn Bridge (486 m, 1883) pioneered steel-wire suspension construction. The record passed through the George Washington Bridge (1,067 m, 1931), the Golden Gate Bridge (1,280 m, 1937), and the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge (1,298 m, 1964), before the Humber Bridge (1981) and then the Asian giants of the 1990s took the lead. The Akashi Kaikyō Bridge held the record for 24 years until the Çanakkale Bridge surpassed it in 2022.
