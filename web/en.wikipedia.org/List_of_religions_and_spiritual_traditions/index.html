List of religions and spiritual traditions

This list of religions and spiritual traditions groups the world's faiths by origin and family. Major world religions by adherents are Christianity (about 2.4 billion), Islam (about 1.9 billion), Hinduism (about 1.2 billion), Buddhism (about 300–500 million), folk religions, Sikhism, Judaism, Baháʼí, Jainism, Shinto and others.

Abrahamic religions

Judaism, Christianity (Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant and other churches), Islam (Sunni, Shia and other branches), the Baháʼí Faith, Rastafari, Druze and Samaritanism trace their traditions to Abraham and monotheism in the Middle East.

Indian (Dharmic) religions

Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism and Sikhism originated on the Indian subcontinent and share concepts such as dharma, karma, samsara and liberation, interpreted differently by each.

East Asian and other traditions

Taoism, Confucianism, Shinto, Chinese folk religion, Korean shamanism and Vietnamese folk religion form the East Asian sphere. Indigenous and folk traditions worldwide, African traditional religions, Afro-American religions such as Santería and Vodou, and modern movements including Neopaganism, Theosophy, Scientology and new religious movements are also catalogued.
