Lennon–McCartney

Lennon–McCartney is the songwriting partnership of John Lennon and Paul McCartney, the two principal writers of the Beatles. Under an agreement made before fame, songs by either — whether written together or alone — were credited jointly, producing one of the most successful catalogues in popular music.

Collaboration

Early songs such as She Loves You and I Want to Hold Your Hand were written genuinely eyeball to eyeball. From the mid-1960s the partners more often completed each other fragments or wrote separately, yet continued to sharpen each other work in the studio.

Legacy

The catalogue, published by Northern Songs, passed through contested ownership — notably Michael Jackson purchase of ATV in 1985 — and McCartney later regained US rights under copyright reversion rules. Roughly 180 jointly credited songs were recorded by the Beatles.
