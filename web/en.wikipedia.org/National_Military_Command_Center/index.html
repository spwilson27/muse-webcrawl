National Military Command Center

The National Military Command Center (NMCC) is part of the National Military Command System (NMCS). The NMCC is located in the Pentagon in Arlington, Virginia, and houses the primary work area and briefing space used by the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff and the Joint Staff's operations directorate (J-3). It is the main operational centre through which the United States Department of Defense monitors world events and directs U.S. military forces in crises.

Function

The NMCC is staffed around the clock by five rotating watch teams of operations officers. It provides the Secretary of Defense and the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff with information needed for decisions, transmits orders from the President and Secretary of Defense to the commanders of the unified combatant commands, and hosts secure video teleconferences during emergencies. Its Emergency Actions console is associated with the Emergency Action Messages used in nuclear command and control.

Related facilities

The NMCS also includes the Alternate National Military Command Center at the Raven Rock Mountain Complex in Pennsylvania (the "underground Pentagon") and airborne command posts such as the E-4B National Airborne Operations Center, which can assume the NMCC's functions if the Pentagon facility is disabled.
