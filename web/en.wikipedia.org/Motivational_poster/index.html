Motivational poster

A motivational poster is a type of poster commonly displayed in workplaces and schools, designed to inspire or encourage a particular attitude or behaviour. The classic format combines a striking photograph or artwork with a single inspiring word (such as Teamwork, Perseverance, or Success) and a short explanatory caption.

History

Inspirational wall art for offices traces back to motivational lithographs of the early 20th century; Elbert Hubbard's Roycroft press produced framed mottoes from around 1904. The modern photographic format was popularized in the 1970s–1990s by companies such as Successories (founded 1985), whose posters — a rowing crew at dawn for "Teamwork", an eagle for "Leadership" — became ubiquitous in corporate offices.

Parody

The genre's earnestness made it a target for parody. "Demotivational posters" from companies such as Despair, Inc. (founded 1998) invert the format with bleakly comic captions, and the style became a popular internet meme template in the 2000s, in which a framed image with a bold one-word title and a deadpan caption is used for humorous effect.
