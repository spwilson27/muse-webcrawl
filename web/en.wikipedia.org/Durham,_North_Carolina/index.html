Durham, North Carolina

Durham is a city in and the county seat of Durham County, North Carolina, United States. With a population of about 292,000 at the 2020 census, it is the fourth-most populous city in the state and forms part of the Research Triangle metropolitan region together with Raleigh and Chapel Hill.

History

Durham grew after the Civil War around a railroad station and the tobacco industry, becoming famous for Bull Durham tobacco and the Duke family's American Tobacco Company and Duke Power. Washington Duke's endowment transformed Trinity College into Duke University in 1924. The city's Parrish Street was known as "Black Wall Street" for its prominent African American businesses.

Today

Durham is a centre for medicine, research, and technology, anchored by Duke University and Duke University Hospital, and is near Research Triangle Park. The Durham Bulls baseball team was popularised by the 1988 film Bull Durham.
