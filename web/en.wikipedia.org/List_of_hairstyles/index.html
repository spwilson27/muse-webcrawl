List of hairstyles

This is a list of hairstyles — styles in which hair is cut, arranged or dressed. Hairstyles have signalled identity, status, fashion, and subculture throughout history.

Selected hairstyles

HairstyleDescription
AfroHair grown out naturally into a rounded shape
BeehiveTall, conical piled style popular in the 1960s

BunHair pulled back and coiled on top or back of the head
Buzz cutVery short hair clipped uniformly
CornrowsHair braided close to the scalp in rows
Crew cutShort on the sides with slightly longer hair on top
DreadlocksRope-like strands formed by matting or braiding
MohawkSides shaved with a central strip of longer hair
MulletShort at the front and sides, long at the back
PageboySmooth hair turned under at the ends, usually shoulder length
Pixie cutVery short layered cut
PompadourHair swept up and back from the forehead
PonytailHair gathered and tied at the back
QuiffForehead hair brushed upward and backward
