The Lost World (Doyle novel)

AuthorArthur Conan Doyle
Published1912
GenreAdventure / science fiction
SeriesProfessor Challenger

The Lost World is a science fiction novel by British writer Arthur Conan Doyle, published in 1912. It introduces the character of Professor George Edward Challenger and concerns an expedition to a remote plateau in South America where prehistoric animals still survive.

Plot

Journalist Edward Malone joins an expedition led by the irascible Professor Challenger, together with Professor Summerlee, Lord John Roxton, and their servant Zambo, to the Amazon basin. On an isolated tepui plateau they encounter dinosaurs, pterodactyls, and a tribe of ape-men at war with an Indigenous human tribe.

Influence

The novel helped popularize the "lost world" genre and has been adapted many times for film and television, beginning with a 1925 silent film featuring pioneering stop-motion effects by Willis O'Brien.
