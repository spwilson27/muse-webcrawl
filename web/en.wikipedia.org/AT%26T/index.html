AT&T Inc. is an American multinational telecommunications conglomerate headquartered in Dallas, Texas. It is one of the largest telecommunications companies in the world and the largest provider of mobile telephone services in the United States. The company's name derives from the American Telephone and Telegraph Company, founded in 1885 as a subsidiary of the original Bell System.

History

The original AT&T was established in 1885 to operate the first long-distance telephone network in the United States. For most of the 20th century it held a government-sanctioned monopoly over telephone service, operating through the regional Bell operating companies. In 1984, following an antitrust settlement with the U.S. Department of Justice, AT&T divested its local exchange operations into seven "Baby Bells." One of them, Southwestern Bell Corporation, later acquired the AT&T name after purchasing AT&T Corp. in 2005 and rebranding itself AT&T Inc.

Operations

AT&T provides wireless services under the AT&T brand and the prepaid Cricket Wireless brand, as well as fiber-optic broadband through AT&T Fiber. Between 2018 and 2022 the company owned the WarnerMedia entertainment conglomerate, which it spun off in a merger with Discovery, Inc. to form Warner Bros. Discovery. AT&T is a component of the S&P 500 index.
