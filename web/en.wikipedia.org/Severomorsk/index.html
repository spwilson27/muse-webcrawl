Severomorsk

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Severomorsk (Russian: Северомо́рск, lit. "northern sea [town]") is a closed town in Murmansk Oblast, Russia, located about 25 kilometres (16 mi) north of Murmansk on the eastern shore of the Kola Bay. It is the main administrative base of the Russian Northern Fleet. Population: 50,076 (2010 Census).

History

The settlement was founded as the village of Vayenga in the late 19th century; the name comes from the Vayenga River. During the Second World War, Vayenga served as an important naval air station, and Royal Air Force units operated from there in 1941 during Operation Benedict, defending the Arctic convoys to Murmansk.

After the war the village grew rapidly as the principal base of the Northern Fleet. In 1948 it was granted urban-type settlement status, and on April 18, 1951, it was granted town status and renamed Severomorsk. As a closed town, access is restricted to Russian citizens with special permission.

Naval base

Severomorsk hosts the headquarters of the Northern Fleet and berths for major surface combatants, including the aircraft carrier Admiral Kuznetsov when not deployed, the battlecruiser Pyotr Velikiy, and Slava-class cruisers. Nearby facilities include the Safonovo naval air station and submarine bases along the Kola Peninsula coast, such as Gadzhiyevo and Polyarny, the fleet's historic base.

Geography and climate

The town lies above the Arctic Circle at about 69° north latitude. Despite its far-northern location, the Kola Bay remains largely ice-free in winter thanks to the warm North Atlantic Current. The climate is subarctic, moderated by the sea, with long, dark winters — the polar night lasts from early December to mid-January — and short, cool summers with continuous daylight.

Transportation

Severomorsk is connected to Murmansk by road and by a railway branch. The Severomorsk-1 and Severomorsk-3 airfields serve naval aviation. Passenger movement in and out of the closed administrative-territorial formation is controlled at checkpoints.
