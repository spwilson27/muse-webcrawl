Intercontinental GT Challenge

CategoryGrand tourer endurance racing (GT3)
OrganiserSRO Motorsports Group
Inaugural season2016
ChampionshipsManufacturers' and drivers' titles

The Intercontinental GT Challenge is an international sports car racing series organised by the SRO Motorsports Group and contested by GT3-specification grand tourer cars. Founded in 2016, it links a small calendar of prestigious long-distance endurance races on different continents into a single championship, awarding manufacturers' and drivers' titles based on points scored at each event.

Format

Manufacturers nominate a limited number of cars at each round — crewed by professional drivers — to score points toward the manufacturers' championship, while other entries in the same races compete for local honours and class awards without affecting the global standings. Rounds are endurance races of between 8 and 24 hours' duration, attracting factory-supported GT3 teams from brands such as Audi, BMW, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Mercedes-AMG and Porsche.

Races

The calendar has centred on marquee events including the Bathurst 12 Hour in Australia, the 24 Hours of Spa in Belgium (the flagship round), the Suzuka 1000km (later Suzuka 10 Hours) in Japan, the Indianapolis 8 Hour in the United States and the Kyalami 9 Hour in South Africa; other rounds, such as the California 8 Hours at Laguna Seca and the Gulf 12 Hours, have appeared in some seasons. The majority of races predate the series and continue to crown their own overall winners independently of the championship.

History

The first season in 2016 comprised three rounds and was won by Audi in the manufacturers' standings. The series expanded to five rounds by 2019, survived disruption to international travel in 2020–2021 with reduced calendars, and has since consolidated around four to five rounds per season. It is regarded as the premier global championship for GT3 endurance racing, sitting alongside SRO's continental GT World Challenge series in Europe, America, Asia and Australia.
