Keeper of the Great Seal of Scotland

The Keeper of the Great Seal of Scotland is one of the great officers of state in Scotland, custodian of the Great Seal used to authenticate official documents. Since devolution, the office has been held by the First Minister of Scotland, who is keeper ex officio under the Scotland Act 1998.

Historically the keeper was often the Lord Chancellor of Scotland or a senior nobleman or cleric. After the Acts of Union 1707 a separate Scottish seal continued for Scottish affairs. Today the First Minister as Keeper issues documents under the Scottish seal, which is administered by Registers of Scotland.
