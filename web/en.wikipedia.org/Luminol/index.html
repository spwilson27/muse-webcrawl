Luminol

Luminol (C8H7N3O2) is a chemical that exhibits chemiluminescence, emitting a blue glow when mixed with an appropriate oxidising agent. It is a white-to-pale-yellow crystalline solid, first synthesised in the early 20th century.

Forensic use

Forensic investigators spray luminol solution at crime scenes to detect trace amounts of blood: the iron in haemoglobin catalyses the reaction with hydrogen peroxide, producing light visible in darkness. Luminol can react with other substances (some metals, bleach, plant peroxidases), causing false positives, and its application may dilute or damage other evidence, so it is used after other tests.
