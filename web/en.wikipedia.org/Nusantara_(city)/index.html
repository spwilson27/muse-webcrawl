Nusantara, officially the Capital City of Nusantara (Indonesian: Ibu Kota Nusantara, abbreviated IKN), is the future capital of Indonesia, located in East Kalimantan on the island of Borneo. Its construction was launched after President Joko Widodo announced in 2019 that the capital would be moved from Jakarta, owing to severe congestion, flooding, and the sinking of Jakarta, as well as a desire to distribute development more evenly across the archipelago.

Planning and construction

The State Capital Law was passed in January 2022, formally naming the new city Nusantara — an old Javanese term for the Indonesian archipelago. The city is being built on land between the cities of Balikpapan and Samarinda, combining parts of Penajam North Paser and Kutai Kartanegara regencies. The master plan envisions a "forest city" with most of its area as green space, renewable energy, and smart-city infrastructure; the central government area (KIPP) includes a presidential palace shaped after the mythical Garuda.

Timeline

Groundbreaking ceremonies were held in 2022, and Indonesia's 79th Independence Day ceremony was hosted at the new capital in August 2024. The relocation is planned in stages extending to 2045, with civil servants transferring gradually. The move requires a presidential regulation formally transferring capital status from Jakarta; development continued under President Prabowo Subianto, with Nusantara designated a political capital target in subsequent planning.

Criticism

The project, estimated to cost over US$30 billion, has drawn criticism over its environmental impact on Borneo's rainforests and wildlife, effects on indigenous communities, funding shortfalls, and construction delays.
