Ambulatory care

Ambulatory care or outpatient care is medical care provided on an outpatient basis, including diagnosis, observation, consultation, treatment, intervention, and rehabilitation services. This care can include advanced medical technology and procedures even when provided outside a hospital.

Settings

Ambulatory care is delivered in settings such as hospital outpatient departments, physicians' offices, clinics, urgent care centers, and ambulatory surgery centers. A growing share of procedures that once required hospital admission — including many surgeries, diagnostic imaging studies, and chronic disease management — are now performed on an outpatient basis.

Ambulatory care sensitive conditions

Health researchers use "ambulatory care sensitive conditions" (such as asthma, diabetes complications, and hypertension) as an indicator of access to effective primary care, because timely outpatient treatment can often prevent the need for hospitalization for these conditions.
