List of tallest buildings in Paris

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This list of the tallest buildings in Paris ranks buildings and structures in the Paris metropolitan area by height. Because of strict height limits within the historic city proper—imposed to preserve the Parisian skyline—most skyscrapers stand in the La Défense business district just west of the city boundary, in the communes of Courbevoie, Puteaux, and Nanterre. The tallest structure in Paris remains the Eiffel Tower (330 m including antennas).

Height policy

The construction of the Tour Montparnasse in 1973 provoked public backlash that led Paris to ban new buildings over 37 m (later 31 m for housing) within the city limits in 1977. The ban was partly relaxed in 2010 for specific sites, permitting projects such as the Tours Duo and the Tour Triangle (180 m, under construction in the 15th arrondissement).
