The R&A

Formation2004 (from the Royal and Ancient Golf Club, founded 1754)
HeadquartersSt Andrews, Fife, Scotland
PurposeGovernance of golf; organiser of The Open Championship

The R&A is a British golf organisation that governs the sport worldwide, together with the United States Golf Association (USGA), excluding the United States and Mexico. It was formed in 2004 when the Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews separated its rule-making and championship functions into a group of companies collectively known as The R&A.

Role

The R&A jointly issues the Rules of Golf with the USGA, maintains the rules of equipment and amateur status, and organises major championships including The Open Championship (often called the British Open), the Women's British Open, and the Senior Open, as well as amateur events and the Walker Cup in partnership with the USGA.

Home

It is based in St Andrews, Scotland, regarded as the "home of golf", beside the Old Course and the clubhouse of the Royal and Ancient Golf Club.
