Pulmonology

Pulmonology (also called pneumology, respiratory medicine or chest medicine) is a medical specialty dealing with diseases of the respiratory tract, including the airways, lungs, pleura and chest wall. It is considered a branch of internal medicine and is related to intensive care medicine.

Scope

Pulmonologists diagnose and treat conditions such as asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), pneumonia, tuberculosis, lung cancer, interstitial lung disease, pulmonary hypertension, sleep-disordered breathing and respiratory failure. Diagnostic tools include spirometry and other pulmonary function tests, bronchoscopy, endobronchial ultrasound, thoracentesis and imaging.

Training

In the United States, training comprises residency in internal medicine followed by a fellowship in pulmonary medicine, often combined with critical care medicine.
