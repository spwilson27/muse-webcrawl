United Breweries Group (UB Group)
Founded: 1915
Headquarters: Bengaluru, India
Industry: Brewing, beverages
Known brand: Kingfisher beer

The United Breweries Group, or UB Group, is an Indian conglomerate based in Bengaluru whose flagship is United Breweries Limited, India's largest brewer and maker of Kingfisher beer. The group traces its origins to 1915, when Scotsman Thomas Leishman united five breweries in southern India.

Expansion and decline

Under Vittal Mallya and later his son Vijay Mallya, the group expanded into spirits (United Spirits), airlines (Kingfisher Airlines, launched 2005), fertilisers, and sport, including a Formula One team. Kingfisher Airlines collapsed in 2012 under heavy debt; Vijay Mallya left India in 2016 amid loan-default allegations and became the subject of extradition proceedings from the United Kingdom. Control of United Spirits passed to Diageo, while Heineken became the largest shareholder in United Breweries.
