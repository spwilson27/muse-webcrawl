Balkan League

The Balkan League was an alliance formed in 1912 by the Balkan states of Bulgaria, Serbia, Greece and Montenegro, directed against the Ottoman Empire. Brokered with Russian encouragement, the league's members coordinated a series of bilateral treaties and military conventions in the months before the outbreak of war.

First Balkan War

In October 1912 the league declared war on the Ottoman Empire. Its armies won a string of rapid victories: the Serbs at Kumanovo, the Bulgarians at Kirk Kilisse and Lule Burgas, and the Greeks at Sarantaporo and Salonica. By the Treaty of London of May 1913 the Ottomans ceded nearly all their European territory west of the Enos–Midia line.

Dissolution

Disputes over the division of conquered Macedonia destroyed the alliance. In June 1913 Bulgaria attacked its former allies Serbia and Greece, starting the Second Balkan War, in which Romania and the Ottoman Empire also joined against Bulgaria. Defeated, Bulgaria lost much of its gains under the Treaty of Bucharest, and the league ceased to exist.
