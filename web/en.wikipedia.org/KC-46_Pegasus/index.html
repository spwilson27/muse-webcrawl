Boeing KC-46 Pegasus

The Boeing KC-46 Pegasus is an American military aerial refuelling and strategic transport aircraft developed by Boeing from its 767 airliner. Selected by the United States Air Force in 2011 under the KC-X programme to replace the ageing KC-135 Stratotanker fleet, it entered service in 2019.

Development

After the collapse of an earlier lease arrangement and a reopened competition against the Airbus A330 MRTT (offered as the KC-45), Boeing's 767-based KC-767 Advanced Tanker was selected in February 2011. The KC-46 combines the 767-200ER airframe with a 787-style cockpit, a fly-by-wire refuelling boom and a remote vision system in place of a boom operator's window.

Service and issues

Deliveries began in January 2019, behind schedule and with deficiencies including problems with the Remote Vision System, which is being redesigned, and restrictions on cargo and aeromedical missions that were progressively cleared. The USAF plans to acquire 179 aircraft. Japan became the first export customer, and Israel has also ordered the type.
