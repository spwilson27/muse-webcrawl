Jackie Joyner-Kersee

Jacqueline Joyner-Kersee (born 1962) is an American retired track and field athlete widely regarded as the greatest female heptathlete of all time. She won three Olympic gold medals and still holds the heptathlon world record of 7,291 points.

Born3 March 1962, East St. Louis, Illinois
SportTrack and field
EventsHeptathlon, long jump
Olympic medals3 gold, 1 silver, 2 bronze

Career

Joyner-Kersee starred at UCLA in both athletics and basketball. At the 1988 Seoul Olympics she won gold in the heptathlon with her still-standing world record and in the long jump; she defended the heptathlon title at Barcelona 1992 and took long jump bronze at Atlanta 1996, competing despite asthma and injury.

Legacy

Sports Illustrated named her the greatest female athlete of the twentieth century. She founded the Jackie Joyner-Kersee Foundation, which supports youth and families in her hometown of East St. Louis.
