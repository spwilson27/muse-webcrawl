Bled
Country: Slovenia
Region: Upper Carniola
Known for: Lake Bled, Bled Castle

Bled is a town on Lake Bled in northwestern Slovenia, in the Julian Alps. It is one of the country's most popular tourist destinations, famed for its glacial lake with a small island bearing a pilgrimage church, and a medieval castle perched on a cliff above the shore.

Sights

Visitors reach Bled Island by traditional wooden pletna boats. Bled Castle, first mentioned in 1011, is among Slovenia's oldest. The nearby Vintgar Gorge is another attraction.

Traditions

The town is known for its cream cake, kremšnita, and historically was the summer residence of Yugoslavia's Marshal Tito.
