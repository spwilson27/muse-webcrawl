Thermopylae (clipper)

Thermopylae was an extreme composite clipper ship built in Aberdeen, Scotland, in 1868 by Walter Hood & Co. for the White Star Line of Aberdeen. Renowned for her speed, she was a celebrated rival of the Cutty Sark on the tea and Australian wool trades.

Launched1868
BuilderWalter Hood & Co., Aberdeen
TypeComposite clipper
FateTorpedoed as a hulk, 1907

Career

On her maiden voyage in 1868–69 she sailed from London to Melbourne in 63 days, a record for the route that many believed was never bettered under sail. In 1872 she famously raced the newly built Cutty Sark from Shanghai, winning after the Cutty Sark lost her rudder.

Later years

Sold to Canadian and later Portuguese owners, she ended her days as the Portuguese naval training hulk Pedro Nunes and was sunk by torpedo off Cascais in 1907.
