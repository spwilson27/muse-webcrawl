Vera Farmiga

Vera Ann Farmiga (born August 6, 1973) is an American actress and director. She began her career on stage before transitioning to film, receiving critical acclaim for her role in Down to the Bone (2004). She gained wider recognition for her performances in The Departed (2006) and Up in the Air (2009), the latter earning her an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress. Farmiga is also known for playing Lorraine Warren in The Conjuring horror film series and Norma Bates in the television series Bates Motel (2013–2017), for which she received a Primetime Emmy Award nomination.

Early life

Farmiga was born in Clifton, New Jersey, the second of seven children of Ukrainian immigrants. She was raised in an insular Ukrainian-American community in Irvington, New Jersey, and did not learn English until she started kindergarten. She studied at Syracuse University's School of Visual and Performing Arts, graduating in 1995.

Career

Farmiga made her Broadway debut in 1996 and her film debut in Return to Paradise (1998). Her breakthrough came with the independent drama Down to the Bone, in which she played a drug-addicted mother, winning the Sundance Film Festival Special Jury Prize for acting. Roles in The Manchurian Candidate (2004), The Departed (2006), and Joshua (2007) followed. Her portrayal of a frequent business traveller in Up in the Air (2009) brought Academy Award, BAFTA, and Golden Globe nominations.

In 2011 she made her directorial debut with the drama Higher Ground, in which she also starred. From 2013 she played paranormal investigator Lorraine Warren in The Conjuring and its sequels and spin-offs, one of the highest-grossing horror franchises. On television, her performance as Norma Bates in Bates Motel earned critical praise and an Emmy nomination, and she later starred in the miniseries When They See Us (2019) and the Marvel series Hawkeye (2021).

Personal life

Farmiga is married to musician and carpenter Renn Hawkey, with whom she has two children. Her younger sister is actress Taissa Farmiga.
