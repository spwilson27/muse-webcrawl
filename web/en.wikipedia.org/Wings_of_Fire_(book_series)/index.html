Wings of Fire (book series)

Wings of Fire is a series of children's fantasy novels written by author Tui T. Sutherland and published by Scholastic Press. The series, which began with The Dragonet Prophecy in 2012, is set in a world populated by dragon tribes and follows young dragons caught up in wars and prophecies on the continents of Pyrrhia and Pantala.

Premise

The books are organised into arcs of five novels, each arc following a different group of protagonists. In the first arc, five dragonets — Clay, Tsunami, Glory, Starflight, and Sunny — are raised in hiding because a prophecy says they will end a war of succession among the SandWing queen's three daughters. Later arcs introduce new characters and the lost continent of Pantala, home to tribes such as the HiveWings, SilkWings, and LeafWings.

Books

The main series comprises three five-book arcs — The Dragonet Prophecy arc, the Jade Mountain arc, and the Lost Continent arc — together with companion volumes including the Winglets novellas, the Legends special editions such as Darkstalker and Dragonslayer, and graphic novel adaptations of the main novels. The series has been a commercial success, appearing on the New York Times bestseller list and spawning a large fan community among middle-grade readers.

Author

Tui T. Sutherland also contributed to the Warriors series under the Erin Hunter pen name and wrote the Menagerie trilogy. In Wings of Fire she has spoken about wanting to write dragons as full protagonists with their own societies, rather than as mounts or monsters for human heroes.
