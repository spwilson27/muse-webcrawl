United Nations Human Rights Council

The United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC) is a United Nations body whose mission is promoting and protecting human rights around the world. The Council has 47 members elected for staggered three-year terms on a regional group basis.

UN Human Rights Council
Founded2006
HeadquartersGeneva

Work

The Council holds three regular sessions a year, runs the Universal Periodic Review of every UN member state, appoints special rapporteurs, and creates commissions of inquiry. It replaced the discredit-prone Commission on Human Rights. Functions under scrutiny have included reports on Xinjiang.

Criticism

Critics cite election of rights-abusing governments and a disproportionate focus on Israel via Agenda Item 7; the United States withdrew in 2018 and rejoined in 2021.
