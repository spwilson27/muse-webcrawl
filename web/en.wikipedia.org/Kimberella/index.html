Kimberella

Kimberella is a genus of bilaterian organism known from fossils of the Ediacaran period, about 558–555 million years ago, first found in the Ediacara Hills of Australia and well represented in Russia's White Sea region. It reached about 15 cm long.

Description and significance

Oval in outline with a tough outer covering, Kimberella is interpreted as a mollusc-like creature that crept over microbial mats and grazed them, leaving scratch traces (Kimberichnus) attributed to a radula-like feeding apparatus. As one of the earliest plausible bilaterians with such features, it is important evidence that complex animal lineages were diversifying before the Cambrian explosion.
