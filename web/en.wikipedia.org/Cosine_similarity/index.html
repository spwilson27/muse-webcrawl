Cosine similarity

Cosine similarity is a measure of similarity between two non-zero vectors, defined as the cosine of the angle between them: the dot product divided by the product of their magnitudes. It ranges from −1 (opposite) to 1 (identical direction).

Applications

It is widely used in information retrieval and text mining, where documents are represented as term-frequency vectors, and in machine learning for comparing word and sentence embeddings, recommender systems and clustering.

Properties

Cosine similarity ignores vector magnitude, focusing on orientation, which makes it robust where document length varies. For text it is often combined with TF-IDF weighting.
