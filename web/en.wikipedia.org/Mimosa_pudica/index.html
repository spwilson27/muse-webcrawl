Mimosa pudica

FamilyFabaceae (legume)
Common namesSensitive plant, touch-me-not, shameplant
Native toCentral and South America

Mimosa pudica is a creeping annual or perennial flowering plant of the pea family Fabaceae, famous for its leaflets that fold inward and droop when touched or shaken — a rapid plant movement called thigmonasty or seismonasty.

Movement mechanism

At the base of each leaflet are structures called pulvini containing motor cells. Stimulation triggers ion and water movement out of these cells, causing a loss of turgor pressure that collapses the leaflets within seconds; they reopen after several minutes.

Distribution and uses

Native to the tropical Americas, the plant has become a pantropical weed. It is grown as a curiosity and houseplant, used in traditional medicine, and studied as a model organism for plant signaling and habituation.
