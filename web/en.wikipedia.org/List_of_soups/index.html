List of soups

Borscht, a beet soup of Eastern Europe.

This is a list of notable soups from around the world. Soups have been made since ancient times and are found in virtually every cuisine, ranging from clear broths and consommés to thick chowders, cold soups and dessert soups.

Examples

  • Borscht — beet soup from Eastern Europe, served hot or cold with sour cream.
  • Bouillabaisse — Provençal fish soup from Marseille, France.
  • Chowder — thick seafood or vegetable soup, e.g. New England clam chowder.
  • Gazpacho — cold Spanish tomato soup from Andalusia.
  • French onion soup — onion and beef stock, topped with croutons and melted cheese.
  • Miso soup — Japanese soup of dashi stock and miso paste.
  • Minestrone — thick Italian vegetable soup.
  • Mulligatawny — Anglo-Indian curry-flavoured soup.
  • Pho — Vietnamese noodle soup in spiced broth.
  • Ramen — Japanese noodle soup.
  • Tom yum — hot and sour Thai soup.
  • Vichyssoise — cold leek and potato soup.
  • Waterzooi — Belgian stew-soup of chicken or fish.
