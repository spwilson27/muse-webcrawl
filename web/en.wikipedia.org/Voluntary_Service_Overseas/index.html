Voluntary Service Overseas (VSO)
Founded: 1958
Founder: Alec Dickson
Type: International development charity
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom

Voluntary Service Overseas, commonly known as VSO, is an international development charity that fights poverty and marginalisation through volunteering. Founded in 1958 by Alec Dickson, it recruits skilled professionals to share their expertise with communities in Africa, Asia and the Pacific.

History

VSO began by sending young British graduates to work overseas for a year, an idea Dickson launched with a letter to the Sunday Times following his experience with displaced communities. It helped inspire the creation of similar organisations, including the United States Peace Corps in 1961.

Work

Modern VSO programmes focus on health, education and livelihoods. Volunteers — increasingly including national volunteers from within partner countries — work alongside local partners, and the organisation also engages in advocacy on issues such as inclusive education and youth employment.
