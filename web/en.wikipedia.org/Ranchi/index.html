Ranchi is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Jharkhand. Located on the Chota Nagpur Plateau at an elevation of about 651 metres, it was historically the centre of the Jharkhand statehood movement and became the state capital when Jharkhand was carved out of Bihar in 2000.

Geography and climate

Ranchi sits on the eastern edge of the Chota Nagpur Plateau near the Subarnarekha River. It is known for its waterfalls, including Hundru, Jonha, and Dassam Falls, and for a relatively moderate climate compared with the plains, which made it a popular retreat during the British era.

Economy and education

The city hosts heavy industry, including the headquarters of Central Coalfields and facilities of Heavy Engineering Corporation, along with institutions such as Ranchi University, IIM Ranchi, and the National University of Study and Research in Law.
