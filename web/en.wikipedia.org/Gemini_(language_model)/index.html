Gemini is a family of multimodal large language models developed by Google DeepMind, serving as the successor to LaMDA and PaLM. Announced on 6 December 2023, Gemini models are trained from the outset to process and combine text, images, audio, video, and code, and power Google's chatbot and AI features of the same name (formerly Bard), as well as models available through Google AI Studio and Vertex AI.

Models

The first generation was released in three sizes: Ultra, Pro, and Nano (for on-device tasks). Gemini 1.5, released in February 2024, introduced a context window of up to one million tokens (with larger windows in research previews) using a mixture-of-experts architecture. Gemini 2.0 (December 2024) emphasised agentic capabilities and native tool use, and Gemini 2.5 (2025) added "thinking" models with explicit reasoning before answering, alongside variants such as Flash and Flash-Lite optimised for speed and cost.

Reception

Gemini's launch demonstrations attracted scrutiny after Google acknowledged that a promotional video had been edited to make interactions appear faster and voice-based. Subsequent releases scored competitively on reasoning, coding, and multimodal benchmarks, and the models have been integrated across Google's products, including Search, Workspace, Android, and the Pixel phone line.
