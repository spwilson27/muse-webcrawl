El Santo

El Santo, the "Silver-Masked Man," in his iconic silver mask

Rodolfo Guzmán Huerta (23 September 1917 – 5 February 1984), known professionally as El Santo ("The Saint"), was a Mexican luchador (professional wrestler) and actor. Often called El Enmascarado de Plata ("The Silver-Masked Man"), he is one of the most famous and iconic figures in lucha libre and a folk hero of Mexican popular culture.

Career

Guzmán took up wrestling in the 1930s, adopting the El Santo persona in 1942. He famously never removed his silver mask in public, and maintained his mystique for over four decades in the ring. Beginning in the early 1950s he starred in a long series of comic books, and from 1958 he headlined dozens of low-budget films in which El Santo battled criminals, monsters, vampires, and invading aliens — movies such as Santo vs. the Zombies and Santo in the Wax Museum became cult classics in Mexico and abroad.

Legacy

El Santo retired in 1982 and briefly revealed part of his face on television shortly before his death in 1984; he was buried wearing his mask. His son Jorge Guzmán continues the tradition as El Hijo del Santo. El Santo remains a defining symbol of lucha libre, and his mask is one of the most recognisable icons of Mexican culture.
