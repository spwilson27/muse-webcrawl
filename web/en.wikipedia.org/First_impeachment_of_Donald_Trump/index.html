The first impeachment of Donald Trump occurred when Donald Trump, the 45th president of the United States, was impeached by the House of Representatives on 18 December 2019. The House adopted two articles of impeachment: abuse of power and obstruction of Congress. The charges stemmed from the Trump–Ukraine scandal, in which Trump was alleged to have pressured Ukraine to announce investigations into his political rival Joe Biden and his son Hunter Biden, conditioning a White House meeting and the release of congressionally appropriated military aid on those announcements. The Senate acquitted Trump on both articles on 5 February 2020.

Background

The inquiry began after an anonymous whistleblower complaint in August 2019 reported a telephone call of 25 July 2019 between Trump and Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy, in which Trump asked Zelenskyy to "do us a favour" and investigate the Bidens and a discredited theory about Ukrainian interference in the 2016 election. A memorandum of the call was released by the White House. Speaker Nancy Pelosi announced a formal impeachment inquiry on 24 September 2019, and House committees held closed-door depositions and public hearings with diplomats and officials including Gordon Sondland, Fiona Hill and Lt. Col. Alexander Vindman.

House vote and Senate trial

The House approved the articles largely along party lines: Article I passed 230–197 and Article II 229–198. The Senate trial, presided over by Chief Justice John Roberts, ran from 16 January to 5 February 2020; the Senate voted not to call additional witnesses. On Article I the vote to convict was 48–52 (Mitt Romney was the only Republican voting guilty, becoming the first senator to vote to convict a president of his own party), and on Article II 47–53 — both far short of the two-thirds majority required for removal.

Aftermath

Trump remained in office and campaigned on the acquittal. He was impeached a second time in January 2021 following the attack on the U.S. Capitol, becoming the only U.S. president to be impeached twice.
