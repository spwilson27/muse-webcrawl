Pantothenic acid

Pantothenic acid, known as vitamin B5, is an essential water-soluble vitamin required for the synthesis of coenzyme A, and hence for the metabolism of carbohydrates, proteins and fats.

Sources and function

Its name comes from the Greek pantos, "everywhere", reflecting its wide distribution in foods; good sources include meat, eggs, whole grains and legumes. Deficiency is rare but can cause fatigue, numbness and the "burning feet" syndrome documented in malnourished prisoners of war.

Chemistry

Pantothenic acid is an amide of pantoic acid and β-alanine. It is a component of coenzyme A and the acyl carrier protein used in fatty-acid synthesis.
