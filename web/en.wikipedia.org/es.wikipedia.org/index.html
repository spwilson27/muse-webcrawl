The Spanish Wikipedia (Wikipedia en español) is the Spanish-language edition of Wikipedia. Launched in May 2001, it passed two million articles in the mid-2020s and is one of the most visited websites in the Spanish-speaking world, with contributors from Spain, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile and many other countries.

History

In 2002, a dispute over advertising proposals led many Spanish contributors to fork the project as the Enciclopedia Libre Universal en Español; the fork reunited in practice as Wikipedia's no-ads commitment became clear and editing returned to the main project. The edition has periodically debated policies such as fair use of images (it historically disallowed them) and bot-created articles.

Characteristics

Spanish Wikipedia serves a global readership across dialects, using a neutral pan-Hispanic written standard. It is frequently among the top Wikipedia editions by page views.
