World Aquatics

World Aquatics, formerly known as FINA (Fédération Internationale de Natation), is the international federation recognised by the International Olympic Committee for administering competition in water sports worldwide. It was founded in London in 1908 and adopted its current name in January 2023.

Founded19 July 1908
Former nameFINA
HeadquartersLausanne, Switzerland
SportsSwimming, diving, water polo, artistic swimming, open water, high diving

Role

World Aquatics sets rules for six aquatic disciplines and organises the biennial World Aquatics Championships, the Swimming World Cup, and the World Aquatics Masters Championships. It recognises world records and oversees anti-doping and eligibility rules for its sports.

History

FINA was created during the 1908 London Olympics by eight European swimming federations. Membership grew to over 200 national federations. In December 2022 the federation voted to rename itself World Aquatics.
