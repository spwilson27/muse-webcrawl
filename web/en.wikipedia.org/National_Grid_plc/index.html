National Grid plc is a British multinational electricity and gas utility company headquartered in London, England. Its principal activities are in the United Kingdom and in the northeastern United States, where it owns and operates electricity transmission and distribution networks and gas distribution networks.

History

The company originated in the restructuring of the Central Electricity Generating Board in 1990, when the National Grid Company was created to own and operate the high-voltage transmission network in England and Wales. It was floated on the stock market in 1995. National Grid expanded into the United States from 2000 onward, acquiring utilities including New England Electric System and KeySpan, and later expanded its gas operations by acquiring the gas distribution business of Lattice Group.

Operations

In Great Britain, National Grid Electricity Transmission owns the transmission network in England and Wales, while National Grid Electricity Distribution (formerly Western Power Distribution) operates regional distribution networks. In the United States the company serves millions of customers in New York, Massachusetts and Rhode Island. In 2019 the electricity system operator function in Britain was legally separated as National Grid ESO, which transferred to public ownership in 2024 as the National Energy System Operator (NESO).
