Amelia Jessica "Amy" Pond is a fictional character in the long-running British science fiction television series Doctor Who. She was played by Scottish actress Karen Gillan. Amy is a companion of the Eleventh Doctor, his first companion in the programme.

Amy first appears in "The Eleventh Hour" (2010), meeting the newly regenerated Doctor as a seven-year-old girl (played by Caitlin Blackwood) whose bedroom wall bears a mysterious crack in time; he returns twelve years later than promised, making her "the girl who waited". As an adult she travels with the Doctor alongside her boyfriend, later husband, Rory Williams, and it is eventually revealed that her daughter is River Song — the Doctor's wife — who was conceived aboard the TARDIS and kidnapped by Madame Kovarian to be raised as a weapon against the Doctor.

Departure

In "The Angels Take Manhattan" (2012), Amy allows a Weeping Angel to send her back in time to rejoin Rory, choosing a life without the Doctor; the pair are buried in New York, with Amy's afterword to the Doctor revealing she lived to the age of 87. Gillan's spirited performance and the character's long, family-centred story arc made Amy one of the most popular companions of the revived series.
