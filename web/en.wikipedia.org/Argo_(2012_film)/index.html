Argo is a 2012 American historical drama thriller film directed by and starring Ben Affleck. It dramatizes the "Canadian Caper", the joint CIA–Canadian rescue of six U.S. diplomats from Tehran during the 1979–1981 Iran hostage crisis.

Plot

CIA exfiltration specialist Tony Mendez devises a cover story in which the diplomats pose as a Canadian film crew scouting locations for a fake science-fiction movie called Argo. With the help of Hollywood producer Lester Siegel and makeup artist John Chambers, the ruse succeeds and the six escape from Mehrabad Airport just ahead of the Revolutionary Guards.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success and won three Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Adapted Screenplay, and Best Film Editing. It was also criticized for minimizing the Canadian role and for historical liberties in its climax.
