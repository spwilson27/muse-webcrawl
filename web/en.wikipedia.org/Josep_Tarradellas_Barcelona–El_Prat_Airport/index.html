Josep Tarradellas Barcelona–El Prat Airport

Barcelona–El Prat Airport
IATA: BCN, ICAO: LEBL
Location: El Prat de Llobregat, near Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain
Operator: Aena

Josep Tarradellas Barcelona–El Prat Airport (IATA: BCN, ICAO: LEBL), known simply as Barcelona Airport, is the main international airport serving Barcelona and Catalonia, located 12 km southwest of the city centre in the municipality of El Prat de Llobregat.

History

The airport began operations in 1918. Major expansions included Terminal 2 in the early 1990s for the Olympic Games and the large Terminal 1, designed by Ricardo Bofill, opened in 2009. In 2019 it was renamed in honour of Josep Tarradellas, former president of the Generalitat of Catalonia.

Terminals and operations

The airport has two terminals, T1 and T2, and three runways. It is a hub for Vueling and a focus for Ryanair, easyJet and Iberia, handling over 50 million passengers in peak years before and after the COVID-19 pandemic.

Transport

The airport is connected to Barcelona by metro lines L9 Sud, the Aerobús, commuter rail (R2 Nord from Terminal 2) and motorways.
