DBS Bank Limited is a Singaporean multinational banking and financial services corporation and the largest bank in Southeast Asia by assets. Its parent, DBS Group Holdings, is listed on the Singapore Exchange, and the group's largest shareholder is Temasek Holdings. The bank's name derives from its founding institution, the Development Bank of Singapore.

History

The Development Bank of Singapore was established on 16 July 1968 by the Government of Singapore to take over industrial financing activities from the Economic Development Board. Its early mandate was to support Singapore's industrialisation through loans and equity participation in manufacturing ventures. Over the following decades it grew into a universal bank, expanding into consumer banking, treasury, and capital markets.

In 2003 the group adopted the initials "DBS" as its official name. Under chief executives including Piyush Gupta (from 2009), DBS undertook a major digital transformation, marketing itself as a technology company delivering banking services, and was repeatedly named "World's Best Digital Bank" and "World's Best Bank" by trade publications such as Euromoney and Global Finance.

Operations

DBS operates across Greater China, South Asia, and Southeast Asia, with major markets in Singapore, Hong Kong, mainland China, Taiwan, India, and Indonesia. It acquired the banking operations of ANZ in several Asian markets in 2016–2017, merged with India's Lakshmi Vilas Bank in 2020, and took a stake in China's Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank. Its consumer brand in India operates as digibank, a mobile-first offering.

Services

The bank provides retail banking, wealth management (DBS Private Bank and DBS Treasures), SME banking, corporate and investment banking, and treasury services. Its digital platforms include the DBS PayLah! payments app and the DBS digibank mobile app.
