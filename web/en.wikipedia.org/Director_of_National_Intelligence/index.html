The director of national intelligence (DNI) is a senior cabinet-level United States government official, required by the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 to serve as executive head of the United States Intelligence Community (IC) and to direct and oversee the National Intelligence Program (NIP). The DNI also serves, upon invitation, as the principal advisor to the president of the United States, the National Security Council, and the Homeland Security Council on all intelligence matters. The DNI is appointed by the president and requires confirmation by the Senate.

History

Before the DNI was formally established, the head of the Intelligence Community was the director of central intelligence (DCI), who served concurrently as the director of the Central Intelligence Agency. The 9/11 Commission recommended creating a separate position to improve coordination among the many intelligence agencies following the September 11 attacks. The Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 established the DNI and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), effective in 2005. Ambassador John Negroponte became the first DNI on April 21, 2005.

Duties

The DNI oversees and coordinates the activities of the 18 elements of the Intelligence Community, develops the National Intelligence Program budget, and produces the President's Daily Brief. The DNI has limited authority over the appointment of agency heads and cannot direct the operations of individual agencies such as the CIA, which retains its own director who reports to the DNI. The DNI is a member of the National Security Council principals committee and ranks among the most senior officials in the United States order of precedence.

Office of the Director of National Intelligence

The ODNI, headquartered in McLean, Virginia, supports the DNI with deputy directors, national intelligence managers, and mission centers including the National Counterterrorism Center, the National Counterintelligence and Security Center, and the National Center for Medical Intelligence.
