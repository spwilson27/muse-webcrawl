The Spartan army was the military force of the ancient Greek city-state of Sparta, renowned in antiquity for the discipline and endurance of its citizen hoplites. Unlike other Greek states, Sparta maintained a standing, professional force whose members devoted their lives to military training.

Organisation

Full citizens, the Spartiates, underwent the agoge, a rigorous communal upbringing beginning at age seven. The army fought as a phalanx of heavily armoured hoplites carrying the long dory spear and the large aspis shield, supplemented by perioikoi troops and helot attendants.

History

Spartan arms dominated the Peloponnese and led the Greek defence at Thermopylae and Plataea during the Persian Wars, then defeated Athens in the Peloponnesian War. The army's aura of invincibility was shattered by the Theban victory at Leuctra in 371 BC.
