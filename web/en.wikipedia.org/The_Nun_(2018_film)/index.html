The Nun (2018 film)

The Nun
Directed by: Corin Hardy
Screenplay by: Gary Dauberman
Starring: Demián Bichir, Taissa Farmiga, Jonas Bloquet, Bonnie Aarons
Release date: September 7, 2018
Running time: 96 minutes
Budget: $22 million

The Nun is a 2018 American gothic supernatural horror film directed by Corin Hardy and written by Gary Dauberman, from a story by Dauberman and James Wan. It is a spin-off and prequel to The Conjuring 2 (2016) and the fifth installment in The Conjuring Universe franchise. The film stars Demián Bichir as a Catholic priest and Taissa Farmiga as a young novice sent by the Vatican to investigate a nun's suicide at a remote abbey in Romania, where they confront the demonic entity Valak, played by Bonnie Aarons.

Plot

In 1952, at the Carta Monastery in Romania, a young nun takes her own life after being pursued by an unseen evil. The Vatican dispatches Father Burke, a priest with a haunted past, and Sister Irene, a novitiate who has had visions since childhood, to investigate. Guided by a local villager nicknamed "Frenchie," they discover that the abbey was built by a medieval occultist to summon a demon, Valak, and that the nuns have prayed in perpetual adoration for centuries to keep the rift sealed. In the climax, Irene seals the rift with a vial of the Blood of Christ, and Frenchie — revealed in a framing device as Maurice, later exorcised by Ed and Lorraine Warren — carries the demon's taint out into the world.

Production

Filming took place in Romania in 2017, using castles and the real Cârța Monastery region for exteriors. Abel Korzeniowski composed the score. An advertisement for the film featuring a jump scare was removed from YouTube in 2018 for violating the platform's policy on shocking content, an incident that itself generated publicity.

Reception

The film received generally negative reviews, with criticism of its plot and logic but praise for its atmosphere and performances. Commercially it was a major success, grossing $366 million worldwide against a $22 million budget — the highest-grossing film in the Conjuring franchise at the time. A sequel, The Nun II, was released in 2023.
