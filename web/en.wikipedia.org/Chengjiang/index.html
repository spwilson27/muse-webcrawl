Chengjiang

Chengjiang is a county-level city in Yuxi, Yunnan Province, China, located on the north shore of Fuxian Lake. It is best known worldwide for the Chengjiang Fossil Site.

The Chengjiang biota, discovered in 1984 in the Maotianshan Shales, preserves an exceptional assemblage of Early Cambrian marine fossils about 518 million years old — one of the earliest and richest records of the Cambrian explosion. Over 250 species have been described, including arthropods, chordates such as Myllokunmingia (an early vertebrate relative), sponges, and the predator Anomalocaris.

The Chengjiang Fossil Site was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2012. The city also administers part of Fuxian Lake, one of China's deepest freshwater lakes, and the earlier Xingyun Lake area.
