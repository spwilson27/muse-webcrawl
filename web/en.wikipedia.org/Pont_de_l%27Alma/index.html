The Pont de l'Alma is a road bridge in Paris crossing the Seine. It was named to commemorate the Battle of Alma during the Crimean War, in which the Franco-British alliance achieved victory over the Russian army in 1854.

History

The original stone bridge was built between 1854 and 1856 under Napoleon III. Its piers were decorated with statues of soldiers representing the regiments at Alma; the Zouave statue became a popular informal gauge of Seine flood levels—when the water reached the Zouave's chin in 1910, Paris suffered its great flood. The bridge was rebuilt as a steel arch structure in the 1970s, and only the Zouave was retained.

Diana memorial

The underpass beside the bridge is where Diana, Princess of Wales, was fatally injured in a car crash on 31 August 1997. The Flame of Liberty monument at the bridge's northern end has become an unofficial memorial to her.
