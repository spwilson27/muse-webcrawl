Bentley

Bentley Motors Limited is a British designer, manufacturer and marketer of luxury cars and SUVs. Founded by W. O. Bentley in London in 1919, the company is headquartered in Crewe, England, and since 1998 has been a subsidiary of the Volkswagen Group.

History

Bentleys won the 24 Hours of Le Mans five times in the 1920s, driven by the "Bentley Boys". Financial difficulties led to Rolls-Royce taking over in 1931; for decades Bentleys were largely rebadged Rolls-Royces. After the brands were split in 1998, Volkswagen took Bentley while BMW acquired rights to Rolls-Royce.

Models

Current models include the Continental GT coupé, Flying Spur saloon and Bentayga SUV, which became the brand's best-seller. Many cars use W12 or V8 engines and extensive hand-crafted wood and leather interiors.

Electrification

Bentley's "Beyond100" strategy announced a move to an all-electric line-up in the 2030s.
