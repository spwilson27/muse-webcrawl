List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is an American media franchise and shared universe centered on a series of superhero films produced by Marvel Studios, based on characters from Marvel Comics. The films are grouped into phases; Phases One through Three form "The Infinity Saga", and Phases Four through Six form "The Multiverse Saga". The MCU is the highest-grossing film franchise of all time.
