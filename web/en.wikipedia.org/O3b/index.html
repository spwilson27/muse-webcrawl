O3b Networks

[Image placeholder: O3b Networks]

O3b Networks was a satellite communications company that operated a constellation of satellites in medium Earth orbit (MEO) to provide high-throughput, low-latency broadband connectivity. The name "O3b" stands for "Other three billion", referring to the population of the world without reliable internet access.

History and system

Founded in 2007 by Greg Wyler, the company launched its first four satellites in June 2013, built by Thales Alenia Space, orbiting at about 8,062 km altitude — much lower than geostationary orbit — which reduced latency and allowed smaller ground terminals. The constellation grew to 20 satellites serving telecom operators, governments, ships and remote industries.

Acquisition

In 2016 SES S.A. agreed to acquire O3b Networks outright. The technology was carried forward in SES's second-generation system, O3b mPOWER, which began launching in 2022.
