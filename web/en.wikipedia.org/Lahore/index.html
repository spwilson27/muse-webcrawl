Lahore (Punjabi/Urdu: لاہور) is the capital and largest city of the Pakistani province of Punjab. With a population of roughly 13 million, it is Pakistan's second-largest city after Karachi and one of the most populous cities in the world. Located on the Ravi River near the border with India, it is widely regarded as the cultural capital of Pakistan.

History

According to legend Lahore was founded by Lava, son of the Hindu deity Rama, and the city is mentioned in early medieval sources before coming under Ghaznavid rule in the eleventh century. It rose to greatness as a favoured residence of the Mughal emperors between 1584 and 1598 and again under Shah Jahan and Aurangzeb, who built or expanded the Lahore Fort, the Shalimar Gardens, the Wazir Khan Mosque and the vast Badshahi Mosque (1673). After Mughal decline the city passed through Afghan and Sikh rule — Ranjit Singh made it the capital of the Sikh Empire in 1799 — before British annexation in 1849. Lahore was central to the independence movement: the All-India Muslim League passed the Lahore Resolution there in 1940, calling for separate Muslim states, and at Partition in 1947 the city was awarded to Pakistan amid devastating communal violence.

Culture

Lahore is famed for its Mughal and colonial architecture, its bazaars and food culture, its gardens, and its roles in Punjabi and Urdu literature, poetry and cinema (the film industry nicknamed "Lollywood"). Landmarks include the Walled City, Anarkali Bazaar, the Lahore Museum, Minar-e-Pakistan, and the Wagah border ceremony nearby. The city hosts major festivals such as Basant and the Lahore Literary Festival, and leading institutions including the University of the Punjab, Government College University and LUMS.
