The Lamborghini Aventador is a mid-engine sports car produced by the Italian manufacturer Lamborghini from 2011 to 2022. Named after a prize-winning fighting bull, it served as the flagship V12 model of the range, succeeding the Murciélago.

Design and performance

The Aventador is built around a carbon-fibre monocoque and is powered by a 6.5-litre naturally aspirated V12 producing 690 hp in the original LP 700-4, driving all four wheels through a seven-speed single-clutch automated manual gearbox. It accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in about 2.9 seconds, with a top speed near 350 km/h (217 mph).

Variants

The range expanded to include a Roadster, the lighter and sharper Superveloce (SV), the facelifted Aventador S, and the track-focused SVJ, which set a Nürburgring production-car lap record in 2018. Production ended in 2022 with the Ultimae edition, closing the era of the pure, non-hybrid Lamborghini V12 flagship.
