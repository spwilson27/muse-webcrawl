Allan Jeffrey "Alfie" Langer AM (born 30 July 1966) is an Australian former professional rugby league footballer and coach. A halfback, he captained the Brisbane Broncos to four premierships and is regarded as one of the finest players of his era.

Career

Langer joined the Broncos for their inaugural season in 1988 and captained them to premierships in 1992, 1993, 1997 (Super League), and 1998. He played 34 State of Origin matches for Queensland and represented Australia in 24 Tests. After a stint with the Warrington Wolves in England, he made a celebrated comeback for Queensland in the deciding third game of the 2001 Origin series, guiding the Maroons to victory.

Honours and later life

Langer won the Clive Churchill Medal in 1992 and the Rothmans Medal in 1996. A bronze statue of him stands outside Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane. He has since worked as an assistant coach and trainer with the Broncos and Queensland sides.
