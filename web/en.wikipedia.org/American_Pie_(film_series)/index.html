American Pie (film series)

American Pie is a series of American sex comedy films. The first film was released on July 9, 1999, by Universal Pictures, and became a worldwide pop culture phenomenon. The original theatrical series comprises four films: American Pie (1999), American Pie 2 (2001), American Wedding (2003), and American Reunion (2012), followed by a series of direct-to-video spin-offs under the "American Pie Presents" banner.

American Pie
Created by: Adam Herz
Production company: Universal Pictures
First film: American Pie (1999)
Theatrical films: 4
Spin-offs: 5 (direct-to-video)

Main series

The original films follow a group of friends from East Great Falls, Michigan — including Jim Levenstein (Jason Biggs), Oz (Chris Klein), Kevin (Thomas Ian Nicholas), Finch (Eddie Kaye Thomas), and Steve Stifler (Seann William Scott) — from a high-school pact to lose their virginity through college, a wedding, and a reunion. Eugene Levy appears in every film as Jim's father, Noah Levenstein.

Reception

The theatrical films grossed over US$989 million worldwide collectively, making the series one of the most successful comedy franchises of its era.
