Associazione Sportiva Roma (AS Roma) is a professional football club based in Rome, Italy. Founded in 1927 by a merger of three Roman clubs, Roma have played in the top division of Italian football for nearly their entire history, and contest the Derby della Capitale with city rivals Lazio, with whom they share the Stadio Olimpico.

Honours

Roma have won Serie A three times (1941–42, 1982–83 and 2000–01), the Coppa Italia nine times, and the Supercoppa Italiana twice. In Europe they won the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup in 1961 and the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2022 under José Mourinho, the inaugural edition of that competition, and were runners-up in the 1984 European Cup and the 1991 and 2023 UEFA Cup/Europa League finals.

Identity

The club's crest features the Capitoline Wolf suckling Romulus and Remus, drawn from the founding myth of Rome. Its greatest icon is Francesco Totti, the one-club forward who made 786 appearances and scored 307 goals for Roma between 1992 and 2017, captaining the 2001 Scudetto-winning side. Other club legends include Daniele De Rossi, Bruno Conti, Agostino Di Bartolomei and Aldair.
