Naval Station Newport

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Naval Station Newport (NAVSTA Newport) is a United States Navy base located in the city of Newport and the town of Middletown, Rhode Island. It is a center for education and training, home to the Naval War College, the Naval Justice School, the Navy's Officer Candidate School and Officer Development School, and the Naval Undersea Warfare Center, among some 50 tenant commands.

History

The Navy's presence in Narragansett Bay dates to the Civil War, when the United States Naval Academy temporarily relocated to Newport. A naval training station was established on Coasters Harbor Island in 1883, and in 1884 Stephen B. Luce founded the Naval War College there. The station became a center for torpedo development and a major fleet base; the Naval Torpedo Station on Goat Island tested and manufactured torpedoes for decades. Although the fleet's destroyer force departed in the 1970s, the base survived realignment rounds because of its concentration of schools and staff colleges, and remains one of Rhode Island's largest employers.

Present day

Several thousand students, staff, and families are attached to the station. Its senior officer training, legal education, and surface-warfare schools make it a focal point of professional military education. See also Naval War College and Newport.
