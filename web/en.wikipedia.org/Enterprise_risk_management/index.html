Enterprise risk management

Enterprise risk management (ERM) is a framework for identifying, assessing and managing risks across an entire organisation in an integrated way, rather than in separate silos.

Frameworks

Widely used frameworks include the COSO ERM framework and ISO 31000. ERM covers strategic, operational, financial, compliance and hazard risks, aligning risk appetite with strategy and governance.

Practice

Boards and senior management oversee risk registers, assessments and mitigation, a discipline strengthened after financial crises and codified in regulation for banks and insurers.
