Bartolomeo Berrecci

Bartolomeo Berrecci (c. 1480 – 1537) was an Italian Renaissance architect and sculptor who spent most of his career in Poland, in the service of King Sigismund I the Old.

Works

His masterpiece is Sigismund's Chapel at Wawel Cathedral in Kraków, often called the finest work of the Italian Renaissance north of the Alps. He also rebuilt Wawel Castle after a fire and worked on other Polish royal and noble projects.

Legacy

Berrecci introduced High Renaissance forms to Poland and trained a generation of artists working there.
