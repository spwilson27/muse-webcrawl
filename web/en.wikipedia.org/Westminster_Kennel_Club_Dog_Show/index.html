Westminster Kennel Club Dog Show
First held: 1877
Location: New York City (Madison Square Garden; USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center since 2023)
Frequency: Annual
Organizer: Westminster Kennel Club

The Westminster Kennel Club Dog Show is an annual all-breed conformation dog show held in New York City. First held in 1877, it is one of the oldest continuously held sporting events in the United States — older than the Kentucky Derby's show tradition in its modern form and famously predating the invention of the automobile and the zipper. It is the most prestigious dog show in America.

Format

Some 2,500–3,000 champion dogs compete in breed judging; Best of Breed winners advance to seven group competitions (Sporting, Hound, Working, Terrier, Toy, Non-Sporting, and Herding), and the group winners compete for Best in Show. Wire Fox Terriers have won Best in Show a record 15 times. Since 2023 the show has been held at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Queens, after decades at Madison Square Garden. The event also includes agility and obedience championships and Junior Showmanship.
