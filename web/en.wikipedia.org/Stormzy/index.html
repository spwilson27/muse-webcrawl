Stormzy

Born Michael Ebenezer Kwadjo Omari Owuo Jr., 26 July 1993, Croydon, London
Genres Grime, British hip hop, gospel
Occupation Rapper, singer, songwriter

Stormzy performing live

Michael Ebenezer Kwadjo Omari Owuo Jr. (born 26 July 1993), known professionally as Stormzy, is a British rapper, singer, and songwriter. In 2014, he gained attention on the UK underground music scene via his Wicked Skengman series of freestyles over classic grime beats. His debut studio album, Gang Signs & Prayer (2017), was the first grime album to reach number one on the UK Albums Chart.

Career

Stormzy's breakthrough single "Shut Up" reached the UK top ten in 2015 after a campaign to make it Christmas number one. Gang Signs & Prayer won British Album of the Year at the 2018 Brit Awards. In 2019 he became the first British black solo artist to headline the Glastonbury Festival, and his second album, Heavy Is the Head (2019), produced the UK number-one single "Vossi Bop". His third album, This Is What I Mean, followed in 2022.

Activism and philanthropy

Stormzy is known for political commentary and charitable work, including the Stormzy Scholarship for black students at the University of Cambridge and his #Merky Books publishing imprint with Penguin Random House.
