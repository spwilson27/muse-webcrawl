The Bodyguard: Original Soundtrack Album is the soundtrack to the 1992 film The Bodyguard, starring Whitney Houston and Kevin Costner. It is the best-selling soundtrack album of all time and one of the best-selling albums of any kind, with worldwide sales exceeding 45 million copies.

Content

The first side of the album features six songs by Houston, including the singles "I Will Always Love You", "I'm Every Woman", "I Have Nothing", "Run to You", "Queen of the Night" and "Someday (I'm Coming Back)". The second side collects songs by other Arista artists, including Kenny G, Aaron Neville, Lisa Stansfield, Curtis Stigers and Joe Cocker.

"I Will Always Love You"

Houston's recording of Dolly Parton's "I Will Always Love You", arranged with its famous a cappella opening and key change, spent a then-record 14 weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and became the best-selling single by a female artist. The album and single swept the 1994 Grammy Awards, winning Album of the Year and Record of the Year.

Commercial performance

The album spent 20 non-consecutive weeks atop the Billboard 200 and was certified Diamond in the United States. It remains the best-selling album by a female artist in several markets and the best-selling soundtrack worldwide, ahead of Saturday Night Fever.
