Westdeutscher Rundfunk

Westdeutscher Rundfunk Köln (WDR)
Type: Public broadcaster
Founded: 1 January 1956
Headquarters: Cologne, Germany
Broadcast area: North Rhine-Westphalia
Parent: ARD

Westdeutscher Rundfunk Köln (WDR, "West German Broadcasting Cologne") is a German public-broadcasting institution based in Cologne and the largest member of the ARD consortium of regional broadcasters. It serves the state of North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany's most populous state.

History

WDR was created on 1 January 1956 when the Nordwestdeutscher Rundfunk (NWDR) was split into WDR and Norddeutscher Rundfunk (NDR). Radio broadcasting in Cologne dated back to the 1920s.

Services

WDR operates the television channel WDR Fernsehen and contributes to national channels Das Erste, KiKa, Arte, 3sat and Phoenix. Its radio stations include 1LIVE, WDR 2, WDR 3 (culture and classical), WDR 4, WDR 5 and the children's and youth service KiRaKa. Well-known productions include the crime series Tatort (Münster and Cologne editions), the children's programme Die Sendung mit der Maus, and the talk shows of the WDR's Cologne studios. The WDR maintains orchestras and ensembles including the WDR Symphony Orchestra Cologne.
