Nelson Angelo Tamsma Piquet Souto Maior (born 25 July 1985), known as Nelson Piquet Jr. or Nelsinho Piquet, is a Brazilian racing driver. The son of three-time Formula One world champion Nelson Piquet, he raced in Formula One for Renault in 2008 and 2009 and subsequently reinvented his career in North American and electric racing, winning the inaugural FIA Formula E Championship in 2014–15.

Early career

Born in Heidelberg while his father raced in Europe, Piquet Jr. began karting in Brazil in 1993. He won the South American Formula Three championship in 2002 with the family-run Piquet Sports team, then the British Formula 3 championship in 2004. In GP2 in 2006 he finished runner-up to Lewis Hamilton after a season-long title fight.

Formula One and "Crashgate"

Piquet Jr. joined Renault as a test driver in 2007 and was promoted to a race seat alongside Fernando Alonso for 2008, scoring his only F1 podium with second place at the German Grand Prix. Dropped midway through 2009, he then alleged that at the 2008 Singapore Grand Prix he had been instructed by senior team personnel to crash deliberately to aid Alonso, who won the race. The resulting scandal — "Crashgate" — led to bans (later partly overturned in court) for team boss Flavio Briatore and engineer Pat Symonds and remains one of Formula One's most notorious controversies.

Later career

From 2010 Piquet Jr. raced in NASCAR's national series, becoming the first Brazilian to win in the Truck Series (2012, Michigan and Las Vegas) and in the Nationwide (now Xfinity) Series (2012, Road America), and won Global Rallycross races. He won the first Formula E title in 2014–15 with China Racing by a single point. He later competed in the FIA World Endurance Championship, Stock Car Brasil and other categories.
