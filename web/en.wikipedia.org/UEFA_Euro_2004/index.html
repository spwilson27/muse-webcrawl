The 2004 UEFA European Football Championship was held in Portugal from 12 June to 4 July 2004. It produced one of the greatest upsets in football history as Greece, 150–1 outsiders before the tournament, won the title.

Tournament

Sixteen teams competed in ten stadiums. Greece, coached by Otto Rehhagel, combined disciplined defending with set-piece goals, beating hosts Portugal in both the opening match and the final — Angelos Charisteas headed the only goal in Lisbon on 4 July. Along the way Greece also eliminated holders France and the Czech Republic, the latter by a "silver goal" in the semi-final.

Legacy

Euro 2004 was the last tournament to use the silver goal rule, and it introduced a new generation of stars, including Cristiano Ronaldo and Wayne Rooney.
