Supersport racing

Supersport racing is a category of motorcycle road racing for production-based, middleweight sports bikes. The premier international series is the Supersport World Championship (WorldSSP), run as a support class to the Superbike World Championship since 1990 (as a full world championship since 1999).

Machines and rules

Historically the class was dominated by 600 cc four-cylinder machines (with larger capacities permitted for twins and triples), ridden with limited modifications from the showroom models. From 2022, the "Next Generation" rules broadened eligibility to larger machines such as the Ducati Panigale V2 and MV Agusta F3 800, balanced by rev limits and spec electronics.

Other series

Supersport classes feature in national championships worldwide, including British Supersport, MotoAmerica Supersport, and the middleweight races at events such as the Daytona 200 and the Isle of Man TT's Supersport races. The class is regarded as a key stepping stone to Superbike competition.
