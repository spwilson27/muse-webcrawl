Ursa Minor

Ursa Minor
Nickname: The Little Bear
Brightest star: Polaris
Area: 256 sq. deg.

Ursa Minor, the Little Bear, is a constellation in the far northern sky. It contains the asterism known as the Little Dipper and, most importantly, Polaris, the North Star, which sits very close to the north celestial pole and has long served as a navigational reference.

Stars

Polaris (Alpha Ursae Minoris) is a yellow supergiant and Cepheid variable. The bowl of the Little Dipper includes Kochab and Pherkad, once themselves pole stars around 1500 BC, sometimes called the "Guardians of the Pole."

Mythology

In Greek myth Ursa Minor is associated with Arcas, son of Callisto (Ursa Major), both placed among the stars by Zeus.
