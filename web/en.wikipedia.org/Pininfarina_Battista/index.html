Pininfarina Battista

The Pininfarina Battista is an electric performance car manufactured by Automobili Pininfarina GmbH which is headquartered in Munich, Germany, and is a subsidiary of the Indian automotive company Mahindra & Mahindra. The name Battista is a tribute to the founder of the Pininfarina coachbuilding company, Battista "Pinin" Farina.

The car was publicly unveiled at the 2019 Geneva Motor Show. Production began in 2022 at a dedicated atelier in Cambiano, Italy, and is limited to 150 examples worldwide, each largely hand-assembled and extensively customisable.

Powertrain and performance

The Battista is powered by four electric motors, one at each wheel, supplied by Rimac Automobili, in combination with a 120 kWh lithium-ion battery pack in a T-shaped layout behind the seats and under the cabin. Combined output is about 1,900 PS (1,400 kW) and 2,340 N·m of torque, making it one of the most powerful production road cars ever built.

Claimed performance figures include acceleration from 0 to 100 km/h (62 mph) in under two seconds, 0–300 km/h in under 12 seconds, and a top speed of about 350 km/h (217 mph). Driving range on the WLTP cycle is approximately 476 km (296 miles).

Design

The Battista's body is built from carbon fibre over a carbon-fibre monocoque chassis, a platform related to that of the Rimac Nevera. It features butterfly doors, a full-width LED light bar at the front, and an active rear wing. The interior combines leather and aluminium trim with three digital displays oriented toward the driver.

Special editions

Special editions include the Battista Anniversario, limited to five cars, celebrating 90 years of the Pininfarina design house, and the open-top B95 "hyper Barchetta", based on the same electric architecture.
