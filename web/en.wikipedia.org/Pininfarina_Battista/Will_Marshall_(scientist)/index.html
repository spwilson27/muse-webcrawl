Will Marshall (scientist)

Known forCo-founding Planet Labs
FieldsAstrophysics, space science
InstitutionsNASA Ames, Planet Labs

Will Marshall is a British scientist and entrepreneur, best known as the co-founder and chief executive of Planet Labs (now Planet), the company that operates the largest constellation of Earth observation satellites. Planet's fleet of small "Dove" CubeSats images the entire land mass of the Earth every day.

Career

Marshall studied physics and received a doctorate in astrophysics from the University of Oxford. Before founding Planet in 2010 with Robbie Schingler and Chris Boshuizen, he worked at NASA Ames Research Center, where he was involved in small satellite projects and served as a systems engineer on missions including the LCROSS lunar impactor.

Planet Labs

Under Marshall, Planet applied consumer-electronics components and agile aerospace methods to build shoebox-sized satellites at scale, and has made its daily global imagery available to governments, companies and researchers for purposes including agriculture, forestry monitoring and disaster response.
