Earth observation satellite

An Earth observation satellite or Earth remote sensing satellite is a satellite used or designed for Earth observation (EO) from orbit, including spy satellites and similar ones intended for non-military uses such as environmental monitoring, meteorology, cartography and others.

Types and instruments

The most common type are Earth imaging satellites, which take optical satellite imagery analogous to aerial photography; some satellites carry radar instruments such as synthetic-aperture radar, which can image through cloud and at night. Other instruments include multispectral and hyperspectral imagers, altimeters for measuring sea level, and atmospheric sounders for weather forecasting.

Programmes

Major long-running programmes include the United States' Landsat series, operating since 1972, and Europe's Copernicus programme with its Sentinel satellites. Commercial providers now operate large constellations of small satellites, including many CubeSats, imaging the whole Earth daily. Data from Earth observation satellites underpin remote sensing science, disaster response, agriculture and climate research.
