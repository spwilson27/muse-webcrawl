Phosphoric acid

NamesPhosphoric acid; orthophosphoric acid
FormulaH3PO4
AppearanceColourless solid or syrupy liquid

Phosphoric acid (also called orthophosphoric acid) is a weak acid with the chemical formula H3PO4. It is normally encountered as a colourless, syrupy solution of 85% concentration in water.

Production

Most phosphoric acid is made by the wet process, in which phosphate rock is treated with sulfuric acid. The rock itself originates from ancient marine sediments—the geological sink of the phosphorus cycle.

Uses

The dominant use is in fertiliser manufacture (as ammonium phosphates and triple superphosphate). It is also used as a food additive (E338), a rust remover, and in detergents and pharmaceuticals.
