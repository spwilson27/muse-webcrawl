Cineworld

Cineworld Group plc is a British cinema chain and, through its subsidiaries, one of the largest cinema operators in the world. Founded in 1995, it operates cinemas under the Cineworld and Picturehouse brands in the United Kingdom and Ireland, Cinema City in Central and Eastern Europe, and Regal Cinemas in the United States.

History

Cineworld grew rapidly in the UK, acquiring the UGC chain in 2004 and listing on the London Stock Exchange in 2007. It merged with Cinema City International in 2014 and in 2018 completed a $3.6 billion acquisition of Regal Entertainment Group, making it the second-largest cinema company globally by screens. A proposed acquisition of Canada's Cineplex was abandoned in 2020.

Financial difficulties

Hard hit by the COVID-19 pandemic's cinema closures, the company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in the United States in September 2022 and restructured its debt, emerging in 2023 with the listed parent entering administration and shareholders wiped out.
