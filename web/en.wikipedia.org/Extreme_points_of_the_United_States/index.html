Extreme points of the United States

The extreme points of the United States are the locations that are the furthest north, south, east or west within United States territory.

Cardinal points

The northernmost point is Point Barrow, Alaska (71°23′N). The southernmost point of the contiguous United States is Key West, Florida, while the southernmost point of all U.S. territory is Rose Atoll, American Samoa. The easternmost point by longitude is Pochnoi Point, Semisopochnoi Island, Alaska — in the Aleutian chain, which crosses the 180th meridian — while West Quoddy Head, Maine, is the easternmost point of the mainland. The westernmost point by longitude is also in the Aleutians (Amatignak Island); on the mainland it is Cape Alava, Washington.

Other extremes

The highest point is Denali in Alaska, at 6,190 metres (20,310 ft); the lowest is Badwater Basin in Death Valley, California, 86 metres below sea level. The geographic centre of the fifty states is near Lebanon, Kansas; the centre of population is tracked by the Census Bureau and has moved steadily westward and southward, most recently to Missouri.
