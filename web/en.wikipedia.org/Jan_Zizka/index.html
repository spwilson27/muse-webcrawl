Jan Zizka

Jan Zizka (c. 1360–1424), in Czech Jan Žižka, was a Bohemian military leader and Hussite general considered a Czech national hero. Despite losing sight in both eyes, he never lost a battle.

Early life

Born at Trocnov in southern Bohemia, Zizka served as a mercenary, possibly at the Battle of Grunwald in 1410, and became a follower of Jan Hus.

Hussite Wars

After Hus's execution and the Defenestration of Prague in 1419, Zizka led Hussite forces against crusades by Sigismund. He pioneered the use of war wagons (wagenburg), field artillery and disciplined infantry recruited from peasants, winning at Sudoměř, Vítkov Hill and Kutná Hora.

Death and legacy

He died of plague in 1424 near Přibyslav. A monumental equestrian statue in Prague commemorates him.
