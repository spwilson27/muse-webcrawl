Filmfare Awards South

The Filmfare Awards South are annual awards that honour artistic and technical excellence in the South Indian film industries — Telugu cinema (Tollywood), Tamil cinema (Kollywood), Malayalam cinema, and Kannada cinema (Sandalwood). Presented by the Filmfare magazine group (The Times Group / Worldwide Media), they have been held since 1954 and are among the most prominent award ceremonies for South Indian cinema.

History

The first Filmfare Awards South ceremony was held in 1954 in Chennai, initially honouring Tamil and Telugu films; Malayalam and Kannada categories were added in later decades as separate language industries grew. Like the Hindi Filmfare Awards, winners receive the "Black Lady" statuette. The ceremony has traditionally been hosted in Chennai or Hyderabad, rotating among the four industries' capitals.

Categories

Awards are presented separately for each language industry in categories including Best Film, Best Director, Best Actor, Best Actress, Best Supporting Actor and Actress, Best Music Director, Best Lyricist, Best Playback Singer, and technical categories such as cinematography and editing. Lifetime Achievement Awards have been conferred on legends of South Indian cinema.

Records

Frequent winners include Kamal Haasan, Mammootty, Mohanlal, Chiranjeevi, and composer A. R. Rahman, reflecting the stature of the ceremony within the southern industries. Alongside the main Filmfare Awards for Hindi cinema, the South ceremony anchors the magazine's film-awards franchise, which has since expanded to regional and streaming categories.
