Raw (comics magazine)


Placeholder image: Raw (comics magazine)

Raw was an American comics anthology magazine edited by Art Spiegelman and Françoise Mouly and published from 1980 to 1991. It was a flagship of the alternative comics movement and the first magazine to serialise Spiegelman's Maus.

History

Frustrated with the underground comix scene, Spiegelman and Mouly produced Raw from their SoHo loft with a deliberate emphasis on comics as an art form — large format, high production values, and contributions from cartoonists including Charles Burns, Gary Panter, Sue Coe, Kim Deitch, Ben Katchor and Joost Swarte, alongside international artists and reprinted classics.

Legacy

Maus, serialised as an insert from issue 2, won a Pulitzer Prize after book publication. Raw's graphic-design ambition influenced later anthologies, and Mouly went on to found the Toon Books line of children's comics.
