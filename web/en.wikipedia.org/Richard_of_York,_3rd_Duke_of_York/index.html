Richard of York, 3rd Duke of York

Richard of York, 3rd Duke of York (21 September 1411 – 30 December 1460), also named Richard Plantagenet, was an English prince and magnate, a member of the royal house of York. He inherited vast estates and served as Lord Protector of England during the mental illness of King Henry VI.

Wars of the Roses

Through his mother Anne Mortimer, Richard had a strong claim to the throne, arguably superior to that of the reigning Lancastrian line. His rivalry with the court faction of Queen Margaret of Anjou and the Duke of Somerset led to the outbreak of the Wars of the Roses in 1455. After the Battle of Northampton in 1460 he pressed his claim and was named heir apparent under the Act of Accord, disinheriting Henry VI's son. Weeks later he was killed at the Battle of Wakefield. His sons became kings Edward IV and Richard III.
