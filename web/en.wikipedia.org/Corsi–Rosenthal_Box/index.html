Corsi–Rosenthal Box

The Corsi–Rosenthal Box is a do-it-yourself air purifier design created in August 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic by Richard Corsi, dean of engineering, and Jim Rosenthal, a filter manufacturer.

Design

Four or five MERV-13 furnace filters form the sides of a cube, taped together with a 20-inch box fan on top blowing upward and a cardboard shroud sealing the base. The design removes airborne particles including virus-laden aerosols at a cost far below commercial HEPA purifiers.

Reception

Studies found clean-air delivery comparable to portable HEPA units. Health authorities and schools adopted the design, and it became a widely cited example of open-source, low-cost public-health engineering.
