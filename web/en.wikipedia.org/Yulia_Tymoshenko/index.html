Yulia Volodymyrivna Tymoshenko (born 27 November 1960) is a Ukrainian politician who served twice as Prime Minister of Ukraine, in 2005 and from 2007 to 2010. She leads the Batkivshchyna political party and was a central figure of the 2004 Orange Revolution.

Career

Tymoshenko first built a career in the energy industry in the 1990s before entering parliament. As deputy prime minister for energy she clashed with oligarchic interests. After the Orange Revolution she became prime minister under President Viktor Yushchenko, and returned to the premiership in 2007. She narrowly lost the 2010 presidential election to Viktor Yanukovych.

Imprisonment and later career

In 2011 Tymoshenko was convicted of abuse of office over a 2009 gas agreement with Russia and imprisoned; the European Court of Human Rights later found her detention unlawful. She was released in February 2014 during the Euromaidan revolution and has remained active in parliament, running for president again in 2019.
