United States Naval Forces Central Command

The United States Naval Forces Central Command (NAVCENT) is the United States Navy element of United States Central Command (CENTCOM). Its commander also commands the United States Fifth Fleet and the Combined Maritime Forces. NAVCENT is headquartered at Naval Support Activity Bahrain in Manama, Bahrain.

Responsibilities

NAVCENT oversees naval operations across some 2.5 million square miles of water, including the strategic chokepoints of the Strait of Hormuz, the Suez Canal and the Bab el-Mandeb. Missions include maritime security, counter-piracy, deterrence and coalition operations.

History

The command's lineage runs from the Middle East Force established after the Second World War. Naval Forces Central Command was created with CENTCOM in 1983, and the Fifth Fleet was reactivated under its commander in 1995.

Innovation

NAVCENT's Task Force 59, established in 2021, integrates unmanned systems and artificial intelligence into fleet operations and is frequently cited as a model for manned-unmanned teaming.
