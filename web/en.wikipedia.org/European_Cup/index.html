European Cup

The European Cup was an annual club football tournament organised by UEFA, held from 1955 to 1992. It was the predecessor of the UEFA Champions League, and was contested by the champions of European national leagues.

History

The first tournament was held in 1955-56 and won by Real Madrid, who won the first five editions. The competition was rebranded as the Champions League in 1992.

Format

Unlike the modern competition, the European Cup was a straight knockout tournament with no group stage in its original form.
