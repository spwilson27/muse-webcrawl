Escutcheon (heraldry)

In heraldry, an escutcheon is a shield that forms the main or focal element in an achievement of arms. The word can apply to the physical shield or to its depiction in a coat of arms.

Description

The escutcheon bears the field, ordinaries, and charges of the arms. Its shape has varied by period and country, from the medieval heater shield to elaborate Baroque cartouches. Specific points of the shield — chief, fess point, base, dexter and sinister — are used in blazon to locate charges.

Inescutcheon

A small escutcheon borne within a larger shield is an inescutcheon, often representing arms of pretence or a territorial claim.
