Norman Rockwell Museum

The Norman Rockwell Museum is an art museum in Stockbridge, Massachusetts, dedicated to the art and life of Norman Rockwell (1894-1978), the American painter and illustrator famous for his covers for The Saturday Evening Post. Founded in 1969 with the artist's cooperation, the museum holds the largest public collection of Rockwell's work.

The museum building in Stockbridge

Collection

Rockwell placed his studio and personal archive in the museum's trust near the end of his life. The collection includes 574 original artworks, all 323 of his Saturday Evening Post covers, and the Norman Rockwell Archive of more than 150,000 items — photographs, fan mail, and business documents — used by scholars of American illustration. Rockwell's final studio, moved to the museum grounds, is preserved as he left it.

Exhibitions and mission

Beyond Rockwell, the museum mounts exhibitions on illustration art generally, and in 1999 it moved to its current building on 36 acres. In 2008 it received the National Humanities Medal. Its travelling exhibitions have taken Rockwell's work, including the Four Freedoms paintings, to audiences worldwide.
