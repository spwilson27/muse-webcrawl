Marlborough Region

Country: New Zealand
Main town: Blenheim
Other centre: Picton
Known for: Sauvignon blanc wine, Marlborough Sounds

The Marlborough Region, administered as a unitary authority by Marlborough District Council, lies at the northeastern end of New Zealand's South Island.

Geography

The region includes the drowned valleys of the Marlborough Sounds, the Wairau and Awatere plains, and the ferry port of Picton connecting to Wellington across Cook Strait.

Wine

Marlborough is New Zealand's largest wine-growing region, producing about three-quarters of the country's wine. Commercial vineyards began in the 1970s; its Sauvignon blanc, noted for intense tropical and herbaceous flavours, achieved international fame from the 1980s. Pinot noir and other varieties are also grown.
