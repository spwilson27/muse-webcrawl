Renewable energy in Scotland

The production of renewable energy in Scotland has come to the fore in recent years, as the country's abundant wind, wave and tidal resources have been developed. Scotland generates roughly the equivalent of all its electricity consumption from renewable sources in favourable years, and exports surplus electricity to the rest of the United Kingdom.

Wind power

Onshore and offshore wind are the largest sources of renewable electricity in Scotland. The Whitelee Wind Farm near Glasgow is one of the largest onshore wind farms in Europe, and major offshore projects include Beatrice and Moray East in the Moray Firth.

Hydroelectricity

Hydro power, developed from the 1940s by the North of Scotland Hydro-Electric Board, remains significant, and includes pumped-storage schemes such as Cruachan.

Marine energy

Scotland hosts the European Marine Energy Centre (EMEC) in Orkney, a leading test site for wave and tidal stream devices, and the MeyGen tidal array in the Pentland Firth.
