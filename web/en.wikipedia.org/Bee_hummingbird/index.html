Bee hummingbird

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The bee hummingbird (Mellisuga helenae), known in Spanish as the zunzuncito, is a species of hummingbird endemic to Cuba. It is the smallest known bird in the world and the smallest warm-blooded vertebrate.

Description

Males measure about 57 mm (2.25 in) in length and weigh roughly 1.8 g; females are slightly larger. Males have an iridescent red-to-pink throat and crown that extend into long lateral plumes, bluish upperparts and greyish-white underparts; females are green above and white below. Their tiny eggs are about the size of coffee beans, and their nests about 2.5 cm across.

Behaviour

Bee hummingbirds feed mainly on nectar from flowers such as Solandra and Lysiloma, supplementing their diet with insects. Their wings beat up to 80 times per second in normal flight and far faster in courtship displays. The species is considered Near Threatened due to habitat loss.
