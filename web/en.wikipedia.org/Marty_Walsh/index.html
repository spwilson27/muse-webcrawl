Martin Joseph Walsh
Born: April 10, 1967, Boston, Massachusetts, U.S.
Offices: U.S. Secretary of Labor (2021–2023); Mayor of Boston (2014–2021); NHLPA executive director (2023– )
Party: Democratic

Martin Joseph Walsh (born April 10, 1967) is an American politician and union official who served as the 29th United States Secretary of Labor from 2021 to 2023 and as the 54th mayor of Boston from 2014 to 2021. In 2023 he became executive director of the National Hockey League Players' Association (NHLPA).

Early life and labor career

Walsh was born in the Dorchester neighborhood of Boston to Irish immigrant parents. He joined Laborers' Union Local 223 as a young man and rose to become head of the Boston Building Trades Council and secretary-treasurer of the Massachusetts AFL-CIO. A recovering alcoholic, Walsh has spoken openly about his addiction and recovery, and he founded programs to support treatment for workers.

Political career

Walsh served in the Massachusetts House of Representatives from 1997 to 2014. In 2013 he was elected mayor of Boston, succeeding Thomas Menino, running on his labor background and a platform of economic development and education. As mayor he oversaw rapid construction growth in the city and negotiated with the developers of Boston's Seaport district. President Joe Biden nominated Walsh as Secretary of Labor, and he was confirmed by the Senate in March 2021, becoming the first union leader to hold the post in decades. He stepped down in 2023 to lead the NHL players' union.
