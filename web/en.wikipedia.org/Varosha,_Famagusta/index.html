Varosha, Famagusta

Varosha (Greek: Βαρώσια; Turkish: Maraş) is an abandoned southern quarter of the Cypriot city of Famagusta. Once a fashionable tourist resort, it was fenced off and left deserted after the Turkish military operation in Cyprus in 1974, when its Greek Cypriot inhabitants fled and were not permitted to return.

History

In the early 1970s Varosha was the main tourist area of Cyprus, with high-rise hotels along its beach fronting the Mediterranean. In August 1974, as Turkish forces advanced, the population fled; the area was sealed by the Turkish military and remained empty for decades, its buildings decaying. United Nations Security Council Resolution 550 (1984) called for the area to be transferred to UN administration and resettled only by its original inhabitants.

Partial reopening

In October 2020, Turkish and Turkish Cypriot authorities reopened part of the beachfront to visitors, a move condemned by the Republic of Cyprus and criticised in UN Security Council statements. In 2021 further sections were opened and a small part was demilitarised, amid continuing disputes over the area's future in Cyprus settlement talks.
