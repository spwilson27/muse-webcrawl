Meta Quest

Meta Quest is a line of standalone virtual reality headsets developed by Meta Platforms through its Reality Labs division. Originally launched under the Oculus brand, the headsets are self-contained devices that run software without requiring a connection to a PC or games console, using inside-out tracking from built-in cameras instead of external sensors.

History

The original Oculus Quest was released in May 2019, powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 835. The Quest 2 followed in October 2020 with a faster Snapdragon XR2 chip, higher-resolution displays and a lower price of US$299, becoming the best-selling VR headset in the world. In 2021 Facebook, Inc. rebranded itself as Meta and the headset line was renamed from Oculus Quest to Meta Quest. The premium Quest Pro, aimed at mixed-reality work, launched in October 2022, followed by the Quest 3 in October 2023 with a thinner design, colour passthrough cameras and the Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2, and the lower-cost Quest 3S in 2024.

Hardware and platform

Quest headsets track the user's head and two Touch controllers in six degrees of freedom. They can also track bare hands. Software is distributed through the Meta Quest Store; popular titles include Beat Saber and the social platform Horizon Worlds. The headsets can optionally be tethered to a PC via Quest Link to play PC VR software, including SteamVR titles.

Impact

The Quest line is credited with moving consumer virtual reality from a niche, PC-dependent market to mainstream standalone devices, with tens of millions of units sold. Meta has invested many billions of dollars in Reality Labs in pursuit of its "metaverse" strategy, drawing both praise for technical progress and criticism over sustained financial losses and privacy questions around camera-equipped devices.
