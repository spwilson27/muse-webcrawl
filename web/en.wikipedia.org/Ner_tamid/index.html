Ner tamid

The ner tamid (Hebrew: נֵר תָּמִיד, "eternal flame") is a continuously burning light placed above and in front of the Torah ark in a synagogue. It symbolises both the menorah of the ancient Temple in Jerusalem and the eternal presence of God.

History and symbolism

The practice recalls the western lamp of the Temple menorah, kept perpetually alight, and the pillar of fire that guided the Israelites. Traditionally an oil lamp, the ner tamid is today usually electric, though it is intended never to be extinguished.

Namesakes

Many synagogues and Jewish institutions bear the name Ner Tamid, including congregations across North America and a British Jewish scout group.
