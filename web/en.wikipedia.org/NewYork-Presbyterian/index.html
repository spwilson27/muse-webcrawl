NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital is a nonprofit academic medical center in New York City, formed in 1998 by the merger of the New York Hospital (founded 1771) and the Presbyterian Hospital (founded 1868). It is one of the largest hospitals in the United States.

Campuses and affiliations

The hospital system operates multiple campuses, including the Weill Cornell Medical Center on the Upper East Side of Manhattan and the Columbia University Irving Medical Center in Washington Heights. It is the teaching hospital for two Ivy League medical schools: Weill Cornell Medicine and Columbia's Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons.

Specialties

NewYork-Presbyterian is consistently ranked among the top hospitals in the United States, with nationally recognised programmes in cardiology and heart surgery, neurology and neurosurgery, psychiatry, paediatrics and organ transplantation.
