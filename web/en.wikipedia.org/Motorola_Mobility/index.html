Motorola Mobility LLC is an American consumer electronics company that designs and manufactures mobile devices, primarily smartphones. It was created in January 2011 when Motorola, Inc. split into two independent companies: Motorola Mobility (consumer devices) and Motorola Solutions (enterprise and government equipment).

Ownership history

In August 2011, Google announced its acquisition of Motorola Mobility for about US$12.5 billion, a move widely understood as securing Motorola's large patent portfolio for the Android ecosystem; the deal closed in May 2012. Google sold the company's set-top box business to Arris and, in January 2014, agreed to sell Motorola Mobility itself to China's Lenovo for about US$2.9 billion, retaining most of the patents. The Lenovo sale closed in October 2014.

Products

Motorola Mobility is known for the Moto G and Moto E budget and mid-range smartphone lines, the flagship Moto X and Edge series, the Moto Razr line of foldable phones reviving the classic Razr brand, and the Moto 360 smartwatch. Its phones are sold under the Motorola name, which Lenovo licenses and markets worldwide.
