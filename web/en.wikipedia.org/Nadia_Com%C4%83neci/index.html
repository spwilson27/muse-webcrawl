Nadia Elena Comăneci (born 12 November 1961) is a Romanian retired gymnast and a five-time Olympic gold medallist. At the 1976 Summer Olympics in Montreal, aged 14, she became the first gymnast in Olympic history to be awarded a perfect score of 10.0, an achievement so unexpected that the scoreboard, not programmed to display four digits, showed "1.00".

Career

Comăneci was discovered by coach Béla Károlyi and rose through the Romanian national program. At Montreal 1976 she earned seven perfect 10s and won gold on the uneven bars, balance beam, and in the all-around, becoming a global celebrity. At the 1980 Moscow Olympics she won two further gold medals, on balance beam and floor exercise. She retired from competition in 1984, having won nine Olympic medals and numerous world and European titles. Two skills on the uneven bars bear her name in the Code of Points.

Later life

In 1989 Comăneci defected from Communist Romania, eventually settling in the United States. She married American gymnast Bart Conner in 1996; together they run a gymnastics academy and are active in charitable work, including the Special Olympics. She is widely regarded as one of the greatest gymnasts of the twentieth century and helped transform the sport's popularity worldwide.
