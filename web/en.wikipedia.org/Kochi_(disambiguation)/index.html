Kochi (disambiguation)

Kochi or Kōchi may refer to several places, organisations and other topics. This disambiguation page lists articles associated with the title Kochi.

Places

  • Kochi, India — a major port city in the state of Kerala, India, formerly known as Cochin; see Kochi.
  • Kōchi, Japan — the capital city of Kōchi Prefecture on the island of Shikoku, Japan; see Kōchi.
  • Kōchi Prefecture — a prefecture of Japan located on the south coast of Shikoku, of which Kōchi city is the capital.
  • Kochi, Hiroshima — a former town in Hiroshima Prefecture, Japan, merged into Higashihiroshima in 2005.

  • Kochi-Muziris Biennale — an international contemporary art exhibition held in Kochi, India.
  • Kochi Metro — the rapid transit system serving the Indian city of Kochi.
  • Kochi Tuskers Kerala — a former franchise cricket team of the Indian Premier League, based in Kochi, India.
  • Kōchi Airport — the airport serving Kōchi, Japan (also known as Kōchi Ryōma Airport).
