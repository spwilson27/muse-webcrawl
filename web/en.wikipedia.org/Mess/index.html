Mess

A mess is a term with several related meanings, most prominently a place or occasion where members of the armed forces eat and socialize together, and more generally a state of disorder or untidiness.

Military mess

In military usage, a mess is a social institution within a unit: the officers' mess, sergeants' (or warrant officers' and sergeants') mess, and junior ranks' mess provide dining, drinking, and accommodation facilities and host formal dinners governed by tradition. On naval vessels, the wardroom and mess decks serve similar functions. Members pay "mess bills" for upkeep and events.

Other meanings

"Mess" can also refer to a quantity of food prepared for a group (as in a mess hall or mess kit), a cooked dish of soft food, or a person or situation in disorder ("a hot mess"). The word derives from the Latin missum, a course of food sent to the table.
