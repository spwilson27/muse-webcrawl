Nadia Comaneci

Nadia Comaneci
Born: November 12, 1961, Onești, Romania
Sport: Artistic gymnastics
Olympic medals: 9 (5 gold)
Famous for: The first perfect 10 (Montreal, 1976)

Nadia Comaneci is the unaccented English rendering of the name of the Romanian gymnast Nadia Elena Comăneci, whose perfect scores at the 1976 Montreal Olympics made her one of the most famous athletes of the twentieth century.

The perfect 10

On July 18, 1976, fourteen-year-old Comaneci performed an uneven bars routine that the judges scored a perfect 10.0 — the first in Olympic history. The Omega scoreboard, configured for a maximum of 9.99, displayed her score as 1.00, confusing the crowd until the result was explained. She received six more perfect 10s during those Games and won the all-around title, the youngest gymnast ever to do so.

Legacy

Comaneci's success sparked a global surge of interest in gymnastics and shifted the sport toward younger, smaller athletes. She went on to win further gold medals at the 1980 Moscow Olympics, defected from Romania in 1989, and settled in the United States, where she married gymnast Bart Conner.
