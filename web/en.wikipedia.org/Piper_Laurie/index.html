Piper Laurie

Piper Laurie (born Rosetta Jacobs; 22 January 1932 – 14 October 2023) was an American actress known for her performances in the films The Hustler (1961), Carrie (1976) and Children of a Lesser God (1986), each of which earned her an Academy Award nomination, and for the television series Twin Peaks, for which she won a Golden Globe.

Career

Signed by Universal Studios as a teenager, Laurie grew dissatisfied with ingénue roles and broke her contract in the 1950s, moving to New York to work in live television drama. Her return to films in The Hustler opposite Paul Newman brought critical acclaim. After a further hiatus raising her daughter, she returned as Sissy Spacek's fanatical mother in Carrie.

Later life

Laurie continued acting into her eighties and published a memoir, Learning to Live Out Loud, in 2011. She died in Los Angeles in 2023, aged 91.
