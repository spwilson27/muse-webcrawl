The Touhou Project is a series of Japanese bullet hell shoot 'em up games created almost entirely by the one-man doujin circle Team Shanghai Alice, whose sole member is ZUN. Beginning in 1996 on the PC-98 and continuing on Windows from 2002, the series is renowned for its elaborate bullet patterns, extensive musical scores composed by ZUN, and its large cast of characters.

Setting and characters

The games are set in Gensokyo, a sealed-off land of fantasy inhabited by humans, yōkai and gods. Shrine maiden Reimu Hakurei and magician Marisa Kirisame are the recurring protagonists who resolve the "incidents" that drive each game's plot.

Fandom

Touhou has generated an enormous derivative culture of fan music, art, games and animation, showcased at events such as Reitaisai, a convention devoted solely to the series. Guinness World Records has recognised it as the most prolific fan-made shooter series. ZUN's permissive guidelines for derivative works are often credited with the fandom's scale. Music from the games, especially tracks such as "U.N. Owen Was Her?", has been widely remixed online.
