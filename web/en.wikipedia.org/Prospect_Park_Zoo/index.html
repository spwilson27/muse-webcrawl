Prospect Park Zoo

The Prospect Park Zoo is a 12-acre zoo in Prospect Park, Brooklyn, New York City. Opened in its modern form on 5 October 1993, it is operated by the Wildlife Conservation Society (WCS), which also runs the Bronx Zoo and Central Park Zoo.

History and exhibits

A municipal zoo stood on the site from 1935; after decades of decline and a notorious 1987 incident, the city transferred management to WCS (then the New York Zoological Society), which rebuilt it as a children's zoo emphasizing education. Exhibits include sea lions, red pandas, baboons, prairie dogs, and a barn area with domestic animals, organized along Discovery Trail themes of habitats and adaptation.
