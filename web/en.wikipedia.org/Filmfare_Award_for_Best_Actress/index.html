The Filmfare Award for Best Actress is an award presented annually at the Filmfare Awards by Filmfare magazine to recognise the actress who has delivered an outstanding performance in a leading role in Hindi cinema. It was first awarded in 1954 to Meena Kumari for Baiju Bawra.

Records

Nutan and Kajol hold the record for the most wins in the category, with five each. Madhuri Dixit has received the most nominations. Winners are chosen by a combination of public voting and a panel of experts, a dual system introduced after criticism of earlier popularity-based results.

History

The Filmfare Awards were established in 1954, modelled on the Academy Awards, and remain among the most prominent film awards in India. The Best Actress trophy is a statuette of a woman with upraised arms, commonly called "the black lady".
