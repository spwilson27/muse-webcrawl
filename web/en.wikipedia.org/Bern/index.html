Bern

Bern (German: Bern; French: Berne) is the de facto capital of Switzerland, referred to as the "federal city" (Bundesstadt). With a population of about 135,000 in the city and around 420,000 in the agglomeration, Bern is the fifth-most populous city in Switzerland. It is the capital of the canton of Bern and lies on the Swiss plateau in west-central Switzerland, encircled by the river Aare.

History

According to legend, the city was founded in 1191 by Duke Berthold V of Zähringen, who named it after a bear (Bär) he had hunted in the woods nearby; the bear has been the city's heraldic animal ever since, and live bears have been kept in the city's bear pit (Bärengraben) since the 16th century, today in the adjacent Bear Park. Bern joined the Swiss Confederacy in 1353 and became its largest city-state north of the Alps, ruling extensive territories including the Vaud. In 1848 Bern was chosen as the seat of the new Swiss federal government. A fire in 1405 destroyed much of the wooden old town, which was rebuilt in sandstone.

Geography and landmarks

The medieval Old City, built on a narrow hill bordered on three sides by the Aare, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Landmarks include the Zytglogge, a medieval clock tower with moving puppets; the Bern Minster (Berner Münster), Switzerland's tallest cathedral; the Federal Palace (Bundeshaus), seat of the Swiss parliament and government; and some six kilometres of arcaded walkways (Lauben) lined with shops. Albert Einstein developed his special theory of relativity while working at the Bern patent office in 1905, living in the apartment now preserved as the Einsteinhaus museum. The Paul Klee Zentrum and the Bern Historical Museum hold major collections.

Culture and sport

Bern is home to SC Bern, one of Europe's best-attended ice hockey clubs, and to the football club BSC Young Boys, based at the Wankdorf Stadium. The Gurten, a local mountain accessible by funicular, hosts the annual Gurtenfestival. Bern's dialect of Swiss German is characteristic of the region, and the city consistently ranks among the world's highest for quality of life.
