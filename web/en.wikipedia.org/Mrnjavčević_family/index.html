Mrnjavčević family

The Mrnjavčević family (Serbian Cyrillic: Мрњавчевићи / Mrnjavčevići) was a medieval Serbian noble family prominent in the 14th century, during the decline of the Serbian Empire. Its best-known members were King Vukašin Mrnjavčević and his sons, Prince Marko (Marko Kraljević) and Andrijaš.

Marko's Monastery, founded by the Mrnjavčević family

History

The family rose to prominence under Emperor Stefan Dušan and his successor Stefan Uroš V. Vukašin Mrnjavčević was crowned king and co-ruler of the Serbian Empire in 1365, ruling lands in western Macedonia from Prilep. Vukašin and his brother, Despot Jovan Uglješa, were killed by the Ottomans at the Battle of Maritsa in 1371. Vukašin's son Marko became king, ruling as an Ottoman vassal until his death at the Battle of Rovine in 1395; his brother Andrijaš eventually fled to Hungary.

In epic tradition

Although in historical terms a vassal ruler, Marko Mrnjavčević became, as "Kraljević Marko" (Prince Marko), the greatest hero of Serbian and wider Balkan epic poetry, celebrated as a superhumanly strong protector of the weak against Ottoman oppression.
