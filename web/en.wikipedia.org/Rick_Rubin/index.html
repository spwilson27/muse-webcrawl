Frederick Jay Rubin (born March 10, 1963), known professionally as Rick Rubin, is an American record producer and executive, widely regarded as one of the most influential producers in popular music. He co-founded Def Jam Recordings with Russell Simmons in 1984 and later founded American Recordings.

Career

Emerging from New York's punk and early hip-hop scenes, Rubin produced landmark records for the Beastie Boys, Run-DMC (including their collaboration with Aerosmith on "Walk This Way"), LL Cool J and Slayer. He later worked across genres with artists including Metallica, Red Hot Chili Peppers, Adele, Eminem and Johnny Cash, whose late-career American recordings with Rubin are particularly acclaimed. His production style is noted for minimalism and stripping songs to their essentials. His book The Creative Act: A Way of Being was published in 2023.
