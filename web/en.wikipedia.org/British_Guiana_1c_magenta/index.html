The British Guiana 1c magenta is regarded as the world's most famous and valuable postage stamp. Issued in 1856 as a provisional stamp by the post office of British Guiana (now Guyana), only one copy is known to exist. It was discovered in 1873 by a schoolboy, Vernon Vaughan, among his uncle's papers.

Sales

The stamp has set record prices repeatedly. It was sold in 2014 for US$9.5 million, then a world record for a stamp, and was bought by shoe designer Stuart Weitzman. In 2021 it was sold to the philatelic firm Stanley Gibbons for US$8.3 million.
