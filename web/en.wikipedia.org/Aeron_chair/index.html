Aeron chair

The Aeron chair is an office chair designed by Don Chadwick and Bill Stumpf for the American furniture manufacturer Herman Miller. Launched in 1994, it became an icon of 1990s office design and a symbol of the dot-com era.

Design

Instead of foam upholstery, the Aeron uses a suspended mesh material called Pellicle across the seat and back, which conforms to the body and allows air circulation. It offers adjustable lumbar support, tilt tension and armrests, and was sold in three sizes to fit different body types.

Legacy

The chair is held in the permanent collection of the Museum of Modern Art in New York. Herman Miller released a remastered version in 2016, and the original became so ubiquitous that surplus chairs flooded the second-hand market after the dot-com crash.
