Reiwa era

Reiwa 令和
Began: 1 May 2019
Emperor: Naruhito
Preceded by: Heisei

The Reiwa era (Japanese: 令和, pronounced [ɾeːwa]) is the current era of Japan's official calendar. It began on 1 May 2019, the day Emperor Naruhito ascended the throne following the abdication of his father, Emperor Akihito — the first abdication of a Japanese emperor in about two centuries. The preceding era was Heisei.

Name

The name was announced on 1 April 2019 by Chief Cabinet Secretary Yoshihide Suga, who held up a framed calligraphy of the two characters, earning him the nickname "Uncle Reiwa." For the first time, the characters were drawn from Japanese classical literature — the Man'yōshū, an 8th-century poetry anthology — rather than from Chinese classics. The government gave the meaning as "beautiful harmony," ordering (令) and peace/harmony (和).

Events

The era has included the COVID-19 pandemic and the postponed Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games, the assassination of former prime minister Shinzo Abe in 2022, and Japan's evolving security policy. The transition required updates to documents, coinage and software systems — the character 令和 was added to Unicode as a single square ligature (U+32FF).
