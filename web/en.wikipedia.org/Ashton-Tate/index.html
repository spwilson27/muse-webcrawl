Ashton-Tate Corporation was an American software company best known for developing dBASE, the first widely used database management system for personal computers. Founded in 1980 by George Tate and Hal Lashlee, it grew into one of the "big three" personal computer software companies alongside Microsoft and Lotus.

dBASE

The company marketed Wayne Ratliff's Vulcan database under the name dBASE II (there was no dBASE I), and the product's success made Ashton-Tate a major force in the industry. dBASE III and dBASE III Plus consolidated its position, but the delayed and buggy dBASE IV, released in 1988, badly damaged the company's reputation and market share.

Other products and decline

Ashton-Tate also sold the Framework integrated office suite and acquired the word processor MultiMate. After losses following dBASE IV, the company was acquired by Borland in 1991 for about 40 million, ending its independent existence.
