The Sandman (comic book)

The Sandman is a comic book series written by Neil Gaiman and published by DC Comics, later under its Vertigo imprint. It ran for 75 issues from 1989 to 1996. The protagonist is Dream, also called Morpheus, the personification of dreams and one of the seven Endless, alongside Destiny, Death, Destruction, Desire, Despair, and Delirium.

Story

The series opens in 1916, when an occult ritual accidentally imprisons Dream for seventy years instead of his sister Death. Escaping in 1989, he rebuilds his kingdom, the Dreaming, and recovers his stolen tools — his helm, pouch of sand, and ruby. Across story arcs such as Preludes & Nocturnes, The Doll's House, Season of Mists, and The Kindly Ones, the series blends horror, mythology, history, and literary fantasy, weaving in figures from Shakespeare to Norse and Egyptian gods.

Reception

The Sandman was one of the first graphic novels to appear on the New York Times Best Seller list and won numerous awards, including a World Fantasy Award for the story "A Midsummer Night's Dream". It is widely considered among the greatest works in the comics medium. A television adaptation premiered on Netflix in 2022.
