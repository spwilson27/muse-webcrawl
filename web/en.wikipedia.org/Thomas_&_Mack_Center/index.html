The Thomas & Mack Center is a multi-purpose arena located on the campus of the University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) in Paradise, Nevada. It is home to the UNLV Runnin' Rebels basketball team and has a seating capacity of roughly 18,000 to 19,000 depending on configuration.

History

The arena opened on 16 December 1983 with a gala event featuring Frank Sinatra, Dean Martin and Diana Ross. It is named for Nevada bankers E. Parry Thomas and Jerome H. Mack, who donated the funds for the feasibility study and served as the project's principal private benefactors. A bronze statue of Thomas and Mack stands outside the arena's main entrance.

Events

The Thomas & Mack Center hosted UNLV's celebrated basketball teams under coach Jerry Tarkanian, including the 1990 national championship squad. For many years it hosted the National Finals Rodeo each December, and it has been a major venue for championship boxing — including bouts involving Mike Tyson, Evander Holyfield and Oscar De La Hoya — as well as concerts, the Mountain West Conference basketball tournaments, and NBA preseason and summer league games. The arena underwent a major renovation completed in 2016, adding a new scoreboard, seating upgrades and concourse improvements.

Together with the adjacent Cox Pavilion, which opened in 2001, the complex hosts the NBA Summer League each July.
