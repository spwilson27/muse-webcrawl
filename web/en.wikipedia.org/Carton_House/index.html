Carton House

Carton House is a country house and estate near Maynooth in County Kildare, Ireland. For over seven hundred years it was the seat of the FitzGerald family, Earls of Kildare and later Dukes of Leinster, Ireland's premier aristocratic family. Today the estate operates as a hotel and golf resort set in over 1,000 acres of parkland.

Carton House, former seat of the Dukes of Leinster.

History

The Carton lands came to the FitzGeralds in the 13th century. A house was built in the 17th century by Maurice FitzGerald, and in 1739 the 20th Earl of Kildare, later 1st Duke of Leinster, commissioned the architect Richard Cassels to rebuild it as a Palladian mansion. In the early 19th century the 3rd Duke employed Richard Morrison to remodel the house, enlarging the dining room and creating celebrated interiors including the Chinese Boudoir associated with Queen Victoria's visits.

Estate

The demesne, landscaped in the English natural style, includes the River Rye, a lake with a boathouse, the ornamental Shell Cottage — its interior decorated with seashells — and the Prospect Tower. In the 2000s the estate was developed with two championship golf courses, the O'Meara and the Montgomerie, designed by Mark O'Meara and Colin Montgomerie; the Montgomerie course hosted the Irish Open in 2005, 2006, and 2013.

Modern use

Carton House opened as a hotel in 2006 and became part of the Fairmont group in 2020 following an extensive renovation. The estate also served as a training base for sports teams, including the Irish rugby team and, under a long-term arrangement, Real Madrid.
