Avis Car Rental is an American car rental company headquartered in Parsippany, New Jersey. Together with Budget Rent a Car, Budget Truck Rental and Zipcar, it is part of the Avis Budget Group.

History

Avis was founded in 1946 by Warren Avis at Willow Run Airport in Ypsilanti, Michigan, with three cars, pioneering the airport car rental business. It grew to become the second-largest car rental company in the United States behind Hertz. Its famous advertising slogan "We Try Harder", introduced in 1962 by the agency Doyle Dane Bernbach, candidly embraced its number-two position and became one of the most celebrated campaigns in advertising history.

Operations

Avis operates in North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australasia, largely through company-owned locations and licensees, with a premium market positioning relative to the budget-oriented Budget brand.
