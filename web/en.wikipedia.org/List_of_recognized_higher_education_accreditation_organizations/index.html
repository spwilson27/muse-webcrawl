List of recognized higher education accreditation organizations

This list covers organisations that accredit higher education institutions and are recognised by national authorities or by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation (CHEA) in the United States.

United States institutional accreditors

  • Higher Learning Commission (HLC)
  • Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE)
  • New England Commission of Higher Education (NECHE)
  • Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU)
  • Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC)
  • WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC)
  • Accrediting Commission for Community and Junior Colleges (ACCJC)

Programmatic accreditors cover fields such as business (AACSB), engineering (ABET), medicine, law (ABA) and nursing. Other countries use bodies such as the QAA in the United Kingdom and national accreditation councils worldwide.
