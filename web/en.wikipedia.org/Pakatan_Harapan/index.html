Pakatan Harapan

Pakatan Harapan (abbreviated PH; English: "Alliance of Hope") is a Malaysian political coalition consisting of centre-left political parties. It was founded in 2015 as the successor to the Pakatan Rakyat coalition. The coalition currently consists of the Democratic Action Party (DAP), the People's Justice Party (PKR), the National Trust Party (AMANAH), and the United Progressive Kinabalu Organisation (UPKO).

History

Pakatan Harapan was formed on 22 September 2015, bringing together the DAP, PKR, and the newly formed AMANAH, which had split from the Malaysian Islamic Party (PAS). In 2017 the Malaysian United Indigenous Party (BERSATU), founded by former prime minister Mahathir Mohamad, joined the coalition, and Mahathir was named its chairman.

2018 general election

In the general election of 9 May 2018, Pakatan Harapan won a majority in the Dewan Rakyat, ending the 61-year rule of the Barisan Nasional coalition — the first change of government in Malaysia's history. Mahathir became prime minister for a second time, at age 92, and the coalition implemented reforms including the repeal of the goods and services tax and the release of political leader Anwar Ibrahim, who was later appointed prime minister in 2022.

2020 political crisis and return to government

The PH government collapsed in February 2020 in the "Sheraton Move", when BERSATU and a faction of PKR MPs left the coalition, bringing down Mahathir's administration. After the November 2022 general election produced a hung parliament, PH formed a unity government with other coalitions, and Anwar Ibrahim became Malaysia's tenth prime minister.
