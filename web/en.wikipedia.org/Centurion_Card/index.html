Centurion Card

Issuer: American Express
Launched: 1999
Type: Invitation-only charge card

The Centurion Card, known informally as the Amex Black Card, is an invitation-only charge card issued by American Express. Launched in 1999, it is targeted at a select group of very high-spending Platinum Card members and is famous for its black anodised titanium card, high fees, and concierge services.

Eligibility and fees

American Express does not publish formal criteria; invitations are generally extended to cardholders who spend and pay off several hundred thousand dollars per year. In the United States the card has carried an initiation fee of $10,000 and an annual fee of $5,000 (fees vary by market and have changed over time).

Benefits

Benefits include a dedicated concierge and travel agent, elite status in airline and hotel loyalty programmes, access to airport lounges, and invitations to exclusive events. The card's mystique has made it a cultural symbol of extreme wealth, frequently referenced in film, television, and music.
