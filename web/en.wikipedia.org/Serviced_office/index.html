Serviced office

Serviced offices provide furnished space and shared facilities on flexible terms

A serviced office is an office or office building that is fully equipped and managed by a facility management company, which then rents individual offices or floors to other companies. Serviced offices, also referred to as business centres, executive offices or managed offices, are usually available on shorter, more flexible lease terms than conventional leased office space, and typically come furnished with utilities, internet access, reception services and shared meeting rooms included in a single monthly fee.

Services

Typical services provided include furnished office suites, a staffed reception, mail handling, telephone answering, IT and telecommunications infrastructure, access to meeting and conference rooms, and shared kitchen and cleaning services. Occupants can often expand or contract their space within the building as their needs change.

Users

Serviced offices are popular with start-ups, small businesses, companies entering a new region, project teams and firms that need temporary overflow space, as well as professionals who want a prestigious business address without a long-term lease commitment. From the 2010s, the model expanded rapidly worldwide, alongside the related concept of coworking spaces, with large international operators offering networks of centres in major cities.

Comparison with coworking

Whereas coworking spaces emphasize open-plan shared desks and community events, serviced offices usually provide private, lockable offices within a shared facility. In practice the two models have converged, with many operators offering both.
