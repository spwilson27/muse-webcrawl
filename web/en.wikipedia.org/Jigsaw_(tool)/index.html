Jigsaw (tool)

A jigsaw is a power tool with a reciprocating blade used to cut irregular curves, such as stenciled designs, in wood, metal, or other materials. It may also be called a saber saw.

Albert Kaufmann of Scintilla AG in Switzerland is credited with the invention in 1946, replacing the needle of a sewing machine with a saw blade; Bosch marketed the design. Modern jigsaws are electric, have variable speed and bevel-cutting bases, and take interchangeable blades with different tooth patterns.
