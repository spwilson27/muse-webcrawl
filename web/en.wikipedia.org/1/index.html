1

1 (one, unit, unity) is a number and the numerical digit used to represent it. It is the first positive natural number, following 0 and preceding 2. Any number multiplied by one remains that number, so 1 is the multiplicative identity of the integers, real numbers and complex numbers.

In mathematics

One is neither a prime number nor a composite number: it is a unit in the ring of integers, having a multiplicative inverse within the integers' extension to the rationals and reals. By convention, 0! and 1! both equal 1. The empty product is defined to be 1. In many algebraic structures, 1 denotes the identity element of a multiplicative group or monoid. One is also the first figurate number of every kind, the first triangular, square, pentagonal and hexagonal number, and it is both a Fibonacci and a Catalan number.

In number theory, a probability of 1 in a probability space indicates an event that is certain to occur. In logic, 1 often denotes truth. The decimal representation of fractions with denominator 3, such as 1/3 = 0.333..., illustrates repeating decimals.

In science and technology

The atomic number of hydrogen is 1, making it the lightest and most abundant chemical element in the universe. In physics, several fundamental constants are expressed in systems of natural units where constants such as the speed of light are set equal to 1. The Earth–Sun distance, one astronomical unit, was historically defined in terms of Earth's orbit.

In culture

"Number one" commonly denotes a winner, champion or the leading item in a ranking, as in music charts. A solo performance or a single entity is often described with the number one; monotheistic religions worship one God. In many sports the numeral 1 is worn by players in particular positions, such as the goalkeeper in association football.
