LM358

The LM358 is a dual operational amplifier integrated circuit in an 8-pin package, introduced by National Semiconductor in the 1970s and now made by many manufacturers. It is one of the most widely used general-purpose op-amps in consumer and industrial electronics.

TypeDual operational amplifier
Package8-pin DIP / SOIC
Supply3–32 V (single or split)
RelatedLM324 (quad version)

Characteristics

The LM358 can operate from a single power supply as low as 3 V, and its input common-mode range includes ground, which makes it convenient for single-supply sensor and signal-conditioning circuits. Its output can swing close to ground but not to the positive rail. The quad version is the LM324; the family is known for low cost rather than speed or precision.

Applications

Typical uses include amplifiers, filters, comparators, current sensing and transducer interfaces in power supplies, automotive electronics and hobbyist projects.
