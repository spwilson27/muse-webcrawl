The Eurovision Song Contest 1965 was the tenth edition of the annual Eurovision Song Contest. It took place in Naples, Italy, following Gigliola Cinquetti's victory the previous year with "Non ho l'età". The contest was held on 20 March 1965 and was hosted by Renata Mauro.

Results

Eighteen countries participated, with Ireland making its debut. Luxembourg won for the second time with the song "Poupée de cire, poupée de son", written by Serge Gainsbourg and performed by the French singer France Gall. The United Kingdom finished second for a fifth time, with Kathy Kirby's "I Belong".

Notable events

The contest is remembered for the Swedish entry, Ingvar Wixell's "Absent Friend", which was sung entirely in English rather than Swedish, prompting the introduction of a rule the following year requiring entries to be performed in one of the national languages of the competing country. The Soviet Union broadcast the contest for the first time, and the show was also transmitted to several Eastern European countries via the Intervision network.
