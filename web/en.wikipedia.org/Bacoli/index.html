Bacoli

Bacoli is a comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Naples in the Italian region of Campania, located about 15 kilometres west of Naples on the Phlegraean peninsula, overlooking the Gulf of Naples.

History

Bacoli occupies the site of ancient Bauli, a resort area of Roman Baiae. In the Roman era the coast was lined with the villas of senators and emperors; nearby stood Misenum, base of the imperial fleet. Ruins in the area include the Piscina Mirabilis, a vast Roman cistern, and the so-called Tomb of Agrippina at Bauli.

Geography

The comune includes the frazioni of Baia, Fusaro, Miliscola, Miseno and Torregaveta. Its coastline features Lake Fusaro, Cape Miseno and the Aragonese Castle of Baia, which houses the Archaeological Museum of the Campi Flegrei.
