Tybee Island is a city and barrier island in Chatham County, Georgia, United States, located at the mouth of the Savannah River on the Atlantic coast, about 18 miles (29 km) east of Savannah. It is a popular beach resort for the Savannah metropolitan area.

History

The island was inhabited by the Euchee people before European colonisation; its name is often said to derive from a Euchee word for "salt". The Tybee Island Light, first built in 1736 under James Oglethorpe, is one of the oldest lighthouses in the United States and remains an active aid to navigation. During the Civil War, Union forces on Tybee bombarded Fort Pulaski in 1862 in a famous demonstration of rifled artillery.

Geography

The island has about three miles of public beach. It is also known for a notable Cold War footnote: a Mark 15 hydrogen bomb lost by the US Air Force in nearby Wassaw Sound in 1958 was never recovered.
