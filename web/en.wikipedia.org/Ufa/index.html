Ufa (Russian: Уфа́; Bashkir: Өфө) is the largest city in and the capital of Bashkortostan, Russia, located at the confluence of the Belaya and Ufa rivers in the foothills of the southern Ural Mountains.

History

Founded as a fortress in 1574 on the orders of Ivan the Terrible, Ufa became a trading and administrative center and later an important hub of Islam in Russia; the Russian Central Muslim Board was headquartered there from the Soviet era.

Economy

Ufa is a major center of oil refining and petrochemicals, with large refineries operated by Bashneft, and is a transport hub on the Trans-Siberian Railway.
