Wave-Gotik-Treffen

The Wave-Gotik-Treffen (WGT) is an annual world festival for “dark” music and goth subculture held in Leipzig, Germany, over the Pentecost (Whitsun) weekend. It is the largest festival of its kind in the world, attracting around 20,000 visitors, with roughly 200 performing artists and events spread across dozens of venues throughout the city.

History

The first WGT took place in 1992 at the Eiskeller club in Connewitz, Leipzig, in the aftermath of German reunification, when the East German goth scene first met the larger Western European scene. The festival grew steadily through the 1990s; a financial collapse of one organiser in 2000 briefly threatened it, but it was rescued and continued under new arrangements, expanding to use the agra exhibition grounds as its main camping and concert site.

Programme

Beyond concerts spanning gothic rock, deathrock, dark wave, EBM, industrial, neofolk, dark ambient, medieval music, metal and classical performances, the festival includes club nights, readings, theatre, film screenings, exhibitions, a pagan village, markets, and the Victorian picnic in Clara-Zetkin-Park, where attendees gather in elaborate period costume. Festival wristbands traditionally include free public transport in Leipzig and admission to some museums. The city has become known for welcoming the influx of black-clad visitors each Whitsun weekend.
