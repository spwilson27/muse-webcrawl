Marne (department)

Number: 51
Region: Grand Est, France
Prefecture: Châlons-en-Champagne
Largest city: Reims
Named for: River Marne

Marne is a department in northeastern France, in the Grand Est region, named after the River Marne. Its prefecture is Châlons-en-Champagne, while Reims is its largest city.

Geography and economy

The department covers part of the Champagne wine region; vineyards around Reims and Épernay produce Champagne, and chalk cellars store millions of bottles. Agriculture dominates the chalk plains of Champagne pouilleuse.

History

Created in 1790 during the French Revolution, Marne was a major First World War battleground: the First (1914) and Second (1918) Battles of the Marne were fought here, halting German advances toward Paris.
