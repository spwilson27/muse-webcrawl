Signify N.V.

Signify N.V. is a Dutch multinational lighting corporation headquartered in Eindhoven, Netherlands. It is the world's largest lighting manufacturer, known for the Philips brand of lamps and luminaires and for connected lighting systems. The company was formed in 2016 when Philips spun off its lighting division as Philips Lighting; it adopted the name Signify in 2018.

History

Philips began making incandescent lamps in 1891 and built a global lighting business over the following century. In 2014 Philips announced the separation of its lighting arm; Philips Lighting listed on Euronext Amsterdam in May 2016. The company renamed itself Signify in 2018 while continuing to use the Philips brand under licence. In 2020 it acquired Cooper Lighting Solutions, expanding its position in the North American professional market.

Products

Signify sells LED lamps and luminaires for homes, offices, industry, and public spaces, the Philips Hue smart-home lighting range, and the Interact platform of connected lighting and data services.
