Major League Soccer

Major League Soccer (MLS) is the top professional men's soccer league in the United States and Canada, sanctioned by the United States Soccer Federation. Founded in 1993 as part of the US bid to host the 1994 FIFA World Cup, it began play in 1996 with ten teams and has expanded to 30 clubs (27 in the US, 3 in Canada) as of 2025 with San Diego FC's debut. The league operates on a single-entity structure in which MLS owns all teams and centrally contracts players.

Soccer-specific stadiums have been central to MLS's growth since 1999.

Format

Teams are split into Eastern and Western Conferences. The club with the best regular-season record wins the Supporters' Shield; the top nine in each conference enter the MLS Cup Playoffs, culminating in the MLS Cup final. MLS clubs also contest the US Open Cup and the Canadian Championship, and since 2023 the expanded Leagues Cup against Mexico's Liga MX clubs. A salary cap and allocation-money mechanisms promote parity, with exceptions for up to three high-earning Designated Players per club — the rule introduced when David Beckham joined the LA Galaxy in 2007.

History

After a shaky start — two Florida teams folded in 2002 — the league stabilised with soccer-specific stadiums, beginning with Columbus Crew Stadium (1999), and steady expansion including Toronto FC (2007), Seattle Sounders (2009, drawing record crowds), Atlanta United (2017, which set attendance records and won MLS Cup in its second season), and Inter Miami CF, whose signing of Lionel Messi in 2023 brought global attention and underpinned a landmark ten-year Apple TV streaming deal (2023) for the MLS Season Pass. Average attendances above 23,000 rank among the highest in world football.

Development

Club academies and the MLS Next Pro reserve league form a development pathway, and MLS clubs increasingly sell homegrown players to European teams. The league's growth is tied to soccer's rising profile in North America ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, co-hosted by the US, Canada, and Mexico.
