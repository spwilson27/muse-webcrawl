Kuiper Systems LLC is a subsidiary of Amazon that was established in 2019 to deploy a large satellite internet constellation, known as Project Kuiper (later rebranded Amazon Leo), to provide low-latency broadband internet service to unserved and underserved areas.

Project Kuiper

The system is planned to comprise 3,236 satellites in low Earth orbit at altitudes of roughly 590–630 km. The US Federal Communications Commission licensed the constellation in July 2020, requiring Amazon to deploy half of the satellites by mid-2026. Two prototype satellites, KuiperSat-1 and KuiperSat-2, were launched in October 2023 aboard an Atlas V rocket, and the first production satellites followed in April 2025 on Atlas V, with further launches contracted on Vulcan Centaur, Ariane 6, New Glenn and Falcon 9 vehicles.

Facilities

Kuiper Systems is headquartered in Redmond, Washington, and operates a satellite production facility in Kirkland, Washington, and a processing facility at Kennedy Space Center in Florida. The project competes with SpaceX's Starlink and other broadband constellations.
