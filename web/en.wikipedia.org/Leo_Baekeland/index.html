Leo Baekeland

Leo Hendrik Baekeland (14 November 1863 – 23 February 1944) was a Belgian-born American chemist best known for inventing Bakelite, the first fully synthetic plastic, in 1907, and Velox photographic paper.

Life and work

After a chemistry career in Belgium, Baekeland emigrated to the United States, sold his Velox invention to Eastman Kodak, and used the proceeds to fund his own laboratory in Yonkers, New York. His development of the phenol-formaldehyde resin Bakelite launched the modern plastics industry and made him wealthy; the material was widely used for electrical insulation, telephones and household goods.
