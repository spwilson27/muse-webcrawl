The Rolls-Royce Cullinan, the marque's first sport utility vehicle.

The Rolls-Royce Cullinan is a full-size luxury sport utility vehicle (SUV) manufactured by Rolls-Royce Motor Cars. It is the first SUV produced by the marque and the first Rolls-Royce model with all-wheel drive. Launched in 2018, the Cullinan is named after the Cullinan Diamond, the largest gem-quality rough diamond ever found.

The Cullinan sits above the Phantom in price terms for many configurations and quickly became the best-selling model in the company's history, credited with attracting a younger generation of buyers to the brand.

History

Rolls-Royce announced in February 2015 that it would develop a "high-sided vehicle" in response to growing demand in the luxury SUV segment, following rivals such as Bentley, whose Bentayga had entered production. The production Cullinan was unveiled in May 2018 at the company's Goodwood headquarters.

The vehicle is built on the company's aluminium spaceframe "Architecture of Luxury" platform, shared with the Phantom VIII and the Ghost. A facelifted version, the Cullinan Series II, was revealed in May 2024 with revised styling, an updated interior and a new illuminated radiator grille.

Design

The Cullinan features coach doors (rear-hinged rear doors) in the Rolls-Royce tradition, a tailgate split into upper and lower sections, and an optional "Viewing Suite" in which two seats fold out from the luggage compartment. Interior appointments include lambswool floor mats, extensive leather and open-pore wood trim, and a partition glass separating the passenger compartment from the luggage area in some configurations.

Powertrain

Power comes from a 6.75-litre twin-turbocharged V12 engine producing 563 hp (420 kW) in the standard model and 591 hp (441 kW) in the Black Badge variant. The engine is paired with an eight-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive, supplemented by all-wheel steering. Despite its size, the Cullinan is rated for off-road use, with a wading depth of 540 mm.
