The AGM-158C LRASM (Long Range Anti-Ship Missile) is a stealthy anti-ship cruise missile developed for the United States Navy and Air Force by DARPA and Lockheed Martin, based on the AGM-158B JASSM-ER airframe.

Design

Unlike earlier anti-ship missiles that depend on external targeting throughout flight, LRASM uses onboard sensors and a multi-mode seeker to autonomously detect and engage specific ships within a group, even in GPS-denied and heavily jammed environments. It carries a 1,000-pound penetrating blast-fragmentation warhead.

Service

The missile reached early operational capability on the B-1B Lancer in 2018 and on the Navy's F/A-18E/F Super Hornet in 2019. Integration on other aircraft, including the P-8 Poseidon, has followed.
