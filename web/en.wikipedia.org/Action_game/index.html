Action game

An action game is a video game genre that emphasizes physical challenges, including hand–eye coordination and reaction time. The genre includes a large variety of sub-genres, such as fighting games, beat 'em ups, shooter games, and platform games. Multiplayer online battle arena and some real-time strategy games are also considered action games.

Definition

In an action game, the player typically controls a character (a "protagonist" or avatar) and must navigate a level, collecting objects, avoiding obstacles, and battling opponents with their natural skills as well as weapons and other tools at their disposal. At the end of a level or group of levels, the player must often defeat a boss enemy that is more challenging and often a major antagonist in the game's story. Enemy attacks and obstacles deplete the player character's health and lives, and the player receives a game over when they run out of lives.

Subgenres

Common subgenres include platform games, which emphasize jumping between platforms; shooter games, including first-person and third-person shooters; fighting games built around one-on-one combat; beat 'em ups; hack and slash games; and stealth games.

History

Early action games appeared in arcades in the late 1970s and early 1980s with titles such as Space Invaders (1978) and Pac-Man (1980). The genre expanded rapidly on home consoles in the 1980s and 1990s and remains one of the most popular categories of video games.
