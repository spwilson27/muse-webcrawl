Lennart Poettering

Lennart Poettering (born 15 October 1980) is a German software engineer known for creating several widely used components of the Linux operating system stack, including the systemd init system, the PulseAudio sound server, and the Avahi zeroconf implementation.

Work

Poettering worked for Red Hat for many years before joining Microsoft in 2022. systemd, adopted by most major Linux distributions, has been both influential and controversial within the free software community. He has also authored numerous essays and smaller tools relating to Linux system plumbing.
