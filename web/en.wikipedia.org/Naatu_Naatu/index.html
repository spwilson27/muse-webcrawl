"Naatu Naatu" (Telugu: నాటు నాటు, transl. "Local, Local" or "Ethnic") is an Indian Telugu-language song composed by M. M. Keeravani, with lyrics by Chandrabose and recorded by Rahul Sipligunj and Kaala Bhairava for the soundtrack album of the 2022 Indian film RRR. It was released on 10 November 2021 as the third single from the album.

The song features actors N. T. Rama Rao Jr. and Ram Charan performing a fast-paced, synchronised dance choreographed by Prem Rakshith, filmed at the Mariinskyi Palace in Kyiv, Ukraine. The Hindi version is titled "Naacho Naacho", with versions also released in Tamil ("Naattu Koothu"), Malayalam ("Karinthol") and Kannada ("Halli Naatu").

Reception and legacy

"Naatu Naatu" became a global viral phenomenon, praised for the energy and synchronisation of its hook step, with millions recreating the dance on social media. The song and its choreography were celebrated at screenings in the United States, and the sequence is widely credited with introducing many Western audiences to Telugu cinema.

Accolades

At the 95th Academy Awards in March 2023, "Naatu Naatu" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, becoming the first song from an Indian feature film to win an Oscar. It also won the Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song at the 80th Golden Globe Awards and the Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Song. The song was performed live at the Oscars ceremony.
