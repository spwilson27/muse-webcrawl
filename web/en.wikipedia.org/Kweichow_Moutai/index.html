Kweichow Moutai

Kweichow Moutai Co., Ltd.
Founded1951 (origins older)
HeadquartersMaotai, Guizhou, China
IndustryBaijiu (distilled spirits)

Kweichow Moutai is a Chinese state-owned company that produces Moutai, a brand of baijiu distilled from fermented sorghum in the town of Maotai, Guizhou province. Moutai is China's most famous liquor, served at state banquets — notably to toast Richard Nixon in 1972 and earlier by Zhou Enlai with foreign leaders — and is sometimes called China's "national liquor".

Company

The company, listed in Shanghai in 2001, became one of China's most valuable listed firms, at times the largest drinks company in the world by market capitalisation. Its flagship Feitian ("Flying Fairy") Moutai, at 53% alcohol, is a luxury status good whose retail price often far exceeds its official price.
