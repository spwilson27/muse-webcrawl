Pravasi Bharatiya Divas

Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (Hindi: प्रवासी भारतीय दिवस, "Non-Resident Indian Day") is a celebratory day observed by the Republic of India to mark the contribution of the overseas Indian community to the development of India. It commemorates the return of Mahatma Gandhi from South Africa to Mumbai on 9 January 1915.

The day was first celebrated in 2003, following the recommendations of a high-level committee on the Indian diaspora headed by L. M. Singhvi. Since 2015 the main convention has been held biennially, with smaller interim thematic conferences in the intervening years.

Convention

The convention is organised by the Ministry of External Affairs in partnership with a host state government. It brings together members of the Indian diaspora — non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) — for plenary sessions on investment, culture, science and youth engagement. Recent editions have been held in cities including Indore (2023) and Bhubaneswar (2025), the latter under the theme "Diaspora's Contribution to a Viksit Bharat".

Awards

During the convention the President of India confers the Pravasi Bharatiya Samman Award, the highest honour for overseas Indians, recognising exceptional contributions in fields such as public service, business, medicine, education and community welfare.
