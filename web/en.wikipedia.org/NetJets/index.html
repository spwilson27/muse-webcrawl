NetJets

NetJets Inc.
Founded: 1964 (as Executive Jet Aviation)
Headquarters: Columbus, Ohio, U.S.
Parent: Berkshire Hathaway
Industry: Fractional business aviation

NetJets Inc. is an American company that sells fractional ownership shares in private business jets, allowing multiple owners to share the cost and use of an aircraft. It is the largest private jet operator in the world and a subsidiary of Berkshire Hathaway.

History

Founded in 1964 as Executive Jet Aviation, the company pioneered the fractional ownership model in 1986 under Richard Santulli, launching as NetJets. Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway acquired the company outright in 1998.

Operations

NetJets operates a fleet of several hundred jets from manufacturers such as Bombardier, Cessna, Dassault, Embraer, and Gulfstream, offering fractional shares, jet cards, and leases, with sister operations in Europe and joint ventures elsewhere.
