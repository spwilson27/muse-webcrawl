Johann Olav Koss is a Norwegian former speed skater, widely considered among the greatest in the sport's history, and a physician and humanitarian.

Skating career

At the 1994 Lillehammer Winter Olympics, Koss won three gold medals — in the 1500 m, 5000 m and 10,000 m — setting world records in all three races. He had earlier won gold and silver at the 1992 Albertville Games, and he won the World Allround Championships three times.

Later life

Koss donated his Lillehammer prize money to Olympic Aid, helping inspire the charity that became Right To Play, which he founded to use sport and play to benefit children in disadvantaged areas. He later served as its president and CEO, trained as a doctor, and has held roles in international sport governance.
