List of official languages

An official language is a language given special legal status in a country, state, or international organisation. Official languages are used in government business, legislation, courts, and public education. Some countries have a single official language, others have several; South Africa recognises 12 and Bolivia's constitution recognises 37. Some countries, including the United States at the federal level for most of its history, have had no de jure official language.

Official languages of the United Nations

The United Nations has six official languages: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, and Spanish. All official UN documents are issued in these languages, and interpretation is provided in them at formal meetings.

Most widely official languages

English is an official language in some 58 sovereign states, the most of any language, followed by French (about 29), Arabic (about 26), Spanish (about 21), and Portuguese. This distribution largely reflects colonial history, while regional lingua francas such as Swahili in East Africa and Hindi in India serve official functions across large populations.
