EFL League Two

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The English Football League Two, simply known as League Two in England and for sponsorship purposes as Sky Bet League Two, is the third and lowest division of the English Football League (EFL) and fourth-highest division overall in the English football league system.

Structure

There are 24 clubs in League Two. Each club plays each other twice (once home, once away) for a 46-match season. Three points are awarded for a win and one for a draw. At the end of the season, the top three teams are promoted to League One, together with the winner of a play-off series among the teams finishing fourth to seventh. The bottom two clubs are relegated to the National League, losing their Football League status, and are replaced by the National League champions and play-off winners.

History

The division descends from the Football League Fourth Division, created in 1958 by merging the regional Third Divisions North and South. It became League Two in the 2004 rebranding of the Football League divisions below the Premier League. The introduction of two-up, two-down relegation with the non-League game in 1987 ended the old re-election system.
