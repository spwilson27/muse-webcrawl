Louis Renault (industrialist)

Louis Renault (12 February 1877 – 24 October 1944) was a French industrialist and pioneer of the automobile industry, co-founder with his brothers Marcel and Fernand of the Renault car company.

Founding Renault

In 1898 the young Renault built his first car, the Voiturette, notable for its direct-drive gearbox. In 1899 the brothers founded Société Renault Frères. The company grew rapidly, supplying taxis, trucks and, during the First World War, military vehicles including the pioneering Renault FT tank, widely regarded as the first modern tank with a fully rotating turret.

Later life

After France's liberation in 1944, Renault was arrested on charges of collaborating with the German occupation, whose authorities had used his factories. He died in custody in October 1944 before trial. His company was nationalised in 1945 and became Régie Nationale des Usines Renault.
