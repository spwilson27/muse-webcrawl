ThunderCats is an American animated television series that was produced by Rankin/Bass Animated Entertainment and Leisure Concepts and debuted in 1985, based on the characters created by Tobin "Ted" Wolf. The series follows the adventures of a group of catlike humanoid aliens — the ThunderCats — who flee their dying home planet of Thundera and crash-land on Third Earth.

Premise

The young lord Lion-O, wielder of the mystic Sword of Omens, leads companions including Cheetara, Panthro, Tygra, WilyKit and WilyKat, and the aged Jaga's spirit. On Third Earth they build the Cats' Lair and battle the evil sorcerer Mumm-Ra and the Mutants of Plun-Darr, who seek the Eye of Thundera embedded in Lion-O's sword.

Production and legacy

Animation was produced in Japan, and the show ran in syndication for 130 episodes across four seasons. Like many 1980s action cartoons it was accompanied by a toy line, produced by LJN. The franchise was rebooted with a 2011 animated series on Cartoon Network and again, in a more comedic style, as ThunderCats Roar in 2020. Rankin/Bass, better known for its stop-motion holiday specials such as Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer, made ThunderCats one of its most successful ventures into series animation.
