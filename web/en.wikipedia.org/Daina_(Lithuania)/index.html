Daina (Lithuania)

A daina is a traditional Lithuanian folk song, one of the richest bodies of folk poetry in Europe, with hundreds of thousands of variants recorded. The term parallels Latvian daina.

Themes

Dainos cover work, weddings, funerals, war, the seasons, and mythological subjects, often with refrains and diminutives. Many preserve pre-Christian imagery of the sun, moon, and nature deities.

Collections

Major collections were assembled in the 19th century during the Lithuanian National Revival, including by Antanas Juška. Song festivals (Dainų šventės) remain central to Lithuanian culture and are recognized by UNESCO together with Latvian and Estonian traditions.
