Sony Interactive Entertainment

Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC
Industry: Video games
Founded: 1993 (as Sony Computer Entertainment)
Headquarters: San Mateo, California, U.S.
Parent: Sony Group Corporation

Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC (SIE) is a multinational video game and digital entertainment company owned by Sony Group Corporation. It develops, manufactures, and markets the PlayStation line of home consoles, handheld systems, and the PlayStation Network online service.

History

The company was founded in Tokyo in November 1993 as Sony Computer Entertainment, to handle Sony's entry into the video game industry. The original PlayStation, launched in Japan in December 1994, became the first console to ship more than 100 million units. Successive consoles — PlayStation 2 (the best-selling console of all time), PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5 — made PlayStation one of the dominant brands in gaming.

In 2016 the company was restructured and renamed Sony Interactive Entertainment, with global headquarters moved to San Mateo, California.

PlayStation Studios

SIE's first-party development organisation, PlayStation Studios, includes teams such as Naughty Dog (The Last of Us, Uncharted), Santa Monica Studio (God of War), Insomniac Games (Spider-Man, Ratchet & Clank), Guerrilla Games (Horizon), Sucker Punch, Bend Studio, and Polyphony Digital (Gran Turismo). In 2022 SIE acquired Bungie, creator of Destiny.

Services

The PlayStation Network (PSN), launched in 2006, provides online multiplayer, digital distribution through the PlayStation Store, and the PlayStation Plus subscription service.
