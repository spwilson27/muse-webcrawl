Garden ornament

Garden ornament is a term for decorative items placed in gardens and landscaped environments. Ornaments range from classical statuary and fountains to sundials, bird baths, garden gnomes, and contemporary sculpture.

History

Ornamentation has been part of garden design since antiquity: Roman gardens featured statues and herms, and Renaissance Italian gardens revived classical sculpture on a grand scale. English landscape gardens of the 18th century used temples, grottoes, and follies as ornaments. In the 19th and 20th centuries, mass-produced ornaments such as cast-iron furniture, terracotta pots, and garden gnomes became accessible to suburban homeowners.

Types

Common garden ornaments include statues, fountains and water features, sundials, bird baths and feeders, wind chimes, lanterns, and whimsical figures such as gnomes and pink flamingos.
