Eric Yuan

Born: 20 February 1970, Tai'an, Shandong, China
Occupation: Businessman, engineer
Known for: Founder and CEO of Zoom

Eric Subrah Yuan (born February 20, 1970) is a Chinese-American billionaire businessman, engineer, and the chief executive officer and founder of Zoom Video Communications, of which he owns 22%.

Early life and career

Yuan was born in Tai'an, Shandong, China, and studied applied mathematics and computer science at Shandong University of Science and Technology. After being refused a US visa eight times, he immigrated to the United States in 1997 and joined WebEx, where he rose to become vice president of engineering. When Cisco acquired WebEx in 2007, Yuan became Cisco's corporate vice president of engineering.

Zoom

Frustrated that Cisco was not improving the WebEx product as he wished, Yuan left in 2011 to found Zoom. The company's video-conferencing platform grew explosively during the COVID-19 pandemic, when daily meeting participants rose from about 10 million in December 2019 to 300 million by April 2020. Yuan was named Time's Businessperson of the Year in 2020.

Personal life

Yuan lives in Santa Clara, California, with his wife and three children. His fortune, derived largely from his Zoom stake, has been estimated in the billions of dollars.
