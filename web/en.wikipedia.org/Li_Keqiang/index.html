Li Keqiang

Li Keqiang (1 July 1955 – 27 October 2023) was a Chinese economist and politician who served as premier of China from 2013 to 2023, the country's head of government. He was the second-ranking member of the Politburo Standing Committee of the Chinese Communist Party during much of that period.

Early life and education

Li was born in Hefei, Anhui province. His father was a local official. During the Cultural Revolution he was sent to Fengyang County for manual labour. He later studied law at Peking University and earned a doctorate in economics, writing a thesis on China's rural-urban dual structure that won a national prize.

Political career

Li rose through the Communist Youth League, becoming its first secretary in 1993. He served as governor of Henan and then party secretary of Liaoning, where he promoted housing reform and economic restructuring.

Premiership

As premier from 2013, under party general secretary Xi Jinping, Li oversaw economic policy, streamlining administration, cutting taxes and fees, and promoting mass entrepreneurship and innovation. The term "Likonomics" was coined by analysts for his approach of deleveraging and structural reform. He was succeeded by Li Qiang in March 2023.

Death

Li died of a heart attack in Shanghai on 27 October 2023, aged 68, months after leaving office.
