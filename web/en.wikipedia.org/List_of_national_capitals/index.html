List of national capitals

Reykjavík, Iceland — the world's northernmost capital of a sovereign state.

This is a list of national capitals, including capitals of sovereign states and of dependent territories. A capital is typically a city that physically encompasses the offices and meeting places of a country's government and is legally designated as such. Some countries have multiple capitals for different functions; South Africa, for example, has three: Pretoria (executive), Cape Town (legislative) and Bloemfontein (judicial).

Notes

Several capitals are planned cities built specifically as seats of government, including Brasília, Canberra, Islamabad, Abuja and Naypyidaw. Indonesia has legislated to move its capital from Jakarta to the new city of Nusantara.
