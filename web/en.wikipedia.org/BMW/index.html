BMW

BMW (Bayerische Motoren Werke AG) is a German multinational manufacturer of luxury vehicles and motorcycles headquartered in Munich, Bavaria. Founded in 1916 as an aircraft engine manufacturer, it is one of the world's best-known premium car brands.

History

BMW began by producing aircraft engines during the First World War. After the war it turned to motorcycle and then automobile production, acquiring Fahrzeugfabrik Eisenach in 1928. After the Second World War the company rebuilt in West Germany and grew with models such as the Isetta, the New Class sedans of the 1960s, and the 3 Series launched in 1975.

Products

The company sells cars under the BMW, Mini and Rolls-Royce brands, and motorcycles under BMW Motorrad. Its model range spans the 1 Series through the 8 Series, the X range of SUVs, the M high-performance division, and the electric BMW i sub-brand.

Motorsport

BMW has a long motorsport history in touring cars, Formula One (as an engine supplier and, from 2006 to 2009, with its own team), endurance racing and motorcycle competition.
