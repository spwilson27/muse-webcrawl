Les Misérables (2012 film)

Les Misérables is a 2012 epic historical musical film directed by Tom Hooper, adapted from the stage musical by Claude-Michel Schönberg and Alain Boublil, itself based on Victor Hugo's 1862 novel. It is notable for having its songs recorded live on set rather than pre-recorded.

Plot

Jean Valjean (Hugh Jackman), imprisoned for stealing bread, breaks parole and reinvents himself as a mayor and factory owner, while Inspector Javert (Russell Crowe) pursues him across decades. Valjean adopts Cosette, daughter of the destitute Fantine (Anne Hathaway), and their lives intertwine with the 1832 Paris uprising.

Reception and awards

The film was a commercial success and received eight Academy Award nominations, winning three, including Best Supporting Actress for Hathaway's performance of "I Dreamed a Dream".
