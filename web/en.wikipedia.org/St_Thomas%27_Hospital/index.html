St Thomas' Hospital
Location: Lambeth, London
Founded: by 1215 (origins 12th century)
Trust: Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust

St Thomas' Hospital is a large NHS teaching hospital in Lambeth, central London, England, opposite the Houses of Parliament across the River Thames. It is one of London's oldest and most famous hospitals.

History

The hospital was described as ancient in 1215 and was named for St Thomas Becket. Originally in Southwark, it moved to its present riverside site in 1871. Florence Nightingale founded her nursing school there in 1860, the first secular nursing school in the world.

Today

Part of Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust and King's Health Partners, it houses the Evelina London Children's Hospital and major specialist services.
