Corruption in India

Corruption in India is an issue affecting the economy and public services at central, state and local levels, ranging from petty bribery for licences and permits to large procurement and spectrum-allocation scandals.

Institutions

Anti-corruption bodies include the Central Vigilance Commission, the Central Bureau of Investigation and state Lokayuktas; the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act was passed in 2013 after the India Against Corruption movement led by Anna Hazare.

Major scandals and reforms

Notable cases include the Bofors scandal, the 2G spectrum case and the coal allocation case. Reforms include the Right to Information Act 2005, digitisation of payments, and direct benefit transfers intended to reduce leakages.
