Minecraft

Minecraft is a sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios and originally created by Swedish programmer Markus "Notch" Persson. It was publicly released in an alpha version in 2009 and fully released in November 2011, after which Jens "Jeb" Bergensten took over lead development.

Gameplay

Players explore a procedurally generated, blocky three-dimensional world made of voxels, gathering resources, crafting tools and items, building structures, and — in Survival mode — fighting hostile mobs and managing health and hunger. Creative mode gives players unlimited resources and the ability to fly. The game has no required goals, allowing players wide freedom in how they play.

Impact

Minecraft is the best-selling video game of all time, with over 300 million copies sold. Microsoft acquired Mojang in 2014 for US$2.5 billion. The game is widely used in education and has spawned books, merchandise, spin-off games, and a film adaptation.
