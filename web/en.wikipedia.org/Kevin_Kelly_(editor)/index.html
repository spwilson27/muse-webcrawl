Kevin Kelly (editor)

Kevin Kelly (born 1952) is an American writer, editor and futurist, best known as the founding executive editor of Wired magazine. He previously edited the Whole Earth Review and helped launch The WELL online community.

Works

His books include Out of Control (1994), New Rules for the New Economy (1998), What Technology Wants (2010), and The Inevitable (2016), exploring how technology shapes society.
