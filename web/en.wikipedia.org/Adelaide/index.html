Adelaide

Adelaide is the capital city of South Australia and the fifth-most populous city in Australia, with a metropolitan population of over 1.4 million. It lies on the Adelaide Plains between the Gulf St Vincent and the Mount Lofty Ranges.

History

The Kaurna people are the traditional owners of the Adelaide region. The city was founded in 1836 as the planned capital of a free British settlement and named after Queen Adelaide, consort of King William IV. Colonel William Light designed the city grid, with wide boulevards and large public squares surrounded by park lands.

Geography and climate

Adelaide has a Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers and mild winters. The city centre is roughly rectangular, divided by the River Torrens into Adelaide and North Adelaide.

Culture

Known as the "City of Churches", Adelaide hosts major arts events including the Adelaide Festival, the Fringe, and WOMADelaide. The Barossa Valley and McLaren Vale wine regions lie nearby.
