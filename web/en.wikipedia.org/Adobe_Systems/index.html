Adobe Systems was the former name of Adobe Inc., an American multinational computer software company headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1982 by John Warnock and Charles Geschke, who left Xerox PARC to develop the PostScript page description language, Adobe became a foundational company of desktop publishing and digital media software. The company officially shortened its legal name from Adobe Systems Incorporated to Adobe Inc. in 2018.

History

Adobe's first product, PostScript, was licensed by Apple for the LaserWriter printer in 1985, helping launch the desktop publishing revolution. Adobe Illustrator (1987) and Photoshop (1990, developed by Thomas and John Knoll) followed; the PDF format and Acrobat arrived in 1993. Adobe expanded by acquisition, buying Aldus (PageMaker, After Effects) in 1994 and Macromedia (Flash, Dreamweaver) in 2005.

Business

In 2013 Adobe shifted its creative software to the subscription-based Creative Cloud, a transition that reshaped its revenue model. Its businesses now span digital media (Creative Cloud, Document Cloud) and digital experience (marketing and analytics software, including the Experience Cloud). Adobe is a component of the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100.
