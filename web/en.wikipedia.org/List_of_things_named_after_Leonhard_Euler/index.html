List of things named after Leonhard Euler

[Image: Portrait of Leonhard Euler]
Leonhard Euler (1707–1783), Swiss mathematician and physicist.

The Swiss mathematician Leonhard Euler (1707–1783) made foundational contributions to analysis, number theory, graph theory, mechanics and astronomy. An unusually large number of concepts bear his name; the following is a selection.

Mathematics

  • Euler's number e — the base of the natural logarithm
  • Euler's identity — eiπ + 1 = 0
  • Euler's formula — eix = cos x + i sin x
  • Euler characteristic — V − E + F for polyhedra
  • Eulerian path and Eulerian graph — from the Königsberg bridges problem
  • Euler's totient function φ(n)
  • Euler–Maclaurin formula relating sums and integrals
  • Euler angles describing rotations in three dimensions
  • Euler–Lagrange equation of the calculus of variations
  • Euler's four-square identity and Euler's sum of powers conjecture
  • Euler diagram, Euler line of a triangle, Euler brick
  • Euler–Mascheroni constant γ

Physics and astronomy

  • Euler equations of fluid dynamics and of rigid body rotation
  • Euler force in rotating reference frames
  • Euler's disk, a spinning-disk toy illustrating energy loss
  • Asteroid 2002 Euler and the lunar crater Euler
