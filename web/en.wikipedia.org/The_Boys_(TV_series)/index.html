The Boys is an American superhero television series created by Eric Kripke for Amazon Prime Video. Based on the comic book of the same name by Garth Ennis and Darick Robertson, it follows a group of vigilantes who set out to fight corrupt superpowered individuals ("supes") who abuse their abilities.

Premise

In a world where superheroes are marketed and managed by the powerful corporation Vought International, the most famous team, the Seven, is led by the unstable and violent Homelander. After his girlfriend is killed by a reckless supe, Hughie Campbell joins Billy Butcher and his ragtag team — "the Boys" — in a campaign to expose and bring down Vought and its heroes.

Reception

The series has been acclaimed for its writing, performances, and satirical take on superhero media and celebrity culture, receiving multiple Emmy nominations. A spin-off, Gen V, premiered in 2023.
