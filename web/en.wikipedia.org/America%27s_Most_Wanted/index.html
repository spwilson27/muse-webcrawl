America's Most Wanted
Genre: Reality / crime
Presented by: John Walsh; Elizabeth Vargas
Original network: Fox; Lifetime
Original release: 1988

America's Most Wanted is an American television program whose first run was produced by 20th Television, and is the longest-running program in the history of the Fox network. Hosted by John Walsh, whose son Adam was murdered in 1981, the program profiles fugitives and solicits viewer tips that have led to the capture of more than a thousand fugitives.

History

The show premiered on Fox on February 7, 1988. It was canceled in 2011, briefly continued on Lifetime as America's Most Wanted: America Fights Back, and was revived by Fox in 2021 with Elizabeth Vargas as host, followed by a return of John Walsh with his son Callahan Walsh.

Impact

The program is credited with helping capture numerous fugitives, including several on the FBI Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list, and with popularizing the true-crime reenactment format on American television.
