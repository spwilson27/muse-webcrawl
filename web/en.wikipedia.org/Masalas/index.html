Masala

TypeSpice mix
Place of originSouth Asia
Main ingredientsSpices, herbs

A masala is a mixture of spices and herbs used extensively in South Asian cooking, especially in Indian cuisine. The word comes from the Hindi and Urdu word for spice. Masalas may be dry blends of ground spices or wet pastes made with ingredients such as ginger, garlic, onion, and chillies.

Common blends

Well-known masalas include garam masala, chaat masala, tandoori masala, and sambar powder. Each region and household typically has its own proportions and preferred spices.

Preparation

Spices for a masala are often dry-roasted to release aromatic oils before being ground. Fresh pastes are prepared shortly before cooking to preserve flavour.
