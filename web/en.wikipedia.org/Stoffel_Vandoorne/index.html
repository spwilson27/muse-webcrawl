Stoffel Vandoorne (born 26 March 1992) is a Belgian racing driver who won the 2021–22 Formula E World Championship with Mercedes-EQ and previously raced in Formula One for McLaren from 2016 to 2018. He is also a two-time class competitor at the 24 Hours of Le Mans and has served as a reserve and development driver for several Formula One teams.

Junior career

Vandoorne progressed through karting and single-seaters, winning the F4 Eurocup 1.6 title in 2010 and the Formula Renault 2.0 Eurocup in 2012. As a member of the McLaren Young Driver Programme he finished runner-up in Formula Renault 3.5 in 2013, then dominated the 2015 GP2 Series with ART Grand Prix, breaking records for wins and points — a campaign that marked him as one of the most highly rated juniors of his generation.

Formula One

Vandoorne made his Formula One debut at the 2016 Bahrain Grand Prix, substituting for the injured Fernando Alonso and finishing tenth to score a point. He took a full-time McLaren seat for 2017–2018 alongside Alonso, but the uncompetitive Honda- and Renault-powered cars limited his results; he scored 26 points in 2017 and 12 in 2018, and left the team at the end of 2018.

Formula E and endurance racing

Joining HWA and then the Mercedes-EQ Formula E team, Vandoorne took his first win at Berlin in 2020 and won the 2021–22 World Championship through metronomic consistency. He has also raced in the FIA World Endurance Championship and Super Formula, and won the 2021 24 Hours of Le Mans virtual events circuit prominence as a simulator driver.
