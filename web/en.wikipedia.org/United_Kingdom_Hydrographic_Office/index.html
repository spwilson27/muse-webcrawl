United Kingdom Hydrographic Office

The United Kingdom Hydrographic Office (UKHO) is an executive agency of the Ministry of Defence responsible for surveying and charting the world's oceans. Based in Taunton, Somerset, it produces nautical charts and navigational publications under the Admiralty brand, which are used by the Royal Navy and by merchant mariners around the world.

History

The office was founded in 1795, when the Admiralty appointed Alexander Dalrymple as its first Hydrographer. Under notable successors, including Francis Beaufort (creator of the Beaufort wind scale), the office systematically surveyed coastlines worldwide and published charts that became the global standard. Admiralty charts, tide tables, sailing directions, and lists of lights and radio signals have been produced ever since.

Modern role

Today the UKHO supplies paper and electronic navigational charts (including the Admiralty Vector Chart Service), receives hydrographic data from many national surveys, and supports maritime safety, defence, and the wider "blue economy".
