Apple A11

Designed by: Apple Inc.
Launched: September 2017
Process: 10 nm (TSMC)
Cores: 6 (2 Monsoon + 4 Mistral)

The Apple A11 Bionic is a 64-bit ARM-based system on a chip (SoC) designed by Apple Inc. and manufactured by TSMC using a 10 nm process. It first appeared in the iPhone 8, iPhone 8 Plus and iPhone X, announced in September 2017.

Design

The A11 has a six-core CPU with two high-performance "Monsoon" cores and four energy-efficient "Mistral" cores, all of which can run simultaneously thanks to a second-generation performance controller. It integrates an Apple-designed three-core GPU and, for the first time, a "Neural Engine" — dedicated hardware for machine learning capable of up to 600 billion operations per second, used for Face ID facial recognition and Animoji on the iPhone X.

Performance

Apple claimed the high-performance cores were 25 percent faster than those of the preceding A10, and the efficiency cores 70 percent faster. The chip contains about 4.3 billion transistors.
