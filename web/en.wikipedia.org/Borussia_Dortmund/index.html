Borussia Dortmund

Full name: Ballspielverein Borussia 09 e. V. Dortmund
Founded: 19 December 1909
Ground: Signal Iduna Park (Westfalenstadion)
Colours: Black and yellow
The Südtribüne, the "Yellow Wall", at Signal Iduna Park

Borussia Dortmund, officially Ballspielverein Borussia 09 e. V. Dortmund and commonly shortened to BVB, is a German professional sports club based in Dortmund, North Rhine-Westphalia. It is best known for its men's association football team, which plays in the Bundesliga, the top tier of German football.

Founded in 1909 by eighteen football players from Dortmund, the club has won eight league championships, five DFB-Pokals, and one UEFA Champions League (in 1997), one European Cup Winners' Cup (in 1966, the first German club to win a European title), and one Intercontinental Cup.

Stadium

Dortmund play at Signal Iduna Park, also known as the Westfalenstadion, the largest stadium in Germany with a capacity of over 81,000. Its south stand, the Südtribüne, is the largest free-standing grandstand in Europe and is famous as the "Yellow Wall" (Gelbe Wand) for the massed fans in black and yellow.

Rivalries

The club's main rivalry is with nearby Schalke 04, contested as the Revierderby, and its matches against Bayern Munich are known as Der Klassiker.
