La bohème

La bohème is an opera in four acts by Giacomo Puccini, to a libretto by Luigi Illica and Giuseppe Giacosa, based on Henri Murger's Scènes de la vie de bohème. It premiered in Turin in 1896 under Arturo Toscanini.

Plot

In Paris's Latin Quarter, the poet Rodolfo falls in love with the seamstress Mimì; their happiness is shadowed by poverty and Mimì's consumption, and she dies in the garret surrounded by their bohemian friends Rodolfo, Marcello, Schaunard, Colline and Musetta.

Reception

Initially received coolly, La bohème became one of the most performed operas worldwide, and inspired adaptations including the musical Rent.
