The Battle of Sunda Strait was a night naval battle fought on 28 February – 1 March 1942 in the Sunda Strait between Java and Sumatra, during the Japanese invasion of the Dutch East Indies. The American heavy cruiser USS Houston and the Australian light cruiser HMAS Perth, attempting to escape to the Indian Ocean, blundered into the main Japanese invasion fleet landing at Bantam Bay and were sunk after a fierce close-range fight.

Background

After the Allied defeat in the Battle of the Java Sea, Houston and Perth were ordered to withdraw through the Sunda Strait to Tjilatjap. Unknown to them, a large Japanese convoy carrying the invasion force for western Java was assembling in Bantam Bay that same night, screened by cruisers and destroyers.

The battle

Around 23:30 the two Allied cruisers encountered Japanese destroyers and then the transport fleet itself. In the confused night action they inflicted damage on several transports — the Japanese transport commander General Imamura's ship was sunk — but were overwhelmed by torpedoes and gunfire from the covering force, including the heavy cruisers Mogami and Mikuma. Both Houston and Perth sank in the early hours of 1 March, at a cost of over 1,000 Allied sailors; survivors were taken prisoner.
