Tanggula railway station (唐古拉站) is a station on the Qinghai–Tibet railway in China. At an elevation of 5,068 metres (16,627 ft) above sea level, it is the highest railway station in the world, surpassing Ticlio in Peru. It lies near the Tanggula Pass, the highest point of the line and the highest rail crossing in the world.

Service

The station has three tracks and a platform, but it is primarily a scenic stop: passenger trains pause so travelers can view the Tanggula Mountains, while the station itself has no staff and passengers generally do not disembark for regular service.
