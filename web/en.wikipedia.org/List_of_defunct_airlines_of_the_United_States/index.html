List of defunct airlines of the United States

Hundreds of airlines have operated in the United States since the beginning of commercial aviation. Many disappeared through bankruptcy, merger, or acquisition, particularly after the Airline Deregulation Act of 1978. This list includes notable defunct U.S. airlines.
