List of places in County Antrim

County Antrim is a county in Northern Ireland, containing Belfast in part and the Glens of Antrim along the north coast.

Cities and towns

  • Belfast (partly in County Down)
  • Lisburn (partly in County Down)
  • Antrim
  • Ballymena
  • Ballymoney
  • Carrickfergus
  • Larne
  • Newtownabbey
  • Portrush
  • Ballycastle
  • Cushendall
  • Randalstown

Villages

  • Carnlough, Cullybackey, Dunloy, Glenarm, Kells, Portglenone, Templepatrick and Waterfoot are among the many villages listed.

The county includes Rathlin Island and sites such as the Giant's Causeway area near the border with County Londonderry.
