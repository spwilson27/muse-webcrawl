Corning Incorporated is an American multinational technology company specialising in specialty glass, ceramics and related materials. Headquartered in Corning, New York, it has a history of more than 170 years of applied materials science.

Products and history

Founded in 1851, the company supplied glass for Edison's light bulbs and later developed Pyrex borosilicate glass (1915, brand later licensed), television tubes, and in 1970 the first low-loss optical fibre practical for telecommunications. Its chemically strengthened Gorilla Glass, launched in 2007, became the standard cover glass for smartphones. Corning is also a leading maker of glass substrates for LCD and OLED displays and of ceramic substrates for catalytic converters.

Organisation

The company operates through segments including display technologies, optical communications, specialty materials, environmental technologies and life sciences.
