The Chaos Communication Congress is an annual conference organized by the Chaos Computer Club (CCC), Europe's largest association of hackers. First held in Hamburg in 1984, the congress — known by its sequential designations such as 35C3, 36C3 — is held over four days between Christmas and New Year and is considered the largest hacker gathering in Europe and one of the most influential worldwide.

Programme

The congress features lectures and workshops on technical, scientific, political, and social aspects of computing: computer security research and disclosures, digital civil rights, privacy, freedom of information, science, and art. Talks are streamed and archived online with simultaneous translation. The event is run entirely by volunteers ("angels") and includes elaborate temporary infrastructure — its own telephony (DECT) and network services — plus art installations, hardware hacking areas, and assemblies by affiliated groups.

History

Growing steadily from its 1984 beginnings, the congress moved to Berlin in 1998, returned to Hamburg in 2012, and relocated to Leipzig in 2017 as attendance approached 17,000. The 2020 and 2021 editions were held as the distributed online event "remote Chaos Experience" (rC3) due to the COVID-19 pandemic, before the congress returned in person in Hamburg in 2023 (37C3).
