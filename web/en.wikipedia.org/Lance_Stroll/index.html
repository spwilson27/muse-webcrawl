Lance Stroll

Lance Stroll (born 29 October 1998) is a Canadian racing driver competing in Formula One for Aston Martin. He made his Formula One debut with Williams in 2017, and is the son of businessman Lawrence Stroll, owner of the Aston Martin team.

Early career

Born in Montreal to Lawrence Stroll and Claire-Anne Callens, Stroll rose through karting and was a member of the Ferrari Driver Academy from 2010. He won the Italian F4 Championship in 2014, the Toyota Racing Series in 2015, and the FIA Formula 3 European Championship in 2016 with Prema, earning his move to Formula One.

Formula One career

Stroll joined Williams for 2017, then the second-youngest driver to start a Grand Prix. At the chaotic 2017 Azerbaijan Grand Prix he finished third, becoming the youngest rookie podium finisher in Formula One history at the time at 18 years and 239 days. After two seasons with Williams he moved in 2019 to Racing Point, purchased by his father's consortium; with that team he took his first pole position at the wet 2020 Turkish Grand Prix and further podiums at Monza and Sakhir. The team became Aston Martin in 2021, and Stroll has remained with it since, partnered from 2023 by Fernando Alonso.
