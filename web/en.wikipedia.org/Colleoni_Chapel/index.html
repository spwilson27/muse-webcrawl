Colleoni Chapel

The Colleoni Chapel (Italian: Cappella Colleoni) is a church and mausoleum in Bergamo, Italy, dedicated to Saints Bartholomew, Mark and John the Baptist. It was built between 1472 and 1476 as the funerary monument of the condottiero Bartolomeo Colleoni.

Architecture

Designed by Giovanni Antonio Amadeo, the chapel adjoins the Basilica of Santa Maria Maggiore. Its polychrome marble facade, rose window and sculptural decoration are masterpieces of Lombard Renaissance art. Inside lie the tombs of Colleoni and his daughter Medea, frescoes by Giovanni Battista Tiepolo, and Amadeo's ornate sarcophagi.
