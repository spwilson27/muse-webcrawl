Terence Chi-Shen Tao (born 17 July 1975) is an Australian-American mathematician and a professor of mathematics at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). His research includes harmonic analysis, partial differential equations, combinatorics, number theory, and representation theory.

Early life

A child prodigy, Tao competed in the International Mathematical Olympiad, winning bronze, silver, and gold medals by age 13. He received his PhD from Princeton University at age 20 and became a full professor at UCLA at 24.

Work

With Ben Green, he proved the Green–Tao theorem, showing that the prime numbers contain arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions. He received the Fields Medal in 2006. Tao is also known for work on the Navier–Stokes equations, compressed sensing, and for his widely read mathematics blog.
