Microsoft Flight Simulator is a series of flight simulation video games for Microsoft Windows, developed originally from Bruce Artwick's work at Sublogic and published by Microsoft. It is one of the longest-running and best-known home flight simulator series, notable for its realism and its use as an informal training aid for aviation enthusiasts.

History

The series began in 1982 after Microsoft licensed Artwick's Flight Simulator, itself derived from subLOGIC's 1979 program. Successive versions through the 1980s and 1990s expanded scenery, aircraft and realism, becoming a flagship demonstration of PC capability. Flight Simulator X (2006), by Aces Game Studio, remained widely used for over a decade. After the studio's closure in 2009, the franchise lay dormant until French developer Asobo Studio revived it.

Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020)

The 2020 reboot recreated the entire Earth using Bing Maps satellite imagery, photogrammetry and Azure AI, modelling over two trillion trees and tens of thousands of airports, with real-time weather and air traffic. It was acclaimed as a technical landmark and later released for Xbox Series X/S. A successor, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, followed in November 2024 with a career mode and a slimmer, cloud-streamed client.

Community

A large add-on ecosystem of third-party aircraft, scenery and utilities, along with virtual airlines and networks such as VATSIM, has sustained the series for decades.
