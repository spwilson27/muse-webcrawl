Sounds (magazine)

Sounds was a UK weekly pop/rock music newspaper, published from 10 October 1970 to 6 April 1991. It was one of the "big three" British music weeklies alongside the NME and Melody Maker, and was known in its later years for championing heavy metal, punk, and indie rock.

Founded by Jack Hutton and Peter Wilkinson, who had worked on Melody Maker, Sounds initially focused on progressive rock. It was the first of the British music papers to give substantial coverage to punk rock, and in the late 1970s its writers — including Garry Bushell — championed the Oi! movement. During the 1980s the paper became strongly associated with the New Wave of British Heavy Metal and bands such as Iron Maiden; it was also notable for early coverage of the emerging Madchester and indie scenes, and published the work of writers and photographers including Mick Wall and Ross Halfin.

Closure

Like its rivals, Sounds saw circulation fall in the late 1980s. Its publisher, United Newspapers, closed the paper in April 1991, folding its metal coverage into Kerrang!; several Sounds staff moved to newly launched magazines.
