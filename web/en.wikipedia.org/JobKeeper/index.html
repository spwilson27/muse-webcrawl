CountryAustralia
Announced30 March 2020
Ended28 March 2021
TypeWage subsidy

JobKeeper was an Australian Government wage-subsidy programme introduced during the COVID-19 pandemic. Announced on 30 March 2020, it paid eligible employers a flat A$1,500 per fortnight for each eligible employee — later tapered — so businesses affected by lockdowns could keep staff employed.

Design and scale

Businesses generally had to show a substantial fall in turnover. At its peak the scheme supported around 3.6 million workers and roughly a million businesses, at a total cost of about A$89 billion, making it one of the largest fiscal measures in Australian history. It was administered by the Australian Taxation Office.

Assessment

JobKeeper is credited with sharply limiting unemployment during 2020. It was also criticised because some firms that received payments later reported increased profits, and because certain groups, such as many casual workers and temporary migrants, were excluded.
