Big Tymers

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Big Tymers was an American hip hop duo from New Orleans, Louisiana, consisting of rapper Birdman (Bryan Williams) and producer Mannie Fresh. The duo was a flagship act of Cash Money Records.

Career

Big Tymers released their debut album How You Luv That in 1998, followed by I Got That Work (2000), which produced the single "Get Your Roll On". Their biggest commercial success came with Hood Rich (2002), which debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and included the hit "Still Fly". A final album, Big Money Heavyweight, appeared in 2003 before Mannie Fresh left Cash Money in 2005.

Style

The duo's music exemplified the Cash Money sound of the late 1990s: Mannie Fresh's bouncy, synthesiser-driven production combined with lyrics celebrating wealth and luxury, influential on Southern hip hop of the era.
