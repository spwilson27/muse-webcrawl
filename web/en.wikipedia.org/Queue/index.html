Queue

This is a disambiguation page listing articles associated with the title Queue.

Queue may refer to:

People and lines

A queue, or line, is a sequence of people or vehicles awaiting their turn to be attended to or to proceed. Queueing theory is the mathematical study of waiting lines, used in operations research, telecommunications, and transport planning. "Queueing" is notable as one of the few English words containing five consecutive vowels.

Computing

In computer science, a queue is an abstract data type in which elements are added at the rear (enqueue) and removed from the front (dequeue), following a first-in, first-out (FIFO) principle. Variants include priority queues, circular queues, and double-ended queues (deques). Message queues decouple components in distributed systems.

Other uses

A queue can also refer to a braid of hair worn at the back of the head, famously mandated for men under China's Qing dynasty; a heraldic or anatomical tail; or, in billiards contexts, a misspelling encountered for "cue". In aviation and traffic control, queues describe aircraft or vehicles holding for sequencing.
