Adulthood

Adulthood is the period in the human lifespan in which full physical and intellectual maturity has been attained. Adulthood is commonly thought of as beginning at age 18 or 21 years. Middle age begins around 40 or 45, and old age at 60 or 65, though these boundaries vary across cultures and historical periods.

Preceded by: Adolescence

Biological adulthood

Biological adulthood is reached when growth is complete and reproductive maturity attained, typically by the late teens. Brain development, particularly of the prefrontal cortex, continues into the mid-twenties.

Legal adulthood

The age of majority is the threshold of adulthood as recognized in law, conferring rights such as voting, contracting, and marrying without parental consent. In most jurisdictions it is 18.

Social and psychological views

Developmental psychologists such as Erik Erikson described adulthood through stages of intimacy, generativity, and integrity. Sociologists note that traditional markers — leaving home, finishing education, financial independence, marriage, and parenthood — are increasingly delayed in industrialized societies, giving rise to the concept of "emerging adulthood".
