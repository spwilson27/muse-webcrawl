Nelvana

Nelvana Limited is a Canadian animation studio and entertainment company founded in Toronto in 1971 by Michael Hirsh, Patrick Loubert, and Clive A. Smith. Its name and polar-bear logo were inspired by Nelvana of the Northern Lights, a 1940s Canadian comic-book superheroine.

History

The studio first gained attention with television specials such as The Devil and Daniel Mouse (1978) and produced animated segments for the 1981 film Heavy Metal. It became a major producer of children's television, animating series including Inspector Gadget (with DIC), The Care Bears franchise, Babar, Rupert, Franklin, Rolie Polie Olie, Beyblade, Bakugan, Little Charmers, and The Dog & Pony Show. Nelvana also produced the first animated Star Wars series, Droids and Ewoks, and the animated feature Rock & Rule (1983).

Ownership

Nelvana was acquired by Corus Entertainment in 2000 for roughly C$540 million and remains a division of Corus, distributing and licensing its catalogue internationally.
