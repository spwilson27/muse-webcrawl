DowDuPont


DowDuPont existed from 2017 to 2019 before splitting into three companies.

DowDuPont Inc. was an American conglomerate formed on August 31, 2017 by the merger of Dow Chemical and DuPont in a deal valued at roughly $130 billion. Headquartered in Midland, Michigan and Wilmington, Delaware, it was briefly the world's largest chemical company.

Formation and breakup

The "merger of equals," announced in December 2015 under activist-investor pressure, was structured as a prelude to a three-way split. The combined company organised its operations into agriculture, materials science, and specialty products divisions. On April 1, 2019, the materials science division spun off as the new Dow Inc.; on June 1, 2019, the agriculture division became Corteva, and the remaining specialty-products business adopted the name DuPont de Nemours — completing the dissolution of DowDuPont as planned.

Legacy

The transaction reshaped the global chemical and seed industries, combining major brands such as Pioneer seeds with Dow's plastics and DuPont's performance materials. It also drew antitrust scrutiny in the United States and Europe, prompting divestitures before approval.
