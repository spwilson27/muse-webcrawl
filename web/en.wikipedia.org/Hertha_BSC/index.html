Hertha BSC

Hertha BSC (Hertha Berliner Sport-Club) is a German professional football club based in Berlin, founded on 25 July 1892. The club is nicknamed Die Alte Dame ("The Old Lady") and plays its home matches at the Olympiastadion, its home since 1963. Its name derives from a steamship with a blue-and-white smokestack on which one of the founders had sailed.

History

Hertha was a founding member of the German Football Association in 1900 and enjoyed its greatest success in the late Weimar era, winning German championships in 1930 and 1931. After the Second World War the club, based in West Berlin, was a founding member of the Bundesliga in 1963. Hertha has since oscillated between the first and second divisions, winning the DFB-Ligapokal in 2001 and 2002 and reaching the UEFA Champions League group stage in 1999–2000.

Rivalries

Hertha's main local rival is 1. FC Union Berlin, based in the former East Berlin; meetings between the two are contested as the Berlin derby. See also football in Berlin.
