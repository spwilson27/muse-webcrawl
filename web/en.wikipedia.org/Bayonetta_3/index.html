Bayonetta 3

Bayonetta 3 is a 2022 action-adventure game developed by PlatinumGames and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. It is the third entry in the Bayonetta series.

Gameplay and reception

The story introduces a multiverse plot in which Bayonetta teams with the new playable character Viola to fight the Homunculi and the entity Singularity. New mechanics include Demon Slave, allowing Bayonetta to directly control her Infernal Demons in battle. The game received generally positive reviews, praised for its combat variety, though some criticism was directed at its story, performance and camera.
