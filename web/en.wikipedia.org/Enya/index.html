Eithne Pádraigín Ní Bhraonáin (born 17 May 1961; anglicised as Enya Patricia Brennan), known mononymously as Enya, is an Irish composer and singer-songwriter. Enya is the best-selling Irish solo artist, and the second-best-selling music act from Ireland overall after the rock band U2. Her music has been widely categorised as new-age, Celtic and pop, and she has sold more than 80 million records worldwide.

Early life and Clannad

Born into a musical family in the Gaeltacht of Gweedore, County Donegal, Enya joined her family's band Clannad in 1980, playing keyboards and singing backing vocals. She left in 1982 to work with producer Nicky Ryan and his wife, lyricist Roma Ryan — the partnership that has defined her solo career ever since.

Solo career

After soundtrack work including the BBC series The Celts (1987), Enya's breakthrough album Watermark (1988) produced the international hit "Orinoco Flow", noted for its layered vocals and pizzicato strings. Shepherd Moons (1991), The Memory of Trees (1995) and A Day Without Rain (2000) followed; the last included "Only Time", which became an anthem after the September 11 attacks, and title material associated with The Lord of the Rings film "May It Be" earned an Academy Award nomination. Later albums include Amarantine (2005), And Winter Came... (2008) and Dark Sky Island (2015).

Musical style

Enya's sound is built from hundreds of overdubbed vocal layers recorded by Enya herself, synthesisers, and lyrics by Roma Ryan in English, Irish, Latin, Welsh and invented languages such as "Loxian". She rarely tours or performs live, preferring studio work, and has famously maintained a private life at Manderley Castle in Killiney, County Dublin.

Awards

Enya has won four Grammy Awards for Best New Age Album, an Ivor Novello Award, and numerous World Music Awards, and was named one of the best-selling female artists of all time.
