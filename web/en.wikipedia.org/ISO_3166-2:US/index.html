ISO 3166-2:US

ISO 3166-2:US is the entry for the United States in ISO 3166-2, part of the ISO 3166 standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), which defines codes for the names of the principal subdivisions (e.g., provinces or states) of all countries coded in ISO 3166-1.

Currently for the United States, ISO 3166-2 codes are defined for the following subdivisions:

  • 50 states
  • 1 district (the District of Columbia)
  • 6 outlying areas (American Samoa, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, Puerto Rico, the United States Minor Outlying Islands and the U.S. Virgin Islands)

Each code consists of two parts separated by a hyphen. The first part is US, the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the United States. The second part is two letters, which is the postal abbreviation used by the United States Postal Service — for example, US-CA for California and US-TX for Texas. The codes for the outlying areas are also their own ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes in their country entries.

Relationship to other codes

The ISO subdivision abbreviations coincide with the familiar USPS state abbreviations and descend from the same local system, replacing the numeric codes of the former FIPS PUB 5-2 standard, which was withdrawn in 2008. The United States Board on Geographic Names and ANSI maintain related domestic code lists (ANSI INCITS 38).
