Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur (commonly abbreviated to PACA), also known as Région Sud, is one of the eighteen administrative regions of France, located on the south-eastern coast of the country, bordering Italy and the Mediterranean Sea. Its prefecture and largest city is Marseille, France's second-largest city.

Geography

The region comprises the six departments of Alpes-de-Haute-Provence, Alpes-Maritimes, Bouches-du-Rhône, Hautes-Alpes, Var, and Vaucluse. It includes the French Riviera (Côte d'Azur), the southern French Alps, the Camargue wetlands, and the lavender-growing plateaus of historical Provence.

Tourism

PACA is one of France's most visited regions, known for destinations such as Nice, Cannes, Antibes, Saint-Tropez, Aix-en-Provence, and Avignon, as well as the Calanques National Park and the ski resorts of the southern Alps.
