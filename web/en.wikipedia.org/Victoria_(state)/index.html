Victoria is a state in southeastern Australia. It is the second-smallest state by land area but the second-most populous and the most densely populated. Its capital and largest city is Melbourne, home to roughly three-quarters of the state's population. Victoria is bordered by New South Wales to the north, South Australia to the west, Tasmania across Bass Strait to the south, and the Tasman Sea to the east.

History

European settlement began in the 1830s, and Victoria separated from New South Wales in 1851, the same year gold was discovered — see the Victorian gold rush — transforming the colony's population and economy. Victoria joined the Commonwealth of Australia at federation in 1901, and Melbourne served as the temporary national capital until 1927.

Economy

Victoria has a diversified economy with strong finance, professional services, health care, education, manufacturing, and agriculture sectors; it is a major producer of dairy products and hosts Australia's largest container port at Melbourne.
