Madame

Madame is an honorific title given to a woman, equivalent in usage to "ma'am" and derived from the French madame (pronounced [madam]), itself from ma dame meaning "my lady". In English the word is used both as a formal style of address and, less formally, as a general term for a woman, and its abbreviations and formal conventions differ between French and English usage.

Usage in French

In French, Madame (abbreviated Mme) is the traditional title for a married woman, and historically for older women generally. Since reforms in French administrative practice, marital status is generally not marked in official contexts, and Madame is now the default form of address for adult women regardless of marital status, paralleling Monsieur for men.

Usage in English

In English, "Madam" appears in formal salutations such as "Dear Madam or Sir", in services and retail as a polite address, and in styles such as "Madam President" or "Madam Secretary". The French spelling Madame is also used, sometimes with connotations of refinement or foreignness, and historically the word acquired an additional sense as a term for a woman who runs a brothel.
