Christmas Spectacular

The Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes is an annual musical holiday stage show presented at Radio City Music Hall in New York City. The 90-minute show features more than 140 performers and combines singing, dancing, and humor with traditional scenes, performed by the women's precision dance company the Rockettes.

The Rockettes in the "Parade of the Wooden Soldiers," performed in the show since 1933

History

The Christmas Spectacular debuted in 1933, shortly after Radio City Music Hall opened, and has been staged annually since, with interruptions only in exceptional years. Originally a short program presented between film screenings, it grew into a full stage production and one of New York's signature holiday attractions, seen by millions of visitors.

Production numbers

Signature scenes include the "Parade of the Wooden Soldiers," with choreography unchanged since the show's first edition; the "Living Nativity," with live camels, sheep, and donkeys; "New York at Christmas"; and the "12 Days of Christmas" tap number. In recent productions the show has incorporated large-scale projection mapping, LED scenery, and drone effects while retaining its classic numbers. The score mixes original music with Christmas standards such as those arranged from Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker.
