Interleukin 7

Interleukin 7 (IL-7) is a cytokine secreted by stromal cells in the bone marrow and thymus that is essential for the development and survival of lymphocytes. It signals through the IL-7 receptor, composed of the IL-7 receptor alpha chain and the common gamma chain.

Function

IL-7 supports the survival of naive and memory T cells and drives the early development of B and T cell precursors. In mice it is indispensable for B cell development; in humans its role is most critical for T cells.

Clinical significance

Defects in IL-7 signalling cause severe combined immunodeficiency. Recombinant IL-7 has been investigated as a therapy to restore T cell counts in lymphopenic patients and as an adjunct in cancer and infectious disease.
