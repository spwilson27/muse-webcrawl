Jacksonville, North Carolina

Jacksonville is a city in Onslow County, North Carolina, United States, and its county seat. It is the home of Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune and Marine Corps Air Station New River, and its population is among the youngest of any American city because of the military presence.

History

Jacksonville grew slowly as a centre for tobacco, timber and naval stores on the New River until 1941, when the United States Marine Corps established Camp Lejeune nearby. The base transformed the town into a fast-growing military city during the Second World War.

Geography and demography

The city lies on the Atlantic coastal plain along the New River estuary. Its economy is dominated by the Marine Corps installations, retail and services supporting military families, and it consistently records one of the lowest median ages in the United States.
