NBCUniversal Media, LLC (branded as NBCUniversal) is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate owned by Comcast. Headquartered at 30 Rockefeller Plaza in Midtown Manhattan, New York City, the company is named for its two most significant divisions, the National Broadcasting Company (NBC) — one of the "Big Three" American television networks — and the film studio Universal Pictures.

History

NBC Universal, Inc. was formed in May 2004 by the merger of General Electric's NBC broadcasting properties with Vivendi Universal Entertainment, the film and television arm of Vivendi Universal. GE retained an 80% stake. In December 2009, Comcast announced it would acquire a controlling 51% interest, a deal completed in January 2011. In March 2013 Comcast bought out GE's remaining 49% for $16.7 billion, giving it full ownership.

Operations

The company's television assets include the NBC broadcast network, cable channels such as USA Network, Bravo, E!, Syfy, MSNBC, CNBC, and the Telemundo Spanish-language network, along with the streaming service Peacock, launched in July 2020. Its film arm, Universal Pictures, includes Illumination, DreamWorks Animation, and Focus Features. Universal Destinations & Experiences operates theme parks in Orlando, Hollywood, Japan, Singapore, and Beijing.

In 2024–2025 Comcast moved to spin off most of NBCUniversal's cable networks into a separate publicly traded company, Versant, while retaining NBC, Bravo, Peacock, and the film and theme-park businesses.
