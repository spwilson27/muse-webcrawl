Progressive aspect

The progressive aspect (or continuous aspect) is a grammatical aspect expressing an action in progress at a reference time. In English it is formed with the auxiliary be plus the present participle: "she is reading."

Usage

Combined with tense it yields present progressive, past progressive ("was reading"), and future progressive ("will be reading"), and with perfect aspect the perfect progressives ("has been reading"). It can mark ongoing, temporary, repeated (with irritation, "he's always complaining"), or planned future events. Many languages express progression differently — Spanish uses estar + gerund; others lack a distinct progressive. Stative verbs traditionally resist the progressive, though usage is extending ("I'm loving it").
