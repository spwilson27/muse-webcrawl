Maternal health

FieldPublic health, obstetrics
FocusHealth of women during pregnancy, childbirth and postpartum

Maternal health refers to the health of women during pregnancy, childbirth, and the postnatal period. It encompasses family planning, antenatal care, skilled attendance at delivery, emergency obstetric care, and postnatal services.

Global burden

According to the World Health Organization, roughly 800 women die each day from preventable causes related to pregnancy and childbirth, overwhelmingly in low-income settings. Leading causes include haemorrhage, infection, high blood pressure, and unsafe abortion.

Progress

Global maternal mortality fell substantially between 1990 and 2015, and reducing it further is part of Sustainable Development Goal 3.
