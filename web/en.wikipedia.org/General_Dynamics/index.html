General Dynamics Corporation (GD) is an American publicly traded aircraft and defence corporation headquartered in Reston, Virginia. As of 2020, it was the third-largest defence contractor in the world by arms sales, and sixth largest in the United States by total sales. The company is a Fortune 100 company, and was ranked No. 90 in the 2023 rankings.

History

General Dynamics was formed in 1952 from the Electric Boat Company, a submarine builder founded in 1899, and Canadair. Its Convair division built the F-102 and F-106 interceptors and the Atlas missile family; its Fort Worth division developed the F-111 Aardvark and the F-16 Fighting Falcon (the F-16 programme was sold to Lockheed in 1993). Through acquisitions in the 1990s and 2000s it added armoured vehicles (the M1 Abrams tank, via Chrysler Defense), shipbuilding (Bath Iron Works and NASSCO), Gulfstream Aerospace business jets (1999), ordnance, and large IT services businesses, including CSRA in 2018.

Business segments

The company's operations are grouped into Aerospace (Gulfstream jets and Jet Aviation services), Marine Systems (Electric Boat submarines, Bath Iron Works destroyers, NASSCO), Combat Systems (tanks, armoured vehicles and munitions in the US and Europe), and Technologies (IT services, mission systems and communications products).
