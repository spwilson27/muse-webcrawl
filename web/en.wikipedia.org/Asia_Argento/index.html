Asia Argento (born September 20, 1975) is an Italian actress and filmmaker. The daughter of director Dario Argento and actress Daria Nicolodi, she began acting as a child and became a prominent figure in Italian and international cinema, known for roles in Trauma (1993), The Phantom of the Opera (1998), xXx (2002) and Marie Antoinette (2006).

Career

Argento won the David di Donatello Award for Best Actress twice, for Let's Not Keep in Touch (1994) and Travelling Companion (1996). She moved into directing with Scarlet Diva (2000) and The Heart Is Deceitful Above All Things (2004), and later directed Misunderstood (2014). She has also worked as a model, musician and television personality, serving as a judge on the Italian edition of X Factor.

Public advocacy

In 2017 Argento was among the first women to publicly accuse film producer Harvey Weinstein of sexual assault in reporting by The New Yorker, becoming a prominent voice of the #MeToo movement in Italy and internationally.
