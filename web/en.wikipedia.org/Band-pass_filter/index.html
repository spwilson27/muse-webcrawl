Band-pass filter

A band-pass filter or bandpass filter (BPF) is a device that passes frequencies within a certain range and rejects (attenuates) frequencies outside that range. It is the combination of a high-pass filter and a low-pass filter.

Description

Band-pass filters are characterised by their centre frequency, bandwidth, and Q factor. In signal processing they are used to isolate a particular frequency band, for example a single radio channel in a receiver.

Applications

Applications include radio and telecommunications equipment, audio signal processing (equalisers and crossover networks), optics (interference filters passing a band of wavelengths), and atmospheric and astronomical instruments.

Types

Passive filters use resistors, capacitors and inductors; active filters add amplifiers such as operational amplifiers. Digital band-pass filters are implemented in software via FIR or IIR designs.
