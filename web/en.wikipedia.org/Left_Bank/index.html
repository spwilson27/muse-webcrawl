Left Bank

The Left Bank (French: Rive Gauche) is the southern bank of the River Seine in Paris, France — the side to your left as you face downstream.

Character

The Left Bank is traditionally associated with artists, writers, students and intellectual life, centred on the Latin Quarter, the Sorbonne, Saint-Germain-des-Prés and Montparnasse. It comprises the 5th, 6th, 7th, 13th, 14th and 15th arrondissements, in whole or in part, and includes the Eiffel Tower and Les Invalides.

Contrast

It is conventionally contrasted with the more commercial Right Bank (Rive Droite) north of the river.
