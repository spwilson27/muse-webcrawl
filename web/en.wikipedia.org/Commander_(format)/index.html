Commander (format)

Commander is a multiplayer format for the trading card game Magic: The Gathering, also known as Elder Dragon Highlander (EDH). Created by players in Alaska and popularised by judge Sheldon Menery, it was adopted as an official format by Wizards of the Coast in 2011 and has become one of Magic's most popular ways to play.

Rules

Each player builds a 100-card singleton deck (no duplicates except basic lands) around a legendary creature — the commander — whose colour identity determines which cards may be included. The commander starts in the command zone and can be recast with an additional cost (the 'commander tax') each time. Players start with 40 life; a player dealt 21 combat damage by a single commander loses. Games are typically free-for-all with three or more players, emphasising social play and politics.

Governance

Commander was long overseen by an independent Rules Committee maintaining a ban list and philosophy document; in 2024 management transferred to Wizards of the Coast following controversy, with a bracket system introduced to communicate deck power levels.

Products

Wizards publishes preconstructed Commander decks alongside most sets, plus dedicated sets such as Commander Legends. Variants include Two-Headed Giant, Oathbreaker, and Canadian Highlander.
