Sport in Spain

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Football is Spain's most popular sport

Sport in Spain is dominated by association football, with basketball a strong second. Spain is regarded as one of the world's leading sporting nations, producing world-class competitors in football, tennis, cycling, motorsport, basketball, golf and handball.

Football

Football is the most popular spectator and participation sport. La Liga is among Europe's top leagues, home to Real Madrid and FC Barcelona, whose rivalry — El Clásico — is watched worldwide. The Spain national team won the FIFA World Cup in 2010 and the UEFA European Championship in 1964, 2008, 2012 and 2024.

Other sports

Basketball's Liga ACB is one of Europe's strongest, and Spain's men's national team has won the FIBA World Cup twice. In tennis, Rafael Nadal is one of the most successful players in history. Cycling centres on the Vuelta a España, one of the three Grand Tours; Spanish riders include Miguel Indurain and Alberto Contador. Spain has produced Formula One champion Fernando Alonso and multiple MotoGP champions. Traditional sports include pelota in the Basque Country and bullfighting, a controversial spectacle with deep historical roots.

Major events

Spain hosted the 1992 Summer Olympics in Barcelona and the 1982 FIFA World Cup. Sport is administered nationally through the Consejo Superior de Deportes and the Spanish Olympic Committee.
