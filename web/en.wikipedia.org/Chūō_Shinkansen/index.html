Chūō Shinkansen

Type: Maglev high-speed railway

Route: Tokyo–Nagoya, planned to Osaka

Operator: JR Central

The Chūō Shinkansen is a magnetic-levitation railway under construction in Japan, designed to connect Tokyo with Nagoya and ultimately Osaka at commercial speeds of up to 500 km/h. It is being built by Central Japan Railway Company using superconducting maglev technology tested for decades on the Yamanashi test line.

Project

The largely tunnelled route through the Japanese Alps will shorten Tokyo–Nagoya travel to about 40 minutes. Deep underground stations are planned at Shinagawa and Nagoya, with intermediate stations in several prefectures.

Controversy and delay

Disputes over water flows in Shizuoka Prefecture, environmental impact, cost, and demand have repeatedly delayed the opening originally targeted for 2027. The line is intended both as a faster alternative and as insurance against disruption of the coastal Tōkaidō Shinkansen.
