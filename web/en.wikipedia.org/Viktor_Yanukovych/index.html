Viktor Fedorovych Yanukovych (born 9 July 1950) is a Ukrainian former politician who served as the fourth president of Ukraine from 2010 until his removal in February 2014 following the Euromaidan protests. He had previously served as prime minister under President Leonid Kuchma and again under President Viktor Yushchenko.

Early life and career

Yanukovych was born in the mining town of Yenakiieve in Donetsk Oblast. Orphaned at a young age, he worked as an electrician and mechanic before rising through transport management. He entered politics in the 1990s and was appointed governor of Donetsk Oblast in 1997, a post he held until becoming prime minister in 2002.

2004 presidential election

Yanukovych was the pro-government candidate in the disputed 2004 presidential election. The initial runoff result, which declared him the winner over Viktor Yushchenko, was annulled by the Supreme Court of Ukraine amid mass protests known as the Orange Revolution, and Yushchenko won the re-run vote.

Presidency (2010–2014)

Yanukovych won the 2010 presidential election against Yulia Tymoshenko. His administration negotiated with both Russia and the European Union; his decision in November 2013 to suspend an association agreement with the EU triggered the Euromaidan protests. After violence in Kyiv in February 2014, Yanukovych fled the capital and then the country, and the Verkhovna Rada voted to remove him from office. He subsequently lived in Russia and was convicted of treason in absentia by a Ukrainian court in 2019.
