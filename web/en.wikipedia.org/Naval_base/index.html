Naval base

A naval base, or navy base, is a military base where warships and other naval vessels are based, serviced and supplied when they are not at sea or in need of major repair. Naval bases typically include piers and docks, dry docks, fuel and ammunition depots, workshops, command facilities and accommodation for crews and support personnel.

Functions

Naval bases provide berthing, maintenance, refuelling, rearming, crew rest and training. Major bases also host fleet headquarters, shipyards and schools. The strategic value of a naval base lies in its location, which allows a navy to project power and sustain operations in nearby waters.

Examples

Well-known naval bases include Naval Station Norfolk in the United States, Portsmouth in the United Kingdom, and Toulon in France.
