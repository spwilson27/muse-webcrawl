Bar (unit)

The bar is a metric unit of pressure defined as 100,000 pascals (100 kPa), or 100 kilonewtons per square metre. It is approximately equal to the atmospheric pressure on Earth at sea level (1.013 bar). The bar and the millibar were introduced by the Norwegian meteorologist Vilhelm Bjerknes.

Use

The millibar (mbar) is widely used in meteorology, although the SI-preferred hectopascal (hPa) is numerically identical and increasingly used in weather reports. The bar is common in industry, diving (as a gauge of water pressure), and in describing the pressure in engines and espresso machines. Although not an SI unit, the bar is accepted for use with the SI in some fields.
