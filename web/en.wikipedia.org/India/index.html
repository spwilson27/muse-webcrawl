India, officially the Republic of India, is a country in South Asia. It is the seventh-largest country by area; the most populous country as of June 2023; and since its independence in 1947, the world's most populous democracy. Bounded by the Indian Ocean on the south, the Arabian Sea on the southwest, and the Bay of Bengal on the southeast, it shares land borders with Pakistan to the west; China, Nepal, and Bhutan to the north; and Bangladesh and Myanmar to the east.

History

Modern humans arrived on the Indian subcontinent from Africa no later than 55,000 years ago. The Indus Valley Civilisation, which flourished in the northwestern regions from about 3300 to 1300 BCE, was one of the world's earliest urban cultures. The Vedic period that followed saw the composition of the Vedas and the emergence of the caste system. By the 3rd century BCE, much of South Asia was united under the Maurya Empire of Ashoka; later major powers included the Gupta Empire (the "Golden Age"), the Delhi Sultanate, the Vijayanagara Empire, and the Mughal Empire.

From the early 18th century, the British East India Company gradually annexed India, which became a British colony. A nationalist independence movement, led by Mahatma Gandhi with nonviolent resistance, achieved independence on 15 August 1947, accompanied by the partition into India and Pakistan. India adopted a republican constitution on 26 January 1950.

Geography

India's geography ranges from the Himalayan mountains in the north (including Kangchenjunga, the world's third-highest peak) through the fertile Indo-Gangetic Plain to the Deccan Plateau and the coasts of the peninsula. Major rivers include the Ganges, Brahmaputra, Yamuna, Godavari, and Krishna. The climate is dominated by the southwest monsoon.

Government and politics

India is a federal union of 28 states and 8 union territories, governed as a parliamentary republic. The President is head of state; the Prime Minister heads the government. Parliament consists of the Rajya Sabha (Council of States) and Lok Sabha (House of the People). India has a common-law legal system and an independent judiciary headed by the Supreme Court.

Economy

India has one of the world's largest economies by nominal GDP and the third largest by purchasing power parity. Major sectors include services (notably information technology), agriculture, which employs the largest share of the workforce, and manufacturing. India is a member of the G20, BRICS, and the United Nations.

Demographics and culture

India is the birthplace of four major world religions — Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism — and is home to large populations of Muslims, Christians, and others. Hindi is the most widely spoken language, among many from the Indo-Aryan and Dravidian families. Indian culture is marked by its cinema (including Bollywood), classical music and dance traditions, cuisine, festivals such as Diwali, Holi, and Eid, and the sport of cricket.
