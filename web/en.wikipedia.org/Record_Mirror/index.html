Record Mirror

Record Mirror was a British weekly music newspaper published between 1954 and 1991. Alongside the NME, Melody Maker and Sounds, it was one of the UK's principal pop music papers, known for its charts, interviews and coverage of soul, disco and dance music.

History

Founded by Isadore Green, the paper first appeared in June 1954. It was an early home of British pop journalism and in 1962 began compiling a chart used alongside the Record Retailer listings. In the 1980s, under editors including Eleanor Levy, it championed club culture and published the influential Hi-NRG and disco charts compiled by James Hamilton, whose "Disco" column ran for many years.

Closure

Falling circulation led United Newspapers to close Record Mirror in April 1991, merging it into Music Week as a dance supplement. Its charts archive remains a key source for British pop history.
