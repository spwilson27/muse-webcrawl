Hamilton (Māori: Kirikiriroa) is a city in the Waikato region of New Zealand's North Island, situated on the banks of the Waikato River. It is one of the country's largest and fastest-growing cities and serves as a hub for the surrounding dairy farming region.

History

The area was originally the Māori settlement of Kirikiriroa. The present city was established in 1864 as a military settlement during the New Zealand Wars and named after Captain John Fane Charles Hamilton, a Royal Navy officer killed at the Battle of Gate Pā. Hamilton grew as a service centre for agriculture and was proclaimed a city in 1945.

Attractions and institutions

Hamilton Gardens, a collection of themed gardens on the riverbank, is the city's most visited attraction. The University of Waikato, founded in 1964, is a major institution. The city hosts events such as the National Agricultural Fieldays at Mystery Creek, and its teams include the Chiefs (rugby union) and Northern Districts (cricket).
