Adverb

An adverb is a word or phrase that modifies a verb, adjective, another adverb, a clause or a sentence, typically expressing manner, place, time, frequency, degree or certainty.

English adverbs

Many English adverbs are formed from adjectives with the suffix -ly (quickly, carefully), but others are flat adverbs (fast, hard) or simple forms such as now, here, very and often. Adverbs do not modify nouns, which distinguishes them from adjectives.

Functions

Adverbs can act as adjuncts, disjuncts or conjuncts, and whole clauses can serve adverbial functions. See also Adjunct (grammar), Adjective and English grammar.
