Kempton Park Racecourse is a horse racing venue in Sunbury-on-Thames, Surrey, England, close to the boundary with London. Opened in 1878, it stages both jump racing and all-weather flat racing on its floodlit Polytrack circuit.

Notable races

Kempton's most famous race is the King George VI Chase, run over three miles on Boxing Day (26 December) and one of the most prestigious steeplechases in Britain; the course also hosts the Christmas Hurdle and the Kauto Star Novices' Chase at the same meeting. In 2006 the traditional turf flat course was replaced by an all-weather track, making Kempton a major floodlit evening venue.
