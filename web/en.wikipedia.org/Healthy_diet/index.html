Healthy diet

A healthy diet is a diet that maintains or improves overall health. It provides the body with essential nutrition: fluid, macronutrients such as protein, micronutrients such as vitamins, and adequate fibre and food energy.

Recommendations

A healthy diet may contain fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, and may include little to no ultra-processed food or sweetened beverages. The World Health Organization recommends that free sugars contribute less than 10% of total energy intake, that fats contribute less than 30%, and that adults consume at least 400 grams of fruit and vegetables per day.

Dietary guidelines vary by country and culture, but common advice includes eating a variety of foods, basing meals on starchy wholegrain carbohydrates, consuming several portions of fruit and vegetables daily, eating some protein-rich foods, choosing unsaturated oils, and limiting salt, saturated fat and added sugar. A healthy diet, together with physical activity, reduces the risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, obesity and some cancers.
