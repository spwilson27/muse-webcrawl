Liquid

A liquid is a nearly incompressible fluid that conforms to the shape of its container but retains a constant volume independent of pressure. It is one of the four fundamental states of matter, with particles close together but free to move past one another.

Water is the most common liquid on Earth. Liquids have properties including surface tension, viscosity, capillary action and vapour pressure. Only two elements, mercury and bromine, are liquid at standard temperature and pressure, though several others melt just above room temperature.
