List of adaptations of works by Stephen King

Stephen King is an American author of horror, supernatural fiction, suspense, crime, science fiction, and fantasy. His books have sold more than 400 million copies, and many have been adapted into films, television series, miniseries, and stage productions. The following is a list of notable screen adaptations.
