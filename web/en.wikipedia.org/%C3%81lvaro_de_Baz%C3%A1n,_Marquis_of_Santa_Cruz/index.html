Álvaro de Bazán y Guzmán, 1st Marquis of Santa Cruz (12 December 1526 – 9 February 1588) was a Spanish admiral. He was never defeated in a lifetime of naval commands spanning more than four decades, and is remembered as one of the most successful naval commanders in history.

Career

Born in Granada into a naval family of Basque origin, Bazán went to sea young and commanded squadrons against the Barbary corsairs. He played a decisive part at the Battle of Lepanto in 1571, leading the reserve division. In 1582 he destroyed a French fleet at the Battle of Ponta Delgada in the Azores, securing Spanish control of the islands during the Portuguese succession crisis.

Spanish Armada

Bazán drew up plans for an invasion fleet against England and was appointed to command it, but he died in Lisbon in February 1588 while the Armada was still being prepared. His death deprived Spain of its most experienced admiral; command passed to the Duke of Medina Sidonia.
