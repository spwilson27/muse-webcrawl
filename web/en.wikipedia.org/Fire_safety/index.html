Fire safety

Fire safety refers to the set of practices intended to reduce the destruction caused by fire: preventing ignition, limiting spread, detecting fires early and enabling occupants to escape. It is a core duty for building owners, employers and householders.

Key measures

Common measures include working smoke alarms, safe storage of flammables, electrical safety, clear escape routes, fire extinguishers suited to the fire class present, and regular evacuation drills. In the home, kitchen fires and unattended candles are leading causes of incidents.

Legislation

Regimes such as the UK's Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 place a duty on a "responsible person" to carry out fire risk assessments, while US requirements are set through NFPA 101 (Life Safety Code) and local fire codes.
