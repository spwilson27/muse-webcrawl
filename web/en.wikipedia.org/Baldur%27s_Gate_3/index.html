Baldur's Gate 3

Baldur's Gate 3 is a role-playing video game developed and published by Larian Studios, released in full in August 2023 after an early access period beginning in 2020. The third main installment in the Baldur's Gate series, it is based on the Dungeons & Dragons 5th edition ruleset and set in the Forgotten Realms.

Story and gameplay

The player character, infected with a mind flayer tadpole, journeys across Faerûn seeking a cure while gathering companions — including Astarion, Shadowheart, Gale, and Lae'zel. The game features turn-based combat, extensive branching dialogue, cooperative multiplayer, and highly reactive systems-driven encounters.

Reception

Baldur's Gate 3 received universal acclaim and won numerous Game of the Year awards, including at The Game Awards 2023, the Hugo Award for Best Game, and the BAFTA Games Award for Best Game.
