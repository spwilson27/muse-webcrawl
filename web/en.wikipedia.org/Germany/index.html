Bundesrepublik Deutschland

Official language: German

Currency: Euro (€)

Germany, officially the Federal Republic of Germany, is a country in Central Europe. It lies between the Baltic and North Seas to the north and the Alps to the south. With about 84 million inhabitants, it is the most populous member state of the European Union.

History

Germanic peoples inhabited the region in antiquity. Much of it formed part of the Holy Roman Empire (962–1806). A unified German Empire was proclaimed in 1871, followed by the Weimar Republic, Nazi Germany, division into West and East Germany after World War II, and reunification in 1990. See Hohenstaufen and French Revolution.

Geography

Germany borders Denmark, Poland, Czechia, Austria, Switzerland, France, Luxembourg, Belgium and the Netherlands. Major rivers include the Rhine, Danube and Elbe.

Economy

Germany has Europe's largest economy, known for automobiles, machinery, chemicals and engineering, a strong Mittelstand of medium firms, and exports.

Culture

Germany has made major contributions to philosophy, music (Bach, Beethoven), literature, science and film, and is known for its federal states' regional traditions.
