List of Pokémon

The Pokémon franchise, created by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak and Nintendo, features fictional creatures called Pokémon. Species are introduced in generations corresponding to the core video games; the first generation (1996) had 151 species, and the total passed 1,000 with the ninth generation (2022).

Generations

  • Generation I (Kanto, 151): Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Pikachu, Mewtwo, Mew.
  • Generation II (Johto, 100; total 251): Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile, Lugia, Ho-Oh.
  • Generation III (Hoenn, 135; total 386): Treecko, Torchic, Mudkip, Rayquaza.
  • Generation IV (Sinnoh, 107; total 493): Turtwig, Chimchar, Piplup, Dialga, Palkia, Arceus.
  • Generation V (Unova, 156; total 649): Snivy, Tepig, Oshawott, Zekrom, Reshiram.
  • Generation VI (Kalos, 72; total 721): introduced Mega Evolution and the Fairy type.
  • Generation VII (Alola, 88; total 809): Rowlet, Litten, Popplio, regional (Alolan) forms.
  • Generation VIII (Galar/Hisui, 96; total 905): Grookey, Scorbunny, Sobble.
  • Generation IX (Paldea, 120; total 1,025): Sprigatito, Fuecoco, Quaxly.
