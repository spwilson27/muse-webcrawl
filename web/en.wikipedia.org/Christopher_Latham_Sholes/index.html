Christopher Latham Sholes

Christopher Latham Sholes (1819–1890) was an American inventor who developed the first practical typewriter and the QWERTY keyboard layout.

Invention

A Wisconsin newspaper editor and politician, Sholes worked with Carlos Glidden and Samuel Soulé on a typing machine, patenting it in 1868. Financed by James Densmore, the design was sold to E. Remington and Sons, whose Sholes and Glidden typewriter went on sale in 1874.

QWERTY

The QWERTY arrangement, patented in 1878, separated common letter pairs to prevent typebar jams and persists on computer and phone keyboards worldwide.
