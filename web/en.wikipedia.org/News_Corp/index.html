News Corp

News Corporation, doing business as News Corp, is an American mass media and publishing company headquartered in New York City. It was created in 2013 as a spin-off of the original News Corporation founded by Rupert Murdoch in 1980, which split its publishing businesses from its film and television assets (the latter becoming 21st Century Fox).

Holdings

News Corp's businesses include Dow Jones & Company, publisher of The Wall Street Journal; newspapers such as the New York Post, The Times and The Sun in the United Kingdom, and The Australian and other titles in Australia; book publisher HarperCollins; News Corp Australia; digital real-estate services including Realtor.com operator Move, Inc.; and stakes in subscription television businesses such as Foxtel.

History

The split, announced in 2012 following the phone-hacking scandal that closed the News of the World in 2011, was completed on 28 June 2013, with Rupert Murdoch as chairman of both successor companies. Robert Thomson became chief executive of News Corp. In 2023 Rupert Murdoch stepped down as chairman, becoming chairman emeritus, and his son Lachlan Murdoch took over as sole chair. A proposed re-merger with Fox Corporation, floated in 2022, was abandoned in 2023.
