Imperial Brands plc, formerly the Imperial Tobacco Group plc, is a British multinational tobacco company headquartered in Bristol, England. It is one of the world's largest tobacco companies, listed on the London Stock Exchange and a constituent of the FTSE 100 Index.

History

The company was formed in 1901 as the Imperial Tobacco Company through the merger of 13 British tobacco firms, in response to the threat of the American Tobacco Company. It expanded internationally through acquisitions, including Reemtsma (2002), Altadis (2008), and brands acquired from Reynolds American and Lorillard in 2015. It renamed itself Imperial Brands in 2016.

Brands

Its brands include Davidoff, Gauloises, West, Winston (outside the US), JPS, and the blu vaping brand.
