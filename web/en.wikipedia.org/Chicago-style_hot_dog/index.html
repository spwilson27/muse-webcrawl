Chicago-style hot dog

A Chicago-style hot dog is an all-beef frankfurter on a poppy seed bun, originating in the city of Chicago, Illinois. It is topped with yellow mustard, chopped white onion, sweet pickle relish (often a distinctive neon-green variety), a dill pickle spear, tomato slices or wedges, pickled sport peppers and a dash of celery salt. The complete assembly is sometimes described as being "dragged through the garden" for its many vegetable toppings.

Customs

The most famous rule of the style is that ketchup is never used; many Chicago vendors refuse to serve it on a Chicago dog, and putting ketchup on one is treated as a local taboo. The classic preparation traces to street stands of the Great Depression era, when vendors sold a cheap, filling "meal on a bun". Vienna Beef and Red Hot Chicago are among the best-known suppliers of the frankfurters.
