British Airways (BA) is the flag carrier airline of the United Kingdom. It is headquartered in London, England, near its main hub at Heathrow Airport. The airline is the second largest UK-based carrier, based on fleet size and passengers carried, behind easyJet. In 2011 BA merged with Iberia, creating the International Airlines Group (IAG), a holding company registered in Madrid, Spain.

British Airways was created in 1974 after a British Airways Board was established by the British government to manage the two nationalised airline corporations, British Overseas Airways Corporation (BOAC) and British European Airways (BEA), and two regional airlines. On 31 March 1974 all four companies were merged to form British Airways. After almost 13 years as a state company, BA was privatised in February 1987 as part of the Conservative government's privatisation programme.

History

The airline's origins reach back to 1919, when Aircraft Transport and Travel began the world's first scheduled international airline service between London and Paris. BOAC operated long-haul empire routes and, with Pan American, inaugurated transatlantic jet services with the Comet 4 in 1958. BEA served domestic and European routes. British Airways was a Concorde operator from 1976 until the type's retirement in 2003, flying supersonic services to New York and other destinations.

In the 1990s BA was one of the most profitable airlines in the world — "the world's favourite airline" was its slogan — before facing increased competition from low-cost carriers and the downturn after 2001. The airline opened its dedicated Terminal 5 at Heathrow in 2008. The 2011 merger with Iberia was followed by IAG's acquisitions of BMI, Vueling, and Aer Lingus.

Destinations and fleet

British Airways operates a mixed fleet of Airbus and Boeing aircraft, including the Airbus A320 family for short-haul services and the Boeing 777, Boeing 787 Dreamliner, and Airbus A350 and A380 for long-haul routes. It flies to destinations across Europe, North and South America, Africa, Asia, and Australia, and is a founding member of the Oneworld alliance. Its subsidiaries include BA CityFlyer, operating from London City Airport, and the leisure carrier BA EuroFlyer at Gatwick.
