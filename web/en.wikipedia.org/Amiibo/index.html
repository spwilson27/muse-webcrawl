Amiibo

TypeToys-to-life figurines and cards
ManufacturerNintendo
ReleasedNovember 2014
PlatformsWii U, Nintendo 3DS, Nintendo Switch

Amiibo (stylized amiibo) is a toys-to-life platform by Nintendo, launched in November 2014. It consists of collectible figurines and cards containing near-field communication (NFC) chips that interact with supported video games, unlocking content or transferring data when tapped against a console controller or reader.

Functionality

The first Amiibo were released alongside Super Smash Bros. for Wii U, in which figures could be trained as computer-controlled fighters that learn from the player. Tapping figures in other games unlocked costumes, items, weapons, or side content in series such as Mario Kart, The Legend of Zelda, Splatoon, and Animal Crossing; Amiibo cards served the same purpose for some lines. The platform is supported on Wii U, Nintendo 3DS (with an adapter for older models), and Nintendo Switch.

Sales and reception

Amiibo became a major commercial success, selling tens of millions of units, and some figures became sought-after collectibles. Nintendo has released hundreds of distinct figures across its franchises and third-party characters.
