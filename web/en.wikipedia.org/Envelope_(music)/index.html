Envelope (music)

In music and sound synthesis, an envelope describes how a sound changes over time, most commonly its amplitude from the onset to the end of a note.

ADSR

The classic four-stage envelope is Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release: the sound rises to a peak, falls to a sustain level while the note is held, then fades after release. ADSR generators became standard on synthesizers from the 1960s onward.

Other uses

Envelopes can control filter cutoff, pitch or other parameters, and envelope followers extract the amplitude contour of an audio signal for effects such as auto-wah and compression.
