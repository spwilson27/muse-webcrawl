The Tata Group is an Indian multinational conglomerate headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Founded in 1868 by Jamsetji Tata, the group is among India's oldest and largest business houses, operating in over 100 countries across six continents. Its companies work in sectors including steel, automobiles, information technology, chemicals, energy, consumer products, hospitality, and telecommunications.

History

Jamsetji Tata began with a trading company in 1868 and went on to found ventures including the Empress Mills, the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, and the steel plant at Jamshedpur that became Tata Steel. Under his successors — including J. R. D. Tata, who led the group for over five decades — Tata expanded into aviation (Air India), chemicals, motors, and consultancy. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), founded in 1968, grew into one of the world's largest IT services firms.

Major companies

Major Tata companies include Tata Steel, Tata Motors (owner of Jaguar Land Rover), Tata Consultancy Services, Tata Power, Tata Chemicals, Indian Hotels Company (Taj Hotels), Tata Consumer Products (known for Tetley and Tata Tea), Titan Company, and Tata Communications. The group holding company is Tata Sons, whose majority shareholder is a set of philanthropic trusts endowed by members of the Tata family.

Leadership

Chairmen of Tata Sons have included J. R. D. Tata, Ratan Tata (1991–2012 and interim 2016–2017), Cyrus Mistry, and Natarajan Chandrasekaran, who became chairman in 2017. Ratan Tata oversaw major international acquisitions including Tetley (2000), Corus Steel (2007), and Jaguar Land Rover (2008).
