ASIMO

ASIMO
Manufacturer: Honda
Year of creation: 2000
Height: 130 cm (4 ft 3 in)
Weight: 54 kg

ASIMO (Advanced Step in Innovative Mobility) is a humanoid robot developed by Honda. Created in 2000, it was designed to be a helper around the house and to assist people with reduced mobility. ASIMO stood 130 cm tall and could walk, run, climb stairs, recognize faces and voices, and manipulate objects with its five-fingered hands.

Development

ASIMO grew out of Honda's E series and P series prototype robots developed from 1986 onward. Successive revisions added running (up to about 9 km/h in later versions), autonomous behavior control, and the ability to pour drinks and perform sign language. Honda retired ASIMO in 2018 to focus on practical applications of its robotics technology.

Public appearances

ASIMO toured internationally as a technology ambassador, conducting orchestras, ringing the New York Stock Exchange opening bell, and appearing at museums and trade shows.
