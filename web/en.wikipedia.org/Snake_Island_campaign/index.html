Snake Island campaign

Date24 February – 30 June 2022
LocationSnake Island, Black Sea
ResultRussian withdrawal; Ukraine regained control

The Snake Island campaign was a period of artillery duels and airstrikes during the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2022, centred on Snake Island (Zmiinyi Island), a small but strategically located Ukrainian island in the north-western Black Sea near the Danube delta and Romanian coast.

Initial attack

On 24 February 2022, the first day of the invasion, the Russian warship Moskva ordered the island's small Ukrainian garrison to surrender. The reply attributed to border guard Roman Hrybov — "Russian warship, go fuck yourself" — became a famous symbol of Ukrainian resistance. The garrison was captured after bombardment but later freed in a prisoner exchange.

Occupation and withdrawal

Russia garrisoned the island and repeatedly tried to reinforce it, losing boats, air-defence systems and the Moskva itself, which sank in April after a Ukrainian missile strike. Under sustained Ukrainian attacks, Russia withdrew its forces on 30 June 2022, describing the move as a "gesture of goodwill"; Ukraine raised its flag on the island days later.
