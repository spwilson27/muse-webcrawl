Horacio Pagani

Horacio Pagani (born 10 November 1955) is an Argentine-Italian businessman and engineer, founder of Pagani Automobili, the Modena-based manufacturer of the Zonda and Huayra hypercars. He is noted for pioneering the artistic use of carbon-fibre composites in production cars, blending engineering with a Leonardo da Vinci-inspired philosophy that art and science should walk together.

Early life and Lamborghini

Born in Casilda, Argentina, Pagani built a Formula 3 car as a young man and moved to Italy in 1982 with an introduction to Juan Manuel Fangio. At Lamborghini he rose to chief engineer, worked on the Countach Evoluzione — an early all-composite concept — and founded a composites department, Lamborghini's first. When Lamborghini declined to buy an autoclave, Pagani borrowed money to purchase one himself, founding Modena Design in 1991.

Pagani Automobili

His own company, founded in 1992, debuted the Zonda C12 at the 1999 Geneva Motor Show. The Zonda's many limited editions were followed by the Huayra (2011) and the Utopia (2022), all powered by Mercedes-AMG V12 engines secured partly through Fangio's recommendation. Pagani cars are famed for obsessive detail, exposed carbon-titanium ("carbotanium") weave, and prices in the millions.
