The Georgia Ports Authority (GPA) is an agency of the U.S. state of Georgia that operates the deepwater ports of Savannah and Brunswick and a network of inland terminals. Established in 1945, it oversees some of the busiest container and roll-on/roll-off facilities in the United States.

Facilities

The Port of Savannah is centred on the Garden City Terminal, one of the largest single container terminals in North America, and the Ocean Terminal. The Port of Brunswick specialises in roll-on/roll-off cargo at Colonel's Island Terminal — one of the largest vehicle-handling facilities in the United States — and breakbulk and bulk cargo at Mayor's Point and East River terminals. GPA has also developed inland "dry ports", such as the Appalachian Regional Port near Chatsworth, to move containers by rail.

Growth and projects

The Savannah Harbor Expansion Project, completed in 2022, deepened the Savannah River shipping channel to 47 feet, allowing larger container ships to call. Savannah has repeatedly ranked among the top four U.S. container ports by volume. GPA states that port activity supports hundreds of thousands of jobs across Georgia.
