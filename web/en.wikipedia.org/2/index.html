2 (two) is a number, numeral and digit. It is the natural number following 1 and preceding 3. It is the smallest and the only even prime number. Because it forms the basis of duality, it has religious and spiritual significance in many cultures.

In mathematics

An integer is called even if it is divisible by 2. Two is the first prime number, the first Sophie Germain prime, and the base of the binary number system used in nearly all modern computers. The square root of 2 was the first number proved to be irrational. Two is also the atomic number of helium, and its powers (2, 4, 8, 16, 32...) recur throughout computing and music.

Evolution of the glyph

The modern glyph "2" derives from the Brahmi numeral, written with two horizontal lines, which passed through the Gupta and Nagari scripts into the Arabic numeral tradition and thence to Europe. The cursive joining of the two lines produced the looped form familiar today.

In science and culture

Two-stranded DNA forms the double helix; many animals have bilateral symmetry; and duality — day and night, yin and yang — structures numerous philosophical and religious systems. Two is the number of players in many games, the number of sexes in most animal species, and the basis of the binary opposition used in logic.
