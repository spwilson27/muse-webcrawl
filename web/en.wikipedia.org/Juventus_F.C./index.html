Juventus FC

Juventus Football Club, commonly known as Juventus or Juve, is a professional football club based in Turin, Piedmont, Italy. Founded in 1897, it is the most successful club in Italian football, having won a record number of Serie A titles, and plays its home matches at the Juventus Stadium (Allianz Stadium).

History

Juventus was founded by students in Turin in 1897 and has been controlled by the Agnelli family since 1923, the longest association between a family and a sports club in Italy. The club won five consecutive league titles in the early 1930s and dominated again under Giovanni Trapattoni in the 1970s and 1980s, and under Marcello Lippi in the 1990s, winning the European Cup in 1985 and the UEFA Champions League in 1996.

Honours

Juventus has won 36 official Serie A titles (including nine consecutively from 2011–12 to 2019–20), a record number of Coppa Italia trophies, two European Cup/Champions League titles, three UEFA Cups and the 1985 Intercontinental Cup. Following the 2006 Calciopoli scandal the club was relegated to Serie B for one season before returning to the top flight.

Colours and identity

The club's black-and-white striped shirts, adopted in 1903, were inspired by Notts County. Its nickname, "the Old Lady" (la Vecchia Signora), is a Turin pun on the Latin-derived name Juventus, meaning "youth".
