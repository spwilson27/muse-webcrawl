Regions of Ethiopia

Ethiopia is a federal republic divided into regional states (kililoch), largely organized along ethnic and linguistic lines under the 1995 constitution, plus two chartered cities, Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa.

Regional states

The regions include Tigray, Afar, Amhara, Oromia, Somali, Benishangul-Gumuz, Gambela, Harari, Sidama, South West Ethiopia Peoples', Central Ethiopia, and South Ethiopia — the last several created by referendums that split the former Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region (SNNPR) between 2020 and 2023. Oromia is the largest region by both area and population.

Administration

Regions are subdivided into zones and woredas (districts). The constitution grants nations and peoples the right to self-determination, including, in principle, secession, and new regions have repeatedly been formed through referendums among ethnic groups seeking their own state.
