Editors are a British rock band formed in 2002 in Birmingham by students at Staffordshire University. The band consists of lead singer and guitarist Tom Smith, bassist Russell Leetch, drummer Ed Lay, and later additions Justin Lockey, Elliott Williams and Benjamin John Power (Blanck Mass). Their dark, guitar-driven sound, anchored by Smith's baritone voice, drew comparisons with post-punk bands such as Joy Division and Echo & the Bunnymen.

History

Editors' debut album, The Back Room (2005), featuring the singles "Munich" and "Blood", was nominated for the Mercury Prize and sold over a million copies. The follow-up, An End Has a Start (2007), debuted at number one in the UK. Their third album, In This Light and on This Evening (2009), took a more electronic direction and also topped the UK chart. Founding guitarist Chris Urbanowicz left in 2012 during work on The Weight of Your Love (2013).

Subsequent albums include In Dream (2015), Violence (2018) and EBM (2022), the last recorded with Power as a full member and leaning into electronic dance textures. The band are especially popular in continental Europe, headlining major festivals in Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany.
