The National Microbiology Laboratory (NML) is part of the Public Health Agency of Canada and the central facility for infectious disease research and reference testing in Canada. Located in Winnipeg, Manitoba, it is housed in the Canadian Science Centre for Human and Animal Health alongside the National Centre for Foreign Animal Disease.

Work

The laboratory is one of the few facilities in North America with Biosafety Level 4 containment, enabling work with the most dangerous pathogens, including Ebola and Marburg viruses. The NML played a leading role in Canada's response to the 2003 SARS outbreak by identifying the causative coronavirus genome, contributed the ZMapp-related monoclonal antibody research during the West African Ebola epidemic, and developed the VSV-EBOV vaccine in collaboration with partners. It also provides reference diagnostics and genomic surveillance for provincial laboratories.
