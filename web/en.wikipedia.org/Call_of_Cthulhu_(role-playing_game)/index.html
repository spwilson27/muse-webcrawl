Call of Cthulhu (role-playing game)

DesignerSandy Petersen
PublisherChaosium
First published1981
SystemBasic Role-Playing (percentile)

Call of Cthulhu is a horror fiction role-playing game based on H. P. Lovecraft's story of the same name and the associated Cthulhu Mythos. It was designed by Sandy Petersen and published by Chaosium in 1981; its seventh edition appeared in 2014. Players take the roles of ordinary people — Investigators — who confront cosmic horrors, cults, and forbidden knowledge, most famously in a 1920s setting, though other eras are supported.

Gameplay

The game uses Chaosium's Basic Role-Playing system with percentile (d100) skill rolls. A defining mechanic is Sanity: encounters with the Mythos erode Investigators' sanity points, leading to bouts of madness and potentially permanent insanity. Combat is deliberately lethal and character death common, emphasizing investigation and survival over heroics. The gamemaster is called the Keeper of Arcane Lore.

Reception and legacy

Call of Cthulhu is regarded as one of the most influential and acclaimed role-playing games ever published, winning numerous Origins Awards and being inducted into the Origins Hall of Fame. Classic campaigns and supplements include Masks of Nyarlathotep, Horror on the Orient Express, and the sourcebook-filled Delta Green spin-off setting. A successful 2013 Kickstarter funded the seventh edition, and a Japanese edition became hugely popular, spreading the game widely in Japan.
