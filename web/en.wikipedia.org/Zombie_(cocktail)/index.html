Zombie (cocktail)

Image placeholder: A Zombie cocktail served in a tiki mug

The Zombie is a Tiki cocktail made of fruit juices, liqueurs, and various rums. It first appeared in late 1934, invented by Donn Beach at his Hollywood Don the Beachcomber restaurant. It was popularized in New York at the 1939 World's Fair.

Composition

The original recipe calls for three kinds of rum, lime juice, falernum, Angostura bitters, Pernod, grenadine, and "Don's Mix", a blend of cinnamon syrup and grapefruit juice. Beach kept the recipe secret, using coded references his bartenders could not decode. The high alcohol content was famously limited to two per customer.

Legacy

The Zombie became an emblem of tiki culture; reconstructed recipes by Jeff "Beachbum" Berry restored the drink's original form for modern cocktail culture.
