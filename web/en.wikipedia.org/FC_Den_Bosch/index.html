FC Den Bosch is a professional football club from 's-Hertogenbosch, Netherlands. The club was founded on 18 August 1965 as FC Den Bosch/BVV, from the merger traditions of BVV (founded 1906) and Wilhelmina (1897), and currently competes in the Eerste Divisie, the second tier of Dutch football. Their home ground is De Vliert.

History

The predecessor club BVV won the Dutch national championship in 1948, in the pre-Eredivisie era. FC Den Bosch has since moved repeatedly between the Eredivisie and the Eerste Divisie, earning a reputation as a "yo-yo club"; their best modern top-flight finish was sixth in the 1980s. Ruud van Nistelrooy began his professional career at Den Bosch in 1993 before moving to Heerenveen and PSV. The club has also faced financial difficulties, entering administration in the 2010s before being rescued by new investors.

Supporters and colours

Den Bosch play in blue and white. The club's supporters are known for the M-side stand at De Vliert, and the club maintains regional rivalries with clubs from North Brabant including NAC Breda and Willem II.
