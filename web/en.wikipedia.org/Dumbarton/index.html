Dumbarton

Dumbarton is a town in West Dunbartonshire, Scotland, on the north bank of the River Clyde where the River Leven flows into it. It is dominated by Dumbarton Rock, a volcanic plug crowned by Dumbarton Castle.

History

The rock was the stronghold of Alt Clut, capital of the Brittonic Kingdom of Strathclyde, and the town's name derives from the Scottish Gaelic Dùn Breatann, "fort of the Britons". Dumbarton later became a royal burgh and an important shipbuilding centre on the Clyde, home to yards such as Denny's, and a centre of whisky production and glassmaking.

Present day

Dumbarton is the administrative centre of West Dunbartonshire. Its football club, Dumbarton F.C., was one of Scotland's earliest champions.
