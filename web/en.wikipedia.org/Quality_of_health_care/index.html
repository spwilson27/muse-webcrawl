Quality of health care

The quality of health care is the degree to which health services for individuals and populations increase the likelihood of desired health outcomes. It is based on evidence-based professional knowledge and is critical for achieving universal health coverage.

Definitions

The Institute of Medicine defined quality as care that is safe, effective, patient-centered, timely, efficient and equitable. The World Health Organization similarly emphasizes that quality care should be effective, safe and people-centred, and additionally timely, equitable, integrated and efficient.

Measurement

Avedis Donabedian's influential model assesses quality through structure (facilities, equipment, staff), process (what is done in giving and receiving care) and outcomes (changes in patients' health status). Quality indicators, accreditation, clinical audits and patient-reported outcomes are used to monitor and improve care.

Improvement

Quality improvement uses systematic methods such as plan-do-study-act cycles, clinical guidelines and patient safety programmes to close gaps between actual and achievable performance.
