ISO 22301

ISO 22301 is an international standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) that specifies requirements for a business continuity management system (BCMS). Its full title is Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements. The standard enables an organisation to plan, establish, implement, operate, monitor, review, maintain and continually improve a documented management system to protect against, prepare for, respond to and recover from disruptive incidents.

History

The standard was developed by ISO technical committee ISO/TC 292 (Security and resilience) and was first published in May 2012, drawing substantially on the earlier British standard BS 25999-2. A second edition, ISO 22301:2019, streamlined the requirements — reducing the number of clauses and removing duplicative documentation requirements — while retaining the same overall structure. Guidance on the standard's requirements is given in ISO 22313.

Content

Like other modern ISO management system standards, ISO 22301 follows the Annex SL high-level structure: context of the organisation, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement. Its operational requirements centre on:

  • Business impact analysis — identifying prioritised activities and their dependencies, and setting recovery time objectives;
  • Risk assessment of disruptive threats;
  • Business continuity strategies and solutions (such as alternate sites, backup resources and workarounds);
  • Documented business continuity plans and procedures for response and recovery;
  • An exercise programme to test and validate the plans.

Organisations may be certified against ISO 22301 by accredited certification bodies following an audit, and certification is common in finance, telecommunications, cloud services and other sectors where regulators and customers demand demonstrated resilience.
