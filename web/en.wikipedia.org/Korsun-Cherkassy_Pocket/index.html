Korsun-Cherkassy Pocket


The Battle of the Korsun–Cherkassy Pocket was fought in January–February 1944 on the Eastern Front.

The Battle of the Korsun–Cherkassy Pocket was a Second World War battle fought from 24 January to 16 February 1944 in Ukraine, in which Soviet forces of the 1st and 2nd Ukrainian Fronts encircled German forces in a salient west of Cherkassy along the Dnieper River.

The battle

The encirclement trapped two German corps — roughly 56,000 men under General Wilhelm Stemmermann — in a pocket around Korsun. Soviet pincers closed near Zvenigorodka on 28 January. Relief attempts by German panzer corps under Erich von Manstein's Army Group South fought through mud and snow to within about 12 km of the pocket but could not break through.

On the night of 16–17 February the encircled troops attempted a mass breakout toward Lysyanka. Many reached German lines, but heavy equipment was abandoned and losses were severe; Stemmermann was killed during the breakout. Soviet claims of a "second Stalingrad" were exaggerated, yet the battle marked a significant stage in the Soviet reconquest of right-bank Ukraine and formed part of the wider Dnieper–Carpathian Offensive.
