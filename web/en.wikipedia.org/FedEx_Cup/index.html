FedEx Cup

The FedEx Cup is the season-long points competition and championship trophy of the PGA Tour, the main professional golf tour in the United States. Introduced in 2007 and sponsored by FedEx, it culminates in a series of playoff events ending with the Tour Championship, where the winner receives a very large bonus prize — 8 million in recent editions.

Format

Players earn points based on their finishes in regular-season PGA Tour events. The top 70 players qualify for the first playoff event, whittled to 50 and then to the top 30 who contest the Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta. Since 2019 the Tour Championship has used a strokes-based starting format ("starting strokes"), in which the points leader begins the tournament at ten under par.

Champions

Tiger Woods won the inaugural FedEx Cup in 2007 and again in 2009, and is the only two-time champion of the original format; Rory McIlroy has won the trophy three times (2016, 2019, 2022), the most of any player.
