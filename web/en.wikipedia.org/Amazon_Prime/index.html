Amazon Prime

Amazon Prime is a paid subscription service from Amazon which is available in various countries and gives users access to additional services otherwise unavailable or available at a premium to other Amazon customers. Services include same, one, or two-day delivery of goods, streaming music, video, e-books, gaming services, and grocery shopping services.

History

Amazon launched Prime in the United States in 2005, offering free two-day shipping on eligible items for an annual fee of $79. Prime Video began as Amazon Unbox in 2006 and was bundled with Prime in 2011. Prime Music launched in 2014, and Prime Day, an annual sales event, began in 2015 for the company's 20th anniversary. Amazon reported more than 100 million Prime members in 2018 and more than 200 million in 2021. The annual price in the US rose over time, reaching $139 per year in 2022.

Services

Prime benefits include Prime Delivery (fast free shipping), Prime Video streaming, Amazon Music Prime, Prime Reading, Prime Gaming, Amazon Photos storage, and exclusive deals. In some markets Prime members can use Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market delivery. Amazon has also experimented with drone delivery through Prime Air and in-home delivery via Amazon Key.
