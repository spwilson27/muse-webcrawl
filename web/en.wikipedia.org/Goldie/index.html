Goldie

Goldie (born Clifford Joseph Price, MBE, on 19 September 1965 in Walsall, England) is an English music producer, DJ, and actor, and a pioneering figure in jungle and drum and bass music. He co-founded the Metalheadz record label and is widely credited with bringing drum and bass to mainstream attention in the 1990s.

Career

Raised in children's homes, Price first became known as a graffiti artist in the West Midlands before moving into the UK rave scene. As Rufige Kru and under his own name, he developed an elaborate, orchestral approach to breakbeat production, using time-stretching and layered sampling. His debut album Timeless (1995), featuring the 21-minute title track and "Inner City Life" with vocalist Diane Charlemagne, is considered a landmark of electronic music. His second album, Saturnz Return (1998), and the later The Journey Man (2017) continued his ambitious approach.

Other work

Goldie has acted in films including the James Bond film The World Is Not Enough and Guy Ritchie's Snatch, and appeared in the BBC soap EastEnders. He was appointed MBE in 2016 for services to music and young people.
