Alexis Ohanian

Alexis Kerry Ohanian (born April 24, 1983) is an American internet entrepreneur and investor. He is best known as the co-founder, with Steve Huffman and Aaron Swartz, of the social news and discussion website Reddit.

Career

Ohanian graduated from the University of Virginia in 2005 and co-founded Reddit through the Y Combinator startup program; the site was sold to Condé Nast in 2006. He later co-founded the travel search site Hipmunk and the venture capital firm Initialized Capital, and helped launch the nonprofit Breadpig.

He returned to Reddit in an executive role in the mid-2010s and resigned from its board in 2020, asking that his seat be filled by a Black candidate. Through his firm Seven Seven Six, founded in 2020, he has invested in early-stage technology companies, and he was a founding investor in Angel City FC of the National Women's Soccer League.

Advocacy and personal life

Ohanian was a prominent opponent of the SOPA and PIPA bills and wrote about the experience in his 2013 book Without Their Permission. He married tennis champion Serena Williams in 2017; they have two daughters.
