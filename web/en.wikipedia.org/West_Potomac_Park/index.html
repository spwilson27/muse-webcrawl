West Potomac Park

LocationWashington, D.C., United States
Administered byNational Park Service
FeaturesTidal Basin, Jefferson Memorial, Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial, Korean War Veterans Memorial, cherry trees

West Potomac Park is a national park in Washington, D.C., extending south and west of the National Mall along the Potomac River and the Washington Channel. Created from land reclaimed by dredging the Potomac in the late 19th century, it forms part of the monumental core of the capital and is administered by the National Park Service.

Features

The park includes the Tidal Basin, Constitution Gardens, and the sites of the Jefferson Memorial, the Franklin Delano Roosevelt Memorial, the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial, the Korean War Veterans Memorial and the District of Columbia War Memorial. It is connected to East Potomac Park and to the Lincoln Memorial grounds.

Cherry trees

West Potomac Park is famous for its Japanese cherry trees, descended from trees given by Japan in 1912 and planted around the Tidal Basin. Their spring blossom is the centrepiece of the National Cherry Blossom Festival, which draws over a million visitors annually.
