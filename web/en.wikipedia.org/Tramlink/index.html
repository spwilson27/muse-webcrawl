Tramlink, previously branded as Croydon Tramlink, is a light rail tram system serving Croydon and surrounding areas in South London, England. It is the only tram system in London and began operation in May 2000, the first tram system in the city since the original network closed in 1952.

Network

The network centres on Croydon, with routes to Wimbledon, Beckenham Junction, Elmers End, and New Addington, totalling about 28 kilometres. Much of the alignment reuses former railway lines, including the former West Croydon to Wimbledon line.

Operation

Tramlink is operated by Tram Operations Ltd under contract to Transport for London, using Bombardier CR4000 and Stadler Variobahn trams. A tram derailment at Sandilands in November 2016, in which seven passengers died, led to major safety changes across UK tramways.
