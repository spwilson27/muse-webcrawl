Business card

A business card is a small card bearing business information about a company or individual, shared during formal introductions as a convenience and memory aid. A typical card includes the person's name, job title, company, and contact details such as phone number, email address, and website.

History

Business cards descend from the visiting cards and trade cards of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. In China, "name cards" were exchanged by officials as early as the fifteenth century. As printing became cheaper in the nineteenth century, trade cards merged with visiting-card etiquette to form the modern business card.

Customs

Card exchange is governed by elaborate etiquette in parts of East Asia, most notably Japan, where the meishi is presented and received with both hands and treated with respect. Standard sizes vary by region: 3.5 × 2 inches in the United States and 85 × 55 mm in much of Europe. Digital alternatives using QR codes and NFC have grown in popularity, but printed cards remain common worldwide.
