Kosovo War

The Kosovo War was an armed conflict in Kosovo from 28 February 1998 until 11 June 1999, fought between the forces of the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia and the Kosovo Albanian rebel group known as the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA), with NATO air support from March 1999.

Date1998–1999
LocationKosovo, Yugoslavia
ResultKumanovo Agreement; UN administration

Background

Kosovo, an autonomous province of Serbia within Yugoslavia, had a majority ethnic Albanian population. Autonomy was revoked in 1989 under Slobodan Milošević, fuelling Albanian resistance. See Kosovo and Yugoslav Wars.

War

Clashes escalated in 1998. After failed diplomacy at Rambouillet, NATO began bombing Yugoslavia on 24 March 1999, without UN Security Council authorisation. Hundreds of thousands of Albanians were displaced or expelled.

Aftermath

The Kumanovo Agreement ended the war; UN Security Council Resolution 1244 placed Kosovo under UN administration (UNMIK) with NATO-led KFOR. Kosovo declared independence in 2008, recognised by many but not all states.
