Enrico Betti
Born: 21 October 1823, Pistoia
Died: 11 August 1892, Soiana
Fields: Mathematics, physics

Enrico Betti Glaoui (1823–1892) was an Italian mathematician and engineer, professor at the University of Pisa and the Scuola Normale Superiore.

Work

Betti is remembered for Betti numbers, which classify topological spaces by the rank of their homology groups, and for work on elasticity including Betti's theorem. He also contributed to Galois theory and potential theory, and served in the Italian parliament and senate.
