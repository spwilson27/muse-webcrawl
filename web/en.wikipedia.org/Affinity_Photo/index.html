Affinity Photo is a raster graphics editor developed by Serif for macOS, iPadOS, and Microsoft Windows. It was first released in 2015 and is part of the Affinity suite alongside Affinity Designer and Affinity Publisher. It is marketed as a lower-cost, perpetual-license alternative to Adobe Photoshop.

Features

Affinity Photo supports RAW editing, HDR merging, panorama stitching, focus stacking, PSD file compatibility, unlimited layers, and a non-destructive editing workflow organized into "Personas" for photo, liquify, develop, and tone mapping tasks. The iPad version, released in 2017, was an early demonstration of professional photo editing on tablets and was named Apple's iPad App of the Year.

Reception

The application has been praised for performance and pricing, winning an Apple Design Award in 2015. Version 2 was released in November 2022 with a redesigned interface and live mesh warp and other features.
