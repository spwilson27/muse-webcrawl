Otsego Lake (New York)

LocationOtsego County, New York
LengthAbout 9 miles (14 km)
OutflowSusquehanna River
Surface elevationAbout 1,190 ft (363 m)

Otsego Lake is a glacial lake in Otsego County, New York, about nine miles long and one to two miles wide. It is the source of the Susquehanna River, which flows from the lake's southern end at Cooperstown. The lake is sometimes called the "Glimmerglass" after James Fenimore Cooper's description of it in his novels.

Geography

The lake lies in a valley carved by glaciers and is bordered by Glimmerglass State Park on its eastern shore. Its principal tributaries include Hayden Creek and Shadow Brook, and its waters support lake trout, bass, and other fish.

Cultural associations

Cooperstown, at the southern end, hosts the Glimmerglass Festival of opera and the National Baseball Hall of Fame. The lake features prominently in Cooper's Leatherstocking Tales, including The Pioneers and The Deerslayer.
