Nelson Piquet Souto Maior (born 17 August 1952), known simply as Nelson Piquet, is a Brazilian former racing driver and businessman. He won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship three times, in 1981 and 1983 with Brabham and in 1987 with Williams, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest drivers in the sport's history. His analytical approach to car set-up and race strategy, together with a famously dry wit and mind games with rivals, made him one of the defining figures of 1980s Formula One.

Early career

The son of Estácio Souto Maior, a prominent physician and politician, Piquet briefly followed the family expectation of a tennis career before gravitating to karting, racing under his mother's surname to conceal his activities from his father. He won the Brazilian karting championship in 1971 and 1972 and the national Formula Super Vee title in 1976, then moved to Europe, winning the British Formula Three championship in 1978 with a record number of victories.

Formula One career

Piquet made his Formula One debut in 1978 and joined Brabham full-time in 1979, remaining with the team through 1985. He won his first championship in 1981 and, in 1983, became the first driver to win the title with a turbocharged engine (the BMW-powered Brabham BT52). Moving to Williams in 1986, he fought a celebrated intra-team rivalry with Nigel Mansell; the two collided in the championship battle that year, but Piquet regrouped to take the 1987 crown. Later seasons with Lotus and Benetton yielded three further wins, including memorable victories at the 1990 Japanese and Australian and 1991 Canadian Grands Prix. He retired from Formula One at the end of 1991 and was seriously injured in a crash during practice for the 1992 Indianapolis 500, which effectively ended his top-level single-seater career.

Later life

Piquet built successful business interests in Brazil, including a satellite-tracking and fleet-management company. His sons include Nelson Piquet Jr., Pedro Piquet and Geraldo Piquet; his daughter Kelly Piquet is also well known in motorsport circles. Piquet's later public comments have periodically attracted controversy.
