The Bergiselschanze is a ski jumping hill on the Bergisel mountain in Innsbruck, Austria. It is one of the most famous venues in ski jumping, hosting the third competition of the annual Four Hills Tournament each January, and it served as a venue for the 1964 and 1976 Winter Olympics, for which the Olympic flame was lit at the stadium.

The 2003 tower

The current hill was designed by the architect Zaha Hadid and opened in 2003. The structure combines the jump's inrun with a tower containing a café and viewing terrace about 40 metres above the landing area, offering panoramic views over Innsbruck, and has become a landmark of modern architecture. The hill is a large hill with a K-point of 120 metres and a hill size of 128 metres.

History

Ski jumps on the Bergisel date to the 1920s. The Bergisel is also historically significant as the site of battles in 1809 during the Tyrolean Rebellion under Andreas Hofer, commemorated by a memorial nearby.
