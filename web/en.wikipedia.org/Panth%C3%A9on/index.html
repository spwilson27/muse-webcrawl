Panthéon

The Panthéon is a monument in the Latin Quarter of Paris, France. Commissioned by Louis XV as a church dedicated to Saint Genevieve, it was repurposed during the Revolution as a mausoleum for distinguished French citizens.

Architecture

Designed by Jacques-Germain Soufflot and completed in 1790, the building combines a Greek-cross plan with a large dome inspired by St Paul's Cathedral and the Roman Pantheon.

Burials

Among those interred are Voltaire, Rousseau, Victor Hugo, Émile Zola, Marie Curie and Alexandre Dumas. Foucault's pendulum famously demonstrated the Earth's rotation beneath its dome in 1851.
