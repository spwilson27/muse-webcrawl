HMAS Creswell

HMAS Creswell is a Royal Australian Navy (RAN) training establishment located on the shores of Jervis Bay in the Jervis Bay Territory, Australia. It is home to the Royal Australian Naval College (RANC), the institution responsible for the initial training of officers entering the RAN, and is named after Vice Admiral Sir William Rooke Creswell, regarded as the father of the Australian Navy.

History

The site at Captain's Point, Jervis Bay, was selected in 1911 as the location for the naval college, which opened in 1915 after temporarily operating at Geelong. The college moved to Flinders Naval Depot in Victoria in 1930 because of economic pressures, but returned to Jervis Bay in 1958, and the establishment was commissioned as HMAS Creswell in recognition of Creswell's role in founding the naval service.

Role

New Entry Officers' Courses train midshipmen and junior officers in leadership, seamanship, naval history, and professional skills before they proceed to specialist training. The base also hosts the RAN School of Survivability and Ship Safety, the Beecroft Weapons Range nearby, and other training facilities. Creswell is unusual among Australian bases in sitting within Jervis Bay Territory, surrounded by Booderee National Park.
