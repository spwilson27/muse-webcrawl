Adil Abdul-Mahdi

Born: January 1, 1942, Baghdad, Kingdom of Iraq
Office: Prime Minister of Iraq (2018–2020)
Other offices: Minister of Finance (2004–2005), Vice President (2005–2011), Minister of Oil (2014–2016)

Adil Abdul-Mahdi al-Muntafiki (Arabic: عادل عبد المهدي المنتفكي; born 1 January 1942) is an Iraqi politician who served as the prime minister of Iraq from October 2018 until May 2020. An economist and Shia Islamist politician, he was previously a vice president of Iraq from 2005 to 2011 and served as minister of finance and minister of oil.

Early life and exile

Abdul-Mahdi was born in Baghdad, the son of a minister under the Iraqi monarchy. He joined the Iraqi Communist Party, later splitting with factions aligned with Maoism, then studied economics in France. In exile he became a leader in the Supreme Council for the Islamic Revolution in Iraq (SCIRI), based in Iran.

Prime ministership

Nominated as a compromise candidate in October 2018, he headed a government amid deep political fragmentation. Mass anti-government protests beginning in October 2019—met with a violent crackdown that killed hundreds—led him to announce his resignation on 29 November 2019; he stayed on as caretaker until Mustafa Al-Kadhimi succeeded him in May 2020.
