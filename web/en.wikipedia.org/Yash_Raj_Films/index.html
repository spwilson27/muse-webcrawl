Yash Raj Films (YRF) is an Indian film production and distribution company founded by filmmaker Yash Chopra in 1970. Based in Mumbai, it is one of the largest film studios in India and a defining force in mainstream Hindi cinema (Bollywood).

The YRF Studios complex in Andheri, Mumbai.

History

After directing films for his brother's BR Films, Yash Chopra launched YRF with Daag (1973). The studio produced classics such as Deewaar (1975), Kabhi Kabhie (1976), Silsila (1981), Chandni (1989), and Lamhe (1991). Under Yash's son Aditya Chopra, whose directorial debut Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995) became one of the longest-running films in Indian cinema, the company expanded into a full studio with its own production facilities.

Later developments

YRF built the YRF Spy Universe franchise (Ek Tha Tiger, War, Pathaan) among the highest-grossing Indian film series, and diversified into distribution, music, and streaming content. Aditya Chopra has chaired the company since Yash Chopra's death in 2012.
