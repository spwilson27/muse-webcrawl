The economy of Qatar is one of the richest in the world based on GDP per capita. The country has the world's third-largest proven natural gas reserves and is the second-largest exporter of liquefied natural gas (LNG). Hydrocarbons account for the bulk of government revenue and exports.

Energy sector

Qatar's wealth rests on the North Dome/South Pars offshore gas field, the largest non-associated gas field in the world, shared with Iran. State-owned QatarEnergy dominates the sector. Expansion projects aim to raise LNG capacity substantially during the 2020s.

Diversification

Through the Qatar National Vision 2030, the state has invested in finance, education, tourism, aviation (Qatar Airways), and sport — most visibly hosting the 2022 FIFA World Cup. The sovereign wealth fund, the Qatar Investment Authority, holds major stakes in companies and real estate worldwide. The economy relies heavily on expatriate labour, who make up the large majority of the population.
