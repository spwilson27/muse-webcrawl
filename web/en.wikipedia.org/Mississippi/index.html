Mississippi is a state in the southeastern region of the United States, bordered by Tennessee to the north, Alabama to the east, the Gulf of Mexico to the south, and Louisiana and Arkansas across the Mississippi River to the west. It takes its name from the Mississippi River, which forms its western boundary; the name comes from an Ojibwe word meaning "great river." Its capital and largest city is Jackson.

History

The region was home to Mississippian-culture peoples and historic nations including the Choctaw, Chickasaw, and Natchez. Hernando de Soto's expedition reached the river in 1541; French, Spanish, and British colonies followed. Mississippi became the 20th state in 1817. By 1860 its cotton economy, built on enslaved labour, made it the nation's top cotton-producing state and one of its richest; it was the second state to secede in 1861, and Jefferson Davis, president of the Confederacy, was a Mississippian. After the Civil War and Reconstruction, white Democrats imposed Jim Crow segregation, disenfranchising most Black residents for generations.

Mississippi was a central battleground of the civil rights movement: the murder of Emmett Till (1955), the Freedom Rides, James Meredith's integration of the University of Mississippi (1962), Freedom Summer, and the murders of Chaney, Goodman, and Schwerner (1964) drew national attention. Although it remains among the poorest states by income and health measures, it is renowned for its literary heritage (William Faulkner, Eudora Welty, Richard Wright) and as the birthplace of the blues and of Elvis Presley. In 2020 voters approved a new state flag, replacing the previous design that incorporated the Confederate battle flag.

Geography and economy

Most of the state is lowland: the fertile alluvial Delta in the northwest, pine forests in the south, and a short Gulf Coast with beachfront cities such as Gulfport and Biloxi, which were devastated by Hurricane Katrina in 2005. Agriculture (soybeans, cotton, poultry, catfish farming), manufacturing, gaming on the Gulf Coast and riverboats, and the Stennis Space Center are important to the economy.
