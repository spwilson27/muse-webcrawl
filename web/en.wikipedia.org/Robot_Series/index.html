Robot series

The Robot series is a collection of science fiction short stories and novels by Isaac Asimov connected by the theme of positronic robots, governed by his Three Laws of Robotics. The series includes the story collection I, Robot (1950) and the novels The Caves of Steel (1954), The Naked Sun (1957), The Robots of Dawn (1983) and Robots and Empire (1985).

Content

Many short stories feature robopsychologist Susan Calvin and are framed as her recollections. The novels are detective stories pairing Earth detective Elijah Baley with R. Daneel Olivaw, a humaniform robot, and are set in a future where Earth is overcrowded and resented by the wealthy "Spacer" worlds. Asimov later linked the Robot stories to his Foundation series, making them parts of a single future history.

Three Laws

The stories explore the consequences of the Three Laws: a robot may not injure a human or allow one to come to harm; must obey human orders except where they conflict with the First Law; and must protect its own existence subject to the other two.
