Alphabet Inc.

Alphabet Inc. is an American multinational technology conglomerate holding company headquartered in Mountain View, California. It was created through a restructuring of Google on October 2, 2015, and became the parent company of Google and several former Google subsidiaries. The company is one of the world's most valuable by market capitalisation and is counted among the "Big Tech" companies.

History

Google co-founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin announced the creation of Alphabet in August 2015, explaining that the new structure would allow the core internet business — search, advertising, YouTube, Android, and related products, retained within Google — to be managed separately from longer-term ventures, known as "Other Bets". Page became CEO of Alphabet and Brin its president, with Sundar Pichai taking over as CEO of Google. In December 2019, Page and Brin stepped back from their executive roles and Pichai became CEO of Alphabet as well.

Subsidiaries

Alphabet's principal subsidiary is Google LLC. Other subsidiaries have included the self-driving company Waymo, the life-sciences company Verily, the artificial intelligence laboratory DeepMind (merged into Google in 2023 as Google DeepMind), the drone-delivery company Wing, the cybersecurity firm Mandiant (acquired in 2022), and the access and connectivity group Google Fiber. The bulk of Alphabet's revenue comes from Google advertising, with Google Cloud and subscriptions, platforms and devices providing most of the remainder.

Structure and governance

Alphabet trades on Nasdaq under the tickers GOOGL and GOOG, corresponding to its Class A and Class C shares; the founders' Class B shares carry disproportionate voting power. The company reports results in segments for Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets, and has faced major antitrust cases in the United States and Europe concerning its search and advertising businesses.
