Puma (brand)

Puma SE
Founded1948, Herzogenaurach, Germany
FounderRudolf Dassler
IndustrySportswear and sporting goods

Puma SE is a German multinational corporation that designs and manufactures athletic and casual footwear, apparel and accessories. It is the third-largest sportswear manufacturer in the world. The company was founded in 1948 by Rudolf Dassler after he split from the Dassler Brothers Shoe Factory he had run with his brother Adolf, who founded Adidas.

History

Originally registered as Ruda, the company was renamed Puma within months. Puma equipped many prominent football teams and athletes; its football boots were worn by Pelé, and the company has long-standing sponsorships in football, running, basketball and motorsport, including partnerships with clubs such as AC Milan, Borussia Dortmund and Manchester City.
