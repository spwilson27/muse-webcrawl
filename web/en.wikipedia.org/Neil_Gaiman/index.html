Neil Gaiman

Neil Gaiman
Born: 10 November 1960, Portchester, Hampshire, England
Occupation: Author
Genre: Fantasy, horror, science fiction, comics

Neil Richard MacKinnon Gaiman (born 10 November 1960) is an English author of short fiction, novels, comic books, graphic novels, audio theatre, and films. His works include the comic book series The Sandman and the novels Stardust, American Gods, Coraline, and The Graveyard Book.

Career

Gaiman began his career as a journalist before turning to fiction. His collaboration with Terry Pratchett produced the novel Good Omens (1990). The Sandman, published by DC Comics from 1989 to 1996, became one of the most acclaimed comic series of its era.

Awards

He has won numerous awards, including the Hugo, Nebula, and Bram Stoker awards, and both the Newbery and Carnegie medals — the first author to win both for the same work, The Graveyard Book (2008).
