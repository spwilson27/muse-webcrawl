Twrch Trwyth

The hunt of the Twrch Trwyth is a central episode of Culhwch and Olwen.

Twrch Trwyth (Welsh pronunciation: [tuːrχ trʊɨθ]) is a fabulous wild boar in Welsh mythology, appearing most prominently in the tale of Culhwch and Olwen in the Mabinogion. The boar was once a king who was transformed into a swine because of his sins. Between his ears he carries a comb, a razor, and a pair of scissors which Culhwch must obtain in order to shave Ysbaddaden, the giant father of Olwen, as one of the impossible tasks (anoethau) set before the hero may marry the giant's daughter.

The hunt

Arthur and his warriors pursue the Twrch Trwyth across Ireland, Cornwall, and Wales in one of the longest episodes of the tale. The boar swims the sea with his piglets, devastates regions, and kills many of Arthur's men before the treasures between his ears are seized and he is driven into the sea off Cornwall, his fate left unknown.

Significance

The episode is thought to preserve very old Celtic boar-hunt mythology and possibly memories of a hunting cult; the Twrch Trwyth also appears in other Welsh sources, and the tale is among the earliest Arthurian prose narratives.
