Sender Policy Framework

Sender Policy Framework (SPF) is an email authentication method designed to detect forged sender addresses during the delivery of email. SPF allows the receiving mail server to check that an email claiming to come from a specific domain was submitted by an IP address authorised by that domain's administrators.

Mechanism

A domain publishes an SPF record in its DNS as a TXT record listing the mail servers permitted to send on its behalf. When a message arrives, the recipient's server compares the sending IP with the record and can reject, flag, or accept the message accordingly. SPF was standardised by the IETF in RFC 7208 (2014).

Limitations

SPF authenticates the envelope sender rather than the visible "From" header and breaks under simple forwarding, limitations that led to complementary standards such as DKIM and DMARC.
