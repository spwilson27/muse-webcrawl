DateMarch 24, 2002
VenueKodak Theatre, Hollywood

Most awardsA Beautiful Mind and The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (4 each)

The 74th Academy Awards ceremony, presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, honored the best films of 2001 and took place on March 24, 2002, at the Kodak Theatre in Hollywood, the first ceremony held at that venue. Whoopi Goldberg hosted for the fourth time.

Winners

A Beautiful Mind won Best Picture and Best Director for Ron Howard. Halle Berry won Best Actress for Monster's Ball, becoming the first African-American woman to win in the category, and Denzel Washington won Best Actor for Training Day — the first and so far only ceremony in which both lead acting awards went to Black performers. Sidney Poitier received an Honorary Award the same night.

Other highlights

The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring, the most-nominated film with 13 nominations, won four technical awards. The ceremony introduced the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, won by Shrek. Jennifer Connelly (Best Supporting Actress) and Jim Broadbent (Best Supporting Actor) also won awards.
