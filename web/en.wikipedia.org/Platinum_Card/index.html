The Platinum Card

American Express Platinum Card
IssuerAmerican Express
Introduced1984
TypeCharge card / credit card
Annual fee$895 (US, as of 2025 refresh)

The Platinum Card is a premium charge card issued by American Express. Launched in 1984, it was one of the first "platinum" cards in the payments industry and helped popularise platinum as a tier above gold cards in card marketing.

Features

The card is aimed at affluent travellers and offers benefits such as airport lounge access through the Global Lounge Collection (including Centurion Lounges and Priority Pass), annual airline and hotel credits, elite status with hotel programmes, and premium travel and purchase protections. Since 2017 the US consumer card has been issued as a metal card. Its high annual fee — raised repeatedly over the years — is offset, in the issuer's positioning, by the value of its credits and perks.

History and influence

American Express positioned Platinum above its Gold Card and reserved it initially for long-standing, high-spending customers by invitation. Its success prompted rival issuers to launch competing premium products, and "platinum" became a generic label for top-tier credit cards across the industry. The invitation-only Centurion Card, launched in 1999, sits above it in the American Express range.
