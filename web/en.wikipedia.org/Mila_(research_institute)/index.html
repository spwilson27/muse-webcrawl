Mila (originally the Montreal Institute for Learning Algorithms; French: Institut québécois d'intelligence artificielle) is a non-profit artificial intelligence research institute based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Founded in 1993 by professor Yoshua Bengio at the Université de Montréal, Mila grew out of the university's Laboratoire d'informatique des systèmes adaptatifs and has become one of the world's largest academic research centres devoted to machine learning and deep learning.

Research and community

Mila brings together hundreds of researchers, including faculty and students from the Université de Montréal, McGill University, Polytechnique Montréal, and HEC Montréal, along with industry partners and start-ups housed in its Mile Ex district building. Its research spans fundamental deep learning, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and applications in climate, health, and drug discovery. Mila is one of the three institutes of the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy, alongside Amii in Edmonton and the Vector Institute in Toronto.

AI for social good

The institute has promoted work on the responsible and ethical use of artificial intelligence, including contributions to the Montreal Declaration for a Responsible Development of Artificial Intelligence, and projects applying machine learning to climate change mitigation and public health.
