Libertas (Dubrovnik)

Libertas is a historic symbol and motto of the city of Dubrovnik, Croatia, embodied in the white flag bearing the word LIBERTAS ("freedom" in Latin) that flew over the Republic of Ragusa. The flag was raised above the city, including at Orlando's Column, and proclaimed the republic's supreme value: liberty was not to be sold "for all the gold in the world", in the motto attributed to the Ragusan tradition.

History

The Republic of Ragusa (1358–1808), a maritime city-state, defended its independence between the Ottoman Empire, Venice and the Habsburgs through diplomacy. Slavery was abolished in the republic in 1416, among the earliest such acts in Europe. The white Libertas flag was second in dignity only to the banner of the republic's patron, Saint Blaise.

Modern use

Today the Libertas flag is flown during the Dubrovnik Summer Festival, raised above Orlando's Column, and remains a potent emblem of the city's identity. See also Republic of Ragusa.
