Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park

LocationCanterbury, South Island, New Zealand
Area707 km²
Established1953
Highest pointAoraki / Mount Cook, 3,724 m

Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park is a national park in the Canterbury region of New Zealand's South Island, centred on Aoraki / Mount Cook, the country's highest mountain. The park covers 707 square kilometres of the Southern Alps and forms part of the Te Wahipounamu World Heritage Site.

Geography

The park contains all of New Zealand's peaks over 3,000 metres except Mount Aspiring. Major glaciers include the Tasman Glacier — at about 23 kilometres the country's longest — as well as the Hooker, Mueller, and Murchison glaciers. The village of Mount Cook (Aoraki Village) at the park's edge provides accommodation and services for visitors.

Flora and fauna

Above the tree line, alpine vegetation includes the Mount Cook lily (the world's largest buttercup) and mountain daisies. Birdlife includes the kea, the world's only alpine parrot, and the rock wren. Introduced mammals such as Himalayan tahr are present and are controlled as pests.

Activities

The park is a centre for mountaineering, ski touring, and hiking. Popular walks include the Hooker Valley Track with its swing bridges and views of Aoraki. Sir Edmund Hillary trained in the area, and the Sir Edmund Hillary Alpine Centre stands in the village.
