Siege of Breda (1624)

Date: 28 August 1624 – 5 June 1625
Location: Breda, Netherlands
Result: Spanish victory

The siege of Breda of 1624–25 was a major engagement of the Eighty Years' War in which Spanish forces under Ambrogio Spinola captured the Dutch fortress city of Breda after an eleven-month siege.

Siege

Spinola invested Breda in August 1624, building extensive circumvallation works. Despite relief attempts by Maurice of Nassau and later Frederick Henry, the garrison under Justin of Nassau was starved into surrender. Terms were agreed in June 1625 and the Spanish entered the city honourably.

Legacy

The surrender was celebrated in Spain as a great victory and immortalised by Velázquez in his painting The Surrender of Breda (1634–35). Breda remained Spanish until Frederick Henry recaptured it in 1637.
