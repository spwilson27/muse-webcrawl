The White Lotus is an American black comedy-drama anthology television series created by Mike White for HBO. It follows the guests and employees of the fictional White Lotus resort chain during a week's stay, as a death frames each season and the tensions among the wealthy guests and the staff serving them unravel. The series satirizes the habits, wealth, and privilege of affluent tourists.

Seasons

The first season, set in Hawaii, premiered in July 2021 and starred Murray Bartlett, Connie Britton, Jennifer Coolidge, Alexandra Daddario, Steve Zahn, and Sydney Sweeney. Originally commissioned as a six-episode limited series, its success led HBO to renew it as an anthology. The second season, set in Sicily and premiering in October 2022, brought back Coolidge as Tanya McQuoid alongside new cast members including Aubrey Plaza, Michael Imperioli, F. Murray Abraham, and Theo James. The third season, set in Thailand, premiered in February 2025 with a cast including Walton Goggins, Carrie Coon, Leslie Bibb, Jason Isaacs, and Parker Posey.

Reception

The series has received widespread critical acclaim for its writing, direction, and performances, particularly those of Coolidge and Bartlett. It has won numerous Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Limited or Anthology Series for its first two seasons, and acting awards for Coolidge and Bartlett. Each season opens with a body being discovered, with the identity of the deceased withheld until the finale, structuring the satire as a slow-burning mystery.
