UNHCR

The United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) is a United Nations agency mandated to aid and protect refugees, forcibly displaced communities, and stateless people, and to assist in their voluntary repatriation, local integration or resettlement.

UNHCR
Founded14 December 1950
HeadquartersGeneva, Switzerland
High CommissionerFilippo Grandi (from 2016)
AwardsNobel Peace Prize 1954, 1981

Mandate

Created after the Second World War, UNHCR's work is based on the 1951 Refugee Convention. By the mid-2020s over 110 million people were forcibly displaced worldwide, driven by wars in Syria, Ukraine, Sudan and elsewhere.

Operations

UNHCR provides shelter, food partnerships with the World Food Programme, legal protection, and the principle of non-refoulement.
