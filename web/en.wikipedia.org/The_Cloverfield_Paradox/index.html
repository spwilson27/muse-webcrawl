The Cloverfield Paradox is a 2018 American science fiction horror film directed by Julius Onah and written by Oren Uziel, from a story by Uziel and Doug Jung. It is the third installment in the Cloverfield franchise, following Cloverfield (2008) and 10 Cloverfield Lane (2016).

Plot

In 2028, Earth faces a severe energy crisis. Aboard the orbiting Cloverfield Station, an international crew attempts to fire the Shepard particle accelerator to provide unlimited energy. Instead, the experiment tears a hole in spacetime, shifting the station into a parallel dimension and unleashing strange phenomena — and, implicitly, scattering the monsters of the earlier films across multiple timelines. Communications specialist Ava Hamilton must choose between returning to her own Earth or remaining in a dimension where her children, who died in a fire in her timeline, are still alive.

Production and release

The film was originally developed as a standalone project titled God Particle under Paramount Pictures. After repeated delays, Paramount sold distribution rights to Netflix. In a surprise marketing move, the film was announced during Super Bowl LII on February 4, 2018, and released on Netflix immediately after the game.

Reception

The film received generally negative reviews from critics, who praised the cast and visuals but criticised the convoluted script and its loose connection to the franchise; it holds a low approval rating on review aggregators.
