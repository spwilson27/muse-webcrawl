Sinkhole

TypeGeological depression
Caused byDissolution of soluble bedrock; collapse of cave roofs; subsurface erosion
Common regionsFlorida, Yucatán Peninsula, China, Turkey, Croatia

A sinkhole is a depression or hole in the ground caused by some form of collapse of the surface layer. Most are formed by karst processes — the chemical dissolution of carbonate rocks such as limestone and dolomite — or by suffosion processes in which subsurface material is gradually washed away.

Sinkholes vary in size from less than a metre to more than 600 metres (2,000 ft) in both diameter and depth. They may form gradually over long periods or appear suddenly, sometimes with catastrophic consequences for buildings and infrastructure.

Formation

Sinkholes are most common where the rock below the land surface is limestone, carbonate rock, salt beds, or other rock that can be dissolved by groundwater circulating through it. Rainwater, made slightly acidic by dissolved carbon dioxide, percolates through joints and fractures and slowly enlarges them into caverns. When a cavern roof can no longer support the weight of the material above it, the surface collapses.

Human activities can accelerate sinkhole formation. Groundwater pumping, drilling, construction, leaking water pipes, and changes to natural drainage patterns have all been implicated in sudden collapses.

Types

Geologists commonly distinguish dissolution sinkholes, formed by slow surface lowering; cover-subsidence sinkholes, which develop gradually as sediment sags into underlying cavities; and cover-collapse sinkholes, in which the surface drops suddenly. Sinkholes whose bottoms open into caves are sometimes called cenotes in the Yucatán, where they were sacred to the Maya.

Notable sinkholes

The Xiaozhai Tiankeng in Chongqing, China, is among the largest known sinkholes, roughly 662 metres deep. In 2010, a sinkhole in Guatemala City swallowed a section of a neighbourhood, an event widely reported internationally. Florida's geology makes it the most sinkhole-prone state in the United States.
