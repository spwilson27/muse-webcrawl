Reform Party of Canada

The Reform Party of Canada (French: Parti réformiste du Canada) was a federal political party in Canada that existed from 1987 to 2000. Founded in Winnipeg by Preston Manning and others, it grew out of western Canadian discontent with the Progressive Conservative government of Brian Mulroney.

Rise

Advocating fiscal conservatism, Senate reform (a "Triple-E" elected, equal, effective Senate), and populist democratic reforms under the slogan "The West wants in", Reform won 52 seats in the 1993 election and became the Official Opposition in 1997 with 60 seats, though its support remained concentrated in the West.

Succession

In 2000 the party reorganised as the Canadian Alliance in an attempt to broaden its appeal; the Alliance merged with the Progressive Conservatives in 2003 to form the modern Conservative Party of Canada, led to power by former Reform activist Stephen Harper.
