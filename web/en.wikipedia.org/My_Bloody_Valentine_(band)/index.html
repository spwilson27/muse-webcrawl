My Bloody Valentine (band)

My Bloody Valentine are an Irish-English rock band formed in Dublin in 1983 and based in London. Since 1987, the band's lineup has consisted of founding members Kevin Shields (vocals, guitar, sampler) and Colm Ó Cíosóig (drums, sampler), with Bilinda Butcher (vocals, guitar) and Debbie Googe (bass). Their music is characterised by dense layers of distorted, pitch-bent guitar, ethereal vocals and unorthodox production techniques, and they were a formative influence on the style known as shoegaze. Their second album, Loveless (1991), is widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time.

History

Formation and early releases (1983–1987)

Shields and Ó Cíosóig formed the band in Dublin in 1983, taking the name from the 1981 Canadian slasher film. After relocating to London and several lineup changes, the band released a series of mini-LPs and EPs on independent labels. With the arrival of Butcher and Googe in 1987 and the EPs Strawberry Wine and Ecstasy, their sound began to crystallise.

Isn't Anything and Loveless (1988–1991)

Signing to Creation Records, the band released the EP You Made Me Realise (1988) and their debut album Isn't Anything (1988), a landmark of the emerging shoegaze sound. Its follow-up, Loveless, was recorded over two years in numerous studios and reportedly cost around £250,000, nearly bankrupting Creation. Shields achieved the album's distinctive sound through "glide guitar" — strumming while manipulating the tremolo arm — layered samples and heavily treated vocals woven into the instrumental texture. Released in November 1991, it received enthusiastic reviews and has since topped many critics' lists of the best albums ever made.

Hiatus and return (1993–present)

A difficult third album for Island Records never materialised, and Shields withdrew from public life, though he contributed to other artists and composed music for the film Lost in Translation (2003). The band reunited for live performances in 2007–2008 and toured widely. In February 2013 My Bloody Valentine self-released their third album, m b v, to critical acclaim, and remastered editions of their catalogue followed in 2021 on Domino Records.

Legacy and influence

My Bloody Valentine's innovations — sustained volume, texture over melody, and the subversion of conventional guitar-rock — influenced generations of alternative musicians, from Radiohead and the Smashing Pumpkins to countless shoegaze and dream pop acts.
