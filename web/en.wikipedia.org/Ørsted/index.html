Ørsted

Ørsted A/S is a Danish multinational energy company headquartered in Fredericia, Denmark. It is the world's largest developer of offshore wind power, and it has transformed itself from a fossil-fuel-based utility — formerly known as DONG Energy — into one of the world's most sustainable energy companies.

History

The company originated in 1972 as a state-owned enterprise responsible for managing Danish oil and gas resources in the North Sea. As DONG Energy (an acronym of Danish Oil and Natural Gas) it expanded into electricity generation and distribution. In 2017 the company sold its oil and gas business and renamed itself Ørsted, after the Danish physicist Hans Christian Ørsted, discoverer of electromagnetism, reflecting its strategic pivot to renewable energy.

Operations

Ørsted develops, constructs, and operates offshore and onshore wind farms, solar farms, energy storage facilities, and bioenergy plants. It built many landmark projects, including Hornsea 1 and Hornsea 2 in the United Kingdom — among the largest offshore wind farms in the world — as well as major projects off the coasts of Germany, the Netherlands, Taiwan, and the United States. The Danish state remains the majority shareholder.
