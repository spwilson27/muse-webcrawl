Kilimanoor Palace

Kilimanoor Palace is a palace complex at Kilimanoor, in the Thiruvananthapuram district of Kerala, India. It is the birthplace of the celebrated painter Raja Ravi Varma (1848–1906) and is associated with the royal house of Kilimanoor, whose chiefs were closely connected with the Travancore royal family.

History

The estate passed to the Maharaja of Travancore, Marthanda Varma, in the 18th century through adoption arrangements with the Kilimanoor family. The palace complex covers several acres and includes traditional Kerala-style buildings, ponds, and groves. Raja Ravi Varma was born here in 1848 and spent his early years at the palace before becoming India's most famous academic painter.

A studio used by the painter and parts of the complex are maintained as heritage structures, and the palace remains a point of cultural interest in the region.
