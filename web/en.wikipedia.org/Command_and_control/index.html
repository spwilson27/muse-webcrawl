Command and control

Command and control (often abbreviated C2) is the exercise of authority and direction by a properly designated commander over assigned forces in accomplishing a mission. It encompasses the personnel, procedures, communications, and facilities by which a commander plans, directs, coordinates, and controls operations.

Concepts

Effective command and control provides unity of effort, timely decisions, and situational awareness. Modern doctrine distinguishes the art of command — the creative use of authority, leadership, and judgement — from the science of control, the systems and processes that monitor execution and feed information back to the commander.

Modern developments

Digital networks have transformed C2 through concepts such as network-centric warfare, in which sensors, headquarters, and weapons are linked to share a common operational picture. Extended terms include C3 (adding communications), C4 (adding computers), and C4ISR, which additionally covers intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance. The term "command and control" is also used in cybersecurity for the servers that direct botnets and malware.
