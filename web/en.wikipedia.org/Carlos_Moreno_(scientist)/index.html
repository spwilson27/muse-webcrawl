Carlos Moreno (born 16 April 1959) is a Franco-Colombian urbanist, scientist, and professor at Panthéon-Sorbonne University in Paris. He is best known for originating the concept of the "15-minute city", in which residents can access daily necessities and services within a short walk or bike ride of their homes.

Career

Moreno's research focuses on complex systems, smart and sustainable cities, and the use of technology to improve urban quality of life. He advised Paris mayor Anne Hidalgo, whose 2020 re-election campaign incorporated the 15-minute city, and his ideas influenced urban policies in cities worldwide during and after the COVID-19 pandemic, when local accessibility gained new prominence.

Reception

The 15-minute city concept has been adopted or endorsed by networks such as C40 Cities, but has also attracted controversy, including conspiracy theories falsely claiming it would confine residents to districts. Moreno received the OBEL Award in 2021 for his contribution to sustainable urbanism.
