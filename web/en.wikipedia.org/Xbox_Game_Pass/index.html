Xbox Game Pass

Xbox Game Pass is a subscription service from Microsoft Gaming that provides access to a rotating catalog of video games from Microsoft's Xbox Game Studios, Bethesda Softworks, Activision Blizzard, EA Play, and third-party publishers. Announced in February 2017 and launched on 1 June 2017, the service is available on Xbox consoles and Windows PCs.

The service is frequently described as a "Netflix for games." Subscribers pay a monthly fee to download or stream games from the library for as long as the games remain in the catalog and the subscription is active. Titles published by Xbox Game Studios are typically added on the day of their release ("day one"), a key selling point of the service.

History

Game Pass grew out of Microsoft's experimentation with game rental and digital distribution in the Xbox 360 era. Under Xbox chief Phil Spencer, the service became central to Microsoft's platform strategy, shifting the emphasis from console unit sales to engagement across devices. The 2020 launch of cloud gaming allowed subscribers to stream games to phones, tablets, browsers, and smart TVs.

By January 2022 the service reported more than 25 million subscribers; Microsoft reported 34 million in early 2024, though that figure included the rebranded Core tier. Price increases and tier restructuring followed in 2024. Game Pass releases have included major titles such as Starfield, Forza Motorsport, and Call of Duty: Black Ops 6, the first Call of Duty title to launch into the service following Microsoft's acquisition of Activision Blizzard in October 2023.

Reception

The service is widely praised as one of the best values in gaming for consumers, while critics and some publishers have debated its effect on individual game sales and the long-term sustainability of the model.
