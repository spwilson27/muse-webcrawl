University of Nairobi

Type: Public research university
Established: 1956
Location: Nairobi, Kenya
Students: ~80,000

The University of Nairobi (UoN) is a collegiate research university in Nairobi, Kenya. It began in 1956 as the Royal Technical College of East Africa, became University College Nairobi within the University of East Africa in 1963, and was established as an independent university in 1970. It is Kenya's largest and most prestigious university.

Structure

The university operates faculties of agriculture, arts and social sciences, business and management sciences, built environment and design, education, engineering, health sciences, law, science and technology, and veterinary medicine, spread over several campuses in and around Nairobi. Its teaching hospital is Kenyatta National Hospital.

People

Alumni include Nobel Peace Prize laureate Wangari Maathai, who taught in its veterinary faculty, and numerous Kenyan political leaders. The university is regularly ranked first in Kenya and among the top universities in Africa.
