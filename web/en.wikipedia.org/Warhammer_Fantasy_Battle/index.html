Warhammer Fantasy Battle

PublisherGames Workshop
First released1983
GenreMiniature wargame

Warhammer Fantasy Battle (often abbreviated WFB) was a tabletop miniature wargame created by Games Workshop, set in the Warhammer Fantasy fictional universe. First published in 1983, it ran through eight editions before being discontinued in 2015 and replaced by Warhammer Age of Sigmar.

Gameplay

Two or more players commanded armies of miniatures representing regiments of infantry, cavalry, monsters and war machines. Units were arranged in ranked formations, and combat resolved with dice rolls modified by unit statistics. Points values balanced opposing forces.

Setting

The game was set in the Old World and beyond, a grim fantasy world beset by the forces of Chaos. Playable factions included the Empire, Bretonnia, Dwarfs, High Elves, Orcs and Goblins, Skaven, Vampire Counts, and the Warriors of Chaos.

Legacy

The setting lived on in video games such as the Total War: Warhammer series. In 2024 Games Workshop revived the setting as Warhammer: The Old World.
