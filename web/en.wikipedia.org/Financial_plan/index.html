Financial plan

A financial plan is a comprehensive statement of an individual's or organisation's long-term monetary goals and the strategies to achieve them. It typically evaluates current income, assets, debts and expenses, and maps out saving, investing, insurance, tax, retirement and estate arrangements.

Components

Standard elements include a budget and cash-flow statement, a net-worth statement, investment policy, risk-management and insurance review, retirement projections, tax planning and estate documents such as wills and powers of attorney.

Process

Financial planners generally follow a cycle: gather data, analyse the current position, develop recommendations, implement, and monitor periodically. In many jurisdictions the title "financial planner" is regulated, and certifications such as CFP (Certified Financial Planner) denote professional standards.

Corporate use

Businesses also produce financial plans as part of business plans, covering projected income statements, balance sheets and cash-flow forecasts used to raise capital and guide operations.
