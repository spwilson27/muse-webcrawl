Mario is a character created by the Japanese game designer Shigeru Miyamoto and the mascot of Nintendo. A plumber from the Mushroom Kingdom (originating in Brooklyn in early media), Mario stars in the Super Mario video game franchise, the best-selling video game franchise of all time, with over 430 million copies sold across the platforming series alone. He wears a red cap and shirt, blue overalls, white gloves, and brown shoes, and is known for jumping on enemies, collecting coins, and rescuing Princess Peach from the villain Bowser.


Mario, Nintendo's mascot

Creation and early appearances

Mario debuted in the 1981 arcade game Donkey Kong as "Jumpman," a carpenter rescuing his girlfriend from the ape Donkey Kong. He was named after Mario Segale, the landlord of Nintendo of America's warehouse. Because of hardware limitations, Miyamoto gave him a cap instead of hair and a mustache instead of a mouth. Mario appeared as the antagonist's captive-rescuer again in Donkey Kong Jr. (1982) and co-starred with his brother Luigi in Mario Bros. (1983), where the brothers became plumbers.

Super Mario series

Super Mario Bros. (1985) for the Nintendo Entertainment System established the side-scrolling platform formula and helped revive the video game industry after the crash of 1983. Landmark entries include Super Mario Bros. 3 (1988), Super Mario World (1990), which introduced Yoshi, and Super Mario 64 (1996), a pioneering 3D platformer. Later games include Super Mario Sunshine, the New Super Mario Bros. series, Super Mario Galaxy (2007), Super Mario Odyssey (2017), and Super Mario Bros. Wonder (2023).

Other media and legacy

Mario headlines spin-off series including Mario Kart, Mario Party, sports and role-playing games, making him one of the most prolific characters in gaming; by the 2010s he had appeared in over 200 games. Charles Martinet voiced Mario from the 1990s until 2023. Mario has appeared in comics, animated series, a 1993 live-action film, and the animated The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023), one of the highest-grossing animated films ever. He is widely regarded as the most famous video game character in the world and an icon of popular culture.
