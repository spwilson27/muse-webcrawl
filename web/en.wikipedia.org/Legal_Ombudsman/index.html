Legal Ombudsman

The Legal Ombudsman is an independent complaints-handling body for legal services in England and Wales, established under the Legal Services Act 2007 and opened in October 2010.

Role

Run by the Office for Legal Complaints, it investigates complaints from members of the public about solicitors, barristers, legal executives and other authorised legal providers — for example poor service, overcharging or failure to follow instructions — after the provider has had a chance to resolve the complaint. It can order apologies, refunds, compensation and remedial action.
