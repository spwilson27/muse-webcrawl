Ducati Panigale

Panigale is a family of sports motorcycles manufactured by Ducati since 2011. The name comes from the small manufacturing town of Borgo Panigale in Bologna, Italy, where Ducati is headquartered. The Panigale line replaced Ducati's earlier 1198 superbike family and introduced a frameless design in which the engine serves as a stressed member of the chassis.

Models

The first model, the 1199 Panigale (2011), used the new Superquadro desmodromic V-twin, an extreme oversquare design producing about 195 hp. It was joined by the smaller 899 Panigale and succeeded by the 1299 Panigale. In 2018 Ducati introduced the Panigale V4, powered by the 1,103 cc Desmosedici Stradale V4 derived from its MotoGP programme — the company's first mass-produced four-cylinder motorcycle — followed by the V4 R homologation special and the middleweight Panigale V2 with the 955 cc Superquadro twin.

Technology

Panigale models feature advanced electronics suites including cornering ABS, traction and wheelie control, multiple riding modes and semi-active Öhlins suspension on higher trims, managed through an inertial measurement unit. The monocoque front frame attaches directly to the engine's cylinder heads, reducing weight and parts count compared with Ducati's traditional steel trellis frames.
