Loveless is the second studio album by the Irish-English rock band My Bloody Valentine, released on 4 November 1991 by Creation Records. The album was recorded over two years in as many as nineteen studios, with vocalist and guitarist Kevin Shields dominating the process and reportedly costing the label some £250,000 — a figure that contributed to Creation's financial difficulties, though Shields has disputed the amount.

Music

Loveless is defined by Shields's "glide guitar" technique — strumming while manipulating the tremolo arm — together with heavy sampling, studio manipulation, androgynous and nearly unintelligible vocals buried in the mix, and rhythms sampled from drummer Colm Ó Cíosóig's playing. Tracks such as "Only Shallow", "When You Sleep", and "Soon" merge pop melodies with dense washes of distorted guitar texture. The album's closing track, "Soon", became an unlikely dancefloor hit.

Reception and legacy

Although initial sales were modest, Loveless is now routinely ranked among the greatest albums of all time and is considered the pinnacle of the shoegaze genre. Critics praised its sonic innovation and otherworldly atmosphere; its influence extends across alternative rock, dream pop, and electronic music. The band did not release a follow-up until m b v in 2013.
