Prime Directive

In the fictional universe of Star Trek, the Prime Directive (also known as Starfleet General Order 1) is a guiding principle of the United Federation of Planets prohibiting Starfleet personnel and spacecraft from interfering with the natural development of alien civilizations that have not yet developed faster-than-light travel.

Concept

The directive protects pre-warp cultures from contamination by advanced technology and ideas, forbidding contact, revelation of the Federation's existence, and intervention in internal affairs. Starfleet officers swear an oath to uphold it, even at the cost of their lives or ships.

In the franchise

Introduced in Star Trek: The Original Series, the Prime Directive is a recurring source of moral dilemmas, as captains such as James T. Kirk and Jean-Luc Picard weigh orders against their conscience. Commentators have compared it to real-world debates about colonialism, non-intervention, and anthropology's ethics of non-contact.
