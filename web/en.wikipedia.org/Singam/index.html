Singam (transl. Lion) is a 2010 Indian Tamil-language action film written and directed by Hari and produced by K. E. Gnanavel Raja. It stars Suriya as Durai Singam, an honest and hot-headed police inspector in a small town in Thoothukudi district, alongside Anushka Shetty and Prakash Raj as the antagonist, the extortionist Mayil Vaaganam.

Plot

Durai Singam, a sub-inspector in the village of Nallur, resolves disputes through compromise rather than force. When he is forced to grant conditional bail to the Chennai-based criminal Mayil Vaaganam, humiliation drives the gangster into a vendetta that follows Singam after his promotion to Chennai, where he confronts the criminal's extortion and smuggling empire.

Reception and franchise

The film was a major commercial success and became one of the highest-grossing Tamil films of 2010. It launched the Singam franchise, followed by the sequels Singam II (2013) and Si3 (2017), and was remade in Hindi as Singham (2011) with Ajay Devgn, as well as in Kannada and Bengali.
