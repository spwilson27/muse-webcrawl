Captain America

Captain America
Alter ego: Steve Rogers
First appearance: Captain America Comics #1 (1941)
Created by: Joe Simon, Jack Kirby
Publisher: Marvel Comics
Abilities: Enhanced strength, speed and endurance via the Super Soldier Serum; indestructible vibranium-steel shield

Captain America is a superhero created by Joe Simon and Jack Kirby, first appearing in Captain America Comics #1 (cover-dated March 1941) from Timely Comics, a predecessor of Marvel Comics. His alter ego is Steve Rogers, a frail young artist from Brooklyn who is enhanced to the peak of human physical perfection by an experimental "Super Soldier Serum" and fights the Axis powers during World War II wearing a flag-motif costume and wielding a shield.

Fictional biography

Rejected for military service because of his health, Rogers volunteers for Project Rebirth and is transformed by Dr. Abraham Erskine's serum and "vita-rays". After fighting Nazi agents (including his nemesis the Red Skull) and serving alongside his sidekick Bucky Barnes, Rogers is frozen in ice near the end of the war and revived decades later in the modern era, where he becomes a leader of the Avengers — "a man out of time."

Publication history

Captain America was among the most popular characters of the 1940s Golden Age. His wartime series was cancelled in 1950, briefly revived by Atlas Comics, and successfully relaunched by Stan Lee and Kirby in The Avengers #4 (1964) with the frozen-in-ice retcon. Subsequent acclaimed runs include those by Steve Englehart, Mark Gruenwald, Mark Waid, and Ed Brubaker, whose run reintroduced Bucky Barnes as the Winter Soldier.

In other media

In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Steve Rogers is portrayed by Chris Evans in films including Captain America: The First Avenger (2011), The Winter Soldier (2014), and Civil War (2016); at the conclusion of Avengers: Endgame (2019) he passes the shield to Sam Wilson, who assumes the Captain America mantle in The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021) and later films.
