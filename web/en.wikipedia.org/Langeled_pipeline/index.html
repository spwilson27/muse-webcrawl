The Langeled pipeline, originally known as Britpipe, is an underwater natural gas pipeline connecting Norway to the United Kingdom. Running from the Nyhamna terminal on the Norwegian coast to Easington in England, it was the world's longest subsea pipeline on completion, at about 1,166 kilometres.

Construction

The pipeline was built to carry gas from the Ormen Lange field. Pipe sections were welded at spoolbases and laid across the challenging Norwegian Trench, requiring extensive seabed preparation.

Operation

Gas began flowing in 2006–2007. Langeled can supply a significant share of British gas demand, and flows can be influenced by the Sleipner hub in the North Sea.
