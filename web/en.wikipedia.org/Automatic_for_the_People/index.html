Automatic for the People

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Cover of the 1992 album

Automatic for the People is the eighth studio album by American rock band R.E.M., released on October 5, 1992, by Warner Bros. Records. The album yielded the hit singles "Drive", "Everybody Hurts", "Man on the Moon", "The Sidewinder Sleeps Tonite", "Nightswimming", and "Find the River".

Background and recording

After the international success of Out of Time (1991), R.E.M. set out to make a harder-rocking record but instead produced a slower, somber album preoccupied with mortality and loss. Sessions took place in several cities, with string arrangements by former Led Zeppelin bassist John Paul Jones on several tracks. The title comes from the motto of Weaver D's, a soul food restaurant in Athens, Georgia.

Reception and legacy

The album topped the charts in the United Kingdom and reached number two in the United States, and has sold more than 18 million copies worldwide. "Man on the Moon" is a tribute to comedian Andy Kaufman; "Everybody Hurts" became one of the band's signature songs. Automatic for the People regularly appears on lists of the greatest albums of all time, and a 25th-anniversary reissue appeared in 2017.

Track listing

All songs written by Bill Berry, Peter Buck, Mike Mills, and Michael Stipe: "Drive", "Try Not to Breathe", "The Sidewinder Sleeps Tonite", "Everybody Hurts", "New Orleans Instrumental No. 1", "Sweetness Follows", "Monty Got a Raw Deal", "Ignoreland", "Star Me Kitten", "Man on the Moon", "Nightswimming", "Find the River".
