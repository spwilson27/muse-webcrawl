Numerical optimization

Numerical optimization is the computational study of methods for finding minima and maxima of functions, often subject to constraints, used when analytical solutions are unavailable. It is a core tool in engineering design, economics, operations research and machine learning.

Methods

Unconstrained problems are commonly solved with gradient descent, Newton's method and quasi-Newton methods such as BFGS, which build approximations to the Hessian matrix. Constrained problems use techniques including penalty and barrier methods, the simplex method and interior-point methods for linear programming, and sequential quadratic programming for nonlinear problems.

Modern applications

Stochastic gradient descent and variants such as Adam drive the training of large neural networks. Because many numerical optimisation problems are non-convex, practice relies on heuristics, restarts and careful initialisation, and global optimisation remains an active research area.
