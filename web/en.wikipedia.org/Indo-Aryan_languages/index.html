Indo-Aryan languages
Family: Indo-European, Indo-Iranian
Languages: ~200
Speakers: over 1.5 billion
Examples: Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Marathi, Sinhala

The Indo-Aryan languages, or Indic languages, are a branch of the Indo-Iranian branch of the Indo-European language family spoken mainly in northern and central South Asia.

History

Old Indo-Aryan is represented by Vedic and Classical Sanskrit; Middle Indo-Aryan by Prakrits and Pali (the language of early Buddhist texts associated with the Bodhi Tree tradition and Emperor Ashoka's era), and New Indo-Aryan by modern languages from about 1000 CE.

Distribution

Hindi-Urdu, Bengali and Punjabi are among the world's most spoken languages. Sinhala in Sri Lanka and Dhivehi in the Maldives form a southern island branch. Most are written in Brahmic scripts such as Devanagari and Bengali script.
