FC Barcelona Bàsquet

FC Barcelona Bàsquet, commonly known as Barça, is a Spanish professional basketball club based in Barcelona, Catalonia. Founded in 1926, it is the basketball section of the multi-sports club FC Barcelona and plays in the Liga ACB and the EuroLeague. Its home arena is the Palau Blaugrana.

The Palau Blaugrana, home of FC Barcelona Bàsquet

History

The club is one of the most successful in Spanish and European basketball. Domestically it has won numerous Liga ACB championships and Copa del Rey titles, traditionally battling its arch-rival Real Madrid in Europe's fiercest basketball rivalry. In European competition, Barça won the EuroLeague in 2003 — completing a treble under coach Svetislav Pešić with stars including Dejan Bodiroga — and again in 2010 under Xavi Pascual, led by Juan Carlos Navarro, the club's all-time emblematic player.

Notable players

Legendary players associated with the club include Juan Antonio San Epifanio ("Epi"), Audie Norris, Juan Carlos Navarro, and Pau Gasol, who began his professional career at Barcelona before moving to the NBA. The section has also developed many players through its youth academy.
