Dead Kennedys

Dead Kennedys are an American punk rock band formed in San Francisco in 1978, among the first American hardcore bands and known for frontman Jello Biafra's satirical, politically charged lyrics targeting authority, consumerism and the religious right.

Dead Kennedys
Origin: San Francisco, California
Years active: 1978–1986; 2001–present (without Biafra)
Genres: Hardcore punk, punk rock
Label: Alternative Tentacles
Key members: Jello Biafra, East Bay Ray, Klaus Flouride, D.H. Peligro

History

The classic lineup—Biafra (Eric Boucher), guitarist East Bay Ray, bassist Klaus Flouride and drummer D.H. Peligro (from 1981)—released singles "California Über Alles" (1979) and "Holiday in Cambodia" (1980), and debut album Fresh Fruit for Rotting Vegetables (1980). Later albums included Plastic Surgery Disasters (1982) and Frankenchrist (1985).

Obscenity trial and breakup

Frankenchrist included H.R. Giger's poster "Penis Landscape", leading to a 1986 obscenity prosecution of Biafra and others; the jury deadlocked and charges were dropped, but legal costs contributed to the band's 1986 breakup. Biafra ran Alternative Tentacles and became a spoken-word artist and Green Party activist. The other members reformed Dead Kennedys in 2001 with replacement singers after a royalty lawsuit against Biafra, a split that remains contentious with fans.

Legacy

Songs such as "Kill the Poor", "Nazi Punks Fuck Off" and "Too Drunk to Fuck" remain punk standards, and the band is credited with shaping hardcore's speed and political edge.
