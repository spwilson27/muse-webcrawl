Noxious weed

A noxious weed is a weed that a government authority has designated as injurious to agriculture, natural habitats, ecosystems or humans, and whose control is legally required or encouraged.

Control

Lists vary by jurisdiction. Examples in North America include kudzu, leafy spurge, Canada thistle and giant hogweed. Control methods include mechanical removal, herbicides, grazing and biological control with introduced insects or pathogens.
