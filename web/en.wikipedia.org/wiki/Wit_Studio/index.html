Wit Studio

Wit Studio, Inc. (Japanese: 株式会社ウィットスタジオ) is a Japanese animation studio founded on June 1, 2012, by producers from Production I.G, including George Wada, who became its president. Headquartered in Musashino, Tokyo, the studio is a subsidiary of IG Port and is best known for producing the first three seasons of Attack on Titan.

History and works

Wit Studio's debut television series, Attack on Titan (2013–2019), became an international phenomenon. Other notable productions include Seraph of the End (2015), The Ancient Magus' Bride (2017), Vinland Saga (2019), Great Pretender (2020), Ranking of Kings (2021), and Spy × Family (2022, co-produced with CloverWorks). Its feature films include The Empire of Corpses (2015) and Bubble (2022).

Style and reputation

The studio is praised for detailed background art, expressive character animation, and ambitious action sequences. In 2024 Wit released Grimm Variations for Netflix and announced a remake of the One Piece anime, titled The One Piece, in collaboration with Netflix and Toei Animation.
