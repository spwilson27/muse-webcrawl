National Slate Museum

The National Slate Museum (Welsh: Amgueddfa Lechi Cymru) is a museum in Llanberis, Gwynedd, Wales, part of Amgueddfa Cymru – Museum Wales. It is sited in the Victorian workshops of the former Dinorwic slate quarry, in Padarn Country Park on the shore of Llyn Padarn, beneath the quarry workings on Elidir Fawr.

History

The Dinorwic quarry was once one of the two largest slate quarries in the world, employing more than 3,000 men at its peak in the late 19th century. The Gilfach Ddu workshops, built in 1870, served the quarry's locomotives and machinery and include one of the largest waterwheels in Britain. The quarry closed in 1969; the workshops were preserved and opened as the North Wales Quarrying Museum in 1972, later becoming the Welsh Slate Museum and then the National Slate Museum.

Displays

The museum presents demonstrations of slate splitting and dressing by craftsmen, the working waterwheel and foundry, locomotive sheds, and a row of quarrymen's cottages relocated from Tanygrisiau and furnished to represent periods from 1861 to 1969. Exhibits explain the Welsh slate industry, which roofed much of the industrial world and was inscribed in 2021 as part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site "The Slate Landscape of Northwest Wales".
