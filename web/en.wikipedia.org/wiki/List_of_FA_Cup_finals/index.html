List of FA Cup finals

The FA Cup (Football Association Challenge Cup) is an annual knockout football competition in English football, first played during the 1871–72 season. It is the world's oldest national football competition. The final has been played at Wembley Stadium in London since 1923 (and at the new Wembley since 2007), except for the years 2001–2006, when it was held at the Millennium Stadium in Cardiff while Wembley was rebuilt.

History

The Wanderers won the first final in 1872, beating the Royal Engineers 1–0 at the Kennington Oval. Arsenal are the most successful club in the competition's history, with 14 victories. Manchester United have won 13 times, while Chelsea, Liverpool, and Tottenham Hotspur have each won eight times. Remarkably, only one final has ever been contested by two clubs from outside England: Cardiff City beat Arsenal in 1927 to become the only non-English club to win the cup.

The 1953 final, in which 38-year-old Stanley Matthews finally won a winner's medal with Blackpool, is remembered as the "Matthews Final". The complete list records every final since 1872, including wartime interruptions (1916–1919 and 1940–1945), scorelines, venues, and attendances.
