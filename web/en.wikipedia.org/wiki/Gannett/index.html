Gannett Co., Inc. is the largest newspaper publisher in the United States by circulation, owner of USA Today and hundreds of local newspapers through the USA Today Network. Founded by Frank Gannett in 1923 and long headquartered in McLean, Virginia, it merged in 2019 with GateHouse Media's parent, New Media Investment Group, keeping the Gannett name.

History

Frank Gannett built a New York newspaper chain from 1906-1923 into a national group. The company launched USA Today in 1982 — a colourful national paper that reshaped American newspaper design — and expanded through decades of acquisitions.

Digital transition

Like the industry, Gannett suffered steep classified and print-advertising losses; it spun off its broadcasting arm as Tegna in 2015 and bought digital-marketing firms to diversify.

The 2019 merger

The GateHouse merger created a chain of more than 250 dailies; cost-cutting and newsroom reductions followed, making Gannett a central case in debates about chain ownership and local-news decline.
