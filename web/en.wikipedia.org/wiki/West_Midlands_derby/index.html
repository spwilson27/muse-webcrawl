West Midlands derby

The West Midlands derby is a general term for a football match between teams from the West Midlands region of England. The principal clubs involved are Aston Villa, Birmingham City, West Bromwich Albion, Wolverhampton Wanderers, Coventry City and Walsall.

Principal fixtures

The most intense individual rivalries within the region carry their own names. The Second City derby between Aston Villa and Birmingham City is generally regarded as the fiercest, while the Black Country derby between West Bromwich Albion and Wolverhampton Wanderers is among the oldest derbies in English football, first played in 1886.

History

The clubs were founding forces in early English football: Aston Villa and West Bromwich Albion were founder members of the Football League in 1888, and the region's teams contested several early FA Cup finals. Meetings between the clubs depend on league status, and in recent decades the sides have frequently been in different divisions, making some derbies intermittent.
