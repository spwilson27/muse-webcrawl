Snapsvisa

A snapsvisa (plural snapsvisor) is a traditional Swedish drinking song, sung when toasting with snaps (aquavit or other spirits) at festive gatherings, especially at midsummer feasts, crayfish parties (kräftskiva) and Christmas smörgåsbord (julbord).

Tradition

The songs are usually short, cheerful and humorous, often set to familiar folk or popular melodies with new comic lyrics about drinking, friendship and the effects of the snaps itself. A toastmaster announces the song, the company sings together, drinks, and often follows with calls and cheers. The most famous snapsvisa is "Helan går", which opens many Swedish celebrations; its title ("the whole thing goes down") refers to the first full glass of a sequence named Helan, Halvan, Tersen and so on.

Cultural role

Writing new snapsvisor is a popular pastime, and Swedish organisations and the Museum of Spirits in Stockholm hold annual championships for newly written songs.
