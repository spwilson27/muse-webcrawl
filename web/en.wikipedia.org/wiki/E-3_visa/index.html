E-3 visa

E-3 visa
United States nonimmigrant visa
Eligible nationals: Australia
Occupation: Specialty occupation

The E-3 visa is a United States visa for Australian nationals who wish to work in the United States in a specialty occupation. It was created by the Emergency Supplemental Appropriations Act for Defense, the Global War on Terror, and Tsunami Relief in 2005.

Background

The E-3 category was established in part as a consolation to Australia after the Australia–United States Free Trade Agreement did not include a broader professional visa chapter comparable to those negotiated by Chile and Singapore. It is modelled on the H-1B visa but is exclusive to Australians.

Eligibility and terms

Applicants must have a legitimate offer of employment in a specialty occupation requiring at least a bachelor's degree or its equivalent. Up to 10,500 E-3 visas are available each fiscal year, a cap that has never been reached. The visa is granted for two years and is renewable indefinitely. Spouses may accompany the principal holder on E-3D status and may apply for employment authorisation. Unlike the H-1B, the E-3 requires no petition to USCIS in advance and can be applied for directly at a U.S. consulate.
