Essos

Essos is a fictional continent in George R. R. Martin's A Song of Ice and Fire novels and its television adaptation Game of Thrones. Lying east of Westeros across the Narrow Sea, it is the larger of the two main continents of the known world, stretching from the Free Cities in the west to the Jade Sea and Asshai in the far east.

Geography and peoples

Western Essos is dominated by the nine Free Cities — Braavos, Pentos, Volantis and others — merchant republics colonised by Valyria. Inland lies the Dothraki Sea, grasslands roamed by nomadic horse-lords, the Dothraki. To the south-east, Slaver's Bay cities Astapor, Yunkai and Meereen built their wealth on the slave trade. The ruined peninsula of Valyria, destroyed in the Doom, was home to the dragonlords from whom House Targaryen descends.

Role in the story

Essos is where the exiled Daenerys Targaryen and Viserys begin the saga; Daenerys's arc — hatching dragons, conquering Slaver's Bay, ruling Meereen — unfolds there before her return to Westeros. Tyrion, Arya and others also travel extensively in Essos, which serves as a mirror and counterpoint to feudal Westeros.
