Kunti

Kunti (Sanskrit: कुंती), born Pritha, is a prominent figure in the Hindu epic Mahabharata. She is the wife of King Pandu of Hastinapura and the mother of Karna and of three of the five Pandava brothers — Yudhishthira, Bhima and Arjuna.

Story

Given a boon by the sage Durvasa to invoke any god and bear a child, the young Pritha tested it by calling the sun god Surya and bore Karna, whom she set afloat in a basket out of shame. Later, married to the cursed Pandu, she used the mantra to bear sons by Dharma, Vayu and Indra, and shared it with co-wife Madri, mother of the twins.

Later life

Kunti raised the Pandavas after Pandu's death, survived the lac-house fire at Varnavrata, and endured the family's exile. After the Kurukshetra War she retired to the forest with Dhritarashtra and Gandhari and died in a forest fire. She is revered as one of the Panchakanya, the five exemplary women.
