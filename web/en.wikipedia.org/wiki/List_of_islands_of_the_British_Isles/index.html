List of islands of the British Isles

The British Isles comprise more than 6,000 islands off the north-western coast of Europe. The two largest are Great Britain and Ireland. This list gives the principal islands by area, including those of the United Kingdom, Ireland, and the Crown dependencies.

Island groups

Major groups include the Hebrides (Inner and Outer), Orkney, Shetland, the Isles of Scilly, and the Channel Islands — the latter geographically lying off the coast of France but traditionally associated with the British Isles politically.
