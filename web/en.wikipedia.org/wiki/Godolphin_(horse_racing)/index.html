Godolphin (horse racing)

Godolphin is the Maktoum family's private Thoroughbred horseracing stable and was named in honour of the Godolphin Arabian, one of three foundation stallions of the modern Thoroughbred. Godolphin's founder is Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, ruler of Dubai and prime minister of the United Arab Emirates.

Established in 1992, the stable's horses wear royal blue silks and compete worldwide, wintering at its Al Quoz stables in Dubai and summering at Moulton Paddocks in Newmarket, England, with operations also in Australia, Japan, France, Ireland and the United States. Its principal trainer for decades was Saeed bin Suroor, joined later by Charlie Appleby.

Godolphin has won thousands of races including numerous Group 1 events — among them the Dubai World Cup, the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe (with Enable's conqueror Waldgeist era runners and later stars), the Melbourne Cup with Cross Counter in 2018, and Epsom Derby success with Masar (2018) and Adayar (2021). It is perennially among the world's leading owners by prize money.
