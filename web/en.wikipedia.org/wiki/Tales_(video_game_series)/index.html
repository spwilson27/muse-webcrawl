Tales is a long-running Japanese action role-playing game franchise published by Bandai Namco Entertainment (originally Namco). Debuting with Tales of Phantasia for the Super Famicom in 1995, the series is characterised by its real-time "Linear Motion Battle System", anime-style presentation, character skits, cooking systems, and recurring thematic elements such as conflicts between peoples and the tension between justice and ideals. It is considered one of the major JRPG franchises alongside Final Fantasy and Dragon Quest in Japan.

Main entries

Principal titles include Tales of Phantasia (1995), Tales of Destiny (1997), Tales of Eternia (2000), Tales of Symphonia (2003), Tales of Rebirth (2004), Tales of Legendia (2005), Tales of the Abyss (2005), Tales of Innocence (2007), Tales of Vesperia (2008), Tales of Hearts (2008), Tales of Graces (2009), Tales of Xillia (2011) and its sequel, Tales of Zestiria (2015), Tales of Berseria (2016), and Tales of Arise (2021), which sold over two million copies and won Best Role Playing Game at The Game Awards 2021.

Gameplay

Battles use variants of the Linear Motion Battle System, in which characters move and attack in real time on a line or free-roaming field rather than selecting commands from menus. Other hallmarks include "artes" (special attacks), skits — optional voiced conversations that develop characters — grade points awarded after battle, and elaborate anime opening sequences with theme songs by major Japanese artists.

Design and staff

Long-time character designers include Mutsumi Inomata and Kosuke Fujishima; music has frequently been composed by Motoi Sakuraba. Earlier games were developed by Wolf Team and then Namco Tales Studio before development moved in-house to Bandai Namco Studios. The series has sold more than 25 million copies worldwide, and has expanded into anime adaptations, manga, audio dramas and the annual Tales of Festival fan event in Japan.
