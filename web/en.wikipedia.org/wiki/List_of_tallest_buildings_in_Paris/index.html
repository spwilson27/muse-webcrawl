The Paris metropolitan area has strict height limits within the historic city, so most of its skyscrapers are concentrated in the La Défense business district, just west of the city proper. The tallest structure overall remains the Eiffel Tower.

Notes

The completion of the Tour Montparnasse — for years the only skyscraper within Paris proper — provoked a backlash that led to height restrictions in the city centre. The Grande Arche de la Défense (110 m) is a famous landmark but not among the tallest.
