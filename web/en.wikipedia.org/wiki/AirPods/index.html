AirPods

Manufacturer: Apple Inc.
Type: Wireless earbuds
Release date: 13 December 2016 (1st generation)

AirPods are wireless Bluetooth earbuds designed by Apple Inc. They were announced on 7 September 2016 alongside the iPhone 7 and first released in December 2016. AirPods became one of Apple's most successful accessories, helping to popularise true-wireless earbuds across the industry.

Features

AirPods pair automatically with Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account, play or pause when inserted or removed using optical and motion sensors, and support Siri voice control. They ship with a pocket charging case that extends total listening time to more than 24 hours. Successive generations added the H1 and H2 chips, hands-free "Hey Siri", wireless charging, improved battery life, and — in the AirPods Pro line — active noise cancellation and transparency mode. The over-ear AirPods Max were introduced in 2020, and the fourth-generation AirPods, including a model with active noise cancellation, arrived in 2024.

Reception

Early reviews praised the convenience and pairing experience while criticising the fit and sound relative to price; the product was nonetheless a major commercial hit and a frequent subject of commentary about its distinctive design becoming a status symbol.
