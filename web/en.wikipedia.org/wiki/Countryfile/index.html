Countryfile
BBC One magazine programme
First broadcast: 24 July 1988
Presenters include: John Craven, Matt Baker, Anita Rani

Countryfile is a British television programme on BBC One that reports on rural, agricultural and environmental issues in the United Kingdom. First broadcast in 1988 as a Sunday morning programme, it moved to a prime-time Sunday evening slot in 2009 and regularly attracts several million viewers.

Content

Episodes visit different regions of the country and mix farming and wildlife features with investigations of rural policy. Long-serving presenter John Craven joined in 1989. The programme is known for its annual photographic calendar competition in aid of Children in Need and for the Countryfile magazine.
