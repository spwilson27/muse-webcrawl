The Derde Divisie (English: Third Division), formerly known as the Topklasse (Top Class), is the fourth tier of football in the Netherlands, which had its inaugural season as a third tier in 2010–11 as the Topklasse. The Derde Divisie consists of two groups of 18 teams each, divided historically along Saturday and Sunday amateur football lines, though the distinction has been relaxed in recent years.

History

The league was created in 2010 as the Topklasse, the first bridge between professional and amateur football in the Netherlands, sitting below the Eerste Divisie. When the Tweede Divisie was reintroduced in 2016, the Topklasse was renamed the Derde Divisie and became the fourth tier.

Structure

Champions of each group can be promoted to the Tweede Divisie, subject to licensing requirements, while the lowest-placed clubs are relegated to the Vierde Divisie, with additional clubs entering relegation play-offs against Vierde Divisie sides. Clubs in the Derde Divisie also enter the KNVB Cup, the Dutch national cup competition.
