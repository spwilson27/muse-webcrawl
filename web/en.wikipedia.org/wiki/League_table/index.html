League table

A league table, standings or rankings chart is a chart summarising the position of each team in a sports league. Teams are ranked according to criteria such as points won, with tie-breakers (such as goal difference in association football) used to separate teams that are level.

Contents of a table

A typical association football league table contains columns for:

AbbreviationMeaning
PosPosition
P / PlMatches played
WMatches won
DMatches drawn
LMatches lost
GF / GAGoals for / goals against
GDGoal difference
PtsPoints

Most football leagues award three points for a win and one for a draw, a system first adopted widely in the 1980s and 1990s to encourage attacking play; previously two points for a win was standard. Coloured shading on published tables often marks qualification for continental competitions, play-offs, or relegation.

Other uses

The term "league table" is also used metaphorically, in Britain especially, for published rankings of schools, hospitals, universities, or companies by performance indicators. Such tables are controversial because the choice of criteria can strongly affect the rankings.
