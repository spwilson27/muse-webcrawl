Valle del Cauca Department


Country: Colombia
Capital: Cali
Established: 16 April 1910

Valle del Cauca is a department of western Colombia, on the Pacific coast. Its capital is Santiago de Cali, Colombia's third-largest city. The department takes its name from the Cauca River, whose broad fertile valley runs between the Western and Central Cordilleras of the Andes.

Economy

The department is one of Colombia's most important agricultural and industrial regions. The Cauca valley is a major producer of sugar cane, and the department also produces coffee, plantains and other crops. Buenaventura, on the Pacific coast, is Colombia's principal Pacific port and handles a large share of the country's maritime trade.

Geography and population

Valle del Cauca has a population of roughly 4.5 million. Its territory ranges from the Pacific lowlands and the port of Buenaventura, through the inter-Andean valley, to high mountain ridges. The department contains 42 municipalities and significant Afro-Colombian and indigenous populations.
