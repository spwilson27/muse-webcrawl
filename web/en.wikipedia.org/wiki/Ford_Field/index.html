Ford Field is a domed American football stadium in Downtown Detroit, Michigan. It is the home of the Detroit Lions of the National Football League and opened in 2002, replacing the Pontiac Silverdome.

Design

The stadium is unusual among domed venues: its south wall incorporates the former Hudson's warehouse, a six-story building from the 1920s that now houses suites, restaurants, and retail space, giving the interior an industrial character. The playing surface is FieldTurf.

Major events

Ford Field hosted Super Bowl XL in 2006, WrestleMania 23 in 2007, the 2009 NCAA Men's Final Four, and numerous college bowl games including the Quick Lane Bowl (now the GameAbove Sports Bowl). It also hosts the MAC Championship Game and is a regular venue for concerts.
