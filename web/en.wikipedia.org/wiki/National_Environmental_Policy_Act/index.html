National Environmental Policy Act

The National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) is a United States environmental law enacted on January 1, 1970. Often called the "Magna Carta" of US environmental law, NEPA requires federal agencies to assess the environmental effects of their proposed actions before making decisions.

Requirements

Agencies must prepare an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) for major federal actions significantly affecting the quality of the human environment. Less extensive reviews take the form of an Environmental Assessment (EA), which may conclude with a Finding of No Significant Impact. The process includes public comment.

Council on Environmental Quality

NEPA established the Council on Environmental Quality (CEQ) within the Executive Office of the President to oversee implementation and issue regulations and guidance.
