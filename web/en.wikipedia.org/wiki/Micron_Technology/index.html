Micron Technology

Micron Technology, Inc. is an American producer of computer memory and computer data storage including dynamic random-access memory, flash memory, and USB flash drives. It is headquartered in Boise, Idaho.

History

Micron was founded in 1978 as a semiconductor design consulting firm and began manufacturing DRAM in 1981. It survived repeated memory industry downturns, acquired competitors including Texas Instruments' memory business and Elpida, and became one of the world's three largest DRAM makers alongside Samsung and SK Hynix.

Products

The company sells memory under its own name and consumer products under the Crucial brand, including SSDs and DRAM modules, as well as high-bandwidth memory for artificial intelligence accelerators.
