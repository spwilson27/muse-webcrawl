Silk Spectre

Silk Spectre is the name of two fictional superheroines in the DC Comics series Watchmen (1986–87) by Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons: Sally Jupiter and her daughter Laurie Juspeczyk. The name and costume are passed from mother to daughter.

Characters

Sally Jupiter (born Sally Juspeczyk), the first Silk Spectre, was a member of the 1940s hero team the Minutemen, whose career was as much about publicity and pin-up fame as crimefighting. Her daughter Laurie was pushed into the role by her mother and joined the second generation of heroes; she is in a relationship with Doctor Manhattan, and the revelation of her parentage — that the Comedian is her father — is a key turning point in the story.

Other media

Silk Spectre appears in the 2009 film Watchmen (played by Carla Gugino and Malin Åkerman) and in the 2019 HBO sequel series, in which Laurie (played by Jean Smart) has become an FBI agent going by Laurie Blake.
