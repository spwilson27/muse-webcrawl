Film4

Film4 is a British free-to-air television channel owned by Channel Four Television Corporation, which has been on air since 1 November 1982. It broadcasts films, including British productions through its production arm, Film4 Productions.

History

The channel began as FilmFour, a subscription service launched on 1 November 1982, the day before Channel 4 itself went on air. It was one of the first niche subscription channels in the United Kingdom. In 2006, the channel was relaunched as Film4 and became free-to-air, supported by advertising, joining the Freeview platform and greatly increasing its audience.

Film4 Productions

Film4 Productions is Channel 4's film production division, responsible for backing many notable British films. Productions associated with Film4 include Trainspotting, The Full Monty, Slumdog Millionaire, Four Weddings and a Funeral, 12 Years a Slave, The Favourite, and Poor Things. The original FilmFour production company was closed in 2002 following losses, but film production continued under Channel 4's drama department before the Film4 brand was revived in full.

Programming

Film4 broadcasts a mixture of Hollywood films, independent and art-house cinema, and British films, often organised into themed seasons. Sister channels have included Film4 +1, a timeshift service.
