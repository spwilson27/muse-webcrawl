Miller Lite

Miller Lite, the original light beer, brewed in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

Miller Lite is a 4.2% ABV American light pilsner beer sold by Molson Coors in the United States and internationally. It was the first commercially successful light beer, created by biochemist Joseph L. Owades for the Rheingold Brewing Corporation and launched nationally by the Miller Brewing Company of Milwaukee in 1975.

History

Owades developed a process using an enzyme to break down dextrins, producing a lower-calorie beer under the name Gablinger's Diet Beer in 1967; the recipe passed to Chicago's Meister Bräu as Lite, and then to Miller, which acquired Meister Bräu in 1972. Miller reformulated and test-marketed the beer before a national launch in 1975 with an aggressive advertising campaign featuring retired athletes debating whether the beer was great because it was "Less Filling" or "Tastes Great" — one of the most famous campaigns in advertising history.

Market position

Miller Lite created the light beer category, soon contested by Anheuser-Busch's Bud Light (1982) and Coors Light. It has remained among the best-selling beers in the United States and is a major sports sponsor, long associated with NFL and NASCAR sponsorships and its distinctive white can design, restored in 2014.
