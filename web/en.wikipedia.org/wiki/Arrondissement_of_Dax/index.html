The arrondissement of Dax is an arrondissement of France in the Landes department in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region. It has 152 communes. Its population is roughly 220,000 and its subprefecture is the town of Dax, famous as a spa resort renowned since Roman times for its hot springs and mud therapy.

Composition

The arrondissement covers the southwestern part of Landes, including the Atlantic coast south of the department (with communes such as Hossegor, Capbreton and Soustons), the Chalosse region inland, and part of the Landes de Gascogne forest. Following the 2015 cantonal redistricting and later intercommunal reorganizations, its communes are grouped into cantons including Côte d'Argent, Dax-1, Dax-2, Marensin Sud, Orthe et Arrigans, Pays morcenais tarusate, Pays tyrossais and Seignanx.

History

The arrondissement of Dax was created in 1800, when the arrondissement system was established across France. Its boundaries were adjusted in the 2017 arrondissement reorganization in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, when a small number of communes changed arrondissement.
