Kellogg's

Kellogg's was a brand of the Kellogg Company, an American multinational food manufacturing company headquartered in Battle Creek, Michigan. Founded in 1906 by Will Keith Kellogg, the company became famous for its breakfast cereals, including Corn Flakes, Rice Krispies, Frosted Flakes, Special K, and Froot Loops, as well as snacks such as Pop-Tarts and Pringles.

History

The origins of the company lie in the Battle Creek Sanitarium run by Dr. John Harvey Kellogg, where he and his brother Will Keith accidentally developed flaked cereal in the 1890s. Will Keith founded the Battle Creek Toasted Corn Flake Company in 1906 and built the business through aggressive marketing. In 2023, the Kellogg Company split into Kellanova (global snacking) and WK Kellogg Co (North American cereals).

Products and mascots

Kellogg's products are associated with famous mascots such as Tony the Tiger (Frosted Flakes), Snap, Crackle and Pop (Rice Krispies), and Toucan Sam (Froot Loops). The company sold its cereals in more than 180 countries.
