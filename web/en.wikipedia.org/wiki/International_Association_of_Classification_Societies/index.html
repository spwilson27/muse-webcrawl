International Association of Classification Societies

The International Association of Classification Societies (IACS) is a technically based non-governmental organisation that currently consists of twelve member marine classification societies. It was founded on 11 September 1968 in Hamburg, Germany.

Role

IACS develops unified requirements and interpretations of international maritime regulations, particularly those of the International Maritime Organization. Its Unified Requirements are adopted by member societies and cover matters such as hull structure, machinery and survey procedures. IACS holds consultative status with the IMO.

Members

Members include the American Bureau of Shipping, Bureau Veritas, ClassNK, DNV, Lloyd's Register, the Registro Italiano Navale and the Russian Maritime Register of Shipping, among others.

History

Seven leading societies founded IACS in 1968; the association adopted a formal charter in 1969 and has since expanded to twelve members representing the great majority of the world's cargo-carrying tonnage.
