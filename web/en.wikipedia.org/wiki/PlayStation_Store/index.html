PlayStation Store

DeveloperSony Interactive Entertainment
Launched11 November 2006
PlatformsPlayStation 3, PlayStation Portable, PlayStation Vita, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, web, mobile apps
TypeOnline distribution / digital storefront

The PlayStation Store is a digital media store available to users of Sony's PlayStation consoles via the PlayStation Network. Launched alongside the PlayStation 3 in November 2006, the store offers downloadable content including full games, add-ons and downloadable content (DLC), game demos, themes, avatars, applications, and formerly movies and television shows.

Access and features

The store is accessed on consoles, via web browsers, and through the PlayStation App. Purchases are tied to a PlayStation Network account, and funds can be added with payment cards or prepaid PlayStation Store cards. PlayStation Plus subscribers receive additional discounts.

History

The store received major redesigns over the years, transitioning from a web-based interface to a native application. Sony announced plans to close the stores for PS3, PSP, and Vita in 2021, but reversed course for PS3 and Vita following criticism, keeping them available with some payment restrictions.
