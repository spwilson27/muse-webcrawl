Naruhito (born 23 February 1960) is the Emperor of Japan. He acceded to the Chrysanthemum Throne on 1 May 2019, following the abdication of his father, Emperor Akihito, beginning the Reiwa era. He is the 126th emperor according to Japan's traditional order of succession.

Early life and education

Naruhito was born at the Imperial Household Agency Hospital in the Imperial Palace, Tokyo, the eldest son of then Crown Prince Akihito and Crown Princess Michiko. He was educated at Gakushuin schools and graduated from Gakushuin University in 1982 with a degree in history. He studied at Merton College, Oxford, from 1983 to 1985, researching the history of transportation on the River Thames, and later earned a master's degree from Gakushuin.

Marriage and family

On 9 June 1993 he married Masako Owada, a diplomat and Harvard and Oxford graduate. Their only child, Princess Aiko, was born in 2001. Because Japanese imperial succession law restricts succession to males, Naruhito's younger brother Fumihito, Prince Akishino, is heir presumptive.

Reign

Naruhito's enthronement ceremony took place on 22 October 2019. As emperor he performs constitutional and ceremonial duties as symbol of the state under the Constitution of Japan. His reign has included the COVID-19 pandemic, the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games held in 2021, and state visits promoting international goodwill. He has long championed water issues, serving in honorary roles connected with United Nations water and sanitation advisory work.
