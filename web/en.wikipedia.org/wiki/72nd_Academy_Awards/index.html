72nd Academy Awards
DateMarch 26, 2000
VenueShrine Auditorium, Los Angeles

The 72nd Academy Awards ceremony, organised by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, honoured the best films of 1999 and took place on March 26, 2000, at the Shrine Auditorium in Los Angeles. Billy Crystal hosted for the seventh time. The ceremony is remembered for a pre-show theft of 55 Oscar statuettes (most recovered from a rubbish bin before the event) and for American Beauty's five wins.

Winners

American Beauty won Best Picture, Best Director (Sam Mendes, in his feature debut), Best Actor (Kevin Spacey), Best Original Screenplay (Alan Ball) and Best Cinematography. Hilary Swank won Best Actress for Boys Don't Cry; Michael Caine (supporting actor, The Cider House Rules) and Angelina Jolie (supporting actress, Girl, Interrupted) took the supporting awards. The Matrix won four technical awards, and The Red Violin won Best Original Score. Pedro Almodóvar's All About My Mother won Best Foreign Language Film.

Notes

The ceremony drew over 46 million US television viewers. Andrzej Wajda received an Honorary Award, and Warren Beatty the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award.
