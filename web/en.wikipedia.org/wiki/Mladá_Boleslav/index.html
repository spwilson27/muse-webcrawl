Mladá Boleslav

Mladá Boleslav (Czech pronunciation: [ˈmladaː ˈbolɛslaf]; German: Jungbunzlau) is a city in the Central Bohemian Region of the Czech Republic. It has about 46,000 inhabitants. It lies on the left bank of the Jizera River about 50 kilometres (31 mi) northeast of Prague.

The city is best known as the home of Škoda Auto, one of the largest car manufacturers in Europe, whose presence dominates the local economy. The historic town centre is well preserved and is protected as an urban monument zone.

History

Mladá Boleslav was founded in the second half of the 10th century by King Boleslaus II as a royal castle. A market settlement grew beneath the castle, and the town received its charter in the 14th century. The town's name distinguishes it from the older Boleslav, now Stará Boleslav ("Old Boleslav").

In the 16th century the town became a centre of the Unity of the Brethren, a Protestant church; the Brethren's printing press and school flourished here. After the Battle of White Mountain in 1620 the town was forcibly re-Catholicised and declined.

The modern era of the town began in 1895, when Václav Laurin and Václav Klement founded a bicycle factory that soon turned to manufacturing motorcycles and, from 1905, automobiles. The company was absorbed into the Škoda industrial concern in 1925, giving rise to Škoda Auto. During the 20th century the car works expanded enormously, drawing workers from across the country.

Economy

Škoda Auto, today part of the Volkswagen Group, is headquartered in Mladá Boleslav, where its main plant and museum are located. The plant is one of the largest industrial sites in the Czech Republic and the town's economy is closely tied to the automotive industry and its suppliers.

Sights

Landmarks include Mladá Boleslav Castle, the Old Town Hall with its sgraffito-decorated tower, the Church of the Assumption of the Virgin Mary, the Temple of Peace (Templ) archaeological exhibition, and the Škoda Museum, which displays the history of the marque from the Laurin & Klement era to the present.

Sport

The town is home to the ice hockey club BK Mladá Boleslav and the football club FK Mladá Boleslav, which has competed in the Czech First League and in European competitions.

Twin towns

Mladá Boleslav is twinned with several towns, including Dieburg in Germany and Pezinok in Slovakia.
