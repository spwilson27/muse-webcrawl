Paris Métro

Opened: 19 July 1900
Lines: 16
Stations: Over 300
Operator: RATP

The Paris Métro is the rapid transit system of the Paris metropolitan area in France. Opened in 1900 for the World's Fair, it is one of the oldest and densest metro networks in the world, known for its Art Nouveau station entrances designed by Hector Guimard.

Network

The network comprises 16 numbered lines, mostly underground, serving Paris and its inner suburbs with stations rarely more than a few hundred metres apart in the city centre. Fully automated lines include Line 1 and Line 14.

Grand Paris Express

The Grand Paris Express project is adding four new lines (15–18) and extensions, roughly 200 km of new track, making it one of the largest transport projects in Europe, with phased openings beginning in the mid-2020s.
