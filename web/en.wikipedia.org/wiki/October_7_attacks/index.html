October 7 attacks

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The October 7 attacks were a large-scale, coordinated assault on southern Israel launched from the Gaza Strip on 7 October 2023 by the Palestinian militant group Hamas and other armed groups, at the start of the Gaza war. Hamas called the operation "Al-Aqsa Flood". It was the deadliest day for Jews since the Holocaust and the deadliest attack in Israel's history.

The attack

In the early morning of 7 October — the Jewish holiday of Simchat Torah and the Sabbath — thousands of rockets were fired at Israel while around 3,000 militants breached the Gaza–Israel barrier by land, sea and air (using motorised paragliders), overwhelming border positions of the Israel Defense Forces. Attackers assaulted military bases and civilian communities near the border, including the kibbutzim of Be'eri, Kfar Aza, Nir Oz and Re'im, the towns of Sderot and Ofakim, and an outdoor music festival near Re'im (the Nova festival), where about 364 people were killed.

Casualties and hostages

Approximately 1,200 people were killed in Israel, the great majority of them civilians, including citizens of many other countries. Around 250 people — civilians and soldiers, among them children and the elderly — were abducted to Gaza as hostages. Israeli investigations and journalistic accounts documented massacres, torture and sexual violence during the attacks, findings disputed by Hamas officials. Some Israeli casualties resulted from friendly fire and from the IDF's Hannibal Directive, the extent of which remains debated.

Response and aftermath

Israel declared war, mobilised some 360,000 reservists, and after days of fighting to clear its territory began an intensive aerial campaign and, from late October, a ground invasion of the Gaza Strip, leading to a prolonged war with very heavy Palestinian casualties. More than a hundred hostages were released during a temporary ceasefire in November 2023, and others were freed in a further ceasefire deal in early 2025. The attacks and the ensuing war reshaped regional politics, drawing in Hezbollah in Lebanon, the Houthis in Yemen and direct Israel–Iran exchanges in 2024.

Investigations

The scale of the intelligence and operational failure — despite prior warnings and a captured Hamas battle plan — has been the subject of Israeli military inquiries, completed and published in 2025, and continuing public debate about political responsibility.
