Public holidays in Australia

Public holidays in Australia consist of national holidays observed in all states and territories, together with state- and territory-specific holidays declared under local law.

National holidays

Nationwide holidays include New Year's Day (1 January), Australia Day (26 January), Good Friday, Easter Saturday (in most states), Easter Monday, Anzac Day (25 April), Christmas Day (25 December) and Boxing Day (26 December). The King's Birthday is observed on varying dates by state. States additionally observe holidays such as Labour Day on differing dates, Melbourne Cup Day in Victoria, and Reconciliation Day in the ACT.
