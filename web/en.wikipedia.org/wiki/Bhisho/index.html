Bhisho (formerly spelled Bisho) is the capital of the Eastern Cape province of South Africa. It lies near the port city of East London and adjacent to King William's Town (now Qonce). The name derives from the Xhosa word for buffalo, also the name of the river that runs through it.

History

Bhisho was established as the capital of the nominally independent Ciskei homeland under South Africa's apartheid system. In September 1992 the town was the site of the Bisho massacre, in which Ciskei Defence Force troops fired on African National Congress marchers, killing 28 people — an event that hastened negotiations to end apartheid.

Modern role

Following the 1994 democratic elections and the reincorporation of Ciskei into South Africa, Bhisho became the seat of the Eastern Cape provincial government and legislature, while the province's high court sits in the city as well. It is a largely administrative town forming part of the Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality.
