Hogwarts Legacy

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Hogwarts Legacy is a 2023 action role-playing game developed by Avalanche Software and published by Warner Bros. Games under its Portkey Games label. Set in the Wizarding World of Harry Potter roughly a century before the novels — in the late 1800s — it lets players create a student who begins at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry as a fifth-year.

Gameplay and plot

Players attend classes, learn spells, brew potions, tend magical creatures, and explore an open world encompassing Hogwarts castle, Hogsmeade, the Forbidden Forest, and the surrounding Highlands. The protagonist holds the key to an ancient repository of magic and is pursued by the goblin Ranrok and the dark wizard Victor Rookwood, who seek to harness it; choices made in the story influence relationships and the ending. The game does not feature Quidditch, though broom flight is available.

Reception and sales

Released for PlayStation 5, Windows, and Xbox Series X/S in February 2023 (with ports for older consoles and Nintendo Switch following), Hogwarts Legacy received generally favourable reviews praising its recreation of Hogwarts and combat, with criticism of side content and technical issues on some platforms. It was the best-selling game of 2023, selling over 22 million copies by the end of that year and surpassing 30 million by late 2024. Its launch was accompanied by controversy and boycott calls relating to Harry Potter author J. K. Rowling's public statements, though these had no apparent effect on sales.
