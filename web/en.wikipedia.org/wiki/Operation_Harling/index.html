Operation Harling

Operation Harling was a British Special Operations Executive (SOE) mission in German-occupied Greece during the Second World War. In November 1942 a small team of British officers, working with Greek resistance fighters of EDES and ELAS, demolished the Gorgopotamos railway viaduct on the night of 25–26 November 1942.

The destruction cut the main railway line from Thessaloniki to Athens and Piraeus, interrupting supplies moving toward North Africa at a critical point in the war. It was one of the first major acts of sabotage in occupied Europe and a rare example of cooperation between rival Greek resistance organisations.

The Harling team, led by Brigadier Edmund Myers, remained in Greece and formed the core of the British Military Mission to the Greek resistance.
