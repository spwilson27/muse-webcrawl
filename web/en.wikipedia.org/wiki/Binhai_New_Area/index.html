Binhai New Area (Chinese: 滨海新区) is a sub-provincial district and state-level new area of Tianjin, China, located on the coast of the Bohai Sea east of Tianjin's urban core. It is a major economic development zone, sometimes described as China's third growth pole after Shenzhen and Shanghai's Pudong.

Development

The area grew around the Tianjin Economic-Technological Development Area (TEDA), founded in 1984, and the Port of Tianjin, one of the world's busiest ports. In 2009 the districts of Tanggu, Hangu, and Dagang were merged to form Binhai New Area as a single administrative district.

Economy

Binhai hosts industries including aerospace (including an Airbus A320 final assembly line), petrochemicals, information technology, and finance. Planned sub-zones such as the Yujiapu Financial District were intended to become a northern financial center. On 12 August 2015, a series of massive explosions at a hazardous-goods warehouse in the port area killed more than 170 people and caused extensive damage, becoming one of China's worst industrial disasters.
