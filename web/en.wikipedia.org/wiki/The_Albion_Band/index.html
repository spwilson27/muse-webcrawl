The Albion Band

The Albion Band is an English folk rock band, originally formed in 1972 by bassist and bandleader Ashley Hutchings. Also known at various times as the Albion Dance Band and the Albion Christmas Band, the group has been a central institution of the English folk revival, with a constantly changing line-up that has launched many notable musicians.

OriginEngland
GenresFolk rock, British folk rock
Years active1972–present (intermittently)
FounderAshley Hutchings

History

Hutchings formed the band after leaving Steeleye Span, initially to back singer Shirley Collins on the album No Roses (1971). Early line-ups included members of Fairport Convention. As the Albion Dance Band, the group specialized in English country dance music played on electric instruments; the 1977 album The Prospect Before Us is a landmark of the genre.

Later years

The band worked with the National Theatre in productions such as Lark Rise to Candleford, and members including John Tams became widely known. The Albion Christmas Band has toured seasonal shows since 1999. In 2011 a new generation of the band, featuring Hutchings's son Blair Dunlop, released the album Fighting Room.
