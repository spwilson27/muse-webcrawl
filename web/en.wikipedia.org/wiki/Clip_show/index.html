Clip show

MediumTelevision
TypeEpisode format

A clip show is an episode of a television series that consists primarily of excerpts from previous episodes, usually framed by a small amount of new material in which characters reminisce about past events.

Purpose

Clip shows are inexpensive to produce because most footage already exists, and they have historically been used to fill out a season, recap storylines for new viewers, or celebrate a series milestone. Common framing devices include characters exchanging memories, a character writing a letter or report, or a trial or interview reviewing past events.

Reception

Clip shows are often regarded by audiences as filler and tend to receive lower ratings, though some series have parodied or reinvented the format creatively, notably Community and Clerks: The Animated Series, which used clips of events never previously shown.
