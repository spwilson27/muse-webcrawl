Lamborghini Huracán

The Lamborghini Huracán (Spanish for "hurricane"; pronounced [uɾaˈkan]) is a mid-engine sports car manufactured by Lamborghini from 2014 to 2024, succeeding the Gallardo as the brand's best-selling V10 model line. Named, following Lamborghini tradition, after a famous Spanish fighting bull, the Huracán was unveiled at the 2014 Geneva Motor Show and was produced in coupé and Spyder (convertible) forms at Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy.

The Huracán's 5.2-litre V10, shared in basic architecture with the Audi R8, produced 610 PS (449 kW) in the original LP 610-4, giving 0–100 km/h in about 3.2 seconds and a top speed around 325 km/h (202 mph). A hybrid carbon-fibre and aluminium chassis, magnetorheological dampers and a seven-speed dual-clutch gearbox were standard.

Variants

The range expanded to include the rear-wheel-drive LP 580-2, the track-focused Performante (2017) with active aerodynamics (Aerodinamica Lamborghini Attiva) and a 640 PS engine, the facelifted EVO (2019) with rear-wheel steering and predictive vehicle dynamics control, the hardcore STO (2020) derived from the Super Trofeo race car, and the off-road-styled Sterrato (2022). Racing versions — the Huracán GT3 and Super Trofeo — won multiple endurance races and one-make championships. More than 14,000 Huracáns had been built by 2019, surpassing the Gallardo's total, and production passed 20,000 before the model gave way to the twin-turbo V8 hybrid Temerario in 2024.
