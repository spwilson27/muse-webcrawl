Clean sheet

A clean sheet is a term used in association football and ice hockey to describe a match or game in which a team or goalkeeper concedes no goals. In football, a clean sheet is credited to a goalkeeper when the opposing team fails to score for the entire match; in hockey, the equivalent feat is called a shutout.

Football

Clean sheets are an important measure of a goalkeeper's and a defence's performance. Goalkeepers who keep many clean sheets over a season may win awards such as the Premier League Golden Glove, given to the goalkeeper with the most clean sheets in a season. Petr Čech holds the Premier League record for most career clean sheets.

Ice hockey

In ice hockey a shutout is recorded when a goaltender prevents the opposing team from scoring for a whole game. Martin Brodeur holds the National Hockey League record for career regular-season shutouts.

Terminology

The phrase is thought to derive from the blank scoresheet on which no goals were recorded against a team's name, leaving the sheet "clean".
