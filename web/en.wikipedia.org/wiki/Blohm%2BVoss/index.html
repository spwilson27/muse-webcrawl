Blohm+Voss

Industry: Shipbuilding
Founded: 1877
Founders: Hermann Blohm, Ernst Voss
Headquarters: Hamburg, Germany

Blohm+Voss (historically Blohm & Voss) is a German shipbuilding and engineering company founded in Hamburg in 1877. It built warships, ocean liners and, through its aircraft division Hamburger Flugzeugbau, military aircraft.

History

The yard built the battleship Bismarck, launched in 1939, as well as U-boats during both world wars. Its aircraft works produced designs including the asymmetrical BV 141 reconnaissance aircraft.

Modern era

After the Second World War the yard resumed commercial shipbuilding and became known for naval frigates, fast yachts and cruise-ship work. Today it operates as a repair and refit yard within the NVL Group, specialising in naval vessels and superyachts.
