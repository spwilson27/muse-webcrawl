Arcane (titled onscreen as Arcane: League of Legends) is an adult animated steampunk action-adventure television series created by Christian Linke and Alex Yee for Netflix. Produced by the French studio Fortiche under the supervision of Riot Games, it is set in the fictional universe of the video game League of Legends and focuses on the sisters Vi and Jinx amid the conflict between the utopian city of Piltover and its oppressed undercity, Zaun.

Premise

Orphaned sisters Violet ("Vi") and Powder are raised in the undercity after their parents are killed by Piltover's enforcers. Years later, a heist gone wrong involving experimental "Hextech" gemstones sets in motion events that destroy their family and transform Powder into the unstable criminal Jinx, adopted by the crime lord Silco. Vi, imprisoned and later released, joins the enforcer Caitlyn to hunt Jinx down, while inventor Jayce's Hextech technology and a dangerous drug called shimmer push the two cities toward war.

Release and reception

The first season premiered on 6 November 2021, released in three "acts" of three episodes. It was a major critical and commercial success, topping Netflix charts in numerous countries and becoming the first streaming series to win the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Animated Program (2022); it also won nine Annie Awards. The second and final season was released in November 2024, concluding the story of Vi and Jinx while Riot and Fortiche developed further series set in other regions of Runeterra.
