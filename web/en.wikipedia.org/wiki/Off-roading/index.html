Off-roading

Off-roading is the act of driving a vehicle on unpaved surfaces such as sand, dirt, gravel, riverbeds, mud, snow, rocks, and other natural terrain. Off-roading ranges from casual driving on unpaved roads in ordinary vehicles to competitive events in specially modified vehicles.

Forms

Popular forms include green laning, dune bashing, mudding, rock crawling, and desert racing such as the Dakar Rally and Baja 1000. Off-road vehicles typically feature four-wheel drive, high ground clearance, and reinforced suspension. Environmental impact and land access are subjects of ongoing debate.
