The Dukes of Hazzard

The Dukes of Hazzard is an American action comedy television series created by Gy Waldron that aired on CBS from January 26, 1979, to February 8, 1985, with a total of 147 episodes spanning seven seasons. It follows cousins Bo and Luke Duke as they outrun corrupt county commissioner Boss Hogg and Sheriff Rosco P. Coltrane in their 1969 Dodge Charger, the General Lee.

Overview

Set in fictional Hazzard County, Georgia, episodes typically involve the Duke boys — on probation for moonshine running — foiling Boss Hogg's schemes while evading comic car chases narrated by Waylon Jennings, who also sang the theme song "Good Ol' Boys".

Legacy

The series was a major ratings success and merchandising phenomenon. It spawned reunion television films, a 2005 feature film remake, and a prequel. The Confederate flag imagery on the General Lee has drawn increasing criticism and led to changes in merchandising and syndication.
