Sizewell nuclear power stations

LocationNear Leiston, Suffolk, England
StationsSizewell A (Magnox, closed); Sizewell B (PWR, operating); Sizewell C (approved)
OperatorEDF Energy (B); Magnox Ltd (A decommissioning)

The Sizewell nuclear power stations are a group of nuclear power stations on the Suffolk coast in England, near the village of Sizewell south of Leiston. Sizewell A, a twin-reactor Magnox station, operated from 1966 to 2006 and is being decommissioned. Sizewell B, the United Kingdom's only pressurised water reactor (PWR) in commercial operation, entered service in 1995. A third station, Sizewell C, a pair of EPR reactors, received development consent in 2022 and government backing for construction.

Sizewell A

Sizewell A consisted of two 210 MWe Magnox reactors in a single turbine hall, generating electricity from 1966. After 40 years of operation it closed on 31 December 2006; defuelling was completed in 2014, and the site is in the long care-and-maintenance phase of decommissioning under Magnox Ltd.

Sizewell B

Sizewell B — recognisable by its white containment dome — is a single SNUPPS-derived PWR rated at about 1,188 MWe, supplying roughly 3% of the UK's electricity. Its construction was authorised after the longest public inquiry in British history (1985–87) under Professor Frank Layfield, and the station was commissioned in 1995. It is operated by EDF Energy, with a planned operating life extending to 2055 following life-extension work. Because it is the UK's only PWR, its design and operating experience informed subsequent new-build plans.

Sizewell C

Sizewell C is planned as a near-copy of Hinkley Point C, with two EPR units totalling about 3.2 GW. The project was given a final investment decision by the UK government in 2025, with the state taking a major stake alongside EDF, and is expected to power around six million homes. Local campaigners have contested the project over its cost and its impact on the Suffolk Coast and Heaths Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty and the Minsmere nature reserve.
