Naenara

Naenara (Korean: 내나라, "my country") is the official web portal of the North Korean government, published by the Korea Computer Center in Pyongyang. It is one of the few North Korean websites accessible on the global Internet and carries material from state media and publishing houses in Korean, English, French, Spanish, German, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, and Arabic.

Contents

The portal hosts content from the Korean Central News Agency (KCNA), the Rodong Sinmun newspaper, the Foreign Languages Publishing House, and other state organs: news reports, political essays, the works of Kim Il Sung, Kim Jong Il, and Kim Jong Un, tourism information, trade and investment promotion from entities such as the Korea Foreign Trade portal, and multimedia including music and video. It has at various times offered information about the Koryo Hotel, Air Koryo, North Korean stamps and art, and the country's trade fairs.

Access

Naenara is served from North Korea's small block of IP addresses, and operates under the .kp country-code domain. Within North Korea itself, ordinary citizens cannot access the global Internet; instead, an unrelated domestic portal of the same name runs on the closed national intranet, Kwangmyong, reachable from universities, libraries, and government offices. The external site is aimed at foreign audiences and is a primary — if heavily propagandistic — source for researchers monitoring the country.
