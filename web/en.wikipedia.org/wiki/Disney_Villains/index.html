Disney Villains is a media franchise owned by the Walt Disney Company, based on the animated antagonists of Disney films. Like its sister line, Disney Princess, it unites characters from separate films into a single brand for merchandise, publishing, games and theme-park entertainment, celebrating the villains as characters in their own right.

History and membership

The franchise encompasses villains from across Walt Disney Animation Studios' history, beginning with the Evil Queen from Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937) and extending to modern antagonists such as Dr. Facilier and King Magnifico. The most prominently marketed members include Maleficent, Ursula, Scar, Jafar, Cruella de Vil, Hades, Captain Hook and Gaston. Many of the films they menace were given direct-to-video sequels by DisneyToon Studios, several centring on the villains' offspring or schemes.

In other media

The villains are central to the Descendants film series (whose heroes are the villains' children), the Kingdom Hearts video games, in which a council of villains led by Maleficent opposes Sora, the "Villains" book series by Serena Valentino, Ravensburger's Disney Villainous board game (2018), and live-action reinterpretations such as Maleficent (2014) and Cruella (2021). At the Disney parks, villains feature in stage shows and seasonal events, and in 2024 Disney announced a villains-themed land for Magic Kingdom at Walt Disney World.

Reception

Disney's villains are frequently ranked among the greatest screen antagonists: the American Film Institute's 100 Years…100 Heroes and Villains list (2003) placed the Queen from Snow White tenth among all film villains, and Hannibal-Lecter-era comparisons aside, figures such as Scar and Ursula regularly top popular polls. Critics have both praised the characters' theatricality and debated the coding of villainy through gender, disability and foreignness in the classic films.
