Earth-1610

Earth-1610 is the designation given in the Marvel Comics multiverse to the reality depicted in the Ultimate Marvel imprint, a modernised reimagining of Marvel's superhero universe launched in 2000. Unlike the main Marvel continuity, designated Earth-616, the Ultimate Universe retold the origins of characters such as Spider-Man, the X-Men, the Fantastic Four and the Avengers (as the Ultimates) for contemporary readers, free of decades of accumulated continuity.

Publication history

The imprint began with Ultimate Spider-Man (2000), written by Brian Michael Bendis with art by Mark Bagley, followed by Ultimate X-Men, Ultimate Fantastic Four and The Ultimates. The line was notable for its cinematic pacing, updated character designs, and major divergences from classic storylines: Peter Parker was a teenager throughout, mutants were the product of government experimentation, and many characters met permanent deaths.

Ultimatum and aftermath

The 2009 crossover Ultimatum, written by Jeph Loeb, dramatically reshaped the universe by killing numerous major characters. Afterwards the line was relaunched under the "Ultimate Comics" banner. Peter Parker died heroically in 2011 and was succeeded as Spider-Man by Miles Morales, a character created for this universe who later became popular enough to cross into other media and, eventually, the mainstream Marvel continuity.

Destruction and legacy

Earth-1610 was destroyed during the 2015 Secret Wars storyline, when an incursion with Earth-616 caused the end of the multiverse. Although the universe itself ended, several of its creations — above all Miles Morales — were carried over into the reborn Marvel Universe, and versions of the Ultimate reality have reappeared in later stories and in animated and film adaptations.
