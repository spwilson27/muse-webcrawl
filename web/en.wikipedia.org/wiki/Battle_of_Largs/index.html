The Battle of Largs was an indecisive engagement fought on 2 October 1263 between Scottish forces and a Norwegian expedition under King Haakon IV, near Largs on the Firth of Clyde. It was the last battle of the Scottish–Norwegian War.

Background

Haakon sailed to Scotland with a large fleet to reassert Norwegian authority over the Hebrides and the Isle of Man, claimed by Alexander III of Scotland. Autumn storms drove several Norwegian ships ashore near Largs.

The skirmish

Norwegian crews landing to protect the beached ships were attacked by Scottish knights and infantry. Fighting was limited and confused; the Norwegians withdrew to their fleet, and both sides later claimed victory.

Aftermath

Haakon retired to Orkney, where he died that winter. By the Treaty of Perth (1266) Norway ceded the Hebrides and Man to Scotland — the real strategic outcome of the campaign.
