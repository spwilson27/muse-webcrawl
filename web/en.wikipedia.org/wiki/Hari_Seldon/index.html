Hari Seldon

Hari Seldon is a fictional character in Isaac Asimov's Foundation series of science fiction novels. A professor of mathematics at Streeling University on the planet Trantor, Seldon develops psychohistory, a mathematical science that combines history, sociology, and statistical mathematics to make general predictions about the future behaviour of very large groups of people.

Biography

Seldon was born in the 11,988th year of the Galactic Era on the planet Helicon. After presenting a paper on psychohistory at a mathematics convention on Trantor, he becomes entangled in Imperial politics, coming to the attention of the Emperor Cleon I and his chief of staff Eto Demerzel. As dramatised in Prelude to Foundation and Forward the Foundation, Seldon spends decades refining psychohistory while serving briefly as First Minister of the Empire.

The Seldon Plan

Using psychohistory, Seldon predicts the inevitable fall of the Galactic Empire, followed by a 30,000-year dark age of barbarism. Convinced the fall cannot be prevented, he devises a plan to shorten the interregnum to a single millennium by establishing a colony of encyclopedists — the Foundation — on the remote planet Terminus, ostensibly to compile the Encyclopedia Galactica. After his death, recordings of Seldon appear in the Time Vault at moments of crisis (the "Seldon crises") to guide the Foundation.

Appearances

Seldon appears in person in Prelude to Foundation and Forward the Foundation, and as a recorded hologram throughout the original trilogy: Foundation, Foundation and Empire, and Second Foundation. He is portrayed by Jared Harris in the Apple TV+ television adaptation Foundation (2021–).
