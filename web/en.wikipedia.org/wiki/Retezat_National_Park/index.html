Retezat National Park

Location: Hunedoara County, Romania
Area: 380.47 km²
Established: 1935

The Retezat National Park (Romanian: Parcul Național Retezat) is a protected area located in the Retezat Mountains in Hunedoara County, Romania. Established in 1935, it was the first national park in the country. It covers an area of about 380 square kilometres and includes more than 60 peaks over 2,300 metres and over 80 glacial lakes, including Bucura Lake, the largest glacial lake in Romania.

Geography

The park's highest peak is Peleaga, at 2,509 metres. The massif preserves extensive primeval forests and alpine habitats, and part of the park is designated a UNESCO biosphere reserve. The Gemenele ("The Twins" in Romanian) is a scientific reserve within the park with strict protection status.

Flora and fauna

The park is home to about 1,190 plant species, a large fraction of Romania's flora, as well as wolves, brown bears, lynx, chamois, marmots, and numerous bird species.
