Adi-Buddha

In Mahayana and Vajrayana Buddhism, the Ādi-Buddha is the "First Buddha" or the "Primordial Buddha", the self-existing, timeless source from which the Five Dhyani Buddhas emanate.

Traditions

In Tibetan Buddhism, the Adi-Buddha is represented as Samantabhadra (in Nyingma) or Vajradhara (in Sarma schools). In Nepalese Buddhism, Svayambhu is worshipped as Adi-Buddha. The concept appears in the Kalachakra Tantra and the Namasangiti.

Interpretation

The Adi-Buddha is generally understood not as a creator god but as an embodiment of dharmakaya — ultimate reality and awakened mind itself.
