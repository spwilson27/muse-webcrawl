Salvo Montalbano

Salvo Montalbano is a fictional police inspector created by Italian writer Andrea Camilleri. He is the protagonist of a long-running series of crime novels and short stories set in the fictional Sicilian town of Vigàta, and of the television series Inspector Montalbano.

Character

Montalbano is named in homage to Spanish writer Manuel Vázquez Montalbán. He is a lover of Sicilian food and literature, irascible with superiors and the media, incorruptible but pragmatic, and lives in a seaside house at Marinella with his long-distance girlfriend Livia Burlando in Genoa. His team includes deputy Mimì Augello, loyal Fazio, and comic switchboard operator Catarella.

Books and television

The first novel, The Shape of Water (1994), was followed by more than two dozen books, ending with Riccardino, written in 2006 but published posthumously in 2020 after Camilleri's death. The RAI television adaptation, starring Luca Zingaretti and filmed around Ragusa, Scicli and Modica, ran from 1999 to 2021 and was exported worldwide, including a prequel, The Young Montalbano.
