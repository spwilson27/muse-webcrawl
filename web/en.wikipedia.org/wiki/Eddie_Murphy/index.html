Eddie Murphy

Edward Regan Murphy (born 3 April 1961) is an American comedian, actor and singer. He rose to fame as a cast member of Saturday Night Live from 1980 to 1984 and became one of the most successful film stars of the 1980s and 1990s. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest stand-up comedians of all time.

Career

Born in Brooklyn, New York, Murphy joined Saturday Night Live at nineteen and created characters such as Buckwheat, Gumby and Mr. Robinson. His film debut in 48 Hrs. (1982) launched a run of hits including Trading Places (1983), the Beverly Hills Cop series (beginning 1984), Coming to America (1988) and his stand-up concert films Delirious (1983) and Raw (1987). In the 1990s he headlined The Nutty Professor (1996), playing multiple roles, and later found success in family films such as Mulan (1998, voice), Dr. Dolittle (1998) and the Shrek franchise as the voice of Donkey.

Murphy received an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor and won a Golden Globe for his performance as soul singer James "Thunder" Early in Dreamgirls (2006). He won critical praise again for Dolemite Is My Name (2019), in which he played filmmaker Rudy Ray Moore, and reprised signature roles in Coming 2 America (2021) and Beverly Hills Cop: Axel F (2024).

Honours

Murphy's accolades include the Mark Twain Prize for American Humor (2015), the Cecil B. DeMille Award (2023), a Grammy Award, and an Emmy Award for guest-hosting Saturday Night Live in 2019. His films have grossed billions of dollars worldwide, making him one of the highest-grossing actors in history.
