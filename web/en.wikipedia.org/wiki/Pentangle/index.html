Pentangle

Pentangle are a British folk rock band formed in London in 1967. The original line-up consisted of Jacqui McShee (vocals), John Renbourn and Bert Jansch (guitars), Danny Thompson (double bass), and Terry Cox (drums). Their music blended traditional folk with jazz, blues, and early music influences, and the virtuoso interplay of Renbourn and Jansch on acoustic guitars became a hallmark of their sound.

The band's albums include Pentangle (1968), Sweet Child (1968), and the commercially successful Basket of Light (1969), which contained the single "Light Flight", used as the theme for the BBC television series Take Three Girls.

The original group disbanded in 1973, but various later line-ups led by McShee have continued under the name, and the original members reunited for tours and recordings in later years. Pentangle are regarded as one of the most influential bands of the British folk revival.
