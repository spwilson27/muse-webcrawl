Inglewood, California

Inglewood
City in Los Angeles County, California
Founded: 1888; incorporated 1908
Population: about 108,000 (2020 census)

Inglewood is a city in southwestern Los Angeles County, California, United States, in the South Bay region of the Los Angeles metropolitan area, near Los Angeles International Airport. Founded in 1888 and incorporated in 1908, it developed as a residential and agricultural community before becoming a largely suburban city.

Sports and entertainment

Inglewood is known for its major sports and entertainment venues. The Forum, opened in 1967, was long home to the Los Angeles Lakers and Kings. Hollywood Park Racetrack operated from 1938 to 2013; its site was redeveloped as SoFi Stadium, opened in 2020 and home to the Los Angeles Rams and Chargers of the NFL, host of Super Bowl LVI in 2022. The neighbouring Intuit Dome, home of the Los Angeles Clippers, opened in 2024.

Geography and demographics

The city covers about 9 square miles and had a population of roughly 108,000 at the 2020 census, with large African American and Latino communities that have shaped its cultural life and music scene.
