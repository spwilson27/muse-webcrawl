Noto fonts

Noto is a font family comprising more than 100 individual fonts developed by Google, designed to achieve visual harmony across multiple languages and scripts. The name derives from "no tofu": in computing, tofu is the nickname for the blank rectangle characters (□) shown when a system lacks a glyph for a character; Noto aims to eliminate them by covering all of Unicode.

Coverage

The family includes Noto Sans and Noto Serif variants for scripts such as Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Arabic, Devanagari, Chinese (via the CJK collaboration with Adobe, Source Han Sans / Noto Sans CJK), Japanese, Korean, Hebrew, Armenian, Georgian, Ethiopic, and many minority and historical scripts. By the mid-2010s Noto covered over 800 languages and all of the Unicode standard's scripts then encoded.

Licensing and use

Noto fonts are released under the SIL Open Font License and are default fonts in Android and ChromeOS. They are widely used in multilingual publishing and interface design.
