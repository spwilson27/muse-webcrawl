Natural gas pipeline

A natural gas pipeline is a pipeline used to transport natural gas from producing fields to processing plants, storage facilities and end users. Large-diameter transmission pipelines move gas at high pressure over long distances, while smaller distribution networks deliver it at lower pressure to homes and businesses.

System components

Transmission systems include compressor stations that maintain pressure along the line, metering and regulating stations, block valves and control systems that monitor flow and detect leaks. Gas is often odorised with mercaptan before distribution so that leaks can be detected by smell.

Major pipelines

Among the world's longest gas pipelines are the West–East Gas Pipeline in China, the Yamal–Europe pipeline and the network historically linking Siberian fields to Europe, and extensive interstate systems in the United States, where pipeline safety is overseen by the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration.
