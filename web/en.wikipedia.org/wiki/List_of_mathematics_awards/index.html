List of mathematics awards

This list of mathematics awards collects notable prizes recognising achievement in mathematics. The most prestigious is generally considered to be the Fields Medal, awarded every four years to mathematicians under 40.

National and subject awards

National societies award prizes such as the Cole Prize and Steele Prize (American Mathematical Society), the Sylvester Medal (Royal Society), and the Fermat Prize (France). Subject-specific prizes include the Gödel Prize in theoretical computer science, the Loève Prize in probability, and the Nevanlinna Prize (now the IMU Abacus Medal) in information sciences.

Competitions

For younger mathematicians, major competitions include the International Mathematical Olympiad, the Putnam Competition, and national olympiads feeding into them.
