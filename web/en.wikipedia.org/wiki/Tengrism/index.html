Tengrism
Central deity: Tengri (sky god)
Region: Eurasian Steppe — Turkic and Mongolic peoples
Other figures: Umay, Erlik, Ülgen

Tengrism is the traditional religion of Turkic, Mongolic and other Eurasian steppe peoples, centred on worship of Tengri, the eternal blue sky, along with earth, water and ancestral spirits. Shamans mediated between humans and spirits.

Beliefs

Tengri was seen as supreme but not anthropomorphic; rulers such as Genghis Khan claimed a mandate from the Eternal Blue Heaven (Mönkh Tengri). The world was divided into upper, middle and underworlds, linked by a world tree.

History

Attested in Orkhon inscriptions of the Göktürk Khaganate, Tengrism underlay the Mongol Empire's religious tolerance and has seen modern revivals in Central Asia, Mongolia, Sakha and Tatarstan.
