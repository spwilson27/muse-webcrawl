Reading comprehension

Reading comprehension is the ability to process written text, understand its meaning, and integrate it with prior knowledge. It depends on decoding fluency and language comprehension, as framed by the Simple View of Reading.

Components and instruction

Skilled comprehension involves vocabulary, inference-making, working memory, and use of strategies such as summarising, questioning, predicting, and monitoring understanding. Instruction research by the National Reading Panel identified comprehension strategy instruction, vocabulary teaching, and text discussion as effective supports. Comprehension is assessed in programmes such as PIRLS and PISA.
