The History of Whoo

The History of Whoo (Korean: 더 후) is a luxury Korean skincare and cosmetics brand launched in 2003 by LG Household & Health Care. The brand draws on traditional Korean herbal medicine (hanbang) and the beauty regimens of royal court women.

Products

The brand's best-known lines include Bichup (featuring anti-aging essences), Gongjinhyang, Cheongidan, and Hwanyu. Formulations prominently feature ingredients such as ginseng, deer antler, and other traditional herbal extracts combined with modern cosmetic science.

Market

The History of Whoo became one of the most successful luxury beauty brands in South Korea and China, with annual sales exceeding one trillion won at its peak, and is sold through department stores, duty-free shops, and flagship boutiques across Asia.
