Mian Muhammad Nawaz Sharif (born 25 December 1949) is a Pakistani businessman and politician who served three non-consecutive terms as Prime Minister of Pakistan — 1990 to 1993, 1997 to 1999, and 2013 to 2017. He is the elder brother of Shehbaz Sharif and leads the Pakistan Muslim League (N). No elected prime minister of Pakistan has completed a full five-year term, and Sharif's career has repeatedly been cut short by confrontation with the military establishment and the judiciary.

Early life and entry into politics

Sharif was born in Lahore into the industrialist Sharif family, owners of the Ittefaq Group of steel businesses. He entered politics under the military ruler Zia-ul-Haq, becoming finance minister and then Chief Minister of Punjab (1985–1990). After Zia's death he led the Islami Jamhoori Ittehad alliance to victory in the 1990 election.

Terms as prime minister

His first government pursued economic liberalisation and privatisation before President Ghulam Ishaq Khan dismissed it in 1993; restored briefly by the Supreme Court, Sharif resigned amid deadlock. Returned to power in 1997 with a heavy majority, his second government amended the constitution to strengthen the premiership, conducted Pakistan's nuclear tests in May 1998 in response to India's, and froze foreign-currency accounts amid sanctions. Relations with the army collapsed after the 1999 Kargil conflict, and on 12 October 1999 General Pervez Musharraf overthrew him in a coup. Sharif was convicted and, in December 2000, exiled to Saudi Arabia.

Return, third term and disqualification

Sharif returned to Pakistan in 2007 and won the 2013 election. His third government launched the China–Pakistan Economic Corridor projects, fought an energy crisis and oversaw military operations against militants. In July 2017 the Supreme Court disqualified him from office following the Panama Papers revelations about his family's offshore companies and London properties. In 2018 he was convicted of corruption and imprisoned; released on bail for medical treatment, he left for London in 2019 and remained there in self-exile for about four years. He returned to Pakistan in October 2023 after his convictions were overturned or suspended, and after the February 2024 election his brother Shehbaz became prime minister for a second time, with Nawaz Sharif remaining PML-N leader and later being elected party president again.
