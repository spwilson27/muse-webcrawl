The Baylor Bears men's basketball team represents Baylor University in Waco, Texas, in NCAA Division I college basketball. The Bears compete in the Big 12 Conference.

History

Baylor reached the Final Four in 1948 and 1950. The programme was rebuilt under coach Scott Drew after a 2003 scandal, rising to national prominence in the 2010s.

2021 championship

In 2021 the Bears defeated Gonzaga in the NCAA championship game to win their first national title, finishing 28–2.

Arena

The team long played at the Ferrell Center and moved to the Foster Pavilion in 2024.
