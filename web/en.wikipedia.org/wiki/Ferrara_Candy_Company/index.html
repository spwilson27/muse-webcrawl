Ferrara Candy Company

The Ferrara Candy Company is an American candy manufacturer headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. It is part of the Ferrero Group and makes many of the best-known non-chocolate candy brands in the United States, including Lemonheads, Red Hots, Atomic Fireballs, Nerds, Sweetarts, Trolli gummies, Black Forest gummies, and Brach's confections.

History

The company traces its origins to 1908, when Salvatore Ferrara, an Italian immigrant, began selling sugar-coated almonds (confetti) in Chicago's Little Italy; the Ferrara Pan Candy Company formalized in 1919 in Forest Park, Illinois. In 2012 Ferrara Pan merged with Farley's & Sathers to form the Ferrara Candy Company, and in 2017 the Ferrero Group acquired it. Ferrara later also acquired Nestlé's U.S. candy business (2018) and the Kellogg's fruit snacks and Keebler cookie businesses as part of subsequent Ferrero transactions.
