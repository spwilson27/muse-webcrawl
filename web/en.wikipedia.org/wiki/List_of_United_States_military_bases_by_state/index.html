List of United States military bases by state

The United States Department of Defense operates hundreds of military installations across the fifty states, ranging from major multi-service bases to small reserve centers. This list organizes notable installations by state and service branch (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard).
