The L0 Series may refer to the JR Central L0 Series maglev train (Japanese: リニアL0系), a manned high-speed maglev train under development by Central Japan Railway Company (JR Central) for the Chūō Shinkansen line, and holder of the world speed record for rail vehicles.

Overview

The L0 Series is based on the superconducting maglev (SCMaglev) technology tested since the 1970s. The train is designed for a commercial service speed of 500 km/h (311 mph), which would carry passengers between Tokyo and Nagoya in about 40 minutes on the Chūō Shinkansen.

On 21 April 2015, a seven-car L0 Series train set a world railway speed record of 603 km/h (375 mph) on the Yamanashi Maglev Test Line, surpassing earlier records by JR Central test vehicles and the 574.8 km/h record of a conventional French TGV.

Design

The vehicles feature a long nose (about 15 metres) on end cars to reduce pressure waves (tunnel boom) when entering tunnels at high speed, and ride on superconducting magnets that levitate the train about 10 cm above the guideway once above roughly 150 km/h; below that speed the train runs on rubber tyres. Power is supplied by induction from the guideway.
