John Dominic Crossan

John Dominic Crossan (born 17 February 1934) is an Irish-American New Testament scholar, historian of early Christianity and former Catholic priest who was a prominent member of the Jesus Seminar. He is widely regarded as a leading authority on the historical Jesus.

Life

Crossan was born in Nenagh, County Tipperary, Ireland. He was ordained a priest in the Servite order in 1957 and received his doctorate from Maynooth College in 1959. After two years of studying Semitic languages at the Pontifical Biblical Institute in Rome, he left the priesthood in 1969 and married. He taught at DePaul University in Chicago for over twenty years.

Historical Jesus scholarship

Crossan's major works include The Historical Jesus: The Life of a Mediterranean Jewish Peasant (1991) and Jesus: A Revolutionary Biography (1994). He portrays Jesus as a peasant healer and egalitarian preacher in Roman-occupied Galilee, and argues that the earliest Christian movement was shaped by this social and religious context.

As co-founder of the Jesus Seminar in 1985, Crossan helped promote critical scholarly approaches to the Gospels to a wider public.
