The Apple Vision Pro is a mixed-reality headset developed by Apple Inc., announced on June 5, 2023, at Apple's Worldwide Developers Conference and released in the United States on February 2, 2024. Apple markets the device as a "spatial computer": digital content is blended with the user's physical surroundings through high-resolution colour passthrough cameras, and interaction is by eyes, hands, and voice rather than controllers. It runs visionOS, an operating system derived from Apple's other platforms with a three-dimensional interface.

Vision Pro's front panel uses outward-facing displays for the EyeSight feature.

Hardware

Vision Pro uses two micro-OLED displays totalling 23 million pixels (more than a 4K television per eye), powered by an Apple M2 chip together with a dedicated R1 chip that processes input from 12 cameras, five sensors, and six microphones, streaming images to the displays within 12 milliseconds to minimise latency. The headset is constructed from aluminium and laminated glass, is powered by an external tethered battery pack providing about two to two-and-a-half hours of use, and uses custom ZEISS optical inserts for users who require vision correction. An outward-facing display implements EyeSight, showing a rendering of the wearer's eyes to people nearby, and Optic ID provides iris-based authentication.

Software and content

visionOS supports apps arranged in the user's space, including most iPad apps, native spatial apps from the App Store, immersive environments, spatial photos and videos (which can also be recorded on iPhone 15 Pro and later), a virtual Mac display, and personas — digital avatars used in FaceTime calls. Entertainment partnerships brought immersive content from Disney+ and others, and Apple has produced Apple Immersive Video programming, including sports and documentary content.

Reception and market

Reviewers praised the display quality, eye-and-hand tracking, and build, while criticising the price, weight and front-heavy comfort, short battery life, the uncanny quality of personas, and a limited library of native spatial apps. Sales were modest relative to Apple's other products — analysts estimated first-year shipments in the hundreds of thousands — and Apple reportedly scaled back production plans late in 2024 while continuing development of follow-up models. The device launched internationally in stages through 2024 in countries including China, Japan, the United Kingdom, and Australia.
