The Sacra Corona Unita (SCU; Italian for "United Sacred Crown") is a Mafia-type criminal organisation based in the Apulia (Puglia) region of southern Italy, particularly in the Salento area around Lecce, Brindisi and Taranto. It is generally considered the fourth major Italian criminal syndicate, alongside Sicily's Cosa Nostra, Calabria's 'Ndrangheta and Campania's Camorra.

History

The Sacra Corona Unita emerged in the late 1970s and early 1980s. Its founder, Giuseppe Rogoli, a convicted criminal from Mesagne, is said to have established the organisation while in prison, drawing inspiration from the rituals and structure of the 'Ndrangheta, with the intention of giving Apulian criminals an autonomous organisation rather than leaving the region to outside mafias. The name, with its religious overtones, reflects the quasi-sacral initiation rites, in which new members swear oaths before a crown- or rosary-like emblem.

Activities

The SCU built its wealth through cigarette and drug smuggling across the Adriatic Sea from the Balkans — particularly Albania and Montenegro — as well as extortion, usury, arms trafficking, illegal gambling and money laundering. Exploiting Apulia's long coastline and proximity to the Balkans, its clans moved large quantities of heroin, cocaine, cannabis and contraband cigarettes into Italy and onward into Europe. The organisation has also been involved in trafficking migrants across the Adriatic.

Structure and clans

Unlike the more hierarchical 'Ndrangheta, the SCU has historically been a looser federation of clans, often divided by local rivalries. Its structure includes ranks modelled on other mafias, with initiation rituals involving oaths of loyalty. Internal feuds between factions have periodically erupted into violence, notably in the 1990s.

Law enforcement response

From the 1990s onward, Italian authorities cracked down heavily on the SCU, arresting many of its bosses and securing testimony from numerous pentiti (turncoats). Although weakened by these operations and by internal fragmentation, the organisation's clans remain active in drug trafficking and extortion in Apulia, and SCU affiliates have been reported operating elsewhere in Italy and abroad.
