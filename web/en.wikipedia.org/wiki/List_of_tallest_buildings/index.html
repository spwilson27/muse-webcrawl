The Burj Khalifa in Dubai has been the world's tallest building since 2010. This list of tallest buildings ranks completed skyscrapers by architectural height, as defined by the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH).

History of the record

The Empire State Building held the title for nearly four decades (1931–1970). The original World Trade Center towers, the Sears (Willis) Tower, the Petronas Towers and Taipei 101 each held it before the Burj Khalifa. No building currently under construction is expected to surpass the Burj Khalifa, whose height record has stood since 2010.
