List of rivers of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom's rivers are relatively short, reflecting the size of the islands, but they are numerous and historically important for transport, industry and settlement. The longest river entirely in the UK is the River Severn; the longest in England is the River Thames; and the longest in Scotland is the River Tay, which also has the largest discharge and catchment of any UK river.
