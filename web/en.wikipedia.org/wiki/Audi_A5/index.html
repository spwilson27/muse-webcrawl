The Audi A5 is a series of compact executive cars produced by the German automobile manufacturer Audi since 2007. Based on the same platform as the Audi A4 saloon, the A5 line recast Audi's offering in the segment as a stylish coupé, later joined by a convertible (Cabriolet) and a five-door liftback (Sportback). Performance versions include the S5 and the high-performance RS 5.

First generation (2007–2016)

Unveiled at the Geneva Motor Show in March 2007, the A5 coupé was designed under Walter de Silva, who called it the most beautiful car he had designed. It was built on Audi's new MLB platform with the engine mounted further back for improved weight distribution. Engines ranged from a 1.8-litre turbocharged four-cylinder to a 4.2-litre V8 in the RS 5, with front-wheel drive or quattro all-wheel drive. The Sportback five-door version followed in 2009, and a facelift arrived for 2012.

Second generation (2016–2024)

The second-generation A5, revealed in June 2016, adopted Audi's MLB Evo architecture, lighter construction, matrix LED lighting, and the Virtual Cockpit digital instrument display. The S5 switched to a turbocharged 3.0-litre V6, while the RS 5 used a 2.9-litre twin-turbocharged V6 developed with Porsche.

Third generation (2024–present)

In 2024 Audi restructured its naming so that even numbers denote electric models and odd numbers combustion models; the A4 line was accordingly replaced by a new A5 family, offered as a saloon-style liftback and Avant estate, built on the Premium Platform Combustion (PPC), with a new S5 as the performance flagship.
