Tencent QQ, popularly known simply as QQ, is an instant messaging software service and web portal developed by the Chinese technology company Tencent. QQ offers online social games, music, shopping, microblogging, movies and group and voice chat. For much of the 2000s and 2010s it was one of the world's largest online communities, reporting over 800 million monthly active accounts at its peak, and its penguin mascot became a cultural icon in China.

History

QQ was first released in February 1999 under the name OICQ ("Oh, I Seek You", a play on ICQ). After a trademark dispute with ICQ's owner AOL, the service was renamed QQ. The service grew rapidly with China's internet boom, and users' numeric QQ IDs became a standard item of personal contact information. Tencent built an extensive ecosystem around QQ, including the Qzone social network, QQ Games, and a virtual currency, Q coins.

Decline and current status

From the early 2010s, QQ's user base — especially among adults — migrated to Tencent's newer mobile-first platform WeChat. QQ nevertheless remains popular with younger users in China for file transfer, group communities and gaming features, and continues to receive development, including a rewritten cross-platform client in the 2020s.
