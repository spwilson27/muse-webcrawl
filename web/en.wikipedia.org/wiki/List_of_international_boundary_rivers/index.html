List of international boundary rivers

Rivers form a large share of the world's international land boundaries. A river boundary is usually defined along the thalweg (the line of deepest channel) or the median line of the river, and can become disputed when the river shifts course.

The legal treatment of boundary rivers was addressed in cases such as the Chamizal dispute between the United States and Mexico over shifts in the Rio Grande. Many boundary rivers are managed jointly by commissions, such as the International Joint Commission for the United States–Canada boundary waters.
