Pauillac

Pauillac is a commune in the Gironde department in Nouvelle-Aquitaine in southwestern France, situated on the left bank of the Gironde estuary in the Médoc wine region. It is best known as one of the most prestigious wine appellations in Bordeaux.

Wine

The Pauillac AOC covers about 1,200 hectares of vineyards planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. Three of the five Bordeaux first growths of the 1855 classification are located in Pauillac: Château Lafite Rothschild, Château Latour and Château Mouton Rothschild.

Geography

The town lies roughly 50 kilometres north-west of Bordeaux. Its gravelly soils, deposited by the Gironde, provide excellent drainage that suits Cabernet Sauvignon particularly well.
