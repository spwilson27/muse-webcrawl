Dawn of X

Dawn of X is a 2019 relaunch of Marvel Comics' X-Men line of comic books, following the House of X and Powers of X miniseries written by Jonathan Hickman. The relaunch established a new status quo in which mutants founded the sovereign island nation of Krakoa and offered humans miraculous medicines in exchange for recognition.

Titles

The initial wave of titles included X-Men, Marauders, Excalibur, New Mutants, X-Force, and Fallen Angels, with further series such as Hellions, Wolverine, and X-Factor following. Hickman served as "Head of X," coordinating the line.

Subsequent phases

Dawn of X was followed by the Reign of X (2020), Destiny of X (2022), and Fall of X (2023) phases, concluding the Krakoan Age in 2024.
