Battle of Five Armies

The Battle of Five Armies is a fictional battle in J. R. R. Tolkien's 1937 novel The Hobbit. Fought at the Lonely Mountain (Erebor), it pits the combined forces of Men of the Lake, Elves of the Woodland Realm and Dwarves of the Iron Hills against an army of Goblins (Orcs) and Wargs. It forms the climax of the novel.

The battle at Erebor

Plot

After the dragon Smaug is killed by Bard the Bowman and the Dwarves of Thorin Oakenshield reclaim Erebor, Thorin refuses to share the treasure with the Lake-men and Elves who claim a portion. The Men and Elves besiege the Mountain, and Thorin's cousin Dáin Ironfoot arrives with a Dwarf army; battle between the free peoples is narrowly averted when Gandalf warns of an approaching host of Goblins and Wargs, seeking revenge for the death of the Great Goblin and drawn by news of Smaug's death.

The battle

The Dwarves, Elves and Men unite against the common enemy. The Goblins, commanded by Bolg, son of Azog, attack in overwhelming numbers up the slopes of the Mountain; the allies are driven back and Thorin makes a desperate sally from the gate. The tide turns with the arrival of the Eagles of the Misty Mountains and of Beorn in bear-shape, who kills Bolg and scatters the Goblin host. Thorin is mortally wounded, as are his nephews Fíli and Kíli, who die defending him.

Aftermath

Thorin is buried in Erebor with the Arkenstone on his breast and the sword Orcrist laid on his tomb. Dáin becomes King under the Mountain; the treasure is shared, Dale and Lake-town are rebuilt, and Bilbo returns home to the Shire. The battle established peace in the North that endured until the War of the Ring.
