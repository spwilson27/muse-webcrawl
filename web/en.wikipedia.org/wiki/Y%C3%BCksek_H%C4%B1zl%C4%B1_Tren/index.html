Yüksek Hızlı Tren

OperatorTCDD Taşımacılık (Turkish State Railways)
First service13 March 2009 (Ankara–Eskişehir)
Maximum speed250 km/h (155 mph) in service
TrainsHT65000 (CAF), HT80000 Velaro TR (Siemens)
Main routesAnkara–Istanbul, Ankara–Konya, Ankara–Sivas, Konya–Karaman

Yüksek Hızlı Tren (YHT, "High-Speed Train") is the high-speed rail service of Turkey, operated by TCDD Taşımacılık. Turkey became the sixth country in Europe and eighth in the world to operate high-speed trains when the Ankara–Eskişehir line opened in 2009.

Network development

Services were extended to Konya in 2011, to Istanbul (Pendik, later Söğütlüçeşme and Halkalı via the Marmaray tunnel) in 2014–2019, to Karaman in 2022, and to Sivas in 2023, cutting Ankara–Sivas journey times to about two hours. Further lines toward İzmir, Bursa, Gaziantep and the Bulgarian border are under construction or planned.

Rolling stock and operations

The fleet comprises CAF-built HT65000 sets and Siemens Velaro TR HT80000 sets rated at 250 km/h. Trains run on dedicated standard-gauge high-speed lines separate from the conventional network; services are centred on Ankara Gar station and its adjacent Ankara YHT terminal.
