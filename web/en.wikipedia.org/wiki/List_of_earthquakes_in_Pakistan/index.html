List of earthquakes in Pakistan

Pakistan is situated on the northwestern margin of the Indian Plate, where it collides with the Eurasian Plate. This list of earthquakes in Pakistan records the country's most significant seismic events, concentrated in Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Kashmir.

Seismic zones

The Chaman fault system in the west, the Makran subduction zone off the southern coast, and the Himalayan collision zone in the north are the principal sources of major earthquakes. The 2005 Kashmir earthquake prompted major reforms in building codes and the creation of the Earthquake Reconstruction and Rehabilitation Authority.
