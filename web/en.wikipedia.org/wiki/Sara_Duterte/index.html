Sara Duterte
Born: 31 May 1978, Davao City
Office: Vice President of the Philippines (from 2022)
Father: Rodrigo Duterte

Sara Zimmerman Duterte (born 1978), commonly known as Inday Sara, is a Philippine politician who has served as the 15th Vice President of the Philippines since 2022. She is the daughter of former president Rodrigo Duterte.

Career

A lawyer by training, Duterte entered politics in Davao City, serving as vice mayor (2007–2010) and then mayor (2010–2013 and 2016–2022), succeeding her father in the family's political stronghold. She founded the regional party Hugpong ng Pagbabago. In the 2022 elections she ran for vice president in tandem with Ferdinand "Bongbong" Marcos Jr.; their ticket won in a landslide. She also served as Secretary of Education from 2022 until her resignation in 2024.

Controversies

Her tenure saw disputes with the Marcos administration, scrutiny of confidential funds spent by her offices, and, in 2025, an impeachment complaint passed by the House of Representatives — developments that made her a central figure in Philippine politics and a prospective presidential contender.
