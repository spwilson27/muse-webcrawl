Göta älv

The Göta älv ("River of the Geats") is a river in Sweden that drains Lake Vänern, the country's largest lake, into the Kattegat at Gothenburg. Together with the Klarälven upstream, it forms Sweden's longest river system, about 750 km in total, while the Göta älv proper is about 93 km long.

Göta älv
Length: ~93 km
Source: Lake Vänern
Mouth: Kattegat at Gothenburg
Mean discharge: ~575 m3/s

Course

The river flows southwest from Vänersborg past Trollhättan, where waterfalls were harnessed for hydroelectric power and bypassed by the Trollhätte Canal locks, then past Lilla Edet and Kungälv, where it splits around the island of Hisingen before reaching Gothenburg.

Importance

The Göta älv supplies drinking water to Gothenburg and supports navigation, industry and Sweden's largest river discharge by volume.
