National Crime Records Bureau

NCRB
Formed: 11 March 1986
Headquarters: New Delhi, India
Parent: Ministry of Home Affairs

The National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) is an Indian government agency responsible for collecting and analysing crime data as defined by the Indian Penal Code and special and local laws. Headquartered in New Delhi, it functions under the Ministry of Home Affairs and was set up in 1986 by merging the Directorate of Coordination and Police Computer, the Central Finger Print Bureau, and related units.

Functions

The NCRB's flagship publication is the annual Crime in India report, presenting statistics on reported crimes across all states and union territories. The bureau also publishes Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India and Prison Statistics India. It operates the Crime and Criminal Tracking Network and Systems (CCTNS), which links police stations across the country, and the Central Finger Print Bureau, which maintains fingerprint records and supports the national automated fingerprint identification system.

Other work

The bureau runs the National Digital Police Portal, coordinates the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre's reporting portal for certain functions, and maintains databases on missing persons, stolen vehicles, and property. It also trains police personnel in information technology and fingerprint science.
