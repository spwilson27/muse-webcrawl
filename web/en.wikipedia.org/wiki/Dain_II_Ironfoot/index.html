Dáin II Ironfoot

Dáin II Ironfoot is a fictional character in J. R. R. Tolkien's legendarium. A Dwarf of Durin's Folk, he was Lord of the Iron Hills and later King under the Mountain at Erebor, succeeding Thorin Oakenshield. He is a prominent figure in the appendices to The Lord of the Rings and is mentioned in The Hobbit.

Biography

Dáin was born in the Iron Hills in the year 2767 of the Third Age. As a youth he fought in the War of the Dwarves and Orcs, and at the Battle of Azanulbizar (2799) he slew the Orc chieftain Azog, avenging his father Náin, who had fallen in the same battle — a feat that earned him great renown while still very young by Dwarven standards.

In 2941, after Smaug's death, Dáin led five hundred Dwarves from the Iron Hills to Erebor at Thorin's summons and fought in the Battle of Five Armies. When Thorin and his heirs Fíli and Kíli were slain, Dáin became King under the Mountain and ruled Erebor wisely for nearly eighty years, restoring its wealth and its friendship with the Men of Dale.

During the War of the Ring, Dáin, now over 250 years old, defended Erebor against the Easterlings in the Battle of Dale (3019) and was killed fighting before the gates of the Mountain, defending the body of King Brand of Dale. He was succeeded by his son Thorin III Stonehelm.

Adaptations

Dáin appears in Peter Jackson's The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (2014), played by Billy Connolly.
