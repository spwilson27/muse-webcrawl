Kach

Kach (Hebrew: כ״ך) was an Israeli political party and movement founded by Rabbi Meir Kahane in 1971. It advocated policies derived from Kahane's ideology of Kahanism, including the transfer of Arabs from Israel and the establishment of a Jewish theocracy.

Kach won a single Knesset seat in the 1984 elections, with Kahane as its representative. Before the 1988 elections it was barred from running by the Central Elections Committee under anti-racism legislation, a ban upheld by the Israeli Supreme Court.

After Kahane's assassination in New York in 1990, the movement split, and in 1994 — following the Cave of the Patriarchs massacre committed by a supporter — Kach and its offshoot Kahane Chai were designated terrorist organisations and banned by the Israeli government; they were also listed as terrorist organisations by the United States and other countries.
