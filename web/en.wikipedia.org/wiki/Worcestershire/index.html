Worcestershire

Worcestershire is a ceremonial county in the West Midlands of England, with county town Worcester. It is known for the Malvern Hills, the Vale of Evesham fruit-growing, and Worcestershire sauce.

Region: West Midlands
County town: Worcester
Population: ~610,000

Geography and history

The River Severn flows through Worcester; the Malvern Hills rise in the west and the Cotswolds edge the south-east. The county saw the final battle of the English Civil War at Worcester (1651). From 1974 to 1998 it was merged with Herefordshire as Hereford and Worcester.

Culture

Composer Edward Elgar was born in Lower Broadheath. Lea & Perrins Worcestershire sauce originated in Worcester. See Worcester Cathedral, Malvern Hills.
