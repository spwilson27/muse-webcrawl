List of former and unopened London Underground stations

Over its history, the London Underground has closed a number of stations — because of low usage, wartime, relocation or line closures — while others were built but never opened to passengers. Many survive as "ghost stations" passed by trains today.

Unopened stations

  • North End (Bull and Bush) — on the Northern line near Hampstead; construction abandoned in 1906, platforms built but never fitted out.
  • Brockley Hill, Elstree South and Bushey Heath — planned Northern line extension stations whose works were cancelled after the Second World War.

Aldwych is frequently used as a filming location and for training. The Epping–Ongar section closed in 1994 now operates as the heritage Epping Ongar Railway.
