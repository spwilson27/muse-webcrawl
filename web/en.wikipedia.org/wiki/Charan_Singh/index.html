Charan Singh (23 December 1902 – 29 May 1987) was an Indian politician and independence activist who served as the fifth prime minister of India from July 1979 to January 1980. A champion of farmers' interests, he is remembered for land reform and agrarian advocacy in Uttar Pradesh.

Career

Singh served twice as Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh and as Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister in the Janata government of Morarji Desai. As revenue minister in Uttar Pradesh he was the architect of the state's land ceiling and zamindari abolition measures.

Premiership

He became prime minister in 1979 with Congress support, which was soon withdrawn; he resigned before facing a confidence vote and served as caretaker until the 1980 elections. His birthday, 23 December, is commemorated in India as Kisan Diwas (Farmers' Day).
