Swamp Thing

Swamp Thing is a superhero and antihero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Len Wein and artist Bernie Wrightson, the character first appeared in House of Secrets #92 (1971) before headlining his own series. He is a swamp monster — a plant elemental creature who fights to protect his swamp home, the environment in general, and humanity from supernatural or terrorist threats.

Concept and history

In the original origin, scientist Alec Holland, doused with a burning bio-restorative formula, plunged into the swamp and emerged transformed. Writer Alan Moore's acclaimed run beginning in 1984 (with artists Stephen Bissette and John Totleben) revised this: Swamp Thing was not Holland at all but a plant that had absorbed the dead man's memories, an elemental connected to "the Green", the collective life force of vegetation. Moore's run introduced John Constantine and dealt with environmental, horror, and philosophical themes, helping establish the mature sensibility that led to DC's Vertigo imprint.

In other media

The character has been adapted into two films (1982 and 1989), a 1990–1993 live-action television series, an animated series, and a 2019 streaming series on DC Universe, cancelled after one season despite critical praise. He has also appeared in animated Justice League Dark productions.
