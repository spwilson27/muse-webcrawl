Michael Bakari Jordan (born 9 February 1987) is an American actor, producer and director. He first gained recognition for television roles as Wallace in The Wire (2002), Reggie Montgomery in All My Children and Vince Howard in Friday Night Lights (2009–2011).

Career

Jordan's film breakthrough came with Fruitvale Station (2013), directed by Ryan Coogler, in which he played Oscar Grant. He went on to star as boxer Adonis Creed in Creed (2015) and its sequels, making his directorial debut with Creed III (2023), which was a major commercial success. His portrayal of Erik Killmonger in Marvel's Black Panther (2018) was widely praised as one of the finest performances in a superhero film. Other notable roles include Just Mercy (2019), in which he played civil rights lawyer Bryan Stevenson, and the title role in Without Remorse (2021).

Production and business

Through his company Outlier Society, Jordan produces film and television projects and has advocated for inclusion riders in production. He became a part-owner of the English football club AFC Bournemouth in 2022. Time named him one of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2020 and 2023, and he was named People magazine's Sexiest Man Alive in 2020.
