Katsudon
CourseMain course
Place of originJapan
Main ingredientsTonkatsu, egg, onion, dashi, rice

Katsudon (Japanese: カツ丼) is a Japanese rice bowl dish topped with tonkatsu — a deep-fried breaded pork cutlet — simmered with beaten egg, sliced onion and a sweet-savoury dashi, soy and mirin sauce, served over steamed rice.

Preparation

A leftover or freshly fried tonkatsu is sliced, simmered briefly in seasoned broth with onion, covered with egg until just set, and slid onto rice. Variations include sauce katsudon (with Worcestershire-style sauce, from Fukui) and chicken katsu versions.

Culture

Students traditionally eat katsudon before exams because katsu also means 'to win'. It is a classic donburi alongside oyakodon and gyudon.
