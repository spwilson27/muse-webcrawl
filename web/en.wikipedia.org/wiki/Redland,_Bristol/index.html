Redland, Bristol

Redland is a suburb of Bristol, England, situated between Clifton, Cotham and Westbury Park. The area is known for its large Victorian villas, tree-lined streets, and the landmark Redland Chapel. Redland is also an electoral ward of Bristol City Council.

History

Redland remained rural until the mid-19th century, when Bristol's prosperous merchants built substantial houses there. Much of the suburb is now a conservation area, with many buildings listed. The Redland Green area includes a public park and Redland Green School. Redland railway station, on the Severn Beach line, serves the district, and the area borders the Downs and the shopping street of Chandos Road.

Redland Chapel

Redland Park United Reformed Church, often called Redland Chapel, was built in the 1740s as a proprietary chapel (Redland Chapel) and is a Grade I listed building known for its bust of the benefactor John Cossins and its association with the slave trade era wealth of Bristol merchants.
