Kickboxing is a family of stand-up combat sports based on striking with punches and kicks. The modern sport developed from a combination of karate, Muay Thai and Western boxing. Historically, kickboxing-like arts developed independently in several countries — Muay Thai in Thailand, lethwei in Myanmar, savate in France, and sanda in China among them — but the term "kickboxing" in its narrow sense was coined in Japan in the 1960s and later popularised in the United States and the Netherlands.

History

Japanese boxing promoter Osamu Noguchi, impressed by Muay Thai, worked with karateka Tatsuo Yamada to create a hybrid ruleset in the 1960s; the term itself was coined by Noguchi around 1958–1966, and the first events pitted karate fighters against Muay Thai boxers. In the United States, "full-contact karate" of the 1970s — promoted by the PKA — evolved into American kickboxing, with fighters wearing long trousers and foot pads and kicks above the waist only. The Dutch school, founded by Thom Harinck and Jan Plas after training in Japan and Thailand, combined Muay Thai low kicks and knees with Western boxing hand skills. Japan's K-1 promotion, founded in 1993, brought heavyweights global fame with its annual Grand Prix tournaments, and the sport's organisation was further unified under bodies such as WAKO, which received full recognition from the International Olympic Committee in 2021.

Rules and styles

Rulesets differ principally in which techniques are allowed: Japanese and K-1 rules permit low kicks and limited clinch knees; Muay Thai adds elbows and extended clinch work; American full-contact rules historically required kicks above the waist and a minimum number of kicks per round; point-fighting (semi-contact) styles reward speed and control in a stop-start format. Amateur bouts use protective gear including headguards and shin guards. Kickboxing techniques — jab, cross, hook, uppercut, roundhouse kick, front kick, knee strike — have also become a common striking base for mixed martial arts, and aerobic "cardio kickboxing" classes adapt them for fitness.
