Dublin Area Rapid Transit

The Dublin Area Rapid Transit system (styled as DART) is an electrified commuter rail network serving the coastline and city of Dublin, Ireland. The service is operated by Iarnród Éireann (Irish Rail) and runs on the Irish Rail network. The DART line runs from Greystones in County Wicklow in the south to Howth and Malahide in north County Dublin, passing through Dublin city centre stations including Pearse, Tara Street, and Connolly.

History

The DART began service on 23 July 1984, initially between Bray and Howth, following electrification of the coastal railway line at 1,500 V DC. It was Ireland's first electrified heavy rail service. The line was extended south from Bray to Greystones in 2000, and the northern branch to Malahide opened the same year, with trains alternating between Howth and Malahide north of Howth Junction. New rolling stock classes (8500, 8510 and 8520) joined the original 8100 class trains in the 2000s.

Service

Trains run every 10–20 minutes at peak times through the city centre, following the scenic Dublin Bay coastline through suburbs such as Dún Laoghaire, Dalkey, and Killiney. The DART carries roughly 20 million passengers per year in normal times and is integrated with Dublin's Leap Card smart ticketing system.

DART+ expansion

The DART+ programme is a major expansion plan to extend electrification and DART services to commuter lines serving Drogheda, Maynooth and the M3 Parkway, Celbridge/Hazelhatch, and to increase capacity on the existing line with new battery-electric and electric trains, as part of Ireland's National Development Plan.
