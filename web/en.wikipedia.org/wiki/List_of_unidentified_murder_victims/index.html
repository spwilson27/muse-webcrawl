This is a list of unidentified murder victims — homicide victims whose identities remained unknown for extended periods, or in some cases were only established decades later through advances in forensic science such as DNA profiling and forensic genetic genealogy.

Identification efforts

Organisations such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, the DNA Doe Project, and NamUs (the National Missing and Unidentified Persons System) work to restore names to unidentified victims. Forensic genetic genealogy — famously used to identify the Golden State Killer in 2018 — has resolved many decades-old Doe cases since.
