List of newspapers in Canada

This is a list of newspapers in Canada. Canada has two national newspapers, The Globe and Mail and the National Post, alongside metropolitan dailies in every major city and hundreds of community weeklies. The two dominant chains are Postmedia Network and Torstar. French-language publishing is centred in Quebec, led by Le Devoir, La Presse and Le Journal de Montréal.

Other notable publications

Free commuter papers, ethnic and Indigenous press (such as Windspeaker and the Inuit-language Nunatsiaq News), student newspapers and alternative weeklies round out the Canadian press landscape. Many Postmedia dailies share content across the chain, and several former dailies have moved to digital-only publication.
