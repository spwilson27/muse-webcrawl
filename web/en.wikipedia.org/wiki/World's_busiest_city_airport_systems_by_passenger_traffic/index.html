World's busiest city airport systems by passenger traffic

Many major cities are served by multiple airports. This article ranks the world's busiest city airport systems — the combined passenger traffic of all commercial airports serving a metropolitan area — rather than individual airports. By this measure, London has generally been the world's largest aviation market, with its six international airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, City, and Southend) collectively handling well over 150 million passengers in peak pre-pandemic years, followed by New York City and Tokyo.

Methodology

Totals aggregate total passengers (arriving + departing) at all airports marketed as serving the city, usually those sharing an IATA metropolitan area code (for example LON for London, NYC for New York, TYO for Tokyo). Rankings shift with new airport openings — Beijing Daxing (2019) and Istanbul Airport (2019) both changed the picture for their cities — and traffic fell sharply in 2020–2021 due to the COVID-19 pandemic before recovering.
