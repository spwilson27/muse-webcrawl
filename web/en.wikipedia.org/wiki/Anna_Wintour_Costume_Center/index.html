The Anna Wintour Costume Center is the home of the Costume Institute at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City. The renovated space, named for Vogue editor-in-chief Anna Wintour — a museum trustee and the longtime chair of the Met Gala — opened in May 2014 with the exhibition Charles James: Beyond Fashion.

Contents

The center includes exhibition galleries, the Irene Lewisohn Costume Reference Library, conservation laboratories and storage for a collection of more than 33,000 costumes and accessories spanning seven centuries. The Costume Institute's annual spring exhibition, launched by the Met Gala fundraiser, is the museum's best attended: shows such as Alexander McQueen: Savage Beauty (2011), China: Through the Looking Glass (2015) and Heavenly Bodies (2018) drew record crowds.
