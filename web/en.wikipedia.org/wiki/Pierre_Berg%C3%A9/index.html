Pierre Bergé (14 November 1930 – 8 September 2017) was a French industrialist and patron of the arts. He was the longtime business and life partner of fashion designer Yves Saint Laurent, with whom he co-founded the Yves Saint Laurent fashion house in 1961 and built it into one of the world's leading luxury brands.

Business and arts patronage

Bergé managed the business side of the house while Saint Laurent created the designs. He was also a major figure in French cultural life: he financed the restoration of opera productions, served as president of the Opéra Bastille, supported the composer Pierre Boulez, and co-founded the magazine Têtu and the Sidaction AIDS charity.

Art collection

Together with Saint Laurent he assembled one of the great private art collections of the 20th century, housed in their Paris apartment and the Villa Majorelle in Marrakech. Its sale at Christie's in 2009 realised about €374 million, a world record for a private collection. Bergé was also a prominent political supporter of the French Socialist Party.
