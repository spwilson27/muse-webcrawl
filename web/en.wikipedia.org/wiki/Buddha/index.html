The Buddha ('Awakened One') is the title of Siddhartha Gautama, the Indian sage whose teachings form Buddhism. Renouncing a princely life in search of an end to suffering, he attained enlightenment meditating at Bodh Gaya and taught the Middle Way between indulgence and asceticism.

Teachings

His core doctrines — the Four Noble Truths (existence involves suffering; suffering arises from craving; it can cease; the Noble Eightfold Path leads to its cessation) — spread across Asia, evolving into Theravada, Mahayana and Vajrayana forms including Tibetan Buddhism. Buddhism is a world religion of some hundreds of millions of followers.
