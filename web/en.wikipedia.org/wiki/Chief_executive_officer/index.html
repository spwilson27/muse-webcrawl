Chief executive officer

A CEO addressing a company meeting

A chief executive officer (CEO), also known as a chief executive or managing director, is the highest-ranking executive in a company, organization, or agency. The CEO is responsible for making major corporate decisions, managing overall operations and resources, and acting as the main point of communication between the board of directors and corporate operations.

Responsibilities

The responsibilities of a CEO vary depending on the organization's size, culture, and structure. Typical duties include setting and executing strategy, building and leading the executive team, allocating capital, and serving as the public face of the organization. In many companies the CEO is also a member of the board, and in some cases holds the additional title of chairperson, though governance reformers often advocate separating the roles.

Related titles

Other senior executive titles include the chief operating officer (COO), chief financial officer (CFO), and chief technology officer (CTO), collectively known as the C-suite. In the United Kingdom and Commonwealth countries, "managing director" is often used for an equivalent role.
