Nowa Huta

Nowa Huta (literally "New Steel Mill") is the easternmost district of Kraków, Poland, built after the Second World War as a model socialist city around a giant steelworks.

History

Construction began in 1949 beside the Lenin Steelworks (now ArcelorMittal). Planned in socialist realist style around Plac Centralny, it housed tens of thousands of workers. Intended as an atheist counterweight to religious Kraków, it ironically became a centre of anti-communist resistance, with mass protests demanding a church — granted with the Arka Pana (Lord's Ark) church, consecrated in 1977.
