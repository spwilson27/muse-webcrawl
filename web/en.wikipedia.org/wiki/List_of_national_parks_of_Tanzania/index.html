List of national parks of Tanzania

Tanzania has 22 national parks, managed by the Tanzania National Parks Authority (TANAPA), covering a large share of the country along with game reserves (such as the Selous/Nyerere ecosystem) and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. The system is anchored by Serengeti National Park, famous for the great wildebeest migration, and includes Africa's highest mountain in Kilimanjaro National Park. Tourism based on the parks is a major source of foreign exchange.

Related protected areas

The Ngorongoro Conservation Area (a UNESCO World Heritage Site with the world's largest intact volcanic caldera) is managed separately by the NCAA and allows Maasai pastoralism alongside wildlife. Game reserves such as Selous (the remaining portion), Rungwa, Moyowosi and Grumeti, plus forest reserves, complete the protected-area network.
