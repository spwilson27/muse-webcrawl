Dark Horse Comics

Dark Horse Comics is an American comic book, graphic novel and manga publisher founded in Milwaukie, Oregon, by Mike Richardson in 1986. It is one of the largest independent publishers in the United States, known for creator-owned series and licensed titles, and it was acquired by Embracer Group in 2022.

History

Richardson funded the company from his chain of comic shops and launched it with the anthology Dark Horse Presents. Early successes included Paul Chadwick's Concrete and licensed comics based on Aliens and Predator, which helped pioneer the market for licensed properties. In the 1990s Dark Horse published or distributed Frank Miller's Sin City, Mike Mignola's Hellboy, and manga including Ghost in the Shell and Lone Wolf and Cub, becoming a major force in bringing manga to English-language readers.

Notable titles

Well-known Dark Horse properties include Hellboy and the B.P.R.D. universe, Sin City, 300, The Mask, Usagi Yojimbo (since 2019 published by IDW), Buffy the Vampire Slayer comics, Star Wars comics (until the license returned to Marvel in 2015), and The Umbrella Academy. Its film and television arm, Dark Horse Entertainment, has produced adaptations including The Mask (1994), Hellboy (2004) and the Netflix series The Umbrella Academy.
