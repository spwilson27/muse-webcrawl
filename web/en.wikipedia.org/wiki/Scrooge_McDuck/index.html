Scrooge McDuck is a cartoon character created in 1947 by Carl Barks for the Walt Disney Company. A Scottish-American anthropomorphic duck — Donald Duck's maternal uncle — Scrooge is the richest duck in the world, famous for his Money Bin in Duckburg, where he swims in his coins, and for his motto that he made his fortune by being "tougher than the toughies and smarter than the smarties — and I made it square!"

Character

Scrooge debuted as a one-off, miserly antagonist in "Christmas on Bear Mountain" (1947), his name and temperament borrowed from Ebenezer Scrooge. Barks and later writers, especially Don Rosa in The Life and Times of Scrooge McDuck, developed him into an adventurer whose wealth came from decades of prospecting and enterprise around the world. His first earned coin, the "Number One Dime" he received as a shoeshine boy in Glasgow, is his most treasured possession.

Appearances

Scrooge starred in the long-running Uncle Scrooge comic books and in the animated series DuckTales (1987–1990), voiced by Alan Young, and its 2017 reboot, voiced by David Tennant. His rivals include Flintheart Glomgold and John D. Rockerduck, and his recurring foes the Beagle Boys, who constantly try to rob the Money Bin. Scrooge regularly tops lists of the richest fictional characters.
