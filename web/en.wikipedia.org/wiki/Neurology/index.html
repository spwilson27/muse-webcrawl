Neurology

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Neurology is the branch of Medicine dealing with disorders of the nervous system — the Brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves and muscles — that are primarily managed without surgery (neurosurgery handles surgical cases). Neurologists diagnose through clinical examination refined by imaging and electrical tests.

Conditions

Common neurological diseases include Stroke, epilepsy, dementia, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, migraine and other headaches, neuropathies, motor neuron disease and infections such as Meningitis.

Investigation

MRI and CT imaging, electroencephalography (EEG) for epilepsy and brain activity, nerve conduction studies and electromyography for nerve and muscle disease, lumbar puncture, and genetic testing are the specialty's main tools. Many neurological conditions are chronic, so care emphasises long-term management and rehabilitation.
