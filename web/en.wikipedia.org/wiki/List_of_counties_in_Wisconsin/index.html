List of counties in Wisconsin

There are 72 counties in the U.S. state of Wisconsin. The list below gives each county, its county seat, and basic facts. Wisconsin counties vary enormously in population: Milwaukee County has close to a million residents, while Menominee County, coterminous with the Menominee Indian Reservation, has only a few thousand.

History

The first counties, Brown and Crawford, were created in 1818 under the Michigan Territory. When Wisconsin became a state in 1848 it had 29 counties; the last counties formed were Menominee (1961, after the restoration of the Menominee tribe's reservation) and earlier Forest and Florence in the 1880s. One county, Pepin, is among the smallest by area in the state; Marathon is the largest.
