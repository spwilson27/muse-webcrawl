The Nun II is a 2023 American Gothic supernatural horror film directed by Michael Chaves, with a screenplay by Ian Goldberg, Richard Naing, and Akela Cooper. It is a sequel to The Nun (2018) and the eighth installment in The Conjuring Universe franchise.

Plot

In 1956, four years after the events at Saint Cârța Abbey in Romania, Sister Irene (Taissa Farmiga) serves in a convent in Italy when she is dispatched by the Church to investigate a series of deaths across Europe. In Tarascon, France, the demon Valak (Bonnie Aarons)—the Nun—has returned, possessing Maurice "Frenchie" Theriault (Jonas Bloquet), the handyman who unknowingly carried the demon from Romania. At a boarding school for girls, Irene, aided by novice Sister Debra (Storm Reid), must find the relic known as the Eyes of Saint Lucy to banish Valak once more.

Production and release

A sequel to The Nun was announced in 2019, with Akela Cooper writing and James Wan and Peter Safran producing. Filming took place in France in late 2022. The film was released in the United States on September 8, 2023.

Reception

The film grossed $269 million worldwide against a budget of $38.5 million. Reviews were mixed, though critics generally considered it an improvement over its predecessor, praising Farmiga's performance and the film's atmosphere.
