A Star Is Born (soundtrack)

ReleasedNovember 1976
GenreSoundtrack, soft rock, pop
LabelColumbia Records
ArtistsBarbra Streisand, Kris Kristofferson
Chart performanceNo. 1 on the Billboard 200

A Star Is Born is the soundtrack album to the 1976 film of the same name, performed by its stars Barbra Streisand and Kris Kristofferson and released by Columbia Records in November 1976. The album topped the U.S. Billboard 200 chart and has been certified multi-platinum, ranking among the best-selling soundtrack albums of its era.

Content

The album mixes Streisand's ballads with Kristofferson's rock performances from the film. Songs were contributed by Paul Williams, Roger Nichols, Rupert Holmes, Alan and Marilyn Bergman, and others. Its centerpiece is "Evergreen (Love Theme from A Star Is Born)", composed by Streisand with lyrics by Paul Williams, which won the Academy Award and the Grammy Award for Song of the Year and spent three weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1977.

Legacy

The soundtrack's success reinforced the commercial viability of star-driven film albums in the late 1970s, and "Evergreen" became one of Streisand's signature songs.
