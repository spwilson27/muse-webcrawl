Best boy

Grip and electrical crews at work on a film set

Best boy is a crew position in the film and television industries. The term describes the chief assistant to a department head in one of two departments: the electrical department or the grip department. A best boy electric is the assistant to the gaffer (chief lighting technician), while a best boy grip is the assistant to the key grip.

Responsibilities

The best boy is responsible for the day-to-day operation of their department. Duties typically include hiring and scheduling crew, managing equipment rentals and returns, keeping track of expendables and inventory, coordinating with other departments, and supervising the loading and unloading of trucks. The best boy electric also assists with power distribution and cable runs, while the best boy grip helps supervise the rigging and placement of camera support and lighting modification equipment.

Origin of the term

The exact origin of "best boy" is uncertain. One common explanation dates to the early studio era, when a department head needing an extra hand would ask another department to lend their "best boy" — their most capable assistant. Despite the name, the position has always been open to people of any gender, and the term remains standard in credits in North America. In the United Kingdom, equivalent roles are more often described as the gaffer's or key grip's assistant or deputy.
