Harajuku Station (原宿駅, Harajuku-eki) is a railway station in Shibuya, Tokyo, Japan, operated by the East Japan Railway Company (JR East). It serves the Yamanote Line and is the gateway to the Harajuku district, Meiji Shrine and Yoyogi Park. The adjoining Tokyo Metro Meiji-jingūmae Station provides interchange with the Chiyoda and Fukutoshin lines.

History

The station opened in 1906. Its 1924 station building, a half-timbered Western-style structure, was long beloved as Tokyo's oldest wooden station building, but was demolished after the 2020 Olympics and replaced by a new building in a similar style incorporating modern fire-safety standards. The station has two side platforms; an additional temporary platform, used for events at Meiji Shrine and for the Imperial family, was replaced by a permanent dedicated platform in 2020.

Surrounding area

Exits lead to Takeshita Street, famous for youth fashion; to Omotesandō boulevard; and to the forested grounds of Meiji Jingū, whose New Year hatsumōde crowds make Harajuku one of Japan's busiest stations at that season.
