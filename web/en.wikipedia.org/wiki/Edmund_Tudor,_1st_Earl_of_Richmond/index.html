Edmund Tudor, 1st Earl of Richmond

Edmund Tudor, 1st Earl of Richmond (c. 1430 – 3 November 1456) was the father of King Henry VII of England and a member of the Tudor family of Penmynydd. He was the son of Owen Tudor and Catherine of Valois, widow of Henry V, making him half-brother of Henry VI.

Edmund Tudor
Born: c. 1430, Much Hadham
Died: 1456, Carmarthen Castle
Spouse: Margaret Beaufort
Issue: Henry VII

Life

Raised after his mother's death, Edmund was knighted and created Earl of Richmond by Henry VI in 1452, granted lands in Wales. In 1455 he married Margaret Beaufort, aged 12, heiress of the Beaufort line.

Death and legacy

During the early Wars of the Roses he was captured by Yorkists at Carmarthen Castle and died there, possibly of plague, months before his son Henry was born at Pembroke Castle (January 1457). His tomb is in St Davids Cathedral. His son eventually won the crown at Bosworth in 1485, founding the Tudor dynasty.
