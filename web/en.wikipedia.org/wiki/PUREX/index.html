PUREX

PUREX (plutonium uranium reduction extraction) is a chemical method used to purify and process nuclear material — namely, to extract plutonium and uranium from spent nuclear fuel. It is based on liquid–liquid extraction and is the standard process used in commercial nuclear reprocessing plants worldwide.

History

The PUREX process was developed in the late 1940s at Knolls Atomic Power Laboratory and Oak Ridge National Laboratory in the United States, during the early Cold War effort to recover plutonium for nuclear weapons and uranium for reuse. It succeeded earlier methods such as the bismuth phosphate process, which could recover plutonium but not uranium, and which generated large volumes of waste. The first full-scale PUREX plant was built at the Hanford Site in Washington, beginning operation in 1956, and a plant at Savannah River followed.

Process

Spent fuel is first dissolved in hot nitric acid. The resulting solution, containing uranyl and plutonium nitrates together with fission products, is contacted with an organic solvent — typically about 30% tributyl phosphate (TBP) dissolved in a hydrocarbon diluent such as kerosene or n-dodecane. Uranium(VI) and plutonium(IV) are preferentially extracted into the organic phase, while most fission products remain in the aqueous phase. Plutonium is then selectively stripped back into an aqueous phase by reducing it to plutonium(III), which is not extracted by TBP, thereby separating it from uranium. Further purification cycles produce separate uranium and plutonium product streams, usually converted to oxides.

Applications and proliferation concerns

PUREX is used commercially at La Hague in France, Sellafield in the United Kingdom (historically), Mayak in Russia, and Rokkasho in Japan, among others. Because the process produces separated plutonium that could in principle be diverted to weapons use, it has long been a focus of nuclear proliferation concern, and a number of modified "proliferation-resistant" variants have been proposed that avoid producing a pure plutonium stream.
