Vivienne Medrano

Vivienne Medrano (born October 28, 1992), also known online as VivziePop, is an American animator, writer, director, producer, and voice actress. She is best known as the creator of the adult animated musical series Hazbin Hotel and Helluva Boss, both set in Hell, as well as the webcomic ZooPhobia.

Early life and career

Medrano was born in Maryland and is of Salvadoran descent. She studied at the School of Visual Arts in New York City, graduating in 2014. She began posting her work online as a teenager, gaining a following on YouTube and DeviantArt, and worked freelance on student and independent animation projects, including the web series work of other creators.

Hazbin Hotel and Helluva Boss

In October 2019 Medrano released the pilot of Hazbin Hotel on YouTube, produced largely through crowdfunding on Patreon. The pilot was viewed tens of millions of times, and in 2020 the series was picked up by the production company A24 for a full season on Amazon Prime Video, premiering in January 2024. A spin-off series, Helluva Boss, began in 2019 and continued as a YouTube series produced by her studio, SpindleHorse Toons.

Style and themes

Medrano's work is characterized by fast-paced animation, bold character designs, musical numbers, LGBTQ+ characters, and dark comedy set in a stylized version of Hell.
