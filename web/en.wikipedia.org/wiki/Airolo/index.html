Airolo is a municipality in the district of Leventina in the canton of Ticino in Switzerland. Situated at an elevation of about 1,175 metres in the upper Leventina valley at the southern foot of the Gotthard Pass, it sits beside the Ticino River near the entrances of two of the world's great transport tunnels.

Geography and transport

Airolo lies on the main north–south route across the Swiss Alps. It is the southern portal of both the Gotthard Rail Tunnel (opened 1882) and the Gotthard Road Tunnel (opened 1980), and the Gotthard Base Tunnel (opened 2016) passes deep beneath the area, with Airolo hosting works connected to the project. The old pass road to the Gotthard hospice and the Tremola cobbled switchbacks begin near the village. The municipality includes the hamlets of Valle, Madrano, Brugnasco, Fontana, and Nante.

History and economy

First mentioned in 1210, Airolo grew as a staging point on the Gotthard route. The 1882 tunnel construction brought a boom, and a monument by Vincenzo Vela in the village commemorates the workers who died building it. The avalanche of 1951, which killed ten people, remains one of Switzerland's worst. Today the economy depends on transit services, military installations, cheese production (the Gotthard show dairy), tourism, and the Bedretto valley ski areas.
