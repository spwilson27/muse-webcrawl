Mark S. Miller is an American computer scientist known for his work on secure distributed computing, the object-capability model, and the E programming language. He is a co-founder and chief scientist of Agoric.

Career

Miller worked at Xerox PARC and was a central figure in the Electric Communities project, which built the E language for secure distributed object programming. He later worked at HP Labs and Google, where he contributed to Caja, a system for safely embedding untrusted JavaScript.

Contributions

His research helped formalise capability-based security, promise pipelining for distributed systems, and "agoric" systems — markets and computation combined — an idea developed with K. Eric Drexler. He has been influential in TC39 discussions on JavaScript security features such as SES (Secure ECMAScript).
