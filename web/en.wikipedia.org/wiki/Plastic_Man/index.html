Plastic Man (real name Patrick "Eel" O'Brian) is a fictional superhero first appearing in Police Comics #1 (August 1941). Created by cartoonist Jack Cole, the character was originally published by Quality Comics and later acquired by DC Comics.

Fictional character biography

O'Brian was a small-time crook who, during a botched robbery at a chemical plant, was shot and doused with an experimental acid mixture. He collapsed in the wilderness and was nursed back to health by monks, after which he discovered his body had become rubber-like: he could stretch, compress, and mould himself into virtually any shape. Renouncing crime, he adopted the identity of Plastic Man and fought criminals, often in a comic, slapstick style, aided by his bumbling sidekick Woozy Winks.

Powers and abilities

Plastic Man can stretch his limbs and body to superhuman lengths, alter his size and shape (including disguising himself as objects or other people), and is highly resistant to physical harm, aging, and telepathy. His stories distinguished themselves through a zany, humorous tone unusual for the Golden Age.

In other media

The character has appeared in animated series including The Plastic Man Comedy/Adventure Show (1979) and Batman: The Brave and the Bold, and as a member of the Justice League in various comics.
