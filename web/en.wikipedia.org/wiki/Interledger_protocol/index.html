Interledger Protocol
Developer: Interledger community
Introduced: 2015
Type: Open payments protocol suite

Interledger is an open protocol suite for sending payments across different ledgers and payment networks. Modelled conceptually on the Internet Protocol, it allows value to be routed between accounts on otherwise disconnected systems through intermediate connectors, using packetised payments and cryptographic condition-and-fulfilment mechanisms derived from hashed timelock contracts.

History

The protocol was first described in a 2015 whitepaper by Stefan Thomas and Evan Schwartz of Ripple, and development moved to an open community under the W3C Interledger Payments Community Group. It underpins projects such as Coil's web monetisation experiments and the Rafiki reference implementation.

How it works

Payments are broken into small packets accompanied by a crypto-condition. Each connector along a path forwards the packet if it can later present the fulfilment, allowing value to move hop by hop without any participant needing to trust the others beyond their direct counterparty. STREAM is the accompanying transport protocol for sending money and data in a single connection.
