Death Star

The Death Star, the Galactic Empire's planet-destroying battle station.

The Death Star is a fictional space station and superweapon featured in the Star Wars franchise. Built by the Galactic Empire, it is a moon-sized battle station armed with a superlaser capable of destroying an entire planet. The Death Star first appeared in Star Wars (1977), where it serves as the setting of the film's climax and is destroyed by Luke Skywalker. A second, larger Death Star appears in Return of the Jedi (1983).

Description

The first Death Star is roughly 160 kilometres in diameter, crewed by over a million Imperial personnel, and powered by a hypermatter reactor. Its primary weapon focuses energy from eight tributary lasers into a single beam fired from a concave parabolic dish in the northern hemisphere. The station's surface is covered with turbolaser batteries, hangars and docking bays, and it carries fleets of TIE fighters and support ships within its hull.

In the films

In Star Wars (1977), the Death Star is commanded by Grand Moff Tarkin, who demonstrates its power by destroying the planet Alderaan. Stolen technical plans reveal a vulnerability: a small thermal exhaust port leading directly to the reactor. Luke Skywalker, using the Force, fires proton torpedoes into the port during the Battle of Yavin, destroying the station moments before it can fire on the Rebel base.

The second Death Star, under construction in orbit around the forest moon of Endor in Return of the Jedi (1983), is considerably larger and its superlaser is operational despite the station's incomplete state. It is destroyed when Rebel fighters fly inside its superstructure and detonate its reactor core during the Battle of Endor. The concept was echoed in later films by Starkiller Base in The Force Awakens (2015) and the planet-destroying Star Destroyers of The Rise of Skywalker (2019).

Cultural impact

The Death Star has become one of the most recognisable images in popular culture, frequently referenced in discussions of superweapons and government mega-projects. In 2012 a petition on the White House "We the People" website asking the United States government to build a real Death Star gathered enough signatures to require an official response, which humorously declined, noting among other objections the fundamental flaw of a weapon that could be destroyed by a one-man starship.
