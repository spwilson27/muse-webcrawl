Polydimethylsiloxane

IUPAC namepoly(dimethylsiloxane)
Other namesPDMS, dimethicone, E900
Chemical formula(C2H6OSi)n
AppearanceClear, colourless liquid or rubber

Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS), also known as dimethicone, is a polymeric organosilicon compound that belongs to the group of substances known as silicones. PDMS is the most widely used silicon-based organic polymer, valued for its optical transparency, chemical inertness, non-toxicity, low flammability, and unusual flow properties. When not cross-linked it is a clear, viscous liquid; when cross-linked it forms a flexible rubber-like elastomer.

Structure and properties

The PDMS chain consists of a backbone of alternating silicon and oxygen atoms (a siloxane backbone), with two methyl groups attached to each silicon. The Si–O bond gives the polymer great flexibility, a very low glass transition temperature (around −125 °C), and thermal stability. PDMS is hydrophobic and has one of the lowest surface energies of any polymer, which makes it water-repellent and a good release agent. Its viscosity ranges from that of water-like fluids to thick gums, depending on chain length.

Applications

  • Soft lithography and microfluidics: PDMS is cast against moulds to make microfluidic chips and stamps for microcontact printing; its gas permeability suits cell culture devices.
  • Medicine and cosmetics: Dimethicone is used in skin creams, shampoos and conditioners, in medicinal anti-foaming agents (simethicone), in soft contact lenses, and formerly in breast implants.
  • Food: As food additive E900 it is used as an anti-foaming and anti-caking agent, including in cooking oils.
  • Industry: Lubricants, hydraulic fluids, dielectric fluids, mould-release agents, sealants and adhesives.
  • Consumer products: It is the key polymer in Silly Putty-type materials when cross-linked with boric acid, giving striking non-Newtonian behaviour.

Safety

PDMS is generally regarded as inert and non-toxic, which underlies its medical and food uses. Environmental concerns have focused mainly on the small cyclic siloxanes (D4 and D5) used in its production rather than on the polymer itself.
