Chubu Centrair International Airport

Chubu Centrair International Airport (IATA: NGO, ICAO: RJGG) is an international airport on an artificial island in Ise Bay, near Tokoname, Aichi Prefecture, Japan, serving Nagoya and the Chubu region. It opened on February 17, 2005, replacing Nagoya Airfield (Komaki) for most scheduled flights.

Facilities

The airport has one 3,500 m runway (a second is planned/under construction), two terminals, and is connected to Nagoya by Meitetsu trains (μSky limited express, about 28 minutes). It is a hub for ANA and a focus for low-cost carriers, and hosts the Flight of Dreams Boeing 787 exhibit. "Centrair" is a portmanteau of "Central Japan International Airport".
