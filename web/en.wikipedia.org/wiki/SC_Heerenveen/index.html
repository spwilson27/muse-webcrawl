SC Heerenveen

Full name: Sportclub Heerenveen
Founded: 20 July 1920
Ground: Abe Lenstra Stadion (capacity ~27,224)
League: Eredivisie

SC Heerenveen (Frisian: SK It Hearrenfean) is a Dutch professional football club from Heerenveen, Friesland, playing in the Eredivisie. The club is strongly identified with Frisian identity: the Frisian flag's colours and water-lily leaves (pompeblêden) feature on its shirts and crest, and the Frisian anthem is sung before home matches.

History

Founded in 1920 as Athleta, the club became known as Heerenveen and achieved fame under legendary striker Abe Lenstra in the 1940s and 1950s. It established itself in the Eredivisie in the 1990s under coach Foppe de Haan, reached the UEFA Champions League group stage in 2000–01, and won its first major trophy, the KNVB Cup, in 2009.

Stadium

Since 1994 the club has played at the Abe Lenstra Stadion, named after its greatest player.
