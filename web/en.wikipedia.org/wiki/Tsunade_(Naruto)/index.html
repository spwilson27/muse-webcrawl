Tsunade (Japanese: 線手) is a fictional character in the Naruto manga and anime franchise, created by Masashi Kishimoto. She is known as the world's strongest kunoichi and its greatest medical-nin. After the death of the Third Hokage, Tsunade is persuaded to become the Fifth Hokage (五代目火影, Godaime Hokage) of the Hidden Leaf Village, Konohagakure.

Character

Tsunade is a granddaughter of the First Hokage, Hashirama Senju, and a member of the legendary Sannin alongside Jiraiya and Orochimaru, her former teammates. She is famed for her superhuman strength, her Creation Rebirth healing technique — which lets her maintain a youthful appearance — and her skill with slugs summoned through Katsuyu. Following the deaths of her younger brother Nawaki and her lover Dan Katō during the Second Shinobi World War, she developed a fear of blood and abandoned the shinobi life, wandering in a cycle of gambling (at which she is notoriously unlucky, earning the title "The Legendary Sucker") and drinking, before Naruto Uzumaki's determination convinces her to return and accept the Hokage position.

Role in the story

As Hokage, Tsunade leads Konoha through its conflicts with the criminal organisation Akatsuki, takes Sakura Haruno as her apprentice in medical ninjutsu, and fights in the Fourth Shinobi World War alongside the other Kage. After the war she retires and is succeeded by Kakashi Hatake, and she later appears in the sequel series Boruto.
