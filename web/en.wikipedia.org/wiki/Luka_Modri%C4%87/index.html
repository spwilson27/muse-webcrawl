Luka Modrić (born 9 September 1985) is a Croatian professional footballer who plays as a central midfielder and captains the Croatia national team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest midfielders of all time, he won the Ballon d'Or in 2018, breaking the decade-long dominance of Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo.

Early life and career

Modrić grew up near Zadar during the Croatian War of Independence, during which his grandfather was killed and his family fled their home. He developed at Dinamo Zagreb, with loan spells at Zrinjski Mostar — where he was named the Bosnian league's player of the year as a teenager — and Inter Zaprešić, before starring in Dinamo's three consecutive league titles.

Tottenham and Real Madrid

He joined Tottenham Hotspur in 2008 and became one of the Premier League's outstanding midfielders. In 2012 he transferred to Real Madrid, where under Carlo Ancelotti and Zinedine Zidane he formed a celebrated midfield with Toni Kroos and Casemiro, winning multiple La Liga titles and six Champions League trophies, including three in a row from 2016 to 2018.

International career

Modrić led Croatia to the 2018 FIFA World Cup final, winning the tournament's Golden Ball, and to third place at the 2022 World Cup. He is Croatia's most-capped player and its captain, renowned for his passing range, press resistance, and longevity at the highest level.
