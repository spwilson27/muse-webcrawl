Cao Fang

Cao Fang (232–274), courtesy name Lanqing, was the third emperor of the state of Cao Wei during the Three Kingdoms period of China. He reigned from 239, following the death of Cao Rui, until his deposition in 254.

Reign

Cao Fang ascended the throne as a child of about seven. Power was held by regents Cao Shuang and Sima Yi; after Sima Yi's coup of 249 (the Incident at the Gaoping Tombs), Cao Shuang was executed and the Sima family effectively controlled the Wei court.

Deposition

In 254, after plots against the Simas were uncovered, Sima Shi deposed Cao Fang and installed Cao Mao as emperor. Cao Fang was demoted to Prince of Qi and kept under watch for the rest of his life, dying in 274, almost a decade after Wei itself fell to Jin.

Assessment

Traditional histories portray Cao Fang as a weak ruler overshadowed by the regents, a symbol of the Cao clan's loss of real power to the Sima family.
