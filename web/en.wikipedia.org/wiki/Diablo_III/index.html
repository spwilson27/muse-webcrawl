Diablo III is an action role-playing game developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment as the third instalment in the Diablo franchise. It was released for Windows and macOS in May 2012, with console versions following.

Gameplay

Players choose from character classes including the Barbarian, Wizard, Monk, Demon Hunter and Witch Doctor (later joined by the Crusader and Necromancer), battling the forces of the Burning Hells in the world of Sanctuary. The game emphasises fast-paced combat, randomised loot and cooperative multiplayer.

Launch and evolution

The launch was marred by server errors connected to its always-online requirement, and its real-money auction house proved controversial before being removed in 2014. The expansion Reaper of Souls (2014) and seasonal play substantially improved the game's reception. Diablo III sold over 30 million copies and was succeeded by Diablo IV in 2023.
