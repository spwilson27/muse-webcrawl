Breakfast television

Breakfast television (also called a morning show) is a type of news and infotainment television programme broadcast in the morning, typically from about 5:00 to 10:00 a.m. Its content generally blends news headlines, weather, traffic, interviews, lifestyle features, and light entertainment, aimed at viewers preparing for work or school.

History

The format was pioneered in the United States by NBC's Today, launched in 1952 with host Dave Garroway. Competitors followed with Good Morning America (ABC, 1975) and CBS's successive morning programmes. In the United Kingdom, breakfast television began in 1983 with the BBC's Breakfast Time and TV-am's Good Morning Britain.

Format

Morning shows repeat key segments on a cycle so viewers tuning in briefly can catch news, weather, and travel updates. Local stations typically insert their own news and traffic reports into national programmes. The tone is generally lighter and more conversational than evening newscasts.

Radio

The parallel radio format — a morning "zoo" or drive-time show with hosts, comedy, music, and news — is a staple of commercial radio worldwide.
