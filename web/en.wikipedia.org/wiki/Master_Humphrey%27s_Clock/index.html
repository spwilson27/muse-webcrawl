Master Humphrey's Clock was a weekly periodical edited and written entirely by Charles Dickens, published from 4 April 1840 to December 1841. Framed as the papers of the elderly Master Humphrey and his circle, it serialized Dickens's novels The Old Curiosity Shop and Barnaby Rudge.

Format and contents

Each three-penny weekly number presented stories told by Master Humphrey and his friends, including Mr. Pickwick. The framing material introduced The Old Curiosity Shop, whose popularity—especially the fate of Little Nell—greatly boosted circulation.

Legacy

After Barnaby Rudge concluded, Dickens discontinued the periodical. The experiment in author-run weekly publishing paved the way for his later journals Household Words and All the Year Round.
