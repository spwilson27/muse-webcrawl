Pohang Accelerator Laboratory

Pohang Accelerator Laboratory (PAL)
Location: Pohang, South Korea
Established: 1988
Affiliation: POSTECH

The Pohang Accelerator Laboratory (PAL) is a synchrotron radiation facility located in Pohang, South Korea, associated with the Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH). It operates the Pohang Light Source (PLS-II) and the PAL-XFEL free-electron laser, and serves as South Korea's principal national laboratory for accelerator-based photon science.

History

PAL was established in 1988 and commissioned the Pohang Light Source, a 2 GeV third-generation synchrotron, in 1994 — the fifth such facility in the world at the time. The machine stored electrons at 2.5 GeV in later operation. An upgrade project, PLS-II, completed in 2011–2012, raised the beam energy to 3 GeV, increased the brightness of the photon beams, and expanded the number of insertion-device beamlines.

PAL-XFEL

The laboratory also operates PAL-XFEL, an X-ray free-electron laser facility that began user operation in 2017. PAL-XFEL uses a linear accelerator of about 780 metres to produce ultrafast, coherent X-ray pulses in both hard X-ray (up to around 10 keV and above) and soft X-ray branches, enabling experiments in structural biology, materials science, and ultrafast chemistry at femtosecond timescales. It was among the first XFEL facilities in the world, following installations in the United States, Japan, and Europe.

Research

Beamlines at PAL support techniques including X-ray crystallography, small-angle scattering, photoemission spectroscopy, X-ray absorption spectroscopy, and imaging, used by researchers from universities, institutes, and industry across Korea and abroad.
