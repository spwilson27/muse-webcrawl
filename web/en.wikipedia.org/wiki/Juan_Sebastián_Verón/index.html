Juan Sebastián Verón

Personal information
Full name: Juan Sebastián Verón
Born: 9 March 1975, La Plata, Argentina
Position: Midfielder
Nickname: La Brujita ("The Little Witch")

Juan Sebastián Verón (born 9 March 1975) is an Argentine former professional footballer who played as a midfielder. Nicknamed La Brujita, he was known for his vision, long passing and powerful shooting, and is regarded as one of the finest Argentine playmakers of his generation. He is a son of Juan Ramón Verón, a hero of Estudiantes de La Plata's late-1960s Copa Libertadores-winning side.

Club career

Verón began his career at Estudiantes before a brief spell at Boca Juniors alongside Diego Maradona. He moved to Europe with Sampdoria, then Parma, where he won the Coppa Italia and the UEFA Cup in 1999, and Lazio, where he won the Serie A title, Coppa Italia and Supercoppa Italiana in 2000. In 2001 he joined Manchester United for a then-British record fee, winning the Premier League in 2002–03, before moving to Chelsea and a loan at Inter Milan, where he won further domestic honours. He returned to Estudiantes in 2006 and captained the club to the 2009 Copa Libertadores title, and later had a short comeback with amateur side Estrella de Berisso before becoming Estudiantes' president.

International career

Verón earned 73 caps for Argentina, scoring nine goals, and represented his country at the 1998, 2002 and 2010 FIFA World Cups and at the 2007 Copa América, where Argentina finished as runners-up.
