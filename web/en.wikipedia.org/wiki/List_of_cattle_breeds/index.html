List of cattle breeds

This is a list of cattle breeds, of which there are over a thousand recognised worldwide. Cattle are generally divided into two main types — taurine cattle (Bos taurus), dominant in Europe and temperate regions, and zebu or indicine cattle (Bos indicus), adapted to hot climates — together with many crosses between the two. Breeds are typically classified by their principal use: dairy, beef, draught, or dual- and multi-purpose.

Dairy breeds

  • Holstein Friesian — the world's dominant dairy breed, black-and-white, highest milk yields
  • Jersey — small, fawn, high butterfat milk, from the Channel Islands
  • Guernsey — golden-coloured milk
  • Brown Swiss — large, hardy, high-protein milk
  • Ayrshire — Scottish dairy breed
  • Milking Shorthorn — dual dairy/beef

Beef breeds

  • Angus (Aberdeen Angus) — polled, black (or Red Angus), prized marbling
  • Hereford — red body with white face, hardy and widespread
  • Charolais — large, cream-white French breed, fast-growing
  • Limousin — French beef breed
  • Simmental — Swiss origin, large dual-purpose breed
  • Wagyu — Japanese breeds famed for intense marbling
  • Texas Longhorn — known for long horns, hardy
  • Galloway and Belted Galloway — hardy Scottish breeds
  • Highland — long shaggy coat and horns, from the Scottish Highlands

Zebu and zebu-derived breeds

  • Brahman — developed in the USA from Indian zebu, heat- and parasite-tolerant
  • Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi — Indian dairy zebu breeds
  • Nelore — the dominant beef breed of Brazil
  • Brangus, Beefmaster, Santa Gertrudis — taurine–zebu composites
