Mount San Antonio, commonly known as Mount Baldy, is a 3,069-metre (10,064 ft) summit in the San Gabriel Mountains of Southern California. It is the highest point in Los Angeles County and the third-highest peak in Southern California, visible across the Los Angeles Basin on clear days.

Geography and recreation

The mountain lies within the Angeles National Forest and the San Gabriel Mountains National Monument. The Mount Baldy ski area operates on its southern slopes, and the San Antonio Falls trailhead gives access to popular routes including the Devil's Backbone and the steep Baldy Bowl up the Sierra Club Ski Hut trail. The summit offers panoramic views from the Pacific Ocean to the Mojave Desert.

History and incidents

The peak's nickname derives from the bald, rocky summit and Baldy Notch below it. The mountain is notorious for dangerous winter conditions; ice and wind on the exposed ridges have caused numerous fatalities, including that of actor Julian Sands, who died while hiking there in 2023.
