The Blues and Royals (Royal Horse Guards and 1st Dragoons) (RHG/D) is a cavalry regiment of the British Army, part of the Household Cavalry. It was formed on 29 March 1969 by the amalgamation of the Royal Horse Guards ("The Blues") and the 1st The Royal Dragoons ("The Royals").

Role

Alongside the Life Guards, the regiment provides the mounted squadrons for state ceremonial occasions in London, including Trooping the Colour and state visits; uniquely, its soldiers wear a red plume on their helmets and a chin strap below the bottom lip. In its operational role it serves as an armoured reconnaissance regiment.

History

Both predecessor regiments dated to the 17th century. The Blues and Royals have served in the Falklands War, the Gulf War and in Iraq and Afghanistan, where a squadron fought as armoured reconnaissance in Scimitar vehicles.
