Weltklasse Zürich
Founded1928
VenueLetzigrund, Zürich, Switzerland
SeriesDiamond League

Weltklasse Zürich is an annual track and field athletics meeting held at the Letzigrund Stadium in Zürich, Switzerland. First staged in 1928, it is one of the most prestigious one-day meetings in the sport and a fixture of the Diamond League, traditionally hosting the series finals.

History

The meeting grew in stature during the twentieth century and became famous for high-quality fields and fast races; numerous world records have been set at the Letzigrund. In 1979, Sebastian Coe broke the world mile record there, and the stadium hosted a celebrated night in 1988 when Butch Reynolds set a 400 metres world record that stood for eleven years.

Diamond League finals

Since 2010 Weltklasse Zürich has been part of the Diamond League and, together with the Memorial Van Damme in Brussels, has hosted the series finals, with all Diamond League disciplines decided across the two meetings.
