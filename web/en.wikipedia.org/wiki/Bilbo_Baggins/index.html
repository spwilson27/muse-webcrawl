Bilbo Baggins

Bilbo Baggins is the title character and protagonist of J. R. R. Tolkien's 1937 novel The Hobbit, a supporting character in The Lord of the Rings, and the fictional narrator of many of Tolkien's Middle-earth writings.

Bilbo Baggins
Race: Hobbit
Gender: Male
Family: Bungo Baggins (father), Belladonna Took (mother), Frodo Baggins (cousin/heir)
Home: Bag End, Hobbiton, the Shire

Appearances

The Hobbit

Bilbo, a respectable, comfort-loving hobbit, is swept into a quest by the wizard Gandalf and thirteen dwarves led by Thorin Oakenshield to reclaim the Lonely Mountain and its treasure from the dragon Smaug. He finds a magic ring — later revealed as the One Ring — in Gollum's cave, and grows in courage and resourcefulness.

The Lord of the Rings

At his eleventy-first (111th) birthday Bilbo leaves the Shire, bequeathing the Ring to Frodo. He settles in Rivendell, works on his memoirs, and eventually sails to the Undying Lands with Frodo and the Elves.

Character

Scholars see Bilbo as a modern, middle-class everyman placed in an ancient heroic world, and his journey as a classic coming-of-age and self-discovery story. His name and character draw partly on Tolkien's own experiences.

Adaptations

Bilbo was played by Ian Holm in Peter Jackson's Lord of the Rings films and by Martin Freeman as the younger Bilbo in The Hobbit trilogy.
