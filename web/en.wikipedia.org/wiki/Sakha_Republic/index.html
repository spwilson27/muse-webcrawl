Sakha Republic

The Sakha Republic (Russian: Республика Саха (Якутия), Respublika Sakha (Yakutiya); Yakut: Саха Өрөспүүбүлүкэтэ, Saxa Öröspüübülükete), also known as Yakutia, is the largest federal subject of Russia, occupying the northeastern part of Siberia. It covers an area of about 3.1 million square kilometres (1.2 million sq mi) — roughly the size of India — yet has a population of only around one million, making it one of the most sparsely populated regions in the world.

Geography and climate

Yakutia stretches from the Arctic Ocean southward across the Lena, Yana, Indigirka, and Kolyma river basins. Much of it lies within the Arctic Circle and is underlain by permafrost. The republic contains Oymyakon, famous as one of the coldest permanently inhabited places on Earth, where temperatures below −67 °C (−89 °F) have been recorded, as well as the Northern Hemisphere's Pole of Cold at Verkhoyansk. The dramatic Lena Pillars rock formations along the Lena River are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

History

The Turkic-speaking Sakha (Yakut) people migrated to the middle Lena region from the Lake Baikal area between roughly the 9th and 16th centuries. Russian Cossacks reached the Lena in the 1620s–1630s, founding Yakutsk in 1632, and the region became the springboard for the Russian exploration of the entire Far East and even Russian America. The Yakut Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic was established in 1922 within the RSFSR; in 1990–1992 it became the Sakha Republic within the Russian Federation.

Economy

The economy is dominated by mining: Yakutia is a major world source of diamonds — the company Alrosa, based at Mirny, produces roughly a quarter of the world's rough diamonds — as well as gold, coal, tin, and natural gas. Reindeer herding, horse breeding (the hardy Yakutian horse), and cattle raising are traditional pursuits of the Sakha people.

Demographics

Ethnic Yakuts (Sakha) form roughly half the population, with Russians the largest minority, alongside Evenks, Evens, and other Indigenous peoples of the North. The capital and largest city is Yakutsk, itself one of the coldest major cities in the world.
