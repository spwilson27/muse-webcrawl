Christina Ricci

Christina Ricci (born February 12, 1980) is an American actress known for playing dark, unconventional characters. She debuted in Mermaids (1990) and became famous as Wednesday Addams in The Addams Family (1991) and its sequel.

Christina Ricci
Born: February 12, 1980, Santa Monica, California
Occupation: Actress
Years active: 1990–present

Career

Successful child roles included Casper (1995) and Now and Then (1995). She transitioned to acclaimed adult work in The Ice Storm (1997), Buffalo '66 (1998), Sleepy Hollow (1999), Monster (2003), and later television roles in Pan Am, Yellowjackets (as Misty Quigley), and Netflix's Wednesday.
