Startup company

A startup company (or start-up) is an entrepreneurial venture typically founded to develop a unique product or service and bring it to market. Startups are characterised by high uncertainty, rapid growth potential, and business models designed to be scalable and repeatable.

Funding

Startups commonly finance themselves through bootstrapping, friends and family, angel investors, venture capital rounds (seed, Series A and beyond), and sometimes crowdfunding. Accelerators such as Y Combinator provide mentorship and small investments in exchange for equity.

Characteristics

The lean startup methodology emphasises building a minimum viable product, testing hypotheses with customers, and pivoting when assumptions fail. Most startups fail, but a small number grow into large companies; privately held startups valued above $1 billion are called "unicorns".
