Optometrist

An optometrist is a healthcare professional who provides primary eye and vision care, including eye examinations, detection of eye diseases and abnormalities, and the prescribing and fitting of corrective lenses such as spectacles and contact lenses. In many countries optometrists hold a Doctor of Optometry (OD) degree or an equivalent professional qualification and are licensed or registered by a regulatory body.

Optometrists test visual acuity and refraction, assess binocular vision, screen for conditions such as glaucoma, cataract and macular degeneration, and refer patients to ophthalmologists or other specialists when surgery or medical treatment is needed. In some jurisdictions they may also prescribe certain medications.

Optometry differs from ophthalmology, which is a branch of medicine and surgery, and from dispensing optics, which concerns the fitting and supply of lenses.
