Paraphimosis

Paraphimosis is a medical condition in which the foreskin of an uncircumcised penis becomes trapped behind the glans and cannot be returned to its normal position covering the glans. The trapped foreskin forms a constricting band that can progressively restrict blood flow.

Causes and symptoms

Paraphimosis most often occurs when the foreskin is retracted for examination, cleaning, catheterization, or intercourse and is not replaced afterward. Swelling of the glans and the retracted foreskin then prevents reduction. Unlike phimosis, in which the foreskin cannot be retracted, paraphimosis concerns a foreskin stuck in the retracted position.

Treatment

Paraphimosis is treated as an emergency because prolonged venous congestion can progress to ischemia and necrosis. Initial management includes manual compression to reduce edema and return the foreskin; if manual reduction fails, a dorsal slit procedure or circumcision may be required.
