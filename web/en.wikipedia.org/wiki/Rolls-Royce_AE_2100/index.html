Rolls-Royce AE 2100

The Rolls-Royce AE 2100 is a turboprop aircraft engine developed by Allison Engine Company (acquired by Rolls-Royce in 1995). It is part of the AE family, sharing a common core with the AE 3007 turbofan and AE 1107 turboshaft.

The AE 2100 produces about 4,600 shaft horsepower (3,400 kW) in the D3 version. It features a two-spool design with a 14-stage axial compressor, annular combustor, and two-stage gas generator and power turbines, controlled by dual FADEC. A Dowty six-bladed composite propeller is fitted in most applications.

Applications

The engine powers the Lockheed Martin C-130J Super Hercules (AE 2100D3), the Alenia C-27J Spartan (AE 2100D2), and powered the Saab 2000 airliner (AE 2100A) and the ShinMaywa US-2 amphibian (AE 2100J). First run in 1991, the type entered service in the mid-1990s.
