Factions of Halo

The Halo science fiction franchise features several factions that contend across its games, novels, and other media. The principal powers include the United Nations Space Command (UNSC), representing humanity; the Covenant, a theocratic alliance of alien species; and the Flood, a parasitic organism that threatens all sentient life.

United Nations Space Command

The UNSC is the military, exploratory, and scientific agency of the Unified Earth Government. Its forces include the Spartan supersoldiers, among them Master Chief Petty Officer John-117, the series' protagonist. The UNSC fought a decades-long war of survival against the Covenant beginning in 2525.

The Covenant

The Covenant was a hegemony of alien races united by worship of the Forerunners, whom they regarded as gods. Species included the Prophets (San'Shyuum), Elites (Sangheili), Brutes (Jiralhanae), Grunts (Unggoy), Jackals (Kig-Yar), Hunters (Mgalekgolo), and Engineers (Huragok). The alliance fractured during the Great Schism depicted in Halo 2.

Other factions

The Forerunners, an ancient civilization that built the Halo rings, and their artificial intelligences such as 343 Guilty Spark feature prominently. Later installments introduced the Prometheans, the Banished under Atriox, and the Created, an AI faction led by Cortana.
