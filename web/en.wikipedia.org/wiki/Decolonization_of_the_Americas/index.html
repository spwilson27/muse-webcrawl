Decolonization of the Americas

The decolonization of the Americas is the process by which the countries of the Americas gained independence from European rule, beginning with the United States in 1776 and Haiti in 1804.

During the 1810s and 1820s most of Spanish America won independence through wars led by figures such as Simón Bolívar and José de San Martín, while Brazil became independent of Portugal in 1822. The process continued into the 20th century with the independence of British, French and Dutch Caribbean colonies, and a handful of territories remain dependencies today.
