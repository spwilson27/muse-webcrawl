The National League System (NLS) comprises the six levels ("steps") of the English football league system immediately below the English Football League (EFL). It is administered by the Football Association (the FA) and links hundreds of semi-professional and amateur leagues through a structured system of promotion and relegation. The top step of the system is the National League, whose champion and play-off winner are promoted to EFL League Two each season.

The system covers clubs situated in England and a small number of Welsh clubs that have historically played in the English pyramid. Above the NLS sit the Premier League and the three divisions of the EFL; below Step 6, football continues in regional feeder leagues, which since 2021 have been overseen as part of the wider pyramid restructure.

Structure

The NLS is organised into steps, each step corresponding to a level of the overall pyramid. Step 1 is the fifth tier of English football overall, and Step 6 corresponds to the tenth tier.

Promotion and relegation between steps depends not only on league position but also on ground grading criteria: a club must meet minimum standards for its stadium — such as capacity, seating, floodlighting and spectator facilities — to be promoted to a higher step.

History

For most of the twentieth century there was no automatic promotion from non-League football into the Football League; new members were admitted by election among existing member clubs. The creation of the Alliance Premier League in 1979 — later the Football Conference and now the National League — established a single national division at the top of the non-League game. Automatic promotion to the Football League was finally introduced in 1987, initially for one club per season, and expanded to two in 2003.

The National League System in its present form emerged from FA reviews in the early 2000s intended to rationalise the pyramid, reduce travelling distances, and encourage clubs to improve facilities. A major restructure took place in 2004, creating Steps 1–6 in a recognisable form, and further changes in 2018 and 2021 adjusted the numbers of divisions at Steps 3 and 4 to achieve a "perfect" 1–2–4–8 pattern of promotions.

Administration

The FA's National League System committee oversees the allocation of clubs to leagues each season, applying the NLS regulations. Because the system is regionalised below the top two steps, lateral transfers between leagues at the same step are common as the FA seeks to balance division sizes and minimise travel. Clubs may also apply for voluntary relegation or, in cases of financial failure, be demoted several steps.

The FA Cup and other competitions

Clubs in the National League System enter the FA Cup in the qualifying rounds, and clubs at Steps 1–4 compete in the FA Trophy, while clubs at Steps 5 and 6 compete in the FA Vase, whose finals are held at Wembley Stadium.
