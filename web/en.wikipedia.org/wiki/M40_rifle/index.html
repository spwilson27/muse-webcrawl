M40 rifle

The M40 rifle is a bolt-action sniper rifle used by the United States Marine Corps. Introduced in 1966, it is based on the Remington Model 700 and has been rebuilt and upgraded through several variants, the latest being the M40A7.

M40
Type: Bolt-action sniper rifle
Origin: United States
Cartridge: 7.62×51mm NATO
In service: 1966–present
Manufacturer: Remington Arms / USMC Precision Weapons Section

History

During the Vietnam War the Marine Corps sought a dedicated sniper rifle and adopted a militarised Remington 700 with a heavy barrel and telescopic sight. Each rifle is hand-built by Marine armourers at Quantico, Virginia.

Variants

Successive models — M40A1, A3, A5 and A7 — introduced fibreglass stocks, improved optics, detachable magazines and accessory rails while retaining the short-action 700 receiver.
