Josef Jungmann
Born1773, Hudlice, Bohemia
Died1847, Prague, Austrian Empire
OccupationLinguist, lexicographer, translator
Notable workCzech–German Dictionary (1834–1839)

Josef Jungmann (1773–1847) was a Czech poet, linguist, and a leading figure of the Czech National Revival. Whereas Josef Dobrovský had codified the grammar of Czech, Jungmann set out to prove the language could express modern literature and learning.

Work

Jungmann translated works by Milton, Chateaubriand, and Goethe into Czech, coining many new words in the process. His monumental five-volume Czech–German Dictionary (1834–1839) drew vocabulary from all periods of Czech literature and from other Slavic languages, greatly enriching the modern lexicon. He also wrote a history of Czech literature and taught the language at a time when it had been largely displaced by German in education.
