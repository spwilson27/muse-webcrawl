Causeway Bay

Causeway Bay
District: Wan Chai District
Region: Hong Kong Island
Country: China (Hong Kong SAR)

Causeway Bay is a heavily built-up area of Hong Kong, located on the northern shore of Hong Kong Island. It is one of the city's major shopping districts, known for its department stores, fashion boutiques and some of the highest retail rents in the world.

History

The area takes its name from a causeway built across a sheltered bay in the mid-19th century. Before urban development, the bay was a fishing harbour; land reclamation progressively filled it in. The typhoon shelter that once occupied the bay was moved eastward as reclamation advanced.

Shopping and landmarks

Major retail landmarks include Times Square, Sogo, Hysan Place and Fashion Walk. The district is also home to Victoria Park, Hong Kong's largest urban park, and the Noonday Gun, a naval cannon fired daily at noon, a tradition dating to the 1860s.

Transport

Causeway Bay station on the MTR Island line serves the district, along with numerous tram and bus routes along Hennessy Road and Gloucester Road.
