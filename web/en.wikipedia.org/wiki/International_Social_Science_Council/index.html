International Social Science Council

ISSC
Founded: 1952
Headquarters: Paris, France
Successor: International Science Council (2018)

The International Social Science Council (ISSC) was an international organisation representing the social and behavioural sciences. It was founded in 1952 under the auspices of UNESCO and was based in Paris. Its members were international disciplinary associations — in psychology, sociology, economics, political science and other fields — together with national social science councils and academies.

Activities

The ISSC promoted international social science research, ran comparative research programmes on topics such as poverty, sustainable development and social transformations, and published the World Social Science Report. It co-sponsored interdisciplinary programmes with its natural-science counterpart, the International Council for Science (ICSU).

Merger

In October 2017 the members of the ISSC and ICSU voted to merge, and in July 2018 the two organisations formally united to create the International Science Council, headquartered in Paris.
