Haut-Médoc AOC

Haut-Médoc is an Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée (AOC) for wine in the Bordeaux region of southwestern France, covering the southern, upstream part of the Médoc peninsula along the left bank of the Gironde estuary. It encompasses several more specific appellations, including Saint-Estèphe, Pauillac, Saint-Julien, Margaux, Listrac-Médoc, and Moulis-en-Médoc.

Geography and grapes

The vineyards sit on gravelly ridges deposited by the Garonne and Dordogne rivers, which provide excellent drainage suited to Cabernet Sauvignon, the dominant grape, blended with Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot, Malbec, and Carménère. The appellation covers roughly 4,600 hectares and produces predominantly red wines known for structure and ageing potential.

Classification

Many estates of the Haut-Médoc fall under the Cru Bourgeois classification, while the famous communal appellations within it contain most of the châteaux ranked in the Bordeaux Wine Official Classification of 1855.
