Who shot J.R.?
Series: Dallas
Cliffhanger aired: 21 March 1980
Resolution aired: 21 November 1980

"Who shot J.R.?" was a catchphrase and international media phenomenon generated by the American prime-time soap opera Dallas, after its villainous character J. R. Ewing, played by Larry Hagman, was shot by an unseen assailant in the third-season finale, "A House Divided", broadcast on 21 March 1980.

The phenomenon

The cliffhanger left audiences speculating through the summer of 1980; betting shops took odds on the culprit and the phrase appeared on T-shirts and in political campaigns. The resolution, "Who Done It", aired on 21 November 1980 and revealed the shooter to be Kristin Shepard, J.R.'s sister-in-law and mistress. It was watched by an estimated 83 million viewers in the United States, one of the largest television audiences in history at the time.

Legacy

The event popularised the season-ending cliffhanger in television drama. See also Dallas.
