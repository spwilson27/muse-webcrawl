STMicroelectronics

STMicroelectronics N.V.
Industry: Semiconductors
Founded: 1987 (merger of SGS Microelettronica and Thomson Semiconducteurs)
Headquarters: Plan-les-Ouates, Geneva, Switzerland
Products: Microcontrollers, sensors, power semiconductors

STMicroelectronics is a Franco-Italian multinational semiconductor company, one of Europe's largest chipmakers. It designs and manufactures a broad range of semiconductor products, including the widely used STM32 family of ARM-based microcontrollers, MEMS sensors, power devices (including silicon-carbide MOSFETs for electric vehicles), automotive chips and secure microcontrollers.

History

The company was formed in 1987 by the merger of Italy's SGS Microelettronica and France's Thomson Semiconducteurs, originally under the name SGS-Thomson; it adopted the STMicroelectronics name in 1998. Although legally headquartered in the Netherlands/Geneva area, its operational roots remain in France and Italy, with major sites at Crolles, Agrate and Catania.

Business

ST is a major supplier to the automotive and industrial markets and to consumer electronics makers — its inertial sensors have shipped in devices from Nintendo's Wii to generations of smartphones. Along with Infineon and NXP, it forms the trio of large European semiconductor manufacturers.
