Zotero

Zotero is a free and open-source reference management software used to manage bibliographic data and related research materials, such as PDF files. It was originally developed at the Center for History and New Media at George Mason University and first released in 2006. The name is derived from the Albanian verb zotëroj, meaning "to master".

Features

Zotero allows users to collect references from web pages, library catalogues, and databases via a browser connector; organise items into collections and tag them; attach notes and files; and generate citations and bibliographies in thousands of citation styles based on the Citation Style Language (CSL). Plugins integrate Zotero with word processors such as Microsoft Word, LibreOffice Writer, and Google Docs, enabling in-text citations to be inserted and bibliographies to be updated automatically.

Development

Initially released as a Firefox extension, Zotero became a standalone application in 2011 (Zotero Standalone), running on Windows, macOS, and Linux. The standalone version communicates with browser connectors for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Zotero data can be synchronised across devices through the Zotero servers, with free storage for metadata and a limited amount of file storage.
