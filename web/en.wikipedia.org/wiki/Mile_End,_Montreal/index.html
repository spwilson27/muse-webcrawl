Mile End, Montreal

Mile End is a neighbourhood in the borough of Le Plateau-Mont-Royal in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Bounded roughly by Avenue du Mont-Royal, Saint-Laurent Boulevard, Van Horne Avenue and the Canadian Pacific railway tracks, it is known for its artistic community, cafés, bagel shops and multicultural history.

History

The area takes its name from its distance — about a mile — from the old city limits. In the nineteenth and twentieth centuries it was home to successive waves of Jewish, Greek, Portuguese and other immigrant communities. St-Viateur Bagel and Fairmount Bagel, two of Montreal's famous bagel bakeries, are located here.

Culture

Mile End is a centre of Montreal's indie music and arts scenes, associated with venues and studios linked to bands such as Arcade Fire and Godspeed You! Black Emperor. In the 2010s the neighbourhood also became a hub for video-game and technology companies, including a major Ubisoft studio.
