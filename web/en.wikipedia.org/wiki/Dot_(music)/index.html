Dot (music)

In music notation, a dot placed after a note or rest — an augmentation dot — increases its duration by half its original value. A second dot adds a further quarter, and so on.

Usage

A dotted half note lasts three beats in 4/4 time; dotted rhythms are characteristic of marches, Baroque overtures in the French style, and countless melodies.

Other dots

Dots above or below notes indicate staccato, and dots within repeat signs differ entirely; context distinguishes augmentation from articulation.
