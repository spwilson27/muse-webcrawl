Pulmonology

Pulmonology, also called respiratory medicine, pneumology or chest medicine, is the medical specialty dealing with diseases of the respiratory tract, including the airways, lungs, pleura and chest wall, and with breathing and sleep-related breathing disorders.

Scope

Pulmonologists diagnose and treat asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, pneumonia and other infections including tuberculosis, interstitial lung diseases, pulmonary hypertension, lung cancer, occupational lung diseases and respiratory failure. Many also practise critical care medicine, managing mechanical ventilation in intensive care units.

Diagnosis

Tools include spirometry and other pulmonary function tests, chest imaging, bronchoscopy, pleural procedures, sleep studies and laboratory testing.
