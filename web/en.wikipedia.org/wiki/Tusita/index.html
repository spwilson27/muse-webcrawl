Tusita

Tusita (Sanskrit: Tuṣita; Pali: Tusita) is one of the heavens in Buddhist cosmology, the fourth of the six heavens of the desire realm (Kāmadhātu). Its name means "the Contented" or "the Joyful", and its inhabitants are known as the Tusita gods.

Significance

Tusita holds a special place in Buddhist tradition as the heaven in which a Bodhisattva resides during his final existence before taking rebirth on earth to become a Buddha. According to the scriptures, the Bodhisattva Śvetaketu ("white banner") dwelt in Tusita before descending to be born as Siddhartha Gautama. The future Buddha, Maitreya, is said to reside in Tusita now, awaiting the time of his final birth, and many Mahayana devotees aspire to be reborn there to hear his teaching.

Cosmology

In the traditional scheme, time in Tusita flows far more slowly than on earth: one day and night there equals 400 human years, and the lifespan of its gods is 4,000 Tusita years. Tusita lies above Yāma heaven and below Nirmāṇarati heaven in the vertical arrangement of the desire realm, all situated above Mount Sumeru.
