Howard Panter

Sir Howard Hugh Panter (born 25 May 1949) is a British theatre impresario and producer. He co-founded the Ambassador Theatre Group (ATG) in 1992 with his wife Rosemary Squire, building it into the world's largest theatre-owning and operating company, with venues across the United Kingdom, Broadway and continental Europe. Panter and Squire have repeatedly been ranked among the most influential figures in British theatre.

Career

Panter began his career in stage management and production, working in regional theatre and touring before becoming a producer in London's West End. In 1992 he and Rosemary Squire founded ATG, acquiring venues including the Duke of York's Theatre and expanding through acquisitions such as the Live Nation UK theatre portfolio in 2009, which added major regional houses including the Manchester Opera House and the Bristol Hippodrome. ATG also acquired Broadway's Lyric Theatre and became a major producer of shows including The Lion King (as venue operator), Wicked tours and numerous West End productions.

Trafalgar Entertainment

In 2016 Panter stepped down as joint chief executive of ATG, retaining a shareholding, and in 2017 he and Squire launched Trafalgar Entertainment, which took over London's Trafalgar Studios (renovated and renamed the Trafalgar Theatre), developed the Chiswick Cinema, and expanded into theatre production and distribution. Panter was knighted for services to theatre. He is also known for championing the restoration and commercial revival of regional British theatres.
