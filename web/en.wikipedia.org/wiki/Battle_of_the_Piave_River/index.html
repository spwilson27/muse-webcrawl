Battle of the Piave River

Battle of the Piave River
Date: 15–23 June 1918
Location: Piave River, Italy
Part of: First World War

The Battle of the Piave River, also called the Battle of the Solstice, was fought from 15 to 23 June 1918 between Italy and Austria-Hungary during the First World War. Austria-Hungary's last major offensive in Italy failed against the Italian line on the Piave.

Background

After Caporetto, Italy under General Armando Diaz rebuilt its army along the Piave. Pressed by Germany, Austria-Hungary attacked across the river in June 1918.

The battle

Italian artillery disrupted the crossings; floods swept away bridges, stranding Austro-Hungarian bridgeheads, which were destroyed in counterattacks.

Significance

The victory secured Italy and began the final decline of the Austro-Hungarian army, culminating at Vittorio Veneto.
