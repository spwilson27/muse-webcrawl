Cellosaurus

The Cellosaurus is a cell line knowledgebase maintained by the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics. It is a manually curated resource that attempts to list and describe all cell lines used in biomedical research, assigning each a stable accession identifier (of the form CVCL_xxxx).

Content

Each entry records a cell line's names and synonyms, species and tissue of origin, category (for example, cancer cell line, hybridoma, or stem cell line), characteristics, derived lines, known problems such as misidentification or contamination, and references. The resource covers more than one hundred thousand cell lines from hundreds of species and is integrated with other SIB resources such as Expasy and neXtProt.

Role in reproducibility

Because misidentified and cross-contaminated cell lines are a persistent source of irreproducible research, the Cellosaurus flags problematic lines and serves as a reference vocabulary — its identifiers are used by journals, databases, and Research Resource Identifier (RRID) initiatives to make the materials used in experiments unambiguous.
