Grader
TypeHeavy equipment
UseLevelling and shaping ground surfaces

A grader, also commonly referred to as a road grader or motor grader, is a construction machine with a long blade used to create a flat surface during the grading process. Typical models have three axles, with the engine and cab situated above the rear axles at one end of the vehicle and a third axle at the front end, with the blade between them.

Function

Graders are used in civil engineering and construction to shape and level the ground, to maintain gravel and dirt roads by redistributing surface material, and to prepare base courses prior to paving. The mouldboard blade can be angled, tilted and shifted to control the depth and distribution of material.

History

Early graders were drawn by horses or tractors; self-propelled motor graders emerged in the early 20th century. Modern machines feature articulated frames, all-wheel drive options and increasingly automated blade control using GPS and laser guidance.
