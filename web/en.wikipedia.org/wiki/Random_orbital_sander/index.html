Random orbital sander

TypePower tool
UseSanding wood and other materials
Typical pad size125 mm (5 in) or 150 mm (6 in)

A random orbital sander (also known as a dual-action, or DA, sander) is a hand-held power tool which sands in a random-orbit action. The sanding disc both spins and moves in small ellipses, so that no single abrasive particle follows the same path twice. This random motion greatly reduces the visible swirl marks and cross-grain scratches produced by purely orbital or rotary sanders.

Features

Random orbital sanders usually take round abrasive discs attached with hook-and-loop fasteners, commonly 125 mm (5 inch) or 150 mm (6 inch) in diameter. Most models include holes in the disc and pad for dust extraction into a bag or vacuum. Electric (corded and cordless) and pneumatic versions are produced; pneumatic models are common in automotive body work.

Uses

The tool is widely used in woodworking, furniture finishing, and automotive refinishing for stock removal and fine finishing, and is often the final sanding step before applying a finish.
