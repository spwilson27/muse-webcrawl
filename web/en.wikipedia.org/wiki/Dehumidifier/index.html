Dehumidifier

A dehumidifier is an appliance that reduces and maintains the level of humidity in air, usually for health, comfort and protection of buildings and stored goods.

Types

Refrigerant dehumidifiers condense moisture on cold coils; desiccant units absorb water in materials such as silica gel and suit cooler conditions.

Uses

They prevent mould, dust mites, condensation and corrosion in homes, basements, archives, swimming pools and industry.

Capacity

Capacity is rated by water removed per day; condensate drains to a tank or hose.
