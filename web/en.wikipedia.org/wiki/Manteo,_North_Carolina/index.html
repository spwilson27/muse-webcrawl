Manteo is a town in Dare County, North Carolina, United States, and its county seat. It is located on Roanoke Island in the Outer Banks, between Shallowbag Bay and Roanoke Sound. The population was 1,600 at the 2020 census. The town is named for Manteo, the Croatan Native American who befriended the English colonists of the Roanoke Colony in the 1580s.

History

Roanoke Island was the site of the first English attempts at colonisation in North America, sponsored by Sir Walter Raleigh in 1585 and 1587. The 1587 settlement, the "Lost Colony", had vanished by the time John White returned in 1590; Virginia Dare, the first English child born in the Americas, was born there in 1587. The modern town grew as a port and trading centre in the 19th century and was incorporated in 1899.

Nearby Fort Raleigh National Historic Site preserves the location of the colony, and the outdoor drama The Lost Colony, performed since 1937, dramatises the story each summer. The town is also close to the Wright Brothers National Memorial at Kill Devil Hills.

Geography and tourism

Manteo's restored waterfront on Shallowbag Bay features the Roanoke Marshes Lighthouse, the replica 16th-century ship Elizabeth II at Roanoke Island Festival Park, shops, and tour boats. The North Carolina Aquarium on Roanoke Island is located just north of town. Actor Andy Griffith maintained a home in Manteo for many years, and the town inspired aspects of the fictional Mayberry.
