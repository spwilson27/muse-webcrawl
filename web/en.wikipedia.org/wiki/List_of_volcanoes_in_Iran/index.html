List of volcanoes in Iran

Volcanoes of Iran
TallestDamavand (5,610 m)
ActivityMostly dormant; fumarolic activity at Damavand and Taftan

This is a list of volcanoes in Iran. Iranian volcanism is associated with the collision of the Arabian and Eurasian plates. The highest and best known volcano is Mount Damavand, a dormant stratovolcano in the Alborz range which, at 5,610 metres, is the highest volcano in Asia and a central figure in Persian mythology. Most Iranian volcanoes have no recorded historical eruptions, though several show fumarolic activity.
