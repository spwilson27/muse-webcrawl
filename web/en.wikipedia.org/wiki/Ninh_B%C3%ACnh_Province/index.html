Ninh Bình is a province of Vietnam in the Red River Delta region, located about 90 km south of Hanoi. It is known for its dramatic karst limestone landscapes, rivers and historic temples.

Geography

The province has a short coastline on the Gulf of Tonkin and borders Hòa Bình, Nam Định, Hà Nam and Thanh Hóa provinces. The Day River flows through the province toward the sea.

History

The ancient capital of Hoa Lư, within the province, served as Vietnam's capital in the 10th and early 11th centuries under the Đinh, Early Lê and early Lý dynasties. Temples dedicated to emperors Đinh Tiên Hoàng and Lê Đại Hành remain major sites.

Tourism

Major attractions include the Tam Cốc-Bích Động river caves, the Tràng An Scenic Landscape Complex (a UNESCO World Heritage Site), Bái Đính Pagoda, Phát Diệm Cathedral and Cúc Phương National Park, Vietnam's oldest national park.
