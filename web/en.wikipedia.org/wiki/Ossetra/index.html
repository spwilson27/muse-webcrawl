Ossetra

Ossetra (also spelled osetra, osietra or asetra) is one of the most prized types of caviar, harvested from the Russian sturgeon (Acipenser gueldenstaedtii) native to the Caspian Sea, Black Sea and Sea of Azov basins. It ranks in commerce between beluga caviar, from the larger beluga sturgeon, and sevruga caviar, from the smaller stellate sturgeon.

Characteristics

Ossetra eggs are medium-sized, ranging in colour from dark brown and golden green to the rare pale gold "royal" or "imperial" grade, once reserved for royalty. The flavour is often described as nutty, rich and slightly fruity, with a firm texture and clean finish. Because wild sturgeon populations have collapsed through overfishing and habitat loss, all sturgeon species are protected under CITES and most ossetra on the market today comes from aquaculture in countries including Israel, Italy, Germany, China and the United States.
