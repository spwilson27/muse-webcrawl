Theory of knowledge (IB course)

Theory of knowledge (TOK) is an interdisciplinary course in the IB Diploma Programme of the International Baccalaureate. Rather than a body of content to be learned, it is a course about the nature of knowledge itself, asking students to reflect on how we know what we claim to know.

Course structure

The course is organised around a core theme, "Knowledge and the knower", optional themes such as knowledge and technology or knowledge and language, and five areas of knowledge: history, the human sciences, the natural sciences, mathematics and the arts. Students explore "knowledge questions" — open, contestable questions about knowledge — and the ways different disciplines produce and justify claims.

Assessment

Assessment consists of an internally assessed exhibition, in which students comment on three objects linked to a prescribed prompt, and an externally assessed essay of up to 1,600 words on one of six prescribed titles set by the IB. Together with the Extended Essay, TOK contributes up to three core points toward the diploma.
