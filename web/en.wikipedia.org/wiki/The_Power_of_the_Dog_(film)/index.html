The Power of the Dog (film)

The Power of the Dog is a 2021 Western psychological drama film written and directed by Jane Campion, based on Thomas Savage's 1967 novel. It stars Benedict Cumberbatch, Kirsten Dunst, Jesse Plemons, and Kodi Smit-McPhee.

Plot

In 1925 Montana, wealthy ranch brothers Phil and George Burbank have a strained relationship. When George marries the widowed Rose and brings her and her sensitive son Peter to the ranch, Phil wages a campaign of psychological cruelty against Rose while gradually taking Peter under his wing. The arrangement ends in tragedy when Phil, who has an open cut on his hand, handles rawhide from an anthrax-infected cow that Peter has deliberately provided.

Reception

The film premiered at the Venice International Film Festival, where Campion won the Silver Lion for Best Direction. It received twelve nominations at the 94th Academy Awards, including Best Picture, and Campion won Best Director. Jonny Greenwood's score and Ari Wegner's cinematography were widely praised.
