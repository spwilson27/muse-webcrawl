Smørrebrød

Smørrebrød is a traditional open-faced sandwich in Danish cuisine, consisting of a slice of dark, buttered rye bread (rugbrød) topped with combinations of meats, fish, cheese and spreads. The name literally means "buttered bread" in Danish.

History

Open sandwiches with rye bread arose in the 19th century when factory workers took topped bread slices for lunch; Copenhagen restaurants soon elevated them into an art form with elaborate, named combinations and specialist shops. Butter is spread thickly, acting as a barrier to keep the bread from becoming soggy.

Classic toppings

Traditional toppings include pickled herring, roast beef with remoulade and crispy onions, leverpostej (liver pâté) with bacon, smoked eel, and "dyrlægens natmad" (the veterinarian's night snack). Smørrebrød is typically eaten with knife and fork, accompanied by beer and aquavit.
