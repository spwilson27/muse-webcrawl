Battle of Manila

Battle of Manila may refer to several battles fought in and around Manila, the capital of the Philippines, over the course of several centuries. Because of Manila's strategic position on Manila Bay and its role as a major port and colonial capital, the city has repeatedly been a prize contested by Spanish, British, American, Filipino, and Japanese forces.

Manila, on the eastern shore of Manila Bay, has been the scene of numerous battles.

List of battles

  • Battle of Manila (1570) – forces of Martín de Goiti attacked the Rajahnate of Maynila.
  • Battle of Manila (1574) – the Chinese pirate Limahong attempted to seize the city.
  • Battle of Manila (1762) – British forces captured Manila from Spain during the Seven Years' War.
  • Battle of Manila (1898) – American and Filipino forces took the city from Spain during the Spanish–American War, in a partly staged engagement on 13 August 1898.
  • Battle of Manila (1899) – American forces fought Filipino forces at the opening of the Philippine–American War.
  • Battle of Manila (1945) – American and Filipino forces liberated the city from Japanese occupation in February–March 1945, in one of the most destructive urban battles of the Pacific War.
