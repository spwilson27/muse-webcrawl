Ehsan Jafri (1929–2002)

Ehsan Jafri (1929 – 28 February 2002) was an Indian politician and Member of Parliament from the Indian National Congress. He was killed during the Gulbarg Society massacre in the 2002 Gujarat riots, one of the most widely reported episodes of that violence.

Life

Jafri was a lawyer and trade unionist in Ahmedabad who rose through Congress politics, serving as a member of the Gujarat Legislative Assembly and then as MP for Ahmedabad in the Sixth Lok Sabha (1977–1980). He was also a published Urdu poet and writer.

Death and legal proceedings

On 28 February 2002, the day after the Godhra train burning, a mob attacked the Gulbarg Society housing complex in Ahmedabad, where Jafri had sheltered dozens of Muslim families in his home. According to witnesses, Jafri made repeated phone calls seeking police help before the mob killed him and 68 others. A special court in 2016 convicted 24 people for the Gulbarg massacre, sentencing 11 to life imprisonment. His widow Zakia Jafri pursued a long legal battle alleging a wider conspiracy and official failure to act, culminating in the Supreme Court of India dismissing her petition in 2022, upholding the Special Investigation Team's closure report giving a clean chit to senior officials.
