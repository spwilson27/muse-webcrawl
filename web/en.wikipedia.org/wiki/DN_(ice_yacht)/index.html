DN (ice yacht)

The International DN is a class of ice boat. It is the most popular iceboat class in both North America and Europe, a one-design single-handed vessel about 3.7 m (12 ft) long with a cross plank and three steel runners.

The name DN comes from the Detroit News, which sponsored a design contest in 1936–1937 won by a boat named the Skeeter-influenced design of Archie Arrol and others; the winning plans were published in the newspaper and thousands were built at home. The DN can reach speeds well over 100 km/h in good conditions.

Design

The class is a developmental one-design: hull dimensions, mast bend characteristics and sail size are tightly controlled, while materials and details allow some experimentation. The sailor lies in a cockpit and steers the front runner with the feet.

Racing

World and continental championships are held annually, alternating between North America and Europe, governed by the International DN Ice Yacht Racing Association (IDNIYRA).
