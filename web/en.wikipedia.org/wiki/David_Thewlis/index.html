David Thewlis (born David Wheeler; 20 March 1963) is an English actor, filmmaker and author. He rose to prominence for his performance as the drifting, philosophical Johnny in Mike Leigh's film Naked (1993), for which he won the Best Actor award at the Cannes Film Festival.

Career

Thewlis trained at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama in London. After early roles in Life Is Sweet (1990) and Damage (1992), Naked made his reputation. He reached a worldwide audience as Professor Remus Lupin in the Harry Potter films, beginning with Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (2004). His other credits include Dragonheart (1996), The Island of Dr Moreau (1996), Seven Years in Tibet (1997), The Big Lebowski (1998), Kingdom of Heaven (2005), The Boy in the Striped Pyjamas (2008), War Horse (2011), The Theory of Everything (2014), Wonder Woman (2017) as the villain Ares, Fargo season 3 (2017), for which he received Emmy and Golden Globe nominations, I'm Thinking of Ending Things (2020) and Enola Holmes 2 (2022), and the series The Sandman as John Dee.

Other work

Thewlis wrote and directed the film Cheeky (2003) and published a novel, The Late Hector Kipling, in 2007, followed by the children's book Shooting Martha (2021).
