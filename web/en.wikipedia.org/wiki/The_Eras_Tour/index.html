The Eras Tour

The Eras Tour was the sixth concert tour by the American singer-songwriter Taylor Swift. Conceived as a journey through all of Swift's musical "eras", the tour ran from March 2023 to December 2024, comprising 149 shows across five continents.

Concert structure

The three-and-a-half-hour show was divided into ten acts, each representing one of Swift's studio albums, from her debut through Midnights, with The Tortured Poets Department added to the setlist in 2024. Each performance also included an acoustic "surprise songs" section.

Impact

The Eras Tour became the highest-grossing concert tour of all time and the first to surpass $1 billion and then $2 billion in gross revenue. Demand for tickets overwhelmed Ticketmaster in the US presale, prompting public and political scrutiny of ticketing practices. The accompanying concert film, Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour, became the highest-grossing concert film of all time.
