Choi Min-sik

Choi Min-sik (born 1962) is a South Korean actor regarded as one of the leading performers of modern Korean cinema. He gained international fame for his role as Oh Dae-su in Park Chan-wook's Oldboy (2003).

Career

After a theatre background, Choi rose to prominence in Shiri (1999) and Happy End (1999), followed by acclaimed performances in Painted Fire (2002), I Saw the Devil (2010) and Lucy (2014). He starred in The Admiral: Roaring Currents (2014), the highest-grossing Korean film of its time.
