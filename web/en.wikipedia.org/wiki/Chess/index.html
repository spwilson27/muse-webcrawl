Chess

Chess
TypeAbstract strategy board game for two players
OriginFrom Indian chaturanga, c. 6th century AD
World bodyFIDE
[Image: A chess board mid-game with carved wooden pieces, king and queen prominent]

Chess is a recreational and competitive board game played on an 8×8 checkered board, in which two players move 16 pieces each — king, queen, rooks, bishops, knights and pawns — to checkmate the opponent's king. It descends from the Indian game chaturanga via Persia ('shah mat' — the king is dead).

Modern chess

Governed by FIDE, chess has world champions from Steinitz to Magnus Carlsen, a vast opening theory, and was a milestone for AI when Deep Blue beat Garry Kasparov (1997) — a forerunner of the game-playing systems in the video game and AI pages. Online platforms revived its popularity in the 2020s.
