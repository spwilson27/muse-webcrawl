Santos Futebol Clube is a Brazilian sports club based in Vila Belmiro, a neighbourhood of Santos, São Paulo. Founded in 1912, its football team is one of Brazil's most successful and famous, celebrated above all as the club of Pelé, who played there from 1956 to 1974, and later of Neymar. Santos are renowned for an attacking tradition nicknamed futebol arte and for their prolific youth academy, whose graduates are called Meninos da Vila ("Boys of the Vila").

History

The Pelé-era Santos of the 1960s won two Copa Libertadores and two Intercontinental Cups (1962, 1963), five consecutive Brazilian national titles, and toured the world; the 1962–63 side is considered among the greatest club teams ever. Santos famously scored their 10,000th goal in 1998 and reached 12,000 goals faster than any club in history. A later golden generation led by Diego and Robinho won the 2002 Brazilian championship, and Neymar's team won the 2011 Copa Libertadores. In December 2023, amid a severe crisis, Santos were relegated from Série A for the first time in their history; they won Série B in 2024 and returned to the top flight.

Honours

  • Intercontinental Cup: 1962, 1963
  • Copa Libertadores: 1962, 1963, 2011
  • Brazilian championships: 8 (including the 1961–65 Taça Brasil run)
  • Copa do Brasil: 2010; Campeonato Paulista: 22 titles

Rivalries

Santos contest the Clássico da Saudade with Palmeiras, the San-São with São Paulo, and the Clássico Alvinegro with Corinthians.
