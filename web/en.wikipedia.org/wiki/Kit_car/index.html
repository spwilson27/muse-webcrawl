Kit car

A kit car is an automobile available as a set of parts that a buyer assembles themselves, or has assembled by a third party, into a complete car. Kits range from replica bodies on donor chassis, such as Volkswagen Beetle-based dune buggies and Cobra replicas, to original designs like the Lotus Seven-inspired Caterham and Westfield cars.

Kit cars became popular in the UK due to tax advantages and later in the US and elsewhere for enthusiasts seeking affordable performance or classic styling without original prices. Registration, safety and emissions rules vary by jurisdiction. Manufacturers include Factory Five Racing, Caterham Cars and Ultima.
