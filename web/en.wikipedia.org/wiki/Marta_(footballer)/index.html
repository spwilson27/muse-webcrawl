Marta Vieira da Silva (born 19 February 1986), known mononymously as Marta, is a Brazilian professional footballer who plays as a forward. Widely regarded as the greatest female footballer of all time, she was named FIFA World Player of the Year six times — five of them consecutively from 2006 to 2010 — and is the all-time top scorer of the FIFA Women's World Cup with 17 goals.

Club career

Marta was discovered as a teenager and joined Vasco da Gama before moving to Swedish club Umeå IK in 2004, where she won the UEFA Women's Cup and multiple league titles. She starred in the short-lived American Women's Professional Soccer league, winning championships with FC Gold Pride and the Western New York Flash, and returned to Sweden with Tyresö FF — reaching the 2014 Champions League final — and FC Rosengård. In 2017 she joined the Orlando Pride of the NWSL, where she became a long-serving captain and won the NWSL Championship in 2024.

International career

Marta debuted for Brazil in 2002 and played at six World Cups, winning the Golden Ball at the 2007 tournament, where Brazil finished runners-up. With the national team she won silver medals at the 2004, 2008 and 2024 Olympic Games, and she scored at five successive Olympics. Her 119 international goals make her Brazil's top scorer of either gender. She announced her retirement from international football at the end of 2024.

Legacy

Nicknamed "Pelé in skirts" by Pelé himself, Marta is a global ambassador for women's sport and a UN Development Programme goodwill ambassador. In 2024 FIFA named its new award for the best goal in women's football the Marta Award, and she was its inaugural winner.
