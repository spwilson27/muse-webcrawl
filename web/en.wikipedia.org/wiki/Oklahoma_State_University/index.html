Oklahoma State University (informally OSU) is a public land-grant research university in Stillwater, Oklahoma. Founded in 1890 under the Morrill Land-Grant Acts as Oklahoma Agricultural and Mechanical College (Oklahoma A&M), it adopted its current name in 1957. The university is the flagship institution of the Oklahoma State University System, with additional campuses in Oklahoma City, Tulsa, and Okmulgee.

Academics

OSU offers more than 300 undergraduate and graduate degree programs through colleges of agriculture, arts and sciences, business, education, engineering, human sciences, and veterinary medicine, and operates the Center for Health Sciences in Tulsa. It is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity".

Athletics

The Oklahoma State Cowboys and Cowgirls compete in NCAA Division I as members of the Big 12 Conference. The university is especially renowned for wrestling — its program has won 34 NCAA team championships, the most of any school — and also for its rivalry with the University of Oklahoma, known as Bedlam.
