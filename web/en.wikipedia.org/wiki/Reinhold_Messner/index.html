Reinhold Messner (born 1944) is an Italian mountaineer from South Tyrol, widely regarded as the greatest Himalayan climber of all time. In 1978, with Peter Habeler, he made the first ascent of Mount Everest without supplemental oxygen — a feat many physiologists had thought impossible — and in 1980 he repeated it solo.

Record and later work

In 1986 he became the first person to climb all 14 peaks over 8,000 metres, all without bottled oxygen, pioneering lightweight 'alpine style' in the greater ranges. He also crossed Antarctica and the Gobi Desert on foot. Later he founded the Messner Mountain Museum network in the Alps and served in the European Parliament for the Greens.
