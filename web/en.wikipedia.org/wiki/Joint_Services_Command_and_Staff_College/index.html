The Joint Services Command and Staff College (JSCSC) is a British military academic establishment providing command and staff training for officers of the Royal Navy, British Army, Royal Air Force and civil service, together with officers from allied nations. It forms part of the Defence Academy of the United Kingdom at Shrivenham, Oxfordshire.

History and courses

The college was formed in 1997 by amalgamating the single-service staff colleges — the Royal Naval College at Greenwich, the Staff College at Camberley, RAF Staff College at Bracknell and the Joint Service Defence College — first at Bracknell before moving to a purpose-built facility at Shrivenham in 2000. Its flagship programme is the Advanced Command and Staff Course (ACSC), a roughly year-long course for mid-career officers, delivered in partnership with King's College London, whose Defence Studies Department provides the academic faculty. Graduates may earn a master's degree in defence studies.

The college also runs the Higher Command and Staff Course for senior officers destined for top appointments, the Intermediate Command and Staff Courses, and specialist training such as the Defence Internal Audit and legal and logistics courses. Its motto is "Unity is Strength".
