Entrectinib

Trade nameRozlytrek
TypeTyrosine kinase inhibitor
TargetsTRK A/B/C, ROS1, ALK
RouteOral

Entrectinib, sold under the brand name Rozlytrek, is an anticancer medication used to treat tumours that harbour NTRK gene fusions and ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer. It is a selective tyrosine kinase inhibitor of the tropomyosin receptor kinases TrkA, TrkB and TrkC, as well as ROS1 and ALK.

Medical uses

Entrectinib was approved in the United States in 2019 for adults and children with solid tumours that have an NTRK gene fusion without a known acquired resistance mutation, and for adults with metastatic ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer. It is designed to cross the blood–brain barrier, giving it activity against central nervous system metastases.

Side effects

Common adverse effects include fatigue, constipation, altered taste, dizziness, and weight gain. Serious risks include congestive heart failure, central nervous system effects, and skeletal fractures.
