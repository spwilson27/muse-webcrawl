343 Industries is an American video game developer owned by Microsoft and part of Xbox Game Studios. Based in Redmond, Washington, the studio was founded in 2007 to oversee the Halo franchise after the series' original developer, Bungie, became independent. Its name derives from 343 Guilty Spark, a character in the Halo universe.

History

Microsoft established the studio in 2007, initially as an internal team coordinating Halo media including novels, comics, and the downloadable content for Bungie's games. After Bungie's final Halo title, Halo: Reach (2010), full development responsibility for the series passed to 343 Industries. The studio's first release was Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary (2011), a remake of the original game developed with Saber Interactive, followed by its first original mainline title, Halo 4, in 2012.

Subsequent releases include Halo: The Master Chief Collection (2014), a compilation whose troubled online launch prompted public apologies and corrective updates; Halo 5: Guardians (2015); and Halo Infinite (2021), built on the studio's proprietary Slipspace Engine and released alongside a free-to-play multiplayer component.

Leadership and restructuring

The studio was led for many years by Bonnie Ross, who departed in 2022. Following her departure, leadership was divided among Pierre Hintze (studio head), Bryan Koski (franchise general manager), and Elizabeth Van Wyck (business operations). In 2024 the studio announced it would rebrand as Halo Studios and transition future development to Unreal Engine 5.
