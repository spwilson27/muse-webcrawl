List of countries by external debt

External debt is the portion of a country's debt that is owed to foreign lenders, including commercial banks, other governments, and international financial institutions such as the World Bank and the International Monetary Fund. This list ranks countries by gross external debt in United States dollars, drawing mainly on World Bank and CIA World Factbook figures. Large financial centres tend to carry very high external debt because of the scale of their international banking sectors.
