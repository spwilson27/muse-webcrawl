Swimming at the 2000 Summer Olympics

Swimming at the 2000 Summer Olympics
Venue: Sydney International Aquatic Centre
Dates: 16–23 September 2000
Events: 32 (16 men's, 16 women's)
Competitors: 954 from 150 nations

The swimming competitions at the 2000 Summer Olympics in Sydney took place from 16 to 23 September at the Sydney International Aquatic Centre. A total of 32 events were contested — 16 each for men and women — and 954 swimmers from 150 nations took part. Fourteen world records were broken during the meet.

Highlights

Australian teenager Ian Thorpe became the star of the home Games, winning gold in the 400 metre freestyle in world-record time and anchoring Australia's famous victory over the United States in the men's 4 × 100 metre freestyle relay — the first time the Americans had lost the event at the Olympics. Dutch swimmers Pieter van den Hoogenband and Inge de Bruijn dominated the sprint events; van den Hoogenband defeated Thorpe in the 200 metre freestyle and set world records in the 100 metre freestyle.

Other notable results included gold medals for Australia's Susie O'Neill, Italy's Domenico Fioravanti and Massimiliano Rosolino, Ukraine's Yana Klochkova, and Japan's and Romania's first swimming golds of the Games era. The United States topped the medal table despite Australia's strong home performance.
