Macy's, Inc.

Macy's, Inc. (originally Federated Department Stores, Inc.) is an American department store holding company headquartered in New York City. It is the owner of the Macy's and Bloomingdale's department store chains, as well as the Bluemercury beauty and skincare chain.

History

Federated Department Stores was founded in 1929 as a holding company for stores including Bloomingdale's, Filene's and Lazarus. After a leveraged buyout and bankruptcy in the early 1990s, Federated merged with R.H. Macy & Co. in 1994 and acquired May Department Stores in 2005, converting most regional nameplates to Macy's. In 2007 the company renamed itself Macy's, Inc. Its flagship Macy's Herald Square store in Manhattan is among the world's largest department stores, and the company sponsors the annual Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.
