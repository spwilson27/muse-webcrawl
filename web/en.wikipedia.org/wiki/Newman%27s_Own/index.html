Newman's Own is an American food company founded in 1982 by actor Paul Newman and writer A. E. Hotchner. It began with a homemade salad dressing Newman gave to friends, and grew into a brand selling dressings, pasta sauce, lemonade, popcorn, frozen pizza and pet food. Its defining principle is that all profits are donated to charitable causes.

Charitable giving

Since 1982 the company and the Newman's Own Foundation have given away hundreds of millions of dollars to thousands of charities. Best known is the Hole in the Wall Gang Camp in Connecticut, founded by Newman in 1988, which provides free summer camps and programmes for children with serious illnesses and their families.

Slogan

The brand's tongue-in-cheek packaging carries the motto "Shameless exploitation in pursuit of the common good."
