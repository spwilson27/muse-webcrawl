Casino Royale is a 2006 spy film, the twenty-first in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions, and the first to star Daniel Craig as MI6 agent James Bond. It is based on Ian Fleming's 1953 novel, the first Bond book, and reboots the series, showing Bond at the beginning of his career, earning his licence to kill.

Plot

Newly promoted to 00 status, Bond pursues bomb-maker Mollaka in Madagascar and uncovers a conspiracy involving banker Le Chiffre, who funds terrorists. To recover money lost in a failed short-selling scheme, Le Chiffre stages a high-stakes poker game at the Casino Royale in Montenegro. MI6 bankrolls Bond, accompanied by Treasury agent Vesper Lynd. Bond wins, is betrayed and tortured, and ultimately discovers Vesper's coerced double-dealing; she drowns in Venice after saving him, leaving him hardened and distrustful.

Reception

The film was praised for its grittier realism, Craig's performance, and physical action — including a celebrated parkour chase — and became the highest-grossing Bond film to that date. It received a BAFTA for Best Sound and nine nominations.

Sequels

Craig's tenure continued with Quantum of Solace (2008), Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015), and No Time to Die (2021).
