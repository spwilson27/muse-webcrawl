La Plata County, Colorado

La Plata County
County seat: Durango
State: Colorado
Founded: 1874

La Plata County is a county located in the southwestern part of the U.S. state of Colorado. Its county seat and largest city is Durango. The county takes its name from the La Plata River and the La Plata Mountains, "la plata" being Spanish for "the silver."

Geography

The county lies in the San Juan Mountains region and includes parts of the San Juan National Forest. The Animas River flows south through Durango. The Southern Ute Indian Reservation occupies part of the southern county.

Transportation

Durango is the southern terminus of the Durango and Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad, a heritage railway running through the Animas River gorge to the mining town of Silverton. The Durango–La Plata County Airport serves the region.

Communities

Incorporated communities include Durango, Bayfield, and Ignacio. The county had a population of roughly 56,000 at the 2020 census.
