Big Little Lies is an American drama television series created by David E. Kelley and based on the 2014 novel of the same name by Liane Moriarty. Originally conceived as a limited series, it premiered on HBO on 19 February 2017 and concluded its first season that April; following its success, a second season aired in 2019.

Plot and cast

Set in Monterey, California, the series follows five women — Madeline Mackenzie (Reese Witherspoon), Celeste Wright (Nicole Kidman), Jane Chapman (Shailene Woodley), Renata Klein (Laura Dern) and Bonnie Carlson (Zoë Kravitz) — whose lives unravel around a murder at a school fundraiser, revealed only in the finale. Flashbacks and police-interview fragments frame the story, which explores domestic abuse, sexual assault, friendship and class privilege. Alexander Skarsgård plays Celeste's abusive husband Perry, and Meryl Streep joined the second season as Perry's mother, Mary Louise.

Reception

The first season was directed by Jean-Marc Vallée and won widespread critical acclaim, earning eight Primetime Emmy Awards including Outstanding Limited Series, with acting awards for Kidman, Dern and Skarsgård, as well as four Golden Globes. The second season, directed by Andrea Arnold, received generally positive reviews, though less enthusiastic than the first. A third season has been reported to be in development.
