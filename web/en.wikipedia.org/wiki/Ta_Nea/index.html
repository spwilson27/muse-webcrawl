Ta Nea

Ta Nea (Greek: Τα Νέα, "The News") is a daily newspaper published in Athens, Greece. Founded in 1931 under the name Athenian News, it adopted its current name in 1945.

Overview

Long one of Greece's best-selling newspapers, Ta Nea is associated with the centre-left of Greek politics and was historically published by the Lambrakis Press Group. It continues in print and digital form.
