Bane is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Chuck Dixon and artist Graham Nolan, he made his first appearance in Batman: Vengeance of Bane #1 (cover-dated January 1993). Bane is usually depicted as a brilliant criminal mastermind and formidable physical opponent of the superhero Batman, and is best known for breaking the hero's back in the "Knightfall" storyline of 1993–94.

Fictional character biography

Born in the fictional Caribbean prison of Peña Duro on the island of Santa Prisca, Bane served a life sentence for his father's crimes. In prison he educated himself, becoming exceptionally well read and physically powerful. Subjected to experiments with the super-steroid Venom, which he later received through tubes feeding directly into his body, he escaped and travelled to Gotham City, intent on destroying Batman. In the "Knightfall" arc he systematically wore Batman down by freeing the inmates of Arkham Asylum, then defeated him in combat and broke his spine over his knee.

In other media

Bane was portrayed by Tom Hardy in Christopher Nolan's film The Dark Knight Rises (2012), and appeared in Batman & Robin (1997) and in numerous animated series and video games, including the Batman: Arkham series.
