The People's Liberation Army Marine Corps (PLAMC; Chinese: 中国人民解放军海军陆战队) is the marine corps of China's People's Liberation Army Navy, responsible for amphibious operations, expeditionary warfare, and acting as a rapid reaction force.

History

First organised in the 1950s, the marine corps was disbanded and re-formed over the decades; a PLA Marine Corps was formally re-established in 1979. Historically it consisted of two brigades stationed in the South Sea Fleet area, oriented toward South China Sea garrisons and a possible Taiwan contingency.

Expansion

Beginning in 2017 the corps expanded dramatically, reorganising ground-force units into additional marine brigades and adding aviation, artillery, and special operations elements. Marines have deployed to China's support base in Djibouti and on anti-piracy patrols in the Gulf of Aden. They operate amphibious vehicles such as the ZBD-05 family and deploy from Type 071 amphibious transport docks and Type 075 landing helicopter docks.
