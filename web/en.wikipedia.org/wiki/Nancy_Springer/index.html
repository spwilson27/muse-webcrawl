Nancy Springer

Nancy Springer (born 1948) is an American author of fantasy, science fiction and young adult literature. She has written more than fifty books over a career spanning five decades.

Works

Springer is best known for the Enola Holmes mysteries, about the younger sister of Sherlock Holmes, which began with The Case of the Missing Marquess (2006) and were adapted into popular films. Her earlier work includes the Book of Isle fantasy series and the Rowan Hood books.

Awards

Her novel Larque on the Wing won the Tiptree Award, and her work has received the Edgar Award and multiple Mythopoeic and children's-book honours.
