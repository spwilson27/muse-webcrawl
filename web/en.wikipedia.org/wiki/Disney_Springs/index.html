Disney Springs

Disney Springs is an outdoor shopping, dining, and entertainment complex at the Walt Disney World Resort in Bay Lake and Lake Buena Vista, Florida, near Orlando. The complex opened on March 22, 1975, and has been expanded and renamed over the years; it was renamed Disney Springs in 2015 following a major expansion project.

History

The complex began as the Lake Buena Vista Shopping Village in 1975. It later became the Disney Village Marketplace and, in 1997, was rebranded as Downtown Disney together with the addition of a West Side district. A multi-year transformation announced in 2013 doubled the number of venues and gave the complex a backstory as a turn-of-the-century Florida waterfront town; it reopened as Disney Springs in September 2015.

Districts

Disney Springs is divided into four themed districts: the Marketplace, The Landing, the West Side, and the Town Center. Together they contain more than 150 shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues, including the World of Disney store and a long-running Cirque du Soleil production.

Transportation

Admission and parking are free. The complex is served by Disney Transport buses, watercraft connecting to several resort hotels, and dedicated parking garages along Buena Vista Drive.
