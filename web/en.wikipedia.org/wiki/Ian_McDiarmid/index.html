Ian McDiarmid (born 11 August 1944) is a Scottish actor and director of stage and screen. He is best known worldwide for portraying Emperor Palpatine in the Star Wars film series, a role he first played in Return of the Jedi (1983) and reprised in the prequel trilogy and The Rise of Skywalker (2019).

Early life

McDiarmid was born in Carnoustie, Angus, Scotland. He studied psychology at the University of St Andrews before training in acting at the Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama in Glasgow, where he won the gold medal for his work.

Career

McDiarmid built a distinguished stage career, becoming joint artistic director of the Almeida Theatre in London with Jonathan Kent. His theatre work earned him Olivier and Tony Awards. On screen, besides Star Wars, he has appeared in films such as Dirty Rotten Scoundrels (1988), Restoration (1995) and Sleepy Hollow (1999), and played Denis Thatcher in the television film Margaret.

Palpatine

Cast at age 38 to play the elderly Emperor in Return of the Jedi, McDiarmid returned two decades later to play the character's younger self, Senator and Chancellor Palpatine, in the prequel trilogy (1999–2005). His performance across the saga is widely regarded as one of its highlights.
