North Greenwich

North Greenwich is a district of south-east London, England, on the Greenwich Peninsula in the Royal Borough of Greenwich. Once dominated by gasworks and industry, it was transformed from the late 1990s by the Millennium Dome (now The O2 arena) and the associated Jubilee line station.

North Greenwich
Location: Greenwich Peninsula, London
Known for: The O2, Millennium Dome
Station: North Greenwich (Jubilee line)

History

The peninsula's East Greenwich Gas Works, once among the largest in Europe, closed in the 1980s leaving heavily contaminated but prime riverside land. The site was chosen for the Millennium Dome, opened for the year 2000; after a troubled start the Dome reopened in 2007 as The O2, one of the world's busiest music venues.

Today

North Greenwich station, designed by Will Alsop, opened in 1999 on the Jubilee line extension. Surrounding development has added thousands of homes, Ravensbourne University London, the Emirates Air Line cable car across the Thames, and the IFS Cloud Cable Car, with further phases of the Greenwich Peninsula masterplan continuing.
