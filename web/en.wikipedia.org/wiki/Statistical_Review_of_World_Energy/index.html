Statistical Review of World Energy

The Statistical Review of World Energy is an annual publication providing comprehensive statistics on global energy production, consumption, trade, reserves, and prices. It is one of the most widely used sources of energy data in the world.

History

The review was first published by BP in 1952. For over seventy years it was produced by BP's economics team; its data collected and published on oil was considered the industry standard, sometimes nicknamed the "bible of the energy industry". In 2023, stewardship of the Review passed to the Energy Institute, the UK-based professional body for the energy sector, which now publishes it in collaboration with KPMG and Kearney.

Contents

The Review covers oil, natural gas, coal, nuclear power, hydroelectricity, and renewable energy, including solar, wind, geothermal, and biofuels, as well as key minerals and carbon emissions. It reports data for individual countries and regions, typically going back to 1965, and is accompanied by a freely available dataset that is extensively cited by governments, academics, journalists, and analysts.
