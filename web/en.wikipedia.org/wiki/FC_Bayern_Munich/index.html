Fußball-Club Bayern München e. V. (FCB), commonly known as FC Bayern Munich or simply Bayern, is a German professional sports club based in Munich, Bavaria. It is best known for its men's professional football team, which plays in the Bundesliga, the top tier of the German football league system. Bayern is the most successful club in German football, having won a record 33 national titles, including eleven consecutive league titles from 2013 to 2023, and 20 national cups.

Founded in 1900 by eleven players led by Franz John, Bayern won its first national championship in 1932. The club had its period of greatest success in the mid-1970s when, under the captaincy of Franz Beckenbauer, it won the European Cup three consecutive times (1974–1976). Overall, Bayern has won six European Cup/UEFA Champions League titles, including a treble in 2013 and a second treble in 2020.

History

Early years (1900–1965)

FC Bayern Munich was founded within the Gymnastics Club Munich (MTV 1879) by members who were not allowed to join the German Football Association (DFB) by the club's leadership. On 27 February 1900, eleven young men led by Franz John split from the MTV to form Bayern. The club won its first German championship in 1932, defeating Eintracht Frankfurt 2–0 in the final in Nuremberg.

The golden years (1965–1979)

Bayern was promoted to the newly formed Bundesliga in 1965. Around young stars Franz Beckenbauer, Gerd Müller and Sepp Maier, the team became a national powerhouse. Bayern won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1967 and then three consecutive European Cups from 1974 to 1976, the last under captain Beckenbauer. In 1976 the club also won the Intercontinental Cup against Cruzeiro.

Sustained success and the modern era

After a comparatively leaner period in the early 1980s and 1990s punctuated by league titles, Bayern won the UEFA Cup in 1996 and the Champions League in 2001 under Ottmar Hitzfeld. In 2013, under Jupp Heynckes, Bayern became the first German club to win the treble of league, cup and Champions League. In 2020, under Hansi Flick, the club won a second treble, including an 8–2 quarter-final victory over Barcelona, and completed a sextuple by winning the FIFA Club World Cup.

Stadium

Bayern played at the Olympiastadion from 1972 until 2005, when the club moved to the newly built Allianz Arena, famous for its illuminated exterior panels that glow red when Bayern plays at home. The arena has a capacity of about 75,000 spectators for domestic matches.

Rivalries

Bayern's main national rivalry is with Borussia Dortmund, a fixture known as Der Klassiker. The Munich derby against TSV 1860 Munich has been contested less often since 1860's decline. Internationally, Bayern has historic rivalries with Real Madrid and Manchester United, the latter stemming from the dramatic 1999 Champions League final in Barcelona, which United won with two stoppage-time goals.
