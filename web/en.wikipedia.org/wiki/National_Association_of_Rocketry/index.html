National Association of Rocketry

Founded: 1957
Founder: G. Harry Stine
Type: Non-profit organisation
Focus: Model rocketry, hobby rocketry

The National Association of Rocketry (NAR) is a non-profit organisation in the United States dedicated to hobby rocketry. Founded in 1957 by G. Harry Stine, it is the oldest and largest organisation of its kind in the world.

Activities

The NAR promotes safe model rocketry through its safety code, which became the basis for industry standards and National Fire Protection Association codes. It sanctions competitions, certifies hobbyists for high-power rocketry in three levels, provides insurance for launches, and supports educational programmes including the Team America Rocketry Challenge (TARC) for school students.

Publications and events

The association publishes the magazine Sport Rocketry and holds the annual NARAM launch and competition. Local chapters, called sections, organise launches across the United States.
