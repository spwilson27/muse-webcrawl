2023 Pacific hurricane season

The 2023 Pacific hurricane season was an above-average season in the eastern and central Pacific Ocean, influenced by a strong El Niño. The season officially ran from 15 May to 30 November 2023 in the eastern Pacific (and from 1 June in the central Pacific). It is remembered above all for Hurricane Otis, which underwent explosive rapid intensification and devastated Acapulco, Mexico, in October.

Notable storms

Hurricane Hilary (August) brought rare tropical storm conditions to Southern California — the first tropical storm warning ever issued for the state — causing flooding and mudslides. Hurricane Dora passed south of Hawaii; its strong pressure gradient with a high-pressure system to the north contributed to the fierce winds that fanned the deadly Maui wildfires of August 2023, though Dora itself stayed far offshore. Hurricane Jova became a Category 5 storm in September but remained at sea.

Hurricane Otis was the season's defining disaster. Forming on 22 October, it was forecast to reach landfall as a tropical storm; instead it intensified by over 100 mph in 24 hours and struck Acapulco just after midnight on 25 October as a Category 5 hurricane with 165 mph winds — the strongest landfall on Mexico's Pacific coast on record. At least 50 people were killed and damage was estimated in the billions of dollars; the forecast failure prompted major scrutiny of rapid-intensification prediction.
