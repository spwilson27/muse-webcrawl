League: NFL, AFC East

The New York Jets are a professional American football team in the National Football League, playing in the AFC East division. Founded in 1960 as the Titans of New York, a charter member of the American Football League, the team took the name Jets in 1963. It shares MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, with the New York Giants, and its training facility is in Florham Park.

Super Bowl III

The Jets' defining triumph came on 12 January 1969, when, led by quarterback Joe Namath and coach Weeb Ewbank, they defeated the heavily favoured Baltimore Colts 16–7 in Super Bowl III. Namath had "guaranteed" the victory beforehand. The upset legitimized the AFL and remains one of the most famous results in American sports; the Jets have not returned to a Super Bowl since.

Later history

The team has had intermittent success, reaching the AFC Championship Game in 1982, 1998, and back-to-back seasons in 2009 and 2010 under coach Rex Ryan, but has endured one of the longest playoff droughts in the NFL since 2010. Notable players include Namath, Don Maynard, Curtis Martin, Darrelle Revis and Joe Klecko of the "New York Sack Exchange".
