Blackwater River (Virginia)

Location: Virginia, United States
Length: about 105 miles (169 km)
Mouth: Chowan River

The Blackwater River is a river in southeastern Virginia in the United States, roughly 105 miles (169 km) long. It rises near Petersburg and flows generally southeast through the cities of Franklin and Suffolk's rural hinterland.

Course

The river flows past Franklin and joins the Nottoway River at the North Carolina state line to form the Chowan River, which empties into Albemarle Sound. Its dark, tannin-stained water, typical of coastal-plain "blackwater" streams, gives the river its name.

History

The Blackwater formed part of the boundary in early colonial land grants and served as a transportation corridor for timber and naval stores. Swamps along its banks harbour bald cypress and tupelo gum trees, some of great age.
