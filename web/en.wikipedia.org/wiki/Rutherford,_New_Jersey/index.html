Rutherford is a borough in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States, located about 13 kilometres west of Midtown Manhattan. It is a suburban community in the New York metropolitan area, served by NJ Transit's Bergen County Line at Rutherford station, and was incorporated in 1881 from portions of Union Township.

History

The area, once part of the lands of the Lenape and later of the New Barbadoes Neck, developed in the 19th century as a residential community, aided by the Erie Railroad. Rutherford styles itself the "Borough of Trees" for its leafy streets and the "First Borough of Bergen County" as the first municipality formed under the 1878 borough act in the county.

Notable people

The poet and physician William Carlos Williams, a leading figure of American modernism, lived and practised medicine in Rutherford for most of his life, at 9 Ridge Road. The borough is also home to part of the Felician University campus and lies adjacent to the Meadowlands Sports Complex in neighbouring East Rutherford.
