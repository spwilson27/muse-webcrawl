Charles III (Charles Philip Arthur George; born 14 November 1948) is King of the United Kingdom and the 14 other Commonwealth realms. He acceded to the throne on 8 September 2022 upon the death of his mother, Elizabeth II, having been the longest-serving heir apparent and Prince of Wales in British history.

Early life and career

Born at Buckingham Palace, Charles was educated at Cheam and Gordonstoun schools and read archaeology, anthropology, and history at Trinity College, Cambridge. He served in the Royal Air Force and Royal Navy from 1971 to 1976. In 1981 he married Lady Diana Spencer; they had two sons, William and Harry, before separating in 1992 and divorcing in 1996. Diana died in a car crash in Paris in 1997. In 2005 Charles married Camilla Parker Bowles, now Queen Camilla.

Reign

Charles was crowned at Westminster Abbey on 6 May 2023. As Prince of Wales he founded the Prince's Trust (1976), championed environmental causes, organic farming, and architecture, and his reign has continued his focus on sustainability, interfaith dialogue, and community. In early 2024 he was diagnosed with a form of cancer and temporarily withdrew from public-facing duties while undergoing treatment.
