Oerlikon Contraves

Oerlikon Contraves was a Swiss anti-aircraft artillery manufacturer famous for its Oerlikon 20 mm cannon. It became part of Rheinmetall Air Defence. The firm originated in Werkzeugmaschinenfabrik Oerlikon, founded in 1906 in Oerlikon, a suburb of Zurich.

The Oerlikon 20 mm cannon series, developed in the 1920s from the Becker cannon, was widely used by many navies and air forces during the Second World War and remains in service in updated forms. Later products included the Oerlikon 35 mm twin cannon and the Skyguard / Skyshield air defence systems.
