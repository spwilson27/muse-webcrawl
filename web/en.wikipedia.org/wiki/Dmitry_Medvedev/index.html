Dmitry Medvedev

Dmitry Anatolyevich Medvedev (born 14 September 1965) is a Russian politician who served as President of Russia from 2008 to 2012 and Prime Minister from 2012 to 2020. He has served as deputy chairman of the Security Council of Russia since 2020 and chairman of the United Russia party since 2012.

Early life and career

Born in Leningrad (now Saint Petersburg), Medvedev studied law at Leningrad State University, taught law, and worked with Anatoly Sobchak's city administration alongside Vladimir Putin. He moved to Moscow in 1999, chaired Gazprom's board, and became Putin's chief of staff and then First Deputy Prime Minister overseeing national priority projects.

Presidency

Elected with Putin's endorsement in March 2008, Medvedev presented a modernisation and anti-corruption agenda, the Skolkovo innovation centre, and a "reset" with the United States (including New START, 2010). His term included the 2008 Russo-Georgian War. In 2011 he announced he would not seek a second term; Putin returned to the presidency in 2012 and appointed him prime minister — the "castling" arrangement that sparked protests.

Later career

Medvedev resigned as prime minister in January 2020 and became deputy chairman of the Security Council. Since Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine he has become known for hawkish, threatening social-media statements toward the West.
