History of Libya under Muammar Gaddafi

Period: 1969–2011

Leader: Muammar Gaddafi

Systems: Libyan Arab Republic (1969–1977); Great Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriya (1977–2011)

Muammar Gaddafi ruled Libya from the 1969 coup that deposed King Idris until his death in the 2011 civil war — over 42 years.

Rise and early rule

Gaddafi and fellow Free Officers seized power on 1 September 1969. Oil revenues funded welfare, literacy and infrastructure programmes, while foreign companies were nationalised and dissent suppressed.

Jamahiriya

In 1977 Gaddafi proclaimed the Jamahiriya, governed in theory by people's congresses under his Third International Theory. In practice he retained supreme authority.

Foreign policy and isolation

Libya supported armed groups abroad, clashed with the United States (including the 1986 US bombing), fought Chad, and was sanctioned over the 1988 Lockerbie bombing. In the 2000s Gaddafi renounced weapons of mass destruction and sanctions were lifted.

Fall

In 2011, amid the Arab Spring, protests escalated into civil war; NATO intervened, Tripoli fell in August, and Gaddafi was captured and killed in Sirte on 20 October 2011.
