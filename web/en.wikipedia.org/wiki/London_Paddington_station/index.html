London Paddington is a major London railway and Underground station complex, located in Paddington in the City of Westminster. It has been the London terminus of the Great Western Railway and its successors since 1838. The main-line station was designed by Isambard Kingdom Brunel and opened in 1854, with a triple-arched glazed train shed built in association with Matthew Digby Wyatt; it is a Grade I listed building.

Services

Great Western Railway operates long-distance services to the West of England, South Wales, and the Thames Valley, together with the Night Riviera sleeper to Penzance. The Heathrow Express runs non-stop to Heathrow Airport. London Underground services are provided on the Bakerloo, Circle, District and Hammersmith & City lines, and since 2022 the station has also been served by the Elizabeth line.

Cultural references

The station is famous as the place where Paddington Bear, the fictional character created by Michael Bond, was found by the Brown family, wearing a tag reading "Please look after this bear"; a statue of the character stands on the concourse.
