Willis Towers Watson plc, trading as WTW, is a British-American multinational insurance advisory, broking and risk management firm. It was formed on 5 January 2016 by the merger of the London-based insurance broker Willis Group Holdings and the American professional services firm Towers Watson, in a transaction valued at roughly $18 billion.

History of the predecessors

Willis traced its origins to Henry Willis & Co., founded in London in 1828 and long centred on marine insurance at Lloyd's; it grew into one of the world's largest insurance brokers. Towers Watson was itself created in 2010 by the merger of Towers Perrin — descended from an 1871 Philadelphia firm — and Watson Wyatt, whose roots lay in the British actuarial practice founded by R. Watson & Sons in 1878. The combined company kept the Willis Building and the Willis Tower naming rights among its inherited assets.

Business

WTW advises clients in more than 140 countries on people, risk and capital: employee benefits and retirement plans, talent and rewards consulting, corporate risk and insurance broking, reinsurance, and investment advisory services. In 2020 the firm agreed to be acquired by rival Aon for about $30 billion, but the deal was abandoned in July 2021 after the U.S. Department of Justice sued to block it on competition grounds.
