Eagles

Eagles are an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1971. With five number-one singles and six number-one albums, six Grammy Awards and five American Music Awards, the Eagles were one of the most successful musical acts of the 1970s in North America and are one of the world's best-selling bands, having sold more than 200 million records worldwide. Founding members Glenn Frey (guitars, vocals), Don Henley (drums, vocals), Bernie Leadon (guitars, vocals) and Randy Meisner (bass guitar, vocals) were recruited by Linda Ronstadt as band members; they later secured a record deal with David Geffen's Asylum Records.


The Eagles performing during their Long Road Out of Eden Tour

Their debut album, Eagles (1972), spawned two top-20 singles in the US and Canada: "Take It Easy" and "Witchy Woman". The following year's album, Desperado, peaked at only number 41 in the US, although the title song became a concert staple. In 1974, guitarist Don Felder joined, and On the Border produced the top-40 hit "Already Gone" and the band's first number-one single in the US and Canada, "Best of My Love". It was the band's first album to be certified platinum, following which Leadon left the band due to creative tensions. He was replaced by Joe Walsh.

One of These Nights (1975) included three top-10 singles: "One of These Nights", "Lyin' Eyes" and "Take It to the Limit", the last bringing Meisner his only lead vocal on an Eagles single. In 1975 the band embarked on a world tour to promote the album. Their next album, Hotel California (1976), is one of the best-selling albums of all time; the title track and "New Kid in Town" both topped the Billboard Hot 100. After the release of Hotel California, Meisner left the band and was replaced by Timothy B. Schmit. After the release of The Long Run (1979) in 1979, the band broke up in 1980.

Reunions

The Eagles re-formed in 1994 for the album Hell Freezes Over, a mixture of live and new studio tracks, and toured consistently. In 2007, the Eagles released Long Road Out of Eden, their sixth number-one album in the US. The band was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1998. Following the death of Glenn Frey in 2016, the Eagles re-formed in 2017 with Glenn's son Deacon Frey and Vince Gill sharing lead vocals for Frey's songs.
