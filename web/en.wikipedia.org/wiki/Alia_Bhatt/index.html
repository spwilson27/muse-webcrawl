Alia Bhatt (born 15 March 1993) is a British-born Indian actress who works in Hindi films. One of India's highest-paid actresses, she has received numerous accolades including a National Film Award and six Filmfare Awards. She has appeared in Forbes India's Celebrity 100 list since 2014 and was featured by Forbes Asia in its 30 Under 30 list of 2017.

Early life

Bhatt was born in Bombay (now Mumbai) to filmmaker Mahesh Bhatt and actress Soni Razdan. Her father is of Gujarati descent and her mother is of Kashmiri Pandit and German heritage; she holds British citizenship. She has an elder sister, Shaheen, and two half-siblings, Pooja and Rahul Bhatt. She was educated at the Jamnabai Narsee School, and made a brief childhood appearance in the 1999 thriller Sangharsh.

Career

Bhatt's first leading role was in Karan Johar's teen film Student of the Year (2012). She won acclaim for playing a kidnapping victim in the road drama Highway (2014), and established herself with starring roles in the romances 2 States (2014), Humpty Sharma Ki Dulhania (2014) and Badrinath Ki Dulhania (2017). She won the Filmfare Award for Best Actress for playing a Bihari migrant in the crime drama Udta Punjab (2016), a spy in Raazi (2018), and the possessive girlfriend in Gully Boy (2019).

Her portrayal of the title role in Sanjay Leela Bhansali's Gangubai Kathiawadi (2022) won her the National Film Award for Best Actress and a fifth Filmfare Award. She expanded into production with the dark comedy Darlings (2022) under her company Eternal Sunshine Productions, and made her Hollywood debut in the Netflix spy film Heart of Stone (2023). The romantic comedy Rocky Aur Rani Kii Prem Kahaani (2023) brought her a record-tying fifth Filmfare Best Actress win.

Other work and personal life

Bhatt launched her own clothing label, Ed-a-Mamma, in 2020, and supports charitable and environmental causes. She married actor Ranbir Kapoor in April 2022; their daughter Raha was born in November of that year.
