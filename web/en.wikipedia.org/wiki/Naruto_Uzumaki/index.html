Naruto Uzumaki (Japanese: うずまき ナルト) is the titular protagonist of the manga and anime series Naruto, created by Masashi Kishimoto. A young ninja from the fictional village of Konohagakure, Naruto is the jinchūriki of the Nine-Tailed Demon Fox (Kurama), a beast that attacked his village shortly before his birth. Shunned by the villagers during his childhood, he grows up determined to win their respect and become Hokage, the leader of the village.

Creation and conception

Kishimoto first published a one-shot version of Naruto in 1997. In developing the series, he drew on his own childhood feelings of loneliness and desire for recognition. Naruto's signature orange clothing, love of ramen, and catchphrase ("Believe it!", Japanese dattebayo) became defining traits. His surname, Uzumaki (meaning "spiral" or "whirlpool"), refers both to a ramen topping (narutomaki) and to the spiral motif used throughout his design.

Character biography

Naruto is the son of Minato Namikaze, the Fourth Hokage, and Kushina Uzumaki, who sealed the Nine-Tails into him as a newborn at the cost of their lives. As a genin he is placed on Team 7 under Kakashi Hatake, alongside Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno. Under the tutelage of the legendary sannin Jiraiya, he masters techniques such as the Rasengan and Sage Mode. During the Fourth Great Ninja War he befriends Kurama, unites the tailed beasts' power, and helps defeat Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. As an adult, in the sequel series Boruto, Naruto has achieved his dream and serves as the Seventh Hokage, married to Hinata Hyuga with two children, Boruto and Himawari.

Abilities

  • Shadow Clone Technique — creates tangible copies of himself, used for both combat and accelerated training.
  • Rasengan — a spinning sphere of chakra developed by his father; Naruto creates variants including the Rasenshuriken.
  • Sage Mode — draws on natural energy learned from the toads of Mount Myōboku.
  • Nine-Tails Chakra Mode — after befriending Kurama, Naruto can fully access the fox's immense chakra reserves.

Reception

Naruto has consistently ranked among the most popular characters in Shōnen Jump popularity polls and is regarded as one of the most recognisable anime characters worldwide. Critics have praised his development from an unruly prankster into a mature leader, and his rivalry with Sasuke Uchiha is frequently cited as one of the defining relationships in shōnen manga.
