Robyn Charles Miller (born August 6, 1966) is an American video game designer and composer. He co-founded the studio Cyan with his brother Rand Miller and co-created the bestselling adventure game Myst (1993), for which he also composed the music, and its sequel Riven (1997).

Robyn Miller, co-creator of Myst.

Career

Miller and his brother began making games in the late 1980s, including The Manhole and Cosmic Osmo and the Worlds Beyond the Mackerel. Myst, released in 1993, became the best-selling PC game of its era, praised for its photorealistic pre-rendered environments and its ambient, atmospheric score, composed by Miller. He reprised design, art direction and composition duties on Riven.

Miller left Cyan in the late 1990s, after the release of Riven, pursuing interests in film and independent projects. He later formed a small production company, and in 2013 released The Immortal Augustus Gladstone, a mockumentary-style film project. He has also written a novel, The Book of Atrus having been a collaboration within the family that he contributed to as part of the Myst franchise's expanded fiction.

Music

Miller's soundtracks for Myst (1995) and Riven (1998) were released as albums and are regarded as landmarks in video game music, blending synthesizer textures with world-music instrumentation.
