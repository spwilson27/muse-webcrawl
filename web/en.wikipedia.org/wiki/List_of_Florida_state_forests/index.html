List of Florida state forests

The U.S. state of Florida has over 30 state forests managed by the Florida Forest Service, a division of the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services. The state forest system covers more than one million acres and is managed for timber, wildlife habitat, recreation and water resources.
