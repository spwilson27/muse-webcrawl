Attilan

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Attilan is a fictional city in the Marvel Universe, the home of the Inhumans, a race of superhumans created through Kree experimentation on early humans. It first appeared in Fantastic Four #47 (1966), created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. For most of its history the city was hidden from humanity, protected by advanced technology and its remote locations.

History

Attilan was founded thousands of years ago on an island in the North Atlantic. As human civilisation advanced, the Inhumans repeatedly relocated their city to avoid discovery: first to the Himalayas, then — when air pollution threatened their health — to the Blue Area of the Moon, an ancient Kree outpost with a breathable atmosphere. The city is ruled by the Inhuman Royal Family, headed by the silent king Black Bolt and Queen Medusa, and its society was traditionally organised by the Genetics Council around the superhuman powers each citizen gains through exposure to the Terrigen Mists.

In other media

Attilan, situated on the Moon, is the principal setting of the 2017 television series Marvel's Inhumans, and appears in animated series and video games featuring the Inhumans.
