Beachy Head Lighthouse

Beachy Head Lighthouse
Location: East Sussex, England
Built: 1902
Height: 33 m (108 ft)
Operator: Trinity House

Beachy Head Lighthouse is a lighthouse in the English Channel below the chalk cliffs of Beachy Head in East Sussex, England. It was built in 1902 to improve on the earlier Belle Tout lighthouse, which was often obscured by mist.

History

Constructed from granite at the foot of the cliffs, the tower's red and white bands were painted to make it visible by day. The light was automated in 1983.

Characteristics

The light has a range of about 26 nautical miles and flashes twice every 20 seconds.

Access

The lighthouse stands in the sea at low tide near the Seven Sisters cliffs and is a well-known coastal landmark.
