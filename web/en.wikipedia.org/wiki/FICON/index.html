FICON

DeveloperIBM
Introduced1998
TypeMainframe channel I/O interface

FICON (Fibre Connection) is a fibre-optic channel technology developed by IBM for connecting mainframe computers to storage devices and other peripherals. Announced in 1998, it replaced the older ESCON system, offering much higher data rates and longer distances.

Technology

FICON adapts the Fibre Channel physical and signalling standard to carry the mainframe channel protocol defined by ANSI FC-SB specifications. Successive generations raised throughput from an initial 100 MB/s through 200, 400 and 800 MB/s classes, while supporting distances of tens of kilometres between systems and storage.

Use

FICON remains the standard high-performance I/O interface on IBM Z mainframes, linking processors to enterprise disk arrays, tape systems and channel extension equipment in large data centres.
