Indira Gandhi National Forest Academy

The Indira Gandhi National Forest Academy (IGNFA) is a forestry training institute in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India, under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. It imparts professional training to officers of the Indian Forest Service (IFS), one of the three All India Services.

History

The academy's lineage traces to the Imperial Forest School established in Dehradun in 1878 and the Imperial Forest College of 1906, located on the campus of the Forest Research Institute. The institution was revamped as the Indian Forest College in 1938, renamed the Indira Gandhi National Forest Academy in 1987, and given expanded responsibilities for in-service training of senior forest officers.

Training

Probationary IFS officers undergo a foundational professional course of about two years covering silviculture, forest management, wildlife, soil conservation, forest law, and related subjects, supplemented by field tours across India. The academy also runs mid-career training programmes and refresher courses.
