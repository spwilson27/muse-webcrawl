Twistor theory

Twistor theory is a mathematical and physical framework proposed by the physicist Roger Penrose in 1967, originally intended as a new approach to quantum gravity. Instead of treating the points of spacetime as fundamental and fields as living on them, twistor theory takes as basic the geometry of light rays, encoded in a complex three-dimensional space — twistor space. Penrose's aim was to unite general relativity and quantum mechanics by building spacetime itself out of more primitive, quantum-mechanical, and conformal ingredients: since twistors are naturally associated with spinors and complex numbers, quantum behaviour might be fundamental rather than imposed.

Background and mathematical development

Twistor theory grew out of Penrose's work on spinor methods in relativity. Its core mathematical tools — the twistor correspondence, the Penrose transform, and the nonlinear graviton construction (1976) — showed that self-dual gravitational and gauge fields correspond to elegant objects in complex geometry. Michael Atiyah and collaborators developed these ideas, contributing to the ADHM construction of instantons and to integrable systems. A long-standing obstacle was incorporating fields that are not self-dual and describing massive particles and interacting gravity; "googly" problems (encoding left- and right-handed fields symmetrically) and the absence of a full twistor action for general relativity remained open for decades, with proposals such as twistor actions for gravity developed in the 2010s.

Impact

Although twistor theory has not (yet) yielded a complete theory of quantum gravity, its mathematical offshoots have been remarkably fruitful. Edward Witten's 2003 twistor string theory launched a revolution in scattering-amplitude methods, leading to modern techniques (BCFW recursion, Grassmannian and amplituhedron formulations) used to compute particle interactions. Twistor geometry also feeds into Penrose's conformal cyclic cosmology, in which the conformal structure of spacetime — the part twistors see — links successive cosmic aeons.
