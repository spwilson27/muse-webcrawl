Diamond League

Diamond League
Sport: Athletics (track and field)
Founded: 2010
Organiser: World Athletics
Predecessor: IAAF Golden League

The Diamond League is an annual series of elite track and field athletic competitions comprising fourteen of the best invitational athletics meetings. The series sits in the top tier of World Athletics' one-day meeting competitions.

History

The Diamond League was launched in 2010 as the successor to the IAAF Golden League, which had run from 1998 to 2009. It was designed to expand elite one-day athletics beyond Europe, with meetings in Asia, the Middle East and the United States, and to feature a season-long points race in each event discipline. Meetings have included the Weltklasse Zürich, Memorial Van Damme in Brussels, Herculis in Monaco, the Prefontaine Classic in Eugene, and the London Grand Prix.

Format

Athletes earn points at series meetings according to their finishing position; the leading athletes in each discipline qualify for a final, in which the Diamond League champion of that event is decided. Champions receive the Diamond Trophy and prize money. The format has been revised several times since 2010, including changes to the number of scoring disciplines and the finals structure.
