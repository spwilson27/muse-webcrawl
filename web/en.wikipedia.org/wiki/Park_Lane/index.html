Park Lane

Park Lane is a major road in the City of Westminster, London, England. It runs from Hyde Park Corner northwards to Marble Arch, forming the eastern boundary of Hyde Park. Despite being one of London's busiest thoroughfares, it is famed for its grand hotels and some of the most expensive property in the world.

History

Originally a country lane along the edge of Hyde Park, Park Lane became lined with aristocratic mansions in the eighteenth century; Apsley House, the home of the Duke of Wellington, stands at its southern end. The road was widened in the 1960s and became a dual carriageway.

Landmarks

Hotels on Park Lane include the Dorchester, the Grosvenor House Hotel, and the London Hilton. The road is also one of the properties on the standard British Monopoly board, paired with Mayfair as the most expensive set.
