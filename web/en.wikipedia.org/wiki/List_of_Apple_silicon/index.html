List of Apple silicon

Apple silicon refers to the series of system-on-a-chip (SoC) and system-in-a-package (SiP) processors designed by Apple Inc., primarily using the ARM architecture. They power the iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV, HomePod and, since 2020, Mac computers. This article lists the principal chips by series.

Other series

The S series powers Apple Watch (S1–S10); the H series (H1, H2) powers AirPods and related audio products; the W, U (Ultra Wideband U1/U2) and T series (T1, T2 security chips) serve auxiliary roles; the R1 powers the Vision Pro headset alongside an M-series chip.
