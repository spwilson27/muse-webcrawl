The Lesbian Avengers was a grassroots direct action group formed in 1992 in New York City by six lesbians who were frustrated with the invisibility of lesbian issues in mainstream media and politics. Inspired by groups such as ACT UP and Queer Nation, the Avengers combined theatrical protest tactics with serious political organizing focused on lesbian visibility and what they called "lesbian survival" issues.

History

The group was founded by Sarah Schulman, Ana María Simo, Anne-christine d'Adesky, Anne Maguire, Marie Honan, and Maxine Wolfe. Their first action took place in September 1992, when they handed out balloons in front of a Queens elementary school whose curriculum had been attacked by conservative groups for acknowledging gay families. The balloons read "Ask About Lesbian Lives."

One of their best-known tactics was fire-eating, borrowed from circus performance, which they used to dramatize the violence of homophobic attacks — notably protests against anti-gay violence in which people were burned. Their motto played on the phrase "the fire will not consume us" and they became known for eating fire while chanting "The fire will not consume us."

Dyke March

In 1993 the Lesbian Avengers organized the first Dyke March in Washington, D.C., held the night before the March on Washington for Lesbian, Gay and Bi Equal Rights and Liberation, drawing an estimated 20,000 participants. Dyke Marches subsequently spread to cities across North America and internationally, and remain a fixture of Pride season in many cities.

Legacy

Chapters of the Lesbian Avengers formed in dozens of cities in the United States and abroad through the mid-1990s. The group produced a widely distributed organizing handbook encouraging the formation of autonomous local chapters. Although the original New York chapter declined in activity by the late 1990s, the Dyke March tradition and the group's direct-action style left a lasting mark on LGBTQ activism. The Lesbian Avenger Documentary Project later preserved the group's history in the film Lesbian Avengers Eat Fire, Too (1993).
