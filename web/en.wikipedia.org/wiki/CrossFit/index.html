CrossFit

Founded: 2000, Santa Cruz, California, US

Founder: Greg Glassman (with Lauren Jenai)

Type: Fitness training programme and brand

Headquarters: Washington, D.C., US

CrossFit is a branded fitness regimen and training methodology founded by Greg Glassman in 2000. It is promoted as both a physical exercise philosophy and a competitive fitness sport, practised in thousands of affiliated gyms ("boxes") worldwide. CrossFit workouts combine elements of high-intensity interval training, Olympic weightlifting, powerlifting, plyometrics, gymnastics, strongman and other exercises, performed as "constantly varied functional movements executed at high intensity".

Training

Affiliates program a daily Workout of the Day (WOD). Workouts are typically short and intense — from a few minutes to around half an hour — and may be scored by time or by repetitions completed in a fixed period (AMRAP). Signature benchmark workouts include "Fran" (thrusters and pull-ups), "Murph" (a one-mile run, 100 pull-ups, 200 push-ups, 300 squats and another mile run), and "Grace" (30 clean and jerks for time). Movements are scaled to each athlete's ability, and the group-class format and shared scores are credited with the programme's strong community culture.

CrossFit Games

The CrossFit Games, held annually since 2007, crown the "Fittest on Earth" through an open qualification process (the CrossFit Open) followed by semifinals and a multi-day final. Dominant champions have included Rich Froning Jr., Mat Fraser, Katrín Davíðsdóttir and Tia-Clair Toomey, the most successful individual athlete in the sport's history.

Criticism

CrossFit has been criticised by some exercise professionals over injury risk, the intensity of its programming for beginners, and isolated cases of exertional rhabdomyolysis, which the organisation acknowledges as a risk of extreme exertion. Studies have found injury rates broadly comparable to other recreational strength and conditioning activities. The company has also faced corporate and cultural controversies, including the departure of founder Greg Glassman in 2020.
