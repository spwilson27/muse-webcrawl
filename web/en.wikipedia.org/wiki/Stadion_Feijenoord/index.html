Stadion Feijenoord, better known by its nickname De Kuip ("The Tub"), is a football stadium in Rotterdam, Netherlands, home of Feyenoord. Opened in 1937, it is renowned for its intense atmosphere and steep stands close to the pitch.

History

The stadium was designed by Leendert van der Vlugt and inspired by contemporary stadiums such as Highbury. It has hosted numerous European finals, including the 2002 UEFA Cup Final, in which Feyenoord defeated Borussia Dortmund.

Concerts and events

De Kuip has also staged major concerts and was for many years a regular venue for the Netherlands national team. Long-running plans for a replacement stadium were shelved, and renovation of De Kuip has been pursued instead.
