FADEC

A full authority digital engine (or electronics) control (FADEC) is a system consisting of a digital computer, called an electronic engine controller (EEC) or engine control unit (ECU), and its related accessories that control all aspects of aircraft engine performance.

Operation

A true FADEC has full authority over engine operating parameters with no manual override available. It receives inputs such as air density, throttle position, engine temperatures, and pressures, and computes fuel flow, variable stator vane position, and bleed valve settings many times per second. FADECs reduce pilot workload, improve fuel efficiency, and protect the engine from exceeding its limits; they are standard on modern commercial and military jet aircraft.
