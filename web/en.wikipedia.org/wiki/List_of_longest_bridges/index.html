This is a list of the longest bridges in the world, ranked by total length. The majority of the longest bridges are in China, many of them railway viaducts carrying high-speed rail lines.

Other famous long bridges

  • Øresund Bridge — 7.8 km bridge (within a 16 km combined link) between Sweden and Denmark.
  • Vasco da Gama Bridge — 17.2 km, the longest bridge in the European Union, in Lisbon, Portugal.
  • Confederation Bridge — 12.9 km, joining Prince Edward Island to mainland Canada.
  • Chesapeake Bay Bridge–Tunnel — 28.3 km combined bridge-tunnel in Virginia.
