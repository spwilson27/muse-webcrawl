The Academy of Television Arts & Sciences, also known as the Television Academy, is a professional honorary organization dedicated to the advancement of the television industry in the United States. Founded in 1946, it is best known for presenting the Primetime Emmy Awards.

Activities

The Academy's membership is organized into peer groups representing different professions within television. Through its foundation, it runs educational programs including student internships and the College Television Awards. It also maintains the Television Academy Hall of Fame, founded in 1984.

Emmy Awards

The Emmy Awards were first presented in 1949. Responsibility for the various Emmy ceremonies is divided among the Television Academy (primetime and Los Angeles area awards), the National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences (daytime, sports and news), and the International Academy (international programming).
