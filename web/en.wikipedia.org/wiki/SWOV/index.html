SWOV (Dutch: Stichting Wetenschappelijk Onderzoek Verkeersveiligheid, "Foundation for Scientific Research into Traffic Safety") is the Dutch national scientific institute for road safety research, founded in 1962 and based in The Hague. Its mission is to use scientific research to contribute to improving road safety in the Netherlands and internationally.

Activities

SWOV conducts independent research into the causes of road crashes and the effectiveness of safety measures, covering road user behaviour, vehicle safety, infrastructure design, enforcement and policy. It maintains extensive crash and mobility databases, publishes fact sheets and research reports, and advises the Dutch Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management, municipalities, provinces and international bodies such as the European Commission and the OECD's International Transport Forum.

Sustainable Safety

SWOV researchers were central to the development of Sustainable Safety (Duurzaam Veilig), the influential Dutch vision launched in the 1990s that treats road safety as a property of the whole traffic system: roads should be designed so that their function, layout and use are aligned, conflicts between fast heavy traffic and vulnerable road users are prevented or made survivable, and human error is anticipated so that mistakes do not lead to death or serious injury. The approach is widely credited as part of the background to the Netherlands' strong road safety record and has influenced "Safe System" policies internationally.
