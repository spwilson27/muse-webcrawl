The Primera Nacional (officially, for sponsorship reasons, the Campeonato de Primera Nacional) is the second division of the Argentine football league system, administered by the Argentine Football Association (AFA). It was created in 1986 as the Primera B Nacional (commonly "Nacional B") to integrate clubs from outside the Buenos Aires metropolitan area into the national league structure, and was rebranded Primera Nacional in 2019.

Format

The league has typically comprised a large number of clubs — at times more than 35 — divided into zones. The champion (and usually the winner of a reduced playoff tournament, the Reducido) is promoted to the Primera División. Relegation is determined by points averages over multiple seasons, with clubs dropping either to the Primera B Metropolitana (for teams from the Buenos Aires area) or to the Torneo Federal A (for teams from the interior), reflecting Argentina's dual system of directly and indirectly affiliated clubs.

History

Before 1986, regional clubs reached the top flight mainly through the Nacional championship and regional leagues. The creation of Nacional B gave interior clubs a permanent second-tier competition. Over the years the division has hosted many of Argentina's most historic clubs during spells outside the top flight, and its large, nationwide membership and long travel distances make it one of the most geographically extensive second divisions in world football. Its size and format have changed frequently, with expansions in the 2010s and 2020s raising the number of participating teams substantially.
