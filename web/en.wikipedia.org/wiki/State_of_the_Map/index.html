State of the Map

Frequency: Annual (since 2007)
Organiser: OpenStreetMap Foundation and local teams
Focus: OpenStreetMap community, mapping technology, open geodata
First edition: Manchester, United Kingdom, 2007

State of the Map (SotM) is the annual international conference of the OpenStreetMap (OSM) community, organised under the auspices of the OpenStreetMap Foundation. First held in Manchester, England, in 2007, it brings together volunteer mappers, software developers, researchers, companies, governments and humanitarian organisations to share work on the free, editable map of the world. The conference typically runs for three days of talks, workshops, panels and social events, including an academic track for peer-reviewed research.

Editions

The conference has rotated among cities worldwide, including Amsterdam (2009), Denver (2011), Tokyo (2012), Buenos Aires (2014), Brussels (2016), Aizuwakamatsu (2017), Milan (2018), Heidelberg (2019) and Florence (2022); the 2020 and 2021 editions were held online because of the COVID-19 pandemic, and later editions adopted hybrid formats (Wrocław 2023, Nairobi 2024 — the first global SotM in Africa — and Manila scheduled for 2025). Bids are selected competitively by the Foundation's State of the Map organising committee, venues alternating between continents.

Regional and thematic offshoots

The success of the global conference inspired regional events such as State of the Map US, SotM Europe, SotM Asia, SotM Africa and SotM LatAm, as well as national and thematic conferences (including the humanitarian-focused HOT Summit of the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team). Talks from State of the Map are recorded and published freely, and the conference is a principal venue for announcements about OSM's data model, editors such as iD and JOSM, rendering, routing, licensing and community governance.
