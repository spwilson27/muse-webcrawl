Penny Marshall as Laverne DeFazio

Carole Penny Marshall (15 October 1943 – 17 December 2018) was an American actress, director, and producer. She rose to fame playing Laverne DeFazio on the sitcom Laverne & Shirley (1976–1983) and went on to become one of the most commercially successful film directors of her era.

Acting career

Born in the Bronx to a family in show business — her brother was director Garry Marshall — she studied at the University of New Mexico before moving to Los Angeles. After appearances on The Odd Couple and Happy Days, she and Cindy Williams were spun off into Laverne & Shirley, which became the most-watched American television programme of its late-1970s seasons and earned Marshall three Golden Globe nominations.

Directing career

Marshall's directorial debut, Jumpin' Jack Flash (1986), was followed by Big (1988), which grossed over $150 million and made her the first woman director to helm a film earning more than $100 million at the US box office. Awakenings (1990), starring Robin Williams and Robert De Niro, was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture. A League of Their Own (1992), about the All-American Girls Professional Baseball League, was another major hit and was later selected for the National Film Registry. Her later films included Renaissance Man (1994), The Preacher's Wife (1996), and Riding in Cars with Boys (2001).

Marshall died in Los Angeles in 2018 from complications of diabetes.
