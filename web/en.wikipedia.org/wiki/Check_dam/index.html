Check dam

A check dam is a small, sometimes temporary, dam constructed across a swale, drainage ditch, or waterway to counteract erosion by reducing water flow velocity. Check dams are built from materials such as rock, gravel bags, logs, or concrete blocks.

Function

By slowing stormwater runoff, check dams allow sediment to settle out and promote infiltration into the soil. They are widely used in erosion and sediment control during construction, in gully restoration, and in arid-region water harvesting, where they help recharge groundwater and restore degraded land.

Design considerations

Check dams must be spaced and keyed into banks correctly and inspected after storms; poorly maintained dams can fail or redirect flow and worsen erosion.
