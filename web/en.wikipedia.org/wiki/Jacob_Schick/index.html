Jacob Schick

Jacob Schick

Born: September 16, 1877, Ottumwa, Iowa

Died: July 3, 1937, Montreal, Canada

Occupation: Inventor, entrepreneur, U.S. Army officer

Known for: Inventing the electric razor; founding Schick Dry Shaver, Inc.

Jacob Schick (September 16, 1877 – July 3, 1937) was an American inventor and entrepreneur who founded the Schick razor company and is credited with inventing the first commercially successful electric razor.

Early life and military career

Schick was born in Ottumwa, Iowa, and grew up in New Mexico, where his father operated mines and a railroad. He enlisted in the U.S. Army in 1898 during the Spanish-American War, served in the Philippines, and spent much of his career as an officer, including service in Alaska. In the Alaskan cold, where water froze and shaving with a conventional razor was painful, he began thinking about "dry" shaving. Retiring from the Army as a lieutenant colonel in 1910 (and returning briefly during World War I), he pursued business ventures in Canada and Alaska.

The injector razor and the electric razor

In the early 1920s Schick patented a safety razor with replaceable blades stored in a magazine and loaded by an injector mechanism — the Schick Injector Razor — inspired by the repeating rifle. He sold that business and devoted himself to his bigger idea: a motorized dry shaver. After years of development, his electric razor reached the market in 1931 under Schick Dry Shaver, Inc. Despite the Depression and an initially bulky design requiring a separate motor, sales climbed steeply, reaching over a million units by 1937 as the design was refined into a single handheld unit.

Death and legacy

Schick died in Montreal in 1937 following complications from surgery, while under investigation by a congressional committee examining wealthy Americans who had moved assets to Bahamas holding companies to avoid taxes. His company, later known simply as Schick, became one of the two dominant razor brands of the twentieth century alongside Gillette, and the electric razor he pioneered created an entirely new category of personal grooming appliances.
