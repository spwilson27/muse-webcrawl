Elbit Systems

Elbit Systems Ltd.
Industry: Aerospace, defence
Founded: 1966
Headquarters: Haifa, Israel
Products: Unmanned aerial vehicles, avionics, electro-optics, electronic warfare systems

Elbit Systems Ltd. is an Israeli international defence electronics company headquartered in Haifa. Founded in 1966, it is one of Israel's largest defence contractors and a major global supplier of military technology.

History

Elbit was established in 1966 as Elbit Computers Ltd., combining expertise from the Israeli Ministry of Defense research establishment and private industry. The company expanded from military computers into avionics, electro-optics and unmanned systems, growing through acquisitions in Israel, the United States, Europe and Brazil. In 2018 it acquired the state-owned IMI Systems, the former Israel Military Industries, significantly expanding its land systems and munitions portfolio.

Products

Elbit Systems develops and supplies a wide range of defence products, including the Hermes family of unmanned aerial vehicles, helmet-mounted displays for fighter pilots, electronic warfare and signals intelligence systems, electro-optical payloads, artillery and mortar systems, naval systems, and command, control and communications (C4I) solutions. The company also provides training and simulation systems and commercial avionics.

Operations

The company employs more than 19,000 people worldwide, with major subsidiaries including Elbit Systems of America and Elbit Systems UK. It is listed on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange and NASDAQ under the ticker ESLT.
