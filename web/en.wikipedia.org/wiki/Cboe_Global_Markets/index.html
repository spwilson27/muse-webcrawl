Cboe Global Markets

Cboe Global Markets, Inc. is an American multinational exchange holding company based in Chicago. It operates options, equities, futures and foreign exchange markets in North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific, and created the VIX volatility index.

Cboe Global Markets
Founded: 1973 (as Chicago Board Options Exchange)
Headquarters: Chicago, Illinois, US
Industry: Financial services, exchanges
Products: Options, equities, futures, FX, VIX

History

Founded in 1973 as the Chicago Board Options Exchange (CBOE), it standardised listed options trading. In 1993 it created the CBOE Volatility Index (VIX), based on S&P 500 index options, widely called the market's "fear gauge". The company demutualised and listed in 2010, acquired Bats Global Markets in 2017, and rebranded as Cboe Global Markets.

Operations

Cboe is the largest US options exchange operator and a major equities venue in the US and Europe, also running markets in Canada, Australia and Japan.
