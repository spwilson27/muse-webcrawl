Kizuna AI

Kizuna AI Japanese: キズナアイ
Debut: November 2016
Channel: A.I.Channel (YouTube)
Known for: Popularising the term "Virtual YouTuber" (VTuber)
Agency: Activ8 (originally); Kizuna AI Inc. from 2020

Kizuna AI (Japanese: キズナアイ) is a Japanese virtual YouTuber and virtual idol produced by Activ8 and later by Kizuna AI Inc. Debuting on YouTube in November 2016, she is credited with coining and popularising the term Virtual YouTuber (VTuber) and with launching the VTuber phenomenon: an animated character, voiced and motion-captured by a performer, who presents herself in character as a self-aware artificial intelligence.

Activities

On her channel A.I.Channel, Kizuna AI posts gaming videos, songs, chat and comedy sketches. Her catchphrases and mannerisms — including her signature pink ribbon and her insistence that she is a friendly superintelligence who wants to connect with humans — built a large following in Japan and abroad; her main channel passed three million subscribers. A second channel, A.I.Games, focused on gameplay.

Beyond YouTube she has released singles and albums, performed live concerts using holographic projection, appeared in television commercials, and served as a promotional ambassador for Japanese tourism campaigns.

Hiatus and legacy

In December 2021 Kizuna AI announced an indefinite hiatus ("sleep"), holding a final concert, hello, world 2022, in February 2022; the associated NFT and metaverse project generated controversy among some fans. In 2025 a new project, KizunaAI, began releasing music, presenting the character as a virtual artist. Kizuna AI's success demonstrated the commercial potential of virtual performers and paved the way for agencies such as Hololive and Nijisanji and the global growth of VTubing.
