Porsche Design

Porsche Design (legally Porsche Lizenz- und Handelsgesellschaft mbH & Co. KG) is a German lifestyle brand and product design studio founded in 1972 by Ferdinand Alexander Porsche, the designer of the original Porsche 911. Based in Ludwigsburg, Germany, it is a subsidiary of Porsche Automobil Holding SE.

History

Ferdinand Alexander ("Butzi") Porsche left the family car company in 1972 and established his own design studio in Austria before it moved to Germany. The studio's first product, a black chronograph watch made with Orfina, set the brand's functional, engineering-led design language.

Products

Porsche Design sells and licenses products including watches, sunglasses, luggage, electronics, writing instruments, fragrances, and apparel, and the Studio F. A. Porsche design consultancy works for external clients on industrial design projects ranging from trains to household appliances.

Relationship with Porsche cars

Although distinct from the sports car manufacturer Porsche AG, the brand trades on the Porsche name and design heritage. Porsche Design stores operate in major cities worldwide.
