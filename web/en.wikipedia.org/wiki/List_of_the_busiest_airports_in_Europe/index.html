Passenger numbers recovered strongly after the COVID-19 pandemic, with most major hubs surpassing or approaching their 2019 record levels by the mid-2020s. Istanbul Airport, opened in 2018, rose rapidly to become one of Europe's busiest, while London Heathrow long held the top position and remains among the busiest in the world.
