Chittoor district

Chittoor district is a district in the Rayalaseema region of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh, with its headquarters at Chittoor. Following the 2022 reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh's districts, the district covers an area of roughly 6,855 km² and has a population of about 1.87 million (2011 census, within the new boundaries). Telugu is the principal language.

Geography and economy

The district lies in the far south of Andhra Pradesh, bordering Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Its terrain includes the eastern edge of the Deccan plateau and hill ranges such as Horsley Hills, a popular hill station. Agriculture — groundnuts, mangoes, sugarcane, and dairy farming — dominates the economy; the district is a major milk-producing area, and mango pulp processing is significant around Chittoor town.

Historically the district included the pilgrimage city of Tirupati and the Sri Venkateswara Temple at Tirumala, which since 2022 lie in the newly formed Tirupati district, and the town of Madanapalle, known for its educational institutions and as a health resort.
