Mohe

Mohe (Chinese: 漠河; pinyin: Mòhé) is a county-level city in Daxing'anling Prefecture, Heilongjiang province, China. It is the northernmost city in China, bordering Russia across the Amur River (Heilongjiang), and is known for its subarctic climate and as a destination for viewing the aurora borealis.

Geography and climate

Mohe lies at approximately 53°N in the Greater Khingan Mountains. It has a monsoon-influenced subarctic climate with very cold, dry winters and mild, rainy summers. Winter temperatures regularly fall below −40 °C; the lowest temperature recorded in China, −52.3 °C, was measured at Mohe on 13 February 1969, a record surpassed only in 2023 at nearby Jintao. The region is covered by boreal forest (taiga) of larch, pine and birch.

History

The area was historically inhabited by Tungusic peoples; the name is associated with the ancient Mohe people. Modern settlement grew with gold mining in the late 19th century and later with forestry. The devastating Greater Khingan forest fire of May 1987, one of the largest wildfires in modern Chinese history, burned large areas around Mohe and reshaped national fire policy. Mohe County was upgraded to a county-level city in 2018.

Tourism

Tourist attractions include Beijicun ("Northernmost Village"), China's northernmost settlement; the "northernmost" post office, school and other landmarks; the Arctic Village (Beiji Village), which markets aurora viewing around the summer solstice's midnight twilight and occasional winter auroras; and a memorial museum to the 1987 fire. Mohe is served by Mohe Gulian Airport and by rail from Harbin.
