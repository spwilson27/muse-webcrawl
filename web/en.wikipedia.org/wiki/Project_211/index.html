Project 211

Project 211 (Chinese: 211工程) was a project of the Chinese government, launched by the Ministry of Education in 1995, to strengthen about 100 institutions of higher education and key disciplinary areas as a national priority for the 21st century. The name derives from the slogan of preparing roughly 100 universities for the 21st century.

Scope

Universities selected for Project 211 received substantial government funding to improve research facilities, faculty, and international exchange. Member universities were responsible for training a large share of China's doctoral students and undertook a major portion of state-funded research. Admission to a Project 211 university was highly competitive.

Successors

Project 211, together with the more exclusive Project 985, was later superseded administratively by the Double First-Class Construction initiative announced in 2015–2017, though the "211" designation remained widely used as a marker of prestige in hiring and admissions.
