GE Vernova

GE Vernova Inc. is an American multinational energy technology company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It was formed from the energy businesses of General Electric — power generation, wind energy, and grid electrification — and became an independent public company through a spin-off completed on 2 April 2024, as the final step in GE's break-up into three companies.

History

In November 2021, GE announced it would split into three public companies focused on aviation, healthcare, and energy. GE HealthCare was spun off in January 2023, and the remaining energy portfolio — GE Power, GE Renewable Energy, GE Digital, and GE Energy Financial Services — was combined as GE Vernova in 2022 ahead of its own listing. After the spin-off, GE's aviation business renamed itself GE Aerospace, and the GE Vernova name (from verde and nova, "new green") was chosen to signal the energy transition.

Business segments

The Power segment supplies heavy-duty gas turbines (including the HA-class, among the world's most efficient), steam turbines, generators, and nuclear power technology through the GE Hitachi alliance, plus services for a fleet of roughly 7,000 gas turbines. The Wind segment builds onshore and offshore wind turbines, including the Haliade-X offshore platform, and had been a source of losses amid quality and market problems. The Electrification segment covers grid solutions, power conversion, solar and storage inverters, and related software. GE Vernova equipment helps generate roughly a quarter to a third of the world's electricity.
