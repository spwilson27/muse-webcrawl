Glenmorangie distillery

LocationTain, Ross-shire, Scotland
Founded1843
OwnerLVMH (via Glenmorangie plc)
TypeSingle malt Scotch whisky

Glenmorangie distillery is a Highland single malt Scotch whisky distillery at Tain in Ross-shire, Scotland. It was founded in 1843 by William Matheson on the site of the Morangie brewery.

Production

The distillery is noted for having the tallest pot stills in Scotland, whose long necks are said to produce a light, floral spirit. Water comes from the Tarlogie Springs. Glenmorangie pioneered cask finishing (wood finishes), maturing whisky in ex-bourbon casks before transferring it to port, sherry, or wine casks for a final period.

Range and ownership

Core expressions include the Original ten-year-old and finishes such as Lasanta, Quinta Ruban, and Nectar d'Or. The distillery was owned by the Macdonald family for much of the twentieth century before being acquired by Louis Vuitton Moët Hennessy in 2004.
