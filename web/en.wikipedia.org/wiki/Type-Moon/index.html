Type-Moon (stylised as TYPE-MOON) is a Japanese video game and fiction studio, the brand name of the company Notes Co., Ltd. It was founded by writer Kinoko Nasu and artist Takashi Takeuchi, originally as a dōjin (self-published) circle, and is best known for its visual novels and the sprawling shared "Nasuverse" setting that connects most of its works.

History

The circle first gained attention with the novel series The Garden of Sinners (Kara no Kyōkai) and the dōjin visual novel Tsukihime (2000), whose popularity led to an anime adaptation and a manga. Type-Moon's commercial breakthrough came with Fate/stay night (2004), a visual novel about a battle royale among magi and summoned heroic spirits — the Holy Grail War — which spawned the enormous Fate franchise.

Fate franchise and later works

The Fate series expanded into novels (Fate/Zero), anime by studio Ufotable, and the mobile role-playing game Fate/Grand Order (2015), which became one of the highest-grossing mobile games in the world. Type-Moon also released the visual novel Witch on the Holy Night (Mahōtsukai no Yoru, 2012) and a remake of Tsukihime beginning in 2021.
