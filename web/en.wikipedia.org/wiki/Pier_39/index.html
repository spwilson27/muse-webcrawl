Pier 39

LocationFisherman's Wharf, San Francisco, California
OpenedOctober 4, 1978
DeveloperWarren Simmons
FeaturesShops, restaurants, Aquarium of the Bay, sea lions

Pier 39 is a shopping centre and popular tourist attraction built on a pier in San Francisco, California, at the edge of the Fisherman's Wharf district. Opened in 1978, it contains shops, restaurants, a video arcade, street performances, the Aquarium of the Bay and views of Alcatraz and the Golden Gate Bridge. It is one of the city's most visited attractions.

Sea lions

Pier 39 is famous for its colony of California sea lions, which began hauling out on the K-Dock boat slips after the Loma Prieta earthquake in October 1989 and have remained ever since, protected on floating docks cleared of boats. Numbers fluctuate seasonally, reaching over a thousand in peak season, and the Marine Mammal Center maintains a Sea Lion Center interpreting the colony.

Attractions

The two-level complex, built with timber salvaged from old cargo piers, includes a double-decker carousel, the Aquarium of the Bay (opened 1996), bay cruises departing for Alcatraz and Angel Island, and numerous seafood restaurants serving San Francisco sourdough and clam chowder.
