Endeavor (company)

Endeavor's headquarters in Beverly Hills, California

Endeavor Group Holdings, Inc., commonly known as Endeavor, is an American holding company for talent and media agencies. Formed from the William Morris Agency and Endeavor Talent Agency merger that created WME in 2009 and the subsequent acquisition of IMG in 2014, the company grew into a global sports and entertainment business under chief executive Ari Emanuel. Its headquarters are in Beverly Hills, California.

History

The original Endeavor Talent Agency was founded in 1995. In 2009 it merged with the William Morris Agency to form William Morris Endeavor (WME). The combined agency acquired the sports and media company IMG in 2014 for about $2.4 billion, and the group was rebranded as Endeavor in 2017. Endeavor bought the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) in 2016 as part of a group of investors in a deal valued at roughly $4 billion, then increased its stake over time.

Endeavor held an initial public offering in April 2021, listing on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker EDR. In 2023, Endeavor combined the UFC with WWE into a new publicly traded company, TKO Group Holdings, in a deal that valued the combined business at more than $21 billion; Emanuel became chief executive of TKO while remaining head of Endeavor.

In November 2024, the private equity firm Silver Lake — already Endeavor's largest shareholder — agreed to take Endeavor private in a deal valuing its equity at about $13 billion. The transaction closed in March 2025. As part of the restructuring, the WME Group agency business was separated, while Emanuel moved to lead the agency entity.

Business

Endeavor's operations have included talent representation through WME (film, television, music, sports, fashion and digital creators), the IMG licensing and events portfolio, sports properties such as the UFC, and hospitality and experiences businesses including On Location. Clients represented by WME have included many of the best-known actors, directors, musicians and athletes in the world.
