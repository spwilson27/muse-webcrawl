Genesis Motor

Genesis Motor, LLC, commonly referred to as Genesis, is the luxury vehicle division of the South Korean automobile manufacturer Hyundai Motor Group. Announced as a standalone brand in November 2015, Genesis launched its first model, the Genesis G90 (sold as the EQ900 in Korea), in December 2015.

Industry: Automotive
Founded: 4 November 2015
Headquarters: Seoul, South Korea
Parent: Hyundai Motor Group
Products: Luxury automobiles

History

Hyundai had sold a rear-wheel-drive luxury sedan under the Genesis nameplate since 2008; spinning the name into a separate marque followed the approach of Toyota's Lexus and Nissan's Infiniti. Early models — the G70, G80 and G90 sedans — were joined by the GV60, GV70 and GV80 sport utility vehicles, including dedicated electric models built on the E-GMP platform.

Markets and motorsport

Genesis sells in South Korea, North America, Europe, China, the Middle East and Australia, and has won design and quality awards including high rankings in J.D. Power studies. The brand announced its Genesis Magma Racing programme to enter endurance racing, including the World Endurance Championship, in the mid-2020s.
