David Robert Joseph Beckham (born 2 May 1975) is an English former professional footballer, the president and co-owner of Inter Miami CF and co-owner of Salford City. Renowned for his range of passing, crossing ability, and bending free kicks as a right winger, he is regarded as one of the most recognisable and marketable athletes of his generation.

[ Hero image: David Beckham lining up a free kick for England ]

Club career

Beckham came through Manchester United's youth system as part of the celebrated "Class of '92". With United he won six Premier League titles, two FA Cups, and the 1999 UEFA Champions League as part of the Treble. In 2003 he joined Real Madrid as one of the Galácticos, winning La Liga in his final season, then moved to LA Galaxy in Major League Soccer in 2007 — a signing credited with transforming the league's profile — winning two MLS Cups. After loan spells at AC Milan, he ended his career with Paris Saint-Germain in 2013, becoming the first English player to win league titles in four countries.

International career

Beckham earned 115 caps for England, a record for an outfield player at the time, and captained the side in 58 matches. He played at three World Cups; his red card against Argentina in 1998 made him a national scapegoat, but he redeemed himself by scoring the late penalty against Argentina and the stoppage-time free kick against Greece that secured qualification for the 2002 tournament.

After retirement

Beckham exercised an option to found an MLS expansion team, Inter Miami CF, which began play in 2020 and later signed Lionel Messi. He is a UNICEF goodwill ambassador and remains a prominent figure in fashion and advertising. He is married to Victoria Beckham, formerly of the Spice Girls, with whom he has four children.
