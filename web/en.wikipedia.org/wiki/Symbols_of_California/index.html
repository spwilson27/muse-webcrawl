Symbols of California

The U.S. state of California has a number of official state symbols, emblems and insignia adopted by statute or tradition. The state's motto is "Eureka", Greek for "I have found it", a reference to the discovery of gold in 1848. The state nickname is the "Golden State".

Flag and seal

The Bear Flag, depicting a grizzly bear, a red star, a red stripe and the words "California Republic", was first raised during the Bear Flag Revolt of 1846 and was adopted as the state flag in 1911. The Great Seal of California, adopted in 1849, shows the goddess Minerva, a grizzly bear, a miner, ships on the Pacific, and the word "Eureka" above.
