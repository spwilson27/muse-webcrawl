Steven Levy

Born: 1951, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, U.S.

Occupation: Journalist, author

Known for: Hackers: Heroes of the Computer Revolution

Steven Levy (born 1951) is an American journalist and editor at large for Wired. He has written extensively on computers, technology, cryptography, the internet, cybersecurity, and privacy. He is the author of several books on computing culture, most notably Hackers: Heroes of the Computer Revolution (1984), a classic account of early computer hackers and the hacker ethic.

Career

Levy was a senior editor at Newsweek, where he covered technology weekly, before joining Wired. He was the founder of Backchannel, a technology publication on Medium. His books include Crypto (2001), about the development of public key cryptography; Insanely Great (1994), about the development of the Apple Macintosh; In the Plex (2011), about Google; and Facebook: The Inside Story (2020).
