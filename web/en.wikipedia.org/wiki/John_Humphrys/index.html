Desmond John Humphrys (born 17 August 1943) is a Welsh broadcaster, journalist and author. He presented the BBC Radio 4 programme Today from 1987 until 2019 and hosted the BBC television quiz show Mastermind from 2003 until 2021.

Career

Humphrys began his career with the BBC in 1966 as a district reporter in Liverpool and later served as a foreign correspondent in the United States and South Africa. He was the first full-time television correspondent in Northern Ireland during the Troubles and presented the Nine O'Clock News. His combative interviewing style on Today made him one of the most recognisable political interviewers in Britain, earning him the nickname "the rottweiler" of broadcasting.

Later work

After leaving Today in September 2019, Humphrys continued writing, including books on language and politics, and joined Classic FM as a presenter. He co-founded the charity Kids in Need.
