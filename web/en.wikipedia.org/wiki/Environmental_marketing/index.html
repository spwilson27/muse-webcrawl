Environmental marketing

Environmental marketing, also called green marketing or eco-marketing, refers to the practice of developing and advertising products based on their real or perceived environmental sustainability. It emerged as a distinct field in the 1970s and grew through workshops of the American Marketing Association and books such as Jacquelyn Ottman's Green Marketing.

Strategies

Approaches include eco-labelling and certification (e.g. Energy Star, FSC), redesigning products and packaging, sustainable supply chains, and communicating life-cycle benefits. Green marketing targets consumers willing to pay premiums for lower-impact goods.

Greenwashing

Misleading environmental claims—greenwashing—have prompted regulation such as the US FTC Green Guides and EU rules on environmental claims, requiring substantiation of terms like "recyclable" or "carbon neutral".
