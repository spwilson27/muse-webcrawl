The Horse Whisperer (film)

The Horse Whisperer is a 1998 American drama film directed by and starring Robert Redford, based on the 1995 novel by Nicholas Evans. It also stars Kristin Scott Thomas and Scarlett Johansson in one of her early leading roles.

DirectorRobert Redford
Based onThe Horse Whisperer by Nicholas Evans
StarringRobert Redford, Kristin Scott Thomas, Scarlett Johansson
Release date15 May 1998

Plot

Teenager Grace MacLean is badly injured in a riding accident that leaves her horse Pilgrim traumatised and maimed. Her mother Annie takes Grace and Pilgrim from New York to Montana, where horse trainer Tom Booker, a "horse whisperer" with a gift for understanding horses, slowly heals both horse and girl — and falls in love with Annie.

Production and reception

Filmed in Montana, the picture was Redford's first as both director and lead actor. It was a commercial success, grossing about $187 million worldwide, and was praised for its cinematography by Robert Richardson, though some critics found its nearly three-hour running time slow.
