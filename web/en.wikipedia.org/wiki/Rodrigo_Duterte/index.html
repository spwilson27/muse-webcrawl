Rodrigo Duterte

Rodrigo Roa Duterte (born March 28, 1945) is a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as the 16th president of the Philippines from 2016 to 2022. Before that he was mayor of Davao City for more than 22 years over seven terms, earning a reputation — and the nickname "the Punisher" — for a hardline approach to crime that supporters credited with transforming the city and critics linked to extrajudicial killings by alleged death squads.

Presidency

Duterte won the 2016 presidential election in a landslide on a populist platform centred on an anti-drug campaign. His war on drugs led to the deaths of thousands of suspects — officially around 6,000, with rights groups estimating far more — and drew international condemnation and an International Criminal Court investigation. His administration also pursued an independent foreign policy marked by a pivot towards China and Russia and public hostility towards the United States, launched the "Build! Build! Build!" infrastructure programme, and signed the Bangsamoro peace settlement for Muslim Mindanao.

After the presidency

Duterte left office in 2022 and was succeeded by Ferdinand Marcos Jr., while his daughter Sara Duterte became vice president. In March 2025 he was arrested in Manila on an ICC warrant alleging crimes against humanity in connection with the drug war and transferred to The Hague to face trial.
