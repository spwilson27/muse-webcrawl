Uttarkashi district

Uttarkashi district

State: Uttarakhand, India

Headquarters: Uttarkashi

Notable sites: Gangotri, Yamunotri, Gaumukh

Uttarkashi district is a district of Uttarakhand state in northern India, in the Garhwal Himalaya. Its headquarters is the town of Uttarkashi. The district contains the sources of India's two great sacred rivers: the Bhagirathi (Ganges) at Gangotri and the Yamuna at Yamunotri, two of the four Char Dham pilgrimage sites.

Geography

The district is mountainous, rising from about 1,158 m at Uttarkashi town to the high peaks of the Gangotri group guarding the Tibetan border. The Gaumukh snout of the Gangotri Glacier, source of the Bhagirathi, lies within the district, as does much of Gangotri National Park, home to snow leopards and Himalayan wildlife.

History

The region formed part of the Garhwal kingdom and later of Tehri Garhwal princely state. Uttarkashi was severely affected by a major earthquake in 1991 and by the catastrophic Himalayan floods of 2013, which devastated pilgrim routes and villages across northern Uttarakhand.
