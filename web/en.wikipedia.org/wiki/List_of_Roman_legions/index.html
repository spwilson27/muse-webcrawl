List of Roman legions

This is a list of Roman legions, the heavy infantry units that formed the backbone of the Roman army from the late Republic through the Empire. Legions were numbered and often carried honorific titles (cognomina) recording their origin, victories or loyalty. Under Augustus the standing army settled at 28 legions; the number rose to about 33 under Septimius Severus.

Late Roman legions

From the reforms of Diocletian and Constantine, new legions were created in larger numbers but with smaller establishments, divided between frontier troops (limitanei) and mobile field armies (comitatenses). The Notitia Dignitatum lists dozens of legions still in existence around AD 400.
