The European Molecular Biology Organization (EMBO) is a professional organisation of life scientists in Europe and beyond, founded in 1964. It promotes excellence in molecular biology through fellowships, courses, workshops, publishing, and its elected membership of more than 2,000 leading researchers.

Activities

EMBO funds postdoctoral and young-investigator fellowships, organises scientific meetings, and publishes journals including The EMBO Journal, EMBO Reports, Molecular Systems Biology and EMBO Molecular Medicine through EMBO Press.

Related bodies

EMBO led to the creation of the intergovernmental European Molecular Biology Conference (EMBC) and the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) in Heidelberg, founded in 1974.
