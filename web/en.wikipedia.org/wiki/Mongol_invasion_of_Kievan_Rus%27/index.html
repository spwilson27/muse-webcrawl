Mongol invasion of Kievan Rus'

Date: 1237–1242
Attackers: Mongol Empire (Batu Khan, Subutai)
Result: Rus' principalities become vassals of the Golden Horde

The Mongol invasion of Kievan Rus' was part of the Mongol invasion of Europe, in which the Mongol Empire under Batu Khan and the general Subutai conquered the fragmented principalities of Kievan Rus' between 1237 and 1242.

Course

The campaign began with the destruction of Ryazan in December 1237, followed by Vladimir, and culminated in the siege and sack of Kiev in December 1240. Mongol forces then moved into Galicia–Volhynia and onward into Poland and Hungary.

Aftermath

The Rus' lands fell under the suzerainty of the Golden Horde — the so-called "Mongol yoke" — paying tribute for roughly two centuries. The invasion devastated cities and reshaped the political development of the region, contributing to the rise of Moscow.
