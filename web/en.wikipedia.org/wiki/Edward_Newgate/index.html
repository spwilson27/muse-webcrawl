Edward Newgate

Edward Newgate is a fictionalised pirate captain in Ubisoft's Assassin's Creed franchise, most prominently the grandfather of Connor Kenway and father of Haytham Kenway in Assassin's Creed III and related media.

Note: this page concerns the fictional character Edward James Kenway, whose alias is sometimes rendered with the surname Newgate in secondary material; the historical pirate Blackbeard's name was Edward Teach (also recorded as Thatch).

In the franchise

Edward Kenway (1693–1735) is a Welsh privateer-turned-pirate who becomes embroiled in the conflict between Assassins and Templars in the West Indies during the Golden Age of Piracy in Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag (2013). Captain of the brig Jackdaw, he interacts with historical pirates including Blackbeard (Edward Teach), Benjamin Hornigold, Charles Vane, Calico Jack, Anne Bonny and Mary Read, before eventually joining the Assassin Brotherhood. His story continues in the novel Assassin's Creed: Forsaken, which recounts his assassination in London in 1735 by Templar agents.

Character

Edward is portrayed as a charming, reckless treasure-seeker whose pursuit of wealth costs him his family and friends, and whose arc is one of gradual maturation into responsibility. He is voiced by Matt Ryan.
