Pratt & Whitney is an American aerospace manufacturer with global service operations. It is a subsidiary of RTX Corporation (formerly Raytheon Technologies). Pratt & Whitney's aircraft engines are widely used in both civil aviation (especially airliners) and military aviation.

Founded in 1925 by Frederick Rentschler in Hartford, Connecticut, the company produced its first engine, the R-1340 Wasp radial engine, which became widely used. Its large commercial engines, such as the JT8D, PW4000, and the geared turbofan PW1000G family, power many airliners.

Military engines

Military products include the F119 engine for the F-22 Raptor, the F135 for the F-35 Lightning II, and the F100 for the F-15 and F-16.

Divisions

Pratt & Whitney Canada, based in Longueuil, Quebec, produces smaller engines for regional airliners, business jets, and helicopters.
