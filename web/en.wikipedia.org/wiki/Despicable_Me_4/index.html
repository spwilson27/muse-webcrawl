Despicable Me 4 is a 2024 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. It is the sequel to Despicable Me 3 (2017) and the fourth main instalment — and sixth overall — in the Despicable Me franchise.

Plot

Gru, Lucy and their children — including baby Gru Jr. — are forced into witness protection in the town of Mayflower after Gru's old school rival Maxime Le Mal escapes prison seeking revenge, having transformed himself with cockroach DNA. Meanwhile, five of the Minions are injected with a super serum by the Anti-Villain League, becoming the superpowered but incompetent Mega Minions.

Reception

The film received mixed reviews from critics and grossed about $969 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2024 and cementing Despicable Me as the highest-grossing animated film franchise of all time.
