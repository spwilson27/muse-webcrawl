Nuclear receptor

Nuclear receptors are a class of proteins within cells that bind steroid and thyroid hormones and other ligands, then act as transcription factors regulating gene expression.

Structure and mechanism

They share a DNA-binding domain with zinc fingers and a ligand-binding domain. Upon ligand binding they dimerise, bind hormone response elements on DNA, and recruit coactivators or corepressors. Examples include estrogen, androgen, glucocorticoid receptors, PPARs and thyroid hormone receptors. Humans have 48 nuclear receptors, many important drug targets.
