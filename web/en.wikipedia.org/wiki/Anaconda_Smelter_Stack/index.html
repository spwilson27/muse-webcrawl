Anaconda Smelter Stack

LocationAnaconda, Montana, United States
Completed1919
Height585 ft (178.3 m)
StatusPreserved landmark

The Anaconda Smelter Stack is a tall brick smokestack near Anaconda, Montana, built in 1919 for the Washoe Reduction Works of the Anaconda Copper Mining Company. At 585 feet (178 metres) it is one of the tallest free-standing brick structures in the world, and it remains a prominent landmark of Montana's copper-mining heritage.

Construction

The stack was built by the Alphons Custodis Chimney Construction Company. Its inside diameter tapers from about 75 feet at the base to about 60 feet at the top. It was designed to disperse exhaust gases and dust from the copper smelter below, which processed ore from the mines of nearby Butte.

Closure and preservation

The smelter closed in 1980 and most of its buildings were demolished as part of environmental remediation of the Anaconda Smelter Superfund site. The stack was saved from demolition after a local campaign and is now the centrepiece of a Montana state park, although the structure itself is closed to visitors for safety and contamination reasons.
