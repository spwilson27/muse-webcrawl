Acton is an unincorporated census-designated place in Los Angeles County, California, United States. It lies in the Sierra Pelona Mountains of the Antelope Valley region, along State Route 14 between Palmdale and Santa Clarita, and is served by the Metrolink Antelope Valley Line.

[Image: Acton and the Sierra Pelona Mountains]

History

The community grew up in the 1880s around a railroad siding and copper mining camp. Its old-western character, including the preserved 49er Saloon building in the historic downtown, has made it a filming location. Acton is known for its rural, equestrian character, large residential lots, and the distinctive Vasquez Rocks nearby, where the outlaw Tiburcio Vásquez hid in the 1870s.

Geography and climate

Acton sits in high desert terrain at about 2,700 feet elevation, with hot, dry summers and cool winters that occasionally bring snow. The area is subject to wildfire risk and Santa Ana winds. Nearby attractions include Shambala Preserve, a wild animal sanctuary founded by actress Tippi Hedren.
