MultiMediaCard

TypeFlash memory card
Introduced1997
DevelopersSanDisk and Siemens (Infineon)
SuccessoreMMC (embedded); related to SD card

The MultiMediaCard (MMC) is a flash memory card standard introduced in 1997 by SanDisk and Siemens. About the size of a postage stamp, it stores data for portable devices such as digital cameras, mobile phones, and music players.

Description

An MMC measures 32 × 24 × 1.4 mm and communicates over a serial interface with seven contacts. The standard was maintained by the MultiMediaCard Association. Variants included the smaller RS-MMC and MMCmobile, higher-speed MMCplus cards, and the embedded eMMC format.

Legacy

The Secure Digital (SD) card format was developed from MMC and shares its basic form factor and interface; many SD slots accept MMC cards. While removable MMC cards were largely displaced by SD and microSD, eMMC became a dominant form of embedded storage in smartphones, tablets, and budget laptops before the rise of UFS.
