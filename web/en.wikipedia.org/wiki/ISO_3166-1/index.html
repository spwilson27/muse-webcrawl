ISO 3166-1

ISO 3166-1 is part of the ISO 3166 standard published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), and defines codes for the names of countries, dependent territories, and special areas of geographical interest. The coding system includes 249 entries and provides three sets of country codes for each:

  • Alpha-2: two-letter codes, the most widely used (e.g. internet country-code top-level domains)
  • Alpha-3: three-letter codes, closer to the country name
  • Numeric-3: three-digit codes, identical to those of the UN Statistics Division

Examples

CountryAlpha-2Alpha-3Numeric
FranceFRFRA250
GermanyDEDEU276
JapanJPJPN392
United KingdomGBGBR826
United StatesUSUSA840

Some codes are exceptionally reserved—for example, UK is reserved for the United Kingdom at its request, and EU for the European Union. Country names follow the terminology of the United Nations.
