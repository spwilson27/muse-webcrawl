Yucca Mountain nuclear waste repository

The Yucca Mountain Nuclear Waste Repository, as designated by the Nuclear Waste Policy Act amendments of 1987, is a proposed deep geological repository in Nye County, Nevada, for spent nuclear fuel and high-level radioactive waste from the United States.

Yucca Mountain repository
Location: Nye County, Nevada
Designated: 1987
Status: Licence application withdrawn/defunded 2011
Planned capacity: 70,000 tonnes

Background

The site lies on federal land beside the Nevada National Security Site in the Amargosa Desert, about 130 km northwest of Las Vegas. Tunnels were excavated in the 1990s to study the volcanic tuff geology.

Controversy

The project faced decades of opposition from the State of Nevada, the Western Shoshone (for whom the land is treaty territory), and environmental groups, citing seismic, volcanic, groundwater, and transport risks. The Obama administration cut funding and moved to withdraw the licence in 2010–2011; later administrations and Congress have periodically debated revival, but no waste has been emplaced.

Legacy

US spent fuel remains stored at reactor sites in pools and dry casks while a consent-based siting process is pursued.
