List of rulers of China

This article lists the monarchs of China from the legendary Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors through the dynasties to the last emperor of the Qing dynasty, who abdicated in 1912. Chinese monarchs are conventionally identified by dynastic name together with a temple name, posthumous name or era name (for the Ming and Qing, the era name is most common).

After the empire

After the Xinhai Revolution, China was headed by presidents of the Republic of China (from 1912) and, since 1949, by the leadership of the People's Republic of China, alongside the Republic of China government on Taiwan.
