Tomm Moore

Thomas "Tomm" Moore (born 7 January 1977) is an Irish filmmaker, animator, illustrator, and comics artist. He is a co-founder of the Kilkenny-based studio Cartoon Saloon and is best known for directing the hand-drawn animated features The Secret of Kells (2009), Song of the Sea (2014), and Wolfwalkers (2020), all nominated for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.

Career

Moore was born in Newry, County Down, and raised in Kilkenny. He studied classical animation at Ballyfermot College in Dublin, and in 1999 founded Cartoon Saloon with Paul Young and Nora Twomey. His directorial debut, The Secret of Kells, drew on Irish illuminated manuscripts and mythology; Song of the Sea explored selkie folklore; and Wolfwalkers, co-directed with Ross Stewart, set a tale of a young wolf hunter in 17th-century Kilkenny. Together the three are often called his "Irish folklore trilogy."

Other work

Moore directed a segment of the anthology film Kahlil Gibran's The Prophet (2014), contributed to television projects, and has created graphic novels related to his films. His work is noted for flat, decorative, storybook-influenced design and a commitment to traditional hand-drawn animation.
