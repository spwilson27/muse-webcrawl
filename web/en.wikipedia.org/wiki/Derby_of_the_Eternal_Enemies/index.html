Derby of the Eternal Enemies

The Derby of the Eternal Enemies (Greek: ντέρμπι των αιωνίων αντιπάλων) is the name given to football matches between Olympiacos of Piraeus and Panathinaikos of Athens, the two most successful and popular clubs in Greek football. It is regarded as one of the fiercest derbies in world football.

Background

The rivalry reflects social and geographic divides: Panathinaikos, founded in 1908, was based in central Athens, while Olympiacos, founded in 1925, represents the port city of Piraeus and was historically associated with the working class. The two clubs have won the overwhelming majority of Greek league championships between them, and their meetings have often been marred by crowd trouble, leading at times to matches behind closed doors. The hatred between the sets of supporters also extends to the clubs' basketball and volleyball teams, whose derbies are equally intense.
