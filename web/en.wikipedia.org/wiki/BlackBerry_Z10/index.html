BlackBerry Z10

The BlackBerry Z10 is a high-end LTE touchscreen-based smartphone developed by BlackBerry, then known as Research In Motion. It was the first device to run the BlackBerry 10 operating system and was unveiled on 30 January 2013 at BlackBerry 10 launch events worldwide. Unusually for BlackBerry, the Z10 had no physical keyboard, relying entirely on a gesture-based touch interface.

Hardware and software

The Z10 featured a 4.2-inch display at 356 ppi, a dual-core 1.5 GHz processor, 2 GB of RAM, 16 GB of storage expandable by microSD, and a removable 1800 mAh battery. Its interface centred on the BlackBerry Hub, a unified inbox, and on gestures such as swiping up from the bezel to return home. It launched alongside the keyboard-equipped BlackBerry Q10.

Reception

Reviews praised the hardware and Hub but criticised the small app catalogue of the new platform. Despite an initially promising launch, sales fell short, and BlackBerry recorded a large write-down on unsold Z10 inventory later in 2013.
