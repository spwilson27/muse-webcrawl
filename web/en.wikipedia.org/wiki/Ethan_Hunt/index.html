Ethan Matthew Hunt is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Mission: Impossible film series. He is portrayed by Tom Cruise in all of the films, beginning with Mission: Impossible (1996) and continuing through sequels including Ghost Protocol (2011), Rogue Nation (2015), Fallout (2018) and Dead Reckoning (2023).

Character

Hunt is a senior field agent and team leader of the Impossible Mission Force (IMF), a covert American intelligence agency. Framed for the murder of his team in the first film, he clears his name by exposing a mole; later missions pit him against rogue agents, arms dealers, the anarchist network known as the Syndicate and its successor the Apostles, and a rogue artificial intelligence called the Entity. Hunt is characterised by loyalty to his team — recurring allies include Luther Stickell (Ving Rhames), Benji Dunn (Simon Pegg) and Ilsa Faust (Rebecca Ferguson) — and by executing elaborate stunts, many performed by Cruise himself, such as dangling from a Burj Khalifa glass façade, clinging to a departing A400M transport aircraft, and a motorcycle cliff jump.

Background

Within the films, Hunt was born on March 18, 1964, in Madison, Wisconsin, raised on a dairy farm in Middlebury, New York, and served in the U.S. Army's Delta Force and special reconnaissance units before joining the IMF. His marriage to nurse Julia Meade, and his faking of her death to protect her, forms a recurring emotional thread. The character was created for the film series and does not appear as a lead in the original television series, where the IMF was led by Jim Phelps — who appears in the first film as the traitor Hunt must unmask.

Reception

Hunt is widely regarded as one of Cruise's signature roles, and the character's daredevil set pieces have become a defining feature of modern action cinema.
