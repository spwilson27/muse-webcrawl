Timeline of the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine

This timeline of the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine covers the events of the war launched by Russia on 24 February 2022, when President Vladimir Putin announced a "special military operation" and Russian forces attacked Ukraine from the north, east and south.

February 2022

On 24 February, Russian missiles struck Kyiv, Kharkiv and other cities; ground forces advanced from Belarus toward Kyiv and from Crimea into southern Ukraine. Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy declared martial law. On 25 February fighting reached Kyiv's outskirts. Russia seized Snake Island and, on 26–27 February, heavy fighting occurred around Kharkiv and the Hostomel Airport.

March 2022

Russian forces captured Kherson on 2 March and encircled Mariupol, where a siege caused mass civilian casualties. The Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant was captured on 4 March. Peace talks in Istanbul on 29 March ended without a ceasefire. By month's end Russia announced a "reduction" of activity around Kyiv.

April–May 2022

Ukraine retook the Kyiv region in early April, revealing massacres of civilians in Bucha. The cruiser Moskva, flagship of Russia's Black Sea Fleet, sank on 14 April after being struck by Ukrainian Neptune missiles. Mariupol's last defenders at the Azovstal steel plant surrendered in May.

Summer–autumn 2022

Russia ground forward in the Donbas, capturing Sievierodonetsk and Lysychansk. In September Ukraine launched a rapid counteroffensive in Kharkiv Oblast, liberating Izium and Kupiansk. Russia announced a partial mobilisation and the annexation of four oblasts. On 11 November Ukrainian forces liberated Kherson after Russia withdrew across the Dnipro.

Later phases

The war continued into 2023 and beyond with fighting centred on Bakhmut, a Ukrainian counteroffensive in Zaporizhzhia Oblast, and Russian offensives in Donetsk Oblast. The timeline continues in the phase articles covering subsequent periods.
