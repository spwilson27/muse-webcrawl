Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT)

Related concepts: Industry 4.0, cyber-physical systems, SCADA

Sectors: Manufacturing, energy, logistics, agriculture

The Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) refers to the use of networked sensors, instruments, and machines in industrial settings, connected to software systems that collect and analyse data to improve efficiency, reliability, and safety.

Applications

Common applications include predictive maintenance (detecting equipment failure before it occurs), asset tracking, process optimization, remote monitoring of pipelines and grids, and smart factories under the broader Industry 4.0 concept.

Challenges

IIoT deployments must address interoperability between legacy equipment and new systems, large data volumes, latency, and cybersecurity risks, since connecting industrial control systems to networks enlarges the attack surface.
