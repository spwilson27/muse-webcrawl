The tallest waterfalls in the world are listed below by total height. The tallest is Angel Falls in Venezuela, at 979 metres (3,212 ft), including the world's longest uninterrupted drop of 807 metres. Measurements of very tall, multi-tiered falls such as Tugela Falls in South Africa have been revised repeatedly, and sources differ on some rankings.

Tallest single-drop waterfalls

By single uninterrupted drop, Angel Falls (807 m) is again first, followed by the leap of the Tugela and by Kukenaam Falls in Venezuela and the Mardalsfossen in Norway, depending on the definition used.
