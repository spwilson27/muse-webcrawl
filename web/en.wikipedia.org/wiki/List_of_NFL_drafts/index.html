List of NFL drafts

The List of NFL drafts catalogues every annual player selection meeting held by the National Football League, beginning with the inaugural draft in 1936. The NFL draft is the league's principal mechanism for allocating incoming collegiate talent among its franchises, with the order of selection generally running inversely to the previous season's standings, the Super Bowl champion picking last.

History

The draft was the brainchild of Bert Bell, co-owner of the Philadelphia Eagles, who persuaded the league to adopt it in 1935 to curb the bidding wars for college stars and to spread talent more evenly. The first draft, held on February 8, 1936, selected Jay Berwanger of the University of Chicago as its first-ever pick; Berwanger never played professional football. Over the decades the event grew from a low-key hotel meeting into a multi-day televised spectacle drawing hundreds of thousands of fans.

Format

The modern draft consists of seven rounds spread over three days. Teams may trade picks, and compensatory selections are awarded to clubs that lose more free agents than they sign. Undrafted players become free agents and may sign with any team.
