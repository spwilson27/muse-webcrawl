Portland Timbers–Seattle Sounders rivalry

The Portland Timbers–Seattle Sounders rivalry is a soccer rivalry between the Portland Timbers and Seattle Sounders FC of Major League Soccer, and one of the oldest and fiercest in North American soccer. Together with the Vancouver Whitecaps, the clubs contest the Cascadia Cup.

History

The rivalry dates to the North American Soccer League, where the original Sounders and Timbers first met in 1975. It continued through the Western Soccer League and the USL First Division, marked by passionate supporter groups — Seattle's Emerald City Supporters and Portland's Timbers Army — elaborate tifo displays and occasional hostility. When Seattle joined MLS in 2009 and Portland followed in 2011, the derby became one of the league's marquee fixtures, drawing crowds of over 60,000 in Seattle.

Notable matches

Memorable meetings include the dramatic 2013 and 2018 MLS Cup Playoff series between the clubs, both decided in tense second legs, and a 2015 U.S. Open Cup match in which Seattle finished with seven players after multiple red cards, including one for Clint Dempsey tearing up the referee's notebook.

Culture

The rivalry reflects the broader civic competition between the Pacific Northwest's two largest cities and is characterised by the region's exceptionally large and organised supporter cultures.
