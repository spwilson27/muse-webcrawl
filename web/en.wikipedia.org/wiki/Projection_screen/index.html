Projection screen

A projection screen is an installation consisting of a surface and a support structure used to display a projected image for an audience. Screens are used in cinemas, classrooms, conference rooms and home theatres with film, slide, video and data projectors.

Types and materials

Screens may be rigid or flexible, fixed-frame, pull-down, tripod-mounted or inflatable. Surfaces are usually matte white or grey vinyl or woven fabric; the surface's gain describes how strongly it reflects light toward the viewer. Grey ("high contrast") screens improve black levels in rooms with ambient light, while perforated screens allow loudspeakers to be placed behind the picture in cinemas. Rear-projection screens are translucent and are lit from behind.

Geometry

Screen size and shape are matched to the projection format — historically 4:3 for slides and television, 16:9 for HDTV and widescreen cinema ratios such as 2.39:1. Viewing distance, projector brightness and throw ratio determine a suitable installation.
