List of first ladies of the United States

The first lady of the United States is the hostess of the White House, usually the wife of the president. The role is not an elected or official position and carries no salary, but first ladies have often become influential public figures and advocates for social causes. The first to hold the role was Martha Washington.

Eleanor Roosevelt, the longest-serving first lady, redefined the role through press conferences, a newspaper column and civil-rights advocacy. Hillary Clinton later became a U.S. senator, secretary of state and presidential nominee — the only first lady to hold elected federal office. Frances Cleveland remains the youngest first lady, marrying President Cleveland in the White House at 21.
