So You Think You Can Dance (American TV series)

So You Think You Can Dance
Genre: Reality competition
Created by: Simon Fuller, Nigel Lythgoe
Network: Fox
Premiered: 2005

So You Think You Can Dance is an American televised dance competition show in which contestants perform styles including contemporary, hip-hop, ballroom, and jazz before a panel of judges and a public vote. It premiered on Fox in July 2005.

Format

Dancers audition in major cities; selected contestants advance to a callback round and then to live shows, where they are paired with partners and choreographers in rotating styles. Weekly eliminations, determined by judges and viewer voting, narrow the field to a winner crowned "America's Favorite Dancer".

Reception

The series won multiple Emmy Awards, particularly for choreography, and launched international versions in numerous countries.
