Sawa is a location in the Gash-Barka region of western Eritrea, near the border with Sudan. It is best known as the site of the Sawa Defence Training Center, the principal induction and military training camp for Eritrea's national service programme.

National service

Under Eritrean law, all citizens are required to perform national service, formally eighteen months, beginning with military training at Sawa. Since 2003, students complete their final year of secondary schooling (Grade 12) at the Warsay-Yikealo School located at the Sawa compound, after which they sit the national school-leaving examination; those who do not proceed to higher education or vocational training typically continue in military service.

Criticism

The indefinite extension of national service beyond the statutory eighteen months has been widely criticised by human rights organisations and was a central issue examined by the United Nations Commission of Inquiry on Human Rights in Eritrea. The government defends the programme on grounds of national defence and development. Large numbers of young Eritreans have left the country, with avoidance of indefinite service frequently cited as a reason.

Geography

Sawa lies in Eritrea's arid western lowlands, an area of hot climate and seasonal watercourses draining toward the Gash River basin.
