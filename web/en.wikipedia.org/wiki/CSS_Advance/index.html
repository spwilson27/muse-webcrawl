CSS Advance

Full nameChild Support Scheme (CSS) Advance
CountryUnited Kingdom
Introduced1993
Administered byChild Support Agency

The CSS Advance, formally an advance payment under the United Kingdom's Child Support Scheme, was a mechanism associated with the child support system administered by the Child Support Agency (CSA) in the 1990s.

Under the scheme, arrangements existed for payments to parents with care to be advanced in certain circumstances while maintenance was being assessed or recovered from the non-resident parent.

Background

The Child Support Act 1991 created a statutory formula for child maintenance and established the Child Support Agency, which began work in 1993. The new system replaced court-based assessments and proved administratively troubled, with large backlogs and frequent calculation errors.

Operation

Advance arrangements were intended to prevent hardship where assessed maintenance had not yet been collected. The agency could then recover the advanced amounts from subsequent payments by the liable parent.

Reform

The child support system was repeatedly reformed, with a new gross-income scheme in 2003 and the eventual replacement of the CSA by the Child Maintenance Service in 2012.
