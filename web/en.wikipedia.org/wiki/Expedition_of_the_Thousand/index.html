Expedition of the Thousand

The Expedition of the Thousand (Italian: Spedizione dei Mille) was a military campaign undertaken in 1860 by a corps of volunteers led by Giuseppe Garibaldi during Italian unification, the Risorgimento. The Redshirts conquered the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, paving the way for the Kingdom of Italy.

Expedition of the Thousand
Date: 1860
Commander: Giuseppe Garibaldi
Result: Conquest of Two Sicilies

Course

About 1,089 volunteers sailed from Quarto near Genoa on 5 May 1860, landed at Marsala, Sicily, defeated Bourbon troops at Calatafimi, took Palermo, crossed to Calabria after Milazzo, entered Naples, and won at the Volturno. Garibaldi handed the south to Victor Emmanuel II at Teano.
