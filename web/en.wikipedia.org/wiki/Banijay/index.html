Banijay

Banijay Group is a French multinational television production and distribution company headquartered in Paris. Founded in 2008 by Stéphane Courbit, it grew through a long series of acquisitions to become one of the world's largest independent content producers, supplying formats and scripted series to broadcasters and streaming platforms in dozens of countries.

History

Banijay expanded rapidly by acquiring production companies across Europe, the Americas, and Asia-Pacific, including Zodiak Media in 2016. In 2020 it completed the acquisition of Endemol Shine Group in a deal valued at about €2 billion, creating a catalogue of more than 200,000 hours of programming.

Productions

The group's companies produce internationally known formats and series such as Big Brother, MasterChef, Survivor, Peaky Blinders, Black Mirror, and Wife Swap, along with local-language scripted dramas through labels including Kudos, Tiger Aspect, and Shine TV.

Operations

Banijay operates through more than 100 production labels in over 20 territories, combining format licensing, finished-tape distribution, and branded entertainment.
