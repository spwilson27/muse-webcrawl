SpindleHorse Toons

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

SpindleHorse Toons is an American animation studio founded by animator and artist Vivienne Medrano (known online as VivziePop). The studio is best known for producing the adult animated musical series Hazbin Hotel and Helluva Boss, both set in a shared version of Hell.

History

Medrano developed the Hazbin Hotel pilot while working independently, funding it through Patreon; the pilot was released on YouTube in October 2019 and became a viral success. Spin-off series Helluva Boss, produced by SpindleHorse and released on YouTube, began the same year. Hazbin Hotel was subsequently picked up as a full series by A24 and Amazon, premiering on Prime Video in January 2024.

Productions

The studio's credits include the Hazbin Hotel pilot and series, Helluva Boss, and the short Holidaze. Its productions are noted for their energetic, cartoon-influenced style, original musical numbers and independent, internet-based production model.
