The Canadian Championship (French: Championnat canadien) is an annual soccer tournament contested by Canadian professional teams. The winner is awarded the Voyageurs Cup and a berth in the CONCACAF Champions Cup. It is organized by the Canadian Soccer Association.

The tournament was inaugurated in 2008 and was initially contested by Canada's three professional clubs of the era: Toronto FC, the Montreal Impact (now CF Montréal), and Vancouver Whitecaps FC. Since 2019, the competition has included clubs from the Canadian Premier League, greatly expanding the field, along with the champions of League1 Ontario and the Première ligue de soccer du Québec.

History

Before 2008, Canada had no national cup competition for its professional clubs; the Voyageurs Cup had been awarded informally since 2002 by the Voyageurs supporters' group to the Canadian club with the best results in cross-border league play. The Canadian Soccer Association formalized the championship in 2008, and the trophy was transferred to the new tournament.

Format

The competition is a single-elimination knockout tournament, typically played over several rounds with two-legged ties in earlier editions and single matches in some rounds in later formats. Major League Soccer clubs generally enter in later rounds.

Toronto FC has been the most successful club in the competition's history, winning the trophy numerous times in its first decade.
