Model-based systems engineering

FieldSystems engineering
Key languageSysML
Promoting bodyINCOSE

Model-based systems engineering (MBSE) is an approach to systems engineering in which formalised models — rather than documents — are the primary means of capturing, analysing and communicating system information across the lifecycle, from requirements and architecture through design, analysis, verification and validation.

Approach

In MBSE, engineers build a coherent system model, typically in the Systems Modeling Language (SysML), capturing structure, behaviour, requirements and parametric relationships in a shared repository. Changes propagate consistently through views of the model, supporting traceability from stakeholder needs to components and tests, and enabling simulation and analysis earlier in development. The international systems engineering body INCOSE has promoted MBSE as the successor to document-centric practice in its "Systems Engineering Vision 2020" and later roadmaps.

Applications

MBSE is widely used in aerospace and defence programmes, automotive development, and other complex, software-intensive systems, where it underpins "digital engineering" strategies and the digital thread.
