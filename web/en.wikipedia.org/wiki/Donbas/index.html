Donbas

The Donbas is a historical, cultural and economic region in eastern Ukraine, formed around the Donets Coal Basin. Its name contracts Donets Basin. Major cities include Donetsk and Luhansk.

Geography and industry

Centred on the Kalmius and Donets rivers, the region developed in the late 19th century around coal mining and steel, drawing workers from across the Russian Empire and Soviet Union.

History

After 1991 the Donbas was Ukraine's industrial heartland. In 2014 Russian-backed separatists seized parts of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts; Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022 made the region a principal battlefield.

Demographics

The population is mixed Ukrainian and Russian, with Russian widely spoken; industrial cities grew around mines and metallurgical plants.
