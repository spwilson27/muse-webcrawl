List of islands by highest point

This is a list of islands by their highest point. It ranks islands of the world by the elevation of their highest summit above sea level. Island high points range from the volcanic summit of Puncak Jaya on New Guinea — the highest island peak on Earth — down to low coral atolls only a few metres above the ocean.
