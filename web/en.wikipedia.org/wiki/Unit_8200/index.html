Unit 8200

Unit 8200 (Hebrew: Yehida Shmone Matayim) is an Israeli Intelligence Corps unit responsible for signals intelligence (SIGINT), code decryption, and cyber warfare. It is the largest single unit in the Israel Defense Forces, often compared to the American NSA or Britain's GCHQ.

CountryIsrael
BranchIsraeli Intelligence Corps
RoleSIGINT, cyber warfare

History

The unit traces its origins to pre-state Haganah intelligence and was formalised after 1948, reorganised following the 1973 Yom Kippur War intelligence failures. It recruits many conscripts identified through high-school programmes in computing, mathematics, and Arabic.

Operations and influence

Unit 8200 has been reported — though never officially confirmed — to have cooperated with the NSA on the Stuxnet worm that damaged Iranian centrifuges. Its veterans have founded a remarkable share of Israeli tech companies and startups, including Check Point, Palo Alto Networks figures, NSO Group, and Waze contributors, making it a cornerstone of "Startup Nation." In 2023, reservists from the unit were prominent in protests over judicial reform, and after the 7 October 2023 Hamas attack its warning failures came under scrutiny.
