The Penguin (Oswald Chesterfield Cobblepot) is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an adversary of the superhero Batman. Created by Bob Kane and Bill Finger, he first appeared in Detective Comics #58 (1941) and is one of Batman's most enduring and recognisable enemies.

Characterisation

The Penguin is depicted as a short, portly man with a beak-like nose who dresses in formal attire — a tuxedo, top hat, and monocle — and wields umbrellas concealing weapons and gadgets. Unlike most of Batman's chaotic rogues, the Penguin is a sane, methodical crime boss who fancies himself a "gentleman of crime" and fronts his empire through the Iceberg Lounge nightclub. His aristocratic Cobblepot family background contrasts with Bruce Wayne's: Oswald was bullied for his appearance and his family's fall from high society embittered him toward Gotham's elite.

In other media

Burgess Meredith played the Penguin in the 1960s Batman television series, giving the character his trademark quacking laugh. Danny DeVito portrayed a darker, deformed version in Tim Burton's Batman Returns (1992). Robin Lord Taylor played a young Cobblepot in the series Gotham (2014–2019), and Colin Farrell portrayed him in The Batman (2022) and the spin-off series The Penguin (2024), the latter earning Farrell a Golden Globe Award.
