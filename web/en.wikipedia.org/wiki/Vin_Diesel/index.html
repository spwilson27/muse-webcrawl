Mark Sinclair (born 18 July 1967), known professionally as Vin Diesel, is an American actor and film producer. One of the world's highest-grossing actors, he is best known for playing Dominic Toretto in the Fast & Furious franchise. He also voices Groot in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and leads the XXX and Chronicles of Riddick franchises.

Early life

Sinclair was born in Alameda County, California, and raised in New York City by his mother, an astrologer and psychologist, and his stepfather, a theatre manager and acting instructor. He began acting on stage as a child at the Theater for the New City, and studied creative writing at Hunter College before leaving to pursue a film career in Los Angeles.

Career

After struggling to find roles, Diesel wrote, directed, and starred in the short film Multi-Facial (1995), which was screened at the Cannes Film Festival and led Steven Spielberg to cast him in Saving Private Ryan (1998). He gained leading-man status with Pitch Black (2000), The Fast and the Furious (2001), and XXX (2002), and became a producer on later instalments of his franchises through his company One Race Films. His distinctive deep voice brought him the role of the Iron Giant in the animated film of the same name (1999) and of Groot in Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) and subsequent Marvel films.

Personal life

Diesel is known for guarding his private life. He has three children with his partner, Mexican model Paloma Jiménez. He is an enthusiast of the role-playing game Dungeons & Dragons, which he has played since childhood and has credited with influencing his storytelling.
