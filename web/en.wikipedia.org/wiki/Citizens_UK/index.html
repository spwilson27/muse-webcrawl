Citizens UK

Founded: 1989 (as Citizen Organising Foundation)
Type: Community organising alliance
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom

Citizens UK is a community organising group in the United Kingdom. It is an alliance of local community organising groups in various cities in the UK, mostly based in London (London Citizens), Birmingham (Citizens UK Birmingham), Leeds (Leeds Citizens), Manchester (Greater Manchester Citizens), and others. It is best known for founding the Living Wage campaign in 2001, which led to the creation of the Living Wage Foundation and the independently calculated real Living Wage rate.

History

The organisation grew out of community organising work begun in east London in the late 1980s and 1990s, modelled in part on the Industrial Areas Foundation in the United States. Organisations such as The East London Citizens Organisation (TELCO), founded in 1996, brought together churches, mosques, schools, trade unions, and other civic institutions to campaign on local issues. In 2009–2010 the local alliances federated under the Citizens UK name. In the 2010 general election Citizens UK held a major assembly with the leaders of the three main parties shortly before polling day.

Living Wage campaign

The Living Wage campaign began in 2001 with organising among low-paid contract cleaners in Canary Wharf and east London hospitals. The campaign persuaded employers to pay a wage calculated to cover the basic cost of living, and led to the Living Wage Foundation, which accredits employers paying the real Living Wage. Thousands of employers have since been accredited, and in 2016 the UK government introduced a higher statutory minimum wage for workers aged 25 and over (later extended to younger workers) which it named the “National Living Wage”, though this is a distinct, lower rate not endorsed by the Foundation as equivalent.

Other campaigns

Citizens UK has campaigned on issues including refugee resettlement (including the community sponsorship of refugees), mental health services, affordable housing, hate crime, and workers' rights in the care sector. During the COVID-19 pandemic it campaigned on sick pay and real Living Wage payment for care workers.
