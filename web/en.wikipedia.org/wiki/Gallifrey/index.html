Gallifrey

Gallifrey is a fictional planet in the BBC science-fiction series Doctor Who. It is the home world of the Time Lords, including the Doctor, located in the constellation of Kasterborous. Gallifrey is depicted with orange skies, red grass and silver-leafed trees, its capital Citadel enclosed in a glass dome.


First named: The Time Warrior (1973–74)
Inhabitants: Time Lords / Gallifreyans
Capital: The Citadel

In the series

First seen in The War Games (1969) and named in 1973, Gallifrey became central to the show's mythology: the Time Lords' non-interference policy, Rassilon and Omega's stellar engineering, and the Eye of Harmony. In the revived series the planet was said to be destroyed in the Last Great Time War against the Daleks; the 2013 fiftieth-anniversary special The Day of the Doctor revealed it survived, frozen in a pocket universe, before later storylines depicted its fall again.
