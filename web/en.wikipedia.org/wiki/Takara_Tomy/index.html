Takara Tomy Co., Ltd. (株式会社タカラトミー, Kabushiki-gaisha Takara Tomī), trading as Tomy Company, Ltd. outside Japan, is a Japanese entertainment company that makes children's toys and merchandise. It was formed on March 1, 2006 from the merger of two historic toy makers: Takara (founded 1955) and Tomy (founded 1924 as Tomiyama). The company is one of Japan's largest toy manufacturers.

History

Tomy began in 1924 making tin toys and became famous for die-cast Tomica miniature cars (launched 1970) and the Plarail toy train system (1959). Takara, founded in 1955, created the Licca-chan fashion doll (1967), the Choro-Q "Penny Racer" cars, and toy lines that were combined in the United States to create Hasbro's Transformers franchise (1984), alongside Microman and Diaclone. Financial difficulties at Takara in the 2000s led to the merger with its larger rival Tomy.

Products and brands

Takara Tomy holds or shares major Japanese licences and original brands including Transformers (with Hasbro), Beyblade, Zoids, Tomica, Plarail, Licca-chan, Duel Masters, the Japanese Pokémon merchandise licence (through Pokémon-related licensing arrangements), Pretty Series arcade games (including PriPara and Kiratto Pri Chan), and the Wixoss trading card game. Subsidiaries include T-ARTS (capsule toys and arcade prizes) and Tomy Tec (railway and military models under the Tomix brand). The company also produces board games, baby products and gadgets such as the i-SOBOT robot.
