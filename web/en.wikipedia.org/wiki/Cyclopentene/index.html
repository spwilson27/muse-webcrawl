Cyclopentene

Cyclopentene
Formula: C5H8
Appearance: colourless liquid
Boiling point: 44 °C

Cyclopentene is a cycloalkene with the chemical formula C5H8. It is a colourless liquid with a petrol-like odour and a five-membered carbon ring containing one double bond.

Properties and production

Cyclopentene is produced industrially by catalytic cracking of naphtha and by partial hydrogenation of cyclopentadiene. The double bond sits in a nearly planar portion of the ring, with the remaining carbons puckered out of plane. It is highly flammable and stored with stabilisers to prevent peroxide formation and polymerisation.

Uses

Cyclopentene is used as a monomer and comonomer in polymer chemistry, notably in ring-opening metathesis polymerisation to produce polypentenamers, and as an intermediate in the synthesis of fine chemicals and agrochemicals.
