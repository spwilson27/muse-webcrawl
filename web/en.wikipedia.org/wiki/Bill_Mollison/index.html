Bill Mollison

Bruce Charles "Bill" Mollison (4 May 1928 – 24 September 2016) was an Australian researcher, author, scientist, teacher and biologist. He is best known as the co-creator, with David Holmgren, of permaculture.

Bill Mollison

Life and work

Born in Stanley, Tasmania, Mollison worked in many trades before studying psychology and teaching at the University of Tasmania. His fieldwork in rainforests and ecosystems inspired sustainable design ideas.

Permaculture

With Holmgren he published Permaculture One (1978) and Permaculture Two (1979), and later the comprehensive Permaculture: A Designers' Manual (1988). He founded the Permaculture Institute and taught courses worldwide, helping spread the design system for regenerative agriculture and settlements.

Recognition

He received the Right Livelihood Award in 1981. He died in Hobart in 2016.
