Terry Nichols

Terry Lynn Nichols (born April 1, 1955) is an American domestic terrorist who was convicted as an accomplice of Timothy McVeigh in the Oklahoma City bombing of April 19, 1995, which destroyed the Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building and killed 168 people.

Background and trial

Nichols, a Michigan farmer and former U.S. Army soldier, met McVeigh in the Army and shared his anti-government views. He helped McVeigh acquire and store materials for the truck bomb, including ammonium nitrate fertiliser and fuel. In 1997 a federal jury convicted Nichols of conspiracy and eight counts of involuntary manslaughter of federal officers; he was sentenced to life in prison without parole. In 2004 an Oklahoma state trial convicted him of 161 counts of first-degree murder, again resulting in life sentences.

Imprisonment

Nichols is incarcerated at the federal supermax prison ADX Florence in Colorado.
