Ra's al Ghul is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an adversary of Batman. Created by writer Denny O'Neil and artist Neal Adams, he first appeared in Batman #232 (1971). His name derives from Arabic for "Head of the Ghoul" or "Head of the Demon".

Character

Ra's al Ghul is the centuries-old leader of the League of Assassins, sustained by the restorative Lazarus Pits. A brilliant strategist and eco-terrorist visionary, he seeks to restore balance to the world by drastically reducing its human population, and has repeatedly tried to recruit Batman — whom he calls "the Detective" — as his heir. His daughter Talia al Ghul is a recurring ally and adversary of Batman, and his grandson is Damian Wayne, the fifth Robin.

In other media

The character was played by Liam Neeson in Christopher Nolan's Batman Begins (2005) and by Matt Nable in the television series Arrow, among many other portrayals.
