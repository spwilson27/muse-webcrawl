Kurt Travis

BornJanuary 16, 1984
OriginSacramento, California, U.S.
GenresPost-hardcore, math rock, experimental rock

Kurt Travis is an American singer and musician best known as a former vocalist of the post-hardcore band Dance Gavin Dance and as a member of A Lot Like Birds and Royal Coda.

Career

Travis joined Dance Gavin Dance in 2007, replacing Jonny Craig, and sang on the albums Dance Gavin Dance (2008) and Happiness (2009) before leaving in 2010. He later fronted A Lot Like Birds until 2016 and co-founded Royal Coda in 2017 with members of A Lot Like Birds and Stolas. He has also released solo material, including the EPs Wha Happen? (2012) and full-length solo work, and has collaborated with numerous artists in the progressive post-hardcore scene.
