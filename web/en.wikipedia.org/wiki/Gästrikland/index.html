Gästrikland
Country: Sweden
Region: Norrland
Largest city: Gävle
Area: approx. 4,200 km²

Gästrikland is a historical province (landskap) in the eastern part of central Sweden, in the region of Norrland. Its principal city is Gävle, and it borders Uppland, Västmanland, Dalarna, and Hälsingland, with a coastline on the Gulf of Bothnia.

Geography and economy

The province is known for iron mining and steel production, especially in Sandviken and Hofors, and for the port of Gävle. The terrain mixes coastal lowlands with forested interior.

Culture

Gästrikland is famous for the Gävle goat, a giant straw goat erected each Christmas season, and for its historical ironworks villages. The provincial flower is the lily of the valley.
