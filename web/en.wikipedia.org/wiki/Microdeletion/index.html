Microdeletion

A microdeletion is a chromosomal deletion smaller than 5 million base pairs spanning several genes that is too small to be detected by conventional cytogenetic methods such as karyotyping.

Detection

Microdeletions are detected by specialised techniques including fluorescence in situ hybridisation (FISH), multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification and chromosomal microarray.

Examples

Well-known microdeletion syndromes include 22q11.2 deletion syndrome (DiGeorge syndrome), Williams syndrome (7q11.23), Prader–Willi and Angelman syndromes (15q11–13), and Smith–Magenis syndrome. Clinical features depend on which genes are lost in the deleted segment.
