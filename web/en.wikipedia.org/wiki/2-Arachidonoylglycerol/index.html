TypeEndocannabinoid

2-Arachidonoylglycerol (2-AG) is an endocannabinoid, an endogenous ligand of the CB1 and CB2 cannabinoid receptors. It is an ester formed from arachidonic acid and glycerol. Together with anandamide, it is one of the two best-characterised signalling lipids of the endocannabinoid system.

Physiology

2-AG is present at relatively high levels in the central nervous system and acts as a full agonist at both cannabinoid receptors. It is synthesised on demand from arachidonic acid-containing membrane phospholipids, principally by the enzyme diacylglycerol lipase, and is degraded mainly by monoacylglycerol lipase. It functions as a retrograde synaptic messenger, suppressing neurotransmitter release, and modulates appetite, pain perception, mood, memory, and the immune system. Concentrations of 2-AG in rodent brain are much higher than those of anandamide.
