Spanish Aerocar

The Spanish Aerocar (officially the Whirlpool Aero Car) is a cable car that carries passengers over the Niagara Whirlpool on the Niagara River, between points in Ontario, Canada. In operation since 1916, it was designed by the Spanish engineer Leonardo Torres Quevedo — hence its name — and built by a Spanish company.

Design

The open car is suspended from six interlocked steel cables spanning about 540 metres (1,770 feet) between two Canadian terminals; because the crossing begins and ends in Canada while passing over the river border, passengers technically cross the United States border without entering the country. The car carries up to 35 standing passengers on a round trip of roughly one kilometre, suspended some 60 metres above the rapids. The system was modernised several times, with the car and cables periodically replaced while retaining the original design.

Operation

The attraction, operated by the Niagara Parks Commission, runs seasonally (roughly spring to autumn) and offers views of the Class 6 Whirlpool Rapids and the Niagara Gorge. It has carried millions of passengers without a fatal accident.
