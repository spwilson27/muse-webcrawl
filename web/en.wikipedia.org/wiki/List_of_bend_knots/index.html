List of bend knots

A bend is a knot used to join two lengths of rope together. This list collects well-known bends described in knot references such as The Ashley Book of Knots, in which each knot is assigned an ABOK reference number. See Bend (knot) for the general concept.

Notable bends

  • Sheet bend — joins ropes of equal or unequal diameter; a doubled version adds security.
  • Double sheet bend — a more secure form of the sheet bend.
  • Fisherman's knot and double fisherman's knot — two (doubled) overhand knots drawn together; compact but can jam.
  • Flemish bend (figure-eight bend) — a strong bend traced from the figure-eight knot.
  • Carrick bend — a decorative, easily untied bend used for heavy lines.
  • Zeppelin bend — a secure, jam-resistant bend valued in mountaineering.
  • Alpine butterfly bend — bend version of the butterfly loop.
  • Square knot (reef knot) — widely known but unsafe as a load-bearing bend.
  • Blood knot — used in angling to join fishing lines.
  • Water knot — used to join webbing in climbing.
