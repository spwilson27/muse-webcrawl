Suzuka 8 Hours

The Suzuka 8 Hours is a motorcycle endurance race held at the Suzuka Circuit in Japan since 1978. It has been a round of the FIM Endurance World Championship and is considered the most prestigious endurance motorcycle race in Japan, attracting factory teams from the major Japanese manufacturers.

History

The race was founded in 1978 and quickly became the centrepiece of the Japanese motorcycle racing calendar. In its early decades it drew top Grand Prix riders, with stars such as Wayne Gardner, Mick Doohan and Valentino Rossi among the winners.

Format

Teams of two or three riders share a single production-based superbike over eight hours, starting with a Le Mans-style sprint to the machines. The race traditionally finishes in darkness, adding to its spectacle.

Significance

Victory at Suzuka carries enormous prestige for Honda, Yamaha, Suzuki and Kawasaki, and the event anchors Japan's summer racing season alongside a major music festival atmosphere around the circuit.
