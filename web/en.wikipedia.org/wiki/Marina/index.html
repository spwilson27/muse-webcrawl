Marina

A marina is a specially designed harbour or dock with moorings for small recreational boats and yachts. Marinas typically provide facilities such as fuel, water, electricity, washing and repair services, as well as restaurants, shops and other amenities for boaters.

Facilities

Modern marinas offer floating docks or pontoons, slipways, boat hoists, chandleries, and shore-side services. Many also provide dry storage, winter storage, and facilities for liveaboard residents.

History

The development of marinas grew alongside recreational boating in the 20th century, particularly after the Second World War, as fibreglass boats became affordable to a wider public.
