Durmitor

Durmitor is a mountain massif in northwestern Montenegro, the centrepiece of Durmitor National Park. It comprises dozens of peaks above 2,000 metres, the highest being Bobotov Kuk (2,523 m), and is celebrated for its glacial lakes—known locally as gorske oči, "mountain eyes"—of which the Black Lake (Crno jezero) near the town of Žabljak is the largest and most visited.

Geology and landscape

Part of the Dinaric Alps, Durmitor is a limestone massif carved by glaciers and rivers into canyons, plateaus and karst features. The Tara River Canyon on its northern edge, up to 1,300 metres deep, is one of the deepest river canyons in Europe and is protected within the park. The park covers some 390 square kilometres and supports pine forests, alpine meadows and fauna including brown bears, wolves and chamois.

Tourism

Žabljak, the highest town in the Balkans, is the park's gateway, offering skiing in winter and hiking, mountaineering and rafting on the Tara in summer. The park was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 1980 for its striking landscapes and biodiversity.
