Drona

Other names: Dronacharya
Spouse: Kripi
Son: Ashvatthama
Role: Royal guru; Kaurava commander-in-chief

Drona (Sanskrit: द्रोण), also called Dronacharya, is a major character of the Hindu epic Mahabharata — the royal preceptor (guru) who taught military arts and archery to both the Kaurava and Pandava princes.

Life

Born to the sage Bharadwaja, Drona learned warfare from Parashurama but lived in poverty until he became tutor at Hastinapura. His greatest pupil was Arjuna; he controversially demanded the thumb of the self-taught tribal archer Ekalavya as guru dakshina to keep Arjuna unrivalled. Humiliated in youth by King Drupada, Drona used the Kuru princes to defeat him and took half his kingdom.

Kurukshetra War

Bound by duty to Hastinapura, Drona fought for the Kauravas and became their commander-in-chief after Bhishma fell. Nearly unstoppable, he was killed on the fifteenth day after a ruse — the false report that "Ashvatthama is dead" (an elephant of that name had been killed) — caused him to lay down his weapons in grief, whereupon Dhrishtadyumna beheaded him.
