SESAC, Inc. is an American performance rights organisation (PRO) headquartered in Nashville, Tennessee. It licenses the public performance of the music of its affiliated songwriters and publishers, collecting royalties from broadcasters, streaming services, venues and other music users and distributing them to its members.

History

SESAC was founded in 1930 by German immigrant Paul Heinecke as the Society of European Stage Authors and Composers, initially representing European composers whose works were performed in the United States. It is the second-oldest performance rights organisation in the US, after ASCAP (founded 1914) and predating BMI (1939). Unlike ASCAP and BMI, which are open to all songwriters, SESAC operates on an invitation-only basis and is a for-profit company.

SESAC represents songwriters including Bob Dylan, Neil Diamond, Adele (for US performances), Rush and many writers in country, jazz, hip-hop and Latin music. In 2017, funds managed by the Blackstone Group acquired SESAC. The organisation has expanded internationally and into mechanical licensing through its acquisition of the Harry Fox Agency in 2015.
