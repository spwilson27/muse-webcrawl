The UCLA Bruins are the athletic teams that represent the University of California, Los Angeles. The Bruin men's and women's teams participate in NCAA Division I as part of the Big Ten Conference, which UCLA joined in 2024 after decades in the Pac-12 Conference. UCLA is one of the most successful collegiate athletic programs in the United States, with more than 120 NCAA team championships.

Basketball

The men's basketball program is the most decorated in NCAA history, with 11 national championships, including 10 under legendary coach John Wooden between 1964 and 1975, a run that featured seven consecutive titles. Home games are played at Pauley Pavilion. The women's program has also reached the Final Four in the modern era.

Other sports

UCLA fields nationally prominent teams in volleyball, gymnastics, softball, baseball, soccer, and water polo. Bruin gymnastics has produced Olympic champions, and the softball team is among the most successful in NCAA history. The football team plays its home games at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena and claims a share of the 1954 national championship.

Rivalries

The Bruins' principal rival is the USC Trojans, a crosstown rivalry contested across all sports, with the football meeting for the Victory Bell among the most prominent games in college football.
