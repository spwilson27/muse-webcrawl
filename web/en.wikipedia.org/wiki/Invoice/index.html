Issued bySeller / service provider
Sent toBuyer / client
Typical termsNet 30, Net 60, 2/10 Net 30

An invoice, bill or tab is a commercial document issued by a seller to a buyer relating to a sale transaction, indicating the products, quantities and agreed prices for products or services the seller has provided. The buyer is expected to pay the amount shown by the due date specified on the invoice; payment terms such as "Net 30" require payment within thirty days, while terms like "2/10 Net 30" offer a 2 percent discount for payment within ten days.

Contents

A typical invoice includes the word "invoice", a unique reference number, the date of issue, the names and contact details of seller and buyer, a description of the goods or services, quantities and unit prices, applicable taxes (such as VAT or sales tax), the total amount due, and the payment terms and methods. A pro forma invoice is a preliminary bill sent before goods are shipped or services rendered, often used in international trade for customs purposes. An invoice differs from a receipt, which is proof that payment has been made.

Electronic invoicing

Electronic invoicing (e-invoicing) replaces paper documents with structured digital formats such as EDI, UBL or PDF files exchanged by email or networks such as PEPPOL. Many jurisdictions now mandate e-invoicing for tax compliance, particularly for VAT, to reduce fraud and administrative cost. In accounting systems, issued invoices create accounts receivable for the seller and accounts payable for the buyer.
