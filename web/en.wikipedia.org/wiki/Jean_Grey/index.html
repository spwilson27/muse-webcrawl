Jean Elaine Grey is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in The X-Men #1 (September 1963). Jean is a mutant born with telepathic and telekinetic powers, and was one of the five original X-Men recruited by Professor Charles Xavier. She has been known by the codenames Marvel Girl, Phoenix, and Dark Phoenix.

Fictional character biography

Jean Grey's telepathic powers emerged traumatically in childhood when she witnessed the death of her friend Annie Richardson. Professor Xavier helped her control her abilities and later recruited her to his school, where she fell in love with teammate Scott Summers (Cyclops), whom she eventually married.

During a space mission, Jean was bonded with the Phoenix Force, a cosmic entity of immense power, granting her vastly amplified abilities. The Dark Phoenix Saga (1980), by Chris Claremont and John Byrne, depicted her corruption by that power, the destruction of an inhabited star system, and her suicide to prevent further catastrophe — a storyline widely regarded as one of the greatest in comics. The character was later revealed to have survived, the Phoenix having acted in her form while she healed in a cocoon, and she has died and returned several times since.

Powers and abilities

Jean Grey is an Omega-level mutant. Her telepathy allows her to read and project thoughts, control minds, and create psychic illusions; her telekinesis can move objects from the microscopic to the massive. As host of the Phoenix Force, her powers reach cosmic scale, including energy manipulation, resurrection, and flight through space.

In other media

Jean Grey has appeared in X-Men animated series and in the 20th Century Fox X-Men film series, portrayed by Famke Janssen in the original trilogy and by Sophie Turner in X-Men: Apocalypse (2016) and Dark Phoenix (2019).
