Evonik Industries AG is a German specialty chemicals company headquartered in Essen, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany. It is one of the world's largest specialty chemicals manufacturers, with activities in more than 100 countries and annual sales in the region of €15–18 billion. Its products include amino acids for animal nutrition, silica and carbon black, methacrylates and other performance materials, superabsorbents, and active ingredients for pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and coatings.

History

Evonik was created in 2006 when the mining and technology group RAG separated its chemicals, energy, and real-estate businesses; the chemicals business, which included the former Degussa company (whose roots go back to the 19th century) and Steag, was renamed Evonik Industries in September 2007. The RAG Foundation became the principal shareholder. After several postponed flotations, Evonik's shares were listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in April 2013, and the company subsequently focused its portfolio on specialty chemicals, divesting businesses such as its methacrylates unit (sold in 2019) and acquiring speciality additives operations including parts of Air Products (2017) and the silica business of Huber (2017).

Business

Evonik organises its operations into divisions covering specialty additives, nutrition and care, smart materials, and performance materials. It is the world's largest producer of the feed amino acid DL-methionine, marketed under the name MetAMINO, and a major supplier of precipitated and fumed silica used in tyres, toothpaste, and many industrial products. The company runs major production sites in Germany (including Marl and Hanau-Wolfgang), Belgium, the United States, China, and Singapore.
