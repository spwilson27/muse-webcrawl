Turkish War of Independence

The Turkish War of Independence (1919–1923) was fought between the Turkish National Movement under Mustafa Kemal (Atatürk) and the occupying Allied powers and their proxies after the Ottoman Empire's defeat in World War I and the partition envisaged by the Treaty of Sèvres.

Turkish War of Independence
Date19 May 1919 – 24 July 1923
ResultTurkish victory; Treaty of Lausanne; Republic of Turkey founded

Course

Kemal landed at Samsun on 19 May 1919; the Grand National Assembly opened at Ankara in 1920. Turkish forces fought France in Cilicia, Armenia in the east, and Greece in the west, culminating in the Battle of Sakarya (1921) and the Great Offensive (1922) that retook Smyrna (İzmir).

Outcome

The Treaty of Lausanne (1923) recognised Turkish sovereignty; the Republic was proclaimed 29 October 1923 with Atatürk as president. See Mustafa Kemal Atatürk.
