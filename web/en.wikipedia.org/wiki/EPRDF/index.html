EPRDF

The Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front (EPRDF) was an ethnic federalist political coalition in Ethiopia that existed from 1988 to 2019. The EPRDF came to power after defeating the Derg in the Ethiopian Civil War and dominated Ethiopian politics under Meles Zenawi.

EPRDF
Founded: 1988
Dissolved: 1 December 2019
Successor: Prosperity Party
Ideology: Ethnic federalism

History

The front was formed by the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) together with allied movements. After 1991 it led the transitional government and won successive elections, criticised by observers as authoritarian.

Dissolution

Under Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, most member parties merged into the Prosperity Party in 2019; the TPLF refused to join, a split that preceded the Tigray War of 2020-2022.
