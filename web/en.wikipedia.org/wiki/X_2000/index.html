X 2000

The X 2000, also called SJ X2000 or X2, is an express tilting train operated by SJ in Sweden. It was introduced in 1990 to provide faster services on the country's curving main lines; its car bodies tilt up to about 6.5 degrees in curves, allowing speeds roughly 30 per cent higher than conventional trains. The trains reach a top commercial speed of 200 km/h (124 mph) and a design/test speed of 276 km/h.

Services

X 2000 trains were launched on the Stockholm–Gothenburg route and later operated to Malmö and Copenhagen (as Linx joint services to Oslo and Copenhagen for a time), and to other Swedish destinations. A single trainset was leased to Amtrak in 1992–93 for demonstration runs on the Northeast Corridor, and another was exported to China, where it operated as the "Xinshisu" on the Guangzhou–Shenzhen line before returning to Sweden.

Rolling stock

Each trainset consists of a power car (with a driver's cab at the opposite end car), and intermediate trailers with radial-steering bogies and active tilt. Between 2014 and the mid-2020s the fleet underwent a major refurbishment programme (the "X2000 upgrade") with new interiors, traction equipment and seats, extending its service life.
