Temu

Temu is an online marketplace operated by PDD Holdings, the parent company of Chinese e-commerce platform Pinduoduo. Launched in the United States in September 2022, it sells heavily discounted consumer goods shipped largely directly from manufacturers in China. Its slogan is "Shop like a billionaire".

LaunchedSeptember 2022
ParentPDD Holdings
TypeOnline marketplace

Business model

Temu connects consumers directly with manufacturers, using aggressive marketing, gamified promotions and low prices subsidised by its parent. It expanded rapidly into dozens of markets in 2023 and became one of the most downloaded shopping apps in the US, advertising at the Super Bowl.

Criticism

Temu has faced criticism over product quality, use of the US de minimis customs exemption, labour practices in its supply chain, aggressive data collection concerns and disputes with rival Shein.
