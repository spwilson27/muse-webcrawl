Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe Party

The Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe Party (ALDE Party) is a European political party composed of liberal and liberal-democratic national parties from across Europe. It is one of the main party families of the European Union, advocating individual liberty, market economics, civil rights and European integration.

History

The federation was founded in 1976 as the Federation of Liberal and Democratic Parties in Europe, ahead of the first direct elections to the European Parliament, and later became the European Liberal Democrat and Reform Party (ELDR). In 2012 it adopted the ALDE name, aligning itself with its parliamentary group.

In the European institutions

Member parties' MEPs sit mainly in the Renew Europe group of the European Parliament, the successor to the ALDE group, formed in 2019 with Emmanuel Macron's Renaissance. ALDE member parties have supplied several prime ministers in EU member states and members of the European Commission. Youth organisation: LYMEC; affiliated foundation: the European Liberal Forum.
