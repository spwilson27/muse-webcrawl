Leia Organa, also known as Princess Leia and later General Leia Organa, is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, portrayed by Carrie Fisher. One of the principal characters of the original trilogy, she is the princess of the planet Alderaan, a member of the Imperial Senate, and a leader of the Rebel Alliance; she later becomes a leading stateswoman of the New Republic and the general commanding the Resistance.

Appearances

Original trilogy

Leia is introduced in Star Wars (1977) as the custodian of the stolen Death Star plans, which she hides in the droid R2-D2 before her capture by Darth Vader. Rescued by Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, and Obi-Wan Kenobi, she escapes and helps direct the attack that destroys the Death Star. In The Empire Strikes Back (1980) she commands the Rebel base on Hoth and falls in love with Han Solo; in Return of the Jedi (1983) she infiltrates Jabba the Hutt's palace to free Solo, and it is revealed that she is Luke's twin sister and the daughter of Anakin Skywalker.

Sequel trilogy

In The Force Awakens (2015), Leia is the general leading the Resistance against the First Order; her son with Han Solo, Ben Solo, has fallen to the dark side as Kylo Ren. She trains in the Force under Luke in The Rise of Skywalker (2019), and dies reaching out through the Force to her son, an act that helps precipitate his return to the light. The character also appears as an infant at the end of Revenge of the Sith (2005), born to Padmé Amidala and adopted by Bail and Breha Organa of Alderaan.

Cultural impact

Leia is one of the most enduring characters in popular cinema. Her hairstyle in the 1977 film — hair coiled in two side buns — is among the most imitated in film history, and her gold bikini costume in Return of the Jedi became a famous, if debated, pop-culture image. Critics have long noted Leia as an unusually capable action heroine for her era: a political leader, military commander, and crack shot. Fisher's own wit and public candour made the portrayal a cultural touchstone, and following her death in 2016 the character's farewell in The Rise of Skywalker, assembled from unused footage, was received as a tribute.
