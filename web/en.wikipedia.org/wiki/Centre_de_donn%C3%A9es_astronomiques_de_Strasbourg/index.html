Centre de données astronomiques de Strasbourg

The Centre de données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS; Strasbourg Astronomical Data Centre) is a data centre dedicated to the collection and worldwide distribution of astronomical information. It is located at the Strasbourg Observatory in France and was founded in 1972.

Founded1972
LocationStrasbourg Observatory, France
ServicesSIMBAD, VizieR, Aladin

Services

The CDS operates several services used worldwide by astronomers: SIMBAD, a database of astronomical objects beyond the Solar System; VizieR, a catalogue service giving access to thousands of published astronomical catalogues and tables; and Aladin, an interactive sky atlas allowing users to visualise digitised astronomical images and overlay data from many sources. The CDS also participates in the Virtual Observatory movement, promoting standardised access to astronomical data.
