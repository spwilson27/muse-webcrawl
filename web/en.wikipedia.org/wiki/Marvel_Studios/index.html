Marvel Studios, LLC is an American film and television production company, a subsidiary of Walt Disney Studios, known for producing the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) — the highest-grossing film franchise of all time.

History

Founded in 1993 as Marvel Films within the Marvel Entertainment Group, the studio initially licensed Marvel Comics characters to other studios, leading to hits such as X-Men and Spider-Man. In 2005, under executive Kevin Feige, the studio raised independent financing to produce its own films with full creative control. Its first self-produced film, Iron Man (2008), launched the interconnected MCU, built around post-credits scenes and multi-phase story arcs.

The Marvel Cinematic Universe

The Walt Disney Company acquired Marvel Entertainment in 2009 for about $4 billion. The MCU has since spanned dozens of films — including The Avengers (2012), Black Panther (2018), and Avengers: Endgame (2019), among the highest-grossing films ever made — and, from 2021, television series on Disney+ beginning with WandaVision. The studio's output is organized into "Phases" grouped into sagas such as the Infinity Saga and the Multiverse Saga.

Operations

Marvel Studios is headquartered in Burbank, California, with production frequently based in Atlanta, Georgia, and in the United Kingdom.
