Market segmentation

Market segmentation is the process of dividing a broad consumer or business market into sub-groups of consumers (segments) based on shared characteristics, so that products and marketing can be tailored to each group. It is a core element of marketing strategy, together with targeting and positioning.

Bases for segmentation

Common bases include geographic (region, climate, density), demographic (age, income, gender, occupation), psychographic (lifestyle, values, personality) and behavioural variables (usage rate, loyalty, benefits sought, occasion).

Criteria

Effective segments should be measurable, accessible, substantial, differentiable and actionable. A firm may pursue undifferentiated (mass) marketing, differentiated marketing to several segments, concentrated (niche) marketing, or micromarketing down to the individual customer.
