Howard G. "Ward" Cunningham (born 1949) is an American computer programmer who invented the wiki. He began developing the WikiWikiWeb software in 1994 and installed it on his website in March 1995, creating the first wiki as part of the Portland Pattern Repository — a resource for the design patterns community.

Career

Cunningham worked at Tektronix and Wyatt Software before co-founding Cunningham & Cunningham, Inc. He was a key figure in the patterns movement and an early influence on Extreme Programming and agile software development; with Kent Beck he helped develop CRC cards. He later served as director of Committer Community Development at the Eclipse Foundation and worked at AboutUs and the Wikimedia Foundation.

Legacy

He named the wiki after the Hawaiian word wiki, meaning "quick", recalling the Wiki Wiki shuttle bus at Honolulu airport. His idea of a web that any visitor could edit directly inspired Wikipedia and countless collaborative platforms. "Cunningham's Law" — the tongue-in-cheek observation that the best way to get the right answer on the Internet is to post the wrong answer — is attributed to him.
