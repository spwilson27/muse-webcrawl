Sideswipe (Transformers)

Sideswipe is a fictional character in the Transformers franchise. An Autobot warrior, he is usually depicted as the twin brother of Sunstreaker and transforms into a sports car — originally a red Lamborghini Countach. Dashing, reckless, and fiercely loyal, Sideswipe loves combat and is known for his jetpack and pile-driver arms.

Generation 1

Sideswipe was among the original 1984 Autobots in the toy line and animated series. His tech-spec profile describes him as a brave but rash warrior who "doesn't take orders well" yet follows Optimus Prime unconditionally. In the animated series he occasionally lent his rocket pack to Optimus Prime.

Live-action films

Sideswipe appears in Transformers: Revenge of the Fallen (2009) and Transformers: Dark of the Moon (2011), transforming into a silver Chevrolet Corvette Stingray concept. In the films he is an agile bladesman who moves on wheeled feet and fights with twin arm-mounted swords, famously slicing the Decepticon Sideways in two during the Shanghai battle.

Other appearances

The character has appeared in numerous comic series from Marvel, Dreamwave, and IDW Publishing, and in later animated series and video games, sometimes spelled "Side Swipe" for trademark reasons.
