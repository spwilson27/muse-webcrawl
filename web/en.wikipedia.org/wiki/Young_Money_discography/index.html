Young Money discography

The discography of Young Money, an American record label founded by rapper Lil Wayne, consists of compilation albums, singles and music videos released by the label and its collective of artists. Young Money Entertainment was founded in 2005 as an imprint of Cash Money Records, distributed by Universal Motown and later Republic Records.

The label's roster at its commercial peak included Lil Wayne, Drake, Nicki Minaj, Tyga, Mack Maine, Gudda Gudda, Jae Millz, Cory Gunz, Lil Twist, Shanell, Christina Milian and Austin Mahone. Its artists collectively sold tens of millions of records, and Drake and Nicki Minaj became two of the best-selling music artists of the 2010s.

Singles

Label singles include "Every Girl" (2009), "BedRock" (2009), "Roger That" (2010), "Tapout" (2013, as Rich Gang), "We Alright" (2014) and "Senile" (2014). The label's artists' solo releases — including Drake's "Best I Ever Had" and Nicki Minaj's "Super Bass" — are catalogued in the individual artists' discographies.
