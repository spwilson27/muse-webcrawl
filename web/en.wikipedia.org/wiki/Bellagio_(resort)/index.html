Bellagio is a resort, luxury hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada. It is owned by Blackstone Real Estate and operated by MGM Resorts International. Inspired by the Lake Como town of Bellagio in Italy, the resort was conceived by Steve Wynn and built on the site of the demolished Dunes hotel-casino at a cost of about US$1.6 billion — then the most expensive hotel ever built. It opened on 15 October 1998.

Features

The resort's centrepiece is an 8.5-acre artificial lake between the building and the Strip, home to the Fountains of Bellagio, a choreographed water, light and music show performed afternoons and evenings. Inside, the lobby ceiling displays Fiori di Como, a Dale Chihuly glass sculpture of more than 2,000 hand-blown flowers. Other attractions include the Bellagio Gallery of Fine Art, a botanical conservatory with seasonal floral displays, the Cirque du Soleil aquatic production O, fine-dining restaurants such as Le Cirque and Picasso, and a high-limit poker room long home to the "Big Game".

History

Bellagio set a new standard for luxury in Las Vegas and was credited with accelerating the Strip's shift toward upscale tourism. Mirage Resorts merged into MGM in 2000, bringing the property under MGM Resorts. MGM sold the real estate to a Blackstone-led venture in 2019 for about $4.25 billion, leasing the resort back. In December 2023 a major renovation of the main tower rooms was completed. The resort featured prominently in the film Ocean's Eleven (2001).
