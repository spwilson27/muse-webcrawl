Living Wage Foundation

Living Wage Foundation
Founded: 2011
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom
Type: Charity initiative
Parent: Citizens UK

The Living Wage Foundation is a United Kingdom organisation that campaigns for a "real Living Wage" — a voluntary hourly rate of pay, calculated independently according to the cost of living, that is higher than the statutory National Living Wage set by the government. It was launched in 2011 by Citizens UK, growing out of the London Living Wage campaign that began in 2001.

The real Living Wage

The rates are calculated by the Resolution Foundation and overseen by the Living Wage Commission, based on a basket of goods and services that a household needs for a minimum acceptable standard of living. Separate rates are set for London and for the rest of the UK, reflecting higher living costs in the capital. New rates are announced each autumn, and accredited employers are expected to implement them within six months.

Accreditation

Employers who pay the real Living Wage to all directly employed and contracted staff can become accredited Living Wage Employers. Thousands of organisations — including local authorities, charities, universities and major private companies — have been accredited, and the Foundation also runs programmes such as Living Hours (guaranteeing secure working hours) and the Living Wage Places scheme for towns and cities.
