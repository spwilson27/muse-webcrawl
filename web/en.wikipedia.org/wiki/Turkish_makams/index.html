Turkish makams

This article lists the makams — melodic modes — of Turkish classical music. Each makam combines a scale with rules for melodic progression (seyir), a final tone (karar), and a dominant (güçlü). Theorists have described well over a hundred makams, though a smaller core set accounts for most of the repertoire.

Classification

Makams are grouped as simple (basit), transposed (şed), and compound (birleşik) makams. Compound makams combine two or more çeşni flavours and often modulate between related scales in the course of a piece.
