Districts of Lebanon

The districts of Lebanon (Arabic: qadaa) are the second-level administrative subdivisions of Lebanon, below the governorates (mohafazat). There are 26 districts, distributed among nine governorates. Each district is administered by a district commissioner (qaimaqam).

Examples include the districts of Akkar, Baalbek, Hermel, Batroun, Bsharri, Jbeil (Byblos), Koura, Tripoli, Zgharta, Keserwan, the Chouf, Aley, Baabda, Metn, Hasbaya, Marjeyoun, Nabatieh, Bint Jbeil, Tyre, Sidon, Jezzine, Rashaya, Western Beqaa, Zahle, and Miniyeh-Danniyeh. The capital Beirut forms its own governorate and is not part of any district.

Administration

Below the districts are municipalities, which provide local services. District-level government in Lebanon is largely an arm of the central state rather than an elected tier, though elected municipal councils operate at the local level.
