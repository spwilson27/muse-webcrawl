Giuseppe Chiara

Born1602, Palermo, Sicily
Died24 August 1685, Nagasaki, Japan
OccupationJesuit missionary

Giuseppe Chiara (1602–1685) was an Italian Jesuit missionary who entered Japan in 1643 during the period when Christianity was banned under the Tokugawa shogunate. He is remembered as the historical model for the character Sebastião Rodrigues in Shūsaku Endō's novel Silence.

Mission to Japan

Chiara landed on Oshima island in 1643 with a group of missionaries seeking news of Cristóvão Ferreira, the Jesuit provincial who had apostatized under torture. Chiara's party was captured almost immediately and taken to Nagasaki, where, after torture including the pit (ana-tsurushi), he apostatized.

Later life

Like other apostate missionaries, Chiara was given a Japanese name (Okamoto San'emon), a wife, and a residence in Nagasaki, and was required to assist officials in identifying Christian goods and persons arriving on foreign ships. He lived in Japan for over forty years and died in Nagasaki in 1685.
