List of stars in Aquarius

This is a list of notable stars in the constellation Aquarius, the Water-Bearer — a large but faint zodiacal constellation best seen in autumn in the Northern Hemisphere. Despite its size it has no star brighter than magnitude 3 except Sadalsuud and Sadalmelik. Aquarius is noted for its many planetary systems, including the ultra-cool dwarf TRAPPIST-1 with seven Earth-sized planets.
