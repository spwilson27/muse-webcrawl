Christian eschatology

Christian eschatology is the branch of theology concerning the "last things": death, judgment, heaven and hell, the Second Coming of Christ, the resurrection of the dead, and the renewal of creation. Major sources include Daniel, the Olivet Discourse, Paul's letters, and the Book of Revelation.

Main themes

Traditions differ over the millennium (premillennialism, postmillennialism, amillennialism), the rapture, the tribulation, and the nature of the intermediate state. Ecumenical creeds affirm that Christ "will come again to judge the living and the dead" and confess "the resurrection of the body and the life everlasting".

Views by tradition

Catholic teaching includes particular and general judgment, purgatory, and the beatific vision. Orthodox theology emphasizes theosis and the uncreated light. Protestant views range from dispensational premillennialism to realized eschatology.
