CCSDS

Full name: Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems
Founded: 1982
Type: Standards organisation

The Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems (CCSDS) is an international forum of space agencies founded in 1982 that develops communication and data systems standards for spaceflight.

Standards

CCSDS publishes recommended standards (the "Blue Books") covering telemetry and telecommand packet protocols, space link protocols, data compression, time code formats, and cross-support services. Its standards enable interoperability between agencies' ground stations and spacecraft and underpin systems such as the Delay/Disruption Tolerant Networking bundle protocol.

Members

Member agencies include NASA, ESA and other national space agencies, with numerous observer agencies and industrial associates.
