Harald Bluetooth (Old Norse: Haraldr Blátǫnn Gormsson; Danish: Harald Blåtand Gormsen; died c. 985/86) was a king of Denmark from about 958 and, for a time, king of Norway. He united the tribes of Denmark into a single kingdom, converted the Danes to Christianity, and extended his power over the coasts of the western Baltic. His nickname — explained in later sources by a conspicuous dead or blue-black tooth — gave its name to the Bluetooth wireless technology standard, whose logo combines his initials in runes.

Reign

Harald succeeded his father Gorm the Old around 958. His reign saw a deliberate programme of centralisation: he is credited with rebuilding the Danevirke fortifications, erecting the great ring fortresses (Trelleborg-type forts) at strategic points, and founding towns and bridges, including the Ravning Enge bridge in Jutland. Around 965 he accepted baptism — tradition credits the monk Poppo, who is said to have proved the Christian God's power by carrying hot iron unharmed — and Harald proclaimed on the larger Jelling Stone that he "won for himself all of Denmark and Norway and made the Danes Christian".

The Jelling stones

At Jelling, the royal seat in Jutland, Harald raised a great runestone beside his father's smaller one, between two huge burial mounds and a church. The stone's inscription and its carved image of Christ are often called Denmark's birth certificate, marking the official Christianisation of the kingdom.

Foreign policy and fall

Harald intervened in Norway, where after the death of Harald Greycloak his allies ruled as earls in his name, and contested the Baltic coast with the Slavic Wends and the warrior community of Jomsborg. He fought defensive wars against the Holy Roman Empire. Late in his reign his son Sweyn Forkbeard rebelled; Harald was wounded and died, according to tradition at Jomsborg, around 985 or 986, and Sweyn succeeded him.

Bluetooth technology

In 1997, engineers developing a short-range radio standard adopted "Bluetooth" as a codename honouring Harald's reputation for uniting peoples and facilitating communication; the name stuck. The Bluetooth logo is a bind-rune merging the Younger Futhark runes for H (ᚼ) and B (ᛒ).
