The Gambia national football team, nicknamed the Scorpions, represents the Gambia in international football and is controlled by the Gambia Football Federation. Until 1965 the team and its players were known as British Gambia.

History

The Gambia played its first official match in 1962. For most of its history the team struggled in African competition, rarely progressing in Africa Cup of Nations or World Cup qualifying, and the country withdrew from qualifying tournaments several times for financial reasons.

Africa Cup of Nations breakthrough

The Gambia qualified for the Africa Cup of Nations for the first time in its history for the 2021 edition, played in Cameroon in early 2022 under Belgian coach Tom Saintfiet. In a celebrated debut run, the Scorpions — the lowest-ranked team at the tournament — beat Mauritania, drew with Mali and beat Tunisia in the group stage, defeated Guinea in the round of 16, and reached the quarter-finals, where they lost to hosts Cameroon. They qualified again for the 2023 tournament in Ivory Coast.

Stadium

The team plays home matches at the Independence Stadium in Bakau, near the capital Banjul, which has a capacity of about 40,000.
