Nikon Z-mount

ManufacturerNikon
Introduced2018
Throat diameter55 mm
Flange distance16 mm

The Nikon Z-mount is a lens mount introduced by Nikon in 2018 for its Z-series full-frame and APS-C mirrorless interchangeable-lens cameras, beginning with the Z 6 and Z 7. With an inner diameter of 55 mm — the largest among full-frame mirrorless systems at launch — and a short flange focal distance of 16 mm, the mount was designed to enable faster, optically ambitious lenses, including the 58 mm f/0.95 Noct.

Lenses and compatibility

Native Z-mount lenses carry the Nikkor Z designation and the premium S-Line label. Through Nikon's FTZ and FTZ II adapters, most F-mount SLR lenses can be used with varying autofocus support. In 2023 Nikon began licensing the mount to third parties and acquired cinema camera maker RED in 2024, extending the Z system into professional video.
