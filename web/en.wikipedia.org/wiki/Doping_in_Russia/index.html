Doping in Russia

Doping in Russia concerns the systematic, state-sponsored doping programme uncovered in Russian sport in the 2010s, centred on the Moscow and Sochi anti-doping laboratories, sample swapping at the 2014 Sochi Winter Olympics, and reports by Richard McLaren for the World Anti-Doping Agency.

Background

Allegations grew after whistleblowers including Vitaly and Yuliya Stepanov and former lab director Grigory Rodchenkov described state involvement in covering positive tests.

Consequences

Russia was barred from competing under its flag at several Olympic Games, athletes competed as neutral entrants, and WADA imposed a multi-year ban later reduced by the Court of Arbitration for Sport.

Investigations

The McLaren reports of 2016 documented sample tampering affecting more than a thousand athletes across summer, winter and Paralympic sports.
