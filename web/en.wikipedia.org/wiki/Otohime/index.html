Otohime

Otohime (Japanese: 乙姫, "sound princess" or "youngest princess") is a character in Japanese folklore, the princess of the undersea Ryūgū-jō (Dragon Palace) in the tale of Urashima Tarō. She is the daughter of Ryūjin, the dragon god of the sea.

In the Urashima Tarō tale

In the famous folktale, the fisherman Urashima Tarō rescues a turtle being tormented by children. The grateful turtle — in some versions Otohime herself in disguise — carries him beneath the sea to the Dragon Palace. Otohime welcomes him, lavish feasts and entertainments follow, and what seems a brief stay proves to be hundreds of years in the world above. When Urashima longs for home, Otohime gives him a jewelled box, the tamatebako, warning him never to open it. Returning to find everyone he knew long dead, Urashima opens the box; white smoke escapes and he suddenly ages into an old man, for the box contained his lost centuries.

In mythology

Otohime is also identified with Toyotama-hime, the sea princess who married Hoori, grandson of the sun goddess Amaterasu, in the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki myths, and who transformed into a dragon (or wani) when giving birth to Ugayafukiaezu, grandfather of the legendary first emperor Jimmu.
