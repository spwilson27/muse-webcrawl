Batman: Arkham Asylum is a 2009 action-adventure game developed by Rocksteady Studios and written by Paul Dini. Based on DC Comics' Batman, it follows Batman as the Joker seizes control of Arkham Asylum, the facility for Gotham City's criminally insane, and traps him inside with his enemies.

Gameplay

The game combines free-flowing hand-to-hand combat, stealth takedowns from the shadows ("invisible predator" gameplay), gadget-based exploration in the Metroidvania style, and "Detective Vision", a visual mode highlighting clues and enemies. Kevin Conroy and Mark Hamill reprise their animated-series roles as Batman and the Joker; villains encountered include Harley Quinn, Bane, Poison Ivy, Killer Croc and the Scarecrow, whose fear-toxin hallucinations became signature sequences.

Reception and legacy

Arkham Asylum was a critical and commercial triumph, winning BAFTA awards and holding a Guinness World Record as the most critically acclaimed superhero game ever at its release. It launched the Batman: Arkham series, followed by Arkham City (2011).
