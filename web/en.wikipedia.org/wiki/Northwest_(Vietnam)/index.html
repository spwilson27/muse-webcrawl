Northwest (Vietnam)

The Northwest (Vietnamese: Tây Bắc) is one of the regions of Vietnam, located in the mountainous northwestern part of the country, bordering Laos and China.

Geography

The region conventionally comprises the provinces of Điện Biên, Lai Châu, Sơn La and Hòa Bình, and sometimes Lào Cai and Yên Bái. It is rugged, sparsely populated, and home to many ethnic minority groups including the Thái, Hmong and Mường. The historic battle of Điện Biên Phủ in 1954 took place here.

Economy

Agriculture, including tea, coffee and terraced rice, dominates, along with hydroelectric power from the Đà River, notably the Sơn La Dam.
