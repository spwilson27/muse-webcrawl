Home

A home is a place of residence — a space used as a permanent or semi-permanent dwelling for an individual, group or family. Beyond being a shelter from the elements, the concept of home carries strong psychological and social significance: it is associated with security, comfort, belonging and identity. A home need not be a house; it may be an apartment, a tent, a boat, or any place a person habitually lives and feels attached to. The word is also used more broadly, as in the home of an organisation, an animal's habitat, or a home page on the World Wide Web.

Physical forms

Homes range from rudimentary shelters such as huts, tents and caves to large fixed structures of wood, brick, stone or concrete, and may include flats, houseboats, mobile homes and communal dwellings. Since the earliest human settlements, dwellings have provided protection and a locus for domestic life — cooking, sleeping, storing possessions and raising children. Modern homes typically have facilities for sanitation, water, heating and cooking, and their design reflects climate, available materials, culture and wealth.

Psychological and social aspects

Psychologists and geographers treat the home as a primary territory bound up with personal identity, privacy and emotional attachment; the lack of one — homelessness — is associated with insecurity and social exclusion. "Home" is often contrasted with the neutral physical term "house" or "dwelling": a house is a building, whereas a home is the lived, meaningful place made within or around it. Ideas of home are central to law (the right to housing, protection from eviction), to politics (home ownership as an aspiration) and to literature and philosophy.

Related concepts

The household is the social unit living in a home. The home is the focus of domestic architecture and interior design, and in many cultures the hearth historically formed its symbolic centre. Homesickness is the distress felt when away from home. Animals are also said to have homes or home ranges, and homing behaviour describes the ability of some species to return to them.
