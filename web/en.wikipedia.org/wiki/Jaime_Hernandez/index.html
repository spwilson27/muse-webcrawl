Jaime Hernandez (born 1959) is an American cartoonist, best known as co-creator, with his brothers Gilbert and Mario, of the acclaimed alternative comic book Love and Rockets, first published in 1981. Jaime's contributions centre on the "Locas" stories.

The Locas stories

Set largely in the fictional Los Angeles barrio of Huerta ("Hoppers"), the Locas saga follows Margarita "Maggie" Chascarrillo, a gifted mechanic, and her friend and sometime lover Esperanza "Hopey" Glass, through punk-rock youth into middle age. Drawing on influences from classic newspaper strips, romance and teen comics, Archie, and punk culture, Hernandez draws his characters ageing in something close to real time, and the series is praised for its clean line, humane storytelling and depiction of Chicano life and queer relationships.

Recognition

Hernandez has received numerous honours, including Eisner and Harvey Awards, the PEN Award for graphic literature, and induction into the Will Eisner Comic Book Hall of Fame. Collections such as The Love Bunglers have appeared on critics' best-of lists.
