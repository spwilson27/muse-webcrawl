Super League is the top-level professional rugby league competition in the Northern Hemisphere, featuring clubs from England and France. Founded in 1996 as the flagship of the sport's switch to a summer season, it is administered by the Rugby Football League and is determined by a play-off series culminating in the Grand Final at Old Trafford, Manchester.

History

Super League was created amid the sport's global "Super League war" between Rupert Murdoch's News Limited and the Australian Rugby League; the British game, backed by News Corporation money, moved from winter to summer for the 1996 season. The inaugural championship was won by St Helens. Since then the title has been dominated by a small group of clubs — St Helens, Leeds Rhinos, Wigan Warriors, and Bradford Bulls (the "big four" of the early era) — with Wigan's dynasty of the 2010s and St Helens's four consecutive Grand Final wins in 2019–2022 among the standout runs. Hull KR broke the pattern by winning the League Leaders' Shield in 2025 and reaching the Grand Final.

Structure

The competition normally comprises twelve clubs playing each other home and away plus a Magic Weekend round in which all fixtures are staged at a single stadium. The top six qualify for the play-offs, leading to the Grand Final. A French club, Catalans Dragons of Perpignan, joined in 2006 and won the Challenge Cup in 2018, becoming the first non-British club to lift a major trophy; Toulouse Olympique also spent the 2022 season in the league. Promotion and relegation with the Championship applied for most seasons; from 2025, membership is determined by IMG grading criteria assessing facilities, finance, and fanbase in addition to on-field performance.

Other competitions named Super League

The name is also used by the Swiss Super League and numerous other football leagues (in Greece, Turkey, India, China and elsewhere), by the defunct European Super League football project announced in 2021, and by Greece's top football division (see Super League Greece). In rugby league history, "Super League" also refers to the breakaway Australian competition of 1997 and its governing organisation.
