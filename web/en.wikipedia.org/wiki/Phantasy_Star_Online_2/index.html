Phantasy Star Online 2 is a free-to-play action role-playing game developed and published by Sega, the successor to Phantasy Star Online (2000). It launched in Japan in 2012 for Windows, and was released worldwide in 2020 following years of demand in the West.

Gameplay

Players create a character belonging to ARKS, an organisation exploring new worlds aboard the Oracle fleet, and undertake instanced missions ("quests") in parties of up to four, with larger multi-party areas for up to twelve players. Combat is real-time and action-oriented, with a choice of classes using melee weapons, ranged weapons and techniques (magic), and an extensive character creator. The story was delivered in episodic chapters, culminating in a battle against primordial darkness threatening the universe.

New Genesis

In June 2021 Sega released Phantasy Star Online 2: New Genesis, a major overhaul running alongside the original game, with a new open-world setting on the planet Halpha, updated graphics and revised combat. The original version's story content remained accessible, and player characters and much of their equipment carried between the two games. Phantasy Star Online 2 is notable for its many collaborations with anime and other games, and for large-scale in-game virtual concerts.
