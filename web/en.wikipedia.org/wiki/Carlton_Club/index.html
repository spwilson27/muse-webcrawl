Carlton Club

Formation1832
TypePrivate members' club
LocationSt James's Street, London

The Carlton Club is a private members' club in St James's, London. Founded in 1832, it has long been closely associated with the Conservative Party, which effectively grew out of the club's early organisation after the passing of the Reform Act 1832.

History

The club was founded by Tory peers, MPs and gentlemen at the Thatched House Tavern in 1832. It quickly became the chief coordinating body of the new Conservative Party, registering voters and organising elections. Its first permanent home was in Carlton Gardens, and it later moved to Pall Mall before settling at 69 St James's Street after its Pall Mall clubhouse was destroyed by bombing in 1940.

Membership

Membership is predominantly drawn from Conservative parliamentarians and supporters; the party's leader is traditionally an honorary life member. Women were admitted as full members only in 2008.
