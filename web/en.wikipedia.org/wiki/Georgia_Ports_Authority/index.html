Georgia Ports Authority

The Georgia Ports Authority (GPA) is an agency of the U.S. state of Georgia that operates port facilities in Savannah and Brunswick. It was established by an act of the Georgia legislature in 1945.

Its principal facility, the Port of Savannah, includes the Garden City Terminal—one of the largest single-operator container terminals in North America—and the Ocean Terminal. Savannah is consistently among the busiest container ports in the United States.

The Port of Brunswick specialises in roll-on/roll-off cargo, particularly automobiles, and also handles bulk and breakbulk cargo. The GPA markets Georgia's ports as key gateways for the southeastern United States.
