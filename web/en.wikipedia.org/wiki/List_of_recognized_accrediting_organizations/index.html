List of recognized accrediting organizations

In the United States, accreditation of higher education institutions and programmes is carried out by private, non-profit organizations that are recognized either by the U.S. Department of Education or by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation (CHEA). Recognition determines, among other things, whether an institution's students are eligible for federal student aid under the Higher Education Act.

National and programmatic accreditors

National accreditors include the Accrediting Bureau of Health Education Schools (ABHES), the Accrediting Council for Independent Colleges and Schools (ACICS, derecognized in 2022), the Distance Education Accrediting Commission (DEAC), and the Transnational Association of Christian Colleges and Schools (TRACS). Programmatic accreditors evaluate particular subjects, such as ABET (engineering and computing), AACSB and ACBSP (business), the American Bar Association (law), the Liaison Committee on Medical Education (medicine), and the Commission on Collegiate Nursing Education (nursing).
