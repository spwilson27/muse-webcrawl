John Ross "J. R." Ewing Jr. is a fictional character in the American prime-time soap opera Dallas and its 2012 revival, played by Larry Hagman. The eldest son of oil baron Jock Ewing and Miss Ellie Ewing, J. R. became one of the most famous villains in television history — a ruthless, charming oil tycoon whose schemes drove the series for thirteen years.

Character

J. R. is the president of Ewing Oil, the family company founded by his father. Ambitious and unscrupulous, he spends the series plotting against his younger brother Bobby, his wife Sue Ellen, and rival Cliff Barnes, using blackmail, bribery and betrayal to protect and expand his empire. Despite his villainy, Hagman's charismatic performance made J. R. the show's breakout star and antihero.

"Who shot J. R.?"

In the 1979–80 season finale, an unseen assailant shot J. R. in his office. The mystery of the shooter's identity became a global cultural phenomenon; the episode "Who Done It", broadcast in November 1980, drew one of the largest television audiences in history and revealed Kristin Shepard, J. R.'s sister-in-law and former mistress, as the culprit.

Revival and legacy

Hagman returned as J. R. in the 2012 TNT continuation of Dallas. After Hagman's death in November 2012, the character was killed off on screen, and his elaborate posthumous master plan formed the centrepiece of the second season. J. R. is routinely ranked among the greatest television villains of all time, and his Stetson, drawl and grin defined the show's image.
