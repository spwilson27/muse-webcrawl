German Revolution of 1918–1919

The German Revolution of 1918–1919 (German: Novemberrevolution) was a civil conflict at the end of World War I that replaced the German Empire with a democratic republic — the Weimar Republic.

German Revolution
Date: 3 November 1918 – 11 August 1919
Location: Germany
Result: Abdication of Kaiser Wilhelm II; Weimar Republic established

Course

It began with the Kiel mutiny in late October 1918, when sailors refused a final suicidal sortie. Workers' and soldiers' councils spread across Germany. On 9 November 1918 Philipp Scheidemann proclaimed a republic in Berlin, and Wilhelm II abdicated and fled to the Netherlands. Friedrich Ebert's SPD-led government signed the armistice on 11 November.

Spartacist uprising

In January 1919 the far-left Spartacist uprising in Berlin was crushed by government forces and Freikorps; leaders Rosa Luxemburg and Karl Liebknecht were murdered. A Bavarian Soviet Republic was also suppressed in May 1919.

Outcome

Elections in January 1919 — the first with women's suffrage — produced the Weimar National Assembly, which adopted the Weimar Constitution in August 1919.
