ASUSTeK Computer Inc. (stylised as ASUS) is a Taiwanese multinational computer, phone hardware and electronics manufacturer headquartered in Beitou District, Taipei, Taiwan. Founded on 2 April 1989, it is one of the world's largest personal computer vendors by unit sales. Its products include desktop PCs, laptop PCs, netbooks, mobile phones, networking equipment, monitors, Wi-Fi routers, projectors, motherboards, graphics cards, optical storage, multimedia products, peripherals, wearables, servers, workstations and tablet PCs.

The company is also an original equipment manufacturer (OEM). Asus's name originates from Pegasus, the winged horse of Greek mythology; the founders took the last four letters of the word. Its slogan has been "In Search of Incredible".

History

Asus was founded in Taipei in 1989 by T.H. Tung, Ted Hsu, Wayne Hsieh and M.T. Liao, all formerly of Acer. The company began by producing computer motherboards for Intel; its 486 motherboard designed without access to a sample processor was accepted by Intel and led to a long-standing partnership. Asus launched the Eee PC netbook line in 2007, popularising the low-cost ultra-portable category. In 2014 it launched the ZenFone smartphone line, and its Republic of Gamers (ROG) brand became a major name in gaming hardware.

Products

Asus sells products under brands including ROG (Republic of Gamers) for gaming, TUF Gaming, ProArt for creators, ZenBook and VivoBook laptops, and ZenFone phones. It is consistently the world's largest motherboard manufacturer and a leading maker of graphics cards, monitors and routers.
