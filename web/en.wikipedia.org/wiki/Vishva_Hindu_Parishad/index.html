Vishva Hindu Parishad

The Vishva Hindu Parishad (VHP; World Council of Hindus) is an Indian right-wing Hindu organisation founded in 1964. It is a member organisation of the Sangh Parivar, the family of organisations affiliated with the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS).

Founding and aims

The VHP was founded on 29 August 1964 in Mumbai, with the involvement of RSS leader M. S. Golwalkar and Hindu religious figures including Swami Chinmayananda. Its stated objectives include consolidating Hindu society, promoting Hindu values, and engaging with the global Hindu diaspora. Its youth wing is the Bajrang Dal, and its women's wing is the Durga Vahini.

Activities

The organisation runs social service, education and religious programmes and campaigns against religious conversion. It played a leading role in the Ram Janmabhoomi movement campaigning for a temple at Ayodhya, a movement that culminated in the demolition of the Babri Masjid in 1992 and, decades later, the construction of the Ram Mandir following the Supreme Court of India's 2019 verdict.
