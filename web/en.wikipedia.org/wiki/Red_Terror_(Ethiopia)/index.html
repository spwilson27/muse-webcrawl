Red Terror (Ethiopia)

The Red Terror (Amharic: Qey Shibir) was a violent political repression campaign carried out in Ethiopia and Eritrea from 1976 to 1978 by the Derg military government of Mengistu Haile Mariam against rival leftist groups, chiefly the Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Party (EPRP) and MEISON, during the Ethiopian Civil War.

Date1976–1978
PerpetratorDerg; kebele militias
DeathsEstimates from tens of thousands to several hundred thousand

Events

After urban guerrilla attacks by the EPRP, Mengistu publicly launched the campaign in April 1977. Neighbourhood kebele committees were armed and empowered to arrest and execute suspected opponents; bodies were left in the streets and families charged for the bullets to retrieve them. Victims were often students and young people.

Aftermath

After the Derg fell to the EPRDF in 1991, Mengistu — who had fled to Zimbabwe — was convicted of genocide in absentia by an Ethiopian court in 2006 and sentenced to death in 2008. The Red Terror Martyrs' Memorial Museum opened in Addis Ababa in 2010.
