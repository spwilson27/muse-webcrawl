BBC Cymru Wales is a division of the BBC for Wales, headquartered in Cardiff. It produces television, radio and online content in English and Welsh for audiences in Wales and network programming for the wider United Kingdom.

Services

Television services include BBC One Wales, BBC Two Wales and contributions to the Welsh-language channel S4C. Radio services include BBC Radio Wales in English and BBC Radio Cymru in Welsh. Its Roath Lock studios in Cardiff Bay produce dramas including Doctor Who, Casualty and Pobol y Cwm.

History

The BBC began broadcasting in Wales in 1923 from Cardiff. BBC Wales was formally established in the 1960s with the launch of BBC Wales TV in 1964. In 2020 staff moved to a new headquarters at Central Square in central Cardiff.
