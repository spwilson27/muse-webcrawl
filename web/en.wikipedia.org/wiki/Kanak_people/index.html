Kanak people

The Kanak (French: Canaques) are the Indigenous Melanesian inhabitants of New Caledonia, an archipelago in the South Pacific administered by France. They constitute roughly 40 percent of the territory's population, speak some 28 distinct Kanak languages, and have a distinct customary social organisation centred on clans, chiefdoms, and the ceremonial exchange system known as la coutume.

History

Kanak ancestors, belonging to the Lapita culture, settled New Caledonia around 3,000 years ago. France annexed the islands in 1853 and used them as a penal colony; Kanak were dispossessed of much of their land and confined to reserves. The great chief Ataï led a major revolt in 1878. Under the colonial indigénat regime, Kanak remained second-class subjects until after the Second World War, gaining French citizenship in 1946.

In the 1970s and 1980s a Kanak independence movement, led by the FLNKS (Kanak and Socialist National Liberation Front) under Jean-Marie Tjibaou, pressed for self-determination. A period of violent conflict known as les Événements culminated in the 1988 Ouvéa cave hostage crisis and the Matignon Accords, followed by the Nouméa Accord of 1998, which set a path of progressive transfer of powers and referendums on independence. Votes in 2018, 2020, and 2021 all returned majorities against independence, though the 2021 result was contested by pro-independence groups who boycotted it, and the territory's status remains debated. Unrest in 2024 over proposed electoral reforms caused extensive damage and renewed negotiations over New Caledonia's future.

Culture

Kanak society is organised around clans and customary chiefdoms (chefferies), with land held inalienably by clans. Signature art forms include the carved flèche faîtière roof finials of great houses, ceremonial yam exchanges — the yam being a sacred staple and symbol of masculinity and ancestry — and carved doorposts and masks. The Tjibaou Cultural Centre in Nouméa, designed by Renzo Piano and opened in 1998, is the principal institution of Kanak culture.
