Jee Sin

Jee Sin (Chinese: 至善, pinyin: Zhìshàn), also known as Jee Sin Sim Si, is a figure of southern Chinese martial arts legend, said to have been a Shaolin monk who survived the burning of the temple.

Legend

In the traditions of Hung Gar, Wing Chun, and other southern styles, Jee Sin is described as a master of the Five Animals who taught a generation of revolutionary fighters, including the monk's supposed pupils in the southern temples. He is credited as the teacher of Hung Hei-gun, founder of the Hung Gar system.

Historical status

Like many figures of the Shaolin legends, Jee Sin's historical existence is not documented; the stories belong to the oral traditions and novels of the nineteenth century that framed southern martial arts. See also Shaolin Kung Fu.
