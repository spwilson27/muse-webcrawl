Pookkalam

Pookkalam is an intricate and colourful arrangement of flowers laid on the floor in concentric patterns, traditionally made during the festival of Onam in Kerala, India. The word combines poov (flower) and kalam (drawing or artwork). Pookkalams are assembled afresh each morning of the ten-day festival, growing larger and more elaborate day by day.

Tradition

According to tradition, the floral carpet welcomes the legendary King Mahabali, whose annual visit to his former subjects Onam celebrates. The first day, Atham, begins with a small single-ring design; a new ring of different coloured petals is added daily until the culminating Tiruvonam day. Common flowers include marigold, tulsi, jasmine, chrysanthemum, and the tiny white thumba flower, traditionally gathered by children.

Competitions

Pookkalam competitions are a highlight of Onam celebrations in schools, colleges, offices, and neighbourhood associations across Kerala, judged on design, colour combination, and adherence to tradition. Elaborate modern designs may incorporate geometric patterns, figures, and themes, sometimes reaching several metres in diameter.
