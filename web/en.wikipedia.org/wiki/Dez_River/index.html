Dez River

Dez River
Country: Iran
Province: Khuzestan, Lorestan
Length: about 400 km
Mouth: Karun River

The Dez River (Persian: دز) is a river in southwestern Iran, a tributary of the Karun River. It rises in the Zagros Mountains in Lorestan Province and flows through Khuzestan Province, passing the city of Dezful, before joining the Karun near Band-e Qir.

Dams

The river is impounded by the Dez Dam, a large arch dam completed in 1963, which provides hydroelectric power, irrigation water and flood control. A second major dam, the Upper Gotvand Dam, was built further downstream near Gotvand and completed in the 2010s.

History

The Dez valley has been inhabited since ancient times. The river's waters have irrigated the plains of Khuzestan for millennia, and remains of ancient irrigation works survive near Dezful. During the Iran–Iraq War, the river formed part of the defensive line in Khuzestan.
