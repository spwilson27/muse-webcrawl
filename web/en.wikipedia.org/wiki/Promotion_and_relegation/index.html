Promotion and relegation

Promotion and relegation is a process where teams are transferred between multiple divisions based on their performance for the completed season. The best-ranked team(s) in the lower division are promoted to the higher division for the next season, and the worst-ranked team(s) in the higher division are relegated to the lower division for the following season.

Overview

Promotion and relegation is a central feature of most football (soccer) league systems worldwide, including the English football league system, where clubs move between the Premier League and the divisions of the English Football League. The system is also used in many other sports in Europe, Latin America, Africa, and Asia, including rugby, handball, and basketball leagues.

Contrast with closed leagues

The model contrasts with the closed franchise systems used in major North American sports leagues, where membership is fixed regardless of performance and weak teams are compensated through mechanisms such as reverse-order drafts. Supporters argue that promotion and relegation keeps competition meaningful throughout a season and gives small clubs a route to the top; critics point to the severe financial consequences of relegation from lucrative top divisions.
