List of English monarchs

The list of English monarchs begins conventionally with Alfred the Great, King of Wessex, who asserted sovereignty over parts of England in the late ninth century. His grandson Æthelstan is regarded as the first king of a united England, crowned in 927. The monarchy of England endured until 1707, when the Acts of Union merged England and Scotland into the Kingdom of Great Britain.
