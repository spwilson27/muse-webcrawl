Ultimate Marvel

The Ultimate Marvel imprint (designated as Earth-1610 in Marvel's multiverse) was a line of comic books published by Marvel Comics from 2000 to 2015, featuring reimagined and modernised versions of the company's superhero characters for a new generation of readers, free of decades of accumulated continuity. It was launched under editor Bill Jemas and editor-in-chief Joe Quesada, with writer Brian Michael Bendis central to its creation.

Publisher: Marvel Comics
Launched: 2000 (Ultimate Spider-Man)
Ended: 2015 (original line)
Universe: Earth-1610

Key titles and creators

The line began with Ultimate Spider-Man (2000) by Bendis and Mark Bagley, a record-setting creative run that retold Peter Parker's origin in a contemporary setting. It expanded with Ultimate X-Men (Mark Millar), Ultimate Fantastic Four (Bendis, Millar, Warren Ellis) and The Ultimates (Millar and Bryan Hitch), a cinematic, politically charged take on the Avengers that heavily influenced the later Marvel Cinematic Universe — including its casting-look for Nick Fury, modelled on Samuel L. Jackson.

Storylines and legacy

The imprint's later years featured the controversial Ultimatum event (2009), the death of Peter Parker and the introduction of Miles Morales as Spider-Man in 2011 — one of the imprint's most enduring creations. The line ended with the 2015 Secret Wars storyline, which destroyed the Ultimate Universe, though Miles Morales and a few other elements migrated to the main Marvel Universe. The MCU and animated films (notably Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, 2018) drew heavily on Ultimate concepts. In 2023–2024 Marvel launched a new, distinct "Ultimate Universe" line (Earth-6160) under Jonathan Hickman.
