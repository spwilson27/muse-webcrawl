Hidden track

A hidden track is a song or audio segment on a recording that is not listed on its packaging, placed to surprise listeners who let the album play past its apparent end, or discoverable only by rewinding into a pregap.

Techniques

Common methods include long silences after the final listed track (as on Nirvana's Nevermind, where "Endless, Nameless" follows ten minutes of silence), pregap tracks accessible by rewinding from track 1, and unlisted tracks within a single index. The Beatles' "Her Majesty" on Abbey Road (1969) is an early accidental example.

Decline

Streaming and track-by-track digital sales made hidden tracks impractical, since each file is separately indexed and listed.
