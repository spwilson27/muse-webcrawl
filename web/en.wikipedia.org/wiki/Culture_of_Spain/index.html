Culture of Spain

The culture of Spain is based on a variety of historical influences, including the pre-Roman peoples of the Iberian Peninsula, Roman Hispania, the Visigoths, the centuries of Muslim rule in al-Andalus, the Catholic Monarchs and the Spanish Empire, which spread Spanish language and culture across the Americas and beyond.

Arts

Spain has produced painters such as Velázquez, Goya, Picasso, Miró and Dalí, writers including Cervantes, author of Don Quixote, and architects from Gaudí to Calatrava. Flamenco music and dance from Andalusia, the running of the bulls in Pamplona, and festivals such as La Tomatina and Las Fallas are internationally recognised traditions.

Languages and society

Spanish is the national language, while Catalan, Galician and Basque are co-official in their regions. Spanish social life is noted for late dining hours, the tapas tradition, and strong regional identities within the autonomous communities.
