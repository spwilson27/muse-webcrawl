Insomniac (also known as Insomniac Events) is an American electronic dance music event promoter and distributor, founded in Los Angeles in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella. The company produces some of the largest electronic music festivals in the world, most notably the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), and is a subsidiary partner of Live Nation Entertainment, which acquired a 50% stake in 2013.

History

Rotella began promoting underground raves in Los Angeles warehouses in the early 1990s, adopting the Insomniac name from his Friday-night club events. The first Electric Daisy Carnival took place in 1997 in Los Angeles. After a death at the 2010 Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum edition and ensuing political pressure, EDC moved in 2011 to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, where it grew into a flagship multi-day festival drawing hundreds of thousands of attendees per weekend.

Events and brands

Insomniac's festival portfolio has included Electric Daisy Carnival (Las Vegas, Orlando, Mexico, and international editions), Beyond Wonderland, Nocturnal Wonderland, Escape Halloween, Countdown NYE, Electric Forest (co-produced), and genre-focused brands such as Bassrush, Basscon, Dreamstate, and Factory 93. The company also operates club nights and the Insomniac Records label, and launched its own festival ticketing and livestream initiatives.
