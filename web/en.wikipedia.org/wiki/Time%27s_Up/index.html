Time's Up

Time's Up was a non-profit organization that raised money and provided legal support for people who had experienced sexual harassment, assault, or abuse in the workplace. It was founded on January 1, 2018, by a group of women in the entertainment industry in response to the revelations about Harvey Weinstein and the Me Too movement, announced in an open letter published in The New York Times.

Activities

The organization's Legal Defense Fund, administered by the National Women's Law Center, raised over $20 million in its first year and connected thousands of workers — particularly low-wage workers — with attorneys. Time's Up also campaigned for legislation against harassment and for pay equity, and its slogan was prominent at the 2018 Golden Globe Awards, where attendees wore black and "Time's Up" pins.

Controversies and wind-down

The organization was damaged by revelations in 2021 that its leaders had advised New York governor Andrew Cuomo during his own sexual-harassment scandal, leading to resignations of its leadership. Time's Up announced in 2023 that it would cease operations, transferring the Legal Defense Fund entirely to the National Women's Law Center.
