Siege of Eger (1596)

DateSeptember–October 1596
LocationEger, Hungary
ResultOttoman victory

The Siege of Eger in 1596, during the Long Turkish War, saw Ottoman forces under Sultan Mehmed III capture the fortress of Eger in northern Hungary — in contrast to the famous Hungarian defence of the same castle in 1552.

Background and course

Mehmed III personally led the campaign, the first sultan to do so in decades. The Habsburg garrison and Hungarian defenders, weakened by poor command and low morale, surrendered after a siege of several weeks; many foreign mercenaries mutinied. The fall of Eger opened the way to the Battle of Mezőkeresztes later that October, a costly Ottoman victory.

Aftermath

Eger remained under Ottoman rule for over ninety years, until 1687.
