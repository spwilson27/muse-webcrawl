Penllyn is a village and community in the Vale of Glamorgan in south Wales. The community lies in the rural Vale countryside northwest of Cowbridge and includes the small village of Penllyn itself, the hamlet of Graig Penllyn ("Penllyn rock"), and surrounding farmland and settlements.

Geography and village

The village centres on St Brynach's Church and a small cluster of houses. Penllyn is known locally for the Penllyn "estates" of large houses set in the Vale landscape, including Penllyn Court, a manor house with Tudor origins, and Penllyn Castle, a Norman castle ruin substantially remodelled in later centuries, situated above the River Thaw. The community council area also takes in villages on its edges such as Llansannor and Ystradowen depending on boundary definitions.

History

Penllyn Castle was a frontier stronghold of the Norman lords of Glamorgan; its ruins incorporate an earlier castle site and Elizabethan rebuilding. In modern times the community has remained agricultural, with strong equestrian traditions — the Vale of Glamorgan hunt country surrounds the village — and modest residential growth driven by commuters to Cowbridge, Bridgend and Cardiff.
