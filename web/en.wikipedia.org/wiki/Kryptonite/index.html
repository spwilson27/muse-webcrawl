Kryptonite
First appearanceRadio: The Adventures of Superman (1943); Comics: Superman #61 (1949)
OriginPlanet Krypton

Kryptonite is a fictional mineral in DC Comics' Superman stories — radioactive fragments of the destroyed planet Krypton that weaken and can kill Kryptonians, providing Superman's principal vulnerability.

History

Kryptonite debuted in the 1943 radio serial, partly to let Superman's voice actor rest, and entered comics in 1949. Green kryptonite is the classic form; decades of stories added colours with different effects — red (unpredictable transformations), gold (permanently removes powers), black, blue and others — later pruned back in continuity reboots.

Cultural use

"Kryptonite" has become a general metaphor for an Achilles' heel. Unrelated real materials include the mineral jadarite, whose formula resembles the fictional one shown in Superman Returns (2006), and the element krypton, with which the fictional substance is not connected.
