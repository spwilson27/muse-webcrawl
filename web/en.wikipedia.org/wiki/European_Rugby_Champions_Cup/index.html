The European Rugby Champions Cup is an annual rugby union competition for clubs in Europe who compete in the three major European domestic leagues: the English Premiership, the French Top 14 and the multinational United Rugby Championship. It is the premier club competition in European rugby union and is organised by European Professional Club Rugby (EPCR).

History

The tournament was launched in 1995 as the Heineken Cup, initially with clubs from France, Ireland, Wales, Italy and Romania. Following a governance dispute between the English and French clubs and the existing organisers, the competition was restructured in 2014 under EPCR and renamed the European Rugby Champions Cup, with the second-tier Challenge Cup beneath it.

Format

Twenty-four clubs qualify — the top eight from each of the three leagues. Teams are drawn into pools, play a short round-robin phase, and the leading sides progress to a knockout stage of rounds of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final at a pre-selected neutral venue. Toulouse and Leinster are among the most successful clubs in the competition's history.
