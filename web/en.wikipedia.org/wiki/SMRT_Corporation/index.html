SMRT Corporation

Industry: Public transport
Founded: 6 August 1987 (as Mass Rapid Transit Corporation)
Headquarters: Singapore
Parent: Temasek Holdings
Services: Rail, bus, and taxi operations

SMRT Corporation Ltd is a multi-modal public transport operator in Singapore operating bus and rail services. A subsidiary of the Government of Singapore's investment arm Temasek Holdings, it is one of the two major operators of Singapore's rail services, alongside SBS Transit.

After its privatisation in 2000, it was listed on the Singapore Exchange. In 2016, it became a wholly owned subsidiary of Temasek after a buyout and delisted from the exchange.

History

The company originated as the Mass Rapid Transit Corporation (MRTC), established in 1983 to build Singapore's first metro system. The first section of the Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) opened in November 1987. In 2000 the company was renamed SMRT Corporation and merged with the bus and taxi operator TIBS in 2001, becoming an integrated transport operator.

Operations

SMRT Trains operates the North South, East West, Circle, Thomson–East Coast, and Bukit Panjang LRT lines of the MRT network. SMRT Buses operates a fleet of public buses from several depots, and its taxi arm, Strides, provides taxi and private-hire services. The company also manages retail and advertising space in its stations and engages in engineering and consultancy work abroad.
