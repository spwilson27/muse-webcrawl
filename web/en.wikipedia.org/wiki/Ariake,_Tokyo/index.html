Ariake, Tokyo

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Tokyo Big Sight, the landmark convention centre in Ariake.

Ariake (有明) is a district in Kōtō, Tokyo, Japan. It is best known as the location of the Tokyo International Exhibition Center, commonly known as Tokyo Big Sight. Ariake is a reclaimed-land district on Tokyo Bay in the Tokyo Waterfront City (Rinkai Fukutoshin) development, neighbouring Toyosu, Odaiba, and Shinonome.

Development

The district was created by land reclamation in Tokyo Bay and developed as part of the waterfront sub-centre plan. It is served by the Yurikamome Line (Tokyo International Cruise Terminal, Tokyo Big Sight, Ariake, and Ariake-Tennis-no-Mori stations) and the Rinkai Line (Kokusai-Tenjijō Station), as well as the Shuto Expressway and Ariake-dori avenue.

Facilities

Major facilities in Ariake include:

  • Tokyo Big Sight – Japan's largest convention and exhibition centre, recognisable by its inverted-pyramid conference tower.
  • Ariake Coliseum and Ariake Tennis Park – home of the Japan Open tennis tournament.
  • Ariake Arena – a multi-purpose arena built for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games (volleyball) and Paralympic Games.
  • Ariake Gymnastics Centre – built for the Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
  • Tokyo Garden Theatre, the Differ Ariake area, and large hotels, shopping centres (Ariake Garden), and the Tokyo Baycourt Club.
  • The Museum of Maritime Science and the headquarters of several corporations and research institutes.

Olympic legacy

Ariake formed one of the principal venue clusters for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games, hosting gymnastics, volleyball, tennis, and BMX events. Several venues were retained as permanent sports and entertainment facilities after the Games, making the district a focus of Tokyo's post-Olympic waterfront regeneration.
