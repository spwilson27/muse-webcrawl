DualSense

The DualSense controller, standard colour scheme

The DualSense is a wireless game controller developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment for the PlayStation 5 console. Announced in April 2020 and released alongside the console in November 2020, it succeeded the DualShock 4 and introduced haptic feedback and adaptive triggers as its principal innovations.

Design and features

The DualSense departs from the earlier DualShock design language: it has a two-tone white-and-black colour scheme, rounded grips textured with thousands of tiny symbols based on the PlayStation shapes (triangle, circle, cross, square), and light strips flanking the touch pad. It retains the symmetrical analogue stick layout characteristic of Sony controllers, the touch pad, a built-in microphone with a mute button, a 3.5 mm headset jack, a USB-C port, and a "Create" button replacing the DualShock 4's "Share" button.

Haptic feedback and adaptive triggers

Instead of conventional rumble motors, the DualSense uses voice-coil actuators to produce finely graded haptic feedback, allowing effects such as the sensation of rain or walking on different surfaces. The L2 and R2 triggers incorporate adaptive resistance: motors vary the force needed to press them, enabling effects such as the increasing tension of drawing a bowstring. The pack-in game Astro's Playroom was designed in large part to demonstrate these features.

Variants

Sony has released numerous colour variants, including Midnight Black, Cosmic Red, Nova Pink, Starlight Blue, Galactic Purple and Chroma finishes. In January 2023 Sony launched the DualSense Edge, a premium model with replaceable stick modules, remappable buttons, adjustable trigger stops and back buttons. The DualSense is compatible with PC, Mac, Android and iOS devices via Bluetooth and USB, and its haptics are supported in an increasing number of PC titles.

Reception

The controller was widely praised for its haptics and adaptive triggers, which reviewers described as a genuinely next-generation feature; criticism centred on battery life (the internal battery is rated at 1,560 mAh) and the longevity of the analogue sticks, which use potentiometers susceptible to drift.
