Kachin people

The Kachin peoples are a confederation of ethnic groups inhabiting the Kachin Hills of northern Myanmar (Burma) and adjacent parts of China and India. In Myanmar the umbrella term "Kachin" commonly covers six groups — the Jinghpaw, Zaiwa (Atsi), Lisu, Rawang, Lachik (Lachid), and Lisu-related communities — with the Jinghpaw the most numerous and politically dominant. In Chinese official classification most are grouped as the Jingpo nationality (景颇族), with the Zaiwa classed separately as Achang. The majority of Kachin in Myanmar are Christian, predominantly Baptist and Roman Catholic, though traditional animist practice persists.

Language and culture

Jinghpaw (Jingpho) serves as a lingua franca among the Kachin groups and is a Tibeto-Burman language, while Zaiwa and others belong to related branches. Traditional social organization historically combined hereditary chiefs (duwa) with elaborate kinship and marriage-alliance systems of the gumlao and gumsa types famously analyzed by anthropologist Edmund Leach in Political Systems of Highland Burma (1954), based on fieldwork in the Kachin hills. The central cultural institution is the manau, a ceremonial dance festival led by ritual specialists (naushawng) in which hundreds dance in winding lines led by leaders in headdresses, celebrating communal identity.

History and conflict

Under British colonial administration the Kachin hills were governed largely separately, and Kachin soldiers served with distinction with Allied forces against the Japanese in the Second World War, notably with the American OSS Detachment 101. After Burmese independence in 1948, disputes over autonomy led the Kachin Independence Organisation (KIO) and its armed wing, the Kachin Independence Army (KIA), founded in 1961, to take up arms for a federal or independent Kachin state. A 1994 ceasefire held until fighting resumed in June 2011, displacing around one hundred thousand people; conflict intensified again after Myanmar's 2021 military coup, with the KIA fighting alongside resistance forces and capturing significant territory in Kachin State.
