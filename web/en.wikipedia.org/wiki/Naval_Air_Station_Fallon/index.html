Naval Air Station Fallon

Location: Fallon, Nevada, United States
Type: Naval air station
Controlled by: United States Navy
Known for: TOPGUN / Naval Strike and Air Warfare Center

Naval Air Station Fallon (NAS Fallon) is a United States Navy air facility located in Churchill County, near the city of Fallon in western Nevada. It is the Navy's premier tactical air warfare training centre, home to the Naval Strike and Air Warfare Center (NSAWC), which incorporates the famous TOPGUN school.

History

The airfield was built in 1942 as part of the wartime expansion of naval aviation training bases, initially used for training and as an auxiliary field. After the Second World War it saw varying levels of use before growing in importance as a weapons-training site during the Cold War, thanks to the vast, sparsely populated desert ranges of northern Nevada.

TOPGUN and NSAWC

In 1996 the Naval Fighter Weapons School — TOPGUN — moved to Fallon from NAS Miramar in California and was consolidated with the strike warfare and airborne early warning schools into NSAWC. The centre trains carrier air wings in integrated strike warfare before deployment, using the Fallon Range Training Complex, more than 10,000 square miles of airspace and instrumented bombing ranges east of the station. Adversary squadrons based at Fallon fly aircraft painted and employed to replicate potential enemy fighters.

Facilities

NAS Fallon has one of the longest runways in the Navy, roughly 14,000 feet, suited to heavily loaded strike aircraft and high desert density-altitude conditions. The station supports several thousand military and civilian personnel and is a major employer in Churchill County.
