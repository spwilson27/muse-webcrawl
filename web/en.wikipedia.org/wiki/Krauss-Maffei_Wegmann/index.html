Krauss-Maffei Wegmann

Krauss-Maffei Wegmann GmbH & Co. KG (KMW) is a German defence company headquartered in Munich, producing military vehicles including the Leopard 2 main battle tank and the PzH 2000 self-propelled howitzer. In 2015 KMW merged with the French state-owned Nexter Systems to form KNDS (KMW+Nexter Defense Systems), with KMW becoming its German branch.


A Leopard 2A7, KMW's best-known product.

History

KMW was formed in 1999 when the military vehicle business of Krauss-Maffei AG was merged with Wegmann & Co. The parent families — the von Braunbehrens and Bode families (Wegmann) and the Wegmann family holdings — retained ownership until the KNDS combination. The company's roots reach back to the locomotive builders Krauss & Comp. (1866) and Maffei (1838), which merged in 1931 as Krauss-Maffei.

Products

  • Leopard 1 and Leopard 2 main battle tanks
  • PzH 2000 self-propelled howitzer
  • Boxer armoured transport vehicle (with Rheinmetall, through ARTEC)
  • Puma infantry fighting vehicle (with Rheinmetall, through PSM)
  • Dingo protected vehicle, Mungo transport vehicle, Grizzly
  • RCH 155 and AGM artillery systems, Leguan bridge-laying system

KNDS

On 29 July 2015, KMW and Nexter announced their combination under a joint holding, initially called KANT and later KNDS, owned 50/50 by the KMW shareholders and the French state. The group is headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands.
