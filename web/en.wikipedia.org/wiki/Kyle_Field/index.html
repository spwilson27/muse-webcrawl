Kyle Field

Kyle Field is the American football stadium located on the campus of Texas A&M University in College Station, Texas, United States. It has been the home to the Texas A&M Aggies football team in rudimentary form since 1904, and as a permanent concrete stadium since 1927.

Capacity

With a seating capacity of 102,733, Kyle Field is the largest stadium in the Southeastern Conference, the fourth-largest in the NCAA, and among the largest stadiums in the world. A major redevelopment completed in 2015 rebuilt much of the stadium and expanded its facilities.

History

The field is named for Edwin Jackson Kyle, an 1899 graduate of Texas A&M and professor of horticulture, who donated and fenced part of his own land for an athletic field when the university declined to fund one. The stadium is famous for the 12th Man tradition, the Midnight Yell practice, and one of the loudest home crowds in college football.
