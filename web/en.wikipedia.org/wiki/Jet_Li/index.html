Jet Li

Li Lianjie (born April 26, 1963), better known by his stage name Jet Li, is a Chinese-born Singaporean martial artist, actor, and philanthropist. Widely regarded as one of the greatest martial arts stars of his generation, he is known for his wushu skills and for portraying heroic Chinese folk heroes on screen.

Wushu career

Li began training with the Beijing Wushu Team at age eight and won his first national championship at eleven, eventually winning fifteen gold medals at the Chinese national wushu championships before retiring from competition at nineteen.

Film career

His film debut, Shaolin Temple (1982), was a smash hit in China. He became a major star in Hong Kong cinema playing Wong Fei-hung in the Once Upon a Time in China series, and starred in Fist of Legend, Fong Sai-yuk, and Hero (2002). His first role in a Hollywood production was as the villain in Lethal Weapon 4 (1998), followed by leading roles in Romeo Must Die (2000), Kiss of the Dragon (2001), Unleashed (2005), and The Expendables series.

Philanthropy

After surviving the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami while vacationing in the Maldives, Li founded the One Foundation in 2007, a charitable organization focused on disaster relief and children's welfare in China.
