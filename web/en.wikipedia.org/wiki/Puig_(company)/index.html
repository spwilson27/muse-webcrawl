Puig is a Spanish multinational fashion and beauty company founded in Barcelona in 1914 by Antonio Puig. Family-owned for over a century, it is known for building fragrance and fashion brands, both its own and under licence.

Business

Puig owns brands including Carolina Herrera, Paco Rabanne, Nina Ricci, Jean Paul Gaultier and Charlotte Tilbury, and holds fragrance licences for other fashion houses. The group expanded strongly in prestige beauty and colour cosmetics during the 2010s and 2020s, and listed on the Spanish stock exchanges in 2024.
