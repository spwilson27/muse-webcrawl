Technical support scam

A technical support scam is a type of fraud in which criminals pose as legitimate computer support agents — often claiming to represent companies such as Microsoft or internet service providers — and convince victims that their computer is infected or faulty, then charge for unnecessary "repairs" or steal data and money.

Methods

Scammers cold-call victims, or use pop-up browser warnings that freeze the screen and urge the victim to phone a number. Once in contact, they request remote access to the computer, display fabricated error messages, and demand payment by gift cards, wire transfer or cryptocurrency. Some install malware or steal passwords during the session.

Countermeasures

Technology companies and regulators such as the US Federal Trade Commission warn that legitimate firms never make unsolicited support calls or demand gift-card payment. Law-enforcement actions, including raids on call centres in India, have disrupted major scam operations, and "scambaiters" publicise counter-tactics online.
