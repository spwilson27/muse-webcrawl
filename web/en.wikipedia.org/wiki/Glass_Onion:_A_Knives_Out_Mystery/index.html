Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery

Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery is a 2022 American mystery film written and directed by Rian Johnson and produced by Johnson and Ram Bergman. A standalone sequel to Knives Out (2019), it stars Daniel Craig as detective Benoit Blanc, alongside an ensemble cast including Edward Norton, Janelle Monáe, Kathryn Hahn, Leslie Odom Jr., Jessica Henwick, Madelyn Cline, Kate Hudson, and Dave Bautista.

Plot

Tech billionaire Miles Bron invites his circle of wealthy friends — the "Disruptors" — to his private Greek island for a murder-mystery weekend during the COVID-19 pandemic. Blanc, unexpectedly among the guests, quickly solves Bron's staged game, but the evening turns deadly when one guest is poisoned. As Blanc peels back the layers of the group's shared history — like the layers of a glass onion — he uncovers Bron's theft of his former partner Andi Brand's ideas and her murder, and the film's second half replays events from a new perspective to expose the truth.

Production and release

The film was shot in Greece and Serbia in 2021 with cinematography by Steve Yedlin and a score by Nathan Johnson. Its title comes from the Beatles song "Glass Onion." Following the commercial success of Knives Out, Netflix acquired the rights to two sequels in a deal reported at over $400 million. Glass Onion premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2022, received a one-week theatrical sneak preview in November, and began streaming on Netflix on 23 December 2022.

Reception

The film was widely praised for its screenplay, direction, and performances, particularly Craig's and Monáe's. Johnson was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay. A third Blanc film, Wake Up Dead Man, followed in 2025.
