Tom Monaghan

Tom MonaghanBorn: 25 March 1937, Ann Arbor, Michigan, U.S.
Occupation: Founder of Domino's Pizza

Thomas Stephen Monaghan (born 25 March 1937) is an American entrepreneur who founded Domino's Pizza in 1960. He later became known for his Catholic philanthropy, including founding Ave Maria University in Florida.

Early life

Monaghan was born in Ann Arbor, Michigan. After his father died when he was four, he spent much of his childhood in a Catholic orphanage and foster homes. He briefly served in the United States Marine Corps.

Domino's Pizza

In 1960 Monaghan and his brother James bought a small pizzeria in Ypsilanti, Michigan, called DomiNick's. Tom later bought out his brother and built Domino's into one of the world's largest pizza delivery chains, pioneering franchising methods and delivery systems. He sold his controlling stake in 1998 for a reported sum of about one billion dollars.

Other activities

Monaghan owned the Detroit Tigers baseball team from 1983 to 1992, during which the club won the 1984 World Series. A devout Catholic, he founded the Ave Maria School of Law and Ave Maria University, along with the planned community of Ave Maria, Florida.
