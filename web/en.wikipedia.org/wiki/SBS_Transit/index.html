SBS Transit

Founded1973 (as Singapore Bus Service)
HeadquartersSingapore
ParentComfortDelGro
ServicesBus, MRT and LRT operations

SBS Transit is a major public transport operator in Singapore, part of the ComfortDelGro group. It operates the majority of Singapore's public bus services and several rail lines, including the North East MRT line, the Downtown MRT line, and the Sengkang and Punggol LRT lines.

History

The company began in 1973 as Singapore Bus Service, formed by merging regional bus companies, and was renamed SBS Transit in 2001 after winning the North East line rail contract — becoming a multi-modal operator. It is listed on the Singapore Exchange.

Operations

SBS Transit runs more than 200 bus services with a fleet of several thousand buses. Under Singapore's Bus Contracting Model, introduced from 2016, the government owns the assets and contracts out route packages, several of which SBS Transit operates.
