Alpargatas S.A. is a Brazilian footwear and clothing company headquartered in São Paulo, best known as the manufacturer of Havaianas flip-flops. Founded in 1907 by the Scottish immigrant Robert Fraser together with the British firm Fraser, Neave & Co., the company originally produced canvas espadrilles (alpargatas) for coffee workers.

History and brands

In 1962 the company launched the Havaianas sandal, inspired by Japanese zōri, which became a cultural icon in Brazil and a global fashion product. Alpargatas long belonged to the Camargo Corrêa group; control passed to J&F Investimentos in 2015 and later to a consortium including Itaúsa and Brasil Warrant in 2017. The company sold the Topper and Rainha brands in Argentina and Brazil and in 2021 acquired a stake in the American sustainable footwear brand Rothy's. Alpargatas is listed on the B3 exchange in São Paulo.
