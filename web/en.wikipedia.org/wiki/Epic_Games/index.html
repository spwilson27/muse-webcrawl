Epic Games, Inc. is an American video game and software developer and publisher based in Cary, North Carolina. The company was founded by Tim Sweeney as Potomac Computer Systems in 1991, originally located in his parents' house in Potomac, Maryland. Following the release of its first commercial video game, ZZT (1991), the company became Epic MegaGames, Inc. in early 1992.

History and products

Epic achieved early success with shareware titles such as Jill of the Jungle and Jazz Jackrabbit, and broke through in 1998 with the first-person shooter Unreal and its Unreal Engine, which it licenses widely across games, film, and simulation. In the 2000s Epic created the Gears of War series for Xbox. In 2017 it launched Fortnite, whose Battle Royale mode became a global phenomenon and one of the most successful games ever made. In December 2018 Epic launched the Epic Games Store, a digital storefront offering developers a larger revenue share than rivals, and in 2020 it became the plaintiff in Epic Games v. Apple, a prominent antitrust case over app-store commissions. Tencent holds a major minority stake in the company.
