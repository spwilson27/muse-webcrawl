Charity

Charity is the voluntary giving of help, typically in the form of money, goods, time or labour, to those in need. As a virtue, charity — from the Latin caritas — denotes benevolent love and generosity toward others. In its organisational sense, a charity (charitable organisation) is a non-profit body established for purposes recognised as charitable in law, such as the relief of poverty, the advancement of education or religion, and the promotion of health.

Charitable giving ranges from individual almsgiving to the work of large international foundations.

Religious traditions

Charitable giving is a duty in most world religions. In Christianity, caritas is counted among the theological virtues and the corporal works of mercy define its practice; Islamic zakat is an obligatory alms and one of the Five Pillars, supplemented by voluntary sadaqah; Judaism prescribes tzedakah as a matter of justice rather than optional generosity; Hindu, Buddhist and Sikh traditions practise dāna and, in Sikhism, seva (selfless service) and the langar free kitchen.

Charitable organisations

Modern charities range from small local trusts to international organisations such as the Red Cross, Médecins Sans Frontières and Oxfam. In many jurisdictions, registered charities enjoy tax exemptions and donors receive tax relief, in return for regulation — for example by the Charity Commission in England and Wales, or under section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code. Charitable foundations, including large philanthropic endowments created by wealthy donors, distribute grants rather than delivering services directly.

Effectiveness and criticism

Charity has been debated since antiquity: critics have argued that indiscriminate almsgiving may perpetuate dependency, while movements such as effective altruism evaluate charities by measured cost-effectiveness and direct resources to interventions — such as malaria prevention — that save the most lives per donation. Total global charitable giving runs to hundreds of billions of dollars annually, with the United States among the most generous nations by share of GDP.
