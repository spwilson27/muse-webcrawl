Al Jazeera

Al Jazeera is a Qatari state-owned international news network launched in 1996 from Doha, which transformed Arab media with uncensored live debate and reporting, and launched the English-language Al Jazeera English in 2006.

Reach and criticism

The network gained global attention covering Afghanistan and Iraq wars and the Arab Spring. Governments in the region have at times demanded its closure — a central demand in the 2017 Qatar diplomatic crisis — and it is criticised by some as reflecting Qatari state interests, which it disputes.
