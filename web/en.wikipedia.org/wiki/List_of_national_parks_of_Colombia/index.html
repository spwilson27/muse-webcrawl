List of national parks of Colombia

Colombia's National Natural Parks system (Parques Nacionales Naturales) protects over 60 areas covering roughly a tenth of the country's territory, from Caribbean islands to Amazon rainforest and Andean páramos. Colombia is one of the world's most biodiverse countries.

Notable parks

  • Tayrona — Caribbean coast where the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta meets the sea.
  • Los Nevados — Andean volcanoes and glaciers, including Nevado del Ruiz.
  • Amacayacu — Amazon rainforest on the Amazon River.
  • Chingaza — páramo water source for Bogotá.
  • Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta — the world's highest coastal mountain range.
  • Islas del Rosario (Corales del Rosario) — Caribbean coral reefs.
  • Gorgona — Pacific island, former prison, rich marine life.
  • El Cocuy — glaciated Andean peaks.
  • Los Katíos — Darién wetlands; UNESCO World Heritage.
  • Farallones de Cali, Puracé, Cueva de los Guácharos (the first park, 1960).

Administration

The system is administered by Parques Nacionales Naturales de Colombia under the Ministry of Environment, and also includes flora and fauna sanctuaries and other protected categories.
