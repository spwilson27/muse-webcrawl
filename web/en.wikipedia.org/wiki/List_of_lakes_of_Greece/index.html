List of lakes of Greece

This is a list of lakes of Greece. Greece has relatively few large natural lakes; the largest, Lake Trichonida, covers about 98 km². Many Greek lakes are important wetlands protected under the Ramsar Convention.

Lake Trichonida, the largest natural lake in Greece.
