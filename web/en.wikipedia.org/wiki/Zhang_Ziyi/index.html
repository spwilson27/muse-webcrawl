Zhang Ziyi (Chinese: 章子怡; born 9 February 1979) is a Chinese actress and model. She is regarded as one of the "Four Dan Actresses" of China and is considered one of the most accomplished Chinese actresses of her generation. Zhang first gained international recognition for her role in Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000), and went on to star in Rush Hour 2 (2001), Hero (2002), House of Flying Daggers (2004), Memoirs of a Geisha (2005), and The Grandmaster (2013).

Early life

Zhang was born and raised in Beijing, China. Her father was an accountant and later an economist, and her mother worked at a kindergarten. At age 11, she began studying dance at the Beijing Dance Academy. At 15 she won the national youth dance championship and began appearing in television commercials. At 17, she was admitted to the Central Academy of Drama in Beijing.

Career

Breakthrough

While at the Central Academy of Drama, Zhang was cast by director Zhang Yimou in the female lead of The Road Home (1999), which won the Silver Bear at the Berlin International Film Festival. Her portrayal of Jen Yu in Ang Lee's Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000) brought her to international prominence; the film was a critical and commercial success worldwide and won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.

International success

Zhang subsequently appeared in the Hollywood production Rush Hour 2 alongside Jackie Chan and Chris Tucker, and in a string of acclaimed Chinese-language films including Hero, House of Flying Daggers, and 2046. In 2005 she played the title role in the Hollywood adaptation Memoirs of a Geisha, earning a Golden Globe nomination. She later starred in Wong Kar-wai's The Grandmaster (2013), winning numerous Best Actress awards across Asia for her performance as Gong Er.

Personal life

In 2015, Zhang married Chinese rock musician Wang Feng. The couple have two children. Zhang has also been active in philanthropy, serving as a spokesperson for various charitable causes.
