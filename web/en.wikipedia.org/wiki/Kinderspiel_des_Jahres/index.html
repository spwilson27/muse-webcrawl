Kinderspiel des Jahres

The Kinderspiel des Jahres ("Children's Game of the Year") is a German board game award for children's games, presented annually by the Spiel des Jahres jury. It was introduced in 1978 as a special award (Sonderpreis Kinderspiel) and became a fully independent category in 2001, alongside the main Spiel des Jahres award and the later Kennerspiel des Jahres for connoisseur games.

History

For many years children's games were honoured within the Spiel des Jahres framework as a special prize. As the children's game market grew, the jury made the category independent in 2001, with its own shortlist, nominations announcement and award ceremony. The award is aimed at games typically for children up to about eight years old and is intended as guidance for parents, similar in spirit to the main prize, whose red pawn logo appears on the winning game's box in a blue variant.

Significance

Winning the Kinderspiel des Jahres substantially boosts a game's sales and international licensing, and the award is regarded as one of the most influential honours in the children's game market worldwide.
