Karachi Kings

The Karachi Kings (Urdu: کراچی کنگز) are a Pakistani professional Twenty20 franchise cricket team that competes in the Pakistan Super League (PSL). The team represents the city of Karachi and plays its home matches at the National Bank Cricket Arena (formerly National Stadium, Karachi). The franchise was founded in 2015, ahead of the inaugural PSL season in 2016, and is owned by Salman Iqbal of the ARY Group.

History

Karachi Kings won their first PSL title in 2020, defeating Lahore Qalandars in the final of the COVID-19-delayed fifth season, under the captaincy of Imad Wasim. The team has historically been one of the most popular franchises in the league, though its on-field results have been inconsistent, with several early exits in other seasons.

Players

Notable players to have represented the franchise include Babar Azam, who captained the side before moving to Peshawar Zalmi, Imad Wasim, Mohammad Amir, and Colin Munro. The late Dean Jones served as head coach during the title-winning 2020 season.
