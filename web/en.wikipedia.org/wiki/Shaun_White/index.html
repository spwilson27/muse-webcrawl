Shaun White, three-time Olympic gold medallist in snowboard halfpipe.

Shaun Roger White (born September 3, 1986) is an American former professional snowboarder and skateboarder. He is a three-time Olympic gold medallist in the snowboard halfpipe, a five-time Olympian, and holds the record for the most X Games gold medals by a snowboarder. Recognisable early in his career for his long red hair, he was nicknamed "The Flying Tomato" (Italian: Il Pomodoro Volante).

Early life

White was born in San Diego, California, and grew up in Carlsbad. He was born with a congenital heart defect, Tetralogy of Fallot, and underwent two open-heart surgeries before the age of one. He took up snowboarding at age six, encouraged by his older brother, and was sponsored by Burton Snowboards at age seven. He also excelled at skateboarding and was mentored by Tony Hawk, turning professional in skateboarding as a teenager.

Snowboarding career

White won the first of his many X Games titles in 2003. At the 2006 Winter Olympics in Turin he won gold in the halfpipe, confirming his status as the sport's dominant figure. He developed the Double McTwist 1260 — a double backflip with three and a half twists — and landed it at the 2010 Vancouver Olympics, where he won a second gold on a run whose score he could not improve but performed the trick anyway as a victory lap.

At the 2012 Winter X Games in Aspen, White earned the first perfect score of 100 in the men's snowboard superpipe. He finished a disappointing fourth at the 2014 Sochi Olympics, where he also withdrew from the slopestyle event. At Pyeongchang 2018, in his fourth Olympics, he landed back-to-back 1440s on the final run to score 97.75 and defeat Japan's Ayumu Hirano for a third gold medal. His final competition was the 2022 Beijing Olympics, where he finished fourth and announced his retirement.

Skateboarding

White is one of the few athletes to compete at elite level in both winter and summer action sports. He won X Games gold in skateboard vert in 2007 and 2011 and was the first athlete to win medals at both the Summer and Winter X Games in different sports.

Other ventures and legacy

White has appeared in films, fronted the video game Shaun White Snowboarding (2008), played in the band Bad Things, and founded the Air + Style action-sports festival. In 2024 he launched the Snow League, a professional halfpipe competition series. He is widely credited with bringing snowboarding to a mainstream global audience and pushing the technical progression of halfpipe riding over a career spanning two decades.
