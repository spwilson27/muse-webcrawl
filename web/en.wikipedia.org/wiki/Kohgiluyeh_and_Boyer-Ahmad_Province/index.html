Kohgiluyeh and Boyer-Ahmad Province (Persian: استان کهگیلویه و بویراحمد) is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, located in the south-west of the country in the Zagros Mountains. Its capital is the city of Yasuj.

Geography

The province covers about 15,500 square kilometres of mountainous terrain, much of it covered with oak forests; the Dena mountain range, with peaks over 4,400 metres among the highest in the Zagros, lies on its north-eastern border and is protected as a national park and biosphere reserve. The climate varies from the cool highlands around Yasuj and Sisakht to warm lowlands in the south-west near Gachsaran, an important oil-producing area.

People and administration

The population, about 713,000 at the 2016 census, is predominantly Lur, speaking the Luri language. The province is divided into counties including Boyer-Ahmad, Kohgiluyeh, Gachsaran, Dena, Bahmai, Charam, Landeh and Basht, with principal cities Yasuj, Dehdasht, Gachsaran (Dogonbadan) and Sisakht. Historically the area was governed as part of neighbouring provinces; it became a separate province in the 1970s. The economy rests on agriculture, livestock, oil production around Gachsaran, and growing domestic tourism drawn to its mountains, waterfalls and nomad culture.
