List of multiple Olympic medalists

This is a list of multiple Olympic medalists, ranking athletes by the total number of medals (gold, silver and bronze) won at the Olympic Games. American swimmer Michael Phelps leads the list with 28 medals, including 23 golds. Soviet gymnast Larisa Latynina held the record for nearly half a century with 18 medals and remains the most decorated female Olympian.
