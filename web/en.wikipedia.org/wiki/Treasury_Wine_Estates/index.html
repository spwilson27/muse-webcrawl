Treasury Wine Estates

Type: Public

Traded as: ASX: TWE

Industry: Wine

Headquarters: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia

Founded: 2011 (demerged from Foster's Group)

Treasury Wine Estates (TWE) is an Australian global winemaking and distribution company headquartered in Melbourne. It is one of the world's largest wine companies, with vineyards and wineries in Australia, New Zealand, the United States, Italy, France and China. The company was created in 2011 when the brewing conglomerate Foster's Group demerged its wine business and listed it separately on the Australian Securities Exchange.

History and brands

TWE's portfolio includes some of the most famous names in wine. Its flagship is Penfolds, producer of Grange, Australia's most celebrated wine. Other brands include Wolf Blass, Lindeman's, Rosemount, Wynns Coonawarra Estate and Seppelt in Australia; Beringer, Sterling Vineyards, Stags' Leap Winery and Beaulieu Vineyard in California; and Matua in New Zealand. In 2015 TWE acquired most of the wine business of Diageo, adding Blossom Hill and other brands, and it expanded into Bordeaux and China in subsequent years.

China tariffs and after

From late 2020, punitive Chinese tariffs on Australian wine — exceeding 200 per cent in effect — shut TWE out of what had become its most profitable market, forcing a major reallocation of Penfolds fruit to other markets. The tariffs were removed in March 2024 as relations thawed, and TWE began rebuilding its Chinese business.
