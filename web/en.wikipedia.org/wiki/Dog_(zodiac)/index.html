Dog (zodiac)

The Dog is the eleventh of the 12-year cycle of animals which appear in the Chinese zodiac related to the Chinese calendar. The Year of the Dog is associated with the Earthly Branch symbol 戌.

Dog (zodiac)
Position: 11th
Earthly Branch: 戌 (xū)
Recent years: 2006, 2018, 2030

Years

Recent and upcoming Years of the Dog include 1934, 1946, 1958, 1970, 1982, 1994, 2006, 2018 and 2030, each associated in cycle with one of the five elements (wood, fire, earth, metal, water). People born in these years are said to be Dogs.

Personality traits (traditional)

In Chinese astrology, Dogs are described as loyal, honest, kind, cautious and prudent, with a strong sense of justice — but also anxious, pessimistic and stubborn. Compatibility is traditionally highest with Tiger and Horse, and lowest with Dragon, Ox and Goat.

Legend

In the Great Race legend that ordered the zodiac, the Dog — a good swimmer who stopped to play in the water — finished eleventh, just before the Pig.
