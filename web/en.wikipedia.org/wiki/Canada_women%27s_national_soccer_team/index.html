The Canada women's national soccer team represents Canada in international women's association football and is overseen by the Canadian Soccer Association. The team is one of the strongest in CONCACAF and has competed at every FIFA Women's World Cup since 1995, hosting the tournament in 2015.

History

Canada played its first official international in 1986 against the United States. A breakthrough came at the 2003 Women's World Cup, where Canada finished fourth. Under coach John Herdman, the team won bronze medals at the 2012 London and 2016 Rio Olympics. Led by captain Christine Sinclair — who retired in 2023 as the highest-scoring international footballer of any gender, with 190 goals — Canada won gold at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics (held in 2021), defeating Sweden on penalties in the final under coach Bev Priestman.

World Cup record

Canada's best World Cup results are fourth place in 2003 and a quarter-final appearance as hosts in 2015. The team also won the CONCACAF Women's Championship in 1998 and 2010.
