Regions of Tanzania

Tanzania is divided into thirty-one regions (mikoa), twenty-six on the mainland and five in the semi-autonomous archipelago of Zanzibar. Each region is headed by a Regional Commissioner appointed by the President, and is subdivided into districts.

History

At independence in 1961 Tanganyika had nine provinces. The modern regional system has repeatedly been expanded: new regions created in 2012 included Geita, Katavi, Njombe and Simiyu, and Songwe Region was created in 2016 from the western part of Mbeya Region, bringing the total to thirty-one.

Mainland regions

The mainland regions include, among others, Arusha, Dar es Salaam, Dodoma (the capital region), Geita, Iringa, Kagera, Katavi, Kigoma, Kilimanjaro, Lindi, Manyara, Mara, Mbeya, Morogoro, Mtwara, Mwanza, Njombe, Pwani, Rukwa, Ruvuma, Shinyanga, Simiyu, Singida, Songwe, Tabora and Tanga.

Zanzibar regions

Zanzibar comprises Mjini Magharibi, Kusini Unguja and Kaskazini Unguja on Unguja island, and Kusini Pemba and Kaskazini Pemba on Pemba island.
