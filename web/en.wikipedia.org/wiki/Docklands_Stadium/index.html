Docklands Stadium

Docklands Stadium, known commercially as Marvel Stadium since 2018, is a multi-purpose sports and entertainment stadium in the Docklands area of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. Opened on 9 March 2000, it has a capacity of about 53,000 and features a retractable roof — the largest of its kind in the Southern Hemisphere.

History

The stadium was built on land in the Melbourne Docklands, with construction beginning in 1997. It has been known under several naming-rights sponsors: Colonial Stadium (2000–2002), Telstra Dome (2002–2009), Etihad Stadium (2009–2018), and Marvel Stadium (2018–present) after a deal with the Walt Disney Company.

Uses

The venue is the headquarters of the Australian Football League and a home ground for several AFL clubs, and hosts cricket (home of the Melbourne Renegades in the Big Bash League), soccer, rugby league and union, concerts, and other events. Its movable seating tiers allow the arena to be reconfigured for rectangular-field sports.
