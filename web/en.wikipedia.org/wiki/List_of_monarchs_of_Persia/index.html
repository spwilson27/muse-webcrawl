List of monarchs of Persia

This is a list of monarchs of Persia (modern-day Iran), from the establishment of the Median kingdom and the Achaemenid Empire in the sixth century BC through successive dynasties — Hellenistic, Parthian, Sasanian, and the Islamic-era dynasties — to the last Shah of the Pahlavi dynasty, deposed in 1979. Persian monarchs traditionally bore the title Shah ("king") or, in grand form, Shahanshah ("King of Kings").
