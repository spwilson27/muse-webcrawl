QSFP

The Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable (QSFP) is a compact, hot-pluggable transceiver used for data communications. It interfaces networking hardware — switches, routers and servers — to fibre-optic or copper cables, and supports Ethernet, Fibre Channel, InfiniBand and SONET/SDH standards.

Variants

The original QSFP carried four lanes of 10 Gbit/s (40 Gigabit Ethernet as 40GBASE-SR4/LR4). Successive generations include QSFP+ (4 × 10 G), QSFP28 (4 × 25 G, giving 100 Gigabit Ethernet), QSFP56 (4 × 50 G, 200 G) and QSFP-DD, which doubles the electrical interface to eight lanes for 400 G and 800 G Ethernet.

Form factor

QSFP modules are roughly 18 mm wide and fit into metal cages on host boards; the QSFP-DD ("double density") connector retains backward compatibility with earlier QSFP modules. Optical connectors are usually MPO/MTP for parallel optics or duplex LC for two-fibre variants.
