Trøndelag

Trøndelag is a county and a traditional region in central Norway, centred on the Trondheim Fjord. Since 2018 the county has united the former counties of Nord-Trøndelag and Sør-Trøndelag into a single administrative unit. Its administrative centre and largest city is Trondheim, Norway's third-largest city and its historic first capital. The region is known for fertile agricultural land around the fjord, a strong folk culture, and its distinctive Trøndersk dialect.

History

Trøndelag was one of the petty kingdoms and law districts of Viking Age Norway; its assembly, the Frostating, met near Trondheim. The region fiercely resisted and then supported the early kings of Norway, and the Battle of Stiklestad (1030), where King Olaf Haraldsson fell, took place in Verdal in the north of the region.
