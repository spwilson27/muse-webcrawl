Dalgona coffee

Dalgona coffee is a beverage made by whipping equal proportions of instant coffee powder, sugar, and hot water until creamy, then adding it to cold or hot milk. Also known as whipped or beaten coffee.


Type: Coffee beverage
Ingredients: Instant coffee, sugar, water, milk

History

Similar beaten coffees existed in India and Greece. The craze began in January 2020 when South Korean actor Jung Il-woo tasted whipped coffee in Macau and compared it to dalgona honeycomb toffee. Videos spread on TikTok during COVID-19 lockdowns, making it a global phenomenon in spring 2020.

Preparation

Coffee, sugar, and hot water in a 1:1:1 ratio are whipped to a thick foam and spooned over milk with ice. The stable foam depends on instant coffee's processing.

In culture

It became emblematic of pandemic-era home cooking and social-media food trends.
