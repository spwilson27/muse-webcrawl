Goalkeeper

In many team sports that involve scoring goals, the goalkeeper (sometimes termed the goalie, netminder, goalman, goal guard or keeper) is a designated player charged with directly preventing the opposing team from scoring by blocking or intercepting opposing shots on goal. Such positions exist in bandy, rink bandy, camogie, association football, Gaelic football, international rules football, handball, hurling, ice hockey, water polo and lacrosse, among others.

In association football, the goalkeeper is the only player permitted to handle the ball with the hands, and only within their own penalty area. They traditionally wear a jersey of a distinct colour from both teams and often wear gloves. The position demands shot-stopping, command of the penalty area, distribution and organisation of the defence.

Usually, a goalkeeper stays in the goal area, but variations — such as the sweeper-keeper who advances far from goal — have become prominent in modern tactics.
