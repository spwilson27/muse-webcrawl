ZeniMax Media

ZeniMax Media Inc. is an American video game holding company based in Rockville, Maryland. Founded in 1999 by Christopher Weaver and Robert A. Altman, it is the parent company of Bethesda Softworks and a group of major game development studios. In March 2021 Microsoft acquired ZeniMax for US$7.5 billion, making it part of Xbox Game Studios.

History

Weaver had co-founded Bethesda Softworks in 1986; ZeniMax was created in 1999 as a new parent structure, with attorney Robert A. Altman as chief executive. The company grew through the success of The Elder Scrolls and Fallout series and through acquisitions: id Software in 2009, Arkane Studios and MachineGames in 2010, Tango Gameworks in 2010, and BattleCry Studios in 2012.

Acquisition by Microsoft

Microsoft announced its acquisition of ZeniMax on 21 September 2020 and completed it on 9 March 2021 for $7.5 billion in cash. The deal brought franchises including The Elder Scrolls, Fallout, Doom, Wolfenstein, Dishonored and Starfield under Microsoft control, and later releases such as Starfield (2023) were published through Bethesda.

Studios

ZeniMax's studios include Bethesda Game Studios (Maryland, Montreal, Austin, Dallas), id Software (Texas), Arkane Studios (Lyon and Austin), MachineGames (Sweden), ZeniMax Online Studios (Maryland) and Roundhouse Studios. Tango Gameworks in Japan was closed in 2024 as part of Microsoft cost reductions, and later revived under Krafton.
