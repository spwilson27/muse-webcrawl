Ace the Bat-Hound is a fictional superhero dog appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Bill Finger and artist Sheldon Moldoff, he debuted in Batman #92 (June 1955), during the whimsical Silver Age of comics, shortly after the introduction of Krypto the Superdog.

Character history

In his original incarnation, Ace was a German Shepherd found by Batman and Robin after his owner was kidnapped; he assisted in tracking criminals, wearing a bat-shaped mask to conceal his distinctive star-shaped forehead marking. Ace appeared regularly through the early 1960s but faded as Batman comics became more serious. Later continuities reimagined him as a former fighting or shelter dog adopted by Bruce Wayne or Damian Wayne, usually without mask or superheroics.

In other media

Ace appears in animated series including Krypto the Superdog (as a regular character modelled on the masked Silver Age version), Batman Beyond (as the elderly Bruce Wayne's loyal dog), and DC League of Super-Pets (2022), where he is voiced by Kevin Hart as the leader of a group of shelter animals. He also appears in the video game Lego DC Super-Villains and other spin-offs.
