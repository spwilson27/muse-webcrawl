Rise Records is an American record label based in Portland, Oregon. Founded in 1991 by Craig Ericson in Roseburg, Oregon, the label is primarily focused on alternative rock, heavy metal, and punk rock. In 2015, BMG Rights Management acquired the label.

History

Ericson founded Rise Records in 1991 while still in high school, releasing a small number of 7-inch records before the label went dormant while he attended Oregon State University. After graduating, he revived the label in 1999 in Portland. Rise grew substantially during the 2000s and 2010s, becoming one of the most prominent independent labels for metalcore and post-hardcore, earning gold records with acts such as Sleeping with Sirens.

In May 2015, BMG announced it had acquired Rise Records, with Ericson remaining in charge of day-to-day operations. Rise also operated the imprint Velocity Records.
