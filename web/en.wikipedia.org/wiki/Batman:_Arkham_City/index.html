Batman: Arkham City

Batman: Arkham City is a 2011 action-adventure video game developed by Rocksteady Studios and published by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment. It is the sequel to Batman: Arkham Asylum (2009) and the second instalment in the Batman: Arkham series. Written by veteran Batman writer Paul Dini with Paul Crocker and Sefton Hill, the game features Kevin Conroy as Batman and Mark Hamill as the Joker.

Batman surveys Arkham City, the open-air prison district of Gotham.

Plot and gameplay

A year after the events of Arkham Asylum, a section of Gotham City has been walled off as Arkham City, a vast prison run by psychiatrist Hugo Strange under the authority of Mayor Quincy Sharp. Bruce Wayne is arrested and incarcerated there, and as Batman he must uncover Strange's sinister "Protocol 10" while contending with the Joker, who is dying from the Titan formula and poisons Batman to force him to find a cure developed by Mr. Freeze. Catwoman is a playable character in parts of the game, and side missions feature villains including Two-Face, the Penguin, the Riddler, Bane, Poison Ivy, Ra's al Ghul and Deadshot.

The game expands the free-flow combat and predator stealth of its predecessor into an open-world setting, adding gliding and dive-bombing traversal across the city. It was released for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in October 2011 and for Windows the following month, with later ports to Wii U, macOS and the Nintendo Switch as part of compilations.

Reception

Arkham City received universal acclaim, with review aggregators placing it among the highest-rated superhero games ever made; it won numerous Game of the Year awards and sold millions of copies within its first months. It was followed by the prequel Batman: Arkham Origins (2013) and the sequel Batman: Arkham Knight (2015). The game's vehicles and characters, including its version of the Batmobile mythos, influenced later Batman media.
