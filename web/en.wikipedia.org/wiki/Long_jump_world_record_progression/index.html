Long jump world record progression

The World Athletics long jump world record progression charts the improvement of the world record in the men's and women's long jump since records began to be officially ratified. The first world record in the men's long jump was recognised by the International Amateur Athletics Federation, now known as World Athletics, in 1912. The current men's world record of 8.95 m (29 ft 4¼ in) was set by Mike Powell of the United States in 1991. The current women's world record of 7.52 m (24 ft 8 in) was set by Galina Chistyakova of the Soviet Union in 1988.

Jesse Owens' leap of 8.13 m in 1935 stood for 25 years. Bob Beamon's jump of 8.90 m at the 1968 Mexico City Olympics shattered the record by 55 cm and endured for nearly 23 years — one of the most famous records in sport. It was finally broken by Mike Powell at the 1991 World Championships in Tokyo, in a celebrated duel with Carl Lewis, who himself jumped 8.91 m with an illegal tailwind and 8.87 m legally in the same competition.
