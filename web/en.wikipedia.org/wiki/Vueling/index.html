Vueling

Vueling S.A. is a Spanish low-cost airline based in Viladecans, in Greater Barcelona. It is Spain's largest airline by fleet size and number of destinations. Vueling operates bases across Spain and at several European airports, serving over 100 destinations in Africa, Asia, Europe, and the Middle East.

History

Vueling was established in February 2004 and commenced operations on 1 July 2004 with a flight between Barcelona and Ibiza. The name is a portmanteau of the Spanish word vuelo (flight) and the English suffix -ing. Initially funded by the Apax Partners investment group, the airline grew rapidly across the Spanish domestic and European markets.

In 2009, Vueling merged with rival Spanish low-cost carrier Clickair, retaining the Vueling name. In 2013, International Airlines Group (IAG), the parent of Iberia and British Airways, acquired control of the airline. Vueling flies a fleet of Airbus A320 family aircraft, including A319, A320, A320neo and A321 variants, and is known for its grey-and-yellow dotted livery.
