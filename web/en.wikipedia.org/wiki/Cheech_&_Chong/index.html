Cheech & Chong

Cheech & Chong are an American comedy duo consisting of Cheech Marin and Tommy Chong. The duo found commercial and cultural success in the 1970s and 1980s with their stand-up routines, comedy albums and feature films, built around cannabis-centred counterculture humour and their personas as laid-back stoners.

MembersCheech Marin, Tommy Chong
Years active1969–1988; 2008–present
GenresStand-up, sketch, stoner comedy

Career

Marin and Chong met in Vancouver, Canada, in the late 1960s and developed their act in improvisational theatre before moving to Los Angeles. Their self-titled debut comedy album appeared in 1971, followed by records such as Big Bambu (1972) and Los Cochinos (1973), which won the Grammy Award for Best Comedy Album. Their routines, including "Dave's not here", became counterculture staples.

Films

The duo's first film, Up in Smoke (1978), was a major box-office success and effectively launched the stoner film genre. Sequels and follow-ups included Cheech and Chong's Next Movie (1980), Nice Dreams (1981), Things Are Tough All Over (1982), Still Smokin (1983) and The Corsican Brothers (1984). The partnership dissolved in the mid-1980s as both pursued solo careers, but they reunited for concert tours beginning in 2008 and voiced characters in the animated Cheech & Chong's Animated Movie (2013).
