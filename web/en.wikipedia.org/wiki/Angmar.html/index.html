Angmar

Illustration of Carn Dûm, capital of Angmar

Angmar is a fictional realm in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium, a kingdom in the far north of Eriador founded around T.A. 1300 by the Lord of the Nazgûl, thereafter known as the Witch-king of Angmar. From his capital at Carn Dûm in the Mountains of Angmar, the Witch-king waged a centuries-long war against the Dúnedain kingdoms of Arnor.

History

Angmar drew its strength from evil Men, Orcs, Trolls and other creatures of the north. After Arnor split into Arthedain, Cardolan and Rhudaur, Angmar corrupted and conquered Rhudaur, destroyed Cardolan (whose last prince died in T.A. 1409), and finally overthrew Arthedain in T.A. 1974, ending the North-kingdom. The following year a combined force of Gondor, Elves and surviving Dúnedain crushed Angmar at the Battle of Fornost; the Witch-king fled and Angmar ceased to exist, its lands left largely deserted.
