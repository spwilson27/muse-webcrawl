Saint Joseph

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[hero image: Saint Joseph]
Joseph with the infant Jesus

Joseph (Hebrew: יוסף, Yosef; Greek: Ἰωσήφ) was a 1st-century Jewish man of Nazareth who, according to the canonical Gospels, was married to Mary, the mother of Jesus, and the legal father of Jesus. In Christian tradition he is venerated as Saint Joseph, the patron saint of workers, fathers, families, and a happy death, and since 1870 as patron of the universal Church in Roman Catholicism.

In the Gospels

Joseph is described in the Gospels of Matthew and Luke as a descendant of King David and a righteous man who worked as a tekton, usually translated "carpenter" or craftsman. When Mary was found to be pregnant, Joseph planned to divorce her quietly, but an angel appeared to him in a dream announcing that the child was conceived by the Holy Spirit, and Joseph took her as his wife. After Jesus's birth he received the Magi, fled with his family to Egypt to escape Herod's massacre of the innocents, and later settled in Nazareth. He last appears in the canonical accounts when the twelve-year-old Jesus is found in the Temple.

Veneration

Devotion to Joseph developed gradually. His feast day, 19 March, has been celebrated since at least the 10th century in the West; Pope Gregory XV made it a holy day of obligation in 1621. In 1955 Pope Pius XII introduced the feast of Saint Joseph the Worker on 1 May. Eastern Orthodox and Eastern Catholic churches commemorate him on the Sunday after Christmas as part of the Holy Ancestors. Joseph is the patron saint of many places, including Canada, Croatia, Korea, and Vietnam, and of numerous professions, especially carpenters and workers.

In art

Joseph is typically depicted as an older man with a beard, holding the Christ Child, a lily, or carpenter's tools. Scenes from his life — the betrothal, the flight into Egypt, and his death attended by Jesus and Mary — became especially popular in Counter-Reformation art. Because he is not described as present at the Crucifixion, tradition holds that he died before Jesus's public ministry, in the arms of Jesus and Mary; hence his patronage of a happy death.
