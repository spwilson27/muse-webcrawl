Toys "R" Us is an American toy, clothing, and baby product retailer founded by Charles Lazarus in 1948 as a baby-furniture store, which later became a category-dominant toy superstore chain with locations worldwide. Its mascot, Geoffrey the Giraffe, and its jingle "I don't wanna grow up" became cultural icons.

History

Lazarus converted his Washington, D.C. children's furniture store into a toy supermarket format in 1957, pioneering warehouse-style toy retailing with self-service and deep discounts. The company grew rapidly, went public, and at its peak operated more than 1,500 stores internationally. In 2005 it was taken private in a leveraged buyout by Bain Capital, KKR, and Vornado Realty Trust.

Bankruptcy and revival

Burdened by debt from the buyout and competition from big-box retailers and e-commerce, Toys "R" Us filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in September 2017 and closed all of its US stores in 2018. The brand was later revived in the United States, including shop-in-shop locations inside Macy's department stores from 2022, while international operations continued under other owners.
