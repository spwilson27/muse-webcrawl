Armageddon

Armageddon (from Ancient Greek: Ἁρμαγεδών Harmagedṓn, from Hebrew: הר מגידו har Məgīddō) is the prophesied location of a gathering of armies for a battle during the end times, which is variously interpreted as either a literal or a symbolic location.

Text

The term is used once in the New Testament, in Revelation 16:16. Megiddo in northern Israel was the site of many ancient battles. In Christian eschatology, Armageddon is associated with the return of Christ, the defeat of evil, and the Last Judgment.

Interpretations

Historicist, preterist, futurist and idealist interpretations differ. Jehovah's Witnesses teach Armageddon is God's war against wickedness. In Islamic eschatology similar end-times battles are described. Baha'i interpretations are largely symbolic.

Popular culture

The word has entered general usage for any catastrophic end-of-the-world scenario, and is the title of films including the 1998 film Armageddon.
