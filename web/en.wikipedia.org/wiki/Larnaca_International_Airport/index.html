Larnaca International Airport

Larnaca International Airport – Glafcos Clerides (IATA: LCA, ICAO: LCLK) is an international airport located about 4 km (2.5 mi) southwest of Larnaca, Cyprus. It is the country's main international gateway and the larger of the two commercial airports in the area controlled by the Republic of Cyprus, the other being Paphos International Airport.

History

The airport was built quickly in 1974–75 after the Turkish invasion of Cyprus closed Nicosia International Airport, initially using a short runway and prefabricated facilities. It was repeatedly expanded, and a new terminal opened in November 2009. In 2016 it was renamed in honour of former president Glafcos Clerides. The airport is operated by Hermes Airports under a concession.

Facilities and traffic

Larnaca has a single runway (04/22) of about 3,000 m and a terminal designed for around 7.5 million passengers annually. Before the pandemic it handled over 8 million passengers in 2019, served by Aegean Airlines, Cyprus Airways, Wizz Air (which opened a base in 2020) and many European and Middle Eastern carriers, with heavy seasonal tourist traffic.
