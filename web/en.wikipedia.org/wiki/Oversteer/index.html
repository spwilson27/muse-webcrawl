Oversteer

Oversteer is what occurs when a car turns (steers) by more than the amount commanded by the driver. It happens when the rear tyres lose grip before the front tyres, causing the rear of the vehicle to swing outward and the car to rotate more than intended, potentially leading to a spin.

Oversteer is influenced by weight distribution, rear-wheel drive power application, suspension tuning and road conditions. In contrast, understeer occurs when the front tyres lose grip first and the car runs wide.

Skilled drivers counter-steer to control oversteer, a technique deliberately exploited in drifting and rally driving. Electronic stability control systems brake individual wheels to prevent loss of control from oversteer in everyday driving.
