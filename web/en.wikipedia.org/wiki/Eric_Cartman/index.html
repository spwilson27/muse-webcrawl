Eric Cartman

First appearance: "Cartman Gets an Anal Probe" (1997)
Created by: Trey Parker, Matt Stone
Voiced by: Trey Parker

Eric Theodore Cartman, commonly referred to simply as Cartman, is a fictional character in the animated series South Park, created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone and voiced by Parker. One of the four central characters alongside Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski, and Kenny McCormick, Cartman is an overweight, foul-mouthed, manipulative and often bigoted elementary school student living in the town of South Park, Colorado.

Character

Cartman is frequently portrayed as the series' antagonist: selfish, prejudiced, and scheming, with catchphrases including "Respect my authoritah!" and his feuds with Kyle. His elaborate plots — from founding a Christian rock band for a bet to the infamous chili revenge in "Scott Tenorman Must Die" — are a staple of the show. Despite this, he occasionally shows cunning intelligence and leadership. His single mother, Liane Cartman, indulges his behaviour.

Reception

Cartman is one of the most popular and recognisable animated characters of his era, frequently cited in lists of the greatest cartoon characters, and his voice and catchphrases have entered popular culture. Scholars have analysed him as a vehicle for the show's satire of bigotry and American self-absorption.
