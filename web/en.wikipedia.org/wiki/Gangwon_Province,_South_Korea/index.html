Gangwon Province (Korean: 강원도), officially the Gangwon State since June 2023, is a province of South Korea in the country's northeast, with its capital at Chuncheon. It is bordered by the East Sea (Sea of Japan) to the east and North Korea to the north; the Korean Demilitarized Zone bisects the historical Gangwon region, whose northern part forms North Korea's Kangwon Province.

Geography

The province is dominated by the Taebaek Mountains, which include Seoraksan National Park, one of Korea's most celebrated mountain landscapes. The region is the least densely populated in South Korea and is known for winter sports; Pyeongchang County hosted the 2018 Winter Olympics, and Gangneung hosted the ice events. The 2024 Winter Youth Olympics were also held in the province.

Economy and transport

Agriculture, fishing, mining and tourism are important industries; the province is famed for potatoes, corn and squid. The completion of expressways and the Gyeonggang high-speed rail line to Seoul greatly improved access in the late 2010s.
