Reservoir Dogs

Director: Quentin Tarantino
Released: 1992
Starring: Harvey Keitel, Tim Roth, Michael Madsen, Steve Buscemi

Reservoir Dogs is a 1992 American crime film written and directed by Quentin Tarantino in his feature-length debut. It stars Harvey Keitel, Tim Roth, Michael Madsen, Chris Penn, Steve Buscemi, Lawrence Tierney, Edward Bunker, and Tarantino as a group of jewel thieves whose planned heist goes disastrously wrong.

Plot

The film tells, in nonlinear fashion, the events before and after a botched diamond robbery in Los Angeles. The surviving criminals, known by colour-coded aliases (Mr. White, Mr. Blonde, Mr. Pink, Mr. Orange), regroup at a warehouse, where suspicion grows that one of them is an undercover police officer — in fact Mr. Orange. The heist itself is never shown.

Reception and legacy

Premiering at Sundance, the film was a modest box-office success but became a landmark of independent cinema, acclaimed for its dialogue, nonlinear structure, eclectic 1970s soundtrack framed by the radio show "K-Billy's Super Sounds of the '70s", and its stylized violence — including the infamous ear-cutting scene set to "Stuck in the Middle with You". It launched Tarantino's career and is frequently ranked among the greatest debut films.
