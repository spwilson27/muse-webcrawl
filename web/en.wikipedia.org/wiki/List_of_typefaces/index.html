List of typefaces

This is a list of notable typefaces, arranged by classification. Typefaces are grouped into serif, sans-serif, script, monospaced, and display designs, among other categories.

Serif

  • Times New Roman (1932), designed by Stanley Morison and Victor Lardent for The Times of London.
  • Garamond, revivals of the 16th-century designs of Claude Garamond.
  • Baskerville (1757), by John Baskerville.
  • Bodoni (1798), by Giambattista Bodoni.
  • Georgia (1993), by Matthew Carter, designed for on-screen reading.
  • Caslon (1722), by William Caslon.
  • Palatino (1948), by Hermann Zapf.
  • Book Antiqua, Century Schoolbook, Didot, Plantin, Sabon.

Sans-serif

  • Helvetica (1957), by Max Miedinger and Eduard Hoffmann.
  • Arial (1982), by Robin Nicholas and Patricia Saunders.
  • Futura (1927), by Paul Renner.
  • Gill Sans (1928), by Eric Gill.
  • Frutiger (1976), by Adrian Frutiger.
  • Univers (1957), by Adrian Frutiger.
  • Verdana (1996) and Tahoma (1994), by Matthew Carter.
  • Calibri (2004), by Luc(as) de Groot; Roboto (2011); Open Sans (2010).

Monospaced

  • Courier (1955), by Howard "Bud" Kettler.
  • Consolas, Menlo, Monaco, Source Code Pro, Ubuntu Mono.

Script and display

  • Comic Sans (1994), by Vincent Connare.
  • Zapfino, Brush Script, Papyrus, Impact (1965), Cooper Black (1922).
