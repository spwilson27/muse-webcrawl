The Racecourse Ground (Welsh: Y Cae Ras), currently known as STōK Cae Ras for sponsorship reasons, is a football stadium in Wrexham, Wales. It is the home of Wrexham A.F.C. and is recognised by Guinness World Records as the world's oldest international football stadium still in use, having hosted Wales' first home international match in 1877.

History

The site was originally used for horse racing from 1807 and cricket before Wrexham Olympic, later Wrexham A.F.C., began playing football there in 1864, making it one of the oldest football grounds in the world. It hosted the first Welsh home international in 1877, a match against Scotland.

Stands

The ground's stands include the Mold Road Stand, the Wrexham Lager Stand (formerly the Yale Stand), and the Kop, a large former terrace long closed before redevelopment plans in the 2020s following the club's takeover by Ryan Reynolds and Rob McElhenney.

Other uses

The Racecourse has also hosted rugby league and rugby union internationals, cricket, and concerts.
