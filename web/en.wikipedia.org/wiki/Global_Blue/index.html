Global Blue

Global Blue is a tourism shopping tax refund company headquartered in Switzerland. Founded in Sweden in 1980 as Sweden Tax Free Shopping, the company is best known for its Tax Free Shopping service, which allows shoppers to claim back the value-added tax (VAT) or goods and services tax (GST) on purchases made abroad. Global Blue is listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The company provides services that connect merchants, shoppers, and customs authorities: retailers issue tax-free forms for eligible purchases by international shoppers, who have the forms validated by customs on leaving the country and then receive refunds at refund points or by mail or card credit. Global Blue also offers dynamic currency conversion and payment services through related businesses.

History

The business expanded across Europe and Asia during the 1990s and 2000s, rebranding as Global Blue in 2010. Private equity firm Silver Lake acquired a majority stake in 2012. In 2020 Global Blue went public through a merger with a special-purpose acquisition company, Far Point Acquisition Corporation.
