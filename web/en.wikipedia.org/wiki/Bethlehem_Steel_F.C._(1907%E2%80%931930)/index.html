Bethlehem Steel F.C. (1907–1930)

Founded1907 (as Bethlehem F.C.)
Dissolved1930
GroundBethlehem Steel Athletic Field, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania
LeagueAmerican Soccer League

Bethlehem Steel Football Club was an American professional soccer team based in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, sponsored by the Bethlehem Steel corporation. Founded in 1907, it was one of the most successful early American soccer clubs, winning five U.S. Open Cups and seven American Cups.

History

The club began as the works team of the Bethlehem Steel plant and rose to national prominence in the 1910s and 1920s, drawing heavily on British and Scottish immigrant players. It won the first of its National Challenge Cup (U.S. Open Cup) titles in 1915 and added further titles in 1916, 1918, 1919 and 1926, a record it held for decades. In 1919 Bethlehem Steel toured Scandinavia, one of the first American teams to play in Europe.

Decline

The club was embroiled in the "Soccer War" between the American Soccer League and the U.S. Football Association in 1928–29, and amid the onset of the Great Depression and falling steel employment it folded in 1930.
