Folsom Field is an outdoor college football stadium in the western United States, located on the campus of the University of Colorado in Boulder. It is the home field of the Colorado Buffaloes of the Big 12 Conference. Opened in 1924 as Colorado Stadium, it was renamed in 1944 in honour of coach Fred Folsom.

History and features

The stadium occupies a site on the eastern edge of campus, 5,360 feet above sea level, among the highest venues in major college football. Successive expansions added an upper deck, luxury suites and the Flatiron-facing scoreboard; permanent lighting arrived in the late 1980s. Its west stands look out toward the Flatirons rock formations.

Beyond football, Folsom Field has hosted concerts — including famous shows by the Grateful Dead and the Rolling Stones — and the start/finish of the Bolder Boulder 10K road race on Memorial Day. The buffalo mascot Ralphie traditionally leads the team onto the field, a run that begins from the stadium's north end.
