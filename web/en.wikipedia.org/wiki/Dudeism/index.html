Dudeism

Dudeism is a philosophy and lifestyle inspired by "the Dude", the protagonist of the Coen brothers' 1998 film The Big Lebowski. Its adherents aim to "just take it easy, man" — promoting a relaxed, unhurried approach to life in the spirit of the film's hero, Jeffrey "the Dude" Lebowski, played by Jeff Bridges.

History

Dudeism was founded in 2005 by journalist Oliver Benjamin, who established the Church of the Latter-Day Dude, an organisation that ordains "Dudeist priests" online. The movement grew with the cult following of the film; tens of thousands of people have been ordained, and ordained priests can legally officiate weddings in some jurisdictions.

Beliefs and practice

Dudeism draws loosely on Taoism, Epicureanism and the example of the Dude himself. Its ethos is summed up in the film's line "the Dude abides": going with the flow, resisting excessive ambition and conflict, and enjoying simple pleasures — bowling, friends, baths, rugs and White Russians feature prominently in the movement's humorous iconography. Publications include The Dude De Ching and the book The Abide Guide.
