Alan Fred Titchmarsh (born 2 May 1949) is an English gardener and broadcaster. After working as a professional gardener and a gardening journalist, he established himself as a media personality through appearances on gardening programmes, and later became known as a presenter of programmes on other subjects, including classical music and natural history, as well as a novelist.

Early life

Titchmarsh was born in Ilkley in the West Riding of Yorkshire, the son of a textile mill worker and a plumber. He left school at 15 and worked as an apprentice gardener with Ilkley Council before studying at horticultural college and at the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, where he earned a diploma in horticulture.

Career

After working in publishing as an editor of gardening books, Titchmarsh began broadcasting, appearing as a gardening expert on the BBC magazine programme Nationwide and later on Breakfast Time. He became a household name as the presenter of the BBC gardening show Gardeners' World from 1996 to 2002, and of the garden makeover programme Ground Force, which drew large audiences and made garden design a mainstream television genre in Britain.

He has written numerous gardening books, a series of novels beginning with Only Dad (2001) and Mr Macgregor (2003), and volumes of memoirs. From 2011 he presented the ITV daytime chat programme The Alan Titchmarsh Show, and he has presented Love Your Garden and programmes on classical music for Classic FM. He was appointed MBE in the 2000 New Year Honours for services to horticulture and gardening.

Personal life

Titchmarsh married Alison in 1975 and they have two daughters. He lives in Hampshire and on the Isle of Wight, and is involved with numerous horticultural and charitable organisations.
