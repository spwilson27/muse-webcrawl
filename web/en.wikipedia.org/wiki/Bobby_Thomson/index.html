Robert Brown Thomson (October 25, 1923 – August 16, 2010) was a Scottish-born American professional baseball player, best remembered for hitting the "Shot Heard 'Round the World", the walk-off home run that won the 1951 National League pennant for the New York Giants.

The Shot Heard 'Round the World

On October 3, 1951, in the deciding game of a playoff against the Brooklyn Dodgers, Thomson hit a three-run home run off Ralph Branca in the bottom of the ninth inning, giving the Giants a 5–4 victory and completing a comeback from a 13½-game deficit — celebrated in Russ Hodges's famous call, "The Giants win the pennant!" The moment became one of the most iconic in American sports.

Career

Thomson was a three-time All-Star who hit 264 career home runs, mostly with the Giants, before later stints with several other clubs. Scottish-born and raised on Staten Island, he was nicknamed the "Staten Island Scot".
