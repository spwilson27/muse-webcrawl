Wendy's is an American international fast food restaurant chain founded by Dave Thomas on November 15, 1969, in Columbus, Ohio. Known for its square hamburger patties, sea-salt fries and the Frosty dessert, it is one of the world's largest hamburger chains, long contesting second place in the U.S. with Burger King behind McDonald's.

History

Thomas named the restaurant after his daughter Melinda Lou "Wendy" Thomas, whose likeness — a smiling girl with red pigtails — became the logo. The chain emphasised fresh, never-frozen beef and made-to-order burgers. Thomas became the chain's folksy television spokesman in 1989, appearing in hundreds of ads until his death in 2002. Wendy's merged with Arby's parent Triarc in 2008 before the brands were separated again in 2011.

Products

Signature items include the Dave's Single/Double/Triple burgers, the Baconator, spicy chicken sandwiches, chilli, baked potatoes and salads, and the Frosty — a frozen dairy dessert sold since the beginning in chocolate (and later vanilla and seasonal flavours).

Marketing

The 1984 "Where's the beef?" campaign, starring Clara Peller, became a cultural catchphrase. In recent years Wendy's social media presence, especially a roasting, meme-savvy Twitter account, has won large followings.
