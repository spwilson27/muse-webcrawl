James Russell Mercer (born 26 December 1970) is an American singer-songwriter and guitarist, best known as the founder and frontman of the indie rock band the Shins. He is also one half of Broken Bells, a collaboration with producer Brian Burton (Danger Mouse).

Early life

Mercer was born in Honolulu, Hawaii, and grew up largely in Albuquerque, New Mexico, after spending part of his childhood abroad, including in England, where his father was stationed with the U.S. Air Force. Exposure to British bands of the 1980s shaped his songwriting. In Albuquerque he played in the band Flake, later Flake Music, which released the album When You Land Here, It's Time to Return (1997) and toured with Modest Mouse.

The Shins

Mercer formed the Shins in 1996 as a side project, initially as a duo with drummer Jesse Sandoval. Signed to Sub Pop, the band released Oh, Inverted World (2001), whose songs "New Slang" and "Caring Is Creepy" reached a wide audience after "New Slang" featured prominently in the 2004 film Garden State. Chutes Too Narrow (2003) and the Grammy-nominated Wincing the Night Away (2007), which debuted at number 2 on the Billboard 200, established the Shins as one of the leading indie rock acts of the decade. Mercer later reconstituted the band with new members for Port of Morrow (2012) and Heartworms (2017).

Broken Bells and other work

In 2009 Mercer formed Broken Bells with Danger Mouse; their self-titled debut album appeared in 2010, followed by After the Disco (2014) and Into the Blue (2022). Mercer has also contributed to recordings by artists including Modest Mouse and Sparklehorse.
