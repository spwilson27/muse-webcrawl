Maddy Prior

Maddy Prior MBE (born 14 August 1947) is an English folk singer, best known as the lead vocalist of the folk rock band Steeleye Span, which she co-founded in 1969 with Tim Hart. Her clear, distinctive voice became one of the defining sounds of the British folk revival.

Career

Prior first recorded as a duo with Tim Hart before Steeleye Span's formation. With Steeleye she scored UK hits including "All Around My Hat" (1975). She has also released numerous solo albums, recorded two albums of silences-era duets with June Tabor as the Silly Sisters, and collaborated extensively with the Carnival Band on early music. She was appointed MBE in 2001 for services to folk music.
