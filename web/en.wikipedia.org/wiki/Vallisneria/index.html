Vallisneria


Family: Hydrocharitaceae
Common names: eelgrass, tape grass, vallis

Vallisneria is a genus of freshwater aquatic plants in the family Hydrocharitaceae, commonly called eelgrass or tape grass. Named after the Italian naturalist Antonio Vallisneri, the genus is distributed in tropical and subtropical waters worldwide.

Description

The plants are submerged perennials with long, ribbon-like leaves arising from a basal rosette and spreading by runners. Vallisneria is dioecious: female flowers are carried to the surface on long spiral stalks, where they are pollinated by free-floating male flowers; after pollination the stalk coils and draws the developing fruit underwater — a classic textbook example of water pollination (hydrophily).

Uses

Species such as Vallisneria spiralis and the larger V. americana are popular aquarium plants because they are hardy oxygenators, and they provide important habitat and food for aquatic animals, including manatees and waterfowl, in wild waterways.
