Noon Universe

The Noon Universe (Russian: Мир Полудня, Mir Poludnya) is a fictional future setting created by Soviet science fiction writers Arkady and Boris Strugatsky, shared across a series of novels and stories written between the late 1950s and the 1980s. It depicts an optimistic 22nd-century Earth — technologically advanced, largely communist, and at peace — and humanity's exploration of the stars.

Setting

Named after the novel Noon: 22nd Century, the world features near-abolition of scarcity, voluntary creative labour, and a fleet of starships. Secretive organisations such as COMCON monitor alien contact and the mysterious legacy of the Wanderers, a super-civilisation whose artefacts and interventions shape several plots. Recurring characters include the operative Maxim Kammerer and the progressor Rudolf Sikorski.

Major works

Books in the setting include The Land of Crimson Clouds, Noon: 22nd Century, Far Rainbow, Hard to Be a God, The Inhabited Island, The Kid from Hell, Beetle in the Anthill and The Waves Extinguish the Wind. The "progressors" — agents who covertly guide less advanced civilisations — became one of science fiction's most influential ideas.
