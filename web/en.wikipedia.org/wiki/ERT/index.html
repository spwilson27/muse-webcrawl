Hellenic Broadcasting Corporation (ERT)
Founded: 1938 (radio); TV from 1966
Country: Greece
Type: Public broadcaster

The Hellenic Broadcasting Corporation (Greek: Ellinikí Radiofonía Tileórasi, ERT) is the public broadcaster of Greece. It operates television channels including ERT1, ERT2 and ERT3, national and regional radio stations, and the international service ERT World.

History

ERT traces its origins to the first Greek state radio broadcasts of 1938 and began regular television broadcasts in 1966. It is a member of the European Broadcasting Union and broadcasts Greece's Eurovision Song Contest participation.

Closure and reopening

On 11 June 2013 the Greek government abruptly shut down ERT as an austerity measure, dismissing some 2,600 staff and provoking protests and international criticism; screens went black mid-broadcast. A successor broadcaster, NERIT, operated until ERT was restored by law and relaunched on 11 June 2015, rehiring many former employees.

Funding

ERT is funded mainly through a licence-style fee collected via electricity bills, supplemented by advertising.
