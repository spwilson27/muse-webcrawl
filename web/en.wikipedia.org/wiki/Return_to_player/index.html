Return to player

Return to player (RTP) is a term used in gambling to describe the percentage of all wagered money that a game, such as a slot machine or casino table game, will pay back to players over time. It is the complement of the house edge: a game with a 96% RTP has a 4% house edge and is expected, over a very large number of plays, to return $96 for every $100 wagered.

Interpretation

RTP is a long-run statistical average, not a prediction for any individual session; short-term results vary widely around it because of volatility (variance), which describes how unevenly payouts are distributed. Modern video slots commonly have RTPs between about 90% and 98%, and many jurisdictions require minimum RTPs or require the figure to be published in the game's help files. Table games have RTPs determined by their rules and by player strategy — blackjack played with basic strategy, for example, can exceed 99.5%, while keno and many lotteries are far lower.
