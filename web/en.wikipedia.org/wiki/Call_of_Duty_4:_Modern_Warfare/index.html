Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare is a 2007 first-person shooter developed by Infinity Ward and published by Activision. The fourth main installment in the Call of Duty series, it was the first set in the modern era rather than World War II.

Plot

The campaign follows British SAS operatives, including Captain Price and "Soap" MacTavish, and U.S. Marine Paul Jackson, as they fight a Russian ultranationalist movement and a Middle Eastern coup. The mission "All Ghillied Up" and the game's depiction of a nuclear detonation were widely praised.

Multiplayer and legacy

Its multiplayer introduced the create-a-class system, killstreaks, and prestige ranks that became genre standards. The game won numerous awards, sold over 15 million copies, spawned the Modern Warfare sub-series, and received a remaster in 2016.
