The Boomtown Rats

The Boomtown Rats are an Irish rock band formed in Dublin in 1975, fronted by singer Bob Geldof. They rose to fame during the punk and new wave era, scoring a string of UK hits between 1977 and 1982.

Career

The band's biggest successes were "Rat Trap" (1978), the first punk/new wave single to top the UK chart, and "I Don't Like Mondays" (1979), written after a school shooting in San Diego, which reached number one in the UK and many other countries. Other hits include "Lookin' After No. 1", "She's So Modern" and "Banana Republic".

Aftermath and reunion

Geldof's organisation of Band Aid (1984) and Live Aid (1985) overshadowed the band, which split in 1986. The Boomtown Rats reformed in 2013 and released a new album, Citizens of Boomtown, in 2020.
