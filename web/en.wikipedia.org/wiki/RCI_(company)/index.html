RCI (company)

RCI, formerly Resort Condominiums International, is a timeshare exchange company with over 4,300 affiliated resorts in 110 countries. Founded in Indiana in 1974 by Jon and Christel DeHaan, it is regarded as one of the pioneers of the timeshare exchange concept, alongside Interval International. RCI is part of the Travel + Leisure Co. (formerly Wyndham Destinations) portfolio of vacation ownership and exchange brands.

History

The DeHaans founded the company after purchasing a timeshare themselves and recognizing that owners wanted the flexibility to swap their weeks for stays elsewhere. RCI's exchange network allowed members to deposit their owned week and book a comparable week at another affiliated resort. The company grew rapidly with the timeshare industry during the 1980s and 1990s, was acquired by Cendant in 1996, and later became part of Wyndham Worldwide's hospitality businesses before the spin-off that created Wyndham Destinations, renamed Travel + Leisure Co. in 2021.

Services

RCI offers several membership tiers, including RCI Weeks, RCI Points (launched in 2000 as one of the first large points-based exchange systems), and the premium Registry Collection for luxury properties. Members pay membership and exchange fees; trading power or points values determine what exchanges can be booked. RCI also provides travel-related services and cruise exchanges.

Criticism

Like much of the timeshare industry, exchange companies have been criticized over availability of desirable weeks, fee structures, and the practice of renting deposited inventory to the general public.
