Raw (comics magazine)

Raw was a comics anthology edited by Art Spiegelman and Françoise Mouly, published 1980–1991. A flagship of the alternative comics movement, its avant-garde, oversized issues introduced American readers to European and experimental cartoonists and serialised Spiegelman's Maus.

Raw
Editors: Art Spiegelman, Françoise Mouly
Published: 1980–1991
Issues: 8 (vol.1) + 3 (vol.2)
Genre: Alternative / avant-garde comics
Notable: Maus serialisation; Charles Burns, Gary Panter

History

After Arcade magazine folded, Spiegelman and Mouly launched Raw from New York's SoHo art scene, printing at large format with tipped-in inserts and a deliberately art-object design. Contributors included Gary Panter, Charles Burns, Joost Swarte, Jacques Tardi, Sue Coe, Kim Deitch and Ben Katchor.

Maus

From issue 2 (1980), each Raw carried a chapter insert of Maus, Spiegelman's Holocaust memoir about his father, later a Pulitzer Prize-winning graphic novel (1992).

Legacy

Raw legitimised comics as literary/artistic form, influenced anthologies worldwide, and its second volume (Penguin paperback format, 1989–91) reached wider audiences.
