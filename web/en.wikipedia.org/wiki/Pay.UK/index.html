Pay.UK

Pay.UK is the United Kingdom's retail payments authority and the operator of the country's principal payment systems, including Bacs Direct Credit and Direct Debit, the Faster Payments Service, and the Image Clearing System for cheques. It was launched in 2017–2018 following the Payment Systems Regulator's consolidation of the former schemes (Bacs, Faster Payments Scheme Limited, and the Cheque and Credit Clearing Company) into a single operator, originally called the New Payment System Operator (NPSO), renamed Pay.UK in 2018.

Role

Pay.UK runs the infrastructure that moves money between UK bank accounts, processing billions of transactions each year: salaries and benefits via Bacs, direct debits for bills, near-instant transfers via Faster Payments, and digital cheque clearing. It also manages the Current Account Switch Service and Confirmation of Payee, a name-checking service intended to reduce authorised push payment fraud.

New Payments Architecture

Pay.UK is responsible for the New Payments Architecture (NPA), a programme to modernize the UK's interbank payment infrastructure with common standards based on ISO 20022 messaging.
