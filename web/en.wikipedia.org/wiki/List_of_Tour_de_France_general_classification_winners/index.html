List of Tour de France general classification winners

The general classification of the Tour de France is the most important classification of the race; its leader wears the yellow jersey (maillot jaune), and its winner is considered the winner of the Tour. Four riders have won five times: Jacques Anquetil, Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault, and Miguel Induráin. Lance Armstrong's seven victories (1999–2005) were stripped in 2012 for doping, and those editions have no official winner.

France has produced the most winning riders, followed by Belgium and Spain. The youngest winner was Henri Cornet in 1904 (aged 19); the oldest was Firmin Lambot in 1922 (aged 36).
