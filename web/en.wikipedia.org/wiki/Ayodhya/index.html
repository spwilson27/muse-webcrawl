Ayodhya (Hindi: अयोध्या) is a city situated on the banks of the Saryu River in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. It is the administrative headquarters of the Ayodhya district. Ayodhya is regarded as one of the seven most important pilgrimage sites (Sapta Puri) for Hindus, because, according to tradition, it was the birthplace of Rama, the protagonist of the Ramayana.

The city is identified as the legendary capital of the ancient Kosala Kingdom. According to the Ramayana, it was ruled by King Dasharatha, father of Rama. Ayodhya is also significant in Buddhism and Jainism: the Buddha is said to have spent time here, and several Jain Tirthankaras, including Rishabhdeva, are believed to have been born in the city.

History

Ancient Ayodhya was one of the largest cities of early India, described in epic literature as prosperous and well-fortified. In later centuries it passed through the hands of the Gupta, Gurjara-Pratihara, and other dynasties. Under the Mughals and then the Nawabs of Awadh, the neighbouring city of Faizabad grew in importance, while Ayodhya retained its religious character, lined with temples and ghats along the Saryu.

In 1528, the Babri Masjid was constructed in Ayodhya, traditionally believed to stand on the site of Rama's birthplace (Ram Janmabhoomi). The dispute over the site became one of the most contentious religious and political issues in modern India. The mosque was demolished in 1992, and in 2019 the Supreme Court of India awarded the disputed land for the construction of a Hindu temple. The Ram Mandir (Ram Temple) was consecrated in January 2024, drawing enormous attention and extensive redevelopment of the city.

Religious significance

Ayodhya is one of the Sapta Puri, the seven sacred cities of Hinduism. Pilgrims bathe in the Saryu River, especially during the festivals of Ram Navami and Deepotsav, when hundreds of thousands of earthen lamps (diyas) are lit along the ghats — a display that has set Guinness World Records. Important temples include Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, and the many shrines along the riverfront.

Demographics

As of the 2011 census, the city of Ayodhya had a population of 55,890. Hindi, in its Awadhi dialect, is the predominant spoken language. The majority of the population is Hindu, with a significant Muslim minority.

Transport

Ayodhya is connected by rail (Ayodhya Dham Junction) and road to Lucknow, Gorakhpur, and Varanasi. The Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, Ayodhya Dham, opened in December 2023, significantly improving the city's connectivity following the temple consecration.
