Phantasy Star Online

DeveloperSonic Team
PublisherSega
Release2000 (Dreamcast)
GenreAction role-playing
ModesSingle-player, multiplayer online

Phantasy Star Online is an online action role-playing game developed by Sonic Team and published by Sega for the Dreamcast in 2000. It is a spin-off of the Phantasy Star series and is widely credited as the first successful online role-playing game for home consoles.

Gameplay

Players create a character from several classes and species — hunters, rangers, and forces among human, newman, and android races — and explore the planet Ragol, which the colony ship Pioneer 2 is orbiting after an explosion devastated the surface settlement. Gameplay centers on real-time combat through linear dungeon areas, with up to four players cooperating online in instanced sessions. An innovative "word select" chat system allowed players speaking different languages to communicate through translated preset phrases and symbol chat icons.

Development and release

Produced under Yuji Naka, the game was designed to prove that console online gaming was viable. An expanded version, Phantasy Star Online Ver. 2, followed in 2001. Ports and expanded editions appeared on GameCube and Xbox as Episode I & II (2002), with additional content in Episode III: C.A.R.D. Revolution and the PC release Blue Burst (2004–2005).

Reception and legacy

The game was critically acclaimed for its addictive cooperative play and atmosphere, and sold over a million copies across versions. Official servers were shut down in stages between 2003 and 2010, though fan-run private servers continue to operate. It paved the way for sequels including Phantasy Star Universe (2006) and Phantasy Star Online 2 (2012).
