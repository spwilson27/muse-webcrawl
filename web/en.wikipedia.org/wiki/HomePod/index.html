HomePod

HomePod is a smart speaker developed by Apple Inc. Announced in June 2017 and released in February 2018, it was designed to deliver high-fidelity audio while integrating Apple's Siri voice assistant and the HomeKit smart-home platform.

DeveloperApple Inc.
TypeSmart speaker
Released9 February 2018 (1st generation)
AssistantSiri

Models

The original HomePod used an Apple A8 chip and an array of tweeters and microphones to adapt its sound to the room. It was discontinued in March 2021 in favour of the smaller, cheaper HomePod mini, introduced in 2020. A second-generation full-size HomePod was released in February 2023, with an updated design, temperature and humidity sensors, and support for the Matter smart-home standard.
