Ilan Shor (born 6 March 1987) is a Moldovan-Israeli businessman and politician. He became internationally known for his central role in the 2014 Moldovan bank fraud scandal, in which about US billion disappeared from Moldovan banks. He was convicted in Moldova in connection with the fraud and later fled the country.

Business career

Shor built a business empire in Moldova including duty-free shops, a football club, and banking interests; he chaired the administrative board of Banca de Economii, one of the banks involved in the 2014 fraud. A report by the investigative firm Kroll identified him as a key figure in the scheme.

Legal proceedings and politics

In 2017 Shor was sentenced to 7.5 years in prison for fraud, but remained free pending appeal while serving as mayor of Orhei. He led the populist Shor Party, entered parliament in 2019, and left Moldova that year. In 2023 a Moldovan court increased his sentence in absentia to 15 years, and the Constitutional Court outlawed his party. The United States and other countries imposed sanctions on Shor, accusing him of acting on behalf of Russian interests to destabilise Moldova.
