Femtocell

A femtocell is a small, low-power cellular base station, typically designed for use in a home or small business. It connects to the service provider's network via broadband (such as DSL or cable) and typically supports a small number of simultaneous mobile phones in a residential setting.

Benefits

Femtocells improve indoor coverage where signals from macrocell towers are weak, and offload traffic from the provider's radio network onto the user's broadband connection. Because the mobile device transmits over a very short distance, battery life improves and voice quality increases.

Deployment

Carriers in many countries have marketed femtocells under names such as "Network Extender" or "signal booster". Femtocells operate in licensed spectrum and are managed by the operator, unlike unlicensed Wi-Fi calling.
