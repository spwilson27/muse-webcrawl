Lassi

Lassi is a blended yoghurt-based drink originating from the Punjab region of the Indian subcontinent. It is made by blending yoghurt with water, spices and sometimes fruit, and is traditionally served chilled in a tall glass, often in an earthenware cup.

Varieties

  • Sweet lassi — flavoured with sugar, rosewater, cardamom or saffron, sometimes topped with a layer of malai (clotted cream).
  • Salty lassi (chaas-like) — seasoned with salt, roasted cumin and mint.
  • Mango lassi — blended with mango pulp, popular worldwide in Indian restaurants.
  • Bhang lassi — traditionally prepared with cannabis for festivals such as Holi in some regions.

Cultural notes

Lassi is a staple accompaniment to spicy Punjabi meals and a common street drink across India and Pakistan. Sweet Punjabi lassi from Amritsar and Lahore is famed for its thick, creamy top. Similar yoghurt drinks include Turkish ayran and Iranian doogh.
