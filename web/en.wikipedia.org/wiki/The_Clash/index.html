The Clash

The Clash
Origin: London, England
Years active: 1976–1986
Genres: Punk rock, post-punk, reggae, new wave
Members: Joe Strummer, Mick Jones, Paul Simonon, Topper Headon

The Clash were an English rock band formed in London in 1976, key figures of British punk. Dubbed "the Only Band That Matters", Joe Strummer, Mick Jones, Paul Simonon and Topper Headon fused punk with reggae, dub, funk, ska and rockabilly, with explicitly political lyrics.

Career

Albums include The Clash (1977), the double London Calling (1979) — often ranked among the greatest albums ever — triple Sandinista! (1980) and Combat Rock (1982), with hits "Rock the Casbah" and "Should I Stay or Should I Go".

Legacy

Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2003.
