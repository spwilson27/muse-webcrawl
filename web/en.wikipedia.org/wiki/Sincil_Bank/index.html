Sincil Bank

Sincil Bank, known for sponsorship reasons as the LNER Stadium, is a football stadium in Lincoln, England. It has been the home of Lincoln City Football Club since 1895.

History and description

The ground is named after the Sincil Dyke watercourse that runs alongside it. Its main stands include the Co-op Stand and the Stacey-West Stand, named in memory of two supporters who died in the Bradford City stadium fire of 1985. The stadium's capacity is around 10,000.

Lincoln City, nicknamed the Imps, have played most of their history in the lower divisions of the Football League, with notable recent runs including an FA Cup quarter-final in 2017 as a non-league club.
