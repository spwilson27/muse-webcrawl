Tank (military)

Tank (military)
DefinitionArmoured fighting vehicle on tracks with a turreted main gun
DebutBritish Mark I, Battle of the Somme, 1916
[Image: A main battle tank crossing dusty ground, turret traversed]

A tank is a heavily armoured, tracked combat vehicle designed for front-line combat, combining firepower (a turreted cannon), protection and cross-country mobility. Britain fielded the first tanks in 1916 to break trench deadlock in the First World War (in the wider crawl); tanks dominated land warfare by the Second.

Evolution

Cold War designs culminated in main battle tanks like the Leopard 2, Abrams and T-72 families. Cheap drones and top-attack missiles in 2020s wars have challenged the tank's survivability, prompting active-protection systems and redesigns rather than abandonment.
