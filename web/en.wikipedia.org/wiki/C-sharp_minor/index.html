C-sharp minor

C-sharp minor
Key signature: 4 sharps (F♯, C♯, G♯, D♯)
Relative major: E major
Parallel major: C-sharp major
Notes: C♯ D♯ E F♯ G♯ A B

C-sharp minor is a minor scale based on C♯, consisting of the pitches C♯, D♯, E, F♯, G♯, A, and B. Its key signature has four sharps. Its relative major is E major and its parallel major is C-sharp major.

Notable compositions

Beethoven's Piano Sonata No. 14, the "Moonlight Sonata", is in C-sharp minor, as is Chopin's Fantaisie-Impromptu. Rachmaninoff's famous Prelude in C-sharp minor, composed when he was 19, became one of his most popular works — so much so that he grew to dislike its ubiquity.

Scale forms

The natural minor is modified in the harmonic minor (with B♯ as the raised seventh) and the melodic minor (with A♯ and B♯ ascending). The key is relatively uncommon in orchestral music because of its four-sharp signature, but is common in piano literature.
