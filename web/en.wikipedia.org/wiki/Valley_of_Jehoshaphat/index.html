Valley of Jehoshaphat

LocationTraditionally identified with the Kidron Valley, Jerusalem
SourceBook of Joel 3:2, 3:12 (Hebrew Bible)

The Valley of Jehoshaphat (Hebrew: עמק יהושפט, ʿEmeq Yehoshafat) is a biblical place-name mentioned in the Book of Joel as the site where, in the eschatological future, God will gather and judge all nations. The name means "Yahweh has judged".

Biblical references

Joel 3:2 speaks of God bringing all nations down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat to enter into judgment with them, and Joel 3:12 repeats the image. The valley is not identified in the text with any known geographic location, and it may be a symbolic name — possibly a wordplay connected with King Jehoshaphat's victory over a coalition of enemies, or purely theological, denoting the place of divine judgement.

Traditional identification

From at least the fourth century CE, Jewish, Christian, and later Muslim tradition identified the Valley of Jehoshaphat with the Kidron Valley between Jerusalem's Temple Mount and the Mount of Olives, or with a portion of it. Because of this identification with the Last Judgment, the slopes of the Kidron and the Mount of Olives became densely filled with graves — Jewish, Christian, and Muslim burials placed in the hope of standing near the site of resurrection and judgement.

In later tradition

The name appears in Christian hymns and literature as a byword for the final judgement, and "Jehoshaphat" survives in English as a minced oath in the exclamation "Jumpin' Jehoshaphat!".
