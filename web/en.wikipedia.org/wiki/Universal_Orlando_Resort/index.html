The Universal Orlando Resort, often shortened to Universal Orlando, is an American theme park and entertainment resort complex located in Orlando, Florida. It is the flagship of Universal Destinations & Experiences and one of the most visited resort complexes in the world. It opened on June 7, 1990 with Universal Studios Florida.

History

The resort expanded dramatically in 1999 with the opening of Universal Islands of Adventure and the Universal CityWalk entertainment district, transforming the single park into a multi-day destination. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter opened at Islands of Adventure in 2010 and proved enormously popular, drawing record attendance; a second Harry Potter land, Diagon Alley, opened at Universal Studios Florida in 2014, connected by a Hogwarts Express ride. The water park Volcano Bay opened in 2017 on the site of the former Wet 'n Wild.

Epic Universe

In May 2025 the resort opened Universal Epic Universe, its largest expansion, located on a new campus south of the original resort. Its five themed lands are Celestial Park, Super Nintendo World, the Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Ministry of Magic, How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk, and Dark Universe, themed to Universal's classic monsters. The resort also operates numerous on-site hotels, including properties run in partnership with Loews Hotels.
