Electric Slide

[Hero image placeholder: line dancers performing the Electric Slide]

The Electric Slide is a four-wall line dance set to Marcia Griffiths's 1989 song "Electric Boogie" (written by Bunny Wailer). The choreography was created in 1976 by Broadway dancer and choreographer Ric Silver, who devised an 18-step version for the song's original release by Bunny Wailer; the dance became a worldwide phenomenon in the early 1990s when Griffiths's version of the song became a hit. It remains one of the most popular line dances of all time, a fixture at weddings, parties, and celebrations, especially in the United States.

The dance

Dancers stand in lines facing the same direction ("wall") and perform a sequence of grapevines (side steps with steps behind), backward steps with a touch, a forward step with a touch, and a quarter turn, repeating the sequence facing each of the four walls in rotation. Common embellishments include claps, knee dips, and turns. Because the steps are simple and repetitive, the dance is accessible to beginners and large groups.

History and popularity

"Electric Boogie" was written by Neville "Bunny Wailer" Livingston and first recorded by Marcia Griffiths in the early 1980s; her 1989 version reached the charts in the United States in 1990, propelling the accompanying dance to international popularity. The Electric Slide is listed among the dances that defined social dancing of the late 20th century and is frequently ranked as the most popular line dance in the world. In 2007, choreographer Ric Silver asserted copyright claims over performances and instructional uses of the dance, leading to public controversy and takedown notices for online videos; the dispute drew attention to questions of copyright in choreography.

Variations

Numerous step variations exist — 18-step and 22-step versions are both common — and disputes over the "official" steps have followed the dance since its peak. It is often confused or interchanged with other wedding-dance staples such as the Cha Cha Slide and the Cupid Shuffle.
