Halo 2

Halo 2 is a 2004 first-person shooter game developed by Bungie and published by Microsoft Game Studios for the Xbox console. Released on November 9, 2004, it is the sequel to Halo: Combat Evolved and the second instalment in the Halo franchise; a Windows Vista port followed in 2007.

Gameplay and story

The campaign alternates between the human supersoldier Master Chief, defending Earth from a Covenant invasion and pursuing the Prophets to a second Halo ring, and the Arbiter, a disgraced Covenant Elite commander whose storyline exposes the Covenant's internal fractures and the outbreak of its civil war. New gameplay elements include dual-wielding weapons, vehicle hijacking, and destructible vehicles. Halo 2 was a landmark title for Xbox Live, popularising online matchmaking, downloadable content, and clan support on consoles.

Reception

The game sold more than 8 million copies, becoming the best-selling Xbox game, and earned critical praise, though its abrupt cliffhanger ending was divisive. Its multiplayer remained active for years, and the original Xbox Live servers' shutdown in 2010 ended its original online service. A high-definition remaster, Halo 2: Anniversary, was released in 2014 as part of Halo: The Master Chief Collection.
