Charles Lazarus

Charles Philip Lazarus (October 4, 1923 – March 22, 2018) was an American entrepreneur and businessman, best known as the founder of Toys "R" Us, the toy retail chain.

Career

After serving in the Second World War, Lazarus opened a baby-furniture store, Children's Bargain Town, in Washington, D.C. in 1948. Anticipating the post-war baby boom, he expanded into toys, and in 1957 opened the first Toys "R" Us store in Rockville, Maryland, pioneering the category-killer supermarket format for toys. The company went public in 1978 and became the dominant toy retailer in the United States.

Lazarus stepped down as chief executive in 1994. He died in 2018, one week after Toys "R" Us announced the liquidation of its U.S. stores.
