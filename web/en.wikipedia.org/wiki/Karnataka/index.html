Karnataka (Kannada: ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ) is a state in the southwestern region of India. It was formed on 1 November 1956, as the State of Mysore, with the passage of the States Reorganisation Act, and renamed Karnataka in 1973. The state corresponds to the Carnatic region. Its capital and largest city is Bengaluru.

Karnataka is bordered by the Lakshadweep Sea to the west, Goa to the northwest, Maharashtra to the north, Telangana to the northeast, Andhra Pradesh to the east, Tamil Nadu to the southeast, and Kerala to the southwest. It is the eighth-largest Indian state by area. With over 61 million inhabitants, Karnataka is the ninth-most populous state in India, comprising 31 districts.

History

The recorded history of Karnataka goes back more than two millennia. The region was ruled by the Satavahanas, the Kadambas (who established Banavasi as their capital), the Western Gangas, the Chalukyas of Badami, the Rashtrakutas, the Western Chalukyas, the Hoysalas, and the Vijayanagara Empire, whose capital at Hampi is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Kingdom of Mysore, ruled by the Wadiyar dynasty, later became one of the princely states of British India.

After Indian independence in 1947, the Mysore State was created, and on 1 November 1956 it was enlarged by the addition of Kannada-speaking districts from Bombay, Hyderabad, and Madras states, as well as the state of Coorg. The unified state was renamed Karnataka on 1 November 1973.

Geography

Karnataka lies between 11°30' and 18°45' north latitudes on the western portion of the Deccan Plateau. The state has three principal physical zones: the coastal region of Karavali, the hill ranges of the Western Ghats (Malnad), and the plateau region of Bayaluseeme. Major rivers include the Krishna, Kaveri (Cauvery), Godavari, and Tungabhadra.

Economy

Karnataka is one of India's leading states in industry and technology. Bengaluru is widely known as the "Silicon Valley of India" for its concentration of information technology companies. The state is also a leader in biotechnology, aerospace (home to Hindustan Aeronautics Limited), and the production of silk, sandalwood, and coffee.

Culture

Karnataka has a rich cultural heritage. It is the home of two classical music traditions (Carnatic and Hindustani, the latter flourishing in the north of the state), the Yakshagana theatre form, and a distinguished tradition of literature in Kannada, whose authors have won eight Jnanpith Awards, the most for any Indian language. Major festivals include Mysuru Dasara, Ugadi, and Karaga.
