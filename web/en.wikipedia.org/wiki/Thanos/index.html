Thanos is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer-artist Jim Starlin, the character first appeared in The Invincible Iron Man #55 (1973). A member of the Eternals of Titan born with the Deviant gene, Thanos is one of the most powerful villains in the Marvel Universe and has clashed frequently with the Avengers, the Guardians of the Galaxy and the Fantastic Four.

Thanos with the Infinity Gauntlet

Fictional biography

Obsessed with the personification of Death, Thanos seeks to court her by committing acts of cosmic destruction, most famously in The Infinity Gauntlet (1991), in which he assembles the six Infinity Gems into a gauntlet, becomes effectively omnipotent, and eliminates half of all life in the universe with a snap of his fingers before being undone by the heroes and his own hubris. Later storylines recast him with more ambiguous motives, including a belief that destroying half of life would save the universe from overpopulation.

In other media

In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Thanos (voiced and performed by Josh Brolin) is the overarching antagonist of the Infinity Saga, appearing in The Avengers (2012), Guardians of the Galaxy (2014), Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019). The character's MCU portrayal became a major cultural phenomenon.

Powers and abilities

Thanos possesses immense superhuman strength, durability and energy projection, augmented by bionic implants and his Eternal physiology, together with a genius-level intellect and mastery of strategy and cosmic technology.
