The Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia are two British-administered areas on the island of Cyprus comprising the United Kingdom's last remaining military bases in the eastern Mediterranean. They form a British Overseas Territory retained under full British sovereignty when the Republic of Cyprus became independent in 1960. The Western Sovereign Base Area centres on the Akrotiri peninsula near Limassol and includes RAF Akrotiri; the Eastern Sovereign Base Area centres on Dhekelia near Larnaca and includes a garrison, the Ayios Nikolaos signals station and several small enclaves within the Republic of Cyprus.

History

Under the 1960 Treaty of Establishment, Britain retained sovereignty over about 99 square miles — roughly 3 per cent of the island's land area — selected for military rather than civilian purposes. Cyprus, Greece and Turkey contested the arrangement at various times, and the Republic of Cyprus maintains that the bases should revert if Britain no longer needs them for military purposes. After the Turkish invasion of 1974 and the division of Cyprus, the Dhekelia area came to border the UN buffer zone and the Turkish Cypriot-administered north.

A 2014 arrangement, implemented in 2020, allowed Cypriot landowners in the bases to develop much of their property under planning rules broadly equivalent to those of the Republic, ending decades of restrictions.

Geography and administration

The territory is administered by an Administrator, who is the Commander of British Forces Cyprus, exercising executive and legislative authority on behalf of the monarch. The bases have their own courts, police force (the SBA Police) and customs, but no permanent civilian population of their own: residents are mainly British service personnel, civil servants and their families, plus several thousand Cypriot citizens who live in villages within or enclaved by the territory, such as Xylotymbou and Ormidhia. The euro, rather than the pound sterling, is used, reflecting the areas' economic integration with Cyprus. RAF Akrotiri is a major air base used for operations across the Middle East, and the territory also hosts important signals-intelligence facilities.
