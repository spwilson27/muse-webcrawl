List of people executed in California

Capital punishment is a legal penalty in California, though executions have been halted since 2006 and Governor Gavin Newsom imposed a moratorium in 2019. Since the United States Supreme Court reinstated the death penalty in 1976, California has executed 13 people, all by lethal injection at San Quentin State Prison. Historically, California executed hundreds of prisoners — by hanging, then in the gas chamber at San Quentin from 1938, a method used for the last time on David Mason in 1993.

Robert Alton Harris was the first person executed in California since 1967. Clarence Ray Allen, executed at age 76 and legally blind and diabetic, remains the oldest prisoner executed in the state in the modern era. In March 2019, Governor Newsom signed an executive order granting a reprieve to all 737 inmates on death row and ordering the lethal injection protocol withdrawn; the execution chamber at San Quentin was dismantled.
