Hunter Army Airfield

Hunter Army Airfield (LHW)
Location: Savannah, Georgia, U.S.
Type: Military airfield
Owner/operator: United States Army
Garrison: 3rd Infantry Division units
Runway: 11,375 ft (3,468 m) — among the longest in the Army

Hunter Army Airfield (IATA: SVN, ICAO: KSVN, FAA LID: SVN), located in Savannah, Georgia, is a military airfield and subordinate installation of Fort Stewart, the largest Army post east of the Mississippi River. It is home to aviation units of the 3rd Infantry Division, including the 3rd Combat Aviation Brigade, as well as the 1st Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment and elements of the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment.

History

The field began in 1929 as Savannah Municipal Airport at Hunter Field, named for World War I flying ace Frank O'Driscoll Hunter. Taken over as an Army air base in 1940, it served the Army Air Forces during the Second World War and later as Hunter Air Force Base under Strategic Air Command, whose B-47 bombers used its exceptionally long runway. Transferred from the Air Force to the Army in 1967 and renamed Hunter Army Airfield, it became the aviation annex of Fort Stewart.

Role

With one of the longest runways in the Department of Defense and rail and port facilities nearby in Savannah, Hunter serves as a major power-projection platform: deploying units can load heavy aircraft such as the C-5 Galaxy and C-17 Globemaster III for rapid overseas deployment. The airfield supports rotary-wing operations by Apache, Black Hawk and Chinook units stationed there.
