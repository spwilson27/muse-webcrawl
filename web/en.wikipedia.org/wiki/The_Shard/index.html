The Shard

Location: London Bridge, Southwark, London
Height: 309.6 m (1,016 ft), 95 storeys
Completed: 2012
Architect: Renzo Piano

The Shard, also known as the Shard of Glass and the Shard London Bridge, is a 95-storey mixed-use skyscraper in Southwark, London. Designed by Italian architect Renzo Piano, it rises 309.6 metres (1,016 feet), making it the tallest building in the United Kingdom and among the tallest in Europe.

Design and construction

Piano conceived the tower as a sharp, glass-clad pyramid inspired by London's church spires and the masts of ships on the Thames; the developer Irvine Sellar recounted that Piano sketched the form on a napkin during a lunch meeting. Construction began in 2009 on the site of the demolished Southwark Towers, beside London Bridge station, and the tower was inaugurated on 5 July 2012, timed with the London Olympics. It is majority-owned by the State of Qatar.

Uses

The building contains offices, the Shangri-La hotel, restaurants, residences, and retail. The View from The Shard, a public viewing gallery on floors 68–72, opened in February 2013. The Shard has become a defining landmark of the twenty-first-century London skyline.
