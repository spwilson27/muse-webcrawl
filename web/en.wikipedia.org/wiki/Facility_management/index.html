Facility management

Facility management or facilities management (FM) is a professional management discipline focused on the efficient and effective delivery of support services for the organizations that it serves. It covers the integration of processes within an organization to maintain and develop agreed services which support and improve the effectiveness of its primary activities.

Scope

Facility management is commonly divided into "hard services", such as building maintenance, mechanical and electrical systems, and HVAC, and "soft services", such as cleaning, catering, security, reception, and waste management. Space planning, workplace strategy, and real estate coordination are also central functions.

Standards

The discipline is represented internationally by bodies such as the International Facility Management Association (IFMA) and is standardized under the ISO 41000 series. Building information modeling, computerized maintenance management systems, and sustainability targets increasingly shape professional practice.

Role of the facility manager

Facility managers ensure compliance with health, safety, and environmental regulations, manage vendor contracts and operating budgets, and plan for the lifecycle of building assets.
