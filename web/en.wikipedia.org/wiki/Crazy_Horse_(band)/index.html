Crazy Horse (band)

Crazy Horse is an American rock band best known for its association with Neil Young. The band has backed Young on many of his most famous albums and tours since 1969, and has also released albums in its own right. Its classic line-up consisted of Danny Whitten (guitar, vocals), Billy Talbot (bass) and Ralph Molina (drums), later joined by Frank "Poncho" Sampedro (guitar) from 1975.

History

The band evolved from a Los Angeles vocal group, the Rockets, whose members Talbot, Molina and Whitten met Young in 1968. Renamed Crazy Horse, they backed him on Everybody Knows This Is Nowhere (1969), which produced "Cinnamon Girl", "Down by the River" and "Cowgirl in the Sand", and on After the Gold Rush (1970). Their own debut album, Crazy Horse (1971), is highly regarded. Danny Whitten's drug problems and death from an overdose in 1972 deeply affected Young, who memorialised him in "The Needle and the Damage Done".

With Sampedro replacing Whitten, the band reunited with Young for Zuma (1975), Rust Never Sleeps (1979), Ragged Glory (1990), Psychedelic Pill (2012) and others, earning a reputation as one of rock's great raw, garage-style ensembles. Guitarist Nils Lofgren, an early member, rejoined for later tours and albums in place of the retired Sampedro.
