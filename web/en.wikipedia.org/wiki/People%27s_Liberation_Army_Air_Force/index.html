The People's Liberation Army Air Force (PLAAF; Chinese: 中国人民解放军空军), also known as the Chinese Air Force, is the aerial service branch of the People's Liberation Army. Founded on 11 November 1949, it has grown into one of the largest air forces in the world and the largest in Asia.

History

The PLAAF's first combat experience came in the Korean War, where its MiG-15s, flown by Chinese and Soviet pilots, contested UN air power over "MiG Alley". For decades it relied on large numbers of licence-built Soviet designs, such as the J-6 (MiG-19) and J-7 (MiG-21). After the Cold War it imported Russian Su-27 and Su-30 fighters and invested heavily in indigenous designs.

Modern force

Today the PLAAF operates modern fourth-generation fighters such as the J-10 and J-16 alongside the J-20 fifth-generation stealth fighter, which entered service in 2017 as the world's third operational stealth fighter. Supporting fleets include KJ-500 airborne early warning aircraft, Y-20 strategic transports and aerial refuelling variants, and H-6 bombers. The force is organised under the PLA's five theatre commands and has increasingly emphasised long-range and joint operations.
