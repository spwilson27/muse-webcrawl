Manual (music)

A manual is a musical keyboard designed to be played with the hands, as on a piano, harpsichord, or organ, in contrast to a pedalboard played with the feet. The term is most used for organs, which may have several manuals stacked above one another.

Organ manuals

Each manual on an organ controls a distinct division — for example Great, Swell, Choir, and Solo — with its own set of stops. Typical modern organs have two or three manuals of 61 keys; large concert and cathedral organs may have four or five. The swell manual is enclosed in a swell box whose shutters, operated by a swell pedal, control volume.
