List of Hindi film families

This article lists families in the Hindi film industry (Bollywood) whose members span several generations in acting, direction, production and music. Family dynasties have played an outsized role in Mumbai cinema since its early decades.

Prominent families

Kapoor family

Descended from Prithviraj Kapoor, the family includes his sons Raj Kapoor, Shammi Kapoor and Shashi Kapoor; grandsons Randhir, Rishi and Rajiv Kapoor; and great-grandchildren Karisma Kapoor, Kareena Kapoor and Ranbir Kapoor. It is often called the first family of Indian cinema.

Bachchan family

Founded by poet Harivansh Rai Bachchan and his son Amitabh Bachchan, with Jaya Bachchan (née Bhaduri), Abhishek Bachchan and Aishwarya Rai Bachchan.

Khan families

The "three Khans" — Aamir Khan (of the Hussain–Khan family of Nasir Hussain), Shah Rukh Khan, and Salman Khan (son of screenwriter Salim Khan, of the Salim Khan family that includes Arbaaz and Sohail Khan and Helen). Saif Ali Khan, son of Mansur Ali Khan Pataudi and Sharmila Tagore, heads another notable line, with children Sara Ali Khan and Ibrahim Ali Khan.

Other families

  • Mukherjee–Samarth family — Kajol, Tanuja, Rani Mukerji, director Ayan Mukerji
  • Kumar family — Ashok Kumar, Kishore Kumar and Anoop Kumar
  • Anand family — Dev Anand, Chetan Anand, Vijay Anand
  • Chopra family — B. R. Chopra and Yash Chopra; Aditya Chopra and Uday Chopra
  • Dutt family — Sunil Dutt, Nargis, Sanjay Dutt
  • Dharmendra–Deol family — Dharmendra, Sunny Deol, Bobby Deol, Hema Malini, Esha Deol
  • Roshan family — composer Roshan, Rakesh Roshan, Rajesh Roshan, Hrithik Roshan
  • Bhatt family — Mahesh Bhatt, Mukesh Bhatt, Alia Bhatt, Pooja Bhatt
  • Akhtar–Azmi family — Javed Akhtar, Shabana Azmi, Farhan Akhtar, Zoya Akhtar
  • Sharma–Pataudi and Tagore lines — connected through Sharmila Tagore and Soha Ali Khan
  • Khanna–Kapadia family — Rajesh Khanna, Dimple Kapadia, Twinkle Khanna
  • Sippys — G. P. Sippy, Ramesh Sippy, Rohan Sippy
  • Barjatya family — Tarachand Barjatya, Sooraj R. Barjatya (Rajshri Productions)
  • Johar family — Yash Johar, Karan Johar
