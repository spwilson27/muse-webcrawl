Mongols

The Mongols are an East Asian ethnic group native to Mongolia, China (Inner Mongolia) and parts of Russia (Buryatia and Kalmykia). They are bound together by a common heritage and ethnic identity, and speak Mongolic languages.

PopulationAbout 10 million
LanguageMongolian
ReligionTibetan Buddhism, shamanism

Mongol Empire

In 1206 Genghis Khan united the nomadic tribes and founded the Mongol Empire, which became the largest contiguous land empire in history, stretching from Korea to Eastern Europe. His successors divided it into khanates including the Yuan dynasty in China and the Ilkhanate in Persia.

Culture

Traditional life centred on pastoralism, horses, and the ger (yurt). The Pax Mongolica revived Silk Road trade. Modern Mongolia became independent in the 20th century.
