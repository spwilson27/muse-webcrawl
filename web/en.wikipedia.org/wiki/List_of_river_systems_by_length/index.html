List of river systems by length

This list ranks the world's longest river systems. Lengths are approximate: measuring a river depends on the choice of source, the definition of the mouth, and the scale of measurement, so published figures differ. The two longest systems, the Nile and the Amazon, are close enough that sources disagree on which is longer.

The Nile–Amazon question

The Amazon carries by far the greatest volume of water — roughly a fifth of all river water reaching the oceans — and some studies that trace its most distant Andean source conclude it is slightly longer than the Nile. Most reference works nevertheless list the Nile first at about 6,650 km.
