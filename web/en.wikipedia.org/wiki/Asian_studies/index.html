Asian studies
Field: Area studies
Subfields: East Asian, South Asian, Southeast Asian studies
Professional body: Association for Asian Studies

Asian studies is the term used in North America and Australia for the study of Asian peoples, cultures, languages, history and politics, typically as an interdisciplinary field combining history, language study, literature, political science, anthropology and religious studies. In Europe the field is often organised as Oriental studies, Sinology, Indology and similar disciplines.

History

Western study of Asia developed through philology and colonial administration, then expanded greatly after the Second World War, when area-studies programmes were funded in the United States to build expertise on world regions.

Subfields

Major subdivisions include East Asian studies (China, Japan, Korea), South Asian studies (India, Pakistan and neighbours) and Southeast Asian studies, each with dedicated journals, conferences and scholarly associations.
