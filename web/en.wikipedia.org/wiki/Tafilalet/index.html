Tafilalet

Tafilalet is a historic region and oasis area in southeastern Morocco, in the Drâa-Tafilalet region. It is famed for its palm groves, ksar settlements, and date palms.

History

Tafilalet was the seat of Sijilmasa, a medieval trading city founded in 757 that grew rich on trans-Saharan gold trade. The Alaouite dynasty of Morocco originated from Tafilalet in the 17th century.
