Melbourne Park is a sports venue complex in the Melbourne Sports and Entertainment Precinct in Victoria, Australia, and the home of the Australian Open tennis tournament since 1988. Situated beside the Yarra River near the central business district, it replaced the Kooyong Lawn Tennis Club as the tournament's home and was renamed from Flinders Park to Melbourne Park in 1997. Its centre court, Rod Laver Arena, was a pioneer among tennis stadiums with its retractable roof, later joined by Margaret Court Arena and John Cain Arena, giving the Australian Open more roofed show courts than any other Grand Slam venue.

Use

Outside the January tournament the arenas host concerts, basketball, netball and other indoor sports and entertainment. Repeated Victorian government redevelopments have expanded the precinct with new courts, facilities and public spaces, underpinning Melbourne's claim to be Australia's sporting capital; the tournament's presence at Melbourne Park is secured by long-term agreements into the 2040s.
