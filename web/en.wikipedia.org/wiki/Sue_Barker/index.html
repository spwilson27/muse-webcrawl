Sue Barker

Susan Barker, CBE (born 19 April 1956) is a British former professional tennis player and television presenter. During her playing career she won 15 WTA Tour singles titles, including the French Open in 1976, and reached a career-high singles ranking of world No. 3. After retiring from tennis in 1984, she became one of Britain's best-known sports broadcasters, fronting the BBC's coverage of Wimbledon and the Olympic Games, and presenting the sports quiz A Question of Sport from 1997 to 2021.

Born: 19 April 1956, Paignton, Devon, England
Turned pro: 1973
Career titles: 15 singles, 12 doubles
Grand Slam best: French Open champion (1976)

Tennis career

Barker was born in Paignton, Devon, and coached by Arthur Roberts, who had also coached Angela Mortimer. She won the French Open junior title in 1974 and, two years later, defeated Renáta Tomanová in the final to win the senior French Open title at Roland Garros in 1976 — still the most recent French Open singles title won by a British woman. She reached the semi-finals of Wimbledon and the Australian Open in 1977, and was a member of the British Wightman Cup and Fed Cup teams. Injuries curtailed her later career, and she retired in 1984.

Broadcasting career

Barker joined the BBC in 1993 and became the face of its tennis coverage, presenting Wimbledon highlights and live coverage for three decades; her final Wimbledon as presenter was in 2022. She presented A Question of Sport for 24 years, becoming the programme's longest-serving host, and fronted coverage of the Olympic Games, Commonwealth Games and Grand National. She was appointed MBE in 2000, OBE in 2016 and CBE in 2021 for services to sport and charity.

Personal life

Barker is married to former police officer Lance Tankard. She published her autobiography, Calling the Shots, in 2022.
