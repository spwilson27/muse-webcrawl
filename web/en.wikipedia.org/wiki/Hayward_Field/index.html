Hayward Field is a track and field stadium in the northwestern United States, located on the campus of the University of Oregon in Eugene, Oregon. It has been the home of the university's track and field teams since 1919, and is one of the most famous and prestigious venues in the sport. It was named for Bill Hayward (1868–1947), who coached the Oregon track program from 1904 to 1947.

History

The field opened in 1919 as a football stadium, replacing Kincaid Field, and a cinder track hosted its first meet in 1921. Football moved to Autzen Stadium in 1967, leaving Hayward purely to track. Under coaches Bill Bowerman and later Steve Prefontaine's era, Eugene became known as "TrackTown USA". The venue hosted the U.S. Olympic Trials repeatedly from 1972 onward, and the annual Prefontaine Classic Diamond League meeting.

Reconstruction

The historic stadium, including its beloved East Grandstand (built 1925), was demolished in 2018 and replaced by a new stadium designed by SRG Partnership, which opened in 2020. The new venue seats 12,650, expandable to about 25,000 for major events, and features a 140-foot tower inspired by a torch, with exhibits honouring Oregon track history. The Bowerman Tower and a museum occupy the base.

Major events

  • U.S. Olympic Track and Field Trials: 1972, 1976, 1980, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024
  • NCAA Outdoor Track and Field Championships: numerous editions
  • World Athletics Championships: 2022 — the first held in the United States
  • World Athletics U20 Championships: 2014
