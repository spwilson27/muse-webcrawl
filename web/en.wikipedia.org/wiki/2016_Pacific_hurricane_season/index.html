2016 Pacific hurricane season

Season summary
Season boundaries15 May 2016 (Eastern Pacific) – 30 November 2016
Named storms22 (Eastern Pacific basin)
Strongest stormSeymour — Category 4, 150 mph (240 km/h)

The 2016 Pacific hurricane season was an active season in the Eastern and Central Pacific basins, following the record-breaking 2015 season. The season officially ran from 15 May to 30 November 2016 in the Eastern Pacific and from 1 June in the Central Pacific.

Seasonal activity

The Eastern Pacific produced 22 named storms, 13 hurricanes and six major hurricanes. Notable systems included Hurricane Darby, which threatened Hawaii in July; Hurricane Lester and Hurricane Madeline, which approached the Hawaiian Islands around the start of September; and Hurricane Seymour, the season's strongest storm, which reached Category 4 intensity in late October. Hurricane Newton made landfall in Baja California in September before moving into the southwestern United States.

Central Pacific

Activity in the Central Pacific was above average, with several systems forming in or entering the basin, including tropical storms that brought heavy rain to Hawaii.

Retired names

No names were retired from the Eastern Pacific naming lists following the 2016 season.
