S-duality

S-duality is a symmetry in string theory and quantum field theory that relates a theory with strong coupling to another — possibly the same — theory with weak coupling, exchanging electrically charged objects with magnetically charged ones. It generalises the electric–magnetic duality of Maxwell's equations.

In field theory

The prototype is Montonen–Olive duality in N = 4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory, where the coupling g is inverted (g → 1/g) while electric and magnetic charges are swapped. Work by Seiberg and Witten on N = 2 theories provided exact tests of such dualities.

In string theory

S-duality maps type IIB string theory to itself, and relates the heterotic SO(32) string to the type I string. Together with T-duality, it forms part of the web of dualities pointing to an underlying M-theory. The modular group SL(2, Z) acts on the type IIB coupling in a manifestation of S-duality.
