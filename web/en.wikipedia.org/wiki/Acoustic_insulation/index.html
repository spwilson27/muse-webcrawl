Acoustic insulation

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Acoustic insulation, also known as sound insulation or soundproofing, is the practice of reducing the transmission of sound between spaces, or reducing the level of sound within a space, by means of materials and construction techniques. It is a central concern of building acoustics and is applied in housing, offices, schools, hospitals, recording studios and industrial plant.

Principles

Sound reaches a listener either through the air (airborne sound, such as speech and music) or through the structure of a building (impact or structure-borne sound, such as footsteps). Effective insulation combines four strategies: mass — heavy, dense partitions reflect more airborne sound; absorption — porous materials such as mineral wool convert sound energy to heat within cavities; decoupling — separating the two faces of a wall with resilient channels or staggered studs interrupts vibration paths; and sealing — closing gaps around doors, windows and services, since even small openings can dominate sound leakage.

Materials

Common materials include mineral (rock or glass) wool batts, acoustic plasterboard with high-density gypsum cores, mass-loaded vinyl, acoustic foam panels (principally absorbent rather than insulating), laminated glazing with acoustic interlayers, and floating floor systems that isolate a floor slab on resilient mounts.

Measurement and standards

Airborne sound insulation of a partition is expressed in decibels as a weighted sound reduction index (Rw in ISO standards, or STC — Sound Transmission Class — in North America). Impact sound is measured as a normalised impact sound pressure level. Building regulations in many countries set minimum insulation values between dwellings to protect occupants from noise pollution and its effects on health and sleep.

Applications

Acoustic insulation is required between flats and terraced houses, around plant rooms and lift shafts, in cinemas and concert halls, and in vehicles, where panels, damping mats and seals reduce road and engine noise.
