Shingū, Wakayama

Shingū
Country: Japan
Region: Kansai
Prefecture: Wakayama
Area: Kii Peninsula (coastal city)

Shingū is a city in Wakayama Prefecture, Japan, on the Pacific coast of the Kii Peninsula near the mouth of the Kumano River. It has been a gateway for pilgrims to the Kumano shrines for over a thousand years.

Sights

The city hosts the Kamikura Shrine, famous for the Oto Matsuri fire festival held each February, and lies on the Kumano Kodō pilgrimage routes, part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing "Sacred Sites and Pilgrimage Routes in the Kii Mountain Range". Nearby are the Hayatama and Nachi shrines and the Nachi Falls. Historically a port and timber town, its economy now includes tourism and fishing.
