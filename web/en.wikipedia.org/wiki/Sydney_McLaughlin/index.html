Sydney Michelle McLaughlin-Levrone (born 7 August 1999) is an American hurdler and sprinter who competes primarily in the 400 metres hurdles. She is a two-time Olympic champion in the event and has repeatedly broken her own world record, becoming the first woman to run the distance under 52 and then under 51 seconds.

Career

McLaughlin qualified for the 2016 Rio Olympics as a 17-year-old after a record-setting high-school career in New Jersey, and spent one season at the University of Kentucky before turning professional in 2018. At the Tokyo 2020 Olympics she won gold in the 400 m hurdles in a world record of 51.46 seconds, and added a second gold in the 4×400 m relay. In June 2022 she lowered the record to 51.41, then to 50.68 while winning the World Championships in Eugene — the first woman under 51 seconds. At the Paris 2024 Olympics she defended her title in 50.37, a sixth world record, and again anchored the United States to relay gold. In 2025 she also contested the flat 400 m, winning the world title in Tokyo with a championship record time, among the fastest ever run.

Personal life

She married former NFL player Andre Levrone Jr. in 2022 and is coached by Bob Kersee. She is widely regarded as one of the greatest hurdlers in history.
