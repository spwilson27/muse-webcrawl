List of countries by life expectancy

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article contains a list of countries and dependencies by life expectancy at birth — the average number of years a newborn would live if mortality patterns at the time of birth remained constant. The figures are principally from the United Nations' World Population Prospects. Global life expectancy recovered to about 73 years in 2023 after a dip during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Women outlive men in every country; the gap is widest in Eastern Europe, where it exceeds ten years in some states. The lowest life expectancies in the world, below 58 years, are concentrated in sub-Saharan Africa, reflecting infectious disease burdens, conflict and weaker health systems, although several African countries have made large gains since 2000.

Historical trends

  • World life expectancy rose from about 46 years in 1950 to about 73 years in 2023.
  • Japan has had the highest, or among the highest, life expectancy of any large country since the 1970s.
  • Life expectancy in the United States fell sharply in 2020–2021 due to COVID-19 and drug overdoses, before partially recovering.
  • Russia and other post-Soviet states saw large declines in male life expectancy during the 1990s.
