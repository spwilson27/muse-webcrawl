Noise (electronics)

In electronics, noise is an unwanted disturbance in an electrical signal. Noise generated by electronic devices varies greatly because it is produced by several different effects.

Types

Important types include thermal noise (Johnson–Nyquist noise), arising from the thermal agitation of charge carriers; shot noise, arising from the discrete nature of electric charge; and flicker (1/f) noise, which increases at low frequencies.

Measurement

Noise performance is quantified by measures such as signal-to-noise ratio, noise figure, and noise temperature. In audio and communications systems, noise limits the smallest signal that can be usefully detected or amplified.
