The Algeria national football team represents Algeria in men's international football and is governed by the Algerian Football Federation. Known as the Fennecs, the team is one of Africa's most successful, having won the Africa Cup of Nations twice.

History

Algeria qualified for its first FIFA World Cup in 1982, famously defeating West Germany 2–1 in Spain before being eliminated after the controversial West Germany–Austria match in Gijón. The team won the Africa Cup of Nations on home soil in 1990 and again in Egypt in 2019 under coach Djamel Belmadi, defeating Senegal in the final. Algeria also reached the round of 16 at the 2014 World Cup in Brazil.

Identity

The team traditionally plays in white and green, and its passionate supporters are a prominent feature of Algerian public life, in the country and across the diaspora.
