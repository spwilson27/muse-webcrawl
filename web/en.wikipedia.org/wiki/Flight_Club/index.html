Flight Club

Flight Club is an American consignment retailer specializing in rare and collectible sneakers. Founded in 2005 in New York City, it is one of the best-known stores in sneaker culture, selling limited-edition and vintage athletic shoes — particularly Air Jordans and Nike releases — on behalf of individual sellers, who set their own prices and receive a percentage of the sale.

History

Flight Club opened its first store in Manhattan's Greenwich Village in 2005, when the resale market for collectible sneakers was still a niche subculture. The store's wall-to-wall displays of shoes in shrink-wrap and its consignment model — under which coveted shoes might sell for many times their retail price — became emblematic of the emerging sneakerhead economy. A Los Angeles location followed, and the company later expanded to Miami.

In 2018, Flight Club merged with GOAT Group, operator of the GOAT sneaker resale platform, in a deal that valued the combined business at a reported $250 million and raised $60 million in funding. Flight Club continued to operate as a retail brand under the GOAT umbrella, with its physical stores serving as both shops and cultural landmarks frequently featured in media coverage of sneaker culture.

Business model

Unlike a conventional retailer, Flight Club historically held most of its inventory on consignment: sellers bring in shoes, the store authenticates and displays them, and the seller receives roughly 80 percent of the sale price, with Flight Club taking a commission. The model lets the store carry an enormous, constantly changing selection of rare shoes without buying the stock outright. Prices are set by the market and by sellers, and sought-after models have sold for thousands of dollars.
