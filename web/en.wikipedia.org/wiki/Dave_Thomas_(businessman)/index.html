Dave Thomas (businessman)

Rex David Thomas (2 July 1932 – 8 January 2002) was an American businessman, philanthropist, and fast-food tycoon. He founded the Wendy's hamburger chain in 1969 and became its familiar television pitchman.


Born: 2 July 1932, Atlantic City, New Jersey
Died: 8 January 2002 (aged 69), Fort Lauderdale, Florida
Known for: Founder of Wendy's

Early life and career

Adopted as an infant, Thomas left school to work in restaurants, served as an Army cook, and became one of Colonel Harland Sanders' early KFC franchise successes in Columbus, Ohio, selling his franchises back for a fortune.

Wendy's

In 1969 he opened the first Wendy's in Columbus, named for his daughter Melinda ("Wendy"), emphasising fresh, made-to-order square burgers. The chain grew into the world's third-largest burger chain. From 1989 Thomas starred in hundreds of folksy TV ads that made him a household face. Adopted himself, he founded the Dave Thomas Foundation for Adoption in 1992.
