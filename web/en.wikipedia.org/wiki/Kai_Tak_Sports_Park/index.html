Kai Tak Sports Park

LocationKai Tak, Kowloon, Hong Kong
Area28 hectares
Opened1 March 2025
Main stadium capacity50,000

Kai Tak Sports Park is a multi-purpose sports complex in Kowloon, Hong Kong, built on the northern part of the site of the former Kai Tak Airport. It is the largest sports infrastructure project in Hong Kong's history, costing around HK$30 billion, and officially opened on 1 March 2025.

Facilities

The centrepiece is the Kai Tak Stadium, a 50,000-seat venue with a retractable roof, a flexible pitch surface and a distinctive facade of triangular aluminium panels designed to evoke a "Pearl of the Orient". It can host football, rugby and large concerts. The park also includes the Kai Tak Arena, a 10,000-seat indoor arena; the Kai Tak Youth Sports Ground with 5,000 seats; and extensive public open space, a waterfront promenade and the Kai Tak Mall retail complex.

Events

The stadium hosted the Hong Kong Sevens rugby tournament for the first time in 2025, taking over from the Hong Kong Stadium, and was a venue for the 2025 National Games of China co-hosted by Guangdong, Hong Kong and Macau. Major concert tours also began staging shows at the stadium in its opening year.
