The Storm in action at Climate Pledge Arena.

The Seattle Storm are an American professional basketball team based in Seattle, Washington, playing in the Western Conference of the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA). Founded in 2000, the Storm are one of the league's most successful franchises, having won four WNBA championships (2004, 2010, 2018 and 2020).

Founded: 2000

Home arena: Climate Pledge Arena

Colours: Thunder green, lightning yellow, bright green

Championships: 4 (2004, 2010, 2018, 2020)

Notable players: Sue Bird, Lauren Jackson, Breanna Stewart, Jewell Loyd

History

The Storm began play in 2000 as an expansion team affiliated with the Seattle SuperSonics. After drafting Australian star Lauren Jackson first overall in 2001 and point guard Sue Bird first overall in 2002, the Storm won their first championship in 2004 under coach Anne Donovan, defeating the Connecticut Sun. A second title followed in 2010, when the team went 17–0 at home.

Stewart era and later titles

With top draft pick Breanna Stewart, Jewell Loyd and the veteran Bird, Seattle won further championships in 2018 and in the 2020 "bubble" season. Bird retired in 2022 after 19 seasons, all with the Storm, as the WNBA's all-time assists leader. The franchise was sold in 2008 to local ownership group Force 10 Hoops, keeping the team in Seattle after the SuperSonics' departure.
