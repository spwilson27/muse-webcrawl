Ruby slippers

A pair of the ruby slippers at the Smithsonian

The ruby slippers are the magical shoes worn by Dorothy Gale, played by Judy Garland, in the 1939 MGM film The Wizard of Oz. In L. Frank Baum's original novel the shoes were silver; they were changed to ruby red for the film to take advantage of Technicolor.

In the film

The slippers are transferred to Dorothy's feet after her house kills the Wicked Witch of the East. Coveted by the Wicked Witch of the West, they protect Dorothy throughout her journey. At the end, Glinda reveals that clicking the heels together three times while repeating "There's no place like home" will return her to Kansas.

The surviving pairs

Several pairs were made for the film; four genuine pairs are known to survive. The best-known pair is held by the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History, donated anonymously in 1979. Another pair, stolen from the Judy Garland Museum in Minnesota in 2005, was recovered by the FBI in 2018 and later sold at auction for $28 million in 2024, a record for film memorabilia. Pairs are also held by the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures and a private collector.

Cultural significance

The slippers are among the most iconic props in cinema history and are protected by extensive replicas and merchandising. The phrase "there's no place like home" and the heel-clicking gesture remain enduring references to the film.
