The European Handball Federation (EHF) is the umbrella organisation for handball in Europe, founded in Berlin in 1991 and headquartered in Vienna, Austria. It has 50 member federations and two associated federations.

Competitions

The EHF organises the men's and women's European Handball Championships (EHF EURO), the EHF Champions League, the EHF European League and the EHF European Cup for clubs, as well as beach handball tournaments and youth championships.

History

Founded by eight nations in 1991, the federation grew rapidly after the Cold War as handball expanded across the continent; its club competitions and the EURO tournaments are among the most-watched team-sport events in Europe.
