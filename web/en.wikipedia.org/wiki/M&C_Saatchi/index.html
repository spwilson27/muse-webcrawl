M&C Saatchi is an international advertising and communications network founded in London in 1995 by brothers Maurice and Charles Saatchi after their departure from Saatchi & Saatchi, the agency they had founded in 1970, together with partners Jeremy Sinclair, Bill Muirhead, and David Kershaw.

History

After a shareholder revolt at Saatchi & Saatchi in late 1994 forced Maurice Saatchi out, the brothers and their partners launched the new agency in January 1995, quickly winning major clients including British Airways (taking the account from their old firm), Qantas, and the Conservative Party. The agency expanded internationally through a network of partner offices in cities including Sydney, New York, Los Angeles, and Singapore. Its founding creative philosophy, articulated by Jeremy Sinclair, is "Brutal Simplicity of Thought" — the belief that simple ideas enter the brain quicker and stay there longer.

Work

M&C Saatchi is known for political advertising for the UK Conservative Party, campaigns for British Airways, and work across sectors including government communications, sport, and retail. The group is listed on the London Stock Exchange's AIM market and operates specialist divisions in areas such as PR, sports marketing, and customer experience.
