Turin–Modane railway

Turin–Modane railway
Termini: Turin – Modane (French border)
Key structure: Fréjus Rail Tunnel
Opened: 1871 (through route)

The Turin–Modane railway is a major international rail line linking Turin in Italy with Modane in France through the Fréjus Rail Tunnel under the Alps. It forms the Italian section of the historic route between Paris, Turin and Rome, long marketed as the Fréjus line.

Route

From Turin the line ascends the Susa Valley through Bussoleno, Susa and Bardonecchia before entering the 13.7 km Fréjus Tunnel, opened in 1871 as one of the first great Alpine rail tunnels. On the French side it continues from Modane down the Maurienne valley toward Chambéry and Lyon.

Traffic

The line carries international passenger trains, regional services and substantial freight traffic, though capacity constraints and steep gradients have motivated the long-planned Turin–Lyon high-speed railway with its Mont d'Ambin base tunnel, intended to relieve the historic line.
