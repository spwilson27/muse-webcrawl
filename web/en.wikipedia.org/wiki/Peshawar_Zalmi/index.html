Peshawar Zalmi (Pashto/Urdu: پشاور زلمی; literally "youth of Peshawar") is a Pakistani franchise Twenty20 cricket team representing the city of Peshawar in the Pakistan Super League (PSL). The team was established in 2015 when the Pakistan Cricket Board launched the league, and is owned by businessman Javed Afridi.

History

Zalmi reached the playoffs in the inaugural 2016 season and won the championship in 2017, defeating Quetta Gladiators in the final in Lahore — the first PSL final held in Pakistan. The team finished as runners-up in 2018 and 2019 and has been one of the league's most consistent playoff sides. Long-serving figures include Darren Sammy, the West Indian all-rounder who captained the side to its 2017 title and later became head coach, fast bowler Wahab Riaz, the franchise's leading wicket-taker and later captain, and batsman Kamran Akmal, among the PSL's highest run-scorers. In later seasons Babar Azam became the team's marquee player and captain.

Identity and foundation

The team's yellow kit, lion emblem and the "Zalmi" brand have built one of the largest fan bases in the PSL. Through the Zalmi Foundation, the franchise funds sports development, women's cricket initiatives including regional talent programmes, and charitable causes in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Home ground

For most of the league's history, PSL matches were held in the UAE or major Pakistani venues such as Lahore and Karachi because of security constraints; Peshawar's Arbab Niaz Stadium has been developed to host Zalmi home fixtures as league matches returned fully to Pakistan.
