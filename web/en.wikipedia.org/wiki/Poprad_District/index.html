Poprad District

Poprad District (Slovak: okres Poprad) is a district in the Prešov Region of eastern Slovakia. It is named after its seat and largest town, Poprad, which lies at the foot of the High Tatra mountains and serves as a major gateway for tourism in the region.

Geography

The district covers an area of roughly 1,105 square kilometres in the Spiš and Liptov areas of northern Slovakia. The terrain is dominated by the High Tatras to the north, whose peaks form the highest part of the Carpathian Mountains, and by the Poprad River valley, from which the district takes its name.

Municipalities

The district contains 29 municipalities, including the towns of Poprad, Svit and Vysoké Tatry. Vysoké Tatry, a town formed from a group of mountain settlements, is the centre of the Tatra national park's visitor infrastructure.

Demographics

The population is around 105,000, predominantly Slovak, with small Roma, Rusyn and other minorities. The district's economy is strongly oriented toward tourism, mountain recreation and related services, as well as light industry in the towns of Poprad and Svit.
