Comparative naval enlisted ranks of the world

[Image: Naval enlisted rank insignia]

This article is a comparative table of the enlisted ranks — non-commissioned officers and junior ratings — used by the navies of the world. Grades are aligned using the NATO "other ranks" codes OR-1 to OR-9 set out in STANAG 2116, which permits comparison between systems whose titles, such as seaman, able seaman, leading seaman, petty officer and chief petty officer, reflect different national traditions.

Many navies subdivide these grades further, and some use "warrant officer" appointments within the enlisted structure. The top enlisted sailor of a navy often holds a special advisory post, such as the Master Chief Petty Officer of the US Navy or the Warrant Officer of the Naval Service in the Royal Navy.
