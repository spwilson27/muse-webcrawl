Dave Nada

Dave Nada (real name David Villegas) is an American DJ and producer based in Los Angeles, formerly of Washington, D.C. He is credited with creating moombahton, a fusion genre of house music and reggaeton, in late 2009, when he slowed down Dutch house tracks to reggaeton tempo while DJing a high-school party for his younger cousin in Maryland.

Moombahton

Nada's slowed-down remixes, released as the Moombahton EP with Matt Nordstrom (as the duo Nadastrom) in 2010, defined the genre's sound: 108–110 BPM tracks combining house synths with dembow rhythms. Moombahton spread through producers such as Dillon Francis, Munchi and Diplo, becoming a significant strand of electronic dance music in the early 2010s.

Other work

With Matt Nordstrom, Nada formed Nadastrom, releasing techno and house music on labels such as Dubsided and Boysnoize Records. As a solo artist and remixer he has been associated with the T&A Records label and the Baltimore club scene, and he has toured internationally both solo and with Nadastrom.
