Audrey II (also written Audrey Jr. in the 1960 film) is a fictional character and the main antagonist in the Little Shop of Horrors franchise. It is a man-eating alien plant that arrives on Earth during a total eclipse of the sun and is discovered by the hapless florist's assistant Seymour Krelborn, who names it after his co-worker and love interest, Audrey. The plant thrives on human blood, growing ever larger and more demanding, and eventually reveals its plan for world domination.

Appearances

1960 film

In Roger Corman's The Little Shop of Horrors (1960), the plant (called Audrey Jr.) is created by Seymour Fuchter as a cross between a butterwort and a Venus flytrap. It speaks in a weak voice asking to be fed, and Seymour eventually resorts to murder to satisfy it.

Musical and 1986 film

The 1982 Off-Broadway musical by Alan Menken and Howard Ashman, and Frank Oz's 1986 film adaptation, developed Audrey II into a smooth-talking, rhythm-and-blues-singing creature, voiced in the film by Levi Stubbs of the Four Tops. The plant's songs include "Grow for Me", "Feed Me (Git It)", "Suppertime", and "Mean Green Mother from Outer Space", the latter written for the film and nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song. In the musical's original ending, Audrey II kills Audrey and Seymour and its offspring overrun the world; the film's theatrical cut substituted a happy ending in which the plant is electrocuted, while the original ending was restored in a 2012 director's cut release.

Design and puppetry

For the 1986 film, Audrey II was realized as a series of increasingly large puppets—six stages of growth in all—operated by teams of puppeteers; the largest stood over 12 feet tall and required up to 40 operators for some shots. Scenes of the plant singing were filmed at reduced speed with Rick Moranis acting slowly, then sped up so the plant's mouth movements could sync naturally with the music.
