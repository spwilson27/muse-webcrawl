Laurel, Maryland

Laurel, Maryland
State: Maryland
Counties: Prince George's, Anne Arundel, Howard, Montgomery
Population: approx. 30,000

Laurel is a city in Maryland, United States, located midway between Washington, D.C., and Baltimore on the banks of the Patuxent River. The city lies mostly in northern Prince George's County, with portions extending into three neighbouring counties.

Main Street, Laurel.

History

Laurel grew around a grist mill and, later, a cotton mill established on the Patuxent River in the early 19th century by the Snowden family. Known first as Laurel Factory, the town developed as a mill community and a stop on the B&O Railroad between Baltimore and Washington. It was incorporated in 1870.

Modern Laurel

Today Laurel is a suburban community known for the historic Laurel Park thoroughbred racetrack, founded in 1911, and for its proximity to Fort Meade and the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory, a major local employer. The city's name is said to derive from the mountain laurel growing along the river.
