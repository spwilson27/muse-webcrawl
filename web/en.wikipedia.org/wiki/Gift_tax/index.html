Gift tax

A gift tax is a tax on the transfer of property by one individual to another while receiving nothing, or less than full value, in return. The tax applies whether or not the donor intends the transfer to be a gift. Gift taxes are imposed by a number of jurisdictions; this article concentrates on the federal gift tax of the United States, administered by the Internal Revenue Service.

United States federal gift tax

The modern federal gift tax was enacted in 1932, primarily to prevent avoidance of the estate tax through lifetime transfers and to backstop the income tax. It is imposed on the donor, not the recipient; recipients generally do not report gifts as income. Taxable gifts are calculated on a calendar-year basis and reported on IRS Form 709.

Several exclusions reduce or eliminate liability. The annual exclusion permits a donor to give a set amount per recipient per year free of tax — US$17,000 per recipient in 2023 and US$18,000 in 2024, with married couples able to double the amount by gift-splitting. Payments made directly to educational institutions for tuition and to medical providers are excluded without limit, as are gifts to a spouse who is a U.S. citizen and gifts to qualifying charities and political organisations.

Unified credit and rates

Beyond the annual exclusion, gifts draw first on a lifetime unified credit shared with the estate tax; the combined basic exclusion amount was US$12.92 million per person in 2023 and US$13.61 million in 2024. Only when a donor's cumulative taxable gifts exceed the exclusion does cash tax become payable, at graduated rates reaching 40%. Generation-skipping transfers to grandchildren and beyond may attract an additional generation-skipping transfer tax.

Other countries

Many other countries tax gratuitous transfers, sometimes under the same regime as inheritances: for example, Germany and France impose gift taxes (in France, droits de donation) with allowances that vary by relationship, while the United Kingdom treats substantial gifts as potentially exempt transfers for inheritance tax purposes if the donor survives seven years.
