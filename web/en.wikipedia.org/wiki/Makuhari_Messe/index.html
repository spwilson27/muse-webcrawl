Makuhari Messe (幕張メッセ) is a Japanese convention center located in the Makuhari New City district of Mihama-ku, Chiba City, in the greater Tokyo area. It is one of Japan's largest convention venues, together with Tokyo Big Sight.

Facilities

The complex, designed by architect Fumihiko Maki, comprises the International Exhibition Hall (Halls 1–11), the International Conference Hall and the Makuhari Event Hall, an arena seating about 9,000 used for concerts and sporting events.

Events

Makuhari Messe hosts the Tokyo Auto Salon, the Tokyo Game Show, Jump Festa, Wonder Festival and numerous concerts and trade fairs. During the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games it hosted taekwondo, fencing, wrestling and goalball events.

Access

The venue is a short walk from Kaihin-Makuhari Station on the JR Keiyō Line.
