Kamal Haasan (born 7 November 1954) is an Indian actor, filmmaker, screenwriter, singer, and politician who works predominantly in Tamil cinema. One of the most decorated actors in Indian film history, he has acted in more than 230 films across Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, and Bengali.

Film career

Haasan began as a child artist in Kalathur Kannamma (1960), winning the President's Gold Medal. His breakthrough as a lead actor came in the 1970s with K. Balachander's films, and landmark performances include Moondram Pirai (1982), Nayakan (1987), Indian (1996), Hey Ram (2000), and Vikram (2022). He has won four National Film Awards and numerous Filmfare Awards, and received the Padma Shri (1990) and Padma Bhushan (2014).

Politics

In 2018 Haasan founded the political party Makkal Needhi Maiam ("People's Justice Centre") and contested the Tamil Nadu assembly elections.
