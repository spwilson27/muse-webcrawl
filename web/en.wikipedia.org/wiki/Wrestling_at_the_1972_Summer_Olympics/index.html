Wrestling at the 1972 Summer Olympics

GamesXX Olympiad, Munich 1972
VenueMesse München (fairgrounds), Munich
Events20 (10 freestyle, 10 Greco-Roman)
Participants388 wrestlers from 49 nations

Wrestling at the 1972 Summer Olympics consisted of 20 events — ten weight classes each in freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestling, all for men — contested in Munich, West Germany. The competition was held at the Munich fairgrounds (Messe München).

Competition

Weight classes ranged from light flyweight (48 kg) to super heavyweight (+100 kg), a class added at these Games. Bouts used the "bad points" elimination system then standard in international wrestling.

Highlights

The Soviet Union dominated the medal table. In Greco-Roman, Soviet heavyweight Anatoly Roshchin won gold; Valeriy Rezantsev began his run of two consecutive titles at 90 kg. In freestyle, American Dan Gable won the lightweight gold medal famously without surrendering a single point during the entire tournament — one of the most celebrated feats in Olympic wrestling — while his teammate Wayne Wells also won gold. The Soviet Aleksandr Medved won his third consecutive Olympic heavyweight freestyle title, and Japan's wrestlers continued their traditional strength in the lighter classes.

Medal summary (selected champions)

  • Freestyle 48 kg: Roman Dmitriyev (URS)
  • Freestyle 68 kg: Dan Gable (USA)
  • Freestyle 74 kg: Wayne Wells (USA)
  • Freestyle +100 kg: Aleksandr Medved (URS)
  • Greco-Roman 90 kg: Valeriy Rezantsev (URS)
  • Greco-Roman +100 kg: Anatoly Roshchin (URS)

Medal table

The Soviet Union topped the wrestling medal table, followed by the United States, Bulgaria, Japan and Iran among the other leading nations.
