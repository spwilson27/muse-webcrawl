List of poisonous plants

Plants produce toxins as a defence against herbivores. Many common garden, wild and even food plants contain compounds dangerous to humans or animals if ingested, inhaled or touched. This list covers plants notable for their toxicity.

Food plants with toxic parts

Several familiar foods are poisonous if prepared incorrectly or eaten in the wrong part: raw kidney beans (phytohaemagglutinin), cassava (cyanogenic glycosides if unprocessed), green potatoes (solanine), apple and stone-fruit seeds (amygdalin, in quantity), rhubarb leaves (oxalic acid) and nutmeg in large doses (myristicin).
