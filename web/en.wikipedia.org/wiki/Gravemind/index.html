Gravemind

The Gravemind is a fictional character in the Halo video game franchise created by Bungie. A massive, tentacled intelligence formed from the accumulated biomass and memories of the parasitic Flood, the Gravemind serves as the central coordinating mind of the Flood once an outbreak reaches sufficient scale. It is voiced by Dee Bradley Baker.

Role in the series

The Gravemind first appears in Halo 2 (2004), where it captures both the Master Chief and the Arbiter beneath the surface of Installation 05 and sends each on missions serving its own ends. Speaking largely in trochaic heptameter, it describes itself with the line "I am a monument to all your sins."

In Halo 3 (2007), the Gravemind reaches the Ark aboard the infested Covenant city of High Charity and attempts to prevent the firing of the Halo Array; it is destroyed when the replacement Installation 04 is fired prematurely. Expanded media in the franchise further describe the Gravemind's origins in the ancient war between the Forerunners and the Precursors, whose corrupted remnants became the Flood.

Reception

Critics have discussed the Gravemind as one of the series' most memorable antagonists, noting its literary, verse-speaking style and its role in revealing the history of the Flood and the Forerunners.
