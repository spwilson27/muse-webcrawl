Simple Lie group

In mathematics, a simple Lie group is a connected non-abelian Lie group whose Lie algebra is simple, that is, with no nontrivial connected normal subgroups (in the usual topological sense used in the classification theory).

Classification

Compact simple Lie groups are classified by their Lie algebras: the classical families SU(n+1), SO(2n+1), Sp(n) and SO(2n), together with the exceptional groups G2, F4, E6, E7 and E8. Each simply connected compact simple group has a centre of known order, giving the full list of compact simple groups.

Non-compact real forms are equally important in geometry and physics, including groups such as SL(2,ℝ) and the Lorentz groups.
