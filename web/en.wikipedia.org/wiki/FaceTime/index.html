FaceTime

FaceTime is a proprietary videotelephony product developed by Apple Inc. FaceTime is available on supported iOS mobile devices running iOS 4 and later and Mac computers that run Mac OS X 10.6.6 and later. FaceTime supports any iOS device with a forward-facing camera and any Mac computer equipped with a FaceTime Camera, formerly known as an iSight Camera.

DeveloperApple Inc.
Initial releaseJune 24, 2010
PlatformiOS, macOS, iPadOS, watchOS (audio only), tvOS via Continuity Camera, visionOS, web
TypeVideotelephony, VoIP

History

FaceTime was announced by then-Apple CEO Steve Jobs on June 7, 2010, during the keynote address at the Worldwide Developers Conference, in conjunction with the announcement of the iPhone 4. The name was purchased from FaceTime Communications, which changed its name to Actiance shortly thereafter.

Implementation

FaceTime calls can be placed over Wi-Fi or over a cellular data connection on supported carriers. The service uses a proprietary protocol that combines several technologies, including SIP, ICE, RTP, and SRTP for secure encrypted media transport. Since iOS 15, users on non-Apple devices can join FaceTime calls through a web link.

Group FaceTime

Group FaceTime, introduced with iOS 12.1, allows video calls with up to 32 participants simultaneously. Features such as SharePlay, spatial audio, portrait mode, and Center Stage were added in later releases.
