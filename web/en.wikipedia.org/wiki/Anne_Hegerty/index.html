Anne Solway Hegerty (born 14 July 1958) is a British professional quizzer and television personality. She is best known as "The Governess", one of the Chasers on the ITV game show The Chase, a role she has held since 2010.

Early life and career

Hegerty studied journalism and worked as a reporter and later as a proofreader and editor before becoming involved in competitive quizzing. She appeared as a contestant on shows including Mastermind and Brain of Britain, and reached high rankings in British quizzing competitions.

The Chase

Cast as a Chaser in the show's second series, Hegerty adopted the stern persona of "The Governess" (also "Frosty Knickers", a nickname bestowed by host Bradley Walsh). She has also appeared on the Australian version of the show and on spin-offs including Beat the Chasers.

Other work and advocacy

Hegerty appeared on I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! in 2018. She has spoken publicly about being autistic — diagnosed with Asperger syndrome as an adult — and serves as a patron of autism charities, helping raise awareness of the condition.
