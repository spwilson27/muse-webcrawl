Equality Commission for Northern Ireland

Formed1999
HeadquartersBelfast, Northern Ireland
BasisNorthern Ireland Act 1998

The Equality Commission for Northern Ireland is a non-departmental public body in Northern Ireland established under the Northern Ireland Act 1998. The commission is responsible for implementing the legislation on fair employment, sex discrimination, equal pay, race relations, sexual orientation, and disability.

History

The Commission took over the functions of the Fair Employment Commission, the Equal Opportunities Commission for Northern Ireland, the Commission for Racial Equality for Northern Ireland, and the Northern Ireland Disability Council when it began work in 1999, fulfilling a commitment in the Good Friday Agreement.

Functions

The Commission provides advice and assistance to people who believe they have been discriminated against, issues codes of practice, conducts investigations, and oversees the statutory equality duties placed on public authorities under Section 75 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998.
