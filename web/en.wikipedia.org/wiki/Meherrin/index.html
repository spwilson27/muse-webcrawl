Meherrin

The Meherrin are a Native American people of the Virginia–North Carolina border region, speakers of an Iroquoian language closely related to Tuscarora and Nottoway. Their name is often interpreted as "people of the muddy water", referring to the Meherrin River.

History

The Meherrin lived in towns along the lower Meherrin River at European contact. Pressured by colonial expansion and raiding, they were assigned a reservation near the river's mouth in the early 18th century and were closely allied with the Tuscarora. Many later merged with neighbouring tribes.

Today

The Meherrin Nation, based in Winton, North Carolina, is recognised by the state of North Carolina and maintains tribal government, cultural programmes and an annual powwow, while seeking federal recognition.
