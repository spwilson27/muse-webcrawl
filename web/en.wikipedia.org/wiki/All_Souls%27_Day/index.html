All Souls' Day

Date2 November (3 November if the 2nd is a Sunday)
Also calledCommemoration of All the Faithful Departed
Related toAll Saints' Day, Day of the Dead, Halloween

All Souls' Day, formally the Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed, is a day of prayer and remembrance for the souls of those who have died, observed in the Catholic Church and some other Christian traditions on 2 November. It follows All Saints' Day on 1 November and, together with Halloween, forms the season of Allhallowtide.

Customs

Believers pray for the souls of the departed, traditionally understood to be in purgatory, offer Masses and visit cemeteries to decorate graves with flowers and candles. In many countries, particular foods and processions are associated with the day; in Mexico it overlaps with the Day of the Dead celebrations.

History

The commemoration was promoted by St Odilo of Cluny in the late 10th and early 11th centuries and spread throughout the Western Church.
