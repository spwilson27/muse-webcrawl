Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors

Full name: Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors Football Club
Founded: 1994 (as Jeonbuk Dinos)
Ground: Jeonju World Cup Stadium, Jeonju, South Korea
Capacity: 42,477
Owner: Hyundai Motor Company
League: K League 1

Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors (Korean: 전북 현대 모터스) is a South Korean professional football club based in Jeonju, North Jeolla Province, competing in the K League 1. Owned by the Hyundai Motor Company, the club is one of the most successful in South Korean football, with a record number of K League championships and two AFC Champions League titles.

History

The club was founded in 1994 as the Jeonbuk Dinos and soon renamed after its sponsor, Hyundai Motor Company, whose major manufacturing plant is located in the region. Early years brought Korean FA Cup success, with the club winning the cup in 2000, 2003, and 2005. Under coach Choi Kang-hee, who took charge in 2005, Jeonbuk developed an attacking style nicknamed the "Dak-gong" (shut up and attack) philosophy.

Jeonbuk won its first AFC Champions League in 2006, defeating Al-Karamah of Syria in the final, and reached the Club World Cup. Domestically, the club won its first K League title in 2009 and then dominated the following decade, winning championships in 2011, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, and 2021. A second AFC Champions League crown came in 2016 against Al Ain of the UAE. In 2016 the club was embroiled in a referee bribery scandal dating to 2013, resulting in a points deduction domestically and exclusion from the 2017 AFC Champions League.

Stadium and supporters

The team plays at the Jeonju World Cup Stadium, built for the 2002 FIFA World Cup. The club's colors are green, and its main supporters' group is known as the Mad Green Boys.

Honours

Jeonbuk has won a record nine K League 1 titles, five Korean FA Cups, and two AFC Champions League titles, among other trophies.
