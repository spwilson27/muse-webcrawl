Waco, Texas

City of Waco
State: Texas
County: McLennan
Founded: 1849
Population: ~140,000

Waco is a city in and the county seat of McLennan County, Texas, United States, located along the Brazos River about halfway between Dallas and Austin.

History

The site was inhabited by the Wichita people (the Huaco) before settlement. Founded in 1849, Waco became a stop on the Chisholm Trail; the Waco Suspension Bridge (1870) was a major Brazos crossing. Baylor University, founded in 1845, moved to Waco in 1886. Dr Pepper was invented in Waco in 1885 by pharmacist Charles Alderton. In 1953 an F5 tornado devastated downtown, killing 114. In 1993 the federal siege of the Branch Davidian compound near Waco ended in a fire that killed 76 people.

Attractions

Landmarks include the Dr Pepper Museum, the Texas Ranger Hall of Fame, the Waco Mammoth National Monument (Columbian mammoth fossils), Cameron Park, and the Magnolia Market at the Silos, which draws large visitor numbers.

Education

Waco is home to Baylor University and McLennan Community College.
