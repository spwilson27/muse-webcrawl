Iarnród Éireann (Irish Rail) is the operator of the national railway network of Ireland. Established on 2 February 1987, it is a subsidiary of Córas Iompair Éireann (CIÉ). It provides passenger rail services throughout Ireland, including Intercity, commuter and DART services in the Dublin area, as well as rail freight services.

History

The company was created when CIÉ was split into operating subsidiaries. It inherited a network that had contracted significantly during the twentieth century. Since the 1990s, investment under successive national development plans modernised rolling stock and infrastructure, and passenger numbers grew steadily.

Services

Intercity routes radiate from Dublin to Cork, Belfast (jointly with NI Railways as the Enterprise), Galway, Waterford, Sligo, Westport and Tralee. The DART (Dublin Area Rapid Transit) provides electrified suburban services along the Dublin coast, and diesel commuter services serve the Greater Dublin Area and the Cork suburban network.

Network

The network uses the distinctive Irish broad gauge of 1,600 mm (5 ft 3 in). Major stations include Dublin Connolly, Dublin Heuston, Cork Kent and Belfast Lanyon Place.
