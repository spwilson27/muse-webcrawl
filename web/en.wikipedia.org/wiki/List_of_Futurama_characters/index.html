List of Futurama characters

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of characters appearing in the animated television series Futurama, created by Matt Groening. The series follows the employees of the Planet Express delivery company in the 31st century, beginning with Philip J. Fry, a 20th-century pizza delivery boy who was cryogenically frozen for a thousand years.

Main characters

CharacterVoiced byDescription
Philip J. FryBilly WestA dim-witted but good-hearted pizza delivery boy from 1999 who was frozen for 1,000 years. He works as a delivery boy for Planet Express and is the great-uncle (many times removed) of Professor Farnsworth.
Turanga LeelaKatee SagalThe one-eyed captain of the Planet Express Ship, a skilled martial artist and the most competent member of the crew. Long believed to be an alien, she is revealed to be a mutant from the sewers of New New York.
Bender Bending RodríguezJohn DiMaggioA foul-mouthed, alcoholic, kleptomaniac bending robot built in Tijuana, Mexico. Fry's best friend and roommate; his catchphrase is "Bite my shiny metal ass!"
Professor Hubert J. FarnsworthBilly WestThe elderly, mad-scientist founder of Planet Express and Fry's distant nephew. Known for announcing "Good news, everyone!" before sending the crew on dangerous missions.
Hermes ConradPhil LaMarrThe company's Jamaican bureaucrat (Grade 36) and former Olympic limbo athlete, obsessed with filing, stamping and paperwork.
Amy WongLauren TomA wealthy engineering intern from Mars, daughter of Leo and Inez Wong, owners of the western hemisphere of Mars. Later marries Kif Kroker.
Dr. John ZoidbergBilly WestA lobster-like Decapodian alien who serves as the company doctor despite his poor understanding of human anatomy and his perpetual poverty.
