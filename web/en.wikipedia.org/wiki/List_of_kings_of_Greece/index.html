List of kings of Greece

The Kingdom of Greece was a monarchy from 1832 until its abolition in 1973–74, with an interruption during the Second Hellenic Republic (1924–1935). Its monarchs came from the Bavarian House of Wittelsbach and, after 1863, from the Danish House of Glücksburg. This article lists the kings of Greece.

The Old Royal Palace in Athens, now the seat of the Hellenic Parliament.
