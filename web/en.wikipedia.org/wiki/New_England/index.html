New England is a region comprising six states in the Northeastern United States: Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, and Vermont. It is bordered by the Atlantic Ocean to the east, Canada to the north, and the state of New York to the west. Boston is New England's largest city and its historical and cultural capital.

History

Indigenous peoples, including the Wampanoag, Narragansett, Abenaki, and Pequot, inhabited the region for thousands of years before European settlement. English explorer John Smith named the region "New England" in 1616. The Pilgrims founded Plymouth Colony in 1620, and Puritan settlers established the Massachusetts Bay Colony in 1630; settlements at Connecticut, Rhode Island, and New Hampshire followed. The colonies briefly united as the Dominion of New England (1686–1689).

New England played a central role in the American Revolution: the Battles of Lexington and Concord, the Battle of Bunker Hill, and the Boston Tea Party all took place there. In the 19th century the region became a center of abolitionism, transcendentalist literature, and textile and maritime industry, while fishing, whaling, and shipbuilding shaped its coastal economy.

Geography

The region features a rugged coastline, sandy peninsulas such as Cape Cod, and islands including Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket. Inland lie the Berkshire Hills, Connecticut River Valley, White Mountains of New Hampshire (including Mount Washington), the Green Mountains of Vermont, Acadia National Park in Maine, and the Champlain Valley. The climate is humid continental, with cold, snowy winters and warm summers; the region is famous for its vivid autumn foliage.

Economy and culture

Modern New England's economy centers on education, health care, biotechnology, finance, and technology, anchored by Boston and Cambridge. The region hosts some of the world's most prestigious universities, including Harvard, MIT, and Yale. Cultural hallmarks include town meeting government, distinctive regional dialects and cuisine (clam chowder, lobster rolls, maple syrup), historic covered bridges and lighthouses, and fervent support for sports teams such as the Boston Red Sox, Celtics, Bruins, and the New England Patriots.
