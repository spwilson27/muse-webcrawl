Lim Yo-hwan (Korean: 임요환; born 4 September 1980), known by his in-game ID SlayerS_BoxeR and nicknamed "The Emperor", is a South Korean former professional StarCraft player and poker player. Playing as Terran, he became the most popular and recognisable star of the Brood War era and is widely credited with popularising professional gaming in South Korea.

Career

BoxeR rose to dominance in the early 2000s, winning back-to-back World Cyber Games titles (2001 and 2002) and two OnGameNet Starleague championships. He was famed for creative, micro-intensive strategies — including celebrated dropship play and improbable comebacks — and for showmanship that drew enormous television audiences. His official fan club numbered in the hundreds of thousands.

In 2003 he became a founding star of SK Telecom T1. After completing his mandatory military service (during which he played for an Air Force team), he founded the team SlayerS in 2010 and transitioned to StarCraft II, competing in the GSL before moving into coaching and later taking up professional poker.

Legacy

Lim is routinely cited as the single most important figure in the growth of esports in Korea: the first true celebrity progamer, whose rivalry with Hong Jin-ho ("YellOw") became a national cultural reference point.
