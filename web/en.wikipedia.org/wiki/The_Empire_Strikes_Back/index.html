The Empire Strikes Back

The Empire Strikes Back (later marketed as Star Wars: Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back) is a 1980 American epic space opera film directed by Irvin Kershner, with a screenplay by Leigh Brackett and Lawrence Kasdan based on a story by George Lucas. It is the second film in the Star Wars franchise and the fifth chronological chapter of the Skywalker Saga.

Plot

Three years after the destruction of the Death Star, Imperial forces drive the Rebels from their base on Hoth. Luke Skywalker trains with Jedi Master Yoda on Dagobah while Han Solo, Leia Organa and their companions are pursued by Darth Vader. In Cloud City, Vader reveals to Luke that he is his father, in one of the most famous twists in cinema history.

Reception

Initial reviews were mixed, but the film is now widely regarded as the best of the Star Wars series and one of the greatest films ever made. It won the Academy Award for Best Sound and a Special Achievement Award for visual effects, and was selected for the National Film Registry in 2010.
