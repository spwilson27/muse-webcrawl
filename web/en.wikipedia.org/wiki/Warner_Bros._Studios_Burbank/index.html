Warner Bros. Studios Burbank

Warner Bros. Studios Burbank is a major film and television studio complex in Burbank, California, serving as the principal production facility and headquarters of Warner Bros. since 1928.

Facilities

The lot contains around three dozen sound stages, a large backlot with standing exterior sets — including a small-town square, city streets and a jungle lagoon — production offices, post-production facilities and the Warner Bros. Ranch nearby. The studio's distinctive water tower, painted with the WB shield, is a Burbank landmark. The Warner Bros. Studio Tour Hollywood takes visitors through the working lot.

History

Warner Bros. purchased the Burbank site in the late 1920s after outgrowing its Hollywood facilities, moving there as sound pictures (which the studio had pioneered with The Jazz Singer in 1927) demanded larger stages. Classic films shot on the lot include Casablanca (1942) and Rebel Without a Cause (1955), and long-running television productions such as Friends, ER and The Big Bang Theory were filmed on its stages.
