Cash App

Cash App (formerly Square Cash) is a mobile payment service developed by Block, Inc. (formerly Square, Inc.), launched in 2013. It allows users in the United States and the United Kingdom to send, receive, and request money using a mobile phone application, identified by unique $Cashtag usernames, phone numbers, or email addresses.

Features

Services include peer-to-peer payments, a linked debit card (the Cash Card), direct deposit, stock investing, and buying and selling bitcoin. Businesses can accept payments through Cash for Business, subject to transaction fees. Cash App Taxes offers free tax filing, following Block's acquisition of Credit Karma Tax.

History and reception

The service grew rapidly, reporting tens of millions of monthly active users, and became culturally prominent in hip-hop and social media. It has also attracted scrutiny over fraud, scams, customer support, and regulatory compliance; Block agreed to settlements with regulators over money-laundering and consumer-protection issues in 2025, and the service is frequently targeted by phishing and giveaway scams.
