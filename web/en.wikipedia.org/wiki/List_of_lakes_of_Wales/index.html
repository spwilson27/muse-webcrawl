Wales contains numerous lakes, known in Welsh as llynnoedd (singular llyn), most of them natural glacial lakes in the mountains of Snowdonia (Eyri) and the Cambrian Mountains, together with large artificial reservoirs built mainly to supply water to English cities.

Other notable lakes

  • Llyn Padarn and Llyn Peris — at Llanberis, beneath Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa).
  • Llyn Ogwen — in the Ogwen Valley between the Glyderau and Carneddau.
  • Llyn Idwal — famous cwm lake associated with Darwin's glacial studies.
  • Llyn y Fan Fach — in the Brecon Beacons, linked to the legend of the Lady of the Lake.
  • Llangorse Lake (Llyn Syfaddan) — largest natural lake in south Wales.
  • Llyn Gwynant — in the Nant Gwynant valley.
  • Tal-y-llyn Lake (Llyn Mwyngil) — below Cadair Idris.
  • Llyn Cowlyd — among the deepest lakes in Wales.
