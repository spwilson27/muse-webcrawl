Riva Ridge

Riva Ridge (1969 – 21 April 1985) was an American Thoroughbred racehorse, winner of the 1972 Kentucky Derby and Belmont Stakes. He was bred and owned by Meadow Stable of Christopher Chenery and trained by Lucien Laurin.

Racing career

Champion two-year-old in 1971, Riva Ridge won the 1972 Kentucky Derby under jockey Ron Turcotte. He lost the Preakness Stakes on a muddy track but took the Belmont Stakes, missing the Triple Crown. His earnings are credited with rescuing Meadow Stable from financial difficulty, paving the way for his stablemate Secretariat's historic 1973 campaign.

Later life

Retired to stud at Claiborne Farm, Riva Ridge sired several stakes winners and died in 1985. He was inducted into the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame in 1998.
