Rai 3 is an Italian free-to-air television channel owned and operated by RAI, the country's national public broadcasting company. Launched in 1979 as Italy's third national network, it was conceived as the cultural, educational, and current-affairs channel of the public broadcaster, with a strong mandate for regional programming reflecting Italy's regions and minority languages.

Programming

Rai 3's schedule is built around news and in-depth current-affairs programming — including the long-running TG3 news bulletins and investigative strands such as Report and PresaDiretta — together with documentaries, cultural magazines, and regional opt-outs produced by RAI's regional editorial offices. The channel has a reputation for independent journalism and for giving airtime to linguistic minorities, including Slovene, German, and French-speaking audiences in border regions.
