Guillermo del Toro's Pinocchio

Directors: Guillermo del Toro, Mark Gustafson

Release: 2022

Technique: Stop-motion animation

Based on: Pinocchio by Carlo Collodi

Guillermo del Toro's Pinocchio is a 2022 stop-motion animated musical fantasy film directed by Guillermo del Toro and Mark Gustafson, based on Carlo Collodi's 1883 novel. It reimagines the tale of the wooden puppet in Fascist Italy of the 1930s, where the grieving woodcarver Geppetto carves Pinocchio after losing his son Carlo in the First World War.

Production

Del Toro developed the project for over a decade before Netflix acquired it; it was produced with the Jim Henson Company and animated largely in Portland, Oregon, and Guadalajara, Mexico. Voices include Ewan McGregor as Sebastian J. Cricket, David Bradley as Geppetto, and Gregory Mann as Pinocchio.

Reception

The film was widely acclaimed and won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 95th Academy Awards.
