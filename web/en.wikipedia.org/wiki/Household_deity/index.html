Household deity

A household deity is a god or protective spirit worshipped within the home, guarding the household, its members and its stores. Household cults are among the most widespread forms of religious practice in the ancient and traditional world.

Examples

In ancient Rome the Lares and Penates watched over the family and its pantry, receiving offerings at the household shrine (lararium), while Vesta governed the hearth. Greek homes honoured Hestia, Zeus Herkeios and the Agathos Daimon. Chinese religion venerates the Kitchen God (Zao Jun) and door gods; Japanese homes maintain kamidana shelves; Slavic folklore features the domovoi, a house spirit.

Function

Household worship typically centres on daily or seasonal offerings of food, drink and incense, binds family identity across generations, and marks domestic thresholds, hearths and store-rooms as sacred spaces.
