Digital signage

Digital signage is a sub-segment of electronic signage: digital displays used to show electronic images, video, web pages, menus, directions, or marketing messages. Digital signs use LCD, LED, projection, and e-paper technologies and are managed remotely through content management systems.

Applications

Common settings include retail stores, transport hubs, stadiums, hotels, restaurants (digital menu boards), corporate buildings, and public information points. Networks of advertising displays in public places are called digital out-of-home (DOOH) media.

Technology

A typical installation combines displays, media players, and server or cloud software that schedules and updates content. Modern systems can react to audience data, weather, or inventory, and interactive kiosks add touch input.
