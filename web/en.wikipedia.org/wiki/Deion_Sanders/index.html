Deion Luwynn Sanders (born 9 August 1967), nicknamed "Prime Time" and now "Coach Prime", is an American football coach and former professional player who is the head football coach at the University of Colorado. He is the only athlete to play in both a Super Bowl and a World Series, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest cornerbacks of all time.

Playing career

At Florida State University, Sanders starred in football, baseball and track, winning the Jim Thorpe Award as the nation's best defensive back in 1988. Drafted fifth overall by the Atlanta Falcons in 1989, he played fourteen NFL seasons as a cornerback and kick returner for the Falcons, San Francisco 49ers, Dallas Cowboys, Washington Redskins and Baltimore Ravens, winning Super Bowls with San Francisco (XXIX) and Dallas (XXX) and being named NFL Defensive Player of the Year in 1994. He was elected to the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2011. Simultaneously, Sanders played nine seasons of Major League Baseball as an outfielder, most notably with the New York Yankees and Atlanta Braves, appearing in the 1992 World Series with Atlanta — on one October weekend in 1992 he famously attempted to play in an NFL game and a baseball playoff game on the same day.

Coaching career

After a broadcasting career with NFL Network, Sanders became head coach at Jackson State University in 2020, building the HBCU programme into a national story and winning two SWAC championships. In December 2022 he took over the struggling Colorado Buffaloes, drawing enormous media attention — the "Prime Effect" — and transforming the programme's profile and roster through the transfer portal.
