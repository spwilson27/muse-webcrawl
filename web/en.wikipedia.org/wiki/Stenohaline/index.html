Stenohaline

Stenohaline describes an organism, usually fish, that cannot tolerate a wide fluctuation in the salinity of water. Stenohaline is derived from the words "steno" meaning narrow, and "haline" meaning salt. Many fresh water fish, such as goldfish, tend to be stenohaline and cannot survive in environments of high salinity such as the ocean. Conversely, most marine fish are also stenohaline and cannot live in fresh water.

Alternatively, organisms that can only tolerate a narrow range of salinity are described as stenohaline, whereas euryhaline organisms can adapt to a wide range of salinities. Examples of euryhaline organisms include salmon and eels, which move between fresh and salt water during their life cycles.
