IRAC

IRAC is a legal analysis method and acronym standing for Issue, Rule, Analysis (or Application), and Conclusion. It is the standard framework taught in law schools, chiefly in the United States, for organising legal memoranda, examination answers and case briefs.

Components

Issue — a statement of the legal question or problem to be resolved. Rule — the relevant governing law, drawn from statutes, regulations, precedent or constitutional provisions. Analysis/Application — the application of the rule to the specific facts of the case, weighing arguments on both sides. Conclusion — a summary answer to the issue posed.

Variations

Related frameworks include CRAC (Conclusion, Rule, Analysis, Conclusion), CREAC, and FIRAC (adding Facts at the start). Critics note that strict IRAC can encourage formulaic writing, but it remains the dominant pedagogical structure for legal reasoning.
