Tone deafness

Tone deafness, technically called amusia or congenital amusia in its inherited form, is a deficit in perceiving or producing musical pitch. Affected people struggle to recognise melodies, detect out-of-tune notes, or sing in tune, despite normal hearing and intelligence.

Congenital amusia affects an estimated 1.5–4% of the population and is associated with differences in the auditory cortex and its connections. Acquired amusia can result from brain injury. The colloquial term is often misapplied to people who simply sing poorly due to lack of training.
