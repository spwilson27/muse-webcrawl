Chelsea F.C.

Chelsea Football Club is a professional football club based in Fulham, West London, England. Founded in 1905, the club competes in the Premier League, the top tier of English football. Chelsea play their home games at Stamford Bridge.

The club won their first major honour, the League championship, in 1955. Chelsea won the FA Cup for the first time in 1970, their first European honour, the UEFA Cup Winners' Cup, in 1971, and became the third English club to win the Club World Cup in 2022.

History

Chelsea were founded on 10 March 1905 at The Rising Sun pub (now The Butcher's Hook), opposite the present-day main entrance to Stamford Bridge. Businessman Gus Mears had acquired the Stamford Bridge athletics stadium with the aim of turning it into a football ground, and founded the club to occupy it. The club's early decades brought modest success, but Chelsea gained a reputation for signing big-name players and attracting large crowds.

Abramovich era

In June 2003, Chelsea were bought by Russian billionaire Roman Abramovich. Under manager José Mourinho, appointed in 2004, Chelsea won back-to-back Premier League titles in 2004–05 and 2005–06. The club reached its first UEFA Champions League final in 2008, losing to Manchester United on penalties, before winning the competition in 2012 under Roberto Di Matteo, defeating Bayern Munich on penalties in Munich. Chelsea won a second Champions League title in 2021 under Thomas Tuchel, beating Manchester City in the final in Porto.

Honours

Chelsea are one of five clubs to have won all three main European club competitions, and the only club to have won all four current major UEFA club competitions. Domestically, the club has won six league titles, eight FA Cups, five League Cups and four FA Community Shields. In 2022, following sanctions against Abramovich after the Russian invasion of Ukraine, the club was sold to a consortium led by Todd Boehly and Clearlake Capital.
