Tellico Dam

Tellico Dam is a concrete gravity dam on the Little Tennessee River in Loudon County, Tennessee, built by the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA). Completed in 1979, it became famous for the snail darter controversy, a landmark environmental law case that reached the U.S. Supreme Court.

Snail darter controversy

In 1973 the snail darter, a small fish, was discovered in the river and listed as endangered under the Endangered Species Act. Litigation halted the nearly complete dam; in TVA v. Hill (1978) the Supreme Court upheld the injunction. Congress ultimately exempted the dam, the gates closed in 1979, and snail darters were transplanted to other rivers, where the species later recovered.

Features

Tellico Dam has no powerhouses of its own; a canal diverts water to Fort Loudoun Dam on the Tennessee River for generation. Its reservoir, Tellico Lake, supports recreation and shoreline development including Tellico Village.
