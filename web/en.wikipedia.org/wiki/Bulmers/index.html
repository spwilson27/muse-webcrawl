Bulmers

Bulmer's Cider is a brand of cider produced in Ireland by the C&C Group. Since 1935 the operation in Clonmel, County Tipperary, has produced cider sold in the Republic of Ireland as Bulmers and outside Ireland under the name Magners. The Irish company is separate from the English cider maker H. P. Bulmer of Hereford, founded by Percy Bulmer, though both trace their origins to the same Bulmer family cider-making tradition.

History

In 1935 William Magner bought a stake in a Clonmel cider business, acquiring full rights to use the Bulmers name in Ireland; the firm traded as Bulmer's Ltd. In the 1940s H. P. Bulmer of Hereford took a shareholding, but the two companies later went separate ways — Showerings (later C&C) regained full control of the Irish business, with an agreement that the English firm owned the Bulmers name outside Ireland. Hence the export brand Magners, launched internationally in the late 1990s and 2000s, spearheaded the "over ice" cider boom in Britain from 2005.

Production and products

Bulmers is made from up to 17 varieties of apples, fermented and matured at the Clonmel plant. The range includes Original, Light, Pear, Berry and alcohol-free variants, typically served over ice. The brand is closely associated with Irish pub culture and with sponsorship of Irish sport and music festivals.
