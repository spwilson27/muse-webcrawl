New Century Global Center

Location: Tianfu New Area, Chengdu, Sichuan, China
Opened: 1 July 2013
Floor area: about 1.76 million m² (19 million sq ft)
Height: 100 m
Length × width: about 500 m × 400 m

The New Century Global Center (Chinese: 新世纪环球中心) is a multipurpose building in the Tianfu New Area of Chengdu, Sichuan, China. Opened in 2013, it is one of the largest buildings in the world by floor area, at roughly 1.7–1.8 million square metres, and has frequently been described as the world's largest standalone building. Its promoters noted that it could fit about 20 Sydney Opera Houses or several Pentagons inside.

Contents

The building combines retail, offices, hotels, entertainment, and leisure around a vast central atrium under a glass dome roughly 100 m high. Its best-known attraction is the Paradise Island Water Park, an indoor water park with an artificial beach of about 5,000 m², one of the world's largest indoor LED screens displaying sunrises and sunsets, and a wave pool. The complex also contains:

  • A large shopping mall;
  • Two five-star hotels (including an InterContinental);
  • An IMAX cinema and multiplex;
  • An ice skating rink;
  • Offices, conference facilities, and a Mediterranean-style village replica;
  • A central square and parking for thousands of cars.

Development

The center was developed by the Entertainment Travel Group of local entrepreneur Deng Hong. It was built over about three years and opened on 1 July 2013 as the centrepiece of the planned Tianfu New Area south of central Chengdu. Soon after opening, Deng Hong was reported to be under investigation in connection with corruption cases in Sichuan, and parts of the complex were temporarily closed; the building nevertheless continued operating as a major retail and leisure destination.

Records

With a floor area exceeding that of the previous record holder (Dubai International Airport Terminal 3 is larger as an airport facility but the Global Center is generally cited as the largest building by floor space), the New Century Global Center has been listed by Guinness World Records among the world's largest buildings. The building measures approximately 500 m long, 400 m wide, and 100 m high.
