London

CountryUnited Kingdom (England)
FoundedRoman Londinium, c. AD 47-50
LandmarksBig Ben, Tower of London, Buckingham Palace

London is the capital and largest city of England and of the United Kingdom. Standing on the River Thames in south-east England, it has a population of roughly nine million in Greater London, as estimated in recent years, and is a global centre of finance, culture and government.

Overview

Founded by the Romans as Londinium nearly two thousand years ago, London grew into the largest city of the British Empire and pioneered the underground railway (the Tube, from 1863). Landmarks include the Houses of Parliament and Big Ben, Buckingham Palace, St Paul's Cathedral, the Tower of London and the London Eye, and it hosted the Olympic Games in 1908, 1948 and 2012 — the only city to host three times, as commonly noted.
