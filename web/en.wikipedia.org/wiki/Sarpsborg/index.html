Sarpsborg

Sarpsborg (pronounced [ˈsɑ̀ʂbɔrɡ]) is a city and municipality in Østfold county, Norway, located on the Glomma river near the Swedish border. It is the county capital and has a population of roughly 59,000. The city forms a conurbation with neighbouring Fredrikstad.

History

Sarpsborg was founded in 1016 by King Olaf II Haraldsson (Saint Olaf) under the name Borg, making it one of Norway's oldest cities and its capital for a time. The mighty Sarpefossen waterfall on the Glomma powered sawmills and later industry. In 1567, during the Northern Seven Years' War, Swedish forces burned the town, and the population was moved downstream to what became Fredrikstad. Sarpsborg was re-founded in 1839 and grew as an industrial town: Borregaard, founded in 1889, became one of Norway's largest industrial companies, producing cellulose, chemicals and later fine chemicals from timber.

Economy and culture

Industry remains important, alongside services and retail. The city celebrated its millennium in 2016. Sarpsborg 08 FF plays in Norway's top football division, the Eliteserien. Borgarsyssel Museum and the nearby Hafslund Manor preserve local history.
