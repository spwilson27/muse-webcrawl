NOON state

A NOON state is a quantum-mechanical many-body entangled state of the form (|N,0⟩ + |0,N⟩)/√2, in which all N particles occupy one of two modes and none occupy the other. The name comes from reading the state as "N" plus "0" in each term.

Properties

NOON states are highly sensitive to phase differences between the two modes: acquiring N times the phase of a single particle, they oscillate N times faster. This makes them useful in quantum metrology, where they can in principle achieve Heisenberg-limited precision, surpassing the standard quantum limit.

Applications and challenges

NOON states have been demonstrated with photons, ions and superconducting circuits, typically with small values of N. They are fragile: loss of a single particle destroys the coherence, which limits their practical size.
