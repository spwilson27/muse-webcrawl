Overview

The Pathet Lao ("Lao Nation") was the communist political movement and guerrilla organisation in Laos, analogous to the Viet Minh and Viet Cong in Vietnam, which fought the Royal Lao Government during the Laotian Civil War (1959–1975) and took power in 1975. Organised through the Lao People's Party (1955, later the Lao People's Revolutionary Party) under Kaysone Phomvihane, with Prince Souphanouvong — the "Red Prince" — as its public face, it operated from the liberated zones of Sam Neua and Phongsaly, supplied by North Vietnam via the Ho Chi Minh Trail.

War and victory

Laos's war was the "Secret War": the United States dropped some two million tons of bombs on Laos — per capita the most heavily bombed country in history — while the Pathet Lao, sheltered in the Plain of Jars and the Viengxay caves, survived. After the 1973 Vientiane ceasefire, a coalition government unravelled; through 1975 the Pathet Lao took control of the country almost without a final battle, abolishing the 600-year monarchy (King Savang Vatthana later died in a re-education camp) and proclaiming the Lao PDR on 2 December 1975. "Pathet Lao" also remains the name of the state news agency (KPL) in its French form.
