Celebrity Big Brother

The celebrity spin-off of the Big Brother reality format.

Celebrity Big Brother is a celebrity version of the reality television series Big Brother, in which a group of celebrity housemates live together in a specially constructed house, isolated from the outside world, while being continuously filmed. Housemates are periodically evicted by public vote or, in some versions, by fellow housemates, until a winner remains.

Format

The format follows the parent show: contestants live under constant surveillance, complete tasks to earn a shopping budget, nominate one another for eviction, and face the public vote. Celebrity series are usually shorter than civilian series, often running for about three weeks, and celebrities frequently compete for prize money donated to charity.

United Kingdom

The original Celebrity Big Brother launched in the United Kingdom in 2001 on Channel 4 as a Comic Relief special, won by comedian Jack Dee. It became a regular series alongside the civilian show, moved to Channel 5 in 2011, and after a hiatus returned on ITV in 2024. The UK version produced notable moments and controversies, including the 2007 race row involving Shilpa Shetty, which drew international attention and tens of thousands of complaints.

Other versions

Many countries have produced celebrity editions, including the United States (on CBS, from 2018), Australia, Germany, and India-adjacent formats. The American version adopted the faster competitive style of the US civilian game, with Head of Household and Power of Veto competitions.
