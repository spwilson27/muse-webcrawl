The University of Ottawa (uOttawa) is a bilingual public research university in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, located near Parliament Hill and the Rideau Canal. Founded in 1848 as the College of Bytown by the Missionary Oblates of Mary Immaculate, it is the largest English-French bilingual university in the world.

Academics

The university offers programs in both official languages across ten faculties, including the largest French immersion program among Canadian universities and respected faculties of law (teaching both common law and civil law) and medicine. Enrolment exceeds 48,000 students.

Campus and sport

The main campus lies in the Sandy Hill neighbourhood, with the health sciences campus near the Ottawa Hospital. The varsity teams, the Gee-Gees, take their name from a horse-racing term for the first horse out of the gate, chosen in the 19th century.
