List of largest optical reflecting telescopes

This is a list of the largest optical reflecting telescopes in the world, sorted by aperture. Reflecting telescopes use a curved primary mirror to gather light and form an image. The largest modern telescopes use segmented mirrors made of many hexagonal segments that together act as a single large aperture.

Under construction

Several extremely large telescopes (ELTs) are under construction, including the Extremely Large Telescope (39.3 m, Cerro Armazones, Chile), the Thirty Meter Telescope (30 m, Mauna Kea), and the Giant Magellan Telescope (24.5 m effective, Las Campanas, Chile).
