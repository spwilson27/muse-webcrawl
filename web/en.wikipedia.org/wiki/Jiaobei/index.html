Jiaobei

Jiaobei (Chinese: 蕉北; pinyin: Jiāoběi) is a subdistrict of Jiaocheng District in the city of Ningde, Fujian province, China. It lies in the northern part of the Jiaocheng urban area and serves as a residential and commercial district of the city.

A street in Jiaobei, Ningde.

Geography and administration

Jiaobei is one of the subdistricts that make up the urban core of Ningde, a prefecture-level city on Fujian's northeastern coast. Like much of Ningde, the area sits between coastal lowlands and the forested hills of the interior, and its economy is tied to the city's port, aquaculture and light industry.

History

Ningde's urban area grew rapidly in the late 20th and early 21st centuries as Fujian's coastal economy expanded. Jiaobei developed as part of that growth, with housing, schools and markets serving the expanding population of Jiaocheng District.
