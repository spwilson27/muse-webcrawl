Avi Arad

Avi Arad (Hebrew: אבי ארד; born 1948) is an Israeli-American film producer and businessman. He became the chief creative officer of Marvel Entertainment in 1993 and a key figure in Marvel's emergence from bankruptcy in the late 1990s, driving the licensing of Marvel characters for film and television. He founded Marvel Studios and served as its chairman and chief executive until 2006.

Career

Arad began his career as a toy designer before becoming chief executive of Toy Biz, the toy company whose merger with Marvel helped rescue the publisher. As a producer he was instrumental in launching successful Marvel adaptations including Blade, the X-Men series and Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy, and animated series such as X-Men: The Animated Series. After resigning from Marvel in 2006 he founded Arad Productions, through which he has continued to produce films including Ghost in the Shell (2017), Uncharted (2022), Morbius (2022) and Borderlands (2024), as well as animated features such as Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse.
