Stranda is a municipality in Møre og Romsdal county, Norway. It is part of the Sunnmøre region. The administrative centre is the village of Stranda. Other villages in the municipality include Hellesylt, Geiranger, and Liabygda. Stranda Municipality is known for its ski resort at Strandafjellet and as a gateway to the Geirangerfjord, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Geography

The municipality stretches along the Storfjorden and includes the famous Geirangerfjord branch. Mountainous terrain dominates, with deep fjords and alpine peaks popular for hiking and skiing. The village of Geiranger, at the head of the fjord, is one of Norway's most visited tourist destinations, receiving large numbers of cruise ships in summer.

History and economy

The municipality was established on 1 January 1838 as Stranden; it was later renamed Stranda. The local economy has strong food-processing and furniture industries: Stranda is home to Stabburet's production of Grandiosa frozen pizza — Norway's best-selling frozen pizza brand — as well as the Ekornes furniture group's origins in the neighbouring region and local meat processing (the Stranda brand of cured meats). Tourism and agriculture also play significant roles.
