Palghar district is a district in the Konkan division of Maharashtra state, India. It was created on 1 August 2014 by bifurcating the former Thane district, making it the 36th district of Maharashtra. Its headquarters is the town of Palghar.

Geography and demography

The district lies on the northern Konkan coast, bordered by the Arabian Sea to the west, Thane district to the south, Nashik district to the east, and Gujarat to the north. It includes the municipal cities of Vasai-Virar — part of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region — as well as largely rural and tribal talukas such as Jawhar, Mokhada, Vikramgad, and Wada. Adivasi communities, especially the Warli, form a large share of the population; Warli painting originates from this region. The district is noted for the hill station of Jawhar, the Keelve and Mahalakshmi temples, beaches such as Kelva and Arnala, and Tarapur, home to one of India's oldest nuclear power stations and a large industrial area (MIDC).

Transport

The Western Railway's Mumbai suburban line serves the district up to Dahanu Road, and National Highway 48 (Mumbai–Ahmedabad) passes through it. The proposed Mumbai–Ahmedabad bullet train corridor and the Vadhavan deep-water port project are located partly in the district.
