Prime Minister of Mozambique

The Prime Minister heads the government of Mozambique under the President.

The Prime Minister of Mozambique (Portuguese: Primeiro-Ministro de Moçambique) is the head of government of Mozambique. The prime minister is appointed by the President, coordinates the Council of Ministers, and is answerable to the Assembly of the Republic.

AppointerPresident of Mozambique
Formation17 July 1986
First holderMário da Graça Machungo
ResidenceMaputo

History

The post was created in July 1986 during the presidency of Samora Machel, replacing the earlier transitional arrangement in which the president directly headed the government. The first prime minister was Mário da Graça Machungo, previously governor of the Bank of Mozambique. During the civil war and the subsequent transition to multi-party democracy under the 1990 constitution, the office became established as the chief administrative post beneath the president.

List

  • Mário da Graça Machungo (1986–1994)
  • Pascoal Mocumbi (1994–2004)
  • Luísa Diogo (2004–2010) — first woman to hold the office
  • Aires Ali (2010–2012)
  • Alberto Vaquina (2012–2015)
  • Carlos Agostinho do Rosário (2015–2022)
  • Adriano Maleiane (2022–2025)
  • Maria Benvinda Levy (2025– )

Functions

The prime minister convenes and presides over the Council of Ministers in the president's absence, coordinates government departments, oversees implementation of the government programme approved by the Assembly of the Republic, and serves as a key channel between the executive and the legislature.
