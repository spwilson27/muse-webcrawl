Remora

The remoras, sometimes called suckerfish, are a family (Echeneidae) of ray-finned fish characterised by a suction disc on top of the head, formed from a modified dorsal fin, which allows them to adhere to larger marine animals such as sharks, rays, turtles and whales. There are eight species in three genera.

Remoras benefit from transport, protection and access to scraps of their host's meals and parasitic copepods, in a relationship usually described as commensalism. They range in length from about 30 to 110 cm and are found in warm waters worldwide.
