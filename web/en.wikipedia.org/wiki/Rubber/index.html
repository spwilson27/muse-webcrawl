Rubber is an elastic polymer — natural latex from rubber trees, or synthetic from petroleum — used in tyres, seals, hoses, gloves and countless products. Mesoamerican peoples made rubber balls millennia ago; Europeans encountered it in the Amazon.

Vulcanisation and boom

Charles Goodyear's vulcanisation (heating rubber with sulphur) made it stable and elastic in all weathers, enabling the bicycle and automobile booms — tyres for the bicycles and cars of this crawl. Plantation rubber in Southeast Asia, and synthetic rubber in wartime, built a global industry with a dark history of colonial exploitation in Congo and Amazonia.
