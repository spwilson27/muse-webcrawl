Historical cost

Historical cost is the original monetary value of an economic item. In accounting, assets are recorded at their historical cost — the amount of cash or cash equivalents paid to acquire them — under the historical cost principle.

Use in accounting

Historical cost is the traditional basis of measurement in financial reporting because it is objective and verifiable. However, it may diverge from current market value, particularly for long-lived assets, during inflation, or in volatile markets.

Alternatives

Modern accounting standards such as IFRS mix historical cost with fair value measurement for certain assets and liabilities, such as financial instruments.
