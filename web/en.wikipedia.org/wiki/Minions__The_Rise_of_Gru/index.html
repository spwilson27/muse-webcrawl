Minions: The Rise of Gru, also known as Minions 2, is a 2022 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. It is the sequel to Minions (2015) and the fifth entry in the Despicable Me franchise. Directed by Kyle Balda, the film stars the voices of Steve Carell as a young Gru and Pierre Coffin as the Minions.

Plot

In the 1970s, eleven-year-old Felonious Gru, an aspiring supervillain assisted by the Minions, applies to join the famed villain group the Vicious 6. When the group's leader, Wild Knuckles, is betrayed and cast out, Gru interviews for the vacancy but is dismissed as a child. Gru steals the group's coveted Zodiac Stone, sparking a chase in which the Minion Otto trades the stone for a pet rock. Gru befriends the disgraced Wild Knuckles, who becomes his mentor, and the Minions set out to rescue Gru and recover the stone before the Vicious 6 unleash the power of the Chinese zodiac.

Release and reception

Originally scheduled for 2020, the film was repeatedly delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic before premiering at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival in June 2022. It grossed over $939 million worldwide, becoming the fifth-highest-grossing film of 2022 and the highest-grossing animated film of that year. A viral trend, "Gentleminions", saw groups of young audience members attend screenings dressed in suits. The film received generally positive reviews for its humor, music and 1970s setting.
