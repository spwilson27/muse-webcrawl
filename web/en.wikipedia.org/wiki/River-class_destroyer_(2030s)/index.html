River-class destroyer (2030s)

The River-class destroyer is a planned class of warships for the Royal Canadian Navy under the Canadian Surface Combatant programme. Based on the British Type 26 frigate design (itself shared with Australia's Hunter class), the ships will replace both the Halifax-class frigates and the retired Iroquois-class destroyers.

Programme

Fifteen ships are planned. The first three were named in 2024 as HMCS Fraser, HMCS Saint-Laurent, and HMCS Mackenzie, reviving the name "River class" from earlier Canadian warships and taking the names of Canadian rivers. Construction of the first ship began in the mid-2020s, with delivery expected in the early 2030s. The class will be equipped for anti-submarine, anti-air, and anti-surface warfare, including the Aegis combat system and SPY-7 radar.
