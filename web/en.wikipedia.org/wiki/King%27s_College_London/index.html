King's College London (informally King's or KCL) is a public research university in London, England, founded by royal charter in 1829 under the patronage of King George IV and the Duke of Wellington. It is a founding member institution of the University of London.

History and research

King's is organised into nine faculties across five campuses. Its researchers have contributed to advances including the discovery of the structure of DNA, and the university counts numerous Nobel laureates among its alumni and staff.
