List of Indian flags

This is a list of flags used in India, including the national flag, military ensigns, flags of states and union territories in official or proposed use, and historical flags of the independence movement and earlier states.

National flag

The National Flag of India, the Tiranga ("Tricolour"), consists of horizontal bands of India saffron, white and India green, with the navy-blue Ashoka Chakra — a 24-spoke wheel — at the centre. It was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on 22 July 1947 and is governed by the Flag Code of India. Its design derives from the Swaraj flag of the Indian National Congress, designed by Pingali Venkayya, in which a spinning wheel (charkha) originally stood at the centre.

Military flags

The Indian Navy flies a white ensign bearing the national flag in the canton and the naval crest; its design was revised in 2022 to remove the Saint George's Cross. The Indian Army uses a red field with the national flag in the canton and the army badge in the fly, while the Indian Air Force ensign is sky blue with the national flag in the canton and the air force roundel. Each service and regiment also maintains its own colours and standards.

Historical flags

Precursors include the Calcutta flag of 1906, the flag hoisted by Bhikaiji Cama at Stuttgart in 1907, the Home Rule flag of 1917, and the Congress flags of 1921 and 1931. British India used the Red Ensign defaced with the Star of India, while princely states such as Hyderabad, Mysore, Travancore and Jaipur flew their own banners. The Mughal Empire's standard was the alam, and the Maratha Empire flew the triangular saffron Bhagwa flag.

State flags

Indian states do not generally have official flags under the Constitution; Jammu and Kashmir had an official state flag under its former special status until 2019. Karnataka has an unofficial red-and-yellow Kannada flag, and several states have proposed official designs.
