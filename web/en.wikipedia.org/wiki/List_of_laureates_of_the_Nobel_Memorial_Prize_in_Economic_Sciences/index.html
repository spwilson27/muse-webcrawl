List of laureates of the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences

The Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel, commonly called the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences, was established in 1968 by Sweden's central bank and first awarded in 1969. It is administered by the Nobel Foundation and awarded by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences. As of the mid-2020s, the prize has been awarded more than 55 times to over 90 laureates. Elinor Ostrom (2009) was the first woman laureate; Esther Duflo (2019) is the youngest person to receive it.
