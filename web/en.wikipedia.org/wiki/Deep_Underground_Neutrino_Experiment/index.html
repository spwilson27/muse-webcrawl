Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment

DUNE
LocationFermilab (beam) to SURF, South Dakota
Baseline1,300 km
DetectorLiquid argon time projection chambers

The Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment (DUNE) is an international neutrino experiment under construction, with a beam from Fermilab in Illinois to far detectors 1.5 km underground at the Sanford Underground Research Facility in Lead, South Dakota.

Physics goals

DUNE aims to measure neutrino oscillation over a 1,300 km baseline, determine the neutrino mass ordering, search for CP violation in the lepton sector, detect supernova neutrinos, and seek proton decay.

Detectors

The far detector comprises modules each holding about 17,000 tonnes of liquid argon in time-projection chambers; a near detector complex at Fermilab characterises the beam. It is the flagship of the US-hosted Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility.
