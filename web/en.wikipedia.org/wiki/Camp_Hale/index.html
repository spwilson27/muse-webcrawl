Camp Hale

Camp Hale was a U.S. Army training facility in the Colorado Rocky Mountains, between Red Cliff and Leadville in Eagle County. Built in 1942 at an elevation of about 9,200 feet, it was the training centre for the 10th Mountain Division during World War II.

History

Thousands of soldiers trained at Camp Hale in skiing, rock climbing, and winter survival, including many European refugees and American ski enthusiasts. The 10th Mountain Division deployed to Italy in 1945 and fought at Riva Ridge and Mount Belvedere. The camp was deactivated after the war, briefly reused for other training, and largely dismantled.

Legacy

Veterans of Camp Hale helped found much of the American ski industry, including Vail and Aspen Highlands. In 2022, the Camp Hale–Continental Divide National Monument was designated to protect the site and its surrounding peaks.
