Ibn Majah

Abū ʿAbdillāh Muḥammad ibn Yazīd Ibn Mājah al-Rabʿī al-Qazwīnī (Arabic: ابو عبد الله محمد بن يزيد بن ماجه الربعي القزويني; commonly known as Ibn Majah, 824 – 887 or 889 CE / 209 – 273 or 275 AH) was a medieval scholar of hadith and the author of the Sunan Ibn Mājah, one of the six canonical collections of hadith (Kutub al-Sittah) in Sunni Islam.

Biography

Ibn Majah was born in Qazwin, in present-day Iran, in 824 CE/209 AH. He travelled widely to gather hadith, visiting Iraq, Makkah, the Levant and Egypt. He studied under numerous scholars and in turn taught many students.

Works

His Sunan contains over 4,000 hadith in 32 books (kutub) divided into 1,500 chapters (abwāb). He also wrote a Tafsir (Qur'an exegesis) and a work of history, both largely lost.
