Allan McNish

Allan McNish (born 29 December 1969) is a British former racing driver from Scotland. A three-time winner of the 24 Hours of Le Mans (1998, 2008, 2013) and the 2013 FIA World Endurance Champion with Audi, he also raced one season in Formula One with Toyota in 2002.

Career

McNish rose through karting, Formula Ford and Formula 3 and tested for McLaren and Benetton in F1, but his breakthrough came in sports cars with Porsche, winning Le Mans in 1998. He joined Audi in 2000, winning the American Le Mans Series, and returned to Audi full-time after his F1 season and a year as Renault test driver.

With Tom Kristensen and others he won Le Mans again in 2008 and, with Kristensen and Loïc Duval, in 2013 — the year he also won the World Endurance Championship. He survived a spectacular crash at Le Mans in 2011 unhurt. He retired at the end of 2013 and later became team principal of the Audi Formula E team and a broadcaster and steward.
