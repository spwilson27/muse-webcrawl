Global Reporting Initiative

The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) is an independent international organisation that provides the world's most widely used standards for sustainability reporting. Founded in Boston in 1997 by CERES and the Tellus Institute, in partnership with the United Nations Environment Programme, it is headquartered in Amsterdam.

GRI Standards

The GRI Standards are a modular system of universal, sector and topic standards that organisations use to disclose their impacts on the economy, the environment and people. Topics covered include energy, emissions, water, waste, occupational health and safety, diversity and anti-corruption. The first guidelines (G1) appeared in 2000; the standards were restructured in 2016 and the Universal Standards were revised in 2021.

Adoption

Thousands of organisations in more than 100 countries report with GRI, and the standards are referenced in many stock-exchange and government reporting frameworks. GRI collaborates with bodies such as the IFRS Foundation's International Sustainability Standards Board to align disclosure requirements.
