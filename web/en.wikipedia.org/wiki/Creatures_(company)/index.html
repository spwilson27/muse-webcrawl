Creatures Inc. (Japanese: 株式会社クリーチャーズ) is a Japanese video game development company based in Tokyo. It is one of the three companies — alongside Nintendo and Game Freak — that jointly own the Pokémon franchise through The Pokémon Company, and it is best known for developing Pokémon-related software and for overseeing the Pokémon Trading Card Game.

History

The company was founded on 8 November 1995 by Tsunekazu Ishihara, succeeding the development team Ape Inc., which had created the Mother / EarthBound series with Shigesato Itoi. Creatures took over Ape's role in supporting Nintendo franchises and became a central partner in the launch of Pokémon in 1996, handling aspects of production, licensing and the card game.

Work

Creatures has developed or co-developed numerous Pokémon titles and tools, including the Pokémon Ranger spin-offs (with HAL Laboratory), PokéPark games, Detective Pikachu, and 3D modelling work for mainline Pokémon games. The company maintains the official Pokémon modelling library used across the franchise and produces the Pokémon Trading Card Game in Japan.
