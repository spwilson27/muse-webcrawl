Márta Károlyi

Márta Károlyi
Born: 29 August 1942, Budapest, Hungary
Occupation: Gymnastics coach
Spouse: Béla Károlyi
Known for: Coaching Romanian and US national teams

Márta Károlyi (born 1942) is a Hungarian-born gymnastics coach who, with her husband Béla Károlyi, trained the Romanian national team — including Nadia Comăneci, the first gymnast to score a perfect 10 at the Olympics — before defecting to the United States in 1981.

Career in the United States

The Károlyis built a famous training centre in Texas, and Márta became head coach and later national team coordinator of the US women's gymnastics program. Under her coordination, American teams won numerous World Championship and Olympic medals, including team gold at the 2012 and 2016 Olympics with the "Fierce Five" and "Final Five" squads.

Retirement and controversy

She retired after the 2016 Rio Olympics. Her tenure was later scrutinised in the fallout of the abuse scandal involving team doctor Larry Nassar; Márta Károlyi stated she was unaware of the abuse, and the Karolyi Ranch training centre closed in 2018.
