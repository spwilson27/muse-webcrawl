The British Standards Institution (BSI) is the national standards body of the United Kingdom, founded in 1901 as the first national standards organisation in the world. It produces British Standards (designated "BS"), represents the UK in the international standards organisations ISO and IEC and in the European bodies CEN and CENELEC, and — through the commercial BSI Group — provides testing, certification, training and supply-chain services in more than 190 countries.

History

BSI began as the Engineering Standards Committee, set up in London in 1901 by Sir John Wolfe Barry and others to standardise the sizes of iron and steel sections, reducing the variety of tramway rails and structural sections that manufacturers had to stock. It received a Royal Charter in 1929 and adopted its present name in 1931. The organisation's Kitemark — a mark of quality and safety certification — has its origins in a trade mark registered in 1903 and became one of Britain's best-known consumer symbols. During both world wars, standardisation work supported munitions and civil defence production, and BSI's wartime "utility" standards are credited with saving scarce materials.

Standards and certification

British Standards are developed by committees of industry, consumers, government and experts; many are adoptions of European (EN) or international (ISO) standards with a BS prefix. Widely used BSI-originated management system standards, such as BS 5750 (the ancestor of ISO 9001 quality management), BS 7799 (information security, ancestor of ISO/IEC 27001), BS 25999 (business continuity) and BS 8901 (sustainable event management), have been adopted internationally. BSI certifies products and management systems, awards the Kitemark for independently tested products, and provides regulatory services including medical device approvals.

Structure

The institution is a non-profit distributing organisation incorporated by Royal Charter; its commercial subsidiaries provide assessment and knowledge services, and profits are reinvested in standards work. Its registered office is at Chiswick in west London.
