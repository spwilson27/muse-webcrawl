Cultural identity

Cultural identity is the identity or feeling of belonging to a group. It is part of a person's self-conception and self-perception and is related to nationality, ethnicity, religion, social class, generation, locality or any kind of social group that has its own distinct culture.

Components

Language, dress, food, rituals, values and shared history contribute to cultural identity. It is learned through socialisation and is dynamic: migration, globalisation and intermarriage can produce hybrid or multiple identities.

Study

Cultural studies, anthropology and social psychology examine how identity is formed, negotiated and sometimes politicised. Theorists such as Stuart Hall described identity as a production, always in process, rather than a fixed essence.
