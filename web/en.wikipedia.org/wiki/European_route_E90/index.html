European route E90

European route E90 is an A-Class West-East European route, extending from Lisbon in Portugal in the west to the Turkish–Iraqi border in the east. It passes through Spain, crosses the Mediterranean by ferry to Italy, traverses Sicily, and continues by ferry to mainland Greece before crossing Turkey.

Route

Key points along the route include Lisbon, Madrid, Barcelona, Mazara del Vallo and Palermo in Sicily, Messina, Reggio Calabria, Taranto, Brindisi, Igoumenitsa, Ioannina, Thessaloniki, Alexandroupoli, Istanbul, Ankara, Adana, Gaziantep, Şanlıurfa, Nusaybin, and Silopi near the Iraqi border.
