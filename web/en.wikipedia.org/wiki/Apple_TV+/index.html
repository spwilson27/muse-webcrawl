Apple TV+ is an American subscription streaming service owned by Apple Inc. It launched on 1 November 2019 as part of Apple's expansion into services, offering a catalogue consisting almost entirely of original programming — series, films, documentaries, and children's content — marketed under the name Apple Originals. The service is accessed through the Apple TV app on Apple devices and many smart TVs, streaming sticks, and game consoles.

Notable programming

Early flagship series included The Morning Show, For All Mankind, See, and Dickinson. The comedy-drama Ted Lasso (2020–2023) became a major critical success, and the film CODA won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 2022, making Apple TV+ the first streaming service to win that award. Other acclaimed titles include Severance, Slow Horses, Foundation, and Killers of the Flower Moon.

Business model

Unlike competitors with large licensed libraries, Apple TV+ has pursued a smaller, curated catalogue funded by Apple's hardware profits, and it is bundled with Apple One subscriptions and free trials on new devices. In 2025, Apple rebranded the service as simply Apple TV, aligning its name with the app and hardware.
