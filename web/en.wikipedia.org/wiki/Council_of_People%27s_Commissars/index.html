Council of People's Commissars

The Council of People's Commissars (Russian: Совет народных комиссаров, Sovnarkom) was the highest executive authority of Soviet Russia and later of the Soviet Union from 1917 until 1946, when it was renamed the Council of Ministers.

History

Established by the Second All-Russian Congress of Soviets during the October Revolution, the first Sovnarkom was chaired by Vladimir Lenin and included People's Commissars for foreign affairs (Leon Trotsky), nationalities (Joseph Stalin) and other portfolios. Its decrees on peace and land were among the first acts of the Bolshevik government.
