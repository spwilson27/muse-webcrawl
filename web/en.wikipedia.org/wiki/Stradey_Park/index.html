Stradey Park

Stradey Park (Welsh: Parc y Strade) was a rugby union stadium in Llanelli, Carmarthenshire, Wales. It was the home ground of Llanelli RFC and, from their creation in 2003 until 2008, of the Scarlets regional team. Stradey Park is remembered as one of the most storied grounds in Welsh rugby.

History

Rugby was first played at the ground in the late 19th century, and Llanelli RFC made it their home for well over a century. The stadium witnessed Llanelli's famous 9–3 victory over the touring New Zealand All Blacks on 31 October 1972 — a result celebrated in Welsh rugby folklore and in the song "Sosban Fach" tradition associated with the club. The ground's capacity in its later years was around 10,800.

Closure

In November 2008 the Scarlets and Llanelli RFC moved to the new Parc y Scarlets stadium in Pemberton. The final match at Stradey Park was played in October 2008. The site was subsequently redeveloped for housing, with elements of the ground commemorated in the new development.
