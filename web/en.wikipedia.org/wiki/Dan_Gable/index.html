Danny Mack Gable (born 25 October 1948) is an American former folkstyle and freestyle wrestler and coach. He is regarded as one of the greatest wrestlers of all time, having won a gold medal at the 1972 Summer Olympics without conceding a single point.

Wrestling career

At Iowa State University, Gable compiled a 117–1 record; his only collegiate loss came in his final match, an upset by Larry Owings in the 1970 NCAA finals. He won the 1971 World Championships, the 1971 Pan American Games, and the 1972 Munich Olympic gold medal in the 68 kg freestyle class, outscoring opponents through the tournament without giving up a point.

Coaching

As head coach at the University of Iowa from 1976 to 1997, Gable's Hawkeyes won 15 NCAA team titles, including nine in a row, and he coached dozens of individual national champions and Olympians. His training intensity became legendary in American wrestling culture.

Honours

Gable is a member of multiple halls of fame, and in December 2020 he received the Presidential Medal of Freedom.
