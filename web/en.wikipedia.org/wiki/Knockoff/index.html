Knockoff

A knockoff is a copy or imitation of a product, typically produced to resemble a branded, trademarked, or copyrighted original as closely as possible. Unlike counterfeit goods, which are sold as if they were the genuine article, knockoffs are often sold under a different, similar-sounding brand name or with slight alterations.

Characteristics

Knockoffs are common in fashion, consumer electronics, toys, and watches. They may trade on the reputation and design of the original while avoiding direct infringement of trademarks, occupying a legal grey area that varies by jurisdiction. Design patents and trade dress law are sometimes invoked against them.

Related concepts

Knockoffs differ from counterfeits, which are fraudulent replicas passed off as genuine, and from generic or store-brand products, which do not imitate a specific branded design.
