War in Donbas (2014–2022)

War in Donbas
Date6 April 2014 – 24 February 2022
LocationDonbas, Ukraine
ResultEscalation into the full-scale Russian invasion of Ukraine

The War in Donbas was an armed conflict in the Donbas region of eastern Ukraine, part of the wider Russo-Ukrainian War. It began in April 2014 when Russian-backed separatists seized government buildings in Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts and proclaimed the Donetsk People's Republic (DPR) and Luhansk People's Republic (LPR), following Russia's annexation of Crimea.

Course of the war

Ukraine launched an "Anti-Terrorist Operation" to retake the territory. Fighting intensified over the summer of 2014; Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 was shot down over the conflict zone on 17 July 2014, killing all 298 aboard. Regular Russian forces intervened decisively at Ilovaisk in August 2014 and at Debaltseve in early 2015, inflicting heavy Ukrainian defeats.

Minsk agreements and stalemate

The Minsk Protocol (September 2014) and Minsk II (February 2015) established ceasefires that were never fully implemented. The front line froze into trench warfare, with shelling and sniper fire continuing for years. Roughly 14,000 people were killed between 2014 and early 2022, and over a million were displaced.

Escalation

In February 2022 Russia formally recognised the DPR and LPR, and on 24 February 2022 launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine, subsuming the Donbas conflict into a much larger war.
