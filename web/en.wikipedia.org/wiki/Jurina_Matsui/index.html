Jurina Matsui

Personal information
Born: 8 March 1997, Kasugai, Aichi, Japan
Occupation: Singer, actress, former idol
Years active: 2008–present
Former groups: SKE48, AKB48

Jurina Matsui (born March 8, 1997) is a Japanese singer and actress, best known as a former member of the idol groups SKE48 and AKB48. She joined SKE48 in 2008 as a first-generation member at the age of eleven and quickly became one of the group's central figures.

Career

Matsui was a leading member of SKE48's Team S and held a concurrent position in AKB48, appearing on dozens of singles for both groups. In AKB48's annual general elections she consistently ranked near the top, and in 2018 she won first place, becoming the centre of the single "Sentimental Train". In 2018 she also competed on the South Korean survival show Produce 48 before withdrawing for health reasons. She graduated from SKE48 in April 2021 with a concert at Nippon Gaishi Hall, ending a thirteen-year idol career, and has since worked as an actress and television personality.
