European External Action Service

European External Action Service
Abbreviation:EEAS
Formation:1 January 2011 (launched 1 December 2010)
Headquarters:Brussels, Belgium
Head:High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy

The European External Action Service (EEAS) is the diplomatic service of the European Union. Established by the Treaty of Lisbon and formally launched in December 2010, it helps the EU's High Representative carry out the Union's Common Foreign and Security Policy and manages the EU's diplomatic delegations around the world.

Organisation

The service combines officials from the Council of the European Union, the European Commission, and the diplomatic services of the member states. At headquarters in Brussels it is organised into geographic and thematic directorates, together with crisis management and intelligence structures. Abroad, the EU maintains delegations in most countries and international organisations, representing the Union as a whole since the Lisbon Treaty.

Role

The EEAS supports the High Representative, who also serves as a Vice-President of the European Commission, in conducting foreign policy, chairing the Foreign Affairs Council, and coordinating the EU's external action, including development, trade, enlargement, and the Common Security and Defence Policy.
