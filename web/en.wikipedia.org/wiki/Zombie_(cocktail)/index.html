Zombie (cocktail)

The Zombie is a tiki cocktail made of fruit juices, liqueurs, and various rums. It first appeared in late 1934, invented by Donn Beach at his Don the Beachcomber restaurant in Hollywood, California. It was popularized on the East Coast soon afterwards at the 1939 New York World's Fair.

History

Legend has it that Donn Beach originally concocted the Zombie to help a hung-over customer get through a business meeting. The customer returned several days later to complain that he had been turned into a zombie for his entire trip. Its smooth, fruity taste conceals its extremely high alcohol content, which is why Don the Beachcomber restaurants limited their customers to two Zombies apiece.

Composition

According to the original recipe, the Zombie cocktail included three different kinds of rum, lime juice, falernum, Angostura bitters, Pernod, grenadine, and "Don's Mix", a combination of cinnamon syrup and grapefruit juice. Beach was very cautious with the recipes of his original cocktails; his instructions for his bartenders contained coded references to ingredients, the contents of which were only known to him.

In popular culture

The Zombie was one of the signature drinks of the mid-20th-century tiki bar craze and remains a staple of tiki menus. Imitations and loose interpretations abound, but the secretive original recipe was only reconstructed by tiki historians such as Jeff "Beachbum" Berry from Beach's personal notes.
