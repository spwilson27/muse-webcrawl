RAINN (the Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network) is the largest anti-sexual-violence organisation in the United States. Founded in 1994 by Scott Berkowitz, it operates the National Sexual Assault Hotline and the National Sexual Assault Online Hotline, and works on public policy, prevention education and services for survivors.

Hotlines

The National Sexual Assault Hotline (1-800-656-HOPE, established in 1994) routes callers to their nearest local sexual assault service provider among a network of more than a thousand local affiliates; it was the first decentralised national hotline of its kind. The online hotline, launched in 2006, offers confidential one-to-one chat support. RAINN also operates the Department of Defense Safe Helpline for members of the US military community.

Programs and advocacy

Beyond direct services, RAINN consults on prevention and response programs for colleges, companies and entertainment productions; maintains public statistics on sexual violence; and advocates legislation concerning rape-kit backlogs, statutes of limitations and survivor protections. Singer-songwriter Tori Amos, a survivor herself, was the organisation's first national spokesperson and has long been associated with its fundraising.
