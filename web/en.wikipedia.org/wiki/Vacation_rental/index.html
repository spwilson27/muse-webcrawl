Vacation rental

A vacation rental is the renting out of a furnished apartment, house, or professionally managed resort condominium complex on a temporary basis to tourists as an alternative to a hotel. Vacation rentals have existed for decades in Europe and popular resort areas, but the market expanded dramatically with the rise of online booking platforms in the 2000s and 2010s.

Market

Online marketplaces such as Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com connect property owners and managers with travellers. Rentals range from spare rooms to entire villas and may include kitchens, living space, and amenities not typical of hotel rooms. The sector is sometimes called the short-term rental or holiday let market.

Regulation and controversy

Cities around the world have introduced regulations on short-term rentals, citing effects on housing affordability, neighbourhood character, and the hotel industry. Rules range from registration and night caps to outright bans in some districts. Hosts and platforms dispute the scale of these effects, and legal battles over regulation have occurred in cities including New York, Barcelona, Amsterdam, and Paris.
