Ultium

Developer: General Motors
Announced: 4 March 2020
Type: Electric-vehicle battery and platform system
Partners: LG Energy Solution (Ultium Cells LLC)

Ultium is a family of modular electric-vehicle batteries, motors and platforms developed by General Motors (GM). Unveiled in March 2020, it was designed to underpin a wide range of GM electric vehicles, from compact cars to full-size trucks and SUVs, across the Chevrolet, GMC, Cadillac, Buick and BrightDrop brands.

Technology

The system uses large-format pouch cells that can be stacked vertically or horizontally within a battery pack, allowing capacities from about 50 to 200 kWh. GM's first-generation Ultium cells use a nickel-cobalt-manganese-aluminium (NCMA) chemistry intended to reduce cobalt content. The architecture supports 400-volt packs with fast charging, and 800-volt packs in large trucks, and is paired with a family of modular drive units.

Production and vehicles

Cells are manufactured by Ultium Cells LLC, a joint venture between GM and LG Energy Solution, with plants in Lordstown, Ohio, and Spring Hill, Tennessee. Vehicles built on the platform include the GMC Hummer EV, Cadillac Lyriq, Chevrolet Blazer EV, Equinox EV and Silverado EV. In 2023 GM announced that the Ultium name would also apply as it transitioned future vehicles to the North American Charging Standard.
