Mount Vernon Hospital is a specialist hospital in Northwood, in the London Borough of Hillingdon, England. It is best known for the Mount Vernon Cancer Centre, one of the country's leading cancer treatment and research hospitals, and for its long history of plastic surgery and burns care. It is managed by the Hillingdon Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, with the cancer centre operated by the East and North Hertfordshire NHS Trust.

History

The hospital was founded in 1860 in Hampstead as the North London Hospital for Consumption and Diseases of the Chest, treating tuberculosis patients. Seeking cleaner country air, it moved to its present Northwood site in 1904. During the Second World War, pioneering plastic surgeon Sir Harold Gillies treated wounded servicemen there, and the hospital became a centre for reconstructive surgery.

Services

The Mount Vernon Cancer Centre provides radiotherapy and systemic therapies to a population of about two million across Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire, northwest London and beyond, and hosts research supported by Cancer Research UK.
