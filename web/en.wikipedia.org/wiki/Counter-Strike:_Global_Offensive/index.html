Counter-Strike: Global Offensive

Developer: Valve; Hidden Path Entertainment
Publisher: Valve
Release: 21 August 2012
Genre: Tactical first-person shooter
Engine: Source

Counter-Strike: Global Offensive (CS:GO) is a 2012 multiplayer tactical first-person shooter developed by Valve and Hidden Path Entertainment. It is the fourth game in the Counter-Strike series. Two opposing teams, the Terrorists and the Counter-Terrorists, compete in game modes to complete objectives, such as planting or defusing a bomb or rescuing hostages, across round-based matches.

Gameplay

In the standard competitive mode, two teams of five play a maximum of 30 rounds, switching sides at half-time; the first team to win 16 rounds wins the map. Players earn in-round money for actions such as eliminating opponents or planting the bomb, and spend it on weapons and equipment in subsequent rounds, making economy management a key strategic layer. Game modes include Competitive, Casual, Deathmatch, Wingman, and the battle-royale mode Danger Zone (added 2018).

Esports

CS:GO became one of the most prominent esports titles, with Valve-sponsored Major Championships drawing large audiences and prize pools; legendary teams and players such as Astralis, s1mple, and GeT_RiGhT defined its competitive era. A cosmetic skin economy for weapons grew around the game through the Steam Community Market.

Counter-Strike 2

In September 2023, Valve released Counter-Strike 2, built on the Source 2 engine, as a free upgrade that replaced CS:GO on Steam, retaining players' inventories and continuing the professional scene.
