The University of Connecticut (UConn) is a public land-grant research university in Storrs, Connecticut, about 28 miles east of Hartford. It was founded in 1881 as the Storrs Agricultural College and is the flagship institution of the Connecticut state university system.

History

Brothers Charles and Augustus Storrs donated land and funds to establish an agricultural school in 1881. The institution became Connecticut Agricultural College, then Connecticut State College in 1933, and the University of Connecticut in 1939. The UConn 2000 and Next Generation Connecticut programs invested heavily in facilities in the 1990s–2010s. The university also operates regional campuses at Avery Point, Hartford, Stamford, and Waterbury, plus the UConn Health medical center in Farmington.

Athletics

The UConn Huskies are nationally renowned for basketball: the women's program under Geno Auriemma is the most successful in the sport's history, and the men's program has won multiple national championships, including back-to-back titles in 2023 and 2024. Teams play at Gampel Pavilion in Storrs and the XL Center in Hartford.
