Under the Tuscan Sun

Under the Tuscan Sun is a 1996 memoir by American writer Frances Mayes, recounting her purchase and restoration of an abandoned villa, Bramasole, in Cortona, Tuscany. The book combines travel writing, renovation narrative, recipes, and reflections on Italian rural life, and became a bestseller credited with popularizing Tuscany among American tourists.

Film adaptation

The memoir was adapted into a 2003 romantic drama film of the same name, written and directed by Audrey Wells and starring Diane Lane as Frances. The film departs substantially from the book: its Frances is a recently divorced San Francisco writer who buys the villa impulsively on a tour, and the story centers on her romantic renewal. Lane received a Golden Globe nomination for her performance. The film was shot on location in Cortona, Montepulciano, Positano, Florence, and Rome.

Legacy

Mayes wrote several follow-up volumes, including Bella Tuscany and Every Day in Tuscany. The success of the book and film contributed to a wave of foreign buyers restoring Tuscan farmhouses, and Bramasole remains a pilgrimage site for readers.
