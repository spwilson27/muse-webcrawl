Makuuchi

Makuuchi (幕内), or makunouchi, is the top division of the six divisions of professional sumo wrestling in Japan. Its name literally means "inside the curtain," referring to the curtain that historically separated the seating of the highest-ranked wrestlers. The division is fixed at 42 wrestlers.

Ranks within makuuchi

The makuuchi division contains the sport's senior ranks. At the apex is the yokozuna (grand champion), followed by ōzeki, then the junior san'yaku ranks of sekiwake and komusubi. Below them are the maegashira, numbered from #1 east and west downward. Wrestlers in makuuchi are salaried sekitori, wear their hair in the full oichomage topknot, and perform the dohyo-iri ring-entering ceremony wearing ornate keshō-mawashi aprons.

Tournament play

In each of the six annual grand tournaments (honbasho), makuuchi wrestlers fight once a day for 15 days. Promotions and demotions between makuuchi and the second division, jūryō, depend on win-loss records. The wrestler with the best record wins the Emperor's Cup as top-division champion (yūshō), and outstanding performances can earn special prizes for technique, fighting spirit, and defeating yokozuna.
