Sofia Gubaidulina

Sofia Gubaidulina
Born: 24 October 1931, Chistopol, Tatar ASSR, Soviet Union
Died: 13 March 2025
Occupation: Composer

Sofia Asgatovna Gubaidulina (1931–2025) was a Soviet and Russian composer regarded as one of the leading composers of the late 20th and early 21st centuries. Of Tatar and Russian heritage, she studied in Kazan and Moscow, where she was encouraged by Dmitri Shostakovich.

Music

Gubaidulina's work is marked by deep spirituality, drawn from Russian Orthodox, Catholic, and other traditions, and by exploratory use of instruments and tuning systems. Major works include the violin concerto Offertorium (1980), written for Gidon Kremer, the symphony Stimmen... Verstummen..., and the St John Passion (2000).

Life

Her music was long marginalised by Soviet officialdom; after the USSR's collapse she settled in Germany in 1992. She received numerous honours, including the Praemium Imperiale and the Léonie Sonning Music Prize.
