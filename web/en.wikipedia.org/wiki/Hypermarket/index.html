Hypermarket
Concept byFred DeLuca / Carrefour era, 1960s

A hypermarket is a very large retail store combining a supermarket and a department store under one roof, offering groceries plus general merchandise such as clothing, electronics and household goods, typically in a single-story warehouse-style building with extensive parking.

History

Carrefour opened the first European hypermarket near Paris in 1963. In the United States, Meijer pioneered the format in 1962, followed by Walmart Supercenters and Target. Formats spread widely in Europe, Asia and Latin America.

Characteristics

Hypermarkets typically exceed 5,000 m², stock tens of thousands of lines, and compete on price and one-stop shopping, often anchoring suburban retail parks. See Grocery store.
