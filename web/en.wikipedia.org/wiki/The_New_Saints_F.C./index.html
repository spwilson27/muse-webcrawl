The New Saints F.C.

Founded: 1959 (as Llansantffraid F.C.)
Ground: Park Hall, Oswestry, England
League: Cymru Premier
Nickname: The Saints

The New Saints of Oswestry Town & Llansantffraid Football Club (Welsh: Clwb Pêl-droed y Seintiau Newydd), commonly known as The New Saints or TNS, is a professional football club that plays in the Cymru Premier, the top flight of Welsh football, but is based at Park Hall in Oswestry, Shropshire, England, near the Welsh border. It is the most successful club in Welsh domestic football, with a record number of league titles.

History

The club was founded in 1959 as Llansantffraid F.C., representing the small village of Llansantffraid-ym-Mechain in Powys. After winning promotion to the League of Wales, the club gained sponsorship from Total Network Solutions in 1997 and became Total Network Solutions Llansantffraid, famously winning the 2005 Welsh Cup and playing Liverpool in the UEFA Champions League qualifiers. In 2003 it merged with Oswestry Town, a historic English club (founded 1860) that had long played in Welsh leagues, and moved to Oswestry. In 2006, when Total Network Solutions was taken over, the club was renamed The New Saints, retaining the "TNS" initials and referencing both the saints of Llansantffraid and Oswestry's St Oswald.

Dominance and records

TNS became the first full-time professional club in the Welsh league system and has dominated the Cymru Premier, winning many consecutive titles. In the 2016–17 season the club won 27 consecutive matches in all competitions, breaking the world record of 26 held by Johan Cruyff's Ajax of 1971–72. In 2024 TNS made history by reaching the league phase of the UEFA Conference League, the first Welsh league club to reach the group stage of a major European competition.
