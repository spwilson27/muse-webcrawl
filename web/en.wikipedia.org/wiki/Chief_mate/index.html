Chief mate

A chief mate (also chief officer, first mate, or first officer) is a licensed mariner and head of the deck department of a merchant ship. The chief mate is second in command after the master (captain) and assumes command if the master is incapacitated.

Duties

The chief mate is responsible for cargo operations, including stowage plans and the loading, discharging, and care of cargo; the maintenance of the ship's hull, deck equipment, and safety and firefighting appliances; and the supervision of the deck crew, including the second and third mates, bosun, and able seamen. The chief mate typically stands the 4-to-8 watches at sea.

Certification is governed internationally by the STCW Convention, which sets competency standards for officers in charge of a navigational watch and for chief mates on ships of 500 gross tonnage or more.
