Zootopia (titled Zootropolis in some European countries) is a 2016 American animated buddy cop film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by Byron Howard and Rich Moore from a screenplay by Jared Bush and Phil Johnston, it stars the voices of Ginnifer Goodwin and Jason Bateman. The film follows rabbit police officer Judy Hopps and con artist fox Nick Wilde as they uncover a conspiracy in a city populated by anthropomorphic animals.

Judy Hopps, the first rabbit police officer, and Nick Wilde, a red fox con artist, in downtown Zootopia.

Plot

Judy Hopps, an optimistic rabbit from rural Bunnyburrow, fulfils her childhood dream of becoming the first rabbit officer of the Zootopia Police Department. Assigned parking duty by Chief Bogo, she blackmails small-time con artist Nick Wilde into helping her solve the disappearance of a missing otter. Their investigation uncovers a plot in which predators are reverting to a savage state after being shot with darts made from toxic flowers known as "night howlers". Judy exposes Assistant Mayor Bellwether as the mastermind behind the scheme, which was designed to stoke fear of predators and seize power; Judy and Nick reconcile after Judy publicly acknowledges her own prejudices, and Nick joins the police force as her partner.

Production and themes

The film was animated using Disney's Hyperion renderer and features distinct city districts designed for different climates, including Sahara Square, Tundratown and a rainforest district. Its story explores prejudice, stereotyping and implicit bias through the dynamic between prey and predator animals, themes critics noted were unusually direct for a family film. Shakira voices the pop star Gazelle and performs the theme song "Try Everything".

Reception

Zootopia received widespread critical acclaim for its animation, voice acting, humour and social commentary, and grossed over $1 billion worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing animated films of all time. It won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature at the 89th Academy Awards, as well as the Golden Globe and Annie Award in the same category. A television spin-off series, Zootopia+, premiered on Disney+ in 2022, and a theatrical sequel, Zootopia 2, followed in 2025.
