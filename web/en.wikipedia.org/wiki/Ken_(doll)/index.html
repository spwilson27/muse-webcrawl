Ken is a fashion doll introduced by American toy company Mattel in 1961 as the counterpart of Barbie, who had been introduced two years earlier. Ken's full name is Kenneth Sean Carson. Like Barbie, Ken is from the fictional town of Willows, Wisconsin, and he has been Barbie's boyfriend for most of the brand's history.

History

Ken debuted on March 11, 1961, at the American International Toy Fair in New York City. The first Ken doll stood 12 inches tall, had moulded plastic hair in blond or brunet "flocked" styles, and wore red swimming trunks with a beach towel and sandals. He was named after Kenneth Handler, the son of Mattel founders Ruth and Elliot Handler.

In February 2004, Mattel announced that Barbie and Ken had "split up" after 43 years, a publicity move that coincided with the introduction of an Australian surfer character, Blaine. The couple were officially reunited on Valentine's Day 2011. Over the decades Ken has been sold in numerous versions reflecting changing fashions, careers and body types; a notable controversy was the 1993 "Earring Magic Ken", which became a bestseller and a cult item. In 2017 Mattel introduced Kens with new body types, skin tones and hairstyles, including a man bun.

In film

Ken is voiced by Michael Keaton in Toy Story 3 (2010), where he appears as a flamboyant fashion enthusiast. In the 2023 live-action film Barbie, Ken is played by Ryan Gosling (with other Kens played by Simu Liu, Kingsley Ben-Adir, Ncuti Gatwa and Scott Evans); Gosling's performance, centred on Ken's search for identity beyond being "just Ken", was widely praised and earned an Academy Award nomination.
