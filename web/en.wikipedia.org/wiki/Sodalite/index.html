Sodalite

Sodalite
Category: Tectosilicate mineral
Colour: Royal blue, also grey, white, pink
Hardness: 5.5–6 (Mohs)

Sodalite is a rich royal-blue tectosilicate mineral widely used as an ornamental gemstone. A member of the sodalite group, its chemical formula is Na8(Al6Si6O24)Cl2. Although massive sodalite is opaque, crystals are usually transparent to translucent.

Occurrence

Sodalite occurs in igneous rocks such as nepheline syenites. Notable localities include Bancroft, Ontario in Canada, Brazil, Greenland, Russia, and Myanmar. The related mineral hackmanite shows tenebrescence, changing colour in sunlight.

Uses

Massive sodalite is carved into beads, figurines, and decorative objects and is sometimes confused with lapis lazuli, from which it differs by its lack of golden pyrite flecks and its more violet-blue hue.
