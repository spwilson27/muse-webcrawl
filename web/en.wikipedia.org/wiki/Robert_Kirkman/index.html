Robert Kirkman

Robert Kirkman (born November 30, 1978) is an American comic book writer, screenwriter, and producer best known for creating The Walking Dead and Invincible for Image Comics, and for co-creating the Marvel series Marvel Zombies. He is a partner at Image Comics and the founder of the imprint Skybound Entertainment.

Career

Kirkman's early work included the self-published Battle Pope (2000) with artist Tony Moore. In 2003 he launched Invincible with Cory Walker and The Walking Dead with Tony Moore (soon succeeded by Charlie Adlard) at Image Comics. The Walking Dead ran for 193 issues until 2019 and was adapted by AMC into one of the most successful television series of the 2010s, on which Kirkman served as executive producer.

Skybound

In 2010 Kirkman founded Skybound Entertainment to develop his properties across media, including the horror series Outcast, the animated adaptation of Invincible for Amazon Prime Video (premiering 2021), and the science-fiction comic Oblivion Song. In 2018 he took over writing duties on The Walking Dead creator-owned expansions and became chairman of Skybound.
