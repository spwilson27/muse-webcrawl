Bell Centre

The Bell Centre (French: Centre Bell), formerly known as the Centre Molson, is a multipurpose arena located in downtown Montreal, Quebec, Canada. It is the home of the Montreal Canadiens of the National Hockey League and, by hockey seating capacity, has long been one of the largest arenas in the NHL.

History

Construction began in 1993, and the arena opened on 16 March 1996, when the Canadiens hosted the New York Rangers, replacing the storied Montreal Forum as the team's home. The building was initially named for the Molson brewing family, owners of the club; the naming rights passed to Bell Canada in 2002, and the arena took its present name.

Events

Beyond Canadiens hockey, the Bell Centre hosts major concerts, mixed martial arts cards, figure skating and other sporting events. It was a principal venue for the 2009 IIHF World Junior Championship and has hosted multiple UFC events.

Location

The arena stands beside the Lucien-L'Allier commuter rail and metro station and is connected to Montreal's underground city, making it one of the most transit-accessible sports venues in Canada.
