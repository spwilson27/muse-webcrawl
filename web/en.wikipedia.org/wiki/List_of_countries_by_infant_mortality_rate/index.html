Trends

Global infant mortality has fallen dramatically: from about 65 per 1,000 in 1990 to under 30 per 1,000 in the 2020s, driven by immunisation, oral rehydration therapy, improved nutrition, clean water and access to skilled birth attendants. Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia still account for the great majority of infant deaths, most from preventable causes such as preterm birth complications, pneumonia, diarrhoea and malaria.
