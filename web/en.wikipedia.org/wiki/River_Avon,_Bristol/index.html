River Avon, Bristol

The River Avon is a river in the south west of England, flowing through Wiltshire, Somerset, and Bristol to join the Severn Estuary at Avonmouth. It is 120 km (75 mi) long and is sometimes called the Bristol Avon to distinguish it from other rivers of the same name.

Course

The Avon rises in Gloucestershire and passes through Chippenham, Melksham, Bradford-on-Avon, Bath, and Bristol. At Bristol the tidal river flows through the Avon Gorge, spanned by the Clifton Suspension Bridge, and the city's Floating Harbour was created by diverting the river along the New Cut in the early 19th century.

Navigation

The river is navigable from Avonmouth to Bath, where it joins the Kennet and Avon Canal, providing a through route to the River Thames.
