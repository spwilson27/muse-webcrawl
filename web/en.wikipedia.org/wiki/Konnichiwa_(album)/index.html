Konnichiwa is the fourth studio album by British grime artist Skepta. It was released on 6 May 2016 by Boy Better Know. The album is widely credited with bringing grime to a global audience and won the 2016 Mercury Prize, beating albums by David Bowie and Radiohead.

Background and release

Skepta, a Tottenham MC and co-founder of the Boy Better Know collective, developed the album over several years following his earlier records Greatest Hits (2007), Microphone Champion (2009) and Doin' It Again (2011). The single "That's Not Me" (2014) marked his return to raw grime production and reached the UK top 40, followed by the breakout hit "Shutdown" (2015), which became an anthem of the genre's resurgence.

Reception

The album debuted at number two on the UK Albums Chart and was certified gold in the UK. Critics praised its uncompromising sound and independence — it was released on Skepta's own label. Its Mercury Prize win in September 2016 was seen as a landmark moment for grime music.
