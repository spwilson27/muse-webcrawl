Breakdancing

Breakdancing / Breaking
Origin: The Bronx, New York, 1970s
Culture: Hip hop
Olympic debut: Paris 2024

Breakdancing, also called breaking or b-boying/b-girling, is a dynamic street dance style that originated among African American and Puerto Rican youth in the Bronx, New York City, in the 1970s as one of the elements of hip hop culture, pioneered at DJ Kool Herc's parties.

Elements

Four core elements: toprock (standing steps), downrock (floor footwork), power moves (acrobatic spins such as windmills and headspins), and freezes (stylised poses on hands/elbows/head). Dancers (breakers) battle in cyphers and crew competitions such as Battle of the Year and Red Bull BC One.

Olympics

Breaking debuted as an Olympic sport at the Paris 2024 Games, with B-Girl Ami (Japan) and B-Boy Phil Wizard (Canada) winning gold. It was not retained for Los Angeles 2028. See Hip hop dance.
