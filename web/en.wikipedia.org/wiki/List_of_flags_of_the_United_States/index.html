List of flags of the United States

This list collects the flags associated with the United States: the national flag, the flags of the fifty states, federal district and territorial flags, military flags and historical flags.

National flag

The flag of the United States consists of thirteen horizontal red and white stripes representing the original colonies and a blue canton bearing fifty white stars for the fifty states. The current fifty-star design, by Robert G. Heft, was adopted on 4 July 1960 following the admission of Hawaii, and is the longest-serving design in American history.

State flags

Every US state has its own flag. Most were adopted in the late 19th and 20th centuries; many feature the state seal on a blue field, while others — such as those of Texas, California, New Mexico, Colorado and Arizona — use distinctive designs. Several states, including Minnesota (2024), Utah (2024), Mississippi (2021) and Georgia (2003), have redesigned their flags in recent decades.

Territorial and district flags

  • District of Columbia — three red stars above two red bars, based on George Washington's family arms.
  • Puerto Rico — red and white stripes with a blue triangle and single star (1895 design).
  • Guam — blue field with a red-bordered almond shape enclosing a beach scene and the word GUAM.
  • U.S. Virgin Islands — white field with a stylised eagle between the letters V and I.
  • American Samoa — eagle grasping traditional symbols on a red-bordered blue and white field.
  • Northern Mariana Islands — blue field with a latte stone, star and decorative wreath.

Historical flags

  • Betsy Ross flag — thirteen stars in a circle (legendary attribution).
  • Gadsden flag — yellow field, coiled rattlesnake, "Don't tread on me".
  • Grand Union flag — thirteen stripes with the British Union Jack in the canton (1775–1777).
  • Bennington flag — blue field with an arc of seven-pointed stars.
  • Star-Spangled Banner — the fifteen-star, fifteen-stripe flag that inspired the national anthem.
