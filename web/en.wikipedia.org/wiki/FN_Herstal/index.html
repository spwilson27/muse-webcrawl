FN Herstal

FN Herstal, formally Fabrique Nationale d'Armes de Guerre and trading as FN, is a Belgian firearms manufacturer based in Herstal, near Liège. Founded in 1889, it is one of the world's best-known makers of military and sporting weapons, and a subsidiary of the FN Browning Group.

FN Herstal's manufacturing site in Herstal, Belgium.

History

FN was established in 1889 to manufacture Mauser rifles for the Belgian government. It later collaborated with American designer John Browning, producing many of his designs in Europe. Through the 20th century, FN developed landmark weapons including the FN FAL battle rifle, the FN MAG general-purpose machine gun, and the FN Minimi light machine gun, and later the P90 personal defence weapon, the Five-seven pistol, and the SCAR family of rifles.

Operations

The company supplies armed forces and law enforcement agencies in more than 100 countries. Its American subsidiary, FN America, manufactures many of the company's products for the United States military and commercial markets.
