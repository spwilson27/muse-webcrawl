List of Roman water mills

This is a list of Roman water mills. The ancient Romans used water power extensively for milling grain, sawing stone and wood, ore crushing, fulling and driving bellows. The most famous installation is the Barbegal complex in southern Gaul, where sixteen waterwheels on an aqueduct-fed hillside ground flour on an industrial scale.

Technology

Roman mills used vertical undershot and overshot wheels described by Vitruvius, horizontal wheels, and gearing to drive millstones, trip hammers and saws — an early application of water power at proto-industrial scale.
