Electric vehicle industry in China

The electric vehicle industry in China is the largest in the world. China is both the biggest producer and the biggest market for electric cars: in the mid-2020s roughly half of all new cars sold in China were "new energy vehicles" (battery electric or plug-in hybrid), and Chinese makers accounted for the majority of global EV sales and battery production.

Development

Government policy from 2009 onward — subsidies, licence-plate privileges, purchase mandates and support for battery supply chains — turned China into the centre of the industry. BYD overtook Tesla as the world's largest maker of battery electric cars in late 2023. Other major producers include SAIC, Geely, Li Auto, XPeng, NIO and Xiaomi, while CATL and BYD dominate global battery shipments.

Exports and trade tensions

China became the world's largest car exporter in 2023, propelled by EV shipments. Rapid export growth prompted the European Union to impose additional tariffs on Chinese-built electric cars in 2024, and the United States raised its tariff on Chinese EVs to 100 percent, alleging unfair subsidies and overcapacity.

Domestic market

Fierce competition among more than a hundred brands has produced repeated price wars, rapid model cycles and fast adoption of technologies such as LFP batteries, battery swapping and advanced driver assistance.
