Lists of dinosaur-bearing stratigraphic units

Dinosaur fossils have been recovered from hundreds of named geological formations on every continent, including Antarctica. Wikipedia organizes these in lists of dinosaur-bearing stratigraphic units, subdivided by type of fossil evidence: units yielding body fossils (bones and teeth), units with trackways and other ichnofossils, and units producing dinosaur eggs and nests.

The lists also include stratigraphic units known only from footprints (such as the Moenkopi and Glen Canyon units of the American Southwest) and egg sites (such as the Nemegt Formation of Mongolia and the Auca Mahuevo site in Argentina, famous for titanosaur nesting grounds).
