EGOT

EGOT, an acronym for the Emmy, Grammy, Oscar, and Tony Awards, is the designation given to people who have won all four of the major American performing-arts awards. This article lists the winners of all four awards in competitive categories. The term was coined by actor Philip Michael Thomas in 1984. Richard Rodgers was the first person to achieve the feat (in 1962); others include Helen Hayes, Rita Moreno, Audrey Hepburn, Marvin Hamlisch, Mel Brooks, Whoopi Goldberg, John Legend, Jennifer Hudson, and Viola Davis.

Variations

Some lists also count honorary or non-competitive awards; others note near-winners missing a single award. Robert Lopez is also notable as a "double EGOT" winner, having won each award at least twice, and was the youngest person to reach EGOT status.
