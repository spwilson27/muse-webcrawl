University of Dhaka

The University of Dhaka is a public research university in Dhaka, Bangladesh, established on 1 July 1921. It is the oldest and largest university in Bangladesh, with tens of thousands of students and over 2,000 faculty members.

History

Founded during British rule following advocacy by Nawab Sir Khwaja Salimullah and others, the university played a central role in the Bengali Language Movement of 1952 and in the Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971.

Campus and academics

Its campus features landmarks such as Curzon Hall and the Shaheed Minar nearby. The university comprises more than a dozen faculties and many institutes, and its alumni include Nobel laureate Muhammad Yunus's contemporaries in Bangladeshi public life, scientists such as Satyendra Nath Bose, who worked there early in his career.
