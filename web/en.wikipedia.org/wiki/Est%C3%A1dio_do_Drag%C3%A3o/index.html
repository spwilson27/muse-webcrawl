Location: Porto, Portugal
Opened: 16 November 2003

The Estádio do Dragão (Dragon Stadium) is an all-seater football stadium in Porto, Portugal, and the home ground of FC Porto. It replaced the club's former ground, the Estádio das Antas, and was built for UEFA Euro 2004. The name refers to the dragon on the club's crest.

History

The stadium was inaugurated on 16 November 2003 with a friendly against FC Barcelona in which a 16-year-old Lionel Messi made his senior debut. During Euro 2004 it hosted five matches, including the opening game and a semi-final. In its first season Porto, managed by José Mourinho, won the UEFA Champions League, using the stadium as their home ground throughout the campaign. The Dragão also hosted the 2019 UEFA Nations League final and the 2021 UEFA Champions League final, moved from Istanbul because of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Facilities

The stadium holds just over 50,000 spectators and has a UEFA category four rating. It contains the FC Porto museum, opened in 2013, and is served by its own metro station, Estádio do Dragão, on the Porto Metro.
