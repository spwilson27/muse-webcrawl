Audi Q5

The Audi Q5 is a compact luxury crossover SUV produced by the German manufacturer Audi since 2008. It was unveiled at the 2008 Beijing Auto Show and is built on the Audi MLB platform shared with the A4 and A5.

First generation (8R; 2008–2017)

The original Q5 offered petrol and diesel engines, including a 2.0 TFSI, 3.2 FSI V6 and TDI diesels, with Quattro all-wheel drive standard in most markets. A hybrid version appeared in 2011, and the high-performance SQ5 followed.

Second generation (FY; 2017–2024)

Revealed at the 2016 Paris Motor Show, the second generation was built in a new plant in San José Chiapa, Mexico, and added a longer-wheelbase Q5L for China. Production hybrid and plug-in hybrid (TFSI e) variants were offered.

Third generation (2024–)

The third-generation Q5, on the Premium Platform Combustion (PPC), was presented in 2024 with mild-hybrid petrol and diesel powertrains.

The Q5 has consistently been one of Audi's best-selling models worldwide.
