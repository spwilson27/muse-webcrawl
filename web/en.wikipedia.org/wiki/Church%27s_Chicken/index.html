Church's Chicken is an American fast food restaurant chain specializing in fried chicken, founded by George W. Church Sr. in San Antonio, Texas, in 1952. Outside the United States and Canada it operates largely under the name Texas Chicken.

History

The first restaurant, "Church's Fried Chicken To Go," opened across from the Alamo selling only fried chicken. The chain expanded through the southern United States, went public in 1969, and by the 1980s was the third-largest chicken chain in the US. It was combined with Popeyes under one owner (AFC Enterprises) between 1989 and 2004, and has since passed through several private-equity owners.

Menu

The menu centres on hand-battered fried chicken, chicken tenders, honey-butter biscuits, fries, corn on the cob, and jalapeño peppers, marketed on large portions and low prices.
