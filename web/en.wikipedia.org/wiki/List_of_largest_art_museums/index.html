List of largest art museums

This list of largest art museums ranks art museums by their gallery exhibition space (floor area devoted to display), one of several measures of museum size. By this measure, the Louvre in Paris is the largest art museum in the world. Total building area, collection size and visitor numbers give different rankings; for example, the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York has the largest total floor area of any art museum, while the State Hermitage Museum in Saint Petersburg holds one of the largest collections.

Other very large institutions include the Art Institute of Chicago, the Musée d'Orsay in Paris, the Rijksmuseum in Amsterdam, the National Museum of Korea in Seoul, the Prado Museum in Madrid, the Uffizi in Florence, and the National Palace Museum in Taipei, whose collection of Chinese art is among the finest in the world.

By visitors

Annual visitor rankings, compiled in the TEA/AECOM and Art Newspaper surveys, are usually headed by the Louvre (around 9–10 million visitors in strong years), followed by the Vatican Museums, the National Museum of China, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, Tate Modern and the British Museum in London, and the National Gallery in London.
