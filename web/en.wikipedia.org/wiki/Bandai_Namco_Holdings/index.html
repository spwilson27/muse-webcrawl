Bandai Namco Holdings Inc. is a Japanese multinational holding company headquartered in Shinagawa, Tokyo. It was formed on 29 September 2005 from the management integration of toy and model manufacturer Bandai and video game and arcade company Namco, two of Japan's most storied entertainment firms.

History

Bandai, founded in 1950, grew into Japan's largest toy maker on the strength of character merchandise such as Gundam plastic models, Power Rangers, and Tamagotchi. Namco, founded in 1955 as Nakamura Seisakusho, became an arcade pioneer with Pac-Man, Galaga, and the Tekken and Ridge Racer franchises. The two companies announced their integration in May 2005, creating a group able to exploit characters across toys, games, amusement facilities, and video content.

Business units

The group's operations are organized around strategic business units, including Toys and Hobby (Bandai, Banpresto, MegaHouse), Network Entertainment (headed by Bandai Namco Entertainment, publisher of the Soulcalibur, Tales, and Dark Souls-adjacent catalogues and co-developer of numerous Nintendo titles), Visual and Music Production, Real Entertainment (arcades and amusement facilities), and IP Creation (Sunrise, the anime studio behind Mobile Suit Gundam).

Significance

Bandai Namco is one of Japan's largest entertainment conglomerates and among the world's biggest toy companies. Its corporate strategy, described as an "IP axis strategy," maximizes the value of intellectual properties such as Gundam, Dragon Ball, One Piece, and Pac-Man across every medium the group operates.
