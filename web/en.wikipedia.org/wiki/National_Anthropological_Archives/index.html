National Anthropological Archives

The National Anthropological Archives (NAA) is an archive administered by the Smithsonian Institution in the United States. It collects and preserves historical and contemporary documents relating to anthropology and the peoples of the world, with a particular focus on the Indigenous peoples of the Americas.

Holdings

The archives hold an estimated one million photographs, as well as manuscripts, field notes, sound recordings, film, video, and works of art. Its collections document the history of anthropology in the United States and include the records of the Bureau of American Ethnology, founded in 1879. Materials include documentation of hundreds of Native American languages, many of them no longer spoken, making the NAA an essential resource for linguistic and cultural revitalization efforts.

Location and access

The NAA is part of the Department of Anthropology of the National Museum of Natural History. Its collections are housed at the Smithsonian's Museum Support Center in Suitland, Maryland, and are open to researchers, tribal communities, and the general public.
