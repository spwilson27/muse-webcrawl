National Electronic Funds Transfer

NEFT
Type: Electronic funds transfer system
Country: India
Launched: November 2005
Operator: Reserve Bank of India

National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) is an electronic funds transfer system maintained by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). It enables bank customers in India to transfer funds between bank accounts across different banks on a one-to-one basis.

Operation

NEFT transactions are settled in batches on a deferred net settlement basis. Originally operating only during banking hours, the system has been available 24 hours a day, seven days a week since December 2019, with settlements taking place in half-hourly batches around the clock. To send money, a customer needs the recipient's account number and the Indian Financial System Code (IFSC) of the destination branch.

Usage

There is no minimum or maximum amount for NEFT transfers for most account types, and the RBI has directed banks not to levy charges on savings accounts for online NEFT transactions. NEFT is widely used for salaries, bill payments, and person-to-person transfers, alongside other Indian payment systems such as RTGS for high-value transfers and IMPS and UPI for instant payments.
