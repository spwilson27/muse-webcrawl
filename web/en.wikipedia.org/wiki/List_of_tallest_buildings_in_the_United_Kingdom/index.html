List of tallest buildings in the United Kingdom

This article lists the tallest buildings and structures in the United Kingdom.

The tallest building in the United Kingdom is The Shard in London, completed in 2012 and standing 309.6 metres (1,016 ft) tall. London dominates the national rankings: the skyscrapers of the City of London and Canary Wharf account for nearly all of the country's buildings over 150 metres. Outside the capital, the tallest buildings are found in Manchester, whose Deansgate Square South Tower, completed in 2018, was the first building outside London to exceed 200 metres.

History

Before the late 20th century, the tallest structures in Britain were cathedrals, church spires and industrial chimneys. The post-war decades produced slab-block office towers such as the CIS Tower in Manchester and Centre Point in London, but the modern high-rise era began with the construction of Canary Wharf in the late 1980s. Planning restrictions protecting sightlines to St Paul's Cathedral have shaped the distinctive profile of the City cluster.
