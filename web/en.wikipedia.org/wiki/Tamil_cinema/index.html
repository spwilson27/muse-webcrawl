Tamil cinema

Industry centreKodambakkam, Chennai, Tamil Nadu
NicknameKollywood
First filmKeechaka Vadham (1916/1918, silent)
First talkieKalidas (1931)

Tamil cinema is the segment of Indian cinema dedicated to motion pictures produced in the Tamil language, based primarily in the Kodambakkam neighbourhood of Chennai — hence its informal name Kollywood, a blend of Kodambakkam and Hollywood. It is one of the largest film industries in India.

History

The first Tamil silent film, Keechaka Vadham, was directed by R. Nataraja Mudaliar in the late 1910s. The first Tamil talkie, Kalidas, followed in 1931. From the 1940s onward, stars such as M. G. Ramachandran and Sivaji Ganesan dominated the screen, and cinema became deeply intertwined with Tamil Nadu politics and the Dravidian movement. Directors such as Mani Ratnam, and stars such as Rajinikanth and Kamal Haasan, brought Tamil films national and international recognition.

Characteristics

Tamil films are known for strong music traditions, with celebrated composers including Ilaiyaraaja and A. R. Rahman, and for commercially successful action, family drama, and socially themed films distributed across a large global Tamil diaspora.
