Fountains of Bellagio

LocationBellagio, Las Vegas Strip, Paradise, Nevada, United States
Opened1998
DesignerWET
Lake area8.5 acres (3.4 ha)

The Fountains of Bellagio is a large musical fountain show at the Bellagio resort on the Las Vegas Strip in Paradise, Nevada. Choreographed water displays are performed in the resort's man-made lake to music and light. Designed by the firm WET, the fountains opened with the resort on October 15, 1998, and reportedly cost around $40 million to construct.

Design

The fountain system operates in a lake of about 8.5 acres (3.4 hectares) in front of the hotel. It incorporates a network of pipes with more than 1,200 nozzles, including "shooters" capable of propelling water up to about 460 feet (140 m) into the air, along with more than 4,500 lights. Shows are choreographed to a rotating repertoire of music, ranging from classical and operatic pieces to Broadway and popular songs; notable numbers have included "Viva Las Vegas", "Luck Be a Lady", and "Time to Say Goodbye".

Shows

Performances are free to the public and are held in the afternoon and every 30 minutes to 15 minutes in the evening, weather permitting. The fountains have become one of the most photographed attractions in Las Vegas and have appeared in numerous films, including Ocean's Eleven (2001), whose closing scene features the main characters watching the display.
