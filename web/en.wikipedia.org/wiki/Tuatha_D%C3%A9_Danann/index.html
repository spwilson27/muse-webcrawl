Tuatha Dé Danann

The Tuatha Dé Danann ("the folk of the goddess Danu") are a supernatural race in Irish mythology, representing the pre-Christian gods of Ireland. In the Lebor Gabála Érenn (Book of Invasions), they arrive in Ireland in dark clouds, defeat the Fir Bolg and later the Fomorians at the Second Battle of Mag Tuired, and are in turn defeated by the Milesians (the ancestors of the Irish), after which they retreat into the Otherworld of the sídhe mounds.

Legacy

Christian scribes euhemerized the Tuatha Dé Danann as gifted mortals, and they survive in folklore as the aes sídhe, the "people of the mounds," associated with fairy lore. Figures such as Brigid and Lug passed into folk and saint traditions.
