Team Ninja

Team Ninja (Japanese: チームニンジャ) is a Japanese video game developer and a division of Koei Tecmo. Founded in 1995 by Tomonobu Itagaki within Tecmo, the studio is best known for the Dead or Alive fighting game series, the Ninja Gaiden action series, and the Nioh action role-playing games.

History

Team Ninja's first major success was the original Dead or Alive (1996). The 2004 reboot of Ninja Gaiden for the Xbox was acclaimed as one of the finest action games of its era. After Itagaki's departure in 2008, Yosuke Hayashi became studio head. The team later developed Hyrule Warriors with Omega Force, Fire Emblem: Three Houses support work, Nioh (2017) and Nioh 2 (2020), Stranger of Paradise: Final Fantasy Origin (2022), Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty (2023), and Rise of the Rōnin (2024).
