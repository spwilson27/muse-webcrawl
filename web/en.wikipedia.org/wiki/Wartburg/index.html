Wartburg

The Wartburg is a castle overlooking Eisenach in Thuringia, Germany, founded around 1067.

History

Martin Luther took refuge here in 1521–1522 under Frederick the Wise and translated the New Testament into German. The castle also hosted the Sängerkrieg minstrel contest and the 1817 Wartburg Festival.

Heritage

It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1999, exemplifying medieval and 19th-century restoration architecture.
