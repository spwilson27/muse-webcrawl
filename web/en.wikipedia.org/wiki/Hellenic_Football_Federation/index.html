Hellenic Football Federation

The Hellenic Football Federation (HFF; Greek: Ελληνική Ποδοσφαιρική Ομοσπονδία, ΕΠΟ) is the governing body of association football in Greece. Founded in 1926, it is a member of UEFA and FIFA and is headquartered in Athens.

Responsibilities

The federation organizes the Greek Cup, supervises amateur and youth football, and administers the Greece national football team and other national teams. The Super League Greece, the professional top flight, is organized by the clubs' cooperative under HFF oversight.

History

The HFF joined FIFA in 1927 and was a founding member of UEFA in 1954. The national team's greatest success, victory at UEFA Euro 2004, came under the federation's administration. Greek football has periodically been affected by match-fixing scandals and government intervention, leading FIFA and UEFA to impose monitoring and threatened suspension at various times.
