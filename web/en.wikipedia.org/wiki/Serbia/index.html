Serbia

Serbia, officially the Republic of Serbia, is a landlocked country in Southeast Europe in the Balkans, bordered by Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Montenegro, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Croatia. Its population is about 6.6 million (excluding Kosovo) and its capital is Belgrade, one of Europe's oldest cities, at the confluence of the Danube and Sava.

Serbia was the dominant republic of Yugoslavia, which disintegrated in wars in the 1990s; Serbia was bombed by NATO in 1999 over Kosovo, which declared independence in 2008 — not recognised by Serbia. Serbia is an EU candidate balancing ties with both the EU and Russia. It is known for its tennis stars, led by Novak Djokovic, its brass music festival at Guča, and rakija plum brandy.
