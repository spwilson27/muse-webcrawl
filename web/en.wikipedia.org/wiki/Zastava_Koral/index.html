Zastava Koral

The Zastava Koral (Serbian Cyrillic: Застава Корал), also known by its export name Yugo, is a subcompact hatchback formerly manufactured by Zastava Automobiles, a Yugoslav (later Serbian) car maker. Production began in 1980 and continued, through several model generations, until 2008.

History

The Koral was based on Fiat mechanicals, with styling influenced by the Fiat 127. It was exported widely, and became famous in the United States, where it was sold from 1985 as the Yugo GV at a launch price of $3,990 — then the cheapest new car in America. Initial strong sales gave way to a poor reputation for reliability and safety, and the car became a frequent subject of jokes, although it later gained a cult following and historical interest as one of the last Yugoslav industrial products.

Models

Variants included the Koral 45, 55 and 65, the three-door and convertible Cabrio versions, and the restyled Florida-based derivatives. Production at the Kragujevac plant ended in November 2008 after nearly 800,000 units of the Koral family had been built.
