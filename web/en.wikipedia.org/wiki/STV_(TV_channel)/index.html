STV is a Scottish free-to-air television channel serving Central and Northern Scotland. It is owned by the STV Group and operates the two Channel 3 franchises in Scotland — historically Scottish Television in the Central belt and Grampian Television in the North — as part of the ITV network. Together the two licences reach the great majority of Scotland's population.

History

Scottish Television began broadcasting from Glasgow's Theatre Royal on 31 August 1957, the first ITV franchise in Scotland; Grampian Television followed in the north-east in 1961. The parent company, SMG, rebranded both services under the single name STV on 30 May 2006, with separate news and advertising for the two regions retained. STV is the only member of the Channel 3 network not owned by ITV plc, and its relationship with the network company has periodically involved disputes over programme costs and opt-outs, including STV's former practice of replacing some network programmes with Scottish content.

Programming

Alongside ITV network programming, STV produces its own news service, STV News at Six, with separate editions for its regions, as well as current affairs, entertainment and Gaelic-interest programming. Its streaming service, STV Player, carries live and catch-up content and has become a significant part of the group's business, together with the production arm STV Studios.
