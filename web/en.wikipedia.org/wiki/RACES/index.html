The Radio Amateur Civil Emergency Service (RACES) is a United States emergency radio service composed of licensed amateur radio operators who volunteer their equipment and skills to government emergency management agencies during civil emergencies.

Organisation

Authorised under Part 97 of the Federal Communications Commission's rules, RACES stations are activated by local, county or state emergency management officials and may operate during declared emergencies — including, uniquely among amateur services, periods in which the President invokes war powers and normal amateur operation is suspended. RACES groups conduct regular training nets and drills, and are distinct from the ARRL's volunteer Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES), though many operators serve in both.

Use

RACES volunteers have provided backup communications during hurricanes, floods, earthquakes and other disasters when ordinary telephone and public-safety systems are overloaded or damaged.
