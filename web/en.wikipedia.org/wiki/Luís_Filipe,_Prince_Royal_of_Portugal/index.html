Luís Filipe, Prince Royal of Portugal (21 March 1887 – 1 February 1908) was the eldest son of King Carlos I of Portugal and Queen Amélie of Orléans, and heir apparent to the Portuguese throne. He held the titles Prince Royal, Duke of Braganza, and was a Knight of the Golden Fleece.

Life

Luís Filipe was born in Lisbon during the reign of his grandfather, Luís I. As heir, he received a military education and served as a cavalry officer; he also acted as regent during his father's absence on a diplomatic trip in 1907. Contemporaries described him as intelligent and well prepared for kingship.

Assassination

On 1 February 1908, the royal family was returning to Lisbon from Vila Viçosa when their open landau was attacked in the Terreiro do Paço by republican assassins Alfredo Costa and Manuel Buíça. King Carlos was killed instantly; Luís Filipe was fatally wounded and died about twenty minutes later, surviving his father briefly — for that short interval he was, in the view of monarchists, de facto king. His younger brother Manuel succeeded as Manuel II, the last king of Portugal.
