Jeffrey Jacob Abrams (born June 27, 1966) is an American filmmaker and composer. He is best known for his works in the genres of action, drama and science fiction. Abrams created the television series Felicity (1998–2002), Alias (2001–2006), Lost (2004–2010) and Fringe (2008–2013). He directed the films Mission: Impossible III (2006), Star Trek (2009), Super 8 (2011), Star Trek Into Darkness (2013), Star Wars: The Force Awakens (2015) and Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker (2019).

Early life

Abrams was born in New York City and raised in Los Angeles, the son of television producer Gerald W. Abrams and executive producer Carol Ann Abrams (née Kelvin). He attended Palisades High School and Sarah Lawrence College. His first work in the film industry was at age 16, writing music for the low-budget horror film Nightbeast (1982).

Career

Abrams began his screenwriting career with Regarding Henry (1991) and Forever Young (1992), and co-wrote Armageddon (1998). He moved into television by co-creating Felicity with Matt Reeves, then created the spy drama Alias starring Jennifer Garner. With Damon Lindelof he co-created Lost, one of the most acclaimed series of the 2000s; Abrams directed its pilot. In 2001 he founded the production company Bad Robot.

His feature directorial debut, Mission: Impossible III, was followed by the rebooted Star Trek, the Steven Spielberg-produced homage Super 8, and Star Trek Into Darkness. The Force Awakens, which he directed and co-wrote, grossed over $2 billion worldwide. He returned to direct and co-write The Rise of Skywalker. As a producer, his credits include the Cloverfield films, later Mission: Impossible instalments and the television series Person of Interest, Westworld and Lovecraft Country. Abrams is also known for his frequent collaborations with composer Michael Giacchino and for the "mystery box" storytelling philosophy described in his 2007 TED talk.

Personal life

Abrams is married to public relations executive Katie McGrath; they have three children, including singer-songwriter Gracie Abrams. He is a supporter of Democratic Party causes and, with his wife, has engaged in philanthropy through the Katie McGrath & J. J. Abrams Family Foundation.
