Horse show


Show jumping at a horse show

A horse show is a judged exhibition of horses and ponies. Many horse shows provide a series of different competitive classes, grouped roughly by horse or pony type, breed, rider age, and discipline, including show jumping, dressage, eventing, hunt seat, western pleasure, and halter (in-hand) classes. Prize money, ribbons, and trophies are awarded to top competitors.

Organisation

Horse shows range from informal local fun shows and 4-H or Pony Club events to multi-day national and international competitions run under the rules of bodies such as the Fédération Équestre Internationale (FEI), the United States Equestrian Federation, and breed associations. Classes may be judged on the horse's movement, conformation, and manners, on the rider's equitation, or on objective performance such as time and faults in jumping.

Disciplines

English-style competition includes dressage, show hunters, show jumping, and saddle seat; Western-style competition includes western pleasure, reining, trail, and barrel racing. Breed shows showcase a single breed—such as the Arabian, Morgan, or Quarter Horse—while open shows admit all breeds. Driving, vaulting, and gymkhana games feature at many events, and major shows such as the Royal International Horse Show and the Washington International Horse Show draw top international riders.
