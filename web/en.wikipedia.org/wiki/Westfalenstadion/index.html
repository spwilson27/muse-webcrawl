Westfalenstadion

Westfalenstadion (German pronunciation: [vɛstˈfaːlənˌʃtaːdi̯ɔn]), officially called Signal Iduna Park under a sponsorship arrangement, is a football stadium in Dortmund, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany. It is the home stadium of Borussia Dortmund and, with a league capacity of 81,365 spectators, the largest stadium in Germany.

History

The stadium was built for the 1974 FIFA World Cup and opened on 2 April 1974 with a friendly between Borussia Dortmund and Schalke 04. It was expanded several times, notably in the late 1990s and for the 2006 FIFA World Cup, when it hosted six matches including a semi-final.

The Yellow Wall

The south stand, the Südtribüne, is the largest free-standing grandstand in Europe, holding 24,454 spectators standing. Its steep single tier, packed with singing supporters in yellow and black, is known as Die Gelbe Wand — "The Yellow Wall".

International tournaments

Beyond the 1974 and 2006 World Cups, the stadium hosted matches of UEFA Euro 2024, including a semi-final. The Germany national team has played many internationals there.
