Bugatti is a French luxury automobile manufacturer founded in 1909 in Molsheim, Alsace, by the Italian-born industrial designer Ettore Bugatti. The original company became famous for beautifully engineered and artistically designed cars that dominated Grand Prix racing in the 1920s and 1930s, and for opulent road cars such as the Type 57 Atlantic. After the deaths of Ettore and his son Jean and the disruption of the Second World War, the original firm declined and ceased car production in the 1950s. The brand was revived by Volkswagen Group in 1998 and, since 2021, has been operated by Bugatti Rimac, producing some of the fastest and most expensive production cars in the world, including the Veyron and Chiron.

Original company (1909–1963)

Ettore Bugatti, from an artistic Milanese family, built lightweight, finely crafted cars. The Type 35 Grand Prix car won over a thousand races, including five consecutive Targa Florio victories. Road cars ranged from the luxurious Type 41 "Royale" — one of the largest cars ever built — to the Type 57, designed by Jean Bugatti, whose Atlantic coupé variant is now among the most valuable cars in existence. Jean's death in a testing accident in 1939 and Ettore's in 1947 left the company adrift; the last original Bugatti car appeared in the 1950s, and the firm was sold to Hispano-Suiza in 1963.

Revival

Italian entrepreneur Romano Artioli revived the name in 1987, building a factory in Campogalliano, Italy, and launching the EB110 supercar in 1991; this venture folded in 1995. Volkswagen acquired the brand in 1998 and re-established it at the historic Château Saint-Jean in Molsheim. The Veyron 16.4 (2005–2015) was the first production car to exceed 1,000 horsepower and held world speed records for production cars. Its successor, the Chiron (2016–2024), was followed by limited models such as the Divo, Centodieci, and Bolide. In 2021, control of Bugatti Automobiles passed to Bugatti Rimac, a joint venture between Rimac Group and Porsche, which unveiled the hybrid V16 Tourbillon as the Chiron's successor in 2024.
