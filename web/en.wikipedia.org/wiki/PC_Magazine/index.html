PC Magazine

PC Magazine (often shortened to PCMag) is an American computer magazine and website published by Ziff Davis. Founded in 1982 in the early IBM PC era, it became known for in-depth product reviews, benchmark testing through PC Labs, and columns on personal computing.

History

The magazine grew rapidly with the personal computer market in the 1980s and 1990s, at times exceeding 400 pages per issue. The US print edition ended in 2009, after which the title continued as a digital publication.

Content

PCMag reviews laptops, desktops, phones, software, security products, and networking equipment, and bestows Editors' Choice awards. Its comparative testing and buying guides remain a central part of the brand.
