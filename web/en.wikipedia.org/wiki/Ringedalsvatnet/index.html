Ringedalsvatnet

Ringedalsvatnet is a lake in Ullensvang Municipality in Vestland county, Norway. The lake lies just east of the village of Tyssedal and north of the town of Odda, at the foot of the Hardangervidda plateau. The famous rock formation Trolltunga ("the Troll's Tongue") juts out over the northern shore of the lake, about 700 metres above its surface, and is a popular hiking destination reached by a trail from Skjeggedal.

Hydroelectric use

The lake is regulated for hydroelectric power production. The Ringedals Dam, a concrete gravity dam built in the early twentieth century for the industrial development at Tyssedal, raised the water level; the lake's surface area is about 7 square kilometres. The Tysso I power station, now a museum, was part of Norway's early industrial electrification.
