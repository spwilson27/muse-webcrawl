Identity Crisis (DC Comics)

Identity Crisis is a seven-issue comic book limited series published by DC Comics from June to December 2004. It was created by writer Brad Meltzer and the art team of Rags Morales and Michael Bair.

Plot

The story begins with the murder of Sue Dibny, wife of the Elongated Man, and follows the Justice League's investigation. It explores the heroes' darker secrets, including the revelation that members of the League had previously mind-wiped villains — and Batman himself — to protect their secret identities. Dr. Light is initially suspected because of a past crime against Sue, but the true killer is revealed to be Jean Loring, who staged the attack in an attempt to win back her ex-husband, Ray Palmer, the Atom.

Reception

The series was a commercial success but proved controversial for its dark tone, depictions of violence, and retcons. It is frequently cited in discussions about the tone of mainstream superhero comics in the 2000s.
