Sex shop

A sex shop (also called an adult store or erotic shop) is a retailer selling products intended to enhance sexual experience, such as sex toys, lingerie, contraceptives, lubricants, and erotic literature and films.

History

Among the first modern sex shops was Beate Uhse's business in Germany, which grew from a 1940s mail-order contraceptive service into a major retail chain. Legal liberalisation in many Western countries during the 1960s and 1970s allowed such stores to operate openly, though often subject to zoning rules and age restrictions.

Regulation and modern retail

Most jurisdictions restrict sales to minors and regulate store location and signage. Since the 2000s, much of the trade has moved online, and many mainstream retailers also stock a limited range of sexual wellness products.
