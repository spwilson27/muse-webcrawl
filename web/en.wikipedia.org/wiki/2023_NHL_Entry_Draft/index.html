The 2023 NHL Entry Draft was the 61st NHL Entry Draft, held on 28–29 June 2023 at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee. The Chicago Blackhawks, who had won the draft lottery, used the first overall pick to select centre Connor Bedard of the Regina Pats, widely regarded as a generational prospect.

Top picks

The Anaheim Ducks selected Swedish centre Leo Carlsson second overall, ahead of University of Michigan centre Adam Fantilli, who went third to the Columbus Blue Jackets. Will Smith (San Jose), David Reinbacher (Montreal), Dmitri Simashev (Arizona), Matvei Michkov (Philadelphia) and Ryan Leonard (Washington) rounded out the top eight.

Bedard

Bedard entered the draft after a 71-goal, 143-point season in the WHL and gold medals with Canada at the World Junior Championships. He went on to win the Calder Memorial Trophy as the NHL's rookie of the year in 2024.

Draft lottery

Chicago moved up from third to first in the lottery held on 8 May 2023, while Anaheim took the second pick and Columbus the third.
