Getty Images

Type: Private
Founded: 1995 (as Getty Communications)
Founders: Mark Getty; Jonathan Klein
Headquarters: Seattle, Washington, US
Industry: Stock photography and visual media licensing

Getty Images Holdings, Inc. is an American visual media company and supplier of stock images, editorial photography, video, and music for business and consumers, with a library of over 200 million assets. It is one of the largest stock photography agencies in the world, serving creative, media, and corporate customers in more than 100 countries.

History

The company was founded in 1995 by Mark Getty and Jonathan Klein as Getty Communications, acquiring and digitising established photo archives and agencies, including Tony Stone Images. It consolidated a fragmented industry by buying collections such as the Hulton Picture Library, and built an online licensing platform that displaced traditional picture libraries. Getty Images was taken private by Hellman & Friedman in 2008, acquired by the Carlyle Group in 2012, and control passed to the Getty family in 2018; it returned to public markets via a SPAC merger in 2022.

Business and controversies

Getty licenses rights-managed and royalty-free content and provides assignment photography for news, sport, and entertainment events. It has been criticised for aggressive copyright enforcement, for licensing public-domain images, and for its 2016 EU antitrust complaint against Google's image search practices. In 2023 Getty sued Stability AI, alleging that Stable Diffusion was trained on its watermarked images without licence. In January 2025, Getty Images and rival Shutterstock announced a merger of equals.
