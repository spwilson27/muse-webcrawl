Wayne Douglas Gretzky (born 26 January 1961) is a Canadian former professional ice hockey player and former head coach. Nicknamed "the Great One", he is widely considered the greatest ice hockey player of all time. He played 20 seasons in the NHL and holds or shares 61 league records, including the most goals (894), assists (1,963) and points (2,857); his assists total alone exceeds any other player's total points.

Gretzky led the Edmonton Oilers dynasty to four Stanley Cups in the 1980s, recording four 200-point seasons — no other player has reached 200 even once. His August 1988 trade to the Los Angeles Kings shocked Canada and is credited with popularising hockey in the American Sun Belt. He finished his career with the New York Rangers in 1999, was inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame immediately (the waiting period was waived), and his number 99 was retired league-wide. Alex Ovechkin passed Gretzky's goals record in April 2025, scoring his 895th goal.
