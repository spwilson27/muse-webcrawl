Legion (David Charles Haller) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Bill Sienkiewicz, he first appeared in New Mutants #25 (March 1985). The son of Professor Charles Xavier and Gabrielle Haller, David is an Omega-level mutant whose enormous psionic powers are fragmented among numerous alternate personalities, each of which controls a different ability. His name derives from the biblical phrase "My name is Legion, for we are many."

Publication history

Legion debuted in New Mutants in 1985 as a patient at the Muir Island research facility. He became central to the "Legion Quest" storyline in X-Men (1995), in which he travelled back in time intending to kill Magneto but instead killed his own father, creating the alternate timeline known as the Age of Apocalypse. After years of absence from publication, the character returned in New Mutants (2009) and headlined the series X-Men: Legacy (2012–2014), written by Simon Spurrier, which followed his efforts to master or purge his personalities.

Fictional character biography

David Haller was conceived in Israel, where his mother Gabrielle Haller, a Holocaust survivor, worked with Charles Xavier after he helped her recover from a catatonic state. Gabrielle never told Xavier she was pregnant. David was raised in Paris and, as a child, survived a terrorist attack that burned his attackers to death with a latent psychic blast, leaving him traumatised. He developed a form of dissociative identity disorder, and each emerging personality manifested its own mutant power — including telepathy, telekinesis, and pyrokinesis in his three earliest personas.

Brought to Muir Island for treatment by Moira MacTaggert, David's personalities eventually numbered in the hundreds or thousands. His attempts to prove himself to his father led to the Legion Quest incident and his apparent death. He later returned, at times seeking redemption and at times threatening all of reality, as in the "Age of X" storyline in which he unconsciously rewrote the world. During the Krakoan era of X-Men comics, Legion played a key role in defending the mutant nation and in the events surrounding Onslaught's return.

Powers and abilities

Legion is classified as an Omega-level mutant with the potential for virtually unlimited psionic power, including reality manipulation. In practice, each of his powers is "owned" by a different alternate personality, and David can access a power only when the corresponding persona is dominant or co-operative. Known personas have displayed telepathy, telekinesis, teleportation, time manipulation, energy projection, superhuman strength, shapeshifting, and matter transmutation, among many others. This fragmentation is both his greatest limitation and the defining feature of the character.

In other media

Legion is the protagonist of the television series Legion (2017–2019), created by Noah Hawley for FX, in which he is portrayed by Dan Stevens. The series, noted for its surreal visual style, ran for three seasons and also featured Aubrey Plaza as Lenny Busker and Rachel Keller as Syd Barrett.
