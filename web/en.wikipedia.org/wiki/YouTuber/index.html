YouTuber

A YouTuber is a content creator and social media influencer who produces and uploads videos on the online video-sharing platform YouTube, usually to a personal channel. YouTubers produce content across every conceivable genre — vlogging, gaming, beauty, education, comedy, music, commentary, product reviews, livestreaming, and children's entertainment — and the most successful command audiences rivalling those of traditional television.

History

YouTube was founded in 2005; within a few years, individual creators such as lonelygirl15, Smosh, and Fred built large followings from home production. The YouTube Partner Program, launched in 2007, allowed creators to share in advertising revenue, professionalizing the role. The 2010s saw the rise of multi-channel networks, creator agencies, and full production teams behind nominally personal channels, as well as events such as VidCon and the Creator Awards (Play Buttons awarded at 100,000, 1 million, 10 million, and 100 million subscribers).

Monetization

Income sources include advertising revenue share, channel memberships, Super Chats, sponsorships and brand deals, merchandise, licensing, and crowdfunding. Earnings vary enormously: a small elite of creators earn tens of millions of dollars annually — Forbes and other outlets have published annual rankings since 2015 — while most earn little. MrBeast (Jimmy Donaldson), known for large-scale stunts and philanthropy videos, became the most-subscribed individual creator, surpassing 300 million subscribers in 2024.

Influence and criticism

YouTubers have reshaped entertainment, marketing, journalism, and children's media habits, and surveys have repeatedly found "YouTuber" among the most desired careers for children in the US and UK. The profession has also drawn criticism and regulatory attention concerning advertising disclosure to children, burnout and mental health, algorithmic amplification of controversy, creator–audience parasocial dynamics, and periodic advertiser boycotts ("adpocalypses") that changed monetization policies platform-wide.
