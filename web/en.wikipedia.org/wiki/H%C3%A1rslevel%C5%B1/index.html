Hárslevelű

Hárslevelű
Type: White wine grape
Origin: Hungary (Carpathian Basin)
Key regions: Tokaj, Somló
Meaning: "Linden leaf"

Hárslevelű (Hungarian for "linden leaf") is a white wine grape variety native to the Carpathian Basin, best known as the second most important grape in Hungary's Tokaji wines, after Furmint.

Characteristics

The grape produces aromatic, full-bodied wines with notes of linden flowers, honey, and spice. Its loose bunches are somewhat less susceptible to noble rot (botrytis) than Furmint, but botrytised Hárslevelű contributes richness to Tokaji Aszú blends.

Regions

Besides Tokaj, it is grown in Somló, Eger, and Villány, and across the border in Slovakia's Tokaj region and parts of Austria and Romania. It is also vinified as a dry varietal wine.
