List of social sororities

Social sororities are Greek-letter social organisations for women at colleges and universities, principally in North America. Most belong to the National Panhellenic Conference (NPC), founded in 1902, or to the National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHC), whose historically African American member organisations are known as the "Divine Nine".

Kappa Alpha Theta (1870) was the first Greek-letter fraternity founded for women, while Alpha Delta Pi and Phi Mu, founded as the Adelphean and Philomathean societies at Wesleyan in the 1850s, predate the word "sorority" itself, which was coined in 1882 for Gamma Phi Beta.
