Cinder is a free and open-source C++ library and framework for creative coding — the development of interactive graphics, data visualisation, installations and audiovisual applications. It provides a professional-grade, cross-platform toolkit comparable in spirit to openFrameworks and Processing, but built natively in modern C++.

Features

Cinder wraps platform APIs for 2D and 3D graphics (OpenGL and, in later versions, other renderers), audio, video playback and capture, image I/O, networking and peripherals, and includes extensive support for geometry, shaders, particle systems and camera devices such as Kinect. A system of downloadable "CinderBlocks" packages community extensions.

History

The library grew out of work at the design studio The Barbarian Group and was released publicly in 2010; it has since been used for museum installations, commercial interactive exhibits, stage visuals and generative art, and is maintained by an open-source community.
