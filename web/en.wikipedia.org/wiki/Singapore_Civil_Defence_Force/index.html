The Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) is a uniformed organisation in Singapore under the Ministry of Home Affairs that provides firefighting, rescue, and emergency medical services, and coordinates community first response and civil defence preparedness. Its mission is to protect and save lives and property, and it serves as the national authority for fire safety regulation and hazard-materials response.

History

Organized firefighting in Singapore began with the Singapore Fire Brigade (1888), which over the decades became the Singapore Fire Service. Following recommendations after the 1982 merger of civil defence functions, the Singapore Civil Defence Unit was formed in 1982 and the Singapore Civil Defence Force was formally created in 1986 by amalgamating the Fire Service with civil defence elements, notably after the Hotel New World collapse of March 1986 demonstrated the need for unified rescue capability. The Civil Defence Act 1986 provides its statutory basis, and SCDF became the nation's dedicated authority for emergency preparedness, including the network of MRT-station shelters and public warning systems.

Organisation and operations

SCDF operates from a headquarters at Ubi, four divisions, and around 20 fire stations plus fire posts across the island, staffed by regular officers, full-time national servicemen (consisting a large share of frontline manpower), and operationally ready reservists. It runs Singapore's public emergency ambulance service (995 hotline) and fields specialist units: the Disaster Assistance and Rescue Team (DART) for urban search and rescue and complex rescues, a HazMat unit for chemical, biological, and radiological incidents, and marine firefighting divisions. SCDF also conducts overseas humanitarian deployments, including under the "Operation Lionheart" banner to earthquakes and floods in the region.

Community engagement

SCDF administers the SGSecure-linked Community First Responder scheme and the myResponder app, alerting nearby volunteers to suspected cardiac arrests and minor fires, and manages national programs such as the Community Emergency Preparedness Programme.
