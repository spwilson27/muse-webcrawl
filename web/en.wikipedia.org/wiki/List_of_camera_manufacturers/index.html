List of camera manufacturers

This is a list of notable manufacturers of cameras, both current and historical, including makers of film cameras, digital cameras, and specialised imaging equipment.

Active manufacturers

  • Canon (Japan) — digital SLR and mirrorless cameras under the EOS brand; the world's largest camera maker for much of the digital era.
  • Nikon (Japan) — DSLR and Z-mount mirrorless cameras; also a major lens maker.
  • Sony (Japan) — Alpha mirrorless cameras and image sensors supplied to many other brands.
  • Fujifilm (Japan) — X-series mirrorless cameras and Instax instant cameras.
  • Leica (Germany) — rangefinder and luxury cameras, maker of the M system.
  • Hasselblad (Sweden) — medium-format cameras, famously used on Apollo Moon missions.
  • Panasonic (Japan) — Lumix cameras, co-developer of the Micro Four Thirds system.
  • OM System (Japan, formerly Olympus) — Micro Four Thirds cameras.
  • Pentax (Japan, Ricoh) — DSLRs and the Pentax 67 heritage.
  • SIGMA (Japan) — lenses and Foveon-sensor cameras.
  • Phase One (Denmark) — high-end medium-format digital systems.
  • GoPro (United States) — action cameras.
  • DJI (China) — camera drones and action cameras.
  • Blackmagic Design (Australia) — digital cinema cameras.

Defunct or former manufacturers

  • Kodak (United States) — pioneer of popular photography; exited most camera production after its 2012 bankruptcy.
  • Contax, Yashica (Japan) — brands discontinued in the 2000s.
  • Minolta (Japan) — camera business sold to Sony in 2006.
  • Polaroid (United States) — instant cameras; the brand was later revived by other companies.
  • Agfa, Voigtländer (historic German makers; brands later licensed).
  • Zorki, FED, Kiev (Soviet-era manufacturers).
