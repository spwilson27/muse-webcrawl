Planned Parenthood

Founded: October 16, 1916 (as the American Birth Control League, 1921; renamed 1942)
Founder: Margaret Sanger
Type: Nonprofit organisation
Focus: Reproductive and sexual health care
Headquarters: New York City, United States

The Planned Parenthood Federation of America (PPFA), commonly called Planned Parenthood, is an American nonprofit organisation that provides reproductive and sexual health care in the United States and internationally through partners. It is one of the largest providers of contraception, sexually transmitted infection testing, cancer screening, and — most controversially — abortion services in the country, operating several hundred health centres through local affiliates.

History

The organisation traces its origins to October 16, 1916, when Margaret Sanger, her sister Ethel Byrne, and Fania Mindell opened the first birth control clinic in the United States, in Brownsville, Brooklyn. Sanger founded the American Birth Control League in 1921, which merged with other groups and adopted the name Planned Parenthood Federation of America in 1942. The organisation supported the research that produced the first oral contraceptive pill in 1960 and played a central role in the legal battles that established constitutional protections for contraception and abortion, including Griswold v. Connecticut (1965) and Roe v. Wade (1973).

Services and funding

The great majority of Planned Parenthood's medical services are preventive: contraception, STI testing and treatment, Pap tests and breast exams, pregnancy testing, and vaccinations, alongside abortion, which constitutes a small single-digit percentage of services by its own reporting. Funding comes from a mixture of private donations, patient fees, and government reimbursements (chiefly Medicaid and Title X) for non-abortion care; federal law (the Hyde Amendment) bars federal funds from paying for most abortions. Because of its abortion services, Planned Parenthood is a perennial focus of American political conflict, with repeated legislative attempts to exclude it from public funding, litigation over state restrictions, and protests — occasionally violent — at its clinics.

International work

Through its global arm and its membership in the International Planned Parenthood Federation (founded 1952), the organisation supports family-planning and reproductive-health programmes in dozens of countries, chiefly in Africa and Latin America.
