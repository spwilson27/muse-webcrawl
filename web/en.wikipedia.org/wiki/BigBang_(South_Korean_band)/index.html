BigBang (Korean: 빅뱅; stylized in all caps) is a South Korean boy band formed by YG Entertainment. The group currently consists of three members: G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung. Originally a five-member group, T.O.P left in 2023, and Seungri retired from the entertainment industry in 2019 amid the Burning Sun scandal.

Career

Debuting in 2006, BigBang achieved breakthrough success with "Lies" (2007), followed by hits such as "Haru Haru", "Fantastic Baby", and "Bang Bang Bang". The band is credited with helping spread the Korean Wave (Hallyu) globally and with pioneering self-produced idol music; leader G-Dragon is a prominent songwriter and fashion figure. Their Made World Tour (2015–2016) drew record audiences for a Korean act. BigBang have sold over 140 million records worldwide and are often dubbed the "Kings of K-pop".

Hiatus and return

The group went on hiatus from 2017 as members completed mandatory military service. They returned in April 2022 with the single "Still Life", which topped South Korean charts. G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung performed together again at the 2024 MAMA Awards.
