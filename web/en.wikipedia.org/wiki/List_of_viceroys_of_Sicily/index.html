List of viceroys of Sicily

This is a list of the viceroys of the Kingdom of Sicily. The island, joined to the Crown of Aragon in the 15th century, was governed by viceroys appointed first by the kings of Aragon and then by the Spanish Habsburgs. After the War of the Spanish Succession, Sicily passed briefly to Savoy (1713–1720) and then to Austria (1720–1734), before Charles of Bourbon united it with Naples in 1734.
