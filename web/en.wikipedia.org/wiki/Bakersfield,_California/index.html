Bakersfield is a city in and the county seat of Kern County, California, United States. It is the most populous city in Kern County and the 48th-most-populous city in the United States. The population was 403,455 at the 2020 census. Bakersfield lies near the southern end of the San Joaquin Valley, roughly 110 miles (180 km) north of Los Angeles.

History

The area was inhabited for thousands of years by the Yokuts people. The modern city traces its founding to 1869, when Colonel Thomas Baker, a settler and former officer, reclaimed swampland along the Kern River; "Baker's field" became a stopping place for travellers, and the town was formally laid out in 1873. Gold discoveries in the Kern River valley and, decisively, oil transformed the town's fortunes: the Kern River Oil Field, discovered in 1899, made the region one of America's great petroleum centres.

Economy

Bakersfield's economy rests on agriculture and petroleum. Kern County is among the most productive agricultural counties in the United States, producing almonds, grapes, citrus, carrots, pistachios and dairy products, and it accounts for a large share of California's oil output. The city was also the birthplace of the "Bakersfield sound", a raw, electrified style of country music developed in the 1950s and 1960s by artists such as Buck Owens and Merle Haggard, centred on Owens's Crystal Palace venue.

Geography and climate

The city occupies flat valley floor terrain at the southern end of the Central Valley, with the Sierra Nevada foothills to the east. It has a hot desert-influenced climate with very hot, dry summers, mild winters, and frequent tule fog in winter; air quality is a persistent public-health concern because the valley traps pollutants.

Demographics and education

Bakersfield is a majority-minority city with a large Hispanic and Latino population. It is served by California State University, Bakersfield and Bakersfield College. The 1952 Kern County earthquake (magnitude 7.3), one of the strongest in California history, caused extensive damage in the city.
