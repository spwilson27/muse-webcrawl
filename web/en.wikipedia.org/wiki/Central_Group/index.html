Central Group is a Thai multinational conglomerate controlled by the Chirathivat family, consisting of a variety of diverse investments in various corporations, each managed separately as distinct industry-specific subsidiaries. Central Group's core businesses consist of retailing (under Central Retail Corporation), real estate development (under Central Pattana), hospitality (under Centara Hotels and Resorts), and restaurants. It is one of Thailand's largest private companies and one of the most powerful families' business empires in Southeast Asia.

History

The group traces its origins to Tiang Chirathivat, an immigrant from Hainan, China, who founded a small import shop in Bangkok in 1927 and, in 1947, established the business that became Central. His son Samrit Chirathivat opened the first Central department store in Bangkok's Wang Burapha district in 1956 and pioneered modern retailing in Thailand, including fixed prices — replacing haggling — and superior customer service. In 1974 the group opened Central Ladprao, one of the first large integrated shopping complexes in the country.

Businesses

Retail

Central Retail Corporation is the group's retail arm and one of Thailand's largest retailers, operating department stores under the Central, Robinson, and (historically licensed) marks, and chains such as Tops supermarkets, Big C (in Vietnam), Power Buy, Supersports, and OfficeMate. Central Retail listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand in 2020 in what was then Thailand's largest-ever initial public offering. Internationally, the group has acquired European department store businesses, including interests in Selfridges Group, KaDeWe Group in Germany, Globus in Switzerland, and La Rinascente in Italy.

Property and hospitality

Central Pattana (CPN) develops and manages large shopping centres, including CentralWorld in Bangkok, one of the largest shopping complexes in the world. Centara Hotels and Resorts operates hotels across Thailand and internationally.
