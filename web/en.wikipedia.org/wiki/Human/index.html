Humans (Homo sapiens) are the only extant members of the genus Homo, upright bipedal primates distinguished by large brains, complex language, cumulative culture and technology from stone tools to spacecraft such as the Voyagers. Anatomically modern humans evolved in Africa roughly 300,000 years ago and spread across the world.

Society

Humans live in societies organised by kinship, custom and law, building cities, states and global institutions. The human population passed eight billion in the 2020s, raising central questions of sustainability — food, energy and climate — that run through this entire crawl.
