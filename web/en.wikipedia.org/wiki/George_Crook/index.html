George Crook

George Crook (September 8, 1828 – March 21, 1890) was a United States Army officer.

Born: Taylorsville, Ohio
Died: Chicago, Illinois
Allegiance: United States (Union)
Rank: Major general

George Crook was a career United States Army officer, most noted for his distinguished service during the American Civil War and the Indian Wars. During the 1880s, the Apache nicknamed Crook Nantan Lupan, meaning "Chief Wolf".

Early life and Civil War

Crook was born near Taylorsville, Ohio, and graduated from the United States Military Academy at West Point in 1852. He served in California and the Pacific Northwest during the 1850s. When the Civil War began he became colonel of the 36th Ohio Infantry, fought in western Virginia and at Antietam, and rose to command in the Army of West Virginia. He played a significant role in Sheridan's Shenandoah Valley campaigns of 1864 and in the Appomattox campaign of 1865, finishing the war as a major general of volunteers.

Indian Wars

After the war Crook campaigned in the Pacific Northwest against the Paiute and then, from 1871, in Arizona, where his use of Apache scouts and pack mules brought the Apache and Yavapai to surrender in 1873. In 1876 he campaigned in the Great Sioux War, fighting at the Rosebud. Returning to Arizona in 1882, he pursued Geronimo into the Sierra Madre of Mexico, and in 1883 secured the surrender of many Chiricahua Apache. In 1886 Geronimo surrendered to Crook at Cañón de los Embudos, but escaped; he later surrendered to General Nelson Miles. Crook was angered when the government exiled even loyal Apache scouts to Florida and spent his later years advocating for Apache rights.

Later life

Promoted to major general in 1888, Crook commanded the Division of the Missouri until his death in Chicago in 1890. He was buried at Arlington National Cemetery. Fort Crook, Nebraska, and Crook County, Wyoming, are named for him. The Apache campaigner Bourke recalled that Crook earned the confidence of the Apache more than any other white man; the Apache leader Red Cloud said of him, "He never lied to us."
