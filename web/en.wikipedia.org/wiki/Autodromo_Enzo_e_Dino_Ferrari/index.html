Autodromo Internazionale Enzo e Dino Ferrari

Imola Circuit
Location: Imola, Emilia-Romagna, Italy
Opened: 25 April 1953
Length: 4.909 km (3.050 mi)
Turns: 19
Major events: Formula One Emilia Romagna Grand Prix; San Marino Grand Prix (1981–2006)

The Autodromo Internazionale Enzo e Dino Ferrari, commonly known as Imola, is a motor racing circuit in the town of Imola, near Bologna, Italy. The circuit is named after Enzo Ferrari and his son Dino. It hosted the Formula One San Marino Grand Prix from 1981 to 2006 and returned to the calendar in 2020 as the Emilia Romagna Grand Prix.

History

Originally a fast, flowing road circuit opened in 1953, Imola became notorious at the 1994 San Marino Grand Prix, during which Roland Ratzenberger and three-time world champion Ayrton Senna were killed in separate accidents. The circuit was subsequently modified with chicanes at Tamburello and Villeneuve and other safety changes. Formula One left after 2006 amid renovation issues, but the circuit was upgraded, re-graded by the FIA, and returned to the F1 calendar in 2020.
