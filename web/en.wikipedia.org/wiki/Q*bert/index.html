Q*bert

Q*bert is an arcade video game developed and published by Gottlieb in 1982. Created by Warren Davis and Jeff Lee, it stars an orange, long-snouted character who hops on an isometric pyramid of cubes, changing their colours while avoiding enemies. It was one of the most popular arcade games of its era and is remembered for its distinctive synthesized "swearing" sound effect when Q*bert is caught.

DeveloperGottlieb

Gameplay

The player uses a diagonally oriented joystick to hop Q*bert between cubes of a 28-cube pyramid. Each hop changes the cube's colour; the level is completed when all cubes match the target colour. Enemies include Coily the snake, Ugg, Wrongway, and bouncing balls. Flying discs at the pyramid's sides provide an escape from pursuers.

Legacy

Q*bert was ported to numerous home systems, spawned merchandise and a Saturday-morning cartoon segment, and the character has appeared in later media including the film Pixels (2015). A sequel, Q*bert's Qubes, followed in 1983.
