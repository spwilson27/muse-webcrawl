Tom Wilkinson

Thomas Geoffrey Wilkinson (5 February 1948 – 30 December 2023) was an English actor. Trained at RADA, he gained wide recognition for The Full Monty (1997), winning a BAFTA, and received Academy Award nominations for In the Bedroom (2001) and Michael Clayton (2007).

His extensive filmography includes Shakespeare in Love, Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind, Batman Begins, John Adams — for which he won an Emmy and Golden Globe as Benjamin Franklin — and The Best Exotic Marigold Hotel.
