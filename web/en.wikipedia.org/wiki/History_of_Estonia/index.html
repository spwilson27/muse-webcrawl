History of Estonia

The history of Estonia begins with human settlement after the last Ice Age. In the 13th century the land was conquered during the Northern Crusades and ruled successively by Denmark, the Teutonic Order, Sweden, and the Russian Empire.

Independence

Estonia declared independence on 24 February 1918 and defended it in the War of Independence (1918–1920). In 1940 it was occupied and annexed by the Soviet Union, then by Nazi Germany (1941–44), then again by the USSR until 1991.

Modern Estonia

The Singing Revolution helped restore independence on 20 August 1991. Estonia joined NATO and the European Union in 2004 and adopted the euro in 2011, becoming known for its digital government.
