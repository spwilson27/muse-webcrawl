Cambridge Assessment English, formerly known as Cambridge English Language Assessment and originally as the University of Cambridge ESOL Examinations, is a department of the University of Cambridge providing English language qualifications for learners and teachers. Its first exam, the Certificate of Proficiency in English, was offered in 1913 to just three candidates.

Examinations

The suite is aligned to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), running from Pre A1 Starters for young learners through A2 Key, B1 Preliminary, B2 First, C1 Advanced, and C2 Proficiency, alongside the IELTS test (offered jointly with the British Council and IDP) and the adaptive Linguaskill and BULATS business tests. Results are reported on the Cambridge English Scale.

Recognition

Cambridge qualifications are accepted by thousands of universities, employers, and governments worldwide as evidence of English proficiency for study, work, and immigration.
