Ecosystem services
Categories: Provisioning, regulating, cultural, supporting
Key assessment: Millennium Ecosystem Assessment (2005)

Ecosystem services are the many and varied benefits to humans provided by the natural environment and healthy ecosystems, such as clean water, pollination, climate regulation and food.

Categories

The Millennium Ecosystem Assessment classified services as provisioning (food, water, timber), regulating (climate, disease, flood control), cultural (recreation, spiritual value) and supporting (nutrient cycling, soil formation). Wetlands filter water; forests store carbon; bees and other insects pollinate crops worth billions annually.

Valuation and policy

Economists attempt monetary valuation and payments for ecosystem services to support conservation, though valuation is contested. Loss of biodiversity and ecosystem degradation threaten many services. See also Biodiversity and Natural capital.
