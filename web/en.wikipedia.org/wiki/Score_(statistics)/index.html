Score (statistics)

In statistical inference, the score (or score function) is the gradient of the log-likelihood function with respect to the parameter vector. Evaluated at a particular parameter value, it measures how sensitive the likelihood is to infinitesimal changes in the parameters.

Properties

Under regularity conditions, the expected value of the score at the true parameter value is zero — the basis of estimating equations, since maximum likelihood estimates solve the equation score = 0. The variance of the score is the Fisher information.

Uses

The score underlies Rao's score test (Lagrange multiplier test), which evaluates the score at the null hypothesis without fitting the full model, and appears in Fisher scoring, generalized estimating equations, and the theory of efficient estimation.
