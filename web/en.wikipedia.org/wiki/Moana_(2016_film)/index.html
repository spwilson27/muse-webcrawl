Moana (2016 film)

Moana is a 2016 American animated musical adventure film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. Directed by John Musker and Ron Clements, it is Disney's 56th animated feature film. The film stars Auliʻi Cravalho in her acting debut as the voice of Moana, alongside Dwayne Johnson as the demigod Maui, with Rachel House, Temuera Morrison, Jemaine Clement, Nicole Scherzinger and Alan Tudyk in supporting roles. The songs were written by Lin-Manuel Miranda, Opetaia Foaʻi and Mark Mancina.

Plot

On the Polynesian island of Motunui, the inhabitants worship the goddess Te Fiti, who brought life to the ocean using the piko stone as her heart and source of creation. A thousand years before the story begins, the demigod Maui stole Te Fiti's heart, causing darkness to spread across the ocean and the island's plants and fish to die. Moana, the headstrong daughter of Chief Tui, is chosen by the ocean itself to return the heart. Against her father's rule that no one sail beyond the reef, she sets out with a hapless rooster, Heihei, finds the exiled Maui, and persuades him to help her. After recovering Maui's magical fishhook from the crab Tamatoa in the realm of monsters, they confront the lava demon Te Kā, who is revealed to be Te Fiti herself, corrupted by the loss of her heart. Moana restores the heart, Te Fiti blooms back to life, and Moana returns home to lead her people as a wayfinder, reviving their voyaging tradition.

Production

Musker and Clements developed the film after researching Polynesian mythology, making extensive research trips to Fiji, Samoa, Tahiti and other Pacific islands and consulting an "Oceanic Trust" of cultural advisers. The animation combined hand-drawn and computer techniques, with groundbreaking simulations for water, hair and Maui's animated tattoos, which were hand-drawn by animator Eric Goldberg. Cravalho, a native Hawaiian teenager, was discovered through a casting call and became one of Disney's youngest voice leads.

Music

The soundtrack blends contemporary songwriting with South Pacific musical traditions, including Tokelauan and Samoan-language passages by Foaʻi and his group Te Vaka. Signature songs include "How Far I'll Go" (performed by Cravalho, and by Alessia Cara over the credits), "You're Welcome" (Johnson), "We Know the Way" and "Shiny" (Clement). "How Far I'll Go" was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song.

Reception

Moana received widespread acclaim for its animation, music, humour and respectful, research-driven portrayal of Polynesian culture, and grossed about $690 million worldwide. It was nominated for two Academy Awards, including Best Animated Feature. A sequel, Moana 2, was released in 2024, and a live-action remake has been announced.
