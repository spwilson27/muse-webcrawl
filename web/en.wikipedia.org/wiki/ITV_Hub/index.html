ITV Hub

ITV Hub
Owner: ITV plc
Launched: 2008 (as ITV Catch Up)
Closed: 2022 (replaced by ITVX)

The ITV Hub was the online video-on-demand service of the British broadcaster ITV, offering catch-up television and live streaming of ITV's channels, including ITV (ITV1), ITV2, ITV3, ITV4, ITVBe and CITV. Launched in 2008 as ITV Catch Up and later known as ITV Player, it was rebranded as ITV Hub in 2015.

Service

The service let viewers watch programmes for up to 30 days after broadcast on computers, mobile devices, smart TVs and games consoles, funded mainly by advertising; an optional Hub+ subscription removed adverts and allowed downloads. Scottish viewers were directed to the STV Player for STV content. In December 2022 the ITV Hub was replaced by the expanded streaming service ITVX.
