The 2022 FA Cup Final was an association football match played at Wembley Stadium on 14 May 2022 between Chelsea and Liverpool, deciding the winners of the 2021–22 FA Cup. It was the 150th anniversary final of the competition and a repeat of that season's EFL Cup final between the same clubs.

Match summary

As in the EFL Cup final three months earlier, the match finished goalless after 90 minutes and extra time despite chances at both ends, with goalkeepers Édouard Mendy and Alisson Becker in outstanding form. Liverpool won the ensuing penalty shoot-out 6–5: after César Azpilicueta and Sadio Mané had missed, Mason Mount's kick was saved by Alisson and Kostas Tsimikas converted the winning penalty.

Significance

The victory gave Liverpool their eighth FA Cup and kept them in contention for an unprecedented quadruple late into May, though they ultimately finished runners-up in the Premier League and Champions League. It was the first FA Cup final to end goalless since 2005.
