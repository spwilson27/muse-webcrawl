Argentina national football team records and statistics

[Image: Argentina national football team — The Argentina squad celebrating the 2022 FIFA World Cup title]

The following is a list of the records and statistics of the Argentina national football team, one of the most successful national teams in world football, with three FIFA World Cup titles (1978, 1986, 2022), a record number of Copa América titles, and multiple Olympic and Confederations Cup honours.

Lionel Messi surpassed Javier Mascherano as Argentina's most-capped player during the 2021 Copa América and became the first Argentine to reach 100 international goals, in a friendly against Curaçao in March 2023. Diego Maradona (91 caps, 34 goals) and Gabriel Batistuta (56 goals) remain among the most celebrated figures in the team's history.

Team records

  • Biggest win: Argentina 12–0 Ecuador, 1942 South American Championship.
  • Longest unbeaten run: 36 matches (2019–2022), ended by Saudi Arabia at the 2022 World Cup.
  • World Cup finals: six appearances (1930, 1978, 1986, 1990, 2014, 2022), three titles.
  • Copa América titles: 16, the most of any nation, including 2021 and 2024.

Head-to-head records

Argentina's oldest and fiercest rivalry is with Uruguay, dating to 1901 and one of the most-played fixtures in international football. The rivalry with Brazil, the Superclásico de las Américas, and meetings with England, Germany and the Netherlands also feature prominently in the team's statistical history.
