Stridsvagn 122

Type: Main battle tank
Place of origin: Germany / Sweden
In service: 1997–present
Used by: Swedish Army
Main armament: 120 mm Rheinmetall L/44 smoothbore gun
Crew: 4

The Stridsvagn 122 (Strv 122; English: Tank 122) is a Swedish main battle tank based on the German Leopard 2A5. Like the Leopard 2A5, the Strv 122 is based on the German Leopard 2 Improved variant and features increased internal and external protection. Externally, the vehicle can be distinguished from the Leopard 2A5 by the French GALIX smoke dispensers, a different storage box, and thicker crew hatches.

A total of 120 Strv 122 were delivered to the Swedish Army, 29 manufactured in Germany by Krauss-Maffei Wegmann and the remaining 91 in Sweden by Land Systems Hägglunds and Bofors. The tank entered service in 1997 and became the main battle tank of Sweden, replacing the Stridsvagn 103 and supplementing the Stridsvagn 121 (Leopard 2A4).

Features

The Strv 122 has reinforced armour on the turret top and forward hull, improved command and control systems (the Swedish Tank Command and Control System, TCCS), and the ability to fire Swedish-developed 120 mm ammunition including the slpprj 95 APFSDS-T round. It is considered one of the best-protected Leopard 2 variants in service.

Upgrades and transfers

Sweden has planned upgrades to part of its fleet, designated Strv 123A, incorporating a new gun and modernised electronics. In 2023 Sweden donated ten Stridsvagn 122 tanks to Ukraine following the Russian invasion, where they saw combat service.
