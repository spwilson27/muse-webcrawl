Iram of the Pillars

Iram of the Pillars (Arabic: إِرَم ذَات ٱلْعِمَاد, Iram dhāt al-ʿimād), also called Ubar or Wubar, is a legendary lost city of Arabia mentioned in the Quran. According to the Quran, Iram was built by the tribe of ʿĀd and destroyed by God for their arrogance and disobedience.

The legend

The Quran (Surah Al-Fajr 89:6–8) speaks of "Iram, of the lofty pillars, the like of which were not created in the lands." Later tradition elaborated that King Shaddad, warned by the prophet Hud, sought to build a paradise on earth, and the city was swallowed by the desert after a catastrophic storm or cry from the sky.

Search for Ubar

Explorer Bertram Thomas and later T. E. Lawrence popularised the idea of a lost "Atlantis of the Sands." In the early 1990s, a team including Nicholas Clapp and using NASA satellite imagery announced the discovery of ruins at Shisr in Oman, on ancient frankincense trade routes, as a candidate for Ubar — an identification that remains debated among archaeologists.

In popular culture

Iram features in the One Thousand and One Nights, the fiction of H. P. Lovecraft, and the video game Uncharted 3: Drake's Deception.
