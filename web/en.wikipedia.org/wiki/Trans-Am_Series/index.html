Trans-Am Series

The Trans-Am Series presented by Pirelli is a sports car racing series held in North America. Founded in 1966, the series is known for competition between American "pony cars" and muscle cars such as the Ford Mustang, Chevrolet Camaro, and Dodge Challenger, alongside imported sports cars. The series is sanctioned by the Sports Car Club of America (SCCA) Pro Racing and is among the longest continuously operating road racing championships in the United States.

History

The Trans-American Sedan Championship was created in 1966 by SCCA president John Bishop. The early years became legendary for factory-backed battles between the Ford Mustang, Chevrolet Camaro, AMC Javelin, Dodge Challenger, and Plymouth Barracuda, with drivers such as Mark Donohue, Parnelli Jones, George Follmer, and Dan Gurney. The "win on Sunday, sell on Monday" era directly influenced the showroom pony car wars, and models such as the Chevrolet Camaro Z/28 and Ford Mustang Boss 302 were created specifically to homologate equipment for the series.

The series evolved through the 1970s and 1980s toward tube-frame silhouette cars, with marques such as Porsche, Jaguar, and Chevrolet Corvette competing, and drivers including Paul Newman, who raced extensively in the series, and Willy T. Ribbs. After a period of decline, the series was revived in 2009 and reorganized under the Trans Am Race Company, featuring modern TA-class tube-frame cars with 850-horsepower V8 engines.

Classes

  • TA — the premier class: purpose-built tube-frame silhouette cars powered by high-output V8 engines, with bodywork resembling the Mustang, Camaro, Challenger, and Corvette.
  • TA2 — a cost-controlled class of tube-frame cars styled after the Mustang, Camaro, and Challenger, popular for close spec-style racing.
  • XGT, SGT and GT — production-based classes for sports cars and GT cars including exotics and American performance cars.

Notable champions and drivers

Champions have included Mark Donohue, Tommy Kendall, and Ernie Francis Jr., who became the series' most successful driver of the modern era. The series races at classic North American road courses including Road America, Watkins Glen, Circuit of the Americas, Sebring, and Mid-Ohio.
