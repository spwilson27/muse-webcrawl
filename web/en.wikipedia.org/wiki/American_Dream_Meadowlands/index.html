American Dream mall at the Meadowlands, New Jersey

American Dream is a large retail and entertainment complex at the Meadowlands Sports Complex in East Rutherford, New Jersey, United States. Originally proposed as Meadowlands Xanadu in 2003, the project endured years of construction delays, financing failures and changes of ownership before opening its first sections on 25 October 2019 under developer Triple Five Group.

Attractions

The complex, of roughly 3 million square feet, includes an indoor theme park (Nickelodeon Universe), an indoor water park (DreamWorks Water Park), the Big Snow indoor ski slope, an NHL-regulation ice rink, a Legoland Discovery Center, observation wheel, and hundreds of retail stores and restaurants. The project has faced persistent financial difficulties, including a large municipal bond default in 2022, though it remains open and a major visitor draw near MetLife Stadium.
