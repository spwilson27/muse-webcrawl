Registers of Scotland

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Registers of Scotland (RoS) is the non-ministerial department of the Scottish Government responsible for compiling and maintaining records relating to property and other legal documents. It is headed by the Keeper of the Registers of Scotland, an office held since 2018 by Jennifer Henderson. Its main office is at Meadowbank House in Edinburgh, with an additional office in Glasgow.

History

The keeping of public registers in Scotland dates back centuries. The General Register of Sasines, established in 1617, was the first national land register of its kind in the world, recording deeds transferring ownership of land. In 1979 the Land Register of Scotland began operation under the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979, gradually replacing the Sasine Register with a map-based register of title. The Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012 modernised the system, and the Keeper is working toward completing the Land Register so that all land in Scotland is registered.

Registers maintained

Registers of Scotland maintains, among others, the Land Register of Scotland, the Register of Sasines, the Books of Council and Session, the Register of Inhibitions, the Register of Deeds and Probative Writs, the Register of Community Interests in Land, and the Register of Persons Holding a Controlled Interest in Land, launched in 2022. Its online services include ScotLIS (Scotland's Land Information Service), which provides public access to land and property information.
