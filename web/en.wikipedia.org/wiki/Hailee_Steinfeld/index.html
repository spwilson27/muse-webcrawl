Hailee Steinfeld (born December 11, 1996) is an American actress and singer. She gained wide recognition for her role as Mattie Ross in the Coen brothers' western True Grit (2010), for which she was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress at the age of 14.

Career

Following True Grit, Steinfeld appeared in films including Ender's Game (2013), Begin Again (2013), Pitch Perfect 2 (2015) and Pitch Perfect 3 (2017), and starred in the coming-of-age film The Edge of Seventeen (2016), earning a Golden Globe nomination. She played Charlie Watson in Bumblebee (2018) and voiced Gwen Stacy / Spider-Woman in the animated Spider-Verse films (2018–present).

On television, Steinfeld starred as Emily Dickinson in the Apple TV+ series Dickinson (2019–2021) and joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Kate Bishop in the Disney+ series Hawkeye (2021); she also voices Vi in the animated series Arcane (2021–2024).

As a recording artist, she released the EP Haiz (2015) and singles including "Love Myself", "Starving" (with Grey, featuring Zedd), "Most Girls" and "Let Me Go".
