Yokozuna (sumo)

Image: Yokozuna (sumo)

Yokozuna (横綱) is the highest rank in professional sumo wrestling. The term literally refers to the heavy white rope (tsuna) worn around the waist of a wrestler at this rank, which symbolises his status.

Promotion

Promotion to yokozuna is decided by the Japan Sumo Association's Yokozuna Deliberation Council. A candidate is typically an ōzeki who has won two consecutive championships (yūshō) or an equivalent performance. Beyond results, a yokozuna must demonstrate dignity and grace (hinkaku) befitting the rank.

Role and retirement

Unlike other ranks, a yokozuna can never be demoted. If his results fall below expectations he is expected to retire. The yokozuna performs a distinctive ring-entering ceremony (dohyō-iri) wearing the tsuna, accompanied by attendants. Historically there have been only just over seventy yokozuna since the rank was formalised.
