CAC Next 20

The CAC Next 20 is a stock market index of Euronext Paris, the French securities market. It consists of the 20 most liquid stocks listed on Euronext Paris after those included in the benchmark CAC 40 index of the 40 largest listed companies. Its constituents are therefore typically candidates for future promotion into the CAC 40, and stocks demoted from the CAC 40 often join it. The index was launched on 3 January 2005 with a base level of 3,000 points as of 31 December 2004. Its composition is reviewed quarterly by Euronext's index steering committee.

Selection

Companies are selected based on free-float market capitalisation and trading liquidity among Euronext Paris listings not in the CAC 40. Together the CAC 40 and CAC Next 20 form the CAC Large 60; adding the CAC Mid 60 gives the CAC Mid & Small, and all Paris-listed stocks tracked by Euronext form the CAC All-Tradable.

Constituents

Members change regularly at quarterly reviews. Over its history the index has included companies such as Bureau Veritas, Edenred, Sartorius Stedim Biotech, Dassault Aviation, Amundi, Eurofins Scientific, and Gecina, several of which were later promoted to the CAC 40 — illustrating the index's role as the CAC 40's "waiting room".
