Home Retail Group plc was a home and general merchandise retailer in the United Kingdom and Ireland. It was the parent company of Argos and Homebase, and also owned the Habitat furniture brand in the UK. The business was formed as the retail arm of the conglomerate GUS plc and was demerged as a separately listed company on the London Stock Exchange in October 2006, entering the FTSE 100 Index.

Operations

Argos, the group's largest business, was a catalogue and online retailer selling general merchandise through a network of stores and its website, known for its catalogue showrooms and later for same-day collection and Fast Track delivery services. Homebase was a home improvement and garden retailer competing with B&Q. In 2011 the group acquired the UK and Ireland rights to the Habitat brand after the furniture chain entered administration.

Sale and break-up

In February 2016 the group sold Homebase to the Australian company Wesfarmers for £340 million. In April 2016 the supermarket chain Sainsbury's agreed to acquire Home Retail Group, chiefly for Argos, in a deal valued at about £1.4 billion; the takeover was completed in September 2016. Argos was integrated into Sainsbury's, with many standalone Argos stores replaced by Argos concessions inside Sainsbury's supermarkets.
