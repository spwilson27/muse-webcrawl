Threefold Training

ComponentsVirtue (sila), concentration (samadhi), wisdom (panna)
Related pathNoble Eightfold Path

The Threefold Training (Pali: tisikkha) is a framework in Buddhism that organizes the path to enlightenment into three higher trainings: higher virtue (adhisila-sikkha), higher mind or concentration (adhicitta-sikkha), and higher wisdom (adhipanna-sikkha).

Contents

Virtue consists of right speech, right action, and right livelihood; concentration consists of right effort, right mindfulness, and right concentration; and wisdom consists of right view and right resolve. In this way the eight factors of the Noble Eightfold Path are fully contained within the three trainings.

Development

The trainings are understood as mutually supporting and progressively deepening: ethical conduct provides the foundation for stable meditation, and concentrated meditation in turn gives rise to insight into impermanence, suffering, and non-self, which culminates in liberation from the cycle of rebirth.
