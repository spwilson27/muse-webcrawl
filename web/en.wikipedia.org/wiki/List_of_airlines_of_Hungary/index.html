List of airlines of Hungary

This is a list of airlines operating in Hungary. Hungary's former flag carrier, Malév Hungarian Airlines, ceased operations in February 2012 after 66 years. Since then the Hungarian market has been dominated by low-cost carrier Wizz Air, founded in Hungary in 2003 and now one of Europe's largest ultra-low-cost airlines, along with foreign carriers at Budapest Ferenc Liszt International Airport.
