Coruscant

Coruscant is a fictional planet in the Star Wars universe. An ecumenopolis — a city-covered planet — it serves as the capital of the Galactic Republic and later of the Galactic Empire, home to the Galactic Senate, the Jedi Temple and a population counted in the trillions. Coruscant first appeared on screen in the 1997 Special Edition of Return of the Jedi and was central to the prequel trilogy.

Coruscant

Universe: Star Wars

Type: Ecumenopolis (city planet)

Role: Capital of the Republic and Empire

Depiction

The planet's surface is covered by a single planet-wide city organised into thousands of vertical levels, with the wealthy living in towers above the clouds and an extensive underworld below. Locations include the Senate District, the Jedi Temple, the Works industrial zone and Level 1313. The name derives from the Latin coruscans, meaning "glittering" — the planet's city lights glitter from space.

Development

The concept drew on earlier science-fiction city worlds such as Isaac Asimov's Trantor. The planet, named "Imperial City" in early drafts, was developed extensively for The Phantom Menace (1999).
