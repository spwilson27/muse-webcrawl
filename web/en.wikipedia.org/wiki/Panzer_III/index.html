Panzer III

The Panzerkampfwagen III, commonly known as the Panzer III, was a German medium tank developed in the 1930s and used extensively in World War II. The official designation was Sd.Kfz. 141.

Design and service

Intended as the Wehrmacht's main tank-fighter, it was first armed with a 37 mm gun and later up-gunned to 50 mm and long-barrelled 50 mm weapons, with armour increased over successive variants (Ausf. A through N). It fought in Poland, France, North Africa, and on the Eastern Front.

Legacy

By 1943 the Panzer III was outmatched as a gun tank, but its chassis remained valuable: turretless versions formed the highly successful Sturmgeschütz III assault gun, the most-produced German armoured fighting vehicle of the war. About 5,700 Panzer IIIs were built.
