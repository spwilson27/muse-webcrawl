The Line (Arabic: ذا لاين) is a planned smart city in Neom, Saudi Arabia, designed as a single linear structure originally projected to run 170 kilometres across the desert, standing 500 metres tall and 200 metres wide, clad in a mirrored facade. Announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in January 2021, it is a flagship project of Saudi Vision 2030.

Concept

The design promises a car-free city in which all daily necessities lie within a five-minute walk, districts are stacked vertically, and a high-speed rail line would carry residents end to end in 20 minutes. The project claims it will run entirely on renewable energy and preserve 95 percent of Neom's land for nature.

Development and criticism

Construction began with earthworks in the early 2020s, but reports from 2024 indicated the initial phase had been scaled back dramatically, with only a few kilometres expected by 2030. The project has drawn criticism over its feasibility, immense cost (estimated in the hundreds of billions of dollars or more), environmental claims, and the displacement of members of the Huwaitat tribe from the site.
