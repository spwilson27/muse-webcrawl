The New York State Canal Corporation is a New York State public-benefit corporation responsible for operating and maintaining the New York State Canal System, including the Erie Canal. Since 2017 it has been a subsidiary of the New York Power Authority (NYPA).

History

The Canal Corporation was created in 1992 when management of the state canals was transferred from the Department of Transportation to the New York State Thruway Authority. Governor Andrew Cuomo's 2016 reorganisation moved the corporation to the New York Power Authority effective 1 January 2017, reflecting the canals' close relationship with hydroelectric power and water management.

Operations

The corporation maintains the canal system's locks, dams, channels and movable bridges, sets the navigation season (typically mid-May to mid-October), and promotes recreational and commercial use and tourism along the canal corridor, including the Erie Canalway National Heritage Corridor.
