Hovgården

LocationAdelsö, Lake Mälaren, Sweden
PeriodViking Age
StatusUNESCO World Heritage Site (with Birka)

Hovgården is an archaeological site on the island of Adelsö in Lake Mälaren, Sweden, across the strait from the Viking Age trading town of Birka. It is interpreted as the royal estate from which kings supervised the trade at Birka.

Archaeology

The site contains large burial mounds, including several "king's mounds", the foundations of a great hall, and a harbour. A rune stone (U 11) records that it was commissioned by Tolir, a steward of King Haakon. Excavations have also found the remains of a medieval brick palace, Alsnö hus, where the Ordinance of Alsnö was issued around 1280.

World Heritage

Hovgården was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1993 together with nearby Birka, as an exceptionally preserved example of the Vikings' trading networks.
