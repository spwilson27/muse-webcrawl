Jnanpith Award

The Jnanpith Award is the oldest and highest Indian literary award, presented annually by the Bharatiya Jnanpith to an author for outstanding contribution to literature. It was instituted in 1961 and first awarded in 1965.

Details

The award is open to Indian writers in the languages listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution, as well as English since 2013. It carries a cash prize, a citation and a bronze replica of Saraswati, the goddess of learning.

Recipients

The first recipient was Malayalam poet G. Sankara Kurup for Odakkuzhal. Other notable laureates include Sumitranandan Pant, Mahadevi Varma, U. R. Ananthamurthy, M. T. Vasudevan Nair, Amitav Ghosh — the first English-language winner — and Damodar Mauzo.
