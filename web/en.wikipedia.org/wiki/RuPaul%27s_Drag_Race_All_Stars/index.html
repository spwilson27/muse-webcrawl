RuPaul's Drag Race All Stars

Genre: Reality competition
Presented by: RuPaul
Country of origin: United States
Original release: October 22, 2012 – present

RuPaul's Drag Race All Stars is an American reality competition television series and a spin-off of RuPaul's Drag Race, in which former contestants return to compete for a place in the Drag Race Hall of Fame and a cash prize.

Format

Returning queens compete in challenges similar to the parent series. From the second season onward, the format changed so that the top contestants of each episode lip-sync for the win, with the winner choosing which of the bottom contestants to eliminate. Season 7 (2022) featured an all-winners cast competing for the title "Queen of All Queens".

Series overview

The first season aired on Logo in 2012 and was won by Chad Michaels. Subsequent winners include Alaska, Trixie Mattel and Monét X Change (joint winners of season 4), Shea Couleé, Kylie Sonique Love, Jinkx Monsoon, Jimbo, and Angeria Paris VanMicheals. The series moved to VH1 and later to Paramount+ and MTV.
