Cyprus Government Railway


Image placeholder: Cyprus Government Railway

The Cyprus Government Railway was a 2 ft 6 in (762 mm) narrow gauge railway that operated in Cyprus from 1905 to 1951. At its greatest extent it ran for about 76 miles (122 km) from Famagusta on the east coast, through Nicosia, to Morphou Bay in the west.

History

Proposed under Ottoman rule and built after the British administration began, the railway opened in stages: the Famagusta–Nicosia section in 1905 and the Nicosia–Morphou section in 1907, with a later extension to Evrychou serving the Troodos foothills and the Skouriotissa copper mines.

Closure

The railway carried passengers, mail, timber, and minerals but never made a profit in its later years and faced road competition. Losses led to closure; the last train ran on 31 December 1951. Some locomotives and buildings survive, including a station building at Evrychou used as a railway museum.
