Exor (company)

Exor N.V. is a Dutch holding company controlled by the Agnelli family through Giovanni Agnelli B.V. Based in Amsterdam and listed on Euronext Amsterdam, Exor is one of Europe's largest diversified holding companies, with a net asset value of roughly €40 billion. Its roots go back to the founding of Fiat in Turin in 1899 by Giovanni Agnelli.

Portfolio

Exor's principal investments include controlling or leading stakes in Ferrari, Stellantis (formed by the merger of Fiat Chrysler Automobiles and PSA Group), CNH Industrial, Iveco Group, and Juventus Football Club. It also holds stakes in Philips, Christian Louboutin, Institut Mérieux, The Economist Group, and Shang Xia, and operates the venture arms Exor Ventures and Lingotto investment funds.

History

The holding grew out of IFI, founded in 1927, and took the Exor name in 2009 when IFI merged with its subsidiary IFIL. In 2015 Exor acquired the reinsurer PartnerRe for about $6.9 billion, later selling it to Covéa in 2022. John Elkann, great-great-grandson of Fiat's founder, serves as chief executive and chairman. In 2016 the company moved its legal seat to the Netherlands, and in 2022 it delisted from Milan in favour of Amsterdam alone.
