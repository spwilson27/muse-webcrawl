SOCAR

Industry: Oil and gas
Founded: 13 September 1992
Headquarters: Baku, Azerbaijan
Owner: Government of Azerbaijan
Products: Petroleum, natural gas, petrochemicals

The State Oil Company of the Republic of Azerbaijan (Azerbaijani: Azərbaycan Respublikası Dövlət Neft Şirkəti), commonly known by its acronym SOCAR, is a wholly state-owned national oil and gas company headquartered in Baku, Azerbaijan. The company produces oil and natural gas from onshore and offshore fields in the Azerbaijani segment of the Caspian Sea.

SOCAR operates refineries, pipelines, and petrochemical plants in Azerbaijan and has expanded internationally, with trading, retail, and midstream operations in countries including Turkey, Georgia, Ukraine, Romania, Switzerland, and Austria. Its headquarters, the SOCAR Tower in Baku, is one of the tallest buildings in the Caucasus.

History

SOCAR was established by presidential decree in 1992, consolidating Azerbaijan's Soviet-era oil enterprises Azerineft and Azarneftkimya following independence from the Soviet Union. In 1994 it became a party to the "Contract of the Century" with a BP-led consortium to develop the Azeri–Chirag–Gunashli offshore field complex, the foundation of Azerbaijan's modern oil industry.

Operations

The company is a major shareholder in export pipelines including the Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan oil pipeline and the Southern Gas Corridor (TANAP and TAP), which carries Azerbaijani gas to Europe. In Turkey, SOCAR built the STAR Refinery and operates the Petkim petrochemical complex. Its trading arm, SOCAR Trading, is based in Geneva.
