Draw (sports)

A draw (called a tie in North America) is an inconclusive result in a sport or game, where no side is declared the winner because the competitors finish with equal scores or comparable achievements. Some sports allow draws as final results, while others use tiebreakers, extra time, or shoot-outs to force a winner.

By sport

In association football league matches, draws are permitted and each team typically receives one point. In chess, a game may be drawn by agreement, stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule, or insufficient material. In cricket, a draw differs from a tie: a drawn match is unfinished when time expires, while a tie means both sides completed their innings with equal runs. Boxing and mixed martial arts bouts judged by scorecards can end in unanimous, majority, or split draws.

Tiebreakers

Many knockout tournaments eliminate draws through extra time and penalty shoot-outs; chess uses rapid and blitz play-offs, and tennis uses tiebreak games and sets.
