Gemological Institute of America

The Gemological Institute of America (GIA) is a nonprofit institute dedicated to research and education in the field of gemology and the jewelry arts. Founded in 1931, GIA's mission is to protect all buyers and sellers of gemstones by setting and maintaining the standards used to evaluate gemstone quality. The institute does so through research, gem identification and diamond grading services, and a variety of educational programs.

History

GIA was established in 1931 by Robert M. Shipley, a retail jeweler who recognized the need for professional training in gemstones. Shipley had taken courses through the National Association of Goldsmiths of Great Britain and founded the institute in Los Angeles to bring comparable education to American jewelers. Under his direction, GIA developed correspondence courses in gemology and began issuing laboratory reports.

In the 1950s, GIA developed the International Diamond Grading System and the "Four Cs" framework — color, clarity, cut, and carat weight — created by Shipley and popularized under his successor Richard T. Liddicoat. The D-to-Z color scale and the Flawless-to-I3 clarity scale became industry standards adopted worldwide.

Grading and services

GIA is known for its diamond grading reports, which describe a diamond's characteristics using standardized methodology. Its laboratories, located in major diamond centers around the world, also issue reports for colored gemstones and pearls. GIA developed the first internationally accepted grading scales and continues to train jewelers and gemologists through its Graduate Gemologist diploma and other programs offered on campuses in several countries and online.

Research

The institute conducts ongoing research on gemstone treatments, synthetic gems, and identification techniques, publishing findings in its quarterly journal Gems & Gemology, published since 1934. GIA researchers have made significant contributions to detecting HPHT- and CVD-grown synthetic diamonds and treated colored stones.
