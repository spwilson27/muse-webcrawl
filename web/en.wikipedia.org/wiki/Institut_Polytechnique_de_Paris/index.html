Institut Polytechnique de Paris

Established: 2019
Type: Public research university (grand établissement)
Location: Palaiseau, Essonne, France
Students: ~10,000

The Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris) is a public technological university located in Palaiseau, France. It was established on 31 May 2019 as a federation of five prestigious French engineering schools — grandes écoles — centred on the Plateau de Saclay research and technology cluster south of Paris.

Member schools

The founding member schools are:

  • École Polytechnique ("l'X"), founded in 1794, France's most famous engineering school;
  • ENSTA Paris (École nationale supérieure de techniques avancées);
  • ENSAE Paris, specialising in statistics, economics and data science;
  • Télécom Paris, specialising in information technology;
  • Télécom SudParis, based in Évry.

Academics and research

Institut Polytechnique de Paris offers bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes in science and technology, together with the member schools' engineering degrees (diplôme d'ingénieur). Its research is organised into departments covering fields such as mechanics, computer science, mathematics, physics, economics, and electronics, and it hosts interdisciplinary centres on artificial intelligence (Hi! PARIS, shared with HEC Paris), energy and climate, and biomedical engineering. The institute quickly entered the top tier of international rankings, placing among the world's top 50 universities in the QS World University Rankings.

History

The institute grew out of plans to consolidate the schools of the Saclay plateau into a world-class science and technology university capable of competing internationally. After an earlier project to merge into the University of Paris-Saclay stalled, École Polytechnique and its partners formed a separate collegiate institution, formally created by decree in 2019.
