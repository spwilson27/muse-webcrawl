Thena

Thena is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Jack Kirby, she first appeared in Eternals #5 (November 1976). She is a member of the Eternals, a race of superhumans created by the Celestials, and the daughter of Zuras, leader of the Eternals of Olympia.

Fictional biography

Born Azura — later renamed Thena in honour of the goddess Athena, with whom she formed a pact — she is a warrior-scholar who has lived among humanity for millennia, at times acting as Athena's representative. She formed a close bond, and at times a Uni-Mind union, with the Deviant warlord Kro, and later became Prime Eternal after her father's death, though the role has passed between her and Ikaris. In some storylines she suffers from the Mahd Wy'ry, a breakdown of the mind caused by the Eternals' immense age.

In other media

Thena appears in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Eternals (2021), portrayed by Angelina Jolie.
