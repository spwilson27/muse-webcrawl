Chainsaw Man (Japanese: チェンソーマン) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Tatsuki Fujimoto. Its first part, the Public Safety Arc, was serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump from December 2018 to December 2020; its second part began serialization in Shōnen Jump+ in July 2022.

Plot

The story follows Denji, a young devil hunter living in poverty who works to pay off his deceased father's debt to the yakuza. After being betrayed and killed, he is revived by his pet devil Pochita, who becomes his heart, transforming him into a human-devil hybrid with chainsaws protruding from his head and arms. Denji joins the Public Safety Devil Hunters, a government agency fighting devils, under the command of the mysterious Makima.

Characters

Key characters include Denji, the impulsive protagonist; Makima, a high-ranking Public Safety hunter; Power, the Blood Fiend; and Aki Hayakawa, a stoic devil hunter contracted with several devils.

Reception

The manga has been widely acclaimed for its unconventional storytelling, violence, and dark humor, winning the Harvey Award for Best Manga multiple times. An anime television series adaptation by MAPPA premiered in 2022, and a film covering the Reze Arc was released in 2025.
