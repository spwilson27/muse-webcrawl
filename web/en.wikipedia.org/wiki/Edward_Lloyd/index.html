Edward Lloyd

Edward Lloyd (c. 1648 – 1713) was a Welsh-born proprietor of Lloyd's Coffee House in London, the meeting place that gave its name to the marine insurance market Lloyd's of London and to Lloyd's List, one of the world's oldest continuously published journals.

Coffee house

Lloyd opened his coffee house on Tower Street around 1686 and moved to Lombard Street in 1691. It became the favoured gathering place of merchants, shipowners, and underwriters, who valued the shipping intelligence Lloyd gathered and published for his customers, laying the foundations of the modern marine insurance market.

Legacy

Although Edward Lloyd had no direct connection with the later insurance corporation's management, his name endured in Lloyd's of London, Lloyd's Register, and Lloyd's List, which began as a news sheet for the coffee house clientele.
