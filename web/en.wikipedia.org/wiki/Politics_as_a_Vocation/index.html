Politics as a Vocation

Politics as a Vocation
Author: Max Weber
Delivered: 1919 (lecture, Munich)
Original title: Politik als Beruf

"Politics as a Vocation" (German: Politik als Beruf) is a lecture given by the German sociologist Max Weber in Munich in January 1919, in the aftermath of the German Revolution. It is one of Weber's most famous works.

Contents

Weber defines the state as the human community that successfully claims the monopoly of the legitimate use of physical force within a given territory. He distinguishes politicians who live "for" politics from those who live "off" it, and contrasts the ethic of conviction (Gesinnungsethik) with the ethic of responsibility (Verantwortungsethik), arguing that a mature politician must combine passion with perspective and a sense of responsibility for consequences.

Legacy

The lecture's definition of the state and its discussion of charismatic, traditional, and legal-rational authority remain standard reference points in political science.
