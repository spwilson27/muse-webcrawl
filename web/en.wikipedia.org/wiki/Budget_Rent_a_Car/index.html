Budget Rent a Car

Budget is one of the world's largest car rental brands.

Budget Rent a Car System, Inc. is an American car rental company founded in Los Angeles, California in 1958 by Morris Mirkin. It pioneered value-priced car rental — its original rates of $5 a day and 5 cents a mile undercut competitors and inspired the name "Budget". The company grew through franchising into one of the world's largest rental brands, with thousands of locations in more than 100 countries, many at airports.

Ownership

Budget passed through several owners in the late 20th century, including Transamerica and Ford Motor Company. In 2002 it was acquired out of bankruptcy by Cendant Corporation, which combined it with Avis; Cendant's vehicle rental division became the Avis Budget Group in 2006. Budget today operates alongside Avis and the car-sharing service Zipcar within that group, positioned as the value-oriented brand of the portfolio.

Operations

Budget rents cars and trucks to leisure and business travellers, with a substantial franchise network and a truck rental business in the United States. Its long-running advertising slogan was "We try harder"–style value positioning aimed at cost-conscious renters; later campaigns used the tagline "Get into the Budget spirit".
