List of role-playing video games

This is a comprehensive index of commercial role-playing video games (RPGs), sorted chronologically. The genre descends from tabletop role-playing games such as Dungeons & Dragons and early mainframe titles like dnd (1974) and Rogue (1980), and divides broadly into Western RPGs (WRPGs), Japanese RPGs (JRPGs), action RPGs, tactical RPGs and massively multiplayer online RPGs (MMORPGs).

Selected notable titles by era

Early years (1975–1984)

  • dnd (1974/75), pedit5, Dungeon
  • Akalabeth: World of Doom (1979)
  • Rogue (1980)
  • Wizardry: Proving Grounds of the Mad Overlord (1981)
  • Ultima series (1981 onward)
  • The Bard's Tale (1985)

Golden age (1985–1994)

  • Dragon Quest (1986) and Final Fantasy (1987) — foundations of the JRPG
  • Phantasy Star (1987)
  • Pool of Radiance (1988) — first Gold Box game
  • Dungeon Master (1987)
  • Ultima VII: The Black Gate (1992)
  • Secret of Mana (1993)
  • Chrono Trigger (1995, just after this period)

3D era (1995–2004)

  • Final Fantasy VII (1997)
  • Fallout (1997) and Fallout 2 (1998)
  • Baldur's Gate (1998) and Baldur's Gate II (2000)
  • Planescape: Torment (1999)
  • Diablo (1997) and Diablo II (2000) — action RPGs
  • The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind (2002)
  • Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (2003)
  • Pokémon series (1996 onward)
  • EverQuest (1999), Final Fantasy XI (2002), World of Warcraft (2004) — MMORPGs

Modern era (2005–present)

  • The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion (2006) and Skyrim (2011)
  • Mass Effect trilogy (2007–2012)
  • The Witcher series (2007–2015)
  • Dark Souls (2011) and Elden Ring (2022)
  • Persona 5 (2016)
  • Divinity: Original Sin II (2017)
  • Baldur's Gate 3 (2023)

Subgenres

  • Action role-playing game
  • Tactical role-playing game
  • Roguelike
  • MMORPG
  • Dungeon crawler
