Breaking Atoms

Breaking Atoms

Artist: Main Source

Released: 23 July 1991

Genre: Hip hop

Label: Wild Pitch

Breaking Atoms is the debut studio album by Canadian-American hip hop group Main Source, released in 1991 on Wild Pitch Records. The album is widely regarded as a landmark of golden-age hip hop, noted for Large Professor's innovative sampling and production.

Content

The album features the tracks "Looking at the Front Door", "Just Hangin' Out", and "Live at the Barbeque", the latter of which marked the debut recording appearance of a young Nas. The production makes extensive use of samples from soul, funk, and jazz records.

Reception and legacy

Breaking Atoms is frequently cited by critics and producers as one of the most influential hip hop albums of its era, and Large Professor's work on it helped shape the sound of 1990s East Coast hip hop.
