SKE48
Origin: Sakae, Nagoya, Japan
Years active: 2008–present
Label: Avex Trax / Nippon Crown
Producer: Yasushi Akimoto
Sister group of: AKB48

SKE48 is a Japanese idol girl group produced by Yasushi Akimoto, based in Sakae, Nagoya. Formed in 2008 as the first sister group of AKB48, SKE48 performs at its own dedicated theatre in the Sunshine Sakae building and is known for energetic, highly synchronised dance performances.

History

Auditions for the first generation were held in mid-2008, and the group debuted that October. SKE48 made its major-label single debut in 2009 with "Tsuyoki Mono yo". Members such as Jurina Matsui — who was selected for AKB48 singles while still in elementary-school age range at her debut — and Rena Matsui became nationally prominent; the pairing was nicknamed "W Matsui". The group has released a long run of chart-topping singles in Japan, including "Pareo wa Emerald", "Kataomoi Finally" and "Coquettish Juutai Chuu".

Structure

Like AKB48, SKE48 is divided into teams (Team S, Team KII and Team E) plus trainees (kenkyuusei), and has participated in AKB48 Group events such as the general elections and rock-paper-scissors tournaments.
