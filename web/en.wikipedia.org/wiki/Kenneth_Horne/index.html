Kenneth Horne (27 February 1907 – 14 February 1969) was an English comedian and BBC radio broadcaster, remembered as the host and straight man of the radio comedy series Beyond Our Ken (1958–1964) and Round the Horne (1965–1968).

Life and career

Horne worked in business — he was a sales manager for Tri-ang toys — and served in the RAF during the Second World War, broadcasting in the RAF show Merry-Go-Round. His urbane, unflappable delivery made him the perfect foil for Kenneth Williams, Hugh Paddick and Betty Marsden. Round the Horne, written by Barry Took and Marty Feldman, became a landmark of British comedy, famous for its wordplay, innuendo and characters such as Julian and Sandy, who spoke the gay slang Polari to an audience of millions.

Death and legacy

Horne suffered a heart attack in 1969 while hosting an awards ceremony and died aged 61, shortly after a fourth series of Round the Horne had been planned. The programmes remain widely repeated and are regarded as classics of radio comedy.
