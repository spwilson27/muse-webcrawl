Light Yagami (Japanese: 夜神 月, Yagami Raito) is the main protagonist of the manga series Death Note, created by Tsugumi Ohba and Takeshi Obata. A brilliant but bored Japanese high-school student, Light finds the Death Note, a supernatural notebook dropped by the shinigami Ryuk that allows its user to kill anyone whose name and face they know.

Character

Light is characterised as an exceptionally intelligent, athletic, and charismatic young man who is also popular with girls. Initially an idealistic top student with a strong sense of justice inherited from his police chief father, Soichiro Yagami, he becomes corrupted by the power of the Death Note. Adopting the public identity of "Kira" (from the Japanese pronunciation of "killer"), he sets out to purge the world of criminals and create a utopia over which he will reign as god.

Story role

As Kira, Light draws the attention of the enigmatic detective L and later of L's successors, Near and Mello, leading to an extended battle of wits. Light joins the investigation team hunting Kira himself, manipulating allies and enemies — including the Kira-worshipper Misa Amane and the prosecutor Teru Mikami — while concealing his identity. His plans ultimately unravel when Near exposes him in a final confrontation; a wounded Light dies after Ryuk writes his name in the Death Note, as the shinigami had promised at the start.

Reception

Light is frequently ranked among the most popular and complex villains and anti-heroes in anime and manga. Commentators have analysed the series' moral ambiguity through him: Ohba deliberately avoided making Light wholly sympathetic or wholly evil, presenting him as a character whose sense of justice becomes indistinguishable from megalomania.
