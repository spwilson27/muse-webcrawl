The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild is a 2017 action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch and Wii U. It is the nineteenth entry in The Legend of Zelda series and is set at the end of the series' timeline. The player controls Link, who awakens from a hundred-year slumber to defeat Calamity Ganon and save the kingdom of Hyrule.

Gameplay

Unlike most games in the series, Breath of the Wild presents a vast open world that can be explored freely with minimal direction. Link can climb almost any surface, glide from heights with a paraglider, cook food and elixirs from gathered ingredients, and use a physics-driven chemistry engine that lets systems—fire, wind, electricity, magnetism—interact in emergent ways. Four enormous mechanical beasts, the Divine Beasts, hold the game's main dungeon quests, while more than 120 small Shrines offer puzzles and combat trials. Weapons and shields degrade and break with use, encouraging experimentation and scavenging.

Plot

Ten thousand years before the game, Hyrule defeated Calamity Ganon using an army of autonomous machines. When Ganon returned a century before Link's awakening, he corrupted the machines and killed the four Champions chosen to pilot the Divine Beasts; Princess Zelda sealed herself in Hyrule Castle, holding Ganon at bay. Link, gravely wounded, was placed in the Shrine of Resurrection. Awakening without his memories, he must free the Divine Beasts, recover the Master Sword, and confront Ganon.

Development and release

Development began in 2010, with the team deliberately breaking series conventions established by Ocarina of Time. The game was introduced in 2014 and released on 3 March 2017 as a launch title for the Nintendo Switch and the final Nintendo-published game for the Wii U. Two downloadable content expansions followed in 2017.

Reception and legacy

Breath of the Wild received universal acclaim, with critics praising its open-world design, systemic gameplay, and sense of discovery. It won numerous awards, including Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2017, and is frequently cited among the greatest video games ever made. It is the best-selling Zelda title, with over 33 million copies sold across both platforms. A direct sequel, Tears of the Kingdom, was released in May 2023.
