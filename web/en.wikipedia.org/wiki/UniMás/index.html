UniMás

UniMás is an American Spanish-language free-to-air television network owned by TelevisaUnivision. Launched 14 January 2002 (as TeleFutura); rebranded as UniMás on 7 January 2013.

UniMás (stylized as UniMÁS) is an American Spanish-language free-to-air television network owned by TelevisaUnivision USA. It is the sister network of Univision, the leading Spanish-language broadcaster in the United States, and principally targets younger Hispanic American viewers, particularly bilingual Latinos aged 18–34.

History

The network launched on 14 January 2002 as TeleFutura, created after Univision Communications purchased the USA Broadcasting stations. TeleFutura differentiated itself from Univision with a counter-programming strategy featuring feature films dubbed into Spanish, sports, reality shows, and telenovelas from Latin American producers such as RCTV, Venevisión, and Caracol. Live sports, notably boxing and soccer, became signature offerings. On 7 January 2013 the network was rebranded as UniMás, with a greater emphasis on original programming — including series produced by Televisa and the popular reality competition Reto 4 Elementos — alongside Liga MX soccer.

Programming and distribution

UniMás airs dramas, reality series, sports, and films, and serves as an overflow outlet for Univision's soccer rights. It is distributed through owned-and-operated stations and affiliates in major US markets, reaching the large majority of Hispanic households, and is also available by subscription streaming through its parent company services.
