Winter Sonata (Korean: 겨울연가) is a 2002 South Korean television drama series directed by Yoon Seok-ho, the second installment of his season-themed "Endless Love" tetralogy after Autumn in My Heart. It stars Bae Yong-joon and Choi Ji-woo as former high-school sweethearts reunited ten years after an accident and a case of amnesia separated them.

Impact

The series is credited with igniting the Korean Wave (Hallyu) in Japan, where its broadcasts on NHK from 2003 made Bae Yong-joon — affectionately nicknamed "Yon-sama" — a cultural phenomenon and spurred tourism to filming locations such as Namiseom (Nami Island), where a statue of the lead couple stands. Winter Sonata boosted Korean cultural exports and is frequently cited as a turning point in South Korea–Japan cultural exchange.

Adaptations

The drama was adapted into an anime series in 2009, with Bae and Choi voicing their roles in Korean, as well as into a stage musical in both South Korea and Japan.
