OutRight Action International (formerly the International Gay and Lesbian Human Rights Commission, IGLHRC) is an international non-governmental organization that advocates for the human rights of lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, intersex and queer (LGBTIQ) people worldwide. Founded in 1990, it was the first organization of its kind to receive consultative status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC), granted in 2010.

Work

OutRight documents human rights abuses against LGBTIQ people, advocates at the United Nations and regional human rights bodies, supports frontline activists through grant-making and training, and produces research such as reports on conversion practices and on violence during crises. During the COVID-19 pandemic it ran an emergency fund for LGBTIQ organizations, raising several million dollars.

History

The organization was founded in 1990 by Julie Dorf and others as the International Gay and Lesbian Human Rights Commission. In 2015 it adopted the name OutRight Action International. Its annual Celebration of Courage gala and its OutSummit conference bring together activists from dozens of countries.
