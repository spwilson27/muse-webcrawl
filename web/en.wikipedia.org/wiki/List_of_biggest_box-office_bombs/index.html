List of biggest box-office bombs

In the film industry, a box-office bomb is a film that is considered highly unsuccessful or unprofitable during its theatrical run, often failing to recoup its production and marketing budget. This is a list of the biggest box-office failures, ranked by estimated worldwide losses. Because studios generally do not disclose exact figures, and because exhibitors keep a share of ticket revenue, the losses shown are estimates made by industry publications such as Bomb Report and Variety, typically assuming a studio receives roughly half of the worldwide gross.

Several of these failures had major consequences for their studios: Cutthroat Island bankrupted Carolco Pictures, Titan A.E. closed Fox Animation Studios, and the failure of Heaven's Gate (1980) — one of the most famous bombs of an earlier era — led to the sale of United Artists. Later ancillary revenue from home video, television, and streaming can reduce a film's eventual loss, but the theatrical losses listed here made these titles bywords for box-office disaster.
