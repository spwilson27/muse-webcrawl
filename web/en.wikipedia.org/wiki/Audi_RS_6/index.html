Audi RS 6

Audi RS 6
Manufacturer: Audi Sport GmbH
Production: 2002–present
Class: Executive performance car
Layout: Front-engine, all-wheel drive (quattro)
Related: Audi A6, Audi S6

The Audi RS 6 is a high-performance variant of the Audi A6 range, produced by Audi Sport GmbH since 2002. As with other Audi RS models, the name derives from RennSport ("racing sport"), and the car combines supercar-level performance with the practicality of a large estate car; the Avant estate is the best-known body style, though saloon versions were offered in earlier generations.

Generations

The first RS 6 (C5, 2002–2004) used a 4.2-litre twin-turbo V8 producing 450 PS, with a higher-output RS 6 Plus following. The C6 generation (2008–2010) adopted a 5.0-litre twin-turbo V10 derived from the Lamborghini Gallardo engine, producing 580 PS and making it one of the most powerful production Audis ever built. The C7 (2013–2018) returned to a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8, with the Performance model reaching 605 PS. The C8 (from 2019) keeps the 4.0-litre V8 with mild-hybrid assistance, producing 600 PS, and was for the first time officially sold in North America as the RS 6 Avant.

Performance

Successive RS 6 models have been able to accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in roughly 3.5–4.6 seconds depending on generation, with top speeds limited to 250 km/h (155 mph) unless optional dynamic packages raise the limit toward 305 km/h (190 mph). All versions use quattro all-wheel drive and adaptive suspension systems.
