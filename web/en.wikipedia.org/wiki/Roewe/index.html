Roewe is a vehicle marque created by the Chinese automaker SAIC Motor in 2006. The brand name is derived from the defunct British marque Rover, whose intellectual property SAIC acquired after the collapse of MG Rover in 2005; because Ford had exercised its option to buy the Rover name itself, SAIC launched its cars under the similar-sounding name Roewe, a portmanteau loosely honouring the original.

History

The first Roewe model, the 750, was based on the Rover 75 platform and launched in 2006. The range expanded with models such as the 350, 550, the RX5 crossover, and the Marvel X electric SUV. Roewe has emphasised new-energy vehicles, including plug-in hybrids and battery-electric models such as the Ei5 estate and the ER6 sedan. Roewe cars are sold primarily in China, though sister brand MG carries related products abroad.
