Princess Fiona

Princess Fiona is a fictional character in DreamWorks Animation's Shrek franchise, first appearing in the animated film Shrek (2001). Voiced by Cameron Diaz, Fiona is a princess under a curse that transforms her into an ogre each night; she is locked in a tower guarded by a dragon and awaits rescue by her true love.

Character

Fiona initially embodies the traditional fairy-tale princess, but subverts the archetype: she is an expert martial artist, burps, and ultimately chooses to remain an ogre after true love's first kiss takes "love's true form". In the first film she is rescued by the ogre Shrek and his companion Donkey, sent by Lord Farquaad; instead of Farquaad breaking her curse, she falls in love with Shrek and marries him.

Appearances

Fiona appears in all four Shrek films, as well as specials and spin-offs. In Shrek 2 she introduces Shrek to her parents, King Harold and Queen Lillian of Far Far Away. By Shrek the Third she is a mother, and in Shrek Forever After an alternate-timeline Fiona leads a resistance of ogres against Rumpelstiltskin. The character was loosely inspired by William Steig's 1990 picture book Shrek!

Reception

Fiona is frequently cited as a feminist icon in animation for rejecting the passive damsel role and embracing her ogre identity rather than being "fixed" by the story's resolution.
