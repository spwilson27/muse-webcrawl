Armando Vittorio Diaz, 1st Duke of Victory (5 December 1861 – 29 February 1928) was an Italian general and statesman, best known as Chief of Staff of the Royal Italian Army during the final year of the First World War.

Career

Diaz served in the Italo-Turkish War and, after Italy entered the First World War in 1915, rose to divisional and corps command. Following the disaster at Caporetto in October–November 1917, he replaced Luigi Cadorna as Chief of Staff. Diaz reorganised the army, improved soldiers' conditions, and halted the Austro-Hungarian offensive on the Piave in June 1918, before launching the decisive offensive that won the Battle of Vittorio Veneto and ended the war on the Italian front in November 1918.

Later life

Created Duke of Victory (Duca della Vittoria), he served as Minister of War in Mussolini's first cabinet (1922–1924) and was made a Marshal of Italy in 1924.
