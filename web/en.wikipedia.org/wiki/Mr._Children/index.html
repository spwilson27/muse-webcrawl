Mr. Children

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Mr. Children, one of Japan's best-selling rock bands.

Mr. Children (ミスター・チルドレン), commonly abbreviated as Misu-chiru (ミスチル), is a Japanese rock band formed in 1989 by Kazutoshi Sakurai, Kenichi Tahara, Keisuke Nakagawa, and Hideya Suzuki. One of the most successful Japanese recording acts of all time, they have sold over 60 million records and are frequently ranked alongside Southern All Stars and B'z among the defining bands of modern J-pop and Japanese rock.

History

The band's core members met in high school and played under the name Beatnik before settling on Mr. Children, a name inspired by a phrase in a restaurant menu ("Mr." items for adults alongside children's items). They made their major-label debut in 1992 with the mini-album Everything on Toy's Factory. Their breakthrough came with the 1994 single "Innocent World" and the album Atomic Heart, followed by the smash single "Tomorrow Never Knows" (1994), one of the best-selling Japanese singles of all time, and the album Bolero (1997), which contained the hit "Everything (It's You)".

The band took a hiatus in 1997–98, during which vocalist and primary songwriter Kazutoshi Sakurai worked with producer Takeshi Kobayashi on the side project Bank Band. Returning with Discovery (1999) and the number-one single "Owaranaki Tabi", Mr. Children remained a dominant chart presence through the 2000s with albums such as Q (2000), Shinkai, It's a Wonderful World (2002), Shifuku no Oto (2004), and Home (2007), and singles including "Sign" (winner of the Japan Record Award in 2004), "Hanabi", and "Inori ~Namida no Kidou". Their 30th anniversary in 2022 was marked by stadium and dome tours and the best-of collections Mr. Children 2011–2015 and Mr. Children 2015–2021 & Now.

Musical style and legacy

Sakurai's literate, introspective lyrics and the band's blend of rock, pop, and occasional experimental textures have made Mr. Children a generational touchstone in Japan. Longtime producer Takeshi Kobayashi shaped much of their sound from the mid-1990s until 2015. Ap Bank, the non-profit lending institution co-founded by Sakurai and Kobayashi in 2003, funds environmental projects, and its ap bank fes festivals became major summer events.
