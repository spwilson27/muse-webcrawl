Paradise Island

Paradise Island is an island in the Bahamas, lying just off the north shore of the city of Nassau on New Providence Island, to which it is connected by two bridges across Nassau Harbour. Formerly known as Hog Island, it is about 2.6 square kilometres in area and is best known as the site of the vast Atlantis Paradise Island resort.

History and development

The island was purchased in stages in the twentieth century — William B. Leeds, Axel Wenner-Gren and later Huntington Hartford, who renamed it Paradise Island, developed it as an exclusive resort area. In the 1990s, Sol Kerzner's company transformed the island by building the Atlantis resort, opened in 1998, which dominates the island with its hotel towers, casino, water park, marinas and one of the world's largest open-air marine habitats.

Features

Beyond Atlantis, the island has beaches including Cabbage Beach, private residences, a golf course designed by Tom Weiskopf, a harbour-side village, and the cloisters of a fourteenth-century French Augustinian monastery, reassembled on the island in the 1960s. The Paradise Island bridges link the island to downtown Nassau.
