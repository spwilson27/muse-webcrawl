List of stadiums by capacity

This is a list of the world's largest stadiums by seating capacity. The largest is India's Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, a cricket ground holding 132,000. Many of the next-largest are American college football stadiums. Capacities given are standard permanent seating for the venue's principal sport; figures for North Korea's Rungrado Stadium are disputed (officially 150,000, often assessed at about 113,000).
