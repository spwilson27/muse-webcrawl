Caryophyllales

KingdomPlantae
CladeEudicots
OrderCaryophyllales

Caryophyllales is a diverse order of flowering plants, one of the largest orders of eudicots, containing roughly 11,000 species in about 37 families. It includes familiar plants such as carnations, beets, spinach, quinoa, rhubarb and the cactus family.

Characteristics

Members of the order are notable for producing betalain pigments in place of the anthocyanins found in most other flowering plants, and many are adapted to arid or saline environments, including succulents such as cacti, ice plants and portulacas. The order also contains the carnivorous plant families Droseraceae and Nepenthaceae in some classifications.
