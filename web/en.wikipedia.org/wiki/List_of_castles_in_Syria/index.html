List of castles in Syria

This is a list of castles in Syria, including Crusader, Byzantine, Arab, and Ottoman fortresses. Syria's castles guarded the frontiers between Crusader states, Muslim principalities, and earlier empires; several, including Krak des Chevaliers and the Citadel of Aleppo, are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Krak des Chevaliers is among the best-preserved medieval castles in the world.
