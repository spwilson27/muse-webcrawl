List of volcanoes in Ethiopia

Ethiopia's volcanoes are associated with the East African Rift, where the African Plate is splitting apart. Volcanism is concentrated in the Afar Depression — one of the few places on Earth where a mid-ocean ridge system emerges on land — and along the Main Ethiopian Rift. Ethiopia has around 50 volcanoes with recorded Holocene activity.

Geological setting

The Afar triple junction, where the Red Sea Rift, Gulf of Aden Rift, and Main Ethiopian Rift meet, is the most volcanically active part of the country. The 2005 Dabbahu rifting episode opened a fissure some 60 km long, demonstrating seafloor-spreading processes on land.
