Other markets

France

France is the second-largest manga market after Japan. Major publishers include Glénat, Kana, Pika Édition, Ki-oon, Kurokawa and Kazé/Crunchyroll Manga.

Germany

Carlsen, Egmont Manga, Tokyopop Germany, Kazé and Manga Cult are leading distributors.

Italy and Spain

In Italy: Panini Comics (Planet Manga), Star Comics and J-Pop. In Spain: Norma Editorial, Planeta Cómic, Ivrea and Milky Way Ediciones.

Asia

Publishers such as Elex Media (Indonesia), Tong Li (Taiwan) and Chuang Yi (Singapore, defunct) serve regional markets.

Digital distribution

Digital platforms including Crunchyroll Manga, Azuki, BookWalker, ComiXology (until its integration into Kindle) and publisher apps such as Manga Plus (Shueisha) and K Manga (Kodansha) have become major distribution channels, often offering simultaneous publication with Japan.
