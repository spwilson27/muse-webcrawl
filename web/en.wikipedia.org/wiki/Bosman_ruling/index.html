Bosman ruling
Case: Union Royale Belge v Bosman
Court: European Court of Justice
Date: 15 December 1995

The Bosman ruling (1995) is a European Court of Justice decision concerning freedom of movement for workers, freedom of association, and direct effect of Article 39 (now 45) of the EC Treaty. It effectively prohibited restrictions on foreign EU players and transfer fees for out-of-contract players.

Background

Belgian footballer Jean-Marc Bosman challenged his club RFC Liège and the Belgian FA after a failed transfer to Dunkerque, arguing restraint of trade.

Judgment

The court held that transfer fees for out-of-contract players moving between EU member states, and quotas on foreign EU nationals, breached EU law.

Impact

Player bargaining power and wages rose, clubs signed longer contracts, and squad compositions internationalised across Europe.
