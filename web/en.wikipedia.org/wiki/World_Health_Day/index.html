World Health Day

World Health Day is a global health awareness day celebrated every year on 7 April, under the sponsorship of the World Health Organization (WHO), marking the anniversary of WHO's founding in 1948.

Themes

Each year WHO chooses a theme highlighting a priority area of public health — past themes have included universal health coverage, depression, food safety, antimicrobial resistance, healthy ageing, and 'Our planet, our health' (2022). Governments and organisations worldwide hold events, campaigns and health screenings around the day.

History

The first World Health Day was observed in 1950. It remains one of WHO's official global health campaigns alongside days for tuberculosis, malaria, hepatitis and AIDS.
