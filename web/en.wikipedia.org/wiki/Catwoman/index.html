Catwoman

Catwoman (Selina Kyle) is a character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Batman. Created by Bill Finger and Bob Kane, she debuted in Batman #1 (1940) as "the Cat".

Catwoman
Alter ego: Selina Kyle
First appearance: Batman #1 (1940)
Creators: Bill Finger, Bob Kane
Publisher: DC Comics
Abilities: Expert thief, martial artist, whip

Character

Originally a villain and jewel thief, Catwoman evolved into a morally ambiguous antiheroine and Batman's most enduring love interest. Her whip, cat-like agility and affinity for cats define the character.

In other media

She has been portrayed by Julie Newmar and Eartha Kitt in the 1960s Batman TV series, Michelle Pfeiffer in Batman Returns (1992), Halle Berry in Catwoman (2004), Anne Hathaway in The Dark Knight Rises (2012), and Zoë Kravitz in The Batman (2022).
