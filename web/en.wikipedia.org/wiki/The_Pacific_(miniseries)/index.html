The Pacific is a 2010 American war drama miniseries produced by HBO, a companion piece to the 2001 series Band of Brothers. It follows three United States Marines — Robert Leckie, Eugene Sledge and John Basilone — of the 1st Marine Division through the Pacific theatre of the Second World War, from Guadalcanal to Okinawa.

Synopsis

Based principally on the memoirs of Leckie (Helmet for My Pillow) and Sledge (With the Old Breed), the series depicts the battles of Guadalcanal, Cape Gloucester, Peleliu, Iwo Jima and Okinawa, and the men's difficult return home. Basilone's story follows his Medal of Honor, war-bond tour and death on Iwo Jima.

Production and reception

Filmed mainly in Australia with a budget of over $200 million, it was at the time the most expensive television miniseries ever made. It premiered on 14 March 2010 to strong reviews for its unflinching realism and won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Miniseries, along with several other Emmys.
