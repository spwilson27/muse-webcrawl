The London Underground S Stock is a fleet of sub-surface trains built by Bombardier Transportation to replace the A, C and D stocks on the London Underground's Metropolitan, Circle, District and Hammersmith & City lines. The designation "S" stands for "sub-surface". Two variants were built: the eight-car S8 for the Metropolitan line and the seven-car S7 for the other three lines.

Design

The S Stock was the first Underground fleet with air-conditioning throughout and with fully interconnecting walk-through gangways between cars, allowing passengers to move along the whole train. Wider doors and dedicated wheelchair spaces improved accessibility, and the trains introduced regenerative braking and in-cab signalling displays for the sub-surface lines' new automatic train operation system. Because the trains share a common design, S7 units were temporarily formed as eight cars to cover Metropolitan line duties.

Introduction

The first S8 entered Metropolitan line service on 31 July 2010. Deliveries continued through the decade: S7 trains replaced C Stock on the Circle and Hammersmith & City lines from 2012 and D Stock on the District line by 2017, completing the renewal of the sub-surface fleet. A total of 192 trains — 58 S8 and 134 S7 — were built at Derby.
