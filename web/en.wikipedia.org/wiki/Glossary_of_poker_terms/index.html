Glossary of poker terms

The game of poker has developed a rich vocabulary of its own. This glossary collects common terms used around the table, in card rooms, and in writing about the game.

Action
A player's turn to act; also, loosely, lively betting in a game.
All-in
Betting all of one's remaining chips on a hand.
Ante
A small forced bet posted by all players before a hand, used in some variants and tournament stages.
Bad beat
A hand that loses despite having been a strong statistical favourite.
Bankroll
The money a player sets aside for poker.
Big blind
The larger of the two forced bets in flop games, posted by the player two seats left of the button.
Bluff
A bet or raise made with a weak hand, intending to make better hands fold.
Board
The community cards face-up in the centre of the table.
Button
The marker indicating the nominal dealer, whose position is last to act post-flop.
Call
To match the current bet.
Check
To pass the action without betting, when no bet is owed.
Community cards
Cards dealt face-up and shared by all players, as in Texas hold 'em and Omaha.
Flop
The first three community cards, dealt together, and the betting round that follows.
Fold
To discard one's hand and forfeit the pot.
Heads-up
Play between only two players.
Kicker
A side card that breaks ties between otherwise equal hands.
Muck
The pile of discarded cards; also, to fold without showing.
Nuts
The best possible hand at a given point.
Pot odds
The ratio of the current pot size to the cost of a contemplated call.
Raise
To increase the size of the current bet.
River
The fifth and final community card, and its betting round.
Showdown
The revealing of hands after the final betting round to determine the winner.
Slow play
Playing a strong hand passively to disguise its strength.
Tell
A behavioural clue that gives away information about a player's hand.
Tilt
Emotional frustration that degrades a player's decisions.
Turn
The fourth community card, and its betting round.
