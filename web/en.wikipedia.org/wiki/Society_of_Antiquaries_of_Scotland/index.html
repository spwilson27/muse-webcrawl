Society of Antiquaries of Scotland
Founded: 1780
Founder: David Erskine, 11th Earl of Buchan
Headquarters: Edinburgh

The Society of Antiquaries of Scotland is Scotland's oldest antiquarian society, founded in 1780 by David Erskine, 11th Earl of Buchan, and incorporated by royal charter in 1783. Its members are known as Fellows (FSAScot).

History

The society's early collections formed the basis of what became the National Museum of Antiquities of Scotland, now part of National Museums Scotland. The society has long published the Proceedings of the Society of Antiquaries of Scotland, a leading journal of Scottish archaeology and history.

Activities

The society promotes the study of Scotland's past through lectures, conferences, grants, and publications, and comments on heritage policy and planning matters.
