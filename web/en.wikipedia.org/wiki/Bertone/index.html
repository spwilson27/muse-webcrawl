Bertone

[Image placeholder: Bertone-designed automobile]

Gruppo Bertone, commonly known simply as Bertone, was an Italian industrial design company which specialized in car styling, coachbuilding and manufacturing. The company was based in Grugliasco, northern Italy. Founded by Giovanni Bertone in 1912 in Turin, it was for decades led by his son Nuccio Bertone and became one of the most celebrated names in automotive design.

Bertone styled cars for manufacturers including Abarth, Alfa Romeo, Citroën, Ferrari, Fiat, Iso Rivolta, Lancia, Lamborghini, Mercedes-Benz, Opel and Volvo, among others. Under chief designer Giorgetto Giugiaro and later Marcello Gandini, Bertone created landmark designs such as the Lamborghini Miura, Countach and Stratos Zero.

History

Giovanni Bertone started a carriage-building business in Turin in 1912. After the First World War the firm turned to automobile bodies. Nuccio Bertone joined after the Second World War and transformed Bertone into a styling and limited-production house. The company built famous production cars including the Alfa Romeo Giulietta Sprint.

Financial difficulties led Bertone into bankruptcy in 2014; the brand was acquired in 2016 by AKKA Technologies, and in 2022 the Ideactive group relaunched Bertone, presenting the GB110 hypercar.
