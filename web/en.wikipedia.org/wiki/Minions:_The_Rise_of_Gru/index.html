Minions: The Rise of Gru is a 2022 American animated comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. It is the sequel to Minions (2015) and the fifth entry in the Despicable Me franchise. Directed by Kyle Balda, the film stars Steve Carell as the voice of young Gru and Pierre Coffin as the Minions, with Taraji P. Henson, Michelle Yeoh, RZA, Jean-Claude Van Damme, Lucy Lawless, Dolph Lundgren, Danny Trejo, Russell Brand, Julie Andrews and Alan Arkin in supporting roles.

The film is set in 1976, following 11-year-old Gru's first clash with supervillains.

Plot

In 1976, 11-year-old Gru idolises the Vicious 6, a team of supervillains led by Wild Knuckles. When the team betrays and discards Knuckles, Gru interviews for the vacancy and, slighted, steals the team's prized Zodiac Stone. Pursued by the Vicious 6 and befriending the disgraced Knuckles, Gru is helped by his Minions — Kevin, Stuart, Bob and the braces-wearing Otto — who seek a "mini-boss" to serve. After a kung fu-training detour in San Francisco's Chinatown with acupuncturist Master Chow, Gru and the Minions confront the Vicious 6 in a final battle during which Gru begins to learn the value of teamwork and of his mentor.

Release and reception

Originally scheduled for 2020, the film was repeatedly delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic and finally released in the United States on 1 July 2022. It grossed over $939 million worldwide against a budget of $80 million, becoming the fifth-highest-grossing film of 2022 and the highest-grossing animated film of that year. Reviews were generally favourable, praising its humour, soundtrack of 1970s covers, and visuals. The film sparked the viral "#GentleMinions" trend, in which groups of young fans attended screenings wearing formal suits — prompting some cinemas to restrict suited groups after rowdy incidents.
