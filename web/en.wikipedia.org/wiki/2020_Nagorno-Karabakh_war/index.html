2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war

The 2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war was an armed conflict fought between 27 September and 10 November 2020 between Azerbaijan (supported by Turkey) and the self-proclaimed Republic of Artsakh together with Armenia, over the disputed Nagorno-Karabakh region. Azerbaijan launched a large-scale offensive to retake territories lost in the First Nagorno-Karabakh War (1988–1994). After 44 days of fighting, a Russian-brokered ceasefire ended the war with significant Azerbaijani territorial gains.

Second Nagorno-Karabakh War

Date: 27 September – 10 November 2020
Location: Nagorno-Karabakh and surrounding districts
Result: Azerbaijani victory; ceasefire agreement
Casualties: Several thousand soldiers and over a hundred civilians killed (estimates vary)

Course of the war

Azerbaijan's offensive made initial gains in the southern districts along the Aras river, capturing Jabrayil, Fuzuli and Hadrut. Azerbaijani use of Turkish Bayraktar TB2 and Israeli Harop loitering munitions proved highly effective against Armenian armour and air defences. In early November Azerbaijani forces captured the strategically vital town of Shusha (Shushi), the second-largest settlement in Nagorno-Karabakh, bringing them within reach of the capital, Stepanakert. On the final day of the war an Azerbaijani air-defence unit shot down a Russian Mi-24 helicopter over Armenia, for which Baku apologised.

Ceasefire and aftermath

The ceasefire agreement of 9 November confirmed Azerbaijan's battlefield gains and required Armenian withdrawal from the Aghdam, Kalbajar and Lachin districts, with Russian peacekeepers deployed to the remainder of Nagorno-Karabakh. Tens of thousands of ethnic Armenians fled the territories transferred to Azerbaijan. The war was followed by a border crisis between Armenia and Azerbaijan from 2021, a blockade of the Lachin corridor beginning in December 2022, and Azerbaijan's offensive of September 2023, which led to the dissolution of the Republic of Artsakh and the exodus of nearly its entire Armenian population.
