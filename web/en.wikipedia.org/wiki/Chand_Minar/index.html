Chand Minar

The Chand Minar ("Tower of the Moon") is a medieval tower at Daulatabad Fort, Maharashtra, India. Built in 1445 by Ala-ud-din Ahmed Shah II of the Bahmani Sultanate to commemorate his capture of the fort, it is modelled on the Qutb Minar of Delhi.

Description

The tower is about 63 metres (207 ft) high and divided into four storeys, with balconies and Persian-influenced glazed tile decoration, originally turquoise. It stands beside the Chini Mahal within the fort complex and served as a watchtower and symbol of victory.
