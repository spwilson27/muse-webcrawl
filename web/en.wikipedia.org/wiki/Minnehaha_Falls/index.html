Minnehaha Falls

Minnehaha Falls in Minnehaha Park, Minneapolis

Minnehaha Falls is a waterfall on Minnehaha Creek in Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States, near the confluence of the creek with the Mississippi River. The falls are 53 feet (16 m) high and are the centrepiece of Minnehaha Park, one of the city's oldest parks.

Name and literature

The name derives from the Dakota words mni (water) and haha (waterfall); it is often popularly translated as "laughing water". The falls were made famous by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow's 1855 epic poem The Song of Hiawatha, whose heroine Minnehaha gives the falls their literary association.

Park

Minnehaha Regional Park preserves the falls and surrounding glen. Features include a limestone bluff, a path behind the falling water, the Minnehaha Depot, and the John H. Stevens House. In winter the falls can freeze into a curtain of ice while water continues to flow behind it.
