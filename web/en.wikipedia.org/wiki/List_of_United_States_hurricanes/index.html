List of United States hurricanes

The List of United States hurricanes compiles the tropical cyclones of at least hurricane strength that have affected the United States, including its territories. Florida has been struck by more hurricanes than any other state, followed by Texas and Louisiana. Costs and death tolls have been dominated historically by storms such as the 1900 Galveston hurricane, and economically by Katrina (2005) and Harvey (2017).

Intensity records

The strongest landfalling U.S. hurricane by pressure was the 1935 Labor Day hurricane in the Florida Keys (892 mbar). Hurricane Camille (1969) and Michael (2018) are among the most intense mainland landfalls. The 2024 season added Helene and Milton, both catastrophic in the Southeast.
