Hank Hill

Hank Rutherford Hill is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the animated television series King of the Hill, created by Mike Judge and Greg Daniels. Voiced by Judge, Hank is a proud salesman of propane and propane accessories at Strickland Propane in the fictional town of Arlen, Texas.

Character

Hank is a staunch traditionalist: hardworking, honest, reserved and uncomfortable with displays of emotion. He idolises his father Cotton Hill, a World War II veteran, despite Cotton's verbal abuse. Hank's catchphrases include "I tell you what" and his startled exclamation "Bwah!" His great passions are propane, his lawn, the Dallas Cowboys, and drinking beer in the alley with his friends Dale Gribble, Bill Dauterive and Boomhauer. He suffers from a narrow urethra, a running joke in the series.

Family

Hank is married to Peggy Hill, a substitute Spanish teacher and former Boggle champion, and is the father of Bobby Hill, whose interests in comedy and cooking Hank struggles to understand. The family also takes in Peggy's niece Luanne Platter.

Reception

Hank has been praised as one of television's most fully realised animated characters, frequently ranked among the greatest cartoon characters of all time. King of the Hill ran on Fox from 1997 to 2010, and a revival was later produced for Hulu.
