The Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets is the name used for all of the intercollegiate athletic teams that represent the Georgia Institute of Technology in Atlanta, Georgia. The Yellow Jackets compete in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision and are members of the Atlantic Coast Conference, which Georgia Tech helped found in 1979 after earlier membership in the Southeastern Conference.

Football

The football team plays at Bobby Dodd Stadium at Hyundai Field, the oldest continuously used on-campus stadium in Division I FBS. Georgia Tech claims four national championships (1917, 1928, 1952 and 1990); the 1917 team coached by John Heisman was the first Southern team to win a national title. Legendary coaches include Heisman, William Alexander and Bobby Dodd. The team's fiercest rivalry is with the University of Georgia, known as "Clean, Old-Fashioned Hate."

Other sports

Georgia Tech fields 17 varsity teams. The men's basketball team reached the NCAA Final Four in 1990 and the championship game in 2004. The baseball team is a perennial power based at Russ Chandler Stadium, and the golf programme has produced numerous PGA Tour players. School colours are white and gold, with navy blue as an accent.
