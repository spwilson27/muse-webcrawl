Arena of Nîmes
LocationNîmes, France
Builtc. 70 AD
Capacityc. 24,000 (antiquity); c. 13,800 today

The Arena of Nîmes (French: Arènes de Nîmes) is a Roman amphitheatre in the city of Nîmes in southern France. Built around 70 AD, shortly after the Colosseum in Rome, it is one of the best-preserved Roman amphitheatres in the world.

History

The elliptical arena measures 133 metres long and 101 metres wide, with two storeys of 60 arcades rising 21 metres. In the Middle Ages it was fortified and filled with houses, becoming a small fortified town; the interior dwellings were cleared in the 19th century.

Modern use

Today the arena hosts bullfights during the Feria de Nîmes, concerts, and historical re-enactments. Along with the Maison Carrée and the Tour Magne, it anchors Nîmes' Roman heritage; the Maison Carrée was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2023.
