89th Airlift Wing

The 89th Airlift Wing is a United States Air Force unit assigned to Air Mobility Command and stationed at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland. It provides global airlift, logistics, aerial port and communications support for the President of the United States, the Vice President, and other senior leaders.

The wing operates the aircraft popularly known as Air Force One when the President is aboard, including the VC-25A, as well as the C-32, C-37 and C-40 fleets used for distinguished-visitor airlift. Its lineage traces to the 89th Troop Carrier Group of the Second World War era.

The wing's motto relationship with the Special Air Mission reflects its role: whenever the President travels by air, the 89th is responsible for the aircraft, crews and much of the supporting communications.
