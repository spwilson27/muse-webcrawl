List of volcanoes in Chile

Chile, situated on the Pacific Ring of Fire where the Nazca and Antarctic plates subduct beneath the South American Plate, has one of the largest numbers of volcanoes in the world — over 2,000 volcanic features, of which roughly 500 are considered geologically active and about 90 have recorded eruptions in historical times. Volcanism is organized into the Northern, Central, Southern, and Austral Volcanic Zones of the Andes.

Monitoring

Chilean volcanoes are monitored by SERNAGEOMIN (National Geology and Mining Service) through its Southern Andes Volcano Observatory (OVDAS) and the National Volcanic Monitoring Network, which issues alert levels for the most dangerous volcanoes.
