Mass Effect is an action role-playing video game developed by BioWare and originally published by Microsoft Game Studios for the Xbox 360 in 2007. It is the first game of the Mass Effect series. Set within the Milky Way galaxy in the year 2183, the game follows Commander Shepard, an elite human soldier who must stop a rogue agent from bringing about the return of the Reapers, a race of sentient machines that harvest advanced civilisations every 50,000 years.

Gameplay

Players customise Shepard's appearance, gender, background and combat class, and make dialogue choices through a radial conversation wheel. Moral choices are tracked on a Paragon (charming/cooperative) and Renegade (intimidating/ruthless) scale. Combat is real-time third-person shooting combined with squad commands and special abilities powered by "biotics" and technology. Between missions aboard the SSV Normandy, players explore the galaxy via a star map, visiting story worlds and uncharted planets, some traversed in the M35 Mako armoured vehicle. Choices and character progression carry over to the sequels through save-game import.

Plot

Shepard, executive officer of the Normandy, is sent to the human colony of Eden Prime to recover a Prothean beacon. The mission is attacked by the geth, synthetic troops led by the turian Spectre Saren Arterius, and the beacon gives Shepard a vision of destruction. Granted Spectre status — the first human so honoured — Shepard assembles a multi-species crew, uncovers Saren's alliance with the Reaper Sovereign, and learns of the Conduit, a back door onto the Citadel space station. In the climax, Shepard prevents Saren and Sovereign from opening the Citadel relay to dark space, delaying the Reapers' return.

Development and reception

Directed by Casey Hudson, the game was conceived as the first chapter of a planned trilogy with a persistent, player-shaped story. It was widely acclaimed for its writing, world-building, characters and voice acting; criticism was directed at its inventory system, vehicle handling and technical issues. It sold over two million copies in its first three weeks and won numerous awards. Ports followed for Windows (2008) and PlayStation 3 (2012). A sequel, Mass Effect 2 (2010), was followed by Mass Effect 3 (2012), completing Shepard's trilogy. The game was remastered as part of Mass Effect Legendary Edition (2021).
