The International Finance Centre (IFC) is an integrated commercial development on the waterfront of Hong Kong's Central District, built over Hong Kong Station. It consists of two skyscrapers, the IFC Mall, and the Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong.

Two IFC

Two International Finance Centre, completed in 2003, rises 412 metres with 88 storeys and was the tallest building in Hong Kong until the International Commerce Centre surpassed it in 2010. It was designed by César Pelli.

One IFC

One International Finance Centre, completed in 1998, stands 210 metres tall with 39 storeys. It houses financial institutions including the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's offices nearby.
