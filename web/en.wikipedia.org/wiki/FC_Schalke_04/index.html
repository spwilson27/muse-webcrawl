FC Schalke 04

Fußballclub Gelsenkirchen-Schalke 04 e. V., commonly known as FC Schalke 04 or simply Schalke, is a German professional association football club based in the Schalke district of Gelsenkirchen, North Rhine-Westphalia. Founded on 4 May 1904, the club is nicknamed Die Königsblauen (The Royal Blues) for its blue-and-white colours and Die Knappen (The Miners), reflecting its roots in the coal-mining communities of the Ruhr.

History

Schalke dominated German football in the 1930s and early 1940s, winning six German championships between 1934 and 1942 with their famous Schalker Kreisel short-passing style. The club won its seventh and most recent league title in 1958. On the European stage, Schalke's greatest triumph came in 1997, when they defeated Inter Milan on penalties to win the UEFA Cup. The club has also won the DFB-Pokal five times, most recently in 2011.

Stadium and supporters

Since 2001 Schalke have played at the Veltins-Arena (Arena AufSchalke), a retractable-roof stadium with a capacity of about 62,000 for league matches. Schalke is one of Germany's best-supported clubs and has a fierce rivalry with Borussia Dortmund, contested in the Revierderby. The club's membership exceeds 160,000, making it among the largest sports clubs in the world.

Recent years

After financial difficulties and relegation from the Bundesliga in 2021, Schalke won the 2. Bundesliga in 2021–22 but were relegated again the following season, and have since competed in the second tier while rebuilding.
