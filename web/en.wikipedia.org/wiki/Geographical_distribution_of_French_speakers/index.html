Geographical distribution of French speakers

The geographical distribution of French speakers covers the countries and regions where the French language is spoken as a first or second language. French is an official language in 29 countries and is spoken by roughly 300 million people worldwide, including second-language speakers.

Europe

In Europe, French is an official language in France, Belgium (with Dutch and German), Switzerland (with German, Italian and Romansh), Luxembourg (with Luxembourgish and German) and Monaco. Large speaker communities also exist in parts of Italy (Aosta Valley) and the Channel Islands historically.

Africa

Africa has the largest number of French speakers of any continent, a legacy of French and Belgian colonial rule. French is an official language in many West and Central African states, including Senegal, Ivory Coast, Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger, Guinea, Benin, Togo, Congo, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Cameroon, Gabon, Chad and Madagascar. The Democratic Republic of the Congo is the most populous Francophone country in the world.

Americas

In the Americas, French is an official language of Canada (with English), where it is the mother tongue of most residents of Quebec and significant minorities elsewhere. It is also official in Haiti (with Haitian Creole) and French Guiana, and spoken in the French Caribbean territories and in pockets of Louisiana and New England.

Asia and Oceania

French retains a presence in Lebanon, Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, and is official in the Pacific territories of New Caledonia, French Polynesia and Wallis and Futuna, as well as in Vanuatu (with Bislama and English).
