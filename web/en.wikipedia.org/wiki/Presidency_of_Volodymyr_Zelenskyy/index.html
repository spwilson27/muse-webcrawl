Presidency of Volodymyr Zelenskyy

The presidency of Volodymyr Zelenskyy began on 20 May 2019, when Volodymyr Zelenskyy was inaugurated as the sixth president of Ukraine. A former actor and comedian with no prior political office, Zelenskyy won the 2019 presidential election in a landslide, defeating incumbent Petro Poroshenko with about 73 per cent of the second-round vote, on a platform of fighting corruption and ending the war in Donbas.

Early presidency

Zelenskyy's party, Servant of the People, won an outright majority in snap parliamentary elections in July 2019, the first single-party majority in Ukrainian history. His government pursued judicial and anti-corruption reforms, land-market reform, and digital government initiatives, though progress was uneven and his popularity declined by 2021.

Russian invasion of Ukraine

On 24 February 2022 Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Zelenskyy refused evacuation offers, remaining in Kyiv and becoming the wartime leader of the Ukrainian resistance; his nightly video addresses and speeches to foreign parliaments drew worldwide attention and rallied international military and financial support. Ukraine applied for European Union membership days after the invasion and was granted candidate status in June 2022. Elections scheduled for 2024 were postponed because martial law, imposed at the start of the invasion, prohibits holding them.
