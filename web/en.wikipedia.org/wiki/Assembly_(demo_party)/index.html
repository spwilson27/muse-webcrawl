Assembly (demo party)

Assembly is a demoparty held annually in Helsinki, Finland, since 1992. It is one of the oldest and largest gatherings of the demoscene — the computer subculture centred on creating demos, real-time audiovisual presentations — and has grown into a major digital culture festival attracting several thousand visitors.

History

The first Assembly was organised in 1992 by members of the Amiga and PC demoscene groups. Over the 1990s the event outgrew school halls and moved to the Helsinki Ice Hall and later to Hartwall Arena, and in recent years to the Messukeskus convention centre. A second annual event, Assembly Winter, was added in 2007 with a stronger emphasis on gaming.

Competitions

The heart of Assembly is its competitions ("compos"), in which demoscene groups present demos, intros (size-limited demos, typically 64k or 4k), graphics, and music, judged by audience vote. Winning entries at Assembly have often become landmarks of the demoscene. The party also hosts esports tournaments and seminars on digital creativity.

Cultural recognition

In 2020 Finland added the demoscene to its national inventory of living heritage under the UNESCO Convention for the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage, with Assembly cited as a central institution of the culture.
