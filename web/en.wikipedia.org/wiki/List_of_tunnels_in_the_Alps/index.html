List of tunnels in the Alps

Portal of a major Alpine rail tunnel

The Alps form a major barrier to transport between northern and southern Europe, and have been pierced by numerous road and rail tunnels since the nineteenth century. The longest of these, the Gotthard Base Tunnel in Switzerland, opened in 2016, is the longest railway tunnel in the world.

Under construction

The Brenner Base Tunnel between Austria and Italy, some 55 km long (64 km including the Innsbruck bypass), is expected to become the longest rail tunnel connection in the world when completed. The Mont d'Ambin Base Tunnel forms the cross-border section of the Turin–Lyon high-speed railway between France and Italy.
