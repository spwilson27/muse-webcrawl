Law & Order: Special Victims Unit (often abbreviated to Law & Order: SVU or simply SVU) is an American police procedural drama television series created by Dick Wolf for NBC. The first spin-off of Law & Order, it premiered on September 20, 1999, and follows detectives of the NYPD's Special Victims Unit, who investigate sexually based offenses and crimes against children and the vulnerable.

Premise and cast

Mariska Hargitay stars as Olivia Benson, who rises from detective to captain of the unit over the show's run — the longest-running role in the franchise. Ice-T has played Sergeant Odafin "Fin" Tutuola since the second season. Christopher Meloni starred as Elliot Stabler for the first twelve seasons and later returned in the spin-off Law & Order: Organized Crime. Kelli Giddish, Raúl Esparza, and Peter Scanavino have also been long-running cast members.

Records and reception

With its 21st season in 2019, SVU became the longest-running American primetime live-action series, surpassing Gunsmoke and the original Law & Order. Hargitay won an Emmy and a Golden Globe for the role. The show is frequently noted for "ripped-from-the-headlines" storylines and for popularizing awareness of sexual assault issues; Hargitay founded the Joyful Heart Foundation in response to viewer letters.
