Buzzcocks

Buzzcocks are an English punk rock band formed in Bolton in 1976 by singer-songwriter-guitarist Pete Shelley and singer-songwriter Howard Devoto. They are regarded as a seminal influence on the Manchester music scene, independent record labels, and the punk, power pop, and pop punk genres, with songs such as "Ever Fallen in Love (With Someone You Shouldn't've)" becoming classics.

History

Inspired by seeing the Sex Pistols, Shelley and Devoto organised the Pistols' famous Lesser Free Trade Hall gigs in Manchester. Buzzcocks self-released the Spiral Scratch EP in 1977, a landmark of DIY independent music. After Devoto left to form Magazine, Shelley led the band through three albums for United Artists, including Love Bites (1978). The band split in 1981, reformed in 1989, and continued after Shelley's death in 2018 with Steve Diggle fronting.
