Pigs in blankets

Pigs in blankets is a dish with different definitions in the United Kingdom and the United States. In the UK, it consists of small sausages (usually chipolatas) wrapped in streaky bacon; in the US, it generally refers to hot dogs or sausages wrapped in pastry dough and baked.

United Kingdom

British pigs in blankets are a traditional accompaniment to roast turkey at Christmas dinner, and are also served with other roast meals. They are typically made by wrapping a chipolata sausage in a rasher of streaky bacon and roasting until the bacon is crisp.

United States

American pigs in a blanket are cocktail sausages or hot dogs encased in croissant, biscuit, or crescent-roll dough, served as an appetiser. A related breakfast version wraps sausages in pancakes. National Pigs in a Blanket Day is celebrated on April 24 in some listings.
