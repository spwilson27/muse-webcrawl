Marian shrine

Illustration: A Marian pilgrimage shrine (placeholder image)

A Marian shrine is a church, chapel or other sacred place dedicated to the Virgin Mary that serves as a destination of pilgrimage and devotion. Marian shrines are found throughout the Catholic world (and some in Orthodox countries), and many are associated with reported Marian apparitions, miraculous images, or healings. The greatest of them — such as the Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe in Mexico City, the Sanctuary of Fátima in Portugal, and the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes in France — receive millions of pilgrims each year; Guadalupe is often cited as the most visited Catholic pilgrimage site in the world.

Origins

Many shrines originate in apparitions of the Virgin Mary reported by visionaries: Lourdes (1858, to Bernadette Soubirous), Fátima (1917, to three shepherd children), Guadalupe (1531, traditionally to Juan Diego), Knock in Ireland (1879), and La Salette and Pontmain in France. Others centre on venerated images or statues, such as Our Lady of Częstochowa in Poland, Our Lady of Aparecida in Brazil, and Our Lady of Luján in Argentina, where Mary is honoured as national patroness. The Church investigates claimed apparitions and may grant formal recognition to a shrine as diocesan, national, or international.

Features and pilgrimage

Shrines typically include a basilica or chapel housing the image, facilities for confession and Mass, and often healing rituals — at Lourdes, immersion in the spring water revealed by Bernadette; elsewhere, processions, novenas and the rosary. Pilgrimage to Marian shrines peaks on the shrine's feast day and on the great Marian solemnities. Pilgrims often leave ex-voto offerings in thanksgiving for favours received.

Notable shrines

Major Marian shrines include, in Europe: Lourdes and La Salette (France), Fátima (Portugal), Loreto and Pompeii (Italy), Częstochowa (Poland), Santiago de Compostela's Marian devotion, Mariazell (Austria), Altötting (Germany), and Einsiedeln (Switzerland); in the Americas: Guadalupe (Mexico), Aparecida (Brazil), Luján (Argentina), Chiquinquirá (Colombia), and Our Lady of Charity of El Cobre (Cuba); in Asia: Our Lady of Velankanni (India) and Our Lady of La Vang (Vietnam); in Africa: Our Lady of Kibeho (Rwanda), the only Vatican-approved apparition site in Africa.
