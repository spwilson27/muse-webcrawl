Eliphalet Remington

Eliphalet Remington (October 28, 1793 – August 12, 1861) was an American engineer and inventor who founded what became the Remington Arms firearms company. Born in Suffield, Connecticut, and raised in Herkimer County, New York, he began as a blacksmith.

Career

According to legend, the young Remington forged his own rifle barrel around 1816 because he could not afford to buy a rifle, and its quality brought orders from neighbours. His forge at Ilion, New York, grew into E. Remington and Sons, which supplied arms to the United States government during the Mexican–American War and the Civil War. The company later diversified into typewriters and sewing machines, spawning the Remington Rand corporation.
