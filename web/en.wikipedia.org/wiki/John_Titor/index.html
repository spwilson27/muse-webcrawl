John Titor was the name used by a person or persons who posted on several bulletin boards during 2000 and 2001 claiming to be an American military time traveller from 2036. The posts made numerous vague and specific predictions about coming events, which did not come to pass, and Titor has since become one of the best-known internet urban legends, widely regarded as a hoax.

The claims

Posting first as "TimeTravel_0" on the Art Bell BBS forum in November 2000 and later on Coast to Coast AM-related boards, Titor said he was a soldier based in Tampa, Florida, assigned to a government time-travel project. He claimed to have been sent back to 1975 to retrieve an IBM 5100 computer — needed, he said, to debug legacy systems in 2036 — and to have stopped in 2000 for personal reasons. He described his time machine as a "C204" unit installed in a 1966 Chevrolet Corvette, and posted photographs of the device and pages from its manual.

Predictions

Titor predicted a civil war in the United States beginning around 2004–2005, escalating to a brief nuclear Third World War in 2015 that killed some three billion people, after which society reorganised into five regions and life became more rural and community-based. He invoked the many-worlds interpretation to explain away discrepancies. None of the predicted events occurred.

Aftermath

Titor's final posts appeared in March 2001, announcing his departure back to 2036. The story has been investigated by journalists and enthusiasts; the Italian television programme Voyager and later reporting suggested the hoax may have been created by Florida brothers Larry and Morey Haber together with entertainment lawyer John Rick Haber. A company, the John Titor Foundation, later published a book of the posts.
