Prism (geometry)

In geometry, a prism is a polyhedron comprising an n-sided polygon base, a second base that is a translated copy of the first, and n other faces (necessarily parallelograms) joining corresponding sides of the two bases.

Properties

A prism whose bases are aligned in a specific way and whose side faces are rectangles is a right prism. The volume of a prism is the product of the area of its base and its height (the perpendicular distance between bases). Its surface area is twice the base area plus the product of the base perimeter and height.

Types

Prisms are named for their bases: triangular, square (a cuboid), pentagonal, and so on. Uniform prisms have regular polygon bases and square sides. Prisms form one family of prismatoids and have duals in the bipyramids.
