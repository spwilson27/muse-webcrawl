Young Sheldon, a prequel spin-off of The Big Bang Theory.

Young Sheldon is an American coming-of-age sitcom created by Chuck Lorre and Steven Molaro for CBS. A spin-off prequel to The Big Bang Theory, it follows child prodigy Sheldon Cooper growing up in the fictional town of Medford, East Texas, beginning in 1989. Iain Armitage stars in the title role, and Jim Parsons — who played the adult Sheldon in the parent series — narrates as the voice of Sheldon looking back on his childhood and executive-produces.

Premise

The series begins with nine-year-old Sheldon starting high school, where his extraordinary intellect and rigid personality clash with classmates, teachers and his own family. His family includes his mother Mary (Zoe Perry), a devout Baptist; his father George Sr. (Lance Barber), the high school football coach; his older brother Georgie (Montana Jordan); his twin sister Missy (Raegan Revord); and his maternal grandmother, "Meemaw" (Annie Potts). Across seven seasons, the show follows Sheldon's academic progress, including his enrolment at the fictional East Texas Tech, alongside family dramas and milestones.

Production and reception

The series premiered with a special preview on 25 September 2017 and ran for seven seasons and 141 episodes, concluding on 16 May 2024. Unlike the multi-camera, studio-audience format of The Big Bang Theory, Young Sheldon was filmed in single-camera style without a live audience. It was consistently one of the most-watched comedies on American television during its run and received generally positive reviews, with praise for Armitage's performance and the show's more dramatic tone. A sequel spin-off, Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage, premiered in 2024, continuing the story of Sheldon's brother.
