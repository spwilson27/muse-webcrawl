AS1 (networking)

AS1 (Applicability Statement 1) is a specification about how to transport structured business-to-business data securely and reliably over the Internet. Security is achieved by using digital certificates and encryption. The AS1 protocol involves an electronic document interchange (EDI) protocol developed by the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) to implement secure and reliable business communications over the Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) email protocol.

Overview

AS1 was one of a family of "Applicability Statements" standardised by the IETF's EDIINT working group. Its siblings include AS2, which transports EDI data over HTTP, and AS3, which uses FTP. Of these, AS2 became the most widely adopted, particularly in retail supply chains.

How it works

Business data is wrapped in a MIME message, which can be signed and encrypted using S/MIME. The message is sent over SMTP to the trading partner, who can request a Message Disposition Notification (MDN) to confirm receipt and successful processing, providing a form of non-repudiation.
