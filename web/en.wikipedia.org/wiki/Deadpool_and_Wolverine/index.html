Deadpool & Wolverine is a 2024 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics characters Deadpool and Wolverine. Produced by Marvel Studios, it is the 34th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and the first Deadpool film in that franchise, following Deadpool (2016) and Deadpool 2 (2018).

Plot

Wade Wilson is recruited by the Time Variance Authority, which offers him a place in the Marvel Cinematic Universe but plans to destroy his own timeline. Wilson instead steals a time-travel device to find a Wolverine variant who can save his universe, and the two are banished to the Void, where they confront the powerful mutant Cassandra Nova.

Production

Development began at Marvel Studios after Disney acquired 21st Century Fox in 2019. Hugh Jackman agreed to return as Wolverine in 2022 despite the character's death in Logan (2017); the film explains his role through the multiverse. Filming took place in England in 2023, interrupted by the Hollywood strikes.

Reception

The film became the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time, earning over $1.3 billion, and was generally praised for the chemistry between Reynolds and Jackman and its humour, though some critics found the plot convoluted.
