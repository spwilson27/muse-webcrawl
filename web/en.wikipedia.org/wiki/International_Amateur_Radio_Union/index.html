International Amateur Radio Union

The International Amateur Radio Union (IARU) is an international confederation of national organisations that allows a forum for common matters of concern to amateur radio operators (radio amateurs or "hams") worldwide, and collectively represents matters to the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). It was founded in Paris in 1925.

Structure

The IARU is organised into three regional organisations corresponding to the ITU regions: Region 1 (Europe, Africa, Middle East, and northern Asia), Region 2 (the Americas), and Region 3 (Asia-Pacific). Its administrative secretariat is provided by the American Radio Relay League (ARRL).

Activities

The IARU represents amateur radio spectrum interests at World Radiocommunication Conferences, coordinates band plans, sponsors World Amateur Radio Day (18 April), and organises international championships such as the IARU HF Championship and amateur radio direction finding competitions.
