Music Man

IndustryMusical instruments
Founded1974

Music Man is an American guitar and bass guitar manufacturer. It was founded in 1974 by Forrest White, Tom Walker and Leo Fender, who had previously founded Fender Musical Instruments. The company was acquired by Ernie Ball in 1984 and is now known as Ernie Ball Music Man.

History

Music Man began as a manufacturer of guitar and bass amplifiers before introducing instruments, including the StingRay bass in 1976, designed with input from Leo Fender. The StingRay, with its active electronics and distinctive headstock design, became one of the most influential electric basses. After financial difficulties in the early 1980s, the company was purchased by Ernie Ball.

Instruments

The company's product line includes the StingRay, Sterling, Bongo and Cutlass basses, and electric guitars such as the Silhouette, Axis, Albert Lee and John Petrucci signature models. Music Man instruments are manufactured in San Luis Obispo, California.
