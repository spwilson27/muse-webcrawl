Crypto.com Arena

Crypto.com Arena (stylized as crypto.com Arena; originally and still commonly known as Staples Center) is a multi-purpose indoor arena in downtown Los Angeles. Opened on October 17, 1999, it is home to the Los Angeles Lakers of the NBA and the Los Angeles Kings of the NHL, and was previously home to the Los Angeles Clippers and the WNBA's Los Angeles Sparks.

Crypto.com Arena
Former names: Staples Center (1999–2021)
Location: 1111 S. Figueroa Street, Los Angeles, California
Opened: October 17, 1999
Owner/operator: Anschutz Entertainment Group (AEG)
Capacity: 19,067 (basketball); 18,230 (ice hockey)

History

The arena was built at a cost of about $375 million and designed by NBBJ. It anchors the L.A. Live entertainment district developed by AEG. In November 2021, the Singapore-based cryptocurrency exchange Crypto.com acquired the naming rights in a reported 20-year, $700 million deal — among the most expensive naming-rights agreements in sports — and the venue was renamed on December 25, 2021, though many fans continue to call it Staples Center.

Events

The arena has hosted multiple NBA Finals and Stanley Cup Finals, the Grammy Awards on numerous occasions, the 2000 Democratic National Convention, numerous major concerts and boxing/UFC events, and memorial services including that of Michael Jackson in 2009. It is scheduled to host events during the 2028 Summer Olympics.
