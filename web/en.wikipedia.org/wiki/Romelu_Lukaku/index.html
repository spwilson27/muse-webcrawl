Romelu Lukaku

Born: 13 May 1993, Antwerp, Belgium
Position: Striker
National team: Belgium

Romelu Lukaku Bolingoli (born 13 May 1993) is a Belgian professional footballer who plays as a striker. He is Belgium's all-time top scorer and one of the most prolific goalscorers of his generation, known for his physical power, pace and finishing.

Club career

Lukaku began his career at Anderlecht, winning the Belgian Pro League and finishing as its top scorer as a teenager, before joining Chelsea in 2011. After loan spells at West Bromwich Albion and Everton, he signed permanently for Everton in 2014, then moved to Manchester United in 2017. In 2019 he joined Inter Milan, where he won Serie A in 2020–21 and was named the league's MVP. He returned to Chelsea in 2021 for a club-record fee, later returning to Inter on loan and then to Roma, before joining Napoli in 2024, where he won another Serie A title in 2024–25.

International career

Of Congolese descent, Lukaku debuted for Belgium in 2010 and has earned well over 100 caps, becoming his country's record goalscorer with more than 80 goals. He played at the World Cups of 2014, 2018 (where Belgium finished third) and 2022, and at several European Championships.
