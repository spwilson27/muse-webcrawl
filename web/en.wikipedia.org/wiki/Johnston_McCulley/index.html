Johnston McCulley

Johnston McCulley (2 February 1883 – 23 November 1958) was an American author, best known as the creator of the masked hero Zorro, who first appeared in the serialised novel The Curse of Capistrano (1919) in the pulp magazine All-Story Weekly.

A prolific pulp writer, McCulley produced hundreds of short stories and dozens of novels, many featuring recurring heroes and villains such as the Crimson Clown, Black Star and Thubway Tham. The success of the 1920 film The Mark of Zorro starring Douglas Fairbanks made Zorro a lasting figure in popular culture, inspiring films, serials, comics and television series.
