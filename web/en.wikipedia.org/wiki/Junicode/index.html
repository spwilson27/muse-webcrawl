Junicode
Designer: Peter S. Baker
Type: Serif, Unicode font
Licence: SIL Open Font License
Designed for: Medieval studies

Junicode (short for "Junius-Unicode") is a free and open-source serif typeface designed by Peter S. Baker of the University of Virginia for scholars of the Middle Ages. It is based on the typefaces used by the Clarendon Press and on the seventeenth- and eighteenth-century "Junius" types associated with the Oxford philologist Franciscus Junius.

Features

Junicode provides an exceptionally large repertoire of characters needed by medievalists, including runes, Old English and Insular letterforms, phonetic symbols and the complete Medieval Unicode Font Initiative (MUFI) character set, together with extensive OpenType features such as variant letterforms, ligatures and old-style figures. A second version, Junicode 2, reorganised the family with regular, semibold and bold weights and a variable-font design. It is distributed under the SIL Open Font License and is widely used in academic publishing on medieval texts.
