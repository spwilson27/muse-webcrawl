The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari

Das Cabinet des Dr. Caligari
Director: Robert Wiene
Year: 1920
Starring: Werner Krauss, Conrad Veidt
Style: German Expressionism

The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari (German: Das Cabinet des Dr. Caligari) is a 1920 German silent horror film directed by Robert Wiene. The sinister Dr. Caligari exhibits the somnambulist Cesare, who commits murders; a frame story twists the narrator's reliability.

Style

Its painted, jagged, distorted sets are the quintessential example of German Expressionist cinema, influencing horror, film noir and directors from Tim Burton onward.

Interpretation

Siegfried Kracauer read it as an allegory of authoritarian tyranny in post-war Germany in From Caligari to Hitler.
