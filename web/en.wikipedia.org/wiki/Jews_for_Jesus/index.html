Jews for Jesus
Founded: 1973, San Rafael, California
Founder: Moishe Rosen
Type: Evangelical Christian missionary organisation

Jews for Jesus is an evangelical Christian missionary organisation that seeks to convert Jews to belief in Jesus as the Messiah. Founded in 1973 by Moishe Rosen, a Baptist minister born to Jewish parents, it grew out of the Jesus movement of the early 1970s and is headquartered in San Francisco, with branches in cities around the world.

Activities and theology

The organisation is known for street evangelism, distinctive broadsides and slogans ("Jews for Jesus" itself began as a slogan), music and drama groups, and campaigns in cities with large Jewish populations. Theologically it holds standard evangelical positions — including the Trinity and salvation through faith in Jesus — while presenting Jesus's Jewishness as evidence that belief in him is compatible with Jewish identity.

Reception

Mainstream Jewish organisations across denominations reject the group's claims, holding that belief in Jesus as Messiah and God is incompatible with Judaism, and counter-missionary groups respond to its campaigns. Jews for Jesus has also been involved in litigation over trademarks and the use of its name by critics online.
