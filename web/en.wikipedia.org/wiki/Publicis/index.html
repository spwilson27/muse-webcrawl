Publicis Groupe S.A. is a French multinational advertising and public relations company. One of the oldest marketing organisations in the world and among the largest advertising holding companies, it is headquartered in Paris and competes with groups such as WPP, Omnicom and Interpublic. Following its acquisition-driven growth in digital services, Publicis became the world's largest advertising group by market capitalisation and, by the mid-2020s, by revenue.

History

Founding and early years

The firm was founded in 1926 by Marcel Bleustein-Blanchet, then aged twenty, who set up the agency in Paris; the name "Publicis" combines publicité (advertising) with six, the founder's lucky number. Publicis pioneered radio advertising in France and, after the Second World War — during which Bleustein-Blanchet served in the French Resistance under the codename "Blanchet" — rebuilt and expanded rapidly. In 1946 it opened its famous drugstore concept on the Champs-Élysées, the Publicis Drugstore, which became a Parisian landmark.

Expansion

From the 1970s the group internationalised, and after Bleustein-Blanchet's death in 1996 his daughter Élisabeth Badinter became a leading shareholder and board chair. Maurice Lévy, who joined in 1971 and led the company as CEO from 1987, transformed Publicis through acquisitions, including the purchases of Saatchi & Saatchi (2000) and Leo Burnett (2002), and later a series of digital agencies including Digitas (2007), Razorfish (2009) and Sapient (2015), and the data firm Epsilon (2019). A proposed merger with Omnicom Group, announced in 2013 to create the world's largest advertising company, was abandoned in 2014.

Recent years

Arthur Sadoun succeeded Lévy as chairman and CEO in 2017. The group reorganised around a "Power of One" model integrating creative, media, data and technology, invested heavily in its Marcel artificial-intelligence platform, and outperformed rivals during the early 2020s, overtaking WPP in market value.

Operations

Publicis networks and agencies include Leo Burnett, Saatchi & Saatchi, BBH, Publicis Worldwide, MSL (public relations), Publicis Media (Starcom, Zenith, Spark Foundry), the digital consultancy Publicis Sapient, and the data and identity business Epsilon. The group employs roughly 100,000 people in more than 100 countries.
