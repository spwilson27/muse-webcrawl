This Is Our Youth

This Is Our Youth is a play by American playwright Kenneth Lonergan, first performed in 1996. Set in March 1982 on Manhattan's Upper West Side, it follows three privileged but drifting young people over the course of roughly forty-eight hours.

Plot

Nineteen-year-old Warren Straub, kicked out of his father's home, steals $15,000 from his father, a lingerie magnate, and arrives at the apartment of his friend Dennis Ziegler, the charismatic son of a famous painter. Warren hopes to recover the money by buying and reselling cocaine. Complications follow when Jessica Goldman, a fashion student Warren admires, enters the picture, and the stolen cash is spent on a nostalgic spree including a night at the Plaza Hotel and the purchase of Warren's childhood toys — sold years earlier by his father.

Productions

The play premiered Off-Broadway in 1996 with the New Group and has been frequently revived. Notable productions include a 2002 London staging and a 2014 Broadway premiere at the Cort Theatre, directed by Anna D. Shapiro, starring Michael Cera, Kieran Culkin and Tavi Gevinson, following a run at Chicago's Steppenwolf Theatre.

Themes

Written against the backdrop of the Reagan era, the play examines aimlessness, wealth, grief — Warren's sister was murdered years before — and the painful transition from adolescence to adulthood.
