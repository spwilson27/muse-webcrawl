Amaretto sour

Amaretto sour
Type: Cocktail
Ingredients: amaretto liqueur, lemon juice, simple syrup, (optional) egg white, bitters
Served: on the rocks or up

An amaretto sour is a cocktail made with amaretto liqueur and lemon juice, belonging to the sour family of mixed drinks. It is typically sweetened with simple syrup and may include a small amount of lemon-lime soda or sour mix in simpler versions, while modern craft recipes often add a dash of bourbon, egg white, and aromatic bitters to balance the liqueur's sweetness.

Preparation

A common recipe combines about 45 ml amaretto, 30 ml fresh lemon juice, and 7–15 ml simple syrup, shaken with ice and strained over fresh ice in an old fashioned glass, garnished with a lemon twist and a cherry. A widely praised modern variant, popularised by bartender Jeffrey Morgenthaler, adds cask-proof bourbon and egg white, dry-shaken and then shaken with ice for a frothy texture.
