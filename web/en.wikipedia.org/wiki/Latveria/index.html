Latveria

Castle Doom, seat of Latveria's ruler, Victor von Doom

Latveria is a fictional country appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. It is a small, isolated nation in Central/Eastern Europe, landlocked between Hungary, Romania, and Serbia, and is ruled by the supervillain Doctor Victor von Doom. Latveria first appeared in Fantastic Four Annual #2 (1964), created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby.

Description

Latveria is depicted as a largely rural, mountainous kingdom of a few hundred thousand inhabitants, with its capital at Doomstadt, dominated by Castle Doom. The country is surrounded by the Carpathian Mountains and is often shown as frozen in a quasi-medieval character despite Doom's advanced technology, which keeps the population prosperous by fictional standards but under absolute authoritarian control. Doom maintains a network of Doombot robots to enforce his rule and project his presence.

History in the comics

Victor von Doom, born to a Romani family in Latveria, seized control of the country by overthrowing its oppressive monarchy and declared himself its ruler. Under Doom, Latveria became a recurring setting in Fantastic Four and Avengers storylines, variously invaded, liberated, or temporarily ruled by others during Doom's absences, though he almost invariably returns to reclaim it. The nation's diplomatic status is unusual: Doom's personal standing and arsenal make larger powers reluctant to intervene.

In other media

Latveria has appeared, or been referenced, in various animated series and in the Fantastic Four films, and is mentioned in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, notably in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (where Sokovia's fall is linked to it by fans) and in dialogue references in other productions.
