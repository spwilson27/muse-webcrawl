IAAF Golden League

Former annual series of track and field meetings, 1998–2009

A Golden League meeting

The IAAF Golden League was an annual series of outdoor track and field meetings organised by the International Association of Athletics Federations (IAAF, now World Athletics) from 1998 to 2009. It succeeded the earlier Golden Four series and was itself replaced by the Diamond League in 2010.

Format and jackpot

The series was famous for its jackpot: athletes who won their event at every meeting of the season shared a prize pool, originally one million US dollars in gold bars, later paid in cash. In some years the prize was split so that athletes winning at five of the six meetings shared half of the pot. Meetings in the series included the Bislett Games in Oslo, the Golden Gala in Rome, the Meeting Areva in Paris, the Weltklasse in Zürich, the Memorial Van Damme in Brussels and the ISTAF in Berlin, though the line-up varied by year.

History

Only a handful of athletes ever claimed the full jackpot, and the format made the Golden League events among the most prestigious one-day meetings in the sport. When the Diamond League launched in 2010 with a season-long points race across more meetings and disciplines, the Golden League ended.
