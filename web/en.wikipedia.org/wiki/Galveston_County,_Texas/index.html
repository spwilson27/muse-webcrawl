Galveston County is a county located in the U.S. state of Texas, on the Gulf Coast adjacent to Galveston Bay. Its county seat is the city of Galveston, while League City is its largest city by population. The county is part of the Houston–The Woodlands–Sugar Land metropolitan area and had a population of roughly 350,000 in the 2020 census.

History

The county was established in 1838 during the Republic of Texas era and named for Bernardo de Gálvez, the Spanish colonial governor and viceroy. Galveston was one of Texas's most important ports in the 19th century. The Galveston hurricane of September 8, 1900, the deadliest natural disaster in United States history, devastated the island city, killing an estimated 6,000–12,000 people; afterward the island was raised and protected by a seawall. Hurricane Ike struck the county in 2008, causing extensive damage.

Geography and communities

The county includes Galveston Island and part of Bolivar Peninsula, as well as mainland communities such as Texas City, Dickinson, League City, Friendswood and Kemah. Major industries include the Port of Galveston, petrochemical plants around Texas City, healthcare, tourism and the University of Texas Medical Branch in Galveston.
