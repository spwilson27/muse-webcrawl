Mindan

Mindan (Korean: 민단; formally the Korean Residents Union in Japan, 在日本大韓民国民団) is an organisation representing South Korea-aligned ethnic Koreans (Zainichi Koreans) permanently resident in Japan. Founded in 1946, its counterpart aligned with North Korea is Chongryon.

Role

Mindan supports Korean residents with community services, cultural and language education, liaison with Japanese authorities, and campaigning on issues such as local voting rights for permanent residents. It maintains ties with the government of South Korea, and special permanent residents affiliated with it generally hold South Korean nationality.

Relations with Chongryon

The two organisations long mirrored the division of the Korean peninsula, though joint statements in the 2000s explored reconciliation. Mindan's membership has declined as younger generations naturalise as Japanese citizens or identify less with peninsula politics.
