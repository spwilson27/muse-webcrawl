Godzilla vs. Kong is a 2021 American monster film directed by Adam Wingard and produced by Legendary Pictures. It is the fourth film in the MonsterVerse franchise, following Godzilla: King of the Monsters (2019) and Kong: Skull Island (2017), and pits the two iconic monsters against each other for the first time since the 1962 Japanese film King Kong vs. Godzilla.

Plot

Five years after Godzilla defeated King Ghidorah, the organisation Monarch confines Kong on Skull Island for his protection, since two alpha Titans cannot coexist. When Godzilla inexplicably attacks a facility of Apex Cybernetics, Apex enlists geologist Nathan Lind and Kong expert Ilene Andrews to use Kong to find a power source in the Hollow Earth. Kong and Godzilla clash in the Tasman Sea and again in Hong Kong, where Apex's secret weapon, Mechagodzilla, powered by Ghidorah's telepathic remains, goes out of control; the two monsters unite to destroy it.

Release and reception

The film was released simultaneously in cinemas and on the HBO Max streaming service in March 2021, during the COVID-19 pandemic, and became one of the most successful films of the year, grossing about $470 million worldwide despite its streaming availability. Critics praised its visual effects and monster sequences while finding the human storylines thin. A sequel, Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire, followed in 2024.
