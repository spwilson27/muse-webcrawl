George Manneh Oppong Weah (born 1 October 1966) is a Liberian politician and former professional footballer who served as the 25th president of Liberia from 2018 to 2024. As a striker, he is regarded as one of the greatest African footballers of all time; in 1995 he became the first and only African player to win both the Ballon d'Or and the FIFA World Player of the Year award.

Football career

Weah began his career in Liberia and Cameroon before being brought to Europe in 1988 by coach Arsène Wenger, who signed him for Monaco. He won the French Cup with Monaco in 1991, then moved to Paris Saint-Germain, winning Ligue 1 in 1994 and reaching the semi-finals of the European Cup. In 1995 he joined AC Milan, where he won two Serie A titles (1996 and 1999) and scored a famous coast-to-coast goal against Verona in 1996, dribbling the length of the pitch. Later spells followed at Chelsea (where he won the 2000 FA Cup), Manchester City and Marseille. Although he starred for the Liberian national team for nearly two decades, Liberia never qualified for a World Cup; Weah famously helped fund the team during its 2002 qualification campaign.

Political career

After retiring, Weah entered politics. He ran for president in 2005, losing the run-off to Ellen Johnson Sirleaf, and was elected senator for Montserrado County in 2014. Running again for the presidency at the head of the Coalition for Democratic Change, he defeated Vice-President Joseph Boakai in the December 2017 run-off and was inaugurated on 22 January 2018. His presidency pursued the "Pro-Poor" agenda of infrastructure, education and economic reform but faced criticism over corruption and economic hardship. In the 2023 election he lost the run-off to Boakai and conceded peacefully, earning international praise for a democratic transfer of power.
