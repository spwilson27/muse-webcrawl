Special unitary group

In mathematics, the special unitary group of degree n, denoted SU(n), is the Lie group of n × n unitary matrices with determinant 1. Together with the orthogonal and symplectic groups it forms one of the classical families of Lie groups.

Properties

SU(n) is a compact, simply connected (for the standard topology) Lie group of dimension n² − 1. Its Lie algebra, su(n), consists of traceless skew-Hermitian matrices. SU(1) is trivial; SU(2) is diffeomorphic to the 3-sphere and double-covers the rotation group SO(3), making it fundamental to the description of spin in quantum mechanics.

Applications in physics

Unitary groups structure the Standard Model of particle physics: SU(3) is the gauge group of quantum chromodynamics, whose generators are the Gell-Mann matrices, while SU(2) forms part of the electroweak gauge group SU(2) × U(1). The generators of SU(n) generalize the Pauli matrices of SU(2).
