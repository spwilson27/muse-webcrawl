Templar (disambiguation)


The red cross of the Knights Templar

Templar most commonly refers to the Knights Templar, a medieval Catholic military order founded in 1119 and dissolved in 1312. It may also refer to:

Religious and fraternal orders

  • Knights Templar (Freemasonry), a Masonic appendant body
  • Templars of Honor and Temperance, a fraternal organisation
  • Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem, a modern ecumenical chivalric order
  • Order of the Solar Temple (Ordre du Temple solaire), a twentieth-century religious group

People

  • Simon Templar, fictional character known as "The Saint", created by Leslie Charteris
  • Templar (surname), borne by several individuals

Places

  • Templar, a location associated with Temple churches and commanderies in several European countries
  • The Temple, London, site of the Temple Church and the Inns of Court

Other uses

  • Templar (typeface), a display typeface
  • Templar, a fictional class of characters and organisations in several video games
  • Good Templars, members of the International Organisation of Good Templars, a temperance movement
