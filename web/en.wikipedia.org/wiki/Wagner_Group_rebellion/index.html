Wagner Group rebellion
Date: 23–24 June 2023
Location: Russia
Leader: Yevgeny Prigozhin
Result: Rebellion halted; deal brokered by Alexander Lukashenko

The Wagner Group rebellion was a short-lived armed mutiny in Russia on 23–24 June 2023, during the Russian invasion of Ukraine. Yevgeny Prigozhin, head of the Wagner private military company, accused the Russian Ministry of Defence of attacking his forces and announced a "march for justice" against the military leadership.

Events

Wagner forces seized Rostov-on-Don, including the Southern Military District headquarters, and advanced in convoys toward Moscow, shooting down several Russian aircraft. President Vladimir Putin denounced the move as treason. After negotiations mediated by Belarusian leader Alexander Lukashenko, Prigozhin halted the advance about 200 km from Moscow and agreed to relocate to Belarus; criminal charges were dropped and Wagner fighters were offered contracts with the Ministry of Defence.

Aftermath

Two months later, on 23 August 2023, Prigozhin died in a plane crash in Tver Oblast. Wagner's operations were subsequently restructured and largely absorbed into state-controlled structures, including the Africa Corps.
