Chisel

A chisel is a hand tool with a characteristically shaped cutting edge at the end of a blade, used for carving or cutting hard materials such as wood, stone and metal. Force is applied by hand or with a mallet or hammer.

Types

Woodworking chisels include bench chisels, mortise chisels and paring chisels; stonemasons use point, tooth and flat chisels; cold chisels are hardened for cutting metal. The cutting edge is typically ground to a bevel of about 25–30 degrees.

History

Chisels are among the oldest tools: flint and bronze examples date to prehistory, and chisel marks are visible on ancient Egyptian stonework.
