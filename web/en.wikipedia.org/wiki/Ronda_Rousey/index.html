Ronda Jean Rousey (born February 1, 1987) is an American professional wrestler, actress and former mixed martial artist and judoka. She won a bronze medal in judo at the 2008 Beijing Olympics — the first American woman to medal in Olympic judo — before becoming the first female fighter signed by the UFC and its inaugural women's bantamweight champion.

MMA career

Rousey defended the UFC title six times, finishing most opponents in the first round with her signature armbar. Her reign made women's MMA mainstream and made her one of the biggest pay-per-view stars in the sport. She lost the title to Holly Holm in November 2015 and a comeback fight to Amanda Nunes in December 2016, then effectively retired from MMA.

Wrestling and other work

Rousey joined WWE in 2018, winning the Raw Women's Championship at SummerSlam that year and headlining WrestleMania 35 in the event's first all-women main event. She has also acted in films including The Expendables 3 and Furious 7, and published the autobiography My Fight / Your Fight. She was inducted into the UFC Hall of Fame in 2018.
