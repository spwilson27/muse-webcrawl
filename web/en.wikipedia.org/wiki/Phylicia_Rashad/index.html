Phylicia Rashad (born Phylicia Ayers-Allen; June 19, 1948) is an American actress, singer, and theatre director. She is best known for her role as Clair Huxtable on the NBC sitcom The Cosby Show (1984–1992), which established her as a prominent figure in American television.

Early life and education

Rashad was born in Houston, Texas, the daughter of Vivian Ayers, a Pulitzer Prize–nominated artist, poet, and publisher, and Andrew Arthur Allen, an orthodontist. Her sister is the actress and choreographer Debbie Allen. She graduated magna cum laude from Howard University in 1970.

Career

After early stage work and a role on the soap opera One Life to Live, Rashad achieved national fame as attorney Clair Huxtable. On Broadway she won the Tony Award for Best Actress in a Play for A Raisin in the Sun (2004), becoming the first Black actress to win in that category. Her later work includes For Colored Girls (2010), the Creed film series, and This Is Us. In 2021 she became dean of the Chadwick A. Boseman College of Fine Arts at Howard University.
