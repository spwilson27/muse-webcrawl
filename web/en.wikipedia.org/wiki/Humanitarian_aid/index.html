Humanitarian aid

Humanitarian aid is material and logistic assistance given to people in need — typically in response to crises including natural disasters, epidemics and armed conflict — intended to save lives, alleviate suffering and maintain human dignity. It is distinguished from development aid by its emergency, needs-based character, and is guided by the principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence.

Major providers include UN agencies (OCHA for coordination, the World Food Programme, UNHCR, UNICEF), the Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, NGOs such as Médecins Sans Frontières, and government agencies. Aid is delivered as food, water, shelter, healthcare and cash; challenges include access in war zones, security of aid workers, funding shortfalls against assessed needs, and the 'humanitarian–development nexus' of protracted crises.
