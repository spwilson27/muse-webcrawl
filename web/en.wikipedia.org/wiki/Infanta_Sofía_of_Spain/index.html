Infanta Sofía of Spain (born April 29, 2007) is a member of the Spanish royal family. She is the younger daughter of King Felipe VI and Queen Letizia and the younger sister of Leonor, Princess of Asturias. She is second in the line of succession to the Spanish throne, after her sister.

Life and education

Sofía was born in Madrid and baptised in the Zarzuela Palace; she was named after her paternal grandmother, Queen Sofía. Like her sister, she was educated at Santa María de los Rosales school in Madrid and later attended UWC Atlantic College in Wales, where she completed the International Baccalaureate, graduating in 2025. As an infanta — the title borne by children of the Spanish monarch who are not the heir — she accompanies her family at official ceremonies such as National Day, the opening of the Cortes, and family engagements, and occasionally represents the Crown at events such as the Spanish national football team's celebrations.
