Tunicate

A tunicate is a marine invertebrate in the subphylum Tunicata (Urochordata), a group of chordates that includes sea squirts, salps and larvaceans — over 3,000 species worldwide.

Biology

Adult tunicates are mostly sessile filter feeders enclosed in a tunic of tunicin, a cellulose-like material. Their free-swimming tadpole-like larvae possess a notochord and dorsal nerve cord, revealing their chordate affinities; molecular evidence places tunicates as the closest relatives of vertebrates. Many, such as salps, are pelagic and colonial.
