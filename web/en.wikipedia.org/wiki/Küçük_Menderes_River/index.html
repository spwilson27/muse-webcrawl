Küçük Menderes River

The Küçük Menderes River ("Little Meander"), known in antiquity as the Kaystros or Cayster River, is a river in western Turkey, in İzmir Province, south of the better-known Büyük Menderes (the classical Meander).

The river rises in the mountains east of Ödemiş and flows westward through a fertile agricultural plain, passing the towns of Ödemiş, Tire region and Selçuk, before emptying into the Aegean Sea near Pamucak beach. It has a length of roughly 160 kilometres.

The lower valley lies close to the ancient city of Ephesus; in antiquity the river's silt contributed to the silting of the harbour of Ephesus, which today lies several kilometres inland from the coast.
