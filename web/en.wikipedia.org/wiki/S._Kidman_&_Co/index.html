S. Kidman & Co
Industry: Beef cattle
Founded: 1899
Founder: Sidney Kidman
Headquarters: Adelaide, South Australia

S. Kidman & Co is one of Australia's largest beef producers, operating a string of cattle stations across the outback. The company was founded by Sidney Kidman, the legendary "Cattle King", who built an empire of pastoral leases stretching along stock routes from the Channel Country of Queensland to markets in the south.

Holdings

At its height, Kidman's holdings covered more than 200,000 square kilometres — an area larger than many European countries. Well-known stations in the portfolio have included Anna Creek Station in South Australia, long described as the world's largest cattle station, together with properties such as Durham Downs, Innamincka and Macumba.

Sale

In 2016 the Kidman portfolio was sold to a consortium led by Gina Rinehart's Hancock Prospecting (with a minority stake held by Shanghai CRED Real Estate), after the Australian government blocked earlier proposed sales to foreign buyers on national-interest grounds. The company continues to trade under the Kidman name.
