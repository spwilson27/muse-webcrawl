Millennium Stadium

Millennium Stadium
Current name: Principality Stadium
Location: Cardiff, Wales
Opened: 1999
Capacity: 73,931

The Millennium Stadium, known since 2016 for sponsorship reasons as the Principality Stadium, is the national stadium of Wales. Located in Cardiff, it is the home of the Wales national rugby union team and is owned by the Welsh Rugby Union. It was built for the 1999 Rugby World Cup, which Wales hosted.

Features

The stadium has a capacity of 73,931 and was the first stadium in Britain with a fully retractable roof, allowing play in all weathers; its natural grass pitch was long installed on a system of pallets. Four 90-metre masts support the roof structure. While Wembley Stadium in London was rebuilt (2001–2007), the Millennium Stadium hosted the FA Cup and League Cup finals and other major English football events.

Events

Beyond rugby — including Six Nations matches and Rugby World Cup fixtures in 1999, 2007 and 2015 — the stadium hosted football at the 2012 Olympics and the 2017 UEFA Champions League Final. It is also a major concert venue, having hosted acts such as U2, Madonna, the Rolling Stones and Beyoncé.
