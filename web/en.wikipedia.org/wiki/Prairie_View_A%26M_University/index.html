The Prairie View A&M campus

Prairie View A&M University (PVAMU) is a public historically black land-grant university in Prairie View, Texas. Founded in 1876, it is the second-oldest public institution of higher education in Texas and a member of the Texas A&M University System.

History

The university was established under the Morrill Act as the state's first public college for African Americans, opening as Prairie View State Normal School. It offers bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees through colleges including the Roy G. Perry College of Engineering and the School of Architecture.

Athletics

The Prairie View A&M Panthers compete in NCAA Division I in the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC); the football team plays at Panther Stadium.
