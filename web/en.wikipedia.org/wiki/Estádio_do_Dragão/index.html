Estádio do Dragão

Estádio do Dragão (Dragon Stadium) is a football stadium in Porto, Portugal, and the home ground of FC Porto. Designed by architect Manuel Salgado, it opened on 16 November 2003, replacing the club's old Estádio das Antas, and was built as a venue for UEFA Euro 2004.

History

The stadium was inaugurated with a friendly between FC Porto and FC Barcelona, in which the 16-year-old Lionel Messi made his first-team debut for Barcelona. During Euro 2004 it hosted five matches, including the opening game, in which Greece upset hosts Portugal 2–1. In 2019 the stadium hosted the first UEFA Nations League final, won by Portugal, and in 2021 it stepped in to host the UEFA Champions League Final, in which Chelsea defeated Manchester City 1–0.

Name

The name derives from the dragon on FC Porto's crest. Earlier names proposed for the stadium included keeping the Antas name, but the club chose Dragão to reflect its symbol and identity.
