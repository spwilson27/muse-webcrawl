Potato

Potato
TypeStarchy tuber (Solanum tuberosum)
OriginAndes, South America
RankWorld's fourth-largest food crop (after maize, wheat, rice)
[Image: A pile of freshly dug potatoes with soil still on them]

The potato is a starchy tuberous vegetable native to the Americas, domesticated in the Andes and brought to Europe in the 16th century, where it became a staple whose yields transformed populations — and whose blight-driven failure caused Ireland's Great Famine of the 1840s (see the wider crawl's Ireland history).

Importance

Potatoes are the world's fourth-largest food crop after maize, Wheat and Rice, grown on every inhabited continent; China and India lead production. Chips, fries, vodka and starch all descend from this Columbian-exchange tuber, and its wild relatives' genes guard the crop against new blights.
