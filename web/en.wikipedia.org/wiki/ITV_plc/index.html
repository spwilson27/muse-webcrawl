ITV plc is a British media company that holds 13 of the 15 licences in the ITV network, the UK's largest commercial television network, and operates the streaming service ITVX. It was formed on 2 February 2004 from the merger of Granada plc and Carlton Communications, uniting most of the ITV franchises in England and Wales under one company.

Operations

The company's Media & Entertainment division broadcasts channels including ITV1, ITV2, ITV3, ITV4 and ITVBe, funded mainly by advertising. Its production arm, ITV Studios, is one of the world's largest content producers, making programmes such as Coronation Street, Love Island and The Voice formats sold internationally, with production bases in the UK, US, Australia, Europe and elsewhere.

History

Before the 2004 merger, the ITV network consisted of regional franchise companies; Granada and Carlton progressively acquired most of them during the 1990s. ITV plc subsequently bought the remaining Channel Television (2011) and UTV in Northern Ireland (2016); STV in Scotland remains independent. The company launched ITVX, its ad-funded streaming platform, in December 2022, replacing ITV Hub.
