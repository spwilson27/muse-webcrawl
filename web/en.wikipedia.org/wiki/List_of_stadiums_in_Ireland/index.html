The following is a list of stadiums in Ireland, covering both the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland, ordered by capacity. The largest is Croke Park in Dublin, headquarters of the Gaelic Athletic Association and the third-largest stadium in Europe. The list includes grounds used for Gaelic games, association football and rugby union.

Defunct stadiums

Lansdowne Road, demolished in 2007 and replaced by the Aviva Stadium on the same site, was the world's oldest rugby ground still in use at the time of its closure. Tolka Park and other historic League of Ireland grounds are also recorded in club histories.
