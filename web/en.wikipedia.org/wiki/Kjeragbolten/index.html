Kjeragbolten

Kjeragbolten is a boulder on the Kjerag mountain in Rogaland county, Norway. The rock is a 5-cubic-metre (180 cu ft) glacial deposit wedged in a crevice in the mountain, suspended above a 984-metre (3,228 ft) deep abyss above the Lysefjorden.

Geology and tourism

The boulder was deposited during the last glacial period, around 50,000 BC, as glacial meltwater washed away weaker surrounding rock. It is a popular hiking destination and tourist attraction, reachable without climbing equipment, and visitors commonly stand on the boulder for photographs, despite the sheer drop beneath it. Kjerag and Kjeragbolten are also popular sites for BASE jumping.

Access

The hike to Kjeragbolten begins at the Øygardsstølen visitor centre above Lysebotn and takes several hours round trip over steep, chained sections of rocky terrain.
