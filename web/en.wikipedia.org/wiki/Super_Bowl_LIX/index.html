Super Bowl LIX

Super Bowl LIX was an American football championship game played on February 9, 2025, at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana, to determine the champion of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2024 season. The National Football Conference (NFC) champion Philadelphia Eagles defeated the American Football Conference (AFC) champion Kansas City Chiefs, 40–22, denying Kansas City an unprecedented third consecutive Super Bowl title.

Background

The game was a rematch of Super Bowl LVII two years earlier, which the Chiefs had won 38–35. Kansas City, seeking the first "three-peat" of the Super Bowl era, finished the regular season 15–2, while Philadelphia went 14–3 behind running back Saquon Barkley, the NFL Offensive Player of the Year. It was the record eighth Super Bowl hosted at the Superdome and the eleventh in New Orleans overall.

Game summary

Philadelphia's defense dominated from the start, pressuring Patrick Mahomes without blitzing and intercepting him twice in the first half — including a pick-six by rookie Cooper DeJean on his 22nd birthday, and a Zack Baun interception that set up a touchdown pass to A.J. Brown. The Eagles led 24–0 at halftime and 34–0 late in the third quarter on Jalen Hurts' 46-yard touchdown to DeVonta Smith. Hurts was named Super Bowl MVP, accounting for three touchdowns (two passing, one rushing) and 221 passing yards against an interception.

Entertainment

Jon Batiste performed the national anthem, and Kendrick Lamar headlined the halftime show — his second Super Bowl halftime appearance and first as a solo headliner — with a guest appearance by SZA. The broadcast, split between Fox and Tubi, set a U.S. television record with an average audience of about 127.7 million viewers.
