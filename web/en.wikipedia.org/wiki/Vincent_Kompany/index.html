Vincent Jean Mpoy Kompany (born 10 April 1986) is a Belgian professional football manager and former player who played as a centre-back. He is regarded as one of the greatest defenders and captains in Premier League history, best known for captaining Manchester City through the most successful era in the club's history. He is the current head coach of Bayern Munich.

Vincent Kompany captaining Manchester City.

Playing career

Kompany began his career at Anderlecht, winning two Belgian league titles and the Belgian Golden Shoe, before moving to Hamburger SV in Germany in 2006. In 2008 he joined Manchester City for around £6 million, just before the club's takeover by Abu Dhabi United Group. Made captain in 2011, he led City to four Premier League titles, two FA Cups, and four League Cups. His long-range winning goal against Leicester City in May 2019, in the penultimate match of the title race, is one of the most famous goals in Premier League history.

For Belgium, Kompany earned 89 caps and was a leader of the country's "golden generation", reaching the semi-finals of the 2018 World Cup. Recurring calf injuries limited his appearances in his later seasons.

Managerial career

In 2019 Kompany returned to Anderlecht as player-manager, retiring from playing in 2020 to coach full-time. In 2022 he took over at Burnley, winning the EFL Championship with a record points haul in his first season; after Burnley's relegation from the Premier League in 2024, he was appointed head coach of Bayern Munich, winning the Bundesliga title in his first season in Germany.
