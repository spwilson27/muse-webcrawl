Highbury is an area in the London Borough of Islington, north London, centred on Highbury Fields and Highbury Barn. Developed from the late 18th century onwards as a suburb of terraced houses and villas around the old manor of Highbury, it is a predominantly residential district known for its Georgian and Victorian squares and for Highbury Fields, one of the largest open spaces in the borough.

Arsenal Stadium

Highbury gave its name to Arsenal Stadium, universally known as "Highbury", the home of Arsenal Football Club from 1913 until 2006. Designed in part by Archibald Leitch and redeveloped in the 1930s with celebrated Art Deco East and West Stands, the stadium held over 38,000 spectators in its final years. After Arsenal moved to the Emirates Stadium in 2006, the ground was redeveloped as Highbury Square, a residential complex that preserved the listed façades of the East and West Stands and turned the pitch into a communal garden.

Transport

The area is served by Highbury & Islington station (Victoria line, London Overground and National Rail), and by Arsenal and Holloway Road stations on the Piccadilly line nearby.
