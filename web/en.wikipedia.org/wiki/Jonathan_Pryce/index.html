Jonathan Pryce

Born: John Price, 1 June 1947, Carmel, Flintshire, Wales
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 1970–present
Awards: Two Tony Awards; two Olivier Awards

Sir Jonathan Pryce CBE (born John Price; 1 June 1947) is a Welsh actor who is known for his performances on stage and in film and television. After studying at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art, he began his career as a stage actor in the early 1970s and has since been praised for his versatility, receiving two Tony Awards and two Laurence Olivier Awards.

Career

Pryce came to prominence at the Royal Shakespeare Company and Nottingham Playhouse, winning acclaim for his Hamlet (1980). On film he is known for Terry Gilliam's Brazil (1985), in which he played the dreamer Sam Lowry, and for roles in Glengarry Glen Ross (1992), Evita (1996), in which he played Juan Perón, the James Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies (1997), and the Pirates of the Caribbean series as Governor Weatherby Swann. He created the role of the Engineer in the musical Miss Saigon (1989), winning Tony and Olivier Awards.

Later work

Pryce played the High Sparrow in Game of Thrones, Cardinal Wolsey in Wolf Hall, and Pope Francis in The Two Popes (2019), earning his first Academy Award nomination. In 2022 he joined the cast of The Crown as Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh. He was knighted in 2021 for services to drama and charity.
