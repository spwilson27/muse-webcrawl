Sergio Pininfarina

Sergio Pininfarina
Born: 8 September 1926, Turin, Italy
Died: 3 July 2012, Turin
Occupation: Automobile designer, businessman, politician

Sergio Pininfarina (born Sergio Farina; 8 September 1926 – 3 July 2012) was an Italian automobile designer and businessman who led the Pininfarina design house founded by his father, Battista "Pinin" Farina. Under his leadership the company became one of the most influential car design firms in the world, creating the styling of numerous Ferrari, Maserati, Peugeot, and Alfa Romeo models.

Career

After graduating in mechanical engineering from the Polytechnic University of Turin, Pininfarina joined the family firm in 1950 and became its president in 1966. He oversaw designs such as the Ferrari Testarossa, the Fiat 124 Sport Spider, and the Peugeot 205 and 406 Coupé, and built the company's wind tunnel and independent production facilities.

Later life

Pininfarina served as a Member of the European Parliament and was appointed a Senator for Life of the Italian Republic in 2005. He died in Turin in 2012.
