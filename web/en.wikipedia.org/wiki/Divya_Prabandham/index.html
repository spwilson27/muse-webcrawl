Divya Prabandham

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


The Alvars, authors of the Divya Prabandham

The Divya Prabandham (Tamil: "Divine Collection") is a collection of 4,000 Tamil verses (Naalayiram) composed by the 12 Alvars, the poet-saints of South India devoted to the Hindu god Vishnu. It is regarded in the Sri Vaishnava tradition as the "Tamil Veda" and is recited alongside the Sanskrit Vedas in temples.

The hymns, composed between roughly the 6th and 9th centuries CE, were collected and systematized by Nathamuni in the 9th-10th century, after the tradition holds that he recovered the lost verses. The most famous portion is the Tiruvaymoli of Nammalvar, considered the essence of the work; other major contributors include Periyalvar, Andal (the only female Alvar, author of the Tiruppavai), Kulasekhara Alvar, and Tirumangai Alvar.

Content and significance

The verses are devotional in character, praising Vishnu and his avatars, especially Krishna and Rama, and celebrating the 108 Divya Desams, the holy abodes of Vishnu sung by the Alvars. The Divya Prabandham is central to the liturgy and festivals of Sri Vaishnava temples, where it is chanted in the original Tamil during temple rituals and processions.
