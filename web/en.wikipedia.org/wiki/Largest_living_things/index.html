The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever existed.

This article lists the largest living things — the biggest organisms, animals, plants and fungi known, both by mass and by dimensions. The largest animal ever known is the blue whale (Balaenoptera musculus), which can reach lengths of around 30 metres (98 ft) and masses approaching 180–200 tonnes. The largest land animal alive today is the African bush elephant.

Among individual organisms, the largest known single-stem tree by volume is the giant sequoia known as General Sherman, while the clonal quaking aspen colony known as Pando in Utah covers about 43 hectares and consists of tens of thousands of genetically identical stems connected by a single root system. A honey fungus, Armillaria ostoyae, in Oregon's Malheur National Forest spans roughly 9.6 km² and is often described as the largest single organism by area.

Largest plants and fungi

The tallest living tree is Hyperion, a coast redwood in California measured at about 115.9 m. The stoutest trees include the baobabs and the Árbol del Tule Montezuma cypress in Mexico. Clonal colonies such as Pando and vast seagrass meadows of Posidonia oceanica in the Mediterranean rank among the largest organisms by area and mass.
