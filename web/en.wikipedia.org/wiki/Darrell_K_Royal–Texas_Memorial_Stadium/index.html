Home of the Texas Longhorns football team.

Darrell K Royal–Texas Memorial Stadium (abbreviated DKR), located in Austin, Texas, on the campus of the University of Texas, has been home to the Longhorns football team since 1924. The stadium was dedicated in 1924 as a memorial to Texans who died in World War I, and was named in 1996 for former Longhorns head coach Darrell K Royal.

History

Construction began in 1924 with funds raised by students, alumni and the athletics council, and the stadium opened with a seating capacity of about 27,000. Successive expansions added an upper deck, suites and club seating, and a major addition to the north end zone. In 2009 the official capacity exceeded 100,000 for the first time, making it one of the largest stadiums in the United States and the largest in the Big 12 Conference at the time. A subsequent expansion of the south end zone added the "Godzillatron" era's successor scoreboards and premium facilities.

Traditions

The stadium is known for its pageantry, including the Longhorn Band's "Showband of the Southwest" performances and the cannon "Smokey" fired after Texas scores. Since 2021, the stadium's south end zone has featured the Moody Center-adjacent expansion and a design intended to keep crowd noise contained on the field.
