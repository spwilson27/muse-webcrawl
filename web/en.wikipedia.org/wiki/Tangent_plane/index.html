Tangent plane

A tangent plane to a surface at a given point is the plane that "just touches" the surface at that point, generalizing the tangent line to a curve. If the surface is given by a differentiable function z = f(x, y), the tangent plane at (x0, y0) has the equation z = f(x0, y0) + fx(x − x0) + fy(y − y0), and provides the best linear approximation to the surface near the point.

Properties

The tangent plane contains the tangent lines to every smooth curve on the surface passing through the point. A vector perpendicular to it is the surface normal, obtainable from the gradient of an implicit defining function F(x, y, z) = 0. Tangent planes underpin linear approximation, optimization, and differential geometry of surfaces.
