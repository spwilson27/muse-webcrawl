MediaCityUK is a mixed-use property development on the banks of the Manchester Ship Canal at Salford Quays, Greater Manchester, England. Developed by the Peel Group, it was designed to become a major centre for the media and creative industries in the north of England.

History

The project arose from the BBC's decision, announced in the mid-2000s, to move significant operations out of London. In 2006 Salford Quays was chosen as the site, and construction began in 2007. The BBC began moving departments — including BBC Sport, BBC Children's, BBC Radio 5 Live, and BBC Breakfast — into three buildings at the site from 2011, relocating around 2,500 staff. ITV Studios later moved its northern operations, including the set of Coronation Street, to an adjacent site across the canal.

The development

MediaCityUK includes television and radio studios, offices, a hotel, residential apartments, retail and leisure space, and a public piazza used for events and outdoor broadcasts. The University of Salford operates a campus building there. The studios complex, later operated as dock10, provides some of the largest television studio facilities in Europe.

Transport

The site is served by the MediaCityUK tram stop on the Manchester Metrolink network, with services to Manchester city centre, and by local bus routes.
