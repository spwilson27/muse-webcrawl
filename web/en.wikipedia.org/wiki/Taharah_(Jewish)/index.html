Taharah (Jewish)

Taharah in Jewish practice refers to ritual purity, and specifically to the ritual washing and preparation of a deceased person's body before burial, performed by the chevra kadisha (burial society).

The ritual

The body is washed, purified with water poured in an unbroken stream, and dressed in simple white shrouds (tachrichim). The ritual is accompanied by prayers and readings, and is considered a profound act of loving-kindness, since it can never be repaid.
