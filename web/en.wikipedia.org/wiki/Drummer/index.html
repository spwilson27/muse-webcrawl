Drummer

A drummer is a musician who plays drums, particularly a drum kit, or other percussion instruments. In most popular music, rock, jazz and contemporary ensembles, the drummer provides rhythm, timekeeping and dynamic foundation, playing bass drum, snare drum, toms, hi-hat and cymbals with sticks and pedals.

Roles and techniques

Drummers use techniques including rudiments, matched and traditional grip, foot techniques for bass drum and hi-hat, and styles ranging from military and marching drumming to jazz ride patterns, rock backbeats and metal double-bass playing. Military drummers historically conveyed orders and kept marching pace.

Equipment

A standard kit comprises a bass drum, snare, rack and floor toms, hi-hat, crash and ride cymbals. Drummers may also use brushes, mallets, electronic pads and auxiliary percussion.
