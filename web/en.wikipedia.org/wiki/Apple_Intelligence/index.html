Apple Intelligence

Developer: Apple Inc.

Announced: 10 June 2024 (WWDC24)

Initial release: 28 October 2024

Platforms: iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS

Apple Intelligence is an artificial intelligence platform developed by Apple Inc. Announced at the Worldwide Developers Conference on 10 June 2024, it integrates generative models and personal context into Apple's operating systems, with an emphasis on on-device processing and privacy. Features began rolling out with iOS 18.1, iPadOS 18.1, and macOS Sequoia 15.1 in October 2024.

Features

Apple Intelligence powers system-wide Writing Tools that can proofread, rewrite, and summarise text; notification and email summaries and priority messages; image generation tools including Genmoji and Image Playground; photo editing features such as Clean Up; and a redesigned Siri with richer language understanding and on-screen awareness. Later updates added Visual Intelligence for identifying objects and text through the camera, and integration with OpenAI's ChatGPT for certain requests, routed only with user permission.

Technology and privacy

Many requests are processed on-device using compact foundation models running on Apple silicon. More complex requests can be sent to Private Cloud Compute, Apple's server architecture built on Apple silicon, designed so that personal data is not stored or made accessible to Apple. The platform requires devices with an A17 Pro chip or M-series chips and at least 8 GB of memory, limiting it to recent iPhone, iPad, and Mac models.

Reception and rollout

The staggered rollout of features drew mixed reviews, with some headline capabilities — notably the more personalised Siri — delayed beyond their originally advertised timeframe, leading to criticism and legal challenges over marketing claims. Availability also varies by language and region, with some features restricted in the European Union and China due to regulatory requirements.
