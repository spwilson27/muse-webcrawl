The flag of Åland

The flag of Åland is the national flag of the Åland Islands, an autonomous and demilitarised Swedish-speaking region of Finland. It consists of a red Nordic cross, outlined (fimbriated) in yellow, on a blue field. It was officially adopted on 3 April 1954.

Design and symbolism

The blue field and yellow cross derive from the flag of Sweden, reflecting Åland's Swedish language and culture, while the red cross comes from the coat of arms and flag tradition of Finland, symbolising the islands' political belonging to Finland. The design thus combines the colours of both countries: blue and yellow for Sweden, red added from Finland's arms.

History

An unofficial Åland flag from the 1920s, a horizontal tricolour of blue, yellow, and blue, was banned by Finnish authorities. After Åland received its own flag rights under the Autonomy Act, a competition produced the present Nordic cross design, first raised officially in Mariehamn in 1954. Åland celebrates its Flag Day on the last Sunday of April.
