Stagecoach Festival

The Stagecoach Festival is an annual outdoor country music festival held at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, in the Coachella Valley. First held in 2007, it is organised by Goldenvoice, the company behind the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival, and takes place on the same site, usually the weekend after Coachella's second weekend, in late April. It is widely described as the largest country music festival in the world.

Overview

Stagecoach presents a mixture of mainstream country stars, country rock, bluegrass, folk and Americana acts across several stages, the largest of which is the Mane Stage. Past headliners have included Kenny Chesney, Carrie Underwood, Luke Bryan, Eric Church, Tim McGraw, Shania Twain and Luke Combs. Alongside the music, the festival is known for its barbecue competitions, line dancing, a Ferris wheel, and Guy Fieri's barbecue stage demonstrations.

The inaugural festival in May 2007 featured George Strait and Kenny Chesney among its headliners and drew around 55,000 attendees over two days; it expanded to three days and grew steadily to sell out at roughly 85,000 attendees per day, making it the highest-grossing country music festival in the United States. The event was cancelled in 2020 and 2021 because of the COVID-19 pandemic and returned in 2022.

Related events

Stagecoach shares its venue and promoter with Coachella but has a distinct identity, audience culture — including extensive RV camping and tailgating — and dress codes that were the subject of public discussion in its early years. It should not be confused with the British transport operator Stagecoach Group.
