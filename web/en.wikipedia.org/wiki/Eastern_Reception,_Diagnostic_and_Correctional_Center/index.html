Eastern Reception, Diagnostic and Correctional Center

The Eastern Reception, Diagnostic and Correctional Center (ERDCC) is a state prison for men located in Bonne Terre, Missouri, operated by the Missouri Department of Corrections. It serves as one of the state's reception and diagnostic centres, where newly sentenced offenders are processed, assessed, and classified before assignment.

Operations

The facility opened in 2003 and houses offenders at multiple custody levels, including diagnostic, minimum, medium, and maximum security units. New arrivals undergo medical, mental health, educational, and security assessments during the reception process. The centre has a capacity of roughly 2,700 offenders.
