Kenmore (brand)

Kenmore laundry appliances

Kenmore is an American brand of household appliances owned and sold by Transform Holdco, historically the house brand of Sears. Kenmore products — including washing machines, dryers, refrigerators, dishwashers, vacuum cleaners and sewing machines — have traditionally been manufactured by major appliance makers such as Whirlpool, LG, Electrolux and Panasonic and sold under the Kenmore name.

History

The Kenmore name first appeared in 1913 on sewing machines sold through the Sears catalogue. The brand was extended to washing machines in 1927 and, over the following decades, to the full range of major and small appliances, becoming one of the most recognised appliance names in the United States. Model numbers on Kenmore products encode the actual manufacturer in their prefix, a well-known quirk used by repair technicians.

Decline of Sears and licensing

As Sears declined in the 2010s, the Kenmore brand was increasingly licensed to other retailers. In 2017 Sears began selling Kenmore appliances through Amazon, including Alexa-enabled "Kenmore Smart" models; Kenmore products later appeared at other retailers as well. Following Sears Holdings' bankruptcy in 2018, the brand passed to the reorganised Transformco organisation, which continues to market Kenmore appliances, water heaters, grills and floor-care products through various channels.
