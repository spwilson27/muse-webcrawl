Air New Zealand Limited is the flag carrier of New Zealand. Based in Auckland, it operates scheduled passenger flights to destinations in New Zealand and internationally, including Australia, the Pacific Islands, Asia and North America. Its main hub is Auckland Airport.

History

The airline began in 1940 as Tasman Empire Airways Limited (TEAL), a flying-boat service across the Tasman Sea. It became wholly New Zealand-owned in 1961, was renamed Air New Zealand in 1965, and was nationalised in 1978 following its merger with New Zealand National Airways Corporation. It was partly privatised in 1989, renationalised in 2001 after the collapse of its ownership of Ansett Australia, and the government subsequently sold down its stake.

Fleet and services

The fleet includes Boeing 777 and 787 Dreamliner aircraft for long-haul services, Airbus A320 family aircraft for short-haul international and domestic trunk routes, and ATR 72 and De Havilland Q300 turboprops operated by regional subsidiaries. The airline is known for its distinctive black livery and koru logo, and for creative safety videos.

Subsidiaries

Regional services are operated under the Air New Zealand Link brand. The airline is a member of the Star Alliance.
