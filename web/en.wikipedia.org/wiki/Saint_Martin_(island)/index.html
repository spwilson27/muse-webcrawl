Saint Martin (island)

Saint Martin
Location: Caribbean Sea (Leeward Islands)
Area: 87 km²
North: Collectivity of Saint Martin (France)
South: Sint Maarten (Netherlands)
Population: ~74,000 (combined)

Saint Martin is an island in the northeast Caribbean Sea, approximately 300 km east of Puerto Rico. Its 87 km² are divided roughly 60:40 between the French Republic (53 km²) and the Kingdom of the Netherlands (34 km²), making it the smallest inhabited island divided between two nations.

History

Christopher Columbus sighted the island on 11 November 1493, the feast day of Saint Martin of Tours. Although claimed by Spain, the island was settled by the French and Dutch in the 1620s–30s; the 1648 Treaty of Concordia formalised the division, which endures today. The northern French side forms the Collectivity of Saint Martin; the southern Dutch side, Sint Maarten, is a constituent country of the Netherlands.

Tourism

The island is a major tourist destination, famed for Maho Beach beside Princess Juliana International Airport, where aircraft pass low over the beach on final approach. Hurricane Irma devastated the island in September 2017.
