Overview

The Norte del Valle Cartel (Spanish: Cártel del Norte del Valle) was a Colombian drug cartel that operated principally in the northern Valle del Cauca department in southwestern Colombia. It rose to prominence in the 1990s following the fragmentation of the Cali Cartel, and at its height was estimated to be responsible for a large share of the cocaine shipped from Colombia to the United States.

History

The cartel was led by figures including the brothers Luis Enrique and Javier Antonio Calle Serna, known as "Los Comba", Diego León Montoya Sánchez ("Don Diego"), and Wilber Varela ("Jabón"). Montoya was listed on the FBI Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list before his capture in 2007; Varela was murdered in Venezuela in 2008.

Internal feuds and sustained pressure from Colombian and United States authorities dismantled the organisation during the 2000s. Successor groups, notably Los Rastrojos, emerged from its remnants.

Operations

The cartel moved cocaine by sea and air through Central America and Mexico, working with Mexican trafficking organisations, and used bribery of officials to protect its operations.
