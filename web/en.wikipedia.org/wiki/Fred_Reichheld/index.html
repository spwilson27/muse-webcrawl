Fred Reichheld

Frederick F. Reichheld (born April 6, 1952) is an American business author and loyalty consultant, a fellow of the management consultancy Bain & Company. He is best known as the creator of the Net Promoter Score (NPS), a widely used measure of customer loyalty.

Career

Reichheld joined Bain & Company in 1977 and founded its loyalty practice. His research, beginning with the 1990 Harvard Business Review article "Zero Defections: Quality Comes to Services" (with Earl Sasser), established statistical links between customer retention, employee loyalty and sustained profit growth.

Net Promoter Score

In his 2003 Harvard Business Review article "The One Number You Need to Grow", Reichheld proposed that companies measure loyalty by asking customers a single question — "How likely is it that you would recommend our company to a friend or colleague?" — and subtracting the percentage of detractors from the percentage of promoters. NPS has since been adopted by thousands of companies worldwide, though its statistical basis has been debated by academic critics.

Books

Reichheld's books include The Loyalty Effect (1996), Loyalty Rules! (2001), The Ultimate Question (2006) and its sequel The Ultimate Question 2.0 (2011, with Rob Markey), and Winning on Purpose (2021), which proposed the "earned growth rate" metric.
