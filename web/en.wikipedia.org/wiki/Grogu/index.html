Grogu

Grogu, colloquially referred to as Baby Yoda, is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, appearing as one of the main characters in the Disney+ television series The Mandalorian and its spin-offs. He is a toddler member of the same species as the Jedi Grand Master Yoda and the Jedi Master Yaddle. Grogu was created by series creator Jon Favreau and developed with executive producer Dave Filoni.

Character

In The Mandalorian, the bounty hunter Din Djarin is hired to capture a fifty-year-old "asset", who proves to be an infant of Yoda's species. Djarin rescues the child from Imperial remnants and becomes his protector and adoptive father. In the second season, the Jedi Ahsoka Tano reveals the child's name, Grogu, and explains that he was raised at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant and hidden after the fall of the Republic. Luke Skywalker later offers to train him; Grogu ultimately chooses to return to Djarin rather than complete his Jedi training, though he continues to display Force abilities, including telekinesis and Force healing.

Creation and design

The character was largely realised as a puppet created by Legacy Effects, supplemented by visual effects; the puppet cost about five million dollars to produce. Grogu's appearance in the series was kept secret before broadcast, an unusual step that meant no merchandise was available for the 2019 holiday season. His vocalisations were created from recordings of infants and animals, including a bat-eared fox and a kinkajou.

Reception

Grogu became a breakout character and a widespread internet meme under the name "Baby Yoda", generating enormous demand for toys and merchandise and drawing praise for his design and for the emotional relationship between him and Djarin. Critics have cited him as central to the success of The Mandalorian and to the launch of Disney+.
