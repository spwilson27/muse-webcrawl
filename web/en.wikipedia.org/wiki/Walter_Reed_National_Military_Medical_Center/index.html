Walter Reed National Military Medical Center

The Walter Reed National Military Medical Center (WRNMMC), formerly known as the National Naval Medical Center and colloquially as Bethesda Naval Hospital, is a United States military medical center located in Bethesda, Maryland. It is one of the most prominent military hospitals in the United States and has provided care for every U.S. president since Franklin D. Roosevelt.

History

The Naval Medical Center was commissioned in 1942, its tower modelled on the Nebraska State Capitol. In 2011, under the Base Realignment and Closure process, the Walter Reed Army Medical Center in Washington, D.C., was consolidated with the Bethesda hospital, creating the present joint facility named for Major Walter Reed, the Army physician who led the research confirming that yellow fever is transmitted by mosquitoes.

The center serves active-duty service members, veterans, and their families, and is a leading center for the treatment of wounded service members, prosthetics, and rehabilitation. Its presidential suite has treated presidents including John F. Kennedy, Lyndon B. Johnson, Ronald Reagan, and Donald Trump during his COVID-19 illness in 2020.
