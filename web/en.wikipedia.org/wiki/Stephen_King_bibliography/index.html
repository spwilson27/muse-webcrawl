Stephen King bibliography

Stephen King is an American author of horror, supernatural fiction, suspense, crime, science-fiction, and fantasy novels. His books have sold more than 350 million copies worldwide, and many have been adapted into films, television series, comic books, and stage plays.

King has published over 60 novels, including seven under the pen name Richard Bachman, and five non-fiction books. He has also written approximately 200 short stories, most of which have been published in book collections.

The Dark Tower series

The Dark Tower series, written over a period of more than three decades, comprises eight books, beginning with The Gunslinger (1982) and concluding with The Wind Through the Keyhole (2012).
