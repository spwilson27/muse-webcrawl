Real Club Deportivo de La Coruña, commonly known as Deportivo La Coruña, is a Spanish professional football club based in A Coruña, Galicia. Founded in 1906, the club plays its home matches at the Estadio Riazor.

History

Deportivo enjoyed its golden era in the 1990s and early 2000s under coach Javier Irureta, winning La Liga in 1999–2000 and the Copa del Rey in 1995 and 2002. The team famously overturned a 4–1 first-leg deficit against AC Milan in the 2003–04 UEFA Champions League quarter-finals, winning 4–0 at Riazor.

Honours

La Liga (1), Copa del Rey (2), Supercopa de España (3).
