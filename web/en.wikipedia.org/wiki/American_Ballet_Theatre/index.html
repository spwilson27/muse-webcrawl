American Ballet Theatre (ABT) is a classical ballet company based in New York City. Founded in 1939 by Richard Pleasant and Lucia Chase, it gave its first performances in 1940 as Ballet Theatre, with the aim of building a great American company and a "gallery" repertory preserving the classics alongside new American works. In 2006 it was recognised by an act of Congress as America's national ballet company.

Repertory and artists

ABT performs full-length classics such as Swan Lake, Giselle, The Sleeping Beauty, and Don Quixote, together with works by George Balanchine, Antony Tudor, Agnes de Mille, Jerome Robbins, Twyla Tharp, Alexei Ratmansky, and others. Agnes de Mille's Rodeo (1942) and Tudor's Pillar of Fire are landmarks of its early repertory. Star dancers associated with the company include Alicia Alonso, Mikhail Baryshnikov, Gelsey Kirkland, Natalia Makarova, Misty Copeland, and Herman Cornejo.

Leadership

Lucia Chase directed the company for decades; Baryshnikov was artistic director from 1980 to 1989, followed by Jane Hermann and Oliver Smith, then Kevin McKenzie (1992–2022). Susan Jaffe became artistic director in 2022. ABT tours widely in the United States and abroad and runs the Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis School.
