Dniester Canyon

The Dniester Canyon (Ukrainian: Дністровський каньйон) is a canyon formed by the Dniester River in western Ukraine, stretching roughly 250 km through the Ternopil, Ivano-Frankivsk, Chernivtsi, and Khmelnytskyi regions. It is one of the longest river canyons in Europe and one of the Seven Natural Wonders of Ukraine.

Geology and nature

The canyon cuts through Devonian and Silurian sedimentary rocks — limestones, sandstones, and shales — exposing strata around 400 million years old. Its steep slopes support rare steppe and forest flora and fauna. The area is protected as the Dniester Canyon National Nature Park, established in 2010, and is popular for rafting, hiking, and paragliding. Notable sights along the canyon include the Zalishchyky panorama, waterfalls, caves, and travertine formations.
