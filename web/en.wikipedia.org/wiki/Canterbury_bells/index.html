Canterbury bells

Canterbury bells (Campanula medium) is a flowering plant in the bellflower family, native to southern Europe. It is a popular biennial garden plant grown for its large bell-shaped blue, pink or white flowers.

Scientific name: Campanula medium
Family: Campanulaceae
Origin: Southern Europe

Cultivation

Plants are sown one year and flower the next, preferring sun or partial shade and well-drained soil. Numerous cultivars have been selected for flower colour and form.
