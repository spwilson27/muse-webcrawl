Solar dynasty

Rama, the most celebrated king of the solar dynasty

The Solar dynasty (Sanskrit: सूर्यवंश, Sūryavaṁśa, "solar lineage") is a legendary Hindu dynasty said to descend from Surya, the sun god, through his descendants Manu and Ikshvaku. Together with the Lunar dynasty (Chandravansha), it is one of the two great lineages from which the Kshatriya rulers of Indian epic tradition claimed descent.

Legendary genealogy

According to the Puranas and epics, the line runs from Surya (Vivasvan) to Vaivasvata Manu, and from Manu's son Ikshvaku, founder of the royal house of Ayodhya (Kosala). Famous kings of the Ikshvaku line include Sagara, Bhagiratha (who is said to have brought the Ganges down to earth), Raghu, Aja, Dasharatha, and Dasharatha's son Rama, hero of the Ramayana. Kalidasa's Sanskrit poem Raghuvaṁśa ("Dynasty of Raghu") celebrates this lineage. Lists in the Mahabharata and Puranas extend the solar line down to Brihadbala, said to have been killed by Abhimanyu in the Kurukshetra War.

Historical claims

Various historical ruling houses claimed Suryavanshi descent to legitimize their rule, including the Sisodia rulers of Mewar, who styled themselves descendants of Rama's line, and several Rajput clans and other dynasties of northern and western India. Claims of solar descent also spread beyond India with Indian cultural influence, appearing in the traditions of royal houses in Southeast Asia.
