Asia-Pacific (APAC)
Region: East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Oceania, Pacific rim
Major economies: China, Japan, India, South Korea, Australia

The Asia-Pacific (APAC) is the region of the world adjoining the western Pacific Ocean. The region's precise boundaries vary depending on context, but it generally includes East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Australasia and Oceania, and sometimes the Pacific coast countries of the Americas and Russia. The term became popular in the 1980s as the region's economies grew rapidly.

Economy

The Asia-Pacific accounts for a majority share of world population and a large and rising share of global GDP. Bodies such as APEC promote trade and integration among its economies.

Usage

The term is used by international organisations, businesses and media to group countries for economic, political and statistical purposes. Related terms include "Indo-Pacific", which extends the scope westward to the Indian Ocean littoral.
