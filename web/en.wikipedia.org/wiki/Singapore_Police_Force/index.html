The Singapore Police Force (SPF) is the national law enforcement agency of the Republic of Singapore, responsible for the prevention, detection and investigation of crime within the country. Founded in 1820, it is one of the oldest government organisations in Singapore.

Organisation

The SPF is headed by the Commissioner of Police and organised into land divisions, staff departments and specialist units including the Criminal Investigation Department, the Police Coast Guard, the Special Operations Command and the Traffic Police. Neighbourhood policing was modelled in part on the Japanese kōban system.

Personnel

The force comprises regular officers, full-time national servicemen, civilian staff, and volunteers of the Volunteer Special Constabulary, supported by the auxiliary Police National Service. Singapore consistently records one of the lowest crime rates in the world.
