The Fault in Our Stars

The Fault in Our Stars is a novel by American author John Green, published in January 2012. It narrates the story of Hazel Grace Lancaster, a sixteen-year-old with terminal thyroid cancer, who falls in love with Augustus Waters, a fellow patient at a cancer support group. The title derives from Shakespeare's Julius Caesar: "The fault, dear Brutus, is not in our stars, / But in ourselves".

Plot

Hazel and Augustus bond over a novel, An Imperial Affliction, whose abrupt ending mirrors their own unfinished lives. Using Augustus's remaining "wish", they travel to Amsterdam to question its reclusive author, Peter Van Houten, who disappoints them. Augustus's cancer returns and he dies, leaving Hazel to confront grief, meaning and the value of a short life fully lived.

Reception and adaptation

The novel debuted at number one on The New York Times Best Seller list and was praised for its wit and emotional honesty. A 2014 film adaptation starring Shailene Woodley and Ansel Elgort was a major commercial success.
