Internet ethics

Internet ethics is a branch of computer ethics concerned with the moral and social issues arising from the use of the Internet and networked communication technologies.

Topics

Major topics include online privacy and surveillance, freedom of expression and censorship, intellectual property and file sharing, cyberbullying and harassment, the digital divide, and the ethics of data collection by platforms.

Privacy

The large-scale collection of personal data by services and advertisers has made privacy a central concern of internet ethics, reflected in regulations such as the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation.

Access

Debates over net neutrality and universal access ask whether internet service should be treated as a public utility and whether providers may prioritise some traffic over others.
