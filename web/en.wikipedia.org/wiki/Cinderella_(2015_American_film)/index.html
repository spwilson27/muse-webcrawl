Cinderella (2015 American film)

Cinderella is a 2015 American romantic fantasy film directed by Kenneth Branagh and written by Chris Weitz. Produced by Walt Disney Pictures, it is a live-action remake of Disney's 1950 animated film of the same name, itself adapted from the fairy tale by Charles Perrault.

Plot

Ella, orphaned after the death of her beloved father, is left at the mercy of her cruel stepmother Lady Tremaine and stepsisters Anastasia and Drizella. Through kindness and courage — her mother's dying words: "Have courage and be kind" — she befriends a young prince in the forest, attends the royal ball in a gown fashioned by her fairy godmother, and leaves behind a glass slipper.

Production

Principal photography took place at Pinewood Studios in England in 2013. Costume designer Sandy Powell created Ella's blue ballgown, requiring multiple layers and three weeks of fitting; the film's shoe designer reportedly made the glass slipper unwearable, so CGI was used for close-ups.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews, with praise for the performances of Blanchett and James and Powell's costume design, which earned an Academy Award nomination. It grossed over $543 million worldwide and helped launch Disney's series of live-action fairy-tale remakes.
