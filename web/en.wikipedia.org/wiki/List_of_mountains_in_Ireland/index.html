List of mountains in Ireland

This is a list of the highest mountains in Ireland. Ireland's mountains are modest by world standards: the highest summit, Carrauntoohil in County Kerry, rises to 1,038.6 metres (3,407 ft). Irish peaks are classified by walkers using lists such as the Vandeleur-Lynams (summits over 600 m with prominence over 15 m) and the Arderins (summits over 500 m with prominence over 30 m).
