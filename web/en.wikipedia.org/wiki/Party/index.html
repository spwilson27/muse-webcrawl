Party

A party is a gathering of people invited by a host for socialising, conversation, recreation or celebration, often marking a special occasion or festival. Parties typically feature food and drink, music, dancing and entertainment, and may be private or public, formal or informal.

Types

Common types include birthday parties, dinner parties, cocktail parties, house parties, garden parties, tea parties, wedding receptions, holiday parties (such as Christmas and New Year's Eve), block parties, pool parties, and themed or costume parties. A surprise party is kept secret from the guest of honour until their arrival. Parties for children often feature games, cake and party favours.

Customs

Customs vary by culture: guests may bring gifts, food (potluck) or drink; hosts provide refreshments and entertainment. Large commercial parties include raves and club nights. Social etiquette governs invitations (RSVPs), dress codes and departure customs. Parties play a role in community bonding and in marking life-cycle events throughout the world.
