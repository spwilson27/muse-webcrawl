List of million-plus urban agglomerations in India

An urban agglomeration (UA) in the Census of India is a continuous urban spread constituting a town and its surrounding outgrowths, or two or more physically contiguous towns. At the 2011 census, India had 53 urban agglomerations with a population of one million or more, home to roughly 160 million people — about 43 per cent of the country's urban population.

Full list and growth

The remaining million-plus UAs in 2011 included Thrissur, Vadodara, Agra, Varanasi, Srinagar, Aurangabad, Dhanbad, Amritsar, Allahabad, Ranchi, Howrah, Jabalpur, Gwalior, Vijayawada, Jodhpur, Madurai, Raipur, Kota, Guwahati, Chandigarh, Solapur, Hubballi–Dharwad, Tiruchirappalli, Moradabad, Tirupur, Gorakhpur, Tenali, Jalandhar, Bhubaneswar, Salem, Warangal, Guntur, Bhiwandi, Saharanpur, Cuttack, Firozabad, Kochi and others. The number of such cities has grown steadily with each census: there were 35 in 2001 and 53 in 2011.
