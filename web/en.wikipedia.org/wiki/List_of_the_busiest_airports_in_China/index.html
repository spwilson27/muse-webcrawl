List of the busiest airports in China

This is a list of the busiest airports in the People's Republic of China by passenger traffic, compiled from statistics published by the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC). China has one of the world's largest and fastest-growing aviation markets, with dozens of airports handling more than 10 million passengers annually.

Figures fluctuate by year; the COVID-19 pandemic sharply reduced traffic in 2020–2022, after which Guangzhou Baiyun retained its position as China's busiest airport, a title it first took in 2020.

Notes

Beijing and Shanghai are multi-airport cities; combined, Shanghai's two airports handle well over 100 million passengers per year. Hong Kong International Airport and airports in Macau and Taiwan are typically listed separately in CAAC-related rankings.
