Hobgoblin (comics)

The Hobgoblin is the alias of several fictional supervillains appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most commonly as an enemy of Spider-Man. Created by writer Roger Stern and artist John Romita Jr., the character first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #238 (1983). Modelled on the earlier Green Goblin, the Hobgoblin wears an orange-hooded variant of the goblin costume and uses similar weaponry, including a glider and pumpkin bombs.

Publication history and identity mystery

The Hobgoblin's secret identity was a long-running mystery in the Spider-Man titles. The original Hobgoblin was eventually revealed to be Roderick Kingsley, a ruthless fashion designer and criminal who had discovered one of Norman Osborn's hidden caches of Goblin equipment and improved upon the Goblin formula, gaining strength without the insanity that afflicted Osborn. The revelation was made in the 1997 miniseries Spider-Man: Hobgoblin Lives, after years in which other characters, including Ned Leeds, had been wrongly implicated.

Other Hobgoblins

Others who have used the identity include mercenary Jason Macendale (later Demogoblin), Daniel Kingsley, and Phil Urich, who operated as a heroic-turned-villainous Hobgoblin. The character has appeared in animated series and other media adaptations of Spider-Man.

Powers and equipment

Depending on the incarnation, the Hobgoblin possesses enhanced strength and reflexes from a modified Goblin serum, and uses a winged Goblin Glider, explosive pumpkin bombs, razor-edged bat-shaped throwing blades, and electrically charged gloves.
