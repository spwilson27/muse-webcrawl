Regimental sergeant major

Regimental sergeant major (RSM) is an appointment held by a warrant officer class 1 (WO1) in the British Army and in many Commonwealth armies. The RSM is the senior soldier of a regiment or battalion and advises the commanding officer on matters concerning the non-commissioned ranks.

Role

The RSM is responsible for discipline, drill, dress and deportment within the unit, for the organisation of parades and ceremonial, and for the professional development and welfare of junior soldiers. On parade the RSM traditionally carries a pace stick. The appointment is a post of great prestige, and RSMs often go on to receive commissions as late-entry officers.

Equivalents

Similar appointments exist at other levels: a company sergeant major (CSM) serves a company, squadron or battery, and garrison and academy sergeant majors serve formations and training establishments. In the United States Army the roughly analogous senior enlisted position is the command sergeant major, though the roles differ in detail.
