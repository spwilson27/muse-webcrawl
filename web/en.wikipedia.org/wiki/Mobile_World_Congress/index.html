MWC Barcelona (formerly the Mobile World Congress, and before that the GSM World Congress) is an annual trade show organised by the GSMA and held in Barcelona, Spain, since 2006. It is the world's largest exhibition and conference for the mobile communications industry.

History

The event began in 1987 as a GSM industry conference and was held in cities including Cannes before settling in Barcelona, first at Fira Montjuïc and from 2013 at Fira Gran Via in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat. Major manufacturers traditionally used the show — held each February or March — to launch flagship smartphones and network equipment. The 2020 edition was cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic, the first major international event to be called off; it resumed in reduced form in 2021.

Related events

The GSMA also runs regional editions including MWC Shanghai and MWC Las Vegas, and the startup-focused 4YFN event runs alongside the Barcelona show.
