JEE Main

The Joint Entrance Examination – Main (JEE Main) is an Indian standardised computer-based test for admission to undergraduate engineering and architecture programmes at institutions including the National Institutes of Technology (NITs), Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs), and other centrally funded technical institutions. It is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

Structure

JEE Main comprises Paper 1 for B.E./B.Tech admission and Paper 2 for B.Arch and B.Planning. Paper 1 covers physics, chemistry, and mathematics. The examination is held twice a year, typically in January and April, and students may use their better score. Top performers in JEE Main qualify to sit JEE Advanced, the entrance test for the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

History

JEE Main replaced the All India Engineering Entrance Examination (AIEEE) in 2013, when the two-tier JEE system was introduced. With well over a million candidates in some years, it is among the largest entrance examinations in the world.
