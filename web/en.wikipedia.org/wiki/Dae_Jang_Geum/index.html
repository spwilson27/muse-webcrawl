Dae Jang Geum

Dae Jang Geum (Korean: 대장금; Hanja: 大長今), also known internationally as Jewel in the Palace, is a 2003 South Korean historical drama television series directed by Lee Byung-hoon and broadcast on MBC from 15 September 2003 to 23 March 2004, where it was the number one programme for much of its run. Starring Lee Young-ae in the title role, the series tells the story of an orphaned kitchen cook who goes on to become the king’s first female personal physician.

Plot

Set in Joseon-dynasty Korea around the turn of the 16th century, the story follows Seo Jang-geum, a girl of low birth whose parents die during political purges. She enters the palace as an apprentice court lady in the royal kitchen, enduring rivalry and injustice — including banishment as a slave to Jeju Island — before returning to the palace as a medical trainee. Through talent, perseverance and integrity she rises to become the first woman appointed as the king’s chief physician, receiving the unprecedented title "Dae" (Great) Jang-geum. The drama is loosely based on Jang-geum, a historical figure mentioned briefly in the Annals of the Joseon Dynasty as a female physician trusted by King Jungjong.

Reception and cultural impact

The series achieved peak ratings above 57 per cent in South Korea and was exported to more than 90 countries, becoming one of the landmark successes of the Korean Wave (Hallyu). It was a particular phenomenon in China, Iran, and Southeast Asia, and is credited with worldwide promotion of Korean royal court cuisine and traditional Korean medicine. A theme park, Dae Jang Geum Park, was built on the series’ filming sets in Yongin, and the drama has been adapted into a musical and an animated series. In 2014 Lee Young-ae was reported to be considering a sequel, though the project did not proceed as planned.
