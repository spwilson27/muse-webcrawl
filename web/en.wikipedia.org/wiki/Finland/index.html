Finland

Finland, officially the Republic of Finland, is a Nordic country in Northern Europe bordered by Sweden, Norway and Russia, with coasts on the Baltic Sea. It has a population of about 5.6 million and its capital is Helsinki.

Known as the 'land of a thousand lakes' (there are in fact about 168,000), Finland was part of Sweden for centuries, then a Grand Duchy of Russia, winning independence in 1917. In the Winter War of 1939-40 Finland resisted Soviet invasion at great cost. Finland joined the EU in 1995 and, after Russia's invasion of Ukraine, abandoned decades of neutrality to join NATO in 2023. It regularly tops the World Happiness Report and is famed for saunas (there are more saunas than cars), education, and technology firms such as Nokia.
