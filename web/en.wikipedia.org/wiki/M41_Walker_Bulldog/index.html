M41 Walker Bulldog

The M41 Walker Bulldog is an American light tank developed for armed reconnaissance purposes. It was produced by Cadillac between 1951 and 1954 and marketed successfully to many United States allies.

M41 Walker Bulldog
Type: Light tank
Origin: United States
Main armament: 76 mm gun
Produced: 1951–1954
Named for: General Walton Walker

Development

The M41 was designed to replace the M24 Chaffee with a faster, better-armed reconnaissance vehicle. It was named after General Walton Walker, commander of the Eighth Army in Korea, who died in a jeep accident in 1950.

Service

Although it arrived too late for extensive Korean War service, the Walker Bulldog saw combat with the Army of the Republic of Vietnam and was widely exported; upgraded versions remained in service with several countries into the 21st century.
