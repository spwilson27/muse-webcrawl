University of Balamand

The University of Balamand (Arabic: جامعة البلمند) is a private university in the Koura district of northern Lebanon, founded in 1988 by the Greek Orthodox Patriarch of Antioch, Ignatius IV Hazim. Its main campus lies on the hill of Balamand near Tripoli, beside the historic Balamand Monastery, founded by Cistercian monks in 1157.

History

The university grew out of the St. John of Damascus Institute of Theology, which had operated at the monastery since 1970 to train clergy for the Orthodox Church. In 1988 the institute became part of the newly founded university, which has since expanded to include faculties of arts and sciences, business and management, engineering, medicine, health sciences, library and information studies, and tourism and hotel management.

Campuses and students

Besides the main campus at Balamand, the university maintains campuses in Beirut (at the Saint George Hospital University Medical Center) and at Souk El Gharb in Mount Lebanon. It enrolls several thousand students from Lebanon and the wider region, and teaching is conducted in English, French, and Arabic.
