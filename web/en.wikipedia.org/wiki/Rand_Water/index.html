Rand Water

TypeState-owned water utility
Founded1903 (as the Rand Water Board)
HeadquartersGlenvista, Johannesburg, South Africa
Area servedGauteng and parts of neighbouring provinces

Rand Water is a South African water utility that supplies potable water to the Gauteng province and other areas of the country, serving a population of well over ten million people. It is one of the largest water utilities in Africa, supplying bulk water to municipalities, mines and industries, which in turn distribute it to consumers.

Operations

Rand Water abstracts most of its raw water from the Vaal Dam, treating it at large purification works at Vereeniging and Zuikerbosch and pumping it through an extensive network of pipelines and reservoirs to the Witwatersrand and Pretoria. Its infrastructure includes thousands of kilometres of pipelines and numerous reservoirs and pumping stations.

History

The Rand Water Board was established in 1903 to provide a reliable water supply to the rapidly growing gold-mining towns of the Witwatersrand, where local sources were inadequate. Under South Africa's National Water Act of 1998, it operates as a water board accountable to the Department of Water and Sanitation.
