The Remarkables

The Remarkables are a mountain range and ski field in the Otago region of the South Island of New Zealand. Located on the southeastern shore of Lake Wakatipu, the range lives up to its name by rising sharply to create a remarkable backdrop for the waters.

The mountains are world famous for their dramatic jagged peaks, which are a prominent landmark of the resort town of Queenstown. The highest point in the range is Double Cone, at 2,319 metres (7,608 ft) above sea level.

Ski area

The Remarkables ski area is one of the commercial ski fields serving Queenstown, along with Coronet Peak. It lies in a north-facing sun-drenched bowl at the head of the Rastus Burn valley, with terrain that appeals particularly to freestyle skiers and snowboarders thanks to its terrain parks, though it also offers beginner areas and advanced chutes.

Name

The range is said to have been named by early surveyor Alexander Garvie, either because of its striking appearance or because it is one of only two mountain ranges in the world that run directly from north to south (the other being in South America).
