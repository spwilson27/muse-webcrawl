Charles Heath

Charles Heath (1761 – 1 November 1831) was a British engraver, currency printer and art critic. He worked in London and was known for his engraving work and for publishing fine-art books.

Life and work

Heath trained as an engraver and became known for banknote engraving and for his involvement with the Heath family of engravers and print publishers. He published works on architecture and topography, and contributed criticism on contemporary art exhibitions.

Legacy

Heath's prints and publications survive in numerous collections; his family continued in the engraving and steel-plate printing trade into the mid-nineteenth century.
