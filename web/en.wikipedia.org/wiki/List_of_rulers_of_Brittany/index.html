List of rulers of Brittany

This is a list of the rulers of the Duchy of Brittany and of the earlier Breton kingdoms. Brittany was ruled by kings in the early Middle Ages, then by counts and dukes under nominal Frankish and later French suzerainty, until the union of the duchy with the French crown in the 16th century, following the marriage of Duchess Anne of Brittany.

Early Breton kings and princes

  • Waroch I and Waroch II (6th century)
  • Judicael (c. 590–658)
  • Morvan Lez-Breiz (d. 818) — first recorded "king of Brittany"
  • Nominoe (d. 851) — often regarded as the father of Brittany (Tad ar Vro)
  • Erispoe (851–857)
  • Salomon (857–874)
  • Pasquitan and Gurvand (874–876)
  • Judicael (876–888) and Alain I "the Great" (876–907)
