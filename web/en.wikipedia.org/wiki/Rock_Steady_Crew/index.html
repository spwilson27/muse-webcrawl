Rock Steady Crew

OriginThe Bronx, New York City, U.S.
Years active1977–present
GenreB-boying / breakdance, hip hop

The Rock Steady Crew is an American b-boying (breakdancing) and hip hop group established in the Bronx, New York City, in 1977. The group, founded by b-boys Jimmy Dee and Jojo, became the most widely known breaking crew in the world after media coverage in the early 1980s brought their battles with rival crews to international attention.

Rock Steady Crew members performing.

History

The crew's best-known members include Crazy Legs (Richard Colón), who became its president, as well as Frosty Freeze, Prince Ken Swift, Mr. Wiggles and Buck 4. Their celebrated 1981 battle against the Dynamic Rockers at Lincoln Center, and appearances in the films Style Wars (1983) and Flashdance (1983), helped carry breaking from New York block parties to a global audience. Their 1983 recording "(Hey You) The Rock Steady Crew" became a hit single in several countries.

The crew continues to hold anniversary events and to promote the preservation of hip hop culture; its annual Rock Steady Anniversary in New York is a major gathering of the breaking community.
