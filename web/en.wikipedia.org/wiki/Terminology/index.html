Terminology

Terminology is the study of terms and their use, and also refers to the body of specialized words belonging to a particular subject, field, or profession. Terms are words and compound words that are used in specific contexts and carry specialized meanings distinct from, or more precise than, their everyday usage.

Terminology as a discipline

As a discipline, terminology deals with the development, compilation, and management of specialized vocabularies. Terminologists analyze concepts within a domain, identify the terms used to designate them, and record definitions, usage notes, and equivalents in other languages. The work supports technical writing, translation, lexicography, and standardization.

Concepts and terms

A central idea in terminology science, associated with Eugen Wüster and the Vienna School, is that work should proceed from the concept to the term (onomasiology), distinguishing terminology from lexicography, which typically proceeds from word to meaning. Terminological resources include glossaries, term banks, thesauri, and controlled vocabularies.

Applications

Consistent terminology is essential in medicine, law, engineering, and information technology, where ambiguity can cause errors. International standards such as ISO 704 and ISO 1087 set out principles and vocabulary for terminology work, and organizations maintain term bases to harmonize usage across documents and languages.
