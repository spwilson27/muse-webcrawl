Sociology

Sociology is the study of human society, social institutions and social relationships, founded as a discipline in the 19th century by thinkers including Auguste Comte, Émile Durkheim, Karl Marx and Max Weber.

Methods and topics

Sociologists use surveys, interviews, ethnography and statistics to study topics such as class, race, gender, family, education, crime, religion and urban life.
