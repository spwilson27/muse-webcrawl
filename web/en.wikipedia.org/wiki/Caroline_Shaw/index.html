Caroline Adelaide Shaw (born 1 August 1982) is an American composer, violinist, singer, and music producer. In 2013 she became the youngest recipient of the Pulitzer Prize for Music, for her a cappella work Partita for 8 Voices, written for the vocal ensemble Roomful of Teeth, of which she is a member.

Life and career

Shaw was born in Greenville, North Carolina, and began playing violin at age two, following the Suzuki method. She studied at Rice University and Yale University, and completed a Ph.D. in composition at Princeton University. While building her composing career she also performed widely as a violinist and singer.

Works

The Partita for 8 Voices (2009–2012) weaves together baroque dance forms, spoken text drawn from Sol LeWitt's wall drawings, and extended vocal techniques including Tuvan throat singing and Inuit katajjaq. Shaw has since received major commissions from orchestras and ensembles worldwide. Her works include the oratorio-like The Listeners, the string quartet pieces Entr'acte, Punctum, Blueprint, and Plan & Elevation for the Attacca Quartet, and the album Orange (2019), which won the Grammy Award for Best Chamber Music/Small Ensemble Performance. She composed the score for the film To Keep the Light and has collaborated with popular artists including Kanye West (on The Life of Pablo and ye) and Rosalía. Her album Evergreen with the Attacca Quartet appeared in 2022, and The Wheel with I Giardini followed.
