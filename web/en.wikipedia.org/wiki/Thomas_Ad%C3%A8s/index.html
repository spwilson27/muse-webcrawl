Thomas Adès

Thomas Adès
Born: 1 March 1971, London, England
Occupations: Composer, pianist, conductor

Thomas Joseph Edmund Adès (born 1 March 1971) is a British composer, pianist and conductor. He is regarded as one of the leading composers of his generation, known for music that combines technical brilliance, lyrical intensity, and a wide range of historical references.

Life and career

Adès studied piano at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama and music at King's College, Cambridge. He came to prominence in the 1990s with works such as Living Toys (1993) and the Chamber Symphony. His first opera, Powder Her Face (1995), brought international attention.

Major works

His orchestral work Asyla (1997) won the Grawemeyer Award. The opera The Tempest (2004), after Shakespeare, was a critical success at the Royal Opera House. Other major works include the violin concerto Concentric Paths (2005), Tevot (2007), Polaris (2010), the ballet-derived orchestral work In Seven Days, the opera The Exterminating Angel (2016, after Buñuel's film), and a piano concerto (2018) premiered by Kirill Gerstein with the Boston Symphony Orchestra.

Performing career

Adès is also an acclaimed pianist and conductor; he has been artistic partner of the Boston Symphony Orchestra and has conducted leading orchestras worldwide. He was appointed CBE in 2018.
