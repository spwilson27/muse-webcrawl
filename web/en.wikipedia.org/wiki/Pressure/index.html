Pressure

Symbol: p or P
SI unit: Pascal (Pa)
Derivation: 1 Pa = 1 N/m²
Scalar quantity

Pressure (symbol: p or P) is the force applied perpendicular to the surface of an object per unit area over which that force is distributed: p = F/A. Gauge pressure is relative to ambient atmospheric pressure.

Units

The SI unit is the pascal (Pa). Other units include bar (100,000 Pa), atmosphere (101,325 Pa), torr, and psi.

Types and applications

Atmospheric pressure, hydrostatic pressure increasing with fluid depth (p = ρgh), vapour pressure, osmotic pressure, blood pressure, and partial pressure in gas mixtures. Pressure is a scalar in ideal fluids and a key variable in thermodynamics, weather, diving, and engineering.

Measurement

Measured by barometers, manometers, Bourdon gauges, and piezoelectric sensors.
