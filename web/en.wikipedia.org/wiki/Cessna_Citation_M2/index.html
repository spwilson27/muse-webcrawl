Manufacturer: Cessna / Textron Aviation
First flight: 2012
Introduced: 2013
Crew: 1–2; Passengers: up to 7
Engines: 2 × Williams FJ44-1AP-21 turbofans
Range: about 1,550 nmi (2,870 km)

The Cessna Citation M2 is an American light business jet produced by Textron Aviation under the Cessna Citation brand. It belongs to the CitationJet (Model 525) family and was developed as a faster, updated successor to the Citation CJ1+, featuring Garmin G3000 avionics and more powerful Williams FJ44 engines.

Design

The M2 seats up to seven passengers plus a pilot in a club arrangement, with a cruising speed of about 404 knots (748 km/h) and a service ceiling of 41,000 ft. A refreshed "Gen2" version, announced in 2021, added a redesigned interior, ambient lighting and wireless charging.

Role

Certified for single-pilot operation, the M2 is popular with owner-pilots stepping up from turboprops as well as with charter operators. It competes with aircraft such as the Embraer Phenom 300 and the HondaJet.
