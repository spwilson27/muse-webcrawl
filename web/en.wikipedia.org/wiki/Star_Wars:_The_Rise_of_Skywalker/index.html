Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker, also known as Star Wars: Episode IX – The Rise of Skywalker, is a 2019 American epic space opera film produced, co-written and directed by J. J. Abrams. It is the third installment of the Star Wars sequel trilogy, following The Last Jedi (2017), and the final episode of the nine-part "Skywalker Saga".

Plot

A year after the Battle of Crait, the Resistance learns that Emperor Palpatine has somehow returned. Rey continues her Jedi training under Leia Organa while Finn, Poe Dameron and Chewbacca search for a Sith wayfinder that can lead them to Palpatine's hidden world of Exegol. Kylo Ren, having found a wayfinder on Mustafar, confronts Palpatine, who orders him to kill Rey. Rey discovers she is Palpatine's granddaughter. In the climax, the Resistance and a vast citizen fleet battle the Sith Eternal's Final Order armada above Exegol; Rey, aided by the spirits of past Jedi, destroys Palpatine, and Ben Solo sacrifices himself to save her. Rey later visits Tatooine, buries the lightsabers of Luke and Leia, and adopts the name "Rey Skywalker".

Production

The film was produced by Lucasfilm and Bad Robot Productions and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. Colin Trevorrow was originally attached to direct but departed over creative differences; Abrams returned to conclude the trilogy. Principal photography ran from August 2018 to February 2019. The film incorporated unused footage of Carrie Fisher from earlier films following her death in 2016.

Reception

The Rise of Skywalker received mixed reviews from critics, who praised the performances and action but criticized the pacing and story choices. It grossed over $1 billion worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2019, though it was the lowest-grossing entry of the sequel trilogy.
