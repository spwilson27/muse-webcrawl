DALL-E (stylized DALL·E) is a series of text-to-image models developed by OpenAI that generate digital images from natural language descriptions ("prompts"). The name is a portmanteau of the artist Salvador Dalí and Pixar's robot character WALL-E.

Versions

The original DALL-E, announced in January 2021, was based on a transformer model derived from GPT-3 trained on text–image pairs. DALL-E 2, released in 2022, used diffusion models guided by CLIP image embeddings to produce more realistic, higher-resolution images. DALL-E 3, released in 2023 and integrated into ChatGPT and Microsoft's image tools, improved prompt fidelity, following detailed descriptions much more closely and rendering legible text.

Capabilities and reception

DALL-E systems can create images in numerous styles, combine concepts, perform inpainting and outpainting (editing and extending images), and generate variations. Their release to the public helped spark broad debate about AI-generated art, creativity, copyright, deepfakes, and the impact of generative AI on illustrators and photographers, alongside other systems such as Midjourney and Stable Diffusion.
