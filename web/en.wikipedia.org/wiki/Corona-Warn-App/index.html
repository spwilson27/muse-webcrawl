Corona-Warn-App

Initial release: 16 June 2020
Developer: SAP and Deutsche Telekom for the Robert Koch Institute
Platform: Android, iOS
License: Apache License 2.0 (open source)

The Corona-Warn-App (CWA) is the official and open-source COVID-19 contact tracing app for Germany. It was developed by SAP and Deutsche Telekom on behalf of the Robert Koch Institute, the German federal agency for disease control, and released on 16 June 2020. Downloads surpassed tens of millions within its first year.

Functionality

The app uses the decentralized Exposure Notification framework developed by Apple and Google. Smartphones exchange rotating anonymous identifiers over Bluetooth Low Energy and store contacts locally; if a user tests positive, they can voluntarily share their temporary keys via a verification server so that phones that encountered them can compute exposure risk on-device. No location data is collected and participation is voluntary.

Later versions added features such as digital vaccination and test certificates interoperable with the EU Digital COVID Certificate, event check-in via QR codes, and statistics dashboards.

Development and reception

The project was notable for developing fully in the open on GitHub, and privacy advocates praised the decentralized design after the German government abandoned an earlier centralized approach. Debates continued over the app's effectiveness, digital divides, and integration with health-office processes. The app was placed into a dormant "standby" mode in 2023 as the pandemic situation eased.
