Kakashi Hatake (Japanese: はたけ カカシ, Hatake Kakashi) is a fictional character in the Naruto manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. In the story, Kakashi is the teacher of Team 7, consisting of the series' primary characters, Naruto Uzumaki, Sasuke Uchiha, and Sakura Haruno. He is initially portrayed as a detached and apathetic figure, but as the series progresses, his loyalty to his friends, students, and the village becomes increasingly apparent.

Kakashi's past has been extensively explored in the series, resulting in a gaiden series dedicated to his past experiences. He is known as the "Copy Ninja" for his Sharingan eye — implanted by his dying teammate Obito Uchiha — which allows him to copy the techniques of others; he is said to have copied over a thousand jutsu. His signature technique is the Lightning Blade (Raikiri). Kakashi's face is kept covered by a mask for nearly the entire series, a running mystery for readers. Following the Fourth Great Ninja War, he succeeds Tsunade as the Sixth Hokage.

Creation and conception

Kishimoto originally intended Kakashi to debut much earlier and designed him as a laid-back but highly skilled ninja who ended sentences with the archaic polite form "de gozaru". The author decided to keep Kakashi's face hidden, which he later noted made drawing him both easier and a source of fan curiosity. Kakashi's rivalry with Might Guy and his grief over his father's suicide and the deaths of his teammates were developed to give the mentor figure emotional depth.

Reception

Kakashi has appeared in several popularity polls of the series, at times taking first place, and is regarded by critics as one of anime's most popular mentor characters. Reviewers have praised his contrast between a relaxed, book-reading exterior (he habitually reads the fictional Make-Out Paradise novels) and his fearsome combat skill.
