Buford Dam

Buford Dam
Location: Forsyth and Gwinnett counties, Georgia, USA
Impounds: Chattahoochee River
Reservoir: Lake Lanier
Construction: 1950–1957
Height: 192 ft (59 m)

Buford Dam is a dam on the Chattahoochee River in northern Georgia, United States, near the city of Buford. Built by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers between 1950 and 1957, the dam created Lake Sidney Lanier, a major reservoir serving metropolitan Atlanta.

Construction

Authorized by Congress in 1946, the project required relocating communities and flooding the town of Oscarville and other settlements in the river valley. The rolled-earth and concrete dam was dedicated in 1957, and its powerhouse began generating electricity the same year.

Purpose

The dam serves multiple purposes: hydroelectric power generation, flood control, water supply for the Atlanta metropolitan area, navigation support downstream, and recreation. Lake Lanier is one of the most visited Corps of Engineers lakes in the United States, supporting boating, fishing, and lakeside parks.

Water disputes

Withdrawals from Lake Lanier have been at the centre of the long-running "tri-state water dispute" among Georgia, Alabama, and Florida over allocation of water in the Apalachicola–Chattahoochee–Flint river basin.
