SAP SE is a German multinational software company based in Walldorf, Baden-Württemberg, and the world's largest vendor of enterprise resource planning (ERP) software. SAP develops enterprise software to manage business operations and customer relations. The company is a component of the DAX and Euro Stoxx 50 stock indices and is among the most valuable technology companies in Europe.

History

SAP was founded in 1972 by five former IBM engineers — Dietmar Hopp, Hasso Plattner, Claus Wellenreuther, Klaus Tschira and Hans-Werner Hector — as Systemanalyse und Programmentwicklung. Its flagship products evolved from the mainframe-era R/2 through the client-server R/3 of the 1990s to the in-memory SAP HANA database platform and the S/4HANA ERP suite. Major acquisitions include Business Objects, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur and Qualtrics.

Products

SAP's portfolio spans ERP, supply-chain management, human capital management, customer experience, analytics and the SAP Business Technology Platform. In recent years the company has pivoted toward cloud subscriptions and embedding generative AI (its "Joule" assistant) across the suite.
