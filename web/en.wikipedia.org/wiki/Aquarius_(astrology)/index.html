Aquarius (♒︎; Greek: Ὑδροχόος, Hydrokhóos, "the water-pourer" or "water bearer") is the eleventh astrological sign in the zodiac, originating from the constellation Aquarius. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this sign between approximately January 20 and February 18; under the sidereal zodiac, the transit is about a month later. Aquarius is one of the three air signs, along with Gemini and Libra, and one of the four fixed signs, along with Taurus, Leo, and Scorpio. Despite its name and its symbol, the Water Bearer, it is classified as an air sign — the "water" of its glyph's waves is usually interpreted as waves of water, air, or energy.

Mythology

The constellation and sign are associated in Greek myth with Ganymede, the beautiful youth carried off to Mount Olympus to serve as cupbearer to Zeus. In Babylonian astronomy, the constellation was known as GU.LA, "The Great One", and was associated with Ea, the god of waters, who was often depicted holding an overflowing vase. In Egyptian tradition the water bearer was linked with Hapi, god of the annual flooding of the Nile.

Associations and traits

Traditional astrology assigns Aquarius to the rulership of Saturn; modern astrologers commonly assign Uranus (discovered in 1781) as its ruler. In popular astrology, people born under Aquarius are described as independent, original, intellectual, humanitarian, and unconventional, sometimes also as detached or rebellious. Such characterisations belong to the tradition of sun-sign astrology popularised in twentieth-century newspaper columns; astrology itself is regarded by the scientific community as a pseudoscience, and controlled studies have not found its claims to be supported.

The Age of Aquarius

The "Age of Aquarius" is an astrological concept referring to the roughly 2,150-year period during which the vernal equinox point lies in Aquarius, one of the twelve ages of the precessional cycle. The idea — that humanity is entering an age of harmony, understanding, and technological or spiritual transformation — became a popular theme of the 1960s counterculture, notably through the musical Hair and its song "Aquarius" (1967), later a hit for The 5th Dimension. Astrologers disagree widely about when the age begins or began, with proposed dates spanning centuries.
