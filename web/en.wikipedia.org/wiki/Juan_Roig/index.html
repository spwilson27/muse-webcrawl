Title: Honorary president / executive president of Mercadona

Juan Roig Alfonso (born 8 October 1949) is a Spanish billionaire businessman, best known as the executive president and principal shareholder of Mercadona, Spain's largest supermarket chain.

Career

Roig grew up in Valencia, where his parents ran a chain of butcher shops. In 1977 he and his wife Hortensia Herrero took over some of the family shops, and in 1981 he acquired control of Mercadona. Under his leadership the company grew into a nationwide chain of more than 1,600 stores, built on a model he calls "total quality", with a strong emphasis on in-house supplier relationships, low prices and treating customers — in company language, "the Boss" (el Jefe) — as the centre of decision-making. Mercadona's own-brand labels, such as Hacendado, became household names in Spain.

Other activities

Roig is one of Spain's richest people and a prominent patron of entrepreneurship and sport: his Marina de Empresas complex in Valencia houses the EDEM business school, the Lanzadera start-up accelerator and the Angels investment fund, and he sponsors Valencia Basket and the Roig Arena project.
