Smart thermostat

A smart thermostat is a home automation device that controls a home's heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) and connects to the internet, allowing control from smartphones and integration with other smart home systems. Smart thermostats can learn occupants' schedules and preferences, sense occupancy, use weather data, and provide energy-use reports intended to reduce consumption.

The category was popularised by the Nest Learning Thermostat, launched in 2011, followed by devices such as the ecobee and Honeywell Lyric. Features may include geofencing, remote room sensors, voice-assistant support and participation in utility demand-response programmes. They are a prominent example of the Internet of Things in the home.

Energy savings

Manufacturers cite heating and cooling savings of roughly 10–15% from scheduling and learning features, though results vary by household.
