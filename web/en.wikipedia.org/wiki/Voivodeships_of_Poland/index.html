Voivodeships of Poland

A voivodeship (Polish: województwo) is the highest-level administrative division of Poland, corresponding to a province in many other countries. Since 1 January 1999, Poland has been divided into 16 voivodeships, replacing the 49 smaller voivodeships that had existed since 1975.

Government

Each voivodeship has a dual administration: a voivode (wojewoda), appointed by the central government, and a regional assembly (sejmik) elected by the inhabitants, headed by a marshal (marszałek). Voivodeships are further subdivided into powiats (counties) and gminas (communes).
