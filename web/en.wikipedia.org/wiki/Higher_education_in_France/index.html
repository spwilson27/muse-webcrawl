Higher education in France

Higher education in France is organised in a dual system: open-admission public universities, attended by the majority of students at low tuition cost, alongside the selective grandes écoles — including the écoles normales supérieures, Polytechnique and HEC — entered by competitive examination after classes préparatoires.

Structure

Since the Bologna reforms, degrees follow the LMD structure: licence (3 years), master (5 years) and doctorat (8 years). Specialist routes include BTS, DUT/BUT in university institutes of technology, and engineering and business schools. Student life is supported by the CROUS network of housing and restaurants.

History

France's universities are among Europe's oldest (Paris, Montpellier, Toulouse); Napoleon reorganised them into an imperial university, and the post-1968 Faure law reshaped the modern system.
