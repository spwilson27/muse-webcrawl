Preslav

Country: Bulgaria
Province: Shumen
Population: ~7,500

Preslav (Bulgarian: Преслав) is a town in northeastern Bulgaria, in Shumen Province, about 20 km from Shumen. The modern town lies near the ruins of Veliki Preslav (Great Preslav), capital of the First Bulgarian Empire from 893 to 972.

History

Simeon I moved the capital from Pliska to Preslav in 893. It became a centre of Slavic letters through the Preslav Literary School, where the Cyrillic script was developed and works were translated from Greek. The Round (Golden) Church dates to this flourishing.

Byzantine emperor John I Tzimiskes captured Preslav in 971, and Sviatoslav of Kiev and later Byzantines ended its role as capital. It declined under Ottoman rule after 1388.

Archaeology

Extensive ruins — palaces, churches, workshops, the painted Preslav ceramics and treasure — are preserved in an open-air museum and the Veliki Preslav Archaeological Museum.
