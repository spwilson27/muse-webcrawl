Sita Ramam is a 2022 Indian Telugu-language period romantic drama film written and directed by Hanu Raghavapudi. It stars Dulquer Salmaan as Lieutenant Ram, an orphaned Indian Army officer stationed in Kashmir in 1964, and Mrunal Thakur (in her Telugu debut) as Sita Mahalakshmi, with Rashmika Mandanna in a pivotal role set in 1985. The film was a critical and commercial success and was widely praised for its cinematography, music and the performances of its leads.

Plot

In 1964, letters addressed to the bachelor officer Ram from an anonymous woman calling herself Sita spark a correspondence that blossoms into love when the two finally meet; their romance unfolds against communal tensions and a covert military operation, and ends in separation and tragedy. Twenty years later, in 1985, the rebellious Pakistani student Afreen is entrusted — against her will — with delivering Ram's final, long-undelivered letter to Sita, and her journey across India to find the mysterious addressee gradually reveals the lovers' story and transforms her own views.

Production and music

The film was shot in Kashmir, Hyderabad and other locations, with cinematography by P. S. Vinod and Shreyaas Krishna. Vishal Chandrashekhar's soundtrack — including the songs "Oh Sita Hey Rama" and "Inthandham" — was a major success and is credited with drawing audiences to the film, which was dubbed and released in Tamil, Hindi and Malayalam.

Reception

Sita Ramam opened to strongly positive reviews praising its old-fashioned romance, period detail and emotional climax, and it grossed over ₹90 crore worldwide. It won several awards, including honours at the Filmfare Awards South and SIIMA, and Mrunal Thakur's performance was singled out as a breakthrough.
