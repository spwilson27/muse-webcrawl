Ulsan HD FC (Korean: 울산 HD FC), formerly known as Ulsan Hyundai FC, is a South Korean professional football club based in Ulsan, owned by HD Hyundai. Founded in 1983 as Hyundai Horang-i, it is one of the founding members of the K League and has played in the country's top flight ever since.

History

The club entered the K League in 1984 as Hyundai Horang-i ("Horang-i" meaning tiger) and became Ulsan Hyundai Horang-i after moving to Ulsan in 1990. The club won its first league championship in 1996, defeating Suwon Samsung Bluewings in the final. A second title followed in 2005. Ulsan became a continental power in the 2010s, winning the AFC Champions League in 2012 — unbeaten throughout the tournament — and again in 2020, beating Iran's Persepolis in the final.

After several near misses, Ulsan won three consecutive K League 1 titles in 2022, 2023 and 2024, powered by coach Hong Myung-bo's side. At the end of 2023, the club rebranded as Ulsan HD FC following its parent company's renaming to HD Hyundai. The club also won the Korean FA Cup in 2017 and has represented Asia at the FIFA Club World Cup, including the expanded 2025 tournament.

Honours

K League 1 champions (5): 1996, 2005, 2022, 2023, 2024. AFC Champions League winners (2): 2012, 2020. Korean FA Cup winners: 2017. Korean League Cup winners (5).
