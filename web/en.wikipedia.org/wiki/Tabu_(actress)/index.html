Tabassum Fatima Hashmi (born 4 November 1971), known professionally as Tabu, is an Indian actress who works primarily in Hindi films, with additional work in Telugu, Tamil, and English-language productions. Regarded as one of the finest actresses of Indian cinema, she is known for choosing demanding, unconventional roles and has won two National Film Awards for Best Actress, along with multiple Filmfare Awards. In 2011, the Government of India honored her with the Padma Shri.

Career

Born in Hyderabad to a family with literary and academic roots, Tabu first appeared on screen as a child in Bazaar (1982) and played a small role in Hum Naujawan (1985). Her first leading role came in the Telugu film Coolie No. 1 (1991), and her Hindi breakthrough arrived with Vijaypath (1994), followed by the commercial success Raja (1995) and Saajan Chale Sasural (1996).

Critical acclaim followed with Gulzar's Maachis (1996), about militancy in Punjab, for which she won her first National Film Award for Best Actress, and his family drama Hu Tu Tu (1999). She won a second National Award for Madhur Bhandarkar's Chandni Bar (2001), playing a Mumbai bar dancer. International audiences noticed her in Mira Nair's The Namesake (2006), based on Jhumpa Lahiri's novel, and in Ang Lee's Life of Pi (2012).

Later work

Tabu's second phase has been marked by critically acclaimed performances in Vishal Bhardwaj's Shakespeare adaptations Maqbool (2003) and Haider (2014), the thriller Drishyam (2015) and its sequel, the black comedy Andhadhun (2018), and the police drama Kuttey (2023). She also headlined the streaming adaptation A Suitable Boy (2020) and appeared in the ensemble hit Bhool Bhulaiyaa 2 (2022) and Crew (2024). Reviewers consistently cite her restraint, expressiveness, and longevity across four decades of cinema.
