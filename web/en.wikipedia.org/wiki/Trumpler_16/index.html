Trumpler 16

Trumpler 16 within the Carina Nebula

Trumpler 16 is a massive open star cluster in the Carina Nebula, in the constellation Carina, roughly 8,000–9,000 light-years from Earth. It is home to some of the most massive and luminous stars known in the Milky Way, and is named after the astronomer Robert Julius Trumpler, who catalogued open clusters in the early 20th century.

Members

The cluster's most famous member is Eta Carinae, an extremely luminous hypergiant and luminous blue variable that underwent a "Great Eruption" in the 1840s, creating the surrounding Homunculus Nebula. Trumpler 16 also contains several Wolf–Rayet stars, including WR 25, and numerous hot O-type stars whose intense ultraviolet radiation and stellar winds sculpt the surrounding nebula.

Together with the neighbouring cluster Trumpler 14, it forms the heart of the Carina OB1 association, one of the largest star-forming regions in the galaxy.
