Calpine

Calpine Corporation
Industry: electric power generation
Founded: 1984
Headquarters: Houston, Texas
Known for: natural gas and geothermal power

Calpine Corporation is an American power generation company headquartered in Houston, Texas. Founded in 1984 in California (its name combines "California" and "alpine"), it grew into one of the largest generators of electricity from natural gas and geothermal resources in the United States, with a fleet of dozens of plants, many of them efficient combined-cycle facilities.

History

Calpine expanded rapidly during the deregulation of electricity markets in the 1990s and early 2000s, but heavy debt led it to file for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in 2005; it emerged in 2008. In 2018 a consortium led by Energy Capital Partners took the company private in a deal valued at about $17 billion including debt. In January 2025, Constellation Energy announced an agreement to acquire Calpine for about $16.4 billion in cash and stock, creating the largest power generator in the United States.

Operations

Calpine operates The Geysers in northern California, the world's largest complex of geothermal power plants, and sells power at wholesale as well as to retail customers. Its gas fleet is among the most efficient in the country, and the company has invested in carbon capture projects.
