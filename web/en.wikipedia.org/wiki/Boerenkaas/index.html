Boerenkaas

Boerenkaas
Country: Netherlands
Source: Raw cow, goat, sheep or buffalo milk
Texture: Semi-hard to hard
Status: EU TSG since 2007

Boerenkaas (Dutch for "farmer's cheese") is a variety of Dutch cheese, most often a Gouda-type, made on farms from raw milk in the traditional artisanal way.

Production

Fresh raw milk from the farm's own herd is curdled, the curd cut, pressed in wooden or plastic moulds, brined, and ripened for at least four weeks — often much longer — producing a firmer, more complex flavour than factory cheese, which is usually made from pasteurised milk. At least half the milk must come from the farm's own animals.

Protection

Since 2007 Boerenkaas has held Traditional Speciality Guaranteed (TSG) status in the European Union. Varieties include Boeren-Gouda, Boeren-Edam, and Leiden (cumin) farmhouse cheeses. See Gouda cheese, Cheese, Netherlands.
