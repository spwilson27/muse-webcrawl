ANSI X12

ANSI X12
Type: EDI standard
Developer: ASC X12
First published: 1979

ANSI X12 (American National Standards Institute X12) is a set of standards for Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) developed by the Accredited Standards Committee X12. It defines transaction sets for business documents such as purchase orders and invoices.

Overview

X12 is widely used in North America in healthcare, retail, transportation and finance. Common transactions include 850 Purchase Order, 810 Invoice, 856 Advance Ship Notice, and 837 Health Care Claim.

Structure

Messages consist of segments terminated by a delimiter, each segment containing data elements. Envelopes using ISA, GS and ST/SE segments wrap functional groups and transaction sets.
