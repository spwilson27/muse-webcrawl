Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate

The Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate (Norwegian: Norges vassdrags- og energidirektorat, NVE) is a Norwegian government agency subordinate to the Ministry of Energy. Founded in 1921, it is responsible for managing Norway's water and energy resources, balancing the use of those resources against environmental protection.

Hydropower development is central to NVE's responsibilities

Responsibilities

NVE processes licences for hydropower plants, power lines, wind farms, and district heating; supervises the power market and the technical operation of the electricity system; and works to ensure security of supply. It is also Norway's national authority for flood and landslide risk: it operates the national flood forecasting service, maps hazard zones, carries out preventive works in watercourses, and issues warnings through the national natural-hazard warning portal. The directorate additionally monitors glaciers and hydrology, maintaining long records of river flow, snow, and ice that are important for both power production and climate research.

Organisation

The directorate's head office is in Oslo, with regional offices in Tønsberg, Hamar, Førde, Trondheim, and Narvik. It employs several hundred staff, including engineers, hydrologists, geologists, and economists. See also hydroelectricity, energy in Norway, and Norway.
