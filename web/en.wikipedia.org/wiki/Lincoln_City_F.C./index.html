Lincoln City F.C.

Lincoln City Football Club is a professional association football club based in Lincoln, Lincolnshire, England. The team compete in League One, the third tier of the English football league system. Nicknamed the Imps after the legend of the Lincoln Imp, the club play at Sincil Bank (the LNER Stadium).

History

The club was formed in 1884 and turned professional soon after. Lincoln were founder members of the Football League Second Division in 1892. Their early years were spent largely in the second tier, but the club endured frequent relegations and re-elections in the 20th century.

Under manager Graham Taylor, Lincoln won the Fourth Division in 1975–76 with a then-record points total. In 1987 they became the first club to suffer automatic relegation from the Football League, but returned the following season as Conference champions.

In the 2016–17 season, managed by Danny Cowley, Lincoln won the National League and reached the quarter-finals of the FA Cup — the first non-league club to do so since 1914 — beating Premier League Burnley away on the way. They won the EFL Trophy in 2018 and the League Two title in 2019.

Stadium

Sincil Bank, renamed the LNER Stadium for sponsorship reasons, has been the club's home since 1895 and holds around 10,600 spectators.

Honours

Lincoln's major honours include the League Two (fourth tier) championship in 2018–19, the Fourth Division title in 1975–76, the Conference/National League title in 1987–88 and 2016–17, and the EFL Trophy in 2018.
