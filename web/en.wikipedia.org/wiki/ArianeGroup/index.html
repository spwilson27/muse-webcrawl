ArianeGroup

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Ariane 6 launcher, designed and built by ArianeGroup.

ArianeGroup is a European multinational aerospace company founded in 2015 as a joint venture between Airbus and Safran. Headquartered in Paris, it is the prime contractor for the Ariane 6 expendable launch system and develops and produces Europe's Ariane family of space launch vehicles, as well as missiles for the French nuclear deterrent, including the M51 submarine-launched ballistic missile. Its subsidiary Arianespace markets and operates launches from the Guiana Space Centre in French Guiana.

History

The company was created as Airbus Safran Launchers in 2015, merging the space launcher activities of Airbus Defence and Space with Safran's rocket engine businesses (including Snecma and Herakles). It was renamed ArianeGroup in 2017. The restructuring was driven by the decision of the European Space Agency member states in 2014 to develop Ariane 6 and to reorganize Europe's launcher industry under a single prime contractor.

Products

  • Ariane 6 – heavy-lift launch vehicle, flown in Ariane 62 and 64 configurations; maiden flight on 9 July 2024.
  • Ariane 5 – the company's earlier heavy launcher, retired in 2023 after 117 flights.
  • Vega – via its stake in Avio-related structures and its ELV participation (historically).
  • M51 – ballistic missile equipping France's Triomphant-class nuclear submarines.
  • Engines – including the Vulcain, Vinci, and HM7B cryogenic engines and the Prometheus reusable engine demonstrator.

ArianeGroup employs roughly 8,000 people in France and Germany, with major sites at Les Mureaux, Vernon, Kourou, Bremen, Lampoldshausen, and Ottobrunn.

Subsidiaries

Subsidiaries and holdings include Arianespace (launch services), Europropulsion, MaiaSpace (reusable small launcher), and Nuclétudes. The company is also involved in space surveillance through its GEOTracker network.
