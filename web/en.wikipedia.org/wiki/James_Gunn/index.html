James Gunn

Born: August 5, 1966, St. Louis, Missouri, U.S.

Occupation: Filmmaker, studio executive

Notable works: Guardians of the Galaxy, The Suicide Squad, Superman (2025)

James Francis Gunn Jr. (born August 5, 1966) is an American filmmaker and the co-chairman and co-CEO of DC Studios, alongside Peter Safran. He is best known for directing the Marvel film trilogy Guardians of the Galaxy and for rebooting the DC cinematic universe with Superman (2025).

Early career

Gunn began his career at Troma Entertainment, writing the cult comedy-horror film Tromeo and Juliet (1996). He wrote the screenplays for the live-action Scooby-Doo films (2002, 2004) and made his directorial debut with the horror film Slither (2006), followed by the dark superhero comedy Super (2010).

Marvel and DC

Marvel Studios hired Gunn to write and direct Guardians of the Galaxy (2014), an unexpected commercial and critical hit that made a relatively obscure comic team into a major franchise; he completed the trilogy with Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017) and Vol. 3 (2023). In between, he directed The Suicide Squad (2021) and created the television series Peacemaker for DC. In 2022 he was appointed co-head of DC Studios, launching the DC Universe with Creature Commandos (2024) and Superman (2025).
