Let's Move!

Let's Move! was a public health campaign in the United States launched by First Lady Michelle Obama in February 2010. Its stated goal was to reduce childhood obesity and encourage healthier lifestyles among children within a generation.

Launched9 February 2010
FounderMichelle Obama
FocusChildhood obesity, nutrition, physical activity

Program

The initiative combined several strands: improving nutrition information for parents, raising the quality of school meals, increasing access to affordable healthy food in underserved communities, and promoting daily physical activity for children. It worked with mayors, schools, chefs, athletes and food companies, and supported changes under the Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act of 2010.

Activities

Prominent elements included the White House Kitchen Garden, the Chefs Move to Schools program, and public campaigns encouraging an hour of play a day. The initiative ran throughout the Obama administration and wound down in 2017.

Reception

Supporters credited it with raising national attention to childhood obesity; critics questioned federal involvement in school lunch standards and personal food choices.
