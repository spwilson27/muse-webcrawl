Gwadar Port

The Port of Gwadar (Urdu: گوادر بندرگاہ) is a deep-sea port situated on the Arabian Sea at Gwadar in Balochistan province, Pakistan. It is the flagship project of the China–Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC) and is operated by the China Overseas Port Holding Company under a long-term concession.

The first phase of the port was completed in 2007 with assistance from China. Located about 120 kilometres from the Iranian border and near the mouth of the Persian Gulf, the port gives China a potential trade and energy route that bypasses the Strait of Malacca, and is planned to be linked by road and rail to Kashgar in Xinjiang. A free-trade zone and the New Gwadar International Airport are being developed alongside the port, though security concerns in Balochistan have slowed progress.
