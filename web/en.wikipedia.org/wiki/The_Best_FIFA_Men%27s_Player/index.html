The Best FIFA Men's Player

The Best FIFA Men's Player is an association football award presented annually by the sport's governing body, FIFA, to the world's best male player. It has been awarded since 2016 as part of The Best FIFA Football Awards, succeeding the FIFA World Player of the Year and the FIFA Ballon d'Or era.

History

From 2010 to 2015 FIFA and France Football jointly awarded the FIFA Ballon d'Or. When the partnership ended, FIFA relaunched its own awards under "The Best" branding. The winner is chosen by national team captains and coaches, journalists, and fans voting online.

Winners

Cristiano Ronaldo won the first two editions (2016, 2017). Subsequent winners include Luka Modrić (2018), Lionel Messi (2019, 2022, 2023) and Robert Lewandowski (2020, 2021). Messi's three wins are the most by any player.
