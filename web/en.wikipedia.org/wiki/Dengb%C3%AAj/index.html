Dengbêj

A dengbêj is a Kurdish singer-storyteller who performs epic songs (deng means "voice" and bêj means "to say/sing"). Dengbêjs preserve Kurdish history, legends and genealogy through long, largely unaccompanied sung narratives.

Tradition

Historically dengbêjs performed in the guest rooms of village notables, recounting tales of heroes, battles and forbidden love. The tradition was suppressed in Turkey during periods when public use of Kurdish was restricted, but survived and has seen renewed interest, with dengbêj houses (Mala Dengbêjan) established in cities such as Diyarbakır.
