Hiranyagarbha

Hiranyagarbha (Sanskrit: "golden womb" or "golden egg") is a concept in Hindu philosophy describing the source from which the universe and all beings emerged.

Scriptural sources

The Hiraṇyagarbha Sūkta of the Rigveda (10.121) praises the golden embryo that arose in the beginning and became the lord of creation. The Upanishads and the Laws of Manu develop the image of a golden egg that divides into heaven and earth.

Interpretation

Later traditions variously identify Hiraṇyagarbha with Brahmā, Viṣṇu, or the universal soul, and in Sāṃkhya and Yoga it denotes the cosmic intellect.
