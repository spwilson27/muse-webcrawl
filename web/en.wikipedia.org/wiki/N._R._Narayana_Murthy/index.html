N. R. Narayana Murthy

Born: 20 August 1946, Shidlaghatta, Karnataka, India
Education: University of Mysore; IIT Kanpur
Known for: Co-founding Infosys
Awards: Padma Vibhushan (2008)

Nagavara Ramarao Narayana Murthy (born 20 August 1946) is an Indian businessman and engineer. He is the founder of Infosys, a multinational corporation providing business consulting, technology, engineering and outsourcing services, and served as its chairman, chief executive officer, president and chief mentor before retiring in 2011.

Career

Murthy graduated in electrical engineering from the University of Mysore and earned a master's degree from IIT Kanpur. After working at IIM Ahmedabad, where he worked on India's first time-sharing computer system, and co-founding a short-lived firm, he founded Infosys in 1981 with six colleagues and initial capital of US$250. Under his leadership Infosys pioneered the Global Delivery Model for IT services and became one of the first Indian companies listed on NASDAQ, in 1999. He returned briefly as executive chairman in 2013–14.

Honours

Murthy has been awarded the Padma Shri (2000) and Padma Vibhushan (2008), and was made an honorary Commander of the Order of the British Empire. His wife, Sudha Murty, is an engineer, author and philanthropist. His son-in-law Rishi Sunak served as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom.
