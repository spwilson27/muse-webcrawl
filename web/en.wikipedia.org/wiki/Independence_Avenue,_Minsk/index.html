Overview

Independence Avenue (Praspiekt Niezaliežnasci) is the main thoroughfare of Minsk, the capital of Belarus, running about 15 km from Independence Square in the centre to the city's eastern edge. Largely rebuilt after the Second World War — which destroyed some 80% of Minsk — it is one of the most complete surviving ensembles of Stalinist Empire-style (socialist classicist) architecture and was placed on UNESCO's tentative list as an architectural complex.

Landmarks and life

The avenue passes the House of Government and the red-brick Church of Saints Simon and Helena ("Red Church") on Independence Square, the KGB headquarters (which retains its Soviet name in Belarus), the GUM department store, the Academy of Sciences, Victory Square with its 38-metre obelisk, and the National Library (the rhombicuboctahedron "diamond" of 2006) at its far end. Minsk's metro line runs beneath it. Mass protests of 2020 marched along the avenue.
