Fateh Sagar Lake

Fateh Sagar Lake is an artificial lake situated in Udaipur, Rajasthan, India, north-west of Lake Pichola, to which it is connected by a canal. Named after Maharana Fateh Singh of Mewar, it is one of the principal lakes of the "City of Lakes".

LocationUdaipur, Rajasthan, India
TypeArtificial lake, freshwater
Surface areaapprox. 2.4 km²
Max. depthapprox. 13.4 m

History

An earlier earthen dammed lake, Dewali Talab, was built in the 1680s under Maharana Jai Singh. After floods destroyed it, Maharana Fateh Singh II rebuilt it in 1889, with the foundation stone laid by the Duke of Connaught; the enlarged reservoir took his name.

Geography

The lake contains three islands: the largest holds Nehru Park, the second a public park with a solar observatory predecessor and jet fountains, and the third the Udaipur Solar Observatory. A drive along its eastern shore, popularly Bombay Market / Marine Drive of Udaipur, is a major promenade.

Hydrology and ecology

Runoff from surrounding hills and inflow from Pichola via Swaroop Sagar link the lake system. Monsoon inflow, siltation, water hyacinth and algal blooms are recurring management concerns; the lake supports fish, migratory birds and recreation.

Tourism

Boating, the annual Hariyali Amavasya fair, and lakeside food stalls draw visitors. Nearby attractions include Saheliyon-ki-Bari and Moti Magri overlooking the lake.
