Duck face

Duck face or duck lips is a photographic pose that was popular on social networking sites, in which the lips are pressed together and pushed outward as in a pout, often accompanied by sucked-in cheeks. The pose became ubiquitous in self-portraits ("selfies") on platforms such as MySpace, Facebook and Instagram in the 2000s and 2010s, and was widely parodied.

History and usage

The expression — lips pursed and protruded to resemble a duck's bill — was popularised as a deliberate, flirtatious pose in profile photographs on early social media, especially MySpace. It was sometimes combined with the "peace sign" gesture in East Asian photo culture. As the pose spread, it drew extensive mockery: websites and social media campaigns catalogued and satirised duck-face photos, and the pose became a stock joke in discussions of selfie culture. Studies of online dating profiles found that duck-face poses in profile pictures were perceived as less competent but more sexually attractive by some raters, and the pose was associated in one frequently reported (and methodologically debated) 2015 OkCupid-related analysis with users' self-presentation strategies.

Legacy

Although its popularity as an earnest pose declined in the later 2010s in favour of subtler expressions, "duck face" remains a common humorous or ironic pose, and the term is a fixture of slang dictionaries. The Oxford Dictionaries added "duck face" as an entry in 2014, the year it also added words such as "adorbs" and "side boob".
