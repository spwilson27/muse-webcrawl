Juddmonte is a thoroughbred horse racing and breeding operation founded in 1980 by Prince Khalid bin Abdullah of Saudi Arabia (1937–2021). From studs in England, Ireland and Kentucky, Juddmonte-bred horses have won major races around the world, and its stallions have reshaped modern thoroughbred bloodlines.

Frankel, undefeated in 14 starts, became Juddmonte's flagship stallion.

History

Prince Khalid began buying yearlings in the late 1970s. Early successes included Dancing Brave, winner of the 1986 Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe. Operating from Banstead Manor Stud near Newmarket and Juddmonte Farms in Kentucky, the operation pursued a policy of breeding its own mares to a small band of elite stallions rather than buying many horses in training.

Notable horses and stallions

Juddmonte's stallion roster has included the hugely influential sires Danehill (in partnership), Dansili, Oasis Dream, Kingman and above all Frankel, who retired unbeaten after 14 races and became one of the world's most valuable sires. The great mare Enable, winner of three King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes and two Arcs, raced in the Juddmonte colours — pink, emerald green and white. Other homebred champions include Zafonic, Commander in Chief, Rail Link and Arrogate, the United States-based winner of the Breeders' Cup Classic and, at the time, racing's highest earner.

After Prince Khalid's death in January 2021, the operation continued under his family, with a reduced but still elite broodmare band.
