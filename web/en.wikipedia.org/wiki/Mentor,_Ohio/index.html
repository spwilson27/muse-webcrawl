Mentor is a city in Lake County, Ohio, United States, situated on the southern shore of Lake Erie about 25 miles (40 km) northeast of Cleveland. It is one of the largest suburbs in Northeast Ohio and a major retail centre for the region.

History

Mentor was settled beginning in 1797 as part of the Connecticut Western Reserve; its name derives from the Greek figure Mentor in Homer's Odyssey. The area was long known for plant nurseries, a legacy remembered in its nickname, "City of Roses," reflecting its historic rose-growing industry. President James A. Garfield's home, Lawnfield, is preserved in Mentor as a National Historic Site.

Geography and economy

The city includes Headlands Beach State Park, the largest natural sand beach in Ohio. Its economy mixes retail along Mentor Avenue, manufacturing, and insurance and corporate offices.
