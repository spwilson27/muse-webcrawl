Alpes-Maritimes (French pronunciation: [alp maʁitim]; Occitan: Aups Maritims) is a department of France located in the country's extreme southeast, on the border with Italy and the Mediterranean Sea. Named after the Maritime Alps range that dominates its northern half, it contains most of the French Riviera (Côte d'Azur), including the cities of Nice, Cannes and Antibes. Its prefecture is Nice.

Geography

The department stretches from the Mediterranean coast to the high peaks of the southern Alps. Mercantour National Park occupies much of the north, where Mont Gélas rises to 3,143 m. The Var river crosses the department, and the coastline extends from the Estérel massif in the west to Menton on the Italian border. The principality of Monaco is an enclave within the department's coastal strip.

The climate on the coast is Mediterranean, with mild winters that historically attracted aristocratic winter visitors; the mountains receive heavy snowfall, and the department hosts ski resorts such as Isola 2000 and Auron — a combination marketed as allowing visitors to ski in the morning and swim in the sea in the afternoon.

History

A first department of Alpes-Maritimes existed from 1793 to 1814, when France annexed the County of Nice and Monaco during the Revolutionary Wars. The modern department was created in 1860 after the Treaty of Turin, by which the Kingdom of Sardinia ceded the County of Nice to France; the arrondissement of Grasse was detached from the Var department and added. Small territorial adjustments followed the Second World War, when the communes of Tende and La Brigue were transferred from Italy in 1947.

Economy and tourism

Tourism dominates the economy: the Côte d'Azur is one of the world's leading resort regions, famous for the Cannes Film Festival, the Nice Carnival, the port of Antibes (the largest marina in Europe), and resorts such as Juan-les-Pins and Menton. Inland, Grasse is the historic centre of the French perfume industry. The Sophia Antipolis technology park near Antibes is one of Europe's largest science and technology clusters.
