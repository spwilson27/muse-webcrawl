Pep Guardiola


Born: Josep Guardiola Sala, 18 January 1971, Santpedor, Spain
Position (as player): Defensive midfielder
Current team: Manchester City (manager)

Josep "Pep" Guardiola Sala (born 18 January 1971) is a Spanish football manager and former player, widely regarded as one of the greatest managers of all time. He has managed FC Barcelona, Bayern Munich, and, since 2016, Manchester City.

Playing career

A product of Barcelona's La Masia academy, Guardiola was the deep-lying playmaker of Johan Cruyff's "Dream Team", winning six La Liga titles and the 1992 European Cup. He later played for Brescia and Roma in Italy, Al-Ahli in Qatar, and Dorados in Mexico, and earned 47 caps for Spain.

Management

Appointed Barcelona head coach in 2008 after a season with the B team, Guardiola won the treble in his first season and 14 trophies in four years, refining the possession-based tiki-taka style around Lionel Messi. After a sabbatical he joined Bayern Munich (2013–2016), winning three Bundesliga titles. At Manchester City he has won multiple Premier League titles—including four in a row from 2021 to 2024—the FA Cup, and the 2023 UEFA Champions League, completing a continental treble, making him the first manager to win trebles with two different clubs.

Style and influence

Guardiola's teams are defined by positional play (juego de posición), intense pressing, a high defensive line, and full-backs or midfielders inverting into central areas. His ideas have reshaped tactics across world football.
