Meixian dialect

The Meixian dialect (Chinese: 梅線話), also known as the Moiyan dialect, is the prestige dialect of Hakka Chinese, named after Meixian District (Meizhou) in Guangdong Province, China, the heartland of Hakka culture.

Features

Meixian Hakka is considered the standard form of Hakka used in broadcasting and education in Hakka-speaking regions of mainland China. Like other Hakka varieties, it preserves many features of Middle Chinese, including checked tones and initial consonants lost in Mandarin.
