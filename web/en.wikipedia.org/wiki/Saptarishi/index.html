Saptarishi

The Saptarishi are identified with the stars of the Big Dipper
TraditionVedic Hinduism
Meaning"Seven sages"

The Saptarishi (Sanskrit: सप्तर्षि, "seven sages") are the seven great sages (rishis) of ancient India revered in the Vedas and later Hindu literature. They are regarded as the patriarchs of Vedic religion and the progenitors of the gotra lineages.

Lists of the seven

The names of the seven vary between texts and traditions. A commonly cited list for the current age (Vaivasvata Manvantara) is: Kashyapa, Atri, Vasishtha, Vishvamitra, Gautama, Jamadagni, and Bharadvaja. Other lists include Bhrigu, Agastya, and others. The earlier Vedic lists in the Shatapatha Brahmana name yet another set.

Astronomy

In Indian astronomy, the Saptarishi are identified with the seven bright stars of the asterism of the Big Dipper in the constellation Ursa Major, known in Sanskrit as the Saptarishi Mandal. The two stars near Vasishtha (Mizar) include a faint companion, Arundhati (Alcor), the wife of Vasishtha, who is shown to newlywed couples as a model of marital devotion.
