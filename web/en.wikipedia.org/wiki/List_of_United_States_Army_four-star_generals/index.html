List of United States Army four-star generals

The List of United States Army four-star generals records every officer who has held the rank of general (O-10) in the United States Army. The four-star grade is the highest routinely attainable rank in the modern Army; officers above it, such as General of the Army, belong to exceptional wartime grades. Four-star appointments are made by the President and confirmed by the Senate, and are tied to specific positions of great responsibility.

History

Ulysses S. Grant became the first American officer promoted to the revived grade of lieutenant general since George Washington, and in 1866 Congress created the grade of General of the Army of the United States for him. The modern four-star rank took shape during the Second World War, when officers such as George C. Marshall, Dwight D. Eisenhower, and George S. Patton held it in large numbers. By law the number of four-star officers on active duty is strictly limited.

Statutory positions

Four-star billets include the Chief of Staff of the Army, the Vice Chief of Staff, the commanders of the geographic combatant commands with Army leadership, and the commanding generals of Army Forces Command, Training and Doctrine Command, Materiel Command, and Futures Command.
