Raton (Spanish for "mouse") is a city in Colfax County, New Mexico, United States, and its county seat. It lies just south of Raton Pass, on the historic Santa Fe Trail, near the border with Colorado. The population was roughly 6,000 at the 2020 census.

History

Raton was founded in 1879 with the arrival of the Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway over Raton Pass, and grew as a coal-mining and railroad town. The surrounding Raton Basin coalfields supported mines at places like Blossburg, Gardiner and Sugarite, and the city was a division point on the railroad. The historic Shuler Theater (1915) still anchors the downtown.

Geography and recreation

The city sits at an elevation of about 6,680 feet at the foot of the Raton Range. Nearby attractions include Sugarite Canyon State Park, Capulin Volcano National Monument, and the NRA Whittington Center shooting range. Interstate 25 and Amtrak's Southwest Chief serve the city.
