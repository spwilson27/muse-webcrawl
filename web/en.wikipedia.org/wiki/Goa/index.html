Goa

Goa
Country: India
Capital: Panaji (Panjim)
Largest city: Vasco da Gama
Statehood: 30 May 1987
Official language: Konkani

Goa is a state on the southwestern coast of India within the Konkan region, bordered by Maharashtra, Karnataka and the Arabian Sea. It is India's smallest state by area and among its richest by GDP per capita.

History

Ruled successively by the Kadambas, Vijayanagara, Bahmani and Bijapur sultanates, Goa was captured by Afonso de Albuquerque in 1510 and remained the centre of Portuguese India for 451 years — far longer than British rule elsewhere in India. India annexed Goa in 1961; it became a union territory and then, in 1987, the 25th state.

Culture and tourism

Goa blends Indian and Portuguese influences in cuisine, music and architecture. The Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa holds the relics of St Francis Xavier; the churches and convents of Goa are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Famous for beaches from Baga to Palolem, Goa draws millions of tourists and is known for feni, seafood and the Carnival.

Economy

Tourism, mining (historically iron ore), fishing, agriculture and pharmaceuticals drive the economy.
