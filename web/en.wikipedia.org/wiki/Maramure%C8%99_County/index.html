Maramureș County (Romanian: Județul Maramureș) is a county of Romania in the Maramureș region, in the north of the country, bordering Ukraine. Its capital and largest city is Baia Mare.

Geography

The county covers 6,304 km², about half of it mountainous (the Rodna and Maramureș ranges of the Eastern Carpathians), with the rest rolling hills and the Tisza river plains. Gutâi and Țibleș are volcanic ranges.

Culture

Historical Maramureș is famed for its wooden churches, eight of which are UNESCO World Heritage Sites, its carved wooden gates, and preserved folk traditions. The region's northern half lies across the border in Ukraine's Zakarpattia Oblast.

Economy

Historically a mining region (Baia Mare was a gold and non-ferrous metals centre), the county's economy now includes wood processing, agriculture, tourism and services.
