Acting rank

An acting rank is a designation in military organisations that allows a person to assume the role, authority and insignia of a military rank that is higher than the rank they substantively hold. Acting rank is typically granted temporarily — for the duration of an operation, a deployment, or while filling a vacant appointment — and may not carry the pay of the higher rank.

Usage

In the British armed forces, acting rank has historically been distinguished from substantive rank and from temporary or local rank; officers could be promoted in an acting capacity during wartime expansion and revert afterwards. Similar arrangements exist in other Commonwealth and NATO forces, where a service member may be "acting" in a higher appointment pending confirmation or promotion. Analogous temporary designations occur in police, fire and civil services.
