Alpha Pictoris (α Pictoris, abbreviated Alpha Pic) is the brightest star in the southern constellation of Pictor, the painter's easel. With an apparent magnitude of 3.27 it is visible to the naked eye under dark southern skies. It lies about 97 light-years from the Sun.

Properties

Alpha Pictoris is a white main-sequence star of spectral type A7 V, somewhat larger, hotter and more luminous than the Sun. It is a fast rotator — its projected rotational velocity is around 200 km/s — which gives the star an oblate shape and makes it a shell-type variable candidate. It has been identified as a Lambda Boötis star, showing a peculiar surface underabundance of heavy elements. The star also serves as the south polar star of Mercury.
