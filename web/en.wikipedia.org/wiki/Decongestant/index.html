Decongestant

A decongestant is a type of pharmaceutical drug that relieves nasal congestion caused by colds, allergies and sinusitis.

Most decongestants, such as pseudoephedrine and phenylephrine, are sympathomimetics that constrict swollen blood vessels in the nasal mucosa. Topical agents like oxymetazoline work quickly but can cause rebound congestion (rhinitis medicamentosa) if used for more than a few days. Saline sprays are a non-drug alternative.
