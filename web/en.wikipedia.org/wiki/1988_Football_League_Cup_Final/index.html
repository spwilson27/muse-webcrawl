The 1988 Football League Cup Final was played on 24 April 1988 at Wembley Stadium in London between Luton Town and Arsenal. It was the final match of the 1987–88 Football League Cup, the 28th season of the competition. Luton won 3–2 in one of the most dramatic finals in the competition's history, securing the first major trophy in the club's history.

Match summary

Luton took an early lead through Brian Stein. Arsenal came back in the second half with goals from Martin Hayes and Alan Smith to lead 2–1, and were awarded a penalty which Nigel Winterburn took; Luton's goalkeeper Andy Dibble saved it, keeping his side in the game. In a frantic finish, Danny Wilson equalised for Luton before Stein scored his second goal in the final minute to complete a famous victory.

The win was a remarkable achievement for Luton manager Ray Harford's side, who defeated the holders, Arsenal, managed by George Graham. The match is still remembered as one of the great Wembley cup final upsets.
