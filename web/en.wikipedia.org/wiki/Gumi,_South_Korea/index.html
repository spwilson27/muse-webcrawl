Gumi (Korean: 구미) is a city in North Gyeongsang Province, South Korea, situated on the Nakdong River. It is one of the country's principal electronics manufacturing centres and the birthplace of former president Park Chung-hee.

Industry

Gumi's national industrial complex, developed from the 1970s, hosts major plants of Samsung Electronics and LG Electronics, producing semiconductors, displays and mobile devices. The city is sometimes called the "Silicon Valley of South Korea" for its concentration of electronics firms.

Geography and culture

The city lies in the Nakdong River basin beneath Mount Geumo (976 m), whose provincial park is a popular hiking destination with temples, a waterfall and a lake. Gumi is served by the Gyeongbu railway line and expressway linking Seoul and Busan.
