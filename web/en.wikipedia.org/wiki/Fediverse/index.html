Fediverse

The fediverse (a portmanteau of "federation" and "universe") is an ensemble of social networking services that can communicate with each other using a common protocol, principally ActivityPub, while remaining independently hosted.

History

The fediverse originated with projects such as GNU social and identi.ca using the OStatus protocol. The World Wide Web Consortium standardized ActivityPub in 2018, and it was adopted by Mastodon (launched 2016), PeerTube, Pixelfed, Lemmy and others. Interest grew sharply in 2022 as users sought alternatives to Twitter following its acquisition.

How it works

Users on any participating server (instance) can follow, reply to, and share content from users on other instances. Each instance sets its own moderation rules and can block ("defederate") others. Services are usually free and open-source, and unlike centralized platforms there is no single owner of the network.
