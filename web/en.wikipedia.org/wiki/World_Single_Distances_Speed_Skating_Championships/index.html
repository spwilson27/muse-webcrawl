World Single Distances Speed Skating Championships

The World Single Distances Speed Skating Championships are a series of speed skating competitions organised by the International Skating Union (ISU) in which world titles are awarded separately for each individual race distance, in the same format as Olympic speed skating. The first edition was held in Hamar, Norway, in 1996.

Events

The programme comprises the 500 m, 1000 m, 1500 m, 5000 m and 10,000 m for men; the 500 m, 1000 m, 1500 m, 3000 m and 5000 m for women; plus team pursuit and mass start events for both sexes, and a team sprint added in 2019. Titles are decided in single races (the 500 m historically over two races, later one), unlike the allround championships which combine four distances into one classification.

History and scheduling

The championships are generally held annually in the late season, except in Winter Olympic years when they are omitted because the Olympic programme serves the same purpose; they were also not held in 2021's altered calendar arrangements—instead a combined event took place in Heerenveen under pandemic conditions. Dutch skaters have historically dominated the medal tables, with stars such as Sven Kramer, Ireen Wüst, Irene Schouten and Patrick Roest among the most successful competitors.
