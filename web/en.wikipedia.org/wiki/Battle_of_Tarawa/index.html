Battle of Tarawa
Date: 20–23 November 1943
Location: Betio, Tarawa, Gilbert Islands
Part of: Pacific War

The Battle of Tarawa was fought from 20 to 23 November 1943 between the United States and Japan on Betio island in the Tarawa atoll. U.S. Marines of the 2nd Marine Division captured the heavily fortified island in 76 hours at heavy cost.

Background

Tarawa's airfield was needed for the advance across the central Pacific. Some 4,800 Japanese defenders under Rear Admiral Shibasaki prepared bunkers, seawalls and interlocking fields of fire.

The battle

An unexpectedly low tide left landing craft stranded on reefs, forcing Marines to wade ashore under fire. After brutal fighting, the island was secured; nearly all Japanese defenders died, and over 1,000 Marines were killed.

Legacy

Shocking casualty figures and photographs prompted reforms in amphibious doctrine, fire support and communications.
