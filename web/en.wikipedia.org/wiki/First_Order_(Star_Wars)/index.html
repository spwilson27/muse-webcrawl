First Order (Star Wars)

The First Order is a fictional military and political organization in the Star Wars franchise. Introduced in the 2015 film Star Wars: The Force Awakens, it is formed from the remnants of the Galactic Empire, having been formed after the Empire's defeat at the Battle of Jakku and its withdrawal into the Unknown Regions, in accordance with Imperial contingency plans. Based on ideology similar to that of the prior regime, the First Order represents the principal antagonists of the sequel trilogy, opposing the New Republic and the Resistance.

Description

The First Order is led by Supreme Leader Snoke, and later by Kylo Ren after he kills Snoke. Its forces include stormtroopers taken from their families as children and indoctrinated, TIE fighter pilots, and a large fleet of Star Destroyers secretly constructed in the Unknown Regions. In The Force Awakens, the First Order uses the superweapon Starkiller Base to destroy the Hosnian system, the capital of the New Republic.

In The Last Jedi (2017) the First Order pursues the Resistance fleet across the galaxy, and in The Rise of Skywalker (2019) it is revealed that the resurrected Emperor Palpatine, leader of the Sith Eternal, stood behind Snoke; the First Order is drawn into the Final Order fleet and defeated by a citizens' armada at Exegol.
