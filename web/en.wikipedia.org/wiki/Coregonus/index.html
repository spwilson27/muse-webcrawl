Coregonus is a diverse genus of freshwater whitefish in the family Salmonidae, distributed across the cold lakes and rivers of northern Europe, Asia and North America. The genus contains some 68 species in the broad traditional sense, though the taxonomy is notoriously contentious: many "species" are lake-specific forms that evolved after the last glaciation from a small number of glacial refugium lineages, and authorities disagree sharply on how many to recognize. Members include the vendace, powan, schelly, gwyniad, pollan, the European whitefish (C. lavaretus complex), the broad whitefish, the lake whitefish (C. clupeaformis), and the ciscoes sometimes placed in a subgenus Leucichthys.

Ecology and importance

Coregonus species are typically silvery, schooling, plankton- or benthos-feeding fish of cold, well-oxygenated waters, spawning in autumn and winter over gravel. They have been staple food fishes for northern peoples for millennia and support important commercial and subsistence fisheries — notably the lake whitefish and cisco fisheries of the North American Great Lakes, and the omul of Lake Baikal (C. migratorius), a prized delicacy in Siberia.

Conservation

Many populations have collapsed from overfishing, eutrophication, acidification and competition or predation by introduced species; several named species are extinct, including the houting of the North Sea (whose status is debated), the fera of Lake Geneva, the gravenche, and the deepwater ciscoes of the Great Lakes devastated by the sea lamprey. Conservation measures include stocking, fishery regulation and habitat restoration.

Taxonomy debate

The "Coregonus problem" — explosive postglacial diversification combined with extensive phenotypic plasticity and hybridization — has made the genus a celebrated case study in speciation (including sympatric speciation within single lakes such as Lake Constance and Lake Baikal) and a persistent headache for taxonomy, with species counts between roughly 50 and over 100 depending on the authority and species concept used.
