Slice knot

A slice knot is a knot in three-dimensional space that bounds a smooth, properly embedded disk in the four-dimensional ball. The study of slice knots is a central topic in knot theory and four-dimensional topology.

A knot that is both slice and the boundary in the classical sense is called a ribbon knot; every ribbon knot is slice, and whether every slice knot is ribbon is a long-standing open question known as the slice-ribbon conjecture. Invariants such as the knot signature, the Alexander polynomial (via the Fox–Milnor condition) and modern Floer- and Khovanov-homology invariants such as Rasmussen's s-invariant obstruct sliceness.

Examples

The unknot, the stevedore knot and the figure-eight knot (via its mirror combination in concordance) illustrate the theory; the set of slice knots forms the kernel of the knot concordance group.
