Dune (2021 film)

Dune (titled on-screen as Dune: Part One) is a 2021 American epic science fiction film directed by Denis Villeneuve, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jon Spaihts and Eric Roth. It is the first of a two-part adaptation of the 1965 novel Dune by Frank Herbert. The film stars Timothée Chalamet as Paul Atreides, alongside Rebecca Ferguson, Oscar Isaac, Josh Brolin, Zendaya, Jason Momoa, Javier Bardem, and Stellan Skarsgård.

Plot

In the distant future, Duke Leto Atreides accepts control of the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of the spice melange, the most valuable substance in the universe. When House Atreides is betrayed and destroyed by the rival House Harkonnen with the Emperor's backing, Leto's son Paul and his mother Jessica flee into the desert and encounter the native Fremen, among whom Paul begins to fulfil a destiny intertwined with his visions of the future.

Production and release

The film was produced by Legendary Pictures and filmed in Jordan, Abu Dhabi, Norway, and Hungary, with a score by Hans Zimmer. It premiered at the Venice International Film Festival in September 2021 and was released theatrically and on HBO Max in the United States in October 2021. It grossed over $400 million worldwide.

Reception

Dune received widespread critical acclaim for its direction, visuals, and score, and won six Academy Awards, including Best Original Score and Best Cinematography, from ten nominations. The sequel, Dune: Part Two, was released in 2024.
