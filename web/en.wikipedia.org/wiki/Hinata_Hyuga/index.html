Hinata Hyuga (日向 ヒナタ, Hyūga Hinata) is a fictional character in the anime and manga franchise Naruto, created by Masashi Kishimoto. She is a kunoichi of Konohagakure and the eldest daughter of the Hyuga clan's main house, heir to one of the village's most prestigious families. Hinata possesses the Byakugan, the clan's inherited visual ability, and fights with the Gentle Fist taijutsu style, which targets an opponent's chakra pathway system.

Character

Kind, polite, and initially painfully shy, Hinata was deemed a failure by her father Hiashi because of her gentle nature and early struggles, losing her position as heir to her younger sister Hanabi. She draws determination from Naruto Uzumaki, whose refusal to give up she admires from afar, and gradually grows into a capable and courageous ninja — notably confronting Pain during his assault on the village despite overwhelming odds. Over the course of the sequel story depicted in The Last: Naruto the Movie, her long-standing feelings for Naruto are reciprocated; the two marry and have two children, Boruto and Himawari, who feature in the follow-up series Boruto.

Reception

Hinata has consistently ranked among the most popular characters in the series in fan polls and is frequently cited for her character development and her role in the story's emotional core. Her design — including her lavender-tinted Byakugan eyes and long dark hair — is one of the franchise's most recognisable.
