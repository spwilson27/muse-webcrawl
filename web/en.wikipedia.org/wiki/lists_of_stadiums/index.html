Lists of stadiums

The following are lists of stadiums around the world, ordered by capacity, sport, region, and other criteria. Stadiums are large venues used primarily for outdoor sports, concerts, and other events.

By capacity

The largest stadium in the world by capacity has traditionally been contested between venues such as the Rungrado 1st of May Stadium in Pyongyang and large American college football stadiums. Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, India, with a capacity of about 132,000, is the largest cricket stadium and the largest stadium overall.

Categories

Stadium lists are maintained by sport (association football, American football, baseball, cricket, rugby), by country, and by type, including lists of indoor arenas and domed stadiums.
