Fluorouracil

Fluorouracil (5-FU) is a chemotherapy medication used to treat various cancers including colorectal, stomach, pancreatic, breast, and cervical cancers, and applied topically for skin conditions such as actinic keratosis and superficial basal cell carcinoma.

Mechanism

As a pyrimidine analogue, its metabolites are incorporated into RNA and DNA and inhibit thymidylate synthase, disrupting synthesis of thymidine needed for DNA replication in rapidly dividing cells.

Side effects

Adverse effects include myelosuppression, mucositis, diarrhoea, and hand–foot syndrome; patients deficient in the enzyme dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase can suffer severe toxicity.

It is on the World Health Organization's List of Essential Medicines.
