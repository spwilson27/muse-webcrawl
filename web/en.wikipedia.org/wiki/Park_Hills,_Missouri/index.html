Park Hills, Missouri


Park Hills is a city in St. Francois County, Missouri, United States.

Park Hills is a city in St. Francois County, Missouri, United States. The population was 8,759 at the 2020 census. The city was formed in 1994 through the merger of the towns of Flat River, Elvins, Esther, and Rivermines under the name Park Hills. It lies in Missouri's lead-mining region, historically known as the Lead Belt.

History

The area of present-day Park Hills was settled largely in connection with lead mining in southeastern Missouri. Flat River, the largest of the communities that merged, developed as a mining and commercial center in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. The four municipalities voted to consolidate, and the merger took effect on January 1, 1994.

Geography

Park Hills is located in the eastern Ozarks foothills region. According to the United States Census Bureau, the city has a total area of about 20.4 square miles, of which a small portion is water. The Flat River flows through the area.

Demographics

At the 2020 census there were 8,759 people residing in the city. The economy historically centered on lead mining, and Mineral Area College, a public community college, is located in Park Hills.

Education

The city is served by the Central R-III School District. Mineral Area College provides two-year degree programs and workforce training for the region.
