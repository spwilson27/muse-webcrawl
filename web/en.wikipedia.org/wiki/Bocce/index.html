Bocce

Bocce is a ball sport belonging to the boules family, closely related to French pétanque and British bowls. Developed in Italy, it is played with eight large balls and one smaller target ball called the pallino or boccino.

Play

Players take turns throwing or rolling their balls toward the pallino, scoring points for balls closer to it than the opponent's nearest ball. Matches are typically played to 12 points on a court about 27.5 metres long.

History

Games of throwing balls at a target date to ancient Egypt, Greece and Rome. Modern bocce was codified in Italy, and the first Italian league was formed in 1947. Variants include raffa and volo, governed internationally by the Confédération Mondiale des Sports de Boules. Bocce is also a Special Olympics sport. See also Bowls.
