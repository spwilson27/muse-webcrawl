Carbomer

Carbomers are a class of synthetic high-molecular-weight polymers of acrylic acid, cross-linked with allyl ethers of pentaerythritol or sucrose. They are used as thickening, suspending, and emulsifying agents in pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and personal-care products, where they form clear gels at low concentrations.

Carbomer
Type: Cross-linked polyacrylic acid polymer
Uses: Gels, creams, hand sanitisers
Trade name origin: Carbopol

Properties

Dry carbomer is a white, fluffy, acidic powder. Dispersed in water it forms a low-viscosity acidic solution; neutralisation with a base such as sodium hydroxide or triethanolamine produces a highly viscous, clear gel. Viscosity depends on grade, concentration, pH, and electrolytes.

Applications

Carbomers are the standard gelling agent in alcohol-based hand sanitiser gels, ultrasound and lubricating gels, eye drops and artificial tears, toothpastes, shampoos, and topical drug formulations.
