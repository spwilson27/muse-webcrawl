Real Sociedad

Full name: Real Sociedad de Fútbol, S.A.D.
Founded: 7 September 1909
Ground: Reale Arena, San Sebastián
League: La Liga

Real Sociedad de Fútbol, commonly known as Real Sociedad (Spanish for "Royal Society of Football"), is a professional football club based in San Sebastián, Basque Country, Spain. Founded on 7 September 1909, the club plays in La Liga, the top tier of Spanish football, and holds its home games at the Reale Arena.

History

The club won the La Liga title in 1980–81 and 1981–82, under manager Alberto Ormaetxea and with players such as Luis Arconada, Jesús María Zamora and Jesús María Satrústegui. Real Sociedad were also founding members of La Liga in 1929. The club has won the Copa del Rey three times, most recently in the 2019–20 edition, whose final was played in 2021 against Basque rivals Athletic Bilbao.

For many years Real Sociedad followed a policy of fielding only Basque players, similar to Athletic Bilbao, before signing Irish striker John Aldridge in 1989. The club's youth academy at Zubieta is regarded as one of the most productive in Spain.

Rivalries

The club's main rival is Athletic Bilbao, with whom it contests the Basque derby. The derby is notable for the friendly mixing of supporters of both clubs in the stands.

Honours

  • La Liga: 1980–81, 1981–82
  • Copa del Rey: 1909, 1986–87, 2019–20
  • Supercopa de España: 1982
