Redland railway station is a small railway station in Bristol, England, on the Severn Beach line. It serves the districts of Redland and Bishopston, about 1.3 miles (2.1 km) from Bristol Temple Meads.

History

The station was opened in 1897 by the Great Western Railway on the line from Clifton Down towards Avonmouth. It has a single platform and retains a small Edwardian-era ticket office building. The other platform and station facilities were removed when services on the line were reduced in the mid-20th century.

Services

Trains operated by Great Western Railway run between Bristol Temple Meads and Avonmouth or Severn Beach, typically every 40 minutes to an hour. Passenger numbers grew substantially in the 2000s and 2010s with the popularity of the line among commuters and students.
