Vaughan Jones

Sir Vaughan Frederick Randal Jones (31 December 1952 – 6 September 2020) was a New Zealand mathematician known for his work on von Neumann algebras and knot polynomials. He was awarded the Fields Medal in 1990 for his discovery of the Jones polynomial, an unexpected connection between operator algebras and knot theory.

Born in Gisborne, Jones studied at the University of Auckland and earned his doctorate in Geneva under André Haefliger. While studying subfactors of von Neumann algebras, he discovered an index theorem with surprising restrictions, which led to a new polynomial invariant of knots. The Jones polynomial revitalised knot theory and later found applications in statistical mechanics, quantum field theory, and molecular biology.

Jones spent much of his career at the University of California, Berkeley, and later Vanderbilt University. He was knighted in 2002 and strongly supported mathematics education in New Zealand.
