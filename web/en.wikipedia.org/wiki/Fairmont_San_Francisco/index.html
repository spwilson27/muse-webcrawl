Fairmont San Francisco

The Fairmont San Francisco is a luxury hotel at 950 Mason Street, atop Nob Hill in San Francisco, California. The hotel was named after mining magnate and U.S. Senator James Graham Fair (1831–94), by his daughters Theresa Fair Oelrichs and Virginia Fair Vanderbilt, who built the hotel as a monument to him.

Location950 Mason Street, San Francisco, California
OpenedApril 18, 1907
ArchitectReid & Reid; Julia Morgan (restoration)
OperatorFairmont Hotels and Resorts (Accor)

History

Construction was nearly complete when the 1906 San Francisco earthquake struck; the structure survived but its interiors were gutted by fire, delaying the opening by a year. Architect Julia Morgan supervised the restoration. The hotel's Tonga Room & Hurricane Bar, a tiki bar built around the hotel's former swimming pool, opened in 1945.

Notable events

Delegates drafting the United Nations Charter met at the hotel in 1945, and Tony Bennett first performed "I Left My Heart in San Francisco" in its Venetian Room in 1961. The hotel appeared in Alfred Hitchcock's Vertigo and numerous other productions.
