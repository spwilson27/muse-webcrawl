Mau5trap (pronounced "mouse trap") is a Canadian independent record label founded by electronic music producer Joel Zimmerman, known professionally as deadmau5, in 2007. The label releases the output of deadmau5 himself alongside a roster of electronic music artists working in progressive house, electro house, techno and related genres.

History

Zimmerman established Mau5trap as a vanity imprint while releasing material through larger labels, but it developed into a standalone operation based in Toronto. Its first releases included deadmau5's own albums Random Album Title (2008) and For Lack of a Better Name (2009), which helped bring progressive house into the mainstream electronic music scene. The label became known for its distinctive mouse-head branding and for releasing records from artists such as Feed Me, Chris Lake, Noisia and Skrillex in its early years.

Artists

Artists associated with Mau5trap have included deadmau5, Feed Me, Rezz, i_o, Blackgummy, Noisia, Tommy Trash and Matt Lange. The label has also released compilation series such as We Are Friends. Since 2016, Mau5trap has worked in partnership with the Canadian label coalition Believe, and has distributed via Universal Music Canada.
