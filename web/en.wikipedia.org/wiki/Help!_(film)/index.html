Help! (film)

Help!
Directed by: Richard Lester
Starring: The Beatles
Released: 1965
Music: The Beatles

Help! is a 1965 British musical comedy-adventure film directed by Richard Lester, starring the Beatles — John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison and Ringo Starr — and produced by Walter Shenson. It is the second Beatles feature film, following A Hard Day's Night (1964).

Plot

A sacrificial ring sent by a fan becomes stuck on Ringo's finger, making him the target of a sinister cult whose rituals require the ring's wearer to be sacrificed, as well as of two mad scientists. The chase moves from London to the Austrian Alps and the Bahamas.

Music

The soundtrack album Help! includes "Help!", "You've Got to Hide Your Love Away", "Ticket to Ride" and "Yesterday". Filmed in colour, the film's musical sequences are regarded as forerunners of the modern music video.
