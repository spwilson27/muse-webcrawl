Minute Maid is an American brand of beverages, best known for its orange juice, owned by The Coca-Cola Company. It was the first company to market frozen orange juice concentrate, which transformed the juice industry after the Second World War.

History

The company began in 1945 as the Florida Foods Corporation, which won a United States Army contract to supply powdered orange juice for soldiers. Working with the US Department of Agriculture's citrus products research, the team instead developed frozen concentrated orange juice. The war ended before the army contract was fulfilled, so the product was sold to the public in 1946 under the name Minute Maid — a name suggesting quick preparation, trademarked in 1946 and shipped nationally in 1949.

Frozen concentrate, reconstituted with water, made orange juice affordable and available year-round across the United States and became a breakfast staple. Bing Crosby became a famous advertising spokesman for the brand. In 1960 Minute Maid merged with Coca-Cola — Coca-Cola's first major acquisition beyond soft drinks — giving the juice brand global distribution. The Minute Maid Company remains headquartered in the Houston area (Sugar Land, Texas).

Products

Beyond its flagship 100% orange juice, the brand sells lemonade, fruit punches and juice drinks under names such as Minute Maid Light and, historically, Hi-C in some markets. In 2023 Coca-Cola announced it would discontinue the frozen canned concentrates in the United States and Canada as consumer habits shifted to ready-to-drink juices.

Naming rights

From 2004 to 2024 the Houston Astros' baseball stadium carried the Minute Maid Park name; it was renamed Daikin Park in 2025.
