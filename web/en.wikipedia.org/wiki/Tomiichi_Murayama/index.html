Tomiichi Murayama

Tomiichi Murayama (村山 富市, born 3 March 1924) is a Japanese politician who served as Prime Minister of Japan from 1994 to 1996. He was the first prime minister from the Japan Socialist Party in nearly half a century.

Murayama was born in Ōita Prefecture and worked in local politics before being elected to the Diet. He led the Japan Socialist Party and became prime minister at the head of an unusual coalition with the Liberal Democratic Party and the New Party Sakigake, after the LDP lost its long monopoly on power in 1993.

Premiership

On 15 August 1995, the fiftieth anniversary of the end of World War II, Murayama issued a landmark statement expressing "deep remorse" and a "heartfelt apology" for Japan's wartime colonial rule and aggression. Known as the Murayama Statement, it has been cited by successive governments as Japan's official position. His government also dealt with the Great Hanshin earthquake and the Tokyo subway sarin attack of 1995. He resigned in January 1996 and was succeeded by Ryutaro Hashimoto.
