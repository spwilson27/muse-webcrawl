Vikingskipet ("The Viking Ship"), officially the Hamar Olympic Hall, is a multi-purpose indoor arena in Hamar, Norway. Its roof is shaped like an upside-down Viking ship, from which the nickname derives. It was built for the 1994 Winter Olympics in Lillehammer, for which it hosted the speed skating events.

History and use

The arena opened in December 1992 and, at the time of the Games, was the world's largest indoor speed skating venue. During the 1994 Olympics the Norwegian skater Johann Olav Koss won three gold medals there with world-record performances, making Vikingskipet famous in Norwegian sporting history. The hall has since hosted World Speed Skating Championships and World Cup events.

Beyond skating, the building's huge floor area accommodates trade fairs, exhibitions, concerts and conferences. Since 1996 it has hosted The Gathering, one of the world's largest computer parties (demoparties), held annually at Easter. The arena has also staged major handball events, including matches of the European and World women's championships.
