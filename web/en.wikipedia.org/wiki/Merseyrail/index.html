Merseyrail

Merseyrail's Class 777 trains entered service from 2023.

Merseyrail is an urban rail network serving Liverpool and the surrounding Liverpool City Region in North West England, together with adjacent areas of Cheshire and Lancashire. Unusually among British regional railways, it is an electrified commuter network with its own dedicated infrastructure, frequent metro-style services on its core sections, and — since 2003 — a locally specified concession overseen by the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority through Merseytravel.

Network

The network's two self-contained electric lines — the Northern line (to Southport, Ormskirk, Kirkby/Headbolt Lane and Hunts Cross) and the Wirral line (to West Kirby, New Brighton, Chester and Ellesmere Port) — meet in a city-centre underground loop and link tunnel beneath Liverpool, built in the 1970s on the foundations of the Victorian Mersey Railway tunnel of 1886, one of the world's oldest underground railways. Diesel-worked City line services to Manchester, Wigan and Blackpool share some stations but are operated separately by Northern.

History and operation

The Merseyrail brand dates from the 1970s modernisation. On privatisation the network became a franchise, then in 2003 a 25-year concession let by Merseytravel — the first of its kind — allowing local control over fares, staffing and stations. The franchise is operated by a Serco–Abellio (now Transport UK) joint venture. The original Class 507 and 508 trains, dating from the late 1970s, were replaced from 2023 by a fleet of 53 Stadler Class 777 trains owned by the Combined Authority, including battery-equipped variants enabling extensions beyond the third-rail network, such as the new Headbolt Lane station.
