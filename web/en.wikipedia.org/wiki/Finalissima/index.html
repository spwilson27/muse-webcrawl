Finalissima

The Finalissima, formerly the Artemio Franchi Cup, is an intercontinental football match organised by CONMEBOL and UEFA and contested by the winners of the Copa América and UEFA European Championship.

History

The match was played twice in its original form, in 1985 and 1993, before being discontinued. It was revived in 2022 as part of a renewed partnership between the confederations; Argentina, winners of the 2021 Copa América, defeated European champions Italy 3–0 at Wembley Stadium on 1 June 2022.

Format

The match is a single game played at a neutral venue, with no extra time — a penalty shoot-out decides the winner if the score is level after 90 minutes.
