Rian Johnson

Rian Craig Johnson
Born: 17 December 1973, Silver Spring, Maryland, U.S.
Occupations: Director, writer, producer
Known for: Brick, Looper, Star Wars: The Last Jedi, Knives Out

Rian Craig Johnson (born December 17, 1973) is an American filmmaker. He made his directorial debut with the neo-noir mystery Brick (2005), a high-school detective story filmed in his home town of San Clemente, California, which won a special jury prize at Sundance. He followed it with the con-artist film The Brothers Bloom (2008) and the acclaimed science-fiction thriller Looper (2012).

Television and Star Wars

Johnson directed three episodes of the television series Breaking Bad, including "Fly" and "Ozymandias", the latter frequently cited among the greatest television episodes ever made. He then wrote and directed Star Wars: The Last Jedi (2017), the eighth episode of the main Star Wars saga, which grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide and received strong critical reviews while dividing audience opinion.

Knives Out and later work

Johnson returned to the whodunit with Knives Out (2019), an ensemble murder mystery starring Daniel Craig as detective Benoit Blanc; it earned Johnson an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Screenplay. A sequel, Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery, followed in 2022, with a third film announced thereafter. With his producing partner Ram Bergman he runs the company T-Street, and he co-created the mystery television series Poker Face (2023) starring Natasha Lyonne.
