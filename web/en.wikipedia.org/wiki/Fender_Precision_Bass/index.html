Fender Precision Bass

The Fender Precision Bass (often shortened to P-Bass) is a model of electric bass guitar manufactured by Fender Musical Instruments Corporation. Introduced in 1951, it was the first mass-produced electric bass guitar to earn widespread attention and use, and it remains one of the best-selling and most widely copied instruments of its kind.

History

Designed by Leo Fender, the Precision Bass was created to give bassists a portable, amplified alternative to the double bass, with frets that allowed notes to be played with "precision". The original model had a slab ash body, a single-coil pickup and a Telecaster-like headstock. A major redesign in 1957 introduced the contoured body, larger headstock and the split-coil humbucking pickup that became the model's signature sound.

Influence

The Precision Bass has been a staple of rock, soul, funk, punk and popular music generally. Notable players include James Jamerson, Carol Kaye, John Paul Jones, Pino Palladino and Steve Harris.
