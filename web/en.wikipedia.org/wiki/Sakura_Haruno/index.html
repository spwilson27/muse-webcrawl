Sakura Haruno

Sakura Haruno (春野 サクラ, Haruno Sakura) is a fictional character in the Japanese manga and anime series Naruto, created by Masashi Kishimoto. Sakura is a kunoichi (female ninja) from the village of Konohagakure and a member of Team 7 alongside Naruto Uzumaki and Sasuke Uchiha, under the leadership of Kakashi Hatake. In the anime she is voiced by Chie Nakamura in Japanese and Kate Higgins in English.

Creation and conception

Kishimoto designed Sakura as the heroine of the series, modelling her on a typical young girl — somewhat vain and concerned with her appearance — while giving her pink hair and a large forehead, the latter a source of childhood teasing in the story. He has said she was intended to be an ordinary, relatable girl rather than a genius, in contrast to Naruto and Sasuke.

Character outline

In Part I, Sakura is intelligent and excels at chakra control and academics, but contributes little in combat and is preoccupied with her crush on Sasuke, which strains her friendship with her rival Ino Yamanaka. After Sasuke defects from the village, Sakura resolves to become stronger and asks the legendary medical ninja Tsunade to train her. In Part II (Naruto: Shippuden) she has become a highly skilled medical-nin with superhuman strength produced by precise chakra control, able to heal grievous wounds and fight on the front line. During the Fourth Great Ninja War she completes the Strength of a Hundred Seal, matching her teacher's mastery. In the sequel series Boruto, she is married to Sasuke, directs the village's medical department and hospital, and is the mother of Sarada Uchiha.

Reception

Sakura has regularly placed in the series' official popularity polls, though critical and fan opinion has been divided: her limited role in early arcs was often criticized, while her growth as a medic and combatant in Part II drew praise. Merchandise including figures and clothing has been produced featuring the character.
