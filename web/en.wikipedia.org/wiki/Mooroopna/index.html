Mooroopna

Mooroopna is a town in the Goulburn Valley in northern Victoria, Australia, located just across the Goulburn River from the larger regional centre of Shepparton, to which it has effectively become a suburb while retaining its own identity. It is part of the City of Greater Shepparton local government area.

The name is believed to derive from an Aboriginal word meaning "deep water hole" or "meeting of the waters", reflecting the town's position near the junction of the Goulburn and Broken Rivers. European settlement began in the 1840s–1850s, and the town developed as a service centre for the surrounding irrigated agricultural district, particularly fruit growing and dairying.

Mooroopna is known for the SPC Ardmona fruit-processing industry in the region, for its hospital (Goulburn Valley Base Hospital is in Shepparton; Mooroopna has local health services), and for community events. The town has a railway station on the Tocumwal line and is connected to Shepparton by the Peter Ross-Edwards Causeway across the river floodplain.

At recent Australian censuses the population of Mooroopna has been around 8,000. The town was affected by major flooding of the Goulburn River, notably in October 2022, when extensive areas were inundated.
