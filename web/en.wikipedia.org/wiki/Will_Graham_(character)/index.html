Will Graham (character)

Will Graham is a fictional character appearing in Thomas Harris's 1981 novel Red Dragon and its adaptations. He is a former FBI special agent and criminal profiler with the ability to empathise with — to think like — the serial killers he hunts, a gift that leaves him psychologically damaged.

In the novel

Graham is introduced as a retired profiler living in Florida, having left the FBI after capturing Dr. Hannibal Lecter — an encounter that left him physically and mentally scarred, since Lecter nearly disembowelled him. Jack Crawford lures him back to help catch the serial killer Francis Dolarhyde, "the Tooth Fairy". Graham reluctantly consults Lecter in prison, setting in motion the events of the novel.

Portrayals

The character was first played by William Petersen in Michael Mann's film Manhunter (1986), then by Edward Norton in Red Dragon (2002). Hugh Dancy portrays Graham as the lead character of the television series Hannibal (2013–2015), which expands his relationship with Lecter before the events of the novel and reimagines his empathy as a form of "pure empathy" shading into an autism-spectrum condition.
