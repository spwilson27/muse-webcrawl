Let's Stay Together (song)

"Let's Stay Together" is a song by American singer Al Green, released in 1971 as the title track of his album Let's Stay Together. It became Green's signature song and reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100, and was later covered successfully by Tina Turner.

ArtistAl Green
Released1971
GenreSoul, R&B
SongwritersAl Green, Willie Mitchell, Al Jackson Jr.
ProducerWillie Mitchell

Background and recording

The song was written by Green with producer Willie Mitchell and drummer Al Jackson Jr., and recorded at Royal Studios in Memphis with the Hi Records house band. Its gentle groove, strings and Green's tender vocal made it a defining recording of Memphis soul.

Reception and legacy

The single topped both the pop and R&B charts in early 1972. Tina Turner's 1983 version revived her career and was a hit internationally. The song was selected for preservation in the United States National Recording Registry in 2010, and gained renewed attention when President Barack Obama sang its opening lines at a 2012 fundraiser at the Apollo Theater.
