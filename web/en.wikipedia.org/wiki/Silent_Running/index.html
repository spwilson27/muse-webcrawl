Silent Running

Silent Running is a 1972 American environmental science fiction film directed by Douglas Trumbull, the special-effects supervisor of 2001: A Space Odyssey. It stars Bruce Dern as Freeman Lowell, a botanist aboard the space freighter Valley Forge, one of several ships carrying geodesic domes containing the last forests of a deforested Earth.

Plot

When orders come to jettison and destroy the domes and return the ships to commercial service, Lowell revolts and, aided by three drone robots — Huey, Dewey and Louie — preserves one dome, steering the ship beyond Saturn and ultimately sending the forest drifting into deep space under the drones' care.

Reception

The film is noted for its ecological theme, Joan Baez's songs, and the influential design of its drones.
