S.T.A.L.K.E.R.

S.T.A.L.K.E.R.
Genre: First-person shooter, survival horror
Developer: GSC Game World
First release: S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl (2007)

S.T.A.L.K.E.R. is a series of first-person shooter video games developed by Ukrainian studio GSC Game World. The series is set in an alternate-reality version of the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone in Ukraine, where a second catastrophic disaster in 2006 transformed the area into "the Zone", a place filled with dangerous anomalies, artefacts, mutants and mercenaries known as stalkers.

Games

The main trilogy comprises S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl (2007), S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Clear Sky (2008), a prequel, and S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Call of Pripyat (2009). A long-awaited sequel, S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl, was released in November 2024 after a development cycle repeatedly delayed, including by Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

Setting and influences

The series draws on the 1972 novel Roadside Picnic by Arkady and Boris Strugatsky and Andrei Tarkovsky's 1979 film Stalker, from which the term "stalker" derives. Players assume the role of a stalker exploring the Zone in search of valuable artefacts spawned by anomalies, while contending with rival factions such as Duty, Freedom, the Monolith and the Ukrainian military.

Gameplay

The games combine first-person shooting with survival elements: radiation, hunger, weapon degradation and dynamic weather affect the player, and an artificial-life system ("A-Life") simulates the behaviour of creatures and stalkers across the Zone even outside the player's view.
