Xicheng District

Xicheng District (Chinese: 西城区; pinyin: Xīchéng Qū) is a district of Beijing, China. Lying in the western part of the city's historic core, it contains many of Beijing's most important political, cultural, and commercial landmarks. The former Xuanwu District was merged into Xicheng in 2010, enlarging it to about 50 square kilometres.

Landmarks

Xicheng contains the Zhongnanhai leadership compound, the Great Hall of the People on the western side of Tiananmen Square, and the Financial Street (Jinrongjie) business area. Cultural sites include Beihai Park, the White Dagoba, Prince Gong's Mansion, the former residences of Song Qingling and Guo Moruo, and the historic Shichahai lake area with its hutong neighbourhoods.

Commerce and education

The Xidan commercial district is one of Beijing's busiest shopping areas. Xicheng is also home to many of the capital's most prestigious primary and secondary schools, and to institutions such as the Central Conservatory of Music and the People's Bank of China headquarters.

Administration

The district is divided into 15 subdistricts, including Xichang'anjie, Xinjiekou, Desheng, Jinrongjie, Taoranting, and Dashilan. It is administered from offices on Ertiao in the north of the district.
