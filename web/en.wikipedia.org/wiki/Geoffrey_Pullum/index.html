Geoffrey Pullum

Geoffrey Keith Pullum (born 1945) is a British-American linguist specialising in the syntax of English. He is a professor emeritus at the University of Edinburgh, having previously taught at University College London and the University of California, Santa Cruz.

Work

Pullum was a co-author of The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language (2002, with Rodney Huddleston), a landmark descriptive grammar, and earlier of the GPSG textbook Generalized Phrase Structure Grammar (1985). He is also known for the essay "The Great Eskimo Vocabulary Hoax" (1989), and for humorous and critical writing on usage, including contributions to the linguistics blog Language Log and the book Far from the Madding Gerund (2006, with Mark Liberman).
