Cindy Sherman

Cynthia Morris Sherman (born January 19, 1954) is an American artist whose photography often casts herself as model, makeup artist, stylist and director in staged portraits that examine identity, gender and representation in mass media.

Cindy Sherman
Born: January 19, 1954, Glen Ridge, New Jersey, U.S.
Education: Buffalo State College
Known for: Conceptual photography
Notable work: Untitled Film Stills (1977–1980)

Early life and education

Sherman grew up on Long Island and studied visual arts at Buffalo State College, co-founding the Hallwalls art space in Buffalo.

Work

Her Untitled Film Stills (1977–1980), 70 black-and-white photographs evoking 1950s–60s film heroines, brought international fame and are held by the Museum of Modern Art. Later series include centerfolds, fashion photographs, history portraits, clowns, society portraits and, more recently, digitally altered self-images shared on social media. Her Untitled #96 and Untitled #153 have achieved major auction prices.

Recognition

Sherman received a MacArthur Fellowship in 1995 and major retrospectives at MoMA (2012) and elsewhere. She is regarded as a key figure of the Pictures Generation.
