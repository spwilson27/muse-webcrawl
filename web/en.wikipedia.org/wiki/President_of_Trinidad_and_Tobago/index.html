President of Trinidad and Tobago

The president of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago is the head of state of Trinidad and Tobago and the commander-in-chief of the Defence Force. The office replaced the British monarch as head of state when the country became a republic in 1976; executive power rests mainly with the prime minister.

Election

The president is elected by an electoral college composed of both houses of Parliament for a five-year term, renewable once. Sir Ellis Clarke was the first president, having previously served as the last governor-general. Christine Kangaloo became president in 2023.

Functions

The president formally appoints the prime minister and senators, assents to legislation, issues proclamations, and performs ceremonial duties. The official residence is the President's House in Port of Spain.
