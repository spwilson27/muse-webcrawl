"Despacito" (Spanish for "Slowly") is a song by Puerto Rican singer Luis Fonsi featuring Puerto Rican rapper Daddy Yankee, released in January 2017. A reggaeton and Latin pop song written by Fonsi, Erika Ender and Daddy Yankee, it became a global phenomenon and one of the most successful Spanish-language songs in pop music history.

Commercial performance

The song topped the charts in 47 countries. A remix featuring Canadian singer Justin Bieber, released in April 2017, propelled it to number one in the United States, where it spent 16 consecutive weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100, tying the then record for the longest-running number-one single. It was the first predominantly Spanish-language song to reach number one on the Hot 100 since "Macarena" in 1996.

Music video and records

The music video, filmed in the La Perla neighbourhood of San Juan, Puerto Rico, became the most-viewed video on YouTube in August 2017 and was the first video on the platform to reach three, four, five, six and seven billion views. "Despacito" also became the most-streamed song of all time at its peak and is credited with mainstreaming Spanish-language pop in the global market, boosting tourism to Puerto Rico.

Accolades

The song won four Latin Grammy Awards in 2017, including Record of the Year and Song of the Year, and received three Grammy Award nominations, including Record and Song of the Year.
