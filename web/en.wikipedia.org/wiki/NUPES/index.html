NUPES — the Nouvelle Union populaire écologique et sociale (New Ecological and Social People's Union) — was a French left-wing political coalition formed in May 2022 ahead of that year's legislative elections. Negotiated under the leadership of Jean-Luc Mélenchon of La France Insoumise (LFI), fresh from his third-place finish in the presidential election, it united LFI with the Socialist Party (PS), Europe Ecology – The Greens (EELV), the French Communist Party (PCF) and smaller parties around a shared programme of over 650 measures and a single candidate per constituency.

2022 legislative elections

In the June 2022 elections NUPES won around 131 seats in the National Assembly (counts vary by affiliation method), becoming the principal opposition force and depriving President Emmanuel Macron's Ensemble coalition of its absolute majority. The alliance campaigned to make Mélenchon prime minister and proposed lowering the retirement age to 60, raising the minimum wage, price controls and an ecological planning programme; its name deliberately echoed the Popular Front of 1936.

Breakup

The coalition was strained by internal disputes — over Europe, nuclear power and the response to the October 2023 Hamas attack on Israel — and effectively collapsed after the 2024 European elections, when the Socialists ran separately. For the snap legislative elections of June–July 2024 the left parties instead formed the New Popular Front (NFP), a looser successor alliance, which won the most seats.
