Dental follicle

The dental follicle, also called the dental sac, is a sac of fibrous connective tissue surrounding the enamel organ and dental papilla of a developing tooth. It is of ectomesenchymal origin.

Development

Cells of the dental follicle give rise to the supporting structures of the tooth: cementum, the periodontal ligament and parts of the alveolar bone. The follicle also plays a role in regulating tooth eruption by coordinating bone resorption above and bone formation below the developing tooth.
