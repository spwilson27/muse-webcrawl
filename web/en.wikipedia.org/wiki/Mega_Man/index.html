Mega Man

Mega Man (known as Rockman in Japan) is a science fiction video game franchise created by Capcom, starring a series of robot characters, each known by the moniker "Mega Man". The original game was released for the Nintendo Entertainment System in 1987 and spawned numerous sequels, spin-off series, and adaptations.

The classic series

The original series follows Mega Man, a lab assistant robot built by Dr. Thomas Light and converted into a fighting robot to stop the schemes of the villainous Dr. Albert Wily and his army of Robot Masters. The games are side-scrolling action-platformers: the player can choose the order in which to tackle stages, and defeating each Robot Master grants Mega Man that boss's weapon, creating a rock-paper-scissors system of weaknesses. Eleven numbered entries were released in the classic series between 1987 and 2018, most famously Mega Man 2 (1988), widely regarded as one of the greatest games of its era.

Spin-off series

  • Mega Man X (1993–): a darker, faster series set a century later, starring the android X.
  • Mega Man Zero, ZX: follow-ups to the X storyline.
  • Mega Man Battle Network and Star Force: role-playing series set in a networked world.
  • Mega Man Legends: 3D action-adventure entries.

Legacy

With well over a hundred titles across all series and tens of millions of copies sold, Mega Man is one of Capcom's flagship franchises and a defining series of the platform genre, known for its precise controls, challenging difficulty, and chiptune soundtracks. The character has appeared in comics, animation (including the 1994 American cartoon), and the Super Smash Bros. series.
