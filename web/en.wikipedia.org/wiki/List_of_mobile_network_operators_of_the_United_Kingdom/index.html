List of mobile network operators of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom has four mobile network operators (MNOs) owning radio access networks, plus many mobile virtual network operators (MVNOs) reselling capacity.

Virtual operators

Notable MVNOs include Tesco Mobile and giffgaff (on O2), Lebara and Asda Mobile (on Vodafone), Smarty (on Three), and Sky Mobile. MVNOs compete on price, SIM-only deals, and niche markets such as international calling.

History

The UK mobile market began in 1985 with Cellnet and Vodafone analogue services. Ofcom regulates spectrum auctions, including the 4G auction of 2013 and subsequent 5G awards in the 700 MHz and 3.6–3.8 GHz bands.
