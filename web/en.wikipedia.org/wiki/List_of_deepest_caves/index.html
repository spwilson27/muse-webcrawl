List of deepest caves

The List of deepest caves ranks the world's known natural caves by vertical depth — the difference in elevation between the highest entrance and the lowest explored point. Only two caves are confirmed deeper than 2,000 metres, both in the Arabika Massif of Abkhazia, Georgia, and explorations continue to extend known systems.

Exploration

Krubera Cave was the first cave known to exceed 2,000 metres, a milestone reached in 2004 by the Ukrainian Speleological Association. Veryovkina surpassed it in 2018 when its terminal siphon was plumbed. Expeditions to these depths require weeks underground, kilometres of rope, and staged camps.
