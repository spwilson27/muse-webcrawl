Laos

Laos, officially the Lao People's Democratic Republic, is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia, bordered by Myanmar, China, Vietnam, Cambodia and Thailand. It has a population of about 7.5 million and its capital is Vientiane, on the Mekong River.

Laos was the most heavily bombed country in history per capita: US forces dropped more than two million tons of ordnance during the Vietnam War era (1964-1973), and unexploded bombs still kill and maim. The one-party socialist state opened its economy after 1986. The Mekong and its tributaries give Laos huge hydropower ambitions — the 'battery of Southeast Asia' — while Luang Prabang, a former royal capital of temples, is a UNESCO World Heritage town. The mysterious Plain of Jars holds thousands of megalithic stone jars.
