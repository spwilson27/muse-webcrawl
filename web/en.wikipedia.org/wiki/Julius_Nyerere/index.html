Julius Nyerere

Born13 April 1922, Butiama, Tanganyika
Died14 October 1999, London
OfficePresident of Tanzania, 1964–1985

Julius Kambarage Nyerere (1922–1999), known as Mwalimu ("teacher"), was a Tanzanian anti-colonial activist and politician who led Tanganyika to independence (1961) and served as first president of Tanzania until 1985.

Ujamaa

His Arusha Declaration (1967) launched ujamaa — African socialism based on familyhood, self-reliance, nationalisation and villagisation. Though it built literacy, health and national unity (promoting Swahili) and avoided ethnic strife, forced villagisation and economic policies performed poorly.

Foreign policy

Nyerere championed Pan-Africanism, supported liberation movements (ANC, FRELIMO, ZANU), hosted the OAU Liberation Committee, and led the 1978–79 war that overthrew Uganda's Idi Amin. He retired voluntarily in 1985 and remained an elder statesman; the Catholic Church declared him a Servant of God.
