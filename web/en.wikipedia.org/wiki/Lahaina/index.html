Lahaina
Location: Maui County, Hawaii, United States
Former capital of the Kingdom of Hawaii

Lahaina (Hawaiian: Lāhainā) is a census-designated place in Maui County, Hawaii, United States. It was the capital of the Kingdom of Hawaii from 1820 to 1845, and a major whaling port in the 19th century. Much of the historic town was destroyed by a devastating wildfire in August 2023.

History

Kamehameha I made Lahaina his royal residence, and his successors ruled from there until the capital moved to Honolulu. In the mid-1800s, Lahaina became one of the principal ports of call for the Pacific whaling fleet. The historic Front Street district preserved many buildings from this era.

2023 wildfire

On 8–9 August 2023, a wind-driven wildfire fuelled by Hurricane Dora's distant passage destroyed most of the town, killing at least 102 people and displacing thousands. The fire was the deadliest in the United States in over a century. Among the losses was much of the historic district; the famed banyan tree in Courthouse Square survived but was badly burned.

Geography

Lahaina lies on the leeward (western) coast of Maui, sheltered by the West Maui Mountains, giving it a dry, sunny climate.
