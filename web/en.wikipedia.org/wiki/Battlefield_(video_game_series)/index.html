Battlefield is a series of first-person shooter video games developed by the Swedish studio DICE and published by Electronic Arts. Beginning with Battlefield 1942 in 2002, the series is noted for large-scale multiplayer battles combining infantry combat with tanks, aircraft and other vehicles, for class-based squad play, and — in later entries built on the Frostbite engine — for destructible environments.

Gameplay

The signature mode, Conquest, pits two teams against each other for control of flags spread across large maps while a ticket counter depletes with each death. Players choose soldier classes (such as assault, medic, support and recon) and fight on foot or operate vehicles ranging from jeeps and tanks to helicopters and jets. Matches have supported up to 64 players on PC for much of the series' history, and Battlefield 2042 raised the count to 128 on newer platforms.

History

Battlefield 1942 (2002), set in the Second World War, was followed by Battlefield Vietnam (2004) and the modern-day Battlefield 2 (2005). The series moved to consoles and the Frostbite engine with Bad Company (2008) and Bad Company 2 (2010), which added single-player campaigns and environmental destruction. Battlefield 3 (2011) and Battlefield 4 (2013) returned to large-scale modern warfare. Battlefield 1 (2016), set in the First World War, was a major commercial success, and Battlefield V (2018) revisited the Second World War. Battlefield 2042 (2021), a near-future, multiplayer-only entry, launched to a mixed reception. The series' next mainline title, Battlefield 6, was released in 2025.

Reception

The franchise has sold tens of millions of copies worldwide and is one of Electronic Arts' flagship properties, frequently compared with the rival Call of Duty series; its emphasis on scale, vehicles and teamwork distinguishes it within the genre.
