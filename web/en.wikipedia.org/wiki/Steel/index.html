Steel

Steel
MaterialAlloy of iron and carbon
PropertiesStrong, versatile, recyclable

[Image: Molten steel pouring in a shower of sparks at a steelworks]

Steel is an alloy of iron with carbon (and often other elements) that is stronger and less brittle than iron alone. The Bessemer and open-hearth processes (19th century) made steel cheap, enabling skyscrapers, railways, ships and bridges; today over a billion tonnes are made yearly, mostly in China.

Making and recycling

Steel is produced from iron ore and coke in blast furnaces, or by melting scrap in electric arc furnaces; it is the world's most recycled material. Stainless steel adds chromium for corrosion resistance. Steel's production emits significant CO₂, driving 'green steel' projects using hydrogen instead of coal.
