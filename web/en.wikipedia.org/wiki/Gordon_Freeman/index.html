Dr. Gordon Freeman is the silent protagonist of the Half-Life video game series created by Valve. A theoretical physicist with a PhD from MIT, Freeman is employed at the Black Mesa Research Facility when a failed experiment — the "resonance cascade" — tears open a portal to the alien world of Xen and triggers an invasion of Earth.

Role in the series

In Half-Life (1998), Freeman fights his way through the ruined facility in his orange Hazardous Environment (HEV) suit, armed initially with his iconic crowbar, and ultimately travels to Xen to defeat the Nihilanth. At the game's end he is placed in stasis by the mysterious G-Man.

In Half-Life 2 (2004), Freeman awakens roughly two decades later in City 17, where humanity lives under the rule of the interdimensional Combine. He becomes a symbol and spearhead of the human Resistance alongside Alyx Vance, Dr. Eli Vance, and Dr. Judith Mossman. The story continues in Episode One and Episode Two (2006–2007), and Freeman's legend is referenced in the prequel Half-Life: Alyx (2020).

Character

Freeman never speaks and is never seen from a third-person perspective during gameplay, a design choice intended to immerse the player. Despite this, other characters treat him as a messianic figure — "the One Free Man" — and his image appears on Resistance graffiti throughout City 17. Freeman has consistently ranked among the most popular video game characters in polls and lists.
