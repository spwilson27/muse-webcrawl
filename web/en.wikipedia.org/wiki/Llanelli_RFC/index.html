Llanelli Rugby Football Club is a Welsh rugby union club founded in 1872 and based in Llanelli, Carmarthenshire. One of the oldest and most successful clubs in Wales, it is nicknamed the Scarlets for its famous scarlet jerseys, and it supplied the identity and much of the heritage of the Scarlets regional team formed in 2003.

History

Llanelli built its reputation at Stradey Park, its home for over a century. The club's most celebrated result came on 31 October 1972, when Llanelli defeated the touring New Zealand All Blacks 9–3, a victory immortalised in Welsh rugby folklore and song. Llanelli also beat the 1991 World Cup-winning Australian side. The club won the Welsh Cup many times and was a dominant force in Welsh club rugby.

Regional era

With the introduction of regional rugby in 2003, the professional Scarlets region was based at Llanelli, and Llanelli RFC continued as a semi-professional club in the Welsh Premiership, acting as a development side. In 2008 the club moved with the region to the new Parc y Scarlets stadium.

Colours and identity

The club plays in scarlet jerseys, and its supporters sing "Sosban Fach", a Welsh folk song associated with the town's tinplate industry.
