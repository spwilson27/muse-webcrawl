Wakhan National Park

Wakhan National Park is a national park in northeastern Afghanistan. Established in 2014, it covers the whole of Wakhan District in Badakhshan Province. The park is situated between the Pamir Mountains in the north and the Hindu Kush in the south, bordering Tajikistan, Pakistan, and China. It comprises about 10,950 km² (4,230 sq mi) and is Afghanistan's second national park, after Band-e Amir National Park.

Flora and fauna

The high mountain landscape is home to snow leopards, Marco Polo sheep, ibex, urial, brown bears, wolves, and numerous bird species. Several thousand Wakhi and Kyrgyz people live within the park's boundaries, and community-based conservation, supported by organisations such as the Wildlife Conservation Society, underpins its management.
