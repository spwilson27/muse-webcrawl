Ostomy system

An ostomy system (or pouching system) is a medical device used to collect waste from a stoma — a surgically created opening in the abdomen — following procedures such as a colostomy, ileostomy or urostomy, in which part of the bowel or urinary tract is diverted after disease, injury or cancer surgery.

A typical system consists of a skin barrier (wafer) that adheres to the skin around the stoma and protects it from effluent, and a collection pouch. Systems are either one-piece, with barrier and pouch combined, or two-piece, in which the pouch can be changed separately while the barrier remains in place. Pouches may be drainable or closed-end, opaque or transparent, and often include filters to release gas and reduce odour.

Proper fitting and skin care are essential to prevent leakage and peristomal skin complications. Ostomy care is commonly supported by specialist stoma care nurses.
