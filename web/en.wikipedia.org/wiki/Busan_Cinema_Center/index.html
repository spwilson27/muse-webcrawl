The Busan Cinema Center, with its record-breaking cantilevered LED roof.

The Busan Cinema Center (Korean: 영화의전당) is a performing arts and film complex in the Centum City district of Haeundae, Busan, South Korea. It is the official headquarters and main venue of the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF), one of Asia's most significant film festivals.

Architecture

Designed by the Austrian firm Coop Himmelb(l)au, selected through an international competition in 2005, the building is dominated by the "Big Roof" — a cantilevered roof of about 85 metres, one of the largest cantilevers in the world, covered with some 42,600 LED lights that can display images and colour patterns. Beneath the roof lies an outdoor cinema seating several thousand, while the complex also contains indoor theatres, a cinémathèque, offices, and rehearsal spaces.

Use

The center opened on 29 September 2011, shortly before the 16th Busan International Film Festival. Beyond the festival it hosts year-round screenings, concerts, and theatrical performances, and has become a landmark of Busan's skyline and a symbol of the city's ambition as a film industry hub.
