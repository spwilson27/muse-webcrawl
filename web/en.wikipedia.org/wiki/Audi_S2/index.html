Audi S2

The Audi S2 is a high-performance car produced by Audi from 1990 to 1995. Developed with input from Porsche on its turbocharging, it succeeded the original Audi Quattro as the flagship performance model of the B3/B4 range.

Overview

The S2 was offered as a three-door coupé, a saloon (sedan) and an Avant (estate). It used a 2.2-litre turbocharged five-cylinder engine: initially 220 PS (3B engine) and later 230 PS (ABY engine), driving all four wheels through a five- or six-speed manual gearbox and the Quattro system. The S2 Coupé accelerated from 0–100 km/h in about 5.9 seconds.

RS2 connection

The S2 Avant formed the basis of the Audi RS2 Avant (1994–1995), co-developed with Porsche, often regarded as the car that created the high-performance estate genre.

The S2 was replaced by the Audi S4/S6 line in the mid-1990s.
