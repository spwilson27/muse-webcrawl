Milford Haven

Milford Haven (Welsh: Aberdaugleddau) is a town and community in Pembrokeshire, Wales, on the north shore of the Milford Haven Waterway, one of the finest deep-water natural harbours in the world.

History

The town was founded in 1790 by Sir William Hamilton as a planned whaling and shipbuilding port, laid out on a grid pattern. In the 19th century it became a Royal Navy dockyard town and a major fishing port. From the 1960s the waterway developed into one of Britain's principal oil ports, with large refineries and terminals, and later liquefied natural gas terminals at South Hook and Dragon.

Port and economy

The Port of Milford Haven handles a substantial share of the UK's seaborne energy imports. The town retains a marina in the old docks, ferry links to Ireland from nearby Pembroke Dock, and a fishing and tourism sector.
