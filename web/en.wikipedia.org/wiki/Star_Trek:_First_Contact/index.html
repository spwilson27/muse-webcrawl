Star Trek: First Contact

Directed by: Jonathan Frakes
Starring: Patrick Stewart, Jonathan Frakes, Brent Spiner, LeVar Burton, Michael Dorn, James Cromwell, Alice Krige
Release date: 22 November 1996
Running time: 111 minutes
Budget: $45 million
Box office: $146 million

Star Trek: First Contact is a 1996 American science fiction film directed by Jonathan Frakes. It is the eighth film in the Star Trek franchise and the second to star the cast of Star Trek: The Next Generation, following Captain Jean-Luc Picard and the crew of the USS Enterprise-E as they battle the cybernetic Borg.

Plot

After destroying a Borg cube attacking Earth, the Enterprise follows a Borg sphere back in time to 2063, where the Borg intend to prevent humanity's first warp flight and first contact with the Vulcans, thereby assimilating Earth in the past. While part of the crew fights a Borg incursion aboard the ship — including Picard's obsessive determination and Data's capture and temptation by the Borg Queen (Alice Krige) — Commander Riker's team on the surface must persuade the warp drive's inventor, the hard-drinking Zefram Cochrane (James Cromwell), to make his flight on schedule despite his reluctance to become a hero.

Production and reception

The film introduced the Sovereign-class Enterprise-E and was the directorial debut of Frakes, cast as Riker. Jerry Goldsmith composed the score. First Contact was a critical and commercial success, grossing $146 million worldwide, receiving an Academy Award nomination for Best Makeup, and is frequently ranked as the best of the Next Generation films and among the best Star Trek films overall. Its Borg Queen, time-travel plot, and Cochrane's first-contact flight became central elements of later franchise entries, including Star Trek: Enterprise and Star Trek: Picard.
