Revolution

Revolution
DefinitionFundamental and rapid change in political power or social structures
ExamplesAmerican (1776), French (1789), Russian (1917), Industrial Revolution
[Image: A dramatic painting of revolutionaries waving flags over a barricade]

A revolution is a fundamental and relatively sudden change in political power and organisation, typically occurring when the population revolts against a government. The word, borrowed from astronomy's revolutions, covers the American and French revolutions, the Russian one and Haiti's slave revolution.

Wider sense

By extension, 'revolution' names deep transformations without barricades: the Neolithic, Industrial, scientific and digital revolutions. Political scientists study why revolutions erupt (fiscal crisis, elite splits, mass grievance) and why they so often devour their children.
