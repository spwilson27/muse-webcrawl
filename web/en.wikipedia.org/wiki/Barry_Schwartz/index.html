Barry Schwartz

Barry Schwartz (born August 15, 1946) is an American psychologist and professor. He was the Dorwin Cartwright Professor of Social Theory and Social Action at Swarthmore College until his retirement in 2016. He is best known for his work on decision-making and the psychology of choice.

Work

Schwartz's book The Paradox of Choice: Why More Is Less (2004) argues that eliminating consumer choices can greatly reduce anxiety for shoppers, and that having too many options can lead to decision paralysis and dissatisfaction. His 2006 TED talk on the subject has been widely viewed. He distinguishes between "maximizers", who seek the best possible option, and "satisficers", who settle for a good-enough option, finding that maximizers tend to be less happy with their decisions.

His later books include Practical Wisdom: The Right Way to Do the Right Thing (2010, with Kenneth Sharpe), which argues that rules and incentives crowd out wise judgment, and Why We Work (2015), which explores what gives work meaning beyond pay.
