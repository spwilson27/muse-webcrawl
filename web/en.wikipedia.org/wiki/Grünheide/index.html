Grünheide

Grünheide (full name Grünheide (Mark)) is a municipality in the Oder-Spree district of Brandenburg, Germany, located southeast of Berlin. It is known for its lakes and forests, including the Müggelspree-Löcknitzer Wald- und Seengebiet nature area, and has long been a local recreation destination.

The municipality was formed in 2003 by merging six villages, including Grünheide itself, Kagel, Kienbaum, Hangelsberg, Mönchwinkel, and Spreeau. Kienbaum is home to a federal training centre for Olympic sports.

Grünheide gained international attention as the site of Tesla's Giga Berlin (Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg), the company's first manufacturing plant in Europe, which opened in March 2022 and produces electric vehicles and, later, battery cells. The factory's construction and expansion were accompanied by debate over water use and forest clearance in the surrounding area.
