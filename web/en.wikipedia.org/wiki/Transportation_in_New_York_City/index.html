Transportation in New York City

The transportation system of New York City is a network of complex infrastructural systems. New York City, being the most populous city in the United States, has an extensive public transport network that is among the largest and most heavily used in the world. Unlike most American cities, a majority of New Yorkers do not own cars, and public transport accounts for a far higher share of journeys than anywhere else in the country.

Public transport

The New York City Subway, operated by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA), is one of the largest rapid transit systems in the world, with 472 stations. The MTA also runs the city's bus network, the Staten Island Railway, and the Long Island Rail Road and Metro-North Railroad commuter lines. NJ Transit and PATH connect the city with New Jersey. The subway operates 24 hours a day, a rarity among world metro systems.

Roads and vehicles

The city is linked to the surrounding region by numerous bridges and tunnels, including the George Washington Bridge, among the busiest motor vehicle bridges in the world. The city's famous yellow taxicabs, regulated by the Taxi and Limousine Commission, are an iconic symbol. Traffic congestion in Manhattan has long been severe; a congestion pricing scheme for the central business district was approved and introduced in the mid-2020s.

Air and water

The New York metropolitan area is served by John F. Kennedy International Airport, LaGuardia Airport and Newark Liberty International Airport, making it the busiest airport system in the United States. The Port of New York and New Jersey is one of the largest natural harbours in the world. Ferries, including the free Staten Island Ferry, carry millions of passengers across the harbour and rivers each year.

Walking and cycling

New York has one of the highest rates of walking of any large American city. Since the 2000s the city has expanded its network of protected cycle lanes, and the Citi Bike bicycle-sharing system, launched in 2013, became one of the largest in the world.
