GRU

GRU may refer to several unrelated topics:

  • GRU (Russian Federation) — the Main Directorate of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation (Russian: Главное разведывательное управление, Glavnoye razvedyvatelnoye upravleniye), Russia's military intelligence agency, historically known by the acronym GRU.
  • Gated recurrent unit — a gating mechanism in recurrent neural networks (also abbreviated GRU), introduced in 2014 as a simpler alternative to long short-term memory (LSTM) networks in machine learning.
  • São Paulo/Guarulhos International Airport — IATA airport code GRU, Brazil's busiest airport.
  • Felonious Gru — the fictional protagonist of the Despicable Me animated film franchise.
  • Grande Ravine and other minor place names.
