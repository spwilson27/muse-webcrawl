Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey Circus

Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey Circus, billed as "The Greatest Show on Earth", was a famous American travelling circus formed by the merger, in 1919, of the Ringling Bros. circus and P. T. Barnum and James Anthony Bailey's Barnum & Bailey Circus.

Founded1919 (merger)
FoundersRingling brothers; P. T. Barnum; James A. Bailey
OwnerFeld Entertainment
Closed / Relaunched2017; relaunched 2023 (without animals)

History

The combined show toured the United States by train, growing into one of the largest circuses in the world. It was purchased by the Feld family in 1967. Facing declining attendance and the retirement of its performing elephants in 2016 amid animal-welfare pressure, the circus gave its final performance under canvas-era format in May 2017.

Relaunch

Feld Entertainment revived the show in 2023 as a reimagined, animal-free production built around human performers, aerial acts, and technology.
