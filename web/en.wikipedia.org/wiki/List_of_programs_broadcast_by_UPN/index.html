List of programs broadcast by UPN

The United Paramount Network (UPN) was an American broadcast television network that operated from January 16, 1995, to September 15, 2006. This is a list of television programs formerly broadcast by UPN. The network was known for its science fiction franchises, African-American sitcoms, and youth-oriented drama.

Drama

  • Star Trek: Voyager (1995–2001) — the network's flagship launch series.
  • Star Trek: Enterprise (2001–2005)
  • Buffy the Vampire Slayer (2001–2003; previously on The WB)
  • Veronica Mars (2004–2006; moved to The CW)
  • Roswell (2001–2002; previously on The WB)
  • Seven Days (1998–2001)
  • The Sentinel (1996–1999)
  • Nowhere Man (1995–1996)
  • Marker (1995)
  • Swift Justice (1996)

Comedy

  • Moesha (1996–2001)
  • The Parkers (1999–2004)
  • Girlfriends (2000–2006; moved to The CW)
  • Everybody Hates Chris (2005–2006; moved to The CW)
  • Malcolm & Eddie (1996–2000)
  • The Hughleys (2000–2002; previously on ABC)
  • Half & Half (2002–2006)
  • One on One (2001–2006)
  • All of Us (2003–2006; moved to The CW)
  • In the House (1996–1999; previously on NBC)

Reality and other programming

  • America's Next Top Model (2003–2006; moved to The CW)
  • WWE SmackDown (1999–2006, as WWF/WWE Friday Night SmackDown)
  • UPN Blockbuster Shockwave movie presentations

Children's programming

UPN aired the UPN Kids block in the late 1990s and later carried Disney's One Too block (1999–2003), featuring shows such as Recess and Sabrina: The Animated Series.
