Open University

The Open University (OU) is a public research university and the largest university in the United Kingdom by number of students. Founded in 1969, it pioneered distance learning and open entry — most undergraduate courses require no prior qualifications.

History

Championed by Prime Minister Harold Wilson and Jennie Lee, the OU received its Royal Charter in 1969 and admitted its first students in 1971. Early courses used BBC television and radio broadcasts late at night, correspondence materials, and residential schools.

Teaching

Today courses are delivered online via virtual learning environments, printed materials, tutorials, and assessments, serving mature and part-time learners worldwide. It also produces free learning via OpenLearn and co-productions with the BBC.
