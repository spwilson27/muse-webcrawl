Port Everglades

Cruise ships docked at Port Everglades.

Port Everglades is a seaport in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, in Broward County. It is one of the busiest cruise ports in the world and one of Florida's leading container ports, as well as a major petroleum storage and distribution hub for South Florida. Port Everglades is a man-made harbour created in the 1920s on the site of Lake Mabel.

History

The port was dedicated on February 22, 1928, as Bay Mabel Harbor, and was renamed Port Everglades as part of a promotional effort. Its development was intertwined with the growth of Fort Lauderdale and of the Florida tourism industry.

Activities

  • Cruise: The port is a homeport for major cruise lines including Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Princess and Celebrity, and has regularly ranked among the world's top three cruise ports by passenger volume. On peak days in winter, numerous ships sail on the same day.
  • Cargo: Containerised cargo, bulk and break-bulk goods, and refrigerated produce from Latin America and the Caribbean.
  • Petroleum: The port receives and distributes a large share of the fuel consumed in South Florida.

Harbour deepening

A long-running project to deepen and widen the port's channels to accommodate larger post-Panamax ships has been pursued with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, accompanied by environmental mitigation for nearby coral reefs.
