The Last of Us is an American post-apocalyptic drama television series created by Craig Mazin and Neil Druckmann for HBO. Based on the video game franchise developed by Naughty Dog, the series is set decades after a mass fungal infection caused by a mutation of Cordyceps destroys modern civilisation. It follows Joel (Pedro Pascal), a smuggler tasked with escorting the teenage Ellie (Bella Ramsey) across the post-apocalyptic United States.

Premise

Twenty years after an outbreak of a mutated fungus infects most of humanity, transforming its hosts into aggressive creatures, Joel Miller is hired to smuggle fourteen-year-old Ellie Williams out of an oppressive quarantine zone in Boston. Ellie is immune to the infection, and a resistance group, the Fireflies, hopes her immunity can be used to develop a cure. The journey across a brutal, collapsed America becomes a surrogate father-daughter relationship that is tested by violence, loss, and the moral cost of survival. The second season adapts the events of The Last of Us Part II (2020), introducing Abby (Kaitlyn Dever) and shifting perspectives between Ellie and Abby.

Production

The series was filmed primarily in Alberta, Canada, one of the largest television productions in Canadian history. Druckmann, who wrote and co-directed the games, worked with Mazin (creator of Chernobyl) to adapt the source material, retaining key scenes and dialogue while expanding backstories, most notably in the acclaimed third episode, "Long, Long Time". Gustavo Santaolalla, composer of the games' scores, returned to score the series.

Reception

The first season premiered on 15 January 2023 to widespread critical acclaim for its writing, direction, production design, and the performances of Pascal and Ramsey; it became one of HBO's most-watched debut seasons. The series received numerous accolades, including eight Primetime Emmy Awards from twenty-four nominations for its first season. It is widely regarded as one of the finest video game adaptations ever produced. The second season premiered in April 2025.
