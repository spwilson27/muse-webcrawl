TTF-TCNQ

TTF-TCNQ is an organic charge-transfer salt composed of tetrathiafulvalene (TTF) and tetracyanoquinodimethane (TCNQ). It was one of the first organic compounds shown to exhibit metallic electrical conductivity.

Properties

First reported in 1973, TTF-TCNQ forms crystals in which stacks of donor and acceptor molecules allow electrons to move along one-dimensional chains. Its conductivity rises sharply on cooling before a Peierls transition at low temperature makes it insulating.

Significance

The discovery of TTF-TCNQ helped launch the field of organic metals and molecular electronics.
