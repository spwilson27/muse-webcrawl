Mark Six

The Mark Six (Chinese: 六合彩) is a lottery game organised by the Hong Kong Jockey Club. Launched on 23 September 1976, it is one of Hong Kong's most popular forms of legal gambling, with proceeds going to the Lotteries Fund, which finances charitable and community projects.

Game format

Players select six numbers from 1 to 49. In each draw, seven numbers are drawn: six main numbers and one "extra number" (特別號碼). Prizes are awarded in seven divisions, from matching three main numbers (seventh prize) up to the jackpot for matching all six. If no ticket wins the first prize, the jackpot snowballs into the next draw, sometimes with added "snowball" pools that have produced jackpots exceeding HK$100 million.

History

The lottery was introduced in 1976 with numbers from 1 to 36 and was intended in part to combat illegal lotteries. The format was expanded over the years — to 42 numbers in 1990, 45 in 1996, 47 in 2002 and the current 49 in 2006. Draws are typically held three times a week (Tuesday, Thursday and a weekend day) and broadcast live on television.

Betting types

Besides single entries, players may buy multiple entries (choosing more than six numbers), banker entries, and Quick Pick tickets with randomly generated numbers. Bets can be placed at Jockey Club off-course betting branches, via telephone and online accounts.
