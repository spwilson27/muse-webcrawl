Best practice

[Image placeholder: Best practice benchmarking cycle]

A best practice is a method or technique that has been generally accepted as superior to other known alternatives because it often yields results that are superior to those achieved with other means, or because it has become a standard way of complying with legal or ethical requirements.

Best practices are used to maintain quality as an alternative to mandatory legislated standards and can be based on self-assessment or benchmarking. They are employed in fields including business management, software engineering, healthcare, education, construction and government administration.

Criticism

The term has been criticized as vague managerial jargon: a practice that works in one context may not transfer to another, and labelling something "best" can discourage improvement beyond the current standard. Scholars note the distinction between "best practice" and the more defensible "good practice" or "smart practice".
