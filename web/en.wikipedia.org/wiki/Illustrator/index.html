Illustrator

An illustrator is an artist who specialises in creating images that accompany, explain or decorate a text or concept. Illustrations appear in books, magazines, newspapers, advertisements, packaging, posters, video games and film and television production art.

Techniques

Traditional media include pen and ink, watercolour, gouache, acrylic and printmaking techniques such as woodcut and etching. Since the late twentieth century, digital tools — raster and vector graphics editors and drawing tablets — have become standard in commercial illustration.

History

Illustration flourished in the nineteenth-century "golden age" of book and magazine publishing, with artists such as Arthur Rackham, Edmund Dulac and N. C. Wyeth. In the twentieth century, editorial and advertising illustration became major professions, and professional bodies such as the Society of Illustrators (founded 1901) were established.

Specialisations

Illustrators may specialise in children's books, scientific and medical illustration, fashion, technical illustration, concept art or editorial cartoons.
