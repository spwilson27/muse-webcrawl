Haitian cuisine

Haitian cuisine consists of cooking traditions and practices from Haiti, blending French, African, Taíno, Spanish and Middle Eastern influences. It is known for bold seasoning with epis (a blend of peppers, garlic and herbs), Scotch bonnet peppers, and pikliz, a spicy pickled vegetable relish.

Staple dishes

Well-known dishes include griot (fried pork), diri ak djon djon (rice with black mushrooms), diri kole ak pwa (rice and beans), soup joumou (pumpkin soup eaten on Independence Day, 1 January), tassot, banan peze (fried plantain), akra (malanga fritters), and lalo (jute leaf stew). Joumou was inscribed by UNESCO on its Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2021.

Ingredients and drinks

Staples include rice, beans, cornmeal (mayi moulen), plantains, yams and tropical fruits. Drinks include rum, clairin, crémas, and fresh juices. French-derived patisserie and Levantine-influenced kibbeh (kibi) also feature.
