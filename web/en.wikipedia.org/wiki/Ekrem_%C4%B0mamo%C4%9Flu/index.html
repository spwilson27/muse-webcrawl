Ekrem İmamoğlu (born 3 June 1970) is a Turkish politician who served as mayor of Istanbul from 2019. A member of the Republican People's Party (CHP), he rose to national prominence by defeating the governing AKP's candidate in the 2019 Istanbul mayoral elections and became widely regarded as a leading rival to President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.

2019 elections

İmamoğlu narrowly won the March 2019 mayoral election, but the result was annulled by the Supreme Electoral Council. In the June 2019 re-run he won by a much larger margin — over 800,000 votes — ending 25 years of AKP-aligned control of Istanbul's municipality.

Mayoralty and legal cases

As mayor he focused on public transport, green space and social aid. Prosecutors brought several cases against him, including a 2022 conviction for "insulting public officials" that carried a political ban; the verdict remained under appeal. In March 2025 he was arrested on corruption and other charges shortly before the CHP was expected to name him its presidential candidate, triggering widespread protests across Turkey. His Istanbul University diploma was annulled the day before his arrest.
