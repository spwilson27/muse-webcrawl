Hawker centre

A hawker centre, or cooked food centre, is an open-air complex found in Malaysia, Hong Kong, Singapore, Indonesia, and Thailand, housing many stalls that sell a variety of inexpensive food. They are typically found near housing estates or transport hubs and arose when itinerant street hawkers were relocated into purpose-built premises for hygiene and regulation.

History

In Singapore, hawker centres were built from the 1970s to the 1980s to resettle street hawkers; today there are more than 100 centres, and hawker culture there was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2020. Famous centres include Maxwell Food Centre, Old Airport Road Food Centre, and Amoy Street Food Centre.

Food

Stalls serve dishes such as Hainanese chicken rice, char kway teow, laksa, roti prata, satay, and nasi lemak, reflecting the multicultural heritage of the region. Prices are kept low, making hawker centres a daily dining staple for all income groups.
