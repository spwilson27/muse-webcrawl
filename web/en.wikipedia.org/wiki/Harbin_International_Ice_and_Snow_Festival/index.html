Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival
Location: Harbin, Heilongjiang, China
Founded: 1985
When: Early January to late February
Venues: Sun Island, Ice and Snow World

The Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival (哈尔滨国际冰雪节) is an annual winter festival in Harbin, China, and one of the world's largest ice and snow festivals. It began in 1985, developing from Harbin's traditional ice lantern shows.

Features

Thousands of workers cut blocks of ice from the frozen Songhua River to build full-scale illuminated buildings, palaces and slides at Ice and Snow World, while Sun Island hosts monumental snow sculptures. Night lighting with coloured LEDs embedded in the ice is a signature attraction.

Scale

The festival draws millions of visitors in sub-zero temperatures (often below −20 °C). Competitions in ice sculpting attract teams from around the world, and activities include ice swimming, sledding and winter sports.
