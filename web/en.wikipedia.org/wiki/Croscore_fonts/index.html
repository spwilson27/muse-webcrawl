Croscore fonts

The Croscore fonts are a collection of TrueType fonts produced by Ascender Corporation for Google. They are metric-compatible with the three most widely used Microsoft core fonts: Arial, Times New Roman, and Courier New. The name is a portmanteau of "Chrome OS" and "core fonts", reflecting their development for the ChromeOS operating system.

Because they are metrically identical to the Microsoft fonts, documents formatted with Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New can be displayed and printed with the Croscore fonts substituted without changing line breaks or page layout — important for document interchange between Windows systems and ChromeOS or Linux.

Licensing and derivatives

The fonts were initially released by Google under the Apache License 2.0. In 2013, updated versions with improved coverage were released under the SIL Open Font License as part of the related Crosextra fonts, which added Carlito (metric-compatible with Calibri) and Caladea (metric-compatible with Cambria). The Croscore fonts are widely bundled with Linux distributions and with the LibreOffice office suite.
