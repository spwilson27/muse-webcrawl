Defence industry of Australia

The defence industry of Australia comprises companies and government facilities that design, manufacture and sustain equipment for the Australian Defence Force.

Overview

Major activities include shipbuilding in Adelaide and Henderson, aerospace support, munitions, electronics and armoured vehicles. Firms include Austal, Thales Australia, BAE Systems Australia and ASC.

Policy

Australian governments emphasise sovereign industrial capability, local content in major projects such as frigates and submarines, and export promotion.

Exports

Australia exports patrol boats, radar, Bushmaster vehicles and components, mainly to partners in the Indo-Pacific and Five Eyes.
