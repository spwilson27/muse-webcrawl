Vocational school

A vocational school — also called a trade school, technical school or career school — is an educational institution designed to provide the practical skills and training needed for a specific job, trade or craft, rather than a purely academic education.

Programmes

Courses cover fields such as automotive repair, electrical work, plumbing, carpentry, healthcare assistance, cosmetology, culinary arts, information technology and welding, typically lasting from months to two years and leading to certificates, diplomas or associate degrees. Many systems combine classroom study with apprenticeships, notably Germany's dual education system.

By country

In the United States vocational training is offered by high schools, community colleges and for-profit colleges; in the United Kingdom by further education colleges; in Australia by TAFE institutes. Vocational routes have periodically been promoted as alternatives to university amid skilled-labour shortages.
