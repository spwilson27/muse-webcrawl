Geography

Geography is a field of science devoted to the study of the lands, features, inhabitants, and phenomena of Earth. It bridges the natural sciences and the social sciences: physical geography studies landscapes, climate and ecosystems, while human geography studies populations, cultures, economies and cities.

History

The word comes from Greek words meaning 'Earth description'. Geography was practised in ancient Babylon, Greece and China; figures such as Eratosthenes, who calculated Earth's circumference, and Ptolemy foundational to mapmaking. Exploration, cartography and, later, aerial photography and geographic information systems (GIS) transformed the field.

Branches

Branches include geomorphology, climatology, biogeography, cartography, urban geography, economic geography and political geography.
