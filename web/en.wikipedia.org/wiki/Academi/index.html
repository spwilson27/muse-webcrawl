Academi

Academi is an American private military company founded in 1997 by former Navy SEAL officer Erik Prince. Originally founded as Blackwater, the company became widely known — and controversial — for its security contracts during the Iraq War. It was renamed Xe Services in 2009 and Academi in 2011. In 2014 it merged with Triple Canopy to form the Constellis group.

History

Prince established the company in 1997 and built a large training facility in Moyock, North Carolina, near the Great Dismal Swamp. The company expanded rapidly after the September 11 attacks, providing security for US diplomats and installations in Iraq and Afghanistan. In 2004, four Blackwater contractors were killed in an ambush in Fallujah, an event that contributed to the First Battle of Fallujah.

Nisour Square shooting

On 16 September 2007, Blackwater guards escorting a US State Department convoy shot and killed 17 Iraqi civilians and wounded 20 others in Nisour Square, Baghdad. The incident provoked outrage in Iraq and the United States, intensified scrutiny of private military contractors, and led to prosecutions; four guards were convicted in US federal court in 2014, and were pardoned in 2020.

Rebranding and merger

The company lost its Iraq security licence in 2009 and rebranded as Xe Services, then Academi in 2011 after being acquired by a group of private investors. In 2014 Academi merged with Triple Canopy and other companies to form Constellis Holdings.
