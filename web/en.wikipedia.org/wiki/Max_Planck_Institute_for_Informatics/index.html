Max Planck Institute for Informatics

The Max Planck Institute for Informatics (German: Max-Planck-Institut für Informatik, MPI-INF) is a research institute in computer science located in Saarbrücken, Germany, on the campus of Saarland University. It is part of the Max Planck Society and was founded in 1990, with Kurt Mehlhorn as founding director.

Research

The institute conducts basic research across computer science, organized in departments and research groups covering algorithms and complexity; computer vision and machine learning; computer graphics; databases, information and knowledge management (known for the YAGO knowledge base); visual computing; and the Internet architecture and network systems. It also hosts Max Planck Research Groups and works closely with Saarland University, the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI), and the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems, forming the Saarbrücken computer science cluster.

Education

Together with partner institutions, the institute participates in the Saarbrücken Graduate School of Computer Science and the International Max Planck Research School for Computer Science, training doctoral students from around the world.
