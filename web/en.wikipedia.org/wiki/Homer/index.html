Homer

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: A marble bust of Homer: blind eyes, aged bearded face, in the Hellenistic portrait tradition]

Homer was a Greek poet who is credited with the authorship of the The Iliad and the The Odyssey, two epic poems that are foundational works of ancient Greek literature and among the oldest surviving works of Western literature. Homer is considered one of the most revered and influential authors in history.

Nothing certain is known of his life; tradition makes him a blind bard from Ionia, perhaps of the 8th century BC. The 'Homeric Question' — whether one poet or a tradition of oral-formulaic composition produced the epics — remains a classic scholarly debate. The Iliad dramatises a few weeks in the final year of the Trojan War; the Odyssey follows Odysseus's ten-year voyage home.
