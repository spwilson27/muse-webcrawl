The American Legion Auxiliary (ALA) is a patriotic service organisation affiliated with The American Legion, a veterans' organisation in the United States. Founded in 1919 shortly after the Legion itself, it is one of the nation's largest organisations for women (and, since 2019, for spouses of any gender) who are related to veterans or servicemembers, and it works to support veterans, military families, and communities.

Membership

Membership is open to grandmothers, mothers, sisters, spouses, and direct or adopted female descendants of members of The American Legion, of deceased veterans who served during the Legion's eligibility periods, and of veterans who died in service; in 2019 the rules were amended so that male spouses of veterans may also join. Members are organised into local units attached to Legion posts, grouped into departments at state level.

Programs

The Auxiliary is best known for the red crepe-paper poppies distributed around Memorial Day, a fundraiser for disabled and needy veterans that dates to the 1920s. It sponsors ALA Girls State and Girls Nation, civic-education programs for high-school students founded in 1937, and awards scholarships, supports veterans' hospitals and rehabilitation, assists military families, and promotes Americanism and youth programs such as junior membership activities.
