Nick Park

Nicholas Wulstan Park CBE RDI (born 6 December 1958) is an English animator, director and writer best known as the creator of Wallace & Gromit and Creature Comforts. He is a leading figure at Aardman Animations and is widely regarded as one of the foremost practitioners of stop-motion clay animation.

Nick Park

Born: 6 December 1958, Preston, Lancashire, England

Occupation: Animator, director, writer

Known for: Wallace & Gromit, Creature Comforts, Shaun the Sheep

Awards: Four Academy Awards

Early life and career

Park was born in Preston, Lancashire, the son of a photographer. He studied Communication Arts at Sheffield Hallam University (then Sheffield Polytechnic) and later attended the National Film and Television School, where he began work on the first Wallace & Gromit film, A Grand Day Out. He joined Aardman Animations in Bristol in 1985.

Works

Park's short film Creature Comforts (1989) and the Wallace & Gromit films The Wrong Trousers (1993) and A Close Shave (1995) each won Academy Awards for Best Animated Short Film. He co-directed Aardman's first feature, Chicken Run (2000), and directed Wallace & Gromit: The Curse of the Were-Rabbit (2005), which won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. Later works include Early Man (2018) and Wallace & Gromit: Vengeance Most Fowl (2024).

Style

Park's films are characterized by meticulously crafted plasticine models, gentle humour, affectionate depictions of Northern English life, and elaborate visual gags often involving improbable mechanical contraptions.
