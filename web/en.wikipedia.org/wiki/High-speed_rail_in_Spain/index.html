High-speed rail in Spain

High-speed rail in Spain is operated mainly by Renfe under the AVE brand, on a network managed by Adif. Spain has the largest high-speed network in Europe and the second largest in the world after China, with around 3,900 km of lines in service.

History

The first AVE line, Madrid–Córdoba–Seville, opened in April 1992 for Seville Expo '92, built to standard gauge unlike Spain's Iberian-gauge conventional network. Madrid–Barcelona–French border followed in stages (2003–2013), enabling through services to France.

Network

Radial lines from Madrid reach Valencia, Alicante, Málaga, Granada, León, Burgos, Galicia and Seville. Low-cost services Avlo, Ouigo and Iryo introduced competition after liberalisation in 2021.

Impact

High-speed services dramatically cut journey times — Madrid–Barcelona in about 2.5 hours — shifting travel from air to rail, though critics question the cost and low use of some lines.
