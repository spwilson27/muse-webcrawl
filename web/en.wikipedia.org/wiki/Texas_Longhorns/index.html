The Texas Longhorns are the athletic teams representing the University of Texas at Austin. The Longhorns compete in NCAA Division I and joined the Southeastern Conference (SEC) on 1 July 2024, after nearly three decades in the Big 12 Conference. Texas fields teams in 20 varsity sports and is consistently among the most successful and highest-grossing athletics programmes in the United States.

History and identity

The name "Longhorns" derives from the Texas Longhorn breed of cattle. The university's colours, burnt orange and white, were adopted in the early 1900s, and its "Hook 'em Horns" hand sign, introduced in 1955, is one of the most recognisable gestures in American college sport. The live mascot, Bevo — a Texas longhorn steer — has been present at games since 1916.

Football and major sports

The football programme, playing at Darrell K Royal–Texas Memorial Stadium, claims four national championships (1963, 1969, 1970, 2005), the most recent culminating in Vince Young's victory over USC in the 2006 Rose Bowl. Famous rivalries include the Red River Rivalry with Oklahoma and the series with Texas A&M, renewed when Texas joined the SEC. Texas baseball has won six College World Series titles; the men's and women's swimming and diving programmes are perennial national powers, and Texas teams have won more than 50 NCAA team championships overall across sports. The Longhorn Network, launched with ESPN in 2011, was folded into the SEC Network upon the conference move.
