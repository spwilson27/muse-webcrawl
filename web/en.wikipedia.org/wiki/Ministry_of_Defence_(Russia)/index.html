The Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation (Russian: Министерство обороны Российской Федерации, Минобороны России) is the governing body of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation. It is responsible for defence policy, military administration, logistics, finance, and the day-to-day management of the armed forces, while strategic command is exercised through the General Staff.

History

The ministry was established on 16 March 1992, shortly after the dissolution of the Soviet Union, succeeding the Soviet Ministry of Defence within the Russian Federation. Its first minister was Pavel Grachev, who oversaw the withdrawal of forces from Eastern Europe and the Baltic states and the First Chechen War. Later ministers included Sergei Ivanov, the first civilian to hold the post, Anatoly Serdyukov, who launched sweeping structural reforms, and Sergei Shoigu, who served from 2012 to 2024. In May 2024 economist Andrei Belousov became minister.

Structure

The minister directs the ministry through deputy ministers responsible for armaments, finance, logistics, personnel, and other areas. The General Staff of the Armed Forces, headed by the Chief of the General Staff, is subordinate to the ministry and handles operational planning and command. The ministry's main building is on Znamenka Street in Moscow, and the National Defense Management Center, opened in 2014 on Frunzenskaya Embankment, serves as the principal command-and-control facility in crisis situations.
