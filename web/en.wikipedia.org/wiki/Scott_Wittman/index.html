Scott Whittman is an American costume designer and theatre artist known for his work on Broadway and in regional theatre in the United States. His designs span musicals, plays, and opera, and he has been associated with several long-running productions noted for period-accurate and character-driven wardrobes.

Career

Whittman trained in theatrical design before working as an assistant and associate designer on New York productions, later taking principal designer credits on touring and regional stagings. His approach emphasizes silhouettes and fabric choices that track a character's arc across a production, and extensive collaboration with directors and wig and makeup departments.

Recognition

His work has been acknowledged by regional theatre awards and trade coverage of Broadway design teams. In addition to production work, Whittman has taught masterclasses on costume rendering and the business of theatrical design.
