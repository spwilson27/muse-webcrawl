Chief Executive of Hong Kong

The Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is the representative of Hong Kong and head of the Government of Hong Kong. The position was established in 1997 at the transfer of sovereignty over Hong Kong from the United Kingdom to China, replacing the colonial Governor of Hong Kong.

Selection and powers

Under the Hong Kong Basic Law, the Chief Executive is selected by an Election Committee and formally appointed by the Central People's Government of China, serving a five-year term renewable once. The Chief Executive leads the Executive Council, signs legislation and budgets passed by the Legislative Council, and is responsible for implementing the Basic Law and national laws applied to Hong Kong.

Officeholders

The first Chief Executive was Tung Chee-hwa (1997–2005), followed by Donald Tsang (2005–2012), Leung Chun-ying (2012–2017), Carrie Lam (2017–2022), and John Lee, who took office on 1 July 2022. The official residence is Government House on Upper Albert Road in Central.
