Tokyo Big Sight (東京ビッグサイト, Tōkyō Biggu Saito), officially the Tokyo International Exhibition Center, is a convention and exhibition center in Ariake, in the Koto ward of Tokyo, Japan. Opened in April 1996, it is the largest exhibition venue in Japan and one of the city's most recognisable landmarks, noted for its Conference Tower formed of four inverted pyramids.

Facilities

The complex comprises the Conference Tower, East and West Exhibition Halls, and rooftop exhibition space, together offering more than 100,000 square metres of display area. It hosts major trade shows, including the Tokyo Motor Show (Tokyo Mobility Show), the Tokyo International Anime Fair's successor events, and Comiket, the world's largest doujinshi fair, which draws hundreds of thousands of visitors twice a year.

Olympic role

During the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games, Tokyo Big Sight hosted the International Broadcast Centre and Main Press Centre; nearby Ariake venues held competition events.

Access

The venue is served by the Yurikamome line (Tokyo Big Sight and Kokusai-tenjijo stations) and the Rinkai Line, connecting it with central Tokyo across the Rainbow Bridge area.
