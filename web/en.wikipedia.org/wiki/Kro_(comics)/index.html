Kro (comics)

Kro, leader of the Deviants

Kro is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Jack Kirby, he first appeared in Eternals #1 (July 1976). Kro is a member of the Deviants, an evolutionary offshoot of humanity created by the Celestials, and is unusual among his people for his near-immortality and shape-shifting stability, which allow him to live for tens of thousands of years.

Fictional character biography

Unlike most Deviants, whose genetic instability produces grotesque and differing forms each generation, Kro retains a stable humanoid shape and a lifespan measured in millennia. He has often served as a military leader and warlord of the Deviant empire based in Lemuria, and has covertly lived among humans across history—implied at times to be the origin of figures such as the Roman god Pluto. Kro shares a long, tangled romantic history with Thena of the Eternals, the Deviants' hereditary enemies, with whom he has twin children.

Powers and abilities

Kro possesses limited shape-shifting, allowing him to alter his appearance (he often conceals vestigial horns by reshaping or shaving them), an exceptionally long lifespan, rapid healing and enhanced physical endurance. He is also an experienced military strategist and marksman.

In other media

Kro appears as an antagonist in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film Eternals (2021), voiced and performed by Bill Skarsgård, depicted as a Deviant predator who absorbs the powers of the Eternals he kills.
