Lupin III (Japanese: ルパン三世), also written as Lupin the Third, is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Monkey Punch (Kazuhiko Katō), which follows the escapades of master thief Arsène Lupin III, the grandson of Maurice Leblanc's Arsène Lupin. It debuted on 10 August 1967 and has become a media franchise including several anime television series, animated and live-action films, television specials, video games, and musicals.

Characters

Lupin's gang consists of the cool-headed marksman Daisuke Jigen, the modern-day swordsman Goemon Ishikawa XIII, and the femme fatale Fujiko Mine, whose loyalty is always uncertain. They are relentlessly pursued by Inspector Koichi Zenigata of the ICPO (Interpol), whose life's mission is to capture Lupin.

Adaptations

The first anime series (1971) was initially directed by Hayao Miyazaki and Isao Takahata. Miyazaki made his feature directorial debut with the film The Castle of Cagliostro (1979), now considered a classic of anime. Numerous later series — Part II, Part III (1984), The Woman Called Fujiko Mine (2012), Part 4 (2015), Part 5 (2018), and Part 6 (2021) — and roughly annual TV specials, plus the CGI film Lupin III: The First (2019), have kept the franchise running for over five decades.

Influence

Lupin III helped define the caper genre in Japanese animation and has influenced creators from Shinichiro Watanabe (Cowboy Bebop) to the makers of numerous heist stories worldwide.
