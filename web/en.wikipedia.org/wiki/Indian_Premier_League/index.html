The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India, contested annually by ten city-based franchise teams. Founded by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) in 2007 under Lalit Modi, it is the most-attended and richest cricket league in the world, and one of the richest sports leagues of any kind by revenue per match.

Teams are assembled at a player auction held before each season (with periodic "mega auctions"), drawing the world's best T20 players — including Virat Kohli, who has played his entire IPL career for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, and MS Dhoni, who captained Chennai Super Kings to five titles. Franchises include the Mumbai Indians, Kolkata Knight Riders, Sunrisers Hyderabad and Rajasthan Royals, winners of the inaugural 2008 season. The league's media rights for 2023–2027 sold for over US$6 billion.

Impact

The IPL transformed cricket's economics, popularised the T20 format globally and inspired franchise leagues in other sports and other countries. Its season, played in March–May, regularly out-rates every other television property in India; matches are played at venues such as Mumbai's Wankhede Stadium and the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.
