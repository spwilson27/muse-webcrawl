Portugalete

Portugalete is a town in the province of Biscay in the autonomous community of the Basque Country, northern Spain. It lies on the left bank of the Nervión estuary, at the mouth of the Bilbao Abra bay.

History

Portugalete was founded in 1322 by María Díaz de Haro. It grew as an iron-exporting and fishing port serving Bilbao. The town is linked to Getxo across the estuary by the Vizcaya Bridge (Puente Colgante), a transporter bridge opened in 1893 and designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Landmarks

Landmarks include the Basilica of Santa María, the Salazar Tower, and the promenades along the estuary. The town's fiestas honour Saint Nicholas and Saint Roch.
