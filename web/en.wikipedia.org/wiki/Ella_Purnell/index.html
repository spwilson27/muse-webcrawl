Ella Summer Purnell (born 17 September 1996) is an English actress. She began her career as a child actress, appearing in Never Let Me Go (2010), Intruders (2011) and Maleficent (2014), in which she played the teenage version of Angelina Jolie's character.

Purnell gained wider recognition for roles in Miss Peregrine's Home for Peculiar Children (2016), Army of the Dead (2021), and the television series Sweetbitter (2018–2019) and Yellowjackets (2021–2023). She voices Jinx in the animated series Arcane (2021–2024) and Gwyn in Star Trek: Prodigy, and stars as Lucy MacLean in the post-apocalyptic drama series Fallout (2024–present).

Early life

Purnell was born in Whitechapel, London, and grew up in Bethnal Green. She attended the Sylvia Young Theatre School and took classes at the Young Actors Theatre Islington.

Career

Cast at age 12 in the West End production of Oliver!, Purnell made her film debut in Mark Romanek's Never Let Me Go. She continued with leading film roles including WildLike (2014) and Tim Burton's Miss Peregrine's Home for Peculiar Children (2016), playing Emma Bloom.

On television, she starred in the Starz drama Sweetbitter and appeared in the Agatha Christie adaptation Ordeal by Innocence (2018). Her voice performance as Jinx in Netflix's Arcane won acclaim, and her leading role in Amazon Prime Video's Fallout, adapted from the video game series, brought her further prominence. She also headlined the dark comedy thriller series Sweetpea (2024).
