Celebration, Florida

Celebration
County: Osceola County, Florida
Developed by: The Walt Disney Company
First residents: 1996
Population: about 11,000

Celebration is a master-planned community and census-designated place in Osceola County, Florida, United States, located near Walt Disney World Resort. It was developed by The Walt Disney Company in the 1990s as a prominent example of New Urbanism, with a walkable downtown, front porches, and a mix of housing styles modelled on pre-1940s American towns.

Development

Disney announced the project in 1991 and held a lottery for its first homesites in 1995; the first residents moved in during 1996. The town centre, built around a lake, includes shops, restaurants, a cinema and a hotel. Disney divested most of its control by 2004, and the community is now governed by homeowners' associations and the county.

Design

Architects including Robert A. M. Stern and Jaquelin Robertson planned the town with distinct neighbourhood "villages," allées, and public buildings designed by notable architects such as Michael Graves and Philip Johnson.
