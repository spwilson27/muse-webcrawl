Lamu is a port town on Lamu Island in the Lamu Archipelago of Kenya's coast, and the headquarters of Lamu County. Lamu Old Town, the oldest and best-preserved Swahili settlement in East Africa, was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2001. The town is famous for having almost no motor vehicles: transport is by donkey, of which the town supports several thousand, and by boat.

History

Lamu was founded in the 14th century as one of the Swahili city-states trading across the Indian Ocean in gold, ivory, mangrove poles and slaves. The Portuguese arrived in the early 16th century and dominated the coast for nearly two centuries, after which Lamu came under the influence of the Sultanate of Oman; its golden age came in the late 17th to 19th centuries under Omani protection, when it became a centre of poetry, politics, arts and crafts, and a major entrepôt defended by Lamu Fort (built 1813–1821 by the Mazrui and Omanis). A famous naval victory over Pate and Mombasa forces at Shela in 1812 confirmed its position. The town declined economically after the British colonial administration favoured Mombasa and required trade to pass through it, but retained its religious and cultural importance, notably through the Maulidi festival celebrating the Prophet's birthday, held at the Riyadha Mosque (founded 1900), which draws pilgrims from across East Africa.

Architecture

The old town is built in coral stone and mangrove timber, with narrow winding streets, carved wooden doors, inner courtyards and flat roofs characteristic of Swahili architecture, showing Arab, Persian, Indian and European influences. Notable buildings include Lamu Fort, the Lamu Museum (housed in a former residence of the local royal family), the Donkey Sanctuary run by an animal charity, and dozens of mosques. The neighbouring village of Shela, 3 km south, is known for its beaches and dunes.

Modern Lamu

Tourism is the mainstay of the local economy alongside fishing and traditional boat-building. The town is reached by boat from Mokowe on the mainland or by air to Manda Island. Kenya's planned Lamu Port (part of the LAPSSET corridor project) at Manda Bay, and security incidents in the region in the 2010s linked to al-Shabaab, have both affected the town's fortunes in recent decades.
