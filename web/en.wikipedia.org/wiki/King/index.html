King

A king is a male monarch, the head of state of a kingdom or monarchy. The female equivalent is a queen, who may reign in her own right or be a king's consort. Kingship is one of the oldest forms of government, and the powers of kings have ranged from absolute rule to purely ceremonial constitutional roles.

History

The title derives from Old English cyning, related to "kin". Historically, kings claimed legitimacy by heredity, election, conquest or divine right. Modern kings survive in constitutional monarchies such as the United Kingdom, Spain, the Netherlands, Japan (whose monarch is styled Emperor) and in absolute or semi-absolute monarchies such as Saudi Arabia and Eswatini. A king's realm is a kingdom, and succession is typically hereditary.
