Israeli Premier League

Founded: 1999 (as Premier League; top flight since 1931 in earlier forms)
Country: Israel
Confederation: UEFA
Number of teams: 14
Current champions: Maccabi Haifa / Maccabi Tel Aviv era varies by season

The Israeli Premier League (Hebrew: ליגת העל, Ligat HaAl) is the top division of the Israeli football league system, contested by 14 clubs. It was founded in 1999, replacing Liga Leumit as the top tier.

Format

After a double round-robin regular season, the league splits into a championship round of the top six clubs and a relegation round of the bottom eight. The two lowest clubs are relegated to Liga Leumit. Champions enter the qualifying rounds of the UEFA Champions League, with other leading clubs qualifying for the UEFA Europa Conference League.

History

Football leagues operated in Mandatory Palestine from the 1930s. The most successful clubs in the top flight's history are Maccabi Tel Aviv, Maccabi Haifa, Hapoel Tel Aviv and Beitar Jerusalem. Maccabi Haifa won three consecutive titles in the early 2020s, while Maccabi Tel Aviv remains the record champion overall.
