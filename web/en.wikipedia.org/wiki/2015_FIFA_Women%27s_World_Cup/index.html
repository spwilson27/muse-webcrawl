2015 FIFA Women's World Cup

The 2015 FIFA Women's World Cup was the seventh edition of the FIFA Women's World Cup, held in Canada from 6 June to 5 July 2015. It was the first edition contested by 24 teams.

Final

The United States defeated Japan 5–2 in the final at BC Place, Vancouver, a rematch of the 2011 final. Carli Lloyd scored a hat-trick in the opening 16 minutes, including a goal from the halfway line, and won the Golden Ball.

Significance

Matches were played on artificial turf, a decision criticized by some players; total attendance exceeded 1.35 million and American television audiences set records for women's football.
