Hanshi

Hanshi (範士) is a Japanese title used in many Japanese martial arts for an expert or senior instructor. It is commonly translated as "exemplary teacher" or "model master". The title is one of the senior teacher titles (shōgō) in the ranking system administered historically by bodies such as the Dai Nippon Butoku Kai.

Meaning and requirements

The characters mean roughly "model person" or "exemplar". The hanshi rank is typically awarded only to instructors of 8th dan and above, after decades of practice and teaching, and after first holding the lower teacher titles of renshi ("polished instructor") and kyōshi ("expert teacher"). The holder is expected to embody the spirit and technique of the art and to serve as an example for others.

Usage

The title is used in arts including kendo, iaido, karate, judo and aikido, though criteria vary by organisation. In some modern usage, particularly outside Japan, the related word shihan ("master instructor") is used for a similar role.
