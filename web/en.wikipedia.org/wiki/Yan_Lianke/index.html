Yan Lianke

Yan Lianke (Chinese: 阎连科; born 1958) is a Chinese writer of novels, satire and short stories, widely translated and regarded as one of China's leading contemporary authors. His "mythorealism" style blends myth, fable and stark social reality.

Life and works

Yan was born in rural Henan Province and served in the People's Liberation Army before becoming a full-time writer. His novel Serve the People! (2005) was banned in mainland China. Dream of Ding Village addresses the Henan blood-selling AIDS scandal; The Four Books (2011), set during the Great Leap Forward, received the Franz Kafka Prize (2014), making Yan its first Chinese recipient. Other works include Lenin's Kisses and The Explosion Chronicles, the latter a finalist for the Man Booker International Prize.
