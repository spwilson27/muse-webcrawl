International Mobile Station Equipment Identity

IMEI structure
Length15 digits
ComponentsTAC (8) + serial (6) + check digit (1)

The International Mobile Station Equipment Identity (IMEI) is a unique number, usually 15 digits, used to identify mobile phones and some other wireless devices operating on GSM, UMTS, LTE and 5G networks.

Structure

The IMEI consists of an eight-digit Type Allocation Code (TAC) identifying the device model and origin, a six-digit serial number, and a final check digit computed with the Luhn algorithm. Devices with two SIM slots have two IMEIs.

Uses

Network operators can use the IMEI to block a stolen phone from accessing the network even if the SIM card is changed, through shared databases known as Equipment Identity Registers. The IMEI can typically be displayed by dialling *#06#.

IMEI and identity

Unlike the IMSI, which identifies the subscriber, the IMEI identifies the equipment itself and is transmitted by the device to the network during registration.
