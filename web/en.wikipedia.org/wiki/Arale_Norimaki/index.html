Arale Norimaki (Japanese: 則巻 アラレ) is a fictional character and the protagonist of Akira Toriyama's manga series Dr. Slump (1980–1984), published in Weekly Shōnen Jump. She is a childlike android built by the inventor Senbei Norimaki of Penguin Village. Despite her appearance as a small girl with glasses, Arale possesses superhuman strength and speed, boundless energy, and a naive, cheerful personality that drives the series' slapstick and parody humour.

Character

Senbei built Arale as a supposedly perfect robot, but she is nearsighted — hence her glasses — and endlessly curious and mischievous. She attends middle school in Penguin Village, where her strength routinely causes chaos. Her trademark greetings include "N'cha!" and "Hoyoyo", and her signature move, the "Arale Kick", together with her habit of running with arms outstretched, became iconic in Japanese pop culture. She runs on a fuel called Robovitamin A and is fond of playing with animal droppings on sticks, a running gag of the series.

Appearances

Arale starred in the Dr. Slump manga (18 volumes), two anime television series (1981–1986 and 1997–1999), and numerous animated films. She has made crossover appearances in Toriyama's later series Dragon Ball, appearing in the General Blue saga and, decades later, in Dragon Ball Super (2016), where her gag-manga abilities prove comically overwhelming even against Dragon Ball's strongest fighters. She also appears in video games including Jump Super Stars and J-Stars Victory VS.

Reception and legacy

Dr. Slump was Toriyama's breakthrough hit and won the Shogakukan Manga Award in 1981. Arale became one of the most recognisable manga characters in Japan; her design strongly influenced later "moe" and gag characters, and her silhouette — glasses, cap with wings, and overalls — remains a staple of cosplay and merchandise.
