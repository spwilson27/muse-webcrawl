2019 Istanbul mayoral election

The 2019 Istanbul mayoral election refers to two elections for Mayor of Istanbul held in 2019. In the initial vote on 31 March, Ekrem İmamoğlu of the opposition Republican People's Party (CHP) narrowly defeated Binali Yıldırım of the ruling Justice and Development Party (AKP). Following an AKP appeal, Turkey's Supreme Electoral Council annulled the result, and a re-run was held on 23 June 2019, in which İmamoğlu won by a much larger margin.

Election summary

First vote: 31 March 2019 (annulled)
Re-run: 23 June 2019
Winner: Ekrem İmamoğlu (CHP)
Runner-up: Binali Yıldırım (AKP)
Re-run margin: ~806,000 votes (54.2% to 45.0%)

March election and annulment

The 31 March local elections saw the AKP lose control of both Istanbul and Ankara for the first time in 25 years. In Istanbul, İmamoğlu led by roughly 13,000 votes after recounts. The AKP alleged irregularities, and on 6 May the Supreme Electoral Council voted 7–4 to annul the Istanbul mayoral result and order a re-run — a decision widely criticised by the opposition and international observers. İmamoğlu, who had served as mayor for 18 days, was removed from office.

June re-run

İmamoğlu campaigned on the slogan "Everything will be fine" (Her şey çok güzel olacak). In the 23 June re-run he won 54.2 percent of the vote — a margin of about 806,000 votes — taking 28 of Istanbul's 39 districts. Yıldırım conceded on election night, and President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan congratulated İmamoğlu. He officially took office on 27 June 2019.
