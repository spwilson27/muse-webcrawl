Dancing with the Stars is the name of several international television series based on the format of the British BBC series Strictly Come Dancing, in which celebrities are paired with professional dancers to perform predetermined dances, competing for judges' points and audience votes.

Format

Each week couples perform routines in styles such as ballroom and Latin dances. Scores from a panel of judges are combined with public voting, and the couple with the lowest combined total is eliminated until champions lift the mirrorball trophy (in the US, the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy).

United States version

The American version premiered on ABC on 1 June 2005 and became one of the network's signature reality series, hosted for many years by Tom Bergeron, with co-hosts including Samantha Harris, Brooke Burke-Charvet and Erin Andrews. Later seasons moved to Disney+ and back to ABC simulcast, with Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough hosting. Judges have included Len Goodman, Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough.

International versions

The format has been licensed to dozens of countries, including Australia, Germany (Let's Dance), France, Italy, India (Jhalak Dikhhla Jaa) and many others, making it one of the most successful television formats worldwide.
