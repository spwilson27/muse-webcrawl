Israeli–Palestinian conflict

Date20th century – present
LocationIsrael, West Bank, Gaza Strip

The Israeli–Palestinian conflict is an ongoing political and military conflict between Israelis and Palestinians over the territory of the former Mandate for Palestine, encompassing competing national aspirations, land, borders, security, and the status of Jerusalem and Palestinian refugees.

The conflict's roots lie in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, with the rise of Zionism and Arab nationalism. International efforts to resolve it, including the UN Partition Plan of 1947, the Oslo Accords of the 1990s, and various two-state proposals, have not produced a final settlement.

History

Key events include the 1948 Arab–Israeli War and the creation of the State of Israel, the 1967 Six-Day War and Israel's occupation of the West Bank and Gaza Strip, the First and Second Intifadas, and repeated Gaza wars.

Core issues

Central unresolved issues include mutual recognition, borders, Israeli settlements, control of Jerusalem, military control, and the right of return of Palestinian refugees.
