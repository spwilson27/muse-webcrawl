Nat Geo Wild

Nat Geo Wild (stylised as Nat Geo WILD) is a cable and satellite television network jointly operated with the National Geographic Society that focuses primarily on wildlife and natural history non-fiction programming. It is a sister network to the National Geographic Channel. The channel first launched internationally in 2006 (in some markets replacing Adventure One) and in the United States on March 29, 2010, replacing the Fox Reality Channel.

Programming

The network's schedule centres on wildlife documentaries, predator–prey series, and veterinarian and animal-rescue reality shows. Notable programs include The Incredible Dr. Pol, Dr. K's Exotic Animal ER, Secrets of the Zoo, Big Cat Week (an annual programming event), Dog Whisperer with Cesar Millan (reruns), and wildlife specials featuring big cats, sharks, and African savanna animals.

International versions

Localised versions of Nat Geo Wild have been broadcast across Europe, Asia, Latin America, Africa, and Australia, generally in both standard and high definition; some regional feeds have closed as distribution moved to the Disney+ streaming service.
