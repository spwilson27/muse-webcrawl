Brony fandom
Origin: 2010, 4chan
Focus: My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic

A brony (plural bronies) is a member of the adult fandom of the animated television series My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic (2010–2019), launched by Lauren Faust. Although the show was aimed at young girls, it attracted a large following of older fans, initially mostly young adult men, who embraced the term "brony" — a blend of "bro" and "pony".

Culture

The fandom emerged on 4chan in 2010 and spread to dedicated forums, fan art, music, fiction and conventions such as BronyCon in the United States and BUCK in the United Kingdom. Female fans are sometimes called "pegasisters". Fan works include the long-running fan fiction Fallout: Equestria and extensive remix music scenes.

Reception

The phenomenon surprised Hasbro and the show's creators, who engaged with the fandom; the show incorporated nods to fans, including the popular background character Derpy Hooves. The fandom has also been noted for charity fundraising.
