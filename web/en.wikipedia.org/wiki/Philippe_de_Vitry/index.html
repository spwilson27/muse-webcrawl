Philippe de Vitry

Philippe de Vitry (31 October 1291 – 9 June 1361) was a French composer, music theorist, poet and bishop. He was a leading figure of the Ars nova musical style in fourteenth-century France, and the treatise bearing that name is traditionally attributed to him in whole or in part.

Vitry served at the courts of French kings and became Bishop of Meaux. His surviving motets demonstrate sophisticated isorhythmic techniques, and contemporaries praised him as a master of music, arithmetic, astronomy and rhetoric.
