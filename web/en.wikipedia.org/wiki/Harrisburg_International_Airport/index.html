Harrisburg International Airport (IATA: MDT, ICAO: KMDT) is a public airport in Middletown, Dauphin County, Pennsylvania, United States, about 15 kilometres southeast of Harrisburg. It is the primary commercial service airport for the Harrisburg region and south-central Pennsylvania.

History

The airport site was originally Olmsted Field, an Army Air Corps base established during the First World War era and later Olmsted Air Force Base. After the base closed in 1969, the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania acquired it for civil aviation, and commercial flights moved there from Capital City Airport. A new terminal building opened in 2004.

Facilities and airlines

The airport has a single runway, 13/31, of 10,001 feet, capable of handling most commercial aircraft. The passenger terminal has two concourses. Major US carriers and low-cost airlines offer nonstop flights to hub cities such as Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas/Fort Worth, and Orlando. The airport also handles significant air cargo operations and hosts an Air National Guard presence.
