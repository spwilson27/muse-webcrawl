Minister of Public Enterprises

The Minister of Public Enterprises is a minister in the Cabinet of South Africa who heads the Department of Public Enterprises. The department is the shareholder representative of the South African government in several large state-owned enterprises (SOEs), and the minister exercises oversight over their governance, finances, and strategic direction.

Portfolio

State-owned companies falling under the department have included the electricity utility Eskom, the transport and logistics group Transnet, the arms manufacturer Denel, South African Airways, the South African Forestry Company (Safcol), and Alexkor, a diamond mining company. Because these entities are central to electricity supply, freight rail, and ports, the portfolio has been one of the most economically significant in government, particularly during the years of load-shedding and logistics constraints.

History

The Department of Public Enterprises was established in its modern form after the first democratic elections in 1994, when the restructuring and possible privatisation of apartheid-era state corporations became a major policy question. Successive ministers have overseen restructuring programmes, bailouts, and governance interventions at the SOEs. In 2024, following the formation of a government of national unity, the department's functions were slated for reorganisation, with oversight of SOEs to be moved into a new holding-company model under the Presidency.
