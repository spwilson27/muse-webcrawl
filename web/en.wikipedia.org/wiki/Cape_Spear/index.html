Cape Spear
LocationAvalon Peninsula, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada
SignificanceEasternmost point of North America
Lighthouses1836 (heritage) and 1955 towers

Cape Spear, located on the Avalon Peninsula near St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, is the easternmost point of land in Canada and in North America (excluding Greenland). It is a National Historic Site of Canada and one of the province's most visited attractions.

Lighthouses

A lighthouse has operated at Cape Spear since 1836; the original stone tower, with its attached keeper's dwelling, is among the oldest surviving lighthouses in Newfoundland. A modern concrete tower was built nearby in 1955. The site was designated a national historic site in 1962, and the old light station has been restored to its 1839 appearance.

Second World War

During the Second World War, Cape Spear guarded the approach to St. John's harbour. A coastal defence battery with two 10-inch guns was installed, along with bunkers and tunnels that remain visible today.

Visiting

Because of its position, Cape Spear is the first place in North America to see the sunrise, and it is a popular spot for watching icebergs, seabirds and humpback whales in season.
