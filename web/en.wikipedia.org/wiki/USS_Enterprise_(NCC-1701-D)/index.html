The USS Enterprise (NCC-1701-D) is a fictional starship in the Star Trek franchise, serving as the primary setting of the television series Star Trek: The Next Generation (1987–1994). A Galaxy-class vessel operated by Starfleet for the United Federation of Planets, it is the sixth Federation starship to bear the name Enterprise and is commanded by Captain Jean-Luc Picard.

Design

The Enterprise-D was designed by Andrew Probert, who updated Matt Jefferies's classic configuration of saucer section, engineering hull, and twin warp nacelles for the larger 24th-century vessel. At roughly 642 metres long, the ship carried a crew of about 1,000, including civilian family members, reflecting Starfleet's long-duration exploration missions. A distinctive feature was saucer separation, allowing the saucer section to detach from the battle section in emergencies. Production models included a large filming miniature built by Industrial Light & Magic for the pilot episode "Encounter at Farpoint", later supplemented by smaller models and, eventually, computer-generated imagery.

Depiction

Throughout The Next Generation, the Enterprise-D undertook missions of exploration, diplomacy, and defence, encountering species and phenomena including Q, the Borg, and the Romulans. The ship was destroyed in the 1994 film Star Trek Generations: after battle damage caused a warp-core breach, the saucer section crash-landed on the planet Veridian III. In the third season of Star Trek: Picard (2023), a reconstructed Enterprise-D — rebuilt around the recovered saucer and components of other Galaxy-class ships by Geordi La Forge at the Fleet Museum — returned as a central element of the story.
