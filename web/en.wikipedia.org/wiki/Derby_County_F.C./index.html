Derby County Football Club is a professional football club based in Derby, England. Founded in 1884 as an offshoot of Derbyshire County Cricket Club, Derby were one of the twelve founder members of the Football League in 1888.

History

The club's most successful period came in the 1970s under Brian Clough and later Dave Mackay, winning the First Division title in 1971–72 and 1974–75. Derby won the FA Cup in 1946. They have played at Pride Park since 1997, having previously played at the Baseball Ground.
