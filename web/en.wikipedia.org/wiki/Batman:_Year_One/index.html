"Batman: Year One" is a four-issue comic book storyline published by DC Comics in Batman #404–407 (1987), written by Frank Miller and illustrated by David Mazzucchelli. It retells Batman's origin for the post-Crisis era and is considered one of the greatest Batman stories ever published.

Story

The narrative runs in parallel: Bruce Wayne returns to Gotham City after twelve years abroad and begins his first, mistake-ridden year as Batman — choosing the bat as his symbol after one crashes through his study window — while honest police lieutenant James Gordon arrives in a corrupt Gotham, struggles against police brutality and organised crime, and gradually becomes Batman's ally. The story also introduces Selina Kyle's first steps toward becoming Catwoman.

Influence

Its grounded, noir tone shaped nearly every later version of the character, heavily influencing the films Batman Begins (2005) and The Batman (2022), and it was adapted as an animated film in 2011.
