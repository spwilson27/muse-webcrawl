Majipoor is a fictional planet created by the American science fiction author Robert Silverberg. It is the setting of the Majipoor series, beginning with the Hugo- and Nebula-nominated novel Lord Valentine's Castle (1980).

The world

Majipoor is a vast, low-density planet, mostly covered by ocean, with three great continents: Zimroel, Alhanroel and Suvrael. It was settled by humans some ten thousand years before the novels, and later by other species — the two-headed Su-Suheris, the four-armed Skandars, the reptilian Hjorts, the Liimen and others — who live together in a largely peaceful, agrarian, low-technology society. The native inhabitants, the shape-shifting Piurivar ("Shapeshifters" or Metamorphs), were displaced into reservations by the human colonists, a historical injustice central to later books.

The population is enormous — some twenty billion — yet the planet's size keeps it sparsely settled. Government is divided between two rulers: the Coronal, a secular emperor who rules from the immense city atop Castle Mount, and the Pontifex, a senior figure who governs from the underground Labyrinth. Other powers include the King of Dreams and the Lady of the Isle of Sleep, who send benevolent dreams to the population.

The books

The series includes Lord Valentine's Castle (1980), Majipoor Chronicles (1982), Valentine Pontifex (1983), The Mountains of Majipoor (1995), Sorcerers of Majipoor (1997), Lord Prestimion (1999) and King of Dreams (2001), together forming a blend of science fiction and fantasy — a far-future world in which technology has softened into something resembling magic.
