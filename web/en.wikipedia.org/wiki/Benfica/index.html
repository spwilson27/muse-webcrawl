S.L. Benfica
Founded: 1904
Stadium: Estádio da Luz, Lisbon
Nickname: Águias (Eagles)

Sport Lisboa e Benfica, commonly known as Benfica, is a Portuguese professional football club based in Lisbon, founded in 1904, and one of the 'Big Three' clubs of Portugal alongside FC Porto and Sporting CP.

History

Benfica dominated Portuguese football in the 1960s under coach Béla Guttmann, winning the European Cup in 1961 and 1962 with Eusébio as its star. The club plays at the Estádio da Luz.

Honours

Benfica is among Portugal's most successful clubs, with a record number of Primeira Liga titles, and holds a famous place in European football history.

Other sports

The multi-sport club also fields teams in basketball, futsal, handball, volleyball and other sports.
