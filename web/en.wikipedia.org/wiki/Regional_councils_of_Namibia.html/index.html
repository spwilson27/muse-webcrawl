Regional councils of Namibia

The regional councils of Namibia are the upper tier of local government in Namibia. The country is divided into 14 regions, each governed by a regional council composed of councillors elected from the region's constituencies. Regional councils are responsible for regional development planning and certain service delivery functions, and they elect members to the National Council, the upper house of Parliament.

The regions include Khomas (containing the capital, Windhoek), Erongo, Omusati, Oshana, Zambezi and Karas (officially ||Kharas). Regional and local elections are held every five years.
