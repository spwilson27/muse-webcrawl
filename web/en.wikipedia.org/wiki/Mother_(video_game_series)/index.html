Mother, known outside Japan as EarthBound, is a series of role-playing video games created by Japanese writer Shigesato Itoi and published by Nintendo. Unlike most role-playing games of its era, which used medieval fantasy settings, the Mother series is set in contemporary (1980s–1990s) America-inspired towns, and its child protagonists fight with baseball bats, frying pans and psychic powers (PSI) rather than swords and sorcery.

Gameplay and style

The games use turn-based combat viewed in the first person, with a distinctive "rolling HP meter" in the second and third entries that lets a character survive a mortal blow if healed before the counter runs down. Enemy encounters include whimsical foes such as New Age Retro Hippies and Ramblin' Evil Mushrooms. Itoi's writing mixes humour, Americana and darker themes of family, loss and childhood.

Legacy

Although EarthBound sold poorly on its initial North American release, the series built a devoted cult following. Ness and Lucas appear as fighters in Nintendo's Super Smash Bros. series, and the games have influenced later independent RPGs such as Undertale. Mother 3's lack of an official Western release, and fans' celebrated unofficial translation (2008), remain frequent subjects of discussion among players.
