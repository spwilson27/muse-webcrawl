Charles Fleischer (born August 27, 1950) is an American actor, stand-up comedian and voice artist. He is best known as the voice of Roger Rabbit in the 1988 film Who Framed Roger Rabbit and its follow-up cartoons, and for his distinctive stand-up routines built around unusual props and voices.

Charles Fleischer, voice of Roger Rabbit

Career

Fleischer was born in Washington, D.C., and developed his comedy act in clubs during the 1970s, becoming a regular at The Comedy Store in Los Angeles. His film roles include appearances in A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984), Back to the Future Part II (1989), Dick Tracy (1990), Straight Talk (1992) and The Polar Express (2004).

Roger Rabbit

Fleischer voiced the animated title character in Robert Zemeckis's Who Framed Roger Rabbit (1988), famously wearing a Roger Rabbit costume on set to help the live-action actors play against the character. He reprised the role — along with the voices of Benny the Cab and two weasels — in the Roger Rabbit theatrical shorts Tummy Trouble (1989), Roller Coaster Rabbit (1990) and Trail Mix-Up (1993), and at Walt Disney theme parks.

Other work

Fleischer has also written for television, performed his one-man shows, and pursued an interest in science and mathematics, including presenting ideas about "moleeds" — his term for musical note patterns — in lectures and online. He remains a familiar voice in animation and a fixture of American stand-up comedy.
