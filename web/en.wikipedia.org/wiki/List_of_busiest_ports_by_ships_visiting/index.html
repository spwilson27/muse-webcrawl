List of busiest ports by ships visiting

This article lists seaports ranked by the number of vessel calls — individual visits by ships — in a given year. Vessel-call rankings differ markedly from rankings by cargo tonnage or container throughput: ports served by frequent ferry and short-sea services, archipelagic ports with many small domestic calls, and major bunkering and transhipment hubs can record very large numbers of arrivals even when individual vessels are small.

Overview

The Port of Singapore has consistently reported among the highest numbers of vessel arrivals of any port in the world, with roughly 130,000–140,000 vessel calls per year in recent years, reflecting its position on the Strait of Malacca shipping lane. The Port of Dover in the United Kingdom handles tens of thousands of ferry movements across the English Channel, while ports in Indonesia and the Philippines record large call counts from inter-island domestic fleets.

Comparisons between ports are complicated by differing national definitions: some authorities count every harbour movement, including tugs, barges, and fishing craft, while others count only sea-going commercial vessels. Rankings should therefore be treated as approximate.
