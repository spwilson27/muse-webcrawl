UN/CEFACT

UN/CEFACT is the United Nations Centre for Trade Facilitation and Electronic Business, a subsidiary body of the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE). It develops standards, recommendations, and technical specifications to improve the ability of business, trade, and administrative organizations to exchange products and services effectively.

Full nameUnited Nations Centre for Trade Facilitation and Electronic Business
Parent bodyUN Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE)
HeadquartersGeneva, Switzerland

Work

UN/CEFACT is best known for the development and maintenance of UN/EDIFACT, the international standard for electronic data interchange (EDI), and for its XML-based standards family, which includes the Core Component Technical Specification (CCTS) and standards for electronic invoicing and supply-chain data exchange. Its recommendations cover trade facilitation topics such as codes for units of measure, countries, currencies, and modes of transport, as well as the layout key for trade documents aligned with the United Nations Layout Key for Trade Documents.

Organization

Experts from national governments, intergovernmental organizations, and the private sector participate in open working groups and forums, meeting principally in Geneva. Its outputs are freely available and are used by customs authorities, logistics providers, and software vendors worldwide.
