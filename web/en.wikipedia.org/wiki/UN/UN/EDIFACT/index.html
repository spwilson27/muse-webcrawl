UN/EDIFACT

Full nameUnited Nations/Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport
Maintained byUN/CEFACT
TypeEDI standard

UN/EDIFACT (the United Nations/Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport) is an international electronic data interchange (EDI) standard developed under the United Nations and maintained by UN/CEFACT, the United Nations Centre for Trade Facilitation and Electronic Business.

Structure

EDIFACT messages are composed of segments, which are in turn composed of data elements and composites, delimited by special characters: the segment terminator (apostrophe), the data element separator (plus sign), and the component element separator (colon). Each message type is defined in official directories, such as the widely used D.96A release. Common message types include ORDERS (purchase order), INVOIC (invoice), DESADV (despatch advice), and INVRPT (inventory report).

Usage

The standard is used extensively in international trade, shipping, customs, retail, automotive supply chains, and healthcare administration. In North America, ANSI X12 is the predominant EDI standard, while EDIFACT dominates in Europe and much of the rest of the world. A related subset, EANCOM, was developed by GS1 for the consumer goods sector.
