Omnimover

The Omnimover is a ride system developed by Walt Disney Imagineering for use in Disney theme parks. It consists of an endless chain of vehicles that move continuously along a track at a constant speed, allowing guests to board and alight from a moving walkway travelling at the same speed. The system can handle very large numbers of guests per hour—around 2,000 or more—because loading never stops.

Design

Each vehicle is linked to the chain and can be rotated by rails in the track so that guests' attention is directed toward particular scenes, an innovation associated with Imagineer Bob Gurr; the vehicles were dubbed "Doom Buggies" in the Haunted Mansion. Individual speakers in the vehicles deliver narration and sound synchronised to the show scenes.

Attractions

The system debuted at Disneyland in 1967 with Adventure Thru Inner Space and was used for the Haunted Mansion (1969) and later for many Epcot attractions, including Spaceship Earth, The Living Seas' Seacabs, and Journey Into Imagination. Variants using the same continuous-motion principle appear in attractions such as Buzz Lightyear's Space Ranger Spin and Under the Sea ~ Journey of the Little Mermaid.
