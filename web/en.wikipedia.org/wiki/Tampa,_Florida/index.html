Tampa is a city on Tampa Bay, along Florida's Gulf Coast, and the county seat of Hillsborough County. It is the third-most populous city in Florida and the anchor, with St. Petersburg and Clearwater, of the Tampa Bay metropolitan area.

History

The area was inhabited for millennia by Indigenous cultures including the Tocobaga and Pohoy. Fort Brooke was established in 1824, and the town grew after the arrival of Henry Plant's railroad in the 1880s. In 1885 Vicente Martinez Ybor moved his cigar operations to what became Ybor City, making Tampa the "Cigar Capital of the World" and drawing a distinctive immigrant community of Cubans, Spaniards, and Italians. MacDill Air Force Base, home to U.S. Central Command, is a major presence.

Modern city

Tampa's economy centres on Port Tampa Bay — Florida's largest port by tonnage — finance, healthcare, tourism, and defence. Landmarks include the Tampa Riverwalk, Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, and the annual Gasparilla Pirate Festival. Its professional teams are the Tampa Bay Buccaneers (NFL), the Tampa Bay Lightning (NHL), and (across the bay) the Tampa Bay Rays (MLB); Tampa Stadium and Raymond James Stadium have hosted multiple Super Bowls, including Super Bowl LV, won by the Buccaneers in 2021 — the first team to win a Super Bowl in its home stadium.
