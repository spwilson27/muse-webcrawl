Cloud Native Computing Foundation

The Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) is a Linux Foundation project founded in 2015 to advance container technology and align the tech industry around its evolution. It serves as the vendor-neutral home for many of the most widely used open-source infrastructure projects, including Kubernetes, Prometheus, and Envoy.

History

The CNCF was announced in July 2015 with Google's contribution of Kubernetes — its container orchestration system, derived from the internal Borg platform — as the seed project. Founding members included Google, IBM, Intel, Cisco, Red Hat, and VMware. The foundation grew rapidly as cloud providers and enterprises standardised on Kubernetes.

Project maturity levels

Hosted projects progress through sandbox, incubation, and graduation stages, judged on adoption, governance, and community health. Graduated projects include Kubernetes, Prometheus, Envoy, containerd, Helm, etcd, and others forming the core of the cloud-native landscape. The CNCF also runs conformance certification (Certified Kubernetes), training programmes such as the CKA, and research including end-user technology radars.

Events

Its flagship conference, KubeCon + CloudNativeCon, is held in North America, Europe, and Asia and draws tens of thousands of attendees.
