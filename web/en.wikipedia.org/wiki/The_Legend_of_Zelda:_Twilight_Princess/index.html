The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess is an action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo, released as a launch title for the Wii in November 2006 and for the GameCube the following month. With a darker, more realistic art direction answering fan demand after The Wind Waker, it follows Link as he defends Hyrule from the encroaching Twilight Realm.

Story and gameplay

When the usurper Zant blankets Hyrule in twilight, the ranch hand Link is transformed into a wolf in the Twilight and is aided by the imp Midna, exiled princess of the Twili. Alternating between human and wolf forms, Link restores light to the provinces, gathers the Fused Shadows and the shards of the Mirror of Twilight, and ultimately battles Ganondorf, who stands behind Zant's invasion. The game features the series' largest overworld to that date, mounted combat on Epona, and motion-controlled swordplay in the Wii version (whose world is mirrored relative to the GameCube original).

Reception

The game was a major commercial success, selling nearly nine million copies across both versions, and received near-universal acclaim for its dungeons, scale and Midna's characterisation. An HD remaster released for Wii U in 2016.
