YG Entertainment Inc. (Korean: YG 엔터테인먼트) is a South Korean multinational entertainment agency founded in 1996 by Yang Hyun-suk, a former member of the pioneering group Seo Taiji and Boys. It is one of the largest entertainment companies in South Korea and a leading force in the global spread of K-pop, operating as a record label, talent agency, concert producer and brand licensing company.

History

Yang Hyun-suk founded the company after the disbandment of Seo Taiji and Boys. Early successes included the hip-hop duo Jinusean and the group 1TYM, which established YG's hip-hop-oriented identity. The label grew into a major power with Se7en, BigBang (debuted 2006), and 2NE1 (2009); BigBang in particular became one of the best-selling boy bands in the world.

Artists

YG's best-known acts include BigBang and its members' solo careers, 2NE1, Winner, iKon, Blackpink — one of the most successful girl groups in K-pop history — Treasure, and Babymonster. The company has also managed actors and expanded into cosmetics, fashion (through the NONAGON brand) and golf management.

Business

The company listed on the Korea Exchange in 2011. Founder Yang Hyun-suk resigned from all positions in 2019 amid a series of scandals affecting the agency; he later returned to an executive producer role. YG is counted among the traditional "Big Three" South Korean entertainment agencies alongside SM Entertainment and JYP Entertainment.
