Jelly Belly

Type: Private
Industry: Confectionery
Founded: 1898 (as Goelitz); Jelly Belly brand launched 1976
Headquarters: Fairfield, California, U.S.
Products: Jelly beans and other candies

The Jelly Belly Candy Company, formerly the Herman Goelitz Candy Company, is an American manufacturer of jelly beans and other confections, headquartered in Fairfield, California. Its signature product, the Jelly Belly jelly bean, was introduced in 1976 and is known for intense, true-to-life flavors, a small size, and a flavored center as well as shell.

History

The company traces its roots to Gustav Goelitz, a German immigrant who began making candy in Belleville, Illinois, in the 19th century; the Herman Goelitz Candy Company was formally established around 1898 and later expanded to California. In 1976, Los Angeles candy distributor David Klein commissioned Goelitz to create a gourmet jelly bean using natural flavorings; the result, sold initially at the Fosselman's ice cream parlor in Alhambra, was named "Jelly Belly." Klein later sold his share of the trademark.

Jelly Belly beans gained national fame in the 1980s as the favorite candy of President Ronald Reagan, who kept jars of them in the Oval Office and aboard Air Force One; a portrait of Reagan made from thousands of jelly beans hangs at the company's visitor center. The company officially changed its name to the Jelly Belly Candy Company in 2001.

Products

The company produces around 50 standard flavors, from Very Cherry and Buttered Popcorn to novelty "BeanBoozled" flavors mimicking unpleasant tastes, and licensed products based on Harry Potter's Bertie Bott's Every Flavour Beans. A single bean takes roughly 7 to 21 days to produce through the starch-molding process. The Fairfield factory offers public tours.
