Spacecraft cemetery

The spacecraft cemetery, known more formally as the South Pacific Ocean Uninhabited Area, is a region in the southern Pacific Ocean, around Point Nemo, to which decommissioned spacecraft are steered for controlled re-entry.

Point Nemo

Point Nemo, the oceanic pole of inaccessibility, is the place in the ocean farthest from land — about 2,688 km from the nearest shores. Its remoteness makes it the safest target for debris that survives re-entry.

Usage

Since 1971 hundreds of spacecraft, including Russia's Mir station (2001), many Progress cargo ships, and Jules Verne ATV, have been ditched in the wider area. The International Space Station is planned to be deorbited there in the early 2030s.
