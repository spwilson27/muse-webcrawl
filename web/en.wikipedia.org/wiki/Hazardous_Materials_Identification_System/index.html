Hazardous Materials Identification System

The Hazardous Materials Identification System (HMIS) is a numerical rating system developed by the American Coatings Association that conveys the health, flammability, and physical hazards of chemical products in the workplace. HMIS labels use coloured bars — blue for health, red for flammability, orange for physical hazard — each marked with a number from 0 (minimal) to 4 (severe).

Elements

The health bar may include an asterisk indicating a chronic health hazard, and a personal protective equipment (PPE) code letter specifies recommended protective gear. HMIS is designed for workplace labelling under OSHA's Hazard Communication Standard and is similar to, but distinct from, the NFPA 704 diamond used for emergency response.

Revisions

The third version, HMIS III, replaced the yellow "reactivity" bar with the orange "physical hazard" bar and added pictograms. Employers using HMIS must train workers to interpret the ratings alongside safety data sheets.
