Allegiant is a science fiction novel for young adults by American author Veronica Roth, published in October 2013. It completes the Divergent trilogy that Roth began with Divergent (2011) and continued with Insurgent (2012). Unlike its predecessors, Allegiant alternates between the first-person viewpoints of Tris Prior and Tobias "Four" Eaton.

Plot

Following the revelation of a video from Edith Prior explaining that Chicago was an experiment, the faction system collapses. Tris, Tobias and their allies join a rebel group, the Allegiant, and escape beyond the fence surrounding the city. There they discover the Bureau of Genetic Welfare, a United States government agency that has been running the city as a genetic experiment: the factions were designed to heal "genetically damaged" people, and the Divergent are considered genetically pure. Horrified by the Bureau's plans to wipe the memories of Chicago's population, Tris leads a mission to stop it — a mission that ends with her death, leaving Tobias and Christina to carry her memory back to a rebuilding Chicago.

Reception

The novel debuted at number one on bestseller lists and sold strongly, but its ending — the death of the narrator-heroine — proved sharply divisive among readers. Reviews praised Roth's ambition in reframing the series' premise while criticising the dual narration for making the two voices sound similar. Roth defended the conclusion as the necessary completion of Tris's arc of self-sacrifice.

Film adaptation

The Divergent Series: Allegiant (2016), starring Shailene Woodley and Theo James, adapted roughly the first part of the novel; a planned second film, Ascendant, was cancelled after Allegiant underperformed at the box office, leaving the screen series unfinished.
