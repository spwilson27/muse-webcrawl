Media of Turkey

The media of Turkey comprise a large and historically vibrant press, television and radio sector. Turkey's first newspaper appeared in the Ottoman era in the 19th century, and private television broadcasting expanded rapidly from the 1990s. Major outlets include the state broadcaster TRT, newspapers such as Hürriyet, Sabah, Cumhuriyet and Sözcü, and news channels including NTV and CNN Türk.

Press freedom

International monitors have documented a sharp decline in media freedom since the 2010s. Large media groups passed into the hands of owners close to the government, many journalists were dismissed or prosecuted, and Turkey has at times jailed more journalists than any other country. The state advertising agency and broadcast regulator RTÜK are criticised for penalising critical outlets.

Broadcasting and online media

Television remains the dominant news source, while social media and independent online outlets have grown in importance, prompting successive laws tightening state oversight of internet content.
