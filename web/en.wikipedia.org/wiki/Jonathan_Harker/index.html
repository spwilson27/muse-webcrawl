Jonathan Harker

Jonathan Harker is a fictional character and one of the protagonists of Bram Stoker's 1897 Gothic horror novel Dracula. An English solicitor, Harker travels to Castle Dracula in Transylvania to assist Count Dracula in purchasing an English estate, and his journal forms the opening section of the epistolary novel. He is the husband of Mina Harker.

Role in the novel

Harker, a newly qualified solicitor from Exeter, is sent by his employer, Mr. Peter Hawkins, to Transylvania to conclude Count Dracula's purchase of the Carfax estate near London. His journal records his growing horror as he realizes he is a prisoner in the castle, encounters the Count's three vampire brides, and witnesses Dracula's supernatural nature, including his crawling down the castle wall.

Harker escapes by scaling the castle wall and flees to Budapest, suffering a severe nervous fever. He marries his fiancée Mina Murray, who has nursed him back to health. Returning to England, the couple join Professor Abraham Van Helsing, Dr. John Seward, Arthur Holmwood, and Quincey Morris in the hunt for Dracula after the Count attacks Mina. In the final pursuit back to Transylvania, Harker fights alongside the group; it is his kukri knife, together with Quincey Morris's bowie knife, that destroys Dracula in many readings of the climax.

Portrayals

Harker has been portrayed on screen by many actors, including John Van Eyck in Nosferatu (as "Hutter", 1922), David Manners in Dracula (1931), Barry Norton in the Spanish-language version, John Van Eyck, Bruno Ganz in Nosferatu the Vampyre (1979), and Keanu Reeves in Bram Stoker's Dracula (1992).
