University of Akron

The University of Akron is a public research university in Akron, Ohio, United States. Founded in 1870 as Buchtel College by the Universalist Church, it became a municipal university in 1913 and a state university in 1967.

Academics

The university enrolls roughly 15,000 students and is internationally known for polymer science and engineering, reflecting Akron's history as a rubber and tire manufacturing center. It offers programs through colleges of business, engineering, law, health professions, and arts and sciences.

Athletics

Its teams are the Akron Zips, named for a rubber overshoe, competing in the Mid-American Conference; the men's soccer team won the NCAA national championship in 2010.
