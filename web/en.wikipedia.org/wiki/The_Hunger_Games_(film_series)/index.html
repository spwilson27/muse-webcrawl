The Hunger Games film series is a series of American dystopian science-fiction adventure films based on the novels by Suzanne Collins, distributed by Lionsgate and produced by Nina Jacobson's Color Force. The series stars Jennifer Lawrence as Katniss Everdeen and Josh Hutcherson as Peeta Mellark, alongside Liam Hemsworth, Woody Harrelson, Elizabeth Banks and Donald Sutherland.

The first three novels were adapted into four films — the third, Mockingjay, split into two parts — released between 2012 and 2015. A prequel, The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, based on Collins's 2020 novel, followed in 2023. The series has grossed over $3 billion worldwide and received generally positive reviews, with praise for Lawrence's performance and for its themes of media spectacle, war and authoritarianism.

Story overview

The films are set in Panem, a post-apocalyptic nation of a wealthy Capitol and twelve impoverished districts. As punishment for a failed rebellion, each district must supply two children as "tributes" to a televised death match, the Hunger Games. Katniss Everdeen's defiant victory in the 74th Games makes her the "Mockingjay", symbol of a second rebellion that eventually overthrows President Snow's regime. The prequel follows the young Coriolanus Snow as a mentor in the 10th Hunger Games, decades earlier.

Reception and influence

The series was a critical and commercial success and influenced a wave of young-adult dystopian adaptations. Its three-finger salute was adopted by real-world pro-democracy protesters, notably in Thailand after the 2014 coup and in Myanmar in 2021.

Future

A further prequel film, Sunrise on the Reaping, based on Collins's 2025 novel about Haymitch Abernathy's Games, has been announced by Lionsgate.
