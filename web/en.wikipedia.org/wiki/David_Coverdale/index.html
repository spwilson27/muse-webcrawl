David Coverdale


Image placeholder: David Coverdale

David Coverdale (born 22 September 1951) is an English singer and songwriter best known as the lead vocalist of the hard rock band Whitesnake, which he founded in 1978, and for his tenure as lead singer of Deep Purple from 1973 to 1976.

Career

Born in Saltburn-by-the-Sea, Yorkshire, Coverdale answered a magazine advertisement to join Deep Purple, with whom he recorded the albums Burn, Stormbringer, and Come Taste the Band. After Deep Purple split, he formed Whitesnake, which achieved major commercial success in the 1980s with albums such as Slide It In and the multi-platinum Whitesnake (1987), featuring the hits "Here I Go Again" and "Is This Love".

Later work

Coverdale collaborated with Jimmy Page on the 1993 album Coverdale–Page and continued touring and recording with Whitesnake into the 2020s. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2016 as a member of Deep Purple.
