Women in Poker Hall of Fame

The Women in Poker Hall of Fame (WiPHoF) honours women who have made significant contributions to poker as players, industry leaders and pioneers. It was founded in 2007 and held its first induction ceremony in 2008 in Las Vegas.

Inductees

Early inductees included Barbara Enright, Linda Johnson, Marsha Waggoner and Susie Isaacs. Later classes have honoured players such as Jennifer Harman, Kathy Liebert, Kristy Gazes, Vanessa Selbst-era professionals, and industry figures who promoted women's participation in the game.

Purpose

The Hall celebrates achievement and works to increase women's visibility in a historically male-dominated field, alongside events such as ladies' championships at the World Series of Poker.
