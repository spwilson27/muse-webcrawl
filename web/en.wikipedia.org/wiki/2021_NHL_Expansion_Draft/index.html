2021 NHL Expansion Draft

DateJuly 21, 2021
LocationGas Works Park, Seattle, Washington
Expansion teamSeattle Kraken
Selections30 (one from each existing team except Vegas)
Notable picksMark Giordano (Calgary), Jordan Eberle (NY Islanders), Yanni Gourde (Tampa Bay)

The 2021 NHL Expansion Draft was held on July 21, 2021, to stock the roster of the Seattle Kraken, the National Hockey League's 32nd franchise, ahead of its inaugural 2021–22 season. The Kraken selected one unprotected player from each of the league's 30 other teams, with the exception of the Vegas Golden Knights, who were exempt as the most recent expansion team. The event was staged outdoors at Gas Works Park in Seattle and televised by ESPN2 in the United States.

Rules

The draft followed essentially the same framework as the 2017 Vegas expansion draft. Each existing team could protect either seven forwards, three defencemen and one goaltender, or eight skaters and one goaltender. Players with no-movement clauses had to be protected, while first- and second-year professionals and unsigned draft picks were exempt. Seattle was required to select at least 14 forwards, nine defencemen and three goaltenders, and at least 20 players under contract for the coming season, within a prescribed salary-cap range.

Selections

Unlike Vegas in 2017, Seattle made no side trades with other clubs during the draft itself. Its picks included former Norris Trophy-winning defenceman and Calgary Flames captain Mark Giordano, who became the Kraken's first captain; scoring winger Jordan Eberle from the New York Islanders; two-time Stanley Cup champion Yanni Gourde from the Tampa Bay Lightning; goaltender Chris Driedger from Florida; and defencemen Adam Larsson (Edmonton), Jamie Oleksiak (Dallas) and Vince Dunn (St. Louis). Several observers noted that the Kraken passed on high-priced available stars such as Carey Price and Vladimir Tarasenko, favouring salary-cap flexibility.

Aftermath

The Kraken played their first season in 2021–22 at the rebuilt Climate Pledge Arena, finishing near the bottom of the standings before improving markedly in their second season to reach the playoffs.
