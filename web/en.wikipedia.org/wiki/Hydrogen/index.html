Hydrogen is the chemical element with symbol H and atomic number 1 — the lightest and most abundant element in the universe, making up roughly three-quarters of normal matter. Stars, including the Sun, fuse hydrogen into helium, releasing the energy discussed under solar energy.

Uses

On Earth hydrogen occurs mainly in water and organic compounds. Industrially it is used for ammonia fertiliser and refining; as a fuel it burns to water, and 'green hydrogen' made by electrolysis with renewable electricity is promoted for decarbonising steel and shipping — a sustainability strategy. Hydrogen's spectrum was key evidence for early atomic theory, alongside the decays of radium.
