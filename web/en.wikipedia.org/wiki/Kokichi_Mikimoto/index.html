Kokichi Mikimoto

Kokichi Mikimoto
Born: 25 January 1858, Toba, Japan
Died: 21 September 1954
Known for: Cultured pearls; Mikimoto brand

Kokichi Mikimoto (御木本 幸吉, 1858–1954) was a Japanese entrepreneur credited as the inventor of the cultured pearl and the founder of the Mikimoto luxury pearl company.

Life and work

Born in Toba, in present-day Mie Prefecture, the son of a noodle-shop owner, Mikimoto pursued the idea of inducing oysters to produce pearls. After years of failures from red tides and other setbacks, he obtained his first semi-spherical cultured pearls in 1893 on the island now called Mikimoto Pearl Island, and received a patent in 1896. Perfecting spherical cultured pearls by about 1905 (using techniques also developed by Mise and Nishikawa), he built a global business that transformed pearls from rare natural luxuries into widely available jewels.

Legacy

Mikimoto aggressively marketed his pearls internationally, opened stores in London, Paris and New York, and became known as the "Pearl King". His company remains a leading luxury pearl jeweller.
