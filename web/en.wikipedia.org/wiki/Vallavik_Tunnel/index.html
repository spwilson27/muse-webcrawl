Vallavik Tunnel

The Vallavik Tunnel (Norwegian: Vallaviktunnelen) is a 7,510-metre (24,640 ft) long road tunnel in Vestland county, Norway. The tunnel begins at Vallavik in the municipality of Ulvik and ends at Dagnosi in Voss Municipality.

History

The tunnel was built as part of the Hardanger Bridge project to provide a ferry-free connection across the Hardangerfjord. Construction started in 2009 and the tunnel opened on 17 August 2013, the same day as the Hardanger Bridge. It carries National Road 13.

Features

Inside the Vallavik Tunnel is a roundabout connecting to the road that leads onto the Hardanger Bridge; the tunnel is notable for its illuminated roundabout cavern. The tunnel replaced the ferry between Bruravik and Brimnes when combined with the bridge.
