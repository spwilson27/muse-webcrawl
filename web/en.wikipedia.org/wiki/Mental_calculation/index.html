Mental calculation
DefinitionArithmetical calculation performed solely in the mind
CompetitionMental Calculation World Cup (since 2004)
Famous practitionersShakuntala Devi, Alexis Lemaire

Mental calculation (also called mental arithmetic) is the performance of arithmetical calculations using only the human brain, without the aid of paper, calculators or other devices. Practitioners develop strategies for addition, multiplication, roots, logarithms and calendar calculations.

Methods

Common techniques include breaking numbers into parts, cross-multiplication methods for two-digit products, factoring, rounding and compensation, and memorising tables of squares, cubes and logarithms. Some exceptional calculators, known historically as "lightning calculators," performed for audiences as entertainers.

Competitions

The Mental Calculation World Cup has been held in Germany every two years since 2004, and Memoriad events test mental addition, multiplication, square roots and calendar dating under timed conditions. World records include extracting the 13th root of 100-digit numbers in seconds.
