Peter Frampton

Peter Kenneth Frampton (born 22 April 1950) is an English-American guitarist, singer and songwriter. He first gained fame with the Herd and Humble Pie before becoming one of the best-selling solo artists of the 1970s.

Career

Frampton's breakthrough was the 1976 live album Frampton Comes Alive!, one of the best-selling live albums of all time, featuring "Show Me the Way", "Baby, I Love Your Way", and "Do You Feel Like We Do", the latter showcasing his signature talk box guitar effect. His follow-up studio album I'm in You (1977) was also a major seller, though his commercial momentum later slowed.

Later work

Frampton continued recording and touring, played on David Bowie's 1987 Glass Spider tour, and won a Grammy Award for his instrumental album Fingerprints (2006). In 2019 he announced a farewell tour after being diagnosed with inclusion body myositis, though he has continued to perform seated in subsequent "Never Say Never" tours. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2024.
