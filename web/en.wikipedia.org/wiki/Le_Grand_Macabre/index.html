Le Grand Macabre

Le Grand Macabre is an opera in two acts (four scenes) by György Ligeti, his only opera, with a libretto by the composer and Michael Meschke after Michel de Ghelderode's 1934 play La Balade du grand macabre. It premiered at the Royal Swedish Opera in Stockholm on 12 April 1978; Ligeti substantially revised it in 1996.

Set in the imaginary land of Brueghelland, the opera follows Nekrotzar, the Grand Macabre, who arrives to announce the end of the world but becomes too drunk to carry it out. The work is a grotesque, absurdist anti-anti-opera mixing references to Monteverdi, Rossini and Verdi with avant-garde techniques, car horns, and popular styles.

The revised version has entered the repertoire of major opera houses and is widely regarded as a modern classic.
