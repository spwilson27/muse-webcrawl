AS4 (Applicability Statement 4) is an OASIS specification that defines a subset — a "conformance profile" — of the ebXML Messaging Services (ebMS) 3.0 standard for exchanging business documents securely and reliably over the Internet using web services. It was designed as the successor to AS2, applying the successful AS2 design principles to the SOAP-based ebMS protocol stack.

Overview

Like AS2, AS4 uses HTTP as its transport and supports message signing, encryption, compression, and receipts (non-repudiation of receipt). Unlike AS2, it is built on SOAP with Attachments and WS-Security, and it adds support for a "pull" message exchange pattern suited to lightweight clients that cannot accept inbound connections.

Adoption

AS4 has been mandated or recommended in several sectors. The European Commission's eDelivery building block profiles AS4 for cross-border document exchange, and it is used in networks such as Peppol for e-procurement documents. The energy industry, through organisations such as ENTSOG, has adopted AS4 for gas market communications.
