Chamoli district is a district of Uttarakhand state in northern India, in the Garhwal Himalaya. Its administrative headquarters is the town of Gopeshwar. The district is the second largest in the state by area.

Geography

Chamoli is a mountainous district containing peaks such as Nanda Devi, Kamet and Trishul, and the Valley of Flowers National Park. The Alaknanda River and its tributaries drain much of the district.

Places of note

The district includes the major Hindu pilgrimage town of Badrinath, the ski resort of Auli, the town of Joshimath, and Hemkund Sahib, a Sikh pilgrimage site. In February 2021 the district was affected by a major flood disaster caused by a rock and ice avalanche in the Rishiganga valley.
