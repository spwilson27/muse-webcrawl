Chris Cuomo


Placeholder image: Chris Cuomo

Christopher Charles Cuomo (born August 9, 1970) is an American television journalist and anchor. He is the younger brother of former New York governor Andrew Cuomo and the son of former governor Mario Cuomo. He is best known for hosting Cuomo Prime Time on CNN from 2018 to 2021, and since 2022 has hosted Cuomo on NewsNation.

Early life and education

Cuomo was born in Queens, New York City, and educated at The Albany Academy. He earned an undergraduate degree from Yale University and a law degree from Fordham University School of Law, and was admitted to practice law in New York before entering journalism.

Career

Cuomo worked as a correspondent and anchor at Fox News and CNBC before joining ABC News, where he co-anchored 20/20 and served as chief law and justice correspondent and as a co-anchor of Good Morning America. He joined CNN in 2013 as co-host of the morning programme New Day, and moved to prime time in 2018 with Cuomo Prime Time, which became one of CNN's highest-rated shows, noted for his interviews and for his on-air recovery from COVID-19 in 2020.

Departure from CNN

In late 2021, documents released by the New York Attorney General showed that Cuomo had advised aides to his brother Andrew Cuomo as the governor faced sexual harassment allegations — a deeper involvement than he had publicly acknowledged. CNN suspended him in November 2021 and terminated his employment on December 4, 2021. In 2022 he joined NewsNation, where his nightly programme Cuomo debuted that October; he has also hosted a programme on SiriusXM.
