PECO, formerly the Philadelphia Electric Company, is an energy company founded in 1881 and based in Philadelphia. It is a subsidiary of Exelon and the largest electric and natural gas utility in Pennsylvania, serving approximately 1.7 million electric customers and more than half a million natural gas customers in southeastern Pennsylvania, including the city of Philadelphia and its suburbs.

Operations

Since Pennsylvania's electricity deregulation, PECO primarily operates the distribution network — poles, wires, meters and gas mains — while customers may choose their generation supplier. The company is headquartered at the PECO Building at 2301 Market Street in Philadelphia, whose rooftop "crown lights" display scrolling messages, a city landmark.

History

The Philadelphia Electric Company was formed in 1902 from earlier electric light companies. It merged with Unicom of Chicago in 2000 to form Exelon. Its former generation assets included the Peach Bottom and Limerick nuclear stations, operated by Exelon Generation (now Constellation).
