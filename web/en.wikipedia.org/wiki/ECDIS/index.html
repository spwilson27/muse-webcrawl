Full nameElectronic Chart Display and Information System
PurposeMaritime navigation
StandardsIMO performance standards; IHO S-57 / S-52; IEC 61174
RegulationSOLAS Chapter V

An Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS) is a computer-based navigation system that complies with International Maritime Organization (IMO) regulations and can be used as an alternative to paper nautical charts. An ECDIS system displays the information from electronic navigational charts (ENCs) and integrates position information from the Global Positioning System and other navigational sensors, such as radar, Navtex, automatic identification systems (AIS) and depth sounders.

Regulation

The IMO's SOLAS convention requires ships to carry nautical charts for voyage planning and position monitoring. Amendments adopted in 2009 made ECDIS carriage mandatory for most large commercial vessels on international voyages, phased in between 2012 and 2018. To serve as a legal replacement for paper charts, an ECDIS must conform to the IMO performance standards, use official ENC data conforming to the IHO S-57 standard, and be backed up by an independent secondary system.

Functionality

ECDIS provides continuous position monitoring and route planning. It can give alarms for hazards such as crossing a safety contour, deviating from a planned route, or approaching a prohibited area. Chart data are presented according to the IHO S-52 presentation library so that symbols and colors are consistent across manufacturers, and charts are kept up to date through electronic updates issued by hydrographic offices.

ECDIS versus ECS

Electronic chart systems (ECS) that do not meet the full IMO performance standards may be useful navigation aids but do not satisfy SOLAS chart-carriage requirements and cannot legally replace paper charts.
