Hide (musician)


hide with X Japan

Hideto Matsumoto (Japanese: 松本 秀人, December 13, 1964 – May 2, 1998), better known by his stage name hide, was a Japanese musician, singer-songwriter, and record producer. He was the lead guitarist of the rock band X Japan from 1987 until their disbandment in 1997, and enjoyed a successful solo career, releasing albums such as Hide Your Face (1994), Psyence (1996), and Ja, Zoo (1998, released posthumously).

Career

Born in Yokosuka, Kanagawa, hide formed the band Saver Tiger before being recruited by Yoshiki to join X (later X Japan) in 1987. With his flamboyant visual style — including his trademark brightly dyed hair — and innovative guitar work, he became one of the most recognizable figures of the visual kei movement. As a solo artist he experimented with alternative rock, industrial, and pop sounds, and later fronted the American-based band Zilch.

Death and legacy

hide died on May 2, 1998, at the age of 33; his death was widely reported as a suicide, though close associates have suggested it may have been accidental. His funeral drew some 50,000 mourners in Tokyo. He remains one of the most influential guitarists in Japanese rock history: the hide Museum in Yokosuka operated from 2000 to 2005, his music continues to sell, and the annual hide memorial summit concerts celebrate his legacy. Numerous Japanese musicians cite him as a formative influence.
