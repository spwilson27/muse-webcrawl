Hicham El Guerrouj is a retired Moroccan middle-distance runner, widely regarded as the greatest miler of all time. He set the world record for the 1500 metres, 3:26.00, in Rome in 1998, and for the mile, 3:43.13, in 1999 — records that still stand.

Career

After heartbreak at the 1996 and 2000 Olympics, where he took silver in Sydney, El Guerrouj won double gold in the 1500 m and 5000 m at Athens 2004, the first man since Paavo Nurmi in 1924 to achieve that double. He won four consecutive World Championship 1500 m titles from 1997 to 2003.

Honours

He was World Athlete of the Year three times and later became a member of the International Olympic Committee.
