PlayStation Studios

Parent: Sony Interactive Entertainment

Founded: 2005 (as SCE Worldwide Studios)

PlayStation Studios is the first-party video game development division of Sony Interactive Entertainment, overseeing Sony's wholly owned development studios and their games for PlayStation consoles and other platforms. It was founded in 2005 as SCE Worldwide Studios and rebranded as PlayStation Studios in 2020.

Studios and games

Member studios include Naughty Dog (The Last of Us, Uncharted), Santa Monica Studio (God of War), Insomniac Games (Spider-Man, Ratchet & Clank), Guerrilla Games (Horizon), Sucker Punch (Ghost of Tsushima), Bend Studio, Housemarque, Media Molecule, Team Asobi, and Japan Studio's successor teams, among others. Since 2020, PlayStation Studios titles have increasingly been released on PC as well as PlayStation consoles.

Organization

The division manages internal development, support studios such as XDEV and Nixxes Software, and publishing of first-party titles under the PlayStation Studios brand, introduced with a Marvel-style opening animation on games from 2020 onward.
