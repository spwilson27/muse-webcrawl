GABAA receptor

The GABAA receptor is an ionotropic receptor and ligand-gated ion channel. Its endogenous ligand is γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA), the major inhibitory neurotransmitter in the central nervous system. When activated, the receptor selectively conducts chloride ions through its pore, hyperpolarizing the neuron and reducing its excitability.

The receptor is a pentamer assembled from combinations of subunits (α, β, γ, δ, and others), and it is the target of many drugs: benzodiazepines, barbiturates, alcohol, certain anaesthetics, and nonbenzodiazepine "Z-drugs" all modulate it at distinct binding sites, producing anxiolytic, sedative, anticonvulsant, and amnesic effects.
