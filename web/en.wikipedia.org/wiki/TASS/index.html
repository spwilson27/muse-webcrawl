TASS is a major Russian news agency founded in 1904 (as the Saint Petersburg Telegraph Agency), which became the Soviet Union's central news organ as the Telegraph Agency of the Soviet Union — the source of the acronym. Today it is a state-owned Russian agency and the country's largest, filing in Russian, English and other languages.

History

TASS was the authorised voice of the Soviet state, famous for first announcements of major events, and the only source of official Soviet news for foreign media. After 1991 it briefly carried the ITAR-TASS name (1992–2014) before reverting to TASS. Its reporting reflects the Russian government's positions, and Western regulators have treated it as state media in the sanctions era.
