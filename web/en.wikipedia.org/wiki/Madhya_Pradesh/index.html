Madhya Pradesh

Madhya Pradesh (Hindi: Madhyapradeś, meaning "central province") is a state in central India. Its capital is Bhopal, and the largest city is Indore. Other major cities include Jabalpur, Gwalior, and Ujjain. Madhya Pradesh is the second largest Indian state by area and the fifth largest by population, with over 85 million residents. It borders Uttar Pradesh to the northeast, Chhattisgarh to the east, Maharashtra to the south, Gujarat to the west, and Rajasthan to the northwest.

History

The region includes the rock shelters of Bhimbetka, inhabited more than 30,000 years ago and now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In ancient times the area formed part of the Maurya and Gupta empires; the rock-cut caves and temples of Udayagiri and the great stupa at Sanchi date from this era. In the medieval period, the Paramara dynasty ruled from Dhar, and the Chandela dynasty built the temples of Khajuraho. The region later came under the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughal Empire, and the Marathas, before passing to British rule and princely states such as Gwalior and Indore. The modern state was formed on 1 November 1956; in 2000 its southeastern districts were separated to form Chhattisgarh.

Geography

Madhya Pradesh occupies a central position on the Indian subcontinent. The Narmada and Tapti rivers flow westward through rift valleys between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges, while the Chambal, Betwa, and Son rivers drain north and east. Forests cover roughly a quarter of the state's area, and its national parks — including Kanha, Bandhavgarh, and Pench — are noted for Bengal tiger populations; the state is often called the "Tiger State" of India.

Economy and culture

Agriculture is the mainstay of the economy; the state is a leading producer of soybeans, wheat, and pulses. It also has significant mineral resources, including diamonds from the Panna mines. Cultural attractions include the Khajuraho Group of Monuments, the Buddhist monuments at Sanchi, and the historic cities of Mandu and Orchha, all drawing substantial tourism.
