Scott Lang

Scott Lang is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by David Michelinie, Bob Layton, and John Byrne, he first appeared in The Avengers #181 (March 1979) and took over as the second Ant-Man in Marvel Premiere #47 (April 1979), succeeding Hank Pym.

Fictional character biography

Lang is an electronics engineer and reformed thief who stole Pym's Ant-Man suit and Pym Particles in order to rescue the doctor who could save his ill daughter, Cassie. Impressed by his motives, Pym gave him the Ant-Man identity. Lang became a long-time member of the Avengers and the Fantastic Four, and later ran his own security business, Ant-Man Security Solutions. His daughter Cassie became the hero Stature/Stinger.

In other media

Paul Rudd portrays Scott Lang in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Ant-Man (2015) and appearing in Captain America: Civil War, Ant-Man and the Wasp, Avengers: Endgame, and Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania.
