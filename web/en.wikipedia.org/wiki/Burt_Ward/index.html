Burt Ward (born Bert John Gervis Jr.; July 6, 1945) is an American actor and activist best known for his portrayal of Robin, the sidekick of Batman (played by Adam West), in the television series Batman (1966–1968), its theatrical feature film, and later animated revivals of the role.

Early life

Ward was born in Los Angeles, California. As a youngster he was an avid reader of comic books and an accomplished martial artist. He adopted the stage name Burt Ward — his mother's maiden name combined with a shortening of Burton — when he auditioned for Robin at the age of 20.

Career

The campy Batman series became a pop-culture phenomenon, making Ward a teen idol, but typecasting limited his later acting career. He reprised Robin in animated productions including The New Adventures of Batman (1977) and the animated films Batman: Return of the Caped Crusaders (2016) and Batman vs. Two-Face (2017). His 1995 autobiography, Boy Wonder: My Life in Tights, recounts his years on the show.

Animal rescue work

With his wife Tracy, Ward founded Gentle Giants Rescue and Adoptions, a charity devoted to rescuing large-breed dogs, along with an associated pet food line whose proceeds support the rescue work.
