Los Angeles FC

The Los Angeles Football Club, commonly known as Los Angeles FC or LAFC, is an American professional soccer club based in Los Angeles, California, competing in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Western Conference. Founded in 2014, the club began play in 2018 at Banc of California Stadium (now BMO Stadium) in Exposition Park.

History

LAFC was awarded as an expansion franchise in October 2014 following the dissolution of Chivas USA, with an ownership group that eventually included figures such as Magic Johnson, Mia Hamm, Nomar Garciaparra and actor Will Ferrell. Bob Bradley became the first head coach. The team made an immediate impact, setting a points record for an expansion side in 2018 and winning the Supporters' Shield in 2019 behind captain Carlos Vela, who set a league single-season scoring record with 34 goals. LAFC won its first MLS Cup in 2022 under Steve Cherundolo, defeating the Philadelphia Union in a dramatic final decided on penalties, and again won the Supporters' Shield that season. The club also won the U.S. Open Cup in 2024.

Stadium and supporters

BMO Stadium, a 22,000-seat soccer-specific venue, sits on the site of the former Los Angeles Memorial Sports Arena. The north stand is home to the "3252" supporters' union, named for the number of seats in the standing section, noted for continuous chanting and tifo displays.

Rivalry

LAFC's rivalry with the LA Galaxy is known as El Tráfico, one of MLS's most prominent derbies, famous for Zlatan Ibrahimović's debut heroics in its first edition in 2018.
