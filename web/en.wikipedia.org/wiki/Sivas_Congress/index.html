Sivas Congress

The Sivas Congress was an assembly of the Turkish National Movement held in Sivas from 4 to 11 September 1919, during the Turkish War of Independence. Chaired by Mustafa Kemal (later Atatürk), it united regional defence associations into a single national organisation.

Decisions

Building on the earlier Erzurum Congress, the delegates reaffirmed that the Ottoman territories inhabited by a Turkish-Muslim majority were indivisible, rejected any mandate or protectorate (a proposal for an American mandate was debated but defeated), and elected a Representative Committee with Mustafa Kemal at its head to act as a provisional leadership.

The congress laid the organisational groundwork for the opening of the Grand National Assembly in Ankara in 1920 and the eventual founding of the Republic of Turkey.
