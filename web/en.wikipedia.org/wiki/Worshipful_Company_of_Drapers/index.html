Worshipful Company of Drapers

The Worshipful Company of Drapers is one of the historic livery companies of the City of London, ranking third in the order of precedence of the Great Twelve City Livery Companies. Founded by royal charter in 1364, it was originally an association of merchants who bought and sold woollen cloth.

History and modern role

The Drapers' Company is based at Drapers' Hall on Throgmorton Street. Having long ceased to regulate the cloth trade, it now operates principally as a charitable institution, administering trusts, supporting education—including its founding association with Bancroft's School and Thomas Adams school—and granting funds. Its almshouses and schools have included foundations dating to the early modern period.
