Ian Thomas Garner MacKaye (born 16 April 1962) is an American musician and record label founder. He was the frontman of the influential hardcore punk band Minor Threat, a founding member of the post-hardcore band Fugazi, and co-founder, with Jeff Nelson, of Dischord Records, the Washington, D.C. independent label that documented the city's punk scene. His song "Straight Edge" (1981) gave its name to the straight edge movement within punk subculture.

Early bands and Minor Threat

MacKaye grew up in Washington, D.C., and was drawn to punk by bands such as the Bad Brains. After the Slinkees and the Teen Idles — whose "Minor Disturbance" EP was the first release on Dischord (1980) — he formed Minor Threat in 1980 with Lyle Preslar, Brian Baker, and Jeff Nelson. In under three years the band released two EPs and the album Out of Step, defining the speed and directness of American hardcore. The 46-second song "Straight Edge", in which MacKaye described his personal abstinence from alcohol, drugs, and promiscuous sex, was adopted as the creed of a movement he never intended to found and from which he has repeatedly distanced himself as it hardened into dogma.

Fugazi

Following Embrace, MacKaye formed Fugazi in 1987 with Guy Picciotto, Joe Lally, and Brendan Canty. Fugazi broadened hardcore into a rhythmically intricate post-hardcore, releasing albums including Repeater (1990), Steady Diet of Nothing (1991), In on the Kill Taker (1993), and The Argument (2001). The band was as notable for its ethics as its music: inexpensive all-ages shows with a strict $5 ticket policy for many years, refusal of major-label deals and merchandising, and insistence on playing unconventional venues. Fugazi has been on indefinite hiatus since 2003.

Later work

MacKaye performs with his partner Amy Farina as The Evens and, since 2018, in Coriky with Farina and Joe Lally. He continues to run Dischord Records and its extensive archive of D.C. punk recordings and photographs, and is frequently interviewed and sampled as a spokesman for do-it-yourself ethics in music.
