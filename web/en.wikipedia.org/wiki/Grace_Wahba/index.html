Grace Wahba

[Image: Grace Wahba]

Grace Goldsmith Wahba (born 3 August 1934) is an American statistician and I. J. Schoenberg-Hilldale Professor Emerita of Statistics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. She is known for her work on smoothing splines, cross-validation, and reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces.

Career

Wahba studied at Cornell University and Stanford University, where she received her PhD in 1966. Before joining Wisconsin in 1967, she worked at IBM. She developed generalized cross-validation (GCV) as a method for choosing smoothing parameters, a technique now standard in nonparametric regression.

Honours

Wahba was elected to the United States National Academy of Sciences in 2000 and received the International Prize in Statistics in 2025. Her 1990 monograph Spline Models for Observational Data is a standard reference.
