USS Arizona (BB-39)

ClassPennsylvania-class battleship
Launched19 June 1915
Commissioned17 October 1916
FateSunk at Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941

USS Arizona (BB-39) was the second and last Pennsylvania-class super-dreadnought battleship built for the United States Navy. Commissioned in 1916, she served in the Atlantic and Pacific Fleets during the interwar period.

Loss at Pearl Harbor

On 7 December 1941, during the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor, Arizona was struck by an armor-piercing bomb that detonated her forward magazines, causing a catastrophic explosion that destroyed the forward part of the ship. She sank within minutes, killing 1,177 of her crew — nearly half of all American fatalities in the attack. Her wreck remains on the floor of Pearl Harbor, where it continues to leak small amounts of fuel oil, sometimes called the "tears of the Arizona."

Memorial

The USS Arizona Memorial, built over the wreck and dedicated in 1962, is one of the most visited sites in Hawaii and a principal component of the Pearl Harbor National Memorial. The ship's bell and one of her anchors are preserved at the memorial and elsewhere.
