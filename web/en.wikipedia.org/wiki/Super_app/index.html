Super app

A super app bundles messaging, payments, shopping and services in one platform

A super app (or superapp) is a mobile or web application that provides multiple services within a single platform, typically including messaging, payments, e-commerce, food delivery, transport and financial services, often extended by third-party "mini-apps" or "mini-programs". The model was pioneered in Asia — above all by Tencent's WeChat in China — and contrasts with the Western pattern of single-purpose apps. The term was popularised by BlackBerry founder Mike Lazaridis in 2010.

Characteristics

Super apps share a single sign-on and user identity, an integrated payment system (often a wallet or QR-code standard), and a platform layer that hosts lightweight third-party services without separate downloads. Because users live inside one ecosystem, super apps benefit from strong network effects and rich data across services, and they can cross-subsidise new offerings — for example, using a dominant messaging or ride-hailing base to launch lending, insurance or groceries.

Examples

WeChat (launched 2011) is the archetype: with over a billion users, it combines chat, social media, payments, government services and millions of mini-programs. Alipay follows a similar path from payments. In Southeast Asia, Grab and Gojek grew from ride-hailing into food, payments and banking; in India, Paytm and Tata Neu pursue the model; in Latin America, Rappi; in the Middle East, Careem. In the West, companies such as Uber, PayPal and Block have added multiple services, and Elon Musk has repeatedly described ambitions to turn X into an "everything app" modelled on WeChat.

Criticism

Critics cite market concentration and gatekeeping (platform operators control access and take commissions), privacy risks from consolidated data, financial-system risks when apps become banks, and regulatory exposure — Chinese authorities' 2020–21 tech crackdown, which halted Ant Group's IPO and tightened rules on platform finance, was aimed largely at super-app ecosystems.
