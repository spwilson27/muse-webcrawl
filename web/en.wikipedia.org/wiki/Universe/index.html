The universe is all of space and time and their contents — planets, stars, galaxies and all forms of matter and energy. The Big Bang theory describes its expansion from a hot, dense early state 13.8 billion years ago.

Composition and fate

Most of the universe is not ordinary matter: dark matter (~27%) and dark energy (~68%) dominate, understood mainly through their gravitational and expansion effects. The observable universe spans about 93 billion light-years because space itself has expanded while light travelled. Whether expansion accelerates forever or changes course is an open question.
