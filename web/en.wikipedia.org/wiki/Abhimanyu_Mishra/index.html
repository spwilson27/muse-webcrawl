Abhimanyu Mishra

Abhimanyu Mishra (born 5 February 2009) is an American chess grandmaster and prodigy. In June 2021, at 12 years, 4 months and 25 days old, he broke Sergey Karjakin's record to become the youngest grandmaster in chess history, a record that had stood since 2002. Born in Long Branch, New Jersey, to parents who emigrated from India, he learned chess at the age of two and a half.

Records

Mishra set a succession of youngest-ever records in American and world chess: he became the youngest United States Chess Federation expert (2000 rating) at age 7, the youngest US national master at 9 years and 2 months, and the youngest international master in history at 10 years, 9 months and 20 days in November 2019, breaking the record of R Praggnanandhaa. He earned his three grandmaster norms in norm tournaments in Budapest in the spring of 2021 — at the Vezerkepzo GM and First Saturday events — and crossed the 2500 Elo threshold at the Vezerkepzo tournament ending 30 June 2021 to complete the title requirements.

Career

Mishra's record-setting pace, including several trips to norm tournaments in Hungary, attracted wide media coverage and sponsorship. He has since competed in strong open and invitational events in the United States and Europe, including the US Junior Championship and the Chessable Sunway Sitges festival, consolidating a rating in the mid-2600s as a teenager.
