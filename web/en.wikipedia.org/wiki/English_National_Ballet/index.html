English National Ballet

English National Ballet is a classical ballet company founded by Dame Alicia Markova and Sir Anton Dolin as London Festival Ballet and based in London, England. Along with The Royal Ballet, Birmingham Royal Ballet, Northern Ballet and Scottish Ballet, it is one of the five major ballet companies in Great Britain.

History

The company was founded in 1950 as the Gala Performances of Ballet, taking the name London Festival Ballet in 1951. Markova and Dolin had been leading dancers with the Ballets Russes, and they modelled their new touring company on its ambition and star-led programming. The company built its reputation on gala performances, lavish productions of the classics, and appearances by guest stars such as Rudolf Nureyev, who frequently performed with the company and staged productions for it.

In 1989 the company adopted its present name, English National Ballet. Its touring mission — taking large-scale ballet to regional theatres across the United Kingdom — remained central to its identity. In 2012 the Spanish ballerina Tamara Rojo became artistic director, commissioning acclaimed new work including Akram Khan's reimagining of Giselle (2016), alongside full-length classics. Under her tenure the company also launched the Dance for Parkinson's programme and expanded its engagement work.

Home and facilities

In 2019 English National Ballet moved to a purpose-built home, the Mulryan Centre for Dance at London City Island in Canning Town, east London, which it shares with English National Ballet School. The building provides rehearsal studios, a production studio, wardrobe and technical facilities, and space for public engagement work.

Repertoire and touring

The company's repertoire centres on the nineteenth-century classics — Swan Lake, The Nutcracker, The Sleeping Beauty, Coppélia and Cinderella — alongside twentieth-century works and new commissions. Its annual Christmas season of The Nutcracker at the London Coliseum is a fixture of the London arts calendar. The company employs roughly 67 dancers and a full orchestra, English National Ballet Philharmonic.
