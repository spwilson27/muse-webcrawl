Optoelectronics

Optoelectronics (or optronics) is the study and application of electronic devices and systems that find, detect and control light, usually considered a sub-field of photonics. In this context, light often includes invisible forms of radiation such as gamma rays, X-rays, ultraviolet and infrared, in addition to visible light. Optoelectronic devices are electrical-to-optical or optical-to-electrical transducers, or instruments that use such devices in their operation.

Devices

Important optoelectronic devices include light-emitting diodes (LEDs), laser diodes, photodiodes, solar cells, image sensors, and opto-isolators. These devices are typically based on semiconductor materials and on effects such as electroluminescence, the photovoltaic effect, and photoconductivity.

Applications

Optoelectronics underlies optical fibre communications, barcode readers, remote controls, compact-disc and Blu-ray players, digital cameras, displays, and optical computer mice. Solar photovoltaics, used for renewable electricity generation, is one of the largest application areas by market value.
