Global Witness is an international non-governmental organisation (NGO) founded in 1993 that works to expose the links between natural resource exploitation, corruption, conflict and human rights abuses. It is headquartered in London and is known for pioneering undercover investigations and campaigning that helped establish international transparency initiatives in the extractive industries.

History

Global Witness was founded in 1993 by Charmian Gooch, Simon Taylor and Patrick Alley. One of its earliest campaigns documented how timber revenues from Cambodia and illegal logging along the Thai–Cambodian border helped fund the Khmer Rouge insurgency, work that contributed to the closure of the border to the timber trade. The organisation subsequently turned its attention to the role of natural resources in fuelling civil wars in Africa.

Campaigns

The organisation's investigation into "blood diamonds" in Angola, published in the 1998 report A Rough Trade, was instrumental in the creation of the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme, which seeks to prevent conflict diamonds from entering the legitimate market. Global Witness also campaigned on oil and mining revenue transparency and was a founding member of the Publish What You Pay coalition and an early supporter of the Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative (EITI).

Later work has included investigations into anonymous shell companies, money laundering through the international banking system, deforestation and land rights, and the financing of armed groups through the trade in minerals such as coltan, cassiterite and gold from the Democratic Republic of the Congo. In 2015 the organisation began publishing an annual count of environmental and land defenders killed worldwide.

Recognition

Global Witness and its co-founders have received several awards, including the Gleitsman International Activist Award. In 2014, co-founders Charmian Gooch, Simon Taylor and Patrick Alley jointly won the Skoll Award for Social Entrepreneurship, and Gooch delivered a widely viewed TED talk on anonymous companies the same year.
