Rain gutter

A rain gutter, eavestrough, or surface water collection channel is a component of a water discharge system for a building. It is a shallow trough fixed below or along the edge of the roof, designed to collect rainwater and carry it to downpipes (downspouts) that discharge it at ground level, away from the foundations.

Types and materials

Common profiles include half-round, K-style (ogee) and box gutters. Materials include uPVC, galvanised or coated steel, aluminium, copper, cast iron and, historically, timber. Integral or parapet gutters are built into the roof structure itself. Blockages from leaves and debris can cause overflow and damp; guards and periodic cleaning reduce this.
