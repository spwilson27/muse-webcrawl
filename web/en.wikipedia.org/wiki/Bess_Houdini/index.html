Wilhelmina Beatrice "Bess" Houdini (née Rahner; 23 January 1876 – 11 February 1943) was an American stage assistant and the wife of escape artist Harry Houdini.

Life

Bess Rahner was performing with the Floral Sisters song-and-dance act at Coney Island when she met Harry Houdini in 1894; they married within weeks. She became his stage assistant, joining his act and appearing with him for the rest of his career, including in the Metamorphosis trunk illusion, which they performed together in their early years.

After Houdini's death in 1926, Bess held annual séances on Halloween for ten years, hoping to receive a prearranged coded message ("Rosabelle believe") that the couple had agreed would prove genuine contact from beyond the grave. No message was received, and in 1936 she held a widely publicised "final séance" on the roof of the Knickerbocker Hotel in Hollywood, after which she declared she had given up hope of communication. The tradition of Halloween séances for Houdini has nevertheless been continued by others.

Bess Houdini died in 1943 in Needles, California, while travelling home to New York.
