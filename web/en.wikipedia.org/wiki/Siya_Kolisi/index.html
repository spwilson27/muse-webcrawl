Siya Kolisi

Siya Kolisi
Born: 16 June 1991, Gqeberha, South Africa
Position: Flanker
Team: South Africa (Springboks)

Siyamthanda Kolisi (born 16 June 1991) is a South African professional rugby union player who captains the South Africa national team, the Springboks. He plays as a flanker. Kolisi is the first black man to captain the Springboks, and he led the team to victory at the 2019 Rugby World Cup in Japan and again at the 2023 Rugby World Cup in France, becoming one of only two captains to win consecutive World Cup titles.

Early life

Kolisi was born in the township of Zwide in Port Elizabeth (now Gqeberha) and raised by his grandmother. He earned a rugby scholarship to Grey High School after being spotted at a youth tournament, beginning his path to professional rugby with Eastern Province and then Western Province and the Stormers in Cape Town.

Career

Kolisi made his Springbok debut in 2013 against Scotland in Nelspruit, being named man of the match. He became a regular starter and in May 2018 was appointed Springbok captain by coach Rassie Erasmus. At the 2019 World Cup he captained South Africa to a 32–12 victory over England in the final in Yokohama. In 2021 he captained the Springboks to a series victory over the British and Irish Lions. Kolisi led South Africa again at the 2023 World Cup, where the Springboks defeated New Zealand 12–11 in the final in Paris.

At club level he played for the Stormers, the Sharks, and French club Racing 92, before returning to the Sharks in 2024.

Personal life and philanthropy

Kolisi and his wife Rachel founded the Kolisi Foundation, which works to address inequality, food security, and gender-based violence in South African communities. He has been widely recognised as an influential figure beyond sport, appearing on lists of the world's most influential people.
