Fedora Project

The Fedora Project is an independent project to coordinate the development of Fedora Linux, a popular Linux-based operating system, operated by a global community of free and open-source software developers and sponsored primarily by Red Hat (a subsidiary of IBM).

History

The project was founded in 2003 by Warren Togami as an undergraduate project and merged with Red Hat's community efforts, becoming the upstream source for Red Hat Enterprise Linux. The Fedora Council is its top-level leadership body.

Editions and principles

Fedora Linux is released in editions including Workstation, Server, IoT, and numerous "spins" and immutable variants (such as Silverblue). The project is guided by its "Four Foundations": Freedom, Friends, Features, and First — emphasizing free software and rapid innovation.
