Stasi Records Agency

The Stasi Records Agency (German: Bundesbeauftragte für die Stasi-Unterlagen, BStU) was the German federal authority that preserved and administered the archives of the Stasi, the secret police of East Germany, from 1991 until its integration into the Federal Archives in 2021.

History

Created under the Stasi Records Act passed after German reunification, the agency gave citizens the right to view the files kept on them, supported research and journalism, and assisted in vetting public officials. Its first commissioner was Joachim Gauck, later President of Germany, and it was often called the "Gauck Authority".

Holdings

The archive holds roughly 111 kilometres of files, millions of photographs, and sacks of shredded documents painstakingly reconstructed in a long-running project.
