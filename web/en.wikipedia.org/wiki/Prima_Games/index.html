Prima Games is an American publisher of video game strategy guides, founded in 1990 in Roseville, California. For three decades its printed walkthroughs — large, richly illustrated books giving maps, item locations, boss tactics and complete solutions — were a fixture of game shops, sold alongside major releases. The imprint became the largest strategy-guide publisher in North America, producing guides for thousands of titles.

History

Prima began as a general computer-book publisher and found its niche with "Secrets of the Games", a bestselling unofficial guide series in the early 1990s, before moving to officially licensed guides produced with game publishers' cooperation. It was acquired by Random House in 2001 and folded into its information group. In 2013 Prima merged with its main US rival, BradyGames, under the Prima name, effectively uniting the two biggest guide publishers in the market.

Digital transition and revival

The rise of free online walkthroughs, wikis and video platforms eroded print guide sales through the 2010s. In November 2018 DK, by then Prima's parent, announced the imprint would close in 2019, ending its US print guide operation. The brand was instead relaunched as a digital-first business: Prima Games continued as a video game news and guides website under new ownership (GAMURS Group acquired the site in 2022), publishing written walkthroughs, tips and news online, while licensed print guides for selected titles continued to appear occasionally through DK.
