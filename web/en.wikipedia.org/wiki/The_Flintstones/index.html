The Flintstones is an American animated sitcom produced by Hanna-Barbera Productions. The series originally aired on ABC from 1960 to 1966 and was the first animated series to hold a prime-time slot on American television.

Premise

Set in the fictional Stone Age town of Bedrock, the show follows quarry worker Fred Flintstone, his wife Wilma, and their neighbours and best friends Barney and Betty Rubble. Modern suburban life is transposed into a prehistoric world of stone houses, foot-powered cars, and household appliances powered by animals, who often remark, "It's a living." Later seasons added the Flintstones' daughter Pebbles and the Rubbles' adopted son Bamm-Bamm.

History and legacy

modelled in part on The Honeymooners, the series was a major ratings success and launched numerous spin-offs, specials, and feature films, including the 1994 live-action film. Its theme song, "Meet the Flintstones," and Fred's catchphrase "Yabba Dabba Doo!" became enduring parts of popular culture. In 2013, TV Guide ranked it among the greatest television cartoons of all time.
