Liepaja

Liepaja is the unaccented ASCII spelling of Liepāja, a city in western Latvia on the Baltic Sea coast. The name is written "Liepaja" in contexts where the Latvian diacritic ā cannot be reproduced, such as in some international domains, databases, and transliteration systems; historically the city was also known by its German name, Libau.

For the full article on the city — its history as a major ice-free port, the naval district of Karosta, its population, and its music and cultural life — see Liepāja.
