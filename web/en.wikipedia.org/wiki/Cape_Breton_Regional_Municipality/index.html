Cape Breton Regional Municipality

The Cape Breton Regional Municipality (CBRM) is a regional municipality in Nova Scotia, Canada, on Cape Breton Island. Created in 1995 by amalgamating eight municipalities, it includes Sydney, Glace Bay, New Waterford, North Sydney and Sydney Mines.

Province: Nova Scotia, Canada
Established: 1 August 1995
Seat: Sydney

Economy

Historically based on coal mining and steelmaking, the region's economy has shifted toward services, tourism, education and the port of Sydney.

Population

CBRM is the second-largest municipality in Nova Scotia by population, with roughly 90,000-95,000 residents.
