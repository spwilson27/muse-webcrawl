Bronco

Bronco
Type: horse
Origin: western North America
Use: rodeo, ranch work

A bronco, or bronc, is a type of horse used in rodeo bucking events, or more generally an untrained or partially trained horse of the American West. The word derives from the Spanish bronco, meaning "rough" or "rude".

Rodeo broncs

In rodeo, broncs are used in bareback bronc riding and saddle bronc riding, events in which a rider attempts to stay on a bucking horse for eight seconds. Rodeo broncs are bred for athleticism and bucking ability and are valuable stock animals cared for by stock contractors; many are not truly wild but perform when wearing a flank strap.

Other uses

The term has been applied to vehicles and sports teams, notably the Ford Bronco sport utility vehicle and the Denver Broncos of the NFL.
