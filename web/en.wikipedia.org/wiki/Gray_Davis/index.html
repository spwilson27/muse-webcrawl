Gray Davis

Joseph Graham "Gray" Davis Jr. (born December 26, 1942) is an American attorney and former politician who served as the 37th governor of California from 1999 until he was recalled in 2003. A member of the Democratic Party, Davis previously served as chief of staff to Governor Jerry Brown (1975–1981), state assemblyman (1983–1987), state controller (1987–1995) and lieutenant governor (1995–1999).

Governorship and recall

Davis was elected governor in 1998 and re-elected in 2002. His second term was dominated by the California electricity crisis of 2000–2001, a state budget shortfall, and unpopular measures such as an increase in the vehicle license fee. In 2003 he became only the second governor in United States history (after Lynn Frazier of North Dakota in 1921) to be removed by a recall election; voters simultaneously elected Arnold Schwarzenegger as his successor. A decorated Vietnam War veteran, Davis had earned a Bronze Star for his Army service.
