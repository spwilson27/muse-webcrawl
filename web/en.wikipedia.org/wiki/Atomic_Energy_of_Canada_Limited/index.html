Atomic Energy of Canada Limited (AECL) is a Canadian federal Crown corporation responsible for nuclear research and development. Founded in 1952, it developed the CANDU reactor design, a pressurised heavy-water reactor exported to several countries.

History

AECL grew out of Canada's wartime nuclear work at Chalk River Laboratories, site of the NRX and NRU research reactors. CANDU reactors were built across Ontario, Quebec and New Brunswick and exported to Argentina, China, India, Pakistan, Romania and South Korea.

Restructuring

In 2011 AECL's commercial reactor division was sold to SNC-Lavalin (now AtkinsRéalis) as Candu Energy. AECL retained its laboratories, managed under a government-owned, contractor-operated model by Canadian Nuclear Laboratories from 2014, and oversees decommissioning and radioactive waste liabilities.
