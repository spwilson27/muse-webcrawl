Erin Marie Moran Fleischmann (October 18, 1960 – April 22, 2017) was an American actress, best known for playing Joanie Cunningham on the television sitcom Happy Days and its spin-off Joanie Loves Chachi. She played the younger sister of Richie Cunningham in the 1970s Milwaukee-set series, one of the most popular American television programmes of its era.

Career

Moran began acting as a child, appearing in the series Daktari and in commercials before joining Happy Days in 1974 at age thirteen. She remained with the show until its end in 1984, with a break to headline Joanie Loves Chachi (1982–83) opposite Scott Baio. Later work included guest appearances on The Love Boat, Murder, She Wrote and the reality series Celebrity Fit Club, as well as the cult horror film Galaxy of Terror (1981) early in her career.

Later life and death

Moran's later years were marked by financial and personal difficulties widely reported in the press. She died on 22 April 2017 at her home in Corydon, Indiana; the coroner determined the cause to be complications of stage-four squamous cell carcinoma of the throat.
