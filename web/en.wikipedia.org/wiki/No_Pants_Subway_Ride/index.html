No Pants Subway Ride

No Pants Subway Ride
Genre: Annual public prank
Location: Cities worldwide
First held: 2002
Organizer: Improv Everywhere

The No Pants Subway Ride (also called the No Trousers Tube Ride in London) is an annual global event in which participants ride public transit wearing ordinary winter clothing but no trousers or pants. The event is organized by the New York City-based prank collective Improv Everywhere.

History

The first ride took place in 2002 in New York City with seven participants, all male members of Improv Everywhere. The event grew steadily: by 2006 roughly 150 people participated, and police briefly detained and handcuffed several participants before charges were dismissed. In subsequent years the ride spread internationally, with events held in dozens of cities including London, Toronto, Sydney, Mexico City, and Berlin.

Format

Participants gather at a designated meeting point, divide into groups, and board trains at successive stops, removing their trousers once aboard. They are instructed to behave as normally as possible and to keep a straight face; if asked why they are not wearing pants, they typically reply that they "just forgot" them. In winter editions, participants wear coats, hats, scarves, and gloves, making the absence of trousers more conspicuous.
