Square metre

The square metre (international spelling) or square meter (American spelling), symbol m2, is the unit of area in the International System of Units (SI). It is defined as the area of a square with sides measuring exactly one metre.

SI multiples

Multiples and submultiples follow the usual SI prefixes: the square decimetre (dm2), square centimetre (cm2) and square millimetre (mm2) are common for small areas, while the hectare (10,000 m2) and the square kilometre (km2) are standard for land areas.

Usage

The square metre is the standard unit for measuring floor area in real estate, room sizes, construction materials, and land parcels in most of the world. Care is needed with prefixes: a square megametre is a trillion square metres, because the prefix applies to the metre before squaring.
