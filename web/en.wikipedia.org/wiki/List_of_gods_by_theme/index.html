List of gods by theme

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article lists deities from the world's mythologies organized by theme or domain — the sphere of nature or human activity over which a god or goddess presides. Similar portfolios recur across unconnected cultures: sky fathers, earth mothers, storm gods, dying-and-rising gods and tricksters are near-universal figures in comparative mythology.

Sun and moon

  • Sun: Ra and Amun-Ra (Egyptian), Helios and Apollo (Greek), Sol Invictus (Roman), Surya (Hindu), Amaterasu (Japanese — a goddess), Inti (Inca), Tonatiuh (Aztec), Baldr-associated light (Norse), Shamash (Mesopotamian), Lugh (Celtic).
  • Moon: Artemis and Selene (Greek), Luna and Diana (Roman), Khonsu and Thoth (Egyptian), Chandra (Hindu), Tsukuyomi (Japanese), Máni (Norse), Sin/Nanna (Mesopotamian), Ix Chel (Maya).

Sea and water

  • Sea: Poseidon (Greek), Neptune (Roman), Aegir and Ran (Norse), Yam (Canaanite), Ryūjin (Japanese), Mazu (Chinese sea goddess), Olokun and Yemọja (Yoruba), Kanaloa (Hawaiian), Tangaroa (Māori/Polynesian), Tiamat (Mesopotamian primordial salt water), Varuna (Hindu, originally sky then sea).
  • Rivers and rain: Tlaloc (Aztec rain), Chaac (Maya rain), Anuket and Hapi (Nile), Hebo (Yellow River), Mimir (Norse wisdom/waters, also listed under wisdom).

War and victory

  • Ares and Athena (Greek — Athena also wisdom and strategy), Mars and Bellona (Roman), Tyr (Norse), Odin (Norse, also wisdom/death), Kartikeya/Murugan and Durga (Hindu), Sekhmet (Egyptian lioness war goddess), Ishtar/Inanna (Mesopotamian — also love), Morrigan (Celtic war and fate), Huitzilopochtli (Aztec war and sun), Bishamonten (Japanese), Guan Yu (deified Chinese general).

Love, beauty and fertility

  • Aphrodite and Eros (Greek), Venus and Cupid (Roman), Freya and Frigg (Norse), Ishtar/Inanna (Mesopotamian), Isis and Hathor (Egyptian), Lakshmi and Kamadeva (Hindu), Oshun (Yoruba), Xochiquetzal (Aztec), Astarte (Phoenician), Inanna; fertility and agriculture: Demeter and Persephone (Greek), Ceres (Roman), Osiris (Egyptian rebirth/crops), Freyr (Norse), Dagda (Celtic), Pachamama (Andean earth mother), Gaia/Terra (primordial earth), Prithvi (Hindu earth).

Death and the underworld

  • Hades/Pluto and Persephone (Greek), Osiris and Anubis (Egyptian), Hel (Norse), Odin (psychopomp aspects), Yama (Hindu and Buddhist judge of the dead), Yanluo Wang (Chinese), Izanami and Emma-Ō (Japanese), Mictecacihuatl and Mictlantecuhtli (Aztec), Ereshkigal (Mesopotamian queen of the dead), Mot (Canaanite), Baron Samedi (Vodou loa of the dead), Santa Muerte (Mexican folk saint).
