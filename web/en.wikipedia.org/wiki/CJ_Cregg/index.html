C. J. Cregg

Claudia Jean "C. J." Cregg
Series: The West Wing
Portrayed by: Allison Janney
Roles: White House Press Secretary; Chief of Staff

Claudia Jean "C. J." Cregg is a fictional character in the American television drama The West Wing, played by Allison Janney. She serves as White House Press Secretary under President Josiah Bartlet for most of the series, and is promoted to White House Chief of Staff in the sixth season.

Character

Cregg is depicted as a highly capable media strategist from Ohio, previously a Hollywood publicist. Her press briefings — witty, combative and meticulously prepared — were among the show's signature set pieces. Her storylines include a death threat requiring Secret Service protection, a relationship with reporter Danny Concannon, and her handling of a military shuttle leak investigation.

Reception

Janney won four Primetime Emmy Awards for the role, two as supporting actress and two as lead actress after her character's promotion. Cregg is frequently cited as one of the best-written women in television drama.
