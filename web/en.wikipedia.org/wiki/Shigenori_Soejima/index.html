Shigenori Soejima

Shigenori Soejima (副島 成記, born 1974) is a Japanese video game artist and character designer best known for his work with Atlus, where he serves as art director and character designer for the Persona series of role-playing games.

Career

Soejima joined Atlus in the 1990s and initially contributed to titles such as Devil Summoner and early Persona games, working alongside longtime series artist Kazuma Kaneko. He became the lead character designer for Persona 3 (2006), where his cleaner, fashion-influenced character designs and bold use of color helped define the series' modern visual identity. He continued as character designer and art director for Persona 4 and Persona 5, and designed characters for the puzzle game Catherine and its expansion Catherine: Full Body.

Style

Soejima's art is noted for its elegant linework, expressive faces, and strong use of color symbolism—each Persona title under his direction is associated with a signature color, such as blue for Persona 3, yellow for Persona 4, and red for Persona 5. He works as part of Atlus's internal P-Studio team.

Art books collecting his work for the Persona series have been published in Japanese and English editions.
