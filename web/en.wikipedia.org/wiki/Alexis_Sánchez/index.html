Alexis Alejandro Sánchez Sánchez (born 19 December 1988), nicknamed El Niño Maravilla ("The Wonder Child"), is a Chilean professional footballer who plays as a forward. Widely regarded as Chile's greatest-ever player, he is the national team's all-time top goalscorer and most-capped player, and a key member of the "golden generation" that won the Copa América in 2015 and 2016.

Club career

Sánchez began his career with Cobreloa in Chile and, after being signed by Udinese, had loan spells at Colo-Colo and River Plate, winning league titles with both. He starred for Udinese in Serie A before joining Barcelona in 2011, where he won La Liga (2012–13), the Copa del Rey and the FIFA Club World Cup. In 2014 he moved to Arsenal, becoming one of the Premier League's most dynamic attackers and winning two FA Cups. A high-profile transfer to Manchester United in January 2018 proved disappointing, and he revived his career at Inter Milan, winning Serie A in 2020–21, before a season at Marseille and a return to Inter, where he won a second Scudetto in 2023–24. He subsequently returned to Udinese.

International career

Sánchez debuted for Chile in 2006. He played at the 2010 and 2014 World Cups and starred as Chile won back-to-back Copa América titles — on home soil in 2015, and the Copa América Centenario in the United States in 2016, where he was named player of the tournament. With more than 50 international goals, he surpassed Marcelo Salas as Chile's record scorer.
