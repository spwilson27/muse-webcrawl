Daredevil (character)

Daredevil is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Bill Everett, with an unspecified amount of input from Jack Kirby. The character first appeared in Daredevil #1 (April 1964). Daredevil is the alias of Matthew Michael Murdock, a blind lawyer from Hell's Kitchen, New York, whose remaining senses were heightened to superhuman levels by a childhood accident involving radioactive material.

Nicknamed the "Man Without Fear", Daredevil is known for his acrobatic fighting style, his radar sense, his Catholic faith, and his double life as a defence attorney. Frank Miller's run in the early 1980s redefined the character with a noir tone and introduced or popularised figures such as Elektra and the Kingpin as his nemesis.

Publication history

Daredevil debuted in 1964 wearing a mostly yellow costume, soon changed to the now-iconic all-red suit. The series was reinvented by Frank Miller (artist from 1979, writer-artist from 1981), whose storylines "Born Again" (with David Mazzucchelli, 1986) and the graphic novel The Man Without Fear (with John Romita Jr., 1993) are regarded as classics. Later acclaimed runs include those of Brian Michael Bendis, Ed Brubaker, Mark Waid and Chip Zdarsky.

Fictional biography

Matt Murdock was raised by his father, boxer "Battlin' Jack" Murdock, in Hell's Kitchen. Blinded as a boy when he saved a stranger from a truck carrying radioactive waste, he developed a radar-like sense and heightened hearing, smell, touch and taste. After his father was murdered by gangsters, Matt became both a lawyer — founding the firm Nelson & Murdock with his friend Foggy Nelson — and a vigilante. His principal enemies include Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin; the assassin Bullseye; and the ninja clan the Hand. His great love and frequent adversary is Elektra Natchios.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Ben Affleck in the 2003 film Daredevil and by Charlie Cox in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, headlining the Netflix series Daredevil (2015–2018) and the Disney+ revival Daredevil: Born Again (2025).
