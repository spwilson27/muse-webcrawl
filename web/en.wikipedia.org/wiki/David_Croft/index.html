David Croft

David Croft may refer to several people, most notably David John Croft (born 7 September 1970), a British television comedy writer, producer and director, son of the sitcom writer David Croft (1922–2011).

David Croft (1922–2011)

Major David John Croft, OBE (7 September 1922 – 27 September 2011) was an English writer, producer and director best known for co-writing classic BBC sitcoms with Jimmy Perry, including Dad's Army (1968–1977), It Ain't Half Hot Mum and Hi-de-Hi!. With Jeremy Lloyd he later wrote 'Allo 'Allo! and Are You Being Served?. Croft served in the Royal Artillery during the Second World War and drew on his military experience for Dad's Army.

David Croft (born 1970)

The younger David Croft is a television producer and director who worked on shows including Hyperdrive and produced revivals and documentaries related to his father's work.
