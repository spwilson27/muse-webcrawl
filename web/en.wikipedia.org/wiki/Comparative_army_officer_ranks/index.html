Comparative army officer ranks of the world

[Image: Comparative officer rank insignia]

This article is a comparative table of army officer ranks used by land forces around the world. Rank systems are aligned using the NATO rank codes (OF-1 to OF-10) established by the standardisation agreement STANAG 2116, which allows grades in different national systems to be compared even where titles and insignia differ.

Not all countries use every grade: many armies have no OF-10 rank in peacetime, and some systems interpose additional grades such as senior colonel or sub-lieutenant variants. Commonwealth, Francophone, Germanic and post-Soviet systems each preserve distinctive titles reflecting their military traditions, while Asian systems such as those of China, Japan and India combine indigenous titles with NATO-equivalent grades.
