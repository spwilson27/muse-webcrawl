Huntington Bank Field

Huntington Bank Field is an American football stadium located in Cleveland, Ohio, United States. It is the home field of the Cleveland Browns of the National Football League (NFL) and also serves as a venue for concerts, soccer matches, and other events. The stadium has a seating capacity of about 67,431.

History

The stadium opened in 1999 as Cleveland Browns Stadium, built on the site of the former Cleveland Stadium on the lakefront south of Lake Erie. It was known as FirstEnergy Stadium from 2013 to 2023 under a naming rights agreement. In September 2024 it took its current name under a 20-year naming rights deal with Huntington Bank.

Features

The open-air stadium is known for the "Dawg Pound", the bleacher section at the east end occupied by famously devoted Browns fans. Its natural grass playing surface is heated from below to cope with Cleveland winters.
