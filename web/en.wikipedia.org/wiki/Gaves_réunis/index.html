The Gaves réunis (French for "the united Gaves") is a short river in southwestern France, formed by the confluence of the Gave de Pau and the Gave d'Oloron near Peyrehorade, in the department of Landes. It flows for about 9.4 kilometres (5.8 mi) before joining the Adour river.

Course

The streams known as gaves descend from the Pyrenees. The Gave de Pau passes through Lourdes and Pau, while the Gave d'Oloron drains the western Pyrenean valleys. After their union at Peyrehorade, the combined waters form the Gaves réunis, which flow westward to merge with the Adour, which in turn reaches the Atlantic Ocean at the border between Landes and Pyrénées-Atlantiques.
