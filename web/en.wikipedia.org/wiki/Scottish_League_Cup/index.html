The Scottish League Cup is a football knockout competition open to all clubs in the Scottish Professional Football League (SPFL). First held in the 1946–47 season, it is Scotland's second most prestigious cup competition after the Scottish Cup. It has carried various sponsor names, including the Coca-Cola Cup, the Skol Cup and the CIS Insurance Cup, and is currently known as the Premier Sports Cup.

History

The competition grew out of the Southern League Cup played during the Second World War and was formally established in 1946, with Aberdeen winning the first final in 1947. Since 2016–17 the early rounds have been played as a group stage in July, involving SPFL clubs not in European competition plus invited clubs from the Highland and Lowland leagues, followed by knockout rounds; the final is usually held at Hampden Park in the winter.

Records

Rangers are the most successful club in the competition's history, with more than 25 titles, followed by Celtic. Under earlier formats, the winners occasionally qualified for the UEFA Cup; at present the competition carries no European place. Its schedule — concluded by mid-season — distinguishes it from most other major domestic cups.
