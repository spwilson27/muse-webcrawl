The House of the People or Wolesi Jirga (Pashto: ولسي جرګه‎, "People's Assembly") was the lower house of the bicameral National Assembly of Afghanistan, alongside the upper House of Elders (Meshrano Jirga). It was established under the 2004 Constitution of Afghanistan and dissolved in practice after the Taliban takeover of August 2021.

Composition and elections

The Wolesi Jirga had a maximum of 249 members, directly elected by the people through the single non-transferable vote system in multi-member provincial constituencies, for terms of five years. Seats were allocated to provinces in proportion to population. At least 64 seats (roughly two per province) were reserved for women, and ten seats were reserved for the nomadic Kuchi population. Members had to be Afghan citizens at least 25 years of age.

Role

The House of the People was the principal legislative chamber: it initiated and passed laws, approved the national budget, ratified international treaties, and could interpellate ministers and pass votes of no confidence in individual members of the cabinet. Its decisions generally prevailed over those of the House of Elders in the event of disagreement, except on the budget, where special procedures applied.

Elections

Parliamentary elections to the Wolesi Jirga were held in 2005, 2010, and — after long delays — in October 2018. Elections were administered by the Independent Election Commission and were frequently marred by allegations of fraud, low turnout, and insecurity.
