Arianespace SA is a French company founded in 1980 as the world's first commercial space transportation company. It markets and operates launches for the Ariane heavy-lift and Vega light-lift launch vehicles from the Guiana Space Centre in Kourou, French Guiana, on behalf of the European Space Agency and commercial customers.

History

Arianespace was created after the first successful Ariane 1 launch in December 1979. Through the Ariane 4 and Ariane 5 eras it held roughly half the world commercial launch market, carrying large telecommunications satellites to geostationary transfer orbit. From 2011 to 2022 it also operated the Russian-built Soyuz ST from Kourou, an arrangement ended after Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

Current operations

The company's majority shareholder is ArianeGroup, a joint venture of Airbus and Safran. Arianespace introduced the Ariane 6, which made its maiden flight in July 2024, and the Vega C small launcher. Notable missions include the December 2021 launch of the James Webb Space Telescope on an Ariane 5, praised for its precision.
