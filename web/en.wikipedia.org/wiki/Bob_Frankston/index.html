Bob Frankston
Born: 14 June 1949, New York City, US
Known for: Co-creating VisiCalc

Robert M. Frankston (born 14 June 1949) is an American software engineer and businessman who co-created, with Dan Bricklin, VisiCalc, the first spreadsheet program for personal computers, in 1979.

Career

Frankston studied at MIT and worked at Interactive Data Corporation before co-founding Software Arts with Bricklin, where he implemented VisiCalc for the Apple II. He later worked at Lotus Development, where he created Lotus Express, and at Microsoft from 1993 to 1998, where he promoted home networking. He has since written and spoken extensively on telecommunications policy and the end-to-end principle of the Internet.

Honours

Frankston is a Fellow of the ACM and received awards for his contributions to computing. See also Dan Bricklin and Spreadsheet.
