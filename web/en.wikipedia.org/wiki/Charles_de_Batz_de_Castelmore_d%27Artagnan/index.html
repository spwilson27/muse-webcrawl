Charles de Batz de Castelmore d'Artagnan

Bornc. 1611, Lupiac, Gascony, France
Died25 June 1673, Maastricht, Netherlands
ServiceMusketeers of the Guard

Charles de Batz de Castelmore, Count d'Artagnan (c. 1611 – 25 June 1673) was a French soldier who served Louis XIV as captain of the Musketeers of the Guard. He was the historical inspiration for d'Artagnan, the hero of Alexandre Dumas's novel The Three Musketeers (1844).

Life

Born in Gascony, d'Artagnan travelled to Paris in the 1630s and joined the Musketeers. He became a trusted agent of Cardinal Mazarin and later of Louis XIV, carrying out delicate missions including the arrest of Nicolas Fouquet in 1661.

Death

D'Artagnan was killed by a musket ball to the throat during the Siege of Maastricht in the Franco-Dutch War, where he commanded the King's Musketeers.
