Sky Group Limited is a British media and telecommunications conglomerate, a division of Comcast, headquartered in London. It operates pay television, streaming, and broadband services across the United Kingdom, Ireland, Italy, Germany, and Austria, and is Europe's largest media company by subscriber base in its markets.

History

Sky originated with Brian Haynes's Satellite Television Ltd (1981), relaunched as Sky Channel. In 1990, amid heavy losses, Sky Television plc merged with rival British Satellite Broadcasting to form British Sky Broadcasting (BSkyB). Under Sam Chisholm and later executives, BSkyB turned profitable on the strength of exclusive sports rights — above all Premier League football from 1992 — and film channels. Rupert Murdoch's News International held a controlling minority stake for decades.

Expansion and Comcast acquisition

In 2014 BSkyB acquired Sky Italia and a majority of Sky Deutschland, renaming itself Sky plc. After a bidding war between Comcast and 21st Century Fox/Disney, Comcast acquired Sky in October 2018 for about £30 billion.

Services

Sky's offerings include the Sky Q and Sky Glass platforms, the NOW streaming service, Sky Broadband, Sky Mobile, and channels such as Sky Sports, Sky News, Sky Cinema, and Sky Atlantic. It is a major commissioner of original drama and a major rights holder in European sport.
