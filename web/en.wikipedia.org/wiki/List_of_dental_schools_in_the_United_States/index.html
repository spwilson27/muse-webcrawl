List of dental schools in the United States

This is a list of dental schools in the United States accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA) of the American Dental Association. Graduates earn either the Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) or Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree; the two degrees are equivalent in scope of practice. There are roughly 70 accredited dental schools in the United States.
