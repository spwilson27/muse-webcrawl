Jean-Pierre Jeunet

Jean-Pierre Jeunet (born 3 September 1953) is a French film director and screenwriter known for his distinctive, visually inventive style.

Career

Jeunet first gained attention co-directing with Marc Caro the dark fantasies Delicatessen (1991) and The City of Lost Children (1995). He directed Alien Resurrection (1997) in Hollywood before returning to France.

Amélie and later work

His film Amélie (2001), starring Audrey Tautou, became an international success and one of the most beloved French films of its era. Later works include A Very Long Engagement (2004) and Micmacs (2009). See also cinema of France.
