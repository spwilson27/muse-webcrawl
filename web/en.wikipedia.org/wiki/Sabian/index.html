Sabian is a Canadian manufacturer of cymbals, based in Meductic, New Brunswick, and one of the largest cymbal makers in the world. The company was founded in 1981 by Robert Zildjian, a member of the Zildjian family that had produced cymbals for centuries, following a family dispute that split the original Zildjian company.

History

The Zildjian family's cymbal-making tradition traces back to Avedis Zildjian in 17th-century Constantinople. In the late 1970s, a disagreement between the brothers Armand and Robert Zildjian over the family business and its Canadian factory led Robert to leave and establish his own company. He took the Canadian plant at Meductic and named the new firm "Sabian" from the first two letters of his children's names — Sally, Billy and Andy. Sabian inherited the right to make cymbals from the traditional B20 bronze alloy using the family's secret processes.

Products

Sabian's catalogue is organised into series such as AA (vintage bright), AAX (modern bright), HH (Hand Hammered), HHX (dark, modern), Artisan and the entry-level B8 and SBr lines. The company is known for extensive hand-hammering, experimental prototypes offered through its "Vault" programme, and endorsements by prominent drummers across rock, jazz and metal. Along with Zildjian, Paiste and Meinl, Sabian is counted among the "big four" cymbal manufacturers.
