Denel

Founded1992
HeadquartersCenturion, South Africa
IndustryDefence, aerospace
OwnerGovernment of South Africa

Denel SOC Ltd is a South African state-owned aerospace and defence technology conglomerate. It was created in 1992 when the manufacturing subsidiaries of Armscor were spun off. Its products include the Rooivalk attack helicopter, G6 howitzer, missiles, ammunition and unmanned aerial vehicles.

Challenges

Denel suffered severe financial difficulties in the late 2010s, linked in part to state capture-era contracts, requiring government bailouts and restructuring.
