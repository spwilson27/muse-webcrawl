Robert John Downey Jr. (born 4 April 1965) is an American actor and producer. One of the most commercially successful actors of his generation, he is known for his early critical acclaim, a period of career decline due to substance abuse, and a remarkable comeback that made him one of the highest-paid actors in the world.

Early career

The son of filmmaker Robert Downey Sr., Downey made his film debut as a child in his father's film Pound (1970). He gained attention in the 1980s with films such as Weird Science and Less than Zero, and won widespread acclaim — and an Academy Award nomination — for portraying Charlie Chaplin in Chaplin (1992).

Troubles and comeback

From the mid-1990s, Downey's career was derailed by drug addiction, arrests, and imprisonment. After achieving sustained sobriety in the early 2000s, he rebuilt his career with films such as Kiss Kiss Bang Bang, Zodiac, and Tropic Thunder, for which he received a second Oscar nomination. His casting as Tony Stark in Iron Man (2008) launched the Marvel Cinematic Universe; he reprised the role in numerous films culminating in Avengers: Endgame (2019). He also starred as Sherlock Holmes in Guy Ritchie's Sherlock Holmes (2009) and its sequel.

Later work

Downey won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his portrayal of Lewis Strauss in Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer (2023). With his wife Susan Downey he runs the production company Team Downey, and he has pursued environmental initiatives through the FootPrint Coalition.
