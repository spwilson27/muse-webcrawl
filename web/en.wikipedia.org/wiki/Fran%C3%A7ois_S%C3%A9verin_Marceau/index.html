François Séverin Marceau

Born1 March 1769, Chartres, France
Died21 September 1796, Altenkirchen, Germany
RankGeneral of division
BattlesWar in the Vendée, War of the First Coalition

François Séverin Marceau-Desgraviers (1 March 1769 – 21 September 1796) was a French general of the Revolutionary Wars, celebrated for his youth, courage and chivalrous character.

Career

Marceau enlisted at sixteen and rose rapidly during the Revolution, distinguishing himself in the War in the Vendée and in the campaigns on the Rhine. Promoted general of division at twenty-four, he commanded a division in the Army of Sambre-et-Meuse under Jourdan.

Death

He was mortally wounded covering the French retreat at Altenkirchen in September 1796, aged twenty-seven. Both armies honoured him; the Austrians returned his body with military honours, a rare tribute that became part of his legend.
