2026 FIFA World Cup

The 2026 FIFA World Cup will be the 23rd FIFA World Cup, scheduled to take place from 11 June to 19 July 2026. It will be jointly hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico — the first World Cup hosted by three countries — across 16 cities, and will be the first edition to feature 48 teams, expanded from 32. The final is scheduled for MetLife Stadium in New Jersey.

Host selection and format

The "United 2026" bid defeated Morocco at the FIFA Congress in Moscow on 13 June 2018, by 134 votes to 65. The tournament format, confirmed in 2023, comprises twelve groups of four teams, with the top two in each group plus the eight best third-placed teams advancing to a new round of 32 — 104 matches in total, the largest World Cup ever staged. Mexico will become the first country to host World Cup matches at three separate tournaments (1970, 1986, 2026), and the Estadio Azteca the first stadium to host three opening matches.

Venues

Eleven venues are in the United States (including Atlanta, Boston, Dallas, Houston, Kansas City, Los Angeles, Miami, New York/New Jersey, Philadelphia, San Francisco Bay Area and Seattle), three in Mexico (Guadalajara, Mexico City and Monterrey) and two in Canada (Toronto and Vancouver). The final draw is scheduled for December 2025. Argentina enter as defending champions, having won the 2022 tournament in Qatar.
