Sovereign Hill

Sovereign Hill is an open-air museum in Golden Point, a suburb of Ballarat, Victoria, Australia. Opened in 1970, it recreates the first ten years of Ballarat's history after the discovery of gold there in 1851, which sparked the Victorian gold rush.

Attractions

Set on about 25 hectares of a former gold-mining site, the museum's Main Street is lined with some 60 historically recreated buildings: shops, hotels, a theatre, a school, factories and churches, staffed by costumed interpreters demonstrating period trades such as blacksmithing, candle-making and confectionery. Visitors can pan for real gold in the Red Hill Gully creek and take underground tours of quartz-reef mines, including a dramatised recreation of the Welcome Nugget discovery.

Blood on the Southern Cross

From 1992 the site presented "Blood on the Southern Cross", a sound-and-light show telling the story of the 1854 Eureka Rebellion; it was succeeded in 2019 by the immersive evening experience "Aura". The adjoining Gold Museum displays nuggets, coins and artefacts of the goldfields era.
