The Voyager program is an American scientific program employing two robotic probes, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2, launched in 1977 to study the outer Solar System and interstellar space. Their planetary encounters — and the Golden Record each carries — made them icons of exploration; NASA's own mission profiles are recreated in this crawl at science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager.

Grand Tour

The probes exploited a planetary alignment occurring once every 176 years: Voyager 2 visited all four giant planets. Both later crossed the heliopause into interstellar space, Voyager 1 in 2012 and Voyager 2 in 2018, and remain humanity's most distant emissaries, linked conceptually with the newer Europa Clipper and Psyche missions.
