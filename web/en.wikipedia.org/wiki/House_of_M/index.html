House of M

House of M is a 2005 comic book storyline published by Marvel Comics, written by Brian Michael Bendis with art by Olivier Coipel. The eight-issue limited series, with numerous tie-ins, depicts an alternate reality created by the Scarlet Witch (Wanda Maximoff) in which mutants are the dominant class of humanity and her father Magneto rules the world as head of the House of Magnus.

Plot

Following the events of Avengers Disassembled, Wanda Maximoff suffers a breakdown over the loss of her children. When the Avengers and X-Men debate how to deal with her instability, her brother Quicksilver persuades her to reshape reality into a world where everyone receives their heart's desire. In the new reality, mutants are the majority, Homo sapiens are an oppressed minority, and Magneto rules from Genosha. Only Wolverine retains his memories of the original world; with the help of the young mutant Layla Miller, who can restore others' memories, he assembles a resistance to confront Magneto.

Decimation

When the truth emerges that Quicksilver, not Magneto, prompted the change, an enraged Magneto kills his son. In despair, Wanda restores reality but utters the words "No more mutants", de-powering the vast majority of Earth's mutant population — an event known as M-Day or the Decimation. Only a few hundred mutants retained their powers, reshaping the X-Men books for years afterwards.

Legacy

House of M is regarded as one of the defining Marvel crossover events of the 2000s, and its premise influenced later storylines, including the eventual reversal of the Decimation during the Krakoan era of X-Men comics.
