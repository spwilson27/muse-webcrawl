Enterprise Rent-A-Car

Enterprise Rent-A-Car is an American car rental company, the flagship brand of the privately held Enterprise Holdings, which also owns the National Car Rental and Alamo Rent A Car brands. Founded in 1957, Enterprise grew to become the largest car rental company in the world by revenue and fleet size, with thousands of locations — distinguished from its airport-focused rivals by its dense network of neighbourhood branches serving local renters whose own cars are being repaired, as well as replacement rentals arranged through insurance companies.

History

The company was founded in St. Louis, Missouri, by Jack C. Taylor, a former naval aviator who had flown fighters from the aircraft carrier USS Enterprise, from which he took the company name. Taylor began with a small leasing operation and seven rental cars in 1957, concentrating on the local market while Hertz and Avis battled over airports. The strategy of locating branches near where customers live, and of picking the customer up at home or at the repair shop — advertised for decades by the slogan "We'll pick you up" — proved highly successful. In 2007 Enterprise acquired Vanguard Automotive Group, parent of National and Alamo, for a reported sum approaching $3 billion, cementing its lead in the industry. The Taylor family retains ownership; the company is consistently ranked among the largest private companies in the United States.

Operations

Enterprise Holdings operates a fleet of well over a million vehicles and thousands of branches across North America and, increasingly, Europe and elsewhere, through owned operations and franchises. Beyond daily rental, the group runs car-sharing (Enterprise CarShare), vanpooling, used-car sales, truck rental and fleet management services. The company is famous for its management-training programme, which recruits heavily among recent university graduates and promotes branch managers from within; its emphasis on customer-service scores as a driver of promotion has been widely studied in business literature.
