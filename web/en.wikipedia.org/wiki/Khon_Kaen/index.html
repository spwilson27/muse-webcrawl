Khon Kaen

Location: Isan, northeastern Thailand
Province: Khon Kaen Province
Known for: Khon Kaen University, silk, dinosaur fossils

Khon Kaen is one of the four major cities of Isan, Thailand, and the capital of Khon Kaen Province. It is a regional centre of education, government, finance and transport in the northeast, situated on the Khorat Plateau.

Highlights

The city's landmarks include Wat Nong Wang, a nine-storey royal temple overlooking Kaen Nakhon Lake. Khon Kaen University, founded in 1964, was the first university in northeastern Thailand. The region is also known for dinosaur fossils — Phu Wiang National Park yielded early Thai dinosaur discoveries — and for mudmee (ikat) silk weaving.

Geography

Khon Kaen lies about 450 km northeast of Bangkok in the Chi River basin, on the Korat plateau, and is served by Highway 2 (Mittraphap Road) and the northeastern railway line.
