Arnold Schwarzenegger

Arnold Alois Schwarzenegger (born July 30, 1947) is an Austrian-American actor, film producer, businessman, retired professional bodybuilder and politician who served as the 38th governor of California (2003–2011).

Bodybuilding

Schwarzenegger won the Mr. Universe title at age 20 and the Mr. Olympia contest seven times. The documentary Pumping Iron (1977) brought bodybuilding to wider attention.

Acting career

His breakthrough was Conan the Barbarian (1982), followed by The Terminator (1984) directed by James Cameron, and hits including Commando, Predator, Total Recall, Terminator 2: Judgment Day (1991), True Lies and comedies such as Twins and Kindergarten Cop.

Politics

Elected in the 2003 California recall election as a Republican, he was re-elected in 2006, promoting environmental measures including AB 32 climate legislation. He was married to Maria Shriver from 1986 to 2021.
