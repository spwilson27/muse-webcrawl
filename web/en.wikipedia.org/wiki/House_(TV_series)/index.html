House (also called House, M.D.) is an American medical drama television series created by David Shore that aired on Fox from 2004 to 2012. It stars Hugh Laurie as Dr. Gregory House, a misanthropic, painkiller-addicted medical genius who leads a team of diagnosticians at the fictional Princeton–Plainsboro Teaching Hospital in New Jersey.

Premise

Each episode typically follows House's team as they puzzle through a patient's baffling symptoms using differential diagnosis, frequently breaking rules, invading patients' homes, and testing House's maxim that "everybody lies". The character of House was consciously modelled in part on Sherlock Holmes — both share a drug habit, an exceptional observational mind, a single close friend (Wilson/Watson), and even address details (221B).

Reception

House was among the top ten rated shows in the United States for several seasons and was, in 2008, the most watched television program in the world. It won multiple Emmy Awards and two Golden Globes for Laurie. The series concluded with the episode "Everybody Dies" on 21 May 2012.
