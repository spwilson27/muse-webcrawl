Council of State (Cuba)

The Council of State (Spanish: Consejo de Estado) is a body of the National Assembly of People's Power of Cuba that represents the legislature between its sessions and carries out its decisions. Under the 2019 constitution, its president is the head of the legislature.

Functions

The Council of State issues decree-laws, calls sessions of the National Assembly, and supervises other state organs. Before the 2019 constitutional reform, its president served as Cuba's head of state; that role passed to the President of the Republic, while the Council retained legislative functions between assembly sessions.
