Sausage roll

Sausage rolls, cut to show the filling.

A sausage roll is a savoury pastry snack consisting of seasoned sausage meat wrapped in puff pastry and baked. It is a staple of bakeries in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Canada and other Commonwealth countries, sold both freshly baked and as a frozen bake-at-home product.

Composition

A typical sausage roll uses a tube of pork sausage meat, often seasoned with herbs and spices, rolled in a sheet of puff pastry, glazed with egg or milk, and baked until the pastry is crisp and golden. Rolls range from small cocktail-sized pieces to large individual portions. Variants use beef, chicken, or vegetarian and vegan fillings based on soya, cheese, or vegetables.

History and popularity

Meat baked in pastry dates back to the classical world, and the modern British sausage roll took shape in the 19th century, becoming an inexpensive working-class staple. In Britain the bakery chain Greggs has made the sausage roll a cultural icon; its vegan sausage roll, launched in 2019, attracted widespread publicity. In Australia, the sausage roll is, alongside the meat pie, a fixture of bakeries, school canteens and sporting events, often eaten with tomato sauce.
