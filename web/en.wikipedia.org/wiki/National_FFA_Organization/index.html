National FFA Organization
Founded: 1928
Type: Youth organization
Headquarters: Indianapolis, Indiana, United States
Membership: Over 1 million student members

The National FFA Organization is an American nonprofit, student-led youth organization focused on agricultural education. It was founded in 1928 as the Future Farmers of America and adopted its current name in 1988 to reflect the growing diversity of agriculture beyond farming alone.

Overview

FFA members are students enrolled in agricultural education courses at middle and high schools, organized into local chapters, state associations, and the national organization. The organization's distinctive official dress includes a blue corduroy jacket bearing the FFA emblem, which features a cross-section of an ear of corn, a rising sun, a plow, an owl, and an eagle. The FFA motto is "Learning to Do, Doing to Learn, Earning to Live, Living to Serve."

Activities

Members compete in Career and Leadership Development Events, carry out Supervised Agricultural Experience projects, and attend the annual National FFA Convention & Expo, one of the largest student conventions in the United States. In 1950, the United States Congress granted FFA a federal charter.
