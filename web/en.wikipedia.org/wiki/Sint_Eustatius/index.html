Sint Eustatius, known locally as Statia, is a Caribbean island and special municipality (public body) of the Netherlands, lying in the northern Leeward Islands southeast of Saba and northwest of Saint Kitts. The island covers 21 square kilometres and is dominated by the dormant volcano The Quill in the southeast and the hills of the Boven National Park in the northwest, with the capital, Oranjestad, on the sheltered western coast between them.

History

Colonised by the Dutch in 1636, Statia became one of the busiest entrepôts of the eighteenth-century Atlantic world. As a free port nicknamed the "Golden Rock", it traded with all nations, and at its height thousands of ships a year called at Oranjestad; goods, including arms and gunpowder, flowed through its warehouses to the rebellious American colonies. On 16 November 1776, Fort Oranje fired what is celebrated as the "First Salute" — an official gun salute to the American brig Andrew Doria, one of the first foreign acknowledgements of American independence. Britain, enraged by the island's trade, captured and sacked Statia under Admiral Rodney in 1781, looting its warehouses and deporting merchants; the island never regained its commercial prominence. The island changed hands repeatedly between Dutch, British and French powers before settling permanently under Dutch rule in 1816.

Modern status and economy

Following the dissolution of the Netherlands Antilles on 10 October 2010, Sint Eustatius became a special municipality of the Netherlands proper, together with Bonaire and Saba (the BES islands). Its economy centres on government employment, an oil transhipment terminal, diving and eco-tourism, and heritage tourism built on its remarkably preserved colonial ruins. The Quill/Boven National Park protects the lush crater forest of The Quill, and the surrounding marine park is noted for wreck and reef diving. In 2018 the Dutch government temporarily took over the island's administration citing mismanagement, a controversial intervention that led to reforms and the gradual restoration of local democracy.
