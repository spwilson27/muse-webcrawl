Kildare Place Society

The Kildare Place Society, formally the Society for Promoting the Education of the Poor of Ireland, was founded in Dublin in 1811. Based at Kildare Place, it was a pioneering non-denominational educational society that promoted teacher training, published cheap schoolbooks and supported schools open to children of all religious backgrounds.

Activities and decline

The Society ran a model school and training institution in Dublin and assisted hundreds of schools across Ireland. After the establishment of the national school system in 1831, government funding was withdrawn and the Society's influence declined, though its publishing and teacher-training work left a lasting mark on Irish primary education.
