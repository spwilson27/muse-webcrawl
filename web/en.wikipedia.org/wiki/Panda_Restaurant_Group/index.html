Panda Restaurant Group

Panda Restaurant Group, Inc. is an American restaurant company headquartered in Rosemead, California, best known as the parent company of Panda Express, the largest Asian-American fast food chain in the United States. The group also operates the Panda Inn restaurants and Hibachi-San.

History

The company was founded by Andrew Cherng and his father, Ming-Tsai Cherng, who opened the first Panda Inn in Pasadena in 1973. Andrew Cherng and his wife Peggy Cherng launched Panda Express in 1983 as a fast-food adaptation in the Glendale Galleria mall. The chain grew through mall food courts and later freestanding and drive-through locations, surpassing 2,000 stores; its best-known dish is Orange Chicken, developed in 1987.

Business

Unusually for a chain of its size, Panda Express locations are predominantly company-owned rather than franchised. The Cherngs remain owners through the Panda Restaurant Group, and the company is a significant employer and philanthropist, funding the Panda Cares foundation and children's hospital programmes.
