The Azure Kinect DK is a developer kit and spatial computing device developed by Microsoft, announced in February 2019. Unlike its gaming predecessors in the Kinect line (Xbox 360, 2010; Kinect v2, 2013), the Azure Kinect was aimed at enterprise and developer applications in computer vision, robotics, healthcare and logistics, designed to pair with Microsoft Azure AI services.

Hardware

The device integrates a 1-megapixel time-of-flight depth sensor derived from HoloLens 2, a 12-megapixel RGB camera, a seven-microphone array for far-field speech and sound capture, and an inertial measurement unit (accelerometer and gyroscope). Multiple units can be synchronised, and an SDK supported body tracking with an AI model that tracks skeletal joints. The form factor is compact (about 103 × 39 × 126 mm) and connects over USB-C.

Discontinuation

Microsoft ended production of the Azure Kinect DK in 2023, licensing its depth-sensing technology to partners such as Orbbec (Femto series) and Analog Devices, and directed developers toward those partner devices.
