Malice Mizer was a Japanese visual kei rock band active from 1992 to 2001, notable for its elaborate baroque and French Rococo-inspired stage presentation, which combined music with theatrical costume, dance and set design. The band's name derives from the French words malice and misère ("misery"), summed up by guitarist Mana as expressing "nothing but malice and misery."

History

The band was formed in 1992 by guitarists Mana and Közi with bassist Yu~ki, drummer Gaz and vocalist Tetsu. After Gaz's departure, drummer Kami joined in 1993. Following Tetsu's exit in 1994, Gackt became vocalist in 1995, and the band achieved major-label success with the album Merveilles (1998), one of the best-selling releases of the visual kei era.

Gackt left in 1999 to pursue a solo career, and Kami died later that year of a subarachnoid hemorrhage. With vocalist Klaha, the band released Bara no Seidou (2000), its most explicitly gothic and neoclassical work, before announcing an indefinite hiatus at the end of 2001.

Legacy

Mana went on to found the band Moi dix Mois and the Gothic & Lolita fashion label Moi-même-Moitié, becoming a central figure in that fashion movement. Malice Mizer's members have reunited for select concerts, including the Deep Sanctuary shows, where guest vocalists performed Kami-era and later material.
