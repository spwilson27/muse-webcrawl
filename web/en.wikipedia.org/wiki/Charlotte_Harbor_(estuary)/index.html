Charlotte Harbor (estuary)

LocationGulf Coast of Florida, USA
Fed byPeace River, Myakka River, Caloosahatchee River
Area~270 sq mi (700 km²)

Charlotte Harbor is an estuary on the Gulf of Mexico coast of southwestern Florida, formed by the confluence of the Peace and Myakka rivers north of Fort Myers. It is one of the largest estuaries in Florida and is largely preserved through a series of state aquatic preserves.

Geography and ecology

The harbor is separated from the Gulf by barrier islands including Gasparilla Island and Cayo Costa. Its mangrove shorelines and seagrass beds support important fisheries, manatees, and wading birds. The Charlotte Harbor National Estuary Program coordinates protection of its watershed.
