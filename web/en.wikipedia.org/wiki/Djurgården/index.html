Djurgården

Djurgården is an island and recreational area in central Stockholm, Sweden. Once a royal hunting ground — the name means "the game park" — it is today one of the city's most popular leisure destinations, containing museums, gardens, an amusement park, and extensive parkland.

Attractions

The island hosts the Vasa Museum, displaying the 17th-century warship Vasa; the Nordic Museum (Nordiska museet); the open-air museum Skansen, founded in 1891; the Gröna Lund amusement park; the ABBA Museum; and the Rosendal Palace and gardens. Djurgården forms part of Ekoparken, the world's first national city park, established in 1995.

The name Djurgården is also associated with the sports club Djurgårdens IF, founded on the island in 1891.
