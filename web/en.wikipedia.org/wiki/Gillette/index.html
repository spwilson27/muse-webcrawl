Gillette is an American brand of safety razors and other personal care products, owned by Procter & Gamble. Founded in 1901 by King Camp Gillette, the company revolutionised shaving by selling inexpensive razors with thin, disposable blades — the classic "razor and blades" business model — and grew into the dominant global maker of shaving products.

History

King C. Gillette, a travelling salesman, conceived the disposable-blade safety razor in 1895; with engineer William Nickerson he solved the manufacturing problem of cheap, thin steel blades, and the American Safety Razor Company began selling razors in 1903. Supplying razors to American soldiers in the First World War created a mass market. Through the twentieth century Gillette introduced successive systems — the Trac II twin blade (1971), the pivoting Atra (1977), the Sensor (1990), the three-blade Mach3 (1998) and the five-blade Fusion (2006) — each generation marketed on a closer shave.

As part of P&G

Procter & Gamble acquired the Gillette Company in 2005 in a deal worth about $57 billion, bringing in brands such as Braun, Duracell and Oral-B as well. The brand faces competition from subscription rivals such as Dollar Shave Club and Harry's, and from changing grooming habits. Its advertising slogan "The Best a Man Can Get", launched in 1989, was revisited in a widely discussed 2019 campaign addressing masculinity.
