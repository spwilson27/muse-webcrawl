J.C.Staff

J.C.Staff Co., Ltd. is a Japanese animation studio founded in January 1986 by Tomoyuki Miyata, who previously worked at Tatsunoko Production. It is headquartered in Musashino, Tokyo.

History

The studio's first release was the 1987 OVA Yotoden. It grew into one of Japan's most prolific anime studios, producing hundreds of television series, films and OVAs.

Notable works

Well-known J.C.Staff productions include Revolutionary Girl Utena, Azumanga Daioh, Honey and Clover, Toradora!, A Certain Magical Index, A Certain Scientific Railgun, Food Wars!: Shokugeki no Soma, and One-Punch Man (season 2 onwards).
