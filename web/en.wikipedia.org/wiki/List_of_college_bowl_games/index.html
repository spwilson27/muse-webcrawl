List of college bowl games

Bowl games are postseason college football games in the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS). The tradition began with the Rose Bowl in 1902. Today there are more than 40 FBS bowl games, including the "New Year's Six" bowls that rotate as semifinals of the College Football Playoff, culminating in the College Football Playoff National Championship.
