International Science Olympiad

The International Science Olympiads are a group of annual competitions in various fields of science for secondary school students from around the world. They are among the most prestigious academic competitions for school students.

Competitions

The best-known olympiads include the International Mathematical Olympiad (first held in 1959), and those in physics, chemistry, biology, informatics, astronomy, earth science, geography, linguistics and philosophy, as well as the International Junior Science Olympiad for younger students.

Format

Each participating country typically sends a small team selected through national competitions. Contests usually combine theoretical examinations with practical or experimental tasks, and medals are awarded to top-scoring individuals rather than teams.
