Arkham Asylum, formally the Elizabeth Arkham Asylum for the Criminally Insane, is a fictional psychiatric hospital in the DC Comics universe, located in Gotham City. It houses many of Batman's most dangerous adversaries — criminals deemed legally insane — including the Joker, Two-Face, the Riddler, Poison Ivy, Harley Quinn and the Scarecrow. Despite its purpose, its inmates escape with notorious frequency.

History

Arkham Asylum first appeared in Batman #258 (October 1974), created by Dennis O'Neil; Len Wein later developed its history. Its name pays homage to the fictional town of Arkham in the works of H. P. Lovecraft, who set stories around the sinister Miskatonic University. In the comics' backstory, the asylum was founded by Dr. Amadeus Arkham, named for his mother Elizabeth, and Amadeus himself later died an inmate there. The graphic novel Arkham Asylum: A Serious House on Serious Earth (1989) by Grant Morrison and Dave McKean explored this Gothic history.

In other media

Arkham Asylum is the setting of the acclaimed video game Batman: Arkham Asylum (2009) by Rocksteady Studios, which launched the Arkham game series, and appears throughout Batman films, animation (including Batman: The Animated Series), and the television series Gotham.
