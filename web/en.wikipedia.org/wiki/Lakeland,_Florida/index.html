Lakeland, Florida

Lakeland is a city in Polk County, Florida, United States, located between Tampa and Orlando along Interstate 4. The population was about 112,000 at the 2020 census. It is the principal city of the Lakeland–Winter Haven metropolitan area.

Founded in the 1880s after the arrival of the railroad, Lakeland developed around the citrus and phosphate industries. It is named for the many lakes within and around the city, including Lake Parker, Lake Hollingsworth and Lake Morton, the last famous for its swans descended from birds gifted by Queen Elizabeth II in 1957.

Lakeland is home to Florida Southern College, which holds the largest single-site collection of Frank Lloyd Wright architecture, and to Southeastern University. Publix Super Markets, founded in nearby Winter Haven, has its corporate headquarters in Lakeland. The Detroit Tigers hold spring training at Publix Field at Joker Marchant Stadium.
