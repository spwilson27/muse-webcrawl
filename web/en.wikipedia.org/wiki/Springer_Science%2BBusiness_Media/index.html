Springer Science+Business Media

Springer Science+Business Media, commonly known as Springer, was a major German publishing company of scientific, technical, and medical books and journals. Founded in Berlin in 1842 by Julius Springer, it grew into one of the world's largest academic publishers. In 2015 it merged with the publishing operations of Macmillan to form Springer Nature.

History

The firm began as a Berlin bookshop and publishing house and expanded with German science in the late nineteenth century. After the Second World War it rebuilt as an international publisher, opening offices in New York and elsewhere, and pioneered early electronic publishing through its LINK platform.

Publishing programme

Springer published thousands of book titles annually and over a thousand peer-reviewed journals across science, technology, medicine, business, and the humanities and social sciences, including influential series such as the Lecture Notes in Mathematics. Its imprints included Springer-Verlag, BioMed Central, and Apress.
