Penn & Teller

Penn & Teller are an American magic duo: Penn Jillette (born 1955), the tall, talkative partner, and Teller (born Raymond Joseph Teller, 1948), the shorter partner who generally performs in silence. They have performed together since the late 1970s, combining magic with comedy and a distinctive skeptical, irreverent style that often exposes the methods behind tricks while still astonishing audiences.

The duo rose to prominence with their Off-Broadway and Broadway shows in the 1980s and numerous television appearances. They hosted the Showtime series Penn & Teller: Bullshit! (2003–2010), which applied skeptical scrutiny to pseudoscience and popular beliefs, and since 2011 they have presented the competition show Penn & Teller: Fool Us. They have had a long-running headline show at the Rio All-Suite Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas since 2001.

Both are prominent advocates of atheism, libertarianism, and scientific skepticism.
