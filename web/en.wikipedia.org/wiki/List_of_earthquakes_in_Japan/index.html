List of earthquakes in Japan

Japan is situated in a volcanic zone on the Pacific Ring of Fire. Frequent low-intensity earth tremors and occasional volcanic activity are felt throughout the islands. Destructive earthquakes, often resulting in tsunamis, occur several times a century. This is a partial list of earthquakes in Japan.

Four tectonic plates meet beneath Japan, making it one of the most seismically active countries on Earth.

Background

The Japanese islands lie at the convergence of the Pacific Plate, the Philippine Sea Plate, the Eurasian Plate and the North American Plate (Okhotsk Plate). Subduction zones off the Pacific coast — the Japan Trench, the Nankai Trough and the Kuril Trench — generate great megathrust earthquakes, while shallow crustal faults produce destructive local events such as the 1995 Kobe earthquake.
