Bellerophon

Bellerophon or Bellerophontes is a hero of Greek mythology. He was the greatest hero and slayer of monsters, alongside Cadmus and Perseus, before the days of Heracles, and is best known for slaying the Chimera and taming the winged horse Pegasus.

Bellerophon
Abode: Corinth, Lycia
Symbol: Pegasus
Parents: Glaucus and Eurynome (or Poseidon)

Mythology

Exiled after accidentally killing a man, Bellerophon was sent by Proetus to King Iobates of Lycia bearing a sealed letter ordering his own death. Iobates instead set him impossible tasks: killing the fire-breathing Chimera, fighting the Solymi and the Amazons, all of which he accomplished with Pegasus's help.

Fall

In his pride, Bellerophon attempted to fly Pegasus to Mount Olympus. Zeus sent a gadfly that stung Pegasus, causing Bellerophon to fall to earth, where he wandered blind and lame until his death.

Legacy

His story appears in Homer's Iliad. The Bellerophon story is a classic example of hubris, and his letter is an early literary reference to writing.
