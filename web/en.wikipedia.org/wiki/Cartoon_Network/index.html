Cartoon Network (often abbreviated as CN) is an American cable television channel owned by Warner Bros. Discovery. It is a part of The Cartoon Network, Inc., a division that also has the broadcasting and production activities of Boomerang, Cartoonito, Adult Swim and Pibby under its purview. Launched on October 1, 1992, the channel primarily broadcasts animated television series, mostly children's programming, ranging from action to animated comedy.

History

Cartoon Network was conceived after Turner Broadcasting System acquired the Hanna-Barbera animation studio and its library in 1991, along with the MGM cartoon library it already owned. The channel launched in 1992 as the first 24-hour single-genre cable channel devoted entirely to animation, initially drawing its programming from the vast Warner Bros., MGM, and Hanna-Barbera cartoon libraries, with shows such as Looney Tunes, Tom and Jerry, and The Flintstones.

Original programming

In the mid-1990s, under the "What a Cartoon!" showcase developed by Fred Seibert, Cartoon Network began producing its own original series. These "Cartoon Cartoons" included Dexter's Laboratory (1996), Johnny Bravo (1997), Cow and Chicken (1997), The Powerpuff Girls (1998), Ed, Edd n Eddy (1999), and Courage the Cowardly Dog (1999). Later successful originals have included The Grim Adventures of Billy & Mandy, Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends, Ben 10, Adventure Time (2010), Regular Show (2010), The Amazing World of Gumball (2011), Steven Universe (2013), and We Bare Bears (2015).

Adult Swim and other blocks

In 2001, Cartoon Network launched Adult Swim, a late-night programming block aimed at adult audiences, which became treated as a separate channel for ratings purposes. A companion channel, Boomerang, launched in 2000 to showcase classic cartoons. Cartoonito, a preschool block, was introduced in 2021.

International

Cartoon Network operates numerous international and regional feeds around the world, many producing local original programming. Its studios arm, Cartoon Network Studios in Burbank, California, produces much of the network's original content.
