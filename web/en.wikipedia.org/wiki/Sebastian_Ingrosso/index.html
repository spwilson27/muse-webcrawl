Sebastian Carmine Ingrosso (born 20 April 1983) is a Swedish DJ, record producer, and founder of the label Refune Music. He is best known as a member of the supergroup Swedish House Mafia, alongside Axwell and Steve Angello, and as one half of the duo Axwell Λ Ingrosso.

Career

Ingrosso grew up in Stockholm and is of Italian descent. He began releasing house music in the early 2000s and founded Refune Music in 2003. His early productions included collaborations with Steve Angello and Axwell; his remix work and tracks such as "Laktos" and "Kidsos" established him in the European club scene. In 2009 he co-produced the hit "Leave the World Behind" with Axwell, Angello, and Laidback Luke featuring Deborah Cox.

Swedish House Mafia

Formed in 2008, Swedish House Mafia became one of electronic dance music's biggest live acts, scoring international hits with "Don't You Worry Child," "Save the World," "Greyhound," and "One (Your Name)." The group announced a farewell tour in 2012–2013, closing with the One Last Tour, before reuniting with a surprise performance at Ultra Music Festival in 2018 and releasing the album Paradise Again in 2022.

Axwell Λ Ingrosso

Following the group's hiatus, Ingrosso and Axwell performed as the duo Axwell Λ Ingrosso, releasing singles including "Something New," "On My Way," "Sun Is Shining," and "More Than You Know," and the album More Than You Know (2017).

Personal life

Ingrosso is a cousin of singer Benjamin Ingrosso. He has been ranked multiple times in DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs poll, both solo and with Swedish House Mafia.
