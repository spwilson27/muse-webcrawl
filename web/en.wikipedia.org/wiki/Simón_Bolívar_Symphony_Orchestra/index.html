The Simón Bolívar Symphony Orchestra of Venezuela (Spanish: Orquesta Sinfónica Simón Bolívar de Venezuela) is a Venezuelan symphony orchestra based in Caracas. It is the flagship ensemble of El Sistema, Venezuela's publicly financed music education programme founded by José Antonio Abreu in 1975; the orchestra itself was founded in 1978.

History and reputation

The orchestra is composed largely of musicians who came through El Sistema's network of community music schools (núcleos) and youth orchestras. Under its founding conductor Eduardo Marturet and, most prominently, Gustavo Dudamel — who became music director in 1999 at the age of eighteen — the orchestra gained international fame for the energy and technical brilliance of its performances, particularly of Mahler and Latin American repertoire. It has toured worldwide, performed at the BBC Proms (its 2007 appearance was a sensation), and recorded for Deutsche Grammophon.
