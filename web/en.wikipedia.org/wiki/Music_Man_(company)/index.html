Music Man is an American guitar and bass guitar manufacturer, a division of the Ernie Ball corporation. It was founded in 1974 by Forrest White and Tom Walker, with Leo Fender as a silent partner, and became known for instruments such as the StingRay bass.

History

The company's first products were amplifiers, followed in 1976 by guitars and the StingRay bass, one of the first production instruments with active electronics. After disputes over quality and finances, Leo Fender left to focus on G&L. Ernie Ball acquired Music Man in 1984.

Instruments

Notable models include the StingRay and Sterling basses, and the Silhouette, Axis, and John Petrucci signature guitars. Artists associated with the brand include Albert Lee, Steve Morse, and Steve Lukather.
