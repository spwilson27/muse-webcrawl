Heat Wave (comics)

Heat Wave (Mick Rory) is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly as an enemy of the Flash and a member of the Rogues. Created by writer John Broome and artist Carmine Infantino, the character first appeared in The Flash #140 in November 1963.

Fictional character biography

Mick Rory grew up obsessed with fire — and, paradoxically, with an intense fear and fascination that grew from a childhood trauma: after playing with matches and setting his family's house ablaze, he became fixated on flames while also developing a phobia of cold. As an adult working in a meatpacking plant, he designed a protective asbestos-lined suit and a handheld flamethrower and turned to crime in Central City as Heat Wave, clashing repeatedly with the Flash (Barry Allen).

Heat Wave became a core member of the Rogues, the loosely organised fraternity of Flash villains led informally by Captain Cold, whose freeze gun made him both Rory's partner and rival. Unlike many villains, Rory has periodically attempted reform, including a period of work with the FBI and as a reluctant antihero, but his pyromania repeatedly pulls him back to crime.

Powers and abilities

Rory has no superhuman powers. His hand flamethrower can project intense streams of flame able to melt the Flash's boots to the ground and counter Captain Cold's absolute-zero blasts. His suit protects him from heat and flame. Later incarnations have occasionally given him metahuman fire powers, but the classic version relies entirely on his equipment and expertise.

In other media

The character reached a wide audience through The CW's Arrowverse television series, where Dominic Purcell played Mick Rory / Heat Wave, first as a villain in The Flash (2014) and then as a regular, increasingly comic and heroic member of the time-travelling team in Legends of Tomorrow (2016–2022), including storylines revealing him as the secret science-fiction author "Rebecca Silver". Heat Wave has also appeared in animated series such as Justice League Unlimited and Batman: The Brave and the Bold.
