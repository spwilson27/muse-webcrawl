Maala (municipality)

Maala is a rural locality in the South Ostrobothnia region of western Finland, historically associated with the parish and municipal organisation of the Malax (Maalahti) area near the Gulf of Bothnia coast.

Geography

The area lies on the flat Ostrobothnian coastal plain, characterised by farmland, post-glacial land uplift, and proximity to the Kvarken archipelago, a UNESCO World Heritage site shared with Sweden.

History

Swedish-speaking settlement along this coast dates to the medieval period. Small coastal municipalities in the region were consolidated in the Finnish municipal reforms of the 1970s, when many tiny communes merged into larger units such as Malax.
