Development of a Bottle in Space

Development of a Bottle in Space (Italian: Sviluppo di una bottiglia nello spazio) is a 1912 bronze sculpture by the Italian artist Umberto Boccioni, a leading figure of the Futurist movement. The work depicts a bottle rendered as an unfolding sequence of planes and volumes, dissolving the boundary between the object and the space around it.

Boccioni created the sculpture as part of his effort, set out in his 1912 Technical Manifesto of Futurist Sculpture, to break with traditional statuary and express dynamism, simultaneity, and the interpenetration of object and environment. Along with Unique Forms of Continuity in Space (1913), it is among his most celebrated works.

Casts of the sculpture are held by major museums, including the Museum of Modern Art in New York and the Metropolitan Museum of Art.
