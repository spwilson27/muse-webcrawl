Kelvin Kiptum

Placeholder image: Kelvin Kiptum during a marathon.

Kelvin Kiptum Cheruiyot (2 December 1999 – 11 February 2024) was a Kenyan long-distance runner and the former marathon world record holder. In only his third competitive marathon, he set a world record of 2:00:35 at the 2023 Chicago Marathon, becoming the first person to run a marathon under 2 hours and 1 minute in a record-eligible race.

Career

Kiptum grew up in Chepsamo village in Elgeyo-Marakwet County and began running as a teenager, herding cattle and training without a formal coach for much of his early career. He made his marathon debut in Valencia in December 2022, winning in 2:01:53 — then the fastest marathon debut in history. In April 2023 he won the London Marathon in 2:01:25, and in October 2023 he broke Eliud Kipchoge's world record in Chicago with 2:00:35.

Death

Kiptum died on 11 February 2024, aged 24, in a road traffic accident near Kaptagat, Kenya, in which his coach Gervais Hakizimana also died. He was buried in his home village of Chepkorio, and his death prompted tributes from across the athletics world.

Records

Kiptum ran three of the fastest marathons in history at the time of his death and remains the only athlete to have run three marathons under 2:02 within such a short span.
