Rocketman is a 2019 biographical musical fantasy drama film based on the life and music of British musician Elton John. Directed by Dexter Fletcher and written by Lee Hall, it stars Taron Egerton as John, with Jamie Bell as his longtime lyricist and friend Bernie Taupin, Richard Madden as his former manager and lover John Reid, and Bryce Dallas Howard as his mother Sheila Eileen. The film is named after John's 1972 song "Rocket Man".

The film follows John from his early days as a prodigy at the Royal Academy of Music through his musical partnership with Taupin, and depicts his struggles with depression, substance abuse, and acceptance of his sexual orientation. Egerton performed the songs himself.

Plot

The film opens with John, dressed in an elaborate orange devil costume, entering a rehabilitation session and recounting his life in flashback. Born Reginald Dwight in Pinner, Middlesex, he is raised by his emotionally distant mother and his strict father, a Royal Air Force officer. A child prodigy at the piano, he wins a scholarship to the Royal Academy of Music.

As a young session musician, Reggie joins the band Bluesology and answers an advertisement that pairs him with lyricist Bernie Taupin. Taking the stage name Elton John, he is signed to DJM Records and travels to Los Angeles, where a triumphant debut at the Troubadour in 1970 launches his American career. Fame brings a relationship with manager John Reid, alcohol and drug addiction, and a suicide attempt. Estranged from his parents and trapped in a lavish, lonely lifestyle, John eventually enters rehab, confronts his demons, and begins recovery, reuniting with Taupin.

Production

Development began in the 2000s, with John and his husband David Furnish pursuing a film that would be a musical fantasy rather than a conventional biopic. After Tom Hardy was attached at one stage, Egerton was cast following Fletcher's involvement. Principal photography took place in 2018 at Shepperton Studios and around London.

Music

The soundtrack album, Rocketman: Music from the Motion Picture, features Egerton performing John's classic songs, rearranged by Giles Martin. The original song "(I'm Gonna) Love Me Again", written by John and Taupin and performed by John and Egerton, won the Academy Award for Best Original Song and the Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song.

Release and reception

The film premiered at the Cannes Film Festival on 16 May 2019 and was released in the United Kingdom on 22 May 2019 and in the United States on 31 May 2019. It grossed $195 million worldwide against a $40 million budget. Critics praised Egerton's performance and the musical sequences; Egerton won the Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy. The film received some criticism for edits and censorship in certain international markets, including Russia, Egypt, and Samoa.
