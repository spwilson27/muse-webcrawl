Avatar (franchise)

Avatar

Created by: James Cameron
First film: Avatar (2009)
Setting: Pandora

Avatar is a media franchise created by James Cameron, beginning with the 2009 film Avatar, which became the highest-grossing film of all time. The franchise is set on Pandora, a lush moon inhabited by the Na'vi, and follows conflicts between human colonists and the indigenous population.

Films

The first film (2009) introduced Jake Sully, a paraplegic Marine who operates a Na'vi avatar body. The sequel Avatar: The Way of Water (2022) also grossed over two billion dollars worldwide. Further sequels, including Avatar: Fire and Ash, have been planned and produced.

Other media

The franchise includes the theme-park land Pandora – The World of Avatar at Disney's Animal Kingdom, video games such as Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, novels and comics.
