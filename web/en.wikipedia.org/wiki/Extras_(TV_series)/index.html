Extras is a British sitcom created, written, and directed by Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant, broadcast on BBC Two in the UK and HBO in the US from 2005 to 2007. Following their success with The Office, the series stars Gervais as Andy Millman, a struggling background actor ("extra") desperate for speaking lines and fame, and Ashley Jensen as his friend and fellow extra Maggie Jacobs.

Premise and episodes

Each episode is set on the production of a (fictionalised) real film or show and features a celebrity guest star playing a distorted, usually vain or unhinged version of themselves — among them Ben Stiller, Kate Winslet, Samuel L. Jackson, Patrick Stewart, Orlando Bloom, Daniel Radcliffe, David Bowie, Chris Martin, and George Michael. Stephen Merchant also appears as Andy's hopeless agent Darren Lamb, alongside Shaun Williamson ("Barry from EastEnders") as himself. In the second series Andy's script When the Whistle Blows becomes a hit broad sitcom — much to his horror — and the 2007 Christmas special concludes his arc as he briefly tastes celebrity on a reality show and rejects it.

Reception

The series won a Rose d'Or, a Golden Globe for Gervais, BAFTA awards, and an Emmy for Gervais's acting; the finale special also won critical praise for its satire of fame and reality television.
