Barbara Joan "Barbra" Streisand (born April 24, 1942) is an American singer, actress, and filmmaker. With a career spanning over six decades, she has achieved success in multiple fields of entertainment and is among the few performers who have won an Emmy, a Grammy, an Oscar, and a Tony Award (EGOT).

Early life

Streisand was born in Brooklyn, New York City, to Diana Ida (née Rosen) and Emanuel Streisand, both of Jewish descent. Her father, a high school teacher, died when she was fifteen months old. She attended Erasmus Hall High School in Brooklyn, where she sang in the choir alongside classmate Neil Diamond. As a teenager she took acting classes and began singing in nightclubs, winning a talent contest at the Lion, a gay nightclub in Greenwich Village, in 1960.

Career

Streisand made her Broadway debut in the musical I Can Get It for You Wholesale (1962) and achieved stardom originating the role of Fanny Brice in Funny Girl (1964), which she reprised in the 1968 film adaptation, winning the Academy Award for Best Actress. Her film career includes Hello, Dolly! (1969), The Way We Were (1973) with Robert Redford, and A Star Is Born (1976), whose theme song "Evergreen" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, making her the first woman to be honored as a composer.

As a recording artist, Streisand is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with more than 150 million records sold worldwide. She is the only recording artist to have a number-one album in each of the last six decades, a feat achieved with the release of Partners (2014). Her albums include The Barbra Streisand Album (1963), Guilty (1980) with Barry Gibb, and the live recording One Voice (1987).

Filmmaking

Streisand directed and starred in Yentl (1983), becoming the first woman to win the Golden Globe Award for Best Director. She also directed The Prince of Tides (1991), nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture, and The Mirror Has Two Faces (1996).

Legacy and activism

A lifelong supporter of the Democratic Party, Streisand has been an outspoken advocate for civil rights, women's rights, and environmental causes. Through the Streisand Foundation she has supported numerous charitable organizations. In 2015 she was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom by President Barack Obama. Her memoir, My Name Is Barbra, was published in 2023.
