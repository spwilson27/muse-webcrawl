Miguel Mario Díaz-Canel Bermúdez (born 20 April 1960) is a Cuban politician and engineer who has served as President of Cuba since 2019 and as the country's head of state since 2018. He is the First Secretary of the Communist Party of Cuba, succeeding Raúl Castro in 2021.

Early career

Trained as an electronics engineer, Díaz-Canel rose through the Communist Party in the provinces of Villa Clara and Holguín, gaining a reputation as a competent administrator. He became Minister of Higher Education in 2009 and Vice President of the Council of Ministers in 2012.

Leadership

Elected President of the Council of State in April 2018 — the first person outside the Castro family to lead Cuba since 1959 — he became President of the Republic under the new 2019 constitution. His tenure has been marked by a severe economic crisis, currency reform, the nationwide protests of July 2021, mass emigration, and continuing U.S. sanctions.

Political position

Díaz-Canel presents himself as a continuity leader committed to socialism while cautiously expanding limited private enterprise and internet access; he has supported social changes including the 2022 Family Code legalizing same-sex marriage.
