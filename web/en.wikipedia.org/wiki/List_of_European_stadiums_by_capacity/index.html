List of European stadiums by capacity

This is a list of European stadiums by capacity, covering sports stadiums in Europe with a capacity of 25,000 or more. The largest stadiums in Europe are mostly used for association football, with some also used for rugby union, rugby league, cricket, athletics and American football.
