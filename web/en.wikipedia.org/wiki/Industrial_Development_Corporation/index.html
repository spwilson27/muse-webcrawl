Industrial Development Corporation (IDC)

Founded: 1940

Headquarters: Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa

Type: State-owned development finance institution

The Industrial Development Corporation (IDC) is a South African state-owned development finance institution established in 1940 to promote economic growth and industrial development in South Africa.

Activities

The IDC finances industrial projects across sectors including mining and beneficiation, manufacturing, agro-processing, energy, and tourism, often taking equity stakes alongside loans. It has played a role in founding major enterprises such as Sasol and Foskor.

Role

As a development finance institution, the IDC funds projects considered too risky or long-term for commercial lenders, with a mandate that includes job creation, regional development, and support for black industrialists.
