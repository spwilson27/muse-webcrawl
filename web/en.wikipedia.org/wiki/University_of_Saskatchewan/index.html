The University of Saskatchewan (USask) is a public research university in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada, founded in 1907. Its campus, on the east bank of the South Saskatchewan River, is noted for its Collegiate Gothic stone architecture and is one of the largest university campuses in Canada.

Research

USask is a member of the U15 group and hosts unique national facilities, including the Canadian Light Source synchrotron and VIDO, the Vaccine and Infectious Disease Organization. It is a leader in agricultural research, water security, and Indigenous studies, and one of few Canadian universities offering both veterinary medicine and agriculture colleges.

Community

The university enrols roughly 25,000 students. Its varsity teams, the Huskies, have won national championships in football, hockey, and other sports.
