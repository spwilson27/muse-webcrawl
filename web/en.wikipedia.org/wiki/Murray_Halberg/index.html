Murray Halberg

Sir Murray Gordon Halberg (7 July 1933 – 30 November 2022) was a New Zealand distance runner who won the gold medal in the 5000 metres at the 1960 Rome Olympic Games.

Halberg overcame a serious rugby injury that left his left arm withered, turning to running under coach Arthur Lydiard. He won gold in the three miles at the 1958 and 1962 Commonwealth Games and set world records, including becoming one of the first runners to break four minutes for the mile's era records for two miles.

Legacy

In 1963 he founded the Halberg Disability Sport Foundation, supporting disabled children in sport. The Halberg Awards, New Zealand's premier sporting awards, are named in his honour. He was appointed a Member of the Order of the British Empire and later knighted, and in 2008 became a member of the Order of New Zealand.
