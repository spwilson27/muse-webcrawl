Natanz

Natanz (Persian: نطنز) is a city in the Central District of Natanz County, Isfahan province, Iran, serving as capital of both the county and the district. Located about 70 kilometres southeast of Kashan in the Karkas Mountains, it is known for its pleasant climate, fruit gardens, and historic monuments including the Jameh Mosque complex and the tomb of the Sufi Sheikh Abd al-Samad.

Nuclear facility

Internationally, Natanz is best known for the Natanz Nuclear Facility, Iran's principal uranium enrichment complex, located roughly 30 kilometres from the city. The site contains the underground Fuel Enrichment Plant, housing thousands of gas centrifuges, and an above-ground Pilot Fuel Enrichment Plant. Its existence was revealed in 2002, becoming a central issue in the international dispute over Iran's nuclear program.

The facility was the target of the Stuxnet computer worm discovered in 2010, widely attributed to a joint US–Israeli operation, and of subsequent sabotage incidents in 2020 and 2021 that Iran blamed on Israel. The site was also struck during the June 2025 Israeli–American attacks on Iranian nuclear facilities.
