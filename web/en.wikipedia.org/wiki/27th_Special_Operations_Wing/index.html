27th Special Operations Wing

The 27th Special Operations Wing (27 SOW) is a wing of the United States Air Force assigned to Air Force Special Operations Command (AFSOC). It is headquartered at Cannon Air Force Base, New Mexico, and is one of the Air Force's principal special operations wings, tasked with infiltration, exfiltration, resupply, precision fire support, and specialized refueling for special operations forces.

History

The wing traces its lineage to the 27th Bombardment Group of the Second World War, whose A-24 Banshee dive bombers fought in the Philippines and New Guinea. After the war the unit flew fighters and fighter-bombers — including the F-84, F-100, F-111, and F-16 — as the 27th Fighter Wing at Cannon AFB. On October 1, 2007, the wing was redesignated the 27th Special Operations Wing as Cannon transferred from Air Combat Command to AFSOC.

Aircraft and mission

The wing operates the AC-130W Stinger II gunship, the MC-130J Commando II, the CV-22 Osprey, the MQ-9 Reaper remotely piloted aircraft, and other special operations platforms through its subordinate groups and squadrons.
