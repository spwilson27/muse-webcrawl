Śnieżka (Czech: Sněžka; German: Schneekoppe) is a mountain on the border between Poland and the Czech Republic. At 1,603 metres it is the highest point of the Czech Republic and of the Krkonoše mountain range.

Features

The summit, a distinctive rocky pyramid, is crowned by the Polish mountain hut and meteorological observatory — a striking disc-shaped building completed in 1974 — and the Baroque Chapel of St. Lawrence, built in the 17th century. A chairlift from Karpacz serves the lower slopes.

Climate and nature

Śnieżka is known for fierce weather, with fog on most days of the year and very high winds. The mountain lies within Karkonosze National Park in Poland and Krkonoše National Park in the Czech Republic, protecting alpine tundra ecosystems rare in central Europe.
