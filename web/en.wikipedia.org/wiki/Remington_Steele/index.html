Remington Steele

Remington Steele is an American television series co-created by Robert Butler and Michael Gleason. The series, starring Stephanie Zimbalist and Pierce Brosnan, was broadcast on NBC from October 1, 1982, to February 17, 1987, running for five seasons and 94 episodes.

Premise

Private investigator Laura Holt (Zimbalist) opens her own detective agency but finds clients reluctant to hire a woman, so she invents a fictitious male boss, "Remington Steele". A charming former thief and con man (Brosnan), who adopts the Steele identity after becoming obsessed with the agency's sign, turns up and assumes the role in public, while Holt does the actual detective work. The show mixed mystery-of-the-week plots with romantic comedy and screwball banter between the leads.

Cast and production

Doris Roberts played agency secretary Mildred Krebs from the second season. The series was produced by MTM Enterprises. Pierce Brosnan's film commitments briefly threatened the show: NBC cancelled it after the fourth season, then reversed the decision, complicating Brosnan's assumption of the James Bond role, which consequently went to Timothy Dalton.

Legacy

The series is credited with helping to popularise the blend of romance and detective procedural later seen in shows such as Moonlighting and Castle, and it made Brosnan a star in the United States a decade before he played Bond.
