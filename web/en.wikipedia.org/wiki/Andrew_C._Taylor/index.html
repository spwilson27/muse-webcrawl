Andrew C. Taylor

Andrew C. Taylor is an American businessman best known as the chairman of Enterprise Holdings, the parent company of the Enterprise Rent-A-Car, National Car Rental and Alamo Rent a Car brands. He is the son of company founder Jack C. Taylor and led the business through a period of major expansion, including the 2007 acquisition of Vanguard Automotive Group, which brought the National and Alamo brands under the Enterprise umbrella.

Early life and education

Taylor grew up in St. Louis, Missouri, where his father founded Executive Leasing Company in 1957, the business that later became Enterprise Rent-A-Car. He attended the University of Denver, graduating with a degree in business administration. After college he joined the family company at the entry level, working his way up through the rental operations in the manner his father required of family members.

Career

Taylor became president of Enterprise in the 1990s and later chief executive officer, succeeding his father as the company's leader. Under his tenure, Enterprise grew from a regional rental firm into the largest car rental company in North America by fleet size and revenue, expanding its neighborhood rental model, truck rental, car sales and fleet management lines. The 2007 purchase of Vanguard, owner of National Car Rental and Alamo Rent A Car, made the combined company the largest in the industry. In 2013 he became executive chairman of the newly reorganized parent company, Enterprise Holdings, and his daughter Chrissy Taylor later succeeded him in senior leadership roles.

Philanthropy and civic work

The Taylor family is among St. Louis's most prominent philanthropic families. Andrew Taylor has been involved with institutions including the Missouri Botanical Garden and Washington University in St. Louis, and the family's foundation has supported education, the arts and environmental causes. Washington University's Taylor Family Center for Student Success is named in the family's honor.
