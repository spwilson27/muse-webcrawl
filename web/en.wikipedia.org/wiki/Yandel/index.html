Llandel Veguilla Malavé (born 14 January 1977), better known by his stage name Yandel, is a Puerto Rican singer and songwriter. He rose to fame as one half of the reggaeton duo Wisin & Yandel, one of the genre's best-selling acts.

Career

Wisin & Yandel emerged in the late 1990s and achieved international success in the 2000s with albums such as Pa'l Mundo (2005), Los Extraterrestres (2007), and La Revolución (2009), winning a Grammy Award and multiple Latin Grammy Awards. Yandel launched a solo career with De Líder a Leyenda (2013) and Dangerous (2015), which topped the Billboard Top Latin Albums chart; songs like "Encantadora" and "Nunca Me Olvides" were major hits. The duo reunited for Los Campeones del Pueblo (2018) and the farewell album La Última Misión (2022).
