Xbox Live is an online multiplayer gaming and digital media delivery service created and operated by Microsoft for the Xbox family of game consoles and Windows PCs. Launched in November 2002 for the original Xbox, it pioneered unified online play on consoles, with features such as a single user identity (the Gamertag), friends lists, voice chat and matchmaking.

History

Xbox Live debuted a year after the original Xbox console, requiring a broadband connection — a distinctive requirement at a time when many users still had dial-up. The service grew rapidly with the Xbox 360 in 2005, adding the Xbox Live Marketplace for downloadable games, demos and video, the Gamerscore achievement system, and the free Silver and paid Gold membership tiers. Online multiplayer generally required a Gold subscription.

The service passed through several evolutions on Xbox One and Xbox Series X/S, expanding into apps, streaming video and cross-platform play. In 2021 Microsoft rebranded the underlying service as the Xbox network, while the "Xbox Live Gold" subscription name persisted until 2023, when it was replaced by Xbox Game Pass Core.

Features

  • Online multiplayer gaming and matchmaking
  • Gamertag profiles, achievements and Gamerscore
  • Friends lists, messaging, parties and voice chat
  • Digital storefront for games, downloadable content and media
  • Cloud saves and cross-device synchronisation

Availability

At its peak the service reported around 100 million monthly active users, counting players across consoles, PC and mobile cloud streaming.
