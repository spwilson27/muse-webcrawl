Jujamcyn Theaters

Jujamcyn Theaters is an American theatrical production and theatre ownership company, one of the three major landlords of Broadway theatres in New York City alongside the Shubert Organization and the Nederlander Organization. The company owns five Broadway houses: the St. James, Al Hirschfeld, August Wilson, Eugene O'Neill, and Walter Kerr theatres.

History

The company's name derives from the names of founder William J. McKnight's grandchildren: Judie, James, and Cynthia. Under longtime president James H. Binger and later Jordan Roth, Jujamcyn produced or co-produced numerous Tony Award-winning shows. In 2023 it was announced that Jujamcyn would combine operations with Ambassador Theatre Group.
