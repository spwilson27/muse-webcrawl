Khandala

Location: Pune district, Maharashtra, India
Elevation: ~550 m (1,800 ft)

Khandala is a hill station in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra, India, located at the top of the Bhor Ghat pass between Mumbai and Pune. Together with neighbouring Lonavala, it is a popular monsoon getaway known for misty valleys, waterfalls and viewpoints.

Geography and climate

Khandala sits on the Deccan Plateau edge of the Sahyadri range. Heavy monsoon rains feed seasonal waterfalls, and the surrounding forests include the Rajmachi and Tiger Point viewpoints. Its climate is cooler and wetter than nearby Mumbai.

Transport

The Mumbai–Pune Expressway and the Mumbai–Pune railway both climb through Khandala; the railway's steep ghat section historically required reversing stations and banking locomotives.
