Husqvarna Motorcycles

Husqvarna Motorcycles GmbH is a Swedish-origin manufacturer of motorcycles, founded in Huskvarna, Sweden in 1903 as a subsidiary of the Husqvarna armaments firm. Today it is owned by the Austrian Pierer Mobility group (KTM) and is based in Mattighofen, Austria.

History

Husqvarna began producing bicycles in the late 19th century and built its first motorcycle in 1903. The company became famous for off-road competition, winning numerous motocross and enduro world championships, particularly in the 1960s and 1970s with riders from Sweden. The motorcycle division was sold to Cagiva in 1987, later passed to BMW Motorrad in 2007, and was acquired by Pierer Industrie in 2013, reuniting it operationally with KTM and later GasGas.

Models

The modern range includes motocross (FC, TC), enduro (FE, TE), supermoto, and street models such as the Svartpilen and Vitpilen, as well as the Norden 901 adventure motorcycle.
