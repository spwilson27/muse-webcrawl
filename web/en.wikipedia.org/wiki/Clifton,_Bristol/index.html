Clifton, Bristol

Clifton is an affluent suburb of Bristol, England, situated west of the city centre above the Avon Gorge. It is noted for its Georgian architecture, terraced crescents and the Clifton Suspension Bridge.

History

Clifton grew during the 18th and early 19th centuries as wealthy merchants built grand houses away from the busy port below. The Clifton Suspension Bridge, designed by Isambard Kingdom Brunel and completed in 1864, spans the gorge to Leigh Woods.

Features

The area includes Clifton Down, Bristol Zoo's former site, the University of Bristol buildings, and the village-like shopping streets of Clifton Village. Many buildings are Grade I or II listed.
