Wheels of Fire

Wheels of Fire
Studio/live album by Cream
Released: August 1968
Label: Polydor / Atco

Wheels of Fire is the third album by the British rock supergroup Cream. It was released in August 1968 as a two-disc set, with one disc recorded in the studio and the other recorded live at the Fillmore and Winterland in San Francisco. It was a major commercial success, becoming the first platinum-selling double album and topping the charts in the United States.

Content

The studio disc includes some of the band's best-known songs, including "White Room", "Politician", "Born Under a Bad Sign", and "Deserted Cities of the Heart". The live disc features extended improvisations such as the sixteen-minute "Spoonful" and the harmonica-driven "Train Time", showcasing the trio's virtuosic interplay. The album cover, designed by Martin Sharp, features a distinctive silver foil design.

Reception and legacy

Wheels of Fire is widely regarded as one of the landmark albums of the psychedelic and blues rock era. It has appeared on numerous lists of the greatest albums of all time, and its success helped establish the double album as a viable commercial format for rock bands. Cream disbanded only months after its release, following a farewell tour in late 1968.
