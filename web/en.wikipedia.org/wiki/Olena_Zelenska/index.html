Olena Zelenska

Olena Volodymyrivna Zelenska (born 6 February 1978) is a Ukrainian screenwriter and architect who has served as First Lady of Ukraine since 2019, as the wife of President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Early life and career

She was born Olena Kyiashko in Kryvyi Rih. She studied architecture at Kryvyi Rih National University but went on to write for the Kvartal 95 comedy studio, which she co-founded with Zelenskyy and others; she became one of its leading writers.

First Lady

As First Lady, Zelenska has promoted school nutrition reform in Ukraine, the spread of the Ukrainian language and culture abroad (including Ukrainian-language audio guides in museums worldwide), barrier-free accessibility, and mental health. Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, she became a prominent international advocate for Ukraine, addressing the United States Congress in July 2022 — the first spouse of a foreign leader to do so — and founding the Olena Zelenska Foundation to channel humanitarian aid. Her initiatives include the "How are you?" national mental health programme and hosting the Summit of First Ladies and Gentlemen in Kyiv.
