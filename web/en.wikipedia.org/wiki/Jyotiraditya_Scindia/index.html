Jyotiraditya Madhavrao Scindia (born 1 January 1971) is an Indian politician and a scion of the Scindia royal family of Gwalior. He has served in the Union Council of Ministers under both Congress-led and BJP-led governments, and is currently Minister of Communications and Minister of Development of North Eastern Region in the government of Narendra Modi.

Background and education

Scindia is the son of Madhavrao Scindia, a senior Congress politician, and grandson of Jiwajirao Scindia, the last ruling Maharaja of Gwalior. He was educated at The Doon School, Harvard University (BA in Economics) and Stanford Graduate School of Business (MBA), and worked as an investment banker with Merrill Lynch and Morgan Stanley before entering politics.

Political career

After his father's death in a plane crash in 2001, Scindia was elected to the Lok Sabha from Guna, Madhya Pradesh, in a February 2002 by-election as a Congress candidate. He served as Minister of State for Communications and Information Technology (2007), for Commerce and Industry (2009), and as Minister of State (Independent Charge) for Power (2012–2014) in the Manmohan Singh government.

He lost Guna in the 2019 general election. In March 2020, after a long-running feud within the Madhya Pradesh Congress, he resigned from the party and joined the Bharatiya Janata Party; the defection of MLAs loyal to him brought down the Kamal Nath state government. He was elected to the Rajya Sabha and, in July 2021, became Union Minister of Civil Aviation, additionally holding Steel from 2022. After winning the Guna Lok Sabha seat in 2024, he took charge of Communications and the Development of North Eastern Region ministry.

Other roles

Scindia has been closely involved in Indian cricket administration, serving as president of the Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association. He married Priyadarshini Raje, of the Gaekwad family of Baroda, in 1994; they have two children.
