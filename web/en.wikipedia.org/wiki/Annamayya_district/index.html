Annamayya district is a district in the Rayalaseema region of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It was formed on 4 April 2022 from parts of the former Kadapa and Chittoor districts, as part of the state's reorganisation of its districts. The district headquarters is Rayachoti, and the largest town is Madanapalle.

Name

The district is named after Annamacharya (Annamayya), the 15th-century saint-poet and composer of devotional sankirtanas in praise of Lord Venkateswara, who was born at Tallapaka in the district.

Geography and administration

The district comprises revenue divisions centred on Rayachoti, Madanapalle and Rajampet, subdivided into mandals. It is bordered by Kadapa, Chittoor, Sri Sathya Sai and Anantapur districts and by Karnataka. Agriculture, horticulture (notably tomatoes around Madanapalle) and sericulture are economic mainstays.
