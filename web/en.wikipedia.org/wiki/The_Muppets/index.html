The Muppets are an American ensemble cast of puppet characters known for their self-aware burlesque and slapstick style of variety-sketch comedy. Created by Jim Henson in 1955, the Muppets are the namesakes of the media franchise that encompasses television, film, music, and other media associated with the characters. The name is often said to combine "marionette" and "puppet," though Henson explained he coined it simply because he liked the sound.

History

Henson's earliest Muppet characters appeared on the local Washington, D.C. television show Sam and Friends (1955–1961), which featured an early version of Kermit the Frog. The characters gained national exposure through commercials, late-night television appearances, and, beginning in 1969, Sesame Street, for which Henson's company created characters such as Bert and Ernie, Big Bird (performed by Caroll Spinney), and Oscar the Grouch. In 1976 Henson launched The Muppet Show, a variety program produced in England that became a worldwide hit, running for five seasons and 120 episodes with celebrity guest stars. The success led to feature films beginning with The Muppet Movie (1979), followed by The Great Muppet Caper (1981) and The Muppets Take Manhattan (1984).

After Henson

Following Jim Henson's death in 1990, the characters continued in films such as The Muppet Christmas Carol (1992) and Muppet Treasure Island (1996). In 2004 the Walt Disney Company acquired the Muppets franchise (excluding the Sesame Street characters, owned by Sesame Workshop). Disney revived the characters with the 2011 film The Muppets, its 2014 sequel Muppets Most Wanted, a 2015 ABC mockumentary series, and the Disney+ series Muppets Now (2020) and The Muppets Mayhem (2023).

Characters

The core troupe includes Kermit the Frog, the group's level-headed leader; Miss Piggy, a diva pig; Fozzie Bear, an aspiring comedian; Gonzo the Great, a daredevil performance artist; Rowlf the Dog, a laid-back pianist; Scooter; the Swedish Chef; Statler and Waldorf, heckling old men in the balcony; Dr. Bunsen Honeydew and his assistant Beaker; and Animal, the wild drummer of Dr. Teeth and the Electric Mayhem. Principal performers over the years have included Henson, Frank Oz, Dave Goelz, Jerry Nelson, Richard Hunt, Steve Whitmire, and Matt Vogel.
