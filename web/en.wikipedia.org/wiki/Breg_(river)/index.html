Breg (river)

Breg
Country: Germany (Baden-Württemberg)
Length: about 46 km
Mouth: Danube at Donaueschingen
The Breg in the Black Forest

The Breg is a river of Baden-Württemberg, Germany, and the longer of the two headstreams of the Danube. It rises in the southern Black Forest near Furtwangen, at an elevation of about 1,078 metres, close to the source of the Elz. From its source at the Martinskapelle it flows some 46 km south-east through Furtwangen, Vöhrenbach, Hammereisenbach and Bräunlingen before joining the Brigach at Donaueschingen, where the two streams unite to form the Danube proper.

Source of the Danube

Because the Breg is longer and carries more water than the Brigach, geographers regard it as the true main source stream of the Danube; its spring near Furtwangen is therefore claimed as the genuine origin of the great river, in rivalry with the symbolic Danube source (Donauquelle) in the castle park at Donaueschingen. Measured from the Breg spring, the Danube's total length is about 2,888 km to the Black Sea.
