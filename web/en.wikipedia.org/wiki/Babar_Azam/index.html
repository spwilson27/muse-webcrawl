Mohammad Babar Azam (Urdu: محمد بابر اعظم; born 15 October 1994) is a Pakistani international cricketer who captained the Pakistan national team in all formats. A right-handed top-order batter, he is widely regarded as one of the finest batters of his generation, noted especially for his elegant cover drive and consistency across formats.

Early life and domestic career

Born in Lahore, Babar grew up in the Walled City and honed his game in local tape-ball and club cricket. He represented Pakistan at under-15 and under-19 level, playing in two Under-19 World Cups. He is a first cousin of the Akmal brothers, Kamran, Umar and Adnan.

International career

Babar made his ODI debut against Zimbabwe in Lahore in May 2015 and his Test debut against the West Indies in Dubai in October 2016, scoring 69. In 2016 he became the first batter to score three consecutive ODI centuries against one opponent in a series (against the West Indies in the UAE). He became the fastest player to 2,000 ODI runs at the time relative to innings played, and in April 2021 he reached the number one spot in the ICC ODI batting rankings, ending Virat Kohli's long hold on the position.

Appointed white-ball captain in 2019–2020 and Test captain in 2020, Babar led Pakistan to the semi-finals of the 2021 T20 World Cup and the final of the 2022 edition. He holds numerous Pakistan records, including the most runs in a calendar year in ODIs and most T20I runs for his country, and is among the fastest to 5,000 ODI runs.

Playing style

Babar is praised for his orthodox technique, timing, and ability to score all around the wicket, with a particularly admired cover drive. He has frequently been grouped by commentators among the leading batters of the modern era.
