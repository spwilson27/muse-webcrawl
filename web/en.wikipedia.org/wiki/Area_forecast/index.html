Area forecast

An area forecast (FA) is an aviation weather product that provides a forecast of visual meteorological conditions, clouds, and general weather conditions over a large area, typically for a period of 12 to 18 hours. Area forecasts are used by pilots for pre-flight planning, especially for visual flight rules (VFR) operations.

United States

In the United States, area forecasts were issued by the National Weather Service for the conterminous states and Alaska. In 2017 the FAA and NWS replaced the textual area forecast for the conterminous United States with the Graphical Forecasts for Aviation (GFA), while Alaska retained the product.

Contents

A traditional area forecast was divided into a synopsis and sections covering clouds and weather, icing, and turbulence for defined geographic regions, using standard meteorological abbreviations.
