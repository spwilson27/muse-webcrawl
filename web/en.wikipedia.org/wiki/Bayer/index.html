Bayer AG is a German multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology company and one of the largest pharmaceutical companies and biomedical and agricultural enterprises in the world. Headquartered in Leverkusen, Bayer's areas of business include pharmaceuticals, consumer health care products, agricultural chemicals, seeds and biotechnology products.

History

Founded in Barmen in 1863 as a dyestuffs factory by Friedrich Bayer and Johann Friedrich Weskott, Bayer became famous for aspirin, synthesised in pure form by Felix Hoffmann in 1897 and marketed from 1899. The company was part of IG Farben from 1925 to 1951. After the Second World War it was re-established as an independent company.

Bayer acquired Monsanto in 2018 for about US$63 billion, becoming a major producer of seeds and pesticides, including glyphosate-based Roundup, which brought extensive litigation in the United States over alleged health effects.

Operations

Bayer is organised into Pharmaceuticals, Consumer Health and Crop Science divisions. Well-known products include aspirin, Aleve, Claritin, Xarelto and Adempas. The Bayer cross, two intersecting circles with BAYER written horizontally and vertically, is one of the world's best-known corporate logos.
