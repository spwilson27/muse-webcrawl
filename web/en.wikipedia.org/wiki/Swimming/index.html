Swimming (sport)

Swimming (sport)
DefinitionSelf-propulsion through water, for sport, exercise or survival
StrokesFreestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly
[Image: A swimmer doing freestyle in a lane of a bright blue pool]

Swimming is the self-propulsion of a person through water, typically for recreation, sport, exercise or survival. Competitive swimming — freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly races — is a marquee Olympic sport (in the wider crawl), dominated in eras by stars from Spitz to Phelps.

Safety

Drowning is a leading cause of accidental death worldwide, making swimming lessons a public-health intervention; lifeguarded beaches and pools, and the simple survival float, save children's lives every summer in every city with a pool.
