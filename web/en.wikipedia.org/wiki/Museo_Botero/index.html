Museo Botero

The Museo Botero is a museum in La Candelaria, the historic centre of Bogotá, Colombia. It opened in 2000 in a former archbishop's residence, housing art donated by the Colombian artist Fernando Botero to the Banco de la República.

The collection includes more than 120 works by Botero himself — paintings, drawings and sculptures in his signature voluminous "Boterismo" style — together with around 85 works by international artists from his personal collection, including pieces by Picasso, Monet, Renoir, Dalí, Matisse and Miró.

Access

Admission is free, in accordance with Botero's wish that the collection be open to all. The museum forms part of the Banco de la República's cultural complex alongside the Museo Casa de Moneda and the Luis Ángel Arango Library.
