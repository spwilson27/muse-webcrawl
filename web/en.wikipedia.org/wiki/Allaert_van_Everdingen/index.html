Allaert van Everdingen

Allaert van Everdingen (1621 – 8 November 1675) was a Dutch Golden Age painter and printmaker known for Scandinavian landscapes of waterfalls, rocky coasts and pine forests, which he introduced into Dutch art after travelling to Norway and Sweden.

Born1621, Alkmaar, Netherlands
Died8 November 1675, Amsterdam
Known forLandscape painting, etching
MovementDutch Golden Age

Life and work

Van Everdingen studied in Utrecht with Roelandt Savery and in Haarlem with Pieter de Molijn. In 1644–45 he travelled to Scandinavia — tradition says his ship was storm-driven to Norway — and sketched the wild scenery he mined for decades. He settled in Haarlem and then Amsterdam.

His craggy northern scenes, often with sawmills and waterfalls, influenced Jacob van Ruisdael. He also painted Dutch beach and river views and made notable etchings, including illustrations for a 1682 edition of Reynard the Fox (published posthumously).
