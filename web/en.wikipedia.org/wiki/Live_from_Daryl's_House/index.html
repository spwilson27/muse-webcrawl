Live from Daryl's House is an online music series created by singer-songwriter Daryl Hall, one half of Hall & Oates, which premiered in late 2007. In each episode Hall welcomes a guest artist to his home — first in Millerton, New York, and later at his Daryl's House club in Pawling, New York — where they perform songs from each other's catalogues together with Hall's band, interspersed with conversation and a shared meal.

Daryl Hall and a guest performing on the show

Format and guests

Episodes typically feature a mix of the guest's hits, Hall & Oates classics, and deep cuts, arranged collaboratively. Guests have included Smokey Robinson, CeeLo Green, Cheap Trick, Rob Thomas, Jason Mraz, Fitz and the Tantrums, KT Tunstall, Elle King, Wyclef Jean, and many others across soul, rock, country, and pop.

Distribution and reception

Originally distributed free on its own website, the series won a Webby Award and later moved into television syndication (including on Palladia/MTV Live and AXS TV). It is credited as an early, influential example of a web-native music performance series, praised for its intimate, high-quality live recordings.
