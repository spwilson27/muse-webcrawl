Baby of the House

Baby of the House is the unofficial title given to the youngest member of a legislative house, such as a parliament or congress. The term is used in several Westminster-style legislatures and elsewhere, complementing the honorific "Father of the House" for the longest-serving member.

Usage

The title carries no official duties or privileges; it is a courtesy designation noting the member's status as the youngest sitting member. As members age and new elections occur, the title passes to whoever is youngest at a given time.

United Kingdom

In the UK House of Commons, the title is used informally for the youngest MP. Notable holders have included Bernadette Devlin, elected in 1969 at age 21, and Mhairi Black, elected in 2015 at age 20, who became the youngest MP since the Reform Act era. Sam Carling, elected in 2024 at 22, held the title in the Parliament elected that year.

Other countries

Similar designations are used in Canada, Australia, Ireland, and other countries with parliamentary systems. In the United States, the youngest member of Congress is occasionally referred to in the press by the same term, though it has no formal standing.
