International Airlines Group

International Consolidated Airlines Group, S.A., commonly known as International Airlines Group or IAG, is a British-Spanish multinational airline holding company formed in January 2011 through the merger of British Airways and Iberia, the flag carriers of the United Kingdom and Spain.

Member airlines

  • British Airways
  • Iberia
  • Aer Lingus
  • Vueling
  • Level

IAG also owns IAG Cargo and the Avios loyalty programme. It is one of the world's largest airline groups by revenue, and its airlines are members of the Oneworld alliance. The group is listed on the London and Madrid stock exchanges.

History

Following its formation in 2011, IAG acquired bmi (2012), Vueling (2013), and Aer Lingus (2015). It has pursued consolidation and cost synergies while allowing its airlines to retain distinct brands.
