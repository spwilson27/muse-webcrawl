List of human clusters of differentiation

The cluster of differentiation (CD) nomenclature was established at international workshops on human leukocyte differentiation antigens, beginning in 1982. CD molecules are cell-surface markers used to identify and characterise leukocytes and other cells. This article lists human clusters of differentiation.

CD markers are routinely measured by flow cytometry to phenotype immune cells.
