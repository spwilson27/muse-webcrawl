Mission is a railway station in Mission, British Columbia, Canada. It is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express commuter rail service to downtown Vancouver and is also served, on request, by Via Rail's transcontinental train The Canadian.

Services

The West Coast Express operates trains westbound to Waterfront station in Vancouver on weekday mornings and eastbound returning trains in the afternoon and evening, serving the Fraser Valley and the eastern suburbs of Metro Vancouver. The station is located on the Canadian Pacific Railway main line near the historic centre of Mission, close to the Mission Bridge over the Fraser River to Abbotsford. TransLink provides connecting bus services, and the station has park-and-ride facilities. Via Rail's The Canadian, running between Vancouver and Toronto, stops at Mission only on request.
