List of Australian flags

This is a list of flags of Australia, including the national flag, state and territory flags, ensigns, and other official and historical flags.

National flags

  • Flag of Australia — a defaced Blue Ensign: a blue field with the Union Jack in the canton, a large white seven-pointed Commonwealth Star below the canton, and the Southern Cross in the fly. Adopted in 1901 following a design competition, and formally specified by the Flags Act 1953.
  • Australian red ensign — the same design on a red field, the ensign of the Australian merchant navy.
  • Australian white ensign — a white field with the Southern Cross and Commonwealth Star in blue; the naval ensign of the Royal Australian Navy since 1967.
  • Royal Australian Air Force Ensign — an air-force blue ensign with the RAAF roundel in the fly.

State and territory flags

  • New South Wales — blue ensign with the state badge (a lion passant on a red cross).
  • Victoria — blue ensign with the Southern Cross surmounted by a crown.
  • Queensland — blue ensign with a light blue Maltese cross and crown.
  • South Australia — blue ensign with a piping shrike on a gold disc.
  • Western Australia — blue ensign with a black swan on a gold disc.
  • Tasmania — blue ensign with a red lion passant.
  • Northern Territory — ochre panel with a stylised Sturt's desert rose beside a black panel with the Southern Cross (adopted 1978).
  • Australian Capital Territory — blue and gold flag with the Southern Cross and the modified arms of Canberra (adopted 1993).

Indigenous flags

  • Aboriginal flag — designed by Harold Thomas in 1971: black (the Aboriginal people), red (the earth and ochre), and a yellow sun disc. Officially proclaimed a flag of Australia in 1995.
  • Torres Strait Islander flag — designed by Bernard Namok; green, blue, and black panels with a white dhari (headdress) and five-pointed star; proclaimed in 1995.

Other flags

The list also covers the Queen's Personal Australian Flag (1962–2022), the Governor-General's flag, defence and civil aviation ensigns, the Eureka flag of 1854, and various historical and regional flags.
