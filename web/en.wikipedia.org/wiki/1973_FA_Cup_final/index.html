1973 FA Cup final

The 1973 FA Cup final was played on 5 May 1973 at Wembley Stadium, London, between Sunderland and Leeds United. It is remembered as one of the greatest FA Cup upsets.

Match

Sunderland, then a Second Division side managed by Bob Stokoe, defeated Don Revie's powerful Leeds United 1–0 thanks to a first-half goal by Ian Porterfield. Goalkeeper Jimmy Montgomery produced a celebrated double save in the second half to preserve the lead.

Legacy

Sunderland became the first Second Division team to win the FA Cup since West Bromwich Albion in 1931, and Stokoe's celebratory run across the Wembley pitch in a trilby hat and tracksuit is an iconic image of English football.
