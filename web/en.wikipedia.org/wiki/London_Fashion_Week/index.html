London Fashion Week

London Fashion Week (LFW) is a clothing trade show held in London, United Kingdom, twice a year, in February and September. Organised by the British Fashion Council, it is one of the "Big Four" fashion weeks alongside New York, Milan, and Paris, and is regarded as a showcase for both established houses and emerging, avant-garde designers.

History

The first London Fashion Week was held in 1984 in a car park in Kensington, organised by the British Fashion Council with government support for the British fashion industry. Long based at the Natural History Museum and then Somerset House (2009–2015), the event later moved to Brewer Street in Soho and to The Store Studios at 180 Strand. Designers associated with London's reputation for creativity include John Galliano, Alexander McQueen, Vivienne Westwood, and Christopher Kane; London also hosts London Fashion Week Men's (formerly London Collections: Men), launched in 2012.

Digital firsts

In 2010 London became the first of the Big Four fashion weeks to livestream all its catwalk shows online. During the COVID-19 pandemic it moved to a digital, gender-neutral format in June 2020, later returning to physical shows augmented by digital showcases.

London Fashion Week Festival

A consumer-facing event, the London Fashion Week Festival (formerly London Fashion Weekend), follows the trade shows, offering catwalk presentations, talks, and shopping events open to the public.
