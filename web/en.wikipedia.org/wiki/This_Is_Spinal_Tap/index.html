This Is Spinal Tap

This Is Spinal Tap
Director: Rob Reiner
Released: 1984
Starring: Christopher Guest, Michael McKean, Harry Shearer

This Is Spinal Tap is a 1984 American mockumentary comedy film directed by Rob Reiner (in his directorial debut) and starring Christopher Guest, Michael McKean, and Harry Shearer as members of the fictional English heavy metal band Spinal Tap. It satirises the behaviour and musical pretensions of rock bands and the hagiographic tendencies of rock documentaries.

Plot

Filmmaker Marty DiBergi (Reiner) documents Spinal Tap's 1982 American comeback tour, which descends into chaos: concerts are cancelled, backstage riders go unfulfilled, a miniature Stonehenge stage prop becomes a laughing stock, and tensions rise between lead singer David St. Hubbins (McKean) and his girlfriend. Lead guitarist Nigel Tufnel (Guest) famously shows off an amplifier whose knobs "go to eleven".

Reception and legacy

Though only a modest success on release, the film became a cult classic on home video and is routinely cited as one of the funniest films ever made. Its heavily improvised script popularised the mockumentary form and phrases such as "up to eleven". In 2002 it was selected for preservation in the US National Film Registry. A sequel, Spinal Tap II: The End Continues, was released in 2025.
