Anna is a fictional character who appears in Walt Disney Animation Studios' Frozen franchise. Voiced by Kristen Bell, she is the younger sister of Elsa and princess — later queen — of the kingdom of Arendelle. She is the 12th member of the Disney Princess line-up.

Character

Anna is optimistic, energetic and somewhat awkward, in contrast to her reserved elder sister. After Elsa's ice powers plunge Arendelle into winter and Elsa flees, Anna journeys with the ice harvester Kristoff, his reindeer Sven and the snowman Olaf to bring her home. In Frozen II (2019) Anna becomes queen of Arendelle after Elsa chooses to remain in the Enchanted Forest.

Origins

The character draws loosely on Gerda from Hans Christian Andersen's fairy tale "The Snow Queen". Critics praised the franchise's focus on the sisters' relationship rather than a romantic rescue.
