Pokémon (anime)

Pokémon (Japanese: ポケットモンスター, Pocket Monsters) is a Japanese anime television series produced by OLM (formerly Oriental Light and Magic), based on the Pokémon video games by Game Freak and Nintendo. It premiered on TV Tokyo on 1 April 1997 and for 26 years followed the journey of Pokémon Trainer Ash Ketchum (Satoshi) and his partner Pikachu as they travelled the world aiming to become a Pokémon Master. One of the most successful anime exports in history, it has aired in over 190 countries, run for more than 1,200 episodes across 25 seasons, and spawned over twenty feature films.

Overview

The series is organised into sagas mirroring the games: the original Kanto–Johto journey, Hoenn (Advanced Generation), Sinnoh (Diamond and Pearl), Unova (Best Wishes), Kalos (XY), Alola (Sun & Moon), and the world-touring Journeys series, in which Ash finally won the World Coronation Series and became Monarch (world champion) in November 2022. Ash's story concluded in 2023; the successor series, Pokémon Horizons, follows new protagonists Liko and Roy. Recurring elements include companions such as Misty, Brock, and Dawn; the villainous Team Rocket trio (Jessie, James, and Meowth); and Professor Oak's laboratory in Pallet Town.

Notable events

In December 1997, the episode "Dennō Senshi Porygon" caused photosensitive epileptic seizures in hundreds of Japanese children due to rapidly flashing red and blue frames; broadcast standards worldwide were tightened in response, and the episode was never aired again. The franchise's films, beginning with Pokémon: The First Movie (1998), have grossed well over US$1 billion, making Pokémon the highest-grossing media franchise of all time when merchandising is included.

Reception and influence

Constantly renewed for each game generation, the anime introduced Pokémon to much of the world and anchored "Pokémania" in the late 1990s. While produced by OLM rather than a major rival like Toei Animation, its longevity and global syndication rival any anime in history, and its fan culture — including the doujinshi scene — is enormous.
