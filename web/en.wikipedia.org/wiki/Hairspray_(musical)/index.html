Hairspray (musical)

Hairspray is an American musical with music by Marc Shaiman, lyrics by Scott Wittman and Shaiman, and a book by Mark O'Donnell and Thomas Meehan. It is based on John Waters's 1988 film Hairspray. The musical premiered in Seattle in 2002 and opened on Broadway at the Neil Simon Theatre on August 15, 2002.

Plot

Set in 1962 Baltimore, the story follows teenager Tracy Turnblad, whose dream is to dance on The Corny Collins Show, a local TV dance programme. When she earns a spot on the show, she campaigns to racially integrate it, confronting the prejudices of the station's management. Well-known songs include "Good Morning Baltimore", "Welcome to the '60s", "I Can Hear the Bells", "You Can't Stop the Beat", and the ballad "I Know Where I've Been".

Productions and awards

The original Broadway production starred Marissa Jaret Winokur as Tracy and Harvey Fierstein as her mother Edna, and won eight Tony Awards, including Best Musical. It ran for 2,642 performances, closing in January 2009. A West End production opened in 2007 and won four Olivier Awards. The musical was adapted into the 2007 film and performed live on television as Hairspray Live! in 2016.
