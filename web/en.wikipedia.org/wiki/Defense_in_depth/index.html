Defense in depth

Defense in depth is an approach to security and defence that uses multiple layers of protection so that failure of one layer does not compromise the whole system.

Military use

Historically it meant trading space and successive defensive lines to wear down an attacker rather than relying on a single line.

Information security

In computing it combines firewalls, access control, encryption, monitoring, segmentation and backups.

Nuclear safety

Nuclear plants apply layered physical barriers, redundant cooling and emergency planning.
