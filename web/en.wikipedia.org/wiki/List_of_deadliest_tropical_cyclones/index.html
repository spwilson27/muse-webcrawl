List of deadliest tropical cyclones

The deadliest tropical cyclones in recorded history have overwhelmingly struck the low-lying delta of the Ganges and Brahmaputra rivers in the Bay of Bengal, where enormous storm surges inundate densely populated land barely above sea level. The deadliest single storm, the 1970 Bhola cyclone, killed an estimated 300,000 to 500,000 people in what is now Bangladesh — the deadliest tropical cyclone, and one of the deadliest natural disasters, ever recorded. Fatality figures for historical storms are estimates and often vary widely between sources.

Most deaths in tropical cyclones are caused by storm surge and inland flooding rather than wind. Improved forecasting, cyclone shelters, and early-warning systems in Bangladesh reduced the death toll of comparable storms dramatically between 1970 and the 21st century — Cyclone Sidr (2007) and Amphan (2020) killed a small fraction of the numbers lost in earlier storms.
