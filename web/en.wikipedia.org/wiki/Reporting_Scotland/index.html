Reporting Scotland is the BBC's national television news programme for Scotland, broadcast on BBC One Scotland. First aired on 1 April 1968, it is one of the longest-running television news programmes in the United Kingdom.

Overview

The programme is produced by BBC Scotland from its headquarters at Pacific Quay in Glasgow and includes reports from BBC newsrooms across Scotland. The main edition airs at 6:30 pm on weeknights, forming part of the early-evening schedule after the BBC News at Six, with shorter bulletins at breakfast, lunchtime, and late evening. It covers Scottish news, politics at Holyrood, sport, and weather.

Presenters

Long-serving presenters have included Mary Marquis, Jackie Bird, who presented from 1987 to 2019, and Sally Magnusson. The programme has been a mainstay of Scottish television and one of BBC Scotland's flagship productions.
