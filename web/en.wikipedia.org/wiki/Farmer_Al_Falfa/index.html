Farmer Al Falfa

Farmer Al Falfa is an animated cartoon character created by American animator Paul Terry. A cantankerous old farmer with a long beard, he debuted in the silent era and became one of the earliest recurring stars of theatrical animation, appearing in Terry's Aesop's Fables series of the 1920s and in later Terrytoons shorts into the sound era.

Created by:Paul Terry
First appearance:1916 (in Terry's early farmer cartoons for Bray Productions)
Series:Aesop's Fables, Terrytoons

History

Terry developed the farmer character while working for Bray Productions around 1916, making him among the first animated characters with a continuing identity. At Terry's own studio, Farmer Al Falfa headlined entries in the Aesop's Film Fables (1921–1933), produced by Amadee J. Van Beuren, in which the farmer clashed with mice, cats and other barnyard animals in loosely moralised slapstick stories. He survived the transition to sound and continued appearing in Terrytoons cartoons in the 1930s and 1940s, later reaching new audiences through early television packages of the old shorts.

Character

The Farmer is drawn as a rustic, irascible old man, typically beset by the animals on his farm and by the mischievous mice that were a Terrytoons staple. Though overshadowed in fame by later Terry creations such as Mighty Mouse, Farmer Al Falfa is a significant figure in animation history as one of the medium's pioneering star characters.
