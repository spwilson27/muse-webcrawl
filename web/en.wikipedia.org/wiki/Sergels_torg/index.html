Sergels torg
Location: Norrmalm, Stockholm, Sweden
Named after: Johan Tobias Sergel

Sergels torg is a major public square in central Stockholm, Sweden, widely regarded as the city's modern centre point. It is named after the 18th-century sculptor Johan Tobias Sergel, whose workshop stood nearby.

Layout

The square has two levels: a lower pedestrian plaza with a distinctive black-and-white triangular-patterned floor, and street level above with traffic. At its centre stands the Kristallvertikalaccent, a 37-metre glass obelisk designed by Edvin Öhrström, erected in 1974.

Surroundings

The square is flanked by the Kulturhuset (House of Culture), large department stores, and the central station of the Stockholm Metro, T-Centralen, making it one of the busiest transport and meeting points in Sweden. It is also a traditional gathering place for demonstrations and celebrations.
