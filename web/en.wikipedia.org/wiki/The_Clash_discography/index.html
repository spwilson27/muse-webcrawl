The Clash discography

The discography of the English punk rock band The Clash consists of six studio albums, two live albums, and numerous compilations, EPs, and singles. Formed in London in 1976, the band became one of the most celebrated acts of the punk era and beyond, expanding their sound into reggae, dub, funk, and rockabilly.

Live and compilation albums

From Here to Eternity: Live (1999) and Live at Shea Stadium (2008) document the band in concert. Notable compilations include The Story of the Clash, Volume 1 (1988), the rarities set Super Black Market Clash (1993), and the box set Sound System (2013), which gathered the band's studio recordings remastered under Mick Jones's supervision.
