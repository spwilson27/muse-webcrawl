Magazine

[Image: A newsstand rack of glossy magazine covers]

A magazine is a periodical publication, usually weekly or monthly, mixing articles, essays, photography and illustration for a general or specialised audience, bound (or published digitally) as an issue. The form began in the 18th century (The Gentleman's Magazine, 1731) and flowered into mass titles, newsweeklies, glossy fashion and special-interest press.

Economics and change

Magazines lived on newsstand sales, subscriptions and luxury or category advertising; the internet hollowed out both, pushing titles toward digital subscriptions, events and brand extensions, while prestige titles (The New Yorker, The Atlantic) reinvented themselves around membership.
