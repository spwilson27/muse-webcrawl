Admiral Gorshkov-class frigate

Class overview
Name: Admiral Gorshkov class (Project 22350)
Builders: Severnaya Verf, St. Petersburg
Operators: Russian Navy
Displacement: about 5,400 tonnes full load
Length: 135 m
Speed: 29.5 knots
Armament: VLS for Kalibr, Oniks and Zircon missiles; Poliment-Redut air defence system; 130 mm gun; torpedoes; Ka-27 helicopter

The Admiral Gorshkov class, Russian designation Project 22350, is a class of frigates of the Russian Navy. The ships are the first large surface combatants designed and built in Russia after the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and are intended for long-range operations in distant ocean zones, performing anti-submarine, escort, and strike missions.

Design

Designed by the Severnoye Design Bureau, the class incorporates stealth features in its hull and superstructure and is built around a modern combat information system. The standard displacement is about 4,550 tonnes (5,400 tonnes full load), with a length of 135 metres and a combined diesel-and-gas (CODAG) propulsion plant combining two diesel engines and two gas turbines for a maximum speed of about 29.5 knots and a range of roughly 4,500 nautical miles.

Armament and sensors

The principal strike armament is a 16-cell UKSK vertical launching system forward, which can fire Kalibr land-attack and anti-ship cruise missiles, supersonic P-800 Oniks missiles, and hypersonic Tsirkon (Zircon) missiles; later ships add further cells. Air defence is provided by the Poliment-Redut system with up to 32 cells and an active electronically scanned array radar in the distinctive pyramidal mast, supplemented by close-in weapon systems. The class also carries a 130 mm A-192M naval gun, Paket-NK anti-submarine torpedo launchers, and a hangar for a Kamov Ka-27 helicopter.

Ships and service

The lead ship, Admiral Gorshkov, was laid down in 2006, launched in 2010, and commissioned in July 2018 after protracted trials, including development problems with the Poliment-Redut system and, for later hulls, the substitution of Russian-built gas turbines for Ukrainian ones after 2014. She was followed by Admiral Kasatonov (2020) and Admiral Golovko (2023), with further ships under construction at Severnaya Verf in St. Petersburg for the Northern and Pacific fleets. In January 2023 Admiral Gorshkov deployed armed with Zircon missiles, drawing wide attention.
