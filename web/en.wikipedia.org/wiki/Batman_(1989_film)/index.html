Batman (1989 film)

Batman
Directed by: Tim Burton
Starring: Michael Keaton, Jack Nicholson, Kim Basinger
Music: Danny Elfman; songs by Prince
Released: 23 June 1989
Budget: ~$48 million
Box office: $411.6 million
Studio: Warner Bros.

Batman is a 1989 superhero film directed by Tim Burton, based on the DC Comics character. Michael Keaton stars as Bruce Wayne / Batman and Jack Nicholson as the Joker. It was the first instalment of Warner Bros.' initial Batman film series and a landmark in modern blockbuster and merchandising history.

Plot

In Gotham City, crime boss Carl Grissom's lieutenant Jack Napier is betrayed and falls into a vat of chemicals, emerging as the disfigured Joker. As the Joker terrorises Gotham with poisoned consumer products, Batman — secretly millionaire Bruce Wayne — investigates, romances photojournalist Vicki Vale (Kim Basinger), and climaxes in a confrontation in Gotham Cathedral, where Batman reveals (and avenges) his parents' murder by a young Napier.

Production

Burton and production designer Anton Furst gave Gotham a dark, gothic, Art-Deco-meets-industrial look, shot largely at Pinewood Studios in England. Nicholson received top billing and a lucrative profit share. Danny Elfman's score and Prince's soundtrack album were both hugely successful.

Reception and legacy

The film grossed over $400 million worldwide, won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction, and sparked "Batmania". Its dark tone redirected superhero cinema, and it was followed by Batman Returns (1992).
