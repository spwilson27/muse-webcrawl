Francisco Pareja

Bornc. 1570, Auñón, Spain
Died25 January 1628, Mexico
OccupationFranciscan missionary, linguist

Francisco Pareja (c. 1570 – 25 January 1628) was a Spanish Franciscan missionary in Spanish Florida who is chiefly remembered as the author of the earliest grammatical and devotional works in the Timucua language.

Missionary work

Pareja arrived in St. Augustine in 1595 and worked among the Timucua people at missions including San Juan del Puerto. He became the leading authority on the language and defended the Florida friars against accusations of laxity.

Writings

His Catecismo en lengua castellana y timuquana (1612) was among the first books printed in an Indigenous language of North America, and his Arte y pronunciación en lengua timuquana y castellana (1614) remains the principal source for the Timucua language, now extinct.
