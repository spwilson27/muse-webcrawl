Declaration of Independence (Israel)

Israeli Declaration
Date14 May 1948
LocationTel Aviv
Proclaimed byDavid Ben-Gurion

The Israeli Declaration of Independence, formally the Declaration of the Establishment of the State of Israel, was proclaimed on 14 May 1948 by David Ben-Gurion in Tel Aviv as the British Mandate for Palestine expired.

Content

The text traces Jewish historical connection to the Land of Israel, cites the Balfour Declaration and the 1947 UN Partition Plan, declares a Jewish state open to immigration, and pledges equality of social and political rights to all inhabitants and appeals for peace with Arab neighbours.

Aftermath

The United States and Soviet Union recognised Israel quickly; neighbouring Arab states invaded, beginning the 1948 Arab-Israeli War. The declaration functions as a founding document in Israeli constitutional law.
