The Azerbaijan Democratic Republic (ADR) was the first secular democratic republic in the Turkic and Muslim worlds, proclaimed on 28 May 1918 in Tiflis following the collapse of the Russian Empire, with its government soon moving to Ganja and then to Baku.

History

Led by the Musavat Party and figures such as Mammad Amin Rasulzade and Fatali Khan Khoyski, the republic adopted the tricolour flag, established a parliament, and in 1919 became one of the first states to grant women the vote. It secured de facto recognition from the Allied powers in January 1920.

Downfall

Weakened by internal disputes and territorial conflicts with Armenia, the republic fell when the Red Army invaded in April 1920; Azerbaijan was incorporated into the Soviet Union. The modern Republic of Azerbaijan regards the ADR as its predecessor and celebrates 28 May as Republic Day.
