Regions of Slovakia

Number: 8 regions (kraje)
Most populous: Prešov Region
Contains capital: Bratislava Region
Subdivided into: 79 districts

Slovakia is divided into eight regions (Slovak: kraje, singular kraj), the highest tier of the country's administrative division. Since 2002 the regions have also functioned as self-governing units with elected assemblies and chairpersons, responsible for areas such as regional roads, secondary education and culture.

The eight regions

Each region is named after its principal city: Bratislava, Trnava, Trenčín, Nitra, Žilina, Banská Bystrica, Prešov and Košice. The regions are subdivided into 79 districts (okresy), which are further divided into municipalities. Bratislava Region, though the smallest in area, includes the capital city; Prešov Region in the north-east has the largest population.

History

The present eight regions were created in 1996, reviving a regional tier that had existed in various forms under Czechoslovakia. Self-governing powers were added by reforms in 2001–2002, and the regions also serve as Slovakia's NUTS-3 statistical units grouped into four NUTS-2 areas.
