IT service management

IT service management (ITSM) refers to the activities performed by an organisation to design, build, deliver, operate and control information technology services offered to customers and internal users. It treats IT as a set of services aligned with business needs rather than as a collection of technologies.

Processes and frameworks

Core ITSM processes include incident management, problem management, change management, service level management, configuration management, release management and service catalogue management, typically coordinated through a service desk. The best-known framework is ITIL, first developed by the UK government in the 1980s and now in its fourth edition (ITIL 4). Alternatives and complements include COBIT, Microsoft Operations Framework, and the ISO/IEC 20000 standard, against which organisations can be certified. Modern ITSM increasingly draws on DevOps and agile practices and on automation and self-service tooling.
