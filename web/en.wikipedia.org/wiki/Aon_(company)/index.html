Aon plc (Irish: [eːˈən]) is an Irish-domiciled global professional services firm that provides risk management, insurance and reinsurance brokerage, and human resource solutions and outsourcing services. It is one of the world's largest insurance brokers, operating in around 120 countries with about 50,000 employees.

History

Aon was formed in 1982 when the Ryan Insurance Group merged with Combined Insurance Company of America, a company founded by W. Clement Stone in 1919. Under founder Patrick G. Ryan, the company grew through a long series of acquisitions in insurance brokerage and consulting. The name Aon, the Irish word for "one" or "oneness", was adopted in 1987.

Major acquisitions included Hudig-Langeveldt, Alexander & Alexander, Benfield Group (2008, reinsurance), and Hewitt Associates (2010), which made Aon a major provider of HR consulting and outsourcing. In 2012 the company moved its corporate headquarters to London and later re-domiciled to Ireland in 2020.

Business

Aon's operations are organised into solution lines covering commercial risk solutions, reinsurance solutions (Aon Reinsurance), health solutions, wealth solutions, and data and analytic services. It competes with firms such as Marsh McLennan and Willis Towers Watson; a planned merger with Willis Towers Watson, announced in 2020, was abandoned in 2021 after regulatory opposition in the United States.

Sponsorship

Aon was the principal sponsor of Manchester United football club from 2010 to 2014, appearing on the team's shirts, and subsequently sponsored the club's training ground and kit until 2021.
