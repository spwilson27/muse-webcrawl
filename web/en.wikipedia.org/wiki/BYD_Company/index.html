BYD Company Limited is a Chinese multinational manufacturing conglomerate headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong. Founded in 1995 by chemist Wang Chuanfu as a manufacturer of rechargeable batteries, BYD grew into one of the world's largest battery makers before expanding into automobiles, consumer electronics components, semiconductors, solar energy, and monorail transit systems. Its subsidiary BYD Auto became the world's best-selling manufacturer of electric vehicles in the mid-2020s, and in 2023 BYD overtook Tesla in quarterly global sales of battery electric vehicles for the first time.

History

Wang Chuanfu founded BYD in 1995 with about 20 employees, producing nickel–cadmium rechargeable batteries that undercut Japanese competitors through labour-intensive manufacturing. The company listed in Hong Kong in 2002. In 2003 BYD acquired the struggling Xi'an Qinchuan Automobile Company, entering the car industry over the objections of some investors. Its 2008 plug-in hybrid, the F3DM, was the world's first mass-produced plug-in hybrid car, and that same year Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway bought roughly 10% of the company, drawing international attention.

BYD progressively shifted its car business toward "new energy vehicles". In 2020 it launched the lithium iron phosphate Blade Battery, noted for its safety in nail-penetration tests, and its e-Platform 3.0 architecture. In March 2022 BYD ended production of purely internal-combustion cars, becoming one of the first major automakers to go all-in on electrified vehicles. By 2024 the company was selling more than four million plug-in vehicles a year and expanding factories in Thailand, Brazil, Hungary, Turkey and elsewhere.

Business areas

The group's operations include: BYD Auto, which sells passenger cars under the BYD (Dynasty and Ocean series), Denza, Yangwang and Fangchengbao brands, plus commercial vehicles and buses; BYD Electronics, a major contract manufacturer and components supplier for smartphones and other devices; battery production under FinDreams Battery, supplying lithium iron phosphate cells including the Blade Battery; semiconductors through BYD Semiconductor; photovoltaic and energy-storage products; and the SkyRail and SkyShuttle monorail systems for urban transit.

Name

The initials BYD were originally simply the pinyin initials of the company's registered Chinese name. The company later adopted the backronym "Build Your Dreams" as an English-language slogan, displayed on its vehicles and branding.

Criticism and controversies

BYD has faced scrutiny over labour conditions in its overseas construction projects, including a 2024 Brazilian investigation into conditions at a factory construction site, as well as trade friction: the European Union imposed additional tariffs on Chinese-made electric vehicles in 2024, and Chinese EVs face steep tariffs in the United States. The company is frequently discussed in the context of Chinese industrial policy and state support for the EV sector.
