Cobra

Cobra is a common name for various venomous snakes, most of them species of the genus Naja in the family Elapidae. Cobras are known for their threat display, in which they raise the front of the body and spread a hood formed by elongated ribs.

Species and behaviour

Well-known species include the Indian cobra (Naja naja), the Egyptian cobra and the forest cobra. The king cobra (Ophiophagus hannah), the world's longest venomous snake, is not a "true" cobra of the genus Naja. Some cobras can spit venom at the eyes of a threat. Cobra venom is primarily neurotoxic, and bites can be fatal without antivenom.
