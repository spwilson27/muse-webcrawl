Annapurna Pictures is an American media company founded in 2011 by Megan Ellison. It is known for financing auteur-driven films, including Zero Dark Thirty (2012), Her (2013), American Hustle (2013), Foxcatcher (2014), Phantom Thread (2017) and If Beale Street Could Talk (2018); its films have collectively received dozens of Academy Award nominations.

Expansion

The company launched its own distribution arm (releasing films such as Detroit and Vice), later partnering with MGM in the United Artists Releasing joint venture. Its video-game division, Annapurna Interactive (founded 2016), published acclaimed independent games including What Remains of Edith Finch, Gorogoa, Outer Wilds, Sayonara Wild Hearts and Stray. In 2024 the Interactive staff resigned en masse after a dispute over spinning off the division, leaving the label to rebuild with new teams.
