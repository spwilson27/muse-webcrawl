Bushehr

Bushehr (Persian: بوشهر) is a port city in the south of Iran and the capital of Bushehr Province. Located on the coast of the Persian Gulf, it was historically one of Iran's main ports for trade with India and East Africa, and the seat of the British political resident in the Persian Gulf during the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. The city's population is around 200,000.

History

Ancient settlements near Bushehr date back to Elamite times. The modern town grew in the eighteenth century under Nader Shah as a naval base, and later became a significant commercial port. It was occupied by British forces on several occasions, including during the Anglo-Persian War of 1856–57 and the First World War.

Nuclear power plant

Near the city stands the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant, Iran's first civilian nuclear power station. Begun by German contractors in the 1970s, abandoned after the 1979 revolution and damaged during the Iran–Iraq War, it was completed with Russian assistance and connected to the grid in 2011.
