Maverick (film)

Maverick
Directed by: Richard Donner
Starring: Mel Gibson, Jodie Foster, James Garner
Release: 1994
Genre: Western comedy

Maverick is a 1994 American Western comedy film directed by Richard Donner, written by William Goldman, and based on the 1957–1962 television series of the same name created by Roy Huggins. It stars Mel Gibson as Bret Maverick, a charming poker-playing con artist, with Jodie Foster as the pickpocket Annabelle Bransford and James Garner — the original television Maverick — as Marshal Zane Cooper.

Plot

Bret Maverick needs $25,000 to enter a winner-take-all poker tournament aboard the riverboat Lauren Belle. Along the way he teams up with, and repeatedly tries to out-con, Annabelle, crosses paths with the duplicitous Angel and the Comanchero-style villain the story reveals, and uncovers a twist concerning Marshal Cooper's true identity.

Reception

The film was a commercial success, grossing around $183 million worldwide, and received an Academy Award nomination for Best Costume Design. It is noted for its many cameos by Western and country music stars.
