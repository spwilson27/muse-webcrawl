Aiko, Princess Toshi

Born: 1 December 2001, Imperial Household Agency Hospital, Tokyo
House: Imperial House of Japan
Parents: Naruhito and Masako

Aiko, Princess Toshi (born 1 December 2001) is the only child of Emperor Naruhito and Empress Masako of Japan. Her name, Aiko, means "love" and "child"; her imperial title, Princess Toshi, means "a person who respects others".

Life and education

Princess Aiko's birth, the first child of the then Crown Prince after more than eight years of marriage, renewed public debate in Japan over the Imperial Household Law of 1947, which restricts succession to males in the male line. A government panel in 2005 recommended allowing female succession, but the discussion was set aside after the birth of her cousin Prince Hisahito in 2006. She attended Gakushuin Primary School, Girls' Junior and Senior High School, and then Gakushuin University, where she studied Japanese language and literature and graduated in March 2024.

Public role

After her coming-of-age in December 2021, Princess Aiko began performing official duties at Imperial Court events. In April 2024 she began working with the Japanese Red Cross Society while continuing her imperial duties. Under current Japanese law, she is not in the line of succession, and a princess who marries a commoner must leave the Imperial Family; debate about the shrinking Imperial Family and possible reforms continues.
