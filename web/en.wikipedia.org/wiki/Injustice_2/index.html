Injustice 2

Injustice 2 is a 2017 fighting video game developed by NetherRealm Studios and published by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment. It is the sequel to Injustice: Gods Among Us (2013) and features characters from the DC Comics universe. The game was released for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in May 2017, with a Windows version following later that year, and a mobile game of the same name.

Gameplay

Injustice 2 retains the one-on-one fighting, stage transitions and character traits of its predecessor, and introduces a "Gear System" — a loot-dropping customisation system that lets players earn and equip armour pieces that alter characters' appearance and statistics. A story mode continues the alternate-universe narrative in which a tyrannical Superman's Regime has fallen; Batman's Insurgency must contend with Brainiac's invasion and the villain alliance known as the Society. The roster launched with 28 characters including debutants Supergirl, Gorilla Grodd, Doctor Fate and Blue Beetle, expanded by downloadable fighters such as Hellboy and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

Reception

The game received generally favourable reviews, praised for its story mode, visuals and depth of content, though the randomised gear system and microtransactions drew some criticism. It won Best Fighting Game at The Game Awards 2017. A tie-in comic series and animated film (Injustice, 2021) adapted the broader storyline.
