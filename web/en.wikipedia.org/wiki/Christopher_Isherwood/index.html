Christopher Isherwood

Christopher Isherwood (1904–1986) was an Anglo-American novelist best known for his Berlin stories — Mr Norris Changes Trains (1935) and Goodbye to Berlin (1939), including the "Sally Bowles" story that inspired the musical Cabaret — and for A Single Man (1964).

Christopher Isherwood
Born: 1904, Cheshire, England
Died: 1986, Santa Monica, California
Notable works: Goodbye to Berlin, A Single Man
Partner: Don Bachardy

Life

Isherwood lived in Weimar Berlin from 1929 to 1933, documenting its cabaret world and the Nazi rise ("I am a camera"). He travelled in China with W. H. Auden (Journey to a War), emigrated to California in 1939, became a US citizen, embraced Vedanta under Swami Prabhavananda, and lived with artist Don Bachardy for over 30 years.
