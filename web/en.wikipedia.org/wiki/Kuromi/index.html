Kuromi

Kuromi (Japanese: クロミ) is a fictional character created by the Japanese company Sanrio. She is a white rabbit-like imp and the self-proclaimed rival of My Melody, introduced in 2005 in the anime series Onegai My Melody. Kuromi wears a black jester's hat with a pink skull on the front — its expression matching her own — and a black "devil's tail", presenting a punky, mischievous image that contrasts with My Melody's sweetness. Her birthday is Halloween, 31 October 2005 being her debut year.

Character and popularity

In the Onegai My Melody anime, Kuromi escapes from Mari Land to the human world with her sidekick Baku, a purple tapir, seeking revenge on My Melody for (unintentionally) causing her past embarrassments, which she records in her "Kuromi Note". Despite her villainous pose, she is portrayed sympathetically and has a soft, romantic side.

Originally a supporting antagonist, Kuromi developed a large independent fanbase, especially among teenagers and young adults drawn to her rebellious "punk-cute" aesthetic. She consistently ranks among the top characters in Sanrio's annual Character Ranking poll (placing in the top three in several recent years), stars in her own merchandise lines, short video content and social-media accounts, and has been a major driver of Sanrio's popularity with older fans alongside characters such as Cinnamoroll and My Melody.
