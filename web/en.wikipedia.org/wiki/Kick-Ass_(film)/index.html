Kick-Ass (film)

Kick-Ass is a 2010 superhero black comedy film directed by Matthew Vaughn, based on the comic book of the same name by Mark Millar and John Romita Jr. It stars Aaron Johnson, Christopher Mintz-Plasse, Chloë Grace Moretz and Nicolas Cage.

Plot

New York teenager Dave Lizewski, an ordinary high-school student and comic-book fan, decides to become a real-life superhero despite having no powers, calling himself Kick-Ass. His exploits, filmed and posted online, make him an internet sensation, but also draw him into conflict with crime boss Frank D'Amico, and into alliance with the father-daughter vigilante duo Big Daddy and Hit-Girl.

Reception

The film received generally positive reviews for its humour, action and Moretz's performance, though its violence and profanity by a young character proved controversial. It was a modest box-office success and spawned a sequel, Kick-Ass 2 (2013).
