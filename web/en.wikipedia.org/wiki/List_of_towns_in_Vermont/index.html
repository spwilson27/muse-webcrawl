List of towns in Vermont

The U.S. state of Vermont is divided into 14 counties, which are subdivided into 251 municipalities, including 237 towns, 9 cities, and 5 unincorporated places (gores and grants). In Vermont, a "town" is a full municipal corporation covering a defined territory; villages may exist within towns as incorporated sub-units. Town government is famously exercised through the annual town meeting.

Most populous towns

  • Colchester — the most populous town in Vermont (about 17,500 residents), adjoining Burlington.
  • Essex — about 22,000 before the village of Essex Junction separated as a city in 2022; now about 11,000.
  • South Burlington — a city, about 20,000.
  • Rutland Town, Milton, Brattleboro, Bennington, Williston, Hartford, and Shelburne are among the other large towns.

Governance

Towns are governed by a selectboard and conduct business at an annual town meeting, traditionally held on the first Tuesday in March (Town Meeting Day, a state holiday). Many towns date their charters to the 1760s–1780s, when they were granted by New Hampshire, New York, or the independent Republic of Vermont.
