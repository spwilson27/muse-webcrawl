Skin care

Skin care is the range of practices that support skin integrity, enhance its appearance and relieve skin conditions. It includes cleansing, moisturising, sun protection and the treatment of concerns such as acne, eczema and photoaging.

Basic practices

Core routines generally involve a gentle cleanser, a moisturiser to support the skin barrier, and a broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day, which dermatologists regard as the most effective measure against premature skin aging and skin cancer risk. Active ingredients commonly used include retinoids, vitamin C, niacinamide and alpha- and beta-hydroxy acids.

History

Skin care dates to antiquity — Egyptians used oils and unguents, and Greek and Roman medicine described topical preparations. The modern cosmetics industry emerged in the 20th century, and the late 2010s saw enormous growth in multi-step routines influenced by Korean beauty culture.

Professional care

Dermatologists diagnose and treat skin disease, while aestheticians provide non-medical treatments such as facials and chemical peels.
