Game & Watch is a series of handheld electronic games developed by Nintendo and released from 1980 to 1991. Designed by Gunpei Yokoi, each unit contains a single game played on an LCD screen, along with a clock and alarm function — hence the name "Game & Watch". The series sold approximately 43.4 million units worldwide and helped establish Nintendo in the video game industry.

History

Yokoi conceived the product after watching a bored businessman playing with an LCD calculator on a train. The first title, Ball, was released in April 1980. The series grew to include the Silver, Gold, Wide Screen, Multi Screen, Tabletop, Panorama, New Wide Screen, Super Color and Micro VS. systems, totaling 59 games. The Multi Screen models, with their clamshell design and dual screens, directly inspired the Nintendo DS, and the character Mr. Game & Watch — a generic stick-figure mascot derived from the games' simple characters — later appeared in the Super Smash Bros. series.

Legacy

Nintendo has re-released Game & Watch titles in the Game & Watch Gallery compilations and as downloadable DSiWare, and issued modern commemorative units such as the 2020 Super Mario Bros. and 2021 The Legend of Zelda Game & Watch devices.
