Online poker

Online poker is the game of poker played over the internet. Real-money online poker began in the late 1990s, with Planet Poker dealing the first real-money hand in 1998, and grew explosively in the 2000s — the "poker boom" — helped by televised poker and by amateur Chris Moneymaker winning the 2003 World Series of Poker Main Event after qualifying online.

Gameplay and software

Players download client software or use browser-based rooms, deposit funds, and play cash games or tournaments against other players for a rake charged by the operator. Software features typically include multi-tabling, hand histories, and heads-up displays tracking opponents' statistics.

Legality

The industry was reshaped by the US Unlawful Internet Gambling Enforcement Act of 2006 and by "Black Friday" (15 April 2011), when US authorities indicted major operators. Since then, online poker has been licensed and taxed in many jurisdictions and in individual US states such as New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Michigan.
