Muhammad XI (Arabic: محمد الحادي عشر; died 1455), known in Spanish chronicles as El Chiquito ("the Little One"), was a Nasrid sultan of the Emirate of Granada in al-Andalus from 1453 to 1455.

Reign

Muhammad XI succeeded Muhammad IX, whose long and turbulent reign had seen repeated civil wars between rival Nasrid factions backed alternately by Castile and the Abencerrages clan. Muhammad XI's own accession was contested: he was raised to the throne by one faction while the powerful Abencerrages supported a rival claimant, Sa'd ibn Ali. In 1455 Muhammad XI was defeated, captured and executed, and Sa'd took the throne. His brief reign came during the final decades of the emirate, which was steadily weakened by dynastic infighting and Castilian pressure before its fall in 1492.
