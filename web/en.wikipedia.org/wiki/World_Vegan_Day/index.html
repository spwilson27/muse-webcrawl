World Vegan Day is an annual event celebrated by vegans around the world every 1 November. The benefits of veganism for animals, humans and the natural environment are celebrated through activities such as setting up stalls, hosting potlucks and planting memorial trees.

History

The event was established in 1994 by Louise Wallis, then Chair of The Vegan Society in the United Kingdom, to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the founding of the society and the coining of the terms "vegan" and "veganism". Speaking in 2011, Wallis said the date was chosen because 1 November is also the Day of the Dead and Samhain, traditional feasting and memorial days. The Vegan Society had been founded in November 1944 by Donald Watson and others who split from the Vegetarian Society.

Observance

World Vegan Day marks the beginning of World Vegan Month, during which vegan organisations run outreach campaigns, restaurants offer special menus and individuals may take month-long vegan pledge challenges. In some countries the day coincides with public festivals and marches.
