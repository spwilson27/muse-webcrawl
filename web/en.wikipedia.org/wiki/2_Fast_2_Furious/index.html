2 Fast 2 Furious

2 Fast 2 Furious is a 2003 American action film directed by John Singleton and written by Michael Brandt and Derek Haas. It is the sequel to The Fast and the Furious (2001) and the second installment in the Fast & Furious franchise. The film stars Paul Walker as former police officer Brian O'Conner, alongside Tyrese Gibson, Eva Mendes, Cole Hauser, Chris "Ludacris" Bridges, and James Remar.

Plot

Now a fugitive street racer in Miami, Brian O'Conner is recruited by the FBI and given a chance to clear his record: he must go undercover with childhood friend Roman Pearce (Gibson) to bring down drug lord Carter Verone (Hauser), who uses street racers to launder money. Undercover agent Monica Fuentes (Mendes) aids the pair as the operation builds to a climactic run to the Florida Keys.

Reception

The film grossed over $236 million worldwide against a budget of about $76 million. Critical reception was mixed to negative, though it established Tyrese Gibson's Roman Pearce as a franchise regular and helped cement the series' Miami street-racing identity.
