List of World Heritage Sites in Turkey

UNESCO designates World Heritage Sites of outstanding universal value. Turkey accepted the World Heritage Convention in 1983, and its first sites were inscribed in 1985. As of 2023, Turkey has 21 World Heritage Sites, 19 cultural and two mixed (cultural and natural), with a further large number of sites on its tentative list — one of the longest in the world.
