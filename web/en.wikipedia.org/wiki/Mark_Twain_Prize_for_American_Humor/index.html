Mark Twain Prize for American Humor

The Mark Twain Prize for American Humor is an American award for humor presented by the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts in Washington, D.C. annually since 1998. It recognizes individuals who have had an impact on American society in ways similar to the distinguished 19th-century novelist and humorist Mark Twain.

History

The prize was created by the Kennedy Center and first awarded to comedian Richard Pryor in 1998. Recipients receive a copy of an 1884 bronze bust of Twain sculpted by Karl Gerhardt.

Recipients

Honorees have included Jonathan Winters, Whoopi Goldberg, Bob Newhart, Lily Tomlin, Lorne Michaels, Steve Martin, Billy Crystal, George Carlin, Tina Fey, Will Ferrell, Ellen DeGeneres, Jay Leno, Eddie Murphy, Bill Murray, Dave Chappelle, Jon Stewart and Adam Sandler.

The ceremony

The award is presented at a gala performance at the Kennedy Center, featuring tributes and performances by the recipient's peers, later broadcast on television.
