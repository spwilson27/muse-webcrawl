Ray Mears

Raymond Paul Mears (born 7 February 1964) is a British woodsman, instructor, and television presenter known for his expertise in bushcraft and survival skills. His programmes emphasize traditional skills and the knowledge of indigenous peoples around the world.

Born7 February 1964, Kenley, Surrey, England
OccupationBushcraft instructor, author, TV presenter
Known forRay Mears' Bushcraft, Woodlore school

Career

Mears founded Woodlore, the School of Wilderness Bushcraft, in 1983, one of the first such schools in Britain. He came to prominence through the BBC series Tracks and went on to present Ray Mears' Bushcraft, Extreme Survival, Wild Food, Northern Wilderness, and Survival with Ray Mears, travelling to deserts, jungles, and Arctic regions to learn skills from local communities. He has written numerous books on bushcraft and has advised military and emergency services on survival matters.

Approach

Mears distinguishes bushcraft — the knowledge and skills for living comfortably in the natural world — from short-term military survival, stressing craft, natural history, and respect for the environment and for indigenous knowledge holders.
