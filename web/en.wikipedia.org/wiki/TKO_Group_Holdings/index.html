TKO Group Holdings, Inc. is an American sports and entertainment holding company formed in September 2023 through the merger of WWE (World Wrestling Entertainment) with the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), which was owned by Endeavor Group Holdings. Endeavor retained a controlling 51% stake in the new public company, with former WWE shareholders holding 49%; Vince McMahon served as executive chairman until his resignation in January 2024, after which Ari Emanuel combined the roles of chairman and chief executive.

Operations

TKO operates two principal segments: WWE, the professional wrestling promotion headed by president Nick Khan and chief content officer Paul "Triple H" Levesque, and the UFC, run by Dana White. The company negotiates media-rights deals for both properties—among them WWE's flagship show Raw moving to Netflix in 2025. In October 2024 TKO agreed to acquire IMG, On Location and Professional Bull Riders from Endeavor in an all-stock deal valued at about $3.25 billion, completed in 2025, broadening TKO into sports consulting, hospitality and additional league operations.

Name

The name TKO derives from "technical knockout", a term common to both boxing/MMA and wrestling. The company's stock trades on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker TKO.
