Steven Spielberg filmography

Steven Spielberg is an American filmmaker. His diverse and successful career includes work as a director, producer and screenwriter. Since his directorial debut with The Sugarland Express (1974), Spielberg has directed numerous critically acclaimed and commercially successful films, many considered among the greatest ever made.

Spielberg won the Academy Award for Best Director for Schindler's List (1993) and Saving Private Ryan (1998). His films have grossed over $10 billion worldwide, making him the most commercially successful director in history. He co-founded Amblin Entertainment (1981) and DreamWorks Pictures (1994), producing films including the Back to the Future, Men in Black, and Transformers series.
