The Spanish royal family, a branch of the House of Bourbon, consists of the current king, Felipe VI, his wife Queen Letizia, and the close relatives who hold official status under Spanish law. The "royal family" in the strict legal sense is defined by Royal Decree 2917/1981 and subsequent reforms, and is narrower than the broader "family of the King," which includes the king's sisters, their children, and his parents.

Members

The core royal family comprises:

  • King Felipe VI (born 1968), who acceded on 19 June 2014 following the abdication of his father.
  • Queen Letizia (born 1972), a former journalist, married to Felipe since 2004.
  • Leonor, Princess of Asturias (born 2005), heir presumptive; she swore the oath to the Constitution on her 18th birthday, 31 October 2023.
  • Infanta Sofía (born 2007), second in the line of succession.

Also holding royal status are the king's parents:

  • King Juan Carlos I (born 1938), king from 1975 until his abdication in 2014; he left Spain to live in the United Arab Emirates in 2020 amid financial investigations, which were later closed without charges.
  • Queen Sofía (born 1938), born a princess of Greece and Denmark.

The wider family

The extended family includes the king's elder sisters, Infanta Elena, Duchess of Lugo (born 1963), and Infanta Cristina (born 1965), and their children. Infanta Cristina and her husband Iñaki Urdangarin were central figures in the Nóos case corruption scandal; Urdangarin was convicted of embezzlement and influence peddling in 2017 and imprisoned. Following the scandals, Felipe VI undertook reforms to the royal household's transparency, and in 2020 renounced any personal inheritance from his father and withdrew Juan Carlos's royal allowance.

Line of succession

Succession follows male-preference cognatic primogeniture under Article 57 of the Constitution. After Leonor and Sofía, the line continues with Infanta Elena and her children, then Infanta Cristina and her children, followed by more distant Bourbon relatives.

Titles and styles

Children of the monarch and of the heir hold the dignity of Infante or Infanta of Spain with the style Royal Highness. The heir to the throne bears the titles Prince/Princess of Asturias, of Girona, and of Viana, among others.
