Tardis (disambiguation)

TARDIS most commonly refers to the time machine and spacecraft in the BBC television series Doctor Who, piloted by the Doctor.

Tardis or TARDIS may also refer to:

  • TARDIS (transport system) — other fictional and real uses of the acronym.
  • Tardis, a data system / tardis in astronomy software contexts.
  • TARDIS, a radiative transfer code used in astrophysics for supernova spectra — see astronomical spectroscopy.
  • Places and projects named in homage to the Doctor Who craft.
