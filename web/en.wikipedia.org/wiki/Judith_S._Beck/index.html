Judith S. Beck

Judith S. Beck (born May 5, 1954) is an American psychologist known for her work in cognitive therapy and cognitive behavioral therapy. She is the daughter of Aaron Beck, the founder of cognitive therapy, and president of the Beck Institute for Cognitive Behavior Therapy in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania.

Career

Beck earned her doctorate from the University of Pennsylvania, where she is a clinical associate professor of psychology in psychiatry. Her textbook Cognitive Behavior Therapy: Basics and Beyond is widely used in training clinicians. She has also written popular works applying cognitive therapy to weight management, including The Beck Diet Solution. At the Beck Institute she oversees training for health and mental health professionals worldwide.
