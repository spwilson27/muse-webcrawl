Mission: Impossible (film series)

Mission: Impossible
Starring: Tom Cruise
First film: 1996
Studio: Paramount Pictures

Mission: Impossible is a series of American action spy films, based on the 1966 television series of the same name created by Bruce Geller. The series follows Ethan Hunt, an agent of the Impossible Missions Force (IMF), played by Tom Cruise in all instalments.

Films

The series began with Mission: Impossible (1996), directed by Brian De Palma, and includes Mission: Impossible 2 (2000), Mission: Impossible III (2006), Ghost Protocol (2011), Rogue Nation (2015), Fallout (2018), and Dead Reckoning (2023).

Reception

The franchise is noted for Cruise's increasingly elaborate practical stunts, including climbing the Burj Khalifa and hanging from an airborne aircraft, and has grossed billions of dollars worldwide.
