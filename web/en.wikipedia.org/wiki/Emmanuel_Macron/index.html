Emmanuel Jean-Michel Frédéric Macron (born 21 December 1977) is a French politician who has served as President of France since 2017. He is the youngest president in French history and the first born after the establishment of the Fifth Republic. As president he is also ex officio Co-Prince of Andorra.

Early life and career

Macron was born in Amiens, the son of two physicians. He studied philosophy at Paris Nanterre University, earned a master's in public affairs at Sciences Po, and graduated from the École nationale d'administration (ÉNA) in 2004. He joined the Inspectorate General of Finances, then moved into investment banking at Rothschild & Cie, where he became a partner. Under President François Hollande he served as deputy secretary-general of the Élysée (2012–2014) and then as Minister of the Economy, Industry and Digital Affairs (2014–2016), where he sponsored the pro-business "Macron Law."

Presidency

In April 2016 Macron founded the centrist political movement En Marche!, resigned from the government, and announced his candidacy for the 2017 presidential election. Running as a pro-European centrist, he defeated Marine Le Pen of the National Front in the runoff with about 66% of the vote. His movement, renamed La République En Marche!, won a National Assembly majority in the ensuing legislative elections.

Macron's first term featured labour-law reform, a reduction of the wealth tax, and the yellow vests protests of 2018–2019, which forced concessions on fuel taxes and purchasing power. He pursued deeper European Union integration and an assertive foreign policy. He was re-elected in April 2022, again defeating Le Pen, by about 58.5% to 41.5% — the first French president in twenty years to win a second term while holding a parliamentary majority bid. His second term was dominated by a contested pension reform raising the retirement age from 62 to 64, enacted in 2023 amid mass protests, and by political fragmentation after his party lost its absolute majority in 2022 and after the snap legislative elections of 2024.
