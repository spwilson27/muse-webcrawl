Nuclear navy

A nuclear navy, or nuclear-powered navy, is a navy that operates vessels — principally submarines and aircraft carriers — propelled by nuclear reactors.

Advantages

Nuclear propulsion gives submarines effectively unlimited submerged endurance limited only by crew supplies, and gives carriers high speed without refuelling for over 20 years. The first nuclear submarine was USS Nautilus, launched in 1954 under Admiral Hyman Rickover's program.

Operators

The United States, Russia, China, France, the United Kingdom and India operate nuclear-powered vessels; Brazil and Australia (under AUKUS) pursue nuclear submarines.
