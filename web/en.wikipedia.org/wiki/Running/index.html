Running

Running
DefinitionGait in which both feet leave the ground; sport and exercise
EventsSprints to ultramarathons; marathon 42.195 km
[Image: Marathon runners filling a city avenue at sunrise]

Running is a method of terrestrial locomotion allowing humans to move rapidly on foot, and one of the most popular sports and forms of exercise worldwide — from school sprints to the marathon (42.195 km) and 100-mile ultramarathons.

Running boom

Mass-participation racing grew from the 1970s jogging boom; city marathons (Boston, London, New York, Berlin, Tokyo, Chicago) draw tens of thousands, and parkrun's free weekly 5ks spread globally. Running's minimal equipment — shoes and a road — is its democratic charm.
