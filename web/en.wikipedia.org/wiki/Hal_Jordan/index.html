Harold "Hal" Jordan, also known as Green Lantern, is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer John Broome and artist Gil Kane, he first appeared in Showcase #22 in 1959 as the Silver Age reinvention of the Green Lantern concept. He is a founding member of the Justice League and one of the most prominent members of the Green Lantern Corps.

Fictional character biography

Hal Jordan is a test pilot for Ferris Aircraft in Coast City. Chosen by the power ring of the dying alien Lantern Abin Sur, Jordan becomes the Green Lantern of Sector 2814, wielding a ring powered by willpower that can create solid-light constructs limited only by his imagination. Trained by Kilowog and initially mentored — and later opposed — by Sinestro, Jordan became renowned as one of the greatest Lanterns.

In the 1994 storyline "Emerald Twilight", driven to madness by the destruction of Coast City, Jordan destroyed the Corps and became the villain Parallax. He later redeemed himself by sacrificing his life to reignite the Sun in The Final Night, served as host of the Spectre, and was resurrected in Green Lantern: Rebirth (2004), which revealed his villainy had been caused by possession by the fear entity Parallax.

Powers and abilities

Jordan's power ring grants flight, life support, translation, and the ability to form energy constructs. It is powered by his willpower and must be recharged periodically at a power battery. As one of the strongest-willed Lanterns, his constructs are exceptionally powerful.

In other media

Hal Jordan was portrayed by Ryan Reynolds in the 2011 film Green Lantern and has appeared in numerous animated series and DC animated films, voiced by actors including Nathan Fillion and Josh Keaton.
