Stephanie

Stephanie is a female given name derived from the Greek name Stephanos (Στέφανος), meaning "crown" or "garland", via the French form Stéphanie. It is the feminine counterpart of Stephen.

Popularity

The name became popular in the English-speaking world in the twentieth century, reaching its peak in the United States in the 1970s and 1980s, when it was consistently among the most common names for newborn girls. Variants include Stefanie, Steffanie and the diminutives Steph, Steffi and Steff.

Notable people

Well-known bearers include Princess Stéphanie of Monaco; Stephanie, Hereditary Grand Duchess of Luxembourg; the singer Stefani Germanotta, known as Lady Gaga; the author Stephenie Meyer; and the wrestler and executive Stephanie McMahon.

Fictional characters

Stephanie appears as a character name in numerous works, including Stephanie Forrester in The Bold and the Beautiful and Stephanie Tanner in Full House.
