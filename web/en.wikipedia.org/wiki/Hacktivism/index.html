Hacktivism

Hacktivism (a portmanteau of hack and activism) is the use of computer hacking techniques to promote a political agenda or social change. Tactics range from website defacement and virtual sit-ins to information leaks and distributed denial-of-service attacks presented as protest.

History and groups

The term emerged in the 1990s around groups such as Cult of the Dead Cow and the Electronic Disturbance Theater. In the 2000s and 2010s the decentralised collective Anonymous became its most prominent face, alongside groups like LulzSec and Telecomix. Debate continues over whether hacktivism is civil disobedience or criminal vandalism.
