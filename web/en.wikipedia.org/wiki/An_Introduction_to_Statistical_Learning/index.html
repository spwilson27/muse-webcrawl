An Introduction to Statistical Learning (often abbreviated ISLR or ISL) is a textbook on statistical learning and machine learning by Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie and Robert Tibshirani, first published by Springer in 2013. It is a more accessible companion to the authors' advanced text The Elements of Statistical Learning.

Content

The book introduces the main tools of statistical learning with an emphasis on practical application rather than mathematical theory. Topics include linear regression, classification, resampling methods, shrinkage methods such as ridge regression and the lasso, tree-based methods, support vector machines, clustering, and — in the second edition — deep learning, survival analysis and multiple testing. Each chapter includes worked examples and laboratory exercises; the first edition used the R programming language, and companion versions provide Python code.

Reception and availability

ISL has been adopted widely in university courses and is frequently recommended as a first text on machine learning. The authors make the book available free of charge as a PDF from the book's website, which has contributed to its popularity among students and practitioners.
