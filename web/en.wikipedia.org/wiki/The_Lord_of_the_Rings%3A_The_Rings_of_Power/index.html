The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is an American fantasy television series developed by J. D. Payne and Patrick McKay for Amazon Prime Video. Based on the appendices of J. R. R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings, it is set in the Second Age of Middle-earth, thousands of years before the original trilogy.

Premise

The series follows an ensemble of Elves, Dwarves, Harfoots and Men as evil resurfaces in Middle-earth: Galadriel hunts the vanished enemy Sauron, while the Rings of Power are forged and the island kingdom of Númenor rises and begins its fall.

Production

Amazon acquired the television rights in 2017 for a reported $250 million, committing to five seasons and making it the most expensive television series ever produced. Season one was filmed in New Zealand; production later moved to the United Kingdom. Reception has been mixed-to-positive from critics and more divided among audiences.
