Desmos

Desmos is an advanced graphing calculator implemented as a web application, founded by Eli Luberoff in 2011. It allows users to graph functions, plot data, evaluate equations, and explore transformations interactively.

Features

The calculator supports functions, inequalities, parametric and polar curves, regressions, sliders, and lists, and is widely used in mathematics education. Desmos also produces a scientific calculator and geometry tool, and runs an annual international math art contest. In 2022 Desmos was acquired by Amplify.
