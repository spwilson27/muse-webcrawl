Red Dot

Red Dot most commonly refers to the Red Dot Design Award, an international design prize awarded by the Design Zentrum Nordrhein Westfalen in Essen, Germany. The term may also refer to several other topics.

Red Dot Design Award

The award was founded in 1955 as an industrial design prize and is today one of the world's largest and most recognized design competitions. It is awarded in three disciplines: Product Design, Brands & Communication Design, and Design Concept. Winning entries receive the Red Dot quality seal and are exhibited in Red Dot Design Museums in Essen, Singapore, and Xiamen. The highest distinctions are the "Red Dot: Best of the Best" and, for product design, selection as part of the Design Team of the Year.

Other uses

  • Red dot sight — a type of reflector sight for firearms and cameras that projects a red aiming dot onto a lens.
  • "Little red dot" — a nickname for Singapore, originating from a remark about its small size on maps.
  • Red Dot (2021) — a Swedish thriller film.
