Tesla Model Y

The Tesla Model Y is a battery electric compact crossover SUV manufactured by Tesla, Inc. Unveiled by CEO Elon Musk in March 2019, it entered production at Tesla's Fremont factory in January 2020, with first deliveries beginning in March 2020. The Model Y shares roughly three quarters of its parts with the Model 3 sedan, including its platform, battery packs and electric motors, but sits higher and offers an optional third row of seating for up to seven occupants.

History

Tesla had hinted at a crossover companion to the Model 3 since 2013, completing the "S3XY" naming pattern of its passenger vehicles. The production version was revealed on 14 March 2019 at Tesla's design studio in Hawthorne, California. Production later expanded to Gigafactory Shanghai, Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg and Gigafactory Texas, making the Model Y Tesla's highest-volume vehicle.

Sales

In 2023 the Model Y became the world's best-selling car of any kind, with roughly 1.23 million units sold, the first electric vehicle to top global annual sales charts. It repeated the feat in 2024.

Design and specifications

The Model Y offers a minimalist interior dominated by a 15-inch touchscreen, a panoramic glass roof, and ranges between roughly 260 and 330 miles depending on variant. Variants have included Standard Range, Long Range and Performance trims. A redesigned version, codenamed "Juniper", was launched in early 2025 with revised styling, a light bar, and an improved interior.
