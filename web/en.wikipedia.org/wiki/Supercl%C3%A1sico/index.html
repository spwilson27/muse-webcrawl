Superclásico

The Superclásico is the name given to the football rivalry between the two most popular Argentine clubs, Boca Juniors and River Plate, both of Buenos Aires. It is regarded as one of the fiercest and most famous derbies in world football.

Origins

Both clubs originated in the La Boca neighbourhood of Buenos Aires in the early 20th century; River Plate later moved to the wealthier Núñez district, adding a class dimension to the rivalry. Boca traditionally draws support from the working class, River from the middle and upper classes, reflected in the nicknames "Xeneizes" and "Millonarios".

History and notable matches

The first official match was played in 1913. The rivalry has featured hundreds of meetings in league, cup and continental competition. In 2018 the two sides met in the Copa Libertadores final — the first between Argentine clubs — with the second leg controversially moved to Madrid after crowd violence; River Plate won the trophy. The fixture regularly tops lists of matches football fans should see before they die.
