Frost (disambiguation)

Frost most commonly refers to a deposit of ice crystals on surfaces exposed to sub-freezing air. It may also refer to many other things:

Science and weather

  • Frost — ice formed when water vapour deposits on a cold surface
  • Hoar frost, rime ice, and frost flowers — related ice formations
  • Frost (temperature) — air temperatures at or below freezing, and the associated frost dates used in gardening
  • Frostbite — freezing injury to body tissues
  • Frost heaving and frost weathering in geology

People

  • Robert Frost (1874–1963), American poet, author of "The Road Not Taken" and "Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening"
  • David Frost (1939–2013), British broadcaster known for the Nixon interviews
  • Nick Frost (born 1972), English actor and comedian
  • Frost (rapper), American rapper (Kid Frost)

Arts and media

  • Frost (1964), a novel by Thomas Bernhard
  • Frost/Nixon, a 2006 play by Peter Morgan and its 2008 film adaptation
  • Frost, a DC Comics character (Killer Frost) and a Marvel Comics character (Emma Frost)
  • Jack Frost, the personification of frost and winter

Places

  • Frost, Minnesota; Frost, Texas; and Frost, West Virginia — communities in the United States
  • Frost (crater), a lunar crater
