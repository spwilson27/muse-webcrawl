Giga Berlin

Location: Grünheide, Brandenburg, Germany

Opened: March 22, 2022

Operator: Tesla, Inc.

Products: Tesla Model Y, battery cells

Employees: ~12,000

Tesla Giga Berlin (also known as Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg and Gigafactory 4) is a manufacturing plant for electric vehicles and batteries operated by Tesla, Inc. in Grünheide, Brandenburg, Germany, near Berlin. It is Tesla's first manufacturing location in Europe and produces the Model Y crossover for the European market.

History

Tesla CEO Elon Musk announced the factory in November 2019 at an awards ceremony in Berlin, surprising observers after a competitive site selection among several European countries. Construction began in early 2020 under preliminary permits while the full environmental approval process continued. The project cleared a pine forest plantation at the site and proceeded despite legal challenges from local environmental groups concerning water usage and protected species.

The state of Brandenburg granted final approval on March 4, 2022, and the factory was officially opened on March 22, 2022, with Musk handing over the first 30 German-built Model Y vehicles to customers. Chancellor Olaf Scholz attended the opening.

Production

The plant includes a press shop with some of the largest aluminium die-casting machines ("Giga Press") in automotive use, body shop, paint shop (described by Musk as the world's most advanced), general assembly, and battery cell and pack production using Tesla's 4680-format cells. Its initial annual capacity target was 500,000 vehicles, expandable to over one million with additional phases, together with up to 100 GWh of battery cell output.

Controversies

The factory has faced persistent disputes over water consumption in the drought-prone region, rejected expansion votes in a local referendum, and protests including a 2024 arson attack on a nearby power pylon that halted production for several days. Tesla has nonetheless continued an expansion application for the site.
