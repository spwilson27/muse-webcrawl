Powered wheelchair


A mid-wheel-drive powered wheelchair

A powered wheelchair, also called an electric wheelchair or motorised wheelchair, is a wheelchair that is propelled by means of an electric motor and navigational controls, usually a small joystick mounted on the armrest, rather than by manual power.

History

The first commercially successful powered wheelchair was developed in the 1950s by Canadian inventor George Klein and his team at the National Research Council of Canada, originally to assist veterans injured in the Second World War.

Design

Powered wheelchairs are usually four-wheeled or six-wheeled and non-folding, though some folding designs exist. Drive configurations include front-wheel, mid-wheel and rear-wheel drive, each offering different handling characteristics. Power sources are typically rechargeable deep-cycle batteries, and specialised seating systems can be fitted for users with significant postural needs.
