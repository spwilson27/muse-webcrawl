Roh Tae-woo

Roh Tae-woo (December 4, 1932 – October 26, 2021) was a South Korean politician and army general who served as the sixth president of South Korea from 1988 to 1993.

A member of the Hanahoe military faction and ally of Chun Doo-hwan, Roh participated in the 1979 coup and the suppression surrounding the 1980 Gwangju Uprising. Designated Chun's successor, his June 29 Declaration of 1987 accepted direct presidential elections amid mass pro-democracy protests, and he won the December 1987 election.

As president he pursued Nordpolitik, establishing relations with the Soviet Union (1990) and China (1992), oversaw the 1988 Seoul Olympics, and saw both Koreas join the United Nations in 1991. After leaving office he was convicted in 1996 of treason, mutiny and corruption for his role in the coup and Gwangju, sentenced to prison, and pardoned in 1997.
