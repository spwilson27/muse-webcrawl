Lopar is a village and municipality in Primorje-Gorski Kotar County, in western Croatia, occupying the northern peninsula of the island of Rab in the Adriatic Sea. Unlike most of the Croatian coast, which is rocky, the Lopar peninsula is known for its sandy beaches — the area has more than twenty of them.

Tourism

The best known is Rajska plaža ("Paradise Beach"), a roughly 1.5 km shallow sandy beach that has been a popular family resort since the early 20th century and regularly flies a Blue Flag. San Marino tourist resort and a large campsite are located there. The sheltered, shallow bays around Lopar are well suited to children and to naturism — the Sahara beach near Lopar is among the Adriatic's recognised naturist beaches.

Tradition

Local tradition holds that Saint Marinus, the stonemason who founded San Marino, was born in Lopar, and the village celebrates the connection with its Italian twin city. Lopar is connected to the mainland by ferry from Valbiska on Krk to Lopar port, as well as via the town of Rab.
