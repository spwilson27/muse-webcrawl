Nohsngithiang Falls

Nohsngithiang Falls
Location: East Khasi Hills, Meghalaya, India
Type: Segmented
Total height: 315 m (1,033 ft)
Number of drops: 1

Nohsngithiang Falls (also known as the Seven Sisters Falls and Mawsmai Falls) is a segmented waterfall located in the East Khasi Hills district of Meghalaya, India, about one kilometre south of the village of Mawsmai near Cherrapunji (Sohra).

Description

The falls plunge over the limestone cliffs of the Khasi Hills in seven distinct segments, which give the falls their popular name, the Seven Sisters — a reference also associated with the seven states of Northeast India. With a height of about 315 metres (1,033 ft) and an average width of about 70 metres (230 ft), Nohsngithiang is one of the tallest waterfalls in India.

Season

The falls are fed by rainwater and are at their most spectacular during the monsoon season, when the Cherrapunji plateau receives some of the heaviest rainfall on Earth. During the dry season the flow diminishes considerably and the segmented streams may nearly disappear.
