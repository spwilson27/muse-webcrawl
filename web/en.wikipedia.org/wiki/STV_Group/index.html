STV Group plc (previously known as SMG plc and Scottish Television) is a Scottish media company based at Pacific Quay in Glasgow. It operates the STV television channel, which holds the Channel 3 (ITV network) licences for central and northern Scotland, as well as the STV Player streaming service and the production arm STV Studios.

History

Scottish Television began broadcasting in 1957. The company expanded under the name SMG (Scottish Media Group) in the 1990s, acquiring Grampian Television (the northern Scotland franchise) in 1997, the Herald newspaper group, and for a period Virgin Radio. After divesting its newspaper, cinema advertising and radio assets, the company refocused on television and rebranded as STV Group in 2008. The two Scottish Channel 3 regions were unified under the single STV brand.

Operations

STV broadcasts ITV network programming alongside Scottish news and current affairs, led by STV News at Six and Scotland Tonight. STV Studios produces programmes for broadcasters in the UK and internationally, including the game shows Catchphrase and Bridge of Lies and drama productions. The STV Player is the group's streaming and catch-up service.
