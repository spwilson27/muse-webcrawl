Temporomandibular joint dysfunction

Temporomandibular joint dysfunction (TMD, TMJD) is an umbrella term covering pain and dysfunction of the muscles of mastication and the temporomandibular joints, which connect the mandible to the skull. Symptoms include jaw pain, restricted jaw movement, headaches, ear pain and clicking or locking of the joint.

SpecialtyDentistry, oral and maxillofacial surgery
SymptomsJaw pain, clicking, limited opening

Causes

Causes are multifactorial and debated; contributing factors include bruxism (teeth grinding), trauma, malocclusion, stress and joint disorders such as disc displacement and osteoarthritis. Most cases are self-limiting or fluctuating.

Treatment

Management is usually conservative: education, soft diet, physiotherapy, occlusal splints and medication. Irreversible treatments and surgery are reserved for severe, refractory cases.
