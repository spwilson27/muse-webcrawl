RefSeq

Image placeholder: RefSeq provides curated reference sequences.

The Reference Sequence (RefSeq) collection is a publicly available, curated database of genomic, transcript and protein sequences maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) of the United States National Library of Medicine. It aims to provide, for each organism, a single non-redundant reference example of naturally occurring molecules, from chromosomes to mRNAs and proteins.

Features

RefSeq records are distinguished by accession prefixes such as NC_ for chromosomes, NM_ for protein-coding transcripts, NR_ for non-coding transcripts and NP_ for proteins. Unlike the archival GenBank database, RefSeq records are curated and updated by NCBI staff and collaborators, with a defined review status, and they integrate information about genes, transcripts, variants and clinical relevance.

Uses

The collection underpins genome annotation for model organisms, clinical variant reporting, comparative genomics and the design of sequencing assays. It is closely coordinated with the NCBI Gene database and with international partners through the INSDC.
