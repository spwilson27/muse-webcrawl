Pokémon Go

Pokémon Go
Developer: Niantic
Publisher: Niantic / The Pokémon Company
Released: July 2016
Platforms: iOS, Android
Genre: Augmented reality, location-based

Pokémon Go is a 2016 augmented reality (AR) mobile game developed and published by Niantic in collaboration with Nintendo and The Pokémon Company for iOS and Android. Using GPS and the device camera, players locate, capture, train, and battle virtual Pokémon that appear in the player's real-world surroundings.

Gameplay

Players create an avatar and explore a map based on their actual location, where PokéStops (often at landmarks) provide items and Gyms host battles. Pokémon spawn in the environment and are caught by throwing Poké Balls; later updates added trading, player-versus-player battles, raids against powerful Pokémon, and special events such as Community Days.

Release and impact

Released in July 2016, the game became a global phenomenon: it was downloaded more than 500 million times by the end of 2016 and surpassed one billion downloads by 2019, generating billions of dollars in revenue. It popularised location-based AR gaming, drove unusual increases in physical activity among players, and also prompted public-safety controversies and legal questions about virtual items placed on private or sensitive property.
