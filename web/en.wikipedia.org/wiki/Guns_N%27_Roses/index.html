Guns N' Roses

Origin: Los Angeles, California, US

Formed: 1985

Genre: Hard rock, heavy metal

Key members: Axl Rose, Slash, Duff McKagan

Guns N' Roses is an American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1985, fronted by singer Axl Rose with lead guitarist Slash and bassist Duff McKagan. Their 1987 debut album Appetite for Destruction became the best-selling debut album of all time in the United States, powered by songs including "Sweet Child o' Mine", "Welcome to the Jungle" and "Paradise City".

Career

The band followed with G N' R Lies (1988) and the twin albums Use Your Illusion I and II (1991), whose stadium tour and hits like "November Rain" cemented their fame. Line-up turmoil in the 1990s left Rose as the sole original member; the long-delayed Chinese Democracy appeared in 2008. Slash and McKagan rejoined in 2016 for the hugely successful Not in This Lifetime... Tour.

Legacy

Guns N' Roses were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012 and have sold more than 100 million records worldwide.
