TriStar Pictures, Inc. is an American film studio and production company that is a member of the Sony Pictures Motion Picture Group, part of Sony Pictures Entertainment. Founded in 1982 as a joint venture between Columbia Pictures, HBO and CBS, it was created when the three companies pooled resources to share the growing costs of film production.

History

TriStar's first release was The Natural (1984). CBS left the venture in 1985 and HBO in 1986; Columbia (and later Sony, which bought Columbia Pictures Entertainment in 1989) consolidated ownership, producing the combined "Columbia TriStar" branding. The studio's winged-horse Pegasus logo debuted in the 1990s and became one of the most recognized studio emblems.

Notable films

TriStar released numerous successful films, including Rambo: First Blood Part II (via Carolco), About Last Night, Steel Magnolias, Terminator 2: Judgment Day (distribution), Sleepless in Seattle, Philadelphia, Jerry Maguire (co-production), As Good as It Gets, The Mask of Zorro, Godzilla (1998) and, in later years under a revived specialty banner, Baby Driver (distribution), A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood and The Woman King.

The label today

After a period focused mainly on distributing specialty and acquired titles, TriStar was relaunched in 2013 as a production label under Sony, making films in partnership with other financiers alongside its theatrical distribution role.
