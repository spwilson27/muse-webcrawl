Mjøsa

Lake Mjøsa near Hamar

Mjøsa is Norway's largest lake, located about 100 km north of Oslo in the counties of Innlandet and Akershus. It covers some 365–369 km², stretches about 117 km from Lillehammer in the north to Minnesund in the south, and reaches a maximum depth of around 449–453 m. The main inflow is the Gudbrandsdalslågen river; the lake drains via the Vorma into the Glomma.

Use and notable features

The towns of Lillehammer, Gjøvik and Hamar lie on its shores. The lake is home to the paddle steamer Skibladner, launched in 1856 and the world's oldest paddle steamer still in scheduled service. Mjøsa is an important drinking-water source and supports a notable fishery, including large brown trout.
