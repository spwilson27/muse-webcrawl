Stamford Bridge is a football stadium in Fulham, London, and the home ground of Chelsea Football Club. Opened in 1877 as an athletics venue, it was offered to Fulham F.C. before being taken over by the newly formed Chelsea in 1905.

History

The stadium once held huge crowds — its record attendance is 82,905 for a match against Arsenal in 1935 — and hosted FA Cup finals in the 1920s. Financial troubles in the 1970s and 1980s, when the club lost ownership of the freehold, were resolved after a long campaign by the Chelsea Pitch Owners.

Stands

The four stands are the Matthew Harding Stand, the East Stand, the Shed End and the West Stand. Long-discussed plans to redevelop or replace the stadium remain unresolved.
