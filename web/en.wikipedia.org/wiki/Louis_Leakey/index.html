Louis Leakey

Louis Seymour Bazett Leakey (7 August 1903 – 1 October 1972) was a Kenyan-British palaeoanthropologist and archaeologist whose work at Olduvai Gorge in Tanzania demonstrated that humans evolved in Africa.

Louis Leakey
Born: 1903, Kabete, British East Africa
Died: 1972, London
Field: Palaeoanthropology
Spouse: Mary Leakey
Known for: Olduvai Gorge hominin fossils

Work

With his wife Mary Leakey, he excavated Zinjanthropus boisei (now Paranthropus boisei, 1959) and Homo habilis, transforming understanding of early human evolution. He mentored the primatologists Jane Goodall, Dian Fossey and Biruté Galdikas — "Leakey's Angels".

Legacy

The Leakey family's second and third generations, including Richard Leakey, continued major fossil discoveries in Kenya's Turkana Basin.
