Joseph Frank Pesci (born February 9, 1943) is an American actor and musician. He is known for portraying tough, volatile characters in a variety of genres, and for his collaborations with Martin Scorsese and Robert De Niro in the films Raging Bull (1980), Goodfellas (1990), Casino (1995), and The Irishman (2019). He won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his role as the psychopathic gangster Tommy DeVito in Goodfellas.

Career

A child actor and musician from Newark, New Jersey, Pesci was cast by De Niro's recommendation in Raging Bull, earning his first Oscar nomination. After further acclaim in Once Upon a Time in America (1984) and Goodfellas, he demonstrated a flair for comedy in Home Alone (1990), Home Alone 2 (1992), and My Cousin Vinny (1992), and played Leo Getz in the Lethal Weapon sequels. Pesci announced his retirement from acting in 1999 to pursue music, releasing jazz albums, but returned for De Niro's The Good Shepherd (2006) and, prominently, for Scorsese's The Irishman in 2019, earning another Academy Award nomination. He also starred in the comedy series Bupkis (2023).
