Gamla Stan

Gamla Stan (Swedish for 'the Old Town') is the old town of Stockholm, Sweden. It consists primarily of the island Stadsholmen, together with the islets Riddarholmen, Helgeandsholmen and Strömsborg.

Dating from the 13th century, Gamla Stan is criss-crossed by medieval alleyways and cobbled streets. It contains Stockholm Cathedral (Storkyrkan), the Nobel Museum, Riddarholm Church, and the Royal Palace, one of the largest palaces in Europe.

Sights

Stortorget, the main square, was the scene of the Stockholm Bloodbath of 1520. Mårten Trotzigs gränd, the narrowest street in Stockholm, is barely 90 centimetres wide at its smallest point.

The district is one of the best-preserved medieval city centres in Europe and a major tourist attraction.
