Graceland is a mansion on a 13.8-acre estate in Memphis, Tennessee, United States, that was the home of singer Elvis Presley from 1957 until his death in 1977. It is located at 3764 Elvis Presley Boulevard in the Whitehaven community. Presley purchased the property for $102,500 when he was 22 years old.

History

The Colonial Revival house was built in 1939 and named by its original owners after the daughter of the land's earlier owner. Presley extensively remodelled it, adding features including the famous music gates, a swimming pool, and later the Jungle Room and a racquetball building. Presley died at Graceland on August 16, 1977, and is buried there in the Meditation Garden alongside his parents and grandmother.

Museum

Opened to public tours on June 7, 1982, Graceland became one of the most visited private homes in the United States, drawing some 600,000 visitors annually. The mansion's interior is preserved much as it was in 1977, with famous rooms including the Jungle Room, the TV room, and the trophy building displaying Presley's gold records, costumes, and cars. In 2006 Graceland was designated a National Historic Landmark. A large entertainment complex, Elvis Presley's Memphis, opened across the street in 2017.
