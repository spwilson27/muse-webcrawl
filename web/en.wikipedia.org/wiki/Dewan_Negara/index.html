The Dewan Negara (English: Senate; literally "State Council") is the upper house of the Parliament of Malaysia. It consists of 70 senators: 26 elected by the state legislative assemblies (two per state) and 44 appointed by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong (King) on the advice of the prime minister, including representatives of the federal territories and of minority communities.

Functions

The Dewan Negara reviews legislation passed by the lower house, the Dewan Rakyat, and may initiate bills except money bills. Its power is largely revisory and delaying: it cannot ultimately block bills, which may be presented for royal assent after specified delays. Senators serve three-year terms, renewable once, and the chamber is not dissolved when the Dewan Rakyat is.

Membership

Appointed senators are chosen for distinguished public service or to represent special interests, including indigenous peoples (Orang Asli) and ethnic minorities. Members must be at least 30 years old and Malaysian citizens resident in the Federation.
