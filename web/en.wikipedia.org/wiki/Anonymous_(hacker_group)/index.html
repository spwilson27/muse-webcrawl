Anonymous is a decentralized international activist and hacktivist collective. Originating around 2003–2004 on the imageboard 4chan, it has no leadership or membership structure; participants act under a shared name and iconography, most famously the Guy Fawkes mask from the film V for Vendetta. The group is known for cyberattacks against governments, corporations and religious organizations, as well as for street protests.

History

Early Anonymous actions were pranks and raids organized for amusement ("lulz"). The turning point came in 2008 with Project Chanology, a campaign against the Church of Scientology that combined distributed denial-of-service attacks, prank calls and worldwide masked street demonstrations. Subsequent operations supported WikiLeaks (Operation Payback, 2010), the Arab Spring movements, Occupy Wall Street, and campaigns against child exploitation sites.

Methods and splinter groups

Anonymous operations typically employ DDoS attacks using tools such as the Low Orbit Ion Cannon, website defacements, doxing and leaks. Splinter groups including LulzSec and AntiSec broke away in 2011; arrests of key figures such as Hector Monsegur ("Sabu"), who became an FBI informant, led to prosecutions of participants in several countries.

Later activity

Activity has waxed and waned: Anonymous-affiliated accounts declared "cyber war" on the Islamic State after the 2015 Paris attacks, resurfaced during the 2020 George Floyd protests by disrupting police websites, and in 2022 announced operations against Russian government sites following the invasion of Ukraine. Scholars debate whether Anonymous is best understood as a movement, a brand, or a shared pseudonym open to anyone.
