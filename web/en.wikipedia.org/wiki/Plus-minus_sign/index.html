Plus-minus sign

The plus-minus sign, ±, is a mathematical symbol with multiple meanings.

  • In mathematics, it generally indicates a choice of exactly two possible values, one of which is obtained through addition and the other through subtraction.
  • In experimental sciences, it commonly indicates the confidence interval or uncertainty bounding a measurement.
  • In chess notation, ± means White has a clear advantage, and the reversed sign ∓ means Black has a clear advantage.

History and usage

The sign appears in works from the 17th century and was popularized in algebra for expressing both roots of a quadratic equation: x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a. It is encoded in Unicode as U+00B1, and in HTML as &plusmn;.

The related minus-plus sign ∓ is used alongside ± to compactly write expressions such as cos(x ± y) = cos x cos y ∓ sin x sin y.
