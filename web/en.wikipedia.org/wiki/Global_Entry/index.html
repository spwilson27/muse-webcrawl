Global Entry

Administered byU.S. Customs and Border Protection
Launched2008
Membership term5 years

Global Entry is a program of U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) that allows pre-approved, low-risk travelers to receive expedited clearance upon arrival in the United States. Members use automated kiosks at participating airports instead of waiting in standard passport control lines.

Eligibility and enrolment

Applicants — U.S. citizens, lawful permanent residents, and citizens of a number of partner countries — apply online, pay a non-refundable fee, and undergo a background check and in-person interview with fingerprinting at an enrolment centre. Membership lasts five years. Global Entry membership also includes TSA PreCheck benefits for domestic flights.

At the airport

Members proceed to Global Entry kiosks, where they confirm their identity (increasingly by facial comparison rather than passport scan and fingerprints), answer customs declaration questions, and then see a CBP officer before exiting. Misuse or customs violations can lead to revocation of membership.
