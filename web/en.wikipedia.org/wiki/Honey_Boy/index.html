Honey Boy

Honey Boy is a 2019 American drama film directed by Alma Har'el from a screenplay by Shia LaBeouf, based on his childhood and his relationship with his father. LaBeouf plays a character based on his own father, while Noah Jupe and Lucas Hedges play Otis Lort, a stand-in for LaBeouf, at different ages.

Plot

In 2005, actor Otis Lort, after a car crash and arrest, is sent to court-ordered rehab, where he is pressed to confront childhood trauma. Flashbacks to 1995 show 12-year-old Otis, a child actor living in a motel with his volatile father James, a former rodeo clown and recovering addict who serves as his chaperone and manager.

Production

LaBeouf wrote the screenplay while in rehab, initially as therapy. Har'el, who had previously directed the documentary Bombay Beach and collaborated with LaBeouf, made her narrative feature debut. FKA Twigs appears as a neighbour who befriends young Otis.

Reception

The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival on January 25, 2019, and was released by Amazon Studios. It received positive reviews for its performances and direction, and won a U.S. Dramatic Special Jury Award at Sundance.
