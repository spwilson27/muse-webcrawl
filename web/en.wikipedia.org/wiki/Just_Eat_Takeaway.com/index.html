Just Eat Takeaway.com

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Just Eat Takeaway.com logo]

Just Eat Takeaway.com N.V. is a Dutch multinational online food ordering and delivery company headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands. It was formed in 2020 by the merger of British company Just Eat and Dutch company Takeaway.com, and operates food delivery marketplaces in numerous countries under brands such as Lieferando, Thuisbezorgd.nl, Menulog, SkipTheDishes, and Grubhub (the latter sold to Wonder in 2024–25).

History

Takeaway.com was founded in 2000 by Jitse Groen as Thuisbezorgd.nl, an online takeaway ordering site in the Netherlands. Just Eat was founded in Denmark in 2001 and grew from London into a major international platform, listing on the London Stock Exchange in 2014. Their merger created one of the world's largest online food delivery groups outside China.

Operations

The company connects consumers with local restaurants through websites and apps, earning commissions on orders, and in some markets operates its own delivery courier networks.
