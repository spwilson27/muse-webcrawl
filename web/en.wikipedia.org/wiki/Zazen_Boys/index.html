Zazen Boys (stylised ZAZEN BOYS) is a Japanese rock band formed in 2003 by vocalist and guitarist Shutoku Mukai following the dissolution of his earlier band Number Girl. The group's music combines math-rock rhythmic complexity, post-punk, funk and noise, and its albums are released through Mukai's own Matsuri Studio imprint. Zazen Boys are regarded as one of the most influential Japanese alternative bands of the 2000s.

History

Number Girl, Mukai's previous band, broke up in 2002. In 2003 he formed Zazen Boys with bassist Yoshikane Ichiro, formerly of the band Qunto, and drummer Atsushi Matsushita; Soichiro Nakamura joined as support and later full member. Their self-titled debut album appeared in January 2004, followed the same year by Zazen Boys II. Subsequent albums include Zazen Boys III (2006), Zazen Boys 4 (2008), Suryoumono (2012), Gissiri (2021) and Ra (2024).

Musical style

The band's songs are built on tightly interlocking, syncopated guitar, bass and drum parts, abrupt stops and restarts, and Mukai's declamatory, often chant-like vocals. Lyrics mix abstract imagery, wordplay and scatological humour. Live performances are notable for their precision despite the complexity of the arrangements, and several tracks have become live staples through extended improvisation.

Legacy

Zazen Boys influenced a generation of Japanese math-rock and indie acts, and Mukai's guitar style — dry, percussive and heavily palm-muted — is widely imitated. The band has toured internationally, including appearances in the United States and Europe, while remaining largely independent of major labels.
