Glossary of sumo terms

This is a glossary of terms used in the sport of sumo wrestling in Japan.

Selected terms

Banzuke – the ranking list of wrestlers published before each tournament. Basho – a sumo tournament; six honbasho are held each year. Dohyō – the clay ring in which bouts take place. Gyoji – the referee. Heya – a stable where wrestlers live and train. Kimarite – the winning technique used to decide a bout; the Japan Sumo Association recognises 82 standard kimarite. Makuuchi – the top division. Mawashi – the belt worn by wrestlers. Ozeki – the second-highest rank. Rikishi – a professional sumo wrestler. Tachiai – the initial charge at the start of a bout. Yokozuna – the highest rank, grand champion. Yorikiri – the most common kimarite, forcing the opponent out of the ring while holding the mawashi.
