SUN 'n FUN

SUN 'n FUN Aerospace Expo is a major annual airshow and fly-in held each spring in Lakeland, Florida, United States. Founded in 1974, it is one of the largest aviation conventions in the world, sometimes described as "spring break for pilots", and raises funds for aerospace education through the Aerospace Center for Excellence.

LocationLakeland Linder International Airport, Florida
First held1974
AttendanceRoughly 200,000 (recent years)
OrganiserSUN 'n FUN Fly-In, Inc. / Aerospace Center for Excellence

Event

The week-long expo features thousands of show aircraft, warbird and homebuilt displays, daily airshows with military demonstration teams, hundreds of commercial exhibitors, and forums and workshops on aviation topics. Proceeds support STEM scholarships and the Florida Air Museum on the Lakeland campus.

Notable incidents

On 31 March 2011, a severe thunderstorm with a possible tornado struck the grounds during the event, injuring several people and damaging aircraft and tents; the show resumed the following day.
