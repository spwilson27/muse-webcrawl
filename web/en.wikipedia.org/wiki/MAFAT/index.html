MAFAT (Hebrew acronym מפא"ת) is the Directorate of Defense Research & Development (DDR&D) of Israel's Ministry of Defense, a joint body of the Ministry of Defense and the Israel Defense Forces. It is responsible for directing and coordinating the research, development, and technological infrastructure of Israel's military technology.

Role and organisation

MAFAT oversees the development of weapons systems and military technologies, working with Israel's defence industries — including Israel Aerospace Industries, Rafael Advanced Defense Systems, and Elbit Systems — with military research units, and with academia. Its remit spans missile defence, space and satellite programmes (through its space administration), unmanned systems, cyber, electro-optics, and advanced materials. It also promotes dual-use innovation and defence exports, and maintains liaison offices abroad, notably in the United States. Programmes publicly associated with the directorate include the Arrow and David's Sling missile defence systems and the Ofek reconnaissance satellites.

Talpiot

MAFAT administers the elite Talpiot programme, founded in 1979, which recruits outstanding science students into an intensive academic and military track intended to produce technological leaders for the defence establishment.
