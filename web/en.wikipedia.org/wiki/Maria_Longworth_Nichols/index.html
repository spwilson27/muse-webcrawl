Maria Longworth Nichols

Maria Longworth Nichols Storer (March 20, 1849 – April 30, 1932) was an American philanthropist and a founder of Rookwood Pottery in Cincinnati, Ohio. She is considered the founder of the American art pottery movement.

Life and work

Born into the wealthy Longworth family of Cincinnati, granddaughter of the horticulturalist and winemaker Nicholas Longworth, she took up china painting in the 1870s, a fashionable pursuit popularised by ceramics shown at the 1876 Philadelphia Centennial Exhibition. In 1880 she founded Rookwood Pottery, named after her father's estate, employing skilled artists and chemists and winning international acclaim for its art pottery. In 1885 she married Bellamy Storer. She later moved to France, where she died in 1932.
