Retail trading via mobile apps

A retail investor, also known as an individual investor, is a non-professional investor who buys and sells securities for their own personal account, usually in smaller amounts than institutional investors such as pension funds, mutual funds, and banks.

Characteristics

Retail investors trade through brokerage firms, increasingly via online platforms and mobile apps, and hold much of their wealth in retirement accounts. Regulation treats them differently from professional clients, with disclosure and suitability rules intended to compensate for their lesser expertise and bargaining power.

Influence

Commission-free trading and social media coordination brought retail investors to prominence in the early 2020s, notably in the "meme stock" episode surrounding GameStop in January 2021, when retail buying forced large losses on short-selling hedge funds.
