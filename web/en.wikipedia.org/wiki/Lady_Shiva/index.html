Lady Shiva is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Dennis O'Neil and artist Ric Estrada, she first appeared in Richard Dragon, Kung Fu Fighter #5 (1975). She is depicted as one of the most skilled martial artists and deadliest assassins in the DC Universe.

Fictional character biography

Born Sandra Wu-San, she grew up in a village in China with her sister Carolyn. After Carolyn was murdered by the martial artist David Cain — acting on behalf of Ra's al Ghul — Sandra pursued vengeance, mastering numerous martial arts and developing the ability to read an opponent's body language to predict their moves. She became the assassin and mercenary known as Lady Shiva, constantly seeking worthy opponents who might one day defeat and kill her.

Shiva has served the League of Assassins, trained and fought heroes including Batman, Richard Dragon, the Question, and Black Canary, and is the mother of Cassandra Cain, who became Batgirl. She briefly joined the Birds of Prey under the name Jade Canary. In the New 52 continuity she was reintroduced as a younger assassin and member of the League of Assassins.

Powers and abilities

Lady Shiva possesses no superhuman powers but is regarded as perhaps the greatest hand-to-hand combatant in the DC Universe. Her signature skill is reading body language so precisely that she can anticipate attacks before they are made. She has mastered many martial arts disciplines and is an expert with numerous weapons.
