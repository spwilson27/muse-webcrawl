State emblem of Pakistan

The state emblem of Pakistan was adopted in 1954. It features a crescent and star at the top, a shield in the centre quartered with depictions of Pakistan's main crops — cotton, jute, tea, and wheat — a floral wreath of jasmine surrounding the shield, and a scroll below bearing the national motto in Urdu: Iman, Ittehad, Nazm ("Faith, Unity, Discipline").

Symbolism

The crescent and star represent Islam and progress; the shield's crops reflect the agricultural economy at independence; the jasmine is the national flower. The emblem appears on government documents, currency, and official buildings. The motto was given by Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan.
