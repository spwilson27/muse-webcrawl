EQT

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

EQT's headquarters in Stockholm.

EQT AB is a Swedish global investment organisation and one of the world's largest private equity firms. Founded in 1994 in Stockholm in association with Investor AB, the Wallenberg family's holding company, and the US firm AEA Investors, EQT manages funds investing in private equity, infrastructure, real estate, growth equity and venture capital. Its assets under management exceed €200 billion, making it the largest private-equity firm headquartered in Europe and consistently among the top few worldwide by funds raised.

History

EQT grew out of the Scandinavian buyout market of the 1990s, raising its first fund in 1995. It expanded from mid-market Nordic buyouts into large-cap global private equity, then into infrastructure (from 2008), real estate, venture capital (EQT Ventures, launched 2016) and growth investments. The firm listed on Nasdaq Stockholm in 2019. Major acquisitions include the purchase of Exeter Property Group in 2021, which created a large global real estate platform rebranded EQT Exeter, and the 2022 merger with Baring Private Equity Asia (rebranded EQT Private Capital Asia), one of the largest-ever mergers between private equity firms, giving EQT a major presence in Asia.

Strategy

EQT describes its approach as thematic, "future-proofing" companies in sectors such as healthcare, technology, and services, and emphasises digitalisation and sustainability in portfolio development, supported by a network of industrial advisers drawn from the Wallenberg sphere and beyond. Its portfolio has included companies such as Anticimex, IFS, Sitecore, IVC Evidensia, and the hearing-care business WS Audiology. EQT was also known for Galderma, the former Nestlé Skin Health business it led a consortium to acquire in 2019 and listed in Switzerland in 2024.
