Hinge (app)

Hinge is an online dating application founded by Justin McLeod and launched in 2012. It markets itself as "designed to be deleted", emphasising profiles built around prompts and photos that users like or comment on individually, rather than simple swiping.

History

Originally launched as a web and Facebook-based service, Hinge relaunched as a mobile app in 2013. Match Group acquired a controlling stake in 2018 and full ownership in 2019.

Features

Users create profiles with photos and written prompts; others respond to specific elements, which can start a conversation. The app provides limited daily likes to encourage selectivity.
