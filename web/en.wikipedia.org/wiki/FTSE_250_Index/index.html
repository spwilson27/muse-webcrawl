FTSE 250 Index

The FTSE 250 Index is a capitalisation-weighted index consisting of the 101st to the 350th largest companies listed on the London Stock Exchange, after the 100 companies of the FTSE 100 Index. It is maintained by FTSE Russell and is widely regarded as a better gauge of the UK domestic economy than the internationally exposed FTSE 100.

Composition

Constituents are reviewed quarterly; companies promoted above rank 90 enter the FTSE 100, while FTSE 100 companies falling below rank 111 are demoted to the FTSE 250. Together the FTSE 100 and FTSE 250 make up the FTSE 350. The index includes mid-cap companies across sectors such as industrials, financial services, consumer discretionary, and real estate, including many investment trusts.

Characteristics

Because FTSE 250 companies earn a larger share of their revenues within the United Kingdom than FTSE 100 multinationals, the index tends to react more strongly to UK economic and political news, such as the market movements following the 2016 Brexit referendum.

Related indices

The FTSE SmallCap Index covers companies below the FTSE 250, and the FTSE All-Share combines the FTSE 100, 250, and SmallCap.
