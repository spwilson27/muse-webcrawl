Boulder is a city in and county seat of Boulder County, Colorado, United States, at the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, about 25 miles northwest of Denver.

Geography

Its landmark is the Flatirons, tilted sandstone slabs west of the city, surrounded by over 36,000 acres of protected open space and 155+ miles of trails. The Boulder Creek runs through the city.

Economy and culture

Home to the University of Colorado Boulder (founded 1876), federal labs (NIST, NOAA, NCAR), and a major tech and natural-foods sector, plus Pearl Street Mall. Known for outdoor recreation, cycling, climbing, and the Bolder Boulder 10K race. See Colorado, University of Colorado Boulder.
