Wisin & Yandel were a Puerto Rican reggaeton duo consisting of Wisin (born Juan Luis Morera Luna) and Yandel (born Llandel Veguilla Malavé). They began their career together in 1998 and became one of the most successful acts in reggaeton history, frequently billed as "El Dúo de la Historia" (The Duo of History).

Among their best-known albums are Pa'l Mundo (2005), which included the hit "Rakata", Los Extraterrestres (2007), La Revolución (2009), and Los Vaqueros: El Regreso (2011). Their 2009 single "Abusadora" won a Latin Grammy Award, and the duo won a Grammy Award for Best Latin Urban Album for Los Extraterrestres. They collaborated widely with Latin and mainstream pop artists, including appearances on songs with Enrique Iglesias, Jennifer Lopez, and 50 Cent.

Career

The duo emerged from the Puerto Rican underground reggaeton scene, appearing on DJ compilations before releasing their debut album Los Reyes del Nuevo Milenio in 2000. Through the 2000s they rose to the top of the genre alongside acts such as Daddy Yankee, selling out stadiums across Latin America and topping Billboard's Latin charts repeatedly. Their production and business ventures included their label WY Records.

Solo projects and reunion

In 2013 the duo announced a hiatus to pursue solo careers; both released successful solo albums, with Yandel's Dangerous and Wisin's Victory among them. They reunited in 2018, releasing the album Los Campeones del Pueblo: The Big Leagues, and later La Última Misión (2022), accompanied by a farewell tour billed as their final run as a duo.
