Gigafactory

A Gigafactory is a very large-scale factory for the production of lithium-ion battery cells and electric vehicles. The term was coined by Tesla CEO Elon Musk, derived from "giga-", for output measured in gigawatt-hours of batteries per year.

Tesla factories

Tesla's first Gigafactory, Gigafactory Nevada, opened near Reno in 2016 to supply battery cells for its vehicles and energy products. It was followed by Gigafactory New York (solar products), Gigafactory Shanghai (2019), Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg (2022), and Gigafactory Texas (2022).

Use by other companies

As battery demand grew, other manufacturers and governments adopted the term for their own large cell plants; factories described as gigafactories have been announced or built by companies including Panasonic, CATL, LG Energy Solution, and Northvolt.
