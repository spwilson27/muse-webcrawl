Sherrie Levine

Sherrie Levine
Born: 17 April 1947, Hazleton, Pennsylvania
Movement: Appropriation art, Pictures Generation

Sherrie Levine (born 1947) is an American photographer and appropriation artist associated with the Pictures Generation. She became famous for rephotographing canonical modernist works and presenting near-identical copies as her own.

Work

Her breakthrough series After Walker Evans (1981) rephotographed Evans's Depression-era photographs from an exhibition catalogue, challenging notions of authorship, originality and authenticity. Other series rework images by Edward Weston, and later works include cast-bronze versions of Marcel Duchamp's Fountain and sculptural pieces after modernist furniture and Brâncuși. Her work is central to postmodern critiques of originality.
