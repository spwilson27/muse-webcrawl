2 euro commemorative coins

2 euro commemorative coins are special euro coins issued by eurozone member states to commemorate particular people, events, or anniversaries. They have the same technical specifications and common reverse as regular €2 coins, but carry a special design on the national side. Unlike collector coins, they are legal tender throughout the eurozone and are issued at face value into circulation.

Rules

Since 2004, each member state may normally issue one commemorative coin per year (raised to two from 2012), in limited numbers. In addition, all eurozone members may jointly issue a common commemorative coin marking a shared anniversary — as was done for the 50th anniversary of the Treaty of Rome (2007), ten years of Economic and Monetary Union (2009), ten years of euro cash (2012), the 30th anniversary of the EU flag (2015), and 35 years of the Erasmus programme (2022).

Notable issues

Greece issued the first commemorative €2 coin in 2004 for the Athens Olympic Games. Some issues, such as Monaco's 2007 coin honoring Princess Grace, trade at large premiums because of very low mintages.
