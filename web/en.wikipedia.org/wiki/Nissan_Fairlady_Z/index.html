Nissan Fairlady Z

Nissan Fairlady Z is the Japanese-market name for Nissan's Z-car sports car line, sold internationally successively as the Datsun 240Z, 280Z, 280ZX, 300ZX, 350Z, 370Z and Nissan Z. The name — after the musical My Fair Lady, a favourite of Nissan president Katsuji Kawamata — had earlier been used on the Datsun Fairlady roadsters of the 1960s.

Generations

The line began with the S30 (1969), styled under Yoshihiko Matsuo, which transformed Nissan's export image. Successive generations: S130 (280ZX, 1978), Z31 (1983) and Z32 (1989) 300ZX models, a hiatus in many markets in the late 1990s, then the Z33 350Z revival (2002), Z34 370Z (2009) and RZ34 Nissan Z (2022). Over 1.8 million Z-cars have been sold, making it one of the best-selling sports car series of all time.
