Indo-Pakistani wars and conflicts

Since the Partition of India in 1947, India and Pakistan have fought several wars and numerous skirmishes, principally over Kashmir.

Major wars

  • 1947–48: First Kashmir War after tribal invasion; UN ceasefire and Line of Control origins.
  • 1965: War over Kashmir following Operation Gibraltar; Tashkent Agreement restored status quo.
  • 1971: War over East Pakistan resulting in Bangladesh.
  • 1999: Kargil War, a limited high-altitude conflict under the nuclear shadow.

Other conflicts

These include the Siachen conflict (from 1984), insurgency in Kashmir, border skirmishes, the 2001–02 standoff, and crises after the 2008 Mumbai attacks and 2019 Pulwama–Balakot episode. Both states tested nuclear weapons in 1998.
