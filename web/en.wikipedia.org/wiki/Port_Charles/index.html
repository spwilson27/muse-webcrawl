Port Charles

Port Charles is a fictional city in upstate New York, on the shore of Lake Ontario, serving as the setting for the ABC daytime soap opera General Hospital and its spin-off Port Charles (1997–2003). The city is portrayed as a port community whose social life revolves around General Hospital, the Metro Court Hotel, and the waterfront.

Setting and storylines

Major locations include General Hospital, the Quartermaine mansion, Wyndemere castle on Spoon Island, Kelly's diner, and the Haunted Star yacht. The city has hosted storylines ranging from medical drama and organised crime — centred on mobster Sonny Corinthos — to espionage sagas involving the WSB and the Cassadine family. Signature events include the annual Nurses' Ball fundraiser.

The spin-off

The half-hour spin-off Port Charles premiered in 1997, initially focusing on hospital interns, and later adopted supernatural "telenovela"-style story arcs involving vampires and angels in thirteen-week "books". Despite a cult following, low ratings led to its cancellation in 2003, with its cliffhanger resolved obliquely through General Hospital.
