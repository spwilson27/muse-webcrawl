Bilberry

SpeciesVaccinium myrtillus
FamilyEricaceae
RangeEurope and northern Asia

The bilberry (Vaccinium myrtillus) is a low-growing Eurasian shrub bearing small, dark-blue edible berries. Unlike the cultivated blueberry, its flesh is deeply pigmented throughout, staining hands and mouths.

Habitat and use

Bilberries grow wild on heaths, moors and acidic woodland soils across Europe and northern Asia and are gathered from the wild rather than farmed. They are eaten fresh and used in jams, pies, liqueurs and traditional herbal medicine, and are rich in anthocyanin antioxidants.

Name

In Britain the fruit is also called whortleberry, blaeberry (Scotland) and winberry in parts of England and Wales.
