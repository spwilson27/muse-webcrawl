Province of Pavia

Country: Italy
Region: Lombardy
Capital: Pavia
Area: 2,965 km²
Population: about 535,000

The Province of Pavia (Italian: Provincia di Pavia) is a province in the Lombardy region of northern Italy. Its capital is the city of Pavia, a historic university city on the Ticino River south of Milan.

Geography

The province comprises three traditional districts: the Pavese, a flat rice-growing plain between the Po and Ticino rivers; the Lomellina to the west, also noted for rice cultivation; and the Oltrepò Pavese to the south, a hilly wine-producing area extending into the Apennine foothills across the Po.

Economy

Agriculture — especially rice, maize, and wine grapes such as Pinot nero — remains important, alongside manufacturing around Pavia and Vigevano, long a centre of shoemaking. The province has 186 municipalities (comuni).
