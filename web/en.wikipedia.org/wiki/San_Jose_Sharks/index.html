San Jose Sharks

The San Jose Sharks are a professional ice hockey team based in San Jose, California. They compete in the National Hockey League (NHL) as a member of the Pacific Division of the Western Conference. The team plays its home games at the SAP Center at San Jose, known locally as the "Shark Tank". The franchise was founded in 1991, returning the NHL to the San Francisco Bay Area after the California Golden Seals departed in 1976.

History

The Sharks played their first two seasons at the Cow Palace in Daly City before moving to the San Jose Arena (now SAP Center) in 1993. In their third season, 1993–94, the team engineered one of the great playoff upsets in NHL history, defeating the heavily favoured Detroit Red Wings in the first round. San Jose developed into a perennial playoff contender in the 2000s and 2010s, winning the Presidents' Trophy in 2008–09 and reaching the Stanley Cup Final for the first time in 2016, where they lost to the Pittsburgh Penguins.

Identity

The team's colours are Pacific teal, black, and orange, and its logo features a shark biting through a hockey stick. The name "Sharks" was chosen by fan vote, referring to the sharks that inhabit the Pacific waters off Northern California. At home games, the team skates onto the ice through a giant shark-head entrance while the theme from Jaws plays.

Notable players

Franchise cornerstones have included Owen Nolan, Patrick Marleau — who became the NHL's all-time leader in games played — Joe Thornton, Joe Pavelski, Logan Couture, and defencemen Brent Burns and Erik Karlsson.
