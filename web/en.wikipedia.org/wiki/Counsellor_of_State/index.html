Counsellor of State

Counsellors of State are senior members of the British royal family who can be delegated certain official duties of the monarch under the Regency Acts 1937 to 1953, such as attending Privy Council meetings or signing routine documents, when the sovereign is abroad or temporarily incapacitated.

Eligibility

By law the counsellors are the sovereign's spouse and the first four people in the line of succession who are over 21 and domiciled in the United Kingdom. The Counsellors of State Act 2022 added the Princess Royal and the Earl of Wessex (now Duke of Edinburgh) to the pool for their lifetimes.
