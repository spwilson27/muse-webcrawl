Taurus KEPD 350

TypeAir-launched cruise missile
ManufacturerTaurus Systems GmbH
Rangeover 500 km
Warhead481 kg MEPHISTO penetrator
OperatorsGermany, Spain, South Korea

The Taurus KEPD 350 is a German–Swedish air-launched cruise missile developed by Taurus Systems GmbH, a joint venture of MBDA Deutschland and Saab. KEPD stands for "Kinetic Energy Penetrator and Destroyer".

Design

The missile is powered by a turbofan engine and flies at about Mach 0.95 at very low altitude using terrain-reference, image-based and GPS/INS navigation, allowing it to operate in GPS-denied environments. Its MEPHISTO warhead uses a two-stage penetrator with a programmable fuze that can count layers or voids inside a structure, enabling it to destroy hardened and buried targets such as bunkers and bridges.

Service

The Taurus entered service with the German Air Force on Tornado and Eurofighter aircraft, and has been exported to Spain and South Korea. Ukraine repeatedly requested the missile after 2023, a politically debated question in Germany.
