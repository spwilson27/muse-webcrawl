Cava may refer to several different topics. This disambiguation-style article summarises the principal meanings of the word.

Cava (Spanish wine)

Most prominently, Cava is a Spanish sparkling wine produced mainly in Catalonia by the traditional method of second fermentation in bottle, like Champagne. It is made chiefly from the Macabeo, Xarel·lo and Parellada grapes and is protected by a Denominación de Origen. See the dedicated article Cava (Spanish wine).

Other uses

In anatomy, "cava" appears in the names of the two great veins returning blood to the heart, the superior vena cava and inferior vena cava. In geology and archaeology, cava (Italian and Spanish for "quarry" or "cave") appears in place names. CAVA is also the name of an American fast-casual restaurant chain serving Mediterranean food, founded in the Washington, D.C. area. In Britain, CAVA (the Conference of Administrative and related roles) and various acronyms use the term. The word also appears in astronomy (a dark lunar feature naming convention) and in botany and zoology as part of scientific names.
