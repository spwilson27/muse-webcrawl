Maihar gharana

The Maihar gharana is a school (gharana) of Hindustani classical music founded in the princely state of Maihar in present-day Madhya Pradesh by the sarod maestro Allauddin Khan (c. 1862 – 1972), known reverently as "Baba". Uniquely among gharanas, it is centred on instrumental music and is noted for producing musicians on many different instruments trained within a single stylistic discipline.

Origins and style

Allauddin Khan, court musician of the Maharaja of Maihar, synthesised elements of the dhrupad tradition, the been and rabab heritage, and the sarod style of his teachers into a rigorous teaching system. The gharana emphasises a structured raga presentation influenced by dhrupad — alap, jor and jhala — combined with layakari (rhythmic play) and the composition of new bandishes. Baba taught large numbers of students, many of them orphans and poor children whom he raised in his household.

Notable musicians

The gharana's celebrated disciples include sitarists Ravi Shankar and Nikhil Banerjee, sarodist Ali Akbar Khan (Allauddin Khan's son), Annapurna Devi (his daughter), flautist Pannalal Ghosh, violinist V. G. Jog, and guitarists such as Vishwa Mohan Bhatt in the following generation. Through Ravi Shankar and Ali Akbar Khan especially, the Maihar style achieved worldwide influence in the twentieth century.
