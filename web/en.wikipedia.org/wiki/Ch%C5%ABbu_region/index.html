The Chūbu region (中部地方, Chūbu-chihō, "central region") is the central portion of Honshu, Japan's main island. It comprises nine prefectures: Aichi, Fukui, Gifu, Ishikawa, Nagano, Niigata, Shizuoka, Toyama and Yamanashi.

Geography

Chūbu is dominated by the Japanese Alps — the Hida, Kiso and Akaishi mountain ranges — which divide the region into the Pacific-facing side (Omote-Nihon) and the Sea of Japan side (Ura-Nihon), the latter known for heavy winter snowfall. Mount Fuji, Japan's highest peak, stands on the border of Shizuoka and Yamanashi prefectures.

Economy

The region is a manufacturing heartland centred on Nagoya, home to Toyota and its supplier network, as well as ceramics, aerospace and precision machinery. Its subregions — Hokuriku on the Sea of Japan coast, Kōshin'etsu in the mountains, and Tōkai on the Pacific — retain distinct identities.
