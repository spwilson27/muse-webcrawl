Racing Victoria

Racing Victoria Limited is the governing body for thoroughbred horse racing in the Australian state of Victoria. It was established in 2001 as an independent body responsible for the strategic direction, funding and integrity of the Victorian thoroughbred racing industry, which includes famous events such as the Melbourne Cup, Cox Plate and Caulfield Cup.

Racing Victoria works with metropolitan clubs including the Victoria Racing Club, Melbourne Racing Club and Moonee Valley Racing Club, as well as country racing clubs across the state. Its responsibilities include race programming, licensing, handicapping, veterinary and integrity services.
