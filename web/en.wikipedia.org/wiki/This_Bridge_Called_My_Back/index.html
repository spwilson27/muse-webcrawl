This Bridge Called My Back
Full title: This Bridge Called My Back: Writings by Radical Women of Color
Editors: Cherríe Moraga and Gloria E. Anzaldúa
Published: 1981

This Bridge Called My Back: Writings by Radical Women of Color is a feminist anthology edited by Cherríe Moraga and Gloria E. Anzaldúa, first published in 1981 by Persephone Press. It is considered a landmark work of third-wave feminism and women-of-color feminism.

Contents

The anthology collects essays, poems, letters, and testimonios by Black, Latina, Asian American, Native American, and other women of color, addressing racism within the feminist movement, sexism within communities of color, class, sexuality, and the intersections of oppression. Contributors include Audre Lorde, Barbara Smith, Cheryl Clarke, Mitsuye Yamada, Nellie Wong, and the editors themselves.

Impact

The book helped found the "women of color" political identity in the United States and introduced many readers to ideas later formalised as intersectionality. It has been continuously influential in women's studies, ethnic studies, and queer studies, and has gone through several editions, including a 4th edition in 2015.

Publication history

After Persephone Press folded, the anthology was republished by Kitchen Table: Women of Color Press, the publishing house co-founded by Barbara Smith, and later by Third Woman Press and SUNY Press.
