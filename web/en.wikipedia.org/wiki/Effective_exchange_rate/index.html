Effective exchange rate

The effective exchange rate is an index describing the strength of a currency relative to a basket of other currencies, weighted typically by trade shares. The nominal effective exchange rate (NEER) measures value; the real effective exchange rate (REER) adjusts for inflation differentials.

Calculation

Central banks and the BIS and IMF publish NEER/REER indices as geometric weighted averages of bilateral rates. An increase in REER implies appreciation in real terms, often interpreted as reduced price competitiveness, though productivity changes complicate interpretation (Balassa–Samuelson effect).

Uses

REER is used to assess competitiveness, external imbalances, and whether a currency is over- or under-valued relative to fundamentals.
