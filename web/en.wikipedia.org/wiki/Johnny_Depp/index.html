John Christopher Depp II (born 9 June 1963) is an American actor and musician. Known for his eclectic and often eccentric roles, he is one of the most commercially successful film stars of his generation, and received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 1999.

Career

Depp made his film debut in A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984) and became a teen idol on the television series 21 Jump Street (1987–1990). Rejecting conventional leading-man roles, he built his reputation in auteur films, beginning with Tim Burton's Edward Scissorhands (1990) — the first of eight collaborations with Burton, including Ed Wood (1994), Sleepy Hollow (1999), Charlie and the Chocolate Factory (2005) and Sweeney Todd (2007), the last earning him a Golden Globe and an Oscar nomination.

His portrayal of Captain Jack Sparrow in Disney's Pirates of the Caribbean series (2003–2017) made him a global box-office star and brought another Academy Award nomination, as did Finding Neverland (2004). Other notable films include Donnie Brasco (1997), Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas (1998), Public Enemies (2009) and Black Mass (2015). He also plays guitar with the supergroup Hollywood Vampires.

Legal disputes

Depp's marriage to actress Amber Heard ended in divorce in 2017 amid allegations of abuse. He lost a libel case against The Sun newspaper in London in 2020, but in 2022 a Virginia jury found in his favour in a widely televised defamation trial against Heard, awarding him substantial damages while also awarding Heard damages on one counterclaim; the parties settled later that year. The disputes led to his removal from the Fantastic Beasts franchise, after which he returned to acting in the French film Jeanne du Barry (2023).
