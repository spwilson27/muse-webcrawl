Micro-Star International

Micro-Star International Co., Ltd. (commonly known as MSI) is a Taiwanese multinational information technology corporation headquartered in New Taipei City, Taiwan. It designs, develops and provides computer hardware, related products and services, including laptops, desktops, motherboards, graphics cards, all-in-one PCs, servers, and peripherals.

History

MSI was founded in 1986 by five former Sony engineers. It became known first for motherboards and graphics cards, and later expanded strongly into gaming laptops and esports sponsorship under the MSI Gaming brand.

Products

The company's product lines include the MEG, MPG and MAG gaming series, the Raider and Stealth gaming laptops, and a range of monitors, components and accessories.
