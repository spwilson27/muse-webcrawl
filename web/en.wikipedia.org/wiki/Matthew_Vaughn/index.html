Matthew Vaughn

Matthew Vaughn
Born: 7 March 1971, Paddington, London, England
Occupation: Film director, producer, screenwriter
Years active: 1996 – present
Spouse: Claudia Schiffer

Matthew Allard Robert Vaughn (born 7 March 1971) is an English filmmaker. He is best known for directing the films Layer Cake (2004), Stardust (2007), Kick-Ass (2010), X-Men: First Class (2011), and the Kingsman franchise.

Career

Vaughn began as a producer, working with Guy Ritchie on Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels (1998) and Snatch (2000). He made his directorial debut with the crime film Layer Cake, starring Daniel Craig. He moved to large-scale filmmaking with the fantasy Stardust (2007), adapted from Neil Gaiman's novel, and the comic-book adaptations Kick-Ass (2010) and X-Men: First Class (2011), both co-written with Jane Goldman.

In 2014 Vaughn co-wrote and directed Kingsman: The Secret Service, based on the comic book by Mark Millar and Dave Gibbons, which became a successful franchise with the sequel Kingsman: The Golden Circle (2017) and the prequel The King's Man (2021). He later directed the spy film Argylle (2024).

Personal life

Vaughn is married to the German supermodel Claudia Schiffer, with whom he has three children. He was born in London and educated at Stowe School and University College London, where he studied anthropology and ancient history before leaving to pursue film.
