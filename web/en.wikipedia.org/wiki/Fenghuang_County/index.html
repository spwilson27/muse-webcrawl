Fenghuang County

LocationXiangxi Prefecture, Hunan, China
County seatTuojiang Town
Known forFenghuang Ancient Town

Fenghuang County (Chinese: 凤凰县; pinyin: Fènghuáng Xiàn; literally "phoenix county") is a county of Hunan Province, China, administered by the Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture. It is best known for its exceptionally preserved ancient town, named after the mythological phoenix, which is a major tourist destination.

The ancient town

Fenghuang Ancient Town, centred on Tuojiang Town along the Tuo River, was founded in its present form during the Ming and Qing dynasties. Its stilted wooden houses (diaojiaolou) line the riverbanks, and the old city preserves temples, ancestral halls, narrow stone streets and sections of wall. The town was the home of the writer Shen Congwen, whose novel Border Town drew on the region, and of the painter Huang Yongyu.

People and culture

The county's population includes large Tujia and Miao communities, whose festivals, embroidery and silver jewellery are prominent in local culture. Tourism transformed the local economy from the 2000s; a controversial entry-ticket policy for the ancient town was introduced in 2013 and later relaxed after protests by local businesses.
