Irina Borisovna Krush (born 24 December 1983) is a Ukrainian-born American chess player who holds the FIDE title of Grandmaster (GM). She is an eight-time U.S. Women's Champion, having won the title in 1998, 2007, 2010, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015 and 2020.

Krush was born in Odesa and immigrated to the United States with her family when she was five years old, settling in Brooklyn, New York. She learned to play chess at five and quickly emerged as one of the strongest young players in the country.

Chess career

In 1998, at age 14, Krush won her first U.S. Women's Championship, becoming the youngest champion in the event's history at the time. In 2013 she became the first American woman to earn the outright Grandmaster title through the standard norm-and-rating requirements, reaching a peak FIDE rating of 2502.

Krush has represented the United States in numerous Women's Chess Olympiads, helping the team to a silver medal in 2004 and a bronze medal in 2008. She won the U.S. Women's Championship again in 2020, in an online rapid format. Beyond competition, she is known for chess commentary, coaching and writing, and has served as a commentator for major events including the Saint Louis Chess Club broadcasts.

Education

Krush graduated from New York University in 2006 with a degree in international relations.
