Battle of Bạch Đằng

The Battle of Bạch Đằng refers to famous battles on the Bạch Đằng River in northern Vietnam, notably in 938, when Ngô Quyền defeated the Southern Han fleet, ending a millennium of Chinese domination, and in 1288, when Trần Hưng Đạo defeated the Mongol-Yuan fleet.

Battle of Bạch Đằng (938)
Date: 938 AD
Location: Bạch Đằng River, Vietnam
Result: Vietnamese victory, independence

938 battle

Ngô Quyền planted iron-tipped wooden stakes in the riverbed, invisible at high tide. Vietnamese boats lured the Southern Han fleet in, then retreated as the tide fell, impaling the enemy ships on the stakes.

1288 battle

Trần Hưng Đạo repeated the stake tactic against the Yuan invasion fleet, destroying it and helping end the third Mongol invasion of Đại Việt.
