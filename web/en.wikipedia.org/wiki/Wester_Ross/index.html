Wester Ross

Wester Ross is an area of the northwest Highlands of Scotland, forming the western portion of the historic county of Ross and Cromarty. It is renowned for its dramatic mountain and coastal scenery, including the Torridon hills, Beinn Eighe and the Applecross peninsula.

Geography

The area is sparsely populated, with small settlements such as Gairloch, Ullapool (on its northern edge), Lochcarron and Plockton. Much of Wester Ross falls within a National Scenic Area and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve designated in 2016. The geology is dominated by ancient Lewisian gneiss and Torridonian sandstone, among the oldest rocks in Britain.

Tourism

The North Coast 500 touring route passes through Wester Ross, crossing the steep Bealach na Bà pass to Applecross. Inverewe Garden, created by Osgood Mackenzie in the 19th century, is a major visitor attraction, notable for exotic plants thriving at a northern latitude thanks to the Gulf Stream.
