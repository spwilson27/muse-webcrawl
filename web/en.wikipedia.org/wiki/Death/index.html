Death

Death
DefinitionPermanent cessation of all biological functions that sustain an organism
DeterminationCardiopulmonary and brain criteria
[Image: A quiet cemetery at dawn with rows of headstones in mist]

Death is the permanent cessation of all biological functions that sustain a living organism. Medicine defines it by irreversible loss of circulatory and respiratory functions or of all brain functions (brain death) — the definition that makes organ transplantation possible.

Meanings

Every culture surrounds death with ritual — funerals, mourning, memorials — and every religion answers it (the Dalai Lama's tradition teaches rebirth; others teach resurrection or dissolution). Philosophy from Socrates' calm hemlock (Socrates) to modern hospice care asks how to die well; demographers simply count it, balancing Population.
