Ilham Aliyev

Ilham Heydar oghlu Aliyev (born 24 December 1961) is an Azerbaijani politician who has served as the fourth president of Azerbaijan since 2003. He is the son of Heydar Aliyev, president from 1993 to 2003, whom he succeeded.

Early life and career

Born in Baku, Aliyev studied at the Moscow State Institute of International Relations. Before entering politics he was a senior executive at SOCAR, the state oil company, and served as a member of parliament and head of Azerbaijan's delegation to the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe.

Presidency

His presidency has overseen oil-fuelled economic growth, large infrastructure projects and the 2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war, after which Azerbaijan regained surrounding territories, followed by the 2023 offensive restoring control over Nagorno-Karabakh itself. International observers have criticised elections under his rule and the concentration of power in the ruling family.
