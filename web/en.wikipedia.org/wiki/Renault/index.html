Renault

Renault SAS is a French multinational automobile manufacturer established in 1899. The company produces a range of cars and vans and has been involved in motor racing, including Formula One. It is part of the Renault–Nissan–Mitsubishi Alliance, one of the world's largest automotive groups by sales.

History

The company was founded as Société Renault Frères by brothers Louis, Marcel and Fernand Renault. Louis Renault built his first car, the Voiturette, in 1898. Renault became a major supplier of trucks, tanks and aircraft engines during the First World War. Nationalised after the Second World War — following Louis Renault's death in prison while awaiting trial on collaboration charges — the company launched successful models such as the 4CV, the Dauphine and later the Renault 4 and 5.

Privatisation and alliance

Renault was privatised in 1996. In 1999 it entered an alliance with Japan's Nissan, taking a stake in the company, and Mitsubishi Motors joined in 2016. Renault also owns the Romanian brand Dacia and the Alpine sports-car marque, and the French state remains a significant shareholder. Long-time CEO Carlos Ghosn, architect of the alliance, was arrested in Japan in 2018 in a major corporate scandal.

Motorsport

Renault has a long record in rallying and Formula One, winning constructors' championships in 2005 and 2006 with Fernando Alonso. Its Formula One team was rebranded as Alpine in 2021.
