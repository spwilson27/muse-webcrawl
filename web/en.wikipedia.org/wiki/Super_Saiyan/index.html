The Super Saiyan (Japanese: 超サイヤ人, Hepburn: Sūpā Saiya-jin) is a fictional transformation and power-up assumed by members and hybrids of the Saiyan race in Akira Toriyama's Dragon Ball franchise. The form first appeared in the original manga in 1991 and in the anime adaptation Dragon Ball Z the same year, during the climax of the Frieza Saga.

A Super Saiyan is recognisable by spiked hair that stands on end and turns golden blond, green or teal eyes, a golden aura of crackling energy, and a dramatic increase in strength, speed and energy output. The transformation is typically triggered by intense emotional upheaval — most famously rage — in a Saiyan of sufficient power and a degree of purity of heart.

Overview

Within the fiction, the Super Saiyan was long regarded as a legend: a warrior of unparalleled power said to appear only once every thousand years among the Saiyans. The tyrant Frieza's fear of the legend was a principal motive for his destruction of Planet Vegeta, the Saiyan homeworld. Son Goku became the first Super Saiyan seen in the series after Frieza murdered his best friend Krillin on the planet Namek.

The base Super Saiyan form multiplies the user's power many times over — guidebooks quantify it as a fifty-fold increase over the user's normal maximum. Later story arcs introduced ascended and successive numbered stages (Super Saiyan 2 and Super Saiyan 3), as well as godly variants such as Super Saiyan God and Super Saiyan Blue in Dragon Ball Super.

Concept and creation

Toriyama has explained that the design served practical as well as dramatic purposes: giving Goku blond hair meant his assistant no longer had to spend time inking Goku's hair black, saving considerable labour in weekly serialisation. The piercing eyes of the form were modelled on Bruce Lee's glaring expression. Toriyama initially intended the transformation to be a unique event for Goku, but its popularity led to other characters attaining it.

Forms and related transformations

Beyond the first three numbered stages, the franchise features numerous related states, including the giant golden Great Ape (Golden Oozaru), Super Saiyan 4 in Dragon Ball GT, the berserker-like Legendary Super Saiyan form associated with Broly, and the divine forms of Dragon Ball Super. Fusion characters such as Gotenks and Vegito can also transform. Mastery of the form — the "Full Power" state perfected by Goku and Gohan during the Cell Saga — removes the restlessness and energy waste of the basic state.

Reception and legacy

The Super Saiyan transformation is one of the most iconic elements of the Dragon Ball franchise and of anime and manga generally. Goku's first transformation is frequently cited among the most influential scenes in shōnen manga, and the spiked golden hair has become visual shorthand for a character powering up, parodied and referenced across popular culture worldwide.
