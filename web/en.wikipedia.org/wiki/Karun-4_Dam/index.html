Karun-4 Dam

The Karun-4 Dam is an arch dam on the Karun River in Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari Province, Iran, located about 180 km southwest of Shahrekord. Completed in 2010 and inaugurated in 2011, it is one of a cascade of hydroelectric dams built on the Karun, Iran's largest river by discharge.

Design

The double-curvature arch dam is about 230 metres high and 440 metres long at the crest, making it among the tallest dams in Iran. Its reservoir holds roughly 2.2 billion cubic metres of water. The powerhouse contains four 250 MW Francis turbines, giving an installed capacity of 1,000 MW and an average annual generation of about 2,100 GWh.

Purpose

The project generates electricity, regulates the flow of the Karun for downstream dams such as Karun-3, and provides flood control and water supply. Construction, carried out by Iranian firms, was largely domestically financed and engineered.
