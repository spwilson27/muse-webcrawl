Broadcom Inc. is an American multinational designer, developer, manufacturer, and global supplier of a wide range of semiconductor and infrastructure software products. The company was known as Broadcom Limited from 2016 to 2018, following the acquisition of the original Broadcom Corporation by Avago Technologies.

History

The original Broadcom Corporation was founded in 1991 by Henry Nicholas and Henry Samueli and became a leading supplier of chips for broadband communications, set-top boxes, and wireless devices. Avago Technologies, itself spun out of Agilent, acquired Broadcom Corporation in 2016 for about $37 billion and adopted the Broadcom name. Under CEO Hock Tan the company pursued large acquisitions including CA Technologies (2018), Symantec's enterprise security business (2019), and VMware (2023, about $69 billion).

Products

Broadcom's semiconductor lines include Ethernet switching and routing silicon (such as the Tomahawk and Jericho families), Wi-Fi and Bluetooth combo chips used in smartphones, broadband access chips, and storage adapters. Its software division includes mainframe and enterprise software from CA Technologies and virtualization and cloud software from VMware.
