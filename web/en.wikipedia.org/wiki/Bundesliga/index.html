The Bundesliga is a professional association football league in Germany and the country's primary football competition, contested by 18 teams. Founded in 1963 — later than the other major European leagues — it has the highest average stadium attendance of any football league in the world, sustained by affordable tickets, safe standing areas and the "50+1" rule that keeps clubs majority-owned by their members.

Bayern Munich is by far the most successful club, winning eleven consecutive titles from 2013 to 2023 before Bayer Leverkusen's unbeaten 2023–24 season under Xabi Alonso ended the streak; Bayern reclaimed the title in 2024–25, Harry Kane's first major trophy. Borussia Dortmund, famous for the 80,000-capacity Westfalenstadion and its "Yellow Wall", is the principal challenger. Gerd Müller's 365 league goals and Robert Lewandowski's 41-goal season (2020–21) are the league's towering records.
