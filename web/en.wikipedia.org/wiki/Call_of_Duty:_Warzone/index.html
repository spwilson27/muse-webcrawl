Call of Duty: Warzone is a free-to-play battle royale video game developed by Infinity Ward and Raven Software and published by Activision. Released on March 10, 2020, it was initially a mode of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare (2019) and later integrated content from subsequent series entries.

Gameplay

Matches feature up to 150 players dropping into a large map — originally the fictional city of Verdansk — where they scavenge weapons and fight as a closing circle of gas forces confrontations. The game introduced the Gulag, a one-versus-one arena where eliminated players can win re-entry, as well as buy stations, contracts, and custom loadouts.

Reception

Warzone attracted tens of millions of players within weeks of launch and became one of the most successful battle royale games. A sequel, Warzone 2.0, launched in 2022 alongside Modern Warfare II, with the game later renamed simply Warzone.
