ASEA

ASEA AB
Former type: Public
Founded: 1883, Västerås, Sweden
Defunct: 1988 (merged into ABB)
Industry: Electrical engineering

ASEA AB (Swedish: Allmänna Svenska Elektriska Aktiebolaget, English: General Swedish Electrical Limited Company) was a Swedish industrial company and a major manufacturer of electrical equipment, locomotives and high-voltage systems. In 1988 it merged with Brown, Boveri & Cie to form ABB.

History

Founded in 1883 in Västerås, ASEA pioneered three-phase power transmission, HVDC technology — including the first commercial HVDC link to Gotland in 1954 — industrial robots and nuclear power plants in Sweden.
