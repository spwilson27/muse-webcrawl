Amsterdamsche Football Club Ajax, also known as AFC Ajax, Ajax Amsterdam, or simply Ajax, is a Dutch professional football club based in Amsterdam that plays in the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football. Founded in 1900 and named after the mythological Greek hero Ajax, the club is one of the three giants of Dutch football alongside Feyenoord and PSV Eindhoven, and is historically among the most successful clubs in Europe. Ajax is renowned worldwide for its youth academy and its commitment to attacking, possession-based football.

History

Ajax was founded on 18 March 1900 by Floris Stempel, Carel Reeser and Han Dade. After early decades of domestic success, the club's golden era arrived in the late 1960s and early 1970s under coach Rinus Michels and star player Johan Cruyff, playing the system that became known as "Total Football". Ajax won the European Cup three consecutive times, in 1971, 1972 and 1973. A second golden generation under Louis van Gaal won the UEFA Champions League in 1995 with a famously young side, completing a treble of European trophies (European Cup/Champions League, UEFA Cup in 1992, and Cup Winners' Cup in 1987).

Honours

Ajax is the most successful club in the Netherlands, with a record number of Eredivisie titles (36) and KNVB Cups (20). Internationally the club has won four European Cup/Champions League titles, the UEFA Cup, the Cup Winners' Cup, two UEFA Super Cups and two Intercontinental Cups, making it one of only five clubs to have won all major European club trophies (before the creation of the Conference League). Ajax's academy, De Toekomst, has produced generations of celebrated players including Cruyff, Marco van Basten, Frank Rijkaard, Dennis Bergkamp, Patrick Kluivert, Clarence Seedorf, Wesley Sneijder, Matthijs de Ligt and Frenkie de Jong.

Identity and supporters

The club plays in a distinctive white shirt with a broad vertical red stripe. Its home ground since 1996 is the Johan Cruyff Arena (renamed in 2018 in honour of Cruyff after his death), previously the Amsterdam ArenA and before that De Meer and the Olympic Stadium. Ajax supporters maintain a fierce rivalry with Feyenoord of Rotterdam, contested in De Klassieker, and the club has a long-standing informal association with Amsterdam's historically Jewish community, reflected in supporter culture.

Playing philosophy

Ajax is institutionally committed to a recognisable style: a 4–3–3 formation, high pressing, technical skill and the promotion of academy players into the first team, with the same formation and principles applied at every age level of the academy. The club's model of developing and selling elite players sustains it financially in the modern transfer market, a strategy dramatically illustrated by the 2018–19 Champions League run to the semi-finals with a young, largely homegrown side.
