African Women's Footballer of the Year
Awarded by: Confederation of African Football (CAF)
First awarded: 2001
Most wins: Asisat Oshoala
Recent notable winners: Perpetua Nkwocha, Asisat Oshoala, Thembi Kgatlana

The African Women's Footballer of the Year is an annual award presented by the Confederation of African Football (CAF) to the best African female footballer of the year.

History

CAF first awarded the women's prize in 2001. Nigerian players have dominated the honour: Perpetua Nkwocha won it four times, and Asisat Oshoala surpassed her with a record fifth award in 2022 and a sixth in 2023. South Africa's Thembi Kgatlana won in 2018.

Selection

Winners are chosen through votes involving CAF technical committees, media representatives, and national team coaches and captains, as part of the annual CAF Awards ceremony.
