Itanagar

Itanagar (pronounced [iːtʌˈnʌɡʌr]) is the capital and largest town of Arunachal Pradesh in northeastern India. Located at the foothills of the Himalayas in Papum Pare district, it is the seat of the state government and the state's principal hub for administration, education, and commerce.

History

The town takes its name from the Ita Fort ("fort of bricks"), a 14th–15th-century brick fortress attributed to the Ahom kingdom, whose southern gate still stands. Itanagar became the capital when Arunachal Pradesh was constituted as a Union Territory in 1972 (previously under the North-East Frontier Agency), and remained the capital when the state achieved full statehood in 1987.

Places of interest

  • Ita Fort — the medieval brick fortification giving the city its name.
  • Jawaharlal Nehru Museum — displays on the tribal cultures, crafts, and history of Arunachal Pradesh.
  • Ganga Lake (Gekar Sinyi) — a natural lake amid forested hills, a popular local retreat.
  • Theravada Buddhist Temple and the Gompa.

Transport

Itanagar is connected by road to Guwahati and other northeastern cities, by rail via Harmuti–Naharlagun, and by air through Hollongi (Itanagar) Airport, opened in 2022. The twin capital settlement of Naharlagun lies about 10 km away.
