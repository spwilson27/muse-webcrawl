Campeonato Carioca

The Campeonato Carioca, officially the Campeonato Estadual do Rio de Janeiro, is the annual state football championship of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. First held in 1906, it is one of the oldest football competitions in the country and has traditionally been contested by the state's "big four" clubs: Flamengo, Fluminense, Botafogo, and Vasco da Gama. The championship is organised by the Federação de Futebol do Estado do Rio de Janeiro (FERJ).

Format

The competition is traditionally divided into two stages: the Taça Guanabara and the Taça Rio, originally the first and second rounds of the championship respectively. Under the modern format, the teams compete in the Taça Guanabara as a single group; the best-placed teams advance to the semi-finals and final of the state championship, while the Taça Rio is contested by the teams that finish outside the top positions in the Guanabara. The overall champion is decided in two-legged finals, frequently played at the Maracanã Stadium.

History

The first Carioca championship was played in 1906, only a few years after the Campeonato Paulista, making it the second-oldest state league in Brazil. Fluminense won the inaugural edition and dominated the early years. The 1910s and 1920s saw the rise of professionalism debates and rival leagues, while the professional era from the 1930s onward consolidated the dominance of the big four clubs. Flamengo is the most successful club in the competition's history, having won the title more than thirty-five times, followed by Fluminense, Vasco da Gama, and Botafogo. Smaller clubs such as América and Bangu also won titles in earlier eras.

Champions

  • Flamengo – most titles, including multiple three-peats ("tricampeonatos").
  • Fluminense – winner of the first edition in 1906 and frequent champion throughout the 20th century.
  • Vasco da Gama – first title in 1923 with a team famed for including Black and working-class players.
  • Botafogo – notable for the record 24–0 win over Mangueira in 1909, among the largest margins in Brazilian football history.

Rivalries

The championship features some of Brazil's most storied derbies: the Fla-Flu (Flamengo vs. Fluminense), the Clássico dos Milhões (Flamengo vs. Vasco), and the Clássico da Amizade (Botafogo vs. Fluminense).
