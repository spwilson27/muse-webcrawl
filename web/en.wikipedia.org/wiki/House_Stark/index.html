From: A Song of Ice and Fire / Game of Thrones

House Stark is a fictional noble family in George R. R. Martin's fantasy series A Song of Ice and Fire and its television adaptation Game of Thrones. The Starks rule the North, the largest region of Westeros, from their ancestral seat of Winterfell, and serve as the principal protagonists of the story.

History in the novels

The Starks descend from Brandon the Builder, who legendarily raised Winterfell and the Wall thousands of years before the main narrative. They ruled as Kings in the North until Torrhen Stark knelt to Aegon the Conqueror. At the series' opening, Lord Eddard "Ned" Stark is the head of the house; his execution in King's Landing ignites the War of the Five Kings, in which his son Robb is declared King in the North before being murdered at the Red Wedding.

Members

Principal members include Ned and his wife Catelyn (née Tully), and their children Robb, Sansa, Arya, Bran, and Rickon, along with Ned's supposed bastard Jon Snow. Stark children are associated with direwolf companions, and Bran and others display skinchanger abilities.

Themes

The house motto, "Winter Is Coming," functions both as warning and philosophy. The Starks are characterised by honour, duty, and stoicism, often to their political cost.
