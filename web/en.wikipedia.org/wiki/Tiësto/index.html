Tiësto


Tiësto performing live

Tijs Michiel Verwest (born 17 January 1969), known professionally as Tiësto, is a Dutch DJ and record producer. Formerly a leading figure in trance music, he later moved toward electronic dance music and big-room house. He has been called "the Godfather of EDM" and was named "the Greatest DJ of All Time" by Mix magazine in a 2010/2011 poll.

Career

Tiësto rose to prominence in the late 1990s with remixes and tracks such as his remix of "Silence" by Delerium featuring Sarah McLachlan. His debut solo album In My Memory (2001) included several hits, and in 2004 he became the first DJ to perform live at the Olympic Games, playing at the opening ceremony in Athens. Albums such as Just Be (2004) and Elements of Life (2007) — the latter nominated for a Grammy — cemented his global popularity.

Later work

In the 2010s Tiësto shifted toward mainstream dance and pop collaborations, scoring international hits with singles such as "Red Lights", "Secrets" with KSHMR, "The Business" (2020), and "Don't Be Shy" with Karol G. He won the Grammy Award for Best Remixed Recording in 2015 for his remix of John Legend's "All of Me", and he remains one of the world's highest-earning and most in-demand DJs.
