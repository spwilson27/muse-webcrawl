Las Vegas, often known simply as Vegas, is the most populous city in the U.S. state of Nevada and the county seat of Clark County. It is an internationally renowned resort city known primarily for gambling, shopping, fine dining, entertainment and nightlife. Most of the famous casino-hotels stand not in the city proper but along the Las Vegas Strip in the unincorporated community of Paradise — the densest concentration of mega-resorts on Earth, with themed landmarks from a pyramid (Luxor) to a half-scale Eiffel Tower.

History

Spanish traders named the site las vegas ("the meadows") for its springs. The railroad town of 1905 boomed when Nevada legalised gambling in 1931 and construction of the nearby Hoover Dam brought workers and federal money. Organised crime figures such as Bugsy Siegel (the Flamingo, 1946) built the early Strip; Howard Hughes and then corporations (Steve Wynn's Mirage, 1989) corporatised it. The city reinvented itself repeatedly — Rat Pack glamour, family-friendly 1990s, luxury-and-spectacle 2000s — and added major-league sports with the NFL's Raiders (2020) and the NHL's Golden Knights, plus Formula One's Las Vegas Grand Prix (2023) and Super Bowl LVIII (2024). Its water comes overwhelmingly from the Colorado River via Lake Mead, making drought a strategic issue for the desert metropolis.
