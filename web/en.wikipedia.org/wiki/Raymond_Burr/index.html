Raymond Burr

Raymond William Stacy Burr (May 21, 1917 – September 12, 1993) was a Canadian actor known for his lengthy Hollywood film career and his title roles in the television dramas Perry Mason and Ironside.

Early life

Burr was born in New Westminster, British Columbia, to Minerva Annette and William Johnston Burr. He spent part of his youth in China, where his father worked as a trade agent, and attended Berkeley High School in California.

Career

Burr began acting on stage and in radio before appearing in films, often playing villains in film noir, including the murderer in Alfred Hitchcock's Rear Window (1954). In 1957 he was cast as defense attorney Perry Mason in the CBS series based on Erle Stanley Gardner's novels, winning two Emmy Awards. From 1967 to 1975 he starred as Robert T. Ironside, a wheelchair-using police consultant, in Ironside. He reprised the Perry Mason role in a series of television films from 1985 until his death.

Personal life and death

Burr was a noted philanthropist and cultivated orchids as a business and hobby. He died of liver cancer in 1993 at his ranch in Healdsburg, California.
