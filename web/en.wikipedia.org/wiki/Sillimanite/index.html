Sillimanite

Sillimanite is an aluminosilicate mineral with the chemical formula Al2SiO5. It is named after the American chemist Benjamin Silliman (1779–1864). Sillimanite is one of three polymorphs of Al2SiO5, the others being andalusite and kyanite.

Properties and occurrence

Sillimanite is the high-temperature polymorph, stable in high-grade metamorphic rocks such as gneiss and hornfels. It typically forms slender prismatic or fibrous crystals; the fibrous variety is called fibrolite. It is used as a refractory material, and its distribution helps geologists estimate the temperature and pressure conditions of metamorphism.
