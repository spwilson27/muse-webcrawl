Voyah (Chinese: 岚图, Lántú) is a Chinese luxury electric vehicle brand owned by the state-owned automaker Dongfeng Motor Corporation. Announced in 2019 and formally launched in July 2020, Voyah is Dongfeng's premium new-energy vehicle marque, headquartered in Wuhan, and is operated through Voyah Automobile Technology Company.

History and models

Voyah's first production model, the Free, a mid-to-large electric SUV offered in battery-electric and range-extender forms, went on sale in 2021. It was followed by the Dreamer (also marketed as the Dream), a luxury MPV launched in 2022 and billed as among the fastest production MPVs, and the Passion (Chinese name Zhuiguang), an executive electric sedan launched in 2023 featuring semi-solid-state battery options. A smaller SUV, the Courage (Zhiyin), co-developed with Huawei technology, followed in 2024.

Technology and markets

Voyah vehicles use Dongfeng's ESSA electric architecture and the SOA electronic/electrical platform, with features such as air suspension, triple-screen dashboards, and — in later models — Huawei's Qiankun intelligent driving system and HarmonyOS cockpit. The brand began exporting in 2022, entering Norway first and subsequently other European and Middle Eastern markets, positioning itself against both domestic premium EV makers such as Nio and established European luxury brands.
