Harvard Law School

Harvard Law School (HLS)
Founded: 1817
Location: Cambridge, Massachusetts
Parent: Harvard University
Degrees: JD, LLM, SJD

Harvard Law School is the law school of Harvard University, founded in 1817 and the oldest continuously operating law school in the United States.

History

Under Dean Christopher Columbus Langdell from 1870, HLS pioneered the case method of legal education and the Socratic classroom model adopted nationwide. Its library in Langdell Hall is the world's largest academic law library.

Academics

HLS grants the Juris Doctor, LL.M. and S.J.D., runs extensive clinics, and publishes the Harvard Law Review (founded 1887), edited by students. It is consistently among the most selective and highly ranked law schools.

Alumni

Graduates include US presidents Barack Obama and Rutherford B. Hayes, many Supreme Court justices including Chief Justice John Roberts, and leaders in law, business and politics.
