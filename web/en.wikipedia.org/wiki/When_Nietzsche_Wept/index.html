When Nietzsche Wept

Author: Irvin D. Yalom
Published: 1992
Genre: Philosophical novel

When Nietzsche Wept is a 1992 novel by Irvin D. Yalom, the American existential psychiatrist and author. Set in Vienna in 1882, it imagines a fictional course of treatment in which the physician Josef Breuer — a mentor of the young Sigmund Freud — is persuaded by Lou Andreas-Salomé to treat the philosopher Friedrich Nietzsche for his despair and suicidal thoughts, without Nietzsche knowing he is being treated.

Plot and themes

Under the pretext of treating Nietzsche's migraines, Breuer engages him in daily conversations; in exchange, Nietzsche undertakes to "treat" Breuer's own obsession with his former patient, Anna O. (Bertha Pappenheim). The two men thus conduct a mutual, disguised analysis — a fictional origin story for psychotherapy — exploring themes of free will, fate, love, mortality and the burdens of genius. The novel became an international bestseller and was adapted into a film in 2007.
