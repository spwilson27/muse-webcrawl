JoongAng Ilbo

The JoongAng Ilbo (Korean: 중앙일보; lit. "Central Daily") is a South Korean daily newspaper published in Seoul. Founded on September 22, 1965, by Lee Byung-chul, the founder of the Samsung Group, it is one of the three major newspapers in South Korea, alongside the Chosun Ilbo and the Dong-a Ilbo.

History

The newspaper grew out of the Tongyang Broadcasting Corporation media empire. It later became associated with the JoongAng Media Network. The JoongAng Ilbo formerly published the English-language Korea JoongAng Daily in alliance with the International Herald Tribune.

Position

The paper is generally regarded as holding a conservative editorial stance in South Korean politics.
