Clanwilliam, Western Cape

ProvinceWestern Cape
CountrySouth Africa
Established1808

Clanwilliam is a town in the Olifants River valley in the Western Cape province of South Africa, about 200 kilometres (120 mi) north of Cape Town. It is the seat of the Cederberg Local Municipality and lies at the foot of the Cederberg mountains.

Economy

The town is the centre of the rooibos tea industry: the endemic rooibos plant grows only in the surrounding Cederberg region, and Rooibos Ltd, a major processor, is based there. Citrus farming and tourism — including rock art sites and the Clanwilliam Dam on the Olifants River — are also important.

History

Originally called Jan Disselsvlei, the town was renamed in 1814 by Sir John Cradock after his father-in-law, the Earl of Clanwilliam. It is one of the ten oldest towns in South Africa.
