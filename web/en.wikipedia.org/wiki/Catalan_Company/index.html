Catalan Company

The Catalan Company, or Company of the East, was a free company of mercenaries led by Roger de Flor, formed in 1302 from Catalan and Aragonese Almogavar veterans of the War of the Sicilian Vespers and hired by Byzantine emperor Andronikos II to fight the Ottoman Turks.

History

After victories in Anatolia, friction with Byzantium led to Roger's assassination in 1305 and a devastating Catalan revenge campaign in Thrace. The Company then seized the Duchy of Athens after crushing Walter of Brienne at the Battle of Halmyros (1311), ruling Athens and Thebes under Aragonese sovereignty until the 1380s. Ramon Muntaner's chronicle is the main source.
