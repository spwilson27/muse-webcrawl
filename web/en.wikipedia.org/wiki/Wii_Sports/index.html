Wii Sports

Developer: Nintendo EAD

Publisher: Nintendo

Release: November 19, 2006

Genre: Sports simulation

Wii Sports is a 2006 sports simulation video game developed and published by Nintendo for the Wii video game console. The game was released in North America along with the Wii on November 19, 2006, and in Japan, Australia, and Europe the following month. It was included as a pack-in game with the console in all territories except Japan, making it the first sports game included with the launch of a Nintendo system since Mario's Tennis for the Virtual Boy in 1995.

The game is a collection of five sports simulations — tennis, baseball, bowling, golf, and boxing — designed to demonstrate the motion-sensing capabilities of the Wii Remote. Players use their Mii characters as avatars and mimic real-life sporting actions, such as swinging the controller like a tennis racket.

Gameplay

Each of the five sports can be played alone or with others, and most modes allow up to four players. The game tracks players' skill levels in each sport, awarding points that raise or lower their rating toward the "Pro" class of 1000 points. A fitness test mode estimates a player's "Wii Fitness Age" based on performance in a selection of challenges.

Reception

Wii Sports received generally favorable reviews and was a massive commercial success. By the end of 2017, it had sold over 82 million copies worldwide, making it Nintendo's best-selling game of all time and one of the best-selling video games overall. Critics praised its accessibility and its role in drawing non-gamers to the console, though the simplicity of some sports was noted.

Legacy

The game received sequels and spin-offs including Wii Sports Resort (2009), Wii Sports Club (2013), and Nintendo Switch Sports (2022). It is frequently cited as a key example of the Wii's appeal to casual and family audiences.
