Overview

The Secret of Monkey Island is a 1990 point-and-click adventure game developed and published by Lucasfilm Games. Set in a fictional version of the Caribbean during the age of piracy, it follows Guybrush Threepwood, a young man who arrives on Mêlée Island hoping to become a pirate. To prove himself he must complete three trials, and along the way he falls in love with Governor Elaine Marley and clashes with the ghost pirate LeChuck, who kidnaps Elaine and flees to the mysterious Monkey Island.

Gameplay and design

Designed by Ron Gilbert with Tim Schafer and Dave Grossman, the game uses the SCUMM engine and a verb-based interface. It is celebrated for its comic writing, insult sword fighting — duels won by trading clever insults rather than reflexes — and player-friendly design that avoided unwinnable states and player death, a deliberate departure from contemporary adventures.

The Secret of Monkey Island was a critical and commercial success and spawned a long-running series, including Monkey Island 2: LeChuck's Revenge (1991) and Return to Monkey Island (2022). A special edition with redrawn graphics and voice acting was released in 2009. It is widely ranked among the greatest video games of all time.
