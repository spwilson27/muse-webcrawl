Turn: Washington's Spies

Turn: Washington's Spies (stylised as TURИ: Washington's Spies) is an American historical drama television series that aired on AMC from April 6, 2014, to August 12, 2017, running for four seasons and 40 episodes. Developed by Craig Silverstein, it is based on Alexander Rose's 2006 book Washington's Spies: The Story of America's First Spy Ring.

Premise

Set during the American Revolutionary War, the series follows Abraham Woodhull, a cabbage farmer in British-occupied Setauket, Long Island, who is recruited by his childhood friend, Continental officer Benjamin Tallmadge, to spy for General George Washington. Woodhull and his associates form the Culper Ring, passing intelligence on British troop movements to the Continental Army while evading capture by British officers and Loyalist forces.

Reception

The series received generally favourable reviews for its period atmosphere, production design and performances, though some critics noted its slow pacing in the first season. It has been praised for popularising the history of the Culper Ring, while historians have observed that it dramatises and alters a number of historical events and details.
