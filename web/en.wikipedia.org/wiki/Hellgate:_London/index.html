Hellgate: London

DeveloperFlagship Studios
PublisherNamco Bandai Games / Electronic Arts
Release31 October 2007
GenreAction role-playing
PlatformMicrosoft Windows

Hellgate: London is an action role-playing video game developed by Flagship Studios, a company founded by former Blizzard North staff including Bill Roper. Set in a demon-invaded, post-apocalyptic London in the year 2038, it combined first- and third-person combat with the randomised loot systems of the Diablo series.

Gameplay and reception

Players choose among six classes in three factions — Templar, Cabalist and Hunter — and fight through procedurally generated levels based on London locations such as the Underground. Despite anticipation, the game launched with numerous bugs and a controversial subscription model; reviews were mixed. Flagship Studios closed in 2008 and the original servers shut down in 2009. The game was later revived by HanbitSoft and T3Fun, and a single-player version was released on Steam in 2018.
