London Museum of Water & Steam
Location: Kew Bridge, London
Founded: 1975 (as Kew Bridge Steam Museum)

The London Museum of Water & Steam is a museum at Kew Bridge in Brentford, London, housed in a historic waterworks pumping station. It preserves large steam engines once used to pump London's drinking water.

Collection

Highlights include the Dancers End and Waddon engines and the 90-inch and 100-inch Cornish beam engines, among the largest surviving examples of their kind, some demonstrated in steam on special days.

Site

The Grade I listed standpipe tower and engine houses date from the Victorian expansion of London's water supply. The museum was formerly known as the Kew Bridge Steam Museum.
