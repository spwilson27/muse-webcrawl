Single coil

A single coil is a type of magnetic transducer, or pickup, for the electric guitar and bass. It electromagnetically converts string vibration into an electrical signal using a single coil of wire wound around one or more magnets.

Characteristics

Single-coil pickups are known for a bright, clear, cutting tone, as heard in classic Fender Stratocaster and Telecaster guitars. Their main drawback is susceptibility to electromagnetic interference, producing mains hum; this problem motivated the invention of the humbucker, whose second coil cancels the noise.

Notable designs include the Fender single coils, the Gibson P-90 (a wide single coil with a fatter sound), and noiseless stacked-coil variants that retain single-coil character with reduced hum.
