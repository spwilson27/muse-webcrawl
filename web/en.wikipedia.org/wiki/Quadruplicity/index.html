Quadruplicity

In astrology, a quadruplicity is a grouping of three zodiac signs belonging to the same quality or modality: cardinal, fixed, or mutable. Each quadruplicity contains four signs — one of each element (fire, earth, air, water) — and each sign belongs to exactly one quadruplicity.

The three qualities

  • Cardinal signs — Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn — begin the seasons and are associated in astrological interpretation with initiative and action.
  • Fixed signs — Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius — fall in the middle of the seasons and are associated with persistence and stability.
  • Mutable signs — Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces — end the seasons and are associated with adaptability and change.

Astrologers examine the balance of planets among the quadruplicities in a natal chart as part of assessing temperament and emphasis. Quadruplicities, together with the triplicities (elements), form the basic classification of the twelve signs.
