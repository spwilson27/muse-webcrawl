Joomla is a free and open-source content management system (CMS) for publishing web content, developed by Open Source Matters, Inc. It is built on a model–view–controller web application framework that can be used independently of the CMS.

History

Joomla was created in 2005 as a fork of the Mambo CMS after disagreements between the Mambo development team and its corporate sponsor. Its name derives from the Swahili word jumla, meaning "all together" or "as a whole".

Features

The system includes page caching, RSS feeds, printable versions of pages, blogs, search, and support for language internationalization. Thousands of free and commercial extensions are available from the Joomla Extensions Directory.
