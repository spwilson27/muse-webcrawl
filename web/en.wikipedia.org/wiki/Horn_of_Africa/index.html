Horn of Africa

The Horn of Africa is the easternmost extension of African land that juts into the Arabian Sea and the Indian Ocean, south of the Gulf of Aden. It comprises Ethiopia, Eritrea, Somalia and Djibouti (and often Kenya), about 2 million square kilometres inhabited by well over 100 million people.

The Horn was home to the Aksumite Empire and the Land of Punt known to ancient Egypt. Its position commanding the Bab el-Mandeb strait between the Red Sea and the Indian Ocean makes it one of the world's most strategic regions. It is also one of the most conflict- and drought-stricken: the Ogaden War, the Eritrean independence war, Somalia's state collapse in 1991 and civil war, Ethiopia's Tigray war (2020-2022) and recurrent famines shape its recent history.
