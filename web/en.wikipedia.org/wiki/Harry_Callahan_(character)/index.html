Inspector Harold Francis "Dirty Harry" Callahan is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Dirty Harry film series, portrayed by Clint Eastwood in five films between 1971 and 1988. Callahan is a San Francisco Police Department homicide inspector known for his unorthodox, violent methods and his disregard for rules in pursuit of criminals.

Character

Callahan carries a Smith & Wesson Model 29 revolver chambered in .44 Magnum, which he famously describes as "the most powerful handgun in the world". His catchphrases include "Go ahead, make my day" (from Sudden Impact) and the "Do I feel lucky?" speech from the first film. He is nicknamed "Dirty Harry" because he is assigned the cases nobody else wants and is willing to get his hands dirty.

Films

The series comprises Dirty Harry (1971), Magnum Force (1973), The Enforcer (1976), Sudden Impact (1983), and The Dead Pool (1988). The first film, directed by Don Siegel, pitted Callahan against a serial killer based on the real Zodiac Killer and became one of the most influential police films ever made.

Legacy

Callahan became an iconic figure in American cinema, sparking lasting debate about vigilantism, police conduct, and the Miranda rights era in which the films appeared. The character is frequently ranked among the greatest screen heroes and antiheroes.
