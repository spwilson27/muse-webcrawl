Beyond (band)

OriginHong Kong
Years active1983–2005 (reunions 2003)
GenresRock, Cantopop rock
Key membersWong Ka Kui, Wong Ka Keung, Wong Chung Yin (Yip Sai Wing), Paul Wong

Beyond is a Hong Kong rock band formed in 1983, widely regarded as the most influential and successful Chinese-language rock group. The band is best known for the songwriting and vocals of its founder Wong Ka Kui, whose anthems — including "Boundless Oceans, Vast Skies" (海闊天空), "Glorious Years" (光輝歲月) and "Really Love You" (真的愛你) — addressed themes of idealism, peace and social concern and became classics across the Chinese-speaking world.

Career

Beyond began as an underground band before signing to a major label in the late 1980s. Albums such as Secret Police (1988), Beyond IV (1989) and Deliberate (1991) established them commercially. In the early 1990s the band moved to Japan to develop its career.

On 30 June 1993, Wong Ka Kui died after falling from a stage platform during a television show recording in Tokyo. The remaining members — Wong Ka Keung, Paul Wong and Yip Sai Wing — continued as a trio through the 1990s before pursuing solo work; the band formally disbanded in 2005. Beyond's music remains enormously popular, and "Boundless Oceans, Vast Skies" is a staple at gatherings and protests in Hong Kong and beyond.
