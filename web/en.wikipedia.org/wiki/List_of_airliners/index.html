List of airliners

An airliner is a type of aircraft for transporting passengers and air cargo. This list covers notable civil airliners by manufacturer, from early piston types through the jet age to modern wide-body aircraft.

Boeing

  • Model 247 (1933) — early all-metal monoplane airliner
  • 314 Clipper (1938) — flying boat
  • 307 Stratoliner (1938) — first pressurized airliner
  • 377 Stratocruiser (1947)
  • 707 (1958) — first successful U.S. jetliner
  • 720 (1960)
  • 727 (1964) — trijet
  • 737 (1968) — best-selling jetliner family (incl. 737 MAX)
  • 747 (1970) — first wide-body "jumbo jet"
  • 757 (1983) — narrow-body twinjet
  • 767 (1982) — wide-body twinjet
  • 777 (1995) — large twinjet ("triple seven")
  • 787 Dreamliner (2011) — composite midsize twinjet

Airbus

  • A300 (1974) — first Airbus type; first twin-aisle twinjet
  • A310 (1983)
  • A318 / A319 / A320 / A321 (1988–) — A320 family incl. A320neo
  • A330 (1994) — wide-body twinjet (incl. A330neo)
  • A340 (1993) — four-engine long-haul
  • A350 (2015) — composite wide-body twinjet
  • A380 (2007) — double-deck superjumbo; largest passenger airliner
  • A220 (2016; originally Bombardier CSeries)

Douglas / McDonnell Douglas

  • DC-3 (1936) — revolutionary piston airliner
  • DC-4, DC-6, DC-7 — post-war piston types
  • DC-8 (1959) — jetliner
  • DC-9 (1965) — short-haul twinjet; developed into MD-80/MD-90 and Boeing 717
  • DC-10 (1971) — trijet wide-body
  • MD-11 (1990)

Lockheed

  • Model 10 Electra (1935)
  • Constellation / Super Constellation (1943/1951)
  • L-188 Electra (1959) — turboprop
  • L-1011 TriStar (1972) — trijet wide-body
