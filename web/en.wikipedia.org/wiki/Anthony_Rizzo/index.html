Anthony Vincent Rizzo (born 1989) is an American former professional baseball first baseman. He played in Major League Baseball for the San Diego Padres, Chicago Cubs and New York Yankees.

Career

Drafted by the Boston Red Sox in 2007, Rizzo was traded to San Diego and debuted in 2011, then was traded to the Cubs in 2012. In Chicago he became a three-time All-Star, won a Gold Glove Award, and was a core member of the 2016 Cubs team that won the World Series, the franchise's first championship in 108 years; Rizzo recorded the final out of Game 7. He was traded to the Yankees in 2021.

Personal life

A survivor of Hodgkin's lymphoma, diagnosed in 2008, Rizzo founded the Anthony Rizzo Family Foundation to support cancer research and families affected by the disease.
