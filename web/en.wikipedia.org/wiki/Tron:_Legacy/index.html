Tron: Legacy

Tron: Legacy is a 2010 American science fiction action film directed by Joseph Kosinski (in his feature directorial debut) and written by Edward Kitsis and Adam Horowitz, based on a story by Brian Klugman and Lee Sternthal. It is the sequel to the 1982 film Tron. The film stars Garrett Hedlund, Olivia Wilde and Jeff Bridges, who reprises his role as Kevin Flynn, alongside Bruce Boxleitner, James Frain, Beau Garrett and Michael Sheen.

Plot

Sam Flynn, the rebellious 27-year-old son of Kevin Flynn, investigates his father's disappearance and is pulled into the Grid, the digital world his father created. There he finds Kevin trapped for two decades by Clu, a tyrannical program made in his likeness. With the help of Quorra, a unique "isomorphic algorithm", Sam and his father race to reach the portal back to the real world before Clu can use it to invade the physical world with his army.

Production

Development of a sequel to Tron stretched back to the 1990s. Filmed in Vancouver in 2009, the production pioneered techniques for digital de-aging, creating a younger version of Bridges for Clu. The French electronic duo Daft Punk composed the acclaimed orchestral-electronic score and appear in cameo as DJs. The film was released in December 2010 in Disney Digital 3D, RealD 3D and IMAX 3D.

Reception

The film grossed over $400 million worldwide against a $170 million budget. Reviews praised its visuals, production design and score, while criticizing its plot and character development. It was nominated for an Academy Award for Best Sound Editing. The film spawned an animated series, Tron: Uprising, and after years of development a follow-up film, Tron: Ares, entered production.
