Imaging

Examples of images produced by different imaging modalities

Imaging is the representation or reproduction of an object's form; especially a visual representation (i.e., the formation of an image). Imaging technology is the application of materials and methods to create, preserve, or duplicate images.

Computer imaging is the use of computers to create, preserve, or duplicate images. Computer imaging is a subfield of computer science that studies computational commanding methods and mathematical models of image scenarios.

Imaging chain

The foundation of imaging is the imaging chain, which is the system of components involved in forming, capturing, processing, and displaying an image. The imaging chain involves a series of steps, including image capture (acquisition), image storage, image processing, and image display.

Techniques

Imaging techniques span many scientific fields. Medical imaging includes radiography (X-rays), computed tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), ultrasound, and nuclear medicine techniques such as PET and SPECT. In astronomy, telescopes produce images across the electromagnetic spectrum. Microscopy provides images of structures too small for the naked eye to resolve, while remote sensing produces images of the Earth's surface from satellites and aircraft.

Digital imaging

Digital imaging creates digital images, typically from a physical object, through the use of image sensors and scanners. The resulting images are stored as arrays of pixels, each carrying intensity and color information, and can be processed, compressed, transmitted, and displayed electronically.
