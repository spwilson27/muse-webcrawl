Ethylene

Ethylene (IUPAC name: ethene) is a hydrocarbon with the formula C2H4. It is a colourless, flammable gas with a faintly sweet and musky odour. It is the simplest alkene and the most produced organic compound in the world, mainly used to make polyethylene.

FormulaC2H4
Molar mass28.054 g/mol
AppearanceColourless gas
Boiling point−103.7 °C
StructurePlanar, C=C double bond

Production and uses

Ethylene is produced by steam cracking of hydrocarbons such as ethane, propane and naphtha. Its major uses are polyethylene plastics, ethylene oxide (for ethylene glycol antifreeze and detergents), ethylene dichloride for PVC, and ethylbenzene for styrene.

Plant hormone

In plants ethylene is a gaseous hormone that promotes fruit ripening — bananas and tomatoes are commercially ripened with it — as well as leaf abscission and senescence. The "triple response" in seedlings is a classic assay.
