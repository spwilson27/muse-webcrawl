The Bombardier Dash 8 or Q-Series, previously known as the de Havilland Canada Dash 8, is a series of twin-engine, medium-range turboprop regional airliners. Introduced by de Havilland Canada in 1984, it has been produced in four series with capacities from 37 to 90 passengers.

Variants

The Series 100 (37 seats) was followed by the stretched Series 200 and 300, and the substantially redesigned, faster Q400 (70–90 seats), which features active noise and vibration suppression — hence the "Q" for quiet. Over 1,250 Dash 8s have been built.

Operators

Major operators have included Air Canada Jazz, Alaska Airlines/Horizon Air, QantasLink and Widerøe. In 2019 Bombardier sold the program to Longview Aviation Capital, which revived the De Havilland Aircraft of Canada name. See also De Havilland Canada.
