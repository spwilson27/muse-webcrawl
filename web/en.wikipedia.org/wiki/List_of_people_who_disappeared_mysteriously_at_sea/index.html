List of people who disappeared mysteriously at sea

This is a list of people who disappeared at sea in circumstances that remain unexplained, excluding those lost in known shipwrecks or air crashes. Many cases involve vessels found abandoned with their crews missing, yachtsmen lost during solo voyages, and passengers or crew who vanished from ships underway.

Crew of the Kaz II2007Australian catamaran found drifting off Queensland with engines running and crew of three missing
Andrew McAuley2007Australian kayaker lost near the end of a solo Tasman Sea crossing; his kayak was recovered

Other frequently cited disappearances include the crew of the wartime freighter Ourang Medan (a story of disputed authenticity), the yachtsman Arthur Jones ("Buster" Crabb cases are separate), and numerous solo sailors lost during circumnavigation attempts. Theories commonly advanced include storms, piracy, mutiny, insurance fraud, and voluntary disappearance, but in most listed cases no conclusion has been established.
