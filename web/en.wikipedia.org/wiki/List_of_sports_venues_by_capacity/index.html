The following is a list of sports venues by capacity, ranked by the maximum number of spectators each venue can accommodate. Motor racing circuits dominate the very top of the list because of their enormous grandstand and infield capacities, while the largest enclosed stadiums are used for association football, American football and mass games.

Notes on capacity

Capacities are the official figures reported for each venue and may vary by configuration: many stadiums reduce capacity for concerts or particular events, and standing-room figures are treated differently by different leagues and governing bodies. Lists of stadiums by sport apply stricter criteria, usually seated capacity only.
