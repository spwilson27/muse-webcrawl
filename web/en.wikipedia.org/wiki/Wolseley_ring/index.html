Wolseley ring

A Wolseley ring is a type of liquid-propellant rocket engine thrust chamber cooling and fuel-ring arrangement used in experimental rocket propulsion, in which propellant is distributed around the chamber through a ring manifold before being injected. The design takes its name from the Wolseley engineering works associated with its development work in British rocket research.

Function

The ring serves as a plenum that evens out pressure differences around the circumference of the injector face, helping to ensure uniform mixture ratios across the injection elements and reducing combustion instability. Similar annular manifolds remain common in modern liquid rocket engines.
