John Elway

John Albert Elway Jr. (born June 28, 1960) is an American former professional football quarterback who spent his entire 16-season NFL career with the Denver Broncos. Regarded as one of the greatest quarterbacks of all time, he led Denver to two Super Bowl championships at the end of his career.

Playing career

A two-sport star at Stanford, Elway was drafted first overall in 1983 by the Baltimore Colts but refused to play for them and was traded to Denver; he had also been drafted by the New York Yankees in baseball. Famous for late comebacks — most notably "The Drive" against Cleveland in the 1987 AFC Championship Game — Elway reached five Super Bowls. After three losses, he won Super Bowls XXXII and XXXIII, earning MVP honours in the latter, and retired in 1999 with then-record totals of 51,475 passing yards and 300 touchdowns.

Executive career

Elway returned to the Broncos as executive vice president of football operations and general manager in 2011, building the team that won Super Bowl 50 behind Peyton Manning and a dominant defence. He was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2004.
