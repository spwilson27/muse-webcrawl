Atal Bihari Vajpayee

Atal Bihari Vajpayee
Born: 25 December 1924, Gwalior, British India
Died: 16 August 2018, New Delhi, India
Prime Minister of India: 1996; 1998–2004
Party: Bharatiya Janata Party
Award: Bharat Ratna (2015)

Atal Bihari Vajpayee (25 December 1924 – 16 August 2018) was an Indian politician and poet who served three terms as the prime minister of India, first for a term of 13 days in 1996, then for a period of 13 months from 1998 to 1999, followed by a full term from 1999 to 2004. He was the first non-Congress prime minister of India to serve a full term in office.

A co-founder of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), Vajpayee was a member of the Indian Parliament for over five decades, having been elected ten times to the Lok Sabha and twice to the Rajya Sabha. He also served as the Minister of External Affairs from 1977 to 1979 in the cabinet of Morarji Desai.

Prime ministership

During his third term as prime minister, India conducted the Pokhran-II nuclear tests in May 1998, asserting itself as a nuclear weapons state despite international sanctions. His government pursued economic liberalisation and major infrastructure projects, notably the Golden Quadrilateral highway network connecting India's four largest metropolitan cities. Vajpayee also made repeated efforts to normalise relations with Pakistan, travelling by bus to Lahore in 1999, although the Kargil War followed months later; further peace initiatives culminated in the 2004 Islamabad summit.

Poetry and later life

Vajpayee was also a noted Hindi poet, whose published collections include Meri Ikyaavan Kavitayen ("My Fifty-One Poems"). He retired from active politics in 2005–06 due to health concerns. In 2015 he was awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour, by President Pranab Mukherjee. He died on 16 August 2018 in New Delhi at the age of 93, and his birthday, 25 December, is observed in India as Good Governance Day.
