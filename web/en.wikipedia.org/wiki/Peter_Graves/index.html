Peter Graves

Peter Graves (born Peter Duesler Aurness; March 18, 1926 – March 14, 2010) was an American actor best known for starring as Jim Phelps in the CBS television series Mission: Impossible (1967–1973) and its revival (1988–1990), and for his deadpan role as Captain Clarence Oveur in the comedy film Airplane! (1980).

Life and career

Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota, Graves served in the US Army Air Forces during World War II, attended the University of Minnesota, and moved to Hollywood in the early 1950s. He appeared in films including Stalag 17 (1953) and Charles Laughton's The Night of the Hunter (1955), and starred in the western series Fury (1955–1960). He was the younger brother of actor James Arness of Gunsmoke fame.

Mission: Impossible and later work

Graves joined Mission: Impossible in its second season, replacing Steven Hill as the leader of the Impossible Missions Force, and won a Golden Globe Award in 1971. He later hosted and narrated the A&E documentary series Biography for many years, and sent up his authoritative image in commercials and in Airplane! and its sequel. He died of a heart attack at his Los Angeles home four days short of his 84th birthday.
