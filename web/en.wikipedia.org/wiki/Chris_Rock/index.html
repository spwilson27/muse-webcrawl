Chris Rock

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Christopher Julius Rock (born February 7, 1965) is an American comedian, actor, and filmmaker. Known for his work in stand-up comedy, television, and film, he is widely regarded as one of the greatest stand-up comedians of all time. Rock gained prominence as a cast member of Saturday Night Live from 1990 to 1993.

Stand-up career

After leaving SNL, Rock achieved a breakthrough with his HBO specials, beginning with Bring the Pain (1996), which won two Emmy Awards and made him one of the most acclaimed comedians in America. Subsequent specials include Bigger & Blacker (1999), Never Scared (2004), Kill the Messenger (2008), Tamborine (2018) and Selective Outrage (2023), the latter streamed live on Netflix.

Film and television

Rock created and narrated the sitcom Everybody Hates Chris (2005–2009), loosely based on his youth, and headlined The Chris Rock Show on HBO. His film credits include New Jack City, Lethal Weapon 4, Dogma, the Madagascar animated films (as the voice of Marty the zebra), Grown Ups, Top Five (which he wrote and directed), and Spiral (2021). He has hosted the Academy Awards twice, in 2005 and 2016.

Oscars incident

At the 94th Academy Awards in March 2022, actor Will Smith walked onstage and slapped Rock during the live broadcast after Rock joked about Smith's wife, Jada Pinkett Smith; the incident drew worldwide attention and led to Smith's resignation from the Academy and a ten-year ban from its events.
