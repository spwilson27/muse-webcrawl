Twelve Variations on "Ah vous dirai-je maman"

The Twelve Variations on "Ah vous dirai-je maman", K. 265/300e, is a piano composition by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, written in Paris in 1778 when the composer was 22 years old. The work consists of a theme and twelve variations on the French folk song "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman", a melody best known in English as "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star".

Composition

Mozart composed the variations in the spring or summer of 1778, during his visit to Paris, probably as teaching material or for performance at private concerts. For a long time the work was wrongly dated to 1781–82 in Vienna. It was first published in Paris in 1785.

Music

The simple theme in C major is followed by twelve variations exploring virtuoso passagework, hand-crossing, a minor-mode variation (Variation VIII), a lyrical Adagio (Variation XI) and a brilliant finale in 3/4 time. Despite the simplicity of the tune, the set displays Mozart's inventiveness in figuration and character.

Legacy

The melody is shared with "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star", "Baa, Baa, Black Sheep" and the "Alphabet Song". Mozart's variations remain among his most popular piano works and are frequently performed and recorded.
