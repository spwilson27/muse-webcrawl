QuakeCon is an annual video game convention and bring-your-own-computer (BYOC) LAN party held in the Dallas, Texas area. Founded in 1996 by fans of id Software's game Quake, it grew into one of the largest LAN parties in the world and serves as a showcase event for id Software and, since the ZeniMax acquisition, Bethesda Softworks.

History

The first event, organised via IRC by Jim "Jaeger" Elson and others, drew about 30 people to a hotel in Garland, Texas, where John Carmack and other id staff visited informally. id Software soon adopted the event officially. QuakeCon became famous for its huge BYOC hall, volunteer-run organisation — long dubbed the "Woodstock of gaming" — and competitive Quake tournaments that helped pioneer professional esports, with players such as Johnathan "Fatal1ty" Wendel rising to prominence there.

Events

The convention traditionally features keynote presentations by id Software, panels, exhibitor booths, tabletop gaming, and tournaments in Quake, Doom and other titles. The 2020–2022 events were held virtually as "QuakeCon at Home" during the COVID-19 pandemic, returning to an in-person format in Grapevine, Texas, in 2023.
