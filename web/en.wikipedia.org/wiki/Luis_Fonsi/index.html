Luis Alfonso Rodríguez López-Cepero (born 15 April 1978), known professionally as Luis Fonsi, is a Puerto Rican singer, songwriter and actor. He is best known worldwide for the 2017 single "Despacito" featuring Daddy Yankee, whose music video became one of the most-viewed videos of all time on YouTube and which helped popularise Spanish-language music in the global mainstream market.

Early life and career beginnings

Fonsi was born in San Juan, Puerto Rico, and moved with his family to Orlando, Florida, at the age of ten. He studied voice performance at Florida State University's School of Music, where he sang with the university choir and recorded demo tapes in Miami. In 1998 he released his debut album, Comenzaré, which included the hit "Si Tú Quisieras" and established him in the Latin American market.

Breakthrough and ballad era

Through the 2000s Fonsi became one of Latin pop's leading romantic balladeers. His 2008 album Palabras del silencio included "No Me Doy por Vencido", which spent many weeks atop the Billboard Hot Latin Songs chart and became his signature ballad. The album Tierra firme (2011) produced the hits "Respira" and "Claridad".

"Despacito" and global success

In January 2017 Fonsi released "Despacito", featuring Puerto Rican rapper Daddy Yankee. The song topped charts in nearly 50 countries; a remix featuring Justin Bieber reached number one on the US Billboard Hot 100, where it tied the then-record of 16 weeks at the summit. The original video became the first on YouTube to reach billions of views. Fonsi followed the success with "Échame la Culpa" with Demi Lovato (2017) and the album Vida (2019), which also included "Calypso" and "Imposible".

Later work

Fonsi served as a coach on La Voz, the Spanish and Latin American versions of The Voice. His eleventh studio album, Ley de gravedad, appeared in 2022, followed by El viaje in 2024. He also performed at the opening ceremony of the 2019 Pan American Games in Lima.
