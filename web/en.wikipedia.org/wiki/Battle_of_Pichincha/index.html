The Battle of Pichincha took place on 24 May 1822, on the slopes of the Pichincha volcano above the city of Quito, in what is now Ecuador. It was a decisive engagement of the Spanish American wars of independence, in which the patriot army commanded by General Antonio José de Sucre defeated the Spanish royalist forces of Field Marshal Melchor Aymerich. The victory secured the liberation of Quito and its annexation to Gran Colombia.

Background

After the proclamation of the Free Province of Guayaquil in October 1820, patriot forces campaigned to liberate the highland cities of the Real Audiencia de Quito. Following setbacks at Huachi and successes at Riobamba, Sucre's army — composed of Gran Colombian troops, Guayaquileños, and auxiliaries from Peru and the Río de la Plata — advanced on Quito by an indirect route through the mountains, hoping to outflank the royalist defenses.

The battle

On the night of 23–24 May, Sucre's army climbed the slopes of the Pichincha volcano in order to descend on Quito from above. The ascent over difficult terrain in darkness and rain left the troops exhausted and strung out, and at around 9 a.m. the patriot vanguard unexpectedly encountered the royalist army, which had moved out to meet them. The fighting, at an altitude of some 3,500 metres, was confused and fierce. A decisive moment came when the British-led Albion Battalion ascended still higher ground to resupply the patriot battalions with ammunition and then joined the attack, turning the royalist flank. The young lieutenant Abdón Calderón became a patriot hero, reportedly continuing to fight despite grave wounds.

Aftermath

Royalist losses were heavy, with some 400 dead and over 1,200 taken prisoner in the subsequent capitulation, against roughly 200 patriot dead. On 25 May, Sucre entered Quito and accepted the surrender of the royalist forces. The territory of the former Audiencia was incorporated into Gran Colombia as its Southern Department. The 24th of May is commemorated in Ecuador as a national holiday, the Battle of Pichincha Day.
