Device

Devices range from simple mechanisms to complex electronics

A device is a thing made or adapted for a particular purpose, especially a piece of mechanical or electronic equipment. The word is used broadly for instruments, machines, tools and contrivances, and in computing for hardware components and peripherals — a usage extended in phrases such as "mobile device" for smartphones and tablets.

The term appears in many specialised senses:

  • Technology: a medical device is any instrument or implant used to diagnose, prevent or treat disease; a device driver is software that operates a hardware peripheral.
  • Law: "device" may denote a scheme or plan, and in heraldry an emblem or motto.
  • Literature and rhetoric: a literary or rhetorical device is a technique used by a writer to produce a particular effect.
  • Theatre: a plot device advances the narrative of a work of fiction.

In everyday modern usage, "device" increasingly refers to personal electronic equipment — phones, tablets, e-readers and wearable technology — connected to networks and services.
