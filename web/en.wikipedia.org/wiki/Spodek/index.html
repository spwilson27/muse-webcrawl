Spodek is a multipurpose arena complex in Katowice, Poland. Its name means "saucer" in Polish, a reference to the main hall's distinctive shape, which resembles a flying saucer tilted at an angle. Opened in 1971, it was for many years the largest indoor venue in Poland and remains an icon of Polish modernist architecture.

Architecture

The main arena's roof is an inclined tensegrity-like dome — one of the first large structures of its kind — giving the building its characteristic UFO silhouette. The complex also includes an ice rink, a gymnasium, and a hotel.

Events

Spodek has hosted major sporting events, including the 1976 Ice Hockey World Championships, European and World volleyball championships (Poland won the 2014 FIVB Volleyball Men's World Championship final there), handball tournaments, and the 2018 United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP24). It is also a noted esports venue, having hosted Intel Extreme Masters finals, and a regular stop for international concert tours.
