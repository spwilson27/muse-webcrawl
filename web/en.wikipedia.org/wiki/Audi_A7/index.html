The Audi A7 is an executive five-door liftback (also marketed as a four-door coupé) produced by the German manufacturer Audi since 2010. It shares its platform with the Audi A6 and is positioned between the A6 and the flagship A8 in Audi's range. A high-performance variant, the S7, and the range-topping RS 7 are also offered.

First generation (Type 4G; 2010–2018)

The first-generation A7 was unveiled at the Pinakothek der Moderne art museum in Munich in July 2010 and went on sale later that year. It was previewed by the Audi Sportback concept shown at the 2009 Detroit Auto Show. Engines included turbocharged petrol V6 units and a 3.0-litre TDI diesel, paired with a seven-speed S tronic dual-clutch gearbox or an eight-speed automatic, with quattro all-wheel drive on most versions. The S7 used a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8, while the RS 7, launched in 2013, produced up to 605 PS in Performance form.

Second generation (Type 4K; 2018–present)

The second-generation A7 was revealed in Ingolstadt in October 2017 and launched in 2018. Built on the MLB Evo platform, it adopted Audi's newer design language, a mild-hybrid system on most engines, and a dual touchscreen MMI infotainment system. A plug-in hybrid TFSI e variant joined the range, and the RS 7 Sportback continued with a 600 PS twin-turbo V8.
