Avolon is an Irish aircraft leasing company headquartered in Dublin. Founded in 2010 by Dómhnal Slattery and backed initially by private equity firms Cinven, CVC Capital Partners, and Oak Hill Capital, Avolon grew rapidly to become one of the world's largest aircraft lessors, owning and managing a fleet of several hundred passenger aircraft leased to airlines in dozens of countries.

History

Avolon launched in May 2010 with an initial capital raise of about $1.4 billion including debt facilities. It listed on the New York Stock Exchange in December 2014. In 2015–2016 the company was acquired by Bohai Leasing, part of China's HNA Group, in a deal valuing it at roughly $7.6 billion including debt, and it was taken private.

In 2017 Avolon completed the acquisition of the aircraft leasing business of CIT Group for about $10 billion, roughly doubling its fleet to more than 550 aircraft and consolidating its position as the world's third-largest aircraft lessor after AerCap and GECAS (later AerCap). The company has been a significant customer for new Airbus and Boeing aircraft, including large orders for the Airbus A320neo family and A330neo, and in 2023 it placed a major order for Boeing 737 MAX aircraft, and ordered Airbus A321neos. Avolon also took a leading role among lessors in the dispute over aircraft stranded in Russia following the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, recording impairments and pursuing insurance claims.

Operations

From its Dublin headquarters and offices in the United States, Singapore, Hong Kong, and elsewhere, Avolon buys new aircraft directly from manufacturers and through sale-and-leaseback transactions, funding them through the capital markets, where it is a frequent issuer of unsecured bonds. Its customers include many of the world's major airlines.
