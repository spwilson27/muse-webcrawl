Declaration on the Elimination of Discrimination against Women

The Declaration on the Elimination of Discrimination against Women is a human rights proclamation adopted by the United Nations General Assembly on 7 November 1967 (Resolution 2263 (XXII)).

The Declaration called on states to abolish laws and practices discriminating against women and to guarantee equality in political rights, education, employment and marriage. Though non-binding, it laid the groundwork for the binding 1979 Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW).
