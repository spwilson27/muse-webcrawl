List of prime ministers of the United Kingdom

The prime minister of the United Kingdom is the head of government. The office developed in the early 18th century; Robert Walpole, in office from 1721 to 1742, is generally regarded as the first prime minister. The prime minister is appointed by the monarch and is usually the leader of the party able to command a majority in the House of Commons. The official residence is 10 Downing Street, London.
