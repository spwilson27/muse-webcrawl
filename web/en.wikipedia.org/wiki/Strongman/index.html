Strongman is a strength sport in which athletes compete in events involving moving, lifting, and carrying extremely heavy or awkward objects. Unlike powerlifting, which tests maximal strength in three standard lifts, strongman events vary from contest to contest and reward overall athleticism, grip strength, and endurance as well as raw power. The term also refers historically to the stage performers of the 19th and early 20th centuries who demonstrated feats of strength in circuses and vaudeville.

Events

Signature events include the Atlas stones (loading heavy spherical stones onto platforms), the log lift for maximum weight or repetitions, the yoke walk, the farmer's walk with heavy implements in each hand, vehicle pulls, and deadlift variants — Hafþór Júlíus Björnsson set a deadlift world record of 501 kg in 2020.

History and competitions

The modern sport was popularised by the World's Strongest Man competition, first held in 1977. Mariusz Pudzianowski of Poland won the title a record five times. The Arnold Strongman Classic and the Rogue Invitational are other premier events, and the sport includes women's competitions, with athletes such as Donna Moore and Olga Liashchuk winning World's Strongest Woman.
