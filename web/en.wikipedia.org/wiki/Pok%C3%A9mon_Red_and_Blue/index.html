Pokémon Red and Blue

Pokémon Red and Blue
Developer: Game Freak
Publisher: Nintendo
Released: 1996 (Japan, as Red and Green); 1998 (North America)
Platform: Game Boy
Genre: Role-playing

Pokémon Red Version and Pokémon Blue Version are 1996 role-playing video games developed by Game Freak and published by Nintendo for the Game Boy. They are the first instalments of the Pokémon video game series, released in Japan as Pocket Monsters Red and Green (with a special Blue version following), and in North America and Europe in 1998–1999.

Gameplay

Players control a young Trainer travelling the Kanto region to catch and train creatures called Pokémon, battling other Trainers and the Gym Leaders on the way to the Pokémon League. The paired versions each contain some Pokémon unavailable in the other, encouraging players to trade via the Game Link Cable — a social mechanic conceived by creator Satoshi Tajiri from his childhood hobby of insect collecting. A famous glitch, MissingNo., became part of the games' lore.

Reception and legacy

Red and Blue ignited the worldwide Pokémon craze, selling over 31 million copies combined and launching one of the highest-grossing media franchises of all time. They were remade as Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen (2004) and re-released on the Nintendo 3DS Virtual Console in 2016.
