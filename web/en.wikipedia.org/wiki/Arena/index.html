Arena

An arena is a large enclosed platform, often circular or oval-shaped, designed to showcase theatre, musical performances, or sporting events.

Etymology and history

The word derives from Latin harena, sand, which covered the floor of ancient Roman amphitheatres such as the Colosseum, where gladiators fought. Arenas are surrounded by tiered seating for spectators.

Types

Indoor arenas host ice hockey, basketball, indoor soccer, wrestling, boxing, mixed martial arts and concerts. Examples include Madison Square Garden in New York. Modern arenas often bear corporate naming-rights sponsorship and include luxury suites, large video screens and retractable features.

Design

Unlike a stadium, which is typically larger and often open-air for field sports, an arena is generally enclosed and smaller, optimised for close viewing and acoustics. Sightlines, ice or court surfaces, and multi-purpose conversion are key design concerns.
