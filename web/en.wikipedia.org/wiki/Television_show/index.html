Television show

A television show, also called a television programme or simply a TV show, is any content produced for viewing on a television set and broadcast via over-the-air, satellite, cable, or streaming media. Most shows are either broadcasts of already-produced material, such as films, or original productions divided into episodes grouped in seasons or series.

Formats and genres

Television shows include scripted genres — drama, comedy, soap opera, animation, miniseries, and children's programming — and unscripted formats such as news, talk shows, game shows, reality television, sports, and documentaries. Episodes commonly run 22 minutes (half-hour slots with advertising) or about 42–45 minutes (hour slots), though streaming has made lengths more flexible.

Production and distribution

Shows are typically developed from a pitch and pilot episode, produced by a studio, and licensed to networks or platforms. Streaming services such as Netflix transformed distribution in the 2010s with full-season releases and on-demand viewing, while awards such as the Emmys recognize excellence in the medium.
