Institute of Energy

The Institute of Energy was a British professional body for engineers and others working in energy-related industries. Founded in 1927 as the Institution of Fuel Technology (later the Institute of Fuel), it promoted the scientific study and efficient use of fuels and energy.

History

The Institute of Fuel developed over the 20th century alongside the coal, gas, and electricity industries, publishing journals and holding conferences on combustion and energy technology. In 2003 it merged with the Institute of Petroleum to form the Energy Institute, a chartered professional body for the global energy sector based in London.

Activities

The institute organized technical meetings, published the Journal of the Institute of Energy, awarded medals and prizes for work in fuel and energy science, and accredited professional qualifications for its members, who could use post-nominal designations reflecting their grade of membership.
