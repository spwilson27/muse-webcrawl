World Science Festival Brisbane

World Science Festival Brisbane is an annual science festival held in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia, presented by Queensland Museum in partnership with the World Science Festival in New York.

History

Launched in 2016, it was the first international edition of the New York festival founded by physicist Brian Greene and Tracy Day. Brisbane secured exclusive rights for the Asia-Pacific region.

Programme

Held each March at the Queensland Cultural Centre and venues across the city — with regional touring — the festival features talks by leading scientists, panels, workshops, street science and performances, including turtle-hatching events at Mon Repos. It draws tens of thousands of visitors and aims to bring science to broad public audiences.
