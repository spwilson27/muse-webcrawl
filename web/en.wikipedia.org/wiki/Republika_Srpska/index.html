Republika Srpska (Serbian Cyrillic: Република Српска, lit. "Serb Republic") is one of the two entities of Bosnia and Herzegovina, the other being the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. It is situated in the northern and eastern parts of the country. Inhabited mainly by ethnic Serbs, it was proclaimed in January 1992 during the breakup of Yugoslavia and was internationally recognised as an entity within Bosnia and Herzegovina by the Dayton Agreement that ended the Bosnian War in 1995.

History

Republika Srpska was created by Bosnian Serb leaders under Radovan Karadžić as war broke out in 1992. During the Bosnian War its army (VRS) carried out campaigns of ethnic cleansing against Bosniak and Croat populations, including the Srebrenica genocide of July 1995, in which more than 8,000 Bosniak men and boys were murdered. The war ended with the Dayton Agreement, under which Republika Srpska received 49% of the territory of Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Geography and government

The entity consists of two main parts connected by the Brčko District corridor. It has its own government, parliament (the National Assembly), president, police, and judiciary, while defence, customs, and certain other functions belong to the state level. Its administrative centre is Banja Luka; East Sarajevo is also a major city.
