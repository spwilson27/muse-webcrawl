Landau "Lando" Calrissian is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise. Created by George Lucas, he was portrayed by Billy Dee Williams in the original and sequel trilogies, and by Donald Glover as a younger Lando in Solo: A Star Wars Story (2018).

Lando is introduced in The Empire Strikes Back (1980) as an old friend and former rival of Han Solo — the original owner of the Millennium Falcon, which he lost to Solo in a game of sabacc — and the Baron Administrator of Cloud City on Bespin. Forced by Darth Vader into betraying his friends, he redeems himself by helping them escape, then joins the Rebel Alliance, becoming a general and leading the starfighter attack on the second Death Star in Return of the Jedi (1983), piloting the Falcon.

Appearances

Beyond the films, Lando appears in the animated series Star Wars Rebels, the sequel trilogy finale The Rise of Skywalker (2019), and numerous novels and comics, including the earlier Lando Calrissian Adventures trilogy (1983) by L. Neil Smith. In Solo, Glover's Lando is a flamboyant gambler and smuggler partnered with the droid L3-37.

Characterisation

Dashing, smooth-talking and fond of capes and high-stakes games, Lando embodies the charming rogue archetype; Williams's performance and the character's moral arc — from self-interested administrator to hero of the Rebellion — have made him one of the saga's enduring figures.
