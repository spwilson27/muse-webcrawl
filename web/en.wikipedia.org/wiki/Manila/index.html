Manila

Manila, officially the City of Manila, is the capital and second-most populous city of the Philippines, with about 1.85 million residents in the city proper — one of the most densely populated cities in the world — and around 14 million in the Metro Manila region it anchors.

Founded by Spain in 1571 on the site of earlier settlements, Manila centres on Intramuros, the walled Spanish colonial city, and fronts Manila Bay, famed for its sunsets. The city was devastated in the 1945 Battle of Manila, among the deadliest urban battles of the Second World War. Today it is the Philippines' political, economic and cultural hub, known for jeepneys, Rizal Park, San Agustin Church (a UNESCO site) and the huge malls of the surrounding metropolis, including Quezon City, the country's most populous city.
