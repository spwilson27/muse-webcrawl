Denver Art Museum

Established1893
LocationDenver, Colorado, United States
TypeArt museum

The Denver Art Museum is an art museum in downtown Denver, Colorado, known for one of the largest collections of Native American art in the United States. Its campus includes the titanium-clad Frederic C. Hamilton Building, designed by Daniel Libeskind (opened 2006), and the North Building designed by Gio Ponti.

Collections

Holdings span Indigenous arts of North America, Western American art, Latin American art, modern and contemporary art, textiles, photography and Asian art, totalling more than 70,000 works.
