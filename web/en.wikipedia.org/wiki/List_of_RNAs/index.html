List of RNAs

Ribonucleic acid (RNA) occurs in many different forms in living cells. Beyond the classical protein-coding messenger RNA, cells contain a wide variety of non-coding RNAs that catalyse reactions, regulate gene expression, and guide molecular machines. This list summarises the principal types.

Coding RNA

TypeFunction
Messenger RNA (mRNA)Carries the coding sequence for protein synthesis from DNA to the ribosome

Non-coding RNAs involved in protein synthesis

TypeFunction
Transfer RNA (tRNA)Adaptor that delivers amino acids to the ribosome according to the codon sequence
Ribosomal RNA (rRNA)Structural and catalytic core of the ribosome
Transfer-messenger RNA (tmRNA)Rescues stalled ribosomes in bacteria (trans-translation)

Regulatory RNAs

TypeFunction
MicroRNA (miRNA)~22 nt RNAs that repress translation and destabilise target mRNAs
Small interfering RNA (siRNA)Guides Argonaute proteins to cleave complementary RNA in RNA interference
Piwi-interacting RNA (piRNA)Silences transposons in animal germlines
Antisense RNA (asRNA)Regulates expression by pairing with a complementary sense transcript
Long non-coding RNA (lncRNA)Transcripts >200 nt with roles in chromatin, scaffolding and regulation, e.g. Xist, HOTAIR
Enhancer RNA (eRNA)Transcribed from enhancers; implicated in enhancer–promoter activation
Circular RNA (circRNA)Covalently closed loops formed by back-splicing; often act as miRNA sponges
Small nucleolar RNA (snoRNA)Guides chemical modification (methylation, pseudouridylation) of rRNAs and other RNAs
Small nuclear RNA (snRNA)Components of the spliceosome (U1, U2, U4, U5, U6) that remove introns
Small Cajal body RNA (scaRNA)Guides modification of spliceosomal snRNAs

Catalytic and processing RNAs

TypeFunction
RibozymesCatalytic RNAs, including self-splicing introns and the peptidyl transferase of the ribosome
RNase P RNACatalytic subunit that matures the 5′ end of tRNAs
RNase MRP RNAInvolved in pre-rRNA processing and mitochondrial DNA replication priming
Signal recognition particle RNA (SRP RNA / 7SL)Directs nascent secretory proteins to the endoplasmic reticulum
Telomerase RNA component (TERC)Template for telomere repeat synthesis
Guide RNA (gRNA)Directs RNA editing in kinetoplastids; also the CRISPR guide RNAs (crRNA and tracrRNA) that direct Cas nucleases
Y RNAComponent of Ro ribonucleoproteins; involved in DNA replication initiation
Vault RNAComponent of vault cytoplasmic ribonucleoprotein particles
RiboswitchesCis-acting mRNA elements that change conformation on binding small metabolites

Parasitic and viral RNAs

TypeFunction
ViroidSmall circular infectious RNA of plants, lacking a protein coat
Retrotransposon RNAIntermediate in copy-and-paste transposition via reverse transcription
Viral genomic RNAGenome of RNA viruses (positive-sense, negative-sense, and double-stranded)
