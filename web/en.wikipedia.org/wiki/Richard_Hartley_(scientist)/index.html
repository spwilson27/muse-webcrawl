Richard Hartley (scientist)

Richard Hartley is a British computer scientist and professor at the Australian National University (ANU) in Canberra, known for his work in computer vision and multiple-view geometry.

Career

Hartley studied mathematics at the University of Toronto and Stanford University. He worked for many years at the General Electric Corporate R&D Center in Schenectady, New York, on computer vision and image understanding, before moving to Australia to join ANU and the Australian Centre for Robotic Vision, where he served as a chief investigator and research leader.

Work

With Andrew Zisserman, he co-authored Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision (Cambridge University Press, 2000; second edition 2004), a standard reference on the geometry of images from multiple cameras covering epipolar geometry, camera calibration, and 3D reconstruction. His research also covers robust statistical estimation (including work related to RANSAC), camera models, and structure from motion. He is a Fellow of the IEEE and of the Australian Academy of Science.
