Yvan Muller, four-time WTCC champion.

Yvan Muller (born 16 August 1969) is a French auto racing driver and one of the most successful touring car drivers in history. He won the World Touring Car Championship (WTCC) four times — in 2008, 2010, 2011 and 2013 — driving for SEAT and Chevrolet, and claimed ten consecutive titles in the Andros Trophy ice racing series in France. He is also a former British Touring Car Champion, winning the BTCC in 2003 with Vauxhall.

Career

Muller began his career in karting and single-seaters, winning the French Formula Renault championship and competing in Formula 3000 before switching to touring cars in the 1990s. After success in the French Supertouring Championship, he spent six seasons in the British Touring Car Championship, first with Audi and then Vauxhall, culminating in the 2003 title. When the World Touring Car Championship was revived in 2005, Muller became its dominant figure: he won the championship with SEAT in 2008, and after moving to the Chevrolet factory team he added titles in 2010, 2011 and 2013.

Alongside circuit racing, Muller excelled in ice racing, winning the Andros Trophy ten times, and made appearances in rallying and the Dakar Rally. He scored a podium finish in the World Rally Championship's Rallye de France in 2012. After leaving full-time WTCC competition, he founded his own team, Yvan Muller Racing, later Cyan Racing's partner in running the Lynk & Co programme in the World Touring Car Cup, and mentored his nephew Yann Ehrlacher, who himself became a world touring car champion.
