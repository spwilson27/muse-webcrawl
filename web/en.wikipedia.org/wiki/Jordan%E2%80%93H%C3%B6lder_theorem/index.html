Jordan–Hölder theorem

The Jordan–Hölder theorem is a fundamental result in group theory concerning composition series. It states that any two composition series of a given group have the same length and the same composition factors, up to permutation and isomorphism.

Background

A composition series is a maximal chain of subgroups in which each term is a normal subgroup of the next with simple quotient groups, called the composition factors. The theorem is named after Camille Jordan and Otto Hölder; Jordan proved the equality of the lengths of the series, and Hölder proved the stronger uniqueness of the factors.

Generalizations

Analogous results hold for modules over a ring and in more general algebraic settings such as exact categories, where the Schreier refinement theorem provides the corresponding refinement statement.
