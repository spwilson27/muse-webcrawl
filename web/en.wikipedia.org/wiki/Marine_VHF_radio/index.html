Marine VHF radio

Marine VHF radio is a worldwide system of two-way radio transceivers on ship and watercraft used for bidirectional voice communication between marine vessels and shore stations. It operates on very high frequency (VHF) maritime channels, generally between 156 and 174 MHz.

Channels

Channel 16 (156.800 MHz) is the international distress, safety and calling frequency. Channel 70 is used for Digital Selective Calling (DSC). Other channels are allocated for port operations, ship movement, bridge-to-bridge safety and commercial working.

Regulation

Use of marine VHF is regulated internationally by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) and nationally by coast guard and communications authorities. Operators generally require a certificate of competency.
