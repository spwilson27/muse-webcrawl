Equality and Human Rights Commission

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is a non-departmental public body in Great Britain that was established by the Equality Act 2006. The commission has responsibility for the promotion and enforcement of equality and non-discrimination laws in England, Scotland and Wales, and for encouraging compliance with the Human Rights Act 1998.

Formation1 October 2007

History

The EHRC opened on 1 October 2007, merging the work of three predecessor bodies: the Commission for Racial Equality, the Equal Opportunities Commission and the Disability Rights Commission. It also took on responsibility for other aspects of equality including age, sexual orientation, and religion or belief, together with a human rights remit. Its first chair was Trevor Phillips.

Powers and work

The Commission can conduct inquiries and investigations, issue unlawful act notices, support or bring strategic legal cases, intervene in court proceedings, publish guidance and codes of practice, and monitor how Britain complies with equality law and international human rights treaties. It is accredited as an "A status" national human rights institution by the United Nations. Unlike the rest of the United Kingdom, Northern Ireland has its own Equality Commission.
