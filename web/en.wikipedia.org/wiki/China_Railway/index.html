China Railway

A Fuxing high-speed train operated by China Railway

China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., operating as China Railway (CR), is the national railway operator of the People's Republic of China. It is a state-owned enterprise under the administration of the central government, responsible for passenger and freight rail services across the country. The company was formed on March 14, 2013, from the former Ministry of Railways, and converted to a wholly state-owned corporation in 2019, headquartered in Beijing.

Network

China Railway operates the largest railway network in Asia and the second-largest in the world, with over 155,000 kilometres of track in operation. Its high-speed rail network, marketed as China Railway High-speed (CRH), is the world's largest, exceeding 42,000 kilometres and accounting for more than two-thirds of the world's total high-speed track. Trains of the Fuxing series operate at up to 350 km/h, the fastest conventional commercial service in the world.

Operations

The group manages its operations through 18 regional railway bureaus and subsidiary companies. It carries billions of passenger journeys annually, with extraordinary peaks during the Spring Festival travel season (Chunyun), the largest annual human migration in the world. Freight operations include the China–Europe freight train services linking Chinese cities with destinations across Eurasia.
