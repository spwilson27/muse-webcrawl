Land development

Land development is the process of altering a landscape in various ways, typically to make land suitable for a new use. Such activities include subdividing land into lots, constructing access roads and utilities, landscaping, building structures, and converting land from one use (for example agriculture) to another (residential, commercial, or industrial).

Process

Development usually proceeds through feasibility study and site analysis, land acquisition, planning and entitlement approvals under zoning and planning law, civil engineering design (grading, drainage, roads, water and sewer), construction, and finally sale or lease of finished lots or buildings. Developers coordinate architects, engineers, surveyors, contractors, and financiers.

Regulation and impacts

Land development is regulated through zoning ordinances, subdivision rules, building codes, and environmental review. Poorly managed development can cause habitat loss, increased stormwater runoff, traffic congestion, and urban sprawl, whereas well-planned development can provide housing, employment, and public amenities.
