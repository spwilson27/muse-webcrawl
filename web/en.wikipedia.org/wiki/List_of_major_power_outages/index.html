List of major power outages

This is a list of notable power outages — widespread interruptions of electricity supply affecting large numbers of customers. Major blackouts are usually measured by the number of people affected, the area covered, and the duration of the interruption.

Causes

Major outages typically result from cascading failures: an initial fault — a tripped transmission line, a failed generator, severe weather, or a software error — overloads neighbouring equipment, which then fails in turn. The 2003 Northeast blackout, for example, began with sagging lines contacting trees in Ohio combined with a race-condition bug in a control-room alarm system.
