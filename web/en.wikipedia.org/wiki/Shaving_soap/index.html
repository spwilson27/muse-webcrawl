Shaving soap

Shaving soap is a hard soap that is used to produce lather with a shaving brush to facilitate shaving. Shaving soaps are typically formulated with a high stearic acid content and potassium hydroxide, producing a dense, stable, lubricating lather that softens beard hair and protects the skin during shaving. They are sold as pucks, sticks, or in tubs, and are distinct from softer shaving creams and from pressurised aerosol foams and gels.

A traditional shaving soap puck in a bowl, worked with a brush to produce lather.

History

Soaps designed for shaving appeared in the early 19th century; by the mid-1800s, purpose-made shaving soaps were common in barbershops in England and the United States. Well-known historical and modern makers include Williams (founded 1840 in the United States), Mitchell's Wool Fat, Tabac, and D. R. Harris. Shaving sticks, in which the soap is applied directly to the wet face, became popular in the early 20th century. The convenience of brushless creams and, after 1949, aerosol foams reduced the market share of hard soaps, but traditional tallow- and glycerin-based soaps retained a following among wet-shaving enthusiasts.

Composition

Shaving soaps differ from ordinary bath soaps in their fatty acid profile. Stearic acid, often supplied by tallow or palm stearin, gives the lather body and stability; coconut oil contributes cleansing and bubbly lather; and castor oil improves lather density. Potassium hydroxide produces a softer, more easily loaded soap than the sodium hydroxide used in bath bars, and many shaving soaps use a dual-lye blend. Glycerin and lanolin are frequently added for skin feel. Traditional formulas were tallow-based; many modern artisans produce vegan soaps based on vegetable butters such as kokum and shea.

Use

A wet brush is swirled over the puck to load soap, then worked in a bowl, in the palm, or directly on the face to build lather. The lather is applied in circular motions, re-lathered between passes of the razor, and the soap is allowed to dry between uses to prolong its life.
