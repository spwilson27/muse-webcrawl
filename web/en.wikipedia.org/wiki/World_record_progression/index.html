World record progression

A world record progression is the chronological sequence of world record performances in a sport or other measured activity, showing how the record improved over time. Wikipedia maintains progression articles for many events in athletics, swimming, speed skating, weightlifting, cycling and other sports, usually as tables or graphs listing the athlete, mark, date and venue of each successive record.

Uses

Progressions illustrate long-term trends such as physiological limits, technological change (for example clap skates or fibreglass poles) and the effects of professionalisation. Famous progressions include the men's 100 metres, the mile run, and the pole vault.
