Route du Rhum

The Route du Rhum is a transatlantic single-handed yacht race held every four years in November, sailing from Saint-Malo, Brittany, France, to Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe, in the Caribbean — a course of about 3,542 nautical miles.

History

The race was created in 1978, famously won by Canadian Mike Birch in the small trimaran Olympus Photo, beating Michel Malinovsky's much larger monohull by just 98 seconds. It has since become one of France's most popular sporting events, with well over a hundred entrants across classes including Ultim maxi-trimarans, IMOCA 60s, Multi50s, Class40s and amateur Rhum classes.

Records

In 2022 Charles Caudrelier set the outright record of 6 days, 19 hours, 47 minutes aboard the Ultim Maxi Edmond de Rothschild. Earlier winners include Florence Arthaud (1990), Laurent Bourgnon, Michel Desjoyeaux, Ellen MacArthur (in the monohull class, 1998), Francis Joyon and Loïck Peyron.
