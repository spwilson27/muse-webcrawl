GoldenEye 007 (1997 video game)

DeveloperRare
PublisherNintendo
PlatformNintendo 64
ReleaseAugust 1997

GoldenEye 007 is a first-person shooter video game developed by Rare and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo 64 in 1997. Based on the 1995 James Bond film GoldenEye, it follows secret agent James Bond as he fights to prevent a criminal syndicate from using a satellite weapon against London.

Gameplay

The single-player campaign spans missions drawn from and expanding on the film, emphasising stealth, objective-based tasks, and varied weapons rather than pure run-and-gun shooting. The game is especially remembered for its split-screen multiplayer mode for up to four players, with customisable scenarios, characters, and weapon sets — a feature added late in development that became a landmark of console multiplayer gaming.

Development and reception

Directed by Martin Hollis, the game was developed by a small, largely inexperienced team over about two and a half years. It sold more than eight million copies, received widespread critical acclaim, and is frequently cited as one of the greatest video games of all time and as proof that first-person shooters could succeed on home consoles. Its influence is visible in later console shooters such as Halo and Perfect Dark, Rare's own spiritual successor.

Legacy

A remake, GoldenEye 007 (2010), was released for the Wii. The original was re-released on Nintendo Switch Online and Xbox in 2023, with online multiplayer added to the Switch version.
