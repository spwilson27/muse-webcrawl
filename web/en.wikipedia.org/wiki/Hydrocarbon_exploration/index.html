Hydrocarbon exploration (or oil and gas exploration) is the search by petroleum geologists and geophysicists for deposits of hydrocarbons, particularly petroleum and natural gas, beneath the Earth's surface. It is the first stage of the upstream sector of the oil and gas industry, preceding appraisal, development and production.

The petroleum system

Exploration is guided by the concept of the petroleum system: a source rock rich in organic matter generates hydrocarbons when buried and heated; the oil and gas migrate upward through permeable carrier beds until they are trapped in a reservoir rock sealed by an impermeable caprock. A viable prospect requires all elements — source, migration pathway, reservoir, seal and trap — to be present and correctly timed.

Methods

Early exploration relied on surface indications such as oil seeps and anticlinal outcrops. Modern exploration is dominated by geophysics: seismic reflection surveys (2D, 3D and increasingly 4D time-lapse) image the subsurface by recording sound waves reflected from rock boundaries. Gravity and magnetic surveys, geochemical sampling and remote sensing provide supporting data. Promising structures are tested by drilling an exploration well, often called a wildcat well; measurements made while drilling, together with wireline well logs and core samples, determine whether hydrocarbons are present in commercial quantities.

Risk and economics

Exploration is high-risk: historically only a minority of wildcat wells have found commercial accumulations, though modern 3D seismic has raised success rates substantially in mature basins. Companies manage risk through prospect portfolios, farm-in agreements and detailed volumetric estimates of resources in place, expressed with uncertainty ranges such as P90, P50 and P10 estimates.

Licensing

Most countries own subsurface mineral rights and award exploration licences through competitive bidding rounds or open-door policies. Licence terms typically include work commitments, relinquishment schedules and royalty or tax obligations.
