Golden Team

The Golden Team (Hungarian: Aranycsapat), also called the Mighty Magyars, was the Hungary national football team of the 1950s, widely regarded as one of the greatest national teams in football history.

The team

Coached by Gusztáv Sebes and built around players such as Ferenc Puskás, Sándor Kocsis, Zoltán Czibor, József Bozsik, and Nándor Hidegkuti, Hungary pioneered an early form of fluid, "total" football. The team went unbeaten in 32 matches, won gold at the 1952 Helsinki Olympics, and famously defeated England 6–3 at Wembley in 1953 and 7–1 in Budapest the following year.

1954 World Cup and aftermath

Hungary entered the 1954 World Cup as favourites but lost the final 3–2 to West Germany in the "Miracle of Bern" after leading 2–0. The 1956 Hungarian Revolution ended the era, as leading players emigrated during the Honvéd world tour and never returned.
