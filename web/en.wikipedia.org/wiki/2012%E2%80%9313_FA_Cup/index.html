The 2012–13 FA Cup (also known as the FA Cup with Budweiser for sponsorship reasons) was the 132nd season of the FA Cup, the main domestic cup competition in English football and the oldest football knock-out competition in the world. A total of 758 clubs took part in the competition.

Final

The final was played at Wembley Stadium on 11 May 2013 between Manchester City and Wigan Athletic. Wigan won the match 1–0 thanks to a stoppage-time header from substitute Ben Watson, securing the first major trophy in the club's history in one of the great FA Cup final upsets. Remarkably, Wigan were relegated from the Premier League just three days later.

Semi-finals

In the semi-finals, Wigan defeated Millwall 2–0, while Manchester City beat holders Chelsea 2–1.

Notable results

The competition featured several upsets, including non-league Luton Town's victory over Premier League Norwich City in the fourth round — the first time a non-league side had beaten a top-flight team in the FA Cup since 1989.
