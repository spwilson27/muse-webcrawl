Mingrelian language

Mingrelian
Native name: მარგალური, margaluri
Region: Samegrelo, western Georgia
Speakers: roughly 300,000–500,000
Family: Kartvelian (Zan)

Mingrelian is a Kartvelian language spoken in the Samegrelo region of western Georgia by the Mingrelian people. With Laz it forms the Zan branch of the family; the two are sometimes treated as one language, Zan.

Status and writing

Mingrelian has no official status and is primarily a spoken language; written Georgian, the national literary language, is used in education and media. When written, Mingrelian uses the Georgian Mkhedruli script. UNESCO classifies the language as definitely endangered, as younger speakers increasingly shift to Georgian.
