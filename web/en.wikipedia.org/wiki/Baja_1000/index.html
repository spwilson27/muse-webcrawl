Baja 1000

The Baja 1000 is an annual Mexican off-road motorsport race held on the Baja California Peninsula. First run in 1967, it is one of the most prestigious off-road races in the world, attracting competitors on motorcycles, ATVs, production cars, buggies and unlimited Trophy Trucks. It is sanctioned by SCORE International.

Course

The race usually starts in Ensenada; some editions run point-to-point down the peninsula to La Paz, others use a loop course returning to Ensenada. Terrain includes high-speed desert roads, silt beds, rocky passes and beach sections, raced continuously, often through the night, with teams pre-running the course for weeks.

History

The event grew out of a 1962 timed motorcycle run by Dave Ekins and others from Tijuana to La Paz. The first organised race ran in 1967 under NORRA; SCORE took over in the 1970s. Famous participants include Parnelli Jones, Ivan "Ironman" Stewart — who single-handedly drove the full distance and won overall — and Larry Ragland.
