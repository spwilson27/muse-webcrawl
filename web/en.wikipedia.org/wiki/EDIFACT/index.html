EDIFACT

EDIFACT
Full nameUnited Nations/Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport
DeveloperUnited Nations Centre for Trade Facilitation and Electronic Business (UN/CEFACT)
StandardISO 9735
PurposeElectronic data interchange

EDIFACT is the international EDI standard developed under the United Nations. The acronym stands for Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport (in full, United Nations/Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport, UN/EDIFACT). The EDIFACT standard provides a set of syntax rules to structure data, an interactive exchange protocol (I-EDI), and a set of standard messages which allow multi-country and multi-industry exchange.

Work on EDIFACT began in the early 1980s, drawing on the North American ANSI ASC X12 standard and the European Tradacoms standard, and the first syntax rules were published as ISO 9735 in 1988. The standard is maintained by UN/CEFACT, a body of the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe.

Structure

An EDIFACT transmission, called an interchange, is made up of messages, which consist of segments, which in turn consist of data elements and composites. Segments are terminated by an apostrophe ('), elements are separated by a plus sign (+), and components within a composite by a colon (:). A typical interchange envelope begins with a UNB segment and ends with a UNZ segment, while each message begins with UNH and ends with UNT.

UNB+UNOC:3+SENDER+RECEIVER+200101:1200+1'
UNH+1+ORDERS:D:96A:UN'
BGM+220+PO12345+9'
...

Messages

The EDIFACT directory defines hundreds of standard messages for trade, transport, finance, customs and other domains. Widely used examples include:

  • ORDERS — purchase order
  • INVOIC — invoice
  • DESADV — despatch advice
  • ORDRSP — purchase order response
  • IFTMIN — instruction message used in transport
  • CUSDEC — customs declaration
  • PAYORD — payment order

Message directories are released twice a year and are identified by version labels such as D.96A.

Usage

EDIFACT is widely used in retail supply chains, shipping and logistics, automotive manufacturing, customs administration and healthcare, particularly in Europe. In North America the ANSI X12 standard plays a comparable role.
