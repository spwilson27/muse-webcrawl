Adolf Erik Nordenskiöld
Born: 18 November 1832, Helsinki
Died: 12 August 1901, Dalbyö, Sweden
Known for: Northeast Passage (Vega expedition)

Nils Adolf Erik Nordenskiöld (18 November 1832 – 12 August 1901) was a Finland-Swedish aristocrat, geologist, mineralogist and Arctic explorer.

Career

Born in Helsinki in the Grand Duchy of Finland, Nordenskiöld settled in Sweden after political conflict with the Russian authorities. He led expeditions to Spitsbergen and Greenland and made collections in mineralogy and cartographic history.

Vega expedition

Aboard the steamship Vega in 1878–1880 he completed the first transit of the Northeast Passage from the Atlantic to the Pacific along Siberia's Arctic coast, wintering ice-bound near the Chukchi Peninsula before reaching Yokohama — a landmark of polar exploration.
