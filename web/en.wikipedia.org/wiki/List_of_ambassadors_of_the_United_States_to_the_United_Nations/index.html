List of ambassadors of the United States to the United Nations

The Ambassador of the United States to the United Nations is the leader of the U.S. delegation and the U.S. Mission to the United Nations, holding the title Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary and serving as the U.S. representative on the UN Security Council. The position has at times been a Cabinet-level post and has often been held by prominent political figures.
