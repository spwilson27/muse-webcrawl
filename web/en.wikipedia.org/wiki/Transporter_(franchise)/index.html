Transporter (franchise)

The Transporter franchise is a French action film and television franchise created by Luc Besson and Robert Mark Kamen. It centres on Frank Martin, a professional freelance courier driver — a "transporter" — who delivers packages without asking questions, following a strict personal code, and inevitably becomes entangled in criminal plots.

Films

The original trilogy — The Transporter (2002), Transporter 2 (2005) and Transporter 3 (2008) — starred Jason Statham as Frank Martin and was noted for its elaborate car chases and fight choreography, including work by Corey Yuen. The series was rebooted with The Transporter Refueled (2015), starring Ed Skrein in the lead role. The films were produced by Besson's EuropaCorp and distributed internationally, performing strongly on modest budgets.

Television

A television series, Transporter: The Series, ran for two seasons (2012–2014) with Chris Vance as Frank Martin, following the character's jobs across Europe. An animated series was also developed.

The character

Frank Martin is a former special forces soldier living on the French Riviera who works as a driver for hire. His rules — never change the deal, no names, never open the package — structure the plots, which typically begin when he breaks one of them.
