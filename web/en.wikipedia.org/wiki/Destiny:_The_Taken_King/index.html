Destiny: The Taken King is a major expansion for Bungie's first-person shooter Destiny, released in September 2015. The expansion centres on Oryx, the Taken King, who arrives in the Solar System with his Taken army to avenge his son Crota.

Content

The Taken King added a new location, the Dreadnaught orbiting Saturn, a new raid (King's Fall), new subclasses for each Guardian class, and a substantial overhaul of questing and levelling systems. It was widely praised as a major improvement on the base game.
