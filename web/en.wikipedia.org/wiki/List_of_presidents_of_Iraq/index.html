List of presidents of Iraq

The President of Iraq is the head of state of the Republic of Iraq. The office was created after the 14 July Revolution of 1958 overthrew the Hashemite monarchy. Under the Ba'athist regime the presidency held effective executive power; since the 2005 constitution it has been a largely ceremonial post, with executive authority held by the prime minister. By political convention since 2005 the president has been a Kurd.
