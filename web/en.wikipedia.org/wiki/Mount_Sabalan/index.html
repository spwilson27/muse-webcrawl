Mount Sabalan (Persian: Sabalān; Azerbaijani: Savalan) is an inactive stratovolcano in Ardabil Province of northwestern Iran. At 4,811 metres (15,784 ft), it is the third-highest mountain in Iran, after Damavand and Alam-Kuh.

Geography

The mountain has a permanent crater lake formed at its summit, one of the highest lakes in the world. Sabalan is a popular mountaineering destination, with climbing routes from the hot springs resort of Sareyn on its northeastern flank. The slopes feature alpine meadows used by nomadic Shahsavan herders, and there are several hot springs around the base, associated with the volcano's residual geothermal activity.

Cultural significance

Sabalan is a holy mountain in Zoroastrian tradition, and some local traditions hold it to be the place where Zoroaster meditated. It features in local folklore and poetry, and the Shahsavan nomads regard it as sacred, traditionally circumambulating its glaciers and springs.
