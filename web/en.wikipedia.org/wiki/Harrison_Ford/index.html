Harrison Ford

Harrison Ford (born July 13, 1942) is an American actor. One of the highest-grossing actors of all time, he is best known for playing Han Solo in the Star Wars saga and the title character in the Indiana Jones films. His seven-decade career also includes acclaimed roles in Blade Runner, Witness, The Fugitive, and Air Force One.

Early life

Ford was born in Chicago, Illinois, to a former actress mother and an advertising executive father. He attended Ripon College in Wisconsin, where he took a drama class in his final year, and moved to Los Angeles in 1964 to pursue acting. Struggling for roles, he taught himself carpentry and worked as a professional carpenter to support his family.

Career

After minor roles and a part in American Graffiti (1973), Ford was hired by George Lucas — for whom he had built cabinets — to read lines with actors auditioning for Star Wars (1977), and won the role of Han Solo. He reprised the role in the sequels and again in The Force Awakens (2015). As Indiana Jones, beginning with Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), he headlined five films through The Dial of Destiny (2023).

Ford was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Actor for Witness (1985). Other notable films include Apocalypse Now, Working Girl, Patriot Games, Clear and Present Danger, and Blade Runner 2049. In the 2020s he moved into television with the comedy Shrinking and the Western drama 1923, and joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Thaddeus Ross.

Personal life

Ford is a licensed pilot and conservationist who has served as vice-chair of Conservation International. He has been married to actress Calista Flockhart since 2010.
