Menz Confectionery

Menz products include FruChocs and Crown Mints
IndustryConfectionery
HeadquartersAdelaide, South Australia
ProductsFruChocs, Violet Crumble (formerly), Crown Mints

Menz Confectionery is a South Australian confectionery manufacturer based in Adelaide. The company traces its origins to the nineteenth century and the biscuit and confectionery businesses of the W. Menz and Co. family firm. It is best known for FruChocs, chocolate-coated fruit balls (in apricot and peach, strawberry, and cherry varieties) that are regarded as an icon of South Australia.

History

The Menz business began as a grocery and bakery concern in Wakefield Street, Adelaide, and grew into a major biscuit manufacturer under the Menz Yo-Yo biscuit brand. In the twentieth century the company also made the Violet Crumble chocolate bar, created in 1913, which for decades was Menz's flagship confectionery product. The Menz biscuit business was sold and passed through several owners, while the confectionery arm continued in South Australian hands.

Robern Menz and revival

In 1992 the Polkinghorns family acquired the confectionery factory, trading as Robern Menz; the company reclaimed the historic Menz name in the twenty-first century. In 2018 Menz purchased the Violet Crumble brand back from Nestlé and resumed production of the bar in Adelaide, its first manufacture in Australia in years. Menz also produces Crown Mints, Choc Honeycomb, and seasonal and fundraising confectionery, and its products carry South Australian heritage branding.
