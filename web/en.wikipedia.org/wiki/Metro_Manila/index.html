Metro Manila (Filipino: Kalakhang Maynila), officially the National Capital Region (NCR), is the seat of government and one of the three defined metropolitan areas in the Philippines. It consists of 16 cities — including Manila, Quezon City, Caloocan, Pasay, Makati and Pasig — plus the municipality of Pateros.

Geography and demography

Located on the eastern shore of Manila Bay, between Laguna de Bay and the bay, the region covers about 620 km² (240 sq mi) and is the country's most densely populated region, home to roughly 13.5 million people as of the 2020 census. It is governed administratively with oversight from the Metropolitan Manila Development Authority (MMDA).

Economy

Metro Manila is the political, economic and cultural centre of the Philippines, generating roughly a third of national GDP. Its central business districts include Makati, Bonifacio Global City in Taguig, and Ortigas Center. The region is served by Ninoy Aquino International Airport, the Port of Manila, and expanding rail lines including the LRT and MRT systems.
