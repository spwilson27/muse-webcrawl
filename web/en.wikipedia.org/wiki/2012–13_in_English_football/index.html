2012–13 in English football

The 2012–13 season was the 133rd season of competitive football in England. It began in August 2012 and ended in May 2013, spanning the Premier League, the Football League, and the domestic cup competitions.

Premier League

Manchester United won the Premier League title, their 20th English league championship, in Sir Alex Ferguson's final season before retirement. Queens Park Rangers, Reading and Wigan Athletic were relegated to the Championship.

Cup competitions

Wigan Athletic produced one of the great FA Cup upsets by beating Manchester City 1–0 in the final at Wembley. Swansea City won the League Cup, defeating Bradford City 5–0 in the final — Bradford having reached the final from the fourth tier. Chelsea won the UEFA Europa League, while Bayern Munich won the UEFA Champions League final held at Wembley.

Football League

Cardiff City won the Championship and promotion to the Premier League, joined by Hull City and play-off winners Crystal Palace. Doncaster Rovers won League One and Gillingham won League Two.

National team

England continued their qualification campaign for the 2014 FIFA World Cup under manager Roy Hodgson, remaining unbeaten in their group through the season.
