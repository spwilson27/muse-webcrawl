Inisheer

Inisheer (Irish: Inis Oírr, "east island"; also Inis Oirthir) is the smallest and most easterly of the Aran Islands in Galway Bay, Ireland, lying closest to Doolin and the Cliffs of Moher in County Clare. It is roughly 3 km by 2 km, with a population of a few hundred, and is an Irish-speaking Gaeltacht community served by ferries from Doolin and Rossaveal.

Sights

Landmarks include O'Brien's Castle (Caisleán Uí Bhriain), a 15th-century tower house inside an earlier stone fort on the island's hill; the sunken church of St Kevin (Teampall Chaomháin), the island's patron saint, whose grave is visited on his feast day; and the wreck of the cargo ship Plassey, driven ashore in 1960 and famous from the opening titles of the sitcom Father Ted. The island also has a lighthouse, a small airstrip and an arts centre (Áras Éanna).

Life on the island

The landscape of limestone pavement and dry-stone-walled fields mirrors that of its larger neighbours. Fishing, farming and tourism support the community, and Irish-language summer schools draw students each year.
