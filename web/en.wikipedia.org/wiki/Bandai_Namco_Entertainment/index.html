Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc. is a Japanese multinational video game publisher and the core content business unit (CBU) of Bandai Namco Holdings. It was formed on 31 March 2006 from the merger of the video game divisions of Bandai and Namco following their 2005 corporate integration, initially under the name Namco Bandai Games; the current name was adopted in 2015 to unify the global brand. The company develops games through its studio arm, Bandai Namco Studios, and publishes titles from internal and external developers.

Franchises

The company's catalogue combines Namco's arcade heritage — Pac-Man, Galaga, Tekken, Soulcalibur, Ace Combat and Ridge Racer — with Bandai's licensed anime and manga properties, including games based on Dragon Ball, Naruto, One Piece, Gundam and Digimon, plus the Tales role-playing series. Bandai Namco also publishes FromSoftware's Dark Souls series outside Japan and published Elden Ring (2022), one of the best-selling games of its era. Other notable properties include Little Nightmares, Ni no Kuni (with Level-5) and the Taiko no Tatsujin rhythm games.

Structure

The company operates regional publishing arms including Bandai Namco Entertainment America and Bandai Namco Entertainment Europe, and maintains development and support studios in Japan, Singapore and elsewhere. Its parent, Bandai Namco Holdings, also oversees toys, amusement facilities and visual/music production businesses.
