People Power Revolution

The People Power Revolution, also known as the EDSA Revolution, was a series of popular demonstrations in the Philippines, mostly in Metro Manila from 22 to 25 February 1986, that overthrew President Ferdinand Marcos and restored democracy.

People Power Revolution
Date22–25 February 1986
LocationEDSA, Metro Manila, Philippines
ResultMarcos ousted; Corazon Aquino becomes president

Background

After the disputed snap election of February 1986 between Marcos and Corazon Aquino, widow of assassinated Benigno Aquino Jr., and a failed military reform coup, Cardinal Jaime Sin called citizens to protect defecting soldiers camped on EDSA.

Events

Millions of unarmed civilians, nuns and priests blocked tanks and offered soldiers food and flowers. On 25 February both Marcos and Aquino held rival inaugurations; that night Marcos fled by U.S. helicopter to exile in Hawaii, ending martial-law era rule.

Legacy

The largely nonviolent revolution inspired later democratic movements worldwide and is commemorated annually on 25 February.
