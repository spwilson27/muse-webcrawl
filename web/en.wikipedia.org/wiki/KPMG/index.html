KPMG

KPMG International Limited is a multinational professional services network and one of the "Big Four" accounting organisations, alongside Deloitte, EY and PwC. Its name derives from the initials of the founding firms' members: Klynveld, Peat, Marwick and Goerdeler.

The network provides audit, tax and advisory services through member firms in more than 140 countries, employing well over 270,000 people worldwide. Its global headquarters are in Amstelveen, Netherlands, and the organisation is structured as a network of independent member firms affiliated with KPMG International.

The firm's history traces to accounting practices founded in the nineteenth century; the modern organisation was formed by the 1987 merger of Peat Marwick International and Klynveld Main Goerdeler, the first mega-merger of large accounting firms.
