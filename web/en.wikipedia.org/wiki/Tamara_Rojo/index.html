Tamara Rojo (born 17 May 1974) is a Spanish ballet dancer and artistic director. Born in Montreal to Spanish parents and raised in Spain, she trained in Madrid and won the gold medal at the Paris International Dance Competition in 1994.

Dancing career

Rojo danced with Scottish Ballet and then English National Ballet, where she became a principal and originated roles created for her. In 2000 she joined The Royal Ballet in London as a principal dancer, performing the great classical roles — Odette/Odile, Giselle, Juliet, Kitri, and the Sugar Plum Fairy — and works by choreographers such as Kenneth MacMillan, Frederick Ashton, and Wayne McGregor. She was appointed CBE in 2016 for services to ballet.

Artistic direction

In 2012 Rojo became artistic director of English National Ballet, a post she combined for a decade with continuing to perform. At ENB she commissioned bold new work, notably Akram Khan's reimagined Giselle (2016), championed female choreographers, and oversaw the company's move to a new home at London City Island. In 2022 she left London to become artistic director of San Francisco Ballet, the first woman to hold that post.
