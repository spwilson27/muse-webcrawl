Houston

Houston is the most populous city in Texas and the fourth-most populous in the United States, with about 2.3 million residents and 7.5 million in its metropolitan area. It lies on the Gulf Coastal Plain of southeast Texas, near Galveston Bay.

Founded in 1836 and named for Sam Houston, the city grew on cotton, rail and then oil after Spindletop (1901). Houston is the energy capital of the world, home to the Texas Medical Center — the world's largest medical complex — NASA's Johnson Space Center ('Houston' was the first word spoken from the Moon in 1969), the Port of Houston, and a famously zoning-free sprawl. The city is one of America's most diverse, with renowned food scenes. Hurricane Harvey (2017) dropped over 1.5 metres of rain on parts of the metro area, among the worst US flooding events.
