Global News

Global News is the news and current affairs division of the Canadian television network Global, owned by Corus Entertainment. It produces national and local newscasts for Global's owned-and-operated stations across Canada, and operates the news website globalnews.ca.

Programming

Its flagship national broadcast is Global National, anchored from Vancouver and launched in 2001; Dawna Friesen became its chief anchor in 2010. Local stations broadcast morning and evening newscasts under the Global News banner (e.g. Global News at 6). The division also produces the investigative programme 16x9 (2008–2016) and the political show The West Block.

History

Global's news operations grew from the original Global Television Network launched in 1974. CanWest Global acquired the WIC stations in 2000, unifying local news production; Shaw Media succeeded CanWest in 2010, and Corus acquired the network in 2016. Global News has also supplied radio news to Corus radio stations.
