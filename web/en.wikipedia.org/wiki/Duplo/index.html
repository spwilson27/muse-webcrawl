Duplo

Lego Duplo
Manufacturer: The Lego Group
Introduced: 1969
Target age: 1½–5 years

Duplo (trademarked as LEGO DUPLO) is a core product range of the construction toy Lego, designed for children from 1½ to 5 years old. Duplo bricks are twice the length, height, and width of traditional Lego bricks — eight times the volume — making them safer and easier for toddlers to handle, while remaining compatible with standard Lego bricks.

History

The name derives from the Latin duplex, meaning double. Duplo bricks were first launched in 1969 in limited markets and rolled out internationally through the 1970s. Duplo figures, introduced in 1976 and redesigned several times, accompany sets themed around farms, zoos, towns, trains, and licensed properties.

Compatibility

A standard Lego brick can be fitted onto Duplo studs, and Duplo bricks sit on larger Lego bases through their hollow underside tubes, allowing children to graduate from Duplo to classic Lego without discarding their collection. See Lego.
