List of countries by proven oil reserves

Proven oil reserves are quantities of petroleum that geological and engineering data indicate can be recovered in the future from known reservoirs under existing economic and operating conditions. This list ranks countries by proven reserves as published in sources such as the BP/Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy and the US Energy Information Administration. Figures differ between sources because of differing treatment of unconventional oil — extra-heavy crude in Venezuela's Orinoco Belt and Canada's oil sands together account for a large share of the world total.

Total world proven reserves are approximately 1.7 trillion barrels, enough for roughly 50 years of production at recent rates — a "reserve life" figure that has stayed near that level for decades as new discoveries and improved recovery replace production. OPEC members hold around 70 per cent of the world total. Proven-reserve figures are politically sensitive: several OPEC countries sharply raised reported reserves in the 1980s when production quotas were tied to reserves, and the numbers have been disputed ever since.
