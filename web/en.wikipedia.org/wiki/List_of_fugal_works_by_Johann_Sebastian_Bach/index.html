List of fugal works by Johann Sebastian Bach

J. S. Bach (1685–1750)
Bach's contrapuntal mastery reached its height in his fugues for keyboard, organ and voices.

Johann Sebastian Bach composed fugues throughout his career, as standalone works, as movements within suites and concertos, and as components of large-scale collections such as The Well-Tempered Clavier and The Art of Fugue. Works are catalogued by their BWV (Bach-Werke-Verzeichnis) numbers.

Vocal and choral fugues

  • Mass in B minor, BWV 232 — fugal choruses including the Kyrie and "Cum Sancto Spiritu".
  • Magnificat, BWV 243 — closing chorus "Sicut erat in principio".
  • Motets, BWV 225–231 — double-choir fugues.
  • Cantata choruses and the fugal movements of the Brandenburg Concerto No. 4 and orchestral suites.
