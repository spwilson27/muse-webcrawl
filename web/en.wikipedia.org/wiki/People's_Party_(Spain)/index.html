The People's Party (Spanish: Partido Popular, PP) is a conservative and Christian-democratic political party in Spain, one of the country's two major parties alongside the Spanish Socialist Workers' Party (PSOE). It was refounded in 1989 from the People's Alliance of Manuel Fraga.

History in government

Under José María Aznar the PP governed from 1996 to 2004, privatising state companies and taking Spain into the euro, before losing office after the 2004 Madrid train bombings. Mariano Rajoy returned the party to power from 2011 to 2018, implementing austerity during the eurozone crisis; he was removed by a no-confidence motion after the Gürtel corruption case. Alberto Núñez Feijóo became leader in 2022; the PP won the most seats in the July 2023 general election but could not form a government, remaining the main opposition party.
