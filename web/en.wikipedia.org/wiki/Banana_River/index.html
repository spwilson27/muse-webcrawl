Banana River

The Banana River between Cape Canaveral and Merritt Island

The Banana River is a 31-mile-long (50 km) lagoon on the Atlantic coast of Brevard County, Florida, in the United States. It lies between Cape Canaveral and Merritt Island, and together with the Indian River forms part of the Indian River Lagoon system, one of the most biologically diverse estuaries in North America.

Geography and wildlife

The river connects southward to the Indian River via a narrow strait near Cocoa Beach and the Port Canaveral locks. Its shores include Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, Kennedy Space Center facilities and the communities of Cocoa Beach, Cape Canaveral and Satellite Beach. The Banana River Aquatic Preserve protects much of the lagoon. The waters support seagrass beds, oysters, dolphins and a significant manatee population; "no wake" zones were established to protect manatees. Like the rest of the lagoon system, the river has suffered from algal blooms and seagrass die-offs linked to nutrient pollution and septic leakage, prompting large restoration efforts. Its name is sometimes said to derive from the banana-like shape of the lagoon or from wild bananas once growing along its banks.
