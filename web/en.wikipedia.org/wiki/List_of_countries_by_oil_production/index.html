List of countries by oil production

This is a list of countries by oil production, principally based on statistics from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), OPEC and the Energy Institute's Statistical Review of World Energy. "Oil production" here generally means total petroleum and other liquids, including crude oil, condensate, natural gas liquids and biofuels.

Overview

World oil production is around 100 million barrels per day. The United States became the world's largest producer in 2018 on the strength of the shale (tight oil) revolution, overtaking Saudi Arabia and Russia. Together the top three producers account for roughly 40% of global output. OPEC members collectively produce around 27–30 million barrels per day of crude oil, and OPEC+ production agreements are a major influence on prices.
