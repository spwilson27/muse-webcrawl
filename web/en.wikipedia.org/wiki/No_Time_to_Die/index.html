No Time to Die

No Time to Die is a 2021 spy film and the twenty-fifth in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions. Directed by Cary Joji Fukunaga, it is the fifth and final outing of Daniel Craig as James Bond. The cast includes Rami Malek as the villain Lyutsifer Safin, Léa Seydoux, Lashana Lynch, Ben Whishaw, Naomie Harris, Jeffrey Wright, Christoph Waltz and Ralph Fiennes.

Plot

Five years after the capture of Ernst Stavro Blofeld, Bond has retired to Jamaica. His old friend Felix Leiter of the CIA asks for help rescuing a kidnapped scientist, which puts Bond on the trail of Safin, who has stolen Project Heracles — a nanobot bioweapon programmed to target specific DNA. Bond discovers that Madeleine Swann has a daughter, Mathilde, and in the climax sacrifices himself to open the facility's blast doors so that missiles can destroy the weapon, after being infected with nanobots keyed to Swann and her daughter.

Production and release

The film was repeatedly delayed, first by the departure of original director Danny Boyle and then by the COVID-19 pandemic, before premiering in London on 28 September 2021. It grossed over $774 million worldwide. The title song, performed by Billie Eilish, won the Academy Award for Best Original Song. It is notable as the first Bond film in which James Bond dies.
