La traviata

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: Violetta in a white ballgown raising a glass in the Brindisi, Alfredo beside her, Parisian guests in evening dress around]

La traviata ('The Fallen Woman') is an opera in three acts by Giuseppe Verdi set to an Italian libretto by Francesco Maria Piave. It is based on La Dame aux camélias (1852), a play by Alexandre Dumas fils, adapted from his own novel. The opera was originally titled Violetta, after the main character.

The courtesan Violetta Valéry finds true love with Alfredo Germont, renounces it under pressure from his father to save the family's honour, and dies of consumption in poverty as Alfredo returns too late. Premiered at La Fenice, Venice, in 1853 — a fiasco partly blamed on casting — it triumphed a year later and is today among the most performed operas in the world.
