Diageo plc is a British multinational alcoholic beverage company, with its headquarters in London, England. It operates in more than 180 countries and produces at over 140 sites around the world. It is a major distributor of Scotch whisky and other spirits, and was the world's largest distiller before being overtaken in the 2020s.

History

Diageo was formed in 1997 from the merger of Guinness plc and Grand Metropolitan plc. The name, coined for the new company, combines the Latin dies ("day") and the Greek geo ("world"), suggesting "every day, everywhere". The company later sold off non-drinks businesses such as Pillsbury and Burger King to focus on premium alcohol.

Brands

Principal brands include Johnnie Walker, Crown Royal, J&B, Buchanan's and Windsor whiskies; Smirnoff, Cîroc and Ketel One vodkas; Captain Morgan rum; Baileys liqueur; Don Julio and Casamigos tequilas; Tanqueray and Gordon's gins; and Guinness stout. Diageo is the largest producer of Scotch whisky, with brands such as Lagavulin, Talisker and Oban.
