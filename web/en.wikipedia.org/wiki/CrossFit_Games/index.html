CrossFit Games

The CrossFit Games is an annual athletic competition owned and operated by CrossFit, LLC. Athletes compete in a series of events which may be various standard CrossFit workouts consisting of metabolic conditioning exercises, weightlifting, and gymnastics movements, as well as a range of activities from other sports such as swimming and cycling. The Games seek to determine the "Fittest on Earth".

CrossFit Games
First held: 2007
Location: Madison, Wisconsin (2017–2023); Fort Worth, Texas (2024)
Most titles: Mat Fraser and Tia-Clair Toomey

History

The first CrossFit Games were held in 2007 on a ranch in Aromas, California, with roughly 70 athletes competing for a top prize of $500. As the popularity of CrossFit grew, so did the Games, gaining sponsors such as Reebok (title sponsor from 2011 to 2020) and later NoBull. From 2017 to 2023 the Games were held at the Alliant Energy Center in Madison, Wisconsin.

Qualification

The competition season begins with the worldwide CrossFit Open, a multi-week online competition open to anyone. Top performers advance through quarterfinals and semifinals to qualify for the Games. Divisions include individual men and women, teams, masters (age-group), teenagers, and adaptive athletes.

Champions

Notable champions include Rich Froning Jr., who won four consecutive individual titles (2011–2014), Mat Fraser, who won five (2016–2020), and Tia-Clair Toomey, the most successful female competitor with multiple consecutive titles from 2017 onward. Katrín Davíðsdóttir, Annie Thorisdottir, Justin Medeiros, and Laura Horvath are among other individual champions.
