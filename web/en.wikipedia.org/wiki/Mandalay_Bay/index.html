Mandalay Bay

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Mandalay Bay is a 43-story luxury resort and casino at 3950 Las Vegas Boulevard South in Paradise, Nevada, United States. It is owned by Vici Properties and Blackstone Real Estate and operated by MGM Resorts International. The resort opened on March 2, 1999, on the former site of the Hacienda.

Facilities

The complex has 3,209 rooms, a 135,000-square-foot casino, the Mandalay Bay Convention Center, and an 11-acre beach complex with a wave pool and lazy river. The Shark Reef Aquarium houses more than 2,000 animals. The upper floors contain the Four Seasons Hotel Las Vegas, and the connected Delano Las Vegas (formerly THEhotel) adjoins the property. The adjacent Allegiant Stadium opened in 2020.

2017 shooting

On 1 October 2017, a gunman firing from a suite on the 32nd floor of Mandalay Bay killed 60 people attending the Route 91 Harvest music festival across the street, in the deadliest mass shooting by a lone gunman in modern American history. Security and screening practices at hotels changed nationwide in its aftermath.
