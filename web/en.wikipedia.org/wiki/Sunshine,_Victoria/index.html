Sunshine is a suburb in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia, located about 12 kilometres west of the Melbourne central business district. It is the administrative centre of the City of Brimbank local government area.

History

The area was originally known as Braybrook Junction. In 1907 the industrialist H. V. McKay established his Sunshine Harvester Works there, and the locality was renamed Sunshine in honour of his factory's name. Sunshine grew into a major manufacturing centre during the twentieth century, and the suburb became known for its working-class character and strong multicultural community, with large Vietnamese, Maltese, and other migrant populations.

Transport

Sunshine railway station is a major junction on the Melbourne suburban network, served by the Sunbury line and by regional trains to Ballarat and Bendigo. It is also a hub for bus services across Melbourne's western suburbs.
