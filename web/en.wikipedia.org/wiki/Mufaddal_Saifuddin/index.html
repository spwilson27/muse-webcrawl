Mufaddal Saifuddin (born 23 August 1946) is the 53rd and current Da'i al-Mutlaq (absolute missionary) of the Dawoodi Bohras, a denomination within Ismaili Islam. He succeeded his father, Mohammed Burhanuddin, the 52nd Da'i, upon his death in January 2014.

Early life and education

Saifuddin was born in Surat, Gujarat, on 20 Ramadan 1365 AH (23 August 1946 CE), the second son of Mohammed Burhanuddin. He was educated in religious and secular studies, memorised the Quran at a young age, and was granted the title Saifuddin ("sword of the faith") along with scholarly honours by his grandfather, the 51st Da'i Taher Saifuddin. He studied at Aljamea-tus-Saifiyah, the community's Arabic academy in Surat.

Succession

In 2011, while recovering in a London hospital, Mohammed Burhanuddin publicly proclaimed (nass) Mufaddal Saifuddin as his successor in the presence of witnesses. The proclamation was repeated publicly in Mumbai shortly afterwards. After Burhanuddin died in January 2014, Saifuddin assumed leadership. His succession was contested by his uncle Khuzaima Qutbuddin, who claimed an earlier private nass; the dispute was litigated in the Bombay High Court, which in 2024 ruled in favour of Saifuddin.

Leadership

As Da'i al-Mutlaq, Saifuddin leads a global community of around one million Dawoodi Bohras. His tenure has emphasised education, women's empowerment through community programmes, environmental initiatives such as tree planting and cleanliness drives, and the FMB (Faiz al-Mawaid al-Burhaniyah) community kitchen programme that supplies meals to households. He has overseen the Saifee Burhani Upliftment Project in Bhendi Bazaar, Mumbai, a large urban redevelopment scheme. He frequently travels to address congregations across India, the Middle East, East Africa, Europe and North America, particularly during Ashara Mubaraka, the annual Muharram commemorations.
