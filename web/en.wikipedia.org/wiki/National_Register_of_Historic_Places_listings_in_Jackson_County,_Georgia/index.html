National Register of Historic Places listings in Jackson County, Georgia

This is a list of properties and historic districts in Jackson County, Georgia that are listed on the National Register of Historic Places (NRHP), the United States federal government's official list of districts, sites, buildings, structures and objects deemed worthy of preservation.

Listings

Jackson County, in northeast Georgia, has more than a dozen NRHP listings, concentrated in the county seat of Jefferson and in Commerce and Braselton. They include historic commercial and residential districts, churches, a courthouse, and individual antebellum and Victorian houses. Notable entries include the Jackson County Courthouse in Jefferson, the Commerce Commercial Historic District, and the Braselton Historic District.

Listings are typically presented in a table giving the property name, location, date listed, and a summary of significance.
