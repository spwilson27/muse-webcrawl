CNN

CNN (Cable News Network) is an American cable news channel founded in 1980 by Ted Turner, the first television channel to provide 24-hour news coverage and the first all-news channel in the United States.

Influence

CNN rose to global prominence with live coverage of the 1991 Gulf War from Baghdad, and grew into an international network with sister channels and a large digital operation, amid later debates about ratings, ownership changes and the opinion/news mix.
