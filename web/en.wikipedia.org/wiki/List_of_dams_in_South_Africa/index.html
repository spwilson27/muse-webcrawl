List of dams in South Africa

This is a list of dams and reservoirs in South Africa. The country has several hundred large dams, most of them operated or overseen by the Department of Water and Sanitation, and they supply water for irrigation, municipal use, mining and hydroelectric power generation.

The Gariep Dam, completed in 1971, is the largest dam in South Africa by storage volume. The Vaal Dam is the principal water source for the Gauteng economic hub. The Lesotho Highlands Water Project, although located in Lesotho, also supplies water to the Vaal system.
