Liu Xiang (born 13 July 1983) is a Chinese former track and field athlete who specialized in the 110 metres hurdles. He won the gold medal at the 2004 Athens Olympics, becoming China's first male Olympic champion in track and field, and he is a former world record holder in the event with a time of 12.88 seconds set in 2006.

Career

Liu emerged internationally in 2002 and won the 110 m hurdles title at the 2004 Athens Olympics in 12.91 seconds, equalling the then world record. In July 2006 in Lausanne he broke the world record with 12.88 seconds. He went on to win the world title at the 2007 World Championships in Osaka, completing a "grand slam" of Olympic, world championship, and world record honours.

Injuries and retirement

An Achilles tendon injury forced Liu to withdraw from the 2008 Beijing Olympics in front of a home crowd, and a recurrence of the injury ended his 2012 London Olympic campaign in the heats. He announced his retirement in April 2015.

Legacy

Liu is regarded as one of China's most celebrated athletes and a pioneer for Asian sprint hurdlers. His achievements inspired a generation of Chinese track athletes.
