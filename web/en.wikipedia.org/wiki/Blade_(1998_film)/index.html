Blade (1998 film)

Directed by: Stephen Norrington
Written by: David S. Goyer
Starring: Wesley Snipes, Stephen Dorff, Kris Kristofferson
Release date: August 21, 1998
Box office: $131.2 million

Blade is a 1998 American superhero horror film directed by Stephen Norrington and written by David S. Goyer. Based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name, it stars Wesley Snipes as Blade, a human-vampire hybrid who hunts vampires.

Plot

Blade, whose mother was bitten by a vampire while pregnant, possesses vampire strength without most of their weaknesses, though he must take a serum to suppress his thirst for blood. With his mentor Abraham Whistler, he battles the vampire Deacon Frost, who seeks to summon the blood god La Magra.

Reception and legacy

The film was a commercial success and is credited with helping revive superhero films based on Marvel properties. It was followed by Blade II (2002) and Blade: Trinity (2004).
