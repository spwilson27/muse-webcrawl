The Marching 101 is the marching band of South Carolina State University in Orangeburg, South Carolina. It is one of the best-known historically Black college and university (HBCU) marching bands in the United States, famed for its high-stepping show style.

History

The band programme dates to 1918, and the "101" name is said to reflect the original number of members. Under long-serving directors the band became a cornerstone of Bulldog football Saturdays and of HBCU band culture.

Style and performances

The Marching 101 combines precision drills, dance routines and a powerful brass sound. It has performed at the Honda Battle of the Bands, NFL halftime shows and presidential-related events, and regularly competes in band battles against rivals such as Bethune-Cookman's Marching Wildcats.

Sections

The band includes the "Champagne" dance team and featured twirlers. Its drum line and tuba section are particularly noted within the HBCU band community.
