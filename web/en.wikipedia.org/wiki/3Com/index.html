3Com

3Com Corporation was an American digital electronics manufacturer best known for its computer networking products. It was founded in 1979 by Robert Metcalfe, Howard Charney, Bruce Borden, and Greg Shaw, and its name derives from the company's focus on "Computers, Communication and Compatibility." Metcalfe had co-invented Ethernet at Xerox PARC, and 3Com became a leading supplier of Ethernet network interface cards for personal computers.

History

The company shipped the first Ethernet adapter for the IBM PC in 1982 and grew into a major vendor of hubs, switches, routers, and modems. Its 1997 merger with U.S. Robotics brought the popular PalmPilot handheld line into 3Com; Palm was spun off as an independent company in 2000. Among its best-known products were the EtherLink adapters, the OfficeConnect line for small businesses, and the Audrey internet appliance.

Acquisition

After years of competition with Cisco Systems and others, Hewlett-Packard acquired 3Com in April 2010 for approximately $2.7 billion, ending the company as an independent firm.
