Evernote is a note-taking and task-management application developed by the Evernote Corporation. It is designed for note taking, organising, task management, and archiving, allowing users to create notes containing text, drawings, photographs, audio, and saved web content.

History

Evernote was founded by Stepan Pachikov and launched in 2008, with Phil Libin as CEO during its rapid growth. The service reached 100 million users by 2014 and was one of the early "unicorn" productivity startups. In 2023 the company was acquired by the Italian software developer Bending Spoons, which subsequently restructured the company and moved its operations to Europe.

Features

Notes are stored in notebooks and can be tagged, searched (including text within images via optical character recognition), and synchronised across devices. The Web Clipper browser extension saves web pages, and features such as templates, tasks, and calendar integration were added over time. Evernote operates on a freemium model, with paid tiers offering larger uploads and additional features.

Data storage and privacy

Data is stored both on the user's devices and on Evernote's servers. In 2013 the service suffered a security breach that led to a company-wide password reset; in 2016 a proposed privacy-policy change allowing employee access to notes prompted backlash and was revised.
