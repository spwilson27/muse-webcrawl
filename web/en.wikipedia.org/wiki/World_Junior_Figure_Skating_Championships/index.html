World Junior Figure Skating Championships

The World Junior Figure Skating Championships, commonly called World Juniors, is an annual figure skating competition sanctioned by the International Skating Union for junior-level skaters.

Eligibility and events

Skaters must meet ISU age criteria — typically at least 13 and under 19 (21 for male pair skaters and ice dancers) on the preceding 1 July. Medals are awarded in men's singles, women's singles, pair skating and ice dance.

History

The first championships were held in 1976 in Megève, France. Many future Olympic and senior World champions first won medals here, making the event a key showcase of emerging talent. Qualification is by country, based on the previous year's results.
