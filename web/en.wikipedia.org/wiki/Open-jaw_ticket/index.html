Open-jaw ticket

Diagram of an open-jaw itinerary: fly into one city, depart from another.

An open-jaw ticket is an airline ticket in which a passenger flies to one destination and returns from a different one, or departs from a different origin on the return, leaving a "gap" (the open jaw) that the traveller covers by other means of transport.

Types

In a destination open-jaw, the traveller flies A→B and C→A (for example, into Paris and out of Rome). In an origin open-jaw, the return departs from a different city than the outbound arrival's counterpart. Double open-jaw tickets combine both. A "turnaround" open-jaw's surface segment is shorter than either flown segment for the fare to qualify.

Use

Open-jaw tickets are popular for multi-city tours, cruises beginning and ending at different ports, and overland journeys, often costing little more than a simple return fare.
