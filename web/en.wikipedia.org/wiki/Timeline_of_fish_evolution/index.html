Timeline of fish evolution

The timeline of fish evolution traces some 500 million years from early chordates in the Cambrian to the more than 34,000 living species of fish, the largest group of vertebrates.

Palaeozoic origins

Cambrian chordates such as Haikouichthys and Myllokunmingia (c. 518 Ma) are among the earliest vertebrates. Ordovician–Silurian jawless ostracoderms were followed by jawed gnathostomes: armoured placoderms, acanthodians, early sharks and bony fish. In the Devonian 'Age of Fishes', lobe-finned sarcopterygians gave rise to tetrapods, while ray-finned actinopterygians began their long radiation.

After the end-Cretaceous extinction, teleosts—especially percomorphs such as perches, tunas and cichlids—came to dominate marine and freshwater ecosystems.
