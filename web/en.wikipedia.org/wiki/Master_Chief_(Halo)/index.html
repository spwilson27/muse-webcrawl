Master Chief (Halo)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Master Chief in his MJOLNIR powered armour

Master Chief Petty Officer John-117, more commonly known as Master Chief, is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Halo video game series, originally developed by Bungie and later by 343 Industries. A Spartan-II supersoldier of the United Nations Space Command (UNSC), he wears distinctive green MJOLNIR powered armour and is almost never seen without his helmet.

Character

John was taken from his home as a child of six and conscripted into the SPARTAN-II program, where he was trained, augmented and indoctrinated for war against human insurrectionists — a role soon overtaken by the war against the alien Covenant. Raised to leadership among the Spartans, John-117 became humanity's most celebrated soldier during the events of the games, fighting alongside the artificial intelligence Cortana.

The character is deliberately laconic, speaking rarely, which allows players to project themselves into the role. Steve Downes provides the character's voice in the games. Master Chief has become the mascot of the Xbox brand and one of the most recognisable characters in gaming.

Appearances

Master Chief first appeared in Halo: Combat Evolved (2001) and stars in Halo 2 (2004), Halo 3 (2007), Halo 4 (2012), Halo 5: Guardians (2015) and Halo Infinite (2021), as well as numerous novels and other media. A live-action version, portrayed by Pablo Schreiber, appears in the Halo television series.
