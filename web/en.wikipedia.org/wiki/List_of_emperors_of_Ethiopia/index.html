List of emperors of Ethiopia

The Emperor of Ethiopia (Ge'ez: ንጉሠ ነገሥት, nəgusä nägäst, "King of Kings") was the hereditary ruler of the Ethiopian Empire until the monarchy's abolition in 1975. The Solomonic dynasty claimed descent from Menelik I, son of King Solomon and the Queen of Sheba.

Haile Selassie was deposed in 1974 following the Ethiopian Revolution, and the monarchy was formally abolished in 1975. Between the imperial period's legendary beginnings and its end, Ethiopia was ruled by the Zagwe dynasty in the 12th–13th centuries and endured the Zemene Mesafint ("Era of the Princes") in the 18th and early 19th centuries, when emperors were largely figureheads.
