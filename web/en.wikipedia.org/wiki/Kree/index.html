The Kree are a fictional alien race appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, they first appeared in Fantastic Four #65 (August 1967). The Kree are a militaristic, blue-skinned humanoid species native to the planet Hala in the Large Magellanic Cloud, and the rulers of the vast Kree Empire.

Publication history

The Kree debuted in the late 1960s during Lee and Kirby's run on Fantastic Four. Their first emissary to Earth was the Sentry, followed by the officer Mar-Vell, who later became the hero Captain Marvel. The Kree–Skrull War, a major storyline in The Avengers (1971–72) written by Roy Thomas, established the Kree as one of the principal alien powers of the Marvel Universe.

Fictional species biography

Millions of years ago, the reptilian Cotati and the humanoid Kree competed in a contest staged by the Skrulls on Earth's Moon; when the Skrulls favoured the Cotati's creation, the Kree massacred both the Cotati and the Skrull delegation, seized Skrull technology and began their own expansion. In time the Kree came to be ruled from Hala by the Supreme Intelligence, a vast organic computer composed of the minds of the race's greatest thinkers.

The original pure-blooded Kree have blue skin, though interbreeding has produced pink-skinned Kree who resemble Caucasian humans. The Kree possess greater strength and endurance than humans, requiring higher gravity and nitrogen-rich environments. Because the race had reached an evolutionary dead end, Kree scientists experimented on other species — leading to the creation of the Inhumans on Earth.

Notable Kree

  • Ronan the Accuser — Supreme Accuser of the Kree Empire, wielder of the Universal Weapon.
  • Captain Mar-Vell — Kree officer who defected to defend Earth; the first Captain Marvel.
  • Yon-Rogg — Kree commander and nemesis of Mar-Vell.
  • The Supreme Intelligence — the collective-mind ruler of the empire.
  • Hala the Accuser and Phyla-Vell — later generations of Kree heroes and enforcers.

In other media

The Kree appear prominently in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) with Ronan the Accuser as the antagonist, and featuring in Captain Marvel (2019), in which Carol Danvers' powers derive in part from a transfusion of Kree blood. Kree characters also appear in the television series Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.
