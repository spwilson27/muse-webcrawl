Jerry Maguire

Written by: Cameron Crowe

Jerry Maguire is a 1996 American romantic comedy-drama sports film written and directed by Cameron Crowe. It stars Tom Cruise as a successful sports agent who, after a crisis of conscience, is fired and rebuilds his career with a single client, football player Rod Tidwell, played by Cuba Gooding Jr.

Plot

Sports agent Jerry Maguire writes a mission statement urging his agency to put clients before money, and is fired for it. Leaving with secretary Dorothy Boyd (Renée Zellweger) and only one client, the brash Arizona Cardinals wide receiver Rod Tidwell, Jerry struggles to keep his fledgling agency alive. Tidwell demands Jerry "show me the money," while Jerry falls in love with Dorothy and bonds with her young son Ray. A Monday Night Football performance by Tidwell secures him a lucrative contract, and Jerry's authenticity wins the day.

Reception and legacy

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing over $273 million worldwide. It received five Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture and Best Actor for Cruise; Gooding won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor. Its catchphrases — "Show me the money!" and "You had me at 'hello'" — entered popular culture, and the film is frequently ranked among the greatest romantic comedies and sports films.
