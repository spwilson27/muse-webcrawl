The United States Army Cadet Command is the senior command responsible for the Army's Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC) and Junior ROTC programmes. Established in 1986 and headquartered at Fort Knox, Kentucky, it is a major subordinate command of the U.S. Army Training and Doctrine Command (TRADOC).

Cadet Command oversees Senior ROTC programmes at colleges and universities across the United States, organised into regional brigades, and commissions thousands of second lieutenants each year — the largest single source of officers for the U.S. Army.

Programmes

In addition to Senior ROTC, the command administers Junior ROTC in high schools, intended to develop citizenship and leadership among students. Cadet Command also runs Cadet Summer Training at Fort Knox, including the Cadet Leader Course, which most cadets attend between their junior and senior years.
