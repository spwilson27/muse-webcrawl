Lil Jon

Born: Jonathan H. Smith, January 17, 1971, Atlanta, Georgia, U.S.
Genres: Hip hop, crunk
Occupations: Rapper, DJ, record producer

Jonathan H. Smith (born January 17, 1971), better known by his stage name Lil Jon, is an American rapper, DJ, and record producer. He was instrumental in the commercial breakthrough of the hip hop subgenre crunk in the early 2000s, fronting the group Lil Jon & the East Side Boyz, and is widely known for his catchphrases "Yeah!", "OK!", and "What?!".

Career

Born and raised in Atlanta, Georgia, Lil Jon worked as a DJ and as an A&R executive at So So Def Recordings before forming Lil Jon & the East Side Boyz. The group's albums included Get Crunk, Who U Wit: Da Album (1997), We Still Crunk!! (2000), Put Yo Hood Up (2001), Kings of Crunk (2002), and Crunk Juice (2004), the latter yielding the hit single "Get Low" and reaching the top of the charts. As a producer he crafted hits for other artists, including Usher's "Yeah!" (2004), which won a Grammy Award, and later worked on DJ Snake's EDM hit "Turn Down for What" (2013).

Later work

Lil Jon released the solo album Crunk Rock in 2010 and appeared on television, including The Celebrity Apprentice. In 2024 he released the guided-meditation album Total Meditation, reflecting a turn toward wellness themes, and performed during Usher's Super Bowl LVIII halftime show.
