Warren Murphy

Warren Murphy (1933–2015) was an American author best known as the co-creator, with Richard Sapir, of The Destroyer, the long-running men's adventure series featuring Remo Williams and his Korean master Chiun.

Career

Murphy was a New Jersey journalist and political operative before turning to fiction. Created, The Destroyer (1971), written with Sapir, introduced Remo Williams, a former policeman recruited by a secret government agency, CURE, and trained in the Korean martial art of Sinanju by the elderly master Chiun. The series ran to more than 150 volumes and spawned the film Remo Williams: The Adventure Begins (1985).

Other work

After Sapir's death in 1987, Murphy continued the series with other collaborators and wrote screenplays, including for the television series Remo Williams and the film The Eiger Sanction (1975), co-written with Rod Whitaker (Trevanian). He won two Edgar Awards from the Mystery Writers of America.
