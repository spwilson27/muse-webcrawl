The Narendra Modi Stadium is a cricket stadium in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. With a seating capacity of 132,000 spectators, it is the largest stadium in the world. It was built on the site of the former Sardar Patel (Motera) Stadium and named after Narendra Modi, prime minister of India and a former president of the Gujarat Cricket Association. The stadium was inaugurated in February 2020 with the "Namaste Trump" event during the state visit of US president Donald Trump, and hosted its first match, a day-night Test between India and England, in February 2021.

The ground hosted the final of the 2023 Cricket World Cup, in which Australia defeated India before a crowd reported at over 90,000, and is the home ground of the Gujarat Titans franchise of the Indian Premier League, champions in their debut 2022 season. Its facilities include an Olympic-sized swimming pool, four dressing rooms and 76 corporate boxes within the stadium complex.
