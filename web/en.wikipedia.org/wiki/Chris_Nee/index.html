Chris Nee

Chris Nee is an American children's television writer and producer. She is best known as the creator of the Disney Junior animated series Doc McStuffins.

Career

Nee began her career writing for children's programs, contributing to series such as Sesame Street, Blue's Clues, The Backyardigans, Wonder Pets!, and Little Bill. In 2012 she created Doc McStuffins, about a young girl who fixes toys; the series was widely praised for its positive portrayal of its African-American lead character and for inspiring children's interest in medicine. She later created Vampirina (2017) for Disney Junior.

In 2018, Nee signed an overall deal with Netflix to write and produce animated and live-action series aimed at children and families, under which she produced titles including We the People (2021) and an animated adaptation of Ada Twist, Scientist.
