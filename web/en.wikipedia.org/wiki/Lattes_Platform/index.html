The Lattes Platform (Portuguese: Plataforma Lattes) is an information system maintained by the Brazilian federal agency CNPq (National Council for Scientific and Technological Development) that integrates databases of curricula vitae, research groups, and institutions connected with science and technology in Brazil. Its central component, the Lattes Curriculum (Currículo Lattes), has become the standard national format for the CVs of researchers, students, and other science and technology professionals in the country.

History

The platform was launched in August 1999 and named in honour of the physicist César Lattes (1924–2005), one of Brazil's most renowned scientists, known for his role in the discovery of the pi meson. Registration of a Lattes CV became effectively mandatory for anyone applying for CNPq funding, scholarships, or admission evaluations throughout the Brazilian research system, and the database grew to hold several million curricula.

Contents and use

A Lattes CV records education, professional experience, publications, supervised theses, projects, patents, and numerous other categories in a structured, standardised form. The platform is used by funding agencies, university administrators, and evaluation committees to analyse scientific production and allocate resources; aggregate data are published through statistical panels. The system is linked internationally through the ScienTI network, with analogous platforms adopted in other Latin American countries.

Criticism

The platform has been criticised for encouraging excessive quantification of academic output, for data that is self-reported and thus vulnerable to inflation, and for the bureaucratic burden of its highly detailed forms. It has also suffered technical outages, notably a major server failure in 2021 that temporarily took the database offline and prompted discussion about data preservation and backups.
