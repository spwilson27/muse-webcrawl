Hunspell is a free and open-source spell checker and morphological analyser designed for languages with rich morphology and complex word compounding, such as Hungarian — the language for which it was originally developed. It is the spell checker used by many major software projects, including LibreOffice, OpenOffice.org, Mozilla Firefox and Thunderbird, Google Chrome, and earlier versions of macOS and other applications.

Features

Hunspell is a successor to the earlier Myspell checker and improves on Ispell-style affix handling. Its dictionaries consist of a word list with affix flags plus an affix file describing prefix and suffix rules. Key features include:

  • Support for complex compounding and agglutinative morphology.
  • Twofold suffix stripping and conditional affixes.
  • Handling of characters beyond ASCII through UTF-8 encoded dictionaries.
  • Suggestion mechanisms that account for typical typing errors and phonetic similarity.
  • Stemming, morphological analysis, and thesaurus integration.

History

Hunspell was created by Hungarian biologist and free-software contributor László Németh, initially to provide adequate Hungarian spell checking for OpenOffice.org. Because Hungarian forms words by extensive suffixation and compounding, existing checkers performed poorly; Hunspell's extended affix model addressed this and proved valuable for many other languages, including German, Turkish, Finnish, and numerous minority languages. Through its adoption by LibreOffice and the Mozilla and Chromium projects, Hunspell dictionaries became available for well over a hundred languages.

Usage

Hunspell can be used as a command-line tool and as a library linked into applications. Users and language communities maintain dictionaries for their languages, typically distributed as .dic and .aff file pairs. Command-line use allows checking files, piping text, and generating word forms from stems.
