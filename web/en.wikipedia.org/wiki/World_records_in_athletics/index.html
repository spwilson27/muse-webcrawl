World records in athletics are ratified by World Athletics (formerly the IAAF), the international governing body for track and field. A record must be set in a bona fide competition conducted under World Athletics rules, on a compliant facility, with accurate timing and measurement, and is subject to conditions including limits on wind assistance (2.0 m/s tailwind for relevant events) and post-competition doping controls.

Categories

Records are maintained for outdoor events, indoor (short track) events, road running, race walking and under-20 categories, for men and women. Standard events include sprints, middle- and long-distance races, hurdles, steeplechase, relays, jumps, throws, combined events and road races such as the marathon.

Famous records

Among the most celebrated marks are Usain Bolt's 9.58 seconds for 100 metres and 19.19 for 200 metres (2009), Florence Griffith Joyner's women's 100 m record of 10.49 (1988), and long-standing field event records such as Javier Sotomayor's 2.45 m high jump (1993) and the men's and women's hammer and javelin records of the 1980s, some of which were set with since-discontinued implement specifications.
