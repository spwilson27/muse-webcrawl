Västra Götaland County

Västra Götaland County (Swedish: Västra Götalands län) is a county on the western coast of Sweden, bordering Norway and the Skagerrak strait. It is the second most populous of Sweden's 21 counties, with about 1.7 million inhabitants, and its capital and largest city is Gothenburg. The county comprises much of the historical provinces of Västergötland, Bohuslän, and Dalsland.

History

The county was created on 1 January 1998 through the merger of the former counties of Gothenburg and Bohus, Älvsborg, and Skaraborg. The corresponding regional council, Region Västra Götaland, is Sweden's largest regional authority and is responsible for healthcare, public transport (through Västtrafik), and regional development.

Geography and economy

The county includes the long Bohuslän archipelago coast, the plains of Västergötland between Sweden's two largest lakes — Vänern and Vättern (both partly within the county) — and the forested hills of Dalsland. Gothenburg is Scandinavia's largest port and a major industrial centre, home to Volvo Cars and Volvo Group; other significant towns include Trollhättan, Borås, Skövde, and Uddevalla. Tourism along the coast and around the lakes is an important industry.
