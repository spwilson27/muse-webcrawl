Water cooler

TypeDrinking water dispenser
FunctionCools and dispenses drinking water

A water cooler (also called a water dispenser) is a machine that dispenses drinking water, typically cooled, and often also heated. Water coolers are commonly fed either by large refillable bottles mounted on top of the unit or by a direct connection to a building's water supply with filtration.

Types

Bottled water coolers use large bottles, commonly 3 or 5 US gallons, inverted into a reservoir. Point-of-use (POU) coolers connect to the mains supply and filter the water. Wall-mounted drinking fountains, a related fixture, are common in schools and public buildings.

Cultural significance

The water cooler has become a symbol of informal office socialisation: the phrase "water cooler conversation" describes casual workplace talk, and "water cooler moment" describes a television event widely discussed the next day. The water cooler effect has been studied in organisational psychology as a driver of informal communication and team cohesion.
