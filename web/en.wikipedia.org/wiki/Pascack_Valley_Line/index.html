Pascack Valley Line

The Pascack Valley Line is a commuter rail line operated by NJ Transit in northeastern New Jersey and southern New York. It runs from Hoboken Terminal through the Pascack Valley of Bergen County to Spring Valley, New York, a distance of about 31 miles (50 km). Service on the northern portion in Rockland County is provided under contract with Metro-North Railroad.

History

The line's origins lie in the Hackensack and New York Railroad, chartered in 1856 and opened to Hillsdale in 1870, later extended to Haverstraw, New York. It became part of the Erie Railroad system and passed to Conrail in 1976 before NJ Transit assumed passenger operations in 1983. Historically single-track with passing sidings, the line long offered only peak-direction express patterns on weekdays.

Service

Trains serve communities including Hackensack, River Edge, Oradell, Emerson, Westwood, Hillsdale, Woodcliff Lake, Park Ridge, Montvale, Pearl River, Nanuet, and Spring Valley. Weekend service was introduced in 2007 following the installation of additional passing sidings. Most trains operate to and from Hoboken, with a small number using the Secaucus Junction connection to New York Penn Station.
