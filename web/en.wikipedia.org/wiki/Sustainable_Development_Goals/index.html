Sustainable Development Goals

SDGs
Adopted: 25 September 2015
By: United Nations General Assembly
Goals: 17; Targets: 169; Deadline: 2030

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are 17 interlinked global goals adopted by the United Nations in 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, succeeding the Millennium Development Goals.

The goals

  1. No Poverty
  2. Zero Hunger
  3. Good Health and Well-Being
  4. Quality Education
  5. Gender Equality
  6. Clean Water and Sanitation
  7. Affordable and Clean Energy
  8. Decent Work and Economic Growth
  9. Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
  10. Reduced Inequalities
  11. Sustainable Cities and Communities
  12. Responsible Consumption and Production
  13. Climate Action
  14. Life Below Water
  15. Life on Land
  16. Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions
  17. Partnerships for the Goals

Progress and criticism

UN reports track 169 targets with global indicators. Progress has been uneven, set back by COVID-19, conflict and climate change; critics note the goals are non-binding and sometimes internally conflicting, e.g. growth versus environment.
