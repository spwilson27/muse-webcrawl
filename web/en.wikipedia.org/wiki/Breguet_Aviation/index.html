Breguet Aviation

Founded: 1911

Founder: Louis Breguet

Fate: Merged into Dassault (1971)

Breguet Aviation was a French aircraft manufacturer founded by aviation pioneer Louis Breguet. The company produced pioneering aircraft from the earliest years of flight, including the Breguet 14 bomber of World War I, the long-range Breguet 19, and record-breaking aircraft such as the Point d'Interrogation.

History

Louis Breguet built his first aircraft in 1909 and founded the company in 1911. After World War II, Breguet developed the Br.1050 Alizé anti-submarine aircraft, the two-deck Br.763 Deux-Ponts airliner, and contributed to the SEPECAT Jaguar programme. In 1971 the company merged with Dassault, forming Avions Marcel Dassault-Breguet Aviation.
