Sibylla, Queen of Jerusalem

Reign1186–1190
Bornc. 1160
Died1190, Acre
SpouseWilliam of Montferrat; Guy of Lusignan

Sibylla (c. 1160 – 1190) was Queen of Jerusalem from 1186 to her death. The elder daughter of King Amalric I and Agnes of Courtenay, she inherited the throne after the deaths of her brother Baldwin IV, the "Leper King," and her young son Baldwin V.

Reign

Sibylla's coronation was controversial: the High Court had tried to force her to divorce her unpopular husband Guy of Lusignan, but she outmanoeuvred them, crowning Guy herself. Guy's defeat by Saladin at Hattin in 1187 led to the fall of Jerusalem. Sibylla joined the siege of Acre during the Third Crusade and died there in an epidemic in 1190, along with her young daughters, ending her line's direct claim.

Legacy

She is portrayed in the 2005 film Kingdom of Heaven by Eva Green, in a heavily fictionalised account.
