Metric prefix

A metric prefix is a unit prefix that precedes a basic unit of measure to indicate a multiple or submultiple of the unit. All metric prefixes used today are decadic: each prefix has a unique symbol that is prepended to any unit symbol. The prefixes are part of the International System of Units (SI) and are maintained by the International Bureau of Weights and Measures (BIPM).

The most recent prefixes — ronna, quetta, ronto, and quecto — were adopted in 2022, driven in part by the needs of data science to name very large quantities. Familiar examples include the kilometre (1,000 metres), the millimetre (one thousandth of a metre), and the gigabyte (109 bytes in SI usage).

Usage notes

Prefix symbols are case-sensitive: m denotes milli while M denotes mega. Prefixes are never combined (a millionth of a metre is a micrometre, not a "millimillimetre"), and the kilogram is the exceptional SI base unit that already incorporates a prefix.
