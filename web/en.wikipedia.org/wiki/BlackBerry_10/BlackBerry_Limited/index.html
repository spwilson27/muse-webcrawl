BlackBerry Limited

BlackBerry Limited is a Canadian software company specialising in cybersecurity and embedded systems. In the context of BlackBerry 10, it is the company — founded in 1984 as Research In Motion (RIM) in Waterloo, Ontario — that developed the operating system as the intended saviour of its smartphone business. The company renamed itself BlackBerry in January 2013 at the launch of BlackBerry 10.

BlackBerry 10 and the company's fate

BlackBerry 10, built on the QNX real-time operating system acquired in 2010, was launched on the Z10 and Q10 handsets in 2013 after long delays. Despite competent reviews, the platform failed to attract developers or buyers, and the company's handset market share collapsed. Under CEO John Chen from late 2013, BlackBerry pivoted away from hardware, licensed its phone brand to partners in 2016, and refocused on enterprise security software and QNX for automotive systems. Legacy BlackBerry services, including those supporting BlackBerry 10 devices, were shut down on 4 January 2022.
