BlackBerry OS

DeveloperBlackBerry Limited (formerly Research In Motion)
First released1999 (pagers); 2002 (phones)
KernelProprietary (Java-based platform)
SuccessorBlackBerry 10

BlackBerry OS is a proprietary mobile operating system developed by BlackBerry Limited (formerly Research In Motion) for its BlackBerry line of handheld devices. Introduced for pagers in 1999 and for smartphones from 2002, it powered the devices that made mobile email mainstream.

Features

The operating system was designed around push email delivered through BlackBerry Enterprise Server or BlackBerry Internet Service, the BlackBerry Messenger (BBM) messaging service, and hardware keyboards and trackballs or trackpads. Applications were written largely in Java (J2ME and BlackBerry-specific APIs). Later versions added touch support for the Storm and Torch lines.

Decline and end of support

BlackBerry OS peaked in the late 2000s before losing ground to the iPhone and Android. It was superseded by the QNX-based BlackBerry 10 in 2013, and BlackBerry ended services for the legacy OS in January 2022.
