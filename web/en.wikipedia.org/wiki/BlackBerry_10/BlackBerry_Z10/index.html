BlackBerry Z10

ManufacturerBlackBerry Limited
ReleasedJanuary-March 2013
Operating systemBlackBerry 10
Display4.2 in touchscreen, 1280x768

The BlackBerry Z10 is a touchscreen smartphone developed by BlackBerry Limited. Launched in early 2013, it was the first device running the new BlackBerry 10 operating system and marked the company's move away from its traditional keyboard-first designs.

Features

The Z10 has a 4.2-inch touchscreen with no physical buttons on the front; the BlackBerry 10 interface is driven by gestures, with the BlackBerry Hub unifying email, messages, and social accounts. The software also introduced BlackBerry Balance for separating work and personal data, and a camera with a "Time Shift" feature for adjusting faces in group photographs.

Reception

Reviews praised the hardware and interface but criticized the limited app ecosystem. Sales were disappointing, and BlackBerry recorded a large writedown on unsold Z10 inventory in 2013; the phone was followed by keyboard-equipped models such as the Q10 and, later, the Classic.
