Frank Williams (Formula One)

Born16 April 1942, South Shields, England
Died28 November 2021, London, England
OccupationFounder of Williams Grand Prix Engineering

Sir Francis Owen Garbett Williams (16 April 1942 – 28 November 2021) was a British businessman, racing car driver and the founder of the Williams Formula One team. He led the team from its creation in 1977 until his death, overseeing nine Constructors' Championships.

Career

After a modest career as a driver and mechanic, Williams founded his own racing operation in the late 1960s. With designer Patrick Head he built Williams Grand Prix Engineering into one of Formula One's most successful teams, winning drivers' titles with Alan Jones, Keke Rosberg, Nelson Piquet, Nigel Mansell, Alain Prost, Damon Hill and Jacques Villeneuve.

Accident and later life

A car crash in France in 1986 left Williams tetraplegic, but he continued to run the team from his wheelchair for more than three decades. The family sold its controlling stake in 2020, and Williams died the following year aged 79.
