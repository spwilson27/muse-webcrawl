Intranet

An intranet is a private computer network within an organisation that uses Internet technologies — principally web browsers and TCP/IP — to share information, collaboration tools and operational systems among its members, and is not accessible to the general public.

Uses

Intranets commonly host internal websites with news, policies and directories, document libraries, forms and workflow tools, and team workspaces. They became widespread in the 1990s as organisations adopted web technology for internal communication.

Extranets

An extranet extends part of an intranet to authorised external users such as partners or suppliers, typically over a VPN or with access controls, while remaining closed to the open Internet.

Benefits and design

Advantages include low deployment cost using standard web tools, faster internal communication and easier document management. Effective intranets emphasise search, usability and up-to-date content governance.
