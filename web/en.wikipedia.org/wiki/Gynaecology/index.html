Gynaecology
Field: Medicine
Focus: Female reproductive system
Related specialty: Obstetrics
Practitioner: Gynaecologist

Gynaecology (or gynecology) is the area of medicine concerned with the health of the female reproductive system — the uterus, ovaries, fallopian tubes, cervix, and vagina. It deals with the diagnosis and treatment of conditions such as menstrual disorders, infections, endometriosis, fibroids, ovarian cysts, pelvic pain, and cancers of the reproductive organs.

Scope

Gynaecologists provide preventive care including cervical screening (Pap smears) and HPV vaccination, contraception, fertility assessment, and menopause care. Many practise jointly with obstetrics (OB/GYN), which covers pregnancy and childbirth.

Procedures

Common investigations include pelvic examination, ultrasound, colposcopy, and hysteroscopy. Surgical procedures include laparoscopy, hysterectomy, and ovarian surgery.

History

The field developed in the 19th century; J. Marion Sims is a controversial figure in its history. Modern gynaecology emphasises evidence-based care and patient consent.
