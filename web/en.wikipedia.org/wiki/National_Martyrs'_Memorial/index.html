National Martyrs' Memorial

The National Martyrs' Memorial (Jatiya Smriti Soudho) is the national monument of Bangladesh, located in Savar, about 35 kilometres northwest of Dhaka. It commemorates those who died in the Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971.

Design

Designed by architect Syed Mainul Hossain, the monument consists of seven pairs of triangular concrete walls of increasing height, the tallest rising about 150 feet (46 metres). The complex, completed in 1982, includes mass graves, a lake, and landscaped grounds planted with trees by visiting heads of state.

Significance

The memorial is the focal point of national commemorations on Victory Day (16 December) and Independence Day (26 March), when the President and Prime Minister lay wreaths there at dawn.
