As It Was

"As It Was" spent 15 non-consecutive weeks atop the Billboard Hot 100

"As It Was" is a song by English singer Harry Styles, released on 1 April 2022 as the lead single from his third studio album, Harry's House. Written by Styles with producers Kid Harpoon and Tyler Johnson, the synth-pop track reflects on personal change and loneliness.

Commercial performance

The song debuted at number one in numerous countries, including the UK and US. On the Billboard Hot 100 it spent 15 non-consecutive weeks at number one, among the longest runs in the chart's history, and it was named the biggest song of 2022 worldwide by the IFPI. It became one of the most-streamed songs on Spotify.

Music video and reception

Directed by Tanu Muino and filmed in London, including at the Barbican and a penguin-less art installation setting, the video shows Styles on a rotating platform unable to connect with a dance partner. Critics praised the song's melancholic contrast with its bright, 1980s-influenced production.
