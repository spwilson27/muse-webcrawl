Household Division

The Household Division forms part of the British Army and consists of the regiments that traditionally guard the Sovereign and the royal palaces. It comprises the Household Cavalry (the Life Guards and the Blues and Royals) and the five regiments of Foot Guards: the Grenadier, Coldstream, Scots, Irish and Welsh Guards.

Role

The division provides the guard at Buckingham Palace, St James's Palace, the Tower of London and Windsor Castle, and performs the great state ceremonial occasions in London, including Trooping the Colour, state visits and the State Opening of Parliament. Its regiments are also fully operational combat units: the Household Cavalry serve as armoured reconnaissance, and the Foot Guards as infantry, and all have deployed on operations worldwide.

The Major General commanding the Household Division, who also commands London District, is responsible for the Army's ceremonial duties in the capital.
