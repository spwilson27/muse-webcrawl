The UCI Road World Championships are the annual world championships for road bicycle racing, organised by the world governing body, the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI). The championships consist of events for road race and individual time trial, contested by national teams in categories for men and women, under-23, and junior riders.

History

The first world championship road race for amateurs was held in 1921 in Copenhagen. A professional men's championship followed in 1927 at the Nürburgring in Germany, won by Alfredo Binda of Italy. The women's road race was added in 1958, and the individual time trial became a championship event in 1994. Since 2019 the programme has also included a mixed relay team time trial.

Rainbow jersey

Winners of each event earn the right to wear the rainbow jersey—a white jersey with bands of blue, red, black, yellow, and green—in that discipline for the following year. Past champions may display rainbow piping on their jersey collars and sleeves for the rest of their careers.

Events

  • Men's and women's elite road race
  • Men's and women's elite individual time trial
  • Under-23 and junior road races and time trials
  • Mixed relay team time trial

Until 2023 the Road World Championships were held as a standalone event; from 2023, every four years they form part of the combined UCI Cycling World Championships alongside other disciplines such as track, mountain bike, and BMX.
