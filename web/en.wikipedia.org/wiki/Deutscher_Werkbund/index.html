Deutscher Werkbund

The Deutscher Werkbund ("German Association of Craftsmen") was an association of artists, architects, designers, and industrialists founded in Munich in 1907 by Hermann Muthesius and others. Its aim was to improve the design quality of German industrial products through cooperation between art, craft, and industry.

Influence

The Werkbund's 1914 Cologne exhibition, which included model factories by Walter Gropius and Henry van de Velde's theatre, became famous for the debate over standardisation versus individual expression. The association's ideas fed directly into the founding of the Bauhaus in 1919. Dissolved in 1934, it was refounded after the Second World War.
