Laurel Park is an American thoroughbred racetrack in Laurel, Maryland, between Baltimore and Washington, D.C. Opened in 1911, it has long been one of Maryland's principal racing venues, alongside sister track Pimlico, home of the Preakness Stakes.

History and races

The track's best-known race was the Washington, D.C. International, inaugurated in 1952 as one of the first American races to attract top overseas horses and a precursor of the Breeders' Cup Turf. Laurel's current stakes programme includes the Frank J. De Francis Memorial Dash and the Barbara Fritchie Stakes. In recent years Maryland racing has been restructured, with plans to consolidate racing at a rebuilt Pimlico and convert Laurel Park into a training facility.
