List of smoked foods

This is a list of notable foods that are prepared by smoking — exposing food to smoke from burning or smouldering wood or other material. Smoking preserves food, adds flavour, and may be done hot (cooking the food) or cold (at low temperature, for flavour and partial preservation). Many smoked foods are also cured with salt first.

Meats and sausages

  • Bacon — smoked pork belly or back cuts (streaky bacon, back bacon, pancetta, speck)
  • Ham — including Black Forest ham, country ham, prosciutto affumicato, smoked gammon
  • Pastrami — brined, seasoned and smoked beef (usually navel or brisket)
  • Montreal-style smoked meat
  • Biltong-adjacent smoked meats, jerky (often smoked)
  • Kielbasa and other smoked Polish sausages; andouille; mettwurst; teewurst; smoked chorizo; Chinese lap cheong variants
  • Smoked turkey, smoked duck, smoked chicken
  • Salmon alternatives: smoked brisket (barbecue), pulled pork — products of American barbecue's low-and-slow smoking

Fish and seafood

  • Smoked salmon — cold-smoked lox and hot-smoked salmon
  • Smoked mackerel, smoked herring (kippers, buckling, bloaters, red herring)
  • Arbroath smokie — hot-smoked haddock from Scotland
  • Finnan haddie — cold-smoked haddock
  • Smoked trout, smoked eel, smoked sturgeon, smoked whitefish
  • Smoked oysters, smoked mussels
  • Bombay duck (smoked/dried), smoked cod roe, bottarga
  • Surströmming is fermented rather than smoked; rakfisk likewise

Cheeses

  • Smoked Gouda, smoked cheddar, smoked Gruyère
  • Rauchkäse (German smoked cheese)
  • Smoked mozzarella and scamorza affumicata
  • Oscypek — smoked sheep's-milk cheese from the Polish Tatra Mountains
  • Smoked provolone, smoked Jarlsberg

Other foods

  • Smoked eggs (Chinese iron eggs are braised; Japanese ajitama are sometimes smoked)
  • Smoked tofu and smoked seitan
  • Lapsang souchong — Chinese black tea dried over pinewood fires
  • Smoked paprika (pimentón de la Vera)
  • Chipotle — smoke-dried jalapeño peppers
  • Smoked salt, smoked garlic, smoked almonds and other nuts
  • Rauchbier and whiskies made with peat-smoked malt acquire smoke flavour in production
  • Smoked aubergine/eggplant preparations
