Kemenche

The kemenche (Turkish: kemençe; Greek: κεμεντζές; also spelled kemençe, kementche or kemanche) is a name given to several types of bowed string instrument originating in the Eastern Mediterranean and Black Sea regions, related to the Byzantine lyra and the Persian kamancheh.

Types

The Classical kemençe (Politiki lyra) is a pear-shaped, three-stringed instrument held upright, central to Ottoman classical music. The Black Sea kemençe (Karadeniz kemençesi) is a bottle-shaped instrument played in the Pontic Greek and Laz traditions and in Turkish folk music of the eastern Black Sea, accompanying the horon dance.

Construction and playing

Instruments are carved from a single block of wood with a pegbox and played with a bow while held vertically on the knee or standing. Fingernails press against the strings from the side in the classical form.
