Hotel (novel)

AuthorArthur Hailey
Published1965
GenreNovel

Hotel is a 1965 novel by British-Canadian author Arthur Hailey. Set over five days in the fictional St. Gregory Hotel in New Orleans, it interweaves the stories of the hotel's staff and guests as the financially troubled independent hotel faces takeover by a chain.

Plot

General manager Peter McDermott struggles to keep the St. Gregory solvent while handling crises ranging from a hit-and-run cover-up and a would-be rapist to a thief operating in the rooms, ageing plumbing, and the rival ambitions of a union organiser and a hotel magnate. Owner Warren Trent must decide whether to sell to Curtis O'Keefe's chain or accept a rescue offer from a Montreal buyers' group.

Adaptations

The novel was filmed in 1967 with Rod Taylor as McDermott, and adapted again as a television series, Hotel, which ran on ABC from 1983 to 1988 with the setting moved to San Francisco.
