Thar Desert

The Thar Desert, also known as the Great Indian Desert, is an arid region in the northwestern part of the Indian subcontinent covering about 200,000 square kilometres, mostly in the Indian state of Rajasthan, extending into Haryana, Punjab and Gujarat and into Sindh and Punjab provinces of Pakistan. It is the most densely populated desert in the world, with a population density far above other deserts.

The desert is dotted with historic fort cities such as Jaisalmer, Bikaner and Jodhpur on the border of the desert. The Indira Gandhi Canal brings water from Himalayan rivers to irrigate parts of the Thar, and the region is rich in folk culture, camel fairs at Pushkar, and wind and solar energy potential.
