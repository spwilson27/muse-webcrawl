A city is a large, permanent and densely settled place, generally with extensive systems for housing, transport, sanitation, utilities and governance. Legal definitions vary by country — some small settlements are 'cities' by charter, while vast urban areas may lack the title.

Urbanisation

More than half of humanity now lives in urban areas, a share still rising; the world's largest metropolitan areas include Tokyo, Delhi, Shanghai and — in this crawl — Karachi. Cities concentrate economic activity, culture and innovation, and also congestion, pollution and inequality; planning them well is a core sustainability challenge. Historic Geneva, Lhasa and Byzantium show the city's many forms.
