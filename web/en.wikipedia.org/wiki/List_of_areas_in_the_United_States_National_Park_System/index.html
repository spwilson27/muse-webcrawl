List of areas in the United States National Park System

The National Park System of the United States is the collection of physical properties owned or administered by the National Park Service (NPS). The system comprises more than 430 units covering more than 85 million acres in all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and US territories. Units include national parks, national monuments, national memorials, national historic sites, national recreation areas, national seashores, national battlefields and other designations.

The system's oldest unit is Yellowstone National Park, established in 1872 — the world's first national park. The largest unit is Wrangell–St. Elias National Park and Preserve in Alaska at over 13.2 million acres.

Other unit types

Beyond the 63 national parks, the system includes roughly 130 national monuments (some administered with the Bureau of Land Management and other agencies), national memorials such as Mount Rushmore and the Lincoln Memorial, national historic sites, national historical parks such as Independence and Harpers Ferry, national battlefields and military parks such as Gettysburg, national recreation areas, national seashores and lakeshores, national rivers, national scenic trails, and the parkways including the Blue Ridge Parkway and Natchez Trace.
