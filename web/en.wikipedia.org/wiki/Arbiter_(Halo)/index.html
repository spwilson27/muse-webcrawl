Arbiter (Halo)

Thel 'Vadam, the Arbiter in Halo 3.

The Arbiter is a fictional character in the Halo franchise. "Arbiter" is a title given to a Sangheili (Elite) warrior by the Covenant's Hierarchs in times of crisis — a rank that is at once a great honour and a death sentence, since Arbiters are expected to carry out suicidal missions. The Arbiter featured in the games is Thel 'Vadam (formerly Thel 'Vadamee), voiced by Keith David. He first appears as a playable protagonist in Halo 2 (2004).

Role in the series

Thel 'Vadamee was the Supreme Commander of the Covenant fleet that destroyed the human colony of Reach and pursued the UNSC ship Pillar of Autumn to Installation 04. After the ringworld's destruction, he was stripped of rank, tortured, and branded, then offered the mantle of Arbiter by the Prophets. Sent on missions against heretics and the Flood, he gradually uncovered the Prophets' deceptions; when the Covenant civil war (the Great Schism) erupted, he allied his Sangheili with humanity. In Halo 3 he fights alongside the Master Chief and helps kill the Prophet of Truth, ending the war.

Reception

The decision to make an alien a second playable character in Halo 2 initially divided fans, but the Arbiter has since become one of the series' most popular characters, praised for the depth his storyline added to the Covenant. He appears in Halo 3, Halo 5: Guardians, and several novels.
