Abdul Hamid Dbeibah (Arabic: عبد الحميد الدبيبة; born 1958) is a Libyan businessman and politician who has served as prime minister of the Government of National Unity (GNU) of Libya since March 2021. His surname is also transliterated Dbeibeh, which is treated in the companion article under that spelling.

Early life and business career

Dbeibah was born in Misrata into a prominent business family. Under Muammar Gaddafi he headed the state-owned Libyan Investment and Development Company (LIDCO), overseeing major construction projects, and was associated with the Al-Ittihad football club. His family's business empire made him one of Libya's wealthiest men, a background that later fuelled both his political base in Misrata and corruption allegations he denies.

Prime minister

On 5 February 2021 the UN-led Libyan Political Dialogue Forum, meeting in Geneva, elected the joint list headed by Mohamed al-Menfi (Presidential Council) and Dbeibah as prime minister-designate. The House of Representatives granted his GNU confidence on 10 March 2021, and he took office on 15 March, tasked with unifying state institutions and holding elections scheduled for 24 December 2021. The elections failed to take place, and in February 2022 the eastern-based parliament appointed Fathi Bashagha as a rival prime minister, leaving Libya with competing governments; attempts by Bashagha to enter Tripoli failed, and he was later replaced by Osama Hamada. Dbeibah remained in control of the capital and western Libya, promising to leave office only after elections, while critics accused him of consolidating power and of presiding over continued militia influence and disputed spending.
