Eleventh Doctor

The Eleventh Doctor is an incarnation of the Doctor, the protagonist of the BBC science fiction television series Doctor Who. He was played by Matt Smith, who took over from David Tennant's Tenth Doctor in 2010 and remained in the role until 2013, becoming at 26 the youngest actor to play the part. Steven Moffat was the showrunner throughout the Eleventh Doctor's tenure.

Characterisation

The Eleventh Doctor is energetic, eccentric and apparently youthful, with a childlike enthusiasm masking an ancient and sometimes ruthless mind. His costume — tweed jacket, bow tie (his catchphrase: "Bow ties are cool"), braces and boots, later joined by a fez — became iconic. Signature episodes and arcs include the crack in the wall, the Silence, the mystery of River Song, and the Doctor's apparent death at Lake Silencio.

Companions and story arcs

His principal companions were Amy Pond (Karen Gillan) and Rory Williams (Arthur Darvill), and later Clara Oswald (Jenna Coleman). In the 50th-anniversary special "The Day of the Doctor" (2013) he joined the Tenth Doctor and the War Doctor to change the outcome of the Time War. In the 2013 Christmas special "The Time of the Doctor", having spent centuries defending the town of Christmas on Trenzalore, he received a new regeneration cycle from the Time Lords and regenerated into the Twelfth Doctor (Peter Capaldi).

Reception

Smith's performance was widely praised and earned him a BAFTA nomination; his series coincided with a marked growth of Doctor Who's popularity in the United States.
