List of compositions by Johann Sebastian Bach

Johann Sebastian Bach (1685–1750) composed over 1,000 works, catalogued by Wolfgang Schmieder in the Bach-Werke-Verzeichnis (BWV). The catalogue is organised by genre rather than chronology. This article lists the principal works.

Organ works (BWV 525–771)

  • Toccata and Fugue in D minor, BWV 565
  • Great Fantasia and Fugue in G minor, BWV 542
  • Passacaglia and Fugue in C minor, BWV 582
  • Six trio sonatas, BWV 525–530
  • Schübler Chorales, BWV 645–650
  • "Great Eighteen" chorale preludes, BWV 651–668
  • Orgelbüchlein (Little Organ Book), BWV 599–644

Keyboard works (BWV 772–994)

  • The Well-Tempered Clavier, Books 1 and 2, BWV 846–893
  • Two- and three-part inventions, BWV 772–801
  • French Suites, BWV 812–817; English Suites, BWV 806–811
  • Partitas, BWV 825–830
  • Goldberg Variations, BWV 988
  • Italian Concerto, BWV 971; Chromatic Fantasia and Fugue, BWV 903

Orchestral and chamber works

  • Brandenburg Concertos, BWV 1046–1051
  • Four orchestral suites, BWV 1066–1069 (No. 3 includes the "Air")
  • Violin concertos, BWV 1041–1043
  • Harpsichord concertos, BWV 1052–1065
  • Sonatas and partitas for solo violin, BWV 1001–1006
  • Cello suites, BWV 1007–1012
  • The Musical Offering, BWV 1079
  • The Art of Fugue, BWV 1080
