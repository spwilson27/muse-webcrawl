1992 Summer Olympics medal table

The 1992 Summer Olympics medal table is a list of National Olympic Committees ranked by the number of gold medals won during the 1992 Summer Olympics, held in Barcelona, Spain, from 25 July to 9 August 1992. A total of 9,356 athletes from 169 countries participated in 257 events in 25 sports.

Barcelona 1992: the first Games since 1972 without a superpower boycott.

Athletes from 64 countries won medals, and 47 won at least one gold medal. The Unified Team — comprising the former Soviet republics other than the Baltic states — topped the table in its only Summer Olympics appearance. Hosts Spain won 13 gold medals, a national record at the time. Germany competed as a single team for the first time since 1964 following reunification, while South Africa returned to the Games for the first time since 1960 after the end of apartheid.

The table is sorted by the number of gold medals, then silver, then bronze, following the convention used by the International Olympic Committee. The United States won the most medals overall after the Unified Team, and notable performances included gymnast Vitaly Scherbo winning six gold medals for the Unified Team, and the American men's basketball "Dream Team" winning gold in dominant fashion.
