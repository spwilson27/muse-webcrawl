E.ON

E.ON SE is a German multinational electric utility company based in Essen, North Rhine-Westphalia. It is one of the world's largest investor-owned electric utility service providers, operating energy networks and retail supply businesses serving tens of millions of customers, principally in Europe.

History

E.ON was formed in 2000 from the merger of the German energy conglomerates VEBA and VIAG. It expanded internationally, acquiring companies including Powergen in the United Kingdom and Ruhrgas, and at its peak operated generation, gas, and trading businesses worldwide.

In a major restructuring announced in 2014, E.ON split off its conventional power generation, global energy trading, and exploration and production businesses into a new company, Uniper, which was spun off in 2016. E.ON retained renewables, networks, and customer solutions. In a complex asset swap with RWE agreed in 2018 and completed in 2019–2020, E.ON acquired innogy's network and retail businesses and transferred its renewables activities to RWE, becoming a pure networks and retail utility.
