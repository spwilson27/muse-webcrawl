About Damn Time

"About Damn Time" is a song by American singer and rapper Lizzo, released on 14 April 2022 as the lead single from her fourth studio album, Special (2022). A disco-funk track produced by Blake Slatkin and Ricky Reed, it became Lizzo's second number-one single on the US Billboard Hot 100 and won Record of the Year at the 65th Grammy Awards.

Composition and release

Written by Lizzo (Melissa Jefferson) with Slatkin, Reed, Larry Price, Malcolm McLaren, Ronald Larkins, Stephen Hague and Theron Thomas, the song interpolates "Hey DJ" by the World's Famous Supreme Team. Built on a funk guitar groove and a flute riff played by Lizzo herself — she is a classically trained flautist — the song is a self-empowerment anthem about recovering from stress and exhaustion. It was accompanied by a dance created by choreographer Jaeden Gomez that went viral on TikTok, driving the single's chart run in the summer of 2022.

Reception and accolades

Critics praised its buoyant retro production and Lizzo's charisma. The song topped the Hot 100 for two weeks, reached the top ten in numerous countries, and won Record of the Year at the 2023 Grammy Awards, where Lizzo dedicated the award to Prince, a major influence. It was also nominated for Song of the Year and Best Pop Solo Performance.
