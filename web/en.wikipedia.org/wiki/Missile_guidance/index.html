Missile guidance

Missile guidance refers to methods of guiding a missile to its intended target. Guidance systems vary by mission: air-to-air, surface-to-air, anti-tank, ballistic, and cruise missiles use different combinations of sensors, navigation, and control.

Categories

Systems are broadly classed as Go-Onto-Target (GOT), following a target, and Go-Onto-Location-In-Space (GOLIS), navigating to fixed coordinates. Methods include inertial navigation, GPS/GNSS, terrain contour matching (TERCOM), and stellar navigation.

Homing

Homing guidance includes active radar homing (the missile carries its own radar), semi-active radar homing (illuminated by the launch platform), passive homing on infrared or anti-radiation emissions, laser guidance, and command guidance such as MCLOS and SACLOS. Proportional navigation is a common guidance law for interception.
