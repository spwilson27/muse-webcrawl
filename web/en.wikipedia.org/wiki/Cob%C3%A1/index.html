Cobá

Cobá is a ruined ancient Maya city in the Mexican state of Quintana Roo, near the town of Cobá. It was one of the largest Classic-period Maya settlements in the northern Yucatán Peninsula, connected to other cities by an extensive network of raised stone causeways called sacbeob.

Peak populationUp to about 50,000 (c. 600–900 CE)

Site

The site covers some 80 km² around two lagoons. Its tallest structure, the Nohoch Mul pyramid, rises about 42 metres and is among the tallest Maya pyramids in the Yucatán. A 100 km sacbé linking Cobá to Yaxuná is the longest known Maya causeway.
