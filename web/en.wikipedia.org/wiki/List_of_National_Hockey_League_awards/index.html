List of National Hockey League awards

The National Hockey League (NHL) presents numerous annual awards and trophies to recognize its teams and players. The oldest, the Stanley Cup, was donated by Lord Stanley of Preston in 1892 and is awarded to the league playoff champion.

The NHL's major trophies are presented at an annual awards ceremony.

History

Many trophies were donated by individuals or organizations in the league's early decades. The NHL Awards ceremony is typically held in late June, following the Stanley Cup Final.
