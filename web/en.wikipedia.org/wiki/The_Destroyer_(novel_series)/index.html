The Destroyer (novel series)

The Destroyer is a series of paperback adventure novels created by Warren Murphy and Richard Sapir, first published in 1971. The series follows Remo Williams, a former police officer recruited by the secret government organisation CURE and trained in the fictional Korean martial art of Sinanju by the aged master Chiun. Combining action with satire of contemporary politics and culture, the series ran to more than 150 volumes.

Premise

Remo Williams, framed for murder and officially executed, is revived and recruited by CURE, an organisation answerable only to the President, which uses assassination to defend the Constitution by working outside it. His trainer Chiun, the reigning Master of Sinanju — the "sun source" of all martial arts from which Chiun claims all others descend — provides much of the series' comedy through his devotion to soap operas, his contempt for Western food, and his chauvinistic pride in his village and art.

Publication and adaptations

Early volumes were written by Murphy and Sapir together; later entries involved ghostwriters and collaborators including Molly Cochran and Will Murray. The series was adapted as the 1985 film Remo Williams: The Adventure Begins, starring Fred Ward, and as a 1988 television pilot. A rebooted "New Destroyer" sequence began in 2007.
