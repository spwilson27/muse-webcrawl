Tilburg University is a public research university in Tilburg, in the southern Netherlands. It was founded in 1927 as a Catholic school of economics, the Roomsch Katholieke Handelshoogeschool, and has since developed a strong reputation in economics, business, law, social sciences, and the humanities. Its motto is "Understanding Society".

History

The institution grew from an economics school into a broad university, formally taking the name Tilburg University in 2010. It was historically Roman Catholic in character, a heritage still reflected in its school of Catholic theology, while today it is a publicly funded university open to all.

Organisation

The university comprises schools of economics and management, law, social and behavioral sciences, humanities and digital sciences, and Catholic theology, along with research institutes such as CentER for economic research. Its campus is set in parkland on the western edge of Tilburg, near the Tilburg Universiteit railway station.
