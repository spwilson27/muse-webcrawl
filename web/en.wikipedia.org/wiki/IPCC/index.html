Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) is the United Nations body for assessing the science related to climate change, established in 1988 by the World Meteorological Organization and the UN Environment Programme.

Work

The IPCC does not conduct original research; thousands of volunteer scientists assess published literature in cycles of Assessment Reports, plus special reports on topics such as limiting warming to 1.5 °C. Its reports underpin international climate negotiations and shared the 2007 Nobel Peace Prize with Al Gore.
