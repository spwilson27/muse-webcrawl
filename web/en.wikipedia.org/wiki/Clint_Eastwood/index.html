Clint Eastwood

Clinton Eastwood Jr. (born 31 May 1930) is an American actor and filmmaker, an enduring cultural icon of masculinity. He rose to fame in the TV Western Rawhide and as the 'Man with No Name' in Sergio Leone's Dollars trilogy of spaghetti Westerns in the 1960s, then as Harry Callahan in the Dirty Harry films.

Directing career

From Play Misty for Me (1971) onward, Eastwood directed a long series of acclaimed films through his Malpaso Productions, winning Academy Awards for Best Director and Best Picture for Unforgiven (1992) and Million Dollar Baby (2004). Other notable works include Mystic River, Gran Torino, Letters from Iwo Jima, American Sniper — his biggest commercial success — and Sully.

Politics and personal life

A registered Republican of libertarian leanings, he served as nonpartisan mayor of Carmel-by-the-Sea, California, from 1986 to 1988. He is also a jazz enthusiast and composer of several of his films' scores.
