Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion

Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion
Location: Axum, Tigray Region, Ethiopia
Denomination: Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church
Claimed relic: Ark of the Covenant (in adjacent chapel)

The Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion is an Ethiopian Orthodox church in Axum, Ethiopia. It is one of the holiest sites in Ethiopian Christianity, claimed to house the Ark of the Covenant in its Chapel of the Tablet.

History

The original church is traditionally dated to the reign of Ezana, the first Christian emperor of Axum, in the 4th century. It was repeatedly destroyed and rebuilt; the present old church dates largely to the 17th century under Emperor Fasilides, and a new, larger church was built beside it under Haile Selassie in the 1950s–60s, notable for allowing women entry, unlike the old church.

The Ark

Ethiopian tradition, recorded in the Kebra Nagast, holds that Menelik I brought the Ark from Jerusalem. A single guardian monk tends the chapel, and no one else is permitted to view the relic. Axum was also the coronation site of Ethiopian emperors.
