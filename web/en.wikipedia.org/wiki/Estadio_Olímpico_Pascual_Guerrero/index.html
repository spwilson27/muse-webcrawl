Estadio Olímpico Pascual Guerrero

LocationSan Fernando, Cali, Valle del Cauca, Colombia
Opened20 July 1937
Capacityabout 37,900
TenantsAmérica de Cali; Atlético F.C.; Boca Juniors de Cali

The Estadio Olímpico Pascual Guerrero is a multi-purpose stadium in Cali, Colombia, located in the San Fernando neighbourhood. It is home to América de Cali and is named after the poet and politician Pascual Guerrero.

History

The stadium was inaugurated in 1937 on land donated by its namesake, and has been rebuilt and expanded several times. It was a central venue of the 1971 Pan American Games in Cali and hosted matches of the 2001 Copa América and the 2011 FIFA U-20 World Cup, as well as group matches of the 2022 FIFA Women's World Cup qualifiers and the 2023 South American U-20 Championship.

Use

For decades the ground was shared by América de Cali and Deportivo Cali, until the latter moved to its own stadium in Palmira in 2010. Besides football, the Pascual Guerrero hosts athletics and concerts, and forms part of a larger sports complex in San Fernando. Its capacity has varied across renovations, stabilising around 38,000 after modern seating was installed.
