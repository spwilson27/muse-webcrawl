United National Congress

The United National Congress (UNC) is one of two major political parties in Trinidad and Tobago, along with the People's National Movement. It is a centre to centre-left party whose support base is concentrated among Indo-Trinidadian communities. The party's symbol is the rising sun above the Trinity Hills.

History

The UNC was founded in 1988 by Basdeo Panday, a former leader of the United Labour Front, after a split in the ruling National Alliance for Reconstruction. The party won the 1995 general election in coalition with the NAR, making Panday the country's first Indo-Trinidadian prime minister. The UNC returned to power in 2010 as the senior partner in the People's Partnership coalition under Kamla Persad-Bissessar, the country's first woman prime minister. The party returned to government following the 2025 general election.
