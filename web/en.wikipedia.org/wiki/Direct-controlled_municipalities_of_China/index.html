Direct-controlled municipalities of China

A direct-controlled municipality is the highest level of classification for cities in the People's Republic of China. Such municipalities have the same rank as provinces and are directly administered by the central government. There are four: Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai and Chongqing. Chongqing, re-established in 1997, is by area and population the largest, encompassing much of the former eastern Sichuan region. A direct-controlled municipality is governed by a mayor and is divided into districts and, in Chongqing's case, counties.
