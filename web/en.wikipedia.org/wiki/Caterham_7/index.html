Caterham 7

Manufacturer: Caterham Cars
Production: 1973–present
Designer: Colin Chapman (original Lotus Seven)
Class: Sports car / kit car

The Caterham 7 (or Caterham Seven) is a lightweight sports car produced by Caterham Cars in England. It is based on the Lotus Seven designed by Colin Chapman, whose production rights Caterham acquired from Lotus in 1973.

Design

The car retains the Seven's formula: a steel spaceframe chassis, aluminium body panels, front-mid engine, rear-wheel drive, and minimal equipment, giving weights around 500 kg and exceptional handling. Models have ranged from the modest Classic to the extreme 620R and the electric-era concepts.

History

Caterham has continuously developed the car with modern engines from Ford, Rover, Suzuki, and others, selling it both fully built and as a self-assembly kit. The Seven is popular in club racing, including dedicated Caterham championships.
