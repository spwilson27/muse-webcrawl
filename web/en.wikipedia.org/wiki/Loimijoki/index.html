Loimijoki
Location: Finland
Mouth: Kokemäenjoki

The Loimijoki is a river in southwestern Finland, a tributary of the Kokemäenjoki. It flows through the region of Southwest Finland and Satakunta past settlements including Loimaa, from which it takes its name.

Course

The river runs through agricultural lowland, and its valley has long been an important farming area. Small rapids were historically used for mills.

Significance

The Loimijoki is noted for its cultural landscape and for fishing, and flooding has periodically affected its low-lying banks.
