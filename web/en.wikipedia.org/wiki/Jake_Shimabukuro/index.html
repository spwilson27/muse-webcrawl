Jake Shimabukuro

Jake Shimabukuro (born November 3, 1976) is a Japanese-American ukulele virtuoso and composer from Honolulu, Hawaii. He is renowned for expanding the technical and musical possibilities of the ukulele through fast, complex finger work and arrangements spanning rock, jazz, classical, and traditional Hawaiian music.

Career

Shimabukuro began playing ukulele at age four, taught by his mother. In the 1990s he performed with the groups Pure Heart and Colón before launching a solo career in Japan (2000) and signing with Epic Records International. In 2006 a video of him performing George Harrison's While My Guitar Gently Weeps in Central Park went viral on YouTube, bringing him international fame as one of the first viral music-video stars.

He has toured extensively, performed with artists such as Jimmy Buffett, Bette Midler, Béla Fleck, and Yo-Yo Ma, and released acclaimed albums including Gently Weeps (2006), Peace Love Ukulele (2011), and Nashville Sessions (2016). In 2021 he released Jake & Friends, featuring collaborations with artists including Jack Johnson, Willie Nelson, and Warren Haynes. He is regarded as the world's foremost ukulele performer and an ambassador of Hawaiian music.
