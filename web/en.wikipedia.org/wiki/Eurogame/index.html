Eurogame

A Eurogame, also called a German-style board game, is a class of board games that emphasise indirect player interaction, abstract physical components, and strategy over luck or conflict. The genre emerged in Germany in the late 20th century and was popularised internationally by The Settlers of Catan (1995).

Characteristics

Common features include victory-point scoring rather than player elimination, themes that are secondary to mechanics, wooden tokens, relatively short playing times, and an emphasis on resource management and engine building. Players usually remain in the game to the end and compete on points rather than direct confrontation.

History

Modern Eurogames grew out of the German family-games market and awards such as the Spiel des Jahres (founded 1978). Titles such as Carcassonne, Puerto Rico, Ticket to Ride and Agricola helped define and spread the style worldwide.
