Oracle (comics)

Oracle is a superhero identity used by Barbara Gordon in American comic books published by DC Comics. Barbara Gordon, the daughter of Gotham City police commissioner James Gordon, originally debuted as the first modern Batgirl in 1967. After the Joker shot and paralyzed her in the 1988 graphic novel Batman: The Killing Joke, she reinvented herself as Oracle — a master computer hacker and information broker who provides intelligence and coordination to Batman and other heroes.

Fictional character biography

As Oracle, operating from the Gotham Clock Tower, Barbara built an unparalleled information network and became a central figure of the superhero community. She founded and led the Birds of Prey, working closely with Black Canary and Huntress, and played key roles in major storylines including No Man's Land. Her portrayal as a highly capable hero who uses a wheelchair was widely praised as positive representation of disability in comics.

The New 52 and later

In DC's 2011 New 52 relaunch, Barbara regained the use of her legs through experimental treatment and returned to the role of Batgirl, though she retained her skills from her time as Oracle. The Oracle identity has since also been connected to other characters and arrangements in subsequent continuity, and elements of the Oracle persona have appeared in adaptations such as the Batman: Arkham video games and animated series.
