Elvis impersonator

An Elvis impersonator is someone who impersonates or copies the look and sound of musician Elvis Presley. Professional Elvis impersonators, also sometimes known as Elvis tribute artists (ETAs), work all over the world as entertainers, and such tribute acts remain in great demand due to the unique iconic status of Presley.

Types

Impersonators typically portray one of several eras of Presley's career: the rockabilly 1950s Elvis, the movie-star Elvis of the 1960s, or the jumpsuited Las Vegas Elvis of the 1970s, the latter — complete with white rhinestone jumpsuit, cape, and tinted sunglasses — being the most commonly copied image.

Culture

There are estimated to be tens of thousands of Elvis impersonators worldwide. Competitions such as the Ultimate Elvis Tribute Artist Contest are held in Memphis during Elvis Week. Impersonators perform at weddings, festivals, and casinos, particularly in Las Vegas, where "Elvis" wedding chapels are a longstanding tradition.

History

Impersonation of Presley began during his lifetime, and the phenomenon grew enormously after his death in 1977. Elvis impersonators have become a recognizable part of American popular culture and kitsch.
