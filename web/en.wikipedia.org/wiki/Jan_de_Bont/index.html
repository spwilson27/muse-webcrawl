Jan de Bont

Jan de Bont (born 22 October 1943) is a Dutch-American film director and cinematographer, best known for directing the action films Speed (1994) and Twister (1996), after a career as the director of photography on films including Die Hard and The Hunt for Red October.

Career

De Bont began as a cinematographer in the Netherlands and Hollywood, lensing Paul Verhoeven's Dutch films and major American productions. His directorial debut Speed, starring Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock, was a box-office hit, followed by the tornado disaster film Twister.

Later work

Later directorial efforts included Speed 2: Cruise Control (1997), The Haunting (1999) and Lara Croft: Tomb Raider – The Cradle of Life (2003). Critical and commercial success declined, though Speed and Twister remain influential action films.
