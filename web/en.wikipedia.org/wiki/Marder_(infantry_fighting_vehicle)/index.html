The Schützenpanzer Marder 1 is a tracked German infantry fighting vehicle designed for the West German Army and continuously modernised since entering service in 1971. It was the first IFV fielded by a Western army.

Design

The Marder carries a 20 mm Rheinmetall MK 20 Rh202 autocannon in a two-man turret, a coaxial 7.62 mm machine gun, and in later variants a MILAN anti-tank missile launcher. Its MTU diesel engine gives a road speed of about 65 km/h.

Variants and upgrades

The improved Marder 1A3 (from 1989) added armour, a new turret and thermal sights; the 1A5 added mine protection for Afghanistan service. Several thousand were built, with the Puma IFV intended as its successor.

Operators

Besides Germany, Marders were exported to Chile, Indonesia, Greece and Jordan. From 2023 Germany transferred Marder 1A3s to Ukraine for use in the Russo-Ukrainian War.
