Fifty Shades Freed is a 2018 American erotic romantic drama film directed by James Foley and written by E. L. James, based on her 2012 novel of the same name. It is the third and final instalment in the Fifty Shades film series, following Fifty Shades of Grey (2015) and Fifty Shades Darker (2017). Dakota Johnson and Jamie Dornan star as Anastasia Steele and Christian Grey.

Plot

Newly married, Christian and Ana enjoy a European honeymoon that is cut short by an arson attack at Grey Enterprises. Ana returns to work at Seattle Independent Publishing, and the couple's marriage is tested by Christian's controlling behaviour, the return of Ana's former boss Jack Hyde—who kidnaps Christian's sister Mia in a ransom plot—and Ana's unplanned pregnancy. Ana outwits Hyde, who is arrested, and the film ends with the couple settled with their young son, expecting another child.

Production

The film was shot back-to-back with Fifty Shades Darker beginning in February 2016, with filming in Vancouver and in France, including Nice and Paris. The soundtrack featured the single "For You" by Liam Payne and Rita Ora.

Reception

Released by Universal Pictures on February 9, 2018, Fifty Shades Freed grossed $371 million worldwide against a $55 million budget, the lowest of the trilogy but still a major commercial success. Like its predecessors it received negative reviews from critics, who criticised its script and performances, though it completed a franchise that grossed over $1.3 billion in total.
