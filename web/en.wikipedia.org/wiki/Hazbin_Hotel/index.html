Hazbin Hotel

Hazbin Hotel is an American adult animated musical black comedy television series created by Vivienne "VivziePop" Medrano. Set in Hell, it follows Charlie Morningstar, the princess of Hell, who opens a hotel aimed at rehabilitating sinners so they can earn entry into Heaven and reduce Hell's overpopulation without the annual exterminations carried out by angels.

Production and release

A pilot episode was released on YouTube in October 2019 and attracted millions of views; the independent studio A24 subsequently picked up the series. The first season premiered on Amazon Prime Video in January 2024, with a cast including Erika Henningsen as Charlie, Stephanie Beatriz as Vaggie, Alex Brightman as Sir Pentious, and Blake Roman as Angel Dust. A spin-off series, Helluva Boss, is set in the same universe.

Reception

The series was praised for its animation, music, and voice performances, and became one of Prime Video's most-watched animated premieres; further seasons were ordered.
