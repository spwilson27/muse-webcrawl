Mission impossible

Mission impossible is an idiomatic phrase describing a task that is extremely difficult or apparently impossible to accomplish. Its widespread use derives from the American television series Mission: Impossible (1966–1973), created by Bruce Geller, and the blockbuster film franchise of the same name starring Tom Cruise that began in 1996.

Usage

The phrase is used in journalism, business, and everyday speech for daunting undertakings ("a mission impossible"), and each instalment of the franchise opens with the formula "Your mission, should you choose to accept it..." followed by a self-destructing message — lines that have entered popular culture.

The franchise

The TV series followed the Impossible Missions Force (IMF), a team of secret agents using elaborate deception and technology. The film series, one of the highest-grossing in history, features Ethan Hunt and large-scale practical stunt sequences.
