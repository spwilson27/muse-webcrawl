Richard John "Dick" Grayson is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Batman. Created by writer Bill Finger and artist Bob Kane, with contributions by Jerry Robinson, he first appeared in Detective Comics #38 in April 1940 as the original and most popular incarnation of Robin, Batman's crime-fighting partner and sidekick.

Fictional biography

Dick Grayson was the youngest member of the Flying Graysons, a family of circus trapeze acrobats. After gangster Tony Zucco murdered his parents by sabotaging their act, Bruce Wayne took the orphaned boy in as his ward, and Dick became Robin, the Boy Wonder. Over time he co-founded and led the Teen Titans. As an adult, seeking independence from Batman, he relinquished the Robin identity (which passed to Jason Todd) and became Nightwing, a name drawn from Kryptonian legend, eventually settling as the protector of the city of Blüdhaven.

Other identities and reception

Grayson has twice taken up the mantle of Batman himself — briefly in the 1990s and again after Bruce Wayne's apparent death in 2009 — and later operated as the undercover "Agent 37" of the Spyral organisation. Consistently among DC's most popular characters, he is noted for his acrobatics, leadership of the Titans, escrima-weapon fighting style, and his role as the emotional heart of the Batman family.
