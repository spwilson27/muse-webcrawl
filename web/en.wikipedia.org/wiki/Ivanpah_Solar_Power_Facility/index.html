Ivanpah Solar Power Facility

The Ivanpah Solar Electric Generating System is a concentrated solar thermal power plant in California's Mojave Desert, near the Nevada border. With a gross capacity of 392 megawatts, it was the world's largest solar thermal plant when it opened in 2014.

LocationSan Bernardino County, California, US
Opened13 February 2014
Capacity392 MW (gross)
TechnologySolar power tower; 173,500 heliostats
OwnersNRG Energy, BrightSource, Google

Design

Three boiler towers, each about 140 metres tall, receive sunlight reflected by more than 173,000 computer-controlled heliostat mirrors spread over roughly 3,500 acres. The concentrated heat produces steam that drives conventional turbines.

Issues and future

The plant was criticised for underperforming output targets, for the deaths of birds in the solar flux, and for its use of natural gas to warm up each morning. In 2025 the owners announced plans to retire two of the three units ahead of schedule as utility contracts end.
