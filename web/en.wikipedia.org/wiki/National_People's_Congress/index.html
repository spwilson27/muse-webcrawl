National People's Congress

National People's Congress (NPC)
Chinese: 全国人民代表大会
Type: Unicameral national legislature
Seats: ~2,980 deputies
Meeting place: Great Hall of the People, Beijing

The National People's Congress (NPC) is the national legislature of the People's Republic of China. With just under 3,000 deputies, it is the largest parliamentary body in the world. Under the Chinese constitution it is the highest organ of state power.

Structure and sessions

Deputies are elected for five-year terms by provincial-level people's congresses, the armed forces and other constituencies. The full NPC meets in session for about two weeks each spring in the Great Hall of the People in Beijing. When it is not in session, its permanent body, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (about 175 members), exercises legislative functions and meets every two months.

Powers

The NPC elects the President and Vice President of China, decides on the Premier of the State Council upon the President's nomination, elects the chair of the Central Military Commission and the heads of the supervisory, judicial and procuratorial organs, and approves the state budget and national economic plans. It can amend the constitution by a two-thirds majority. In practice, the NPC operates under the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party, and its annual session coincides with the national session of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference — together known as the "Two Sessions".
