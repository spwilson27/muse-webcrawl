Daniel Georg Ek (born 21 February 1983) is a Swedish billionaire entrepreneur and technologist. He is the co-founder and chief executive officer of the music streaming service Spotify.

Early life and career

Ek grew up in the Rågsved district of Stockholm. A self-taught programmer, he started his first business at age 13 building websites for local companies. He later founded the advertising company Advertigo, sold to Tradedoubler in 2006, and served as CEO of the file-sharing company µTorrent and as chief technology officer of the browser-based game company Stardoll.

Spotify

In 2006 Ek co-founded Spotify with Martin Lorentzon, developing a legal streaming service as an alternative to music piracy. Spotify launched in Europe in 2008 and in the United States in 2011, growing under Ek's leadership into the world's largest music streaming platform, with hundreds of millions of users. Ek took the company public on the New York Stock Exchange in April 2018 through an unusual direct listing rather than a conventional initial public offering.

Other ventures

Ek co-founded the preventative-health startup Neko Health, which launched in 2023 offering full-body scans. Through his investment vehicle Prima Materia, he has committed substantial funding to European technology ventures, including defence technology company Helsing, in which he has taken a leading role.
