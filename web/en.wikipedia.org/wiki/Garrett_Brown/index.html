Garrett Brown

Garrett Brown (born 6 April 1942) is an American inventor and cinematographer best known as the creator of the Steadicam, the camera stabiliser that allows smooth moving shots while the operator walks or runs. His invention transformed film and television production.


Born: Long Branch, New Jersey, USA
Known for: Inventing the Steadicam
Awards: Academy Award for technical achievement

Steadicam

Brown developed the stabiliser in the early 1970s; it debuted to acclaim in Bound for Glory (1976), Rocky (1976) — including the famous museum steps run — and The Shining (1980), where Brown operated the system through the hotel corridors and hedge maze.

Other inventions

Brown also created the Skycam for aerial cable-suspended coverage of sports, the Divecam and MobyCam for swimming events, and other specialised camera systems. He received an Academy Award and an Emmy-related honours for his contributions, and has worked as a Steadicam operator on dozens of major films.
