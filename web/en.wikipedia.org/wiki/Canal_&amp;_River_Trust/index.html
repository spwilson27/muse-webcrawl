Canal & River Trust

The Canal & River Trust is a charitable trust in England and Wales responsible for about 2,000 miles (3,200 km) of canals, rivers, reservoirs and docks. It was founded on 2 July 2012, taking over the functions of British Waterways.

Responsibilities

The Trust maintains locks, bridges, aqueducts, towpaths and reservoirs across the network — including famous structures such as the Pontcysyllte Aqueduct — funded by government grant, boat licences, property income and donations.

Governance

As one of the UK's largest charities by assets transferred from the public sector, it is governed by trustees and a council including boaters and users. In Scotland, canals remained with Scottish Canals.

Activities

Beyond navigation, the Trust promotes towpaths for walking and cycling, volunteering, wildlife conservation and wellbeing under slogans such as 'Life by Water' / 'By Water'. See Canals of the United Kingdom.
