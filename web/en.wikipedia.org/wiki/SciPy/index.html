SciPy is a free and open-source Python library used for scientific computing and technical computing. Built on top of NumPy, it provides modules for optimization, linear algebra, integration, interpolation, special functions, FFT, signal and image processing, ODE solvers, and other tasks common in science and engineering.

Structure

SciPy is organized into subpackages including scipy.optimize (minimization and root finding), scipy.integrate (quadrature and differential equations), scipy.linalg (wrapping BLAS/LAPACK), scipy.sparse (sparse matrices), scipy.stats (statistics), scipy.signal, scipy.fft, and scipy.spatial (KD-trees, Delaunay triangulation).

History

The project grew out of work by Travis Oliphant, Pearu Peterson, Eric Jones and others in the early 2000s, consolidating code such as wrappers of the Fortran MINPACK and QUADPACK libraries. The landmark 1.0 release arrived in 2017; a 2020 Nature Methods paper documented the library's architecture and community governance. SciPy is also the name of an annual conference on scientific Python.
