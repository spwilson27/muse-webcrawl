Ash Ketchum

Ash Ketchum
Japanese name: Satoshi
Franchise: Pokémon
First appearance: "Pokémon, I Choose You!" (1997)
Partner: Pikachu

Ash Ketchum, known as Satoshi in Japan, is a fictional character in the Pokémon franchise and the protagonist of the Pokémon animated television series from its debut in 1997 until 2023. A boy from Pallet Town in the Kanto region, Ash travels the world with his partner Pikachu, catching and training Pokémon in pursuit of his dream of becoming a Pokémon Master.

Character

Ash is characterised by determination, optimism, and a deep bond with his Pokémon. Voiced in Japanese by Rica Matsumoto and in English initially by Veronica Taylor and later Sarah Natochenny, he remained ten years old throughout most of the series, an agelessness that became a noted feature of the show.

Journey and achievements

Over more than 1,200 episodes Ash competed in the leagues of many regions, typically falling short in tournament climaxes. He won the Orange League, conquered the Battle Frontier, and in 2019 won the Alola League, his first official regional championship. In Pokémon Ultimate Journeys (2022) he defeated Leon to win the World Coronation Series and become world champion — a culmination widely reported in real-world news.

Departure

In 2023 Ash's journey concluded with the miniseries Pokémon: To Be a Pokémon Master, after which the anime continued with new protagonists Liko and Roy in Pokémon Horizons.
