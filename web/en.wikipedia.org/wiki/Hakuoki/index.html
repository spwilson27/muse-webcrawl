Hakuoki (薄桜鬼, Hakuōki, lit. "Demon of the Fleeting Blossom") is a Japanese otome visual novel series developed by Idea Factory under its Otomate label. First released for the PlayStation 2 in 2008, the series is set in the final years of the Tokugawa shogunate and follows the Shinsengumi, the shogunate's special police force, blending historical fiction with supernatural elements.

Plot

The protagonist, Chizuru Yukimura, travels to Kyoto in search of her missing father, a doctor who had been working for the Shinsengumi. After witnessing a clash between Shinsengumi swordsmen and inhuman, blood-crazed creatures known as Furies (rasetsu), she is taken into the custody of the Shinsengumi. As she lives alongside its captains — including Toshizo Hijikata, Souji Okita, Hajime Saito, and Sanosuke Harada — she becomes entangled in the political upheaval of the Bakumatsu period and the secret of the "Water of Life" that created the Furies.

Releases and adaptations

The original game has been ported and expanded many times, including Hakuoki: Kyoto Winds and Hakuoki: Edo Blossoms, which together retell the story with additional routes. The franchise has spawned anime television series (beginning in 2010), original video animations, feature films, manga adaptations, stage plays, and musicals. English localizations were published by Aksys Games and Idea Factory International.

Reception

Hakuoki is credited with helping popularize otome games in Western markets, where its PSP release in 2012 was among the first commercially localized otome titles. It remains one of the best-known franchises in the genre.
