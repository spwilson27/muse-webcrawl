Batman is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The character was created by artist Bob Kane and writer Bill Finger, and first appeared in Detective Comics #27 in 1939. Originally named "the Bat-Man", the character is also referred to by epithets such as the Caped Crusader, the Dark Knight, and the World's Greatest Detective.

Batman's secret identity is Bruce Wayne, a wealthy American playboy, philanthropist, and industrialist who resides in Gotham City. His origin story features him swearing vengeance against criminals after witnessing the murder of his parents, Thomas and Martha Wayne, as a child. He trains himself physically and intellectually, crafts a bat-inspired persona, and monitors the Gotham streets by night from the Batcave beneath Wayne Manor.

Publication history

Batman was created in early 1939 in response to the success of Superman in Action Comics. Finger contributed most of the character's distinctive elements, including the cowl, cape, gloves, and the dark tone of the stories, while Kane received sole official credit for decades; DC began crediting Finger as co-creator in 2015. Batman soon became popular enough to star in his own self-titled ongoing comic book series in 1940.

During the 1960s the character was softened under the Comics Code and popularised by the campy 1966 television series starring Adam West. Writers such as Dennis O'Neil and Neal Adams in the 1970s returned Batman to his darker roots, and Frank Miller's The Dark Knight Returns (1986) and Batman: Year One (1987) redefined the character for the modern era.

Characterisation

Unlike most superheroes, Batman possesses no superpowers; he relies on his intellect, detective skills, martial-arts training, and an arsenal of specialised equipment, including the Batmobile and the utility belt. He operates alongside allies such as his butler Alfred Pennyworth, Police Commissioner James Gordon, and a succession of sidekicks named Robin, beginning with Dick Grayson. His rogues' gallery includes the Joker, the Penguin, the Riddler, Two-Face, and Catwoman.

In other media

Batman has been portrayed in numerous films, including Tim Burton's Batman (1989) with Michael Keaton, Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy (2005–2012) with Christian Bale, and The Batman (2022) with Robert Pattinson. Kevin Conroy's voice performance in Batman: The Animated Series (1992–1995) is widely acclaimed. The character is one of the most commercially successful superheroes in history and a central figure of the DC Universe.
