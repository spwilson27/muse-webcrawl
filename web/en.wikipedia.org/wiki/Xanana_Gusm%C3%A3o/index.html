Kay Rala Xanana Gusmão (born José Alexandre Gusmão, 20 June 1946) is an East Timorese politician who has served as Prime Minister of Timor-Leste since 2023, a position he previously held from 2007 to 2015. He was the first President of East Timor, serving from independence in 2002 to 2007, and was the leader of the FRETILIN resistance forces (FALINTIL) during the Indonesian occupation.

Resistance and imprisonment

After Indonesia invaded East Timor in 1975, Gusmão became a guerrilla commander and reorganised the armed resistance in the 1980s, adopting the nom de guerre Xanana. He was captured by Indonesian forces in 1992 and sentenced to life imprisonment (later commuted), becoming an international symbol of the East Timorese cause — sometimes compared to Nelson Mandela — while continuing to direct the resistance from prison in Jakarta.

Independent Timor-Leste

Released in 1999 amid the territory's vote for independence, Gusmão won the 2002 presidential election overwhelmingly. He later founded the CNRT party, became prime minister in 2007, oversaw the withdrawal of international forces and petroleum-funded development, and returned to the premiership in 2023 after his party's election victory.
