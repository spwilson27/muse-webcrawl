US Dax

Union Sportive Dacquoise, commonly known as US Dax, is a French rugby union club based in Dax, in the Landes department of Nouvelle-Aquitaine. Founded in 1904, the club plays in red and white and has spent much of its history in the upper divisions of French rugby.

History

US Dax reached the final of the French championship five times between 1956 and 1973 but never won the title, earning a reputation as one of the strongest clubs never to be crowned champion. The club won the Challenge Yves du Manoir several times. Players such as Pierre Albaladejo became synonymous with Dax rugby.

Stadium and recent years

The club plays at the Stade Maurice Boyau. In the professional era Dax has moved between the Top 14, Pro D2 and Nationale divisions, winning promotion back to Pro D2 in 2023.
