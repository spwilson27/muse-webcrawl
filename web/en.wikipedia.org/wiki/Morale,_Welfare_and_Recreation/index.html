Morale, Welfare and Recreation

AbbreviationMWR
PurposeQuality-of-life services for U.S. military personnel and families
Parent organisationsU.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard

Morale, Welfare and Recreation (MWR) is a network of support and leisure services provided by the United States military services for soldiers, sailors, airmen, marines, coast guardsmen, their families, and other eligible users. Each branch runs its own MWR organisation, such as the Army's Family and MWR Programs and Navy MWR.

Services

MWR facilities include fitness centres, libraries, golf courses, bowling alleys, outdoor recreation and equipment rental, childcare and youth programmes, auto hobby shops, travel offices, and clubs. Major resorts such as Shades of Green at Walt Disney World, the Hale Koa Hotel in Hawaii, the Edelweiss Lodge in Germany, and the Dragon Hill Lodge in Korea are operated for service members.

Funding

MWR activities are funded through a mix of congressional appropriations and non-appropriated funds generated by the programmes' own revenue, including exchanges and user fees. The services are intended to sustain readiness, retention, and quality of life, particularly for personnel stationed far from home or deployed.
