Jor-El

Jor-El is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, created by Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster. He is the biological father of Superman (Kal-El) and a leading scientist on the planet Krypton.

Foreseeing Krypton's destruction and unable to persuade its governing council, Jor-El and his wife Lara sent their infant son to Earth in a small spacecraft. In later retellings he survives as an artificial intelligence in the Fortress of Solitude, guiding his son. The character has been portrayed on screen by Marlon Brando in Superman (1978) and by Russell Crowe in Man of Steel (2013).
