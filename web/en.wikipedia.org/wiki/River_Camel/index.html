River Camel

The River Camel (Cornish: Dowr Kammel) is a river in Cornwall, England, rising on the edge of Bodmin Moor and flowing about 48 km (30 mi) to the Celtic Sea at Stepper Point near Padstow.

Course

The Camel passes Camelford and Wadebridge before widening into the Camel Estuary between Rock and Padstow, noted for the Doom Bar sandbank at its mouth. Its tributaries include the River Allen and the River Ruthern.

Recreation and wildlife

The Camel Trail, a popular cycling and walking route along a disused railway, follows the river from Wenfordbridge to Padstow. The river supports salmon and sea trout fisheries and otter populations, and much of its valley is protected for its wildlife.
