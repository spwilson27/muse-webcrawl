Recital

A recital is a musical event in which a single performer — commonly a singer or instrumentalist — or a small group performs, usually with piano accompaniment for vocal and instrumental soloists. The term may also refer to a dance performance by students or a soloist.

History

The solo recital emerged in the 19th century; Franz Liszt is credited with popularizing the solo piano recital (and the word "recital" for such concerts) in London in 1840. Song recitals of German Lieder became a staple of concert life.

Formats

Recitals range from student performances, a rite of passage in music education, to professional debut and farewell recitals in major halls. Programmes typically group works by period or theme, often with printed programme notes.
