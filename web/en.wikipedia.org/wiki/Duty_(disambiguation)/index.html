Duty (disambiguation)

Duty most commonly refers to a moral or legal obligation. It may also refer to:

Common uses

  • Duty, a concept in ethics and law describing an obligation to perform an act or refrain from acting.
  • Customs duty, a tax levied on goods transported across international borders; see tariff.
  • Stamp duty, a tax on legal documents and certain transactions.
  • Duty in engineering contexts, such as the duty cycle of a periodic signal.

Other uses

  • Duty (1918 film) and other works of film and television titled Duty.
  • Duty, in the sense of a shift or tour of service, as in "on duty" for police, military, and medical personnel.
  • Jury duty, the obligation of citizens to serve on a jury.
