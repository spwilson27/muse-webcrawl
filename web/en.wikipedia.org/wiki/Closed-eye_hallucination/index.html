Closed-eye hallucination

Closed-eye hallucinations and closed-eye visualizations (CEV) are hallucinations that occur when one's eyes are closed or when one is in a darkened room. They can be a form of phosphene. Some people report CEV under the influence of psychedelics; these are reportedly of a different nature than the "open-eye" hallucinations of the same compounds.

Levels

Five levels of CEV perception are commonly described:

  • Level 1: Visual noise — random, formless specks of light and dark, similar to the noise of the visual system described by the eigengrau.
  • Level 2: Light/dark flashes — flashes of light, moiré patterns, and blobs of colour.
  • Level 3: Patterns, motion, and colour — distinct, often fractal-like geometrical patterns.
  • Level 4: Objects and things — recognizable objects and scenes appear.
  • Level 5: Overriding physical perception — the imagery replaces external perception, as in some dissociative or deliriant states.
