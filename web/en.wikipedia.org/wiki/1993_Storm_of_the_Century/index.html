1993 Storm of the Century

The 1993 Storm of the Century, also known as the Great Blizzard of 1993, was a large cyclonic storm that formed over the Gulf of Mexico on 12 March 1993 and affected eastern North America through 15 March.

Impact

The storm produced blizzard conditions from Canada to the Gulf Coast, hurricane-force wind gusts, record low pressures and snowfall as far south as Alabama and Georgia; snow fell even in the Florida Panhandle. Approximately 318 people died, and damage was estimated in the billions of dollars. Every major airport on the U.S. East Coast was closed at some point.

Meteorology

The storm was remarkable for the accuracy with which numerical weather models forecast it days in advance, a milestone in operational meteorology.
