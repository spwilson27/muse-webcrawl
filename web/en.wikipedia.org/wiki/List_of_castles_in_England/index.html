List of castles in England

This is a list of castles in England. "Castle" is a difficult term to define precisely; the list includes fortified residences built from the Norman Conquest of 1066 through to sham castles of the 19th century, but it generally excludes unfortified country houses and fortifications built solely for military defence in later centuries. England has well over 1,500 castle sites recorded, though many survive only as earthworks or fragmentary ruins.

Notable castles by county

Northumberland and the North

  • Alnwick Castle — seat of the Duke of Northumberland
  • Bamburgh Castle — coastal fortress on a basalt outcrop
  • Durham Castle — Norman castle, now part of Durham University
  • Dunstanburgh Castle — dramatic coastal ruin
  • Carlisle Castle — border fortress against Scotland
  • Richmond Castle — one of the earliest Norman stone castles
  • Middleham Castle — childhood home of Richard III
  • Skipton Castle — remarkably well preserved

The South and London

  • Tower of London — founded 1066, home of the Crown Jewels
  • Windsor Castle — the world's oldest and largest inhabited castle
  • Dover Castle — "the key to England"
  • Leeds Castle (Kent) — set on islands in a lake
  • Arundel Castle — seat of the Duke of Norfolk
  • Bodiam Castle — classic moated quadrangular castle
  • Hever Castle — childhood home of Anne Boleyn
  • Corfe Castle — ruined stronghold in Dorset

The Midlands and West

  • Warwick Castle — developed from a wooden fort of 1068
  • Kenilworth Castle — scene of Elizabethan pageantry
  • Ludlow Castle — seat of the Council of Wales and the Marches
  • Stokesay Castle — fortified manor house
  • Goodrich Castle — red sandstone ruin on the Wye
  • Lincoln Castle — houses a copy of Magna Carta
  • Tintagel Castle — associated with the legend of King Arthur
  • Pendennis and St Mawes — paired Henrician coastal forts
