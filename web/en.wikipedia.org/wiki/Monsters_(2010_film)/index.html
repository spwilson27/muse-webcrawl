Monsters is a 2010 British science fiction film written and directed by Gareth Edwards, in his feature film debut. It stars Whitney Able and Scoot McNairy as two travellers journeying through an "infected zone" in Mexico where giant extraterrestrial creatures roam, six years after a NASA probe carrying samples of alien life crashed and the creatures spread across the region, prompting the United States to build an enormous wall along its border.

Production

The film was made on a very small budget with a tiny crew, shot on location in Mexico, Belize, Guatemala, Costa Rica and the United States using non-professional actors encountered along the way. Edwards created the visual effects himself on consumer software, completing some 250 effects shots. Much of the dialogue was improvised from an outline.

Reception

Monsters premiered at the South by Southwest festival in 2010 and received generally positive reviews, with praise for its atmosphere, character focus and Edwards' achievement on limited resources. It won several British Independent Film Awards. The film's success led to Edwards directing Godzilla (2014), and a sequel, Monsters: Dark Continent, followed in 2014.
