Iberia, legally incorporated as Iberia Líneas Aéreas de España, S.A. Operadora, is the flag carrier of Spain. Founded in 1927, it is based in Madrid and operates an international network of services from its main base at Madrid–Barajas Airport. Iberia is Spain's largest airline and a founding member of the Oneworld alliance.

In January 2011, Iberia merged with British Airways to create the International Airlines Group (IAG), one of the world's largest airline groups. Iberia operates alongside sister carriers including Aer Lingus and Vueling; its regional services are flown by Iberia Regional (Air Nostrum), and Iberia Express operates short- and medium-haul routes.

Network and fleet

Iberia is particularly strong in markets between Europe and Latin America, operating long-haul services to destinations across the Americas as well as domestic and European routes. Its long-haul fleet centres on the Airbus A330 and A350, with A320-family aircraft on shorter routes.

History

Iberia was founded on 28 June 1927 and began operations that December with flights between Madrid and Barcelona. It was nationalised in 1944, privatised around the turn of the century, and joined Oneworld in 1999. The airline's frequent-flyer programme is Iberia Plus, whose currency (Avios) is shared across IAG carriers.
