Cheng Dawei

Cheng Dawei (1533–1606), courtesy name Ruisi, was a Chinese mathematician and writer on the abacus of the Ming dynasty. He is best known as the author of the Suanfa Tongzong ("General Source of Computational Methods"), published in 1592, one of the most influential Chinese mathematical works of its era.

Suanfa Tongzong

The Suanfa Tongzong is a comprehensive treatise of seventeen chapters covering arithmetic and abacus computation, with hundreds of problems. It helped spread the use of the suanpan (Chinese abacus) and remained a standard reference for centuries; editions and adaptations circulated widely in China and influenced mathematics in Japan and Korea.

Legacy

A museum dedicated to Cheng Dawei and the abacus stands in his home region of Anhui province, and his work is commemorated in the history of computing in China.
