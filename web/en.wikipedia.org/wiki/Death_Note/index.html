Death Note is a Japanese manga series written by Tsugumi Ohba and illustrated by Takeshi Obata. It follows Light Yagami, a brilliant high-school student who finds a supernatural notebook — the Death Note — dropped by the shinigami Ryuk: anyone whose name is written in it while the writer pictures their face will die.

Plot

Using the notebook to kill criminals, Light adopts the alias "Kira" and sets out to create a crime-free world ruled by himself. A police task force led by his father and the eccentric detective L attempts to identify and capture him, producing an extended battle of deduction between Light and L and, later, L's successors Near and Mello.

Media and legacy

The manga was serialised in Weekly Shōnen Jump from 2003 to 2006 and collected in twelve volumes. It was adapted into a 37-episode anime series by Madhouse (2006–07), several live-action Japanese films, a television drama, a musical and an American Netflix film in 2017. The series is one of the best-selling manga of all time and is frequently ranked among the greatest anime series.
