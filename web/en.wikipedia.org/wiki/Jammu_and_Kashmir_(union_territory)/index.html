Jammu and Kashmir (union territory)

Capitals: Srinagar (summer), Jammu (winter)

Region: Kashmir and Jammu divisions

Established: 31 October 2019

Status: Union territory of India

Jammu and Kashmir is a region administered by India as a union territory. It was created on 31 October 2019 following the reorganization of the former state of Jammu and Kashmir under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, after the special status granted by Article 370 of the Indian Constitution was abrogated in August 2019.

Geography

The territory comprises the Kashmir Valley, Jammu region, and surrounding mountain ranges of the western Himalayas. The region is known for its lakes, including Dal Lake, its Himalayan valleys, and religious sites such as the Vaishno Devi temple and the Amarnath cave shrine.

History

The region has a long and complex history, from ancient Hindu and Buddhist kingdoms through Mughal, Afghan, Sikh, and Dogra rule. Following the partition of British India in 1947, the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir acceded to India, leading to a territorial dispute with Pakistan that continues to this day.

Government

As a union territory with a legislature, Jammu and Kashmir is governed by a Lieutenant Governor appointed by the President of India and an elected Legislative Assembly. Elections to the Assembly were held in 2024, and a government was formed with a Chief Minister.
