Rehearsal

A rehearsal is an activity in the performing arts that occurs as preparation for a performance in music, theatre, dance, and related arts, and also in training contexts generally. The term refers to practice sessions in which performers refine and coordinate their work before presenting it to an audience.

Types

In theatre, rehearsals typically progress from read-throughs and blocking rehearsals to technical rehearsals, in which lighting, sound, and scenery are integrated, and dress rehearsals, held in full costume shortly before opening. In music, ensembles rehearse under a conductor or leader; a sitzprobe in opera brings singers and orchestra together for the first time. Rehearsal may also refer to the mental practice of information in psychology — maintenance rehearsal in the working-memory model of Atkinson and Shiffrin describes repeating information to keep it in short-term memory.
