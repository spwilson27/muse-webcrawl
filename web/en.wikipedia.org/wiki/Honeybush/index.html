Honeybush

Honeybush is a herbal tea made from the leaves and stems of Cyclopia species, shrubs of the fynbos of South Africa's Western and Eastern Cape. Named for the honey-scented yellow flowers, it is naturally sweet, caffeine-free and low in tannins.

Production

Harvested plants (notably Cyclopia intermedia and C. subternata) are cut, fermented (oxidised) to develop flavour and colour, then dried and sieved. Like its relative rooibos, honeybush is exported as a health tea rich in antioxidants such as mangiferin.

Use

It is drunk hot or iced, plain or with milk, and used in blends and cosmetics.
