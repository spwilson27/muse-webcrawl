Firestorm (character)

Firestorm is the name of several superheroes appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. The original Firestorm was created by writer Gerry Conway and artist Al Milgrom and first appeared in Firestorm #1 (March 1978). The character is distinctive for being a composite hero: high-school student Ronnie Raymond and Nobel Prize-winning physicist Martin Stein, fused together into a single nuclear-powered being by a reactor accident, with Stein existing as a disembodied voice of guidance inside Raymond's mind.


Firestorm, the Nuclear Man — a composite of Ronnie Raymond and Professor Martin Stein.

Publication history

The first Firestorm series (1978) was cancelled after five issues during the "DC Implosion". The character was revived in 1982 with The Fury of Firestorm, initially written by Conway, which ran for 100 issues (through 1990, retitled Firestorm the Nuclear Man). Later series followed in 2004 (featuring Jason Rusch), 2011 as part of The New 52 (The Fury of Firestorm: The Nuclear Men), and subsequent appearances in team books and crossover events.

Fictional character biography

Student Ronnie Raymond and physicist Martin Stein were caught in a nuclear reactor accident (in some versions, sabotage by a terrorist group) that fused them into the Firestorm matrix. Raymond controlled the body while Stein, unconscious of events in physical form, advised him as a voice in his head. Later configurations included Russian scientist Mikhail Arkadin (in the Soviet-era Firestorm of the late 1980s storyline), and Jason Rusch, a teenager from Detroit who inherited the Firestorm matrix and merged with various partners, including (during Brightest Day) a revived Ronnie Raymond. Firestorm has been a member of the Justice League.

Powers and abilities

Firestorm's signature power is molecular reconstruction: he can rearrange the atomic and subatomic structure of inorganic matter to create objects or transmute elements — for example turning air into cushioning material or reshaping metal. He can fly, project nuclear energy blasts, phase through solid objects (at some risk), and absorb radiation. His chief limitation is that in most versions he cannot affect organic matter, and the fusion is unstable if the two components disagree or are separated for too long; in some versions prolonged fusion is fatal to one partner.

In other media

Firestorm appears in animated series including Super Friends, Justice League Unlimited, and Batman: The Brave and the Bold. In live action, the Ronnie Raymond / Martin Stein version appeared in The CW's The Flash (played by Robbie Amell and Victor Garber), and the Jefferson Jackson / Martin Stein version became a regular on Legends of Tomorrow.
