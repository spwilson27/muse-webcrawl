Darrell K Royal–Texas Memorial Stadium (DKR), located in Austin, Texas, is the home field of the Texas Longhorns football team of the University of Texas at Austin. With an official capacity of 100,119, it is one of the largest stadiums in the United States and the largest in the Southeastern Conference.

History

The stadium opened in 1924 as War Memorial Stadium, dedicated to Texans who died in the First World War, with an original capacity of about 27,000. Successive expansions over the following century enclosed the horseshoe, added upper decks and filled in the south end zone, completed in 2021. In 1996 the stadium was renamed for Darrell K Royal, the Longhorns' legendary head coach from 1957 to 1976, who won three national championships.

Records

The record attendance is 105,213, set on 15 October 2022 for a game against Alabama. The stadium has also hosted concerts, high-school championship games and other events, and is famous for its game-day atmosphere and the "Hook 'em Horns" tradition.
