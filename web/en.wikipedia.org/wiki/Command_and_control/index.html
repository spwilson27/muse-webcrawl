Command and control

Command and control
Short formC2
DomainMilitary science, emergency management

Command and control (C2) is a "set of organizational and technical attributes and processes ... [that] employs human, physical, and information resources to solve problems and accomplish missions" — in military usage, the exercise of authority and direction by a properly designated commander over assigned forces in the accomplishment of a mission.

Concept

Command involves authority and decision-making; control involves monitoring, feedback, and the direction of subordinate forces. Modern doctrines emphasise mission command — conveying intent and allowing subordinates initiative — in place of detailed central direction.

Systems

C2 systems integrate sensors, communications, and decision aids to build situational awareness; extended acronyms such as C4ISR add computers, communications, intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance. In computing security, "command and control" also denotes the infrastructure used by malware operators to direct compromised machines.
