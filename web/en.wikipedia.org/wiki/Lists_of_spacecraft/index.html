Categories

Spacecraft are also listed by operator (NASA, ESA, Roscosmos, CNSA and commercial operators such as SpaceX), by status (active, retired, cancelled), and by record — such as the list of heaviest spacecraft and lists of objects that have left the Solar System's vicinity (Voyager 1 and 2, Pioneer 10 and 11, New Horizons).
