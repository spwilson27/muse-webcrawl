Sir Lucian Charles Grainge CBE (born 29 February 1960) is a British record executive who has served as chairman and chief executive officer of Universal Music Group (UMG) since 2011. Over a career in the music industry spanning more than four decades, Grainge has worked with artists including ABBA, Jay-Z, Elton John, Metallica, Queen, Rihanna, The Rolling Stones, Sam Smith, U2, and Amy Winehouse. He has frequently been described as one of the most powerful figures in the global music business, and has topped Billboard's Power 100 list several times.

Early life and career

Grainge was born in London in 1960. He left school at 18 and began his career in 1978 as a runner at a talent agency, before moving into music publishing. He joined CBS Records' publishing division as a talent scout, and later worked at RCA Records, MCA Records, and PolyGram, rising through A&R and label management roles.

In 1986 he founded PolyGram Music Publishing in the United Kingdom. He subsequently became managing director of Polydor Records in 1997, overseeing a period of commercial success for the label.

Universal Music Group

Following the merger of PolyGram with Universal in 1998, Grainge held senior roles within UMG, becoming chairman and chief executive of Universal Music Group International in 2005. In that role he expanded the group's operations in emerging markets and led its international recorded music business.

Grainge succeeded Doug Morris as chief executive officer of Universal Music Group on 1 January 2011, and became chairman as well in March 2011. Under his leadership, UMG acquired EMI's recorded music business in 2012 (with certain assets, including Parlophone, divested to Warner Music Group), and the company expanded into film and television production and artist services. Grainge was a prominent advocate within the industry for subscription streaming as the successor to download sales, licensing UMG's catalogue to services including Spotify and Apple Music.

In September 2021, UMG was listed on the Euronext Amsterdam stock exchange, with an initial valuation of around €46 billion. Grainge's compensation packages, including bonuses tied to the listing, attracted significant press attention.

Personal life and honours

Grainge was appointed Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in the 2010 Birthday Honours for services to the creative industries, and was knighted in the 2016 Birthday Honours. In 2020 he was hospitalised with COVID-19 and recovered after a serious illness. His son, Elliot Grainge, founded the record label 10K Projects and became chief executive of Atlantic Records in 2024.
