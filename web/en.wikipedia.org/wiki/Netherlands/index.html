Netherlands

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Amsterdam canal houses and a bridge over a canal with bicycles parked along the railings]

The Netherlands is a country in Northwestern Europe, informally also called Holland, known for its flat landscape of canals, tulip fields, windmills and cycling routes. Its capital is Amsterdam, while the government sits in The Hague. Much of the country lies at or below sea level, protected by an elaborate system of dikes and polders. The Netherlands has about 17–18 million inhabitants and one of the world's most open, trade-oriented economies (Rotterdam is Europe's largest port).

Contents1. History

History

The Dutch Republic became a maritime and commercial great power in the 17th-century Dutch Golden Age, founding a global trading empire. The modern kingdom emerged after the Napoleonic era; the Netherlands was occupied by Germany in World War II and afterwards was a founding member of NATO, the Benelux and the European Community.
