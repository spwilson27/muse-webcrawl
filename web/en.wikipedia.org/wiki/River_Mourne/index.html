River Mourne

The River Mourne (Irish: An Múrn) is a river in County Tyrone, Northern Ireland. It is formed by the confluence of the River Derg and the Strule at Newtownstewart and flows north-west to Strabane, where it joins the River Finn to form the River Foyle.

Fishing

Although only about 11 km (7 mi) long, the Mourne is renowned as one of Ireland's finest salmon and sea trout rivers, with fishing centred on the Sion Mills area, where a former linen mill village and weir are notable landmarks.
