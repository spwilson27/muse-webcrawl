Kingdom of Khotan

CapitalKhotan (modern Hotan, Xinjiang)
Existedc. 1st century BCE – 1006 CE
LanguageKhotanese (Saka)
ReligionBuddhism

The Kingdom of Khotan was an ancient Iranian Saka Buddhist kingdom located on the southern branch of the Silk Road around the oasis of Khotan, in present-day Xinjiang, China. It flourished for roughly a thousand years as a centre of trade, jade production and Mahayana Buddhism.

History

Khotan was famous for its white jade, silk and carpets. The Chinese pilgrim Xuanzang visited in the 7th century and described its Buddhist monasteries. The kingdom fell to the Muslim Kara-Khanid Khanate around 1006, after which Islam gradually replaced Buddhism in the region.
