Lists of National Historic Sites of Canada

National Historic Sites
Total sitesNearly 1,000
Designated byMinister of Environment, on advice of the Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada
AdministeredParks Canada (about 170 sites)

National Historic Sites of Canada are places designated by the federal government as being of national historic significance. There are nearly 1,000 National Historic Sites, organized in lists by province and territory and by city for the largest urban collections. Designations are made by the Minister of the Environment on the recommendation of the Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada; Parks Canada administers about 170 of the sites directly, while the remainder are owned by other governments, organizations and private owners.

Lists by city

Separate lists exist for cities with large concentrations of sites, including Montreal, Quebec City, Ottawa, Toronto, Kingston, Halifax, Winnipeg, Vancouver and Victoria.
