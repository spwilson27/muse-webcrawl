Royal Canadian Air Force

The Royal Canadian Air Force (RCAF; French: Aviation royale canadienne) is the air and space force of Canada, responsible for all Canadian Armed Forces aircraft operations, the security of Canadian airspace, and support to the Royal Canadian Navy and Canadian Army. Founded on 1 April 1924, it became one of the largest Allied air forces in the Second World War; it was amalgamated into the unified Canadian Armed Forces in 1968 as Air Command and regained the Royal Canadian Air Force name in 2011. Its motto is Sic itur ad astra ("Such is the pathway to the stars").

History

Canada's first military aviation dated from the short-lived Canadian Aviation Corps of 1914. The post-1918 Canadian Air Force was made permanent as the RCAF in 1924. During the Second World War the RCAF grew to over 215,000 personnel, operated the British Commonwealth Air Training Plan — which trained more than 130,000 Allied aircrew at bases across Canada — and fielded squadrons in the Battle of Britain, the bombing offensive over Europe, the Battle of the Atlantic and the campaigns in the Mediterranean and Southeast Asia, becoming the fourth-largest Allied air force by 1945.

In the Cold War the RCAF contributed fighter forces to NATO in Europe, operated the Canadair-built Sabres, CF-100 Canucks, CF-101 Voodoos and CF-104 Starfighters, and participated in NORAD air defence of North America. Unification in 1968 dissolved the RCAF as a separate service; its traditions were carried on by Air Command. The force flew combat operations in the 1991 Gulf War, Kosovo (1999), Afghanistan, Libya (2011) and against ISIL. In 2022 Canada selected the F-35 Lightning II to replace the CF-18 Hornet fleet, and in 2020–2022 the RCAF assumed responsibility for the Canadian Armed Forces' space activities.

Structure and aircraft

The RCAF is organised principally into 1 Canadian Air Division (headquartered in Winnipeg), which commands operational wings across Canada, and 2 Canadian Air Division for training. Major aircraft types include the CF-188 Hornet fighter, CP-140 Aurora maritime patrol aircraft, CC-177 Globemaster III and CC-150 Polaris transports, CC-130J Hercules, CH-147F Chinook and CH-139 helicopters, and CT-114 Tutor jets of the Snowbirds demonstration team. Major bases include CFB Cold Lake, Bagotville, Trenton, Winnipeg, Greenwood and Comox.
