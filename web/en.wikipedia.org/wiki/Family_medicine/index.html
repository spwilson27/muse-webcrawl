Family medicine

Family physicians provide continuing, comprehensive care for individuals and families across all ages.

Family medicine is a medical specialty within primary care that provides continuing and comprehensive health care for the individual and family across all ages, genders, diseases, and parts of the body. The specialist, who is usually called a family physician or family doctor, is often the first point of contact with the health care system and is distinguished by an ongoing, personal patient–physician relationship focused on integrated care.

Scope of practice

Family physicians deliver a range of acute, chronic, and preventive medical care services. In addition to diagnosing and treating illness, they provide preventive care, including routine checkups, health-risk assessments, immunisation, and screening tests, and personalised counselling on maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Family doctors often care for several generations of the same family and manage patients with multiple chronic conditions.

History

Family medicine emerged in the 1960s as a reaction against the increasing specialisation and fragmentation of medical care. In the United States, the specialty was formally recognised in 1969 with the creation of the American Board of Family Practice. In the United Kingdom, the related role is performed by general practitioners (GPs), with training organised through the Royal College of General Practitioners.

Training

After medical school, family physicians typically complete a residency of three to four years covering internal medicine, paediatrics, obstetrics and gynaecology, surgery, psychiatry, and community medicine. Many countries require periodic re-certification and continuing professional development.

Role in health systems

Health systems built on strong primary care and family medicine tend to have better population health outcomes at lower cost. Family physicians act as coordinators of care, referring patients to specialists when necessary and integrating advice from across the health system.
