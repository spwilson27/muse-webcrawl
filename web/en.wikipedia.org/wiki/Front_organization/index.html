Front organization

A front organization is an entity set up by and controlled by another organisation — such as an intelligence agency, criminal group, political movement or corporation — while appearing to operate independently. Fronts may raise funds, recruit, spread messages or provide a legitimate face for covert activity.


Related: Shell company, astroturfing, cut-out

Types

Intelligence services have used front companies for procurement and propaganda; organised crime uses front businesses to launder money; political fronts present a broad coalition while directed by a smaller group. During the Cold War both blocs accused each other's cultural and peace organisations of being fronts.

Detection and law

Unusual funding, overlapping leadership and lack of independent governance are common indicators. Many jurisdictions require registration of agents acting for foreign principals.
