Docklands, Victoria

Docklands is an inner-city suburb of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia, located on Victoria Harbour immediately west of the central business district. Once a working port area of wharves and rail yards, it has been redeveloped since the 1990s into a mixed-use precinct of offices, apartments, marinas, and entertainment venues.

Development

The Docklands renewal, overseen first by the Docklands Authority and later VicUrban/Development Victoria, is one of Australia's largest urban renewal projects, covering about 200 hectares of land and water. Landmarks include Docklands Stadium (Marvel Stadium), the Melbourne Star observation wheel (now closed), the NAB and ANZ headquarters, and the Victoria Harbour and NewQuay promenades. The suburb is served by Southern Cross station and free City Circle trams.
