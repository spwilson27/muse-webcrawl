Sir Keir Rodney Starmer (born 2 September 1962) is a British politician and lawyer who has served as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom since July 2024 and as Leader of the Labour Party since 2020. He previously served as Director of Public Prosecutions from 2008 to 2013 and has been Member of Parliament for Holborn and St Pancras since 2015.

Early life and legal career

Starmer was born in Southwark, London, and raised in Oxted, Surrey, the son of a nurse and a toolmaker. He read law at the University of Leeds and took a postgraduate degree at Oxford, becoming a barrister specializing in human rights. He co-founded Doughty Street Chambers, was legal adviser to the Policing Board of Northern Ireland, and in 2002 was appointed Queen's Counsel. As head of the Crown Prosecution Service from 2008 to 2013, he oversaw prosecutions arising from the phone-hacking scandal and the 2011 England riots. He was knighted in 2014 for services to law and criminal justice.

Political career

Elected to Parliament in 2015, Starmer served in Jeremy Corbyn's shadow cabinet as Shadow Secretary of State for Exiting the European Union, where he advocated a close post-Brexit relationship and supported a second referendum. After Labour's 2019 election defeat, he won the 2020 leadership election on a platform of party unity and competence, subsequently moving Labour toward the political center. He led Labour to a landslide victory in the 2024 general election, ending fourteen years of Conservative government, and became prime minister on 5 July 2024.

Premiership

Starmer's government has emphasized economic growth, planning reform, public-service investment, and closer relations with the European Union, while facing challenges over public finances, immigration, and support for Ukraine in the Russo-Ukrainian War.
