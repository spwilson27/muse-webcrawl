SF Airlines

Founded2009
HubEzhou Huahu Airport; Shenzhen Bao'an
ParentSF Holding (SF Express)
TypeCargo airline

SF Airlines is a Chinese cargo airline, a subsidiary of SF Express, one of China's largest courier and logistics companies. Founded in 2009 and based in Shenzhen, it operates China's largest air cargo fleet, supporting the parent company's express delivery network.

Fleet and network

The airline's fleet centres on Boeing 737, 747, 757 and 767 freighters, exceeding 80 aircraft in the 2020s. It flies domestic routes across China and international services in Asia and beyond. In 2022 it began operating from the new Ezhou Huahu Airport in Hubei — China's first dedicated cargo hub airport, developed with SF — which became its principal hub.

Role

SF Airlines' growth mirrors the expansion of Chinese e-commerce, giving SF Express guaranteed overnight air capacity unusual among private Chinese couriers.
