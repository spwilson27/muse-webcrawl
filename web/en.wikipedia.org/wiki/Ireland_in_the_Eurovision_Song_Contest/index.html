Ireland in the Eurovision Song Contest

Ireland has participated in the Eurovision Song Contest since 1965, represented by RTÉ. With seven victories, Ireland held the record for most wins for decades, a mark equalled by Sweden in 2023.

The wins

Ireland's victories came with Dana ("All Kinds of Everything", 1970), Johnny Logan ("What's Another Year", 1980; "Hold Me Now", 1987), Linda Martin ("Why Me?", 1992), Niamh Kavanagh ("In Your Eyes", 1993), Paul Harrington and Charlie McGettigan ("Rock 'n' Roll Kids", 1994), and Eimear Quinn ("The Voice", 1996). The three wins of 1992–1994 (four in five years counting 1996) made Ireland the only country to win three consecutive contests, and Logan the first artist to win twice as a performer.

Later years

Since the introduction of semi-finals in 2004, Ireland has struggled, failing to qualify on several occasions, but reached the final in 2018 with Ryan O'Shaughnessy and in 2024 with Bambie Thug's "Doomsday Blue", which finished sixth — Ireland's best result in over two decades.
