1958 NFL Championship Game

DateDecember 28, 1958
VenuePolo Grounds, New York City
ResultBaltimore Colts 23, New York Giants 17 (OT)
Known asThe Greatest Game Ever Played

The 1958 NFL Championship Game was the National Football League title game played on December 28, 1958, at the Polo Grounds in New York. The Baltimore Colts defeated the New York Giants 23–17 in sudden-death overtime, the first NFL championship decided in overtime. It is often called “The Greatest Game Ever Played” and is credited with accelerating professional football's rise on national television.

Game summary

Baltimore, led by quarterback Johnny Unitas and coached by Weeb Ewbank, built a 14–3 halftime lead. The Giants rallied in the second half behind Frank Gifford and Charlie Conerly to lead 17–14. In the final minutes Unitas drove the Colts into range for Steve Myhra's tying field goal. In overtime, Unitas directed a long drive capped by Alan Ameche's one-yard touchdown run.

Significance

The nationally televised finish introduced many viewers to the drama of professional football and helped make the NFL a dominant American television sport.
