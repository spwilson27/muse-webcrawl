Catalan cuisine

Catalan cuisine is the traditional cuisine of Catalonia and the wider Catalan-speaking lands, a Mediterranean tradition combining seafood and mountain produce — the mar i muntanya (surf and turf) principle — with olive oil, garlic and tomato as staples.

Dishes

Signature items include pa amb tomàquet (bread rubbed with tomato), escalivada, calçots with romesco sauce, escudella stew, fideuà, suquet fish stew, crema catalana dessert, and sauces such as allioli and picada. Cava from the Penedès is the emblematic sparkling wine. Medieval Catalan cookbooks such as the Llibre de Sent Soví (c. 1324) are among Europe's oldest.

Modern era

Catalonia became a centre of avant-garde gastronomy through Ferran Adrià's elBulli. See Catalonia and Spanish cuisine.
