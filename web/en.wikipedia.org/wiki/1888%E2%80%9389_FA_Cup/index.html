The 1888–89 FA Cup was the 18th edition of the Football Association Challenge Cup, the world's oldest association football cup competition. It was the first FA Cup season to include qualifying rounds, introduced as the number of entrants grew following the creation of the Football League.

Final

Preston North End defeated Wolverhampton Wanderers 3–0 in the final at Kennington Oval on 30 March 1889, with goals from Fred Dewhurst, Jimmy Ross, and Sam Thomson. Preston, who also won the inaugural Football League championship without losing a match that season, completed the first League and Cup "Double" and earned the nickname "The Invincibles".

Notable events

Preston did not concede a single goal during the entire competition, a remarkable feat. The season cemented the club's dominance of early professional football in England.
