'Allo 'Allo! is a British television sitcom broadcast on BBC One from 1982 to 1992, created by David Croft and Jeremy Lloyd. Set in German-occupied France during the Second World War, it parodies war dramas such as Secret Army rather than the war itself.

Premise and characters

Café owner René Artois (Gorden Kaye) is reluctantly drawn into the schemes of the French Resistance, the Gestapo and his German officer customers, all while hiding two British airmen, a stolen painting — the Fallen Madonna with the Big Boobies — and his affairs from his wife Edith. Characters speak English with exaggerated accents to represent different languages, and René regularly addresses the audience to recap the convoluted plot, opening with "You may recognise me from the last episode…".

Legacy

The show ran for nine series and popular catchphrases including "Listen very carefully, I shall say this only once" and "Good moaning". It remains one of the best-known British sitcoms internationally.
