Sing 2

Sing 2 is a 2021 American animated jukebox musical comedy film produced by Illumination and distributed by Universal Pictures. The sequel to Sing (2016), it was written and directed by Garth Jennings and follows koala theatre impresario Buster Moon and his troupe of animal performers as they travel to the entertainment capital Redshore City to stage an ambitious new show, and must persuade reclusive rock star Clay Calloway to return to the stage. The film stars the voices of Matthew McConaughey, Reese Witherspoon, Scarlett Johansson, Taron Egerton, Tori Kelly, Nick Kroll, Bobby Cannavale, Halsey, Pharrell Williams, Letitia Wright, Eric André, Chelsea Peretti and Bono.

Plot

After the success of his rebuilt theatre, Buster Moon dreams of taking his show bigger. When a talent scout dismisses the troupe, Buster sneaks them into an audition for entertainment mogul Jimmy Crystal in glittering Redshore City, winning approval by pitching a sci-fi musical, "Out of This World", supposedly starring the legendary lion rocker Clay Calloway — who has not performed in 15 years since his wife's death. While rehearsing under Crystal's threats, and with Crystal's spoiled daughter Porsha cast in a role she cannot handle, Ash and Buster visit Calloway's secluded home and gradually coax him back to music. Despite Crystal's attempt to sabotage the opening night, the troupe performs the show, Porsha finds her own stage presence, and Calloway delivers a triumphant comeback performance.

Music

Like its predecessor, the film is built around cover versions of popular songs, including numbers by Billie Eilish, Drake, BTS, Taylor Swift, The Weeknd and Elton John. The centrepiece is U2's "I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For", performed in the film by Johansson and Bono, who also contributed the original song "Your Song Saved My Life".

Release and reception

Sing 2 premiered at AFI Fest in November 2021 and was released theatrically in the United States on December 22, 2021. Despite the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic it grossed about $408 million worldwide against an $85 million budget, becoming the highest-grossing animated film of 2021, and received generally positive reviews.
