List of constellations

The International Astronomical Union (IAU) recognizes 88 official constellations, which together cover the entire celestial sphere. Most derive from the 48 constellations listed by the Greek astronomer Ptolemy in the 2nd century CE; the remainder, mostly in the southern sky, were added by European astronomers in the 16th–18th centuries. The IAU adopted official boundaries, drawn by Eugène Delporte, in 1928.

The 88 constellations tile the whole celestial sphere.

History

Forty-eight constellations come from Ptolemy's Almagest. Southern-sky constellations were added by Dutch navigators Pieter Dirkszoon Keyser and Frederick de Houtman, by Nicolas-Louis de Lacaille (who added 14), and others.
