Buryatia

Buryatia (Russian: Буря́тия), officially the Republic of Buryatia, is a republic of Russia located in the Russian Far East. Formerly part of the Siberian Federal District, it has been administered as part of the Far Eastern Federal District since 2018. Its capital is the city of Ulan-Ude. It is home to the Buryats, a Mongolic people, and covers much of the southern shore of Lake Baikal.

Geography

Buryatia borders Mongolia to the south, Irkutsk Oblast to the west, Zabaykalsky Krai to the east and Tuva to the far west. Its terrain is largely mountainous taiga and steppe, and it includes a large portion of Lake Baikal, the world's deepest lake.

People and culture

The population is about one million, roughly two-thirds ethnic Russian and one-third Buryat. Buddhism (of the Tibetan Gelug tradition) is a major religion among Buryats, and the republic hosts important centres such as Ivolginsky datsan; shamanism and Orthodox Christianity are also practised.
