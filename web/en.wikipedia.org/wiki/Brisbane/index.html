Brisbane

Brisbane is the capital and most populous city of the Australian state of Queensland and the third-most populous city in Australia, with about 2.7 million residents in Greater Brisbane. It lies on the Brisbane River on the country's east coast, near Moreton Bay.

Brisbane was founded as a penal settlement in 1824-25 and named for Governor Thomas Brisbane. The city, nicknamed 'Brisvegas', has a subtropical climate and Queenslander timber architecture. Catastrophic floods hit in 1974, 2011 and 2022. Landmarks include South Bank Parklands (site of World Expo 88), the Story Bridge and Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary. Brisbane hosted the 1982 Commonwealth Games and will host the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.
