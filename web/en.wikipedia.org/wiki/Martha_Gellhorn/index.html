Martha Gellhorn

Martha Gellhorn
Born8 November 1908, St. Louis
Died15 February 1998, London
OccupationWar correspondent and novelist
[Image: Gellhorn in Spain during the Civil War]

Martha Gellhorn (1908–1998) was an American novelist and journalist regarded as one of the greatest war correspondents, who reported nearly every major conflict from the Spanish Civil War to Panama over six decades — famously stowing away on a hospital ship to witness D-Day, since women correspondents were barred from the landing, and reporting the liberation of Dachau.

Life

Gellhorn's brief marriage to Ernest Hemingway (1940–45) overlapped their rivalrous war reporting. Her credo was to record 'what I saw' on the side of the victims of war; her dispatches are collected in The Face of War (1959).
