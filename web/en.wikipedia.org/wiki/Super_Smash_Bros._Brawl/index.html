Super Smash Bros. Brawl

Developer: Sora Ltd., Game Arts

Publisher: Nintendo

Director: Masahiro Sakurai

Platform: Wii

Release: 2008

Genre: Fighting

Super Smash Bros. Brawl is a 2008 crossover fighting video game developed by Sora Ltd. and Game Arts and published by Nintendo for the Wii. It is the third installment in the Super Smash Bros. series and the successor to Super Smash Bros. Melee (2001). The game was designed by Masahiro Sakurai, who also directed its predecessors.

Gameplay

Following its predecessors, Brawl uses a battle system unlike that of typical fighting games. Players can choose from a roster of characters drawn from Nintendo and third-party franchises—including newcomers such as Sonic the Hedgehog and Solid Snake—and attempt to knock their opponents off a stage as damage percentage accumulates. The game introduced the Final Smash, a powerful special attack obtained from a Smash Ball, and Assist Trophies that summon supporting characters.

Modes

Brawl features a substantial single-player adventure mode, The Subspace Emissary, a side-scrolling beat-'em-up campaign with an original storyline and pre-rendered cutscenes. It was the first game in the series to include online multiplayer via the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection, and also added a stage builder, stickers, and an extensive soundtrack of arranged Nintendo music.

Reception

The game received critical acclaim for its content, multiplayer, and music, though the online play and loading times were criticised. It sold more than thirteen million copies worldwide, making it one of the best-selling Wii games.
