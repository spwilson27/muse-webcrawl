Swedish House Mafia is a Swedish house music supergroup consisting of Axwell, Steve Angello, and Sebastian Ingrosso. The group placed at number one on the worldwide DJ Mag Top 100 DJs poll in 2023, after placing at number eleven in 2011. They have been called "the faces of mainstream progressive house music" and have been credited with helping to bring electronic dance music (EDM) into the mainstream in the early 2010s.

History

The three members had collaborated informally since the mid-2000s before formally forming Swedish House Mafia in 2008. Their breakthrough singles included "One" (2010), "Miami 2 Ibiza" with Tinie Tempah (2010), "Save the World" (2011), and "Antidote" with Knife Party (2011). Their compilation albums Until One (2010) and Until Now (2012) collected their singles and productions.

In June 2012 the group announced that they would split up after a final world tour, the One Last Tour, which concluded at Ultra Music Festival in Miami in March 2013. Their single "Don't You Worry Child", featuring John Martin, became their biggest hit, reaching number one in several countries and receiving a Grammy nomination.

On 25 March 2018, the group reunited with a surprise closing performance at Ultra Music Festival in Miami, exactly five years after their farewell show. They subsequently released their debut studio album, Paradise Again, in April 2022, which included collaborations with The Weeknd, Sting, A$AP Rocky, and others.

Members

  • Axwell (Axel Hedfors) – producer of "Feel the Vibe" and founder of Axtone Records.
  • Steve Angello (Steven Angello Josefsson Fragogiannis) – founder of Size Records; released the solo album Wild Youth (2016) and Human (2018).
  • Sebastian Ingrosso (Sebastian Carmine Ingrosso) – founder of Refune Music; also one half of Axwell Λ Ingrosso.
