Rail Delivery Group

The Rail Delivery Group (RDG) is the membership body for the British railway industry, bringing together train operating companies, freight operators and Network Rail. It was formed in 2011 as the policy and leadership body of the rail industry and absorbed the functions of the Association of Train Operating Companies (ATOC) in 2016.

Functions

RDG coordinates industry-wide services such as the National Rail brand and enquiries service, railcards, ticketing settlement through its subsidiary Rail Settlement Plan, and the allocation of revenue between operators. It represents the industry to government and works on reform, franchising policy and customer information.
