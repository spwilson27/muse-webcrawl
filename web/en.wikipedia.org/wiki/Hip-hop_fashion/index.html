Hip-hop fashion

Hip-hop fashion comprises the distinctive styles of dress originating with hip-hop culture in New York City in the 1970s and 1980s, which have since had a major influence on global fashion.

History

Early hip-hop style drew on streetwear and sportswear: tracksuits, sneakers such as Adidas Superstars (famously worn by Run-DMC), Kangol hats, and oversized jewellery. In the 1990s, baggy jeans, Timberland boots, and workwear brands became prominent, alongside African-inspired colours and patterns. Brands founded by hip-hop artists, such as FUBU, Rocawear, and Sean John, brought the aesthetic into the mainstream fashion industry.

Influence

By the 2000s and 2010s, hip-hop fashion merged with luxury fashion, with artists collaborating with major fashion houses and streetwear brands such as Supreme achieving worldwide prominence.
