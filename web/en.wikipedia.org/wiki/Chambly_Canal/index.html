Chambly Canal

The Chambly Canal is a National Historic Site of Canada in Quebec. It runs about 20 km between Chambly and Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu along the Richelieu River, bypassing rapids, with nine locks.

History

Construction began in 1831 and the canal opened in 1843, forming part of the water route between the St. Lawrence River and Lake Champlain and the Hudson River. It carried timber and agricultural goods; today it serves recreational boating and is managed by Parks Canada.
