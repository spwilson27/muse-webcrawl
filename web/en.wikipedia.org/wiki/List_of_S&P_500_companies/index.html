List of S&P 500 companies

The S&P 500 is a stock market index maintained by S&P Dow Jones Indices comprising 503 common stocks issued by 500 large-cap companies traded on American stock exchanges. Constituents are selected by a committee based on market capitalization, liquidity and other factors, and are classified by the Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS).
