Tilburg

Tilburg is a city and municipality in the southern Netherlands, in the province of North Brabant. With a population of over 220,000, it is the second-largest city in the province after Eindhoven and one of the ten largest in the country.

History

Tilburg grew from a collection of hamlets around sheep pastures and developed in the 19th century into a major centre of the woollen textile industry, earning it the nickname "wool city". King William II of the Netherlands favoured the city and commissioned a palace there, now the city hall.

Education and culture

The city is home to Tilburg University, founded in 1927, and Fontys University of Applied Sciences, giving it a large student population. Cultural venues include the De Pont museum of contemporary art, the TextielMuseum, the 013 pop venue, and the annual Tilburg Fair (Tilburgse Kermis), one of the largest funfairs in the Benelux. The football club Willem II is based in the city.
