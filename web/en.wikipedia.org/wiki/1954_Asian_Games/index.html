The 1954 Asian Games, officially the Second Asian Games and commonly known as Manila 1954, were a multi-sport event held in Manila, Philippines from May 1 to 9, 1954. A total of 970 athletes from 18 Asian National Olympic Committees competed in 76 events in eight sports—the second edition of the Asian Games after the inaugural 1951 Games in New Delhi.

Participating nations

Eighteen nations took part, up from eleven in 1951. New participants included Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaya, North Borneo, South Korea, Thailand, and Vietnam. Japan, which had topped the inaugural medal table, again fielded the largest and strongest delegation.

Sports

The programme comprised athletics, basketball, boxing, cycling, football, shooting, swimming (including diving and water polo), weightlifting, and wrestling—grouped into eight sports. Basketball was a particular point of pride for the host nation; the Philippines won the gold medal, a highlight of the Games for local spectators.

Medal table

Japan led the medal standings by a wide margin, winning 38 gold medals. The Philippines finished second with 14 golds, followed by South Korea. In total, medals were won by nine of the eighteen participating delegations.

Legacy

The Manila Games consolidated the Asian Games as a quadrennial fixture under the Asian Games Federation (later the Olympic Council of Asia) and demonstrated the event's growth beyond its founding nations. The next edition was held in Tokyo in 1958.
