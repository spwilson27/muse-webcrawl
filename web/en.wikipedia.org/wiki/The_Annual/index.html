The Annual

The Annual is a series of compilation albums released by the British record label and club brand Ministry of Sound, summarising the biggest dance and house tracks of each year. The first edition, The Annual, appeared in 1995, mixed by Pete Tong and Boy George, and the series continued annually — later as The Annual 2000, The Annual 2001 and so on — typically as double or triple CD sets mixed by prominent DJs.

History and impact

The series became one of the UK's best-selling compilation franchises of the late 1990s and 2000s, with editions released internationally, including Australian versions through local licensees. Its track selection and DJ mixes documented the evolution of commercial club culture from handbag house through trance, electro house and EDM. Sales declined with the shift to streaming in the 2010s, though themed editions and retrospectives have continued.
