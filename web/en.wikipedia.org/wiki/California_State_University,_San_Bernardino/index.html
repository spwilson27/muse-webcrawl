California State University, San Bernardino (CSUSB) is a public university in San Bernardino, California. Founded in 1965, it is one of 23 campuses in the California State University system and serves the Inland Empire region, enrolling around 20,000 students.

History

The campus opened as San Bernardino-Riverside State College in 1965 on a 441-acre site at the foot of the San Bernardino Mountains. It became a university in 1984. A branch campus in Palm Desert serves the Coachella Valley.

Academics

CSUSB offers degrees through five colleges and is known for programs in criminal justice, cybersecurity (designated a National Center of Academic Excellence), and its Jack H. Brown College of Business and Public Administration. A majority of its students are the first in their families to attend college.
