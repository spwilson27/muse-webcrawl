Merrill's Marauders

Merrill's Marauders (named after Frank Merrill) or Unit Galahad, officially designated the 5307th Composite Unit (Provisional), was a United States Army long-range penetration special operations jungle warfare unit that fought in the China–Burma–India Theater of World War II.

Operations

Modelled on the British Chindits, the unit undertook deep-penetration missions behind Japanese lines in Burma in 1944, fighting at Walawbum, Shaduzup, Inkangahtawng, and finally at the siege of Myitkyina. Of some 2,750 men who entered Burma, only about 200 were fit for duty at the end of the campaign.

Legacy

The unit was disbanded in August 1944. Its lineage is continued by the 75th Ranger Regiment, and its members were collectively awarded the Congressional Gold Medal in 2020.
