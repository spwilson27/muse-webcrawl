List of tallest buildings in New York City

New York City, the most populous city in the United States, is home to more skyscrapers than any other American city, including the tallest building in the Western Hemisphere, One World Trade Center. Most of the tallest buildings are in Manhattan, concentrated in Midtown and Lower Manhattan. Heights are given to the architectural tip (including spires, excluding antennas) as commonly tabulated.

The original World Trade Center's Twin Towers (1,368 and 1,362 ft) held the record from 1971–1973 as the world's tallest buildings before being surpassed by Chicago's Sears (Willis) Tower; they were destroyed in the September 11 attacks of 2001.
