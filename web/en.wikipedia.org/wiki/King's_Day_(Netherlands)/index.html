King's Day (Netherlands)

King's Day (Dutch: Koningsdag) is a national holiday in the Netherlands celebrated on 27 April, the birthday of King Willem-Alexander. Until 2013, when Queen Beatrix abdicated, it was celebrated as Queen's Day on 30 April.

Celebrations

The day is marked by nationwide street parties, concerts, flea markets (vrijmarkt) where residents sell used goods without needing a permit, and crowds dressing in orange — the colour of the Dutch royal House of Orange-Nassau. The king and members of the royal family visit one or more municipalities each year. Amsterdam draws especially large crowds and boat parties on its canals.
