Canyon of Heroes

Canyon of Heroes
LocationBroadway, Lower Manhattan, New York City
RouteBattery Park to City Hall Park
Known forTicker-tape parades

The Canyon of Heroes is a nickname for the section of lower Broadway in the Financial District of Manhattan, New York City, that is the traditional route of ticker-tape parades. It stretches from Battery Park, at Bowling Green, north along Broadway to City Hall Park, hemmed in by tall office towers that give the street a canyon-like character.

Parades

The first spontaneous ticker-tape celebration occurred in 1886 for the dedication of the Statue of Liberty, when office workers threw ticker tape from windows onto the parade below. The first officially organised parade along the route honoured Admiral George Dewey in 1899 after the Spanish–American War. Since then New York City has held more than 200 parades on the route, honouring heads of state, military heroes, aviators such as Charles Lindbergh, astronauts of the Apollo programme, returning veterans, and championship sports teams.

Commemoration

Each parade is commemorated by a granite strip set into the sidewalk along the route, inscribed with the date and the name of the honouree. More than 200 such markers line the Canyon of Heroes, maintained with the help of the Downtown Alliance.
