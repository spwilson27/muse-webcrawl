Đurđevića Tara Bridge

LocationTara River Canyon, Montenegro
TypeConcrete arch bridge
Length365 m
Height above riverAbout 172 m
Built1937–1940

The Đurđevića Tara Bridge is a concrete arch bridge spanning the Tara River Canyon in northern Montenegro, near the village of Đurđevića Tara. When completed in 1940 it was the largest concrete arch bridge in Europe.

History

The bridge was designed by engineer Mijat Trojanović and built between 1937 and 1940 in the Kingdom of Yugoslavia. During the Second World War, in 1942, Yugoslav Partisans demolished one arch with the help of the bridge's own engineer, Lazar Jauković, who was captured and executed by Italian forces; a memorial to Jauković stands at the bridge. The span was rebuilt after the war.

Tourism

Today the bridge is a landmark of the Tara canyon region, offering views over one of Europe's deepest river canyons and serving as a starting point for rafting and zip-line activities.
