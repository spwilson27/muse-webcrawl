List of airports in Switzerland

This is a list of airports in Switzerland, sorted by location. Switzerland has seven airports with scheduled commercial service, the largest being Zurich Airport, followed by Geneva Airport and EuroAirport Basel–Mulhouse–Freiburg (which lies on French territory but serves Switzerland). In addition there are many regional airfields, military air bases and glider fields.
