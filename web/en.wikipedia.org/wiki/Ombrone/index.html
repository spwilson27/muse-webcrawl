Ombrone

The Ombrone near its mouth in the Maremma, Tuscany.

The Ombrone is a 161-kilometre (100 mi) river in Tuscany, central Italy. It rises in the hills southeast of Siena and flows southwest through the Maremma to empty into the Tyrrhenian Sea near Grosseto.

Course

The river passes near Buonconvento and receives tributaries including the Orcia, Merse and Bruna. Its wide lower valley forms part of the Maremma Regional Park (Parco della Maremma), an important wetland and nature reserve.

History

Known in antiquity as the Umbro, the river long formed a boundary of Etruscan territory. Its historically marshy delta was drained in major land-reclamation works under the Grand Duchy of Tuscany and again in the 20th century.
