Near-field communication
Range: up to ~4 cm (typical)
Frequency: 13.56 MHz
Standards: ISO/IEC 18092

Near-field communication (NFC) is a set of communication protocols that enables communication between two electronic devices over a distance of 4 centimetres (1.6 in) or less. NFC offers a low-speed connection through a simple setup that can be used for bootstrapping more capable wireless connections.

Applications

NFC is widely used for contactless payments through services such as Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as for access control, transit ticketing, and pairing Bluetooth devices.

Technology

NFC devices operate at 13.56 MHz and can work in reader/writer, peer-to-peer, and card emulation modes. NFC builds on radio-frequency identification (RFID) technology.
