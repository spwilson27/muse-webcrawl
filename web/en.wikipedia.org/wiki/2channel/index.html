2channel

2channel (Japanese: 2ちゃんねる, ni channeru), often abbreviated 2ch, was a Japanese anonymous textboard founded on May 30, 1999, by Hiroyuki Nishimura during his time as a student at the University of Central Arkansas. At its peak it was the largest discussion forum in the world, receiving millions of posts per day, and it exerted a major influence on Japanese internet culture.

Culture and influence

The site allowed fully anonymous posting across roughly 600 active boards covering news, hobbies, computing, and everyday life. It spawned numerous internet memes, the fictional railway-otaku phenomenon Densha Otoko ("Train Man"), and a large vocabulary of ASCII art characters such as Giko Cat. 2channel also influenced the structure of later anonymous boards, most directly 4chan in the English-speaking world.

Ownership disputes

In 2009 Nishimura transferred ownership to a Singapore company amid legal pressure from defamation suits he had ignored. In 2014 the site's domain was seized by its registrar in a dispute with administrator Jim Watkins, who then took control of the site; Nishimura subsequently relaunched his community as 5channel, while Watkins' version retained the 2channel name (2ch.sc).
