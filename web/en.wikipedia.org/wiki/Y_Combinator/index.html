Y Combinator Management, LLC (YC) is an American technology startup accelerator and venture capital firm launched in March 2005, credited with pioneering the seed-accelerator model that has since been imitated worldwide. It has been used to launch more than 4,000 companies, including Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, Reddit, Twitch, DoorDash, and Coinbase. YC operates under the slogan "Make something people want."

Programme

YC funds startups in batches — historically two per year, now four — providing a standard investment (since 2020, $500,000 in total: $125,000 on a post-money SAFE plus $375,000 on an uncapped MFN SAFE) in exchange for equity, in return for a roughly three-month programme of mentorship, weekly talks, and group-partner advice, culminating in a Demo Day for investors. The accelerator was founded in Cambridge, Massachusetts, moved largely to Silicon Valley, and under presidents including Sam Altman (2014–2019) expanded into later-stage and continuity funds. Garry Tan, a YC alumnus, became president and CEO in 2023. YC also runs the free Startup School course and maintains the Hacker News community site.

Name

The firm is named after the Y combinator, a fixed-point combinator in lambda calculus — a fitting name, Paul Graham has explained, for a company that helps start companies, in the same way the combinator lets functions bootstrap recursion.
