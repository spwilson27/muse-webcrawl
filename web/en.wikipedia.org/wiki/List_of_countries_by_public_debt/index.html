List of countries by public debt

This is a list of countries by public debt, showing general government gross debt as a percentage of gross domestic product (GDP), based mainly on International Monetary Fund (IMF) World Economic Outlook data. Japan has for decades carried the highest public-debt ratio of any major economy, at more than two and a half times its annual GDP.

Lowest debt ratios

Countries with very low public debt include oil- and gas-rich states that have accumulated sovereign wealth, such as Kuwait, Brunei and Turkmenistan, along with jurisdictions such as Liechtenstein, Hong Kong and Estonia, whose gross debt has often been below 20–25% of GDP — the lowest in the European Union.
