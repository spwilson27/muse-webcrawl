Timket

Timket
Observed by: Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Christians
Type: Religious festival
Date: 19 January (20 January in leap years)

Timket (Amharic: ጥምቀት) is an Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church and Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo Church festival celebrating the Epiphany, commemorating the baptism of Jesus in the River Jordan. It is held on 19 January, corresponding to the 11th day of Terr in the Ethiopian calendar.

Celebrations

During the ceremony, the Tabot, a model of the Ark of the Covenant, is reverently wrapped and carried in procession on the head of a priest. On the eve of the festival, the Tabots of each church are taken to a nearby body of water, where the faithful gather for an all-night vigil of prayer and hymns. The following morning the water is blessed and sprinkled on the participants, and some people immerse themselves in the water, symbolically renewing their baptismal vows.

Recognition

The festival, particularly famous in Gondar and Lalibela, draws large crowds of worshippers and tourists. In 2019 UNESCO inscribed Timket on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
