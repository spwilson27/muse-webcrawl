Mike and Ike

Mike and Ike is a brand of fruit-flavored, chewy candies produced by the Just Born company of Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. Introduced in 1940, the candies are oblong, soft jelly-style pieces similar in form to jelly beans, and are sold in movie theaters, convenience stores, and supermarkets across the United States.

Flavors

The Original Fruits assortment consists of cherry, lemon, strawberry, lime, and orange. Numerous other varieties have been introduced over the years, including Berry Blast, Tropical Typhoon, Mega Mix, RedRageous!, and sour lines such as Sour Mega Mix, along with limited-edition seasonal flavors.

The "breakup" campaign

In 2012 Just Born ran a widely noted marketing campaign in which Mike and Ike were portrayed as having "split up" over creative differences, with packaging in which one name or the other was scribbled out. The duo "reunited" in 2013 after fan response, with new packaging and a redesigned, juicier candy.

Origin of the name

The origin of the name "Mike and Ike" is uncertain. Just Born has acknowledged the mystery; suggested sources include a company contest, a vaudeville song ("Mike and Ike (They Look Alike)"), and a comic strip of the era. The characters Mike and Ike appear as mascots on modern packaging.
