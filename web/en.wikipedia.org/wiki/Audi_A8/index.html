Audi A8

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Audi A8 is a full-size luxury sedan manufactured by the German automaker Audi since 1994, succeeding the Audi V8 as the brand's flagship. It competes with the Mercedes-Benz S-Class and BMW 7 Series. The high-performance variant is sold as the Audi S8, and long-wheelbase models carry the "L" designation.

First generation (D2, 1994–2002)

The original A8 pioneered the Audi Space Frame (ASF): an all-aluminium monocoque body that reduced weight and improved rigidity, a technology previewed by the Audi Space Frame concept car at the 1993 Frankfurt Motor Show. It offered V8 engines and standard quattro all-wheel drive, and a 6.0-litre W12 engine was added in 2001.

Later generations

The second generation (D3, 2002) introduced the MMI infotainment interface and adaptive air suspension; the S8 used a 5.2-litre V10 derived from Lamborghini's engine. The third generation (D4, 2010) added LED lighting technology and an eight-speed automatic. The fourth generation (D5, unveiled 2017) featured a 48-volt mild-hybrid system across the range, touchscreen-based MMI touch response, and was designed for Level 3 conditional automation with its optional AI traffic jam pilot, though regulatory hurdles limited its activation.
