Fitzgerald Stadium (Irish: Staidiam Mhic Gearailt) is the principal GAA stadium in Killarney, County Kerry, Ireland. It is the home ground of the Kerry county Gaelic football team, and one of the largest stadiums in Ireland, with a capacity of roughly 38,000 (reduced for all-seated requirements in some configurations). Named after Dick Fitzgerald, one of Kerry's greatest early footballers, the stadium was opened in 1936.

History

The stadium was built on a site beside Killarney's railway line and was officially opened on 31 May 1936, when Kerry played Mayo. Fitzgerald Stadium has hosted numerous Munster Senior Football Championship finals and other major fixtures, and is renowned for its scenic setting beneath the MacGillycuddy's Reeks. Redevelopments over the decades added a terraced stand, seating, and floodlights, allowing evening matches.
