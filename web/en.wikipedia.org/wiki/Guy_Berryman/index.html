Guy Rupert Berryman (born 12 April 1978) is a Scottish musician, best known as the bassist of the rock band Coldplay.

Early life and Coldplay

Born in Kirkcaldy, Fife, Berryman was educated in Edinburgh and Kent before studying at University College London, where in 1996 he formed a band with Chris Martin, Jonny Buckland and Will Champion that became Coldplay. He dropped his studies to pursue the band, which went on to sell over 100 million records worldwide with albums from Parachutes (2000) onward.

Other work

Berryman is a member of the supergroup Apparatjik with members of a-ha and Mew. Known for his interests in design, photography and classic cars, he founded the clothing label Applied Art Forms and has run a vintage car business. He is left-handed but plays bass right-handed.
