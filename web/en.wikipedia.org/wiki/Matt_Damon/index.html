Matthew Paige Damon (born October 8, 1970) is an American actor, film producer, and screenwriter. He is one of the highest-grossing actors of all time and has received various accolades, including an Academy Award and two Golden Globe Awards, in addition to nominations for three British Academy Film Awards and seven Primetime Emmy Awards.

Damon began his acting career by appearing in high school theater productions. He made his professional acting debut in the film Mystic Pizza (1988). He gained prominence in 1997 when he and Ben Affleck wrote and starred in Good Will Hunting, which won them the Academy Award and Golden Globe for Best Screenplay. He continued to garner praise for his starring roles in Saving Private Ryan (1998), The Talented Mr. Ripley (1999), the Bourne franchise (2002–2007; 2016), Syriana (2005), The Departed (2006), The Informant! (2009), Invictus (2009), True Grit (2010), Contagion (2011), Ford v Ferrari (2019), Stillwater (2021), Air (2023), and Oppenheimer (2023), the last of which is his highest-grossing feature. He received the Academy Award for Best Actor for Good Will Hunting and was nominated for his performances in Invictus and The Martian (2015).

Early life

Damon was born in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the second son of Kent Telfer Damon, a stockbroker, and Nancy Carlsson-Paige, a professor of early childhood education at Lesley University. He attended Harvard University, where he was a member of the class of 1992, but left before graduating to take a lead role in the film Geronimo: An American Legend (1993). While at Harvard he wrote an early draft of the screenplay for Good Will Hunting as an assignment for an English class.

Career

After early roles in School Ties (1992) and Courage Under Fire (1996), Damon's breakthrough came with Good Will Hunting (1997), directed by Gus Van Sant. The film was a critical and commercial success. The following year he played the title role in Steven Spielberg's Saving Private Ryan. In 2002, Damon began portraying Jason Bourne in The Bourne Identity, a role that made him an action star. He also starred in the Ocean's trilogy of heist films.

In 2015, Damon starred as astronaut Mark Watney in Ridley Scott's The Martian, based on Andy Weir's novel, earning a Golden Globe Award for Best Actor and an Academy Award nomination. In 2023 he starred in the biographical sports drama Air and played General Leslie Groves in Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer.

Philanthropy

Damon is a co-founder of Water.org, a nonprofit organization that provides access to safe water and sanitation. He has also been involved with the ONE Campaign and Feeding America, and co-founded the production company Artists Equity with Ben Affleck in 2022.

Personal life

Damon married Argentine Luciana Bozán Barroso in 2005. They have three daughters together, and Damon is stepfather to Barroso's daughter from a previous marriage. He resides in Los Angeles, California.
