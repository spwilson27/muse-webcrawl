Snapchat is an American multimedia instant-messaging app developed by Snap Inc. Its defining feature was photos and messages that disappear after being viewed, alongside Stories (copied widely by rivals including Instagram), playful augmented-reality Lenses and filters, and a camera-first interface.

Snapchat was created by Stanford students Evan Spiegel, Bobby Murphy and Reggie Brown, growing out of a class project. It became especially popular with younger users, introduced the vertical-video and stories formats to a mass audience, and later added Spotlight short video, a Snap Map, and the My AI chatbot.
