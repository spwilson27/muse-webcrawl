José Antonio Abreu Anselmi (7 May 1939 – 24 March 2018) was a Venezuelan musician, conductor, composer, economist and politician. He founded El Sistema (Fundación Musical Simón Bolívar) in 1975, a publicly financed music-education programme that taught classical music to hundreds of thousands of Venezuelan children, especially from poor backgrounds, and became a model for music-for-social-change programmes around the world.

Early life

Abreu was born in Valera, in the Andean state of Trujillo. He studied piano, harpsichord and organ, and composition in Caracas, while also earning a degree in economics and entering politics — he served as a deputy in the Venezuelan Congress and briefly as minister of culture. This combination of musical training, economic expertise and political skill proved decisive in building El Sistema.

El Sistema

In 1975 Abreu gathered eleven young musicians in an underground car park in Caracas for a first rehearsal. From that seed grew a national network of núcleos (community music centres) and youth orchestras, funded by the state as a social programme. Its flagship ensemble, the Simón Bolívar Symphony Orchestra, won international fame under conductors including its alumnus Gustavo Dudamel, music director of the Los Angeles Philharmonic. At its peak El Sistema enrolled several hundred thousand children, and its methods inspired programmes in dozens of countries.

Recognition and criticism

Abreu received numerous honours, including the Right Livelihood Award (2001), Japan's Praemium Imperiale-related honours, the Polar Music Prize (2009, shared), and an honorary membership of the Royal Philharmonic Society. Later scholarship, notably Geoffrey Baker's El Sistema: Orchestrating Venezuela's Youth (2014), questioned aspects of the programme's discipline, governance and claimed social outcomes, but its global influence on music education is undisputed.

Death

Abreu died in Caracas on 24 March 2018, aged 78. Venezuela observed national mourning, and orchestras around the world performed in his memory.
