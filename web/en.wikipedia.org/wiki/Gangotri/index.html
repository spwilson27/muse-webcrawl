Gangotri

Gangotri
State: Uttarakhand, India
District: Uttarkashi
Elevation: 3,415 m (11,204 ft)
Significance: Source region of the Bhagirathi / Ganges; Char Dham pilgrimage site

Gangotri is a town and Hindu pilgrim centre (Nagar Panchayat) in Uttarkashi district in the Indian state of Uttarakhand. It lies on the banks of the river Bhagirathi, at an altitude of about 3,415 metres in the Greater Himalayas, and is one of the four sites of the Char Dham pilgrimage circuit.


Gangotri temple and the Bhagirathi

Significance

Gangotri is the traditional origin of the river Ganges in Hindu belief, associated with the goddess Ganga and the penance of King Bhagiratha. The actual glacial source of the Bhagirathi is the Gangotri Glacier at Gaumukh, about 19 km from the town.

Gangotri Temple

The Gangotri Temple, dedicated to the goddess Ganga, was built in the 18th century by the Gurkha general Amar Singh Thapa. The temple opens in late spring (around Akshaya Tritiya) and closes for winter around Diwali, when the deity's idol is moved to the village of Mukhba.
