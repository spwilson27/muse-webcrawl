The 95th Academy Awards ceremony, presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, honoured the best films of 2022 and took place on 12 March 2023 at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood, Los Angeles. Jimmy Kimmel hosted for the third time.

Winners

The night was dominated by Everything Everywhere All at Once, which won seven awards from eleven nominations, including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Original Screenplay for Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert, Best Actress for Michelle Yeoh — the first Asian winner of the award — and both supporting acting awards, for Ke Huy Quan and Jamie Lee Curtis. Brendan Fraser won Best Actor for The Whale. All Quiet on the Western Front won four awards including Best International Feature, and "Naatu Naatu" from RRR won Best Original Song, a first for an Indian production.

Context

The ceremony followed the controversy of the 94th Academy Awards the previous year, and the Academy put a crisis-management team in place. All 23 categories were presented live after the previous year's contested decision to pre-record some craft awards. Television viewership in the United States rose modestly to about 18.7 million.
