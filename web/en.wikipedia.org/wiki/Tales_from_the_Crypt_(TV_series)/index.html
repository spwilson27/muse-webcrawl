Tales from the Crypt, sometimes titled HBO's Tales from the Crypt, is an American horror anthology television series created by William Gaines and Steven Dodd, based on the 1950s EC Comics series of the same name. It ran for seven seasons on HBO from 1989 to 1996.

Overview

Each episode was introduced by the wisecracking corpse-like Cryptkeeper, voiced by John Kassir, and adapted stories from EC's horror comics including Tales from the Crypt, The Haunt of Fear, and The Vault of Horror, as well as crime titles such as Crime SuspenStories and Shock SuspenStories. Stories typically featured ironic twist endings in which wrongdoers met gruesome fates.

Production and legacy

Because it aired on premium cable, the series could depict graphic violence, nudity, and profanity uncommon on television at the time. Executive producers included Richard Donner, David Giler, Walter Hill, Joel Silver, and Robert Zemeckis. The show spawned feature films including Demon Knight (1995) and Bordello of Blood (1996), a children's animated series Tales from the Cryptkeeper, and a radio series.
