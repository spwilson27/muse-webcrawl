Fei-Fei Li

Born: 1976, Beijing, China
Alma mater: Princeton University; Caltech (PhD)
Known for: ImageNet; computer vision
Institution: Stanford University

Fei-Fei Li (born 1976) is a Chinese-American computer scientist known for establishing ImageNet, the dataset that enabled rapid advances in object recognition and deep learning. She is a professor at Stanford University and co-director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI).

Career

Born in Beijing and immigrating to the United States as a teenager, Li earned her physics degree from Princeton and a PhD in electrical engineering from Caltech. At Stanford she led the creation of ImageNet (2009) and its associated Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge, whose 2012 edition catalyzed the deep learning revolution in computer vision.

Other work

Li served as chief scientist of AI/ML at Google Cloud (2017–2018), co-founded the nonprofit AI4ALL to promote diversity in AI education, and later founded World Labs, a spatial intelligence company. Her memoir is The Worlds I See (2023).
