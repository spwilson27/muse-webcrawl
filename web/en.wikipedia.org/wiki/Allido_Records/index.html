Allido Records

Allido Records is an American record label founded in 2004 by the musician and producer Mark Ronson together with entrepreneur and manager Rich Kleiman. The label's name derives from Ronson's production company and was formed within the J Records/RCA orbit, later associating with Interscope Records for distribution.

Artists

Allido's best-known signing was the Chicago rapper Rhymefest, whose 2006 album Blue Collar was released through the label; Rhymefest had co-written Kanye West's "Jesus Walks". Other acts associated with Allido included the singer-songwriter Daniel Merriweather in some territories, the group The Rumblestrips for US release, and rapper Saigon at an early stage of his career. Ronson's own remix and production projects were also issued under the imprint.

Later activity

The label became largely dormant as Ronson's career as a solo artist and producer expanded in the late 2000s, although the Allido name continued to appear as a production entity.
