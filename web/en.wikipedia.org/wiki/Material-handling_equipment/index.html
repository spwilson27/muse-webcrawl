Material-handling equipment

Material-handling equipment (MHE) refers to machinery used to move, store, control, and protect materials, goods, and products throughout the processes of manufacturing, warehousing, distribution, consumption, and disposal. It forms a core part of logistics and supply chain management.

Categories

Transport equipment moves materials between locations and includes industrial trucks such as forklifts and pallet jacks, conveyors (belt, roller, and overhead), cranes and hoists, and automated guided vehicles (AGVs).

Positioning equipment handles material at a single location, such as lift tables, hoists, balancers, and manipulators used at workstations and assembly lines.

Unit load formation equipment groups items together for easier handling: pallets, skids, containers, bins, stretch wrap, and strapping equipment.

Storage equipment holds materials over time, including racking systems (pallet racks, drive-in racks, shelving), automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS), and mezzanines.

Identification and control equipment includes barcode scanners, RFID readers and tags, weigh scales, and the warehouse management software that coordinates material movements.

Design considerations

Material handling design balances throughput, flexibility, cost, ergonomics, and safety. Because injuries from manual handling are common, engineers aim to minimise manual lifting through mechanisation and automation, and modern distribution centres operate highly automated systems with robotic picking and conveying.
