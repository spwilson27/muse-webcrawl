Dalian Wanda Group


Image placeholder: Dalian Wanda Group

Dalian Wanda Group is a Chinese multinational conglomerate founded by Wang Jianlin in Dalian in 1988. It grew from a property developer into one of China's largest private companies, with interests in commercial real estate, entertainment, tourism, sports, and finance.

Business

Wanda is known for its Wanda Plaza mixed-use complexes, combining shopping malls, hotels, and offices across China. Its entertainment arm once owned AMC Theatres (acquired in 2012) and Legendary Entertainment (acquired in 2016), making Wang one of the most prominent Chinese investors in Hollywood. Wanda also held a stake in Atlético Madrid and organised sporting events through its sports division.

Restructuring

From 2017, under pressure from Chinese regulators over debt-fuelled overseas expansion, Wanda sold many assets, including hotel and tourism projects and stakes in film and sports businesses, to refocus on asset-light property management through its commercial management arm.
