M. Karunanidhi

Muthuvel Karunanidhi (3 June 1924 – 7 August 2018) was an Indian writer and politician who served as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu for almost two decades over five terms. He was the leader of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) party for nearly five decades.

M. Karunanidhi
Born: 3 June 1924, Thirukkuvalai, Madras Presidency
Died: 7 August 2018, Chennai
Party: Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam
Office: Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu (five terms)

Early life

Born in Thirukkuvalai village in present-day Nagapattinam district, Karunanidhi became involved in the Dravidian movement as a teenager and began writing for Tamil journals and cinema.

Political career

He first became Chief Minister in 1969 following the death of C. N. Annadurai, and went on to win or lead numerous state elections. A prolific screenwriter and author, he was popularly known by the honorific "Kalaignar" (artist).
