Watch

A watch is a portable timepiece intended to be carried or worn, most commonly as a wristwatch.

History

Spring-driven watches appeared in the 16th century. Wristwatches became popular after World War I. Quartz watches revolutionized accuracy in the 1970s, and smartwatches added computing functions in the 2010s.

Types

Mechanical, quartz, and smart watches; complications include chronographs, calendars, and tourbillons.
