BMG Rights Management

BMG Rights Management GmbH
Founded: 2008
Headquarters: Berlin, Germany
Parent: Bertelsmann
Industry: Music publishing and recordings

BMG Rights Management is an international music company based in Berlin, a division of Bertelsmann. Founded in 2008 after Bertelsmann sold its stake in Sony BMG, the new BMG built a business combining music publishing and recorded music under one roof, positioning itself as a fairer, service-oriented alternative for artists and songwriters.

Catalogue and artists

BMG administers publishing catalogues including works by Bruno Mars, John Legend, Mick Jagger and Keith Richards, and acquired labels and catalogues such as Mute, Trojan, Sanctuary and numerous independent imprints. Its recorded roster has included Kylie Minogue, Jason Aldean, Jelly Roll and many others across genres.

Operations

The company operates offices in major music markets including Los Angeles, Nashville, New York, London and Sydney, licensing music for film, television, advertising and streaming.
