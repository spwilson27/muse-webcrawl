List of American League pennant winners

This is a list of American League pennant winners: the teams that won the championship of the American League (AL) of Major League Baseball each season since the league's founding in 1901. Before the introduction of the American League Championship Series (ALCS) in 1969, the pennant was awarded to the team with the best regular-season record. Since 1969 the pennant winner is decided by the ALCS, and since 1995 by the expanded playoff format, with the winner advancing to the World Series against the National League champion.

History

The American League was founded as a major league in 1901. Its dominant franchise historically has been the New York Yankees, who have won far more pennants (over 40) than any other club. From 1969, the league was split into East and West divisions, and the pennant was decided in the ALCS; the wild card era beginning in 1994 added further rounds.
