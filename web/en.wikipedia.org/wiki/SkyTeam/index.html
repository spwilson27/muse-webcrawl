SkyTeam is one of the world's three major airline alliances. Founded in June 2000, SkyTeam was the last of the three to be formed, after Star Alliance (1997) and Oneworld (1999). Its annual passenger count is roughly 624 million (2023), the second largest of the three major alliances. As of 2024, SkyTeam consists of 19 active member airlines.

SkyTeam sells its passengers a global network with services such as through check-in, reciprocal frequent-flyer benefits, and shared airport lounges. Member airlines operate more than 10,000 daily flights to over 1,000 destinations in more than 160 countries.

History

SkyTeam was announced on 22 June 2000 by Aeroméxico, Air France, Delta Air Lines, and Korean Air. CSA Czech Airlines joined in 2001, followed by Alitalia and KLM. Following the merger of Air France and KLM in 2004, the two carriers' participation was consolidated within the alliance. Continental Airlines, Copa Airlines and Northwest Airlines joined in 2004 as part of the "Wings" expansion; Continental and Copa later left in 2009 following Continental's merger with United Airlines.

China Southern Airlines became the first mainland Chinese carrier to join a global alliance when it entered SkyTeam in 2007; it left at the end of 2018. In 2023, Virgin Atlantic joined the alliance. Scandinavian Airlines (SAS) joined SkyTeam on 1 September 2024 after leaving Star Alliance, following Air France–KLM's investment in the carrier.

Member airlines

As of 2024, full members include Aerolíneas Argentinas, Aeroméxico, Air Europa, Air France, China Airlines, China Eastern Airlines, Delta Air Lines, Garuda Indonesia, ITA Airways, Kenya Airways, KLM, Korean Air, Middle East Airlines, Saudia, SAS, TAROM, Vietnam Airlines, Virgin Atlantic, and XiamenAir.

Premium and frequent-flyer benefits

SkyTeam's two-tier elite programme is branded SkyTeam Elite (comparable to silver) and SkyTeam Elite Plus (comparable to gold). Elite Plus members receive lounge access, priority boarding, and extra baggage allowances across member airlines. In 2012 the alliance launched SkyPriority, a set of priority airport services for premium customers.

SkyTeam Cargo

SkyTeam Cargo is the cargo division of the alliance, launched in September 2000. Its members coordinate freighter capacity and sell joint cargo products under brands such as Equation, Cohesion, Variation, and Dimension.
