Ciudad de la Paz

Ciudad de la Paz ("City of Peace"), formerly known as Oyala, is a planned city under construction in mainland Equatorial Guinea, designed to supersede Malabo as the capital city. It is located in the province of Djibloho, carved out of Wele-Nzas province in 2017, in a heavily forested area.

Planning

The city was designed by a Portuguese architecture firm and is planned to accommodate some 200,000 inhabitants, with a presidential palace, university, and parliament buildings. Its location was justified by the government on grounds of accessibility, climate, and security, with abundant room for expansion.

Status

Construction began in the early 2010s. The city became the country's administrative capital in 2017 when Djibloho province was created, though much of the government apparatus has remained in Malabo and Bata during the build-out.
