The People's Liberation Army Navy (PLAN) is the maritime service branch of China's People's Liberation Army. Founded in 1949 from defecting Nationalist vessels and coastal forces, it has grown from a brown-water coastal defense force into the world's largest navy by number of hulls, with aircraft carriers, nuclear submarines, large destroyers and an expanding global presence.

Organization

The PLAN is organized into three fleets — the North Sea Fleet (headquartered at Qingdao), East Sea Fleet (Ningbo) and South Sea Fleet (Zhanjiang) — aligned with China's theater commands. It includes a naval air force, the PLA Marine Corps, coastal defense forces and a submarine arm operating both diesel-electric and nuclear-powered ballistic missile and attack submarines.

Modernization

From the 1990s China invested heavily in indigenous warship construction: Type 052D and the large Type 055 destroyers, Type 054A frigates, Type 075 amphibious assault ships, and a carrier program that progressed from the refitted Liaoning (2012) to the domestically built Shandong (2019) and the catapult-equipped Fujian (launched 2022). Strategic priorities include defending China's maritime claims in the South and East China Seas, deterring intervention in a Taiwan contingency, protecting sea lanes, and counter-piracy and evacuation operations abroad; China opened its first overseas naval support base in Djibouti in 2017.
