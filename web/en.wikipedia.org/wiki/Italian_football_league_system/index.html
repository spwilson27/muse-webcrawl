Italian football league system

The Italian football league system is a hierarchy of interlinked leagues for association football clubs in Italy, governed overall by the Italian Football Federation (FIGC). It comprises thousands of clubs from the fully professional top flight down to local amateur divisions, connected by promotion and relegation.

Structure

The top three tiers are fully professional and national in scope: Serie A (20 clubs), Serie B (20 clubs), and Serie C (60 clubs in three regional groups). Below them, Serie D is the highest amateur level, organised in nine groups. Further regional tiers — Eccellenza, Promozione, and Prima, Seconda, and Terza Categoria — are administered by the Lega Nazionale Dilettanti and regional committees.

History

The first Italian championship was played in 1898. A single national top division, Serie A, was established in the 1929–30 season. The league system also feeds the national cup competitions, the Coppa Italia and the Supercoppa Italiana.
