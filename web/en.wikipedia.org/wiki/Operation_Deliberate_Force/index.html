Operation Deliberate Force

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Date30 August – 20 September 1995
LocationRepublika Srpska, Bosnia and Herzegovina
BelligerentsNATO vs. Army of Republika Srpska
ResultNATO victory; Bosnian Serb forces brought to negotiations, leading to the Dayton Agreement

Operation Deliberate Force was a sustained air campaign conducted by the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation (NATO), in concert with United Nations Protection Force (UNPROFOR) ground operations, to undermine the military capability of the Army of Republika Srpska (VRS), which had threatened and attacked UN-designated "safe areas" in Bosnia and Herzegovina during the Bosnian War of 1992–95.

Background

Following the fall of Srebrenica and Žepa in July 1995 and the second Markale massacre in Sarajevo on 28 August 1995, in which a mortar round killed 37 civilians, NATO ambassadors authorised large-scale air strikes. The "dual key" arrangement with the UN was streamlined so that strikes could proceed without the delays that had hampered earlier responses.

The campaign

Beginning on 30 August 1995, aircraft from eight NATO nations flew some 3,515 sorties from bases in Italy and from US aircraft carriers in the Adriatic, dropping roughly 1,026 bombs on 338 targets, including command bunkers, ammunition depots, bridges and air-defence sites around Pale, Banja Luka and Sarajevo. One French Mirage 2000 was shot down by a surface-to-air missile; its two crewmen were captured and held until December. Simultaneously, the UN Rapid Reaction Force artillery on Mount Igman shelled Bosnian Serb positions around Sarajevo, while Croat and Bosniak ground offensives in western Bosnia recovered large areas of territory.

Outcome

The bombing was suspended on 14 September 1995 after the Bosnian Serbs agreed to withdraw heavy weapons from the Sarajevo exclusion zone and lift the siege of the city. Deliberate Force, combined with ground offensives, shifted the military balance and compelled the parties to negotiate, culminating in the Dayton Agreement of November 1995, which ended the Bosnian War.
