List of natural gas fields

This is a list of the world's largest natural gas fields, ranked by estimated recoverable reserves. The largest by far is the shared Iranian–Qatari South Pars / North Dome field in the Persian Gulf.

Notes

Reserve figures are estimates that vary by source and by whether shale gas and associated gas are counted. Giant conventional fields were mostly discovered in the mid-20th century; recent growth has come from shale plays and deepwater discoveries.
