Duke of Grafton is a title in the Peerage of England, first created in 1675 by Charles II for Henry FitzRoy, his illegitimate son by Barbara Villiers, Countess of Castlemaine. The family seat is Euston Hall in Suffolk.

History

The first Duke was killed at the siege of Cork in 1690. The most prominent holder was Augustus FitzRoy, 3rd Duke of Grafton, who served as Prime Minister of Great Britain from 1768 to 1770, one of the youngest men to hold the office. The 2nd Duke was a notable courtier and patron; the family name FitzRoy means "son of the king".

The current holder is Henry FitzRoy, 12th Duke of Grafton, who succeeded in 2011 and is a music promoter credited with reviving the family's interest in staging concerts at Euston Hall.
