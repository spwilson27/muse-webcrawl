Mark Getty

Mark Harris Getty (born 9 July 1960) is an American businessman and a member of the Getty family. He is the co-founder and chairman of Getty Images, the world’s largest supplier of stock photography and other visual content, which he established in 1995 with Jonathan Klein.

Life and career

Getty is the youngest son of J. Paul Getty Jr. and grandson of oil tycoon J. Paul Getty. Educated in England and at Oxford University, he worked in finance in London and New York before founding Getty Images in London. Recognising that photo licensing would move online, the company pioneered the digitisation of picture archives, acquired rival agencies including Tony Stone Images, and grew into the dominant picture agency before going private in an acquisition by the Getty family and Carlyle Group in 2012.

Other interests

Getty is a former chairman of the trustees of the National Gallery in London and has been involved in philanthropy through the Getty family trusts. He is a keen cricket supporter and was awarded an honorary knighthood (KBE) for services to the arts and philanthropy.
