Carolina Power & Light (CP&L) was an electric utility company headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina, serving customers across the Carolinas. It grew out of early hydroelectric and streetcar power ventures and by the late twentieth century operated major coal and nuclear generating stations.

History

The company was formed in 1908 from the merger of several central North Carolina power concerns. CP&L built a series of hydroelectric dams and later constructed the Brunswick and Harris nuclear power plants. In 2000 it became the principal subsidiary of Progress Energy; after Duke Energy acquired Progress Energy in 2012, CP&L's service territory was rebranded as Duke Energy Progress.
