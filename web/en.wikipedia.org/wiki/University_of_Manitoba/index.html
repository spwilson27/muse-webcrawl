The University of Manitoba (U of M) is a public research university in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Founded in 1877, it was Western Canada's first university and is the province's largest, with about 35,000 students across the Fort Garry and Bannatyne campuses.

History and research

The university began by conferring degrees on students of its founding colleges and moved to its present Fort Garry site along the Red River in the early 20th century. It is a member of the U15 research group, with strengths in infectious disease research (in association with the nearby National Microbiology Laboratory), Arctic science, and agriculture.

Athletics

The Manitoba Bisons compete in U Sports; the football team has won the Vanier Cup three times, most recently in 2007.
