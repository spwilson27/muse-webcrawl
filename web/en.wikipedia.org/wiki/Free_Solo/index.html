Free Solo

Free Solo
2018 documentary film
Directed by Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi and Jimmy Chin
Starring Alex Honnold
Running time: 100 minutes

Free Solo is a 2018 American documentary film directed by Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi and Jimmy Chin that profiles rock climber Alex Honnold on his quest to perform the first-ever free solo climb — a climb without ropes or safety equipment — of the 900-metre (3,000 ft) vertical rock face of El Capitan in Yosemite National Park, California.

Production and climb

The film follows Honnold's preparation over two years, including his training, his relationship with girlfriend Sanni McCandless, and the ethical questions facing the filmmakers about documenting an attempt that could end in death. On 3 June 2017, Honnold completed the ascent of the Freerider route in 3 hours and 56 minutes, a feat widely regarded as one of the greatest athletic achievements of all time.

Reception

Free Solo premiered at the Telluride Film Festival in August 2018 and won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature at the 91st Academy Awards. It also won the BAFTA Award for Best Documentary and grossed over $29 million worldwide. Critics praised its cinematography, tension, and portrait of Honnold's singular focus and psychology.
