Giants Live

Giants Live is a professional strongman competition tour and the official qualifying series for the World's Strongest Man contest. Founded in 2009 after the end of the IFSA-era qualifying structure, Giants Live stages arena shows — principally in the United Kingdom, including long-running events at the First Direct Arena in Leeds and the Royal Albert Hall in London, together with international stops — at which leading strongmen compete in classic events such as the deadlift, log press, axle press, Atlas stones, and vehicle pull.

Format

Each Giants Live show typically features around a dozen invited athletes contesting five or six events in a single day, with the podium finishers earning invitations to the World's Strongest Man. Named events under the Giants Live banner have included Britain's Strongest Man, Europe's Strongest Man, the Strongman Classic, the World Tour Finals, and the World Deadlift Championships, at which the all-time deadlift record has repeatedly been attacked before large arena crowds.

Role in the sport

By packaging strongman as ticketed arena entertainment with live commentary, television production, and fan engagement, Giants Live is credited with much of the sport's growth in popularity during the 2010s and 2020s. Champions of its era include athletes such as Eddie Hall, Thor Björnsson, Martins Licis, Oleksii Novikov, Tom Stoltman, and Mitchell Hooper, several of whom went on to win World's Strongest Man titles secured through the tour.
