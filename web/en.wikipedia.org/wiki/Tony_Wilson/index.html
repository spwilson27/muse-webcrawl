Tony Wilson

Anthony Howard Wilson (20 February 1950 – 10 August 2007) was an English record label owner, radio and television presenter, nightclub manager, and journalist. He was a central figure in the Manchester music scene.

Wilson, a Cambridge graduate and Granada Television presenter, co-founded Factory Records in 1978, the label that released Joy Division, New Order, and Happy Mondays. Factory was famous for its artistic idealism, distinctive Peter Saville artwork, and catalogue numbers applied to everything from posters to the label's office cat. Wilson also co-owned the Haçienda nightclub, which became the epicentre of the late-1980s "Madchester" and acid house scenes, though it lost money persistently.

Legacy

Wilson promoted Manchester relentlessly and is credited with helping transform the city's cultural identity. He was played by Steve Coogan in the 2002 film 24 Hour Party People. He died of a heart attack in 2007 while being treated for kidney cancer.
