9-Euro-Ticket

Country: Germany

Valid: June, July and August 2022

Price: €9 per calendar month

Scope: Unlimited local and regional public transport nationwide

Tickets sold: about 52 million

Successor: Deutschlandticket (€49/month, from May 2023)

The 9-Euro-Ticket was a German public transport pass sold during June, July, and August 2022. For €9 per calendar month it allowed unlimited travel across Germany on local and regional public transport — buses, trams, U-Bahn and S-Bahn systems, and regional trains — but not on long-distance trains such as ICE, IC and EC services. It was introduced by the federal government as part of a relief package responding to the sharp rise in energy and fuel prices that followed Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

Impact

About 52 million tickets were sold over the three months, in addition to some 10 million existing season-ticket holders who received the discount automatically. Studies estimated that the ticket saved around 1.8 million tonnes of CO2 emissions, with roughly one in five trips replacing a car journey, and regional trains to tourist destinations were often crowded. The experiment significantly boosted the popularity of flat-rate public transport fares.

Successor

After months of political negotiation over funding, the 9-Euro-Ticket was succeeded in May 2023 by the permanent Deutschlandticket, initially priced at €49 per month, covering the same local and regional services as a subscription.
