Critérium du Dauphiné

The Critérium du Dauphiné is an annual professional road cycling stage race held in the Dauphiné region of southeastern France each June. First run in 1947 and organised by the newspaper Le Dauphiné libéré before passing to the Amaury Sport Organisation, it is part of the UCI World Tour.

Significance

Taking place a few weeks before the Tour de France over mountainous terrain in the French Alps, the week-long race is widely used by Tour contenders as their final preparation event.

Notable winners

The race has been won by many of cycling's greats, including Jacques Anquetil, Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault, Miguel Induráin, and Chris Froome. Nello Lauredi, Luis Ocaña, Charly Mottet, and Froome are among the riders with three victories; Primož Roglič and Jonas Vingegaard are recent winners.
