Moody chart

The Moody chart, or Moody diagram, is a graph in fluid mechanics plotting the Darcy–Weisbach friction factor against the Reynolds number for various relative roughnesses of pipe. It allows engineers to determine the pressure drop or flow rate in a pipe from the pipe's dimensions, the fluid properties, and the surface roughness.

History

Lewis Ferry Moody published the chart in 1944, consolidating experimental data (notably that of Johann Nikuradse) and the theoretical laminar-flow relation f = 64/Re, together with the Colebrook–White equation for turbulent flow. The chart spans laminar, transitional, and fully turbulent regimes and remains a standard tool in mechanical and chemical engineering teaching and practice, though it is often replaced by direct solution of the Colebrook equation in computer calculations.
