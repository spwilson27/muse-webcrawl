2019 United Kingdom general election

The 2019 United Kingdom general election was held on 12 December 2019. The Conservative Party under Boris Johnson won a landslide majority of 80 seats with the slogan 'Get Brexit Done', defeating Jeremy Corbyn's Labour Party.


Date: 12 December 2019
Conservatives: 365 seats, 43.6%
Labour: 202 seats, 32.1%
SNP: 48 seats
Turnout: 67.3%

Campaign and results

Called to break parliamentary deadlock over Brexit, the election saw the Conservatives win many traditionally Labour seats in the Midlands and northern England — the so-called 'red wall'. Labour recorded its worst seat total since 1935; Corbyn announced he would step down. The SNP won 48 of 59 Scottish seats.

Aftermath

Johnson's majority enabled the UK to leave the European Union on 31 January 2020 under the revised Withdrawal Agreement.
