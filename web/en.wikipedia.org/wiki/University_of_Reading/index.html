University of Reading

The University of Reading is a public research university in Reading, Berkshire, England. It originated in 1892 as University College, Reading, an extension college of Oxford, and received its royal charter in 1926 — the only university created in Britain between the two world wars.

Campus and academics

Its main Whiteknights campus, set in parkland around a lake, hosts most teaching; other sites include London Road and Henley Business School at Greenlands. Reading enrolls around 19,000 students and is noted for meteorology, agriculture, food science, real estate and typography.

Collections

The university houses the Museum of English Rural Life and the Beckett archive of Samuel Beckett materials.
