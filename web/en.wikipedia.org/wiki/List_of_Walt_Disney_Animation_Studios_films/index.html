List of Walt Disney Animation Studios films

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Walt Disney Animation Studios, headquartered in Burbank, California, is the oldest-running animation studio in the world. Its official canon of animated features, often called the "Disney Animated Canon", began with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs in 1937. The studio's films are traditionally released under the Walt Disney Pictures banner.
