List of endangered species in the Philippines

The List of endangered species in the Philippines covers the threatened fauna and flora of one of the world's most biodiverse archipelagos. The Philippines is recognised as a megadiverse country and a global biodiversity hotspot, with very high endemism; it is also among the most threatened, owing to deforestation, hunting, and the illegal wildlife trade.

Conservation

Philippine law protects threatened species under the Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act of 2001. Captive breeding of the Philippine eagle at Davao, reintroduction of the Philippine crocodile in northern Luzon, and community-managed marine sanctuaries are prominent conservation efforts.
