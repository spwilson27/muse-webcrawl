"Hotel California" is a song by the American rock band the Eagles, released as a single in February 1977 and the title track of their album Hotel California. Written by Don Felder, Don Henley and Glenn Frey, it is one of the best-known songs of the album-oriented rock era, famous for its long guitar coda featuring a duet between Felder and Joe Walsh.

Composition and recording

Felder composed the music as an instrumental demo — a progression in B minor influenced by reggae and Mexican music — recorded at his beach house. Henley and Frey wrote the lyrics, describing a weary traveller who stops at a luxurious hotel that turns out to be impossible to leave: "You can check out any time you like, but you can never leave." The song's ending features an extended sequence of guitar harmonies played by Felder and Walsh over an arpeggiated pattern in 12/8 time.

Reception and legacy

The single topped the Billboard Hot 100 for one week in May 1977 and won the Grammy Award for Record of the Year at the 20th Grammy Awards. It has been certified platinum and consistently appears near the top of greatest-song polls; its guitar solo was voted the best of all time by readers of Guitarist magazine in 1998. The song was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame and is one of the songs selected for the Library of Congress's National Recording Registry discussions of culturally significant recordings. The Eagles' live acoustic reworking on the 1994 reunion album Hell Freezes Over introduced it to a new generation.

Interpretation

The lyrics have inspired numerous interpretations, including readings about hedonism and excess in the Los Angeles music industry, the loss of innocence in the American dream, and — in persistent urban legends the band has repeatedly denied — occult or satanic meanings. Henley described the song as being about "the underbelly of the American dream" and the dark side of the California lifestyle the band had experienced.

Personnel

Don Henley (lead vocals, drums), Glenn Frey (backing vocals, rhythm guitar), Don Felder (lead guitar, backing vocals), Joe Walsh (lead guitar, organ), Randy Meisner (bass, backing vocals).
