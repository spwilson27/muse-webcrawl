Isuna Hasekura

Isuna Hasekura (支倉 凍砂, Hasekura Isuna, born December 27, 1982) is a Japanese author. He is best known for the light novel series Spice and Wolf (Ōkami to Kōshinryō), which combines fantasy with medieval economics and trade.

Career

Hasekura, from Chiba Prefecture, studied physics at Rikkyo University. In 2005 he entered the first volume of Spice and Wolf in ASCII Media Works' 12th Dengeki Novel Prize, where it won the Silver Prize. The series was published under the Dengeki Bunko imprint from 2006 to 2011, running to 17 volumes, and inspired manga, anime, and video game adaptations; a sequel series, Wolf and Parchment: New Theory Spice & Wolf, began in 2016. Hasekura's interest in economics — a recurring subject in his work — came in part from reading and investing during his university years.

Other works

Hasekura also wrote the science fiction novel Billionaire Girl and the visual novel series World End Economica, developed with his doujin circle Spicy Tails; the latter, an economic thriller set on the Moon, was released in episodes from 2011 and later published internationally. He has also contributed scenarios to other visual novels.
