British Columbia Coast

[Image placeholder: British Columbia Coast]

The British Columbia Coast, popularly called the BC Coast, is the westernmost part of the Canadian province of British Columbia, comprising the mainland coast along the Pacific Ocean and the province's islands, including Vancouver Island and Haida Gwaii. The geography is dominated by the rugged fiords and inlets of the Coast Mountains and by the Inside Passage shipping route.

Geography and communities

The coast stretches roughly 25,000 km of shoreline including its maze of inlets such as Howe Sound, Burrard Inlet, Bute Inlet and Knight Inlet. Major communities include Vancouver, Victoria, Nanaimo, Prince Rupert, Kitimat and Campbell River. The region's temperate rainforest, salmon rivers and ferry-dependent coastal towns are defining features, as is the resource economy of forestry, fishing and port trade.
