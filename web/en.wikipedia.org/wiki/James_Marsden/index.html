James Marsden

Born: September 18, 1973, Stillwater, Oklahoma, U.S.

Occupation: Actor, singer

James Paul Marsden (born September 18, 1973) is an American actor known for his roles in both film and television, including Cyclops in the X-Men film series, Prince Edward in Enchanted, and the dual role of Teddy Flood in the HBO series Westworld.

Early life and career

Marsden grew up in Oklahoma and studied broadcast journalism at Oklahoma State University before moving to Los Angeles. He gained early attention in television roles and made his feature film debut in the 1990s.

Film and television

Marsden played Cyclops (Scott Summers) in X-Men (2000) and its sequels, and appeared in major films including Superman Returns (2006), Hairspray (2007), Enchanted (2007), 27 Dresses (2008), The Notebook (2004), and Sonic the Hedgehog (2020). On television he starred in Westworld (2016–2022) and Dead to Me (2019–2022), earning critical acclaim and a Golden Globe nomination for the latter.

Music

Marsden is also a singer, having performed several musical numbers in Hairspray and Enchanted, and has appeared in music videos.
