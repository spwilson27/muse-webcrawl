Come Dancing

Come Dancing is a British ballroom dancing competition show that ran on BBC television from 1950 to 1998. It was one of the longest-running programmes on British television and a forerunner of the modern dance competition format.

Format

Teams of amateur formation dancers from regions of the United Kingdom competed against each other in ballroom and Latin dances, with professional demonstrations. The programme popularised ballroom dancing in post-war Britain.

Legacy

Come Dancing inspired the BBC's Strictly Come Dancing (2004–), which pairs celebrities with professional dancers and became one of the most successful entertainment formats in the world.
