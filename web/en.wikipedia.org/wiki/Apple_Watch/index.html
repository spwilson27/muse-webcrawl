The Apple Watch is a line of smartwatches produced by Apple Inc. It incorporates fitness tracking, health-oriented capabilities, and wireless telecommunication, and integrates with watchOS and other Apple products and services. The first Apple Watch was announced by Tim Cook on September 9, 2014, and released on April 24, 2015, quickly becoming the best-selling wearable device worldwide; Apple has remained the largest smartwatch maker by shipments, and the Watch is credited with popularising the modern smartwatch category.

The Activity rings — Move, Exercise, and Stand — are central to Apple Watch's fitness tracking.

Features

Successive models added GPS, cellular connectivity (Series 3, 2017), an always-on display (Series 5), and larger edge-to-edge screens (Series 7 and later). Health sensors have progressively expanded: heart-rate monitoring and irregular-rhythm notifications, an ECG app (Series 4), blood-oxygen sensing (Series 6), skin temperature sensing for cycle tracking (Series 8), and sleep-apnoea notifications (2024). Safety features include fall detection, Emergency SOS, crash detection, and international emergency calling. The rugged Apple Watch Ultra line, introduced in 2022, targets endurance athletes with a larger titanium case, longer battery life, a programmable Action button, and a depth gauge and dive computer functions. The budget Apple Watch SE line omits some sensors at a lower price.

Software

watchOS receives annual updates alongside iOS. The watch supports apps from the App Store, Apple Pay contactless payment, Siri, Walkie-Talkie messaging, and unlocking paired Macs and iPhones. Setup and most management occur through the Watch app on iPhone, although cellular models can operate independently for calls, messages, and streaming.

Health research and disputes

Apple has partnered with academic institutions on large-scale studies (the Apple Heart and Movement Study among them) using Watch data. The product has also been involved in patent disputes: in December 2023, an International Trade Commission import ban over pulse-oximetry patents held by Masimo forced Apple to pause U.S. sales of Series 9 and Ultra 2 briefly, after which the models returned with the blood-oxygen feature disabled in the United States pending litigation and redesign.
