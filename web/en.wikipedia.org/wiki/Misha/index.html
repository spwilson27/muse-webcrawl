Misha

Misha (Russian: Миша) is a diminutive of the Russian male name Mikhail (Michael), and is also used as a name in its own right. Because the word for bear in Russian folklore is often affectionately associated with the name, "Misha" is also a common nickname for bears.

Mascot

The best-known bearer is Misha the Bear, the mascot of the 1980 Summer Olympics in Moscow, designed by illustrator Victor Chizhikov. Depicted as a smiling bear cub wearing a weightlifter's belt with the Olympic rings, Misha became one of the most commercially successful Olympic mascots; a large figure of Misha famously "flew" away carried by balloons at the closing ceremony.

Other uses

Misha is also the name of a Dutch singer (Misha Angèle), characters in anime and manga, and a given name in several Slavic and non-Slavic countries.
