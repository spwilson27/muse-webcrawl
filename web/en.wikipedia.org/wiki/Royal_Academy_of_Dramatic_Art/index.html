Royal Academy of Dramatic Art

The Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (RADA) is a drama school in London, England. Founded in 1904 by Sir Herbert Beerbohm Tree, it is one of the world's most famous acting schools.

Training

RADA offers a three-year BA in Acting and courses in theatre production, directing and technical theatre, based in Bloomsbury with theatres including the Vanbrugh and the GBS Theatre. Admission is by audition and highly competitive.

Alumni

Graduates include John Gielgud, Vivien Leigh, Anthony Hopkins, Alan Rickman, Fiona Shaw and Phoebe Waller-Bridge.
