Laci Denise Peterson (née Rocha; May 4, 1975 – c. December 24, 2002) was an American woman from Modesto, California, who was murdered while eight months pregnant. Her disappearance on Christmas Eve 2002 and the discovery of her body in San Francisco Bay in April 2003 became one of the most widely covered criminal cases in the United States.

Disappearance and investigation

Laci Peterson was reported missing from the home she shared with her husband Scott Peterson on December 24, 2002. Scott said he had gone fishing at the Berkeley Marina that morning. As the investigation proceeded, police learned he had been having an affair and had taken out a life insurance policy on his wife. In April 2003 the remains of a male fetus and of a woman washed ashore near Point Isabel in San Francisco Bay, close to where Scott said he had fished; DNA testing identified them as Laci and her unborn son, Conner.

Trial and aftermath

Scott Peterson was arrested on April 18, 2003, and tried in Redwood City in 2004. He was convicted of first-degree murder of Laci and second-degree murder of Conner and sentenced to death. His death sentence was overturned by the California Supreme Court in 2020, and in 2021 he was resentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole. The case spurred passage in 2004 of the federal Unborn Victims of Violence Act, also called "Laci and Conner's Law."
