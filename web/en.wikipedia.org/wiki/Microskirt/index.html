Microskirt

A microskirt, or micro-mini skirt, is an extremely short miniskirt, generally with a hemline well above mid-thigh and considerably shorter than a conventional miniskirt.

History

The miniskirt rose to prominence in the 1960s through designers such as Mary Quant. Even shorter microskirts appeared in later fashion revivals, notably in Japanese street fashion in the 1990s and in low-rise styles of the early 2000s.

In popular culture

Microskirts feature prominently in fashion photography, pop music costuming and school-uniform-inspired styles depicted in media, and have periodically prompted dress-code debates in schools and workplaces.
