Chemours

The Chemours Company is an American chemical company founded in 2015 as a spin-off of DuPont's performance chemicals division. Headquartered in Wilmington, Delaware, it is the world's largest producer of titanium dioxide (Ti-Pure) and a major manufacturer of fluoroproducts, including Teflon coatings and Opteon refrigerants, and other chemical solutions.

History

DuPont announced the spin-off in 2013 and completed it on 1 July 2015, distributing Chemours shares to DuPont shareholders. The new company inherited DuPont's performance chemicals businesses together with a number of environmental liabilities relating to historic sites.

Environmental issues

Chemours has faced litigation and regulatory scrutiny over per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), notably GenX chemicals discharged from its Fayetteville Works plant in North Carolina into the Cape Fear River, and PFOA contamination around its Washington Works plant near Parkersburg, West Virginia — a legacy of DuPont's manufacture there that prompted settlements and consent orders, including a 2019 consent order in North Carolina requiring extensive remediation and emissions controls.
