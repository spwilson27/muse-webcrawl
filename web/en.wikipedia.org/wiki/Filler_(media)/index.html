Filler (media)

In media, filler is material of lower cost or quality produced to occupy a set length or space. The term is most commonly used for television episodes—especially in anime—that are not adapted from source material, and for content added to albums, newspapers, or broadcasts to fill an allotted slot.

Television and anime

When an anime adaptation catches up with its ongoing manga source, studios may produce filler episodes or entire filler arcs with original storylines so the manga can advance. Such episodes are often criticised by fans for lower quality and for stalling the main plot, though they can also provide character development or comedy. Long-running series such as Naruto, Bleach, and One Piece are well known for substantial filler content.

Other media

In music, a "filler track" is a song included to complete an album's length. In newspapers and magazines, filler items are short pieces used to occupy leftover space. In radio and television broadcasting, filler programming fills gaps in schedules.
