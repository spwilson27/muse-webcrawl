Hawkei

The Hawkei is an Australian light four-wheel-drive protected mobility vehicle developed by Thales Australia for the Australian Defence Force. Named after Leiopython albertisii, a python species (in turn named for naturalist Luigi D'Albertis), the vehicle is a seven-tonne, air-transportable platform designed for command, liaison, reconnaissance, and utility roles.

Development

Under the LAND 121 Phase 4 project, Australia selected the Hawkei in 2015 to replace part of the Land Rover fleet, ordering 1,100 vehicles and trailers. The vehicle offers high levels of blast and ballistic protection for its size and can be carried underslung by a CH-47 Chinook helicopter.

Service

Deliveries began in the late 2010s, though early production was slowed by reliability and driveline issues that delayed full-rate production. The Hawkei is built at Thales's Bendigo facility in Victoria. Australia later supplied Hawkei vehicles to Ukraine following the 2022 Russian invasion.
