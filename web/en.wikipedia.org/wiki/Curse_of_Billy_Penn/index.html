Curse of Billy Penn

The Curse of Billy Penn was a sports curse associated with the city of Philadelphia. From 1987 to 2008, no major professional sports team based in Philadelphia won a championship, a drought popularly attributed to a violation of the tradition that no building in the city should rise taller than the statue of William Penn atop City Hall.

Origin

For decades, a "gentlemen's agreement" kept Philadelphia buildings below the 548-foot height of City Hall, topped by Alexander Milne Calder's statue of Penn. In March 1987, One Liberty Place became the first building to exceed that height. The city's four major teams subsequently endured a long championship drought despite reaching finals.

Breaking the curse

When the Comcast Center was completed in 2007, ironworkers placed a small figurine of William Penn on its highest beam, restoring Penn as the highest figure in the city. The Philadelphia Phillies won the 2008 World Series the following year, and the curse was said to be broken. The tradition was repeated atop the Comcast Technology Center, completed before the Eagles won Super Bowl LII in 2018.
