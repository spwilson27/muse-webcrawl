Municipality (Israel)

In Israel, a municipality (Hebrew: עירייה, iriya) is a local government authority governing a city or large town. Municipal status is granted by the Minister of the Interior to localities that usually have more than 20,000 residents and urban characteristics.

Below municipalities in size are local councils governing smaller towns, and regional councils governing groups of rural communities. As of recent counts Israel has around 75 municipalities, including Jerusalem, Tel Aviv-Yafo, Haifa, Beersheba and Rishon LeZion.

Responsibilities

Municipalities are responsible for local planning, roads, water, sanitation, education administration, cultural services and emergency preparedness. They are led by a directly elected mayor and an elected council, with elections held every five years.
