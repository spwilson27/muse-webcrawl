Welsh Government

SeatCardiff
HeadFirst Minister of Wales

The Welsh Government (Welsh: Llywodraeth Cymru) is the devolved government of Wales, comprising the First Minister, cabinet ministers and counsel general, accountable to the Senedd (Welsh Parliament).

History

Devolution followed the 1997 referendum; the National Assembly for Wales opened in 1999. The Government of Wales Act 2006 formally separated the executive from the legislature, and the 2011 referendum granted primary law-making powers in devolved areas. Reserved powers remain with the UK Government at Westminster.

Responsibilities

Health (NHS Wales), education, local government, transport, environment, agriculture, culture and the Welsh language, with partial income tax powers since 2019. Labour has led every Welsh Government; recent first ministers include Mark Drakeford and, from 2024, Eluned Morgan (after Vaughan Gething's brief tenure).
