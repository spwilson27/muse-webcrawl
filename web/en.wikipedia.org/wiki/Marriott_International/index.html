Marriott International

Marriott International, Inc. is an American multinational hospitality company headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland. It is the world's largest hotel chain, operating, franchising, and licensing some 30+ brands including Marriott, Ritz-Carlton, Sheraton, and Westin.

Founded1927, Washington, D.C.

History

Founded as a root beer stand in 1927 by J. Willard and Alice Marriott, the company opened its first hotel in 1957. It split in 1993, acquired Renaissance and Ritz-Carlton interests, and in 2016 acquired Starwood Hotels in a deal creating the largest hotel company, with thousands of properties in over 130 countries.

Brands and loyalty

Brands span luxury (Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, W), premium (Marriott, Sheraton, Delta), and select service (Courtyard, Fairfield). The Marriott Bonvoy programme, launched 2019, unified earlier loyalty schemes.
