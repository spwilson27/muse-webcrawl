Industrial Light & Magic
Founded: May 26, 1975, by George Lucas
Headquarters: Letterman Digital Arts Center, San Francisco, California
Industry: Visual effects, CGI

Industrial Light & Magic (ILM) is an American motion picture visual effects company founded by George Lucas in 1975 to create the special effects for Star Wars, at a time when no existing facility could realize the required shots. It is a division of Lucasfilm, itself a subsidiary of Walt Disney Studios since 2012.

Achievements

ILM has pioneered numerous techniques in visual effects: the Dykstraflex motion-control camera system, digital compositing, computer-generated characters, and photorealistic CGI, with landmark work on The Empire Strikes Back, Raiders of the Lost Ark, E.T., Terminator 2: Judgment Day, Jurassic Park, Pirates of the Caribbean, and the Star Wars and Marvel films. Its artists have won numerous Academy Awards for Best Visual Effects; notable figures include John Dykstra, Dennis Muren, Ken Ralston, and John Knoll, creator of Photoshop's precursor work and of the StageCraft virtual production system used on The Mandalorian.

Studios

In addition to its San Francisco headquarters, ILM operates studios in Vancouver, London, Singapore, Sydney, and Mumbai, and produces effects for film, television, and themed entertainment.
