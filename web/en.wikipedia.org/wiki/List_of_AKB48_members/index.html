List of AKB48 members

AKB48 is a Japanese idol girl group named after the Akihabara area in Tokyo, where the group's theater is located. The group has expanded to include over 100 members at its peak, divided among several teams (Team A, Team K, Team B, and Team 4, among others) and a pool of trainees (kenkyūsei). Members are recruited through auditions, held roughly every year, and each recruiting round is called a "generation". Members who leave the group are said to "graduate".

Notable members and former members

  • Yasushi Akimoto — producer and lyricist of the group.
  • Atsuko Maeda — first-generation member and longtime "centre" of the group; graduated in 2012.
  • Yuko Oshima — first-generation member of Team K; won the 2010 and 2012 general elections; graduated in 2014.
  • Minami Takahashi — first general manager (sōkantoku) of the AKB48 Group; graduated in 2016.
  • Rino Sashihara — later a member of HKT48; won the general election four times.
  • Sayaka Yamamoto — captain of NMB48's Team N and concurrent member of AKB48's Team K.
  • Jurina Matsui — core member of SKE48 who held concurrent positions in AKB48.
  • Sakura Miyawaki — HKT48 member with concurrent AKB48 membership; later a member of Iz*One and Le Sserafim.

Structure

Members are organised into teams that perform rotating stage shows at the AKB48 Theater in Akihabara. Trainees may be promoted to full team membership when vacancies arise. Sister groups — SKE48 (Nagoya), NMB48 (Osaka), HKT48 (Fukuoka), NGT48 (Niigata), and STU48 (Setouchi), as well as overseas groups such as JKT48 in Jakarta and BNK48 in Bangkok — maintain their own member lists, and members sometimes hold concurrent memberships or transfer between groups.

General election

Until 2018, the group's annual Senbatsu General Election allowed fans to vote for the members who would perform on the group's next single, making the member rankings a major public event in Japanese popular culture.
