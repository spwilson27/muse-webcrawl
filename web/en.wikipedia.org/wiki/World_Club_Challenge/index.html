World Club Challenge

The World Club Challenge is an annual rugby league match between the winners of the Australasian National Rugby League (NRL) and the European Super League, deciding the world's best club side.

History

First played in 1976 between Eastern Suburbs and St Helens, the concept became regular from 1987. The 1997 edition expanded controversially to a multi-club tournament during the Super League war. British clubs dominated early editions; NRL clubs have prevailed more often since the 2010s.

Records

Wigan Warriors are among the most successful clubs in the competition. Sydney Roosters, Melbourne Storm, Leeds Rhinos and St Helens have also won multiple titles. Matches are usually held in February, in England or Australia.
