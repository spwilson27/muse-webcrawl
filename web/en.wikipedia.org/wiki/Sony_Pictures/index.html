Sony Pictures Entertainment Inc. (commonly known as Sony Pictures or SPE) is an American diversified multinational mass media and entertainment studio conglomerate that produces, acquires, and distributes filmed entertainment (theatrical motion pictures, television programs, and recorded videos) through its many subsidiaries. Based at the Sony Pictures Studios lot in Culver City, California, it is a division of Sony Entertainment, itself a subsidiary of the Japanese conglomerate Sony Group Corporation.

History

Sony entered Hollywood in 1989, purchasing Columbia Pictures Entertainment from Coca-Cola for about $3.4 billion, and acquired the historic MGM studio lot in Culver City. The company was renamed Sony Pictures Entertainment in 1991. Under successive managements it operated the Columbia and TriStar labels, rebuilt Screen Gems as a genre label, and built major franchises including Spider-Man (produced with Marvel), Men in Black, Jumanji, Ghostbusters, and the James Bond films (distributed in partnership with MGM/Amazon). In November 2014 the studio suffered a major cyberattack, attributed by the FBI to North Korea and linked to the comedy The Interview, which leaked unreleased films, executives' emails, and personal data.

Business units

The Motion Picture Group includes Columbia Pictures, TriStar Pictures, Screen Gems, Sony Pictures Classics, Sony Pictures Animation (producer of the Spider-Verse films), Stage 6 Films, and Affirm Films. Sony Pictures Television is one of the largest US television producers and distributors, with shows such as Breaking Bad, The Crown, and Jeopardy!, which it distributes. Sony also owns the anime streaming service Crunchyroll (acquired from AT&T in 2021) and operates international networks and the premium cable channels formerly under Sony Pictures Television Networks.
