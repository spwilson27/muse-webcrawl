CBS News is the news division of the American broadcaster CBS, heir to one of the great traditions of broadcast journalism: Edward R. Murrow's wartime and See It Now broadcasts, Walter Cronkite's tenure as 'the most trusted man in America', and the newsmagazine 60 Minutes, on air since 1968.

History

CBS built its reputation under news president Fred Friendly and Murrow, whose 1954 report on Senator Joseph McCarthy is a landmark of television journalism. Cronkite anchored the CBS Evening News from 1962 to 1981; Dan Rather and Katie Couric followed in later eras.

Programmes and digital

Flagships include the CBS Evening News, CBS Mornings, Face the Nation, Sunday Morning and 60 Minutes. CBS News runs a 24/7 streaming channel and the cbsnews.com site; the division passed to new ownership arrangements as Paramount changed hands in the mid-2020s.
