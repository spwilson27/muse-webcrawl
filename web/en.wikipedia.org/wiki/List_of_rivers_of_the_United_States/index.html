List of rivers of the United States

The United States has more than 250,000 rivers, totalling about 5.6 million kilometres of waterways. The longest is the Missouri, slightly longer than the Mississippi into which it flows; together the Mississippi–Missouri system is one of the largest drainage basins on Earth.
