Delmarva Peninsula

The Delmarva Peninsula is a large peninsula on the East Coast of the United States, occupied by the state of Delaware and parts of Maryland and Virginia (the Eastern Shore). Its name combines syllables of the three states. It is bordered by the Chesapeake Bay to the west and the Delaware Bay, Delaware River and Atlantic Ocean to the east.

Geography and economy

The peninsula is about 170 miles long and largely flat and rural. Agriculture — especially poultry farming and corn and soybean production — dominates the economy, alongside beach tourism at resorts such as Ocean City, Maryland, and Rehoboth Beach, Delaware. The Chesapeake Bay Bridge–Tunnel connects its southern tip to the Virginia mainland.
