Even Stevens is an American comedy television series that aired on Disney Channel from June 17, 2000, to June 2, 2003, running for three seasons and 65 episodes. Created by Matt Dearborn, it follows the Stevens family of Sacramento, California, and centres on the rivalry between the overachieving Ren Stevens and her mischievous younger brother Louis, played by Christy Carlson Romano and Shia LaBeouf in his breakout role.

Premise and cast

The series contrasts Ren, a perfectionist student, with Louis, a prank-loving underachiever, alongside their parents Steve and Eileen Stevens and older brother Donnie. Recurring characters include Louis's friends Alan Twitty and Tawny Dean and the eccentric neighbour Beans. The show concluded with The Even Stevens Movie, a television film released in June 2003 in which the family is tricked into appearing on a reality show on a remote island.

Reception

The series won a Daytime Emmy Award and helped launch LaBeouf's film career; it has remained a well-remembered part of Disney Channel's early-2000s programming and is available on Disney+.
