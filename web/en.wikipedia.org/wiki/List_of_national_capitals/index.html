List of national capitals

This is a list of national capitals — the capital cities of the sovereign states of the world. Some states have more than one capital (for example, South Africa has three), some have a capital that is not the seat of government (such as the Netherlands, where Amsterdam is the constitutional capital but The Hague holds the government), and a few states have no official capital at all (Nauru, and Switzerland de facto).

Notes

Planned and purpose-built capitals include Brasília, Canberra, Islamabad, Abuja, Naypyidaw (Myanmar) and Nusantara (Indonesia). Sucre is the constitutional capital of Bolivia while La Paz is the seat of government. Sri Lanka's legislative capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, while Colombo remains the executive and judicial capital.
