Trinity College London (TCL) is an international examination board based in London, England, that offers graded and diploma qualifications in the performing arts, English language, and teaching. It provides examinations in music, drama, dance, and English language in more than 60 countries worldwide.

History

Trinity College London was founded in 1877 as the external examinations board of Trinity College of Music. The college had been established in 1872 by the Reverend Henry George Bonavia Hunt to improve the standard of music teaching and performance, and its external examinations quickly became a principal activity. In its early years the board pioneered the idea of travelling examiners who visited local centres so that candidates did not have to come to London to be assessed.

Over the twentieth century the board expanded from classical music into drama and speech, dance, jazz, rock and pop, and English language assessment. In 2004 the examinations board became a separate charitable company from the music college, which continued as Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance.

Examinations

Music

Trinity's graded music examinations run from Initial Grade to Grade 8 in instruments and singing, followed by diploma qualifications (ATCL, LTCL and FTCL) at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Syllabuses cover classical and jazz instruments, singing, theory of music, and group performance. In 2012 Trinity launched Rock & Pop syllabuses covering vocals, guitar, bass, drums and keyboards, based on well-known popular music repertoire.

Drama and dance

Drama qualifications include graded examinations in acting, speech and drama, musical theatre and performance communication, as well as certificates and diplomas for teachers and performers. Dance examinations are offered under the Trinity brand through syllabuses developed for a range of styles.

English language

Trinity is a major provider of English language qualifications. Its Integrated Skills in English (ISE) examinations assess reading, writing, speaking and listening. The Graded Examinations in Spoken English (GESE) run across 12 grades. Trinity's Secure English Language Tests (SELTs) are approved by UK Visas and Immigration for visa, settlement and citizenship applications.
