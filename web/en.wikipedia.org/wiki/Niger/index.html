Niger

Niger, officially the Republic of Niger, is a landlocked country in West Africa named after the Niger River. It is the largest country in West Africa, mostly in the Sahara and Sahel, bordered by Libya, Chad, Nigeria, Benin, Burkina Faso, Mali and Algeria. Its population is about 27 million — with the world's highest fertility rate — and its capital is Niamey.

Niger is one of the world's poorest and hottest countries, sitting on the front line of desertification and of the Sahel's jihadist insurgency. Under French colonial rule and after independence in 1960, it has had repeated coups — most recently in July 2023, when the military ousted President Mohamed Bazoum, prompting ECOWAS threats and, ultimately, Niger's withdrawal from the bloc with Mali and Burkina Faso (the Alliance of Sahel States) and the expulsion of French and US forces. Uranium from Arlit long tied Niger to France's nuclear industry.
