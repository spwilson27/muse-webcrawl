Clamato

Clamato
Type: Tomato juice blend with clam broth
Introduced: 1969, California
Owner: Keurig Dr Pepper (Mott's)
Key use: Caesar cocktail (Canada), michelada (Mexico)

Clamato is a commercial drink made of reconstituted tomato juice concentrate, spices and dried clam broth — its name a portmanteau of 'clam' and 'tomato'. It was created in California in 1969 and is produced by Mott's.

Uses

Clamato is the essential base of Canada's national cocktail, the Caesar, invented in Calgary in 1969 the same year Clamato launched, combining vodka, Clamato, hot sauce and Worcestershire sauce in a celery-salt-rimmed glass. In Mexico it is widely used in the michelada and seafood cocktails. A beer-and-Clamato mixture is sold as Budweiser Chelada in the United States.

Variants

Variants include Clamato Preparado (with added sauces and spices) and picante versions. Despite the clam content, the seafood flavour is mild, and the drink is not suitable for those with shellfish allergies.
