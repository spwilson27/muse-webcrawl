Trends in International Mathematics and Science Study

The Trends in International Mathematics and Science Study (TIMSS) is a series of international assessments of the mathematics and science knowledge of students around the world. It is conducted by the International Association for the Evaluation of Educational Achievement (IEA) and has been administered every four years since 1995.

Design

TIMSS assesses students in the fourth and eighth grades, measuring achievement against a scale centred at 500. Participating countries and benchmarking entities administer standardised tests along with questionnaires for students, teachers, and school principals about curriculum and learning conditions.

Results

East Asian systems — including Singapore, South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, and Hong Kong — have consistently been among the highest performers, particularly in mathematics. Results are widely used by policymakers to benchmark national education systems. TIMSS is comparable in purpose to the OECD's PISA study, which tests 15-year-olds in reading, mathematics, and science.
