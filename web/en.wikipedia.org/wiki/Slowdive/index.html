Slowdive are an English rock band formed in Reading, Berkshire, in 1989. The band consists of vocalists and guitarists Neil Halstead and Rachel Goswell, guitarist Christian Savill, bassist Nick Chaplin, and drummer Simon Scott. They are regarded as one of the defining acts of the shoegaze genre.

The band signed to Creation Records and released their debut album Just for a Day in 1991. Their second album, Souvlaki (1993), was initially received coolly by the British music press but has since been acclaimed as one of the greatest shoegaze albums of all time. After the more experimental Pygmalion (1995), the band were dropped by Creation and split; Halstead, Goswell and drummer Ian McCutcheon went on to form the country-influenced Mojave 3.

Reunion

Slowdive reformed in 2014 to play festivals including Primavera Sound, and released their self-titled fourth album in 2017 to critical acclaim. Their fifth album, Everything Is Alive (2023), reached the UK top ten. The band's influence on dream pop and later shoegaze revivals has grown steadily since their reunion.
