Nuclear power in India

Nuclear power in India is the country's fifth-largest source of electricity, with over twenty reactors operated by the Nuclear Power Corporation of India at sites including Tarapur, Kudankulam, Kaiga, Kakrapar and Rawatbhata.

Program

India's three-stage programme, devised by Homi Bhabha, aims to use abundant thorium reserves after uranium-fuelled pressurised heavy water reactors and plutonium fast breeders. India remained outside the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty, developing technology largely indigenously, with later cooperation after the 2008 NSG waiver.
