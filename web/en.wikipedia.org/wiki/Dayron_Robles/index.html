Dayron Robles (born 19 November 1986) is a retired Cuban track and field athlete who specialised in the 110 metres hurdles. He won the gold medal at the 2008 Beijing Olympics and set a world record of 12.87 seconds in the event at the Ostrava Golden Spike meeting in June 2008, breaking the previous mark of Liu Xiang. His record stood until Aries Merritt ran 12.80 in 2012.

Career

Robles emerged internationally as a junior, winning silver at the 2004 World Junior Championships, and reached the semi-finals at the 2005 World Championships. He won the 60 metres hurdles silver medal at the 2006 World Indoor Championships in Moscow and steadily lowered his personal best outdoors. The 2008 season was his peak: after winning the world indoor title in Valencia over 60 metres hurdles, he broke Liu Xiang's world record with 12.87 in Ostrava on 12 June 2008, and at the Beijing Olympics he won gold comfortably in 12.93, with Liu injured and absent from the final.

Robles won the world indoor title again in Doha in 2010. At the 2011 World Championships in Daegu he crossed the line first in the 110 metres hurdles final but was disqualified after a protest that he had impeded Liu Xiang with a trailing arm over the final hurdles; the gold medal passed to Jason Richardson. Injuries hampered his later seasons, including the 2012 Olympic Games, where he reached the final but did not finish at his peak. In 2013 he was dropped from the Cuban national team after a dispute with the federation over competing independently, and later returned to international competition in 2017.

Major results

  • 2006 World Indoor Championships – silver, 60 m hurdles
  • 2008 World Indoor Championships – gold, 60 m hurdles
  • 2008 Olympic Games, Beijing – gold, 110 m hurdles
  • 2010 World Indoor Championships – gold, 60 m hurdles
  • 2011 Pan American Games – gold, 110 m hurdles

Robles is considered one of the finest high hurdlers of his era, renowned for his height, speed between the hurdles, and technical hurdling form.
