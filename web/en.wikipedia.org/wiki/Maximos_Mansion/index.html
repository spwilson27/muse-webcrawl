Maximos Mansion

Maximos Mansion (Megaro Maximou)
Location: Herodes Atticus Street, Athens, Greece
Use: Official seat of the Prime Minister of Greece
Built: 1910s–1921

The Maximos Mansion (Greek: Μέγαρο Μαξίμου, Megaro Maximou) is the official seat of the Prime Minister of Greece, located on Herodes Atticus Street in central Athens, adjacent to the Presidential Mansion and the National Garden.

History

The neoclassical building was constructed for the shipowner and politician Dimitrios Maximos, with work completed around 1921 to designs associated with architect Anastasios Metaxas. Maximos, who served as Prime Minister in 1947, bequeathed the mansion to the Greek state. It served various government functions before becoming the prime minister's office in 1982 under Andreas Papandreou; Greek prime ministers work there but generally do not reside in it.

Description

The mansion hosts the prime minister's office, cabinet meetings and official receptions for visiting leaders, and is a frequent backdrop for political announcements in Greek public life.
