Persona, previously marketed outside Japan as Shin Megami Tensei: Persona, is a video game franchise developed and published by Atlus, and a spin-off of the Megami Tensei franchise. Beginning with Revelations: Persona in 1996, the series centres on groups of high-school students who gain the ability to summon Personas — manifestations of their inner psyches — to battle supernatural threats.

Gameplay

From Persona 3 (2006) onward, the games combine dungeon exploration and turn-based combat with a calendar-based life simulation: players attend school, hold part-time jobs and build relationships ("Social Links", later "Confidants"), which in turn strengthen the Personas they can create by fusion in the Velvet Room, presided over by the enigmatic Igor. The series draws heavily on Jungian psychology — persona, shadow, and archetypes — and on tarot symbolism.

Main entries

  • Revelations: Persona (1996)
  • Persona 2: Innocent Sin (1999) and Eternal Punishment (2000)
  • Persona 3 (2006), with FES, Portable and Reload (2024) versions
  • Persona 4 (2008), expanded as Persona 4 Golden (2012)
  • Persona 5 (2016), expanded as Persona 5 Royal (2019)

Reception and legacy

The series is acclaimed for its stylish presentation, music by Shoji Meguro and others, and its treatment of psychological and social themes. It has expanded into anime, films, manga, stage plays and spin-off games including fighting games (Persona 4 Arena), rhythm games and the Persona Q dungeon crawlers.
