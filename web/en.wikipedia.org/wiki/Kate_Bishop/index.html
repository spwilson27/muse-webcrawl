Katherine Bishop is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. A wealthy young socialite who becomes a superhero, she takes up the mantle of Hawkeye as a member of the Young Avengers.

Fictional biography

Kate Bishop was created by writer Allan Heinberg and artist Jim Cheung and first appeared in Young Avengers #1 (2005). Trained in fencing, archery and martial arts, she joins the team despite having no superpowers, and later shares the Hawkeye name with Clint Barton, starring alongside him in the acclaimed Hawkeye series by Matt Fraction and David Aja.

In other media

Hailee Steinfeld portrays Kate Bishop in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in the Disney+ series Hawkeye (2021).
