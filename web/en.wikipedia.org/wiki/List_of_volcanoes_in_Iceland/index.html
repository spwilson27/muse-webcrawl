List of volcanoes in Iceland

Iceland sits astride the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and above a mantle hotspot, making it one of the most volcanically active places on Earth. The island has about 30–32 active volcanic systems and has experienced an eruption on average every four to five years since settlement in the 9th century.

Volcanic zones

Icelandic volcanism is organized into the Reykjanes, Western, Mid-Iceland, Eastern, and Northern Volcanic Zones, plus the Öræfajökull and Snæfellsnes belts. Eruption styles range from effusive fissure eruptions to explosive subglacial eruptions producing jökulhlaups (glacial outburst floods).
