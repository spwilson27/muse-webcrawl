Disney+
Launched: 12 November 2019
Owner: The Walt Disney Company
Type: Subscription streaming (OTT)
Brands: Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, National Geographic
Related: Hulu, Walt Disney Company

Disney+ is an American subscription video on-demand streaming service owned by The Walt Disney Company. It distributes films and television series from Walt Disney Studios and Walt Disney Television.

Content

The service features the libraries and new originals of Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars and National Geographic, including series such as The Mandalorian, WandaVision and Loki. Outside the U.S., the Star hub added general entertainment content in many markets.

Launch and growth

Launching in November 2019, Disney+ gained tens of millions of subscribers in its first year, reshaping Disney toward direct-to-consumer distribution. It introduced ad-supported tiers in 2022 and has progressively integrated Hulu content in the United States.

Availability

Disney+ is available in dozens of countries across the Americas, Europe and Asia-Pacific, on smart TVs, mobile devices, game consoles and the web.
