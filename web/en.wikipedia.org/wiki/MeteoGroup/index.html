MeteoGroup was a private weather forecasting company, one of the largest in Europe, supplying weather services to media, transport, energy, and other industries. It originated in the Netherlands in 1986, when Harry Geurts founded Meteo Consult in Wageningen, initially serving inland shipping and agriculture. The company expanded internationally through offices and acquisitions in the United Kingdom, Germany, Belgium, Sweden, and other countries, and operated the WeatherPro and MeteoEarth consumer brands and apps.

Services

MeteoGroup provided forecasts and presentation systems for broadcasters — in the UK its forecasters appeared on Channel 4 and Channel 5, and it supplied data behind many newspaper and online forecasts. Beyond media, it sold road-weather services to highway authorities, route forecasts for shipping, and wind and solar forecasts for the energy industry.

Ownership

The company was owned for a period by PA Group (parent of the Press Association). In 2018 MeteoGroup was acquired by DTN, a US-based weather and data company. Under DTN the MeteoGroup brand was progressively retired and integrated into DTN's operations in the early 2020s.
