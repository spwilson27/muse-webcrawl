The Rex parade is the signature parade of the New Orleans Mardi Gras, organized by the Rex organization (formally the School of Design). Rex, styled the "King of Carnival", reigns over the celebration, and his parade rolls on Mardi Gras morning (Shrove Tuesday) following the Krewe of Zulu parade, along the St. Charles Avenue route.

History

Rex was founded in 1872, reportedly in part to honour the visit of Grand Duke Alexis Romanoff of Russia during Reconstruction-era Carnival. The organization established many enduring Mardi Gras traditions: the official colours of purple, green and gold (signifying justice, faith and power), the anthem "If Ever I Cease to Love" (associated with the Grand Duke's admiration for the song in a burlesque show), and the King of Carnival institution itself. Rex's monarch issues an official edict closing schools and businesses for the day, and receives the keys to the city from the mayor.

The parade

The parade is known for its artistically crafted floats, built by the School of Design — an organization historically rooted in New Orleans' community of artists and float builders. Signature elements include the Butterfly King float, the Royal Barge, and the tossing of decorated doubloons and beads. The parade ends at the Municipal Auditorium, near where the Rex and Comus balls were traditionally held. The King of Rex is chosen from the organization's membership and his identity is kept secret until Lundi Gras, when he arrives by riverboat.
