Runic Games was an American video game developer based in Seattle, Washington. It was founded in 2008 by former members of Flagship Studios and Blizzard North, including Travis Baldree and Max Schaefer, who had previously worked on the Diablo series and Fate.

History

The studio's first game, Torchlight (2009), was an action role-playing game in the style of Diablo that received critical acclaim and strong sales. Its sequel, Torchlight II (2012), added cooperative multiplayer and is often cited as a key competitor to Diablo III. In 2010 a majority stake in Runic Games was acquired by the Chinese publisher Perfect World.

Runic released its third game, the adventure game Hob, in 2017. In November 2017, Perfect World announced the closure of the studio as part of a restructuring of its publishing business. Several former staff later formed a new studio, Echtra Games, which developed Torchlight III.
