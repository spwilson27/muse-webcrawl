El Tráfico

El Tráfico is a soccer rivalry between the two Major League Soccer clubs based in Los Angeles, California: the LA Galaxy and Los Angeles FC (LAFC). The name, coined by supporters around LAFC's first season in 2018, is a play on "El Clásico" and a nod to Los Angeles's notorious traffic congestion. The derby quickly became one of the most intense and high-scoring rivalries in MLS.

History

The Galaxy, an MLS founding member from 1996 based in Carson, had previously contested the SuperClasico with Chivas USA until that club folded in 2014. LAFC began play in 2018 at Banc of California Stadium (later BMO Stadium) in Exposition Park, and the first El Tráfico, on 31 March 2018, became an instant classic: LAFC led 3–0 before Zlatan Ibrahimović, making his Galaxy debut as a substitute, scored twice — including a spectacular long-range volley — to inspire a 4–3 comeback win. The rivalry has featured frequent multi-goal thrillers, dramatic late winners, and meetings in the MLS Cup Playoffs, including LAFC's 5–3 playoff victory in 2019 and their Western Conference Final win in 2022 on the way to MLS Cup. A 2023 meeting at the Rose Bowl drew an MLS-record standalone crowd of over 82,000.

Supporters

The Galaxy's supporter groups include the LA Riot Squad and Angel City Brigade, while LAFC is backed by the 3252 supporters' union in the stadium's north end. The clubs' stadiums are separated by roughly 20 kilometres (12 miles), making it one of the most geographically compact derbies in the league.
