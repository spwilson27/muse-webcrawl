UL Solutions

UL Solutions Inc. is an American safety science and certification company headquartered in Northbrook, Illinois. Founded in 1894 as Underwriters' Electrical Bureau — an outgrowth of fire-insurance underwriting — it became Underwriters Laboratories, and for most of its history operated as a not-for-profit testing organisation before restructuring in 2012 and becoming a for-profit public company in 2024. Its UL Mark appears on billions of products worldwide.

History

Electrical engineer William Henry Merrill founded the bureau in 1894 after work for insurers examining fire risks at the 1893 Chicago World's Columbian Exposition. Underwriters Laboratories grew into the preeminent American product-safety certification body, developing safety standards through its standards organisation (now UL Standards & Engagement) and testing electrical devices, fire equipment, building materials and countless consumer products.

Operations

UL Solutions provides testing, inspection, certification, auditing, advisory and software services across industries including consumer electronics, energy, life safety, building products and cybersecurity. It operates laboratories in dozens of countries and employs more than 15,000 people. The familiar circular UL certification mark indicates that representative samples of a product were tested against applicable safety requirements, with continuing factory follow-up inspections.
