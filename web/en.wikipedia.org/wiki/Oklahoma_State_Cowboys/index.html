Oklahoma State Cowboys and Cowgirls

The Oklahoma State Cowboys and Cowgirls are the varsity intercollegiate athletic teams of Oklahoma State University in Stillwater, Oklahoma. The men's teams are called the Cowboys and the women's teams the Cowgirls. Oklahoma State competes in NCAA Division I as a member of the Big 12 Conference.

Sports and achievements

The university is especially renowned for its wrestling program, which has won more NCAA team championships than any other school, and for men's golf, which has produced numerous national titles and professional stars. The football team plays at Boone Pickens Stadium, and basketball teams play at Gallagher-Iba Arena.

Rivalries

The Cowboys' principal rivalry is the Bedlam Series against the University of Oklahoma, contested across all sports.
