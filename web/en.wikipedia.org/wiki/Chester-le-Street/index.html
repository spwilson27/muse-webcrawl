Chester-le-Street

Chester-le-Street is a market town in County Durham, England, around 11 kilometres south of Newcastle upon Tyne and a similar distance north of Durham. Its name refers to the Roman fort of Concangis ("Chester") and the Roman road ("street") that ran through it.

History

The parish church of St Mary and St Cuthbert was the seat of the Bishop of Lindisfarne from 883 to 995, and it was there that the Lindisfarne Gospels were annotated with the earliest surviving Old English translation of the Gospels.

Sport

The town is home to the Riverside Ground, the home of Durham County Cricket Club, which has hosted Test and World Cup matches.
