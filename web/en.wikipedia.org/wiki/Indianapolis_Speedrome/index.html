Indianapolis Speedrome

The Indianapolis Speedrome is a short-track automobile racing facility located on the southeast side of Indianapolis, Indiana, at Kitley Avenue. The paved oval, roughly one-fifth of a mile long, has operated for decades as a grassroots venue for weekly stock car racing.

Racing

The Speedrome is best known for Figure 8 racing — in which cars cross over at the centre of the track, creating near-miss intersection action — and for late model, street stock and front-wheel-drive divisions. It hosts the annual World Figure 8 Championship, a long-distance figure-8 event (traditionally run as a three-hour endurance race) that draws competitors from across the United States and is regarded as the premier event of its type.

History

Short-track racing at the site dates back to the mid-20th century, and the Speedrome has long promoted itself with the slogan of being the home of figure-8 racing. The track has also hosted school bus figure-8s, demolition derbies and other novelty events popular at American short tracks.
