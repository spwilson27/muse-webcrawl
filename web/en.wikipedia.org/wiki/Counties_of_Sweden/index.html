Counties of Sweden

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The counties of Sweden (Swedish: Sveriges län) are the top-level geographic subdivisions of Sweden. The country is divided into 21 counties, most of which correspond to an elected regional council (region) responsible mainly for healthcare and public transport, and to a county administrative board headed by a governor appointed by the national government.

Organisation

Each county administrative board (länsstyrelse) represents the state at regional level, supervising areas such as environmental protection and civil defence. In parallel, 18 of the counties have an elected region; Gotland, a single island municipality, discharges both roles. Counties are identified by county codes and letters used, for example, in vehicle registration and statistics.

List

The counties include Stockholm, Västra Götaland, Skåne, Östergötland, Uppsala, Jönköping, Norrbotten, Dalarna, and Västernorrland, among others. The largest by area is Norrbotten in the far north; the most populous is Stockholm.
