The Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics (SIB) is a non-profit academic foundation federating bioinformatics research groups across Switzerland. Founded in 1998, it connects some 800 scientists at Swiss universities and research institutes and provides core data resources used by life-science researchers worldwide.

Resources

SIB is best known for creating and maintaining Swiss-Prot, the manually curated protein sequence database founded by Amos Bairoch, which became a founding component of UniProt, the world's principal protein knowledgebase. The institute's ExPASy portal provides access to dozens of databases and software tools, and SIB groups maintain resources such as PROSITE, Swiss-Model (protein structure homology modelling), STRING (protein interactions) and the SwissLipids and neXtProt knowledgebases.

Training and role

Beyond infrastructure, SIB coordinates bioinformatics education and training in Switzerland, supports ELIXIR — the European intergovernmental organisation for life-science data — as the Swiss node, and has been central in setting standards for open, FAIR biological data.
