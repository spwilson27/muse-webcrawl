Humungous Fungus

The "Humungous Fungus" is a popular name for individual clonal colonies of the honey fungus genus Armillaria that rank among the largest and oldest living organisms on Earth. The two most famous examples are in Michigan and Oregon.

Michigan and Oregon colonies

A colony of Armillaria gallica near Crystal Falls, Michigan, was reported in 1992 to cover about 15 hectares and weigh some 10,000 kg; later work suggested it may span roughly 70 hectares and be at least 1,500 years old. A still larger Armillaria ostoyae colony in Oregon's Malheur National Forest covers an estimated 965 hectares (about 3.7 square miles) and may be several thousand years old, making it by area the largest known single organism.

Biology

These colonies spread underground through root-like rhizomorphs, infecting tree roots and causing white rot; the mushrooms seen above ground are only the fruiting bodies of one genetically identical individual.
