Ulsan

Ulsan is a metropolitan city in southeastern South Korea, on the coast of the Sea of Japan (East Sea), with a population of about 1.1 million. It is South Korea's industrial powerhouse, sometimes described as the country's industrial capital.

Industry

Ulsan hosts the world's largest automobile assembly plant, operated by Hyundai Motor Company, one of the world's largest shipyards, run by HD Hyundai Heavy Industries, and a major oil refinery complex operated by SK Energy. These industries drove rapid growth from the 1960s under South Korea's industrialization plans.

Geography and culture

The Taehwa River flows through the city, whose districts include historic whaling heritage at Jangsaengpo and prehistoric petroglyphs — the Bangudae Petroglyphs — along the Daegokcheon stream. Ulsan was one of the host cities of the 2002 FIFA World Cup.
