Scottish Football Hall of Fame

The Scottish Football Hall of Fame is a permanent exhibition at the Scottish Football Museum, located at Hampden Park in Glasgow. Established in 2004, it honours players, managers, and other figures who have made an outstanding contribution to football in Scotland. Inductees are chosen by a panel of football experts and historians, with new members added at an annual dinner.

History

The Hall of Fame was launched in 2004 with an initial intake of 20 figures, including Denis Law, Kenny Dalglish, Jock Stein, Bill Shankly, Jim Baxter, and Alex Ferguson. Subsequent inductions have broadened the honours beyond players to referees, administrators, and pioneering figures in the women's game, such as Rose Reilly. Inductees receive a place in the exhibition alongside memorabilia, and the project is intended as both a celebration and a historical record of the Scottish game.

Selected inductees

  • Players: Denis Law, Kenny Dalglish, Jim Baxter, Jimmy Johnstone, John Greig, Graeme Souness, Ally McCoist, Henrik Larsson (honorary, as a non-Scot whose career was centred in Scotland), Julie Fleeting
  • Managers: Jock Stein, Alex Ferguson, Bill Shankly, Matt Busby, Walter Smith, Willie Maley
  • Others: referee Hugh Dallas; journalist and historian Bob Crampsey
