Internal affairs

Internal affairs (often known as IA) is a division of a law enforcement agency that investigates incidents of, and possible suspicions of, law-breaking and professional misconduct attributed to officers on the force. It is therefore a mechanism of limited self-governance, "a police force policing itself". The internal affairs unit is typically separate from the chain of command of the officers it investigates.

Function

Internal affairs units receive and investigate complaints from members of the public as well as internally generated complaints regarding officer conduct, including excessive use of force, corruption, and violations of departmental policy. Investigations may result in disciplinary action, referral for criminal prosecution, exoneration, or policy changes.

Criticism

Critics argue that internal investigations can lack independence, which has led some jurisdictions to establish civilian review boards and inspector-general offices as complementary oversight mechanisms.

In popular culture

The internal affairs function has been dramatized in films such as Internal Affairs (1990) and in numerous police procedural television series.
