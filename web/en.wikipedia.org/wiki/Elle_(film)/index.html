Elle (French for "she" or "her") is a 2016 French psychological thriller film directed by Paul Verhoeven and written by David Birke, based on the 2012 novel Oh… by Philippe Djian. The film stars Isabelle Huppert as Michèle Leblanc, the head of a successful video game company who, after being raped in her home by a masked assailant, declines to report the attack and instead begins to track down the rapist herself while continuing with her complicated private life.

Production and release

Verhoeven originally developed the project in English for the United States but, unable to interest an American leading actress or financing, made the film in French in Paris. Elle premiered in competition at the Cannes Film Festival on 21 May 2016, where Huppert's performance won wide acclaim.

Reception

The film was praised by critics for its direction and especially for Huppert's performance, though its provocative treatment of sexual violence sparked debate. It was selected as the French entry for Best Foreign Language Film at the 89th Academy Awards. Huppert won the Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama and received an Academy Award nomination for Best Actress; the film won the Golden Globe for Best Foreign Language Film and César Awards including Best Film and Best Actress.
