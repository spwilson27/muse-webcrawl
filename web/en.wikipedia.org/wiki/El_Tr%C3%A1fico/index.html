El Tráfico

El Tráfico is the nickname of the soccer rivalry between the LA Galaxy and Los Angeles FC (LAFC), the two Major League Soccer clubs based in the Los Angeles metropolitan area. The name, coined by supporters, is a pun on "El Clásico" and Los Angeles's notorious traffic.

Origins

The rivalry began in 2018, LAFC's inaugural MLS season. The first match, on 31 March 2018 at the Galaxy's home stadium, became an instant classic: LAFC led 3–0 before the Galaxy, sparked by substitute Zlatan Ibrahimović scoring twice on his debut — including a spectacular long-range volley — won 4–3.

Notable matches

Subsequent meetings have produced high-scoring, dramatic games, including a 2019 playoff encounter in which LAFC won 5–3, their first victory in the fixture. The teams meet multiple times per season in league play and have also clashed in the US Open Cup and MLS Cup Playoffs, often before sellout crowds, with the Galaxy at Dignity Health Sports Park in Carson and LAFC at BMO Stadium in Exposition Park.

Character

El Tráfico is regarded as one of MLS's premier rivalries, notable for star players, large away followings, and its contrast between the Galaxy — the league's historically most successful club — and LAFC, the ambitious expansion-era challenger.
