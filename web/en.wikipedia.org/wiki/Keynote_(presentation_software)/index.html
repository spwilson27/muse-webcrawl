Keynote (presentation software)

Developer: Apple Inc.
Initial release: January 2003
Platform: macOS, iOS, iPadOS, web

Keynote is a presentation software application developed by Apple Inc. as part of the iWork productivity suite, alongside Pages and Numbers. Announced by Steve Jobs in January 2003, it was originally created for Jobs's own keynote presentations at Macworld Expo.

Features

Keynote is known for its polished templates, cinematic slide transitions such as Magic Move, and emphasis on typography and imagery over dense bullet points. It supports real-time collaboration through iCloud, presenter displays, and export to PowerPoint, PDF and QuickTime formats.

History

Keynote 1.0 was sold as a standalone product in 2003; from 2005 it was bundled into iWork. Versions for iPhone and iPad followed in 2010, and a web version via iCloud arrived in 2013.
