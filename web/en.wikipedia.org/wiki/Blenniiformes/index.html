Blenniiformes

Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Chordata
Class: Actinopterygii
Order: Blenniiformes
Families: 6 (including Blenniidae)

Blenniiformes is an order of ray-finned fishes that includes the blennies and their close relatives. Members are mostly small, bottom-dwelling marine fishes of shallow coastal waters, though some occur in brackish or fresh water.

Characteristics

Blennies typically have elongated bodies, long dorsal fins, and fleshy appendages called cirri on the head. Many lack scales. Males of numerous species guard eggs laid in burrows, shells or crevices.

Families

The order includes the combtooth blennies (Blenniidae, the largest family), the clinids (Clinidae), labrisomids, chaenopsids (pike blennies), dactyloscopids (sand stargazers) and tripterygiids (triplefins).
