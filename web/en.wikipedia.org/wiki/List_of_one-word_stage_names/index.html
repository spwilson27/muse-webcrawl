List of one-word stage names

Mononyms
Also calledMononym
FieldsMusic, film, modelling, sport

This is a list of notable people best known by a single name — a mononym — whether a stage name, a given name, or a surname used alone. Mononymous performers include singers such as Madonna, Cher, Prince and Beyoncé, rappers such as Eminem and Drake, and footballers such as Pelé and Ronaldo.
