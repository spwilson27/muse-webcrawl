Palmerston Park is a football stadium on Terregles Street in Dumfries, Dumfries and Galloway, Scotland. It is the home ground of Scottish League One club Queen of the South, who have played there since the stadium opened in 1919. The ground is named after Robert Jenkinson, 2nd Earl of Liverpool, whose title included Baron Palmerston.

History

Queen of the South were formed in 1919 from an amalgamation of local clubs, and Palmerston Park was their home from the start. The site had previously been a farm and was developed with a main stand in the 1920s. The ground's record attendance of 26,552 was set for a Scottish Cup tie against Heart of Midlothian on 23 February 1952.

Floodlights were installed in 1958. The Portland Drive terracing and the covered Terregles Street end are notable features; the stadium retains an old-fashioned character, with terracing on three sides. In the 2010s the natural grass pitch was replaced by a 3G artificial surface, allowing community use and the ground-sharing arrangement with local side Heston Rovers.

Stands

The ground comprises the Main Stand (the oldest structure), the covered Portland Road Terrace, the Terregles Street End, and the Oakbank Services Stand, a seated stand opposite the Main Stand. The arena also hosted the club's centenary celebrations in 2019.
