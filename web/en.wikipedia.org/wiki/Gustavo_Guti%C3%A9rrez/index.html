Gustavo Gutiérrez

Gustavo Gutiérrez Merino
Born: 8 June 1928, Lima, Peru
Died: 22 October 2024, Lima
Occupation: Dominican priest, theologian, philosopher
Known for: Founder of liberation theology

Gustavo Gutiérrez Merino (1928–2024) was a Peruvian Dominican priest and theologian regarded as a founder of liberation theology. His book A Theology of Liberation (1971) argued that the Church should show a preferential option for the poor and confront structural injustice.

Life

Born in Lima of mestizo and Quechua descent, Gutiérrez suffered osteomyelitis as a youth. He studied medicine, philosophy and theology in Peru and Europe, was ordained in 1959, and taught at the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru.

Theology

Gutiérrez defined poverty as a scandalous condition contrary to human dignity, distinguished material poverty from poverty as spiritual openness, and drew on social analysis and scripture to call for liberation from sin and oppression. His work influenced Latin American bishops at Medellín (1968) and drew scrutiny from the Vatican in the 1980s, though he was never condemned.

Later years

He entered the Dominican Order in 1998, received numerous honours, and was welcomed by Pope Francis. He died in Lima aged 96.
