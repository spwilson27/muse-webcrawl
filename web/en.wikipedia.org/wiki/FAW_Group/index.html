FAW Group Corporation is a Chinese state-owned automobile manufacturer headquartered in Changchun, Jilin. Founded in 1953 as the First Automobile Works, it built China's first domestically produced truck and passenger car and remains one of the country's largest vehicle makers.

History

Construction of the First Automobile Works began in 1953 with Soviet technical assistance. Production of the Jiefang (Liberation) truck started in 1956, followed in 1958 by the Hongqi (Red Flag) limousine, long used by senior Chinese officials. In the reform era FAW formed major joint ventures with Volkswagen, Audi, Toyota and others.

Brands and operations

FAW's own brands include Hongqi, Jiefang, Bestune and Haima, alongside extensive joint-venture production. The group manufactures passenger cars, trucks and buses, and has invested heavily in electrification and in premium positioning for the Hongqi marque.
