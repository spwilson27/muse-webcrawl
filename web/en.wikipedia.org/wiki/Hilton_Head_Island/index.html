Hilton Head Island, sometimes referred to as simply Hilton Head, is a Lowcountry resort town and barrier island in Beaufort County, South Carolina, United States. It is known for its 12 miles of Atlantic Ocean beachfront and as a destination for golf, tennis and cycling.

History

The island is named for Captain William Hilton, who in 1663 identified its headland. It was the site of plantations worked by enslaved people and, during the Civil War, a major Union base after the Battle of Port Royal. In the 1950s Charles Fraser began developing Sea Pines Resort with an environmentally sensitive master plan that shaped the island's modern character.

Tourism and culture

Hilton Head hosts the RBC Heritage PGA Tour golf tournament at Harbour Town Golf Links, whose lighthouse is an island landmark. The island has a significant Gullah community, descendants of enslaved West and Central Africans who preserved a distinct language and culture.
