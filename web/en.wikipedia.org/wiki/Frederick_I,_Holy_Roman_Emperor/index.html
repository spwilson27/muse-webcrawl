Frederick I, Holy Roman Emperor

Frederick Barbarossa (December 1122 – 10 June 1190), also known as Frederick I (German: Friedrich I), was Holy Roman Emperor from 1155 until his death during the Third Crusade. A member of the Hohenstaufen dynasty, he was King of Germany from 1152 and King of Italy from 1155. His byname Barbarossa ("red beard") comes from the Italian campaigns of his reign.

Reign: 1155–1190 (emperor); 1152–1190 (German king)
Predecessor: Conrad III
Successor: Henry VI
Dynasty: Hohenstaufen
Died: 10 June 1190, Saleph River, Anatolia

Accession and policy

Frederick, Duke of Swabia, was elected king at Frankfurt in 1152 and crowned at Aachen; in 1155 he was crowned emperor in Rome by Pope Adrian IV. His programme, pursued with the revived study of Roman law, aimed to restore imperial authority in Germany and Italy: the Roncaglia decrees of 1158 claimed extensive regalian rights in Lombardy. His long conflict with the Lombard League of Italian cities ended in defeat at the Battle of Legnano (1176) and the Peace of Constance (1183). In Germany he built up the imperial territories, founded towns, and humbled the powerful Henry the Lion, Duke of Saxony and Bavaria, who was outlawed in 1180.

Crusade and death

At nearly seventy, Frederick took the cross for the Third Crusade to recover Jerusalem from Saladin. He led one of the largest crusading armies overland through the Balkans and Anatolia, defeating the Seljuk Turks at Iconium in May 1190. On 10 June 1190, crossing the Saleph (Göksu) River in Cilicia, he drowned — accounts differ on whether he fell from his horse or was swept away while bathing. His army largely dispersed, though some remains were carried on toward Acre. Legend later attached him to the sleeping-emperor myth in the Kyffhäuser mountain, a role originally belonging to his grandson Frederick II.

Legacy

Barbarossa was remembered as a model ruler in German national mythology; Adolf Hitler named the 1941 invasion of the Soviet Union "Operation Barbarossa" after him. Historians credit him with strengthening the empire's legal and administrative order while exhausting it in Italy.
