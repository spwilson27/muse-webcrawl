Italian cuisine

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Image: Tagliatelle with ragù and a glass of red wine at a trattoria]

Italian cuisine is a Mediterranean cuisine consisting of the ingredients, recipes and cooking techniques developed across the Italian Peninsula since antiquity, and later spread around the world together with the Italian diaspora. Significant changes occurred with the colonisation of the Americas and the introduction of potatoes, tomatoes, capsicums and maize from the New World, alongside sugar, coffee and chocolate.

The cuisine is characterised by its regional diversity — the butter-and-rice cooking of the north, the olive-oil, tomato and pasta cooking of the south — and by an emphasis on few, high-quality ingredients. Signature dishes include Pizza, Pasta in hundreds of shapes, risotto, polenta and tiramisu, accompanied by some of the world's most famous Wines and Cheeses.

History

Italian food descends from Roman cooking, which itself absorbed Greek and Etruscan traditions; Apicius preserves Roman recipes. In the Middle Ages and Renaissance, court cuisines flourished, and Bartolomeo Scappi's Opera (1570) codified Renaissance cooking. Pellegrino Artusi's Science in the Kitchen and the Art of Eating Well (1891) united regional recipes in a national cookbook after Italian unification. Mass emigration in the late 19th and 20th centuries carried Italian food to the Americas, where Italian-American cuisine developed its own classics.

Regional cuisines

Emilia-Romagna is famed for Parmigiano-Reggiano, prosciutto di Parma, balsamic vinegar and fresh egg pasta; Naples for pizza and mozzarella di bufala; Liguria for pesto; Sicily for citrus, seafood, arancini and cannoli; Tuscany for bistecca alla fiorentina and ribollita. Many products hold protected DOP status under EU law.

Meals and structure

A formal Italian meal proceeds through antipasto, primo (pasta, rice or soup), secondo (meat or fish) with contorno, and dolce, followed by coffee and a digestivo. Everyday eating is simpler: pasta or risotto, bread, salad, fruit and wine or water.
