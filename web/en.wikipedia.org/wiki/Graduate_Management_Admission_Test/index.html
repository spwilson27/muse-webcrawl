The Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT) is a computer-adaptive test intended to assess certain analytical, quantitative, verbal, and data-insight skills for use in admission to graduate management programs, such as Master of Business Administration (MBA) programs. Knowledge of business is not tested. The GMAT is administered by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC).

Format

The current GMAT Focus Edition, introduced in late 2023, consists of three 45-minute sections: Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning, and Data Insights, for a total testing time of just over two hours. Scores range from 205 to 805. An earlier version, offered until January 2024, included an Analytical Writing Assessment and Integrated Reasoning section and a 200–800 score scale.

History

The test was created in 1953 by nine business schools working with an admissions organization that became GMAC, initially as the Admission Test for Graduate Study in Business. Taken well over 200,000 times a year at its peak, the GMAT is accepted by more than 7,700 programs at over 2,400 institutions worldwide. The test can be taken at Pearson VUE test centres or online.
