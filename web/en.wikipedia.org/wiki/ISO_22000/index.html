ISO 22000

ISO 22000
DeveloperInternational Organization for Standardization (ISO)

Latest revisionISO 22000:2018
SubjectFood safety management systems

ISO 22000 is an international standard that specifies the requirements for a food safety management system (FSMS) for organizations anywhere in the food chain, from primary producers and feed manufacturers to processors, transporters, retailers, and caterers. First published in 2005 and substantially revised in 2018, it combines generally recognized elements of food safety practice — interactive communication, system management, prerequisite programmes (PRPs), and the principles of Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP) — within the ISO management-system framework.

Structure

The 2018 edition adopts the Annex SL high-level structure common to ISO management system standards, organized around context of the organization, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement, and applies the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle at both the overall system level and the operational level (hazard control through PRPs, operational PRPs, and critical control points). Sector-specific prerequisite programmes are given in the ISO/TS 22002 series, and requirements for certification bodies in ISO/TS 22003. ISO 22000 forms the management-system basis of the FSSC 22000 certification scheme, which is benchmarked by the Global Food Safety Initiative.
