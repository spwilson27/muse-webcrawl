Draisine

A draisine is a light auxiliary rail vehicle, driven by its occupants, used by maintenance personnel and for inspection of tracks. The name derives from the German inventor Karl Drais, whose 1817 Laufmaschine (precursor of the bicycle) inspired human-powered rail trolleys. Draisines include handcars propelled by a walking-beam pump handle, pedal-powered versions and small motorised inspection cars; recreational draisine tours operate on disused lines in several countries.
