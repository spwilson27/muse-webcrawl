Gigafactory Nevada

LocationTahoe Reno Industrial Center, Storey County, Nevada, United States
OwnerTesla, Inc.
Opened2016
ProductsLithium-ion battery cells, battery packs, electric motors

Gigafactory Nevada (also known as Giga Nevada or Gigafactory 1) is a lithium-ion battery and electric vehicle component factory in Storey County, Nevada, owned and operated by Tesla, Inc. The facility produces battery cells in partnership with Panasonic, as well as battery packs, energy storage products, and drive units.

History

Tesla announced the site in September 2014 after a competition among several states, attracted by tax incentives and proximity to lithium resources and Tesla's California vehicle plant. Construction began in 2014, and the factory held its grand opening in July 2016 while still partially complete.

Scale and production

The factory was designed so that its full build-out would make it one of the largest buildings in the world by footprint. Cell production is carried out by Panasonic within the plant, while Tesla assembles packs and powertrain components. Output expanded through the late 2010s to supply the Model 3 and Tesla's stationary storage products.

Expansion

In January 2023 Tesla announced a major expansion of the site, including new capacity for 4680 battery cells and the Tesla Semi truck, representing a multi-billion-dollar investment and thousands of additional jobs.
