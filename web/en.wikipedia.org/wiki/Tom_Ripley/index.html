Tom Ripley

Tom Ripley
Created by: Patricia Highsmith
First appearance: The Talented Mr. Ripley (1955)
Portrayed by: Matt Damon, Alain Delon, Andrew Scott, John Malkovich, Dennis Hopper

Thomas Ripley is a fictional character in a series of five crime novels — the 'Ripliad' — by American author Patricia Highsmith. He is a con artist, art lover and serial murderer who almost always gets away with his crimes.

Character

Raised by an aunt in Boston after his parents drowned, Ripley is charming, intelligent, cultured and amorally self-interested. In The Talented Mr. Ripley (1955) he is sent to Italy to retrieve Dickie Greenleaf, whose identity he assumes after murdering him. Later novels — Ripley Under Ground, Ripley's Game, The Boy Who Followed Ripley and Ripley Under Water — show him living comfortably in France on an inherited fortune and art-fraud income, murdering when threatened.

Adaptations

Films include Purple Noon (1960, Alain Delon), The American Friend (1977, Dennis Hopper), The Talented Mr. Ripley (1999, Matt Damon) and Ripley's Game (2002, John Malkovich). Andrew Scott played Ripley in the 2024 Netflix series Ripley. See Patricia Highsmith.
