Jebel Ali

Jebel Ali is a port town in the United Arab Emirates, about 35 kilometres southwest of central Dubai, on the Persian Gulf coast. It is one of the largest industrial and logistics zones in the Middle East.

Port and free zone

Jebel Ali Port, opened in 1979, is among the largest container ports in the world, operated by DP World. The surrounding Jebel Ali Free Zone (Jafza), established in 1985, hosts thousands of companies from many countries and is a major driver of Dubai's economy.

Development

The area includes Al Maktoum International Airport, planned residential districts such as Jebel Ali Village, and major industrial plants. See also Dubai.
