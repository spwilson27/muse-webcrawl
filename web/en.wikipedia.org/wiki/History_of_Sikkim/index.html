History of Sikkim

The history of Sikkim, a Himalayan region now a state of India, begins with the Lepcha people and the arrival of Tibetan Buddhism. The Namgyal dynasty founded the Kingdom of Sikkim in 1642, ruled by Chogyals (priest-kings).

British protectorate

Through treaties in the 19th century Sikkim became a British protectorate; Darjeeling was ceded in 1835. After Indian independence in 1947 Sikkim remained a protectorate of India under the 1950 treaty.

Merger with India

Political unrest and a 1975 referendum led to the abolition of the monarchy and Sikkim becoming the 22nd state of India on 16 May 1975.
