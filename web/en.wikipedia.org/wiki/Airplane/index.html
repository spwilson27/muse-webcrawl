An airplane (aeroplane) is a fixed-wing aircraft powered by engines — propellers or jets — in which forward motion makes air flow over wings to generate lift. The Wright brothers achieved the first controlled, sustained powered flight at Kitty Hawk on 17 December 1903.

Development

Aviation advanced through two world wars (see the crawl's World War I and World War II pages from earlier waves) into the jet age: the Boeing 707, the wide-body 747 (1970), and today's efficient twin-jets. Air travel globalised business and tourism while raising the emissions questions of the Sustainability era.
