Comparison of email clients

An email client is an application used to access and manage a user's email. This article compares notable email clients by platform, protocol support, features, and licensing.

Clients

Well-known desktop clients include Microsoft Outlook, Mozilla Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Evolution, Mutt, and eM Client. Webmail services such as Gmail and Outlook.com are sometimes included for comparison.

Features compared

Comparisons typically cover support for POP3, IMAP, SMTP, and Exchange protocols; encryption support (PGP, S/MIME); spam filtering; calendaring; and extensibility through add-ons.

Release history

Many clients listed have decades-long development histories, from Unix mail user agents such as Mutt and Pine (Alpine) to modern cross-platform applications.
