This is a list of women footballers with 100 or more international goals. Scoring 100 goals at senior international level is a rare achievement in women's football: around twenty players have reached the mark. The first was the United States' Mia Hamm; the all-time record belongs to Canada's Christine Sinclair, whose 190 goals are the most by any international footballer, male or female.
