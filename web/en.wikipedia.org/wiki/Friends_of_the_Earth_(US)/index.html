Friends of the Earth (US)

Founded: 1969
Founder: David Brower
Type: Environmental advocacy organisation
Headquarters: Washington, D.C., and Berkeley, California
Affiliation: Friends of the Earth International

Friends of the Earth U.S. is a non-profit environmental advocacy organisation based in the United States. Founded in 1969 by the conservationist David Brower, it is the American member of Friends of the Earth International, a network of grassroots environmental organisations in more than 70 countries. The organisation campaigns on climate change and clean energy, sustainable food and agriculture, protection of oceans and forests, and reform of economic policy, including the environmental practices of international financial institutions.

History

Brower founded Friends of the Earth after resigning as executive director of the Sierra Club amid disagreements over strategy, including his opposition to nuclear power and to a proposed compromise on dams in the Grand Canyon. The new organisation quickly established companion groups in other countries, and in 1971 the international network was formally organised. Early campaigns included opposition to the Alaska pipeline's environmental effects, supersonic transport aircraft, and the expansion of nuclear power.

Campaigns

Friends of the Earth U.S. combines policy advocacy, litigation and shareholder and corporate campaigning. It has campaigned against fossil fuel subsidies, for mandatory disclosure of climate-related financial risks, against industrial bee-toxic pesticides, and for restrictions on genetically engineered foods. Its political arm, Friends of the Earth Action, endorses candidates for office. The organisation is known for a comparatively confrontational style among large US environmental groups and for linking environmental protection to economic justice.
