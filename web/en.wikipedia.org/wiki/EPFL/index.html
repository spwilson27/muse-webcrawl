EPFL

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Type: Public research university

Established: 1853 (as École spéciale de Lausanne); federal institute from 1969

Location: Lausanne, Switzerland

Students: c. 12,000

Campus: Ecublens, adjoining Lake Geneva, plus satellite campuses

EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) is a public research university in Lausanne, Switzerland. Founded in 1853 as a private school and taken over by the Swiss Confederation in 1969, it is one of the two Swiss Federal Institutes of Technology, alongside ETH Zurich, and is consistently ranked among the world's leading science and engineering universities.

History

The school grew out of the École spéciale de Lausanne, modelled on the École Centrale in Paris. It became the engineering faculty of the University of Lausanne before federalisation in 1969, when it acquired its current name and began moving to a purpose-built campus at Ecublens on the shore of Lake Geneva, shared with the University of Lausanne. Under president Patrick Aebischer (2000–2016) EPFL expanded into life sciences and attracted major philanthropic and industry partnerships; Martin Vetterli succeeded him in 2017.

Campus and schools

The campus is noted for the Rolex Learning Center (2010), designed by SANAA, and for art and architecture installations. EPFL is organised into schools covering basic sciences, engineering, computer and communication sciences, life sciences, architecture and civil and environmental engineering, and management of technology, plus a college of humanities. Satellite campuses operate in Neuchâtel, Sion, Fribourg and Geneva. The university hosts national research facilities, the Swiss Plasma Center and the Blue Brain Project in neuroscience.

Students and research

EPFL teaches around 12,000 students, a large share of them international, and is known for start-up spin-offs, its Innovation Park, and research in robotics, microtechnology, neuroscience, energy and data science. Tuition and teaching are principally in French at bachelor's level and English at master's and doctoral level.
