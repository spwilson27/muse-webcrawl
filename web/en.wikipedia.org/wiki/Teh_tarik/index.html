Teh tarik

TypeHot milk tea
OriginMalaysia
IngredientsBlack tea, condensed milk

Teh tarik (literally "pulled tea") is a hot milk tea beverage popular in Malaysia and Singapore, regarded as Malaysia's national drink. It is made from strong black tea mixed with condensed milk.

Preparation

The drink takes its name from the dramatic pouring process: the tea is repeatedly poured back and forth between two vessels from a height, which cools it slightly, mixes the milk and creates a thick frothy top. Skilled practitioners make a spectacle of the pull, and competitions are held for the longest or most artistic pour.

Culture

Teh tarik is a staple of mamak stalls — Indian Muslim eateries — where it accompanies roti canai and other dishes at all hours. Variants include the less sweet teh tarik kurang manis and ginger-flavoured teh halia.
