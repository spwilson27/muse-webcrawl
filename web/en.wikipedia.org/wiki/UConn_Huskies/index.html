UConn Huskies

The UConn Huskies are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing the University of Connecticut (UConn), located in Storrs, Connecticut. The Huskies compete in NCAA Division I, primarily as members of the Big East Conference. UConn is best known nationally for its basketball programs: its women's basketball team is the most successful in NCAA history, and its men's team has won multiple national championships.

Basketball

The UConn women's basketball program, built by coach Geno Auriemma from 1985, has won a record number of NCAA championships — twelve titles through 2025 — and produced legendary winning streaks of 111 and 90 consecutive games. The men's program, coached to prominence by Jim Calhoun and later Dan Hurley, has won six national championships, including back-to-back titles in 2023 and 2024. In 2004, UConn became the first school to win the men's and women's basketball championships in the same year, a feat it repeated in 2014.

Other sports

UConn fields teams in football (competing as an FBS independent in recent years), ice hockey, soccer, baseball, field hockey, and other sports. Its women's field hockey and men's soccer teams have also won national titles, and the baseball program has produced numerous major-league players.

Identity

The Husky nickname dates to 1934, and the mascot is named Jonathan after Jonathan Trumbull, Connecticut's Revolutionary War-era governor. The school's colours are national flag blue and white.
