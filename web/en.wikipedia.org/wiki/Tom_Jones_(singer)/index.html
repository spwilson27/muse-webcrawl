Tom Jones (singer)

Tom JonesBorn: Thomas John Woodward, 7 June 1940, Treforest, Wales
Occupation: Singer
Years active: 1963–present

Sir Tom Jones (born Thomas John Woodward; 7 June 1940) is a Welsh singer whose powerful voice and wide-ranging repertoire have sustained a career spanning more than six decades. His hits include "It's Not Unusual", "Delilah", "Green, Green Grass of Home" and "She's a Lady".

Early life

Jones was born in Treforest, Pontypridd, in South Wales, the son of a coal miner. He sang in school choirs and local bands before signing with Decca Records in 1964.

Career

His second single, "It's Not Unusual" (1965), reached number one in the United Kingdom. Jones became a major star in Las Vegas, hosted the television show This Is Tom Jones, and won a Grammy Award for Best New Artist in 1966. A late-career resurgence came with the album Reload (1999), featuring the hit "Sex Bomb".

Honours

Jones was knighted in 2006 for services to music and has served as a coach on The Voice UK.
