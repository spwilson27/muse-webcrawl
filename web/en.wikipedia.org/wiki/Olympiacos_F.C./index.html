Olympiacos F.C.

Olympiacos Football Club is a Greek professional football club based in Piraeus, Attica. Founded on 10 March 1925, it is the most successful club in Greek football history, having won a record number of Super League Greece titles and Greek Cups. The club is part of the major multi-sport club Olympiacos CFP.

History

Olympiacos quickly became the dominant team of Piraeus and, after the Second World War, of Greece. Its fierce rivalry with Panathinaikos of Athens is known as the "Derby of the Eternal Enemies". The club plays at the Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium, named after a Greek War of Independence hero.

In 2024, Olympiacos became the first Greek club to win a major European trophy, defeating Fiorentina in the UEFA Europa Conference League final in Athens; its youth team also won the UEFA Youth League in the same season.

Colours and crest

The team's colours are red and white, worn as vertical stripes, and its emblem depicts the head of an Olympic victor wearing a laurel wreath. Supporters know the club by the nickname Thrylos ("Legend").
