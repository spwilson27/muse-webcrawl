Volga

The Volga is the longest river in Europe, flowing 3,531 km through central Russia into the Caspian Sea.

Course

It rises in the Valdai Hills, passes cities including Tver, Yaroslavl, Nizhny Novgorod, Kazan, Samara, Saratov, and Volgograd, and forms a large delta near Astrakhan.

Significance

The Volga is central to Russian history, transport, hydroelectric power, and culture, celebrated in folklore as "Mother Volga".
