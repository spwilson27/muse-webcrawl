Tilikum (c. December 1981 – January 6, 2017) was a captive male orca who spent most of his life at SeaWorld Orlando in Florida. He was captured in Iceland in 1983 at Hafnarfjörður, near Reykjavík. A year later, he was transferred to Sealand of the Pacific in Victoria, British Columbia, and in 1992 to SeaWorld Orlando.

Incidents

Tilikum was involved in three human deaths during his captivity: trainer Keltie Byrne at Sealand of the Pacific in 1991 (with two other orcas); Daniel Dukes, a visitor found dead at SeaWorld Orlando in 1999; and senior trainer Dawn Brancheau, whom he pulled into the water and killed during a performance on 24 February 2010. The Brancheau incident led to an OSHA case against SeaWorld and lasting changes to trainer–orca interaction rules.

Blackfish and legacy

Tilikum was the central subject of the 2013 documentary Blackfish, which argued that captivity caused his aggression and drove a major public backlash against orca captivity. SeaWorld ended its orca breeding programme and theatrical shows in the years that followed. Tilikum, the largest orca in captivity at about 5.7 tonnes, was also a prolific sire, fathering 21 calves. He died in 2017 of a bacterial lung infection.
