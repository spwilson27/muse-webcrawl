Kozhikode

Kozhikode, also known as Calicut, is a city on the Malabar Coast of Kerala, India. Once the capital of the Zamorin's kingdom and a famed centre of the spice trade, it is where Vasco da Gama first landed in India in 1498, at nearby Kappad.

History

Ruled by the Samoothiri (Zamorin), Calicut traded spices with Arabs, Chinese and later Europeans. The Portuguese, Dutch, French and British successively contested it. It gave its name to calico cloth.

Today

Kozhikode is a major trading and educational centre, home to NIT Calicut and an IIM campus nearby, famous for Malabar cuisine including Kozhikodan halwa and biryani. UNESCO named it India's first City of Literature in 2023.
