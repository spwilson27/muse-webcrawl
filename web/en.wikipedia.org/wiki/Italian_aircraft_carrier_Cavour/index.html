Italian aircraft carrier Cavour

Name: Cavour (550)
Namesake: Count Camillo Benso di Cavour
Launched: 20 July 2004
Commissioned: 2008/2009
Displacement: about 27,900 tonnes (full load ~30,000+)
Aircraft: AV-8B Harrier II; F-35B (after refit)

Cavour (pennant number 550) is an aircraft carrier of the Italian Navy and its flagship. Named after the statesman Count Camillo Benso di Cavour, she was built by Fincantieri, launched in 2004 and entered service at the end of the decade.

Design and service

Cavour has a through flight deck with a ski-jump, a hangar that can double as a vehicle deck for amphibious operations, and facilities for a joint task force command staff. She first operated AV-8B Harrier II jump jets; a major refit in 2019–2020 adapted her to operate the F-35B Lightning II, and she conducted trials with the type in 2021. The ship has also taken part in humanitarian missions, including relief work after the 2010 Haiti earthquake.
