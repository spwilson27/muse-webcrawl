Identity verification

Identity verification is the process of confirming that a claimed identity matches the real-world identity of a person or entity. It is widely used in banking, online services, border control and employment screening to prevent fraud and comply with regulations such as Know Your Customer (KYC) rules.

Methods

Traditional verification relies on government-issued documents such as passports and driving licences. Modern digital methods include biometric checks (facial recognition, fingerprints), document authenticity analysis, database cross-checks, and knowledge-based authentication using personal history questions.

Applications

Financial institutions verify customers before opening accounts; age-restricted services verify age; and employers verify work eligibility. Remote verification expanded greatly with online banking and digital services.
