Zeughaus

Zeughaus

Location: Unter den Linden, Berlin, Germany

Built: 1695–1730

Current use: Deutsches Historisches Museum

The Zeughaus (Arsenal) is a Baroque building on Unter den Linden in Berlin, Germany. It is the oldest surviving building on the boulevard and today houses the permanent exhibition of the German Historical Museum (Deutsches Historisches Museum).

History

Construction began in 1695 under Elector Frederick III of Brandenburg; the arsenal was completed in 1730. Sculptors including Andreas Schlüter contributed its ornamentation, notably the keystones depicting dying warriors on the courtyard facade. The building served as Prussia's arsenal and later as a military museum and hall of fame.

Museum

After wartime damage and use as a museum of German history in the GDR, the Zeughaus became the seat of the Deutsches Historisches Museum, founded in 1987, whose permanent exhibition on German history opened in the building in 2006.
