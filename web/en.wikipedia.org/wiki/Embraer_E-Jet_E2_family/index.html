Embraer E-Jet E2 family

Manufacturer: Embraer
First flight: May 23, 2016 (E190-E2)
Introduction: April 24, 2018, with Widerøe
Status: In production, in service

The Embraer E-Jet E2 family is a family of narrow-body, twin-engine, medium-range jet airliners developed by the Brazilian aerospace manufacturer Embraer. It is the successor to the first-generation E-Jet family, launched at the Paris Air Show in 2013.

Development

The E2 family features new Pratt & Whitney geared turbofan engines, a redesigned wing, full fly-by-wire flight controls, and updated avionics, offering significantly lower fuel burn per seat than the original E-Jets. The family comprises the E175-E2, E190-E2, and E195-E2 variants.

Variants

The E190-E2 entered service with Norwegian carrier Widerøe in April 2018, followed by the larger E195-E2, which entered service with Azul in 2019. The smallest variant, the E175-E2, was delayed because its size and weight fall outside the scope clauses in United States pilot contracts that limit regional airline aircraft.

Competition

The E2 family competes primarily with the Airbus A220 in the 100–130 seat segment.
