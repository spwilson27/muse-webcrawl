Central and Western District

The Central and Western District is one of the 18 districts of Hong Kong, located on the northwestern shore of Hong Kong Island. It is the political and financial centre of the city, containing the Central business district, the Government Secretariat at Tamar, the Court of Final Appeal, and the financial towers of Central, as well as older neighbourhoods such as Sheung Wan, Sai Ying Pun, and Kennedy Town, and the slopes of Victoria Peak and Mid-Levels.

Areas and landmarks

The district includes Admiralty, the Peak, and parts of Pok Fu Lam's northern edge. Landmarks include Hong Kong City Hall, the Legislative Council Complex, Exchange Square, the International Finance Centre, Lan Kwai Fong, SoHo, Man Mo Temple, and the Western Market. The University of Hong Kong is located in the district.

Governance and population

The district has a population of roughly 240,000 and is governed locally by the Central and Western District Council, one of Hong Kong's district councils. It is among the wealthiest districts by median household income, reflecting the Mid-Levels and Peak residential areas.
