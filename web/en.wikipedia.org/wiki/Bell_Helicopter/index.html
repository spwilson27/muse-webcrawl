Bell Helicopter, now known simply as Bell, is an American rotorcraft manufacturer headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, a subsidiary of Textron. It designs and manufactures military and commercial helicopters and tiltrotor aircraft.

History

The company began as the Bell Aircraft Corporation, founded in Buffalo, New York, in 1935 by Lawrence Dale Bell. Bell Aircraft built fighter aircraft during the Second World War and the Bell X-1, the first aircraft to break the sound barrier in level flight (1947). Under designer Arthur M. Young, Bell developed the Bell 47, which in 1946 became the first helicopter certified for civilian use.

Products

Military products have included the UH-1 Iroquois ("Huey") and AH-1 Cobra of the Vietnam era, the OH-58 Kiowa, and the V-22 Osprey tiltrotor developed with Boeing. Current military types include the AH-1Z Viper and UH-1Y Venom, and the V-280 Valor was selected for the US Army's Future Long-Range Assault Aircraft programme. Commercial models include the Bell 206 JetRanger, 407, 412, 429 and 525 Relentless. The company rebranded as Bell in 2018.
