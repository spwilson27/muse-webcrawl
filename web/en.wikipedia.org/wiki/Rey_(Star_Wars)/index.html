Rey (Star Wars)

Rey is a fictional character in the Star Wars franchise, portrayed by Daisy Ridley. Introduced in Star Wars: The Force Awakens (2015), she is the protagonist of the sequel trilogy, appearing also in The Last Jedi (2017) and The Rise of Skywalker (2019).

Character

Rey is introduced as a scavenger surviving alone on the desert planet Jakku, selling salvaged parts from wrecked starships, while waiting for the family that left her there. After encountering the droid BB-8 and the defecting stormtrooper Finn, she is drawn into the Resistance's fight against the First Order and discovers her own powerful connection to the Force. She seeks training from the exiled Luke Skywalker, forms a complex Force bond with Kylo Ren, and ultimately confronts the resurrected Emperor Palpatine — revealed in the final film to be her grandfather, making her parentage a much-discussed retcon of the second film's suggestion that her parents were nobodies. At the trilogy's end, Rey rejects the Sith lineage, masters her abilities, and adopts the name Rey Skywalker.

Reception

Ridley's performance was widely praised, and Rey became a popular figure, particularly noted as the first female lead protagonist of a Star Wars theatrical trilogy. Debate among fans concerned her rapid mastery of Force abilities and the handling of her origins across the trilogy.
