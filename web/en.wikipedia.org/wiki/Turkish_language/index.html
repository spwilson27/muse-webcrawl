Turkish language

Turkish (Türkçe) is the most widely spoken Turkic language, the official language of Turkey and Northern Cyprus and one of Cyprus's official languages, spoken by over 80 million people as a first language.

Features

Turkish is agglutinative with vowel harmony, subject-object-verb order and no grammatical gender. Ottoman Turkish was written in Arabic script; Atatürk's 1928 alphabet reform adopted a Latin-based alphabet of 29 letters.

History

Descended from Oghuz Turkic brought to Anatolia in the 11th century, Turkish was standardised on the Istanbul dialect. See Turkic languages and Vowel harmony.
