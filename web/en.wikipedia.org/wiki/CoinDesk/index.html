CoinDesk

CoinDesk is a news site specialising in bitcoin and digital currencies. Founded in 2013, it is known for the Bitcoin Price Index (XBX) and for its annual Consensus conference, one of the largest gatherings in the cryptocurrency industry.

History

CoinDesk was founded in 2013 by entrepreneur Shakil Khan. In 2016 it was acquired by Digital Currency Group for a reported sum of around $500,000. In November 2023, the crypto exchange operator Bullish acquired CoinDesk from DCG.

Bitcoin Price Index

CoinDesk launched its Bitcoin Price Index in September 2013, aggregating prices across major exchanges; it became a widely cited benchmark and was referenced in the first US bitcoin futures and ETF filings.
