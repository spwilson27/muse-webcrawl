The Premier Lacrosse League (PLL) is a professional field lacrosse league in North America. It was founded in 2018 by brothers Mike and Paul Rabil, a star player who left Major League Lacrosse (MLL) to build a player-focused league offering full-time salaries and benefits, health care, and equity stakes. The PLL played its inaugural season in 2019 with six teams operating on a touring model, in which all teams travel to a single host city each weekend rather than being based permanently in home markets.

In December 2020 the PLL merged with MLL, absorbing the historic Boston Cannons as an expansion club, and from 2021 the league operated with eight teams. In 2023 the league announced that, beginning with the 2024 season, its eight clubs would be assigned home cities and markets, shifting away from the purely touring format toward a hybrid model with home venues.

Teams

The league's clubs include the Archers, Atlas, Cannons, Chaos, Chrome, Redwoods, Waterdogs and Whipsnakes. With the move to assigned home markets, teams were matched with cities such as Boston, California, Carolina, Denver, Maryland, New York, Philadelphia and Utah. The Whipsnakes won the first two PLL championships (2019 and 2020); subsequent champions have included the Chaos (2021), Waterdogs (2022) and Archers (2023).

Format

Each PLL season runs from early summer to mid-September, culminating in playoffs and a championship game. The league also stages an indoor "Championship Series" played in the sixes format — the shorter, faster variant adopted for lacrosse's return to the Olympics at the 2028 Los Angeles Games. Player salaries, health insurance and league equity made the PLL distinctive among lacrosse leagues, and its media strategy emphasised social media and direct-to-consumer streaming before signing a multi-year rights deal with ESPN in 2022.

History

Founding

Paul Rabil, the sport's most marketable player, announced the PLL in September 2018 with backing from investors including The Raine Group, Creative Artists Agency and Joe Tsai. More than 140 players initially signed on, most defecting from MLL.

Merger with MLL

On 16 December 2020 the PLL and MLL announced their merger under the PLL name, unifying professional field lacrosse in North America. The Boston Cannons were retained and rebranded as Cannons Lacrosse Club.
