Mafia Queens of Mumbai: Stories of Women from the Ganglands is a 2011 non-fiction crime book by Indian journalist and author Hussain Zaidi, written with Jane Borges. It profiles thirteen women connected with the Mumbai underworld, drawing on police records, interviews and Zaidi's years of crime reporting.

Contents

The best-known profile is that of Haseena Parkar, sister of fugitive gangster Dawood Ibrahim, who ran much of his Mumbai business after he fled India. Other chapters cover figures such as Jenabai Daaruwali, a bootlegger who became an underworld mediator; Mahalaxmi Papamani, a drug dealer; and women who were victims, informers or operators within the city's criminal networks.

Reception and adaptation

The book was well received for documenting a largely unreported side of Mumbai's gang history and became a bestseller. The Haseena Parkar chapter was adapted into the 2017 Hindi film Haseena Parkar, starring Shraddha Kapoor, and the book's wider material later served as a source for the 2021 film Mumbai Saga era interest in the genre and for the web series and film Gangubai Kathiawadi (2022), based on its chapter on the Kamathipura brothel-keeper Gangubai.
