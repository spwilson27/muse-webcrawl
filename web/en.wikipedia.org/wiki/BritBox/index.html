BritBox is an over-the-top subscription streaming service devoted to British television programmes. It was launched in the United States in March 2017 as a joint venture between the BBC (through BBC Worldwide, later BBC Studios) and ITV. The service specialises in classic and contemporary British drama, comedy, documentary and factual programming, including long-running series such as Doctor Who (classic episodes), Poirot, Midsomer Murders, Coronation Street and Emmerdale.

History

BritBox debuted in the US in March 2017 and in Canada in early 2018. A United Kingdom version launched in November 2019, although in 2022 the British service was folded into ITV's new streaming platform ITVX, with BritBox content made available as part of ITVX Premium. The service also expanded to Australia (2020), South Africa (2021) and the Nordic countries (2022). In February 2024, BBC Studios acquired ITV's 50 per cent stake in the international BritBox business (outside the UK), taking full ownership of the service.

Programming

BritBox carries an extensive catalogue of British television, including classic comedies such as Fawlty Towers and Keeping Up Appearances, crime dramas such as Vera, Shetland and Silent Witness, and soap operas shown shortly after their UK broadcast. It has also commissioned original programming, including the crime drama The Beast Must Die, the comedy The Dry, and adaptations and revivals such as Spitting Image. Living content from BBC and ITV archives forms the core of the library, alongside programmes acquired from other British broadcasters such as Channel 4 and Channel 5.

Availability

The service is available through its own website and apps for smart TVs, streaming devices, mobile phones and tablets, and as an add-on channel through services such as Amazon Prime Video Channels and Apple TV Channels in some markets.
