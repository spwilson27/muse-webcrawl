Iron Man (2008 film)

Iron Man is a 2008 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Paramount Pictures, it is the first film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Directed by Jon Favreau, the film stars Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark / Iron Man, alongside Gwyneth Paltrow, Terrence Howard, Jeff Bridges and Shaun Toub.

Plot

Weapons manufacturer Tony Stark is captured by a terrorist group in Afghanistan and ordered to build them a missile. Instead he builds a powered suit of armour and escapes. Back in the United States, he refines the suit and, discovering that his mentor Obadiah Stane has been selling weapons to his captors, confronts him in a larger armoured suit. In a post-credits scene, Nick Fury recruits Stark into the Avengers Initiative.

Reception and legacy

The film premiered on 14 April 2008 and grossed over $585 million worldwide, becoming the eighth-highest-grossing film of 2008. Critics praised Downey's performance and Favreau's direction. It was selected by the American Film Institute as one of the ten best films of 2008 and was followed by Iron Man 2 (2010) and Iron Man 3 (2013).
