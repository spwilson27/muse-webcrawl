Harivamsa

The Harivamsa (Sanskrit: हरिवंश, "the lineage of Hari [Vishnu/Krishna]") is an important Sanskrit text, regarded as a supplement (khila) to the Mahabharata.

Contents

The text contains about 16,374 verses in its critical edition, arranged in three books: the Harivamsa Parva (creation and royal genealogies), the Vishnu Parva (the life of Krishna, including his childhood in Vrindavan) and the Bhavishya Parva (prophecies and concluding material).

Significance

The Harivamsa is among the earliest sources for Krishna mythology, including stories of his youth that later became central to devotional traditions. It is traditionally attributed to Vyasa and is sometimes counted within the Puranic literature.
