JAG (TV series)

JAG (an abbreviation of Judge Advocate General) is an American legal drama television series created by Donald P. Bellisario. It aired on NBC for one season and then on CBS, running from 1995 to 2005 for a total of ten seasons and 227 episodes.

Premise

The series follows officers of the U.S. Navy's Judge Advocate General's Corps, principally Harmon "Harm" Rabb Jr. (David James Elliott), a former naval aviator turned lawyer, and Marine Lieutenant Colonel Sarah "Mac" MacKenzie (Catherine Bell). The JAG lawyers investigate crimes, prosecute and defend service members, and travel to carriers, submarines, and bases around the world.

Legacy

Though cancelled by NBC after its first season, the show was revived by CBS and became a long-running success. It launched the NCIS franchise: a pair of JAG episodes in 2003 introduced the Naval Criminal Investigative Service team that became NCIS, itself spawning multiple spin-offs.
