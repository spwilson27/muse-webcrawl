Beecher is an unincorporated community and census-designated place (CDP) in Genesee County, Michigan, United States. It lies within Mount Morris Township, just north of the city of Flint, and is served by the Beecher Community School District.

1953 tornado

On June 8, 1953, Beecher was struck by one of the deadliest tornadoes in United States history. The Flint–Beecher tornado, rated F5, killed 116 people, injured 844, and destroyed hundreds of homes along Coldwater Road. It remains the deadliest tornado in Michigan history and was among the ten deadliest in US history; it struck the same evening as the catastrophic Worcester, Massachusetts tornado.

Community

Beecher developed as a suburban community adjacent to Flint during the automobile industry boom. Its school district, Beecher Community School District, is known for its high school basketball programme, which has won multiple Michigan state championships. The area code for Beecher is 810 and its ZIP code is 48458.
