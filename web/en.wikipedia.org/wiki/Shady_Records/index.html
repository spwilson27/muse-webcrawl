Shady Records

Shady Records is an American record label founded in 1999 by rapper Eminem (Marshall Mathers) and his manager Paul Rosenberg. The label was established following the success of Eminem's major-label debut The Slim Shady LP and operates as an imprint associated with Interscope Records (and, historically, Aftermath Entertainment).

History

The label's first major signing was the Detroit group D12, whose albums Devil's Night (2001) and D12 World (2004) topped the charts. Obie Trice and 50 Cent followed; 50 Cent's Get Rich or Die Tryin' (2003), released jointly through Shady and Aftermath, was a major commercial success. Other artists have included Stat Quo, Bobby Creekwater, Ca$his, Yelawolf, the supergroup Slaughterhouse, Boogie, Westside Gunn and Conway the Machine (Griselda), and Grip. The label issued the compilation Eminem Presents: The Re-Up (2006) and the 15th-anniversary compilation Shady XV (2014), which included the single "Guts Over Fear".

Roster and associated acts

As of the 2020s the label's roster is headed by Eminem himself, with newer signings including Ez Mil. Acts formerly on the label include D12, Obie Trice, 50 Cent (whose releases came via a joint venture), Yelawolf, and Slaughterhouse. Shady Records also ran the Shade 45 channel on Sirius XM satellite radio, launched in 2004.
