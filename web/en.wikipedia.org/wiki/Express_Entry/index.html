Express Entry

Image placeholder: Express Entry is administered by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada

Express Entry is an online system used by the Canadian government to manage applications for permanent residence under certain economic immigration programs. Introduced on January 1, 2015, by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), it replaced the previous first-come, first-served processing for skilled worker applications.

Programs covered

Express Entry manages applications for three federal programs: the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Federal Skilled Trades Program, and the Canadian Experience Class. Candidates who are also nominated by a province or territory under the Provincial Nominee Program may receive additional points and be invited through Express Entry draws.

How it works

Eligible candidates create an online profile and are entered into a pool, where they are scored under the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) on factors such as age, education, official-language proficiency, and work experience. The highest-ranking candidates in each draw receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) for permanent residence, after which they submit a full application, which IRCC aims to process within a service standard of about six months.
