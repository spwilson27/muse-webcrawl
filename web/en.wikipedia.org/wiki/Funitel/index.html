Funitel

A funitel is a type of cable car, generally used to transport skiers, in which the cable car is suspended from two cables. The name is a portmanteau of the French words funiculaire and telepherique. Funitels have two widely separated haul ropes, which makes them more stable in high winds than a standard gondola lift and allows larger cabins, typically carrying 20 to 30 passengers.

The system was developed in the 1980s and first installed at Val Thorens in France. Funitels use detachable grips, allowing cabins to slow in stations while the haul rope continues at full speed. Notable installations include those at Val Thorens, La Plagne, and Squaw Valley, and the system is prized for reliability in exposed alpine terrain.
