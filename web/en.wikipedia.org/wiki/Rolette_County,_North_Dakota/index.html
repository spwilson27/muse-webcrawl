Rolette County, North Dakota

Rolette County is a county in the U.S. state of North Dakota, on the Canadian border. As of the 2020 census its population was about 14,000. Its county seat is Rolla, and its largest city is Belcourt.

The county was created in 1873 and named for Joseph Rolette Jr., a fur trader and territorial legislator. It contains most of the Turtle Mountain Indian Reservation, home of the Turtle Mountain Band of Chippewa Indians, and part of the wooded Turtle Mountain plateau, a striking contrast to the surrounding plains.

Communities include Rolla, Belcourt, Dunseith and St. John. The International Peace Garden lies partly in the county near Dunseith, on the border with Manitoba. The economy rests on agriculture, tribal government and services, and the county has one of the highest Native American population shares in the United States.
