Doctor Stephen Strange is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by artist Steve Ditko with Stan Lee, he first appeared in Strange Tales #110 (1963). A former brilliant but arrogant neurosurgeon, Strange becomes the Sorcerer Supreme, Earth's primary protector against magical and mystical threats.

Fictional biography

After a car accident irreparably damages his hands, ending his surgical career, Strange travels the world seeking a cure and finds the Ancient One at Kamar-Taj in the Himalayas. Studying under her, he masters the mystic arts, aided by artefacts such as the Cloak of Levitation and the Eye of Agamotto, and takes up residence at the Sanctum Sanctorum in Greenwich Village, New York, assisted by Wong.

Powers

Strange wields powerful sorcery: energy projection, teleportation, astral projection, dimensional travel, and the conjuring of shields and weapons, drawing on entities such as the Vishanti. He is among the most powerful magic-users in the Marvel Universe.

In other media

Benedict Cumberbatch portrays Strange in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, debuting in Doctor Strange (2016) and appearing in Avengers: Infinity War, Avengers: Endgame, Spider-Man: No Way Home and Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022).
