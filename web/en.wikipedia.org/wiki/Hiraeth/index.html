Hiraeth

Hiraeth is a Welsh word for a deep longing for something, especially for one's home. It combines homesickness, nostalgia, and grief for a place or time that may no longer exist, or may never have existed.

Meaning

The word has no exact equivalent in English. It is closely associated with Welsh cultural identity and with the experience of the Welsh diaspora, evoking a longing for Wales itself as much as for any particular home.

Related concepts

Similar untranslatable feelings include the Portuguese saudade and the German Sehnsucht.
