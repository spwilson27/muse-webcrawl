Nobel Assembly at the Karolinska Institute

The Nobel Assembly at the Karolinska Institute is a body of 50 professors of medicine at the Karolinska Institute in Stockholm, Sweden, which awards the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

Nobel Assembly
Members: 50 professors
Role: Selects Nobel laureates in Physiology or Medicine
Working body: Nobel Committee for Physiology or Medicine (5 members)

Function

Each year the Assembly appoints a Nobel Committee to evaluate nominations (invited from thousands of nominators worldwide), commission expert reports and propose laureates; the Assembly votes in early October. The prize is presented on 10 December in Stockholm.

History

Alfred Nobel's will (1895) assigned the medicine prize to the Karolinska Institute. The Assembly was formally separated from the Institute's full faculty in 1977 to focus the decision among 50 members. See Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, Karolinska Institute.
