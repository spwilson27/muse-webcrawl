Karl Liebknecht

Karl Liebknecht
Born: 13 August 1871, Leipzig
Died: 15 January 1919, Berlin
Occupation: Lawyer, politician
Party: SPD, USPD, KPD

Karl Liebknecht (13 August 1871 – 15 January 1919) was a German socialist and anti-militarist. A member of the Social Democratic Party and later co-founder of the Communist Party of Germany, he was murdered with Rosa Luxemburg after the Spartacist uprising of January 1919.

Early life

Liebknecht was the son of Wilhelm Liebknecht, a co-founder of the SPD. He studied law and political economy in Leipzig and Berlin and practised as a lawyer defending political defendants.

Political career

Elected to the Reichstag in 1912, Liebknecht became known for his opposition to militarism. On 2 December 1914 he cast the sole vote against war credits for the First World War. Imprisoned for anti-war agitation in 1916, he was released in the revolutionary days of October 1918.

Death

On 9 November 1918 Liebknecht proclaimed a "Free Socialist Republic" from the Berlin Palace. After the failed Spartacist uprising he was arrested by Freikorps troops and murdered on 15 January 1919.
