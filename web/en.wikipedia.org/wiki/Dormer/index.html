Dormer

A dormer is a roofed structure, often containing a window, that projects vertically beyond the plane of a pitched roof. Dormers add usable space, light and ventilation to an attic or upper storey.

Dormer
Type: Roof structure
From: French dormir, to sleep
Types: Gable, hip, shed, eyebrow, wall dormer

Types

Common forms include the gable dormer (with a peaked roof), the hip dormer, the shed dormer (single sloping plane, maximizing interior space), the eyebrow dormer (low, wavy roofline), and the wall dormer, whose face is a continuation of the wall below. Blind or fake dormers provide exterior appearance only. The name derives from the French dormir, reflecting their use in sleeping rooms under roofs.

History

Dormers appeared in medieval and early-modern European building, flourished in French mansard roofs of the 17th century, and became characteristic of Cape Cod, Colonial Revival and Arts and Crafts houses in North America.
