Enzyme Commission number

Maintained byIUBMB / ExplorEnz database
FormatEC X.Y.Z.W (four numbers)

Enzyme Commission numbers (EC numbers) are a numerical classification scheme for enzymes, based on the chemical reactions they catalyze. Strictly speaking, the system does not classify enzymes themselves but the enzyme-catalysed reactions; two different enzymes that catalyse the same reaction receive the same EC number.

Format

Every EC number consists of four numbers separated by periods. The first number indicates the class of reaction: EC 1 oxidoreductases, EC 2 transferases, EC 3 hydrolases, EC 4 lyases, EC 5 isomerases, EC 6 ligases, and EC 7 translocases (added in 2018). Subsequent numbers refine the subclass, sub-subclass, and the specific reaction.

History

The scheme was first published in 1961 by the Enzyme Commission of the International Union of Biochemistry, and it is now maintained with the ExplorEnz database.
