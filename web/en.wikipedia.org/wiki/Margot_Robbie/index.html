Margot Elise Robbie (born 2 July 1990) is an Australian actress and producer. Known for her work in both blockbuster and independent films, she has received various accolades, including nominations for three Academy Awards, six BAFTA Awards and four Golden Globe Awards. Time magazine named her one of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2017, and Forbes named her one of the world's highest-paid actresses in 2019 and the highest-paid actress in 2023.

Born and raised in Queensland, Robbie began her career in 2008 on the television series Neighbours, on which she was a regular until 2011. After relocating to the United States, she led the television series Pan Am (2011–2012) and had her breakthrough in 2013 with Martin Scorsese's black comedy film The Wolf of Wall Street. She achieved wider recognition with starring roles as Jane Clayton in The Legend of Tarzan (2016), and as Harley Quinn in the DC superhero films Suicide Squad (2016), Birds of Prey (2020) and The Suicide Squad (2021).

Early life

Margot Elise Robbie was born on 2 July 1990 in Dalby, Queensland, to Sarie Kessler, a physiotherapist, and Doug Robbie, a farmer and sugarcane tycoon. She grew up in the Gold Coast hinterland, spending much of her time on her grandparents' farm. She studied drama at Somerset College, graduating in 2007.

Career

2008–2012: Early work

Robbie made her feature film debut in the Australian slasher film I.C.U. (2009). Her breakthrough came with the role of Donna Freedman in the long-running soap opera Neighbours (2008–2011), for which she was nominated for two Logie Awards. After moving to Los Angeles, she starred in the ABC drama series Pan Am (2011).

2013–2019: Breakthrough and critical acclaim

Robbie's international breakthrough came as Naomi Lapaglia in The Wolf of Wall Street (2013), opposite Leonardo DiCaprio. She went on to star in Focus (2015), Z for Zachariah (2015) and The Legend of Tarzan (2016), and gained widespread fame playing Harley Quinn in Suicide Squad (2016).

In 2014, Robbie founded the production company LuckyChap Entertainment with Tom Ackerley and others. Under its banner she starred in and produced the biographical film I, Tonya (2017), playing figure skater Tonya Harding; the performance earned her a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Actress. She received a second Oscar nomination, for Best Supporting Actress, for Bombshell (2019), and further acclaim for Mary Queen of Scots (2018) and Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019), in which she played Sharon Tate.

2020–present: Barbie and continued success

Robbie reprised Harley Quinn in Birds of Prey (2020) and The Suicide Squad (2021), and starred in Amsterdam (2022) and Babylon (2022). In 2023 she starred in the title role of Greta Gerwig's fantasy comedy Barbie, which she also produced through LuckyChap. The film grossed over $1.4 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2023 and the highest-grossing film ever directed by a woman; Robbie received a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Picture as a producer. LuckyChap also produced the acclaimed films Promising Young Woman (2020) and Saltburn (2023).

Personal life

Robbie met British assistant director Tom Ackerley on the set of Suite Française in 2013; they married in December 2016 in Byron Bay, New South Wales. They co-founded LuckyChap Entertainment together.
