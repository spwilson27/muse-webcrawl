List of largest stadiums

The following is a list of the largest stadiums in the world by seating capacity. The largest stadium in the world is the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, India, a cricket ground with a capacity of 132,000. The largest stadium used primarily for association football and athletics-style events is the Rungrado 1st of May Stadium in Pyongyang, North Korea. The largest stadiums in the United States are American college football venues, several of which exceed 100,000 seats.

Historically, some stadiums held far larger crowds: the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro officially held nearly 200,000 for the 1950 World Cup final, and the old Wembley and Hampden Park hosted crowds well above 130,000 before all-seater conversions reduced capacities across world football.
