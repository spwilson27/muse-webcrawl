Aida-Weddo

Aida-Weddo (also spelled Ayida-Wedjo, Ayida-Weddo and Aida Wedo) is a loa — a spirit — in Haitian Vodou and Louisiana Voodoo. She is the serpent of the rainbow, consort of Damballa, and together they embody creation, fertility, water and the continuity between sky and earth.

Role and symbolism

Aida-Weddo derives from the Fon spirit Ayida-Wedjo of Dahomey (present-day Benin), a rainbow serpent who, in Fon cosmology, supports the world and whose coils and movements are tied to earthquakes, rain and fertility. In Haitian Vodou she belongs to the Rada nation of spirits. Along with Damballa — the ancient serpent creator — she forms a primal pair: Damballa is associated with the sky/cosmic egg and Aida-Weddo with the rainbow and the waters, and in ritual the two are often depicted as intertwined serpents.

Veneration

Offerings to Aida-Weddo include white foods, eggs, milk, rice and chicken, and her colours are white and the hues of the rainbow. Possession by her manifests serpentine movement in the devotee. Her sacred day and imagery connect her to rain, rivers and the fertility of the land.
