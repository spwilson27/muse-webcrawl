List of Transformers films

Transformers is a series of science-fiction action films based on the Hasbro toy line of the same name. The franchise began in 1986 with the animated film The Transformers: The Movie and was relaunched in live action by director Michael Bay in 2007. The films centre on the war between the heroic Autobots and the villainous Decepticons, alien robots that disguise themselves as vehicles and machinery. The series has grossed over US$5 billion worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing film franchises.
