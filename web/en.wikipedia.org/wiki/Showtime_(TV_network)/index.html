Showtime (TV network)

LaunchedJuly 1, 1976
OwnerParamount Global
CountryUnited States

Showtime is an American premium television network owned by Paramount Global. Launched in 1976 by Viacom, it was one of the earliest cable movie channels and later became known for original programming.

Programming

Showtime's acclaimed original series have included Dexter, Homeland, Shameless, Billions, Twin Peaks: The Return, and Yellowjackets, alongside feature films, documentaries, and championship boxing.

Streaming era

In 2023 the service was integrated with Paramount+ as "Paramount+ with Showtime," and the linear channel was rebranded accordingly in 2024.
