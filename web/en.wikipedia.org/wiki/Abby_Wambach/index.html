Abby Wambach

Mary Abigail Wambach (born June 2, 1980) is an American retired soccer player and coach. A forward, she played for the United States women's national team from 2001 to 2015 and finished her international career with 184 goals, a world record for international goals at the time of her retirement.

Wambach won two Olympic gold medals (2004 and 2012) and the 2015 FIFA Women's World Cup, and she was named FIFA World Player of the Year in 2012. Renowned for her aerial ability and diving headers, her stoppage-time equaliser against Brazil in the 2011 World Cup quarter-final is one of the most famous goals in the tournament's history.

She played club soccer for the Washington Freedom, magicJack and the Western New York Flash, and after retiring became an author and advocate for gender equality in sport.
