Alex Kidd in Miracle World is a platform video game developed and published by Sega for the Master System. First released in Japan on 1 November 1986, it stars Alex Kidd, a monkey-like boy with oversized ears and a powerful punch, who served as Sega's mascot until the arrival of Sonic the Hedgehog in 1991.

Gameplay

The player guides Alex through seventeen stages set on the planet Aries. Alex can run, jump, crouch and punch blocks and enemies; stages also include swimming and vehicle sections using a pedal-powered helicopter (the "petti-copter") and a motorbike. Unusually, boss encounters are decided not only by combat but also by games of rock-paper-scissors ("Janken"), a motif drawn from Japanese children's games. Currency collected from broken blocks can be spent in shops on items such as extra lives and power bracelets.

Plot

Alex Kidd, a prince of the Radactian kingdom who has trained in the Shellcore martial art on Mount Eternal, learns that the tyrant Janken the Great has kidnapped Prince Egle and his fiancée Princess Lora. Alex sets out across Miracle World to defeat Janken and his henchmen and restore peace.

Legacy

The game was later built into many Master System II consoles, making it one of the best-known titles for the system. It spawned several sequels, including Alex Kidd: The Lost Stars and Alex Kidd in the Enchanted Castle. A remake, Alex Kidd in Miracle World DX, was released in 2021.
