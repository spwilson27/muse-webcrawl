Explicit Congestion Notification

Explicit Congestion Notification (ECN) is an extension to the Internet Protocol and the Transmission Control Protocol defined in RFC 3168 (2001). ECN allows end-to-end notification of network congestion without dropping packets.

Operation

Two bits in the IP header form the ECN field. A sender marks packets as ECN-capable; a congested router, instead of dropping the packet, may set the Congestion Experienced codepoint. The receiver echoes this signal back to the sender via TCP flags, allowing it to reduce its transmission rate. ECN depends on support from both endpoints and intermediate routers, and its deployment on the public Internet grew substantially in the 2010s.
