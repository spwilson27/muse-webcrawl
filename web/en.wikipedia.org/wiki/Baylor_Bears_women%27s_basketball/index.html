Baylor Bears women's basketball

Baylor Bears (Lady Bears)
University: Baylor University
Location: Waco, Texas
Conference: Big 12
National titles: 2005, 2012, 2019

The Baylor Bears women's basketball team, historically known as the Lady Bears, represents Baylor University in NCAA Division I women's college basketball and competes in the Big 12 Conference.

History

Under coach Kim Mulkey from 2000 to 2021, Baylor became a national power, winning NCAA championships in 2005, 2012 — with an undefeated 40–0 season led by Brittney Griner — and 2019.

Later years

Nicki Collen became head coach in 2021 after Mulkey departed for LSU, and the programme has remained a regular Big 12 contender and NCAA tournament team.

Players

Notable players include Brittney Griner, Odyssey Sims, NaLyssa Smith and Nina Davis.
