Nederlands Dans Theater

Nederlands Dans Theater (NDT, Netherlands Dance Theatre) is a Dutch contemporary dance company founded in 1959 in The Hague by Benjamin Harkarvy, Aart Verstegen and Carel Birnie, breaking away from the Nederlands Ballet.

History

Under long-serving artistic director Jiří Kylián (1975–1999), NDT gained world renown for works blending classical technique with modern dance. The company comprises NDT 1 (main company) and NDT 2 (younger dancers), performs at Amare in The Hague, and tours internationally. Later artistic directors include Paul Lightfoot.
