List of pseudonyms

A pseudonym is a fictitious name used by a person instead of their real name — as a pen name by writers, a stage name by performers, or an alias for privacy or safety. This list gives well-known examples.

Writers

  • Mark Twain — Samuel Clemens
  • George Orwell — Eric Blair
  • George Eliot — Mary Ann Evans
  • Currer, Ellis and Acton Bell — Charlotte, Emily and Anne Brontë
  • Lewis Carroll — Charles Lutwidge Dodgson
  • Dr. Seuss — Theodor Seuss Geisel
  • J.K. Rowling / Robert Galbraith — Joanne Rowling
  • Stephen King / Richard Bachman
  • Voltaire — François-Marie Arouet
  • Molière — Jean-Baptiste Poquelin
  • O. Henry — William Sydney Porter
  • Saki — H. H. Munro

Performers and artists

  • Marilyn Monroe — Norma Jeane Mortenson
  • John Wayne — Marion Morrison
  • David Bowie — David Jones
  • Lady Gaga — Stefani Germanotta
  • Banksy — anonymous street artist
  • Satoshi Nakamoto — pseudonymous creator of Bitcoin

Reasons

Pseudonyms have been adopted to hide gender, avoid censorship or persecution, separate genres, satisfy publishers, or create a memorable brand.
