Larry Ellison

Born: Lawrence Joseph Ellison, August 17, 1944, New York City, U.S.
Occupation: Businessman, investor
Known for: Co-founding Oracle Corporation
Title: Executive chairman and CTO of Oracle

Lawrence Joseph Ellison (born August 17, 1944) is an American businessman and entrepreneur who co-founded the software company Oracle Corporation in 1977. He served as Oracle's chief executive officer until 2014 and remains its executive chairman and chief technology officer. Ellison is consistently ranked among the wealthiest people in the world, with a fortune built on his large Oracle shareholding and other investments.

Early life

Ellison was born in New York City and adopted as an infant by relatives in Chicago, where he was raised in a lower-middle-class Jewish household on the South Side. He attended the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign and the University of Chicago but left both without a degree, teaching himself computer programming. In California he worked for companies including Ampex and Amdahl, building databases, including a project for the CIA code-named "Oracle".

Oracle

In 1977 Ellison co-founded Software Development Laboratories with Bob Miner and Ed Oates, inspired by Edgar F. Codd's papers on the relational database model. The company, later renamed Oracle, commercialised one of the first relational database management systems and grew into one of the world's largest enterprise software companies through aggressive sales and acquisitions — including PeopleSoft, Siebel Systems, BEA Systems, Sun Microsystems (bringing in Java and MySQL), and NetSuite. Ellison stepped down as CEO in 2014.

Other ventures and interests

Ellison invested heavily in competitive sailing, founding the Oracle Team USA syndicate that won the America's Cup in 2010 and 2013, and later co-founding the SailGP league. In 2012 he bought 98 percent of the Hawaiian island of Lanai. He has been a major investor in Tesla, served on its board from 2018 to 2022, and in the 2020s became a backer of major technology ventures including the Stargate AI infrastructure project and a stake in the media company Paramount. Ellison is also a signatory of the Giving Pledge.
