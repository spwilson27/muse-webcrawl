Wynn Resorts, Limited is an American developer and operator of luxury hotels and casinos, founded by casino magnate Steve Wynn in 2002. The company operates flagship resorts in Las Vegas and Macau and is known for high-end properties that have repeatedly earned Five-Star ratings from the Forbes Travel Guide.

History

Steve Wynn, who had built Mirage Resorts (The Mirage, Treasure Island, Bellagio), lost control of that company to MGM Grand in 2000. He founded Wynn Resorts and purchased the site of the old Desert Inn in Las Vegas, opening Wynn Las Vegas in April 2005 at a cost of about $2.7 billion, then the most expensive resort ever built. Its sister tower, Encore Las Vegas, followed in 2008. In Macau, Wynn Macau opened in 2006 and the larger Wynn Palace in Cotai in 2016. Encore Boston Harbor opened in Everett, Massachusetts, in 2019, and the company has been developing Wynn Al Marjan Island in the United Arab Emirates.

2018 scandal and aftermath

In January 2018, the Wall Street Journal reported allegations of sexual misconduct by Steve Wynn spanning decades. Wynn denied the allegations but resigned as chairman and CEO in February 2018 and later divested his shares, ending his association with the company that bears his name. Wynn Resorts paid substantial penalties, including a $20 million fine from the Nevada Gaming Commission and a $35 million fine in Massachusetts, while retaining its licences under new leadership headed by CEO Matt Maddox and later Craig Billings.

Properties and business

The company's revenue is dominated by its Macau operations, which historically generated the majority of earnings, while its Las Vegas resorts anchor the premium segment of the Strip. Wynn Resorts is included in the S&P 500 and has pursued online sports betting through WynnBET, later scaled back.
