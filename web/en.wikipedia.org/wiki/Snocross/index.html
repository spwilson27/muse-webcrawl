Snocross

Snocross (also snowcross) is a racing sport consisting of snowmobiles racing over a specially made track of snow, with large banked corners, jumps and moguls. It is essentially motocross translated to snowmobiles and winter conditions: riders race head-to-head in heats rather than against the clock, and the machines — lightweight, long-travel-suspension sleds — are purpose-built by manufacturers such as Ski-Doo, Polaris and Arctic Cat.

Format

Events typically run a series of qualifying heats leading to a final (LCQ — last chance qualifier — rounds are common). Production-based classes are limited to 600cc-class engines with restricted modifications; amateur, junior, women's and masters classes race shorter or gentler formats. Tracks are usually 0.5–1 km (0.3–0.6 mi) long, sculpted from natural or machine-made snow, with tabletops, doubles, rhythm sections and steep banked turns. Races are short — a few minutes of full-contact racing — making starts and the first corner decisive.

Series and history

Snocross grew out of informal snowmobile racing in the 1980s–90s and gained television exposure through the Winter X Games, where it was a headline event from the late 1990s until its removal after 2022. In North America the professional circuit is the ISOC (International Series of Champions) AMSOIL Championship Snocross series; a competing series, and regional circuits, run across the US snow belt and Canada. The World Championship Snowmobile Derby at Eagle River, Wisconsin — better known for oval racing — is a related landmark of American snowmobile racing. Riders such as Tucker Hibbert (the sport's most decorated racer), Blair Morgan and Elias Ishoel became its best-known stars.

Equipment and safety

Race sleds weigh around 190–220 kg, use paddle tracks and aggressive carbide ski runners, and are fitted with tether kill switches, protective tunnel and track guards. Riders wear motocross-style helmets, goggles, chest/back protection and often neck braces; injuries typical of motocross — wrist, shoulder and knee trauma — are the sport's main risks.
