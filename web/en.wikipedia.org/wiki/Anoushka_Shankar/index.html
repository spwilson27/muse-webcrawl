Anoushka Shankar (born 9 June 1981) is a British-American sitar player and composer. The daughter of sitar maestro Ravi Shankar and half-sister of singer Norah Jones, she is known both as a leading exponent of Indian classical music and for collaborations across flamenco, jazz, electronica and orchestral music. She has been nominated for multiple Grammy Awards.

Early life and training

Shankar was born in London and grew up between London, Delhi and California. She began training seriously on the sitar under her father at age nine, gave her first public performance at 13, and signed a recording contract at 16. She performed with Ravi Shankar around the world and conducted his Concerto No. 3 premiere projects after completing studies instead of attending university.

Career

Her early albums — Anoushka (1998), Anourag (2000) and Live at Carnegie Hall (2001) — established her classical credentials. Later work explored fusion: Rise (2005), Traveller (2011), which traced the shared roots of Indian music and Spanish flamenco, Traces of You (2013), featuring Norah Jones, Home (2015), Land of Gold (2016), a response to the refugee crisis, and the trilogy of mini-albums Chapter I: Forever, For Now, Chapter II: How Dark It Is Before Dawn and Chapter III: We Return to Light (2023–2025). She has collaborated with artists including Herbie Hancock, Sting, Nitin Sawhney and Arooj Aftab.

Activism and writing

Shankar is a supporter of animal rights and women's causes; she publicly identified herself as a survivor of childhood sexual abuse in a 2013 video for the One Billion Rising campaign. She wrote a biography of her father, Bapi: The Love of My Life (2002).
