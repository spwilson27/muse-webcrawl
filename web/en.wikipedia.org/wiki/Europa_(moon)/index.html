Europa (moon)

Europa is the smallest of the four Galilean moons orbiting Jupiter and the sixth-closest to the planet. Discovered by Galileo Galilei in 1610, it is slightly smaller than Earth's Moon and has one of the smoothest surfaces in the Solar System.

DiscoveryGalileo Galilei, 1610
Orbital period3.55 days
Mean radius1,560.8 km
SurfaceWater ice, lineae
AtmosphereThin oxygen

Subsurface ocean

Beneath Europa's icy crust is believed to lie a global saltwater ocean perhaps 100 km deep, kept liquid by tidal flexing from Jupiter. This makes Europa a leading candidate in the search for extraterrestrial life. Hubble observations suggested intermittent water plumes.

Exploration

Pioneer, Voyager, and especially Galileo (1995–2003) spacecraft studied Europa, as did Juno flybys. NASA's Europa Clipper, launched in October 2024, is due to arrive in 2030 to study habitability in detail; ESA's JUICE mission will also make Europa flybys.
