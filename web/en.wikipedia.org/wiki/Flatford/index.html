Flatford

Flatford is a small hamlet in East Bergholt, Suffolk, England, on the River Stour near the Essex border. It is best known as the setting of several paintings by John Constable, including The Hay Wain (1821), one of the most famous works of English art.

Constable country

Flatford lies in the Dedham Vale Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, often called "Constable Country" because the landscape painter John Constable (1776–1837) was born in nearby East Bergholt and painted the Stour valley throughout his career. Willy Lott's House, a cottage beside Flatford Mill, appears in many of his works; the view of the mill pond and the house forms the scene of The Hay Wain.

Buildings and visitor sites

Flatford Mill, a Grade I listed watermill owned by the Constable family in the artist's lifetime, is now owned by the National Trust and leased to the Field Studies Council, which runs courses there. Bridge Cottage, a 16th-century cottage at Flatford, houses an exhibition on Constable's life and work. Flatford lock and the dry dock beside the mill also survive from the days when the Stour was a working navigation.
