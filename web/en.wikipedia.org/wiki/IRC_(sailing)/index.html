IRC (sailing)

IRC is a system of handicapping sailboats and yachts for the purpose of racing. It is managed by the Royal Ocean Racing Club (RORC) in the United Kingdom through their dedicated Rating Office, and the Union Nationale pour la Course au Large (UNCL) in France. IRC stands for International Rating Certificate.

The rule

IRC is a rating rule, not strictly a measurement rule: boats are described by a set of measurements, and the rating office calculates a time correction factor (TCC) using a partly unpublished formula, which discourages design optimisation against the rule. Ratings are issued annually on certificates.

Use

The rule is used in over 40 countries for offshore and inshore races, including the Fastnet Race, the Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race, and many club racing series. It replaced earlier systems such as CHS (Channel Handicap System), from which it evolved.
