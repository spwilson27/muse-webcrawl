River Colne, Hertfordshire

The River Colne is a river in Hertfordshire and the London fringe, in southern England. It rises near North Mymms and flows roughly 30 km (19 miles) south-west through London Colney, Watford, and Rickmansworth, where it is joined by the Chess and the Gade, then along the Colne Valley past Denham to join the River Thames at Staines-upon-Thames.

Features

The Colne is a chalk stream in its upper reaches, supporting watercress beds historically and a sequence of lakes in the Colne Valley Regional Park — flooded gravel pits now important for wildfowl. The Grand Union Canal follows much of its valley and shares its water at several points. Its name is shared by several English rivers, including the Colne in Essex.

The river has suffered periodic low flows from groundwater abstraction for public supply, and catchment partnerships work to restore its chalk-stream ecology.
