Chretien de Troyes

Chretien de Troyes is the unaccented form of the name of Chrétien de Troyes (flourished c. 1160–1191), the French poet credited with founding the Arthurian romance tradition. This article title uses the spelling without diacritics; see the accented entry for the full biography and list of works.

Chrétien wrote five verse romances — Erec and Enide, Cligès, Yvain, the Knight of the Lion, Lancelot, the Knight of the Cart, and Perceval, the Story of the Grail — which introduced figures and motifs such as Lancelot's love for Guinevere and the Grail quest into European literature.
