Hail the Sun is an American post-hardcore band from Chico, California, formed in 2009. The band is known for its technically intricate instrumentation, odd time signatures and the wide vocal range of drummer and lead vocalist Donovan Melero, a combination associated with the "swancore" style of progressive post-hardcore.

History

The band self-released its debut album POW! Right in the Kisser! in 2010 and the EP Elephantitis in 2012, then signed with Blue Swan Records for Wake (2014). After moving to Equal Vision Records, they released Culture Scars (2016), Mental Knife (2018), New Age Filth (2021) and Divine Inner Tension (2023), touring widely within the progressive post-hardcore scene.

Musical style

Hail the Sun's music combines melodic post-hardcore with progressive rock complexity, featuring interlocking guitar lines and drumming that accompanies Melero's simultaneous singing.
