Railway junction

A railway junction is a location where two or more rail routes converge or diverge. Junctions may be simple connections between lines of the same company, or links between different routes permitting trains to pass from one line to another.

Types

A flat junction brings routes together at grade using turnouts and often a diamond crossing, which can create conflicting movements that limit capacity. A flying junction grade-separates the converging routes with a bridge or dive-under so that flows do not conflict. In British usage, the name "junction" also appears in station names where lines divide, such as Clapham Junction.
