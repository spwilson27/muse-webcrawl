Looney Tunes

Looney Tunes is an American animated comedy short film series produced by Warner Bros. from 1930 to 1969, alongside sister series Merrie Melodies. It introduced Bugs Bunny, Daffy Duck, Porky Pig, Tweety, Sylvester, Yosemite Sam, Foghorn Leghorn, the Tasmanian Devil and many others.

Looney Tunes
Created by: Warner Bros. Cartoons
Original run: 1930–1969 (classic shorts)
Directors include: Tex Avery, Chuck Jones, Bob Clampett, Friz Freleng
Music: Carl Stalling

History

Initially made to promote Warner's music catalogue, the shorts became famous for fast-paced gags, sophisticated animation and voice work by Mel Blanc. Directors at "Termite Terrace" developed the characters' personalities through the 1940s and 1950s golden age.

Legacy

The franchise has continued in TV series, films such as Space Jam (1996), comics and streaming revivals, and is regarded as among the greatest animation ever made.
