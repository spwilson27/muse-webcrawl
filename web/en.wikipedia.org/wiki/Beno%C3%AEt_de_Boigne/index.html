Benoît de Boigne

Benoît de Boigne
Born: 1751, Chambéry
Died: 1830

Benoît Leborgne, comte de Boigne (8 March 1751 – 21 June 1830), was a French military adventurer from Savoy who became a highly successful general in the service of the Maratha ruler Mahadaji Scindia in India.

Career in India

After service in Europe and Russia, de Boigne arrived in India, entered Maratha service in 1784, raised disciplined brigades trained in European fashion, and won major victories that expanded Scindia's power in northern India.

Return

He returned to Europe wealthy in the 1790s, settled in Chambéry, and became a notable benefactor of the city, which commemorates him with the Fontaine des Éléphants.
