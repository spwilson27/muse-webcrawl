Soloist (ballet)


A soloist is a ballet dancer who performs featured solo roles, ranked above the corps de ballet.

In ballet, a soloist is a dancer in a company who performs solo and small-group roles, standing above the corps de ballet and below the principal dancers in the company hierarchy.

Role

Soloists dance variations, pas de trois, and character or demi-character parts, and they frequently understudy principal roles. In many companies the rank is subdivided — for example, into first soloist and second soloist, or junior and senior soloist — and promotion to soloist is a major career milestone for a dancer emerging from the corps.

Company hierarchies

Ranking systems vary: the Paris Opera Ballet uses sujet and premier danseur grades, while many other companies run corps de ballet, coryphée, soloist, first soloist, principal. Soloists are typically featured in divertissements and in the solo variations of full-length classics such as Swan Lake and The Sleeping Beauty.
