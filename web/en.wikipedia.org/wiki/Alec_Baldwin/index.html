Alec Baldwin

Alexander Rae Baldwin III (born 3 April 1958) is an American actor and comedian. The eldest of the four Baldwin brothers, he first gained recognition appearing on seasons six and seven of the CBS prime-time soap opera Knots Landing, and went on to leading and supporting roles in films including Beetlejuice (1988), Working Girl (1988), The Hunt for Red October (1990), and Glengarry Glen Ross (1992).

Career highlights

Baldwin received an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor for The Cooler (2003). His greatest critical success came on television: as network executive Jack Donaghy in the NBC sitcom 30 Rock (2006–2013) he won two Primetime Emmy Awards, three Golden Globes, and seven Screen Actors Guild Awards. A frequent host of Saturday Night Live — a record 17 times — he won a further Emmy for his recurring portrayal of Donald Trump during and after the 2016 presidential campaign.

Rust shooting

On 21 October 2021, while filming the western Rust in New Mexico, a revolver Baldwin was holding discharged a live round, killing cinematographer Halyna Hutchins and wounding director Joel Souza. The film's armourer was convicted of involuntary manslaughter in 2024; the charge against Baldwin was dismissed with prejudice in July 2024 after the court found prosecutors had withheld evidence.
