AmaZulu F.C.

AmaZulu Football Club
Founded: 1932
Ground: Moses Mabhida Stadium, Durban
Nickname: Usuthu
League: South African Premiership (DStv Premiership)

AmaZulu Football Club is a South African professional football club based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. Founded in 1932, the club is one of the oldest in South African football and plays in the country's top division. Its nickname, Usuthu, is a Zulu war cry.

History

The club was originally known as Zulu Royals and later became AmaZulu. It won the NPSL title in 1972 and has won domestic cup competitions including the Coca-Cola Cup in 1992. AmaZulu traditionally plays in green and white and draws much of its support from KwaZulu-Natal. The club's home ground is the Moses Mabhida Stadium, built for the 2010 FIFA World Cup.

Ownership and recent years

In 2020 the club was acquired by businessman Sandile Zungu. Under coach Benni McCarthy, AmaZulu finished second in the 2020–21 season, its best-ever top-flight finish, and qualified for the CAF Champions League for the first time.
