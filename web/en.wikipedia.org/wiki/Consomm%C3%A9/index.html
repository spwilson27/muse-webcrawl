Consommé

Consommé is a type of clear soup made from richly flavoured stock that has been clarified, a process using egg whites to remove fat and sediment. A classic of French cuisine, a properly made consommé is crystal clear, amber in colour and intensely savoury.

Preparation

Ground meat, mirepoix, tomatoes and egg whites are mixed into cold stock, then slowly heated; the egg whites coagulate into a floating "raft" that traps impurities as it rises, and the liquid is carefully ladled out through a hole in the raft and strained. Varieties include consommé double (extra-strength) and consommés named for their garnishes, such as consommé madrilène, flavoured with tomato and served sometimes chilled.

Modern variations

Contemporary chefs clarify stocks by gelatin filtration — freezing and slow thawing — which achieves similar clarity with less loss of flavour.
