Anderson da Silva (born 14 April 1975) is a Brazilian former mixed martial artist and professional boxer. Widely regarded as one of the greatest mixed martial artists of all time, he was the UFC Middleweight Champion from 2006 to 2013, holding the title for 2,457 days — the longest title reign in UFC history — and defending it a then-record ten consecutive times. His sixteen-fight UFC winning streak also remains a promotion record.

Career

Silva began his professional career in Brazil in 1997 and fought in Shooto, where he won the middleweight title, and in Pride Fighting Championships and Cage Rage in Japan and England. He joined the UFC in 2006, knocking out Chris Leben in 49 seconds, and won the middleweight title from Rich Franklin that October with a first-round knockout by knees from the Muay Thai clinch. His defences included wins over Dan Henderson, Patrick Côté, Demian Maia, and Vitor Belfort — knocked out with a celebrated front kick in 2011 — and two victories over Chael Sonnen, the second at UFC 148 after a comeback triangle-armbar submission in their first fight, one of the most famous in the sport.

Later career

Silva lost the title to Chris Weidman in 2013 and broke his leg in the rematch later that year. He returned to defeat Nick Diaz in 2015 (later a no contest after failed drug tests), lost decisions to Michael Bisping and Daniel Cormier, beat Derek Brunson, and lost to Israel Adesanya and Jared Cannonier before a final UFC bout against Uriah Hall in October 2020. He then returned to professional boxing, defeating former champion Julio César Chávez Jr. in 2021 and Bruno Machado in 2022, and lost an exhibition-era bout to Jake Paul in October 2022. He was inducted into the UFC Hall of Fame in 2023.

Fighting style

Silva is noted for extraordinary striking accuracy, head movement, timing, and counter-punching, blending Muay Thai clinch work, taekwondo kicks, and later boxing, backed by a Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt under the Nogueira brothers.
