Oppenheimer is a 2023 epic biographical thriller film written, directed and produced by Christopher Nolan. Based on American Prometheus, the Pulitzer Prize–winning biography by Kai Bird and Martin J. Sherwin, it chronicles the life of J. Robert Oppenheimer, the theoretical physicist who directed the Los Alamos Laboratory during the Manhattan Project and is often called the "father of the atomic bomb". Cillian Murphy stars as Oppenheimer, alongside Emily Blunt, Matt Damon, Robert Downey Jr. and Florence Pugh.

Plot and structure

The film interweaves two timelines: Oppenheimer's rise from student to wartime laboratory director, culminating in the Trinity test of July 1945, told largely in colour; and the 1954 Atomic Energy Commission security hearing that stripped him of his clearance, together with the 1959 Senate confirmation hearing of Lewis Strauss (Downey Jr.), shot partly in black and white. Nolan wrote the Oppenheimer scenes in the first person, an unusual device for a screenplay.

Production and release

The film was shot on 65 mm IMAX film by Hoyte van Hoytema, with the Trinity explosion recreated using practical effects rather than CGI. Released by Universal Pictures on 21 July 2023 — the same day as Barbie, giving rise to the "Barbenheimer" phenomenon — it grossed $976 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing biographical film of all time.

Reception and awards

Oppenheimer received widespread critical acclaim and won seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director for Nolan, Best Actor for Murphy and Best Supporting Actor for Downey Jr., as well as five Golden Globes and seven BAFTAs.
