Gwydir Shire is a local government area located in the New England and North West Slopes regions of New South Wales, Australia. Its principal towns are Bingara and Warialda, and it is named after the Gwydir River.

History

The shire was formed in 2004 by the amalgamation of Yallaroi Shire, Bingara Shire, and part of Barraba Shire. The area is predominantly agricultural, known for cattle, sheep, and cropping.

Geography and communities

Communities in the shire include Bingara, Warialda, Gravesend, North Star, Coolatai, and Upper Horton. The shire is noted for community initiatives such as the "Living Classroom" environmental project in Bingara and for attracting new residents through rural renewal schemes.
