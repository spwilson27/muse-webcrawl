Rover Group

The Rover Group plc was a British motor manufacturer, the successor to British Leyland. It was formed in 1986, privatised and sold to British Aerospace in 1988, then sold to BMW in 1994.

History

The group owned brands including Rover, Land Rover, MG, Mini and Austin. Its partnership with Honda produced models such as the Rover 200 and 600 series. In 2000 BMW broke up the group: Land Rover went to Ford, Mini was retained by BMW, and the remaining Rover and MG operations were sold to the Phoenix Consortium as MG Rover, which collapsed in 2005.
