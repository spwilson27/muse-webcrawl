Sailing at the 2024 Summer Olympics

Sailing at the 2024 Summer Olympics in Paris was held from 28 July to 8 August 2024 at the Marseille Marina on the Mediterranean coast. A total of 330 sailors competed in ten events. The programme included the Olympic debut of kiteboarding (Formula Kite), while the men's and women's kite and windsurfing classes used foiling equipment.

Competition format

Each event consisted of an opening series of fleet races, in which boats scored points equal to their finishing position, followed by a medal race (or medal series for the board classes) for the leading competitors. The low-point scoring system applied, with the worst race score discarded in most classes.

Medal summary

Australia's Matt Wearn successfully defended his title in the men's ILCA 7, while Marit Bouwmeester of the Netherlands won the women's ILCA 6, becoming the most decorated female Olympic sailor. The inaugural Formula Kite gold medals went to Valentin Bontus of Austria and Ellie Aldridge of Great Britain. Italy, the Netherlands, and other traditional sailing nations featured prominently in the medal table.
