The ARM Cortex-A55 is a microarchitecture implementing the ARMv8.2-A 64-bit instruction set designed by ARM Holdings' Cambridge design centre. The Cortex-A55 is a 2-way decoded in-order superscalar pipeline. It is the successor of the ARM Cortex-A53 and aims for significantly improved performance in the mid-range segment, both in performance and energy efficiency.

Features

The Cortex-A55 serves as the successor of the Cortex-A53, designed to improve performance and energy efficiency over the A53. ARM states the A55 should have 15% improved power efficiency and 18% increased performance relative to the A53. Memory access and branch prediction are also improved relative to the A53.

The Cortex-A75 and Cortex-A55 cores are the first products to support ARM's DynamIQ technology. The successor to big.LITTLE, this technology is designed to be more flexible and scalable when designing multi-core products.

Licensing

The Cortex-A55 is available as a SIP core to licensees, and its design makes it suitable for integration with other SIP cores into one die constituting a system on a chip (SoC).
