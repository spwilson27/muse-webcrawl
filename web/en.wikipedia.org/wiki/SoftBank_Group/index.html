SoftBank Group Corp. is a Japanese multinational investment holding company headquartered in Minato, Tokyo. Founded in 1981 by Masayoshi Son as a software distributor, it grew into one of the world's largest technology investors, best known for the SoftBank Vision Fund, the largest technology-focused venture capital fund in the world.

History

SoftBank built its fortune through telecommunications (acquiring Vodafone Japan in 2006 and US carrier Sprint in 2013) and landmark investments, most famously an early US$20 million stake in Alibaba that grew to be worth over US$100 billion. In 2016 it acquired British chip designer ARM Holdings for £24 billion. It launched the Vision Fund in 2017 with nearly US$100 billion, investing in companies such as Uber, WeWork, DoorDash, and ByteDance.

Business

The group holds stakes in telecommunications companies (SoftBank Corp. in Japan), semiconductor design (Arm), e-commerce, robotics, and hundreds of technology startups through its Vision Funds. WeWork's failed 2019 IPO caused large losses and led to a more cautious investment strategy, while Arm's 2023 Nasdaq listing was one of the largest IPOs of that year.
