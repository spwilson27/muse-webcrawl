Đông Hà is the capital city of Quảng Trị Province in the North Central Coast region of Vietnam. It lies on National Highway 1A and near the intersection with Highway 9, the east–west corridor linking Vietnam to Laos.

History

During the Vietnam War, Đông Hà was the northernmost town of South Vietnam, situated just south of the Vietnamese Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) along the Bến Hải River. It was the site of a major United States Marine Corps base and figured prominently in the 1972 Easter Offensive, when North Vietnamese forces captured the city. The nearby Đông Hà Bridge became famous after Marine Captain John Ripley destroyed it to slow the advance.

Geography

The city sits on the banks of the Thạch Hãn River near the coast of the South China Sea. Its location makes it a transport hub for trade between central Vietnam and Laos via the Lao Bảo border gate.

Transport

Đông Hà is served by the North–South Railway and the Ho Chi Minh Highway. Highway 9 connects it westward to Savannakhet in Laos.
