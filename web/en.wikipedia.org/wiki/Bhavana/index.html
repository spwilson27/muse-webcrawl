Bhavana

Bhāvanā (Pali; Sanskrit: भावना) literally means "development", "producing" or "calling into existence". It is an important concept in Buddhist practice, referring to mental development or meditation — the deliberate cultivation of wholesome mental states and insight.

In Buddhism

In the Pali Canon, bhavana is frequently used in compounds such as citta-bhavana (development of mind), kaya-bhavana (development of body), metta-bhavana (cultivation of loving-kindness) and panna-bhavana (development of wisdom). Two broad kinds of meditation are distinguished: samatha-bhavana, the development of calm and concentration, and vipassana-bhavana, the development of insight.

Wider usage

The term appears across Indian religions and philosophy, including Hindu and Jain usage, where it denotes contemplation, cultivation or the bringing of something into being. In everyday South Asian usage, related words such as bhavna can mean feeling or sentiment.
