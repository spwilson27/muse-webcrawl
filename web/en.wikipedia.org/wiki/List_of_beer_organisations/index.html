List of beer organisations

This is a list of organisations concerned with beer and brewing: trade associations, consumer groups, certification bodies, research institutes, and competition organizers.

Trade and industry associations

  • Brewers Association (United States) — trade group for small and independent craft brewers; publishes the Beer Advocate-independent style guidelines and runs the Great American Beer Festival
  • Beer Institute (United States) — represents larger brewers and importers
  • Brewers of Europe — umbrella body for European brewers' associations
  • British Beer and Pub Association (BBPA) (United Kingdom) — represents brewers and pub companies
  • Society of Independent Brewers (SIBA) (United Kingdom)
  • Deutscher Brauer-Bund (German Brewers Federation)
  • Brewers Association of Japan
  • Canadian Brewers / Beer Canada
  • Institute of Brewing and Distilling (IBD) — professional qualifications body, founded as the Laboratory Club (1886)

Consumer organisations

  • CAMRA — Campaign for Real Ale (United Kingdom, founded 1971) — champions traditionally brewed cask ale
  • European Beer Consumers' Union (EBCU) — federation of national consumer groups
  • RateBeer and BeerAdvocate — online rating communities (commercial communities rather than formal organisations)
  • American Homebrewers Association (AHA) — founded 1978 by Charlie Papazian; part of the Brewers Association

Style, judging and competitions

  • Beer Judge Certification Program (BJCP) — trains and certifies beer judges; publishes widely used style guidelines (founded 1985)
  • Great American Beer Festival (GABF) — U.S. competition run by the Brewers Association
  • World Beer Cup — international competition, Brewers Association
  • European Beer Star — competition in Germany
  • Brussels Beer Challenge / International Brewing Awards (UK, since 1886)

Research and technical institutes

  • Siebel Institute of Technology (Chicago, 1872) — brewing education
  • Versuchs- und Lehranstalt für Brauerei (VLB Berlin) — brewing research institute
  • Weihenstephan (TUM School of Life Sciences) — brewing science at Freising, Germany
  • American Society of Brewing Chemists (ASBC) — methods of analysis
  • Master Brewers Association of the Americas (MBAA)
  • Campden BRI (UK) — food and drink research including brewing
  • Brewing, Food & Beverage Industry Suppliers Association and related trade bodies

Historical

  • Brewers' Guilds (medieval Europe) — e.g., the London Brewers' Company (chartered 1438)
  • United States Brewers' Association (USBA) (1862–1986) — predecessor trade body
