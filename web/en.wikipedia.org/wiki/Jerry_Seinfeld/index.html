Jerome Allen Seinfeld (born April 29, 1954) is an American stand-up comedian, actor, writer, and producer. He is best known as the co-creator, with Larry David, and star of the sitcom Seinfeld (1989–1998), in which he played a semi-fictionalized version of himself. The series, often described as "a show about nothing," became one of the most popular and influential television comedies of all time, dominating American ratings in the 1990s. Seinfeld specializes in observational comedy about the minutiae of everyday life and is regarded as one of the most successful comedians in history.

Early life and stand-up career

Seinfeld was born in Brooklyn, New York, and grew up in Massapequa, Long Island. He graduated from Queens College with a degree in communications and theater, and began performing at open-mic nights in New York clubs in 1976 while working jobs such as selling light bulbs by phone. Through the late 1970s and 1980s he built a reputation as a precise, writerly stand-up, appearing frequently on The Tonight Show Starring Johnny Carson and Late Night with David Letterman, and starring in HBO specials.

Seinfeld

In 1989 NBC aired The Seinfeld Chronicles, the pilot for the sitcom created by Seinfeld and Larry David, in which the comedian's stand-up routines framed episodes built around trivial social predicaments. With its ensemble — Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Jason Alexander, and Michael Richards — the show grew into a cultural phenomenon, coining phrases such as "master of your domain," "yada yada," and "no soup for you." Seinfeld the actor won an Emmy and a Golden Globe, and the series won the Emmy for Outstanding Comedy Series in 1993. He famously turned down a record-breaking offer to continue and ended the show in 1998 after nine seasons; the finale drew over 76 million viewers.

Later work

After the sitcom, Seinfeld returned to touring as a stand-up and documented the process in the film Comedian (2002). He co-wrote, produced, and voiced the animated film Bee Movie (2007), and from 2012 to 2019 hosted the web and streaming series Comedians in Cars Getting Coffee, combining his comedy with his famous collection of vintage automobiles, especially Porsches. Netflix specials Jerry Before Seinfeld (2017) and 23 Hours to Kill (2020) continued his stand-up, and in 2024 he directed and starred in the film Unfrosted. His book Is This Anything? (2020) collected his favorite material from five decades of writing.

Personal life

Seinfeld married Jessica Sklar in 1999; they have three children. He is known for his car collection, philanthropy, and practice of Transcendental Meditation.
