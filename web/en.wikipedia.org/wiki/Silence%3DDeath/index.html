Silence=Death

"Silence = Death" is a slogan and graphic emblem of AIDS activism, created in New York City in 1986–87 by the Silence=Death Project, a collective of six gay men. The poster pairs the slogan with a pink triangle on a black background.

Design and origins

The pink triangle reclaimed a Nazi concentration-camp badge used to mark homosexual prisoners, inverted to point upward as a symbol of resistance. The poster declared that silence about the AIDS epidemic — by government, media and individuals — amounted to death for those affected.

ACT UP

In 1987 the newly founded AIDS Coalition to Unleash Power (ACT UP) adopted the image as its central emblem, and it became one of the most recognisable political graphics of the late twentieth century, reproduced on posters, T-shirts and banners at demonstrations worldwide.
