Bostik

Bostik is a French manufacturer of adhesive and sealing products for construction, industry, and consumers. Founded in 1889, the company is a subsidiary of the Arkema group and is one of the largest adhesive producers in the world, employing several thousand people across dozens of countries.

Headquarters: Colombes, France

History

The company traces its origins to the Boston Blacking Company, founded in 1889 in Chelsea, Massachusetts, which made adhesives and dyes for the shoe industry. Its British arm produced the glue that gave the Bostik name its prominence; the brand became so familiar in Britain and Australia that "Bostik" is used generically for sticky tack and glue sticks. The business passed through owners including Emhart, Total, and finally Arkema, which acquired it in 2015 for about €1.7 billion.

Products

Bostik makes construction sealants and tile adhesives, assembly adhesives for transport and packaging, and consumer products including Blu Tack reusable adhesive putty, glue sticks, and super glues. Its mascot, a gecko, appears across its branding worldwide.

Sponsorship

The brand has a long association with cycling sponsorship, including title sponsorship of French professional cycling events, reflecting its French corporate base.
