List of presidents of France

The President of France is the head of state of the French Republic. The office was created in 1848 for the Second Republic; France has had presidents under the Second, Third, Fourth and Fifth Republics. The longest-serving president was François Mitterrand (14 years), and the shortest-serving was Jean Casimir-Perier (about six months).
