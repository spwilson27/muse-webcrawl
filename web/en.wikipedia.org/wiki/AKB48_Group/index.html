AKB48 Group

The AKB48 Group (AKB48グループ) is a family of Japanese idol groups produced by Yasushi Akimoto, built around AKB48 of Akihabara, Tokyo, and its sister groups in Japan and across Asia.

Japanese sister groups

Sister groups include SKE48 (Sakae, Nagoya), NMB48 (Namba, Osaka), HKT48 (Hakata, Fukuoka), NGT48 (Niigata), STU48 (Setouchi) and the rival official Nogizaka46 family, which is often associated with the wider 48/46 ecosystem.

International groups

Overseas sister groups have included JKT48 (Jakarta), BNK48 (Bangkok), MNL48 (Manila), AKB48 Team SH (Shanghai), AKB48 Team TP (Taipei), CGM48 (Chiang Mai) and others, each performing local-language versions of the AKB48 repertoire in dedicated theatres.

Concept

The groups follow the "idols you can meet" concept: daily theatre performances, handshake events, and fan-voted general elections (Senbatsu Sōsenkyo) that selected the line-ups of AKB48 singles. See also AKB48.
