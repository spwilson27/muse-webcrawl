Pratt & Whitney Canada

Pratt & Whitney Canada (PWC or P&WC) is a Canadian aircraft engine manufacturer based in Longueuil, Quebec. A subsidiary of the American company Pratt & Whitney (itself part of RTX Corporation), it specialises in small and medium gas turbine engines for regional airliners, business jets, helicopters, and general aviation. The company is best known for the PT6 turboprop, one of the most successful aircraft engines in history, and the PW100 family that dominates the regional turboprop airliner market.

History

The company was founded in 1928 as a service and repair organisation for Pratt & Whitney engines in Canada. During the Second World War it manufactured Wasp radial engines under licence. In the 1950s it began designing its own engines, leading to the PT6, first run in 1960 and certified in 1963, whose free-turbine layout and reliability made it the standard engine for small turboprop aircraft.

Products

Major families include the PT6 turboprop and turboshaft; the PW100 series powering ATR and Dash 8 regional airliners; the PW200 for light helicopters; the PW300, PW500, and PW600 turbofans for business jets; the PW800 for large business jets; and auxiliary power units. More than 100,000 engines have been produced, and PWC engines power aircraft in over 200 countries.
