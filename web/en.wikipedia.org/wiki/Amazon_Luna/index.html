Amazon Luna

Amazon Luna
Developer: Amazon
Launched: 2020 (early access); 1 March 2022 (full launch)
Type: Cloud gaming service

Amazon Luna is a cloud gaming platform developed and operated by Amazon. Announced in September 2020, it allows subscribers to stream games over the internet to supported devices including Fire TV, Windows PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, and mobile devices, without dedicated game hardware downloads or installations.

Model

Unlike catalogue-wide subscriptions, Luna originally organised content into "channels" — including Luna+, a Retro channel, and partner channels from publishers such as Ubisoft — each with its own monthly fee. Amazon Prime members receive access to a rotating selection of games at no extra cost. Amazon also sold a dedicated Luna Controller, which can connect directly to the service's servers over Wi-Fi to reduce input latency.

History

Luna entered early access in the United States in October 2020 and launched fully in March 2022, later expanding internationally. The service competed with Google Stadia (discontinued in 2023), Xbox Cloud Gaming, and GeForce Now in the cloud gaming market.
