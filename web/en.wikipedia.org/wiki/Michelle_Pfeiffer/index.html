Michelle Marie Pfeiffer (born April 29, 1958) is an American actress. One of the most acclaimed performers of her generation, she rose to prominence in the 1980s and has received three Academy Award nominations, a Golden Globe Award and a BAFTA Award.

Career

Pfeiffer first gained wide notice as Elvira Hancock in Scarface (1983) and went on to leading roles in The Witches of Eastwick (1987), Married to the Mob (1988) and Tequila Sunrise (1988). Her performance as Susie Diamond in The Fabulous Baker Boys (1989) won her a Golden Globe and an Oscar nomination. She earned further Academy Award nominations for Dangerous Liaisons (1988) and Love Field (1992), and played Catwoman in Batman Returns (1992), an interpretation widely regarded as definitive. Other notable films include The Age of Innocence (1993), Dangerous Minds (1995), What Lies Beneath (2000) and Hairspray (2007).

Later work

After a period of reduced activity to raise her family, Pfeiffer returned to prominence with roles in Mother! (2017), Murder on the Orient Express (2017) and as Janet van Dyne in Marvel's Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018) and its sequels. In 2017 she received an Emmy nomination for playing Ruth Madoff in the television film The Wizard of Lies, and she later starred in French Exit (2020) and the anthology series The First Lady (2022), portraying Betty Ford.
