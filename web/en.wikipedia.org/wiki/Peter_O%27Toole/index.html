Peter Seamus O'Toole (2 August 1932 – 14 December 2013) was an English actor known for his leading roles on stage and screen. He achieved international recognition playing T. E. Lawrence in Lawrence of Arabia (1962), for which he received his first of eight Academy Award nominations for Best Actor. He holds the record for the most Oscar nominations for acting without a competitive win, and received an Honorary Academy Award in 2003.

Career

O'Toole trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art and made his name at the Bristol Old Vic in the 1950s. His film breakthrough as Lawrence brought worldwide fame. Subsequent acclaimed performances included Henry II in both Becket (1964) and The Lion in Winter (1968), Goodbye, Mr. Chips (1969), The Ruling Class (1972), The Stunt Man (1980), My Favorite Year (1982) and, late in his career, Venus (2006). He also voiced Anton Ego in Pixar's Ratatouille (2007).

Personal life and legacy

Celebrated for his flamboyant lifestyle as well as his talent, O'Toole was part of a generation of charismatic British and Irish actors. He retired from acting in 2012 and died the following year. He is frequently ranked among the greatest actors of the twentieth century.
