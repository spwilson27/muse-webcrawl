King's Lynn

King's Lynn, known until 1537 as Bishop's Lynn and locally as Lynn, is a port and market town in the borough of King's Lynn and West Norfolk in the county of Norfolk, England, on the River Great Ouse near the Wash.

History

The town developed from the 11th century around the salt trade and became one of England's most important medieval ports, trading with the Hanseatic League; its Hanseatic Warehouse survives. Notable buildings include the Custom House (1683), the Guildhall and two large parish churches, St Margaret's and St Nicholas' Chapel. Once a major fishing and shipbuilding port, it remains a working port and market centre.
