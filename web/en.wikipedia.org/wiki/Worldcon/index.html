Worldcon

The World Science Fiction Convention, generally called Worldcon, is an annual convention of science fiction and fantasy fans held since 1939. It administers the Hugo Awards.

First held: 1939, New York
Organiser: World Science Fiction Society
Awards: Hugo Awards

Organisation

Each Worldcon is run by a local committee chosen by members of the World Science Fiction Society two years in advance; sites have included London, Helsinki, Dublin, and Chengdu. Members vote for the Hugo Awards and future sites.

History

Interrupted only by World War II, Worldcon anchored organised fandom, spawning traditions, fanzines, and filk. Recent years saw debates over Hugo voting slates. See Science fiction convention.
