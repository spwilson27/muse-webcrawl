Chi River

The Chi River (Thai: แม่น้ำชี) is the longest river wholly within Thailand, flowing about 765 kilometres through the northeastern region of Isan before joining the Mun River in Si Sa Ket province, a short distance before the Mun flows into the Mekong.

Course

The Chi rises in the Phetchabun Mountains of Chaiyaphum province and flows eastward through Khon Kaen, Maha Sarakham, Roi Et and Yasothon provinces. Its basin, together with that of the Mun, forms the largest river system of the Khorat Plateau.

Use

The river supports irrigation, fisheries and local transport, and several dams and weirs regulate its flow for agriculture in the dry season.
