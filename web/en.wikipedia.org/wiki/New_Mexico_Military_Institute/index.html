New Mexico Military Institute

The New Mexico Military Institute (NMMI) is a public military junior college and high school in Roswell, New Mexico. Founded in 1891, it is one of five military junior colleges in the United States and operates under the authority of the state of New Mexico with support from its Board of Regents.

History

NMMI was founded as the Goss Military Institute in 1891 by Colonel Robert S. Goss and Captain Joseph C. Lea, and was taken over by the territory in 1893–94. Its historic campus, built largely in a uniform Gothic Revival style of buff brick, is centred on the Sally Port and Hagerman Barracks. The school adopted the motto "Duty, Honor, Achievement".

Academics and corps

The institute enrolls cadets in grades 9–12 ("Old Cadets" in the high school program) and a two-year college program awarding an Associate of Arts degree. All students are members of the Corps of Cadets, live in barracks, wear uniforms, and follow a regimented daily schedule. NMMI hosts an Early Commissioning Program through Army ROTC, allowing college cadets to commission as second lieutenants after two years. Its preparatory program also sends cadets to the federal service academies.

Notable alumni

Alumni include hotelier Conrad Hilton, Dallas Cowboys owner-era executive and broadcaster figures, actor Owen Wilson (who attended), and journalist Sam Donaldson; the most famous graduate is Roger Staubach, the Heisman Trophy-winning Naval Academy quarterback who later led the Dallas Cowboys, though he attended NMMI for a postgraduate year.
