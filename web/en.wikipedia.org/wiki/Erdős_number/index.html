Erdős number

Erdős number
Named afterPaul Erdős (1913–1996)
MeaningCollaborative distance from Erdős in the co-authorship graph
Erdős's own number0
His direct collaborators511 (Erdős number 1)

The Erdős number describes the "collaborative distance" between mathematician Paul Erdős and another person, as measured by authorship of mathematical papers. Erdős himself has Erdős number 0; his 511 direct co-authors have number 1; their other collaborators have number 2, and so on.

Distribution

The Erdős Number Project at Oakland University maintains the data. Almost every publishing mathematician with a finite Erdős number lies within a few steps: the median is around 5, and the great majority of Fields medallists have numbers of 3 or less. A person with no chain of co-authorships connecting to Erdős is said to have an infinite Erdős number.

Related notions

The idea inspired the Bacon number for film actors (via Kevin Bacon) and the combined Erdős–Bacon number. Analogous measures appear in network science as shortest-path distances in collaboration graphs — a playful illustration of "small world" phenomena.
