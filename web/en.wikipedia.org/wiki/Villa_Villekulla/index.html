Villa Villekulla

Villa Villekulla (Swedish: Villa Villekulla) is a fictional house that is the home of Pippi Longstocking, the main character in the series of children's books by Swedish author Astrid Lindgren. The house is described as a dilapidated and colourful villa at the end of a small Swedish town, next door to the Settergren family's well-kept house, where Pippi's friends Tommy and Annika live.

Description

In the books, Villa Villekulla is an old, rather shabby house with a garden, a porch and a large oak tree in which Pippi keeps lemonade. Pippi lives there alone with her monkey, Mr Nilsson, and her horse, Lilla Gubben, who lives on the porch. She moved in at the age of nine, believing her father, a sea captain, would return; the house had been bought by her father so that they could live there together when he retired from the sea.

In adaptations

The house used in the 1969 Swedish television series and the subsequent films is located on the island of Gotland, near Visby, at the Kneippbyn holiday resort. After filming, the house was preserved as a tourist attraction; it was later moved a few kilometres to its present site at Kneippbyn, where visitors can enter the rooms decorated as in the films. A replica of the villa also features in Astrid Lindgren's World (Astrid Lindgrens Värld), the theme park in Lindgren's hometown of Vimmerby, where scenes from the stories are performed daily.
