Ben Wa balls

Ben Wa balls, also known as rin-no-tama, Venus balls, or geisha balls, are small, marble-sized balls, usually hollow and containing a smaller, heavier ball inside, which are used as vaginal exercise weights or as a sex toy. Traditionally made of metal or stone, modern versions are often made of silicone-covered weighted spheres, sometimes joined by a cord.

History

The use of vaginal weights is traced in popular accounts to Japan and China in earlier centuries, where hollow balls containing a smaller ball (and sometimes a tiny quantity of mercury or a chime) were said to be used in erotic practice; the name rin-no-tama is Japanese. Historical documentation of the practice is thin, and many origin claims are difficult to verify.

Use

As exercise devices, weighted balls are retained by the pelvic floor muscles, and their use is related to Kegel exercises intended to strengthen the pelvic floor. As a toy, the inner ball rolls or shifts with movement, producing vibration or a sense of fullness. Weighted vaginal devices are also used clinically in some continence rehabilitation programmes.

Safety

Devices intended for vaginal use should be made of non-porous, body-safe materials and cleaned thoroughly; unjoined loose balls are discouraged because of retrieval difficulty and hygiene concerns.
