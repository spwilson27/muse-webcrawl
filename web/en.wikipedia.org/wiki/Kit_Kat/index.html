Kit Kat is a chocolate-covered wafer bar of joined fingers that can be broken apart, created by Rowntree's of York, England, in 1935 as "Rowntree's Chocolate Crisp" and renamed Kit Kat in 1937.

History and marketing

The name came from the 18th-century Kit-Cat Club. The slogan "Have a break… have a Kit Kat," used since 1958, is among advertising's most famous. Nestlé acquired Rowntree in 1988; in the United States the bar is made under licence by Hershey.

Variants

Kit Kat is globally popular, especially in Japan, where Nestlé has released hundreds of limited flavours — matcha, sake, sweet potato and many more — and where "Kitto Katsu" ("surely win") makes it a good-luck gift for students. Sizes range from two-finger bars to Kit Kat Chunky.
