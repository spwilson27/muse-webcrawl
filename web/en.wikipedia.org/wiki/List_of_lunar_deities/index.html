List of lunar deities

A lunar deity is a god or goddess associated with the Moon in mythology. Lunar deities occur in most cultures and traditions; unusually among pantheons, the Moon is more often represented by a male god in some traditions (Mesopotamian, Hindu, Japanese and Germanic mythology) and by goddesses in others (Greek, Roman and Egyptian traditions include both). The Moon's phases, its influence on tides and its role in calendars made it a natural object of worship.
