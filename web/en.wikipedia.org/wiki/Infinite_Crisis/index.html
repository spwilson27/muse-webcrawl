Infinite Crisis

Infinite Crisis is a 2005–2006 comic book crossover storyline published by DC Comics, written by Geoff Johns with art principally by Phil Jimenez. The seven-issue limited series was a sequel to the landmark 1985 Crisis on Infinite Earths and served as a major restructuring event for the DC Universe, following the lead-in miniseries Countdown to Infinite Crisis, The OMAC Project, Rann–Thanagar War, Day of Vengeance and Villains United.

Plot

The story follows the return of the Kal-L Superman and Lois Lane of Earth-Two, together with Alexander Luthor Jr. and the Superboy of Earth-Prime, who had survived the original Crisis in a paradise dimension. Concluding that the post-Crisis Earth had become corrupted and its heroes darker, they attempt to restore the multiverse and recreate a "perfect" Earth. Superboy-Prime, increasingly unstable, becomes a central antagonist, while the heroes of Earth confront the OMAC cyborgs, a fractured Justice League and the magic-based crisis triggered by the Spectre.

Aftermath

The series concluded with the restoration of a multiverse of 52 parallel Earths, the deaths of several characters, and a relaunch of DC's line under the banner "One Year Later", in which ongoing titles jumped forward twelve months. The weekly series 52 filled in the missing year. Infinite Crisis was followed by Final Crisis in 2008, completing an informal Crisis trilogy.
