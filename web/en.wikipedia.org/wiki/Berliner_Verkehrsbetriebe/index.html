Berliner Verkehrsbetriebe
Founded: 1928
Headquarters: Berlin
Modes: U-Bahn, tram, bus, ferry

The Berliner Verkehrsbetriebe (BVG) is the main public transport company of Berlin, Germany, operating the U-Bahn, trams, buses and ferries of the capital.

Operations

Founded in 1928, BVG runs Berlin's underground network, an extensive tram system mainly in the east, hundreds of bus routes and Fährten ferries, within the Verkehrsverbund Berlin-Brandenburg tariff area.

Branding

BVG is known for its yellow vehicles and humorous, self-deprecating advertising campaigns, including the slogan 'Weil wir dich lieben' ('Because we love you').
