Columbus is the capital and most populous city of the U.S. state of Ohio. Located near the centre of the state at the confluence of the Scioto and Olentangy rivers, it is the county seat of Franklin County. Founded in 1812 specifically to serve as the state capital, Columbus grew through successive annexations into the largest city in Ohio by population and one of the fastest-growing large cities in the Midwest.

History

The site was chosen in 1812 as a compromise capital location; the neighbouring town of Franklinton, founded in 1797, preceded it across the river. The National Road and later canals and railroads spurred growth, and the city became a manufacturing and transportation hub. The statehouse, completed in 1861, is a notable Greek Revival building without a dome.

Economy and culture

Columbus has a diverse economy anchored by government, education, insurance, retail and logistics; it is home to Ohio State University, one of the largest universities in the United States, and to companies such as Nationwide Insurance and L Brands. Cultural institutions include the Columbus Museum of Art, COSI science centre and the Arena District sports venues; the Columbus Crew (soccer) and Blue Jackets (ice hockey) are its major professional teams.
