Glamorgan County Cricket Club

Glamorgan County Cricket Club (Welsh: Criced Morgannwg) is one of eighteen first-class county clubs in the domestic cricket structure of England and Wales, and the only Welsh club among them. Founded in 1888, it joined the County Championship in 1921.

History

Glamorgan won the County Championship in 1948, 1969, and 1997, the last under the captaincy of Matthew Maynard with a famous victory sealed at Taunton. Its home ground is Sophia Gardens in Cardiff, which has hosted Test cricket since the 2009 Ashes. Notable players include Wilf Wooller, Tony Lewis, Viv Richards, Robert Croft, and Marnus Labuschagne.

Honours

  • County Championship: 1948, 1969, 1997
  • One-Day Cup / Sunday League titles in 1993, 2002, 2004; One-Day Cup 2021 and 2024
