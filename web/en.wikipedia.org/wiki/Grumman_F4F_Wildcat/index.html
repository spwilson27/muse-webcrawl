The Grumman F4F Wildcat is an American carrier-based fighter aircraft that entered service in 1940 with the United States Navy and the British Royal Navy, where it was initially known as the Martlet. First used by the British in the North Atlantic, the Wildcat was the only effective fighter available to the United States Navy and Marine Corps in the Pacific Theater during the early part of the Second World War.

The less powerful but more maneuverable Mitsubishi A6M Zero proved to be a tough opponent for the Wildcat, but the F4F's ruggedness, heavy armament, and self-sealing fuel tanks, combined with tactics such as the Thach Weave, gave it a credited kill-to-loss ratio of 5.9:1 in 1942 and 6.9:1 for the entire war. Lessons learned from the Wildcat were later applied to the faster F6F Hellcat.

Design and development

Grumman's fighter design work began with the two-seater GF biplane; the company progressed to its first monoplane fighter, the XF4F-1, which lost a fly-off competition to the Brewster F2A Buffalo. Redesigned as the XF4F-2 and then the XF4F-3 with a supercharged Pratt & Whitney Twin Wasp radial engine, the aircraft entered production as the F4F-3. A distinctive feature was the Sto-Wing, a manually folded wing patented by Leroy Grumman that allowed more aircraft to be stowed aboard carriers.

Operational history

Wildcats served with distinction at the battles of the Coral Sea, Midway, and during the Guadalcanal campaign, where Marine squadron VMF-223 and naval squadrons held the line against Japanese air attacks. From 1943, production shifted to the FM series built by General Motors' Eastern Aircraft Division; the FM-2, powered by a more powerful Wright Cyclone engine, operated from escort carriers until the end of the war.

Variants

  • F4F-3: early production model with four 0.50-inch guns and non-folding wings.
  • F4F-4: folding wings, six guns, and armour; the principal 1942 variant.
  • FM-1 / FM-2: General Motors-built versions; the FM-2 had a taller tail and more powerful engine.
  • Martlet: British Fleet Air Arm designation for F4F variants.

Specifications (F4F-4)

Crew: 1. Length: 28 ft 9 in. Wingspan: 38 ft. Maximum speed: 320 mph (515 km/h) at 18,800 ft. Range: 770 mi (1,240 km). Armament: six 0.50 in (12.7 mm) M2 Browning machine guns and up to two 100 lb bombs.
