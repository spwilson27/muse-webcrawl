Journal of Fluid Mechanics

The Journal of Fluid Mechanics is a peer-reviewed scientific journal covering all aspects of fluid mechanics. Founded in 1956 by George Batchelor and published by Cambridge University Press, it is widely regarded as the leading journal in the field, publishing theoretical, computational, and experimental research on the mechanics of fluids.

History

George Keith Batchelor, a Cambridge applied mathematician and collaborator of G. I. Taylor, founded the journal in 1956 to provide an international forum for the rapidly growing field of fluid mechanics, and edited it for four decades. Under Batchelor the journal set exacting standards for clarity and rigour, famously returning manuscripts for stylistic revision as well as scientific review. It published many landmark papers of twentieth-century fluid mechanics, spanning turbulence, boundary layers, waves, convection, multiphase flow, and geophysical and astrophysical flows.

Scope and publication

The journal publishes original research papers, and since the 2010s has included "Focus on Fluids" review and commentary articles highlighting significant recent papers. It is issued in multiple volumes per year with both print and online editions, and is abstracted in the major scientific indexes. Its impact factor has typically been around 3–4, and it remains a central venue for work in theoretical, experimental, and computational fluid dynamics.
