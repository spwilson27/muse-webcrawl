Department of Justice and Constitutional Development

The Department of Justice and Constitutional Development is a department of the South African government responsible for the administration of justice and the courts. Headed by the Minister of Justice and Constitutional Development, it oversees court services, the Office of the Chief Justice's support functions, and bodies such as the Legal Aid Board.

Role

The department promotes constitutional development, manages state litigation through the State Attorney, and supports the lower courts and Masters of the High Court. Prosecutions are handled by the constitutionally independent National Prosecuting Authority.
