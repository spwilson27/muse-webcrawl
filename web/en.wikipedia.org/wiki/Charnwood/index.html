Charnwood

Charnwood may refer to several places and things, most notably the Borough of Charnwood in Leicestershire, England, and Charnwood Forest, the upland area within it.

Borough of Charnwood

The Borough of Charnwood is a local government district in northern Leicestershire named after Charnwood Forest. Its largest town is Loughborough, home to Loughborough University, and the administrative centre of the borough.

Charnwood Forest

Charnwood Forest is an area of rocky upland famous for its ancient Precambrian rocks, among the oldest in England, which yielded the Ediacaran fossil Charnia. The area includes Bardon Hill, the highest point in Leicestershire.

Other uses

Charnwood is also a suburb of Canberra, Australia, and the name appears in various institutions and businesses.
