Junior Certificate

The Junior Certificate (Irish: Teastas Sóisearach) was an educational qualification awarded in Ireland by the State Examinations Commission to students who completed the junior cycle of secondary education and passed the associated examinations, usually at age 15 or 16 after three years of study.

History and reform

Introduced in 1989 to replace the Intermediate Certificate, the Junior Certificate examined subjects at Higher, Ordinary and Foundation levels, with results graded from A to NG. From 2017 it was progressively replaced by the Junior Cycle Profile of Achievement under the Framework for Junior Cycle, which combines classroom-based assessments with a final examination in a reduced number of subjects.
