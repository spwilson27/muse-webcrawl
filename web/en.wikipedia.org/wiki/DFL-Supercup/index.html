DFL-Supercup

The DFL-Supercup, known since 2025 as the Franz Beckenbauer Supercup, is a German football super cup contested between the winners of the Bundesliga and the winners of the DFB-Pokal. It is organized by the Deutsche Fußball Liga (DFL) and traditionally played shortly before the start of the league season.

History

The competition's predecessor, the DFB-Supercup, was played from 1987 to 1996 before being discontinued in favour of the Ligapokal. After the Ligapokal was itself abolished, the Supercup returned as an official competition under the DFL in 2010. Unofficial editions, such as the 2008 match between Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund, were staged by the clubs in the interim.

Format

The match is a single game, usually hosted by the DFB-Pokal winner or the league runner-up, since 2020 typically in the stadium of the cup winner. If one team wins both the league and the cup, the Bundesliga runners-up qualify instead. Drawn matches go directly to a penalty shoot-out. Bayern Munich is the record winner, with more than ten titles.
