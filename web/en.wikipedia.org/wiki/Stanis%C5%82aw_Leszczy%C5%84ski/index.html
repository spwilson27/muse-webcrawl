Stanisław Leszczyński (1677–1766) was King of Poland, Grand Duke of Lithuania and later Duke of Lorraine. Crowned in 1705 under Swedish protection during the Great Northern War, he was deposed in 1709 and elected king a second time in 1733, triggering the War of the Polish Succession.

Lorraine

Under the Treaty of Vienna (1738), Stanisław abdicated the Polish throne in exchange for the Duchy of Lorraine, which he ruled until his death. At his court in Lunéville and Nancy he was a generous patron of the arts, building the celebrated Place Stanislas in Nancy.

Family

His daughter Maria Leszczyńska married Louis XV of France, making Stanisław the grandfather of three French kings.
