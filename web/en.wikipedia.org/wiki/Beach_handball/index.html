Beach handball is a team sport played on sand in which two teams pass a ball using their hands, aiming to throw it into the goal of the other team. A variant of indoor handball adapted for the beach, it is governed internationally by the International Handball Federation and is known for its fast pace and acrobatic, spectator-friendly goals.

Rules

Matches are played on a sand court measuring 27 by 12 metres, with goals at each end. Each team fields four players, including a goalkeeper (or "specialist" when attacking). A match consists of two halves of ten minutes; each half is scored separately and must have a winner, decided by a "golden goal" if tied. If each team wins one half, the match is decided by a shootout in which players attempt one-on-one throws against the goalkeeper. Spectacular goals — such as in-flight ("alley-oop") goals, 360-degree spin shots and goals scored by the specialist — count for two points, encouraging acrobatic play. Players play barefoot, and physical contact is more restricted than in the indoor game.

Competitions

The first official beach handball tournaments were held in Italy in the early 1990s. The IHF Beach Handball World Championships have been held every two years since 2004 for both men and women; Brazil and Croatia have been among the most successful nations. Beach handball has featured at the World Games since 2001 (as an invitational sport and later officially) and at the Youth Olympic Games in Buenos Aires in 2018, replacing indoor handball.
