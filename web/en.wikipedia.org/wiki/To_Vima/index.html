To Vima

To Vima (Το Βήμα)
Type: Weekly newspaper (formerly daily)
Founded: 1922
Headquarters: Athens, Greece
Language: Greek

To Vima (Greek: Το Βήμα, "The Tribune") is a Greek newspaper founded in 1922. Historically one of Greece's most influential daily newspapers, it has been associated with the centre and centre-left of Greek politics and long formed part of the Lambrakis Press Group alongside the daily Ta Nea.

History

Founded by publisher Dimitrios Lambrakis's circle in 1922, the paper became a central voice in Greek public life through the 20th century. Following financial difficulties at its parent group, the daily print edition ceased in 2010; the title continued as a Sunday weekly and an online edition.

Content

To Vima covers politics, economics, culture and science, and its Sunday edition is known for long-form reporting, book supplements and opinion writing by prominent Greek academics and politicians.
