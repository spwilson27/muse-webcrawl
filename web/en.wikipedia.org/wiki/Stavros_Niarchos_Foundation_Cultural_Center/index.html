The Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Center (SNFCC; Greek: Κέντρο Πολιτισμού Ίδρυμα Σταύρος Νιάρχος) is a complex in the bay of Faliro in Athens which includes new facilities for the National Library of Greece and the Greek National Opera, as well as the 210,000 m² Stavros Niarchos Park. The center was designed by architect Renzo Piano and funded entirely by the Stavros Niarchos Foundation. The project cost about €630 million and was completed in 2016; it was donated to the Greek state in February 2017.

Design

The buildings sit beneath an artificial sloping hill covered by the park, rising toward a panoramic terrace topped by a lightweight photovoltaic "energy canopy" supported on slender columns. A rectangular canal runs along the site's esplanade. The opera house contains a 1,400-seat main auditorium and a smaller alternative stage; the library includes a large public reading room and a book tower. The complex was designed to high environmental standards, achieving LEED Platinum certification.

History

The foundation announced plans in 2006 to fund a flagship cultural complex on the site of a former racetrack in Kallithea. Construction began in 2012. The SNFCC opened to the public in August 2016 and was formally handed over to the Greek state in February 2017. It has since become one of Athens' most visited public spaces, hosting festivals, concerts and exhibitions.
