Bill Hader (born June 7, 1978) is an American actor, comedian, writer, producer and director. He was a cast member on NBC's Saturday Night Live from 2005 to 2013, known for impressions and characters such as the flamboyant city correspondent Stefon.

Career

Hader appeared in films including Superbad (2007), Tropic Thunder (2008) and Trainwreck (2015), and voiced characters in animated films such as Inside Out (2015). He co-created and starred in the HBO dark comedy Barry (2018–2023), playing a hitman turned aspiring actor, winning Emmy Awards for his performance and acclaim for his directing.
