Taurus (astrology)

SymbolThe Bull
Dates (tropical)20 April – 20 May
ElementEarth
ModalityFixed
Ruling planetVenus
Glyph♉

Taurus (♉) (Latin for "bull") is the second astrological sign in the modern zodiac. It spans from 30° to 60° of celestial longitude. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this region from around April 20 to May 21. Taurus is an earth sign, one of the four fixed signs, and is traditionally ruled by the planet Venus.

Mythology

The sign is associated with the bull of Greek myth in which Zeus, transformed into a gentle white bull, carried the Phoenician princess Europa across the sea to Crete. In Babylonian astronomy the constellation was known as the Bull of Heaven, connected with the goddess Ishtar and the hero Gilgamesh. The sign corresponds to the constellation Taurus, which contains the bright star Aldebaran and the Pleiades and Hyades star clusters.

Traits in astrology

In popular astrology, Taurus is associated with steadiness, sensuality, practicality, loyalty and determination, and negatively with possessiveness and stubbornness. As an earth sign ruled by Venus, it is linked with the material world, comfort, food, money and the pleasures of the senses. Like the other signs, these associations have no empirical support and astrology is regarded by the scientific community as a pseudoscience.
