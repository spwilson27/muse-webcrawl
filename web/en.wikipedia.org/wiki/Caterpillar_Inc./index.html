Caterpillar Inc.

Industry: Heavy equipment
Founded: April 15, 1925
Headquarters: Irving, Texas, United States
Products: Construction and mining equipment, diesel engines, locomotives

Caterpillar Inc., also known simply as CAT, is an American construction, mining and other engineering equipment manufacturer. The company is the world's largest manufacturer of construction equipment.

In 2018, Caterpillar was ranked number 73 on the Fortune 500 list and number 265 on the Global Fortune 500 list. Caterpillar stock is a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average.

History

Caterpillar Tractor Company was formed in 1925 from the merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company of Stockton, California, and the C. L. Best Tractor Company of San Leandro, California. The company's early track-type tractors were used extensively in the First World War, and the "caterpillar" track design was associated with early tanks. In 1986 the company reorganised itself as Caterpillar Inc.

Products

Caterpillar manufactures a wide range of machinery, including bulldozers, excavators, wheel loaders, motor graders, off-highway trucks and backhoe loaders, as well as diesel and natural gas engines and industrial gas turbines. Its Financial Products segment provides financing and insurance services to customers and dealers. Caterpillar equipment is distributed through a worldwide dealer network and is recognisable by its distinctive "Caterpillar Yellow" trade dress.
