Giorgetto Giugiaro (born 7 August 1938) is an Italian automobile designer regarded as one of the most influential car designers of the twentieth century. He founded the design and engineering company Italdesign Giugiaro in 1968.

Career

Giugiaro began his career at Fiat before joining the coachbuilder Bertone, where he designed cars such as the Alfa Romeo Giulia Sprint GT, and later worked at Ghia. His Italdesign studio produced landmark designs including the Volkswagen Golf Mk1, the Lotus Esprit, the DeLorean DMC-12, the BMW M1, and the Fiat Panda.

Style and legacy

His crisp, angular "folded paper" styling defined much of 1970s automotive design. In 1999 he was named Car Designer of the Century by a jury of journalists. Beyond automobiles, Italdesign has designed trains, cameras, furniture, and other industrial products.
