Synthwave

Synthwave / Outrun
Origin: mid-2000s, internet / France
Stylistic roots: 1980s film soundtracks, Italo disco, electronic
Artists: Kavinsky, Perturbator, Carpenter Brut, Gunship

Synthwave (also outrun, retrowave or futuresynth) is a microgenre of electronic music drawing on 1980s film soundtracks, video games and pop culture, with a retro-futuristic neon aesthetic.

History

Emerging from mid-2000s French house (Kavinsky's "Testarossa Autodrive", 2006) and soundtrack-inspired producers such as College and Electric Youth — whose "A Real Hero" featured in Drive (2011) — synthwave spread via YouTube and Bandcamp. Substyles include darksynth (Perturbator, Carpenter Brut) and chillwave-adjacent retrowave.

Aesthetics

Artwork uses chrome lettering, wireframe grids, sunsets, palm trees, Testarossas and DeLoreans, evoking 1980s Miami and arcade culture. The genre influenced games (Hotline Miami, Far Cry 3: Blood Dragon), Stranger Things scoring, and The Weeknd's retro hits.
