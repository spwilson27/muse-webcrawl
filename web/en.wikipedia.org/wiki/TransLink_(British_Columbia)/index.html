TransLink (British Columbia)

TransLink, legally the South Coast British Columbia Transportation Authority, is the statutory authority responsible for the regional transportation network of Metro Vancouver in British Columbia, Canada, including public transport and major roads and bridges. It was created in 1998 and began operating in 1999, succeeding the transit functions of BC Transit in the Vancouver region. It adopted the public brand name TransLink in 2007.

Services

TransLink plans, finances, and oversees services delivered by its operating subsidiaries and contractors: bus services through Coast Mountain Bus Company, the automated SkyTrain network (Expo, Millennium, and Canada lines), the SeaBus passenger ferry across Burrard Inlet, the West Coast Express commuter railway, and HandyDART paratransit. It introduced the Compass Card smart fare card in 2015. TransLink is also responsible for the Major Road Network and several bridges, including the Golden Ears Bridge and the Pattullo Bridge.

Governance and funding

TransLink is governed by a board of directors and a Mayors' Council of Metro Vancouver mayors. Its funding comes from fares, a regional fuel tax, property taxes, parking taxes, and other levies.
