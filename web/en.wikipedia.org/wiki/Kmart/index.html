Kmart

Kmart
Founded: 1899 as S. S. Kresge Co.; Kmart stores from 1962
Headquarters: Hoffman Estates, Illinois, US
Industry: Retail

Kmart is an American retail chain that operated a large network of discount department stores. It grew out of the S. S. Kresge Company dime-store business founded by Sebastian S. Kresge in 1899. The first Kmart opened in Garden City, Michigan, in 1962.

History

Kmart expanded rapidly in the 1960s and 1970s, becoming one of the largest retailers in the United States, famous for its "Blue Light Specials." It struggled against competition from Walmart and Target, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2002, and in 2005 merged with Sears under Sears Holdings. A second bankruptcy in 2018 and transfer to Transformco were followed by the closure of almost all remaining stores, leaving only a handful of locations by the 2020s.

Operations

Stores sold clothing, electronics, home goods, groceries in Super Kmart formats, and operated Little Caesars and pharmacy departments in many locations. An Australian chain of the same name, owned separately by Wesfarmers, continues to trade in Australia and New Zealand.
