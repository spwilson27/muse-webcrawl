Junipero Serra Peak

Junipero Serra Peak is the highest mountain in the Santa Lucia Range of Monterey County, California, rising to 5,857 feet (1,785 m). It lies within Los Padres National Forest near the Ventana Wilderness.

Name and setting

Originally known as Santa Lucia Peak, it was renamed for Saint Junípero Serra, the Franciscan founder of the California missions; Mission San Antonio de Padua lies in the valley to the east. The summit, reached by a steep trail, offers views from the Pacific Ocean to the San Joaquin Valley and supports a relict stand of sugar pines near its top.
