Nuclear power in the United Kingdom

Nuclear power in the United Kingdom began in 1956 with Calder Hall, the world's first commercial nuclear power station.

Fleet

Britain built Magnox and Advanced Gas-cooled Reactor fleets, most now closed or closing, plus one pressurised water reactor at Sizewell B. Nuclear generates roughly 15% of UK electricity. New build includes Hinkley Point C in Somerset and planned Sizewell C, amid debate on cost, waste at Sellafield, and energy security.
