Trollhättan is a city and the seat of Trollhättan Municipality in Västra Götaland County, Sweden, located on the Göta älv river at the Trollhättan Falls. Its name roughly means "troll's cap" or "troll's bonnet".

Industry

The falls were long an obstacle to navigation, overcome by the Trollhätte Canal and locks, and later harnessed for hydroelectric power. Trollhättan was the home of Saab Automobile, founded in the city in 1947, and of the aero-engine manufacturer that became part of Volvo Aero and later GKN Aerospace. The city's economy suffered with Saab's bankruptcy in 2011.

Culture

Trollhättan hosts Film i Väst, one of Scandinavia's largest film production facilities, earning the city the nickname "Trollywood"; many Swedish films, including works by Lukas Moodysson and Lars von Trier productions, have been shot there. The annual Fallens Dagar festival celebrates the release of water over the falls.
