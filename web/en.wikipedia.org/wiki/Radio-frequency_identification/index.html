Radio-frequency identification

Radio-frequency identification (RFID) uses electromagnetic fields to automatically identify and track tags attached to objects. A system consists of a reader and tags containing electronically stored information; passive tags harvest energy from the reader's radio waves, while active tags have a battery.

Uses

RFID is used in supply chains and retail inventory, access cards, passports, toll collection, livestock and pet identification, library systems, and contactless payment related technologies. Frequencies range from low frequency (125 kHz) through UHF (860–960 MHz) and microwave bands.

Concerns

Privacy and security issues include unauthorised reading and cloning of tags, addressed by shielding, encryption, and kill commands.
