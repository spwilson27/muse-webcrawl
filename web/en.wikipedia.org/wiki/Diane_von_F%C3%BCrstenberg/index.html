Diane von Fürstenberg (born Diane Simone Michele Halfin, 31 December 1946) is a Belgian fashion designer best known for her iconic wrap dress, introduced in 1974, which became a symbol of women's liberation and power dressing.

Career

Born in Brussels to a Romanian-born father and a Greek-born mother, a Holocaust survivor, she married Prince Egon von Fürstenberg in 1969 and moved to New York. Her jersey wrap dress was an enormous commercial success, landing her on the cover of Newsweek in 1976. After selling and later relaunching her brand, the DVF label expanded into a global luxury lifestyle brand.

Philanthropy and honours

She has served as president of the Council of Fashion Designers of America (CFDA) and, with her husband Barry Diller, is a noted philanthropist, supporting the High Line in New York and the Statue of Liberty Museum.
