Pike Place Fish Market is an open-air fish market at Pike Place Market in Seattle, Washington, United States. Founded in 1930, it is famous worldwide for the tradition of its fishmongers throwing fish to one another across the counter when an order is placed.

The fish throwing

The custom began as a way to save steps: a monger at the display would toss the ordered fish to a colleague behind the counter for wrapping, calling out the order, which the crew repeats in unison. The spectacle draws large crowds and has made the stall one of Seattle's best-known tourist attractions alongside the nearby original Starbucks store.

Business philosophy

Under owner John Yokoyama, who bought the market in 1965, the business adopted the "world famous" service philosophy later popularised in the management book and training film Fish!, which used the market's playful, customer-focused atmosphere as a model of workplace motivation.
