Welfare reform

Welfare reform refers to changes in the operation of social welfare programmes, typically aiming to reduce dependency, impose work requirements, or limit eligibility.

United States

The Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996 replaced AFDC with TANF, adding time limits and work requirements. Caseloads fell sharply; effects on deep poverty remain debated.

Other countries

Reforms in the UK, including Universal Credit, and in other OECD states have pursued similar activation policies.
