List of Academy Award records

This list of Academy Award records is current as of the most recent ceremony held by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, which presents the Academy Awards (Oscars) each year for excellence in film.

Most awards for a film

Three films share the record of eleven Academy Awards won: Ben-Hur (1959), Titanic (1997) and The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (2003). The Return of the King uniquely won every category in which it was nominated. All About Eve (1950), Titanic and La La Land (2016) share the record for most nominations for a film, with fourteen each.

Age records

The youngest competitive Oscar winner is Tatum O'Neal, who won Best Supporting Actress for Paper Moon at age ten. The oldest acting winner is Anthony Hopkins, who won Best Actor for The Father at age 83. Adrien Brody became the youngest Best Actor winner at 29 for The Pianist.

Other notable records

Midnight Cowboy (1969) remains the only X-rated film to win Best Picture. Grand Hotel (1932) won Best Picture without a single other nomination. Three films — It Happened One Night (1934), One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest (1975) and The Silence of the Lambs (1991) — have won all five major categories: Picture, Director, Actor, Actress and Screenplay.
