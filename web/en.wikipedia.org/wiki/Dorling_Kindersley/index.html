Dorling Kindersley

Dorling Kindersley Limited, commonly known as DK, is a British multinational publishing company specialising in illustrated reference books for adults and children in 63 languages. Founded in London in 1974 by Christopher Dorling and Peter Kindersley as a book-packaging company, DK began publishing its own titles in the early 1980s and became renowned for its distinctive design style combining photography, cutaway illustrations, and concise text.

History

The company's Eyewitness series, launched in 1988, became an international success and defined its visual approach. In 2000, DK was acquired by the Pearson media group and became part of Penguin; following the Penguin Random House merger of 2013, DK sits within Bertelsmann's publishing operations. DK publishes across categories including travel guides (the Eyewitness Travel Guides), science, history, cookery, gardening, and children's non-fiction.
