Molson Coors Brewing Company

Molson Coors Beverage Company (known until 2020 as Molson Coors Brewing Company) is a Canadian-American multinational drink and brewing company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, with major operations in Montreal and Golden, Colorado. It was formed in 2005 by the merger of Canada's Molson Brewery (founded 1786) and the United States' Adolph Coors Company (founded 1873).

Its best-known brands include Coors Light, Miller Lite, Molson Canadian, Blue Moon, and Carling. In 2008 the company combined its United States operations with those of SABMiller in a joint venture called MillerCoors; when Anheuser-Busch InBev acquired SABMiller in 2016, Molson Coors purchased the remaining stake in MillerCoors, giving it full ownership of the Miller brand portfolio in the United States. The company is one of the largest brewers in the world by volume and has in recent years expanded into flavored beverages, hard seltzers, and non-alcoholic products.
