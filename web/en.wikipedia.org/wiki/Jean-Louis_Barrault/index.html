Jean-Louis Barrault

Jean-Louis Barrault (8 September 1910 – 22 January 1994) was a French actor, director, and mime artist who appeared in many French films and founded several important theatre companies.

Career

Barrault studied with Charles Dullin and Étienne Decroux, and joined the Comédie-Française in 1940. With his wife Madeleine Renaud he formed the Compagnie Renaud-Barrault in 1946, which staged classical and contemporary works in Paris.

Film work

He is best remembered internationally for playing Baptiste the mime in Marcel Carné's Children of Paradise (1945), widely regarded as one of the greatest French films. Other films include Drôle de drame and The Longest Day. See also mime artist.

Legacy

Barrault later directed the Théâtre d'Orsay and the Théâtre des Nations, and was a central figure in postwar French theatre.
