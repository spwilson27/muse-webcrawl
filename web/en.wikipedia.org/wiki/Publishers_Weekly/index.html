Publishers Weekly

Publishers Weekly (PW) is an American weekly trade news magazine targeted at publishers, librarians, booksellers, and literary agents. Published continuously since 1872, it bills itself as "the international news magazine of book publishing and bookselling."

Coverage

The magazine is best known for its pre-publication book reviews: PW reviews thousands of titles annually in short, unsigned notices, and its starred reviews are used by librarians and booksellers in purchasing decisions. It also publishes bestseller lists, industry news, author interviews, and special issues devoted to categories such as children's books (through its Children's Bookshelf coverage) and seasonal announcements issues listing forthcoming titles.

History

Founded in 1872 by Frederick Leypoldt as the Publishers' Weekly, the magazine was long associated with the R. R. Bowker company. It changed owners several times in the 2000s and was acquired in 2010 by PWxyz, LLC, led by former Bowker executive George Slowik Jr. The magazine maintains a substantial digital presence alongside its print edition.
