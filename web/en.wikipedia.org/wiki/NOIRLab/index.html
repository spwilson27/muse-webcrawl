NOIRLab
Full name: NSF National Optical-Infrared Astronomy Research Laboratory
Established: 2019
Operator: AURA, for the U.S. National Science Foundation
Headquarters: Tucson, Arizona, US

NOIRLab (National Optical-Infrared Astronomy Research Laboratory) is the United States national center for ground-based, nighttime optical and infrared astronomy. It was formed on 1 October 2019 by merging the operations of the National Optical Astronomy Observatory (NOAO), the Gemini Observatory, and the Vera C. Rubin Observatory construction project under a single management structure.

Facilities

NOIRLab operates the international Gemini Observatory (twin 8.1-metre telescopes in Hawaii and Chile), the Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory and Kitt Peak National Observatory, and the Community Science and Data Center. It partners in operating the Vera C. Rubin Observatory in Chile, whose Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST) uses the 8.4-metre Simonyi Survey Telescope.

Organization

NOIRLab is operated by the Association of Universities for Research in Astronomy (AURA) under a cooperative agreement with the U.S. National Science Foundation. Its programs provide open access to telescopes for U.S. astronomers, data archives, and community instrumentation development, as well as education and public outreach.
