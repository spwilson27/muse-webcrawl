The panther chameleon (Furcifer pardalis) is one of the largest and most colourful species of the genus.

Furcifer is a genus of chameleons whose member species are mostly endemic to Madagascar, with two species also found in the Comoros and one (Furcifer cephalolepis) endemic there. The generic name comes from the Latin furci- ("forked") and -fer ("bearing"), referring to the animals' forked feet. More than twenty species are currently recognised, and new ones continue to be described from Madagascar's forests.

Description

The genus contains some of the most strikingly coloured chameleons, with strong sexual dimorphism: males of species such as the panther chameleon (F. pardalis) show bright bands of blue-green, orange, and red, while females are smaller and more subdued. Size varies widely, from the small F. rhinoceratus group to Oustalet's chameleon (F. oustaleti) and the Parson's chameleon (F. parsonii) — relatives in the genus Calumma — which are among the largest chameleons by length. Many males bear casques, rostral appendages or horn-like projections used in display.

Habitat and behaviour

Furcifer species occupy habitats from eastern rainforests to dry western deciduous forests and spiny thickets. They are diurnal, arboreal ambush predators of insects, using independently rotating eyes and a ballistic tongue longer than the body. Most species are short-lived by vertebrate standards; the extreme case is Labord's chameleon (F. labordi), whose adults live only about four to five months after hatching — the shortest lifespan known for a terrestrial vertebrate — spending most of the year as eggs in the leaf litter.

Conservation and trade

Several Furcifer species are popular in the international pet trade, especially the panther chameleon, and all are listed under CITES Appendix II, with export quotas for wild-caught specimens from Madagascar. Habitat loss from deforestation is the principal threat; many species have restricted ranges in remaining forest fragments and are assessed as threatened on the IUCN Red List.
