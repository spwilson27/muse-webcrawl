Spinnin' Records is a Dutch electronic music record label founded in 1999 by Eelko van Kooten and Roger de Graaf. Based in Amsterdam, it became one of the most prominent labels in the electronic dance music (EDM) boom of the 2010s, launching or releasing music by artists including Martin Garrix, Afrojack, Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike, Oliver Heldens, Don Diablo, Tiësto and many others. Martin Garrix's breakthrough single "Animals" (2013) was released on the label and became an international hit.

The label operates numerous sublabels, among them Spinnin' Deep, Spinnin' Records itself for mainstage releases, and imprints such as Doorn Records (founded by Sander van Doorn), Musical Freedom's distribution partnership, Heldeep Records (Oliver Heldens) and Maxximize Records (Blasterjaxx). Spinnin' built a huge online following, with one of the most-subscribed music label channels on YouTube, and is known for its "Talent Pool" demo platform, which allows unsigned producers to submit tracks for community voting and possible signing.

Acquisition

In September 2017 Warner Music Group acquired Spinnin' Records in a deal reported to be worth over $100 million, as the major labels moved to consolidate leading independent EDM brands.
