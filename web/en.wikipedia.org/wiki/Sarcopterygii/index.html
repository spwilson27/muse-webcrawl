Sarcopterygii

Sarcopterygii — the lobe-finned fishes — is a class of vertebrates traditionally including coelacanths and lungfishes, and in cladistic terms also the tetrapods (four-limbed vertebrates), which evolved from lobe-finned ancestors in the Devonian.

Sarcopterygii
Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Chordata
Groups: Coelacanths, lungfish, tetrapods
Fossil range: Silurian–present

Characteristics

Lobe-finned fishes have fleshy, lobed paired fins supported by a central axis of bones, homologous to tetrapod limbs. Enamel-covered teeth and a distinctive skull pattern also characterise the group.

Evolution

Sarcopterygians appeared by the late Silurian. In the Devonian, tetrapodomorph fishes such as Tiktaalik bridged the transition to land vertebrates. Living non-tetrapod sarcopterygians are only eight species: two coelacanths and six lungfishes.
