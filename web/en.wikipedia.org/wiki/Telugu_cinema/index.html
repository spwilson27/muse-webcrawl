Telugu cinema

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Telugu cinema, popularly known as Tollywood, is the segment of Indian cinema dedicated to the production of motion pictures in the Telugu language, widely spoken in the states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. It is based in Film Nagar, a neighbourhood of Hyderabad, and is one of the largest film industries in India by output and box office.

History

The first Telugu feature film, Bhishma Pratigna (1921), was a silent film by Raghupathi Venkaiah Naidu, regarded as the father of Telugu cinema. The first talkie, Bhakta Prahlada, followed in 1932. The industry shifted from Madras to Hyderabad in the 1990s. Hyderabad hosts Ramoji Film City, one of the largest film studio complexes in the world.

International recognition

Telugu films gained worldwide prominence with S. S. Rajamouli's Baahubali duology (2015–2017) and RRR (2022), whose song "Naatu Naatu" won the Academy Award for Best Original Song. Earlier icons of Telugu cinema include N. T. Rama Rao, Akkineni Nageswara Rao, and directors such as K. Viswanath.

Industry

The industry produces several hundred films annually and has a large diaspora market, particularly in the United States, where Telugu films regularly achieve wide releases.
