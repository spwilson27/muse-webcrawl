Coca-Cola Amatil

Coca-Cola Amatil Limited was one of the largest bottlers of non-alcoholic ready-to-drink beverages in the Asia-Pacific region and one of the world's top Coca-Cola bottlers. Headquartered in Sydney, Australia, it distributed Coca-Cola products and other drinks in Australia, New Zealand, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea, Fiji and Samoa.

History

The company originated as British Tobacco Company (Australia) and diversified into beverages, taking its name from Amatil, the tobacco group. It acquired the Australian Coca-Cola bottling franchise and grew through acquisitions across the region. In 2021, Coca-Cola European Partners acquired Coca-Cola Amatil, and the business was renamed Coca-Cola Europacific Partners Australia, ending its separate listing on the Australian Securities Exchange.

Products

Besides Coca-Cola soft drinks, the company bottled water, energy drinks, juices and ready-to-drink coffee, and distributed alcoholic beverages in some markets.
