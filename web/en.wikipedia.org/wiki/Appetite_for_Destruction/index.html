Appetite for Destruction

Appetite for Destruction is the debut studio album by the American hard rock band Guns N' Roses, released on July 21, 1987, by Geffen Records. It contains the singles "Welcome to the Jungle", "Sweet Child o' Mine" and "Paradise City", and is widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time.

Background and reception

The album initially sold slowly, but after extensive touring and the success of "Sweet Child o' Mine", it reached number one on the US Billboard 200 in 1988. It has since sold over 30 million copies worldwide, making it one of the best-selling debut albums ever. The record's raw sound and depictions of urban life in Los Angeles set it apart from the polished glam metal of the era. Its original cover art, based on a painting by Robert Williams, was replaced after retailers objected, and the substitute cross-and-skulls artwork became iconic.

Track listing

Side one includes "Welcome to the Jungle", "It's So Easy", "Nightrain", "Out ta Get Me", "Mr. Brownstone" and "Paradise City"; side two includes "My Michelle", "Think About You", "Sweet Child o' Mine", "You're Crazy", "Anything Goes" and "Rocket Queen".
