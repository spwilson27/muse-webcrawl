Iron Fist (countermeasure)

Iron Fist (Hebrew: חץ דורבן, Hetz Dorban, lit. "Porcupine Arrow") is a hard-kill active protection system (APS) designed by Israel Military Industries (IMI, now part of Elbit Systems) for armoured fighting vehicles. It intercepts and destroys incoming anti-tank rockets, anti-tank guided missiles and kinetic-energy penetrators with a small explosive projectile.

Design

The system uses radar sensors (developed by IAI's Elta division) and passive electro-optical detectors to track threats, then fires a small explosive round from one of two rotating launchers. The blast destroys or destabilises the incoming projectile at a distance from the vehicle, minimising collateral damage compared with earlier hard-kill systems.

Variants and users

A lighter version, Iron Fist Light, is intended for lighter vehicles. The system has been selected for Israel Defense Forces vehicles including the Namer and Eitan, integrated on the British Army's Challenger 3 programme as part of trials, and demonstrated on the US Bradley Fighting Vehicle.
