Dance Gavin Dance

Dance Gavin Dance is an American post-hardcore band from Sacramento, California, formed in 2005. The band is known for its distinctive blend of post-hardcore, math rock, progressive rock and funk influences — a style often dubbed "swancore" after guitarist Will Swan — and for its dual-vocal approach pairing clean singing with harsh screamed vocals.

History

The band released its debut EP, Whatever I Say Is Royal Ocean, in 2006, followed by its first full-length album, Downtown Battle Mountain (2007), on Rise Records. Its early line-up featured vocalists Jonny Craig and Jon Mess alongside guitarists Will Swan and Sean O'Sullivan. The band has undergone numerous line-up changes, particularly among vocalists; singers have included Kurt Travis and Tilian Pearson, who joined in 2012 and fronted the band for a decade.

Musical style and albums

Studio albums include Dance Gavin Dance (2008), Happiness (2009), Downtown Battle Mountain II (2011), Acceptance Speech (2013), Instant Gratification (2015), Mothership (2016), Artificial Selection (2018), Afterburner (2020) and Jackpot Juicer (2022). The group's intricate guitar work, shifting time signatures and interplay of melodic and screamed vocals have made it a leading act in its scene.

Longtime bassist Tim Feerick died in 2022. Following Tilian Pearson's departure, the band continued with Jon Mess and Andrew Wells sharing vocal duties.
