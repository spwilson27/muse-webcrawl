Surah

Surah (Arabic: سورة)
Total: 114 surahs in the Quran
Divided into: Ayahs (verses)
First: Al-Fatiha; Longest: Al-Baqarah (286 ayahs)

A surah (Arabic: سُورَة, romanized: sūrah, plural: سُوَر, suwar) is a chapter of the Quran. There are 114 surahs, each divided into verses called ayahs.

Characteristics

Surahs range from Al-Baqarah with 286 verses to Al-Kawthar with only 3. They are conventionally classified as Meccan or Medinan according to where their verses were revealed to Muhammad. Except for Surah At-Tawbah, each begins with the Basmala: "In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

Names and ordering

Surahs are known by names derived from a distinctive word or theme, such as Al-Baqarah (The Cow), Yusuf (Joseph), and Maryam (Mary). Their order in the mushaf is roughly longest to shortest after Al-Fatiha, not chronological. Muslims recite Al-Fatiha in daily prayer (salah).
