The Bahamas

Commonwealth of The Bahamas
Capital: Nassau
Official language: English
Population: approx. 410,000 (2023)
Currency: Bahamian dollar (BSD)
Government: Unitary parliamentary constitutional monarchy

The Bahamas, officially the Commonwealth of The Bahamas, is an island country in the Atlantic Ocean north of Cuba and east of Florida. It consists of more than 3,000 islands, cays and islets, of which about 30 are inhabited, spread over some 13,880 square kilometres of land. The capital and largest city is Nassau, on the island of New Providence, where roughly 70 per cent of the population lives. The name is generally derived from the Spanish baja mar, "shallow sea".

History

The islands were first settled by the Lucayan people, a branch of the Taíno, from around the 9th century. Christopher Columbus made his first landfall in the Americas at an island he named San Salvador in October 1492. Spain claimed the islands but never settled them, instead enslaving and transporting the Lucayans to Hispaniola, which destroyed the indigenous population within a generation. English settlers arrived from Bermuda in 1648, and the Bahamas became a British crown colony in 1718, when Woodes Rogers suppressed the pirates — including the notorious Blackbeard — who had made Nassau their "Pirate Republic". After the American Revolution, Loyalists resettled the islands with enslaved Africans, laying the demographic foundation of the modern population, which is overwhelmingly of African descent.

Independence and economy

The Bahamas attained internal self-government in 1964 and full independence from the United Kingdom on 10 July 1973, remaining a Commonwealth realm with Charles III as head of state. Sir Lynden Pindling, leader of the Progressive Liberal Party, was the first prime minister. The economy rests on tourism — which accounts for around half of GDP and employment — and offshore financial services. The country is famous for its clear waters, the pink-sand beaches of Harbour Island, the swimming pigs of Exuma, and the Atlantis resort on Paradise Island. In September 2019 Hurricane Dorian devastated Abaco and Grand Bahama, the strongest storm in the country's recorded history.
