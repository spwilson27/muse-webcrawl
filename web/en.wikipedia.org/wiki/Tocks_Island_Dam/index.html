Tocks Island Dam

Tocks Island Dam
Location: Delaware River, New Jersey / Pennsylvania, U.S.
Status: Cancelled
Authorised: 1962; deauthorised 2002

The Tocks Island Dam was a proposed dam on the Delaware River between New Jersey and Pennsylvania in the United States, just north of the Delaware Water Gap. Authorised by Congress in 1962 for flood control, water supply, hydroelectric power and recreation, the project would have created a 37-mile-long reservoir lake.

Opposition and cancellation

The project required acquiring roughly 72,000 acres and displaced thousands of residents through eminent domain. Growing environmental opposition, geological concerns about the dam site, cost increases and changing priorities stalled construction for decades. The surrounding lands were transferred to the National Park Service, forming the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area, and Congress formally deauthorised the dam in 2002.

Legacy

The abandoned project left behind emptied communities and remains a notable case study in American environmental politics and the use of eminent domain.
