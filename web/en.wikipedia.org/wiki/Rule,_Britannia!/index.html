Rule, Britannia!

"Rule, Britannia!" is a British patriotic song, first performed in 1740. It originated from the masque Alfred, co-written by the Scottish poet James Thomson and the English poet David Mallet, with music by Thomas Arne.

History

The song was first performed at Cliveden, the country home of Frederick, Prince of Wales, on 1 August 1740, as part of the masque Alfred. Its refrain — "Rule, Britannia! Britannia, rule the waves! / Britons never, never, never shall be slaves" — celebrates British maritime power and liberty, reflecting the growth of the British Empire and the Royal Navy.

Modern performances

"Rule, Britannia!" is traditionally performed at the Last Night of the Proms in London, where it forms part of a maritime fantasia arranged by Sir Henry Wood. It is also sung at sporting events, on naval occasions, and was formerly used by the BBC as a signature tune.
