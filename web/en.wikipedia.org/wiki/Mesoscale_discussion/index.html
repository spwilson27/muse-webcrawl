A mesoscale discussion (MD) is a product issued by the Storm Prediction Center of the United States National Weather Service concerning the current state of, and short-term trend in, severe or hazardous weather on the mesoscale (tens to hundreds of kilometres). Discussions provide an up-to-date meteorological reasoning for areas where conditions are being monitored, frequently including a probability (in percent) that a severe thunderstorm watch or tornado watch will be issued within the next few hours.

Contents

A typical mesoscale discussion summarises recent observations — surface features, satellite and radar trends, and atmospheric soundings — and discusses whether convection is expected to initiate or intensify, the anticipated storm mode and hazards, and the evolution of ingredients such as instability, moisture, lift, and wind shear. Mesoscale discussions are also issued for heavy snow, extreme rainfall (by the Weather Prediction Center as mesoscale precipitation discussions), and fire weather in some contexts.

Use

MDs are widely used by broadcast meteorologists, emergency managers, and storm spotters to anticipate watch issuance and short-fuse convective trends between the issuance of convective outlooks and warnings.
