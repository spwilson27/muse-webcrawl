Protected areas of Colombia

The protected areas of Colombia are administered through the National System of Protected Areas (SINAP), coordinated by Parques Nacionales Naturales de Colombia. Colombia is one of the world's megadiverse countries, and its national natural parks cover roughly 14 million hectares of land and marine territory, in addition to regional and private reserves.

National natural parks

The system includes more than 60 national natural parks. Among the best known are Tayrona National Natural Park on the Caribbean coast, Los Nevados, Cocuy, Amacayacu in the Amazon region, Gorgona Island in the Pacific, and the Serranía de la Macarena. Chiribiquete National Park, expanded in 2018 to about 4.3 million hectares, is the largest tropical rainforest national park in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Categories

Besides national parks, the system recognises natural reserves, flora and fauna sanctuaries, a unique natural area (Los Estoraques), a parkway (Vía Parque Isla de Salamanca), regional integrated management districts, and civil society nature reserves.

Biodiversity

Colombia ranks first in the world in bird and orchid species and second in plants, amphibians, butterflies and freshwater fish. Protected areas span the Andean páramos, Amazon and Orinoco basins, Caribbean dry forest, and Pacific Chocó rainforests.
