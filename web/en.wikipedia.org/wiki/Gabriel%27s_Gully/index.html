Gabriel's Gully

LocationNear Lawrence, Otago, New Zealand
Named forGabriel Read
Gold discovered1861

Gabriel's Gully is a locality near Lawrence in Otago, New Zealand, where prospector Gabriel Read discovered gold in May 1861, triggering the Otago gold rush — the first major gold rush in New Zealand.

The discovery

Read, a Tasmanian prospector who had worked the Californian and Victorian fields, found gold in a gully off the Tuapeka River on 20 May 1861, reporting that he "shovelled away about two and a half feet of gravel" and found gold "shining like the stars in Orion on a dark frosty night".

The rush

News of the find spread quickly. Within months the Tuapeka goldfield held thousands of miners, and Dunedin boomed as its port and commercial centre. The field transformed Otago's economy and population and led to further rushes at places such as Naseby and the West Coast. A historic reserve now marks the site, and the discovery's centenary was celebrated in 1961.
