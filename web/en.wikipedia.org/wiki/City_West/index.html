City West

City West is a district of Berlin, Germany, forming the commercial centre of the former West Berlin. It is located in the Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf borough, roughly around the Kurfürstendamm boulevard, Breitscheidplatz and the Zoologischer Garten railway station. In Berlin usage, "City West" is distinguished from the historic city centre (Mitte) in the former East Berlin; during the division of the city it developed as the principal shopping and entertainment centre of West Berlin.

Kurfürstendamm, the main boulevard of City West.

Features

Key landmarks include the Kaiser Wilhelm Memorial Church (Gedächtniskirche) at Breitscheidplatz, the Europa-Center, the KaDeWe department store on Wittenbergplatz — one of the largest department stores in continental Europe — and the Zoological Garden. The Kurfürstendamm, often compared to the Champs-Élysées, is lined with flagship shops, hotels, theatres such as the Theater des Westens, and cafés.

History

The area grew rapidly in the late nineteenth century as the "New West" of prosperous Berlin. After the Second World War and the division of Berlin, City West became the commercial heart of West Berlin, symbolised by the rebuilt Memorial Church and modernist buildings such as the Bikini-Haus. After reunification in 1990, commerce shifted partly back to Mitte and Potsdamer Platz, but City West underwent substantial reinvestment in the 2010s, including new high-rise buildings around Zoologischer Garten station such as the Zoofenster and Upper West towers.
