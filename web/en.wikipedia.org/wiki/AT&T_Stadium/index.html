AT&T Stadium is a retractable-roof stadium in Arlington, Texas, United States. It serves as the home of the Dallas Cowboys of the National Football League (NFL) and was completed on May 27, 2009. Originally called Cowboys Stadium, it is nicknamed "Jerry World" after Cowboys owner Jerry Jones. At US$1.3 billion it was one of the most expensive sports venues ever built.

Design

The stadium is dominated by two monumental steel arches, each 1,225 feet (373 m) long, that support one of the largest domed roofs in the world. Inside hangs a center-hung video board once listed as the world's largest high-definition screen. The seating capacity is 80,000, expandable past 100,000 with standing room, which enabled a record NFL regular-season crowd of 105,121 in 2009 and the NBA All-Star Game record of 108,713 in 2010.

Events

The stadium hosted Super Bowl XLV in 2011, the first College Football Playoff National Championship Game in 2015, WrestleMania 32 (2016) and WrestleMania 38 (2022), numerous boxing matches and concerts, and the annual Cotton Bowl Classic. It is one of the venues selected for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, renamed "Dallas Stadium" for the tournament under FIFA's naming rules.
