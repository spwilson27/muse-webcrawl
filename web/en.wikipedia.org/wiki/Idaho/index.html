Overview

Idaho is a state in the Pacific Northwest and Mountain West of the United States, bordered by Washington and Oregon to the west, Nevada and Utah to the south, Montana and Wyoming to the east, and Canada to the north. Its capital and largest city is Boise.

Idaho is famed for potatoes — it produces roughly a third of the US crop and styles itself 'Famous Potatoes' on licence plates — but its economy also rests on agriculture, timber, mining (it is nicknamed the Gem State for its mineral and gemstone variety), technology around Boise, and outdoor recreation.

Geography

The state is dominated by the Rocky Mountains. The Snake River Plain arcs across the south, carved by the Snake River, which also forms Hells Canyon on the Oregon border — the deepest river gorge in North America. Craters of the Moon National Monument preserves stark lava fields, the Sawtooth Range is a centrepiece of alpine scenery, and the panhandle holds large lakes such as Coeur d'Alene and Pend Oreille. Idaho was among the fastest-growing US states in the 2010s–2020s.
