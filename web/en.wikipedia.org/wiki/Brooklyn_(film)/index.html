Brooklyn is a 2015 romantic period drama film directed by John Crowley and written by Nick Hornby, adapted from Colm Tóibín's 2009 novel of the same name. The film stars Saoirse Ronan as Eilis Lacey, a young Irish immigrant who leaves Enniscorthy, County Wexford, in the early 1950s to build a new life in Brooklyn, New York.

Plot

Eilis Lacey, having few prospects at home, emigrates to Brooklyn with the help of a Catholic priest, Father Flood. After severe homesickness she settles into work at a department store, studies bookkeeping at night, and falls in love with Tony Fiorello, an Italian-American plumber. When her sister Rose dies suddenly, Eilis returns to Ireland, where she is drawn into a comfortable life and a courtship with Jim Farrell. Torn between two homes and two futures, she must choose where her life belongs.

Reception

The film premiered at the 2015 Sundance Film Festival to widespread acclaim. It was nominated for three Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Actress for Ronan, and won the BAFTA Award for Outstanding British Film. Critics praised Ronan's restrained performance and the film's evocation of the immigrant experience.
