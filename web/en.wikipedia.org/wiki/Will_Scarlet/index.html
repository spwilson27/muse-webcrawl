Will Scarlet

First appearance: Early Robin Hood ballads

Group: Merry Men

Will Scarlet (also Scarlok, Scarlock, Scadlock, and Shacklock) is a prominent member of Robin Hood's Merry Men in English folklore. He is present in the earliest ballads, such as A Gest of Robyn Hode, and is one of the longest-standing characters of the legend along with Little John and Much the Miller's Son.

Character

In early tales Will Scarlet is a formidable fighter, sometimes associated with wielding two swords; in the ballad "Robin Hood and the Newly Revived" and others he appears as a close companion — in some versions a nephew or relative — of Robin Hood. His surname is often explained by the scarlet cloth or clothing he wears, and popular retellings dress him in red in contrast to Robin's green.

A local tradition in Nottinghamshire associates him with the village of Blidworth, where a grave in the churchyard of St Mary's is said to be his.

Portrayals

Will Scarlet has appeared in nearly every film and television version of the Robin Hood story, from early silent films to the 1938 The Adventures of Robin Hood, Disney's 1952 The Story of Robin Hood, the 2006 BBC series, and the 2010 and 2018 films. His personality varies from hot-headed youth to dandyish swordsman depending on the adaptation.
