List of cities in Austria

This is a list of cities and towns in Austria with a population of more than 10,000. Vienna, the capital, dominates the country demographically with roughly two million inhabitants — about a fifth of the national population.

Statutory cities

Fifteen cities hold the status of Statutarstadt (statutory city), combining municipal and district administration. They include the state capitals Eisenstadt, Graz, Innsbruck, Klagenfurt, Linz, Salzburg, St. Pölten, and Vienna, plus Krems, Rust, Steyr, Villach, Wels, Wiener Neustadt, and Wolfsberg.
