Smart speaker

A smart speaker is a type of loudspeaker and voice-command device with an integrated virtual assistant that offers interactive actions and hands-free activation with the help of one "hot word" (or several "hot words"). Some smart speakers can also act as a smart device that utilizes Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and other protocol standards to extend usage beyond audio playback, such as to control smart home devices, and can include other features like interoperability among a number of services and platforms, peer-to-peer connection through mesh networking, virtual assistants, and others.

History

The category was popularised by the launch of Amazon Echo in 2014, which brought the Alexa voice assistant into homes. Google followed with Google Home (now Google Nest) in 2016, powered by Google Assistant, and Apple released the HomePod in 2018 with Siri. Other manufacturers, including Sonos and Bose, have incorporated third-party assistants into their speakers.

Features

Typical functions include playing music from streaming services, providing weather forecasts, news briefings and sports results, setting timers and reminders, answering questions via web search, making calls, and controlling connected smart home appliances such as lights, thermostats, locks and cameras. Many devices connect through a companion smartphone app for setup and configuration.

Privacy concerns

The always-listening microphones required for wake-word detection have raised privacy concerns, particularly after reports of unintended recordings being stored or reviewed by human contractors. Manufacturers have responded with mute buttons, recording indicators, and controls allowing users to review and delete voice recordings.
