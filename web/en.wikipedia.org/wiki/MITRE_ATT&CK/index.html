MITRE ATT&CK

The ATT&CK matrix organizes adversary tactics and techniques
DeveloperMITRE Corporation
Released2013
TypeCybersecurity knowledge base / framework

MITRE ATT&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge) is a knowledge base and framework of cyber adversary behavior, developed by the MITRE Corporation beginning in 2013. It catalogs the tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs) used by real-world threat actors, based on observed intrusions, and is freely available to the cybersecurity community.

Structure

ATT&CK is organized as matrices for different environments — Enterprise, Mobile and Industrial Control Systems (ICS). Each matrix is arranged by tactics, the adversary's goals (such as initial access, persistence, privilege escalation, defense evasion, credential access, lateral movement and exfiltration), under which sit techniques and sub-techniques describing how those goals are achieved, along with documented procedures, mitigations and detection guidance.

Usage

Security teams use ATT&CK for threat intelligence, red teaming and adversary emulation, detection engineering, and assessing defensive coverage. It is complemented by related MITRE projects including the adversary emulation library, CALDERA, D3FEND (a knowledge graph of defensive countermeasures) and the ATT&CK Navigator tool for visualizing coverage.
