The Walt Disney Company, commonly known as Disney, is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate headquartered at the Walt Disney Studios complex in Burbank, California. Disney was founded on October 16, 1923, by brothers Walt and Roy O. Disney as Disney Brothers Studio; it also operated under the names Walt Disney Studio and Walt Disney Productions before changing its name to The Walt Disney Company in 1986.

Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, the company's headquarters since 1940.

History

The company established itself as a leader in the animation industry with Mickey Mouse (1928) and the first full-length animated feature, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937). Disneyland opened in Anaheim in 1955 and Walt Disney World in Florida in 1971. Under Michael Eisner and later Bob Iger, Disney acquired Pixar (2006), Marvel (2009), Lucasfilm (2012) and most of 21st Century Fox (2019), and launched the streaming service Disney+ in 2019.

Business segments

Disney operates through Entertainment (studios, television networks and streaming), Experiences (theme parks, resorts and cruise line) and Sports (ESPN). It is one of the world's largest media companies by revenue.
