List of cryptids

Cryptids are animals that cryptozoologists believe may exist somewhere in the wild but whose existence has not been confirmed by mainstream science. This list includes well-known creatures from folklore, reported sightings and hoaxes. Many cryptids are associated with particular regions, and some famous examples — such as the coelacanth, okapi and giant squid — were once considered legendary before being scientifically documented, though those confirmed species are generally no longer classified as cryptids.
