Waylon J. Smithers Jr. is a fictional character in the American animated television series The Simpsons, voiced by Harry Shearer. He is the loyal, sycophantic executive assistant to Charles Montgomery Burns, the owner of the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant. Smithers is characterised by his devotion to his boss — a running theme of the show — his competence in contrast to the plant's other staff, and his collection of Malibu Stacy dolls.

Role in the series

Smithers first appeared, initially as a disembodied voice over the plant's public-address system, in the series premiere episode "Simpsons Roasting on an Open Fire" (1989); his first on-screen appearance was in "Homer's Odyssey" (1990), where an animation error gave him dark skin and blond hair before his standard design was settled. His father, Waylon Smithers Sr., was Mr. Burns's original assistant and died heroically preventing a nuclear meltdown when Smithers was a baby — a backstory revealed in "The Blunder Years". Smithers handles the day-to-day running of the plant, is frequently shown performing every aspect of Burns's care, and usually acts as the voice of practical moderation to Burns's villainy, though he is often complicit in it.

Characterisation

For most of the series, Smithers's romantic devotion to Burns was conveyed through jokes and fantasy sequences while the character remained officially closeted. In the 2016 episode "The Burns Cage", Smithers comes out as gay and begins a relationship storyline, an arc developed by writer Rob LaZebnik, whose own son's coming out inspired the episode. Earlier episodes such as "Three Gays of the Condo" and the future-set "Holidays of Future Passed" also addressed his sexuality.

Cultural impact

Smithers is routinely listed among the show's most prominent supporting characters, and his evolving portrayal is frequently discussed in commentary on LGBTQ representation in animation.
