Hank Zipzer is a series of children's books co-authored by actor Henry Winkler and Lin Oliver, published by Grosset & Dunlap. The protagonist, Hank Zipzer, is a fourth-grader with dyslexia who lives in New York City.

Overview

The series began with Niagara Falls, or Does It? (2003) and ran to seventeen books. Hank's learning difficulties, based on Winkler's own experiences with dyslexia, are central to the stories, which emphasize creativity and perseverance. Font designed to be dyslexia-friendly was used in later editions.

Adaptations

A CBBC television series Hank Zipzer aired from 2014 to 2016, starring Nick James, with Winkler as music teacher Mr Rock. A Christmas film followed in 2016. A follow-up book series, Here's Hank, targets younger readers.
