Gunnar Nordahl (19 October 1921 – 15 September 1995) was a Swedish footballer who played as a striker. He is best known for his years at AC Milan, where he remains the club's all-time top scorer, and is regarded as one of the greatest Swedish players ever.

Career

Nordahl began his career in Sweden with IFK Norrköping, winning four Swedish championships, and scored 43 goals in just 33 appearances for the Swedish national team, including at the 1948 London Olympics, where Sweden won gold. In 1949 he joined AC Milan, forming the famous Swedish "Gre-No-Li" attacking trio with Gunnar Gren and Nils Liedholm.

Milan record

For Milan Nordahl scored 221 goals in 268 matches, winning two Serie A titles and finishing as the league's top scorer (capocannoniere) a record five times. He later played two seasons for Roma before returning to Sweden. His total of 225 Serie A goals ranked among the highest in the league's history.
