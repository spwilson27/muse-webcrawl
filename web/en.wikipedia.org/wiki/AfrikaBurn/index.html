AfrikaBurn
Location: Tankwa Karoo, Northern Cape, South Africa
First held: 2007
Type: Regional Burning Man event
Temporary city: Tankwa Town

AfrikaBurn is an annual arts and culture festival held in the Tankwa Karoo in South Africa's Northern Cape. It is the largest official regional event of the Burning Man network and one of the biggest participatory art festivals in Africa.

The event

Participants build a temporary settlement, Tankwa Town, featuring theme camps, large-scale artworks, mutant vehicles and performances. Following Burning Man's principles — including radical self-reliance, gifting, decommodification and leaving no trace — the event culminates in the burning of major artworks, including the San Clan sculpture.

History

AfrikaBurn was first held in 2007 on a private farm near the Tankwa Karoo National Park, drawing a few hundred participants; it has since grown to attract more than ten thousand attendees in peak years.
