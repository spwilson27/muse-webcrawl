Resident Evil

Resident Evil, known in Japan as Biohazard, is a Japanese horror game series and media franchise created by Capcom. It consists mainly of survival horror games, and also includes live-action and animated films, novels, comics and other media. The franchise centres on outbreaks of the T-virus and other biological weapons developed by the Umbrella Corporation, which transform humans and animals into zombies and other monsters.

History

The first Resident Evil was released for the PlayStation in 1996. Players control Chris Redfield or Jill Valentine, members of the Raccoon City police's S.T.A.R.S. unit, as they investigate a mansion filled with traps and undead creatures. The game popularised the survival horror genre with its fixed camera angles, limited resources and emphasis on puzzle solving. It was followed by Resident Evil 2 (1998) and Resident Evil 3: Nemesis (1999), set during the destruction of Raccoon City.

Resident Evil 4 (2005) reinvented the series with an over-the-shoulder camera and a greater emphasis on action, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made. After the more action-oriented fifth and sixth main entries, Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (2017) returned to the series' horror roots with a first-person perspective, followed by Resident Evil Village (2021). Remakes of the second, third and fourth games were released between 2019 and 2023 to critical acclaim.

Other media

A series of live-action films written and produced by Paul W. S. Anderson, beginning in 2002, starred Milla Jovovich as Alice and became the highest-grossing film series based on video games. Capcom has also produced computer-animated films and a Netflix animated series.
