Little Earthquakes

Little Earthquakes is the debut solo album by American singer-songwriter Tori Amos, released on 13 January 1992 in the United Kingdom and 25 February 1992 in the United States by Atlantic/East West Records. Amos had previously fronted the synth-pop band Y Kant Tori Read, whose 1988 album had failed commercially; Little Earthquakes re-established her as a piano-based confessional songwriter.

Background and songs

Built around Amos's piano and voice, the album addresses religion, sexuality, trauma and self-discovery. The a cappella song "Me and a Gun" recounts her own experience of sexual assault. Other tracks include "Crucify," "Silent All These Years," "Winter," "China," "Leather," "Precious Things" and "Girl." "Silent All These Years" became her signature song and was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Female Rock Vocal Performance (for the live version).

Reception

The album was a critical success and reached number 14 in the UK and number 54 on the US Billboard 200, eventually selling over two million copies worldwide. It is frequently included in lists of the greatest albums of the 1990s and of all time.
