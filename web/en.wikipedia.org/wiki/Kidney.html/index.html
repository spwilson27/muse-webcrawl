Kidney

The kidneys are two reddish-brown, bean-shaped organs in vertebrates, located in the retroperitoneal space on either side of the spine. They filter blood to remove waste and excess fluid as urine, and regulate electrolytes, blood pressure, and red blood cell production.

Anatomy and function

Each human kidney contains about a million nephrons, whose glomeruli filter blood and whose tubules reabsorb needed substances. The kidneys also secrete hormones including erythropoietin, renin and calcitriol (active vitamin D).

Disease

Kidney diseases include chronic kidney disease, acute kidney injury, glomerulonephritis and kidney stones. Severe failure is treated with dialysis or kidney transplantation.
