Aortic aneurysm

Aortic aneurysm
Specialty: Vascular surgery
Types: Abdominal (AAA), Thoracic (TAA)
Risk: Smoking, hypertension, atherosclerosis

An aortic aneurysm is an enlargement of the aorta to more than 1.5 times normal size. It usually causes no symptoms except when ruptured.

Classification

Abdominal aortic aneurysm (most common, infrarenal) and thoracic aortic aneurysm. Causes include atherosclerosis, connective tissue disorders such as Marfan syndrome, infection, and trauma.

Management

Surveillance ultrasound for small aneurysms, blood pressure control, smoking cessation. Repair by open surgery or endovascular aneurysm repair (EVAR) typically at 5.5 cm for AAA. Rupture is a surgical emergency with high mortality — see aortic rupture.
