Heart (radio network)

Heart is a network of adult contemporary radio stations in the United Kingdom, owned and operated by Global Media & Entertainment. The network began with Heart 106.2 in London in 1995 and expanded across the country through acquisitions and rebranding of local stations, notably the former Galaxy and One Network stations.

Format and programming

Heart plays adult contemporary music aimed mainly at 25–44-year-olds, with the slogan "Turn Up the Feel Good". Since 2019 most programming has been networked from London, including the national breakfast show hosted by Jamie Theakston and Amanda Holden, with only limited local opt-outs such as travel news and drivetime in some areas.

Expansion

The brand has extended to sister stations Heart 70s, Heart 80s, Heart 90s, Heart 00s, Heart Dance and Heart Christmas, available nationally on digital radio. Heart is consistently among the UK's most-listened-to commercial radio brands.
