Samir Geagea (Arabic: سمير جعجع; born 25 October 1952) is a Lebanese politician and former militia commander who leads the Lebanese Forces political party, one of the main Christian parties in Lebanon.

Civil war and imprisonment

During the Lebanese Civil War (1975–1990), Geagea rose to command the Lebanese Forces militia, the principal Christian armed faction, taking over its leadership in 1986. After the war he opposed Syrian domination of Lebanon and refused to join the post-war government. In 1994 he was arrested and convicted of wartime assassinations in trials his supporters consider politically motivated; he spent eleven years in solitary confinement, the only major war-era leader imprisoned.

Later career

Released under an amnesty in 2005 after the Syrian withdrawal following the Cedar Revolution, Geagea rebuilt the Lebanese Forces as a leading party of the March 14 Alliance and later emerged as one of the strongest critics of Hezbollah's armed status.
