Tokaido Shinkansen

The Tōkaidō Shinkansen (Japanese: 東海道新幹線) is a Japanese high-speed rail line operated by Central Japan Railway Company (JR Central). Opened in 1964 for the Tokyo Olympics, it was the world's first high-speed railway, connecting Tokyo and Shin-Ōsaka over 515.4 km.

Services include the fastest Nozomi, intermediate Hikari, and all-stations Kodama. Trains reach 285 km/h. The line is among the world's busiest, carrying over a billion passengers cumulatively and serving the Tōkaidō corridor cities including Yokohama, Nagoya and Kyoto.
