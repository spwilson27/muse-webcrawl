Philip III of Spain

King of Spain
Reign: 13 September 1598 – 31 March 1621
Born: 14 April 1578, Madrid, Spain
Died: 31 March 1621 (aged 42), Madrid
Predecessor: Philip II
Successor: Philip IV
Spouse: Margaret of Austria
House: Habsburg

Philip III (Spanish: Felipe III; 14 April 1578 – 31 March 1621) was King of Spain and Portugal (as Philip II of Portugal) from 1598 until his death in 1621. A member of the House of Habsburg, he was the son and successor of Philip II of Spain and his fourth wife, Anna of Austria.

Early life

Philip was born in Madrid, the only son of Philip II to survive to adulthood. Unlike his father, who was renowned for personally managing the affairs of his vast empire, Philip III was considered a rather passive monarch who delegated the business of government to his favourites, above all the Duke of Lerma, Francisco Gómez de Sandoval y Rojas, who effectively ruled Spain as valido (royal favourite) for much of the reign.

Reign

Philip III inherited an empire at war with England and the Dutch Republic, and deeply in debt. A policy of retrenchment followed: Spain made peace with England in the Treaty of London (1604), and in 1609 agreed the Twelve Years' Truce with the Dutch, an effective recognition of Dutch independence. Within the Iberian Peninsula, the reign is remembered for the expulsion of the Moriscos (1609–1614), in which some 300,000 descendants of converted Muslims were driven from Spain, with lasting economic damage to regions such as Valencia.

Abroad, Spain intervened in the early stages of the Thirty Years' War, sending troops under Ambrogio Spinola to the Palatinate in 1620. The court moved from Valladolid back to Madrid in 1606. Philip was a patron of the arts and an avid collector; the painter Diego Velázquez arrived at court shortly after his death.

Marriage and issue

In 1599 Philip married his cousin Margaret of Austria, sister of Emperor Ferdinand II. They had eight children, including Philip IV, who succeeded him, and Anne of Austria, queen consort of Louis XIII of France and mother of Louis XIV. Philip died in Madrid on 31 March 1621.
