Circumflex

The circumflex (◌̂) is a diacritic mark used in the Latin and Greek scripts. The character in precomposed form is also used in mathematical symbols, variously known as a hat, roof, house, caret or wedge.

Uses

In French, the circumflex marks vowels that historically followed an s that has since been lost (for example forêt, from Latin foresta), and can distinguish homophones (dû vs du). It is also used in Portuguese, Romanian, Welsh, Esperanto, and in the transliteration of various languages.

Computing

The free-standing caret character ^ (U+005E) is used in computing for exponentiation in some languages, as an XOR operator in others, and in regular expressions to mark the beginning of a line or string.
