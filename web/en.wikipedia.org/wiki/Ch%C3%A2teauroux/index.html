Châteauroux

Châteauroux is a commune in central France and the prefecture (capital) of the Indre department in the Centre-Val de Loire region. The city grew up around a castle founded in the 10th century by Raoul of Déols — the château Raoul, which gave the town its name — and lies on the river Indre about 270 km south of Paris.

History and economy

Châteauroux developed as a cloth-manufacturing town and later as a garrison and railway centre. From 1951 to 1967 it hosted a major United States Air Force base, Châteauroux-Déols Air Base, one of the largest American logistics installations in Europe. Today the Marcel-Dassault airport and the surrounding logistics zone occupy the former base site, and the city's football club, LB Châteauroux, plays at the Stade Gaston Petit.
