Wētā FX

Wētā FX, formerly known as Weta Digital, is a New Zealand digital visual effects and computer animation company based in Miramar, Wellington. It was founded by Peter Jackson, Richard Taylor, and Jamie Selkirk in 1993 to produce the visual effects for the film Heavenly Creatures.

Major work

The company rose to worldwide prominence with The Lord of the Rings film trilogy (2001–2003), for which it developed the MASSIVE crowd-simulation software and created the digital character Gollum. Its team won Academy Awards for Best Visual Effects for The Fellowship of the Ring, The Two Towers, The Return of the King, King Kong (2005), Avatar (2009), and The Jungle Book (2016). Other major projects include the Planet of the Apes reboot series, noted for its performance-capture apes, and Avatar: The Way of Water (2022).

Technology

Wētā FX is known for advances in performance capture, digital creatures, water and fire simulation, and virtual production. In 2021 Unity Technologies acquired its tools, technology, and engineering division for US$1.625 billion, after which the remaining visual effects company rebranded from Weta Digital to Wētā FX.
