List of fractals by Hausdorff dimension

A fractal is a mathematical set that typically displays self-similarity and has a Hausdorff dimension strictly greater than its topological dimension. This article lists fractals ordered by increasing Hausdorff dimension.

The Sierpiński triangle has Hausdorff dimension log(3)/log(2) ≈ 1.585.
