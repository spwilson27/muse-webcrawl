Industry: Artificial intelligence, drug discovery

Isomorphic Labs is a British artificial intelligence company applying AI to drug discovery. It was announced in November 2021 as a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., spun out from Google DeepMind, and is led by DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis as chief executive. The company's name reflects the idea of finding a mapping — an isomorphism — between the domains of biology and information science, so that tools from machine learning can be applied to fundamental biological problems.

Background and technology

The company builds on DeepMind's AlphaFold 2, the system that in 2020 effectively solved the problem of predicting protein structures from amino acid sequences at the CASP14 assessment, and on its successors. In May 2024, DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs jointly introduced AlphaFold 3, which extended accurate structure prediction to interactions between proteins and other biomolecules such as DNA, RNA and small-molecule ligands — precisely the interactions needed to design drugs. Isomorphic Labs aims to use such models to build a general "AI-first" drug discovery platform that can design candidate medicines computationally, including for diseases without existing treatments, and to work both on its own internal pipeline and with pharmaceutical partners.

Partnerships and funding

In January 2024 the company announced multi-target research collaborations with Eli Lilly and Novartis, worth up to about US$82.5 million upfront in total and up to roughly US$2.9 billion in potential milestone payments. In 2025 Isomorphic Labs raised its first external funding round, a US$600 million round led by Thrive Capital, as it moved toward first-in-human clinical trials of AI-designed drugs.
