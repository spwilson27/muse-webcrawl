Ranbir Kapoor

Ranbir Kapoor (born 28 September 1982) is an Indian actor known for his work in Hindi-language films. One of the highest-paid actors in India, he has received numerous accolades, including seven Filmfare Awards, and is regarded as one of the finest actors of his generation. He belongs to the fourth generation of the Kapoor film family: he is the son of actor Rishi Kapoor and the grandson of actor-director Raj Kapoor.

Early life and debut

Kapoor studied filmmaking at the School of Visual Arts in New York and method acting at the Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute. He worked as an assistant director on his father's Aa Ab Laut Chalen (1999) and Sanjay Leela Bhansali's Black (2005) before making his acting debut in Bhansali's Saawariya (2007), a commercial failure for which he nevertheless won the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut.

Career

Kapoor established himself with the coming-of-age film Wake Up Sid (2009), the comedy Ajab Prem Ki Ghazab Kahani (2009) and the political drama Raajneeti (2010). He won consecutive Filmfare Best Actor awards for portraying a troubled musician in Rockstar (2011) and a deaf-mute man in Barfi! (2012). Further success came with the romantic comedy Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani (2013) and the drama Ae Dil Hai Mushkil (2016), interspersed with acclaimed performances in Tamasha (2015).

His portrayal of actor Sanjay Dutt in the biopic Sanju (2018) became one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time and earned him another Best Actor award. After a four-year absence he returned with Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva (2022), the fantasy action film Animal (2023), which was a major commercial success, and is cast as Rama in Nitesh Tiwari's two-part adaptation of the Ramayana.

Personal life

Kapoor married actress Alia Bhatt in April 2022; their daughter was born later that year. He is a co-owner of the Indian Super League football team Mumbai City FC and supports charitable causes.
