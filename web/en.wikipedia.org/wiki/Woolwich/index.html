Woolwich

Woolwich is a district in south-east London, in the Royal Borough of Greenwich, on the south bank of the River Thames. It grew around its dockyard and the Royal Arsenal.

Borough: Royal Borough of Greenwich, London
River: Thames
Known for: Royal Arsenal, Woolwich Arsenal F.C. origins

History

From Tudor times Woolwich Dockyard built warships, and the Royal Arsenal manufactured armaments for centuries, giving its name to Arsenal Football Club, founded by arsenal workers in 1886. The Woolwich Ferry has crossed the Thames since the 14th century.

Regeneration

Arsenal closure led to redevelopment of Royal Arsenal Riverside housing; the Elizabeth line station opened in 2022 improving connections. See Greenwich and Arsenal F.C.
