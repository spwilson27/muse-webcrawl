The AFL Grand Final is the championship match of the Australian Football League (AFL), the pre-eminent professional competition in Australian rules football. Played each year between the two surviving teams of the finals series, traditionally at the Melbourne Cricket Ground on the last Saturday afternoon in September, it is the largest annual domestic sporting event in Australia and one of the most attended club championship games in world sport, routinely drawing crowds near 100,000.

History

The Victorian Football League (VFL) staged its first grand final in 1898, when Fitzroy defeated Essendon. A formal "grand final" was not held every year in the earliest seasons — premierships were sometimes decided by round-robin finals or by a challenge system — but by the 1930s the single deciding match was entrenched. The 1970 Grand Final between Collingwood and Carlton drew a record crowd of 121,686 and is remembered for Carlton's comeback from a 44-point half-time deficit and Alex Jesaulenko's famous mark. When the VFL expanded interstate and rebranded as the AFL in 1990, the Grand Final remained at the MCG under long-term contracts, interrupted only by the Waverley Park final of 1991 and the COVID-affected finals of 2020 (Brisbane) and 2021 (Perth).

Traditions

Grand Final week includes the Brownlow Medal count, the Grand Final parade through Melbourne, and pre-match entertainment by prominent musicians. The winning players each receive a premiership medallion, the winning club receives the premiership cup and flag, and the player judged best on ground receives the Norm Smith Medal, first awarded in 1979. The coach of the winning team receives the Jock McHale Medal. Since 2015 the Friday before the match has been a public holiday in Victoria.

Drawn grand finals

Three grand finals have been drawn: 1948 (Essendon–Melbourne), 1977 (Collingwood–North Melbourne) and 2010 (Collingwood–St Kilda). Until rule changes in 2016, drawn grand finals were replayed the following week; the 2010 replay, won by Collingwood, was the last. Drawn finals are now resolved by extra time.
