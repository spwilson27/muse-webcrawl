The Atari Falcon, officially the Atari Falcon030 Multimedia System, was the final personal computer released by Atari Corporation, launched in 1992 and discontinued in 1993 as Atari turned to the Jaguar console.

Hardware

Built around a Motorola 68030 processor with a Motorola 56001 digital signal processor, the Falcon offered true-colour graphics modes, 16-bit stereo audio with up to eight channels, direct-to-disk recording, and expansion via internal IDE and SCSI interfaces — capabilities that made it popular with musicians and in desktop audio production.

Legacy

Though commercially brief, the Falcon developed a devoted following; software such as Steinberg's Cubase Audio exploited its DSP for hard-disk recording, and clone machines were produced by third parties after Atari's exit.
