House of Gucci (film)

DirectorRidley Scott
Released2021
StarringLady Gaga, Adam Driver, Jared Leto, Al Pacino, Jeremy Irons

House of Gucci is a 2021 American biographical crime drama film directed by Ridley Scott, based on Sara Gay Forden's book about the Gucci fashion dynasty. It tells the story of Patrizia Reggiani (Lady Gaga), who marries into the Gucci family and whose ambition triggers a spiral of betrayal ending in the 1995 murder of her husband Maurizio Gucci (Adam Driver).

Cast and production

The film also stars Jared Leto as Paolo Gucci, Al Pacino as Aldo Gucci, Jeremy Irons as Rodolfo Gucci, and Salma Hayek as the clairvoyant Pina Auriemma. It was filmed largely in Italy in early 2021.

Reception

Reviews were mixed: the performances, particularly Gaga's and Leto's, drew attention, while the film's tone and length were criticised. Members of the Gucci family objected to their portrayal. The film grossed over 150 million dollars worldwide.
