Dale Chihuly

Dale Chihuly (born September 20, 1941) is an American glass artist. Born in Tacoma, Washington, he studied at the University of Washington and the Rhode Island School of Design, and learned Venetian glass techniques as a Fulbright fellow in Murano, Italy.

In 1971 Chihuly co-founded the Pilchuck Glass School in Washington. After injuries — loss of vision in one eye in a 1976 car accident and a dislocated shoulder — he shifted from blowing glass himself to directing a team, producing large-scale series such as Macchia, Persians, Seaforms and Chandeliers.

His work is held in more than 200 museum collections. A permanent exhibition, Chihuly Garden and Glass, opened at Seattle Center in 2012. His practice has also prompted debate about authorship in workshop-produced art.
