List of tallest buildings in Detroit

The Detroit skyline, dominated by the Renaissance Center

This list of the tallest buildings in Detroit, Michigan, ranks skyscrapers by height. The tallest building in Detroit is the Detroit Marriott at the Renaissance Center, the central tower of the Renaissance Center complex, which rises 727 feet (222 m) and was completed in 1977. The Renaissance Center, owned by General Motors, is also the tallest all-hotel skyscraper in the Western Hemisphere.

Detroit's skyline is notable for its concentration of Art Deco skyscrapers built during the 1920s, including the Guardian Building, the Penobscot Building, and the Fisher Building. Construction slowed dramatically after mid-century as the city's population declined, but the 2000s and 2010s brought new projects such as One Detroit Center and the Hudson's Site development.

History

Detroit's first high-rise boom coincided with the growth of the automobile industry in the early twentieth century. The Hammond Building, completed in 1889, is considered the city's first skyscraper. The interwar period produced the bulk of the city's landmark towers, designed in ornate Art Deco and Neo-Gothic styles. The Great Depression and suburbanization halted major construction for decades; the Renaissance Center, built by Henry Ford II in the 1970s, was intended to spur downtown revival.
