Notice (legal)

A public notice posted for legal service.

Notice is the legal concept describing a requirement that a party be aware of legal process affecting their rights, obligations or duties. It is a fundamental element of due process in common law systems.

Types of notice

Actual notice occurs when a party directly receives information. Constructive notice is a legal fiction: information is treated as received because it was available in public records or would have been discovered by reasonable inquiry. Implied notice arises from circumstances that would prompt a reasonable person to investigate further.

Applications

Notice requirements appear in property law (recording statutes giving constructive notice of interests in land), civil procedure (service of process), corporate law and administrative law, where agencies must publish proposed rules to give the public notice and an opportunity to comment.
