Dodge Caravan

ManufacturerChrysler (FCA US)
Production1983–2020
ClassMinivan

The Dodge Caravan is a series of minivans produced by Chrysler from the 1984 to 2020 model years. Marketed as the Dodge version of the Chrysler minivans, the Caravan was marketed alongside the Plymouth Voyager and the Chrysler Town & Country; from 1997 it was sold in long-wheelbase form as the Grand Caravan.

History

Introduced in November 1983 for the 1984 model year, the Caravan and its siblings effectively created the modern minivan segment, combining front-wheel drive, a car-like ride and sliding doors with the cargo capacity of a van. The model spanned five generations. Production ended in August 2020, with the Grand Caravan nameplate discontinued after the 2020 model year and its role taken over by the Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager.
