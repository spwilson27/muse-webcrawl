David Ellison (born January 9, 1983) is an American film producer and media executive. He is the founder and chief executive of Skydance Media, which he established in 2006, and following Skydance's merger with Paramount Global in 2025 he became chairman and chief executive of the combined company, Paramount Skydance. He is the son of Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison and the brother of producer Megan Ellison.

Early life

Ellison grew up in California and attended the University of Southern California, leaving its film school before graduating to pursue acting and producing. An aviation enthusiast and licensed aerobatic pilot, he appeared as an actor in the 2006 aviation film Flyboys before turning to production.

Career

Ellison founded Skydance in 2006 and raised substantial capital in 2010 to co-finance films with Paramount Pictures, beginning with the Coen brothers' True Grit (2010). Through Skydance he produced or executive-produced major franchise films including Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol (2011) and later instalments of the series, Star Trek Into Darkness (2013) and Star Trek Beyond (2016), World War Z (2013), Jack Reacher (2012), Terminator Genisys (2015), Baywatch (2017), Gemini Man (2019) and Top Gun: Maverick (2022), as well as the television series Grace and Frankie. He expanded the company into animation (Skydance Animation, which hired John Lasseter in 2019), interactive media and sports. In July 2024 Skydance reached agreement to merge with Paramount Global; the transaction closed in August 2025, with Ellison becoming CEO of the combined Paramount Skydance, after which the company agreed to acquire Warner Bros. Discovery assets in a deal announced later that year.
