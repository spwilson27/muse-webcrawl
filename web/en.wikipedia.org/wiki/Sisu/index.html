Sisu

Sisu
LanguageFinnish
Literal rootsisus — "insides", "guts"
MeaningStoic determination, grit, perseverance in the face of adversity

Sisu is a Finnish concept described as stoic determination, tenacity of purpose, grit, bravery, resilience and hardiness, and is held by Finns to express their national character. It is generally considered not to have a literal equivalent in English, though "guts" and "perseverance" come close. The word derives from the Finnish root sisus, meaning the inside or "guts" of a person.

Meaning

Sisu is not momentary courage but the ability to sustain action against long odds and to continue even when repeated failures seem decisive. Finnish researcher Emilia Lahti describes it as an "action mindset" — a reserve of power that enables a person to push beyond their preconceived capacities. The concept became closely associated with Finland's defence during the Winter War (1939–40) against the Soviet Union, when a much smaller Finnish force resisted for months.

Cultural impact

The term has been adopted widely in English-language writing on Finland and in popular psychology. It appears in Finnish brand names, such as Sisu trucks and the icebreaker Sisu, and in the 2022 Finnish action film Sisu, about a legendary gold prospector fighting German soldiers in Lapland during the Second World War.
