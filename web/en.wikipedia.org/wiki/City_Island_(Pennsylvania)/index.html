City Island (Pennsylvania)

City Island is a mile-long island in the Susquehanna River in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. It lies directly across from the city's downtown and is a popular recreation site, reachable by the Walnut Street Bridge (pedestrian) and the Harvey Taylor Bridge (vehicles).

Attractions

The island is home to FNB Field, home of the Harrisburg Senators minor-league baseball team, as well as miniature golf, a carousel, a miniature steam train (the Crown Jewel), batting cages, marinas, and the Skyline Sports Complex.

History

Archaeological evidence indicates Native American settlement on the island. During the Civil War, Union soldiers trained at Camp Curtin nearby, and the island has hosted public recreation since the early 20th century.
