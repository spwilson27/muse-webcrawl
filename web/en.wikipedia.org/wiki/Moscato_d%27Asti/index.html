Moscato d'Asti

TypeSparkling white wine (frizzante)
RegionProvince of Asti, Piedmont, Italy
GrapeMoscato Bianco (Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains)
Alcoholabout 5–5.5%
AppellationDOCG since 1993

Moscato d'Asti is a DOCG sparkling white wine produced mainly in the province of Asti, north-west Italy, and in the neighbouring provinces of Alessandria and Cuneo in Piedmont. It is made from the Moscato Bianco grape and is known for being sweet, lightly sparkling (frizzante), and low in alcohol.

Production

The wine is made by a single fermentation in pressurised tanks (the Charmat, or "Asti", method): the must is chilled and partially fermented until it reaches about 5% alcohol, then fermentation is stopped by refrigeration and filtration, preserving natural sweetness and fresh aromatic character. It is closely related to Asti (formerly Asti Spumante), which comes from the same zone and grapes but is fully sparkling and slightly higher in alcohol.

Character and service

Moscato d'Asti shows aromas of peach, apricot, orange blossom, and sage. It is served cold, traditionally with desserts such as panettone and hazelnut cake, and increasingly as an aperitif. The production area lies within the UNESCO-listed Vineyard Landscape of Piedmont.
