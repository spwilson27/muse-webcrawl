The Osbournes is an American reality television series featuring the domestic life of heavy metal singer Ozzy Osbourne and his family. The series premiered on MTV on 5 March 2002 and concluded on 21 March 2005. It was one of MTV's most-viewed series and is widely credited with popularising the celebrity reality-television format.

Overview

The show follows Ozzy Osbourne, his wife and manager Sharon Osbourne, and their children Kelly and Jack at their home in Beverly Hills, California. The family's older daughter, Aimee, declined to participate and does not appear. Episodes centre on everyday domestic life: arguments, pets, parties, Sharon's battle with cancer, and Ozzy's struggles with sobriety.

Reception and legacy

The first season won an Emmy Award for Outstanding Reality Program in 2002. The series is regarded as a forerunner of later celebrity family reality shows. A revival podcast and reunion specials followed in later years, and the family remained prominent in reality television.
