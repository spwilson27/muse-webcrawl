Swedish Red Cross

The Swedish Red Cross (Swedish: Svenska Röda Korset) is a Swedish humanitarian organisation and a member of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement. Founded in 1865, it is one of Sweden's largest voluntary organisations, with hundreds of local branches across the country and tens of thousands of volunteers and members.

Activities

The Swedish Red Cross works with disaster preparedness, first aid training, support for asylum seekers and migrants, tracing and restoring family links for people separated by war and disaster, and international relief and development cooperation. During the Second World War, the organisation was noted for the "White Buses" operation in 1945, led by Count Folke Bernadotte, which evacuated thousands of concentration camp prisoners to Sweden.

The Swedish Red Cross University College (Röda Korsets Högskola) in Stockholm trains nurses and is affiliated with the organisation. The Swedish Red Cross is bound by the fundamental principles of the Red Cross movement: humanity, impartiality, neutrality, independence, voluntary service, unity and universality.
