Charles Sturt University (CSU) is an Australian multi-campus public university located in New South Wales, the Australian Capital Territory and Victoria. Established in 1989, it was named in honour of the explorer Charles Sturt.

Campuses

The university has campuses in Albury-Wodonga, Bathurst, Canberra, Dubbo, Goulburn, Orange, Parramatta, Port Macquarie and Wagga Wagga, and is known for distance education and courses in areas such as agriculture, health, policing and veterinary science.

History

Charles Sturt University was formed from the amalgamation of several predecessor institutions, including the Mitchell College of Advanced Education and the Riverina College of Advanced Education.
