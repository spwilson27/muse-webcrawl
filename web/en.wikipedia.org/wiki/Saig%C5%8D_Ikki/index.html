Saigō Ikki

Saigō Ikki (西郷 育己)
Born: 22 September 1961, Fukuoka, Japan
Occupation: Theatre director, writer
Known for: Director of the Bungakuza theatre company

Saigō Ikki (西郷 育己, born 22 September 1961) is a Japanese theatre director and writer. He is a leading figure of the Bungakuza, one of Japan's major modern theatre (shingeki) companies, where he has served as a director and representative director.

Career

Saigō joined the Bungakuza's training institute and built his career staging contemporary Japanese drama and adaptations of literary works. His productions are noted for detailed psychological realism consistent with the company's long tradition. He has also been involved in theatre education and in the administration of the company.
