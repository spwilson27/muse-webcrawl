Bally Sports

Bally Sports was a group of regional sports networks (RSNs) in the United States owned by Diamond Sports Group, a joint venture between Sinclair Broadcast Group and Entertainment Studios. The networks were rebranded from Fox Sports Networks on March 31, 2021, under a licensing agreement with Bally's Corporation, a casino and gaming company.

History

The networks originated as Fox Sports regional networks, acquired by Sinclair in 2019 after The Walt Disney Company divested them as a condition of its purchase of 21st Century Fox assets. Under the Bally Sports name, the networks held regional broadcast rights for dozens of NBA, NHL, and Major League Baseball teams.

Bankruptcy and rebrand

Diamond Sports Group filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in March 2023 amid cord-cutting and rights-fee pressures. As part of its restructuring, Diamond terminated the Bally's naming agreement, and on October 21, 2024, the networks were rebranded as the FanDuel Sports Network.
