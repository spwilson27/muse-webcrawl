Multiverse (Marvel Comics)

The Multiverse in Marvel Comics is the collection of parallel universes in which Marvel's stories take place. Each universe is designated by a number; the principal setting is Earth-616, while the Ultimate Marvel imprint was set on Earth-1610.

Structure and history

The concept developed from early alternate-reality stories in the 1960s and 1970s and was formalised in the 1980s, notably in the Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe and in Captain Britain stories that introduced the numbering system and the idea of an Omniverse containing all multiverses.

Major events

Crossover events have repeatedly reshaped the Multiverse. In the 2015 Secret Wars storyline, incursions destroyed the multiverse, which was then rebuilt by Reed Richards and the Future Foundation. Alternate worlds host variants of familiar characters, enabling stories such as What If...? and the Spider-Verse sagas.

In other media

The Marvel Cinematic Universe — itself designated Earth-199999 in reference works — adopted the multiverse as a central theme in its "Multiverse Saga", including Loki, Spider-Man: No Way Home, and Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.
