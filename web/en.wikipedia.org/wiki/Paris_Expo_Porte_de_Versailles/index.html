Paris Expo Porte de Versailles is an exhibition and conference centre in the 15th arrondissement of Paris, France, near the Porte de Versailles. It is the largest exhibition park in France and one of the biggest in Europe, hosting around 120 trade fairs and events each year.

Events

The venue hosts the Paris Motor Show (Mondial de l'Automobile), the Paris International Agricultural Show, the Paris Book Fair, Retromobile, and the Paris Games Week. Its halls are spread across several pavilions on both sides of the Boulevard Périphérique.

Development

Built in 1923 on the site of former military grounds, the complex has been repeatedly modernised; a recent redevelopment added the Paris Convention Centre in Pavilion 7, with one of Europe's largest conference plenary rooms.
