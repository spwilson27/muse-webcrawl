Joint Light Tactical Vehicle

The Joint Light Tactical Vehicle (JLTV) is a United States military programme to partially replace the Humvee with a more survivable, payload-capable family of light tactical vehicles. The vehicle selected in 2015 was the Oshkosh L-ATV, designated M-ATV successor class JLTV in US Army and Marine Corps service.

The JLTV provides MRAP-level protection against mines and improvised explosive devices in a vehicle light enough for helicopter transport, with variants including a two-seat utility truck, four-seat general purpose and close combat weapons carrier versions. Deliveries began in 2019, with tens of thousands planned across the services.
