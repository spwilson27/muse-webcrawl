Kościuszko Mound

Kościuszko Mound

Location: Kraków, Poland

Completed: 1823

Height: about 35.5 m including the hill

Kościuszko Mound (Polish: Kopiec Kościuszki) is an artificial mound in Kraków, Poland, erected by Cracovians in commemoration of the Polish national leader Tadeusz Kościuszko.

History

Construction began in 1820 and finished in 1823. Soil from battlefields where Kościuszko fought, including Racławice and Maciejowice, was placed in the mound, as was soil from American battlefields of the Revolutionary War. A boulder from the Tatra Mountains crowns the summit. The Austrian authorities later incorporated the site into the Kraków Fortress ring.

Today

The mound stands on Sikornik Hill in the Zwierzyniec district and offers panoramic views of Kraków. A museum and exhibition on Kościuszko operate at its base.
