Tres (instrument)

The tres is a three-course, six-string chordophone native to Cuba. It has three groups of two strings (occasionally three groups of three) and is a central instrument in Cuban son music, where it plays rhythmic, repeating melodic figures called guajeos.

Design and tuning

The Cuban tres resembles a small guitar but with a distinctive bright, percussive timbre. A common tuning raises the guitar's strings to a C-major open tuning (G–C–E, with octave pairs). The Puerto Rican and Dominican variants differ in size and string arrangement.

Players

Famous tres players include Arsenio Rodríguez, who expanded the son ensemble and used the tres prominently, and Nelson González. The instrument is also heard in salsa and Latin jazz. See son cubano.
