Rome process

The Rome Process is an international initiative on migration launched by Italy in 2023 under Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni. It was inaugurated at the International Conference on Development and Migration held in Rome on 23 July 2023, bringing together leaders and representatives of Mediterranean, Middle Eastern and African states, the European Union and international financial institutions.

Aims

The process seeks to address the root causes of irregular migration toward Europe through development cooperation, investment in countries of origin and transit, climate adaptation funding, and cooperation against human smuggling and trafficking. Its approach links migration management with economic development, energy partnerships and support for legal migration channels.

Development

The Rome Process is connected with Italy's Mattei Plan for Africa, unveiled in early 2024, which promotes equal-footing partnerships in energy, agriculture, water, education and health. A follow-up summit in Rome in 2024 gathered European and African leaders and announced financial commitments from institutions including the World Bank and the African Development Bank, as well as joint initiatives on security, development and migration management.
