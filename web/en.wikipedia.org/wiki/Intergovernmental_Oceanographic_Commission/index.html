Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission

The Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission (IOC) is a body within UNESCO that promotes international cooperation in marine science, ocean observation, and capacity development. Founded in 1960 and headquartered in Paris, it has about 150 member states.

Programmes

The IOC coordinates the Global Ocean Observing System (GOOS), the International Oceanographic Data and Information Exchange (IODE), and the international tsunami warning and mitigation systems, notably for the Pacific and Indian Oceans. It also supports ocean literacy, marine spatial planning, and the scientific assessment of ocean health.

UN Decade of Ocean Science

The IOC is the coordinating agency for the United Nations Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development (2021–2030), which aims to mobilize scientific knowledge to reverse declining ocean health and support sustainable development.
