FL Studio

FL Studio
Developer: Image-Line
Initial release: 18 December 1997 (as FruityLoops)
Operating system: Windows, macOS
Type: Digital audio workstation
License: Proprietary

FL Studio (formerly known as FruityLoops) is a digital audio workstation (DAW) developed by the Belgian company Image-Line. It features a graphical user interface based on a pattern-based music sequencer, and is available in several editions with different feature sets.

History

The software was created by Didier Dambrin, who developed the first version as a four-channel MIDI drum machine in the late 1990s. It evolved from FruityLoops into a full DAW with audio recording, editing, mixing, and mastering capabilities. The macOS version, long requested, was released with FL Studio 20 in 2018, which also celebrated the product's 20th anniversary.

Features

FL Studio includes a channel rack and step sequencer, a piano roll widely praised for its editing tools, a playlist for arranging patterns and audio clips, and a mixer with effects chains. It ships with a large library of native synthesizers and effects (including Sytrus, Harmor, Gross Beat, and Fruity Parametric EQ) and supports VST plugins, ReWire, and MIDI controllers. Its lifetime free updates policy is a distinguishing feature: licensed users receive future versions at no additional cost.

Editions and mobile

Editions include Fruity, Producer, Signature, and All Plugins editions. FL Studio Mobile is available for iOS, Android, and Windows touch devices.

Users

The program became especially popular in hip hop and electronic dance music production, used by producers including Avicii, Martin Garrix, Metro Boomin, and Deadmau5 early in their careers.
