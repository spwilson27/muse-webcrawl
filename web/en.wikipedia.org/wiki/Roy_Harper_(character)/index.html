Roy Harper (character)

Roy Harper is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. He first appeared as Speedy, the sidekick of Green Arrow, in More Fun Comics #73 (1941).

Fictional biography

Orphaned and raised on a Navajo reservation, Roy became Oliver Queen's ward and an expert archer. He was a founding member of the Teen Titans. The acclaimed 1971 story "Snowbirds Don't Fly" depicted his struggle with heroin addiction. As an adult he took the names Arsenal and later Red Arrow, and served with the Justice League, the Outsiders and the Titans. He is the father of Lian Harper.

In other media

Roy Harper was played by Colton Haynes in the television series Arrow.
