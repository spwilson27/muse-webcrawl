Football pitch
Standard dimensions: 105 m x 68 m (international)
Surface: Grass or artificial turf
Governed by: Laws of the Game, Law 1

An association football pitch is the rectangular playing surface for the game of association football, defined in Law 1 of the Laws of the Game maintained by the IFAB. For international matches the pitch must be 100-110 metres long and 64-75 metres wide, with 105 by 68 metres the standard recommendation.

Markings

The pitch is marked with boundary lines, a halfway line, a centre circle of 9.15 m radius, and at each end a goal area, a penalty area, a penalty spot 11 m from the goal, and corner arcs. Goals stand on the centre of each goal line, 7.32 m apart and 2.44 m high.

Surface

Matches are usually played on natural grass; artificial surfaces meeting FIFA quality standards are permitted. Major grounds use hybrid pitches combining grass with synthetic fibres.
