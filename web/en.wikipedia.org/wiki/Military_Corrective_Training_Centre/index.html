Military Corrective Training Centre

The Military Corrective Training Centre (MCTC) is a facility at Colchester in Essex, England, that serves as the principal military prison and detention centre of the British Armed Forces. It is the only dedicated corrective training establishment of its kind in the United Kingdom and is operated by the Military Provost Staff.

Role

The MCTC accommodates personnel of all three services — the British Army, the Royal Navy and the Royal Air Force — who have been sentenced to detention by court martial or summary dealing. Unlike a civilian prison, its purpose is corrective training: most detainees are rehabilitated and returned to their units to continue their military careers, while those sentenced to dismissal are prepared for civilian life.

Regime

Detainees follow a structured daily regime of physical training, drill, education, vocational training and work. The establishment distinguishes between "A Company", for detainees returning to service, and "D Company", for those who are to be discharged. The regime emphasises resettlement, with courses in literacy, numeracy and trade skills.

History

Military detention in Colchester dates back to the nineteenth century, when the town became a major garrison. The modern MCTC developed from the wartime detention barracks and moved to its current site at Berechurch, south of the town centre. Successive reports by HM Inspectorate of Prisons have generally praised the centre's regime and low reoffending rates among those returned to service.
