The sambar (Rusa unicolor) is a large deer native to the Indian subcontinent, southern China and Southeast Asia. It is among the largest deer species, with stags standing up to about 160 cm at the shoulder and bearing rugged three-tined antlers.

Description and behaviour

Sambar have a coarse, dark brown coat, a long bushy tail and large ears. They are primarily nocturnal or crepuscular, feeding on grasses, leaves, fruits and aquatic vegetation, and they readily enter water to feed or escape predators. Tigers and dholes are their main natural enemies.

Status

Populations have declined sharply from hunting and habitat loss, and the species is listed as Vulnerable. Introduced populations exist in Australia, New Zealand and parts of the United States, where they are prized game animals.
