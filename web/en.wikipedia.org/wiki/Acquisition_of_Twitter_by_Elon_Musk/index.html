Acquisition of Twitter by Elon Musk

TargetTwitter, Inc.

The acquisition of Twitter by Elon Musk was completed on 28 October 2022, when the American business magnate and investor Elon Musk purchased the social media company Twitter, Inc. for US$44 billion, taking the company private. Musk began acquiring shares of Twitter in January 2022 and by April had become its largest shareholder with a 9.1% stake.

Background and offer

After declining a seat on Twitter's board, Musk made an unsolicited offer on 14 April 2022 to buy the company for $54.20 per share. Twitter's board initially adopted a poison pill defence but unanimously accepted the offer on 25 April.

Attempted termination and litigation

In July 2022 Musk announced he intended to terminate the deal, alleging Twitter had understated the proportion of spam and fake accounts. Twitter sued in the Delaware Court of Chancery to enforce the merger. In early October, Musk reversed course and agreed to close at the original price, and the acquisition was completed on 28 October 2022.

Aftermath

Musk dismissed CEO Parag Agrawal and other executives, became the company's chief executive, and implemented sweeping changes including mass layoffs, revised content moderation and verification policies, and in July 2023 the rebranding of Twitter as X.
