Wrexham Association Football Club (Welsh: Clwb Pêl-droed Wrecsam) is a professional association football club based in Wrexham, Wales. Founded in 1864, it is the oldest club in Wales and the third-oldest professional football team in the world. The club plays at the Racecourse Ground, the world's oldest international football stadium still in use, which has hosted matches since 1864 and staged Wales's first home international in 1877. Wrexham compete in the English football league system.

History

Wrexham were founder members of the Football League's Third Division North in 1921 and spent much of their history in the lower divisions, winning the Welsh Cup a record 23 times. Their greatest era came under manager John Neal and then Arfon Griffiths in the late 1970s, reaching the quarter-finals of the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1976 and winning promotion to the Second Division in 1978. The club produced one of the FA Cup's most famous upsets in 1992, beating reigning league champions Arsenal 2–1 at the Racecourse. Financial difficulties in the 2000s led to administration and, in 2008, relegation from the Football League after 87 years. The club was bought by its supporters' trust in 2011.

Hollywood ownership and revival

In February 2021 the club was purchased by actors Ryan Reynolds and Rob McElhenney. Their ownership, chronicled in the documentary series Welcome to Wrexham, brought the club global attention and investment. After losing in the play-offs in 2022, Wrexham won the National League title in 2022–23 with a record 111 points, returning to the EFL, and then secured back-to-back promotions by finishing second in League Two in 2023–24 and second in League One in 2024–25, reaching the Championship.
