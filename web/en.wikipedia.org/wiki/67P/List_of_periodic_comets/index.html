List of periodic comets

Periodic comets (also known as short-period comets) are comets with orbital periods of less than 200 years or that have been observed during more than one perihelion passage. They are given a permanent number prefix, assigned by the Minor Planet Center once an orbit is securely established, usually after the comet has been observed at two perihelia. As of recent counts there are several hundred numbered periodic comets.

Nomenclature

A periodic comet's designation combines its number, the letter "P" for periodic, and the surname(s) of its discoverer(s). The suffix "D" denotes a comet that has disintegrated or been lost, as with 3D/Biela and 5D/Brorsen. Halley's Comet (1P/Halley) is the most famous periodic comet, recorded since 240 BC. 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko was the first comet orbited and landed upon by spacecraft, both by the Rosetta mission. 95P/Chiron is notable as an object classified both as a comet and as a minor planet (2060 Chiron).
