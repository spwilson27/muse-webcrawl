List of periodic comets

Nuclei of periodic comets imaged by visiting spacecraft.

A periodic comet is a comet whose orbit brings it back to the inner Solar System on a predictable cycle of less than 200 years, or which has been observed through more than one perihelion passage. After a second return has been observed, the comet receives an official serial number from the Minor Planet Center on behalf of the IAU, placed before the "P/" in its designation (for example, 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko).

Families of periodic comets

Periodic comets are commonly grouped as Jupiter-family comets (periods under 20 years, low inclinations, controlled by Jupiter) and Halley-type comets (periods of 20–200 years, any inclination, of Oort cloud origin). Numbered comets now run into the high 400s, while hundreds more periodic comets remain unnumbered.

Some once-periodic comets have disintegrated or been lost: 3D/Biela split in two in the 1840s and produced the Andromedid meteor storms, and others, such as 5D/Brorsen, vanished after a few returns.
