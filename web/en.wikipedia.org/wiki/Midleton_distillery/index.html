Midleton distillery

The Midleton distillery in Midleton, County Cork, Ireland, is the largest distillery in Ireland and the home of Irish Distillers, a subsidiary of Pernod Ricard. It produces many of Ireland's best-known whiskeys, including Jameson, Powers, Redbreast, Green Spot, and the Midleton Very Rare range.

History

Whiskey making at Midleton dates to the Old Midleton Distillery, established in 1825 in a converted woollen mill with a giant waterwheel and what was then the world's largest pot still. In 1975 production moved to a new purpose-built distillery beside the old site after the Irish whiskey industry consolidated. The old distillery now houses the Jameson Experience visitor centre.

Production

Midleton produces pot still and grain whiskeys, both triple distilled, for blending and single pot still releases. A major expansion in 2013 added a new brewhouse and stillhouse to meet growing international demand for Irish whiskey.
