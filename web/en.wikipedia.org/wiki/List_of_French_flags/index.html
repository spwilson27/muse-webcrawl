List of French flags

This is a list of flags of France, including the national flag, military and naval ensigns, regional flags and the principal historical banners of the French state.

National flag

The Tricolore — vertical bands of blue, white and red — was adopted during the French Revolution (first in 1790 in naval form, and as the national flag in 1794), combining the colours of Paris (blue and red) with the royal white. It was abandoned under the Bourbon Restoration (1815–1830) in favour of a white flag, then restored after the July Revolution of 1830 and has remained the national flag ever since. The shade of blue was lightened under Valéry Giscard d'Estaing in 1976 and restored to the darker navy blue in 2020. The naval ensign differs slightly in the proportions of its bands (30:33:37), so that it appears balanced when flying.

Historical flags

Under the monarchy France used the royal arms — gold fleurs-de-lis on blue, the banner of the Capetian kings — and the white Bourbon flag sprinkled with fleurs-de-lis. The oriflamme, the red banner of the Abbey of Saint-Denis, was the war banner of the early kings. Revolutionary France used the Tricolore alongside regimental colours; the Napoleonic armies carried eagle standards modelled on Roman legionary aquilae.

Regional flags

Many regions fly historic provincial banners: Brittany's Gwenn-ha-du (black and white stripes with ermine spots), the Basque Ikurrina, the Catalan Senyera in Roussillon, the Cross of Lorraine, the Occitan cross of Languedoc, and the flags of Corsica (the Moor's head) and Normandy (the two leopards of William the Conqueror).

Overseas flags

French overseas collectivities generally use the Tricolore officially; some, such as French Polynesia, New Caledonia (alongside the Kanak flag) and Wallis and Futuna, also have recognised local flags.
