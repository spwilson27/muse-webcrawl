Football Club Twente, sometimes known internationally as FC Twente Enschede, is a Dutch professional football club from the city of Enschede. Formed in 1965 by the merger of Sportclub Enschede and the Enschedese Boys, the club plays in the Eredivisie, the top tier of Dutch football, and won its first and only national championship in the 2009–10 season.

History

FC Twente was created on 1 July 1965 from the merger of Sportclub Enschede — champions of the Netherlands in 1926 — and Enschedese Boys. The club's early decades brought steady performances and European football; in 1975 Twente reached the final of the UEFA Cup, losing to Borussia Mönchengladbach, and in 1977 it won the KNVB Cup, its first major trophy. A second cup followed in 2001.

Under coach Steve McClaren, Twente rose to the top of Dutch football at the end of the 2000s, finishing as runners-up in 2008–09 before winning the Eredivisie title in 2009–10, sealed with a victory at NAC Breda on the final day of the season. The triumph brought Champions League football to Enschede. Twente added a third KNVB Cup and two Johan Cruyff Shields in 2010–2011.

Financial problems then engulfed the club: heavy spending and an opaque deal with an external investment group led to sanctions from the KNVB, points deductions, and the revelation of debts that threatened the club's licence and existence. In 2018 Twente was relegated to the Eerste Divisie for the first time, but it won the second-tier title at the first attempt in 2019 and returned to the Eredivisie, subsequently stabilising and returning to European competition.

Stadium

The club's home is De Grolsch Veste, opened in 1998 as the Arke Stadion and expanded in 2008 to a capacity of about 30,000. It replaced the old Diekman Stadion. During the 2011 expansion works, a section of the roof under construction collapsed, killing two workers — a tragedy commemorated at the stadium. Supporters, known as Tukkers after the nickname for people from the Twente region, are reputed for their loud support, and the Vak-P ultras group occupies the stand behind one goal.

Honours

  • Eredivisie: 2009–10
  • KNVB Cup: 1976–77, 2000–01, 2010–11
  • Johan Cruyff Shield: 2010, 2011
  • Eerste Divisie: 2018–19
  • UEFA Cup runners-up: 1974–75

Rivalries

Twente's traditional rival is Heracles Almelo, its neighbour in the Twente region; their meetings form the Twentse derby. Matches against the traditional "big three" of Ajax, PSV, and Feyenoord also draw particular attention given the club's role as the leading side of the eastern Netherlands.
