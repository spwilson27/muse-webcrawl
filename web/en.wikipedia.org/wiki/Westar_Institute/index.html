Westar Institute

Founded: 1985
Founder: Robert W. Funk
Headquarters: Willamette University, Salem, Oregon

The Westar Institute is a nonprofit research and educational institute dedicated to the advancement of religious literacy. It was founded in 1985 by biblical scholar Robert W. Funk and is based at Willamette University in Salem, Oregon.

Jesus Seminar

Westar is best known for the Jesus Seminar, begun in 1985, in which a group of scholars voted on the authenticity of the sayings and deeds attributed to Jesus in the Gospels, publishing their conclusions in works such as The Five Gospels (1993). Its scholars used coloured beads to grade sayings from red (most likely authentic) to black (least likely), a method that attracted both wide popular attention and scholarly criticism. The institute later launched the Acts Seminar and other collaborative research projects, and publishes through its imprint Polebridge Press.
