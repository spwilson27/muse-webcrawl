Aconcagua

[Image: Aconcagua, the highest peak of the Andes]
Aconcagua, the highest peak of the Andes

Aconcagua is a mountain in the Principal Cordillera of the Andes in Mendoza Province, Argentina. At 6,961 metres above sea level, it is the highest mountain in the Americas, the highest outside Asia, and the highest in both the Western and Southern Hemispheres.

The mountain is a straightforward but demanding high-altitude climb, attempted by thousands of mountaineers each season via the normal route from the Horcones Valley, though its weather and altitude make it dangerous. Its name may derive from Quechua or Mapuche words meaning 'stone sentinel'. The first recorded ascent was by Matthias Zurbriggen in 1897.
