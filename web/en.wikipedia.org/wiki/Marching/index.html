Marching

Marching is a rhythmic, organised form of walking, typically performed by soldiers in formation, by marching bands, or by demonstrators in protest marches. Military marching emphasises uniformity of step, posture, and timing.

Forms

Common forms include the quick march, slow march, double time, and ceremonial steps such as the goose step and high step. Marching orders measure distance in marches per day. In civilian life, marching appears in parades, protest movements such as civil-rights marches, marching band field shows, and endurance events like the Nijmegen Four Days Marches.
