Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows is a fantasy novel written by British author J. K. Rowling and the seventh and final novel in the Harry Potter series. It was released on 21 July 2007 and follows Harry Potter as he is tasked by Albus Dumbledore's memory with finding and destroying Lord Voldemort's remaining Horcruxes.

Plot

With the Ministry of Magic fallen to Voldemort and Dumbledore dead, Harry, Ron Weasley, and Hermione Granger go on the run, hunting the Horcruxes that anchor Voldemort's soul. Along the way they learn of the Deathly Hallows — the Elder Wand, the Resurrection Stone, and the Cloak of Invisibility — three legendary objects that together make their possessor "Master of Death". The novel culminates in the Battle of Hogwarts, in which Harry, revealed to be himself an unintended Horcrux, offers himself to Voldemort, returns from a limbo-like King's Cross, and finally defeats him when the Elder Wand's allegiance proves to lie with Harry. An epilogue, "Nineteen Years Later", shows the survivors sending their own children off to Hogwarts.

Publication and records

Deathly Hallows broke sales records on release, selling about 15 million copies in its first 24 hours worldwide and around 8.3 million in the United States alone — the fastest-selling book in history to that date. It won the 2008 Colorado Blue Spruce Book Award and was named among the best books of 2007 by several publications.

Film adaptations

The novel was adapted into two films, directed by David Yates: Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1 (2010) and Part 2 (2011), the latter briefly becoming the third-highest-grossing film of all time.
