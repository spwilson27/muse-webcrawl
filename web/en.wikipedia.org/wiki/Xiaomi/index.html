Xiaomi Corporation is a Chinese multinational electronics company founded in Beijing in 2010 by Lei Jun and partners. It is one of the world's largest smartphone manufacturers and also produces smart home devices, wearables and consumer electronics, operating an ecosystem model of hardware, software and internet services. The company is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.

History

Xiaomi launched its first smartphone, the Mi 1, in 2011, selling high-specification phones online at low margins and building a fan community around its MIUI Android-based operating system. It expanded rapidly in China and India, briefly becoming the world's third-largest smartphone maker by 2014, and later expanded into Europe and global markets. Sub-brands include Redmi (budget phones) and POCO.

Electric vehicles and ecosystem

In 2024 Xiaomi entered the electric vehicle market with the SU7 sedan, produced in Beijing, which received strong demand in China. Its broader "smart ecosystem" includes televisions, laptops, air purifiers, robot vacuums and thousands of connected devices sold through its IoT platform, one of the largest consumer IoT networks in the world.
