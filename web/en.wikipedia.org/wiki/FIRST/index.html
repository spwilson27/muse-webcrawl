FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a non-profit organisation founded in 1989 by inventor Dean Kamen to inspire young people's interest in science and technology through mentor-based robotics programmes.

Programmes

FIRST runs a progression of competitions by age: FIRST LEGO League for younger children, the FIRST Tech Challenge for middle and high school students, and the flagship FIRST Robotics Competition, in which high school teams design and build large industrial-scale robots in a few weeks to compete in a themed game each season.

Philosophy

The organisation promotes "gracious professionalism" and "coopertition", values coined by co-founder Woodie Flowers, emphasising respect, cooperation and helping opponents even within competition. Hundreds of thousands of students worldwide participate each year.
