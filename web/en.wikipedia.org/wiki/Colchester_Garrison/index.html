Colchester Garrison

Colchester Garrison is a major garrison of the British Army located in Colchester, Essex, England. The town has hosted a military presence since Roman times — Colchester (Camulodunum) was the first Roman capital of Britain and housed a legionary fortress — and a permanent barracks town was established in the nineteenth century. Today the garrison, centred on Merville Barracks, is the home of 16 Air Assault Brigade, the British Army's air assault and rapid reaction formation, including the Parachute Regiment.

History

Barracks were first built at Colchester during the Napoleonic era, and the Victorian garrison expanded with cavalry and artillery barracks and a military hospital. The town's military detention facility, the Military Corrective Training Centre (MCTC), is the armed forces' sole remaining corrective establishment. In the 2000s the garrison was comprehensively rebuilt under a private finance initiative, with Merville Barracks opening on the site of the former Goojerat and Hyderabad Barracks; older barracks land was released for housing development.

Units

Principal units have included the headquarters and battalions of 16 Air Assault Brigade, elements of the Parachute Regiment (2 and 3 PARA), 13 Air Assault Support Regiment RLC, 16 Medical Regiment, and 216 (Parachute) Signal Squadron.
