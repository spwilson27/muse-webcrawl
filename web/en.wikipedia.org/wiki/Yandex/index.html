Yandex LLC (Russian: Яндекс) is a Russian multinational technology company that operates the largest search engine in Russia and provides a wide range of internet-related products and services, including mapping, email, ride-hailing, food delivery, e-commerce, and online advertising. The name is a backronym for "Yet Another iNDEXer".

History

The Yandex search engine was launched on 23 September 1997 by Arkady Volozh and Ilya Segalovich, building on search technology developed at CompTek in the early 1990s. The company was formally established in 2000. Yandex was designed with particular strengths in processing the Russian language, including its rich morphology, and it grew to hold a majority share of the Russian search market, ahead of Google.

In May 2011, the company's parent, Yandex N.V., held an initial public offering on the NASDAQ stock exchange, one of the largest internet IPOs of its time. Following the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2022, Yandex faced sanctions pressure and the departure of many employees; in 2024, Yandex N.V. sold its Russian business to a consortium of Russian investors and rebranded its remaining international operations as Nebius Group, while the Yandex brand continued in Russia under new ownership.

Services

Yandex's services include Yandex Search, Yandex Mail, Yandex Maps, Yandex Translate, Yandex Browser, the ride-hailing service Yandex Go (Yandex.Taxi), the marketplace Yandex Market, music streaming (Yandex Music), and the virtual assistant Alice. The company has also invested in machine learning, autonomous vehicles, and cloud computing through Yandex Cloud.
