Cameron Jerrell Newton (born May 11, 1989) is an American former professional football quarterback. Nicknamed "Superman" for his touchdown celebration, Newton combined passing with exceptional rushing ability and is regarded as one of the greatest dual-threat quarterbacks in NFL history; he holds the NFL record for career rushing touchdowns by a quarterback.

College career

Newton began at the University of Florida before transferring to Blinn College, where he won a junior-college national championship. At Auburn in 2010 he won the Heisman Trophy and led the Tigers to victory in the BCS National Championship Game, becoming one of only a few players to win the Heisman and a national title in the same season.

Professional career

The Carolina Panthers selected Newton first overall in the 2011 NFL draft. He was named Offensive Rookie of the Year after breaking several rookie passing records. In 2015 he was voted NFL Most Valuable Player and led the Panthers to a 15–1 record and Super Bowl 50, where they lost to the Denver Broncos. Injuries later diminished his play; after a season with the New England Patriots in 2020 he briefly returned to Carolina in 2021.

Legacy

Newton's size, speed and charisma made him one of the most recognisable players of his era and helped popularise the modern dual-threat quarterback. He is also known for his philanthropy through the Cam Newton Foundation, his fashion sense, and later media work.
