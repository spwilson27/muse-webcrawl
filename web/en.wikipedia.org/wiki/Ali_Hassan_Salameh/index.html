Ali Hassan Salameh

Ali Hassan Salameh (1 April 1940 – 22 January 1979) was a Palestinian militant, chief of operations of the Black September Organization and founder of Force 17. Israel held him responsible for planning the 1972 Munich Olympics massacre, and he was assassinated by Mossad in Beirut.

Life

Son of Sheikh Hassan Salameh, a commander killed in the 1948 war, Ali was educated in Egypt and Germany and joined Fatah. He rose in Yasser Arafat's security apparatus, heading Force 17, the PLO leader's personal guard, and served as a covert contact with the CIA in the 1970s.

Munich and aftermath

After the Munich attack in which 11 Israeli athletes and coaches died, Israel launched Operation Wrath of God against those it held responsible. Salameh evaded several attempts — including the 1973 Lillehammer affair, in which an innocent Moroccan waiter was mistaken for him and killed.

Assassination

On 22 January 1979 a remotely detonated car bomb in Beirut killed Salameh, his bodyguards and bystanders. His funeral drew large crowds in Beirut.
