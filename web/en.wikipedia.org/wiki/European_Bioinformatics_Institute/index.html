European Bioinformatics Institute
Abbreviation:EMBL-EBI
Formation:1992
Location:Wellcome Genome Campus, Hinxton, Cambridgeshire, United Kingdom
Parent organisation:European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL)

The European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) is an intergovernmental organisation and a centre for research and services in bioinformatics. It forms part of the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) and is located on the Wellcome Genome Campus in Hinxton near Cambridge, England.

Role

EMBL-EBI maintains and freely distributes a comprehensive collection of biological databases, covering genes, genomes, proteins, chemical compounds, and scientific literature. Major resources include Ensembl, the European Nucleotide Archive, UniProt (in collaboration with partners), the Protein Data Bank in Europe (PDBe), ChEMBL, and Europe PMC. These services receive billions of requests per year from researchers worldwide.

Research and training

Alongside its service mission, the institute carries out research in computational biology and provides training in bioinformatics tools and data analysis for scientists at all career stages.
