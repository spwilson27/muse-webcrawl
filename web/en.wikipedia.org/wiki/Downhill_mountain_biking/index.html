Downhill mountain biking

Downhill mountain biking is a gravity-assisted cycling discipline in which riders descend steep, rough courses against the clock, using long-travel full-suspension bikes, body armour and full-face helmets.

Competition

The UCI Mountain Bike World Cup downhill series and World Championships headline the sport; courses feature rock gardens, jumps and drops, and runs last only a few minutes.

Equipment

Downhill bikes have around 200 mm of suspension travel, slack geometry, strong brakes and heavy tyres, and riders are usually lifted to the start by chairlift or shuttle.
