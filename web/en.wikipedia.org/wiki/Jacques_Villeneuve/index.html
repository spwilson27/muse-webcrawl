Jacques Joseph Charles Villeneuve (born 9 April 1971) is a Canadian racing driver who won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 1997 with Williams. He is the son of the late Formula One driver Gilles Villeneuve. Villeneuve also won the 1995 Indianapolis 500 and the 1995 CART IndyCar World Series championship, making him one of only a handful of drivers to win both the Indy 500 and the F1 world title.

Early career

Villeneuve began racing in Italian Formula Three before moving to the Japanese Formula Three championship and then to North America, where he raced in the Toyota Atlantic series and then CART with the Forsythe-Green team, winning Rookie of the Year honours in 1994.

Formula One

Joining Williams in 1996, Villeneuve won four races in his debut season and finished second in the championship to team-mate Damon Hill. In 1997 he won seven races and took the title in a dramatic final race at Jerez, where rival Michael Schumacher was disqualified from the championship after a collision between the two. Villeneuve later drove for BAR, Renault and Sauber/BMW Sauber before leaving Formula One in 2006, having scored 11 wins and 23 podiums in 163 starts.

Later career

After Formula One, Villeneuve competed in NASCAR, the Le Mans 24 Hours (finishing second in 2008), rallycross and Formula E, and has worked as a television pundit.
