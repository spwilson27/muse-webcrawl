List of deadliest floods

This article lists the deadliest floods in recorded history, ranked by estimated death toll. The deadliest floods have overwhelmingly occurred in China, where the Yellow River, Yangtze and Huai rivers have repeatedly burst their banks across densely populated floodplains.

The 1931 China floods are generally considered the deadliest natural disaster in recorded history, combining river flooding with subsequent famine and disease. The 1938 flood was deliberately caused when the Nationalist government breached Yellow River dikes to slow the Japanese advance. The 1975 Banqiao Dam failure followed Typhoon Nina and remains the deadliest dam failure in history.
