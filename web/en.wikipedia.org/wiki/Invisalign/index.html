Invisalign

Invisalign is a brand of clear aligners manufactured by Align Technology, used in orthodontics to straighten teeth. Founded in 1997 by Zia Chishti and Kelsey Wirth in San Jose, California, the system uses a series of custom-made, removable transparent plastic trays that gradually shift teeth into position.

Treatment

Treatment begins with a digital scan or impression of the patient's teeth, from which a computer model plans the tooth movements stage by stage. Each set of aligners is worn for about one to two weeks, for 20–22 hours per day, and removed for eating and cleaning. Treatment commonly lasts 6 to 18 months depending on complexity.

Comparison with braces

Clear aligners are less conspicuous than fixed metal braces and can be removed for meals and oral hygiene, but they depend heavily on patient compliance and may be unsuitable for some complex malocclusions. Align Technology reports that more than ten million patients have been treated with the system worldwide.
