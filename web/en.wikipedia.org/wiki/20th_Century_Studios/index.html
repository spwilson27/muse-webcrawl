20th Century Studios, Inc. is an American film production and distribution company owned by the Walt Disney Studios, the film and television production division of The Walt Disney Company. It was originally formed on May 31, 1935, as 20th Century Fox, through the merger of the Fox Film Corporation and Twentieth Century Pictures. For most of its history it was one of the "Big Five" major American film studios.

History

Twentieth Century Pictures was founded in 1933 by Joseph Schenck and Darryl F. Zanuck, while William Fox's Fox Film Corporation dated to 1915. Their 1935 merger created Twentieth Century-Fox Film Corporation, headquartered at the Fox Studio Lot in Century City. Under Zanuck the studio became famous for prestige dramas, Shirley Temple films, and later for CinemaScope epics such as The Robe (1953), the Star Wars saga, Alien, Die Hard, Home Alone, Titanic (1997), and Avatar (2009), the highest-grossing film of all time.

Disney acquisition

On March 20, 2019, The Walt Disney Company completed its acquisition of 21st Century Fox for $71.3 billion, bringing the studio into the Walt Disney Studios group alongside Walt Disney Pictures, Pixar, Marvel Studios and Lucasfilm. On January 17, 2020, Disney renamed the studio 20th Century Studios, dropping "Fox" to disassociate it from the spun-off Fox Corporation. Its sister label, Searchlight Pictures, likewise dropped "Fox" from its name.

Recent productions

Under Disney the studio has released films including Free Guy (2021), West Side Story (2021), The Last Duel (2021), Avatar: The Way of Water (2022), The Creator (2023), Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes (2024) and Alien: Romulus (2024), with many titles distributed jointly in theatres and on streaming services Hulu and Disney+.
