Athlete's foot

Athlete's foot, medically known as tinea pedis, is a fungal infection of the skin of the feet caused by dermatophytes, most commonly Trichophyton rubrum. It typically causes itching, scaling, redness and cracking, often beginning between the toes.

Causes and transmission

The fungi thrive in warm, moist environments such as showers, swimming pools and locker rooms, and spread through contact with contaminated surfaces or towels. Tight or non-breathable footwear increases the risk.

Treatment

Most cases respond to over-the-counter topical antifungal creams such as terbinafine or clotrimazole applied for one to four weeks; severe or recurrent infections may require oral antifungal medication. Keeping the feet clean and dry helps prevent recurrence.
