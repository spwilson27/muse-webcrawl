Genesee County, New York

Genesee County is a county in western New York State, United States, situated between Rochester and Buffalo. Its county seat and largest city is Batavia. As of the 2020 census the population was about 58,000.

County seatBatavia
Founded1802
Named forSeneca word for "pleasant valley"
Area495 sq mi

History

The county was created in 1802 from Ontario County, originally covering much of western New York before later counties were split off. The land was largely purchased through the Holland Purchase. Agriculture and the New York State Thruway corridor remain central to its economy.
