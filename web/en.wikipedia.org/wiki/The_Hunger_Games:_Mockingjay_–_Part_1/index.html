The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 is a 2014 American dystopian science fiction film directed by Francis Lawrence. It is the third instalment of The Hunger Games film series and the first of two films based on Suzanne Collins' 2010 novel Mockingjay.

Plot

After the destruction of District 12, Katniss Everdeen takes refuge in underground District 13 and, at the urging of President Alma Coin and Plutarch Heavensbee, reluctantly becomes the "Mockingjay," the filmed symbol of the rebellion against the Capitol. As the districts rise, President Snow uses the captured Peeta Mellark in broadcasts to counter her, and a rescue mission is mounted to extract him from the Capitol.

Reception

The film grossed over $755 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2014, and received generally positive reviews, though some critics questioned the decision to split the final novel into two films.
