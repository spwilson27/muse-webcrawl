Gareth James Edwards (born 1975) is a British film director and visual effects artist. He first gained attention for the independent science fiction film Monsters (2010), which he shot on a tiny budget while creating its visual effects himself, and went on to direct the blockbusters Godzilla (2014) and Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (2016), as well as The Creator (2023) and Jurassic World Rebirth (2025).

Career

Edwards began in visual effects for television documentaries such as the BBC's Perfect Disaster and won recognition for the drama-documentary End Day. His feature debut Monsters, made with a crew of about five people and effects produced on his laptop, won several British Independent Film Awards. Its success led Warner Bros. and Legendary Pictures to hire him for the 2014 Godzilla reboot, which grossed over 20 million worldwide. He then directed Rogue One, the first Star Wars anthology film, which grossed over billion. His original science fiction epic The Creator (2023) continued his interest in shooting effects-heavy films on location with modest crews.
