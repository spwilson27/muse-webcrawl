The Dengeki Novel Prize (電撃小説大賞, Dengeki Shōsetsu Taishō) is a Japanese literary award for unpublished light novels, presented annually since 1994 by ASCII Media Works (now part of Kadokawa). Winning works are published under the Dengeki Bunko imprint. The prize is one of the most important routes for new light novel authors to debut, and regularly receives more than ten thousand entries a year.

History and structure

The award was founded in 1994 as the Dengeki Game Novel Prize, when Dengeki Bunko was launched, and later renamed the Dengeki Novel Prize. Awards are given in several tiers, principally the Grand Prize, the Gold Prize, and the Silver Prize, along with reader-selected and sponsor categories. Winning authors receive prize money and publication of their manuscript, often with editorial development into a series.

Notable winners and works

Many commercially successful and critically recognised light novel series began as Dengeki Prize entries, including Ryohgo Narita's Baccano! (Gold Prize), Kazuma Kamachi's A Certain Magical Index, Isuna Hasekura's Spice and Wolf (Silver Prize), and works by authors such as Hiro Mashima-era contemporaries in the imprint's early years. Numerous prize-winning novels have gone on to anime, manga, and game adaptations.
