Interest (emotion)

Interest is an emotion that accompanies or causes special attention to an object or a class of objects. Psychologist Silvan Tomkins treated interest–excitement as one of the basic affects, the mild form of which is curiosity; Paul Ekman likewise included interest among the emotions elicited by novelty and challenge.

Function

Interest focuses attention, promotes exploration and learning, and motivates engagement with new information. It is closely related to curiosity and is triggered by stimuli that are novel, complex, or uncertain yet understandable. In education research, interest is distinguished into situational interest, sparked by features of an environment, and individual interest, a stable personal disposition toward a topic.

Expressions and effects

The facial expression of interest involves raised or knit brows, directed gaze, and an open, attentive posture. Sustained interest supports creativity and intrinsic motivation, and its absence can manifest as boredom or disengagement.
