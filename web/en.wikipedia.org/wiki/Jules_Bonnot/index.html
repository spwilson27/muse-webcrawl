Jules Bonnot

Jules Joseph Bonnot (14 October 1876 – 28 April 1912) was a French anarchist and leader of the Bonnot Gang, a criminal anarchist group that carried out a series of robberies and murders in France and Belgium in 1911–1912. The gang pioneered the use of the automobile as a getaway vehicle, outpacing horse-drawn police and bicycles.

Life and crimes

Bonnot worked as a chauffeur and mechanic and became involved in anarchist circles and illegalism. In December 1911 the gang robbed a Société Générale branch in Paris, escaping by car, and went on to steal cars, rob gun shops, and kill police officers and bystanders. Bonnot was killed by police in a dynamite-assisted siege at Choisy-le-Roi in April 1912; surviving gang members were tried in 1913, several being executed or sent to penal colonies.
