USA Network (or simply USA) is an American basic cable television channel owned by the NBCUniversal Media Group division of Comcast's NBCUniversal. It was originally launched in 1977 as the Madison Square Garden Sports Network, one of the first national sports cable channels, and relaunched under its current name in 1980.

Programming

USA became one of the most-watched cable networks in the United States on the strength of original drama series such as Monk, Psych, Burn Notice, Suits, White Collar, and Mr. Robot, promoted under the "Characters Welcome" and "blue skies" branding of light, character-driven shows. It has also been a long-running home of professional wrestling, broadcasting WWE's Raw for most of the period since 1993, and carries sports including Premier League football overflow coverage, golf, and NASCAR at various times, as well as extensive syndicated reruns of series such as Law & Order: Special Victims Unit and NCIS.

History

After passing through joint ownership involving Paramount Pictures and MCA, the channel became wholly associated with Universal and then NBCUniversal following the 2004 merger of NBC and Vivendi Universal Entertainment. For many years in the 2000s and 2010s USA ranked as the most-watched ad-supported cable entertainment network in the United States.
