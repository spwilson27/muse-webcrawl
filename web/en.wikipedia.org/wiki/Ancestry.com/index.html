Ancestry.com

Ancestry.com LLC is an American genealogy company based in Lehi, Utah. It operates a network of genealogy and historical record websites and the consumer DNA service AncestryDNA, and is one of the world's largest for-profit genealogy companies.

History

Ancestry originated in publishing in the 1980s–90s, launched its website in 1996, and grew through digitised census, military and immigration records. It acquired Find a Grave and other sites, went public in 2009, and was acquired by Blackstone in 2020 for about $4.7 billion.

Services

Subscribers build family trees and search tens of billions of records; AncestryDNA has tested over 20 million people. See family history and DNA testing.
