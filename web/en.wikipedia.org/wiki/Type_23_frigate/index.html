Type 23 frigate

The Type 23 frigate or Duke class is a class of frigates built for the United Kingdom's Royal Navy. Sixteen ships were built between 1985 and 2002, named after British dukes. Originally designed as anti-submarine warfare platforms for the North Atlantic, they evolved into general-purpose frigates.

Service

Type 23s are noted for their quiet electric propulsion for anti-submarine work and for the towed-array sonar fitted to most of the class. Eight ships received extensive life-extension refits with the Sea Ceptor missile system and Artisan radar. Three ships were sold to Chile in the 2000s. In Royal Navy service the class is being replaced by the Type 26 and Type 31 frigates.
