Roronoa Zoro is a fictional character in the Japanese manga series One Piece, created by Eiichiro Oda. He is the combatant of the Straw Hat Pirates and the first member to join Monkey D. Luffy's crew. A master swordsman, Zoro fights with a distinctive style called Santoryu ("Three Sword Style"), wielding a katana in each hand and a third in his mouth. His lifelong goal is to become the world's greatest swordsman, a promise made to his childhood friend and rival Kuina.

Creation and conception

Oda originally conceived Zoro as a member of Buggy the Clown's crew before making him Luffy's first recruit. His surname derives from the French pirate François l'Olonnais. Zoro's poor sense of direction is a long-running gag in the series, despite his otherwise formidable discipline and endurance.

Appearances

Zoro is introduced as a feared pirate hunter in the East Blue, bound to a post in Shells Town for defying the Marine Captain Morgan's son. Freed by Luffy, he joins the Straw Hats and travels with them through the Grand Line. During the two-year timeskip he trains under Dracule Mihawk, the world's strongest swordsman and his ultimate rival. Zoro is known for his fierce loyalty, famously enduring Luffy's pain at Thriller Bark and declaring that a crew whose captain loses respect is doomed to fall apart.

Abilities

Zoro possesses immense physical strength, stamina and mastery of swordsmanship, including the ability to cut steel and, later, to imbue his blades with Haki. His signature techniques include Tatsu Maki, Onigiri and the nine-sword Asura illusion. He carries the swords Wado Ichimonji, Sandai Kitetsu and Enma.

Reception

Zoro is consistently among the most popular characters in One Piece popularity polls, and his loyalty and strength have made him a fan favourite. See also One Piece and Straw Hat Pirates.
