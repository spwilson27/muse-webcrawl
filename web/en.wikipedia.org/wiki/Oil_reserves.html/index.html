Oil reserves

World map of proven oil reserves by country.

Oil reserves denote the amount of crude oil that can be technically recovered at a cost that is financially feasible at the present price of oil. Proven reserves are those with a high degree of confidence (at least 90%) of being recoverable under current economic and political conditions.

World reserves

Total world proven reserves are estimated at around 1.7 trillion barrels. Venezuela holds the largest share, followed by Saudi Arabia and Canada, whose reserves consist largely of oil sands. OPEC countries together hold roughly 70% of the total.

Classification

Reserves are classified as proven, probable and possible depending on the probability of recovery. Estimates depend on geological data, technology and economics, and are periodically revised by sources such as the Statistical Review of World Energy and the U.S. Energy Information Administration.
