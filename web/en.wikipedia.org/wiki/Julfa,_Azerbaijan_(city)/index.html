Julfa, Azerbaijan (city)

Julfa (Azerbaijani: Culfa) is a city and the administrative capital of Julfa District in the Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic of Azerbaijan, on the north bank of the Aras River opposite the Iranian town of Jolfa.

History

Old Julfa, a few kilometres east, was a major Armenian trading town on the silk road, famous for its vast cemetery of carved khachkars. In 1604 Shah Abbas I deported its population to Isfahan, where they founded New Julfa. The medieval cemetery's thousands of khachkars were destroyed in the late 1990s and 2000s, a loss documented by satellite imagery and condemned internationally. The modern city is a rail and border-crossing point between Azerbaijan's exclave and Iran.
