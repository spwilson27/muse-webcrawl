The Chinese Dream (Chinese: 中国梦) is a political slogan and concept promoted by Xi Jinping, General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party, shortly after he took office in November 2012. It describes the collective aspiration of the "great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation."

Content

In official usage, the Chinese Dream links national prosperity and military strength with the happiness of the people. It is associated with the "Two Centenaries" goals: building a moderately prosperous society by 2021 (the Communist Party's centenary) and a modern socialist country by 2049 (the People's Republic's centenary).

Interpretation

Commentators contrast the Chinese Dream with the individualist American Dream: in official framing, personal ambitions are encouraged insofar as they contribute to national rejuvenation, though the slogan has also been popularly adopted to describe individual hopes for education, homes and upward mobility.
