Thomas Wintour (1571 or 1572 – 31 January 1606), also spelled Winter, was a member of the group of provincial English Catholics who planned the failed Gunpowder Plot of 1605, an attempt to assassinate King James I and blow up the House of Lords during the State Opening of Parliament.

Background

Wintour was a lawyer and scholar from a prominent recusant family in Worcestershire. He served as a soldier in Flanders before being recruited into the conspiracy in early 1604 by his brother Robert Wintour, at the instigation of Robert Catesby. Thomas travelled to Spain seeking support for the plot and helped recruit Guy Fawkes.

The plot and capture

After the plot was betrayed by an anonymous letter to Lord Monteagle, the surviving conspirators fled to Holbeche House in Staffordshire, where Thomas was wounded in a final shoot-out on 8 November 1605. He was tried at Westminster in January 1606 and hanged, drawn and quartered at Old Palace Yard on 31 January 1606.
