Leon

Leon is a name and a word used for many places, people and other entities. Notable uses include the city of León in Spain and León in Mexico, the León Province of Spain, and the historical Kingdom of León.

Places

  • León, Spain — capital of the province of León in Castile and León
  • León, Guanajuato, Mexico — a major industrial city
  • León, Nicaragua — a city and department in Nicaragua
  • Leon, Iowa and other communities in the United States

People

Leon is a given name derived from the Greek word for "lion," borne by kings of Sparta, popes, and many others. As a surname it appears in Spanish-speaking families, sometimes as de León.

Other uses

  • Léon (film), a 1994 film also released as The Professional
  • Seat León, a car model
  • Leon (restaurant chain), a British fast-food chain
