Introduction

Sport pertains to any form of competitive and organised physical activity or game intended to use, maintain or improve physical ability and skills while giving enjoyment to participants and entertainment to spectators. Hundreds of sports exist, from those between single contestants to those with hundreds of simultaneous participants, either in teams or competing as individuals.

The most-watched events in world sport include the Olympic Games, the FIFA World Cup, the Cricket World Cup, the Super Bowl and the Tour de France.

Selected article

Association football, more commonly known as football or soccer, is a team sport played between two teams of 11 players each, who almost exclusively use their feet to propel a ball around a rectangular field called a pitch. The objective of the game is to score more goals than the opposing team by moving the ball beyond the goal line into a rectangular-framed goal defended by the opposing side. It is the world's most popular sport, played by roughly 250 million players in over 200 countries.

Selected biography

Sachin Tendulkar (born 24 April 1973) is an Indian former international cricketer who is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. He is the all-time highest run-scorer in international cricket, the only player to score one hundred international centuries, and a member of India's 2011 Cricket World Cup-winning team.
