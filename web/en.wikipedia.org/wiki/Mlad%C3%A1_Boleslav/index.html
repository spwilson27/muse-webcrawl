Mladá Boleslav

Mladá Boleslav Castle above the Jizera river

Mladá Boleslav (German: Jungbunzlau) is a city in the Central Bohemian Region of the Czech Republic, on the Jizera river about 50 km northeast of Prague. It has a population of roughly 46,000. The city is best known as the home of the automobile manufacturer Škoda Auto, founded there in 1895 as Laurin & Klement.

History

A castle was founded in the 10th century by Boleslaus II; the town below it was first mentioned in 1130. Mladá Boleslav was a centre of the Unity of the Brethren, and later an important Jewish community centre. Industrial development accelerated after Václav Laurin and Václav Klement opened their bicycle factory, which became Škoda Auto, today the city's dominant employer.

Sights

Landmarks include Mladá Boleslav Castle, the Old Town Hall, the Gothic Church of the Assumption of the Virgin Mary, and the Škoda Museum.
