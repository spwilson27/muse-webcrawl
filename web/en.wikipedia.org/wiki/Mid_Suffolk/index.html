Mid Suffolk

Mid Suffolk is a local government district in Suffolk, England. Its council is based in Ipswich, the county town, outside the district itself. The district's largest town is Stowmarket, with other settlements including Needham Market and Eye.

History

The district was formed on 1 April 1974 under the Local Government Act 1972, by merging several rural districts and urban districts of East Suffolk. It covers a predominantly rural area in the centre of the county, characterised by small villages, agricultural land, and the valley of the River Gipping.

Governance

Mid Suffolk District Council works in partnership with neighbouring Babergh District Council, sharing staff and services. The area is represented in Parliament mainly by the Central Suffolk and North Ipswich constituency.
