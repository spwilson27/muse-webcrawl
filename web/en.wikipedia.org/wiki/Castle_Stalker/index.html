Castle Stalker

Castle Stalker is a four-storey tower house set on a tidal islet in Loch Laich, near Port Appin in Argyll, Scotland. One of the best-preserved and most photographed medieval tower houses in the country, it became famous worldwide as the Castle of Aaargh in the film Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975).

LocationLoch Laich, Argyll, Scotland
Builtc. 1446 (present tower)
BuildersStewarts of Appin
StatusRestored, privately owned

History

The name derives from the Gaelic Stalcaire, meaning "hunter" or "falconer". A fortalice stood on the islet by the 1320s under the MacDougalls; the present tower was built around 1446 by the Stewarts of Appin. The castle passed between Stewarts and Campbells amid feuds and Jacobite risings — it was garrisoned during the 1745 rising — and was abandoned and roofless by the late 19th century.

Restoration

In 1965 the castle was bought by Lt. Col. D. R. Stewart Allward, who spent a decade restoring it by hand with his family. It remains in family ownership and opens to the public on limited days each year, reached by boat at suitable tides.
