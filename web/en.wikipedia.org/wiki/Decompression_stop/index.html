Decompression stop

A decompression stop is a pause during a diver's ascent at a specified depth for a specified time, allowing inert gas absorbed under pressure to leave the tissues safely and reducing the risk of decompression sickness.

Stops are prescribed by decompression tables or dive computers based on the depth and duration of the dive. Distinct from mandatory decompression stops, a "safety stop" of about three minutes at 3–6 metres is a precautionary practice on recreational no-stop dives.
