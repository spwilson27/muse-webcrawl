The World Rally-Raid Championship (W2RC) is the premier international championship for rally raid (cross-country rallying), jointly sanctioned by the FIA for cars, trucks and lightweight vehicles and by the FIM for motorcycles and quads. Launched in 2022, it unified the previous FIA World Cup for Cross-Country Rallies and FIM Cross-Country Rallies World Championship into a single series promoted by the Amaury Sport Organisation, organiser of the Dakar Rally.

Format

Seasons consist of about five long-distance desert rallies. The cornerstone is the Dakar Rally, held in Saudi Arabia each January over two weeks. Other regular rounds have included the Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge, the Sonora Rally in Mexico, the Desafío Ruta 40 in Argentina, the BP Ultimate Rally-Raid in Portugal and the season-ending Rallye du Maroc in Morocco. Drivers, riders, co-drivers and manufacturers score points at each round across categories headlined by the Ultimate (T1+) car class and the RallyGP motorcycle class.

Champions

Qatar's Nasser Al-Attiyah dominated the early seasons in the car category, winning the drivers' title in 2022 and 2023 with Toyota and again in 2024 after switching to Dacia's new programme. In the motorcycle category, Britain's Sam Sunderland won the inaugural 2022 title, Argentina's Luciano Benavides took the 2023 crown for Husqvarna, and Botswana's Ross Branch won in 2024 with Hero MotoSports, becoming one of the sport's most popular champions. Spanish veteran Carlos Sainz, Sébastien Loeb and Stéphane Peterhansel have been among the championship's star competitors.

Background

Rally raid grew from events such as the Paris–Dakar Rally, founded in 1979, and for decades the Dakar itself overshadowed the season-long world cups, which suffered from small and shifting calendars. The creation of the W2RC gave manufacturers — including Toyota, Audi (whose hybrid RS Q e-tron won the 2024 Dakar), Ford, Dacia and Prodrive — a single marketing platform, standardised sporting rules and television coverage across a full season.
