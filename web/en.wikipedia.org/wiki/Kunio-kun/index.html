Kunio-kun is a Japanese video game franchise created by Technōs Japan, starring the hot-blooded high-school delinquent Kunio. The series began with the 1986 arcade beat 'em up Nekketsu Kōha Kunio-kun, released in the West as Renegade.

Games

The best-known entry is Downtown Nekketsu Monogatari (1989), localised as River City Ransom, which mixed brawling with role-playing elements. Numerous sports spin-offs followed, including dodgeball and soccer titles such as Nintendo World Cup in the West.

Legacy

After Technōs closed, rights passed to Arc System Works, which has continued the series with entries such as River City Girls (2019), developed by WayForward.
