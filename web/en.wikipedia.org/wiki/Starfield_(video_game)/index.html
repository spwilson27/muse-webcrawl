Starfield (video game)

Starfield is a 2023 action role-playing video game developed by Bethesda Game Studios and published by Bethesda Softworks. It was released for Windows and Xbox Series X/S in September 2023, the studio's first new intellectual property in over 25 years.

Gameplay

Set in the "Settled Systems" in the 24th century, the player joins Constellation, a group of space explorers seeking mysterious artefacts. The game features more than 1,000 procedurally generated planets alongside handcrafted locations, ship building and customisation, first- and third-person combat, and character creation with backgrounds and traits.

Reception

Starfield received generally positive reviews praising its scale and role-playing systems, with criticism directed at its reliance on loading screens and segmented exploration. An expansion, Shattered Space, was released in 2024.
