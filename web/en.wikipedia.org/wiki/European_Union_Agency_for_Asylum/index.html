European Union Agency for Asylum (EUAA)
Founded2011 (as EASO); EUAA since 19 January 2022
HeadquartersValletta, Malta
TypeEU agency

The European Union Agency for Asylum (EUAA) is an agency of the European Union that supports member states in implementing the Common European Asylum System. It succeeded the European Asylum Support Office (EASO), which had been established in 2011, when a strengthened mandate entered into force in January 2022.

Functions

The agency provides operational and technical assistance to national asylum authorities, deploys asylum support teams to member states under pressure, produces country-of-origin information and guidance, offers training for asylum officials, and monitors the application of EU asylum law.

Operations

The EUAA runs operating plans in countries such as Greece, Italy, Cyprus, Malta and Spain, deploying hundreds of experts to assist with registration, interviews and reception.
