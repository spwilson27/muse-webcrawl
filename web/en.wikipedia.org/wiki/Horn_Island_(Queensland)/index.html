Horn Island, known in local languages as Narupai (also spelled Ngurupai), is an island in the Torres Strait, in the Australian state of Queensland. It lies south of Thursday Island and is the site of Horn Island Airport, the main airport serving the Torres Strait region. The island's township is Wasaga.

History

Horn Island was the site of a gold rush in the late 19th century, and gold mining continued intermittently into the 20th century. During the Second World War the island became an important forward air base for Australian and American forces; its airfield was attacked by Japanese aircraft, and gun emplacements, slit trenches, and a war cemetery remain as reminders. Torres Strait Islander servicemen of the Torres Strait Light Infantry Battalion were based in the region.

Transport and community

Horn Island Airport receives scheduled flights from Cairns and provides connections by ferry to Thursday Island, the administrative centre of the strait. Because Thursday Island lacks room for an airport, Horn Island functions as its gateway. The island has a hotel, a museum documenting its wartime history, and the Wasaga community, home largely to people relocated from other islands and to descendants of wartime and pearling-era families.
