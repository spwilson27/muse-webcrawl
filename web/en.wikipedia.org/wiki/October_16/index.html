October 16

October 16 is the 289th day of the year (290th in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar; 76 days remain until the end of the year.

Events

1793 – Marie Antoinette, queen consort of France, was executed by guillotine during the French Revolution. 1962 – The Cuban Missile Crisis began as US President John F. Kennedy was informed of Soviet missile sites in Cuba. 1978 – Cardinal Karol Wojtyła was elected Pope John Paul II.

Births and deaths

1854 – Oscar Wilde, Irish writer, was born. World Food Day is observed on 16 October, marking the founding of the Food and Agriculture Organization in 1945.
