Castletownbere (Irish: Baile Chaisleáin Bhéarra, meaning "the town of the castle of Beara"), also called Castletown Bearhaven, is a port town in County Cork on the southwest coast of Ireland, on the Beara Peninsula beside Bantry Bay. It is one of the largest whitefish ports in Ireland, and the principal town serving the Beara Peninsula. Nearby Bere Island, across the sheltered harbour, was the site of a major naval anchorage and military base used by the Royal Navy into the 20th century.

History

The town grew around the fishing trade and the copper mines of nearby Allihies, worked extensively in the 19th century by the Puxley family of Dunboy Castle. Dunboy, the historic seat of the O'Sullivan Beare clan, was destroyed after the siege of 1602 during the Nine Years' War; Donal Cam O'Sullivan Beare's famous retreat to Leitrim followed. Bearhaven's deep-water anchorage was one of the Treaty Ports retained by Britain after Irish independence in 1922, until handed over in 1938.

Economy

Fishing and fish processing dominate the local economy; the harbour hosts a substantial trawler fleet and onshore processing facilities. Tourism, drawn by the Ring of Beara scenic route, walking and sailing, also contributes. A seasonal ferry serves Bere Island from the town's pier.
