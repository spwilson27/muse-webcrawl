Pierce Brendan Brosnan (born 16 May 1953) is an Irish actor and film producer. He is best known as the fifth actor to play the fictional secret agent James Bond in the Bond film series, starring in four films from 1995 to 2002 (GoldenEye, Tomorrow Never Dies, The World Is Not Enough and Die Another Day).

Early life

Brosnan was born in Drogheda, Ireland, and raised largely by his grandparents and in a Christian Brothers school in Navan after his father left the family and his mother moved to London to work as a nurse. At 11 he joined his mother in London, later training at the Drama Centre London.

Career

After stage work and small film roles, Brosnan achieved fame in the United States as the title character of the NBC detective series Remington Steele (1982–1987). Film roles in The Fourth Protocol, Mrs. Doubtfire and Dante's Peak followed.

James Bond

Brosnan had first been linked to Bond in the 1980s but was blocked by his television contract. Announced as Bond in 1994, his debut GoldenEye (1995) revived the franchise after a six-year hiatus and is widely credited with modernising it; the film also spawned a landmark video game. His four Bond films grossed over $1.4 billion combined.

Later work and activism

Through his company Irish DreamTime, Brosnan produced and starred in films including The Thomas Crown Affair (1999) and The Matador (2005), and he sang in the film musical Mamma Mia! (2008) and its sequel. He appeared in The Ghost Writer, Percy Jackson & the Olympians and Black Adam. Brosnan is a prominent environmental campaigner, notably with the International Fund for Animal Welfare on whaling, and was appointed an honorary OBE in 2003.
