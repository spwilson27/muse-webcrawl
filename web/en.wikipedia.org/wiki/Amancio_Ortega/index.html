Amancio Ortega Gaona is a Spanish billionaire businessman. He co-founded the Inditex fashion group with his late former wife Rosalía Mera and is best known as the creator of Zara, the flagship of the "fast fashion" model. He is one of the richest people in Europe.

Early life and Zara

The son of a railway worker, Ortega grew up in A Coruña, Galicia, leaving school at 14 to work as a shop hand for a shirtmaker. In 1963 he started a small workshop making quilted bathrobes, and in 1975 he and Mera opened the first Zara store in A Coruña — the name reportedly chosen after "Zorba" proved unavailable.

Inditex and fast fashion

Zara's model — designing, manufacturing near its Galician base and restocking stores twice weekly in tiny batches — revolutionised fashion retail by shortening design-to-rail time to weeks. The holding company Inditex, formed in 1985, added chains including Massimo Dutti, Bershka, Pull&Bear and Stradivarius, and listed in 2001 in Spain's largest IPO to that date. Ortega stepped down as chairman in 2011, succeeded by Pablo Isla; his daughter Marta Ortega became chair in 2022.

Shunning publicity — he gave his first press interviews only in old age — Ortega invests his dividends through the Pontegadea vehicle in prime real estate in cities including London, New York and Madrid, and in energy and telecoms stakes. His foundation funds public-health projects in Galicia, including oncology equipment.
