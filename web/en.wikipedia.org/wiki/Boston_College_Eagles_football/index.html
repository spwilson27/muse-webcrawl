The Boston College Eagles football team represents Boston College in NCAA Division I FBS college football. The Eagles play at Alumni Stadium in Chestnut Hill, Massachusetts, and have competed in the Atlantic Coast Conference since 2005, having previously been a founding member of the Big East football conference.

[ Image: The Eagles take the field at Alumni Stadium in maroon and gold ]

History

Boston College first fielded a team in 1893. The programme's high point came under Frank Leahy, whose 1940 team went undefeated and beat Tennessee in the 1941 Sugar Bowl, earning a claim to the national championship. Later highlights include the 1984 season of Heisman Trophy-winning quarterback Doug Flutie — immortalised by the "Hail Flutie" pass that beat Miami — and Matt Ryan's 2007 team, which reached No. 2 in the national rankings. The Eagles have a long record of bowl appearances and notable NFL alumni including Luke Kuechly.

Rivalries

Traditional rivals include Notre Dame — the "Holy War" series between the two Catholic schools — along with Virginia Tech, Clemson and Syracuse. Boston College also contests the Green Line Rivalry name in other sports.
