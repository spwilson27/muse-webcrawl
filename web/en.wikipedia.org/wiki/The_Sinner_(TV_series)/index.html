The Sinner is an American crime drama anthology television series developed by Derek Simonds for USA Network. Each season follows Detective Harry Ambrose (Bill Pullman) as he investigates a crime whose perpetrator is known from the outset, focusing on uncovering why the crime was committed rather than who committed it.

Seasons

The first season (2017), based on Petra Hammesfahr's 1999 novel, stars Jessica Biel as Cora Tannetti, a young mother who inexplicably stabs a man to death on a public beach; Ambrose's investigation uncovers buried trauma in her past. It was originally conceived as a limited series but its success led to further seasons: the second (2018) concerns a boy who poisons two people at a motel and Ambrose's return to his hometown and a local commune; the third (2020), starring Matt Bomer, follows a car crash that exposes a planned crime and a dangerous game of cat and mouse; the fourth and final season (2021) is set in Maine, where a retired Ambrose witnesses a young woman's apparent suicide and unravels a wealthy family's secrets.

Reception

The series received positive reviews, particularly for Pullman's performance and the first season's psychological depth. Biel was nominated for a Golden Globe Award and a Primetime Emmy Award for her role in season one.
