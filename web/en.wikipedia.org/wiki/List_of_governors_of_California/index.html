List of governors of California

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The governor of California is the head of government of the U.S. state of California. California was admitted to the Union on September 9, 1850, and its first governor under statehood, Peter Hardeman Burnett, had already taken office in December 1849 under the pre-statehood civil government. The longest-serving governor is Jerry Brown, who served four terms across two periods (1975–1983 and 2011–2019); Earl Warren, the only California governor to serve three full consecutive terms, left to become Chief Justice of the United States.

Notable facts

  • Two governors went on to the presidency-track: Leland Stanford founded Stanford University; Ronald Reagan became U.S. president; Earl Warren became Chief Justice.
  • Gray Davis is one of only two governors in U.S. history removed by recall (the other being North Dakota's Lynn Frazier in 1921).
  • Under term limits adopted in 1990, governors may serve at most two four-year terms.
