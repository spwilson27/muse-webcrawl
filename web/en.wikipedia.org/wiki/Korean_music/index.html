Korean music encompasses the traditional folk and court music of Korea (gugak) as well as the popular music of modern South and North Korea, most famously K-pop.

Traditional music

Korean traditional music is typically divided into court music (jeongak), folk music and ritual music. Pansori, an epic sung storytelling form accompanied by a single drummer, and samulnori, a lively percussion ensemble, are among the best-known genres. Characteristic instruments include the gayageum zither and the janggu hourglass drum.

Popular music

Modern Korean popular music developed through trot in the early 20th century and was transformed in the 1990s by groups such as Seo Taiji and Boys. In the 21st century K-pop idol groups including BTS and Blackpink achieved global success as part of the Korean Wave (Hallyu).

North Korea

Music in North Korea is state-controlled and largely devoted to patriotic and revolutionary themes, performed by ensembles such as the Moranbong Band.
