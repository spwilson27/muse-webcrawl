Background

French cantons are electoral subdivisions used to elect departmental councils; the 2015 reform halved their number nationally and redrew boundaries to equalise population, pairing each canton to elect one man and one woman. Indre's cantons group its roughly 240 communes across four arrondissements: Châteauroux, Le Blanc, La Châtre and Issoudun.
