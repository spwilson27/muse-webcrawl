Rich Hickey

Rich Hickey is an American computer programmer and speaker, best known as the creator of the Clojure programming language, a functional Lisp dialect that runs on the Java Virtual Machine. He is also a co-developer of the Datomic database system.

Work

Before Clojure, Hickey worked for about two decades in commercial software, including scheduling and automation systems. He released Clojure in 2007 after taking time off to design it. Clojure emphasizes immutability, software transactional memory, and a pragmatic approach to concurrency, and it influenced a generation of language design.

Talks and influence

Hickey's conference talks — including "Simple Made Easy", "Are We There Yet?", "The Value of Values", and "Spec-ulation" — are widely cited in the programming community for their arguments about simplicity, value-oriented programming, and the problems of state and complecting concerns. He served as chief technology officer of Cognitect, which stewarded Clojure and Datomic before its acquisition by Nubank in 2023.
