Distributed social network

A distributed social network or federated social network is an Internet social networking service that is decentralized and distributed across distinct providers. It consists of multiple social websites, where users of each site can communicate with users of any of the involved sites — a model often called the Fediverse when the sites interconnect through common protocols.

Protocols

ActivityPub, standardized by the W3C in 2018, is the most widely used protocol for federation; it underpins Mastodon and many other services. Earlier efforts included OStatus and the protocol developed for the Diaspora network.

Comparison with centralized services

Users of a federated service choose an instance whose operator and moderation policies they trust, while retaining the ability to follow and interact with users on other instances. This avoids a single point of control, though it complicates discovery, moderation of remote content, and account migration.
