Rockumentary

A rockumentary is a documentary film or television programme about rock music musicians or bands. The term is a portmanteau of "rock" and "documentary". Rockumentaries typically combine concert footage, backstage material, and interviews to give an account of a performer's career or a particular tour.

Notable examples

Landmarks of the genre include D. A. Pennebaker's Monterey Pop (1968) and Dont Look Back (1967), about Bob Dylan; the Maysles brothers' Gimme Shelter (1970), about the Rolling Stones; Martin Scorsese's The Last Waltz (1978), about the Band; and Woodstock (1970). Later examples include Metallica: Some Kind of Monster (2004) and Anvil! The Story of Anvil (2008).

Parodies

The genre was famously satirised by Rob Reiner's mockumentary This Is Spinal Tap (1984), whose fictional band's misadventures have become a byword for rock-star excess and pretension.
