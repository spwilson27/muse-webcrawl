Secrets & Lies is a 1996 British drama film written and directed by Mike Leigh. It tells the story of Hortense, a black optometrist in London who, after the death of her adoptive mother, seeks out her birth mother — Cynthia, a working-class white woman — with profound consequences for Cynthia's family.

Production

As is typical of Leigh's method, the film was developed through months of improvisation with the cast; Brenda Blethyn was not told the identity of her character's daughter until the scene of their first meeting was filmed.

Reception

The film won the Palme d'Or at the 1996 Cannes Film Festival, where Blethyn also won Best Actress. It was nominated for five Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Actress.
