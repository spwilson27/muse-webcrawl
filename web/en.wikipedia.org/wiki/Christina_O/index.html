Christina O is a private motor yacht that formerly belonged to the Greek shipping magnate Aristotle Onassis. Originally built as the Canadian River-class frigate HMCS Stormont, launched in 1943, she was purchased by Onassis after the Second World War and lavishly converted into one of the most famous luxury yachts of the twentieth century. She was named after Onassis's daughter, Christina Onassis.

History

As HMCS Stormont, the vessel served as a convoy escort during the Battle of the Atlantic and was present at the Normandy landings. Onassis bought the war-surplus frigate for a reported $34,000 and spent some $4 million converting her at the Howaldtswerke yard in Kiel, Germany, completing the work in 1954.

The yacht became a floating salon for the era's most famous figures. Guests included Winston Churchill, John F. Kennedy, Frank Sinatra, Marilyn Monroe, Elizabeth Taylor, Richard Burton, and Greta Garbo. In 1956 she hosted the wedding reception of Prince Rainier III of Monaco and Grace Kelly. Onassis's own wedding to Jacqueline Kennedy in 1968 was celebrated in connection with the yacht on the island of Skorpios.

Features

The yacht's amenities included a swimming pool with a mosaic floor that could be raised to form a dance floor, a bar whose stools were famously upholstered in unusual leather, a fireplace, a library, and staterooms decorated with lapis lazuli and other precious materials. Her original master suite included a bathtub modelled on that of King Minos of Crete.

Later history

After Onassis's death in 1975, Christina Onassis donated the yacht to the Greek government for use as a presidential vessel; renamed Argo, she fell into neglect. In the late 1990s she was purchased by Greek shipping magnate John Paul Papanicolaou, a family friend of Onassis, who restored her at great expense and restored the name Christina O. She has since operated as a luxury charter yacht.
