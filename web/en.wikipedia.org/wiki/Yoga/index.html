Yoga is a group of physical, mental and spiritual practices originating in ancient India, aimed at stilling the mind and, in Hindu philosophy, uniting with the divine. Modern global yoga emphasises asanas (postures), breath control and meditation.

Practice and evidence

From studio classes to prison and school programs, yoga is practiced by hundreds of millions; trials support benefits for back pain, balance, stress and flexibility (a complement to exercise, not a replacement for medicine). Its commercialisation and cultural roots are debated, as is the line between exercise and spiritual practice.
