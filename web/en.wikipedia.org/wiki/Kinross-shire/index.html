Kinross-shire

Kinross-shire, also called the County of Kinross, is a historic county and registration county in central Scotland. One of the smallest Scottish counties, it lies around Loch Leven, with its county town at Kinross.

Geography and history

The county is dominated by Loch Leven, whose castle island held Mary, Queen of Scots prisoner in 1567–68. Since 1975 Kinross-shire has been administered together with Perthshire, today within the Perth and Kinross council area, though it remains a lieutenancy area in its own right.
