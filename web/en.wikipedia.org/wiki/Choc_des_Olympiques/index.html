Choc des Olympiques

The Choc des Olympiques (Clash of the Olympics), also known as the Derby des Olympiques, is the name of the football rivalry between French clubs Olympique Lyonnais (OL) and Olympique de Marseille (OM). It is one of the most prominent rivalries in French football, contested between two of the country's most successful and popular clubs.

History

The two clubs first met in 1951. The rivalry intensified during the 2000s, when Lyon dominated French football with seven consecutive Ligue 1 titles (2002–2008), challenging Marseille's status as France's most popular club. Marseille remains the only French club to have won the UEFA Champions League, in 1993.

Notable matches

One of the most memorable encounters was the 5–5 draw at Lyon's Stade de Gerland on 8 November 2009, one of the highest-scoring matches in Ligue 1 history. Matches between the clubs are frequently high-scoring and emotionally charged, with strong travelling support despite the distance between the two cities.

Head-to-head

The all-time record between the clubs is closely balanced, with each side having won dozens of meetings across league and cup competitions, and individual players such as Juninho Pernambucano (Lyon) and Didier Drogba and Dimitri Payet (Marseille) leaving their mark on the fixture.
