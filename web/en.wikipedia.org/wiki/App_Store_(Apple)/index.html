The App Store is an app marketplace developed and maintained by Apple Inc., for mobile apps on its iOS and iPadOS operating systems. The store allows users to browse and download approved apps developed with Apple's iOS SDK. Apps can be downloaded to iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch devices, and most can also be used on Macs, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro. The App Store opened on July 10, 2008, with an initial 500 applications available; the number of apps peaked at around 2.2 million in 2017 and has since hovered somewhat lower as Apple removed older or non-compliant titles.

History

When the first iPhone launched in 2007, Apple initially offered no native third-party applications, directing developers to web apps. Following developer and consumer demand, Apple announced the iPhone SDK in October 2007 and the App Store in March 2008. The store launched alongside iPhone OS 2.0 in July 2008. Developers receive 70 percent of app sale revenue (85 percent for eligible small businesses and for subscriptions after the first year), with Apple retaining the remainder. The model proved transformative: the store passed 10 billion downloads in January 2011, 50 billion in 2013, and 100 billion in 2015.

App review and policies

Every app submitted to the store undergoes App Review against Apple's App Store Review Guidelines, covering safety, performance, business practices, design, and legality. Apple requires most apps distributed on iOS to come through the store, a policy that has drawn sustained criticism and regulatory scrutiny over commissions, anti-steering rules, and restrictions on alternative marketplaces. The 2020 lawsuit Epic Games v. Apple challenged these rules; a 2021 injunction restricted Apple's anti-steering provisions in the United States. The European Union's Digital Markets Act of 2022 obliged Apple, as a designated "gatekeeper", to permit alternative app marketplaces and sideloading in the EU, changes Apple implemented with iOS 17.4 in March 2024 under a new fee structure.

Features

The App Store presents editorial stories in its Today tab, curated collections, search, and charts of top free and paid apps. Features include in-app purchases and subscriptions, Family Sharing, Apple Arcade (a subscription game service launched in 2019), privacy "nutrition labels" (introduced in 2020) that disclose data practices, and App Tracking Transparency, which since 2021 requires apps to request permission before tracking users across other companies' apps and websites.

Economic impact

Apple reports that the App Store ecosystem facilitated over $1 trillion in developer billings and sales globally in 2022, and that the store averages over 650 million weekly visitors across 175 storefronts. The storefront is localised into dozens of languages and supports region-specific payment methods.
