Choirmaster
OccupationMusic director of a choir
Also calledChoir director, choral conductor, director of music
SettingsChurches, cathedrals, schools, community and professional choirs

A choirmaster (also choir director or choral conductor) is the person responsible for leading, training, and directing a choir. The role combines conducting technique with vocal pedagogy: the choirmaster selects repertoire, runs rehearsals, develops the ensemble's tone and technique, and directs performances. In churches and cathedrals the choirmaster is often also the organist, holding the combined title of organist and choirmaster or director of music.

Duties

The choirmaster's work includes auditioning and placing singers into voice parts (soprano, alto, tenor, bass), teaching music reading and vocal technique, planning programmes suited to the choir's ability and the occasion, and coordinating with clergy, school administrators, or concert promoters. For liturgical choirs, the choirmaster chooses anthems, psalm settings, and service music appropriate to the church calendar.

History

Formal direction of choirs dates to the chant traditions of medieval monasteries and cathedrals, where the precentor or succentor led the singing. In the Renaissance, the maestro di cappella (master of the chapel) — a post held by composers such as Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina and William Byrd — directed court and church choirs and composed much of their repertoire. The modern choirmaster emerged with the choral-society movement of the 19th century, when large amateur choirs made the specialist choral conductor a distinct profession.

Training

Choirmasters commonly train in music education, choral conducting, or church music, often earning degrees with study in conducting gesture, score analysis, diction in multiple languages, and vocal health. Professional bodies such as the American Choral Directors Association and national church-music organizations offer certification and continuing education.

Notable choirmasters

  • John Eliot Gardiner — founder of the Monteverdi Choir
  • Robert Shaw — director of the Atlanta Symphony Chorus
  • Dale Warland — founder of the Dale Warland Singers
  • Harry Christophers — founder of The Sixteen
  • David Willcocks — long-serving director of the Choir of King's College, Cambridge
