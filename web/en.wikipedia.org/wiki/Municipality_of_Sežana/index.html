The Municipality of Sežana (Slovene: Občina Sežana) is a municipality in the Littoral region of Slovenia, near the border with Italy. Its seat is the town of Sežana. The municipality covers part of the Karst Plateau, a limestone region that gave its name to karst topography worldwide.

Geography

The municipality lies in southwestern Slovenia, adjacent to the Italian city of Trieste. The landscape is classic karst: dry limestone plateaus, dolines, dry valleys, and caves. The area is known for the bora wind (burja), for Teran wine produced from the local red soils (terra rossa), and for dry-cured Karst prosciutto (kraški pršut).

Settlements

In addition to the municipal seat of Sežana, the municipality includes settlements such as Dane pri Sežani, Dutovlje, Lipica, Lokev, Pliskovica, Štanjel (transferred in boundary adjustments over the years), Štorje, Tomaj, and Vrabče. Lipica is famous as the original home of the Lipizzaner horse breed, where the stud farm was founded in 1580.

Attractions

Notable sites include the Lipica Stud Farm, the Škocjan Caves regional park area nearby, the village of Lokev with its fortified church tower, and numerous wine cellars along the Karst Wine Route. Tomaj was the home village of the Slovene poet Srečko Kosovel, who is commemorated there.
