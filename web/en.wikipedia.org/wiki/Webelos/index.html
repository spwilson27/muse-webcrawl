Webelos

Webelos is a program in the Cub Scouts programme of Scouting America (formerly the Boy Scouts of America) for fourth- and fifth-grade youth, roughly ages nine to eleven. The name is a contraction of "We'll Be Loyal Scouts". Webelos is designed as a transition programme between Cub Scouts and Scouts BSA, with more challenging activities and a greater emphasis on camping and outdoor skills.

Programme

Webelos Scouts work in dens led by adult den leaders, with increasing help from patrol methods borrowed from Scouts BSA. They earn adventure pins for completing required and elective adventures in areas such as first aid, aquatics, building, engineering and outdoor ethics. The highest rank in Cub Scouting, the Arrow of Light, is earned in the second Webelos year, traditionally in the fifth grade, and prepares a Scout to cross over into a Scouts BSA troop.

History

The Webelos programme was introduced in 1954 with the Webelos den for older Cub Scouts and its own badge. Over the decades the programme was expanded from a single transition year into a two-year programme, and following the admission of girls to Cub Scouting in 2018, Webelos dens may be single-gender dens within packs serving all youth.
