Kawa no Nagare no Yō ni

"Kawa no Nagare no Yō ni"
Artist: Hibari Misora
Released: 1989
Genre: Enka
Lyrics: Yasushi Akimoto; Music: Akira Mitake

"Kawa no Nagare no Yō ni" (Japanese: 川の流れのように, "Like the Flow of the River") is a song by Japanese singer Hibari Misora, released in 1989 shortly before her death. It is regarded as one of the greatest Japanese songs of all time.

Background

The song, with lyrics by Yasushi Akimoto and music by Akira Mitake, reflects on a life lived like the flow of a river. It was Misora's final single and became a signature farewell for the beloved enka singer.

Legacy

In a 1997 NHK nationwide poll it was voted the greatest Japanese song of all time. It is frequently performed at commemorative events and was sung, in tribute, at the closing ceremonies of national celebrations.
