The Fox Corporation (stylized in all-caps as FOX Corporation) is an American multinational mass media corporation headquartered at 1211 Avenue of the Americas in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. It was formed on March 19, 2019, as a result of the acquisition of 21st Century Fox by The Walt Disney Company, with the assets that Disney did not acquire being spun off to the new company. It is primarily owned by the Murdoch family through a family trust.

History

Fox Corporation's origins lie in 21st Century Fox, which itself succeeded News Corporation in the 2013 split of Rupert Murdoch's media empire. In December 2017, Disney announced it would acquire most of 21st Century Fox's entertainment assets for $52.4 billion, a deal later raised to $71.3 billion after a competing bid from Comcast. The spin-off of the remaining assets — principally the Fox broadcast network, Fox Television Stations, Fox News, Fox Business, and the national operations of Fox Sports — was approved by shareholders and completed in March 2019.

The company began trading as a standalone public company on March 19, 2019. Lachlan Murdoch serves as chair and chief executive officer. In 2025, the company's board approved a plan to separate its cable networks into a distinct company as part of broader restructuring in the American television industry.

Assets

The company's principal assets include the Fox Broadcasting Company (the Fox television network), Fox News Media (including the Fox News Channel and Fox Business Network), Fox Sports Media Group (including FS1, FS2, and the Big Ten Network), Fox Television Stations, the streaming service Tubi, and the entertainment production studio Fox Entertainment, whose units include Fox Alternative Entertainment and the animation studio Bento Box Entertainment.

Corporate governance

Fox Corporation has a dual-class share structure. Class A shares are traded publicly, while Class B shares carry greater voting rights and are largely held by the Murdoch family trust, giving the family effective control of the company despite holding a minority of the equity.
