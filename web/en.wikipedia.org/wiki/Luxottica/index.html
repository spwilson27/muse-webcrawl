Luxottica

Luxottica Group S.p.A.
Type: Subsidiary (EssilorLuxottica)
Industry: Eyewear
Founded: 1961, Agordo, Italy
Founder: Leonardo Del Vecchio
Brands: Ray-Ban, Oakley, Persol

Luxottica Group S.p.A. is an Italian eyewear company founded in 1961 by Leonardo Del Vecchio in Agordo, in the Venetian eyewear district. It became the world's largest eyewear manufacturer, designing, making, and distributing prescription frames and sunglasses, and in 2018 it merged with the French lens maker Essilor to form EssilorLuxottica.

History

Del Vecchio began by supplying frame components to other makers, then launched his own complete spectacles under the Luxottica name. The company grew through vertical integration and acquisitions, buying brands such as Ray-Ban and Oakley and retail chains including LensCrafters, Sunglass Hut, and Pearle Vision.

Business

Luxottica also produces eyewear under licence for fashion houses including Chanel, Prada, and Giorgio Armani, and operates optical retail in North America, Asia-Pacific, and elsewhere. Its scale has periodically drawn criticism and competition scrutiny over pricing and market dominance in the eyewear industry.

Merger

The combination with Essilor, announced in 2017 and completed the following year, joined the largest frame maker and retailer with the largest ophthalmic lens producer, creating a group covering nearly every stage of the optical value chain.
