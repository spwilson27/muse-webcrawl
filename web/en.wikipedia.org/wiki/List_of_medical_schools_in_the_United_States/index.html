List of medical schools in the United States

This is a list of medical schools in the United States. The United States has more than 150 accredited allopathic (MD-granting) medical schools accredited by the Liaison Committee on Medical Education, and more than 40 osteopathic (DO-granting) colleges accredited by the Commission on Osteopathic College Accreditation. The oldest surviving medical school is the Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania (1765).
