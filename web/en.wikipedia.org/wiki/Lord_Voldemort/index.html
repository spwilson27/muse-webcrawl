Lord Voldemort (born Tom Marvolo Riddle) is a fictional character and the main antagonist in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series of novels. He first appears in Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (1997) and returns as the central villain throughout the series, culminating in Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows (2007). Within the wizarding world he is so feared that most characters refuse to speak his name, referring to him instead as "You-Know-Who" or "He-Who-Must-Not-Be-Named".

Character biography

Tom Riddle was born in 1926 to the witch Merope Gaunt, a descendant of Salazar Slytherin, and Tom Riddle Sr., a wealthy Muggle whom she had enchanted. Raised in a London orphanage, he attended Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Slytherin and proved a brilliant but ruthless student. Obsessed with escaping death, he split his soul by murder into seven Horcruxes, adopted the name Lord Voldemort (an anagram of "Tom Marvolo Riddle"), and gathered followers known as Death Eaters. His reign of terror ended when his Killing Curse against the infant Harry Potter rebounded, destroying his body; he returned to full power fourteen years later before being finally defeated at the Battle of Hogwarts.

Reception

Voldemort is widely ranked among the greatest literary and film villains. Ralph Fiennes's portrayal in the films (from Goblet of Fire onwards) was critically acclaimed. Rowling has compared Voldemort's ideology of blood purity to real-world bigotry and described him as a psychopath devoid of empathy or love.
