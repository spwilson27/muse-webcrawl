List of universities in Switzerland

Switzerland has twelve public universities: two federal institutes of technology and ten cantonal universities. The oldest, the University of Basel, was founded in 1460. In addition, Switzerland has a system of universities of applied sciences (Fachhochschulen / hautes écoles spécialisées) and universities of teacher education.
