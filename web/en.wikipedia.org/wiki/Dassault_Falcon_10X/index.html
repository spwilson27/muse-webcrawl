Dassault Falcon 10X

The Dassault Falcon 10X is an ultra-long-range business jet under development by French manufacturer Dassault Aviation. Announced in May 2021, it is designed to be the flagship of the Falcon family, with the largest cabin of any purpose-built business jet and a range of 7,500 nautical miles (13,900 km), linking city pairs such as New York–Shanghai and Paris–Santiago nonstop.

Design

The Falcon 10X has a cabin 2.77 m wide and 2.03 m high, with a cabin volume of about 78.7 cubic metres, and windows roughly 50 percent larger than those of the Falcon 8X. It is powered by two Rolls-Royce Pearl 10X turbofans, each producing more than 18,000 lbf of thrust. The aircraft features a carbon-fibre composite wing, Dassault's Digital Flight Control System derived from the Rafale fighter, and the FalconEye combined vision system. Its cabin can be fitted with a full queen-size bed and an optional shower.

Development

Dassault unveiled the 10X on 6 May 2021 as a competitor to the Bombardier Global 7500 and Gulfstream G700/G800. Entry into service, initially targeted for 2025, was subsequently pushed back as development progressed.
