Gruppo Campari

Davide Campari-Milano N.V.
Trade name: Campari Group
Industry: Beverages
Founded: 1860, Milan, Italy

Gruppo Campari, legally Davide Campari-Milano N.V., is an Italian multinational beverage company and one of the world's largest producers of spirits and aperitifs. It was founded in 1860 by Gaspare Campari, inventor of the red bitter liqueur Campari, and developed by his son Davide.

Portfolio

The group's brands include Campari, Aperol (whose Spritz helped drive major growth in the 2010s), SKYY Vodka, Wild Turkey bourbon, Grand Marnier (acquired in 2016), Appleton Estate rum, Glen Grant whisky, Espolòn tequila, and Cinzano vermouths and sparkling wines. The company distributes in over 190 countries and is listed on the Milan Stock Exchange as a constituent of the FTSE MIB index.

History

From a single Milan café, the company expanded through Davide Campari's international marketing in the early 20th century and accelerated acquisitions from the 1990s under the Garavoglia family, which remains the controlling shareholder through the holding company Lagfin.
