Settlement (finance)

In finance, settlement is the process by which a trade is completed: the buyer receives the securities (or other assets) purchased and the seller receives payment. It follows trade execution and is typically handled by clearing houses and central securities depositories.

Settlement cycles

Historically, trades settled several business days after execution, expressed as T+n. Many markets, including the United States since May 2024, use T+1 settlement for equities — one business day after the trade date. Delivery versus payment (DvP) arrangements ensure that securities are delivered only when payment is made, reducing counterparty risk.

Failures

A settlement fail occurs when one party does not deliver on time; regulations in many jurisdictions impose penalties or buy-in procedures to resolve fails and protect market integrity.
