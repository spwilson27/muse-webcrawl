Nissan Z

The Nissan Z (model code RZ34; Fairlady Z in Japan) is the seventh generation of Nissan's Z-car sports car line, unveiled in production form in August 2021 and sold from 2022 as successor to the 370Z. Its styling deliberately echoes the original S30 240Z at the front and the Z32 300ZX at the rear.

Specifications

The Z uses the 3.0-litre VR30DDTT twin-turbocharged V6 producing 400 hp (around 420 hp in the 2024 Nismo version), driving the rear wheels through a six-speed manual with rev-matching or a nine-speed automatic. It rides on an updated version of the 370Z's FM platform with a stiffer body.

Motorsport

A GT4 racing version was developed with Nismo, and the car has been campaigned in Japan's Super Taikyu series. See also Nissan Fairlady Z.
