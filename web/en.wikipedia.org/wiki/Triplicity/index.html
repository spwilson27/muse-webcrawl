Triplicity

In astrology, a triplicity is a grouping of three zodiac signs belonging to the same element. There are four triplicities, corresponding to the four classical elements:

  • Fire — Aries, Leo, Sagittarius
  • Earth — Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn
  • Air — Gemini, Libra, Aquarius
  • Water — Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces

Triplicity rulers

In traditional (Hellenistic and medieval) astrology, each triplicity is governed by a set of triplicity rulers — planets assigned as lords of the element, differing for day (diurnal) and night (nocturnal) charts, with a third "participating" ruler in the system of Dorotheus of Sidon. For example, the fire triplicity is ruled by the Sun by day and Jupiter by night, with Saturn participating. The condition of a planet's triplicity ruler was one of the five essential dignities used to assess planetary strength.

Modern astrology generally uses the triplicities simply as the element groupings, emphasising the shared temperament of the three signs.
