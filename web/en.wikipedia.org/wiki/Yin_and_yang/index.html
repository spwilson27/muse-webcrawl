Yin and yang is a concept in Chinese philosophy describing how opposite or contrary forces are complementary, interconnected and interdependent: dark and light, receptive and active, female and male, moon and sun. Neither pole is superior; each contains the seed of the other (shown by the dots in the taijitu symbol), and imbalance between them is the root of disharmony in the body, society and cosmos.

Applications

The idea, systematised in the I Ching tradition and correlative cosmology of the Han dynasty, underlies traditional Chinese medicine (health as balanced qi), feng shui, martial arts and Taoism and Confucianism alike. In the West it has become a popular emblem of balance and holistic thinking.
