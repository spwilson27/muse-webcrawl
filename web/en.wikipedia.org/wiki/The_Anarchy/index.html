The Anarchy
Date: 1138–1153
Location: England and Normandy
Combatants: Stephen of Blois vs Empress Matilda
Result: Treaty of Wallingford; Henry II succeeds Stephen

The Anarchy was a civil war in England and Normandy between 1138 and 1153 over succession to Henry I. His daughter, Empress Matilda, contested the crown seized by his nephew Stephen of Blois after Henry's death in 1135.

Course

Stephen was captured at Lincoln in 1141, then Matilda was driven from London; her husband Geoffrey of Anjou conquered Normandy. Chroniclers described a breakdown of law — "Christ and his saints slept".

Settlement

The Treaty of Wallingford (1153) let Stephen reign for life, with Matilda's son Henry as heir; he became Henry II in 1154, founding Plantagenet rule.
