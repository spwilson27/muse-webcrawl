Family tree of Muhammad

This article summarizes the family tree of Muhammad, the Islamic prophet, including his ancestors of the Quraysh tribe of Mecca, his wives, children and descendants. Muhammad belonged to the Banu Hashim clan of the Quraysh, whose lineage was traditionally traced back to Adnan and, through him, to Ishmael and Abraham.

Ancestry

Muhammad's father was Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib, and his mother was Amina bint Wahb of the Banu Zuhra clan. His grandfather, Abd al-Muttalib, was the guardian of the Kaaba and a leading figure in Mecca. Earlier ancestors in the traditional genealogy include Hashim ibn Abd Manaf, Abd Manaf, Qusayy — who united the Quraysh in Mecca — and Fihr, the eponym of the tribe.

Wives and children

Muhammad's first wife was Khadija bint Khuwaylid, to whom he was married for 25 years until her death. Their children included sons Qasim and Abdullah, who died in childhood, and daughters Zaynab, Ruqayya, Umm Kulthum and Fatima. After Khadija's death Muhammad married several women, including Aisha bint Abi Bakr, Hafsa bint Umar, and the others among the "Mothers of the Believers". With Maria al-Qibtiyya he had a son, Ibrahim, who also died young.

Descendants

Of Muhammad's children, only Fatima lived long enough to bear children who survived into adulthood. Fatima married Ali ibn Abi Talib, Muhammad's cousin and son-in-law, and their sons Hasan and Husayn continued the line. Descendants of Muhammad through Fatima and Ali are known as Sayyids and Sharifs, and the families descended from Hasan are generally called Sharifs while those from Husayn are called Sayyids.
