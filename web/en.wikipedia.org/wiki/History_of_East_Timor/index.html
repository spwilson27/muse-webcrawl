History of East Timor

The history of East Timor spans human settlement tens of thousands of years ago, Portuguese colonisation from the 16th century, Japanese occupation in World War II, Indonesian occupation from 1975 to 1999, and independence in 2002.

Portuguese Timor

Portugal claimed the eastern half of Timor island, trading in sandalwood; the western half came under Dutch control. The border was formalised in the 19th–20th centuries.

Occupation and independence

After a brief declaration of independence in November 1975, Indonesia invaded and annexed East Timor; the occupation was marked by widespread violence and famine. A 1999 UN-sponsored referendum chose independence, followed by UN administration and full independence on 20 May 2002, making Timor-Leste Asia's youngest nation at the time.
