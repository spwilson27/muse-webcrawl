The 1988 Winter Olympics, officially the XV Olympic Winter Games, were held in Calgary, Canada, from 13 to 28 February 1988. They were the first Winter Games held in Canada and the last Winter Olympics in which the Soviet Union and East Germany competed.

Highlights

Finnish ski jumper Matti Nykänen won three gold medals. The Games are fondly remembered for the Jamaican bobsleigh team's debut and British ski jumper Eddie "the Eagle" Edwards, whose crowd-pleasing but last-place finishes embodied the Olympic spirit. Super-G and team events in ski jumping and Nordic combined were added to the program.

Legacy

Calgary extended the Games to 16 days, spread over three weekends, a format retained since. The facilities, including the Olympic Oval and Canada Olympic Park, remain in use, and the Games set records for television audiences.
