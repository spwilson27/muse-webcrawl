Context: Sicilian Mafia, American Mafia

Consigliere

Consigliere (Italian for "counselor" or "adviser") is a position within the leadership structure of the Sicilian, Calabrian, and Italian-American Mafia. The consigliere is the adviser to the boss, serving as his trusted confidant and representative in meetings with other families and with the family's own soldiers and capos.

Role

Traditionally the consigliere is an older, respected member chosen by the boss, often acting as a mediator in disputes and as one of the few who can challenge the boss's decisions. The position ranks alongside the boss and underboss at the top of the family hierarchy, though its holder commands no crew directly. The role is intended to provide the boss with impartial advice, free of the ambitions of other members.

In popular culture

The term was popularized by Mario Puzo's novel The Godfather (1969) and its film adaptations, in which Tom Hagen serves as consigliere to Don Vito Corleone. Puzo's depiction of the adviser as a calm intermediary between the boss and the outside world fixed the word in English usage.
