Overview

Makerere University, in Kampala, is Uganda's largest and oldest university and one of Africa's most prestigious institutions — long known as the "Harvard of Africa". Founded in 1922 as a technical school, it became a university college of the University of London in 1949 and of the University of East Africa in 1963, and an independent national university in 1970.

History

Makerere educated a generation of East African independence leaders and writers (the journal Transition was founded there in 1961; an influential 1962 conference of African writers was held at Makerere). It suffered under the Amin and civil-war years, revived after 1986, and now enrols tens of thousands of students across colleges including medicine (Mulago), agriculture and law. The main administration building, the Ivory Tower, burned in September 2020 and has been rebuilt.
