Indexing

Indexing may refer to several related concepts:

Publishing and information retrieval

The creation of a book index or database index — an organised list of terms, subjects, or records with pointers to their locations, enabling rapid lookup. Professional indexing is governed by standards such as ISO 999, and indexers analyse a text's concepts to provide access points for readers.

Computing

In databases, an index is a data structure (such as a B-tree) that speeds up data retrieval at the cost of extra storage and slower writes. Search engines index web pages by crawling and storing inverted indexes of terms. Array indexing refers to accessing elements of an array by position.

Finance and economics

Indexation is the adjustment of payments or prices by reference to a price index, such as inflation-indexed wages or bonds. Index investing is a strategy of buying securities that replicate a market index.

Other uses

In mechanical engineering, indexing describes the intermittent rotary motion of a workpiece between machining positions. In genealogy and archives, indexing projects transcribe records into searchable databases.
