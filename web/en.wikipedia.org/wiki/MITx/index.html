MITx is the massive open online course (MOOC) initiative of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Announced in December 2011, it offered MIT courses online, free of charge, to a worldwide audience, with certificates available to learners who completed them.

History

The prototype course, 6.002x Circuits and Electronics, launched in March 2012 and attracted around 155,000 registered students. In May 2012 MIT and Harvard University jointly founded the non-profit edX platform, on which MITx courses were delivered alongside those of other universities. MIT's experiment built on its earlier OpenCourseWare project, which had published course materials online since 2001.

Offerings

MITx offers individual courses and structured programmes including MicroMasters credentials, some of which can count towards credit for learners subsequently admitted to related master's programmes at MIT. Subjects range across computer science, engineering, data science, business and the humanities. Following the sale of edX to 2U in 2021, MIT also launched MITx Online as its own platform for its online courses.
