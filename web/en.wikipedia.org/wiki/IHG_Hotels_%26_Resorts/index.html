InterContinental Hotels Group, marketed as IHG Hotels & Resorts, is a British multinational hospitality company headquartered in Denham, Buckinghamshire, England. It is one of the world's largest hotel groups by number of rooms, operating thousands of hotels across more than 100 countries.

History

IHG traces its origins to the Bass brewing business, which expanded into hotels in the 20th century. Bass acquired Holiday Inn in 1989 and the InterContinental brand in 1998. The hotels and soft drinks businesses were demerged in 2003, creating InterContinental Hotels Group as an independent listed company.

Brands

The portfolio spans luxury brands such as Six Senses, Regent and InterContinental; premium brands including Kimpton and Hotel Indigo; and mainstream brands such as Holiday Inn, Holiday Inn Express, Crowne Plaza, and Staybridge Suites. Most hotels are franchised or managed rather than owned.
