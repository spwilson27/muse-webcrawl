Meteoroid

Meteoroid
DefinitionSmall rocky or metallic body in outer space
SizeFrom dust grains to about 1 metre
FateBecomes a meteor in atmosphere; meteorite if it lands
[Image: A small rocky meteoroid tumbling through space, stars behind]

A meteoroid is a small rocky or metallic body travelling through space, significantly smaller than an asteroid — roughly from a grain of dust up to a metre across. The terminology is by location: in space a meteoroid, in the atmosphere a meteor, on the ground a meteorite.

Significance

Earth sweeps up tens of tonnes of meteoroid material daily, mostly as dust. The 2013 Chelyabinsk meteor (about 20 metres) injured over a thousand people, mostly by broken glass — the largest documented modern event. Studying meteorites recovered on the ground gives samples of the early Solar System, complementing missions like Psyche.
