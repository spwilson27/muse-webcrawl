Nickelodeon Universe

Nickelodeon Universe is the name of two indoor theme parks in the United States themed to characters and shows from the Nickelodeon television network. The original park is located in the center of the Mall of America in Bloomington, Minnesota, and a second opened at the American Dream mall in East Rutherford, New Jersey.

Mall of America

The Minnesota park opened with the mall on August 11, 1992, as Camp Snoopy, themed to the Peanuts characters. After the park lost the Peanuts licence, it briefly operated as The Park at MOA before reopening on March 15, 2008, as Nickelodeon Universe. Covering about seven acres beneath a glass roof, it is one of the largest indoor theme parks in the United States, with attractions themed to SpongeBob SquarePants, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Dora the Explorer, and other Nickelodeon properties. Signature rides include the SpongeBob SquarePants Rock Bottom Plunge and the Fairly Odd Coaster.

American Dream

The New Jersey location opened on October 25, 2019, as part of the long-delayed American Dream entertainment and retail complex in the Meadowlands. At about 8.5 acres, it is among the largest indoor theme parks in the Western Hemisphere, featuring record-setting coasters such as the TMNT Shellraiser, a steep Gerstlauer Euro-Fighter, and The Shredder, a spinning coaster.
