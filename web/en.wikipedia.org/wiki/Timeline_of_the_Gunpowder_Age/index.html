Timeline of the Gunpowder Age


Gunpowder transformed warfare over six centuries

The Gunpowder Age spans the period from the invention of gunpowder in China to the replacement of black powder by smokeless propellants in the late nineteenth century. This timeline tracks the invention, diffusion, and military application of gunpowder technology.

Timeline

DateEvent
9th centuryChinese alchemists discover gunpowder while seeking an elixir of life.
1044The Wujing Zongyao records the earliest surviving gunpowder formulas.
13th centuryFire lances, bombs, and early guns spread across Eurasia; gunpowder reaches Europe.
1320sCannon appear in European records and sieges.
1453Ottoman great bombards batter the walls of Constantinople.
16th centuryMatchlock muskets transform infantry warfare; bastion fortresses evolve in response to artillery.
17th–18th centuriesFlintlock weapons, line infantry tactics, and naval broadsides dominate.
1807Forsyth patents the percussion ignition system, forerunner of the percussion cap.
1846Guncotton (nitrocellulose) invented, beginning the era of high explosives and smokeless powders.
1884Paul Vieille develops Poudre B, the first practical smokeless powder, ending the Gunpowder Age.
