Roma (2018 film)

Roma is a 2018 drama film written and directed by Alfonso Cuarón, who also produced, shot, and co-edited it. A semi-autobiographical take on Cuarón's upbringing in the Colonia Roma neighbourhood of Mexico City, the film stars Yalitza Aparicio as Cleo, a Mixtec live-in housekeeper for a middle-class family in 1970 and 1971, set against the backdrop of the Corpus Christi massacre.

Production

Cuarón shot the film in black and white on large-format digital cameras, using long takes and detailed recreations of his childhood home and streets. Aparicio, a preschool teacher with no acting experience, was cast after an open search in Oaxaca. The film was produced by Participant Media and released by Netflix, with a limited theatrical run.

Reception

Roma premiered at the Venice International Film Festival, where it won the Golden Lion. At the 91st Academy Awards it won Best Director, Best Cinematography, and Best Foreign Language Film, and it is frequently ranked among the finest films of the 2010s. Its Netflix distribution reignited debate over streaming releases and theatrical exhibition.
