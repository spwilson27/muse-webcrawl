Tevatron
Location: Fermilab, Batavia, Illinois
Type: Proton–antiproton collider, 6.28 km ring
Energy: 1.96 TeV centre-of-mass
Operated: 1983 – 30 Sep 2011

The Tevatron was a circular particle accelerator at Fermilab in Illinois, the world's highest-energy collider until the Large Hadron Collider surpassed it in 2009. It collided protons and antiprotons in a superconducting synchrotron ring.

Discoveries

Its CDF and DØ experiments discovered the top quark in 1995, the heaviest known elementary particle, and made precision measurements of the W boson and Higgs constraints.

Shutdown

It shut down on 30 September 2011 after funding ended and the LHC took over the energy frontier.
