Riyad Karim Mahrez (born 21 February 1991) is a professional footballer who plays as a right winger for Saudi Pro League club Al-Ahli. Born in France, he captains the Algeria national team. Regarded as one of the best wingers of his generation, he is known for his dribbling, first touch and ability to cut inside onto his left foot.

Club career

Mahrez began his career in French amateur football with AAS Sarcelles and Quimper before turning professional with Le Havre in Ligue 2. In January 2014 he joined English club Leicester City, helping them win the Championship and promotion to the Premier League. In the 2015–16 season Mahrez was the creative star of Leicester's improbable Premier League title triumph, scoring 17 league goals and winning the PFA Players' Player of the Year award — the first African to receive it.

In July 2018 he signed for Manchester City for a club-record £60 million. With City he won four Premier League titles, two FA Cups, three EFL Cups and, in 2023, the UEFA Champions League as part of a continental treble. In July 2023 he transferred to Al-Ahli of Saudi Arabia.

International career

Mahrez made his debut for Algeria in 2014 and represented them at the 2014 FIFA World Cup. He was a key member of the Algeria team that won the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations in Egypt, scoring a famous stoppage-time free kick against Nigeria in the semi-final, and he later became team captain.
