SBB Cargo

ParentSwiss Federal Railways (SBB)
HeadquartersOlten, Switzerland
IndustryRail freight transport

SBB Cargo is the freight division of the Swiss Federal Railways (SBB), responsible for rail freight transport in Switzerland and, through subsidiaries, on the north–south corridor through the Alps between Germany and Italy.

Operations

SBB Cargo operates wagonload networks, combined (intermodal) transport and block trains, carrying goods such as containers, chemicals, steel, paper and foodstuffs. Its subsidiary SBB Cargo International handles transit freight over the Gotthard and Lötschberg axes, greatly expanded by the Gotthard Base Tunnel opened in 2016.

Ownership

In 2020, logistics companies Planzer, Camion Transport, Galliker and Bertschi acquired a combined minority stake (35%) via Swiss Combi AG, with SBB retaining control. SBB Cargo has undergone repeated restructuring amid pressure to cover its costs in the competitive European freight market.
