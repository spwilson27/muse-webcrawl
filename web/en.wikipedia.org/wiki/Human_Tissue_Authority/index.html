Human Tissue Authority

The Human Tissue Authority (HTA) is an executive non-departmental public body of the Department of Health and Social Care in the United Kingdom. It regulates the removal, storage, use and disposal of human bodies, organs and tissue.

History

The HTA was established by the Human Tissue Act 2004 and became operational in 2006. The Act was passed largely in response to organ-retention scandals at hospitals such as Alder Hey Children's Hospital, where organs had been retained after post-mortem examination without families' knowledge or consent.

Role

The Authority licenses and inspects establishments across sectors including anatomy, post-mortem examination, public display, research and transplantation, and approves living organ donations. Its founding principle is that consent is the fundamental requirement for the lawful storage and use of human tissue. The HTA also issues codes of practice giving practical guidance to professionals.
