Chicken tikka

Chicken tikka
RegionPunjab, Indian subcontinent
Main ingredientsChicken, yogurt, spices (chilli, turmeric, garam masala)
CookingMarinated and roasted in a tandoor or grilled on skewers
VariationsPaneer tikka; chicken tikka masala

Chicken tikka is a dish of small pieces of boneless chicken marinated in yogurt and spices and baked in a clay oven (tandoor) or grilled on skewers. It is popular throughout the Indian subcontinent and in the South Asian diaspora worldwide. The word tikka derives from a Turkic/Persian term for a morsel or piece of meat.

Preparation

Chicken pieces are marinated — typically in yogurt with red chilli powder, turmeric, ginger, garlic, lemon juice, and garam masala — giving the dish its characteristic orange-red colour (sometimes deepened with food colouring), then roasted at high heat until lightly charred at the edges. It is served with onion rings, lemon wedges, and mint chutney, often sizzling on a hot plate.

Related dishes

Chicken tikka is the base of chicken tikka masala, in which the grilled chicken is simmered in a spiced tomato-cream sauce — a dish developed in Britain and sometimes called a British national dish. Vegetarian versions use paneer or vegetables.
