Kraków Cloth Hall

Kraków Cloth Hall (Sukiennice)
LocationMain Market Square, Kraków, Poland
StyleRenaissance
Rebuilt1555–1560; restored 1875–1879

The Kraków Cloth Hall (Polish: Sukiennice) is a Renaissance trading hall at the centre of Kraków's Main Market Square. One of the city's most recognisable landmarks, it is often described as one of the world's oldest shopping malls.

History

A cloth hall stood on the square from the 13th century. After a fire in 1555 it was rebuilt in Renaissance style; the arcades visible today were added during a 19th-century restoration. The hall hosted trade in cloth, spices, silk and salt, and welcomed visiting merchants from across Europe.

Today

The ground floor holds stalls selling amber jewellery, handicrafts and souvenirs. The upper floor houses the Gallery of 19th-Century Polish Art, a division of the National Museum, featuring works by Jan Matejko and Józef Chełmoński.
