Cancer Research Institute

The Cancer Research Institute (CRI) is a nonprofit organisation in the United States dedicated to advancing cancer immunology and immunotherapy. Founded in 1953, it funds research at universities and medical centres worldwide.

Founded: 1953
Type: Nonprofit
Focus: Cancer immunotherapy

History

CRI was founded by Helen Coley Nauts, building on the work of her father William Coley, a pioneer of cancer immunotherapy. It has supported generations of immunologists.

Programs

The Institute funds postdoctoral fellowships, clinical trials and laboratory research aimed at harnessing the immune system to treat cancer.
