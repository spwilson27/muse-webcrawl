Dragonslayer

Dragonslayer is a 1981 American fantasy film directed by Matthew Robbins from a screenplay he co-wrote with Hal Barwood. A co-production of Paramount Pictures and Walt Disney Productions, it stars Peter Maconicol as a young sorcerer's apprentice sent to kill a dragon.

Dragonslayer
Director: Matthew Robbins
Released: 26 June 1981
Starring: Peter Maconicol, Caitlin Clarke, Ralph Richardson
Dragon: Vermithrax Pejorative

Plot

In the 6th-century kingdom of Urland, the dragon Vermithrax Pejorative is appeased by twice-yearly sacrifice of virgins chosen by lottery. The aging sorcerer Ulrich (Ralph Richardson) is killed, and his apprentice Galen journeys to slay the dragon with a magical amulet and a forged spear, while Valerian — a young woman disguised as a boy to evade the lottery — exposes the system's injustice.

Production and reception

The dragon, realized by Phil Tippett's "go motion" animation and full-scale models, is widely regarded as one of cinema's finest. Darker and more violent than typical Disney fare, the film underperformed theatrically but gained a lasting cult reputation and an Academy Award nomination for visual effects.
