List of Category 5 Pacific hurricanes

Category 5 is the highest classification on the Saffir–Simpson hurricane wind scale, reserved for tropical cyclones with one-minute sustained winds of at least 137 knots (157 mph; 252 km/h). In the eastern and central North Pacific basins, a few dozen hurricanes have reached this intensity since reliable records began in 1949. The strongest was Hurricane Patricia (2015), the most intense tropical cyclone ever reliably measured in the Western Hemisphere.

Climatology

Category 5 Pacific hurricanes occur most often during strong El Niño years, when warm sea-surface temperatures and low wind shear extend across the basin — 1997, 2015 and 2018 were notably prolific. Most such storms remain at sea; notable landfalls include Kenna in Mexico (2002), Patricia in Mexico (2015, after weakening), and Otis, which struck Acapulco in 2023 after explosive rapid intensification from a tropical storm to Category 5 in about a day.
