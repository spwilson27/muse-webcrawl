HM Prison and Probation Service

His Majesty's Prison and Probation Service (HMPPS) is an executive agency of the Ministry of Justice responsible for prisons and probation services in England and Wales. It was created in 2017, replacing the National Offender Management Service (NOMS).

Responsibilities

HMPPS manages around 120 public sector prisons, oversees contracted private prisons, and delivers probation supervision for offenders serving community sentences and those released on licence. Its stated aims are to protect the public and reduce reoffending.
