Niche market

A niche market is the subset of the market on which a specific product is focused. The market niche defines the product features aimed at satisfying specific market needs, as well as the price range, production quality and the demographics that it is intended to target. It is also a small market segment. Sometimes, a product or service can be entirely designed to satisfy a niche market.

Not every product can be defined by its market niche. The niche market is highly specialized, and aiming to survive among the competitors consists of many super companies. Even established companies create products for different niches; Hewlett-Packard (HP) has all-in-one machines for printing, scanning and faxing targeted for the home office niche, while at the same time having separate machines with one of these functions for bigger businesses.

Niche audience

Production companies often define a niche audience to market to when developing entertainment media. Audience research can drive productions. Sometimes, a production will appeal to a broader audience than originally intended by accident, growing beyond the niche. Television producers in particular aim for a specific niche audience.

Niche marketing

Niche marketing is the process of finding and targeting a specific, well-defined group of customers, sometimes called a "niche." Businesses that employ niche marketing concentrate their marketing efforts on a particular segment and tailor their products or services to the needs and preferences of that segment. Because niche customers tend to be more loyal and willing to pay premium prices, niche marketing can be attractive to smaller firms that cannot compete with large firms on price or reach. Online commerce and search advertising have made niche marketing more practical by making it easier to reach thinly spread audiences.
