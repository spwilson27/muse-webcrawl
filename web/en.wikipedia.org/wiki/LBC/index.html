LBC

LBC (originally the London Broadcasting Company) is a British national radio station owned and operated by Global, broadcasting talk and phone-in programming. It began transmissions on 8 October 1973 as the first licensed commercial radio station in the United Kingdom, beating Capital Radio by eight days.

History

LBC launched in 1973 from studios in Gough Square, London, providing a news and talk service for the capital. The station underwent several changes of ownership and format over the following decades; its FM news operation was at times separated as News Direct 97.3 while LBC continued on AM. Under Global's ownership from 2007, the station relaunched and in 2014 went national on DAB digital radio with the slogan "Leading Britain's Conversation."

Programming

LBC's schedule is built around presenter-led phone-in shows mixing news, politics, and listener calls. Well-known presenters have included Nick Ferrari (long-running breakfast host), James O'Brien, Shelagh Fogarty, Eddie Mair, and Iain Dale. The station's mid-morning programme hosted by James O'Brien, including its "Mystery Hour," has attracted particular attention in coverage of British politics.

Audience

Since going national, LBC's audience measured by RAJAR has grown to roughly three million weekly listeners, making it one of the UK's most listened-to commercial stations.
