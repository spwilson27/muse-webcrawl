HNA Group

HNA Group Co., Ltd. was a Chinese conglomerate headquartered in Haikou, Hainan, founded in 2000 and built around Hainan Airlines. During the 2010s it expanded aggressively into aviation, tourism, logistics, financial services and real estate, acquiring stakes in companies including Hilton Worldwide and Deutsche Bank.

Collapse

Heavy debt from its acquisition spree led Chinese regulators to pressure the group to sell assets. In 2021 HNA entered bankruptcy restructuring; its aviation and airport businesses were separated under new strategic investors, including Fangda Group taking control of the airline arm.
