Marble Hornets

Genre: Horror, found footage, alternate reality game
Created by: Troy Wagner, Joseph DeLage
Platform: YouTube
Original run: 2009 – 2014
Episodes: 87 entries (plus totheark responses)

Marble Hornets is an American found footage horror web series created by Troy Wagner and Joseph DeLage. It was one of the first works to popularise the Slender Man mythos, presenting the figure as "the Operator", a tall, faceless entity in a suit that stalks the characters.

Plot

Film student Jay Merrick receives tapes from his friend Alex Kralie, who abandoned his student film project, Marble Hornets, under mysterious circumstances. Reviewing the footage, Jay discovers a strange faceless figure appearing in the background of shots and evidence that the production was stalked by the entity. His investigation — uploaded as a numbered series of "Entries" — draws in Tim Wright, Brian Thomas, and Jessica Locke, and is complicated by the masked figures and by cryptic response videos from the channel "totheark".

Influence

The series is credited with defining the found-footage horror ARG on YouTube and inspiring numerous successor series such as EverymanHYBRID and TribeTwelve. A film adaptation, Always Watching: A Marble Hornets Story, was released in 2015.
