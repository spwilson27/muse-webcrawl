Registered agent

A registered agent (also known as a resident agent or statutory agent) is a person or company designated by a business entity, such as a corporation or limited liability company, to receive service of process, legal notices and official government correspondence on its behalf in a state where it is registered to do business.

Requirements

Most United States jurisdictions require every corporation and LLC to continuously maintain a registered agent with a physical street address (the registered office) in the state of formation and in each state where the entity is qualified as a foreign corporation. The agent must be available during regular business hours to accept legal documents. Many businesses use commercial registered agent services, which provide an address in all states and forward documents to the company.
