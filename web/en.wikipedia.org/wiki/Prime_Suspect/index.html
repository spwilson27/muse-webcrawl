Prime Suspect

Prime Suspect is a British police procedural television series created by Lynda La Plante, first broadcast on ITV in 1991. The series stars Helen Mirren as Detective Chief Inspector (later Detective Superintendent) Jane Tennison, one of the first senior female detectives in the Metropolitan Police, who battles institutional sexism while leading murder investigations. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest British television dramas and a landmark of the crime genre.

Overview

Each series typically follows a single murder investigation over multiple feature-length episodes. The first series (1991) sees Tennison take over a case after the sudden death of her predecessor, confronting both a serial killer suspect, George Marlow, and the hostility of her male colleagues. Subsequent series tackled subjects including child murder, racism within the police, and organised crime.

Reception and legacy

The series won numerous awards, including BAFTAs and an International Emmy; Mirren won three consecutive BAFTA Awards for Best Actress for the role. A prequel series, Prime Suspect 1973, aired in 2017, and an American adaptation starring Maria Bello was broadcast by NBC in 2011–2012. The character of Tennison also appears in La Plante's novels.
