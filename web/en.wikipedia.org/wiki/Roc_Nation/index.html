Roc Nation is an American entertainment company founded by rapper and businessman Jay-Z in 2008. Based in New York City, the company operates as a record label, talent agency, media production company, and sports management agency, and was formed in partnership with concert promoter Live Nation.

The Roc Nation logo.

History

Jay-Z founded Roc Nation after departing his post as president of Def Jam Recordings, signing a wide-ranging deal with Live Nation covering recorded music, touring and management. The label and management rosters grew to include artists such as Rihanna, J. Cole, Shakira and Megan Thee Stallion, among many others.

Divisions

Roc Nation Sports, founded in 2013, represents professional athletes in football, basketball, baseball and other sports. The company's philanthropic and social-justice arm, Team Roc, campaigns on criminal-justice reform and education issues. In 2019 Roc Nation entered a partnership with the National Football League covering entertainment programming, including the Super Bowl halftime show, and social-justice initiatives under the Inspire Change banner.
