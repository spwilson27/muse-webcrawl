Rupert Murdoch

Rupert Murdoch
Born11 March 1931, Melbourne
OccupationMedia proprietor (News Corp, Fox)
[Image: Murdoch at a media conference in the 2010s]

Rupert Murdoch (born 1931) is an Australian-born American media proprietor who built a global empire from an Adelaide newspaper inheritance: Britain's Sun, Times and Sunday Times; the New York Post; the Wall Street Journal (2007); the Fox broadcast network (1986), Fox News (1996), Sky and HarperCollins. He shaped conservative politics in Australia, Britain and the US for half a century.

Succession and scandals

The News of the World phone-hacking scandal (2011) closed that paper and derailed his full BSkyB takeover. In 2023 he stepped down as chairman of Fox and News Corp in favour of his son Lachlan, remaining chairman emeritus; a 2024 Nevada probate fight over the family trust, which he lost, tested the succession.
