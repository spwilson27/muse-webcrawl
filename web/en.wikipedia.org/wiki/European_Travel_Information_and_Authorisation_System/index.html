European Travel Information and Authorisation System

European Travel Information and Authorisation System (ETIAS)
Type: Electronic travel authorisation
Region: European Union / Schengen Area
Legal basis: Regulation (EU) 2018/1240 (adopted September 2018)
Applies to: Visa-exempt third-country nationals
Administered by: eu-LISA, with Frontex hosting the ETIAS Central Unit

The European Travel Information and Authorisation System (ETIAS) is an electronic travel authorisation system of the European Union for visitors from countries whose citizens do not require a visa to enter the Schengen Area and other participating European states. Modelled in part on the US ESTA and similar systems, it requires travellers to apply online and receive authorisation before departure.

How it works

Applicants complete an online form with identity, travel document and background information and pay a fee (set at €7 in the original regulation, with exemptions for minors and older travellers). Applications are screened automatically against EU security databases; most are expected to be approved within minutes, while flagged cases are assessed manually by national units. An authorisation is valid for three years, or until the passport expires, and permits short stays of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. ETIAS is not a visa, and it does not guarantee entry.

Timeline

The system was legislated in 2018 and is developed by eu-LISA alongside the Entry/Exit System (EES). Its launch has been postponed several times; following the start of EES operations, ETIAS is expected to follow, becoming mandatory after a transitional grace period.
