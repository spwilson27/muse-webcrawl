2008 Pittsburgh Steelers season

2008 Pittsburgh Steelers season

Owner: The Rooney family

Head coach: Mike Tomlin

Home stadium: Heinz Field

Record: 12–4

Division place: 1st AFC North

Playoffs: Won Super Bowl XLIII (vs. Cardinals) 27–23

The 2008 Pittsburgh Steelers season was the franchise's 76th season in the National Football League (NFL). The Steelers won their sixth Lombardi Trophy, becoming the first team to win six Super Bowls, by defeating the Arizona Cardinals 27–23 in Super Bowl XLIII. The season was Mike Tomlin's second as head coach.

Regular season

Pittsburgh finished 12–4, winning the AFC North, behind the league's top-ranked defense, coordinated by Dick LeBeau and led by linebacker James Harrison, who was named the NFL Defensive Player of the Year, and safety Troy Polamalu. Quarterback Ben Roethlisberger led the offense, with wide receivers Hines Ward and Santonio Holmes as his principal targets. The Steelers faced what was widely considered the league's toughest schedule.

Playoffs

After a first-round bye, the Steelers defeated the San Diego Chargers 35–24 in the divisional round and the Baltimore Ravens 23–14 in the AFC Championship Game, highlighted by Polamalu's game-sealing interception return for a touchdown.

Super Bowl XLIII

In Super Bowl XLIII in Tampa, the Steelers built a 20–7 lead, aided by Harrison's 100-yard interception return for a touchdown to end the first half — the longest play in Super Bowl history at the time. Arizona rallied behind Kurt Warner and Larry Fitzgerald to lead 23–20 late in the fourth quarter, but Roethlisberger drove Pittsburgh 78 yards, and Santonio Holmes's toe-tapping touchdown catch in the corner of the end zone with 35 seconds left gave the Steelers a 27–23 win. Holmes was named Super Bowl MVP.
