Television antenna

A television antenna (or TV aerial) is an antenna specifically designed for the reception of over-the-air broadcast television signals, which are transmitted in the VHF and UHF frequency bands. Outdoor rooftop antennas typically use a Yagi–Uda array of aluminium elements; indoor antennas include "rabbit ears" for VHF and loop antennas for UHF.

Design

Element lengths are cut to fractions of the wavelength of the channels to be received. Directional antennas such as the Yagi provide gain in one direction and are aimed at the transmitter; log-periodic designs cover wide frequency ranges. Amplifiers may be added to compensate for cable losses or weak signals.

Use

Antenna use declined with cable and satellite television but revived with digital terrestrial television and cord-cutting, as modern broadcasts can be received free with an antenna in most populated areas.
