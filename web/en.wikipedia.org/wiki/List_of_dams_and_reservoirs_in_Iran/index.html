List of dams and reservoirs in Iran

Iran has pursued an extensive dam-building programme since the mid-20th century, driven by demands for irrigation water, municipal supply, flood control, and hydroelectric power in a largely arid country. Several hundred dams are in operation, under construction, or under study, concentrated on the rivers draining the Zagros and Alborz mountains — above all the Karun, Dez, and Karkheh rivers in the southwest. The programme has also been controversial, with critics linking dam building and inter-basin transfers to the drying of wetlands and Lake Urmia.

Major dams

  • Karun-3 Dam — double-curvature arch dam on the Karun River, 205 m high, about 2,280 MW of generating capacity
  • Karun-1 (Shahid Abbaspour) Dam — arch dam on the Karun, completed 1976, about 2,000 MW
  • Masjed Soleyman (Karun-2) Dam — rock-fill dam on the Karun, about 2,000 MW
  • Karun-4 Dam — arch dam, about 1,000 MW
  • Dez Dam (Mohammad Reza Shah Pahlavi Dam) — arch dam on the Dez River completed 1963, 203 m high
  • Karkheh Dam — one of the largest earth-fill dams in the world by volume, on the Karkheh River; its reservoir is among Iran's largest
  • Upper Gotvand Dam — rock-fill dam on the Karun, 180 m high; salinity problems in its reservoir have been widely debated
  • Sefidrud (Manjil) Dam — buttress dam on the Sefidrud River, completed 1962
  • Lar Dam, Latyan Dam, and Amir Kabir (Karaj) Dam — major suppliers of drinking water to Tehran
  • Zayanderud Dam — supplies Isfahan and regulates the Zayandeh River
  • Marun Dam — rock-fill dam in Khuzestan
  • Sivand Dam — controversial for its proximity to Pasargadae and ancient sites in Fars
  • Rudbar-e Lorestan, Dez-2, and Sardasht dams — among more recent additions
