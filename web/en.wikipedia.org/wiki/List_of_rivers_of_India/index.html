List of rivers of India

India's rivers fall into two great groups: the Himalayan rivers of the north — the Indus, Ganges and Brahmaputra systems, fed by snow and glaciers as well as monsoon rain — and the peninsular rivers, which are mainly rain-fed and seasonal. The Ganges is the most sacred river in Hinduism, and the Ganges–Brahmaputra delta is the largest river delta in the world.

Draining into the Bay of Bengal

  • Ganges — 2,525 km; tributaries include the Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Son, Chambal and Betwa
  • Brahmaputra — rises in Tibet as the Yarlung Tsangpo; enters India through Arunachal Pradesh and joins the Ganges in Bangladesh
  • Godavari — 1,465 km; the longest peninsular river, sometimes called the Dakshin Ganga
  • Krishna — 1,400 km; tributaries include the Bhima and Tungabhadra
  • Mahanadi — 858 km; site of the Hirakud Dam
  • Kaveri (Cauvery) — 800 km; rises at Talakaveri in Karnataka
  • Brahmani, Baitarani, Subarnarekha
  • Vaigai, Thamirabarani, Palar, Pennar
  • Meghna (via Bangladesh)

Draining into the Arabian Sea

  • Indus — 3,180 km total; flows through Ladakh before entering Pakistan; tributaries include the Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas and Sutlej (the "Punjab" rivers)
  • Narmada — 1,312 km; the largest west-flowing peninsular river, following a rift valley
  • Tapti (Tapi) — 724 km; also west-flowing through a rift valley
  • Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni (endorheic, ending in the Rann of Kutch)
  • Periyar, Bharathappuzha, Pamba, Mandovi, Zuari, Sharavati, Kali (Karnataka), Netravati
