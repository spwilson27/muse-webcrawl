Seanad Éireann

Seanad Éireann
Type: Upper house of the Oireachtas
Seats: 60
Meeting place: Leinster House, Dublin
Leader: Cathaoirleach (chair)

Seanad Éireann (the Senate of Ireland) is the upper house of the Oireachtas, the legislature of Ireland; the lower house is Dáil Éireann. It consists of 60 members known as senators. The Seanad meets at Leinster House in Dublin.

Composition

Eleven senators are nominated by the Taoiseach, six are elected by graduates of Irish universities, and forty-three are elected by an electorate of politicians from five vocational panels (culture and education, agriculture, labour, industry and commerce, and public administration). Elections take place within 90 days of a Dáil dissolution.

Powers

The Seanad's powers are weaker than the Dáil's: it can delay and recommend amendments to legislation but cannot permanently block bills, and money bills can only be delayed briefly. A 2013 referendum proposal to abolish the Seanad was rejected by the Irish electorate.
