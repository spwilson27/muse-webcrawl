Mandals in Andhra Pradesh

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A mandal is a local government area in the Indian states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, roughly equivalent to a tehsil or taluka elsewhere in India. Each district of Andhra Pradesh is divided into revenue divisions, which are in turn divided into mandals; each mandal is administered by a tahsildar (Mandal Revenue Officer) and comprises a group of villages and towns.

Structure

The mandal system was introduced in 1985 by the government of N. T. Rama Rao, replacing the older taluka system, in order to bring administration closer to the people. After the reorganisation of districts in 2022, Andhra Pradesh has 26 districts with a total of 679 mandals, grouped into 77 revenue divisions.

Functions

Mandals handle land records and revenue collection, registration of births and deaths, and implementation of welfare schemes at the local level. Parallel elected bodies, the Mandal Parishads, form the intermediate tier of the Panchayati Raj system.
