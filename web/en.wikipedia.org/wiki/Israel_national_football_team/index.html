Israel national football team

The Israel national football team represents Israel in men's international football and is governed by the Israel Football Association. It qualified for the FIFA World Cup once, in 1970, and won the AFC Asian Cup in 1964 when Israel competed in Asian competition.

AssociationIsrael Football Association (IFA)

History

A national side representing Mandatory Palestine played in the 1930s. After 1948 the Israel team played in the Asian Football Confederation, winning the 1964 Asian Cup on home soil and qualifying for the 1970 World Cup in Mexico, where Mordechai Spiegler scored Israel's only World Cup goal against Sweden.

Move to UEFA

Expelled from the AFC in 1974 amid political pressure, Israel spent years in various qualifying arrangements before becoming a full UEFA member in 1994. The team reached the Euro 2000 qualifying play-offs and has produced players such as Yossi Benayoun and Eran Zahavi, the national team's record scorer.
