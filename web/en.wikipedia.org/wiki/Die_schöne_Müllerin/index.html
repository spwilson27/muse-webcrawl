Die schöne Müllerin

Die schöne Müllerin ("The Fair Maid of the Mill"), Op. 25, D. 795, is a song cycle by Franz Schubert composed in 1823 to poems by Wilhelm Müller. It is one of the earliest extended song cycles and a cornerstone of the Lied repertoire.

Story

A young miller follows a brook to a mill, falls in love with the miller's daughter, and at first believes his love returned. A rival — a huntsman dressed in green — wins her favour, and the miller, despairing, drowns himself in the brook, which sings him a final lullaby. The twenty songs include "Das Wandern", "Wohin?", "Mein!", "Der Jäger", "Die liebe Farbe" and "Des Baches Wiegenlied".

Music

The brook is a unifying presence in the piano accompaniment, its flowing figures reflecting the protagonist's changing moods from hope to obsession and despair.
