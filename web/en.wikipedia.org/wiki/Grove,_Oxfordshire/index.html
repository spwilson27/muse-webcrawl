Grove, Oxfordshire

Grove
Village and civil parish in Oxfordshire, England
District: Vale of White Horse

Grove is a village and civil parish in the Vale of White Horse district of Oxfordshire, England, about 1.5 miles (2.4 km) north of Wantage and 13 miles (21 km) southwest of Oxford. Historically part of Berkshire until the boundary changes of 1974, Grove grew substantially in the later 20th century and is effectively a suburb of Wantage.

History

The village developed around a crossing of the Letcombe Brook. A former RAF airfield, RAF Grove, operated nearby during and after the Second World War. Grove is best known in motorsport as the home of the Williams Formula One team, whose factory and conference centre have been based in the village since 1996.

Amenities

Grove has a parish church dedicated to St James, primary schools, a library, pubs, and local shops. The village is served by the Wantage Community Hospital nearby and by road links to the A34 and the M4.
