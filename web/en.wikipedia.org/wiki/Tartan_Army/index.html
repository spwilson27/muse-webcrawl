Tartan Army

The Tartan Army is the nickname given to the fans of the Scotland national football team. Renowned worldwide for their colour, humour, and good-natured behaviour, they travel in large numbers wearing kilts, Scotland jerseys, and often Glengarry or tam o' shanter bonnets.

History and reputation

Although Scottish supporters in the 1970s had a mixed reputation, from the 1980s onward the Tartan Army deliberately rebuilt its image around carnival behaviour and charity, winning fair-play recognition from UEFA and from host cities at tournaments. Their mass descents — such as the famous trips to Wembley for the old British Home Championship and, more recently, around 200,000 fans in Germany for UEFA Euro 2024 — have become events in themselves.

Culture

Signature songs include "Flower of Scotland", "We Have a Dream" (1982), and "Yes Sir, I Can Boogie", adopted during the Euro 2020 campaign. The Tartan Army's supporters' associations also raise substantial sums for children's charities in Scotland and in countries they visit.
