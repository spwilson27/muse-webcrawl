Pitch (sports field)

A pitch or a sports ground is an outdoor playing area for various sports. The term pitch is most commonly used in British English, while the comparable term in American and Canadian English is playing field or sports field. For most sports, the official term is field of play.

A football pitch, with the markings prescribed by the Laws of the Game

The word "pitch" also carries sport-specific meanings. In cricket, the pitch is the central prepared strip — 22 yards (20.12 m) long and 10 feet (3.05 m) wide — between the two sets of stumps, on which the ball is bowled. In association football, "pitch" refers to the whole playing area defined by the Laws of the Game, whose dimensions for international matches are 105 × 68 metres.

Surfaces

Pitches are usually made of natural grass, maintained by groundskeepers, though many sports also permit artificial turf. Surface quality influences the style and safety of play; cricket pitches in particular vary in pace and bounce according to their preparation and climate, an important tactical factor in matches. Modern professional pitches may include undersoil heating and hybrid surfaces combining natural grass with synthetic fibres.

Usage in different sports

  • Association football — the whole playing area is the pitch.
  • Cricket — the pitch is the central strip only, not the entire ground.
  • Rugby — the field of play is commonly called the pitch in British usage.
  • Field hockey — officially called a pitch, with prescribed dimensions of 91.4 × 55 m.
