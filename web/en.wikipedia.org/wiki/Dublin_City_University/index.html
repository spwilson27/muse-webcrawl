Dublin City University

Dublin City University (DCU; Irish: Ollscoil Chathair Bhaile Átha Cliath) is a public university based on the Northside of Dublin, Ireland. Created as the National Institute for Higher Education, Dublin in 1975, it enrolled its first students in 1980 and was elevated to university status in September 1989 by statute. DCU is known for its focus on enterprise, innovation, and work placement (its INTRA programme), and it regularly ranks among the world's top young universities.

History

The institution was founded in 1975 as a national institute modelled partly on technological universities abroad, offering degrees validated by the National Council for Educational Awards before gaining its own degree-awarding authority as a university in 1989. Its first president was Danny O'Hare. In 2016 DCU expanded substantially through the "incorporation" of St Patrick's College, Drumcondra, the Mater Dei Institute of Education, and the Church of Ireland College of Education, creating one of Ireland's largest faculties of education, the DCU Institute of Education.

Campuses

The main Glasnevin campus covers about 50 acres (20 hectares) between Glasnevin, Ballymun, and Whitehall, about 5 km from the city centre. The incorporation added the St Patrick's campus in Drumcondra and the All Hallows campus. Facilities include The Helix performing arts centre, opened in 2002, a major sports complex, and the university's research centres.

Academics

DCU is organised into five faculties: the DCU Business School; the Faculty of Engineering and Computing; the Faculty of Science and Health; the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences; and the DCU Institute of Education. It has roughly 17,000–19,000 students. DCU pioneered Ireland's first degree in several applied fields and was an early adopter of interdisciplinary programmes; its business school holds international accreditations including AACSB. The university is also known for widening access: its Access programme supports students from disadvantaged backgrounds at rates above national norms.
