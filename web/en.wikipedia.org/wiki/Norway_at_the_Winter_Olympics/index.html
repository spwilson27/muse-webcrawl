Norway at the Winter Olympics

Norway has competed at every edition of the Winter Olympic Games since the inaugural Games at Chamonix in 1924, and is the most successful nation in Winter Olympic history, topping the all-time medal table.

Dominance in Nordic sports

Norwegian success is concentrated in cross-country skiing, biathlon, ski jumping, Nordic combined and speed skating. Cross-country skiers such as Bjørn Dæhlie, Marit Bjørgen and Johannes Høsflot Klæbo rank among the most decorated Winter Olympians of all time, while biathlete Ole Einar Bjørndalen won thirteen Olympic medals.

Hosting

Norway has hosted the Winter Games twice: the 1952 Games in Oslo and the 1994 Games in Lillehammer, the latter widely praised and delivered by a small town two years after Norway's previous bid efforts matured. Norway also hosted the 2016 Winter Youth Olympics in Lillehammer.

Records

At the 2018 Pyeongchang Games Norway won 39 medals, then a record for a single Winter Games, and at Beijing 2022 it set a new record for gold medals at one Winter Olympics with sixteen.
