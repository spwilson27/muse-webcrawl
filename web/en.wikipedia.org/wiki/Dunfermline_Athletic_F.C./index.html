Dunfermline Athletic Football Club is a Scottish professional football club based in the city of Dunfermline, Fife. Founded in 1885, the club play their home matches at East End Park. Their nickname is "the Pars", a name whose origins are the subject of several competing theories.

The club's most successful period came in the 1960s under manager Jock Stein and his successor George Farm, when Dunfermline regularly competed in European competition and won the Scottish Cup twice, in 1961 and 1968. The 1961 triumph came under Stein, who left to manage Hibernian and then Celtic; the 1968 victory, a 3–1 win over Heart of Midlothian at Hampden Park, remains the club's most recent major honour.

History

Dunfermline Athletic was formed on 2 June 1885 when members of Dunfermline Cricket Club decided to form a football section. The club joined the Scottish Football League in 1912 and won promotion to the top flight for the first time in 1926.

After decades of moving between divisions, the club entered its golden era in the 1960s. Under Jock Stein, appointed in 1960, Dunfermline won the Scottish Cup in 1961, beating Celtic 2–0 in a replay, and reached the quarter-finals of the European Cup Winners' Cup. Under George Farm the club finished third in the league in 1968–69 — its highest ever position — won the Scottish Cup again in 1968 and reached the semi-finals of the Cup Winners' Cup in 1969, losing to eventual winners Slovan Bratislava.

The following decades brought financial difficulty and spells in the lower divisions, interspersed with top-flight returns under managers such as Jim Leishman and Jimmy Calderwood, the latter guiding the club to the 2004 Scottish Cup Final. The club entered administration in 2013 and was relegated, but was rescued by the supporters' group Pars United and returned to the second tier, winning League One titles in 2016 and 2023.

Honours

  • Scottish Cup: Winners 1960–61, 1967–68
  • Scottish League One / Second Division: Champions on several occasions, most recently 2022–23
  • Scottish Challenge Cup: Runners-up 2007–08

Rivalries

Dunfermline's fiercest rivalry is the Fife derby with Raith Rovers of Kirkcaldy, with additional local rivalries against Cowdenbeath and Falkirk.
