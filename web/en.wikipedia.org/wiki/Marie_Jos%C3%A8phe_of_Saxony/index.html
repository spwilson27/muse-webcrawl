Marie Josèphe of Saxony (1731–1767) was Dauphine of France as the second wife of Louis Ferdinand, Dauphin of France, and the mother of three kings of France: Louis XVI, Louis XVIII and Charles X.

Marriage

Daughter of Augustus III of Poland and Maria Josepha of Austria, she married the widowed Dauphin in 1747, sealing ties between France and Saxony. She gradually won over her initially reluctant husband, and the marriage became a close one.

Family and court

The couple had eight children. Devout and reserved, Marie Josèphe was close to her father-in-law Louis XV and maintained ties with her Saxon family, correspondences that historians value as sources on the court.

Death

Her health broke down after her husband's death from tuberculosis in 1765; she died of the same disease at Versailles in 1767, never becoming queen.
