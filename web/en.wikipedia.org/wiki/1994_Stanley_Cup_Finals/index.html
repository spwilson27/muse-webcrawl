1994 Stanley Cup Finals

TeamsNew York Rangers vs Vancouver Canucks
Dates31 May – 14 June 1994
ResultRangers won 4–3
MVPBrian Leetch

The 1994 Stanley Cup Finals was the championship series of the National Hockey League's 1993–94 season, contested between the New York Rangers and the Vancouver Canucks. The Rangers won the series in seven games, ending their 54-year Stanley Cup drought dating back to 1940.

The series

The Rangers, led by captain Mark Messier and coach Mike Keenan, won the Presidents' Trophy with the league's best record. Vancouver, the seventh seed in the West, pushed the series to a deciding game in Madison Square Garden, which New York won 3–2.

Legacy

  • Brian Leetch became the first American-born player to win the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP.
  • The phrase "The Curse" — the Rangers' 54-year wait — entered hockey lore as it was lifted.
