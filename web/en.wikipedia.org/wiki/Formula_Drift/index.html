Formula Drift (officially the Formula Drift Black Magic Pro Championship) is an American professional drifting series founded in 2003 by Jim Liaw and Ryan Sage, the organisers of the earlier D1 Grand Prix exhibitions in the United States. Its first full season ran in 2004.

Format

Events are held on closed courses at race circuits and temporary street venues. Drivers first make judged solo qualifying runs, scored on line, angle, speed and style. The top qualifiers then compete in tandem elimination battles, in which a lead car sets the course and a chase car attempts to mirror it while staying close; judges award the win or call for "One More Time" re-runs.

Champions

Multiple champions include Chris Forsberg, the series' first three-time champion, Vaughn Gittin Jr., Fredric Aasbø and James Deane, who won three consecutive titles in 2017–2019. The series also operates the Prospec (formerly Pro 2) feeder category for up-and-coming drivers.
