John Ross "J.R." Ewing Jr. is a fictional character in the American television series Dallas and its 2012 continuation series. The character was created by David Jacobs and portrayed by Larry Hagman from the show's premiere in 1978 until Hagman's death in 2012.

The eldest son of Jock and Miss Ellie Ewing, J.R. is the president of Ewing Oil, the family petroleum company based in Dallas, Texas, and lives at the family's Southfork Ranch. Ruthless, scheming, and charismatic, he is one of the most famous villains in television history, frequently ranked among the greatest TV characters of all time.

"Who shot J.R.?"

In the third-season finale, broadcast on March 21, 1980, J.R. was shot by an unseen assailant. The mystery of the shooter's identity became an international cultural phenomenon over the following summer, with "Who shot J.R.?" appearing on T-shirts, in betting markets, and in magazine covers. The resolution episode, "Who Done It", aired on November 21, 1980 and revealed Kristin Shepard, J.R.'s sister-in-law and mistress, as the shooter; it was watched by an estimated 83 million viewers in the United States, among the highest-rated television broadcasts in history.

Character

J.R.'s schemes characteristically involved manipulating his younger brother Bobby, his wife Sue Ellen, and business rivals such as Cliff Barnes. Despite his villainy, the character was written with wit and occasional vulnerability, and Hagman received two Emmy Award nominations and four Golden Globe nominations for the role, winning a Golden Globe in 1984.

In the 2012 revival, the character returned as an aging but still formidable schemer. After Hagman's death from cancer in November 2012, the series wrote J.R.'s death into its second season, staging his funeral in the episode "J.R.'s Masterpiece" and revealing that he had orchestrated his own murder as part of a final master plan against his enemies.
