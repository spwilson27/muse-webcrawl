FIFA Marta Award

The FIFA Marta Award is an association football award presented by FIFA to the player deemed to have scored the best goal in women's football during the eligibility period. It was created in 2024 as the women's counterpart to the FIFA Puskás Award, which thereafter became the equivalent prize for men's football, and is named in honour of the Brazilian forward Marta, widely regarded as one of the greatest women's players of all time.

History

The award was announced ahead of The Best FIFA Football Awards 2024. In a fitting inaugural result, the first winner was Marta herself, honoured for her goal for Brazil against Jamaica in a friendly on 1 June 2024 — striking the ball into the top corner from outside the area. The winning goal is chosen by a combination of fan voting and a panel of FIFA experts.
