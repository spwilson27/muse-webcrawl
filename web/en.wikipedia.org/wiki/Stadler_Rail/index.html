Stadler Rail

Stadler Rail AG is a Swiss manufacturer of railway rolling stock, headquartered in Bussnang, canton Thurgau. Founded in 1942 as an engineering office by Ernst Stadler, it has grown into one of the world's major builders of multiple-unit trains, locomotives, trams, metro trains and rack railway vehicles, with factories in Switzerland, Germany, Poland, Hungary, Spain, the United States and elsewhere.

History

Ernst Stadler established the company in Zurich in 1942 and moved to Bussnang in 1962. After his death, his widow led the firm until 1989, when Peter Spuhler — who would become its long-serving CEO and chairman — bought it with ten employees. Under Spuhler the company expanded rapidly through modular train concepts and acquisitions, including the Berlin works of Adtranz and the Spanish manufacturer Vossloh España. Stadler listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange in 2019.

Products

Best-known product families include the FLIRT (Fast Light Intercity and Regional Train) electric and bi-mode multiple units, of which well over two thousand have been sold; the GTW articulated railcar; the Regio-Shuttle; the double-deck KISS; the Tango and Tramlink trams; and the Eurodual and Euro9000 hybrid freight locomotives. Stadler is also the world's leading builder of rack railway rolling stock and supplies bespoke trains for mountain lines such as the Jungfrau Railway. Its trains operate in more than 45 countries, including Greater Anglia services in Britain and Caltrain in California.
