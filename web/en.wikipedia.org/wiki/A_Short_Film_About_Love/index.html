A Short Film About Love
Polish: Krótki film o miłości
Director: Krzysztof Kieślowski
Release: 1988

A Short Film About Love (Polish: Krótki film o miłości) is a 1988 Polish romantic drama film directed by Krzysztof Kieślowski, an expanded version of Dekalog: Six, based on the commandment "Thou shalt not commit adultery."

Plot

Teenage postal clerk Tomek spies through a telescope on artist Magda across a Warsaw housing block. Their confrontation turns obsession into a painful, inverted lesson about love and voyeurism, with an ending different from the television version.
