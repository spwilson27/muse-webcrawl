List of waterfalls

A waterfall is a point in a river or stream where water flows over a vertical drop or a series of steep drops. This list presents notable waterfalls of the world, organized by continent. The tallest waterfall in the world is Angel Falls in Venezuela, with an uninterrupted plunge of 807 metres (2,648 ft) and a total height of 979 metres (3,212 ft).

By height

  • Angel Falls, Venezuela — 979 m total height
  • Tugela Falls, South Africa — 983 m (measurement disputed)
  • Tres Hermanas Falls, Peru — 914 m
  • Olo'upena Falls, Hawaii, United States — 900 m
  • Yumbilla Falls, Peru — 896 m

By flow rate

The waterfalls with the greatest average flow include Boyoma Falls in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Khone Phapheng Falls in Laos, and Inga Falls on the Congo River. Niagara Falls, on the border of Canada and the United States, combines great width and flow and is among the most famous waterfalls in the world. Victoria Falls on the Zambezi is the largest sheet of falling water in the world.

Types

Waterfalls are classified by form, including plunge, horsetail, cataract, multi-step, block, cascade, segmented, tiered, fan and punchbowl falls.
