NATO phonetic alphabet

The NATO phonetic alphabet is a spelling alphabet used to ensure clear voice communication, especially by radio. Its code words are: Alfa, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, Foxtrot, Golf, Hotel, India, Juliett, Kilo, Lima, Mike, November, Oscar, Papa, Quebec, Romeo, Sierra, Tango, Uniform, Victor, Whiskey, X-ray, Yankee, Zulu.

Adopted in the 1950s and also known as the ICAO phonetic alphabet, it is used by militaries, civil aviation, emergency services and businesses worldwide. Digits also have prescribed pronunciations, such as "tree", "fower" and "niner".

History

It replaced earlier national alphabets after international testing selected words recognisable and pronounceable across languages.
