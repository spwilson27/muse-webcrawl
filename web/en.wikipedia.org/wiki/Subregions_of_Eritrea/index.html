Subregions of Eritrea

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The subregions of Eritrea are the second-level administrative divisions of the country, below the six regions (zobas). Eritrea is divided into 58 subregions (districts). Each region is administered by a governor, and each subregion by a local administrator. The current structure dates from a 1996 reorganisation, which replaced the provinces inherited from the Ethiopian administration period with the present regions and subregions.

The Maekel region, the smallest but most populous, contains the capital Asmara, whose neighbourhoods are administered as districts within the region. Gash-Barka, in the west, is the largest region by area and contains the greatest number of subregions. The Dahlak subregion in the Northern Red Sea region administers the Dahlak Archipelago in the Red Sea.
