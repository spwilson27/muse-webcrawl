National Housing Federation

The National Housing Federation (NHF) is the trade association for housing associations in England. Its members — more than 800 housing associations — own and manage around 2.7 million homes, providing housing for about six million people.

Role

The federation represents housing associations to government, campaigns on housing policy, and publishes research on housing need, affordability and building safety. It has campaigned prominently on the UK housing crisis and for increased funding for social and affordable housing.

History

The organisation traces its origins to 1935 as the National Federation of Housing Societies, becoming the National Housing Federation as the housing association sector grew in the later twentieth century.
