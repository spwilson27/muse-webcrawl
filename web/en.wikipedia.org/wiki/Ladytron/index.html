Ladytron

Ladytron
Origin: Liverpool, England
Genres: Electropop, synth-pop, electronic
Years active: 1999–present
Members: Helen Marnie, Mira Aroyo, Daniel Hunt, Reuben Wu

Ladytron are an English electronic band formed in Liverpool in 1999. The group consists of Helen Marnie (lead vocals, synthesisers), Mira Aroyo (vocals, synthesisers), Daniel Hunt (synthesisers, guitar, vocals) and Reuben Wu (synthesisers). Their name is taken from the 1972 song "Ladytron" by Roxy Music.

History

Producers and DJs Daniel Hunt and Reuben Wu met in Liverpool in the 1990s and recruited Bulgarian-born Mira Aroyo and Scottish singer Helen Marnie. Their debut single "He Took Her to a Movie" appeared in 1999. Their first album, 604 (2001), included the singles "Playgirl" and "The Way That I Found You". Light & Magic (2002) produced the international club hits "Seventeen", "Blue Jeans" and "Evil".

Their third album, Witching Hour (2005), featuring "Destroy Everything You Touch" and "Sugar", was widely acclaimed and expanded their audience. It was followed by Velocifero (2008), recorded in Paris, and Gravity the Seducer (2011). After a hiatus during which members pursued solo projects — Marnie released solo work as Marnie — the band returned with the self-titled album Ladytron in 2019 and Time's Arrow in 2023.

Style

Ladytron's sound combines vintage analogue synthesisers, drum machines and icy, detached vocals sung in English and, by Aroyo, in Bulgarian. They have been described as electropop and synth-pop, and were associated with the electroclash movement, a label the band resisted. Their aesthetic emphasises uniformity and retro-futurist design.
