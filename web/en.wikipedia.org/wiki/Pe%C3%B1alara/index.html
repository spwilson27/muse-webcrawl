Peñalara

Peñalara is the highest mountain in the Sierra de Guadarrama, a mountain range in the Sistema Central, in central Spain. Rising to 2,428 metres (7,966 ft) above sea level, it is also the highest point of both the Community of Madrid and the province of Segovia, whose border runs across its summit.

Geography

The peak stands on the boundary between the autonomous community of Madrid to the south and the province of Segovia in Castile and León to the north. The surrounding terrain forms the Peñalara Natural Park, an area protecting glacial cirques, small high-altitude lagoons — most famously the Laguna Grande de Peñalara — and pine forests below the treeline. The massif was shaped by Pleistocene glaciation, leaving moraines and a chain of cirques on its northern and eastern flanks.

Access and recreation

Peñalara is a popular destination for hikers and mountaineers from Madrid. The most common starting point is Puerto de Cotos, a mountain pass served by road and by the C-9 commuter rail line from Cercedilla. From there a well-marked trail climbs past the visitor centre and the Laguna Grande to the summit ridge. In winter the peak is used for ski mountaineering and ice climbing, with routes on the north-facing cirque walls.
