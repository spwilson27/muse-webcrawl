Q1 Tower (short for Queensland Number One) is a supertall residential skyscraper in Surfers Paradise on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. Completed in November 2005, it rises 322.5 metres to the tip of its spire and was, at its completion, the tallest residential building in the world, a title it held until surpassed by The Marina Torch in Dubai in 2011. It remains the tallest building in Australia when measured to its spire, and the tallest in the Southern Hemisphere by that measure.

Design and construction

The tower's design, by Sunland Group's architectural team, was inspired by the Sydney 2000 Olympic torch and the Sydney Opera House. Its elliptical plan wraps around a central core, and a dramatic spire rises from the roof, illuminated at night and visible from far along the coast. The building contains 526 apartments over 78 floors, serviced by some of the fastest lifts in Australia. Construction began in 2002 and required deep foundations into the sandy coastal soil.

Observation deck

Levels 77 and 78 house the SkyPoint Observation Deck (formerly QDeck), offering panoramic views of the coast and hinterland, and the SkyPoint Climb, an external building climb opened in 2012. The deck sits at about 230 metres above ground, the only beachside observation deck in Australia.
