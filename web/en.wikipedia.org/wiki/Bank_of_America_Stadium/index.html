Bank of America Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium in Uptown Charlotte, North Carolina, United States. It is the home of the Carolina Panthers of the National Football League and, since 2022, of Charlotte FC of Major League Soccer. The stadium opened for the 1996 NFL season as Ericsson Stadium and took its current name in 2004 under a naming-rights agreement with Bank of America, whose headquarters are in Charlotte.

Design and history

The stadium was designed by HOK Sport (now Populous) and financed principally by the Richardson family through the then-novel sale of Permanent Seat Licenses, a scheme that helped Charlotte win the NFL expansion franchise awarded in 1993. Two bronze panther statues — among the largest cast bronzes in the United States — flank the main entrance. The playing surface was natural grass for most of the stadium's life before a switch to artificial turf in 2021 to accommodate both tenants and heavy event use.

Events

Beyond Panthers football, the stadium hosts the Duke's Mayo Bowl college football game, the ACC Championship Game, international soccer matches including CONCACAF Gold Cup fixtures, and major concerts. Major renovations in the 2010s and 2020s added escalators, ribbon boards, upgraded clubs, and fan plazas as part of publicly supported agreements between the team and the City of Charlotte.
