List of largest stars

The following is a list of the largest stars known, ordered by radius. The unit of measurement is the solar radius (R☉ ≈ 695,700 km). The largest stars are cool red supergiants and hypergiants—highly evolved, massive stars whose outer layers have expanded enormously. Their measured radii carry large uncertainties because their "surfaces" are ill-defined, their sizes vary with wavelength and pulsation, and their distances are often imprecise.

Figures vary considerably between studies; more recent interferometric and Gaia-based work has often revised radii downward, and rankings of "the largest star" change accordingly.

Measurement challenges

Stellar radii are determined from measured angular diameters combined with distances, or from luminosity and effective temperature via the Stefan–Boltzmann law. For red supergiants both routes are problematic: extended atmospheres and circumstellar dust blur the photosphere, distances to galactic stars were historically uncertain (improved by the Gaia mission), and luminosity-based radii depend on adopted temperatures and bolometric corrections. Massive stars near the end of their lives also pulsate and lose mass prodigiously, so a single star can change size appreciably.
