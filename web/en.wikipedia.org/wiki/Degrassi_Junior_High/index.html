Degrassi Junior High

Degrassi Junior High is a Canadian teen drama television series created by Linda Schuyler and Kit Hood. It aired on CBC Television from 1987 to 1989, running for three seasons and 42 episodes. The second series in the Degrassi franchise, it followed the lives of students at the fictional Degrassi Junior High School in Toronto and won international acclaim for its realistic depiction of adolescence.

Premise and production

The series used a large ensemble of non-professional teenage actors of the same age as their characters, many recruited from the earlier Kids of Degrassi Street series and from local schools. Episodes interwove everyday school life with serious topics rarely addressed in youth television of the period, including teenage pregnancy (the character Spike, played by Amanda Stepto), eating disorders, shoplifting, child abuse, homosexuality, drug use and suicide.

It was produced by Playing With Time with WGBH Boston and filmed at a former school building in Toronto's east end. Writers included Yan Moore and Susin Nielsen. The show's theme song, "Everybody Wants Something", was performed by the fictional band The Zit Remedy, formed by characters Joey Jeremiah, Snake Simpson and Wheels.

Reception and legacy

Degrassi Junior High won two International Emmy Awards and numerous Gemini Awards, and was broadcast in the United States on PBS and in many other countries. It was followed in 1989 by Degrassi High, in which most characters moved on to high school, and characters and actors from the series later appeared in Degrassi: The Next Generation. The series is frequently discussed in studies of educational and issue-based television.
