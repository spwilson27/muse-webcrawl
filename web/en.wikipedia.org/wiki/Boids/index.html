Boids

Boids is an artificial life program, developed by Craig Reynolds in 1986, which simulates the flocking behaviour of birds, and was also extended to simulate fish schools. It demonstrates emergence: complex flocking arises from agents following three simple local rules.

Model details

Each boid steers according to: separation (steer to avoid crowding local flockmates), alignment (steer towards the average heading of local flockmates) and cohesion (steer to move toward the average position of local flockmates). Additional rules can model obstacle avoidance and goal seeking.

Applications

Boids underpinned the bat swarms in Batman Returns (1992), the first major film use of flocking simulation, and the stampede in The Lion King (1994). The model is widely used in computer graphics and swarm robotics research.
