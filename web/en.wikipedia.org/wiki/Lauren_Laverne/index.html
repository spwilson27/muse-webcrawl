Lauren Laverne

Lauren Cecilia Fisher (born 28 April 1978), known professionally as Lauren Laverne, is an English radio DJ, model, television presenter, author, and singer. She was the lead singer of the alternative rock band Kenickie and went on to become one of the BBC's best-known music broadcasters, presenting on BBC Radio 6 Music and, from 2018, Desert Island Discs on BBC Radio 4.

Early career and Kenickie

Laverne was born in Sunderland. As a teenager she formed the band Kenickie with friends; the band achieved UK chart success in the mid-1990s Britpop era with singles such as "In Your Car", and appeared on Top of the Pops before splitting in 1998.

Broadcasting

Laverne moved into television with Channel 4 and later presented The Culture Show on BBC Two, coverage of Glastonbury Festival, and the Mercury Prize. On radio she hosted the XFM breakfast show before joining BBC Radio 6 Music in 2008, where her morning show became a flagship programme. In 2018 she succeeded Kirsty Young as host of Desert Island Discs, one of Radio 4's longest-running programmes. She has also written a novel, Candlemoth (2010), and co-founded the women's online magazine The Pool.
