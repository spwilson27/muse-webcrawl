Falmer Stadium

Falmer Stadium, known for sponsorship purposes as the American Express Stadium and commonly as the Amex, is a football stadium in the village of Falmer, near Brighton and Hove, England. It is the home ground of Premier League club Brighton & Hove Albion.

LocationFalmer, Brighton and Hove, England
Capacity31,876
Opened2011
TenantsBrighton & Hove Albion

History

Brighton had been without a permanent home since the sale of the Goldstone Ground in 1997, ground-sharing at Gillingham and then playing at the Withdean Stadium while campaigning for a new ground. Planning approval for Falmer was granted in 2007 after a long public inquiry, and the stadium opened in July 2011.

Events

Besides Brighton's matches, the stadium hosted matches at the 2015 Rugby World Cup — including Japan's famous upset victory over South Africa — and games at UEFA Women's Euro 2022. It is served by Falmer railway station on the East Coastway line.
