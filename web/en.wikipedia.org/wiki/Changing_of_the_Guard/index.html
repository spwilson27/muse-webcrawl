Changing of the Guard

Changing of the Guard is a formal ceremony in which sentries guarding a royal or official residence are relieved by a new guard. The most famous takes place at Buckingham Palace, London, where the King's Guard hands over responsibility accompanied by a military band.

Ceremonies worldwide

Similar ceremonies occur at Windsor Castle, the Tower of London, Horse Guards, and in many other countries – at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier in Athens and Arlington, Rashtrapati Bhavan in Delhi, and elsewhere – often becoming major tourist attractions.
