The Mother 3 fan translation is an English localisation of the 2006 Nintendo Game Boy Advance role-playing game Mother 3, produced by members of the EarthBound fan community Starmen.Net and released on October 17, 2008. Because Nintendo never released Mother 3 outside Japan, the project became the only way for most English-speaking players to experience the game, and it is widely regarded as a landmark of fan translation.

Background

Mother 3, the third entry in Shigesato Itoi's Mother series, was released in Japan in April 2006 to strong acclaim. Year after year, Nintendo of America gave no indication of an official localisation, and the game's predecessor, EarthBound, had sold poorly on its original release despite a devoted fandom. In November 2006 the Starmen.Net community formally announced its own translation project, led by professional translator Clyde Mandelin ("Tomato"), who had previously translated games including Kingdom Hearts II drafts for fansites and worked in the commercial localisation industry.

Development

The project took roughly two years and involved extensive ROM hacking: the team rewrote text-rendering code to support variable-width fonts, translated nearly a million Japanese characters of script, translated the game's hundred-plus enemy names full of puns, produced a translated handbook, and released the result as a free patch to be applied to a Japanese ROM. The translation was praised for capturing Itoi's tone and humour. Version 1.3 and later updates fixed bugs and small errors; the team has stated the patch reached 100,000 downloads in its first week.

Reception

Gaming press covered the release unusually extensively for a fan work, with outlets noting Nintendo's silence. The project is frequently cited in discussions of game preservation, the legitimacy of fan translation, and fan-driven demand; the community's campaigns around the Mother series are also credited with influencing Nintendo's eventual Virtual Console releases of EarthBound and, in 2015, of the first Mother game as EarthBound Beginnings.
