Coldplay are a British rock band formed in London in 1997 (initially in 1996 as Starfish / Pectoralz by university friends). Vocalist and pianist Chris Martin, guitarist Jonny Buckland, bassist Guy Berryman and drummer Will Champion met at University College London. They are among the best-selling music acts of all time.

History

Their debut album Parachutes (2000), featuring "Yellow", won the Grammy for Best Alternative Album. A Rush of Blood to the Head (2002) included "In My Place", "The Scientist" and "Clocks". X&Y (2005) was then the best-selling album worldwide that year. Viva la Vida or Death and All His Friends (2008), produced with Brian Eno, gave them a UK and US number-one single with "Viva la Vida".

Later albums include Mylo Xyloto (2011), Ghost Stories (2014), A Head Full of Dreams (2015), Everyday Life (2019), Music of the Spheres (2021) — featuring the BTS collaboration "My Universe" — and Moon Music (2024). Their stadium tours, notably the Music of the Spheres World Tour, became among the highest-grossing in history.

Style and activism

The band are known for melodic piano-led alternative rock expanding into electronic and pop, colourful live shows with LED wristbands, and support for charities including Oxfam and environmental causes, including efforts to make touring more sustainable.
