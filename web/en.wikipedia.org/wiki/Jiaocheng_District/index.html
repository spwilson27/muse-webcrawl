Jiaocheng District

Jiaocheng District (蕉城区)
City: Ningde, Fujian, China
Area: about 1,500 km²
Population: roughly 600,000 (2020 census)

Jiaocheng District (Chinese: 蕉城区; pinyin: Jiāochéng Qū) is a district of the prefecture-level city of Ningde in north-eastern Fujian province, China. It forms the urban core of Ningde, facing the East China Sea on the Sandu Bay (Sandu'ao) inlet, an important natural harbour and aquaculture area.

Administration and economy

The district administers the city centre of Ningde and surrounding towns and townships, including Zhangwan and Qidu. Sandu Bay is known for large-scale marine farming of yellow croaker and other seafood, and the district hosts port facilities and, in the wider Ningde area, major battery and new-energy industries. The name Jiaocheng derives from the banana (蕉, jiao) and the old city walls.

Transport

Jiaocheng is served by the Wenzhou–Fuzhou railway through Ningde station and by coastal expressways linking Fujian's north-eastern coast.
