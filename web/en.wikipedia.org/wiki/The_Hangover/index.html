The Hangover

The Hangover is a 2009 American comedy film directed by Todd Phillips, co-written by Phillips with Jon Lucas and Scott Moore, and starring Bradley Cooper, Ed Helms, Zach Galifianakis, Heather Graham, Justin Bartha, Ken Jeong, and Jeffrey Tambor. It tells the story of a bachelor party in Las Vegas that goes disastrously wrong.

Plot

Two days before his wedding, Doug Billings travels to Las Vegas with his friends Phil and Stu and his eccentric future brother-in-law Alan for a bachelor party in a suite at Caesars Palace. The next morning Phil, Stu, and Alan wake with no memory of the previous night; Doug is missing, Stu has lost a tooth, there is a tiger in the bathroom, a baby in the closet, and a naked man, the gangster Leslie Chow, locked in the trunk of their car. Retracing their steps — including a quickie wedding for Stu, a stolen police car, a fortune won at blackjack counting cards, and Mike Tyson's stolen tiger — they recover Doug from the hotel roof, where Alan had spiked the group's drinks with Rohypnol, and make it back in time for the wedding.

Production

Principal photography took place in Las Vegas and Southern California over fifteen days of shooting on location at Caesars Palace. The plot element of the missing groom was loosely inspired by a real incident involving a friend of executive producer Chris Bender.

Reception and legacy

The film was a critical and enormous commercial success, becoming the highest-grossing R-rated comedy of its era and winning the Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy. It launched Galifianakis and Cooper as leading men and spawned two sequels, The Hangover Part II (2011) and The Hangover Part III (2013).
