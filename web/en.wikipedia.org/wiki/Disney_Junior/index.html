Disney Junior is an American pay television network and programming brand owned by the Walt Disney Company, aimed at children aged two to seven. It began as a programming block on Disney Channel on 14 February 2011, replacing Playhouse Disney, and launched as a standalone 24-hour channel in the United States on 23 March 2012, taking over the channel space of Soapnet.

Programming

Disney Junior's schedule mixes classic Disney characters with educational preschool series. Notable programmes include Mickey Mouse Clubhouse (inherited from Playhouse Disney), Jake and the Never Land Pirates, Sofia the First, Doc McStuffins, The Lion Guard, Puppy Dog Pals, Vampirina, Fancy Nancy, Mira, Royal Detective, Spidey and His Amazing Friends and Bluey (in the United States). Programming is designed to emphasise early maths, language, healthy habits and social skills.

International

Disney Junior channels and blocks were rolled out across Latin America, Europe, Asia and Australia. From 2020 onward, many international Disney Junior channels closed as part of Disney's shift toward Disney+, although the brand continues as a block and as a content category on the streaming service, and the US channel remains on air.
