Cherokee, North Carolina

Cherokee is a census-designated place and the principal community of the Qualla Boundary, the tribal land trust of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians, in western North Carolina. It lies in the Oconaluftee River valley at the entrance to Great Smoky Mountains National Park.

Culture and attractions

Cherokee is a centre of Cherokee culture, home to the Museum of the Cherokee People, the Oconaluftee Indian Village, and the outdoor drama "Unto These Hills". Harrah's Cherokee Casino Resort is a major employer and tourist draw.

Geography

The community sits at the junction of U.S. Routes 19 and 441, near the southern terminus of the Blue Ridge Parkway.
