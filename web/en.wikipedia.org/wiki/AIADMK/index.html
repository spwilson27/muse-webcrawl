All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam

AIADMK
Founded: 17 October 1972
Founder: M. G. Ramachandran
Headquarters: Royapettah, Chennai, Tamil Nadu
Ideology: Tamil nationalism, Dravidian politics, populism

The All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK) is an Indian regional political party with influence in the state of Tamil Nadu and the union territory of Puducherry. It is one of the two major Dravidian parties in Tamil Nadu, alongside the DMK.

History

The party was founded in 1972 by the film star M. G. Ramachandran (MGR) after he broke away from the DMK. Named after DMK founder C. N. Annadurai, the AIADMK first won power in Tamil Nadu in 1977, and MGR remained Chief Minister until his death in 1987.

Later leadership

After a succession struggle, J. Jayalalithaa emerged as leader and served multiple terms as Chief Minister, introducing extensive welfare schemes. Following her death in 2016, the party went through factional disputes before reuniting. Its election symbol is two leaves.
