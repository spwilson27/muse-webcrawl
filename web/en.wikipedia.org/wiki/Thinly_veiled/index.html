Thinly veiled

[Hero image placeholder: illustration of a roman à clef cover, suggesting real people behind barely disguised characters.]

Thinly veiled is an idiom describing something that is only slightly or superficially disguised. A "thinly veiled threat", for instance, is one whose meaning is easy to see through; a "thinly veiled reference" alludes to a person or event while pretending not to name them.

In literature and media

The term is most often used of fiction based on real people and events — the characters in a roman à clef are frequently described as thinly veiled versions of their real-life counterparts, with names and minor details changed. The device offers authors a degree of legal or social cover while letting readers recognise the originals. Thinly veiled portrayals have figured in literary disputes and defamation claims, since courts may treat an identifiable portrayal as a statement about the real person.
