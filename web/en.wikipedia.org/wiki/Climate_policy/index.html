Climate policy

ScopeMitigation and adaptation
Key instrumentsCarbon pricing, regulation, subsidies

Climate policy comprises the decisions and actions taken by governments and other actors to address climate change, including both mitigation (reducing greenhouse gas emissions) and adaptation to its impacts.

Instruments

Common policy instruments include carbon taxes, emissions trading systems, performance standards, subsidies for clean energy, and land-use regulation. International policy is coordinated largely through the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and agreements such as the Kyoto Protocol and the Paris Agreement.

Levels of governance

Climate policy is made at international, national, regional and municipal levels. Nationally determined contributions under the Paris Agreement set out each country's planned emissions reductions.
