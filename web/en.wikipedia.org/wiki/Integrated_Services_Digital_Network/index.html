Integrated Services Digital Network

Integrated Services Digital Network (ISDN) is a set of communication standards for the simultaneous digital transmission of voice, video, data and other network services over the traditional circuits of the public switched telephone network.

Interfaces

The Basic Rate Interface (BRI) provides two 64 kbit/s bearer (B) channels and one 16 kbit/s signalling (D) channel, marketed for homes and small businesses. The Primary Rate Interface (PRI) offers 23 B channels plus a D channel in North America, or 30 B channels in Europe, for larger installations.

History

ISDN was defined by the CCITT (now ITU-T) in the 1980s and deployed widely in countries such as Germany and Japan. It served as an early form of home internet access faster than analogue modems, but was largely superseded by DSL, cable and fibre broadband; many carriers have since retired ISDN services as part of the migration to all-IP networks.
