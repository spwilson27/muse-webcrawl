HuffPost
Founded2005
FounderArianna Huffington (with partners)
OwnerBuzzFeed (from 2020)
[Image: The Huffington Post's early blog-era homepage design]

HuffPost, founded in 2005 as The Huffington Post by Arianna Huffington and partners, began as a group blog and news aggregator mixing original reporting, opinion and aggregation. Sold to AOL in 2011 for $315 million, it won a Pulitzer Prize in 2012, passed through Verizon's media arm, and was acquired by BuzzFeed in 2020.

Style and reach

HuffPost built enormous traffic with search- and social-optimised headlines and a large contributor platform (closed in 2018). It launched many international editions; its politics coverage and personal-essay 'Voices' tradition are hallmarks.
