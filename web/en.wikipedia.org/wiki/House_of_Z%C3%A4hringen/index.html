House of Zähringen

The House of Zähringen was a dynasty of Swabian nobility in medieval Germany. Named after Zähringen castle near Freiburg im Breisgau, the dukes of Zähringen built a territorial lordship in what is now south-western Germany and Switzerland.

City foundations

The Zähringen dukes were notable founders of cities, establishing or refounding Freiburg im Breisgau (1120), Fribourg (1157), Bern (1191) and other towns as fortified market centres of their duchy.

Decline

The main line died out with Berthold V in 1218, when much of the family's inheritance passed to the counts of Kyburg and Urach and, in Switzerland, eventually to the rising Habsburgs and the city of Bern itself. The margraves of Baden descend from a cadet line of the house.
