Rikki-Tikki-Tavi

Author: Rudyard Kipling
Published: 1894, in The Jungle Book
Genre: Children's short story
Setting: Segowlee cantonment, India

"Rikki-Tikki-Tavi" is a short story in Rudyard Kipling's The Jungle Book (1894). It tells of a young Indian grey mongoose adopted by a British family in India who defends them from a pair of murderous cobras, Nag and Nagaina.

Plot

Washed from his burrow by a flood, Rikki-tikki is taken in by the family of a boy named Teddy at their bungalow in Segowlee cantonment. After killing the krait Karait, he learns that the cobras Nag and Nagaina plan to kill the family so they can reclaim the garden for their eggs. Rikki kills Nag in the bathroom, destroys the cobra eggs in the melon bed, and follows Nagaina down her hole to finish the fight, emerging triumphant.

Adaptations

The story was adapted as a 1975 animated television special directed by Chuck Jones, and a Soviet animated film in 1965.
