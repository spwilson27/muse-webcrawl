Spooky Tooth

OriginCarlisle, England
GenresHard rock, progressive rock, psychedelic rock
Years active1967–1970, 1972–1974, 1998–1999, 2004, 2008–2009
LabelsIsland, A&M
Key membersMike Harrison, Gary Wright, Luther Grosvenor, Greg Ridley, Mike Kellie

Spooky Tooth were an English rock band principally active in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Formed in Carlisle in 1967 from the remnants of the band Art (formerly the V.I.P.'s), the group's classic line-up featured dual lead vocalists and keyboardists Mike Harrison and the American Gary Wright, guitarist Luther Grosvenor, bassist Greg Ridley and drummer Mike Kellie. Their sound combined heavy Hammond organ, twin keyboards and soulful vocals.

The band signed to Island Records and released their debut album It's All About in 1968, which included their hit single "Tobacco Road". Their second album, Spooky Two (1969), is often regarded as their masterpiece, featuring songs such as "Better by You, Better than Me" (later covered by Judas Priest, whose recording became the subject of a notorious 1990 subliminal-message lawsuit) and "Evil Woman". Ceremony (1969), an electronic mass recorded with French composer Pierre Henry, divided fans, and Wright and Ridley departed, Ridley co-founding Humble Pie.

Later years

A re-formed Spooky Tooth released The Last Puff (1970) before disbanding; Wright, Harrison and Kellie revived the band for the albums You Broke My Heart So I Busted Your Jaw (1973) and The Mirror (1974). Wright went on to solo success with "Dream Weaver" (1975). Various reunions of surviving members took place from the late 1990s onwards. The band remains a cult favourite of the early British hard and progressive rock era.
