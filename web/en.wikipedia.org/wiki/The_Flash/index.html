The Flash is the name of several superheroes appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gardner Fox and artist Harry Lampert, the original Flash first appeared in Flash Comics #1 (cover date January 1940). Nicknamed the "Scarlet Speedster", all incarnations of the Flash possess "superspeed", which includes the ability to run, move, and think extremely fast, use superhuman reflexes, and seemingly violate certain laws of physics.

Thus far, at least five different characters — each of whom somehow gained the power of "the Speed Force" — have assumed the role of the Flash in DC's continuity: college athlete Jay Garrick (1940–1951, 1961–present), forensic scientist Barry Allen (1956–1985, 2008–present), Barry's nephew Wally West (1986–2011, 2016–present), and Barry's grandson Bart Allen (2006–2007).

Publication history

Golden Age

The Flash first appeared in the Golden Age of Comic Books in Flash Comics #1, published by All-American Publications, one of the three companies that would eventually merge to form DC Comics. This version, Jay Garrick, gained his speed by inhaling hard water vapors while asleep in his laboratory. He became a founding member of the Justice Society of America.

Silver Age and Barry Allen

In 1956, DC revived the Flash in Showcase #4, introducing Barry Allen, a police scientist who gained speed when lightning struck a shelf of chemicals that splashed onto him. The revamped Barry Allen is generally considered the start of the Silver Age of comic books. Barry was a founding member of the Justice League and appeared to die heroically in the 1985–86 crossover Crisis on Infinite Earths, before returning in 2008's Final Crisis.

Wally West and later Flashes

Wally West, who had been Barry's sidekick Kid Flash, took up the mantle for over two decades. Barry's grandson Bart Allen briefly became the Flash in the 2000s. The 2011 Flashpoint storyline, in which Barry alters history, rebooted DC continuity; Barry remains the best-known Flash in other media.

Powers and abilities

All Flashes draw their power from the Speed Force, an energy field that grants speedsters their abilities. Using it, a Flash can run faster than light, vibrate through solid objects, create vortexes, heal rapidly, and travel through time — famously by means of the "cosmic treadmill". Barry Allen has also used his speed to generate lightning and think at superhuman rates.

Enemies

The Flash's rogues' gallery, centred on Central City and Keystone City, includes the immortal Vandal Savage, the telepathic Gorilla Grodd, the "Rogues" (Captain Cold, Mirror Master, Heat Wave, Weather Wizard, the Trickster and Captain Boomerang), and the evil speedsters known as the Reverse-Flashes, chiefly Eobard Thawne (Professor Zoom) and Hunter Zolomon (Zoom).

In other media

The Flash has appeared in numerous animated series, a 1990 live-action television series starring John Wesley Shipp, and the Arrowverse series The Flash (2014–2023) starring Grant Gustin as Barry Allen. A feature film, The Flash, was released in 2023 with Ezra Miller in the role.
