Comcast Corporation

Type: Public
Founded: 1963, Tupelo, Mississippi
Headquarters: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Key people: Brian L. Roberts (chairman/CEO)
Brands: Xfinity, NBCUniversal, Sky

Comcast Corporation is an American multinational telecommunications and media conglomerate headquartered at the Comcast Center in Philadelphia. It is one of the largest broadcasting and cable television companies in the world by revenue and the largest home Internet service provider and cable TV provider in the United States.

History

Founded in 1963 as American Cable Systems in Tupelo, Mississippi, and reincorporated in Pennsylvania as Comcast in 1969, the company grew through cable acquisitions under Ralph J. Roberts and his son Brian L. Roberts. Major moves included acquiring AT&T Broadband (2002), a controlling stake then full ownership of NBCUniversal (2011–2013), and the British broadcaster Sky (2018).

Businesses

Comcast operates Xfinity residential cable, broadband and wireless services; Comcast Business; NBCUniversal (NBC, Telemundo, cable networks, Universal Pictures, Universal theme parks, and the Peacock streaming service); and Sky Group in Europe. It has frequently ranked poorly in cable-industry customer-satisfaction surveys while remaining highly profitable.
