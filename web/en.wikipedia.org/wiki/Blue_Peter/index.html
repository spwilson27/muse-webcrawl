Blue Peter is a British children's television entertainment programme created by John Hunter Blair. It is the longest-running children's TV show in the world, having been broadcast since October 1958.

Format

The programme traditionally mixes studio items with films, makes (craft projects often built from household items such as sticky-backed plastic and washing-up liquid bottles), cookery, competitions and appeals. Presenters award Blue Peter badges to viewers for letters, poems and pictures, and the show is famous for its pets, its garden, and its annual charity appeals.

History

Early presenters included Christopher Trace and Leila Williams; the 1960s and 1970s team of Valerie Singleton, John Noakes and Peter Purves became iconic, with Noakes's daredevil stunts — including a record-breaking free-fall parachute jump — a defining feature. The show moved from BBC One to the CBBC channel in 2012 and is produced in Salford.
