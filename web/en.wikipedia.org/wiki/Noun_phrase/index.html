Noun phrase

A noun phrase (NP) is a phrase that has a noun or pronoun as its head and functions in a sentence as subject, object or complement, for example "the tall woman in the red coat".

Structure

A noun phrase may consist of a single noun or pronoun, or a head noun with determiners, adjective phrases, prepositional phrases and relative clauses as modifiers. In generative grammar, many analyses treat the determiner as head, making noun phrases determiner phrases (DPs).
