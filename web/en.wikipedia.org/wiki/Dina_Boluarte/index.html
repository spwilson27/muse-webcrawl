Dina Ercilia Boluarte Zegarra (born 31 May 1962) is a Peruvian politician, civil servant and lawyer who served as the president of Peru from 7 December 2022 until her removal by Congress on 10 October 2025. She became Peru's first female president after succeeding Pedro Castillo, under whom she had served as vice president and as minister of development and social inclusion, following his attempt to dissolve Congress and his subsequent impeachment and arrest.

Early life and career

Boluarte was born in Chalhuanca, in the Apurímac region, and is a native speaker of Quechua. She studied law at the University of San Martín de Porres and worked from 2007 at the National Registry of Identification and Civil Status (RENIEC), eventually heading its Surco office. She entered electoral politics with the Marxist Free Peru party, running unsuccessfully for mayor of Surquillo in 2018, and was chosen as Pedro Castillo's running mate for the 2021 general election, which the ticket won in the runoff against Keiko Fujimori.

Presidency

Boluarte was sworn in hours after Castillo's removal on 7 December 2022. Her accession triggered widespread protests, especially in the southern Andes, demanding her resignation, early elections and a constituent assembly; the government's response left around 50 civilians dead in clashes in December 2022 and January 2023, including massacres in Ayacucho and Juliaca, which drew condemnation from human rights organisations and severely damaged her popularity — her approval ratings fell to among the lowest recorded for a Peruvian president. In government she allied with the conservative bloc controlling Congress. In 2024 she faced the "Rolexgate" scandal, an investigation into undeclared luxury watches and jewellery, and raids on her home and the presidential palace. On 10 October 2025 Congress voted overwhelmingly to declare the presidency vacant on grounds of "permanent moral incapacity"; she was succeeded by José Jerí, the president of Congress.
