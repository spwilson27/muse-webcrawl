Kirin

The kirin is the Japanese and Korean reading of the Chinese qilin, a mythical hooved creature in East Asian mythology said to appear with the imminent arrival or death of a sage or illustrious ruler. Often described with dragon-like features, scales, antlers and an ox or horse body, it is a gentle, auspicious beast that punishes the wicked and symbolises prosperity, serenity and longevity.

In Japan, the kirin is ranked among the holiest animals and lends its name to Kirin Company, the brewery founded in 1888. In modern fantasy and games, kirin appear as unicorn-like beings. Giraffes were historically called kirin in Japan after early encounters linked them to the legend.
