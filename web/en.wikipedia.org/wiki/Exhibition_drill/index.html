Exhibition drill

Exhibition drill is a variant of drill and ceremony in which a drill team performs elaborate, precision routines, often involving spinning, tossing, and exchanging (demilitarised) rifles with fixed bayonets, for public performance and competition rather than for strictly military purposes.

Exhibition drill is performed by military units such as honour guards and by JROTC and cadet drill teams in schools. Famous professional teams include the United States Marine Corps Silent Drill Platoon and the United States Air Force Honor Guard Drill Team, both known for intricate rifle routines performed without verbal commands.

Competitions judge teams on precision, difficulty, bearing, and showmanship. Routines may be performed armed or unarmed, in categories for individuals, pairs, and full teams.
