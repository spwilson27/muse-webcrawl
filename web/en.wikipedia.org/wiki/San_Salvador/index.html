San Salvador

San Salvador is the capital and the largest city of El Salvador, with about 570,000 residents in the city and 2.4 million in the metropolitan area. It lies in the Valle de las Hamacas ('valley of the hammocks', for its seismic activity) at the foot of the San Salvador volcano.

Founded in 1525, San Salvador has been repeatedly destroyed by earthquakes (1854, 1917, 1986, 2001). It was a centre of the Salvadoran Civil War (1979-1992); Archbishop Óscar Romero was assassinated while saying Mass in 1980 and canonised in 2018. Since 2019, President Nayib Bukele's government has waged an extraordinary crackdown on the Mara Salvatrucha and Barrio 18 gangs under a state of exception (from 2022), dramatically reducing one of the world's highest murder rates while imprisoning about 2 percent of the adult population — a policy both popular at home and condemned by human-rights groups. The city adopted bitcoin enthusiasm symbolised by national policy in 2021.
