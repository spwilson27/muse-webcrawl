Chemicals in a laboratory

A chemical, or chemical substance, is a form of matter that has a constant chemical composition and characteristic properties. A chemical is any material with a definite composition: unlike a mixture, it cannot be separated into its constituents by physical separation methods without breaking chemical bonds. The term covers chemical elements and chemical compounds, and is the subject matter of the science of chemistry.

Definition

A chemical substance may be a single element, such as oxygen (O2) or iron, or a compound of two or more elements in fixed proportions, such as water (H2O) or sodium chloride (NaCl). Every sample of a pure chemical has the same composition and the same properties regardless of its source — water distilled from a river and water synthesised in a laboratory are identical. Substances are identified by names and registry numbers (notably CAS Registry Numbers) and characterised by properties such as melting point, density, reactivity and spectrum.

Chemicals in industry and daily life

The chemical industry converts raw materials — petroleum, natural gas, minerals, water and air — into tens of thousands of products: fertilisers, plastics, pharmaceuticals, solvents, detergents, paints and fuels. Bulk or commodity chemicals are produced on a vast scale, while specialty and fine chemicals are made in smaller quantities. In everyday usage the word "chemical" is often (misleadingly) used to imply synthetic or hazardous materials, but all matter — food, air, the human body — is composed of chemicals; safety depends on the substance and the dose, not on whether it is natural or manufactured.

Regulation and safety

Because some chemicals are toxic, flammable, corrosive or environmentally persistent, their manufacture, labelling, transport and disposal are regulated by frameworks such as the EU's REACH regulation and the Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling (GHS), whose hazard pictograms appear on containers worldwide. Safety data sheets accompany commercial chemicals, describing hazards and handling precautions.
