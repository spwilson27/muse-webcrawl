ProPublica is an American non-profit investigative newsroom founded in 2007–08, based in New York, and the first online news organisation to win a Pulitzer Prize (2010). Funded mainly by philanthropy (initially the Sandler Foundation), it publishes accountability investigations free online and often co-publishes with legacy outlets.

Work

ProPublica's projects — on health care, criminal justice, technology platforms, tax inequity ('The Secret IRS Files') and government — have won multiple Pulitzers and frequently prompted policy change. Its Local Reporting Network funds investigative reporters at local newsrooms across the US.
