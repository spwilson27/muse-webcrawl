Zhengzhou

Zhengzhou is the capital and largest city of Henan Province in the central part of the People's Republic of China. Located on the southern bank of the Yellow River, it is a major national transportation hub and one of the principal cities of the Central Plains. The prefecture-level city has a population of over 12 million.

History

Zhengzhou is one of the oldest inhabited areas of China. It was a capital of the Shang dynasty some 3,600 years ago; the remains of the Shang city walls, built of rammed earth, are still preserved in the city centre. It became the provincial capital of Henan in 1954, moved from Kaifeng, and grew rapidly as the junction of China's north–south (Beijing–Guangzhou) and east–west (Longhai) railways.

Economy and transport

The city is a centre for electronics manufacturing — Foxconn's Zhengzhou plant is one of the world's largest smartphone factories — as well as food processing, aluminium and textiles. Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport and the Zhengzhou East high-speed railway station anchor its role as a logistics hub, including China–Europe freight trains. In July 2021 the city suffered catastrophic flooding when record rainfall overwhelmed its drainage and metro system, killing hundreds across Henan.

Culture

Nearby attractions include the Shaolin Temple at Dengfeng, associated with Chan (Zen) Buddhism and kung fu, and the Henan Museum, one of China's largest museums. The Erqi Memorial Tower commemorates a 1923 railway workers' strike.
