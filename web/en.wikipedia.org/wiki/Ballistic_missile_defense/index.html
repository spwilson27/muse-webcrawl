Ballistic missile defense

Ballistic missile defense (BMD) is a system, weapon, or technology involved in the detection, tracking, interception, and destruction of attacking ballistic missiles. Because a ballistic missile crosses boost, midcourse, and terminal phases of flight, defences are organized in layered tiers intended to engage the missile at each stage, using ground-, sea-, and space-based sensors and interceptors.

Phases of interception

Boost-phase interception targets a missile while its rockets are still burning: the target is bright and slow relative to later phases, but the interceptor must be positioned very close to the launch site. Midcourse interception occurs in space during the missile's long ballistic coast; it offers the most engagement time but must discriminate warheads from decoys and debris. Terminal-phase interception attacks the descending warhead in the atmosphere, defending a relatively small area but with mature technology, as in the Patriot and THAAD systems.

Major systems

The United States Missile Defense Agency fields the Ground-based Midcourse Defense system in Alaska and California against limited intercontinental attacks, the sea-based Aegis BMD with SM-3 interceptors, THAAD batteries, and Patriot PAC-3 for point defence; these are linked by a command-and-control network and early-warning radars and satellites. Other missile defence powers include Russia (the A-135/A-235 systems around Moscow and the S-400/S-500), Israel (Arrow, David's Sling, and Iron Dome), India, and China.

History and debate

Anti-ballistic missile efforts began in the late 1940s; the 1972 Anti-Ballistic Missile Treaty limited US and Soviet deployments, and the US Strategic Defense Initiative of the 1980s revived interest in space-based defences. The United States withdrew from the ABM Treaty in 2002. Critics argue that missile defences are expensive, remain vulnerable to countermeasures such as decoys and salvo launches, and may destabilize deterrence; supporters contend that even limited defences are valuable against small arsenals and accidental launches.
