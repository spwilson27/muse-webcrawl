Romeo Santos

Anthony "Romeo" Santos (born July 21, 1981) is an American singer, songwriter and record producer. Known as the "King of Bachata", he rose to fame as the lead singer of the bachata group Aventura before launching a successful solo career that helped popularise the genre worldwide.

Born: July 21, 1981, The Bronx, New York City, U.S.

Genres: Bachata, Latin pop

Years active: 1994–present

Career

Born in the Bronx to a Dominican father and Puerto Rican mother, Santos co-founded Aventura in the 1990s. The group fused traditional Dominican bachata with R&B and hip hop, achieving international success with the 2002 hit "Obsesión". After the group went on hiatus in 2011, Santos released his solo debut Fórmula, Vol. 1, featuring the hit "You" and collaborations with Usher and Lil Wayne.

His follow-up albums Fórmula, Vol. 2 (2014), Golden (2017), Utopía (2019) and Fórmula, Vol. 3 (2022) consolidated his status as one of the best-selling Latin artists. In 2014 he became the first Spanish-speaking artist to headline and sell out two consecutive shows at Yankee Stadium. Aventura reunited for tours in 2020 and 2024.
