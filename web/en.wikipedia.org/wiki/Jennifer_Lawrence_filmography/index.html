Jennifer Lawrence filmography

Lawrence in 2018

Jennifer Lawrence is an American actress who has appeared in films, television series, and a music video. Her first major role was on the TBS sitcom The Bill Engvall Show (2007–2009). Her film breakthrough came as Ree Dolly in Winter's Bone (2010), earning her first Academy Award nomination.

Accolades

Lawrence won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Silver Linings Playbook (2012), becoming at 22 the second-youngest Best Actress winner in history.
