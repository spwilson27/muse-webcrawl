List of oil-producing states

This article lists U.S. states by crude oil production. Petroleum production in the United States is concentrated in a handful of states, above all Texas — which alone accounts for more than two-fifths of national output — thanks to the Permian Basin, which Texas shares with New Mexico. The shale revolution after 2008 elevated North Dakota (Bakken Formation) and New Mexico to the top ranks. Figures below are approximate recent annual averages in thousands of barrels per day.

Notes

Federal offshore production in the Gulf of Mexico — attributed to no state — adds roughly 1.8 million barrels per day and would rank second if counted separately. State rankings shift with drilling activity, prices and pipeline capacity; Texas has led the nation continuously since the early twentieth century, interrupted only briefly by Oklahoma in the 1920s.
