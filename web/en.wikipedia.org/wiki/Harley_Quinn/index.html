Harley Quinn

Harley Quinn (Dr. Harleen Frances Quinzel) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. She was created by Paul Dini and Bruce Timm for Batman: The Animated Series (1992) as a henchwoman and love interest of the Joker, and later became an antiheroine in her own right.

Origin

Harleen Quinzel was a psychiatrist at Arkham Asylum in Gotham City who fell in love with the Joker while treating him, helped him escape, and adopted the Harley Quinn persona — a play on “harlequin.” Her relationship with the Joker is often depicted as abusive, and later stories focus on her independence.

Characterisation

Harley is energetic, unpredictable and acrobatic, skilled in gymnastics and hand-to-hand combat, often wielding an oversized mallet or baseball bat. She has a close, later romantic, relationship with Poison Ivy.

In other media

Margot Robbie portrayed Harley in Suicide Squad (2016), Birds of Prey (2020) and The Suicide Squad (2021). An adult animated series Harley Quinn (2019–) starring Kaley Cuoco further popularised her solo identity. Lady Gaga played a version in Joker: Folie à Deux (2024).
