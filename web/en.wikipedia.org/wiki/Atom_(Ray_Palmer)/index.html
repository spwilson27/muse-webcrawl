The Atom (Ray Palmer) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by editor Julius Schwartz, writer Gardner Fox, and artist Gil Kane, the character first appeared in Showcase #34 (1961) and is the second character to use the name Atom, succeeding the Golden Age Atom, Al Pratt. Palmer is a physicist who can shrink to subatomic size while retaining his full strength.

Fictional character biography

Ray Palmer is a physics professor at Ivy University in Ivy Town. Experimenting with a lens made from a fragment of a white dwarf star, Palmer discovered a way to compress matter and shrink objects — though test objects tended to explode. Caught in a cave-in while exploring, Palmer used the lens on himself to shrink small enough to escape, and found his body's unusual chemistry allowed him to survive the process and control his size and weight. Building a size-changing belt apparatus, he became the superhero the Atom, fighting criminals such as the Thinker, Chronos, and Bug-Eyed Bandit, and later serving as a long-standing member of the Justice League of America.

Palmer married lawyer Jean Loring; their later divorce and Loring's role in the events of Identity Crisis (2004) became defining parts of his modern history. Palmer also explored subatomic worlds, notably in the Sword of the Atom miniseries, where he lived among a miniature alien civilization. He was succeeded for a time by Ryan Choi, and later returned to prominence, serving as a mentor figure in the Legends of Tomorrow television series' source continuity.

Powers and abilities

Using white dwarf star matter, Palmer can shrink to any size down to the subatomic while controlling his mass — able to retain his normal strength and weight at tiny size, glide on air currents when light, or punch with full force. He famously travels by shrinking and riding telephone-line electrical impulses between phone calls. Palmer is also a brilliant physicist.

In other media

Ray Palmer appears in the Arrowverse television franchise, played by Brandon Routh, as a regular on Arrow, The Flash, and Legends of Tomorrow, wearing a powered exo-suit (the A.T.O.M. suit). The character has also appeared in animated series including Justice League Unlimited and Batman: The Brave and the Bold.
