Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre

Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre (CMLL; "World Wrestling Council") is a Mexican professional wrestling promotion based in Mexico City. Founded in 1933 by Salvador Lutteroth as Empresa Mexicana de Lucha Libre, it is the oldest professional wrestling promotion in the world still in operation.

History

Lutteroth is regarded as the father of lucha libre in Mexico, staging one of the first shows on 21 September 1933. The promotion's home venue, Arena México, opened in 1956 and remains its flagship. CMLL is known for its traditional style of lucha libre, masked wrestlers (luchadores enmascarados) and high-flying technique, weekly shows on Fridays (Super Viernes), and marquee events such as the Aniversario show, held each September.
