David Bowie

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
[Image: David Bowie as Ziggy Stardust: scarlet mullet, lightning-bolt makeup, glittering bodysuit on stage in 1973]

David Robert Jones (8 January 1947 – 10 January 2016), known professionally as David Bowie, was an English musician and actor. A leading figure in the music industry, he is regarded as one of the most influential musicians of the 20th century. Bowie was acclaimed by critics and musicians for his innovative work during the 1970s, in particular his reinvention and visual presentation.

His breakthrough came with 'Space Oddity' (1969) and the glam-rock persona Ziggy Stardust (The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars, 1972). Chameleonic reinventions continued: the 'Thin White Duke', the experimental Berlin trilogy with Brian Eno (Low, 'Heroes', 1977), pop stardom with Let's Dance (1983) and a late renaissance culminating in Blackstar, released two days before his death from liver cancer, two days after his 69th birthday.
