The Walt Disney Company, commonly known as Disney, is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate headquartered at the Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, California. Founded on October 16, 1923, by brothers Walt and Roy O. Disney as the Disney Brothers Studio, it grew from a small animation company into one of the world's largest media companies, owner of film studios including Pixar, Marvel Studios and Lucasfilm, television networks such as ABC and ESPN, the Disney+ and Hulu streaming services, and twelve theme parks around the world. Its streaming service competes with Netflix.

History

Disney achieved early fame with Steamboat Willie (1928), among the first cartoons with synchronised sound, which introduced Mickey Mouse, and with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), the first full-length cel-animated feature film. In 1955 Disneyland opened in Anaheim, California; Walt Disney World followed in 1971 in Florida, begun before Walt's death in 1966. Under CEO Michael Eisner from 1984 the company enjoyed an animation "Renaissance" (The Little Mermaid, Beauty and the Beast, The Lion King) and expanded into television and publishing.

Bob Iger became CEO in 2005 and pursued marquee acquisitions: Pixar (2006), Marvel (2009), Lucasfilm (2012) and most of 21st Century Fox (2019), building the industry's strongest portfolio of franchises. Disney launched the Disney+ streaming service in November 2019, reaching very rapid subscriber growth. Iger retired in 2020, was succeeded by Bob Chapek, and returned as CEO in November 2022 as the company restructured around streaming profitability and its parks business.

Business segments

Disney reports three segments: Entertainment (film studios, television networks including ABC, and the Disney+, Hulu and ESPN+ streaming services, which compete with Netflix), ESPN (sports networks), and Experiences (theme parks, resorts, cruise ships and consumer products).

Corporate affairs

Disney is commonly ranked the world's most valuable media brand. It has also been drawn into political controversies, including a 2022–2023 dispute with the State of Florida over the governance of the special district around Walt Disney World.
