Sandoz

Sandoz Group AG
Industry: Pharmaceuticals
Founded: 1886 (as Kern & Sandoz)
Headquarters: Basel, Switzerland

Sandoz is a Swiss pharmaceutical company specialising in generic and biosimilar medicines. It was founded in Basel in 1886 as Kern & Sandoz, originally a manufacturer of dyes and chemicals, and later became a major pharmaceuticals business.

History

Founded by Alfred Kern and Edouard Sandoz, the company moved from dyestuffs into pharmaceuticals in the early 20th century. Sandoz researchers are notable in the history of LSD, which Albert Hofmann synthesised and studied at Sandoz laboratories in the 1930s and 1940s. In 1996 Sandoz merged with Ciba-Geigy to form Novartis, with Sandoz surviving as the generics division's brand. In October 2023 Sandoz was spun off from Novartis as an independent publicly listed company.

Business

Sandoz is one of the world's largest producers of generic medicines and a leading maker of biosimilars — follow-on versions of biological drugs. Its products include antibiotics (its Kundl, Austria site is a major penicillin production centre), cardiovascular and oncology medicines, and biosimilars such as those for adalimumab and etanercept.
