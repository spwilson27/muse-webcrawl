Spot (robot)

Manufacturer: Boston Dynamics
Type: Quadruped robot
Released: 2019 (commercial)
Mass: ~32.5 kg

Spot is a four-legged robot developed by the American robotics company Boston Dynamics. Marketed as an agile mobile robot for industrial inspection and data collection, Spot can traverse rough terrain, climb stairs, avoid obstacles, and carry sensor payloads. It went on general sale in June 2020 at a price of $74,500, after an earlier leasing programme in 2019.

Development

Spot descends from Boston Dynamics' earlier military-funded quadrupeds BigDog and LS3, and from the smaller research robot SpotMini, which was unveiled in 2016 with an articulated arm in place of a head. The production Spot is electrically powered, with a battery providing roughly 90 minutes of operation, stereo cameras for navigation, and optional payloads including inspection cameras, LiDAR, gas sensors, and the Spot Arm manipulator.

Applications

Spots have been deployed for construction progress monitoring, oil-rig and plant inspection, warehouse and factory data capture, and research. High-profile trials included patrol and public-safety pilots by police agencies, which attracted scrutiny over surveillance, and a demonstration unit used by the New York City Fire Department for search-and-rescue support. Videos of Spot dancing and working have made it one of the most recognisable robots in the world.
