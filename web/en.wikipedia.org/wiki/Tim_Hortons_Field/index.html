Tim Hortons Field is a multi-purpose stadium in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. Built on the site of the former Ivor Wynne Stadium, it opened in 2014 and is the home of the Hamilton Tiger-Cats of the Canadian Football League and Forge FC of the Canadian Premier League. The stadium is named for the Tim Hortons coffee chain, which was founded in Hamilton in 1964 and holds the naming rights.

History

The stadium was constructed as part of the facilities for the 2015 Pan American Games, hosting soccer during the Games, when it was officially known as the CIBC Hamilton Pan Am Soccer Stadium. Construction delays pushed completion into the 2014 CFL season; the Tiger-Cats played their first game there on Labour Day, 1 September 2014, defeating the Toronto Argonauts. The venue has hosted Grey Cup games in 2021 and 2023, as well as the 2022 NHL Heritage Classic outdoor hockey game.

Design

Tim Hortons Field seats about 23,000 spectators permanently, with capacity expandable to roughly 40,000 using temporary seating for major events such as the Grey Cup. It was among the first stadiums in Canada designed in part for soccer configuration, and includes large social and patio areas at the south end.
