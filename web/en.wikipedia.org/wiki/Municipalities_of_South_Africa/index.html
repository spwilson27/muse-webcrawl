Municipalities of South Africa

South Africa is divided for local government purposes into municipalities. Since the 2016 demarcation there have been 205 local municipalities, 44 district municipalities and 8 metropolitan municipalities, for a total of 257 municipalities.

Categories

Category A municipalities are metropolitan municipalities governing large cities such as Johannesburg, Cape Town, eThekwini (Durban), Tshwane (Pretoria) and Ekurhuleni. Category B are local municipalities, which share authority with Category C district municipalities covering wider, mostly rural areas.

Governance

Municipal councils are elected every five years in local elections. Municipalities deliver services including water, electricity reticulation, sanitation, refuse removal and local roads, funded by property rates, service charges and national transfers.
