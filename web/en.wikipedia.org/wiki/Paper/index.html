Paper

Paper
InventedChina — Cai Lun's process, AD 105 (traditionally)
Made fromPlant fibres (wood pulp, rags, bamboo)
[Image: Sheets of handmade paper drying on racks in a workshop]

Paper is a thin sheet material produced by pressing moist fibres — usually cellulose from wood, rags or grasses — and drying them into flexible leaves. Invented in China by the 2nd century AD (Cai Lun's process, 105 AD), it spread via the Islamic world to Europe by the 12th century.

Impact

Cheaper than parchment or papyrus, paper enabled mass books after the printing press (see the crawl's Printing press link), bureaucracy, newspapers and packaging. Digital media — from the E-book to email — reduced some uses while packaging grew; recycled fibre supplies much production.
