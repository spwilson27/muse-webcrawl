Sainsbury Centre for Visual Arts

Sainsbury Centre
Location: University of East Anglia, Norwich
Opened: 1978
Architect: Norman Foster (Foster + Partners)
Type: Art museum and gallery

The Sainsbury Centre for Visual Arts is an art gallery and museum at the University of East Anglia in Norwich, England. It opened in 1978 in one of the first major public buildings designed by Norman Foster, a light-filled hangar-like structure clad in aluminium panels.

Collection

The Centre houses the collection assembled by Robert and Lisa Sainsbury, given to the university in 1973. It includes works by Henry Moore, Francis Bacon, Alberto Giacometti and Amedeo Modigliani alongside significant holdings of tribal and world art. The building also hosts major temporary exhibitions.

Architecture

The Foster design places galleries, teaching spaces and a restaurant within a single open volume, with services in a double wall. An underground extension, the Crescent Wing, was added in 1991.
