Manipal Academy of Higher Education

The Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), formerly Manipal University, is a private deemed university in Manipal, Karnataka, India. It grew from the Kasturba Medical College founded in 1953 by T. M. A. Pai, and was granted deemed-university status in 1993.

Campuses and programmes

MAHE's constituent institutions cover medicine, dentistry, engineering (Manipal Institute of Technology), pharmacy, nursing, allied health, management, and liberal arts. It has campuses in Mangalore, Bangalore, Jamshedpur, and Jaipur is a sister university, plus international campuses in Dubai and Malaysia. It enrols tens of thousands of students from many countries and is regularly ranked among India's top private universities.
