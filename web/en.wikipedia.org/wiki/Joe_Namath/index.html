Joe Namath

Born: Joseph William Namath, 31 May 1943, Beaver Falls, Pennsylvania, U.S.

Nickname: Broadway Joe

Position: Quarterback

College: Alabama

Teams: New York Jets (1965–1976); Los Angeles Rams (1977)

Joseph William Namath (born May 31, 1943), nicknamed "Broadway Joe", is an American former football quarterback who played in the American Football League (AFL) and National Football League (NFL) for 13 seasons, primarily with the New York Jets.

Career

Namath played for coach Bear Bryant at the University of Alabama, winning a national championship in 1964. He signed a record contract with the Jets in 1965 and became the first professional quarterback to pass for 4,000 yards in a season in 1967.

His fame rests chiefly on Super Bowl III in January 1969, when he famously guaranteed that the underdog Jets would beat the heavily favoured Baltimore Colts — and then delivered a 16–7 victory that legitimised the AFL and earned him the game's MVP award. Chronic knee injuries hampered the rest of his career; he finished with a single season for the Los Angeles Rams in 1977.

Legacy

Namath was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1985. A flamboyant celebrity as well as an athlete, he appeared in films and advertisements and remains one of the most recognisable figures in American sporting history.
