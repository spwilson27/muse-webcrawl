Matilda the Musical is a stage musical adaptation of Roald Dahl's 1988 children's novel Matilda, with a book by Dennis Kelly and music and lyrics by Australian comedian and musician Tim Minchin. It tells the story of Matilda Wormwood, a precocious five-year-old with neglectful parents and a terrifying headmistress, Miss Trunchbull, who discovers she has psychokinetic powers and uses them to stand up for herself and her beloved teacher, Miss Honey.

Productions

The musical was developed by the Royal Shakespeare Company and premiered at the Courtyard Theatre in Stratford-upon-Avon in November 2010, directed by Matthew Warchus. It transferred to London's West End in October 2011, winning a record seven Olivier Awards, and opened on Broadway in April 2013, winning five Tony Awards, including Best Book of a Musical. Signature songs include "Naughty", "When I Grow Up", "Revolting Children", and "My House".

Film adaptation

A film adaptation, Roald Dahl's Matilda the Musical, directed by Warchus with a screenplay by Kelly, was released in 2022, starring Alisha Weir as Matilda, Emma Thompson as Miss Trunchbull, and Lashana Lynch as Miss Honey.
