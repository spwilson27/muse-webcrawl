List of Secretaries of State for Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Affairs

The Secretary of State for Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Affairs, commonly called the Foreign Secretary, is a senior minister of the Crown in the United Kingdom, heading the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO). The office of Foreign Secretary was created in 1782; in 1968 it merged with the Commonwealth Secretary, and in 2020 it absorbed the Department for International Development. Office-holders include:
