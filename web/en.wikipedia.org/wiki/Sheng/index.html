Sheng (Chinese: 盛; pinyin: Shèng) is a Chinese surname, and Sheng (笙, pinyin: Shēng) is also a Chinese given name element. The surname Sheng originated from a fief in the state of Cheng during the Zhou dynasty, and its bearers are found throughout China and the Chinese diaspora. Characters pronounced Sheng include 盛 ("flourishing"), 笙 (the mouth organ), 升 ("to rise") and 勝 ("victory", simplified 胜).

Notable people with the surname Sheng

  • Sheng Xuanhuai (1844–1916), Qing dynasty official and industrialist who founded major enterprises including China Merchants Steam Navigation Company and helped establish Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
  • Sheng Shicai (1895–1970), warlord who governed Xinjiang from 1933 to 1944.
  • Sheng Siong, the Singapore supermarket chain, bears the name of its founder Lim Huan Sheng.

Given name

As a given name, Sheng appears in combinations such as Sheng Jian and reflects meanings of the chosen character — flourishing, rising, or victory. Notable bearers include the mathematician Chern Shiing-shen (whose given name is romanised differently) and the composer Bright Sheng (born 1955), a Chinese-American composer and MacArthur Fellow.

Other uses

"Sheng" is the romanisation for the Chinese free-reed mouth organ (see Sheng (instrument)), for a category of raw pu-erh tea (shēng chá), and for the Sheng (linguistics) slang of Nairobi, Kenya.
