Ghostbusters is an American supernatural comedy media franchise created by Dan Aykroyd and Harold Ramis. It centres on a team of eccentric parapsychologists in New York City who run a ghost-catching business using high-tech equipment such as proton packs and ghost traps. The franchise began with the 1984 film Ghostbusters, one of the highest-grossing comedies of its era.

Films

The original film, directed by Ivan Reitman and starring Bill Murray, Aykroyd, Ramis and Ernie Hudson, was followed by Ghostbusters II (1989). A reboot directed by Paul Feig appeared in 2016 with an all-female lead team. The series returned to the original continuity with Ghostbusters: Afterlife (2021), directed by Jason Reitman, and its sequel Ghostbusters: Frozen Empire (2024).

Other media

The animated series The Real Ghostbusters (1986–1991) and its sequel Extreme Ghostbusters (1997) expanded the franchise for younger audiences. Numerous video games have been released, including Ghostbusters: The Video Game (2009), which reunited the original cast. The franchise's "no-ghost" logo, the Ecto-1 car, the Stay Puft Marshmallow Man and Ray Parker Jr.'s theme song "Ghostbusters" have become enduring pop-culture icons.
