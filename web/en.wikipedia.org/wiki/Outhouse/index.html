Outhouse

An outhouse is a small structure, separate from a main building, which covers a toilet. This is typically either a pit latrine or a bucket toilet, but can also be a chemical toilet. The term may also refer to any outbuilding of a house, but in North American usage it specifically means an outdoor toilet.

Outhouses were common in rural areas and small towns before indoor plumbing became widespread. A typical outhouse is a wooden shed with a bench seat over a pit, often marked with a crescent moon cut into the door.

Although largely replaced by flush toilets, outhouses remain in use at campsites, parks and remote properties, and modern designs such as composting toilets address sanitation and groundwater concerns associated with traditional pits.
