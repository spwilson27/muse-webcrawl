Prešov Region

The Prešov Region (Slovak: Prešovský kraj) is one of the eight Slovak administrative regions, lying in the north-eastern part of the country. Its capital and largest city is Prešov. With an area of about 8,974 km², it is the second largest region of Slovakia, and with roughly 800,000 inhabitants it is the most populous.

Geography

The region includes part of the High Tatras, Slovakia's highest mountains, including Gerlachovský štít, the country's highest peak at 2,655 metres. It contains the Tatra National Park and borders Poland to the north, Ukraine to the east, and the Košice and Žilina regions to the south and west.

Districts

The region is divided into 13 districts, including Bardejov, Humenné, Kežmarok, Levoča, Poprad, Prešov, Stará Ľubovňa and Vranov nad Topľou. The wooden churches of the Slovak Carpathians, several of which lie in the region, are inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list.
