Kölsch (beer)

Kölsch
TypeHybrid ale/lager beer
OriginCologne, Germany
Alcoholapprox. 4.4–5.2% ABV
ColourPale gold, brilliantly clear
Serving0.2 L cylindrical glass (Stange)

Kölsch is a style of beer originating in Cologne (Köln), Germany. It is a clear, pale, top-fermented beer that is cold-conditioned (lagered), combining ale yeast fermentation at warm temperatures with a period of cold storage, giving a crisp, delicately hopped and lightly fruity character.

Protection and convention

Kölsch is a protected geographical indication within the European Union: only breweries in Cologne (and a small number of grandfathered breweries nearby) that signed the Kölsch Convention of 1985 may call their beer Kölsch. Around two dozen breweries produce it.

Service

In Cologne's traditional brewpubs (Brauereien), Kölsch is served in small 0.2-litre cylindrical glasses called Stangen, carried in circular trays (Kränze) by waiters known as Köbes, who traditionally replace empty glasses with full ones until the drinker places a coaster on top of the glass.
