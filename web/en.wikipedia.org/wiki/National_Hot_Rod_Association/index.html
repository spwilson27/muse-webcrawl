National Hot Rod Association

The National Hot Rod Association (NHRA) is a drag racing sanctioning body in the United States, founded by Wally Parks in 1951 to promote safe, organised drag racing and discourage street racing. It is the largest motorsport sanctioning body of its kind, with tens of thousands of members.

Championship series

The NHRA Camping World Drag Racing Series (now the Mission Foods Drag Racing Series) features professional classes including Top Fuel, Funny Car, Pro Stock and Pro Stock Motorcycle. Top Fuel dragsters and Funny Cars produce over 11,000 horsepower and cover 1,000 feet in under four seconds.

Events

Flagship events include the U.S. Nationals at Indianapolis, held since 1955, the Winternationals at Pomona, and the Gatornationals at Gainesville.
