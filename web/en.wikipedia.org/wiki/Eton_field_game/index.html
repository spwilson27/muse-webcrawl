Eton field game

The Eton field game is a form of football played at Eton College in England. Together with the Eton wall game, it is one of the school's two traditional football codes, and it is regarded as one of the forerunners of association football.

Play

The game is played between teams of eleven on a pitch smaller than a standard football field. Unlike the wall game, it is played on an open field, and handling the ball is not permitted except by the goalkeeper. Goals are scored as in association football, but a distinctive feature is the "rouge", a point scored when an attacker touches the ball down behind the goal line after it has crossed in play, similar in spirit to a try in rugby.

Points and results

A goal counts for three points and a rouge for one. If a match ends level on goals, rouges can decide the winner. The rules were among those consulted as school football codes were codified in the nineteenth century, and Etonians played a notable part in early organized football in England.
