The Sandman (TV series)

Genre: Fantasy drama, horror
Developed by: Neil Gaiman, David S. Goyer, Allan Heinberg
Starring: Tom Sturridge
Original network: Netflix
Original release: 5 August 2022

The Sandman is an American fantasy drama television series based on the 1989–1996 comic book series written by Neil Gaiman and published by DC Comics. Developed by Gaiman, David S. Goyer and Allan Heinberg for Netflix, the series tells the story of Dream (also called Morpheus), one of the seven Endless, who is captured in an occult ritual in 1916 and, after escaping in the present day, sets out to restore order to his realm, the Dreaming.

Premise and episodes

After 106 years of imprisonment, Dream (Tom Sturridge) escapes and journeys to recover his stolen tools — his pouch of sand, his helm and his ruby — meeting figures including his siblings Death (Kirby Howell-Baptiste) and Desire, the nightmare the Corinthian (Boyd Holbrook), and Johanna Constantine. The first season, released on 5 August 2022 with ten episodes plus a surprise bonus episode adapting "Dream of a Thousand Cats" and "Calliope", draws chiefly on the comic collections Preludes & Nocturnes and The Doll's House. The episode "24/7" and "The Sound of Her Wings" were singled out by critics for praise.

Cast

The ensemble includes Gwendoline Christie as Lucifer, Charles Dance as Roderick Burgess, Vivienne Acheampong as Lucienne, Patton Oswalt as the voice of Matthew the Raven, Jenna Coleman as Johanna Constantine, David Thewlis as John Dee, Stephen Fry as Gilbert, and Mark Hamill as the voice of Mervyn Pumpkinhead.

Production and reception

After decades of failed film adaptations, the series was produced by Warner Bros. Television with Gaiman as executive producer. It was received positively by critics for its faithfulness, visuals and performances, and was a major streaming success for Netflix. A second and final season was confirmed in November 2022 and released in two volumes in July 2025, concluding the series after sexual-assault allegations against Gaiman emerged in 2024, which he has denied.
