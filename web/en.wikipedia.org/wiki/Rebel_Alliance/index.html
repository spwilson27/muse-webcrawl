Rebel Alliance

The Rebel Alliance (formally the Alliance to Restore the Republic) is a fictional resistance movement in the Star Wars universe. Formed from disparate rebel cells opposed to the Galactic Empire, it fights the Galactic Civil War to overthrow the Emperor and restore the Galactic Republic.

History

Senators including Mon Mothma and Bail Organa covertly supported early resistance groups. After the Declaration of a New Order, the cells united into a formal alliance, operating from bases such as Yavin 4 and Hoth. The Alliance destroyed both Death Stars and, following victory at Endor, reorganized as the New Republic.

Organization

The Alliance combined a civilian government-in-exile with starfighter commands, ground forces, and intelligence networks. Its starbird insignia derived from the crest of the Marek family.
