Colonial National Historical Park

Colonial National Historical Park

Location: Virginia, United States
Managed by: National Park Service
Includes: Jamestown, Yorktown Battlefield, Colonial Parkway, Cape Henry Memorial

Colonial National Historical Park is a National Park Service unit in southeastern Virginia protecting sites associated with early English colonisation and the American Revolutionary War. Its components are linked by the 23-mile scenic Colonial Parkway.

Components

Historic Jamestowne is the site of Jamestown, the first permanent English settlement in North America (1607), jointly interpreted with Preservation Virginia, and distinct from the nearby Jamestown Settlement living-history museum. Yorktown Battlefield preserves the site of the 1781 Siege of Yorktown, where British forces under Lord Cornwallis surrendered to George Washington's allied American and French army, effectively ending the Revolutionary War. The Cape Henry Memorial marks the 1607 first landing.

History

The park was authorised as Colonial National Monument in 1930 and redesignated a National Historical Park in 1936. Archaeology at Jamestown, especially since the 1994 Jamestown Rediscovery project, recovered the original James Fort.
