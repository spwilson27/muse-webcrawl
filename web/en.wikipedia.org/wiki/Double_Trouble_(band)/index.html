Double Trouble (band)

Double Trouble is the American blues rock band that backed guitarist Stevie Ray Vaughan from the late 1970s until his death in 1990, featuring bassist Tommy Shannon and drummer Chris Layton, later joined by keyboardist Reese Wynans.

Career

The band powered Vaughan's albums Texas Flood, Couldn't Stand the Weather and In Step, and continued after 1990 with other guitarists and as a recording act in its own right.

Recognition

Double Trouble was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame together with Stevie Ray Vaughan in 2015.
