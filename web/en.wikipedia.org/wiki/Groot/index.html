Groot is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee, Larry Lieber, and Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Tales to Astonish #13 (November 1960). Groot is a Flora colossus, a tree-like extraterrestrial being from Planet X. Originally depicted as an invader intent on capturing humans for experimentation, the character was reintroduced in 2006 as a heroic member of the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Fictional character biography

In his original appearance, Groot was an extraterrestrial tree-like creature who landed on Earth seeking human test subjects. A later retcon presented that creature as an impostor or juvenile, and established Groot as a member of the sapient Flora colossi. Groot joined the Guardians of the Galaxy during the Annihilation: Conquest storyline, forming a close bond with Rocket Raccoon, who is one of the few able to understand his language — which, owing to the stiffness of his larynx, consists almost entirely of the phrase "I am Groot", with meaning conveyed by inflection.

Powers and abilities

Groot can absorb wood as food and regenerate, regrowing from a single sprig. He can manipulate plant life, increase his size and mass enormously, and is highly resistant to injury and fire. He possesses a genius-level intellect in his species' scholarly traditions, particularly quasi-dimensional super-positioning engineering, despite his limited apparent vocabulary.

In other media

Groot appears in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, voiced by Vin Diesel, debuting in Guardians of the Galaxy (2014). The sacrificial death of the original Groot in that film led to his offspring, commonly called "Baby Groot", who grows through subsequent films including Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017), Avengers: Infinity War (2018), Avengers: Endgame (2019), and Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023). The animated shorts series I Am Groot premiered on Disney+ in 2022.
