Lisbon derby

Other namesDerby de Lisboa; Derby Eterno; Derby da Capital
LocationLisbon, Portugal
TeamsS.L. Benfica and Sporting CP
First meeting1 December 1907
StadiumsEstádio da Luz; Estádio José Alvalade

The Lisbon derby (Portuguese: Derby de Lisboa) is the name given to the football matches between the two biggest clubs of Lisbon, Portugal — S.L. Benfica and Sporting CP. It is one of the fiercest rivalries in Portuguese football and among the most prominent city derbies in Europe.

History

The rivalry began in 1907, shortly after several players left Benfica for the wealthier Sporting, and the first match took place on 1 December 1907, ending in a 2–1 win for Sporting. The two stadiums, Estádio da Luz and Estádio José Alvalade, stand only a few kilometres apart. Together, Benfica and Sporting — with FC Porto — form Portugal's "Big Three" and have dominated the Primeira Liga.

Honours and records

Benfica hold the record for league titles and also the record derby win, 7–2 in 1948; Sporting's biggest derby victory came in the Portuguese Cup with a 7–1 win in 1986. The all-time top scorer in the fixture is Benfica's Fernando Peyroteo-era rivalries notwithstanding, the derby remains the centrepiece of the Lisbon football calendar.
