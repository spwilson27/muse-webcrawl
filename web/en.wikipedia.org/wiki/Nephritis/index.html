Nephritis

Nephritis is inflammation of the kidneys and may involve the glomeruli, tubules, or the surrounding interstitial tissue. It is one of several different types of kidney disease.

Types

  • Glomerulonephritis – inflammation of the glomeruli, the filtering units of the kidney
  • Interstitial nephritis – inflammation of the spaces between the renal tubules, often caused by drugs or infection
  • Pyelonephritis – inflammation usually resulting from a urinary tract infection that has reached the renal pelvis
  • Lupus nephritis – kidney involvement in systemic lupus erythematosus

Signs and diagnosis

Symptoms may include reduced urine output, blood in the urine (hematuria), proteinuria, swelling (edema) and high blood pressure. Diagnosis relies on blood and urine tests, imaging and often kidney biopsy. Treatment depends on the cause and may involve antibiotics, corticosteroids or other immunosuppressants; untreated nephritis can progress to kidney failure.
