Ryan Choi is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Grant Morrison, Gail Simone, and John Byrne, the character first appeared in DCU: Brave New World #1 (2006) and succeeded Ray Palmer as the fourth Atom, a hero able to shrink to subatomic size.

Fictional character biography

Ryan Choi was born in Hong Kong and became a brilliant young physics professor at Ivy University in Ivy Town, where he had long corresponded with Ray Palmer, his childhood hero. After Palmer disappeared following the events of Identity Crisis, Choi discovered Palmer's size-changing bio-belt and took up the mantle of the Atom, starring in the series The All-New Atom written by Gail Simone. His adventures involved bizarre threats including the microscopic alien civilization of the "Waiting" and the machinations of his colleague Chronos.

In the Titans storyline, Choi was murdered by Deathstroke's Titans East team, a controversial death that was later revisited. Following the DC Rebirth relaunch in 2016, Choi was reintroduced as a graduate student working with Palmer, restoring the character to continuity.

In other media

Ryan Choi appears in the Arrowverse crossover event "Crisis on Infinite Earths" (2019–2020), portrayed by Osric Chau, as a scientist at Ivy Town University recruited as the Paragon of Humanity. The character was also intended to appear in Zack Snyder's Justice League (2021), played by Zheng Kai, where he is depicted as a S.T.A.R. Labs scientist; his role was expanded in the director's cut of the film.
