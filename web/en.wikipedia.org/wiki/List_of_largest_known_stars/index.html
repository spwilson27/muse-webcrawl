List of largest known stars

The following is a list of the largest known stars, ordered by radius relative to the Sun (solar radii, R☉). The largest stars are red supergiants and red hypergiants — highly evolved, massive stars whose outer layers have expanded enormously. Precise radii are difficult to measure: estimates depend on the assumed distance, the definition of the stellar "surface" in an extended atmosphere, and the star's pulsation, so figures vary between studies and the ranking of individual stars changes over time.

Well-known examples include UY Scuti, once widely cited as the largest known star, VY Canis Majoris, Stephenson 2-18, NML Cygni, WOH G64 (in the Large Magellanic Cloud), Betelgeuse and Antares. Stephenson 2-18 is frequently listed at or near the top, with an estimated radius of roughly 2,150 R☉, though its parameters are uncertain.

If placed at the centre of the Solar System, the largest of these stars would engulf the orbits of the inner planets and reach close to, or beyond, the orbit of Jupiter or even Saturn.

Measurement issues

Stellar radii are derived from angular diameters (often measured by interferometry), luminosity and effective temperature. Red supergiants have no sharp surface; their extended, dusty envelopes and large-amplitude pulsations produce substantial uncertainty. Different catalogues therefore order the top stars differently, and former record-holders such as UY Scuti have been revised downward when better distances (for example from Gaia) became available.
