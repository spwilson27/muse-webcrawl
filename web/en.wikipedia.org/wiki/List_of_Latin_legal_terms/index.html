List of Latin legal terms

A number of Latin terms are used in legal terminology and principles in legal systems around the world. These are some of the most common.

TermLiteral meaningUsage
actus reusguilty actThe physical element of a crime
mens reaguilty mindThe mental element of a crime
habeas corpusyou shall have the bodyWrit requiring a person to be brought before a court
stare decisisto stand by decisionsDoctrine of precedent
ratio decidendithe reason for the decisionThe binding part of a judgment
obiter dictumsaid in passingRemarks in a judgment not essential to the decision
prima facieat first sightEvidence sufficient unless rebutted
bona fidein good faithGenuine; acting honestly
mala fidein bad faithActing dishonestly or fraudulently
de factoin factExisting in practice, regardless of law
de jurein lawExisting by legal right
in camerain chambersProceedings held in private
sub judiceunder judgmentA matter currently before the court
pro bonofor the goodProfessional work done free of charge
amicus curiaefriend of the courtA non-party offering information to a court
corpus delictibody of the crimeFacts proving a crime was committed
res ipsa loquiturthe thing speaks for itselfNegligence inferred from the accident itself
quid pro quosomething for somethingA reciprocal exchange
nolo contendereI do not wish to contendA plea accepting conviction without admitting guilt
voir direto speak the truthPreliminary questioning of jurors or witnesses
sui generisof its own kindUnique; in a class of its own
ultra viresbeyond the powersActing beyond legal authority
ex partefrom one partyApplication made by one side only
inter partesbetween the partiesProceedings involving all parties
in remagainst a thingJurisdiction over property
in personamagainst a personJurisdiction over a person
certiorarito be made certainOrder of a higher court to review a lower court's case
mandamuswe commandOrder compelling performance of a public duty
nolle prosequito be unwilling to pursueProsecution's decision not to proceed
affidavithe has swornA written sworn statement
