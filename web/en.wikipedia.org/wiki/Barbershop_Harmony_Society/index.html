The Barbershop Harmony Society, legally the Society for the Preservation and Encouragement of Barber Shop Quartet Singing in America, Inc. (SPEBSQSA), is the largest all-male singing organization in the world, founded in 1938 in Tulsa, Oklahoma, by Owen C. Cash and Rupert I. Hall. It promotes barbershop harmony — close four-part a cappella singing in the lead, tenor, baritone, and bass voicings — through local chapters, choruses, quartets, contests, and music education.

Activities

The Society holds an annual international convention and quartet and chorus contests, judged on music, performance, and singing. Its Harmony University programme trains singers, directors, and arrangers. In the late 2010s the organization moved under its "Everyone in Harmony" initiative to admit women as members and to welcome mixed ensembles.

Related organizations

Sweet Adelines International and Harmony, Incorporated serve women's barbershop singing, while affiliated societies exist in the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Scandinavia, and elsewhere.
