NERIT

NERIT (New Hellenic Radio, Internet and Television; Greek: Νέα Ελληνική Ραδιοφωνία, Ίντερνετ και Τηλεόραση, Néa Ellinikí Radiofonía, Ínternet kai Tileórasi) was the Greek public service broadcaster from 2013 to 2015. It was established by the government of Antonis Samaras following the abrupt closure of the previous broadcaster, ERT, in June 2013.

History

NERIT began television broadcasts in May 2014, operating channels including NERIT 1 and NERIT Sports and several radio stations, with a reduced staff compared to its predecessor. Its creation was controversial, with many former ERT employees continuing occupation broadcasts and regarding NERIT as an illegitimate successor. In 2015, the new government of Alexis Tsipras legislated to revive ERT, and NERIT ceased operations on 11 June 2015 when ERT returned to air.
