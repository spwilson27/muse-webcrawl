Star number

A star number is a centred figurate number that represents a centred hexagram (six-pointed star), such as the Star of David. The n-th star number is given by the formula 6n(n − 1) + 1.

Sequence

The first star numbers are 1, 13, 37, 73, 121, 181, 253, 337, 433, and 541. Every star number except the first is divisible... the digital root of a star number is always 1 or 4. Star numbers that are also triangular numbers, and those that are prime, have been studied as curiosities in recreational mathematics. The pair 13 and 37 are notable: 13 is both a star number and appears in the centred figures formed by the Chinese checkers board, which has 121 holes — also a star number.
