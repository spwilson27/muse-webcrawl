National Assembly of People's Power
Asamblea Nacional del Poder Popular
Type: Unicameral legislature
Seats: 605
Meeting place: Palacio de las Convenciones, Havana

The National Assembly of People's Power (Spanish: Asamblea Nacional del Poder Popular) is the supreme organ of state power and the legislative parliament of the Republic of Cuba. It is the only body with constituent and legislative authority in the country.

Composition and elections

The Assembly is composed of deputies elected for five-year terms by municipal assemblies and by direct vote in elections in which candidates are nominated by mass organizations rather than by the Communist Party directly. The Assembly meets for brief sessions twice a year; between sessions, its powers are exercised by the Council of State, elected from among its members.

Powers

The National Assembly elects the President of Cuba and the Council of State, approves laws and constitutional amendments, and ratifies the national budget and development plans. Under the 2019 constitution, the Assembly remains the supreme body of state power.
