President Coriolanus Snow is a fictional character in The Hunger Games franchise by Suzanne Collins. He is the tyrannical president of Panem and the main antagonist of the original trilogy, ruling the Capitol and the twelve districts with an iron fist and using the annual Hunger Games to keep the districts in line.

Snow is characterised by his signature white roses, which mask the smell of blood from mouth sores caused by a poison he once drank from the same cup as a victim to deflect suspicion. He is manipulative, calculating, and insists that he never lies, presenting his threats as plain statements of fact.

In the novels

In The Hunger Games and Catching Fire, Snow pressures Katniss Everdeen to quell the rebellion her actions have inspired, threatening those she loves. In Mockingjay, he is captured after the fall of the Capitol; he reveals to Katniss that the bomb that killed her sister Prim was sent by rebel leader Alma Coin, not by him. He is found dead shortly afterwards, having laughed and choked — or been trampled — during Coin's assassination and the ensuing chaos.

Prequel

The 2020 prequel novel The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes follows the eighteen-year-old Snow during the 10th Hunger Games, when he is assigned as mentor to the District 12 tribute Lucy Gray Baird. Orphaned and impoverished by the war despite his family's former wealth, Snow's ambition, his romance with Lucy Gray, and his service under the gamemaker Dr. Volumnia Gaul trace his descent into the ruthless ruler of the trilogy. A film adaptation was released in 2023, with Tom Blyth as the young Snow.
