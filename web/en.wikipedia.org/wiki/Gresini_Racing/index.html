Gresini Racing

Gresini Racing is a motorcycle racing team from Italy that competes in the MotoGP World Championship. It was founded in 1997 by Fausto Gresini, himself a two-time 125cc world champion, and is based in Faenza. The team has competed in the premier class as a satellite operation of Honda, Aprilia and Ducati at different periods of its history.

History

Gresini entered Grand Prix racing in the 500cc class in 1997. Over the following decades its riders included Alex Barros, Daijiro Kato, Sete Gibernau, Marco Melandri, Toni Elías and Marco Simoncelli. The team endured tragedies: Kato died after a crash at Suzuka in 2003, and Simoncelli was killed at the Malaysian Grand Prix in 2011. From 2015 to 2021 Gresini ran Aprilia's factory effort in MotoGP. Fausto Gresini died in February 2021 from complications of COVID-19, after which his widow Nadia Padovani took over the team. From 2022 Gresini returned to independent status running Ducati machinery, achieving wins with Enea Bastianini, and in 2024 fielded Marc Márquez, whose victories returned the team to the front of the championship. The team has also won world titles in the Moto3 and Moto2 classes and in the MotoE World Cup.
