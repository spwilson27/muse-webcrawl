Real Madrid CF

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Real Madrid CF
Founded: 1902, Madrid, Spain
Stadium: Santiago Bernabéu
Honours: 15 European Cups/Champions Leagues; record La Liga titles

Real Madrid Club de Fútbol is a Spanish professional football club based in Madrid, founded in 1902. It is one of the most successful and valuable sports clubs in the world, named by FIFA as the best club of the twentieth century.

Under president Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid won the first five European Cups (1956–60) with a team built around Alfredo Di Stéfano and Ferenc Puskás. Later eras include the "Quinta del Buitre" of the 1980s, the Galácticos policy of president Florentino Pérez — signing Zinedine Zidane, Ronaldo, David Beckham and later Cristiano Ronaldo and Gareth Bale — and Carlo Ancelotti's sides, which extended the club's record to fifteen European titles by 2024. Their rivalry with FC Barcelona, El Clásico, is the most-watched club fixture in world football.

Unlike most giants, Madrid is owned by its members (socios). The renovated Bernabéu, with its wraparound shell and retractable roof, reopened in stages from 2023 as a year-round entertainment venue emblematic of modern football economics.
