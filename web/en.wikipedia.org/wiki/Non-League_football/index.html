Non-League football

Non-League football describes association football in England played at levels below the 92 clubs of the Premier League and the English Football League (EFL). The term does not imply amateur status — many non-League clubs are professional or semi-professional — but simply that they sit outside "the League".

Structure

Below the EFL, the National League forms Step 1 of the National League System, followed by the National League North and South at Step 2 and the regional feeder leagues (Northern Premier League, Southern League and Isthmian League) at Steps 3 and 4, with county and regional leagues below. Promotion and relegation link the system to the EFL: two clubs are promoted from the National League each season.

Cups and culture

Non-League clubs enter the FA Cup, in which they occasionally defeat league opposition in famous "giant-killings", and compete in the FA Trophy (for higher steps) and FA Vase (for lower steps). The culture is associated with small grounds where spectators can stand pitchside, affordable admission, and strong volunteer traditions. "Non-League Day", held during an international break, encourages fans of big clubs to visit their local non-League side.
