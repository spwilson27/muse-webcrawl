Matlock Bath

Matlock Bath
Location: Derbyshire, England
River: Derwent
Known for: Spa resort, Heights of Abraham

Matlock Bath is a village and civil parish in Derbyshire, England, in the Derwent gorge south of Matlock. Developed as a spa resort in the late 17th and 18th centuries around its warm springs, it became a fashionable destination visited by Lord Byron, who compared it to Switzerland.

Attractions

The Heights of Abraham, reached by cable car, offers show caverns (former lead mines) and views over the gorge. Other attractions include the Peak District Mining Museum, an aquarium, and the annual Illuminations along the river. The village has a seaside-resort character despite being inland, popular with motorcyclists.

Transport

Matlock Bath station lies on the Derwent Valley line to Derby.
