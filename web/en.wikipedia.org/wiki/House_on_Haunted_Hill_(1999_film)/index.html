House on Haunted Hill (1999 film)

DirectorWilliam Malone
Released1999
StarringGeoffrey Rush, Famke Janssen, Taye Diggs, Ali Larter, Chris Kattan

House on Haunted Hill is a 1999 American supernatural horror film directed by William Malone, a remake of the 1959 William Castle film of the same name. It was the first production of Dark Castle Entertainment.

Plot

Amusement-park magnate Stephen Price (Geoffrey Rush, styled after Vincent Price's original role) offers five strangers one million dollars each to survive a night in a former asylum for the criminally insane, where a sadistic doctor once experimented on patients. The staged scares are overtaken by genuine supernatural forces, and the guests begin to die.

Reception

Produced for about 19 million dollars, the film grossed roughly 65 million worldwide. Reviews were generally negative, though its production design and opening roller-coaster sequence were praised. A direct-to-video sequel, Return to House on Haunted Hill, followed in 2007.
