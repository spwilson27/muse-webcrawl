Second Happy Time
Date: January – August 1942
Location: East coast of North America, Caribbean, Gulf of Mexico
Part of: Battle of the Atlantic

The Second Happy Time (German: Zweite glückliche Zeit) was the period in the Battle of the Atlantic from January to about August 1942 during which German U-boats achieved great success against merchant shipping off the eastern seaboard of North America. It followed the United States' entry into the Second World War and the launch of Operation Drumbeat (Paukenschlag).

Background and course

American anti-submarine preparations were initially inadequate: shipping sailed unescorted and unblacked-out coastal cities silhouetted vessels at night, making them easy targets. U-boats sank hundreds of ships — roughly two million tons in the first months — for very few losses. Losses fell as the United States adopted coastal convoys, air patrols and blackout measures from the spring of 1942, and the U-boats shifted to the Caribbean and Gulf of Mexico before the "happy time" finally ended.
