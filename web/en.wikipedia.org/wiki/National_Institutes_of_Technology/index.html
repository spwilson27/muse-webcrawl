National Institutes of Technology

Number: 31 institutes
Country: India
Established: From 1959 (as RECs)
Governing act: NITSER Act, 2007
Admission: JEE Main

The National Institutes of Technology (NITs) are a group of 31 public technical universities in India, declared Institutes of National Importance by Parliament. They are among the country's premier engineering institutions, second in prestige only to the Indian Institutes of Technology.

History

Most NITs began as Regional Engineering Colleges, founded from 1959 onward as joint ventures between the Government of India and state governments. Between 2002 and 2007 they were converted into NITs with greater autonomy, and the National Institutes of Technology Act of 2007 (later the NITSER Act) brought them under a common statutory framework.

Admissions and academics

Undergraduate admission is through the JEE Main examination, with seat allocation by the Joint Seat Allocation Authority; half of each institute's seats are reserved for students from its home state. NITs such as Tiruchirappalli, Surathkal, Warangal and Rourkela consistently rank among India's top ten engineering schools and produce large numbers of graduates for industry and research.
