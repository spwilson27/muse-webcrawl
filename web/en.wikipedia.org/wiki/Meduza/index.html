Meduza (Russian: Медуза) is a Russian- and English-language independent news website based in Riga, Latvia. It was founded in 2014 by Galina Timchenko, the former editor-in-chief of the Russian online newspaper Lenta.ru, together with a group of journalists who left Lenta after Timchenko's dismissal.

Meduza was established in Latvia in part to protect editorial independence from pressure within Russia. It covers Russian and world news, publishes long-form reporting, and produces podcasts and an English-language edition with reporting and newsletters aimed at international readers.

History

After Timchenko was removed as editor of Lenta.ru in March 2014 and much of the editorial staff resigned in protest, the team regrouped in Riga and launched Meduza in October 2014. The outlet grew rapidly, becoming one of the most-read independent Russian-language media sources.

Status in Russia

In April 2021, the Russian authorities designated Meduza a "foreign agent," a label that severely affected its advertising revenue; the outlet responded by appealing to readers for donations. In January 2023, Russia declared Meduza an "undesirable organization," effectively banning its work in the country and exposing anyone cooperating with it to prosecution. Its website is blocked in Russia, though it continues to reach readers inside the country through apps, newsletters, and mirror distribution channels.
