Preston North End Football Club, commonly referred to as Preston, North End or PNE, is a professional football club in Preston, Lancashire, England. The club was a founding member of the Football League in 1888 and won the first two league championships. Since the early 1960s the club has played outside the top flight, competing in recent decades mainly in the second tier, the EFL Championship. Its home ground, Deepdale, has been in continuous use by the club since 1878 and is recognised as one of the oldest continuously used football league grounds in the world.

History

The Invincibles

Preston North End began as a cricket club in 1863 and adopted association football in 1878–1880. Under manager William Sudell, the club assembled a formidable side, many of them Scottish players ("Scotch professors"), and in the inaugural Football League season of 1888–89 went through the entire league campaign unbeaten — 18 wins and 4 draws — while also winning the FA Cup without conceding a goal, completing the first "Double". The team became known as "The Invincibles", a nickname later associated with Arsenal's unbeaten 2003–04 side. Preston retained the championship in 1889–90, finished runners-up three further times in the 1890s, and won the FA Cup again in 1938, beating Huddersfield Town 1–0 with a penalty in the last minute of extra time.

Post-war years and decline

In the 1950s Preston were a strong First Division side built around Tom Finney, one of the greatest English players of his era, who spent his entire career at the club, and contested the 1954 FA Cup Final, losing to West Bromwich Albion. Following Finney's retirement the club was relegated in 1961 and has not returned to the top division since, dropping as low as the Fourth Division in the 1980s.

Recent history

Under David Moyes, Preston won the third-tier title in 2000 and reached the second-tier play-off final in 2001; under Billy Davies they reached the 2005 play-off final, losing to West Ham United. The club was relegated to League One in 2011 and returned to the Championship via the play-offs in 2015, and has remained a mostly mid-table Championship side since.

Honours

  • Football League First Division champions: 1888–89, 1889–90
  • FA Cup winners: 1889, 1938
  • Football League Second Division champions: 1903–04, 1912–13, 1950–51
  • Third tier champions: 1970–71, 1999–2000; play-off winners 2015
