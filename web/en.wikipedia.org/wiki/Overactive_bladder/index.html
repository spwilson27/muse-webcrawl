Overactive bladder

Overactive bladder (OAB) is a common condition where there is a frequent feeling of the need to urinate to a degree that it negatively affects a person's life. Symptoms include urinary urgency, frequency, nocturia, and sometimes urge incontinence.

Causes can involve detrusor overactivity and neurological or idiopathic factors. Treatment includes bladder training, pelvic floor exercises, anticholinergic or beta-3 agonist medications, and in refractory cases botulinum toxin or neuromodulation.
