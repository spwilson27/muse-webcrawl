Portal: Current events

Current events is a Wikipedia portal that presents news stories and ongoing events in a structured, encyclopedic format. It serves as a gateway to articles about recent developments in politics, science, culture, sport, armed conflicts, and disasters, updated daily by volunteer editors.

In the news

  • Ongoing conflicts and elections are summarised with links to in-depth articles.
  • Recent deaths of notable people are listed.
  • Major sporting events, spaceflights, and scientific announcements are featured.

How it works

Each day has its own page, archived by month and year. Editors add sourced items, usually drawn from reliable news media, and link them to background articles so readers can understand context rather than only headlines. The portal follows Wikipedia policies on neutrality, verifiability, and biographies of living persons.

Topics

Recurring sections include armed conflicts and attacks, arts and culture, business and economy, disasters and accidents, health and environment, international relations, law and crime, politics and elections, science and technology, and sports.
