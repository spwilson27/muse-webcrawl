Special:Random

Special:Random is a special page on Wikipedia that redirects the reader to a randomly selected article. It is one of the best-known ways to discover unexpected topics and to sample the breadth of the encyclopedia.

Usage

Visiting the page, or clicking Random article in the sidebar, loads an article chosen at random from the main namespace. Variants can select random pages in other namespaces, random files, categories, or redirects. Readers use it for browsing and games; editors use it for quality checks, cleanup, and finding articles needing attention.

Implementation

MediaWiki, the software that runs Wikipedia, assigns each page a random value used to pick a page efficiently from millions of entries. The feature has existed since Wikipedia's early years and inspired similar random-article features on other wikis and reference sites.
