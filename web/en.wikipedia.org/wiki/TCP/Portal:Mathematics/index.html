Portal: Mathematics

The Mathematics portal introduces Wikipedia's coverage of mathematics: the science of structure, order, and relation that evolved from counting, calculation, measurement, and the study of shapes and motion.

Selected article

Mathematics includes algebra, geometry, calculus, number theory, probability, statistics, logic, and topology. Mathematicians seek patterns, formulate conjectures, and establish truth by rigorous deduction from axioms and definitions.

Areas

  • Foundations: logic, set theory, category theory
  • Number theory: primes, Diophantine equations, conjectures such as the Tate conjecture in arithmetic geometry
  • Algebra: groups, rings, fields, linear algebra
  • Analysis: calculus, real and complex analysis, differential equations
  • Geometry and topology
  • Applied mathematics: statistics, optimisation, mathematical physics, cryptography

Did you know?

The word mathematics comes from the Greek mathēmatikos, meaning inclined to learn. Many results once considered purely abstract, such as number theory, now underpin modern cryptography and computing.
