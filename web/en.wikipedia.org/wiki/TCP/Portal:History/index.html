Portal: History

The History portal is a gateway to Wikipedia's coverage of the human past, from prehistory and the ancient world through the medieval and early modern periods to contemporary history.

Selected article

History is the study and documentation of the past, focused on written records and other evidence. Events before writing are considered prehistory. Historians use sources such as documents, artefacts, chronicles, and oral traditions, and debate interpretations in historiography.

Branches and periods

  • Ancient history, classical antiquity, and archaeology
  • Medieval history and the Byzantine and Islamic worlds
  • Early modern history, revolutions, and industrialisation
  • Modern history, world wars, decolonisation, and the Cold War
  • History by region: Africa, Asia, Europe, the Americas, and Oceania

Featured topics

The portal highlights selected biographies, battles, empires, and anniversaries, and maintains lists of historians and historical journals.
