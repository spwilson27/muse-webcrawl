Wikipedia: Contact us

This project page explains how readers, article subjects, journalists, and organisations can contact Wikipedia and the Wikimedia Foundation, and which channel suits each kind of question.

For readers

Questions about article content are best asked on the article's talk page or at the Help Desk and Reference Desk. Because Wikipedia is written by volunteers, there is no central editorial office that assigns or approves articles.

For article subjects

People written about in articles can request corrections. Straightforward factual errors can be raised on the talk page; sensitive problems — such as serious errors in a biography of a living person, defamation, or private information — can be emailed to the volunteer response team (OTRS/VRT).

Legal and press

Copyright and licensing questions, legal notices, and press enquiries are directed to the Wikimedia Foundation, the non-profit host. Contact details differ for donors, who use a dedicated donation support channel.
