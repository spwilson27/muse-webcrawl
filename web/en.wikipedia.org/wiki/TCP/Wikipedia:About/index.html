Wikipedia: About

Wikipedia is a free online encyclopedia, created and edited by volunteers around the world and hosted by the Wikimedia Foundation, a non-profit organisation. This project page explains what Wikipedia is, how it works, and how to use and contribute to it.

Overview

Launched on 15 January 2001 by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger, Wikipedia has grown into one of the most visited websites in the world, with editions in more than 300 languages. Articles are written collaboratively; anyone can edit most pages, and changes are visible immediately.

Principles

Wikipedia's content is governed by core policies: neutral point of view, verifiability through reliable sources, and no original research. Content is freely licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike, so it can be reused and adapted with attribution.

Who runs it

The Wikimedia Foundation operates the servers and fundraising, while editorial decisions rest with the volunteer community of editors and elected administrators. Sister projects include Wiktionary, Wikimedia Commons, Wikinews, Wikibooks, and Wikidata.
