Portal: Technology

The Technology portal introduces Wikipedia's coverage of technology: the application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes, especially in industry, computing, engineering, transport, and communication.

Selected article

Technology ranges from simple tools to complex systems such as the Internet, whose core protocols include the Transmission Control Protocol and the User Datagram Protocol, and services such as SMTP for electronic mail.
