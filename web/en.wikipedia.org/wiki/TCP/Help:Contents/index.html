Project: Wikipedia help pages
Purpose: Guidance for readers and editors

Help:Contents is the main help page of Wikipedia, directing readers and editors to documentation on searching, reading, editing, citing and contributing to the free encyclopedia.

Reading

Help covers searching Wikipedia, using article features such as references, tables of contents, categories, and other languages, and accessing content on mobile devices and offline.

Editing

New editors are guided to tutorials on creating an account, the VisualEditor and source editor, formatting, links, images, citations, creating new articles, and Wikipedia's core policies: neutral point of view, verifiability and no original research. Community help is available at the Teahouse and Help Desk.
