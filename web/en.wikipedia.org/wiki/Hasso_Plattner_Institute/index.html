Hasso Plattner Institute

The Hasso Plattner Institute (Hasso-Plattner-Institut für Digital Engineering gGmbH, abbreviated HPI) is a German institute for information technology and a faculty of the University of Potsdam. Located in Potsdam-Babelsberg near Berlin, it was founded in 1998 by Hasso Plattner, co-founder of the software company SAP, and is financed entirely through his foundation.

The HPI campus in Potsdam-Babelsberg.

Studies

HPI offers tuition-free bachelor's and master's degrees in IT systems engineering and related digital-engineering subjects, in close cooperation with the University of Potsdam, which awards the degrees. Admission is selective, and teaching emphasises small-group, project-based work, including the bachelor project carried out with industry partners. The affiliated HPI School of Design Thinking, founded in 2007, was the first institution of its kind in Europe, modelled on the d.school at Stanford University.

Research and outreach

Research groups at HPI work on topics including cybersecurity, data engineering, human-computer interaction, internet technologies, and artificial intelligence. The institute runs openHPI, a massive open online course platform launched in 2012, offering free interactive courses on computing topics to a worldwide audience, and operates the annual German national digital summit activities and youth computer-science competitions.
