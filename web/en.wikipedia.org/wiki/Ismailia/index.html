Ismailia

Ismailia (Arabic: الإسماعيلية) is a city in north-eastern Egypt, situated on the west bank of the Suez Canal at Lake Timsah, roughly midway between Port Said to the north and Suez to the south. It is the capital of the Ismailia Governorate and has a population of around 430,000 (with close to 1.5 million in the governorate).

Ismailia lies on Lake Timsah beside the Suez Canal.

History

The city was founded in 1863 by the Khedive Isma'il Pasha, after whom it is named, during the construction of the Suez Canal, and served as the headquarters of the canal works. Ismailia hosted the headquarters of the Suez Canal Authority. The city was evacuated during the wars with Israel (1967–1973) and repopulated after the canal reopened in 1975.

Economy and culture

Known for its gardens and relatively unhurried pace, Ismailia is nicknamed the "City of Beauty and Enchantment". It is home to Suez Canal University and the football club Ismaily SC, one of Egypt's major teams.
