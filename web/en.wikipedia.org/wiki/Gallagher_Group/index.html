Gallagher Group Limited is a privately held New Zealand engineering and manufacturing company headquartered in Hamilton, in the Waikato region. Founded in 1938, the company is a global leader in electric fencing and animal management systems, and also manufactures electronic security systems and fuel dispensing equipment.

The company began with Bill Gallagher Sr., who devised an electric fence system in the 1930s to stop his horse scratching itself on his car, using an ignition coil to deliver a pulse to a fence wire. The invention was commercialised, and under his son Sir William Gallagher the firm expanded internationally from the 1970s onward. Gallagher's electric fencing energizers and weighing and electronic identification (EID) systems for livestock are used in more than 100 countries.

Divisions

Gallagher operates three main businesses: Animal Management (electric fencing, weighing and EID), Security (access control and perimeter security, sold under the Gallagher Security brand and used at government and commercial sites worldwide), and Fuel Systems (fuel pumps and forecourt equipment, largely in Australasia).
