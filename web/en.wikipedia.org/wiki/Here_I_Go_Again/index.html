Here I Go Again

"Here I Go Again" is a song by the English hard rock band Whitesnake. Written by lead singer David Coverdale and guitarist Bernie Marsden, it was first released on the band's 1982 album Saints & Sinners.

1987 version

Coverdale re-recorded the song for Whitesnake's 1987 self-titled album; the new version, featuring a more polished production and guitar by John Sykes and Adrian Vandenberg, became the band's only number-one single on the US Billboard Hot 100, reaching the top in October 1987. Its promotional video, featuring actress Tawny Kitaen, became an MTV staple.

Legacy

The song, with its chorus about walking alone "like a drifter," is regarded as Whitesnake's signature song and a staple of 1980s rock radio. The lyric was slightly altered between versions, with "hobo" in the original replaced by "drifter" in 1987.
