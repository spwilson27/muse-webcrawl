List of largest shopping malls in the United States

This list ranks the largest enclosed shopping malls in the United States by total retail floor area. The United States was the birthplace of the modern enclosed mall, with the first fully enclosed, climate-controlled centre, Southdale Center, opening in Minnesota in 1956.
