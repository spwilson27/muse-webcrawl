List of manga magazines

Manga magazines are periodicals, usually published weekly or monthly in Japan, that serialise manga chapters by many different authors in a single thick issue printed on cheap paper. Magazines are typically aimed at a specific demographic: shōnen (boys), shōjo (girls), seinen (adult men) and josei (adult women). Successful series are later collected into bound volumes (tankōbon). The best-selling manga magazine of all time is Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump, whose circulation peaked at over 6.5 million copies per week in the mid-1990s.

Magazines outside Japan

English-language anthologies have included Viz Media's Shonen Jump (print 2003–2012, then digital), Shojo Beat, and Dark Horse and Tokyopop anthologies. Many publishers now release chapters simultaneously online through platforms such as Manga Plus and the digital Weekly Shōnen Jump/Shonen Jump app.
