Mission: Impossible is a 1996 American action spy film directed by Brian De Palma and produced by and starring Tom Cruise. Based on the 1966 television series of the same name, it follows IMF agent Ethan Hunt, who is framed for the murder of his team during a botched mission in Prague and must uncover the real mole while evading his own agency.

Plot

After his team is killed during an operation to recover a stolen list of undercover agents, Hunt becomes the prime suspect. He assembles a team of disavowed agents, breaks into CIA headquarters at Langley to steal the genuine list, and lures the mole into the open in a climax aboard a TGV train in the Channel Tunnel.

Reception

The film grossed over $457 million worldwide, becoming the third-highest-grossing film of 1996. It launched the long-running Mission: Impossible film series, and its scenes — notably the silent heist in the CIA vault and the Lalo Schifrin-derived theme as arranged in 5/4 time — became iconic.
