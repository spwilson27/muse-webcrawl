Hydra Head Records

Hydra Head Records was an American independent record label specialising in extreme and experimental metal, founded in 1993 by Aaron Turner, frontman of the post-metal band Isis. Based at various times in New Mexico, Boston and Los Angeles, the label became one of the defining imprints of underground metal in the 1990s and 2000s.

History and roster

Turner started Hydra Head while still a teenager, initially as a distribution effort that grew into a label. Its roster included many of the most influential acts in sludge metal, doom metal, grindcore and post-metal: Botch, Converge, Cave In, Pelican, Khanate, Boris, Jesu, Neurosis-related projects, Agoraphobic Nosebleed, and Turner's own bands Isis and Old Man Gloom. The label was known for elaborate, artful packaging, often designed by Turner himself, and for a sister imprint, Tortuga Recordings.

Closure

In September 2012 Turner announced that Hydra Head would cease releasing new music, citing financial difficulties and his own waning enthusiasm for running a label, while continuing to sell and reissue its back catalogue. The catalogue has remained available, with occasional reissues and benefit releases following the announcement.
