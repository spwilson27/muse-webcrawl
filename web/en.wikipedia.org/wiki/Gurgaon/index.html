Gurgaon, officially renamed Gurugram in 2016, is a city in the Indian state of Haryana, within the National Capital Region of Delhi. From a small agricultural town it has grown into one of India's leading financial and technology hubs.

Development

Growth accelerated in the 1980s when Maruti Suzuki established a plant, and exploded after the late 1990s when firms such as Genpact and many multinational corporations built offices there, taking advantage of proximity to Indira Gandhi International Airport and relatively liberal Haryana land policies. The privately developed DLF Cyber City became a landmark IT park.

Issues

The city's largely private, unplanned growth produced gleaming towers alongside chronic deficits in public infrastructure: waterlogging during monsoon rains, groundwater depletion, traffic congestion and unreliable municipal services. Gurgaon is connected to Delhi by the Delhi Metro Yellow Line and the Rapid Metro.
