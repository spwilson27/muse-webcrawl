Master labor agreement

A master labor agreement (or master contract) is a collective bargaining agreement that covers a large number of workers under common terms, often negotiated by an international or national union with a major employer or employers' association, and supplemented by local agreements on site-specific issues.

Use

Master agreements are common in industries such as automobiles, steel, entertainment, and construction, where they standardise wages, benefits, and working conditions across many plants or productions while local supplements handle scheduling and plant-level matters.

Relation to project labor agreements

In construction, related project labor agreements set terms for a specific project. Disputes over whether an issue falls under the master agreement or a local supplement are resolved through grievance and arbitration procedures.
