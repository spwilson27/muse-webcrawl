Football Club Internazionale Milano, commonly referred to as Inter Milan or simply Inter, is an Italian professional football club based in Milan, Lombardy. Founded in 1908 following a schism within the Milan Cricket and Football Club (now AC Milan), Inter is the only Italian side to have always competed in the top flight since its debut season.

The club has won 20 Serie A titles, nine Coppa Italia trophies, and eight Supercoppa Italiana titles. On the European stage Inter has won the European Cup/UEFA Champions League three times (1964, 1965 and 2010) and the UEFA Cup three times. In 2010, under José Mourinho, Inter became the first and only Italian club to win a continental treble of league, cup and Champions League in one season.

History

Inter was founded after disagreements over the acceptance of foreign players — hence the name Internazionale. The club's colours are black and blue vertical stripes. Under coach Helenio Herrera in the 1960s, the "Grande Inter" side perfected the catenaccio system and won three league titles and two European Cups. Later eras of success came under Giovanni Trapattoni in 1989, Roberto Mancini in the 2000s, Mourinho's treble team of 2010, Antonio Conte in 2021, and Simone Inzaghi, whose side won the club's 20th league title in 2024, earning a second star on the badge.

Rivalries

Inter contest the Derby della Madonnina with city rivals AC Milan and the Derby d'Italia with Juventus. The club has one of the highest average attendances in Italian football.
