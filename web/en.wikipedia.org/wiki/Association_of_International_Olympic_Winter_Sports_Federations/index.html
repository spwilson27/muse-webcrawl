The Association of International Olympic Winter Sports Federations (AIOWF) is the collective body of the international federations governing the sports of the Olympic Winter Games. It represents its members' common interests in dealings with the International Olympic Committee and organising committees.

Members

Its member federations govern ice hockey (IIHF), skating (ISU), skiing and snowboarding (FIS), biathlon (IBU), bobsleigh and skeleton (IBSF), curling (World Curling) and luge (FIL). In recent years the AIOWF has been folded into the wider coordination of Olympic federations through the Association of International Olympic Sports Federations structures.

Role

The association coordinates calendars, discusses the distribution of Olympic revenues, and speaks for winter federations on issues such as the sports programme of future Winter Games.
