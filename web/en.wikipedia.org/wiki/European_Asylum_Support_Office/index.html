European Asylum Support Office

European Asylum Support Office
Abbreviation:EASO
Formation:2010 (operational 2011)
Headquarters:Valletta Harbour, Malta
Successor:European Union Agency for Asylum (EUAA)

The European Asylum Support Office (EASO) was an agency of the European Union established by Regulation (EU) No 439/2010 to enhance practical cooperation on asylum matters and to help member states fulfil their European and international obligations to give protection to people in need. It became operational in June 2011 and was based in Valletta, Malta.

Role

EASO supported member states whose asylum and reception systems were under particular pressure, organised training for asylum officials, collected and analysed country-of-origin information, and published statistics and reports on asylum in the EU. Its support teams were deployed notably in Greece and Italy during the European migrant crisis from 2015 onwards.

Transformation into the EUAA

On 19 January 2022 EASO was replaced by the European Union Agency for Asylum (EUAA), established by Regulation (EU) 2021/2303 with an expanded mandate, including a fundamental rights officer, a complaints mechanism, and a larger reserve pool of member state experts for operational support.
