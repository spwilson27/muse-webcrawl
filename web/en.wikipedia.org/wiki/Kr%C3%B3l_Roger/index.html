Król Roger (King Roger), Op. 46, is an opera in three acts by the Polish composer Karol Szymanowski, written between 1918 and 1924 and premiered in Warsaw in 1926. It is regarded as one of the masterpieces of 20th-century Polish opera.

Synopsis

Set in 12th-century Norman Sicily, the opera tells of King Roger II, whose Christian court is unsettled by the arrival of a mysterious Shepherd preaching a Dionysian gospel of beauty and pleasure. The Queen, Roxana, is drawn to the Shepherd, and in the final act Roger follows them to a ruined Greek theatre, greeting the rising sun transformed.

Music

The score blends lush late-Romantic harmony, Byzantine liturgical chant, Arabic colour and ecstatic orchestral writing, including the famous aria for Roxana in Act 2.
