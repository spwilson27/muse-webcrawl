Robot

Robot
DefinitionMachine that carries out tasks automatically, often programmable
Word originCzech robota (forced labour), via Čapek's play R.U.R. (1920)
[Image: A white humanoid robot waving beside an orange industrial robot arm]

A robot is a machine — especially one programmable by computer — that can carry out a complex series of actions automatically. Robots range from industrial arms welding cars, to surgical assistants, warehouse movers, vacuum cleaners, planetary rovers such as the Mars craft in this crawl's NASA pages, and humanoid research platforms.

Issues

Robotics combines mechanical and electrical engineering with computer science and AI. Automation displaces and creates jobs; safety standards govern human–robot collaboration; ethics debates address autonomous weapons and care robots. The word comes from Karel Čapek's 1920 play R.U.R., from Czech robota, forced labour.
