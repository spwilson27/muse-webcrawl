RFC

RFC is a three-letter initialism that may refer to:

Computing and technology

Request for Comments, a series of documents describing the Internet and Internet-connected systems, maintained by the Internet Engineering Task Force. The first RFC, RFC 1, was published in 1969 by Steve Crocker. RFCs include the core specifications of the Internet protocol suite, such as IP, TCP, HTTP, and SMTP, and are the standard reference for Internet engineering.

Military

Royal Flying Corps, the air arm of the British Army during the First World War, formed in 1912 and merged into the Royal Air Force in 1918.

Sport

Rugby football club, a common suffix in the names of rugby clubs (e.g., many British and Commonwealth clubs). Rangers F.C., a Scottish football club based in Glasgow, are nicknamed "the Gers" and their name is sometimes abbreviated RFC, as is that of other football clubs.

Other uses

Reconstruction Finance Corporation, a United States government corporation (1932–1957); Royal Forestry Commission; and various other organizations and terms.
