Simon Templar

Simon Templar, also known as The Saint, is a fictional character created by British author Leslie Charteris. He first appeared in the novel Meet the Tiger (1928) and became the hero of a long-running series of novels, novellas and short stories.

Templar is a wealthy adventurer, gentleman thief and amateur detective who steals from rich villains, helps the innocent, and leaves behind a distinctive calling card depicting a stick figure with a halo. His initials, S.T., inspired the nickname "The Saint".

Character

Templar is charming, witty, daring and driven by a personal code of justice. Charteris portrayed him variously as a former soldier and a man of independent means who travels the world righting wrongs outside the law. His companions include Patricia Holm, and he is frequently pursued by Inspector Claude Teal of Scotland Yard.

Adaptations

The Saint has been adapted for radio, film and television. The best-known portrayal is by Roger Moore in the 1960s ITV television series The Saint (1962–1969). A 1997 film starred Val Kilmer, and earlier films in the 1930s and 1940s starred actors including Louis Hayward and George Sanders.
