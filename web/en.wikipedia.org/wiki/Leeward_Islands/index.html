Leeward Islands

The Leeward Islands are a group of islands situated where the north-eastern Caribbean Sea meets the Atlantic Ocean. Starting at the Virgin Islands east of Puerto Rico, they extend southeast to Guadeloupe and its dependencies. In English, the term originally referred to the islands sheltered from the prevailing trade winds compared with the Windward Islands further south, though in French and other languages the grouping and division differ.

Geography

The principal islands of the group are the United States Virgin Islands, the British Virgin Islands, Anguilla, Saint Martin (divided between France and the Netherlands as Sint Maarten), Saint Barthélemy, Saba, Sint Eustatius, Saint Kitts, Nevis, Barbuda, Antigua, Montserrat, and Guadeloupe with its dependencies (La Désirade, Les Saintes and Marie-Galante). The islands are mostly low coral formations or the peaks of submerged volcanic mountains, and many are major tourist destinations.

History

The islands were inhabited by Arawak and then Carib peoples before European colonisation began in the seventeenth century, when England, France, the Netherlands, Spain and Denmark established competing colonies based on sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans. From 1671 to 1956 the British Leeward Islands formed a federal colony administered from Antigua, comprising Antigua, Barbuda, Saint Kitts, Nevis, Anguilla, Montserrat, the British Virgin Islands and (until 1940) Dominica.

Terminology

In sailing usage the "leeward" islands are those away from the wind: because the trade winds blow from the northeast, the Leeward Islands lie downwind of the Windward Islands for vessels arriving from the Atlantic. In French, both groups are simply the Îles du Vent in the northern Lesser Antilles terminology, while Spanish usage differs again, which can cause confusion between language editions.

Cricket

The Leeward Islands cricket team competes in West Indian domestic cricket and has produced players such as Viv Richards of Antigua, one of the greatest batsmen in the history of the game.
