Records

Demetrious Johnson holds the record for most consecutive title defences in UFC history with 11 (2012–2018). Anderson Silva defended the middleweight title 10 times. Jon Jones is widely regarded as one of the greatest fighters in the sport's history, holding championships at light heavyweight and heavyweight.
