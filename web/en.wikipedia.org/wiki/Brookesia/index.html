Brookesia

KingdomAnimalia
FamilyChamaeleonidae
GenusBrookesia Gray, 1864
DistributionMadagascar
Species~30

Brookesia is a genus of chameleons, lizards in the family Chamaeleonidae, endemic to Madagascar. They are among the smallest reptiles in the world: members are known collectively as leaf chameleons because of their drab brown coloration and leaf-litter habitat.

Description

Unlike the large, brightly coloured arboreal chameleons, Brookesia species are small, cryptic, and spend much of their time among dead leaves on the forest floor, climbing low vegetation only to sleep at night. The genus includes some of the world's smallest vertebrates: Brookesia nana, described in 2021, has a body length of about 22 mm in males, and Brookesia micra reaches about 29 mm total length.

Conservation

Many species have extremely restricted ranges in Madagascar's forests and are threatened by habitat loss from deforestation. Several are listed as endangered or critically endangered on the IUCN Red List.
