Australian Aboriginal Flag

The Australian Aboriginal Flag is a flag representing Aboriginal Australians. Designed in 1971 by Harold Thomas, a Lardil artist from Central Australia, it was first flown on National Aborigines Day in Adelaide on 12 July 1971.

Design and symbolism

The flag is divided horizontally into a black upper half and a red lower half, with a yellow circle in the centre. The black represents the Aboriginal people, the red represents the earth and the spiritual relationship to the land, and the yellow circle represents the sun, the giver of life.

History and status

The flag gained prominence during the Aboriginal Tent Embassy in Canberra (1972) and was officially proclaimed a "Flag of Australia" under the Flags Act 1953 in 1995. After years of copyright dispute over Thomas's ownership and licensing, the Australian Government purchased the copyright in January 2022 for over $20 million, making the flag freely available for public use.

The flag is flown permanently at many public buildings and is displayed alongside the national flag and the Torres Strait Islander Flag.
