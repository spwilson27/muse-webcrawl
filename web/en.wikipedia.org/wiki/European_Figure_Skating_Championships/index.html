European Figure Skating Championships

Image: European Championships skating (placeholder)

The European Figure Skating Championships is an annual figure skating competition sanctioned by the International Skating Union (ISU), in which skaters compete for the title of European champion in men's singles, women's singles, pair skating, and ice dance. First held in Hamburg in 1891 for men, it is the oldest annual championship in the sport.

History

The women's and pairs events were added in 1930, and ice dance in 1954. Eligibility is restricted to skaters from ISU member nations in Europe, with a limited exception historically for some non-European competitors in the earliest years. The championships are one of the four annual ISU championship events, alongside the World, Four Continents, and World Junior Championships.

Notable champions

Record holders include Ulrich Salchow and Karl Schäfer in men's skating, Irina Slutskaya and Katarina Witt in the women's event, and Irina Rodnina in pairs. Since the 2022–23 season, Russian and Belarusian skaters have been suspended from ISU events following the Russian invasion of Ukraine.
