Vänersborg
Country: Sweden
County: Västra Götaland
Province: Västergötland
Population: ~24,000

Vänersborg is a locality and the seat of Vänersborg Municipality in Västra Götaland County, Sweden, situated at the southern shore of Lake Vänern, Sweden's largest lake, near the outflow of the Göta älv river.

History

A market place existed here by the mid-17th century; Vänersborg received its town charter in 1644. It served as the county seat of Älvsborg County until the county merger of 1998. The town grew as a trading and administrative centre at the head of navigation on the Göta älv between Lake Vänern and Gothenburg, a route later developed into the Trollhättan Canal.

Geography

The town lies between the twin table mountains Halleberg and Hunneberg, famed in Swedish tradition for elk hunting — the area is sometimes called the home of the "King's hunt." The Vassbotten bay and the lake shore dominate the town's setting.

Culture

Vänersborg is known as the birthplace of poet and songwriter Birger Sjöberg, whose songs made "Frida" famous, and of composer and conductor. The Vänersborg Museum, founded in the 19th century, is one of Sweden's oldest regional museums.
