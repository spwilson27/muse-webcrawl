Hit Me Hard and Soft is the third studio album by American singer-songwriter Billie Eilish, released on 17 May 2024. It was written and produced with her brother Finneas O'Connell and conceived to be listened to in one sitting, in track order.

Music and themes

The album blends alternative pop and electropop, moving between quiet, intimate songs and intense, maximalist passages within single tracks such as "The Greatest" and "Chihiro." Themes include sexuality, body image, and youthful disorientation.

Singles and reception

"Lunch" was released as the lead single alongside the album, followed by "Birds of a Feather," which became a major international hit, and "Wildflower." The album debuted at number two on the US Billboard 200 and received widespread critical acclaim and multiple Grammy nominations.

Tour

Eilish supported the album with the Hit Me Hard and Soft: The Tour, beginning in September 2024. See also Billie Eilish.
