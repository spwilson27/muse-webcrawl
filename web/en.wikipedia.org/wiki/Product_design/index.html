Product design

Product design is the process of identifying a market opportunity, clearly defining the problem, developing a proper solution for that problem and validating the solution with real users. It overlaps with and draws on industrial design, user experience design, and engineering, and it spans both physical goods and digital products such as software applications.


Product design moves from research and sketches to prototypes and testing.

Process

Product design typically proceeds through research into user needs, ideation and concept generation, sketching and computer-aided design, prototyping, testing, and refinement for manufacture. Approaches such as design thinking emphasise empathy with users, iterative prototyping, and cross-functional collaboration between designers, engineers, and product managers.

Considerations

A successful product must balance function, aesthetics, ergonomics, cost, manufacturability, and sustainability. Designers consider the whole life cycle of the product, from materials and production through use, maintenance, and eventual disposal or recycling. In digital products, the discipline emphasises interaction design, information architecture, and usability testing.
