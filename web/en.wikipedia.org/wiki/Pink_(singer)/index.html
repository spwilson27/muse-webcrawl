Alecia Beth Moore (born 8 September 1979), known professionally as Pink (stylised P!NK), is an American singer and songwriter. Recognized for her powerful vocals and acrobatic live performances, she has sold over 135 million records worldwide and won three Grammy Awards.

Career

Pink first gained notice with the girl group Choice before signing solo with LaFace Records. Her debut album Can't Take Me Home (2000) was R&B-oriented; she reinvented herself with the pop-rock Missundaztood (2001), featuring "Get the Party Started" and "Just Give Me a Reason"'s predecessors "Don't Let Me Get Me" and "Just Like a Pill". Subsequent albums include Try This (2003), I'm Not Dead (2006), Funhouse (2008) — with the number-one single "So What" — The Truth About Love (2012), Beautiful Trauma (2017), Hurts 2B Human (2019), and Trustfall (2023). She also recorded the folk project rose ave. (2014) as You+Me with Dallas Green.

Artistry and performances

Pink's tours are famous for aerial acrobatics performed while singing. Her Summer Carnival tour of 2023–24 became one of the highest-grossing concert tours of all time. She is also known for advocacy on animal rights and LGBTQ rights.

Personal life

She married motocross racer Carey Hart in 2006; they have two children.
