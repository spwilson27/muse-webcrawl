Pedro Castillo

Born: 19 October 1969, Puña, Cajamarca, Peru
President of Peru: 28 July 2021 – 7 December 2022
Party: Free Peru (2020–2022)

José Pedro Castillo Terrones (born 19 October 1969) is a Peruvian politician, former elementary school teacher and union leader who served as President of Peru from 28 July 2021 until his removal from office on 7 December 2022, after he attempted to dissolve Congress and rule by decree — an act widely characterised as a self-coup — and was arrested the same day.

Early life and union career

Castillo was born in the village of Puña, Cajamarca region, to a peasant family. He worked as a primary-school teacher in rural Cajamarca for more than two decades and was long active in teachers' unionism, gaining national prominence as a leader of the 2017 teachers' strike. He became known nationally for his wide-brimmed Cajamarca hat and his slogan "No more poor people in a rich country."

Presidency

Running for the Marxist Free Peru party, Castillo narrowly defeated Keiko Fujimori in the runoff of the 2021 general election, winning by about 44,000 votes. His government faced constant conflict with a hostile Congress, frequent cabinet turnover, corruption investigations and mass protests. He survived two impeachment attempts. On 7 December 2022, hours before a third impeachment vote, Castillo announced the dissolution of Congress, a curfew and government by decree; Congress instead voted to remove him for "moral incapacity", and he was arrested while travelling to the Mexican embassy. Vice President Dina Boluarte succeeded him, sparking prolonged protests in which dozens were killed. In 2025 Castillo was sentenced to a lengthy prison term for rebellion in connection with the attempted self-coup.
