Lygia Pape

Lygia Pape (7 April 1927 – 3 May 2004) was a Brazilian visual artist, sculptor, engraver and filmmaker, who was an important figure in the Concrete movement and a co-founder of the Neo-Concrete movement in Brazil during the 1950s and 1960s.

Work

Pape was a founding member of the Grupo Frente (1954) and a signatory of the Neo-Concrete Manifesto (1959). Her works include the Tecelares woodcut prints, the Livro da Criação (Book of Creation, 1959–60), a set of sixteen pop-up geometric "pages" narrating the creation of the world, and the participatory performance Divisor (1968), in which participants poke their heads through slits in a large white sheet. Her later installations, such as Ttéia 1, C (2002), use gold thread to create webs of light in darkened spaces.
