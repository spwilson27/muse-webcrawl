California State University, Fullerton (CSUF or Cal State Fullerton) is a public university in Fullerton, California. Founded in 1957 as Orange County State College, it is the largest university in the California State University system by enrollment, with over 40,000 students.

History

Classes began in 1959 at Sunny Hills High School before the campus moved to its present 236-acre site in northeast Fullerton, built on former citrus groves. The university became California State College at Fullerton in 1964 and took its current name in 1972.

Academics

CSUF offers more than 100 degree programs across eight colleges, including the Mihaylo College of Business and Economics, one of the largest undergraduate business schools in the nation. Its baseball team, the Titans, has won four NCAA national championships.

Campus

The campus features the Fullerton Arboretum, Clayes Performing Arts Center, and Titan Stadium. Notable alumni include filmmaker James Cameron, actor Kevin Costner, and singer Gwen Stefani.
