Club Atlético Peñarol

Club Atlético Peñarol is a Uruguayan professional football club based in Montevideo. Founded on 28 September 1891 as the Central Uruguay Railway Cricket Club (CURCC), it is one of the most successful clubs in South American football and, alongside Nacional, one of the two giants of Uruguayan football.

History

Peñarol won the first two editions of the Copa Libertadores, in 1960 and 1961, and went on to win the tournament five times, most recently in 1987. The club has also won three Intercontinental Cups. Its rivalry with Nacional, known as the Superclásico del fútbol uruguayo, dates to 1900 and is among the oldest football derbies in the world.

Honours

Domestically, Peñarol has won the Uruguayan Primera División title more than 50 times. Internationally its honours include five Copa Libertadores titles (1960, 1961, 1966, 1982, 1987) and three Intercontinental Cups (1961, 1966, 1982).
