Alliance 90/The Greens – Global Greens membership

Alliance 90/The Greens
Global affiliation: Global Greens
Regional federation: European Green Party
Global Greens Charter: Adopted Canberra, 2001

Alliance 90/The Greens, Germany's green party, is a member — through the European Green Party — of the Global Greens, the international network of political parties and movements which work to implement the Global Greens Charter.

The Global Greens Charter

The Charter, adopted at the first Global Greens Congress in Canberra, Australia, in 2001 and revised at later congresses (Dakar 2012; Liverpool 2017), sets out shared core values: ecological wisdom, social justice, participatory democracy, nonviolence, sustainability and respect for diversity.

German role

The German Greens are among the world's most electorally successful green parties and a major contributor to the Global Greens' federations of Europe, providing organisational and policy models — including the Energiewende — for green parties worldwide. The party-affiliated Heinrich Böll Foundation also supports green political work internationally.

Structure

The Global Greens is a partnership of four federations: the European Green Party, the Federation of Green Parties of the Americas, the African Green Federation and the Asia-Pacific Greens Federation, coordinated by a Global Greens Secretariat.
