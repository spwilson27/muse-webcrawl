Alliance 90 (German: Bündnis 90) was an alliance of three non-communist political groups in East Germany: New Forum, Democracy Now, and the Initiative for Peace and Human Rights. It was formed for the March 1990 Volkskammer elections, the first and only free parliamentary elections in the German Democratic Republic.

History

The component groups had been leading voices of the peaceful revolution of 1989, organising protests and round-table talks that preceded the fall of the Berlin Wall. In the March 1990 election Alliance 90 won 2.9% of the vote and 12 seats in the Volkskammer. It participated in the transitional government of Lothar de Maizière.

At the first all-German federal election in December 1990, its list association with the East German Greens won eight seats in the Bundestag. On 21 September 1991 Alliance 90 formally constituted itself as a political party. On 14 May 1993 it merged with the (western) Green Party to form Alliance 90/The Greens, which remains a major force in German politics. Notable members included Joachim Gauck, later President of Germany, who had served as the federal commissioner for the Stasi records.
