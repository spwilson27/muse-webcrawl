Alliance 90/The Greens – European Green Party membership

Alliance 90/The Greens (Bündnis 90/Die Grünen)
European affiliation: European Green Party
European Parliament group: Greens/EFA
Founded (party): 1993 merger (Greens 1980; Alliance 90 1991)

Alliance 90/The Greens (German: Bündnis 90/Die Grünen), often simply the Greens, is a member party of the European Green Party (EGP), the federation of green political parties in Europe founded in 2004 as successor to the European Federation of Green Parties.

Background

The German Greens were a founding force of European green cooperation from the first directly elected European Parliament in 1979 onward, helping create the coordination that became the European Federation of Green Parties in 1993 and the EGP in 2004. In the European Parliament its MEPs sit with the Greens–European Free Alliance (Greens/EFA) group.

Role

As the largest green party in Europe by membership and votes, the German Greens have often supplied leading EGP figures and European Parliament candidates, and their policy positions on climate, energy transition (Energiewende) and civil rights have shaped the European green platform. The party entered the German federal government in coalition with the SPD and FDP from 2021.
