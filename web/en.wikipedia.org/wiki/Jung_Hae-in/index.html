Jung Hae-in

Personal information
Born: 1 April 1988, Seoul, South Korea
Occupation: Actor
Years active: 2013–present
Agent: FNC Entertainment

Jung Hae-in (born April 1, 1988) is a South Korean actor. He first appeared in AOA's music video for "Moya" in 2013 and made his acting debut in the television series Bride of the Century (2014), after completing his mandatory military service.

Career

Jung gained attention with supporting roles in the hit dramas While You Were Sleeping and Prison Playbook (both 2017). He rose to leading-man status with the romance Something in the Rain (2018), followed by One Spring Night (2019) and the film Tune in for Love (2019). He won acclaim for his starring role as a military policeman hunting deserters in the Netflix series D.P. (2021–2023), and headlined the romance drama Snowdrop (2021–22), the thriller series Connect (2022) and the hit action film I, the Executioner (2024), the sequel to Veteran.
