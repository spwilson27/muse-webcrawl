Ocado Group plc is a British technology and online grocery company headquartered in Hatfield, England. Founded in 2000 by former Goldman Sachs bankers including Tim Steiner and Jonathan Faiman, it began as an online supermarket delivering Waitrose products before developing into a technology business licensing its automated warehouse platform to retailers worldwide.

History

Ocado launched its delivery service in 2002 in partnership with Waitrose, the supermarket arm of the John Lewis Partnership. The company floated on the London Stock Exchange in 2010. Its large automated customer fulfilment centres, staffed by swarms of robots moving across a grid known as "the hive", became the core of its Ocado Smart Platform, which it licenses to international partners including Kroger in the United States, Groupe Casino in France, Coles in Australia, and Aeon in Japan.

Ocado Retail

In 2019 Ocado formed a joint venture with Marks & Spencer, Ocado Retail, which took over the Ocado.com grocery business and began delivering M&S food in September 2020, replacing Waitrose as the range supplier.

Technology

The Ocado Smart Platform combines warehouse automation, route planning, and e-commerce software. Its fulfilment centres use thousands of cube-shaped robots that travel over a grid, lifting crates of groceries to picking stations.
