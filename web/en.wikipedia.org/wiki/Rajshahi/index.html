Rajshahi (Bengali: রাজশাহী) is a metropolitan city and a major urban, commercial and educational centre of Bangladesh. It is the administrative seat of the Rajshahi Division and Rajshahi District, and lies on the northern bank of the Padma River, near the border with India.

History

The region formed part of the ancient Pundravardhana kingdom. The modern city grew during British rule as a centre for the silk and indigo trades, and Rajshahi College was founded in 1873. During the Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971, the city and its university witnessed significant fighting and atrocities.

Economy and education

Rajshahi is renowned across Bangladesh for its silk industry and for its mangoes and lychees. It hosts the University of Rajshahi (founded 1953), Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology, and numerous other institutions, earning it a reputation as an "education city." It is also consistently ranked among the cleanest and greenest cities in the country, and has received international recognition for reducing air pollution.
