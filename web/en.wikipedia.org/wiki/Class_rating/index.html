Class rating

A class rating is an allowance to fly a certain group of aircraft that require training common to all aircraft within the class. Examples of class ratings include single-engine piston, multi-engine piston, single-engine turboprop, and seaplane classes under EASA and FAA licensing systems.

Distinction from type ratings

Unlike a type rating, which covers a specific aircraft model (required for large, turbojet, or otherwise complex aircraft), a class rating authorises a pilot to fly any aircraft within the defined class, subject to differences or familiarisation training for particular variants.

Validity and revalidation

Under EASA rules, single-engine piston and touring motor glider class ratings are typically valid for two years and can be revalidated by experience in the final year or by a proficiency check. Multi-engine class ratings are generally valid for one year.
