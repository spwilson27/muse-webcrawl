Mare

A mare is an adult female horse or other equine animal. In most cases, a mare is a female horse over the age of three; a female under that age is called a filly. A mare that has been bred and is used for reproduction is often called a broodmare.

Reproductive cycle

Mares are seasonally polyestrous, cycling roughly every 21 days during the spring and summer breeding season, with an estrous ("heat") period of about five to seven days. Gestation lasts approximately 11 months (about 320–370 days), after which the mare gives birth, usually to a single foal. Nursing foals are weaned at around four to six months of age.

Use and behavior

Mares are used for riding, competition, racing, and work in the same disciplines as male horses, and many horsemen value them for their sensitivity and intelligence, though some note mood changes associated with the estrous cycle. In racing, notable mares such as Zenyatta and Winx have achieved fame comparable to celebrated stallions and geldings.
