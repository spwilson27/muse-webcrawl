European Defence Agency

European Defence Agency
Abbreviation:EDA
Formation:12 July 2004
Headquarters:Brussels, Belgium
Membership:EU member states (except Denmark, which joined in 2023 following its referendum)

The European Defence Agency (EDA) is an agency of the European Union that promotes and facilitates integration between member states within the EU's Common Security and Defence Policy. Established in 2004 and headquartered in Brussels, it is headed by the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, and its steering board meets at the level of defence ministers.

Tasks

The agency works to develop defence capabilities in the EU, foster armaments cooperation, strengthen the European defence technological and industrial base, and promote defence research. It manages collaborative projects in areas such as military airworthiness, satellite communications, cyber defence, and ammunition procurement, and supports the Permanent Structured Cooperation (PESCO) framework together with the European External Action Service.

History

The EDA was set up by a Council Joint Action on 12 July 2004. Denmark, which long held an opt-out from EU defence cooperation, voted in a June 2022 referendum to abolish the opt-out and subsequently joined the agency in 2023, making membership complete among EU states.
