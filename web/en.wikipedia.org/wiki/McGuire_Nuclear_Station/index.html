McGuire Nuclear Station

McGuire Nuclear Station
Location: Huntersville, North Carolina, USA
Operator: Duke Energy
Units: 2 pressurised water reactors
Commercial operation: 1981 (Unit 1), 1984 (Unit 2)

The McGuire Nuclear Station is a nuclear power plant in Huntersville, North Carolina, on the shore of Lake Norman, about 17 miles (27 km) north of Charlotte. It is operated by Duke Energy and named for William B. McGuire, a former president of the company.

Plant description

The station has two Westinghouse pressurised water reactors with a combined generating capacity of roughly 2,300 megawatts. The reactors use an ice condenser containment system, and Lake Norman — created by the Cowans Ford Dam — provides cooling water.

History

Unit 1 entered commercial operation in 1981 and Unit 2 in 1984. The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission has renewed the units' operating licences, allowing operation into the 2040s. The plant is periodically evaluated for seismic and flooding safety along with the rest of the US fleet.
