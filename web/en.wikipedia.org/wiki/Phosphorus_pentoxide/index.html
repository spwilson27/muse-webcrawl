Phosphorus pentoxide

Phosphorus pentoxide is the chemical compound with the molecular formula P4O10 (its empirical formula, P2O5, gives its common name). This white crystalline solid is the anhydride of phosphoric acid and is a powerful desiccant and dehydrating agent.

FormulaP4O10
Molar mass283.89 g/mol
AppearanceWhite solid
UsesDesiccant, dehydrating agent

Structure and preparation

The molecule consists of four phosphorus atoms and ten oxygen atoms arranged as an adamantane-like cage of PO4 tetrahedra. It is prepared by burning white phosphorus in excess oxygen.

Applications

Phosphorus pentoxide reacts exothermically with water to form phosphoric acid, making it an aggressive drying agent in laboratories and synthesis, where it can dehydrate amides to nitriles and carboxylic acids to anhydrides.
