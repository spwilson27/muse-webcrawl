Land Rover

Land Rover is a British brand of predominantly four-wheel drive, off-road capable vehicles, owned by Jaguar Land Rover (JLR), a subsidiary of India's Tata Motors. The original Land Rover, launched by the Rover Company in 1948, was inspired by the wartime Jeep and built with an aluminium alloy body because of post-war steel rationing.

History

The design for the original vehicle was started in 1947 by Maurice Wilks, Rover's chief designer, who used a Jeep chassis and running gear on his Welsh farm. The Series I, II and III vehicles became icons of British agricultural, military and expedition use. The Range Rover, introduced in 1970, created the luxury SUV segment. The Defender, Discovery and Freelander followed. Land Rover became a separate company under British Leyland, passed through BMW and Ford ownership, and was sold with Jaguar to Tata Motors in 2008.

Models

Current and recent models include the Defender, Discovery, Discovery Sport, Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, Range Rover Evoque and Range Rover Velar. In 2023 JLR reorganised Land Rover into "House of Brands" marques under the JLR umbrella.

Military and utility use

Land Rovers have served with the British Army and many other armed forces, and modified versions are used by aid agencies, explorers and emergency services worldwide.
