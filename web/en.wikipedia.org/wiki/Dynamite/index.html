Dynamite

Dynamite is an explosive made of nitroglycerin absorbed into an inert material such as diatomaceous earth (kieselguhr), invented by Alfred Nobel and patented in 1867.

Impact

Far safer to handle than pure nitroglycerin, dynamite transformed mining, tunnelling, demolition and warfare, and made Nobel's fortune — the fortune that later funded the Nobel Prizes.
