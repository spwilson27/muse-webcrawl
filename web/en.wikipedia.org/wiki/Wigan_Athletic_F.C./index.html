Wigan Athletic Football Club is a professional association football club based in Wigan, Greater Manchester, England. Nicknamed the Latics, the club was formed in 1932 after the winding-up of Wigan Borough and spent decades in non-league football before election to the Football League in 1978.

History

Backed from 1995 by local businessman Dave Whelan, Wigan climbed rapidly through the divisions, reaching the Premier League in 2005 and remaining there for eight seasons under managers including Paul Jewell and Roberto Martínez. The club's crowning moment came on 11 May 2013, when Ben Watson's stoppage-time header beat Manchester City 1–0 to win the FA Cup — one of the competition's great upsets — though the club was relegated from the Premier League days later, becoming the first team to win the FA Cup and be relegated in the same season.

Recent years

Financial turmoil in 2020 placed the club in administration with a points deduction and relegation, beginning a turbulent period of ownership changes, further deductions, and rebuilding in the lower divisions of the EFL.
