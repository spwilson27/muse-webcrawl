High Point is a city in the Piedmont Triad region of North Carolina, United States, lying mostly in Guilford County with portions extending into three neighbouring counties. With a population of 114,059 at the 2020 census, it is the ninth-most populous city in the state and forms part of the Greensboro–High Point metropolitan area.

History and economy

The city was named for being the highest point on the North Carolina Railroad between Goldsboro and Charlotte when it was laid out in the 1850s. After the arrival of the railroad, High Point grew into a centre for furniture, hosiery and textile manufacturing. It hosts the High Point Market, the world's largest furnishings industry trade show, held each spring and autumn, which draws tens of thousands of buyers and exhibitors. The city is also home to High Point University, founded in 1924, and to the "World's Largest Chest of Drawers", a 38-foot roadside building that symbolises the furniture trade.

Geography

High Point sits in the rolling Piedmont at an elevation of about 286 metres (940 ft). Unusual among US cities, its territory spans four counties, a legacy of successive annexations.
