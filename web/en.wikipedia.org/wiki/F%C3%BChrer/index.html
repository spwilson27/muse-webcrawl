Führer

LanguageGerman
Literal meaningLeader; guide

Führer is a German word meaning "leader" or "guide". In the political sphere it became inseparably associated with Adolf Hitler, who adopted it as his official title — most fully as Führer und Reichskanzler ("Leader and Chancellor of the Reich") — after becoming head of state of Nazi Germany in 1934.

Usage

Before the Nazi era, Führer was an ordinary word used for leaders of parties, organisations and expeditions, and in compounds such as Reiseführer (travel guide). Under the Nazi regime the term was elevated into a cult title expressing the Führerprinzip, the doctrine that the leader's will stood above written law.

After 1945

Because of its association with Hitler, the word is today generally avoided in German as a standalone term for a political leader, though it survives in neutral compounds such as Führerschein (driving licence) and Bergführer (mountain guide).
