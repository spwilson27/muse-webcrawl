Royal Observatory of Belgium

The Royal Observatory of Belgium (French: Observatoire royal de Belgique; Dutch: Koninklijke Sterrenwacht van België), often known by the abbreviation ROB, is a Belgian federal scientific research institute located in Uccle, in the Brussels-Capital Region. Founded in 1826/1828 under the impetus of Adolphe Quetelet, it carries out research in astronomy and astrophysics, seismology, gravimetry, space geodesy, solar physics and the study of the Earth's rotation and reference systems.

History

The observatory was founded on the initiative of the statistician and astronomer Adolphe Quetelet, who petitioned King William I of the Netherlands for an observatory in Brussels; a royal decree of 8 June 1826 authorised the project, and observations began in the early 1830s at a site near the Saint-Josse gate in Brussels. Quetelet became its first director. As the city grew and light and vibration increased, the observatory was transferred to a new site at Uccle, inaugurated in 1890 under director François Folie.

During the 20th century the institute diversified beyond classical astronomy: a seismological service was established (the first Belgian seismograph dates from 1904), along with gravimetric and time services. The observatory maintained Belgium's official time for many years and coordinated the national seismological network.

Research

Today's Royal Observatory of Belgium hosts departments devoted to reference systems and planetology, seismology and gravimetry, solar physics and space weather, and astronomy and astrophysics. It operates the Royal Belgian Institute for Space Aeronomy and the Royal Meteorological Institute as neighbouring federal institutes on the Uccle plateau—the three are collectively known as the "Space Pole". ROB contributes to ESA missions, operates solar telescopes and the PROBA2/SWAP and Solar Orbiter instrument consortia contributions, monitors earthquakes in Belgium and surroundings, and provides space-weather services through the Solar-Terrestrial Centre of Excellence (STCE).
