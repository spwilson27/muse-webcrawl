Jamie Adenuga (born May 4, 1985), known professionally as Jme (stylized JME), is a British grime MC, songwriter, producer, and streamer. He is a co-founder, with his brother Skepta, of the grime collective and record label Boy Better Know. Jme is noted for doing relatively well commercially without signing to a major record label, releasing music independently and retaining ownership of his work.

Career

Jme began making music in the early 2000s, initially producing ringtones and beats before turning to MCing. He released a series of mixtapes and the albums Famous? (2008), Blam! (2010), and Integrity> (2015), the latter reaching the UK Albums Chart top 20 and helping grime re-enter the mainstream. His best-known tracks include "Man Don't Care" (featuring Giggs) and "Test Me." In 2019 he released Grime MC, sold notably in physical formats first, and in 2020 collaborated again with his brother and others on Boy Better Know projects. Outside music, he is known for gaming and streaming, and for his candid public commentary.

Personal life

Jme is of Nigerian descent and is the younger brother of Skepta and of radio DJ Julie Adenuga. He is known for being a vegetarian and teetotal.
