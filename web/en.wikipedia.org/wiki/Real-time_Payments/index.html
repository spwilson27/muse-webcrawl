Real-time Payments

Real-time Payments (RTP) is a payment system in the United States, operated by The Clearing House, that allows for the instant transfer of funds between participating banks. Launched in November 2017, it was the first new core payments infrastructure in the US in more than 40 years.

Features

The RTP network operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, providing immediate clearing and settlement, confirmation of payment, and request-for-payment messaging. All federally insured depository institutions are eligible to participate. The transaction limit was raised over time, reaching $10 million in 2025.

Comparison

RTP is a credit-push system distinct from the later Federal Reserve service FedNow (launched 2023), with which it competes as an instant payments rail.
