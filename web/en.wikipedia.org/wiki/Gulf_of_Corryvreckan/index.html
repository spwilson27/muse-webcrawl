Gulf of Corryvreckan

LocationBetween Jura and Scarba, Argyll and Bute, Scotland
Coordinates56°09′N 5°43′W
TypeStrait with tidal whirlpool

The Gulf of Corryvreckan is a narrow strait between the islands of Jura and Scarba in Argyll and Bute, off the west coast of mainland Scotland. It is famous for the Corryvreckan whirlpool, one of the largest permanent whirlpools in the world.

The whirlpool

The whirlpool is caused by strong Atlantic tidal flows meeting an underwater rock pinnacle and a deep hole in the seabed. At the flood tide, currents can exceed 8.5 knots, creating waves and standing swirls audible miles away. The Royal Navy once classified the whirlpool as unnavigable. The name derives from Gaelic Coire Bhreacain, "cauldron of the speckled seas", associated in legend with the Norse prince Breacan.

In culture

George Orwell, who lived on Jura while writing Nineteen Eighty-Four, nearly drowned in the gulf in 1947 when a boat outing with his children went wrong at the whirlpool. The strait also featured in the 1945 film I Know Where I'm Going!
