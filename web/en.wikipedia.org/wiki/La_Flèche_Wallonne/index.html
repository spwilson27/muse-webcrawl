La Flèche Wallonne (French for "The Walloon Arrow") is a professional men's and women's road cycling race held each April in the Wallonia region of Belgium. Together with Liège–Bastogne–Liège, held the following Sunday, it forms the centrepiece of the Ardennes classics week; Amstel Gold Race, held the preceding Sunday in the Netherlands, completes the Ardennes trilogy.

Course

The race is famous for its finish on the Mur de Huy, a climb of about 1.3 kilometres with an average gradient near 10% and a steepest section around 26%, which the peloton ascends several times before the final finishing ascent. The short, explosive climb favours punchy climbers known as puncheurs, and the race is usually decided on the final ramp to the line above the town of Huy.

History

The men's race was first held in 1936. Alejandro Valverde holds the men's record with five victories (2006, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017); Julian Alaphilippe and Davide Rebellin each won three times. A women's edition was added in 1998 and became one of the most prestigious races on the women's calendar; Anna van der Breggen won seven consecutive editions from 2015 to 2021, and Marianne Vos won five.
