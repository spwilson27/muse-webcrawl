Eusko Alkartasuna (EA; Basque for "Basque Solidarity") is a Basque nationalist and social-democratic political party active in the Basque Autonomous Community and Navarre in Spain. It was founded in 1986 by former lehendakari Carlos Garaikoetxea following a split from the Basque Nationalist Party (PNV).

History

EA advocatedes Basque self-determination through democratic means combined with centre-left policies. It has participated in Basque regional governments in coalition with the PNV, and later formed electoral alliances including Amaiur and EH Bildu.

Youth and affiliations

The party's youth wing is Gazte Abertzaleak, and EA is a member of the European Free Alliance of regionalist parties in the European Parliament.
