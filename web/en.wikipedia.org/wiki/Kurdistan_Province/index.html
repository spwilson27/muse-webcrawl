Kurdistan Province

Kurdistan Province (Persian: استان کردستان; Kurdish: پارێزگای کوردستان) is one of the 31 provinces of Iran, located in the west of the country along the Zagros Mountains and bordering Iraq. Its capital and largest city is Sanandaj.

Geography and population

The province covers about 29,137 km² of mountainous terrain. Its population was about 1.6 million in the 2016 census, predominantly ethnic Kurds speaking Sorani Kurdish, with Persian also used officially. Major cities include Saqqez, Marivan, Baneh, Qorveh, Bijar, Kamyaran and Divandarreh.

History

The region formed part of the historical Ardalan emirate, a Kurdish principality that governed the area for centuries under Persian suzerainty. After the First World War and border settlements with the Ottoman Empire, the modern province took shape; areas further south and north were later separated into the provinces of Kermanshah, West Azerbaijan and Ilam.

Economy

Agriculture, livestock and border trade with Iraqi Kurdistan underpin the provincial economy; Marivan's Lake Zarivar and the terraced village of Uraman Takht are major attractions, the latter within the UNESCO-listed Cultural Landscape of Hawraman/Uramanat (2021).
