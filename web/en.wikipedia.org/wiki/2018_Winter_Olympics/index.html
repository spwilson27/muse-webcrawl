2018 Winter Olympics

The 2018 Winter Olympics, officially the XXIII Olympic Winter Games, were held in Pyeongchang, South Korea, from 9 to 25 February 2018. A record 2,922 athletes from 92 National Olympic Committees competed in 102 events across 15 sports. The Games were notable for North Korea's participation, including a unified Korean women's ice hockey team.

Selection

Pyeongchang was awarded the Games at the IOC Session in Durban in July 2011, defeating Munich and Annecy in the first round of voting. It was South Korea's second Olympic Games, after the 1988 Summer Olympics in Seoul, and its first Winter Games.

Inter-Korean participation

Following diplomatic talks in January 2018, North Korea sent a delegation including athletes, cheerleaders and the ceremonial head of state Kim Yong-nam. The two Koreas marched together at the opening ceremony under the Korean Unification Flag, and fielded a joint women's ice hockey team — the first unified Korean team at any Olympic Games.

Highlights and medal table

Norway topped the medal table with 39 medals, including 14 golds, and Norwegian cross-country skier Marit Bjørgen became the most decorated Winter Olympian of all time with 15 medals. Russian athletes competed as "Olympic Athletes from Russia" under a neutral flag following the suspension of the Russian Olympic Committee over doping. New events included big air snowboarding, mixed doubles curling and mass start speed skating.
