Munster Senior Football Championship

The Munster Senior Football Championship, known for sponsorship reasons as the Munster GAA Football Senior Championship, is an annual Gaelic football competition organised by the Munster Council of the Gaelic Athletic Association (GAA). It is contested by the county teams of Clare, Cork, Kerry, Limerick, Tipperary and Waterford.

Format

The championship is played on a knockout basis during the summer. The winners receive the Munster Cup and advance in the All-Ireland Senior Football Championship series, while beaten teams historically progressed through the qualifiers.

History

The championship was first held in 1888. Kerry are by far the most successful county, with the great majority of titles, followed by Cork. The dominance of the Kerry–Cork rivalry is a defining feature of the competition, although other counties have won occasional titles.
