Milonga (dance)

Milonga is a term for a place or event where tango is danced, and also the name of a faster, livelier dance that is a precursor and companion of Argentine tango. People who attend milongas are known as milongueros.

The dance

Milonga music is in 2/4 time with a brisk, syncopated rhythm. The dance is danced in close embrace with simple, grounded steps that play on the beat, in contrast to the slower, more dramatic tango and the flowing tango vals. It developed in the Río de la Plata region in the late 19th century from the habanera and candombe traditions.

The event

A milonga event typically alternates tandas (sets) of tango, vals and milonga music separated by cortinas, and follows traditional codes (codigos) such as the cabeceo — invitation by eye contact and a nod.
