Stockholm County

Capital: Stockholm
Area: 6,500 km²
Population: approx. 2.4 million
Municipalities: 26

Stockholm County (Swedish: Stockholms län) is a county on Sweden's Baltic coast, the most populous in the country, containing the capital Stockholm and its metropolitan area.

Geography

The county borders Lake Mälaren to the west and the Stockholm archipelago — some 30,000 islands — to the east. It was formed in 1968 by merging Stockholm city with the surrounding county.

Administration

Region Stockholm is responsible for healthcare and public transport (SL). The Stockholm County Administrative Board represents the national government locally.
