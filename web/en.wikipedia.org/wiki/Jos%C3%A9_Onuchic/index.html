José Nelson Onuchic is a Brazilian-born American physicist and theoretical biophysicist. He is the Harry C. and Olga K. Wiess Professor of Physics at Rice University and a co-director of the Center for Theoretical Biological Physics.

Research

Onuchic is a leading developer of energy landscape theory and the principle of minimal frustration, which explain how proteins fold rapidly and reliably into their functional structures. His work combines statistical mechanics with molecular simulation to study protein folding, electron transfer, and the dynamics of biomolecular machines. He was elected to the National Academy of Sciences of the United States.
