Museum of Flight

The Museum of Flight is a private air and space museum in the Seattle metropolitan area. It is located at the southern end of King County International Airport (Boeing Field) in the city of Tukwila, immediately south of Seattle, Washington. It was established in 1965 and is fully accredited by the American Alliance of Museums.

As the largest private air and space museum in the world, it also hosts large educational programs throughout the year. The museum attracts more than 500,000 visitors every year, and serves more than 140,000 students annually through its onsite and outreach programs. The museum serves multiple Pacific Northwest school districts through its educational programs.

History

The Museum of Flight can trace its roots back to the Pacific Northwest Aviation Historical Foundation, founded in 1965 to recover and restore a 1916 Boeing Model 1 seaplane, the first product of The Boeing Company. The foundation initially displayed its collection at the Seattle Center, where the Boeing Model 1 was restored and exhibited. In 1975 the museum's collection moved into the William E. Boeing Red Barn, the company's original factory building, which had been transported to the site in 1983.

The Great Gallery opened in 1987, followed by the Personal Courage Wing in 2004, which displays aircraft from the two world wars. Other major additions include the J. Elroy McCaw Personal Courage Wing, the Charles Simonyi Space Gallery, and the outdoor Aviation Pavilion, which opened in 2016 and shelters large aircraft including a Boeing 747 and a Concorde.

Collection

The museum's collection numbers more than 175 aircraft and spacecraft, as well as related artifacts. Highlights include an Air Force One (a Boeing VC-137B that served several presidents), a British Airways Concorde, the first Boeing 747 airframe, a Lockheed SR-71 Blackbird reconnaissance aircraft, and the last surviving Boeing 247. The Red Barn houses early aviation artifacts and exhibits on the founding years of the Boeing Company.

Facilities

The museum campus covers about 15 acres and includes the T. A. Wilson Great Gallery, the Red Barn, the Personal Courage Wing, the Aviation Pavilion, a theater, a library and archives, and the Raisbeck Aviation High School, a public STEM-focused school that opened adjacent to the museum in 2013.
