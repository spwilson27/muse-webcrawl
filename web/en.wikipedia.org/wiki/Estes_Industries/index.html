Estes Industries

Estes Industries, also known as Estes-Cox Corp., is an American company based in Penrose, Colorado, that manufactures model rockets, model rocket engines, and related accessories for hobbyists. Founded in 1958 by Vernon Estes, the company is widely credited with popularizing model rocketry as a safe, mass-market hobby in the United States.

History

Vernon Estes founded the company in 1958 after designing "Mabel", a machine that could mass-produce solid-fuel model rocket engines cheaply and safely. Before Mabel, enthusiasts had to pack their own engines by hand, a dangerous practice. Estes began selling engines by mail order and soon expanded into complete rocket kits.

During the Space Race of the 1960s, demand for model rockets surged, and Estes became the dominant supplier in the hobby. The company was acquired by Damon Corporation in 1969. In 2018, Estes was purchased by Langford Group, which also owned other hobby brands.

Products

Estes sells rocket kits rated by skill level, from ready-to-fly models to advanced multi-stage designs, along with single-use black powder and composite engines classified by impulse class (such as A through E), launch pads, controllers, parachutes, and recovery wadding. Famous kits include the Big Bertha, the Alpha, and scale models of real rockets.

Safety

Estes engines and kits are designed around the safety code of the National Association of Rocketry (NAR), and the company's products are certified for hobby use across the United States.
