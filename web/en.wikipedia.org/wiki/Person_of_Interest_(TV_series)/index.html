Person of Interest is an American science fiction crime drama television series created by Jonathan Nolan and executive produced by J. J. Abrams, which aired on CBS from 2011 to 2016. It stars Jim Caviezel as John Reese, a former CIA operative presumed dead, and Michael Emerson as Harold Finch, a reclusive billionaire software engineer.

Premise

After the September 11 attacks, Finch built a supercomputer system — "the Machine" — for the United States government that processes data from mass surveillance to predict future terrorist attacks. The Machine also identifies ordinary people involved in violent crimes, whether as perpetrators or victims; because the government deems these cases "irrelevant", Finch recruits Reese to act on the numbers the Machine provides, which identify a person of interest by Social Security number alone. The series blends a crime procedural format with a serialised science-fiction arc about artificial intelligence, mass surveillance, and the rivalry between the Machine and a second, malevolent superintelligence called Samaritan.

Cast and reception

The main cast also includes Taraji P. Henson as Detective Joss Carter, Kevin Chapman as Detective Lionel Fusco, Amy Acker as the hacker Root, and Sarah Shahi as the operative Sameen Shaw. The series received critical acclaim, particularly for its later seasons' exploration of artificial intelligence and privacy, and won the People's Choice Award for Favorite TV Crime Drama in 2014 and Favorite New TV Drama in 2012.
