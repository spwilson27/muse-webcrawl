Deloitte

A Deloitte office

Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Limited, commonly referred to simply as Deloitte, is a British multinational professional services network. It is the largest professional services network in the world by revenue and number of employees, and is considered one of the "Big Four" accounting firms, along with EY, KPMG and PwC.

History

The firm traces its origins to 1845, when William Welch Deloitte opened an office in London. It expanded through a series of mergers, including with Haskins & Sells in the United States and with the Japanese firm Tohmatsu in the late twentieth century, producing the modern Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu name in 1989.

Services

Deloitte provides audit, consulting, financial advisory, risk advisory, tax and related services to clients in more than 150 countries. The network is organized as a set of legally separate member firms coordinated by the global parent entity, which is headquartered in London.
