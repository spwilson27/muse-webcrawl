Interactive theatre

Interactive theatre is a presentational or theatrical form that breaks the "fourth wall" separating performers from the audience, inviting spectators to participate in, influence, or become part of the performance.

Forms include forum theatre, developed by Augusto Boal as part of the Theatre of the Oppressed, in which audience members ("spect-actors") intervene in scenes of oppression; murder-mystery dinner theatre; and immersive productions such as Punchdrunk's Sleep No More, in which masked audience members wander freely through the performance space.

Interactive techniques are also used in education, therapy, and community development.
