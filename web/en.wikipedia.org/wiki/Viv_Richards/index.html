Viv Richards

Sir Isaac Vivian Alexander Richards (born 7 March 1952) is an Antiguan former cricketer who captained the West Indies and is regarded as one of the greatest and most destructive batsmen in cricket history.

Born7 March 1952, St John's, Antigua
BattingRight-handed
TeamsWest Indies, Leeward Islands, Somerset

Career

Richards debuted in 1974 and was central to the dominant West Indies team that won the 1975 and 1979 World Cups and went unbeaten in Test series for fifteen years. He scored 8,540 runs in 121 Tests at an average near 50, including a then-fastest Test century off 56 balls in 1986, and 6,721 ODI runs. Famous for batting without a helmet against fast bowling, he also played county cricket for Somerset.

Honours

Knighted in 1999, he gives his name to the Sir Vivian Richards Stadium in Antigua, built for the 2007 World Cup. He is regularly ranked alongside Garfield Sobers and Brian Lara among the Caribbean's greatest players.
