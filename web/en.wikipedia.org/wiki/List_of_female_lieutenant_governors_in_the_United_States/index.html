List of female lieutenant governors in the United States

This is a list of female lieutenant governors in the United States. The lieutenant governor is the second-highest executive officer in most U.S. states and typically first in line to succeed the governor. Several women who served as lieutenant governor later became governors of their states, including by succession when a governor resigned.

Milestones

Matilda Dodge Wilson of Michigan, appointed in 1940 to fill a vacancy, was the first woman to serve as a lieutenant governor. The office became increasingly open to women from the 1970s onward; notable firsts of the modern era include Marlene Johnson of Minnesota (1983), the first woman elected lieutenant governor in her state and later a long-serving officeholder. By the mid-2020s, women had held the office in a majority of states and served as lieutenant governor in roughly twenty states simultaneously.
