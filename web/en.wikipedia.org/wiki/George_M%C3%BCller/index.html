George Müller (born Johann Georg Ferdinand Müller; 27 September 1805 – 10 March 1898) was a Prussian-born Christian evangelist who settled in Bristol, England, where he founded orphanages that cared for more than 10,000 orphans during his lifetime.

Life and work

After a dissolute youth, Müller converted to Christianity while studying divinity at Halle and came to England in 1829. In Bristol he co-founded the Scriptural Knowledge Institution in 1834 and opened his first orphan house in 1836. At Ashley Down he built five large orphan houses accommodating over 2,000 children. Müller famously refused to solicit donations or incur debt, relying instead on prayer and unsolicited gifts — a practice he documented in his published Narrative.

Later years

From 1875 Müller undertook worldwide preaching tours. He was associated with the Brethren movement and the Bethesda Chapel in Bristol. The George Müller Charitable Trust continues his work in Bristol.
