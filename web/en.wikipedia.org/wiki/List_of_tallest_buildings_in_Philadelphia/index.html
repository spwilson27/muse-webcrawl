List of tallest buildings in Philadelphia

Philadelphia, the largest city in Pennsylvania, is home to a skyline of high-rise buildings concentrated in Center City. For much of the twentieth century, an informal "gentlemen's agreement" held that no building should rise above the statue of William Penn atop City Hall (548 feet / 167 m). That tradition was broken in 1987 by One Liberty Place.

History

Philadelphia City Hall, completed in 1901, was the tallest habitable building in the world from 1894 to 1908 and remained the city's tallest until 1987. The "Curse of Billy Penn" was a popular explanation for the city's championship drought in professional sports between 1983 and 2008; it was said to be lifted when a small Penn figurine was placed atop the Comcast Center, after which the Phillies won the 2008 World Series.
