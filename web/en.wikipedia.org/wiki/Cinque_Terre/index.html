Cinque Terre

LocationLiguria, Italy
VillagesMonterosso, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola, Riomaggiore
[Image: Manarola at dusk above its harbour]
Manarola at dusk above its harbour

The Cinque Terre ("Five Lands") are five colourful fishing villages — Monterosso, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola and Riomaggiore — strung along a rugged stretch of the Ligurian coast of Italy, protected as a national park and UNESCO site.

Character

Built on dry-stone terraces carved over a thousand years for vines and olives, the villages are linked by the cliffside Sentiero Azzurro (Blue Path) and a railway tunnelled through the headlands; cars barely penetrate. Sciacchetrà sweet wine comes from the terraces. Devastating floods in 2011 damaged Vernazza and Monterosso, and the park now manages visitor numbers on the most fragile paths; love-lock-laden Via dell'Amore between Riomaggiore and Manarola reopened in 2024 after a long closure following a landslide.
