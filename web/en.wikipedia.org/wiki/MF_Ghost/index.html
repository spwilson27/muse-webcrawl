MF Ghost

MF Ghost is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Shuichi Shigeno. It is a sequel to his earlier street-racing series Initial D, and was serialised in Kodansha's Weekly Young Magazine from 2017 to 2025. An anime television series adaptation premiered in 2023.

MF Ghost
Author: Shuichi Shigeno
Publisher: Kodansha
Magazine: Weekly Young Magazine
Original run: 2017–2025
Genre: Racing, action

Plot

Set in the 2020s, when electric and fuel-cell cars dominate and internal-combustion cars have become a hobbyist niche, the story follows Kanata Katagiri (racing as Kanata Livington), a British-Japanese driver trained by Takumi Fujiwara, who enters Japan's MFG racing series driving a Toyota 86.

Media

The manga concluded with over twenty collected volumes. The anime adaptation by Felix Film aired its first season in late 2023, with further seasons following.
