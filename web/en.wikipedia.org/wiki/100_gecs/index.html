100 gecs is an American musical duo consisting of Dylan Brady and Laura Les, formed in 2015 in St. Louis, Missouri. They are widely credited as pioneers of the hyperpop genre, combining heavily distorted, maximalist electronic production with elements of pop, hip hop, ska, dubstep, metal and bubblegum pop, often at extreme tempos and pitch-shifted vocals.

History

Brady and Les, who grew up in the St. Louis area and met through the local music scene, first collaborated in 2015 and released a self-titled EP in 2016. Having moved to Chicago and Los Angeles respectively, they produced their debut album largely by exchanging files online. 1000 gecs (2019), featuring the singles "Money Machine" and "Ringtone", received critical acclaim and appeared on numerous year-end lists. A remix album, 1000 gecs and the Tree of Clues (2020), featured contributions from Charli XCX, Rico Nasty, Fall Out Boy and others.

Second album and style

Their second studio album, 10,000 gecs (2023), incorporated more live instrumentation and influences from ska, nu metal and pop punk, including the singles "Doritos & Fritos" and "Hollywood Baby". Critics have described the duo's sound as chaotic and internet-native, while Brady and Les have become influential producers and collaborators across contemporary pop.
