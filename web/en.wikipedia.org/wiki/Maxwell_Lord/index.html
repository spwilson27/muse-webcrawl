Maxwell Lord

Maxwell Lord IV is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Keith Giffen, J. M. DeMatteis, and Kevin Maguire, he first appeared in Justice League #1 (May 1987). Originally depicted as a shrewd, humorous businessman who sponsored the Justice League International, the character was later retconned into a villainous mastermind with mind-control powers.

Fictional biography

As the wealthy head of the Maxwell Lord Enterprises and later leader of the organization Checkmate, Lord maneuvered heroes for his own ends. After the events of Invasion! he developed metahuman powers of mental persuasion, initially signaled by nosebleeds when he pushed too hard. In the lead-up to Infinite Crisis, Lord was revealed as the head of a conspiratorial Checkmate and murdered his former ally Ted Kord (Blue Beetle) when Kord uncovered his plans; he then took control of Superman's mind, prompting Wonder Woman to kill him — an act broadcast worldwide that damaged her reputation. Lord was later resurrected during Brightest Day and has continued to appear as an antagonist, including storylines involving the OMAC Project and Brother Eye.

In other media

Maxwell Lord appeared in the television series Supergirl (played by Peter Facinelli) and was portrayed by Pedro Pascal in the film Wonder Woman 1984 (2020), where he is the principal antagonist who acquires a wish-granting artifact.
