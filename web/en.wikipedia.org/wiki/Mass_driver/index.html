Mass driver

A mass driver or electromagnetic catapult is a proposed method of non-rocket spacelaunch that uses a linear motor to accelerate and catapult payloads to high speeds. Sequential electromagnets accelerate a payload attached to a armature along a track, analogous to a railgun or coilgun.

Concepts

Gerard K. O'Neill proposed mass drivers in the 1970s to launch lunar material into space for building colonies. Terrestrial versions would face atmospheric drag and extreme acceleration forces, limiting them to cargo. Electromagnetic aircraft launch systems on aircraft carriers are a working application of related technology.

Challenges

Problems include switching very large currents rapidly, track wear, guidance at hypersonic speeds, and the g-forces involved.

References

O'Neill, The High Frontier; space-launch concept studies.
