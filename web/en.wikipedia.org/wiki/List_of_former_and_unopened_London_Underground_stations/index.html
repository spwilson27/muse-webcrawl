List of former and unopened London Underground stations

The London Underground is a metro system in the United Kingdom that serves Greater London and adjacent districts. Over its history, a number of stations have been closed down, and several planned stations were never opened. This article lists former and unopened London Underground stations.

Aldwych, closed in 1994, is now used for filming and training.
