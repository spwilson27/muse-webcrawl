"Candle in the Wind 1997", also known as "Goodbye England's Rose", is a re-recording of Elton John's 1973 song "Candle in the Wind", with new lyrics by Bernie Taupin, performed as a tribute to Diana, Princess of Wales, following her death in August 1997. John performed the song publicly for the only time at Diana's funeral in Westminster Abbey on 6 September 1997.

Release and reception

The studio version, produced by George Martin, was released as a double A-side single with "Something About the Way You Look Tonight" on 13 September 1997. It became the biggest-selling single since UK and US charts began in the 1950s, selling about 33 million copies worldwide, and is the best-selling physical single of all time. In the UK it sold 1.5 million copies in its first week.

Charity

All artist and composer royalties and record-company profits were donated to the Diana, Princess of Wales Memorial Fund. The single won the Grammy Award for Best Male Pop Vocal Performance at the 1998 ceremony.
