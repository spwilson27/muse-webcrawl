Jiangmen (江门; Cantonese: Gongmoon) is a prefecture-level city in Guangdong Province in southern China, located on the western side of the Pearl River Delta. The city administers three urban districts and four county-level cities—Taishan, Kaiping, Heshan, and Enping—and had a population of about 4.8 million in the 2020 census.

Overseas Chinese homeland

Jiangmen and its surrounding counties, historically known as the Sze Yup ("Four Counties") region together with neighbouring areas, are famous as the ancestral homeland of a very large share of the Chinese diaspora, particularly early emigrants to North America. Overseas Chinese from Jiangmen number in the millions worldwide, exceeding the city's own resident population, and remittances and return migration have shaped the region's architecture and economy for over a century.

Heritage and economy

The Kaiping Diaolou—multi-storey fortified watchtowers built in the early 20th century by returned emigrants, blending Chinese and Western architectural styles—were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2007. Jiangmen is also known for its production of motorcycles, household appliances, textiles, and paper products, and for the hot springs of Enping and the bird paradise (Xiao Niao Tian Tang) in Xinhui, a centuries-old banyan tree island habitat for tens of thousands of birds described by the writer Ba Jin.
