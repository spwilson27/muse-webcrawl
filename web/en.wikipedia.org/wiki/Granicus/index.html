Granicus

The Granicus (modern Turkish: Biga Çayı) is a river in north-western Turkey, flowing through the ancient Troad into the Sea of Marmara. It is famous as the site of the Battle of the Granicus in 334 BC, the first major victory of Alexander the Great over the Persian Empire.

The battle

In May 334 BC Alexander crossed into Asia and met a Persian satrapal army drawn up on the steep far bank of the Granicus. Despite the disadvantageous position, Alexander attacked across the river, narrowly escaping death in the melee, and routed the Persians. The victory opened Asia Minor to the Macedonian invasion.
