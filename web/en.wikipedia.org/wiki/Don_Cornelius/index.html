Don Cornelius

Donald Cortez Cornelius (September 27, 1936 – February 1, 2012) was an American television show host and producer best known as the creator of the nationally syndicated dance and music franchise Soul Train, which he hosted from 1971 to 1993. Cornelius was known for his deep, smooth voice and his signature sign-off: "and you can bet your last money, it's all gonna be a stone gas, honey. I'm Don Cornelius, and as always in parting, we wish you love, peace and soul!"

Early life and career

Cornelius was born in Chicago's Bronzeville neighborhood and grew up in the DuSable area. After graduating from high school in 1954, he joined the United States Marine Corps and served in Korea. He later worked as a salesman and insurance agent before enrolling in a broadcasting course in 1966, which led to jobs in radio and as a news reporter and disc jockey at Chicago station WVON. He later worked at WCIU-TV, hosting a news programme and the show A Black's View of the News.

Soul Train

In 1970, Cornelius launched Soul Train as a local programme on WCIU. The show, featuring teenagers dancing to the latest soul and R&B records, proved popular and entered national syndication on October 2, 1971. As host and producer, Cornelius gave national exposure to many Black artists who received little mainstream television coverage, and the programme became a cultural institution, running for 35 years. Cornelius stepped down as host in 1993 but continued as producer; the show ended in 2006.

Later life and death

Cornelius founded Don Cornelius Productions and developed other ventures, including the Soul Train Music Awards. He died on February 1, 2012, at his home in Sherman Oaks, California, from a self-inflicted gunshot wound; he had reportedly been in declining health.

Legacy

Cornelius is credited with opening television to Black music, fashion, and dance culture. Soul Train's influence extended to generations of musicians and dancers, and Cornelius received numerous honours, including a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.
