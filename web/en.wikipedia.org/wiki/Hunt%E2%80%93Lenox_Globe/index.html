The Hunt–Lenox Globe is a small copper globe dating from about 1510, held by the New York Public Library. One of the oldest surviving terrestrial globes, it is the first known globe to show the Americas in a recognisable form and the only known map to bear the phrase HC SVNT DRACONES — "here be dragons" — written over Southeast Asia.

Description

The hollow copper sphere, about 112 mm in diameter, was probably once part of an armillary sphere. It depicts Europe, Africa and Asia with considerable accuracy for its time, alongside a large island representing the newly encountered lands across the Atlantic. It came to light in Paris in 1855, was purchased by architect Richard Morris Hunt, and donated to the Lenox Library, now part of the New York Public Library.
