Roorkee

Roorkee is a city in the Haridwar district of Uttarakhand, India, situated on the banks of the Ganges Canal. It is best known as the home of the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee), one of India's oldest and most prestigious engineering institutions.

History

Roorkee grew in importance during British rule as the headquarters of the Ganges Canal works, built under Colonel Proby Cautley in the 1840s–50s; the canal's Solani aqueduct became a local landmark. The Thomason College of Civil Engineering, founded in 1847 to train engineers for the canal and public works, became the University of Roorkee in 1949 and was converted into an IIT in 2001. The city also hosts a major cantonment, the Bengal Engineer Group (Bengal Sappers) of the Indian Army, and the Central Building Research Institute.

Geography and demographics

Roorkee lies on National Highway 334 and the Delhi–Dehradun corridor, between Haridwar and Saharanpur, at the foot of the Shivalik hills. Its population is roughly 120,000 (2011 census), with a large student and military presence.
