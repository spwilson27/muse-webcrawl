List of fictional elements, materials, isotopes and subatomic particles

Fictional works — especially science fiction, fantasy and comic books — frequently invent elements, materials, isotopes and subatomic particles with extraordinary properties. These substances often serve as plot devices: super-strong metals, unlimited energy sources, or materials that enable faster-than-light travel or magic.

Fictional isotopes and particles

  • Tachyon — hypothetical faster-than-light particle widely used in science fiction (real in physics speculation).
  • Chronoton — fictional time-related particle in Star Trek and DC Comics.
  • Pym particles — Marvel particles that shrink or grow matter (Ant-Man).
  • Midi-chlorians — microscopic life forms in Star Wars mediating the Force.
  • Kryptonite isotopes — green, red, gold, blue and other variants with different effects on Superman.
  • Element 115 (moscovium) — real superheavy element featured in UFO lore and fiction as an antimatter fuel source.
