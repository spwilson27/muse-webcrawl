Greasy spoon

A greasy spoon is a small, inexpensive restaurant — typically an American diner or coffee shop, or a British café — that serves fried foods at low prices. The term, dating to the early 20th century, derives from the suggestion of unwashed cutlery and has long carried a slightly derogatory connotation, though many such establishments are celebrated as community institutions.

Food and culture

Menus center on fried breakfasts — in Britain the "fry-up" of eggs, bacon, sausage, beans and toast, often served all day — alongside burgers, chips (fries), pies, tea and filter coffee. Greasy spoons are associated with working-class diners, truck drivers and builders (the "builder's breakfast"), and are noted for counter seating, laminated menus and fast service. Despite pressure from chain coffee shops, traditional cafés persist in British towns and are sometimes defended as part of local heritage.
