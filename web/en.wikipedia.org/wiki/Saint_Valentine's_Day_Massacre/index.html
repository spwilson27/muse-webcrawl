Saint Valentine's Day Massacre

The Saint Valentine's Day Massacre was the murder of seven members and associates of Chicago's North Side Gang on 14 February 1929, in a Lincoln Park garage, during the Prohibition-era gang war with Al Capone's Chicago Outfit.

The crime

The victims were lined up against a wall and shot by men, some dressed as police officers, apparently in a staged raid. The gang's leader, Bugs Moran, was the presumed target but escaped by arriving late. No one was ever convicted, though the killings are widely attributed to Capone's organisation; the massacre shocked the public and intensified federal pressure on Capone.
