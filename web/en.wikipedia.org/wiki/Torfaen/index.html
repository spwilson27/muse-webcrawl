Torfaen

TorfaenCountry: Wales
Administrative centre: Pontypool
Population: around 92,000

Torfaen (Welsh: Tor-faen) is a county borough in South Wales, named after the River Torfaen (the Afon Lwyd). Its principal towns are Pontypool, Cwmbran and Blaenavon.

Geography and history

The borough stretches from the coalfield uplands around Blaenavon in the north to the planned new town of Cwmbran in the south. Blaenavon's industrial landscape, shaped by iron and coal, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Administration

Torfaen County Borough Council administers the area. The borough was created in 1974 as a district of Gwent and became a unitary authority in 1996.

Transport

The A4042 and A472 roads run through the borough, and railway stations at Pontypool and New Inn and Cwmbran serve the Welsh Marches line.
