Governorates of Lebanon

The governorates of Lebanon (Arabic: محافظات لبنان, muḥāfaẓāt Lubnān) are the first-level administrative divisions of the Lebanese Republic. Lebanon is divided into nine governorates, which are subdivided into districts (qaḍāʾ) and municipalities.

List

The governorates are: Akkar, Baalbek-Hermel, Beirut, Beqaa, Keserwan-Jbeil, Mount Lebanon, North Lebanon (North), Nabatiyah, and South Lebanon (South). Beirut, the capital, forms its own small governorate entirely urban in character, while Mount Lebanon surrounds it and contains much of the country's population.

Administration

Each governorate is headed by a governor (muḥāfiẓ) appointed by the central government. Two governorates, Akkar and Baalbek-Hermel, were created in 2003 and fully implemented later; Keserwan-Jbeil was split from Mount Lebanon in 2017.
