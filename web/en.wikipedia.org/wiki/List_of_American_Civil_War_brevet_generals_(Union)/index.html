List of American Civil War brevet generals (Union)

During the American Civil War, the United States Army made extensive use of brevet promotions, which conferred the honorary rank of brigadier general or major general on officers for gallant or meritorious service without raising their substantive rank or pay. Nearly 1,400 officers of the Union Army and volunteers received brevet appointments as generals, many of them dated 13 March 1865, the date used for a mass round of awards near the end of the war.

Background

The President could nominate officers for brevet rank, subject to confirmation by the Senate. Brevet appointments were made in both the Regular Army and the United States Volunteers. The practice produced the largest number of general-grade appointments of any period in American military history, and led to the informal description of some officers as "brevet brigadier generals".
