List of world records in athletics

Usain Bolt holds the 100 m and 200 m world records

World records in athletics (track and field) are ratified by World Athletics. This list covers the principal outdoor events contested at the Olympic Games and World Championships. Records marked with a wind reading require a following wind of no more than 2.0 m/s for sprint and horizontal jump events.
