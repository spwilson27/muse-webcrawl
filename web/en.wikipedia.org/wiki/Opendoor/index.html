Opendoor Technologies Inc. is an American online real-estate platform headquartered in San Francisco. Founded in 2014, it pioneered the "iBuying" model, in which the company uses pricing algorithms to make near-instant cash offers on homes, buys them directly, carries out light repairs, and resells them.

Business model

A homeowner requests an offer online; Opendoor estimates the home's value from comparable sales and property data, deducts fees and repair costs, and, if accepted, closes on the seller's timeline. The company earns revenue from the service fee and the resale margin, and has added adjacent services such as mortgage and title.

History

Opendoor went public in December 2020 through a merger with a special-purpose acquisition company led by Chamath Palihapitiya, at a valuation of about .8 billion. Housing-market swings hit the model hard: in 2022 the company recorded large losses as homes bought at pandemic-era prices were resold for less, and it laid off staff and changed chief executives. In 2022 it also agreed to pay 2 million to settle Federal Trade Commission charges that it misled sellers about how much they could earn versus listing traditionally.
