Llanarmon

Llanarmon is a village in Wrexham County Borough, Wales, situated in the Ceiriog Valley. The name, sometimes given in full as Llanarmon Dyffryn Ceiriog, refers to the church of Saint Garmon, to whom the parish church is dedicated.

Geography

The village lies in the valley of the River Ceiriog, near the Berwyn mountains, in a largely rural area historically part of Denbighshire. The surrounding landscape is characterised by steep hillsides, sheep farming, and narrow lanes, and the area is popular with walkers.

History and community

Llanarmon is a small settlement centred on its church, village hall, and inn. Like much of the Ceiriog Valley, it has ties to the Welsh poet John Ceiriog Hughes, known by the bardic name Ceiriog, who was born nearby at Pen-y-Bryn. The valley was sometimes promoted in the Victorian era as a "little Switzerland" for its scenery.
