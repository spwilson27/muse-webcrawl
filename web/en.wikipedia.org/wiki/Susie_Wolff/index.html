Susie Wolff

Susie Wolff (née Stoddart; born 6 December 1982) is a Scottish former professional racing driver and motorsport executive. She spent seven seasons in the DTM touring car championship with Mercedes-Benz, became a development and test driver for the Williams Formula One team — the first woman to take part in a Formula One race weekend in 22 years — and later founded the Dare to be Different initiative and led the Venturi Formula E team. Since 2023 she has been managing director of the F1 Academy.

Career

Born in Oban, Wolff began in karting before progressing through Formula Renault and Formula 3 in Britain. From 2006 to 2012 she raced in the DTM for Mercedes, scoring points finishes but no podiums in a highly competitive midfield. In 2012 she joined Williams F1 as a development driver, testing at Silverstone in 2013. At the 2014 British Grand Prix she drove in the first free practice session — the first woman to participate in an F1 weekend since Giovanna Amati in 1992 — though an engine problem cut her running short; she also ran in practice in Germany that year and tested again in 2015. She announced her retirement from driving at the end of 2015.

After racing

In 2016 Wolff founded Dare to be Different, a programme encouraging girls into motorsport, which later merged into the FIA's Girls on Track initiative. In 2018 she became team principal of the Venturi Formula E team, later also a shareholder, guiding it to race wins before leaving in 2022 when the team became Maserati MSG Racing. In March 2023 she was appointed managing director of F1 Academy, the all-female single-seater series launched by Formula One. She was appointed MBE in 2017 for services to women in sport. Her husband is Toto Wolff, team principal and co-owner of the Mercedes F1 team.
