Hippeastrum

Hippeastrum is a genus of about 90 species of perennial bulbous plants in the family Amaryllidaceae, native to tropical and subtropical regions of the Americas, from Argentina north to Mexico and the Caribbean.

Description

Species grow from large bulbs and produce hollow leafless stems bearing two to five large trumpet-shaped flowers, typically in shades of red, pink, orange, or white. The plants commonly sold as "amaryllis" for indoor flowering, especially around Christmas, are usually Hippeastrum hybrids rather than true Amaryllis.

Cultivation

Hybrid cultivars are popular houseplants, grown from bulbs that can be brought into flower indoors in winter by potting, watering, and providing warmth and light. After flowering, the bulbs require a period of leaf growth followed by dormancy to reflower.
