The Concert for Bangladesh

The Concert for Bangladesh was a pair of benefit concerts organised by former Beatles member George Harrison and Indian sitar virtuoso Ravi Shankar. The concerts were held on Sunday 1 August 1971 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, to raise international awareness of, and fund relief for, refugees from East Pakistan (now Bangladesh) fleeing the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War and the devastation of the 1970 Bhola cyclone.

Performers

The shows featured an all-star line-up including Harrison, Ringo Starr, Bob Dylan, Eric Clapton, Billy Preston, Leon Russell, and the band Badfinger, with Shankar and other Indian musicians opening with a set of Indian classical music. John Lennon and Paul McCartney did not take part. Harrison wrote the song "Bangla Desh" for the occasion.

Legacy

The event was a landmark in rock music philanthropy and a precursor of later benefit concerts such as Live Aid. The subsequent live triple album The Concert for Bangladesh won the Grammy Award for Album of the Year, and a concert film was released in 1972. Proceeds and later royalties, administered through UNICEF, raised millions of dollars for relief, although the release of funds was long delayed by tax and legal complications.
