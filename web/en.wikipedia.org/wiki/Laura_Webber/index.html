Laura Webber

Laura, one of the most iconic characters in American soap opera history

Laura Webber (also Laura Spencer) is a fictional character from the American soap opera General Hospital, broadcast on ABC. The character was introduced in 1974 and is most famously portrayed by Genie Francis, who joined the show in 1977 and has returned for numerous runs over the following decades. Laura is best known as one half of the supercouple "Luke and Laura", whose 1981 wedding episode drew about 30 million viewers, the largest audience in American daytime television history.

Storylines

Laura is the daughter of Lesley Webber. As a teenager she was involved in the killing of David Hamilton, and her romance with Luke Spencer — which began controversially with an assault storyline the show later reframed — became the centre of the series in the early 1980s, including the famous "Ice Princess" adventure plot. Luke and Laura married in November 1981. In later years Laura's storylines included her children Nikolas, Lucky, and Lulu; a catatonic state following psychological trauma; and repeated conflicts with the Cassadine family. In more recent storylines she has served as the mayor of Port Charles.

Reception

Laura is widely regarded as one of the defining characters of the soap opera genre. Genie Francis won the Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Drama Series in 2007 for her portrayal. The character's 1993 return to the show was one of the most publicised events in daytime television.
