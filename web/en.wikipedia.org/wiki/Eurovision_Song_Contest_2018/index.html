The Eurovision Song Contest 2018 was the 63rd edition of the annual Eurovision Song Contest. It took place in Lisbon, Portugal, following Salvador Sobral's victory in 2017 with "Amar pelos dois". It was the first time Portugal hosted the contest, under the slogan "All Aboard!".

Result

Israel won with Netta Barzilai performing "Toy", an electro-pop song incorporating a distinctive chicken-like vocal hook and looping techniques, scoring 529 points under the split jury–televote system. Cyprus finished second with Eleni Foureira's "Fuego", a fan favourite that topped many polls before the final, and Austria's Cesár Sampson came third. During the United Kingdom's performance, a stage invader snatched the microphone from singer SuRie; she declined the offer to perform again.

Participation

Forty-three countries took part, equalling the record. Russia returned after its 2017 absence. For the first time since 2011, no competing song was performed entirely in English by all finalists' winner — a trend toward national-language entries continued following Sobral's Portuguese-language win.
