King's College is a constituent college of the University of Cambridge, formally named The King's College of Our Lady and Saint Nicholas in Cambridge. It was founded in 1441 by King Henry VI.

Chapel

King's College Chapel is one of the finest surviving examples of late medieval English architecture and is famous for its fan vaulting and its choir, whose Festival of Nine Lessons and Carols is broadcast worldwide each Christmas Eve.

Alumni

Notable alumni include Alan Turing, John Maynard Keynes, E. M. Forster and Salman Rushdie.
