A PT6A turboprop engine

The Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6 is a turboprop aircraft engine produced by Pratt & Whitney Canada. It is one of the most popular turboprop engines in history, powering aircraft such as the Beechcraft King Air, the de Havilland Canada DHC-6 Twin Otter, the Pilatus PC-12 and the Cessna 208 Caravan.

Design

The PT6 is a free-turbine design with a reverse-flow gas path: air enters at the rear and exhausts near the front, a layout that simplifies installation and maintenance. Power output ranges from roughly 500 to over 1,900 shaft horsepower across variants; the PT6C and PT6T turboshaft versions power helicopters.
