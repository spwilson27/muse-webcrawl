Israel Kamakawiwoʻole

Israel Kaʻanoʻi Kamakawiwoʻole (20 May 1959 – 26 June 1997), known as Bruddah Iz, was a Native Hawaiian singer and musician celebrated for his ukulele playing and his medley of "Somewhere Over the Rainbow/What a Wonderful World".

Born20 May 1959, Honolulu, Hawaii, US
Died26 June 1997, Honolulu, Hawaii
GenresHawaiian, folk, reggae
InstrumentsVocals, ukulele

Career

Kamakawiwoʻole co-founded the group Mākaha Sons of Niʻihau in 1976. His solo album Facing Future (1993) became the best-selling Hawaiian album of all time, and his gentle medley, recorded in a single take in 1988, achieved worldwide fame after his death through film and television use.

Legacy

He was also an advocate for Hawaiian sovereignty and indigenous rights. At his funeral, the Hawaiian state flag flew at half-staff, and his ashes were scattered into the Pacific Ocean at Mākua Beach.
