Mozambique is a country in Southeast Africa bordered by the Indian Ocean to the east, Tanzania to the north, Malawi and Zambia to the northwest, Zimbabwe to the west, and Eswatini (Swaziland) and South Africa to the southwest. It is one of the larger countries of southern Africa, with a total area of about 801,590 square kilometres.

Physical geography

The country is divided by the Zambezi River into two broad topographic regions. North of the Zambezi, the landscape is dominated by hills, plateaus and rugged highlands, rising toward the shores of Lake Malawi (known in Mozambique as Lago Niassa). South of the Zambezi the land is mostly lowland, broadening toward the coast and forming part of the great coastal plain of southeastern Africa.

Mozambique's highest point is Mount Binga (2,436 m), part of the Chimanimani Mountains on the Zimbabwean border. Other notable high ground includes Mount Gorongosa and the Mulanje massif near the Malawi frontier.

Rivers and lakes

Of Mozambique's many rivers, the Zambezi is by far the largest, entering the country at Tete and flowing southeast to a broad delta on the Indian Ocean. Other major rivers include the Rovuma (forming much of the northern border with Tanzania), the Limpopo in the south, the Save, the Buzi, the Pungwe and the Licungo. The Cahora Bassa Dam on the Zambezi impounds one of the largest artificial lakes in Africa.

Climate

Mozambique has a tropical climate with two seasons: a wet season from October to March and a dry season from April to September. Temperatures and rainfall vary with altitude and distance from the coast. The country is periodically struck by tropical cyclones moving across the Mozambique Channel, and the low-lying river deltas are prone to severe flooding.

Environment

Habitats range from coastal mangroves and coral reefs to miombo woodland, savanna and montane forest. Notable protected areas include Gorongosa National Park, Niassa Reserve and the Bazaruto Archipelago. Wildlife includes elephants, lions, hippos and a rich marine fauna of dugongs, turtles and reef fish.
