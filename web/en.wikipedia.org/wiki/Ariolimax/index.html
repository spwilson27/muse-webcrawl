Ariolimax is a genus of North American terrestrial slugs in the family Ariolimacidae, commonly known as banana slugs for the bright yellow colouration of several species. They are among the largest land slugs in the world, with some individuals exceeding 25 centimetres.

Species and habitat

Recognised species include Ariolimax columbianus, A. californicus, and A. dolichophallus. Banana slugs inhabit the moist coniferous forests of the Pacific coast from Alaska to California, where they feed on leaves, detritus, fungi, and animal droppings, and serve as decomposers and as prey for birds and mammals.

Biology

Like other slugs, they are simultaneous hermaphrodites. Their mucus contains compounds that can numb the tongues of predators. The banana slug is the mascot of the University of California, Santa Cruz.
