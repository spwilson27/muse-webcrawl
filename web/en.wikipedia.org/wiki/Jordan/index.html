Jordan

Jordan, officially the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, is a country in the Levant region of West Asia, bordered by Syria, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Israel and Palestine, with a short coast on the Gulf of Aqaba. Its capital and largest city is Amman; its population is about 11 million, including very large numbers of Palestinian and Syrian refugees.

Jordan is home to the Nabataean city of Petra, carved into rose-red rock and one of the New7Wonders of the World, as well as the Dead Sea shore, Roman ruins at Jerash, and the desert valley of Wadi Rum. The Hashemite monarchy, under King Abdullah II, is known for relative stability in a turbulent region and for peace with Israel since 1994. Water scarcity is among Jordan's most severe challenges — it is one of the most water-poor countries on Earth.
