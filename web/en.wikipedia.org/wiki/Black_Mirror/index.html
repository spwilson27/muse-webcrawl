Black Mirror

Black Mirror is a British science fiction anthology television series created by Charlie Brooker. Individual episodes explore a wide variety of genres but are mostly set in near-future dystopias with a science fiction or satirical speculative-fiction premise, examining modern society's relationship with technology and its unforeseen consequences. The title refers to the black screen of a switched-off phone, tablet or television.

History

The series premiered on Channel 4 in December 2011. After two series and a Christmas special, it moved to Netflix in 2016, which commissioned longer runs with larger budgets and international casts. Notable episodes include "The National Anthem", "Fifteen Million Merits", "San Junipero", "USS Callister", "Nosedive" and "White Christmas". In 2018 Netflix released Black Mirror: Bandersnatch, an interactive film.

Reception

The series has won multiple Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Television Movie for "San Junipero", "USS Callister" and "Bandersnatch". It is frequently compared to The Twilight Zone for its standalone, twist-driven storytelling, and its name has entered common usage as shorthand for unsettling technology scenarios.
