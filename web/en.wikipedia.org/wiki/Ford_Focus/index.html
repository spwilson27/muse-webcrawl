Ford Focus

ManufacturerFord Motor Company
Production1998–2025
ClassCompact car (C-segment)

The Ford Focus is a compact car manufactured by Ford from 1998, as the replacement for the Ford Escort in most markets. Styled under Ford's "New Edge" design language, it was sold as a hatchback, sedan, and estate in Europe, North America, and many other markets.

Generations

The first generation (1998–2004) was praised for its handling and was European Car of the Year in 1999. Four generations followed; performance versions included the Focus ST and the all-wheel-drive Focus RS (2016), producing 350 hp. A battery-electric Focus Electric was also sold in limited numbers.

Motorsport and end of production

The Focus was a mainstay of the World Rally Championship, winning the manufacturers' title in 2006 and 2007 with Ford's factory team. Ford ended North American sales after 2018 as it shifted toward SUVs and trucks; global production at Saarlouis, Germany, concluded in 2025, closing one of Ford's most successful nameplates.
