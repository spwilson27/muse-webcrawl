Hartry Field

Hartry H. Field (born November 30, 1946) is an American philosopher. He is Silver Professor of Philosophy at New York University and works in philosophy of mathematics, philosophy of science, epistemology, and philosophy of logic.

Nominalism

In his book Science Without Numbers (1980), Field defended nominalism by arguing that mathematical entities such as numbers and sets are useful fictions: he showed how Newtonian physics could be reformulated without quantifying over mathematical objects, and argued that mathematics is "conservative" over nominalistic physics. This programme became a central reference point in debates about the indispensability argument for mathematical realism.

Later work

Field has also written influentially on truth and paradox, developing deflationary and paracomplete accounts of truth in works such as Saving Truth from Paradox (2008), on vagueness, and on a priori knowledge.
