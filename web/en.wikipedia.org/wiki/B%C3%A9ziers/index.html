Béziers is a city in the Hérault department of southern France, on the Orb river a few kilometres from the Mediterranean. It is one of France's oldest cities, with settlement evidence dating back to Neolithic times and a major Gallo-Roman past.

History

Béziers is infamous for the massacre of 1209 during the Albigensian Crusade, when crusaders stormed the city and killed much of its population — the episode associated with the (probably apocryphal) phrase "Kill them all; God will know his own." The Canal du Midi passes through the city over the Orb aqueduct, and the Fonseranes locks are nearby.

Culture

The city is known for the Féria de Béziers, a major August bullfighting festival, and for its rugby union club, AS Béziers Hérault.
