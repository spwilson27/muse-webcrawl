The Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) was an international body created by the Financial Stability Board in December 2015 to develop consistent, voluntary climate-related financial risk disclosures for companies, banks and investors. It was chaired by Michael Bloomberg.

Recommendations

In June 2017 the TCFD published recommendations structured around four thematic areas: governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics and targets. Organisations were urged to disclose the actual and potential impacts of climate-related risks and opportunities, including through scenario analysis such as a 2 °C or lower scenario.

Legacy

The framework was widely adopted by regulators and stock exchanges, and its architecture was incorporated into the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards issued by the International Sustainability Standards Board in 2023. Having fulfilled its remit, the TCFD disbanded in October 2023, with monitoring of disclosure progress passing to the IFRS Foundation.
