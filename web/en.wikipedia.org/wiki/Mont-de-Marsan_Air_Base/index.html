Mont-de-Marsan Air Base

Mont-de-Marsan Air Base (French: Base aérienne 118 Mont-de-Marsan) is a major air base of the French Air and Space Force located near Mont-de-Marsan in the Landes department of southwestern France. It is one of the principal bases of the French air force and plays a central role in France's nuclear deterrent and in military flight testing.

The base was established in its modern form in 1945. It hosts the military flight test centre (CEAM, Centre d'expertise aérienne militaire), created under Colonel Constantin Rozanoff, after whom the base is named ("Colonel Rozanoff"). Units at the base have operated aircraft including the Mirage 2000 and the Dassault Rafale; the base hosts a Rafale squadron assigned to the nuclear strike role with the ASMP-A missile, as well as air defence and experimentation units. During the Cold War testing era, the surrounding Landes region also hosted the missile test range at Biscarrosse.
