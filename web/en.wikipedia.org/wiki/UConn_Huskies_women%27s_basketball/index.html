The UConn Huskies women's basketball team is the intercollegiate women's basketball program representing the University of Connecticut in Storrs, Connecticut. Coached by Geno Auriemma since 1985, the Huskies have become the most successful program in NCAA Division I women's basketball history.

History

UConn won its first national championship in 1995, completing an undefeated 35–0 season by defeating Tennessee in the title game. The program went on to win numerous further titles, including back-to-back-to-back-to-back championships from 2013 to 2016 under stars such as Breanna Stewart. The Huskies hold the longest winning streaks in college basketball history, including a 111-game streak from 2014 to 2017 and an earlier 90-game streak from 2008 to 2010.

Players

The program has produced many of the sport's greatest players, including Rebecca Lobo, Sue Bird, Diana Taurasi, Maya Moore, Tina Charles, Breanna Stewart, and Paige Bueckers, many of whom became WNBA stars and Olympic gold medalists.

Rivalries

UConn's rivalry with the Tennessee Lady Volunteers, coached by Pat Summitt, defined the sport for decades. The Huskies also developed prominent rivalries with Notre Dame and South Carolina.
