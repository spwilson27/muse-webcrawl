Rheinmetall

Rheinmetall AG
Industry: Defence, automotive
Founded: 1889
Headquarters: Düsseldorf, Germany
Products: Armoured vehicles, weapons, ammunition

Rheinmetall AG is a German defence and automotive technology group headquartered in Düsseldorf. Founded in 1889 as a munitions company, it is today Germany's largest defence contractor and one of the largest arms manufacturers in the world, structured into divisions covering vehicle systems, weapons and ammunition, and electronic solutions, alongside civilian power systems.

Products

Rheinmetall is best known for the Rh-120 120 mm smoothbore tank gun fitted to the Leopard 2 and licence-built for the M1 Abrams, and for its role in armoured vehicles: it co-produces the Leopard 2 and developed the Lynx infantry fighting vehicle and the KF51 Panther main battle tank concept. Its subsidiary Rheinmetall MAN Military Vehicles builds military trucks, and the company is a major producer of artillery and medium-calibre ammunition, air-defence systems such as Skynex, and soldier electronics.

History

The company supplied the Imperial German Army in the First World War and the Wehrmacht in the Second, a period in which it used forced labour — a history addressed in post-war compensation funds. Demilitarised after 1945, it rebuilt from the 1950s with the rearmament of the Bundeswehr. Rheinmetall expanded internationally through acquisitions, including Mauser, Oerlikon Contraves and the Spanish ammunition maker Expal. Demand surged after Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine, when Germany's Zeitenwende rearmament programme brought the company large orders and admission to the DAX stock index in 2023.
