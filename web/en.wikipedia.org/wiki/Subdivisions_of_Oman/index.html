Subdivisions of Oman

The Sultanate of Oman is divided into eleven governorates (muhafazah), which are further subdivided into provinces (wilayat, singular wilayah). This system was established by royal decree in October 2011, which reorganized the country's previous regions and governorates into eleven governorates in order to promote decentralization and regional development.

Provinces (wilayat)

Oman comprises 61 wilayat, each administered by a wali appointed by the government. Wilayat handle local administration, including municipal services, local development projects, and the registration of certain legal matters, under the authority of the Ministry of Interior and the governors of the governorates.

History

Before 2011, Oman was divided into five regions (mintaqah) — Ad Dakhiliyah, Al Batinah, Al Wusta, Ash Sharqiyah, and Ad Dhahirah — and four governorates: Muscat, Dhofar, Musandam, and Al Buraimi. The 2011 decree split Al Batinah and Ash Sharqiyah into northern and southern governorates to bring administration closer to local populations.
