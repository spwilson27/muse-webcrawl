Rhode Island School of Design

The Rhode Island School of Design (RISD, pronounced "RIZ-dee") is a private art and design school in Providence, Rhode Island. Founded in 1877, it is consistently ranked among the foremost art institutions in the United States and the world.

History

RISD was founded by a group of women, led by Helen Adelia Rowe Metcalf, using surplus funds from Rhode Island's exhibit at the 1876 Centennial Exposition in Philadelphia. The school grew alongside the RISD Museum, which holds a collection of roughly 100,000 works of art and design.

Academics

The school enrolls roughly 2,500 students and offers bachelor's and master's degrees across some 19 majors, including painting, sculpture, graphic design, industrial design, architecture, photography, and illustration. Its campus sits adjacent to Brown University on College Hill, and the two institutions operate a dual degree program. RISD is accredited by the New England Commission of Higher Education.

Notable alumni

Alumni include glass artist Dale Chihuly, designer Shepard Fairey, filmmaker Gus Van Sant, and Talking Heads members David Byrne, Tina Weymouth, and Chris Frantz, who met as students in the 1970s.
