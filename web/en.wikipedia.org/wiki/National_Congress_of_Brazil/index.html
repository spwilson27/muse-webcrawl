National Congress of Brazil

National Congress of Brazil
Congresso Nacional do Brasil
Type: Bicameral
Houses: Federal Senate; Chamber of Deputies
Meeting place: Nereu Ramos Palace, Brasília

The National Congress of Brazil (Portuguese: Congresso Nacional do Brasil) is the legislative body of Brazil's federal government. It is bicameral, consisting of the Federal Senate (upper house) and the Chamber of Deputies (lower house).

Composition

The Federal Senate has 81 seats, three for each of Brazil's 26 states and the Federal District, with senators serving eight-year terms. The Chamber of Deputies has 513 seats, apportioned among the states by population, with deputies serving four-year terms.

Building

Since 1960 the Congress has met in Brasília, in a palace designed by architect Oscar Niemeyer. Its twin towers are flanked by a dome (housing the Senate) and a bowl (housing the Chamber of Deputies), symbols of the two chambers.
