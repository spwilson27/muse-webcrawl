Meeting

A meeting is when two or more people come together to discuss one or more topics, often in a formal or business setting, but meetings also occur in many other environments. Meetings may be convened for decision-making, problem-solving, information sharing, planning or social purposes.

Types and conduct

Meetings range from informal conversations to highly structured proceedings governed by parliamentary procedure such as Robert's Rules of Order, with a chairperson, an agenda circulated in advance, and minutes recording decisions. Common workplace formats include team briefings, board meetings, annual general meetings and, increasingly, video conferences that allow remote participation.

Effectiveness

Meetings consume a large share of managerial time, and management literature frequently criticises poorly run meetings as unproductive. Recommended practices include setting a clear purpose, inviting only necessary participants, keeping to time, and recording agreed actions with owners and deadlines.
