Electronicore
Other names: synthcore
Origins: late 2000s, metalcore + electronic music
Typical elements: breakdowns, synthesisers, autotuned vocals

Electronicore, also called synthcore, is a fusion genre that combines metalcore and post-hardcore with electronic music, incorporating synthesisers, sequenced beats, dance and dubstep influences, and heavily processed vocals alongside screamed singing and breakdowns.

Characteristics

Songs typically alternate aggressive metalcore passages — down-tuned guitars, double-kick drumming, and breakdowns — with melodic choruses and electronic interludes drawn from trance, dubstep, and chiptune. Production often features prominent Auto-Tune on clean vocals.

History

The style emerged in the late 2000s. British band Enter Shikari were early popularisers, while American acts such as Attack Attack! and I See Stars brought the sound to a wide metalcore audience around 2009–2010. Japanese acts including Fear, and Loathing in Las Vegas developed a parallel scene.

Reception

Electronicore was divisive among metal purists but commercially successful on the Warped Tour circuit, and its dubstep-influenced breakdowns anticipated later crossovers between metal and electronic dance music.
