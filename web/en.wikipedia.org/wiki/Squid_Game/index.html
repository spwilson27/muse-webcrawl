Squid Game (Korean: 오징어 게임) is a South Korean survival drama television series created by Hwang Dong-hyuk for Netflix. It follows 456 deeply indebted players who risk their lives playing a series of children's games for the chance to win a ₩45.6 billion prize; losing a game means death.

Contestants in the dormitory beneath the growing prize piggy bank

Premise

Seong Gi-hun, a divorced chauffeur and gambling addict, is invited to compete after losing a game of ddakji with a recruiter. On a remote island he joins 455 other desperate players, guarded by masked staff in pink jumpsuits overseen by the Front Man. The games — Red Light, Green Light; the dalgona candy challenge; tug of war; marbles; the glass bridge; and the titular squid game — eliminate players one round at a time.

Reception

The first season became Netflix's most-watched series at launch, topping charts in 94 countries and drawing over 142 million household viewers in its first four weeks. It won several Primetime Emmy Awards, including Outstanding Lead Actor for Lee Jung-jae and Outstanding Directing for Hwang. A second season premiered in December 2024 and a third and final season in June 2025.

Themes

The series is widely read as an allegory of South Korean household debt, income inequality and the human cost of late-stage capitalism, contrasting childhood nostalgia with lethal competition.
