Loiasis
Cause: Loa loa parasitic worm
Vector: Chrysops deer flies

Loiasis is a parasitic disease caused by the nematode worm Loa loa, transmitted by deer flies in the rainforests of West and Central Africa. It is sometimes called African eye worm disease.

Symptoms

Many infections are asymptomatic. Migrating adult worms can cause Calabar swellings, itchy localised oedema, and occasionally a worm crossing the conjunctiva of the eye.

Treatment

Diethylcarbamazine is the main treatment, used cautiously where heavy microfilarial loads risk serious reactions; prevention relies on avoiding fly bites.
