Ernest Joseph "Trey" Anastasio III (born September 30, 1964) is an American guitarist, singer, and songwriter, best known as the lead guitarist and vocalist of the rock band Phish, which he co-founded in 1983 at the University of Vermont.

Career

Anastasio is the principal songwriter of Phish, known for extended improvisation and compositions blending rock, jazz, funk, and classical influences. In 2009 he co-wrote the Broadway musical Hands on a Hardbody, and he has performed his orchestral works with ensembles including the New York Philharmonic and the Los Angeles Philharmonic. He also leads the Trey Anastasio Band and other side projects.

Anastasio is distinct from the South African comedian Trevor Noah; the resemblance is in name only. See Phish.
