Morris Motors

Morris Motors Limited was a British privately owned motor vehicle manufacturing company formed in 1919 to take over the assets of William Morris's WRM Motors Limited. Founded in Cowley, Oxford, it became one of Britain's largest car manufacturers, famous for models such as the Morris Oxford, the Morris Eight, and the Morris Minor.

History

William Morris began building cars in 1912; his first car, the "Bullnose" Oxford, appeared in 1913. By the mid-1920s Morris was Britain's largest car maker, using American-style mass production and bought-in components. The company took over Wolseley, MG, and other marques, forming the Nuffield Organisation. In 1952 Morris merged with Austin to form the British Motor Corporation (BMC).

Notable models

  • Morris Oxford (1913–1971, various generations)
  • Morris Eight (1935–1948)
  • Morris Minor (1948–1971), designed by Alec Issigonis, the first British car to sell over a million units
  • Morris Marina (1971–1980)
