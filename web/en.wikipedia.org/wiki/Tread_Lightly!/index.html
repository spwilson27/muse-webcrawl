Tread Lightly!

Tread Lightly! is a nonprofit organization in the United States dedicated to promoting responsible outdoor recreation, particularly motorized and mechanized recreation on public and private lands. Founded in 1985 as a program of the United States Forest Service, it became an independent nonprofit in 1990.

Principles

The organization's outreach is summarized by the TREAD principles:

  • Travel responsibly on trails and in designated areas.
  • Respect the rights of others, including private property owners and other trail users.
  • Educate yourself before your trip by obtaining travel maps and regulations.
  • Avoid sensitive areas such as meadows, lakeshores, and wetlands.
  • Do your part by modelling appropriate behaviour and leaving the area better than you found it.

Programs

Tread Lightly! runs education campaigns, trail stewardship and restoration projects, and partnerships with manufacturers, land managers such as the Bureau of Land Management, and recreation clubs. Its message complements the Leave No Trace ethic, with an emphasis on vehicle-based recreation including off-roading, boating, hunting, and shooting sports.
