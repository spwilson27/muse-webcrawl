Yas Island

Yas Island (Arabic: جزيرة ياس) is an artificial island in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, developed by the emirate's investment and tourism authorities as a major leisure and entertainment destination. The island covers about 25 square kilometres (9.7 sq mi) and is home to the Yas Marina Circuit, several theme parks, a waterpark, hotels, a marina, a golf course, shopping centres, and residential districts. Development began in 2006 under the developer Aldar Properties, and the island's attractions have made it one of the most-visited destinations in the UAE.

Yas Marina Circuit

The island's centrepiece, the Yas Marina Circuit, was designed by Hermann Tilke and opened in October 2009; it has hosted the season-closing Abu Dhabi Grand Prix of Formula One every year since, notable for its twilight race start, the pit lane that passes under the circuit through a tunnel, and the Yas Hotel (now the W Abu Dhabi), which straddles the track.

Attractions

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, opened in 2010, is the largest indoor theme park in the world and home to Formula Rossa, the world's fastest roller coaster (240 km/h). Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi opened in 2018 as the largest indoor theme park in the Middle East. Yas Waterworld, a water park with over 40 rides, opened in 2013, and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi — the first SeaWorld outside the United States — opened in 2023. Other facilities include the Yas Links golf course, the Yas Mall shopping centre, the du Forum live-music venue, and Etihad Park.

Development

Conceived as part of Abu Dhabi's Plan 2030 diversification of its oil-dependent economy, the island continued expanding in the 2020s with new residential zones and further leisure projects under the operator Miral, including Warner Bros. World expansions and the Yas Island waterfront.
