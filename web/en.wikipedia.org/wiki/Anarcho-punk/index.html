Anarcho-punk is a subgenre of punk rock that promotes anarchist politics explicitly, rather than treating rebellion as a general attitude. Emerging in Britain in the late 1970s, anarcho-punk bands combined aggressive, often deliberately crude music with detailed political lyrics and a do-it-yourself infrastructure of independent labels, fanzines, squats, and benefit gigs.

History

The band Crass, formed in Essex in 1977, is credited with founding the movement. Living communally at Dial House, Crass released records on their own label at low fixed prices ("pay no more than…"), printed political essays on their sleeves, and championed causes including nuclear disarmament (CND), animal liberation, and opposition to the Falklands War — their single "How Does It Feel (To Be the Mother of a Thousand Dead)" addressed Margaret Thatcher directly. Around them grew a scene including Poison Girls, Zounds, Flux of Pink Indians, Conflict, the Subhumans, and Rudimentary Peni, connected by venues, squats, and the Stop the City demonstrations of 1983–84.

Ideology and culture

Anarcho-punk promoted a practical anarchism: vegetarianism and veganism, opposition to vivisection and hunting (including support for hunt saboteurs), squatting and communal living, anti-militarism, and hostility to organised religion, the monarchy, and the music industry. The movement popularised the DIY ethic — self-released records, photocopied zines, and gigs in non-commercial spaces — and its visual style of stencilled graphics and the circled-A symbol. Chumbawamba, formed in this scene, later achieved mainstream success with "Tubthumping" (1997) while retaining their anarchist commitments.

Influence

Anarcho-punk fed directly into crust punk, grindcore's political wing, and the American hardcore scene's straight-edge and vegan movements. Its infrastructure of independent labels and all-ages, low-cost shows became a template for underground music worldwide, and its animal-rights and anti-globalisation activism anticipated movements of the 1990s and 2000s.
