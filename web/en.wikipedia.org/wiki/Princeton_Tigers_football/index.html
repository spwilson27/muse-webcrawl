Princeton Tigers football

University: Princeton University
Conference: Ivy League
Division: NCAA Division I FCS
Stadium: Princeton Stadium (formerly Palmer Stadium)
First season: 1869
Claimed national titles: 28

The Princeton Tigers football program represents Princeton University in college football at the NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision level, as a member of the Ivy League. Princeton played Rutgers in the first intercollegiate football game on 6 November 1869, winning 6–4 under rules resembling soccer.

Early dominance

In the sport's formative decades Princeton was a national power, claiming 28 national championships, the last in 1950, when Dick Kazmaier starred for an undefeated team; Kazmaier won the Heisman Trophy in 1951, Princeton's only Heisman winner. The Tigers' orange and black colours and their rivalry with Yale and Harvard date from this era.

Ivy League era

Since the Ivy League formally adopted football in 1956, Princeton has won numerous shared and outright Ivy titles, including a perfect 10–0 season in 2018 under coach Bob Surace. Ivy rules bar the Tigers from the FCS playoffs. Home games were long played at Palmer Stadium (1914–1996), replaced by Princeton Stadium on the same site.
