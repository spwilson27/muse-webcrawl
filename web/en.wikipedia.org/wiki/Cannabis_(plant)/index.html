Cannabis (plant)

Cannabis (commonly known as marijuana and hemp, among many other names) is a genus of flowering plants in the family Cannabaceae. The number of species within the genus is disputed; three species may be recognized: Cannabis sativa, C. indica, and C. ruderalis, though alternatively they may be treated as subspecies of a single species, C. sativa. The plant is indigenous to Eastern Asia, but is now of cosmopolitan distribution due to widespread cultivation.

Botany

Cannabis is an annual, dioecious, herbaceous flowering plant. The leaves are palmately compound with serrate leaflets. Male plants produce pollen-bearing flowers in loose panicles; female plants produce dense clusters of flowers whose glandular trichomes secrete a resin rich in cannabinoids. Wild cannabis is wind-pollinated. Chemical varieties ("chemovars") are classified by their cannabinoid profile — drug-type plants selected for high THC, and fibre-type hemp selected for low THC and tall, fibrous stems.

History

Cannabis is among the earliest cultivated plants. Evidence of its use for fibre and seed in China dates back several thousand years, and it appears in ancient Indian, Assyrian, and Greek sources; Herodotus described Scythian ritual inhalation of cannabis smoke. Hemp was a strategic commodity for rope and sailcloth in the age of sail. In the 20th century, most countries criminalized the plant, following campaigns in the United States culminating in the Marihuana Tax Act of 1937 and the 1961 UN Single Convention.

Uses and legal status

The plant is used as a recreational and medicinal drug, for industrial fibre and seed oil (hemp), and in religious rituals. Since the late 20th century many jurisdictions have decriminalized possession or legalized medical and recreational use; Uruguay (2013) and Canada (2018) were the first modern nations to legalize recreational cannabis nationally. Hemp cultivation has been re-legalized in many countries, including the United States under the 2018 Farm Bill, which defines hemp as cannabis with no more than 0.3% THC.
