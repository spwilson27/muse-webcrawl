Rodrigues

Rodrigues (French: Île Rodrigues) is a volcanic island in the Indian Ocean, about 560 km east of Mauritius, of which it is an autonomous region. It is the smallest of the Mascarene Islands, about 18 by 8 km, surrounded by a large lagoon.

Named by Portuguese explorer Diogo Rodrigues in 1528, the uninhabited island was later settled by Dutch, French and British colonists. It was home to the extinct Rodrigues solitaire, a flightless relative of the dodo, and to endemic species including the Rodrigues flying fox and the Rodrigues warbler.

The capital is Port Mathurin. The population of around 43,000, mostly of African and mixed descent speaking Rodriguan Creole, depends on fishing, agriculture, handicrafts and growing tourism. Since 2002 Rodrigues has had its own Regional Assembly within the Republic of Mauritius.
