St Briavels Castle

St Briavels Castle is a medieval moated castle in the village of St Briavels, Gloucestershire, England, overlooking the River Wye on the edge of the Forest of Dean. Built in the twelfth century, it served as an administrative centre for the forest and as a hunting lodge for King John.

History

The castle was constructed between roughly 1075 and 1129 and became an important royal arsenal: crossbow bolts and other munitions for the king's armies were manufactured in the Forest of Dean and stored here. The gatehouse, built under Edward I, features unusual spiked portcullis defences. Later used as a prison and a school, the castle is now a youth hostel — often described as one of the most atmospheric hostel locations in Britain — and is managed as a scheduled ancient monument and Grade I listed building.
