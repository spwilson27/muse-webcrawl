Lost Ark (video game)

DeveloperSmilegate RPG
PublisherSmilegate; Amazon Games (West)
Released2019 (South Korea); 2022 (West)
GenreAction role-playing, MMORPG

Lost Ark is a free-to-play isometric action role-playing game developed by Smilegate RPG, a subsidiary of South Korean developer Smilegate. First released in South Korea in November 2019, it was published in Europe, North America and South America by Amazon Games in February 2022, where it quickly became one of the most-played games on Steam.

Gameplay

Players explore the fantasy world of Arkesia from an isometric perspective, choosing from classes grouped into archetypes such as Warrior, Martial Artist, Gunner, Mage and Assassin, each with advanced subclasses. Combat emphasises fast, spectacle-heavy skills, and endgame content centres on cooperative raids, dungeons and boss encounters demanding precise team mechanics. The game also features sailing between continents, strongholds, life skills and collectible completion systems.

Reception

Lost Ark was praised for its combat feel, cinematic dungeons and generous free content, while criticism in the West focused on repetitive daily tasks, monetisation and bot problems. It won several awards in South Korea, including at the 2019 Korea Game Awards, and its Western launch reached peak concurrent player counts above 1.3 million on Steam.
