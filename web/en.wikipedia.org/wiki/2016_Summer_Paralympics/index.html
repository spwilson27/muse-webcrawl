The 2016 Summer Paralympics, the 15th Summer Paralympic Games, were held in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, from 7 to 18 September 2016. They were the first Paralympics held in Latin America and the Southern Hemisphere's second, following the 2016 Summer Olympics in the same city.

Overview

About 4,300 athletes from 159 National Paralympic Committees competed in 22 sports, with paracanoe and paratriathlon making their Paralympic debuts. A small Independent Paralympic Athletes team of refugee athletes also competed. China topped the medal table for the fourth consecutive Games, followed by Great Britain and Ukraine.

Russian ban and controversies

The Games were overshadowed by the Russian doping scandal: following the McLaren report, the International Paralympic Committee banned the entire Russian team — a stricter line than the IOC's — and Russia's appeal was rejected. Financial troubles in Rio also forced budget cuts and fears of low attendance, but strong late ticket sales, cheap prices and school programmes produced packed venues and a celebratory atmosphere widely compared to London 2012.

Highlights

Iranian cyclist Bahman Golbarnezhad died after a crash in the road race, the first death of a Paralympian in competition. On the track, Algeria's Abdellatif Baka won the T13 1500 m in a time faster than the Olympic champion's in the same stadium weeks earlier. Host Brazil finished eighth in medals, led by sprinter Petrucio Ferreira and swimmer Daniel Dias.
