Skin

StatusLargest organ of the human body

[Image: A close cross-section diagram of human skin showing epidermis, dermis and glands]

Skin is the layer of tissue covering the body of vertebrates — in humans the largest organ, with a surface area around two square metres. It protects against microbes, chemicals and UV radiation, regulates temperature through sweat and blood flow, senses touch, pressure and pain, and makes vitamin D in sunlight.

Structure and care

The skin's layers — epidermis, dermis and hypodermis — contain hair, glands and nerves. Skin colour varies with melanin, an adaptation to ultraviolet levels. Conditions range from eczema and acne to skin cancer (melanoma), against which sun protection is the main defence; burns are graded by depth through the layers.
