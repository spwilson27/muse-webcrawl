Manchester Airport

Manchester Airport (IATA: MAN, ICAO: EGCC) is an international airport in Manchester, England, located about 9 miles (14 km) south-west of the city centre. It is the third-busiest airport in the United Kingdom by passenger traffic and the busiest outside London, handling around 28–30 million passengers a year in recent years. The airport is owned by Manchester Airports Group and has three passenger terminals and two runways.

History

The airport opened in 1938 at Ringway, a name still used locally. It served as RAF Ringway during the Second World War, where parachute training and aircraft assembly took place. Post-war growth brought transatlantic services; the main terminal opened in 1962, Terminal 2 in 1993, and a second runway in 2001 after a high-profile environmental protest campaign. A £1 billion transformation programme has consolidated much traffic into an extended Terminal 2.

Operations

Manchester is a base for Ryanair, easyJet, Jet2, TUI and Virgin Atlantic, among others, with scheduled services across Europe and long-haul flights to North America, the Middle East and Asia. Concorde G-BOAC was retired to the airport's Runway Visitor Park, which also displays other historic aircraft.

Ground transport

The airport station offers direct trains to Manchester Piccadilly and across northern England, and the Metrolink tram line connects the airport with the city centre. It is served by the M56 motorway.
