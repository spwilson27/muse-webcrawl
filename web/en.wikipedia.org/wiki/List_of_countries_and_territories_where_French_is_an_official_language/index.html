List of countries and territories where French is an official language

French is an official language in 29 countries across several continents, making it one of the most geographically widespread languages in the world. Most of these countries are members of the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie (OIF).

Dependent territories

French is also official in territories including French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Mayotte, Réunion, New Caledonia, French Polynesia, Saint Pierre and Miquelon, Wallis and Futuna, and the French Southern and Antarctic Lands.
