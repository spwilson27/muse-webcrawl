The Swiftsure class was a class of nuclear-powered fleet submarines (SSNs) in service with the Royal Navy from the mid-1970s until 2010. Six boats were built by Vickers at Barrow-in-Furness. They were the first Royal Navy submarines built with a pump-jet propulsor rather than a conventional propeller, making them quieter than their predecessors.

Design

The class was a development of the preceding Valiant class, with a shorter hull, revised bow planes, and improved sensors and weapons control. The boats were armed with torpedo tubes capable of firing Tigerfish and later Spearfish torpedoes, Sub-Harpoon anti-ship missiles, and Tomahawk cruise missiles in later service.

Service

Swiftsure-class boats served throughout the Cold War and afterwards. HMS Spartan and HMS Splendid deployed to the South Atlantic during the 1982 Falklands War, and Splendid later fired Tomahawk missiles during the 2003 Iraq War. The last of the class, HMS Sceptre, paid off in December 2010, ending 36 years of service.

Boats

The six boats were HMS Swiftsure, HMS Sovereign, HMS Superb, HMS Sceptre, HMS Spartan, and HMS Splendid.
