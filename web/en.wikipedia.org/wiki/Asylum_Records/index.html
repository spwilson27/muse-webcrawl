Asylum Records is an American record label, founded in 1971 by David Geffen and partner Elliot Roberts. It was taken over by Warner Communications (now Warner Music Group) in 1973, and later merged with Elektra Records to become Elektra/Asylum Records.

After various incarnations, it is currently a division of the Warner Music Group, and focuses principally on hip hop and rock acts.

History

Geffen founded Asylum after being unable to secure a record deal for his client Jackson Browne with Atlantic Records. The label quickly became a home for the West Coast singer-songwriter movement, signing Browne, Joni Mitchell, Linda Ronstadt, and the Eagles, whose 1972 debut album was an Asylum release. Tom Waits, Warren Zevon, and Judee Sill also recorded for the label.

Elektra/Asylum and later years

In 1973 Asylum was acquired by Warner and merged operationally with Elektra Records, with Geffen running the combined company until 1975. The label's identity was revived several times: as a country label in the 1990s, and from 2004 as an urban and hip-hop imprint associated with artists such as Gucci Mane. In the 2010s and 2020s Asylum again operated as a frontline label within Warner, including as the UK label of Ed Sheeran's early releases through a partnership with Atlantic.
