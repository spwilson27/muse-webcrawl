The 2010 San Marino motorcycle Grand Prix was the twelfth round of the 2010 Grand Prix motorcycle racing season. It took place on the weekend of 3–5 September 2010 at the Misano World Circuit in Misano Adriatico, Italy. The MotoGP race was won by Dani Pedrosa of the Repsol Honda team.

Background

The San Marino Grand Prix, held near the Adriatic coast in the Emilia-Romagna region, had been a fixture of the championship calendar since its return to Misano in 2007. Jorge Lorenzo arrived at the race leading the championship standings for Fiat Yamaha.

MotoGP classification

Dani Pedrosa took pole position and converted it into victory, winning ahead of Jorge Lorenzo and Valentino Rossi, who completed the podium in front of his home crowd. Andrea Dovizioso finished fourth and Casey Stoner fifth.

Moto2 and 125cc races

The Moto2 race was won by Toni Elías, while the 125cc race was won by Marc Márquez. The event was overshadowed by the death of Japanese Moto2 rider Shoya Tomizawa, who suffered fatal injuries in a crash during the Moto2 race.

Championship standings after the race

Lorenzo retained a comfortable championship lead over Pedrosa, with Rossi, Dovizioso and Stoner completing the top five in the standings.
