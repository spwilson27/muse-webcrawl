Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts

Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts, formerly The Waldorf-Astoria Collection, is a luxury hotel and resort brand of Hilton Worldwide. The brand is positioned as the flagship luxury brand within Hilton's portfolio, with properties distinguished by landmark architecture, distinctive history, or notable settings. Its namesake is the Waldorf Astoria New York on Park Avenue in Manhattan, opened in 1931.

History

The original Waldorf Hotel was built by William Waldorf Astor on Fifth Avenue in 1893; his cousin John Jacob Astor IV added the adjoining Astoria Hotel in 1897, and the combined Waldorf-Astoria was replaced by the Empire State Building site development. The current Waldorf Astoria New York opened in 1931. Hilton launched the Waldorf Astoria brand in 2006, extending the name to luxury properties worldwide.

Properties

Brand hotels are located in cities and resorts across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, including Amsterdam, Beverly Hills, Chicago, Dubai, Jerusalem, Rome, and Shanghai.
