TypeUmbrella organisation for international sports federations

The Global Association of International Sports Federations (GAISF) was an umbrella organisation for international sports federations, founded in 1967 in Lausanne, Switzerland. It was previously known as SportAccord and the General Association of International Sports Federations.

Role

GAISF grouped together Olympic and non-Olympic international federations, along with organisers of multi-sports events. Its members were organised into groups such as ASOIF (summer Olympic federations), AIOWF (winter Olympic federations), ARISF (IOC-recognised federations), and AIMS (alliance of independent recognised members of sport). It organised the annual SportAccord convention, a major meeting of the sports industry.

Dissolution

Following growing tensions with the International Olympic Committee and financial difficulties, GAISF's member federations voted in November 2022 to dissolve the organisation, with the process completed in 2023 and its activities largely absorbed elsewhere, notably through SportAccord as a separate entity.
