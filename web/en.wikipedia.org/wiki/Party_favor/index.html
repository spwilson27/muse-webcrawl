Party favor

A party favor (also spelled party favour) is a small gift given to the guests at a party as a souvenir, as a thank-you for attending, and to extend the memory of the occasion. Party favors are commonly given at weddings, birthday parties, baby showers, bridal showers, and children's parties.

History

The tradition of giving party favors is centuries old. At European weddings, aristocratic hosts gave guests small trinket boxes made of crystal, porcelain, or precious stones, known as bonbonnières, filled with sugar cubes or delicate confections. Sugar was expensive at the time, so the gift symbolized wealth and luxury. Over the centuries, as sugar became more affordable, the custom spread to the general population, and hosts began giving almonds coated in sugar, traditionally five Jordan almonds representing health, wealth, happiness, fertility, and longevity.

Types

Modern party favors range from edible items such as chocolates, candies, and small bottles of wine, to practical items like bottle openers, candles, seed packets, and personalized keepsakes. At children's parties, favors often consist of small toys, stickers, and treats placed in a "goody bag" or "loot bag." Many hosts now theme their favors to match the party's decor or the guest of honor's interests.

Etiquette

Party favors are generally considered optional. When given, they are typically placed at each guest's seat at a seated event, handed out as guests leave, or distributed by the host during the party. Personalized favors bearing the names of the couple or the date of the event are popular at weddings.
