Orarion

The orarion (Greek: ὀράριον) is a liturgical vestment of deacons and subdeacons in the Eastern Orthodox and Byzantine Catholic churches. It is a long, narrow stole, typically richly embroidered and often decorated with crosses and the words "Holy, Holy, Holy".

A deacon wears the orarion over the left shoulder, with the front end held in the right hand during litanies, traditionally raising it when leading prayers and petitions. Subdeacons wear it crossed around the body. The corresponding vestment for priests and bishops is the epitrachelion.

The name is usually derived from the Latin orare, "to pray", reflecting its association with the deacon's role in leading the congregation's prayers.
