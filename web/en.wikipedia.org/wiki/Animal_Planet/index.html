Animal Planet is an American pay television channel owned by Warner Bros. Discovery, dedicated to programming about animals and nature.

History

The channel launched on 1 October 1996 as a joint venture between Discovery Communications and the BBC. Its early programming consisted largely of wildlife documentaries and nature series, many produced by the BBC Natural History Unit.

Programming

From the late 2000s the channel shifted toward reality and entertainment formats under the slogan "Surprisingly Human". Notable series have included River Monsters, The Crocodile Hunter (in syndication), Pit Bulls & Parolees, Tanked, Crikey! It's the Irwins, and the long-running annual Puppy Bowl. A 2018 rebrand returned the channel to a stronger emphasis on animals and conservation-minded family programming.

Reach

Animal Planet is distributed internationally in numerous regional versions and is available in tens of millions of households in the United States.
