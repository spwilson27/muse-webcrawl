Universal Pictures

Universal Pictures (legally Universal City Studios LLC, formerly Universal Studios) is an American film production and distribution company founded in 1912 by Carl Laemmle and others. It is the oldest surviving film studio in the United States and a subsidiary of Comcast's NBCUniversal.

Universal Pictures
Founded: April 30, 1912
Founder: Carl Laemmle et al.
Headquarters: Universal City, California
Parent: NBCUniversal (Comcast)

History

Laemmle merged several companies into Universal and in 1915 opened Universal City, then the largest studio complex in the world. The studio became famous in the 1930s for its Classic Monsters — Dracula, Frankenstein, The Mummy, and The Wolf Man.

Notable films and franchises

Universal's library includes Jaws, E.T., Jurassic Park, Back to the Future, and franchises such as Fast & Furious, Despicable Me (via Illumination), and Jason Bourne. Directors closely associated include Alfred Hitchcock and Steven Spielberg.

Theme parks

The Universal Destinations & Experiences division operates theme parks in California, Florida, Japan, Singapore, and Beijing.
