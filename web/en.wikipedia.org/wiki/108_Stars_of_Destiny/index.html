The 108 Stars of Destiny (地煞天罡一百零八星) are the 108 outlaw heroes of the Chinese classic novel Water Margin (水滸傳), one of the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature. The heroes are divided into two groups: the 36 Heavenly Spirits (三十六天罡) and the 72 Earthly Fiends (七十二地煞). They gather at Liangshan Marsh to form an outlaw army under the leadership of Song Jiang, and their exploits form the core of the novel.

Origins of the concept

The earliest mention of the 108 outlaws appears in the Song dynasty text Dongxuan Zhuren Shiju Yougui Zhuan and in the Southern Song miscellaneous notes. The concept draws on Taoist and Buddhist numerology: the number 108 is significant in Buddhism, representing the 108 earthly desires or defilements that a person must overcome to attain enlightenment. In the novel's framing story, the demon king suppressed beneath Mount Longhu escapes and is reincarnated as the 108 stars.

Ranking

In chapter 71 of the 120-chapter version of Water Margin, the heroes assemble and a stone tablet ranking all 108 of them descends from heaven. The top-ranked hero is Song Jiang, titled the "Timely Rain" (及時雨), followed by Lu Junyi, Wu Yong, and Gongsun Sheng. Chao Gai, the original leader of the Liangshan band, had died earlier and is not included in the 108.

The 36 Heavenly Spirits

The first 36 stars include Song Jiang (Protector of Justice), Lu Junyi (Jade Qilin), Wu Yong (Knowledgeable Star), Gongsun Sheng (Dragon in the Clouds), Guan Sheng (Great Blade), Lin Chong (Panther Head), Qin Ming (Fiery Thunderbolt), Huyan Zhuo (Double Clubs), Hua Rong (Little Li Guang), Chai Jin (Little Whirlwind), Li Ying (Striking Hawk), Zhu Tong (Bearded Gentleman), Lu Zhishen (Flowery Monk), and Wu Song (Pilgrim).

The 72 Earthly Fiends

The 72 Earthly Fiends include Zhu Wu, Huang Xin, Sun Li, Xuan Zan, Hao Siwen, Han Tao, Peng Qi, Shan Tinggui, Wei Dingguo, Xiao Rang, Pei Xuan, Ou Peng, Deng Fei, Yan Shun, Yang Lin, Ling Zhen, Jiang Jing, Lü Fang, Guo Sheng, An Daoquan, Huangfu Duan, Wang Ying, Hu Sanniang, Bao Xu, Fan Rui, Kong Ming, Kong Liang, Xiang Chong, Li Gun, Jin Dajian, Ma Lin, Tong Wei, Tong Meng, Meng Kang, Hou Jian, Chen Da, Yang Chun, Zheng Tianshou, Tao Zongwang, Song Qing, Yue He, Gong Wang, Ding Desun, Mu Chun, Cao Zheng, Song Wan, Du Qian, Xue Yong, Shi En, Li Zhong, Zhou Tong, Tang Long, Du Xing, Zou Yuan, Zou Run, Zhu Gui, Zhu Fu, Cai Fu, Cai Qing, Li Li, Li Yun, Jiao Ting, Shi Yong, Sun Xin, Gu Dasao, Zhang Qing, Sun Erniang, Wang Dingliu, Yu Baosi, Bai Sheng, Shi Qian, and Duan Jingzhu.

Influence

The 108 Stars concept has strongly influenced later works, including the Suikoden video game series by Konami, which adapts the motif of recruiting 108 characters. It has also inspired Japanese works such as the manga and anime adaptations of the Water Margin legend.
