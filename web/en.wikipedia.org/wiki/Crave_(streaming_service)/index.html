Crave is a Canadian subscription video on demand service owned by Bell Media, launched in December 2014 as CraveTV. It is Canada's largest domestic streaming service and the Canadian home of HBO and Showtime programming.

History

The service originally launched as a low-cost add-on for television subscribers. In 2018 it merged with the premium film channel network then known as The Movie Network, adopting the Crave name across linear and streaming services, and adding HBO content directly to the platform.

Content

Crave offers current and library HBO series, Showtime series, Warner Bros. films, French-language programming, and Canadian originals such as the comedy Letterkenny and its spin-off Shoresy.
