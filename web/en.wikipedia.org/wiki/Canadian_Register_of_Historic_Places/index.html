Canadian Register of Historic Places

The Canadian Register of Historic Places (CRHP) is an online database of historic places in Canada that have been formally recognised for their heritage value by federal, provincial, territorial or municipal authorities. Launched in 2004, it is a central element of the Historic Places Initiative, a collaboration between the Government of Canada and provincial and territorial governments.

Purpose

The Register provides a single searchable source of information on Canada's designated historic places — buildings, structures, districts, landscapes and archaeological sites. Each listing describes the place, explains its heritage value in a Statement of Significance, and identifies the character-defining elements that should be conserved. Listing on the Register is informational: legal protection flows from the designating jurisdiction's own legislation, not from the Register itself.

Background

The Historic Places Initiative was launched in 2001 to encourage the conservation of Canada's historic places. Its other components include the Standards and Guidelines for the Conservation of Historic Places in Canada and a certification programme for federal funding. Places formally recognised by a competent authority using the initiative's criteria become eligible for inclusion in the Register.

The Register includes National Historic Sites of Canada, federal heritage buildings, provincially and municipally designated properties, and places recognised under provincial registers such as those of Ontario and British Columbia. It contains listings for tens of thousands of places across the country.
