Horn Island Airport


Horn Island Airport terminal area

Horn Island Airport (IATA: HID, ICAO: YHID) is a regional airport on Horn Island in the Torres Strait, Queensland, Australia. It is the principal air gateway to the Torres Strait Islands and to Thursday Island, which is reached by ferry. The airport is owned by the Torres Shire Council.

History

The airfield was constructed during the Second World War, when Horn Island was an important Allied air base defending the Torres Strait and supporting operations toward New Guinea. It was attacked several times by Japanese aircraft in 1942. After the war the airstrip continued in civilian use, serving the administrative centre on nearby Thursday Island.

Facilities and services

The airport has a sealed main runway and a shorter secondary strip. QantasLink operates scheduled services to Cairns, and regional carriers such as Skytrans provide flights to communities throughout the Torres Strait and Cape York Peninsula. The airport handles tens of thousands of passengers annually and is an essential lifeline for freight, medical evacuations, and government services in the far north of Queensland.
