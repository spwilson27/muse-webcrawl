The Disneyland Resort, commonly known as Disneyland, is an entertainment resort in Anaheim, California, owned and operated by The Walt Disney Company through its Experiences division. It consists of two theme parks — Disneyland Park, the original park built under the direct supervision of Walt Disney and opened in 1955, and Disney California Adventure, opened in 2001 — together with three hotels and the Downtown Disney shopping, dining, and entertainment district.

History

Walt Disney conceived a clean, family-oriented amusement park beside his Burbank studios, but the project outgrew the site and was built instead on 160 acres of orange groves in Anaheim. Disneyland opened on July 17, 1955, and despite a chaotic preview day became an immediate cultural landmark. For decades it remained a single park surrounded by motels and parking lots. In the 1990s Disney purchased surrounding land and, following the model of Walt Disney World in Florida, transformed the property into a multi-day resort: the expansion, completed in 2001, added the second park, the Grand Californian hotel, Downtown Disney, and the Mickey & Friends parking structure. Further investment in the 2010s added Cars Land, a reimagined California Adventure, and Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (2019) in Disneyland Park.

Attendance and impact

Disneyland Park is perennially among the most-visited theme parks in the world, drawing roughly 17–18 million guests in strong years, with California Adventure adding about 9–10 million. The resort is one of Orange County's largest employers and a anchor of Anaheim's tourism economy, centred on the Anaheim Resort district. In 2024 the Anaheim City Council approved DisneylandForward, a zoning framework enabling major new development on resort land over coming decades.
