Choupette (born 15 August 2011) is a French Birman cat, formerly owned by the fashion designer Karl Lagerfeld. One of the most famous celebrity pets in the world, she became a fashion icon in her own right, with a large social media following, advertising campaigns, and reported earnings in the millions of euros.

Life with Lagerfeld

Choupette originally belonged to the model Baptiste Giabiconi, who left her with Lagerfeld's staff while travelling in 2011; Lagerfeld refused to return her. She lived in pampered circumstances, attended by maids, eating at the table, and travelling by private jet. Lagerfeld credited her as a muse, featuring her in campaigns for Shu Uemura ("Shupette") and Opel, and designing cat-inspired accessories; she was the subject of the book Choupette: The Private Life of a High-Flying Fashion Cat (2014).

After Lagerfeld's death

Following Lagerfeld's death in February 2019, reports stated that he had made financial provisions for Choupette's care; under French law animals cannot directly inherit, but arrangements can be made through caretakers. She remained in France with her caretaker and continued public appearances and brand work. In 2023, Jared Leto attended the Met Gala — whose theme honoured Lagerfeld — in a full Choupette costume, cementing her place in fashion culture.
