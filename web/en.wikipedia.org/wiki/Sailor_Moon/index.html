Sailor Moon (Japanese: 美少女戦士セーラームーン, Bishōjo Senshi Sērā Mūn) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Naoko Takeuchi. It follows Usagi Tsukino, a schoolgirl who transforms into the heroine Sailor Moon and leads a group of Sailor Guardians to protect Earth and the Solar System from villains.

Plot

Usagi learns from the talking cat Luna that she is the reincarnation of the Moon Princess and must awaken her fellow Guardians — Sailors Mercury, Mars, Jupiter and Venus — to find the Legendary Silver Crystal and defeat the Dark Kingdom and later enemies, including the Black Moon Clan, the Death Busters, the Dead Moon Circus and Shadow Galactica.

Impact

The franchise is credited with popularising the magical-girl team format and revitalising the genre, and became one of the best-selling shōjo manga series, selling over 46 million copies worldwide. It spawned a long-running anime, films, musicals, video games and a live-action series.
