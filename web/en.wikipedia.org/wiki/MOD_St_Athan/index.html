MOD St Athan (Welsh: Sain Tathan) is a large Ministry of Defence site in the Vale of Glamorgan, Wales. Formerly RAF St Athan, it was for much of its history the Royal Air Force's principal aircraft maintenance station and the home of its engineering training.

History

The station opened in 1938 and expanded rapidly during the Second World War, when it handled the repair and maintenance of large numbers of aircraft. After the war it remained the RAF's main maintenance unit, and its No. 4 School of Technical Training taught aircraft engineering trades to generations of technicians.

During the 2000s, plans were made to concentrate all British military training at St Athan under the Defence Training Review, and a "super-hangar" was built for the Red Dragon fast-jet maintenance project, which was later cancelled. Civil aviation activity grew on the site, with Cardiff Aviation and other companies maintaining airliners, and the airfield became known as St Athan Aerodrome.

Present use

Remaining military occupants have included No. 4 School of Technical Training, the University of Wales Air Squadron, and 614 (County of Glamorgan) Squadron of the Royal Auxiliary Air Force. The site also hosts the Aston Martin Lagonda car factory, built in former hangars, which produces the DBX SUV.
