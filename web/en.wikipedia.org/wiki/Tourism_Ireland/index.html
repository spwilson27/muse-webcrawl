Tourism Ireland

Formed: 2002
Type: North–South implementation body
Headquarters: Dublin and Coleraine
Purpose: Overseas marketing of the island of Ireland as a tourist destination

Tourism Ireland (Irish: Turasóireacht Éireann) is the marketing organisation responsible for marketing the island of Ireland overseas as a tourist destination. It is a cross-border body established under the Good Friday Agreement of 1998 and became operational in 2002.

Tourism Ireland markets both the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland as a single destination in overseas markets. Domestic and Northern Ireland market promotion is handled separately by Fáilte Ireland (in the Republic) and the Northern Ireland Tourist Board.

Campaigns

The organisation has run global campaigns including "Jump into Ireland", the "Global Greening" initiative — in which landmarks worldwide are illuminated green around Saint Patrick's Day — and promotions built around the Wild Atlantic Way, Ireland's Ancient East, and filming locations for productions such as Game of Thrones in Northern Ireland.
