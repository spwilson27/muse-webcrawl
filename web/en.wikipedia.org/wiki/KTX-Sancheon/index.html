The KTX-Sancheon (formerly called the KTX-II) is a South Korean high-speed train built by Hyundai Rotem and operated by Korail. Introduced in 2010, it was the first domestically developed commercial high-speed train in South Korea, succeeding the French-derived KTX-I.

Development

The train grew out of the HSR-350x experimental programme, which achieved 352.4 km/h in 2004. Named after the sancheoneo (cherry trout), the KTX-Sancheon features articulated trailer cars, aluminium bodies, onboard entertainment, and rotating seats in first class.

Service

KTX-Sancheon trainsets operate on the Gyeongbu, Honam, Jeolla, and other KTX lines, including services serving the 2018 Winter Olympics through the Gangneung Line. Later orders added improved sets sometimes designated KTX-Sancheon B, and the type formed the basis for further domestic designs such as the KTX-Eum.
