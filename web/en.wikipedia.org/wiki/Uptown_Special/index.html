Uptown Special is the fourth studio album by British-American musician and producer Mark Ronson, released on 13 January 2015 by RCA Records. A homage to the funk, soul, and R&B music Ronson grew up with — in particular the sounds of New York in the early 1980s — the album features guest appearances by Bruno Mars, Stevie Wonder, Mystikal, Kevin Parker of Tame Impala, and Andrew Wyatt, among others. Novelist Michael Chabon contributed lyrics to several tracks.

"Uptown Funk"

The album is best known for its lead single, "Uptown Funk", featuring Bruno Mars. Released in November 2014, the song topped charts in numerous countries, spent 14 consecutive weeks at number one on the US Billboard Hot 100, and won Grammy Awards for Record of the Year and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance. It became one of the best-selling singles of all time, though its writing credits grew through legal settlements over similarities to earlier funk recordings.

Reception

Uptown Special received generally favourable reviews and debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart and number five on the US Billboard 200. Critics praised its musicianship and affectionate recreation of vintage funk sounds.
