Telangana is a state in southern India, located on the Deccan Plateau. It was formed on 2 June 2014 as the 29th state of India, when it was separated from Andhra Pradesh after a decades-long movement for statehood. Its capital and largest city is Hyderabad, an information technology and pharmaceutical hub.

History

The region was ruled successively by the Satavahanas, Kakatiyas, the Delhi Sultanate, the Bahmani Sultanate, the Qutb Shahis of Golconda, and the Nizams of Hyderabad. After Indian independence, the princely state of Hyderabad was integrated into India in 1948 and merged with Telugu-speaking Andhra State in 1956 to form Andhra Pradesh.

Geography and economy

Telangana is drained by the Godavari and Krishna rivers. Agriculture, particularly rice, is important, while Hyderabad contributes a large share of revenue through IT services ("HITEC City"), biotechnology, and the pharmaceutical industry. The state emblem features the Kakatiya Kala Thoranam and the Charminar.
