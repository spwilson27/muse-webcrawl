Smart building

A smart building is a building that uses integrated automation and connected sensors to control and optimise its operations — including heating, ventilation and air conditioning, lighting, security, energy use and maintenance — often through a central building management system (BMS) increasingly augmented by Internet of Things devices and data analytics.

Goals include reducing energy consumption and operating costs, improving occupant comfort and productivity, and enabling predictive maintenance. Smart building technologies range from occupancy-aware lighting and demand-controlled ventilation to digital twins that model a building's performance in real time.

Related concepts

Smart buildings are a component of the broader smart city concept and of home automation in residential settings.
