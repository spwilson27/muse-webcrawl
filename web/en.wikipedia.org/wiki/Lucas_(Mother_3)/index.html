Lucas (Mother 3)

Lucas is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the 2006 role-playing video game Mother 3, developed by Brownie Brown and HAL Laboratory for the Game Boy Advance. He is a young boy from Tazmily Village who possesses psychic powers known as PSI.

Character

Lucas is the younger twin brother of Claus and the son of Flint and Hinawa. Timid and gentle at the start of the story, he grows in resolve after tragedy strikes his family and the Pig Mask Army begins transforming the Nowhere Islands. Over the course of the game he learns powerful PSI abilities, including the unique PK Love, which is needed to pull the Seven Needles that seal the Dark Dragon beneath the islands.

Appearances

Outside Mother 3, Lucas is best known as a playable fighter in the Super Smash Bros. series, debuting in Super Smash Bros. Brawl and returning in later entries, where he fights using PSI attacks, a rope snake and his stick. He also appears in merchandise and in an amiibo figure.
