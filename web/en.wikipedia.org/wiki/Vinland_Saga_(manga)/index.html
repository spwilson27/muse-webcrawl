Vinland Saga (Japanese: ヴィンランド・サガ) is a Japanese historical manga series written and illustrated by Makoto Yukimura. Set in early 11th-century England and the North Atlantic during the Viking Age, it follows Thorfinn, a young Icelandic warrior drawn into the Danish invasion of England, and his eventual quest to reach Vinland — the land across the ocean described in the Norse sagas — and found a peaceful settlement there.

Story

The series opens with Thorfinn serving the mercenary Askeladd, the man who killed his father, while dreaming of revenge. Through arcs covering the war in England, Thorfinn's enslavement on a Danish farm, and an expedition to North America, the story shifts from revenge-driven action to themes of pacifism, slavery and the cycle of violence, interweaving historical figures such as Canute the Great, Thorkell the Tall and Leif Erikson.

Publication and reception

The manga began in Weekly Shōnen Magazine in 2005 before moving to the seinen magazine Monthly Afternoon, concluding in July 2025 after a twenty-year run. It won the Japan Media Arts Festival's Grand Prize for manga (2009) and the Kodansha Manga Award for Best General Manga (2012). An anime television adaptation by Wit Studio aired in 2019, with a second season produced by MAPPA in 2023; both were widely acclaimed.
