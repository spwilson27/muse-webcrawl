Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd. (MHI) is a Japanese multinational engineering, electrical equipment and electronics company headquartered in Tokyo. It is one of the core companies of the Mitsubishi group and one of the world's largest heavy industry manufacturers.

History

The company's origins trace to 1884, when Yatarō Iwasaki leased the Nagasaki Shipyard from the Japanese government and founded the Nagasaki Shipyard & Machinery Works. The business grew into Japan's largest shipbuilder and a major armaments producer; during the Second World War its factories built warships including the battleship Musashi and aircraft including the Mitsubishi A6M Zero fighter. After the war the company was split into three firms under the Allied occupation, which re-merged in 1964.

Products

MHI's products span shipbuilding, power systems (gas turbines, nuclear power plant equipment), aviation and space systems (including Japan's H-IIA and H3 launch vehicles), defence equipment, industrial machinery, air conditioning, and environmental systems. It led the development of the Mitsubishi SpaceJet regional airliner, a programme cancelled in 2023.
