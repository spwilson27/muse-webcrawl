Myricitrin

Chemical classFlavonoid glycoside
FormulaC21H20O12
AglyconeMyricetin

Myricitrin is a flavonoid glycoside — specifically the 3-O-rhamnoside of the flavonol myricetin. It occurs naturally in the bark of bayberry (Myrica cerifera), in Myrica species more generally, and in various other plants, including walnut leaves and sumac.

Properties and research

Myricitrin is a yellowish crystalline compound that is hydrolysed in the body to myricetin and the sugar rhamnose. Like other flavonoids, it has been studied in preclinical research for antioxidant, anti-inflammatory and other biological activities, including reported effects in experimental models of pain, anxiety and bone loss. Myricitrin is also a major constituent of some plant extracts used in traditional medicine.
