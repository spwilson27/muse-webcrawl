Tugen Hills

The Tugen Hills (also known as the Tugen-Kamasia Hills) are a mountain range in Baringo County, west-central Kenya, west of Lake Baringo in the Great Rift Valley.

Geology and fossils

The hills expose a long sedimentary sequence spanning roughly the last 16 million years. They are famous for fossils including Orrorin tugenensis (Millennium Man), a ~6-million-year-old early hominin described in 2001, and Lukeino and Chemeron hominin finds important to human evolution studies.

People

The area is home to the Tugen people, a Kalenjin subgroup. See Orrorin and Lake Baringo.
