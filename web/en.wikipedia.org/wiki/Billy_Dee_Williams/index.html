William December Williams Jr. (born April 6, 1937), known professionally as Billy Dee Williams, is an American actor and artist. He is best known for portraying Lando Calrissian in the Star Wars franchise, first appearing in The Empire Strikes Back (1980) and returning in Return of the Jedi (1983) and The Rise of Skywalker (2019).

Early life

Williams was born in New York City and raised in Harlem. He studied painting at the National Academy of Fine Arts and Design and made his Broadway debut as a child actor in 1945. He later pursued acting alongside his work as a visual artist.

Career

Williams gained wide acclaim for his Emmy-nominated portrayal of football player Gale Sayers in the television film Brian's Song (1971), and starred opposite Diana Ross in Lady Sings the Blues (1972) and Mahogany (1975). He played district attorney Harvey Dent in Tim Burton's Batman (1989), and lent his voice to Lando Calrissian in animated series and video games. His paintings have been exhibited in galleries and institutions including the National Portrait Gallery.
