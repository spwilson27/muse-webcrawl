UK Astronomy Technology Centre

The UK Astronomy Technology Centre (UK ATC) is a national centre based at the Royal Observatory, Edinburgh, on Blackford Hill. Operated by the Science and Technology Facilities Council (STFC), it designs and builds instruments for ground-based and space telescopes.

Established1998
LocationRoyal Observatory, Edinburgh, Scotland
ParentSTFC / UKRI

Work

The centre led instruments including the SCUBA-2 submillimetre camera for the James Clerk Maxwell Telescope, contributed to the MIRI instrument on the James Webb Space Telescope (leading its European consortium), and works on ELT instruments such as HARMONI and METIS, as well as technology for the Square Kilometre Array and the Vera Rubin Observatory.

History

Formed in 1998 from the Royal Observatory Edinburgh's technology division, the UK ATC shares its site with the University of Edinburgh's Institute for Astronomy and the Higgs Centre for Innovation.
