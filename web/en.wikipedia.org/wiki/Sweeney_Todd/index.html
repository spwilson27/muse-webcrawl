Sweeney Todd

Sweeney Todd
First appearance: The String of Pearls (1846–47)
Occupation: Barber, murderer (fictional)

Sweeney Todd is a fictional character who first appeared in the Victorian penny dreadful serial The String of Pearls (1846–47), probably by James Malcolm Rymer and/or Thomas Peckett Prest. A barber in Fleet Street, London, Todd murders customers and, with accomplice Mrs Lovett, disposes of bodies in meat pies.

Adaptations

The tale was repeatedly staged as melodrama. Christopher Bond's 1973 play reimagined Todd as a wronged man (Benjamin Barker) seeking revenge on Judge Turpin. Stephen Sondheim and Hugh Wheeler turned it into the musical Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street (1979), winner of major Tony and Olivier awards, filmed by Tim Burton in 2007 with Johnny Depp and Helena Bonham Carter.

Historical claims

Claims that Todd was a real murderer are unsupported; no contemporary records match the legend.
