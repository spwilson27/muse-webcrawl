Metairie (/ˈmɛtəri/ MET-ər-ee) is a census-designated place (CDP) in Jefferson Parish, Louisiana, United States, and is part of the New Orleans metropolitan area. With a population of about 143,500 at the 2020 census, it is the largest community in Jefferson Parish and one of the largest unincorporated communities in the United States.

History

The name derives from the French métairie, a small tenant farm. Metairie grew along Metairie Ridge, a natural levee and former distributary of the Mississippi River that served as a route for the Acolapissa and other Native American peoples. Suburban development boomed after the Second World War and following the opening of the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway in 1956.

Geography and economy

Metairie lies between New Orleans to the east and Lake Pontchartrain to the north. It suffered flooding during Hurricane Katrina in 2005. Lakeside Shopping Center, one of the region's largest malls, and the offices along Causeway Boulevard form a major commercial hub; the New Orleans Saints' training facility is also located in Metairie.
