The Kuban is a river in the North Caucasus region of Russia, flowing 870 km from its source near Mount Elbrus through Karachay-Cherkessia, Stavropol Krai, Krasnodar Krai and Adygea to the Sea of Azov.

Course

Fed by glaciers on Elbrus, the Kuban descends through mountain gorges before emerging onto the Kuban steppe plain. It passes the cities of Cherkessk, Nevinnomyssk, Armavir and Krasnodar, then forms a broad delta near Temryuk.

Importance

The river gives its name to the Kuban region, historically settled by Kuban Cossacks. Its waters support irrigation, rice farming and fisheries in one of Russia's most productive agricultural areas.
