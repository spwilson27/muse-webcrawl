Patrizia Reggiani

Patrizia Reggiani (born 2 December 1948) is an Italian woman convicted of arranging the murder of her ex-husband Maurizio Gucci, former head of the Gucci fashion house, who was shot dead in Milan on 27 March 1995.

Marriage and divorce

Reggiani met Maurizio Gucci around 1970 and married him in 1972 despite his family's opposition; they had two daughters and became a prominent Milanese society couple. Gucci left her in 1985 and their bitter divorce was finalised in 1994 with a large settlement.

Murder and trial

After Maurizio's murder, a two-year investigation led to Reggiani's arrest in 1997. Prosecutors argued she had hired a hitman, Benedetto Ceraulo, through intermediaries including her friend and psychic Giuseppina Auriemma, motivated by resentment and fear of losing her settlement if Maurizio remarried. In 1998 she was convicted and sentenced to 29 years, later reduced; she was released in 2016 after serving about 18 years, including on work release. Her story was dramatised in the 2021 film House of Gucci, in which she was played by Lady Gaga.
