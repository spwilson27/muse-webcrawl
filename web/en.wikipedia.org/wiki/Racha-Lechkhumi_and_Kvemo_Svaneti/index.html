Racha-Lechkhumi and Kvemo Svaneti (Georgian: რაჭა-ლეჩხუმი და ქვემო სვანეთი) is a region (mkhare) in northwestern Georgia. It combines the historical provinces of Racha, Lechkhumi and Lower Svaneti (Kvemo Svaneti), and is one of the least densely populated regions of the country. Its administrative centre is the town of Ambrolauri.

Geography

The mountainous region lies on the southern slopes of the Greater Caucasus. The Rioni River rises here, and the area is known for forests, alpine meadows, and the resort of Shovi. The region includes the districts of Ambrolauri, Lentekhi, Oni and Tsageri.

Culture

Racha is celebrated for its Khvanchkara wine, produced from Aleksandrouli and Mujuretuli grapes grown on the slopes near Ambrolauri, and for the region's distinctive folk traditions and cuisine, including Rachuli ham and lobiani (bean-filled bread).

Subdivisions

The region is subdivided into four municipalities: Ambrolauri, Lentekhi, Oni and Tsageri. Much of its territory is affected by depopulation as residents migrate to larger cities.
