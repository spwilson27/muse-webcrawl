Photos (Apple)

Photos is a photo management and editing application developed by Apple Inc. It was released as a bundled app in iOS 8 on September 17, 2014, replacing Camera Roll, and was later released for OS X Yosemite in 2015 as the successor to iPhoto and Aperture. Versions are also available for tvOS and watchOS.

The Photos app library view.

Features

Photos organises images and videos into a library that can be synchronised across devices with iCloud Photo Library (later iCloud Photos). The app groups media into Moments, Collections and Years, and uses on-device machine learning to build curated "Memories", recognise faces and scenes, and support natural-language search. Editing tools include auto-enhance, filters, cropping, retouching and adjustments for exposure, colour and black-and-white conversion, all applied non-destructively. Shared Albums allow users to share photos with other iCloud users, and Live Photos, Portrait mode depth edits and video trimming are supported on compatible devices.

History

Apple announced Photos for OS X at its Worldwide Developers Conference in June 2014, explaining that it would unify iPhoto and Aperture libraries. The Mac version shipped with OS X 10.10.3 in April 2015. Subsequent releases added Memories (2016), improved search and people recognition, and in later versions support for the ProRAW format and Hide My Email-style sharing controls. In 2024 the iOS version received a major redesign with a single scrolling view combining the library grid and collections.
