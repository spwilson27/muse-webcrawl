National Salvation Front (Romania)

The National Salvation Front (Romanian: Frontul Salvării Naționale, FSN) was the governing body that seized power in Romania during the Romanian Revolution of December 1989, following the overthrow of Nicolae Ceaușescu. Led by Ion Iliescu, it formed the Council of the National Salvation Front as a provisional government.

History

The FSN announced the abolition of one-party rule and organised the first free elections in May 1990, which it won decisively. It subsequently registered as a political party. In 1992 the organisation split, with Iliescu's faction forming what became the Social Democratic Party, while Petre Roman's faction retained the FSN name and later evolved into the Democratic Party.
