Mike Judge

Michael Craig Judge (born 17 October 1962) is an American animator, writer, director, actor and musician. He is the creator of the animated series Beavis and Butt-Head (1993–1997, revived 2011 and 2022) and King of the Hill (1997–2010), and co-creator of the HBO comedy Silicon Valley (2014–2019).

Early life

Judge was born in Guayaquil, Ecuador, where his father worked in international development, and raised in Albuquerque, New Mexico. He graduated from the University of California, San Diego with a degree in physics in 1985 and worked as an engineer in Silicon Valley and as a touring bass player before turning to animation.

Work

His short Frog Baseball (1992), featuring Beavis and Butt-Head, was picked up by MTV's Liquid Television and became a defining series of the decade; Judge voiced both title characters and many others. King of the Hill, co-created with Greg Daniels, offered a gentler portrait of small-town Texas life, with Judge voicing Hank Hill and Boomhauer.

His live-action films include Office Space (1999), which became a cult classic; Idiocracy (2006), later celebrated for its satire; and Extract (2009). Judge also appeared as an actor in the Spy Kids films and provided voices for many of his own productions.
