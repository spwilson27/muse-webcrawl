Filippino Lippi

Filippino Lippi (April 1457 – April 1504) was an Italian Renaissance painter working in Florence, Italy during the later years of the Early Renaissance and first few years of the High Renaissance. He was the son of the painter Fra Filippo Lippi and Lucrezia Buti.

Biography

After his father's death, Filippino completed his apprenticeship in the workshop of Sandro Botticelli. His early works show Botticelli's influence, but he developed a more restless, expressive style admired for its decorative complexity.

Works

His major works include the frescoes of the Brancacci Chapel in Santa Maria del Carmine (completing Masaccio's cycle), the Strozzi Chapel in Santa Maria Novella, and the Carafa Chapel in Rome. He died in Florence in 1504.
