Itachi Uchiha

Itachi Uchiha (うちはイタチ, Uchiha Itachi) is a fictional character in the Naruto manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. Introduced as an antagonist — the elder brother of Sasuke Uchiha and the man who massacred their entire clan — he is later revealed to be one of the series' most tragic and morally complex figures.

Character

Itachi is presented initially as a cold, ruthless prodigy who slaughtered the Uchiha clan to test his own abilities, sparing only his younger brother Sasuke. In truth, he was ordered by Konohagakure's leadership to exterminate the clan after it plotted a coup d'état, choosing the village over his family to prevent a civil war that would have destroyed the village and been exploited by rival nations.

Abilities

A prodigy who graduated the Academy at seven and joined ANBU at eleven, Itachi wielded the Mangekyō Sharingan, granting him Tsukuyomi (a paralyzing illusion), Amaterasu (inextinguishable black flames), and the Susanoo, which carries the Sword of Totsuka and Yata Mirror. He was also a master of genjutsu, shurikenjutsu, and tactical analysis.

Story arc

Itachi joins the criminal organization Akatsuki as a double agent, feeding intelligence to Konoha while dying of an incurable illness. He finally faces Sasuke in a climactic duel, dying from his illness while allowing his brother to believe he had avenged the clan. Reanimated during the Fourth Great Ninja War, Itachi breaks free of the control jutsu and defeats Kabuto Yakushi with the forbidden Izanami, finally expressing his love for Sasuke before his soul is released.

Reception

Itachi is widely regarded as one of the most beloved and well-written characters in Naruto, frequently topping popularity polls. Critics praise the revelation of his true motives as one of the series' strongest plot twists, transforming him from a one-dimensional villain into a symbol of self-sacrifice.
