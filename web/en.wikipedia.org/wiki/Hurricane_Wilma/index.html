Hurricane Wilma was the most intense tropical cyclone ever recorded in the Atlantic basin, reaching a minimum central pressure of 882 mbar (hPa) in October 2005. A powerful Category 5 hurricane of the record-breaking 2005 Atlantic season, Wilma struck Cozumel and the Yucatán Peninsula of Mexico, then crossed South Florida, causing widespread damage in Cancún, the Riviera Maya, and the Miami–Fort Lauderdale metropolitan area.

Meteorological history

Wilma formed from a large tropical disturbance in the Caribbean Sea on 15 October 2005. It then underwent one of the most extreme episodes of rapid intensification on record: over about 24 hours its pressure fell from near 975 mbar to 882 mbar, and its winds rose to 185 mph (295 km/h), while its eye contracted to barely 2–3 nautical miles across — the smallest known in an Atlantic hurricane. Wilma weakened slightly before stalling over the northeastern Yucatán for about a day, then accelerated northeast across the Gulf of Mexico and made landfall near Cape Romano, Florida, on 24 October as a Category 3 hurricane, crossing the peninsula in a few hours and passing over the Miami metropolitan area.

Impact

In Mexico, Wilma's slow passage devastated Cancún and Cozumel, flooding resorts, destroying piers, and stranding tens of thousands of tourists; looting followed in Cancún. Record rainfall caused severe flooding and landslides in parts of the Yucatán and earlier in Haiti and Central America. In South Florida the storm caused extensive wind damage, toppled trees and power lines, shattered windows in high-rise buildings, and left millions without electricity. Wilma killed 52 people and caused roughly $30 billion in damage, at the time among the costliest hurricanes on record.

Retirement

The name Wilma was retired by the World Meteorological Organization in 2006 and replaced with Whitney. Because it began with the letter "W", Wilma was widely noted at the time as the first storm to exhaust the standard alphabetical list, after which the Greek alphabet was used for the remainder of the 2005 season.
