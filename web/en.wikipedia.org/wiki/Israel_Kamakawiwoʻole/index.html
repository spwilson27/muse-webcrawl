Israel Kamakawiwoʻole

Israel Kaʻanoʻi Kamakawiwoʻole (20 May 1959 – 26 June 1997), known as IZ, was a Native Hawaiian musician and singer celebrated for his ukulele playing and his medley of "Somewhere over the Rainbow" and "What a Wonderful World", which became an international hit years after his death.

Kamakawiwoʻole, nicknamed "the Gentle Giant".

Life and career

Born in Honolulu, Hawaii, Kamakawiwoʻole began playing music with his brother Skippy; in 1976 they formed the Makaha Sons of Niʻihau, one of Hawaii's most successful groups, promoting Hawaiian music and culture. His solo career took off with the album Facing Future (1993), which included the famous medley recorded in a single take in 1988. The album became the best-selling Hawaiian record of all time.

Kamakawiwoʻole was also a prominent advocate for Hawaiian sovereignty and indigenous rights. He suffered from severe obesity, weighing up to about 343 kg (757 lb), and died of respiratory failure in 1997 aged 38. Hawaii's state flag flew at half-staff, and some 10,000 people attended as his ashes were scattered at sea.
