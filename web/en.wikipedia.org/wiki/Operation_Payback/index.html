Operation Payback

Operation Payback was a coordinated, decentralized group of attacks on high-profile opponents of Internet piracy by Internet activists using the collective name "Anonymous". At the beginning, the operation targeted anti-piracy organizations and law firms in September 2010. Later in the operation, in December 2010, the focus shifted to support WikiLeaks, targeting the websites of companies such as PayPal, MasterCard, Visa, and PostFinance that had withdrawn services from WikiLeaks.

Background

In 2010, several Bollywood studios hired the Indian firm AiPlex Software to launch distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks against torrent websites that did not respond to takedown notices. In response, activists on 4chan's /b/ board organized counterattacks against AiPlex, the MPAA, and the RIAA, beginning Operation Payback under the banner "Operation: Payback Is A Bitch."

Later operations

Following the 29 November 2010 publication of leaked US diplomatic cables by WikiLeaks, financial intermediaries blocked donations to the organization. Anonymous retaliated with DDoS attacks on PayPal, Visa, MasterCard and others, temporarily disrupting their public websites. The operation became one of the most widely reported Anonymous campaigns and led to arrests and prosecutions of participants in several countries.
