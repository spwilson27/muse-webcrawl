Downshifting

Downshifting is a social behaviour or trend in which individuals live simpler lives to escape what some criticise as the economic rat race, pursuing a better work–life balance. Downshifters may reduce working hours, accept lower income, leave high-pressure careers, move to cheaper locations or reduce consumption, in exchange for more leisure, family time or lower stress. The concept has roots in voluntary simplicity and gained prominence in the 1990s and 2000s; critics note it is often only feasible for affluent professionals.
