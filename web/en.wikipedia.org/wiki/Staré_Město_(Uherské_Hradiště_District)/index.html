Staré Město (Czech pronunciation: [ˈstarɛː ˈmɲɛsto]; German: Altstadt) is a town in the Uherské Hradiště District in the Zlín Region of the Czech Republic. It has about 6,600 inhabitants. It lies on the Morava River, directly across from Uherské Hradiště, with which it forms a conurbation.

History

The area was one of the principal settlements of Great Moravia in the 9th century, known as Veligrad — a fortified agglomeration with churches, including a rotunda, associated with the mission of Saints Cyril and Methodius. Archaeological excavations since the 20th century have uncovered extensive remains. The settlement was first written about in 1141; after the founding of royal Uherské Hradiště in 1257, the older market town declined and acquired the name Staré Město, "Old Town". It was promoted to a town in 1997.

Sights

The Great Moravia Memorial (Památník Velké Moravy), built over the foundations of a 9th-century church, presents the archaeology of the Veligrad agglomeration. The Church of Saint Michael the Archangel and the local section of the Baťa Canal are also notable.
