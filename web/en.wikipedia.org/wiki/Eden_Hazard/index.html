Eden Michael Walter Hazard (born 7 January 1991) is a Belgian former professional footballer who played as a winger or attacking midfielder. Renowned for his dribbling, balance, close control and creativity, he is widely regarded as one of the best players of his generation and one of the finest players in Chelsea's history.

Club career

Hazard began his career in France with Lille, breaking into the first team as a teenager and winning the Ligue 1 and Coupe de France double in 2010–11, earning Ligue 1 Player of the Year honours twice. In 2012 he transferred to Chelsea for a fee reported around £32 million. Over seven seasons in London he became the team's talisman, winning two Premier League titles (2015, 2017), the FA Cup, the League Cup and two UEFA Europa League titles, and was named PFA Players' Player of the Year in 2014–15.

In 2019 Hazard joined Real Madrid for a fee that could exceed €100 million, one of the most expensive transfers in history. His time in Spain was blighted by a series of injuries; he never reproduced his Chelsea form, though he was part of squads that won La Liga titles and the 2022 Champions League. His contract was terminated by mutual consent in 2023, and in October 2023 he announced his retirement from professional football at the age of 32.

International career

Hazard captained Belgium's celebrated "golden generation", earning 126 caps and scoring 33 goals between 2008 and 2022. He starred at the 2018 FIFA World Cup in Russia, where Belgium finished third — its best-ever result — and Hazard won the Silver Ball as the tournament's second-best player. He retired from international football after the 2022 World Cup. His younger brothers Thorgan and Kylian Hazard are also professional footballers.
