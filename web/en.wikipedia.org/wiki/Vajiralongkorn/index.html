Vajiralongkorn

Vajiralongkorn (born 28 July 1952) is King of Thailand since 2016, reigning as Rama X. He is the only son of King Bhumibol Adulyadej and Queen Sirikit.

Early life and education

Educated in Thailand, the United Kingdom and Australia, he attended the Royal Military College, Duntroon. He served as an officer in the Royal Thai Army and qualified as a military pilot.

Reign

He ascended the throne on 13 October 2016 following his father's death, though his coronation was held 4–6 May 2019. His reign has seen amendments to the constitution, expansion of royal influence over crown property, and continued political tensions in Thailand.

Personal life

He has been married several times and has seven children. Queen Suthida is his current consort, married in 2019.
