Frederick John Inman (28 June 1935 – 8 March 2007) was an English actor and singer best known for his role as Mr Humphries in the BBC sitcom Are You Being Served?, which he played from 1972 to 1985. His catchphrase "I'm free!" became widely popular.

Career

Inman worked as a window dresser before turning to acting, appearing in pantomime and repertory theatre. His portrayal of the flamboyant menswear assistant Mr Humphries made him one of Britain's most recognisable comic actors. He also starred in the spin-off Grace & Favour and appeared in numerous pantomimes, earning him a reputation as one of the country's leading pantomime dames.

Later life

Inman entered a civil partnership with his long-term partner Ron Lynch in 2005. He died in London in 2007 from complications of hepatitis.
