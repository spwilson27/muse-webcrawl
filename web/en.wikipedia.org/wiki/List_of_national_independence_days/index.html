List of national independence days

An independence day is an annual event commemorating the anniversary of a nation's independence or statehood, usually after ceasing to be a colony or part of another state. Many countries observe such a day as their national day, typically a public holiday. The dates below are a selection from the full list of more than 150 countries.


Independence days are often marked with fireworks, parades and flag-raising ceremonies.
