Steve Cropper (born October 21, 1941) is an American guitarist, songwriter and record producer. He is best known as the guitarist of the Stax Records house band Booker T. & the M.G.'s, which backed artists including Otis Redding, Sam & Dave, Carla Thomas and Rufus Thomas, and as a member of the Blues Brothers band.

Career

Raised in Memphis, Cropper joined the Mar-Keys, whose 1961 instrumental "Last Night" was Stax's first big hit. As part of Booker T. & the M.G.'s he played on the classic "Green Onions" (1962) and co-wrote, with Otis Redding, "(Sittin' On) The Dock of the Bay" (1968), as well as Wilson Pickett's "In the Midnight Hour" and Eddie Floyd's "Knock on Wood". His economical, rhythmically precise Telecaster style—chopped chords, double-stops and spare fills—defined the Memphis soul guitar sound.

Cropper left Stax in 1970, worked as a producer and session player in Los Angeles and Nashville, and in 1978 joined John Belushi and Dan Aykroyd's Blues Brothers band, appearing in the 1980 film. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with Booker T. & the M.G.'s in 1992. Rolling Stone ranked him among the greatest guitarists of all time.
