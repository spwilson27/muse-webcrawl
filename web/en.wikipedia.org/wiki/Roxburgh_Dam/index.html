Roxburgh Dam

The Roxburgh Dam is a hydroelectric dam on the Clutha River in Otago, New Zealand, near the town of Roxburgh. Completed in 1956, it was the first major dam on the Clutha and one of New Zealand's largest hydro stations at the time.

Description

The concrete gravity dam impounds Lake Roxburgh. Its power station has an installed capacity of about 320 megawatts from eight generating units. Construction created the village later developed at Roxburgh and required major works in the narrow gorge.

Issues

Sedimentation in Lake Roxburgh has caused flooding problems upstream at Alexandra, a long-running concern for the scheme.
