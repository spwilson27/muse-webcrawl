The Montreal City Council (French: Conseil municipal de Montréal) is the governing body in the mayor–council government in the city of Montreal, Quebec, Canada. The council is the main decision-making body of the city and oversees municipal services, budgets, and bylaws.

Composition

The council consists of 65 elected members: the mayor of Montreal, who also serves as mayor of the borough of Ville-Marie; the borough mayors of the city's 19 boroughs; city councillors elected in each electoral district; and borough councillors in certain boroughs. Members are elected every four years in municipal elections held across Quebec.

Structure and powers

The mayor chairs an executive committee, composed of councillors, which functions much like a cabinet and prepares matters for council approval. The city council adopts the municipal budget, enacts bylaws, and sets policy directions, while borough councils are responsible for local services such as recreation, road maintenance, and zoning matters within their territories.
