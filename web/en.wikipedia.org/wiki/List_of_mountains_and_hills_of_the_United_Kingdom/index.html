List of mountains and hills of the United Kingdom

The mountains and hills of the United Kingdom are concentrated in Scotland, Wales and northern England. The highest summit is Ben Nevis in the Scottish Highlands at 1,345 metres (4,413 ft). British hills are classified in many popular lists, including Munros (Scottish peaks over 3,000 ft), Corbetts, Wainwrights in the Lake District, and Marilyns (peaks of any height with at least 150 m of prominence).

Classifications

Scotland's Munros number 282 peaks over 3,000 ft (914.4 m) with sufficient separation; Corbetts are 2,500–3,000 ft with 500 ft prominence (222 peaks); Grahams are 600–762 m; Donalds cover the Lowlands. In England, the Wainwrights are the 214 fells described by Alfred Wainwright in the Lake District. Marilyns (1,556 across Britain) require 150 m prominence regardless of height.
