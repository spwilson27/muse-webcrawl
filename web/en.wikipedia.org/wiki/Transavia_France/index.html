Transavia Airlines S.A.S., trading as Transavia France and formerly transavia.com France, is a French low-cost airline based in Paray-Vieille-Poste, near Paris Orly Airport. It is part of the Air France–KLM group and the French arm of the Transavia brand, sharing it with the Dutch airline Transavia.

History

The airline was established in November 2006 and began operations in May 2007, initially flying charter and scheduled leisure services from Paris Orly. It was owned jointly by Air France and the Dutch Transavia; Air France–KLM later moved to take full ownership. In the mid-2010s the brand was unified with its Dutch sister company under the single Transavia name and livery.

Operations

Transavia France operates scheduled and charter flights to leisure destinations around the Mediterranean, in Europe, North Africa and the Middle East, from bases including Paris Orly, Nantes, Lyon and Montpellier. Following the withdrawal of Air France from many domestic routes from Orly, Transavia France expanded significantly on French domestic and European services in the early 2020s. Its fleet consists of Boeing 737-800 aircraft, with Airbus A320neo-family aircraft introduced from 2024.
