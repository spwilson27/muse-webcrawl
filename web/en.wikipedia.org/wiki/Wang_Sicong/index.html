Wang Sicong

Wang Sicong
Born: 3 January 1988, Dalian, China
Occupation: Businessman, investor
Parent: Wang Jianlin

Wang Sicong (Chinese: 王思聪; born 3 January 1988) is a Chinese businessman and the only son of Wang Jianlin, founder of the Dalian Wanda Group, one of China's largest real estate developers. Wang Sicong is among China's most prominent fuerdai ("rich second generation") and became a celebrity in his own right through his outspoken presence on social media and his investments in esports and entertainment.

Early life and education

Wang was born in Dalian, Liaoning. He was educated abroad, attending school in Singapore and Winchester College in England, and later studied philosophy at University College London.

Career

In 2009 Wang founded Prometheus Capital (Pusi Capital), a private equity fund, with capital provided by his father. In 2011 he acquired the esports team Invictus Gaming (iG), which won the League of Legends World Championship in 2018, the first Chinese team to do so. He also founded the streaming platform Panda TV in 2015; it shut down in 2019 amid financial difficulties. Wang has served as a director of Wanda Group and pursued ventures in entertainment, film and hospitality.

Public image

Wang accumulated tens of millions of followers on Weibo, where his frank criticism of celebrities and companies, and displays of lavish spending, frequently made headlines. Sometimes dubbed "China's most eligible bachelor" and "the people's husband" by fans, he has also faced controversies and, in 2019, was listed as a judgment debtor in a dispute involving Panda TV before the matter was settled.
