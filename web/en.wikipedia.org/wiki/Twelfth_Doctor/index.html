The Twelfth Doctor is an incarnation of the Doctor, the protagonist of the BBC science fiction television series Doctor Who. He is portrayed by Scottish actor Peter Capaldi, who first appeared in the role in the 2013 Christmas special "The Time of the Doctor" and headlined series 8 to 10, from 2014 to 2017.

Characterisation

Capaldi's Doctor was conceived as a sharp break from his youthful predecessors: older, fiercer and more socially abrasive, with "attack eyebrows" and a stripped-back, magician-like wardrobe of dark coat, white shirt and (in later series) a velvet jacket. Initially uncertain whether he was "a good man", this incarnation gradually softened; his arc explored kindness as a chosen discipline rather than a nature. He was accompanied first by Clara Oswald, whose relationship with him grew dangerously co-dependent, and later by Bill Potts and Nardole, during a period when the Doctor guarded a vault beneath a university in Bristol while posing as a lecturer.

Stories

The Twelfth Doctor's era included the return of Missy — a female incarnation of the Master, played by Michelle Gomez — whose redemption became a central thread, as well as the acclaimed episodes "Heaven Sent", a one-hander largely featuring Capaldi alone, and "World Enough and Time", which paired him with his first incarnation. He encountered Davros as a boy and as a dying tyrant, fought the Zygons and the Boneless, and lost Clara in the series 9 finale arc. In the 2017 Christmas special "Twice Upon a Time", having resisted regeneration, he finally accepted change and regenerated into the Thirteenth Doctor, played by Jodie Whittaker.

Reception

Capaldi's performance was widely praised by critics, who noted his gravitas and comic timing, though his early, harsher characterisation divided some viewers. "Heaven Sent" is frequently ranked among the greatest episodes of the revived series.
