JCDecaux

JCDecaux Group is a French multinational corporation based in Neuilly-sur-Seine, known for its outdoor advertising systems, including bus-stop advertising, billboards and street furniture. It is one of the largest outdoor advertising companies in the world.

History

Jean-Claude Decaux founded the company in 1964, pioneering the concept of funding street furniture — beginning with bus shelters — through advertising. The company expanded internationally and is listed on Euronext Paris.

Operations

JCDecaux operates in street furniture, transport advertising (airports, subways, buses) and billboard advertising, and runs self-service bicycle hire schemes in many cities.
