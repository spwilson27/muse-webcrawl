T1 (esports)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

T1 (previously known as SK Telecom T1 or SKT T1) is a South Korean esports organisation operated by T1 Entertainment & Sports, a joint venture between SK Telecom and Comcast Spectacor. Founded in 2002, the organisation is best known for its League of Legends team, which competes in the LCK and is widely regarded as the most successful in the game's history.

League of Legends

The team rose to prominence in 2013, winning the Season 3 World Championship with rookie mid laner Lee Sang-hyeok, "Faker", who became the face of the organisation and of professional League of Legends generally. T1 went on to win the World Championship again in 2015 and 2016 — the first back-to-back titles and the first three titles by any organisation — and added further world titles in 2023 and 2024 with a core built around Faker and younger players including Zeus, Oner, Gumayusi and Keria. The organisation has also won numerous LCK domestic championships and two Mid-Season Invitational titles (2016, 2017).

Other divisions

Originating in StarCraft competition in the early 2000s, where it fielded legendary players such as BoxeR, T1 has over the years maintained teams in Valorant, Dota 2, Apex Legends, Overwatch, Tekken and other titles, with varying degrees of success. In 2019 the organisation rebranded from SK Telecom T1 to simply T1 as part of the joint venture with Comcast Spectacor.

Organisation

T1 is based in Seoul, with its headquarters and training facilities in the Gangnam area. The organisation is known for a famously demanding practice culture, a large global fanbase, and commercial partnerships built heavily around Faker's star power.
