Jürgen Norbert Klopp (born 16 June 1967) is a German football executive and former manager and player. He is widely regarded as one of the best football managers of his generation, and is currently the global head of soccer at Red Bull GmbH, a role he began on 1 January 2025.

Klopp spent the majority of his playing career at Mainz 05. Initially employed as a striker, he was later moved to defence. Upon retiring in 2001, he immediately became the club's manager, and in 2004 led them to promotion to the Bundesliga for the first time in their history.

After suffering relegation with Mainz in the 2006–07 season, Klopp resigned and was appointed manager of Borussia Dortmund. He guided them to Bundesliga titles in 2010–11 and 2011–12, as well as the DFB-Pokal in 2011–12 (the club's first domestic double), the DFL-Supercup in 2013 and 2014, and their second appearance in the UEFA Champions League final in 2013.

Klopp was appointed manager of Liverpool in 2015. He guided the club to Champions League finals in 2018 and 2022, winning the latter as well as the FIFA Club World Cup and UEFA Super Cup in 2019. In the 2019–20 season he ended Liverpool's 30-year league title wait, winning the Premier League.

Managerial style

Klopp is a notable proponent of Gegenpressing, a tactic in which the team immediately attempts to win back possession after losing it. His sides have also been noted for their heavy metal-inspired attacking play, fast counter-attacking, and the charismatic, emotionally expressive personality he displays on the touchline.
