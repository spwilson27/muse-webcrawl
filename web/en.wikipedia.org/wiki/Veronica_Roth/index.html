Veronica Roth

Veronica Anne Roth (born 19 August 1988) is an American novelist and short-story writer, best known for the Divergent trilogy of dystopian young adult novels, which has sold tens of millions of copies and was adapted into a film series.

Life and career

Roth was born in New York City and raised in Barrington, Illinois. She wrote Divergent while studying creative writing at Northwestern University; it was published in 2011, followed by Insurgent (2012) and Allegiant (2013), with the companion collection Four (2014). The novels depict a post-apocalyptic Chicago divided into five factions based on virtues.

Later work

Her subsequent books include the Carve the Mark duology (2017–2018), the short-story collection The End and Other Beginnings (2019), and the adult fantasy novels Chosen Ones (2020) and Poster Girl (2022), as well as When Among Crows (2024).
