InterAcademy Partnership

The InterAcademy Partnership (IAP) is a global network of more than 140 national and regional academies of science, engineering, and medicine. It brings together academies to provide independent expert advice on scientific, technological, and health issues to policymakers and the public.

History

The IAP in its current form was established in 2016 by merging earlier bodies, including the InterAcademy Panel (founded 1993) and the InterAcademy Medical Panel, together with regional academy networks for Africa, the Americas, Asia, and Europe. Its secretariat is hosted in Trieste, Italy, and Washington, D.C.

Activities

The partnership issues consensus statements and reports on topics such as climate change, food security, health, and science education; supports young academies and women in science; and works to strengthen the capacity of academies in developing countries to advise their governments.
