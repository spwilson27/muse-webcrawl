List of Grey Cup champions

The Grey Cup is the championship game of the Canadian Football League (CFL) and the trophy awarded to the victorious team. The trophy is named after Albert Grey, the Governor General of Canada from 1904 to 1911, who donated it in 1909 as a prize for the Dominion amateur football championship of Canada. Since 1954 it has been awarded exclusively to professional teams in the CFL.

The Toronto Argonauts have won the most Grey Cups of any team, while the Edmonton Elks (formerly the Eskimos) won five consecutive championships from 1978 to 1982. The game is traditionally held on a Sunday in late November.

The 1995 Baltimore Stallions are the only American-based team to win the Grey Cup. No game was held from 1916 to 1919 because of the First World War and the influenza pandemic, and the 2020 game was cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
