Fiat 125

The Fiat 125 is a large family car manufactured and marketed by the Italian company Fiat from 1967 to 1972. Derivatives were built under licence outside Italy until the 1990s.

Design

The 125 combined the floor pan of the outgoing Fiat 1300/1500 with styling resembling the smaller 124 and a new twin-overhead-camshaft 1,608 cc engine derived from the 124's unit. It offered lively performance for its class and was followed by the revised 125 Special.

Licensed production

The model was produced in Poland by FSO as the Polski Fiat 125p (later FSO 125p), forming the basis of the Polonez, and versions were assembled in several other countries including Argentina, Egypt, and Morocco.
