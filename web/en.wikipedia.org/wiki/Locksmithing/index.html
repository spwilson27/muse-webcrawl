Locksmithing
Occupation: Tradesperson
Activity: Locks, keys, security systems

Locksmithing is the science and art of making and defeating locks and other security mechanisms. A locksmith installs, repairs, rebuilds and services locks, safes, doors and security systems, and cuts and duplicates keys.

('History', 'The craft dates to ancient Egypt and Babylon, where wooden pin locks were used, and developed through Roman metal locks, medieval guilds, and the patent locks of the 18th and 19th centuries by inventors such as Robert Barron, Joseph Bramah and Linus Yale Jr.')

('Modern practice', 'Modern locksmiths work with electronic access control, keyless entry, automotive transponder keys and safes, and are often licensed. Ethical and legal rules restrict possession of lock-picking tools and entry services to verified owners.')
