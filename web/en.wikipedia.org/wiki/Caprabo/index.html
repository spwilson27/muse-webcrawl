Caprabo is a Spanish supermarket chain founded in Barcelona in 1959, whose name is an acronym of the surnames of its founding families — Carbó, Prat and Botet. It operates mainly in Catalonia, where it is one of the most recognised regional grocery brands, with a network of several hundred supermarkets and franchises.

History

The first Caprabo shop opened on Barcelona's Carrer de Sant Antoni Maria Claret in 1959 and is often credited with pioneering the modern self-service supermarket format in Spain, including early innovations such as a customer loyalty card ("My Caprabo") and check-guarantee cards. The company expanded across Catalonia and into the Balearic Islands, Navarre and Madrid.

Ownership

In 2007 the Basque cooperative retailer Eroski acquired a majority stake in Caprabo in a deal valuing the company at over one billion euros; the Caprabo brand was retained for its Catalan stores. The chain later restructured, selling some stores outside Catalonia and focusing on its home region.
