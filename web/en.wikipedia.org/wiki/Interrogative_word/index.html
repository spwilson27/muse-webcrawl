Interrogative word

An interrogative word or question word is a function word used to ask a question, such as what, which, when, where, who, whom, whose, why and how. In English most question words begin with wh- and are often called wh-words.

Usage

Interrogative words introduce information questions, as opposed to yes–no questions. They may function as pronouns (who, what), determiners (which, whose) or adverbs (where, when, why, how).

Wh-movement

In many languages the question word or phrase moves to the front of the clause, a phenomenon linguists call wh-movement. In embedded questions, word order may differ from direct questions.

Other languages

Languages vary widely: some place question words in situ, and many use question particles for yes–no questions instead of or alongside word order changes.
