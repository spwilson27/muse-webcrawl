Christine Sinclair

Christine Margaret Sinclair
Born: 12 June 1983, Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada
Height: 1.75 m (5 ft 9 in)
Position: Forward
National team: Canada (2000–2023)

Christine Margaret Sinclair (born 12 June 1983) is a Canadian former professional soccer player who played as a forward. She spent most of her club career with the Portland Thorns of the National Women's Soccer League and captained the Canada women's national team from 2006 until her international retirement in 2023.

Sinclair is the world's all-time leading international goal scorer for both men and women, with 190 goals in 331 appearances for Canada. She surpassed the previous record held by American striker Abby Wambach in January 2020. She competed in five FIFA Women's World Cups and four Olympic Games, winning a gold medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics and bronze medals at London 2012 and Rio 2016.

Career

Sinclair played college soccer at the University of Portland, winning two NCAA championships. In the professional game she played for FC Gold Pride and Western New York Flash in Women's Professional Soccer — winning championships with both — before joining Portland Thorns for the inaugural NWSL season in 2013, winning three NWSL Championships with the club. She announced her club retirement in 2024.

Honours and recognition

She was named Canada Soccer Player of the Year 14 times and was appointed an Officer of the Order of Canada. In 2023 she was inducted into Canada's Sports Hall of Fame. Her hometown of Burnaby has honoured her extensively, and she is widely regarded as Canada's greatest-ever soccer player.
