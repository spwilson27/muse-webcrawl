Will Swan is an American guitarist and songwriter best known as a founding member and lead guitarist of the Sacramento post-hardcore band Dance Gavin Dance. He is also known for his side projects, including the supergroup Sianvar and the band Royal Coda, and for founding the independent record label Blue Swan Records, named after his surname.

Career

Swan formed Dance Gavin Dance in Sacramento in 2005, and the band quickly developed a following for its fusion of post-hardcore, funk, math rock and progressive elements. Swan's intricate, tapped guitar work and the band's rotating cast of vocalists became central features of its sound across a long run of studio albums.

In 2013 Swan launched Blue Swan Records, an imprint that signed a number of progressive and post-hardcore acts from the Sacramento scene and beyond, including Hail the Sun, Stolas and Icarus the Owl. He later played in the supergroup Sianvar alongside members of Circa Survive, Hail the Sun and A Lot Like Birds, and joined Royal Coda with former Dance Gavin Dance vocalist Kurt Travis and guitarist Sergio Medina.

Musical style

Swan's playing is characterised by fast legato runs, two-handed tapping, funk-influenced rhythm work and frequent use of delay and modulation effects. His style has been widely imitated within the subgenre sometimes nicknamed "Swancore", a term fans coined for the cluster of bands associated with his label and sound.
