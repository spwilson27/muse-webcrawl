Het Nieuwsblad is a Flemish daily newspaper published in Belgium by Mediahuis. Founded in 1929, it is known for popular journalism, extensive sports coverage — especially cycling — and regional editions across Flanders.

History

The paper grew through mergers with titles including Het Volk in 2008. It organises the Tour of Flanders-related cycling heritage and the Omloop Het Nieuwsblad opening classic race, first held in 1945.

Circulation

It is one of the largest Dutch-language newspapers in Belgium, sold alongside its sister title De Standaard within the Mediahuis group.
