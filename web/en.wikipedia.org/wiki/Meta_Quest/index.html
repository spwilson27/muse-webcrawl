Meta Quest is a line of standalone virtual reality headsets developed by Reality Labs, a division of Meta Platforms. The headsets run Meta Horizon OS, based on Android, and do not require a PC or external sensors: inside-out tracking cameras follow the headset and its Touch controllers, while the headsets can also be tethered to a PC via Quest Link to play PC VR software.

Models

  • Oculus Quest (2019): the first model, launched at $399 with a Snapdragon 835 and OLED display.
  • Meta Quest 2 (2020): originally Oculus Quest 2; lighter, faster (Snapdragon XR2) and cheaper, it became the best-selling VR headset in the world.
  • Meta Quest Pro (2022): a high-end mixed-reality headset with colour passthrough and face/eye tracking, aimed at professionals; discontinued in 2024.
  • Meta Quest 3 (2023): Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2, pancake lenses, colour mixed-reality passthrough; followed by the budget Quest 3S in 2024.

History

The Quest line descends from Oculus VR, founded by Palmer Luckey and acquired by Facebook (now Meta) for about $2 billion in 2014. As part of Meta's corporate rebranding in 2021–2022, the Oculus name was retired in favour of Meta Quest. The headsets anchor Meta's "metaverse" strategy and its Horizon social platform, and by early 2023 Meta reported nearly 20 million Quest units sold, though Reality Labs accumulated tens of billions of dollars in operating losses.
