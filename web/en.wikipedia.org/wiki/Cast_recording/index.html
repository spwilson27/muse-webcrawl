Cast recording

A cast recording is a recording of a stage musical that is intended to document the songs as they are performed in the show and to preserve them for listeners. Cast recordings are typically made after a show's opening and feature the original theatre cast, hence the designation original cast recording, often abbreviated as OCR. A recording of the songs of a stage musical made with the original cast is called an original cast recording; if the cast is from a Broadway production it is commonly called an original Broadway cast recording (OBCR), and similarly original London cast recording (OLCR) for the West End.

History

In the early decades of the 20th century, recordings of musical theatre songs were usually made by individual singers rather than complete stage casts. The practice of recording an entire score with the cast of a production began in Britain in the 1920s and 1930s, and a landmark in the United States was the 1943 Decca album of Oklahoma!, recorded with its original Broadway cast and orchestra. Its enormous commercial success established the original cast album as a fixture of American popular music; cast albums from shows such as My Fair Lady and The Sound of Music became among the best-selling records of their eras.

Terminology

Cast recordings are distinguished from soundtrack albums, a term properly reserved for recordings from films and television. A studio cast recording is made with performers who did not appear in a particular stage production, while a revival cast recording documents a later production. Concept albums, such as the original recording of Jesus Christ Superstar, may precede and shape a stage production.

Production

Cast albums are traditionally recorded quickly — often in a single long session held shortly after opening night — capturing the cast's performances while preserving the orchestrations used in the theatre. Producers may adjust tempos, restore cut material, or record songs cut from the production for inclusion as bonus tracks. Since 1971, cast recordings have been eligible for the Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album.
