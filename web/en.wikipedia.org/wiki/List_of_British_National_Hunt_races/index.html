List of British National Hunt races

This is a list of notable National Hunt horse races which take place annually in Great Britain under the rules of the British Horseracing Authority. National Hunt racing comprises hurdle races and steeplechases, run mainly through the winter months.

Principal handicaps

The most famous National Hunt race of all, the Grand National at Aintree, is a handicap steeplechase over 4 miles 2½ furlongs and 30 fences. Other major handicaps include the Coral Gold Cup (formerly the Hennessy Gold Cup) at Newbury, the Welsh Grand National at Chepstow, the Scottish Grand National at Ayr, the Bet365 Gold Cup at Sandown and the Ultima Handicap Chase and County Hurdle at the Cheltenham Festival.

Festivals

The season's principal meetings are the Cheltenham Festival in March, featuring 28 races over four days; the Grand National meeting at Aintree in April; the Punchestown Festival in Ireland; and the Christmas meeting at Kempton Park, headlined by the King George VI Chase on Boxing Day.
