Valais

Valais (French: Valais; German: Wallis) is one of the 26 cantons of Switzerland, situated in the southwestern part of the country, around the valley of the upper Rhône River. Its capital is Sion. The canton is bilingual, with French spoken in the west (Lower Valais) and German in the east (Upper Valais).

Geography

Valais contains many of the highest peaks of the Alps, including the Matterhorn, Monte Rosa (the highest point in Switzerland on its border with Italy), the Dom, and the Weisshorn. The Aletsch Glacier, the largest glacier in the Alps, lies partly in the canton, and the Rhône Glacier is the source of the river. The canton borders Italy and France, and major transalpine routes cross it, including the Great St Bernard Pass and the Simplon Pass.

Economy

Tourism dominates the economy, with resorts such as Zermatt, Verbier, Saas-Fee, and Crans-Montana. The dry, sunny climate of the central Rhône valley also supports extensive vineyards — Valais is Switzerland's largest wine-producing canton — and orchards, notably apricots. Hydroelectric power generation is another major industry.
