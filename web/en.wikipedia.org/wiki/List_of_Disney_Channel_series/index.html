List of Disney Channel series

This is a list of television series broadcast on Disney Channel in the United States, including original live-action and animated series. The channel launched on April 18, 1983, initially as a premium channel before becoming a basic cable service.

Selected original series

  • The Mickey Mouse Club (revivals, 1989–1994)
  • DuckTales (syndicated/Disney Afternoon, closely associated era programming)
  • Lizzie McGuire (2001–2004)
  • Even Stevens (2000–2003)
  • Kim Possible (2002–2007)
  • That's So Raven (2003–2007)
  • The Suite Life of Zack & Cody (2005–2008)
  • Hannah Montana (2006–2011)
  • Wizards of Waverly Place (2007–2012)
  • Phineas and Ferb (2007–2015)
  • Good Luck Charlie (2010–2014)
  • Gravity Falls (2012–2016)
  • Girl Meets World (2014–2017)
  • Star vs. the Forces of Evil (2015–2019)
  • Amphibia (2019–2022)
  • The Owl House (2020–2023)
  • Big City Greens (2018– )
