ACL Anthology

The ACL Anthology is a digital archive of research papers in computational linguistics and natural language processing. It is maintained by the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL) and contains the proceedings of the major conferences and workshops in the field, including ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLING, EACL, and LREC, as well as journal publications such as Computational Linguistics and Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics.

History

The Anthology grew out of earlier efforts to digitize conference proceedings in the late 1990s and early 2000s. It was substantially rebuilt and relaunched in 2019 with a new static-site architecture, richer metadata, and stable identifiers (ACL Anthology IDs) for every paper. By the mid-2020s it held well over 100,000 papers.

Features

Each paper has a landing page with its abstract, authors, venue, BibTeX entry, and a PDF. The Anthology provides bulk exports of its metadata for bibliometric research and is a key data source for tools such as Semantic Scholar. It is developed as open-source software with community contributions through GitHub.
