Santa Monica Boulevard is a major east–west thoroughfare in Los Angeles County, California, running from Ocean Avenue in downtown Santa Monica through West Los Angeles, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, and Hollywood to Sunset Boulevard in Silver Lake. For much of the 20th century it carried the western end of U.S. Route 66, which terminated at the Santa Monica Pier.

Route and districts

From the coast the boulevard passes the commercial centre of Santa Monica, the civic and office corridor of West LA, and Century City. In Beverly Hills it runs past the city's gardens and civic centre; the segment through West Hollywood — with the rainbow-flag crosswalks and the densest stretch of LGBTQ bars and clubs in the region — is a centrepiece of that city's identity and of LA Pride events. Eastward it crosses Hollywood, passing studios and the Hollywood Forever Cemetery, before continuing through East Hollywood and Los Feliz. In 2004–2005 the West Hollywood portion was extensively landscaped with a wide median.

In culture

The street is the subject of Sheryl Crow's 1993 hit "All I Wanna Do", whose opening line names the boulevard, and it appears throughout Los Angeles-set film and television. A separate, older parallel street, Little Santa Monica Boulevard, runs through Beverly Hills and Century City.
