Screenwipe

Charlie Brooker's Screenwipe
Genre: Comedy, review
Created by: Charlie Brooker
Original network: BBC Four
Original release: 2006–2008

Charlie Brooker's Screenwipe is a British television review programme created and presented by Charlie Brooker, broadcast on BBC Four from 2006 to 2008. In each episode Brooker satirically reviewed other television programmes, genres and industry practices, mixing clips, analysis and acerbic monologues delivered to camera from his living room.

Format and legacy

The show combined TV criticism with comedy, examines formats such as news, advertising, children's television and reality shows. It led to spin-offs and successors including Newswipe, the panel show You Have Been Watching, and Brooker's annual Wipe specials reviewing the year across media. Regular contributors included Tim Key and Barry Shitpeas (played by Al Campbell).
