Bhanubhakta Acharya

Born13 July 1814, Chundi Ramgha, Tanahun, Nepal
Died23 April 1868
OccupationPoet, translator
Known forTranslating the Ramayana into Nepali; first Nepali poet (Adikavi)

Bhanubhakta Acharya (1814 – 1868) was a Nepali poet, translator and writer who is regarded as the first poet (Adikavi) of the Nepali language. He is celebrated for his translation of the Sanskrit epic Ramayana into Nepali, which made the story accessible to ordinary people and helped establish Nepali as a literary language.

Life and work

Born into a Brahmin family in Chundi Ramgha in the Tanahun district, Acharya received a traditional education from his grandfather. Legend holds that an encounter with a grasscutter inspired him to devote his life to literature. His version of the Ramayana, composed in simple, lyrical verse, was published posthumously and became enormously popular.

Legacy

Acharya's birthday, 29 Ashadh in the Nepali calendar, is celebrated nationwide as Bhanu Jayanti. Statues of him stand across Nepal and in Nepali-speaking regions of India, and he is honoured as a unifying figure of Nepali cultural identity.
