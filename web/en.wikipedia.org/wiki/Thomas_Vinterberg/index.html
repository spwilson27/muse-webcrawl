Thomas Vinterberg

Thomas Vinterberg (born 19 May 1969) is a Danish film director who, along with Lars von Trier, co-founded the Dogme 95 movement in filmmaking. His films include Festen (The Celebration, 1998), the first Dogme film; The Hunt (2012); and Another Round (2020), which won the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film.

Career

Vinterberg graduated from the National Film School of Denmark in 1993. In 1995 he and Lars von Trier drafted the Dogme 95 manifesto and its "Vow of Chastity", a set of rules favouring story, acting and theme over technology and special effects. Festen (1998), about a family gathering at which a son accuses his father of abuse, won the Jury Prize at the Cannes Film Festival and brought Dogme international attention.

After English-language films including It's All About Love (2003) and Dear Wendy (2005), Vinterberg returned to Danish subjects with Submarino (2010) and The Hunt (2012), in which Mads Mikkelsen plays a kindergarten teacher falsely accused of child abuse; Mikkelsen won Best Actor at Cannes. Far from the Madding Crowd (2015) and the submarine-disaster film Kursk (2018) followed. Another Round (2020), reuniting him with Mikkelsen in a story about teachers experimenting with alcohol, won the Academy Award and BAFTA for best international/foreign-language film.
