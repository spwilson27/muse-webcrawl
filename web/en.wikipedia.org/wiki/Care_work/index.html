Care work

Care work is the work of caring for others — including childcare, eldercare, healthcare, and domestic work — whether paid or unpaid. It is essential to human wellbeing and the functioning of economies, yet is often undervalued, low-paid, and disproportionately performed by women.

Care work
Forms: Childcare, eldercare, nursing, domestic labour
Sectors: Households, health, social care
Key issue: Unpaid and undervalued labour

Unpaid care

The International Labour Organization estimates that unpaid care work amounts to billions of hours daily, worth a significant share of global GDP if valued at market wages. Women perform roughly three-quarters of it, a disparity sometimes called the "care penalty".

Paid care

Paid care workers — nurses, home helps, childcare workers, cleaners — are a large and growing workforce, especially as populations age. Feminist economists and the ILO promote the "5R" framework: recognise, reduce, and redistribute unpaid care, and reward and represent care workers.
