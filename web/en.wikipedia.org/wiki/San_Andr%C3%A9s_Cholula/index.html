San Andrés Cholula is a municipality and town in the Mexican state of Puebla, part of the Puebla metropolitan area and one half of the historic city of Cholula, together with San Pedro Cholula.

History and sights

The Cholula area was one of the great cities of pre-Hispanic Mesoamerica. San Andrés is dominated by the Great Pyramid of Cholula — the largest pyramid by volume known in the world — topped by the colonial church of Nuestra Señora de los Remedios. The town has numerous colonial churches and a lively student population owing to the nearby Universidad de las Américas Puebla.

Today

Rapid urban growth has made San Andrés one of the wealthier municipalities in the region, with modern residential and commercial development alongside its archaeological and colonial heritage.
