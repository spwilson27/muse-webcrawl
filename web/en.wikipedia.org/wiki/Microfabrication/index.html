Microfabrication

Microfabrication is the process of fabricating miniature structures of micrometre scales and smaller. Historically, the earliest microfabrication processes were used for integrated circuit fabrication, also known as semiconductor manufacturing.

Processes

Key steps include photolithography, thin-film deposition, etching (wet and dry/plasma), ion implantation, diffusion, oxidation and planarisation. These steps are repeated in sequence on silicon wafers inside cleanrooms to build transistors and interconnects layer by layer.

Applications

Beyond integrated circuits, microfabrication enables microelectromechanical systems (MEMS), microfluidic lab-on-a-chip devices, sensors and displays. Nanofabrication extends the same principles below 100 nanometres.
