NatWest Group

NatWest Group plc
Founded: 1968 (as National and Commercial Banking Group)
Headquarters: Edinburgh, Scotland
Brands: NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland, Ulster Bank, Coutts
Industry: Banking and financial services

NatWest Group plc is a British banking and insurance holding company based in Edinburgh, Scotland. It operates through the brands NatWest and Royal Bank of Scotland in Great Britain and Ulster Bank in Ireland, serving personal, business and corporate customers, and owns the private bank Coutts.

History

The group originated in 1968 with the merger that formed the National and Commercial Banking Group, renamed Royal Bank of Scotland Group in 1985. Its 2000 takeover of NatWest made it one of the world's largest banks, followed in 2007 by the disastrous consortium purchase of ABN AMRO. During the 2008 financial crisis the group received a £45.5 billion UK government bailout, leaving the state with a majority stake of about 84% at its peak. The bank was renamed NatWest Group in 2020 to reflect its largest customer brand. The UK government progressively sold down its holding, returning the group fully to private ownership in 2025.

Operations

NatWest Group is concentrated on UK and Irish retail and commercial banking; its former global investment banking arm was wound down or sold after the crisis. It is a FTSE 100 constituent.
