Pori Jazz

Pori Jazz
Location: Pori, Finland
Years active: 1966–present
Genre: Jazz, popular music

Pori Jazz is a large international jazz festival held annually in Pori, on the west coast of Finland. Founded in 1966, it is one of the oldest and best known jazz festivals in Europe, drawing around 120,000 to 150,000 visitors each year.

The main concerts are held at the Kirjurinluoto Arena, an outdoor venue in a park on an island near the town centre, while smaller events take place at venues and streets across the city, including the pedestrian "Jazz Street" along the Kokemäenjoki river.

History

The first Pori Jazz festival was held in July 1966 with an audience of about 600. Over the decades the programme broadened from jazz to include blues, soul, funk, hip-hop, reggae and popular music. Artists who have performed at Pori Jazz include B. B. King, Miles Davis, Bob Dylan, Sting, Alicia Keys and many others.
