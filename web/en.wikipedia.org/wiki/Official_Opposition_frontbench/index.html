Official Opposition frontbench

The Official Opposition frontbench faces the government frontbench across the floor of the House of Commons.

The Official Opposition frontbench in the United Kingdom consists of the senior members of the Shadow Cabinet and other shadow ministers of His Majesty's Most Loyal Opposition, who sit on the front benches of the House of Commons opposite the government and scrutinise their corresponding government departments and ministers.

Composition

The frontbench is led by the Leader of the Opposition and includes the shadow chancellor of the exchequer, shadow home secretary, shadow foreign secretary, the opposition chief whip, and shadow secretaries of state and ministers shadowing each government department. Members are appointed by the Leader of the Opposition, usually following a general election or a reshuffle.

Role

Shadow ministers question their counterparts at departmental question times, respond to ministerial statements and urgent questions, lead for the opposition in debates, and develop alternative policies in preparation for a possible change of government. Frontbenchers are bound by collective responsibility for opposition policy and are normally expected to vote with the party line.

Related arrangements

Smaller opposition parties also maintain their own frontbench teams of spokespeople, though without the "Official Opposition" status, funding (Short Money), and guaranteed parliamentary time accorded to the largest opposition party. Equivalent shadow frontbenches exist in the devolved legislatures and in other Westminster-system parliaments, including Canada and Australia.
