John Michael Green (born August 24, 1977) is an American author, YouTuber, and podcaster. He is best known for his young adult novels, including Looking for Alaska (2005), The Fault in Our Stars (2012), and Turtles All the Way Down (2017). His works have been translated into dozens of languages and have sold millions of copies worldwide.

Early life and education

Green was born in Indianapolis, Indiana, and grew up in Michigan, Alabama, and Florida. He attended Kenyon College, graduating in 2000 with a double major in English and religious studies. Before becoming a full-time writer, he worked as a book reviewer and as a chaplain at a children's hospital.

Career

Green's debut novel, Looking for Alaska, won the Michael L. Printz Award in 2006. He has also written An Abundance of Katherines (2006), Paper Towns (2008), and Will Grayson, Will Grayson (2010), the last co-written with David Levithan. Along with his brother Hank Green, he co-created the Vlogbrothers YouTube channel and co-founded the educational media company Complexly, which produces series such as Crash Course and SciShow.

Nonfiction and advocacy

In 2021 Green published The Anthropocene Reviewed, a collection of essays rating aspects of the human-centered planet. His 2025 book Everything Is Tuberculosis examines the history and present-day burden of tuberculosis. Green is a prominent advocate for global health equity and for reducing maternal mortality in Sierra Leone.
