Hotel is an American primetime soap opera that aired on ABC from 1983 to 1988. Produced by Aaron Spelling and based on Arthur Hailey's 1965 novel — previously filmed in 1967 — the series is set at the fictional St. Gregory Hotel in San Francisco, filmed at the Fairmont Hotel, and follows the intertwined lives of its staff and a rotating cast of guest stars in anthology-style storylines, in the manner of The Love Boat on land.

Premise and cast

James Brolin played general manager Peter McDermott, with Connie Sellecca as his assistant and later wife Christine Francis. Anne Baxter, in her final role, played the hotel's owner Victoria Cabot; Bette Davis had played the part in the pilot. Shari Belafonte, Nathan Cook, and Michael Spound were among the regular staff, while each episode featured prominent guest stars of the era.

Reception

A top-25 ratings performer in its early seasons, Hotel won a People's Choice Award and was nominated for Golden Globes. Ratings declined after Baxter's death in 1985 and cast changes, and ABC cancelled the series in 1988 after five seasons and 114 episodes. It continued in syndication and has been released on DVD.
