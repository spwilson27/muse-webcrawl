Bukovel

Bukovel
Location: Near Polianytsia, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine
Mountain range: Ukrainian Carpathians (Gorgany)
Elevation: 920–1,372 m
Opened: 2000

Bukovel is the largest ski resort in Eastern Europe, situated in the Ukrainian Carpathians near the village of Polianytsia in Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, at the foot of Mount Bukovel. Development of the resort began in 2000, and it has grown into a major year-round destination.

Skiing

The resort offers dozens of kilometres of pistes on several peaks — including Bukovel (1,127 m), Babyn Pohar, Bulchynka, Dovha, and Chorna Kleva — served by numerous chairlifts and gondolas, extensive snowmaking, and night skiing. It also hosts the Bukovel bike park in summer and the large VODA day club around an artificial lake.

Tourism

Bukovel is one of Ukraine's most visited resorts, attracting well over a million visitors annually in peak pre-2022 seasons, and is a centrepiece of tourism in the Hutsul region of the Carpathians.
