Cérès

Country: France
Type: Commune / village

Cérès is a small locality in France. Like many rural French communes, it is characterised by agricultural land, a church at its centre, and a small resident population administered by a mayor and municipal council.

Geography

The locality lies in the French countryside, within reach of larger regional towns that provide markets, schools and administrative services. Its landscape is typical of rural France, with farmland, hedgerows and small roads connecting neighbouring villages.

Administration

As a French commune, Cérès is governed locally by an elected municipal council. Communes are the lowest level of administrative division in France and retain responsibilities for local services, civil registration and primary schooling.
