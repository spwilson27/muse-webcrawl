West Edmonton Mall

West Edmonton Mall
Location: Edmonton, Alberta, Canada
Opened: 1981
Developer: Triple Five Group (Ghermezian family)
Gross leasable area: about 3.8 million sq ft (total floor area ~5.3 million sq ft)

West Edmonton Mall (WEM) is a large shopping mall in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, developed by the Ghermezian family's Triple Five Group. Opened in phases beginning in 1981, it was the world's largest shopping mall until 2004 and remains the largest in North America, with more than 800 stores and services and parking for over 20,000 vehicles.

Attractions

Beyond retail, the mall is famous for its leisure attractions: Galaxyland, one of the world's largest indoor amusement parks, home to the Mindbender triple-loop roller coaster (removed in 2023); the World Waterpark, with one of the largest indoor wave pools; an NHL-sized ice rink (the Ice Palace); a sea-life exhibit with sea lions; themed streets such as Bourbon Street and Europa Boulevard; and the attached Fantasyland Hotel with themed rooms.

History

The mall was built in four phases between 1981 and 1998. A 1986 storm caused roof collapses and flooding, and the Mindbender accident of June 1986, in which three riders died, remains one of the worst roller-coaster accidents in history. The mall attracts tens of millions of visitors annually and is one of Alberta's top tourist destinations.
