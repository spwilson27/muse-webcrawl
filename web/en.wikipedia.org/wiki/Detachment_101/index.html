Detachment 101

Detachment 101, also known as the OSS Detachment 101, was a United States special operations unit active during World War II in the China Burma India Theater. Established on 14 April 1942 under the Office of Strategic Services (OSS) — the precursor of the CIA — it was the first American unit formed specifically for guerrilla warfare.

Operating in Japanese-occupied Burma, Detachment 101 trained and led indigenous Kachin fighters. Its missions included rescuing downed Allied airmen, gathering intelligence, sabotaging Japanese supply lines, and ambushing enemy patrols. General William Donovan's OSS credited the detachment with rescuing hundreds of aircrew, and it is often cited as one of the most effective American guerrilla units of the war relative to its size.

Detachment 101 was disbanded on 12 July 1945. Its Kachin Rangers received a Presidential Unit Citation.
