1729 (number)

1729 is the natural number following 1728 and preceding 1730. It is famously known as the Hardy–Ramanujan number after an anecdote involving mathematicians G. H. Hardy and Srinivasa Ramanujan.


Cardinal: one thousand seven hundred twenty-nine
Ordinal: 1729th
Factorization: 7 × 13 × 19
Binary: 110110000012

Hardy–Ramanujan number

Hardy recalled visiting Ramanujan in hospital and remarking that taxi number 1729 seemed dull. Ramanujan replied that it was very interesting: it is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two positive cubes in two different ways: 1729 = 13 + 123 = 93 + 103.

Properties

1729 is a Carmichael number, the third such number, divisible by the sum of its digits (19), a Zeisel number, and a centered cube number. It is the product of three distinct primes, 7, 13 and 19, all in arithmetic progression in a sense related to its Carmichael property.

In popular culture

The number recurs in Futurama and other works as an homage, including starship registry numbers and taxi cabs.
