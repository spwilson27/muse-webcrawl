Centre de données astronomiques de Strasbourg

CDS
Founded: 1972
Location: Strasbourg Observatory, France
Services: SIMBAD, VizieR, Aladin
Parent: CNRS / University of Strasbourg

The Centre de données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS; Strasbourg Astronomical Data Centre) is a French data centre dedicated to the collection and worldwide distribution of astronomical information. Founded in 1972 and hosted by the Strasbourg Observatory, it is one of the founding members of the International Virtual Observatory Alliance.

Services

The CDS operates three major services used daily by astronomers worldwide: SIMBAD, a database of astronomical objects beyond the Solar System with cross-identifications and bibliography; VizieR, a catalogue service giving access to thousands of published astronomical catalogues and tables; and Aladin, an interactive sky atlas allowing users to visualise digitised astronomical images and overlay catalogue data.

Role

Designated by the International Astronomical Union as the world centre for astronomical object identification, the CDS plays a central role in standardising astronomical data formats and in the development of the Virtual Observatory.
