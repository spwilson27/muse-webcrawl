Global Biodiversity Information Facility

The Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF) is an international organisation that focuses on making scientific data on biodiversity available via the internet using web services. The data are provided by many institutions from around the world; GBIF's information architecture makes these data accessible and searchable through a single portal.

Data available through the GBIF portal includes distribution records for plants, animals, fungi, and microbes; scientific names; and occurrence records drawn from museum specimens, field observations and citizen-science platforms. GBIF was founded in 2001 following a recommendation of the OECD's MegaScience Forum, and its secretariat is hosted in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Data and use

GBIF indexes billions of species occurrence records and millions of species and dataset pages. The data, published under open licences (CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-NC), are used in thousands of research papers each year on topics such as climate change, invasive species, conservation planning, and agriculture. GBIF also maintains the Catalogue of Life-derived taxonomic backbone used to match species names across datasets.

Member countries and organisations participate through national nodes, and data publishers register datasets via endpoints using standards such as Darwin Core.
