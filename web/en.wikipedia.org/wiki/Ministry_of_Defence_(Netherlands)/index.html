Ministry of Defence (Netherlands)

The Ministry of Defence (Dutch: Ministerie van Defensie, MoD) is the Dutch government ministry responsible for the Netherlands Armed Forces and for military policy. It is headquartered at the Plein in The Hague. The Minister of Defence is a member of the Council of Ministers; the ministry's senior military officer is the Chief of Defence, the highest-ranking commander of the armed forces.

Responsibilities

The ministry directs the Royal Netherlands Army, the Royal Netherlands Navy, the Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force, and the Royal Marechaussee (military police), together with support organisations responsible for materiel, personnel, and real estate. Its tasks include defending Dutch and allied (NATO) territory, supporting international legal order and stability through deployments, and assisting civil authorities in disasters and emergencies.

History

Separate ministries for war and for the navy existed through much of Dutch history; they were merged into a single Ministry of Defence in 1959. After the Cold War the armed forces were restructured toward expeditionary operations, and conscription was suspended in the 1990s, leaving an all-volunteer force.
