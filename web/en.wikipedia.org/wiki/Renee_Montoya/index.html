Renee Montoya is a fictional character appearing in media published by DC Comics. Created for Batman: The Animated Series in 1992, she was retroactively introduced into the comics shortly before the show aired. Montoya is a detective with the Gotham City Police Department who frequently works alongside Harvey Bullock and Commissioner James Gordon in stories centred on Batman.

Biography

The daughter of Dominican immigrants, Montoya rose to detective in the Major Crimes Unit. In the story arc "Half a Life" (Gotham Central), she was forcibly outed as a lesbian by the villain Two-Face, a landmark storyline in mainstream comics; Gotham Central won critical acclaim for its portrayal of her. After leaving the police force, she was chosen by Vic Sage to succeed him as the masked investigator the Question, a role she assumed in the weekly series 52 (2006–2007).

In other media

Montoya has appeared in animated series including Batman: The Animated Series and Gotham Girls, in the television series Gotham (played by Victoria Cartagena), and in the film Birds of Prey (2020), played by Rosie Perez.
