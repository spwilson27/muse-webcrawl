Street

Street
DefinitionPublic thoroughfare in a built environment, lined with buildings
FunctionsMovement, access, public space, utilities
[Image: A lively city street with shops, pedestrians and a tram]

A street is a public thoroughfare in a built environment, used by people and vehicles and lined with buildings — the basic unit of urban public space in every city. Unlike a highway, a street serves access and social life as much as movement.

Design

Street design balances traffic with walking, cycling, transit, trees and trade; movements like Complete Streets and pedestrianisation remake car-era streets. Names and numbers organise addresses (see Roman roads for the ancestor of them all).
