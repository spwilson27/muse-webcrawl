Kathleen Booth

Kathleen Hylda Valerie Booth (1922–2022) was a British computer scientist who wrote one of the first assembly languages and designed the assembler and autocode for early computers built at Birkbeck College, London, together with her husband Andrew Booth.

Career

Booth joined Birkbeck's computing laboratory in 1947 and worked on the ARC series of machines. Her 1950 book with Andrew Booth, Automatic Digital Calculators, was among the earliest on computer design, and she is credited with the first assembly language.

Later work

She later worked on neural networks and machine translation research, and continued an academic career in Canada and the United States. She died in 2022, aged 100.
