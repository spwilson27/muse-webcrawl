San Bernardino is a city in and the county seat of San Bernardino County, California, in the Inland Empire region of Southern California. Located about 60 miles (97 km) east of Los Angeles at the foot of the San Bernardino Mountains, it is one of the oldest communities in the state, named in 1810 for Saint Bernardino of Siena by Spanish missionaries. The city had a population of about 222,000 at the 2020 census, and anchors the eastern end of the Riverside–San Bernardino–Ontario metropolitan area.

History

The area was inhabited by the Serrano people before Spanish missionaries established an outpost of Mission San Gabriel in 1810. In 1851 a large colony of members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints purchased the Rancho San Bernardino and founded the modern city, laying out its street grid; most were recalled to Utah in 1857 during the Utah War. The city later grew with the arrival of the railroads — it became a major Santa Fe Railway centre with the Santa Fe Depot (1918) — and with citrus agriculture and Route 66 traffic. In 1940 Richard and Maurice McDonald opened their first McDonald's restaurant in San Bernardino. Norton Air Force Base was a major employer until its closure in 1994.

San Bernardino struggled economically in the late twentieth century; in 2012 it filed for Chapter 9 bankruptcy, at the time one of the largest municipal bankruptcies in United States history, exiting in 2017. In December 2015 the city was the site of a terrorist attack at the Inland Regional Center in which 14 people were killed.

Geography and economy

The city sits in the San Bernardino Valley, crossed by the San Andreas and San Jacinto faults, and is subject to hot, dry summers. Major employers include California State University, San Bernardino, logistics and warehousing firms serving the Inland Empire distribution hub, health care providers, and government. The San Bernardino International Airport occupies the former Norton base. The city hosts the annual Route 66 Rendezvous classic car event and the National Orange Show.
