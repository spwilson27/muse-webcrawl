Linotype (company)

Linotype GmbH
IndustryTypography, type foundry
Founded1890 (as Mergenthaler Linotype Company)
HeadquartersBad Homburg, Germany
ParentMonotype Imaging

Linotype GmbH is a German type foundry and former manufacturer of typesetting equipment, best known for the Linotype machine invented by Ottmar Mergenthaler, which revolutionised printing by allowing an operator to set complete lines of metal type from a keyboard.

The original American company, the Mergenthaler Linotype Company, was founded in 1890 to commercialise Mergenthaler's invention. Linotype machines dominated newspaper and book composition for much of the twentieth century.

Typefaces

Linotype's type library includes some of the most widely used typefaces in the world, among them Helvetica, Univers, Optima, Palatino, Frutiger (later versions), and Avenir. Many were designed under the direction of noted designers such as Hermann Zapf and Adrian Frutiger.

Later history

With the shift to phototypesetting and then digital type, Linotype became a digital type foundry. It was acquired by Monotype Imaging in 2006.
