Automobile Club de Monaco

Founded: 1890 (as Sport Vélocipédique Monégasque); renamed 1925
Headquarters: Monaco
Known for: Monaco Grand Prix, Monte Carlo Rally

The Automobile Club de Monaco (ACM) is the motorsport governing and organising body of the Principality of Monaco. It organises the Monaco Grand Prix, first held in 1929, and the Monte Carlo Rally, first held in 1911, among other events including the Monaco ePrix and historic racing meetings.

History

The club traces its origins to a cycling association founded in 1890. As motoring grew, it became the Sport Automobile et Vélocipédique de Monaco and, in 1925, the Automobile Club de Monaco. Under president Antony Noghès, the club created the Monaco Grand Prix, run on a circuit through the streets of Monte Carlo.

Events

The ACM organises the Formula One Monaco Grand Prix, the Monte Carlo Rally in the World Rally Championship, the Grand Prix de Monaco Historique and the Monaco ePrix in Formula E.
