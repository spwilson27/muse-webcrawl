Dr. Amy Wong, later Amy Wong-Kroker, is a fictional character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by Lauren Tom. She is a ditzy but brilliant engineering graduate student from a fabulously wealthy family on Mars, employed as an intern at Planet Express — partly, it is joked, because she shares Professor Farnsworth's blood type.

Character

Amy is the heiress of the Wong family, who own the western hemisphere of Mars, vast herds of buggalo (beetle-like cattle) and the Mars Vegas casino empire. Despite her party-girl reputation and trademark pink sweat suit, she holds (after fourteen years of graduate study) a PhD in applied physics from Mars University, earning her doctorate in the episode "That Darn Katz!". She is famously clumsy, sprinkles her speech with 31st-century slang and Cantonese-derived curses, and briefly dated Fry before forming a long-term relationship with the Amphibiosan lieutenant Kif Kroker, with whom she becomes a "fonfon ru" (Amphibiosan life-partner) and, in the revived series, a mother to their children.

Development

Series creator Matt Groening and the writers designed Amy as a parody of the rich, spoiled heiress archetype whose scientific competence subverts the stereotype. Lauren Tom's performance and improvised Cantonese exclamations became recurring jokes. Amy's character develops across the series' Fox, Comedy Central and Hulu runs from comic relief toward a more rounded role as Planet Express's most emotionally perceptive member.
