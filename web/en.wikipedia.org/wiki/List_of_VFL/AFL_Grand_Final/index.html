List of VFL/AFL Grand Finals

This is a list of VFL/AFL Grand Finals, the championship matches of the Victorian Football League (VFL) and its successor, the Australian Football League (AFL), the premier competition in Australian rules football. The Grand Final has been played annually since 1898 (with the exception of 1924, when a round-robin format determined the premier), almost always at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.

History

The inaugural Grand Final in 1898 saw Essendon defeat Melbourne. The match became the centrepiece of the Victorian sporting calendar, routinely drawing crowds above 90,000. Notable matches include the drawn Grand Finals of 1948, 1977, and 2010 — each replayed the following week under the rules then in force — and the 1970 Grand Final, famous for Carlton's comeback and Alex Jesaulenko's celebrated mark. Since 1990 the competition has been known as the AFL, and interstate clubs including West Coast, Adelaide, Sydney, Brisbane, and Port Adelaide have won premierships.
