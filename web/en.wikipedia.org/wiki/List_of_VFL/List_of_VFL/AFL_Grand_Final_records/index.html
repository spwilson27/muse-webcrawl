List of VFL/AFL Grand Final records

This page lists records from VFL/AFL Grand Finals, the championship matches of the Victorian Football League and Australian Football League since 1898, including attendance, scoring, and individual milestones.

Attendance records

The largest Grand Final crowd was 121,696 for the 1970 Grand Final between Carlton and Collingwood at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. Following redevelopment of the MCG, modern crowds are capped near 100,000; the 2010 drawn Grand Final drew 100,016. The smallest crowds came in the wartime and early years, when matches were staged at smaller venues.

Scoring records

The highest winning score in a Grand Final is 28.9 (177) by Hawthorn in 1988; Geelong's 24.19 (163) in 2007 produced the largest winning margin, 119 points. The highest losing score is Geelong's 21.12 (138) in 1989. The lowest winning score was Essendon's 3.6 (24) in the wet 1898 final era conditions, and Melbourne's 4.10 (34) in 1960 remains among the lowest of the modern game.

Individual records

Gary Ablett Sr. kicked nine goals in a losing side in 1989, sharing the record for goals in a Grand Final with Gordon Coventry (1928). Michael Long, Andrew McLeod (twice), Luke Hodge, and Dustin Martin (three times) are among the Norm Smith Medal winners for best on ground; Martin is the only player to win three Norm Smith Medals, each alongside a premiership and, in those seasons, the Brownlow or finals honours.

Team records

Carlton, Collingwood, and Essendon share the record of 16 premierships. Collingwood has appeared in the most Grand Finals and has also lost the most, earning the rueful nickname "the Collywobbles" during a long drought between 1958 and 1990. Melbourne's 2021 premiership ended a 57-year drought, the longest in league history broken by a flag.
