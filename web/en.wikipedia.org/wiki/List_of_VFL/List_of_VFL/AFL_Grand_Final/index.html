List of VFL/AFL Grand Finals

The AFL Grand Final is the championship match of the Australian Football League, played annually at the Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG), traditionally on the last Saturday in September. It determines the premiers of the season. The competition was known as the Victorian Football League (VFL) until 1989, and every grand final since 1898 is listed here, together with the winning club, scores, crowd, and the recipient of the Norm Smith Medal (awarded since 1979 to the best player on the ground).

Facts

Drawn grand finals occurred in 1948, 1977, and 2010 and were settled by full replays the following week; since 2016 the rules provide for extra time instead. Collingwood has appeared in more grand finals than any other club, while Carlton and Essendon lead the premiership tally with 16 each. The premiership cup and the "Norm Smith Medal" ceremony are central traditions of the day.
