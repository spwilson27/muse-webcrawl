List of VFL/AFL premiers

This is a list of premiers of the Australian Football League (AFL), known as the Victorian Football League (VFL) until 1990. The premiership is decided by the winner of the AFL Grand Final, played each year at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.

Essendon won the first VFL premiership in 1897 under a round-robin finals format; the first Grand Final was played in 1898, won by Fitzroy. Collingwood remains the only club to win four consecutive premierships (1927–1930).
