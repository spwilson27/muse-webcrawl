List of VFL: Australian rules football

This page serves as a navigational list for articles relating to the Victorian Football League (VFL) and Australian rules football more broadly, reached via the nested path "List of VFL / Australian rules football". Australian rules football, commonly called "footy", was codified in Melbourne in 1859 and became the dominant football code in Victoria and several other Australian states.

The VFL and its successors

The Victorian Football League was founded in 1897 by eight breakaway clubs of the Victorian Football Association. In 1990 the competition was renamed the Australian Football League (AFL) to reflect its national expansion, with clubs in Western Australia, South Australia, New South Wales, Queensland, and Tasmania (from 2028). The name "VFL" survives as a second-tier Victorian competition, now a state league aligned with AFL reserve teams.

Clubs

The original 1897 clubs were Carlton, Collingwood, Essendon, Fitzroy, Geelong, Melbourne, St Kilda, and South Melbourne. Later additions included Richmond, Footscray (Western Bulldogs), Hawthorn, and North Melbourne; expansion-era clubs include the West Coast Eagles, Adelaide Crows, Fremantle Dockers, Port Adelaide, Brisbane Lions, Sydney Swans, Gold Coast Suns, and Greater Western Sydney Giants.
