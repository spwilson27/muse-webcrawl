Jutland Code

The Jutland Code (Danish: Jyske Lov) is a Danish provincial law code promulgated by King Valdemar II in 1241 for the province of Jutland, including Southern Schleswig, Funen and nearby islands. One of the earliest European law codes issued in the vernacular, it remained in force in parts of Schleswig until 1900.

Contents

The code's famous preface declares that "with law shall the land be built" (mæth logh scal land byggæs). Its three books regulate inheritance, property, agriculture, theft, violence and legal procedure, prescribing ordeals, oaths and fines. Together with the Scanian Law and Zealand codes it forms the corpus of medieval Danish provincial laws, later largely superseded by Christian V's Danish Code of 1683.
