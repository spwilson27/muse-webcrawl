Dongcheng District, Beijing

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Dongcheng District (simplified Chinese: 东城区; traditional Chinese: 東城區; pinyin: Dōngchéng Qū, "East City District") is a district of Beijing covering the eastern half of the old city. It has an area of about 41.8 square kilometres and a population of roughly 700,000, and it contains many of the capital's most famous landmarks.

Landmarks

Dongcheng includes the eastern side of Tiananmen Square, the Forbidden City and Jingshan Park, the Temple of Heaven, the Yonghe (Lama) Temple, the Temple of Confucius and Imperial Academy, the Wangfujing shopping street, Beijing railway station, and extensive surviving hutong neighbourhoods such as Nanluoguxiang. The former崇文区 (Chongwen District) was merged into Dongcheng in 2010, bringing the Temple of Heaven and Longtan Park into the district.

Administration

The district is divided into 17 subdistricts, including Donghuamen, Jingshan, Jiaodaokou, Andingmenwai, and Tiantan. It hosts numerous national government and cultural institutions.
