Interpublic Group of Companies

Type: Public (NYSE: IPG)
Industry: Advertising, marketing
Founded: 1930 (as McCann-Erickson)
Headquarters: New York City, United States

The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. (IPG) is an American publicly traded advertising company. It is one of the "Big Four" global advertising holding companies, alongside WPP, Omnicom, and Publicis Groupe. The company consists of dozens of agencies across hundreds of offices worldwide, providing services in advertising, public relations, media planning and buying, and marketing.

History

IPG's roots lie in the McCann-Erickson agency, formed in 1930 by the merger of the H.K. McCann Company and Erickson. The holding-company structure dates to 1960, when McCann-Erickson reorganised as Interpublic, widely regarded as the first marketing services holding company. IPG expanded through major acquisitions, including the Lowe Group, the Allied Communications Group, and the 2001 acquisition of True North Communications, which brought Foote Cone & Belding.

Agencies

Major IPG networks and agencies have included McCann Worldgroup, FCB (Foote Cone & Belding), MullenLowe, IPG Mediabrands (which includes UM and Initiative), and public relations firms such as Weber Shandwick and Golin.

Merger

In November 2024, Omnicom announced an agreement to acquire Interpublic in an all-stock transaction, which would create the world's largest advertising holding company; the deal moved through regulatory review during 2025.
