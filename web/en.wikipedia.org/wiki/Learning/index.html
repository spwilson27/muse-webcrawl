Learning

Learning
DefinitionAcquisition of knowledge or skills through experience, study or teaching
MechanismsConditioning, observation, insight, instruction
[Image: A child learning to read with a teacher pointing at a picture book]

Learning is the process of acquiring new understanding, knowledge, behaviours and skills. It occurs in humans and other animals through experience — classical and operant conditioning (Pavlov, Skinner), imitation and social learning (Bandura), and formal instruction in schools.

Science of learning

Research shows spaced practice, retrieval practice, feedback and sleep improve retention, while cramming fades fast. Machine learning borrows the term for algorithms that improve with data — cousins of the Bayesian updaters in this crawl. Learning differences (dyslexia, ADHD) and lifelong learning are active educational concerns.
