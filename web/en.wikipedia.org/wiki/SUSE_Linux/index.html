SUSE Linux
Developer: SUSE S.A. / openSUSE community
Initial release: 1994
Products: SUSE Linux Enterprise, openSUSE (Tumbleweed, Leap)
Mascot: Geeko the chameleon

SUSE Linux is a family of Linux distributions built on the Linux kernel and developed by the German company SUSE and the openSUSE community. SUSE was founded in 1992 in Nuremberg — originally as Gesellschaft für Software- und Systementwicklung ("S.u.S.E.") — making it one of the oldest commercial Linux companies; its first distribution appeared in 1994 as a German edition of Slackware before becoming an independent distribution with the YaST configuration tool.

Editions

The flagship commercial product is SUSE Linux Enterprise (Server and Desktop), known for long support cycles, mainframe and high-availability support, and the Btrfs-based transactional update features. The community distribution openSUSE is offered as Tumbleweed (rolling release) and Leap (aligned with the enterprise codebase). SUSE also acquired Rancher Labs in 2020, expanding into Kubernetes management.

Ownership

SUSE passed through Novell (2003) and Attachmate/Micro Focus ownership before becoming an independent company under EQT in 2019; it listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in 2021.
