Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows

Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows (D.R.U.G.S.)
Origin: Pontiac, Michigan, US
Years active: 2010–2012, 2020–present
Genres: Post-hardcore, alternative rock
Labels: Warner Bros., Sire, Velocity, Equal Vision

Destroy Rebuild Until God Shows (abbreviated D.R.U.G.S.) is an American post-hardcore supergroup formed in 2010. The band was assembled by Craig Owens, formerly of Chiodos, together with Aaron Stern and Matt Good (From First to Last), Nick Martin (Sleeping with Sirens, Underminded), and Adam Russell (Story of the Year). Their self-titled debut album was released in February 2011 and reached the top 30 of the Billboard 200.

History

The band was announced in 2010 after Owens's departure from Chiodos, and the group quickly signed to Warner Bros./Sire. The debut album, produced by John Feldmann, yielded the singles "Sex Life", "If You Think This Song Is About You, It Probably Is", and "My Swagger Has a First Name". Following extensive touring, internal tensions and Owens's return to Chiodos led to the group's dissolution in 2012.

In 2020, Owens revived D.R.U.G.S. as his principal project with a new line-up, releasing the single "King I Am". The second album, Destroy Rebuild, was released in June 2022 through Velocity Records, preceded by singles including "Satellites in Motion" and "Gold". The revived project toured with acts from the 2000s post-hardcore scene.
