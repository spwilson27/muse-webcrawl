Braselton, Georgia

Braselton is a town in Barrow, Gwinnett, Hall, and Jackson counties in the U.S. state of Georgia, United States. It is part of the Atlanta–Sandy Springs–Roswell metropolitan statistical area. The town grew significantly with suburban and warehouse development along Interstate 85 and is home to the Château Élan winery and resort.

History

The town is named for the Braselton family, whose members settled in the area in the late 19th century; John Oliver Braselton built a store around which the community formed. Braselton was incorporated in 1916. For much of the 20th century it remained a small agricultural crossroads. In 1989 the actress Kim Basinger and other investors purchased much of the town's land with plans to develop it as a tourist attraction and film studio, but financial difficulties ended the venture by 1995 — an episode that attracted national media attention.

Geography and growth

Braselton lies in northeastern Georgia along Interstate 85, northeast of Atlanta. Unusually, the town's limits extend across four counties. Since the 1990s it has experienced rapid growth driven by distribution centers, the Château Élan resort and winery, and nearby Road Atlanta, a well-known road-racing circuit in Hall County. Northeast Georgia Medical Center Braselton opened in 2015 as the first new hospital in Georgia in nearly twenty years.

Demographics

The town's population was a few hundred in the mid-20th century and roughly 1,200 at the 2000 census; it grew to about 7,500 by 2010 and more than 13,000 by the 2020 census, reflecting its transformation into a fast-growing exurban community.
