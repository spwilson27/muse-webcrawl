Bell Media

Bell Media Inc. is a Canadian media company and the mass media subsidiary of BCE Inc., operating television, radio, digital media and out-of-home advertising assets across Canada. It is one of the country's largest media conglomerates.

History

Bell Media was created in 2011 after Bell Canada acquired CTVglobemedia, whose assets included the CTV television network. In 2013 the company acquired Astral Media's television and radio properties, significantly expanding its portfolio of specialty channels and radio stations, including French-language services based in Montreal.

Assets

The company's holdings include the CTV and CTV 2 television networks, specialty channels such as TSN, RDS, Much, CP24 and BNN Bloomberg, the Crave streaming service (which absorbed The Movie Network and HBO Canada output deals), dozens of radio stations under iHeartRadio Canada branding, and Astral's outdoor advertising network.

Operations

Bell Media produces Canadian programming including national newscasts through CTV News, sports coverage through TSN and RDS, and entertainment programming for broadcast and streaming. The company is headquartered at 299 Queen Street West in Toronto, the historic home of Much and CP24.
