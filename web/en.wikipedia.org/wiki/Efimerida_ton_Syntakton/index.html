Efimerida ton Syntakton (Greek: Εφημερίδα των Συντακτών, "Newspaper of the Editors") is a Greek daily newspaper published in Athens. It is notable for being run as a workers' cooperative, founded by journalists who had lost their jobs during the Greek debt crisis, many of them formerly employed by the newspaper Eleftherotypia.

History

The paper was launched in November 2012, at the height of the Greek financial crisis, by a group of unemployed journalists. Rather than being owned by a media proprietor, it was structured as a cooperative in which the journalists themselves are shareholders and decisions are taken collectively. The first issue appeared on 5 November 2012.

Editorial stance

The newspaper is generally described as left-wing and independent, critical of austerity policies and of the traditional Greek media establishment. It publishes political reporting, investigative journalism, and cultural coverage, and also maintains a website with continuous news updates.

Significance

Efimerida ton Syntakton is frequently cited as an example of cooperative and alternative media in Europe, demonstrating a model of journalist-owned publishing that emerged in response to mass layoffs and newspaper closures during the crisis.
