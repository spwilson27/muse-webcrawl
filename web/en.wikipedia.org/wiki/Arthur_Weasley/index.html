Arthur Weasley

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Mark Williams as Arthur Weasley

Arthur Weasley is a fictional character in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. He is the patriarch of the Weasley family and works at the Ministry of Magic, initially in the Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office. He is married to Molly Weasley, with whom he has seven children, including Ron Weasley, Harry Potter's best friend. Arthur is portrayed by Mark Williams in the film adaptations.

Character

Arthur is characterised by his fascination with Muggles (non-magical people) and their technology — including plugs, batteries, and escalators — which he collects and tinkers with, sometimes in defiance of the very regulations he enforces. He authored the Muggle Protection Act, which made him unpopular with pure-blood supremacist families such as the Malfoys, and he has a longstanding feud with Lucius Malfoy.

During the Second Wizarding War, Arthur is a member of the Order of the Phoenix. In Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix he is attacked by Voldemort's snake Nagini while on guard duty, an attack Harry witnesses through his connection with Voldemort; Arthur is rescued and treated at St Mungo's Hospital. He is later promoted to head the Office for the Detection and Confiscation of Counterfeit Defensive Spells and Protective Objects.

Family

The Weasleys live at The Burrow near Ottery St Catchpole in Devon. Arthur and Molly's children are Bill, Charlie, Percy, Fred, George, Ron, and Ginny. J. K. Rowling has said she originally planned to kill Arthur in the fifth book but changed her mind, sparing him because she considered him one of the few good fathers in the series.
