American Dream is a large retail and entertainment complex in the Meadowlands Sports Complex in East Rutherford, New Jersey, United States. Developed by the Triple Five Group, it opened on October 25, 2019, after a long and troubled development history stretching back to 2003 under successive developers and the earlier name Xanadu. Roughly half of its space is devoted to entertainment attractions rather than conventional retail.

Attractions

The complex contains the Nickelodeon Universe indoor theme park, the largest indoor theme park in the Western Hemisphere; DreamWorks Water Park, a large indoor water park; Big Snow, an indoor ski and snowboard slope; an NHL-regulation ice rink; a Legoland Discovery Center and Sea Life aquarium; mini-golf, mirror maze, and observation wheel attractions; and a performing arts and concert venue. Its retail component includes flagship stores and a luxury wing anchored by Saks Fifth Avenue.

Development history

The project was originally proposed in 2002–2003 as Meadowlands Xanadu by the Mills Corporation. Construction began in 2004 but stalled repeatedly amid financing problems, the 2008 financial crisis, and the bankruptcy of a lender. Colony Capital took over in 2006, and Triple Five, the developer of the Mall of America and West Edmonton Mall, assumed control in 2011 and renamed the project American Dream. A $2.8 billion financing package completed in 2017 allowed construction to finish, and attractions opened in phases beginning in October 2019. The retail portion opened in October 2020, delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic.
