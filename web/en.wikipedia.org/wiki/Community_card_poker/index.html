Community card poker

Community card poker refers to any game of poker that uses community cards (also called "shared cards" or "window cards"), which are cards dealt face up in the center of the table and shared by all players. Each player is also dealt an incomplete hand of private ("hole") cards, which they combine with the community cards to make their best possible five-card hand.

Games

The most popular community card games today are Texas hold 'em, originating in the 1920s, and Omaha hold 'em, dating from the 1980s. Older games include Manila and Pinatubo.

Texas hold 'em

In Texas hold 'em, each player receives two private cards. Five community cards are dealt in three stages: the flop (three cards), the turn (one card), and the river (one card), with betting rounds between each stage.

Omaha

In Omaha, each player is dealt four hole cards and must use exactly two of them together with exactly three of the five community cards.
