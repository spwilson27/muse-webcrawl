Cannaregio

Cannaregio is the northernmost of the six historic sestieri (districts) of Venice, Italy. It is the second largest sestiere by land and the most populous, home to the Jewish Ghetto and the Cannaregio Canal.

City: Venice, Italy
Type: Sestiere

Sights

Notable sites include the churches of Madonna dell'Orto and Santa Maria dei Miracoli, the Ca' d'Oro palace nearby in the district's orbit, and the Venetian Ghetto established in 1516.

Transport

The district contains Venezia Santa Lucia railway station, Venice's main station, facing the Grand Canal.
