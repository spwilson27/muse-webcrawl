Vernon Estes (born January 4, 1930) is an American businessman and hobbyist who founded Estes Industries, the company that made model rocketry a popular and safe hobby in the United States. He is regarded as one of the founding figures of model rocketry.

Vernon Estes, founder of Estes Industries.

Career

Estes became interested in rocketry as a young man. In the late 1950s, model rocket enthusiasts such as G. Harry Stine needed a reliable supply of safe, ready-made miniature rocket motors. Estes, whose family ran a construction business in Denver, Colorado, designed and built a machine — nicknamed "Mabel" — that could mass-produce model rocket motors quickly and safely. In 1958 he founded Estes Industries in Denver, later moving the company to Penrose, Colorado.

The company's mail-order catalogues and inexpensive kits — including famous models such as the Astron Alpha — brought model rocketry to hundreds of thousands of young people. Estes also helped establish the safety standards that allowed the hobby to flourish and supported the National Association of Rocketry.

Legacy

Estes sold the company in 1969, but his name remains on the Estes brand, still the best-known name in model rocketry. The Vern Estes name is commemorated in the hobby community, and the Estes company continues to operate from Penrose, Colorado.
