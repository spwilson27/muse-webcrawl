Covilhã is a city and a municipality in the Centro region of Portugal, in the district of Castelo Branco. It lies on the southeastern slopes of the Serra da Estrela, the highest mountain range in mainland Portugal, and is sometimes called the "gateway to the Serra da Estrela".

History

Covilhã grew as a centre of the Portuguese woollen textile industry, with factories powered by the streams descending from the mountains. Its high altitude — it is one of the few Portuguese cities where snow is common in winter — shaped its development as both an industrial town and a mountain resort base.

Economy and education

The city is home to the University of Beira Interior, founded in 1979, which occupies former textile factory buildings. Tourism related to the Serra da Estrela ski resort and the Penhas da Saúde area is an important part of the local economy.
