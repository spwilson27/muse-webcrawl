Momofuku Ando

Born5 March 1910, Puzi, Japanese Taiwan
Died5 January 2007 (aged 96), Ikeda, Osaka, Japan
Known forInventing instant noodles; founding Nissin Foods

Momofuku Ando (Japanese: 安藤 百福, 5 March 1910 – 5 January 2007) was a Japanese inventor and businessman who founded Nissin Food Products Co., Ltd. He is widely known as the inventor of instant noodles and the creator of the brands Top Ramen and Cup Noodles.

Early life

Ando was born Go Peh-hok in 1910 to a wealthy family in Kagi (present-day Chiayi), Japanese-occupied Taiwan, and raised by his grandparents in Tainan. At 22 he started a clothing company with money inherited from his family, and after the Second World War he became a Japanese citizen and moved to Japan, where he ran several businesses and served briefly in prison for tax evasion in connection with a scholarship fund.

Invention of instant noodles

Japan was suffering food shortages after the war, and the Ministry of Health encouraged people to eat bread made from American wheat-flour aid. Ando wondered why bread was recommended instead of noodles, a staple more familiar to Japanese people. In 1958, after a year of experimentation in a small shed behind his house in Ikeda, Osaka, he perfected "flash-frying" noodles in tempura oil, which dehydrated them and allowed long storage; they could be prepared simply by adding hot water. On 25 August 1958 he launched Chicken Ramen, the world's first instant noodle product, through his company Nissin.

In 1971, after observing American buyers breaking ramen blocks into cups at a supermarket, he introduced Cup Noodles, served in a waterproof polystyrene cup to which only hot water needed to be added. In 2005, shortly before his death, Nissin developed "Space Ram", a vacuum-packed instant noodle eaten aboard the Space Shuttle Discovery.

Legacy

Instant noodles became a global staple; worldwide consumption now exceeds 100 billion servings a year. The Momofuku Ando Instant Ramen Museum (CUPNOODLES Museum) in Ikeda commemorates his work. In a Japanese poll he was voted the country's most significant inventor of the 20th century.
