List of national liquors

A national liquor is an alcoholic beverage — usually a distilled spirit — strongly identified with a particular country or culture. Some are legally protected designations (such as Scotch whisky or tequila), some are officially designated national drinks, and many are simply traditional favourites. The term "liquor" here encompasses spirits such as vodka, whisky, ouzo, rakı and pisco.
