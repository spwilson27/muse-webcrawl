Chalus, Iran

Chalus (Persian: چالوس) is a city in Mazandaran Province, Iran, on the Caspian Sea coast, capital of Chalus County. It is a popular tourist destination for Iranians, known for its humid climate, forests and the Chalus Road to Tehran through the Alborz mountains.

Geography

The city lies near Namak Ab Rud and the Sardab River. The historic Chalus Road (Road 59), built in the 1930s, is famed for its tunnels and scenery, now supplemented by the Tehran–Shomal Freeway.
