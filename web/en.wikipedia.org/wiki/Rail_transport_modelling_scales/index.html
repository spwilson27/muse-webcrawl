Rail transport modelling scales

Rail transport modelling scales are standardised ratios between a model railway and the real thing, each generally associated with a particular track gauge. Because the same names are used with slightly different ratios in different countries — Britain's OO uses a different scale from continental HO despite sharing track gauge — the standards bodies NMRA (North America) and MOROP/NEM (Europe) maintain the definitions.

Terminology

Strictly, "scale" is the proportion of the model, while "gauge" is the distance between the rails; narrow-gauge prototypes are modelled with suffixes such as HOn3 (HO scale, narrow gauge) or On30. Confusion is common because hobbyists often refer to the combination by the scale letter alone.

Popularity

HO is the most popular scale worldwide, followed by N, which allows large layouts in small spaces. OO dominates in Britain. Larger scales such as G are popular for garden railways.
