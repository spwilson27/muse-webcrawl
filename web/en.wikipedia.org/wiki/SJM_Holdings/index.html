SJM Holdings Limited (Chinese: 澳門博彩控股有限公司) is a Hong Kong–Macau gambling and hospitality company listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. It is the holding company of Sociedade de Jogos de Macau, S.A. (SJM), one of the six companies authorised to operate casinos in Macau.

History

SJM's roots lie in Sociedade de Turismo e Diversões de Macau (STDM), founded by Stanley Ho and partners, which held the monopoly concession for casino gaming in Macau from 1962 until the market was liberalised in 2002. SJM Holdings was listed in Hong Kong in 2008. The group operates flagship properties including the Grand Lisboa and Casino Lisboa on the Macau Peninsula, as well as satellite casinos operating under its licence.

In 2021 SJM opened the Grand Lisboa Palace, its first integrated resort in Cotai, featuring hotel towers under brands including Palazzo Versace and Karl Lagerfeld. Under Macau's new gaming law, SJM was awarded one of the six ten-year gaming concessions that began on 1 January 2023.
