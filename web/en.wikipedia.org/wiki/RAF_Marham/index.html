Royal Air Force Marham, commonly abbreviated RAF Marham, is a Royal Air Force station and military airbase near the village of Marham in Norfolk, England. It is the home of the United Kingdom's F-35B Lightning force, operated jointly by the RAF and the Royal Navy's Fleet Air Arm, and previously hosted the Tornado GR fleet for nearly four decades.

History

The airfield opened in 1916 as a Royal Flying Corps night landing ground. In the Second World War it hosted Wellington, Stirling and Mosquito bombers. During the Cold War it operated Canberra and Valiant aircraft of the V bomber force, and later Victor tankers. From 1982 until 2019 Marham was the principal base of the Panavia Tornado strike aircraft.

Current use

The station underwent major infrastructure work ("Project Anvil") to receive the F-35B Lightning, the first of which arrived in June 2018. No. 617 Squadron RAF — the "Dambusters" — re-formed at Marham as the first operational British F-35 squadron, joined by 207 Squadron as the operational conversion unit, and aircraft from Marham deploy aboard the Royal Navy's Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carriers.
