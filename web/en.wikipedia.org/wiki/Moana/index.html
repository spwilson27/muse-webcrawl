Moana

Moana (released as Vaiana in some European territories) is a 2016 American animated musical adventure film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios. Directed by John Musker and Ron Clements, it tells the story of Moana, the strong-willed daughter of a Polynesian village chief, who is chosen by the ocean itself to reunite a mystical relic — the heart of the goddess Te Fiti — with its owner, and who sails across the Pacific with the legendary demigod Maui.

Plot

On the island of Motunui, the inhabitants worship the goddess Te Fiti, who brought life to the ocean using a pounamu stone as her heart. A thousand years before the story begins, the demigod Maui stole Te Fiti's heart, unleashing a darkness that spreads from island to island. Moana, daughter of Chief Tui, is drawn to the ocean despite her father's rule that no one sail beyond the reef. When blight strikes Motunui's crops and fish, Moana sets out to find Maui and compel him to return the heart. After adventures involving the coconut-armoured Kakamora pirates and the giant crab Tamatoa, and a confrontation with the lava demon Te Kā, Moana realises that Te Kā is Te Fiti herself, corrupted by the loss of her heart. She restores the heart, Te Fiti is healed, and Moana returns home to lead her people as voyagers once more.

Music

The soundtrack combines traditional South Pacific sounds with contemporary songwriting. Songs include "How Far I'll Go," Moana's signature number, performed in the film by Cravalho and in a pop version by Alessia Cara; Maui's showpiece "You're Welcome," performed by Johnson; and "We Know the Way," celebrating Polynesian voyaging. "How Far I'll Go" was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Original Song.

Reception

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing over $690 million worldwide. Reviewers praised its animation, music, and its respectful engagement with Polynesian culture, developed in consultation with the Oceanic Story Trust, a group of Pacific Islander cultural advisors. It received Academy Award nominations for Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song. A sequel, Moana 2, was released in 2024, and a live-action remake followed.
