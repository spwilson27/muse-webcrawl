Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality

The Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality (Turkish: İstanbul Büyükşehir Belediyesi, İBB) is the municipal government of Istanbul Province in Turkey, responsible for city-wide services across the metropolis of more than 16 million residents.

History and structure

Metropolitan municipalities were established in Turkish law in 1984; Istanbul's first metropolitan mayor under the system was Bedrettin Dalan. The municipality is headed by a directly elected mayor and a municipal council, and oversees transport (through İETT and Metro Istanbul), water (İSKİ), and numerous municipal companies. Its historic headquarters is in Saraçhane, Fatih.

Recent mayors

Ekrem İmamoğlu of the Republican People's Party won the mayoralty in 2019 — in an initial election annulled and re-run, which he won by a larger margin — and was re-elected in 2024.
