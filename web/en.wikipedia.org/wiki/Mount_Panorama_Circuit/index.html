Mount Panorama Circuit

Mount Panorama Circuit is a motor racing track located in Bathurst, New South Wales, Australia. Built on public roads around Mount Panorama (Wahluu), the 6.213 km (3.861 mi) circuit is famous for the Bathurst 1000 touring car race, held there each October since 1963, and is regarded as one of the world's great racing circuits.

Layout

The circuit has an unusual configuration: a steep mountain section with gradients of up to 1 in 6 and an elevation difference of 174 metres, connected by the long, fast Conrod Straight and the climbing Mountain Straight. Famous corners and features include Hell Corner, The Cutting, Sulman Park, Skyline, the Esses, the Dipper, Forrest's Elbow, and The Chase, a kink added in 1987 to slow cars before Murray's Corner.

Events

The circuit's main event is the Bathurst 1000 for Supercars; it also hosts the Bathurst 12 Hour endurance race for GT cars, the Bathurst 6 Hour, and motorcycle and Easter meetings historically. When not used for racing, the circuit is a two-way public road with a 60 km/h speed limit, with houses along its length.

Records

The outright lap record was set informally by Jenson Button in a McLaren Formula One car during a 2011 promotional run. Race lap records in Supercars trim are around the 2:03–2:04 mark.
