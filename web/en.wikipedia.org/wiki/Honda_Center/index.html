Honda Center

The Honda Center is an indoor arena located in Anaheim, California, United States. It is the home of the Anaheim Ducks of the National Hockey League (NHL). The arena opened on June 19, 1993, at a construction cost of $123 million, initially named the Pond of Anaheim and sponsored as the Arrowhead Pond of Anaheim from 1993 to 2006.

History

The arena was built as the home of the expansion Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, owned by the Walt Disney Company. It hosted the 2007 Stanley Cup Finals, in which the Ducks defeated the Ottawa Senators to win California's first Stanley Cup. Honda purchased the naming rights in July 2006 under a 15-year agreement that has since been extended.

Events

Beyond hockey, Honda Center seats about 18,336 for basketball and 19,000 for concerts. It has hosted UFC events, the NCAA basketball tournament, the 2005 World Badminton Championships, and numerous concerts and professional wrestling shows. It was also the home of the Los Angeles Kiss of the Arena Football League.

Location

The arena stands in Anaheim's Platinum Triangle district, across Katella Avenue from Angel Stadium and near the Anaheim Regional Transportation Intermodal Center (ARTIC).
