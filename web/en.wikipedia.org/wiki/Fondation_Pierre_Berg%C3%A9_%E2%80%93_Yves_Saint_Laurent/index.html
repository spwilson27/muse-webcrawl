Fondation Pierre Bergé – Yves Saint Laurent

Established2002 (recognized as a public-interest foundation, 2002)
Location5 avenue Marceau, Paris, France
FoundersYves Saint Laurent, Pierre Bergé
PurposePreservation of the Yves Saint Laurent heritage; support of cultural and educational causes

The Fondation Pierre Bergé – Yves Saint Laurent is a French foundation based in Paris that preserves and promotes the work of the couturier Yves Saint Laurent and the legacy of his partner Pierre Bergé. It was created after the closure of the Yves Saint Laurent haute couture house in 2002 and was recognized as being of public utility that same year.

Collections and activities

The foundation conserves an exceptional archive of Saint Laurent's work: some 35,000 garments, accessories, sketches, and objects spanning four decades of haute couture, along with the designer's personal collections. It organizes exhibitions at its Paris headquarters at 5 avenue Marceau — the former couture house — which in 2017 became the Musée Yves Saint Laurent Paris. A companion museum opened in Marrakech, Morocco, the same year.

Philanthropy

Following Pierre Bergé's wishes, the foundation also supports research against AIDS, contemporary art, and artistic education, and administers the Jardin Majorelle in Marrakech through related entities. See also Yves Saint Laurent and Pierre Bergé.
