Buckley Space Force Base

Buckley Space Force Base
Location: Aurora, Colorado
Operator: United States Space Force
Known for: large radomes ("golf balls") of its missile-warning mission

Buckley Space Force Base is a United States Space Force base in Aurora, Colorado. Its distinctive white radomes house antennas for missile warning and space surveillance. The host unit is Space Base Delta 2.

Mission

The base supports the Missile Defense Agency and Space Force missions including strategic missile warning using the Defense Support Program and Space-Based Infrared System (SBIRS) satellites, whose data are received and processed on site. It is home to the 460th Space Wing's successor organizations, the Aerospace Data Facility-Colorado, and Colorado Air National Guard units including the 140th Wing, which flies F-16 fighters.

History

The site began as an Army Air Forces bombing range and gunnery school during the Second World War and was named for First Lieutenant John Harold Buckley, a Colorado flier killed in the First World War. It became an Air Force base in 2000 after decades as an Air National Guard installation, and was redesignated a Space Force base in 2021.
