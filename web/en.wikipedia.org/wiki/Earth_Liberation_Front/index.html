Earth Liberation Front

Earth Liberation Front (ELF)
Founded: 1992, Brighton, England
Type: Autonomous activist cells
Ideology: Radical environmentalism, green anarchism
Methods: Ecotage, arson, property destruction

The Earth Liberation Front (ELF) is the collective name for autonomous individuals and covert cells that use direct action—typically economic sabotage ("ecotage") against property—to protest environmental destruction. Founded in Brighton, England, in 1992 by members of Earth First! who rejected that movement's turn away from illegal tactics, the ELF spread to the United States and other countries.

The ELF emerged from the radical wing of the Earth First! movement in the early 1990s.

Structure and philosophy

The ELF has no central leadership, membership or headquarters; any individual acting under its published guidelines—chiefly to cause economic damage to those profiting from environmental destruction while taking all precautions not to harm life—may claim the name. This "leaderless resistance" model mirrors that of the Animal Liberation Front, with which ELF cells have often cooperated.

Actions

Actions attributed to the ELF in the United States peaked in the late 1990s and early 2000s and included arson attacks on a ski resort expansion at Vail, Colorado (1998), sport-utility vehicle dealerships, logging companies, a university horticulture centre and suburban housing developments. The FBI classified the ELF among the most serious domestic terrorism threats, and the crackdown known as Operation Backfire (2005–06) led to the prosecution of a network of American activists dubbed "The Family". No ELF action is known to have caused a human death or injury.
