Kia EV6

Manufacturer: Kia
Production: 2021–present
Class: Compact crossover SUV (electric)
Platform: Hyundai-Kia E-GMP

The Kia EV6 is a battery electric compact crossover SUV produced by Kia since 2021. It was Kia's first dedicated electric vehicle and the first model built on the Electric Global Modular Platform (E-GMP), shared with the Hyundai Ioniq 5.

Overview

The EV6 offers battery options around 58 and 77.4 kWh, with ranges up to roughly 500 km (WLTP) and an 800-volt architecture allowing DC fast charging from 10 to 80 percent in about 18 minutes. Rear-wheel and all-wheel drive versions are offered, topped by the high-performance EV6 GT with 577 PS.

Awards

The EV6 won the 2022 European Car of the Year award and numerous other design and EV honours, alongside its platform sibling the Ioniq 5.
