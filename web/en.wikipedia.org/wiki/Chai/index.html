Chai

Chai is the word for tea in many languages, derived from Chinese cha via Persian chay. In English, "chai" often refers specifically to masala chai, spiced tea originating in India, prepared by brewing black tea with aromatic spices, milk and sweetener.

Masala chai

Traditional ingredients include Assam black tea, ginger, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, black pepper, milk and sugar or jaggery. The mixture is simmered to extract flavour. It is served ubiquitously at tea stalls (chai wallahs) across India, Pakistan and Bangladesh.

Global spread

Chai lattes became popular in Western coffee shops from the 1990s. Variations use different spice blends and milks.
