Stockholm Municipality

Country: Sweden

County: Stockholm County

Seat: Stockholm

Population: ~1,000,000 (largest municipality in Sweden)

Stockholm Municipality or the City of Stockholm (Swedish: Stockholms kommun or Stockholms stad) is a municipality in Stockholm County in east central Sweden. Its seat is located in the city of Stockholm, which is also the capital of Sweden. With a population of roughly one million, it is the most populous of Sweden's 290 municipalities.

Geography and administration

The municipality contains the central districts of Stockholm, including Norrmalm, Södermalm, and Östermalm, as well as the suburban districts of the south and west, such as Farsta, Skärholmen, Hässelby-Vällingby, and Spånga-Tensta. The municipality is governed by the Stockholm City Council (kommunfullmäktige), elected every four years, and the City Executive Board. Stockholm City Hall, completed in 1923, is one of Sweden's best-known buildings and the venue of the annual Nobel Prize banquet.

History

The city of Stockholm was founded around 1252. The modern municipality took shape through incorporations of neighbouring parishes in the early 20th century, notably Brännkyrka in 1913 and Bromma in 1916, and through the municipal reforms of 1971, when the term "city" was officially abolished in favour of "municipality", though Stockholm retains the name Stockholms stad (City of Stockholm) for many purposes.
