List of prime ministers of Canada

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The prime minister of Canada is the head of government of Canada. Since Confederation in 1867 the office has been held by 24 people. William Lyon Mackenzie King is the longest-serving prime minister, with three stints totalling 21 years; John Turner and Kim Campbell are among the shortest-serving at under three months each. Justin Trudeau, who served from 2015 to 2025, is the son of Pierre Elliott Trudeau (1968–1979, 1980–1984) — the only father-son pair to hold the office.

Records

  • Longest-serving: William Lyon Mackenzie King — 21 years, 154 days over three periods.
  • Shortest-serving: Charles Tupper — 68 days (1896).
  • Two prime ministers died in office: John A. Macdonald (1891) and John Thompson (1894).
  • Kim Campbell is the only woman to have held the office; Joe Clark took office the day before his 40th birthday, the youngest ever.
  • Liberal prime ministers have held office for roughly two-thirds of the period since 1867.
