Tekken (Japanese: 鉄拳, "Iron Fist") is a Japanese fighting-game franchise developed and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. It centres on the fictional King of Iron Fist Tournament, hosted by the Mishima Zaibatsu corporation, and on the multigenerational feud of the Mishima family. The series is one of the best-selling fighting-game franchises of all time, with more than 50 million units sold.

Gameplay

Tekken is distinguished by its four-button control scheme, in which each button corresponds to one of the character's limbs, and by its emphasis on close-range hand-to-hand combat, juggles, and sidestepping in three dimensions. Its roster draws on real martial arts — karate, capoeira, taekwondo, Muay Thai, wrestling, and others — alongside fantastical fighters such as robots, animals, and demons.

Story

The central narrative follows the cursed Mishima bloodline: Heihachi Mishima, his son Kazuya, and grandson Jin Kazama, whose struggles for control of the Mishima Zaibatsu repeatedly escalate into global conflict. Each mainline entry advances the family saga, which concluded a major arc with Tekken 8 (2024).

Legacy

The franchise has spawned films, an animated series, comics, and a long-running competitive scene culminating in the Tekken World Tour. It holds a Guinness World Record for the longest-running video game storyline without reboots or retcons.
