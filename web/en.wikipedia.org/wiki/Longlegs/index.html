Longlegs is a 2024 American horror-thriller film written and directed by Osgood Perkins and starring Maika Monroe, Nicolas Cage, Blair Underwood and Alicia Witt. Monroe plays FBI agent Lee Harker, who is assigned to an unsolved serial-killer case: over several decades, entire families have been murdered by their fathers, who then killed themselves, leaving only coded letters signed "Longlegs" at the scenes. As Harker — who appears to have psychic abilities — deciphers the occult pattern behind the killings, the investigation turns toward her own childhood and her devout mother.

Production and release

Cage, who also produced, plays the title killer in heavy prosthetic makeup; his appearance was withheld from trailers. The film was distributed by Neon, whose cryptic marketing campaign — teaser clips, a phone number playing recordings, and coded messages — attracted wide attention. It premiered at the Los Angeles Beyond Fest in June 2024 and was released theatrically in the United States on 12 July 2024.

Reception

Longlegs became Neon's highest-grossing release and one of the most commercially successful independent films of 2024, grossing around $127 million worldwide against a budget reported at under $10 million. Critics praised its atmosphere, cinematography and Cage's performance, frequently comparing its police-procedural horror tone to The Silence of the Lambs and Se7en, though some found its supernatural third act polarising.
