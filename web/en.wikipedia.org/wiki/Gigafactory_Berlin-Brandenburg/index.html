Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg

Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg (also known as Giga Berlin or Gigafactory 4) is a manufacturing plant for Tesla, Inc. in Grünheide, Germany. The campus is located 35 kilometres (20 mi) south-east of central Berlin on the Berlin–Wrocław railway. The facility's opening was delayed by local administrative and legal reviews of environmental impact, particularly concerning water usage and effects on the surrounding forest.

Location: Grünheide, Brandenburg, Germany
Opened: 22 March 2022
Operated by: Tesla, Inc.
Products: Tesla Model Y, battery cells

Announced by Elon Musk in November 2019 at an awards ceremony in Berlin, the factory is Tesla's first manufacturing location in Europe. Construction began in 2020, and the plant was officially opened on 22 March 2022, when the first production Model Y vehicles were delivered to customers.

Production

The factory produces the Tesla Model Y crossover for the European market and was designed for an eventual capacity of 500,000 vehicles per year, alongside on-site battery cell production. It employs more than 10,000 people, making it one of the largest industrial employers in Brandenburg.

Controversies

The project drew opposition from local residents and environmental groups concerned about water consumption in a drought-prone region and the clearing of pine forest. Protests in 2024 included an arson attack on a nearby electricity pylon that temporarily halted production, as well as demonstrations against a planned expansion of the site.
