SummerSlam is an annual professional wrestling premium live event produced by WWE. First held in 1988 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, it is one of WWE's original four big events, alongside WrestleMania, Royal Rumble, and Survivor Series, and is traditionally held in August — branded "The Biggest Party of the Summer".

History

The inaugural SummerSlam, on 29 August 1988, was headlined by the Mega Powers (Hulk Hogan and Randy Savage) against the Mega Bucks (Ted DiBiase and André the Giant). The 1992 event, held at Wembley Stadium in London, drew over 80,000 fans and is remembered for the British Bulldog defeating Bret Hart for the Intercontinental Championship in the main event — often cited as one of the greatest matches in company history.

From 2009 to 2014 SummerSlam was anchored at the Staples Center in Los Angeles, and from 2015 to 2018 at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, before moving to large stadiums: recent editions have been staged at venues such as Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas (2021), Nissan Stadium in Nashville (2022), Ford Field in Detroit (2023), and Cleveland Browns Stadium (2024), reflecting its status as WWE's second-biggest event of the year after WrestleMania.

Notable matches and moments

  • 1991 — "Match Made in Heaven/Hell": Hulk Hogan and the Ultimate Warrior vs. Sgt. Slaughter, Colonel Mustafa and General Adnan; Randy Savage and Miss Elizabeth's on-screen wedding.
  • 1998 — "Highway to Hell": Stone Cold Steve Austin vs. The Undertaker.
  • 2000 — the first Tables, Ladders and Chairs (TLC) match, between the Hardy Boyz, Dudley Boyz, and Edge and Christian.
  • 2002 — Shawn Michaels's return match against Triple H, and Brock Lesnar winning the WWE Championship from The Rock.
  • 2013 — Daniel Bryan defeating John Cena, followed by Randy Orton cashing in Money in the Bank.

Mr. SummerSlam is an unofficial nickname associated with performers with notable records at the event; Brock Lesnar headlined a record run of consecutive SummerSlams from 2014 to 2022.
