Nostra aetate

Nostra aetate (Latin: "In our time") is the Declaration on the Relation of the Church with Non-Christian Religions of the Second Vatican Council, promulgated by Pope Paul VI on 28 October 1965.

Content

The brief declaration rejects nothing true and holy in other religions, urges dialogue and respect toward Hindus, Buddhists, Muslims and others, and in its fourth section repudiates antisemitism and states that Jews should not be presented as rejected or accursed by God, reversing centuries of teaching of contempt.

Impact

It transformed Catholic–Jewish relations and founded modern interreligious dialogue in the Catholic Church.
