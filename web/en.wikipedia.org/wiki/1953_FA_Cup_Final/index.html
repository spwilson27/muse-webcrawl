The 1953 FA Cup Final was contested by Blackpool and Bolton Wanderers at Wembley Stadium on 2 May 1953. Blackpool won 4–3 in one of the most famous cup finals of all time, remembered as the "Matthews Final" for the performance of 38-year-old Blackpool winger Stanley Matthews, who finally won an FA Cup winner's medal at the third attempt.

Match summary

Bolton led 3–1 with just over 20 minutes remaining, despite having to reshuffle after injuries in an era before substitutes were permitted. Blackpool then staged a remarkable comeback: Stan Mortensen completed a hat-trick — still the only hat-trick in a Wembley FA Cup final — to level the score, and in injury time Matthews set up Bill Perry for the winning goal. Mortensen's achievement was largely overshadowed in public memory by Matthews' starring role.

Significance

The final was one of the first great televised sporting occasions in Britain; an estimated record television audience watched in the year of the Coronation, and many households bought their first television sets to see it. It cemented Matthews' status as the most popular English footballer of his generation.
