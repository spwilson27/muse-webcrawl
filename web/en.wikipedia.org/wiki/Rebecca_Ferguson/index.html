Rebecca Louisa Ferguson Sundström (born October 19, 1983) is a Swedish actress. She began her career in the Swedish soap opera Nya tider (1999–2000) and gained international recognition as Elizabeth Woodville in the BBC series The White Queen (2013), which earned her a Golden Globe nomination.

Career

Ferguson's breakthrough in film came as the spy Ilsa Faust in Mission: Impossible – Rogue Nation (2015), a role she reprised in Fallout (2018) and Dead Reckoning (2023). She starred as Jenny Lind in the musical The Greatest Showman (2017), appeared in Life (2017), Doctor Sleep (2019) and Men in Black: International (2019), and played Lady Jessica in Denis Villeneuve's Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024). On television she has led the Apple TV+ science-fiction series Silo (2023–present) as Juliette Nichols, also serving as an executive producer.

Early life

Ferguson was born in Stockholm and raised in the Vasastan district. Her mother is English and her father Swedish; she attended an English-speaking school in Sweden and worked as a model and dance teacher before and during her early acting career.
