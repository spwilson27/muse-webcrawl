Nigeen Lake

LocationSrinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India
TypeEutrophic lake
Surface areaapprox. 1.6 km²
Max. depth6 m (20 ft)

Nigeen Lake (sometimes spelled Nageen Lake) is a mildly eutrophic lake in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India. It is connected to the better-known Dal Lake by a narrow channel and strait, and to the Khushal Sar and Gil Sar lakes via the Nallah Amir Khan waterway. The name "Nigeen" is thought to derive from the local phrase meaning "jewel in the ring".

Description

Nigeen Lake is surrounded by large numbers of willow and poplar trees and is considered quieter and cleaner than Dal Lake. Its eastern shore is overlooked by the Hari Parbat hill and the Hazratbal shrine district lies nearby. The lake is famous for its rows of moored houseboats, which cater to tourists, and for swimming and water-skiing in summer. Shikara boats ferry visitors between the houseboats and the shore.

Conservation

Like other lakes in the Kashmir valley, Nigeen Lake suffers from encroachment, siltation, and pollution from surrounding settlements. De-weeding and dredging programmes have been undertaken periodically by the Lakes and Waterways Development Authority to limit the spread of aquatic weeds and restore open water.
