Pokémon Detective Pikachu
Director: Rob Letterman
Starring: Ryan Reynolds, Justice Smith, Kathryn Newton
Release: 2019
Box office: $450 million

Pokémon Detective Pikachu is a 2019 fantasy film based on the Pokémon franchise. It was directed by Rob Letterman from a screenplay he co-wrote, and is a live-action/animated adaptation loosely based on the 2016 video game Detective Pikachu.

Ryan Reynolds stars as the voice and facial motion capture of Pikachu, a talking detective, with Justice Smith as Tim Goodman, a young man searching for his missing father in Ryme City, a metropolis where humans and Pokémon live side by side and Pokémon battles are banned. Kathryn Newton co-stars as reporter Lucy Stevens.

Reception

The film grossed about $450 million worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing video game film adaptations at the time, and received generally mixed-to-positive reviews, with praise for the creature designs and Reynolds's performance.
