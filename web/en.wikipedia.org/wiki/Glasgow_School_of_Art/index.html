Glasgow School of Art

The Glasgow School of Art (GSA) is a higher education art school in Glasgow, Scotland, founded in 1845 as the Glasgow Government School of Design. It is one of a small number of independent art schools in the United Kingdom and consistently ranks among the leading institutions for art and design education.

The Mackintosh Building

The school's main building on Renfrew Street, designed by Charles Rennie Mackintosh and built in two phases (1897–1899 and 1907–1909), is regarded as a masterpiece of early modern architecture. It suffered major fires in 2014 and again in 2018, the latter gutting the building during its restoration; a faithful reinstatement has been planned.

Alumni

Graduates include many Turner Prize winners and nominees, and the school is historically associated with the Glasgow Boys, the Glasgow Girls, and the group known as "The Four".
