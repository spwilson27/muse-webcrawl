ANYmal
Type: Quadrupedal robot
Manufacturer: ANYbotics
Origin: Switzerland, ETH Zurich

ANYmal is a four-legged robotic platform developed at ETH Zurich's Robotic Systems Lab and commercialized by the spin-off ANYbotics. It is designed for autonomous inspection in industrial environments such as offshore platforms, mines and power plants.

Design

ANYmal uses series-elastic actuators in its legs, giving compliant, torque-controlled locomotion over rough terrain, stairs and gaps. Sensors include LiDAR, depth and optical cameras for mapping and inspection payloads for thermal and acoustic measurements.

Applications

Deployments include autonomous inspection rounds reading gauges, detecting thermal anomalies and gas leaks in energy and chemical facilities.
