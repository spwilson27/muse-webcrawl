Hélène Cixous (born 5 June 1937) is a French feminist writer, philosopher, playwright, and literary critic, born in Oran, Algeria. She is a central figure in poststructuralist feminist theory.

Écriture féminine

In her landmark essay "The Laugh of the Medusa" (1975), Cixous called for an écriture féminine — a feminine writing that disrupts phallocentric language and celebrates women's bodies and difference. Her work draws on Derrida (a close friend), psychoanalysis, and deconstruction.

Career

Cixous co-founded the experimental University of Paris VIII (Vincennes) and its women's studies programme, the first in Europe. She has written dozens of books of fiction, essays, and plays, many staged with the Théâtre du Soleil.
