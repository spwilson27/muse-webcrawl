2021 Copa América

The 2021 Copa América was the 47th edition of the Copa América, the international men's football championship organised by South America's CONMEBOL. Originally scheduled for 2020 in Argentina and Colombia, it was postponed by the COVID-19 pandemic and ultimately hosted by Brazil from 13 June to 10 July 2021. Argentina won the tournament, defeating Brazil 1–0 in the final at the Maracanã.

Host changes

The tournament was to be the first co-hosted Copa América, shared by Argentina and Colombia. Colombia was removed in May 2021 amid civil unrest and the pandemic, and Argentina withdrew days later because of a COVID-19 surge, leaving Brazil — itself heavily affected by the virus — as a last-minute host, a decision that drew domestic criticism. All ten CONMEBOL nations participated after guest teams Australia and Qatar withdrew; matches were played behind closed doors in Brasília, Cuiabá, Goiânia and Rio de Janeiro.

Final and significance

In the final on 10 July 2021, Ángel Di María scored the only goal in the 22nd minute, chipping Ederson from Rodrigo De Paul's long pass. The victory ended Argentina's 28-year major-trophy drought and gave captain Lionel Messi his first senior international title. Messi was named best player and finished joint top scorer with Colombia's Luis Díaz, while Emiliano Martínez won the Golden Glove. Brazil's Neymar was named the tournament's other best player by CONMEBOL.
