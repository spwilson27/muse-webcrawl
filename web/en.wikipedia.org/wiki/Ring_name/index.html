Ring name

A ring name is a type of stage name used by an athlete such as a professional wrestler, mixed martial artist, or boxer whose real name is considered unattractive, dull, difficult to pronounce or spell, amusing for the wrong reasons, or projecting the wrong image. Ring names are also used in other performance combat traditions, such as the shikona of sumo wrestlers and the adopted names of lucha libre performers.

Use in professional wrestling

Since the advent of the Internet, it is relatively easy to discover a fighter's real name, but performers continue to use ring names to construct a distinct character or gimmick. Some wrestlers have used many different ring names over a career, while others become so identified with one that it eclipses their birth name. Promotions such as WWE have historically sought to own the ring names of their performers as trademarks, leading wrestlers to adopt new names when changing companies.

Use in boxing and other sports

Boxers from Jack Dempsey to Muhammad Ali competed under names differing from their birth names, sometimes adding a nickname into the billing itself. In Japan, sumo wrestlers take a shikona on entering a stable, and may change it during their careers for luck or to reflect promotion.
