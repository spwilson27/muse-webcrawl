Kyrgyzstan

Kyrgyzstan, officially the Kyrgyz Republic, is a landlocked country in Central Asia defined by the Tien Shan mountains, which cover most of its 200,000 square kilometres. It borders Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and China. Its population is about 7 million and its capital is Bishkek.

Kyrgyzstan is known for alpine Lake Issyk-Kul, the world's second-largest mountain lake, which never freezes; for the epic of Manas, among the longest epic poems in the world; and for nomadic traditions including yurt dwelling, horse games such as kok-boru, and eagle hunting. Since independence in 1991 Kyrgyzstan has been Central Asia's most politically turbulent state, with revolutions in 2005, 2010 and 2020, and a recentralisation of power under President Sadyr Japarov.
