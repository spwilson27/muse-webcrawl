Ministry of Health and Family Welfare

Formed1947
HeadquartersNirman Bhawan, New Delhi
JurisdictionGovernment of India

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) is an Indian government ministry responsible for health policy in India. It is also responsible for all government programmes relating to family planning in India. The minister is a cabinet-level member of the Council of Ministers.

Structure

The ministry comprises two departments: the Department of Health and Family Welfare and the Department of Health Research. It oversees national health programmes, medical education, drug regulation through the Central Drugs Standard Control Organization, and major institutions such as the All India Institute of Medical Sciences.

Programmes

Major initiatives have included the National Health Mission, the Universal Immunisation Programme, efforts to eradicate polio and control tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS and vector-borne disease, and the Ayushman Bharat scheme providing health insurance cover to economically vulnerable families. The ministry coordinates family welfare and population stabilisation programmes and played the central administrative role in India's response to the COVID-19 pandemic, including the national vaccination drive.
