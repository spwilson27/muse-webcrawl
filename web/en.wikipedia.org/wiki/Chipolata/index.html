Chipolata

A chipolata is a type of fresh sausage, believed to have been created in France, similar to the Italian cipollata, from which its name derives. Chipolatas are traditionally made with coarse-ground pork seasoned with salt and pepper together with herbs — such as sage, thyme, pimento, or nutmeg.

Characteristics

Chipolatas are distinguished from most other sausages by being quite thin and short: typically about 2 cm in diameter and 12 cm long, they are often twisted into links and may be sold as a string of eight or more. In the United Kingdom, chipolatas are a standard component of a full breakfast and of the traditional Christmas dinner, frequently served wrapped in streaky bacon as "pigs in blankets" (in British usage). Variants made from beef, lamb, or meat-free ingredients are also sold.
