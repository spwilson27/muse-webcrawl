Johnson County is a county in the U.S. state of Kansas, located immediately southwest of Kansas City, Missouri. Its county seat is Olathe and its most populous city is Overland Park. With roughly 610,000 residents at the 2020 census, it is the most populous county in Kansas.

History

The county was established in 1855 as one of the original counties of Kansas Territory and was named for Thomas Johnson, a Methodist missionary and territorial legislator. It developed as part of the Kansas City metropolitan area, growing rapidly after the Second World War as suburban communities expanded.

Economy and communities

Johnson County is known for its affluence, highly rated public schools, and corporate headquarters. Its cities include Olathe, Overland Park, Lenexa, Shawnee, Leawood, and Prairie Village.
