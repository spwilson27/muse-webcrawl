Illustration

An illustration is a decoration, interpretation or visual explanation of a text, concept or process, designed for integration in published media such as books, magazines, posters, films, video games and animations.

History

Illustration reaches back to cave paintings and illuminated manuscripts; woodcut and engraving made mass illustration possible with printing. The 19th century saw a golden age of book and magazine illustration, with artists such as Gustave Doré and Arthur Rackham.

Modern practice

Today illustration spans editorial, advertising, scientific, technical and children's-book work, produced in traditional media and digitally. Notable modern illustrators include Norman Rockwell, Beatrix Potter and Quentin Blake.
