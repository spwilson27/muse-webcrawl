Memphis is a city in the southwestern corner of Tennessee, the county seat of Shelby County, situated on bluffs above the Mississippi River at the state's border with Mississippi and Arkansas. With a population of about 633,000 at the 2020 census, it is Tennessee's second-most populous city and the anchor of a metropolitan area of about 1.3 million people. Memphis is world-renowned for its foundational role in American music — blues, soul and rock and roll — and as the home of FedEx, whose global air hub makes Memphis International Airport one of the busiest cargo airports in the world.

History

The site, long inhabited by the Chickasaw, was visited by Hernando de Soto in 1541 and later contested by France, Spain and the United States. Memphis was founded in 1819 by John Overton, James Winchester and future president Andrew Jackson, and named for Memphis, Egypt, on the Nile. It grew as a cotton and river-trade centre and, under slavery, a major slave market. Yellow fever epidemics in the 1870s devastated the city, which lost its charter in 1879 before recovering.

In the twentieth century Memphis became central to African American music and to the civil rights movement. Martin Luther King Jr. was assassinated there on 4 April 1968 at the Lorraine Motel — now the National Civil Rights Museum — while supporting striking sanitation workers.

Music

Beale Street was a centre of blues music, home to W. C. Handy, "Father of the Blues". In the 1950s, Sam Phillips's Sun Studio recorded Elvis Presley, Johnny Cash, Jerry Lee Lewis and Howlin' Wolf, effectively launching rock and roll; Elvis's home, Graceland, is one of America's most visited house museums. In the 1960s Stax Records and Hi Records defined Memphis soul with Otis Redding, Isaac Hayes and Booker T. & the M.G.'s.

Economy and culture

Anchored by the river, rail lines, interstates and the FedEx Express World Hub, Memphis styles itself "America's Distribution Center". Healthcare (St. Jude Children's Research Hospital) and tourism are other major sectors. The city is famous for its dry-rub and wet barbecue styles, celebrated each May at the World Championship Barbecue Cooking Contest, and fields the NBA's Memphis Grizzlies.
