Men's major golf championships

The men's major golf championships, commonly known as the major championships or simply the majors, are the most prestigious tournaments in professional golf. The four majors are:

Masters TournamentAprilAugusta National Golf Club (fixed)1934

The Open ChampionshipJulyLinks courses in the United Kingdom (rotating)1860

Significance

Winning a major is one of the highest achievements in golf. The golfer who wins all four in a single calendar year is said to have won the Grand Slam; Bobby Jones achieved a version of this in 1930 by winning the U.S. and British Open and Amateur championships. Five golfers have completed the modern "career Grand Slam" by winning each of the four majors at least once: Gene Sarazen, Ben Hogan, Gary Player, Jack Nicklaus, and Tiger Woods.

Most major wins

Jack Nicklaus holds the record with 18 major championships, followed by Tiger Woods with 15 and Walter Hagen with 11. Nicklaus also finished runner-up in majors 19 times.
