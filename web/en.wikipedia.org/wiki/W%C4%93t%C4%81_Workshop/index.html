Wētā Workshop
Founded: 1987, Wellington, New Zealand
Founders: Richard Taylor, Tania Rodger
Industry: Special effects, design, collectibles

Wētā Workshop is a special effects and prop company based in Miramar, Wellington, New Zealand, that produces effects for television and film. Founded in 1987 by Richard Taylor and Tania Rodger as RT Effects, the company initially produced creatures and effects for New Zealand productions before gaining worldwide fame through Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings film trilogy.

Film work

Wētā Workshop designed and fabricated the weapons, armour, creatures, miniatures ("bigatures") and prosthetics for The Lord of the Rings (2001–2003) and The Hobbit trilogies, winning Academy Awards for makeup and visual effects contributions. Other major credits include King Kong (2005), The Chronicles of Narnia, Avatar (2009), District 9 (2009), Mad Max: Fury Road (2015), Blade Runner 2049 (2017), and Dune (2021).

The company is distinct from Wētā FX (formerly Weta Digital), the separate digital visual effects company co-founded by Peter Jackson.

Other work

Beyond film, Wētā Workshop creates museum exhibitions — most notably the scale models and dioramas for the "Gallipoli: The Scale of Our War" exhibition at Te Papa — as well as collectibles, books, costumes, theme-park experiences, and the Wētā Cave visitor attraction in Wellington.
