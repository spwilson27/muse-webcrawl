Myelodysplastic syndrome

Myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS) are a group of cancers in which immature blood cells in the bone marrow do not mature or become healthy blood cells. The different types are classified based on the type of blood cells affected and bone marrow findings.

Symptoms may include feeling tired, shortness of breath, easy bruising, and frequent infections, although many people initially have no symptoms. Risk factors include previous chemotherapy, radiation therapy, smoking, and exposure to benzene.

Treatment

Treatment includes supportive care, blood transfusions, growth factors, hypomethylating agents such as azacitidine, lenalidomide for del(5q) cases, and stem cell transplantation, the only potentially curative option. Some cases progress to acute myeloid leukaemia.
