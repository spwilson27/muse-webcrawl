Dogger Bank Wind Farm

Dogger Bank Wind Farm is a group of offshore wind farms under construction in the North Sea off the coast of Yorkshire, England, on the shallow Dogger Bank sandbank. When complete, with a planned capacity of around 3.6 GW across its first phases (Dogger Bank A, B, and C, plus the Sofia project), it will be the world's largest offshore wind farm.

Development

The project is being developed by SSE Renewables, Equinor, and Vårgrønn. It uses GE Haliade-X turbines of 13–14 MW — among the largest ever installed — transmitting power ashore via HVDC connections to substations near Beverley and Lackenby. Dogger Bank A generated its first power in October 2023. A fourth phase, Dogger Bank D, has also been proposed.
