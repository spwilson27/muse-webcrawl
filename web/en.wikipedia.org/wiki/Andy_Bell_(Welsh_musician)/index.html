Andrew Piran Bell (born 11 August 1970) is a Welsh musician, singer and songwriter. He was a founding member, guitarist and co-vocalist of the shoegaze band Ride, and later played bass guitar in Oasis from 1999 until their split in 2009, continuing with Liam Gallagher in Beady Eye. Since Ride's reformation in 2014 he has balanced the band with solo work released under his own name and the alias GLOK.

Ride

Bell formed Ride in Oxford in 1988 with Mark Gardener, Laurence Colbert and Steve Queralt. The band became leaders of the British shoegaze scene with the albums Nowhere (1990) and Going Blank Again (1992), on which Bell wrote songs including "Vapour Trail" and "Leave Them All Behind". Internal tensions and a turn toward retro rock on Tarantula (1996) led to the band's split that year.

Oasis and Beady Eye

After the short-lived Hurricane No. 1, Bell joined Oasis in 1999 as bassist, replacing Paul McGuigan. Though primarily the bass player, he contributed songs including "Turn Up the Sun" and "Keep the Dream Alive" to Don't Believe the Truth (2005) and "The Nature of Reality" to Dig Out Your Soul (2008). When Oasis split in 2009 he switched back to guitar in Beady Eye, which released two albums before disbanding in 2014. He also played in the supergroup Mantra of the Cosmos.

Later work

Ride reformed for touring in 2014 and have since released the albums Weather Diaries (2017), This Is Not a Safe Place (2019) and Interplay (2024). Bell's solo albums include The View from Halfway Down (2020) and Flicker (2022); as GLOK he makes electronic music.
