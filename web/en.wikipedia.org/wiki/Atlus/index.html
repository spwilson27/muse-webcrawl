Atlus Co., Ltd. is a Japanese video game developer, publisher and distribution company based in Tokyo. A subsidiary of Sega, it is best known for the Megami Tensei, Persona, Etrian Odyssey and Shin Megami Tensei role-playing game franchises. Its mascot is Jack Frost, a snowman-like demon from the Megami Tensei series.

History

Atlus was founded in 1986 and initially developed games for other publishers. It released the first Megami Tensei in 1987, adapted from a series of novels. The company expanded into arcades and karaoke before financial troubles at parent Index Corporation led to Atlus being sold to Sega in 2013, after which it continued as a distinct brand and development house.

Notable series

  • Shin Megami Tensei – dark, demon-summoning RPGs beginning in 1992.
  • Persona – spin-off series; Persona 5 (2016) became a worldwide critical and commercial hit.
  • Etrian Odyssey – first-person dungeon-crawling RPGs.
  • Catherine (2011) and Metaphor: ReFantazio (2024), from Studio Zero.

Western publishing

Its former American arm, Atlus USA (later Atlus West), localised its own games and niche Japanese titles by other studios, building a strong reputation among Western RPG fans.
