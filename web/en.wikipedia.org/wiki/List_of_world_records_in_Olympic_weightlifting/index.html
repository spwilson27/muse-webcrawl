List of world records in Olympic weightlifting

This is a list of world records in Olympic weightlifting, ratified by the International Weightlifting Federation (IWF). Records are kept in the snatch, the clean and jerk, and the total, for each bodyweight category. The IWF restructured its weight classes in 2018, and records set thereafter began from designated "world standards" where no mark had yet been achieved.

Historically, some of the most famous weightlifting records include the 263.5 kg clean and jerk of Leonid Taranenko (1988, under the old weight classes) — the heaviest lift in competition for many years — and the super-heavyweight marks of Hossein Rezazadeh of Iran in the early 2000s.
