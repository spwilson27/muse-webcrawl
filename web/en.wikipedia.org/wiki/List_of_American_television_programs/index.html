List of American television programs

The following is a list of American television programs, organised by genre. American television broadcasting began experimentally in the 1920s and commercially in the 1940s; the United States has since produced many of the world's most widely distributed and influential series. This article indexes notable programs in drama, comedy, animation, reality, news, and other categories; many entries lead to dedicated genre lists.
