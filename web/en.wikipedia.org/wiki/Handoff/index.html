Handoff

Handoff (or handover) may refer to the transfer of responsibility, control or an object from one party to another in several fields:

Telecommunications

In mobile telephony, a handover (called handoff in North American usage) is the process by which an active call or data session is transferred from one cell site or channel to another without disconnecting, as the user moves through the network.

Sport

In American football, a handoff is a play in which the quarterback directly transfers the ball to a running back. In relay races, the baton handoff (exchange) within the marked zone is decisive to performance.

Other uses

In healthcare and aviation, a handoff (handoff communication) is the structured transfer of responsibility for a patient or task between shifts or providers — a recognised patient-safety concern. In computing, Apple's Handoff feature lets users begin a task on one device and continue it on another. Business and project management likewise use "handoff" for transferring work between teams.
