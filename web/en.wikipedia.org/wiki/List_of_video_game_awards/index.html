List of video game awards

This is a list of notable awards presented for excellence in video games, including industry, press and fan-voted honours. The most prestigious "Game of the Year" ceremonies include the Game Awards (United States), the BAFTA Games Awards (United Kingdom), the D.I.C.E. Awards, the Game Developers Choice Awards, the Golden Joystick Awards, and Japan's Japan Game Awards.

Historically, magazine awards such as those of Electronic Gaming Monthly, GameSpot and IGN shaped early "Game of the Year" discussions, while ceremonies like the NAVGTR Awards and the SXSW Gaming Awards recognize craft categories including performance, writing and audio design.
