eu-LISA, the European Union Agency for the Operational Management of Large-Scale IT Systems in the Area of Freedom, Security and Justice, is an agency of the European Union that runs the EU's major border, asylum and law-enforcement databases around the clock. It was established by Regulation (EU) No 1077/2011 and began operations on December 1, 2012, taking over systems previously managed by the European Commission and member-state consortia.

Systems managed

The agency's core systems are the second-generation Schengen Information System (SIS II), the Visa Information System (VIS) and Eurodac, the fingerprint database for asylum applicants. A 2018 regulation broadened its mandate and name within the same acronym, adding development and operation of the Entry/Exit System (EES), the European Travel Information and Authorisation System (ETIAS), the European Criminal Records Information System for third-country nationals (ECRIS-TCN), and interoperability components linking the databases, along with the DubliNet communications network.

Structure

eu-LISA's seat is in Tallinn, Estonia, its main operational and development site is in Strasbourg, France, with a technical backup site in Sankt Johann im Pongau, Austria, and a liaison office in Brussels. It is governed by a Management Board of member-state and Commission representatives and led by an Executive Director.
