Kalibr (Russian: Калибр, "calibre"), also referred to as 3M54/3M14 by its GRAU designations and SS-N-27 "Sizzler" and SS-N-30 in NATO reporting, is a family of Russian cruise missiles developed by the Novator Design Bureau. It includes anti-ship, land-attack, and anti-submarine variants launched from ships, submarines, aircraft, and ground launchers.

Design

The export version is marketed as the Club missile system. The anti-ship 3M54 flies subsonically for most of its trajectory and accelerates to supersonic speed in its terminal phase, while the land-attack 3M14 is a long-range subsonic missile comparable to the American Tomahawk, with reported ranges of 1,500–2,500 km depending on variant.

Operational use

Kalibr missiles were first used in combat in October 2015, when Russian warships in the Caspian Sea launched strikes into Syria. They have since been used extensively in Syria and in the Russian invasion of Ukraine from 2022 onward.
