MGM Macau, formerly known as the MGM Grand Macau, is a luxury hotel and casino resort in Macau, a special administrative region of China. The property opened on December 18, 2007, and was developed by a joint venture between MGM Resorts International and Pansy Ho, daughter of casino magnate Stanley Ho.

Design

The resort's 35-storey tower is distinguished by three undulating horizontal bands of gold, silver and copper-colored glass, evoking waves of the South China Sea. Inside, the Grande Praça — a Portuguese-inspired central plaza beneath a soaring glass ceiling — anchors the property's restaurants and retail. The hotel has roughly 600 rooms and suites, a large casino floor with table games and slots, a spa and convention facilities.

Operations

MGM Macau was the first Macau venture for MGM Resorts and trades through MGM China Holdings, listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2011. The company later opened a second property, MGM Cotai, in 2018. The resort operates under Macau's gaming concession system alongside the city's other major operators.
