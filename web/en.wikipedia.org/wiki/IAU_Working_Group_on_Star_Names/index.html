IAU Working Group on Star Names

The IAU Working Group on Star Names (WGSN) is a working group of the International Astronomical Union (IAU) established in 2016 to catalogue and standardize proper names for stars. Before its creation, star names such as Sirius, Betelgeuse and Vega were traditional but had no official status; the WGSN reviews historical and cultural names and approves official designations for bright and notable stars.

Activities

The group's first bulletin in 2016 approved an initial list of 227 star names, drawing on traditional Arabic, Greek, Latin and other sources. In 2019, through the IAU100 NameExoWorlds project, the WGSN also approved names for exoplanets and their host stars submitted by national campaigns around the world.

Guidelines

Approved names generally honour cultural and historical heritage, avoid names of living individuals and commercial terms, and retain spellings consistent with the original language where practical. The group maintains the IAU Catalog of Star Names as the definitive reference.
