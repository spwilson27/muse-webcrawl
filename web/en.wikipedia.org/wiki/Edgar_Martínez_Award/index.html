Edgar Martínez Award

The Edgar Martínez Outstanding Designated Hitter Award, commonly referred to as the Edgar Martínez Award and originally known as the Outstanding Designated Hitter Award, is presented annually to the outstanding designated hitter in the American League of Major League Baseball.

History

The award was established in 1973, the year the American League adopted the designated hitter rule. It was renamed in 2004 in honour of Edgar Martínez, the Seattle Mariners star widely regarded as one of the greatest designated hitters in baseball history.

Winners

David Ortiz has won the award a record eight times. Other multiple winners include Hal McRae, Don Baylor, Edgar Martínez himself (five times) and Nelson Cruz.
