List of airports in China

This is a list of airports in China, grouped by province-level division. By the mid-2020s China had more than 250 civil transport airports, administered by the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC), with continuing rapid expansion. The busiest are Beijing Capital, Shanghai Pudong, and Guangzhou Baiyun, which regularly rank among the world's busiest airports by passenger traffic.
