Shravana

Shravana (Sanskrit: श्रवण, "listening") is the 22nd nakshatra (lunar mansion) in Hindu astronomy and astrology, spanning from 10°00' to 23°20' in the sidereal sign of Capricorn. Its ruling deity is Vishnu, and its symbol is an ear or three footprints.

Mythology

Shravana is associated with Vishnu's Vamana avatar, whose three strides measured the universe. The star Altair (Alpha Aquilae) is its brightest marker. In Jain and Hindu tradition, the month of Shravana (July–August) is also considered sacred, associated with listening to scriptures — the root meaning of the name.

Astrological character

Traditional texts describe those born under Shravana as good listeners, scholars, and seekers of fame through knowledge.
