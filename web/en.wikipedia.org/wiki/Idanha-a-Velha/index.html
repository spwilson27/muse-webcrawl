Idanha-a-Velha

Idanha-a-Velha
Country: Portugal
Municipality: Idanha-a-Nova
District: Castelo Branco

Idanha-a-Velha is a village and former civil parish in the municipality of Idanha-a-Nova, in the district of Castelo Branco, central Portugal. It is one of the oldest and most historically significant settlements in the country, and is classified among the Historical Villages of Portugal.

History

The site was founded by the Romans as Civitas Igaeditanorum, a town on the road between Emerita Augusta (Mérida) and Bracara Augusta (Braga). It became an early episcopal see in the Visigothic period, declined after the Muslim invasions, and was donated by Afonso Henriques to the Knights Templar, who built its castle. Surviving monuments include Roman walls and a bridge over the River Ponsul, a Visigothic baptistery, the Romanesque cathedral-church and the castle ruins.
