Alt Penedès is a comarca (county) in the Penedès region of Catalonia, Spain, in the province of Barcelona. Its capital is Vilafranca del Penedès. Together with Baix Penedès and Garraf it forms the historic Penedès territory.

Economy

The comarca is one of Spain's most important wine regions, forming the heart of the Penedès DO and the principal production zone for cava, Spain's sparkling wine, centred on Sant Sadurní d'Anoia. Vineyards of xarel·lo, macabeu, and parellada grapes dominate the rolling landscape, alongside wine-related industry and logistics.

Culture

Vilafranca is renowned for its castells (human towers), performed by the Castellers de Vilafranca, and the comarca hosts major wine and food festivals.
