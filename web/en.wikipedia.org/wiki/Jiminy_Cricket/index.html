Jiminy Cricket is the Walt Disney version of the Talking Cricket, a fictional character created by Carlo Collodi for his 1883 children's book The Adventures of Pinocchio. In Disney's 1940 animated film Pinocchio, Jiminy is appointed by the Blue Fairy to serve as Pinocchio's official conscience, and he narrates the story.

Role in Pinocchio

Jiminy, a vagabond cricket who takes shelter in Geppetto's workshop, witnesses the Blue Fairy's visit and is appointed "Lord High Keeper of the Knowledge of Right and Wrong, Counselor in Moments of Temptation, and Guide along the Straight and Narrow Path." He accompanies the wooden puppet through temptations — Stromboli's puppet show, Pleasure Island — attempting, not always successfully, to keep him out of trouble. At the end he receives a gold badge as an official conscience.

Jiminy sings two of the film's best-known songs: "When You Wish Upon a Star," which became an anthem of The Walt Disney Company, and "Give a Little Whistle."

Name

"Jiminy Cricket" was a genteel minced oath in common American usage — an exclamation of surprise — long before the film. Disney's character popularized the expression further.

Other appearances

Jiminy narrated and hosted numerous Disney productions, including the film Fun and Fancy Free (1947), the Christmas special From All of Us to All of You, educational shorts for The Mickey Mouse Club, and he appears in the Kingdom Hearts video games as the keeper of the party's journal. Cliff Edwards, his original voice, defined the character with his ukulele-style charm and falsetto singing.
