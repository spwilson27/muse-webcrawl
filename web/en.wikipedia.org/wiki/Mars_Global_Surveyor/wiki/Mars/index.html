Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet in the Solar System. Known as the Red Planet for its iron-oxide-rich surface, Mars has a thin atmosphere, polar ice caps, the largest volcano in the Solar System (Olympus Mons), and a vast canyon system, Valles Marineris.

Characteristics

A Martian day (sol) is about 24.6 hours and its year is 687 Earth days. Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. Geological evidence from orbiters — including Mars Global Surveyor, which operated from 1997 to 2006 and imaged gullies and layered terrain — shows Mars once had liquid water on its surface. Today water survives mainly as ice, and the planet is a major target in the search for past life and future human exploration.
