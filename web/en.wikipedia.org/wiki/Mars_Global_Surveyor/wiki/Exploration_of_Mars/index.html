Exploration of Mars

The exploration of Mars has been carried out by robotic spacecraft since the 1960s, driven by the planet's resemblance to Earth and the search for past or present life. Dozens of flyby, orbiter, lander, and rover missions have been launched by the United States, the Soviet Union and Russia, Europe, Japan, India, the United Arab Emirates, and China.

History

Early successes included Mariner 4's first flyby in 1965 and the Viking landers of 1976. Mars Global Surveyor, launched in 1996, mapped the planet in high resolution and found evidence of past water. Later highlights include the rovers Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance; the orbiters Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter and MAVEN; India's Mars Orbiter Mission; and China's Tianwen-1 with the Zhurong rover. Sample return and eventual human missions remain long-term goals.
