A coach is a person involved in the direction, instruction and training of a sports team or individual athlete. A coach may also be a teacher, mentor, tactician and facilitator. In professional team sports, the head coach is typically responsible for selecting the team, devising tactics, directing training, and making decisions during competition; in association football the equivalent role is usually called the manager.

Roles and responsibilities

Coaches analyse their own athletes' strengths and weaknesses and those of the opposition, design training programmes to improve physical fitness, technique and teamwork, and provide motivation and psychological support. Most professional teams employ a staff of assistant and specialist coaches — for example, goalkeeping, strength and conditioning, or video analysis coaches — under the head coach.

Qualifications

Many sports require formal coaching licences graded by level, such as the UEFA Pro Licence in football or national federation awards in athletics and swimming. At the amateur and youth levels, coaches are often volunteers, and safeguarding and first-aid certification is commonly required.
