National Indigenous Australians Agency

The National Indigenous Australians Agency (NIAA) is an Australian Government agency responsible for coordinating policy development, program design, and service delivery for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. It was established on 1 July 2019 by Prime Minister Scott Morrison and sits within the portfolio of the Prime Minister, accountable to the Minister for Indigenous Australians.

Establishment

The NIAA was created following the 2019 federal election, replacing the Indigenous Affairs Group of the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet. Its creation was announced on 26 May 2019, with the agency intended to take a more regional and place-based approach to Indigenous policy, working in partnership with Indigenous communities.

Functions

The agency's responsibilities include leading and coordinating Commonwealth policy on Indigenous affairs, delivering programs under the Indigenous Advancement Strategy, supporting the Closing the Gap framework agreed with state and territory governments and the Coalition of Peaks, and advising on land rights, native title, and heritage matters. It operates through regional offices across Australia, including in remote communities.

Closing the Gap

The NIAA plays a central role in implementing the National Agreement on Closing the Gap, signed in 2020, which sets targets in areas such as life expectancy, early childhood development, education, and incarceration rates, and commits governments to shared decision-making with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.
