Komatsu

Komatsu Ltd.

Industry: Heavy machinery

Founded: 1921

Headquarters: Tokyo, Japan

Products: Excavators, bulldozers, mining equipment, presses

Komatsu Ltd. (株式会社小松製作所) is a Japanese multinational corporation that manufactures construction, mining, forestry and military equipment, as well as industrial equipment like press machines and lasers. It is the world's second-largest manufacturer of construction equipment, after Caterpillar.

History

The company takes its name from the city of Komatsu, Ishikawa, where it was founded in 1921 as a mining equipment works separated from the Takeuchi Mining Company. Komatsu expanded internationally after the Second World War, becoming known for bulldozers and, later, hydraulic excavators and autonomous haulage systems for mines.

Operations

Komatsu sells equipment under its own brand worldwide and owns subsidiaries including the mining equipment maker Joy Global, acquired in 2017 and renamed Komatsu Mining.
