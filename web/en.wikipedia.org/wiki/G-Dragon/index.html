G-Dragon

Kwon Ji-yong (born 18 August 1988), best known by his stage name G-Dragon, is a South Korean rapper, singer, songwriter, record producer, entrepreneur and fashion designer, known as the "King of K-pop". He is the leader of the boy band BigBang, which debuted in 2006 under YG Entertainment and became one of the best-selling Korean acts.

His solo work includes the albums Heartbreaker (2009), Coup d'Etat (2013), and the EP Kwon Ji Yong (2017), featuring hits such as "Crooked" and "Untitled, 2014". G-Dragon is also a prominent fashion figure, collaborating with brands including Chanel and Nike, and launching his own label Peaceminusone. After military service and a hiatus, he returned with the single "Power" in 2024 and the album Übermensch in 2025.
