Eroski

Eroski is a Spanish supermarket chain organised as a cooperative within the Mondragón Corporation, with over 1,600 outlets across Spain.

Eroski is a Spanish distribution company and one of the country's largest supermarket and hypermarket chains. Founded in 1969 in the Basque Country through the merger of several small consumer cooperatives, it is part of the Mondragón Corporation, the world's largest worker-cooperative federation. Its name is a contraction of the Basque words erosi ("to buy") and toki ("place").

Structure

Eroski is unusual among large retailers in being a hybrid cooperative: it is jointly owned by its worker-members and by consumer-members, who participate in its governance. The chain operates several formats — large Eroski hypermarkets, Eroski Center and Eroski City supermarkets, the Caprabo chain in Catalonia and the Balearic Islands (acquired in 2007), Eroski Viajes travel agencies, petrol stations and perfumeries. It is a major distributor of Basque and Spanish regional products and a founding patron of consumer information initiatives; its magazine Consumer Eroski is a widely read source of product testing in Spain.

History

The first Eroski stores opened in the industrial towns of Gipuzkoa and Bizkaia, offering worker families an alternative to company stores. Expansion through the 1980s and 1990s made it a national chain. The 2007 purchase of Caprabo made Eroski the largest food retailer in Spain by store count at the time, but the ensuing debt burden coincided with the financial crisis and a price war, forcing store sales and restructuring through the 2010s, including the sale of hypermarkets to competitors. An alliance with other regional chains in the Euromadi buying group and a renewed focus on fresh, local food and its franchise network underpin its more recent strategy.
