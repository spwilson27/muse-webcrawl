Hoverla

Elevation2,061 m (6,762 ft)
RangeChornohora, Carpathians
LocationIvano-Frankivsk / Zakarpattia oblasts, Ukraine

Mount Hoverla, at 2,061 metres (6,762 ft), is the highest mountain in Ukraine. It lies in the Chornohora range of the Eastern Carpathians, on the border of Ivano-Frankivsk and Zakarpattia oblasts, within the Carpathian National Nature Park.

Geography

The mountain's slopes are covered with beech and spruce forests giving way to alpine meadows (polonyna). The Prut River rises on its slopes. Its name is thought to derive from a Hungarian word for a snow-covered peak.

Tourism

Hoverla is a popular national symbol and climbing destination; the standard route from the Zarosliak base takes two to three hours. Mass ascents are traditionally held on Ukrainian holidays, and a Ukrainian flag and trident monument mark the summit.
