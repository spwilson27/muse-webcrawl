Grenadine

Grenadine is a sweet, deep-red syrup used as an ingredient and flavouring in many cocktails and other drinks. Despite its colour, it is traditionally made from pomegranate juice — the name derives from the French grenade, meaning pomegranate — reduced with sugar, although many commercial varieties are made from other fruit juices, citric acid, colourings and flavourings with little or no pomegranate.

Use

Grenadine gives drinks sweetness, a red or pink colour and a slightly tart fruit flavour. It is an essential ingredient of the Shirley Temple and the Roy Rogers, and appears in classic cocktails such as the Tequila Sunrise, the Jack Rose, the Pink Lady, the Zombie and the Planter's Punch, often drizzled so that it sinks through the drink to create a layered, sunrise-like effect. Because it is non-alcoholic, it is widely used in mocktails as well. Traditional producers in France and the Caribbean still make grenadine from real pomegranate juice, which has a more complex, tart flavour than mass-market substitutes.
