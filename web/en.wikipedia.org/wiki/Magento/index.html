Magento is an open-source e-commerce platform written in PHP. Originally developed by Varien Inc. and released in 2008, it became one of the most widely used systems for building online stores, with a large marketplace of extensions and themes. The software is now owned by Adobe as part of Adobe Commerce.

History

Varien, a Los Angeles-based consultancy, began building Magento in 2007 and released version 1.0 in March 2008. eBay acquired a stake in 2010 and full ownership in 2011, then sold the business to private equity firm Permira in 2015, when Magento Commerce was spun out as an independent company. Magento 2, a major rewrite, was released in November 2015. Adobe acquired Magento Commerce in 2018 for $1.68 billion and folded it into the Adobe Experience Cloud; the commercial product was rebranded Adobe Commerce in 2021, while the open-source edition retained the Magento name.

Features

The platform provides catalogue management, marketing and SEO tools, multi-store and multi-language support, and integrations with payment gateways and shipping carriers. Magento Open Source is free under the Open Software License, while Adobe Commerce adds cloud hosting, business-intelligence and B2B features under a commercial licence.

Community

Magento has an extensive ecosystem of developers and solution partners. Following the Adobe acquisition, the community fork OpenMage and, from 2022, the Mage-OS project emerged to continue open development of the platform.
