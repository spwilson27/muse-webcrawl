Fédération Aéronautique Internationale

The Fédération Aéronautique Internationale (FAI; English: World Air Sports Federation) is the world governing body for air sports, founded on 14 October 1905 in Paris, France. It is recognized by the International Olympic Committee. The FAI establishes standards and certifies records for aeronautical and astronautical activities, including the 100-kilometre Kármán line definition of the boundary of space.

Its disciplines include aerobatics, aeromodelling, ballooning, gliding, hang gliding, paragliding, parachuting, and rotorcraft. The FAI maintains world records and organizes the World Air Games. Its headquarters are in Lausanne, Switzerland.
