New Jalpaiguri Junction railway station

Station code: NJP
Location: Siliguri, West Bengal, India
Zone/Division: Northeast Frontier Railway / Katihar
Opened: 1960
Gauges: Broad gauge (1,676 mm) and narrow gauge (610 mm)
Platforms: 8 (plus narrow-gauge platform)

New Jalpaiguri Junction railway station (station code NJP) is a major railway station in Siliguri, West Bengal, India. It serves as the principal rail gateway to the Northeast of India and to the hill stations of Darjeeling and Sikkim, and is one of the busiest stations of the Northeast Frontier Railway. The station is notable for having both broad-gauge platforms and a narrow-gauge platform for the UNESCO-listed Darjeeling Himalayan Railway.

History

After Partition severed the historic rail route through East Pakistan, a new link to Assam was built through the Siliguri corridor. The station was established around 1960 as the broad-gauge railhead for the "chicken's neck" corridor connecting mainland India to the northeast. The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, originally starting at Siliguri, was extended to New Jalpaiguri in the early 1960s so that passengers could transfer between the broad-gauge network and the narrow-gauge "toy train" to Darjeeling.

Services

New Jalpaiguri is served by trains to Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Guwahati, and other major cities, including Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Vande Bharat services. It is the starting point for trains up the Barauni–Guwahati line and for branch services toward Alipurduar and Haldibari (with onward cross-border links toward Bangladesh planned or in operation). Darjeeling Himalayan Railway services run from NJP to Darjeeling via Siliguri, Kurseong, and Ghoom.

Importance

Because almost all rail traffic between mainland India and the eight northeastern states passes through the Siliguri corridor, NJP is strategically one of the most important stations in the country, and it is also the arrival point for tourists heading to Darjeeling, Sikkim, Kalimpong, and Bhutan (via nearby roadheads).
