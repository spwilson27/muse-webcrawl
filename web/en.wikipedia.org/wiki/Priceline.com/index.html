Priceline.com is an American online travel agency (OTA) for finding discount rates for travel-related purchases such as airline tickets and hotel stays. Founded in 1997 by entrepreneur Jay S. Walker, it became famous for its "Name Your Own Price" reverse-auction model, in which travelers bid for unsold travel inventory without knowing the exact supplier until after purchase.

History

Priceline launched in 1998 with airline tickets and expanded into hotels, rental cars, and other products. It went public in March 1999 in one of the emblematic initial public offerings of the dot-com boom, and its advertising became widely recognized through spokesman William Shatner, who portrayed the "Priceline Negotiator." The company survived the dot-com crash, refocused on travel, and grew into the core of the Priceline Group, which acquired Booking.com, Agoda, Kayak, and OpenTable. The group renamed itself Booking Holdings in 2018.

Products

Priceline is best known for opaque booking products: "Name Your Own Price" (discontinued for flights in 2016 and later phased out for other categories) and its successor "Express Deals," which reveal the hotel or airline only after a non-refundable purchase at a discounted rate. Priceline also sells conventional published-rate flights, hotels, rental cars, cruises, and vacation packages, and operates a VIP loyalty program. Its headquarters are in Norwalk, Connecticut.
