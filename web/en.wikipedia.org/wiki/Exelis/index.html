Exelis Inc.

Exelis Inc. was an American global aerospace, defense, information and services company. It was spun off from ITT Corporation in 2011 as the defense and information solutions arm of the former conglomerate, and was headquartered in McLean, Virginia.

History

Exelis's businesses included electronic warfare systems, night-vision equipment, air traffic management, and communications systems. In 2015 the company was acquired by Harris Corporation for approximately .75 billion; the combined company later became part of L3Harris Technologies. Its geospatial imaging business, descendant of the Kodak remote-sensing operations, passed through several owners after the acquisition.
