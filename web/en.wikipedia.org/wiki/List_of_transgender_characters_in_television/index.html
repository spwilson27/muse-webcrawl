List of transgender characters in television

Transgender characters have appeared in television drama and comedy since the 1970s, initially mostly in one-off "issue" episodes. Representation expanded considerably in the 2010s with acclaimed series featuring trans characters in leading roles, increasingly played by transgender actors.

Earlier examples include Alexis Meade in Ugly Betty (played by Rebecca Romijn) and the character of Zoe Slater-era storylines in British soap operas; British soaps later introduced permanent trans characters such as Hayley Cropper in Coronation Street (1998), the first permanent transgender character in a British soap, played by Julie Hesmondhalgh.
