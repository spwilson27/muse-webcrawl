Stéphane Charbonnier (21 August 1967 – 7 January 2015), known as Charb, was a French satirical caricaturist and journalist. He was editor in chief of the satirical newspaper Charlie Hebdo from 2009 until his death.

Career

Charb contributed cartoons to numerous French publications and became a central figure at Charlie Hebdo, where his character Maurice et Patapon and his editorial cartoons defended secularism and freedom of expression. He lived under police protection after threats and the 2011 firebombing of the paper's offices.

Death

On 7 January 2015, Charb was killed along with eleven others in the terrorist attack on the Charlie Hebdo offices in Paris. His posthumous book, an open letter on blasphemy and Islamophobia, was published days after his death.
