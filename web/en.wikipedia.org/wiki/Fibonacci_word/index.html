Fibonacci word

A Fibonacci word is a specific sequence of binary digits (or, more generally, a sequence of letters over a two-letter alphabet) formed by repeated concatenation in a manner analogous to the way the Fibonacci numbers are formed by repeated addition.

Definition

The Fibonacci words are defined by S0 = "0", S1 = "01", and Sn = Sn−1 Sn−2, the concatenation of the two previous words. The sequence begins:

S0 = 0
S1 = 01
S2 = 010
S3 = 01001
S4 = 01001010
S5 = 0100101001001

The length of Sn is the Fibonacci number Fn+2. The words converge to an infinite limiting word, the Fibonacci word, which is a classic example of a Sturmian word.

Properties

Fibonacci words are rich in combinatorial structure. They never contain the substrings "11" or "000"; the ratio of 0s to 1s tends to the golden ratio; and the infinite Fibonacci word is aperiodic but highly ordered, appearing in the study of quasicrystals, combinatorics on words, and fractal geometry — the arrangement of its letters traces out the fractal known as the Fibonacci word fractal.
