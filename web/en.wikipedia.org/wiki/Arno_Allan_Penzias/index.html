Arno Allan Penzias

Arno Allan Penzias (April 26, 1933 – January 22, 2024) was an American physicist and radio astronomer. Along with Robert Woodrow Wilson, he discovered cosmic microwave background radiation, for which he shared the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1978.

Life

Born in Munich to a Jewish family, Penzias escaped Nazi Germany via the Kindertransport to Britain and then to the United States. He studied at City College of New York and Columbia University, then joined Bell Labs.

Discovery

Working with Wilson at Holmdel with a horn antenna in 1964–65, they detected persistent microwave noise interpreted by Princeton researchers as the cosmic microwave background — key evidence for the Big Bang.

Later career

Penzias became a senior executive at Bell Labs and later a venture capitalist in Silicon Valley.
