Social science

Social science is the branch of science devoted to the study of societies and the relationships among individuals within them, including anthropology, economics, geography, history, political science, psychology and sociology.

Methods

Social scientists use quantitative methods such as surveys and statistics alongside qualitative methods such as interviews, participant observation and archival research, debating how far the field can or should mirror the natural sciences.
