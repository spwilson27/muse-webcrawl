Joko Widodo

Joko Widodo
Born: 21 June 1961, Surakarta, Central Java, Indonesia
Office: 7th President of Indonesia (2014–2024)
Previous offices: Governor of Jakarta; Mayor of Surakarta
Party: PDI-P (until 2024)

Joko Widodo (born 21 June 1961), popularly known by his nickname Jokowi, is an Indonesian politician and businessman who served as the seventh president of Indonesia from 2014 to 2024. He was the first Indonesian president not to come from an elite political or military background.

Early life and business

Widodo was born in Surakarta (Solo), Central Java, into a poor family; his father was a timber merchant and the family was evicted from riverside housing during his childhood. He studied forestry at Gadjah Mada University and worked in the timber and furniture business, building a successful furniture export company.

Mayor and governor

He entered politics as Mayor of Surakarta (2005–2012), winning praise for pragmatic, anti-corruption administration, improving public services, and his populist walkabout style of direct engagement with citizens, known as blusukan. He then became Governor of Jakarta in 2012, alongside deputy Basuki Tjahaja Purnama ("Ahok"), launching reforms in transport, flood control, health, and education.

Presidency

Widodo won the 2014 presidential election against Prabowo Subianto and defeated him again in 2019, with Ma'ruf Amin as his second vice-president. His administration prioritised infrastructure development — toll roads, ports, airports, and mass transit — maritime policy, and bureaucratic reform. Signature late-term projects included the planned new capital city, Nusantara, in Kalimantan, to replace Jakarta, and downstream industrial policy (notably nickel export restrictions to force domestic processing). His presidency also drew criticism over democratic backsliding concerns, the weakening of the anti-corruption commission, and environmental damage from development.

He was succeeded in October 2024 by Prabowo Subianto, whose running mate was Widodo's son, Gibran Rakabuming Raka. After leaving office Widodo's relationship with his former party, PDI-P, broke down and he was expelled from it.
