The following is a list of restaurant chains in the United States. The American restaurant industry is one of the largest in the world, spanning fast-food giants such as McDonald's, Subway and Starbucks, casual-dining chains such as Applebee's and Olive Garden, and coffee and snack chains led by Starbucks and Dunkin'. Subway has historically had the most US locations of any chain, while McDonald's leads in sales.
