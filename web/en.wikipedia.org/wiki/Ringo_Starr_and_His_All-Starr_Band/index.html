Ringo Starr and His All-Starr Band

Ringo Starr and His All-Starr Band is a rotating supergroup led by former Beatles drummer Ringo Starr. Since 1989 the band has toured regularly, with each edition featuring Starr alongside a changing cast of musicians, each of whom performs hits from their own careers as well as Starr's songs and Beatles numbers.

OriginUnited Kingdom / United States
Years active1989–present
LeaderRingo Starr (drums, vocals)

Concept

The concept, devised by producer David Fishof, is that "everyone on stage is a star in their own right." Concerts alternate songs fronted by Starr with songs by each guest member, backed by the whole ensemble.

Notable members

Across its many editions the band has included Joe Walsh, Billy Preston, Dr. John, Levon Rickey, Todd Rundgren, Nils Lofgren, Peter Frampton, Sheila E., Edgar Winter, Steve Lukather, Gregg Rolie, Colin Hay, and many others.
