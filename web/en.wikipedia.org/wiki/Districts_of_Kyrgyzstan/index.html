Districts of Kyrgyzstan

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


Kyrgyzstan's regions and districts

The districts of Kyrgyzstan (Kyrgyz: rayon) are the second-level administrative subdivisions of the Central Asian country of Kyrgyzstan. The country is divided into seven regions (oblasts), plus the two independent cities of Bishkek and Osh, and the regions are subdivided into districts. In total there are 40 districts, with the city districts of Bishkek and Osh administered separately.

Administration

Each district is administered by an akim (district head), appointed by the governor of the region, and by a district state administration. Districts are further divided into rural communities (ayyl aymak) and towns. The system derives from the raion structure of the Soviet period, when Kyrgyzstan was the Kirghiz Soviet Socialist Republic.

Examples

Districts include Sokuluk and Alamudun districts in Chuy Region surrounding the capital Bishkek, Issyk-Kul District in the region of the same name around Lake Issyk-Kul, and Suzak District in Jalal-Abad Region in the southwest of the country.
