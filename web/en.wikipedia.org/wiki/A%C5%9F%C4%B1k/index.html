Aşık

An aşık (Azerbaijani: aşıq; Turkish: aşık) is a singer-poet and wandering bard who performs while accompanying himself on the saz, a long-necked lute. The tradition is widespread in the cultures of Azerbaijan, Turkey, Iranian Azerbaijan, Georgia, Armenia, and the wider Turkic world, with roots reaching back to the shamanic ozan bards of the early Turks.

Tradition

Aşıks commonly apprentice under a master, learn a repertoire of epic tales (dastan), lyric songs, and riddles, and improvise in performance, including in dueling exchanges (atışma) with rival aşıks, judged on wit and poetic skill. Their repertory blends love lyrics, heroic epics such as the tales of Köroğlu, religious mysticism, and social commentary.

Recognition

Famous aşıks include Aşık Veysel of Turkey and Ashig Alasgar of Azerbaijan. In 2009, UNESCO inscribed the art of Azerbaijani Ashiqs on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
