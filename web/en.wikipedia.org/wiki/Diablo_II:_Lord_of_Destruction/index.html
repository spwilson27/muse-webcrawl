Diablo II: Lord of Destruction is an expansion pack for the action role-playing game Diablo II, developed by Blizzard North and released in 2001. It is widely regarded as one of the finest expansion packs ever made, substantially reshaping the game's endgame.

Content

The expansion adds a fifth act set in the Barbarian highlands around Mount Arreat, where the player pursues Baal, the Lord of Destruction. It introduces two new character classes, the Assassin and the Druid, along with a larger stash, thousands of new items, runes and runewords, charms, jewels, and mercenaries who can be equipped and resurrected.

Legacy

Lord of Destruction kept Diablo II active for decades, and its systems were carried into the remaster Diablo II: Resurrected (2021).
