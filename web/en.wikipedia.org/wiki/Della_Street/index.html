Della Street

Della Street is a fictional character in the Perry Mason novels and adaptations created by Erle Stanley Gardner. She is the loyal, efficient, and sharp-witted confidential secretary of criminal defence attorney Perry Mason, and one of the most recognisable supporting characters in American legal fiction.

Character

Street manages Mason's office, fields his clients, conducts research, and frequently assists in his investigations alongside private detective Paul Drake. Gardner portrayed her as far more than a typist: she is resourceful, brave, and occasionally an active participant in Mason's courtroom stratagems. Mason proposed marriage to her on more than one occasion in the novels; she declined, reasoning that marriage would end her role in his professional life. Her surname is said to have been borrowed from Gardner's own admired secretary.

Portrayals

In the CBS television series Perry Mason (1957–1966), Della Street was played by Barbara Hale, whose performance — opposite Raymond Burr as Mason — became the definitive portrayal and won her an Emmy Award. Hale reprised the role in the Perry Mason television films from 1985 onward; in those films her son, William Katt, played Paul Drake Jr. Earlier film and radio adaptations featured several other actresses. In HBO's Perry Mason (2020), a reimagined Della Street was played by Juliet Rylance.
