Meltdown

Meltdown may refer to several distinct topics:

Nuclear

  • Nuclear meltdown: a severe nuclear reactor accident in which the reactor core overheats and melts, as at Three Mile Island (1979), Chernobyl (1986, a steam explosion following core damage), and Fukushima Daiichi (2011).

Computing

  • Meltdown (security vulnerability): a hardware vulnerability disclosed in January 2018 affecting many microprocessors, notably Intel x86 CPUs. Meltdown (CVE-2017-5754) breaks the isolation between user applications and the operating system kernel by exploiting speculative execution and out-of-order execution, allowing a process to read kernel memory. It was disclosed together with the related Spectre vulnerabilities. Software mitigations such as kernel page-table isolation (KPTI) reduced performance in some workloads, and later CPU generations fixed the flaw in silicon.

Other uses

  • A figurative collapse or loss of control, as in a market meltdown (financial crisis) or an emotional meltdown (overwhelming emotional response, a term also used in autism contexts).
  • Meltdown in entertainment: titles of albums, songs, films, and games.
