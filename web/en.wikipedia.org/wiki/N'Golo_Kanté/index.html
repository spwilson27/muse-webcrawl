N'Golo Kanté

Born: 29 March 1991, Paris, France

Height: 1.71 m (5 ft 7 in)

Position: Defensive midfielder

National team: France

N'Golo Kanté (born 29 March 1991) is a French professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder. Widely regarded as one of the finest midfielders of his generation, he is known for his extraordinary work rate, ball-winning ability, stamina and tactical intelligence, covering vast areas of the pitch.

Club career

Kanté began his senior career at Boulogne before joining Caen, where his performances in Ligue 1 attracted attention. In 2015 he signed for Leicester City, becoming a central figure in the club's astonishing 2015–16 Premier League title win — considered one of the greatest upsets in sporting history. He moved to Chelsea in 2016 and won the Premier League again in his first season, becoming the first outfield player to win back-to-back league titles with two different clubs since Eric Cantona. With Chelsea he also won the FA Cup, the UEFA Europa League, the UEFA Champions League (2020–21, in which he was named Player of the Match in the final), the UEFA Super Cup and the FIFA Club World Cup. In 2023 he joined Al-Ittihad in the Saudi Pro League.

International career

Kanté debuted for France in 2016, played at Euro 2016 (runners-up), and was a key member of the side that won the 2018 FIFA World Cup in Russia. He later won the UEFA Nations League in 2021.

Honours and style

Individually, Kanté won the PFA Players' Player of the Year and FWA Footballer of the Year awards in 2016–17, and was named French Player of the Year in 2017. Renowned for his humility and modest lifestyle, he is one of the most widely admired players in world football.
