List of Messier objects

The Messier objects are a set of 110 astronomical objects catalogued by the French astronomer Charles Messier in his Catalogue des Nébuleuses et des Amas d'Étoiles (Catalogue of Nebulae and Star Clusters), first published in 1774. Messier, a comet hunter, compiled the list so that fuzzy permanent objects would not be mistaken for comets. The catalogue includes galaxies, nebulae, and star clusters, and remains a standard target list for amateur astronomers; a "Messier marathon" attempts to observe all 110 in a single night.
