Justify (foaled 28 March 2015) is an American Thoroughbred racehorse who in 2018 became the thirteenth winner of the American Triple Crown. A massive chestnut colt trained by Bob Baffert and ridden by Mike E. Smith, he accomplished the feat in a whirlwind career of just six races in 112 days, having never raced as a two-year-old — thereby breaking the so-called "Curse of Apollo" that had stood since 1882.

Background

Justify was bred in Kentucky by Canadian John D. Gunther from his mare Stage Magic, and sired by Scat Daddy, who died in 2015 before Justify raced. He was sold as a yearling for $500,000 to a partnership including WinStar Farm, China Horse Club and SF Bloodstock; he raced in the colours of WinStar and partners, later including the Coolmore group. Unusually large for a racehorse, he was compared in build to Secretariat.

Racing career

Justify did not debut until 18 February 2018, winning a maiden race at Santa Anita by over nine lengths, then added an allowance win and the Grade I Santa Anita Derby over Bolt d'Oro to qualify for the Kentucky Derby. At Churchill Downs on 5 May, over a sloppy track in record rainfall, he broke cleanly despite the curse-of-Apollo statistic, led or pressed the pace throughout, and won by two and a half lengths from Good Magic. Two weeks later, in fog and mud at Pimlico, he held off Bravazo and Tenfold to win the Preakness Stakes by half a length. On 9 June 2018 at Belmont Park, Justify led from start to finish in the Belmont Stakes, beating Gronkowski by one and three-quarter lengths to complete the Triple Crown, remaining undefeated.

Retirement and stud career

Ankle filling delayed Justify's summer campaign, and in July 2018 his retirement was announced; he was syndicated for a reported record stud valuation. He stands at Ashford Stud (Coolmore America) in Kentucky and shuttles to Australia. His first crops have produced elite performers including 2023 Breeders' Cup Juvenile Fillies winner Just F Y I and City of Troy, the champion European two-year-old of 2023 and Derby winner of 2024. Justify was inducted into the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame in 2024.
