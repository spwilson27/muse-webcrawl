White Fox Co., Ltd. is a Japanese animation studio founded in April 2007 by Gaku Iwasa, formerly of the studio OLM. It is best known for producing critically acclaimed anime series including Steins;Gate (2011), Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World (2016–), Akame ga Kill! (2014), The Devil Is a Part-Timer! (2013), and Goblin Slayer (2018).

History

The studio's first production was Tears to Tiara (2009). Its breakthrough came with Steins;Gate, adapted from the visual novel by 5pb. and Nitroplus, which was widely praised and followed by the film Steins;Gate: The Movie − Load Region of Déjà Vu and the sequel series Steins;Gate 0. In 2016 the studio adapted Tappei Nagatsuki's light novel series Re:Zero, which became a major international hit and received further seasons.

Selected works

Television series include Katanagatari (2010), Jormungand (2012), Is the Order a Rabbit? (2014), Matoi the Sacred Slayer (2016), Girls' Last Tour (2017), Arifureta (2019, with Asread), and Cautious Hero (2019).
