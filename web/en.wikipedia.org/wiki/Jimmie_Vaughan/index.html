Jimmie Vaughan

Jimmie Lawrence Vaughan Jr. (born March 20, 1951) is an American blues rock guitarist and singer based in Austin, Texas. He is the elder brother of the late Texas blues guitarist Stevie Ray Vaughan.

Career

Vaughan moved to Austin in the late 1960s and in 1974 co-founded The Fabulous Thunderbirds with harmonica player Kim Wilson. The band blended blues, R&B, and rock and roll, achieving mainstream success in the 1980s with songs such as "Tuff Enuff" and "Wrap It Up" before Vaughan left in 1990.

Solo work

In 1990 he recorded the album Family Style with his brother Stevie Ray, released shortly after Stevie Ray's death in a helicopter crash. His solo career continued with Strange Pleasure (1994) and subsequent blues albums; he has won multiple Grammy Awards, including for the Vaughan Brothers album and for later traditional blues recordings. Vaughan is also known for his vintage car and hot rod collection, a passion reflected in his album artwork.
