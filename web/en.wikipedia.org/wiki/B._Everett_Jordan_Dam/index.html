B. Everett Jordan Dam

B. Everett Jordan Dam
Location: Chatham County, North Carolina
Coordinates: Haw River near Moncure
Construction began: 1967
Opening: 1974
Type: Earthfill embankment
Impounds: Haw River
Creates: Jordan Lake

The B. Everett Jordan Dam is an earthen embankment dam on the Haw River in Chatham County, North Carolina, United States. Built by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, it impounds Jordan Lake, a major reservoir serving the Research Triangle region.

History

The project was authorised following severe flooding in the Cape Fear River basin, notably after Hurricane Hazel in 1954. Construction ran from 1967 to 1974. The dam and lake are named for B. Everett Jordan, a United States Senator from North Carolina who championed the project.

Purpose

The reservoir provides flood control, water supply for surrounding communities including Cary, Apex and parts of Durham County, low-flow augmentation for water quality downstream, and recreation. The surrounding Jordan Lake State Recreation Area offers boating, fishing, camping and swimming.

Geography

The dam sits on the Haw River shortly before its confluence with the Deep River to form the Cape Fear River. The lake covers about 13,900 acres at normal pool with roughly 180 miles of shoreline.
