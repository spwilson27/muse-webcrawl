Claus Philipp Maria Schenk Graf von Stauffenberg (15 November 1907 – 21 July 1944) was a German army officer best known for his failed attempt on 20 July 1944 to assassinate Adolf Hitler at the Wolf's Lair, as part of Operation Valkyrie, a plan to use the Reserve Army to seize control of the government and end the Second World War.

Born into the aristocratic Stauffenberg family in Swabia, he joined the Reichswehr in 1926 and served as a staff officer in Poland, France, and the Soviet Union. In April 1943, while serving with the 10th Panzer Division in Tunisia, he was gravely wounded by an Allied strafing attack, losing his left eye, his right hand, and two fingers of his left hand.

Resistance

During his convalescence Stauffenberg was recruited into the military resistance against Hitler by his uncle Alexander von Üxküll-Gyllenband and others. As chief of staff of the Reserve Army's General Army Office under General Friedrich Olbricht, he had direct access to Hitler's briefings and became central to revising the Valkyrie emergency plan into a coup mechanism.

The 20 July plot

On 20 July 1944 Stauffenberg carried a briefcase bomb into a conference at the Wolf's Lair near Rastenburg in East Prussia. The explosion killed four men but Hitler survived, shielded by the heavy oak conference table. Stauffenberg flew to Berlin believing Hitler dead, but the coup in the capital faltered; by evening the conspirators at the Bendlerblock had been overwhelmed. Stauffenberg was arrested and shot by firing squad shortly after midnight on 21 July, reportedly crying "Long live our sacred Germany!" His co-conspirators, including Ludwig Beck, Olbricht, and Albrecht Ritter Mertz von Quirnheim, were executed the same night or in the trials that followed.

Legacy

Initially controversial in post-war Germany, Stauffenberg and the 20 July plotters came to be honoured as symbols of resistance to tyranny. The Bendlerblock in Berlin houses the German Resistance Memorial Center, and 20 July is marked annually by the Bundeswehr. His life has been portrayed in films including Valkyrie (2008).
