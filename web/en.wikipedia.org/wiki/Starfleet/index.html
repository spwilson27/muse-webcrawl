Starfleet

Starfleet is a fictional organisation in the Star Trek universe, serving as the deep-space exploratory, scientific, diplomatic, and military service of the United Federation of Planets. Its headquarters are in San Francisco, and its officer corps is trained at Starfleet Academy.

Overview

Starfleet operates the starships crewed by the principal characters of the franchise, including the USS Enterprise. Its stated mission of peaceful exploration ("to explore strange new worlds") coexists with a defensive role. Ranks follow naval tradition, from ensign to admiral, and personnel wear division-coloured uniforms.

History within the fiction

In the prequel series Star Trek: Enterprise, Starfleet begins as an agency of United Earth in the 22nd century before becoming the Federation's service at its founding in 2161.
