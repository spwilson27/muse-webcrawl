Group One

Group One, Group 1 or Grade I is the highest level of classification for thoroughbred horse races in the international pattern race system. Group One races are the most prestigious events in flat and jump racing, carrying the largest prize funds and attracting the best horses.

Classification

Pattern races are graded by the International Grading and Race Planning Advisory Committee and regional authorities. A race earns Group One status based on the quality of its recent runnings, measured by the ratings of the first four finishers. In North America the equivalent designation is Grade I, administered by the American Graded Stakes Committee.

Famous Group One races

Examples include the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe in France, the Derby and the King George VI and Queen Elizabeth Stakes in Britain, the Japan Cup, and the Melbourne Cup in Australia. Victories in such races greatly increase a horse's value for breeding.
