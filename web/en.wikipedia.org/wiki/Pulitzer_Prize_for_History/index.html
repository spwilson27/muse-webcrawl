Pulitzer Prize for History

The Pulitzer Prize for History is one of the American Pulitzer Prizes awarded annually for letters, drama and music. It is given for a distinguished and appropriately documented book on the history of the United States, published in the preceding year.

History

The prize has been awarded since 1917. Winners include works on subjects from the American Revolution and Civil War to civil rights and foreign policy; notable recipients include Barbara Tuchman, David McCullough and Eric Foner. Some years the prize has been shared or not awarded. The award carries a cash prize administered by Columbia University under the terms of Joseph Pulitzer's bequest.
