Large Professor

Large Professor (born William Paul Mitchell, 21 July 1972), also known as Large Pro and Extra P, is an American hip hop record producer and rapper from Queens, New York. He was a founding member of the group Main Source and is noted for mentoring Nas and producing much of his early work, as well as production for Eric B. & Rakim, A Tribe Called Quest and Pete Rock & CL Smooth.

Career

Mitchell was discovered as a teenager by Paul C, whose production techniques he absorbed. With Main Source he made the acclaimed album Breaking Atoms (1991), which included the debut of Nas on "Live at the Barbeque". After leaving the group he concentrated on production, contributing to Nas's Illmatic (1994), including "It Ain't Hard to Tell", and released solo albums beginning with The LP, recorded in the 1990s but not officially released until 2009, followed by 1st Class (2002) and others.

Style

His production is known for soulful, jazz-inflected sampling with heavy drums, and he is frequently ranked among the most influential East Coast producers of the golden age of hip hop.
