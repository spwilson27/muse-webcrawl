View-Master

The View-Master is a device for viewing stereoscopic pairs of small transparent colour photographs mounted on a cardboard disc ("reel"). Introduced by the Sawyer's company at the 1939 New York World's Fair, it succeeded 19th-century stereoscopes as a mass-market 3D medium.

Format

Each reel holds seven stereo pairs on 14 film frames; the viewer advances images with a lever. Early reels featured scenic and travel subjects; after acquisitions by GAF and later Mattel (Fisher-Price), children's entertainment — Disney, television and film tie-ins — dominated catalogues numbering thousands of titles.

Uses

The US military used View-Masters for training in the 1940s; scenic reels remain in production for tourist sites. In 2015 Mattel relaunched the brand as a smartphone-based virtual reality viewer, later discontinued.

Legacy

The View-Master was inducted into the National Toy Hall of Fame in 1999.
