Heart disease describes a range of conditions affecting the heart — coronary artery disease (narrowed arteries causing angina and heart attacks), heart failure, arrhythmias, valve disease and congenital defects. Cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of death globally, claiming roughly a third of all deaths.

Prevention and treatment

Risk factors overlap the blood pages: high blood sugar, blood pressure, cholesterol, smoking (wider crawl) and inactivity. Treatment spans statins and stents to bypass surgery and transplantation; prevention — exercise, diet, not smoking — remains the cheapest cardiology.
