List of mining disasters

This is a list of notable mining disasters — accidents in mines causing multiple deaths, from explosions, fires, collapses, flooding, and gas poisoning. Coal mining has historically been the deadliest sector.

Causes and prevention

Firedamp (methane) and coal-dust explosions caused most historic mass-casualty events. Modern regulation — ventilation standards, gas monitoring, rock-dusting, and refuge chambers — has sharply reduced deaths in developed mining industries, though disasters continue worldwide.
