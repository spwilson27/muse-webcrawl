Maude Flanders is a fictional character in the animated television series The Simpsons. She was the wife of Ned Flanders and the mother of Rod and Todd. Maude was a devout Christian like her husband and was often seen as a moralising counterpart to the more free-spirited Marge Simpson, with whom she otherwise shared a friendly relationship.

Character

Maude was voiced by Maggie Roswell, with Marcia Mitzman Gaven filling in during a contract dispute in 1999–2000. She was a supporting character, rarely central to plots, known for her prudish activism — she campaigned alongside Reverend Lovejoy's wife Helen against perceived immorality in Springfield, including the burial of Michelangelo's David statue's perceived indecency.

Death

In the eleventh-season episode "Alone Again, Natura-Diddily" (2000), Maude was killed when she was knocked off a racetrack grandstand by a barrage of T-shirts fired from air cannons at a NASCAR race, which Homer ducked to avoid. Her death was motivated in part by the producers' desire to open new storylines for Ned, and by the voice-cast dispute. Maude has since appeared in flashbacks and as a ghost in later episodes and "Treehouse of Horror" segments.
