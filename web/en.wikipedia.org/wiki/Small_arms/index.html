Small arms

Small arms are man-portable, individual, kinetic-energy projectile weapons designed to be fired by a single person or operated by a small crew. In common usage the term includes handguns, rifles, carbines, submachine guns, shotguns and light machine guns; broader definitions used by the United Nations also encompass light weapons such as heavy machine guns, grenade launchers and portable missile systems (together, "small arms and light weapons", SALW).

Small arms are the standard weapons of infantry, police and civilians worldwide. Their proliferation is a focus of international arms control, including the UN Programme of Action on small arms and the Arms Trade Treaty.

History

The category emerged with portable firearms in the late medieval period and developed through muskets, breech-loaders, repeating rifles and modern automatic weapons.
