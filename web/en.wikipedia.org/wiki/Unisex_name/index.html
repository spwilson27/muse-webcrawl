Unisex name
Also calledGender-neutral name, epicene name
ExamplesAlex, Jordan, Taylor, Robin, Sam

A unisex name (also known as an epicene name, a gender-neutral name, or an androgynous name) is a given name that can be used for a person of any gender. Some names are unisex in one culture but exclusively masculine or feminine in another.

In English-speaking countries, names such as Alex, Chris, Jordan, Sam, and Taylor are commonly given to both boys and girls. Many unisex names began as surnames or as masculine names that gradually came into use for girls, a shift that rarely occurs in the opposite direction.

Trends

Gender-neutral naming has grown in popularity since the late 20th century, partly reflecting changing attitudes toward gender. In some languages, most names are strongly gendered by grammar, while in others, such as many Chinese and Korean names, gender associations may be conveyed by the characters chosen rather than the sound.
