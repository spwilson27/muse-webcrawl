Signal

A signal is any function that conveys information about a phenomenon. In electronics and telecommunications, it usually refers to a time-varying voltage, current, or electromagnetic wave carrying data. Signals may be analog, varying continuously, or digital, taking a discrete set of values.

Processing

Signal processing is the discipline concerned with analysing, modifying and synthesising signals such as sound, images and scientific measurements. Key concepts include the Fourier transform, sampling (governed by the Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem), filtering and noise.

Other meanings

The term also denotes a gesture, sign or device used to convey a command or warning, such as railway signals and traffic signals, and, in biology, any feature or behaviour by which an organism communicates (see signalling theory).
