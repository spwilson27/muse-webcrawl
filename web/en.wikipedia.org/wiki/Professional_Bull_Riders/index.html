Professional Bull Riders

Professional Bull Riders, Inc. (PBR) is an international professional bull riding organisation based in Pueblo, Colorado, United States. Founded in 1992 by 20 bull riders who each contributed $1,000 to break away from the traditional rodeo circuit, PBR stages events in which riders attempt to stay aboard a bucking bull for eight seconds, with scores awarded equally for the rider's performance and the bull's difficulty.

Tours and championships

The premier series is the Unleash the Beast tour, culminating in the PBR World Finals, where the year-end world champion receives a $1 million bonus and a gold belt buckle. Lower tiers include the Pendleton Whisky Velocity Tour and the Touring Pro Division, and a team format, the PBR Team Series, launched in 2022. Two-time world champions include Silvano Alves, J.B. Mauney, Chris Shivers and Adriano Moraes; notable bulls include Bushwacker, widely regarded as the greatest bucking bull of all time.

Organisation

PBR was acquired by Endeavor in 2015. It broadcasts events on television and streaming platforms and licenses international tours in countries including Brazil, Australia, Canada and Mexico. Animal welfare is a recurring topic: PBR states that its bulls are valuable athletes, countered by criticism from animal-rights groups about the sport's inherent risks to both animals and riders.
