Gilbert Hernandez (born 1957), often credited as Beto Hernandez, is an American cartoonist best known as co-creator, with his brothers Jaime and Mario, of the alternative comic book Love and Rockets (1981–present).

Work

Hernandez's contributions to Love and Rockets centre on the fictional Central American village of Palomar and its residents, particularly Luba, in a body of stories often called the Heartbreak Soup saga. His work blends magic realism, soap opera, and social commentary, and he is regarded as one of the most important cartoonists of his generation.

Other projects

His solo books include Chance in Hell, The Troublemakers, and Marble Season, a semi-autobiographical graphic novel about childhood in 1960s California. He has also done extensive work in erotic and genre comics.
