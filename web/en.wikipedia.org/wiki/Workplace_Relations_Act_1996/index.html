Workplace Relations Act 1996

The Workplace Relations Act 1996 was an Australian federal labour law enacted by the Howard government, commencing on 1 January 1997. It substantially reformed the industrial relations system established under the Industrial Relations Act 1988.

Provisions

The Act introduced Australian Workplace Agreements (individual statutory contracts), limited the Australian Industrial Relations Commission's arbitral role, restricted industrial action to bargaining periods, and emphasised enterprise-level agreement-making over industry awards.

Legacy

In 2005 the WorkChoices amendments expanded the Act's individual-contract and deregulation agenda, provoking a major union campaign. Most of the Act was repealed and replaced by the Fair Work Act 2009 under the Rudd government.
