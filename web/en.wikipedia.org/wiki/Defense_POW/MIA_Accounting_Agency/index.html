Defense POW/MIA Accounting Agency
Formed: 15 January 2015
Headquarters: Joint Base Pearl Harbor–Hickam, Hawaii
Parent: U.S. Department of Defense

The Defense POW/MIA Accounting Agency (DPAA) is an agency of the United States Department of Defense whose mission is to recover and account for American military personnel listed as prisoners of war (POW) or missing in action (MIA) from past conflicts. It was formed in 2015 by merging the Defense POW/Missing Personnel Office, the Joint POW/MIA Accounting Command (JPAC), and parts of the Air Force Life Sciences Equipment Laboratory.

Mission

DPAA conducts global search, recovery and laboratory identification operations, with forensic laboratories in Hawaii and at Offutt Air Force Base, Nebraska. More than 81,000 Americans remained unaccounted for from conflicts since World War II at the agency's creation; recoveries concern World War II, the Korean War, the Vietnam War, the Cold War, and the Gulf Wars.
