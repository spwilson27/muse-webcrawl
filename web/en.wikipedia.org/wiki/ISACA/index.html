ISACA is an international professional association focused on IT governance, auditing, cybersecurity and risk management. Founded in 1969 in the United States, it originally concentrated on electronic data processing audit controls and grew into a global body with members in more than 180 countries.

Certifications

ISACA administers widely recognized professional certifications: Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA), Certified Information Security Manager (CISM), Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC), and Certified in the Governance of Enterprise IT (CGEIT), along with newer credentials in cybersecurity and emerging technologies.

Frameworks

ISACA is the steward of COBIT, a framework for the governance and management of enterprise information and technology, and publishes research and guidance through its journals and conferences.
