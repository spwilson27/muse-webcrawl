The Thirteenth Doctor is an incarnation of the Doctor, the protagonist of the BBC science fiction television programme Doctor Who. She is portrayed by Jodie Whittaker, the first woman to play the role in the series' regular continuity. The Thirteenth Doctor appeared in three series and several specials between 2018 and 2022, under showrunner Chris Chibnall.

Character biography

Regenerating aboard her TARDIS, the Thirteenth Doctor falls to Earth in Sheffield, where she befriends police officer Yasmin Khan, retired bus driver Graham O'Brien, and his step-grandson Ryan Sinclair. Her tenure features encounters with the Daleks, the Cybermen, the Sontarans, and the Weeping Angels, and the revelations of the "Timeless Child" arc, which retconned the Doctor's origins. Her final adventure, "The Power of the Doctor" (2022), a BBC centenary special, saw her regenerate following a battle with the Master and a CyberMasters plot, with her regeneration unexpectedly producing the Fourteenth Doctor bearing the Tenth Doctor's face.

Characterisation and costume

Whittaker's Doctor is energetic, curious, warm, and socially awkward, with a strong moral stance against violence. Her costume—designed by Ray Holman—consists of a rainbow-striped dark top, blue culottes with yellow braces, brown boots, socks, and a long lilac-grey coat with a hood. Her sonic screwdriver was constructed in-story from Sheffield steel.

Reception

Whittaker's casting and performance were widely praised, and her debut episode drew the highest viewing figures for a new Doctor's first episode in the revived series. The era as a whole received mixed responses, with praise for Whittaker, the cinematography, and individual episodes such as "Rosa", "Demons of the Punjab", and "The Haunting of Villa Diodati", alongside criticism of writing and character development.
