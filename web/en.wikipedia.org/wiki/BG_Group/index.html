BG Group plc was a British multinational oil and gas company headquartered in Reading, England. It was created in 1997 when British Gas plc demerged its exploration and production business as BG plc (later BG Group), spinning off the UK gas supply business as Centrica. BG grew into one of the world's most successful independent exploration companies, with a particular reputation for exploration success and for building a leading liquefied natural gas (LNG) trading portfolio.

Operations

Major assets included giant pre-salt discoveries offshore Brazil (Tupi/Lula and neighbouring Santos Basin fields, with Petrobras), a stake in Kazakhstan's Karachaganak field, Egyptian gas and LNG, Trinidad and Tobago, and the Queensland Curtis LNG project in Australia — the first to convert coal-seam gas to LNG at scale. In 2014 Helge Lund became chief executive amid a share-price slump caused by production downgrades in Egypt and Brazil.

Shell acquisition

In April 2015 Royal Dutch Shell agreed to acquire BG Group for about £47 billion (roughly US$70 billion) in cash and shares, the largest oil-and-gas deal in over a decade. The takeover completed on 15 February 2016, making Shell the world's largest LNG producer and a dominant player in Brazil's deepwater. BG's Reading headquarters and brand were wound down thereafter.
