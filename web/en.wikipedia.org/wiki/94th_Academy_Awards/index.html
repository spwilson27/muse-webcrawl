94th Academy Awards

94th Academy Awards
Date: 27 March 2022
Venue: Dolby Theatre, Hollywood
Hosts: Regina Hall, Amy Schumer and Wanda Sykes.

The 94th Academy Awards ceremony, presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, honoured films released in 2021. It was held on 27 March 2022 at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood, Los Angeles. The coming-of-age drama CODA won Best Picture, becoming the first film distributed primarily by a streaming service, Apple TV+, and the first Sundance-premiered film to win the Academy’s top prize.

Major winners

Jane Campion won Best Director for The Power of the Dog, only the third woman to receive the award. Will Smith won Best Actor for King Richard and Jessica Chastain Best Actress for The Eyes of Tammy Faye. Troy Kotsur won Best Supporting Actor for CODA, becoming the first deaf man to win an acting Oscar, and Ariana DeBose won Best Supporting Actress for West Side Story. Dune led the evening in total awards with six, mostly in technical categories.

The slap incident

The ceremony is widely remembered for an altercation in which Will Smith walked on stage and slapped presenter Chris Rock after Rock joked about Smith’s wife, Jada Pinkett Smith. Smith later returned to his seat and shouted at Rock, then remained for his Best Actor acceptance. The Academy condemned the violence; Smith resigned from the Academy and was banned from Academy events for ten years, while debate continued over the organisation’s response that night and about alopecia, comedy and disability.

Format changes

Eight categories were presented and partly recorded before the live telecast in an effort to shorten the show, a decision criticised by many filmmakers and guilds. A fan-voted “Oscars Fan Favorite” segment was also introduced. Television ratings rebounded from the record low of the previous year but remained far below historic levels.
