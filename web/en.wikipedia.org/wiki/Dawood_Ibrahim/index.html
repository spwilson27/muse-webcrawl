Dawood Ibrahim

Dawood Ibrahim (born 26 December 1955) is an Indian mob boss, drug lord, and terrorist from Dongri, Mumbai, who heads the Indian organised crime syndicate D-Company, which he founded in Mumbai in the 1970s. He is wanted by Indian authorities on charges including murder, extortion, targeted killing, drug trafficking, and terrorism.

Criminal career

Ibrahim rose through the Mumbai underworld and fled India in the 1980s. He is accused by India of masterminding the 1993 Bombay bombings, coordinated with the assistance of his subordinates; the attacks killed 257 people. In 2003, the United States designated him a "Specially Designated Global Terrorist," and India and the United States have sought his extradition. The United Nations Security Council has also listed him under its Al-Qaida sanctions regime.

Status

His whereabouts are disputed; India maintains that he resides in Karachi, Pakistan, a claim Pakistan denies. He has been reported on Interpol's wanted list for cheating, criminal conspiracy, and running an international crime network.
