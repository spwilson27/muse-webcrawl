Avengers: Endgame

Avengers: Endgame is a 2019 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team the Avengers, produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely, it is the sequel to Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and the 22nd film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). In the film, the surviving Avengers assemble to reverse Thanos's extermination of half of all life in the universe.

Plot

Five years after Thanos used the Infinity Stones to disintegrate half of all life (the "Blip"), the surviving heroes discover, via Scott Lang's return from the Quantum Realm, that time travel may let them collect the Stones from the past and undo the snap. Teams revisit the events of earlier films in a "time heist"; Natasha Romanoff sacrifices herself for the Soul Stone, and Bruce Banner uses a new gauntlet to restore the vanished, at grievous cost. A past version of Thanos follows them to the present, leading to a climactic battle in which Tony Stark wields the Stones to destroy Thanos's army, dying in the act. Steve Rogers later returns the Stones and remains in the past, passing his shield to Sam Wilson.

Production

The film was shot back-to-back with Infinity War in Georgia under the working title Mary Lou, concluding principal photography in January 2018, with extensive visual effects by Industrial Light & Magic, Weta Digital and others. Alan Silvestri composed the score.

Reception

Endgame received praise for its direction, acting, emotional weight and culmination of the MCU's 22-film "Infinity Saga". It set numerous box-office records, including the largest opening weekend worldwide, and surpassed Avatar to become the highest-grossing film of all time from July 2019 until March 2021, when an Avatar re-release reclaimed the title. It won awards including Critics' Choice and People's Choice honours and was nominated for Best Visual Effects at the Academy Awards.
