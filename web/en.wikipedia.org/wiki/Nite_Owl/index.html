Nite Owl

Nite Owl is the name of two fictional superheroes in the graphic novel Watchmen (1986–87) by Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons, published by DC Comics. The original Nite Owl was Hollis Mason, and his successor was Daniel Dreiberg.

Hollis Mason

Hollis T. Mason, a former police officer, became the first Nite Owl in 1939 after reading about Hooded Justice, joining the Minutemen. He fought crime with fists and gadgets rather than powers, retired in 1962, and wrote an autobiography, Under the Hood. He is murdered by a gang in the course of the story.

Daniel Dreiberg

Daniel Dreiberg, a wealthy, mild-mannered ornithology enthusiast, took up the mantle with Mason's blessing. As a member of the Crimebusters he operated the owl-themed airship "Archie" and partnered with Rorschach. In the story he forms a relationship with Laurie Juspeczyk (Silk Spectre II) and helps uncover Adrian Veidt's plot.

In other media

Both characters appear in the 2009 film adaptation Watchmen and the 2019 HBO series features Dreiberg's legacy; the prequel comics Before Watchmen include a Nite Owl miniseries.
