Tiruppavai

The Tiruppavai is a collection of thirty Tamil verses (pasurams) composed by the Alvar saint-poet Andal (Kodhai), believed to have lived in the 8th or 9th century CE. It forms part of the Nachiar Tirumoli-era devotional corpus within the Nalayira Divya Prabandham.

Content

In the poems, Andal imagines herself as a gopi of Ayarpadi (Gokulam), inviting her friends in the pre-dawn hours of the Tamil month of Margazhi to observe a vow (pavai nonbu) and seek Krishna. The verses combine vivid descriptions of dawn, village life and nature with deep Vaishnava theology.

Practice

The Tiruppavai is recited daily in Vishnu temples and homes during Margazhi (mid-December to mid-January). It has generated extensive commentaries, beginning with Periyavachan Pillai, and is central to Sri Vaishnava devotion.
