List of districts in India

A district (zilla) is an administrative division of an Indian state or union territory. Districts are further subdivided into sub-divisions, tehsils (taluks) and blocks. Each district is headed by a District Collector (also called District Magistrate or Deputy Commissioner), an officer of the Indian Administrative Service, responsible for administration, revenue and law and order coordination.

The number of districts has grown steadily since independence as states subdivide large districts for administrative convenience: there were 310 districts at the 1961 census, 640 at the 2011 census, and more than 780 by the mid-2020s. Uttar Pradesh has the most districts of any state (75), followed by Madhya Pradesh.
