QantasLink

QantasLink is a regional airline brand of Qantas, Australia's flag carrier, under which regional flights are operated by partner and subsidiary airlines including Sunstate Airlines, Eastern Australia Airlines, National Jet Systems and Alliance Airlines.

Operations

QantasLink services connect major Australian cities with regional destinations across Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria, Tasmania, the Northern Territory and Western Australia. Operations began under the brand in 2002, consolidating Qantas's regional subsidiaries under a single identity.

Fleet

The fleet has centred on the Bombardier Dash 8 Q400 turboprop, alongside Fokker 100 and Boeing 717 jets for larger regional routes, and Airbus A320s for resources-sector charter flying in Western Australia. The Dash 8 fleet is being replaced by new Airbus A220 aircraft operated under the QantasLink banner.
