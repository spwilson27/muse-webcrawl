Marv Wolfman

Marv Wolfman (born May 13, 1946) is an American comic book writer and editor. He is best known for his long runs on Marvel Comics' The Tomb of Dracula, for which he co-created Blade, and DC Comics' The New Teen Titans and the landmark limited series Crisis on Infinite Earths, both with artist George Pérez.

Career

A fan-turned-professional from Brooklyn, Wolfman wrote for DC in the late 1960s before becoming editor-in-chief of Marvel in the mid-1970s. On The Tomb of Dracula (1972–1979) with artist Gene Colan he created the vampire hunter Blade, later a film franchise star. With Pérez he launched The New Teen Titans (1980), creating Cyborg, Raven, and Starfire and reviving the team as one of DC's best-selling titles.

Crisis and later work

For DC's 50th anniversary, Wolfman wrote Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985–1986), the maxi-series that rebooted the DC Universe. He also created the villain Deathstroke the Terminator and the hero Nightwing's modern identity for Dick Grayson. Wolfman has since written novels (including the Crisis novelisation), animation, and video games, and famously won a share of credit disputes over characters he created at Marvel.
