Asian Civilisations Museum (ACM)
Location: 1 Empress Place, Singapore
Established: 1997
Type: History and art museum

The Asian Civilisations Museum (ACM) is an institution in Singapore which forms part of the four national museums of the country. It is one of the pioneering museums in the region to specialise in pan-Asian cultures and histories, and its collection spans China, Southeast Asia, South Asia, West Asia and the Islamic world.

Building

The museum occupies a neoclassical building on the north bank of the Singapore River, originally built in the 19th century as a courthouse. The museum opened in the building in 1997 and expanded with new wings in the 2010s.

Collections

Highlights include the Tang Shipwreck cargo — gold, silver and ceramic objects recovered from a 9th-century Arab dhow wrecked off Belitung Island — along with Peranakan jewellery, Buddhist sculpture and trade ceramics arranged in galleries exploring faith, trade and ideas across Asia.
