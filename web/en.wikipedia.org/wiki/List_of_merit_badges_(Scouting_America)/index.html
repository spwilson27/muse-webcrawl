List of merit badges (Scouting America)

Merit badges are awards earned by youth members of Scouting America (formerly the Boy Scouts of America) based on activities within a subject area, from camping and first aid to robotics and nuclear science. The programme began in 1911 with 57 badges; today there are more than 135. Eagle Scout candidates must earn at least 21 merit badges, including a set of required ("Eagle-required") badges recognisable by their silver borders; optional badges have green borders. Badges are worn on a sash or sleeve, and requirements are revised periodically; many early badges have been discontinued or renamed.

History

The first 57 merit badges were offered in 1911, covering subjects from agriculture and angling to taxidermy and stalking. Over the decades, badges have been added (for example Computers, later replaced by Digital Technology; Robotics in 2011; Game Design in 2013; Animation in 2015) and discontinued (such as Invention, whose single earners are legends of Scouting lore, and the short-lived 2010 Historical merit badge programme reviving four classic badges for the BSA centennial). Each badge requires the Scout to work with a registered merit badge counsellor and complete a published set of requirements, usually including both knowledge and practical demonstration.
