List of governors of Indiana

The governor of Indiana is the head of government of the US state of Indiana. Indiana was admitted to the Union on 11 December 1816, and its governors have served four-year terms since 1851 (previously three years), with a limit of two consecutive terms since 1972.

Territorial governors

Before statehood, the Indiana Territory was governed by William Henry Harrison (1801–1812), who later became the ninth president of the United States, followed by John Gibson (acting) and Thomas Posey (1813–1816).
