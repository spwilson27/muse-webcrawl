The 2028 Summer Paralympics, also known as the 18th Summer Paralympic Games and branded as LA28, are an upcoming international multisport event governed by the International Paralympic Committee scheduled to be held in Los Angeles, California, United States, from 23 August to 3 September 2028. It will mark the first time the city has hosted the Paralympic Games, and the third edition of the Games held in the United States after Atlanta 1996 and New York/Stoke Mandeville 1984.

Host selection

Los Angeles was awarded the Games as part of a dual allocation with the Paris 2024 Olympics and Paralympics agreed by the International Olympic Committee in September 2017. Los Angeles originally bid for the 2024 Games before agreeing to host the 2028 edition instead, receiving additional concessions and funding in exchange.

Venues

The Paralympics will use many of the venues built or renovated for the 2028 Summer Olympics. The Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum is slated to host the opening and closing ceremonies, while para athletics are planned for the Coliseum as well. Swimming events are planned for a temporary pool at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, and wheelchair basketball and other indoor sports will be staged in Downtown Los Angeles arenas including the Crypto.com Arena and the Los Angeles Convention Center.

Sports

The programme is expected to retain the 22 sports contested at Paris 2024, including wheelchair basketball, goalball, boccia, sitting volleyball, para swimming, para athletics, wheelchair rugby, para table tennis, para judo, para taekwondo, para triathlon, para cycling, para rowing, para canoeing, para powerlifting, para archery, para shooting, para badminton, para equestrian, wheelchair fencing, wheelchair tennis, and blind football. The organizing committee also proposed additional sports for consideration by the IPC.
