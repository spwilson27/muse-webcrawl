Sign of the horns

The sign of the horns is a hand gesture made by extending the index and little fingers while holding the middle and ring fingers down with the thumb. It has a long history in Mediterranean folk tradition, where it was used to ward off the evil eye, and in various religious contexts.

Meanings

Meanings vary widely by culture: in Italy the gesture (le corna) can be apotropaic (protective) or an insult implying cuckoldry. In Hindu and Buddhist traditions related mudras appear. In modern Western popular culture the gesture is most strongly associated with heavy metal music, popularised by singer Ronnie James Dio, who said he learned it from his Italian grandmother as protection against the evil eye.

In popular culture

The gesture appears at rock concerts worldwide and is often called the "devil horns" or "metal horns." A similar gesture with the thumb extended means "I love you" in American Sign Language.
