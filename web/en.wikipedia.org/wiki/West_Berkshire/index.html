West Berkshire

West Berkshire is a unitary authority area in the ceremonial county of Berkshire, England. Its administrative centre is Newbury, and other significant settlements include Thatcham, Hungerford, Pangbourne and Lambourn. The district covers much of the North Wessex Downs, an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty.

History

The district was formed on 1 April 1974, as Newbury District, from parts of the former municipal borough of Newbury and surrounding rural districts. It became a unitary authority on 1 April 1998, when Berkshire County Council was abolished, and was renamed West Berkshire.

Geography

The River Kennet and the Kennet and Avon Canal cross the district. The Berkshire Downs, with their chalk hills and racehorse training industry centred on Lambourn, occupy the north and west. Walbury Hill, at 297 metres, is the highest point in South East England. Sites of interest include Highclere Castle, Donnington Castle and the Atomic Weapons Establishment at Aldermaston.
