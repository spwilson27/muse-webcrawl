Euronext Amsterdam is a stock exchange based in Amsterdam, Netherlands, and part of the pan-European Euronext group. It is the successor to the Amsterdam Stock Exchange, widely regarded as the oldest "modern" securities market in the world.

History

The Amsterdam market traces its origins to 1602, when the Dutch East India Company (VOC) offered its shares to the public — the first formal stock exchange trading. A purpose-built exchange, the Beurs van Hendrick de Keyser, opened in 1611. In 2000 the Amsterdam exchange merged with the Brussels and Paris bourses to form Euronext.

Indices and trading

The benchmark index is the AEX, comprising the 25 most actively traded Dutch blue-chip companies, alongside the mid-cap AMX and small-cap AScX indices. Major listings include ASML, Shell, Unilever, ING and Prosus. Trading takes place on Euronext's Optiq electronic platform, shared across the group's markets.
