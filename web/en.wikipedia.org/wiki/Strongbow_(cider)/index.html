Strongbow is a dry cider produced by H. P. Bulmer in England. It is the world's leading cider brand, accounting for more than half of the cider sold in the United Kingdom. Bulmer's is a subsidiary of Heineken International, the multinational Dutch brewer that also owns the Irish cider brand Bulmers (sold as Magners outside Ireland).

Strongbow was launched in 1960 by Bulmers of Hereford, England, and named after the Anglo-Norman knight Richard de Clare, 2nd Earl of Pembroke, nicknamed "Strongbow". It is produced from a blend of bittersweet cider apples and culinary apples, and is sold as a draught product in pubs as well as in cans and bottles.

Production

Strongbow is made at Bulmer's cider mills in Hereford. The cider is made from a blend of more than 50 varieties of apple grown in orchards in Herefordshire and Kent. The apples are pressed, and the juice is fermented with a cultured yeast before being matured and blended. The finished cider is carbonated and, in draught form, served either chilled or "extra cold".

Variants

  • Strongbow Original – the standard dry cider, 4.5% ABV on draught.
  • Strongbow Dark Fruit – launched in 2014, flavoured with blackcurrant and blackberry; it became one of the fastest-growing cider brands in the UK.
  • Strongbow Cloudy Apple – a cloudy, more apple-forward variant.
  • Strongbow Rosé – a pink cider made with red-fleshed apples.

Marketing

Strongbow has been advertised with a string of campaigns centred on the "thwack" of two arrows striking a wooden bar and the rewards earned by hard-working men. It was for many years the sponsor of Leeds United Football Club and has sponsored the Edinburgh Festival Fringe.
