Morticia Addams (née Frump) is a fictional character from the Addams Family multimedia franchise, created by cartoonist Charles Addams. She is the elegant, serene matriarch of the family, the wife of Gomez Addams and mother of Wednesday and Pugsley. Her name derives from mort ("death"); Charles Addams found it in a list of undertakers' terminology.

Character

Morticia is tall, pale, and strikingly beautiful, with long straight black hair and a form-fitting black hobble dress of tattered, tentacle-like fabric that forces her to shuffle rather than walk. She is calm and soft-spoken, devoted to her husband — who is driven wild whenever she speaks French — and serenely indulgent of her children's macabre hobbies. Her pastimes include knitting, playing with her pet lion Kitty Kat, raising carnivorous plants, and cutting the roses off rose stems to keep the thorns, which she arranges in vases. She descends from a family with roots in Salem witchcraft traditions.

Appearances

The character first appeared unnamed in Charles Addams's single-panel cartoons in The New Yorker and was given her name for the 1964–66 television series, in which Carolyn Jones played her. Anjelica Huston portrayed Morticia in the films The Addams Family (1991) and Addams Family Values (1993). In the Netflix series Wednesday (2022– ), she is played by Catherine Zeta-Jones, now depicted as a celebrated alumna of Nevermore Academy with psychic abilities. Morticia has become an enduring icon of gothic elegance in popular culture and a fixture of Halloween costuming.
