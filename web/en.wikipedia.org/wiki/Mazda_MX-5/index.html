Mazda MX-5

Mazda MX-5 (Miata)
Manufacturer: Mazda
Production: 1989–present
Class: Roadster
Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive

The Mazda MX-5 is a lightweight two-seat roadster manufactured by Mazda. Known as the Miata in North America and the Roadster in Japan, it debuted at the 1989 Chicago Auto Show and is the best-selling two-seat sports car in history, with production passing one million units in 2016.

Design philosophy

The MX-5 was conceived as a modern interpretation of classic British lightweight roadsters, emphasising balance, simplicity and the principle of jinba ittai — horse and rider as one. Its front-engine, rear-drive layout and near 50:50 weight distribution give it responsive handling.

Generations

Four generations have been produced: the NA (1989–1997) with pop-up headlights, the NB (1998–2005), the NC (2006–2015) and the current ND (2016–present), which returned to lighter weight and is also the basis of the Fiat 124 Spider.

Motorsport

The car is popular in club racing and one-make series such as the Global MX-5 Cup, and has been called the most-raced sports car model in amateur motorsport.
