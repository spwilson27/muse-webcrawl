Republic of Mountainous Armenia

The Republic of Mountainous Armenia (Armenian: Լեռնահայաստանի Հանրապետություն) was an unrecognized Armenian state proclaimed by the military commander Garegin Nzhdeh in the region of Syunik (Zangezur) on 26 April 1921, after the collapse of the First Republic of Armenia and its Sovietization.

History

Following an anti-Bolshevik uprising in February 1921 and fighting over the strategically important Zangezur region, Nzhdeh's forces held the mountainous south against both Soviet and Azerbaijani claims. The republic controlled territory corresponding roughly to modern Syunik Province and parts of neighbouring regions.

In July 1921, after the Soviet government pledged that Zangezur would remain part of Soviet Armenia, Nzhdeh and his remaining forces withdrew into Persia, and the republic ceased to exist on 13 July 1921. Its stand is credited with keeping Syunik within Armenia.
