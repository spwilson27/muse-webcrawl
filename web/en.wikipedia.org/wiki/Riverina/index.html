Riverina
State: New South Wales, Australia
Major centres: Wagga Wagga, Griffith, Albury, Deniliquin
Known for: Irrigated agriculture, wine, rice
Major rivers: Murray, Murrumbidgee, Lachlan

The Riverina is an agricultural region in south-western New South Wales, Australia, centred on the floodplains of the Murray, Murrumbidgee, and Lachlan rivers. It is one of Australia's most productive farming regions, known especially for irrigated agriculture, wine production, rice, citrus, and wool. The name derives from the Spanish word for a riverbank district.

Geography

The Riverina comprises the land between the Murray River to the south (forming the border with Victoria) and the Lachlan River to the north. Its major urban centres include Wagga Wagga (the largest inland city in New South Wales), Griffith, Leeton, Deniliquin, Albury, Narrandera, and Hay. The region's flat plains and river systems made it suitable for large-scale irrigation schemes, most notably the Murrumbidgee Irrigation Area, developed from the early 20th century following the construction of Burrinjuck Dam.

Agriculture

The region is sometimes called the "food bowl" of Australia. Irrigated crops include rice (the Riverina produces a large share of Australia's rice), citrus fruit, grapes for wine, vegetables, and cotton. Dryland areas support wheat, other cereals, and sheep grazing for wool and meat. The Riverina wine region is one of Australia's largest wine-producing areas by volume, known for bulk production as well as Botrytis Semillon dessert wines.

History

Before European settlement the Riverina was home to Aboriginal peoples including the Wiradjuri, the largest Aboriginal group in New South Wales. Pastoral settlement began in the 1830s and 1840s, and the area became a major wool-producing district served by river trade along the Murray and Murrumbidgee, with paddle steamers carrying wool to market. In the later 19th century a "Riverina separatist" movement periodically agitated for the region to become a separate colony or state, a sentiment that resurfaced in debates over Federation.
