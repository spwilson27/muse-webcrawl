Pepper Keenan

Pepper Keenan (born 8 May 1967) is an American guitarist and vocalist, best known as the frontman of the heavy metal band Corrosion of Conformity (COC). Born in Oxford, Mississippi, and raised in New Orleans, Louisiana, Keenan joined COC in 1989 as a rhythm guitarist before taking over lead vocals.

With Keenan as singer, Corrosion of Conformity moved from their hardcore punk roots toward a Southern and sludge metal sound on albums such as Blind (1991), Deliverance (1994), and Wiseblood (1996), which produced the well-known songs "Albatross", "Clean My Wounds", and "Vote with a Bullet".

Keenan is also a member of the supergroup Down, alongside Phil Anselmo and members of Crowbar, Eyehategod, and Pantera, with whom he has recorded albums including NOLA (1995). He contributed guest vocals to Metallica's Garage Inc. covers album. He returned to Corrosion of Conformity in the 2010s after a period in which the band performed as a trio.
