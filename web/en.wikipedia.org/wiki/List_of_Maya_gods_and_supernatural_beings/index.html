List of Maya gods and supernatural beings

This is a list of deities and supernatural beings in Maya mythology and religion. Maya religion was polytheistic, and its gods were often known by multiple names and aspects. Colonial sources such as the Popol Vuh, the Books of Chilam Balam, and the surviving Maya codices are key sources.

Major deities

NameDomain / description
ItzamnaCreator god, god of wisdom, writing and the heavens; chief of the pantheon
Kukulkan (Gukumatz, Quetzalcoatl)Feathered serpent god of wind and learning
Yum KaaxGod of wild plants and the forest, patron of hunters
ChaacRain god, wielder of lightning; among the most ancient and popular gods
Kinich AhauSun god
IxchelGoddess of the moon, childbirth, medicine and weaving
Yum Cimil (Ah Puch, Kisin)God of death and ruler of Xibalba's lowest level
Ek ChuajGod of merchants and cacao
Buluc Chabtan (God F)God of war, violence and sudden death
Hun HunahpuFather of the Hero Twins; maize god in some interpretations
Hunahpu and XbalanqueThe Hero Twins, ballplayers who defeated the lords of Xibalba
Xmucane and XpiacocGrandmother and grandfather gods, creators and diviners
Vucub Caquix (Seven Macaw)Vain bird demon defeated by the Hero Twins
ZipacnaDemonic caiman, maker of mountains
CabrakanGod of mountains and earthquakes
TohilGod of fire, patron of the K'iche'
AwilixGoddess associated with the moon, of the K'iche'
CamazotzBat god of Xibalba
Xibalba lordsHun Came, Vucub Came and the ten disease lords of the underworld
