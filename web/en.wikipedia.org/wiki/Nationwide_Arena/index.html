Nationwide Arena is a multi-purpose arena in Columbus, Ohio, United States. It opened in 2000 as the home of the Columbus Blue Jackets of the National Hockey League and anchor of the Arena District, a mixed-use development in downtown Columbus.

History

The arena was built by Nationwide Mutual Insurance Company after voters rejected a publicly funded arena proposal. It is regarded as a model of private financing for major-league sports venues. The building hosted the 2015 NHL All-Star Game and regularly stages concerts, basketball tournaments and other events.

Facilities

The arena seats about 19,500 for hockey and features a separate practice ice rink, making it one of the few NHL venues with an attached practice facility open to public skating.
