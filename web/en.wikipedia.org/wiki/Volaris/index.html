Volaris (legally Controladora Vuela Compañía de Aviación, S.A.B. de C.V.) is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline based in Santa Fe, Álvaro Obregón, Mexico City. It operates scheduled flights within Mexico and to the United States, Central America and South America, and is one of the largest airlines in Mexico by passengers carried.

History

The airline was founded in 2004 as Vuela Airlines with investment from, among others, the Discovery Americas fund and the Salvadoran airline TACA. It began operations in March 2006 with a flight between Toluca and Tijuana. Following the exit of Mexicana de Aviación in 2010, Volaris expanded rapidly at Mexico City International Airport.

In 2013 the company held its initial public offering on the Mexican Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange. Subsidiaries were later established abroad, including Volaris Costa Rica (2016) and Volaris El Salvador (2021).

Business model and fleet

Volaris follows the ultra-low-cost carrier model: high-density single-class seating, unbundled ancillary products and a young, fuel-efficient fleet composed entirely of Airbus A320 family aircraft, increasingly of the A320neo family, which the airline credits with among the lowest unit costs in the Americas.

Destinations

The airline serves dozens of domestic destinations in Mexico and international routes to the United States, Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Costa Rica, Colombia and Peru.
