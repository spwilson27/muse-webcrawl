The 2002 FIFA World Cup Final was the final match of the 2002 FIFA World Cup, played on 30 June 2002 at the International Stadium in Yokohama, Japan. Brazil defeated Germany 2–0 to win a record fifth World Cup title.

Match

After a goalless first half, Ronaldo — who had missed the 1998 final's impact through illness — scored twice in the second half, in the 67th and 79th minutes, converting a rebound from Oliver Kahn and finishing a move involving Rivaldo and Kléberson. Ronaldo finished as the tournament's top scorer with eight goals.

Significance

The final was the first World Cup meeting between Brazil and Germany, the two most successful national teams. Brazil remains the only team to have won five World Cups, and was the last South American winner until Argentina in 2022.
