Adam Peaty

Adam George Peaty (born 28 December 1994) is an English competitive swimmer specialising in breaststroke, widely regarded as the greatest breaststroker of all time. He is a three-time Olympic champion, having won the 100 metre breaststroke at the Rio 2016 and Tokyo 2020 Games — becoming the first British swimmer to retain an Olympic title — and a relay silver and gold medallist, with further medals at Paris 2024.

Career

Peaty broke the 100 m breaststroke world record repeatedly, becoming the first man to swim under 58 and then under 57 seconds (56.88 at the 2019 World Championships); he has also held the 50 m breaststroke world record. He won eight World Championship gold medals for Great Britain and multiple European and Commonwealth titles for England, dominating the event for nearly a decade. Coached by Mel Marshall in Loughborough and later Derby, Peaty has spoken openly about mental health, and was appointed OBE for services to swimming.
