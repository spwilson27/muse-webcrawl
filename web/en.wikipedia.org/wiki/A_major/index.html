A major is a major scale based on A, with the pitches A, B, C♯, D, E, F♯, and G♯. Its key signature has three sharps. Its relative minor is F-sharp minor and its parallel minor is A minor.

Characteristics

The A major scale is: A, B, C♯, D, E, F♯, G♯, A. In the guitar, A major is one of the most common keys because the open strings favour it and the dominant E major. For orchestral strings, A major is a bright key in which the open strings of the violin (G, D, A, E) resonate sympathetically.

Notable compositions

Famous works in A major include Mozart's Clarinet Concerto and Quintet, Beethoven's Symphony No. 7 (whose first movement is in A major), Mendelssohn's "Italian" Symphony No. 4, and Schubert's "Trout" Quintet.
