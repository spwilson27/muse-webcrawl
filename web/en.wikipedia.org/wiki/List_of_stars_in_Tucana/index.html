This is the list of notable stars in the constellation Tucana, sorted by decreasing brightness. Tucana is a southern constellation representing a toucan; it is best known for containing the Small Magellanic Cloud and the great globular cluster 47 Tucanae, which lies in the same line of sight.

Stars with exoplanets

HD 215497, an orange dwarf in Tucana, hosts two planets including a hot Neptune-mass world and a Saturn-mass planet in a wide orbit. HD 221287 (Bilbo) and HD 4308 also have confirmed planetary companions.
