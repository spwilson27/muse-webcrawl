GSC Game World
Founded: 1995; Kyiv, Ukraine
Founder: Sergiy Grygorovych
Industry: Video games
Key products: Cossacks series, S.T.A.L.K.E.R. series

GSC Game World is a Ukrainian video game developer based in Kyiv, founded in 1995 by Sergiy Grygorovych. It is best known for the Cossacks real-time strategy series and the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. first-person shooter series set in the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone.

History

Founded in 1995, GSC initially worked on game localization and multimedia encyclopaedias before releasing its own titles. Its breakthrough came with Cossacks: European Wars (2001), a real-time strategy game noted for supporting thousands of units on screen. S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl (2007), after a famously protracted development, became an international success and was followed by Clear Sky (2008) and Call of Pripyat (2009).

Closure and revival

In December 2011 GSC Game World was dissolved and development of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 was cancelled. The studio was revived in 2014 under Grygorovych's brother Evgeniy, releasing Cossacks 3 in 2016 and resuming work on S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl. Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, development was disrupted and partly relocated to Prague; S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 was released on 20 November 2024.
