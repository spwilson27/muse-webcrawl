The Jetsons is an American animated sitcom produced by Hanna-Barbera Productions. It originally aired in prime time on ABC from September 23, 1962, to March 17, 1963, as the futuristic counterpart to The Flintstones, and was revived with new episodes in syndication from 1985 to 1987.

Premise

Set in Orbit City in 2062, the series follows George Jetson, who works a three-hour day, three days a week for the blustering Mr. Spacely of Spacely Space Sprockets; his wife Jane; teenage daughter Judy; young son Elroy; their dog Astro; and Rosie, the family's ageing robot maid. The show imagined a world of flying cars, moving sidewalks, video calls, flat-screen televisions, robot servants and push-button convenience — much of it wryly anticipating real technology. Its only season in prime time was defeated in the ratings by competing programmes, but endless reruns in Saturday-morning slots made it a children's favourite and eventually justified the 1980s revival.

Legacy

The series remains American culture's shorthand for retro-futurism. It spawned specials, the theatrical Jetsons: The Movie (1990) — George O'Hanlon's final role — and crossovers with The Flintstones. "Meet George Jetson", its theme by Hoyt Curtin, is among the most recognisable in television animation.
