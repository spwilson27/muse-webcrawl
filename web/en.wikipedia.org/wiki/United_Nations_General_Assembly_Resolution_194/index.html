Key provisionsConciliation Commission for Palestine; access to holy places; refugee return or compensation (Article 11)

United Nations General Assembly Resolution 194 is a resolution adopted near the end of the 1948 Arab–Israeli War. It established the United Nations Conciliation Commission for Palestine, called for the demilitarization and internationalization of Jerusalem, guaranteed free access to holy places, and addressed the situation of the Palestinian refugees created by the war. It has been reaffirmed by the General Assembly more than one hundred times.

Article 11

The resolution is best known for its eleventh paragraph, which resolves "that the refugees wishing to return to their homes and live at peace with their neighbours should be permitted to do so at the earliest practicable date, and that compensation should be paid for the property of those choosing not to return and for loss of or damage to property which, under principles of international law or equity, should be made good by the Governments or authorities responsible."

Palestinian representatives and many legal scholars cite Article 11 as the foundation of a Palestinian "right of return," while successive Israeli governments have maintained that the resolution is non-binding, conditional on refugees living "at peace with their neighbours," and intended to be implemented within a comprehensive political settlement rather than unilaterally. The Conciliation Commission's efforts, including the 1949 Lausanne Conference, failed to produce agreement, and the refugee question remains a core issue of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.

Other provisions

The resolution also instructed the Conciliation Commission to facilitate the repatriation, resettlement, and economic and social rehabilitation of the refugees, and it reiterated plans for Jerusalem to be placed under effective United Nations control as a corpus separatum, as envisaged by Resolution 181. The following year, Resolution 302 founded the United Nations Relief and Works Agency (UNRWA) to provide direct assistance to the refugees.
