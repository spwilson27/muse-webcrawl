The President of Croatia, officially the President of the Republic of Croatia (Croatian: Predsjednik Republike Hrvatske), is the head of state of the Republic of Croatia. The president is the commander-in-chief of the Croatian Armed Forces and holds the principal responsibility for representing Croatia abroad. Croatia is a parliamentary republic, so most executive power rests with the Prime Minister and the Government, while the president exercises comparatively limited, largely ceremonial powers.

The president is directly elected by universal suffrage for a term of five years and may be re-elected once. Elections use a two-round system: if no candidate wins an absolute majority in the first round, a run-off is held between the two leading candidates two weeks later. The official residence is the Presidential Palace (Predsjednički dvori) in Pantovčak, Zagreb.

Powers and duties

Under the Constitution of Croatia, the president represents the republic at home and abroad, ensures the regular and balanced functioning of state authority, calls elections for the Croatian Parliament (Sabor), and mandates a person to form a government following elections. The president appoints and recalls ambassadors (on the government's proposal), grants pardons and awards decorations, and may dissolve parliament in narrowly defined circumstances. As commander-in-chief, the president appoints and relieves military commanders in accordance with law.

History

The office was created by the Constitution adopted on 22 December 1990, shortly before Croatia's independence from Yugoslavia. The first president was Franjo Tuđman, who dominated Croatian politics throughout the 1990s and died in office in December 1999. Under Tuđman the system was semi-presidential, with the president wielding broad executive authority. Constitutional amendments in 2000–2001, under President Stjepan Mesić, shifted power decisively to parliament and the government, converting Croatia into a parliamentary system. Subsequent presidents include Ivo Josipović (2010–2015) and Kolinda Grabar-Kitarović (2015–2020), the first woman to hold the office. Zoran Milanović, a former prime minister, won the 2019–20 election and took office on 18 February 2020.

List of presidents

Presidents of the Republic of Croatia since 1990: Franjo Tuđman (1990–1999), Vlatko Pavletić (acting, 1999–2000), Zlatko Tomčić (acting, 2000), Stjepan Mesić (2000–2010), Ivo Josipović (2010–2015), Kolinda Grabar-Kitarović (2015–2020), and Zoran Milanović (2020–present).
