CD117

Also known as: KIT, c-Kit
Type: Receptor tyrosine kinase
Gene: KIT (chromosome 4q12)
Ligand: Stem cell factor

CD117 is a cell-surface receptor encoded by the KIT gene, also known as c-Kit. It is a type III receptor tyrosine kinase whose ligand is stem cell factor (SCF).

Function

KIT signalling is essential for the survival, proliferation and differentiation of haematopoietic stem cells, mast cells, melanocytes, germ cells and the interstitial cells of Cajal in the gut. Loss-of-function mutations cause piebaldism in humans, while activating mutations drive tumours.

Clinical significance

CD117 is characteristically expressed in gastrointestinal stromal tumours (GIST), which are treated with the tyrosine kinase inhibitor imatinib. It is also a marker in acute myeloid leukaemia, mastocytosis and seminoma, and is widely used in diagnostic immunohistochemistry.
