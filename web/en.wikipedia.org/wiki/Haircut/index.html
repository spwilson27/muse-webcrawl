Haircut

A haircut is the styling of hair by cutting, trimming or shaving, performed by barbers and hairdressers or by individuals themselves. Haircuts vary widely by culture, fashion, gender norms, religion and profession.

Styles

Common styles include the bob, pixie cut, buzz cut, crew cut, fade, undercut, Mohawk, mullet, bowl cut, French crop, quiff, and long layered styles. Military and institutional haircuts emphasise uniformity and hygiene.

Tools and process

Tools include scissors, electric clippers, razors, thinning shears and combs. A typical salon haircut includes washing, cutting, styling and finishing products.

Cultural significance

Hair has symbolic meaning in many religions and societies: tonsure in monastic traditions, uncut hair in Sikhism, shaving in mourning or military rites, and fashionable cuts as expressions of identity, rebellion or conformity.
