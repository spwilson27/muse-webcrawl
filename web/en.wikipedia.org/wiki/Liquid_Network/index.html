Liquid Network

The Liquid Network is a Bitcoin layer-2 sidechain developed by Blockstream, launched in 2018. It enables fast, confidential settlement of bitcoin and the issuance of other digital assets, such as stablecoins and security tokens, primarily for exchanges, brokers and financial institutions.

Design

Liquid is a federated sidechain: bitcoin are moved onto the network through a two-way peg operated by a federation of functionaries, who also produce blocks roughly every minute using the Strong Federations consensus protocol. The chain's native asset, Liquid Bitcoin (L-BTC), is backed one-to-one by bitcoin held by the federation. Liquid supports Confidential Transactions, which hide amounts (and optionally asset types) from third parties, and atomic swaps between issued assets.

Use and criticism

Member exchanges use Liquid for rapid inter-exchange transfers and arbitrage settlement. Critics note that the federated model requires trusting the federation members, trading some of Bitcoin's decentralisation for speed and features.
