Shen Yun

Shen Yun Performing Arts is a United States-based non-profit performing arts and entertainment company that tours internationally, presenting dance performances and symphony concerts whose stated mission is to revive "the essence of Chinese culture as it was before the Communist revolution." Shen Yun was founded in 2006 in New York by practitioners of the Falun Gong new religious movement. Its productions combine classical Chinese dance, ethnic and folk dances, story-based dances, bel canto solos, and an orchestra blending Chinese and Western instruments, performed in front of a patented interactive digital backdrop.

Shen Yun's productions feature classical Chinese dance with large digital backdrops.

Background

The company was established in 2006 in Orange County, New York, where its headquarters, studios, and training schools (Fei Tian Academy of the Arts and Fei Tian College) are based at the Dragon Springs Temple complex in Deerpark, the seat of Falun Gong's founder Li Hongzhi. Several touring companies — eight by the early 2020s — perform a new production each year, visiting more than 100 cities worldwide with the slogan "China before communism."

Content and themes

Programmes typically consist of about twenty short pieces: opening and closing ensemble dances in Tang- and Qing-dynasty court style, folk and ethnic dances, solo songs, and story dances drawn from Chinese history and literature such as Journey to the West and Romance of the Three Kingdoms. A recurring element of each show depicts the persecution of Falun Gong practitioners in contemporary China and divine salvation themes reflecting Falun Gong beliefs; China has banned the shows and pressured foreign theatres to cancel them, which Shen Yun presents as foreign interference.

Reception

Critics have frequently praised the athleticism of the dancers, costumes, and production spectacle, while debate has centred on the overt religious and political content not always expected by audiences drawn by its extensive street advertising. In 2024, reporting by The New York Times described harsh working conditions for young performers at the company's compound — allegations Shen Yun disputed — prompting public debate and official inquiries.
