Munhwa Broadcasting Corporation

MBC (Munhwa Broadcasting Corporation)
Founded: 1961 (radio); TV from 1969
Headquarters: Sangam-dong, Mapo District, Seoul
Type: Public broadcaster
Channels: MBC TV, MBC Radio, FM4U

The Munhwa Broadcasting Corporation (MBC; Korean: 문화방송주식회사) is one of South Korea's major public broadcasters, operating a national television channel (channel 11 in Seoul), radio networks, and regional stations. "Munhwa" means "culture".

History

MBC began as a radio service in Busan and Seoul in 1961 and launched television broadcasts in 1969. Forced into public ownership under the Chun Doo-hwan government's media consolidation of 1980, it is today mostly owned by the Foundation for Broadcast Culture, with the remainder held by the Jeongsu Scholarship Foundation. Since 2014 its headquarters have been in the Digital Media City district of Sangam, Seoul.

Programming

MBC is known for landmark Korean dramas such as Dae Jang Geum (Jewel in the Palace), influential news magazine PD Note, and the long-running variety phenomenon Infinite Challenge (2005–2018). Along with KBS and SBS, it is one of the three dominant terrestrial networks driving the Korean Wave (Hallyu).
