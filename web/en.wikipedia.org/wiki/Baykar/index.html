Baykar

Baykar (Turkish: Baykar Makina Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş.) is a Turkish private defence company specialising in unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), command-and-control systems, and artificial intelligence for aviation. Founded in 1984 by Özdemir Bayraktar as a manufacturer of precision components for the automotive industry, the company later shifted its focus to unmanned aviation and became Turkey's leading developer of armed drones.

History

Baykar was founded in 1984 in Istanbul. In the 2000s, under the technical leadership of Selçuk Bayraktar, the company began developing indigenous UAV systems for the Turkish Armed Forces. Its Bayraktar Mini UAV, introduced in 2007, was the first indigenous mini-class unmanned system to enter Turkish service. The tactical-class Bayraktar TB2, which first flew in 2014, achieved wide international prominence through its use in conflicts in Syria, Libya, Nagorno-Karabakh, and Ukraine.

Following the death of founder Özdemir Bayraktar in 2021, his sons Haluk Bayraktar (chief executive) and Selçuk Bayraktar (chief technology officer) continued to lead the company. Baykar became Turkey's largest defence exporter, with UAVs sold to more than thirty countries.

Products

Baykar's product line includes the Bayraktar Akıncı high-altitude long-endurance combat drone, the Bayraktar TB2 and its naval TB3 variant, the Bayraktar Kızılelma unmanned fighter aircraft, the Mini UAV, and the Cezeri flying car prototype. The company also develops the Akıncı and TB2's supporting ground control stations, simulators, and payload systems.
