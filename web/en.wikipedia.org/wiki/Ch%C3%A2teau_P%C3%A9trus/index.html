Château Pétrus

Pétrus, commonly called Château Pétrus, is a Bordeaux wine estate located in the Pomerol appellation near its eastern border with Saint-Émilion. A small estate of about 11.5 hectares, it produces a red wine made almost entirely from Merlot grapes grown on distinctive iron-rich clay soil. Pétrus is widely regarded as one of the most celebrated and expensive wines in the world.

AppellationPomerol
AreaAbout 11.5 ha

History

The estate was owned by the Arnaud family from the 18th century; in the 20th century the Loubat family raised its reputation, and the négociant house Jean-Pierre Moueix acquired exclusive distribution rights in 1945 and full ownership by 1969. Under Christian Moueix the wine's quality and fame grew further. The château received no official classification, as Pomerol has never been classified, yet its prices rival or exceed those of the Bordeaux first growths.
