The New Mutants is a superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Bob McLeod, the team first appeared in Marvel Graphic Novel #4 (1982) and was given its own ongoing series, The New Mutants, in 1983. The group consists of teenage mutant students at Professor Charles Xavier's school — a junior counterpart to the adult X-Men — whose stories emphasize adolescence, friendship, and coming of age alongside superhero adventure.

Publication history

Conceived as a younger generation of X-Men, the original lineup comprised five teenagers from around the world: Cannonball (Sam Guthrie) from Kentucky, Karma (Xi'an Coy Manh) from Vietnam, Mirage (Danielle Moonstar), a Cheyenne, Sunspot (Roberto da Costa) from Brazil, and Wolfsbane (Rahne Sinclair) from Scotland. Later members included Magik (Illyana Rasputin), Magma, Cypher, and Warlock. The series is fondly remembered for Bill Sienkiewicz's experimental art on "The Demon Bear Saga" (1984). In the 1990s the team was relaunched as the paramilitary X-Force under Cable's leadership. A later teen series, New Mutants vol. 2 (2003), introduced a new generation (including Elixir and Wind Dancer) and was relaunched as New X-Men: Academy X. The original team reunited for a third volume in 2009, and the characters featured prominently in the Krakoa-era X-Men titles beginning in 2019.

In other media

The team was adapted into the film The New Mutants (2020), a horror-influenced superhero movie starring Blu Hunt as Mirage, Maisie Williams as Wolfsbane, Anya Taylor-Joy as Magik, Charlie Heaton as Cannonball, and Henry Zaga as Sunspot. The film, the final entry in Fox's X-Men film series, follows the young mutants held in a secret facility as they confront manifestations of their fears.
