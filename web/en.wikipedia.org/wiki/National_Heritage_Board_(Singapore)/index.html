National Heritage Board (Singapore)

Formed: August 1, 1993
Headquarters: Singapore
Parent: Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth
Type: Statutory board

The National Heritage Board (NHB) is a statutory board of the Government of Singapore under the Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth, responsible for safeguarding and promoting the nation's heritage.

Role

Formed in 1993, the NHB oversees Singapore's national museums and heritage institutions, including the National Museum of Singapore, the Asian Civilisations Museum, the Peranakan Museum and the National Gallery's predecessor institutions' heritage functions, as well as heritage centres in the cultural precincts.

Programmes

The board designates and maintains national monuments jointly with the Urban Redevelopment Authority's conservation programme, runs the Singapore Heritage Festival, develops heritage trails across the island's neighbourhoods, and collects oral histories and artefacts documenting Singapore's multicultural past.
