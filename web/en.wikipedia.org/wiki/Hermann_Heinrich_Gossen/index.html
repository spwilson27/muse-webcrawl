Hermann Heinrich Gossen

Born7 September 1810, Düren, France (now Germany)
Died13 February 1858, Cologne
FieldEconomics
Known forGossen's laws, marginal utility

Hermann Heinrich Gossen (1810–1858) was a German economist and Prussian civil servant who anticipated marginal utility theory in his 1854 book Entwickelung der Gesetze des menschlichen Verkehrs.

Gossen's laws

His first law states diminishing marginal utility: each successive unit of a good yields less additional satisfaction. His second law states that utility is maximised when the last unit of money spent on each good yields equal marginal utility — an early statement of equimarginal allocation.

Reception

The book sold poorly and Gossen withdrew it; he died obscure. After William Stanley Jevons and Léon Walras developed marginalism independently, Jevons acknowledged Gossen's priority, and the work was republished in 1889.
