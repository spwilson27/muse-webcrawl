Drone metal

Drone metal (also known as drone doom) is a style of heavy metal that melds the lengthy songs and slow tempos of doom metal with the sustained tones of drone music. It is typified by very slow, heavily distorted guitars played at high volume, minimal melody, and an emphasis on physical, resonant sound. Earth is generally regarded as the founding band of the genre.

Characteristics

Drone metal songs often stretch beyond ten minutes, built around slow, downtuned riffs repeated with minimal variation. Volume itself is an instrument: bands such as Sunn O))) perform through massive amplifier stacks, producing sound felt physically by the audience. Vocals, when present, may be growled, chanted or absent entirely.

History

Earth, formed by Dylan Carlson in Seattle in 1989, pioneered the sound with Earth 2: Special Low Frequency Version (1993), an album of extremely slow, minimal guitar drone. Boris, Sunn O))), Nadja and Khanate further developed the style in the 1990s and 2000s, sometimes intersecting with post-metal and dark ambient.
