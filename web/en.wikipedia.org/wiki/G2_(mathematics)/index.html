G2 (mathematics)

In mathematics, G2 is the name of several related structures: the smallest of the exceptional simple Lie groups, its Lie algebra, and the corresponding algebraic group. The group G2 has dimension 14 and rank 2, and can be realized as the automorphism group of the octonions.

Properties

G2 is one of five exceptional simple Lie groups, along with F4, E6, E7 and E8. Its root system consists of 12 roots in two lengths, with the characteristic 30° angle between the simple roots and a root-string pattern reflecting the factor √3 between long and short roots. The group acts naturally on the imaginary octonions, preserving their multiplication, which embeds G2 in SO(7). Its smallest nontrivial representation is 7-dimensional, and the adjoint representation is 14-dimensional.

Applications

Manifolds with holonomy group G2 play an important role in string theory and M-theory compactifications, since they yield supersymmetric vacua in seven dimensions.
