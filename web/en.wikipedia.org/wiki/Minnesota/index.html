Overview

Minnesota is a state in the Upper Midwest of the United States, bordering Canada and Lake Superior. It is known as the Land of 10,000 Lakes (it has more than 11,800 lakes over ten acres), the source of the Mississippi River at Lake Itasca, and for the Twin Cities of Minneapolis and Saint Paul, its metropolitan core. It became the 32nd state in 1858.

Geography and character

The state spans prairie in the south and west, deciduous forest, and the northern boreal forest and Boundary Waters canoe wilderness. Iron mining in the Mesabi Range ("the Range"), agriculture, medical technology (Mayo Clinic at Rochester) and retail headquarters anchor its economy. Minnesota has a strong Scandinavian and German heritage, a distinctive accent in popular imagination, and a tradition of liberal politics — its Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party is unique among US state parties. Winter cold is a defining fact; the Twin Cities are among the coldest major metros in the country.
