Taleh

Taleh (Somali: Taleex) is a historic town in the Sool region of northern Somalia, in the area claimed by the SSC-Khatumo administration. It is best known as the former capital of the Dervish movement led by Mohammed Abdullah Hassan.

History

From about 1910 Taleh served as the headquarters of the Dervish state, which resisted British, Ethiopian, and Italian encroachment in the Horn of Africa. The town was ringed with a complex of stone forts, the largest being the circular Silsilad fortress, among the most impressive monuments of the Somali interior. In February 1920 British forces, using RAF aircraft in one of the earliest aerial campaigns in Africa, bombed Taleh and forced the Dervishes to abandon it, bringing the movement to an end later that year.

Today

Taleh remains a small district centre. Its Dervish-era fortifications are a prominent heritage site, and the town was an early focal point of the Khatumo movement seeking a federal state for the Dhulbahante regions.
