Duke of Rutland is a title in the Peerage of England, created in 1703 for John Manners, 9th Earl of Rutland. The Manners family seat is Belvoir Castle in Leicestershire, overlooking the Vale of Belvoir. The family also owns Haddon Hall in Derbyshire.

History

The Manners family rose under the Tudors through marriage to the Ros family heiress. John Manners, 3rd Duke, was a prominent politician; John Manners, 5th Duke, was a leading figure in Regency society and presided over the rebuilding of Belvoir Castle in the Gothic Revival style after a fire in 1816.

The current holder is David Manners, 11th Duke of Rutland. The heir apparent is Charles Manners, Marquess of Granby.
