Dehradun district

Dehradun district is a district of Uttarakhand in northern India, with its headquarters in the city of Dehradun. It includes the hill towns of Mussoorie and Landour and the pilgrimage and river town of Rishikesh's surroundings in part.

Geography

The district spans the Doon Valley and Himalayan foothills, drained by tributaries of the Ganges and Yamuna.

Administration

It is divided into tehsils including Dehradun, Rishikesh, Vikasnagar, Doiwala, Chakrata and Kalsi.

Demographics

The population exceeds 1.6 million according to the 2011 census, with Hindi and Garhwali widely spoken.
