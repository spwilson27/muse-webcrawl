Belgium national football team records and statistics

This article lists the records and statistics of the Belgium national football team, nicknamed the Red Devils. Belgium played its first official international in 1904 and is one of the founding members of FIFA. Its finest results are third place at the 2018 FIFA World Cup, runners-up at UEFA Euro 1980, and the gold medal at the 1920 Olympic Games in Antwerp.

Romelu Lukaku is Belgium's all-time leading scorer by a wide margin; a controversy long surrounded his tally because three goals scored in a 2014 friendly against Luxembourg were initially not recognised by FIFA owing to an excess of substitutions, though they were later counted.

Team records

Belgium's biggest victory is 10–1, achieved against San Marino in 2001, and also 9–0 wins recorded against Zambia (1994), Gibraltar (2017) and San Marino (2019). Its heaviest defeats include an 11–2 loss to England amateurs in 1909 (not always counted as a full international) and 7–0 defeats to England and Sweden in the early twentieth century. Belgium held the number one position in the FIFA World Ranking from late 2018 to early 2022 despite winning no major trophy in that span, the longest such tenure by a team without a world or continental title. The team's longest unbeaten run exceeded thirty matches under coach Roberto Martínez.

Competition summary

Belgium has qualified for the World Cup more than a dozen times (1930–2022), with its best finishes fourth in 1986 and third in 2018. At the European Championship it reached the final in 1980 (losing 2–1 to West Germany) and the semi-finals in 1972. Belgium won the 1920 Olympic football tournament, after the final against Czechoslovakia was abandoned when the Czechs walked off in protest.
