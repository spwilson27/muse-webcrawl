Blackstar (stylised as ★) is the twenty-sixth and final studio album by the English musician David Bowie. It was released on 8 January 2016, Bowie's 69th birthday, through ISO, RCA, and Columbia Records. Bowie died of liver cancer two days after its release, having kept his illness secret; the album was subsequently read as his farewell work, full of meditations on mortality. Blackstar topped charts in more than 30 countries, was Bowie's only album to reach number one on the US Billboard 200, and won five Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year nominations-era honours and Best Alternative Music Album.

Recording

Bowie recorded the album in secret at the Magic Shop and Human Worldwide studios in New York City with longtime producer Tony Visconti. Rejecting a rock backing band, he recruited local jazz musicians — saxophonist Donny McCaslin, drummer Mark Guiliana, pianist Jason Lindner, bassist Tim Lefebvre, and guitarist Ben Monder — after hearing McCaslin's quartet perform. LCD Soundsystem's James Murphy contributed percussion. Influences included Kendrick Lamar's To Pimp a Butterfly, Death Grips, and Boards of Canada.

Songs

The ten-minute title track, released as a single in November 2015 with an acclaimed Johan Renck video, moves from ritualistic chanting through a jazz midsection. Other tracks include " 'Tis a Pity She Was a Whore," "Lazarus" (written for Bowie's off-Broadway musical of the same name; its video shows Bowie blindfolded on a hospital bed), "Sue (Or in a Season of Crime)," "Girl Loves Me" (sung partly in Nadsat and Polari), "Dollar Days," and the closing "I Can't Give Everything Away," widely interpreted as a goodbye.

Artwork

The cover, designed by Jonathan Barnbrook, is dominated by a large black star — the first Bowie album cover not to feature his image. The star is a visual pun on "black star," a term also associated with a lesion of cancer, and the segments beneath form stylised letters spelling "BOWIE." When the vinyl gatefold is exposed to light or viewed under blacklight, hidden images appear, including a starfield.

Reception

Critics hailed Blackstar as one of Bowie's finest works, praising its daring jazz instrumentation and emotional weight. Following his death, sales surged worldwide. At the 59th Grammy Awards in 2017 the album won all five categories in which it was nominated.
