Dehesa (agriculture)

A dehesa is a multifunctional agro-sylvo-pastoral system in Spain and Portugal (where it is called montado), combining oak parkland with livestock grazing, crops and forestry.

Ecology

Holm oaks and cork oaks provide acorns for Iberian pigs, shade, cork and habitat for wildlife including Iberian lynx and Spanish imperial eagle.

Products

Dehesas produce jamón ibérico, beef, sheep, honey, firewood and cork on poor soils unsuited to intensive farming.

Threats

Oak disease, ageing trees, overgrazing and economic pressure threaten regeneration of the system.
