The Czech Air Force (Czech: Vzdušné síly Armády České republiky, literally "Air Forces of the Army of the Czech Republic") is the air force branch of the Army of the Czech Republic. Together with the Land Forces, it forms the main combat element of the Czech armed forces. The modern air force dates to the dissolution of Czechoslovakia on 1 January 1993, when the Czechoslovak Air Force was divided between the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

A Saab JAS 39 Gripen of the Czech Air Force.

History

Czechoslovak military aviation began in 1918, and Czechoslovak airmen served with distinction in the Royal Air Force during the Second World War. After 1948 the air force operated Soviet-built aircraft as part of the Warsaw Pact. Following the Velvet Revolution and the split of Czechoslovakia in 1993, the Czech Republic inherited roughly two-thirds of the former federal air force's equipment. The Czech Republic joined NATO in 1999.

Supersonic MiG-21s were retired in 2005 and replaced by leased Swedish Saab JAS 39 Gripen fighters. The Czech government later decided to purchase 24 F-35A Lightning II aircraft from the United States, with deliveries planned from the early 2030s.

Structure and bases

The principal bases of the Czech Air Force include:

  • 21st Tactical Air Force Base at Čáslav — JAS 39 Gripen fighters and L-159 ALCA light combat aircraft.
  • 22nd Helicopter Air Force Base at Náměšť nad Oslavou — Mi-171Sh helicopters and, more recently, AH-1Z Viper and UH-1Y Venom helicopters replacing Soviet types.
  • 24th Air Transportation Base at Prague-Kbely — transport aircraft including Airbus A319CJ and CASA C-295.
  • Training is conducted at Pardubice by the state-owned Flight Training Centre (CLV) using L-39NG and other aircraft.

Aircraft

The inventory includes JAS 39C/D Gripen fighters, Aero L-159 ALCA light attack jets, CASA C-295 transports, Airbus A319CJ VIP aircraft, Mil Mi-171Sh transport helicopters, PZL W-3 Sokół helicopters, and the newly delivered Bell AH-1Z and UH-1Y helicopters. The L-39NG is entering service for training and light combat roles.
