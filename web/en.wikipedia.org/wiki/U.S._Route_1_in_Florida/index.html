U.S. Route 1 in Florida

The Florida section of U.S. Route 1 (US 1) runs along the Atlantic coast of the state from its national southern terminus in Key West to the Georgia state line north of Jacksonville. It is the main street of many of Florida's Atlantic coastal cities, passing through Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Daytona Beach, and Jacksonville, and across the Florida Keys on the Overseas Highway.

Route description

US 1 begins in Key West and follows the Overseas Highway across the chain of bridges linking the Florida Keys to the mainland at Florida City. Through Miami-Dade County it is known as the Dixie Highway and Biscayne Boulevard, and further north it serves as a commercial arterial through Broward and Palm Beach counties. North of West Palm Beach it passes through the Treasure Coast, the Space Coast near Cape Canaveral, and Daytona Beach before reaching Jacksonville, where it crosses the St. Johns River and continues north to Georgia.

History

The highway incorporates parts of the historic Dixie Highway and Henry Flagler's Overseas Railroad alignment. The Overseas Highway, completed in 1938 using the railroad's bridges after the Labor Day Hurricane of 1935 destroyed the line, made Key West reachable by car and gave US 1 its island-hopping southernmost stretch, including the famous Seven Mile Bridge.
