Girl Scout Cookies

Thin Mints are the best-selling Girl Scout cookie variety

Girl Scout Cookies are cookies sold by the Girl Scouts of the USA as one of its main fundraisers. Cookie sales began in 1917, when the Mistletoe Troop in Muskogee, Oklahoma, baked and sold cookies in its high school cafeteria; from 1922 a standard sugar-cookie recipe was published in the organisation's magazine, and commercial bakers began producing the cookies in the 1930s.

Varieties

The best-known varieties include Thin Mints (the top seller), Samoas (also sold as Caramel deLites), Tagalongs (Peanut Butter Patties), Do-si-dos (Peanut Butter Sandwich), Trefoils (shortbread), Lemon-Ups, and Adventurefuls. Two licensed bakers, ABC Bakers and Little Brownie Bakers, produce the cookies, so some varieties and names differ by region. New varieties are periodically introduced and retired — the raspberry-filled Raspberry Rally was introduced in 2023 as an online-only cookie.

The programme

Sales run mainly from January to April. Girl Scouts sell door-to-door, at booths outside shops, and (since 2014) through the Digital Cookie online platform. Proceeds fund troop activities, camps, and council programmes, and the programme is presented as teaching goal-setting, money management and business skills. Around 200 million boxes are sold annually in the United States.
