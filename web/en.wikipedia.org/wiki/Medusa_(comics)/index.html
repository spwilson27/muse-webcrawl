Medusa (comics)

Medusa (Medusalith Amaquelin-Boltagon) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, she first appeared in Fantastic Four #36 (March 1965). Medusa is queen of the Inhumans and wife (and first cousin) of Black Bolt, and is known for her long, prehensile red hair, which she can control psychokinetically.

Fictional character biography

Medusalith Amaquelin was born into the Inhuman royal family on the island city of Attilan. Exposed to the mutagenic Terrigen Mist as an infant, she developed control over her hair. After an accident left her amnesiac, she was manipulated into joining the villainous Frightful Four as "Madame Medusa", fighting the Fantastic Four, before recovering her memory and returning to Attilan. She married Black Bolt and has frequently served as the public voice and acting ruler of the Inhumans because her husband's voice is lethally destructive. Their son is Ahura. Medusa has been a member of the Fantastic Four, the Avengers-affiliated Future Foundation, and a central figure in Inhuman storylines including the release of the Terrigen clouds.

Powers and abilities

Medusa can psychokinetically control the movement of her hair, which is far stronger and more durable than ordinary hair: she can elongate it, lift weights exceeding a ton, bind opponents, and manipulate objects with fine precision. She also possesses the enhanced strength, speed, and durability typical of Inhumans.

In other media

Serinda Swan portrayed Medusa in the 2017 television series Inhumans. The character has also appeared in animated series including Fantastic Four (1994), Ultimate Spider-Man, Hulk and the Agents of S.M.A.S.H., and Avengers Assemble, and in video games such as Marvel: Ultimate Alliance.
