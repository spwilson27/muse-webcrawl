A symbol is a mark, sign or word that indicates, signifies or is understood as representing an idea, object or relationship. Symbols allow people to go beyond what is known or seen by creating linkages between otherwise very different concepts and experiences — a red cross for medical aid, a dove for peace, a national flag for a country and its values.

The study of symbols and signs is semiotics (Peirce, Saussure). Symbols are central to religion (cross, crescent, Om), mathematics (π, ∞, +), science (element symbols, hazard pictograms), politics and commerce (logos, trademarks), and to depth psychology, where Jung treated symbols as expressions of archetypes. Unlike a sign with a fixed operational meaning (a traffic light), a symbol typically carries layers of association and meaning that can shift between cultures and eras.
