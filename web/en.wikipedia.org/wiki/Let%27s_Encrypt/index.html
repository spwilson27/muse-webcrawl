Let's Encrypt

Let's Encrypt is a non-profit certificate authority that provides free, automated X.509 digital certificates for TLS encryption. Launched publicly in 2016 by the Internet Security Research Group (ISRG), it dramatically lowered the cost and complexity of enabling HTTPS on websites.

TypeCertificate authority
Founded2014 (public launch 2016)
OperatorInternet Security Research Group
CertificatesFree, 90-day validity

How it works

Let's Encrypt uses the ACME protocol, which automates domain validation and certificate issuance and renewal. Software agents such as Certbot run on web servers to obtain and install certificates without manual intervention. Certificates are valid for 90 days, encouraging automated renewal.

Impact

By removing fees and manual steps, the service has issued billions of certificates and is credited with driving the web's rapid shift to HTTPS. It is supported by sponsors and donors including major technology companies and foundations.
