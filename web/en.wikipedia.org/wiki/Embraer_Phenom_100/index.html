The Embraer Phenom 100 is a very light (VLJ) business jet designed and manufactured by the Brazilian aerospace company Embraer. It was certified in December 2008, becoming the first jet in its class with an enclosed lavatory and oxygen masks for passengers. The aircraft is powered by two rear-mounted Pratt & Whitney Canada PW617F turbofan engines.

Development

Embraer launched the Phenom 100 programme in 2005 to enter the emerging very light jet market. The prototype made its maiden flight on 26 July 2007 at São José dos Campos, Brazil. Following a flight-test programme, Brazilian (ANAC) and US (FAA) certification were achieved in December 2008, and the first customer delivery took place the same month. Production was later expanded to Embraer's facility in Melbourne, Florida.

Variants and upgrades

The Phenom 100E introduced multifunction spoilers in 2014. The Phenom 100EV, announced in 2016, added the Garmin G3000-based Prodigy Touch flight deck and higher-thrust PW617F1-E engines for improved hot-and-high performance. The Phenom 100EX, unveiled in 2023, refreshed the cabin and avionics, including autothrottle as an option.

Specifications

The Phenom 100 typically seats four passengers in a club configuration (with options for up to six or seven occupants including a belted lavatory seat and single-pilot operation). It has a maximum range of about 1,178 nautical miles (2,182 km) with four occupants and NBAA IFR reserves, a maximum cruise speed around Mach 0.70, and a service ceiling of 41,000 ft (12,500 m).
