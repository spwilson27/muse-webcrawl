Incheon International Airport (IATA: ICN, ICAO: RKSI) is the main international airport serving Seoul, the capital of South Korea, and one of the largest and busiest airports in the world. Located on reclaimed land between Yeongjong and Yongyu islands, west of the city of Incheon, it opened on 29 March 2001, replacing Gimpo International Airport as Seoul's principal international gateway.

History

Construction began in 1992 on a tidal-flat site and proceeded in phases. Terminal 1, designed by Fentress Architects, opened in 2001; the satellite Concourse followed in 2008, and the larger Terminal 2 — designed for the 2018 Pyeongchang Winter Olympics and used by Korean Air and its SkyTeam partners — opened in January 2018. A fourth runway opened in 2021, with further expansion of Terminal 2 completing in the mid-2020s.

Facilities and reputation

Incheon is regularly ranked among the world's best airports in Skytrax and Airports Council International surveys, noted for fast processing, cleanliness, a golf course, ice rink, casino, gardens, a Korean culture museum, and transit facilities. It is a major cargo hub as well as a passenger hub for Korean Air and Asiana Airlines, and is connected to Seoul by the AREX airport railway and expressways across the Incheon Bridge and Yeongjong Bridge.
