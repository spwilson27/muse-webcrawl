Þorramatur

Þorramatur (food of the þorri) is a selection of traditional Icelandic food, consisting mainly of meat and fish products cured in various traditional ways, served at the midwinter festival þorrablót.

Contents

The assortment, typically served buffet-style on wooden troughs, includes hákarl (cured shark), svið (singed sheep's head), sviðasulta (head cheese), lifrarpylsa (liver sausage), blóðmör (blood pudding), harðfiskur (dried fish), smoked lamb (hangikjöt), and items pickled in sour whey (súrmatur) such as ram's testicles and seal flippers.

Tradition

These preservation methods date from a time when food had to last through the Icelandic winter. The dishes are washed down with brennivín, Iceland's caraway-flavoured schnapps.
