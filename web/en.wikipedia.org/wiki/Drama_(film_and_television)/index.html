Drama (film and television)

In film and television, drama is a category of narrative fiction intended to be more serious than humorous in tone. Dramas rely on the emotional and relational development of realistic characters facing emotional themes such as loss, addiction, prejudice, moral conflict, and social issues.

Subgenres

Drama encompasses many subgenres, including crime drama, legal drama, medical drama, historical drama, romantic drama, teen drama, and melodrama. In television, the format ranges from continuing serials and soap operas to limited series and single plays. In the early days of television, "kitchen sink" realism influenced British drama, while anthology series defined the American "Golden Age of Television".

Relationship to other genres

Unlike comedy, drama focuses on emotional development rather than laughter, though the two are often blended as dramedy. Award bodies such as the Academy Awards and the Emmys maintain separate drama categories.
