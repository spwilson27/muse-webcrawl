GameStop

GameStop Corp.
Type: Public (NYSE: GME)
Industry: Retail (video games and consumer electronics)
Founded: 1984 (as Babbage's)
Headquarters: Grapevine, Texas, United States

GameStop Corp. is an American video game, consumer electronics, and gaming merchandise retailer. The company is headquartered in Grapevine, Texas, and is the largest video game retailer worldwide. As of early 2024 it operated over 4,000 stores, including locations in Canada, Australia (under the EB Games brand), and Europe.


A GameStop store

History

The company traces its origins to Babbage's, a software retailer founded in Dallas in 1984. Through a series of mergers and acquisitions — including NeoStar Retail Group, Software Etc., FuncoLand and EB Games — it became GameStop in 2000 and went public in 2002. It expanded aggressively in the 2000s before facing pressure from digital game distribution in the 2010s.

Short squeeze

In January 2021, GameStop became the centre of a short squeeze driven by retail investors coordinating on the Reddit community r/wallstreetbets. The stock rose from under $20 to an intraday high of $483, causing large losses at hedge funds such as Melvin Capital and prompting congressional hearings and the film Dumb Money (2023). The episode turned GameStop into the archetypal "meme stock".
