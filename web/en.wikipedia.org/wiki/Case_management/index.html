Case management

Case management is a collaborative process of assessment, planning, facilitation and advocacy for options and services to meet an individual's needs through communication and available resources. The term is used in several fields with related but distinct meanings.

Applications

In health and social care, a case manager coordinates services for clients with complex needs, such as people with chronic illness, disabilities or mental health conditions. In law, case management refers to courts' supervision of the progress of litigation, including scheduling, discovery and settlement conferences. In business and information systems, it describes the handling of discrete units of work — cases — through a defined workflow.
