North County Transit District

The North County Transit District (NCTD) is the public transportation agency for northern San Diego County, California. Created by the California legislature in 1975 (as the North San Diego County Transit Development Board), it provides bus, commuter rail, light rail, and paratransit service to more than 340,000 residents across about 1,020 square miles.

Services

NCTD's best-known service is the Coaster, a commuter rail line running between Oceanside and downtown San Diego along the coastal Surf Line, inaugurated in 1995. The Sprinter, a diesel light-rail line between Oceanside and Escondido, opened in 2008. Bus services operate under the Breeze brand, flexible curb-to-curb services as Flex, and ADA paratransit as Lift.

The district owns the San Diego Subdivision of the coastal rail corridor and the Escondido Branch, hosting Amtrak Pacific Surfliner and Metrolink trains as well as BNSF freight. NCTD coordinates fares and planning with the San Diego Metropolitan Transit System (MTS) and the San Diego Association of Governments (SANDAG).

Governance

NCTD is governed by a board composed of representatives from the nine North County cities (Carlsbad, Del Mar, Encinitas, Escondido, Oceanside, San Marcos, Solana Beach, and Vista) and the San Diego County Board of Supervisors. Its administrative offices are in Oceanside.
