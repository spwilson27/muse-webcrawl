Bud Cort

Bud Cort (born Walter Edward Cox, March 29, 1948) is an American actor. He is best known for starring in two landmark films of the New Hollywood era: Robert Altman's Brewster McCloud (1970) and Hal Ashby's Harold and Maude (1971).

Career

Cort, from Rye, New York, was discovered while studying at New York University and appeared in Altman's M*A*S*H before being cast as the bird-obsessed Brewster McCloud. His performance as the death-obsessed young Harold Chasen opposite Ruth Gordon in Harold and Maude made the film a cult classic and earned him Golden Globe and BAFTA nominations.

Later life and work

In 1979 Cort was seriously injured in a car accident on the Hollywood Freeway, which interrupted his career and required years of recovery and surgeries. He later returned to acting in supporting roles and voice work, appearing in films such as Heat, Dogma, Coyote Ugly, Pollock, and The Life Aquatic with Steve Zissou, and voiced Toyman in DC animated series.
