TV 2 (Denmark)

TV 2 is a Danish government-owned public television station, founded in 1986 and broadcasting since 1 October 1988. Based in Odense, it broke the monopoly of Danmarks Radio (DR) and became Denmark's most-watched television channel. Although publicly owned, TV 2 is financed mainly by advertising, unlike DR, which is funded by the public media licence.

Programming and structure

TV 2's schedule centres on news (TV 2 Nyhederne), sport, Danish drama and entertainment. Regional programming is provided by eight affiliated regional stations, such as TV Syd and TV 2/Fyn, whose bulletins air within the national schedule. The group also operates niche channels including TV 2 Charlie, TV 2 Zulu, TV 2 Film, TV 2 News, and TV 2 Sport, and the streaming service TV 2 Play (formerly TV 2 Sumo). Repeated government plans to privatize TV 2 have been proposed but not carried through.
