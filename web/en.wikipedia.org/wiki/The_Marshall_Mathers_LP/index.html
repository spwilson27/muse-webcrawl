The Marshall Mathers LP

The Marshall Mathers LP is the third studio album by American rapper Eminem, released on May 23, 2000, by Aftermath Entertainment and Interscope Records. Produced largely by Dr. Dre, Mel-Man, and Eminem himself, it was recorded after the sudden fame of The Slim Shady LP and reflects on celebrity, media criticism, and his troubled upbringing.

Content

The album features the singles "The Real Slim Shady", "The Way I Am", and "Stan", the last a narrative about an obsessive fan featuring a sample of Dido's "Thank You", widely regarded as one of the greatest hip hop songs of all time. Other tracks include "Kim", "Kill You", "Bitch Please II", and the D12 collaboration "Under the Influence". Its violent and profane lyrical content drew heavy criticism from politicians and advocacy groups, and it was debated in the United States Senate; it was also criticised for perceived misogyny and homophobia, which Eminem defended as the voice of his Slim Shady persona.

Commercial performance and legacy

The album sold 1.76 million copies in its first week in the United States, then a record for a rap album, and went on to sell more than 11 million copies in the US and over 20 million worldwide, making it one of the best-selling albums of all time. It won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album. A sequel, The Marshall Mathers LP 2, was released in 2013. The album is frequently ranked among the greatest hip hop albums ever made.
