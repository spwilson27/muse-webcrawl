Ofcom

The Office of Communications, commonly known as Ofcom, is the government-approved regulatory and competition authority for the broadcasting, telecommunications and postal industries of the United Kingdom. It was established under the Office of Communications Act 2002 and received its full authority from the Communications Act 2003.

Ofcom regulates television, radio, video-on-demand, fixed and mobile telecoms, postal services and the radio spectrum. Under the Online Safety Act 2023, its remit expanded to regulating online services for harmful content. It is accountable to Parliament and funded by fees from the industries it regulates.
