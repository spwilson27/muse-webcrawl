Walt Disney Studios is a major film studio and entertainment division of the Walt Disney Company based at the Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, California. It produces and distributes live-action and animated feature films under banners including Walt Disney Pictures, Walt Disney Animation Studios, Pixar Animation Studios, Marvel Studios, Lucasfilm, 20th Century Studios and Searchlight Pictures. It is one of the "Big Five" major American film studios.


The Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, California

History

The division traces its origin to 1923, when Walt and Roy O. Disney founded the Disney Brothers Cartoon Studio. Breakthroughs included Steamboat Willie (1928) and Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), the first full-length animated feature produced in the United States. Through later decades the studio expanded into live-action features, television tie-ins and distribution through Buena Vista.

Under the leadership of Jeffrey Katzenberg and later studio heads, Disney enjoyed an animation renaissance beginning in 1989 with The Little Mermaid. Acquisitions transformed the division in the 21st century: Pixar (2006), Marvel Entertainment (2009), Lucasfilm (2012) and the film assets of 21st Century Fox (2019) brought franchises such as the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Star Wars and Avatar under the studio umbrella. Its films have grossed tens of billions of dollars worldwide.

Structure

The studio's theatrical releases are distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures, and its content also feeds the Disney+ and Hulu streaming services. Home entertainment and music operations, including Walt Disney Records and Hollywood Records, are associated with the division's broader entertainment businesses.
