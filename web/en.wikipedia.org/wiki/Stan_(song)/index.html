"Stan" is a song by American rapper Eminem featuring British singer Dido. It was released on November 21, 2000, as the third single from Eminem's third studio album, The Marshall Mathers LP (2000). The song is widely regarded as one of Eminem's best songs and one of the greatest hip hop songs of all time. In 2017, the word "stan" was added to the Oxford English Dictionary, and it was further recognised by Merriam-Webster in 2019, in reference to this song.

Content

The song tells the story of a fictional obsessive fan named Stanley "Stan" Mitchell who writes a series of increasingly unhinged letters to Eminem. Through the letters, Stan's admiration turns to anger and instability; in the final verse, Eminem writes back, realising too late that Stan has driven his car off a bridge with his pregnant girlfriend in the trunk. The track samples Dido's "Thank You" (marking the first use of sampling in Eminem's catalog), which provides the refrain.

Music video

The music video, directed by Dr. Dre and Phillip Atwell, stars Devon Sawa as Stan and Dido as his girlfriend. A censored version was frequently shown on MTV, and the full-length version dramatises the letter-writing narrative and the fatal crash.

Reception and legacy

"Stan" reached number one in several countries, including the United Kingdom and Australia, and peaked at number 51 on the US Billboard Hot 100. It was certified diamond in the United States in 2023. Critics praised its storytelling and psychological depth; Rolling Stone ranked it among the 500 greatest songs of all time. The term "stan" has entered popular usage to describe an extremely devoted, sometimes obsessive, fan.

Live performances

Eminem performed "Stan" with Elton John at the 43rd Annual Grammy Awards in 2001, a performance widely noted at the time given criticism of Eminem's lyrics by gay rights groups.
