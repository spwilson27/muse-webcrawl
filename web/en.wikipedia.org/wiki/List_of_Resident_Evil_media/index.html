List of Resident Evil media

Resident Evil, known in Japan as Biohazard, is a Japanese horror media franchise created by Capcom. Beginning with the 1996 video game, the franchise has expanded into numerous sequels, remakes, spin-offs, live-action and animated films, novels, comics and merchandise, centred on stories of biological weapons and zombie outbreaks, and is among the best-selling video game franchises of all time.
