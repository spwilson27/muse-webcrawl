Jagdpanzer IV

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A Jagdpanzer IV preserved at a museum

The Jagdpanzer IV was a German tank destroyer of the Second World War, built on the chassis of the Panzer IV tank. Developed in response to a requirement for a turretless vehicle mounting a powerful anti-tank gun, it replaced the Sturmgeschütz III in some roles and was produced from late 1943 until the end of the war.

Design

The vehicle carried a 7.5 cm PaK 39 L/48 gun in a low, sloped superstructure with frontal armour up to 80 mm in later variants. A further variant mounted the longer 7.5 cm PaK 42 L/70 gun of the Panther, designated Jagdpanzer IV/70; an interim version used the Alkett design with a raised superstructure. In total roughly 2,000 examples of all variants were built.

Service

Jagdpanzer IVs served with the anti-tank battalions of panzer and panzergrenadier divisions on all fronts from 1944. Crews valued its low silhouette and powerful gun, though the long L/70 gun made early versions nose-heavy and difficult to drive cross-country.
