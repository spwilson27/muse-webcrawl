RAF Rudloe Manor

RAF Rudloe Manor was a Royal Air Force station near Box and Corsham in Wiltshire, England. The site is associated with a manor house, nearby underground workings in the Bath stone quarries, and a long history of communications and support functions.

History

The Rudloe area became important during the Second World War, when dispersed and underground facilities in Wiltshire supported British air and government operations. Rudloe Manor served administrative and communications roles and was connected with the wider Corsham complex, including signals, logistics and later data and communications units.

Later use

After the Cold War the station&rsquo;s role declined as RAF units consolidated. Parts of the site were disposed of or adapted for other defence and commercial purposes, while the manor and surrounding landscape remain reminders of the area&rsquo;s layered military history.
