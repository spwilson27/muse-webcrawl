Match Game

Match Game
Also known as: The Match Game
Genre: Game show
Created by: Mark Goodson
Original network: NBC, CBS, ABC
Original release: 1962 – present (various incarnations)

Match Game is an American television panel game show that premiered on NBC in 1962 and has been revived several times over the decades. Two contestants compete to match answers given by celebrity panelists to fill-in-the-blank questions.

Gameplay

The best-known version of the show, which aired on CBS from 1973 to 1979, was hosted by Gene Rayburn. Contestants answered questions such as "Dumb Dora is so dumb, she tried to..." and attempted to match the answers written on cards by a panel of six celebrities. The celebrity panel in the 1970s version regularly included Brett Somers, Charles Nelson Reilly, and Richard Dawson.

The final round, the "Super Match," offered a big prize in which the contestant tried to match the most popular answer to a phrase, and a celebrity in a head-to-head match for up to $5,000.

Revival versions

After the original NBC run (1962–1969) and the popular CBS run, the show was revived in syndication, on ABC in 1990, in syndication in 1998, and on ABC again in 2016 hosted by Alec Baldwin.

Legacy

Match Game is remembered for its double-entendre humor, loose and comedic atmosphere, and its influential role in the golden age of American game shows. Its format has been adapted internationally, including the British version Blankety Blank.
