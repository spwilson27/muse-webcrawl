The New Mutants (film)

Directed by: Josh Boone
Starring: Maisie Williams, Anya Taylor-Joy, Charlie Heaton, Blu Hunt
Released: 2020
Box office: $49 million

The New Mutants is a 2020 American superhero horror film based on the Marvel Comics team of the same name. Directed by Josh Boone, it is the thirteenth and final instalment of 20th Century Fox's X-Men film series.

Plot

Five young mutants — Dani Moonstar, Rahne Sinclair (Wolfsbane), Illyana Rasputin (Magik), Sam Guthrie (Cannonball), and Roberto da Costa (Sunspot) — are held in a secret hospital run by Dr. Cecilia Reyes, supposedly to learn control of their powers. As terrifying manifestations of their deepest fears stalk the facility, the teenagers discover it is controlled by the Essex Corporation and that they are being groomed as killers.

Production and release

Filmed in 2017, the movie endured a famously troubled path to release: planned reshoots never materialized, Disney's acquisition of Fox intervened, and the COVID-19 pandemic delayed it further. It finally opened in August 2020 to mixed reviews and modest box office, closing out the Fox X-Men era before Marvel Studios absorbed the franchise.
