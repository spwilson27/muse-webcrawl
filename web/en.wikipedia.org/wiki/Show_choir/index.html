Show choir

A show choir (also known as a "glee club" or "swing choir") is a musical ensemble that combines choral singing with choreographed dance, sometimes within an overarching theme. Show choirs are primarily active in the United States, especially in high schools and middle schools, and compete in regional and national competitions.

History

The format developed in the mid-twentieth century from school swing choirs and was popularized in the 1970s by touring collegiate groups. By the 1980s and 1990s, competitive show choir circuits had become established, particularly in the Midwest.

Competitions

Competitive groups are judged on vocals, choreography, show design, and overall effect. Performers are typically accompanied by a live student or professional band, and many shows feature costume changes, props, and elaborate sets.

In popular culture

The television series Glee (2009–2015) brought show choir to a wide international audience and drove renewed interest in school programs.
