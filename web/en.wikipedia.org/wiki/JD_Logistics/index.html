JD Logistics, Inc. (Chinese: 京东物流) is a Chinese supply chain and logistics services provider and a subsidiary of the e-commerce company JD.com. It operates one of the largest integrated supply chain networks in China, providing warehousing, transportation, last-mile delivery, and technology-driven logistics solutions to both JD.com's retail business and external clients.

History

JD.com began building its own logistics network in 2007, a strategic decision that distinguished it from rival platforms that relied on third-party couriers. The network became known for same-day and next-day delivery coverage across most of China. In April 2017 the logistics operation was spun off as an independent business group, JD Logistics, and began opening its infrastructure and services to third-party merchants and enterprises.

Operations

JD Logistics operates an extensive warehouse network, including highly automated "Asia No. 1" fulfilment centres that employ autonomous mobile robots, automated sorting systems, and AI-based inventory planning. It also develops and deploys delivery drones and autonomous delivery vehicles, piloted in rural areas and on university campuses. Its services span express delivery, freight, cold chain logistics for fresh food and pharmaceuticals, and cross-border logistics.

Listing and expansion

JD Logistics listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in May 2021, raising approximately US$3.2 billion in one of the year's largest Hong Kong IPOs. In 2022 it acquired a majority stake in the Chinese logistics company Deppon Logistics, expanding its less-than-truckload freight capabilities. Revenue passed 100 billion RMB annually, with external customers accounting for a growing majority of sales.
