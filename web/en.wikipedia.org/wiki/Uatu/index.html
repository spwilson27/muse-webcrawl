Uatu

Uatu, often known simply as the Watcher, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, he first appeared in Fantastic Four #13 (1963). He is a member of the Watchers, an ancient and immensely powerful race sworn to observe the universe without interfering.

Fictional biography

Assigned to watch over Earth and its solar system from his home in the Blue Area of the Moon, Uatu repeatedly broke his oath of non-interference to aid the Fantastic Four and other heroes, most famously during the first coming of Galactus. His interventions eventually led to his trial by his own people. Uatu also serves as the narrator of the What If...? alternate-reality stories.

In other media

Uatu, voiced by Jeffrey Wright, is the narrator and framing character of the animated series What If...? on Disney+, in which he gradually moves from observer to participant in the multiversal conflict.
