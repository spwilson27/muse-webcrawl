Mehi River

The Mehi River is a perennial river of the Murray–Darling basin in northern New South Wales, Australia. It is a distributary of the Gwydir River.

Course

The Mehi branches from the Gwydir River near Pallamallawa and flows generally westward, passing the town of Moree, before joining the Barwon River near Collarenebri. The river is about 314 kilometres (195 mi) long.

Use

The Mehi supports irrigation agriculture, particularly cotton growing, in the Moree Plains region, and is subject to significant water regulation and extraction.
