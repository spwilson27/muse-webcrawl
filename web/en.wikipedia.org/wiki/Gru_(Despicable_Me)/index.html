Felonius Gru, usually known simply as Gru, is a fictional character and the protagonist of the Despicable Me franchise, produced by Illumination. He is voiced by Steve Carell. Originally a supervillain aided by his army of Minions and the inventor Dr. Nefario, Gru adopts three orphaned girls — Margo, Edith, and Agnes — as part of a scheme to steal the Moon, but instead becomes their devoted father and reforms.

Character

Gru is tall, bald, and broad-shouldered with a distinctive physique, a long striped scarf, and an exaggerated, indeterminate Eastern-European-sounding accent. His villainous ambitions are rooted in a childhood of maternal disapproval from his mother Marlena, who deflated his dream of going to the Moon. His arsenal includes freeze rays, fart guns, and a variety of oversized vehicles.

Appearances

Gru stars in Despicable Me (2010), Despicable Me 2 (2013), in which he is recruited by the Anti-Villain League and marries agent Lucy Wilde; Despicable Me 3 (2017), which introduces his twin brother Dru; and Despicable Me 4 (2024), which introduces his infant son Gru Jr. A young Gru is the protagonist of Minions: The Rise of Gru (2022), set in the 1970s. The character has become one of the most recognisable faces of the franchise and of Illumination's brand generally.

Reception

Carell's performance and the character's arc from villain to father have been widely praised, and the Despicable Me series became the highest-grossing animated film franchise of all time following the success of its later instalments.
