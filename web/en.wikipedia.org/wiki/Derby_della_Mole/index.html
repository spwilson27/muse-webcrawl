Derby della Mole

The Derby della Mole, also known as the Turin Derby, is the local football derby in Turin, Italy, contested between Juventus and Torino. It is named after Turin's most famous landmark, the Mole Antonelliana. The first match was played in January 1907, making it one of the oldest derbies in Italian football.

Background

Torino was founded in 1906 by dissidents from Juventus, including former Juventus president Alfredo Dick, which gave the rivalry an edge from its inception. Traditionally, Juventus drew support from across Italy and the bourgeoisie of the Agnelli family and FIAT, while Torino were seen as the club of the local working class and older Turin families. Torino's finest era, the Grande Torino of the 1940s, won five consecutive Serie A titles before the team was killed in the Superga air disaster of 1949. Juventus have dominated the fixture in recent decades, though Torino's passionate support keeps the derby fiercely contested.
