Police station

A police station (sometimes called a station house) is a facility operated by a police force that serves as the workplace of police officers and other staff. These facilities often contain offices and accommodation for personnel and vehicles, along with locker rooms, temporary holding cells and interview/interrogation rooms.

Functions

Police stations serve as points of contact between the public and the police: members of the public can report crimes, surrender lost property, and seek assistance there. Larger stations may house detective units, custody suites, evidence stores and administrative offices, while small community stations and police boxes handle local enquiries.

The area served by a station is sometimes called a precinct (in US usage) or division. Custody procedures in many jurisdictions require a custody officer to be responsible for detainees held in station cells.
