Manga Plus

Manga Plus (stylised MANGA Plus) is an online manga platform and mobile application operated by the Japanese publisher Shueisha. Launched internationally in January 2019, it offers simultaneous free release of new chapters of series from Weekly Shōnen Jump and other Shueisha magazines alongside their Japanese publication.

Service

The service was created to reach overseas readers directly and combat scanlation piracy. Titles have included One Piece, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, and Spy × Family, with chapters in English and other languages. A subscription tier, Manga Plus MAX, was later added for access to complete back catalogues.
