Steve Ignorant (born Steven Williams, 1957) is a British singer and artist. He co-founded the anarcho-punk band Crass in 1977 with Penny Rimbaud at Dial House in Essex, taking his stage name as a statement of intent. His stark, declamatory vocal style and advocacy of anarchism, animal rights and direct action made Crass central to the anarcho-punk movement.

Crass disbanded in 1984 as planned. Ignorant subsequently performed with Conflict and formed Schwartzeneggar and the Stratford Mercenaries. In 2007–2008 he toured performing Crass songs under the title "The Feeding of the 5000", including dates in the United States, and later fronted the acoustic project Slice of Life. He has also worked as a volunteer lifeboat crew member in Sea Palling, Norfolk, and as a wood sculptor; his memoir and artwork have been exhibited and published.
