Yum! Brands, Inc. is an American multinational fast food corporation based in Louisville, Kentucky. It is one of the world's largest restaurant companies, operating or franchising more than 59,000 restaurants in over 155 countries under the KFC, Taco Bell, Pizza Hut, and The Habit Burger Grill brands.

History

The company was created in 1997 as Tricon Global Restaurants, when PepsiCo spun off its restaurant division, which then consisted of KFC, Pizza Hut, and Taco Bell — chains PepsiCo had acquired in the 1970s and 1980s. In 2002 the company acquired Long John Silver's and A&W Restaurants and renamed itself Yum! Brands, reflecting its stock ticker YUM. It later divested Long John Silver's and A&W in 2011.

Yum! expanded aggressively in China, where KFC became the country's largest restaurant chain. In 2016 the company spun off its Chinese operations into a separate publicly traded company, Yum China Holdings. In 2020, Yum! acquired The Habit Burger Grill for $375 million, its first new major brand in many years.

Operations

Yum! operates through brand divisions for KFC, Taco Bell, Pizza Hut, and Habit Burger, with the overwhelming majority of its restaurants owned and operated by independent franchisees. The company has emphasised digital ordering, delivery, and drive-thru formats, as well as multi-brand expansion in international markets.
