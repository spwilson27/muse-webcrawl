Calvin Lamborn

Calvin B. Lamborn is an American plant breeder credited with developing the sugar snap pea. In 1969, while working for the Gallatin Valley Seed Company in Twin Falls, Idaho, Lamborn crossed a snow pea with a mutant shelling pea to combine thick, edible pods with full-sized sweet peas.

The sugar snap pea

The resulting variety, released in 1979 as the Sugar Snap pea, had plump, entirely edible pods eaten fresh or cooked. It was named an All-America Selections Gold Medal winner in 1979 — the first vegetable to receive the award in decades — and transformed the market for edible-pod peas, spawning the related snap pea industry in fresh produce.

Career

Lamborn devoted his career to pea breeding, developing dozens of pea varieties and later continuing snap pea improvement through his own breeding work in Idaho. His innovation is cited as a landmark of twentieth-century vegetable breeding.
