Marinus of Neapolis

Marinus of Neapolis (c. 440 – c. 500 AD) was a Neoplatonist philosopher born in Flavia Neapolis (modern Nablus) in Samaria. He succeeded Proclus as head (scholarch) of the Academy in Athens in 485.

Bornc. 440, Flavia Neapolis
Diedc. 500
SchoolNeoplatonism

Works

Marinus wrote a biography of his teacher, the Life of Proclus, a key source for late antique Platonism, and commentaries on Plato's Parmenides and Philebus and on Euclid's Data. As a Samaritan convert to pagan Hellenism facing Christian Athens, he briefly fled to Epidaurus.
