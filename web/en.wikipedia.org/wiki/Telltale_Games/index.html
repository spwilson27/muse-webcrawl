Telltale Games

Telltale Games was an American video game developer and publisher based in San Rafael, California, founded in 2004 by former LucasArts employees. The studio specialized in episodic graphic adventure games built around player choice, most famously The Walking Dead (2012), which won numerous Game of the Year awards.

History

Telltale built its reputation with revivals such as Sam & Max Save the World and Tales of Monkey Island, then reached major success adapting licensed properties including The Wolf Among Us, Game of Thrones, Tales from the Borderlands, Minecraft: Story Mode, and Batman: The Telltale Series.

Closure and revival

Citing financial strain, the company underwent a majority studio closure in September 2018, laying off most of its staff mid-development of The Walking Dead: The Final Season, which was completed by Skybound Games. In 2019 LCG Entertainment acquired Telltale's assets and relaunched the brand, later releasing The Expanse: A Telltale Series in 2023.
