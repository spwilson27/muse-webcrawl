List of World Series of Poker Main Event champions

The Main Event of the World Series of Poker (WSOP) is the US$10,000 no-limit Texas hold 'em championship, held annually in Las Vegas since 1970 and widely considered the most prestigious tournament in poker. The winner is considered the world champion of poker for that year and receives a gold bracelet and a multi-million-dollar first prize. The event grew from just 7 entrants in 1971 to a record 10,043 in 2023.

Johnny Moss, Stu Ungar, Doyle Brunson and Johnny Chan are among the few players to win the Main Event more than once; Chan's back-to-back titles in 1987–88 were immortalised in the film Rounders. Chris Moneymaker's 2003 victory as an online satellite qualifier sparked the "poker boom".
