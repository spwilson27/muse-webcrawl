Libya

Libya, officially the State of Libya, is a country in the Maghreb region of North Africa, bordered by Egypt, Sudan, Chad, Niger, Algeria and Tunisia, with the Mediterranean Sea to the north. It is the fourth-largest country in Africa, mostly desert; most of its roughly 7 million people live along the coast. The capital is Tripoli and the second city is Benghazi.

Libya has some of Africa's largest oil reserves. Ruled by Muammar Gaddafi from 1969, the country was torn by civil war in 2011 — with NATO intervention — in which Gaddafi was killed, and again from 2014 to 2020, leaving rival governments in Tripoli and the east. Ancient Cyrenaica was Greek and Roman; the rock art of the Acacus Mountains records a greener Sahara.
