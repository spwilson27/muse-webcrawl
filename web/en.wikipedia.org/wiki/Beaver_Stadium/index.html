Beaver Stadium is an outdoor college football stadium in University Park, Pennsylvania, on the campus of Penn State University. It is the home of the Penn State Nittany Lions football team and is named in honour of James A. Beaver, a former governor of Pennsylvania and president of the university's board of trustees.

History

The stadium opened in 1960 after the steel structure of the earlier Beaver Field was dismantled and moved to the east side of campus, then expanded repeatedly — most notably in 2001, when an upper deck in the south end zone raised capacity above 107,000. A record crowd of 110,823 attended the 2017 game against Michigan.

Significance

With a capacity of 106,572, Beaver Stadium is among the largest stadiums in the world and one of the most recognisable venues in college football. It is famous for its "White Out" games, in which nearly all spectators dress in white, and for its tailgating culture, which temporarily makes it one of the largest gatherings in Pennsylvania.
