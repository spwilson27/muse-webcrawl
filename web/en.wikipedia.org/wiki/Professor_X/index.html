Professor X (Charles Francis Xavier) is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist/co-writer Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in The X-Men #1 (September 1963). Xavier is a powerful mutant telepath, the founder of the X-Men, and the head of Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, where young mutants are trained in secret to use their powers for the benefit of humanity.

Fictional character biography

Charles Xavier was born in New York City to a wealthy family; his father, a nuclear scientist, died when Charles was young. His telepathic powers manifested in childhood, and he read the mind of his future stepfather and later of his stepbrother Cain Marko, who became the villain Juggernaut. After studying at Harvard, Oxford and elsewhere, Xavier travelled the world, served in the Korean War, and met both the mutant who became the Shadow King and Erik Lehnsherr — the future Magneto — whose friendship and later ideological split over mutant–human relations defines much of the X-Men saga.

Confined to a wheelchair after an encounter with the alien Lucifer (in later retellings, after being crushed by falling debris), Xavier founded his school and assembled the original X-Men — Cyclops, Marvel Girl (Jean Grey), Beast, Angel and Iceman — training them to oppose threats such as Magneto's Brotherhood of Evil Mutants while promoting his dream of peaceful coexistence between mutants and humans, a theme frequently read as an allegory for civil-rights struggles.

Powers and abilities

Xavier is among the most powerful telepaths on Earth: he can read and control minds, project illusions, detect mutants at great distances (amplified by the machine Cerebro), project his astral form, and store vast amounts of information. He is also a genius-level scientist, particularly in genetics and mutation.

In other media

The character was portrayed by Patrick Stewart in the 20th Century Fox X-Men films and by James McAvoy as the younger Xavier in the prequel films beginning with X-Men: First Class (2011). Stewart reprised the role in Logan (2017) and Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022). Xavier has also appeared in numerous animated series, beginning with X-Men: The Animated Series (1992–1997).
