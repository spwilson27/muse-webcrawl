Jyothika

Born: 18 October 1978, Bombay (now Mumbai), Maharashtra, India
Occupation: Actress
Years active: 1997–2010, 2015–present
Spouse: Suriya (m. 2006)

Jyothika (born 18 October 1978) is an Indian actress and producer who predominantly appears in Tamil films. She has also acted in some Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi films. One of the most successful actresses in Tamil cinema, she has received several accolades including a National Film Award, five Filmfare Awards South, four Tamil Nadu State Film Awards and the Kalaimamani Award.

Early life

Jyothika was born in Mumbai to a Punjabi father, Shantilal Sadanah, a film producer, and a Maharashtrian mother, Seema Sadanah. She studied at Learner's Academy in Mumbai and later enrolled at Mithibai College. Her sister Nagma is also an actress.

Career

Jyothika made her acting debut in the Hindi film Doli Saja Ke Rakhna (1997), directed by Priyadarshan. She entered Tamil cinema with Vaali (1999), for which she won the Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut – South. She rose to prominence with films such as Mugavaree (2000), Kushi (2000), Rhythm (2000), Dumm Dumm Dumm (2001), Kaakha Kaakha (2003), Perazhagan (2004) and Chandramukhi (2005), the last earning her the Tamil Nadu State Film Award for Best Actress.

After her marriage to actor Suriya in 2006, Jyothika took a sabbatical from acting. She made a comeback with 36 Vayadhinile (2015), a remake of the Malayalam film How Old Are You?, which was a critical and commercial success. She subsequently headlined women-centric films including Magalir Mattum (2017), Naachiyaar (2018), Kaatrin Mozhi (2018), Raatchasi (2019) and Ponmagal Vandhal (2020), and shared screen space with stars in ensemble films such as Chekka Chivantha Vaanam (2018).

Together with Suriya, she founded the production company 2D Entertainment, which has backed several acclaimed films. Her performance in a segment of the anthology Putham Pudhu Kaalai and her later work continued to draw critical praise, and she expanded into Hindi cinema again in the 2020s.

Personal life

Jyothika married her frequent co-star Suriya on 11 September 2006. The couple have two children, a daughter and a son. She is involved with the Agaram Foundation, which works in education.
