EssilorLuxottica SA is a French-Italian multinational corporation specialising in eyewear and ophthalmic optics, headquartered in Charenton-le-Pont near Paris. It is the world's largest company in its industry, active in the design, manufacture and distribution of ophthalmic lenses, frames and sunglasses, and in optical retail.

Formation

The group was created on 1 October 2018 by the merger of France's Essilor, the leading maker of corrective lenses (known for Varilux progressive lenses and Crizal coatings), and Italy's Luxottica, founded by Leonardo Del Vecchio and the largest maker and retailer of frames, owner of the Ray-Ban and Oakley brands and of retail chains such as Sunglass Hut and LensCrafters. The combination joined the two halves of the eyewear value chain in a single vertically integrated group.

Business

EssilorLuxottica sells proprietary and licensed designer frames alongside its lenses, operates thousands of retail stores and e-commerce platforms, and has expanded into smart glasses through a partnership with Meta on the Ray-Ban Meta line, as well as into hearing-aid-enhanced eyewear. The group employs well over 190,000 people worldwide and earns annual revenues in the tens of billions of euros.
