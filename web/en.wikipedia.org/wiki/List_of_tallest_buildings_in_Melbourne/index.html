List of tallest buildings in Melbourne

Melbourne, Australia's second-largest city, has one of the tallest skylines in Oceania, with more than 100 completed buildings over 150 metres. The tallest is Australia 108 in Southbank, completed in 2020, which at 316.7 metres is the tallest building in Australia by roof height. The long-standing previous record holder was Eureka Tower (297.3 m), completed in 2006.

Most of Melbourne's tallest buildings stand in the central business district and the Southbank and Docklands precincts across the Yarra River. A long-planned project, STH BNK by Beulah (the "Green Spine"), has been approved with a tower of about 366 metres that would become Australia's tallest if completed.
