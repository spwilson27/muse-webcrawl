Classic FM (UK)

Classic FM is a British national radio station broadcasting classical music. Launched on 7 September 1992, it was the first national commercial radio station in the United Kingdom and is now owned by Global. It broadcasts on FM (100–102 MHz), DAB digital radio, television platforms and online, and is one of Britain's most listened-to stations, with an audience of several million.

Programming

Classic FM's style is deliberately accessible: it favours famous, melodic works and individual movements over complete long-form pieces, and its presenters use a warm, informal tone unlike the formality of BBC Radio 3. Long-running features include the weekday breakfast and drivetime shows, the evening Smooth Classics, and the annual Hall of Fame chart, voted for by listeners at Easter, in which Rachmaninoff's Piano Concerto No. 2 and Vaughan Williams's The Lark Ascending have repeatedly topped the poll. The station also runs the Classic FM Foundation charity and formerly published a magazine and a record label.

Presenters have included John Suchet, Jane Jones, Alan Titchmarsh, Alexander Armstrong and John Brunning, composer of the drivetime favourite "Pie Jesu".
