Anti-Corruption Foundation

The Anti-Corruption Foundation (Russian: Fond borby s korruptsiyey, FBK) is a non-profit organisation based in Russia founded in 2011 by opposition politician Alexei Navalny. Its aim was to investigate and expose corruption among high-ranking Russian government officials.

Investigations

The FBK published widely viewed online investigations, including Chaika (2015) on the family of the prosecutor general, He Is Not Dimon to You (2017) on Dmitry Medvedev's alleged secret wealth, and Putin's Palace (2021), released after Navalny's return to Russia and arrest, which was viewed over 100 million times on YouTube.

Suppression

Russian authorities repeatedly raided the foundation, froze its accounts and designated it a "foreign agent" (2019). In June 2021 a Moscow court declared the FBK and allied organisations "extremist", forcing their liquidation inside Russia; staff relocated abroad and later relaunched the foundation internationally. Navalny died in prison in February 2024.
