List of largest dams

The largest dams in the world can be ranked by several different measures: the volume of material in the structure, the height of the dam, the capacity (volume) of the reservoir it creates, and the installed hydroelectric generating capacity. Different dams head each list. By structural volume, the Tarbela Dam in Pakistan — an embankment dam across the Indus River — is generally considered the largest. By reservoir volume, Lake Kariba, between Zambia and Zimbabwe, is the largest artificial reservoir. By height, the Nurek Dam in Tajikistan is the tallest dam in the world.

Largest power stations

By installed hydroelectric capacity, the Three Gorges Dam in China (22,500 MW) is the largest power station of any kind in the world, followed by the Baihetan Dam (16,000 MW) and the Itaipu Dam on the Brazil–Paraguay border (14,000 MW).
