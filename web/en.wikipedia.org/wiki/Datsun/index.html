Datsun

Datsun (UK: /ˈdætsən/, US: /ˈdɑːtsən/) is an automobile brand owned by Nissan. It was used for Nissan's exported cars from 1958 to 1986, for its entire production history before that under the company that became Nissan, and again from 2013 to 2022 as a brand of low-cost vehicles for emerging markets.

History

The name derives from the DAT Motorcar Company, whose partners' initials formed "DAT"; its small car of 1931 was called Datson ("son of DAT") and renamed Datsun in 1933 because "son" also means "loss" in Japanese. Nissan-built cars wore the Datsun badge in export markets, most famously the Datsun 240Z sports car, the Bluebird and Sunny saloons, and pickup trucks that established the brand in the United States. In 1981–1986 Nissan phased out Datsun worldwide in favour of unifying its branding as Nissan.

Revival and second discontinuation

Nissan revived Datsun in 2013 as an entry-level marque for Indonesia, India, Russia and South Africa, launching the Datsun Go hatchback. Sales disappointed, and production ended in 2022, closing the brand's second life.
