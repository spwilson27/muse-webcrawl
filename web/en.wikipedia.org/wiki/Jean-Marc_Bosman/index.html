Jean-Marc Bosman

Jean-Marc Bosman (born 30 October 1964) is a Belgian former professional footballer whose legal challenge led to the landmark Bosman ruling of the European Court of Justice in 1995, transforming the transfer system in European football.

The case

When his contract with RFC Liège expired in 1990, Bosman sought a move to French club Dunkerque, but Liège demanded a transfer fee and blocked the move. Bosman sued, and on 15 December 1995 the European Court of Justice ruled that out-of-contract players could move freely within the EU and that quotas on EU foreign players were unlawful.

Aftermath

Although the ruling reshaped football economics, Bosman's own career never recovered; he played in lower divisions and struggled financially despite a settlement.
