Bank of China (Hong Kong) Limited (BOCHK; Chinese: 中國銀行(香港)有限公司) is one of the largest commercial banks in Hong Kong and one of the three note-issuing banks of the Hong Kong dollar, alongside HSBC and Standard Chartered. It is the principal operating subsidiary of BOC Hong Kong (Holdings) Limited, listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK: 2388), and is ultimately controlled by the state-owned Bank of China in Beijing.

History

The Bank of China opened a Hong Kong sub-branch in 1917. During the twentieth century it operated alongside a group of affiliated mainland-linked banks (the "Nanyang group"). In 2001 the Hong Kong operations of Bank of China and its sister banks were merged and reincorporated locally as Bank of China (Hong Kong), and the holding company was listed in 2002 in one of Hong Kong's largest IPOs of the period.

Operations

BOCHK provides retail, corporate and institutional banking, treasury services, insurance and asset management, through one of Hong Kong's largest branch networks. It is the sole clearing bank for renminbi business in Hong Kong, a role it has held since Hong Kong became the first offshore RMB centre. Its headquarters is the Bank of China Tower, the I. M. Pei-designed skyscraper completed in 1990 in Central.
