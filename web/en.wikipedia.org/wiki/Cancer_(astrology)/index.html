Cancer (♋) is the fourth astrological sign in the zodiac, originating from the constellation of Cancer. It spans from 90° to 120° celestial longitude. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this area on average between 21 June and 22 July, beginning at the June solstice; under the sidereal zodiac the dates are roughly a month later because of the precession of the equinoxes.

Symbolism and mythology

The sign is symbolised by the crab. In Greek mythology the crab is Karkinos, sent by Hera to distract Heracles during his fight with the Hydra; Heracles crushed it, and Hera placed it among the stars as the constellation Cancer as a reward. The Moon is the sign's domicile ruler in traditional astrology.

Traits in popular astrology

In popular astrology Cancer is a cardinal water sign, associated with home, family, memory and emotional sensitivity. Astrologers commonly describe those born under the sign as protective, intuitive and tenacious, with a defensive "shell" likened to the crab's. Such characterisations are not supported by scientific evidence; astrology is regarded by the scientific community as a pseudoscience.
