A prefecture-level city (Chinese: 地级市, dìjíshì) is an administrative division of the People's Republic of China ranking below a province and above a county. Despite the name, a prefecture-level city usually governs a large area including an urban core, surrounding towns and extensive rural counties.

Structure

Most provinces are now divided almost entirely into prefecture-level cities; the first, and among the largest, include Guangzhou, Chengdu, Wuhan and Suzhou. Sub-provincial cities form a higher-status subclass with additional economic autonomy. Prefecture-level cities are subdivided into districts, county-level cities and counties.
