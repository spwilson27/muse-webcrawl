Servant of the People
Country: Ukraine
Founded: 2017 (registered 2018)
Key figure: Volodymyr Zelenskyy

Servant of the People (Ukrainian: Слуга народу) is a political party in Ukraine, named after the popular television series of the same name in which Volodymyr Zelenskyy played a schoolteacher who unexpectedly becomes president of Ukraine.

History

The party was founded in late 2017 and registered in 2018. Zelenskyy won the 2019 presidential election in a landslide, and in the snap parliamentary election that followed, Servant of the People won an outright majority in the Verkhovna Rada — the first single-party majority in post-Soviet Ukrainian history.

Ideology

The party has described itself variously as centrist, reformist, and anti-corruption, combining elements of liberalism and populism. Its programme emphasised digital government, economic liberalisation, and ending the war in Donbas.
