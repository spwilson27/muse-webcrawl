Sam & Dave were an American soul and R&B duo who performed together from 1961 until 1981. The duo consisted of Sam Moore (1935–2025) and Dave Prater (1937–1988), whose energetic call-and-response performances earned them the nickname the "Double Dynamite".

Career

Signed first to Roulette Records, the duo found success after moving to Stax Records in Memphis, where they recorded with Booker T. & the M.G.'s and songwriters Isaac Hayes and David Porter. Their hits include "Hold On, I'm Comin'" (1966) and "Soul Man" (1967), the latter winning a Grammy Award and becoming one of the defining records of the soul era.

Legacy

Sam & Dave were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1992. Their stage act — a simulated rivalry and gospel-fuelled interplay — influenced generations of soul, rock and R&B performers.
