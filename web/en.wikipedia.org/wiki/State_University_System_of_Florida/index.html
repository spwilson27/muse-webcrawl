State University System of Florida

The State University System of Florida (SUSF or SUS) is a system of twelve public universities in the US state of Florida. As of 2023, over 430,000 students were enrolled in the system, making it one of the largest public university systems in the United States.

Governance

The system is governed by the Florida Board of Governors, a seventeen-member body created by a constitutional amendment approved by voters in 2002. Each university is additionally overseen by its own board of trustees. The Board of Governors sets system-wide policy, approves new degree programmes, and coordinates strategic planning.
