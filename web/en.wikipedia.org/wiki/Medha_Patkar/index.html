Medha Patkar

Medha Patkar
Born: 1 December 1954, Mumbai, India
Occupation: Social activist
Known for: Narmada Bachao Andolan

Medha Patkar (born 1954) is an Indian social activist known for founding the Narmada Bachao Andolan (Save the Narmada Movement), which campaigned for the rights of people displaced by large dams on the Narmada River, particularly the Sardar Sarovar Dam.

Activism

Trained in social work at the Tata Institute of Social Sciences, Patkar began working with tribal and peasant communities in the Narmada valley in the 1980s. The movement combined mass protest, hunger strikes and litigation to demand fair rehabilitation and to question the costs and benefits of big dams. She is also a co-founder of the National Alliance of People's Movements.

Recognition

Patkar received the Right Livelihood Award in 1991 and has served as a commissioner of the World Commission on Dams. She later entered electoral politics with the Aam Aadmi Party.
