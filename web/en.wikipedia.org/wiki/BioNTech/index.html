BioNTech

BioNTech headquarters in Mainz, Germany.

BioNTech SE is a German biotechnology company based in Mainz that develops and manufactures active immunotherapies for patient-specific approaches to the treatment of diseases. It develops pharmaceutical candidates based on messenger ribonucleic acid (mRNA) for use as individualized cancer immunotherapies, as vaccines against infectious diseases, and as protein replacement therapies for rare diseases.

History

BioNTech was founded in 2008 by the immunologists Uğur Şahin and Özlem Türeci, together with Christoph Huber, based on research at the University Medical Center Mainz and the Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz. The name derives from "Biopharmaceutical New Technologies". The company listed on the NASDAQ stock exchange in 2019.

COVID-19 vaccine

In 2020 BioNTech partnered with the American pharmaceutical company Pfizer (and Fosun in China) to develop an mRNA vaccine against COVID-19. The vaccine, BNT162b2, marketed as Comirnaty, was the first mRNA vaccine ever authorized for general use, receiving emergency authorization in the United Kingdom and the United States in December 2020. Billions of doses were produced, and vaccine revenue transformed the company into one of the largest biotechnology firms in Europe.

Research pipeline

Beyond infectious diseases, BioNTech's pipeline centers on individualized neoantigen-specific immunotherapies for cancer, in which an mRNA vaccine is manufactured for each patient based on the mutation profile of their tumor. The company also works on CAR T-cell therapies, bispecific antibodies, and small-molecule immunomodulators, and acquired the British artificial intelligence company InstaDeep in 2023.
