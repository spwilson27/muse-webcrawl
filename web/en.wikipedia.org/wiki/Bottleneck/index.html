Bottleneck

A bottleneck is a point of congestion in a production system, network, or process that limits overall throughput, named by analogy with the neck of a bottle restricting liquid flow.

Uses

In engineering and manufacturing, the bottleneck is the slowest stage determining capacity; the Theory of Constraints focuses improvement there. In computing, a bottleneck may be CPU, memory, disk or network bandwidth (cf. Von Neumann bottleneck). In traffic, a bottleneck chokes road capacity.

Biology

A population bottleneck is a sharp reduction in population size due to environmental events, reducing genetic diversity — related to the founder effect.

Management

Identifying and elevating constraints, adding buffers, and balancing lines are standard remedies.
