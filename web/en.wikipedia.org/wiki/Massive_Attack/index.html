Massive Attack

Massive Attack are an English electronic music duo from Bristol, formed in 1988 by Robert "3D" Del Naja, Grant "Daddy G" Marshall, and Andrew "Mushroom" Vowles. The group is credited with pioneering the trip hop sound, a genre that emerged from Bristol's sound system culture in the early 1990s, blending hip hop beats, dub, soul, and ambient music.

Career

Massive Attack's debut album, Blue Lines (1991), is widely regarded as the first trip hop album, featuring the hit single "Unfinished Sympathy" with vocalist Shara Nelson. Their second album, Protection (1994), featured collaborations with Everything but the Girl's Tracey Thorn.

The group's third album, Mezzanine (1998), marked a shift toward a darker, rock-influenced sound, with tracks such as "Teardrop" (featuring Elizabeth Fraser of the Cocteau Twins), "Risingson", and "Angel". Mezzanine reached number one in the United Kingdom and is considered a landmark of the genre. Subsequent releases included 100th Window (2003) and Heligoland (2010), as well as the EP Ritual Spirit (2016).

Massive Attack have sold over 13 million albums worldwide and influenced a generation of electronic musicians. Robert Del Naja is also a graffiti artist active before the band's formation and is associated with the artist Banksy. The group is known for its political activism, particularly on climate change and Palestinian rights.
