Mike Richardson (publisher)

Mike Richardson (born 29 June 1950) is an American publisher, writer and producer, best known as the founder of Dark Horse Comics, which he established in 1986 in Milwaukie, Oregon.

Career

Richardson began with the Pegasus Books retail chain before founding Dark Horse, which grew on creator-owned titles and licensed properties including Hellboy, Sin City, The Mask and 300. He has also written comics and produced film and television adaptations through Dark Horse Entertainment.

Recognition

He has been recognised for championing creator rights in comics publishing and for building Dark Horse into one of the largest independent comics publishers in the United States.
