La Défense

Location: West of Paris, France
Type: Central business district
Landmark: Grande Arche

La Défense is a major business district in the Paris metropolitan area, located west of the city proper in the departments of Hauts-de-Seine. It is Europe's largest purpose-built business district, home to the headquarters of many French and multinational corporations.

Development

Planned from the late 1950s by the public development authority EPAD, La Défense was built along the historical axis extending west from the Louvre through the Champs-Élysées and the Arc de Triomphe. Its towers include Tour First and the Société Générale towers.

Grande Arche

The district's emblem is the Grande Arche de la Fraternité, a hollow cube designed by Johann Otto von Spreckelsen and completed in 1989 for the bicentennial of the French Revolution, aligned with the historical axis.
