Borg (Star Trek)

The Borg are an alien group in the Star Trek fictional universe. They are cybernetic organisms (cyborgs) linked in a hive mind called "the Collective," and they seek to assimilate other species' biological and technological distinctiveness into their own through forced cybernetic enhancement. Their emblematic phrase is "Resistance is futile," and their ships are typically vast cubes.

Appearances

The Borg were introduced in the Star Trek: The Next Generation episode "Q Who" (1989), created for the series as a new adversary. They became the franchise's most prominent villains in the two-part episode "The Best of Both Worlds" (1990), in which Captain Jean-Luc Picard is assimilated as Locutus, and in the film Star Trek: First Contact (1996), which introduced the Borg Queen. The character Seven of Nine, a liberated Borg drone, became a central figure in Star Trek: Voyager.

Concept

The Borg embody themes of collectivism, loss of individuality, and technology run amok. Individual Borg, called drones, are surgically altered with prosthetic and nanoprobe technology and share a group consciousness directed toward achieving perfection through assimilation.
