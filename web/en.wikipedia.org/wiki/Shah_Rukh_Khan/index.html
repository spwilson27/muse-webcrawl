Shah Rukh Khan

Shah Rukh Khan (born 2 November 1965), also known by the initialism SRK, is an Indian actor and film producer who works in Hindi cinema. Referred to in the media as the "Baadshah of Bollywood" and "King Khan", he has appeared in more than 100 films and earned numerous accolades, including 14 Filmfare Awards.

Khan began his career with appearances in several television series in the late 1980s and made his film debut in 1992 with Deewana. He established himself with villainous roles in Baazigar (1993) and Darr (1993), and rose to prominence with a series of romantic dramas including Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995), Dil To Pagal Hai (1997), Kuch Kuch Hota Hai (1998) and Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham... (2001).

Career highlights

His acclaimed performances include the title role in Devdas (2002), a NASA scientist in Swades (2004), a hockey coach in Chak De! India (2007) and a man with Asperger syndrome in My Name Is Khan (2010). After a hiatus, he returned with the blockbusters Pathaan and Jawan in 2023.

Other ventures

Khan is co-owner of the production company Red Chillies Entertainment and of the Indian Premier League cricket team Kolkata Knight Riders. He was awarded the Padma Shri by the Government of India in 2005 and has received honorary doctorates for his contributions to cinema and philanthropy.
