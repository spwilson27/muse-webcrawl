Bottle episode

In episodic television, a bottle episode or bottle show is an episode produced cheaply using as few non-regular cast members, effects, and sets as possible. Most bottle episodes are set on a single standing set.

Origin

The term derives from the analogy of a ship in a bottle, or from Star Trek 'ship-in-a-bottle' episodes confined to the starship. They save budget for expensive episodes later in a season.

Examples

Famous examples include Seinfeld's 'The Chinese Restaurant', Breaking Bad's 'Fly', The X-Files' 'Ice', Friends' 'The One Where No One's Ready', and Community's 'Cooperative Calligraphy'. Critics often praise them for character and dialogue focus.

Bottle films

The related concept in film confines action to one location, e.g. 12 Angry Men and Rear Window.
