Maynooth

Maynooth (Irish: Maigh Nuadhat)
County: Kildare, Ireland
Population: about 17,000 (2022)
Known for: Maynooth University and St Patrick's College

Maynooth (Irish: Maigh Nuadhat) is a university town in north County Kildare, Ireland, about 25 km west of Dublin. It is home to Maynooth University and St Patrick's College, the national seminary of the Catholic Church in Ireland, and is one of Ireland's fastest-growing commuter towns.

History

The town's name means "plain of Nuadha," a figure of Irish mythology. Maynooth Castle, founded in the 12th century, was a stronghold of the FitzGerald Earls of Kildare. St Patrick's College was founded in 1795, partly to train Irish priests at home rather than in revolutionary France.

Education

Maynooth University, formally established as an independent university in 1997 from the older college, is one of Ireland's younger universities and draws a large student population relative to the town's size.

Transport

Maynooth lies on the Dublin–Sligo railway line and the Royal Canal, and near the M4 motorway, making it a popular base for Dublin commuters.
