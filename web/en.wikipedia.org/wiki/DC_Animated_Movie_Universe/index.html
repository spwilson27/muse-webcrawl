The DC Animated Movie Universe (DCAMU) is a shared universe of animated direct-to-video superhero films produced by Warner Bros. Animation and DC Entertainment. Loosely based on the New 52 reboot of DC Comics, the series ran from 2013 to 2020 and comprised sixteen films following the Justice League and related characters across a single, continuous storyline — the first such continuity within the DC Universe Animated Original Movies line.

Overview

The continuity was set up by Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox (2013), in which the Flash's alteration of the timeline rebooted the universe. Subsequent films included Justice League: War, Son of Batman, Justice League: Throne of Atlantis, Batman vs. Robin, Batman: Bad Blood, Justice League vs. Teen Titans, Justice League Dark, Teen Titans: The Judas Contract, Suicide Squad: Hell to Pay, The Death of Superman, Reign of the Supermen, Batman: Hush, Wonder Woman: Bloodlines and Superman: Red Son (a loosely connected entry), culminating in Justice League Dark: Apokolips War (2020), in which Darkseid's conquest of Earth led to another reboot of the timeline.

Style and reception

The films shared a consistent character-design style (led by Phil Bourassa) and a darker, more serialised tone than the standalone animated films that preceded them, adapting major New 52-era storylines such as the Court of Owls and the Death of Superman arcs. They were generally well reviewed for their action and long-form storytelling. Following Apokolips War, DC's animated films moved to a new continuity, informally dubbed the "Tomorrowverse", beginning with Superman: Man of Tomorrow (2020).
