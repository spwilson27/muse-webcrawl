The Wolf Man is a 1941 American horror film written by Curt Siodmak and directed by George Waggner for Universal Pictures. It stars Lon Chaney Jr. as Lawrence Talbot, who returns to his father's Welsh estate, is bitten by a wolf while defending a woman, and becomes cursed to transform into a werewolf under the full moon.

Legacy

The film is one of the classic Universal Monsters pictures. Siodmak's script largely created modern werewolf lore, including the ideas that a werewolf can be killed by silver and is marked by a pentagram; make-up artist Jack Pierce designed the famous yak-hair transformation. Chaney reprised the role in several sequels and crossovers, including Frankenstein Meets the Wolf Man (1943). A remake, The Wolfman, appeared in 2010.
