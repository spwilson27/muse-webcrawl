Penn Jillette, the taller and vocal half of Penn & Teller

Penn Fraser Jillette (born 5 March 1955) is an American magician, actor, musician, inventor, television presenter, and author, best known as half of the comedy magic duo Penn & Teller, in which he is the taller, vocal performer, in contrast to his silent partner Teller.

Career

Jillette became interested in magic after seeing a performance by James Randi, whose approach combined conjuring with sceptical debunking. He met Teller in 1974, and the pair — initially with Marc Summers in the Asparagus Valley Cultural Society — developed an act that blended magic with comedy and deliberate exposure of hoaxes and frauds. Penn & Teller became fixtures on Broadway, television, and from 2001 at the Rio in Las Vegas.

Jillette co-hosted the Showtime series Penn & Teller: Bullshit! (2003–2010), which applied sceptical analysis to topics such as alternative medicine, conspiracy theories, and religion. He has written several books, including Cruel Tricks for Dear Friends, God, No! (2011), and the novel Sock. An outspoken atheist and libertarian, Jillette is known for painting one fingernail red — a gesture he has explained variously as a tribute to his mother and as a visual aid during sleight of hand.

Personal life

Jillette is a teetotaller who has never used recreational drugs. He lost a substantial amount of weight in 2014–2015 on a plant-based diet, an experience he described in Presto! (2016). He has served as a fellow of the Cato Institute and was the bassist of the band No God Band.
