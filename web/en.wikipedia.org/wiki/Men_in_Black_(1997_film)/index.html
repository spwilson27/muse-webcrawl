Men in Black (1997 film)

Men in Black is a 1997 American science fiction action comedy film directed by Barry Sonnenfeld, starring Tommy Lee Jones as Agent K and Will Smith as Agent J. It is loosely based on the comic book series The Men in Black by Lowell Cunningham. The film follows two agents of a secret organisation that monitors and polices extraterrestrial activity on Earth, wiping the memories of civilian witnesses with a device called the neuralyzer.

Plot

NYPD officer James Edwards is recruited by the veteran Agent K into the Men in Black after demonstrating unusual ability in pursuing an alien suspect. Renamed Agent J, he is drawn into a crisis: an alien "Bug", Edgar, has crashed on Earth seeking a miniature energy source, "the Galaxy", hidden in a jewel on a cat's collar. To recover it, the Bug steals a human skin suit and launches an attack, while an Arquillian warship threatens to destroy Earth if the Galaxy is not returned. K and J recover the Galaxy, destroy the Bug, and K — after 35 years of service — has his memory wiped and retires, with medical examiner Laurel Weaver joining as the new Agent L.

Reception and legacy

The film was a critical and commercial success, grossing $589 million worldwide and winning the Academy Award for Best Makeup (Rick Baker). Will Smith's title song "Men in Black" was a hit single. The film launched the Men in Black franchise, followed by sequels in 2002 and 2012 and a spin-off in 2019.
