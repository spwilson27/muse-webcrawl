List of the verified oldest people

This list ranks the oldest people whose ages have been verified by an international body such as the Gerontology Research Group (GRG) or LongeviQuest, using birth records and other documents. The oldest verified person ever is Jeanne Calment of France (1875–1997), who died aged 122 years and 164 days — the only person confirmed to have reached 120.

The oldest verified man ever is Jiroemon Kimura of Japan (1897–2013), who died aged 116 years and 54 days. Verification standards require multiple documents spanning a person's lifetime, and many celebrated longevity claims — such as those of Shigechiyo Izumi or various Caucasus claimants — have not been accepted because of inadequate evidence.
