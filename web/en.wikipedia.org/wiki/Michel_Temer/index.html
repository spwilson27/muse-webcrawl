Michel Temer

Michel Miguel Elias Temer Lulia (born 23 September 1940) is a Brazilian politician, lawyer and writer who served as the 37th president of Brazil from 31 August 2016 to 1 January 2019. He previously served as vice president from 2011 to 2016.

Early life and career

Temer was born in Tietê, São Paulo, to Lebanese immigrants. He studied law at the University of São Paulo and became a professor of constitutional law. He entered politics in the 1980s and was elected to the Chamber of Deputies repeatedly, serving as its president three times.

Presidency

Temer assumed the presidency after the impeachment of Dilma Rousseff, whose running mate he had been. His government pursued economic reforms including a constitutional spending cap, labour reform and a proposed pension reform, amid low approval ratings and corruption investigations that did not remove him from office. He was succeeded by Jair Bolsonaro.

Personal life

Temer is the author of several books on constitutional law. He is married to Marcela Temer.
