Henry Hill Jr. (11 June 1943 – 12 June 2012) was an American mobster who was associated with the Lucchese crime family of New York City from 1955 until 1980, when he was arrested on narcotics charges and became an FBI informant. His life story was documented in Nicholas Pileggi's 1985 book Wiseguy and its 1990 film adaptation, Goodfellas, in which he was played by Ray Liotta.

Criminal career

Hill grew up in Brownsville, Brooklyn, of Irish and Sicilian descent, and from age eleven ran errands for the mob crew of caporegime Paul Vario. Because of his mixed ancestry he could never become a "made man", but he participated in numerous rackets alongside Jimmy Burke and Tommy DeSimone. He was involved in the 1967 Air France robbery and, most famously, helped plan the 1978 Lufthansa heist at John F. Kennedy International Airport, at the time the largest cash robbery in American history. He also participated in the Boston College basketball point-shaving scandal of 1978–79.

Informant and later life

Arrested in 1980 for drug trafficking — conducted in defiance of his superiors — and fearing murder by Burke, Hill agreed to cooperate with the FBI. His testimony helped secure dozens of convictions, including those of Vario and Burke. He entered the federal witness protection programme with his family but was expelled in 1987 after further drug offences. He later wrote books, appeared frequently in media, sold artwork and pasta sauce, and struggled publicly with alcoholism. He died of complications of heart disease in Los Angeles in 2012.
