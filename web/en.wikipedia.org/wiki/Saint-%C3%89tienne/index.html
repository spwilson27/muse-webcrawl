Saint-Étienne
Country: France
Region: Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes
Department: Loire (prefecture)
Population: ~172,000 (city)

Saint-Étienne is a city in eastern central France, in the Massif Central, about 60 km southwest of Lyon. It is the prefecture of the Loire department. During the industrial revolution it was a major centre of coal mining, armaments, ribbon-making and bicycle manufacture — the first French railway line, opened in 1827, ran from Saint-Étienne to Andrézieux.

Industry and culture

The city was home to the Manufrance arms and cycle company and the arms manufacturer MAS. Since the decline of heavy industry it has reinvented itself as a centre of design; UNESCO named it a City of Design in 2010, and it hosts the Cité du design and an international design biennial. Its football club, AS Saint-Étienne, was for decades one of France's most successful.
