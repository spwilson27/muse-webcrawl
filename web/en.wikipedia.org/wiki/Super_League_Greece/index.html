The Super League Greece is the highest professional association football league in Greece. Founded in its present form in 2006, replacing the Alpha Ethniki, it is contested by fourteen clubs and operates on a system of promotion and relegation with the Super League Greece 2. The championship's history stretches back to the Panhellenic Championship of 1927–28.

History and dominance

Greek football has been dominated to an unusual degree by the "big three" Athens–Piraeus clubs — Olympiacos, Panathinaikos, and AEK Athens — with Olympiacos, based in Piraeus, winning the great majority of titles (48 league championships), including an era of near-total dominance in the 2000s and 2010s. Only a handful of other clubs have ever been champions: PAOK of Thessaloniki (who won in 2019 and 2024), Aris (in the early championship era), and AEL of Larissa, whose 1988 title remains the only one won by a club outside Athens and Thessaloniki.

Format

The league currently fields fourteen teams. After a regular season in which each club plays the others twice, the top clubs enter championship play-offs and the bottom clubs relegation play-offs. The champions qualify for the UEFA Champions League qualifying rounds, with other European places allocated according to Greece's UEFA coefficient and the Greek Cup result. Historic derbies include the "Derby of the Eternal Enemies" between Olympiacos and Panathinaikos — one of world football's fiercest rivalries — the Athens derby between Panathinaikos and AEK, and PAOK's clashes with the Athenian clubs and with Aris in the Thessaloniki derby.

Challenges

The league has long contended with crowd violence, periodic match suspensions, refereeing controversies — notably the 2018 incident in which PAOK's owner entered the pitch carrying a handgun, leading to a suspension of the league — and financial instability among smaller clubs. Even so, Greek clubs have occasionally excelled in Europe, most notably Olympiacos winning the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2024, the first European trophy for a Greek club.
