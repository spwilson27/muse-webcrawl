The Space Training and Readiness Command (STARCOM) is one of three field commands of the United States Space Force, responsible for the service's training, education, doctrine development, and operational test and evaluation. It was activated on 23 August 2021.

Mission

STARCOM prepares Guardians for the contest in, from, and to space. Its responsibilities include initial skills training and advanced education for Space Force personnel, the development of service doctrine and tactics, the management of the service's ranges and aggressor capabilities, and operational and developmental test of space systems.

Organisation

The command is organised into subordinate deltas, historically including Space Delta 1 (training), Space Delta 10 (doctrine and wargaming), Space Delta 11 (range and aggressor) and Space Delta 12 (test and evaluation), along with detachments and the National Space Test and Training Complex concept for linking live, virtual and constructive training environments.

History

STARCOM grew out of Air Force Space Command's Space Training and Readiness Delta (Provisional), stood up in July 2020 under Colonel Peter J. Flores. On activation as a field command in August 2021, Brigadier General Shawn N. Bratton became its first commander. Its interim headquarters is at Peterson Space Force Base, Colorado.
