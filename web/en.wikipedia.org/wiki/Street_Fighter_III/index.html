Street Fighter III

Street Fighter III is a series of fighting games published by Capcom, beginning in 1997. The first game in the series, Street Fighter III: New Generation, was released for arcades in 1997, followed by two updates: 2nd Impact: Giant Attack (1997) and 3rd Strike: Fight for the Future (1999).

Gameplay

Street Fighter III retained the six-button control scheme of its predecessors but replaced most of the returning cast with new characters, the only veterans being Ryu and Ken. Its signature mechanic is the parry, which lets a player nullify an incoming attack by tapping toward the opponent (or downward for low attacks) at the precise moment of impact, rewarding technical skill. Players also choose one of three "Super Arts" for their character before each match.

Legacy

While the series initially underperformed commercially compared to Street Fighter II, 3rd Strike is now regarded as one of the greatest fighting games ever made, famous for its fluid hand-drawn sprite animation and for "Evo Moment 37", the celebrated 2004 tournament comeback by Daigo Umehara, widely known as the "Daigo Parry".
