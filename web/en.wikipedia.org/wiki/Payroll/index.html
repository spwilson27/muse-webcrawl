Payroll

A payroll is a list of employees of a company who are entitled to receive compensation, as well as other work benefits, and the amounts that each should receive. Along with the amounts that each employee should receive for time worked or tasks performed, payroll can also refer to a company's records of payments that were previously made to employees, including salaries and wages, bonuses, and withheld taxes, or to the company's department that deals with compensation.

A company may handle all aspects of the payroll process in-house or can outsource aspects of it to a payroll processing company. Payroll is a major expense for many businesses and is typically deductible from business income for tax purposes, provided it is accounted for correctly and taxes are remitted on time.

Payroll taxes

Governments require employers to withhold income taxes and social contributions from employees' wages and to remit them to tax authorities. In the United States, payroll taxes fund Social Security and Medicare through the Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA), while employers also pay federal and state unemployment taxes. In the United Kingdom, Pay As You Earn (PAYE) is the system used to collect income tax and National Insurance contributions from employees' pay as it is earned.

Processing

Payroll processing involves tracking hours worked, calculating gross pay, applying deductions for taxes, benefits, retirement contributions and garnishments, and issuing net pay by cheque or direct deposit. Employers must also file periodic reports and year-end summaries (such as Form W-2 in the United States or P60 in the United Kingdom). Errors in payroll can lead to penalties and employee dissatisfaction, so many organisations use dedicated payroll software or bureaus.

Outsourcing

Payroll outsourcing is common among small and medium-sized businesses. Providers typically calculate pay and deductions, make deposits, and handle tax filings. Large multinational employers may instead use global payroll providers to manage different national regulations.
