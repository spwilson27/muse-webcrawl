Worldbuilding

Worldbuilding is the process of constructing a fictional world — its geography, history, cultures, languages, ecologies, and rules — for novels, games, films, and other media.

Notable examples: Tolkien's Middle-earth, Star Wars, A Song of Ice and Fire

Approaches

Creators may build top-down from cosmology or bottom-up from a single village. J. R. R. Tolkien's constructed languages and deep history set the modern benchmark. Worldbuilding serves role-playing games, shared universes, and speculative fiction, balancing internal consistency with storytelling economy — excess detail is sometimes called worldbuilder's disease.
