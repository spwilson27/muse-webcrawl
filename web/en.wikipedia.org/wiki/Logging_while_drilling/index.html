Logging while drilling

Logging while drilling
Field: Petroleum engineering
Part of: Well logging

Logging while drilling (LWD) is a technique of measuring formation properties during the drilling of a borehole, using sensors in the drill string near the bit, rather than lowering instruments on a wireline afterwards.

Measurements

LWD tools measure resistivity, density, porosity, sonic velocity and gamma radiation, transmitting data to the surface by mud-pulse telemetry or storing it in memory.

Advantages

Real-time data helps geosteering and drilling decisions, and measurements are made before drilling fluids invade the formation.
