El Museo del Barrio

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

El Museo del Barrio occupies the Heckscher Building at 1230 Fifth Avenue

El Museo del Barrio, often simply called El Museo, is a museum at 1230 Fifth Avenue, on Museum Mile in East Harlem (El Barrio), New York City. Founded in 1969, it is devoted to the art and culture of Puerto Ricans in New York and, more broadly, of Latin American and Caribbean peoples, presenting exhibitions, a permanent collection, performing arts and educational programmes in Spanish and English.

History

El Museo grew out of the Nuyorican movement and protests by Puerto Rican parents, artists and educators demanding that the neighbourhood's culture be represented in public education. Founded by artist and teacher Raphael Montañez Ortiz, it began in a New York City public school classroom and moved through several East Harlem sites before settling in the Heckscher Building in 1977. Its scope widened over the decades from Puerto Rican art to the wider Latin American and Latinx diaspora; its holiday programmes — notably the Three Kings Day Parade each January and Día de los Muertos celebrations — are neighbourhood institutions.

Collection

The permanent collection of more than 8,500 objects spans over 800 years, from Taíno artefacts and Santos de palo carvings to modern and contemporary painting, sculpture, works on paper, photography, film and installation. The museum undertook a major renovation of its galleries and theatre (El Teatro) in the 2000s–2010s.
