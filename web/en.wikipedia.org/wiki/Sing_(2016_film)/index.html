Sing (2016 film)

Sing is a 2016 American animated jukebox musical comedy film produced by Illumination Entertainment and released by Universal Pictures. It was written and directed by Garth Jennings and co-directed by Christophe Lourdelet. Set in a world inhabited by anthropomorphic animals, the film follows Buster Moon, an optimistic koala theatre owner who stages a singing competition to save his failing theatre. The voice cast includes Matthew McConaughey, Reese Witherspoon, Seth MacFarlane, Scarlett Johansson, John C. Reilly, Tori Kelly, Taron Egerton and Nick Kroll. The film features more than 60 songs from famous artists.

Plot

Buster Moon, a koala who has loved the theatre since childhood, faces foreclosure on his beloved Moon Theatre. Hoping to revive it, he announces a singing competition with a $1,000 prize — but his elderly assistant's glass eye bounces on the keyboard and adds two extra zeros to the flyer, advertising $100,000 he does not have. Hundreds audition; the finalists include Rosita, an overworked pig housewife; Johnny, a gorilla torn between singing and his father's criminal gang; Ash, a punk-rock porcupine; Meena, a shy elephant with a powerful voice; Mike, an arrogant mouse crooner; and Gunter, an exuberant dancing pig. When the deception is exposed and the theatre is destroyed in a flood during a disastrous rehearsal, the disheartened contestants nonetheless regroup, and Buster stages an outdoor show in the ruins that triumphs, winning funds to rebuild the theatre.

Reception

Sing premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2016 and was released in December. It grossed $634 million worldwide against a $75 million budget, becoming one of the most profitable films of 2016. Reviews were generally positive, praising the voice performances and musical numbers. The film was nominated for Best Animated Feature at the Golden Globe Awards.

Sequel

A sequel, Sing 2, was released in December 2021, with the cast reprising their roles as Buster and his troupe attempt to stage a show in glamorous Redshore City.
