Titanic (1997 film)

Titanic is a 1997 American epic romance and disaster film written, directed and co-produced by James Cameron. Starring Leonardo DiCaprio and Kate Winslet, it tells a fictionalised account of the sinking of RMS Titanic in 1912 through the romance between Jack Dawson, a penniless artist, and Rose DeWitt Bukater, a young woman engaged to a wealthy steel heir.

Plot

In 1996, treasure hunter Brock Lovett searches the wreck of the Titanic for the Heart of the Ocean diamond. Elderly Rose recounts her story: boarding the ship in 1912, her romance with Jack, and the collision with the iceberg on 14 April. Jack dies in the freezing water after the sinking; Rose survives, having secretly kept the diamond, which she drops into the ocean at the end of her life.

Production

Cameron, fascinated by the wreck, filmed actual dives to it in 1995. Production built a near-full-scale replica of the ship at Rosarito, Mexico. With a budget of about $200 million it was the most expensive film ever made at the time.

Reception

Released on 19 December 1997, Titanic became the highest-grossing film of all time, earning over $1.8 billion initially and more than $2.2 billion after re-releases. It won 11 Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Director, tying Ben-Hur's record. Celine Dion's theme "My Heart Will Go On" became a global hit.
