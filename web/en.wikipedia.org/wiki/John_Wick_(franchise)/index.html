John Wick (franchise)

John Wick is an American neo-noir action thriller media franchise created by Derek Kolstad and starring Keanu Reeves as the title character, a retired hitman who is forced back into the criminal underworld he had abandoned. The series is noted for its elaborate "gun fu" action choreography and its stylised world of assassins governed by the High Table.

Films

The first film, John Wick (2014), directed by Chad Stahelski, follows Wick as he seeks vengeance against the gangsters who killed the puppy left to him by his late wife. It was followed by John Wick: Chapter 2 (2017), John Wick: Chapter 3 – Parabellum (2019) and John Wick: Chapter 4 (2023), each expanding the mythology of the Continental hotels and the assassin underworld. The films have grossed over $1 billion worldwide in total.

Spin-offs

The franchise expanded with the prequel television series The Continental: From the World of John Wick (2023) and the spin-off film Ballerina (2025), starring Ana de Armas, as well as comic books and video games.

Reception

The series has been praised for its action direction, world-building and Reeves's performance, and is credited with reviving the career of its star and influencing modern action filmmaking.
