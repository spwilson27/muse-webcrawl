List of Jordanian royal consorts

This is a list of Jordanian royal consorts — the spouses of the rulers of the Emirate of Transjordan and the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan since 1921. Jordanian queens have generally held the title of Queen consort; some spouses of kings were accorded the style of Princess instead.
