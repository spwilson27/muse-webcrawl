Kåta is a locality and former farm in Västervik Municipality, Kalmar County, Sweden. The site is notable for the pioneering female farmer and writer associated with the estate and for its long use as a gathering place; the farm buildings and surrounding coastal landscape are typical of the Tjust archipelago region of Småland.

History

The farm at Kåta dates back several centuries as part of the agricultural holdings along the coast near Gamleby and Västervik. In the twentieth century the property gained wider recognition, and it has since been used for educational, scouting, and conference purposes, with visitors drawn by its preserved rural environment and proximity to the Baltic Sea archipelago.

Setting

Kåta lies in a landscape of pasture, oak woodland, and rocky shoreline characteristic of the Småland coast. The surrounding area forms part of a popular region for summer tourism and outdoor recreation.
