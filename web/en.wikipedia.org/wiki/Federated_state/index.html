Federated state

A federated state (also state, province, region, canton, land, governorate, oblast, emirate, or country) is a territorial and constitutional community forming part of a federation.

Definition

Federated states differ from sovereign states in that they do not hold full sovereign powers, as those have been divided between the federated states and the central or federal government. Examples include the states of the United States, the Länder of Germany, the cantons of Switzerland, the states and territories of Australia, and the provinces of Canada.

Powers

The specific powers of federated states vary by federation but commonly include policing, education, transport and local infrastructure. Unlike divisions of unitary states, federated states typically cannot be unilaterally abolished or altered by the central government, and their existence is guaranteed by the federal constitution.
