Na Píobairí Uilleann (Irish for "The Uilleann Pipers") is a non-profit organisation founded in 1968 in Dublin to promote the playing, making and teaching of the uilleann pipes, the characteristic national bagpipes of Ireland. It was established at a time when the instrument had fallen to a handful of active players, and it is widely credited with reviving and sustaining the art.

Activities

The organisation runs classes and the annual Tionól gathering, publishes the journal An Píobaire, maintains an archive and museum at its Henrietta Street headquarters, and supports pipe makers and teachers worldwide. It played a leading role in the successful nomination of uilleann piping to UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, inscribed in 2017.

Na Píobairí Uilleann also develops educational resources, including online lessons and the "PipeCraft" training centre for instrument making in Dublin.
