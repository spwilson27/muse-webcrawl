David Roger Johansen (9 January 1950 – 28 February 2025) was an American singer, songwriter and actor. He was best known as the charismatic lead singer of the proto-punk band New York Dolls and, under the alter ego Buster Poindexter, for the 1987 hit "Hot Hot Hot".

Career

Johansen co-founded the New York Dolls in 1971 in New York City. Their two albums, New York Dolls (1973) and Too Much Too Soon (1974), combined raw rock and roll, glam stage dress and Stones-like swagger; commercially unsuccessful, they proved enormously influential on punk and glam metal. After the Dolls' collapse, Johansen recorded solo albums opening for major acts. In the mid-1980s he reinvented himself as Buster Poindexter, a pompadoured lounge act whose version of the calypso "Hot Hot Hot" (1987) became a mainstream pop hit. He also acted, notably as the Ghost of Christmas Past in Scrooged (1988).

Later years

Johansen returned to roots music with David Johansen and the Harry Smiths, exploring early blues, and reunited the surviving Dolls in 2004 for new albums. Martin Scorsese and David Tedeschi's documentary Personality Crisis: One Night Only (2022) captured his cabaret performances. Johansen died of cancer at his home on Staten Island on 28 February 2025.
