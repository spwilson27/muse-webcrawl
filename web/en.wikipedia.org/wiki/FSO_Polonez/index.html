FSO Polonez

ManufacturerFSO
Production1978–2002
ClassMid-size family car
Body style3/5-door hatchback; saloon, estate, coupé, pick-up variants

The FSO Polonez is a Polish mid-size family car manufactured by FSO in Warsaw from 1978 to 2002. Named after the polonaise dance, it combined bodywork styled with input from Giorgetto Giugiaro with mechanical components derived from the Polski Fiat 125p.

Development and production

Launched as a modern five-door hatchback, the Polonez was exported widely, including to the United Kingdom, and was notable for its safety-oriented design in its early years. Major updates produced the Polonez Caro (1991) with revised styling, plus saloon (Atu), estate (Kombi) and commercial variants. Around 1.06 million were built before production ended in 2002.

Motorsport

The Polonez was rallied in its era, and the rare Polonez 2000 Stratos — of which a handful were built — became a cult performance version using a Fiat twin-cam engine.
