PRINTS (database)

PRINTS is a database of protein family fingerprints — groups of conserved sequence motifs that together form a diagnostic signature for a protein family. It was developed at University College London and later maintained at the University of Manchester.

Approach

Unlike a single consensus pattern, a fingerprint uses several motifs from a multiple sequence alignment, which improves sensitivity and specificity when classifying new sequences. Entries are manually curated with literature references and cross-links.

Use

PRINTS became a founding member of the integrated InterPro resource, through which its fingerprints contribute to automated annotation of protein sequences.
