Excelsior is a city in Hennepin County, Minnesota, United States, on the southern shore of Lake Minnetonka. Founded in 1853 by George Bertram, it is one of the oldest communities on the lake and the first townsite platted on its south shore. The population was 2,355 at the 2020 census.

History

Excelsior grew as a resort town in the late 19th century, served by streetcars from Minneapolis and by steamboats on the lake. The Excelsior Amusement Park operated from 1925 to 1973 and hosted the famous Danceland ballroom. In 1974, the Rolling Stones reportedly visited a local drugstore, an episode linked in local lore to the song "You Can't Always Get What You Want"—a story the band has neither confirmed nor consistently denied.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau, the city has a total area of about 0.69 square miles, of which a portion is water. Its compact downtown along Water Street is known for shops, restaurants, and the Excelsior Commons park on the lake.

Arts and recreation

The restored steamboat Minnehaha, raised from the lake in 1980, offers excursions from Excelsior. The city hosts the Lake Minnetonka 4th of July celebration and other seasonal festivals.
