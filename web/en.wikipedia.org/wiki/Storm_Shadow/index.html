Storm Shadow

Type: Air-launched cruise missile
Manufacturer: MBDA
Range: approx. 550 km (export versions less)
Warhead: BROACH penetrating warhead

Storm Shadow (French service name: SCALP-EG) is a long-range, stealthy, air-launched cruise missile developed jointly by the United Kingdom and France and manufactured by MBDA.

Design

The missile flies at low altitude using terrain-following navigation (GPS/INS and terrain profile matching) and homes in the terminal phase with an imaging infrared seeker matched against a stored target image. Its BROACH two-stage warhead is designed to penetrate hardened bunkers.

Operational use

First used by the RAF in the 2003 Iraq War, Storm Shadow has since been employed in Libya (2011), against Islamic State targets in Syria and Iraq, and supplied by Britain and France to Ukraine from 2023 for strikes on Russian military targets.
