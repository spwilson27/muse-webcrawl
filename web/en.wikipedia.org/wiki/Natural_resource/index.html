Natural resource

Natural resources are resources drawn from nature that are used to support life and meet people's needs. They include sunlight, air, water, land, minerals, fossil fuels, forests, wildlife and fisheries. Any natural substance used by humans can be considered a natural resource.

Classification

Resources are commonly divided into renewable resources, which can be replenished on human timescales — such as timber, freshwater and solar energy — and non-renewable resources, such as coal, oil and metal ores, which form over geological time and can be depleted. Some resources, including clean air and biodiversity, provide ecosystem services that are difficult to replace once lost.

Management

The extraction and consumption of natural resources underpin economic activity but can cause environmental degradation, resource depletion and conflict over access. Sustainable resource management seeks to balance use with conservation for future generations.
