Old Carthusians F.C.

Old Carthusians Football Club is an association football club whose players are former pupils of Charterhouse School in Godalming, Surrey, England. The club was founded in 1876 and is best known for winning the FA Cup in 1881, defeating Old Etonians in the final.

History

Old Carthusians were among the prominent amateur clubs of the early years of organised football in England. They entered the FA Cup from the late 1870s and reached the final in 1881, where they beat the Old Etonians 3–0 at the Kennington Oval. The club were also founder members of the Arthurian League in 1961, a league for clubs of old boys of public schools, and have won the Arthur Dunn Cup, the national cup competition for such clubs, on numerous occasions.

Honours

The club's major honour is the FA Cup victory of 1880–81. Old Carthusians sides have also won multiple Arthurian League titles and Arthur Dunn Cups in the modern amateur era.
