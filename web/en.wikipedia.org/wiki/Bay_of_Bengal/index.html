Bay of Bengal

The Bay of Bengal is the northeastern part of the Indian Ocean, bounded by India and Sri Lanka to the west, Bangladesh to the north and Myanmar and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands to the east. It is the largest bay in the world, covering about 2.6 million square kilometres.

The bay is fed by great rivers including the Ganges, Brahmaputra and Irrawaddy, and its low-lying delta coasts are densely populated and extremely vulnerable to tropical cyclones, which have caused some of the deadliest natural disasters in history, such as the 1970 Bhola cyclone. The summer monsoon forms over the bay, driving rainfall across South Asia.
