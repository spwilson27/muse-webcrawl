Captain Kangaroo

Captain Kangaroo is an American children's television series that aired weekday mornings on CBS for 29 years, from 1955 to 1984, making it one of the longest-running nationally broadcast children's shows. It was created and hosted by Bob Keeshan, who played the kindly Captain in his Treasure House.

Captain Kangaroo
Creator/Host: Bob Keeshan
Network: CBS
Original run: 1955–1984

Format

Set in the Captain's Treasure House, the gentle, low-key programme featured stories, songs, cartoons, and regular characters including Mr. Green Jeans (Hugh Brannum), Bunny Rabbit, Mr. Moose, and Grandfather Clock. Keeshan had previously played Clarabell the Clown on Howdy Doody.

Legacy

The show was widely praised for its calm, respectful approach to children and won numerous awards, including Peabody and Emmy Awards. Keeshan became a prominent advocate for children's television standards.
