Canal & River Trust

The Canal & River Trust is a charitable trust in England and Wales that cares for about 2,000 miles of canals, rivers, reservoirs and docks. It took over the responsibilities of British Waterways in 2012.

Founded: 2012
Type: Charitable trust
Area: England and Wales

Responsibilities

The Trust maintains locks, bridges, aqueducts, towpaths and reservoirs forming the historic inland waterway network, used by boaters, anglers, walkers and cyclists.

Funding

It receives a government grant, boating licence income, property revenue and charitable donations.
