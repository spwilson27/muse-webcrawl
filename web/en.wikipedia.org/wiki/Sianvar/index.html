Sianvar

Sianvar is an American experimental rock supergroup formed in 2013, composed of members of Dance Gavin Dance, A Lot Like Birds, Hail the Sun, and Stolas. The band's name derives from a figure in the lore of guitarist Sergio Medina's projects.

History and music

Sianvar released the EP Sianvar in 2014, followed by the full-length album Stay Lost (2016) on Blue Swan Records and Equal Vision Records. Their sound blends post-hardcore with progressive and math-rock elements, featuring Donovan Melero's high-register vocals and intricate dual-guitar work. The band announced an indefinite hiatus in 2019.

Members

Donovan Melero (vocals), Will Swan (guitar), Sergio Medina (guitar), Michael Franzino (bass), and Joseph Arrington (drums).
