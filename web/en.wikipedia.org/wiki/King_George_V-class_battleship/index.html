King George V-class battleship

The King George V class was a class of five battleships built for the Royal Navy during the Second World War: King George V, Prince of Wales, Duke of York, Anson and Howe. Designed under the limits of the Washington and London naval treaties, they mounted ten 14-inch guns and were protected against 14-inch shellfire.

Service

King George V and Prince of Wales took part in the hunt for the German battleship Bismarck in May 1941; Prince of Wales was sunk by Japanese aircraft off Malaya in December 1941. Duke of York sank the German battlecruiser Scharnhorst at the Battle of the North Cape in 1943. The surviving ships served in the Pacific in 1945 and were scrapped in the 1950s.
