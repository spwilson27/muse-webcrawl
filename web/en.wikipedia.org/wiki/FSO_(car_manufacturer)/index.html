FSO (car manufacturer)

Fabryka Samochodów Osobowych, commonly known as FSO, was a Polish automobile manufacturer based in Warsaw. Founded in 1948 and beginning production in 1951, it built cars under licence from Fiat and later produced its own designs.

History

Early models included the Warszawa and the Syrena; from 1967 FSO built the Polski Fiat 125p, and in 1978 it launched the FSO Polonez, designed with ItalDesign. After the fall of communism, FSO partnered with Daewoo Motors, producing models such as the Lanos and Matiz in Warsaw. The Daewoo-FSO venture collapsed in the 2000s, ownership passed to Ukraine's UkrAVTO, and car production at the Żerań plant ended in 2011.
