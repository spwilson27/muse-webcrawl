PMBOK Guide

A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge, commonly called the PMBOK Guide, is a book published by the Project Management Institute (PMI) that sets out a standard for project management practice. The first edition appeared in 1996, building on a 1987 white paper.

Contents

Editions up to the sixth (2017) organised project management into ten knowledge areas — including scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk and stakeholder management — and 49 processes grouped into five process groups: initiating, planning, executing, monitoring and controlling, and closing. The seventh edition (2021) restructured the guide around twelve project management principles and eight performance domains, reflecting a shift from prescriptive processes toward outcomes and adaptable, value-focused delivery.

Recognition

The PMBOK Guide is recognised as a standard by ANSI and is the principal reference for PMI's Project Management Professional (PMP) certification examinations. It is used worldwide as a common vocabulary and framework for the profession.
