Wonka (film)

Directed by: Paul King

Written by: Simon Farnaby, Paul King

Starring: Timothée Chalamet, Olivia Colman, Hugh Grant, Sally Hawkins, Rowan Atkinson, Keegan-Michael Key

Music by: Joby Talbot; songs by Neil Hannon

Released: 8 December 2023 (UK); 15 December 2023 (US)

Box office: approx. $634 million worldwide

Wonka is a 2023 musical fantasy film directed by Paul King, who co-wrote the screenplay with Simon Farnaby. It serves as a prequel to Roald Dahl's 1964 novel Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, telling the origin story of Willy Wonka, played by Timothée Chalamet, as a young chocolatier arriving in a European city to open his first shop.

Plot

A young Willy Wonka, an inventor and magician with only a handful of silver coins, arrives by ship in a wintry city to seek his fortune selling chocolate made from exotic ingredients gathered on his travels. Tricked into debt by the laundress Mrs. Scrubitt and her enforcer Bleacher, he is trapped working in their wash house alongside other unfortunates, including an orphan girl named Noodle. With their help, and despite the efforts of a corrupt cartel of chocolatiers — Slugworth, Prodnose, and Fickelgruber — who bribe the chocoholic Chief of Police, Wonka sells his miraculous sweets (including "hoverchocs" that let the eater fly) and evades an indenture. A tiny orange Oompa-Loompa named Lofty, who has been pursuing Wonka over stolen cocoa beans, joins his side. In the climax Wonka opens his dream shop, exposes the cartel's hoard of chocolate beneath the cathedral, and learns that Noodle is Slugworth's niece. He finally shares the chocolate his late mother made for him, and resolves to build a factory, hiring Lofty as his taste-tester.

Production

Warner Bros. acquired the rights to the Wonka character in 2016; Paul King, director of the Paddington films, came aboard in 2018. Timothée Chalamet was cast in 2021 without audition, King having been impressed by his high-school musical performances online. Principal photography took place in the United Kingdom, including at Lyme Regis, Oxford, Bath, St Albans, and the Rivoli Ballroom in London. Original songs were written by Neil Hannon of the Divine Comedy, with an underscore by Joby Talbot weaving in melodies from the 1971 film Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory.

Reception

Released in December 2023, Wonka received generally positive reviews praising Chalamet's performance and the film's warmth and production design, and it became a major commercial success, grossing about $634 million worldwide against a budget of around $125 million — the eighth-highest-grossing film of 2023. It was nominated for the BAFTA Award for Outstanding British Film, and Chalamet received a Golden Globe nomination for his performance.
