The King of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan is the head of state of Jordan. The monarchy was established in 1921, when Abdullah, son of Sharif Hussein of Mecca, became Emir of Transjordan under the British Mandate. Transjordan became fully independent in 1946 as the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.

List of rulers

Hussein1952–1999Reigned for 47 years, through the 1967 Six-Day War, Black September, and the 1994 peace treaty with Israel.
Abdullah II1999–presentSon of Hussein and Queen Muna; married to Queen Rania.

Line of succession

The heir apparent is Crown Prince Hussein, the eldest son of Abdullah II, named crown prince in 2009.
