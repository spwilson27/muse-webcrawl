Keysight Technologies, Inc. is an American technology company that manufactures electronic test and measurement equipment and software. Headquartered in Santa Rosa, California, Keysight is the direct descendant of the original Hewlett-Packard test and measurement business: HP's instrument divisions became Agilent Technologies in 1999, and Agilent spun off its electronic measurement group as Keysight in 2014.

History

The company's lineage dates to 1939, when Bill Hewlett and Dave Packard founded HP in a Palo Alto garage with an audio oscillator as their first product. HP's test and measurement operations became the core of Agilent when HP split in 1999. In September 2013 Agilent announced it would separate into two companies; the electronic measurement business took the name Keysight — a blend of "key" and "insight" — and began trading independently on November 1, 2014.

Products

Keysight is the world's largest maker of electronic test equipment. Its catalogue includes oscilloscopes, spectrum and network analysers, signal generators, logic analysers, modular instruments, and design and test software such as the PathWave platform. Its instruments are used in developing 5G and 6G wireless systems, automotive electronics, aerospace and defence, semiconductors and data centres.

Acquisitions

Keysight expanded through acquisitions including Anite (2015), Ixia (2017, for about $1.6 billion), ScienLab, Eggplant (2020), Sanjole, and Spirent Communications (agreed in 2024), strengthening its positions in network test, software test automation and positioning technology.
