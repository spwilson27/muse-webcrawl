Ghosts is an American television sitcom that premiered on CBS on 7 October 2021. It is an adaptation of the British series of the same name created by the Horrible Histories troupe. The series was developed by Joe Port and Joe Wiseman.

Premise

Freelance journalist Samantha "Sam" Arondekar (Rose McIver) and her chef husband Jay (Utkarsh Ambudkar) inherit Woodstone Manor, a crumbling country estate in New York state, and decide to turn it into a bed-and-breakfast. After a fall leaves Sam briefly clinically dead, she awakens able to see and hear the estate's ghosts — spirits of people who died on the property across the centuries, including a Viking explorer, a Revolutionary War officer, a 1920s jazz singer, a hippie, a 1980s scout leader and a 1990s Wall Street broker — while Jay cannot perceive them.

Reception

The series was a ratings success for CBS and has been praised for its ensemble cast and warm tone, receiving multiple renewals. It has run for several seasons and spawned international adaptations of its own, while the contrast between the ghosts' historical viewpoints and modern life provides much of its comedy.
