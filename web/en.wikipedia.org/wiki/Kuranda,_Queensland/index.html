Kuranda, Queensland

Kuranda is a rural town and locality in the Shire of Mareeba, Queensland, Australia. Situated on the eastern edge of the Atherton Tableland some 25 km from Cairns, at the top of the Macalister Range, it is known as the "Village in the Rainforest" and is a major tourist destination, with a population of around 3,000.


The Kuranda Scenic Railway crossing the Stoney Creek Falls bridge.

History

The area is the traditional land of the Djabugay people. Kuranda was surveyed in 1888 as the railway reached the Barron Gorge during construction of the Cairns–Herberton line; the difficult climb, with its tunnels and bridges past Barron Falls, was completed in 1891. In the 1960s and 1970s Kuranda became a centre for alternative-lifestyle settlers and craft markets.

Attractions

Visitors arrive via the Kuranda Scenic Railway or the Skyrail Rainforest Cableway, a 7.5 km gondola over the Wet Tropics World Heritage rainforest. The town's markets, the Australian Butterfly Sanctuary, Birdworld and the Rainforestation Nature Park draw day-trippers from Cairns. Nearby Barron Gorge National Park protects Barron Falls, which flood dramatically in the wet season.
